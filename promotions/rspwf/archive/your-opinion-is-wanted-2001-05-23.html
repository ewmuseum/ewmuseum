Your Opinion is wanted

23 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, I'm new at this type of roleplaying..or "flash" as you all seem to call it. I'm not really new to e-wrestling, I've been doing the AOL version for a good while. However, I just recently found the newsgroup, and the style here varies.
This was just something I typed up for a sample, and I was wondering if its how its done here, or if its any good. Nothing much to mention in it, so I didnt have much to go on..This is where I would like the opinions of such as [name withheld] and the rest who have been doing this for a long time. No need to be nice either, like any of you would be heh.
---------------------------- [A short burst of snow fills the lens of the handheld camera before clearing into the image of the inside of a small gym. About five people in total are inside, two are sparring in a small ring. One is holding the camera, and two more are standing at an old punching bag. One of the men is Johnny Muraco, and the other is the owner of the gym. An older man, he has graying hair that is unkempt from his workouts. He is wearing a pair of stained, old sweats. Muraco, on the other hand, is neatly kept, even though he is pounding away at the punching bag. Sweat covers his face and arms and damps his hair, causing it to gleam in the dim light. He is wearing a white tanktop that is semi-transparent due to the wetness. His pants consist of a pair of old black sweats. The camera pulls in, focusing on the bag before scanning back out. Muraco takes a few more punches at the bag, then stops. He lifts a bottled water, taking a drink and wiping the sweat from his brow before turning towards the camera.
Johnny Muraco: You see this right here? I do this every day, come in here and I train. I train as hard as I can, and when I cant keep training any more, this old guy right here puts the hurt on me and forces me to train some more.
[The camera switches to the older man, who is smoothing his hair out now that he is no longer holding the bag. He says something to Muraco and Muraco nods.]
Muraco: Gotta forgive him, he's a little shy. He's not the reason we're here anyway. We're hear about me, to get a look at Johnny Muraco. Frankly, there isnt much to see right now. Nothing much you can see, unless you want to watch me work out for a while, which isnt much fun to see. That's all gonna change though, when I sign myself a contract with a big time federation. I'm gonna prove to everyone I'm the best thing entering this business. I'm working my butt off every day, and I'm training different styles as soon as I know about them.
Not that I'm the greatest around, I dont claim to be. I do think I can give just about anyone a run for his money, because I'm just naturally competitive.
I dont give in too easily, and I like a challenge. I do my best at what I've decided to do, and I plan to be a success in this business. If I have to step on some toes on the way, then I will. If I have to kiss a little ass, so to speak, then I will. I'm not afraid to do what has to be done, and I'm not afraid to fight for it. I dont have that big of an attitude, but I'm sure of myself and I know I can get it done one way or another.
[Muraco motions for the camera to follow as him and the older man make their way to the ring. Muraco climbs into the ring and one of the two men inside climb out to take a break.]
Muraco: Frank, the old guy, he's taught me a few little tricks of the trade. You wont see his name on any wrestling trophies, but the guys on those trophies, you wont ever see them beat Frank. Anyway, we've been working together for a few years now, and he's shown me exactly what I need to know to get started. For instance, after going through all kinds of moves we've decided that I'm gonna use this little chokehold, its called a Guillotine Choke.
[Muraco gets the other man in position by hitting him with a boot to the stomach. The guy doubles over and Muraco grabs him and sets him up, holding him in a front face type position. He then hooks the mans free arm and drops down, sending his head crashing into the mat with a DDT. However, without releasing the hold Muraco wraps his legs around the other man's midsection, and applies pressure with the arm around the mans neck. The man holds out for a second or two before he taps the mat. Muraco releases and gets back to his feet as the man rolls out of the ring, getting his air back.]
Muraco: It's effective enough to finish a match. I hope to be using it on alot of guys real soon. You see, I'm not about talking as much as I'm about getting the job done. Sure, you can be as flashy as you want..that doesnt help you once you're inside that ring. What helps you in there is knowledge and ability. If you dont have it, you're outta luck. You wont walk away from the Guillotine,
I've found that out for myself. Now, if you'll excuse us I got a bit more training to do. [Frank, the old trainer, climbs into the ring and Muraco locks up with him. Muraco uses his strength to overpower the older man, however he is reversed and yelps in pain as his arm is pulled behind him and locked in position. Frank laughs, holding Muraco's arm with one hand, using his free hand to slap Johnny in the back of the head. Muraco yanks away, getting an upset look on his face as Frank continues to laugh.]
-------- ---------------------- Johnny Muraco 6'4" 240Lbs Master of the Guillotine Choke And the future of E-Wrestling. "I just put my own personal hit on you." Johnny Muraco
