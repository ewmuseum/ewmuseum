[Critique] Another flash for you people to devour...

18 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wait until you see the promo I'm writing for this last day before the 12
Day Tourney finals.
Oh man... talk about something original... heh In the meantime, take a look at this one. Is this what you mean, Scampini the Great? [The camera fades into a group of men and women protesting with picket signs outside of a brick building that almost looks like an office building.
It sits on a high perch overlooking a river, and it's cold enough on this early morning to see the protestors' breath as they shout angry mantras. The man holding the biggest sign, a tall, slender, bearded man, speaks first.]
Male protestor: ABORTION IS HOMICIDE! [The crowd follows suit.] All protestors: ABORTION IS HOMICIDE! ABORTION IS HOMICIDE! [Screeching tires halt the protest, with the protesters all looking to a red Corvette skidding into a parking spot nearby. The Corvette's driver's side door opens, and out pops a young man in a leather jacket with black jeans. He wears sunglasses, but tosses them in the car as he gets out and surveys the protestors. He smiles and chuckles a bit, pushing his shoulder-length black hair back as he approaches the group. He is, as you may have guessed, "Bad Seed"
Devin Demola.] DD: Well I'll be damned. [Devin steps two feet away from the group.] DD: At least, according to you fucks I will be. [The tall, bearded man steps forward to face Devin.] Male protestor: Listen, kid. Who the hell are y- [Devin takes the sign out of the man's hand and reads it.] DD: Abortion is homicide, eh? Not very creative. [He walks towards the entrance of the clinic, sign still in hand.] Male protestor: Hey! You can't do that! DD: Don't worry. I'm not a client. I only believe in murder after birth. [Devin walks through the doors, the protestor shouting after him. Devin, still holding the sign, turns to the camera as he nears the receptionist's desk.]
DD: Welcome, my friends in the 12 hour tournament, to... [Devin turns to the female receptionist.] DD: What the fuck is this place called? [The receptionist turns beet red and puts a hand over her face.] Receptionist: You can't have a camera in here! [The receptionist runs down the hall.] DD: Whatever. Welcome... to an abortion clinic. We're here in my adopted hometown of Portsmouth, New Hampshire to make a point. And to introduce you all to my upcoming first round opponents. [Devin begins walking down the hall, peeking into rooms with shrieks coming from behind the doors each time he does so.]
DD: Some ugly fucking women in here. Men in New Hampshire need better taste. [Devin opens the last door on the left, and chuckles.] DD: This is the place. [Devin walks into the door. He quickly shoves the cameraman into the room as shouting follows them.]
DD: Hey, cameraman. Stand in front of that door, m'kay? Just keep it closed. [Shouting is heard from behind the door, but Devin moves deeper into the room. In the room are a series of glass cabinets, with jars of some orangish liquid in the cabinets.]
DD: Meet... [Devin opens a cabinet and pulls out a jar.] DD: ... my first opponent. [Devin throws the contents of the jar onto a countertop. The orangish liquid drips off the side and onto the floor. On the counter is a tiny mass, barely recognizable as a mini-human figure.]
DD: Here is... Tony Cage. Xtreme Tony Cage, they called him. A former Vegas tag champion in the host federation of this very tournament. Former tag partner of Randall Crane. Don't recognize the name?
[Devin shrugs.] DD: Don't worry, I've never heard of the asshole either. But he doesn't matter. What does matter is Xtreme Tony Cage. A guy who meant little to the sport when he was wrestling, and even less now that he's an afterthought. He has been unceremoniously replaced by Justin Cage as a tag champion, and has been thrown into this tournament as my first victim.
First his ego... and now his body will be dismantled. Pity the kid. [Devin shakes his head in mock-pity. He pulls out another jar and studies the contents.] DD: My second opponent... [Devin rares back... and breaks the jar on the countertop. Glass and liquid fly everywhere, and a second mini-human figure lands next to the first.]
DD: ... Jaaku Onimusha. Described as mysterious. Ooooooh. I'm scared. I shit myself. [Devin laughs out loud.] DD: Well, he should be mysterious, considering nobody's ever seen or heard of the asshole before.
I take that back. Some guy named Phil Latio heard of him a few days ago. Made this guy submit to some move called the "Sexacutor". I can't even believe he has a job.
The fucking sexacutor. Jeeeeezus. [Demola grabs a third jar and looks in its contents. Then he looks at the countertop, orangish liquid dripping amongst pieces of glass and two mini-human figures.]
DD: I've made quite a mess, haven't I? I'll be a little neater here. [Devin takes the lid off the jar... then reaches his hand into the jar...] DD: Oh, this is fucking gross. [Devin pulls out a third mini-human figure and throws it on the countertop.] DD: This is my third opponent. Creep Two. [Demola nudges the mini-human with a finger, then cringes.] DD: He is creepy, that's for sure. The other thing that's sure is he's a loser. Lost to some group called The Rhombus for God's sakes on the last
RAW-Vegas. The Rhombus? If I lost to them, I'd wear a mask too. At least he got on TV last week. More than the other two can speak of.
[Devin turns from the three mini-human figures on the countertop and sets the final jar down. He picks up the protestor's sign he had dropped earlier and looks right into the camera.]
DD: There are two reasons we are here. First and foremost is to introduce my opponents to all of you, since you may not have heard of them before this. I sure hadn't. Had to dig through some RAW-Vegas tapes to find any hint of them. Good wrestling on those cards, by the way, but these guys weren't even on them for more than two minutes combined.
The thing about these guys is they probably won't do as good of a job summing up what they're all about as
I have done right here. You'll hear them talk, you'll see them on camera, and when they're done you'll just reach for the Excedrin. They'll have nothing to say, and no substance to their commentaries.
I, on the other hand, am here to educate. [Demola, raises the sign right next to his face.] DD: Which brings me to the second reason I'm here. I told that bearded asshole outside that I am against pre-birth abortion. And I stand by that. It's inhuman... barbaric.
But when it comes to certain babies... when it comes to horribly disfigured, extremely mentally handicapped babies... sometimes it's the humane thing to do.
[Devin looks to the three mini-humans on the counter.] DD: These three come into this tournament expecting it to give them fame and fortune. Indeed, to give them life. To take their embryonic and indistinguishable careers to this point and give them the dawning of a new day. Bring them into this world, as it were.
[Devin smirks at the camera.] DD: And it is my job, my duty as the new age superstar of this sport, to end their suffering. To put to an end the terrible, half-witted lives these poor saps would've faced had they survived. To spare the world from their grotesque, pitiful existances.
No matter how dirty this task is, my bedside manner is impeccable. I will do it with a smile.
[Devin smiles widely into the camera.] DD: I look up and down this bracket and I see thirty-one nobodies... and me. The man who puts the headlines in the papers and the asses in the seats.
And starting with these three, then moving on to three more saps in the elite eight round, it is my job to ensure that this tournament runs smoothly. That the best man truly does win.
That, beginning with these three, no mutants are allowed to advance. We want to keep this world as good and as functional as possible.
[Devin throws the sign to the side.] DD: So, to the parents of Tony Cage, Jaaku Onimusha, and Creep Two, you need not hold your heads down in shame any longer. Your lives will be made easier in just a few days.
When your children go down for the count at the hands of your own, private, post-birth abortion technician.
[Devin reaches for the latch of a nearby window.] DD: Doctor Devin Demola.
