[Critique] "Shotgun" Shane Valentine

6 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, this is a guy I've been running for a good number of months now and he is recently coming off an extended break. So, I joined [name withheld]'s Triumph (Good fed, great guy.) and sent this out as my first RP. So, gimme your worst, it can only help. :-)
On with the show.. ------------ VOICE: Triumph,.. hmf. [The scene comes in to a Shane Valentine trademark, the dingy bar setting. Shane sits, alone, towards the end of the bar. A cigarette is lit and hanging in an ashtray and a half finished beer in by his hand.]
SSV: I think we're gunna see who _really_ triumphs on Saturday. [Shane takes a swig of the beer, and looks at the camera ready to go on another tangent.] SSV: Looks like good ol' Mikey's got the Shotgun doin' double duty. Actually, he's got the whole gang doin' double duty. Nah, I ain't mad. Not one bit. I'm eager. [Takes his smoke out of the ashtray and takes a puff, then he puts it out.] SSV: Ya see, I like the fights. Some of the guys are cryin' and whinin' about this double duty stuff, but
I like it. I like it, alot. I won't have to stop the carnge at _Smoove._ I can go all out, and take every bastard in that rumble and whip their ass!
But I see what Mikey was doin'. Gettin' the main man all warmed up by giving me a throwaway match against some green wannabe. I can get in the ring, take him out without much trouble, and be on my way to the rumble.
[Takes another swig of his beer, you know, just to break up the monotomy.] SSV: It's a shame that everyone else doesn't have a chance on Saturday. Especially Jake Washington and
Kevin Candido. Two smart ass punks who think they got what it takes to make me hang my head in defeat.
I don't see that happenin'. Washington, I don't got an ego, so I'm not going to give you the token "Who the fuck are you?" line.
But what I am gunna say, is you got alotta balls to work your way down the Triumph roster saying no one has a chance. But balls don't win wrestling matchs, you "Six foot five rookie in a Civic." These do.
[Flashs some scarred knuckles to the camera.] SSV: And Candido. You relate me to Valentine's day. Well, I'll tell ya what. I'll give you a shot right in the "kisser" and we can call it even, alright?
You may be a former champ, but that don't mean _shit_ to me. [Takes one last swig of the beer, finishing it. He points the neck of the bottle towards the camera.]
SSV: I'll see alla you, on Saturday. [Black.] ------------ Well, tell me what you think. -- Josh - Am I actually e-wrestling again!?
