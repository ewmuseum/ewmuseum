15–18 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[15 December 1997 — Loose Cannon4Life] Fedheads, forward this to your leagues, and to other fedheads you know: My name is [name withheld]. I used to run the WIWO and the BWF, two good, solid leagues that could have been great had I had the intelligence to delegate some authority, which I didn't. That's alright, I never really liked being a fedhead all that much anyhow, but one thing I've always loved is statistics. What does that have to do with Ewrestling, you ask? Well, I'll tell you. Every so often, someone comes up with an RSPWF 200 or 500 or mid-year or year end awards, and there is much anticipation, and then everyone finally gets their copy of the awards, and lo and behold, we've never even heard of alot of the guys. That's cause there are SO many leagues out there, it's not possible to follow every one, and know about all the stars out there. So what to do? That's where I come in. What I've come up with are a weekly ratings system, similar to what PWI Weekly does. Basically, I'll give you all categories to vote on, and you send them into me. Deadline for entries will be midnight Wednesday CST. On Thursday evening, I'll post the RSPWF Weekly ratings and also mail them to any fedhead who wants me to. The categories are as follows and keep in mind they are for the current week, not career wise, and also you can vote for up to five in each category: Best wrestler overall: Best face: Best Heel: Best interview: Best worker: Best tag team: Best fedhead: Best match: Best fed: Best quote from a flash:(for this category, I'll pick my favorite from those submitted. Maximum 100 words.) Please submit completed ballots to [detail withheld]@icenter.net by Wednesday midnight CDT. Hope you all enjoy. ------------------------ [18 December 1997 — Loose Cannon4Life] As voted on by you, the readers of RSPWF: A qualifier: There were not many ballots cast, and for that I'm kind of dissapointed, but I hope in the future the response will pick up. I really intend to put alot of effort into this project, but I realize it will take time before people take it seriously. In any event, here are the ratings for the week ended Dec 17. Overall: 1.
