[Sample RP] Eugene "Sparks" Robinson

21 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Below is a sample roleplay of Eugene "Sparks" Robinson all of you <a href=" http://www.geocities.com/globalprowrestling/ ">Global Pro Wrestling</a> followers and Eugene "High Society" Robinson for all the loyal <a href=" http://nwc2k.hypermart.net/ ">National Wrestling Council</a> followers. This post is a direct statement to Tommy Midas of the <a href=" http://news.freehosting.net/ ">New England Wrestling Syndicate</a> for their upcoming match up on Eyewitness NEWS. A critique is more than welcomed and actually wanted if you have time.
Or, just drop me a line and tell me what you think. - - - - - - - - Entitled: To Tommy Midas, the rubber band man. - - - - - - - - [Fade up.] [In the last "High Society" segment we were graced with an interesting audio tape of Eugene Robinson taking it straight to the problem, Lee
Clark. After footage of Robinson successfully returning the NWC U.S.
Title to it's proper home we were taken to a segment of Eugene Robinson speaking of a new face in the crowd, one Tommy Midas.]
[We are immediately taken to a small office in the top floor of the soon to be famous Elite restaurant owned and managed by Eugene
Robinson. The door is slightly cracked open as we peer our camera into the wedge of the door. We walk into an extravagant office filled with the most exquisite materials. From beautiful French doors with a balcony overlooking the Cape Cod Bay too the illustrious furnishings that fill the room. What we then zoom in on is the back of a black leather chair that is faced towards the bay area view. But, much to our surprise a recognizable voice comes screeching into our ears.]
[Voice]: "How many times do I have to tell you. I don't care who he is or how much money they are willing to pay me. There product sucks and my restaurant WILL NOT sponsor it."
[Click.... The sound of someone pressing the "end" key on their cell phone is heard as the man spins around in his chair. The man, Eugene
Robinson shakes his head in disgust as he places the phone down on his desk top.]
[Eugene]: "Who do these people think they are dealing with! Elite
Enterprises will not deal with second rate production company's. We represent the very best and that is all we will tolerate."
[Eugene, yet to realize he is in the presence of a film crew knocks over a cup of pens that spill down to the floor and over his desk. He reaches over to another phone that is plastered onto the desk top and presses a button that brings up a voice.]
[Voice]: "Mister Robinson?" [Eugene]: "Yeah.... We've got a spill up here in my office Stewart. Send someone up here to fix it, NOW!" [Obviously it's been a long day for the NWC U.S. Champion who slaps the speaker phone button shutting it off. He leans back in his chair and folds his mittened covered hands together against yet another fine designer top. This time it's a white Dolce and Gabanna crew neck sweater.]
[Eugene]: "Finally!" [The camera spins around to see Eugene's most recognizable servant,
Stewart. Stewart immediately scrapes the pens off of the floor and places them on Eugene's desk. He grabs several rubber bands and straps them together before placing them back in a mug.]
[Stewart]: "Is that all Mister Robinson?" [Eugene]: "That will be all Stewart." [Stewart]: "Good day Mister Robinson...." [Stewart leaves as Eugene snatches an extra rubber band off of his desk top and begins foiling with it in his hands. Suddenly, something
*sparks* into his head as he peers up towards the camera.] [Eugene]: "Look at this rubber band. It just stretches, and stretches, and stretches. One could say that is stretches so far that one day, like a person, it might just....."
*SNAP* [The rubber band snaps in half as two separate pieces shoot across the room. Eugene's baby blue eyes glare towards the camera, the very camera that is currently zooming in on the black beret that is covering his sandy blond hair.]
[Eugene]: "This rubber band reminds me of a man I have come to know over these last few days. Yes, I'm talking about none other than the imitating young lad Tommy Midas who dreams of the day he walks threw the day in Eugene Robinson's shoes."
[A rubber band? Just how would this resemble the upcoming wrestling sensation Tommy Midas who has questionably earned a shot at Eugene's beloved title.]
[Eugene]: "Ya know why our dear pal Tommy boy reminds me of a rubber band? Well I'll tell you why.... It's because never in my life have I met a man or listened to a man for that matter that <b>stretches</b> the truth further than this pee brain. Like a rubber band Tommy boy, you continue to stretch, and stretch, and stretch. Yet, when the camera's stop rolling you just quite simply, snap.... Why? Because every time the second rate NEWS camera crew interview you, you realize something. Your sub par, second rate, a commoner amongst the small fish in the waiting pool to the big time."
[A smirk, a grin, call it what you want but the NWC U.S. Champ has one from ear to ear across his face. His ear splitting voice that just makes you want to jump into the TV screen in slap him continues to strike into our minds.]
[Eugene]: "Do you honestly think you can out wrestle the master of the counter moves. Have you ever stood in the ring with what many people claim is the greatest NWC Champion of all time, White Tiger, and reversed the Tiger's Fury? Have you ever reacted fast enough to not only dodge J.W. Oswald's rifle kick but to slap him down to the canvas like the bitch that he is all in one motion? I didn't think so Tommy boy, not by a long shot."
[Eugene's hands suddenly move out of our view as he slides open his top desk drawer. Out of it he grabs a stack of video tapes and places them firmly on his desk.]
[Eugene]: "Here you go Tommy boy. These tapes consist of epic battles with men such as Avalon Britton, White Tiger, and Samuel Ten Bears in my LSW days all the way up to the Shawn Arrows NWC World Title match and a memorable conquest at Illuminati. There yours Tommy boy, all of them... Watch them over and over again and study them till you can't stand to look at my face anymore. But ya know what Tommy boy, you'll notice a pattern, a sequence if you will. Here's a little secret that
Spencer Sloan would have loved to know. If you look closely Tommy boy you'll realize this..."
[A sudden and much needed pause by Mr. High Society. He intertwines his fingers together as he slowly nods his up and down.]
[Eugene]: "You'll realize that in all these tapes I never start a match the same way twice. I never go after an opponent the same way whether
I've wrestled him before or not. You'll come to realize that Eugene
Robinson is the most unpredictable man in wrestling. Then you'll realize the most important thing in your life Tommy boy.... You can't beat me...."
[The arrogant smile is glued onto his face and not even the Peerless one could slap it off. His confidence has driven itself so far up that not even a loss to El Matador could push it back down.]
[Eugene]: "I'm not saying you can't counter me Tommy boy but you said it yourself. The only way you can successfully avoid my attacks is if you can see it coming. Well I got some N.E.W.S. for ya Tommy boy, you'll never see it coming. But Tommy boy, if you think I talk a good show just wait to see what kinda award winning performance I am capable of in the ring with my fists, and trust me Tommy boy, there isn't any strings attached to these babies..."
[Eugene flaunts his fist in front of the camera egging Tommy Midas on who is probably sitting inches away from his big screen T.V. in daddy's mansion taking notes on his pad of paper.]
[Eugene]: "So your petty little mind honestly believes that Anton
MacTavish has control of me. One could be fooled by the sole fact that
I abided by Anton's regulations and agreed to wrestle you. But why wouldn't I? You pose no threat to my kingdom. Do you honestly believe you have a chance in beating me. You, Tommy boy, beat me, the
Grandeur_of_Greatness. I won't neglect that Anton has in the front office but he just as well as any other noble man knows that he builds his company around the men who brings the crowds in. The last thing
Anton wants to do is piss me off. But let's get something straight. I don't come to Anton like a dog comes to his master, nah Tommy, it's the other way around. What I want, I Get.... HELL, I TAKE IT!"
[Eugene shoots out of his chair showing off his black Versace V2 brushed cotton pants. His top his neatly tucked into his pants that are kept up by his reversible leather Gucci belt with a silver buckle and an "E" inscribed into it.]
[Eugene]: "But back to <b>stretching</b> the truth. False allegations of me actually reading This Week in Wrestling. I wouldn't pick up that piece of crap magazine if it were covering a diamond necklace. But your ignorance doesn't end there does it Tommy boy. You run your mouth with verbal assumptions that I, Eugene Robinson have no choices. Must
I run the simple logic by your feeble mind once again? I speak of so much because obviously it takes ten times as many words to get the point across into your mind. It's not that I'm not making myself clear. Hell, a ten year old could grasp onto the fact that you'll never amount to a man of my standards. But then, if that wasn't enough you seem to have a problem with the list of does_and_don'ts that the whole world seems to be talking about..."
[Not only is he up and out of his chair but he's made his way clear around to the front of his desk. He leans his two hundred and twenty five pound body against the edge of the desk as he folds his arms over one another.]
[Eugene]: "Your insufficient mind blabbering on about how the NWC wouldn't exist if it weren't for others that went against my theory and prevailed. Let me tell you something Tommy boy. I was in the NWC when you were still working behind a desk for your daddy in what ever seasonal home you reside in. I'm well aware that we are on the cusp_of_a_new_millennium. But didn't you yourself say that you came here to seek out the best competition. Yet at the same time you believe that the NWC isn't the very best. I'm sure there are about two hundred other wrestlers that wouldn't mind proving you wrong. But with all these wild allegations that seem to foil of your tongues as if stupidity were so natural to you, you still never seem to amaze me."
[Eugene shakes his head from side to side as if the "big one" is about to erupt out of his mouth. It's as if he has something so juicy that his mouth is watering from just thinking about it.]
[Eugene]: "You believe that I sneer at Lee Clark for being a former NWC
World Champion. Now correct me if I'm wrong Tommy boy, and I'll say it time and time again, I never am, But..... Lee Clark never held the NWC
World Title. So how can I be jealous of something that has never existed. So much for your number_two_theory and everything else you've ever said. In fact Lee Clark is at the butt end of every joke concerning the NWC World Title as he holds the record for losing the most shots at it. Jealous of mediocrity? I think not...."
[Juicy, indeed. With one simple comment Tommy Midas is left with a dumbfounded look across his face as he watches on at home. Eugene
Robinson, the cream of the crop, the grand daddy of the best, continues to run his mouth.]
[Eugene]: "Tommy boy, continue to truly believe that I am a newbie to the luxury life. Far be it from me to bust your bubble again but I may reside in Mbuji-Mayi, Zaire, Africa but I was born amongst a well funded family in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Since I first began my career in Lone Star Wrestling I've lived on a multi-million dollar contract but it was at Illuminati that I decide to showcase my riches. Daddy may have a big wallet for you at home but a single appearance by myself in the ring is more than your yearly salary in N.E.W.S. So while your getting what you want I'll be taking what I want and your ass is on that list. At first I saw this as just another appearance by myself to please to bring even more ratings to NEWS but now It's about shutting
you up and putting you in your place. But just where is this place Tommy boy, where do you stand on the totem poll of the New England
Wrestling Syndicate?" [Where indeed? Where does the newcomer Tommy Midas stand amongst men like Trey Slater, Tomas Bailey, Steve Sire, J.W. Oswald, Collin
Montgomery, and the Westsiders.] [Eugene]: "I can't quite put my finger on it Tommy boy, but I think it's somewhere near the gravel between the groves in my shoes. Tommy boy, live your life like the rubber band that you are stretching this moment as long as possible. But when it's all said and done and your lying down in the center of the ring tapping out to the most devastating move in sports entertainment your fifteen seconds of being in the spotlight will end. You want to climb the ladder to my life?
You've got a long way to go and getting your ass kicked in by me is one helluva beginning."
[Eugene rifles his fist into the camera's view exclamating his last point beyond belief. We are once left again standing in awe of one of the most amazing men to grace god's earth and the National Wrestling
Council.]
