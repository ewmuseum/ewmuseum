[Rookie] Lex Liberty. Looking for opinions and such.

29 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, people. I've been involved with ewrestling for a little over three months or so now. I have never seriously handled though, just for a couple of months in a really small fed that folded. I'm ready to handle a character seriously now though, and I think i've picked a gimmick and a character that i will be interested in. I'd appreciate any type of response, help, input, etc. Also, if any feds out there are interested, i'd appreciate it, since it is hard to look through all the feds out there for the right one.
Thanks, [name withheld] ------------------ NAME: Lex Liberty HEIGHT: 6’6” WEIGHT: 351lbs HOMETOWN: The US of A THEME MUSIC: “I am a Real American” – Rick Derringer ALIGNMENT: Face WRESTLING STYLE: Mat Technician APPEARANCE: Lex Liberty is a large, rotund man with some definition in his body in certain places like his chest, and arms. His facial features are always hidden underneath a royal blue mask with white stars scattered all about it, but through the mask, it’s evident he has round, baby a face visage. In the ring, Lex Liberty wears his mask of course, along with a pair of tights, red and white stripes running down the length of his legs, and a pair of royal blue speedos over it. Lex
Liberty also wears a cape which is just a replica of the American flag.
Outside the ring, Lex Liberty wears pretty much the same attire minus the cape. For the special occasion he wears actual clothes, he will wear a routine pair of blue jeans and a tee.
BACKGROUND: Lex Liberty is another notch in a long line of American
Patriots in his family and he’s no different. The Kid was brought up to a strict, conservative upbringing. He was the goodie two shoes in high school and college warning the others about the damaging affects of marijuana while everybody was getting high and drunk at five in the morning. He was the one who stayed home studying for the big history exam for Monday while it was Friday and everyone else was out partying.
He realized that wrestling might be his schtick one day when he caught a glimpse of a wrestling show many a year ago which donned former
American wrestling greats, sending that positive vibe out through the screen and into the homes of millions of on looking children in awe.
That’s when he decided it was his time to take up this sport for the sole purpose of giving the children of today a positive role model to look up.
GIMMICK: This man is a model American. He eat, sleeps, and craps the red, white, and blue. He will never utter a curse, never hit a woman, never smoke, drink, or do drugs, never hit anyone with a chair, and the man respects everyone. He’s a real American role model for the youth of
America. He’s here to bring values back to the sport of wrestling, and wrestling back to the sport of wrestling. HOWEVER… in the cynical world of today, unfortunately, he’s not accepted as much as he would have been in the “golden days” of wrestling. Unfortunately, he doesn’t curse every other word, and hang around well-endowed woman, which drives some of the fans away.
THREE STRENGTHS: 1. VALUES – He sticks by them through thick and thin, and will never abandon them. He’s firm in his beliefs, and will never let anyone down, or turn on them during their weak point. When you smack him, he’ll just turn the other cheek. You stab him in the back, he’ll turn and challenge you to stab him in the front. He’s that kind of guy, and his values can never be phased.
2. MOTIVATION – Lex Liberty is a very motivated man. The fans, the children specifically, is what drives him. This man will do anything so has not to let a frown tarnish the pure faces of the children.
3. TECHNICAL CUNNING: He can do just about anything on the mat, and he even has the ability to drop the jaws of the crowd with some spectacular daredevil maneuvers at times. He trains hard and long to be able to do what he can do in the ring at his age, and he tries to get that message of working hard, and practice makes perfect across to the fans.
THREE WEAKNESSES: 1. TRUST – Unfortunately, Lex Liberty rusts way too much in a business where trust isn’t such a good thing. He’s prone to back stabs and this might end his career before it even begins.
2. NICE GUYS FINISH LAST – Lex Liberty is one of a dying breed. In the business of wrestling and ratings today, his approach might not be well accepted among the T&A loving, hormone-drenched teenagers of today.
3. CHEATING – This is big part in winning today, and Lex Liberty just doesn’t have it in him to do so. FAVORITE MOVES: 1. Figure Four Leg Lock 2. Boston Crab 3. Sleeper Hold 4. Ankle Lock 5. Jumping Pile Driver 6. Cradle DDT 7. Inverted Suplex 8. German Suplex 9. Diving Head Butt 10. Gorilla Press Slam 11. Big Boot 12. Fisherman’s Suplex 13. Brain Buster 14. Flying Clothesline 15. Double Arm DDT FINISHER: 1. “Stars and Stripes Super Kick” - A hard standing sidekick to the jaw. 2. “Patriot Plunge” – Top Rope Moonsault SAMPLE ROLEPLAY: [Chirp.] [Chirp. Chip.] [Chirp.] [The scene fades into a effulgent image of green and blue intermingling to form the horizon. The grass is freshly sprouted and glows with a cheery radiance. Birds swoop and swivel in the afternoon sky while others warble songs from their comfortable little branches. The daystar illuminates the boundless heavens and the image on our screen in its entirety just dispels a warm feeling of classic, golden, Americana.]
[Suddenly, a faint whistle can be heard from off screen. Next thing we know, Lex Liberty, dressed in his typical royal blue mask speckled with white stars, and red and white striped tights, overlapped with a pair of royal speedos, saunters on screen chiming “This Land is Our Land”.
He stops and looks around the immense park we are in. A boy and girl running through the sedge carrying a long strand attached to a kite dragging behind abruptly. Lex Liberty cocks his head back and lets out a hearty laugh at the expense of the nature of childhood, and then turns to the camera, sighing with a broad grin apparent through his mask.]
“You know, there’s no better stage in your life than those few years spent as a naïve child. [Chuckles] Remember the days when you thought your mother was the most beautiful woman in the world? Remember when you knew Santa Claus was real because all that was left in the morning of the milk and Chips Ahoy you left was an empty glass and a plate dotted with cookie crumbs? Remember those days when you cheered for the good guy? The guy who always knew the right thing to do during a jam, slapped you five when you poked your tiny little arm over the barricade, and spent that extra twenty minutes after the show to autograph your little notepad even though you were all the way in back of the flood? [Sighs] I remember those days. And if I may abuse an old cliché… those were… the days.”
[As if staged, a group of teenagers smoking cigarettes and causing a ruckus among the pigeons feeding off of elderly-tossed breadcrumbs, walk by, looking at Lex Liberty and snickering at his costume. Lex
Liberty looks at them, shakes his head in disappointment, then turns to the camera and shrugs.]
“ What happened? When did we become a nation obsessed with T&A rather than good ol’ apple pie and baseball? Well, quite frankly, I’m sick of it. I don’t know how much one man can do with a dream, some old fashioned American values, and a wrestling contract, but I know I’m willing to try. You see, the children of today need a role model. And
I’m standing here before you, ready to take the reigns and guide the youth of America into the right direction.”
“When some hoodlum kid offers an admirable, young motivated child with a future, a [curls fingers into quotations] “hit” of “the pot” and that kid doesn’t know what to say, I hope he thinks to himself, “What would
Lex Liberty, defender of justice and all that is good in America, do?”.
And I’d want that kid to know that Lex Liberty says NO to drugs, and that just because you don’t do the… [fingers into quotations once again] “mari-ja-uana”, or drink yourself silly under the table, doesn’t make you a loser. After all, other than a casual sip of champagne every
New Year, not a drop of alcohol or drugs reaches these American lips, and look how I turned out.”
“The decadence of wrestling over the past decade or so has just repulsed me. I can’t turn on the television anymore and find a wrestling show with good wholesome family fun any more. Every show I watch is just one massive soft-porn music video. Nowadays the real stars of the show aren’t the good guys, the guys who overturn the odds in their favors. Heck… excuse my French… the stars aren’t even the bad guys, the guys that get your undies in a know every time they lay a hand on your hero or speak over the microphone with their shrill, presumptuous voices. The REAL stars now are the tight sweaters of the valets. And if you’re lucky, it’s cold in the arena that day and the ratings jump two points.”
“Beautiful women are reduced to clowned-up street walkers. Natural beauty isn’t an option anymore. There’s enough silicon in each federation to fill a ring in need be. Sure, in the old days, there were valets and yes… they were pretty. BUT, they were women. They merely applauded their man, and if a situation came up, handed him a chair.
I’m sure these female pioneers of old though, would be alarmed to see that a role in wrestling they helped make prestigious, is now only there so that acne-ridden teenage boys can download pictures off the internet to paste into their late night spank back. [Shakes his head in dismay] Shame… it really is a shame.”
“Now, I…” [Before Lex Liberty can continue on, a little lad eyeing a hotwheels car in his hands, begins to stroll on screen behind Lex. Lex Liberty leers and turns, place his hand on the youth’s shoulder, the boy’s neck whips up, and he looks into the face of Lex Liberty, mouth open in interest.]
LEX LIBERTY: Hello there little… [Lex Liberty waits for the little guy to fill in the blank, but gets no response. Just the same static, empty look on his face.]
LEX LIBERTY: [Chuckles] Okay, little guy, how about we just call you
Junior? YOUNG BOY: [Silent]… LEX LIBERTY: Okay then, Junior it is. So, Junior, what’s that you got in your hand? [Lex Liberty leans over and places both hands on his knees, pointing to the car by jutting his head in its direction. The kid still doesn’t respond, so Lex Liberty just smirks and playfully rubs his hand briskly through the youngster’s hair, messing it up a tad.]
LEX LIBERTY: [Sniggers] Okay, I see you’re a man of few words. So,
Junior, tell me… would you rather see me on your television set, telling the truth, doing good, and being an upright archetype for you?
Or would you rather see a tube flooded with sweater meat and big stinky old butts? [Lex Liberty can’t resist chortling at his own subtle attempt to sway the answer of the young boy, but his piercing laughter creeps away as he realizes the boy still will not reply, and remains with the same barren look across his rosy face. Lex Liberty pauses and thinks for a second on how he can reach the youngin, probably.]
[At once, his eyes widen through their little holes in the mask, and he snaps his finger. If this were a cartoon, a little bulb would have popped up above his head. Lex gyrates towards us and smirks. We can almost see his flawless ivories through the mask. He turns back to the boy and places his hand on his shoulder in a consoling manner.]
LEX LIBERTY: Little Boy, I figured you out. I know that you wa--- AAAAAHHHHHHHHHHH!! [With one speedy blow from his tiny leg, the little youth sends Lex
Liberty keeling over and tumbling onto the floor with one shot to the groin. While Lex Liberty rolls across the grass, clutching his manhood, and moaning in agony, the lad points to him and shouts somewhere off screen…]
LITTLE BOY: HELP! HELP! HE TOUCHED MY SPECIAL PLACE! HELP! HELP! [He runs off screen wildly dropping his car in the chaos. All that his left on our screen in an image of Lex Liberty rolled up into the fetal position on our screen, all that can be heard over the eerie calm are a few fuzzy wails of pain. In a low, high-pitched, crackling voice, Lex
Liberty asks…]
LEX LIBERTY: Please… can we… [moan] … cut while I have some shreds of
[groan] dignity left?
[Just as the camera is about to cut it seems, the crying of the youth can be heard approaching rapidly. He comes on our screen, pointing and yelling at Lex Liberty.]
LITTLE BOY: HIM! HIM! STRANGER! STRANGER! HE TOUCHED MY SPECIAL AREA! [Next thing we know, an infuriated mother with a colossal purse comes into the picture, reaching back with her pocketbook and whacking Lex
Liberty over the shoulder, back, and head a few times with hit. She reaches down to grab the fallen metal car, gives one last fleet punt to
Lex’s spine, and walks away rapidly with her son’s hand in her’s, only looking back to say…]
MOTHER: Pervert! Rot in hell you pedophile! [A bruised and battered man is all that remains on our screen now. After a few uncomfortable seconds, we hear the wearied voice of Lex
Liberty prevail through our screens, no matter how weak it is.]
“We can all learn a good lesson from today kids. Junior was right. [Moan] Ugh. Never… ever… talk to strangers, and if they touch your special area, do exactly what little Junior did. That is exactly why we… uh… planned this little episode today, boys and girls. [Groan] So remember, scream, kick, and run… [grunt] scream, kick, and run. That’s all from me today gents and gentlewomen, and don’t forget, respect your parents, and stay true to the red, white, and blue. G’night, folks.]
[Throughout that whole little speech, the discomfort can be heard through Lex Liberty ’s fragile words. He lingers unmoved on the grass patch.]
