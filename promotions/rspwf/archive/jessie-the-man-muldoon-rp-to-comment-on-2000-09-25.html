Engraved on the plaque are the words "Dr." and "Mercury". Voices are then heard coming from behind the door, voices which seem to be denoting a hint of anger. The door then opens up and as it does so a man maybe in his late 40's, early 50's is seen standing. The man has a white coat on and a stethoscope is around his neck. The man then just takes a sigh. He then begins to walk off, yet the cameras catch up with him. Fade in.]
"Some people never listen. No matter how much advice I give them, the answer is always the same. They all want to do what they want to do, forget about how ill they might be. Forget about the risk that are putting upon their own life. Well I'm not having it, he is not fit and that's that."
[Seen coming out of the office where Doc. Mercury just came from is the battered and bruised, at deaths door, Jessie "The Man" Muldoon. Jessie then tries to speak, and manages to do so.]
"Doc, I hear what ya are saying, yet I have ta. No matter how bad it might look, if I don' do it, then the faries will think I'm some kind of wimp an' I canna have that feelin'. I canna' let the faries down, in fact, I won't."
[A frown forms on the Doctors face.] "Mister Muldoon, the attack which you suffered caused you to have internal bleeding to the cranial wall of the left side of the brain. You also have internal bruising which could at any time bring about a heamotoma and it's a risk as your medical officer that I am not willing to allow you to take."
[Jessie with an indignent look.] "Ya don' understand, I have ta do this." [The Doctor begins to shake his head.] "No. It's you who doesn't understand. Jessie Muldoon, if I allow you to go out and wrestle against two grown men, who are both as strong as oxes then I would be sending you to your own death. And I don't want the responsibility of knowing that it was me that caused the first ever wrestling death in the history of the professional wrestling industry.
Therefore I state you can't wrestle at Rumble In The Bronx and it's goin' ta be a VERY long time before ya ever step foot in the ring again."
[Anger forms in the voice of Jessie Muldoon.] "WHAT!!!" [The Doctor seems frightened.] "Calm down. I know it looks bad, but what is better, you getting a massive blow to the head and killing yourself or you dropping the UEW
American Championship, having a rest and making a full recovery?" [Jessie again seems indignent.] "No matter what ya say, I'm wrestlin'." [The Doctor then just sighs and begins to mutter.] "Jessie, I'm goin' ta have ta file this against the UEW committee, they must know the extent of your injuries, then if they allow you to wrestle then be it upon their heads. But I shall be reccommending to them that you take a period away from the ring and that you DO NOT wrestle at
Rumble In The Bronx." [Jessie with a questionable tone.] "Oh you will know will ya?" [Doctor Mercury just answers the question.] "I have no choice." [Jessie pleading.] "For the love of all the faries out there across America, I shall at Rumble In The Bronx go to ma people and I shall go up against the danger which is put before me. Mercenary, ya might once have been Ultimate, yet know y're like a washed up sea urchin and Rumble In The Bronx, I may be on deaths door, I may breath ma last breath, but rest assured I shall do what I always do.. I shall turn up, I shall wrestle and above all that for the last time maybe, I shall be known as the Only male that counts.."
[The Doctor then with a stern approach.] "I will not change my mind. My decission is final and that's all I have to say on the matter."
[Jessie now begins to point a finger at the camera.] "Doc, Rumble In The Bronx, I shall find who did this ta me and when I do may that person wish they had never been born. For like it or not, nothin' is gonna' stop me from defendin' this. [Shows the UEW American
Title.] Nothing. Not some old hack and most certainly not some kid who thinks it's Showtime. For like always, once and for all the curtain shall fall and the SHOW SHALL BE OVER!!"
[In a very odd approach, Doctor. Mercury says a last throught prevoking comment, one which could mean anything, Yet what does it mean?
"That's what he thinks." [Jessie is then seen slowly walking up the long corridoor leading out of the doctors surgery and into the hall. Fades as we fade out. The Doc goes back into his room and the door slams shut.]
