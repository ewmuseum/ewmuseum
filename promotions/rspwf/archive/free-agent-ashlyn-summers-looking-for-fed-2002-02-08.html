Free Agent (Ashlyn Summers) Looking for Fed

8 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Howdy, Whenever I do one of these, I get a pretty good response from feds. So, I thought that I would try again. With the closing of AWP, I was looking for a new home for my wrestler, Ashlyn Summers. It doesn't matter what type of fed.
It could be a women's fed, a fed with a women's division, or a fed that allows women to compete equally with men (which is pretty much the role she played in
AWP). Also, she can be either heel or face. Below is a sample of work. Thanks in advance.
[It's morning at the home of "American Beauty" Ashlyn Summers. We find the young woman in her living room, seated Indian-style on a plush, blue couch.
She wears a black, tank top and blue, denim jeans, her feet bare. Ashlyn's long, black hair is tied back in a ponytail and she sips from a mug of steaming coffee, the day's newspaper in her lap. She finally looks up from her reading, placing the mug and newspaper aside, and fixes the camera with a steely gaze.]
Ashlyn: As you can imagine, I'm not in the best of moods, right now. I just saw my tag partner and good friend, Jonathan Conspiracy, beaten mercilessly, his AWP career cut short. (she sighs and shakes her head) Deathrow, I have no idea what you were doing, coming out there and attacking Jonathan, but you're going to pay for your actions. There was no excuse for you to attack him like that. Jon never did anything to you. Yet, you felt the need to lay your filthy hands on him, sending him out of the AWP, maybe for good. That's just something that I can't allow to go unanswered. I know that you're probably not going to take my threats seriously but that would be your mistake. Just ask Tiff Chandler. She can tell you a lot about what I can do, in the ring.
Speaking of Chandler, she's not off the hook either for the part she played in Jonathan's attack. True, she struck after Deathrow had already done most of the damage but she still needs to be put in her place for kicking a man while he's down, especially my man! Tiff, after our match, I had gained a bit of respect for you. You fought well and showed yourself to be both resourceful and tough, a deadly combination. Well, all of that respect went out of the window after the last show! Watching you attack Jon, after he had already been laid out, proved that you are nothing more than an opportunistic coward. You claimed to be hardcore but there's nothing hardcore about beating on an already defeated opponent. I just hope you know that you and I are going to dance again, Tiff. I beat your ass before and it will be more than a pleasure to do so again.
[She suddenly leans closer, crossing her shapely legs.] Ashlyn: But, before I can even focus on Tiff or Deathrow, I've got one of the biggest matches of my career coming up, a shot at Ben Tuckwell and his cruiserweight title. I would be lying if I said that I wasn't looking forward to this opportunity. Since Tuckwell helped cheat me out of my shot at the title, in the tourney, I've been waiting for a chance to get my hands on both him and the title. Now that the opportunity has come, there's no way that I'm going to waste it, especially knowing that Jon will probably be watching this match closely. He's never liked Tuckwell and winning the AWP cruiserweight title was always a dream of his.
Ben, there's no love lost between us. You know it, I know it, and the fans know it. Since I helped Jon evict you from this league, the first time, you and I have been on a collision course. We wrestled before and you earned a tainted victory, using a mask and distractions to eliminate me from the cruiserweight tourney. Now, I have the chance to finally even the score and win the title that should have been mine in the first place. I just hope that you're ready for all that I'm going to be bringing to this match. Since the
AWP created the cruiserweight title, it's been my goal to obtain it. I've worked my butt off against all that the AWP had to offer just for another shot at the gold. Knowing that, you can expect me to come into this match giving 110%. You had better be ready and up to the test because, if you're not, you just may end up getting your feelings hurt.
I know that Brian Taylor is going to be the special referee and I hope that he does his job and calls things down the middle. Brian, I know that you don't like Ben but I hope that you don't do anything stupid to jeopardize my chances because I don't need or want your help. All I need is for you to sit back, make the three count, and watch how a true champion conducts herself.
In fact, if you're lucky, you might just be my first title defense. I would like nothing better than to show you, up close and personal, just what this
"girl" can do.
[She smirks.] Ashlyn: You know, since the AWP did away with the Can Am Tag Titles, I've been a little lonely without some gold around my waist. (grins) Looks like I finally get to remedy that and kick Tuckwell's ass in the process. (she rubs her hands together) And I just can't wait. After all, this one isn't just for me. It's for Jon, as well. So you know there's no way that I'm going to lose.
Tuckwell, prepare to join the ranks of the titleless. [She flashes the camera a devilish grin as the scene fades to black.]
