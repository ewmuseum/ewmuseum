You asked for my roleplay...

1 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a sample roleplay of mine for New Extreme Wrestling. (N.E.W.) «—---——----—-•¤[ Begin Transmission ]¤•—---——----—-» <The camera fades in.> <The camera shows "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh standing on the top of a ten story building in New York City. He is wearing blue-jeans, a black "Ian 1-8-7"
t-shirt, and black and white, Reebok sneakers. The camera is showing Ian Walsh from an angle at the bottom of the building. The camera slowly zooms in and shows Ian Walsh standing on the edge in a crucifix position. His head is pointed into the air and he is frothing. His eyes are closed. The camera gets close enough to show his clean shaven face, and matted down, dirty blonde hair.
An evil grin appears upon his face as he looks down at the camera. His eyes open and blood can be seen gushing out of his forehead.>
<He evily laughs.> <After a minute, the laughing stops and silence prevails over the top of the building. His devilish grin stays upon his face as he spits down at the camera. Ian Walsh begins to speak.>
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh: Jim Whitten... You craven, you make me laugh. How pathetic you must be to take me so lightly. I see that you do have fear. Do you think that I don't have what it takes to defeat you? Do you think that
I will just stand there as a punching bag or a rag doll for your pleasure? Ha!
How wrong you are... Whitten, you talk about me not having what it takes. We will just have to see about that when Friday rolls around. Hell, it's not that far away.
<He stops to think and then starts to talk again...> I will crimp and mangle your pathetic body, Whitten. If you think that I don't have the tools or credential to defeat you, you are sadly mistaken. How could you actually believe than you could defeat a man such as myself? It's easy, that pot smoking that you have been doing has been getting to your brain.
Yeah, you may have the tools to make somebody quit, but you don't have what it takes to make me quit.
<He folds his arms.> I will take Bishop's crosier and beat you to a pulp with it. Oh, hellish leader of the Cro-Magoons, I will beat you and humiliate you. I will show you what torture is really like. I will make an example of you, Jim Whitten. No matter how "great" and how "bad" a man is, I can still take that man out.
<He croons a hymn on top of the building as he looks back into the sky. He froths from the mouth again. Silence overwhelms the area again and Ian Walsh begins to speak once more.>
The criterions have been set. It has all been written and etched in stone. I will beat you, Whitten. It is only a short time away. You will see. I can smell the fear that you have for me. I know that you are scared. Oh yes,
Whitten, you are scared.
<He starts to croon another hymn. Suddenly, a crescendo is heard in the hymn and it gets louder. He suddenly stops and starts to speak again.>
You will be crestfallen when I am finished with you. You have this crothet that you are the absolute best. I have news for you, Whitten... You aren't the best. I will show the world ythat you aren't the best. Since I am reletively new to the N.E.W., I have a crux to settle. I will force you to hide in your cubbyhole or your cubicle. You see, I am on a crusade of my own.
I need to fight my way to the top. What better way is there than to beat Jim
Whitten on Friday? That's how you go to the top. You face top competitors.
<He reaches down onto the roof and grabs a green cruet. He chucks it to the ground and it hits a pedestrian who was walking on the road.>
<Laughing> Whitten, I am truely great. You will find out what I am about first-hand. I don't care who you have with you. You can have Eddie "The Curate" Jacobs there for your moral support. You are the reason why Too Extreme is falling apart.
Ha! I hear that it might be coming back. When hell freezes over, Too Extreme will be back together. You are the curd of Too Extreme. Hell, you are the cyst of Too Extreme. I may be daft and Insane, but I have the skills and talent to get me to the top.
<He notices that the sky turned cloudy and the air turned dank...> It will rain any minute. This rain, however, will be nothing like the rain that I make in you parade, Jim Whitten. Ha! Ha! I will dapple you with bruises and maake you feel the pain. I am the czar of torture. I am the master of pain. I am the darnel of the N.E.W.. I will prove this as true when
I beat you on Friday.
<Ian Walsh's face turns into a dead pan and remains expressionless. The rain starts to fall.>
Here comes the deluge of rain. I best desert this damn building and get back to my training. Jim Whitten, I will do something that you said to me. I won't use words similar to Taz's. Prepare to become desolated. Prepare to be unseemed. Prepare to meet your demise.
<Ian Walsh contemplates on whether or not he should jump to the next building. Alas,... He sees a pole along the side of the building. He grabs a hold of it and quickly slide down it. He gets to the road and disappears into the rain.>
<Camera fades out.>
