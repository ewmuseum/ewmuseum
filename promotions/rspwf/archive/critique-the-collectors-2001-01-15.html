[Critique]The Collectors

15 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All right, sitting on my ass board I figure I would create a new tag team. I also thought that I would like some feedback on the flash I just wrote.
~NOTE: If you could send all critiques back to me through e-mail, that would be greatly appreciated.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- [SCENE: Fade into what appears to be a private office. Two desk facing each other consume most of the space in the room. Pilled atop, both, desks are stacks of folders, with papers shoved inside. Two men currently seated at the desk, run vigoroursly through papers. Going through folder after folder. Both men appear to be well beat, although that cannot be neither confirmed nor denied as their current attire covers their bodys well. Both men dressed in dark blue suits, both with black ties. One of the men shovels a folder accross his desk, and onto the other man's.]
MAN #1: Take a look at those clowns... [The second man runs through the file, looking over each individual paper that lies in the folder. The second individual grins a little...]
MAN #2: Ahhaha, a complete joke! [The second man, lightly, tosses the folder to the corner of his desk. The camera zooms in on the top tab of the folder reading, "Le Resistance." The first man gets up from his chair. Standing an astonishing 6'8," and weighing in somewhere in the vacinity of 270-300 pounds. Quite slim. The first man walks over to a water cooler located in the opposite corner of the room. Lifting a small, paper, cup from the side, he yells back at the second individual.]
MAN #1: A complete joke, is putting it midly. [The first man presses down on the cold button, and proceeds to fill the paper cup to the brim. Spilling the water a little, the man walks back over to the desk, and takes a seat once again.]
MAN #2: You couldn't ask me if I wanted a drink? MAN #1: Well, the water cooler is just right over there, get up and get it yourself. I ain't no damn maid.
MAN #2: I know ya ain't a maid, a selfish prick is what ya are. [The first man peers at the second, as the second man begins to laugh.] MAN #2: I'm just joking with you, you touchy bastard. Can't take a joke? [The first man doesn't respond, and the second just shrugs his shoulders. The second man now stands up. Also standing an astonishing 6'8," and weighing in at about the same weight as his companion. The second man proceeds to walk over, and do the same as the first. Get him a drink of water from the cooler.
Returning to his seat, the second man breaks the silence.] MAN #2: Yo, Bone, whats wrong with you? Your not worried about Sunday night are ya? BONE: Worried? Nah, I'm just a little curious. Who the hell does Victor Caine got to fill that third spot? Thats what I'm wondering, Tax.
TAX: Well, who ever it is, I wouldn't worry to much about it. Caine, can throw whomever the hell he wants in that ring with us for all I care. Doesn't matter, the story is still going to be written the same way. The Collectors, are the new TNL World Tag Team Champions!
B: I feel the exact same way. Like I said, not worried, just a little curious. T: Well, we do know who the second tag team is, infact, we were just looking through their file. You worried about Le Resistance, too?
B[Slamming his hands down in a thunderous rage]: HEY! I said I'm not worried! Were going to go in the ring Sunday Night, and dominate the god damn
Le Resistance, and whomever Caine decides to pull out of his Ass, we'll dominate them too!
T[Kinda grinning]: Touchy~ B: Well, you already knew that. T: Indeed. Right now, don't even think about Caine's team. Most likely its two wrestlers in the back already, that Caine just through together. Caine's team is, most likely, going to be a pair with little to no expierence together in the ring. While you, and me on the other hand have got 3 years of solid ring work together.
B: That is very true. Caine's team will not even be a factor, now that I think about it. T: Le Resistance won't be a factor, either. I'm mean two french rejects, who the hell do they think they are? Having the brass to oppose us. Its just down right silly, and they will pay with their ass'.
B[Slamming a closed fist down onto the desk]: Le Resistance, Team Caine, The
Collectors are here, and both of you teams are deep in debt, so prepare for us to come and COLLECT on your MOTHER[BLEEP]ING ASS'!
[Both men get up from their respective desks, and walk off camera...] V/O: Ladies, and Gentlemen you've just been introduced to The Collectors, BONE and TAX! [Scene fades to black.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- BONE & TAX ... Collecting on that ass for the '01
