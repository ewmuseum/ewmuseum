TooN productions presents.... *FLASH*

4 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey RSPWF junkies, and TooNites, Here is another phenomenal TooN roleplay.. Read in envy!! Go ahead with your lame and false criticism. Cause regardless what you say, you know this is the best flash you'll ever read. And that you can never have quality like this of
pure content. It's creativity at it's finest hour. Not repetitive, or any professional wrestling rip-off content. ************************************************************************** ************* -Set scene 1- [Scene- Every home in America. A rather enormous estate is the scene, the time is 7:54PM pacific/10:54 eastern standard. The picture pans around the enormous estate and a figure is seen seated on a leather couch.]
.......*Focus In* -Open scene 1- [ As you find yourself comfortably seated on your black leather couch. With a sudden movement you reach for the remote control and turn on the T.V. and right there on your T.V screen is the scene for a MVW paid for annoucement, and words from a MVW super-star]
-Set scene 2- [Scene- Upstate, NY. Time is 7:55 pacific/10:55 eastern standard. The picture is focused in on a unoccupied and incapacitated room.]
........*Focus in* -Opening scene 2- [Suddenly with a thud being heard, someone appears into the picture. The man is well built. He is attired in black sweat pants, and no shirt, with a grey mask. The man looks around the room, then takes a seat on a bar stool, and begins to parabolically speak]
-Content- Mario H. Fassely- [joyful] Dragon, it was yet exactly everything I anticipated and so greatly expected. A show of great athleticism, tenacity and abudant resilience. If anything Dragon --- I think you went home the true winner. You took with you the knowledge and the learning experience. And from this, you will only become better. I wish you the best of luck with your MVW career.
And _hopefully_ if I win my upcoming MVW T.V title match, we can meet for a epic third time, with a prestigious title on the line.
[Mario then slants his head in a upward position, and begins to stare at the ceiling in a daze. He then straightens his head towards the camera and begins to speak]
. . ._Second chances_ A second chance is what I was blessed with. A chance at redemption, and inner comfort. Now just when you thought things in
MVW were toning down and fading to nonexistence, it strikes like a fallen comet. It brings huge impact, and unforgetable effects. [pause] _Thats exactly what MVW has done._ MVW is currently putting on wrestling shows like no other respective promotion can near by imagine of putting on. And when me and a very well established MVW super-star by the name of Steve Kline endure in what should be a historic and epic battle; I will then become a representitive for this grand promotion. No longer will the champions of MVW use obscenities and degrade this sport. No longer will the champions lack respect for their peers and even their adversaries.
[Mario then pause for a few short seconds. Seemingly deeply thinking to himself. With a sudden outburst of a deep breath, he begins to speak again]
You see, after I finished my laborious match with Dragon, I decided to stick around and watch the show in my secluded from the public locker-room. And the things I saw had me in disbelief. Understand this, here in MVW I'm alone. I don't know many people in MVW or even communicate with that many people around here. Actually to be quite honest, I don't talk to anyone in MVW!! So everything is new to Mario, and when it happens its absolutely shocking.
[sniff] The negative occurences at the PPV involved Kiljoy. The man went on to win his first bout of the evening against Tommy Rifles. But did his progressing success stop there?? Not a chance!! This undoubtly phenomenal athlete then went on to win the _MVW WORLD TITLE_ in the big battle royal.
. . .You see, at that time right there I knew something had to be done to put a stop to Kiljoy!! And as I was thinking, I started coming down on myself. See, I had that chance, but due to the circumstances of me being
[inhale] _deported_ [exhale] those chances became slim to none. But I'll get my chance to face off with the Legacy again. But this time, you won't be handed such a tainted victory Kiljoy --- you'll have to earn it. [pause]
_But_, I didn't let that occurence damper the fact that I won a major match that night. Because also that night, something far greater then anything else happened. And that was the return of a MVW legend, and my idol, my picture perfect role-model. See, when Shane Slaughter first walked out and down the aisle, and once again graced the squared circle --- his _home_ I was skeptical of being excited.
Lets look at the facts. Shane Slaughter, dominated MVW with ease. Opponent after opponent, match after match, victory after victory. Then the most treacherous, and hideous thing that could ever happen to any athlete, happened to Shane Slaughter. Thats right, he suffered near career ending injuries. In many people's mind, his illustrious career was over. But obviously, he proved them wrong. Now when I saw Shane Slaughter at Shattered Dreams I was discouraged of the anger, and rage that must be boiling up inside of this already devious man. But then, the words were uttered. And Slaughter was like a new man, he was reborn! At that moment and time, a eerie and mystic feeling came over my body.
. . .You see, before this, Slaughter was methodical, manipulative. He personifed the perfect villan. His every move was planned, every step was precisely plotted, everything he did was on cue. But when he was in that ring,
I saw his eyes. Slaughter no longer had those elements of hate inside of him.
His eyes expressed something greater --- they expressed honour, courage, self dignity, and pride. It was the rebirth of a man once filled with hate, now filled with sarrowness. I'd like to be the first to say this. Shane Slaughter
[inhale] _Welcome Home_ [exhale]
[Mario gets up off the bar-stool, and walks to the main entrance door. He then opens the door, and steps outside to a well lit front porch. Marrio then leaps up against a gate, and begins to speak again]
Now with all that aside, I'd like to talk about my upcoming match. It's not just another match. But it's another chance at the MVW television title!!
it's not just another opponent --- it's one by the name of Steve Kline. _Steve_ granted you might be more experienced then me, and have a greater in-ring prowess then me. Those things won't matter when our time comes. It's not whos done that, or what. It's who does _this_ --- this is the present time, and the future which lays before us. The past is gone and no longer to be changed. What lays in front of you Steve Kline is a challenge probably too far to overcome. Kline I've been a great fan of MVW before I became a wrestler two months ago. I've seen you in the ring, and know your credentials. _But_, what you need to know and understand is the burning desire I have. [pause]
I've been elaborating my every technique for this second chance. . . .Kline, before us stands our goal, behind us our past. Which one will you reach for, for guidence?? I don't have much of a wrestling past, so ultimately I reach for the goal which lays in my future. I will be efficacious and very omniscient of all the multifarious aspects which are at hand in this upcoming _MVW --- T.V_ title match. Steve Kline, I'm ready for our match, phyisically, mentally, and emotionally. I am ready for any obstacle which stands in my way to reaching the top of the MVW mountain of success. . . .
Before I go any futher, I'd like to talk about a curtain individual. This individual goes by the name of Chris Magnum, the Silencer. [pause] [rhetoric]
Chris you have objected to this match, and stated that you will be the empiricist; observing this match from ring-side. I hope for your sake, and our sake, you don't become too emulous and cause some dramtic outcome in this match of dreams. Silencer, I'm sure in due time you'll get your respected and earned title shot. But I warn you with vocal cords of calmness, and not hostility, do
_not_ intervene in this match. Take notes, study, and learn, for soon you could be in the ring with either myself or Kline wrestling for your dream --- the MVW television title.
[Mario begins to walk down a fleight of steps. As he gets to the bottom of the steps, he walks out into the grass. Pedestrians are seen walking along the side-walk. And trafficers are seen driving along the streets, honking their horns. Mario continuously walking, looks at the camera and begins to speak]
You know something. . . Wrestling is much like traffic. The cars symbolize the athletes. Some newer then others, some with better preformance. Gas in the car keeps it running. Energy in the human anatomy keeps it running. Cars come and go, as do wrestlers. Cars cause some noise as do wrestlers. Some cars get more attention then others, because they are more flashy. Same thing goes for wrestlers. And then some cars break down, or no longer are able to continue in their life long destination. Same thing happens with wrestlers within this great sport. You see, I'm a car of which nothing is really known about. A car which is like no other car. A car hidden from it's true
identity. One day you'll all understand what I mean. The day this [pointing to his mask] mask comes off, is the day mysteries will be solved. . . .But till that day, this is what stands before you. A new, young, and hungry car, read to travel to new heights, and enbark in endless journies.
And Kline, you are another road of which I haven't travled upon yet --- _but_,
I am getting ready to travel down that unknown road. And if things go my way, then I'll leave this destination, this journy, with the trophy. [sniff] And when I finally have the television title in my grasp, nothing will take it away from me. [sniff] The only thing that could move me from my T.V title, would be a shot at the [inhale] _MVW world title._ [exhale] Kline, I hope you are prepared, and well rested. For which lays in front of you will be a mountian of needs to be carefully climbed. Climbing which need thorough planning and preparations. Being the sport I am, I wish you good luck, and may the best man win. [sniff]
_M_ --- _V_ --- _W_ prepare for a new era in wrestling. Prepare form something you didn't think of. Prepare to be led by a new T.V champion. A champion of honour, courage, honesty, self dignity, and promotional pride.
Prepare for the era of the _Brazilian Sensation_. [Mario sees a soccer-ball laying on the grass. And about a hundred and fifty feet away is a small soccer goal post. Mario picks up the soccer-ball and stares at it for awhile. He then puts it down in front of him, stares at the goal, then at the camera and begins to speak]
Next Monday the ultimate game will begin. And it is my destiny to score the goal, and win. . . ["Smack"]
[Mario kicks the soccer-ball with force, causing the soccer-ball to glide on the surface of the grass. The ball then slowly rolls into the goal post.]
-Picture fade out- [The picture then fades to black, with the last visable image being the soccer-ball resting in the confinements of the soccer goal post.]
