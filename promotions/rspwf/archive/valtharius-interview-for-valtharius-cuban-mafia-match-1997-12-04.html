Valtharius Interview for Valtharius-Cuban Mafia Match

4 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is the interview, written as per the stipulations set. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [The scene slowly opens to a murky blue mist... the hooting of an owl is heard as the full moon can be seen dimly lighting in the cold night sky though the branches of the dead trees. The camera slowly pans over the unforgiving landscape as the sound of a stone door being opened is heard... the owl flies from its perch and off into the distance. The camera pans towards the sound and suddenly a cloaked figure can be seen emerging from a crypt. The figure walks slowly through the mist and approaches the camera. A lumbering giant of a beast can be seen coming from the crypt behind the cloaked figure. He grunts as hot breath escapes his flaring nostrils in the cold night air. The cloaked figure beings to speak......] Karachel: Welcome to my Realm... I am Karachel... known to many as the Lord of the Pits and to others as the Voice of Reason. Behind me is my protege Valtharius.... Valtharius is not happy and wishes to play.... Valtharius: ME VALTHARIUS!!! ME NOT LIKE CIGAR PEE-PUHL SAY ABOUT VALTHARIUS!!! ME MAD AT PEE-PUHL SAY BAD THINGY OF KARROTSMELL!!! K: Cuban Mafia... Valtharius is speaking in context to what you have said about he and I. I am unhappy as well. [Karachel spreads wide his arms and speaks a bit louder] K: Cuban Mafia.... today the sun refused to shine over this war infested land. Beyond the sky, the Lord of Life is humbling himself before our Dark Master. Our Dark Master shall send crossbones tumbling down from the Dark Heavens of Night as you, the flesh imposters, attempt to fight the Master! VtM: NO FIGHT MASTER!!! FIGHT VALTHARIUS!!! ME CHEW CIGAR PEE-PHUL!!! [Karachel places his hand on Valtharius large shoulder] K: Blazing darkness from the fists of Valtharius shall destroy this utopian farce! Your dreams shall be crushed! Join us in your nightmare Cuban Mafia.... Come and join us.... we'll take you to nightmares never dreamt before by mortal minds..... VtM: ME VALTHARIUS NIGHTMARE!!! CIGAR PEE-PUHL NOT NICE TO KARROTSMELL!!! MAKE VALTHARIUS MAD!!! [The camera pulls closer to the darkness of Karachel's cowled cloak... two steel-grey pin points of light can be seen within... occasionally the glint from filed needle-like teeth can be seen as Karachel speaks] K: We are the blind dead Cuban Mafia... We have no eyes inside our skull. Only our senses lead us to your nightmare. By challenging Valtharius you have unleashed the centuries of death we wear on our souls. The Pale Horse we ride trots ever forward to our destiny with you. It is unfortunate that you have crossed our path. [The camera pulls back from the darkness of his cowl and Karachel turns to his side and extends an arm to the darkened seashore he and Valtharius are suddenly standing on] K: The shores of unearthly dead. Your mournful mothers chilled with the thought as they leave you, their offspring, to the savage delights of Valtharius the Mad. You will be as a virgin.... tied to the post screaming as with fangs of lust we hail shall the night. VtM: KARROTSMELL!!! ME LIKE BEACH!!! ME EAT JELLYFISH SANDWICH!!! [Karachel gives Valtharius a sideways glance and waves him away.. Valtharius is running down the night time beach picking up jellyfish and swallowing them] K: As you can see my protege has a weak mind.... however he MORE than makes up for it in stature and savage strength! [Karachel begins walking along the edge of the seashore... he kneels and cups what looks like is water from the waves lapping over his feet. What he pours from his hand is semi-coagulated blood] K: The blood of many victims pours upon the sands of time... Cuban Mafia once the next day has arisen you shall be as slaves of virtue rotting in the sun... your souls will be given over to our Dark Lord for mutilation as they begin their wraith-like pleading to the heavens on their bleeding knees in search of relief... but the only thing shall find is nothing but this nightmare.... A nightmare of nothing but this black euphoria! [The camera pulls closer to Karachel as he pulls off his cowl and the full ugliness of his soul fills his face with the un-holy joy of the carnage yet to come. His needlelike teeth glow in the pale blue moon light as his hot breath pours from his mouth as he speaks] K: For you the sun shall never rise again! You shall have no more visions of victory and like all others before you... you shall be led into a shallow grave. [Valtharius quickly approaches and stands beside Karachel... his massive chest heaving and his hot breath filling the air] K: Cuban Mafia... do you truly realize who you face? I think not! [He places a hand on Valtharius's chest] K: This is Valtharius the Mad, The Ghoul Of All Mankind.... Warrior eternal... in previous lives he has carved his name in the flesh of Venetian princes, raped the daughters of Attila the Hun, and set ablaze the seven hills of Rome. Know him and you shall know Eternal Damnation! VtM: ROME!!! ME WANT GO HOME FIGHT IN PITS!!! K: Perhaps if you defeat the Cuban Mafia you shall have a respite in the Underground Gladiatorial Pits my stupid friend... I will allow you to fight unbarred and unfettered by the rules of a wrestling ring.. would you enjoy that? VtM: YES!!! [spitting pieces of chewed up jellyfish from his mouth as he yells] NO RULES!!! K: Yes... no rules Cuban Mafia.. you see how that places joy upon Valtharius's face don't you? Once you step into the ring Valtharius shall chase the angels away and pull the demons from your mind and make your worst nightmares come to pass! [Turns towards Valtharius and speaks] K: Closer toward your prize Valtharius.... Closer to the shores of Hell you stride! In my Dark Masters infernal world the souls you've sacrificed. The die has been cast... it is far to late for the Cuban Mafia to back out now... Valtharius... you are the King of the Dead... striding the land causing untold chaos and destruction all the while keeping one foot firmly entrenched in the bowels of Hell! VtM: HELL!!! CHAOS!!! DESTRUCTION!!! ME VALTHARIUS HURT CIGAR PEE-PUHL!!! ME EAT WEASEL AFTER MATCH!!! [Valtharius runs out into the bloody surf and splashes around covering himself in the icor of slain souls as Karachel gleams with unholy delight... the camera pans above them both and focuses on the cloud enshrouded moon... a lone owl flits quickly across its silver blue radiance and the scene slowly fades to black]
