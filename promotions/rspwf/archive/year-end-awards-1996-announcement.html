The 1996 First Annual R.S.P-W.F Awards

31 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello readers of r.s.p-w.f, r.s.p.w.i, and r.s.p-w! (Sorry for the cross-post.) I'm [name withheld], President of the CFPW and former President of the CNWL. I'd like to say that the CFPW is not the best, nor is it one of the best. Everone has their own idea which fed is the best in all of e-wrestling. R.S.P-W. has it's awards, now I have set up the r.s.p-w.f awards. To nominate someone for any award e-mail me at [detail withheld]@direct.ca . The nominations end at noon PST on November 15th and at that time, the e-wrestlers nominated will be put into a list in which the readers of r.s.p-w.f can chose a first, second, and third choice. The voting for that will end on December 3rd at noon PST. Then by the end of December, I will post the winners, runners-up, et cetera to the newsgroup for all to read. Now to the 21 different awards: Best Wrestler of The Year Best Tag Team of The Year Best Heel Of The Year Best Face of The Year Best Worker <or Roleplayer> Best Flyer Best Technical Wrestler Best Brawler Most Popular Wrestler Most Overrated Wrestler Best Wrestling Gimmick Best Wrestling Move Best Match of The Year Best Feud of The Year Most Disappointing News Item of The Year Best Manager of The Year Best Announcer/Colour Commentator Best Interviewee Best Angle of The Year Best Wrestling Show and....... Best E-Wrestling Federation of the Year! Thank you for all participating. I hope one of your guys wins. The results will be out by the end of December, yadda, yadda, yadda. Thank you all so very much, [name withheld] [detail withheld]@direct.ca Ballot Taker of the R.S.P-W.F awards
