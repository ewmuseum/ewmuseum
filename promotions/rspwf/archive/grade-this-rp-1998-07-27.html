[the scene is set on a 747 airplane, on its way to Panama City, FL. The camera zooms up and down the isle way. From the back of the plane and on its way to the front. The camera picks up various different scenes. Two children aruging over a bag of peanuts, and their parents trying to calm them down. A newly wed couple kissing, on their way to their honey moon in Panama City Beach.]
[The camera continues to make its way toward the cockpit. Now, we find ourselves in the first class section of this airplane. Nice, leather seats...and in them, sit very wealthy people. The camera finally makes its stop, and slowly turns to the right. Where "Stylish" Steve Milla and his girlfriend, Monica, happen to be sitting. Monica is in the window seat, and she has a pillow behind her head, as she is getting a nice nap before their arrival. Milla has on a set of head phones, and by the sound coming out of them, he is listening to some kind of alternative rock group]
[the camera man sits in the third seat, to get the best possible view of whats going on. Milla is tapped on the shoulder, and he abruptly turns toward his rightside. He see's Monica sound asleep. Milla now turns to his left, and he is shocked at the sight of the camera. He takes off his headphones and speaks...]
MILLA: HOLY GOD!! You scared the living shit out of me! You media people just can't get enough of me can you?
[Milla turns to see the other side of the camera. The camera reads Zeilinski productions, and Milla has a sinister look on his face]
MILLA: Yeah, that's right Z-Cup participants. The "Stylish-One" has decided that it is time to take charge of his career. I heard about this tournament, and I just couldn't help myself. I had to be a part of it.
King Apples, Andrew Tucker, Ian Walsh, Ivan Ramius. Just a few of the "big" name stars that I have had the pleasure of facing one on one in the ring. All four of these men will be competing in the Z-Cup. And
If I run into them on my way to the finals...
...it will be my pleasure to take them down once again! [Milla puts his tray table up and continues] MILLA: BUT... Before I am officialy a participant in this tournament, I have to meet yet another "big" superstar in a qualifying match. There is only one spot left, and in order to make it fair, I have to battle it out with...
...Vinny Vitrone! Yes fans, that's right. My former friend, and now arch-rival, Vinny Vitrone. The man whom I have spiked to hell and back, time and time again. The man, who wears _MY_ N.E.W. Heavyweight Championship.
Vitrone, Vincent, you know as well as I do, that all of your success is thanks to me. Maybe it is fate that we are are to meet for the first time, one on one. Maybe it is luck. Either casenario, I couldn't be happer. I made you
Vitrone, and I can unmake you just like that.
You are N.E.W. Heavyweight Champion for one reason, and one reason only... ...I let you! On both occassions, I took out Pillow, and made you champion. I didn't have to help you Vitrone, and truthfully, I wasn't helping you. I was helping...
myself. But we will meet again. On Friday, July 31st, on N.E.W. programming. It doesn't really matter to me where the hell it takes place, as long as it does happen. NEW, AeWF, hell...we could even do it in INWI. But I know you would piss in your pants if that were the case. Cause you could have all of your butt buddy "Brothers" sit in the front row. That isn't gonna happen Vinny.
Nobody can help you where we are going. I have been waiting months, MONTHS, to get in the ring with you. Vitrone, you've been dogging me for as long as I can remember. Finally, finally it will happen, and there is nothing you can do about it. You can't crawl to the front office, and demand another match. The date is set, its gonna happen, and you are finally going down...
once, and for all! [the flight attendant comes over the PA] Attendant: The captain has lit the seat belt sign, because we may be experiencing some heavy turbulence over the next couple minutes or so. So please, put on your seat belts and remain in your seats. Thank you.
MILLA: Turbulence, that doesn't scare me for one minute Vinny. I have been flying through turbulence all my life! I didn't get here in one night.
Nothing comes easy in this world of eWrestling. Everyone pays their dues
Vitrone. Unfortunately for you, payment comes July 31st for you.
Vincent, your payment is months overdue. And it will happen, I can promise that. Cause I for damn sure is gonna crank up the turbulence next Friday night. Your just gonna have to buckle up, and hope I don't crash land my foot in your ass!!
Vitrone, you are the only thing standing in my way of greatness. The pon in front of the king. We were bound to face off eventually, now is as good a time as any. I hold all the cards here Vitrone. Here in the Z-Cup, there aren't any "Brotha's" here to save your sorry ass. Its one on one, you -vs- me, mono e mono. There is no way around it Vincent.
[the plane begins to bounce around, and the turbulence begins to set in. Monica abruptly wakes up, and turns to Milla] MONICA: Where are we baby? MILLA: We are almost there hun, go back to sleep... [Milla props up her pillow, and kisses her on the cheek] MILLA: You once had it Vitrone. You once had the fire inside of you, the turbulence. But your ass got soft. I didn't wanna do it, but I had no choice but to spike your ass. Now you live for the fans. You used to not care Vinny, you used to live life with no rules. That is what made you great...once.
I utilized your talent. I took you to the top of your game... ...World Champion material. But you fucked it all up. You wanted to step in front of me, and look where it got you. Nothing. Only second best. That's all you will every be.
THERE WAS A LINE VITRONE!! I was in front! I always lead the way, never take a backseat...NEVER!! [a flight attendant walks up to Milla] Attendant: Excuse me sir, could you please quiet down? MILLA: No I can't hun, but can you do me a favor? [Milla pulls a 100 dollar bill out of his pocket and slips it to the flight attendant] MILLA: Vodka martini on the rocks... Attendant: Ok sir, I will be right with you. MILLA: Make it snappy. [the attendant quickly walks off to the back to get his drink] MILLA: You see that Vitrone? I don't listen to authority. I just play them. Just like I have played you. Just like I plan on playing you. You were my right hand man. You were #2, where you have always been. You tried to be #1, but you just didn't have it in you. You will never be half the man that I am. Three months ago you would've told that waitress to stick it.
Two months ago you wouldve told her to stick it, in a nice and cunning way. Just like I have done. Today... You would apologize, and try to put your worthless pick up lines on her. That's your problem Vitrone. Too damn soft. In the end... ...it will also be your downfall. [the turbulence ceases, and the captain comes over the PA system] Captain: Attention ladies and gentlemen. We are approximately 10 minutes from landing. Be sure to have all your tray tables up, and ready for landing.
Enjoy the rest of the day, and thank you for flying American Airlines. MILLA: My time is up. Vitrone, heed this warning. Be prepared. Don't bring the same soft ass Vinny Vitrone to our match next Friday. I want you ready, and primed. Until next time. Im Stylish Steve Milla, and with me...
.....things..... .....are never as they seem! [Milla turns to Monica and whispers something in her ear. She turns to him, and they engage in some heavy kissing. The camera fades, and the scene ends]
