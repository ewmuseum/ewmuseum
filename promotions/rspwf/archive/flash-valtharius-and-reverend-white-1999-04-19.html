[FLASH] Valtharius and Reverend White

19 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I thought some of you with a sense of humor might enjoy the following flash... Do not take it personally. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Scene opens to two nice looking young men in suits walking down the street carrying a book in their hands. An older couple walks past them and the two young men pause to talk to them and gesture towards the book. The old people smile warmly and take a book from one of the young men. The two young men continue on their way as the old couple leave. Suddenly the two young men are pulled into some large bushes by two pairs of hands. The sound of bone being crushed and broken disturbs the quiet warm summer air. The sound of cloth rustling is heard then more rustling and tearing. Finally two figures step out wearing the torn suits of the young men. The suits are an ill fit upon the huge frames of Valtharius the Mad and his partner in violence, Reverend
White. The pair are seen walking away, down the street, as Karachel suddenly pops into a side frame on the screen]
K: Wish to help your fellow man? Wanting to enrich your pathetic lives? [A scene of the Altar Boys trying to steal a hearse outside of a funeral home. Valtharius and Reverend White come running as if to stop them in the act of a crime and save their souls. They wear upon the pockets of their suits a placard that has their names on it... Mr. White and Mr. Valtharius ]
RW: Stop there you young people! Don't you know you are going about this the wrong way? You will learn nothing if you do that!
AB's: [in unison] Thank you sir for pointing out to us the wrong of our ways. Will you help us amend the situation?
RW: By all means! Mr. Valtharius, would you help them please? [Valtharius draws back his ham hock sized fist and smashes it through the drivers side window of the hearse]
RW: There you go you fine young men. Learn to do things the right way with the direct approach!
[The Altar Boys climb inside the hearse and speed off. The side frame appears again with Karachel as the scene changes to Mr. Valtharius and Mr.
White walking up to a white picket fence in front of a very nice house. Mr. Valtharius stomps THROUGH the fence and through the flower garden. Mr. White follows with a grim look on his face] K: Ever feel like spreading the word of righteousness and light? Not me! And not these two either!
[Mr. White knocks on the door of the house and an old woman answers] WOMAN: Hello you fine young men. What can I do for you? VTM: JOIN!!! WOMAN: Join? Join your church? RW: Yes, ma'am. You shall join our church.... NOW! VTM: ME CONVERT OLD BAG!!! [Mr. Valtharius draws back his fist once more and slams it into the chest of the old woman. She flies across her living room and falls limp to the floor.
She raises her head for a split second then it slumps sideways to the floor, blood slowly pouring from her mouth. Mr. White and Mr. Valtharius walk in the house and over to the old woman. Looking down they see her chest is completely caved-in. Mr. Valtharius gives out a resounding fart and scratches his rear]
VTM: ME VALTHARIUS CONVERT HER TOO HARD!!! [Valtharius steps on her chest and plods over to a vase of roses and begins chewing on them furiously]
RW: She has joined us regardless... her soul now belongs to our Master. [The side frame appears once more with Karachel within. The next scene shows Mr. Valtharius and Mr. White wearing the MLW Tag Team Championship belts about their waists as they are walking down the street. Two members of a rival religion are seen walking towards them with bibles in hand]
K: Do you feel persecuted because of your religious beliefs? Do other religions shun you because you believe in blood and in the god of death and violence?
[One of the rival missionaries opens up his bible and begins reading aloud and cursing Mr. Valtharius and Mr. White to eternal damnation in the lowest pits of Hell. Mr. Valtharius looks rather unimpressed as he doesn't understand anything the missionary is saying. This is evident as Mr.
Valtharius's brain is the size of a pea and has the mental capacity of a rotten head of cabbage. Mr. White pulls out his Bible Thumper and whacks one of them over the head knocking him out. The other one finishes one verse and begins another but not before Mr. Valtharius "baptizes" him with a rain of fists]
VTM: NO MORE TALK!!! NO MORE FUNNY WORDS!!! VALTHARIUS NOT LIKE IT!!! RW: I now pronounce last rites upon thee.... [Mr. White makes the sign of the cross.......]
[Slow fade to the next scene, that of Karachel floating on the street on a nice warm summers day. The houses behind him are in shambles as Mr.
Valtharius is seen chasing a small dog around looking for his next meal. Mr. White walks out of a house dragging a screaming telephone repairman behind him. All of a sudden the house explodes as a huge jet black locomotive pulls through it. The locomotive has strange runes upon it as well as the words scratched in blood along the side "Heaven & Hell Express". Reverend White sheds the worn out suit and discards it. He then climbs aboard the huge jet black locomotive and pulls the horn. The lonesome sound of a banshee penetrates the air as windows in the surviving houses explode inwards shredding their occupants. Karachel smiles and laughs with evil intent.
Valtharius comes around the front of the locomotive, a dogs back leg hanging lazily out of his cavernous mouth. Karachel floats to the locomotive then turns to the camera]
K: Ahhhhh... missionary work takes so much out of a person... and a much more out of the neighborhood! If you would like to face Heaven and Hell as a tag team or individually please write to....
[Words appear on the screen: Karachellian Ministries of the Squared Circle Gospel 666 Leviathan Lane Salem, MA 00126 The words fade to be replaced by a phone number: 1-800-GO2-HELL] K: Remember pathetic fools, taking care of your fellow man is OUR business! [Valtharius and Karachel are now aboard the train as it pulls out. The runes pulsate an evil blackish-blue as the locomotive crushes several cars and turns over a fire hydrant. The screams of the damned can be heard coming from the inside passenger cars of the hell-train]
VOICE: This message has been brought to you by the Church of Cheese and Rice of Rattleday Snakes.
[Fade to black] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Hope you enjoyed it... and if you didn't, I have two words for you..... No, not those two.... TOO BAD! -- Listen to The Voice of Reason!
