Anyone wanna critique a match? :)

4 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This was the pre-main event a few weeks back at our Sept. PPV. I would like some thoughts on the overall quality of the match if you have the time and energy. It's a hardcore fed and this particular match was my idea, a
Barb-Wire Hell in the Cell match for the HWO Suicidal title. Came up with what I felt were original high-spots and a creative approach to the match, would like your thoughts about the match: Strentgths, weaknesses, observations about style...anything you care to add is appreciated.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Pre Main Event - Blood Thirst (c) versus "Homicide" Dutch Blackheart for The
Suicidal Title
*Blood Thirst's theme music breaks lose and the HWO fans mark out! Blood makes his way slowly down the ramp sporting the Suicidal Belt around his waist. He stops half way down, and pulls off his shit to reveal his ribs are in perfect shape! They are no longer taped and the fans are showing their support for this match! Blood climbs into the ring....mounts the corners as he holds the belt over his head and plays the crowd for a bit.
Suddenly...a loud church bell is heard over the intercom, a long slow gong, the fans go quiet, as the announcers, referee, and Blood thirst turn to the entrance way.
*The Helltron comes on, and the man in black is shown standing over the Valley of Suffering and Pain, the set is quiet except for the sound of wind blowing. Suddenly, and evil voice utters a single word: CAREER KILLA
*Dutch Blackheart steps through the curtain, Halloween by John Carpenter rocks the building and the Helltron begins to show segments from his previous matches in the HWO. Dutch is shown attacking Tony King. Blackheart is seen in prison ramming a man's head through the bars of a cell. This is followed by a segment of him smashing the Virus in the face with an 8-Ball in a sock. Jordan Michaels is shown being nailed with the Pain Bringer.
Steve White is shown being powerbombed onto someone's knee buy Dutch. Dutch is shown driving Chris Angel into the mat with the Pain Bringer. Dutch is shown driving a sledge hammer into Blood Thirsts ribs. Finally, Dutch is shown as he hoists Steve Blanchard into the air and nails the Pain Bringer through a table onto a pile of belts under the table. The segments depicting Blackheart's most violent moments repeat themselves as he walks to the ring.
*Blackheart slides into the ring, as his music continues to play ominously and he and Blood begin to stare each other down. Ringlord: Jesus that's creepy... Kamoren: There is no doubt the wrestling world is going to see a brutal spectacle tonight.
Ringlord: Right now, the barb-wire Hell in the Cell is being lowered as
Dutch and Blood continue to stare each other down, neither man looks to be anything less than 100% ready!
Kamoren: We've got some commotion down there...what's going on...someone's crawling out from under the ring!
Ringlord: Shit...that's "Deadly" Deke Lucas! What the hell is he doing down there! Kamoren: It's no secret him and Dutch go back a ways...could this be a set up! Christ, if Blood Thirst is trapped in the cage with BOTH men, it'll be a complete massacre!
Ringlord: No, Deke just rolled under the cage as it was being lowered...looks like he has something in his hands too!
Kamoren: Looks like bolt cutters...what the hell is up with that! Ringlord: I don't know, but it can't be good.....I suspect before the night is over we'll all know!
*The cage comes to a halt, a door can be seen with a heavy padlock on it. The referee double checks the door, and in doing so tears his shirt as it is snagged on the barb wire. The time keeper rings the bell, and Blood and
Dutch sail into each other. Both men are in the center of the ring, trading violent forearm shots and punches. Blood starts to take the upper hand before playing the crowd quickly and leveling Blackheart with a giant forearm smash to the side of the head. Dutch is pulled to his feet, and
Blood Thirst outs him to the mat with a stiff body slam. Blood thirst is on fire as he drops an elbow on Dutch before pulling him up by his hair. Dutch blocks a punch and counters with a thumb to the eyes and a knee lift to the ribs. Blood is stunned as Dutch throws him through the ropes to the floor between that mat and the cage. Dutch climbs the top rope, and waits. Blood is on his feet...Dutch crashing down with a double axehandle!!!
Ringlord: That's almost 300 pounds of man coming down there...unbelievable,. Kamoren: So far, this match is rally shaping up to live up to all the hype going in! *Dutch picks Blood Thirst up, signals for a hay maker...but WAIT. Blood just grabbed Dutch by the shoulders! He picks Dutch up, swings him around, and SLAMS DUTCH'S BACK against the cage! He drags Dutch down, digging in the barb wire against Dutch's back! WAIT...Dutch just grabbed Blood Thirst by the head and rammed him FACE FIRST into the cage! Dutch is grinding
Blood's face back and forth across the wires!
Ringlord: that did it! Blood Thirst's face is a crimson mask and Dutch's back looks like he's a drive-by victim!
Kamoren: Early on, both me are open wide! But we didn't expect anything less from these bitter enemies!
*Blood breaks free of Dutch's hold and levels Dutch with an Asiatic Thrust to the throat! Blood Thirst rolls Dutch back into the ring, grabs a steel chair, and slides in behind him. Dutch is stumbling around, Blood lifts the chair...SMACK! Right across Blackheart's bleeding back! Dutch screams in pain as Blood spins him around and waffles Dutch across the side of the headwith a chair shot. Dutch goes down, and Blood makes the cover.
1.......... 2.......... KICKOUT! Ringlord: Unbelievable...how did he find it in him to do that! Kamoren: He's a man on a mission! *Blood throws the steel chair down and signals for the DDT. He has Dutch in the front face lock...Dutch counters! Spin around by Dutch as he slaps on a
Northern Lights Suplex on Blood ONTO the chair! He's got the bridge and the ref makes the cover!
1.......... 2.......... KICKOUT! *Blood Thirst is on his feet, more angry than hurt, Dutch is still down reeling from the chair shots! Blood jams the chair between the second and third ropes in the corner and drags Dutch to the opposite corner. Blood with the Irish Whip...Dutch crashes hard and leaves a big blood stain on the chair! Dutch is on his knees, clutching is back as Thirst turns him inside out with a devastating running kick to the face! Dutch is crawling as Blood pushes Blackheart's neck down across the bottom rope. Blood leaves Dutch hanging there, slides under the bottom rope, and grabs another chair. Blood
Thirst winds un and swings....Dutch falls back! Blood hits HIMSELF in the face with the chair as it bounces off the empty rope and back at him! Dutch gabs the top rope and swings his feet through the bottom ropes! Dutch kicks the steel chair into Blood's face and dries the back of Blood;'s head into the cage in the process! Blood is screaming as the barb wire digs into the back of his skull!
Ringlord: Jesus! There aren't going to be enough stitches in the entire state to put these men back together!
Kamoren: Only in the HWO! *Dutch is on the floor, Blood Thirst is holding his head as blood pours between his fingers. Blood Thirst is trying to crawl under the ring, and
Dutch is moving pretty slow...still shaking off the chair shots. Blood is half under the ring and Dutch has him by his feet, he's struggling to pull him back out. Blood pushes off with his feet and Blackheart stumbles back into the cage with a yell! Blood is pulling something out from under the apron...A SPOOL OF BARBWIRE! Blood throws the big spool into the ring!
Ringlord: That spool of wire was huge! Must weigh 80 pounds! Kamoren: Easily, damn thing was as wide as Blood Thirst! Blood throws
Dutch into the ring as the spool stops rolling. The ref is trying to stay out of the way as Blood thirst levels Dutch with a vertical suplex. Blood is pointing at the spool of wire. He Irish whips Dutch into the ropes...DROP TOE HOLD ONTO THE SPOOL! Dutch's face is a mess as his head and upper body slam into the roll of wire! Blood is signaling for something else....POWERBOMB! He hoists Dutch up over the spool!
Ringlord: If he lands this Dutch is a goner! Kamoren: Don't count either of these guys out until the bell rings my friend! *Dutch is hoisted up and Blood is holding him. Dutch is punching though..Blood stumbles back and falls down! Dutch is still on top of him punching away! Dutch drags Blood out...he's signaling for the PAIN BRINGER!
Dutch sets him up...COUNTER BY BLOOD THIRST! Blood Russian leg sweeps Dutch
ONTO the roll of barb wire! The fans go crazy as Dutch is tore open again!
Blood Thirst is pointing at the spool again...he's got Dutch in a side headlock....going for the Bulldog! NO...Dutch with a counter of his own....he hoists Blood Thirst up...SIDE SALTO SUPLEX onto the spool! Blood
Thirst is head to toe covered in blood, Dutch not much better off. Dutch is dragging Blood to the center of the ring, he places Bloods arms at his side.
Ringlord: What is Dutch up to? Kamoren: Who knows, but it isn't good...you can't bet on that. Blood is in the center of the ring, his arms at his sides, Dutch picks the spool up and DROPS it ion Blood's chest! Now Dutch is rolling the spool up and down Blood Thirsts body! The fans are screaming and Dutch looks fit to kill! He sets the spool down, flips Blood thirst over on his stomach...he's
ROLLING THE SPOOL UP AND DOWN BLOOD'S BACK NOW! Ringlord: Blackheart is one sadistic son of a bitch! Kamoren: I give you a dollar if you say that to his face! *Blood Thirst is pulled to his feet by Dutch, Thirst's blood pools at his feet as hundreds of punctures spew blood. Dutch takes a few steps back....he's signaling for something. WAIT...Blood Thirst lunges out of the corner with a SPEAR! Both men are down and bleeding heavily! Blood Thirst is signaling for his patented move....the Blood Lust! Blood Thirst swings
Dutch to his shoulder....he's locked him in for the leaping sit-down tombstone that he's won so many matches with! Dutch is kicking his feet...Blood is struggling....HE PLANTS DUTCH!
Ringlord: It's all over! Blood Thirst just landed his patented move! Kamoren: Unbelievable.... *The ref drops for the count...... 1.......... 2.......... DUTCH WITH HIS FOOT ON THE BOTTOM ROPE! *Blood Thirst is furious, but he wandered too close to the ropes when Dutch was struggling. He's mad as hell and looking up to the roof of the cage now! The fans go crazy as he points up, points to Dutch, and runs his thumb across his throat! Blood grabs Dutch by the seat of his pants and throws him through the ropes to the floor. Blood thirst goes to the door of the cage and grabs the padlock and chain.
Ringlord: Blood Thirst reaching for something in his trunks... Kamoren: It's the key...he's unlocking the door to the cage!!!! Ringlord: How the hell did he get that! Kamoren: Does it matter! *Blood thirst pulls the chain off the door and kicks it open. Blood Thirst wraps the chain around his arm...RUNNING CLOTHESLINE on Dutch! Dutch drops hard as Blood Thirst begins to whip Blackheart across the back with the chain. Dutch is down and hurting, Blood Thirst pulls him up and throws him through the open door. Dutch stumbles down....Blood Thirst grabs him, Dutch spins around...SMASHES BLOOD THIRST IN THE MOUTH WITH THE PADLOCK!!!!!!!!!
Blood Thirst's mouth and nose are a bloody mess and he spits out a few teeth. Dutch drops the lock and begins to climb the cage. Blood manages to come in behind him....they are half way up the cage, side by side. They are trading weak blows as they climb the cage, trying to avoid the barbed wire!
Blood has managed to pull ahead and he reaches the top first. Dutch is pulling himself onto the top as Blood thirst is standing. Blood Thirst pulls Dutch up and is punching him in the face. Blackheart is stumbling back, teetering on the edge of the cage. Blood lands one final blow as
Dutch stumbles back....DUTCH grabs Blood by the neck!
Ringlord: The only thing separating Dutch from a 20 foot fall into oblivion is his enemies neck!
Kamoren: Only in the HWO! *Blood thirst grabs Dutch's arm and pulls him away from the edge of the cage. Thirst hip tosses Dutch to the center of the roof of the cage. The ref ducks as bits of metal connecting the cage to the supports begins to rain down as the roof begins to sag. Blood has Dutch by the feet, flip over and walk through...BOSTON CRAB! Dutch's face and body are being ground into the barb wire on top of the cage!! The cage is literally raining blood as both men continue to bleed heavily! The referee is looking up as Blood breaks the hold and turns to look at the fallen Dutch Blackheart. Blood is signaling for the Blood Lust!
Ringlord: He slaps that on up there they both might get killed! Kamoren: You ain't kidding! As if the barb wire wasn't bad enough! *Blood Thirst has Dutch, swings him up on his shoulder...he's going to to the jumping sit-don pile driver on the roof of the barb-wire covered cage! WAIT...Dutch is struggling..he's kicking his feet. Blood Thirst is stumbling...he can't get his footing and Dutch is struggling too much! Blood is falling back, Dutch is on his feet and Blood is bent backwards between his legs! Dutch looks worn....he grabs Blood by the waste, spins him around...scoop up...SIT DOWN POWER BOMB!
Ringlord: JESUS CHRIST! The Roof Just CAVED IN! Kamoren: Someone call in the EMTs!!! *The roof buckles as Dutch lands and both men crash through the roof to the mat below. When they hit, both men roll a bit and look unconscious! The referee is checking both men, neither are moving! He's putting on the 10 count!
1.......... 2.......... 3.......... 4.......... 5.......... 6.......... 7.......... 8........... *Dutch is crawling slowly....he drags himself over to Blood Thirst and drapes an arm over his chest. The ref is down and making the count! 1.......... 2.......... DUTCH BREAKS THE COUNT! Ringlord: Dutch just pulled Blood Thirst off the mat! What the hell! Kamoren: What the hell can he be thinking! He's got no right moving at this point so why the hell would he stop the count!
Dutch rolls out of the ring and reaches under the apron for something. The fans are screaming as Dutch drags what appears to be a table from under the canvas The table appears strange and Dutch slides it into the ring it becomes apparent why. Dutch is setting up the table and the table is seen to be wrapped in barb wire with two steel chairs being held in place between the table and the wire. The wire is paid on thick, and the entire device looks dangerous and menacing.
Ringlord: THAT"s What Deke Lucas was doing under there! He was throwing that sick piece of equipment together!
Kamoren: I think you're right...this is SICK! *Dutch has set the table up, as he slowly moves to Blood Thirst. Blood can barely stand as Dutch pulls him to his feet...the referee is completely white and cant believe what he's seen tonight. Dutch hoists Blood Thirst into the torture rack, and his knee buckles....Dutch is down on one knee,
Blood on his shoulder not moving. Dutch grunts and stands erect, with a menacing grin he drives Blood Thirst through the table!
Ringlord: PAIN BRINGER!! PAIN BRINGER! Dutch Blackheart just drove Blood
Thirst through a mess of barb wire, steel chairs, and a folding table with his patented move!
Kamoren: Dutch is making the cover! *The ref drops, and makes the count! 1.......... 2.......... 3!!!!!!!!!! The ref raises Dutch's hand as he hands him the Suicidal Belt. Blackheart crumbles to the mat, a bloody mess as 2 teams of EMT's break into the cage with stretchers and begin applying dressing to the hundreds of wounds both men are carrying.
Ringlord: Can you believe this! Kamoren: That had to be the MOST brutal match in HWO history! Ringlord: One team has Blood thirst on the stretcher ready, Christ he's amess! Kamoren: looks like they are preparing Dutch for the other stretcher as the first team is carrying Blood outa the arena!
Ringlord: Wait a scond...some action down there.... Kamoren: It's Dutch! *Dutch begins to struggle as the EMT's try to strap him to the board. Dutch pushes them off, grabs up his Suicidal Title, and stands. He looks to the crowd who are going crazy over the spectacle...he lift's his belt over his head. The Church Bell is heard again, followed by the word CAREER KILLA.
Music breaks the noise as Tubular Bells, The Exorcist Theme strikes up and
Dutch climbs out of the ring. the EMT's look on in shcok as Dutch slowly walks te ramp, leaving behind blood foot prints and splashes of crimson with each step. He passes through the curtain as hismusic continues, and the
EMT's begin to pack up their equipment.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- *Kamoren: You have gotta be kiddin' me. That match was THE most gruesome of all time, and yet Dutch Blackheart managed to fuckin' walk out of here! *Ringlord: It's strange...but 'Homicide' isn't human! There's something about him that makes you think and KNOW that he isn't. *Kamoren: But we have got to applaud Blood Thirst for giving it his all 'til the very end of this thing, it was sensational! *Ringlord: I don't think we'll EVER forget this match, ever!
