1997 RSPWF 200! Part I

14 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once again, we have a complete RSPWF 200, and this time, it's on time. Before we get to the stuff you care about (who finished where) we need to deal with a few less pleasant items (me rambling) So, without further adieu... Over twice as many people voted this year as last year. This means a few things. Once, thank you for the marvelous response. Two, this list is a much tougher one to crack then last years. If you made last year's list, it is possible that even if you received more votes this year than last year, you may not have ranked as highly, or not made the list this year. (If it makes you feel better, I have a guy in this boat.) RSPWF is bigger, and when any field grows, awards become that much harder to win. The flip side is those of you who made the list, from #200 all the way to the top guy, did extremely well. Actually, the top guy did better than extremely well, but we'll get to that in a minute. I have tried to note where people finished last year if you are a repeater. I may have missed a name change or something, or simply overlooked you, and if so, I'm sorry. About 30 people repeated, and they are to be congratulated again. E-wrestling fame is fleeting, so to excel for two years in a row is amazing. Comments, questions, etc. are welcomed and encouraged, provided a few things are kept in mind. One, I just do the math and keep the records. I don't know why voters preferred someone else over your guy, and I probably couldn't change their minds if I wanted to, which I don't. Two, I have already double-checked the math, and I'm not doing it again. Three, I almost certainly got your vote. Ballots were only rejected if they came after the deadline, or looked suspicious in any way, not because of my opinion of the wrestlers contained therein. Four, if after reading the above, you're still convinced I made a mistake, the proper procedure is to contact me privately by e-mail about it. Flame posts won't change a blasted thing, and I can virtually guarantee it will come off as sour grapes. If I did make a mistake, and that's certainly possible, I assure you I will correct it. Finally, if you insist on believing this was somehow rigged, and you believe the appropriate solution is to do a poll of your own, good luck and God bless. I should warn you, however, it's been tried. Not done. Just tried. On that vein, I want to thank everyone who supported the poll by voting or passing it on, or even by begging for votes on RSPWF. And regarding future polls, if you're interested in doing 1998's, AND YOU MEAN IT, let me know. I may need either help or a replacement next year, courtesy of the ever-intrusive real world. All that said, I realize that most of you stopped reading when you figured out this part would contain no names. But to those few of you who continued to the end, thank you again. And now, starting at #200...
