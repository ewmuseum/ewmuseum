[Critique of Roleplay] Edgar Noyd [Free Agent]

26 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in.] [Slowly, the view fades in to a shot of Comm Ave in Boston, Massachusetts. As the T passes by on the rail road tracks, a crowd of people stand in the distance all walking and jogging. The mass is simply living their lives. As the crowd begins to disperse, as the individuals all continue on their separate journey's, one man remains. Sitting on a dark, wooden bench is a fairly thick man wearing a plain white T-shirt and blue jeans with black boots. His head is completely shaved but he has quite a rough, grubby beard. He looks lost, as he mumbles incoherently to himself.]
MAN: Five O'clock, .. Movie Special.. Automobile, Bici.. Bicycle. Two wheels, bicycle. Bi-.. two. Cycle. Two wheel Bicycle. People walk and run. Busy, busy people. [As he continues mumbling to himself, a small grey cat mischievously strolls towards his feet. The man abruply stops mumbling as his eyes widen, looking at the cat.]
MAN: Kitty! [Lifting the cat up to his lap, the man cradles the feline and begins to pet it.] Nice kitty.. nice kitty. I'm going to take you home. [Suddenly, a woman appears infront of the man panting.] WOMAN: Oh, thank you! You found my cat. He just ran away from me. Thank you so much. MAN: What are you ta.. ta.. talking about? This is my kitten. I'm taking him home. W: Uh, .. no, I'm sorry but that is my cat. His name is Miles. Check his collar, it says so. M: NO! I SAID HE IS MINE AND I AM TAKING HIM HOME! [The woman reaches for the cat and after tugging for a few seconds, pulls the cat from the man's grasp.]
NO, GIVE ME MY KITTEN BACK!! GIVE IT BACK TO ME NOW! [Pedestrians walking by stop and look at the scene the man is making, as the woman quickly becomes embarassed.]
STRANGER: Hey lady, give the guy his cat back! WOMAN: It's my cat, though! He stole it from me! MAN: NO SUH! HIS NAME IS MILES, IT SAYS SO ON HIS COLLAR! [The stranger walks up to the woman and checks the collar. It says 'MILES.'] STRANGER: That is pretty sad, lady. Taking this man's cat. [Pulling the cat from the woman's hands, the stranger shakes his head in disgust and hands the cat to the unknown man.]
STRANGER: It's people like you who take advantage of the mentally challenged who deserve to be shot. Chrissakes. [The woman is completely flustered and shocked as the stranger walks off and the man continues to pet "his" kitten. Then, a young lady passes by, apparently on her way to work.]
MAN: How is your day? <smiles> [Fade to black.]
