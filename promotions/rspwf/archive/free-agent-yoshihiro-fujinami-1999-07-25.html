I'm looking for a fed, preferably booked, where I can have some fun. That's pretty much all I'm looking for, a place where I have a good time. Because to me that's what RPing is all about. So if you have a fed and you need some talent, please send info to
[detail withheld] . Here's my first RP with DFK, just to give a little background on Yoshihiro
Fujinami: [Scene opens with an inside shot of a small television studio. Picture zooms down to a shot of a news desk. Seated behind the desk is a small Japanese man in a gray twill suit. It reads, "TAMPAX Tampons Presents: Ultra Satellite System's

All that and more, right after this word from our sponsor, who reminds you, "If it happened, TAMPAX was there!"

But suddenly, Yoshihiro jumps in the ring, grabs his dad, and takes him down with a Fisherman buster. Adair Cole sits up, dazed, and Yoshihiro follows through with a viscous roundhouse kick that nails his father square in the face. Yoshihiro then removes his mask, while standing over his dad, and proceeds to drop his pants. The picture is blurry, but there is enough to realize that he is wiping his butt with the mask. The footage ends and it cuts back to visibly disgusted Diasuke Omori.]
Diasuke Omori: How a man can betray his father like that, I will never know. But I do know that since that day Yoshihiro Fujinami -- as he now wishes to be called -- has, more or less, disappeared. Only reappearing just recently.
Unfortunately, in the opinion of this man, we were able to contact Yoshihiro and we persuaded him to conduct an exclusive interview with Purosesu Weekly correspondent, Kentaro Liu. But first, we tried to contact Adair Cole and ask him what he thought about his son signing with DFK.
[Adair Cole is shown on the screen. At first he is looking down, as if he is searching for something. Then abruptly looks up with disgust.]
Adair Cole: I have no son by the name of Yoshihiro. [Cole then storms off. Omori is then shown back on the screen.] Diasuke Omori: We'll go to Kentaro Liu and his exclusive interview with Yoshihiro Fujinami, right after this! [Commercial begins. So I figure, what better way to get back into the swing of things then to take over the backyard promotion that Christopher Reese like's to call DFK. And I hardly think it'll be a challenge, I mean, have you seen the talent roster! What the hell?!!
Glam cam? Give me a f*ckin' break! Kentaro Liu: I'd care to differ. Maybe it's you who should take a look at the
'talent roster.' There's definitely a strong presence of great wrestlers and, quite honestly, I don't think you have a chance.

Kentaro Liu: I was just saying. But that and more, right after a word from
TAMPAX Tampons...

Liu by his hair and forces him to his knees. Yoshihiro then gets in his face.]
Yoshihiro Fujinami: Listen here you little rat! Obviously, you know what happened to my dad. You want you're f*ckin' teeth kicked in?!!
[Liu backs down and pleads with Yoshihiro to stop.] Yoshihiro Fujinami: No? Then get the f*ck out of my face! [Liu gets to his feet and he tries to compose himself, before he hurries out of the door. Fujinami then grabs the front of the camera and pulls it over to his chair.]
