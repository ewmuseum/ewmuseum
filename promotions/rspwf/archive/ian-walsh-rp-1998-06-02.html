Ian Walsh-rp

2 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, everyone. I am Ian Walsh. I just figured that I would send a recent roleplay to see what people think. I would like any opinions on this roleplay.
Be honest. About this roleplay . . . This roleplay was written for IeWC for my match against Kool Kid last Wednesday. Enjoy.
Ian Walsh Handler of: "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh (N.E.W., I.W.C., I.e-W.C., A.I.C.W., V.C.W.,
I.I.W.A., A.e-W.F.)
I.e-W.C. World Heavyweight Champion N.E.W. North American Champion http://members.aol.com/NAWfounder/HTR.html ================================================ {--Begin Transmission--} [Camera fades in.] [The camera drop-focuses on Ian Walsh who looks up at Frank Schifano who walks in and smiles at him. He's sitting at a table sipping iced tea.]
[Ian raises a brow at Frank when he walks in from outside. He's been gone since last night.]
IW: "What up, dawg?" [So much for sneaking in, Frank leans against the wall and speaks in a somewhat happy tone of voice.]
FS: "Nothing much, Rack." (Ian's description) [Ian Walsh stands just shy of six feet. His hair is dirty blond, wind swept in style and bleached from many days in the sun. His skin shows signs of losing the tanned bronze from the past day in the sun, soft laughter wrinkles surround his light blue eyes. He is dressed in a pair of black denim shorts that they appear new and stiff. His shirt is a green and black, vertically striped, hanging open at the nape to reveal a few wisps of sandy brown curls.]
[Ian signals for somebody to come to him.] [Enter Rook.] IW: "Frank, I'd like for you to extend a hand in friendship to this man. His name is Rook."
(Rook's description) [Rook is a very tall and built man. He stands around 6 feet, 7 inches tall. He looks to have made a hobby out of working out. He has a long, gray beard and mustache. However, the man hardly looks more than 30 years of age. His black hair is very short and cut almost to the scalp. He is wearing a plain white T-shirt and dark blue jeans.]
(Frank's description) [Frank's long, black hair is filled with sand. His glasses rest on the bridge of his nose. He lazily leans against the wall of this screen porch and looks to be tired. He is wearing a black T-shirt with denim shorts and boots to match.]
[Ian doesn't appear to mind Frank's condition in the least.] FS: "I'm not sure we've been formally introduced . . ." [He juts his hand forward.] FS: "I am Frank Schifano." [Rook extends his hand too and shakes Frank's hand very pleasantly. Indeed, his demeanor is one of welcome.]
FS: "Good to meet you." [He appears to admire Rook and also seems to be amazed at the mamouth size of the man.] RK: "So, Frank . . . I have heard a lot about you. I have a feeling that we will be great friends."
[The camera pans over to where Ian is sitting.] IW: "I'll tell ya . . . These two guys are really going to get along. I know that I can depend on them and I know that they will be there when I need them." [Ian takes a sip of tea and looks to his left and watches little children play outside of the porch. The sound of waves crashing down on the shorline is faintly heard in the distance. The sunlight peers through the screen. He turns toward the camera and speaks.]
IW: "This is peace . . . Peace isn't something that you get much of when you are in the wrestling business." [Ian sighs.] IW: "I guess _peace_ is something that I'll have to work for." [Ian takes another sip of his iced tea.] IW: "Where there is peace, there is pleasure. However, it is not always true in you reverse the saying. The question that comes to my mind is whether or not I will obtain peace this Wednesday as I face Kool Kid."
[Ian puts his cup of tea down.] IW: "Well . . ." [Ian leans back in his chair.] IW: ". . . only time can answer that question. However, I can speculate and I can predict that the answer is going to be
_no_." [Ian puts his hands behind his head and smiles.] IW: "You see . . . I can go out there and beat the hell out of Kool Kid and earn myself another victory. However, peace is far from being acheived. Sure . . . there will always be temporary peace that lingers around and is there only when I am away from the sport I love. And it doesn't matter much about who I beat and how many times I beat them. What matters is the fact that peace will never permanently come into my life."
[Ian leans forward and puts his arms on the table.] IW: "However . . . I see that no other man in this business will achieve the peace that they are searching for . . . the peace that they yearn . . . the peace . . . that people who are tourmented . . .
pray for." "Kool Kid . . . You may think that I am overrated. You may think that you have more talent and skill than the 'Human Torture Rack.' However, we, as well as the world, will all have to wait and see just who the better man and better wrestler is."
[Ian looks down at the white, metal table with a transparent, glass top.] IW: "Kool Kid . . . I can look into the nooks and crevices in the glass and they resemble the 'bumpy' and 'rugged' road that one must take in order to get through life. With a man's wrestling career, the road becomes even bumpier and harder to travel through. Kool
Kid . . . you can make this road easier to travel on or you can face me and I will stop you dead in your tracks."
[Ian sits up and points into the camera with his left index finger and smiles.] IW: "Kool Kid . . . things will _heat_ up once that bell rings to start the match. When that bell rings, that gives me a sign . . ."
[Ian takes his left index finger and points to the side of his head.] IW: ". . . the _sign_ tells me that I have the legal right . . . . . . the legal right . . . . . . to kick your ass." [Ian looks over and his smile becomes even bigger. Rook and Frank reappear in the camera shot.]
RK: "Hey, Ian. Talking about your match for this coming Wednesday?" IW: "Yes, I am, Rook." FS: "Does this kid actually think that he can beat you?" [A smirk appears on Ian's face.] IW: "I really don't know, Frank. All I can tell you is that I know that he _cannot_ beat me."
RK: "Damn straight, Ian . . ." [Rook smiles and points at Ian.] RK: ". . . cuz _you_ are the 'Human Torture Rack.' You are the man that busts his ass day in and day out throwing pieces of trash that don't belong here out of this business. You are the man, Ian.
Don't ever forget that." [Ian smiles.] IW: "Yeah. You are right, Rook. I have nothing to worry about. All I have to do is go into this match and kick some major ass as I always do."
[Frank interrupts.] FS: "But Ian . . . you can't underestimate this kid. Y'know that he has a lot of potential."
[Frank goes silent. After a moment, everyone breaks out in tremendous laughter. Ian speaks again.]
IW: "Oh Frank . . . you don't have to worry about that. I never underestimate my opponents."
[Ian pauses. After a moment, everyone laughs again.] IW: "No, but seriously. I am taking this match seriously. I am determined to win and show this _kid_ what a _man_ can do."
FS: "Yeah . . . this _kid_ should just give it up now." RK: "No, he should show up and get his ass kicked. As we all know, Ian needs all of the money he can get." [They continue to laugh.] IW: "Kool Kid . . . Prepare to meet . . . . . . your demise." [All three men calm down and talk with each other. The audio soon goes silent and the camera begins to lose focus.]
[Camera fades out.] {--End Transmission--}
