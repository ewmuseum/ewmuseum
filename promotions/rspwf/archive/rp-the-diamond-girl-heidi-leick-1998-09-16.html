::RP:: The Diamond Girl Heidi Leick

16 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The Scene opens up backstage in a small Arena where a GDWA Houseshow is going on.. "Feeding The Addiction " by Suicidal Tendencies is playing in the background.. standing there, in her ECW Triple Threat T Shirt , sneakers and stonewashed Levi's 501 jeans with some holes and tears in them is The Diamond
Girl Heidi Leick..., Jerry Styles, her Personal Interviewer and the Voice of
Extreme is also there.. holding the mic.. The camera zooms in ::
Jerry Styles: Good evening GDWAites, once again I'm here with The Diamond Girl
Heidi Leick, just days away from her upcoming match with Bloody Mary... Heidi,
Mary has quite the reputation here of being Extreme.. and she's also had some
.. um, well.. some ~words~directed at you...care to reply?
The Diamond Girl Heidi Leick: Well, ::looking serious at Jerry :: Riddle me this, Riddle me that, ::Looks into the camera stone faced:: who really cares what the heck she's getting at? ::Giggles:: Jerry... do you honestly think I
~care~ like what she says about me?::rolls her eyes:: I mean rea~lly...calling me a ~baby face~?
Diddn't that term go out in the 70's? Is it MY fault that the people ~LOVE~ and
~ADORE~ me no matter WHAT I do? NO... ::grins evilly:: Now I see why they call her Bloody Mary..
JS: Why's that? because of her extreme style? DGHL: No.. It's cause she's always PMSing about something..::sneers:: JS: Lets not go there... DGHL: I agree...:: Sticks her tongue out in disgust:: I mean.. heck.. we got a lot of STUPID People doing STUPID things here in the GDWA recently..::Quite serious attitude:: and , The ~Biggest~ Dummy of them all is Bloody Mary.. I mean.. this wench comes and insults everyone in the GDWA, then picks a fight and challenges ME..:;giggles:: Not a good idea, I Guess she thought ~I~ was going to roll over and play dead like Sweet Innocent Candi Bratton..
Oh..Mary,... I saw your ~match~ vs that little girl.. you and your 200 lb thunder thighs were just too much for a sweet, Innocent little girl with the name ~Candi~ in a streetfight...
JS: Heidi, Candi's not so ~Sweet~ and ~innocent~ DGHL: SHHH!!. you Interupted me~!! Now, Scary Mary..::Giggles psychotically:: Scary Mary.. the announcers seem to have bought into your parade of garbage.. but I haven't... Oh.. I heard them saying.. "All she needs is a few wins to be considered for World Title Status"... but Mary...:: evil stare into the camera as it zooms in :: you picked the wrong BITCH to make a stepping stone out of...
Because this.. :;laughs evilly:: ~This~ ~is~ ~MY~ ~Audition~..... And Oh,
::giggles:: what Glorious fun it will be to see your body, broken and bloody , lying on the mat as I audition for the newest group of bidders for my services.... I care not for gold.. or who the # 1 Contenders are here... I
Just want someone to beat on...and Tuesday Night.. ::Gets very quiet, intimdating and serious :: Scary Mary... you're my punching bag...
JS: Heidi.. Um.. ::backing away a bit :: Don't you think that's a little extreme? I mean.. even for you? ::Seeming very Concerned::
DGHL: Styles.. I can forgive.. but ~I~ never forget.. and all those manes you calle d My Mary.. well... lets say I've heard them before.. and I ~forgive~ you, you pathetic slob... I Honestly do..I ~forgive~ you for trying to bring me down to your level...
I ~forgive~ you for having the gaul to mention my name off you foul breathed tounge... and I even ~forgive~ you for anything you'll ~TRY~ and do to me..
but.. :;Giggles:: Someone has to pay..:;Starts to sing in a weird melody:: they have to ~pay~ for the ~sins~ of the past... And Mary.. that someone is you... Mary.. how can you call me Stupid.. when you don't understand what I say.. how can you say anything about me? Hmm?
JS: Heidi.. she just did an Interview about you.. that's all.. don't get carried away here..
DGHL:HUH!!?!?!? ::looks around nervously:: Carried away?!?!? ::a look of terror on her face:: NO~!!.. not the men in white coats again!!.. NO~!!!...
JS: HEIDI!!.... Just ~calm~ down.. I meant.. DGHL: Jerry..::Whispering very loudly:: Shhh!. they can't find us if you whisper!!.. now...I know they told you that sometimes my mind dosen't work..
but it's a lie... SHH!! I'm not crazy... I promise.. I'm on a mission... just trust me.. I know my Lucks been rotten lately, and I'm feeling like crap..
it'sll all be OK, I'll fell much better after I find someone to hit... I'm sick of talking now... it's time for action.. and I know who I'm gonna jack... hey
Jerry...
JS: :;Whispering back :: What? DGHL: Watch me Tuesday, in my audition for a stable.. :;Giggles:: and Jerry JS: ::Still whispering:: Ok... What? DGHL: :; in a normal voice, very seriously:: Who says that pain has to hurt.. :;Giggles evilly as she starts to walk away and starts to sing:: It's the same old torture every day.. every day.. :;Stops and turns back to the camera:;
Scary Mary.. ~excite me~...::giggles:: ... Come up with something new.. ::walks away :: See you in TJ.. JS: :: in a normal voice:: Sometimes.... ::shaking his head in disbelief:: Sometimes even I don't know about her...
