Looking for an E-fed

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there. I'm the hanlder of Johnny Ziro and many others, but Johnny's my main guy. He's the first e-wrestler I ever created. Anyway, I was in an e-fed that just shut down, so now I'm looking for another. I'm not too picky about an e-fed, as long as it's ran decently. The only think I'm somewhat picky about is how roleplays are done. I'd prefer an e-fed that does roleplays on a message board of some type. I'm not to crazy about e-mail list type of roleplaying.
Here's a little OOC info on myself as a handler. I've been e-wrestling for about 5 years. I've been in many different e-feds. I can't even remember them all. And I stuck with each and everyone of them until they shut down, except for in a few exteme cases.
Some of the titles I've had as Johnny was HDW world champion, PPW world champion, WWEF world champion, WWEF IC champion, WWEF tag team champion, WWA hardcore champion, and a few others of feds that I can't remember the names of right now.
Now here's some info on Johnny. Height: 7'0" Weight: 320 lbs. From: Nashville, TN Race: Caucasion Hair: Black (Long) Attire: Black Jeans, White T-shirt (Sleeves Torn Off), Black Boots, Black
Finger Gloves Facial Hair: Black Gotee Body: Muscular Set up Move: Chokeslam Finisher: Powerbomb Affiliation: Face Theme Music: Hard Workin' Man by Brooks & Dunn Gimmick: Johnny doesn't have a real gimmicky gimmick. He's just a simple man from the south. The most gimmicky thing about him is that he drives a semi-truck and trailor like most people would drive a car. If he needs some milk, he jumps in his semi-truck and goes to the store.
Fighting Style: Powerhouse - He relys on power moves. Here's a sample RP I use for Johnny. For this sample roleplay, I'm pretending that Johnny Ziro has a match against
Stone Cold Steve Austin on an upcoming episode WWF Raw.
(The scene opens. Johnny Ziro is leaning against the front of his Mack truck, with his arms crossed. He's wearing Blue jeans, brown boots, a white T-shirt, a red and black flannel button-up shirt over it, sunglasses, and a black baseball cap that says "Mack" on the front in silver letters. The sleeves on the flannel shirt are rolled up to just above his elbows. Johnny is looking directly into the camera.)
Stone Cold Steve Austin, this Monday on Raw it's me and you. I know you think you're the toughest SOB in the world, but I'm pretty tough myself. Now I'm not gonna stand here and say how I'm gonna run you down. I know you're gonna give me a good fight, but you're not gonna run me down either. I'm anything but a pushover. Now you can run around and flip people off, cuss me out, and drink your little beers, but you're not gonna intimidate me.
(Johnny then stands up straight.) I don't care how many times you've held the championship, or how many matches you've been in. This Monday night, I'm gonna give you a fight the likes of which you've never seen. And after all's said and done, you're gonna be laying in the middle of that ring at my feet...a beaten man.
(Johnny starts to walk around the front, and to the driver's side of his truck. The camera follows. He then props his right foot on the bottom step. ) Stone Cold, you've done a lot in the WWF, and I've got a lot of respect for you. But when we step into that ring, respect goes out the window. When I climb into a wrestling ring, I climb in to win. For me, it's all business in that ring. I don't care if you're my best friend or my worst enemy.
(Johnny reaches up and opens the driver's side door, then climbs into the cab and shuts the door. Johnny looks down at the camera from his seat.)
This Monday Austin, me and you will go into that ring alive and well, but when you leave, you'll be alive, but you won't be well. I know you'll give me a good fight, but it won't be good enough. I'll see ya Monday, Steve.
(Johnny starts the truck, puts it into gear, and drives off. The scene fades to black.)
