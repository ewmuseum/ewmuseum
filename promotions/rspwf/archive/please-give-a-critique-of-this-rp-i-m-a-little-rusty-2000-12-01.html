Sorry to bother you folks but I've been out of the e-wrestling game for a while and I was wandering if you could please give any constructive thoughts to the RP below. The RP is taken from one that
I used in a fed some time ago so its not really a intro ramble or anything. Anyway, if you can help then thanks.
--- (Scene opens to the unfortunate sight of what looks to be a human male, clothed in a dingy stain-covered rain coat, sitting against the curb of a dark and desolate street, absentmindedly sniffing his right index finger. He grins to himself, either blissfully content with his circumstances in life or with the fact he's currently nursing the nib of a large bottle of 'Old Jamsey's Ball Boomer Whiskey' in his left hand. He farts gently, allowing the sickly sweat aroma of half-digested egg curry to consume his immediate vicinity.)
Drunken Man: Ahh..... *hic*... the sssssweeet smell of successful..... hmmphm.... (singing) 'Yin Tong, Yin Tong, Yin Tong, Yin Tong, Yin Tong
Diddley Aye Po....' (Like an enraged antelope picking up the scent of a female in heat, the camera pans quickly until finally settling on the rather impressive figure of a tall, well built man, dressed in a smartly tailored silver suit and walking directly towards the camera's position. The man is impressive, everything about him suggests so, even down to the gait of his walk, which could resemble Moses leading the Israelites if you closed your eyes and stretched your imagination.
Second Man: Jesus Christ Pierre! Where the HELL have you been!?! (The first man, who is now obviously Pierre, drools slightly before raising unsure to his feet.)
Pierre: Piss off Alexander! I'm in a creative mood *hic*!!! Alexander: You mean your drunk again?! Pierre: Me?! "Drunk" you say?!?! NEVER!!! I am mealy perusing the local taverns and boozerys in this town...*hic*.... 'Yin Tong....
Alexander: Pierre!! How many times do I have to say, you are not to drink on the night before negotiations... It looks damn unprofessional!!!
Pierre: Unprofessional?!?! Your call having a small *hic* drink before bedtime "unprofessional"!?! Well if that's unprofessional that we might as well all live in Nazi China and one more thing ....
Alexander: Pierre, shut up... NOW!!!! Pierre: .... okay Alexander: I'm starting to get sick of this kind of behaviour Pierre, what kind of manager does this before a signing?
Pierre: .... one that's physical addicted to alcohol? Alexander: No... well, yeah, actually.. (Pierre grins and promptly stumbles over a couple of empty bins.) Alexander: Who would have thought that I, Alexander Strakoi, the man with so much talent its practically oozing out of his ears would have a manager who's only pleasure in life is to drink himself into quite oblivion? I mean you don't see other wrestlers putting up with this kind of nonsense. You don't see 'The Local Boy' Max Speilman going for an interview only to say that his manager couldn't make it because he was caught trying to interfere with a carrot by the police... you don't see 'The Bad Person' Roger McAffity's manager propping up a
Parisian bar singing 'Oooh Miss Migans, lets have another pie for the road' now do you? No! But what you DO see is MY manager getting so drunk he actually decided to start a 'Save the Aubergine Society' in the GCWA before promptly assaulting the president because 'he was giving you the evil eye'.
Pierre: (From under a mouldy piece of lettuce) He was in league with
Satan! Alexander: Oh Piss off Pierre! I mean, consider my awesome wrestling prowess. I have defeated countless opponents, made grown men gasp and marvel whilst I tear them to pieces and have made an entire crown of thousands unilaterally cum out of shear visual excitement, yet when it comes to my manager I am the laughing stock of the entire business.
Pierre: Well.... laughing stock until you find out who they are and then you politely rip out the prostate glands and use them for pate, eh Alexander?
(Alexander frowns slightly.... and then smiles, as if reminiscing of some past glory.) Alexander: Indeed Pierre. Pierre: Indeed. Alexander: ... Pierre: ... Alexander: ... (Both men burst out in hysterics, stray words of 'cripple' and 'through a straw' can be heard quite clearly through the camera's microphone.) Alexander: Oh come on Pierre! Lets get you home. Pierre: Leave me here Alexander, I'm think *hic* I'm making a new friend. (A squeak can be heard emanating from one of the bins.) Alexander: Suit yourself. (Alexander adjusts himself through his trousers before walking off and leaving Pierre to pass out quietly and in his own time. With the scene fades.... then flashes up again... then fades again....
