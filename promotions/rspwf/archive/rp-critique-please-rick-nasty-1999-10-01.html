[RP Critique please] Rick Nasty

1 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[5, 4, 3,.......] [Are you ready?] [2...] [It's your last chance to turn back..........] [Before you enter the world of..........] [1.......] [The Nasty One.........] [Scene begins..........a loud sizzling sound emitting from a hot grill can be heard as a camera slowly pans around the front counter of an airport in a
yet-undetermined city.......] [Man] "Make sure you get a good shot of the place, it's EIW, it's the big time, and you know how those higher-ups love those crazy camera angles."
[A waitress wearing a red "Jack's Bar and Grill" outfit walks into the shot.......] [Waitress] "You two ready to order or should I come back?" [Off camera...] [Man] "Yeah, just get me a Pepsi, no ice." [Waitress] "And your friend?" [Man] "The same." [Waitress] "Alrighty." [Man] "Turn that thing over here, time I get a little exposure." [The camera moves over to the man previously speaking.........] [Man] "Ah, that's better. Hello world, ESPN viewers, etc., allow me to introduce myself. I have many names........to the waitress over there, I'm 'the customer', to the guy sitting two seats away, I'm 'better-looking', to the girl
I met back in the summer of 94, I'm 'Big Dick Daddy', and to you viewers watching at home, and the rest of the 'EIW superstars', I am simply known as...........Rick Nasty"
[Nasty pauses.......] [Nasty] "Are you done clapping yet?" [Nasty] "I guess I could sit here and talk about how hard of a life I had or how many times daddy beat my ass but I won't do that. Why? Cause I ain't here to put the viewers to sleep, I'm here......to put on a show. Forget the past, forget how great I used to be, how many belts I won, or how many accolades I received. Let's talk about the future, and how I'm gonna take the EIW by storm and bring this company to the top."
[Waitress returns with some drinks.......] [Nasty] "I used to watch EIW back when it was 'popular'. And I'll tell you something, it wasn't all that good, something was missing, a certain element, an element of greatness. I agree, EIW had some talent. They were good, but they weren't 'spectacular'. Actually, some guys just plain sucked. Guys like
'Powerhouse' and Matt Leach, they were bringing this company to the crapper. When I signed with EIW, I asked the Prez why he kept these guys around so long. You know what he told me? He said they 'put in the effort'. Effort my ass. I've seen more effort from an old man beating off to a picture of his granddaughter.
F[bleep] those guys, I invite any former EIW superstar to come back and face
Rick Nasty. They won't stand a chance, and that's a damn fact!"
[Nasty] "I'm told that this organization has such a great past and how many wrestlers went on to become legends of this sport. I laugh at the slightest mention of that statement, cause we all know that it's a lie and the only people that believe that are the ones who made up the statement to begin with.
Wrestlers who become legends of this sport become legends on their own, regardless of what organization they work for. EIW may have some memorable moments, but those moments mean absolutely nothing to Rick Nasty. Rick Nasty is here to make memories, not re-live them."
[Nasty takes a drink of his Pepsi, and continues........] [Nasty] "Speaking of memories, EIW's got some newbie named 'Tyler Rotten' who comes on network television, and cry's 'child abuse'. Did ya see it? It sounded like something straight from a made-for-TV movie. This guy is seriously f[bleep]-up in the head. 'Daddy spanked me', 'Daddy beat me', 'Daddy chased me with a broomstick'. Cry, cry, cry, weep, weep, weep. I'd tell ya I feel sorry for you and all that crap but I won't. Cause Rick Nasty doesn't lie. Every man lives the way he chooses to live. The way I see it, you let your dad beat you, you let your dad treat you unfairly. I don't see how any of your 'I hate daddy'
stories relate to me, unless you want me to beat you like your daddy did, but that's kinda f[bleep]-up altogether."
[Nasty] "So what do you want me to do about your little problem? Do you want me to build you a time machine and send you back into the past so you can watch yourself get beat-up? Or would you rather have me show up on Monday and beat your ass like Daddy did? Either way, you're in for an ass-whuppin'. Maybe after that, I'll buy dead-daddy a drink and hang out at his gravestone. Then him and
I can talk about how we both beat your ass and he can give me tips on some different forms of ass-kicking that I forgot to show you the night before.
[shrugs his shoulders..] Eh, who knows, it could be fun." [Nasty] "All jokes aside, I'm sure you have a lot of problems and you've had a tuff life. Hey, what the helll can I do? And why the hell would I care? Look on the bright side, the man beating your ass on Monday won't be big-drunk daddy.
And you'll actually get paid to get beat-down, I won't even make you buy me cigarettes and beer. Now that I think of it, hell, I'm a pretty nice guy.
You've been treated like sh*t all your life, now you're getting paid for it, what else could you ask for? Life's never been better for you. You're making me feel better about kicking your ass now."
[Rick Nasty sets down a couple bucks for the drink and gets up.........] [Nasty] "Well, my planes here. Tyler Rotten, on Monday night, you're not even gonna remember how bad Daddy used to treat you, stepping in the ring with Rick
Nasty will make your life ten-times worst then it already is. You may have been daddy's little bitch in the past, but on Monday, you belong to Rick Nasty.
Before I go, just remember one thing, it ain't 'absolutely Rotten'.......It's downright Nasty!
[FTB.....] A sample of EIW talent.
