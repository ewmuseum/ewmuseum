Bone Crusher Industries Intro Interview in EIWF

26 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::The camera comes on and focuses in on two wrestlers wearing black trench coats along with a woman in a ring inside an empty arena. The trench coats have hoods on them and they are pulled up so that the shadows from the light darkens their faces.:::
Lina: Well, we're here. The EIWF, hugh? Yea, whatever. Anyways, down to business. My name is Lina and I am here to introduce to you, the greatest tag team in the history of e-wrestling, Bone Crusher Industries!!!!! First of all, we have hailing from Santa Clara, California, weighing in at 219 lbs., the master of the Super DDT, "The Deadly Daredevil" Brute!!!
:::The smaller of the two wrestlers steps up and takes off his hood, unveiling his face to the camera. His face is covered with blue, black, and red paint.
He raises his arms and gives a deadly stare into the camera as he grabs the microphone.::: Brute: Thats right!!! We are the former WIWC tag team champions of the world, where we had to retire as the champions because there was no competition that could stand up for against us!!!! So now, we came here, looking for some new blood to sacrifice before the wrestling gods!!! I got my eyes set on one thing right now, and that is the Cruiserweight Title!! So Doink!!! You no talent clown!! Keep your eyes out for me. I'm comin to get what was mine all along!!! That piece of tin you have wrapped around your bloated gut!!!!
:::Brute hands over the microphone to Lina as he sticks his tongue out at the camera.::: Lina: Yep thats right Brute, your on your way straight to the top of the cruiserweight division. And now for the other member and founder of Bone
Crusher Industries, standing 7'1", weighing in at 355 lbs., from San Jose,
California, the former IWA Heavyweight Champion of the World!!!! Bone
Crusher!!! :::Bone Crusher just stares at the camera with his hood up. You can only see the whites of his eyes as he stares straight into the camera with his arms
crossed.::: Bone Crusher: No one is safe from my wrath. The power of the Industrial Revolution has begun here in the EIWF. Carnage is in the future of every athlete that gets in my way. You will feel the force of an Industrial Drop!!!!
So I am sending out the word to the entire EIWF. You are either with us, or you are going down for the 1, 2, 3!!!! And as you are stuck laying down in some two-bit hospital somewhere, whenever you look to see what is happening here in the EIWF, you will be seeing the one who sent you to the hospital with his hand raised in victory!!! First title I have in my sights is the Internet
Title. I dont care if I have to go through 1 or 50 other wrestlers to get that belt. It's goin around my waist and there is nothing you can do about it!!!!!!
The Revolution is upon you!!!!! :::Bone Crusher gives the thumbs down to the camera as he hands the microphone over to Lina:::
Lina: And as a warm up, we are putting out an open contract to any two wrestlers who think they have what it takes to step into the ring with Bone
Crusher Industries this Friday on Friday Fear!!! So until then, hasta la vista!!!! Oh yea!! I'll leave you with something to think about. Where will the revolution show up at next??? Hahahaha!!!!
:::Bone Crusher takes the camera and smashes it until the picture fuzzes out. The a sign shows up on the screen infront of the fuzz. "BCI: The revolution is upon us.":::
