You know, I have done too much talking but I haven't done any action. Since, Mark doesn't have the 'balls' to accept my proposal, for his own reasons. And I didn't hear any of them, so let it be. I guess I will have to show my works in front of everybody on RSPWF, for those who care or not.
I would like anybody to compare my roleplay from WWO to this roleplay and you tell me what is or isn't different.
3/10/99 ----------- [Without a notice, the arena lights fade out as a green dollar sign spins around, counter clockwise, in the center of the ring. Explosion fires off from each ring post as the music beats into the speaker system but hardly anybody can hear it. Two well-known wrestlers walk out from the back and head toward the ring as the chants of 'Mason',
'Copycat' and 'RatsPack' from the crowd. The lights in the arena brighten up as both of them enter the ring and do their usual pose to the crowd. Before the ring announcer had the chance to hand the mic to
Mason, Mason just walked up to him and punch him out cold as he was about to give the mic to him. The crowd erupt into loud cheers when
Mason did that.]
[Mason] It look like somebody is going home and telling someone that
Mason is the coolest guy around. Mason is the best of the best, Mason is king of HARDCORE. Speaking of HARDCORE, IWF had once again disrespected Copycat and myself. As you can see, I am the REAL
Hardcore Champion. Going into a tournament that I could easily win isn't going to prove a point, so I will let the wannabe champion have his day as a champion but at the next LIVE show. That wannabe champion will have to face the real champion, which is.. the guy who can make your mom become a man and fuck your sister who was then.. a virgin.
And the worst of all, the ages doesn't matter. Also this guy will make you wish that you went 'back in' when you came out from that old bag's pussy. Who's this guy, this guy is Mmmmaaaassssoooonnnnn...!
[Mason tossed the mic to Copycat as he taunts the crowd.] [Copycat] Wow?! You're better than that guy who always asking if we're ready to rumble? What an old cunt?! Speaking of cunt.....
[The crowd's response get louder as Copycat pause.] [Copycat] Eternity, that bewitched-old-bag-hag-toothpick-bitch.. Destiny, isn't going to be beautiful to you, Mase or myself. How so? Just watch and learns... [Mason] Now.. we see that the General Hospital staff of the year,
Gambit have made an Oscar performance for making a promise that is too shocking to us. Like.. it wasn't planned ahead of time? OOPS?! So..
Gambit, I can promise your worthless ass that you won't be ruling anything not even your own dick that you happen to swallow a lot of time. You are like every other members of G-H, all of you are stepping into IWF without bats or balls.
[Copycat] You know, Mase. With the RatsPack invited into the plan, we will be making history before Eternity's next 'brilliant' idea. The guy is having problems getting lost, since he is still finding it. As for the rest of IWF, don't try to be like us or we will have to drop our pants! Get out our corn! And we will make you eat away.
[Mason and Copycat exist the ring as the music beats into the speakers that nobody can hear it.]
