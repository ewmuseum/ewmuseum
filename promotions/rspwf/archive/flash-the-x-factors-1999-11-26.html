{FLASH} The X-Factors

26 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I usually dont do this but this is my new team and my opponent posted his so here.... [[ The Squared Circle camera begins to roll. The scene is a very slummed up area in downtown Seattle Washington. The camera man is standing on the sidewalk right across the street from an almost all run down Catholic church. In front of the church, there is a priest and five nuns all standing behind a table with all different types of Thanksgiving food on it. There is a huge turkey, a large pot full of dressing, and various other items. Aside from the table is a long line of people. Most of the people in the line are wearing almost nothing that can warm them up in the frostbite like conditions. Some of the people are standing there with there whole family. Each person is carrying a plate and an eating utensil. The people are walking in front of the table as the priest and the nuns put various food items on there plates. The line continues to move along past the table but none of the people seem to be leaving. They all walk to the end of the line and just sit there on the steps of the church. All of the sudden the doors of the church open up and out walk none other than Xavior
Hamilton and Xavior Packson, the members of The X-Factors. Packson, the white male, around 6'6, is wearing a pair of Old Navy blue jeans and a large black
Seattle Washington Seahawks coat that is zipped up. Hamilton, the black male, standing in at around 6'10, is wearing a pair of black jeans and a Seattle
Supersonics coat that is zipped up, he also has on a pair of dark Rayban sunglasses. Both of the men walk right by the people standing in line and shake there head as they cross the street towards the camera man. Hamilton makes it across the street first and walks right in front of the camera man and begins speaking.]]
[[Xavior Hamilton]] You know, it kinda makes me sick. Why is it that a holiday brings out everyone who thinks they want a handout? Why is it that only few times a year you see these people wake up nice and early and want to stand out here for a bit of free stuff. Why cant these people wake up nice and early everyday and go to a job? And for the one's that for some crazy reason dont have a job, why cant they go and stand in a line somwhere and fill out a couple applications.
[[Xavior Packson]] Why in the hell would they want to do that. I mean welfare is fine for all of them. Public housing that we pay for is fine for all of them. I mean come on, they couldnt sleep in till eleven o'clock everyday and wake up just in time to watch there hero Jerry Springer. I am surprised they are missing it today. I guess they have an skanky image to up hold. They dont wanna miss there free handout.
[[Xavior Hamilton]] That right there is whats wrong with the world today. Everyone wants something for free, no one wants to work for anything anymore. It kinda reminds me of the way the tag team scene looks in The Squared Circle. We have a bunch of sorry tag teams that dont do a thing except get paid each week to fill up a roster spot. The teams like The Deadly Rebels, The Wildcards, and The Code. All of these teams do not a thing except take up space in a division that we run. And frankly we are already sick and tired of it. We are the kings, and the domain rulers and these teams do not a thing except make us look bad.
[[Xavior Packson]] Then we go from the teams that dont do a thing, right to the teams that try, but are crappy. I mean come on. I would rather you get in there and make it a squash match, I mean at least then no one would get hurt. But no, we have the pure, greenback rookies who want to plain and simply jump right on into the spotlight after doing absolutly nothing. They want to be number one but they also want nothing but a hand out of a title instead of working for it. They want someone to lay down for them so they can have an easy run in the small tournament to take The Squared Circle Tag Team titles. Well, let me tell you this right now to start it off right, Precious Moments, if you think we are going to be the team that lays down for you then you have got to be out of your damn minds.
[[Xavior Hamilton]] Hey Pack-Man, I dont know if I would be talking about laying down to those
Precious Moments, they might try and take it in more way than just an easy victory.
[[Xavior Packson]] Precious Moments, I am gonna give this one little bit of advice real quick since I guess no one told you anything about the birds and the bee's, but I promise you that hole should be marked EXIT ONLY! You wanna take your little unfrequent lifestyle and bring it into a wrestling ring with me on the other side of ring. Not a chance. You can call it an alternate lifestyle all you want, but you keep it up in a division that we run and I promise they will be calling a coma an alternate lifestyle because that is damn sure where we will leave you if you do anything to tarnish what we are making here in The Squared
Circle.
[[All of the sudden across the street a small fight breaks out between two of the people that are standing in line. The preist and the nuns all rush around and break up the incident.]]
[[Xavior Hamilton]] Look at that crap. over there fighting like rats for the last piece of dumpster garbage. Precious Moments, I want you to watch them really closely so you can master the art of fighting for a meal. Watch them, watch they way they dress, because after we beat you in the first round of this tag team title tournament, that is going to be the way you are living. You will be sitting in a nice recliner chair eating mayonaisse sandwiches asking your brother if he wants to take a nap with you. Sunday night, you will be in for a treat that you will never forget, it will be more a treat for us than it will be for you two.
You think your little push over match or so that you have had so far was something, then oh boy's just wait till the match.
[[Xavior Packson]] You know, we have only been in The Squared Circle for about two weeks now and look what has happened. The tag team scene has now caught a spark that has never been seen before. And the tag team titles have now been brought into the picture. I guess that is because Precious Moments have been doing just so damn great isnt it? I think not, these titles are now on the scene because now there is a tag team here that is damn good enough to carry them now. You see, we arent here to make friends with the rest of the teams, or go out and have chit chats with anyone, no! We are here to dominate, anihalate, and to take the tag team titles and wear them around our waist's.
[[Xavior Hamilton]] Let me ask you two fairies a little question, did you see what happened on the last Squared Circle card? Did you see us totally demolish those Deadly Rebels?
Did you see the way we made short work, if you really wanna call it work of them? I really, really hope you did. Because that might make you think twice before you rely on your ways, instead of your so called talent. Or lack there of. I wanna know little boys, how does it feel knowing that all you are is a small stepping block for the greatest tag team that this organization will ever know. How does it feel knowing that in a torunament that most people have you picked to win, you will not only let them down, but you will be brought down yourselves in the mere first round? How does it feel? Just let me know seriously, how does it feel??
[[Xavior Packson]] Ok looks like someone is starting to go over the deep end. Have you been drinking again? You have havent you. Come on, before church. Nothing amazes me about you anymore. But anyways, as much as we have dissed on these Precious
Moments, we cant leave the other two tag teams in this tournament out of the things. We know that in the main event on Saturday we have to fight someone.
And we know that is is either going to be The Wild Cards, or Shwag. My money would be on Shwag, that is if I was a betting man, which I am not though. But I dont say Shwag because I think they are good, hell no, that is far from it. I think they are better than The Wild Cards, and thats really not saying much though is it? I didnt think so. Now Shwag, what in the hell is, Shwag. Is it supposed to be some type of acronym? Or is it something that you thought of all by yourselves with you superior I.Q.'s? Either way, and whatever it may be, it really doesnt matter because the only thing people will be remembering about you two, is the fact that you made it even easier for The X-Factors to win The
Squared Circle Tag team titles. But if by some chance it is the Wild Cards who pull off the surprisinly victory in the not surprising bore fest you two teams will call a match, then the same can be said about you boys as well. You will be nothing but two men standing in our way to what we are destined for.
[[Xavior Hamilton]] Play time's over and the little kiddie games are done. The deciding factor is here and we are known as The X-Factors.
[[The camera begins to fade out to black as Packson and Hamilton both walk down the street away from the camera man.]]
