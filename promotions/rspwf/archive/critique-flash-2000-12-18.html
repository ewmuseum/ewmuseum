[Critique Flash]

18 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Critique, flame, etc;... Any feedback is welcomed and appreciated. - Mike E. ======================================================= [The visibility gradually increases within a dimly lit Locker Room. Hanging on the back wall, held up by a single tac is a National Extreme Wrestling Poster for Hell for the Holidays IV! Standing face to face in the background of the Poster is Andrew "Flash" Tucker, eyeing long time partner "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian. In the forefront, "Carolina Crusher"
Jack Cody, slightly elevated above the bodies of "Rebel" Ricky Price and the
N.E.W. World Champion, "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade.
A long drawn out creaking sound picks up as a Door slowly opens. As it shuts, a small shadow is cast upon the back wall. Stepping into the imagery, back facing the Camera is a man, shaved head - black sleeveless T-shirt and torn, grungy blue jean shorts. The Man shakes his head, eyeing the poster. This all, of course, ends as the Man angrily rips down the Poster and tears it into pieces. Tossing the handful of shreds to the floor, his Voice carries over the picture.]
V/O: Where do I fit in? [Spinning around, James Morgan's dark aqua eyes stare deeply into the Camera
Lens. Slowly sitting down, Morgan's depressing stare is hard to avoid sympathizing with. As he gazes onward, deep into nothingness - his mouth moves, uttering two powerful, to him, words.]
JM : I don't. [Laying back on the tan Steel Chair, with rust spots randomly showing through where chipped paint has fallen from. Cocking his head to the left, Morgan rest both hands on his thighes and emits a long, deep sigh.]
JM : It's a Synthesis Reaction in the finest form. Establishment and Fame breaks down to instant success. How pitiful. I am not Andrew Tucker .. I am not Michael Sebastian .. I am not Jack Cody .. I am not Ricky Price .. or
Christopher Courtade. I am merely James Morgan. A Man who has sacrificed, swallowed, and stepped aside for others all his life. Do you think in fifth
Grade when the Christmas Play was cast, James Morgan was picked? No.. and the pathetic fools you are did not even bother to find out why. It was because I wouldn't conform.. I wouldn't conform to their stupid Script. I wouldn't be a mere Puppet, dancing by their strings. I wouldn't succumb to the Social
Pressure because when you live the life I lived, .. Social Pressure is a Diamond compared to the dirty Pebbles of life. [Morgan sighes as he leans forward, his head swaying to the right, eventually
.. hanging over his right shoulder. Both eyes are surrounding by bags of severe fatigue, as they pierce into the lens.]
JM : One thing that remains constant is the sympathy. People dare tell me to look ahead, and to be focused. But what do they know? Have they experienced my life? Were they subject to two Parents who were more concerned with Dinner and the Television Listing then the welfare of their only son? I wasn't a son.. I wasn't born from my Mothers womb. I spurred from ashes, at the end of her Cigarette Butt as the example of a cancer, infesting her body -- mind -- and soul. I reveled in that. She deserved it .. the self-centered whore. But my Father .. he was a different story. He was much like Marco
Carlson. A Man who rode past accomplishments into new places. Thinking he was worth anything. But Carlson, you - like my Father are not important. It is a harsh realization but don't tell me anything about harsh conditions. You know nothing about strife. You're merely a Pawn in the universally believed in God, who so righteously places people wherever he wishes.
How pathetic. I will forever fail to recogonize a higher power. Never will my actions be thought out for me. My future is as certain as the World itself. Controlled by mindless Souls, held captured by their own ego. How fitting .. Don't you see the comparison, N.E.W.? Your Committee is nothing more then a Panel of Men who wish to force their views upon you. You're not fighting for yourselves.
You're fightin for their wallets. And how perfect is it all? The more you all try to convince yourselves the N.E.W. is a venture where you're an individual, the more realistic it is that this is an Establishment for one reason - to satisfy egos.
[Standing up, Morgan folds the chair up and grabs it with his right hand. As he faces the Camera, Morgan slides the chair under his right arm and continues.]
JM : Marco Carlson - if you will persist in believing Christian Leadfoot is worried about your future, then not only do I despise you but I pity you. A
Man, such as yourself, so easily drawn into a Web of Deceit, brought on by a man with one persuit. To lay back in his Home and watch the Bank Account become larger and larger. All the while, you risk your body, your future, and your life. Only to see five years down the road how insignificant you truly were envisioned and what a waste the past was. But you'll never agree with me.
You have too much pride to agree with me. Because I am not widely accepted. Because if you do agree with me, your peers will no longer hold you in the same light. Well then, Carlson .. come Hell for the Holidays, IV, .. I plan on stripping you of your friend .. forcing my acception upon you, .. and destroying your existance, so the peer pressure you so easily caved into will no longer remain as you will simply Fall .. Into ..
_ A DEEP DEPRESSION! _ [Fade to black.]
