"Fabulous" Freddy Floyd (Looking For Another Fed)

25 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all, My name is Mike, and handle the e-wrestler 'Fabulous' Freddy Floyd. As most of you know this isn't my first time posting on the board. This time I post becasue I am looking for one more federation to add to my ranks. I currently compete in two of the top federations in e-wrestling. The ICW, and The GSWP. So please send any Info Packets, as well as Web Adresses to me.
I am looking to be an IMPACT PLAYER, which means I don't want to just take up roster space. So if your looking for your next superstar to push to the World Title than let it be me. If you just want my name on your roster than please, I'm looking for your fed.
I have posted a sample that I did for the GSWP below. Thanks very much. I have many more samples if you need to see more of my work. Thanks ...
[ Scene opens with a roadside shot of a black hummer heading down route I-87. The cameras shot fades to one inside of the hummer, where the driver is seen to be
'Fabulous' Freddy Floyd. Floyd is wearing a pair of khaki pants, a red polo brand polo shirt, and a pair of sandels ]
[ Freddy reaches down to the radio volumn where he turns it way down. Floyd looks at the camera for a second, and then returns his attention to the road before he begins to speak ]
"Fabulous" FF: "Ladies and gentlmen of the Garden State Wrestling Promotion. Please open your eyes, shut your mouthes, and open your damn ears, becasue you are getting the chance to be with The Fabulous One, Freddy Floyd, live and in person as he travels to the GSWP's next destination in what I call a "Never Ending Journey" to greatness.
And what better place that to continue the "Never Ending Journey" than my hometown of Albany New York! Thats right baby, "The Fabulous One" is coming home, and if you ask me its about time. I have proven myself time and time again in the great city of Albany, and I'll go ahead and do it one more time."
[ Freddy rolls up the window as he aproches a red light. He then reaches down to the cup holder and takes a sip of his water before continuing to talk to the camera.
As he begins to speak the light turns green, and the Hummer begins to move ] "Fabulous" FF: "Now let us discuss this past weeks Impact. I mean what a night for 'The Fabulous" One. First, I went out there and absolutly destroyed that poor young boy named Sean
Copeland. Its a real shame, he tried so hard. And I almost, keyword being almost, felt a little bad when I was leaping off of the top rope and hitting my Finale.
I mean I don't want to put the poor kid on the shelf, but I did need to make an example out of him for the championship commitee, and from the looks of this weeks
Impact Line-up, it looks as if I have done just that." "In just one weeks time I step into the ring with the man that I dispise most on the Garden State Wrestling
Promotion, World Telivision Champion Chris Winters. Now Chris, I have been asked my a lot of people why I didn't just go ahead and bust your head open with that steel chair. But the answer is very simple. Becasue I can. Becasue I know its giving you nightmares. I hold your destiny in my hand. That match ups outcome was up to me, if I wanted you to lose, you would have. But I didn't Chris, I wanted you to be at your best, becasue it is going to be I that that takes the title from you, it is going to be I that puts you on the shelf, and best of all, it is going to be I that gives you the one last injury that puts you on the shelf for good."
"I do admit Chris, you have had a very impressive run as champion, and the question is asked week in and week out 'Can anyone stop Chris Winters?' Well Chris, your looking at the man who is going to to do it. Now I'm not going to do it just becasue I'm a mean and nasty guy, and I could care less about your being, I am doing it becasue its my destiny. I am bound for greatness.
Winning runs in my blood, I thrive on victory, and with each one I grow stronger and stronger. And the sweet taste is already in my mouth. I can feel you crumbling before me, the way that so many others have, and I can hear the announcement as they tell the whole world that
'Fabulous' Freddy Floyd has won the GSWP World Telivison Championship from Chris Winters, and that I put you on the shelf for good." [ Freddy pulls up in front of the Desmond Hotel, where he will spend the night before the making the rest of the trip home to Albany ]
[ The camera man exits the Hummer, as hotel attendants flood out of the doors and helpd carry bags inside of the building. Freddy gives a stern look to a young boy and hands him the keys, instructing him to be oh so careful, for his life depends on it. ]
[ Freddy and the camera man make there way inside, and step onto the elavator. The Camera man once again turns on the camera as the elavator door opens up and into
Freddy's private suite ]
"Fabulous" FF: "Now Chris, I don't want you to go ahead and get the wrong impression about Freddy Floyd. I am in no way under-estimating you. I know what you have done, who you have beaten, and its not that you haven been a good champion, Its just that your time as the Telivision champion is coming to a close. Becasue with each win
I get stronger, and more pumped up. But with each of yours you just add on another injury, and another step closer to being put on the shelf for good."
"So Chris, why don't you just go ahead and do yourself the biggest possible favor that you can, and just give up right now. No one will think you are any less of a man for it. We can all see that your hurt. It would be wise for you to duck out, as to not permanetly injure yourself. And Chrissy, if you think somone like Shane
Alexander is sadistic, just wait till you get in there with Freddy Floyd. I have got more Sadisism in me, than
Shane Alexander does in his little finger. So for me, performaning an act such as breaking your arm in two, you know the one that you have injured week after week. Doing something like that comes with no emotional attachments for me. I feel bad for you Chris, I really do ....
[ Freddy looks away from the camera, and dramatically pretends to wipe a tear from his eye, before breaking
into a manical laugh ] [ The camera man takes one last closeup of Freddy Floyd's face before the screen fades to black ]
Mike, Handler of.... " F A B U L O U S " F R E D D Y F L O Y D I C W ( 4 - 1 ) [ Just recently returned ] G S W P ( 3 - 0 )
