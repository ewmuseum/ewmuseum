[Critique] Flash

10 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sup guys Two Flashes I wrote a while ago. Thought I'd get some feedback on them. Thanks [name withheld] ========================================================== " C O L D H E A R T E D " J A M E S M O R G A N [Slowly, the visibility gradually increases upon your Television Set. Large lights, noisy people, and the sound of Slot Machines ringing out are picked up.
The Las Vegas strip is in full swing tonight. Las Vegas itself is coming off it's Home Town Wrestling Represenative, The Las Vegas Pro Wrestling
Association's Canadian Conquest '00 and the mood was set with that Spectacle. As always, the City of Sin is showcasing it's beautiful women, risky games, and high profile attractions. In front of the Grand Hotel is a large Card Board
Cut Out of James Pillow and Sebastian Myerson, both with large grins on their faces, standing infront of the
LVPWA Arena. People walk by, taking notice to the Sign and just acknowledge it and walk away. Then a man, who is definetly out of place, walks up to the Card
Board. He is wearing cut off Jean Shorts, black boots and two Knee Braces, a solid Black Sleeveless T-Shirt, bald head, short chin goatee, three eye brow rings hanging above his left eye and an unaccepting expression. The man grabs the card board cut out and pushes it over, knocking it to the ground. He steps on the backing and a few onlookers question his motives. He slowly sinks down ontop of the cut out and drops his head, pointing to the cement.]
MAN: The Rules Are About To Change. Las Vegas, bear witness as the entire
Las Vegas ProWrestling Association turns ....... Cold Hearted.
[Fade to black.] -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= "C O L D H E A R T E D" J A M E S M O R G A N [The visibility gradually increases as a stentorian scream rips through your ears and rattles
your mind. A bright light shines down on the screen, blinding in fact.] VOICE: It.. It's a Boy! [A sigh of relief instantly emits from an exhausted mother. Off to the side is a joyful Father, holding his baby boy. Three Doctors scatter out of the room as the main Doctor conducting the Labor stands bed side, congratulating the new mother. The Father hands the baby to the mother and they both smile.]
Father: I hope my little boy grows up to me just like me. Mother: Me too. He can become a wonderful Dad just like his. [As the new Parents rejoice in the birth of their new born son, the image swirls into a black screen. Slowly, in white text a sentence materializes.]
Labels - Titles - Expectations - Censorship: What's This Life For? [The imagery fades into a simple black back drop with a white dim light shining overhead. An unknown man rests on the floor, his left knee raised up and his arm hanging upon his knee. The man is attired in torn Jean Shorts, black boots and kneepads. His upper portion is clothed in an all black T-shirt with "Save
Me? You Can't Even Save Yourself." His head rests against the back wall.
Three metallic eye brow rings hang above his eye as he runs his hair over his nearly shaven head. As he emits a long, drawn out sigh, he speaks.]
Man: Seconds after our arrival, or shall I say exit of our mothers womb we gain a label. All of those around us create these images and expectations they set for us. They want us to live a life, that they vicariously live through.
In my mind life is a waste of time and attempting to reach others standards is a pathetic use of a life. As you strive to reach goals you did not even place, the shores of Salvation slowly drift away as they see no right in setting you free. Expend your energy to maintain an accolade you do not even cherish.
Please those around you and live in misery forever. I understand you might question my words coming from an unknown source, just see past my name and look into my words. Divulge yourself in my concepts and to end your anguish and pain, step across the line of humiliation and despair and gain freedom.
Freedom from the Society that causes us to question our Occupation, Homes,
Appearence, and Life Style. I suppose you gain credibility in living for something, rather then falling for anything. My words do not exclude any of you. Even James Pillow, Sebastion Myerson and the rest of the Las Vegas Pro
Wrestling Association Suits. You all aim to fit in and be accepted. Yet I am always asked that. Do I take on this "role," of an outcast to be accepted as a gimmick? I do not stress my style of living for it to become a main stream acception. Love me, hate me, torture me, but you must.. hear me. I will not be silenced to receive a pleasentry or a notion of gratitude.
I refuse to be a shallow sell-out like the rest of you. Maybe that is the sieve in which my Cold Hearted ways stem from?
[The man slightly sighs as a hateful and depressing expression covers his face. With each passing second, you yearn for me. You know what he is saying is an exact description of your life yet you want to hear more of his speech. He slowly slides his head to the left side, onto his shoulder and looks up at the
Camera.]
Man: You remain constant in listening to what I say. Is it because what I speak of is a form of diction that you all grasp? For the unintelligent minds,
I simply tell you to continue walking down an empty path, alienated from the rest of the world. Commend you, I do, for your choice to disregard them.
Whether they laugh at you, yell at you, or ridicule you, those that walk alone can live alone. The reflection I cast into the minds of the ones who see me is an empty set of words that are a misuse of time and precious breath.
With my time to step into the LVPWA, I see the walls of my past enlarging. No longer am I confined to the Indepent Labyrinth of hate and criticism. Each week, living pay-check to pay-check. The money isn't what drives me, it is the time when you all meet your own Waterloo and realize you are far from invincible. If I can achieve one goal it is to take the darkness that hangs above Las Vegas Pro and kindle a light that is so bright, it cannot be unseen or ignored. You all have
remained hollow beings like cruel winds on an empty street.. is this what you strive for? Is this the Life you think is gaining you infamy and fame? Your foolish ways are embedded upon you like a form of body art. Permanent, symbolic, and forever an open forum for judgement.
Perhaps this is how you choose to live, I am not one to judge. However, it is time for me to look at one man and to focus soley on him. Lord Corwin "H"
Havens.. little do I know about this man yet things remain constant. Heavy
Metal Monday you shall step into a place where you know little to nothing about. My research has begun as I embark upon a mission to slowly break you down. The erosion of your mysterious appearence will become my way to see beyond your hype. You, like I, am a man. Yet a distinct difference remains between us. It is a tangible difference I will not explain but allow you to see. Perhaps you can see it now or you will see it Monday, when it is far to late. Fate is a Mistress with mystic ways and she has decreed Lord Corwin falls into a Deep Depression, remaining safe and happy no more. So end your attempt to Save Me, When You Cannot Save
Yourself. Monday Night, you shall see the Epitaph was in your name, all along.
[Morgan slowly stands up and walks off as the Camera fades to black.]
