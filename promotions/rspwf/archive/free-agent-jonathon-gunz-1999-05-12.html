[Free Agent] Jonathon Gunz

12 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Looking for one booked, possibly closed fash fed for this young up and comer! *Never having been one to be accused of being "underprepared" anytime that he must step into the squared circle, "The Real Deal" takes his match preparation seriously. It's with that disclaimer that the screen fades in.*
*Quaint, perhaps not the word that Jonathon would use for it, but that's the word best used to describe the kitchen of the small Texas home of Jonathon
Gunz. Oak cabinets adorn the walls, some covered with press clippings from
Jonathon's adventures in the squared circle. Upon the refrigerator are several magnets containing slogans or other sayings, among them: "Jesus Is Coming, LOOK
BUSY!", "Go Away! I'm Playing With Myself", and the ever popular "What The
F[beep]K Are You Looking At?".*
*Situated in the center of the room is a small breakfast table. Covered in books, pens, and paper...it resembles the desk of any good student cramming for an exam. Seated in a chair, his back facing a bay window, is our hero. That
"Malcontented Miscreant", "The Shakespeare of the Squared Circle", "The
All-American Kid", "Captain Code Red", "The Master of Disaster", "The Cardinal of Chaos", "The Pope of Pulverization"....*
[The narrator pauses to take a breath] *"The Real Deal" Jonathon "The Machine" Gunz!* *Jonathon appears deep in thought as the sunlight pours in from the window over his shoulder. His jet black hair back in a ponytail to keep it from shielding his face...his cold, steel green eyes in deep concentration. His left hand planted firmly on his forehead as he is immersed in the book before him. To the right he has scribbled several notes on a piece of paper, he holds the pen poised over the paper as if prepared to attack it with more tidbits of knowledge.*
*Slender, long-legged, and having so many curves on her body she could be a circle, Blondy walks through the kitchen and to the cabinets. She pauses for just a second, and then does a double take as she notices Jonathon lost in the mounds of paper and books.*
Blondy: Studying, my dear? I don't think I've ever seen you in such a state. *Jonathon, remotely startled, finally raises his eyes from his task in front of him.* Gunz: Yeah.... *Brief, not a normal statement from the boastful young superstar. Blondy appears a bit puzzled.* Blondy: Studying what? *Without saying a word, Jonathon raises the book in front of him. The title reads, in large block letters across the front, 5 Simple Steps To Dealing With
The Mentally Handicapped.*
Blondy: [puzzled] What's that for?? Gunz: Work... Blondy: Since when did Cooper start passing out homework?? Gunz: Since he added something else to my job description. *Blondy contorts her face in a rather puzzled manner, Jonathon notices this and elaborates.* Gunz: Hokay...I'll explain. Ever since I came to LSW from the sewers of Sin City, I've been asked to do alot. They've asked me to be their "new-breed superstar", they've asked me to help bring the female fans back to the sport, they've asked me to be the boost ticket sales, draw main event crowds...and Cooper has even asked me for tips on spicing up his marriage. All these things I've done, no problems. When you've got as much God-given ability, sex-appeal, intelligence and charisma as I do...that kinda stuff happens if you just show up.
But...not this time. This time Cooper has asked for something that EVEN "The
Real Deal" can't perform with ease.
*Blondy lets out a sarcastic gasp. Jonathon, either ignoring her attempt at wit, or being too conceited to realize that's what it was, continues.* Gunz: That's right, he's given me two retards...and he expects me to teach them to wrestle.
*Jonathon shrugs as he leans back in the chair* Gunz: Even for someone of my superior mental faculties and my extensive amateur background, this is indeed a daunting task. I've performed miracles in the squared circle, I managed to carry "Ride My Ass" Ray Pierce through an entire match, but...this may be beyond the scope of "The Real Deal"'s talent.
*Blondy pretends to share Jonathon's feelings of despair with her body language...which Jonathon acknowledges.* Gunz: I know, I didn't think it was possible either. *Sigh* Blondy: So...what's with all the books?? Gunz: I had to have the study aids. It's not every day that one is forced to tutor people with the motor skills of a roll of toilet paper.
I really feel sorry for the guys. Blondy: [shocked] Jonathon!!! I never knew that you had an ounce of pity for those "less-fortunate" than you!!! That's so sweet!!!
Gunz: What the hell are you talking about? Blondy: Well, you said you feel sorry for them... Gunz: Yeah, but not because they're "less-fortunate" than me. If I went around feeling sorry for everyone out there that's "less-fortunate" than I, which encompasses 99.9% of the population, I wouldn't have time to do anything else with my days.
No, I feel sorry that Cooper thinks so little of these two troglodytes, Olsen and Pelican Boy, that he is actually going to stand by and watch as they get their asses handed to them. You'd think that Cooper'd would at least have some empathy for people who share his mental plight.
But, you never really know what to expect out of Cooper these days. Ever since he stole Croc and Cobra away from Desade and turned them into his "erotic toys", he's been a wild card at best.
*The ravishing young blonde supresses a small laugh, she's learned about the evils of feeding the ego of "The Real Deal".* Blondy: So...what do you get out of all this? Gunz: Other than a chance to beat two men to the point of invalidity? Blondy: Yeah, other than that... *Jonathon cracks his knuckles, and a grin grows across his face.* Gunz: The LSW Television Title...though, I think it'd be easier if they'd just hand it over to the "Double A-K". It'd save everyone pain and embarassment.
Blondy: Like who? Gunz: Like Olsen's mother. It's bad enough her son is a loser, I don't think that "The Real Deal" has to remind her of it in front of a television audience.
I may be several things, but never let it be said that "The Real Deal" isn't concerned about the families of his opponents.
*Blondy rolls her eyes in Jonathon's direction.* Gunz: Now all I have to do is to figure out how to communicate the word "bitch" to someone with a 1st grade vocabulary. *Jonathon thumbs through the book in front of him for a few pages, then gives up. Blondy walks back past the camera and out of the kitchen.* Gunz: Regardless... Coming Monday, March 1st, I'm going to school the "dueling retards" in a universal language. I'm sure that even their underdeveloped brains will be able to comprehend, "Ass-Kicking". It's a fact of life, it's inevitable, and it's written in the stars to happen...
Monday Massacre...Pelican Boy and Jerry Olsen, you will be bitches. *Jonathon lets a wry grin escape him..* Gunz: If you feel "The Real Deal". *Gunz winks and returns his head to the books, as the screen begins to fade out we hear him mumble under his breath.* Gunz: Now...how do you get someone who's under mental enslavement to understand
"perfection"...
*END*
