1 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera fades in on a sold out Madison Square Garden where the fans are waiting for the next match when "Paradise City" by Guns N' Roses blares over the speakers . Then a well-dressed man wearing a business suit and sunglasses walks down the ailse. The crowd recognizes him as "Mastermind"
Marc Ellett and boos him like crazy. Marc struts arrogantly into the ring and poses for the crowd as lasers and fireworks go off around him. He snatches the mic from the suprised announcer and takes off his sunglasses and gazes into the crowd with a smirk.
"It's been about a year since I last stepped into a ring and there are those who thought that I retired. Hell no baby! I am back and even better than I was before and am ready to prove to the world why I am "The
Mastermind"! I hear all the other losers and morons talk about how-they-beat-who but now they have to deal with a REAL CHALLANGE and not just another 5 and dimer! Well I do have a few scores to settle with a moron named Jack Hartzell! Hartzell. Hartzell, thought you ran me out a year ago?
Man you need a serious reality check! You were telling everyone that you were the reason that I was not in the WWPA.....what a crock! The only reason you WERE the champ is because you hid behind the president! He never gave me a title shot.....well you know what......you were the champ but only because you never had the balls to face me! Now the world knows what a little piece of sh@t you are! But I will deal with you in due time my friend! Right now I need go on to bigger and better issues! I have decided to bring back the most elite group in wrestling........THE INNER CIRCLE!
(the camera points down the ailse as 3 men walk towards the ring and give
Marc a high-five).
"Man o man just like 'ol times! The Inner Circle was the most feared and respected group in e-wrestling......and for good reason. We have with us the tag team of Dirty Doug and Terrific Taylor.....better known as "The
Enforcers" and we have our security guard and my former tag partner......Street Punk. (Marc gives the mic to street punk)
SP: It's like a family reunion here brother! We will once again dominate the wrestling world as we always have in the past and if anyone gets in our face.......I feel sorry for the fool who even thinks of trying that!
Dirty Doug: The Enforcers are back and we are ready to break some necks and have a good time doing it. We don't care who we fight.....we just wanna break some necks!
We will become the most dominant group in the new millinium! Marc: You morons are seeing the most awesome group in all of wrestling! We are back and we are ready to raise some hell.....wait a FREAKING minute.......I almost forgot to introduce my main man........GIVE IT UP FOR
OUR MANAGER..........."Deadly" David Winner!
(The camera points to the ailse as David walks to the ring with a briefcase in one hand and thier valet "Destiny" on his other arm. He enters the ring and badmouths the crowd)
DDW: You know Marc it's been too damned long but here we are.....one year later and still kicking ass! Like you said we will dominate wrestling well into the next century and 100 years from know people will remember us like they remember Hogan and Flair today! We have a bond that is tighter than that of brothers and we got each others back! And as far as Jack Hartzell is concerned if he has the balls to show his face again he's a dead man! But he is probally in some back-water hell-hole hiding like the pathetic little ass he is! People stand on you feet and show the respect my men deserve! You are seeing the future world champions before you!
