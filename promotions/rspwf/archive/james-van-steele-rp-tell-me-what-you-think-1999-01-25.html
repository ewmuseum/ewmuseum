James Van Steele RP, tell me what you think....

25 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades into a very dark and eerie looking church, the benches are all covered in puddles of a water/dust mix. The spider webs have been ripped to the ground by the weight of the rain that has leaked through the holes in the roof. The altar is warped and water logged wood, pieces of which have fallen to the ground and the rot worms and digging the the exposed areas. There seems to be a comotion coming from one of the back rooms. As the camera gets closer to the room, you can hear the faint sound of music and a conversation of people.]
[The camera man pushes the door open and a small cloud of smoke pours out from the small room.
Sitting there are four figures, two of which are the Van Steele brothers, James and Eric and the other two are a pair of women, who seem to be enjoying themsevles as they each have a bottle in hand and are laughing there asses off. James quickly hides something behind his back and Eric tries to catch the attention of the camera as it pushes through the smoke. Eric stands up and opens the lid of the cooler that he is sitting on, he pulls out a bottle of beer and hands to his brother.
Eric is the first to speak up....] EVS: "You maybe wondering to yourself, what the hell are these people doing partying in the house of God. Well, I will tell you. On this weeks PRIMETIME, James is going to be taking on ,
Gabrielle Cross you know one of these freaky gothic people. Or as the normal members of society call, "losers who need to get laid"...
[Everyone begins to laugh at this not so funny joke...] EVS: "And thinking that the best way for us to get a good idea to what these people are like, you know that whole know your opponent thing, we decided to stay in this haunted church. It is abandonded and I don't think that anyone is going to be coming here for awhile, so we are going to spend the night here in this place. But, James was not to hot on the idea at first, so I made him a 'small'
bet that I could last longer then him in here then he could. Now, as for everything else, well we Van
Steele's like to make the best out of every situation we get into. So, I brought the beer and he brought these two lovely females! And as for all the smoke....James thought it would be funny to bring a smoke machine...yeah, that's it...and it went a little nutso due to the all water....
[Eric begins to coughs as he laughs and points to allow James do some of the talking. James sits there with a very odd look on his face, he begins to laugh but manages to get it under control...]
JVS: "I know man, you spilled water all over that smoke machine and it clouded up the place. Gives it that sort of eerie feeling don't you think bro? I mean, what happens if one of those vampire freaks comes on in through the smoke and we are sitting here, sober, and unaware that he is sneaking up do to the smoke all over the place...."
EVS: "Now you are just being paranoid........but, that is because you are normally paranoid."
JVS: "Uh, what?....OH yeah you know me! But let me tell you all this, next week on the 30th of
January, Gabrielle Cross, you can try all you want to come down and try to suck my blood, but I am going to come prepared for you, as for the past week and a half me and Eric have been putting together our "OFFICAL World Tail Tour 99 Anti-Vampire Kit"....It is packed with many goodies that we think will keep this match on the normal plane of existance and allow JVS, "The American
Legend", to get you to tap out to the most devestating move in professional wrestling today..the
STF!! Or as I call the.... STEELE THE SHOW!!! And you maybe asking yourself, what is exactly IN this Anti-Vampire Kit well the following items:
[clears throat] EVS: "James, shut up! Don't give away anything, if they want to see the Anti-Vampire Kit in action they are going to have to tune into Primetime and found out.."
JVS: "Oh yeah, why didn't I think about that?...hey hand me another beer, bro...." EVS: "Standard!" [flips beer to James] JVS: "Hey bro, you got the munchies? I mean, since we have been in this place for hours and we didn't bring anything to eat..."
EVS: "Yeah, man, I got the mad munchies! Cause, I haven't all day...why don't one of your ladies take my car and go pick us up some food or something. Me and James are going to sit here and try and figure out what went wrong with the smoke machine..."
[The ladies get up to leave, and James begins to laugh uncontrolably...] JVS: "Yeah, fix the smoke machine...he he he he....we need some tacos as well." [The camera fades out as the ladies walk past the camera man and begin to walk towards the outside door, Eric and James just sit there until the camera completly shuts down the camera.]
