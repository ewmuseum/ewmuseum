Deep inside of USCW Headquarters, two men are about to partake in a history making interview. They are currently rivals in wrestling’s HOTTEST feud. The thought of these two men even being in the same room, is enough to make many executives wipe their brows in utter fear. These men are like no other. The hatred they share could clearly be felt by all in attendance. The crowd didn’t dare utter a word as these two men began speaking. They stayed silent throughout the entire meeting
to hear the words of the greatest wrestler of all time, Daniel Dare, and his hated archrival, USCW World Heavyweight Champion, Viper>
DARE: <disgustedly> I don't know what Spicer drank last night. Is this his idea of compelling television? I think I'm about to vomit being in the same room with vermin like you.
VIPER: Heh, well boyo, I dunno what the fuck he's thinkin, but here we are, so say what the Fuck you gotta say so I can get outta here

VIPER: Dare, We've had two matches (I'll count the one in Sammy Creamer's fed so you can feel vindicated)... One of them was in the UWF, where I pinned yer sorry ass for the UWF Amateur Title, where BOTH of us Tried our hardest, and YOU came up second...
The SECOND one was in that piss ant Creamer fed, where I NEVER said a word, and SOMEHOW ended up in a loser leaves town match with you, which you won... But the past REALLY doesn't matter Boy... Here's what does, you came HERE to the USCW, and now you THINK You're going to take MY
USCW World Heavyweight Belt away... Somehow I don't think so!!! <Dare pauses for a moment> DARE: Viper, the difference between me and you is that I have a reason to be a cocky bastard. You don't. You're nothing but a mediocre little worm in a field full of rattlers. I am the greatest wrestler in the world. You know that deep inside your heart. You wanna talk about Creamer and his bush league??? <Angrily> WE'LL TALK ABOUT
THAT! You interfered in a match between and Grave Tavern to crown a champion of Creamerville. When your fatal error ended up HELPING me WIN the title, you ran away like a little girl. You couldn't handle the fact that I was World Heavyweight Champion in that organization, and you had something to do with it. So instead of facing me like a MAN, you retreated to snake land. BUT Creamer had me on a contract fer another fight, so I said "Ok Sammy make Nut Grabber happy, and job me to him so I can get out of this piss ant fed" So he did, and I left...................Now letz talk about runnin people out Little boy... Tell me WHO WAS it that ran from the UWF like the pussy that they are?? You lost, you couldn't handle it, and you ran like a bitch.... No big surprise there though... You'll run from here after I take yer sorry ass down too...

Didn't your fat ugly mother ever teach you that honesty was the best fucking policy? <smirks>...........As for the UWF....I left that sorry ass hellhole, because the President was an alcoholic, a drug addict, and a total jackass. The company had gone straight to hell. My kissing it goodbye just sent the nail right through the rotting coffin of the UWF.
VIPER: Haha, of course the rest of you were paid that way.... Yer fuckin AMATEURS.... Sammy put ME on a contract cause he was PRAYING I'd stay and save his sorry fed.... By the way, you been hanging around with

I can't help it if Stock, Kelley, and Spicer realize that I am the past, present, and future of this business.
