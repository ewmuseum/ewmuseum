Roleplay , give me ya thoughts

12 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
_=- OUTLAW =-_ _=- Master of the _=- _=- Deathwarrant _=- ( The Scene Fades in as Many People are leaving the Small Catholic Church
Located on Third Street In Monroe,Louisiana... They all have smile's on there faces, for they have now been "Blessed" By God. One Man is still In the Church and that is the Main Pastor of the Church...A revern if you will.... He is doing some Last Minute Counting's of "God's" Money up on the Main Stage. Then a
Man enter's the Church and It put's a shocked Look on the face of the
Pastor...The Man is Outlaw, He is not in Church clothes, he is in his everyday,
Baggy Millitary pants, combat Boot's with a slight brown color, a Gold Necklace and a White shirt which says " Against all oddz". He enter's and set's on the
Aisle closest to the Altar where people ask God to cleanse them of there sin's..... A small fire is going behind the Altar, The Pastor Walk's Over To
Outlaw and set's Beside Him..... Outlaw Keep's his head focused on the Altar, but the Pastor begin's to talk to him even though it Look's as if Outlaw is in deep thought )
[_ Pastor_] My son.... ( Outlaw Nod's his head and The Pastor Put's his Arm on Outlaw's Shoulder ) [_Pastor_] I have not seen you here in a very long time.... Have you done something wrong my son ?
( Outlaw Ignores him and continue's to stare at the Altar ) [_Pastor_] I can only Help ,if you let me help my son. ( Outlaw continues to ignore the Pastor ) [_Pastor_] I sense you wish to be alone, so it shall be done...May God Bless you my child.
( The Pastor stands from his seat and does a Miniature prayer over Outlaw, then walks back behind a curtain located behind the Altar, Outlaw then Begin's to speak as if somebody was there )
[_Outlaw_] A concept which most do not Understand and NONE understand to the fullest extent is God..... I beleive in him, but Know very Little of him. You on the Other hand Know very Little of Him and don't Beleive in him
Nyghtstorm... You are a man in which the devil has took over... A man lost in this _Huge_ Unforgiving world. A man in which Has made a mistake in not
Following God, but is shown no Compassion, Cause no one in this World cares.... They treat you as a Small Bug, who they just squash and move on to the Next
Obstacle... No one has stopped and Talked to you.....Nobody has wandered why,You are what you are, or Tried to help you Become a Better Person.......
Well Nyghtstorm, ( Outlaw Pauses, Then his Lip's move in a motion un-like you would usually see in A church, an Evil grin in which you would see from a Man Plotting to commit a Murder or Break into someone's car is formed....)
[_Outlaw_] I hate to Break it to you, but _I_ Am just like them..... ( The Grin on his face then Produces a bit of Laughter, After the Laughing is done, the Grin on his face goes away )
[_Outlaw_] I have made Plans to Squash you just Like a Bug, Just as Soecity does people like yourself.... I plan to keep these Plan's..... Soecity does not treat me GREAT, So why should I treat someone else Great. "Do Unto other's as they do Unto you" Is My Golden Rule. Nyghtstorm, I came to this Church for a reason today. I came to show you that The Power which you claim to have...You know the so called "Dark" Power is False..For if there is Dark, there is also
Light. There is A God and A Devil, but they do Not let there Power's show...This is a holy Temple..If someone was to mess it up, I bet God would get mad.Don't Ya think ? So , If someone messed with it, he would probally show his
"True" Power..This Should Prove there is No Dark or Any sort of Power of that
Nature... This should Put a Major dent into the way you live your life.
( Outlaw get's up from the Aisle and walks toward's the Altar, He pull's out a knife from his Pant's Pocket and Hold's it to the Wooden Altar, just as he is about to Carve into the Altar, the Small fire, Becomes Large....The Flames become Hotter than A hot summer day. A Low but yet clear voice is then Heard "
You doubt me, That is your own fault, but I hope you beleive what ye see, Do
Not be a Fool, Thy Eye's do Not lie.Not all Receive warning's, Let this Place be .............Or else..." The Voice then Fades Out along with the Fire.
Outlaw with a Shocked Look on his face, step's backward's till he trip's on the
Aisle seat which he came from.He is now back in the same spot in which he started. The Knife still Lies in his Left hand )
[_Outlaw_] Wha.... Wha..... What Happened ? Oh man..... Ok I can't Explain that. ( Just then the Pastor walks from behind the curtain and over toward's Outlaw..This time he does not set down beside though, for he see's the knife located in his Left Hand )
[_Pastor_] You saw it ? You seen the Light didn't you. [_Outlaw_] I guess you could call it that.. My God.... [_Pastor_] Yes he is. ( Outlaw with a puzzled Look on his face, stand's up and put's the knife back into his pants Pocket. He look's to the Pastor and stare's him right in the eye's as he says the following....)
[_Outlaw_] If one can have such Power's as this, Then is the Claim which Jason
Nyghtstorm makes true ? If he really has such Power's.... A man, A Mere Mortal
Like myself might be in some deep shit when it comes to getting that World
Title.
[_Pastor_] There is Powers in the world Like Nyghtstorm Claims to have, but he does not have them. You have nothing to fear, go in thinking this is just another match. If you go in thinking that, you shall exit the same way you always do.With another win and some more money in your pocket.That is a promise.He is just another obstacle to surpass my son. Don't let him Hold you down...Don't let anyone hold you down.
[_Outlaw_] Hey, My Brother was wrong, you preacher's aren't all Bull shit,Just
Partially. I got a whole new out look on life and this match now...Thanks man...Well one thing did stay the same. Jason Nyghtstorm is still gonna feel the
_Deathwarrant_ It is Just that simple. ( The Pastor say's a Prayer with Outlaw, and then the two go there seperate way's..Once Outlaw is outside the Church,He breathes a bit of the Cold Morning
Fresh air, Then Speaks)
[_Outlaw_] OK...For once I was wrong, there is a DARK power, but I was also right in such manner as You DO NOT HAVE THAT POWER. Saturday is getting closer, and I guess the Devil hasn't let you up off ya fucking Knee's to come
Speak ya mind on our Little encounter of the Hardcore kind coming soon. I feel a bit disrespected.It makes me think that you assume I am a Push-Over..That is not the case Nyghtstorm.Soon, you will see,just as the other's will.Soon I will have that world title around my waste and then I shall be respected, and accepted as what I am. That is one
_HARDCORE MOTHER FUCKER_ I might not be the Most Hardcore, but I am the < Most > Determened man in the world.So May God.......Or the Devil...... Well who the fuck ever you beleive in help you....
_Quote The_ _Outlaw_ _No More_ [ The scene slowly fades out, as Outlaw walks down The street of Third, to Lake
Avenue where his Small But Cozy home is located ]
