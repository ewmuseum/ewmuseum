(Critique RP please) Eric "The Undeniable One" Cage

22 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please critique the Following RP and id like to introdice him to you! ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------- (Scene opens to a face shot of the "Undeniable One" Eric Cage. His blonde hair is tied back and the cameras reflection can be seen through his Black
Ray-ban glasses. The camera backs up away from his face. He wears a white tuxdedo with a black tie. His black shoes shimmer on the red carpet. The camera backs up even more as it reveals a stage set. Written all over it says "The Undeniable One" in big bold letters. Eric Cage sits in a white leather chair to go with his matching suit. He sits smiling at the camera in a laid out lazy position)
Cage: Welcome everyone to what i call....MY SHOW!!! with your host of course The "Undeniable One" Eric Cage. Dont you think the name fits? Just look at me, you cant deny this! Anyways enough about how good i look, lets get to the show
(Cage sits up into a formal position. He adjusts his tie a bit and takes a sip of his coffee)
Cage: Today the "Undeniable One" will talk about his feelings in the ELW. He will talk about how he feels about the ELW so called "superstars". Cage doesnt need to know much about a man however. He just takes a look and can tell a rich man when he sees it. Anyways lets get to the Questions from the audience.
(A fan steps up to a microphone to be used for asking questions) Cage: Yes what would you like Miss Beautiful? (the girl blushes and turns away. The men cheer at her like crazy) Girl: How did you get such a great body? Cage: Well, to tell you the truth, i never did a thing. Great bodies come with great men such as The "Undeniable One" himself
(A man steps up to the mic and asks a question) Man: What type of wrestler are you? How do you tear up your opponents? (Cage laughs and sits up in his chair) Cage: Well, just by looking at you i see your the hardcore type of guy. Well The Undeniable One can do hardcore, hell The Undeniable one can do anything he damn wants. Hes just great! You want a submission match with
Cage, he will do it. You want a full out brawler? hell thats what im good at! The question here i think my friend is how do i NOT tear up my opponents and the answer to that is.....well there isnt a answer to that!
(The crowd cheers as another man walks up to the mic) Man: First off Cage, i have to say i love you! Now.. Cage: Hold up there big guy. First off to you, The "Undeniable One" does not want to hear that from a guy like you. In fact i dont want to hear that from any guy!
(the man seems embarrased as he starts to walk away from the stand. The fans boo at the man at the comment he made)\
Cage: Now wait a minute little man. What did you want to ask Eric Cage? (The man turns around excited that Cage is actually talking to him. He runs back down the stairs and jumps to the microphone)
Man: Um....Well ...What are your plans for the ELW....Undeniable One? Cage: I plan to take the ELW by storm. To make everyone realize why i am called the "Undeniable One". To show why it is a privelage to get in the ring with me and why i will be the ELW champion. Anyone who steps in the ring with me will automatically become famous. I dont care whop the opponent is. I dont care if he is the world champion. All i care about is getting to the top and when you are the undeniable one.....thats a easy job.
BECAUSE YOU CANT DENY THE GREAT ONE!! (The camera drops the rolling credits as the crowd cheers. they chant
Cage...Cage...Cage. The credits end and the screen fades to black)
