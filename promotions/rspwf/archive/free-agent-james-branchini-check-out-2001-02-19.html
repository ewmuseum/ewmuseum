PART 1 OF 3 "KILLING A MANS LIFE WITH WORDS OF REALITY JUST FOR FUN" [We fade into a black screen. A man is standing up wearing a white robe. Only one side of the mans face is shown. A confusing silhouette. There is a sheet going across his chest forming an X which follows threw around his waste. Two medal medallions are hanging from his belt. He looks into the camera. Suddenly he speaks. ]
[VoiCe oVeR] Life comes and goes. But never does it stall. There is no pausing. You come into this world as just another soul. It is up to you to decide the amount of fame, the amount of glory you have. You have very little time, do what you can. Although, every life has the same outcome. Death.
Whether your name remains in everyone's mouth is all up to you. Legend, Icon,
Hero, three things that keep a name alive. Three things that make a fighter a warrior.
[The camera fades in to a sky. Pitch black. Dark as fuck. About 5 or 6 stars are scattered threw out the sky. Still, there is very little light. Then, the camera pan to your right and the moon appears. Suddenly from pitch black it goes directly to pitch white. The camera stays focused on the moon. The pale white color is the only light in the area. The whole moon fits on one screen as the surroundings of the moon are black. The sounds of circuits begin to be heard. The audience then gains interest and begins to wonder where we are. No words are spoken. No more chirping sounds are heard. Just the moon. We then pan down as a crash of thunder is heard. As we pan down we come across a house.
But before that we come down from the moon to seeing trees. Rain pouring from the sky. Once we reach the house fly by a window seeing a man sitting on a chair with a very weak light above him. Suddenly, we begin to walk up the hallow, wooden, brown steps. Without knocking we enter the house. With in three seconds we enter the same room as the man. He is sitting on a hay string chair with a tap light which is set on low. He is looking out the window with a look of wonder. The rain is pouring down heavier and heavier by this point. Across from the window, in view point is another house. A swimming pool and a swing set make up the backyard. As we fade into the house the lights slowly dim out.
Leaving nothing but an empty house. Then, we fade into the man. He has a shaven beard but it is not perfectly shaved. Still you can see little hairs sticking out of the mans face. Snap. The man looks up at the camera with his hazel eyes and red hair which is gelled wild. He grins and stares hard into the camera. He speaks.]
| -[james branchini]- | Still here. Still high. Still on top. I ain't got nothing to lose. This is the stage where a boy turns into a man. Where a superstar rises to an icon.
This is where James Branchini raises his head, and shows the world what he holds inside of him along with what he lacks. My mind, body, and soul will be broken down and gnawed at by a rabid beast. By "Stalker". Your "Legend". But the sad thing is, your "legends" are men that I can defeat or in this case, I have defeated. Call it luck Stalker, I wouldn't expect anything less. A man with the caliber of yours would do anything to paint over a win against me. You would do anything to cover up the simple fact that James Branchini played your same game twice, and still walked out the winner. There is no luck in that my friend. No luck at all, that is the truth. That is reality.. And in reality, there is nothing but truth. Heal your wounds, because in the past James
Branchini has caused a lot of damage to your ever so "infamous" 'Suicidal
Psycho'. The games shall begin. Enter bitches. This is my world, you are nothing but a figment of my imagination.
[The rain begins to pour harder and harder. As the trees blow heavily in the winds the sound of the rain is loudly heard outside. Outside, the grass is getting swampy as each drop of rain hits the grass causing water to shoot up in retaliation. The moon begins to get covered up by the clouds as the get darker and darker by the minute. Suddenly, the moon is completely gone. We than begin to zoom out on something. Some think it is the moon others think it is just the sky but as we get further away we can tell we are in the same room as James
Branchini. Slowly, the camera zoomed out of his dark hair. Still, he is sitting there looking out of the window, at the rain. Dropping from the clouds, splatting on the ground, dripping from the gutters landing on the window sill.
James looks down at the floor for a few seconds and rubs his goatee. Slowly he looks up, grinning...with a confused look on his face.. Branchini laughs, and begins to speak.]
| -[james branchini]- | Stalker, the battle of a life time. For you, maybe not. But for me. Definitely. The world will be watching. One card. One main event. Stalker vs. James Branchini. My chance to shine. To show the world what I made of. To you this may be just another walk in the park. Just another day, another person.
Stalker, I am not here to take nothing away from you. Just to thrive off of your success. From what you have done, will make take one step higher.
Everything you have done, will never be repeated. Your story is yours. It is sealed. Although, James Branchini still has a story of his own. A story to be
told. A story to be written. There will never be another like you, and I ain't out to destroy you. I am out to show you that there are little bitches like me crawling up the ladder day by day catching up to these ..
[Holds up hands as quotations.] ...Legends... [Puts his hands back down.] Here you are ... lacking off. Day after day. Nothing but fading. Nothing but losing your will. Your are slowly dying like a rapid fire who has just been introduced to the wind. Although, you are still the same man presidents love. Love because of your name. Because of what you have done. Caught up on your achievements not even realizing the new blood. The new generation.
Stalker, you are the type who is looked at and make a president afraid of making you lose. Simply because they do not want to lose you. Lose your name.
Lose your legacy. Caught up on the past. Blinded by their own ignorance to even notice the future. So basically Stalker, you are riding to survive.
Keeping me from being noticed, and it is time for that to change. It is time for me to show the world just who is knocking the king off it's throne.
[James Branchini digs into his pocket and pulls out a pack of "Newport" Cigarettes. He pulls one out and puts it in his mouth. Weekly being held by his lips, the cigarette flops down. Laying there helpless. Suddenly. James gets a firm grip of the cigarette and makes a cup out of his hands and lights the sonbitch. Slowly he inhales and exhales threw his nose. He then sits back to relax. Forgetting the rain pouring. He looks into the camera and takes another inhale, followed by and exhale. Branchini flicks the cigarette causing the ashes to fall. Once again he speaks.]
| -[james branchini]- | Stalker will be doing nothing but falling victim to me. Let's forget the past between me and you because in that category I win. You lose. Let's focus on now. Let's focus on you. For every point you will try and make, will be just another point I turn around on. Hitting you from every angle. I will be teaching the "teacher" a lesson that will never be forgot.
[Grins] ...The odds are stacked against me. [Stops grinning.] .... Fuck the odds. [James flips the cigarette on the floor and steps on it. Suddenly the camera switches to it and we see nothing but the smoke rising from the red tip. Than we switch again. Back to James. Back to the rain. Still pouring. Branchini looks out and smiles. He stands up and looks into the camera.]
| -[james branchini]- | Soon those odds will be replaced with countless bodies. Starting with one suicidal psycho. Followed up by a surplus amount. As you paint your face with
"war paint" James Branchini will sit and laugh. Laugh at you, and at the same time pity you. I need no nothing disguise me. As I look in the mirror every day
I see the same reflection. I see the same man. Although, no one ever looks deeper. Everyone just looks at what they can see. Not even realizing what is within. Stalker, look deep into my soul. Find the core of a man. Walk in my shoes and than you can criticize me.
[Pauses] ..But until than, [Grins] ...You have no fucking idea. ....For every cause there will be an effect. For every ounce of blood shed will just mean another point proven. For every "legend" knocked down, will just mean one less bitch in my path. Stalker, don't try to hard. You might hurt yourself... You might do something you don't want to. You might actually try.
[Branchini looks up at the sky again. A grin then rises upon his face as he focuses on the camera. Although, before he gets a chance to speak he turns his head to cough. When he looks back up. he stares into the camera. Only to speak.]
| -[james branchini]- | Survival will be your only exit back to the world. You must stay and fight this battle with hopes of leaving as the winner. With my head high, I will march to the ring. I will salute you, and I will shock the world as James
Branchini rises another level. Win - Lose - or draw, there will be no shame. I have already gotten what I want. I am just asking for more. So, round up the troops Stalker. Sleep no more. The psycho shall be woken and woken at last.
Once woken, Stalker will be nothing but a pawn in my game of chess. He will be controlled like a puppet on a string.. He will be controlled by me.
[Long Pause.] Doubt that? When we meet, there will no dark side, there will be no light side. There will just be reality. There will be life. Because, you see. You have everything all misunderstood. You have yet to view things from different angles. Different perspectives. There is no "darkside" Stalker. The place you dwell in. The place you call home. The place is earth. In this earth, there are many characteristics. Many attributes that all link together and form a society.
Where you come from is the same place I come from. Where you were raised I was raised the same place. You claim darkside -- I claim reality. Darkside,
Lightside. Good bad. Hate, Love. They all make up earth. There are no missing links. They are just missing in your picture. They are just missing in your life because you are too blind to open your eyes and search. Ignorance is no bliss. How can you be happy out of being ignorant. Stalker, hate me. Love me.
It all won't matter. I will rise. I will reign supreme. I will walk out with everlasting pride.
[Long dramatic pause.] You will be doing nothing but shooting blanks. You can't hunt the hunter and that is what you'll be trying to do. You can't catch me. You can't hold me. You can't touch me. You can't break the unbreakable. For every end you reach, it will just be another beginning. For, there is no beginning, there is no end. There is only change.
... I am that change ... And whether you accept that or not it is all up to you. You have the choice to allow me to move on or stay down. By beating me you are starting a trend which can murder me, but by me beating you will just move me up. To the top of the fucking mountain. To destroy the motherfuckers you all worship. To destroy the men you guys call your "icons". It is only a matter of time before
I light the torch and burn every bitch out there. One by one starting with
Stalker. Stalker, this will be a battle worth fighting. I have seen you in the past. I have seen what you can do. I seen you beat the unbeatable. I seen you destroy the invincible. But now, you are probably thinking "than what makes you think you can beat me?". I have no clue. With all honesty. I don't know. My side of insanity? Or maybe, I am possessed by my sacrifice. Stalker, the beast shall be woken.
SLEEP NO MORE. [Branchini grins and we fade to black.]
