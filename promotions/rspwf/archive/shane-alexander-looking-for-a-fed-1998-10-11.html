[The scene opens up to a shot of Shane Alexander in a black Armani suit. He is sitting in front of a group of reporters and camera-men. He stands up and begins to speak.] SA - "Welcome, everyone. This is Shane Alexander. I have been around the wrestling world for about 3 months now. If not more. I currently wrestle in the OWF and SWP. But I am looking for one more federation. So let the invites begin. My answer will come next week, Saturday, October 17th, 1998. Thank you.
