[RP] Intro Flash: Super Deluxe

24 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Critique if you feel like. Feedback welcome. ***** [Fade in.] [SCENE: A large banquet hall inside a very nice hotel in Landover, Maryland. The place is lined with large buffet tables full of elaborate platters and decorations. Hundreds of people from their early twenties to their sixties pass by the camera, laughing and smiling. They are all dressed to the nines.
These are the beautiful people. Their chatter and laughter as they mingle is quite loud, but it hushes to complete silence with the creek of a door. Then, the room explodes with the popping of champagne bottles and a rousing clap as two young gentlemen enter the banquet hall, grins across their faces. Both are a shade under six feet tall. The man on the right has short, caramel brown hair and piercing dark blue eyes. He is dressed in a pair of black dress shoes, nice khakis, a grey turtleneck sweater, and a stylish black leather coat. This is Cal Clarke. The man on the left has blond hair, lighter blue eyes, and a deep tan. He is wearing black dress shoes, black slacks, and a dark navy ribbed t-shirt tucked in. He carries an expensive looking jacket over his shoulder. This is Zach Mowers.]
[As the two walk into the room, they are met by a flood of people. The men shake their hands and pat them on the back; the women hug them or kiss their cheeks. Both look flattered, but surprisingly, not overwhelmed. It's as if they expected nothing less.]
CAL CLARKE: Ladies and gentlemen -- [The group quiets as he speaks.] CAL CLARKE: My partner and I would like to thank you all for coming here tonight. Many of you have guessed as to the reason for us inviting our closest friends, family, and associates. Now, I would like to make it official. It is with great pleasure that we publically announce our signing with the APWA wrestling company!
[A rousing ovation! The duo smile, soaking it in.] ZACH MOWERS: As you can see, there are tables full of the most delictable foods and the finest wines. We have spared no expense in providing this celebration feast. Enjoy.
[He gives a polite smile, then gives Cal a look. The two break through the crowd and head for a far corner, away from the horde of people.]
CAL CLARKE: Fucking jackals, aren't they? I still say we could've done this without our families. Half of them aren't even accustomed to this sort of celebration. Look at them drooling like animals. They're probably used to pizza rolls and cheap beer. [sighs] I hate these functions.
ZACH MOWERS: Easy, Cal. This is our night. We worked damn hard to get this deal with APWA, and we have every right to celebrate. If we didn't invite all these people, we'd look like stuck up assholes.
CAL CLARKE: [smug] Wouldn't want that, would we? [The two exchange a chuckle. Then, Cal notices that they're on camera. He elbows Zach in the arm, nudging him. The two become aware of the fact they're being filmed.]
CAL CLARKE: Well, camera boy, we didn't know if you'd make it past security. Is that a rental tux? [smirks] Ah, you're not even worth it. Just sit there and record. This is our moment, and you can bet for damn sure we're gonna make it one to remember.
[He pauses, clears his throat...] CAL CLARKE: APWA, take a good look. No, we're not gods, we just look like it. We are the ones that are going to put a little shine on your dollar-and-dime operation. For the few of you who don't know by now, direct from Madison
Avenue and Hollywood and Vine, respectively, I'm Cal Clarke, he's Zach Mowers, and we --
ZACH MOWERS: -- are Super Deluxe. CAL CLARKE: And you are not. It's really that simple. Zach and I have been in this business for a few years, now, working in some pathetic little indies while we "paid our dues." Well, after getting our names out in some of the poorest excuses for wrestling promotions across the country, we finally signed a deal that could get us national exposure.
ZACH MOWERS: The promotion's not established, but that's okay. The money was right, the TV deal is a plus, and the fact is, it doesn't really matter _where_ we wrestle, just as long as some promoter has the brains to sign the hottest tag team of the 21st century. There's a whole lot of people out there who don't know of our greatness, and that's a real shame.
[Cal nods his head in agreement.] CAL CLARKE: My partner's right. Why should the world be deprived of Super
Deluxe? We're young, we're attractive, we've got money, we've got class, and we've got more wrestling talent on our worst days than the so called greats have on their best. So what's the deal? Why has it taken so long for the wrestling world to take notice?
ZACH MOWERS: Because the wrestling world is full of blind, ignorant people afraid of looking bad. Well, be afraid, boys. If your worried about being outwrestled, outclassed, and outshined, your fears are justified. There's a simple undeniable truth in the words we speak.
[Mowers pauses, pulling on his jacket. He reaches into a pocket and pulls on a pair of expensive black sunglasses while Clarke does the same. They stand there, heads tilted upwards, for what seems like an eternity, but is rea only an annoying ten seconds.]
CAL CLARKE: We _are_ Super Deluxe. [A condescending smirk from both men.] ZACH MOWERS: And you are not. [The two share a laugh. Cal pushes his sunglasses down with a finger, while the camera zooms in a bit closer per his hand motion. He gives his best charming face.]
