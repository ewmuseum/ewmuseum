[Flash] "Homeless" Joe

30 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWFites, Here is a flash I did for my match with Mad Goat at MTTM. You won't find it funny unless you've met PizzaJazz, but oh well, it's still a decent flash I think.
Enjoy, [name withheld] (The following is entitled "The IM") [black. Black. More Black. The screen is black. Not blue, not red, but black. Black like Metallica's CD, you know, the one with "Enter Sandman" on it. Black. In white letters, several words appear...]
THURSDAY DECEMBER 2ND, 1999... [the screen goes black again. Coal black. Ebony Black. Ivory black. Wait, that's not right. Scratch that last one]
AT THE MARCH OF THE MILLENNIUM SUPERCARD... [the screen goes black again. Black black black black....] A BUM.. [a picture of "Homeless" Joe appears on the left side of the screen under the word "BUM"] AND A GOAT... [a picture of Mad Goat appears on the right side of the screen, next to Joe's picture, under the word "GOAT". The screen fades to black again..] WILL CROSS PATHS... [the screen fades to white. Just kidding. It fades to black. FOOLED YOU!] AND WHAT DO YOU GET WHEN YOU CROSS A GOAT WITH A BUM? [the words fade to black as we ponder this interesting question...] WELL ISN'T IT OBVIOUS? [the screen goes black. Michael Jackson black. Wait, scratch that last one. Just black. Plain old boring black.] YOU GET A HIDEOUS HALF GOAT-HALF MAN CREATURE WHO LIKES TO EAT TRASH AND DOESN'T SHOWER OFTEN. THIS CREATURE WILL KILLS US ALL! RUN! SAVE YOURSELVES..AHHHHHH...whoops...I FELL OVER MY COFFEE TABLE...SOMEONE HELP ME UP! I HAVE TO GET AWAY FROM THIS EVIL GOAT BUM MAN!!! AAAAAHHHHHH!!! IT'S BITING MY LEG!! AAAHHHHHH!!!!!! SOMEONE HELP ME!!! [I'm running out of black jokes. I guess I could resort to racist jokes...nah, I better not. Black.]
OK..I THINK I GOT AWAY FROM IT! OK...UH...I THINK I DROPPED MY NOTECARDS WHILE I WAS RUNNING....SO...UH...LETS JUST SHOW THE FLASH NOW... [The screen goes to black.] [there is a flash. Once we can see again, we realize that the screen is no longer black, but that we are in some kind of electronics store. We see a faint glow coming from some where near the back. The store is obviously closed, so it's unusual that any of the electronic devises would still be on. As we get closer to the glowing light, we see that it's coming from one of the computers. Sitting at the computer, is wrestling's favorite homeless guy, "Homeless" Joe Boxcar. We hear him snicker, and the sound of AOL IM's ringing. The camera focuses in on the screen. Joe has signed on to his agents account and is talking to various people...]
[detail withheld]: You know anyone online now? [detail withheld]: Uh...yeah [we hear Joe snicker again] [detail withheld]: Mad Goat.. [detail withheld]: really? What's his screen name... [more evil snickering] [detail withheld]: [detail withheld] [Joe explodes with laughter. We pointer clicks on the AOL Instant Messenger buddy list, opening it. We see that [detail withheld] is in fact, online. Joe clicks
Mad Goat's private AOL screen name to instant message him] [detail withheld]: HAHA! [detail withheld]: [detail withheld] is going to bother you now... [detail withheld]: I gave him your screen name.. [detail withheld]: this is a taste of what's to come Goat! [detail withheld]: See you at MTTM! [detail withheld]: you bastard [detail withheld]: revenge is coming bastard [we hear foot steps] Voice- "HEY! WHO'S THERE!?" [Joe quickly turns off the computer and the screen fades to black...for the last time.] -- [name withheld] The ORIGINAL 5 star General of the RSPWF Army RSPWF Army Founder KNI Founder [detail withheld] Handler of: "Homeless" Joe Boxcar [NAW] Record: 24-2-0 #76 on the 1998 RSPWF 200 #2 on the 1999 Top 50 Brawlers poll #12 on the RSPWF Mid-Year 200 #22 on the EW-Zone top 100 #1 on your Mom's poll MTTM Participant B.U.M- "Homeless" Joe and Larry [VSW] Record: 0-0-0 Quote: "NOW for even more great news on the UWF and [name withheld]!
