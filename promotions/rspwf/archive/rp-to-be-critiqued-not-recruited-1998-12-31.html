[RP] To be critiqued, not recruited

31 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE: Downtown Norfolk, VA. 12AM.] [The red-light district is bustling in the city. Dance clubs and bars are all over the place, people crammed in lines outside the doors waiting to get in.
Music and flashing lights seep through the windows and out into the darkness of the city. Drug stores close down, resturants close shop. The calm, still night is giving way to the thrillseeker's midnight party time. Cut to a quieter part of the neighborhood. A chain link fence ten feet high surrounds a blacktop basketball court. Chalk lines create the boundries. In the far corner of the court, a figure is slumped against the fence, seemingly half-asleep. It is a man, a rather large man, dressed in sneakers, jeans, and a white muscle shirt. The goosebumps on his on reveal it's way too cold for an outfit like this. His blue eyes are blinking open and shut as he tries to remain awake. The wind rustles his sandy brown hair, sending strands into his face. Suddenly, he stands up. His eyes flutter open. Cracking his knuckles, the man stares full on into the camera.]
BK: Oscar Wilde once said, "The truth is rarely pure and never simple." What posses a man to leave the comfort of his three story home in the rich, upper class district of town? What posses an Ivy League graduate to drop out of med school to pursue a rebel's dream of graduating from wrestling school? What posses a man to shun the lifestyle he's always known in favor of a grass-roots style basic approach? Insanity? No, the truth of the matter is dedication and intelligence. You see, I very well could've followed the path they chose for me. I could've become a doctor, sipping Starbucks coffee between breaks, helping cure the sick... and eventually, I'd wither into a self-absorbed creep.
I'd only be interested in how much money I could make to buy that ranch in
Arizona or that summer home in California. Thankfully, that's not me... that's
_them_. I stopped letting them brainwash me a long time ago.
[The man smiles ever so faintly.] BK: My name is Blake Kennedy. I have lived a rather uneventful life until now. No longer will I let myself fall into the combine. The thought of working a nine-to-five job, coming home to two point five kids, and ending up a forty-five year old gray haired jerk who ran away with the gardener sickens me.
It's not a life I choose to lead. I dropped out of med school to defy my parents, I supposed. My friends and I joined up in a local wrestling school, perhaps as a joke it started. Somewhere along the line, it became much more than that to me. It became a passion. In this lifetime, we should not choose to do things simply because they're easy and in front of us. We should choose to do what we are passionate about, no matter how crazy it may seem. If one wants to be a ballerina, I say hand him his tutu. If one wants to be a racecar driver, I say hand him his helmet. And if I want to be a pro wrestler... well, who's going to be stop me?
[Blake pauses, nodding his head in satisfaction.] BK: Why would a guy like me get involved in a sport like this? For one, my competitive nature drives me to the ring. Physical emotion is fascinating, really. The extent of pain man suffers in order to survive is amazing. By entering wrestling, it also gave me a mission... a mission to prove my points.
Actions speak louder than words, they say. Kicking some poor sap around until he sees things the right way sounds like a plan to me. Afterall, all I'm doing is speeding up Darwin's selection. To me, the number of enemies in this organization is great. Youngsters who walk in and think that they, overnight, are god's gift to the world and that they run the place... so-called good guys who are nothing but lying, cheating, hypocritcal jerks... old-timers long past their prime who feebly attempt to cling on and hog the glory... self-centered, egotistical, glamorous villans who fancy themselves ladies men... these are all my enemies. In layman's terms, if you don't see it my way, you'll be seeing my fist in your face.
Make no mistake about it, I'm not here to make money or win titles. I'm here to prove that substance goes over style, that I don't need fancy fireworks, catchphrases, nor t-shirts to sell to prove my success. Success isn't measured in how many digits your salary is nor how many expensive cars you drive.
Success is measured by the amount of people's lives you impact and touch... success is measured by how much you improve the quality of life within and around you. I _will_ succeed greatly in FSW, that I promise.
Wilde also said, "There is no sin except stupidity." You can rest assured, practice that sin...
[A serious look overlaps Blake's smile.] BK: I _will_ strike you down. [Kennedy turns his back on the camera. Fade to black.]
