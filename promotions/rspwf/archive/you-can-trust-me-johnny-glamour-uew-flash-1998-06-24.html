"YOU CAN TRUST ME" JOHNNY GLAMOUR... UEW FLASH

24 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens up to the aftermath of the greatest pay per view of all-time
Summer Dreams '98, inside the locker room of the man you love to hate, "You can trust me!!" Johnny Glamour. Glamour who is sitting down in a chair set up by a press conference table is accompanied by his ditzy airhead valet, Bambi.
Glamour's forehead is bandaged up due to the gash he suffered in his match with
King Snake and is dressed in his world famous "IMA SUCKER" UEW T-shirt and a pair of khaki slacks. Glamour's long blonde hair is still blood soaked and his left eye is swollen. Located on the press conference table is Glamours bible,
"1,001 ways to scam a buck," a microphone and a bottle of mineral water that
Glamour picks up and takes a sip of. As the room fills with reporters, Glamour clears his throat and begins to speak...]
JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- [in his politician style voice] Ladies and gentlemen of the press, I'm glad that you all decided to stop by for a bit of Q and A with the unstoppable force that is taking UEW by storm... I will be more than happy to answer any and every question you fine citizens have for me. But before we begin, I would like to promote my award winning hotline... Pick up your phones right and dial 1-900-4-RIPOFF and select option #9 for the real scoop on the behind the scenes details of what is going on right now in UEW and the rest of the wrestling world. Remember, it is only $4.99 a minute, so don't hesitate and make that call ASAP! [Glamours, takes a big breath of air and continues...] OK, with that said, are there any questions?
[The room of reporters all begin to throw out questions for Glamour to answer. Glamour spots a blonde haired beauty in the back and points to her...] JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- Yo, babe in the blue... What did you want to ask your hero, your inspiration, your life, Johnny Glamour?
Blonde babe-- Thank you Mr. Glamour, I think I can speak for everyone here when I ask you this... What exactly is the deal with you and the Widow Makers
Inc... In particular, what is the deal with you and the man that brought you to UEW, Magnus Colby? I mean, he actually helped you back to the dressing room at the end of your grueling match with King Snake! Care to comment on that?
[Glamour appears a bit hesitant] JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- What? He did? Are you sure? Magnus Colby? Really? Hmmm.. I must have taken a serious bump. I don't even remember... You can trust me on that one... Did I mention my hotline yet at 1-900-4-RIPOFF? Just $4.99 a minute. OK, next question!
[The blonde reporter rolls her eyes as Glamour points to a fat, sweaty man in the back who is apparently wearing a toupee.]
JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- Yo, carpet head... What did you want to ask Johnny Glamour? Man with bad toupee-- Well, I never! Anyway, Mr. "You can trust me!!" First of all, nobody is buying your lies... I heard that you can't really even wrestle, either. Everyone knows that you are a con man and get by on manipulating others and my sources have told me that you already inked a deal with "Organized Chaos." I don't suppose you have any comments on that one, do you?
[Glamour is a bit surprised by the question but plays it off and remains cool as he continues...]
JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- Organized chaos? Johnny Glamour, the peoples choice, the man that you can trust would _NEVER..._ _EVER..._ stoop so low as to join a group like that. I am an upstanding young citizen in this community that will take no short cuts in going for my dreams and reaching for the stars... And my dream is to represent the wonderful people in UEW as their heavyweight champion of the world! I would never let the people down, you can trust me on that!!
By the way, on option #2 of my hotline right now, I share with all the wonderful people out there, the secrets to my over night success! Call now!
An operator is standing by for your call. OK, next question. [A middle aged, ordinary looking reporter in the middle of the room begins to speak...] Middle aged nobody-- Mr. Glamour, what can you tell us about what is going on with Jonathan Witt, Callista Fairchild and Caitlin? Jonathan Witt helped you win, said you couldn't get the job when Caitlin paid you off and then turned on you? And are you and Callista an item? Where does this leave Bambi? I have even heard outrageous rumors that this is all an elaborate plan by Caitlin
Fairchild to gain your services!!! Please comment, if you would.
JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- [Laughing] Where do I begin? I don't know who the bigger bitch is, Caitlin Fairchild or Johnny Witt. I personally think that old John is just a bit jealous over the fact that Caitlin came and contacted me. You see, just like every other group in UEW, she yearns for my services... Unlike the other groups though, she wants a lot more than just my services in the ring. [Glamour looks down to his groin as Bambi giggles] Hey, but Bambi is game for that. You see, she is always down and boy does she get down, to try new things... If Jonathan Witt wants to take a ride to "GlamourVille" that is fine and dandy with me. As soon as something pops up on my busy schedule, I'll get back to him. Then again, with Caitlin Fairchild around, something always does seem to "pop up!" As far as the deal with Callista and I, what can I say?
She is a big fan! A very _BIG_ fan... But isn't everybody? She calls my hotline every day and I always have good advice to give her. And at just $4.99 a minute, how can she lose? Once again, that number is 1-900-4-RIPOFF.
[Just as Glamour finishes plugging his hotline AGAIN, a startled looking reporter enters the room and begins to talk in a startled and disturbed voice...]
Outraged reporter-- Johnny Glamour, I have just received word that the third degree burns that _YOU_ gave to King Snake will probably result in him hanging up his wrestling trunks for good! You nearly killed him! What do you have to say to his family? Do you feel bad over what happened?
[Glamour rubs his chin and begins to talk in an insincere voice] JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- Oh no, that is awful! [trying to restrain from laughing] I can't begin to tell you how bad I feel. [Glamour tries to make himself cry as he pinches himself under the table for the effect of pain.] This is truly a sad day, indeed. I'll tell you what I am going to do to make it up to the great King Snake... If you call my hotline at 1-900-4-RIPOFF and select option
#1, I will tell you all how truly awful I feel about it. And to make it really special, Ill donate 1% of the profit to the "save the snakes" foundation in honor of good old King Snake... Snake, we'll miss you...
[Glamour gets a tissue and blows his nose, still pretending to cry...] JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- This is too painful and difficult to talk about. Next question please!!!
[The room of reporters all appear to be annoyed, outraged and disgusted over the comments made by Johnny Glamour. There is a lot of rumbling in the room but nobody is asking him any questions. Glamour finally begins to speak...]
JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- I know you are all speechless after hearing my truthful and straight from the heart comments. I know I have made you all at a loss for words so I'll ask and answer the questions for you.
[No interest from the reporters as some of them begin to leave the room] JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- Well, I'm about out of time. I hate to do this to you but
I'm going to have to wrap this thing up. Let me end this successful press conference by telling you what a pleasure it has been to answer all of your questions as truthfully and honestly as I could... And let me also say that
Johnny Glamour will continue to fight for the common man until justice and order are finally restored in UEW and until each and every responsible culprit has felt the wrath that "GLAMOURVILLE" has to offer. People, you can trust me on that!!!
[By this time the entire lockeroom is empty as every reporter vacated the room. Glamour lets out a deep sigh as if he just blew his big break as Bambi gives him a blank stare and then a silly giggle as the scene fades out...]
