[FLASH] 'The Phoenix' Evan Manchester -2nd-

17 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's another one incase anyone cares :o) +-+-+-+-+-+ [Fade into an old, junkyard - no one appears to be there. Rows and rows of old, beat up cars can be seen, seemingly for miles. There is are a few huge machines in the middle of the yard. A huge yellow crane, as well as an old, rusted light blue car compactor stick out high above anything, and everything else. We hear a noice, clanging and screaming, coming from a huge pile of cars. As the cameraman seemingly walks along, looking for the source of this noice, we hear him kicking old car parts aside. He makes his way to the larger stack of cars, set stacked up in a semi-circle, next to the heavy machinery, seemingly ready for compaction. We walk around to where the opening is, and we see something.]
[We see a man - a very angry man - throwing hub caps and smashing car windows with a a black baseball bat. He screams with each movement he makes. As this man turns around in a frenzy of anger, still throwing everything in site, we catch a glimpse of his face. He is 'The Phoenix' Evan Manchester.]
[Manchester is wearing faded, ripped blue jeans, and a light blue tee shirt - both stained with grease. He also has on a pair of black boots, as well as his trademark black, leather motorcycle jacket. His long, dry, black hair, swings around with the movement of his body.]
[Manchester is still ferosiously swinging the bat at each and everyone of the soon-to-be dead cars. He grabs a hub-cap and throws it at the window of a car.
When the two objects collide, the window shatters, and the hub cap is left sitting in the front seat of an old, blue, 84 Ford. Evan takes a couple swings, denting, and eventually breaking through the door of that old, blue car. The bat is stuck in the door, but Evan easily pulls it out and continues to destroy other cars.]
[Eventually, he seems to calm down just a bit - or does he. He stops, and turns around. He sees the cameraman, who zooms in onto Evan's eyes. Those dark brown eyes are full of rage and anger, and their stare sends a chill down your spine.
The cameraman soons zooms out to see the full body of Evan Manchester. As we look over the dirty body of 'The Phoenix' we can 't help but notice that he has a few open cuts on his face, knees, and chest. He is breathing very heavily and blood is running down his body. He has fought himself into a bloody mess, and doesn't seem to care. He just stands there, staring coldly at the cameraman.
Evan than suddenly seems to calm down. He drops his bat and sits on the ground. As we wonder what he will do next, he begins to speak in his normal, slow, monotoned voice.]
EVAN MANCHESTER: Come closer. I have a few things to say. [The cameraman reluctantly comes a bit closer, but he still seems cautious, knowing that Evan could burst in a fury of rage at any second. The cameraman is not stupid, he knows how un-stable this man is, and shows caution accordingly.]
EVAN MANCHESTER: There is no need to be afraid, for I will harm you in any way. [The cameraman, still hesitant, moves closer. As he gets about 8 feet away from
Evan, 'The Phoenix' interrupts.]
EVAN MANCHESTER: But not too close, for you never know what I will do next. [Chuckles.] EVAN MANCHESTER: I often come here, to this old junkyard, to unleash some of my deepest anger, because I know, that if I let the anger take over my body, than
I have lost. I battle is lost if I let the rage overcome me. Not only can I handle myself in this state, but neither can anyone else. Not only will I cause tremendous pain to myself, but also to anyone who comes within a few feet of me. Now, this rage has not fully taken over my body in quite a while. Well than how do I explain that little spat a few minutes ago you ask? That was just a small amount of the anger built up inside of me, and that is what is necessary to keep me in a semi-sane state of mind.
[Evan pauses, and runs his bloody hand through his dark hair. He whips some of the sweat from off his face, and than takes a deep breath and continues.]
EVAN MANCHESTER: Jack Vars, you may be 'all-pro', but you aren't good enough to beat Evan Manchester, and that was proven just last night, at Fallout, when I gave you the SunSet Drop, and covered your sorry a** for the 1, 2, 3. Vars,
I've beaten you once, and I know that I can beat you again if the situation arises, so take that as fair warning, and stay away from me. If I feel the need to beat on your sorry carcas again, I will give Pillow a call and arrange a meeting.
Vars, you are, of course, still the North American Champion, and that title just may be around your waist for a long time to come, considering Evan
Manchester has no desire to own it, or any other belt for that matter. As I have said before, titles have no importance to 'The Phoenix'. Therefore, I have no need to suck up to the 'suits' asking and begging to get a shot at the belt.
And if I do get a shot, it's because I earn it, plain and simple. It is because
I have fought my way up the ladder of success, and am almost on the top.
[Evan pauses, and pulls his hair out of his face once again.] EVAN MANCHESTER: But I am still not the best. As the old saying goes, 'To be the man, you gotta beat the man', and, well, that's my goal. I want to beat the best that the ICW has to offer, and once I have done that, my time here will be done.
To work my way up the ladder of success, many battles must be won. I have already beaten one of the ICW's top men in Jack Vars, and next, I must move on to the another newcomer, Arthur Essex - the 'Dark Knight'.
Essex, you are a newcomer to the ICW, a mear rookie in my eyes. I am not suggesting that I am not also a newcomer, but I am not a rookie. I don't go and make an open challenge to let anyone except when I don't know the competition.
And Essex, by making that statement yesterday, you signed your death warrant. The challenge has been met, and your demise has been scheduled - for next
Friday at Fallout.
[The wind has kicked up now, and Evan pulls his hair back once again, but this time, the force of the wind just pushes it back into his face.]
EVAN MANCHESTER: Essex, bring your swords; bring your lady, but be prepared to fall before me; for when you face a foe such as 'The Phoenix', a defeat, is to be expected. Essex, when you and I step in the ring, you will feel pain worse than you could've dreamed up. Essex, my SunSet Drop is feared by many men, but should be feared by all. Some men are are just too arrogent to realize that when I hook you for the SunSet Drop, you _WILL_ lose.
[Evan stands up, picking up his bat and leaning against it.] EVAN MANCHESTER: As far as this battle royal goes, I could care less. I don't feel the need to subject myself to this type of matchup, after fighting such a worthy opponent as Arthur Essex. So ICW, don't be suprised if I dont' show.
Sweet Dreams! [Evan picks up the bat and walks to the cameraman, and than blows right by him, walking into the rows of cars as the camera fades to black.]
