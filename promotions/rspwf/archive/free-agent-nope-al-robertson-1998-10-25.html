[FREE AGENT!? NOPE.] Al Robertson

25 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene fades into Al Robertson, who is sitting at his old IBM PS/2 reading newsgroup messages with AOL DOS 1.6 and a 2400 bps modem. He is wearing his white "Pin me Pay me" t-shirt with blue jeans.) Al Robertson: Following the example that Lebec and Matthews have set, it is now time for Al Robertson to announce that he is ... NOT a free agent!!! You see, I'm already in UEW and MWF, and I don't have any room left! (Al looks at these messages on the screen.) Robertson: Look at these people ... bragging about who they've beaten ... well, it's time for Al Robertson to brag about ... WHO HE'S LOST TO!!! Lesse ... Nevermind ... 3 times. The Candyman ... twice. Johnny Glamour ... twice. (Al looks up.) Robertson: Man, this is gonna take too long. Let me calculate my record! Lesse ... MUW - 2-0 IWC - 11-11-3 UEW - 1-1-0 PWU - 0-2-0 IeWC - 3-5-0 MWF - 0-0-0 Hmm ... that makes me ... 17-19-3!!! That's a losing record, folks. So ... if I was a free agent, I'd be saying ... HIRE AL ROBERTSON!!! PIN ME ... ... PAY ME!!! (Fade to a screen with the letters ... (c) 1998 No Gimmicks Necessary, Inc.) -- -Dan M.
