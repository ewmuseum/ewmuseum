[CRITIQUE] Mad Valentine flash...

11 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, Just thought I'd stick this up here, see if I'm as good as I think I am... /:-) And, although I'd be flattered by any offers, I'm currently _not_ looking for another fed. But thanks a bunch anyway.
Comments, criticisms, whatever, they're all appreciated. Oh, almost forget - this is just my latest flash for Global Pro-Wrestling
<blatant plug> which, I might add, is a top-class fed that any handler of any ability would be wise to take a good look at... </blatant plug>
[The scene opens outside a large, hotel building, deep in the heart of
Nashville, Tennessee. Pedestrians stroll about their daily business, enjoying the crispness of the morning. It's German-engineered engine purring as it glides into our field of vision, a long, black car pulls up to the curb, slowing to a halt. A door opens, and out steps the figure of Kingsley
Sweete, dressed in a fine, silver-grey suit beneath a big, black overcoat that engulfs his tiny frame.]
[As Sweete tells the driver to which room he should have their luggage sent, his long-time buddy, and the GPW's 'Smoking Barrel', Mad Valentine, slides out of the back seat on his rear end, the foot of his injured right leg elevated. One crutch, and then another appears as well, and, as he gets out of the car, MJV uses them to push himself upright. He is sharply dressed, as always, in a black, round-necked Italian suit, over a matching, white satin shirt, and he hobbles, somewhat awkwardly, up the carpeted steps in front of the hotel. As his agent skips up to join him, a uniformed doorman performs his duty, and the two step inside.]
[The hotel's lobby is plush, mainly based around white marble. There is a pleasant lull in the room, perhaps three or four other people besides
Valentine and his advisor, all seeming very relaxed under the chandeliered lighting. The pair spend a split-second admiring the lobby, before approaching the reception desk, where a young woman, with soft, brown hair and striking blue eyes flashes them a pearly smile and asks...]
[RECEPTIONIST]: "May I help you at all, sirs?" [SWEETE]: "Err, yes - we have a reservation. Valentine." [The young lady begins tapping away at her computer, with the 'Smoking
Barrel' still glancing around the impressive foyer...]
[RECPETIONIST]: "Here we are, sir. Room 112. Here are your keys..." [She hands the two cards to Kingsley...] [RECEPTIONIST]: "Your room is on the second floor. First on the left, right after the elevator. Enjoy your stay..."
[SWEETE]: "Thank you." [With a smile and a nod, both men head across the lobby to an elevator. Sweete presses the button to call it, and the two wait patiently as the lights above the doors begin to flash...]
[SWEETE]: "Nice place, huh?" [VALENTINE]: "Certainly is, Kingsley. But, as nice as it is for an able-bodied person like you, it doesn't cater too well for members of the disabled community, such as my good self."
[SWEETE]: "Disabled?" [VALENTINE]: "Yes - disabled! Did you see the number of steps outside? How is an invalid like me supposed to manage all those steps without hurting themselves?"
[SWEETE]: "Err..." [VALENTINE]: "Exactly." [With a 'ping!', the elevator finally arrives. The doors slide open, and two businessmen step out into the foyer, as Mad Valentine and his agent take their place. Sweete reaches across, and, with the press of a button, the doors close and they begin to rise up to the second floor.]
[VALENTINE]: "So, did you call GPW? Did you tell them the match was off?" [He nods in the affirmative...] [VALENTINE]: "So, what did they say?" [SWEETE]: "Err... well, they're not too - how shall I put this? - they're not too _convinced_ by your injury..."
[VALENTINE]: "Not convinced! What do they want from me? I was carried out of the show on a stretcher, for chrissakes! I spent over an hour at the hospital, getting this knee checked out! I've been hobbling around like Long
John frickin' Silver on these crutches all week - and they reckon I'm putting it on?! What's wrong with these morons?"
[SWEETE]: "Well, they want a doctor's report, M-J. They say that, unless you can show them a doctor's report confirming your injury, then you are
_contractually required_ to defend the title against Shadowman on Sunday..." [Valentine, quite clearly, is not very impressed...] [VALENTINE]: "Y'know - that's the worst part of it. 'Scary Man'. The fact that they want to steal the MJV Championship away from me is bad enough, but the thought of that pathetically inept, hopelessly untalented mid-carder with my belt around his waist is enough to make me cringe. Beyond a shadow of a doubt, Kingsley, no matter how tainted his victory might be, losing my title to that retard would be the greatest embarrassment of my career, not to mention an embarrassment to the sport of wrestling in general..."
[Sweete nods in agreement, as the doors slide open and the two men step out into the finely-decorated, plastic-fauna-riddled corridor.]
[VALENTINE]: "I can't believe that they're doing this to me - their number-one man, the man solely responsible for the continuing success of their two-cent federation. After all I've done for them, all the ratings
I've boosted, all the cards I've saved with my very appearance, they're still determined to screw me over at every opportunity. I don't know why I bother sometimes..."
[SWEETE]: "First on the right? Second on the left? What was it she said?" [He glances at the number on the keys...] [SWEETE]: "A-ha. 112. Here we are..." [VALENTINE]: "Say Kingsley - did you remember to call Carl and Vic?" [SWEETE]: "Sure I did, M-J. I told them it was 'business as usual for 'Team
Ego' this weekend', just like you said to..."
[He slides the key into the door, as Valentine allows a wry smile to slither across his clean-shaven face. He smoothes a hand over his perfectly-styled, short dakr hair, as the door swings open...]
[VALENTINE]: "Good, good. Is there a lightbulb above my head, Kingsley?" [SWEETE]: "What... have you had an idea?" [VALENTINE]: "Of course I've had an idea, imbecile. And if we can pull it off, it'll show everyone - especially those front office cretins - that
'Team Ego' plays by their own rules. Here's what we do..."
[He begins to explain the details of his plan to Sweete, as the two men step into Room 112, closing the door behind them. The screen fades to black...]
