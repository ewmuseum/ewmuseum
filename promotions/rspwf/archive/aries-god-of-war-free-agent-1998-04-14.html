Aries "God of War" - free agent

14 April 1998

Several months ago I came to seek a new fed, many of you offered me oppurtunities but nobody was able to impress me. Right now
Aries is participating in the NEFW where he currently holds the NEFW Pete's
Wicked Ale PPV Title. He is also in a small fed called the TDWF where he is the current Heavyweight Champion. I myself have been around for almost a year, most noticeable I run the NWC affiliate fed Golden State Wrestling which hosted it's first PPV yesterday, Opening the Doors. Aries is also participating in the World Cup of Wrestling Main Event hosted by former NECW presidnet NECW. I am looking to expand Aries to one last fed where he can make himself at home. I will only come to your fed if you can really impress me and you have been up for awhile, NO NEW FEDS NO MATTER WHAT! I want to start at the rock bottom of a fed with a solid foundation and try and work my way to the top. There is a sample roleplay I just wrote below, if interested please e-mail me at
[detail withheld] (camera dawns in on a man standing a top a hillside in the cold and dark night. The mountains overcast the setting as the standout beyond the eye can see and a man dressed in black lurks in front of the camera. The coyotes can be seen in a near distance howling upon the moon as the little light in the sky gleams upon the feet of this mysterious man. The creatures of the night surround this individual as he looks into the heavens above as a large shadow covers his identity. The man steps into the light to reveal himself as)
. . . Aries "The God of War" . . . (Aries is standing with his black trench coat covering his colf body. Through an opening near is waist his NEFW Pete's Wicked Ale PPV Title can be seen and it reflects the moon's light. His black wavy hair is slicked back and it falls upon his shoulders as stares into yonder and leaving his mind blank.
Aries looks straight into the lens of the camera and speaks his first words) Aries: A pcture perfect night has fallen upon the earth as nightness has taken over. The dark skies touch my body filling me with coldness beyond imagine.
As I stand here with my title around my waist I think to myself, who will be the next one in line to feel my arms squeeze their soul to unworthy death. As
I look into the heavens above I see myself formed by the stars above me and I wonder why the world has brought this upon themselves.
(Aries begins to move towards his *infamous* oak tree with multiple slashings, broken branches, leaves fluttered near the trunk and old roots caressing the ground.)
Aries: This tree, it has suffered numerous battles yet it still stands upon the earth and you wonder what this has to do with me. I've said it before and
I'll say it again, like the tree, I am here to stay. You can pound me into the ground, you can break my arms, you can tear the skin right off my body, but the fact of the matter is I'm not going anywhere. I have been blessed into this world to teach you all the ways of life, my ways of life. I am sick of seeing all these men brag about winning, whine about losing, and kiss up to those that hold their careers in their hands. That's the difference between the world and myself, I hold my own future, and only I can change it for the better, of for the worse.
(Aries begins to walk again as he reaches a long narrow path created by stones and and rocks. Aries takes his first step onto the path and he speaks again.)
Aries: I have taken the first step to a new beginning. I have reached phase one of this new plan. As I watch the new generation of wrestlers scatter into new cities and purchase houses in their new homes I am reminded of how it once was in wrestling, and in life. Yes, it is my duty to torcher, execute, and or kill anything and anyone who imerges into my path, but is it my wrong doing if
I hurt someone who invades my territory. Is it my fault if someone did not listen to me. I can't help it if hundreds of nieve men jump into the first band wagon they see. It's their own fault when the look up to see that the driver of their ride, of their careers, is myself and now I hold them in my hands. Am I suppose to let it go, let them be, NO! I take their bodies, their souls, and their careers and I place them in my hands and I shatter their dreams, I ruin their fantasies, I crush their hopes of being the best they can be. It is my job! And I'm gonna damn well keep doing it the only way I can! With power, complete control, and for eternity, Total Anniliation is near, so look to the skies and the heavens above but only one name will ever be remembered, and that is the name of Aries, The God of War!!!
