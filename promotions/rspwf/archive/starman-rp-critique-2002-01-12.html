Starman RP Critique

12 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I know that RPs involving celebrities usually don't go over well. But idiot that I am, I thought I'd try it...and then came up with the idea to cram celebrities into every RP for a week.
So here's the first, and possibly last, if it doesn't go over well, installment of Starman Celebrity week.
Oh, and here's a cheap join SEWF plug too. Please join SEWF http://sewfv2.cjb.net http://groups.yahoo.com/group/SEWFV2 And now, onto the RP. Thanks for reading. [We fade in to a hotel room, where, we find none other than Tony Quint, aka
STARMAN, along with his usual entourage of Unatraktiv Guhrlfrend, STARDOG, and "Tiny" Johnson...the group is sitting around playing cards...Tony is of course, as per usual, wearing his traditional non-wrestling attire of jeans, sneakers, and a t-shirt bearing a catchy slogan, in this case "I'm More Over
Than Ever." on the front, and "Is What I Would Say If I Was In A Hot Air
Balloon Above Someone Named Ever." on the back. Mr. Quint takes a moment away from playing cards to make a very important announcement.]
Tony: I'm going to call Lexi. Tiny: Are you going to call collect? Tony: You betcha. [Tony begins to dial.] Unatraktiv: Are you dialing of zero? Tony: Of course...what else would I... [The door crashes down as Mr. T enters the fray.] Mr. T: Damn Sucka! I pity the fool who dial zero! Tony: Woah! It's Clubber Lang! Tiny: HOLY COW! IT'S B.A. Barrakas! Mr. T: No son, I'm Mista T! Tony: I said you're Clubber Lang! Mr. T: Anyway Foo...whatchyou doin' dialin' zero!?!?! Tony: I'm making a collect call, sir. [This statement sends Mr. T into a frenzy as he yanks the phone right out of the wall, ripping the cord right out of the phone, as it dangles from the jack.]
Tony: What'd you do that for!?!?!? Mr. T: You tryin' to dial zero foo! Tony: Wouldn't it have been a better idea to give me another option as opposed to tearing the phone out of the hotel wall.
Mr. T: I didn't think that far ahead. [In walks Dolph Lundgren dressed in a bellhop getup.] Dolph: I must break you. Tony: WOAH! It's IVAN DRAGO! Tiny: HOLY COW! IT'S ONE OF THE UNIVERSAL SOLDIERS! Dolph: I am Dolph Lundgren. Tony: Who? Dolph: Dolph Lundgren. Tony: So you're not Ivan Drago? Dolph: I played him in Rocky IV. Tony: I'm confused. Are you Drago or not? Unatraktiv: He ees saying of heem portraying Drago in feelm. Tony: Oh. So you're not Drago. [Dolph smacks his hand on his head.] Stardog: BARK! Tony: Yeah, good question...what's a guy who may or may not be star of
Rocky IV Ivan Drago doing working in a place like this?
Dolph: My career has gone downhill. I am a bellhop here now. And...when we saw Mr. T here rip out the phone, on our survailance cameras, I was sent to take care of him.
Mr. T: I pity the fool who try to rough up Mista T! Dolph: I was the more popular Rocky character. Mr. T: No way sucka! Clubba Lang was the man! Tony: There's only one way to settle this. Mr. T: You're right sucka. Dolph: I agree. [We fade out...and fade back in, the time elapsed is about 1 hour...Mr. T is sitting staring at the TV, as is Dolph Lundgren...on the TV...Rocky III.]
Dolph: I must agree, Clubber Lang is a superior character. Mr. T: Yeah sucka, but Ivan Drago was a tough guy too. And we're both better than... [What's left of the door breaks down as Hulk Hogan enters the room.] Hulk: Ya know somethin' Mr. T, brother...I am the man that made Rocky III. Rocky III wouldn't have been anything without Thunderlips and the 24 inch pythons. Tony: WOAH! It's Mr. NANNY!!!!!! Tiny: It's Santa... Unatraktiv: With having of muscles! Hulk: No way dudes and dudette, I'm The Hulkster, straight out of Venice
Beach, California, I rode my Harley all the way here...
Tony: From Venice Beach? Hulk: Yep. I was saying prayers, training, and eating vitamins all the way. Tony: But Suburban Commando, there's an ocean between here and Venice
Beach, how in the heck did you ride over it?
Hulk: I rode underwater dude. Because I'm the Hulkster. Tony: Wouldn't you have died, run out of oxygen? And I find it hard to believe that even a fine piece of american machinery such as a Harley
Davidson could have gone underwater, and still functioned properly. Hulk: Woah! I just heard someone in New Zealand say that they didn't like
Thunder In Paradise...we'll finish this discussion another time DUDE.
[Hulk departs.] Tony: I don't like him. Mr. T: Ain't noone does, sucka! [A small radio on Dolph Lundgren's belt comes to life.] Dolph: I have to go. There is baby vomit on the third floor. [Exit Dolph.] Tony: Well looks like it's just you and us again Clubber. Mr. T: My name ain't Clubba...foo. Tony: Alright, well in spite of the fact that I no longer have the option, how exactly should I dial a collect call.
Mr. T: It's real simple...sucka...just dial 1-800-C-O-L-L-E-C-T...save whoeva you're callin' a buck or two.
Tony: But really, is a buck worth dialing the extra ten digits? It's not even my buck, or two. Anyone who I'm calling can probably afford the cash...
[The last shard of door falls down, as, in a puff of smoke, Alyssa Milano enters the room.]
Tony: It's Samantha Micelli! Tiny: No way! It's one of the witches from Charmed! Alyssa: Actually, I'm... Unatraktiv: Eva Saving Lots yes? Alyssa: No, I'm Alyssa Milano... Stardog: BARK! WOOF! GRRRRUFFFF! Tony: You saw her what? Stardog: BARK! Tiny: In a movie? SWEET! Stardog: BARK! Tony: Wow... Alyssa: I try to forget that. But I looked really hot, didn't I? Stardog: BARK! RUFF! GRRRRRUFFF! BARK! Alyssa: Listen, it's very important that you dial 1-800-C-O-L-L-E-C-T for all your collect calls. Zero is easier, but we're cheaper, and your other alternatives are either Terry Bradshaw or Carrot Top endorsed.
Tony: Carrot Top! WOW! What one does he endorse!?!?!?! Alyssa: Never mind that. Look at my boobs. Tony: Well, I really shoul... Tiny: OK. I will. [Tiny stares at the incredibly hot Alyssa Milano.] Tony: Well, Mike Masters, pretty soon, I'm going to be facing you down, and well, you're probably going to be kicking my butt. But you know what, that's OK, because while you've got an attractive lesbian in the form of
Narcissa with you, I've got an attractive brunette starlet who doesn't know my name, and an Unatraktiv Guhrlfrend with me.
Plus I've got Clubber Lang. So there. And if all works as I hope it will, even after Samantha and Clubber leave, and our match is through, I'll have that Unatraktiv Guhrlfrend...
AND...my North American Championship Belt. There's no question that you're one heck of an athlete, and a great technical wrestler, and that I'm probably not exactly in the upper echelon of athletics, OR technical wrestling, but really, that doesn't mean that I can't beat you. And it doesn't mean that it will be luck.
Sometimes the lesser man, lesser team, whatever, digs down, and finds that little bit of something extra, and does the job that they need to do.
You've given me all kinds of incentive when your manager stole my Ughleey
Guhrlfrend. Mister Masters, I'm not really happy with you, but darnit, this week Michael, I'm not going to take it out on you. Nah, it's not worth it.
If Ughleey is happy, that's all that matters to me. So this week, I will represent the continent of North America, on a completely different continent...I'm doing it for me, I'm doing it for my dog, for my "Tiny" Johnson, and my Unatraktiv Guhrlfrend...
And I'm doing it for... THE PEOPLE! Because no matter the language, no matter the dialect, there is one thing that people from Montana to Moscow understand...one thing that they will never, EVER, question...one thing that they know deep down in their hearts, and one thing that will be your downfall.
Tony Quint has the world's Smallest Package! Alyssa: That's a shame. You're kinda cute. Tony: Huh? Alyssa: It's a shame about you having that small package and all. C'mon
Clubber... Mr. T: I pity da foo' who gots a small package! Alyssa: So why do they call you Clubber anyway? Mr. T: Because I ain't got a small package. Alyssa: Mmmmm... [Mr. T and Alyssa Milano exit.] Tony: What was that all about? Stardog: BARK! Tony: She thought what? Stardog: RUFF! Tony: You're saying that she thought I was cute...and then...thought that...OH GOD NO! SAMANTHA MICELLI!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Tony runs out the door in pursuit.] Stardog: BARK! Tiny: I guess that means... Unatraktiv: We are fadeeng of out. [PEACETHEFADEOUT]
