<Scene> 1am outside of a shady bar near Upper Manhattan. A man is standing int he shadows of a lampost with a 40oz. in hand looking at a cd case..he laughs softly....
Man: Hmm...My name?... <he looks up..> <walks out of the shadows..> Man: My name is....Slim Shady... Slim Shady: I find quite ironic how a a new rap star has the same name I do..I guess when you're as popular as I am, people are bound to rip your name off. I like his song though, and hell, since we practically share the same name..even though I have it copyrighted..in some ways..it might as well be my music...well..from now on after...
Slim: Now with all of these pre-pubesent punks running around claiming they're the s*beep* in the world o fProfessional Wrestling, it's time for Mr.Shady to step his white ass on out and prove once again that I still am the damn best thing since sliced bread, 'cause baby, as my friend The Smooth Operater (#189 on
RSPWF Ttop 200) "I ain't foreplay baby, I'm the whole damn orgasm." Had to give my brudda a plug, the guy deserves it. Anyway, now with all of these crack-head , punk-upstarts running around, there ain't a damn other thing I have to do other than announce my free agency. So if you need one of the damn best in the whole industry, you got my number, and if ya' don't....look it up in da'
pages.... <Scene> He steps back into the shadows as a group of people walk out of the bar and into the dimly lit street...
