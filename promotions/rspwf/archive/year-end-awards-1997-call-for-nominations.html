R.S.P.-W.F. 1997 Year End Awards Call for Nominations

27 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello RSP-WF! It's [name withheld] here again. For those of you who don't know and do care, I'm the Cyber Federation of Professional Wrestling and the RSP-WF Awards Tabulator. Last year, I ran a relatively successful awards, in my opinion. This year, the kinks have been removed, I hope, and two categories have been dropped while several have been added. The categories of Best Wrestling Show and Most Overrated Wrestler are both gone. The Best Wrestling show was split into two categories, Best Weekly Wrestling Show and Best Supercard or Pay Per View. The Most Overrated category was dropped for several reasons. The main ones were that these awards celebrate the successes; there is enough bad in e-wrestling we do not need to honor it. The new categories, I hope, will make the awards more complete and honor many more wrestlers. These new categories are Most Improved Wrestler, Best Rookie, Best Jobber, Most Exciting News Item of The Year, Best Interviewer, Best Weekly Wrestling Show, Best Supercard or Pay Per View, and Best Federation Head of the Year. These categories will be explained along with the old categories below. The process of these awards are as follows: a. Discussion with RSP-WF about changes to awards (December '96 & September '97) b. Nominations for recipients (now through midnight GST October 9, '97) c. Call for votes (?October 16, '97? through midnight GST November 11, '97) d. Votes tabulated & posted to RSP-WF (before December 23, '97) e. Discussion for 1998 Awards (after posting of awards through whenever discussion dies down) With a total of twenty-seven categories, up six from last year, the awards will be the probable climax of the rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy year. Each award will be described in the following fashion: Award # Name of the Award Description of the award. Past Winners: 96: 1996 RSP-WF Award Winner HY: 1997 Half Year RSP-WF Award Winner For each category, you, the people of RSP-WF are allowed to submit nominees for the awards. To do so, you must send the category name, the wrestler's name, and known feds of that wrestler to me, [name withheld], at [detail withheld] Now to the categories... 1. Best Wrestler of the Year: The best all around e-wrestler, this considers workrate, interview skills, talent, role in angles, popularity of the character. Past Winners: 96: "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor HY: Survival & Caleb Temple (tie) 2. Best Tag Team of the Year The top two-e-wrestler team that takes the factors for Best Wrestler into consideration but adds teamwork. 96: Damage Incorporated HY: The Lethal Leaders 3. Best Face of the Year Best good guy e-wrestler. The character that is so nice he could only exist in e-wrestling. 96: Dan "Flash" Kauffman HY: "Hardcore" Matt Harris 4. Best Heel of the Year Best bad guy e-wrestler. The character that is so evil the street gangs cringe in fear. 96: Super Scott HY: Caleb Temple & Casey "Blackheart" James (tie) 5. Best Worker Most frequent wrestling e-wrestler, the character that gets more than his share of time in the ring, and often deserves it. 96: Dan "Flash" Kauffman HY: Caleb Temple 6. Best High-Flyer Best e-wrestler in the air. Shows talent bouncing off the ropes, leaping over the ropes, dropping from the turnbuckle, diving off the apron, and falling into absolutely anything. 96: Youth Gone Wild HY: "Enigma" Takezo Mushashi 7. Best Technical Wrestler Best e-wreslter with technical moves. The wrestler who has the most effective locks, weardown moves, and mat maneuvers. 96: Chris Degeau HY: Lord Byron 8. Best Brawler E-wrestler who is best with his fists, this wrestler will fight anyone with or without anything. 96: Otto "The Butcher" Verhoveon HY: Otto "The Butcher" Verhoveon & Survival (tie) 9. Most Popular Wrestler The fans of e-wrestling's favorite e-wrestler, the wrestler who shows the special quality that make him stand out from the pack. 96: "Dreamlover" Trey Porter HY: - NOT AWARDED - 10. Most Improved Wrestler The wrestler who over the last twelve months has improved in overall workrate, interview, talent, and popularity either positive or negative. 96: - NOT AWARDED - HY: - NOT AWARDED - 11. Best Rookie The wrestler, out of the thousands, who made his e-wrestling debut in the last twelve months and has shown the greatest potential. 96: - NOT AWARDED - HY: - NOT AWARDED - 12. Best Jobber The quasi-wrestler, often controlled by the fed-head which has the greatest success in making a rival greater than usual. 96: - NOT AWARDED - HY: - NOT AWARDED - 13. Best Wrestling Gimmick Best e-wrestling character. This wrestler has a well-defined character, giving excellent, consistent interviews, and holds true to his character in all cases. 96: The Awesome Alliance HY: - NOT AWARDED - 14. Best Wrestling Move The most creative or best move in e-wrestling, often a finisher. 96: Alex Kidd's Summersault Bulldog HY: - NOT AWARDED - 15. Best Match of the Year The best single confrontation between wrestler A and wrestler B. This match may stand out because of the stipulations or just the talent in the ring. 96: "Offensive" Alex Adams vs. "Dreamlover" Trey Porter (EWA's Offensive Dreams) HY: - NOT AWARDED - 16. Best Feud of the Year Best constant wrestler A and wrestler B battle over the year. This battle pulls in others and runs wild across at least one federation. 96: "Offensive" Alex Adams vs. "Dreamlover" Trey Porter (EWA) HY: - NOT AWARDED - 17. Most Exciting News Item of The Year The story in e-wrestling that showed the greatest interest and possibly strengthened the world of e-wrestling. 96: - NOT AWARDED - HY: - NOT AWARDED - 18. Most Disappointing News Item of The Year The item that upset you the most, this item brought the e-wrestling community closer and make it stronger as it remembers the disappointment. 96: "Dreamlover" Trey Porter leaving the EWA HY: - NOT AWARDED - 19. Best Manager of the Year Best character that accompanies the e-wrestler to the ring. The manager that increases the wrestler's advantage time and time again. 96: Cherry Cheesecake HY: - NOT AWARDED - 20. Best Announcer or Color Commentator Best character that adds entertainment or flavor to the matches of a federation. This character could be loudmouthed, wise, or just truthful. 96: Mr. Flawless HY: - NOT AWARDED - 21. Best Interviewee Best e-wrestler to be interviewed. The e-wrestler that puts up great answers to all the questions. 96: "Spotlight" Billy Shakespeare HY: - NOT AWARDED - 22. Best Interviewer The interviewer that can throw out the questions, no matter how controversial, and can take a hit or two during an interview. 96: - NOT AWARDED - HY: - NOT AWARDED - 23. Best Angle of the Year Best increase of suspense or excitement in e-wrestling. The angle that always left a little bit more to come and it was always better when it arrived. 96: [name withheld]'s shoots to make Super Scott the megaheel of all time HY: - NOT AWARDED - 24. Best Weekly Wrestling Show The event each week that stars new wars, continues old ones, and end others; it brings the federation together each week for great enjoyment. 96: EWA Sunday Night RARE HY: - NOT AWARDED - 25. Best Supercard or Pay Per View The event which wrestlers work towards; where excellent old feuds end and new feuds with great potential start, and something very special happens. 96: -NOT AWARDED - HY: EWA Extreme Dreams & IIWF Ringwars III (tie) 26. Best Federation Head of the Year The human being who puts in the effort like all the other federation heads but goes this one goes that extra mile to have the excellent cards, good angles, organization, and reliability. 96: - NOT AWARDED - HY: [name withheld] 27. Best E-Wrestling Federation of the Year The top e-wrestling fed in all of cyberspace. The marquee award. The Super Bowl, the World Series, or the Big Kahuna so to speak. 96: IIWF: International Internet Wrestling Federation HY: IIWF: International Internet Wrestling Federation ***** This has been the call for nominations...
