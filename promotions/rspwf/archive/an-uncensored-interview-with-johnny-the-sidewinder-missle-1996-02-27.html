an uncensored interview with Johnny "The Sidewinder" Missle

27 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to a small set. Two chairs are seated in the middle of the set and in one of them sits a middle aged man with short black hair. The man wears a white tuxedo and holds a picture of a man known greatly throughout ewrestling,noeother then Johnny Missle.) Man: Welcome e-wrestling fans,as you already know my name is Landon Little and I'll be your host. Today we will be greeted by the presence of one of the most amazing newcomers to the sport of e-wrestling,Johnny The Sidewinder Missle. (Bullet with Butterfly Wings by the Smashing Pumpkins plays loudly throughout the set. A 6'2" man with spiked and bleached hair emerges from the back. He is wearing a white lesther jacket with the words "Sidewinder" written on the back and also blue jeans) Little: Hell Johnny,it's nice to see you again! Missle: It's nice to be here Landon,y'know I remmebr you taking out those little losers when I was around the age of 13. I saw you win the UeWLoA World Title over the Bad Ass Punk(I just mad up both of those names so if they bear any resemblance to your fed or your wrestler I'm sorry) Little: (grinning wildly and loving the compliments from Missle) Well Missle,although I'd like to hear a few more we are here to talk about your goals,feuds etc.. for the listening pleasure of our fans. Well Johnny,I watched you make your first wrestling appearence in the WIWF,I saw you destroy your opponent and prove that you actually are worty of wrestling in the WIWF. One thing amazes me though,you hold a heel image there but in every other league you are a face. How do you explain that? Missle: Well Mssle there really is now "real" way of explaining this. That's like asking Razor Ramon why he's a face in the WWF but heel in the USWA or Jerry Lawler why he's a face in the USWA and heel in the WWF. It all goes back to deciding which gimmick will sell more. Looking at the roster of the WIWF I had decided that I'd like to play a "heel" character in the WIWF. Little: well let's talk about your WIWF career,you seem to be one of the rising stars there with your unblemished record of 3-0,you also claim that you were granted a North American Title shot. Missle: That's right,I was told that in the fufture I would get a North American Title shot. Well the WIWF has a high level of standards,I guess that's why they found me. I haven't really heard anything from the WIWF recently,I'm not really sure if it shut down or not. Little: Okay let's take a look at your WIWF,one of your first feuds in e-wrestling was against noneother then the Heartbrak Kid,although you two never met in the ring you attacked him numerous times. Missle: Well Landon,it all goes back to who's got the guts and who doesn't. You see I challenged The Heartbreak Kid,or I call him the Headache Kid numerous times. Did I get a reply,NO,I got NO REPLY! Little: Well let's go back to how that big feud started,a hear it was in an independent federation that only lasted a few months or so. The PCW right? Missle: That's right. You see I had a World Title shot on the first card that they held but I lost it in a gruesome battle with the Apocalypse. I came out of the match angry and was heckled by many superstars. If memory serves me correctly the Heartbreak Kid just happened to be one of those "superstars". I agreed to be in Juggernauts corner as he took on Heartbreak Kid. I was mainly there to stop any intereference from Miss L. A week or so before the big match I planned a "special" treat for our little friend the Heartbreak Kid. He was wrestling in the WIWF wrestler of the year tournament and during the opening rounds I ran into the ring and pounded away on him with brass knuckles. The next week I attacked him once again in the match against Juggernbaut in the PCW,but still no acceptance to his challenges. So Heartbreak Kid,if you're listening,NEXT WIWF CARD,ME AND YOU,ONE ON ONE!! Little: Okay well I here you quickly joined the GFWA after the WIWF where you have not had much success. Missle: Well you see the GFWA is a very hard wrestling league to wrestle in. I am not treated properly thus I don't give the fans hat they want to see. And everyone knows that when you don't put all of your heart into anything you can't win. I see the president begin showing a little more respect towards me and if this continues expect to see the Sidewinder holding the GFWA World Heavyweight Title soon. Matthew Dylan Taylor III,watch out cause the Sidewinder is comin' STRAIGHT AT YEAH BABY! Little: Another league I saw you make a big impact on was noneother then the CFWF. Tell us a little about your experiences tere but leave out the feuds,we'll save them for later! Missle: Well right after I joined the CFWF I joined a stable known as the Forces of Justice,we were an ensemble of almsot all fo the faces in the CFWF. I ended up beign one of the last two in the battle royal which means that I wrestled Mayhem in a Ladder match. We went all out in this match but ofcourse the Sidewinder proved to be too much for him. I defended it two more times again and then lost one match to him as a result of a countout because Alex Extreme snatched my title and took off to the dressing room. Alex Extreme and I were scheduled to meet in a ladder match to decide the real North American champion but his "friend" Frank Auston,the Bob Backlund of the CFWF ran out and caught me in a chicken wing. Finally the match happened and unfrotunatly I lost. But Alex Extreme,a word of advice to you,Spring Smash '96 you're going DOWN! Little: Okay let's return to the Alex Extreme feud. I hear that was voted best angle in the CFWF to date,any comments on that? Missle: Well obviously I'm not very proud of that angle,it lead to my loss of the North American Title so why would I be happy with it?!? Little: Well,what's that,I'm getting a message from my staff and I guess that's all the time I hve. It's been great meeting you once again Johnny and the best of luck you yah's in the future. (The screen changes to show numerous Johnny Missle clips including him grabbing the CFWF title by jumping off a ladder and on his way down hitting Mayhem in the head with. Then Johnny Missle pounds away on Heartbreak Kid with brass knuckle. The scene changes to show Johnny Missle holding the bloody brass knuckles infront of his face and smiling evilly. Finally yhe scene changes to show Johnny Missle jumping off of a ladder and missing the North American Title followed by Alex Extreme grabbing it.)
