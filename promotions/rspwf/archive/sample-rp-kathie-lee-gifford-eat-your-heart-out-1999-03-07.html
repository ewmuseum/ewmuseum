[SAMPLE RP] Kathie Lee Gifford, Eat Your Heart Out...

7 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in a dark and dirty sweat shop on Friday morning, March 5th. Dozens of young Filipino children are seen working with sewing machines, carrying heavy boxes around, and doing just about everything else that could be classified as "slave labor". "Killing Me Softly" by The Fugees begins to play over the intercom of the shop ... heard faintly over the workers doing their many jobs. A massive individual emerges from the small sea of sweat shop workers. Suprisingly enough, it's not Kathie Lee Gifford. It's none other than "The Giant Lovah" himself, Gambit. He looks around proudly at all of the young sweat shop employees, who are working diligently at whatever it is that they're being paid to do. Gambit looks directly into the camera and smiles...]
Gambit: Aw yeah. Your usual, run of the mill servants are a dime a dozen. They're all pretty much the same. They live to do their little tasks but they don't serve any particular importance in one's life. They're weak minded nothings with... aw yeah, baby ... low self esteem, minimal confidence, and no intelligence whatsoever. They're expendable ... like a disposal diaper... aw yeah ... something William O'Leary knows a lot about since I'm sure he used up plenty of 'em after he found out he was going one on one with "Smooth G" this
Sunday night.
[Gambit smiles wickedly as he turns around briefly ... to face the huge array of young workers behind him. He cups his hands to his mouth and shouts...]
Hurry it up, Felipe ... and you too, Manuel ... or... aw yeah ... there'll be no Tootsie Rolls for you two come dinner time.
[Gambit turns back around to face the camera ... like nothing ever happened...] Aw yeah ... you tried and tried and tried to get out of this match, Willie. At one point, you even offered me one of your sisters as a gift just so you wouldn't have to face "The Giant Lovah". Well... aw yeah... I'm sorry to inform you that ... first of all... I don't dig bearded chicks with hairy nipples so offering me your sister was _not_ the thing to do to get on my good side. And, secondly, I back down from no man ... especially a little cowardly twerp like you.
[Gambit smirks at the camera ... trying really hard to keep from laughing...] You boast about being having a three and zip win/loss record here in UEW. You brag about being on a huge winning streak. Well... aw yeah ... let's look at who you defeated in this illustrious streak of wins. Well, there's RAGE. Who in UEW _hasn't_ defeated this guy? He's laid down for more men than your own mother, Willie, my boy. Then... aw yeah ... there's Steve Manley... a man who's only claim to fame was being one of Nicolas Christian's Puppets. He's another guy who seems to find great pleasure in losing horribly in matches week after week.
[Gambit can't hold the laugh back any longer. He briefly laughs a hearty laugh before composing himself and continuing...]
Finally, for your third win, we have the "epitome of suck" ... mmm yeah ... the man I L.W.D.-ed on a car... Johnny "Sexy As Charles Nelson Reilly" Bod. Wanna know how important and prestigious _that_ win is? Aw yeah... I can sum the answer up in just two little words...
IT'S BOD. [Gambit chuckles and shrugs indifferently...] Need I say more? Aw yeah... I didn't think so. Sometimes ... like in the case of Johnny Bod ... the reputation precedes the actual competitor. The name
"Johnny Bod" is known throughout the entire wrestling world as a synonym for the words "loser", "nobody", "pushover", and... aw yeah... "homosexual goat pleasurer". Therefore... aw yeah... I don't think you really should be bragging about defeating the sorry likes of him.
[Pondering to himself for a moment, Gambit scratches his goatee...] Aside from the gloating over three less than stellar victories, Big Willie also decided to do what most do when the smell defeat coming their way ... aw yeah
... he started making petty insults.
[Gambit looks up at the ceiling of the shop in disgust as he speaks...] Aw yeah ... that silly silly man poked fun at my name. He made pathetic chess puns as he played chess with some crackly voiced teenage boy. Aw yeah ... man
... who do you think you are? Michael Jackson? "The Giant Lovah" is sickened by your obvious love for stupid puns ... and your even more obvious love for young baseball playin' boys.
On March 7th, at "Sunday Night Heat"... aw yeah ... in a warm-up for my big pay-per-view match in Mexico... [Gambit holds up one finger...] The winning streak of William O'Leary will be... aw yeah ... halted by the biggest, the most seductive, and the most ... mmmm yeah ... _dangerous_ man in
UEW. [ ... And he then holds up two fingers...] UEW's fastest starter will be... aw yeah, baby... _stopped_ once and for all
... and in record time, nonetheless.
[ ... And he then holds up three fingers and smiles smoothly at the camera.] L O V E W I L L D O M--- [Suddenly, Gambit is interrupted in midsentence when "Higher Ground" by the Red
Hot Chili Peppers begins to play over the intercom of the sweat shop. Gambit looks around the room, wide eyed and apparently shocked...]
Aw no ... it couldn't be! It can't be! It isn't! No! It is! It's... it's... aw yeah... THE MOST __ANNOYING__ ATHLETE IN SPORTS ENTERTAINMENT TODAY... T H E S C R E A M I N G M E A N I E ! [A wild eyed and prissy demeanored midget emerges from the crowd of hard working Filipino workers in the sweat shop. His hair is short and perfectly coifed and ... among other things in his wardrobe ... he is wearing an "I Love
The Osmonds!" T-shirt. He walks up to Gambit. Gambit tries his best to not burst out laughing...]
Screaming Meanie: You have _angered_ me, young man! You have caused my frilly panties to roll up in a bunch!
[Gambit looks on, somewhat suprised and _totally_ amused...] Gambit: Calm down, man. Aw yeah ... you need some ritalin. Screaming Meanie: Aw yeah! Aw yeah! AW YEAH! That's all you say! [Meanie starts pointing at Gambit's chest...] I AM A __FORMER UEW TELEVISION TAG TEAM CHAMPION__, BOY!! I lost those titles only because my partner had the motivation of a rotting grape!
Gambit: Ummm ... what does that have to do with anything? [He continues to point at Gambit's huge muscular chest as Gambit looks on, confused but still terribly amused by Meanie's actions...]
Screaming Meanie: SHUT THE HELL -- OH DARN! I SAID THE "H" WORD! I'M GONNA
GET THROWN OUT OF REVOLUTION FOR BEING VULGAR! Can't be swearin' like that!
Special K'll throw me out! Can't be the "Mickey Mouse Club" of wrestling saying "Hell" like that!! OH DARN! I said it again! There goes my Disney movie viewing privileges for this week!
[Gambit scratches his head ... looking down at the annoying midget...] Gambit: Aw yeah, man ... are you finished? Screaming Meanie: No sir! I am most certainly _not_ finished! Gambit: Welp ... now you are ... you little wildebeest... [Gambit grabs the Screaming Meanie by the head ... pushing him off-camera. A loud crash is soon heard off-camera ... followed by a girlish scream. Gambit grins a wicked grin at the camera. He laughs for a few moments before going all serious, staring directly into the camera with a cold stare...]
As I was saying ... whether it be when I stand chest to head with O'Leary ... or when I'm getting "scientific" with Jason Keening... aw yeah... L O V E W I L L D O M I N A T E ! ! [Gambit raises his eyebrow suspiciously...] Aw yeah ... you both will be going down, boys. And ... in case you didn't know
... when you go down at the hands of "Smooth G"...
I T ' S A L O N G W A Y D O W N ! ! [Gambit grins sarcastically...] And, in case you _didn't_ realize, O'Leary ... when I serve you with the most brutal beating of your life this weekend... aw yeah... IT'LL BE SERVICE WITH A
_SMILE_!
[Gambit turns around to face all of the workers...] CHOP CHOP!! Hurry it up with the dresses! Aw yeah ... my new boo needs a vast wardrobe to choose from... aw yeah ... when she makes her big entrance with
"The Giant Lovah" this Sunday night...
[Fade to black.]
