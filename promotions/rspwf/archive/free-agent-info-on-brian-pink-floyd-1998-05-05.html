Free Agent Info On Brian "Pink" Floyd

5 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I'm currently without a federation and I'm looking for one to get involved with. I don't have a current RP for this character because I just made him up. He used to be "Brutal" Brian Floyd but I wanted to try a different gimmick with him. If you're interested email me please.
(Wrestler_Name) Brian "Pink" Floyd (Referred to most often as "Pink" Floyd and sometimes Brian Floyd. He is never referred to as Brian "Pink" Floyd in a match because it sounds kinda dumb if you say it out loud.)
(From) Lawton, Oklahoma (Height) 6'5 (Weight) 245 lbs. (Alignment) Neutral (Strengths) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- 1) Extremely agile 2) Extremely powerful 3) Excellent mat wrestler --------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Weaknesses) --------------------------------------------------------------------------- 1) Cockiness 2) Showboating 3) Failure to respect other wrestler's abilities --------------------------------------------------------------------------- (Style) Brian is a 6'5, 245 pound man full of power. He does all kinds of powerful moves (ex. all sorts of suplexes, slams, etc.) He is also good on the mat, using all sorts of wrestling holds and even having the knowledge to escape them if they're put on him.
(Finisher) Pink Dreams (Sleeper) (Music) "Pretty In Pink" By The Psychedelic Furs (Entrance) [ The lights dim and "Pretty In Pink" By The Psychedelic Furs blasts over the arena sound system as Brian "Pink" Floyd emerges from the wrestler's entrance. He has on bright, neon pink wrestling tights with the word PINK in white, jagged lettering on the side of his right leg and the word
FLOYD in the same style on the side of his left leg. Pink and white fireworks explode and a spotlight follows him as he walks down the aisle, modeling for the crowd and slapping a few hands along the way. He arrives at the ring climbs in through the top and middle ropes. He then goes to the center of the ring as the white spotlight changes to pink and it begins to move about the ring. He holds his hands up to the crowd and is answered by a mixture of cheers and boos. The lights come on, the spotlight ceases, and "Pink" Floyd walks over to his corner. ]
(Apperance) Brian is a rather handsome man. His high cheeck bones, sharp chin, and dark blue eyes make out his good looking face. He also has brown hair with blonde streaks in it, and it curls around the ears and at the back. He wears bright, neon pink wrestling tights and white wrestling boots to the ring. He has PINK in white, jagged lettering down one leg of his tights and FLOYD in the same lettering on the opposite leg.
(History) Brian has competed in the ASW and LAW under the name "Brutal" Brian
Floyd. Those feds were biased and he didn't see himself moving along as he thought he should. He has undergone a new gimmick and hopes for better luck in the FASW.
(Manager) None
