A roleplay to critique from Destruction [XBWL]

27 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, before I post the roleplay, let me explain some stuff. There's probably a tag team in some big league somewhere named Destruction. I didn't rip them. I've been running this team for 4 years. I hope no one thinks I stole their gimmick or anything.
They are currently a face team (it is undecided whether they are the top face team in the XBWL, but it would be cool if they were). The team members are Maximum (big guy, powerhouse technical wrestler) and Wildman (little guy, luchadorish wrestler from Puerto Rico, fluent in English).
This roleplay is from the X-tremely Brutal Wrestling League (XBWL, [detail withheld] or [detail withheld] for information on joining) leading up to an XBWL Unified Tag Team Title Match between Destruction and the current champions, Disgruntled Apporations (Icegod and Corsair), at the
XBWL Halloween Hauntings PPV.
Wildman has been previously hurt, and on a previous card was told not to enter the ring during a three way dance for this title shot (Maximum basically had to go it alone). He interjected himself into the match anyway, helping his team win, and recieved a penality of a 30 day suspension and a $5,000 fine.
Maximum was able to talk to the front office and get this stipulation added to the match: If Destruction wins the match, the suspension is lifted, but until the next PPV, they must take _any and all comers_. This means that if three different teams ask for a title shot in one card, Destruction must face them all.
If Destruction fails to capture the titles, both Maximum and Wildman must serve the suspension, retroactive to 10/26/1999.
Any critiques and comments are welcome. Thanks in advance. -----XBWL----- DESTRUCTION Maximum & Wildman <The camera fades in to a close up of a box with "5000.00" written in it. As the camera pulls back, it can be identified as a check from William
Raiñez, aka Wildman. His street address is blurred from the check. A pen comes down upon the check and Wildman signs his name to it, then tears it from the check book. The camera continues pulling back as Wildman puts the check in an envelope, closes it, and seals it. Wildman is wearing a white sleeveless t-shirt, blue jeans, and his hair tied back. He is sitting at a fold up table across from a man in a black suit wearing a blue and black striped tie, the letters "XBWL" running down its length. Wildman folds the check and throws it at this man.>
Man: Hey, I'm just doing my job. I can't help it that you broke the rules. Wildman: Yeah, whatever. See you in St. Louis. <Wildman leaves the room. The camera cuts to outside the room where Maximum stands waiting. The door he is standing by is labeled by a piece of XBWL stationary taped to the door reading "Accounting - Payments and Fines" in simple black marker. Maximum is wearing a simple grey t-shirt that streches across his massive chest, blue jeans _NOT_ made by Tommy Hilfiger (notice how too many wrestlers wear Tommy :P), his XBWL "X" hat, tennis shoes,a sliver-banded sports watch, and a bandage over his nose. As Wildman opens the door and storms out of the room, Maximum has to pick up his stride to catch up.>
Maximum: Hey, it was just a mistake you made. No big deal right? Wildman: It's not that easy to part with that much money, Maximum. Do you understand that?
Maximum: Yes, I do. Wildman: Then let me be mad for a minute. Maximum: Think you can keep that up until Sunday? Wildman: I don't know. That just gets me thinking about what Corsair did to me, getting me thrown out of the arena.
Maximum: Yeah, well. So, are we getting on the road now? Wildman: I suppose, how far do we have to go? Maximum: God, forever, man. Directions say about 20 hours by car. Wildman: So we're stopping, right? Maximum: Definately. <Maximum and Wildman walk off camera. The camera cuts to a driving scene, and focuses on a sign that says "Welcome to Minnesota" as it goes by quickly. The camera pans to the front seat, where Maximum sits at the wheel and Wildman is in the passenger's seat.>
Maximum: So, what about Disgruntled Apporations? We got them at the pay-per-view. You're finally cleared to wrestle, and I've worked something out with Austrailian.
Wildman: Wonderful, you know, I'm getting a little sick of dealing with him here lately. Maximum: Yeah, but listen to this, if we win the titles tomorrow, then you don't have to serve your suspension, but we have to take on all takers when we defend the belts.
Wildman: So? We do that anyway? Maximum: You don't understand, man. I'm talking about ALL takers. That means we may have to wrestle every match on a card. If DA comes out Tuesday to demand a rematch, we have to accomidate them. If HI wants a title shot the same night, they get one as well. Then IA2 and the Jive Tribe come out wanting a three way, that's another match. You see where I'm going with this?
Wildman: Umm, that's not good. What if we lose? Maximum: Well, if we lose, then we both serve the sentence, retroactive to yesterday. Wildman: Damn, tough bag. You think we can handle it? Maximum: All depends on if we can handle Disgruntled Apporations. Man,
I've been told I'm good, and I don't want to sound concieted or anything, but I'm not too bad a wrestler, but I can't take the both of them on my own.
As much as I bragged about it a month ago, I can't do it. I'm gonna need you at 100% if we're gonna win those belts.
Wildman: So what do you want me to do? Maximum: Simply, we hit the gyms when we stop today, and every day in St. Louis. Light stuff to keep your knee in check. I don't want that joker buckling on me at the 3 minute mark. You've got to be ready to go the long haul, you with me?
Wildman: Yeah. Man, that's gonna suck though. I've been trying to keep away from the pain pills, and it hurts all the time. Kinda like I'm having arthritis problems.
Maximum: Hopefully that'll go away in a day or two. Wildman: Yeah, that's what the doctor said. Anyway, when are we gonna pull over for some food, man? I'm starving!
Maximum: Next exit is thirty miles, it'll be a while. Wildman: I'm gonna sleep till then man. Talk to you when we stop. Maximum: Cool. <Wildman puts some headphones on, reclines his seat back, and closes his eyes, trying to sleep.>
Maximum: Disgruntled Apporations Sunday night. A lot is on the line with this match. I could use the time off with the suspension, but I just can't stand not being able to compete for that long. Then again, if we win, I'm gonna get more competition than I ever dreamed of. I can't let that affect me. We've got to focus on getting those belts back. More importantly, we've got to get back at Icegod and Corsair for personal reasons. Corsair wants to come in and blame Wildman and me for losing the tag straps back when I tagged with Icegod? Damn, I mean, it was his fault in the first place, distracting our matches and blaming piddly little attacks on us.
Then we lose the belts, lose the opportunity to go for the belts together again, and Icegod wants to blame the whole thing on me, when he was the one who kicked me in the chin. We'll show 'em. Take those belts and let's see how much they want to run their mouths then.
