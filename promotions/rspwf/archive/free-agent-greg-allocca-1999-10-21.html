[Free Agent] Greg Allocca

21 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've posted before, but usually all I get is brand new feds who have between 0-3 wrestlers. Now, I don't mind giving a new fed a shot[as long as it has already started or is going to start within the next week or two...I wanna play SOON] although I prefer something that's been around for a few months.
My character is "Sexual Blueberries" Greg Allocca. And the name is just there for humor value, he is NOT a former olympic weightlifter or a sex addict.
Anyway, I'm looking for a league that focuses on angles just as much, if not more than roleplays. Any kind of flash system is fine.
A little background info: Greg is your average everyday freak. He has long blue hair[surfer style], and a chin goatee. Nicely built. On a regular basis he's easy going, and loves to come up with crazy ideas. BUT when things get personal he becomes a very violent person capable of doing very violent things. His major weakness is females...he just can't get enough of the opposing gender, and this often costs him matches, but is also the whole reason he's in the wrestlig business. In his own words "I'm here for two reasons....to get paid, and to get laid."
Now, here's an rp I did when I was with WAR! Not my best work, but not my worst either. [The scene opens to the front of some house. The door flies open, and Greg
Allocca steps out from behind it. In a pair of nylon shorts, and a white
t-shirt. Toxic Shock walks up to him.] Toxic: What're you doing Greg? Greg: Going for my morning jog. Toxic: What!? You mean The Reverend Doctor has decided not to help you out? Greg: Yeah, he's helping me. But I like to jog, so we figured a way where I can jog, and then put all those calories right back on.
Toxic: How is that? Greg: I jog to McDonald's right across the street from it is IHOP, and than another block down is a pizza place. So, I can jog there....eat up at least half the menu from each place, and than take a cab back home make a sunday, and watch some Wrestlevision.
Toxic: Ah, I see. Greg: It slows down my weight gaining a little bit, but he says after I put on some pounds...I'll get tired of jogging.
Toxic: Well, that probably is the case, but enough about this. You've got a
Liberty Title defense coming up against Paul Novak. He is a member of Ground
Zero. A group of people you haven't exactly made friends with by assosciating with Mr. Bennett.
Greg: BAH! Paul Novak is a wuss! He ran away from some chump named Seth
Blitzspear. Can you believe that? Seth isn't such a scary guy. I could probably take him in about 45 seconds.
Toxic: *laughing* You? Take on Seth Blitzspear in 15 seconds? Greg: Hey, it's not funny! You don't think I can do it? I'm almost a full 200 lbs now! Toxic: *laughing* Do you know how powerful Blitzspear is? Greg: Well, not really. Toxic: I can't wait to see what happens when you fight him. He'll knock the blue out of your hair with one move.
Greg: Nope, it's guaranteed to last at least 2 weeks! Anyway, I'm not scheduled to fight Seth. Let's get back on topic, Paul Novak. He's a wuss.
Toxic: Well, I doubt he will be anywhere near as frightened of you as he was of
Blitzsspear.
Greg: What's that supposed to mean? Toxic: Nothing, you just don't have the frame to make someone wet their pants at the sight of you.
Greg: Your right, I'm nowhere near as masculine as...let's say NOVAK'S MAMA! But I'm working on it. Anyway, I don't know too much else about Novak. He's one of those silent boys you know. The kind whose parent's touched them in those "secret places".
Now, he shares that joy with all his male friends in Ground Zero. You know, I was in the showers the other day and I saw Nomad and....well, this isn't a story for television. Hehehehe.
Anyway, no matter how good Novak is...I can promise you that I'm much better. I mean, I've only got one blemish on my record. And that was all due to a major case of scalp-infection.
Toxic: Oh, please. Just accept your loss like a man, and stop whining. Greg: I DON'T WHINE! Toxic: Are we finished here? Greg: No, I'm not....I've got a few more... [A tall blond jogs by Greg Allocca.] Greg: You saw the way she checked me out? Toxic: She didn't seem to notice you. Greg: Your just jealous. I gotta go catch that chick! I'M GONNA GET ME SOME TONIGHT! [Fade to black....nope not the Metallica song.] "Sexual Blueberries" Greg Allocca WAR! (1-0) WCCW (1-0) I AM THE BULLGOD! "It took god 6 days to create the Earth. On the 7th, he rested. On the 8th day....God created Gregkind"
In the words of Greg "It ain't nuttin' but a party!"
