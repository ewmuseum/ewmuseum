Rookie that needs a Fed.

21 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm new to this and I want to join an E-Fed that has characters that are created by their owners instead of the usual WWF or WCW E-Feds.
I'm on WEBTV so anything that is compatible would be appreciated. Here is a sample of the character and what he's about. -------------------------------------------------------------- - Somewhere in the middle of Devils Island, WI. The clock has struck a minute past the witching hour. - - A desolate shack that is his home stands... the shutters have fallen off their hinges and the door has seen better days - - The camera makes it way into the house like a scene from some cheesy overrated horror flick. - - Standing in the middle of the cold dark room stands a man that is all dressed in black... a mask covers most of his face except for his jaw area and the top of his head. He is wearing a black long sleeved top except for his right arm which has silver from his wrist to his shoulder. His tights are long and black and his boots come up to his knees. He has a short Black hair and is wearing a long black coat that trails behind him while he walks -
- He steers into the camera with an expression of loathing - The Darkness - So the time has come... I have spent most of my life in this desolate shack watching and waiting for my destiny to call me forth.
So it begins... Humanity is on the brink of destruction. Fear, loathing, despise, death, depression. My companions in the quest.
I've come in search of a place to call my home. I need to show humanity the error in it's ways.
I haven't seen the light of day since my birth. My mothers womb prepared me for this cold world. Now it's time for the world to feel the message that I must deliver.
Those that cross my path shall feel the wrath of... The Darkness. So it is said... so shall it be done. - The camera man decides that he has heard enough and decides to make a hasty retreat. The Darkness approaches the camera... picks it up and slams it to the ground! - - ZZZZZZZzzzZZZZZZZZZZzzzZZZZZZZ - --------------------------------------------------------------- I hope that some of you may be interested in this character and what he has to offer. If your interested please E-mail me. I will listen to all offers. Thank You
