What can I say? "Big Stupid" RP's are written very very quickly. Perhaps this RP will elucidate on some of the points that you mentioned about the previous one. (Things not seeming to matter, varying from the point, etc.)
___ Fade in. The exterior of the rustic cabin of the Rodland family. Big
Tobias Rodland kneels outside the huge bear cage made of railroad ties against the back wall of the cabin. The cage is strangely empty, and Tobias is staring inside. The gloomy sky seems to reflect the dismal mood of the big man-child.
Tobias: Daddy! A muffled grumbling comes from the side of the cabin. After a moment Daddy dearest appears beside his son. Tobias does not move except to blink as he examines the cage. Daddy does not move as he waits for his son to acknoledge his presence while leaning on his cane.
Tobias: Daddy! Daddy: I'm here already Stupid! What the unholy hell you wantin'? Tobias: Whar's Bucky? Daddy: Bucky? Tobias: Yuh! Daddy: (To the camera.) Uh, ignore him. He's a bit slow. . . Tobias: Whar's muh bahr!? Daddy: The bahr? *Bleep* boy, you never done liked that bahr! Tobias: I wan' muh bahr! Daddy is unusually pensive. Although obviously irritated, he doesn't yet begin his screaming tirade of curses. He rubs his quickly reddening face with his hands, and turns slowly around in a circle.
Daddy: Well. . .boy, y'see, that bahr. . . He weren't trained. . . Tobias: Whar's Bucky Daddy? Still Tobias kneels and stares into the cage. He reaches inside as if to touch the bear, perhaps thinking that the bear is still there, but invisible.
Daddy: You SAID you din' wan' no bahr! Why the *bleepedy bleep* do you even care? Tobias: He's muh bahr. Daddy stares at the back of his huge, huge son's head. He points his hand at Tobias, thumb and forefinger out, as if simulating a gun. Then he recoils his hand back, as if firing, then he blows on his forefinger and puts that hand in his pocket. Meanwhile Tobias shakes the cage as if trying to wake the non-existant bear.
Tobias: (almost crying.)Whar's bucky!? Daddy is now red in the face. Still he restrains himself, but just barely. Daddy: Well, Toby, y'see. . . Bucky's gone to a far better place. . . Tobias: Whar? The zoo? Daddy: No, Tobias, he's gone to his final resting place. . . Tobias: He's sleepin'? He's got 'im a cave? Daddy: No! Bucky's in bahr heaven, boy! Tobias: Whar's that? Daddy finally explodes, he whacks his son one time with his cane. Tobias finally turns his head to his father.
Tobias: OW! Daddy: He's dead, Stupid! Th' damn bahr had to be put down! He was rabid! Tobias: (Crying now.) Uh, uh, uh! Bucky done died? I don' unnerstan!? Daddy: He's like yer momma, y' *bleepbleepbleep*! Stone cold, in the ground! We made a rug outta his skin!
Tobias: We gotta momma rug? Daddy simply whacks Tobias across the face with his cane. Tobias falls on his butt from his kneeling position.
Daddy: NO! Y'stinking piece o' *bleep*!!! Don' you dare desecrate the memory of yer' sweet departed momma like that!
Tobias: Wh-when Bucky die? Daddy: While y'were busy losin' that Big ol' brawl last Teusday! Tobias: I tol' ya, Daddy! Dey ganged up on me! Daddy: I knew y' couldn't cut it without me, y'worthless. . . Tobias: Daddy. . . Um sorry. . . C'n. . .c'n I have?. . . Daddy's demeanor softens just a bit. He still seems the harsh taskmaster, just not quite so abusive. . .
Daddy: What th' *bleep* y'want boy? Tobias: A bunny. . . A knowing grin crosses Daddy's face. He looks to camera, nods, and responds. Daddy: So, y'wanna bunny, eh? Tobias nods. Daddy: (With a big grin.) I got y' a bunny. Tobias: (Smiling, eyes wide open in anticipation.) Yeah!? Daddy: (Reaching into a pocket.) Yeah! Suddenly Daddy whips something out of his pocket and throws it at his sons feet. Tobias screams bloody murder and falls down onto his back.
Daddy: There's y' *bleep**bleep**bleep* bunny! Like 'em? Y'can call 'im GEORGE!!! Tobias: AAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHH!!! Daddy takes the object from the ground and pushes it into his sons face, screaming all the while. . .
Daddy: That's right! Next *bleep*'n time y'gotta match, REMEMBER THIS!!! Next time *bleep*'n win! Now, come on! Daddy pulls Tobias up from the ground by one ear and begins to leave, pulling the oversized manchild along behind him. Tobias is doubled over, keeping his head low enough to not lose his ear to his father's grip. The pair round a corner of the cabin, leaving the cameraman alone. The camera then pans downward, showing the object in question. The object is the corpse of a small rabbit, several days dead. Centered on the bunny, the scene fades away to black. . .
