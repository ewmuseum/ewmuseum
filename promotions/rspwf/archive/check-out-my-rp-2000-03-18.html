Tell me what you think as I'm looking to improve "The means by which enlightened rulers and sagacious generals moved and conquered others, that their achievements surpasses the masses, was advance knowledge. Advance knowledge cannot be gained from ghosts and spirits, inferred from phenomena, or projected from the measures of Heaven, but must be gained from men for it is the knowledge of the enemy's true situation."
-Sun-tzu The Art of War [[SCENE]] Your screen opens up to a black background. You here the sounds of "Savage as fuck" by Dre Dog aka Andre Nickatina. The lyrics hit hard as the sickness of the lyrics comes through and it is somewhat disturbing. Then you get the view of a very dimly lit mansion. It becomes clear that there are no lights in this house, just candles. The camera circles the room and you see no one just pictures. They are all faces of great men of war, and these look like they might authentic from the lands in England where they must have been produced at. The faces of Niccoli Machevelli, Alaxander The Great, and Napoleon are laid next to each other high on the wall. As the camera moves downward to reveal A well built man standing with his back to the camera. He is about six foot and his muscles can be seen as he is wearing no shirt. You see a gun shot wound by his kidney that has healed improperly. The man turns around rubbing his hands together with a real sinister look on his face.
"The Apocalypse" Aeries Strife: This gets so tiring. You got people who just wont stop, they constantly ridicule because they think for them to be right, you must be wrong. I am currently dealing with a man no different from that, he is just another player in the game that I play every single day of my life. Like al the others before him, he doubted everything about me, and like all the others I received nothing but silence when they were finished. You see
I have done enough to say to myself, I am not the average man. I have fought in war, survived more than one gun shot wound. I have been forced to enter what is meant to be a travesty of me everyday, yet when it is all said and done it turns into my triumph. The men who choose to oppose me all have the same things in common, they all have no faith in me or the gods who empower me. They all believe that they are headed down the right path and when we meet at the cross roads ridicule me for taking another way. So be it then, chances are they don't honestly deserve to even be here with me. There are a handful of men who are as deserving of the respect they demand compared to the respect they get. I am most certainly not one of those men, as the words of men, clearly underrate me.
[[Your screen gets very blurry]] My current dealings are with another man, who is certainly my average opponent, has no respect me and ridicules me for not giving him what he refuses me. Todd
Taylor, a man who decided he would talk about me and for references he would use two books. The bible and the dictionary. Those two books are consider probably the most important books to all of the human language. However, I don't believe there is anything in either which would accurately describe me. I am held above all words, that is why your speaking in all honesty means nothing to me. Because nothing you can say can actually compare to the action that I will make. I have set few goals in my life, at least when it concerns wrestling, so rest assure it means nothing if I win or lose to you. The fact remains, I don't believe you can beat me nor do I believe anything you say. You talk of jealousy, but I have nothing to be jealous of. You think I admire the privileges that some men receive when they enter a federation, there is nothing to admire as those who are deserving receive the same. On the very first card of this federation I became a tag team champion. In Eulw, the very first card I entered a battle royal, which led to me becoming a tag team champion there as well. You see, I fight through everything, ask around and everyone who has ever ran a fed I was in will tell you I see no pride in starting at the top. That is the reason why even though people hate Shawn Ryder, all fed heads respect him because he is a fighter and always has been.
[[Screen clears up]] Taylor, you shouldn't use words like jealousy so loosely, because in all honesty I could make that same claim about you. Out of the blue comes an unknown man, or at least he would like to think that, and decides to challenge man who he knows nothing about but the way he thinks. Could it be that you yourself are looking for that stepping stone into the big leagues and of course that is what I would be. Are you jealous that I am a well known figure and your a nobody? Your false accusations never help you, they might sound good to you while you utter them but when they come to the light and are recognized at lies you like a fool. You want to say I sound pathetic, because I am a God, and I let you know it. Perhaps if this was about 320 BC then maybe you would accept what it true. But what was real then remains real now. I don't worry about what might be good for you, but I consider you might want a strong relationship with some God before you past. Now on that table before you laid a bible, but which one was it. You see many religions teach from many different bibles, who do you believe in? It appears you have labeled your own God as yourself, or do you just speak like that? Then you talked about curiosity, it was once said that curiosity is just a few inches away from stupidity. Those few inches is what you passed, but that like everything else you done was average. These are just a few reason why words like average and mediocre will always be associated with you.
As far as the end of you coming tomorrow, that is all in the eyes of the beholders. The end of our match will come tomorrow and that's what I have been talking about. But I will give you credit, I don't know everything but I have always known enough. That in fact has always been my claim to fame. I know what experience means, as it's never been a real challenging word but if you need a dictionary to discuss it then perhaps it just goes to show that maybe wrestling isn't really a sport which one must be a genius to be successful in. I didn't make any mistakes when preparing for this match as there was a mold I needed you to feel and you fit perfectly. Now you wished to challenge me about this
David and Goliath story. The fact is David won that battle with the help of his
God, and even though it was with a mere stone, the fact is Goliath underestimated the little man. I have given you the full credit that you give yourself, therefore I have erased all chances of underestimating as is, no one ever rates themselves properly. But to give you credit, yes if you hit me in the head with a large rock, then chances are I will fall for a moment. You might have just found the way to beat me, because you will never beat me without some help from something else. The most important thing for you to realize is this though, I have nothing to prove to you. So you should just focus in on proving that you are not just another face in the land of the lost.
[[Aeries starts to walk toward a couch centered in the middle of the room. He reaches it and sits down. He then reaches into his pockets and lights a cigar...]]
Three to four week career, I must know where do you get your information. As I have been doing this for years, I am a vet. That's the real reason why I can be jealous of what I already am. There is not a soul in the big leagues who doesn't recognize or respect my name. I am the God of war, and until you have lived through a real war don't ever even comment on it. War is a lot of things, but this wont be it. No one will do and no children shall pass because this is between me and you. There are no armies, but there is one soldier. He is standing before you dressed for the affair and more than ready to except all outcomes. now you asked a good question, so I will answer it. You wanted to know why I don't get the respect I deserve. Now through out history there have always been those men who opposed God. They would disagree with anything that it was said he did. When Jesus walked the earth he was told he wasn't the son of God until he was dead. Now as it was then like I said earlier, all trends never fade they just sometimes become dormant. But when they arise again so commonly we forget that the events have already transpired. That is why people say nothing is new and I assure you they are all correct. Now as far as my importance to the federation, who knows it is not for me judge. Fact is I was torn away from a tag champion I respect but I certainly didn't choose. I don't talk about bringing an apocalypse, I bring the apocalypse to the ring and if you ask any of the four men who didn't walk out the ring winners with me and
Bubbah last week they will tell you, it was the end for them. Now I am no rookie to this sport, so that one thing you got wrong. Next up, I have fought in real wars, and these bullet wounds [[points them out in his lower abdomen]]
are proof that there is no false living here. Now I read the bible, because there is knowledge to be learned in them all. I have faith with the Olympian gods but as I was taught there is more than one God. I know plenty about you, as you yourself have educated me on it. You are a man who believes because your a rookie your whole story is new. But it isn't and if you come in and think I know nothing of you then be prepared to find out how little you know about yourself.
I need to stick with one thing and go with it. Perhaps if I was making up everything I speak about, maybe if I was as fake as you that would be an adequate thing to say. The fact is, you believed in what you wanted to and that's why you think I am... fake. Boy have I heard lectures on the matter, as there once was a time when I talked with Mr. Malik Mustafa about everyday. If you knew him you would certainly understand this whole thing about who is real and fake. I have gone mad, know I said I was angry not crazy. If you believe what your saying then perhaps you are the one who has gone mad. Taylor you full of a lot of things and none of them smell good. You can say what you want and think the same, but there is nothing fake about me. As for my ego, it falls under pride, which there was a nice little segment about the whole thing form
Sleep Stalker not to long ago. Learn about pride and then you might be able to understand where I am coming from. You want to know when was the last time I used the powers given to me, I would say about last Saturday when I won my tag titles. Immortality, is something we all hope for but sorry it's not me. I was born in 1972 so I cant talk to much about the BC time. I just know what is wrote in the history books and the other great books I have read. Your fire is yours, enjoy it but I guarantee it wont be around much longer after the end strikes. You have learned nothing form what I have given and that is just another reason on the long list reason on why you must... EXpErIeNCe ThE eNd.
