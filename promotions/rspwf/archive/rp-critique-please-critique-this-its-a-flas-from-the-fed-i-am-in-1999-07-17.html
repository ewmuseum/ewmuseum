LiVe WiRe Current IWA Hardcore Champion <--[Begin Transmission]--> :::SCeNe::: A family of people are seen sitting in their living room. They are watching the television set as a commercial for BounTy just finishes. The 11:00 o'clock news is on now as the anchor gives his closing remarks. The last thing he says is that everyone should stay tuned because at 11:35, The Jay Leno show will be airing. Tonights specials include Paul Ruben, Jennifer Lopez, and professional wrestler Live Wire. The family starts cheering. The screen of the television flashes black and then there is the opening for the Jay Leno show. Jay is sitting on his set with coffee cup in hand.
Jay Leno- Welcome to the Tonight Show, tonight we have a couple of very interesting guests. Paul Ruben, better known as Pee Wee Herman, and the hottest Latina in the music industry today, Jennifer Lopez, and professional wrestler Live Wire. I am going to bring out my first guest, Paul Ruben.
:::Paul Ruben comes walking out, unshaven, with long hair. He looks rather scruffy. Wearing a plaid shirt and dirty jeans, he goes running to the stage and runs up to Jay. He begins to hug and try to kiss him. Once this begins to occur, Jay Leno calls for security. Two large security guards come running on stage and they pick up Mr. Ruben and throw him out of the studio.:::
Jay Leno- Well, seems like Paul Ruben hasnt learned anything. What a sick bastard. I cant believe this guy was once one of the most loved childrens characters. Sheesh. Well right now I would like to bring out my next guest, she started out in movies, where she glimmered on stage as Selena. She went on to be in Money Train, and other great movies. But now, my next guest has moved her way onto the #1 spot on almost all music top charts. Her first single, if you had my love, is the first song to come off of her album, On the 6. Here she is, the Latina Queen herself....Ms. Jennifer Lopez.
:::Jennifer Lopez walks out from the back onto the stage. She looks beautiful as usual. She is wearing normal street clothes. Baggy jeans and a oversized tee shirt. Jay looks at her weird and then begins to speak.:::
Jay Leno- No disrespect, Ms. Lopez, but since this is national television, couldnt you have worn something a little more, um...Nice?
Jennifer Lopez- Jay, I am really sorry, but for some strange reason, once I got to the studio...all my bags were gone. The were stole from my dressing room. I had to come on stage wearing what I came to the studio in. Believe me, I would have looked alot better than this.
Jay Leno- OH wow, in that case, I am sorry to hear that. See thats what is wrong with society today, anyone will steal anything that isnt bolted down.
Okay, well I will get back to you in a few minutes but right now we have to go to a short break.
<Commercial> Star Wars: Episode 1: The Phantom Menace <Commercial> NYPD Blue Season Finalie <Commercial> American Pie Jay Leno- Well we are back with the beautiful Ms. Jennifer Lopez. Let me ask you, Do you have any idea who it was who stole your luggage from the backstage dressing rooms?
:::Just as Jay asks that question, Paul Ruben comes running down the stairs ontot he stage wearing a pair of panties and a bra. He has on another pair of panties on his head. Jennifer has a look of shock on her face. Jay calls for security once again and they drag him backstage. Jennifer Lopez jumps up and runs to the backstage area.:::
Jay Leno- Well it looks like we finally figured out where all Jennifer Lopezs clothes went to. Wow what a sick individual that man has turned out to be. Who would have thought that Pee Wee Herman would be so sick and twisted. Ah well, since Jennifer Lopez is now in the back probably beating the hell out of Pee
Wee...I am going to take this chance to show the viewers at home some video footage of our next guest.
:::A video comes on to the screen, IWA superstar....Live Wire is seen. There are clips of past matches involving Live Wire. There is his theme music playing in the background. The final picture pops up and it is of Live Wire standing with the IWA Hardcore Title around his waist. The IWA banner is in the background. Then we go to the live set of The Jay Leno show.:::
Jay- And my next guest, is professional wrestler, Live Wire. :::Live Wire comes walking onto stage wearing jeans and an IWA TRADITION tee shirt. He has the hardcore title with him. But it looks a little different than the one that we are use to seeing.:::
Jay Leno- Well it is nice to have you on the show. But that first thing I noticed when you came out here is your belt. Now I know that is not the IWA
Hardcore Title. So what is it? Live Wire- This is my custom IWA hardcore Title. I had this personally made for myself. I paid for it to be made and this is the title that I recognize as the IWA Hardcore Title.
Jay Leno- Well that was an interesting idea. Well, you have seen what transpired here tonight, what do you think of all of this?
Live Wire- Well first of all, I think that Jennifer Lopez has the most bangin body I have ever seen. She has the best ASSets I have ever seen on anyone.
And as for Paul Ruben, I think he is a sick bastard, but hey, if I had the chance to be that close to anything Jennifer Lopez had on her ass, I sure as hell would have done it too. Ah well back to business.
Jay Leno- Well coming at on Thursday Terror, you find yourself in a tag team match with a man by the name of Venom. Now if I remember correctly, I thought that Venom had a problem with you. Mainly because you held that very IWA
Hardcore Title. Live Wire- Yes that is true, Venom was coming after me for a while, but the prez decided to team us together last week on Terror against 2 men that i had no respect for. At first I was a little suspicous, I thought that Venom was going to turn on me and make me lose the match. But all went off without a hitch. And I am really glad to have Venom as my tag team partner. Coming up on this edition of Terror, we are teaming up once again. This time it is going to be against. We are currently the number one contendors for the IWA Tag Team
Titles since we beat Hellfire Club. They were the one of the best tag teams the IWA has to offer. And myself and Venom defeated them. You never know you might see Venom and Live Wire teaming up to go up against the Twin Terrors for the IWA Tag Team Titles. I think it would be interesting to have us fight it out. I am close friends with Twiggy and Demonoid. Oh and one last thing before I go....Twiggy, get better soon.
