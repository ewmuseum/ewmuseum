{Free Agent}Bryan Forrestor

21 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Scene opens to show an empty Soldiers Field , home of the Chicago Bears. There is no-one in the stands , no local press , no players or coaches. Only one man graces the field with his presence. Standing midfield is a man. He is dressed in a pair of Tommy jeans , Nikes and a Bears jersey with the number 49 and the name Forrestor across the back. The camera begins a slow jaunt towards this individual and finally comes to rest and he speaks ....}
Forrestor: In high school , I was voted All-State linebacker sophmore thru senior year.
I never missed a game , no matter how hurt I was. I carried that straight into the University of Syracuse and there I dominated opposing offenses.
Coaches and players alike cringed at the thought of my name! A two time recipiant of the Bronko Nagurski award and I never missed a roster spot on the All-College teams. Then came the Chicago Bears!! A chance for me to dominate at the highest level. I was the chosen first pick in the second round of the '94 draft , but my dreams would soon be shattered after a near fatal car wreck ....
I dwelled in my own pity and sorrow and before you knew it , I had wasted two years of my life. Not even a shell of my former self and there was no chance of ever playing pro ball again! But thanks to a good friend of mine , by the name of Tyler Greene , I found new hope. I found a chance to do what I love and that is compete!! I found that in the sport of pro wrestling. Now here I stand four years past since that faithful night. I find myself in the best physical condition that I have ever been in my entire life. I find myself with a new mind set and a new hunger.
Football is my past , wrestling is my future and gold is my new favorite color! I'll see you in the ring!!!!
