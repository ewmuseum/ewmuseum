"Excellent" Evan Andrews Roleplay. Critique

2 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Time: 3:30 P.M. The scene fades into the view of a bar. The room is dimly lit, and has the smell of acohol and cigarette smoke. The bar is rather crowded, causing the waiters and waitresses to be hustling around. The camera moves, showing different people kicking back cold ones, and light up cigarettes. It moves toward the bar, that is less crowded than the many tables on the floor of place. There are a few people sitting in stools, and the camera moves toward one of them. He is tall and nicely built, causing him to stand out a little bit. A tall glass of beer sits in front of him, it is a little less than half full. He notices the presence of the camera and looks up.]
[The man has on a pair of light pair of jeans that fall down upon his white
Adidas shoes. Covering his chest is covered by a white t-shirt that says
"Abercrombie in green. His short blonde hair is perfectly combed straight down. On his wrist you can see a casual looking watch. He looks to the camera and begins to speak...]
"Excellent" Evan Andrews: Well, nice to see you showed up. [Sarcasticly.] And your actually on time! [Waves his hand.] I really like this place. I found it when I was on my way to my hotel. I've coming over here for a while for a drink or a bite to eat. But, I don't have that much time to chat, so lets get down to business.
Some of you might know me from some other promotions such as SSW and LAW, but if you don't I'm "Excellent" Evan Andrews. And the reason I'm in Pittsburg is to display my talents in the WCWA, in my debut match on Wednesday. Thats right these lucky people will get to see me wrestle.
[Stops and sips his drink. He then continues...] "Excellent" Evan Andrews: You see, tommorow night at In Da House, I'm up against a man that goes by the name of Xenon. Now I saw him wrestle on
Supreme, and let me tell you I saw nothing that was the least bit impressive. I mean hell, he lost the match. But I don't take anything lightly, so I'm gonna walk into this match like its a title match. Its almost as big as one too, because getting off to a good start in a federation is very important.
Now some people have asked, "Do you see anything in Xenon's wrestling ability that worries you?" Let me answer that simple questioin bluntly right now...
HELL NO! I've been told he's much taller, and that he out weighs me by god knows how much but that means absolutely nothing to to me. I think the motto,
"The bigger they are the harder they fall," comes into play for this match.
I'll be the first to admit that I can't match power with somebody like him, but likewise somebody like him can't match my speed or wrestling ability. Now I'm sure Xenon could over power people that try to wrestle his game, but I'm going to dictate how this match is fought. I'll keep at my pace, and do what I want to do. To put it simply, I'm going to _DOMINATE_, and thats a guarantee.
[He takes another drink of his beer, this time taking a larger drink. After that he looks rejuvitnated, and starts to talk again...]
"Excellent" Evan Andrews: [Sarcasticly.] Now Xenon, I see that you have an impressive record of 0-1. I know your hungry for a victory, but Wednesday isn't going to be your night. So when your off bragging to your freak friends, you can go ahead tell them that you're 0-2, because every damn person in this world knows that I'm gonna beat your a$$. And the sky's the limit for me in the WCWA. After I chalk up a victory, I'm gonna tear through everybody that gets in my way. But wait, I'm getting ahead of myself. I have to keep my mindset on Xenon.
[Takes yet another break, and finishes his beer. The bartender sees that his glass is empty and approaches him...]
Bartender: Can I get you another beer sir? "Excellent" Evan Andrews: Ah, what the hell, sure. [The bartender returns in a few seconds with a glass that is filled to rim with beer, and sets it in front of Evan. Evan takes a quick sip and shakes his head, as if saying that it tastes great. He then goes on...]
"Excellent" Evan Andrews: Good beer. Now, I can tell you one thing.. this other freak that goes by the name of Lord Homongous, better not interfere in
Xenon's match again. I know that most people would be hoping for outside interference against a man like Xenon, but I'm gonna with this damn match on my own. I'm somebody you want to upset, because if you do I'll be all over your a$$. [Looks at his watch.] Oh, I don't have any more time to talk.
[Takes one last big gulp of his drink.] "Excellent" Evan Andrews: So Xenon, face the facts. I'm "Excellent", and your not. [Starts to walk out of the bar, but then turns back around with one last thing to say...] "Excellent" Evan Andrews: And think about this before we step into the ring tommrow night...
I'M NOT GOOD....... I'M EXCELLENT! [Fade to black.]
