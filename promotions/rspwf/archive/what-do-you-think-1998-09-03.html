What do you think?

3 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, tell me what you think of this RP. Figured what the hell every one else does it. Please give your opinions.
Thanx marC (Scene opens inside Steve's Bar and Grill. Steve Houston is sitting on a bar stool up at the bar. Steve is wearing a blue FuBu shirt, a pair of baggy blue
Mecca jeans, a gold chain, gold Rolex watch, and a couple of gold rings on his right hand. He sees the camera and begins to speak.)
Steve Houston: Oh it's time. Yep, it's time for me to start gett'n ready for the biggest night in wrestling. Time for me to get ready for the Night of
Glory.
(Steve calls the bartender over) Steve: Alright pal, I'm gett'n ready for the Night of Glory, so get me some real hard stuff over here now!!!
(The bartender goes to the other end of the bar and comes back and hands Steve a bottle of Busch light.)
Steve: What the fuck is this? A god damn Busch. Thats like half the guys, hell, thats like all of the guys that are in this damn match with me. Busch leaguers."Suicide" Sid Snow, Joseph Morningstar,Frazer Fury,"Legacy" Paul
Kiljoy, who the hell are these bums? Then theres a guy named Brass Knuckles.
What the fuck is that all about? Brass knuckles are the things I pull out of my trunks and hit guys with. Not a damn wrestler. Well which ever of you fucking losers gets the honer of bein on the same team as me, better work there ass off. Because everyone knows the Legend Steve Houston, hates to lose. Especially on the big nights. And this defenitly qualifies as one of those. Well what the hell, like these losers, I'll make the best out of it.
(Steve grabs the beer, chugs it, and slams the bottle down.) Steve: See what I mean? Easy work, for a fella like me. Well, let's move on to the people I have atleast heard of in this little match of mine. Thats not sayin that any of these losers are better than the ones I just mentioned. Where should I start? Hmmm....
How about Alan James. Well I fought this bum once. But I don't know a whole lot about him. All I know is that he was a nothing in GSW compared to me, and still he is nothing compared to me. If this pile of horse shit is put on my team, he may just find himself stabbed, if ya know what I mean.
(Steve demands more beer. The bartender brings him down a bottle of Bud Ice) Steve: God Damn it!! I thought I told you I was getting ready for Night of
Glory. Didn't I?
Bartender: Well, yeah. But you did ask for another beer. So thats what I br.... Steve: Shut the fuck up.I din't ask for one more beer. I didn't ask for two more beers. I said I wanted more beer. What the hell good is one more beer gonna do me? You think I'm a god damn light weight that can't handle anything like Jack Vars?
Bartender: Well, how much..... Steve: How about you just shut the fuck up and bring that damn keg of Bud Ice down here? Bartender: Ahh...ok (Bartender walks to the other side of the bar. He lifts the keg up and brings it down with a glass and sets it next to Steve. Steve get's himself a glass of beer and continues to speak.)
Steve: Oh what the hell. Enough about James and on to Vars. Well, I've never fought the guy. But I have been in feds with this jobing bum. How the hell he got a spot in Night of Glory I'll never know. But since he's here, I won't mind beat'n the piss outta him to.
(Steve finishes his beer. He fills up the cup again and continues.) Steve: Ahhh....and finally on to the biggest piece of shit in the whole match. Hell probably the biggest loser in the whole event. He is none other than
Lawrence Williams. well, what can I say? We know each other rather well. But overall we've only fought once. And we all remember that , don't we Williams?
Hahaha....Yes I beat his ass in WFRM for the world title. But the WFRM isn't the only place were we had our differences. Every were we went we still have them. Because it isn't about the WFRM World title. It's about me hating you and you hating me. We were supposed to get it on in GSW, but luckily for you, it never happened. How about HHWO? Hahahaha.....Them chairs feel pretty damn good, eh' Lawrence? Well Williams, no matter how the teams come out you know for damn sure, that me and you will be hooking up at Night of Glory. So you better prepare yourself, because as you can see I sure am.
(Steve lifts his half empty glass of beer in the air, as the scene fades out)
