[The scene opens up with the UCW House Show logo flashing across the screen, as we hear Sir Baltimore doing commentary and
  introducing the audience, fans, and viewers to the show. The camera then goes around the arena getting a shot at the hundreds of signs.
  Then when Sir Baltimore is done speaking, the camera view then switches to the US announcer's table, where Sir Baltimore is sitting with
  Brian Gumble...]   Sir Baltimore: ...Hello ladies and gentlemen, fans of the UCW, I am Sir Baltimore along with your beloved, Brian Gumble. And we sure do  have an action pack evening for you people....Brian...
    Brian Gumble: Indeed we do. Yup, and not too long ago you all witnessed Darkman win the UCW belt, but now we have bad @$$
  news...YES... a new UCW champion.   Sir Baltimore: And in just a couple of minutes your new UCW champion will be out here to give us a few words, or let's say a lot of words
  about last night's match. In fact, right about now, I should be up there to introduce the new champion in...
  [With that, Sir Baltimore gets off of his seat, pulls off his headset, and enters the ring. Then the lights go out...and then slowly dim a bit. We
  then hear the sound of a machine gun, as the crowd jumps to their feet a cheer. Then a loud explosion is heard all over the PA system, and
  then "Nobody Can Stop Me" by Bizzie Bone plays throughout the PA system. The crowd gets a little louder, and finally erupts when
  DreadZ steps onto the ramp, wearing his black trenchcoat and silver Oakley shades, and the UCW belt on his shoulder. At the end of each
  of his dreadlocks is a silver skull. With him, is N-Tice, of course, and she has a briefcase with her. They both stop for a while on the ramp
  and look at the crowd before heading into the ring. The music stops, and lights are restored...]
  Sir Baltimore: Ladies and gentlemen, your new...UCW...CHAMPION...DreadZ!!!!!!!!!   [ The crowd erupts once again, as DreadZ takes the belt with one hand and raises it up, and then gives it to N-Tice.
He turns around to   shake Sir Baltimore's hand, and then grabs the mic from him.]   DreadZ: Ladies and gentlemen.......let it now be known.......I...AM....YOUR NEW CHAMPION!!   [The crowd erupts]   DreadZ: Yes, I know why you're happy. You are happy, because you finally have a champ you people deserve for you dedication, and also
  because the finally damn over, and the length of my reign will probably not shock you all because, hell, now I can proudly say I am on top of
  this f'n fed, and there's nothing you, or anyone in the back can do about. In fact, every single person in the Kaos Corp, is on top of the whole
  damn UCW, and you know it....I know it....and those losers in the back know it. And I am here to represent the guys in the Kaos Corp, and
  winning that belt just a while ago, pretty much writes the word
"represent" on everyone in this fed.
  [DreadZ the drops the hand with the mic next his side and takes his other hand and pulls off his shades...then throws them into the crowd.
  Immediately he wastes no time and pulls the mic back up...]   DreadZ: Now, I would just like to say how long it has taken me to win my first championship....hmmmm.....FOUR WEEKS!!! Now you may
  think that is a long time, but hell, compared to all the other wannabees before, it's a big difference. But for me, it has been kind of long in a
  way, and I have watched The Coroner like a damn eagle, and how
I thought he was the biggest disgrace to all the UCW champs there has
  been. I put challenges up, and no acceptance? Champion? I thought not. Then there came Darkman. I'm not going to say nothing about him,
  because he practically has nothing to say about him, since he has no life, because all he ever does is play around with his little head.
He won   the belt from The Coroner, because Coroner is a loser, and he still was crazy. And this is when I say mind crazy. And you all would probably
  agree with when I say that Darkman needs some serious help, and I mean whole lot of f'n serious help. You listen to this guy almost
  everytime, and I listen to him on how he thinks he takes the souls of his opponents. Well....BULLSH*T!!! Man, get serious, that Mortal
  Kombat crap doesn't work in the real world, and sure definitely, doesn't work in my world, the Dread World.
  Finally, you got a person, who runs around in his make believe abandoned house, talking like his head is straight up his @$$..... "The lords
  have spoken." Well, Greave, whether or not the LORDS have spoken this or spoken that, that will not stop me from taking my foot and making
  you eat it from behind. Meaning that, I will, on Sunday, shove my nice army boots so far straight up your dark@$$, that this time your lords
  will say that you are a great warrior, which I am very sure of, is all another bunch of underground bull crap. You also say that I have your belt.
  Damn, BIATCH! Reck yourself, before you break self, and then re-recognize, that I am the UCW Champion, something that you have never
  been and probably never will be in estimates of a very long time...   [DreadZ then pulls out another set of silver Oakleys and puts them on. He then takes his belt and puts it back on his shoulder, as he leaves
  and the screen FADES TO BLACK...]
