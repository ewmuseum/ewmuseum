Here is some roleplay I just made up. I am a new e-wrestler, so please email at [detail withheld] to know what you though of it. Thanks! (Interviewer and Camera Man are walking down the hall, when they are pulled aside by the new member of SXW)
CoolmanP:Listen, I want you to interview me right now, no if's and's or but's. Just start filming, hand me the mic, and you step aside...(sarcastically)sweetheart.
Interviewer:But we can't just- CoolmanP:Just what? Be late for one interview that doesn't really need to be seen. This will take only a few minutes so just shutup and enjoy the show.
Is that rolling? Camera Man:No. It will be in 5...4...3...2... CoolmanP:Alright, listen up all you twinkies! This is the rookie, the newcomer, the "student." I am issuing an open challenge to anyone man enough...Oh wait, sorry I forgot, I am the only man here...Let me rephrase that. Anyone WOMAN enough here that thinks they can beat me, just bring it.
I can beat you all just as hard as I beat the eggs for my breakfast this morning. So, if there are any so-called "wrestlers" out there good enough to try and wrestle me, BRING IT ON!!! All right stop recording. NOW! STOP
RECORDING BEFORE I COME OVER AND HURT YOU!! Camera Man:OK OK, I stopped.(Looking at interviewer)Come on, let's go. Interviewer:(murmuring)Read my mind. Email me at [detail withheld] to let me know what to fix and give me an overall rating(for a rookie). CoolmanP
