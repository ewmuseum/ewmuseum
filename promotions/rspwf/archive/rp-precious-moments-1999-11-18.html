<RP> Precious Moments

18 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Enjoy, my fellow lovers of PM... ----------------------------- (We open backstage, after Motor City Madness. In particular, we are in what appears to be the most utterly ridiculous dressing room ever. Little paper dolls are strung up around the room, frilly pink and blue lace covers the walls, and a TV monitor sits there, equipped with a built-in VCR, with a red ribbon tied around it. As we take this all in, the door to the dressing room opens, and in walk The Binky Fairy and The Winky Fairy, more commonly known as
Precious Moments. Binky looks rather upset, while Winky has a big grin on his face, and is shuddering with excitement)
The Binky Fairy: What was that out there? We're minding our own business, defending the world against the Chinese, and a couple of guys run out and attack us.
The Winky Fairy: We got the video... TBF: I mean, we had that match won. We were going to take the evil that is the China Connection and show the world that our pursuit of good is pure and just. And what happens? We get attacked by two guys who call themselves "The Threat". TWF: We got the video... TBF: Now if they had a real reason for running in and beating us up, I'd accept that, move on, and beat the evil out of them at a later date. But NOOOO...
they're just trying to "make a name for themselves"... AT OUR EXPENSE! TWF: WE GOT THE VIDEO!!! WOO HOO! TBF: Calm down, Wink! You'd think you had stumbled upon a vat of magic fairy floo. TWF: Mmmm... magic fairy floo. I want some... WHILE WE WATCH THE VIDEO! TBF: Magic fairy floo comes from the Magic Fairy Fields. We can't get any floo here in... ummm... errrrr... OH YEAH! Michigan.
(Winky pouts, but then perks up again) TWF: We got the video, Bink! The video, the video! TBF: Don't you realize what just happened, Wink? We were just attacked by two men that not only have evil in their hearts, but in their very souls! We should be planning on how to win over the evil, and implant the good back into their blackened hearts.
(Winky's face suddenly gets a very stubborn look on it) TWF: We got the video. (Winky gives Binky a nod, as if to reaffirm the fact that they indeed did have the video) TBF: Oh, alright. We got the video. Let's open it up. TWF: OH BOY, OH BOY, OH BOY! (Winky pulls the tape out of the package and looks at it for a few minutes) TWF: What does this do, anyway, Bink? (Binky shrugs) TBF: Got me. Here, give it to me. Let me see here... (Binky looks at the video for several seconds, turning to around and around. He looks over at the TV/VCR, then back at the tape. Over at the TV/VCR... back at the tape. Over at the TV/VCR... and then he pulls open the black flap on the tape, and looks at the long, ribbony tape underneath)
TBF: It's... it's ribbon. But it's all wrapped up in this weird black box. (Binky starts to pull the "ribbon" out of the tape, just a little at first) TBF: Hey, this is kinda cool. (Binky pulls a little more out, then a little more) TBF: Ribbon in a box. This is so cool! It's the solution for all our ribbon needs! TWF: But it's black. TBF: BUT IT'S COOL! (The Binky Fairy suddenly tears the tape in half, and starts yanking it out in large strands. He tosses it in the air, and a look of pure childlike enchantment overcomes his face)
TBF: Whoa! (Binky pulls the tape out faster and faster, letting it fly into the air) TBF: WHEEEE!!! TWF: Hey! Let me try! (Binky hands the tape to Winky, and Winky starts tossing tape left and right) TWF: WOO HOO! THIS IS THE MOST FUN I'VE HAD SINCE I WAS 2! TBF: That was last year. TWF: Oh yeah. (Binky and Winky take turns pulling the tape free from it's black box, sending it sailing through the air in long strands. Finally, the last of the tape falls to the ground, and Binky and Winky just look at each other, huge smiles on both their faces)
TBF: Maybe those Hate guys aren't so bad. TWF: We have to go ask them for more tapes! More, more, MORE! TBF: We'll get the chance! We're all going to be together at that Explosion show. We're in some kind of ten man tag.
TWF: What's that? TBF: I don't know, but it sure does sound fun! Plus, we get to team with Mad Goat! TWF: MAD GOAT!? I haven't seen him in ages! Tapes, Mad Goat's, and ten man tags... SUNDAY IS GOING TO BE THE GREATEST DAY EVER!
TBF: I love you, Wink. TWF: I love you, too, Bink. (Binky and Winky stare at each other a moment, longingly. Then, they start giggling insanely as they play with what was formerly "The Best Of The Fonz", tossing it into the air gaily. We zoom into the TV/VCR, which has just come to life. Suddenly, black tape, much like the tape Binky and Winky had tossed around just moments ago, spirals on the screen. As the last of it falls, we are left with three words written in cursive writing... UNTIL NEXT TIME)
