[Eric Emerson] - Latest and Greatest rp...

19 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[] “What is a Juggalo” by the Insane Clown Posse begins to play[] {Big Heel Pop} [Eric Emerson walks onto the aisle way followed by his bodyguard Joe. Emerson stops looks around and then flexes his muscles. Emerson is wearing a pair of black speedo tights with a rams head upon the rear, black wrestling boots, black knee and elbow pads. Joe is wearing a pair of tan dress pants, a Bulls
T-shirt, black snow boots and a blue and red clip on tie. Emerson walks to the ring in a slow strutting type of style as Joe tries to keep all hands off of
Eric. Joe walks to the ring steps and opens the ropes for Emerson as Emerson begins to walk up the ring steps. As Emerson begins to step through the ropes
Joe lets go of the ropes and the ropes hit Eric in the family jewels.]
{Big Crowd Pop} [Emerson hits the canvas and Joe who has his back to Emerson keeps doing his business. Emerson begins to reach his knees as Joe turns around and walks over to help Eric up, but Emerson just pushes him away. Joe walks over to the ring announcer and grabs the microphone from him. Emerson now standing hunched over is handed the microphone from Joe.]
[] “What is a Juggalo” fades out[] Eric Emerson [Low Voice]: Joe...[pauses]... Will you please do me a favor... [Joe nods his head up and down] Eric Emerson: Go get the fuck out of here... Right now you make me sick, so just leave... [Joe shrugs his arms and says something to Emerson] Eric Emerson: Just leave you over paid piece of crap... [Joe lowers his arms and leaves the ring. He stands in the aisle back to the ring] Eric Emerson: Now that the idiot is out of the ring lets get down to business... I’ve listened to guys spit words out of their mouths that make me want to puke. I have listened to Emu talk about the year of the Emu... Call me crazy but doesn’t that sound a little like a Japanese year of the Whale? I mean what exactly is the year of the Emu... Emu, if this is your year then you better go back and think because your year is about to turn to shit. Your going to be stepping into the ring with a man with talents beyond belief. Your going to step into the ring with me, “The Greatest”. Some people say I’m not, others say I am. In my opinion it doesn’t matter what people think because_I_KNOW_I’m the greatest... [cocky Smile]...
[Emerson looks around at the crowd who are beginning to bad mouth him] Eric Emerson: Moving right along I have a few smart ass remarks heading out to
Mr. Lolli pop himself. CandyMan, you can change all you like, but in he end it’s all the same... I’ve said it once, and I’m going to say it one more time for your ears only... YOUR_a_HAS_BEEN... Your nothing CandyMan, you don’t even have a speck of talent left, and the sad part is this... [pauses]... You know it’s true... [cocky smile]... What’s this I hear about me “barely” beating
Johnny “I wish I had a sexy” Bod? I had him knocked out with the Money Maker, but then that piece of monkey shit Laim Rogan had to stick his nose where it didn’t belong. Face it CandyMan, you don’t know what your talking about...
Hell, you can face one more thing while your pondering that remark... You
CandyMan, have no clue as to what your getting yourself into. You want a match against me, well I have no problem with that. I’ll sign, but your going to have to take that match up with McDaniel because at this point... Well, he said this, “Your to good for CandyMan”. You see CandyMan, even the president of this fine federation knows your a worthless waste of space... Realize this
CandyMan... [pauses]... Your out of my league... If you don’t understand that I think you’ll understand this, I’m one hundred percent better then you in allot of things... Looks, Skills, and for god damn sure women appeal... [cocky smile]
[Emerson’s face goes from cocky smile to a serious look. He looks to the crowd and they begin to chant “Stryker”[]
Eric Emerson: Yeah, chant his stupid ass name all you want because I only have one thing to say to him... Stryker, we’ll see who throws who out... [smirks]...
So I think that sums up most of the things I should say to Stryker and saves me some time to talk about a few pieces of shit... Scotty Sexton, and John
“Hard-core” Carey... Two guys that think they are above me, but two guys that really are below me. Carey, one thing you should realize is I’m above you, so don’t act like your above me. I’ve listened to your crap for far to long and it makes me sick. John, you can call me a waste of sperm, as can I say the same to you. You can say I’m worthless, but we all know it's you that is worthless. No matter what you say to dismiss my talents it all comes straight back to you,
John. Face is “Hard-core”, your jealous of me... You and Sexton may think I am jealous of you, but no...[pauses]... You two high class wanna be’s are the jealous men of the IWC. Jealous that you don’t have the looks, the talents, or the ladies I have...
[Emerson rubs his chin] Eric Emerson: Now Sexton... I can say your an over achiever because quite frankly, you are... Plain and simply put your a guy that goes into matches thinking your great but in the end you end up looking like a big pile of monkey dung. Then we have your ever so clever remark.. “BLOW ME”... I’ve told you this once Sexton and this will be the last time I tell you this... I am not homosexual, I get more ladies at my apartment then you have hair on your testicles... Sexton, you want to make false claims about me, but it won’t work.
Everyone knows the truth and the truth is this... [pauses]... Your a wanna be
Eric Emerson... [cocky smile]... Face it Sexton, you’ll_NEVER_be me... I’ll prove that tonight as I step into the ring with 7 other men, one which will be you and win. Why will I win? Because I_AM_THE_GREATEST_... The International title will be around my waste, so you all better recognize... Later Boys...
