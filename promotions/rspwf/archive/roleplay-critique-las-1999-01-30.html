[Roleplay] Critique - Las

30 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: The camera is placed on the set of a photo shoot. You see a draped white background, some lights, a camera, a photographer, and the man he is photographing. The man is wearing a black t-shirt that says "L.A.W Crew" in yellow writing, jeans, black boots, a silver cross, and has a title draped over his left shoulder. The photographer says something that the camera catches.]
Photographer: Ok Scotty, turn to your left just a tad... [Drake moves a bit.] PG: Good! Right there, hold it... hold it... [*CLICK] PG: Great we got it! SD: Ok, do we need any more? PG: Yeah, but this roll is full... let me go change and then we'll finish up. SD: Ok... [Drake lowers the belt in his hand and walks to a chair. He notices the camera as he walks over.]
Drake: Ahh, L.A.W film crew coming yet again to get words from the most elec... no Simply th.. no... the whole damn orga... dammit! What is it? Oh yeah, the one and _ONLY_ Pacific Title Champion in the world! Now I know all you little people out there in t.v world want to know, "Why Scotty? Why'd you do that to
Steve Houston?"
Well I'll tell all of you why; back when I was first starting in the L.A.W, I bumped into him in the back. Well, he told me that I better watch what the hell
I was doing, or he would be there to kick me when I was down. He thought he was some big bad L.A.W Legend, and he thought he could tell me what to do. Well, I earned my damn title shot, and I didn't get it! But I was willing to do anything to get this damn belt[Holds the belt up.]; and I did do _SOMETHING_! I gave Steve Houston the Jawbreaker heard 'round the world! It's all said and done now and nothing else matters, except for the fact that I am the L.A.W
Pacific Title Champion!
[Scotty pauses and bends down to grab a bottled water. He slugs some down, then tightens it back up and sets it on the ground.]
CameraMan: Ok Scotty, what do you think about your good friend Boltz winning the Heavyweight Title?
SD: I think it's a load of crap! He's not my friend... he thought he was but he isn't. Scotty Drake doesn't have any friends, and doesn't want any! As a matter of fact ya dumbass, I would love to kick Boltz' ass when he gets back from that[He laughs], broken neck! Damn, that must suck, eh Boltz? Winning the
Heavyweight Strap, but not being able to brag about it because you got your ass kicked! Anyways, I gotta get back to this photo shoot... next time you hear from me... y'all will be shocked!
[Drake gets up and walks back to the set as the photographer walks on the set and puts the film back. Drake looks at the video camera, and gives a devilish grin. Then, we slowly fade out.]
-Las [Handles:] (___Scotty Drake - L.A.W | 1 - 1 - 0 | *L.A.W Pacific Coast Champion___) (___"The Lethal Injection" Darren Scott - IMWA & WCWA | 0 - 0 - 0 |___) "Big names don't make a fed; a fed makes the big names." -Myself, 1/24/99
