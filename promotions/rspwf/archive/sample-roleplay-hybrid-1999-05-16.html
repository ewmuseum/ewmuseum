(- Sample Roleplay: Hybrid -)

16 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, this is a roleplay I did for the North American and Intercontinental
Wrestling Federation awhile back. Could you give me a few ideas on how to improve it? I'd appreciate it.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [- The scene opens to a shot of Hybrid walking through the corridor of O'Hare international airport... He is dressed in a very sharp, gray armani suit with matching grey trenchcoat, black dress shoes, and a pair of black Oakley
Sunglasses. Over his shoulder, Hybrid carries his prestigious North American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation Rising Star Championship. Hybrid begins looking around for a sign with his name on it, which should be being held by a limosine driver, ready to take him to his hotel. Hybrid does not see the limosine driver yet, so he walks over towards the baggage claim and grabs his suitcase. Hybrid walks outside, and spots his limosine driver. The driver walks Hybrid to a custom-design black Lincoln Navigator limosine, and the champion climbs in... The scene now shows the inside of the limosine, where
Hybrid is apparently just sitting back and relaxing. He leans over and opens a small refrigorator to his left, and pulls out two bottled waters. He hands one to the camera man, and begins to speak as the limousine pulls away from the airport and heads towards his hotel. -]
Hybrid: North American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation.. I'm here to make a connection with all the fans out there. I am the supreme wrestler of the nineties and beyond... Listen to the entrance I get every time I walk out of that entrance tunnel, that's a good sounding one. I'm not going to lay down for anybody. I'm going to do my job as a wrestler and win in the North American and
Intercontinental Wrestling Federation... I have a Rising Star Title defense coming up against the Mad Russian... You know something Russian? I hear you talking, but I don't see you wrestling any truly qualified talent!! Mad
Russian, you are fortunate that I am not making the decisions around here, otherwise you wouldn't be getting a shot at the North American and
Intercontinental Wrestling Federation Rising Star Title at all!! [- Hybrid pauses, and strokes his goatee. He takes a drink of his water, and continues. -] Hybrid: You know, there are quite a few people here in the North American and
Intercontinental Wrestling Federation who think that they are the main attraction. The "icon," if you will... Well, this argument needs to come to an abrupt halt, because you all know, that Hybrid is on his way to becoming the true icon of professional wrestling today!! I am going to lay down the law in the North American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation, like I have done in other federations... Mad Russian, you have the pleasure of tangling with the man who made wrestling what it is today, and you know as well as I know, that I will kick the living hell out of that fat, out of shape body of yours... I am a technical/submission wrestler, but that doesn't mean that Hybrid can't get alittle extreme it the situation calls for it. I am the supreme being in the
North American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation. Mad Russian, I am about to become your worst nightmare, and once I stepin the ring, I'm gunning for you Mad Russian!! In a split second, I will take you out. However, this not only goes for the Mad Russian... oh no!! Before him, I have a Triple Threat
Television Title match against The Outsider, and Russel Snow....
[- Hybrid pauses for a moment to take a drink of his water. He then continues... -] Hybrid: Outsider, Russel Snow, you two guys talk a big game, but I have got some news for you... As cliche' as it is, Hybrid is the new sheriff in town...
and gentlemen, just as you think you are going to get to go 'round one more time, Hybrid is thrown into the fray!! I am the next World Television Champion here in the North American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation, and gentlemen, this is what I have been striving for ever since I emerged here on the scene in the NAIWF. A chance to make a name for myself, not just as a rookie, but as a goddamn impact player!! That's why I have been in intense training for the past several weeks. Even more so now that I have a Triple
Threat match with two of the top stars in the North American and
Intercontinental Wresling Federation. Outsider, Russel Snow, if you thought you were in for a fight before I was thrown in... [- Hybrid smirks -] get ready for the beating of you goddamn lives!!
[- Hybrid takes a moment to peer out the window of the limousine, looking at the awesome view of the city from the freeway. He then returns his attention to the camera. -]
Hybrid: Now, as for you Mad Russian, do not think I am forgetting about you... Oh no, I have been training, working out, and killing myself to prepare for you as well... and when you are face to face with Hybrid, in the center of the squared circle, and you look dead straight into my eyes... You shall then know fear. Mad Russian, if you think for one second that you will get away with one simple move, you are mistaken my friend... Because Hybrid will not allow it!! I am on a rampage through the North American and Intercontinental Wrestling
Federation, and if you think some Intercontinental and American Wrestling
Federation refugee will take me out, you are sadly... sadly... sadly mistaken!!
Hybrid is a man on a mission, and you are in my way!! I do not intend to sit here and bullshit with you Mad Russian... I will go through you like a runaway freight train, and after you, Pain shall experience the same horrifying fate.
Gentlemen, and I am referring to all four of you: 'Russian, Outsider, Snow, and
Pain, I am the showstopper, the main event, and the future of the North
American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation... I am not going to let anything stand in my way... Be it one man, the Kingdom of Pain and Destruction, or even the mighty Coven!! I am the most magnificent professional athlete in the world today, and you know as well as I do that I don't take my wrestling matches easy. I am going to come at you with six-feet four inches, and two-hundred and eighty-two pounds of pure force and punishement!! I am the heir to the North American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation throne, known as the NAIWF Triple Crown World Title. I do, however, realize that this will not be accomplished in the near future. That is why as of right now I am concerning myself with you, Russel Snow... after I dispose of cripple The
Outsider, take him out of the equation, you shall feel the unbearable force of the Hybrid Bomb. Then, it shall be Hybrid walking in Judgement Day as the North
American and Intercontinental Wrestling Federation Television Champion... I'm out...
[- The scene begins to fade to black, as the limousine pulls into the parking lot of the hotel... -]
- Adam "I pray to Joe Pesci..."
