Home town: Nevada Ring Music: "The Dance of Eternity" by Dream Theater Alignment: heel to the core, but occasionally backs good guys Gimmick: Bob Muretic is a lying, backstabbing, hypocritical, egotistical, overly violent, disgusting piece of garbage. This is his gimmick. He has betrayed basically everyone that he was ever "friends" with, and will do so to anyone on a whim. He has absolutely no respect for anyone regardless of what their name is or what accomplishments they have made in the sport. He enjoys befriending little guys who are less successful than him, helping elevate them to a new level, and then crushing them and ruining their careers. And ruining careers is what Bob Muretic gets most of his kicks from. He's not satisfied with just beating a wrestler. He wants to make them look like a fool in front of the whole world and cause them to lose their respect. He's a thorn in the flesh, and one of the most hated athletes in locker rooms all over the country. Muretic only enjoys this too much, travelling around the world and making hordes of enemies wherever he goes. He doesn't seem to mind the fact that this causes him to get mauled by large groups of
"Bob-haters" wherever he goes. Finishing Move: "Another Won" Description of the finishing maneuver: First Bob hits the SPW Driver somewhere near the corner to nearly knock his opponant out. Then he climbs to the top turnbuckle turned so that he's facing the fans rather than his opponant. As he does this, his opponant groggily gets to his feet. Muretic then jumps backward off the turnbuckle, hooks his opponant's head in mid-air, and drives it into the mat with a DDT.
------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----- E-wrestling background: Title History: Bob Muretic has been wrestling since he's been 16, insisting that one day his experience would provide the stepping stone needed to become a huge gospel music star. He didn't start taking the career seriously until 1996 when he feuded with the Pizzaman in XCW under the name
Flaming Bob. Flaming Bob wrestled and ruined for three years thereafter in feds such as UWA, UIWF, WLW, and EGW with only moderate success, having won the UWA tag team titles along with Vile Vince Viper.
It wasn't until 1999 in SPW that he changed his name to "The Wildcard" Bob Muretic, in order to conform to the more "realistic" nature of the promotion. Muretic won the SPW Heavyweight Title the first night after defeating "Lucky Man" Dylan Dice and then advancing to a 10 man steel cage match to capture the belt. Though it was his greatest accomplishment to date, Muretic would end up throwing the belt down only a month later because of the fact that the SPW President would not stop other wrestlers from interfering in his matches. Muretic refused to be a champion that held the title and never once achieved a legitimate win. This action led to a falling out between Muretic and management, and as a result he was put out to pasture.
The DWF came only a month later, ran by Nick Demola. It was there that Bob Muretic had one of the most memorable feuds of his career with one "The Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop. Muretic would win this tango, capturing the DWF Atlantic Storm title. He lost the belt to Waldrop only a month later when the league was near collapse. Upon the return of the league in October, Bob Muretic put his name in and once again defeated Mike Waldrop to become 2-time DWF Atlantic Storm champion. He is the champion to this day, as the league has collapsed again before his scheduled match with Valtharius the Mad.
Bob Muretic was #190 on the RSPWF 200 in 1999, and #60 in 2000. He hopes to graduate to even higher heights in 2001, and is beginning with
NNEW. ------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------ Sample Roleplay:(this will determine your acceptance into the fed) News Broadcaster: Rumors surfacing from all over the world today that one of professional wrestling's most infamous side-shows...
News Broadcaster: As the sky grows dark over Las Vegas the people have begun to resign themselves to the reality of this tragedy...

News Broadcaster: It'll be a tremendous shock to me as I'm sure it'll be to people all over the world. I don't think the reality of this matter has quite set in yet...
[As the 747 commercial jet comes to a complete halt on the runway, four runway guys roll the stairs up to the hatch so that the passengers can descend from the plane. The press people gather at the foot of the steps as the door slides open. Then to the shock of everyone watching from the airport or from their televisions at home, a swarm of people come pouring from the tiny door. Over 100 people throw themselves from the the plane, some falling and breaking their legs on the pavement as their panicked screams are picked up by the microphones of the reporters that they're falling on. One would think that someone must have yelled
"FIRE!" from inside the plane. But as the last passenger exits and begins to run as far and as fast as he can from the plane, the reason for their panic becomes clear.]
Reporter: A great panic has erupted as the near 200 passengers have just literally thrown themselves from the plane. He has come back...dammit...<muffled> GO TO

Though the right side of his face may be considered attractive to some desparate women, the left side of his face would make the same women vomit. Muretic's mouth turns up in a half grin as he brushes his wavy brown hair back. There is complete silence, except for the flashing of cameras, as his cowboy boots click against the pavement. He walks up to a podium which was conveniently positioned near the terminal, and begins to adjust the microphone so that he can speak in it.]
Man in crowd: We thought you were dead! Other man in crowd: No, we WISH you were dead! [Bob Muretic tosses his hair back again and begins to fluff it around.] Woman in crowd: Will you heal my child? PAX TV here and I wouldn't want to upset Frank Metumucil. Man in crowd: Frank Metumucil...like the constipation formula...that's a good one Bob! Almost as brilliant as the pickled butt cheeks saga! YOU
SUCK BOB!! BOB: Thank you son. <not paying attention> Does anyone else have any questions? So just go away...that's just disgusting. Any more questions?

I just got the news that Nick Demola has placed you in a match for the first card...and your tag team partner is going to be Mike Waldrop!!

You know just like all of my many fans know that I've tagged up with Mick Waldrop on more than one occasion, and the whole thing proved to be frugal every time. [Muretic takes the cigar out of his mouth and stares out at the reporter.] BOB: I will admit that I got a late start in life. But age hasn't mellowed Bob Muretic. Man in crowd: Hey Bob, what's the color of the sky in your world? BOB: Well, I guess it depends on the weather. I sure want to thank you people for this fruitful interview time. It's good to be back in God's country.
[Bob slaps the microphone aside and turns around to walk back toward the terminal. A crowd of press people follow him the entire way, flashing pictures and trying to ask further questions. All of which are ignored as Muretic disappears into the crowd.]
