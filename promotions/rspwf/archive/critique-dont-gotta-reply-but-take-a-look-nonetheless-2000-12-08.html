[Critique] Don't gotta reply but take a look, nonetheless.

8 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just wrote this for N.E.W., fellas. I've put a bit of time into it, writing and rewriting because it's a Topic I'm very concious of so I wanted to represent it as best I could.
Enjoy - Mike E. ======================================================== [The visibility gradually increases upon a typical Northeastern United States
High School. Zooming in, passing the front law with a cement block with
NEW BEDFORD HIGH SCHOOL in red block letters across it, Cars are seen lining the entering Circle and near Parking Lot. Faculty members and students file into the School as Buses, carrying students, pull up to a distant Entrance, unloading it's passengers.
As the mass of people continue to enter the School, the entire imagery cuts inside the Building. Standing on a staircase, a rough amount of students sit patiently waiting for the door at the top to open. Suddenly, a loud bell rings as all of the Students quickly stand up and begin to walk up the Staircase.
Slowly fading into black, a white line of words begin to materialize.] Bells Guide You Through Your Day A Mere Ring Controls Your Entrance and Exit Yet You Say You're Operating On Your Own Accord [The imagery then cuts inside a Class Room. Students are seen, apathetically sitting in seats. Books and notebooks are sprawled over the desk as a Teacher rambles on. Quickly writing things down on the chalk board, remnants of the chalk flake off. As the camera pans around the room, posters on the wall are seen of Geographical maps. Various places of the worlds and facts about history. As the teacher lays the chalk down, the bell rings. With the sound of the bell, quickly the students rise and walk out..]
Teacher : Class is dismissed. Remember, Wednesday -- Study for your final exams tonight and tomorrow! <Sigh>
[The Teacher drops into his chair, leans back and reaches into his pocket. Lighting up a cigarette inside the class, the screen fades to black as the same caption begins to crawl up the screen.]
When the bell rings, you are allowed to leave. From there, predestination has you set for another class. Before you step into school, your day is set for you. Others, openly, pick your schedule. They run _YOUR_ Life! And this is how we are suppose to operate? When will we gain our freedom!? Confinement -- .. yet you're convinced we're all free. [The imagery now constructs within a Computer Room. Only about eight students occupy two pods of Machines while a Teacher sits at his desk, going over some papers. The Students all stare endlessly into the screen, dilligently typing and clicking away. One student reaches into his bag and pulls out a Snickers candy bar. As he peals the rapping off and takes a bite, the Teacher turns to him and is appauled. He quickly stands up and confiscates the candy.]
T : You know eating food near the Computers is out of the question. It's also against the Computer Commandments policy!
Student: But, I am hungry! Lunch isn't for twenty minutes! Why do I get Third Lunch Shift? It's late, food is always gone, and I have to sit in class all morning 'till noon! It sucks!
T : Well, tough. Eat breakfast, tell your mother to feed you. It isn't my fault you're hungry. <Looks Around> That goes for all of you too!
[The view fades to black. Then the screen turns fuzzy but the snow quickly turns black as a white caption appears.]
Being told when you can eat. Being told when you can't eat. Indulging at certain times, in respect to their ideas. Controlled enviroments, containment, and imprisonment. Is this how we think we're free? Do you all think you can do whatever you want? Think about that next time you dare to challenge authority. Your weak minds and inner-concious prevents you. Why? Afraid to get in trouble? Not willing to go against the grain? Your mind is too weak.. Someone telling me what to do? Someone controlling my actions? Never. [The last letters trail off as a new picture fades in. A man is sitting on a staircase with his head down to the ground. The top of his head, covered in very short reddish hair, slowly lifts as the man eyes the Camera. But this is no ordinary man. He's James Morgan. As he adjust his three metallic eye brow piercings, Morgan leans back, resting both elbows on the higher step. A black
T-shirt covers his upperbody with "? FREEDOM ?" on the chest. His typical, dirty and grungy jean shorts cover his lower body while a pair of black boots are tied tightly on his feet. Morgan cocks his head to the right, as he sighs in disgust.]
JM : Who is the bigger fool? The Fool himself or those who listen to him? A Man once told me, a good man learns from his own mistakes.. while a greater man learns from the mistakes of others. But as I look around this supposed
Institute of Education, I see a chain forming. Sheep-to-sheep, a growing constant amongst the paid Educators of this school. All, abiding by a ridiculous Set of Rules, enforcing them upon students. When it is evident the larger purpose is to impose their presence and paper thin power, then their knowledge.
[A deep sigh emits again.] JM : Growing up as a child - rules confined me to a mere room. My Parents disgust for me was devistating. They looked upon me as an omen from God.
Their pathetic God. That I was a message to them, that their sins were accumulating. And I? I was the symbol of what God would give them in return for their sins. I was their Satan. But where a normal man would loath the relation to Satan, I derived enjoyment from it. They thought that Cellar was a
Prison. To me? It was a Haven .. a Sanctuary warranting me security. I was secure from them. From their harsh words, their hands, and their silence.
Physical pain can make your future seem grim but mental pain can bring out your own perdition.
[Morgan sits up, resting both hands on his lap. Both of his eyes stare intently into the lens as an overwhelming expression of pure dejection masks his face.]
JM : They never cared about me. I was a mere prop in their Castle. Tossed the scraps but their silence .. them -- ignoring me .. would make death pale in comparison. Their neglect will forever cast me into emptiness. When alone,
James Morgan is in his true surroundings. But it didn't have to be that way.
It could've been changed. But you .. you _NEVER_ helped me. You could've saved me .. but now? It's too late. And for that, I can never forgive you.
My chronic lack of pardon on you is why you rest where you rest. The Flames of
Redemption burn hotter then the furnace you cast me into.
[Morgan stands up, overcome with emotion.] JM : So now you see .. that you created your own demise, and for that, you will remain permanently a dweller of the Den of Depression, but for now just see ..
[Leaning towards the Lens, Morgan's tone turns raspy and low.] The Epitaph was in your name .. _ALL ALONG!_ [Fade to black.]
