~ An RP From: "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd **

28 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Scene opens up backstage of the All-Pro Wrestling arena. APW's lead correspondant Claudia Battersby makes her way down the hall way with a camera man and begins talking to him ]
CB: Ya know, all of the time and effort I put into this company, and they give me this assignment, they know I hate him.
Camera Man: Claudia, he is one of the biggest draws in the company right now. An Interview with him is a great oppertunity. CB: Talking to this jerk, is never, ever a great oppertunity ... Here we go. [Claudia flinches, then knocks on a door which has a star on it that reads
"Fabulous." After a few seconds the door swings open, and there stands "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd wearing a towel around his waist and a "Fabulous One" T-shirt. He steps aside and gestures inward]
"Fabulous" FF: Oh My Goodness, Ms. Blunderbong, please do come in. I'm sure you have a million questions. I mean after all, if I had been in the audiance tonight, and watched my match up with uh ... umm ...
CB: Bruno Bordeaux Maybe? "Fabulous" FF: Oh yeah, whatever, not like it really matters, I doubt we'll be seeing that loser again anytime soon anyways. By the way, I didn't like the way you said that Ms. Butterbuns, if I was you I would show some respect. I mean after the show I put on out there tonight for these idiots you should have come running back here kissing my ass that you still have a job. Now where was
I? Oh yes, if I was in the audiance and watched my match up with whats his name, I would have been bombarded in the brain with so many questions. Like how did you get so good? Can I have your autograph? Why on earth did you choose to showcase your immense talents in a rinky dink operation like this?
Boy, you must be tickled pink, go right ahead and fire away. CB: What about the rest of the matches tonight? [ Freddy's expression changes to one of exaggerated shock. He looks at Claudia as if she is the stupidest person alive ]
"Fabulous" FF: What are you talking about?? What other matches!? The Grayson/Jap fight? Big deal, wouldn't have been anything without my expert commentary. Television Title match? Come on now, that was a joke. Brother
Boob doesn't deserve that title. I am the only man that these moronic fans want to see, you heard them out there, the sound was deafening. I bring in 90% of the television audiance at least, not to mention the thousands who are turned away each week, forced to dry there tears with there "We Love Freddy"
signs as they head back onto the bus, and off to the trailor park, there dreams of seeing the great one wrestle live dashed like a knife through the one piece of bread that mom has allotted for breakfeast, only to be shared by their fifteen brothers and sisters. Its sad really, very sad.
CB: Well then, how do you think your fans felt about the interference from one Night Prowler during your match.
[ A look of utter disgust forms on the face of the "Fabulous One". He takes a minute to compose himself, and then begins to speak. ]
"Fabulous" FF: It is a crime and a shame that anyone would come out during my match and attempt to interfere. I suppose I can't blame them however, I mean we all know that my match is the only match that the morons in the crowd wake up for. As for the culprate, I refuse to comment at all on the Anal Prowler other than that boy will get his next week on Terror, and he will see just why
Freddy Floyd is the most devastating man in the game today.
CB: Speaking of next week on Terror, you have a match up with Shawn "Scorpian" Viper. Now this is not the first time you two will meet in the squared circle, your battles in the ICW were well chronicaled.
[ The grin slowly slides from Freddy's face, as he turns to Claudia giving her an almost are you feeling alright type of look ]
"Fabulous" FF: Let me put it like this, in the All-Pro Wrestling federation,
Freddy Floyd is the cream of the crop. The best that there is. The climax of what otherwise is one damn weak orgasm is you see what I mean. But I suppose if I had to pick a number two, I mean way ... way ... way ... below me, it would be Shawn Viper. This kid has the determination, he just a moron.
Million dollar body, and a ten cent brain. Its a shame really, I could make this kid a star. Don't worry though Shawn, you'll get your fifteen minutes of fame just like everyone else who steps into the ring with "Fabulous" Freddy
Floyd. Maybe someday this kid will be a winner, but its highly unlikely. He is always so busy concerning himself with those mornic fans. Worst of all he associates himself with prissyboy Freddy Floyd wannabees like Tommy Lionheart.
CB: Your quite critical of Shawn Viper. He is one of the men who are the future of this buisness.
"Fabulous" FF: Let me explain something to you Ms. Beanybaby, Shawn Viper's future in this sport lasts for about one more week, until he steps into the ring with me. This guy doesn't have nothin' on Freddy Floyd. As far as the future of this buisness goes, especially the All-Pro Wrestling Federation, you are looking at it. I am the top guy in the game today, and if you don't belive that, then you are an even bigger loser than I thought you were. I am riding the biggest wave of momentum I have ever been on. In the APW, I am unstoppable. I have the skills, I have the smarts, and you better belive I have the Power of this company in my front pocket, and she's not just sittin there, belive you me. Next week on Terror, this will continue, except this time I am making a promice, and that promice is, someone is gonna get hurt, and
I mean hurt bad. And someone just might be Shawn Viper.
CB: Are you sure your not overlooking Mr. Viper? [ Freddy stands up and walks toward the door with a very annoyed look on his face ] "Fabulous" FF: Alright, look Ms. Batteredshrimp, I'll spell this out for you one more time. Next week on Terror, when I step into the ring with Shawn
Viper, he will recive a wrestling Lesson, like he has never recived in his entire life, and you can bank on that. I know this man you idiot, he has idolized me for years!
CB: Wait a minute, I never heard anything about that! "Fabulous" FF: And I'm sure these morons have yet to hear of your crab infestation, the fact remains its true. Viper has idolized me since first laying eyes on me in the International Championship Wrestling Federation. If you ask me, he's a queer, but thats beside the point. The fact remains, he has wanted to be me for two years. Watch him in the ring, he is always trying to emmulate my moves, my strategy, and everything else I do. Its simply too bad that he will never be even half of the man that I am. And when I am done with him on Tuesday, that idiot won't even have a leg to stand on ... litterally.
Now I suggest Ms. Bloodybush, that you take your skank ass, and get the hell out of here, you couldn't possibly have any more of our annoying questions at this time. Now get out!
[ Freddy grans Claudia and shoves her out the door. He then steps right up the camera ] "Fabulous" FF: Viper, next week, your ass is mine. I own you son, and don't forget it! [ Floyd then pushes the camera man out the door and slams it shut. A Shaken Claudia Battersby quickly makes her way out of the cameras view, and it soon fades to black ]
