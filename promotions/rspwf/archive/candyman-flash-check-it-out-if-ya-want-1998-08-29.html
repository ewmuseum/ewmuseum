[CandyMan Flash] ---> Check It Out If Ya Want..

29 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is my latest IWC Roleplay... =-=-=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=-=- [The scene opens up in a dark dusty road, it seems to be in the middle of nowhere and with that the sounds of the night can be heard.. Then a pair of headlights can be seen in the far distance as the come closer and closer towards the current situation of the camera.. with that the car then hovers over the camera as the scene switches to a place inside the car from the backseat view..]
[You can see the back of a mans head in from the view and on the ground in the backseat of the car there is a bunch of equipment such as shovels, hammers and other materials. The radio is turned of as the constant buzzing of the car begins to get irritating to some. From the mirror you can see the eyes of a man which looks familiar, as he concentrates on the road as he drives..]
[Suddenly the car comes to a stop, as the man takes to keys out of the car and places then in his pocket as he gets out of the car and the scene is all quiet for a few seconds. Out of nowhere a noise can be heard as the side door opens and a man with huge arms takes the utensils which where found on the ground. The camera switches to a view looking on the man as he has his back to the camera ready to close the door. He then slowly turns around to reveal it is...The CandyMan!..]
[The CandyMan then walks towards a gate with the equipment in his hands. He looks at the sign next to the gate as it reads 'Cemetery', as he smirks to himself in a weird manner and continues his way past the gate. During this point he closes the gate behind him, looking towards the car while he says some things..]
CandyMan: "One more day...[Places one finger in the air...] Then this will be your _Permanent_ resting grounds EMT... No more running around rampant like the way you have been since the beginning..[Shakes the one finger..] No, No!
Everything you have always done and looked forward to doing..._No More_, because your end is near...one day away to be exact.."
[CandyMan smirks as he turns around and continues to walk across the path leading towards the actual resting grounds of the dead people. Carrying the equipment way above his head, he proceeds to walk in the darkness as he finally stumbles upon some graves, looking at them as he says a pray to himself...]
CandyMan: "Read these graves here EMT... family lost, look at these tombs..[signals to the tombs..] They lost people in there families due to illnesses, accidents.. god’s hands! These people are living there lives today...without someone that they have always cared about! These people have lost a bit of there lives...[CandyMan nods his head in disappointment..] You don't deserve to be with these people...oh, no...
This people are missed here...There is no way you deserve to be here...why? because _Nobody_ will miss you..._Nobody_!" [CandyMan says another quick prayer for these souls who are at peace as he walks away from them and continues to walk through the path.. He then comes past another set of graves as he once again pauses and stares at these graves...he bows his head down as he says somethings..]
CandyMan: "Look at these graves here EMT... children dead before they have even had a chance to breathe more then once on this earth! [CandyMan looks at the tombs..] There parents awaited for 9 months for there arrival and in the instance of one second there arrival had come to an end! [Upset at this..]
Read what it says... [Pointing to tomb..] 'My son, my blood, my life...gone'! Can you imagine what it would have been like to witness this! Imagine the pain...the suffering! How could something that took so long to make, be ruined in such a sort period...How? [Angered by this..] You don't deserve to be with these people.. oh no..
The people...or should I say the young children which are here...They are so unfortunate...that someone like you gets life in this world over them! But there is no need to worry....your demise is coming near!"
[CandyMan says one more thing as he does the sign of the cross and looks above, he then stop for a second before continuing on in the path.. He then stops as he comes near a bunch of some more graves, which are full of pictures of old people. CandyMan stands there as he pays his condolences to the people, before talking..]
CandyMan: "Now look over here EMT...take a look! [Pointing to the graves in front of him...] They spent there required time on earth, they achieved what they wanted to achieve, there where once young! These people here fulfilled there lives! From the beginning of there lives to The end, they where respected individuals... people loved them, family took care of them and they grew fond of the world...but with old age, there time came! They knew the time was coming for them soon... they did not fear death anymore....they instead grew used to the feeling of death everyday, not knowing if they would ever awake! [Speaks with emotion..] You defiantly do not belong here with these people...Oh No, Defiantly Not..
These people stood for everything good in this world...harmless, happy, similar to butterflies in my own opinion...and most importantly they where
_Brave_! You EMT....your a _Coward_!"
[CandyMan walks away from the graves as he continues to walk on a different path, he then comes across an area filled with nothing, but dirt, rubbish and a huge dog dropping on the ground. He them smirks as he continues..]
CandyMan: "A matter of fact, EMT...you do not belong anywhere over there....because everything there is to good for a man such as you! That is why I come here.... this kind of environment deserves to be for a man such as you! It is full of no environmental life...dirt, rubbish...everything which you stand for! [Looks around..] You don't deserve anything which everyone else has got...oh no, you deserve much, much less EMT! Right there looks like a good spot...[Points to dog left over...] But still in my opinion...that itself is still to good for you! _Everything_ is to good for a man such as you...But it will do...!"
[CandyMan drops all his equipment, as he only remains with a shovel in his hand. He then looks at the camera and smirks as he digs it right where the dog dropping is and takes one huge amount of dirt out of the ground and throws it behind him. He then places the shovel in front of him as he mentions something..]
CandyMan: "EMT... Tonight I prepare your grave...[pointing to grave..] Tomorrow I prepare you....and then on Monday when everything has been settled and finished...I _Commence_ the _Funeral_! [CandyMan smirks as lighting goes of behind him...] Your days are limited from here on in, but remember one thing.....when your Funeral has ended, you will live a life of....
........_ETERNAL SWEET DREAMING_!!!!!!!!!" [CandyMan, begins to laugh as he raises his shovel in the air and continues to dig while the scene fades out..]
