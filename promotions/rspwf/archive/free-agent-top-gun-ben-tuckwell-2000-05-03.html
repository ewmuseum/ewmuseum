[FREE AGENT] "Top Gun" Ben Tuckwell

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, that's right. After a couple of my feds closing their doors, I'm searching for another league to participate in. I have a couple of requirements and if you meet them and like the sample roleplay provided, send me some mail and I'll take a look at your fed.
@@@@@@@@@@ REQUIREMENTS @@@@@@@@@@ - booked - minimum of 10 wrestlers - name doesn't contain the words 'extreme or hardcore' @@@@@@@@@@ SAMPLE ROLEPLAY @@@@@@@@@@ {The scene opens overlooking Ben Tuckwell's house in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. The camera takes a moment to survey the surroundings of beautiful gardens and an outdoor pool before words scroll up from the bottom of the screen which read:
A DAY IN THE LIFE OF RRW'S BEN TUCKWELL SATURDAY JANUARY 29th, 9:33 AM The camera then zooms in at the house until it reaches the front door where a reporter with dark brown hair wearing a black suit is standing. The reporter knocks at the door and after a brief wait, the door swings open to reveal Ben Tuckwell wearing RRW boxer shorts and a long sleeved Milwaukee
Brewers jumper. His hair is all messed up and it is obvious that he has just woken up. Tuckwell invites the reporter and cameraman into his house and he leads the way through the living room and into the kitchen where breakfast is set up on a plate. On the plate is six eggs, a chicken breast and a variety of steamed vegetables with a glass of milk to the left. Tuckwell offers the cameraman a seat and begins to eat his food, talking between mouthfuls.}
Ben Tuckwell: "You know I eat about 1 kilogram of meat a day and to be honest, my food bill costs almost as much as my damned mortgage! For the kiddies at home, I describe the way I eat as clean, very high protein, low salt so I don't retain water and low fat which means lots of lean red meat, turkey and skinless chicken! I need to take a shower before I talk to you properly so why don't you uh... take a commercial or something!
{Tuckwell finishes off his glass of milk before heading off down the hallway as the screen fades to black.}
{The scene re-opens on Ben Tuckwell's bedroom door. The following caption appears on the screen in white letters once again:
BEN TUCKWELL'S HOUSE 01/29/2000, 10:08 AM Then the door opens and Ben Tuckwell steps out looking much neater than previously. He is wearing black sweat pants, Nike running shoes and a ski jacket protecting him from the cold. He goes down the hallway past many doors until he is in the living room which looks like it just came out of the Simpsons cartoon. Tuckwell flops down onto the brown couch and picks up the remote to turn on the VCR and television and he sees Dark Derecho with his latest interview from the hotel room.}
Dark Derecho: "Damn... I got scared for a second. I thought Tuckwell was going to be another Chaos... another Rage, and be a no show until the day of the match. Good thing he showed up. Now I won't be bored to death...
actually, I really can't be bored to death since I'm already there. That interview from Tuckwell almost put me in a coma."
{Ben Tuckwell presses pause on the video as Derecho's valet Samantha starts to talk.} Ben Tuckwell: "Since this is a day in my life, I have to make sure of saying a few words about the start of your interview Dark Derecho! If my interview scared you to death and almost put you in a coma, just imagine this picture.
Before we do this, make sure you close your eyes and take a few deep breaths just so you get the picture!"
{Tuckwell takes a deep breath and thinks for a second before speaking again.} Ben Tuckwell: "Dark Derecho is standing face to face with the Top Gun Ben
Tuckwell with little brown and yellow stains on his tights. I say a couple of words which scare the crap out of him and he begins to run around crying like a chicken with no head. I punch him in the head once and he goes down and I make the pinfall. That's how pathetic you are Dark Derecho and that's how easy it's going to be for somebody as talented as I am! We shall watch more of this interview later but it's time for my 1 mile morning run, so hold on and I'll be back in a couple of minutes!"
{The Top Gun unzips his ski jacket to reveal a tight fitting white t-shirt which shows his ripped chest muscles. Tuckwell exits the living room and out the front door for his run as the screen cuts out to some commercials.}
Voice-Over: "January 30th 2000, Ring Rage Wrestling presents the greatest spectacle the world has ever seen from a wrestling event. Plenty of live action coming to you from San Antonio, Texas with all titles on the line.
Order now and prepare for Initiation, the showcase of superstars!" {After the commercial, we cut back into Ben Tuckwell's house the very moment the front door opens and the Top Gun returns from his run. Tuckwell has a small bead of sweat dripping from his forehead and he wipes it away with the towel that he is carrying and flops down onto the couch once again. A small timer comes up on the screen and it reads '12:45 PM'.}
Ben Tuckwell: "Gee I love being a wrestler! Hey reporter, go get my lunch from the fridge!"
{The reporter reluctantly gets up and goes into the kitchen as Tuckwell looks at the camera.}
Ben Tuckwell: "I ran that one mile in just over 4 minutes and I may even be able to compete in the Sydney 2000 Olympics in Australia! Heh!"
{The reporter returns carrying a white plate that holds six turkey sandwiches and is holding a Sprite drinkbottle in his other hand. He hands them to Tuckwell who begins squirting the beverage down his throat and he eats the food, finishing the task at hand quite easily.}
Ben Tuckwell: "When I'm touring, I usually carry around a cooler around with me to lower the costs. Now you just watch some cable or something cameraman and reporter while I take a nap because I need to get some sleep before catching the plane to San Antonio."
{Tuckwell stands up and picks up a small container with vitamin pills in it. He puts one pill in his mouth and squirts some water from his drinkbottle into his mouth and walks away back to his bedroom as the screen fades to black.}
{The second part of a day in the life of Ben Tuckwell opens in the living room of his house. He is sitting next to the reporter on the couch and they are watching the first interview Dark Derecho did after defeating Erik Rave.
The time comes up on the screen once again: 2:15 PM and Tuckwell stops the video and looks at the camera.}
Ben Tuckwell: "Are you crazy Derecho? I think I misheard you when you said that nobody wants to see Ben Tuckwell become champion and I think I also heard you say that I use 15 minute chinlocks and 30 minute armbars! Well I'm afraid both of these statements are totally false because I am the man that everybody wants to see as champion... I am a boyhood rolemodel! As for the chinlocks and armbars, why would the top aerial daredevil in wrestling use pathetic moves like those? I bring the crowd excitement and that's what they want to see! Let's look at the rest of the other interview shall we?"
{Tuckwell fast forwards past the piece he has already seen until he gets to the part where Samantha exits the room.}
Dark Derecho: "Tuckwell, you tell a lot of lies, you know that? You say my chance is there, but you can't see me taking that step towards superstardom.
You need to wash your eyes with some water, get some glasses or something to correct that horrid vision of yours because I already am in superstardom! I have already taken that step and I am at the world championship..."
Ben Tuckwell: "This is [CENSORED]!" {Tuckwell fast forwards through the interview before pressing stop completely.} Ben Tuckwell: "I've seen most of that crap already! Derecho, you are not in superstardom yet and you won't be until you defeat somebody with the brilliance of myself fairly. You are nowhere near me on the ladder, I'm way above you and there's no chance of you catching me... EVER! As for your comments about having the best record here, 6 wins and 1 draw equals a winning percentage of around 86 percent... 4 wins, 0 losses and 0 draws is
100 percent winning percentage and it can't get any better than that Mr.
Derecho! You know what, if I wasn't on this card for Initiation there wouldn't be a crowd at all! The buyrates would plummet but isn't commissioner Frost lucky that he managed to grab hold of me! I deserve the title and you are just another one of the men on my list to defeat here in
RRW so I can achieve the best winning streak the sport has ever seen!"
{Ben stands up and looks harder into the camera.} Ben Tuckwell: "I'll bring everything to the match and I guess you should too, but the fact of the matter is that you don't even compare to me and
I'll wipe my feet all over your fallen body! Derecho, I live by my words and unlike what you think about them being empty and washed up, all will eventuate and I will have the last laugh of the day. You talk about fans coming to see you but this week it's true, they've come to see you get your ass kicked by a true champion! Send all the threats you got, because I enjoy listening to them because it's kind of like a comedy with Steve Urkel starring, telling somebody that he's going to kill them! Worthless words that don't mean a thing to me and I admit that you probably will be my biggest challenge here in Ring Rage Wrestling but not all challenges are hard and that phrase can be used about you! Talk about who you've defeated so far but that's the past, I am the future... THE FUTURE OF RRW! Time goes on forever Derecho and I am the immortal representation here in RRW, the man who cannot be beaten! Try your best but for the latest craze in fashion...
it's NOT going to be enough! {Tuckwell sighs and looks at his wristwatch.} Ben Tuckwell: "It's not even three o'clock yet so I guess I'll go down to the gym and do a bit of training on my abdominals and triceps before catching that plane! You've been watching a day in the life of 'Top Gun' Ben
Tuckwell, RRW's next World Heavyweight Champion... so long!
