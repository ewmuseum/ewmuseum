[Critque] - [ Uscw Flash for Outlaw . .. ] - [ Feedback appreciated ]

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well some others have done it latley , and had some comments made. So I figured
I would give it a shot, been a while since I done this . .. but what the hell .
. Why not . . Gonna send One for all four of my currenty used characters [ The annoying sound of an older model clock is heard, as we look on at a completly blank screen with a small USCW logo in the bottom left corner, preparing us somewhat for what is to come.]
TICK . .. TOCK . .. .TICK . .. TOCK . .. [ The clock continues to tick at it's same pattern as most clocks would do. The sound continues to be the only thing we can hear, until suddenly . ..]
Voice : " Oh give me a break . .. " [ The screen slowly changes from a blank screen, to a view of the older clock we have been listening to. The clock is perched up on a tan colored wall, setting in the middle of it all alone. The clock reads.]
12 : 01 AM [ Both hands of the clock continue to move, as the gold outline of the clock shines glaringly back at the camera.]
Voice : " I know he's there . ..Look at the time. He isn't even allowed to be out this late, I don't think."
[ The camera pans back a touch, and we see below the clock setting at a small wooden table is the Outlaw. His nose is bandaged slightly cause of the impact of last week's Impact. A phone sets on the table infront of Outlaw.]
Outlaw : " What would an ' All American ' kinda guy be doing at Midnight on a
Sunday night ? I have been trying to get in touch with Lane since the USCW released there next line up, revealing once more there horrible booking talents."
[ Outlaw reaches down picking the phone back up, he dials some numbers quickly before putting the phone to his ear. . .. We set in silence for a moment, as
Outlaw looks towards the camera somewhat aggrevated.]
* SLAM * [ Outlaw slams the phone back down on the table, and rubs his chin with the hand he just slammed the phone with. His other hand rest in his lap, clinched tight enough to be considred a fist.]
Outlaw : " I'm not a hard guy to get along with. This kinda stuff here bothers me when I can't get in touch with someone I need, but I can handle it. I can be very patient, and even a southern gentleman at times, but people can only bend so far. It seem's like people don't want me to be my laid back self, it seem's people want to try and twist my arm a lil to much . . .Really I'm not looking for this, but a couple things kinda twist me the wrong way on the inside."
[ Outlaw stops rubbing his chin, and holds one single finger up into the air.] Outlaw : " Messing with my wife is one . .." [ Outlaw raises another finger, each finger on the side of the middle one is now raised, prolly scaring some of the people at home just tuning in that we are watching an Westside gang member.]
Outlaw : " Secondly is telling me how to treat my wife . . ." [ Outlaw lowers both fingers, then slowly raises the middle one up. A censor covers his finger on the screen.]
Outlaw : " And most importantly is losing . .I hate losing . .. Avoid those three things, and everything will be fine. You leave me alone, I will prolly leave you alone."
[ Outlaw takes a deep breathe, then lets it out slowly as he glances back up towards the clock.]
Outlaw : " Last week though things didn't follow the three step guideline to everyone's safety . .. People got upset about the words I said to _MY WIFE_
People put there nose into _MY BUSINESS_ and in the end _MY TEAM_ lost our match . . .
Which all led to the hanging of a pro wrestler. It was a shame, really it was, but it was .. . damn sure fun to do."
[ Outlaw puts his hands together, putting them up close to his face giving off a look of deep thought on his face.]
Outlaw : " Yes you heard me right. All of you USCW old school guys can put your jaw back where it belongs, and stop letting it drop to the floor . . I said it was _FUN_ to do, and if I could do it all over, I would.
Which means in two weeks I might just DO IT AGAIN . .. Lane Cheney & Outlaw
Versus Kai Lewis & Rebel equals perfect oppurtunity for me to continue where I left off. Uscw might be trying to ' censor ' me, and USCW might be trying to control my actions, but what you saw last week was just the start. The blood .
. .The pain . .. The agony . .. It's just getting started. Alot of you people don't remember me from my deed's in the mid 90's . .. I am known for Barbwire matches, Cactus and Scorpion deathmatches . . .Turning people towards Christ cause there scared they might not live much longer. All that stuff is _ME_..."
[ Startling Outlaw, the phone rings . . .He smirks a bit as he picks it up.] Outlaw : " This is Outlaw . . .<< Long Pause as he listens >> No you can't come home yet.."
* CLICK * [ Outlaw hangs up the phone, and winks to the camera evily.] Outlaw : " That was my lovely wife, who got locked out of the house tonight cause of back talking me infront of others on Impact. Everyone follows my guidelines, or else ANYONE can suffer . ..Usually it's a wrestler, occasionally it's a friend, and sometimes it is even my beautiful wife. Smart & beautiful can sometimes be a problem.
Luckily there isn't any problems that have been formed so far in this world, in which I can't solve one way or another."
[ Outlaw rolls his neck slowly, and continues to speak.] Outlaw : " I am handling the one with my wife, It will be solved by the next time I enter an arena to wrestle a match, but this other problem seem's to not want to just fade away, it appears that problem wants to be _PUT AWAY_ . . .
Which can be arranged . .. I'm not sure why Rebel got mad at me when I threw him out of the rumble at Road to Glory, it was just business . . I am not sure why Kai Lewis has decided to be a white knight in shining armor, but what I am sure of is that this will come to a hault. Not during this tag match, I'm not stupid . .. I have been around the block a few times, but shortly after at the lil Pay Per View USCW is putting together . .. Things will cease ..Things will change .. .Things will never be the same."
[ Outlaw stands up from the table, and glances back up towards the clock.] Outlaw : " I'm not talking about the past . .. Nor am I talking in some ' western ' accent . .. Kai Lewis . . .Rebel . .. Snap into reality. This is
_REAL_. I am angry man of large size, with a partner on my side who knows more wrestling holds than you know words in the American dictionary.
Your currently heading towards a backyard meeting with the big dogs of the pen, and your still only pissing like a puppy...."
[ Outlaw smirks.] Outlaw : " You guys think about that, while I try to contact my partner. As always, You can . ..
Quote The Outlaw . . _NO MORE_. ." [ Outlaw moves forward passing the camera's view completly.] [ Fade Out ]
