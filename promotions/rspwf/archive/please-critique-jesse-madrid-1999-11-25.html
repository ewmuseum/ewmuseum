[please critique] Jesse Madrid

25 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Whoever is reading this now, thanks for critiquing me and give me any suggestions you can. Thanks.
::Scene:: A frosty breeze fills the air in one of the remote corners of Alaksa. The sun is not seen but it's light fills the sky from beyond the horizon. The camera pans from left to right, showing the baren land of the Alasken forest. The grasses and weeds are dried out and the gound looks to be dead.
From amongst the forest comes a man running into the field. It is Jesse Madrid. He is wearing a sweater and sweats, and a gray benie on his head. He breaks through the branches of the trees and continues running in the open land. Hopping over logs and high-stepping through the snow. He stops and he puts his hands on top of his head and begins pacing back and forth. He stops to look in the distance. A light trail of smoke is seen and the smell of burning wood fills the air. Jesse Madrid begins to run again and the camera fades. Fade back to a cabin. Jesse Madrid walks in the door of this, seemingly, newly built cabin. The inside of the cabin is not lavish, it is just a lower floor level and a loft above. A fire trickles out of the fireplace that is set a blaze. Jesse Madrid climbs up to the loft and he comes down with a blanket rapped around him. He walks to the stove were a kettle is steaming. He poors out hot tea into a large tin cup. He walks to the fire place and he sits in front of it. He sips some of the steaming tea and he speaks.
Jesse Madrid: [takes off benie] I would like to thank Guy Winter right now for putting up a hell of a fight. Guy, you took me far in our match. You surprised me with the talent that you did have. And you didn't give up one bit, and for_THAT_I respect you. [sips from cup] I have now advanced into the next round of the UwF IC Title tournement. And my place in that tournement is now well deserved. I think that I proved a lot in my first UwF matchup. One key thing that I showed was that I was able to stand up to anything that was thrown at me. I didn't back down once. I took every hit and I kept coming back for more. I also showed that if neccesery, I can get brutal. That was a hardcore as it comes. I broke Guy Winter as many times as he broke me. But I overcame the pain to gain the advantage. I am not afraid to pick up a weapon and crash it against a mans skull. I fear nothing. This goes out to every man in this federation...If you have any questions to my abilites, you have seen me, and if you still question my abilites, make a chalenge. I will gladly accept any chalenge just so long as I am promised a fight. [removes blanket and he walks outside of the cabin]
Jesse Madrid: I have realized now what it will take to gain my place as the UwF
IC champion. It is going to take pure pain and suffering. Not just giving those things I just stated, but recieving them as well. Strength...stamina, agression...All key factors in the future. I am walking into this tournament as the most prepared man. And to prepare myself even more I have set a standard for myself. I will expect nothing less than perfection from myself.
[opens up a wooden chest and pulls out three blocks of wood] When I step into the ring I will expect to land every move I perform, hit every move with as much power as itended. So when my next match comes...expect perfection....and nothing less.
[takes logs into cabin and puts them on the fire] Jesse Madrid: There are two men that are fighting on Tuesday that are fighting for the right to face me. These two men are BMF and Downer. Both of these men seem to be an equal match for eachother. As for an equal match with me?
[laughs then stares with a serious look] Never. No man could ever equal the talent and perfection that is Jesse Madrid. ESPECIALLY two men like BMF and
Downer.
[sits on a cot] BMF, your skills in the ring and out of the ring are highly amature at best. I've watched you walk out to the ring_COUNTLESS_times and give similar boring speeches. For all the talk you do, I would expect you to have a good skill in the ring. Other wise all of those words would be spoken for nothing.
But then, I watched you in the ring [laughs] It was as if I was watching my son wrestling his friends on his trampoline. You tried to do moves that dont even exist. Some call moves like those "inovative" but they dont qualify as
"inovative" if you miss all of them. What makes you think that you have what it takes to fight against Me? Sure you may beat Downer but the most difficult task comes when you fight me. With the moves you perform and at the rate you land them, you have no chance. BMF, at least you are representative of yourself. More than I can say about Downer. I have not even seen this man speak for himself. But only time will tell about our friend "The Downer."
[takes off boots] Jesse Madrid: I have found it very_BENIFICIAL_to watch the men I may come up against. This Tuesday I plan on watching you two fight it out. I will be watching for weaknesses and watching for what you base your attacks and deffenses on. I hope that you two can put on an entertaining match...otherwise
[smirks] I have a boring night ahead of me. [walks to a closet and pulls out a suit and lays it on the cot]
Jesse Madrid: one guy I want to personaly say fuck you to is Johnathan Talon. I had a little previous knoledge of you and I respected you. But what you did earlier today dissapointed me. You basicly did something that you never do.
You disrespected the federation you are in. For that, your going to be the mark for every man in this federation. You talked about how you have seen no talent in this federation, but yet, we havn't even seen you put up a good effort? Your a hipicritical bastard Talon. And I am going to make you pay for it. You want a chalenge? Heres one for you. I chalenge your bitch ass to a match in the UwF ring. Are you game? If so then bring it on. And stop wasting our time with your little pussy rants about how good you are. [begins to undress] As for the rest of the UwF, be ready to recognize me as your IC champion soon. And as always...Dropping you from Madrid like a bonafide maniac.
::Scene:: Jesse Madrid finishes undressing and puts his suit on. The camera fades out to a clearing near the cabin. The sky is silent and suddenly a loud noise fills the air. A hellicopter is seen. It comes down and lands in the clearing. The co-pilot hops out and walks to the cabin. He enters and he lets Madrid know that their ready to go. Jesse Madrid pauses for a minute, then he whipes off his shoulder and he tells the man that his ready. He enters the helicopter and they fly away.
