[SAMPLE RP] Dark Alleys *PLEASE CRITIQUE*

7 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Two men stand in a dark alley, both in black tights, black shirt, black boots, black gloves, and black ski masks with black mullets poking out the back.]
[But it's dark. Really dark. Really REALLY dark. So you can't see anything.] [They begin to speak, but their voices are filtered, like an "eyewitness" or "car thief" on America's Most Wanted. But I don't know how to type garbled voices, so I'll just type 'em normal.
Pretend, okay?] [The first one, on the left, stands approximately 5'8" - 5'10" and has a stocky build. He is a white male, aged 45 - 50 with no distinguishing marks. He is The Rapist.]
[The other, not on the left, stands approximately 6'1" - 6'3" and has a slender build. He is also a white male, aged 35 - 40 with no distinguishing marks. They call this man The Violator.]
[If you see either one please call your local Police Dept.] Rapist: I am the RAPIST!! Violator: I am the VIOLATOR!! Rapist: We are the shadows which move. The fear in your sister's heart as she moves through the empty downtown streets.
Violator: Whenever she passes an alleyway or turns a corner her heart races. Because of fear, but also because out excitement.
Rapist: Women find us irresistible. They are our sex slaves. Violator: They need to be mistreated and abused. It is God's will. Besides, we have SEX APPEAL!! Rapist: When they see us wrestle they know that men like us are far superior to boys like you.
Violator: I am the VIOLATOR!! Rapist: I am the RAPIST!! Both: And if you don't like it then you shouldn't have dressed that way!! [Fade to black. But that's pointless because it's dark. Remember?] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
