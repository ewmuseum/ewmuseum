[Sample Roleplay: Intro to LVPWA] [Scene opens up to a camera crew and LVPWA representative Brian Clark standing in front of a hotel room door.
Mr. Clark is dressed in a blue pin stripe suit. He looks to be in his early 20's. A tad bit too young to have such an important job as "LVPWA Rep." He stands there rubbing his palms together. Sweat beads down his forehead. He is obviously very nervous. The other two men are dressed in blue jeans and gray LVPWA t-shirts.
One holds a camera. The other holds a long device which has a microphone attached to the end of it. Mr. Clark is about to knock on the door but hesitates and turns around and speaks.]
Clark: A-Are we sure this is h-his room? [The guys assure Mr. Clark it is.] Clark: Positive? Hmm, ok. [Mr. Clark turns around slowly. Takes a deep breath. Exhales and then sheepishly knocks on the wooden door. He waits. No answer.]
Clark: Well ma-maybe we better go. Seems no one is there. [A rumbling is heard on the other side of the door. A voice is heard saying "God damn it!" Some fumbling is heard and the lever on the door starts to turn. Mr. Clark steps backwards letting the door open freely. A figure emerges dressed in a white towel and nothing else. The figures long curly hair dangles in front of his face, very wet. He stands at 6'6" tall. His eyes are sinister in appeal. His muscular frame glisten's with water. It's obvious he was in the middle of taking a shower. He looks up at Mr. Clark with a look of utter disdain.]
Clark: Hello, my name is Brian Clark from the LVPWA. I believe our off-office has been in contact with you the last several weeks about a position is our… federation?
[Camera once again focuses on the figure in the doorway. He cocks his head to the side. A weak smile crosses his familiar face then turns into a evil stare.]
Clark: I mean, I know you told us "no" several times. (looks onto his clipboard) Well it was more like "Go f*ck yourselves," but yeah. However, we believe we have a better offer for you sir.
[As the "familiar" looking man guides Brian Clark into the room, he almost stumbles off his feet at how plain it is... you'd expect a bigger and better room from anyone associated with coming to the LVPWA. The camera zoom around and show a huge Queen size bed in the center of the room with a desk beside of it and a television on the dresser in front of it. Clothing is tossed on the unkempt bed, the dresser and even over the lamp on the far side. The bathroom door is closed; shower still running. The man takes a sit in the only chair in the room. As Mr. Clark is left sitting nervously on the bed. He notices the bed looks "used" but says nothing. He fumbles with some paperwork before speaking.]
Clark: What we have planned on offering you is a lucrative deal. The highest paid talent in the company. That will always been guaranteed mind you. You'll have your own dressing room at every show. Limousines and lear jets. Only the best, for someone of your stature. So Mr. LOCO we believe this is the best deal in both our interests?
[The camera turn to focus on the figures face. He is revealed to be none other than the "Legend" himself. LOCO, yes, THAT LOCO!]
LOCO: You know kid, you got moxy. I've never really considered LVPWA before now. Always seemed to be something less if you get my gist. Do ya?
Clark: (no answer) LOCO: I asked you a question kid! Clark: (hurry's to answer) Oh yes! By all means, you are correct sir. LOCO: But however… [Clark looks like someone just shot his dog. Disappointment covers his face.] LOCO: .. if I'm going to do this we need to add a couple more things to that contract sunshine.
[Clark's face begins to beam again. His spirits picking up.] Clark: Oh yes, yes! Anything! You just name it! LOCO: I have a "friend" I want to have a contract too. My friend needs some work. Also, second… a no "fire"
clause. For my friend also. What do you say about that? [Clark smiles.] Clark: Consider it done. Lets just add that to this contract here. [Clark takes his pen and adds some writing to the contract. He makes the necessary changes. He scribbles some more notes. Then pauses. He looks up at the Legend and speaks again.]
Clark: Here you go champ! [Clark takes the contract off the clipboard and hands it to LOCO. He then takes the clipboard and tosses it back on the bed. LOCO reads over every detail on the contract as Clark patiently waits.]
LOCO: This will make you look good in Pillow's eyes huh kid? [Clark responds confidently.] Clark: How could it not? I mean, ah, you're a legend. You are the cream of the crop. Mr. Pillow is going to love this. He said "do whatever it takes."
[LOCO nods. Then proceeds to finish reading the contract. He takes a pen from the table and signs his name on the "binding" contract. He then stands up and speaks.]
LOCO: I'm sure Pillow is going to be very happy. See you kids soon. [Clark reaches back to grab the clipboard before getting up. His hand reaches over the bed sheets. He hand jerks back and a look of displeasure runs over his face.]
Clark: Whatthehell? LOCO: Just a little bit left from me sealing the deal earlier kid. Trust me… the deal is "iron clad" and tight.
[LOCO smirks. Clark quickly grabs his clipboard. Then he gets up and starts to leave with the camera crew.]
LOCO: Boys, before you go… why don't you let me introduce you to my friend… [As LOCO demonstrates to the bathroom door, Brian Clark steps back, not knowing what to think. His mouth almost drops open as the cameras start at the feet and travel all the way up the most beautiful stems you'd ever see on a girl and directly to their face. It doesn't register at first, as this fox stands there, clad in only a white towel around her body, covering the necessary parts. Her shoulder length blonde hair is drenched wet, lying on her shoulders tamely. She smiles a full smile, almost grimly... this is none other than:
"Sensuous" Samantha Bevins …herself. As you saw her last time, just a lot more sexier and certainly NOT shyer.] Sensuous Samantha: I'm back… and like LOCO said: it is… Iron Clad… [She rubs her slender hands up and down over he towel covered body and lets it rest between her legs.]
Sensuous Samantha: AND tight. [She smiles. Brian Clark and the rest of the crew just stand there shocked. This lady who s wreaked havoc in the past in now in the LVPWA. The reality hits Clark like a ton of bricks and he begins to scream..]
Clark: YOU CAN'T DO THIS! YOU CAN'T! PILLOW WILL KILL ME! [LOCO waves the contract in the air and smiles. He then pushes them out the room. Before closing the door Mr.
Clark looks back at the irresistible Samantha draped in nothing but a towel and a smile. She raises her hands and blows a kiss in their direction.]
Sensuous Samantha: Tell James, I said "Hi!" [As the door closes right in their face. Mr. Clarks hands clutch the door as he drops to his knee's. He start to quiver as tears roll down his face.]
Clark: What have I done… what have I done… what have… [The camera fades to black.] ************************************ * LOCSam Productions * * http://asw.simplenet.com/locosam * ************************************
