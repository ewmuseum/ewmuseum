[Flash] The Reaper [ZzReaperzZ]

26 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Check out this RP. Give me any ideas you might have, tell me what I did wrong, what mistakes I made, or what you think about it. I thought it was good, but that really doesn't matter.
[...Fades in...] ((Scene)) [...It's a bright, sunny day in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma. There have been a few scattered rain showers over the last few days, but other than that, the skies have been the clear, and the days have been warm. There are a few wet spots on the roads, where the water has not yet evaporated. Children are running down the streets playing a game of tag, as others walk there dogs, or are out doing there daily jobs. A slight wind blows, which bends the trees a bit, and sends a few leaves flying. Off in the distance you hear a dog bark, and a beep of a horn. As the camera circles around, you see nice, clean houses. With yards of nice green grass...]
[...It seems like any other day, nothing is different, and everyone is happy. Although, in one week there will be a major event, held by the NcWa. A small child comes running up the side walk, and heads up another driveway. He knocks on the door, and a tall, black haired women answers the door...]
Child: "Hi, is Vinny home?" Woman: "Yes he is, hold on a second. You can come in and wait if you like." [...She opens the door, and let's the small boy in. About ten seconds later the small boy, comes running out, along with another small boy. The quickly run down the street. As they hit the corner, you hear a happy, cheerful music, and then the sound of kids screaming as the Ice Cream man comes driving around the corner. He comes to a stop, and ten kids coming running up to the truck, each waiving money, and asking for there Ice Cream...]
[...As the camera watches, you see something is the sky. Over the tops of the trees. I's what appears to be a large black cloud, the stretches all the way across the sky line, over the horizon. He seems to be rolling in like a big storm, but there is no rain, nor any thunder. The sky slowly becomes black.
The children stop talking, as does the Ice Cream man, as they all turn and look up at the sky, where the large, round, dark clouds are...]
[...All is silent now, besides the Ice Cream song. People start flowing out of there houses, and standing on the streets, starring up at the strange clouds. Some people even are out on their roofs. Suddenly there is a loud crack, as a bolt of lightning strike a large tree that is near the Ice Cream truck. There is screaming as the tree falls into the middle of the road, but hits nothing...]
[...Parents go running out, gathering there kids, and returning safely to there homes. The Ice Cream man closes the window, and gets back into the front seat of his truck, and drives away. In a matter of moments the streets are clear, and everything is silent...]
[...Someone is seen off in the back, walking past the tree toward the camera. It's a very tall, built man, with a black trench coat on. His hood is up over his head, so you cannot see his face. As he reaches the camera, he looks up, and you see a scared face, with multicolored eyes, and you realize that it is the Reaper. In a low, echoing voice, he begins to speak...]
The Reaper: "Oklahoma, I have arrived. The Reaper has made his way across the United
States, and is finally here. In less than a weeks time, I will step into the ring for one of the greatest fight of my life. Not only to survive, for the
Insane title, but to see who really is the legend."
[...A few sprinkles fall from the ground, starts walking up the road. He continues to speak as the camera follows him...]
The Reaper: "Legends... What is a legend? What makes a legend. In the eyes of many, I am a legend, in the eyes of many, Freak of Nature has become a legend. In the eyes of more, and in the record books, War Spirit is a legend. Throughout the years people have said legends are men that have changed a certain something in the world. Be it a sport, something political, or just something common. They say that legends are Gods. They are men, or women that cannot be touched. For they are to powerful. They shall never be overthrown. And over the years, nothing has come to that point. To see what was to happen, if a legend was tested. Now, three men, each a legend in the eyes of many come together.
Looking for the same thing, trying to get it any way they can. Now the question is, what is going to happen? There must be a winner, therefor two men must lose. Will that no longer make them legends? In one match, will they be overshadowed? Or does it just make one, better than the others? Regardless of the outcome, one thing is certain. This will be one of the most gruesome, blood-filled, confrontations the world has ever seen. And it wall all come together, at the Pay Per View entitled 'Fallout'..."
[...The Reaper pauses for a moment, as he kicks a stone across the ground. He walk gets slower, as he starts to talk again...]
The Reaper: "Singapore canes, a steel cage. No where to run, no where to hide. This time, there will be no excuses. Each man, beating the other as bad as he can.
Each man doing what they can, until they can no longer move. It is matches like these, that the crowd loves. It is just what they look for. But see, I am no longer out to please the crowd. They mean little to me anymore. For I have realized they get you no where. I have made myself what I am. The more attention I pay to the ground, the less I get done in the ring. Where it really counts. Now, I am not putting down my fans. My fans have meant much to be for over two years, but now I realize I can do just as much without them."
[...You hear a rumble of thunder off in the background. But the Reaper does not stop, he keeps walking. He is now heading toward the bigger city of
Oklahoma...] The Reaper: "As I find myself coming closer, to perhaps the place where I will lose my
Insane title, it gets me to thinking. Much like Freak, I have thought lately about my career. And I have done many things as well. But unlike Freak, I have not done the things I have wanted to do. Something, but not all things.
I have won the World Heavyweight Championship, the Insane title, and I have beat some of the best the NcWa has to offer. And I have had some of the most memorable match ever in the NcWa. My first match here in the NcWa was perhaps the greatest ever. Jimmy Davidson against myself. Although I lost that match,
I won at the same time. Then there was myself taking on Freak of Nature. Hell in the Cell. Electrical wires all over. 15,000 volts. And the list goes on.
Now I am faced with the same thing. I see another match that is going down in the history books. As I step into a cell, with two other men, each holding a
Singapore cane. The thing that stands out the most, is the fact that it is the fourth time I go up against Freak of Nature, and the three times before, each have gone down as the greatest matches ever. [ pauses ] Now Freak, you named all these people who you have beaten in the past, an as I listened I realized, that I to have beaten everyone you had. Really, this does not matter, but I am keeping your ego in check. Don't let it get to your head. You are not that great.... Yet."
[...The Reaper is now on one of the streets, of the large part of the city. The tall buildings hang over him, as if he was nothing. He stops and looks u at the building. He turns around, and looks at the camera. And not moving, he continues to speak...]
The Reaper: "Freak, the last time you spoke, you used an analogy that has not been used in quite a while. But one that made me realize something. You say you have gone through the Insane division like a hot knife through butter. But toward the end you hit a part of the butter that the knife could not go through. Freak, you have gone through the butter, it's in half. The hard part of the butter that won't cut, it's actually the plastic that holds the butter up. That being me. I am the plastic that holds the butter up. The man that holds the Insane division up. And there is no way to break it, and Freak... There is no way to break me! [ chuckles ] Try as you might, you will never get back to where you once were. Since Judgment Day, it is I that am the leader of the Insane division. And I do not plan to let that slip away from me. I have proved time and time again I am the most Insane man in the NcWa, and the times again for me to prove it. So again I will. And again it will be at the expense of you. As well as War Spirit. But this time there can, nor will be any excuses. This time when it's over, and as I am standing above the beaten bodies of you and
War Spirit, all will know that I am the most Insane man in the NcWa. [ pauses
] Although, there is just one little problem, in which you brought to my attention. That is, you have had a month of rest. While I have been out wrestling each week. But that is the job of the Insane title holder. And by carrying this title, comes that responsibility. But my time will come. I will get rewarded for all I have done. That reward is your soul Freak!"
[...The Reaper turns back around and continues walking...] The Reaper: "The one thing I have not yet taken from you. All else is mine. And this time around, I will gain every last bit of you. [ pauses ] Given all due respect, and the knowledge of knowing, yes Freak, you have been one of my hardest opponents ever. But with each match I learn more, making what I do every week a hell of a lot easier. And as it is now, I am four weeks ahead of you. Although, you had the advantage of watching me for four weeks. Learning yourself. Watching every new move I learned, or any new ideas I might have gained. But see, after four weeks, your body is used to rest, and relaxation.
Even your training will be hard now. [ short pause ] You went on to say that outside of the ring, everyone with great talent if your friend. Or that you respect them. Freak, I did not say that I did not give you respect. I give you more respect than any man in the world. But the bottom line is, I hate you. I hate you with a passion. Inside the ring, and out. Because if life has taught me one thing, that is you can't trust anyone. And either people are your friends, or not. You, are not my friend. So therefor, I despise you.
And perhaps that is a small problem of yours. If you grow fond of someone outside the ring, your performance in the ring will suffer. Each day I try to gain more and more hate for you, that way my performance will be as best as it can be. Now you say go ask Carnage what you did to him. Why don't you ask
Carnage what damage I did to him? I am sure I did just as much, as you did
Freak. The bottom line is, it does not matter what goes on outside of the ring, the way you look at things, or anything such as that. The only thing that matters is what goes on in the ring."
The Reaper: "Freak, you seem to think you are immortal. Saying you have no soul. Carnage could not even take your soul. But Carnage is your friend, is he not? Would he dare do that to a friend? Freak, everyone has some soul. It may be black, may be cold, but we all have a soul inside of us. And I will take whatever it may be, from you. [ pauses ] Freak, I must say one thing. Out of all else, there is one thing I do agree with you on. And that is you War Spirit. You have to forget about the US title, how you are in the Hall of Fame, and most importantly the matches you are going to have after this match. Now, myself and Freak may not be in the Hall of Fame yet, but I am sure we are well on our way. And just because you are there, means nothing. You can be beaten just like anyone else. You had better learn to focus on the task at hand. You are so deep into thinking about Kool Kid and Vertabreaker, you have forgotten you have a match. You seem to already think you are Insane champion, but if you have forgot, I have that title. As of now, it belongs to me. And until Jihad says other wise, I rule you."
[...The Reaper turns the corner, and the camera sees Oklahoma's giant stadium. The camera stops as the Reaper walks toward it, and walks up the sidewalk to the door. He stops and turns around...]
The Reaper: "Oh by the way Freak. I know right where my title is, it's right here." [...The Reaper raises his arms and opens up his trench coat on. He is wearing black jeans with a black shirt, which are both partially covered by the NcWa's
Insane title...]
The Reaper: "And Freak, this is where it is going to stay...." [...He closes his coat, and turns around. He proceeds to open the door and walk in. As the door closes behind him, the sprinkles of rain come to a stop, and the dark sky, filled with black cloud, slowly starts to clear up. The black, round cloud break up, and with a small gust of wind, there are no longer any clouds. The streets are no black, as they are matted with a thin sheet of water. You see people start to file out of their houses and look up to the sky in amazement...]
[...Fades out...]
