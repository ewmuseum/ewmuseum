[Free Agent] Harley Quinn

8 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Apologies for the long-winded post and I thank you in advance for reading this post. I was about to retire Harley Quinn but I thought I'd throw him into the mix for one last run. With work taking up most of my time, I wasn't really too keen to get back into e-wrestling but I could do with a little bit of fun in my spare time. Therefore, I have a few things that I'm looking for in a fed.
1. The fed should be email-based, preferably with a closed flash system with roleplays sent in packets or put together as a roleplay show. I don't mind feds with strictly on-card flashes either.
2. Preferably fortnightly shows so I have two weeks or so to prepare for a match i.e. RP, strat and all that. More than two cards a week are definite no-nos. 3. Preferably a 1-2 RP limit per match. I don't mind if there is no limit but the fedhead will have to understand that I can do at most 2
RPs for a match or card. While I don't mind losing if I don't RP enough, I do mind if the fedhead starts accusing me of being lazy if I am unable to submit an RP per day because I have more important things to do.
4. Preferably have a website or have a means by which I can check up the roster for the fed and be able to find out more about the other wrestlers in the fed. I especially like to find out more about my opponents before a match and a fed that provides me with a means to check out my opponents stats e.g. height, weight, alignment, wrestling style, etc. would be very much appreciated.
5. I can't write matches. I suck at writing matches and I don't want to make commitments I can't fulfil. It's one thing for me to lose a match for failure to RP, it's another if I end up holding back a card because I can't submit a match in time. So I'm looking for a fed that doesn't expect that of me. In return, I won't do much, if any, complaining about late cards.
Most importantly, I'm looking for a spot of fun while at the same time allowing me some sort of continuity in this game, even if it's just a mediocre run. So if anyone doesn't mind taking in this lazy handler of a jobber, then below are the stats for Harley Quinn adapted from his
ACW app. Get back to me with a FAQ, links(if any) and an app, preferably by email at
[detail withheld] . You could reply on here but with the flurry of activity on this NG right now, I might miss your reply. Wrestler's Name: Harley Quinn Hometown: New York, NY Height: 6'1" Weight: 240 pounds Alignment: Looking to play a face but wouldn't mind playing a heel. Wrestling Style: Powerhouse brawler Personality: Harley Quinn is just plain weird. He is in wrestling for the 'fun' of it - money, fame, women and, most importantly, hurting people. While he tries to act dark and gothic, Quinn usually ends up looking goofy.
And a sample RP from his participation in ACW: [Fade in to the previous ACW TV taping.] [SHOCKED POP!] CL: WHAT IN THE HELL?!? JD: Haha! You know who those two are, Chrissy? That's Reverend
Black and Lupe Blanco, the Kill Army! They're HARDCORE~!
[Cut to Harley Quinn, dressed in a grimy white T-shirt and a pair of white boxers with white polka dots on it and, of course, his black and white facepaint, sitting on the couch, 'Chuckie' beside him, a couple of bottles of beer and peanut shells on the table in front of him. The room is dark except for light from the TV.]
HQ: [To 'Chuckie'] Can you believe that? Stick a fork in someone's head and they call you hardcore. I've wrestled women more hardcore than that. You remember what they used to call me when I first started in this business?
[Pauses to listen to 'Chuckie's' response.] HQ: You don't? They used to call me the Clown Prince of Hardcore, that's what they did. Now suddenly these hacks come into Atlantic
Coast Wrestling and they're hardcore?
[Pauses.] HQ: Damn right I'm going to do something about this. In fact, it begins at One Wild Night. [To camera.] Because, on that night, I face another one of those 'hardcore' hacks, the man who claims he is in this business just so he can 'hurt' people. Well, then, get the hell outta the business, because you are an embarassment. The likes of you belong OFF-camera in some underground fighting circuit with the rest of your no-talent, spittle-spewing, scar-ridden, battered kind. The wrestling ring is for people like me, people with the wrestling acumen, people who know how to scientifically smack a chair across someone's head, people who can dish out a technical ass-whooping. Too extreme for EWWF East? There's nothing extreme about your aerial stunts, there's nothing extreme about pissing on a title belt, and there's definitely nothing extreme about being an IDIOT! You want extreme? You want hardcore? 'One Wild Night', Harley Quinn is going to shock Queens, New York and the rest of the world and bring ATLANTIC...
COAST... WRESTLING... to a new level. And Harley Quinn will have the LAST... LAUGH... [Fade out.] HQ[V/O]: And, of course, you too, Chuckie. [END]
