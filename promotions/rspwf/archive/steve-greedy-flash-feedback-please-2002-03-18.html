Steve Greedy Flash - Feedback please

18 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The scene fades into a view of two pairs of finely made Italian shoes going back and forth on a wooden floor.The shoes are making the motion of, if you were trying to "Get something" off the bottom of them...]
Voice : " Ah....New Territory...Just leaving my mark." [ The scene pans up and we now see "The Rich One" Steve Greedy standing infront of us with his trademark smirk on his face. Of course, He is finely dressed as always,and looks to be in the same great shape that were used to seeing him in..Behind Greedy we see a wooden wall, So with the wooden floors, and Wooden walls we see, we assume he is at his house, or someone else's home.]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Thats right IGA you heard me...I am leaving my
_MARK_ on ya precious lil federation.. A man you never thought would be here cause of his hate for all country's except the United States, Is here.."
[ Greedy stops moving his feet back and forth..He now stands still infront of us, Locked into the camera with that cocky smirk still on his face..]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " On yall's lil show I issued a challenge to any
IGA wrestlers that wanted to wrestle me in a finisher only match up. Put's there Move up against my move...I asked people in the back, All said they were scared of me in that type of match cause my finisher is the BEST in the business, I waited for a response on the show...None said a word as they knew what would happen if they accepted...Then I woke up yesterday morning and saw the lil update show IGA puts out...It seems some Japanesse freak accepted my challenge..I don't know if he wasn't in the arena the other night, or he had to go home and sleep on it before accepting, but someone finally accepted my challenge for a finisher's only match up..That someone being "Tornado"
Takara..." [ Greedy chuckles for a moment, and suddenly stops...] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Oh, I am sorry...Yall were serious weren't ya
IGA ? You really want me, Steve Greedy to fight "Tornado" Takara...Well I don't know if my LiL Japanesse friend was sitting at home listening to my interview the other night or not, but I bet ya he was.I bet he was sitting there thinking to himself << Greedy squints his eyes, and talks in his best japanesse voice >>
New guy sucky suck.....Me fight new guy...He run mouth lot, can't back it up...How chow woah..<< Greedy un squint's his eyes and goes back to his normal voice >> Wrong buddy..I am new here, but I am not NEW....My name spreads high and wide in the United States, Sure this isn't the US, but this is still a wrestling ring son..My BANKRUPT DDT is gonna make ya ROARING ELBOW look like an overweight whore...Which means, Not very good at what it does...a big whore, can't do all the tricks a man wants as she can't move around enough, and your elbow can't do its job ethier...Cause it won't be able to finish me...I _MIGHT_
Let you hit your finisher on me, Just so I can kick out..I haven't decided quite yet.."
[ Greedy's smirk goes away, and he stares into the camera, As if he wasn't having fun anymore.]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " The IGA has alot of talent. Don't get me wrong, and I am sure "Tornado" boy has some fine talent, but you guy's haven't seen anything like me quiet yet. I am not running my mouth, or talking to hear myself talk..I am saying this so you all can at least be given a chance to be prepared..I will bribe, manipulate, cheat, steal and use politcs to get to the top of this federation, Like I do every other..Different country's, but still the same damn game."
" I am not like some of America my friends...I am not easy or soft..In Vietnam
America backed off and tried to do damage control with the rest of the world, mainly Japan, but I am not like that...As we did in the second world war...I will drop the _BOMB_ Without even blinking. I will do whatever is nessecary to reach the top of the ladder...Even if it means cut the ladder down, making all the one's on the ladder already FALL straight to the ground...My words may be harsh and cruel, but they do reign out in honesty..I am not a nice man, Nor do
I ever plan to be...As Bobby Knight said " Nice guy's don't win...Nice guy's are pussys." I do like to win, and I love to get in some pussy, but I would hate to think I was one...People who got ahead in this world were men such as great chinnese emperor's who took no crap, General Patten who wanted to fuck people up, then laugh at them or even Bill Clinton who wanted to cheat, lie and get blow jobs....That is what I shall be IGA...I shall be your emperor, Your general, Your Pre.."
[ In the middle of his statement his cell phone rings...Greedy motion's to the camera "One sec" and reaches into his suit packet grabbing the phone..]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Ya.....Sitting here with IGA right now....Yes I do need to get off the phone....Tell him I will sell him the mustang for 50,000 dollars...I love you to...Yes I will tell them..."
[ Greedy almost blushing, Hangs up the phone and looks to the Camera with almost a frown..]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " That was my loving wife << Rolls his eyes >> I am sure you will all meet her soon enough, but she was calling to tell me I had to meet with yall...As If I can't keep up with my own schedule eh ? << Shrugs
>> She also said to tell you all hey...So from my wife Ginger..<< Greedy Waves >> HEY IGA...<< Greedy shakes his head in disgust..>> " IGA, I aint in the mood anymore to hype you up and let you get into the mind of the Greediest man in the game...My wife has that effect on me...She is always getting me out of the mood...<< Greedy looks down at the ground, We assume so we don't see him chuckle after making that statement >> But as a last note...If you didn't listen to anything before this, Just remember << Looks back up at the camera >> Money isn't the only thing, It's everything....And if you don't remember that IGA, And more specefically "Tornado" Takara...You will go
_BANKRUPT_" [ Greedy walks out of our view, and the screen fades to the IGA logo..]
