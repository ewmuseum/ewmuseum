[CRITIQUE] Two Men Walk Into A Bar...

10 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in. The scene opens in Topper's, a seedy strip club somewhere in
Dallas, Texas. The camera shows some revealing (yet slightly censored,
Jerry Springer Show style) shots of buxom young women taking it all off while old, perverted men put money down their g-strings and drool like
Neanderthals. The camera pans across the sea of scantily clad women dancing on-stage over to the club's entrance. We see Outlaw Championship
Wrestling's resident curmudgeon, The Gate Keeper, and his happy-go-lucky little buddy, The Gimp, entering the club. Two guys run over to greet them almost immediately. The first guy - a short, portly man dressed from head to toe in plaid - points his hot sauce stained index finger at a bewildered
Gate Keeper and bellows at his friend...]
Man #1: Oh mah God, Billy Bob! Look who it is! It's thet guy fum TV! [The man's friend, a rather tall gentleman in a black Resistol hat, glances at The Gate Keeper and quickly nods in agreement...]
Man #2: Dawgone, Zeke! Yer right! It IS him - thet guy fum th' TV! [The first guy grabs The Gate Keeper's hand and shakes it...] Man #1: Hey thar, suh! Mah name is Zeke Thomas an' this har is mah fine buddy Billy Bob Ho'ton!
[The second guy shakes the hand of the shaken Gate Keeper as The Gimp looks on, looking equally as stunned...]
Man #2: Mah wife an' ah love yo'! [The first man chuckles...] Man #1: Billy Bob har does an impresshun of yo'! Fry mah hide, suh! It's as funny as hell!
[The Gate Keeper holds his hand up as if to say, "Stop!"...] The Gate Keeper: Listen... [The first guy nudges his buddy's shoulder...] Man #1: Whuffo' don't yo' do it fo' him now? [The second man shakes his head at his friend...] Man #2: Nah! Ah don't reckon he be hankerin' t'be bothard wif some silly impresshun, Zeke!
[The Gate Keeper tries to interrupt the two men again...] The Gate Keeper: Actually, I think your friend is right. I appreciate your support but I--
[The second guy clears his throat and fixes his collar...] Man #2: All right! Hyar it goes! [The Gimp giggles...] The Gimp: Hee-hee-hee! This should be good! I never heard anyone do an impression of you before, boss!
The Gate Keeper: Don't encourage them, you nitwit! Man #2: Howdy, ah's Wi'fo'd Brimley fo' Liberty Medical. Yo' check yer blood sugar an' yo' check it offen! Thar's no reason not to!
The Gimp: HA! The Gate Keeper: Oh dear. Not Brimley! Not this again! [The first guy pats his buddy on the back, chortling like a man possessed...] Man #1: Do th' oatmeal one! Do th' oatmeal one! The Gate Keeper: Oh no! Please don't! The Gimp: Oh yes! Please do! Hee-hee! [Once more, the second man clears his throat, fixes his collar, and treats them with yet another one of his impressions, much to The Gate Keeper's dismay and The Gimp's delight...]
Man #2: Howdy, ah's Wi'fo'd Brimley an' ah's proud t'say thet ah eat delicious, wholesome Quaker Oatmeal etch an' ev'ry mo'nin'. IT'S THE RIGHT
THING TO DO! YEEHAW! [The Gate Keeper snarls at both men...] The Gate Keeper: That was wonderful. Really, it was... but you see... I'm not Wilford Brimley! I look nothing like Wilford freaking Brimley! For the love of all things good and pure, why can't you people see that? If you'd please excuse us, we have important matters to discuss. Come on, Gimp!
[The Gate Keeper grabs The Gimp by the arm and leads him to a table in the corner of the club. Both men sit down. Meanwhile, the women stop dancing and walk offstage while the club's emcee, a lanky fellow with a huge pompadour, walks onstage with a microphone in hand. He holds up five fingers for all of the club's patrons to see...]
Club Emcee: All right, ladies and gentlemen... we'll be back in FIVE... count 'em... FIVE MINUTES... with even MORE LOVELY LADIES! See ya then! [And, with that, the emcee disappears behind the stage curtain. His announcement results in a round of boos and hisses from the hooter hungry men gathered in the club. The Gimp cocks his head sideways and smirks at his robe-clad amigo...]
The Gimp: Okay, boss. When are you gonna tell me why you brought me to this place? This usually isn't your type of thing. Remember the fit that you pitched when I tried to take you to Scores??? You damn near blew a gasket! Now, I'm not one to look a gift horse in the mouth... but I can't help but be curious!
The Gate Keeper: Well, times have changed, Gimp. I've actually... uhhh... well, I've developed a keen interest in clubs such as this place. Their grittiness intrigues me.
[The Gimp smiles at The Gate Keeper...] The Gimp: Oh puhhhleeze! Do you really expect me to believe that load of horse pucky? Why are we REALLY here, boss? Tell me the truth! Is something wrong with the boys?
[The Gate Keeper shakes his head...] The Gate Keeper: No. They're more than ready for their big match. In fact, they're back in the hotel room, meditating as we speak.
The Gimp: Well, has LaGrange been giving you a hard time? [The Gate Keeper shakes his head again...] The Gate Keeper: No, I haven't heard from the guy since the contract signing a couple of weeks ago.
[The Gimp appears to be slightly agitated now...] The Gimp: Well, what is it then? I know something's up, boss! [Slowly, The Gate Keeper leans forward. A distressed expression slowly forms on his face. His eyes grow wider and wider. Then, finally, he responds in a slow, aggrieved tone of voice...]
The Gate Keeper: It's Who-Li! She's driving me up a wall! [The Gimp raises an eyebrow at The Gate Keeper...] The Gimp: What's she doing? [The Gate Keeper rests his face in both of his cupped hands and groans...] The Gate Keeper: She won't leave me alone! When I'm trying to eat my breakfast, she's there. When I'm taking a shower, she's right there, sitting on the toilet, waiting for me. When I'm in the living room, watching "Trading Spaces", she's right there, babbling away like a demented
Chatty Cathy doll! It's driving me MAD! Mad, I tell you!
[Some mild tittering from The Gimp...] The Gimp: How cute. [The Gate Keeper lifts his head back up and scowls at The Gimp...] The Gate Keeper: No, it's not cute. It's awful! I'm starting to question whether or not she and I should even get married! My sanity is slowly dwindling away because of her, Gimp! What should I do???
The Gimp: Well, every relationship has its ups and downs. You just need to be patient. You and Who-Li make an awesome couple. You compliment each other rather well. Try to move beyond the petty problems and make the relationship WORK!
[The Gate Keeper sighs...] The Gate Keeper: Gimp, you found this dame in a newspaper ad! It's not like she and I are high school sweethearts! For God's sake, we've only known each other for what? A month? Maybe it just wasn't meant to be!
[The Gimp places his index finger on The Gate Keeper's lips...] The Gimp: Don't be so pigheaded, boss. The girl loves you... and you appear to have grown quite fond of her as well. It'd be stupid of you to turn your back on all of that, just because she likes being near you. End of story. Now, let's talk about something PRODUCTIVE... like the boys'
advancement in the tag tournament. I dug up a little bit of interesting dirt on their opponents.
[The Gimp removes his finger from The Gate Keeper's lips, allowing his mentor to speak again...]
The Gate Keeper: What about it? I thought I told you that Jayson and
Dagger were more than ready for those Debonair lads.
The Gimp: Boss, I'm not talking about Debonair. I'm talking about the
Kings of Hardcore. Eventually, the boys will have to face them. Wouldn't it be wise if they were ready for them? Hmm?
The Gate Keeper: What kind of information did you uncover about them? The Gimp: I found out that they're a pair of notorious brawlers. They think that they're the best of the best when it comes to extreme warfare.
They love to fight outside the ring... and they almost ALWAYS try to incorporate weapons, like chairs and tables, into their offense.
[The Gate Keeper rolls his eyes at The Gimp...] The Gate Keeper: THAT was your big piece of news??? The Kings of Hardcore actually like to get HARDCORE??? Geez Louise, Gimp, it doesn't take a mastermind to figure that out! When I first saw the name of the team, I didn't exactly think to myself, "Wow, these boys must really be into Morris
Dancing!" I'm well aware of the Kings of Hardcore and what they're capable of doing.
The Gimp: I know, I know! But, you see, this gave me a nifty idea. What if Dagger and Jayson out-hardcored the Kings of Hardcore??? Wouldn't that be SOMETHING???
[The Gate Keeper narrows his eyes at The Gimp. His forehead ripples with disbelief...] The Gate Keeper: Yeah, something that'd get the boys sent to the emergency room! Are you out of your mind? That'd be like having them try to out-eat
Rosie O'Donnell or out-lie Bill Clinton! It's just not possible! I'm sorry!
[The Gimp rubs his hands together...] The Gimp: Anything is possible! The Gate Keeper: WHAT? Are you on crack? The Gimp: Listen, I've gotten the boys an appointment with one of the
MASTERS of hardcore fighting. They're going to see this person after the show. Afterwards, they'll be more than ready for the Kings of Hardcore. I promise!
[The Gate Keeper winces...] The Gate Keeper: Ugh! I guess you're not going to take no for an answer, huh? Well, could you at least tell me the name of this supposed HARDCORE
MASTER that you found? What's his name?
[The Gimp grins...] The Gimp: I actually didn't have to look that long or hard to find this person and it isn't a HE, by the way. It's Who-Li, boss. She's going to prepare the boys for their match with the Kings of Hardcore.
[The Gate Keeper's face turns beet red...] The Gate Keeper: WHAT??? THAT'S ABSURD! YOU HAVE SOME EXPLAINING TO DO... RIGHT NOW! [Meanwhile, the emcee reemerges from behind the curtain...] Club Emcee: All right, folks! Intermission is over! I know you're all anxious to see some more HOT talent so, without further adieu, please let me introduce... NELLY THE NAUGHTY SCHOOLGIRL!
[The emcee sprints offstage and J. Geils Band's "Centerfold" starts blaring over the club's PA system. A tall, leggy blonde comes out from behind the stage curtain, dressed in a Catholic schoolgirl uniform. The Gate Keeper and The Gimp stop bickering long enough to see her come onstage. The Gimp's jaw drops. The Gate Keeper's jaw soon follows...]
The Gimp: Ummm... can we talk about this later, boss? The Gate Keeper: You read my mind! [The Gate Keeper stands up and summons the stripper with a brand new, crisp
ONE dollar bill as we fade to black...]
