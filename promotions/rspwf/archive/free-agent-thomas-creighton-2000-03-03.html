RSPWF Card Archive

[Free Agent: Thomas Creighton]

3 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi all, I'm looking for a home for this character. He's a heelish brawling tweener, who is always looking for ways to get around the rules, for example, before a scaffold match, he "adjusted" the scaffold, so when he graciously allowed his opponent to climb the scaffold first, the scaffold collapsed, and since the opponent fell to the ground first, ol' Tommy was declared the winner. I'm looking for a booked fed, that is stable, and if it is a message board based fed, the HTML is not considered in the outcomes of the match, because I can't do it very well. Also, I want a fed where the swearing is bleeped out, I just hate having to sort through F this, F that, just to figure out who the handler is trying to feud with. I don't care if
Tommy becomes a jobber, cause that's really his place in the grand scheme of things (Although HE might object to that analysis). Well, here goes.
Name: Thomas Creighton Height/Weight: 6'3, 290 lbs. Age: 25 Ring Attire: Jeans with holes in the knees, a black T-Shirt with "Disgruntled Employee of the Month" printed in white on the front, and "Do I look like I play by the rules?" on the back.
Style: Brawler/Hardcore Thomas Creighton is a journeyman, who moves from fed, to fed, to fed. He stays in one place until the money runs out, he makes too many enemies, or he just plain gets fired because of his antics. He moves from place to place, in his beat up, lime green, Chevy pickup truck, with everything he owns piled in the back. He hopes that one day, some how, some way, he'll make it big, and be able to buy a new truck.
Here's a sample role-play from the fed Thomas is currently in. [Scene: In the highest reaches of an unnamed house show arena, while Vandal is speaking in the ring, a camera finds Thomas Creighton sitting, and watching Vandal, carefully. His face is knotted in thought, and Creighton strokes his beard every now and then. Creighton notices the camera, and waves it over to him.]
Creighton, whispering: "You see that guy out there? Vandal? He's the one I was scheduled to fight Saturday night. The boss thinks that he can set things up so he can get the title off of me, and then dump me in the nearest shallow grave, but that ain't gonna happen. A "Hell in a Cell" match. That's downright scary. Wrestlers are never the same after one of those, win OR lose. I'm being set up, and I'm sure that there is going to be more to worry about than Vandal if I defend the belt Saturday."
[Creighton motions to the camera, and heads toward an exit, leaving Vandal's droning voice behind him. He stops in the entranceway to the small auditorium, and turns to the camera again.]
Creighton: "I was getting awful tired of that, ya know? For a guy who doesn't have much to say, he takes a hell of a long time to say it, if you know what I mean. He must have a lot of endurance, that Vandal, to be able to spew so much hot air, for so long. He thinks that just because he has an entourage, and because he has a pretty woman, and because he claims he has a clause in his contract that every match he is in is a no disqualification match, that he is unbeatable. I'm sorry to burst his bubble, but in a cell match, his buddies aren't going to be able to help, his ability to make anything into a weapon isn't going to help, and most of all, his longwinded mouth isn't going to help. Saturday, my music is going to play, after he is standing, dead center, in the ring. The crowd is going to make a lot of noise. It may be cheers, and it may be boos, but the crowd is going to react to me, because I AM the champion, and that is more than you can say, Vandal.
Sure, you defeated Knuckles and me for the Tag belts, but you know what? I hadn't even been in the same ring as Knuckles, let alone teamed with him before the last show, so it isn't much of a surprise that I lost one of my belts. But the championship belt is the one that matters to me, the one that is mine now."
Creighton leans up against a brick wall in the entranceway, and a twinkle in his eye, and a smile on his mouth, let the audience know that he will be in
Cleveland, one way or another, under contract or not.
Creighton: "Anyway, back to the match. My music will play, the crowd will erupt, and then, we'll see, if I come down that aisle, or not. I may show up, and show you what hardcore really is, and hurt you so badly that you will have to spend the rest of your time here in a hospital room, or I may not, and you will look stupid, as you stand in the middle of the ring, waiting for an opponent who is not going to belittle his title by defending it against the likes of you. Vandal, whatever happens, you will be embarrassed, and the people will let you know that they don't like to be bored, and that they don't like a newcomer coming in like you have, and getting a title match of HIS choice. You'll be sent back down to the bottom, earning your way back to the top by fighting guys like Virus, or Terror-Tek, and never, ever, seeing the spotlight of a main event again."
Creighton walks out the door, gets into his beat up pickup truck, and drives off.
