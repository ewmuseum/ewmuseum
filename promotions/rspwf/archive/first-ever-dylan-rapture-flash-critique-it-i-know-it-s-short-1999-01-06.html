First ever Dylan Rapture flash. Critique it, I know it's short.

6 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene - The scene is that of a night club. Women, very attractive ones are dancing all over the place, some with "their men" and others looking for a man.
Several men are scattered around the club too. All dressed in their "player" clothes, trying to pick up a woman. One couple is performing a dance that can only be considered indecent exposure. The camera pans around the room to the bar where a man is being hit on by a very inviting looking woman. As the camera zooms closer, the man is revealed to be none other that "The
Incomparable" Dylan Rapture. He is wearing a pair of baggy khakis pants and a dressier shirt that you ordinarily see a guy wearing in a club of the such.
The woman is dressed in a very low necked belly shit, while wearing a skirt that leaves little for the imagination. She is obviouslly a little on the tipsy side, most likely thanks to some alcohol, and she is presenting herself to Dylan in a very inviting way. He notices the camera out of the corner of his eye.]
"THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - So, what you're telling me is that your friend thinks I'm hot?
WOMAN - Well... yeah. "THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - Well, tell your friend that I think her very curvy friend is gorgeous.
WOMAN - Can I tell ya a secret? "THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - Go for it. WOMAN - I don't really have a friend. "THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - [sarcastic tone] Gee... really? I didnt' know that. WOMAN - Yeah right. [playfully pushes him] "THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - Well, this place just about sucks, so why don't you go freshen up and we'll go somewhere else.
WOMAN - [sounding excited] Okay! "THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - Hurry back. [He leans over and kisses her gently on the cheek. She scampers off towards the bathroom. Dylan turns towards the camera.]
"THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - There, now that she's gone, we can talk a little business. This past Monday night, the world was priviliged enough to see me, Dylan Rapture, make my wrestling debut. Well, I guess you can't call it wrestling, but I guess we could call it my "public ass kicking debut". I mean, I did kick a little ass. Just ask Farmer... if he's awake yet. But I want to clear something up. Some ingrate came up to me and asked if I had formed an alliance with Westside Walavan. Well, let it be known that Dylan
Rapture is too good for friends, and too perfect for alliances. And I do mean perfect, in every aspect of the word.
[Another very attractive woman walks by Dylan, scoping him out the entire way. He returns the look.] "THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - But this coming Monday, I have to face off against a man, who is in essence, my exact opposite. Brian "The Farmdawg"
Farmer. I'm not even going to ask what "The Farmdawg" means, because, quite frankly, I don't even wanna know. I'm just assuming, since I've never taken the time out of my busy schedule to listen to him, that he is just a little on
the scruffy, hicky side. I mean, "Farmdawg" is just sort of a tip-off that this guy needs to be urbanized. Well, he's felt The First Step once before and he knows how deadly it is. Let's see if he's going to take my advice... and that's "Watch the first step... it's a doozy!" I doubt he'll learn though, those farm boys are always a little on the slow side. But this Monday night deal isn't the only time fans will be fortunate enough to see me this week. Oh no, no, no.
[Dylan raises one eyebrow at the camera, as an amazingly attractive woman walks by "accidentally" brushing herself up agaisnt him.]
"THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - Ya see, the VSW is making one more stop before The Liberty Bowl in Memphis. That's right, this Thursday we'll be making a brief stop at The Pyramid... whatever the Hell that is. I'll be in action, but since I don't really pay any attention to the boss, I don't know who I'll be facing yet. I know that Brian Farmer will be in action that night too, though. Maybe I'll take another opportunity to drop him on his head again. Hey, ya can't hurt what isn't there. Ah, I don't know, maybe, maybe not. It all depends on how much this girl tires me out tonight I guess. OH!
Speak of the devil. [The woman returns from the bathroom.] WOMAN - No other women tried to steal you while I was gone did they? "THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - [looks at the camera with an arrogant grin] Oh, know. You ready? Let's go. WOMAN - {seductively] Let's. [She puts her hand on the inside of her pocket so she can pull him away. He looks at the camera and smiles with delight.]
"THE INCOMPARABLE" DYLAN RAPTURE - Oh boy! [The woman drags him out to the door where he scoopes her off of her feet and carries her out past security and out the door.]
[Camera fades to black.]
