GLOW. Anyhow, as it seems, with Duane's retirement, leading to the end of Strictly Business (You may know of them, too. ;), combined with the closing of BAW and UKCW, I'm fedless.
This post, however, is a free agent post for one of my two characters, Andrew "Flash" Tucker.. Before I go into the specifics of the character, let me first let you know what I'm looking for in a federation, so you know if you're wasting your time or not. :)
- Closed flash system. - Has been open for awhile. Alignment [heel,face,neutral] : Neutral Appearance : Andrew is well built for his size, his build looks a lot like that of a Chris Benoit. He has long blond hair that flows a little past his shoulders, but he usually pulls it back into a pony tail when he's doing interviews, and such, then leaves it down during matches. Ring attire : Andrew has primarily three outfits. They're all basically the same, but different colors for the tights. Primarily, he wears long pant-like tights that are adorned with small lightning bolts on them, with matching boots that read "AT" in Roman-Scripted writing. The colors he wears are: Black tights with white lightning bolts (His primary), White tights with black lightning bolts (PPV outfit, mostly) and black tights with neon green lightning bolts. He also has nothing covering his bronzed chest.

------------------------- 3 -> Character's Personality Wrestler's Gimmick [A brief description will do] : Andrew Tucker is definitely a no-nonsense type of guy. He's got a bit, well maybe more than a bit, of an ego, but he doesn't flaunt it
_too_ much. He is the type of guy who doesn't take too much of a liking to anybody talkin' smack on him, and it doesn't take long before he just wails away at a guy who's berating him. He doesn't have a very nice temper, and it flares during matches very often. He'll run into any fight, whether it be 10 on one or one on one, he just really doesn't care.
History : [What has led your wrestler to where he is today?] (Life Bio) Andrew Tucker was born February 26th, 1974 in Beverly Hills, California to Laura and William Tucker, two very successful, and very rich, investors. Andrew had everything he could ever want in life.
Anything he could desire, he could have. Except one thing - attention. With his parents gone 95% of the time, he and his younger sister Angelina had a close bond, and all they ever wanted was their parents to spend some time with them instead of trying to buy their love.
So, one day, when Andrew was 15, and Angelina was 12, they finally had had enough, and they decided to run away. To where, they didn't know. So, they took the money they had in their stashes and hopped on a bus and told the driver to take them as far as their money would go.
That place was Oakland, California. When they got there, they didn't exactly know what they were going to do, so they hopped from homeless shelter to homeless shelter until
Andrew got himself involved in a gang. The gang may not have been the nicest group of people, but they gave Andrew and Angelina the love and attention that they desired. They barely got by, which was a stark contrast to the life they had previously lived, but they were happy. All things were going well until one day (DUM, DUM, DUMMMMMMM!! :).. While in a brawl with a rival gang, Andrew was stabbed in the side. When all was done, the gang Andrew had called family had left him there to bleed to death.
Nobody heard Andrew's cries for help except one person - his sister. She got him to the hospital, and although Andrew had lost a lot of blood, he survived. Andrew was 16 years old then, and while lying in a hospital bed, he was clueless to what he was going to do next. But, by a stroke of good luck, a local wrestling promoter in the area named P.J. Christiansen walked into his room, mistaking it for someone else's, and they got to talking. Andrew told him his story, and P.J. only had one idea that he could possibly do to help the boy in need. He got him into wrestling.
After a few weeks of recuperating from his stab wound, he was in shape to start training for what he dreamed of as a huge wrestling career. What Andrew found instead of a quick buck', was the hardest thing he had ever had to do. The workouts, the long days, everything was a lot more than he had expected. But, Andrew had never met a problem he couldn't solve, and he was hell bent on becoming successful at this as well.
After two and a half years of training, P.J. told him that he thought he still had a lot to learn, yet he was ready to start wrestling. So,
Andrew started to jump from small fed to small fed. And thus began his wrestling career. (Wrestling Career) Tucker started his wrestling career in a small federation named the AWA, and there he found absolutely NO success. He went back to Oakland down and dejected, but as they say, after you fall off of the horse, you have to jump right back on.
So, Tucker went back to wrestling, and signed a small contract with another small federation called the ECWO. There, Tucker had some minimal success, winning a few matches here and there. So, after being in the federation for about a month, the suits of the company decided that he would be a good warmup for the then World Heavyweight Champion, Da
Rapper. Tucker, however, looked at it as his shot at stardom. So, one day Andrew Tucker got his shot at the World Champion - and made the best of it. When he pinned the World Champion for three, not only did he take home a big belt, but he shocked the World, and shot to the top of the federation instantly. He held that title for three months, longer than any Champion before him, and when he did drop it, he captured it back soon after. After Tucker dropped the title for the second time, he decided that he'd go for a little bigger competition.
Tucker jumped from feds like the WecW, UCW and the wWw, and won the World titles in all three feds as well. He was also ranked as the 58th best wrestler in the World in 1997. After accomplishing all this,
Tucker decided it was time to move up to the big leagues, so to speak. Andrew signed contracts with the INWI, N.E.W., and CNWF² and was ready for the competition that was thrown at him. After some mediocre success in all three feds, he started to get on a roll and captured the
Lightheavyweight Championship in the INWI, and became the longest reigning Lightheavyweight Champion ever at over five months. He also captured the CNWF² United States Championship and held it proudly until the federation folded. Tucker also secured the N.E.W. Cruiserweight
Title and held it before a rift came between Tucker and the N.E.W. and he left the organization. As he concentrated mainly on the INWI, he captured the INWI World Heavyweight Title, his biggest prize to date, and held it from October 1998 to January of 1999. During this time,
Andrew Tucker was also ranked as the 13th best wrestler in the World today. 1999 was the year that Tucker took a backseat in singles wrestling, only participating in the INWI in early 1999 to its closure, then moving to the IWE for a short while until that too, closed.
From there, he formed a tag team with his best friend, "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, better known as Strictly Business. Sebastian and Tucker soon topped the EMWC Tag Team Division, capturing the World
Tag Team Titles from the longest reigning champions to date, the Epitome of Cool. From there, the duo held the titles for about 3 and a half months before dropping them to two masked luchadors, which turned out to be the
Down Boys, returning to the EMWC. Strictly Business was not to be denied, however, and they recaptured the titles from the Down Boys on
New Year's Eve 1999. They held the titles for another three months before a rift between the EMWC and themselves formed and they soon lost the titles to the Foundation and made their exit from the EMWC.
About the same time, Tucker returned to the UKCW once again in singles action, and was the number one contender to the Heavyweight
Title and was in the midst of a dangerous feud between the champion Nick Demola when the federation folded. Tucker has also had short stints in federations such as the TOP, CWC and others. He has also been seen in inter-fed events such as: The
