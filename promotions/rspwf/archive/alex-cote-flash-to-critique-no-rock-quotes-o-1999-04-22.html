[Alex Cote] Flash to critique! No ROCK quotes! =o)

22 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the latest "The Hammer" Alex Cote flash. Please read it over and tell me what you all think. I want fully HONEST opinions because if I don't get them, how can I get better?
[~-.._[name withheld]_..-~] [Handles: "The Hammer" Alex Cote] [Lifetime Record: 49-5-5] [Feds: ICW, maybe looking for one more...NO AOL FEDS!!] [Titles: Former ICW Hardcore and Television champions, Former eWa champion.] [Awards: ICW's 3rd Most Popular Wrestler/ RSPWF's 8th Best Worker, 3rd Best
Face, 2nd Most Underrrated.]
R.I.P. IWA!! "Cheese, the other white meat?!" ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Fade in.] [SCENE: Philidelphia, Pennsylvania. TIME: 6:30 P.M. The scene opens up on one of the worst streets in down town Philly. Across the street is a small bar when voices can be heard on the inside. The cameraman starts to cross the road and is almost hit by a truck. The cameraman suddenly takes off anf runs across the road. He stands in front of the bar. He walks on inside. People turn and look at the camera. They all look back down and mind their own business. The camera man moves through the room and notices a large crowd in the back of the bar and they are chanting "HAM-MER!" loudly. The camera is able to sneak his way through the crowd to notice a pool table and "The Hammer" Alex Cote holding a pool stick ready to shoot.]
[APPEARENCE: Alex is dressed in a pair of ripped blue jeans with darker blue tang top revealing his tatto. He is also wearing a pair of black, leather steel toe boots. Around his neck is a golden chain with an anarchy sign on it and on his right hand is a gold high school ring. On his head is bandana which read "TE, TEAM ELITE" on it.]
[Alex examines the table. He puts his hands in the air to hush the crowd and instanly the chanting ceases.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE[in a monotone voice]: Eight ball, right corner pocket. [Alex sets up. His opponent stands across the table looking at him with seat sliding down his brow. Alex slowly pulls the stick back and brings it forward nailing the cue ball. That time in moment passes like it was slow motion. The cue ball nails the eight ball on the side knocking it into the right corner pocket. The crowd cheers. Alex suddenly gains a big smile on his face and his opponent reaches into his back pocket and pulls out his wallet.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE[grins]: Well, that was a double or nothing game for two hundred dollars and if my arthimetic is correct, I would have to say that adding that up says that you owe me four hundred dollars.
[The man sighs and hands Alex his money. Alex tries to shake his hand but the man drops the stick and walks out. Alex just shrugs and walks over to the bar and grabs a beer. He takes a sip and looks back up.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE[yells]: ONE ROUND ON ME! CROWD: YEAH!! [Alex turns and notices the cameraman.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Hey, whats up? I'm just hangin out in my old hang out place. It's good to be back in my hometown and it's even better that tommorow night I will be in the ring in front of all the people who grew up right here in the same city as I did and nothing is better than getting into the ring and listening to everyone you know cheer your name and to pay them back by walking away with the win. Tommorow, in the First Union Center, I am going to walk into the ring with every intent of walking out the winner.
[Alex takes another sip of his beer. He slams it down and does a little laugh.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: I know that last time I was in this situation I got myself cocked outta my mind but tonight I am not going to have more than three beers and this is my first! So, tommorow, I will be in perfect condition for my tag team match. I am teamed up with the man they call Hidden Essence as you all know. That's like the ICW version of the Odd Couple. But tommorow,
Essence and I have made a pact to work together and keep our personal feeling for each other in check so we don't end up beating the hell outta each other.
So, Sylkk, Dragon, I know you though that this match was going to be a breeze since Essence and I don't like each other you better thin again because I am telling you know that you thought wrong!
[Alex puts down the beer and stands up. He walks over to the jukebox and begins to look over the selections as he speaks.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Well, tommorow, Sylkk, you and I step into the ring once more. Last time we ended up in a draw. I have to say that you were one of the best opponents I ever got into the ring with but I hope you noticed that after you through everything you had at me, that you didn't not wear me down and lay me down for the count. But tommorow, the circumstances will be different so i can't expect the same results. Tommorow, one of our teams will walks outta the First Union Arena the winner and I have to state right now that
"The Hammer" is undefeated in Philly! Everything is different Shawn, we both have partners, there is no Longmire sticking his nose into everyone business, and I have Team Elite to have my back. You bring EGO if you want but just remember that Team Elite will be ready for all of you!
[Alex pushes three buttons on the juke box. Suddenly, "A Certain Shade of
Green" by Incubus begins to play over the jukebox. Alex smiles and looks into the camera.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Now thats good music. Just like a wrestler. music has to have certain qualities in order to be a great song. In a wrestler, you need integrity, dexterity, intelligence, and honor and I would have to say that the
ICW North American Champion, Tha Dragon, has none of these. This little punk spends his time talking trash and jumping people and I have to say right now that it is getting kinda old and people aren't going to put up with it anymore.
Now, I know you have beaten Hidden Essence in the past to get the North American title and thats all fine and dandy but there is a big difference between beating him and beating me. And when it comes to beating me, you just don't have what it takes. I vow to you that tommorow won't be the last time you and I get into the ring but the difference will be that the next time it
will be just you and me and it will be for that North American gold! Dragon, granted you have pulled off some upsets here and gained the respect of some people but beating me will be no easy task and it is something that you just can't do. So, you will have to get it through your thick skull and realize that compared to me, you just don't....
~-.._MEASURE_..-~ ~-.._UP_..-~!! [Alex walks away from the jukebox and goes back to the pool table. He sets up a rack. He walks back over to the otherside and sets up the cue ball. He breaks and smacks the balls all over the table sending two into the holes.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Now, to get off tommorow, I have a few things to say to the ICW's new esteemed vice president. You walk on in the ICW and suddenly think you run the place but I have to warn you now that the ICW is my house! I am the Icon of the ICW and I represent it. Now, you bring in this bootleg, wannabe mexican Alex Cote who thinks he is hard and he starts to spread lies about me. Well, if you want me and him in the ring, I will be more than happy to step up to the plate and smack that little boy outta the park. He is just a little midget compared to the sight of perfection you see before you. Now, I will gladly face him but I can't promise he will be able to leave the ring.
Listen, I really dunno where you got this little punk from but I have to tell you now that no matter what trick you are up too, it always goes does to Alex
Cote getting the upper hand and being the winner so don't expect to beat me!
[Alex bends over and levels up with the cue and the two ball. He pulls back and sends the ball across the table. It barely misses the whols and stops.
Alex doesn't look to happy with himself.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: I hate it when I do that. Anyways, the last thing i have to talk about is about a punk who left the ICW and then returns to refuel his personal vendetta against me. That one little bitch is names "Fabulous"
Freddy Floyd. Floyd, first off, stop bitch'n about me beating you in the hardcore style match because it was no rules and pins count anywhere so you have no right to complain about getting Spicolli Drived onto that plow. Then you sit there and blantenly lie saying I am not you're main focus when it is obvious I am. All you have done since your return is talk about me. I have beaten you twice in the past and you just haven't learn yet. Now, I said a man has to go through me before he can take on the champ. Now, I will face you again but I will need to chat with Madison before I can agree to that but I will face you again if you wish but it will only lead to more bruises, bumps, and disappointment for you again because you just can't beat me! Now, Freddy
Floyd, just shut your damn mouth and find someone else to stalk! Now, Floyd,
Longmire, bootleg Alex Cote, and EGO, you better....
~-.._PREPARE_..-~ ~-.._TO_..-~ ~-.._BE_..-~ ~-.._HAMMERED_..-~ Because...... ~-.._I_..-~ ~-.._TAKE_..-~ ~-.._NO_..-~ ~-.._PRISONERS_..-~!!! [Alex resets the balls.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Ok, who's next?! [Alex gets ready to break.] [Fades to black.]
