[SAMPLE RP] Your Candidate For Best Heel & Best Psychotic Wrestler...

6 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens with a shot of a big-screen television set. The set suddenly turns on and a slow motion shot of Shimmer Glitz/Mannifred Lester being counted out at "Holiday Horror 3" begins to play. The clip ends with a freeze frame of the referee holding a victorious Liam Rogan's arm in the air. The camera then pans away from the set ... and Horton Horsfield is seen leaning against the one side of the television. He seems to be totally preoccupied with the bag of chips that he's holding. His head is down and he's devouring the chips ...
handful after handful. There's a whistle heard off-camera and Horton looks up, wide eyed. He drops the bag on the ground, wipes his hands on his pants, and smiles at the camera.]
Horton Horsfield: Hello there... [burps]... I'm Hor-[burp]-ton Horsfield of the famed Freak Show and this is a little something we like to call "Where the
Day Takes You (When You're UEW Television Champion)". In the next few minutes, we will be showing you just how much Liam Rogan's days ... and nights
... have changed since winning the coveted UEW Television Title. Prepare to be intrigued ... prepare to be enthralled ... and you might have to prepare to be sickened, too. This _is_ Liam Rogan we're talking about.
[Horton points to the television set which now has a graphic on it which says,
"A Day in the Life of Liam Rogan: Where the Day Takes You (When You're UEW
Television Champion)".]
Enjoy the presentation. [Horton pauses for a second ... looks around ... and then kneels down to pick up the discarded bag of chips. He dives his hand back into the bag immediately.]
Cameraman: [off-screen] We're still on, Mr. Horsfield. [Horton throws the bag back to the ground.] Horton Horsfield. Oh [BLEEP]!!! [The scene dissolves to an exterior shot of Liam Rogan's country home. The morning sun is shining brightly in the sky.]
Horton Horsfield: [voice-over] The Television Champion's day begins the same way each day. He always begins the day with a strenuous workout that lasts for approximately sixty minutes daily.
[Fade in to a shot of Liam Rogan's living room. Rogan slouches comfortably on his velvet sofa as the sounds of Bob Barker and "The Price is Right" can be heard coming from the television set. Rogan leans forward and sneers at the TV set.]
Liam Rogan: Aw come on, man! You have fifty cents and only one contestant after you ... and she's _EIGHTY_ years old! Why even bother spinning again? I bet Grandma won't even be able to get the damn wheel to go all the way around without having a stroke!
Horton Horsfield: [voice-over] After a rigorous sixty minute workout of valuable cash and prizes, Rogan goes on to begin what will be a long and trying day for the newly crowned UEW Television Champion.
[Dissolve to an exterior shot of a Toys R Us store. A banner outside the store reads, "UEW Wrestler "The Handler" Liam Rogan Signs Autographs TODAY!". The parking lot is completely full with cars.]
Now, as a champion, he has the pleasure of being the role model for thousands and thousands of young children who watch UEW every week. On this particular day in question, he's participating in an autograph signing at a local Toys R
Us.
[Fade in to a shot of the inside of the store. Surrounded by two security guards, Rogan sits behind a small wooden table as a line of children, most of which are accompanied by their parents, line up to receive an autographed picture.]
Liam Rogan: Alright alright... move it up ... who's next? [A little boy, all alone, comes forward from the line.] Little Boy: I am! Liam Rogan: Okay! Gimme the picture you want me to sign! Little Boy: Here you go, sir. [The little boy hands an issue of Pro Wrestling Illustrated to Rogan. This issue of PWI features a cover picture of Rogan holding a giant picture of Susan
B. Anthony and shrugging. The headline reads, "Is This Wrestler The Next Crusader For Women's Rights? Just Ask Erin McCoys!". Rogan takes a Magic
Marker and prepares to sign the magazine. He pauses, looking over at the little boy.]
Liam Rogan: Alright, you little bastard. What's your name? Little Boy: Xavier. Liam Rogan: Aw, now wonder you're alone. Your parents must be a pair of sadistic fiends to give you a name like that!
[Rogan quickly signs his name to the magazine.] Here you go! [The little boy's eyes light up as Rogan hands the magazine back to him.] One more thing, kid. The UEW Television Champion thinks you should start taking self defense classes as soon as possible. With a name like Xavier, you're gonna need it!
Next! [The little boy frowns as one of the Toys R Us security guards escorts him away and tells the next child to move up in the line. Another small boy, accompanied by his young mother, comes walking up to the table.]
Well well well! What do we have here? [The kid hands a small notebook to Rogan to sign. Rogan takes the notebook and opens it. He begins to sign his name ... with his eyes focused on the kid's young mother, who just turned around to look over at the clock on the wall..]
Little Boy #2: Oh man. I think you are so _cool_!! Remember that time ... on
"UEW Anarchy" ... or was it "Sunday Night Heat ... oh, I don't know ... but it was you and the Freak Show.. oh yeah, POOP TOO ... and you had Bod and...
Liam Rogan: Yeah yeah yeah, kid. I remember it. Hey kid... Little Boy #2: Yeah? [Rogan looks over at the kid's mother, leans towards the kid.] Liam Rogan: Is that your Mom? Little Boy #2: Yeah. Why? Liam Rogan: She has a nice ass. [The young mother hears the comment and turns around. She slaps Rogan across the face and grabs her kid by the arm. She walks away from the table as quickly as can be as Rogan smirks at the security guard standing next to him.]
What got into her? You'd think she would be flattered to know that I think she has a nice ass ... especially after having a kid as ugly as that, for God's sake!
[Dissolve to an exterior shot of the Midvale Mall, one of the many local malls found near where Rogan lives. It's now mid-afternoon.] Horton Horsfield: [voice-over] After a few hours of signing autographs and enriching the minds of youngsters, UEW's newest resident role model heads over to Sear's to purchase a new entertainment center for his __bathroom__.
[Fade in to a shot of Rogan in Sear's. He's leaning over the Customer Help counter, giving the employee behind the counter, a young kid barely out of high school, an angry stare.]
Liam Rogan: What the hell do you mean I can't have a television at a reduced discount? Sear's Employee: [voice crackling nervously] Sir, we're not having any television set discounts or sales this week!
Liam Rogan: I don't need no stinkin' sale! [Rogan gives the employee another dirty look, pounds on the counter, and opens his leather jacket ... revealing the UEW Television Title ... secured tightly around his waist.]
See this belt? It's the __UEW TELEVISION TITLE__!!! Do you know what this means, Poindexter? That means that I am the _leader_ of all of these television sets in this store!
[The employee fumbles underneath the counter and presses a small concealed button.] Sear's Employee: S-s-security!! Liam Rogan: Don't security me, kid! I'm the __UEW TELEVISION CHAMPION__!! All of these TV sets are MINE!! [Rogan turns around and points to all of the television sets set up across from the counter. He turns back around to face the employee. He bangs on the counter one last time.]
THOSE TELEVISION SETS ARE MY __KINGDOM__!!! [Security comes out of the wood work to escort Rogan out of Sears as we dissolve to an exterior shot of Liam Rogan's country home again. Night is beginning to fall.]
Horton Horsfield: [voice-over] After calmly rectifying matters with the
Sear's security guards...
[A recorded snippet of Rogan's voice is heard over the soundtrack of the shot.] Liam Rogan: [recorded snippet] YOU CAN'T BAN ME FROM SEAR'S!! I AM THE UEW TELEVISION CHAMPION, YOU BLASPHEMOUS BASTARDS!! Horton Horsfield: [voice-over] ... Rogan heads home for some last minute training before finally retiring to bed to recover from a long and trying day as champion...
[Fade in to a shot of Rogan slouched on his comfortable velvet sofa. He chows down on some Domino's pepperoni pizza as he watches his big-screen television set. The camera gets a brief shot of what's playing on the TV set. A quick shot of a brawl between two scantily clad strippers on "The Jerry Springer
Show" is seen. Rogan takes a break from gnawing on the greasy pizza pie in order to smile and laugh.]
Liam Rogan: JER-RY! JER-RY! JER-RY!! Horton Horsfield: [voice-over] The UEW Television Champion's life is one filled with ups...
[A quick freeze frame shot of Rogan at the Toys R Us autograph signing staring at the young mother's rear end is shown.]
... Downs... [A quick freeze frame shot of Rogan being accompanied out of the local Sear's by a pair of security guards is shown.]
... And a lot of stressful decisions... [A quick live action shot of Rogan at home clicking through the channels on his big-screen television is shown.] ... But he wouldn't change it for the world. [A shot of Liam Rogan sleeping on his velvet couch... UEW Television Title clutched tightly in his arms ... is shown as we fade to blood red.]
