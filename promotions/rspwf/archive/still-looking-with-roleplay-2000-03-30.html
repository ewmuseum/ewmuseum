Still looking (with roleplay)

30 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi   I posted a few days ago about finding a new home and i'm looking for a home, so if you want me read over this and tell me what you think!
  John aka Tyke ~ The packed Nynex Arena Manchester, England is brought into darkness as the arena lights turn off the only light is from the entrance ramp, shadows of cameraman and backstage crew can be scene as the ramp slowly starts to fill with dry ice “Let me Entertain” starts to play over the PA system the packed crowd know that can only mean one thing. Its Britain’s favourite son Tyke making his way down to the ring.
  From the depths of the dry ice you can see the buoyant Tyke jigging and dancing his way forward never cocky and not showing as he steps from behide his smoke screen his fireworks shoot to the arenas roof and he stands arms aloft and milking the crowd nodding his head to the beat of his famous ring music, rising his head and removing his sunglasses his smiles and dances his way to the ring slowly and arrogantly.
  The fans getting more and more involved with this young flamboyant superstar.   Tyke reaches the foot of the ring jumps two footed onto the ring apron, he then looks to the crowd they all know what’s happening next Tyke places his hands on the top rope, he swings back then flips forward into the ring to the delight of the fans.
  He wears a black sport jacket with his gold chain hanging out, with beige combats and Nike trainers, he coolly collects the microphone.
  Deep breath and waits for the fans to settle ~ [Tyke] Manchester England are you ready for the “Entertainer”?? ~ The crowd roars ~ [Tyke] I take that as a yes, well you may have two football teams in this city but you don’t have the best do you coz we all know where that place is.
  Yes you all guess it that little place over the hills, called Leeds.   Now I may have been out of the “game” for some months but I’m sure ready to reclaim my rightful place among the greats in this business, and we all know that I will achieve this much sooner rather than later.
  I always seem to get held back when pushing for that gold but that only tells you one thing that the Americans don’t like there business been spearheaded my a Brit.
But it seems at the minute the Americans can only pay good money for the talents of us Brits.
  Look they are paying millions for Naz and the deals I’m seeing you wont believe the amount of zero’s im seeing but its in dollars when I see that many zero’s pounds then I sign on the dotted line.
  Sorry to say but I don’t think that British companies have what it takes to hold me here, but the day I reach the top I will bring the great American Dream to these shores and show you the true fans of me what I can do you have supported me through thick and thin its only fair that I should return this favour to you my public! I set off on my journey across the Atlantic in search for a home.
  UK I promise you I’ll be back! ~ Tyke bows to his fans and the crowd applauds the young man as he mounts the ring posts the ring fills with camera flashes each time he does so, his music hits in again as he doing this, once he has done this he bounces of all four sides of the ring before doing a moon Sault over the top rope and landing on his feet.
  He dances his way up the ring ramp and turns to the fans winks and disappears behide the curtain ~ Http://www.tyke-online.co.uk
