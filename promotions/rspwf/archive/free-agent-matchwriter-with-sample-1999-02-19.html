[FREE AGENT MATCHWRITER] With sample

19 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Looking for the next last matchwriter to get your booked fed off the ground? Then give me a shot. The following sample is a title match I wrote for a supercard in the now-defunct MCWF back in September.
I tend to prefer an ABS writing style with mainstream promotion (i.e. WWF, WCW) style action. If needed I contribute jobber characters to the fed, be they mainstream, extreme, or luchador. And I can also, if desired, do equal opportunity satire or parody, as can be seen here pre and post-match.
Basicallly, whatever you need, I'll do. To set up the match, the MCWF was a fed where the handlers had the power to, within reason, write and book the matches of their own characters. And in this match, I was writing from the role of the challenger, Mitchell Davis. At the time, Davis was a repackaged ex-midcarder who'd abandoned a previous ersatz extraterrestial gimmick and typical leech manager (whose role would be permanently sealed) for a buildup to main-event status. Davis wrestled mostly face but was regarded as a tweener. This being due to the crowd's mixed perception of him. Some seeing Mitch as a classic wrestler who embodied what was right about the sport. Others seeing him as the fictional commissoner's puppet. The champion, Johnny Blaster Jr, was a heel champion run by the fedhead who was scheduled to cough up the belt soon herreafter had the fedhead's outside life not caused the fed's closure. Blaster had connived his way to the title and was largely playing Owen Hart circa 1994. As for the other characters involved in the post-match altercation, they are the fedhead's jobber characters. If you need any more information or wish to talk business, feel free to e-mail or IM me at the address at the head of this article and by all means, enjoy:
< < < < Dean: Well now that was exciting. But now things are looking to go south. Rexx: Sorry you feel that way, Dean, because now it's time for the main event here on the Main Event. And now it's time to go to our special guest ring announcer Bichael Puffer.
Dean: Oh no! Not that boxing guy! Isn't it bad enough we have the treasonous Mitchell Davis in the main event? Rexx: Uuuuuh, no comment. "Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is the Main Event of the evening and will be for the MCWF World Heavyweight Championship!!! So, tell me, Nashua,
New Hampshire, ARE YOU RRRRRRRRRRRRREADY?!" [BIG CROWD POP] "I repeat, ARE YOU RRRRRRRRRRRRRRRREADY?!!!" [EVEN BIGGER CROWD POP] "Well then, ladies and gentlemen, assembled multitudes in the MCWF Arena here in Nashua, New Hampshire, and all the millions of viewers all over the nation……"
Dean: Uh oh. Here it comes. "LLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLET'S GET READY TOOOOOOOOO……" "BBBBBBBBBBBBBUNGLE" [Puffer shoots his right index finger out to the crowd. Which pops loudly.] "IN THE JJJJJJJJJJJJJJJJJJJUNGLE!!!" [Puffer shoots his left index finger out to the crowd as Dean hides his face in embarrassment.]
Dean: Oh thank you, Jethro Tull. Rexx: Well, Dean, at least the biographical intros should be interesting. "Now, introducing the challenger. He is a staunch defender of the old school and the history of wrestling. Choosing to identify himself with the monolithic legends of wrestling's glorious past. Frank Gotch, Jim Londos, Lou Thesz and others. Since abandoning his Lost Planet Airman gimmick, this man has become the hottest commodity in ALL the MWCF and is proudly accompanied to ringside by Absolutely No One. LLLadies and gentlemen, from Greensboro, North
Carolina. Weighing 222 lbs. The Ppppppurist Mitchellll Daaaaaaaaavis!!!" [Tom Sawyer by Rush fires up over the speakers and on the lyric, "mean, mean pride", Davis bursts out of the curtain wearing an MCWF War of Attrition tee shirt and black wrestling tights. As usual, he pumps his fists to the initial guitar riffs as he comes out. Then he starts to put his game face on during the march to the ring but a fan throws a sign reading, "Mitch, your nose is growing" in his face. Davis tears the sign from his hands and carries it into the ring where he makes a big show of ripping the sign to shreds.]
Rexx: Gee, not your usual focused entrance by the challenger. This could present a problem for Davis.
Dean: Couldn't happen to a better guy Rexx: Debatable. For now, let's go see if Puffer has done his research on Blaster. "And his opponent. A man who since entering the sport this past spring has become one of the fastest rising and most controversial stars in the entire wrestling industry. After a meteoric climb through the rankings, he has, at the tender age of 19, become the youngest professional champion in recorded wrestling history. Thereby shocking many of the greatest minds in the sport including his own brother, MWCF commissioner, [name withheld]. Llllladies and gentlemen, weighing in at 245 lbs, all the way from his hometown of Duluth,
Minnesota, here is your MCWF Heavyweigh champ'n of da whiiiiiiirl!!! JJJJJJJJOHNNY BLLLLLLLASTER JJJJUUUUUNIOR!!!" Dean: Heavyweight champion of da whirl? What is that? Rexx: I think you'll have to ask Bichael that one, Dean. Aside from maybe the names I didn't understand a word he said. I've never been a ring announcer.
I don't speak Hype. Dean: Well, I got most of it and to sum it up… Rexx: Of course you did, Dean. Why do you think I find you so annoying? You actually fall for Puffery like that. Pardon the pun.
[Meanwhile, as Dean and Rexx make light of our special guest, the champion comes out of the curtain strutting like a peacock to a huge heel pop.
Blaster is wearing pant length wrestling tights and has the world title slung over his right shoulder. The crowd chants of 'Blaster, go home' seem to just float over his head as he approaches the ring and steps in between the ropes.]
Dean: The champion looks supremely confident for this matchup. Rexx: That he does, Dean. But that could work against young Blaster. Much as you hate it, Davis has shown in the past that you can't ever take him lightly.
[The champion bends down to hand the world title belt to one of the ring attendants and as he does so the bell rings. Immediately, the Purist charges out of his corner and starts clubbing the champion in the back with a barrage of forearms before snap maring him into the center ring and placing him in a bow and arrow hold.]
Rexx: Quick start by the challenger. And a wise move jumping on the champion early. Sometimes, a person's own accouterments can work against him.
Dean: Acooutri what? Rexx: Accouterments. You know his stuff. The more stuff you have to divest yourself of to wrestle, the longer you're vulnerable to opening rear attacks.
[Davis now releases the hold to pull the champion to his feet and starts to encircle his waist, but Blaster ducks in behind, hammerlocks Davis's arms behind him, and shoves him into the far left corner. Davis hits the turnbuckles hard and stumbles back into a clothesline from young Blaster.
After which, the champion turns his back to Davis and starts tapping the side of his head with his index finger.]
Dean: Gotta agree with the champ. That was pretty smart thinking. Rexx: Yeah, it was a smart reversal but you gotta stay…rollup by Davis! Dean: But the champ kicks out after only a count of one. Rexx: Still, Blaster needn't have suffered that pin attempt at all. He could have avoided it if he'd just stayed focused on his opponent.
[Both men get up and Blaster challenges Davis to a test of strength in the form of a Greco-Roman knucklelock. Davis accepts and ties up with Blaster but neither man gets an immediate advantage. So an impatient Blaster knees his opponent in the solar plexus and takes him to the mat with a shortarm clothesline. Then Blaster floats over and clamps Davis in head scissors.]
Dean: Some solid wrestling there by the champ. Much better to go on the attack than waste time on that test of strength.
Rexx: Perhaps, but Blaster seems impatient. It's like he expected to defend his title just by showing up.
Dean: Well, he is just facing Mitchell Davis. It's not like he's facing a serious contender like Dan Mastriani.
[After being in the scissors for about twenty or thirty seconds, Davis spins himself around and attempts to handstand out of the hold, only to be pushed back down by Blaster. Davis attempts a second time. Same result. So finally he gets annoyed and just pops his head loose and violently clotheslines Blaster to the mat.]
Rexx: There you go, Mitch. No need for the fancy stuff. It's not your style anyway. Dean: Mitch?! Boy, we sure have gotten chummy all of a sudden. Haven't we? Rexx: Gee, there's the pot calling the kettle black. Dean: You calling me a racist? Rexx: No, you pinhead! I'm saying you're always hanging with the talent. Dan Mastriani, Porno Inferno, Johnny Blonde. And wasn't it you who was telling about the Blood Brothers's Hurricane Bonnie party last week?
[Gee, looks like "focus" is our word for the day. Pay attention, Dean and Rexx. While you two talking heads have been bickering, Blaster and Davis have been exchanging punch blocks. Finally Davis gets sick of this pitched battle and attempts to hiptoss Blaster but the champion reverses it. Davis then attempts to return the favor but Blaster again reverses the hiptoss attempt and hurls Davis over the top rope to the concrete floor below.]
Rexx: Good counter wrestling there by both men but looks like advantage Blaster. Davis getting up trying to shake out the cobwebs but here comes Blaster sliding into home. SAFE! Dean: Rexx, I'm confused. I thought this was wrestling. Not baseball. Rexx: Well then, Dean, you need to do more study on wrestling in the nineties. Or even late eighties Japan for that matter. [Blaster continues the slide right out of the ring and nails Davis with a right haymaker before Irish Whipping Davis into the security railing. The champion then charges with designs of clotheslining Davis into the crowd.
But the Purist recovers and instead it's the champion who finds himself taking a root beer bath in the front row.]
Rexx: Blaster again overestimating his advantage. Dean: True, but you can't really blame him. It did look like…NO! NO! Piledriver by Davis in the crowd! Rexx: Whoa, Dean! How'd YOU know that move's name? Dean: Well after I took Scott Keith his birthday copy of Titanic last week, he was reticent to watch it with another male for (sniff) obvious reasons.
(wiping stray tear from eye.) So instead we just watched Wrestlemania 1. Rexx: Will wonders ever cease? An actual use for Paul Orndorff. [Now, the Purist hauls Blaster back over the railing and throws him in the ring. However, either Blaster's a quick healer or Davis didn't really hit that piledriver well enough. For as the Purist rolls under the bottom rope to re- enter the ring, Blaster is there to stomp him quite mercilessly. The champion holding the top rope as he does so for extra leverage and force.]
Rexx: Looks like the champion has decided to throw science out the window and just try mauling Davis.
Dean: That's it, Champ! Open up a can of Whoop Ass on him! Rexx: Whoop ass? Dean, I thought that term was trademarked. [Well, the term may be, but the action it describes certainly isn't for that seems to be what the world champion is doing as he picks up the Purist, shoves into the near corner and begins using the challenger as his own personal hard bag.]
Dean: Atta boy, Champ! Stay on him! Rexx: Good advice as far as it goes Dean. Blaster does need to maintain his focus now he's on the attack. But if he doesn't stiffen up those looping punches, it's gonna hurt him.
[Good call, Rexx. For no sooner do you upbraid Blaster than it happens. Davis catches one of Blaster's loose punches, uses it to duck in behind him and release German suplexes him into the center into the center of the ring.
Then Davis starts pushing his hand in front of him.] Dean: Oh God. Looks like Davis thinks the time is now to take the title. Please somebody stop him! Rexx: Davis whipping the champ to the far side. Davis backing up for momentum. Wait! What's this? What's this? [I'll tell you what this is, Rexx. As the Purist is backing up to perform the Davistator, what appears to be a little old lady jumps out of the front row with surprising agility and grabs the Purist's feet. Blaster, not seeing what's happened, takes advantage by dropping an elbow to the challenger's head and rolling him over for the pin. Hooking the right leg, still unaware that his outside benefactor is holding the Purist's left one down.]
Dean: One…two…THREE!!! Yes!!! Blaster wins!!! Blaster wins!!! "Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match via pinfall and STILL MCWF Heavyweigh Champion Of Dawhirl. JJJJJJJJohnny Bllllllllaster Juuuuuuunior!" Rexx: Yes, Dean. But you can't deny that was a very controversial victory for the champion.
Dean: (still watching towards ringside) Uh oh! Looks like this isn't over! [Indeed not, Dean. While Blaster's hand is being raised in victory, Davis has picked himself off the mat and realized what has happened. He spots the old lady or whoever the person is and jumps out of the ring to confront them.]
Dean: Looks like that traitor Davis has spotted the old woman. This could get ugly. Rexx: I doubt it, Dean. Davis is a gentleman. He'd never strike a woman. [But how about a man, Rexx? For as Davis argues with the interloper, he places his hand on the man's head and sure enough the little old lady hair comes off to reveal a very familiar face.]
Dean: Oh my God! It's Co-mander Coday! It's Co-mander Coday! Rexx: Thanks for the embellishment, Joey Styles. Like we don't know what Commander Cody looks like! [Problem for the Commander is that Davis knows what he looks like. Upon seeing his former manager unwigged, Davis immediately cocks his fist back to punch the man. So Cody starts running for his life on the Sensible Shoe Leather
Express with Davis a mere couple steps behind and Lenny the cameraman a couple behind him.]
Dean: Run, Commander, run! Rexx: He'd better run, Dean. Davis will rip that leech's head off if he gets a hold of the man.
[Cody dashes through the curtain and into the ringside area, where he's cornered at the production crew's refreshment table.]
Dean: No!!!! Looks like Cody's gonna get it now!!! Please let him escape, God! Please let him escape! Rexx: Looks like your prayers are answered, Dean. Coffee straight in the eyes of Davis! [Maybe not, Rexx. The hatred-spawned adrenaline seems to be taking over Davis's body as he rubs the coffee out of his eyes and continues chasing Cody.] Rexx: Davis still in pursuit! Cody still looking for a place to hide! Dean: Somebody stop Mitchell Davis! That traitor's gone berserk! [But nobody seems interested in breaking up this chase. They're too busy dodging the storage trunks Cody keeps throwing in Davis's path. Suddenly in the corridor ahead, Davey and Johnny Blonde are spotted discussing the pork bellies market when they see the chase heading their way and try to escape but fail miserably.]
Dean: Somebody stop the insanity! Commander Cody's just mowed down Davey and Johnny Blonde! Rexx: Dean, look at this! That's not the worst of it. Davis is picking up Johnny Blonde and hurling him at the Commander like a javelin! [But there will be no "Cowabunga, dude" as Davis's aim is most heinous and causes Blonde to take a serious wipeout in the corridor.]
Dean: Oh my God! Human projectiles! This has gotten seriously out of hand! Rexx: Gee, no kidding, Dean! Only question is how much worse can this get. [Plenty. After mowing down the poor unfortunate jobbers, Cody knocks over a couple security guards and flees into the parking garage, his former charge still in tow. Cody looks around and finally spies Risky Williams getting into a green four-door Neon and rushes over to hijack the car.]
Rexx: Uh oh! Bad news for Risky Williams! He should have gotten out of Dodge when he had the chance.
Dean: Risky, give him the damn car! Don't let the Purist kill Commander Cody! [Too late, Dean. For while Risky's refusing to give up the vehicle, Davis spots the Commander and comes sliding over the hood a la John Schneider in an episode of The Dukes of Hazard causing the former manager to continue fleeing for his life.]
Dean: And still this crazy chase continues! How much further can it go?! Rexx: Hopefully not much, Dean. They're heading out of the parking garage and we don't have the camera cable to go much farther than that.
[Don't worry, Rexx. It's not gonna be an issue. For as the Commander dashes out of the parking garage and across the street he's suddenly stopped short by the piercing sound of a siren. Davis sees the source of it and stays on the curb but Cody's already out in the street where…]
Dean: OH MY GOD! COMMANDER CODY, LOOK OUT! [Too late. The Commander turns toward the sound of the siren just in time to see the onrushing emergency vehicle come zooming toward him at frightening speed and drive him violently to the street.]
Dean: OH MY GOD! NO! NOOOOOOOOOOO! Rexx: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! I DON'T BELIEVE IT! COMMANDER CODY STRUCK BY AN AMBULANCE! TALK ABOUT IRONY! AND WE'RE OUT OF TIME! Dean: COMMANDER!!!! COMMANDERRRRRR!!! Rexx: See you next week!
