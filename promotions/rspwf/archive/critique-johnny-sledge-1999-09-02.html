[Critique] Johnny Sledge

2 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, this is the first roleplay I have done in quite some time. This is for the fed LAST. I want total honesty, seeing as I have to figure out what I have to do to get back to my old level of roleplaying. So without further adieu, the roleplay.....
------- [The scene opens up with out any special stuff, no music video, no career montage, nothing... just a simple fade in. As the picture becomes clearer you can see that the camera man is in somebody's office, which right now nobody is in there. By the look of the decorations on the walls this person is somewhat accomplished in the wrestling field. Trophies, plaques, pictures, and other types of wrestling memorabilia cover the two side walls and most of the back wall, but what really grabs your attention is what is covering a majority of that back wall, behind the desk. There are almost twenty belts resting on the shelves. Just as the camera man begins to get a better shot of the belts, a voice comes from behind you.]
Voice: Like my titles? Camera man: Uh........ yeah. [Turning around the camera spots the owner of those titles, awards, and the office... Johnny Sledge. Sledge is wearing an old black sleeveless Johnny Sledge T-shirt, red mesh basketball shorts, and Nike cross trainers. His short brown hair is wet, most likely from working out, and is semi-pushed back, with the rest of it going in different directions. Through the door he came in from, a fairly active gym can be seen. Off in the corner of the other room a wrestling ring can also be seen.]
Johnny Sledge: You know something, I will often times stop and look at all this crap and just be amazed that I was actually able to win all of those titles and awards.
[Sledge walks by the camera man and goes over to a fax machine that is on a table next to the desk and grabs the most recent faxes from the top of it. He then goes and sits down at the desk and begins going through the faxes as he begins talking.]
Johnny Sledge: You know, for a while I thought I that I was going to stay retired. It was relaxing to be able to sit at home with my wife and kid and not to have to worry about leaving them for an extended period of time. And there was no way I was missing the long road trips.
Camera man: Well, if that's the way you felt why did you bother coming back into the ring?
[Sledge puts down the papers for a second to point to the wrestling ring out in the gym area.]
Johnny Sledge: That's the reason, that ring. You see this is the same gym where I was trained to become a wreslter. This is the place where I train young kids who want to learn the basics about the sport.
Kids, like me, who have it in their blood to become a wrestler. After my knee surgery I thought I would be able to walk away, and that would be the end of it. But having this gym, though, and still training the kids kept the love of the sport in me.
When my knee was finally back to 100% I knew that I had to get back in the ring. Wrestling is my life. Ever since I started out, I knew I would not go into it and not give it all I had for as long as I could.
And now that I am healthy again, I know there is only one place for me to be and it is that wrestling ring.
It seems like at this point I am at square one again. You see all these titles behind me... [half turns to point at them]... they are all in my past. I never had any special treatment when I first started out, and I still do not expect anything.....
[During this soliloquy of Johnny Sledge he was still going through the faxes that he had gotten. He stops and reviews one of them carefully and then when he is done he puts down the faxes and begins talking again.]
Johnny Sledge: It appears that the first card is finally been made, and it is sent out. Camera man: Where are you on the card? Johnny Sledge: I am in the main event, against Jarred Matthews and
Mike Levvy. Its going to be a Parking Lot Brawl where falls will count anywhere. Have you ever heard of these two?
Camera man: Yeah, I've heard some stuff about them. From what I have heard they are both pretty good.
Johnny Sledge: Oh, that's good, because I don't want this to be me utterly destroying the two of them. I have a feeling, with only know the real basics about these two that I am definitely going to have fun in this match.
Camera man: What makes you say that? Johnny Sledge: First, let's look at Matthews. I admit I know nothing about him, but he must be able to do something, since he is in the main event. But I mean, come on... how can he be taken seriously when he comes to the ring with that young pop group.
Camera man: Huh? Johnny Sledge: You know Hanson... mmmmmmBop..... Hanson. Camera man: Hehe.... I am not sure if they are the same people. Johnny Sledge: And as far as Levvy goes... just look at the nick name, "Tha Show Stoppa." Hehehe, it's because of people like this that I have been able to enjoy my work so much. I bet I can figure out what his whole shtick is going to be. He comes out and claims that he is the savior of all of us, that he is the best thing to ever happen to this place, and so on. What's funnier though is to see him squirm when I prove that he is really nothing more than a wanna be.
Levvy, I don't know you, I have never heard of you, and to be honest I don't think I would really care to get to know you. The nick name says it all. Honestly, I am going to have a lot of fun showing "Tha
Show Stoppa" up. I have been in the sport long enough to have your type cross my path more than once. You will probably talk tough, and say how you are going to beat the hell out of myself and.... [looks down at the paper]... Matthews, but when push comes to shove, you are going to back into a corner and have no clue what to do.
[Sledge gets up out of his chair and begins walking around his office, the whole time still talking.]
Johnny Sledge: It will be a street fight, nothing more. Me I am used to wars... in and out of the ring. If you want, I will be more than happy to send you a copy of my greatest matches video and you can see for yourself. When the three of us get in the street, don't expect anything less than a total beating at my hands. Because, guys, out there in that parking lot.... the tough talk and fancy nicknames will not save you from me. You will learn quickly what it is like to have to deal with... The One... The Only... The Original... Johnny
Sledge!!! [Johnny Sledge then leaves his office and shuts the door behind him.
