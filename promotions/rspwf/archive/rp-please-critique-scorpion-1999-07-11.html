[RP Please Critique] Scorpion

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a roleplay from the aWA right after I returned this past May. Please be honest as I hope to improve.
------------------- *The scene opens up inside of Scorpion's locker room where he is returning after what happened on RAW. A reporter meets him at the door and starts to question him.* Reporter: Scorpion! What made you do what you did tonight? I thought you wanted to fight Sam. Was your ankle that bad? You seemed fine to me earlier.
What's going on? Scorpion: One question at a time. First of all, my ankle is fine. Sure, it's not 100%, but I can easily wrestle on it. You want to know why I did what I did? Well, I did want to fight Psycho Sam, but I had a choice. I could have fought him tonight for nothing, or I could have waited to see if he wins the
World title, then fight him for the World title. Now which would you choose?
A guaranteed World title shot, or nothing? Reporter: Well obviously I'd choose the... Scorpion: That wasn't a question you answer. Now anyways, Peter Lowry comes down, uses what he says is his authority and makes an @ss out of himself.
Now Peter, you can do nothing to me, commissioner or no commissioner. You hold no authority over me, and tonight you held no authority over Psycho
Sam. If we wanted to fight, we would have, but you making harmless threats put the nail in the coffin for me not to fight. You said you would strip
Psycho Sam of his title shot, but you didn't do it. Why? Because you can't!
You didn't do anything to me because you knew I was close enough to you to beat your @ss for being so stupid. You see, I am an Outlaws, have always been an Outlaw, and I will always be an Outlaw. I created The Outlaws stable in the aWA, the stable that dominated this league last year. But when I re-signed as The Enforcer I signed The Outlaw clause to my contract, which says that I don't have to listen to any authority in the aWA. I also signed a clause that said I have all booking control over myself and anything involved with me. Now I don't use this that often, but on occasions such as tonight, I used it, and it gave me the power to call off the match, secure
Sam's title shot, and basically rip every drop of power that you thought you had out of your hands at that moment. So Sam will be fighting for the World title against Dream King, and I guess that leaves you and me Lowry.
Reporter: Wow, what a confrontation that will be! Scorpion: Damn man! You act like Gene Okerlund now! You serve no purpose except for holding the mic up for me. Get the hell out of here!
*Scorpion chases the reporter off.* Scorpion: Now Peter, you like to use your ankle lock on me, and you like to try and break my ankle, then run. Well Peter, at the PPV why don't you finally step up to me one on one to see what you've got? Sure, you can sneak up with your group and attack me from behind when I'm tired, but your group is gone and we'll be face to face. So Peter, we will have this match, and since you are so obsessed with trying to injure me, why don't we make this a submission match? You can use your ankle lock all you want, but I've got a few submission moves that will make your ankle lock look like a titty twister. Oh, and Peter, seeing as how I do hold booking control over this match, I am naming the winner the number one contender for the World title.
It does me no harm because I've already got matches with the two World title contenders set up for the future, so either way I'm guaranteed a title shot.
Peter, soon you will realize that I mean what I say when I say that Scorpion
6:32 says I'm twice as good as you'll ever be!
*The scene fades as Scorpion goes into his locker room, leaving the cameraman out in the hall. * -------------------- Thanks for your time! -- [name withheld] [detail withheld]
