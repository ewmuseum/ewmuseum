[Free Agent] "Thugged Out" Damon Mack

Yo let me spit this right quick... I am a free agent in need of A GOOD FED. The fed most have a good lightweight/hardcore division. Please get back to me asap.
SAMPLE RP Sample Roleplay: I ain't have much time so I do a lil flow [Cameras fade into a small room in the back of the arena. A man is shown sitting at a desk with what appears to be a black & mild on his ear. He's wearing a white Oakland Raiders Jersey which appears to be #24 Charles Woodson.
The man is wearing a black do-rag and a white Raiders hat. The amn begins to speak.] Man- Let the Truth be known... True Hardcore is hear to school yall. You know I like to spit more than anyone else when it comes to an interview. I got a chance to do a trial tape here for this federation. All I gotta say is I apperciate the people in the fed who gave me this opportunity. But let me put it all on you now.
I am Jay "Thugged Out" Damon Mack... I am a force to be reckoned with. I am probably better than anyone on the roster in this federation but I will try to be easy on them. Everyone in this federation knows nothing about me and thats just how I like it. You see I have a chance to show each and everyone of you who the true professional wrestler is. When it comes to hardcore theres none like me. I can do hardcore better than the greatest hardcore wrestler ever Mick
Foley. So let it be known that "Thugged Out" Damon Mack is here to stay!
Now lets get to a few more points... I found out two things early in life. If you are weak u will be pushed around. If your hard u will dominate and no one will try to get in your way. See they call me a pimp and a playa but you know I can't help it I'm big time. See everyone calls me big time and big dog and a big baller but u know I am just that great!
Now since this is a short interview for time sake I want to end on a good note... REMEBER ME FOR LIFE CAUSE THERE IS NONE BETTER THAN "Thugged Out" Damon
Mack
