[Roleplay] Please critique

30 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up with clips from last week. The first one is Kydd X arriving to the arena with his duffel bag on his shoulder. He just hopped of a bus and is entering the arena in normal street clothes.)
Kydd X: When I arrived at the Pavillion last week, there wasn't a lot of fanfare. I am after all a rookie in this sport. Hell, I'm surprised anyone recognized me at all, and that includes security.
(Clip shows Kydd granting an interview in his locker room.) Kydd X: Even though I really shouldn't have, I even gave an interview for the reporter having a hard time find one. Considering all the massive egos and chipped shoulders around here, I'm surprised there were any interviews in the first place.
(Clips of Kydd X's entrance is shown along with some high spots, such as the 360 degree Rocker Dropper leg drop, the somersault plancha, and finally the X-Plosion to finish off Dave Taylor.)
Kydd X: As I have done throughout my training, I gave it 110% while in the ring and flying out of it. Dave Taylor really didn't stand a chance, and everyone in that building knew it. Yet, something was amiss. Something was missing...respect.
(Clip showing some of the front office personnel.) Kydd X: From the front office not airing my interview til the middle of the show... (Clip of Higor Rosa hitting Kydd X over the head with a beer bottle.) Kydd X: ...to some of the wrestlers who had not even made their inring debut. It became obvious that night that I'm fighting an uphill battle in the SSW. I will go through everyone in the SSW to gain the respect I deserve. I will not be overlooked because I don't have...
(Antwan Carter coming to the ring.) Kydd X: ...an attitude problem... (Clips of Dan The Man) Kydd X: ...an entourage of beautiful women... (Clips of the Jack Vars) Kydd X: ...or a long history in this sport. I went into this match believing that I was going to get a fair shake, and all of us would be considered equal. I was wrong. Now, it's time to shake things up and do it my way.
(The scene changes to a Kydd X workout. He's shown kicking the heavy bag and throwing punches into it. Shouts of intensity are echoed throughout the empty room. After a flurry of punches and kicks to the heavy bag, he stops and looks into the camera. His eyes seem to pierce right through the camera to the viewer.)
Kydd X: Eric Dreagle, you're going to be the first, but definitely not the last. (Scene fades)
