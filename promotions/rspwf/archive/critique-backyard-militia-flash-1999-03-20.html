[Critique] Backyard Militia flash

20 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a recent flash I did, and even though I'm not to proud of it, I'm posting it on the RSPWF for you guys to tear it apart anyways! Normally, my
BYM flashes aren't humorous and I was reluctant to make this one funny.
Please lemme know if you like it. thanks, Rev. Philbert Magweed =========================================== [~~~This is Flash #1 in a series of Backyard Militia flashes for the OTB Tag
Team Tourney~~~]
[the screen is black. A warning appears in white letters] THE FOLLOWING FLASH MAY CONTAIN EXPLICIT LANGUAGE ALONG WITH VIOLENCE. IT
SHOULD NOT BE VIEWED BY YOUNG CHILDREN AND OLD LADIES THAT ARE EASILY
OFFENDED. [the words fade out] [the scene fades in: We see the front of a nursing home. The sign above the entrance reads "THE PLEASANT NURSING HOME" The camera does a nifty little fade thing and we "magically" end up inside the nursing home. The camera focases on the shoes of one of the Nursing home employees. The camera slowy moves up. We see the massive tree-trunk legs, then the huge pecs and bulging arms, we are definately dealing with one BIG man here. The camera slows down as we get a glimpse of the face. The face of....a WOMAN! This very
Chynaesque type woman has a big scowl across her face and a little mustache forming above her lips. She is in her white uniform, and wearing a white hat with a red cross on it that sits on top of her shoulder length, black hair.
From behind, and old man with a walker approches] OM(whinning)- "NURSSH!! NURSSH!!!! *HACK* I need my pillsh. I want a new pillow. MAAATTTTLLLOOOOCCCKKKK!!!!"
[the nurse turns around and literally growls at the man who responds by walking away as fast as he can. The camera shows entrance as two men, each wearing uniforms like the nurse. We know the two men as the Backyard
Militia. They're followed by a police officer. The three men approach the nurse. The policeman pushes the two men towards the nurse]
Policeman- "De's two is heya faw community soivice..." Nurse- "_*GROWLS*_" [the policeman pats the two men on the back, turns around, and RUNS LIKE
HELL!] Nurse- "Well boys....ARE YOU READY TO SUFFER" [William Blaze and Johnny Demolition, the BYM, stare in horror] JD- "Um....no?" Nurse- "TOO BAD!!" [the nurse grabs the two men by the collar of their uniforms and drags them down a hallway.]
Nurse- "Listen to me boys...I'm putting you in charge of THIS hallway. When ever one of these geezers wants something, YOU will get it for them, YOU will hear them complain about their pills, and at 6 O'clock...YOU will be the one that turns on Matlock! If you fail...I WILL REMOVE YOUR TESTICLES
WITH A SPOON!!!" [both men grab their...] Nurse- "ANY QUESTIONS?!!!" WB- "NO!" Nurse- "Good. I'll be checking up on you in half an hour!" [the nurse walks away. As a man in a wheel chair passes her, she turns him around, and gives him a push.]
Man- "AAAAAAHHHHHH!!!!" [we hear a crash(the man ran into a cart of bedpans).] JD- "This is no place for a man of science! I spent the first 15 years of my life mastering the art of making deadly weapons out of common household items. I deserve better than this!"
WB- "I HATE OLD PEOPLE!!" JD- "..I mean, is blowing up a small apartment, ONLY TWO ROOMS MIND YOU...TWO ROOMS!, THAT big of a crime?!" WB- "IT SMELLS FUNNY IN HERE!!! IT REAKS OF OLD PEOPLE!!!" JD- "What kinda of world is it where a grown man recieves community service?!! I MEAN REALLY?!! Community service is what they give juvenilles for speeding!!! It's just NOT RIGHT!"
[an old man walks up] OM- "*HACK* Excuse me there sonny....I have a complaint!" JD- "WHAT?!" OM- "Well....ya see...I was gettin' up outta bed to get my pills when I saw
Edgar Jenkins go by in his wheelchair. It was making such a racket that I nearly choke on my pills...I expect peace and...."
JD- "What's you name, sir?" OM- "What? Oh...uh...it's Sanford" JD- "Well Sanford this is what I think about you and your little problems!" [Johnny pulls out one of his famous homemade bombs] JD- "I'm going to blow you away now..." WB- "YEAH...YOU BEST BE DOWN WITH THA MILITIA!!!!" [Johnny stares at William] JD- "Why is it, when I ever I make a BRILLIANT statement, striking terror into the hearts of the weak, you go and screw it up with your 'DOWN WITH THA
MILITIA' crap?!!"
WB- "B-B-B-B-B-B-ACKYARD!!!!! YEAH!!!! WE ARE THA MILITIA!!!!" JD- "STOP WITH THE SCREAMING! We're inside, I'm standing right next to you, no need to shout!"
[while the two continue to argue, the man runs...er..walks away at a slow pace. Johnny looks at his watch]
JD- "5:59" WB- "MATLOCK..MATLOCK..MATLOCK...." JD- "Quiet, you don't get to watch it!" BW- "Damn" [they walk into a room where several old people are gathered around a small television set]
Old Lady- "HURRY UP!! We're gonna miss it!" JD- "Awww...does da wittle old wadie not want to miss hew pwecious Matwock" [the lady whacks him with her cane on the head] JD- "OWW!" [Johnny turns on the tv, trying to avoid another whack] TV ANNOUNCER- "..NOW...IT'S TIME FOR MATLOCK" [the old people let out a cheer, which is followed by a chorus of hacks.] WB- "THIS SUCKS!" [William grabs the remote and changes the channel. We see the Fabulous Ones wrestling against two unknow wrestlers.]
WB- "I HATE CHINEESE PEOPLE!!" JD- "I think they're from Japan..." WB- "STUPID CHINEESE PEOPLE! YOU BETTER BE DOWN WITH THA MILITIA!!!!" JD- "Stop saying that!" [the old people start screaming "Matlock." Finally, an old woman stands] Old Woman- "First they took away our freedom, NOW THEY'RE TAKING AWAY MATLOCK! WE CAN'T STAND FOR THIS! WE MUST REVOLT!!" [the old people cheer and hack again in approval] JD- "Oh sh*t" Old man- "LET'S GET EM!" [The old people start chasing after William and Johnny. The BYM members run down the hall followed by a mob of old people. Before they can get out, the run into what appears to be a giant wall. Actually, it's the nurse.]
Nurse- "Going somewhere?" [the Nurse notices the mob of old running...er walking towards her] Nurse- "Uh-oh!" [the nurse grabs a phone] Nurse- "THE OLD PEOPLE ARE ATTACKING!! DAMNIT, GET ME SOME BACK-UP!!" [Johnny and William slowly sneak out as the screen fades to black. In white letters the following appears on the screen...]
TO BE CONTINUED..... [fades out]
