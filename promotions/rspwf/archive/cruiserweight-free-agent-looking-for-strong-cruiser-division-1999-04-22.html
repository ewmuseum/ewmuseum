[CRUISERWEIGHT] Free Agent - Looking for Strong Cruiser Division!

22 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am reposting this. I thank everyone for the offers I have received so far. Right now there are a couple of federations I am considering. I'd like, however, to hear from anyone else who might be interested before making a decision. So, please, if you have a competitive cruiser division, feel free to send some info...
--- Wrestler's Name: "Blitz Lightning" Matt Kreig     Weight: 220 lbs. Height:   5'10" Hometown:   Turku, Finland Looks/Ring Attire:   Kreig is very pale, with almost white-blonde hair that comes down to his shoulders, and bright blue eyes. He's clean-shaven, and it is debatable if he even needs to shave more than once a week. Obviously in fantastic shape, but is built like a gymnast, not a bodybuilder. Comes to the ring wearing a white mask and cape, both decorated with the blue lightning bolts (blue because of his Finnish background, and lightning bolts for obvious reasons), but discards both before starting the match. He wrestles in white tights and boots that are decorated with pale blue bolts of lightning. The only break in the white-and-blue motif is a tiny set of Olympic rings on his left calf. During interviews, he usually wears blue jeans and a heavy sweatshirt, obviously accustomed to the cold Finland whether.
Theme Music: "Small Town" John Mellencamp Orientation: Face Ten Moves:   1. Fireman's Carry 2. Northern Lights Suplex 3. Moonsault 4. Top-rope spinning Frankensteiner 5. Shooting Star Press 6. Powerbomb 7. Abdominal Stretch 8. Flying Lariet 9. Dropkick to knee 10. Inverted Piledriver Finishing Move:   Lightning Strikes Twice: top-rope somersault legdrop Major Strengths:   1. Aerial: can master almost any aerial move after seeing it done once or twice. 2. Intelligence: has learned how to execute just about every move under the sun. His size limits him in some cases though. 3. Endurance: years of gymnastics training has left him with the ability to outlast many opponents. Background:   A former three-time co-holder of the TSW World Tag Team Championship (twice with partner Jerry "GoldenFlash" Jenkins as Double Strike, and for a short time with Tank as Speed Chamber), "Blitz Lightning" Matt Kreig has launched his assault on the singles ranks of the wrestling world after partner Jerry Jenkins decided that the tag team division had become the preserve merely of clowns and circus acts.
Matt Kreig grew up in northern Finland, dreaming of being the first Finn to win an Olympic medal in gymnastics. At an early age, it became obvious that his potential was well beyond anything that Finnish gymnastics coaches could handle, so he and his family moved to Chicago, where he entered the camp of famed coach Boris Grunzinsky. Matt had amazing natural talent, and the confidence to believe that he actually could win a gold medal or six. But he soon learned that gymnastics judging is as much about expectations as reality, and while his routines won over the fans, he only had a few second and third- places to show for it.
One day, as he was coming into the gym for yet another long day of training, he came across some wrestlers practicing. They were trying to master the
Shooting Star Press, and failing miserably. Matt watched from the doorway for a while, but soon his derisive laughter was noticed. When the wrestlers sarcastically invited him to give a try, he hopped to the top rope and did a picture-perfect SSP. Startled, they asked him to try a moonsault, and once he figured out what that was, he did it perfectly as well. Soon, he was wrestling in a small Chicago-based promotion, and learning the art of high-flying from aging Mexican superstar El Gato Supremo.
Two years later, Matt had become "Blitz Lightning", and has convinced his reluctant tag-team partner that they were ready to take on the wrestling world.
Which is totally in character for him. Matt firmly believes that, as long as he doesn't have to rely on judges, he can beat every cruiserweight in the world and that he and Jenkins can beat any tag team in the world. But he still wrestles like he is subconsciously trying to impress a set of judges, which leads him to try some totally insane maneuvers, like a SSP from the top rope onto the floor, or a springboard moonsault into the crowd. Luckily, his amazing body control usually lets him pull these stunts off without too much damage.
Matt is the ultimate face, because he always felt that the fans gave him a fair shake during his gymnastics career when the judges would not. He'll delay the beginning of a match for five minutes while he shakes the hands of every fan he can find, and he always goes all out to give them a good show. To that end, he is always looking for spectacular new moves to learn. His love for the fans is also the reason that he will come charging out of the dressing room to help any face that is being outnumbered, even if it is someone that he barely knows, and even if it puts him at a serious disadvantage.
