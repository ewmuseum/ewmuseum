ENFORCER*** Started his in ring career in 1996,for UECW,following extensive training from UECW legend,Ironman and from his recently deceased father, WPWA legend,"The King" Kevin Phillips,he is as well the first ever graduate of the
WPWA base school.
Just about one year after his UECW debut in 1996,he had already won every title in the federation...realized his talent as a heel,whe He and his cohort,Scott Steiner, ambushed and attacked his mentor,Ironman which led to the infamous ENFORCER/Ironman feud...he remained a dominant player in UECW up until its closing in Mid `98.
After touring several independants and working in UECW for most of 1998,he decided he wanted something more...and had his name made when he once again turned against Ironman,whom had followed ENFORCER to WPWA,that move as well as a place in the "Insiders" had cemented his status as a WPWA super star,one,of which he still remains to this day.
In mid 1999, ENFORCER debuted in the BOW,after a couple of false starts,and an adjustment period in which dealt with the death of his grandfater,which brings us to the point were at now...YOU have not seen ALL that there is to ENFORCER yet...and of that,you,my enemies... WILL NOT like what you see..
