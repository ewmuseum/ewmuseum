Do I regret the Open Flash System?

19 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One of the things that has kept WAR! apart from most other feds is it`s Open Flash System. I know other feds use them, but I tend to feel that WAR! is the home of the Open Flash System, and the players use it so effectively. Now, WAR! has had to use OFS out of time & e-mail account restraints. I`ve tried using Closed Flash Systems in the past, but well, it just wasn`t possible. As a result, the heavy load of messages has meant that many big names who could have potentially joined WAR! didn`t (or if they did, they left soon after...) So do I regret it? Do I perhaps wish that WAR! could have been a closed flash system and had all the big names? To be honest, no. You see, WAR! has always been about the action outside the ring more than the action in it, and only the Open Flash System has enabled the story-lines to develop sufficiently and at a fair pace. And it also made WAR! a 24hr fed, where something could happen or could be said at anytime whatsoever. You didn`t have to wait for a big collection of interviews, they`d come through whenever. Now, I know some people prefer the CFS because it gives the fed a more professional, realisitic feel, and to an extent, I agree. It also allows the fedhead to exercise sometimes much necessary editorial control. But I doubt it would ever be as much fun as the Open Flash System, and the bond between many of the wonderful players in WAR! is probably something stronger than you`ll ever see in any CFS fed. Yet if I started up a fed again, I`d probably consider using Closed Flash System, even if only to have a change. Such, my friends, is life. Jake out. http://members.tripod.com/~wartable/index.html
