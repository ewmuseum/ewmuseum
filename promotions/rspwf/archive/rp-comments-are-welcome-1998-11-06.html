RP - COMMENTS ARE WELCOME

6 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene fades in on an old wrestling ring in what looks like an empty, desolate arena. As the camera pans around the place we can see that the windows are all boarded up and the place is infested with rats. The camera zooms in on the ring and we can see the faded lettering on the ring apron. It reads "International Championship Wrestling" All of a sudden we can hear a voice echo through the arena off-camera. It's an almost incoherent, drunken voice]
"Ha! Ha! Here we are again. Since so many people like to talk about the past and only trash talk about what they going to do, and haven't accomplished anything yet, I think I'll just do the same. For it is said that the hyena with the biggest balls does not fear the sleeping lion. And eagle does not fly backwards. And brave buffalo does not cower from wolves and......
[The voice stops suddenly and we can hear a guzzling noise. The camera pans over to the other end of the arena. Standing at what used to be the wrestlers entrance way, is Eddie "Bloody Arrow" Chavez the insane, drunken Native
American. He is wearing his favourite D.D.A.A.M. t-shirt which is plain black with bold white letters on the front, acid stained blue jeans, spurred cowboy boots, several necklaces around his neck and a studded leather jacket. The ever present bottle of Scotch is clasped in his right hand. In the other hand he is holding sacred beads. He looks despondently at the ground then finally looks up at the camera]
"Pale face JT Damon had balls like Matahachutatachutatamachuwata to put
Bloody Arrow in his opening montage but did not tell the whole story. So sit back children grab some booze......."
[He takes a gigantic swig of Scotch and spits some of it out on the ground] "And let Eddie tell you a little story. August 1998. In this very arena. Bloody Arrow. Nightblade. JT Damon. A match for the International Championship Wrestling Heavyweight Title. As usual Bloody Arrow dominates the match. He is on his way to victory. The gold is about to fall right into his lap. The crowd is chanting "Bloody, Bloody" and Bloody Arrow can almost taste victory......"
[The voice of the camera man is heard] "Then what happened Chavez?" "Then uhm about fifty wrestlers came out. Uh yeah. And they beat down the mighty warrior that is Bloody Arrow. He fought back valiantly but it was fifty men to one. I was knocked unconcious long enough for JT to recover from the beating I was giving to him and Nightblade to uhm put me in his little finishing move......"
[Chavez takes another swig then pours Scotch on himself] "Then as I lay on the mat in this pathetic excuse for a submission, I tried to tell the referee "This is fun!" but the beating I took from those fifty wrestlers earlier had caused temporary brain damage so I forgot English. And
I told the ref in Navajo how much fun I was having. But he thought I was saying "I quit!" so he rang the bell and the rest is history......."
"Chavez, did this really happen?" "Are you calling me a liar pale face?" "No, no. Sorry!" [Chavez walks over to the ring, kicks a few cardboard boxes out of the way and climbs in the ring and paces around]
"And before I throw you out of here, there is a couple of pale faces that I feel this urge to briefly, and I mean very briefly address since they are NOT worth the amount of air it takes to mention their names......."
[He spits in the ring then takes a swig of Scotch and simulatenously starts laughing] "Omega you seem determined to end your career this weekend. Enough said. Now there is another chump who has dared mention the name of Bloody Arrow. And If
I remember correctly, he even said that I have to......"
[Chavez doubles over with laughter spilling booze all over the old ring] "Ha! Ha! Worry about him! Armin Poussi, I will forgive you since you are new to the pro ranks, your an idiot and I you only want a title shot. Well rest assured this belt is not staying around my waist for long. As a matter of fact Ha! Ha! YOU CAN TAKE IT FOR ALL I CARE!"
[Chavez pours the last drops of Scotch down his throat and sits down in the corner of the ring]
"This interview is over pale face......."
