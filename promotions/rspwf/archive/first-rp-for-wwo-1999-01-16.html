First RP for WWO.....

16 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As the camera fades in James Van Steele is relaxing on a blue couch, it looks as if he hasn't showered yet and has let his beard grow in a little bit. He is wearing a
EJPW T-shirt, which has stains and rips all over it, also he wearing a pair of Looney Toones boxer shorts, and he is wearing light blue bath robe over all of this, each side of the belt strap looks as if it has been tossed to one side. In his left hand he has got a beer (High Life) covered by one of those foam holders. His right hand his down his boxers alha, Al Bundy and he seems to be enjoying watching the football game on t.v.....The camera moves back, and there seems to be a door of to the side, which suddenly swings open and Eric Van Steele comes limping out fast....]
EVS: "What the hell is this? What the hell are you doing? You got a debut match to work out for!!!
[JVS, looks up, takes a swig of beer and lets out a long burp.....] JVS: "I am watching t.v, what the hell does it look like I am doing! Can't a guy get a Sunday off to just sit back and chill? Is that too much to ask, bro?"
[stern look on his face] EVS:" I don't think you get it, bro....the WWO is not just one of your slacker feds that you are used to fighting in. Yes, I am talking about your Japanese feds in which they just feed you opponents because of your rep. See, you aren't a name over here in the states YET. And until you are a big name I want you to be working out every DAY!! I want you back downstairs and hitting those damn weights with me!"
[big ol' smirk across his face] JVS: "Wait, you can't be joking, not with an ugly look like that on your face! Eric, bro....you know and I know that Willy is just some rookie that the company is throwing to me, just to see how much pain the guy can take, and to try and test my mettle....I am not at all worried. Now, go into the damn fridge pull out a beer and a foam thingy and sit your ass down on this couch and watch the damn game with me! The reason why I took this U.S gig is so that me and you could hang out."
[Eric, moves towards the couch and the fumes hit is nose, he flings back in disgust and waves his hand in front of his face...]
EVS: "Holy Christ! What the hell died over there? And better question, is why the hell did it die on MY couch?!?"
JVS: "This is not your couch, asshole, I know that for a fact, because I BOUGHT this couch in Japan....and besides, it's a natural stench." [EVS, walks to one of the unused pillows of the couch and flips up the cushion, and reads the tag that is on it.]
[rubbing his chin] EVS: "hmmmmm...Made in America, wow. Well, I guess that solves who owns the couch, now doesn't it!! So, get off that couch you lazy sack o' s*it get downstairs, and hit those weights. So, that you can go out and show the ENTIRE world what exactly what it means to be a
VAN STEELE in the ring!! You got me? And if you win on Monday, I will give you tuesday off, ok James?" [JVS takes a long chug of his beer and looks up at EVS again.....with a blank stare.] JVS: "I suddenly see why I was mom's favorite....Ok, ok, I will work out, let me just catch the end of the first quarter. [begins to mumble as Eric walks back through the door he came in] damn WWO, now I gotta do it all over again, this really pumps my nag....I can't believe I that guys brother.."
[James rolls himself up off the couch, flips off the t.v and heads towards the same door as Eric went through, you may assume that is the downstairs, But right before he leaves the room he lets out one last good long burp, and squeezes out a fart....]
