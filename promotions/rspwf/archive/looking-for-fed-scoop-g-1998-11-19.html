Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am looking for a fed, e-mail me if you want me to join yours. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------- [ Scene opens up to a Chinese temple. Monks walking up and down the stairs of the temples. On top of the temple doors is a "Yin Yang" sign. The temple's doors slowly open up. Standing behind the door is a medium built man. He stands
6'4 and weighs around 230 lbs. He is wearing baggy blue jeans, yellow Timbland boots, and a blue Polo jacket. He is also wearing a blue bandana around his head. His ears are pierced, on his arm is a visible tatoo that says, "Scoop G".
Scoop G looks into the camera and raps. A rap beat is playing in the background. ] Scoop G: I am a pro wrestler, the type that put people's career in danger, when I attack their is no turning back. I burst into scene so fast that even
Nostradamus can't foresee me. Like Bruce Lee with jeet kune do, as you consume lo mein. I consume knowledge of ancients now in all my karate classes. I test for advanced placement. I practice helicopter kicks while you're content with shadowboxin'
my concoction. Just the moves of my tiger and crane will annihilate your brain. From the regiment you reseve everlasting pain. From the 9 deadly techniques of my cat paws to the 6 elements of my dragon claws, I stop the beating of your heart. Galaxy stance is my fighting art, force my kay oh ken. Attack to times ten, waiting for the movement again. I'm studying pressure points and studying the structure of your latest joints, for the perfect way to pull you apart peace by peace. Just waiting for my power level to increase, step kicking through cresent moon kicks, and shadow style flexes its ability of fatal hits.
Giving most oponents the sh*ts, training to do the snake. Using my poison bite to cast out the fake, grab moves from ti quan doh. Using my chee to form it in to flow, I'm more deadly than the ebola. My style is everlasting like energizer, when i release my chee energy its like a ever rizeing gizer. My style are full of tactics which include bodily punches and back kicks.
Calculating the point on which to attack with mathematics. Opponents injury status is not existant. Because their brain is mad distant beyond help for more than an instant until they repent with pleads and begs, but I show no mercy.
Irregular heart beats, nose bleeds , severed legs, and knee ligaments get scrambled like eggs, that's how I leave my competition. I stomp and listen while they're wishin, they never fu*ked with the master of Kung Fu fighting.
[ camera fades away ]
