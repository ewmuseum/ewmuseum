[Flash] "The Gate Keeper" Mychael Knyghtwach [An Old Schooler/Flame Me!]

31 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anyone Who Will Read, Well I am A Former Net-Fed Role-Player and I haven't been on the Net for almost 3 years. I am really interested in joining the XBWL or ICW. Both feds I have heard great things about. So here is a
Flash I did in the NMWP the other day to refine my skills.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------- " T h e G a t e K e e p e r " [Fade In] [The camera opens up to complete darkness. The screen is pitch black but the howling of wind is heard. After about 5 seconds of constant wind, there is still no vision on the screen. Then a voice comes over.]
Voice Over: Everyday. . . there are obstacles we must overcome. There are hardships we all must endure. There is pain and strife in all of our lives. It is no surprise to the human race that we live by standards set by others. Everyone is afraid to do what their heart tells them because someone they know does not do it. Come enter the world of misfortune, hate, evil, and anger for a minute or two. . . but if you are one of the more pitiful souls of this world. . . I warn you to shield your eyes and somehow cover your ears because there could be an amazing revilation. . . and it's all in. . . THE TRUTH!
[The camera fades from the darkness to a house. It is not very high but it stretches about 40 Yards across. There is a two car open garage with no doors, just two slots and to the right is a brick stair step into the house. The front of the house is lined beautifully with flowers like roses and annuals.
One window is on the front of the house and there is a door. The camera paces up the steps and into the door. As you walk down a little hall-way of cream colored carpets, you take a left down three wood stained steps and you view a large living room with a Ceiling Fan hanging from the gorgeous wood ceiling.
As you take a left, you walk into the kitchin. A brick stove sits up against the wall and then a countertop stretches down the side of the wall and above it are cabinets. In front of the stobe there is a semi-bar. There are 6 Stools and a marble countertop. As you view the right you see a nice wood table with
6 Chairs and a nice, white fridgerator with two doors and an Ice Dispenser. As you walk to the right, you walk through a Slide-In door and down a brick path and into an enclosed underground pool with glass panels surrounding it from the outside. You can see a nice cut lawn with a large Chinese Maple in the yard.
But back into the pool, the walls are grey marble stones. The pool floor is pebble-like flooring that makes a path as the pool stretches from 1 Foot to 4
Feet. It is an amazing house and then you see a little boy, around 10 years of age, run into the room dressed in a pair of blue jeans with grass stains on the knees and dirty hands and a muddy T-Shirt. His mother walks in after him smiling seeing her little boy having fun. Then as she is washing her hands, he breaks the silence.]
Little Boy: Mommy. . . tomorrow night, can. . . Can I sleep over Jimmy's house?? [The little boy ask with some hope and looks at his Mom with a questioning expression.] Mother: Well Adam. . . you know I don't like you sleeping over Jimmy's because you always seem to get into mischevious over there. Aren't his ever home? Adam: Well. . . yeah they are, sometimes. But normally, Ryan watches us because you know how Mr. Stewart normally works a lot and Mrs. Stewart is always away on Business trips or whatever she calls them.
Mother: Ryan is only 13 years old, how is he fit to watch two little boys and himself? Adam: I don't know. . . but he does! [Smiles] Mother: Adam. . . . how come you never ask to sleep over Kevin's house or maybe Corey's? We know their familys and besides, tomorrow night your Father and I are going to a Party at the Johnson's so maybe you can stay with Corey and Kevin and Kevin's sister Amy and Melissa can watch you two.
Adam: But Kevin and Corey are so boring now! All they want to do is watch that dumb movie, Toy Story. I hate it. We've seen it a [Throws arms into air] GAZILLION TIMES! Besides, Jimmy just bought a B-B Gun and he wants to try it out! So can I Mom?! Can I!?
Mother: Adam honey, why do you have to hang out with a little trouble-maker like Jimmy. You know no one likes him. None of your friends like him and Jennifer's mother told me that he was caught with matches in the
Boys bathroom. You have such nice, classy friends, why can't you hang out with them? And on top of that, none of your friends hang out with Jimmy, so why do you?
Adam: I have a lot of friends, all kinds. Jimmy is a friend. Just because no one else hangs out with him doesn't mean I shouldn't. Different people like different things, Mom. Ju-[Interrupted]
Mother: I think you should stay with Kevin and Corey tonight. I don't want everyone to see that we allow our child to hang out with a little trouble-maker. Adam: But Mom! Why does it mat-[Interrupted Again] Mother: I said No, end of discussion. [The screen becomes fuzzy and then wavy and the wavys cause it to start to fade away as it fades into Mychael Knyghtwach, "The Gate Keeper" sitting atop a rock at the top of a hill. The sun is just beginning to set and the sky is a mixture of reds, blues, yellows, and purples. Knyghtwach is wearing a White
Fur Vest and Blue jeans with brown boots. His normally face paint is covering his face and he has a strange look upon his face. As he gazes into the horizon he begins to speak.]
"TGK" Mychael Knyghtwach: That happens all the time. So worried about what others will think of you, you're scared to show you who truly are. The fear of not being accepted surrounds you and makes you want to die and be born again a different man. What you wish for is something never to be granted. As we come out of our Mothers Womb we are all who we are and for some of us it takes longer to find out who that person is. Regardless of the amount of time it takes, no matter what, you still are that same person and nothing can change that. You can change your appearence a million times over if it fits, you can even change your name, but who you truly are is something that cannot be altered or changed. As you think of something to do, you wonder "If I do this.
. . will they accept me?" You are reluctant to exploit your inner self or your likes, your dis-likes because they do not agree with a friend. . . a boss.
. . a co-worker or even a family member. How sad it truly is that in this day and age we still fear the fact of rejection. Do you care so much that you will even hide your name? A price to pay to be someone you're not but the embarassment that lies in your mind knowing you are not this person. . .or is that just a mere formality that comes with the price you paid to begin with?
[Chuckles] The false nature you live in is one that you cannot avoid for long because soon enough your flaws will be noticed and compared to that of another and then it will ring in the minds of your peers that you two share a lot of common things and simple common sense will be able to piece together the puzzle to show that you are. . . pitiful, weak, dishonorable, and pathetic.
[Knyghtwach slowly slides off the rock and begins to pace down the grassy field. He looks directly into the camera.]
"TGK" Mychael Knyghtwach: These past few days I've been asked some questions. Some men ask me why do I have so much to say. Why am I saying so much. Such things like that. I know I have no match. . . I have nothing within the New Millenium Wrestling Promotion except a contract that has a lot of fine print no one bothered to read but in time they will all soon regret not doing so. It is a fair warning. I merely state my views of the world and the
New Millenium Wrestling Promotion through darkened glasses. I cannot say they are right. . . I cannot say they are wrong. . . but what I can say. . . is their mine! I've seen quite a bit in the New Millenium Wrestling Promotion thus far and all I can say is "That's it?" When I was around a while ago. . .
things were a little different. The souls that lurk within the darkness are so lost and out of control they have no inner guidance and as they travel down a lonely path they begin to question their existance. Now they have liberty, liberty they did not have as a child because their parents held them back. Men like. . . I'll keep you all waiting. . . lingering on. . . sitting on the edge of your seat. . . the road has ended at the edge of the cliff. . . and now. .
. I ask you all to sleep tight. . . because you never know what night. . . _-=. . . WILL BE. . .=-_ . . . . _-=. . .YOUR LAST!. . .=-_ [Knyghtwach continues walking down the hill as the screen becomes fuzzy and fades away.] [Fade Out] M y c h a e l K n y g h t w a c h -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------- -="TGK" Mychael Knyghtwach=- NMWP: 0-0-0 P.S.
