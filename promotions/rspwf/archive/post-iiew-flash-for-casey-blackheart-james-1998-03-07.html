Post IIeW flash for Casey "Blackheart" James

7 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE: A fairly simple set consisting of a blue and white backdrop. The RSPW-TV logo hovers annoyingly in the lower right hand corner of the screen. The focus of the camera shot is the hulking figure of
Casey "Blackheart" James. The shot reveals his massive torso, dressed in a plain black T-shirt. His hair is in small braids, held back with a black bandana.]
CJ: So, it's done... The IIeW is history... The Diamondback took the cup... Congratulations... I guess you're the rookie wonder in the world of wrestling now... S'about damn time someone else took the title. Myers, I hope you won't be upset that I'm not here to talk about you... I got bigger fish to fry... Hell, I had a match of my own in the IIeW... A match I won, I might add.
Gunnar, you're a tough bastard. I ain't gonna deny that. And that hooch of yours, well, let's just say that I got respect for your ability to hold down piss. That stuff was vile. As much as I'd like to elaborate on that topic, though, I don't much want to talk about you either.
My main target right here is Requiem... Eddie Munster himself. Kid, I heard your comments after the match, and I've got some thoughts of my own. All I can say is that it was your own damn fault for buying into the whole "payback" vibe. Yeah, you expected some sort of epic war and such. Sure, maybe I led you to believe that's what I wanted. But you admitted it yourself, big man, I'm not a man to be trusted. Goddamn,
Eddie, this is the IIeW we're talking about here... The biggest inter-fed event of all time. Do you honestly think I was going to allow some personal feelings get in the way of walking out of that event with a win under my belt? If you did, then you're more gullible than I gave you credit for.
Hell yeah, I used Syndicate tactics. News break, Eddie... I'm a member of the Syndicate. You wanna play with men, you gotta expect the tactics of men. I'm not a coward. I could beat you in my sleep... But hell, if you got the tools, you use them, and I did what I thought was right in the big triangle match. Yeah, sure... You're upset. But you ain't a winner. I am.
If you'll look back a bit, you'll realize I never bitched about the tactics you employed. Hell, how could I? All you were doing was what the Syndicate did months before you ever showed up. All I bitched about is you using our tactics and expecting the praise of the fans.
That's one thing you'll never get, Eddie... When you play a man's game, you can't expect the children to appreciate it. You want to talk about honor? Go ahead... While you're busy doing that, I'll have fourteen guys kicking your ass. Survival of the fittest... And the fittest is the guy with the most friends watching his back.
You're right about one thing, Eddie... I do pride myself in the fact that you never beat me... And I'll continue to do so, because you
_still_ haven't beat me. I'm the winner. I'm your superior. I'm THE
MAN. You want to talk about tired tactics? Sure, Claw coming down and caving your skull in with a lethal shin kick might be tired, but the important thing is that it _worked._ Ain't nobody to blame for that but yourself, Eddie. You should'a known... You should'a known that when a man runs in a pack, his pack is going to help out. Dammit,
Requiem, you ran with Genesis. You _should_ have known the game.
But you didn't. And you know what that means? It means you ain't as good a player of the game as you like to think. You play, but you're no master. Best heel? Nah... Biggest chump... That's all you are.
What all the people saw that night was me pinning your bleached blond ass on the mat for the three count. What all the people saw was me, the smarter man, beat you, the guy who fell for a line. In my mind, that makes me the winner. I've taken ass kickings, but I've never been
out-smarted. That makes _me_ the better man. You want me? Come and get me. _You_ are the one with something to prove. _You_ are the one who seeks revenge. _You_ are the one who lost. I don't need to prove anything against you, Eddie. Why should I come hunting you down? I'm sure the suits would love it if I came after you Eddie, because I'd put asses in the seats for them... Forget it... I don't play for the suits. I won, you lost. You come after
_me._
I'm waiting, Angel of Destruction. I'm waiting with baited breath. Scrounge up some manhood, and don't disappoint. [James walks off camera, and the shot fades.] "As an online discussion grows longer, the probability of a comparison involving Nazis or Hitler approaches one."
