[Flash] 'Superstar' Sean Holloway

2 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's one of my roleplays. Please, read it and tell me what you think. Remember, it's my first RP as this guy. [The camera slowly unfolds a beautiful scene. The sun is shining, people are running all over the green grass having fun. A few people walk by the camera, laughing about some unheard joke. The camera slowly weaves in and out of all the people enjoying the day. But not everyone is having fun. Standing back from the crowd is a man. He is gently laid back against a tree, but with a scowl on his face. 'Superstar' Sean Holloway has on a pair of blue jeans and a black tee shirt. The only thing that looks expensive is his pair of green Oakley's. He stares at the camera, motionless. Suddenly, the laughter stops. There is no noise.. only silence. 'Superstar' looks up and begins to speak.]
'SUPERSTAR' SEAN HOLLOWAY: Ya know, every time I climb a rung on that ladder of success, there's always someone waiting to take it all away, and push me back down.
['Superstar' takes off his sunglasses to reveal his piercing blue eyes. In his eyes you can see a violent storm beginning to form. He throws the sunglasses to the side, not caring where they end up. He runs a hand through his blonde hair.
He laughs to himself, as he stares around the now empty park. He looks toward the camera and begins to speak.]
'S'SH: Well, no longer am I gonna be a bystander. No longer am I gonna let someone take away my glory. I'm sick and tired of having to take what's left-over. And the 'Superstar', will no longer pushed around.
['Superstar' climbs to his feet and dusts off his pants. He stands up against the massive tree and begins to speak once again.]
'S'SH: You know, every where I go, no one is willing to give me a chance. They all say that they have no room for another wrestler trying to make it big. They all avoid me. They all turn me away. Why?
['Superstar' seems to ponder this question as he slowly breathes in and out. He turns his head toward the camera, and you can see the pain in his eyes. The pain is quickly replaced by the same cold look.]
'S'SH: Why? Maybe they really don't have room. Or maybe they're like everyone else.. just plain out _SCARED._ Well the 'Superstar' has had it up to here..
[Makes a throat slicing motion with his hand.] 'S'SH: ...with their excuses. Finally, I have found a place that is willing to give me a shot. Willing to let me show them what I've got. And that place is
International Championship Wrestling.. better known as the ICW.
[Sean Holloway pauses for a brief moment before continuing on.] 'S'SH: Well, finally someone has given me the oppurtunity to showcase my talent. And I hear some of the best wrestlers in the sport reside here. Well, I can play hardball with tha best of 'em. And if they want some of tha
'Superstar'... [Pause.] 'S'SH: .. they can come and get it! Now, I know I don't have as much experience as some of these other guys... but what I lack in experience, I make up for it... in _SKILL!_
['Superstar' Sean Holloway begins to walk away from the tree. The camera man has to walk briskly to keep up with the newest edition to the ICW. Suddenly,
Holloway spins around and stops. The camera man stops right behind him.
Holloway stares into the camera, a scowl covering his face.] 'S'SH: Wrestlers of the ICW.. remember... no longer will I be pushed around. The time has come for the 'Superstar' to make his stand. Wrestlers of the ICW, fans all around the world... the 'Superstar' has made his appearance... and ya know what...
[A smile crosses Holloway's face.] 'S'SH: ... _THERE AIN'T NOTHIN' YA CAN DO ABOUT IT!_... So get ready. ['Superstar' Sean Holloway spins on his heel and walks away with a casual strut. He puts his sunglasses back over his eyes and disappears into the horizon.]
