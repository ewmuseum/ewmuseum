{Free Agent}

6 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**This is not a roleplay.  It is simply an advertisment for my free agent.**   {Scene opens to a old , worn down training facilty  in the heart of New Mexico.  There are quiet a few young guys and a few older ones in there training intensively. Working on the basics of their game.  A few in the ring sparring , a couple more over at the weights trying to overcome the pain in order to just squeeze in those last few reps.  In the front door steps a young , slender male dressed in a black t-shirt with a gold figaro link chain around his neck , a pair of blue jeans and a pair of white and black fila's.  As he walks in the door he slaps the hand of an old mexican man sitting the front counter and they speak briefly but it is unknown what they say. With a disheartened look on his face the young man walks over to an office as the cameras follow and zoom in on the conversation...}
  Tony Garza: Yo poppa! What is this I hear about Juan walking away from the sport! Is something the matter within our familia!?!   Jesus Garza: No Tony , everything is fine I don't know the exact reasons that your cousin chose to leave wrestling. It is his life and he's a Garza , so you know he will be just fine no matter what he chooses to do.
  {The young Garza turns away towards the camera and continues to speak.}   Tony: Man this is unbelievable!  I cannot believe Juan has retired! I looked up to him from a distance , always wanting and wishing that someday I could be just like him. To come up like he did , to overcome the obstacles and become who he is ... much love.
Funny thing is that he probably doesn't even remember me. Last time we spoke we were just a couple of little guys wrestling around at uncle Eddie's house ... Man the years have flown by!
  But enough with the reminiscing , that is the past and now it's time to pave my future!  With Juan gone , wrestling is lacking that Garza spirit!  They say I'm not ready , but I think I am!  I'm tired of wrestling at high schools and arenas so small theres only one toliet!  It's time for Tony Garza to make his mark on the sport just like
Juan did! I know it won't be easy and not to many 21 year olds make it into the spotlight but thats a chance I just got to take!  If I stick around this place much longer I will end up like poppy , sitting around dreaming of what could have been and that just ain't my style! 
  It's time for me to step out on my own ... So to any and all federations looking to sign a fresh new face , look no further becuase Tony Garza has arrived!!
  Tony Garza [detail withheld]
