Rick Destiny--Free Agent

7 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
````{Begin Audio/Video Transmission}```` [The scene opens with Rick Destiny sitting his home in his home-town of San Antonio, Texas. He is watching a television show, but changes the channel, and since he can't find anything on the T.V. he decides to go outside for some fresh air. He gets up, signals for the camera man, who has been sitting there for almost 3 hours waiting for an interview, to follow, they walk to his front door,
Destiny opens it, as they both walk out as the camera man closes it] [As they walk outside, they begin to walk down the street,, they see children playing out in their yards, and having fun. They both turn around, making their way back to Rick's home, the camera man flicks on the record button as Rick Destiny begins to speak]
Rick Destiny: "I remember those days as a child, always having fun and always enjoying whatever it was I thought of doing. But those days are past now, the only amusement I get now a days is from a good fight. Speaking of a fight, I am already assigned my first victim on Monday, January 7th and while that might be some time away, it gives myself and my opposition some time to think about our future careers, my opposition being Rob Stryker. I don't think this guy knows much about me, so I will tell you a little right now..."
[He clears his throat and continues to talk] Rick Destiny: "I was raised right here in this very same city, I had very strict parents, most of the time they would yell, I still can't remember one time they were nice, maybe it is just blocked out because of all the bad things they did to me. It wasn't the part that I messed up a lot that made them mean,
I really never knew why, but they have always been. I know now that maybe I could have changed to the better to help them change to the better, but its too late. When I was 16, I was forced to live on my own. I knew the streets was probably where a fighter like me should stay, but it bothered me knowing my parents had no love for me. I was on the streets for about 3 years, surviving as I could, killing for a meal, and doing whatever else to get money. Until one day when I was 19, I was picked up by one of my friends, he was always very kind to me, and he knew I didn't like it on the streets. He has already been living in his own home for awhile now, so I easily said 'yes' when he gave the proposal of moving in with him. The whole time I was with him, we both loved wrestling. We watched it all the time, taped it for eachother whenever one of us was gone, and constantly talked
about it. It was a great sport." [He sits down on his front porch and continues] Rick Destiny: "My friend, his name was Chris, always said I would grow up to be a wrestler, so after about 30 times hearing him say it, I started believing it, and that's where I am today. And already I have been granted the wish of being on the first card. Rob Stryker, knowing I am no easy customer, might be a shock to him. I don't know much about this guy except the fact he is about 5 inches shorter than I am and his weight is a bit lower also. I know size does not
always matter, but in this case it does. I am one of the meanest god d--- brawlers you will ever see. Some people say I am sane, but many others say I am not. Any way, it never bothered me, because the only thing I know about myself is if a job needs done, I am the one to do it! And this Rob Stryker guy looks as if he has a task for me. And I know just what I need to do to make sure I get it done right. I have a question for you Rob, have you ever been put in a match with the most gifted mat wrestler and brawler mixed into one? Trust me, it
does not feel too good." [Rick Destiny stands up and stretches, when he is done, takes out a pack of Marlboro Red cigarettes and begins to light one up, when he has it lit, he sits back down and continues talking]
Rick Destiny: "Now, where was I? Oh yes, about my destiny with you, pardon my pun. Now, this match may not mean too much on your part, but on mine, it means the world. This is a new federation, and the first expression is always the best.
And I don't know about you but I will make this first, be the best as it can. It may seem corny to you but not to me. When you look at the rankings, expect a -1 by your name because after this match, it will +2 for me, -1 for you. It is the truth, so make sure you watch what you say from now on. See you in the ring
Rob Stryker, if not any sooner!"
[Rick Destiny begins to laugh a devilish mean laugh, as the camera slowly fades to black, the scene dies out with the hear of his laugh and it gets slower and slower until it can no longer be heard]
[The camera cuts back to the studio.] [detail withheld]
