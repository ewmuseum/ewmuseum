DJ RAN CURT HENNING FEUD IDEA/ROLEPLAY

12 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(It is Nitro and Mean Gene is in the ring) MG - Ladies and Gentlemen, boys and girls, tonight I wish to introduce to you a man who means alot to all of us, a man who has been on the receiving end of some aggravation at the hands of Curt Hennig, this is the man who is the heart and sole of Ranmania. Please welcome DJ Ran.
(The crowd pop like crazy for the Ranster) (Through the Nitro logo out comes Rikki Rackman wearing his trademark hawaiin shirt, he yells "DJ RAN IS IN THE HOUSE" the crowd pop for him, then Fyre and Storm, two Nitro girls emerge and the crowd pop for them)
MG - Come on, we have had the hors d'oerve, where is the main course (the crowd laugh) (DJ Ran, wearing a thong and his body covered in oil which glistens off the lighting, finally walks through and the crowd go crazy. A cavalcade of fireworks erupt and the Ranster yells "WOOOOO")
(Rackman, Fyre, Storm and Ran all walk down to the ring, glad handing the fans, they get a great pop)
(they get to the ring and climb in, the ranster goes up to Mean Gene) MG - Woahhhh. DJ RAN, they are hanging from the rafters in here and they have ALL come to see you.
DJ RAN - RANMANIA IS RUNNING WILD IN THE WCW (Rackman nods and the Nitro girls wiggles their puppies) (the crowd pop) MG - so you have had a bit of trouble with Curt Henning (DJ Ran picks up a cowboy hat and puts it on, the fans laugh at this hilarious put down to hennig)
DJ RAN - Henning, I HATE your Country and Western Crap (the crowd booo) and I think these people are not keen either (the crowd cheer the ranster). You think you are hard, you think you are clever and you think you can make a mug of the Ranster, well you can NOT
(the crowd pop and the Nitro girls rub DJ Rans finely oiled body) (DJ Ran takes the hat off and stomps on it) (The crowd laugh as Curt Hennig runs down to the ring, he slides in and walks up to DJ Ran, he takes the mic)
CH - Ran, your music sucks (DJ Ran kicks Curt Hennig in the bollocks and as he doubles up he drops an elbow on the back of his neck, the crowd pop for this, and DJ
Ran loving it hits a DDT on Hennig, he then rolls hennig up and Rikki Rackman drops down and does the count) 1 . . . . . . . 2 . . . . . . . . 3 Fyre - Ladies and Gentlemen your winner, by pinfall, DJ RANNNNN the crowd pop like crazy.
