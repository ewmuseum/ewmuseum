FROM THE HANDLER OF iNSANiTY
IN THE UNWF AND SWL (c) 19naughty8 ~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v~^v
Scene=-The camera opens up to a shot of a warehouse in the middle of Boston,
Massachusetts...people are lined up all over the street...they are there to see something...the people in line all have signs but can not be made out at this time...the camera zooms in to one of the fans...the fans see the camera and go absolutely nuts...they are all trying to get on the camera...it looks like a skateboarding party...people with mohawks...grungy clothes...the camera man pulls a fan out of the line and asks him " What is all this about? "...the fan asks his friend something...and then tells the camera man " Well brother are you the police or the bord of censorship?"...the camera man looks puzzled...he answers with " No...Why would you ask? "...the fan answers with..."
Nevermind.....this is an underground fed...people come from all over the New
England area just to watch these wrestling matches...all you need to get in is the password.....that is "...he whispers something in the ear of the cameraman...then the camera fades back out and then fades into a picture of the cameraman in the front and ready to go in...the security guard asks him for the password...he tells him " The insane one shall rise as the sanity shall set
"...the security guard lets in the camera man...when the camera man gets in he sits down in the front row seat.....the match was about to start...the crowd is erupting...as the opening music starts off...the ring announcer walks ut and gets in the ring...he begins to speak..." Are you ready for the most extreme...most hardcore...most insane 2 hours that you have ever seen in your life? "...the crowd erupts with cheers..." Well we have a great surprise awaiting for all of yous as soon as this first match comes to a conclusion...but first here is your broadcast journalist for the night...Steve
Sheraton"...Steve Sheraton walks down the aisle and the crowd is mixed with its reactions...he sits down a the table and the announcer begins to introduce the first man in the opening bout..." Ladies and Gentlemen comming out first is the man that has been seen in many other feds...he is usually described as a jobber...but he is a real man's champion...he is Brooklyn Brawler"...Brooklyn
Brawler walks down the aisle...and the crowd boos him..." And his opponent is none other than...Barry Horowitz "...the crowd cheers him on...The camera then scans to Steve Sheraton...he is gonna call the match...
Steve Sheraton=-Well folks this is gonna be a huge night in the history of...(rather not say)...let's get to the action...collar and elbow tie up...BB pushes BH off...the crowd boos as BB flexes...they lock up again...this time BH flexes and the crowd is cheering...Irish Whip by BH...huge lariet from BH...BH with a figure four...it looks like BB is gonna tap out...
Scene=-The lights go out...the crowd is going crazy...when the lights come back on 45 seconds later...Barry Horowitz and Brooklyn Brawler are both lying unconciouss and are in Straight Jacket...no one is around...the light sgo out again....and when they go back on...iNSANiTY is in the ring...the crowd is going absolutely nuts...they are screaming at the top of there lungs...the ref is in the ring...iNSANiTY points to him...then the lights go out once again...when they come back on....the ref is knocked out and the ref is also in a straight jacket...iNSANiTY goes outside the ring and walks to the back dressing room...when he comes out...he is carrying a table wrapped in barbed wire with a bag of thumbtacks...the crowd is absolutely going nuts...they are screming..." We are iNSANE...We are iNSANE "...he sets up the table on the outside of the ring...he opens the bag of thumbtacks...and pours them on the table...he grabs the ref and climbs the top rope he appears to be setting him up for a jacknife power bomb from the top...but he stops and squeezes...it is the Straight Jacket...then he throws the ref on the table...it splits and the ref is bleeding prefusely...iNSANiTY goes to the outside and gets a microphone...he begins to speak over the fallen bloody body...
iNSANiTY=-Well Steve Rodman....Gaddamnman...Hardcore...I wonder where you got your nickname?...it seems a little familiar...oh yes you had a friend named
Hardcore...and he was in 2 stables with you...who was that man?...it is on the tip of my tongue...oh yes it was me...well those days are over and I am a new man I have changed from the "suits" big selling gimmick to my own thing...I got a lot of respect for you but it is all gonna be thrown out the window when we step in the ring on Friday...you see...I have been with you for a while but I have never faced off against you....and on Friday we will...this is not gonna be for the RoD...not for the NBK...not for the SWL...or even the EWA...this is for me and you to find out who is the better man...let's see if that name is really who you are...let's see if you can carry my name in the UNWF...shall we...we are bout it are you?...
Apocalypse will set you free... he is the death that will be brought... he is the cold air on that hot day... he is iNSANiTY! Scene=-The scene fades out to a commercial as iNSANiTY leaves through the crowd after he spits on the ref...
