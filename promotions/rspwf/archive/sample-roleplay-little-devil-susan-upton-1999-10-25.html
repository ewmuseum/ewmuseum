[Sample Roleplay] "Little Devil" Susan Upton

25 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene fades in from black to a shot of "Little Devil" Susan Upton's bedroom. The room is reminiscent of a young girl's bedroom. There are posters of boy bands hanging on the walls, vintage Barbie dolls decorating the desk and dresser, and pink Hello Kitty sheets and pillow cases on the canopied bed. The camera zooms down towards the pink, carpeted floor... where a pig tailed, Scooby Doo pajama wearing
Susan Upton is sitting with her legs crossed. She giggles to herself, playfully...)
SU: "Tee hee hee! Gee willickers! I've only been in the UWF for... ummm... nearly two whole weeks in a row now and I'm already having a puddle wonderful, super fun time!"
(She rubs her hands together and rocks her body back and forth, smiling...) "I had a GREAT time playing with that silly willy, Miss Miki, at 'Meltdown' and an even GREATER time playing with the 3-Divas and the
Bod Squad at 'Saturday Night Rampage'. I know some people become rather cross with me over what I did but I sure had a neat-o time doing it. Ollie was right. Playing that recording DID lead to a new world of FUN... FUN... FUN! FUN for ALL... and FUN for SUSAN!"
(Susan sighs...) "It would've been a PERFECT two weeks of fun if it wasn't for that stupid ol' meanie... that bad, bad man... that... that... POOP!"
(She fluffs her hair...) "He dumped that icky doo doo all over my head! It got all in my pretty hair! He even called me a naughty name! He was just so RUDE to me!"
(She giggles and shrugs...) "But that's all in the past now. C'est la vie! I'm not going to let some stupid dummy's rudeness affect me when there's so much more fun to be had! No, I will not! I'm going to make a CHALLENGE!"
(She scratches her chin, humming and looking up at the ceiling...) "But who should I challenge? Hmmm. This will be my first match here so it's gotta be a good one. It has to be someone who likes to play games and have fun, too! People who don't like games are no fun at all! They STINK! Hmmm. Who should it be?"
Off-Camera Noise: "MEOW!" (Susan looks off-camera and smiles. She claps her hands excitedly...) SU: "GOOD GOLLY! IT'S OLLIE! He can tell me who to choose!" (Ollie, Susan's cute white kitten enters the camera shot. He rubs up against Susan. She picks him up and cradles him in her arms...)
"I'm so glad you came! You can help me make a very, VERY important decision!" O: "MEOW!" SU: "Yes, it's a really important decision, young man. It's about my very first match in the Universal Wrestling Federation. Who should it be against? Who do YOU think it should be against? Kieran Erotikka?
Erin McCoys?" O: "..." SU: "No? They play too rough? Alrighty then. How about one of the other members of the Bod Squad? Tara Marshall is the big boss of the women's division! Maybe she'd like to play a game with me!"
O: "..." SU: "No? Darn. You're picky. Okay. How'z about another member of the 3-Divas? Like Tara Smith or Penelope Ann Piper... umm, Piper
Laurie... errrmmm, Pippie Longstocking... whatever the heck her name is! She likes to play! Plus, she has a kitty, too! You can have a playmate to frolic with while she and I play!!"
O: "..." SU: "Not any of them, either. That doesn't suprise me. You never did like to share. Hee hee hee! Hmmm. Who should it be then? Hmmm..."
O: "MEOW! M-M-MEOW!!" SU: "Her? I didn't even think about her, Ollie. What made you think of her?" O: "Purrrrrrrrr! Meow!" SU: "Yeppers! She sure is silly! She dresses silly! She acts silly! She's such a silly goose indeed, Ollie! That Anaconda! I think she's a perfect choice for my first match! When do you think the match should happen? Ah nevermind! I'll leave that up to Commissioner
Marshall! She's the big boss man... errr... woman in the division!
She can pick the date of when it all goes down!" (Upton pets Ollie and sighs happily...) "It's like things are back to normal again, Ollie. Things are just like they were when we were in the International Elite Wrestling
Association. Don't you just LOVE it? Ahhh..." O: "Meow! MEOW?" SU: "What would make you even happier? It's a secret? Okay. Tell me. Whisper it in my ear."
(Upton leans forward, putting her ear up to Ollie's mouth. After a few seconds of "listening" to Ollie, Upton starts to talk again.)
"Oh my! Good gracious me! Oliver Wendell Pinkerton! You bad kitty! That's such a VIOLENT suggestion! I could NEVER do that to Anaconda! That'd be playing ROUGH!" (She pauses, stroking Ollie gently...) "Then again, maybe Anaconda would enjoy it. She's a big, muscular girl. Maybe she can handle rough games. She better enjoy games 'cause play time is not over. It's far from over."
O: "Meow! Purrrrr..." SU: "Tee hee! You're right, Ollie! Play time has just begun!" (Fade to black...) Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
