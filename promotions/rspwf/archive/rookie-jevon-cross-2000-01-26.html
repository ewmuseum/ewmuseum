[ROOKIE] Jevon Cross

26 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My new character, Jevon Cross, is a rookie at the moment. I'm looking to join two feds. I won't be able to start for another couple of weeks (exams), but I'd like to get in a fed sometime tomorrow.
Here is a simple little application, and a role play has been attached to the bottom, as well. I haven't role-played since ... let's see ... July, so I'm even worse than I was before.
E-mail me if you need a sucky jobber to the stars. Wrestler Name: Jevon Cross Wrestler Height: 6'4" Wrestler Weight: 249 Wrestler Age: 23 Wrestler Hometown: Los Angeles, California Wrestling Style: Power Background info: Born in Los Angeles, Jevon Cross is a rookie to professional wrestling. He was scouted by some old has-been named Lawrence Williams, and for months the two of them have been working hard to get Jevon into a league.
Attitude info: He's got a tantrum. He gets mad easily. He doesn't like repeating himself. He doesn't like not being respected or not being feared.
He's not a true face or a true heel... he's a whatever the fans think of him that night. He just goes out and, in his words "pounds all of those who doubt me."
Manager: Lawrence Williams Manager info: Lawrence himself is a former professional wrestler. He's won the gold in various federations. He won the EMWC Interplanetary championship by defeating "Lonewolf" Brody Hardin; he captured the UWF Skinnyweight championship by punishing "Playboy" Ronald Dee; but the most important title he captured was the #1 ranking in the 2000 RSPWF 200. Anyway, he retired cuz he wanted a protege. Now he's a manager. He's a loud-mouth, ebonic-talking black motherfucker who loves to piss ayebody off.
same role play (it sucks) Critique if need be ------------ [Cut to the home of former professional wrestler Lawrence Williams. As the camera fades in, we see the back of a man who is assumed to be Williams, decked-out out in a black suit and black shoes. The camera gets a shot of Williams just as he comes to the end of a conversation on his cell phone.]
LW: Yeah... I know, man.. No problem... We'll be there... Yes, you can trust me.. Alright. Peace. [Lawrence folds up the phone and puts it in his right pants pocket. Lawrence, with his back still to the camera, begins walking forward until he comes to a stop in what looks to be the living room.]
LW: Alright, we in. We gotta meet up with __PRESIDENT__ tomorrow t'finalize y'contract. It looks like you gonna be a wrestler, man.
[Lawrence steps to his right and the camera man takes this opportunity to move past him, getting a shot of the man to whom Lawrence was talking. The man is sitting on the edge of a Lay-Z-Boy, with grey sweatpants, a great XXL t-shirt, and white socks. His faces shows a sense of accomplishment.]
LW: Oh, I ain't even hear you guys come in.. Jevon, meet the camera crew.. These are the guys that are gonna be on your ass all the time. These are the guys you bitch to about them other wrestlers.
[Jevon cuts a quick glance at the camera, then back to Lawrence, who is off-camera.] JC: So it's official? I'm a professional wrestler? LW: Nah.. It ain't official yet, but it's gonna be tomorrow. [Jevon's facial expression now turns to aggitation.] LW: I know, I know-- JC: No, obviously you don't, man! That's what you said the past three times.. I'm sick of these "contractual differences", man. I don't give a damn about the money.. I want to step in the ring and do what I do best--dominate!
[The camera quickly cuts to Lawrence who, seeing the camera, rolls his eyes.] LW: Look: you might not care about those contractual differences now, but when you start wonderin how you gonna pay the rent, those differences 'come a huge factor.
[The camera cuts back to Jevon, who's about to speak, but Lawrence cuts him off. The camera turns back to
Lawrence.] LW: Look around here, J. Take a real good look at this room you sit in. [The camera does as Lawrence asks and pans the room. Shots of amateur wrestling trophies, courtesy copies of federation titles, wrestling awards, and various paintings come up. The camera is back on Williams.]
LW: You ain't gonna get this by accepting those other fast-food salaries them other promoters were handin out. That's what ol' Larry here's for.
[Back to Jevon.] JC: Firstly, I *don't* appreciate you cutting me off. Secondly, this crap ain't what's important. Pounding all of those who doubt me is what's important. I can worry about the money, cars, and women after I prove to the world that I'm capable of being the top draw. I'll worry about that after people stop doubting me. And I think you doubt me, Lawrence, because if you didn't, you would realize that you're staring at a future star.
[Cut too Williams.] LW: I know. [Jevon.] JC: No you don't. I train every day. I work on my skills every day. I bust my ass _every_ day. And even though you keep say you're going to strike that big deal *every day*, I'm still sitting here having this same goddamn conversation with you!
*Get*me*a*job*. [Jevon jumps up out of the chair and storms out of the room, slamming the front door on his way out. The camera cuts to Williams who is looking past the camera with a befuddled look on his face. Lawrence then begins yelling at Jevon as if he can hear him through the door.]
LW: What the... How the hell you gonna slam the door and it ain't your house! [mumbling] Ol' punk ass kid.
[Lawrence brings his head down and begins staring at the ground for a couple moments, almost as if he has forgot the camera is still here. After a few seconds, Lawrence, startled, pops his head up and looks at the camera. He points towards the direction Jevon stormed out in.]
LW: Ya see that? Ya see that, promoters? Ya see that, pro wrestlers? Ya see that, fans? If ya don't see it, what you just saw was a star in the making. You might not know it yet--but you will.
Trust me. You will. [Lawrence waves off the camera. Fade out.]
