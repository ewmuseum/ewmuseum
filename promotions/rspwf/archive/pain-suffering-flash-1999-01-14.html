Pain & Suffering flash

14 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[[Scene opens as a cameraman walks to a nice white house. He walks in knowing to be invited. He sees a nice kitchen. He sees someone's room looks like a woman's with a walk-in closet. He sees pictures of a family reunion and he smiles]]
[Cameraman] This is a nice place. I wonder why I don't get jobs like this? [[Then out of no where a noise that can only be described as a demonic scream and he jumps back and falls on his @$$]]
[Cameraman] Why do I keep getting jobs like this? [[The Cameraman walks down ever further into the basement where he thinks he hears the noise and suddenly is blasted with a wave of tremendous heat. He sees two men beaten, scarred, burned, and broken down and they seem to be enjoying it. He sees another man who seems to be huge behind him and in the middle of the basement is a pit of fire and middle of is a platform with a throne on it.
On the throne he zooms in to get a closer look and sees a woman dressed in leather, and has a whip in her hand. Her blone hair covers her eyes as she lifts her head and her eyes seem to pierce through the flames and to the cameraman]]
[Woman] Welcome! Welcome to my kingdom of torture! The place where few walk in. And even LESS walk out. But don't worry. I'll let you leave in one piece. [[She stands up and walks on a small heat resistant plank on top of the fire and doesn't seem to be burned at all when she crossed it.]] My name is Sonia. But you and all those other so called "wrestlers" WILL addressed me as MISTRESS
Sonia. I deserve that rank and you should, no you WILL honor me by calling me the rank I so rightfully deserved. I have came from the IWF/WoW which I so easily dominated the tag team division by crushing their little rebellions. I have conquered them all and brought ORDER to that division and brought it life once again! It was a disgrace and I brought it to GLORY! And that's what I will do to this tag team division! I shall quickly shoot up the ranks and take those belts that are rightfully mine! And to help me take those belts that so rightfully belong to me as the stars belong to the sky are the tools of two emotions, two entities, two reflections of every human soul. These beasts are my pets! They go beyond to standard of humans! They work only for me and show absolute loyality which is something not everyone has. They have men. And men questions things. They don't give absolute loyality. They betray! Lets meet our clash cage gladiators!
[[The Cameraman points to one who seems to be in consent torment. He lunges at the cameraman as if he were prey and snaps at him and trys to devour him]]
[Mistress Sonia] That little cutie is Suffering. He's every childhood nightmare, every fear, everything that causes guilt and remorse. Name your thing. Mother held him too much or not enough. Last to be picked in kickball.
Name it. It'll all go back to him. He's going to show the world his pain. His anguish! His anger in the ring. You'll know what he means by suffering. You'll come in the ring with the use of your legs but leave with the use of a stretcher. He'll pull and turn your body in so many ways to make you give in to his suffering. He's the one half of my order!
[[The cameraman turns to the other chained man who seems to be staring at him all over as if he's waiting for the perfect time to pounce. He seems taller than the last one and a bit more powerful.]]
[Mistress Sonia] That's PAIN! The thing you feel when you have something not right happen to you. [[She walks to Pain and rubs his face]] The lover of scars. The barer or wounds. The generous giver of the ultimate gift. His joy is sharing the world with the gift of Pain. He believes that all should embrace the gift and not look for doctors to get rid of it. He loves the sensation it brings. The tingling feeling it gives at it enters your body and tries to give you satisfaction! How it goes through your entire body and lets it all feel it's sensation. Oh to him it's a sexual experence to break a bone and let it heal without a cast. He is the other half of my order I shall bring to this tag team division!
[[Then he turns into the dark man. Who seems to be at least 6'9" and over 300 pounds. He looks at Mistress Sonia and peers at her]]
[Mistress Sonia] That my friend is my bodyguard Despair. The feeder of death. The claimer of lost souls. The destroyer of LIVES. He won't be wrestling but doing a more important job. Watching my welfare! While Pain & Suffering are destroying the innocent unfortuates in the ring he'll be making sure that I remain protected. Besides, what's a team without a Mistress. He won't interfere with the match. I forbid it. So you won't worry your little heads of dears.
You'll only have to worry about two instead of three. [[The cameraman looks back at Sonia]] [Mistress Sonia] You have heard my roar and you soon shall feel my bite. Single wrestlers are the lucky ones. They won't feel my wrath. They won't be looking over their shoulder for a shadow. They will be safe. But you tag teams. Fate as given you a terrible blow. For I have dominated the IWF/WoW tag team divison and I shall dominate this one as well. Put your ass in line it belongs to me.
