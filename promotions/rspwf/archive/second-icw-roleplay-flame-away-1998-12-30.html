Second ICW roleplay, flame away.....

30 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens outside "O'Malley's" in beautiful downtown Tampa Bay, in fact the bar and grill, is just down the street of The Ice Palace, site of this weeks _ICW ROCKIN' DA WORLD TOUR_. The place seems to be pretty crowded as the camera makes it's way towards it's target, James Van
Steele....The camera men followed JVS as he left the Ice Palace, only 30 minutes after his non-televised match against the now walking-with-a-limp
Inferno. One of the camera crew picks up JVS sitting off to the leftside of the bar...to both his left and right he has the attention of two very attractive women. JVS is decked out in a pair of brown, loose fit cargo shorts, a Hawaiian red and yellow shirt that really makes him stick out, and a white t-shirt that says "ICW WINTER - WARFARE 99" on it. He shells out yet another $20 bucks to the bartender, the two ladies get up and go to the bathroom (I don't understand it either), JVS notices the camera....]
"Well aloha ICW! So, what you keeping track of me or something? Yeah, I left early, but I was done working for the night and besides, the back didn't supply us with any beer!! What kind of animals do you think we are?....Don't answer."
"So, you guys see my match? Of course, you did who am I kidding, It was one of the technical matches ever to go down in ICW history! I bet they taped and are going to run that for the main event, instead of.....of.....whatever they got planned for the big main event! Okay, so they might not....but it would draw a better rating, that's for sure. I must admit, that JVS wasn't 100% on top of his game tonight, however, I haven't wrestled in about two moths, so any ring rust is now gone. And that is the ONLY reason why Inferno had a snowballs chance in hell in getting a move on me!! Next time, it won't be as pretty, I promise you that!"
[The bartender comes back with the three drinks that he order. Two of them, just plain old beer. The third one, on the other hand, is a plastic coconut with a tiny umbrella sticking out of it and a small plastic pirates sword stuck through two cherries. The bartender, attempts to hand JVS his change, a few nickles and dimes, but JVS waves his hand and mouths, "keep the change." JVS smiles as he turns back towards the camera crew.]
"So, where was I, oh yeah....my opponent...what's his face....uh, that guy, you know the one, uhh.....yeah, Mr. Shawn Foster....the one who calls
_himself_ the Deadly Method. First off, where the hell do you find a name like that? And second What the hell is it supposed to mean, the Deadly
Method? Is it like the rhythm method? Well, since it didn't work for you father, I doubt that it will even come close to working for you! So, Shawn
Foster this is your first ever match here in the ICW, well here is what good old JVS is going to do for you; I am going to give you a fighting chance, no not really, just a few minutes in the first few moments of the match to show your stuff, and to make you feel good about yourself, happy holidays from me to you."
"Actually, what I really want to touch up here this whole ICW going under different owners and such, the guy currently in charge Jax Jordan, well....you gotta turn the camera off for this, since it is off the record....
[The camera points down towards the ground, you can see the flip-flops of James Van Steele, and the camera guy claims to JVS that the camera is off and this next part of the conversation is truly off the record and on the
QT] "It's off now...right? Okay good. Well, from what I have heard that this Jax Jordan guy is a real asshole! I am not talking about your average run of the mill asshole like myself, but a fully blown, grand high asshole!! I sure hopes Pillow kicks his ass at Winter Warfare....I may not be the nicest guy, but to see a guy like that running the fed that I bust my ass for, it just breaks my heart. That's why I left early, not because I wanted to pick up on the hot women fast, because these bar skanks hang off any guys jock, I left as a sign of protest, you know, I am on Pillow's side all the way....Okay now you can turn that back on...."
[The camera picks up again to the face of James Van Steele, he begins to drink from his plastic coconut. The two women return from the bathroom, finally, and they sit down next to him and begin to drink their beers.
They finish about half of their beers, each and slam them down on the bar's wood surface, the three then proceed to get up and make their ways through the crowd to the taxi that is waiting outside...the bartender walks over to the camera man and taps him on his shoulder..]
Bartender: "You know that guy?" Camera Guy: "Yeah" Bartender: "Good, then you can pay off his $50 bar tab!!"
