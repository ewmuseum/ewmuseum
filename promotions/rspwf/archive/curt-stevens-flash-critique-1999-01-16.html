Curt Stevens Flash: Critique

16 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in.] [The screen opens up at the SSW Newsroom. The place is buzzing with excitement, as a new signing has only just been announced to the fans and reporters in attendance. Pictures flash off the large "SSW" banner which is hanging at the back of the room. There is a large podium at the front of the room which is currently empty. Suddenly though, a man, dressed in a buisness suit enters the room and then he walks briskly to the podium. Evidently an SSW official, the man quickly gets the attendance off the crowd, and then addresses them, speaking into the microphone which sits on top of the podium.]
OFF : "Ladies, and gentlemen. The SSW's latest signing has just arrived and will now come out to answer your questions. He is currently preparing to come out. He is, the LMWA's World Heavyweight champ and has competed against a lot of top names in his career. He is
'Wonderful' Curt Stevens!"
[Polite applause as the official now motions towards the door, where
Curt Stevens now steps through. The official now leaves, giving SSW's huge signing in the spotlight. Stevens is dressed in a pair of smart black jeans, and a black jacket. He seems to be around 6'2 and seems to weigh around 225 lbs. The Springfield, Massachuttes native then brushes aside his golden blonde hair as he approches the podium. He then turns his back to the crowd, and raises his arms in the air. On the back off his jacket, in bold, white letters are the words "Simply
Wonderful." Stevens then turns back around, and addresses the crowd.]
WCS : "So... after a long, _hard_ search, the SunShine Wrestling _finally_ found their impact player. The man they _know_ is the undoubted _ICON_ of the wrestling world. The man who they know will take the SSW from being a 'minor' federation to being the one that _everyone_ wants to be a part of.
And that man is... simply wonderful!" [Stevens then takes a step backwards and strokes his freshly shaven chin. He then moves back to the podium and once again speaks.]
WCS : "I got a lot of offers from a _lot_ of top wrestling federations. They _all_ wanted to sign the most _wonderful_ superstar in the wrestling world. But my agent told me that the SSW was
_the_ place to be, and so here I am. There are a lot of names I want to get my hands on in this federation. A lot of familiar faces. For one, there is Antwan
Carter. I remember this chump from the Ohio Wrestling
Federation, and I've always wanted to stick my size eleven boots down his throat. And then there is Jack Vars, the man of one thousand wrestling federation. Vars, me and you have never met, but I've heard a lot about you, and you can guarentee that we _will_ meet here in SunShine Wrestling, and when we do, you will find out _just_ how wonderful I _really_ am.
For the guys like Tyrone Parker, Sean Holloway, Juan Garza,
Dusty Love, Fang, Scott Jamison and the rest of the so-called
_big shots_, your time in the sun is now up, because the spot- light is about to be taken over by...
SIMPLY WONDERFUL!" [Pause] WCS : "Try as you might, the SSW _losers_ aren't going to be able to defeat me. So, unite, do whatever you want, but your soon gonna realize just how bad you really are...
And how WONDERFUL _I_ really am!" [Fade out.]
