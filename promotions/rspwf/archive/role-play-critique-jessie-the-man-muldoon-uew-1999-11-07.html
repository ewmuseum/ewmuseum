There's a Creed <<SCENE: Fades in as a voiceover is heard..>> [VO]: "There comes a time in ever mans life where he doesn't know where ta go, what ta do, or who ta trust. There comes a time in a man's life where he is left in a continuous state of confusio'. Where does this man go? Who does this man turn ta? Who can he trust?"
<<The once faded black screen evolves into a blurred vision. A soul figurine can be made out threw all the static. The background, unknown at this time, but the voice picks up again.>>
[VO]: "It's times like this that cause people ta self Distruct and sit on the verge of life and death. Suicide plagues the mind and friends are hard ta come by. This man looks all aroun' for the easiest way outta' it or somethin' ta turn to for any type of answer whether it's right or wron'. Whether that answer is sex, alcohol, drugs, or anythin' else, chances are, this man makes a mistake that will affect the rest of his life."
<<The screen's picture slowly becomes clearer. An unknown man stands leaning over a bridge that is raised over an highway of automobiles. He stands with his arms folded over the railing and his head draped over looking down. Several cars zoom by him making it clear that he is on a public bridge. There is about four feet of sidewalk separating the street and the railing so he is safe at this time.>>
[VO]: "It's times like this that someone, some person, must step threw the shadows of selfishness ta lend a hand ta someone in need. This man needs a savior, this man needs help, everythin' inside of me says call out ta the man and show him ya' way. So with this, I take that step, I lunge forward, I attempt ta safe a man who may or may not want my help."
<<Jessie "The Man" Muldoon steps inta the picture dressed in a tight black tank top that is tucked inta a pair of bright yellow khaki pants.
His short cropped, brown hair floats around on the top of his head as the brisk wind runs threw it. His back his turned as he lays his hand on
the man dressed in rags who hangs his body over the railing not knowing whether or not he wants to live or die.]
[VO]: "Ya must call out ta the man in a manner that will not frighten him or scare him away. The last thing ya want ta happen is for the man ta run or flee from ya. Ya must be careful, very careful."
<<Jessie now has his arm around the man's shoulders and they slowly, very slowly begin to walk away from the railing, but only a few steps at
first. Finally, they pull away from the railing completely and begin to walk along side the city street.>>
[VO]: "Sometimes we get lucky. Sometimes things come out the way we planned. But their are other times when we are less fortunate and they don't work out the way we saw them in our fictional minds. Life in general is a lot like wrestling. It has it's up and downs and can be very emotional, very..."
<<The camera pans away from the scene with Jessie helping the lost soul of a man, it cuts to a typical day with the sun shining down and the weather pretty calm. Several pearly white clouds float above in the light blue skies. As Jessie "The Man" Muldoon stands below at a parkside
bench. He watches the dozens of kids running around playing football and little ones playing on the slides with their mothers. Jessie then turns the corner of the street.. Jessie then looks up and then begins ta speak..>>
[MULDOON]: "When life's problems seem as if they are tumblin' on top of ya.. When it seems as if all hope is lost and the world is no more ya friend... Then take a long hard look at yaself'.. 'Cause ya'll find out that ya are doin' somethin' wron'."
<<Jessie Muldoon looks up..>> [MULDOON]: "There's not a day that goes by when I don't think abou' what has happened ta me.. Yet when I look further, I see a vision.. A vision which one day shall come true.. Jessie Muldoon will be on top of the world, yet for now, I have ta work harder, persevere further and not stop goin', even if there are trouble' ahead.. For ya are, what ya are.
God made me this way, I didn't ask ta be what I am.. And I have ta say,
I'm proud of what I do.. Some might not like my ways of handlinn'
business affairs, yet whos'e gonna' be the one ta tell me otherwise?" <<Jessie Muldoon ponders, he then continues to walk. Jessie then stops at the door of a conglomerate building.. Just like the size of the
Chicago Statedome, yet a little smaller in bredth.. Jessie Muldoon then looks up and then sees the sign..>>
[MULDOON]: "Taday' the day that I have ta put my thinkin' ta the test,
'cause unless I can understan' what a Creed is, then I ain't got no chance in beatin' one.. So this leads me ta the local library..
Hopefully here there'll be able to show me what a Creed is? <<Jessie Muldoon then opens the door, Jessie then walks in. A flower,
Jessie loves flowers.. A pink carnation is standing in a vase on the window sill.. Jessie then motions over ta look at it..>>
Flower.. The intricat' parts which go inta makin' up a Flower, they kinda' remin' me of the parts which make up my life.. For everythin'
will soon be comin' up roses.." <<Jessie Muldoon then walks over to the Recepionists desk.. A loud :: BUUUUZZZZZZZZZ:: Jessie Muldoon then steps foreward..>> [RECEPTIONIST]: "Next." <<Jessie Muldoon then pulls up a chair, sits down and crosses his legs, gets comfterable and then begins ta speak..>>
[MULDOON]: "Excuse me Mam, but I'm not used ta dealin' with women, so is there any chance ya could find me a man?"
<<The lady looks dumbstruck..>> [RECEPTIONIST]: "Ya section Mister is located at Aisle 4 Shelf 3. I belive that's where ya find yaself the books on men. Under the section of Gay Literature."
[MULDOON]: "Exuse me Mam, but I didn't ask for ya ta be so personal, all
I asked.. Ah forget it.. But could ya tell me where ta find a Creed?"
<<The lady receptionist then looks at her computer screen, she then taps away at a few keys.. TAP.. TAP.. TAP.. You get the idea?? The lady then sees a record and smiles..>>
[RECEPTIONIST]: "The Word Creed can be be found in the dictionary, and they are located in Aisle 2 Section: Literature.. Now if that'll be all,
I have a few things ta do."
<<Jessie Muldoon then looks angry..>> [MULDOON]: "This is meant ta be a library.. Not a school.. Ya are meant ta help me.. Why is it I just don't seem ta get on with the opposite sex? Ah, women.. They make me mad.."
<<Jessie Muldoon then uncrosses his legs and then goes over to the
Literature section.. Jessie then pulls out a Dictionary and then looks up the Word Creed.. Flicks a few pages and then finds it..>>
[MULDOON]: "Now there it is.. A Creed.. Defined under the deritive of
Faith. So Creed is a Faith? Oh wait.. There's more.. A Creed can also be a Doctrine.. A belief.. An opinion.. Damn, I never knew there could be so many derivation' of one word.<Jessie then ponders..>> Yet which
Creed am I searchin' for?
<<A sudden flick of the page and then a smirk..>> I've got it.. A Creed is somethin' ta do with religion.. Yet damn, I ain't allowed in a church as they don't think ta high of my gender. Yet ta me this ain't about gender, sex or race, this is abou' equality.. And
Creed, as I don't have a belief in a Supreme bein', and I certainly don't value ya opinion, all I can come up with is that a Creed is a document.."
<<Jessie Muldoon then closes the dictionary..>> [MULDOON]: "A document, now could that be a book, a newpaper, a letter or even a memmo.. No this kind of documen' is more importan'.. This is the kind that when ya seal it, ya have ta seal it real tight, just like the nail which shall be sealed real tight in a coffin.. Your Coffin..
Yet Creed, I see ya now as bein' and havin' somethin' that ya never used ta have.. I see ya have emotions.. Yet Creed, where has an emotio' ever gotten' ya.. Does an emotion allow ya ta be yaself? Does an emotion allow ya to get angry or weep like a new born baby? The answer is I have no clue..
But what I do know is that I shall not be takin' ya lightly. Creed, some might say that ya have returned with an idea of where ya are headin', but I'll be damned if I let ya peel me open on ya way ta the top.. For
Creed, now that I unserstan' ya.. I know where ya are comin' from.. I have seen ya I action and now Creed, take it from a man who knows, ya can only get by if ya want ta.. And Creed do ya want ta?"
<<Jessie then smirks..>> [MULDOON]: "As Creed, I want nothin' more then ta get my reward.. A reward which Dysfunctio' shall be proud ta have.. And a Reward which shall lead Dysfunctio' inta the New Millennium.. And thus Creed, I have the plan, yet do ya know what my plan is? Does anybody know what my plan is? The answer is NO.. All, I say is that if ya bitch tries ta get involved then how about a nice presen' courtesy of Dysfunctio'.. A Trip ta the local hospital.. And I don't care if she's just a girl.. 'Cause ta me, both of ya are bitches and this bitch here has a boyfriend.."
<<Jessie Muldoon then looks up at the cameras..>> [MULDOON]: "And as I prepare ta lie ya down ta the mat, make no bones about it.. Jason Keening, sooner, rather then later, I shall get my title match.. The Crusade shall not triumph till the war is over and why 'cause I'm simply the man known as Jessie Muldoon.."
<<Jessie Muldoon then just smiles a cheeky smile, then winks at the cameras.. They then fade out, Jessie Muldoon then just leaves the library.. The wind blows against the door and then all is no more..>>
