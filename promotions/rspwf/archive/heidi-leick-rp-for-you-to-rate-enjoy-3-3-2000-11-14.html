Heidi Leick RP for you to rate/enjoy (3/3)

14 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Heidi Leick sits on a darkend staircase... she's got her leather jacket on, jeans, combat boots & a black T Shirt, the words cannot be made out from this angle....
Her hair hangs down over the left side of her face... You can barely see the black eye she suffered at the hands of Bloody Mary &
Zaranna.... & The other one she inflicted on herself.
The moon is shining brightly, illuminating the area in a eerie black and white luminescence.. and The Song " Fairies Wear Boots" By Black Sabbath plays in the background ]
Heidi Leick: Heroes... GDWA.... I asked for your heroes, your Old School Heroes, and who do I get.. a washed up version of a wanna be me in Bloody
Mary... and everyone and their mothers trying to get a piece of the action..
[ Heidi looks genuinely disturbed.. pissed even ] Mary, you and your dyke girl Ana Conda can be mad at me all you want.. Fact is I won, you lost. [ sticks out her tongue quickly ] Do you think ~I~ really have time to ~care~ what you feel? [ shakes her head in disbelief, like Mary really wants the answer ] And Zaranna, May Order... if you two really wanted to be down with the action,
I would have accomodated you from the start..
All either of you has beens had to do was sign your name on the paper, and you fools know it.
After all, I did request an old school legend.. And you two are about as ~old~ as they come here, and apparently, also what they consider a GDWA Legend, for some odd reason.
[sighs ] Only The Great Goddess knows why... cause we sure don't. But I guess there's good reasons why you two "legends" didn't get in that ring now.. Isn't there? [ Heidi grins psychotically ] Double O.. as good as you are.. or were.. I know why you didn't.. you have a great excuse.. and of course, it was provided by none other than me..
[ smiles ] Hey, May.. that neck must still be hurting pretty bad.. huh? [ smiles evilly and pats her neck] And Z..., or should I call you ZERO.. that's What Z means after all.. ZERO THE HERO I'll give ya this.. you were good.. but that was once upon a time...in a galaxy far, far away...
But see ZERO.. that time has past... and your age is showing girl... time to put you out to pasture with the rest of the old cows... or should I say
~sheep~.... But of course, you already know that...but refuse to believe it. And that's a good excuse for you to avoid the contract, but still try and steal
~my~ spotlight..
BITCH. [ Heidi looks at the camera with contempt ] Speaking of Age.. [ Heidi smiles a cocked smile ] Being ~old~... [ laughs ] Brings me to My next Victim. Amanda Reese. There's Three things you've been wondering out loud to us all Amanda.. I've heard it.. We've all heard it.. Said in the back, said by you, repeated in whispers in the locker room by others.. And Amanda.. Listen up... For I'm here, to give you the answers you so desperately search for.. [ Heidi raises her right hand , palm facing her, and sticks up her pinky ] One... You wonder out loud if this sport has ~passed~ ~you~ ~by~... [ She raises her ring finger, now two fingers are sticking up ] Two... You wonder out loud if you can hang with this ~new~ generation... [ She raises her middle finger so that three fingers are up now ] Three... You wonder if you've got ~anything~ left to ~give~... [ She looks at the hand, with the three fingers raised and she smiles a genuine smile, almost reminiscing about what exactly that gesture means, or meant to certain individuals... then looks back at the camera ]
The Answer, Amanda... to your first wonder... is Yes.. This Sport has left you on the side of the road like fresh roadkill, waiting to be scraped off, rot away, or picked away at and eaten by vultures looking to make a name for themselves...
The Desperation washes over you like waves.. [ she looks at the camera, expressionless.. ] And your confidence is fading, as are your skills.... Just picture your fading skills and confidence... as a childs sand castle on the beach... and all you can do is watch, as the waves wash it away...
Yes, that wave, washing away the sand castle.. is just a symbol of things to come Amanda...
[ Heidi starts to sing in an eerie melody ] Away, away.. Fade away skills , hopes and dreams... Amanda Reese,... isn't all that she seems... She's lost her desire... She's lost her faith... Her dreams have died... And her body constantly aches... And next week, ... or in a few days Her body, desires, and dreams... Will ~all~ be ~laid~ to ~waste~. [ Heidi giggles and then looks down at the ground ] The Answer, Amanda... to your second wonder is No.. [ She looks back up. determined ] Amanda.. You are right.. You can't hang with us.. The NEW Generation of GDWA Women, kicking ass and not wondering if we've got what it takes.. cause we KNOW we lead the way...
Amanda... We're the young lionesses, taking the pack over... Unlike You Amanda... or should I say.. Follower... [ Heidi pauses, and smiles evilly , with a cheshire cat grin] You were never a hero... to anyone but yourself. And from your recent words.. you've never even had that much faith in yourself
Such a shame... such a waste..
Amanda .. you want to know the real sticker? What makes it worse? You were never a Hero.. true.. sad but true. And you were never one that the Heroes had to worry about either. You were always somewhere in the middle, following people.. riding that wave.. Amanda. Simply said.. Your time has past. The Answer Amanda, to your third wonder , is also ~Yes~... [ smiles evilly ] You've got something left to give.. But only one thing... Your life.. [ Heidi tilts her head to her right side.. that Three Fingered Gesture placed on her Left Cheek ]
Yes, You're life.. it's in my hands Amanda,... And you've got me.. 1 on 1... I'm the goddess who will determine your fate. I'm a Hungry Lioness... making a stand for the young.. Willing to take your old has been ass... snap it up , and help you end it all.. In a gorgeous orgasm of violence and bloodshed. And I can start killing off ~all~ the old timers like you, who enter my ring,..my domain, my lair..
With your heads hung low, and hearts heavy.. wallowing in self doubt.... in self pity. Yes.. Yes Amanda, You've got me.. The Queen of Extreme, Hardcore Bitch, Suidical Legend & Ultraviolent Icon,
Heidi Leick... In Round One, In the tournament for the GDWA Heavyweight Title... The title... [ laughs manically ] ~That~ ~should~ have been ~given~ to ~me~ from the ~start~. [ Looks disturbed & starts rocking her body back and forth ] Amanda.. I'm not a bad person.. No.. in fact.. I'll give you a chance.. I , like any other ~deity~..[ giggles ] can show mercy... Because , after all.. [ She looks around to people and things that aren't there
] Every ~good~ ~leader~ needs followers.. Don't they? [ She looks at the sky in anger ] ANSWER ME DAMNIT!! [ She looks back down,closes her eyes, and an insane smile starts to form on her lips ] Yes.. that's the answer,.. thank you... [ She looks back at the camera.. apparently sated ] Amanda.. I'll let you can follow me.. You can have the honor.. of being my minion, & you can obey my every desire and whim.. and I will make sure you live to see another day...
Yes, Amanda.. I will save you all the pain, heartache and humiliation... of defeat, and certain death and raise you up to underling status.. as my loyal follower..
All you have to do.. Amanda... To prove your loyalty, is... Just ~forfeit~ the match. Hey... [ she looks dead serious ] It's No Big deal.. ~You~ were going to ~lose~ ~anyway~. [ blinks her eyes in a very long blink ] Or.. [ giggles & smiles ] Yes..Amanda,...there IS an alternative here.... You can be Made famous as another woman Heidi Leick put out of wrestling, forever... Hurt, Embarrased, Humiliated... and really have your meaningless career mean ~something~ after all.
You can be passed by, carted out on a stretcher, after I render your carcass lifeless in the ring,...
Left to rot on the road like that Roadkill... Left Alone to ponder what could have been if you actually made an honest effort to push yourself to your limits, and beyond, to the point of no return..
Washed away by the wave of the new GDWA generation led by Me.. Amanda.. [ laughs ] I know what your expecting me to say.. But, I'd be lying if I said I could feel your pain, Fact is.. I can't.. I'm simply not that much of a loser. [ She looks at the hand again, with the three fingers raised and she smiles a genuine smile, reminiscing again.... then looks back at the camera.. and takes her jacket off, reveaing a T Shirt that says ' SYNDICATE' - "We're in Control
", raises her hand with the Three Fingerd Gesture and walks away ]
Wonder Who's running " THE SHOW" now? Hmm? [ laughs evilly as she gets up, grabs her jacket, slings it over her shoulder, and walks away into the darkness]
