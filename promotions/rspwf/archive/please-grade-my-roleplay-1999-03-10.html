Please Grade my RolePlay

10 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey I just want to know what you guys think about my Role Plays. My Charachter is a Goth Manson Loving hardcore Guy. Feel free to ad comments and That kinda think, I want to know how to Improve
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [ The Scene opens in front of what looks like an Abandoned warehouse. There is a large black man standing out side Next to the Door. We hear muffled sounds coming from the building .Jim Boss walks up to the Door and Tries to open it.
The Large Black man Steps in front of the Door] Large Black Man: Can I help you? JB: Uhh…. Yea I'm trying to Find Captain Deathwish, Ya know were I can Find him? Large Black Man: Should he Be Expecting you? JB: Yea..he told me to Meet him here. This is 643 E. Waxion Street. Large Black Man: Yes it is. [ The man reaches up and Touches his hand to his ear.] I just got a message on the radio… you may come in. Up the Stairs on you left first 3rd door on your Left.
[ The man Opens up the door. We are Treated to a barrage of Light and sound. A huge mass of People are Writhing and moving around on the dance floor. On the
Back wall is a DJ on a Platform about 12 feet off the Floor. A Huge rack of lights is Suspended above him, casting out shades and colors of all kinds. On both sides of the DJ Platform are 2 huge Speakers, Both Pumping out the Monster
Sounds of Rob Zombie, Korn, NIN and Marilyn Manson. The People on the Dance
Floor a covered in a piercings , tattoos and other individualistic markings.
Jim Boss Turns to his left and Proceeds up the Staircase . He Passes the First door Marked "Virgins" He hesitates and thinks about going in the Door but then he comes to his senses and walks past another Door marked "Queers" He quickly walks past that door and goes to the next one. This Door is Unmarked, Next to the Door is a Huge Mirror Reflecting the Chaos on the Dance Floor and a Balcony
. He Opens the Door. Sitting inside is Captain Deathwish. He is Lounging in a
Large high backed chair with his feet propped up on the Glass Coffee table covered in Fangora and Ink and Body magazines, Empty cans of beer and other
Assorted junk. The Mirror outside is Really a Window. Deathwish is Wearing a
Tight Ruff Ryders T Shirt Black Jeans and Boots. The Tips of his hair are Dyed
Red. One eye is Completely Blood shot, The other looking like the eye of a cat.
His Finger nails are each painted Black. On His Wrists are Large black metal studded gauntlets. Around His neck is a Spiked Dog Collar.]
DW: Well I'm glad you found us. JB: Well it took forever, What kinda Weirdo directions were those? DW: Hey! At leas you Got here! So do you like my Little Party? JB: Uhh.. Yea its ok I guess, But Captain I was wondering how you felt after what Stone Cold Ki said yesterday.
DW: Well he thinks that Because of What I said I'm gonna Wuss Out on his Challenge. Well that Ain't gonna happen. Stone Cold Ki. Tonight! Me and YOU! I will Take back MY title!
JB: That's wonderful that you have a Positive attitude but Stone Cold Ki is the
Champ, Don't you think that You Might have some Trouble, And Plus he has the
Entire WestSide Connection Backing him.
DW: Yes I quite aware of all THAT! THANK YOU JIM….I DIDDN"T KNOW! JB: You don't have to get Upset, I was just stating the facts. DW: I'm NOT UPSET! IF I WAS UPSET I WOULD BE YELLING!! JB: Ok ok… Just settle Down…. [Deathwish Takes a Few Quick Breaths and calms down] DW: Ok Jim, You know that I have Beaten SCK before and I can do it again. I will ravage his Twiggy Little body Drop you on your head and Walk Step by Step up that Ladder and as You look up with blood blurring you Vision you will see
ME! Captain Deathwish Reach up and Grasp the Title that is Rightfully mine,
There is NOTHING YOU CAN DO ABOUT IT! And about the Westside Connection, It's
Quality not Quantity boys. You might have the Numbers but the Ruff Ryders have the Skill the Prowess the Premiere Wrestling Ability. And oh By the Way Gator,
You can Start a New Stable but you will never be what the Ruff Ryders are and
Will be.
JB: Thank you for that Insight Deathwish, Now How do you Feel about Facing
Compton again for either your or his Title.
DW: Well I feel VERY CONFIDENT that I can turn that Cocky Piece of Trash into a
Greasy little Spot on the mat. But that's My Opinion……. Lets get some random
"Un-biased" opinions. [Deathwish Gets up and Walks out the Door and turns out and walks onto the
Balcony. Deathwish Signals to the DJ. The DJ Turns down "Freak on a Leash" by
KoRn. All the people on the Floor turn and Look at Deathwish wondering what he's gonna say.]
DW: Hello my Goth Contingent of Followers! Party Goers: HELLO DEATHWISH! DW: Now my friend here [he points to Jim] Is wondering how I will fair against
John Compton. What do YOU Fine People think?
[One man in the crowd raises his hands, He is missing little snippets of hair all over his head and has a large metal stud going through his nose. Deathwish
Recognizes him and points to him]
DW: Yes, You there! Nose Guy: Hey that Lousy Piece of Trash has Nuttin on you Deathwish, Like. He
Sux man, and When you and Him get into the Ring I know that your gonna beat the
Living shit outta him!
[The Entire Crowd Roars with Approval] DW: Well do you All Feel this way? Voice in Crowd: NO! [Deathwish looks around in a Frenzy, Looking for the Voice in the Crowd] Voice: Jason Compton is the Most elite Man in Wrestling TODAY DW: Show Your Self Coward! [ A man Steps forward, The Crowd backs away from him, Creating a ring of Nothing around him. He is Wearing a pair of Tan Slacks and a Polo shirt. The
Man is VERY out of place]
DW: How did You get in here?! And How DARE YOU DRESS LIKE THAT! THIS IS A
FORMAL OCCASION! And why do you Sir have the Gaul to come to MY party and praise Jason Compton?
JB: Well what are you going to do about it Deathwish? [Deathwish Turns and looks at Jim with a Devious Smile. The Captain Jumps over the Balcony Landing jut feet from the Man. Deathwish Rises from his Crouched
Position and Looks at the man Square in the Eye]
DW: You sir are Not welcome Here! [He pokes the man in the Chest] And we ALL
Know what happens to Uninvited Guest DON'T WE! [He Turns Lookin at the Crowd with a Wild Eyed Grin.]
Crowd: THE CRUEL WORLD! DW: That's Rite! [he kicks the man in the gut, While he is Doubled over he
Picks him up into a Suplex Position and then Brings him down into a Pile
Driver. The man lies unconscious and bleeding from the Mouth] [The Throng of Follwers all Raise Clenched Fists in the Air and Scream] Crowd: ALL HAIL DEATHWISH! ALL HAIL DEATHWISH! [Deathwish Looks up the Balcony were Jim is standing] DW: Now Jim you Go back and Tell SCK and Compton that Their Days are numbered, DEATHWISH IS COMING!!!!! [ The DJ begins to play "Beautiful People" as the crowd begins to come in toward Deathwish, Jim Boss Looks around and walks down the Stairs. He gets to the Door opens it and goes out side to the cool nite air. The Large Black Man
Is still standing there beside the door.]
Large black man: Good Evening Mr. Boss [Jim Boss Gets into his Limo and Drives off. We Can still hear the odd sounds coming from the Warehouse]
