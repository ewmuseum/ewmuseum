Steve Houston RP

22 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys here's a recent RP I did for INWI. Go ahead and critique it if ya got time or if your bored, like I am right now. Thanx marC
(Scene opens inside Continental Airlines Arena. Steve Houston is walking around the arena on crutches. Steve Houston also has a large amount of white wrapping which appears to be a cast on his left leg.)
Steve Houston: Damn!!! Look at me, I'm really beat up. I know your wondering how I got like this. So let me tell you. Well, it's all thanks to Mark Johnson.
Yeah, these are the results after Mark Johnson's brutal attack on me last week. Look at me I'm on crutches and my right leg is broke. (The camera views in on Houston's legs and the right leg appears to be fine. The left leg is the one in the cast on it.) Steve Houston: Look at me Johnson!! Look at what your superb powers did to me!! Gee.... I really don't know what to do now. I mean I got a match Tuesday against some newcomer Dusty Love. Then the 31st my big match against the all mighty Mark Johnson.
(Steve a tear from his eye.) Steve Houston: All right, take your boots off now, cuz I'm done with the bull shit'n! (Steve throws the crutches down. He then walks over to a chair sits down and rips off his cast. All a long his cast which was made out of toilet paper.)
Steve Houston: Your probably wondering what that was all about eh'? What I was trying to get into all of your heads is that Johnson didn't do jack squat to me. Damn Mark, your not even good enough to hurt me when you attack me during another match.
(Steve gets up from the chair and begins to walk around.) Steve Houston: Speaking of that match. Can you say Screw Job? Well, that's exactly what I got. Tucker, I'm not sure what you gave the ref before the match, but he really must have enjoyed it. Because he really saved your ass.
You know, he knows, I know, and everyone else knows that saw that match, knows I won. Fair and square buddy. Steve Houston got the Dubba U over Andrew Tucker. Enough about that match and on to my upcoming match Tuesday. Haha... (Steve Houston bursts out laughing.) Steve Houston: ...hahaha....Oh God! Pete scheduled me a real tough one before my big Pay Per View match. He'll be no kind of warm up for my US title match.
Wait I shouldn't say that. This joke called Dusty Love can't possibly be any worse than Mark Johnson. Hell Love is probably better than Johnson. I'm not a mathematician but, this basically means if I can beat Love I'll destroy
Johnson. Don't get me wrong there's no doubt in anyone's mind if I can beat either of these two chumps. I was just putting things into easier terms for you lame brainers out there.
(Steve puts his face close to the camera. All you can see is his face.) Steve Houston: Take a real good look INWI. Take a real good long look at the screen. Now say to yourself INWI United States champion. Enjoy it for the few more days you can Johnson. Cuz your looking at the next INWI United States champion!!!
