[CRITIQUE] "Iron Man" Eddie Jacobs Flash..

1 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, everybody. I'm bored, and I haven't done this in a long time, so Ill get right to the flash.. please tell me what you think.. [objectively, if
possible :) ] [Footsteps. The camera cannot be seen fading in, because there is nothing to focus on. Darkness continues to surround the screen, but we know someone in there. We can hear them. Hear their footsteps.]
[As the footsteps approach, an image of this unseen man develops. He is likely a good sized man, and it sounds as though he's wearing some pretty heavy boots. Finally, we see some light.]
[It's not much, just the flashing a match makes when struck. But it's enough light to get a quick image of the man's face. He's closer than the footsteps sounded. He's SCW newcomer "Iron Man" Eddie
Jacobs.] [Jacobs holds the match up to a cigar, and starts to puff away in the darkness. The screen is left with no image but the floating orange embers of the cigar tip, when Eddie breaks the silence with a low, Southern drawl.]
EJ: "How's that fer an introduction, Ace?" [We can hear Eddie faintly chuckling to himself in the black room.] EJ: "Ah always was one ta make a dramatic appearance; first time is as good as any. As Ah'll later explain, this darkness is symbolic.
But Ah guess Ah'll burn that bridge when Ah get there." "First ah reckon Ah oughta give you a little history on good ole Eddie. Ah was an INWI World Champion fer a time, th' same title that one Brody Thunder made famous. Ole Brodes is another guy Ah'll get to in a minute." "Ah've won various accomplishments, titles, accalaides in my career. Accordin' to some list at the end 'o last year, Ah was one of the top thirty wresslers in th' world."
"According to some weekly poll, Ah've been fourth fer a few weeks straight. That oughta give you folks all the background you need about me."
[Eddie silently takes another drag off of the cigar. He lets the smoke sit in his mouth a moment, possibly while thinking, and then blows it invisibly into the darkness.]
EJ: "Now Ah reckon Ah'll give ya what ah know on SCW." "Ah know SCW seems ta have very few guys who can lace 'em up wit' me. John Chapman bein' one. Mike McShane bein' another. But a handful 'a guys ain't enough ta hold me down."
"It never was in the past." "But there still seems ta be a few fellas good enough ta give me a run fer my money. Chris Del Rio seems to be a good bet. And Dick
LeBaron seems like a better one. But Ah'll let you folks be th' first to know, that odds are made to be broken."
[There is some shuffling in the darkness. The cigar embers float about three feet downward. Presumably, Eddie is sitting down, making himself at home.]
EJ: "Now that we've taken care 'o th' preliminary stuff, let's get right down ta business, boys. Ah didn't come here to wait behind some of these local boy contenders who have never had gold around their waists before."
"In fact, Ah didn't come here ta cater to th' older veterans wit' their titles super glued 'round their waists. Ah came here fer one reason alone.."
"Gold." "An' Ah don't care whose gold it is. Ah jus' want some gold. Somethin' ta wear around my waist again would suit me jus' fine, no matter what kind it is." "But there is a diff'rent mindset in the SCW. Guys who think they'll be holdin' their titles forever. That's a diff'rent kind 'o gold."
"Fool's gold." [Eddie sighs, and is heard stretching in the darkness. Knuckles are popped. And the cigar embers start to fly alot closer to the camera lens.]
EJ: "Now.. why am Ah sittin' in darkness?" "It's simple. Y'see, this darkness sorta represents th' SCW. Ah'm comin' into SCW, and Ah don't know what Ah'll find. Ah haven't had much of a chance ta scout, which Ah normally like ta do. So jus' like a dark room, Ah don't know what SCW holds fer me."
[There is a slight rustling, and the cigar starts to float away..] EJ: "Until.. " [Light. Eddie is standing a stone's throw from the camera, his hand still at the switch. We see the chair he was seated in, and the camera also takes in some of the awards in this room. The dominant colour seems to be gold.]
EJ: "As it turns out, th' darkness was jus' in my own l'il trophy room. Ah won't bother ta bore y'all with actual titles, but the volume of it should count for something."
[Indeed it does. There are several trophies and belt replicas in the room.] EJ: "Which also symbolizes SCW. 'Cause soon enough, Ah'mma turn this l'il league into my _own_ personal trohy room. Only question left is.."
"How long will it take ta flip the switch? A day? A week? A month or two?" "Only answer Ah can give is jus' wait an' see, boys. Jus' wait an' see.." [Eddie trudges back to his chair and settles in, as the camera starts to close in on his face.]
EJ: "Ah'm almost done, don't worry. But there is one more name Ah need ta touch on. 'Lone Wolf' Brody Thunder."
[Eddie's face spreads into a calculating and malicious smirk.] EJ: "Brodes, you know me from INWI.. More appropriately, you know me from the end 'a that league when ya came back fer some appearances."
"An' Ah doubt that Ah've forgotten you. We never got ta square off at that three day shindig they called th' "Last Dance", but
Ah'll fill ya in on a little secret, Ace." "My dance card's open now." "So all Ah'm askin' is.. say th' word. You have Chapman now? Did the 'Lone Wolf' finally get lonely? No matter. Ah have some backup of my own. He goes by th' name of . . . "
" 'Rodeo' Nevada Harris . " [Eddie spreads that malevolent grin again.] EJ: "Tag teams are fine, Brodes. Singles are better." "No match is . . . unacceptable." "So mull it over, Brodes. An' this goes fer th' rest of the SCW. Myself an' Harris don't have a match at th' Pay Per View. If anyone has th'
cajones ta step in there wit' us, be my guest." "Or th' cajones ta face me mono e mono. T' be honest, Ah really don't care who Ah beat up these next few weeks. 'Cause after th' Pay Per
View, th' title hunt begins." "An' Ah'm comin' wit' everything Ah got." "So SCW, be on th' lookout. Ah doan' jus' talk th' talk or walk th' walk . . . " " . . . Ah beat th' best an' Ah bury th' rest." [With that, Eddie chomps down on the cigar one more time. The tip glows bright red, but soon it fades. More smoke fills the room, and Eddie leans over to cap the camera lens with the ashes.]
[Fade to black..] ~ Joe B. * Obscure Handler of Eddie Jacobs [U.W.F., N.E.W., G.L.O.W.] "All in all, you're just another brick in the wall" ~ Floyd "Y'know, where it ends still usually depends on where you start" ~Everlast
