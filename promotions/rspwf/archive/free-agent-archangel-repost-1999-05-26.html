-Free Agent- Archangel REPOST

26 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once again, I will announce that former 7-Time World Heavyweight Champion (in one fed, about 25 times including stints in a bunch of bush leagues), Archangel, has returned to the world of wrestling. After being released due to injuries, he is healed and ready to partake in action once again. He's already received over 12 offers from different promotions, but this is the 90's people. Just don't come to him saying you want him to join, or he should prove himself here. Make him an offer he can't refuse...
With that, here's a sample of what you'll be getting: ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Darkness falls, rain hits the streets of Atlanta, as you see a dark figure in the misty night. The wind blows, as peices of trash blow through alleys and into the main streets. The trash blows by, and as it does, lightning crashes and you can see the face of the man before you.
He's over six feet tall, nearing seven but not quite. He wears a pair of blue jeans, which are baggy and a black leather jacket that the water streaks from. By his size, you can see he's a big man, powerful...he has long, blonde hair which stretches down his back in a pony tail, dripping from the precipitation. His eyes are a deep blue, and he wears a goatee...which is black. His Black Fly sunglasses flicker from the lightning, as he opens his mouth]
Well...I guess I'm finally back into working condition. Fixed myself...months in the hospital, suffering from the pain of a broken neck...for the second time. But life's EXTREME, isn't it?
[he reaches into the pocket of his jacket, removes a pack of Camel
Lights and a funky purple lighter...he lights up and inhales]
So what's the deal nowadays? I've been out of the game since...November? Christ, has it been that long? No matter though...give me a few days. Let the rain, the weather, the pain...let them all soak in. Give me a few days to soak up all the world around me, to regain myself. Revive the spirit...release the soul. Yeah...give me a few...then watch as I take another world title, set a new record, or some other death defying feat. I should have died over a year ago...but I don't give up that easy...
[another drag] See, I play this game...not for the money, the fame, the glory...I do it because I get a kick out of watching the fans in those seats cheer my name, as I dive off of a platform 20 feet above the ring and come crashing onto my opponent, driving them through a table or three. I dig the feeling of crushing a chair against their head, as the blood pours from their skull...what, you thought because they call me Archangel, that I'm some chump-ass babyface? You've go to be kidding me...I'm a protector, not some lame ass superhero...so now that I'm not hanging around with a certain promotion, who wants me? Who wants greatness? Who wants the original Innovator of Extreme?
[fade to black]
