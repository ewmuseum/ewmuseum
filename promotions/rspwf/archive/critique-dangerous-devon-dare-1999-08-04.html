[Critique] "Dangerous" Devon Dare

4 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heylo...just a roleplay for my newest guy, "Dangerous" Devon Dare. Please critique. And if flaming is not required, don't use it please :-) ===================================== [The camera fades into the outdoors somewhere...or so it sounds like, with the sounds of rushing water and wildlife.  The camera veers to the left and right and views an entire forest.  The cameraman looks down and it is seen that he is standing on a bridge about 500 feet up from a huge river.  The camera man then looks to the left and right of the bridge. 
When he goes to the right, he notices a group of three men, all dressed in orange jumpsuits and with cords tied around their waists.  He walks over to them, but can't recognize one of them for they are all wearing caps and sunglasses.  However, one of the men takes off his cap and sunglasses to reveal his long dirty blonde hair and fierce blue eyes...it's "Dangerous" Devon Dare.  He looks at the camera man and grins as he is still checking the cord around his waist.  He brushes a hand through his long blonde hair and shakes his head.]
Dare: Always looking for a damn good story, now aren't you? Camera Man: Umm...yes, Mr. Dare.  You are one of the hottest rookie commodities in the ICWF and...
Dare: ONE of the hottest?  I am THE hottest rookie commodity in the
ICWF, get it right you insolent little pimple!  Now...you know the deal.  If you want something out of me...you're gonna [points down]
follow me down. Camera Man: [a bit flustered] Wait...I don't...even have a bungee cord! [Dare smiles and motions to one of his friends.] Dare: Give [points to the camera man] this bastard a bungee cord, will ya??  Friend: Yup, will do. [Dare's friend runs over to his truck positioned only a few hundred feet away.  He comes back a few seconds later with a bungee cord.  He hooks it up to the end of the bridge and runs over to the camera man and straps him in.]
Friend: Not too tight? Camera Man: ...no, it's quite alright. Friend: Okay then, you're all set! [Dare's friend runs back to his original position as Dare looks at the camera man and grins an evil grin yet again.]
Dare: The things you have to put up with just to get something good out of the Dangerous One.  I respect your balls.  They're almost as big as mine.  Right-o, [yelling at his friends] ARE WE READY??
Friends: YEAH! Dare: [shrugging] Alright, we're all set.  Come along Mister Journalist.  The Dangerous One will fix you with your adrenaline fix for the week. [Dare motions the camera man to follow him to the edge of the bridge.  The camera man follows and looks down.] Camera Man: JESUS CHRIST! Dare: No, not quite, I'm the Dangerous One, but close enough. [The camera man looks to the right, where Dare's friends are positioned.  One of them jumps off.  The second one jumps off a few seconds after.  Then, Dare himself jumps off.  The camera man hesitates a moment, then jumps.  The camera views the river accelerating towards him as he descends quickly down the 500 foot stretch of space between the bridge and the river.]
Camera Man: AAAAAAAHHHHH!!! [The camera man hits the water and is flung up, upside down and wet.] Camera Man: Holy God!! Dare: [heard from a distance, but not seen] PRESENT! [The upside down camera man, frozen in shock, views Dare coming down.] Dare: [laughing] Hang on, one of my buddies just got up to the bridge.  He'll give you some slack. [As Dare promised, the Camera Man's bungee stopped bouncing and stayed put as did Dare's.  Dare does a little maneuver and gets the camera man and himself back upright.  The camera man breathes a sigh of relief as
Dare squeezes water out of his long blonde hair.]
Dare: [laughing] You looked like you were gonna piss yourself there, amigo!  Nice job!  Not bad for a first timer!
Camera Man: [visibly shaking] Well..I...I...I... Dare: [cutting him off] I..I...NOTHING.  You did it and that took a lot of balls for a first timer.  Now, the Dangerous One WILL grant you your interview.  Get to it, I only have a few precious minutes.
Camera Man: [still shaking] What...are...your...thoughts... uhhh...on...Jax...Ja...Ja...Ja....Jameson...? Dare: [laughs] First off, calm down before you get an aneurysm or something.  Alright...Jax "The Brick" Jameson.  Ah, such a creative name for the bitch I'm going to slap the next Dy-No-Mite.
Camera Man: [confused and shaking] Cr..cr...cr...creati...creative?? Dare: [taking out a water bottle out of his side pocket and drinking a little bit]  Oh yeah.  It's a real irony that the man I'll be sending falling like a Brick is named...the Brick.  Jax, you've got no idea who you're dealing with...and that's good!  Because it'll make you stronger, unfortunately also, it will make you dumber.  The Dangerous One is faster than premature ejaculation.  I have more endurance than a track star on creatine.  Hell, amigo, if I were classified as a speed, it would be "Holy <beep!> What the <beep!> was that??"  Jax, [laughs] your first rookie mistake is facing the Dangerous One.  Your second rookie mistake will be to go toe to toe with the Dangerous One, because quite frankly, it can't be done!  Being the Brick that you are, when I'm done with you, I'll throw you through the window of your house, hit your mother, father, pet, and hooker with you, and they will all see in 4D as you will after the pounding that I lay upon your concrete ass!
Camera Man: [less shaky] But...Mr Dare...Jax Jameson is a...very respectable...ehh...rookie.
Dare: [grins] Respectable, eh?  The only place respectable will get you is up the rectum of a corporate worker.  Look, pal...Jax Jameson messed with the Dangerous One...and the Dangerous One will therefore beat him till the natural tint of his vision is orange, his hands are so swollen he'll have to use his whore for help to eat, and his entire body so sore that when he lies down to get jiggy with himself at night, he can't because he'll think his willy is orange, his back is aching, and he'll run to Doctor Dare for another round of ass kicking because he needs his original vision back...he will die to see 4D!!
Camera Man: [laughs] Good thoughts...Mr. Dare...now...the cruiserweight title situation... Dare: [Dare's face darkens] Ah yes...the cruiserweight title situation.  How funny of you to ask.  I was just about to address that.  OUR CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION IS A <beep!>-ING FLUKE!  When was the last time he defended the title, huh?  This Monday will be his FIRST DEFENSE in at least three weeks.  Can you PLEASE explain to me why in the flying
<beep!> has The Mule had the luxury of NOT defending the title!  That is a TRAVESTY!  Mendoza, I'm calling you out, boy.  August 16, you got a dance with Black Moss.  Cancel it.  Black Moss is an overrated old bag of <beep!>  The Dangerous One will prove a much better challenge for you.  That is, of course...if you want to challenge yourself...which I seriously doubt due to your lack of title defenses.  I mean, your only title defense was against a damned scrub of a tag team!  Mendoza, you are LOOKING at the best cruiserweight in the business.  I will be more than happy to hand you your ass and ego in exchange for that belt you have BLEMISHED.  August 16, Mendoza.  Think about it.
Camera Man: [not shaking anymore] Very...strong words there, Mr. Dare.  Any closing thoughts? Dare: Yes.  Jax the Brick will be tossed right to his family, signed sealed and delivered by 4D and Vinny THE ASS Mendoza should accept my challenge if he so desires to keep his credibility.  Why?  Because Devon
Dare is purely the best athlete in the ICWF...Devon Dare is the
Dangerous One...and the Dangerous One is JUST TOO DAMN GOOD.  Now, do yourself a favor and...find someone to pull you up.
[Dare ascends to the bridge laughing as the camera looks on] Camera Man: NO!  GODDAMN IT! [Dare continues his ascent as the camera man tries to figure out a way to pull himself up.  Fed up, he throws the camera down to the water. 
The last view we see is the camera impacting with the rocks below and static.  Fade to black.]
