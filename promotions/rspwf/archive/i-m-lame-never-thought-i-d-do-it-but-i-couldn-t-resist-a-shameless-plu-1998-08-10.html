[I'm Lame] Never thought I'd do it, but I couldn't resist a shameless plug. he's an RP of mine!

10 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens up to show a taxi cab, with a metal trash can embedded in its smashed and partially shattered back window, speeding off out of the EWC Palace parking lot and into the distance. Skid marks are left in the road and at the curb of the almost empty parking center. Quickly, the shot cuts back to Fang, standing in the lot, staring after the cab with a glint of fury in his gaze. He looks around the pretty much empty space of land, anticipating a sneak attack, perhaps. Fang's wearing his "WEDNESDAY WRECKAGE" t-shirt and a pair of Levi's
501 jeans. As usual, wrapped around his head is a grey bandana, and draped over his shoulder is his blood-stained, white towel. Fang's sweating and a bruise is forming on his left forearm which is now crossed with his right arm over his chest. Now Fang's miscellaneous glance falls upon the lens of the camera. He speaks.]
Fang: Coward. [Nothing on Fang'sface moves but his lips. The look on his face is one of rage.] Fang: Is there any man more worthy of scorn than the one who assails his enemies from a blind side? A warrior possessed of genuine greatness stands firm with a heart of granite: he seeks conflict and rejoices when his opponent stands before him! To a true competitor, an enemy is more worthy than a friend, for enemies are the essence of his life. He does not cower behind brainless goons from the greater men that pursue him and attack when unexpected... A
_real_ man gathers his nerves and stands up to a challenge.
[He starts to become a little more relaxed, but the look of focus and vengeance still appears in his eyes.]
Fang: James Pillow, you have made the wrestling ring and fields of battle your home, yet you shirk the responsibility of your calling. To you, provoking a man's anger with taunting words and behind the back stabs is more worthy than defeating him in combat. To you, heaping humilation upon the people who provide you your welfare is more worthy than earning their respect.
That is why we are opposites, James Pillow. When you look at me, you see a man who thrives when the challenge is at its highest ebb; a man who earned his rank of honor with his own sweat and blood. When I look at you, I see a man in fear of his own failure; a man who, recognising his own inferiority of spirit, broke down inside and fled into a bitter cloak of shadows.
James Pillow... In my veins, there flows the crimson blood of valor. In yours: the yellow mucous of cowardice!! [Fang shakes his head in disgust.] Fang: Coward. Next time we meet, leave your furtive antics behind. They, and the means by which they are used are not worthy tactics to be used on a man of my legendary rank. Besides... I'll shred through them like a hurricane through a barn.
And don't forget, on Sunday, to take the opportunity to redeem yourself before all of the world. Face the challenge that pursues you. Do not delude yourself that I'm not worthy of your attention. Deep inside, you know that it is you who is dwarfed in stature.
Face the might and fury of Fang, Mr. Pillow. But let me first warn you in all sincerity... To be struck down head on is more devestating than to be struck down from behind...
[Fang's eyes glitter as he appears to be pondering something for a moment.] Continuing on with cowards and attacks from out of the dark, I must speak of
Andru Edwards. Edwards... Your meddling this evening is as unfathomable to me as the void. You wanted to fight Pillow, huh? And well, I just happened to be there... Just remember: to be master of your own destiny makes you a mighty man. Never let the actions of others control your fate.
Edwards, your reason speaks to you in the voice of deception - it may tell you that I have done you some injustice. But my heart speaks to you in the voice of truth - it says that such a thing as justice does not exist. There is no requital for evil; no reward for good... There are no transgressions in the violent world we have made our calling.
[Grinning.] Fang: But still... no doubt you will not heed my words. No doubt you will come for me in the name of vengeance. So be it. I will not turn you away. I welcome you with open arms, Edwards, to your own destruction...
[Fang nods his head to the camera, turns away, and quickly turns back. He has a few more words.]
Fang: How forgetful am I? Almost disregarded my match of "golden" porportions. Survival of the Fittest. The men with iron hearts continue on, while the meager are eliminated. I thrive on conditioning and endurance. I've been known to last hours in the ring, so a match of this entity, is my bag. I dare anyone involved in that 18 man battle for the Midwest title to look into my eyes, right now....
You got several questions to ask yourselves. Can you match the intensity that you see here? Can you match the anger? Can you match the desire to do damage and ruin people? I don't think you can. I don't think you ever will. And that's perfectly all right with me. But when all your precious red blood is spilled all over the ground... When your body is broken and bent ... Ask yourselves one more question. Why did I remind Fang I existed? Why did I even step into the ring with him? I knew he'd bring the Rage down upon me!!
You can answer those on your own time... We'll all find out the answer on
Sunday.... WHEN THE CARNAGE UNFOLDS!!!! [Fang's eyes rest easy as his tense body slowly relaxes. The screen fades to black as Fang starts to walk away into the dark night.]
