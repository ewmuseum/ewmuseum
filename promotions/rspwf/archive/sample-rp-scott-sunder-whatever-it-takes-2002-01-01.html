I disagree with Brody, but as he said...different strokes for different folks. I like the slice of life rp's that show the character in his own settings and only slightly aware of the camera. Mixed in with the usual 'in front of the camera' rp's you can keep a character interesting and some what original for a decent amount of time. So far as the rp goes, very solid. I liked the twist at the end...kind of left you wondering did he or didn't he have anything to do with the car accident. Depends on how heelish you think the character can be, lol. The only improvement I can think is to shorten it up a bit. It's a bit lengthy to read which might make those miss the crucial ending if you don't keep them interested long enough. It's one of those rp's where you write it up because you're really in the writing mood. But then you might put it down for a bit and come back to it later and revise it a bit. Clean up the rough edges, shorten it, etc. All in all, a very good effort and a solid story. Kudos.
