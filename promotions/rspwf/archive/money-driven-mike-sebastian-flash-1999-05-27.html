"Money Driven" Mike Sebastian Flash

27 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Eh... I'm bored. I've never done this for, to be honest. Some people say I suck. They're probably right. But I'm so zoned right now, I could care less. This is a flash I did a while back, but it's the first one I found of mine. Anyhow, critique it, copy it, trade it with your friends, whatever. But here it is.
-- [Nightfall. The scene opens to the deserted streets of Manheim, Germany. A bright light is seen reflecting off the nearby parkway; as the light is seemingly originating from the moon above. The clanging of shoes is heard in the background, and as the picture descends, a dark pair of Gucci loafers are seen being walked down the sidewalk, which lies adjacent to the roadway. The picture slowly moves upward, revealing now this man's cleanly-pressed business suit, accompanied by a leather and gold belt at the waist. The man's cranium is finally reached, and the man is now recognized. It is Mike Sebastian.]
MS: You know, I've always been a bit superstitious. Knocking on wood, not stepping on cracks, wearing the same pair of underwear every day... okay, maybe I wouldn't go _that_ far, but I still believe that certain things have an effect on what will occur later on down the road.
And I really think there is something to the idea of karma -- a series of lives you lead until you learn the lesson that you're supposed to learn.
But, I digress. Back to the idea of superstition. And mainly, that's why you see me walking the streets at this time of night... er, morning, rather. Before every match of mine, I walk amongst the local mudhole. Call me crazy, nuts, moronic, whatever. But you can't exactly say it hasn't worked. I mean, I
_am_ a champion, you know. A lucky rabbit's foot, a special momento of some kind, what have you. Whatever works... whatever floats your boat. Without doing the things you feel are necessary, or fulfilling all the methods that you believe facilitate to your own success, it doesn't work.
Come crunch time, chances are, one won't be able to function properly without doing these "necessary" things, or bringing along those all-important materialistic good-luck charms.
And with me going through my "walking" routine like I am now, and based on what's happened after I've done this in the past, you can go ahead and place your bets on the tee-vee champ and that seven-foot ogre I'm forced to carry Thursday night.
Cause this one's a sure thing. [He nods.] MS: And see, Chris Johnson is still living his life of karma. And he'll continue to do so until he can clearly comprehend and fully understand the lesson he seeks. Until he learns the lesson he was sent to learn...
That Mike Sebastian is, and forever will be one-hundred percent _better_ than himself. And until you can come to terms with that, Johnson, you're nothing. [Sebastian stops, and gleams into the camera's lens in all seriousness.] MS: A nobody. [The camera man halts, as Sebastian resumes his walk down the city street; admiring the German moonlight. Fade to black.]
