[Free Agent] "Fantastic" Brian King

17 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, due to my current fed being "sold" to AWMC, where I already handle "Farmboy" Clement Tyme, I've been forced into free agency. Below is my sample roleplay as well as my info. Contact me if interested.
[The scene fades into a locker room in the historic Madison Square Garden. Towels, boots, tights, and various personal items are strewn on the ground and placed on tables. Sitting in a chair facing his locker, evidenced by the name over the locker, is "Fantastic" Brian King. King is sitting with no shirt on and his hair hanging down beside his bowed head. King stands up and turns his chair around. Now he is facing the camera. He has already put on his long black tights with Fantastic written down both sides in orange. His red knee and elbow pads, with the gold crowns in the middle, are down at his ankles and wrists, respectively. His orange boots are on, but only laced half way up and still untied. King's dirty blond hair is shiny from reflecting light.]
Brian King - Well, it seems that I am back, and a top contender the for the oh so coveted, UWC World Championship. A title formerly held by the once great,
Kerry Powell. Several new names have popped up to challenge me in this new incarnation of the UWC. I saw one name that stands out to me. Ethan Van Ryan.
The man is quite a character. When I first met the man, I, of all people wanted to be friends with him. Then I suddenly remembered, Brian King does not need friends. In the hectic world of the UWC, where crack and chairshots have come into play in the recent days, many people have been injured and turned against by "friends." So, one Ethan Van Ryan will not have the pleasure of not having to face me, because I refuse to be friends with any one who may actually stand a chance against me.
[King has a soft chuckle with himself and then continues.] BK - Now, when I look through the list of the major contenders for the UWC
Title, I've noticed few if not a couple of established UWC stars. Three to be exact. Who? Well, a normal UWC representative would say Brian King, Ric
Beauty, and Obsessive Compulsive Disorder, but I, by no means am normal. No, I was refering to Me, Myself, and I. The only thing worth watching in the entire
UWC Title tournament. Isn't that amazing? It is. Billy, Bobby, Hillbilly,
Bernard, whichever Blair is in charge of this place now, could only scrounge up three proven superstars for something as important as this? It doesn't seem possible! But, at least Blair got the best of all the UWC superstars, and that of course is me.
[King stretches his arms above his head and lets out a big yawn.] BK - Now, I've been paired with one of the several unestablished wrestlers in this tournament. One by the name of Tyrone Lawrence. I've heard that he is one half of some tag team. Well, Tyrone, I'm sure that you are going to do well in the tag team division because we don't have a very strong one yet. But
Tyrone, you've been selected to wrestle me, a suplex artist. I know every single one. You'd be amazed. But, I will put on a "Fantastic" show for all the wrestlers and fans who I know want to be just like me when they grow up or lose some weight. Unfortunately, Tyrone, you will be my dummy for my demonstration, so you will not get to see the match live. But, Tyrone, I'll get someone to tape the match for you and then you can take it and watch it at home. Then, you will know why you got your ass beat in a five star match. And all the new guys coming over from mWa, that cheap fed that my fed, UWC, bought out, and all the other guys who've decided to jump on the ship that I made, you'll all learn why I am the "Originator of the Five Star Match." Everybody knows I carried that match I had with T-Bone and 100 Percent Shit. Do you see either of them back? No? I didn't think so.
[King cracks his knuckles and grabs a "UWC Royalty" shirt. He puts the shirt on and continues.]
BK - It's kind of chilly in here. Even King's get cold sometimes. Now, as I see that Tyrone is all but beaten, I guess I'll be moving on to the second round. And probably face that Ethan Van Ryan. That will be quite a test...to the normal man. But not to UWC Royalty. Yes, that's me. Then, it will be smooth sailing from there and Booby Blair can not make me wrestle the first match. I won't be taking away from the champions because I will be the
Champion. I wouldn't be held down like I was before. Even when Blair was trying to holding me down, he couldn't stop me from rising in the ranks as the wins kept piling up. But now, I'm ready to fight to be the champ. I'll rival the longevity of Powell's reign. I'll reign supreme until I've crippled the entire UWC with my Royal Crowning. And now, I've got to begin preparation for the beginning of my quest to be the best.
[Brian King then turns on his CD player. He takes a CD case from his locker and removes Rob Zombie's "American Made Music To Strip By." He cranks the volume up and "Dragula" blasts through the entire locker room as the camera fades.]

History Brian King was born of Martin and Melissa King in the fall of 1963. He grew up having everything a boy could want. His family was far from poor. He started to show an interest in wrestling while fooling around with his dad in the living room. His father quickly noticed his potential as a actor and wrestler and decided to help put him through wrestling school if that was his dream. It turned out that it was, and Brian King went through the best school his dad could find, the Nemesis School of Wrestling. Little Brian King, now 6'5", took
John Tang as his ring name. Tang ruled the little tiny AOL feds, the kind that opened and folded in less than a month. He was hated, because he was so good.
In one of these feds, he met a man that would later have an impact on him,
Rattlesnake. Tang tried unsuccessfully to make it in the big time, the United
EWF. The measuring stick for a wrestler. He never made, as it ventured into the wrong fed, the DECWF, the most extreme fed of the six in the EWF, he hand his career seemingly ended by the prez, Rob Dalton. He decided not to retire from wrestling completely, but to open his very own fed, the AXWF, which soon folded, like most AOL feds do. Then, he did somethign he swore he never would...he sat on his butt for over 6 months, dreaming he could run a fed like the EWMC. Then, Rattlesnake called Tang up and inquired about his health. Tang lied and said he was in the best shape of his life, and Rattlesnake said he had found a fed that they would have competition in. This gave him the drive to come back. He recuperated his legs and got back into shape. He re-debuted in the IPWA as a member of the Regulators tag team under his given name, Brian
King. The team won their first ever match together as well as the IPWA Tag Team
Championship, in one fell swoop. Later, King wrestled for Empire Wrestling.
Not achieveing much success, King moved on, now to the Unlawful Wrestling
Council. After having minor setbacks, King became one of the major players in the federation, while running a successful angle where he was held down in mid-card status while tearing through the competition. All of King's success was negated when the UWC closed down due to "Beautiful" Bobby Blair's cocaine addiction. UWC was then sold to Blair's brother, Billy Blair. King was faced with proving himself to a new boss. King was placed in the World Title tournament and destroyed his opponent at the first card of the resurrected UWC.
The fed was then sold to a small regional fed in Detroit, Michigan named AWMC. Instead of joining his friends in AWMC, King has decided to test the free agent pool. The arrogant young man is now looking for a new place to call home and then destroy.
Gimmick John Tang was a man that nobody liked in the smaller, independent, AOL feds. He was too good. So he tried his hand at the big boys, the DECWF was his destination. He tried his best to hang with the big boys, but he just couldn't.
The prez decided to kill off this jobber by breaking both his legs...personally. So Tang was killed off. He had surgery and recuperated, but he just sat on his ass and got fat. He felt sorry for himself. Then he had an idea to come back with his friend, and form a tag team. So he got back into shape and called his buddy up. He agreed and they started training together.
Then they made it back and went on a 10 match winning streak. Tang decided to go off by himself and change his name. Now, he has become Brian King and is on the quest to achieve revenge on everyone you laughed and poked fun at him when they saw his career almost ended. King has returned with a vengeance and has mocked all the rednecks and wrestlers who jeered the man who once stood over them when he fell crippled.
Appearance Brian wears long black tights with the word Fantastic written down each side in orange. He wears orange boots. He also wears black elbow and knee pads with gold crowns outlined on them. King has long dirty blond hair. He has a scar above his right eye from a hardcore Tag Title match in the IPWA. His eyes are a fiery green with a tint of brown, which flashes when he is angry. His nose is average and most of the time sunburned and dotted with freckles. His right ear has an circular earring inserted in the lobe. His chin and lips are both average with nothing special about them.

Finisher Name: Royal Crowning
Description of Finisher: The Royal Crowning is begun when King locks up with the opponent. From the locked-up position, King sets his opponent up for a vertical suplex. Then, Brian grabs his opponent's feet and releases his head.
Then he attempts to drop his opponent, but his opponent puts his hands down on the mat and springs himself up. When this occurs, Brian turns the man rightside up and delivers a devastating powerbomb.
Signal/Move Preceding Finisher: Gold Crown Set-Up Move Description: The Gold Crown is starts when King whips his opponent into the ropes. As his opponent bounces back King's way, King grabs him by the waist and delivers a Tilt-A-Whirl Slam.
