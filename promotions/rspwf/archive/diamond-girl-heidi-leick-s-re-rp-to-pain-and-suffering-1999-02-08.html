Subj: The Modern Apocalypse's and Death and Destruction's Heidi Leick Date: 2/7/99 8:35:04 PM Eastern Standard Time From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] , [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] :: Heidi Leick is standing in front of a IWF/WoW banner.. She's got the GDWA
Extreme Television Championship Belt over her shoulder, and she's wearing cut off Daisy Duke style denim shorts, a cutoff at the midriff Black Sabbath T
Shirt, and her blue wrestling boots, her blonde hair is wild, and loose, flowing down her back and over her shoulders ::
The Diamond Girl Heidi Leick: Oh Sonia Sonia Sonia... It goes back and forth , like a See saw.. I challenge you, then you challenge me..
::laughs sarcastically:: Unlike you, I agreed to your little challenge... you can't do it ~yourself~ physically... so you put my fate up against your teams titles...
::Smiles evilly:: fair enough, First it was ten lashes.. Now you whine and cry because I say it doesn't phase me... you try and come up with something else...,
::giggles, then Starts to sing:: it's all the same old torture every day.. every day.. ::stops and giggles:: Sonia.. I'm not agreeing to anything more than the FIRST set of Stipulations.. You laid it down, I agreed... it was Ten lashes.. maybe I'll go ask Mills Lane what he thinks of someone ammending an agreed on agreement? ::smiles evilly::
But...Sonia.. IF you ~really~ want a NEW set in... You'll have to ACCEPT my challenge.. the one that you ran and hid from, ::grins psychotically::
I get you, ONE on ONE, In a match.. No matter WHAT the outcome of the tag matches.. and ::giggles::
Sonia.. I'll add something extra for your meek little mind to ponder... ::grins evilly:: Not IF, but WHEN Neptune and Achillies BEAT your team for the titles.. I'll face you, The Next Mayhem... ONE on ONE.. For the Tag Team Titles that we've just won . You want some Stipulations that your so fond of? ::laughs:: No men can interfere... A catfight... straight out. Now.. The Question is.. Are you WOMAN enough? Do YOU have the fortitude, and courage to take ME on?... I think NOT. :: Heidi stands there and stares into the camera smugly, crossing her arms:: DGHL: Sonia.. :: evil grin:: you talk of ending careers..., what a pity you don't get in the ring to try and end mine yourself in one on one combat...
you're like a politician, hiding yourself away... making others fight for you
..not because you won't... Not because you're ~"Smart"~... No Sonia... It's
~BECAUSE~ ~YOU~ ~CAN'T~... Yes, Sonia, I'm a Marine ... but Sonia, don't you ever.. and I mean EVER compare your Crappy Mistress code or Conduct unbecoming a pissant to My Oath as a United States Marine.... Go back to School you illiterate piece of trash~!
I have Conduct BECOMING a Marine.. Conduct BECOMING a WARRIOR.. And Conduct becoming someone who has just added you to my Hit list Sonia... Where it comes from Nobody knows... Watch your back Sonia.. I..... ::looks into the camera evilly and quite seriously:: I Give you fair warning... :: pauses and turns away, then turns around with a psychotic grin:: Something wicked this way comes.. and Sonia... it's headed ~straight~ ~for~ ~you!~ :: Heidi looks to the camera, and it zooms into her eyes... revealing a psycotic glare:: DGHL: Oh the Punishments~!~.. ::laughs :: I almost forgot... 10 Orders of the
Mistress, Lashes, whatever you read from some medieval fantasy books is out now... forget it... I know what it is.. you think that you can scare people into not accepting the matches you are schedueled to have by ammending stipulations... :: shakes her head no:: The Killing Machines were right!.. You are a farce in this business.. a sad wanna~be product of the 1980's leather and masked teams of Vincent J's empire... and That's pretty damn sad...
::Heidi looks at the ground, then looks up and smiles ::: DGHL: Sonia... Oh how You do love to flatter yourself... :: giggles :: Wasn't it just last week that you were pining away in the locker room.... bragging how that the winner of a Pain and Suffering vs Death and Destruction Match would be offered a Spot in The Modern Apocalypse, and that YOU were going to make sure you won??
You had a IN!!.. An inside person who was going to get you in.. I HEARD you say that! And you said Shh!... Don't tell anyone, thats what you told my friend and ACE reporter Jerry Styles!!!!.. ::laughs::
Sonia... Would you like to talk about ~knowing someone on the inside?~ ::laughs:: Hades and I go back.. WAY back... In fact, He's the reason we SIGNED with the
IWF/WoW!!!. From the Harbingers Of the Apocalypse, to Damage Inc™
Hades and We have always had respect for each others talents... Between Hades and us, we had the biggest and best draw in the history of wrestling... And we'll do it again HERE!... Now Magnus Colby is with Hades, and the four of us together magnifies what we can do by 100 percent... Colby's that good...He's the puzzle piece that was missing other places.. we're the puzzle piece that was missing here... now it comes together, and our plan is almost complete Sonia.. and you were never in it,... I'll say it Once again
Sonia....
You and your fleabitten ~pets~ were ~never~ ~even~ ~being~ considered, Colby and Hades made the decision... and you were ~never~ ~in~ ~the~ ~equation~, In fact, just so you know... the others in the MA don't even care what you do... Sonia, make an enemy out of the MA if you choose..., but that's the choice of fools.. and ::starts to sob, mocking Sonia ::
Hurt feelings... ::laughs:: Go and Pull Bannister and O'Neil out of the hat as your backup.. we don't care....we know it's going to happen..
The facts are simply this.. The MA is the ONE TRUE FORCE in the IWF/WoW...we're a part of it... ::laughs:: and you're NOT. Get over it.. If you can! :: Heidi grins, and shakes her head in disbelief as she remembers Sonia's words ::: DGHL: And now Sonia wants to ~wench~ out , on the rag again, about that match we just had... she wants to scream on the Killing Machines, Badd Attitide, and us... Sonia.. did you expect anything LESS of Killing Machines? They've made thier bread and butter doing run in's... we knew it was coming.. don't act so shocked.. are you that Nieve or.. Or are you just THAT DUMB?
Badd Attitude.. I Commend your effort, I commend The Killing Machines as well.. they saw thier chance at stardome for the night.. and took it.. just like WE'RE going to take the Tag Team Titles. whether it's from Pain and Suffering , The
Killing Machines, or anyone else.
Sonia.. instead of wenching out.. maybe you should THANK the Killing Machines... they saved your boys faces... we had your boys beat... and the
KM's coming in and blindsideing us was the only thing that saved you...
:: Heidi turns away once more laughs, and regains her composure and turns back:: DGHL: Next up.. Badd Attitude... Bad Attitude.. I Pity you.. you stumbled into something your rookie asses can' t handle, can't comprehend... Unlike
Sonia,... I understand you want to strive for the apex, to reach the summit... Consider this youfirst title shot in the I/W, and maybe you'll give us some competition... but, alas,on this attempt, as is what happens with every other team that faces us.. you fall just short..
But you do get to walk away saying you got beat by the team that's captured the most prestigeous tag team titles in history,beat by the team that's beaten
Icons in this business...
beat by the best team there ever was, is, and ever will be.. Death and Destruction... See you Monday Night.. Sonia... See you... SOON.. :: Heidi laughs and walks away as the camera fades out:: Handler of Damage Inc™ Sensational Frankie Starz™ # 149 98 RSPWF 200 The Diamond Girl Heidi Leick GDWA # 136 98 RSPWF 200 Death & Destruction aka Perry Neptune & John Achillies IWF/WOW,NCCW #12 98 RSPWF Tag 200
