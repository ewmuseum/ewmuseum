Ice J: 2nd RP.

8 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone this is JJ handler of TWO World Champ Ice J. I posted an
RP before and now here's another one :-) I think I fixed this to be very good!!!!!! ENJOY!!!!!!!!!
{"Blood on the Ice is heard and all the fans in the arena jump up and start shouting "Ice J!" over and over again. Ice
J is walking to the ring with the belt over his shoulder and Iceberg to his right. Ice J is wearing his old "Ice J: BRRRRRR!!!"
T-shirt with big and baggy black pants. He has blue-shaded sunglasses on but even through those you can see in his eyes that he is focused and ready to take out Ken Saturn. The title is shining under the bright lights of the arena and every person there knows that after Tuesday Night Mayhem Ice J will still have that title.
Ice J gets to the ring and rolls in. He waits for Iceberg and then askes the ring announcer for a mic. He get's it and pats the frightened announcer on the back. Ice J lifts the mic up to his mouth to speak.}
Ice J: Well Ken Saturn finally you respond. You really shouldn't be making matches for next week. We all know you won't come out of that cage alive. And you think that you'll come out with the belt?? This belt?? MY BELT!! No you won't and you know why? Because it's mine. Says right there on it, TWO World Champ.
Otherwise the best. And who's the best, Ken? I am!! Not you, no you're not the best. And don't think you even deserve this shot
'cuz you don't. All you deserve is an ass-whoopin' for beating me. And who's going to give you that ass-whoopin'?? Me. Ice J.
I will and ya know why because you beat me. I admit it. I know you had that big deal of help from Scorpion but what does it matter, you got the W. You did and with that win comes a follow up match. Or a re-match. And it just so happens that I have this great belt right here. And if I need to dangle it infront of you to get my re-match then fine. So be it. And we all know that you've never seen gold or glory before so you'd jump at the oppertunity even though you know it's almost like sucide.
{Ice J looks over to Iceberg and and tells him something. No-one can hear him because he's muffed the mic. Iceberg runs back to the lockerroom to get something. As Iceberg comes back he's got a book in it. It's a brown book with gold letters on it. It's looks very heavy but both Iceberg and now Ice J who has it now are carrying it with ease.}
Ice J: Well everyone as a few of you might have guessed this book infront of me is a dictionary. I am going to tell Ken Saturn what a "SuperStar" is. Because according to one of his latest interviews I'm gonna find out on Tuesday.
{crowd laughs} Ice J: But then I figured that I'll be too busy beatin' on Ken Saturn to find out so I decided to find out now.
{crowd laughs more} Ice J: Iceberg drum roll please.... The definition of "Superstar" is: "(a) a star of great size, (b) term used for a person very skilled at there trade." Well Ken I don't think you fit either of those do ya?
Well it's okay atleast I'll be able to put an end to your misery on Tuesday. And about another thing you said, and I quote: "Tuesday
Mayhem, there's no way in hell I'm not leaving that cell without that damn title". So then I guess that you'll live the rest of your life in that cell. And that's because I am better than you!!!
No one beats Ice J. Not twice!!!!! Remember the Name Ice J!!!!!!!! BRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! {Ice J and Iceberg leave the ring with stern faces. Their music blasts and they each pump up the crowd with high-fives and roof-raises. When they duck through the curtain the fans still don't sit because they are just sooooo reved up from the two BoW members.}
