[CRITIQUE] Harley Dunn

1 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thought I'd chime in with the recent splurge of critques, and toss something up here from one of my underdeveloped characters, and see if I can get some feedback on it. The character hasn't been doing much, but he's in the e-fighting fed Murder 15, which just opened, so, yeah, he's fairly new to me. Thought you'd all like to take a look.
JW. *Jay-Dubbya thanks you in advance ------------------------------------------------ [In his cell.] HD: I guess it's time to air my feelings on Break Out. Well, I'm still the champ, if that's what you want to hear. I still am undefeated, now after two fights. Still am the toughest slackjawed sonuvabitch in M15.
Still have to fight a clown. [Dunn raises a smoke to his lips, and lights up, and continues to drawl as the cigarette hangs out the side of his lips.]
HD: I'm a good guy, I think. I only fight my opponents; I don't kill them. I do what's asked of me here, no doubt, and now I've gotta prove my worth by fighting a clown.
Well, I'm also not one to complain, so I ain't gonna continue with this here schpeel. I'm just a little worried, is all. See, I've got this title, the KODM championship, which was born on the waist of a clown. I beat him for it, and now, seemingly, another clown wants to take this belt away from me, so it seems to me like this belt is for clowns. Problem is....
....I ain't no clown. [Dunn drags on the smoke, takes out from between his lips, flicks the ashes off, and replaces it.]
HD: No, I ain't no clown. So then why the hell am I holding this title? Why am I holding a championship that so much belongs to a clown? It's because I've fought tooth-and-nail in two fights, and come out on top each time. Once against Damien Rage. Once against Kornbread.
Speaking of Kornbread, looks to me at Island Invasion I'm going to have to team up with the man. I sure as hell don't know how he's gotta be feelin'
about me tearin' him up at Break Out, but I know it can't be good. I do see one good thing coming out of that tag match, and that's my oppo- nents......Sean Cannell and the clown, Klumpy Kee. Cannell, you helped me score the win for this fight, which was help you and I both know I did not need, and I'm just lettin' you know that your help is going to go unappreciated. Since it wasn't asked for. And it wasn't needed.
[Another flick of the ashes.] HD: Kee......your a clown, so there's not much more to say other than that.......well, there's a lot more, but not of it's worth thinking about. Just fighting. That's all. See, that's the thing. You spend so much time sitting in these cells, doing nothing, so that all you end up doin' is thinkin'. I'm a thinking man, but my problem is that I'm also a fightin' man. And it's always thinkin' when you should be fightin'.
After all, isn't that what we're all here for, fellas? [Fade.]
