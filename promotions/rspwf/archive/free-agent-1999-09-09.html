"The Real Deal" Jonathon "The Machine" Gunz {15 - 4 - 0} -Former LSW [NWC] TV Champion -Former SCCW [NWC] TV Champion -Former NWC World J-Crown Champion *As the scene opens, we are looking directly at a television in Anytown, USA. It has spent many a year seated in the living room of this modest home, spent many hours providing joy and entertainment to the family within.*
*Instantly a blinding white light shatters the darkness that has covered the screen. Piercing each and every crevace of the screen before you, consuming all, and leaving the viewer no option but to cover his eyes and attempt to regain some of his vision.*
*Have we died? Is this the "light at the end of the tunnel" that has been whispered about since the beginning of time? Perhaps sometime during our viewing we have passed through the corridor of animation and begun our journey down the path to the unknown. Maybe all the religous forboding concerning the coming of the new millenium is true, and the time that the Earth will fall into mass chaos and destruction is finally at hand...*
*Of course, it could also just be time to get the damn TV fixed.* *Our eyes are transfixed on the spectacle of the light. Mesmerizing and hypnotizing, we are drawn towards the light...though we may not exactly know why. As we stare closer and closer at the light, our eyes are beginning to play games with us. We begin to see motion within the aurora before us, we see life and death in a continual struggle, and we are almost sure that we're witnessing something that is beyond our understanding...*
*Perhaps it's time to lay off the caffeine and the drugs.* *Finally, from deep within the recesses of the light, a voice emerges. Booming, authoritarian, omnipotent...could this be the Eternal himself? Could our quaint little television be the vessel of His message? The voice rips through the silence of the empty room....*
Voice: You don't know me.... *The voice grips our soul as if His hand had reached out and clinched it physically, waiting, hanging, anticipating the next words.* Voice: But, your mother calls me "The Real Deal". *Huh? "The Real Deal"? Gimme a break! This isn't the almighty, it's...it's...it's...Jonathon Gunz for goodness sakes. The "Malcontented Miscreant", "The Master of Disaster", "The Cardinal of Chaos", "The Prince of
Pulverization". TURN THE FREAKIN' CHANNEL!!!*
*A mysterious hand rises with the remote control and begins to hit the channel button. The numbers in the right corner of the screen begin to scroll up, but the picture doesn't change.*
Gunz: Don't bother attempting to change the channel, I'm on all one hundred and six of them.
*Slowly the light begins to fade and faintly we are able to make out the silhouette of a man.* Gunz: The name, for those of you who have remained stuck in your trailer for the last year, is Jonathon Gunz. "The Real Deal", "The Machine", the most exciting personality to have ever graced the National Wrestling Council...and if you're between the ages of 1 and 4...I may also be your father.
Regardless, for the last year I've excited crowds around the state of Texas. I've bitch-slapped the likes of Drake Ashida, Jailbreak, and Ray Pierce...and still you don't know me. But..."Toe-Jam" Tomas Bailey, this Saturday night you're going to get an up close and personal introduction to the "Shakespeare of the Squared Circle".
*The light receeds more and details about the man that stands before us are revealed. His shoulder length, jet black hair finally begins to come into view. Along with his perfectly tanned skin that is rapped ever so tightly around a finely sculpted body.*
Gunz: That's right Mr. Bailey, the billing may read "Wonder Dog" Woobs as the challenger to the J-Crown...but you'll find out soon enough that your concerns will be turned towards "The Real Deal". You see Tomas, I am the epitome of athletic excellence, I look better than you do in a pair of tights, and I'm the man that's going to show you that your delusions of grandeur can come true.
You don't know me...but, call me "The Real Deal". I'll be the one that's shoving my foot up your tight little ass.
*The light behind Gunz is fading even further now. We can now make out the handsome, sharp features of his cheeks and his jaw...along with a pair of piercing steel green eyes.*
Gunz: Call me "The Real Deal" because I am the geniune article. Nowhere else on this planet are you going to find so much talent, athletic ability, and sex appeal all wrapped up in a package that is straight up built for bitch-slapping people like you.
Indeed, call me "The Real Deal", Tomas. The man that is going to make you famous, the man that is going to allow your greasy, Upstate New York ass into the same ring with the poetry in motion that is myself. Having to play down to your insignificant ring abilities will be a test for me, Tomas, but...that's what life's like when you're "The Real Deal".
*The light is continually fading, faster and faster, growing so dim that now Jonathon Gunz is beginning to fade from sight completely.* Gunz: Tomas Bailey, you insignificant pimple on the ass of life, come Saturday night all your insecurities will be realized. Another title match, another
J-Crown champion fallen... I am "The Real Deal", Tomas. But, the question is... *The light fades completely now, leaving us in darkness...* Gunz: What the f**k are you gonna do about it? *The screen on the quaint little tv returns to normal and static ends our transmission...* *END*
