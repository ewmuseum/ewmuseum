Voice Over: The street lights are lit up as it is time for rest and relaxation at the Nagpal house. The birds have all gone to sleep, the cars have stopped passing by, and the people have all quited down for a goodnights sleep...that is expect for one person, and that is newcommer to the GFWA, Bill Bluebie. Bill and his camera man Pete are going to suprise Ameet Nagpal here tonight, and

{Camera cuts onto Bill who is wearing all the camouflage gear that he needs. He's got the pants, the shirt, and he's even got the two black streaks that cover his face. The scene looks like it's taken stright from an old Vietnam film. The man from Hunnington Beach is in the belly position as he's making it to his front door.}
Bill: Hey everyone! I'm Bill Bluebie, and tonight I'm going to thank Ameet for letting me into his fed. I can't wait to see the look on his face when I suprise him with a thank you song from me to him. Follow me...

Bill: Okay the coast is still clear. Now I'm pretty sure he keeps his key underneath the floor mat here, and...does he? YES!! Ameet good place to hide a key! Now lets go inside.
{Bill opens the door ever so quietly, and sneaks in. He tips toes over to the staircase and stops.}

OH AMEET!!! GIVE IT TO ME!!!! Bill: Boy is he going to be suprised when he see's me! I think it's coming from that door. Lets bust open the door like CHiP's. Remember CHiP's? With Ponch and
John? Ready...1!! 2!! 3!!!

Bill is still talking to the camera as they run...}
Bill: This fed.....should....be.....lots...of fun....whew.... can't wait!!!
{The picture then freezes, and it cuts back to the show.}
