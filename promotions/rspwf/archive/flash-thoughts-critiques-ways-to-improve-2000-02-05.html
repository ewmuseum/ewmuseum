[Flash] Thoughts, critiques, ways to improve..

5 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well damn, it's been helluva long time since I posted a flash on here. So,
I figured what the hay... Tell me what you think of CIW's own Vincent Zane.
Yes, he's a nutball... :) Gracias.... ------------------------------- [The camera fades in and standing there fiddling with his tie, making other last minute adjustments as well, is CIW Interviewer Danny Peterson. He seems a little neverous and is standing up against a white washed brickwall, he holds up the mic to his face and speaks into it rather quickly.]
DP: Hey once again.. I'm Danny Peterson and I'm going to be getting a quick word with one of the bigger men here in CIW. That's right, none other then
"The Machine" Vincent Zane.
[Almost on que Zane walks towards Peterson. Zane is well over a foot taller then Peterson, and this causes Danny to take one step back as he just is in awe of the size of this man. Zane himself is wearing a black shirt which simply reads "I'm going Nucking Futs", Zane's hair is dyed the color red and seems to just jet out all over the place. It isn't much longer then a buzz cut, but it still could use a good combing. His eyes are fixed right on
Danny and an evil grin grows upon his face.]
DP: Vince, it seems that the CIW isn't going to waste anytime with you and have placed you against one of the top wrestlers in Pedro Kozaro. Care to share some thoughts on this?
[Danny reaches up to put the mic in the face of Zane.] VZ: All I hear, all they tell me, is PED-RO PED-RO! Over and over again. In my sleep they come to me and wake me up, as they chant this name. I do all
I can to escape, but they find me anyways.
[In a confused tone] DP: Who are they? [Vince holds his index finger to his mouth] VZ: Shhhhhh... If you be really quiet you can hear the voices. Listen to them as they chant PED-RO PED-RO, listen to them taunt me.
[Very stupidly, Danny responds quickly] DR: Uh.. I don't hear any voices. [Zane turns his head away from the camera and looks straight down upon, a now very scared, Danny Peterson. He gets right into the face of Danny, who is shaking.]
VZ: PED-RO PED-RO PED-RO. Yes, I hear it too. I hate the name of Pedro
Kozaro, it haunts my dreams, it pushes me to the edge and it echos in my ears. The name dances across my brain, and just as I go to stop it.. it moves somewhere else. However, I have figured out one way to stop the cries, the laughter, and the screams..
[A sadistic smile grows across his face.] End Pedro Kozaro. DR: So uh, do you have any words for any of the other members of the tournament? [The eyes of Zane stay fixed on the camera, and he does not even blink for a second as he says.]
VZ: Just one word... PED-RO. [The six foot seven monster walks off with an evil grin on his face as the camera zooms back onto Danny Peterson.]
DR: Well uh.. I know who I'm picking to win the tournament, back to you guys. [The camera fades to black as Danny throws his arms into the air as if to say 'What the hell was that?']
