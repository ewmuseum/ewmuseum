USCW Flash Central 5/4/98 Pt. 2

5 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Delinquent Daniel Dare The Horsemen Alliance (2) Daniel Stephens VooDoo Spectre -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (Delinquent is sitting at home wearing his Delinquent shirt) Delinquent: This week has been a big week for me. I have decided to back off
Viper for a while and come back after I have made a name for myself. I have been talking with wrestlers and have signed a deal with some of them and soon you will see yours truly hanging with a bunch of superior grapplers. I have been waiting almost all my life for a great fed like this and have set my sights on the small things FIRST. I doubt I will be dealing with the small things for a very long time. I am willing to wait my turn to get what I deserve. I have been tuning my skills and I feel better than I ever have in my whole life.
(He lifts his arm into the air and moves it all around) Delinquent: Painless...the ice fixed it. So now I am not only in the best shape, I am also 100% health. On the other hand the only thing making me sick is the complaining I hear day in and day out...it bores me. I don't have much to complain about, though, except for being robbed at the Battle Bowl. I am gonna give up part of my old in your face attitude and start being a wrestler.
I have had experience, I have had partners with experience and some without, I have won matches, lost matches and even been the main eventer at pay per views.
I am not only a good physical combatant, but I have the heart to clean up in the ring. I have only had one real match so far, but there will be many more to come for Delinquent. I expect to see some new wrestlers enter the federation with as much perseverance and ambition as I have. These will be the wrestlers that I will want to hang with, and already I have found some people to join in the USCW, as I mentioned earlier. It is obviously a stable that
NONE of you would expect...I will be joining a stable that has great wrestlers that I have noted in the past. These wrestlers have had few chances to prove their true talents lately, but I still believe we can get somewhere.
(Delinquent grabs the TV remote and turns the TV on, on the screen you see two men wrestling in the ring)
Delinquent: See that? That is when I finally got my chance to prove myself against the world title holder.
(The Delinquent is picked up and thrown out of the ring and into a full metal table. Then, his opponent follows him out and beats his head on the table, he rolls him back in the ring and pins.)
Delinquent: I blew my only chance right there...when I let him pick me up and throw me out the ring. I am not gonna let that sort of thing happen to me in this federation...
(He turns off the TV when it zooms in on Delinquent's bleeding head) Delinquent: I have been waiting and waiting and waiting, and training and training and training, until finally Spicer and the committee took me out of the training center, and put me in with the big boys. I have to prove why I was put in here with the big boys, and prove to everyone that Delinquent is not a quitter or loser. So, here starts the new Delinquent in his new fed...USCW...
(camera fades) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- <Daniel Dare, the "Legend of Legends," is lying down on the beautiful sands of Virginia Beach. He is wearing just a black bathing suit with the word "Dare" written all over in silver letters. The greatest wrestler of all time is watching as the sun magestically comes down. The sky slowly changes to darker and darker shades of orange and black. The camera zooms up to Dare's face as several powerful waves are seen crashing to shore>
DANIEL DARE: "<smiling> What a moment it was! The flower pot heard 'round the world. Do you remember it, kiddies? Viper sure does <starts cackling>. How did it feel Viper? How did it feel to get the beating of your god damned laugh? <angrily> How the HELL did it feel to get what has been coming to you for almost a year. How did it feel to be handled like a FUCKING rag doll? I HOPE IT WAS AS GOOD FOR YOU AS IT WAS FOR ME!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!"
"<Hatefully> You and I both know what happened almost a year ago. We both know the truth, Viper. We both know that I have ALWAYS had your number. In this business, you have ALWAYS had to take a backseat to
Daniel Dare. Don't you EVER fucking forget that!" "Why is Viper such a 'big star' in professional wrestling? <angrily> I'll tell you why! I MADE HIM! If it wasn't for me, he would still be a two bit jabronie loading toilet paper into remodeled ice cream truck in the middle of Iowa. If it wasn't for Daniel Dare, Viper would not be where he is today. It was because of me that his name got into the news.
The feud that I developed with that man is what made him something in this business. He has no one to thank but me, for all that he has accomplished in the past 10 or 11 months. Because of our matches, because of our feud, I turned a piece of shit garden snake into one of the most feared rattlers in the land."
<Dare pauses for a moment> "You see folks, the Viper that once had to lick the asses of greedy Iowan promoters just to be on the second match of the card, never died. The Viper that had to beg and plead and lick booty just to make it to the lower midcard ranks is still very much alive. Viper may be fooling some of the people some of the time with his 'big man superstar' act, but those who are actually IN this business know the truth about Serpent
Boy. They know and I know that he realizes that he doesn't belong in the ranks of true superstars like Daniel Dare, or USCW President, Rob
Spicer. The man behind the Snake realizes that he is nothing but a two bit flash in the pan. He does not feel comfortable with his 'superstar'
status, because he knows that he did not work to get where he is today. He realizes deep in his heart that he rode the coattails of the 'Legend of Legends' to get to where he is in 1998."
"Viper, my mission here is simple. I have come here to EXPOSE you. Soon every wrestling fan and promoter walking the face of this earth won't see a triumphant snake man who holds the banner of 'WTF' proudly.
They will see a weak, snivelling coward, who rides on the back of other people's humps to get fame and fortune. They will see a man with extraordinarily weak interview skills, a shabby workrate, and very little knowledge of how this industry truly works. In a nutshell, they will see Viper for what he really is, HIMSELF!"
<Dare picks up a seashell and crushes it into a million pieces of with his bare right hand>
"That seashell will soon be your TESTICLES <starts laughing hysterically>. Viper, when I lay you out with my TESTICLE SQUEEZE, the entire wrestling world will see you for what you truly are, a piece of garden dung. That USCW World Heavyweight Title that you wear around your waist belongs to ME. Like I said before, I'll go through each and every one of the pitiful jackasses that call themselves USCW wrestlers, just to get to the snake man. One by one, I'll take them ALL out with the
Squeeze. NO ONE can escape its devastating effects, and NO ONE has ever been the same after being on the receiving end. The entire USCW has just become my personal snake pit. Viper, I hope you realize that my path to you and the title will cause a federation wide massacre. Ha! Ha! Ha!"
<Laughing maniacally, Dare grabs some sand with his fists and throws it right at the camera, as the screen fades to black>
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (Scene: "Detroit" Diesel's garage.) "Big Man" Mountain: Well USCW, how do you like them apples?! There's a new group in town! The Horsemen Alliance! And don't you forget it.
"Detroit" Diesel Jones: We're new and improved. And we're not done yet! "Little" Joe Mountain: We've still got a thing or two to throw at you. But why ruin the surprise.
"Big Man" Mountain: It just started with May Day. The Horsemen Alliance arrived to wreak havoc. And I may not have won, but I dodn't fare so bad in the Battlebowl. I gave Chamberlain a face full of Mat. And Matthews, you may have thrown me out, but I took you with me. If you don't like it, I'd be willing to go one on one in the ring.
The Horsemen Alliance: And that's all we wrote! (The screen fades to black.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (Scene: "Detroit" Diesel garage) "Big Man" Mountain: My oh my, two matches in one night. I get to face the
Mighty Maxwell Chamberlain. What? You can't handle that I rubbed your nose in the mat at the Battle Bowl and threw you out? Well face it, we're going to meet in the ring so I can rub your nose in it again!
"Little" Joe Mountain: And we're going to meet those loser, Da Lench Mob. What a joke. "Detroit" Diesel Jones: Just face it your going to suffer pain again as we grind you in to the mat! Because...
The Horsemen Alliance: That's all you need to know! (The screen fades to black.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- <you see daniel stephens> stephens: did stephens tell you or what? he told you that he would surprise you all in the battle bowl. just look at him if you want to see all the talent what is he weighing, about 175 or something? damn, he was the littlest man in the battle bowl and finished second. by just knowing the weight disadvantage that he had to overcome and amount of time he has been wrestling, you would never of thought he would of mad it that far. but he showed the world that daniel stephens is truly the future legend. no lightweight has ever done so well in a battle bowl or in any other type of royal rumble match. stephens is not trying to brag, but if you had done as greatly as he did, you would just be making sure that everyone new about it, like he is. bullet, at may day stephens had try to undue a little of what wrong you did when you came in and helped sharpe retain the light weight title. now the only thing that stephens has left to do that will completely undue the wrong which you did is for him to win the title. just like he should have the night that you cheated me out of it and lost everyones respect.

VooDoo Spectre: Canadian...You have messed with others...but you messed with the wrong man...Finally...A chance to use the real power of VooDoo...and not a better opponent to test it on...You will be my first opponent...and you will be
the first to be cursed upon. See this voodoo doll? That's you...I and I will use it...by all means..necessary. I plan...to demolish all my opponents...one by one....nobody can stop me this time...not you...not the President...not the world...be prepared....and bad luck...to you...Mwahahahhahahaaa! As for the rest of you USCW wrestlers...some of you...have great talent, and I would be honored to join...but now that I am here...you will see that things will
CHANGE! Those who are my friends...will never be harmed by voodoo...those who are enemies...shall suffer. I am ready to tear apart any wrestler who opposes me or my ways...do any of you have what it takes to stop voodoo? None of you...None of you...NONE! MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAH! Now that I am here...you all might as well...befriend me...or be destroyed...be prepared for utter and eternal damnation...a new era has begun in the USCW...the era...of VOODOO!!!!
