"Hardcore" Bobby Storm sample RP (SCRA)

28 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A black screen appears. It stays until the word, in baby blue letters, pops up on the screen. The word say's "HARDCORE" and then a familiar voice speaks.]
V - "It's time to get hardcore" [The black screen fades out and now, it becomes a messed up locker room. There are towels, clothes, CD's and food lying all over the ground. There is still a sound of some stuff being shuffled around but no one can be seen yet. The camera looks up to see a Pamela Lee Anderson poster, up on the wall. He is sitting in a bed with her head tilted back and she doesn't have many clothes on. The camera stays on the picture for a bit and the shuffling sound has died down and sounds of something being thrown are heard. The camera pans over a dart board with a picture of "Deacon"
Nicolas Joseph on it. Suddenly, a dart flies and hits Nicolas right in the forehead. Laughter is heard as the camera turns around to see that it was
"Hardcore" Bobby Storm throwing the darts. Bobby is wearing blue jeans, black shoes and has on a white t-shirt that has the anarchy symbol on it.
He brown hair is all wet and slicked back. Bobby smiles at the camera. BS - "Talk about getting hardcore. Just thank god it isn't a real person or else I would get in big trouble. Right Alex? If it was really Nicolas, well, I don't think he would be standing there that long but after that head injury, well, you never know."
[Bobby Storm smiles at his attempt at a joke and throws another dart. The camera does not show where the dart went but Bobby saw.]
BS - "Right in the mouth. So now he'll just shutup! Ah. My SCRAPS debut. And what a way to start my career off than beating a former, SCRA World
Champ like Gangs, no wait, Nate Manch, no wait again."
[Bobby Storm throws another dart.] BS - "Nicolas Joseph. That's right. Excuse me, I just lost my head for a second but I guess I shouldn't be the one to talk about losing my head. I don't think that Nicolas is the only problem here in the SCRA. How about this Masked Outlaw. Is it really that hard to unmask him? I just hope someone in that 5-man battle royal can. Now, I am not saying that I am going to go to him and try but for a million dollars, I am going to think about it for a bit."
[Bobby Storm begins to look like he is thinking real hard. He begins to tap the side of his head and tap his feet but then forgets about it. He looks to the camera again.]
BS - "Now, another guy with problems, according to his wife is Derek
Mota. She says you would rather sleep with that North American title of yours than sleep with her. I guess cause the title doesn't care how bad you are. Hell, you are so bad in bed that I have to keep going over, twice a week just so your wife doesn't have to do it all by herself. Not that I mind or anything but I am just surprised that you guys are still married.
Your wife is a very nice girl. I could have any girl I want but, Derek, your wife came to me for love and well, I just had to give it. I find that it is just wrong when she doesn't get some at home so to all you ladies out there. If you are having problems with your husband like Mrs. Mota, then well you give me a call and I can arrange something and if your bubble head boyfriend finds out, well, I am a wrestler. And I can kick his ass so hard that you wouldn't have to worry about him anymore and you can move on with your life. If you want a piece of the next North American champion, well then you just call my number which is at the bottom on the screen right now."
[Bobby Storm looks down, as if he can see the phone number. It is 1-900-555-OYAH] BS - "Oh yeah baby!" [Two great looking girls come from off camera. They are wearing nothing but towels and their hair is all wet.]
G1 - "Hey Bobby, what is taking you so long out here? I thought we were going to" [The girl notices the camera and so she whispers it in Bobby Storm's ear. Bobby Storm smiles and begins to laugh. Both of the girls take his arms. One of the girls looks at the camera.] G2 - "Can you leave that thing here for awhile?" G1 - "Let's go Bobby!" [Bobby Storm closes his eyes. He smiles and then opens his eyes again.] BS - "Alright ladies, I will be there in a second." [The girls go off camera. Bobby sighs and smiles at the camera.] BS - "Just some customers of mine. Well, it looks like we have no more time to chat. I think the girls are getting a little restless now. It's time to get HARDCORE!"
