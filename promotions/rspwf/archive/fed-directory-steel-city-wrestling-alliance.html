Steel City Wrestling Alliance (SCWA) Website: http://scwa.hypermart.net Owner: Guzz Description: The SCWA is a small, independent promotion run out of an abandoned steel mill in Pittsburgh, PA. With a promoter deadset on recreating the oldschool 80's feel with a puro based atmosphere. Our focus is directed primarily on the actual wrestling and feuds built around head on competition and heated rivalries. Keynote Members: n/a Federation Champion: Vacant Flash Show Descripition: Unlike an email fed, SCWA is run on a message board system with a three RP maximum limit. This creates a head-to-head RP battle opposed to a shoot and miss email style. We feel it contributes to better interaction between handlers and more exciting feud Regular Show Description: Steel City 'After Dark' is a weekly show based out of the Blackwater Asylum in downtown Pittsburgh, PA. The building itself is a multi level, abandoned steel mill that was purchased by an unknown proprietor. PPV Description: Supercards are run on average every 4-6 weeks although we treat them more as a Super 'After Dark' so to speak where small feuds come to a close and larger feuds are ignited and refined as we close in our PPV's which are run once every three months.
