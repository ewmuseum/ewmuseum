Roleplay for Critique

29 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<...On a lonely beach late at night, the camera comes into focus, the waves are splashing up on the shore, and going back just as fast as they came, the sea gulls are non apparent but one man stands there watching the waves crash in. He begins walking along the shore line as the water splashes upon his feet just enough...he sees the camera as it comes up. He keeps walking in silence. he stops as he sees a small land crab, he bends over and watches it, he looks upon for a couple of seconds and stands back up as he keeps walking along. Again he walks in silence not breaking the quiet repetition of the waves...he comes up to a bed of rocks, he climbs up high on them and sits down and pears out. He looks down at the camera and back out to the ocean. He sits for a couple of more minutes and then shatters the silence with is words...>
Kyle Moon: These waves symbolize something...they symbolize me. These waves come crashing into the shore, and then they come right back. Every Sunday
I go out and fight...and then the next Sunday I come right back. Once the outcome wasn't in my favor, a mishap on my partners part made me have a blemish on my record...a blemish that will never go away. but as that loss is on my record, it constantly reminds me that I am not immortal, I can be taken down.
Not easily though, I had to be blind sided by a man I trusted...a man that I considered a friend. A man I will probably never be able to forgive again...a man that _STILL_ calls me a friend. But even though I have that loss, I lived to tell about it and lived to fight another day. I went out like a warrior should...and I got right back up and beat my opponent Bullet. Then, I was faced with another challenge, I face four men, one of those men being the one who I consider contributed to that loss, and I forever hold him in part, responsible.
He shall pay for what he did and so will the two others. But they shall be dealt with at a later time, this Sunday, I shall crash upon the beach again, I shall climb into a steel cage and face four men for a title shot, that title shot will be for the Toughman Championship Title...held by "Mr. Wonderful"
Chuck Williams. But I cannot worry about Chuck just yet, I have four very capable men to face, these men are Erebus, Lanier "Strictly Business" Riley,
Tony "The Dream" Morris...and my least favorite "Devastating" Damon Knight.
This man is the man who I hold partly responsible, this man shall be locked in a cage with me and I shall get my revenge, and what is life without revenge?
It's pointless. Mr. Knight has repeatedly said comments that deeply angry me, and he shall be deeply hurt for his words. No man makes a mockery out of me...and gets away with it, _NO_ MAN_....ever. And I will not let it happen now. Yet i despise Damon Knight, I know he is a contender in this match, when first assigned, I thought this match would be a close one, but I soon realized that Morris, Erebus, and Riley did not care for this line up, and I soon went to work on Knight, and in that ring, I will show you what I have been preparing...and you will feel my wrath. Like it or not, someday, sometime, you must face the fact Knight...either face them now, or Sunday...which ever you like.
<...Kyle Moon its steadily on top of the rock as waves continually hit up against the rock and occasionally sprinkle on Kyle Moon, he does not move or budge any time this happens, he just looks out to the ocean and continues to speak out in to the world..>
Kyle Moon: The time has run out. The last grains of sand have fallen through the hourglass. And the final ticks of the clock are ticking away. You have very little time to do what is needed in preparation for this match and I would like to give you some advice from one friend to another...go out and do what you want to do, spend your last moments with your kids if you have them, go out with your wife if you have one....do the things you love before it is too late, cause when you enter that cage with me, that is when it will be too late, there will be no way out, no chances of running out, no way of escape. You shall be stuck with me, and I will have no mercy on any soul.
People don't realize the danger they will enter in when they meet me, they think of me as an incapable fool, but soon I will change everyone's thought on me. For many people life is full of challenges. Different roads that they can take that can alter their life's forever, entering a ring with me can end your life forever. When people look at the lineup for each card and see their name placed against mine. The people laugh, but as the ending bell rings, I am the one left laughing while they are left laying in the middle of the blood stained ring. Even though I am just getting started here in the IWF, does not mean I have not had any experience in the ring. I have been around and back in many different feds, though people have not heard of me, does not make weak, or not dangerous. For I am as powerful, dangerous, and most experienced as they come,
I know what to do in the ring, I'm no stranger at that. I know for some reasons people tend to wave me off. But now, they will stop, once I defeat these four men, and move on to Chuck Williams, people will realize who they are dealing with. And Kyle Moon will be recognized throughout IWF as an elite wrestler. I know this can be done, and I know it will be done. I learned a lot of things since my wrestling career started and I am not finished learning either. I have a lot to accomplish and learn before this ever finishes, I will be on the top someday...I will be sitting on top of the world. Who knows how long it will take, maybe a couple of weeks, maybe months or even years, but I know something, it won't come easy. I know that, and I think you _HAVE_ to know that to make it in this business, and so far I have been doing just fine. But this week, I plan on make another stab at my way to the top. I face four very good men, and they face a very great wrestler. Only one man will prevail, only one man will walk out. And I plan on me being that man.
Kyle Moon: Day after day men are plagued with challenges, and with those challenges face many other challenges, can they take the pressure? Or do they buckle in the struggle to perform? As Erebus did when his stable fell before his own eyes. Dark World Order was no stable at all, it stood for a matter of days and then just went back in do dormancy people thought they could bring it back to defeat the Lords of Darkness, not one battle took place, no one prick of blood was pricked, and yet, Lords of Darkness still stands as Dark World
Order lays in rubble. What will happen now? As normal LoD will go back to it's business like normal and dWo....who knows, and who cares? People have been trying to take me down forever, Dusk tried...he failed. Then "The Franchise"
Tommy Stylz put forth no effort and fell like Dusk. Then I had my first Tag match in the IWF...that is where I fell. Never again shall I ever tag team again. For being a tag team you have to have trust in one another, and trust is something I believe in not and never will again. Then there was Bullet. he fell just the same. I warned those three men about the dangers of stepping in the ring with me...and yet each of their pride got the better of them. That was a big mistake that proved fatal for Dusk and nearly the same for Stylz. Bullet shall fight another day. And I advise the ones that did survive to let it be a valuable lesson for them: Don't let ignorance cloud your better judgment, lest you find yourself faced with something you cannot beat. These three men learned that rule the hard way. But about my tag match. Something I have to say about that. "Devastating" Damon Knight...Aswan Jaguar, you two did not defeat me. I am willing to take each of you head on, one on one. I am willing to prove that
I am better than you two. But yet, my partner defeat me. He decided I would not go on to the other rounds, he decided I didn't deserve that win. Well, I know I do, and if he ever decides to take something out on me, do it the manly way and do to my face. Not my back. if you ever want a match with me, bring it on, cause I am ready for you any day of the week. And once I get me revenge, that loss shall hold no substantial meaning. Once I give my retribution...I will be happy, but until then, I shall ponder of what things could have been...how things shall become. I know I could have those belts if Crimson just would have put forth effort and least of all jumped me. But he knows he can't get away with this, and he knows his is coming. I am just waiting for the right time.
Damon, you claim to have successfully beaten me, but I beg to differ, like I have said, you did not beat me, Crimson did, and I will prove that in the ring, for you know you cannot beat me, I know this, you do too. So once I do beat you down, that will prove it all right there, I am the stronger man here, and you were just in a lucky situation. But I have one thing more to say to DDK...the only thing devastating my friend, will be your loss.
<..Kyle Moon climbs off of the rock and begins to walk along the shore again....> Kyle Moon: As the sun sets each day things new are bound to happen. New things bound to happen is inevitable, the future cannot be stopped, but yet we should not care about the future, we should live for the present. For I am not basing this match on the title shot, I am basing it on the fact that I have a challenge placed in front of me...now deal with it. And I shall, for Erebus I feel is no challenge at all. Sure he is a very good wrestler, but yet he does not compare to me...he does not come close. Now, Erebus, this question plagues me, why do you choose not to compete in this match? You've said nothing...ignored everything. Why? Are you that completely petrified of me that you choose not to wrestle? You've said a total of one sentence on me...and nothing else. I am very disappointed in your showing, I thought you had more passion and desire for wrestling. But yet I was proved wrong and yet...I learned another thing. That learning process keeps popping up everywhere. Have you noticed something there? Life is a learning process. And by skipping out on matches does not provide a learning experience for anyone. And then there is
Lanier "Strictly Business" Riley. this man has enough dignity to accept this match and then go off and dunk his head in water so he can't listen, nor respond to anything we have to say. Why did you even take this match? Why do you continue to waste my time? You are a disgrace to wrestling at best. But then there is a man with enough audacity to squirm his way in to a very important match. Tony "The Dream" Morris, how he is in this match amazes me, why he is in this match puzzles me. But one thing I do know, climbing into that cage shall prove fatal. But when you reach that ring and I am waiting for you to come down that aisle, your music blaring or whatnot, you shall climb in to that ring with all the confidence in the world, but when that bell rings and I look up into your eyes, that tiny pain in your chest shall be your confidence breaking in two. That is when you shall know fear, that is when you shall know death. My eyes will peer up, the cold stare of a heartless man looking deep within your soul, that is when you will know, that is when you will know....there's a Bad Moon Rising.
<..Kyle Moon stops walking and looks into the camera. The cameraman begins to back off as he sees a waves coming in at a very big size. Kyle Moon turns around and sees it and stands his ground. The waves dies out by the time it reaches Kyle Moon as he chuckles it off...the camera fades to black..>
