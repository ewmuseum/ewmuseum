Epic Wrestling (Epic) Website: http://epic.slike.com Owner: [name withheld] Description: Epic Wrestling is a fantasy wrestling website created for the sole purpose of the entertainment and enjoyment for those who are apart of our roster. Unlike, most angle based eFederations; Epic Wrestling features no roleplaying at all. Since we feature no roleplay packets, our roster uses storylines, results, and segments to get their character across to the reader. Our staff works alongside the roster, giving them the creative freedom to input their thoughts on the storylines, results, segments, writing, and booking that we put out on our website. We feel by including them in the creative process of their characters this allows them to better get across their character and how they want that character to be portrayed in the eyes of the reader. Keynote Members: ''The Icon'' Ben Archer (World Heavyweight Champion), Sam Potright (North American Champion), ''The Forgotten Angel'' Seth Anson, ''The Epitome of Greatness'' Onslaught, Shawn Arrows, and Doozer. Federation Champion: ''The Icon'' Ben Archer Regular Show Description: Epic Wrestling operates using two shows a week and one major pay per view after every two months. During this period, storylines that we have worked on will be unfolded to the reader. PPV Description: We opted for a pay per view after every two months, unlike the WWF's month style of one each month is we feel such an approach strengthens the meaning of our pay per views rather.
