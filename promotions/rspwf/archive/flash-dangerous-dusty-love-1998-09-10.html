-[FLASH]- "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE

10 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
_PERFECTION CONNECTION_ [The scene opens up on a room that appears to be a massage parlor. The camera scans over the room and we see a lady standing next to man that is laying on one of the massage beds. The man appears to have a very nicely built body, long dirty-blonde hair, and he's got nothing on except for the towel that's draped over his buttox area. He's laying on his stomach and the woman is massaging his back and shoulders. The lady, then, stops and puts more lotion on her hands. She continues to rub his shoulders, when he turns his head in the direction of the camera. He smiles very widely and motions for the camera to get closer. As the camera gets closer, we notice that this man is none other than one of the best wrestlers that the ICW has to offer... "Dangerous"
Dusty Love. The camera, then, zooms in on his handsome, whisker-covered face as he begins to speak.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Looks like ya caught me at a bad time... But never-the-less, I have a few things to get off my chest. And since you're here... I'll take this time to do so. I wanna address my upcoming match that will take place on Friday Night Fallout. You see, Shane Diamond, Westside
Walavan, D.B. Bugg, and "Pretty Boy" Andrew appear to have a little bit of beef with the Perfection Connection. But ya see, what they don't understand is this
- We're named the Perfection Connection for a reason... And that reason is very simple. It doesn't take a brain surgeon to figure out. Guys, we call ourselves the Perfection Connection because we're just... Simply put...
_PERFECT!_ Just take a look at us. We've got the biggest and baddest man in the ICW... Tony Paseno. Not to mention, he's also the International Champion.
And Diamond, you should know more than the rest of us... That you're outta your league when you step in the ring with him. He's beaten you once and he'll damn sure do it again. Two weeks in a row? Is that what you want? Well, whether you want it or not... It _WILL_ happen, my friend.
[The camera zooms out to a full body shot of Dusty Love, who's still laying on the massage bed. The lady has worked her way down to his lower back now as he continues on.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: I'm not at all worried about this week. I'm not at all worried about Friday night. 'Cause I know, the people know, and you four guys that'll be across the ring from us know that we're bigger... We're stronger... And without a doubt, we're _DEFINANTLY_ smarter! You see... It might look like I'm laid back and relaxing here on this Thursday afternoon...
But actually, I'm in training. That's right... This is how I'm training for this match. That's how _SURE_ I am of not only myself but of my teammates.
Sure, they've got Westside Walavan, the Television Champion... But he's nothin' that a Super Jackhammer won't take care of. Then, you got D.B. Bugg.
A guy that says that I've made a mistake by tellin' him that I know nothin' about him. Well, Bugg... Think what you want... But I don't care how good you are or even who you are... I just know this - Your ass will be left layin'
in the middle of the ring on Friday night in your _OWN_ pool of blood! And that's a promise from the Perfection Connection!
[Dusty, then, puts a smile of conceit back on his face. He, then, begins to speak again.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Bugg... When you hit me in the back of the head, you should've just pulled a gun out and blew your own brains out. Because now you're gonna have to suffer the consequences... And that consequence is a
"Love You To Death" from me to you. But another guy I wanna address is a guy that calls himself "Pretty Boy" Andrew. You think you're pretty? Well, if you wanna _STAY_ pretty, then you won't even bother showin' up on Friday night...
Because if you do... I guarantee ya it _WON'T_ be when it's all said and done! You guys actually think that you can run us over? We're the new wave of wrestling... We're the new blood of the ICW... And we're coming to Hartford to prove every word of it.
[The lady that's giving the massage stops and walks out of the room. Dusty
Love sits up, with the towel covering him up... And begins to speak again.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Shane... Some people say that Diamonds last forever... But in your case... Diamond will last until Friday night. We thought you'd learn a lesson on last Fallout... But looks like we were wrong.
This week... You'll be beaten _AGAIN!_ And then, maybe you'll learn that you can't beat us... And maybe you'll finally just give up the tough-guy act.
It's not scaring the fans... It's not scaring Pillow... And it sure as hell ain't scarin' us. You'll be toned down a notch this week... And hopefully it'll sink into your thick head that we're the _BEST_ thing goin' today! And not even _YOU_ can stop us! Bugg... I've got a can of Raid for you.
Andrew... You stuck your nose in our business... And now I'ma have to knock it off your face. And Walavan... You'll definantly pay a dear, dear price.
You just better hope that mah boy, Paseno, don't get a hold of you. You took his belt... So, now he has to take it back... And in the process... He'll take a chunk of your ass! You guys don't belong in the same ring with guys of our caliber... And you'll find that out first hand on Fallout this week.
Because "The Love Man" and the Perfection Connection are coming to... "...LOVE... ...YOU... ...TO... ...DEATH!!!" [Dusty Love, then, grins a grin of conceit as the camera fades... To... Black...]
