[RP Critique] "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens

30 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please someone take the time to critique. I want to see what you guys think of my new character.
Scene: The camera opens up in what looks to be the middle of an enormous, park, filled with kids, parents, and all different kinds of people playing in the playground, playing sports, having picnics, and overall just having a good time. The camera approaches the playground and heads towards the swing sets.
As the camera focus' towards the swings sets you see none other then "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens. Jumping off the swing set and landing on both feet on the ground. He sees the camera and walks over to the cameraman.
"Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: Whats up man? You my groovy dawg? Cameraman: Huh? "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: Nevermind, dawg. Whats up, ma brotha? Cameraman: (with a confused look) Huh? "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: You not down with me, ma brotha. Slap me some 5, dawg. Cameraman: (still with a confused look) Huh? "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: I guess your not down with ebonics, ma brotha. Oh well. Its so cool man. Did u see that AWMC thingy? I jobbed! That was so tight. I'm so proud of it too. I believe that "Hardcock" Bruce Spencer deserved a grateful win over an underrated son of a b..........buffalo. Gotcha that time!!...........lemme guess.........huh? Am I right? Was that what you were going to say? I bet you were going to say that! I love jobbing, but i can't keep doing that or i'll get sent to the lower divisions of the AWMC also known as Amateur Wheelchair Mountain Coalition or possibly, All White Mother
Chairs, or maybe even A Wicked Mouth of Cokes. hehe i said cokes! Cool.
Cameraman: Am i in the right place? "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: What you talking about, fool? I'm the most jobbed superstar in pro wrestling, dammit, i mean i'm the most feared superstar in pro wrestling. Got it, chump?
Cameraman: I guess. "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: Hey dude!! What this, i think i got myself a girl! Scene: Stevens walks behind this gorgeous girl and proceeds to pinch her ass. The girl turns around and slaps Scotty in the face. Then some man of around
6'5 and 260 lbs comes up and begins to knock some sense into Scotty, pounding him and kick him on the ground. The guy finally leaves but not before Scotty winds up with some scratches on his face. He gets up and looks at the camera man with a smile.
"Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: I paid off that guy. It was all a set up........(Scotty looks over in that guy's direction, whom beat him up).....Later man.....thanks for the help. Anywayz, before my perfect set up for a hilarious scene, i was talking about my terrific, main event type job to one guy named "Hardcock" Bruce Spencer which i was really impressed by, then some so called masked jobbers came out and interupted our stunning match after
Spencer pinned me and canned us both, causing my anger to arise in a grateful manner. Grrrrrrrrrrr
Scene: Stevens walks over to two 8 year olds in the playground and DDTs them into the sand. The kids get up and begin to laugh as Stevens laughs with them and looks back at the cameraman.
"Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: Haha, no! I love playing with kitties which are also known as puppies. No, not those kinds of puppies, you perverts. Yo,
Robert d'Artois, i got the email that i was fighting you on the next Explosion or Madness card, and well I can't wait. I'll prolly end up jobbing to you again, but oh well, i'll just have to move up the ladder a little more in my quest for a number 1 contenders spot in the low level title contention. Not that it hurts me or anything. Mr. d'Artois, I'm a man of a few words, and well
I got a couple for ya. One smart fellow, he felt smart. Two smart fellows, they felt smart. Three smart fellows, they all felt smart. Now say that fast 6 times. Are you ready? I can't even do that. 1 fart smella, he smelt fart........go-----gosh darnit........its hard. One fart smella, he felt smart.
Gosh darnit. Oh well, i'm sick of doing it. Robert or can I call you Rob, did u know, that even if one of us thou shall lose, will move up thou ladder instead of move down thou ladder. Isn't that groovy, man? But anywayz, good luck to you in the match. Hopefully you will kick my sticken butt so I can, take my roody poo...........whoops, i've been watching too much TV. But anywayz, Mr. d'Artois, due to the whooping of the butt, i will be leashing out on the day of the card, i hope your not in the midst of this because you'll be the recieving end of a _SUNSHINE_ day. The Ay-dubbya-emm-see is here for awhile and i'm stepping into the ring, to do for the ay-dubbya-emm-see, what wrestling does for the world. Mwuahahahahahaha!!! Good luck my brotha.
Scene: The camera begins to fade to black but Scotty Stevens stops this from happening. "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens: And you think Sunshine is really to stop. Don't make me take another butt whipping. I'm Mr. Sunshine, Scotty Stevens, so when your in the ring and kicking my butt, look at me directly in the rear end, and well, remember the words of the Sunshine One: One smart fellow, he felt smart. Two smart fellows, they felt smart. Three smart fellows, they all felt smart. Have a good day Robert d'Artois and have plenty of sleep in the upcoming days.
Scene: The camera then fades to black. - The Big Jeffbowski Handler of: Yo MoMMa and - "SuperStar" Sean Copeland [PNWF, EWP] - "Sunshine" Scotty Stevens [AWMC, BTW] By the way: "I like eggs!" The Rock 4:20 says Know your Role, Smoke A Bowl!
