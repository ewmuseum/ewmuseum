Epitome of Cool RP/flash

21 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was bored and figured "Why not?" Anyhow, flames, critiques, compliments, ect. are welcome. I just wanna make something clear. Don't email me asking to join your fed. These guys and me are set with where we're at. :)
later, Roger [SCENE: We open to a lush view of a stop off area in Cade's Cove in the
Smokey Mountains of northeastern Tennessee. The leaves turn color as it's the perfect fall day. The scene cuts away to an open area on one of the many trails.]
[Dan "Cool Cat" Thomas is seated on a downed tree trunk, his legs outstretched with his hands taking the brunt of his weight. He has on a pair of blue jeans, white t-shirt, and Nike hiking boots. "Acme" Andrew
Sterling is standing just to the right of Dan Thomas in khaki pants, hiking boots, and a long sleeved white shirt. Both men are sporting sunglasses.]
AS: Ah yeah, there's nothing like nature to help cure the mind of all the ails that it's been feeling since the MLWO closed down. Ya know, for those of you who witnessed it, I'm sure you're going to be thinking that there's still a rift between the two Coolites.
[Andrew Sterling shakes his head.] AS: Not anymore. Dan and myself were able to come to terms with the problem. It's water under our bridge and the EoC is ready to reign as the kings of cool for a long time to come.
[Dan Thomas smirks looking like an idiot.] DT: Water under the bridge and the revolving door is ready to swing towards success as far as we're concerned. I'm sure that those who watched the MLWO productions may want to believe there's a rift between the pinncale of cool and the cool cat himself.
[Dan Thomas gets to his feet, but suddenly drops to his knees acting as if he's a pious man.]
DT: Father... Father Sterling... 'splain to these people... _'splain_ to
_these_ people what it is that makes the Epit-o-me _ooof_ _Coool_ the
_believers_ that they _is_!
[As if on cue, Andrew Sterling throws his hands in the air.] AS: Give me an _a_-_men_ brother! [Dan Thomas throws his hands in the air, still on his knees. In short, both guys look like complete fools now.]
DT: Amen, father! AS: I can't _hear_ you my son! Gimme an _A_-_MEN_! DT: _A_-_MEN_, father! AS: [doing some funky gesture as he speaks.] You see, _my_ son. It was
_wri_tten down in the Coolible that _the_ world... _the world_ was
_no_thing but darkness! Then _the_ lord said, "Let there be _LIGHT_!" [lowering his voice.] And so there was... DT: Bring it, father Sterling! _Bring_ _it_ _on_! Lemme hear what the lord did to the world and how he made Coolsville, father Sterling! I wanna know! I wanna be a _believer_!
[Andrew Sterling puts his hand on Dan Thomas' head.] AS: So my son, you shall, if you listen to the words of the good father
Sterling. [Andrew Sterling removes his hand.] AS: Then, on the _first_ day, _the_ lord _cre_-a-ted the place of _ultimate_ utopia! The place that _dreams_ are made of; _Coolsville_! The lord wasn't done... [shakes his head.] no _the_ lord wasn't _done_ with his _cre_-a-tion! You see, _the_ lord _wan_ted a people to _in_habit this place we know as Coolsville... [his voice lowers.] yes... yes he did.
[Dan Thomas fakes a few tears.] DT: Father Sterling... I _implore_ you... I _beg_ you to let _me_ know what _the_ lord did for this place of utopia, for the place that dreams are made of, for this place of _coolness_!
AS: My son, the lord gave this town the ultimate gift. He gave them something that the world was not yet ready for. [raises his voice in a progressive manner in this sentence.] _The_ lord gave them... _The_
_E_-pit-_o_-me _ooof_ _COOL_! [Dan Thomas jumps to his feet.] DT: [screaming] _Praise the lord_! _Praise the lord_! [Andrew Sterling turns back to the camera.] AS: Black Heaven... Black Heaven, _the_ lord has demanded that you pay for _your_ sins! _The_ lord has [gestures] _thrown_ down his _law_ upon
[points to the camera.] _thee_! He came to me... oh _yes_! _The_ lord came to me last night! [his voice starts to break like he's starting to sing what he's preaching. Dan Thomas hums in the background.] He said unto me, "Andrew... _father_ Sterlin'... them boys... them boys by the name of _Black Heaven_ done been bad! I need _you_! I _need_ _the_
_E_-pit-_o_-me _ooof_ _COOL_ to strike down _upon_ them with a _great_ and
_furious_ _ven_gance!" The light shone down upon me, I speak the truth!
DT: Bring it! _Bring it_! AS: I looked up... YES_A_! I _looked_ up _into_ that light, and I said
"_Lord_! I am your _chosen_! _Lord_! _We_ are your _chosen_! Black
Heaven... YES_A_! Black Heaven will have your _almighty_ hand of
_ven_gance laid _upon_ them!" Then I looked back up into that light and I said, "_Lord_! _What_ have these men done unto thee? _What_ has _Black
Heaven_ done _unto_ thee to warrant your _almighty_ wrath!?"
[Andrew Sterling looks back into the camera, mocking a look of utter contempt.] AS: The lord said to me... "They have been uncool..." [Dan Thomas sensationalizes as gasp as he drops to his knees, grabbing at his chest.] AS: I looked back up to the lord [looks to the sky] and said, "Lord, then let your chosen, the Epitome of Cool, show the wrong ways that the lambs have strayed upon." The lord said unto me, "Show them the right path,
Andrew. Show them how to be cool."
[Andrew Sterling takes off his sunglasses as Dan Thomas gets back to his feet, grinning from ear to ear.]
AS: It's _real_ simple, Black Heaven. On November first, the Epitome of
Cool becomes your living hell. It's all for one, _simple_ reason.
DT: We're cool... [Andrew Sterling smiles.] AS: ... and you're not! [EoC walk off camera as it fades to black.]
