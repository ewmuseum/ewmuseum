[free agent] "Dangerous" Dan Dixon

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm looking for a booked, original wrestler only, e-mail league with no more than two shows a week. Doesn't really matter if its established or not. This character is a heavyweight, but he's not very big, so if you're looking for a cruiser I would be willing to drop some pounds off him if the situation is right.
If you're interested in having me in your fed, post your fed's URL here, or drop me a line at [detail withheld] . -Name: "Dangerous" Dan Dixon aka "3D" -Hometown: Memphis, TN -Height: 6'2" -Weight: 237 -Alignment: Heel -Style: Technical -Description: Dixon is a caucasian with shoulder-length black hair, green eyes, and a clean-shaven face. He has a lightly tanned complexion, a toned physique, and, more often than not, an arrogant smirk on his face .
-Gimmick: Dan Dixon is an egomaniac. He's very talented, he knows it, and he doesn't mind telling any one who'll listen. He considers himself the consummate lady's man and "the greatest technical wrestler in wrestling history."
-Favorite moves: snap suplex northern lights suplex DDT figure four rear chinlock camel clutch backbreaker superplex brainbuster tombstone piledriver -Finisher: "The Danger Zone" An elevated Boston Crab, just like a Lion Tamer. -Ring Appearance: Dixon's ring attire is as follows: *Black tights with white lettering spelling out "DANGEROUS" down the side of each leg and "3-D" across the rear *black kneepads *black boots *black athletic tape wrapped tightly around each wrist -Out of ring Appearance: Dixon usually wears jeans, a t-shirt, silver-tipped snakeskin boots, and occasionally a long black leather duster.
-Sample RP: (Quick note: Dixon was actually a face when I wrote this roleplay. I prefer to play him as a heel, but I thought this roleplay would make a nice sample.)
[It is Thursday evening in New York, the sun is just beginning to set, and we are inside the central terminal building of La
Guardia Airport. Tom Jones and a CPW cameraman are waiting in one of the terminals as an announcement is made over the loudspeaker.]
Loudspeaker: US Airways flight 120, non-stop from Memphis, now disembarking at gate 3. TJ: (to the cameraman) Gate 3, that's over here... [Jones quickly makes his way over to the aforementioned gate as passengers from flight 120 are filing out into the waiting area.
One of the passengers is Dan Dixon, carrying a small overnight bag and talking on a cell phone. He is wearing jeans, silver- tipped snakeskin boots, a plain white t-shirt, as well as dark, black-framed sunglasses, and his long black hair is pulled back into a ponytail. He glances up at Jones, smiles, and makes his way over to the camera, still talking on his phone. Parts of his conversation can be heard as he approaches.]
3D: ...Just got back ... Yeah, he's here ... No sh*t? (he laughs) Groovy ... Okay, look, I gotta' let you go. Jones wants an interview ... Yeah ... Later.
[Dixon turns off the phone and sticks it in his back pocket, then removes his glasses and folds them up, hanging them on the neck of his shirt. He bends down to set his bag on the floor beside him, then raises up, hands on hips, and gives Jones a sly grin.]
3D: What's up, TJ? TJ: Nothing much. Haven't seen you in a few days, where've you been? 3D: Just got back from the home of the delta blues... [Jones gives him a blank stare.] 3D: On the banks of the mighty Mississippi... [Jones looks confused.] 3D: Memphis, TJ. Get your head out of your ass. [Jones shakes his head, as if coming out of a trance.] TJ: Sorry. You lost me with that 'delta blues' thing. 3D: (chuckling) Figures. Damn yankee... TJ: (interrupting) Anyway, you seem to have recuperated nicely since the incident at last week's Blockbuster.
3D: Yeah, it ain't so bad the second time around. TJ: How was your trip? 3D: It was cool. Haven't been back there in a while, it was nice to visit my old stomping grounds. I took a little stroll down
Beale Street, had a bite to eat at BB King's, took in a show at Mud Island... I'm losin' you again, ain't I? TJ: Yes. 3D: Forget it. Let's move on to somethin' else. TJ: Good idea. Who were you talking to on the cell phone? 3D: My agent. TJ: Ah. He must have told you about the pay per view. 3D: Yep. Triple threat match for the number one contender spot. Good deal. I can kick Malice's ass and earn a title shot at the same time. TJ: Don't forget about Thor. 3D: (he smiles) I haven't. But I ain't worried about Thor, he owes me one. Why, I wouldn't be surprised if he was to just lay down and hand me the win after I get through with Malice.
TJ: I'm not so sure about that... 3D: (chuckling) Yeah, well, neither am I. I don't expect Thor to lay down any more than I expect Malice to. I'll just have to put both of 'em down the hard way.
TJ: That may be easier said than done. You're talking about two men who are former CPW heavyweight champions.
3D: Yeah, they've both held the heavyweight title, but the Dangerous One ain't intimidated, junior. I've beat Malice before, I can do it again. Besides, I've spent some time with him. I know him, just like he knows me. The difference is intelligence. I'm an intelligent man, TJ. Malice is dimmer than a two-watt bulb, end of story. As for Thor ... well, he just needs to stay out of my way long enough for me to take care of Malice, then I'll deal with him.
TJ: Let's talk about your relationship with Dan Stein. How did that come about? 3D: We just happen to be on the same side of the fence right now. We have a common problem.
TJ: The Masters of Malice. 3D: Exactly. They're a thorn in both our sides. But don't worry, they're gonna' get what's comin' to 'em, sooner or later. (he smiles) I'd bet on 'sooner'.
TJ: With the April Reign card set up the way it is, its possible that you could go on to face Stein for the heavyweight title.
How would that affect your newfound alliance? 3D: (laughs) Well, we'll cross that bridge when we come to it, TJ. (he reaches down and picks up his bag) Its been a real treat, but I gotta' run. Take it easy.
TJ: Yeah. You too. [The camera watches Dixon as he walks away, and Jones suddenly remembers something he forgot to ask.]
TJ: (shouting) Dan! Will you be at Blockbuster this weekend? 3D: (not bothering to stop or look back) With bells on, TJ. [Fade to black.] * Sent from RemarQ http://www.remarq.com The Internet's Discussion Network * The fastest and easiest way to search and participate in Usenet - Free!
