Looking for a fed.... Jason "The Bandit" Blake

29 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now I'm not 100% sure if I will join a fed, but I will seriously look at every offer, provided it fits my criteria.
-Email Packets at least twice a week -Has to have a webpage -No more than 2 shows a week -Been around at least 9 months -Roster of at least 20 You can find my bio, and everything else you need for info at [detail withheld] but here is a brief list of my accomplishments for this character... Career Record- 46-9-3 -Current NAWA North American Champion -Current NAWA Tag Team Champion -Former NWWA North American Champion -Former NWWA Tag Team Champion -Former NWWA Canadian Champion -Former NAWC Intercontinental Champion -Former NAWC Television Champion -Former SWL Spotlight Champion (vacated) -Former SWF World Champion (closed during reign) -Former CEWF Hardcore Champion (Closed during reign) Here is a roleplay just so you know I'm not a crappy roleplayer.... --------- (The scene opens just outside Seattle International Airport. You can see people rushing in and out of the terminal and planes are taking off and landing in the background. A long line of taxis in front of the terminal await tourists and businesspeople alike. You can see NAWA reporter Anarchy
Love standing outside the door, ignoring catcalls from men passing by, and continually checks her watch.)
Anarchy Love - How long does it take to get through the baggage check? This is insane. And I thought I took long.
(At that moment, you see a teenager bolt out of the terminal doors at full speed with a duffel bag in his hand, nearly knocking Anarchy over.
Following right behind is none other than Jason Blake. He is wearing an unbuttoned White Sox jersey with a Pentagon shirt underneath and jeans, and a red bandana on his head. He leaps past Anarchy, who is in shock from almost being knocked over, and tackles the teenager, taking the bag away from him.)
Jason Blake - Punk kid. What made you think you could get away from me? Teenager - Hey, a kid's gotta make a living you know. Now get the hell off me. Jason Blake - Well for one, you don't go around ripping off people at an airport baggage claim. And two, you don't steal MY stuff, because I might just kick your ass next time.
Teenager - I don't care. It's a living. I'll do it again too. You're not going to stop me.
Jason Blake - Probably not, but I can at least stop my stuff from getting ripped off. I have too many valuables in my bag to risk losing.
(Two huge security guards come from the doors and each grab a hold of the teenager's arms and carry him back into the terminal. Blake gets to his feet, picks up his bag, and dusts himself off before noticing Anarchy looking at him with a confused expression.)
Jason Blake - Oh hey, I didn't see you there. Anarchy Love - Well how could you see me? You were too busy chasing that kid down, and almost putting me on my ass in the process.
Jason Blake - Well you know crazed fans. Always trying to get a piece of history for themselves. Maybe I should sign more autographs.
Anarchy Love - I highly doubt he was a fan Jason. Get your head out of your ass. But it's about time you showed up. Some of these men are perverts.
Jason Blake - Well sorry. Some women are so impatient. Why are you here anyway? Anarchy Love - What do you think smartass? Jason Blake - I dunno, maybe just to welcome me to Seattle or something? Anarchy Love - Sometimes I wonder about you. So how do you feel about your win over Deadly Force on Rage?
Jason Blake - Well a win's a win, although Jimmy and I really weren't at our best. But I just want to move on and focus on Sunday. Remember that I have two matches for the second straight Pay Per View. People can't get enough of me in this country. Have to keep all the diehard fans happy. It's not easy being the NAWA's main attraction you know. As for poor Crazy Horse, maybe he will learn not to steal the girl of a guy that can kick his ass.
He should've watched the last NWWA Pay Per View and he could've seen Bret
Masters take his punishment.
Anarchy Love - But Amy is with Masters, doesn't that contradict what you're trying to say? Jason Blake - Ummmm. That's besides the point. The point is if you get involved with another guy's girl, you get hurt. Masters did learn that lesson, and he's been nothing since. I think my point's been proven.
Anarchy Love - I guess so, but now you have Damage Inc. to focus on. Do you think you'll be ready for them?
Jason Blake - Let me see.... Yeah, Jimmy and I will be. Damage Inc. is nothing more than a couple of clowns who really don't belong in the ring.
But they'll feel what it is like stepping in the ring with the Pentagon. We've proven ourselves time and time again. But I will be wrestling a conservative match as I have other priorities as well.
Anarchy Love - You must be referring to your match with Scott Roberts for the North American title, am I right?
Jason Blake - You catch on really quick. It's good to see. I'd really have hated to repeat myself. But yes, I am somewhat concerned about my match with Mr. Roberts. You see, when I first entered the NWWA, Scott Roberts WAS the North American Champion. I was just a guy trying to climb the ladder.
But now the roles are reversed, and I am the champ, with Scottie being the one who has to climb the ladder again. Of course Frank gave him a glorious opportunity, and people are wondering why Frank Peregrine would give someone he hates such an opportunity. Well the answer is simple, Frank Peregrine knows how dangerous I am in the ring, and he knows Scott Roberts has no chance in hell of beating me on Sunday, and then once again, he's back at the bottom of the pile. It's a brilliant strategy when you look at the specifics.
Anarchy Love - But what if the fatigue kicks in and he manages to wear you out? You do have two matches after all.
Jason Blake - Let's get one thing straight, as we both know you've never been a part of NAWC. I debuted in the NAWC and my first night I was scheduled for two matches with a possibility of a third if I managed to win the second match. I started off by beating Damien Douglas in no time, then
I was in a triple threat match and managed to win that, so I ended up wrestling none other than Rick Stevens for the TV title, and you know what?
I beat him as well. I then went on to become the most decorated champion the NAWA will ever see. So don't talk to me about fatigue. I can outlast anyone, especially that bum Roberts. So there is not a worry in the world.
Anarchy Love - Did you happen to catch wind of Claw's remarks as of late? Jason Blake - Yeah I did. Anarchy Love - Don't you have a response? Jason Blake - Why bother? He's really not worth my breath. Anarchy Love - Well he basically called you out on a challenge, saying he could beat you. Jason Blake - Yeah? What's the point? Everyone is gunning for me lately. He can take a number and wait in line just like the rest of them. Anarchy Love - What about the things he said about Amy leaving you, and saying you're brainwashed?
Jason Blake - No comment. He's not getting under my skin that easily. I am too smart for those games. He's just trying to get me to challenge him so he can attempt to win the North American title, although everyone knows I am far too talented and wouldn't lose to him anyway. As for the brainwashing,
I'll deal with those accusations in due time.
Anarchy Love - So that's it? You're not scared to face Claw? Jason Blake - Of course not. Anarchy Love - Well then why wait to face him? Jason Blake - Because I don't believe in people weaselling their way into title shots. He's got to earn his ass-kicking just like everyone else.
It's just that simple. But I gotta get to my hotel and check in before the champagne gets warm.
Anarchy Love - You got champagne in your hotel room? Jason Blake - What did you expect? The Pentagon only gets the finest things money has to offer.
Anarchy Love - So where's the limo to pick you up? Jason Blake - Um...... I felt like taking a cab this time. Be one with the commoners. You know what it's like, don't you?
Anarchy Love - Yeah, how noble of you. I'm sure you will be earning your good Samaritan badge in no time.
Jason Blake - Sarcasm isn't going to get you prime interview time with me, you know that? Anarchy Love - I'm quite aware. But I gotta get to the hotel as well. Want a lift? Jason Blake - Sure, I guess so. But I want to sit in the back so I look like I'm being chauffeured around, is that okay?
Anarchy Love - Of course not! I am no damn chauffeur, you are sitting in the front like a "commoner" as you like to put it. If not, then you can take your cab, got it?
Jason Blake - All right. I was just kidding, take it easy. Anarchy Love - Well sarcasm won't get you interviews from the NAWA's head reporter, you know that, right?
Jason Blake - Good one. Let's go. (Blake and Love walk down towards Love's car and she unlocks the doors and they both get in opposite sides. The car starts as the screen fades to black.)
