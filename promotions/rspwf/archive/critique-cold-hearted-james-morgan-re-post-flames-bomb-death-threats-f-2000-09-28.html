[Critique] "Cold Hearted" James Morgan [Re-Post] Flames, Bomb & Death Threats, Feedback All Welcome

28 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RP I wrote for EWC. Kinda pissed me off knowing the Fed was such a waste of time and I think this RP had a good idea to it. So, read it and lemme know what you guys think. Thanks
-MDR ========================================================== [The imagery slowly within the area of an empty park. The night sky is a deep shade of blue with the randomly placed stars, hanging brightly. With patches of clouds passing by, the moon is a full circle of light. As the Camera pans downward, through a small patch of trees, deep within the center of the park, adjacent to a stone fountain, is a solid wood bench. Sitting on the bench, solemn, lonesome, and quiet is a man attired in a torn, tattered black Leather jacket. Also, the man is wearing slightly torn blue Denim shorts, a black
T-shirt beneath his jacket with "Normal" written across the chest, and black boots. Settling in on the straight screen view of the man, you take notice to his disgusted expression upon his pale skinned face with two Metallic eyebrow piercings and short reddish hair. Abruptly, the man speaks.]
Man: All my life, my mind was a pet. Like any dog, cat, goldfish, or other version of a pet, when it was good - I fed it, took care of it, and cared for it. When it was bad, I reprimanded it, punished it, and avoided it. For each positive act, it took two steps back with a negative. Much time, I spent, aiming for a way to control it. An obedient mind was a useful, helpful mind.
An insubordinate mind was a dangerous and unloving mind. To be rather frank, my mind was a constant behavior case, so to speak. Things haven't changed ..
not in the least. [The man slightly chuckles] Man: At times I struggled with reprimanding my mind, other times I basked in the moment of joy at it's willingness to comply with rules I made. However ..
don't be mislead down the path of your own Downfall into the Den of Depression
.. these rules are not what Society has labeled "normal." Do you think I give a damn what Society thinks? If I did, I'd be dead by now and forgotten by anyone who cared about me .. which is no one. It's rather disenchanting to see the rats of Society, hanging upon the leash of those they admire, as they strive for a legacy that was never theirs to begin with. I've asked them quite a few times .. but they never complied. I told them once, and only once .. and now they listen .. I told them something many will hear and none will forget ..
I tell you now .. I warn you now, but ONLY once .. Bear Witness .. As The EWC
Turns Cold Hearted.
[The man, now identified as "Cold Hearted" James Morgan, continues to stare blankly into the distance. With a gaze that makes him seem blind, his head slowly tilts to the left, his eyes squint, but with the panning view, there is nothing there except the green grass, tall trees, and blue water pouring from the simple structure. Morgan rubs his cheeks and chin with his right hand and as his hand slowly draws from his face, he speaks again.]
"CH" JM: Many men think that change is upon the horizon. They feel that within the days of our time, a revolution of sorts is in order. But they fail to realize nothing changes. Stuck in your lifestyle, nothing changes. Nothing you do, contrary to your personal belief, will factor into the death that approaches. Stare into the Mirror and gaze deep into your soul. Flirt with the anger and hatred you have. The same intangibles that rest in the depths of your Soul beneath your image and the person you claim to me. It is fraudulent of you to portray this gimmick of sorts only to mislead the Office Suits into thinking you are an asset to this entire Promotion. Deep down, you let the weak mind you truly are, control your actions above any script or image you have chosen to partake in. Your deprivation of truth is just an added flaw that makes up the entire reflection you see in the Mirror everyday. Resist the temptation as long as you want, but when you Fall into a Deep Depression, you will crawl into a hole all by your lonesome. In doing so, you realize no one cares about you .. and what you once knew, doesn't mean a thing.
[Morgan's eyes shut for a brief moment, he contracts his face muscle into a cringing expression. As his eyes re-open, the entire scenary changes. It appears as if the entire image has inverted itself. The green trees are now a distasteful shade of pink, the blue water now pours disgusting yellow liquid, and the richly soiled dirt is now deprived of all it's nutrients and a shade of blue. The only thing that remains the same is James Morgan. His three metallic eyebrow rings, still metallic. James' black Leather jacket is still such along with his T-shirt bearing "Normal" across the chest. Even his torn blue shorts and black boots remain the same.]
"CH" JM: You might begin to wonder why all around you has changed but the one thing you know nothing about has not. It is simple to comprehend yet your egotistical mind will not allow you to once, open your mind and divulge into another mans life. Everything that evolves, has its axis and it's you.
When tragedy strikes others, all is well because it avoided you. Ignorance is bliss, but to what extent can we repay ignorance with perpetual happiness?
[Morgan's eyes shift to the right with his head cocking to the left. He sighes rather deeply and places his left hand up to his left eyebrow and slightly touches each piercing. Abruptly, Morgan's entire upperbody inverts with his
Leather jacket turning a pure white, along with his once black T-shirt but now the text is a deep black.]
"CH" JM: Everything you thought you once knew .. changed. Lost in a whirlwind of the unknown, you realize harshly that you brought this upon yourself. If you took time to learn, you would know. But that is far from what you should do. Oppressing the truth again .. your eyes must sting when they close. I do not change like the rest of this Park because you do not claim to know me nor do you even have the slightest inclination as to who I am or what I am about. Yet, you think you know everything else. Soon, it is probable you begin to behold concepts and ideals of knowing James Morgan.
Inside and out, you read me like a Map. Or did you? What do you truly know? What exists in your world? Is everything safe? Are you safe? Does order remain? And if so, for how long? It is time you cherish your breath because your last soon approaches.
[Morgan looks down at his body and sees something rather bewildering to the average viewer. His jean shorts begin to fade as a new color takes hold of the denim material. A dark orange now covers his torn Jean shorts as his boots become white with black laces. Morgan silently chuckles to himself, his shoulders moving up and down. For the first time, his eyes shift upwards and pierce through the Camera Lens.]
"CH" JM: With each passing moment, you drift further and further into oblivion. It's suicide at it's best. The more you think you stand a chance in surviving, the quicker they diminish. Your self-imposed confidence leads you to believe it is a mere warm-up match for upcomming greatness, yet you ignore one thing. Continuously thinking you will be the receiving the ultimate gain through this, you couldn't be further from the truth. Wednesday Night, you will not surivive, let alone move on. Once again, deemed by my Opponents arrogance, Morgan will apathetically step past another feeble stone and be the sole benificiary. When you ask what happen, it is simple .. The Epitaph Was In
Your Name ..
[Morgan leans forward and in a raspy, whispering tone..] _ALL ALONG!_ [Fade To Black]
