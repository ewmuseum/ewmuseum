RP critique needed...serious replies only.

27 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am looking for a critique for the RP posted below--it is supposed to be a GENERIC intro RP, not geared towards any specific fed or another. I'm looking to make this RP not just good, but great--it may even serve as an example RP on an e-wrestling info/RP tips page that I'm in the process of designing (
http://www.fas.harvard.edu/~sterk/ewrtips.html ) _Shameless_plug_. Yeah, I know it's awkward right now... My character is called Dark Dragon (I've also, on RSPWF, seen another Dark
Dragon in SJPW...there is no relation between the two)...a 6'9", 338 lb.
monster heel. Gothic and deranged, although still an eloquent speaker, and skillful enough with words to send a previous opponent into a nervous breakdown. Full info can be found here, at
http://www.fas.harvard.edu/~sterk/darkdrgn.html . I'm looking to make this a good intro RP, something that shows how to intimidate and inspire fear with words (something the average mark things can come right off an
Undertaker interview). I ONLY WANT SERIOUS REPLIES TO THIS. That means no punkass who wants to flame someone just to masturbate his own ego, and no "that sucked."
Frankly, I don't give a flying fuck about the RSPWF 200...I've been in e-wrestling for about a year on and off, with "off" predominating, and have hardly stayed in any one fed for longer than a month. PLEASE RESPOND
VIA E-MAIL. Any hints for improvement would be greatly appreciated. Thanks. Dark Dragon -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- [SCENE: Utter void. The epitome of nothing, as if all light and matter were removed from the scene, and time itself had been frozen. When out of nowhere, a sudden, ominous gong initiates a positively bone chilling tune...a song of darkness, mystery, and impending doom..."Those Chosen by the Planet" (FF7 soundtrack)]
*donnnnnnngggg* *lub-dub* *lub-dub* *lub-dub* [The scene feels even more eerie, as if a great presence were soon to break the stillness.]
*donnnnnnngggg* *lub-dub* *lub-dub* *lub-dub* [Over the rhythmical heart-beat of the song comes a ghostly, seemingly longing melody. From the back of the scene, a cerulean blue spotlight flashes on to a dim light, gradually increasing in intensity. The floor shimmers in the light, as if it were a slab of finely polished obsidian...a little more light, and it seems to outline a very angular, gargantuan humanoid form...
The distant manlike image in the light could be mistaken for the apocalypse itself...as the light intensifies, its leviathan size becomes perfectly evident-6'9" perhaps, his black boots adding another inch and a half of presence unto that, not to mention well over three hundred pounds of pure rage-and seemingly commanding a presence well beyond that.
Its massive shoulders and limbs accented by angular, glossy black armor plates that seem to defy the light, scattering it in all directions.
Notably, the front breastplate bears an image of a royal-blue dragon crest. The light again intensifies, and his face becomes visible. He is extremely handsome and clean cut, with his angular, very striking features. Especially his eyes...very dark, shimmering, almost glinting eyes that could stare holes through the camera lens-and into the viewer's soul...yet simultaneously absent. His long, black hair hangs over his shoulders, the light giving him an air of Gothic majesty, of imperial darkness... His features remain somewhat expressionless, somewhat defiant and very troubled, almost set in a death mask. A slight derisive growl escapes his lips...yet he speaks calmly, eloquently in his slightly baritone voice.]
We who were bathed in the darkness...will spend our lives in a future without light. [The words "without light" seem to resonate and echo with a slightly higher pitch each time against the ghostly background tones...pleading and summoning.]
I have come... ["Come" echoes three times, seeming to carry an air of command along with it.] Come with me... [Once again eerie echo again...Dragon holding out his right hand in a seemingly amicable, beckoning gesture.]
To the Netherworld... [Netherworld...netherworld...netherworld...] Tartarus... [Tartarus...Tartarus...Tartarus...] [Pause for an eternal moment...] And you would dare...[fading echo again]...avoid it. [He begins to speak calmly, yet disturbed. Definitely not a man at peace with himself or the world.]
All in due time...the Harbinger of Doom will claim his own, his new kingdom of desperation, his throne of sundered bones, cemented by broken dreams.
Who am I? [This phrase, too, seems to linger in the still air.] I am Dark Dragon. In this world there is good and evil, order and chaos, prosperity and torment... I am the darkness. Who would _you_ believe that I am? [A calculated pause to let this riddle hang in the stillness.] The blood of all that I once held dear has been returned to the earth and scattered to the seas. With every step...it cries out for answers, for the hopes that lay shattered.
Rest in peace, and torment me no longer... [Slightly more stoic.] But not until I spill their blood. So many lost souls have come...so many have already condemned themselves unknowingly, or even unwillingly. Even their destruction remains a painful and unpleasant task. Dealing with this filth that would pass itself off as...
[He hesitates before speaking words that seems like a foreign language to him...] Justice? Goodness? Truth? Honor? Strength? Do you see just how little your bourgeois abstractions mean to me? Just how inadequate these concepts are to explain this plane of chaos and death? Feed me not your clouds of choking dust.
For years you have been given your mercy; once you could have been reformed or destroyed. Now only the latter option remains. Hear my words and be guided...to your pleasant memories of the past. For I assure you that the present will evermore be mine.
[Dark Dragon's eyes begin to slightly glint, then seemingly emanate pure darkness... His voice changes to the deep, forceful growl.]
At last, it is time. Let it all crumble... And LET IT RAIN BLOOD!!! AAAAAAAAAEEEEEEEERRRRRRRRRRGGGGGGGGGHHHHHHH!!!!!!! [Dragon's stentorian roar echoes several times through the surrounding air as the camera fades to black.]
