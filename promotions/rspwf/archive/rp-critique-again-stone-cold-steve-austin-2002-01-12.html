[RP Critique... again] "Stone Cold" Steve Austin.

12 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Same deal as last time... only, actually try to respond this time. Opinions on my take of Austin are appreciated. ~Maz ------------------------------------ [A handful of metal screws laying limp on a wooden folding table.] VO : "Y'know what it's like ta get screwed?" [The camera rears back, picking up the image of "Stone Cold" Steve Austin standing behind the table, in an otherwise empty room. Austin's clad in a black "Austin 3:16" t-shirt, stonewashed blue jeans, and a pair of black work boots.]
SCSA: "Dontcha know what it's like ta have the whole world in yer hands... ta have everything that ya busted yer ass for... ta have it right in front of you, only for some sumbitch ta come from behind and yank the rug out from under ya?"
"Ex Dubbya Ay... Do ya know what that's like?" [In a fit of rage, the Texas rattlesnake kicks over the table.] SCSA: "Well, ah do." [Pause.] SCSA: "Y'see, just this last week... ah stepped into the ring with a buncha otha sumbitches and one by one... ah was a Stunner machine."
"Wham.. Goodnight." [Austin chuckles.] SCSA: "Marty Jannetty and Shane Douglas realized the asswhippin' that they were about to receive.. so they went out and formed a little alliance called "Two pieces of trash that are gonna have sore jaws tomorrow."
"Marty... did ya think yer cute?" "WHAT?" "Marty.. did ya know yer sexy?" "WHAT?" "Marty... did ya have the looks to drive the girls wild?" "WHAT?" "Marty... did ya realize that yer partner wasn't there to save ya as yer ass sailed over Stone Cold's ring?"
"WHAT?" "WHAT?" "WHAT?" [Austin shakes his head in disgust.] SCSA: "Ah thought ya'd know better. Ah thought ya'd know not to screw with people.. afterall, last time all it gotcha was a one way ticket through a barber shop window."
"But, that's all I got ta say about that! The battle royal's over... and although ya pissed me off...I'll be rootin' fer ya this week..."
" 'Cause, at least ya didn't screw me." "And fortunetly fer ya... yer opponent did." [Austin chuckles.] SCSA: "Rob Van Dam... get ready ta live the name, son, because soon... damn's gonna be the only word ta come outta yer mouth. Yer date with destiny's comin' soon, and it's taking the form of 241 pounds of hell in a pair of trunks."
"Enjoy your match this week... because come time for the PPV, ya won't be lockin' horns with Marty Jannetty. You'll be steppin' it up with a rattlesnake... coiled back and ready to strike."
"But that's all I got ta say about that.." [Austin looks up.] SCSA: "This week... Stone Cold Steve Austin... WHAT?" "Ah said... this week, Stone Cold Steve Austin tags up with Mikey Whipwreck ta take on Shane Douglas an' Vader." "Ah.. ah'll be blunt here." [Austin chuckles.] SCSA: "Mikey... ah don't like ya. Now now, it ain't personal... and prolly mostly due to the fact that ah don't like anybody. But, ah'm just here ta make sure that we're on the same page. Ah'm gonna show up this monday, yer music's gonna hit... my music's gonna hit... a bell's gonna ring, and we're gonna whip some ass. Don't expect any pictures before the match... don't expect any handshakes after the match. All ya should expect is that once the timekeeper's beady little eyes locks on the bell, he lifts that little hammer and sets the sumbitch off... it'll be on. You'll be on the team with Stone Cold
Steve Austin, and you'll take part in an asswhippin' like none ya've never experienced before ."
"As fer the two pieces of trash we're steppin' in the ring with... it's simple. Vader, yer jaw's familiar with mah shoulder. Douglas, yours is as well. So.. all, I'm considering this match as is a chance to reacquaint them." [Fade out.] -------- ~Maz
