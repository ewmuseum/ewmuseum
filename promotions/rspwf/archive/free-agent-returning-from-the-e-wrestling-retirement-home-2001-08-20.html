Well, I've decided to give this a shot, as things seemed to have calmed down in my life. Anyway, I figure I want to find a decent fed to join in my comeback, but before I get into details, I'd like to send mad props to [name withheld], who put on a kick-ass retirement scene on one of his OPW cards. It
meant a lot to be "recognized" like that, and I hope he can forgive me for doing this. As for things I am looking for (These are only preferences to make my experience enjoyable, not necessarily demands): -One show per week. -Roleplay limit of 2 per match (can't keep up to 5+ flashes/week. Just not enough hours in a day) -Average cards (Don't have to be spectacular, but the fedhead has to at least put in some effort.) -Needs to have a website. (Gives me something to look at for reference, or just to waste time) -A decent sized roster. I don't mind feds that are just starting out, but I don't want to be facing the same guy each week because there are only 2 or 3 guys who have joined. When I say decent, I would like to see at least 10 active players, but I may make an exception for 8 or 9. As for credentials (Here's where some feds will start drooling for some reason...)
