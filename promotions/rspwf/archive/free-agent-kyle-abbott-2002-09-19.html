[FREE AGENT] Kyle Abbott

19 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've been working on this new character for the past few days now. Below is the character's profile and a sample flash. But before you send your FAQs and the like, please take note of the following requirements I have, some of which are negotiable.
* Booked, e-mail fed. NO board feds. * Prefer a fed that is run on a bi-weekly basis but will consider ones on a weekly schedule. * 1 flash max per week. * Would prefer a fed with a decent women's division as I'd like to eventually find a manager/valet for the character. You can get in contact with me by e-mail ( [detail withheld] ), on AIM - [detail withheld], or IRC - same as AIM. Now on to the important stuff... Name: Kyle Abbott Age: 19 Height: 6'5" Weight: 250 lbs Hometown: Thunder Bay, Ontario, Canada Allignment: Pure Face Appearance: Kyle has short, dark brown hair, hazel blue eyes, and a well defined physique. There is a tattoo of a maple leaf on his right shoulder.
Oh, and let us not forget that picture-perfect smile. Ring Attire: Kyle's wrestling attire is pretty basic; shiny, royal blue shorts, matching elbow pads, black knee pads, and white wrestling boots that go almost all the way up to the knee pads.
Theme Music: "Deep Colors Bleed" by Systematic Gimmick: Kyle is a true fan of wrestling in every sense of the word. It doesn't matter what company he is hired by, he approaches it with enthusiasm. He is extremely ambitious, hoping to someday be at the top of the wrestling business. He is quite charismatic as he is often seen catering to the fans. However, he is a rookie, and he is naive. He will make mistakes from time to time. But unlike some people, Kyle learns from those mistakes. He strongly believes in accomplishing goals through hard work.
Moveslist: swinging neckbreaker, DDT, vertical suplex, release German suplex
(with opponent landing on back of head & neck Benoit-style), flying cross body, powerslam, fisherman's suplex, THE best dropkick in the business (gets great elevation), left punches (he's a Southpaw, his lefts are far better than his rights), missile dropkick, top rope moonsault, spinning back kick, sitout powerbomb, Russian legsweep, high angle belly-to-back suplex, dragon suplex, elbow drop, single leg crab, bulldog, armdrag
Signature Moves: front suplex, double underhook backbreaker, inverted DDT, reverse Russian legsweep, reverse top wrist lock sleeperhold (aka buffalo sleeper)
Setup Move: Spear Description: Kinda obvious :) Finisher: Thunder Bomb Description: A senton bomb Strengths: 1) Confidence - Kyle was well trained. What's more, he knows it. He's confident in his abilities and doesn't let a loss get him down. 2) Size - At 6'5" and 250 pounds, Kyle combines strength and speed. He can compete against a wide range of opponents wiith few difficulties. 3) Endurance - Kyle can easily "go the distance" with any opponent. He can take a beating and still come back to give his opponents a tough match. 4) Determination - Kyle has the will to win. He doesn't let losses set him back. If there's a goal he wants to accomplish, he won't stop until he's achieved it. Weaknesses: 1) Ambition - Although Kyle is very ambitious, helping him strive harder to achieve his goals, it can also have an influence on him in a negative way. His ambitions may cause him to make foolish rookie mistakes. 2) Naive - Kyle is a little too trusting. Sooner or later this is going to bite him in the ass. 3) Inexperience - Kyle has the ability to be a great champion. But until he gets a lot of in-ring experience, he'll be prone to making rookie mistakes from time to time.
Sample Flash: [The scene fades in to the backstage area of the Fort William Gardens in Thunder Bay, Ontario, Canada. The place is swarming with people as the local wrestling promotion, Professional Wrestling of Ontario is currently holding a show here. Various stars of the company rush back and forth, preparing for the night's entertainment. The scene pans around to the right, taking in the sights and sounds. We then stop panning and zoom in to a young man by the name of Kyle Abbott. He is only 19 or 20, has short, dark brown hair and a short goatee that stretches around his chin. He is casually dressed in a short sleeved, navy blue pullover shirt, khakis, and
black shoes. Though most of his body is concealed, his arms are well defined. A small crowd surrounds him. A few of them are young women his own age or slightly older. Aside from them there is a reporter and her own camera crew. Kyle has just finished answering a question and flashes a smile, one that is picture-perfect.]
KA: Wrestling school was really tough. Plus the fact that I was still going to high school in the beginning made it even more difficult. But I'm glad I went through it. It's always been my dream to become a professional wrestler. Now is my chance to fulfill that dream.
Reporter: Now that you have completed your training, have you started looking for a promotion to get hired by? And if so, which ones do you have in mind?
KA: First of all, yes, I have been looking for a promotion to get my career started. I've sent my training videos to several companies all over Canada and in the States. Right now I don't want to be picky. If it means starting at the bottom, that's fine. I'll just work my way up.
Reporter: Have you considered hiring a manager or agent to help you along the way? KA: Yes but right now that's not my biggest concern. For now I just want to get hired. Once that's out of the way, and once I'll know where I'll be relocating to, I'll concentrate on looking into management.
Reporter: Okay. My next question is quite broad, but if you were to say something to any future opponents of yours, what would it be?
[Kyle turns away from the reporter and looks straight into the camera.] KA: I want to say that I respect you all and everything you've done. You all have paved the way for us in the new generation of pro wrestlers. With that in mind, I want to warn you that if by chance we step foot in the ring together, you will be up against one of the most competitive men you will ever face. I may just be a rookie fresh out of training, but I am determined to succeed in this business. I've gotten this far, there isn't much that can stop me now.
Reporter: I think that's about it. [Kyle stands up and holds out his hand. The reporter accepts it in a friendly handshake.] KA: Thanks for giving me this chance to introduce myself to the wrestling world. I appreciate it.
Reporter: You're welcome. Good luck Kyle. KA: Thanks. [The reporter turns and heads off-screen, her camera man close behind. The scene fades out as several wrestlers approach Kyle and another conversation begins...]
