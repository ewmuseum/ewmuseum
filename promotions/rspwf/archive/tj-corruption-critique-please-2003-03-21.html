[TJ Corruption]-[Critique Please]

21 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Fade In... ] [ We see two people going at it in what seems to be a hard game of basketball in a nice sized gym. The smaller man trys a pull up jumper from the top of the key only to be blocked by the bigger man. The bigger man dribbles it down the key getting a quick and easy layup. ]
Voice: That's 10, good game bro. I'll be right back as you can see business awaits. [ The bigger man walks over to a bag, picks it up and brings it towards the direction of the camera. As he comes closer you make out the familiar face of
"God's Gift" TJ Corruption. Corruption places the bag on a bench close to the camera and unzips it. He pulls out a water bottle and gulps it down to about halfway. He puts the top back on and then places a towel over his shoulders. He points the bottle at the camera and begins to speak. ]
"God's Gift" TJ Corruption: You see that? Just in a matter of seconds I'll take a quick block and put it down for the win. The funny thing is, Jimmy over there has no idea that I just played him. He thinks he actually got about 5 points on me, but in reality I was toying with him. I allowed him to score on me, I allowed him to somewhat defend me. But in a matter of seconds I can break the deception and just go for the easy win. Very similar to what is going to go down at this Ladder Rumble.
I'm going to go in, toy with a few kids and hop on over and grab my ticket to the first round of the official tournament. Lots of kids have already been talking about "God's Gift", claiming that he has no credability to his words, claiming that he can't walk the walk. Kids, _LISTEN_, this is no joke. I'm not here to tease you fellas' everything I'm saying is 100% true and it will be backed in the ring. No credability? Will winning this Ladder Rumble give me this so called "credability"? No, it's doubtful that it will. Mainly because kids in this game today just can't accept the fact that someone is that superior to his peers. There will be plenty of excuses to why winning this
Ladder Rumble gives me no credibility, that's great, sweet, who needs credibility anyways. It's not like I need it, because personally I already know that I can easily go toe to toe with anyone in this tournament credibility or not.
[ Corruption opens the water and finishes it off. He tosses the bottle on the bench and then sits on the bench hands folded in front of his chest resting on his thighs. He looks up into the camera smirking. ]
"God's Gift" TJ Corruption: Ronnie Demola, Dave Demola, whoever the hell you guys are. You sirs are the epitome of stupidity. You think your a couple of hot shots because you took apart a group of words and sentences and tried to humor
SWT by making little smart ass remarks to what I had to say? You think that what you did makes you smart? Hell, you can take apart any group of words or sentences anyone says and make them seem non valid and stupid. You could take apart a Shakespeare piece and have the very same results that you two fools had with my views in my last promo. Hell, that's good though, I'm glad you two have taken interest in listening to every god damned thing I say, just keep listening and maybe you'll learn something. Honestly I just think you two want
"God's Gift" to make you famous. I'll do everything but make you two famous come the Rumble, that is unless you feel going over the top into the crowd via
TJ Corruption is a introduction to being famous.
But that's enough talk on the inbreed brothers of SWT. To everyone in this rumble, I want you all to know that TJ Corruption claiming the first ring and the most points in this rumble isn't the end of the world. Hell there will still be three more rings for you idiots to battle it out for and that ain't bad.
[ Corruption takes the towel off his shoulders wipes the sweat off his forehead and tosses it in the bag. He stands up folds his arms across his cest. ]
"God's Gift" TJ Corruption: I'm pretty psyched about my entrance to the first round of the tournament. I mean I might as well consider myself an entrant into the tournament being that there isn't anyone else in the Ladder Rumble that can hold a candle. I'll fly by the first round and then the next round, and the next, until eventually I'm in the finals of this damned thing. Once in the finals, [ Corruption smirks ] it's a simple one, two, three and the crowning of
TJ Corruption as the winner of the tournament. That's all there is to it kids, not even much reason for you guys to be here besides as being my pinning dummies.
If you kids want to book it out of here and go on home already I can clearly understand that. But I'd prefer for some of you guys to hang around simply because I could use a little bit of a workout. Just remember guys, there's no four rings about this Ladder Rumble, the fourth is already spoken for. It's a three ring Ladder Rumble for you guys to have a chance to get to the first round of the tournament. But, from my standpoint it's a jog up a ladder down into a huge box and grabbing a trivial ring. Take my words to the bank kids, they are as good as gold.
[ Corruption walks slowly over to the other who tosses him the basketball. Corruption takes a shot at the top of the key and makes it with ease. The guy tosses the ball back to him and Corruption palms it by his side as he looks into the camera. ]
"God's Gift" TJ Corruption: Prepare yourself kids. I've been prepared since I was brought into this world, why else would they call me "God's Gift"? Your all going to witness the beginning of something great at this Ladder Rumble. When I walk out with the first ring and the one way ticket to the first round of the tournament it is _THEN_ that you will all know truely why I call myself the most physically and mentally sound proffesional in this industry. If I told you otherwise it would be a lie.
[ Corruption turns and takes a final shot making it and then recieving the ball from his friend again at the top of the key. ]
_You've_ _Just_ _Been_ _Blessed._ Goodbye. [ Fade to black.. ] "[]D [] []\/[] []D" [name withheld] ______________________ "God's Gift" TJ Corruption [KaWF]-[0/0/0] [S.W.T.]-[0/0/0]
