<RP> Brian Storm

5 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a roleplay that I included in an app to the AWMC to be part of their King Of Crusiers tournament. Feedback would be appreciated. Thanks in advance.
RPA ====================================== [Fade in. The scene opens to a reveal a large hall. Sitting onto top of a raised stage is a reddish oak podium. Behind the podium is a hanging banner with the letters AWMC. Reporters line the floor cameras flashing as a young gentleman enters from the right. The man slowly walks to the stage, seeming to enjoy the camera flashes as they explode around him.
The man stands nearly six feet tall and is wearing a pair of highly polished black dress shoes, winter white dress pants, a black dress belt and a bluish-grey mock neck sweater. Upon his shoulder rests a golden title belt that gleams in the lights.]
[Slowly the man steps behind the podium and looks out into the sea of reporters. A gleam of joy and satisfaction can be seen in his green eyes. The camera pans in onto the face so that all may get a better view of his feature. The man doesn't appear over 25 as a wrinkle cannot be seen upon his face. His reddish-brown hair falls just below his eyes and with a casual movement of his hand he brushes it back. Almost in anticipation he strokes his thin beard before addressing the reporters.]
Man: "First off I would like to thank you all for coming on short notice to my _first_ ever press conference. Now for those of you don't know I am Brian Storm the /current/ AEWO Cruiserweight Champion. <pats the title on his shoulder> You see a few months ago the AEWO went on a hiatus and I've no form of competition to prove that I am the _best_ junior heavyweight in the game today."
[A young female reporter hand shoots into the air and even before Brian can acknowledge her she is already speaking.)
Reporter 1: "Why haven't you just joined another league? I mean surely someone wold have signed you to a contract."
[A puzzled look falls about the face of Brian. He shakes it off and looks at the reporter.]
Brian: "Alright, let's try this question for you. Have you ever seen me in the ring before? It obvious to me you haven't. You see I'm not about the hardcore <making the quote signs with his fingers>gimmicks or the sports entertainment. I don't walk down that aisle to run off a few catch phrases. I walked that aisle to prove that I am the best pure athlete in the game. I walk that aisle to wrestle and that's what scares the promoters away from me. They feel that I couldn't draw them any money, so Brian Storm is over looked."
Reporter 1: "Then what's this press conference for? Are you stepping away from the sport?" Brian: "Stepping away? Oh no. You see that banner behind me. The AWMC is holding a tournament to crown the King Of The Cruisers. <smiles> And as far as I can tell you're looking at him. So this press conference is to announce my bid for that very tournament."
[Murmuring erupts from the reporters as the flashbulbs go off around him. Slowly an older, well dressed reporters stands up.]
Reporter 2: "Now Brian, what makes you so sure that you will be accepted into the tournament. I mean the AWMC is only looking for the best."
[Brian strokes his beard for as few moments and looks at the gentleman. Slowly Brian rotates his neck slightly back and forth and looks down at the podium.] Brian: "I knew that question was coming. You see I'm not sure. I honestly feel the AWMC is going to be like all the other leagues and over look me, in favor of some high flyer from the Land of the Rising
Sun. I expect the AWMC to _ignore_ my request. In FACT ... I expect to be passed by."
Reporter 2: "<shocked>Then why are you even taking the time to place your bid for a spot?" Brian: "Because I was always taught surprises happen. I mean when I started the sport I never thought I would have a mentor by the name of
Dan 'Flash' Kauffman ... but I did. I never expect to win a gold title
.. but I did. Now I'm not expecting to be part of this tournament ...
Who knows. Somewhere in this world there is a place for a traditionalist who realizes that it's not about how much blood is shed ... or how much money is made by your teddy bear. But how much talent you have that makes you what you are in the sport. Like everything the sports entertainment will fall to the wayside and the talent will shine forth again. And when it does I WILL be on the forefront of that movement."
[Hands fly up in all directions as shouts of questions can be heard from all sides. A smile falls upon Brian's face.]
Brian: "I'm sorry but that's all the time I have. Thank you all for coming and who knows ... the next time you see me I am very well be the
King Of The Cruisers."
[With that Brian walks off of the stage as reporters snap some parting shots of the man know as the 'Wild Child'. Fade out.]
