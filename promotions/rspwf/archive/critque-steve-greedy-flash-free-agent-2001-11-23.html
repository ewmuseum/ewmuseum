23 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TO: Chris Calavaria, Chris Page and Scott Haggarty FROM: "The Rich One" Steve Greedy << The scene doesn't fade in just yet. Our screen view is pure black, but we can hear bell rings of some sort. Suddenly as if from nowhere we hear the loud voice of a man screaming to someone out of normal talking distance..>>
[VOICE] : " I PRAY TO GOD YOUR ALMOST DONE WITH THAT MEAL !!!! "
<< We now have a view of the person hollaring. The person is "The Rich One" Steve Greedy. He is laying in his bedroom suite at one of the finest hotels in Georgia. He is above the covers wearing only his boxers and a gold necklace which hangs against his tan body. His blonde hair isn't parted like normal as he hasn't been up very long obviously.The bell that we heard earlier is laying on a small table by the bed.>>
[GREEDY] : " Here I am waiting on breakfest in bed, As all rich people should get every morning and notice the keyword I said there...."
<< Greedy pauses for a moment, then raises his voice from the normal tone to a little bit of a louder tone.>>
[GREEDY] : " WAITING !!!! I, Steve Greedy am waiting on some low life to cook me breakfest in bed."
<< He lowers his voice back down to normal tone as his sudden fit of anger has passed, Least for the moment.>>
[GREEDY] : " I don't wait on anybody...Espically some girl who will come home with me the first night I meet her at a club. Me and Frankie hit the clubs the other night after defeating The Untouchables.. << A smile comes to his face as the thought of beating them comes to mind >> While we were at the clubs, We of course met some ladies and I took one home with me...While
Frankie took TWO home with him. I was only in the mood for one myself, I sorta had a headache...If you watched the end of the match on Showdown you will know why. I was being a nice guy, Just going to break things up since the match was over as Joe Young said I was doing, and BAM...."
<< Greedy pounds his right fist into his left palm making a loud impact sound >> [GREEDY] : " Page sucker punches me like the coward he is....He calls himself the "American Rage" of GRW, but Really he is the " Yellow Belly" of the GRW.
I have been in the ring with him and so has Frankie...He runs from us both...Page, Shouldn't be in that cage match at Heatwave with my boy
Frankie. I mean nobody on the roster stands a chance against Frankie, Besides maybe myself, but They could find somebody to help put on a better show than
Chris Page. He is a joke and so is his lil partner Chris Calavaria."
<< Just then someone opens the door to the hotel room and Greedy stops before he gets on a rant about Calavaria...A lady wearing a sexy black skirt and a
Skimpy White top comes in with a plate of egg's, Bacon and Sausage....She sits the plate down infront of Greedy and sits on the corner of the bed beside him staring into his blue eyes.>>
[GREEDY] : " Why are you looking at me like that Lady ? " [LADY] : " I am waiting on you to try it and see if it's any good sweetie " << She smiles at Greedy, Who just shrugs and trys some of the egg's..>>
[GREEDY] : " Three things....One, It is decent...Two, Don't ever call me sweetie and Three...Fix me a drink for christ sake."
[LADY] : " Orange Juice ? Milk ? Apple Juice ?...What would you prefer ? " [GREEDY] : " Orange Juice << Rolls his eyes >> Apple Juice << Shakes his head in disgust >> Go fix me a screwdriver you dumb slut...."
<< Greedy points out the door into the next room, and the girl quickly exits the bedroom area frowning.>>
[GREEDY] : " Now if she comes back....WE KNOW SHE'S A SLUT...Anybody that takes that much verbal abuse has issues.."
<< Greedy takes another bite of the egg's, and Then looks up towards the camera smiling. >>
[GREEDY] : " Okay...Back to business...Before the girl came into the room I was about to get started on Chris Calavaria....Chris, Ive beat you twice..Once it might be a fluke. Two times thats a shame on you and Three times...Your out the picture boy. If I beat you again...What we all already know will be offical...It will be in the record books forever that Steve
Greedy is better than Chris Calavaria. Am I painting a clear enough picture for you ?..."
<< Greedy pauses >> [GREEDY] : " Do you really want everybody to know that I am that much better than you ? Do you really want to show up and let me pound on you one more time with my superior skills ? "
<< Greedy takes a bite of the Bacon and then the sausage...>> [GREEDY] : " That's really up to you Chris.If you want me to beat you I can,
If you want to walk away..That's fine to. I could just take the night off, but then again..Wrestling you is sorta like taking the night off so whatever once again works with me."
<< The girl comes back into the room with a mixed drink in hand.We assume it's a screwdriver.Greedy takes the drink from the girl and takes a sip. He pauses, Then motions for the girl to leave. She almost breaks down to tears, but leaves the room before we can see them ourselves >>
[GREEDY] : " Damn good screwdriver...." << He takes another bite of his breakfest, Then places the plate of food on the table by the bed. He sits up in the bed a bit, showing off his tan body,
Only holding his mix drink in hand.>>
[GREEDY] : " If only she cooked and had sex as good as she can make drinks...I might would keep that one around "
<< Greedy takes another sip of the drink, Then also places it on the table...He clears his throat, and once again begins to speak.>>
[GREEDY] : " But she don't.....She bores me.....Just as Chris Calavaria is starting to do. She bores me with her personality, Her Looks and Her horrible cooking...Calavaria you also bore me with your personality, but the other two things that bore me about yourself is....Your wrestling skills and your lack of verbal skills.Calavaria, Heatwave is gonna be just like the title of the show says it will be..It will be heated up to the max and on fire....If you show up, I will BANKRUPT you just as I did the other night. Real quick like,
So we can get on with the night. Maybe to the matches where people actually don't know whose gonna win before hand...."
<< Greedy gets out of the bed showing off his well developed body >> [GREEDY] : " After this is over with you....I will go after MY title...Scott
Haggarty, If you didn't watch the match me and Frankie had against Page and
Calavaria...Be sure to watch my One on One with Calavaria coming up this weekend...I am just warning you now...I am coming for you boy "
<< Greedy walks towards the camera slowly...>> [GREEDY] : " And you can take that to the Bank. My bank prolly << Pops the trademark Greedy Smirk >> Cause After I win the Atlanta City title...I will be once again proving..Money isn't the only thing, It's everything...and that my friends is "Money"..."
<< Greedy puts his hand over the camera lens calmly,and the view we had fades to Black. >>
