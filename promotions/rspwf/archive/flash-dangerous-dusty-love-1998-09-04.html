-[FLASH]- "Dangerous" Dusty Love

4 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"I'll Win... And Look Good Doin' It." [The scene fades in on a beautiful house in Memphis, TN. This house looks familiar because it's been seen before on the ICW TV shows. It appears to be the home of Dusty Love. The man that walked over his opponent on Rockin' Da
World Tour a few days ago. The camera goes around to the backyard where we see a very large swimming pool, hot tub, a nice patio, and a pond surrounded by a forest-like enviornment. The pond is located a nice distance from the pool and patio area. The camera scans over the beautiful scenery as the birds chirp and the whistling wind blows. The camera zooms in on the pond. The water is calm, besides the ripples in the water from the swimming fish and the blowing wind.
Then the camera gets a nice view of the trees surrounding the pond. We then spot, what appears to be, a man laying in a hammock. The hammock swings sofly back and fourth as the camera gets a closer view. The man laying in the hammock is "Dangerous" Dusty Love. He has a drink in one hand and the other is behind his head as he relaxes and soaks up the sun. He's wearing nothing but his bathing suit and his circular lense sunglasses. His hair is wet and pulled back in a pony tail. After a few seconds, he turns his head and notices the camera man standing there. He grins very cockily and begins to speak.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: The return didn't go quite how I planned. Sure, I kicked some ass like I planned... Sure, I looked _GREAT_ kickin' ass like I planned... But I didn't win exactly how I planned. I was planned on getting the one, two, three in the middle of the ring. But that was thrown out the window when Justin Miller attacked me from behind with a metal fold-in chair.
But that's fine. I'm not the one to complain because I would've done the
_SAME_ thing. I know as well as anyone else, that _ALL'S_ fair in love and war. And Justin, this isn't about love... But it's about _WAR!_ You proved a lot to me when you hit me with that chair. That's right... You proved to me, that you actually _DO_ have some guts in that puney body of yours. You showed me that you're gonna actually _TRY_ to put up a fight in this thing. And that's fine by me... Because if you ask me... You have _NO_ choice to. You can run your mouth off about me and Tony Paseno all you want, but the fact of the matter is this - Neither you _OR_ James has _EVER_ beaten me or Paseno!
I've pinned _BOTH_ of you before... And I _CAN_ do it again. And I guess I'ma have to show you one more time, pal. Justin... You remember the EHW. I beat you in the middle of the ring and _TOOK_ your Television Championship.
James... You remember all those times that I kicked you around the ring in the
UHW. I beat you over and over again... Until you _FINALLY_ learned your lesson... Until you _FINALLY_ came _BEGGING_ me to go to your side. James,
Justin... You guys don't stand a chance against me and "The Big Man". You know it... I know it... Paseno knows it... And the fans around the world knows it. It's just a fact of life... "The Miller Brothers" can't beat "The
Family". Simple as that.
[Dusty looks into the camera and lets out a cocky grin.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: But on a lighter note... Corporal Punishment _DID_ fall at the hands of "The Love Man". Just like I said he would. He got dropped on his cranium, just like I said he would. And he has a blemish on his record... Just like I said he would. And I can tell by how easy it was, that it _WON'T_ be the last blemish on his record. [Dusty grins.] But one down, and many, many more to go. But next on the list, is Tommy "Lord" Lionheart.
Friday Night Fallout... Chicago, Illinois... Inside the United Center. [Dusty takes a sip of his drink and continues on.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Tommy Lionheart... I really have nothing against you... I really don't know you... And quite frankly, I've never even _HEARD_ of you. But Tommy... I really don't care all that much about you either.
See... I care about very few things when I get in the squared circle. The only things I care about are - Number one, is winning. That's why I wrestle.
To _WIN!_ And number two... And the most important thing I care about is... How I _LOOK!_ If I'm gonna win, I'm gonna look good doin' it! And Mr. Lionheart, come Friday night, _WHEN_ I beat you... I'm gonna look damn good doin' it. And the funny part about it is, you couldn't stop me if you wanted to. That's how much better I am than you. Don't take it personally... It's just business. But never-the-less... I _ALWAYS_ take care of my business...
And Tommy Lionheart, I'm gonna take care of _YOU!_ [Dusty rubs his whisker beard and smiles a cocky smile and begins to speak again.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: "The Love Man" is on a tear here in the ICW, alreay. That tear was started on Tuesday... And it won't end until I'm on top. And that same tear will continue _THROUGH_ Tommy Lionheart on Friday... And anyone else along the way. I'm not here to make waves... I've been there and done that. I'm here to win gold, just like I do best. And I'll throw out _ALL_ and
_ANY_ stops to get it. Tommy Lionheart... You're in my way. I look at you as a very small obstacle. An obstacle that is in my way on my ride to the top.
I'll dominate, devastate, and decapitate you, if necessary. You're a Juniorweight and I'm a Heavyweight... You do the math. You're chances of beating me are slim and none... And slim just left town. So I guess that means that you're up shit creek now. You signed the contract... And that also means that you wrote your suicide note.
[Dusty, then, gets out of the hammock and begins to walk towards the house.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Lionheart... You show up on Friday night and you'll be making the biggest mistake of your career. And if you decided to show up...
It might be known as your _LAST_ decision as a professional wrestler. Just heed the warning. But as for my partner as well as best friend, Tony Paseno...
He'll definantly show up at Fallout and he'll leave the United Center and "The
Windy City" as the _NEW_ International Heavyweight Champion. Diamond... You better eat your Wheaties, because "The Big Man" is in the best shape of his life. "The Stallion" will come out on top... I'll _GARUNTEE_ ya that. But my main concern at this point in time, isn't much of one... But, it's Tommy
"Lord" Lionheart. And one last thing to you before I go... Just remember this
- "The Love Man" will do what he said he'll do... I _WILL_ win... I _WILL_ look good doing it... And I _WILL_... ..."LOVE... ...YOU... ...TO... ...DEATH!!!" [Dusty Love sets his cup down beside the pool and dives in. The camera then fades out on Dusty Love, who is swimming laps back and fourth in the pool.]
