[Free Agent] "The Sexy One" Jimmy Andrews

27 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have a created a new wrestler and he is looking for a federation to join along with his manager Kimberly Sue who is also his girlfriend. Jimmy was a big wrestling fan and watching WCW every week when he saw Kimberly Sue on TV fell in love with her. Below is exactly how he met Kimberly Sue and came onto the wrestling scene. If anyone is interested in this hot new prospect, please email at
[detail withheld] with information on your federation. Thanks (It is Monday night..... around 9:30pm.... In the Georgia Dome. MONDAY NIGHT
NITRO!!!!!!!!!!!!! In the ring you see The Outsiders, Kevin Nash & Scott Hall.
Ranting & raving.. but then you take a closer look & they are Degrading &
YELLING at their Valet, Kimberly Sue. As they continue to embarrass & humiliate her, Like they do EVERY MONDAY NIGHT, she starts to cry & crumple up like a torn rag..... Yet they still continue to harass her & make a fool out of her. ALL OF A SUDDEN.....this new wrestler runs out from the Dressing rooms into the arena... He's not necessarily new, He's been watching this, this debasement, the way they abuse her for quite sometime now. He FINALLY decided to make his move. Dressed in Jeans & sneakers, he Jumps into the ring, takes
Kevin & Scott out with a few moves ALL his own & they are out cold. During all of this, Kimberly Sue has ended up on the floor mat, in a ball shivering & crying... JIMMY ANDREWS looks for Kimberly Sue as he jumps down from the ring apron. He sees her & walks over. reaches out his hands to her & she SLOWLY takes his hands. He gently but firmly pulls her to him & looks into her trembling eyes & whispers softly, "Its ok now baby, Your with me". He sweeps her up into his arms & carries her off to his dressing room. & from that night forward, Kimberly Sue is ALWAYS with him.
"The Sexy One" Jimmy Andrews: "Well you just saw how I came onto the wrestling scene and I owe it all to this beautiful girl standing next to me. Her name is
Kimberly Sue and now she had found someone who will love her and cherish her forever. You saw how she was treated in that other rinky dink promotion. I have promised this young lady that no one will ever harm her again. It's about time she found someone who will love her for who she is and that person is me.
I am going to work my ass of to get to the top of the promotion that accepts me. I am going to win and I am going to win for Kimberly. She deserves nothing but the best and I am going to provide that for her. If anyone out there thinks for one damn minute that they are going to try anything with Kim, they better think twice. You mess with Kim, then you mess with me!"
