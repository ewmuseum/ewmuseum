[name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) writes: > news flash for you: playboy ronnie d sucks. he gets my vote for most > over-rated wrestler of the year. > News flash for you: Capitalization is a good thing. Use in moderation, though; like in proper names. On a less childish side, he gets my vote for most over-rated, too. In fact, I don't mind if you put it on your web page. In fact, why doesn't Brock bring back most over-rated and hand the trophy over to Ronnie? Michael, here's the real news flash: "Playboy" Ronnie D is still a developing character. I know, I know. 7-8 months, that's a LONG time to develop a character. The fact of the matter is, most can see that he has made leaps and bounds since he started. Hell, he's improved by leaps and bounds since September! "Playboy" Ronnie D has the potential to be a good character in my opinion and in lots of other people's opinions. One day, in two or three years, he could be the mega-heel for the next best fed. Who knows? I see people brave enough to publish their flashes on RSPWF, and all they get is flamed. At least I have gotten more positive responses than negative. I feel that that is a success because I realize that on the internet, there are tons of people that will NEVER say that someone else's work is good and they are the constant flamers. Michael, I don't want to jump all over you, I respect you; but I think most people realise that "Playboy" can be a good character, and I might even say that he IS a good character right now. He may not have 30k flashes, he may not be a people's favorite, but he has potential. A lot more potential than Joe Blow from a SHEF who's only purpose in life is to, "kick some freakin' ass, mang!" I don't know, Duda. Maybe you don't like the fact that "The Best of 'Playboy' Ronnie D" is part of the gimmick. Maybe you don't like the fact that the web page is part of the gimmick. Maybe you can't stand wrestlers who hype themselves to the max and flaunt their looks at girls. Michael, if I tell you what I LIKE about "Playboy," will you tell me what you DISLIKE? Here's what I like: 1) He can pull a one-liner out of his hat most of the time. 2) He's cocky and arrogant and he backs it all up in the ring. (Which I find refreshing. Not all those cocky, arrogant wrestlers win their matches.) 3) He uses his words to get what he wants. (Pushes, matches, etc.) 4) He always finds some creative way of cheating. Now, what do you hate about him? I mean this to be a friendly discussion. In fact, RSPWF could turn this into a "What do you hate about this type of character thread." And if you want to turn this into a flame war, fine. But for everyone's sake let's not turn this into a dumbass, "yer karaktor sux," debate.  -- [name withheld] -=- President & Owner of [detail withheld] [detail withheld] http://[detail withheld].home.ml.org/ As soon as they idiot-proof the world, a better idiot will come along.
