<Flash> Precious Moments

5 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Moze, you took to long to put the card out. So I'm posting the flash now. Tough cookies, wookie. 8) ------------------------- [The scene opens on the same scene as last time... which is that of a comfy brown couch, books on a shelf, and a very serene scene overall. Only this time, the large white guy with the white beard is not there. In his place is a big black guy, who seems to have a familiar air to him, though he has a book in front of his face so you can't see whom it is. Then, he puts the book down.
For some reason, his face seems familiar, though not quite placeable] BBG (Big Black Guy): Yo. Wazzup? I'm Brian J. Blottie, and I'm in da hizous, yo. We's be all up in dis mother to read yo bitch-asses a story from dis 'ere book. Y'all ready?
[Like last time, in comes the skinny white boy, revealed last time as the REAL Brian J. Blottie. He doesn't look happy] BJB (Brian J. Blottie): What the hell is this? Why are you talking like that? [The black man gives Blottie a dirty look] BBG: Wazzit look like I'm doin', honkey? I'm readin' dis damn book, like y'all are paying me to do.
BJB: No. That damn accent. BBG: This ain't no damn accent, mofo. This is da way I's talk. If y'all don't like it, I'll put a cap in yo ass and MAKE ya like it.
BJB: Screw you, LaVar. Listen, it was humorous when you played the blind guy on Star Trek. But we all know that Reading Rainbow was your greatest achievement. So stop talking like your hard.
BBG: What da fug you be talkin' about, boy? I ain't never been in no Readin' Rainbow. You tink I'm gay, bitch?! BJB: No, Precious Moments are gay. You're supposed to be a white acting black guy, though.
BBG: I ain't no honkey. BJB: You're not LaVar Burton? BBG: Who da fug is dat? BJB: GET OUT! GET OFF OF MY SET! BBG: Yo, I don't need dis. Dis is discrimination. I'll be waitin' for yo bitch ass after the show, nigga. Then ya ass is mine.
[The larger black guy leaves, as Blottie starts trembling with anger] BJB: You wait outside! I'll kick your ass like your ass hasn't been kicked before! [The entire 5'8", 135 pound frame of Blottie shakes as the big black guy walks out] BJB: Why do all my heroes have to be such... such... PHONIES! All I asked my publicist for is LaVar Burton. And I get... THAT! I swear, I'm going to fire him...
[Blottie stops shaking, cracks his neck, and sits down] BJB: Looks like I have to sit in for this crap AGAIN. Hello, kids. It's time for another edition of "Pick Your Nose While Trying to Fart the Alphabet... Or
100 Ways To Make Precious Moments Hate You". I'm your unwilling host, Brian J. Blottie, and it's time to read from this book. [Blottie holds up the brown book the big black guy was holding. The title on the cover is "How To Get A Date". Brian looks down at the cover, and turns beet red]
BJB: How'd he get into my secret library?! I swear, I'm going to kill that guy when I get out of here. But for now, I'll save this book for later...
[Blottie slides the book down the back of the seat cushion, and picks up another book that is sitting next to him on the couch. He holds this one up.
On the cover is three words... "Precious Moments Fables"] BJB: Are you ready for another story, kiddies? Too bad. You get to hear one anyway... [Blottie opens the book, and the picture begins to twist and swirl. Then it reforms onto a large tree with a smiley face on it. Sitting under the tree are
The Binky Fairy and The Winky Fairy, also known as Precious Moments. They are both sporting wide smiles]
BJB: Hello, Binky... Winky. TBF: Hiya! TWF: Howsa ya doin'? BJB: Ummm... Winky... TWF: Yessir? BJB: Why do you have an Italian accent? TWF: Because-a I am-a tryin' to be-a like Fire and-a Ice. I am tryin' to relate-a. BJB: Last time I checked... Fire and Ice aren't Italian. TBF: Really?! TWF: Then why do they call that yummy stuff they sell in the arena "Italian Ice"? [A loud smacking sound, that of a hand on a forehead, is heard] BJB: Why are you trying to relate to Fire and Ice, anyway? TBF: We have to team with them against those evil guys, Towers of Havok, and those other two guys that we accidentally hit, Psicko and Kenny Vann.
TWF: I'm sorry... TBF: So am I... [Binky and Winky pout] BJB: Don't be sorry. This is professional wrestling. You're supposed to beat the living crap out of anyone and everyone that's not you. It's just the way things work.
TBF: That's mean, Mr. Narrator-guy. We don't beat ANYONE up. We make people realize the error of their ways.
TWF: And Mr. Vann and Mr. Psicko never did anything wrong... so we can't just hit them and not apologize.
TBF: It's against our Code of Ethics. BJB: Oh God... I don't know why I'm asking this question... what is this Code of Ethics? [Binky and Winky suddenly perk up a bit, and smile] TWF: The Code of Ethics is something that we have to follow at all times! It tells us how to be the best fairies there are...
TBF: The best fairies there were... TWF: And the best... BJB: Knock that off. I'm pretty sure copyright infringement is covered somewhere in that book as "being evil".
TBF: We are who we are because of the Code of Ethics. BJB: So where in there does it say you have to be gay to be a fairy? [Winky starts rooting around in his tights, and then pulls out a pocket-sized book. He begins flipping through it]
TWF: Page 134, Rule 334... "You must be gay at all times". BJB: Explains a lot... though I'm pretty sure you guys don't need a book to tell you to be gay.
TBF: Nope! It comes natural to us fairies! BJB: I would hope so. Otherwise you wouldn't be a fairy. TWF: You're getting smarter! BJB: No, I'm just getting more annoyed. Let's get back to the story... how's that title tree coming?
[Blottie snickers a bit] TBF: It's coming along great! LOOK! [Binky points up at the big tree they are standing under. We pan up, and we see at least two dozen ACWA Tag Team titles hanging from the tree]
BJB: Oh... my... God. You've GOT to be kidding me. TWF: There's only one problem, though... TBF: When we try to take them out of Fairyland, they disappear on us. BJB: Disappear... this I have to hear... TWF: That rhymed! Good job! BJB: Shut up. Tell me about these titles "disappearing". TBF: You see... Fairyland is different than where you're from. The substance held within Fairyland is made completely out of candy...
BJB: What?! What the hell are you talking about? Everything's made out of CANDY?! TWF: Yep! [Winky picks some grass and begins chewing on it] TWF: Mmmm... beefy. BJB: Beef candy? Ugh. TBF: You see, everything hear is edible... including the title belts. So when we try to take them into your world, they melt on us. So our plan to give everyone in the world a beautiful title belt has been foiled.
[Binky and Winky pout] BJB: Ok... and this means? TWF: It means we gotta find someone worthy to take these title belts! They're too pretty for us. We don't deserve them.
TBF: But first, we need to find a way to make Fire and Ice like us. What do you think, Mr. Narrator-guy?
BJB: I don't know... do what you do best. Bend them over and... TWF: ... pat them on the butt! Yeah! I saw those big guys doing it Sunday! TBF: Oh yeah! That Big Bowl thing that all those humans always watch. But those guys are mean to each other...
TWF: True... BJB: How about you pull out your... TBF: ... magic fairy wands! We'll bink them and make them like us! TWF: But our magic fairy wands are only for evil people. Fire and Ice aren't evil. TBF: Fiddlesticks... BJB: Well, Perkins is English. You can always take them out for tea and crumpets. [Blottie chuckles, as Binky and Winky pop up, smiling] TWF: That's it! We'll do that! TBF: WOO! Thank you, Mr. Narrator-guy! [Binky and Winky begins dancing around in circles, when a title belt suddenly falls down off the tree and smacks Binky in the head]
TBF: OW! [Winky picks up the title belt and then takes a big bite out of it] TWF: Liver and onions... mmmmm... BJB: SICK! Liver and onions candy title belts?! Let's get the fug out of here... [The scene begins swirling once again, and when we can see one collected image again, it is of Brian J. Blottie, closing the book with a thud]
BJB: That's just enough of that. I'm outta here. You guys go do something else now. Like... I don't know... change the channel or go make some popcorn.
Better yet, go into the bathroom and get rid of that crap you're so full of. I'm out of here until next time... [Blottie tosses the brown book onto the brown couch and begins walking away. Suddenly, from off-camera, a scream is heard. It is Blottie's voice, and one other one...]
BJB: AH! LET ME GO YOU... YOU... Other Voice: Dat's it, honkey. Your ass is mine. [The sounds of punching, kicking, and metal on metal are heard, as we are left with three words on the screen, in bold, pink writing...]
