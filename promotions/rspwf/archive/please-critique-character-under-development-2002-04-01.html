[Please Critique] Character Under Development

1 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm working on this character and I'm curious if it would work. I think it would but I want your opinions. Also, I don't have an entrance theme for him yet. Any suggestions?
Name: "Everyone's Idol" Quinn Murray Height: 6'2" Weight: 230 lbs Hometown: Hollywood, California Allignment: heel Gimmick: Quinn sees himself as a big-time movie star. Although he has starred in some movies, he has never held the leading role. In the ring, he has the belief that everyone (including the other wrestlers) idolizes him because of his status in the movie business. That's why he calls himself "Everyone's
Idol." He thinks that everyone wishes they had his looks, his success, and his in-ring abilities. Obviously he is wrong. The crowd hate him because of his cockiness and arrogance.
Describe Wrestler: Quinn has short brown hair and blue eyes. He is pretty well built. Not flabby, but not like a bodybuilder either. Overall, he has a well rounded physique. His face shows of his youthfulness. But his eyes show the determination of a man who knows exactly what he wants, and then goes and gets.
Ring Attire: Quinn comes to the ring wearing a robe. A studio-style picture of him is on the back, surrounded by many twinkling yellow stars. Yes, they actually twinkle as light reflects off them. On the front, in a glittering bright orange, are the words "Everyone's Idol." They too, are surrounded by the twinkling yellow stars. The background color of the robe is a navy blue, symbolizing a night sky. Once the robe is removed, his wrestling gear is revealed. He wears navy blue tights that go down to his yellow boots. His tights hold a similar pattern as his robe. They are mainly covered in yellow stars. However, on the outer left leg are the glittering orange letters
"QUINN." The outer right leg has the same style of letters, with the word
"MURRAY" on them. He doesn't wear a shirt, an attempt to impress the crowd with his muscular physique. The word "Idol" is tattoo'ed on his lower back.
He wears navy wristbands, matching the pattern on his tights. Moves: arm drag, missile dropkick, standing dropkick, DDT, snap suplex, cross body block, bulldog, springboard sommersault headbutt (as opponent lays face up on mat), running neckbreaker, backbreaker, baseball slide dropkick, superkick, vertical suplex, tornado DDT, belly-to-back suplex, fisherman's suplex, dragon screw legwhip, abdominal stretch, drop toe hold, bow and arrow submission, swinging neckbreaker, elbow drop, hip toss, snap mare, scoop slam
Setup Move: Hollywood Hotshot Describe Setup: A side kick to the gut Finisher: The Movie-Maker Describe Finisher: top-rope Stunner - Quinn sits his opponent on the top turnbuckle. He then climbs up to the middle turnbuckle, facing away from his opponent, and executes a Stone Cold Stunner from there, using the ropes as a catapult for a little more height on the move.Manager: The Idol's Entourage -
Like the Godfather's escorts, these lovely ladies will accompany Murray down to the ring. They remain at ringside during his matches. Sometimes one of them will hand him a pair of brass knucks, a roll of quarters, etc. that he can use to beat his opponent.
