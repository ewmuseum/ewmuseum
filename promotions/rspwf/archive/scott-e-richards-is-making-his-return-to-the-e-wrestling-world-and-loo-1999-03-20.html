Scott-E Richards is making his return to the E-Wrestling World and looking for a fed

20 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Like everyone has said before, you can't stay away from this game for long. I am back in this great sport of E-Wrestling, and I am looking for a fed. I am also making a few changes to my guy. Instead of being Scott "Skatin" Richards he is now going to be "Hot Stuff" Scott Richards. it fits much better with his new gimmick. I have been in this sport for almost a year. I've been in such feds as HHWO, UKCW (For a very short time), ICW, and others. I am looking for just one fed now. Well, I haven't done a RP since my return so here is an old in-ring I did for the ICW a while back. It was my intro RP for this fed, and I was just trying to get some cheap heat with some of the bigger names in that fed. I can also write cards once in a while for some the fed that I decide to join. Send all offers to [detail withheld] and my AIM is [detail withheld].
[Make you say UHH, by Master P blast all though the arena, and fireworks start to go off near the entrance of the rampway leading towards the ring. Out pops
Scott "Skatin" Richards though the ICW curtains, and the crowd goes wilds. Scotty has no shirt on, and is wearing a pair of black tights that comes down to his ankles, and some black Oakleys. As the crowd is cheering he does the raise the roof sign. He is now walking towards the fans that are leaning against the metal guardrail. He walks up to some real good looking blonde. He then grabs one of her arms real gently, and quickly whispers something into her ear. She shakes her head OK. He then bends over a little and sticks his tongue down her throat. The crowd is loving it, and are going nuts. He then struts the rest of the way to the ring. He climbs the stairs, and goes though the ropes. He goes to the conner of the ring, and grabs a mic. He trys to speak, but the crowd is still going crazy, and he can't talk over them. He waits a few seconds, and they start to calm down. Then the music stops. He begins to speak again.]
Scotty Richards: Damn, it is great to be back in this f[bleep]g sport. [huge pops from the crowd] God, I guess I didn't know how much all of you guys have missed me. Something isn't right. I got to fix this one thing. Hold on.
[The crowd looks a little surprised. They have no clue what Scotty means. Scotty then drops the mic, and then starts climbing through the ropes, and then jumps down. He looks around for a second. He then spots something. He walks over to another blonde. He then grabs here hand, but this time he starts to pull her over the guardrail. She is wearing some blue jeans with a white shirt that says GAP on the front. Together they climb up the stairs and back into the ring. Scotty picks up the mic again.]
.............Well, this is much more like it don't think. [He then has a huge smile on his face, and then grabs her ass. She then just looks into his eyes for a second, and she also has a great smile on her face.] You guys see this is why I came into this sport of wrestling. To pick up chicks. Is this not the life or not? What is your name young girl?
Girl: My name is Allison. Scotty Richards: That is a great name Allison. How old are you? Allison: I'm, uh, uh 24. Scotty Richards: Hey don't be nervous. I am not here to do you any harm now. Maybe tonight it will be different though. So how is your favorite wrestler here in the ICW? Tell me the truth. Just don't say me to be nice.
Allison: Well, it is, "The Diamond" Shane Diamond. He can really turn me on anytime. I have actually never even heard of you into tonight.
Scotty Richards: [He now has a stunned look on his face] What the f[bleep]g hell are you talking about? You b[bleep]h. That was the wrong damn answer.
[He grabs her by the arm] You better got off this ring right now, before, before, well you know what is going to happen. [Allison breaks free and runs out of the ring.]
................As I was saying before it is great to be back. [This time it is mixed with cheers and boos.] Aw, who needs you guys anyway. Shane Diamond, what a joke he is. Just because he is the North American Champion and in EGO he thinks he is all that and stuff. Well, he isn't. I am. I do want to make a shout out to one man in the ICW. He is, yup you guessed it. He is the The
Loose Cannon of the ICW, he is Tommy Grayson. Tommy is one hell of a fighter. People like Triple X and Diamond are at his heel, because of what he is doing with Sam. Tommy, I love the way you are treating those nice Tits that are hanging around you. You have to make Sam get in line a little. She hit with you with a chair. If that was my woman I would have clocked her right there and then. I hate these men like Triple X and the roster of EGO who is saying they are trying to save Sam. She couldn't be getting a better deal being with a winner like Tommy, unless she was with me. Boy would she have a great time then. Grayson like your style, and I can't say that about to many men here.
To show you some good faith I am going to do you a favor. I am going to knock that fly that is keep trying to bother you. "NIGHT HAUNTER" KONRAD CURZE your ass is mine next week. Pillow, there is another match right there that you are going to need to book for fallout. CURZE, you are just a joke, and Tommy doesn't need you trying to cling on to him to get a little cheap heat.
[Scotty then starts to look around, and scanning the crowd. He then ...................Some people might be starting to wonder what I want here in the ICW. At this point in time I must say it has to be the JUNIORWEIGHT TITLE.
It is held by Miss Jasmine who also holds the Woman's title. I wouldn't mind being the ref in some of the womans matches if you know what I mean. [Big smile] That Juniorweight title is going to be fitting around my waist very nicely in just a short time. I don't care if I have to be put up against
Jasmine or Tommy Loinheart. Soon enough my time will come. Speaking of match I got one later on tonight. Against someone that goes by the name of Aaron Michael. Hell, if he wants to be the first one that gets taken out by myself then go be it. He knows what's up by getting into this fight. Maybe he even knows what is down if you can get my drift.
......YOU...... ......BETTER...... ......REPRESENT.... ...............If you know what is good for you. [Make you say UHH, by Master P starts to play again, and Richards leaves the ring. He once again starts to walk over to the guardrail. He then starts to talk to some red head, and then she starts to climb over the rail. The two do a little bum and grind to the music together, and then all of a sudden some hand reaches over the rail, and pulls the lady make over. Scotty looks and it seems to be her boyfriend or husband, so Scott just looks at him and shrugs his shoulders. He then struts back into the locker room.]
Scott Is still thinking why he is coming out of retirement.
