[Roleplay] Organized Violence (Please critique)

7 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first roleplay using this tag team and it's for the MOTR tournament. Please give me opinions on how to improve the gimmick and the teams itself. Thanks. -CJ [The scene is inside JFK Airport in New York. It's about 4:00 in the morning and the camera focuses in on a United Air terminal. Many people are fast asleep and some people are holding their luggage or their children while actively reading a book or listening to a Walkman. Near the front of the terminal are two men who seem to be talking about something. One has tanned skin, long light brown hair tied back into a ponytail with hazel eyes. He has a lot of guff growing, but not so much that can be considered a beard. The other man has darkly toned skin, has a bald head, dark brown eyes, and is clean-shaven. The smaller of the two is wearing black Nike warm-ups and a black Nike tanktop with black Nike sandals. He also has on a black Nike cap turned to the back and dark Ray-Ban sunglasses. The other man has on baggy black Guess jeans that fall to his ankles, covering up most of his black Nike hightops. He also has on a sleeveless plain black shirt on as well along with a black Gatsby hat turned to the back. They're looking over a sheet of paper and they seem to be satisfied. The larger of the two begins to speak.]
MAN - Well, "Showtime" Chris Walker, it seems as if we've made it to the big time. "SHOWTIME" CHRIS WALKER - "Tru Soldja" Marcus Simmons, it seems that you're right. Masters of the Ring, it's definitely "Showtime" as Organized Violence has arrived. Me and my partner Marcus Simmons have been waiting for three years for a chance to come to the big time and have waited for a chance to be apart of something big. Now, it's our chance and we don't intend to blow it.
"TRU SOLDJA" MARCUS SIMMONS - This is our chance to turn three years of hard work into something special. It's time to break out of the mold called mediocrity and rise up to superstardom.
"S" CW - That's right, Marcus. Now, looking over the roster, we're going to be facing a team by the name of Flying High, Feeling Higher. [Smirks] I guess drug tests are nonexistent in the Masters of the Ring tournament. But, it doesn't matter because if you guys weren't drugged before, you'll feel drugged once the match is over. We came here looking for a reputation -- win, lose, or draw.
We plan on making a reputation in this tournament, but we're smart enough to know that our reputation will be etched in stone if we win it. Not just the match, but the whole damn tourney. If that means steamrolling through you two to get the deuce deuce, we'll do it.
"TS" MS - As history goes down, no one remembers who opposed Genghis Kahn when he led Mongolia to total control of Asia, everyone knows he just did it and as the tournament dwindles down -- no one will remember who we beat, the wrestling historians will just remember that Organized Violence were the proverbial
Mongols of the tournament.
"S" CW - Oh . . . kay. Thanks for those words, Marc. Back to the present, back to "Showtime." This tournament is all about to show who's the best and I guarantee that Organized Violence is the best. This is three years in the making and if you're doubting our ability, just look at me. I have more moves than Picaso has paint, more charisma than the beach has sand, and have more greatness running through my veins then Bill Gates has dollar bills. [Smiles]
And my partner's built like a brickhouse, wrestles like there's no tomorrow, and tougher than the press trying to get an honest answer out of Bill Clinton.
I see that HBO has been hyping Titanic lately. Titanic on HBO isn't the thing to be looking for. "Showtime" in the Masters of the Ring Tournament is the hottest ticket around right now and along with my costar, "Showtime" may be the showstarter on night two, but he'll also prove to be the showstealer!
"TS" MS - Seek and Destroy. What an appropriate name. Organized Violence seeks recognition and respect and we're willing to destroy any obstacles in our way to get it.
"S" CW - Thanks for adding in your two cents, now it's time for "Showtime" to add in the rest of the ninety-eight cents. [Arrogant smirk] Flying High,
Feeling Higher . . . I'm going to put this in terms you can understand. You'll get _BLAZED UP_ and _SMOKED OUT_ at the hands of Organized Violence because
"Showtime" Chris Walker and "Tru Soldja" Marcus Simmons are here to establish ourselves as the next big thing in tag team wrestling. Bigger than Mount
Everest, bigger than Alaska . . .
"TS" MS - [Interrupting] . . . bigger than your ego. "S" CW - [Smiling] Hey, when you're "Showtime" Chris Walker, an ego like this is enforced by law. But, you're right, Marc. Organized Violence will be bigger than my ego. Flying High, Feeling Higher . . . your reservations at the
Organized Violence Hotel are confirmed and the only thing left to do is for
Organized Violence to check your asses in with broken bones, severed limbs, and bruised body parts. I hope you guys enjoyed Easter because the next time you'll be talking to your relatives, it'll be through 1-800 Collect from your hospital beds.
"TS" MS - We realize that Flying High, Feeling Higher isn't our only competition in the tournament. But, like Napoleon and France, we're just going in and taking over . . . whether anyone likes it or not. The Eclipse seems to be legendary force. However, these two don't seem to realize that an eclipse can also describe a falling into obscurity or decline and should these two come into the ring with Organized Violence, that definition would most definitely come true.
"S" CW - Cripp and Eagle? One guy sounds like a potato chip brand and the other is a bird. Yeah, they're going to be a force to be reckoned with. [Rolls eyes] If we see you guys in the tournament, we'll turn the chips stale and shoot down the eagle.
"TS" MS - Desolation Row. Desolation means grief, sadness, loneliness, devastation, and ruin . . . a barren wasteland. Very rhadamanthine label. Get in our way in the tournament and desolation would be too good of a word to describe you two.
"S" CW - Nytro and Glyce. Either there's a typo in the program or you two just can't spell . . . and I'm betting on the latter. If you can't spell right, what makes you think you can beat us? It's quite simple, you're beaten down
Pintos and we're brand new 1999 Lincoln Towncars, that just means we're all class and you guys just suck ass.
"TS" MS - Xtreme Blockbusters. A part of this little "extreme" fad. I look at the name of the team and just know you guys are nothing but a joke. And blockbusters is not the appropriate appellation for you two. The rightful label you guys should earn is a dud because . . .
"S" CW - "Showtime" is the _ONLY_ blockbuster in this tournament. Four stars, top credit, multimillion dollars, the leading man . . .
"TS" MS - Chris, just get on to the next team, please. "S" CW - Good, Bad, and Ugly. It's good that you made it in the tournament, bad that you're going to lose, and I guess you two are just plain ugly. I mean, just look at m . . .
VOICE OVER - United Air flight 465 from New York to Los Angeles now boarding. "TS" MS - Chris, it's time to go. "S" CW - Guys, I hate to inform each and every one of the hopeful tag teams in this tournament of this but when we're in the ring . . .
. . . YOU GUYS ARE FLYING COACH . . . "TS" MS and "S" CW - . . . AND ORGANIZED VIOLENCE IS FLYING _FIRST CLASS_ ! ! !
