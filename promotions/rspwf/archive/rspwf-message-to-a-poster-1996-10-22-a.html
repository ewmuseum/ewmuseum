Janis let me state some points of mine. - Booked feds have a simulator, the best one there is. A HUMAN BRAIN! - Booked feds are based on REAL wrestling just as much as a sim league. - Booked feds don't let a 7'11 monster win all the time, or most of the time unless that person is active - Booked feds are like real wrestling just for that reason. They are booked! Vinny Mac and Eric B. don't make their decisions via TNM! - Booked feds offer more flexibility in their stipulations. Yes, TNM 6.2+ is included when I say this. - Booked feds offer MUCH better matches. - Booked feds have more member activity in most cases - Booked feds are more fun (IMHO) because you get rewarded for your hard work, AND have fun at the same time. - Booked feds add more originality, not the same run of the mill stuff. - Booked feds .... RULE! IMHO of course. 'Nough Said! Kent
