Puah the "Hawaiian Love Machine" needs a fed to call home.

I'm looking for an established fed in need of an unique characther. I can offer up around (2) quality roleplays a week , so needless to say I'm looking for a fed where this is the norm amongst all the handlers.
Thanks , Biggz HANDLER INFO Biggz [detail withheld] WRESTLER INFORMATION Wrestler's Name: Puah the "Hawaiian Love Machine" Age: 26 Weight: 310lbs. Height: 6'3" Hometown: Honolulu , Hawaii Manager/Valet Name: n/a Alignment: Face Appearance: Standing in at 6'3" and tipping the scale at 310lbs. , this native Hawaiian is large and in charge.
Now don't be fooled by his weight becuase this Hawaiian loverboy and former
Wolrd's Strongest Man competitior , is pretty damn solid , matter a fact he is less then 12% body fat.
His complextion is a nice golden brown , with short black hair. He wears a golden hoop earring in each of his ears and is always dressed to impress.
Chances are you've never seen a man with more silk in his wardrobe. His ring attire consists of navy blue & gold wrestling tights which are knee length and a white flowered lei around his neck , which is removed and given to some lucky woman in the crowd before the match. He wrestles barefoot to add an unique touch.
Theme Music: Puah enters to a native Hawaiian drumbeat. Finishing Move: The Puah Plunge Finishing Move Description : Basically Puah's version of the Death Valley
Driver. STRENGTHS & WEAKNESSES List 3 Strengths: Power Charisma Strength List 3 Weaknesses Slow Showboat's Stamina BACKGROUND & PROFILE Puah grew up on the islands of Hawaii. From the beginning it was obvious that Puah was a natural born competitor and throughout his whole life , he was the biggest on the block. Pauh's claim to fame was his strength and by the age of 18 he was entering and winning his first Strong Man Competition.
This success as "Worlds Strongest Man" not only brought Pauh tons of money and offers , but it brought about something else. That something else was the admiration of the ladies! Tons and tons of ladies!
Needless to say , money , success and a different woman everyday of the week brought about an ego in Puah and what better place to showcase himself , then professioanl wrestling!
World are you ready for Pauh the "Hawaiian Love Machine"?!
