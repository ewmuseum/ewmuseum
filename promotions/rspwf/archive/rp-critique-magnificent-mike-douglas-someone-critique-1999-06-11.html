[RP CRITIQUE] "Magnificent" Mike Douglas [SOMEONE CRITIQUE]

11 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, This is my latest roleplay I did for the IWF/WOW. Just someone critique it or tell me what you think.
==================================================== ---[A *SPECIAL* Mike Douglas Press Conference]---- [The scene opens up just after this weeks Monday Mayhem has concluded. Mike
Douglas has decided to talk to the press about the latest happenings in the
IWF/WOW. The camera opens up to see a large crowd of reporters sitting in a few rows of seats which have been arranged. We also see a stage at the front of this room with a speaking podium on it. Printed on the podium is "IWF/WOW" with a big circle around it. The crowd stands to their feet as they notice
"Magnificent" Mike Douglas just making his way out to the podium. Mike is wearing a pair of khaki Tommy Hillfiger pants with a black silk vest and no shirt on. The glare from his wrist almost blinds some of the reporters as they notice he is wearing a gold rolex watch. Mike has his hair pulled back in a pony tail and is wearing a pair of black sunglasses. He steps up next to the mic and takes off his sunglasses and lays them on top of the podium. He then begins to speak......]
DOUGLAS: Everyone, Everyone, Calm down please. I know that at the sight of me,
You can't contain yourselves but you have to try to. I came out here to ansewer any questions you guys want to ask me and to discuss my plans for the near future so, Who has the first question?
[A man in a black suit stands up in the crowd] REPORTER #1: Mr.Douglas, We all saw you defeat Hell Spawn and Payne in a
Triple Threat Match tonight. But during that match a man named Razor Ron Jeremy came out and basically told you, Stay out of his match. He even hit you with a
Lawn Chair after the match had concuded. Later that night you came down and you and Ron Jeremy got into some fight. What are your comments on Razor Ron Jeremy?
DOUGLAS: Hmmm....Razor Ron Jeremy. A man who thinks he can come out on Mike
Douglas's time and tell _ME_ what to do. He even went as far as to touch my beautiful face with his filthy lawn chair. Well Ron, You stick your nose in my buisness, Your gonna end up getting it knocked off. You don't _EVER_ tell Mike
Douglas what to do and you found that out at the cost of the US National Title.
A US National Title that _I_ made famous might I add. Ron, Your actually even stupider than you look. Think about it. You had the US National Title won. And what did you do when you had Iceman right where you wanted him? You went after me, Knowing that you were getting yourself into a fight, You couldn't win. So,
We fought, You didn't win the US National Title and now you have the unfortunate task of facing me.
REPORTER #1: How soon can we see Ron Jeremy and yourself in the ring? DOUGLAS: Well, On the way out here I bumped into Presdent Jurkschat. He told me the match has been signed for next week on Mayhem. And I'd be lying if I said that didn't bring a smile to my face. Razor Ron Jeremy, You want a piece of Mike Douglas, You got a piece of Mike Douglas. But Ron, You've bitten off
_WAY_ more than you can handle. Next question.
[A woman stands up in the crowd with a microphone] REPORTER #2: Mike, Before your match with Razor Ron Jeremy, You have an even bigger match. And that is this Saturday on Battlezone, You face Hightower for the North American Championship. What are your thought on this match?
DOUGLAS: Last week was supposed to be the night when Mike Douglas shined. It was supposed to be the night when Mike Douglas took Hightower's North American
Championship and claimed it as his. _BUT_ thanks to some rookie trying to make a name for himself, I didn't get that shot. Hell Spawn knocked me out before I could take you title Hightower, I've already taken care of him. Now it's time to take care of you. This time there won't be any Hell Spawn to save you from losing your title. This time I will be reconized as the North American
Champion, Because I'm just that good. And if you don't like it, Too damn bad. REPORTER #2: Mike, Hightower is one of the IWF/WOW's greats, How can you be so sure that this will be an easy match for you?
DOUGLAS: Miss, First off all, I see you giving me the eye over there like most women do. They all want to be with Mike Douglas and I can see you are no exception. I don't blame you one bit. But just because you asked, This will be an easy match for Mike Douglas. I don't think Hightower is prepared for this match. I bet you he is sitting at home with a bag of potato chips, sitting on his sofa, watching this. He hasn't had a challenge like Mike Douglas yet and that is why he is still the North American Champion. Who has he faced?
REPORTER #2: He did beat Sean Eden for that belt. And he did successfully defend against Randy Bryson.
DOUGLAS: Who? Give me a break. You call those contenders? Both those guys combined can't compare to one Mike Douglas. Trust me, Hightower has faced nobody and now he's gonna have to wake up to reality and realize, The free ride ends this saturday. And you have yours truly to thank for it. Next Question.
[Another man in a black suit stands up] REPORTER #3: Mike, Tonight after you and Ron Jeremy had brawled all the way backstage, A match was announce for the next PPV , Egos and Icons. A four way match involving Iceman, Djinn of Ernham, Razor Ron Jeremy and yourself. What do you think about this?
DOUGLAS: Hmm...A chance to regain my US National Title. A title that I held longer than _ANY_ man in IWF/WOW history. And what do I have to do, Beat three guys? Tonight I proved I can beat two guys, So three will certainly be no problem. Djinn, I've never faced you, But it doesn't really matter. Come the
PPV your gonna get a beating at the hands of Mike Douglas. Like it or not.
Iceman, It looks like we are gonna meet again. You _STOLE_ my US National Title from me with a _CHEAP_ win. This time around things are a little bit different.
Now, I will regain my US National Title from you and extract my revenge at the same time. Iceman, You got lucky once, Lightning doesn't strike twice. And it won't.
[he pauses] DOUGLAS: And finally, The last man in this match is none other than Razor Ron
Jeremy. The same man I face Monday on Mayhem. Ron, One beating won't be enough,
I'll need to give you another one. One on Monday and one at Egos and Icons.
This should be fun. [Mike begins to laugh, then continues] DOUGLAS: And let me just see, By the time Egos and Icons rolls around, I will have face the North American Champion, And at the PPV I will face off for the
US National Championship, And also at the PPV I have a chance to win the
Independence Rumble, To get a shot at the World Title. Man, I could hold every belt with the exception of the Intercontinental Title. I have to be good.
REPORTER #3: Mike, Lets talk about the Independence Day Rumble at Egos and
Icons. You and 19 other superstars will battle it out to see who will face the
World Champion at Independence Day 4. We all know you are 0-2 when it comes to
Battle Royals and we all know you don't like Battle Royals. What si your strategy coming into this match?
DOUGLAS: What the hell do you think this is? You think I'm just going to give away my strategy to the world? You obviously don't know much about wrestling.
REPORTER #3: Okay, Forget I said that. We all know their are some big names in this Battle Royal, Such as "Professor"Steve West, Hightower and "Legacy" Johnny
Skiles. These men have all been in this Independence Day Rumble, You haven't.
Do you have any comments on this? DOUGLAS: You people just don't get it. No matter how many times I seem to tell you. I am the greatest think goin today. It doesn't matter how long these guys have been around or how many Independene Day Rumbles they have been in. They have to deal with the fact they they don't have the skills of Mike Douglas.
They can claim to be a "Professor", they can claim to be a "Legacy". But none of them can claim to be......."Magnificent".
[Mike pauses, he looks at his watch and then continues] DOUGLAS: Whoa. It looks like it's time for this conference to come to an end. I have a lot of things to do. So, Once again, It's time, for the hottest marketing phrase in the history of wrestling. You can think your Good, You can think your Great, But.....
YOU WILL NEVER BE MAGNIFICENT!!!! [Mike steps down off the podium and exits the room] =============
