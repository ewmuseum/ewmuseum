looking for a E-Fed [ Free Agent ] Sample

17 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE] The RaTTle Snake is in a gym somewhere where no one is able to find him and Gate Keeper , as they prepare for the upcoming match with John Myles.
There are several people around him. Through a window you see it is pitch black outside. RaTTle Snake is on the inclined bench, he is pressing about two fifty...he's not having much trouble pumping the iron up and down. After doing fifteen reps he racks the weights. RaTTle Snake slides off the bench picks up a towel and wipes the sweat from his face. He walks out of the view of the camera. [camera fades then cuts to the door of a steam room] RaTTle Snake goes to the door and opens it, as it slowly opens steam pours from the crack.
Snake is wearing a towel around his waste enters the room, where inside we see his friend and trainer Gate keeper. Gate keeper lifts the towel off his face, nods at The Snake, then replaces the towel back over his face. Snake sits across from Gate and closes his eyes. About an hour later the two men get up and leave the steam room heading for the shower...Gate says boy Snake that was the bes workout i have seen from you since X8oD claose it's doors...
RaTTle Snake Much time has past since the last time we have met my friends...Has it not?...My agenda does not always meet that of others...My agenda has put me elsewhere for some time...But now, it seems that I am back for the time being...How long am I to be here you ask?...My answer will have to be...As long as i get a fair shot at the title's i deserve...But there is a time and a place for all explanations...Right now, I will deal with the task at hand...This task will not be an easy one...This task involves the defeat of another man, a man i trust with my life, but for this one night, and one night alone i must treat him like abastard step-child...Though I do have to defeat him i guess i do not have to try to put him out of wrestling, least not for ever, just for a week or so ...In some ways, I must...I have much to prove to everyone out there...Am I able to be as good as I once was?...Can I handle his type of hard core competion?...Am I able to still have the respect of others?...I do not have these answers just yet...For even I, can not answer for it has been a long time since The RaTTle Snake has entered the ring in compition, let alone against a dear and trusted friend as John Myles ...What I can do is earn the respect. The respect that I once had... In the past, I had never asked for respect...I had never asked for acceptance...I still do not ask for acceptance I have never received it in my life and I do not expect it now...But for the first time in my life ...I do want respect...I am not asking for it blindly...I know that I will have to earn it and that will be my agenda...As always, My friend Gate
Keeper will be guiding me through the serious of training and then the altimate, the confrontation ,he will lead me to the correct paths to take...And as always, My built in instincts will lead me not to lye down but to keep going
... Gate Keeper will Lend me a hand by teaching me all that he knows in such a shot amout of time, to defeat John Myles... But do understand this...This is not an agenda sent down from anyone to me...Oh no...This is an agenda that is put forth by me...This is something that I feel that I must do...I do know one thing about this journey that I am about to embark on...If this journey ends with me standing in the middle of the ring in victory...This will be the first time that I will ever grace a wrestling ring to fight a friend, and to put him away in the fasion in which will end a career of an enemy...I have other things to accomplish after this endeavor...But do not worry...This is the only thing that is on my mind right now...The others things will come later so do not be concerned about my focus...
It is a very special match... A few days from now will mark the beginning of an all out war, a war between friend not enemies...This is the time that the spirits come out to embark on this endeavor...Even though it is for only one night...This poor and wretched soul John Myles is destined to be in limbo... He lie somewhere in-between the Heaven and the Earth...That night is The RaTTle
Snake's time to shine and John Myles is going to make the most of it I am sure, as he is that of a great fighter, much like another man that went by the name of NITRO.... You see, there are those who do not understand the importance of me winning this match...They do not understand what the win represents...These people do not think that an old tired RaTTle Snake has the poisious venom that a young one does...They think of it as a begining for the young to take their place on the throne, what they fail to see is that in order to get to the throne the road is got to go through me, true an older Snake but a much smarter one...A game...John has no idea as to what i bring to the table in this match
...I on the other hand do respect all that John Myles can do, but it will not be enough... Everyone should have the freedom to choose what path they want to go down and who to follow down that path...That is the main reason I chose to enter this federation P-Pro, but I will get into that at another place and at another time...
Now is the time for me to come forward and for John Myles to meet his day of reckoning...I will be the one to pass judgment on John...Hopefully for him, his day of reckoning will be fast and swift...but for the poor, fools that must enter my realm after him, whoa be on to them, for it is time to pay the piper...The RaTTle Snake has returned to professional wrestling and with him he brings the stroke...Whether this is a good thing or a bad thing, only time will tell...but I do know one thing...I will once again leave my mark upon everyone involved...I have done it in the not so distance past and there is absolutely no reason why it will not be done now...Are you ready for the night
John?...I certainly hope so... For if you are not...entering my realm will be a very sorry and painful experience that most men can not recover from...
RaTTle Snake Ya' know it took a lot of guts, and a lot of pain to come from retiring to a big main event such as this and Prez Nug i thank you for this shot... I know a lot of you out there are wondering; "why the hell does RaTTle Snake still keep coming back tiem and time again?" It's simple kiddies, it was to prove a point. See, I came back to wrestling to prove one point -- that I still had it in me to be the best. When I got that call from good 'ol Nuggman to hop aboard
P-PRO I couldn't resist it. When he had told me who was all up in P-PRO I couldn't help but get my ass on a plane and head down to P-PRO town. I come back to wrestling to show everyone I still got it in me for one more big bang.
What I'm gettin' at is, if I was capable of winning this match and any title,
I am capable of winning any title and any match i so choose to take a shot for, so think 'bout it. Everyone knows I'm capable of doing it -- just look at me, you'll see why... To bad that i must prove this point at the hands of my friend
John Myles, not to bad for me but for him, that he must face the recking ball of the RaTTle Snake, once and for all !!!
RaTTle Snake John Myles many are called and but a few are choosen !! My friend i am calling you out here and now to bring what you got to the ring , because whatever it is you have as your hopes and desires, it is not enough to defeat the ever sneaky one and to stop me from laying a little southern justice up along side your head, and defeat you 1--2--3 in the ring and go on in life... Now you can live with that idea or not, but it is going to happen real soon ....
