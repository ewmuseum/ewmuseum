[Critique #2] Hannibal Carver

22 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
E-mail address: [detail withheld] Wrestler: Hannibal Carver Height: 6'3" Weight: 260 pounds Alignment: Face Hometown: South Boston, MA Entry Music: "Protest and Survive" by Discharge Style: Submission Specialist / Brawler Physical Appearance - Stocky build, muscular but not really defined. Blue-Green eyes, pale white skin, and dirty blonde hair in a buzzcut, with stubble on his face. His hands and face show a lifetime of hard times. Ring Appearance - Heavily taped hands, a black tank top with varying
"punk rock" band's slogan across the front, loose-fitting black pants, and black boots. Often carries a branding iron with the word "Axxis" on the brand with him to the ring. Strengths: 1. Arsenal: A Combination of powerful brawling skills and a wide array of submission maneuvers can subdue an opponent quickly. 2. Stamina: Much like Greg Valentine, the longer a match goes, the better he does. 3. Adrenaline Rush: Gets an adrenaline rush whenever he's busted open, rocking his opponent with a series of stiff jabs and kicks. Weaknesses: 1. Temper: Easily angered, often gets himself disqualified. 2. Thick Headed: Will often try to fight or power out of situations where he is being double or triple teamed, rather than attempt to escape the situation. 3. Likes to get warmed up, if facing a match with a short time limit, will have problems getting "warmed up." Signature Moves: 1. Sloshed Buster (T-Bone Sitdown Spinebuster) 2. The "How's Ya Fookin' Neck?" Brainbuster (Hanging Brainbuster) 3. Heat Stealer (Sambo Suplex into the turnbuckles. Followed by a Drop Toe Hold floated into a STF) 4. Wrapped Kneedrop 5. Release German Suplex 6. Spinal Meningitis (Sitdown Full Nelson Drop) 7. Reverse Fujiwara Arm Bar 8. Sam Adams Slam (Russian Leg Sweep Fake into a Lariat) 9. Guinness Grinder (Rolling Half Crab into an Anklelock) 10.Second Rope Double Foot Stomp 11.DUI (Fireman's Carry into a Diamond Cutter) 12.Massachusetts Cloverleaf (Tiger Bomb into a Texas Cloverleaf) 13.Snap DDT 14.Figure Four Leg Lock 15.Dragon Sleeper Setup for Finisher: Spinal meningitis (Full Nelson Sitdown Drop) Finishing Move: Silence of the Lambs Description Sitting Chickenwing / Half Nelson Combination. Carver also executes a series of stiff headbutts to the back of his opponent's head after locking in the submission, usually causing the opponent to black out.
Secondary Finisher: Hachiman Crush (Super Tiger Suplex) Snap Fisherman Suplex Stump Puller Background / History: Ever since he was a child, Hannibal had a love for wrestling as an art form. His father was a wrestling coach at his local high school, so he was introduced to Greco-Roman wrestling at an early age. He would often beg his father to let him tag along as the home team had away meets with other schools. Like a human sponge, Hannibal soaked up everything he saw. Around this time he caught an episode of the weekly
NWA television show on TBS. It was everything he loved about the high school wrestling meets, and so much more. After witnessing Eric Flair take on Harley Race in a steel cage, he knew what he wanted to do with his life. The two biggest names in the sport, locked in combat, both men wearing a mask of crimson on his face held such a lure for him. He only wanted a chance to prove to the world that he could be at their level. He persuaded his father to let him join the wrestling team after weeks of begging and pleading. At first he had quite a bit of trouble from his fellow team mates. With his limited ability, it was obvious that he got on the team because of his father.
The final straw came when crouched on all fours, a malicious team mate dropped his knee on Hannibal's ankle, nearly shattering it. Remembering the backyard lessons given to him by his father, he hit a leg sweep on his tormentor, dropping the young man on his face. He then quickly crawled over to him, and applied an Arm Bar, refusing to let go until several coaches, his father included, pulled him off the boy. It was later revealed that he had almost pulled the young man's shoulder out of it's socket. The repercussions of this incident followed him until graduation day, where he was constantly getting into fist fights with members of the team seeking retribution.
After graduation, Carver found his goal, his dream of being a wrestler at a standstill. He worked local independent shows whenever possible, but due to his reputation he more often was asked to leave. Until one day, when his father came home with exciting news. Due to an exchange student program at the high school, his father had been in contact with a promoter from
Japan,
who helped run the Nippon Pro federation. This was the chance of a lifetime. A chance at a clean slate in Japan, where all his heroes had waged epic battles. His embracing of classic wrestling was seen as golden in the eyes of the trainers at the Nippon Pro dojo, and he was quickly put into shows for Nippon Pro. It was at this time that he met
Rick Sturgis, otherwise known as Leo, and Timothy Weathers, also known as Ichiban Gaijin (Best Foreigner). Rick, like Hannibal, was from the states, Timothy was born to American parents, but lived in a Japanese orphanage all his life. The three became fast friends. His first bout was met with much trepidation, for the simple reason that the promoter didn't feel that the name of John Dexter was quite exciting enough. At this point Hannibal had become a great fan of horror movies, punk rock music, and serial killer books. A favorite book at the time was The
Silence of the Lambs by Thomas Harris. He took the name from the main character of the book, altering the last name. Hannibal Carver was born. All was going well for quite some time, as the Japanese crowds seemed to be embracing the newly named Carver's mix of brawling and submission moves.
This would all come to a halt when Hannibal and Leo received what seemed like good news. An American wrestling promotion based in
Carver's hometown was interested in signing them both. The two jumped at the chance to wrestle in front of American crowds, and were on a plane to Logan Airport in Boston, Massachusetts two weeks later. Rick was almost immediately pushed into main event status, still wrestling under the name Leo. Hannibal was another matter. Two of the promotion's most wealthy backers had sons that wanted to be wrestling stars. They also wanted the gimmick of street thugs. The promotion chose Hannibal as a partner for them, and was given the ridiculous name of "Da Masta of Disasta." Gone were the suplexes and arm bars, and in came forty ounce bottles of malt liquor and kissing up to the fans. Hannibal was very successful in his new role, but he knew it was because he was with the two golden boys, not on his own merits. To make matters worse, the two regularly botched the simplest of moves, causing several injuries to other, more competent workers. Because no one dared upset the promotion's two golden eggs, all the hostility was thrown Carver's way. It looked as things might look up when Leo was able to bring in Weathers, now known as Taurus, but that was short-lived as the now egocentric Leo used Taurus as a stepping stone, and Taurus was soon shelved with a laughable "amnesia" storyline.
Before things could get much worse, the promotion folded, leaving Leo rich, Taurus homeless, and Carver with a plane ticket back to Japan.
Upon his return a new side to Carver was evident. Having a good bout was no longer important. Injuring his opponent became paramount. He now snapped at the fans that he once only worked to impress. Every match turned into a shoot fight, and he began brawling with anyone who dared to speak to him backstage. He was soon given his letter of resignation, but he was not upset. He had heard word that another American promotion, the North
American Wrestling federation, was in need of his services. Only this time, it was a very different situation. Time healed the wounds caused by his time apart from Taurus, Leo, who now goes by the name of "Hot
Shit" Rick Sturgis, and an old friend of his in Odin Trollslayer.
Together they had banned together to form Axxis. This too would prove to be another dissapointment. Sturgis was clearly more interested in posing than training, and the thrown together team of Sturgis and
Trollslayer was quickly sent packing due to several lackluster performances. This was a cause of distress to Carver, but even more so to Taurus. While carver's heavy drinking ways had garnered him the nickname of "The Whole Drunken Show", he was always able to handle it, never letting it get in the way of his job. Taurus, on the other hand, was a different story. Taurus took to drinking heavily to deal with the departure of his friends. Sturgis and he had become brothers s in everything but blood over the years, and Rick's departure took a heavy toll on Taurus' soul. The drinking got out of hand, and was finally noticed by the head of NAWF, who gave Taurus his walking papers. On the verge of an emotinal breakdown, Carver requested some time off. Upon his return, he won the NAWF Hardcore Championship in a
Battle Royal, and defeated the former champion the following week in a rematch. All seemed to be going well, but this was only a set up.
Carver was being groomed to be a champ only to take a dive for Nighthawk, an aging NAWF star who's best days were behind him. Upon hearing this Carver was upset, but being a professional he did the job, and would soon after walk out of NAWF forever, with a bitter taste in his mouth.
It was at this point that fate smiled upon Carver for once, as his former employer, BSCW's Richard Vail, gave him a call. Vail had a new job for him, that of Commissioner. This was the perfect opportunity.
Not only could Carver rest up and heal all nagging injuries, he could collect a steady paycheck as well. But this, like all things, had to come to an end. But for once Carver was out of a job with a good outlook. He began to train with renewed vigor, and got his name out to all prospective employers. After all that he's been through Carver still loves the business, and is hoping to resume regular competition soon.
