Hi, the name's [name withheld] & I am looking to handle a brand new character I've come up with, named "Bittersweet" Brian Marcil. I've been involved in e-wrestling off & on since 1997 and have been the president of a federation, booker, card writer, roleplay grader, and handler as well. Here's some generic information about my wrestler, and I'm basically searching for an already established, booked, and e-mail based roleplay federation. I'm not hard to work with, I don't whine, and I RP at least twice in good length for a match, trying to preserve the ancient principle that "quantity is not better than quality." I have a few sample RP's from different characters if you need them.
If you don't like him, that's cool.....if you do, that's even better.....and if you do and you're a fed president in need of a quality member, that would be the best. And now without further ado, I give you Brian Marcil.

"BBM" in white on each one. He will alternate his attire from time to time, but this is his usual outfit of choice. Wears his light brown hair medium-length and styled forward. Good muscular condition and a light tan to his skin, 2 or 3 visible tattoos.
Style: A technical high-impact brawler.....who will wear an opponent down with gradually powerfully-increasing moves throughout the match.

If you're interested, don't hesitate to e-mail me. Thanks for everyone's consideration. Chris T. The Ayatollah of RC Cola Handler of "Bittersweet" Brian Marcil
