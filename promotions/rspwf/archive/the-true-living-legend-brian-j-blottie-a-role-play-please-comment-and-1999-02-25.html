"The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie... a role-play. Please comment and critique.

25 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Scene opens in the training gym connected to Brian J. Blottie's estate. Blottie is seen sitting in the corner, seemingly exhausted. He smiles as he sees the camera and beckons it to him. Shaking his head, he begins*
Blottie: You guys just can't leave me alone, can ya? Well, if you want to watch me train, it's a little too late. I've just been training for the past two hours, and to put it lightly, I'm not exactly about to do it again. But I will tell you like it is, and spread the gospel of the legend... heh heh...
that sounds pretty good, "gospel of the legend". *Blottie rises to his feet and walks over to a water cooler, at which he takes a cup, pours water into it, and downs it in one gulp* Blottie: Anyway, if you want to know what my opinion on world events are, you've come to the wrong place. I don't give a fuck who is sucking off the president or what world leader is planning on bombing the U.S. All I care about it my business, and the sport of wrestling. Call me close minded, but they're the only two things that my life deals with, so I deal with them.
Because I don't worry about who's new movie is coming out when, and who did what to who last week in the White House, it makes me better at what I DO worry about.
The business... well, you really don't want to hear about that. Let's just say that we're doing VERY good. Better than anyone ever expected. But then that's the story of my life. Better than anyone ever expected.
*Blottie walks out of the gym, the camera following him. He walks into a large dining room, with a table that seats ten. The table itself if covered with food from at least four different countries. Kibbie, pork roast, prime rib, three different kinds of steak, some kind of Chinese dish, and more vegetables than could ever be imagined fill the table from one end to the other. Blottie sits at the head of the table, and beckons the cameraman to sit in the seat next to him. The cameraman does so, and Blottie speaks again*
Blottie: In case you are wondering, no, this isn't all for me. I'm having a business meeting with a few associates, and all of them have different tastes that they wish quenched. They have come to expect only the finest from me when they enter this house. The finest meats... the finest wines... the finest decor. As I hope the wrestlers of the NACW, and expecially those in the Rookie
Brawl, hope to expect from me.
Only the finest wrestling will be dealt out. Only the finest punishment. I am a very refined man. Some would call me a "gentlemen". My opponents in the
NACW, however, will learn to call me "sir". Or "master". Or they will finally concede, and call me "legend". For I am all these things, and so much more.
The NACW did not know what they were getting into when I signed that contract. They did not know of my violent tendencies. All they knew was that from what they saw in the tapes I sent them, I could back up my claim as "The True Living
Legend". And what they did not know will come back to haunt them.
Now let me get one thing straight. I am not some Grenwich, Conneticut snob. I am not a rip off of a thousand other "rich and wrestling" gimmicks that have permeated this fine sport. I am different. I still live in Michigan, my home state. And I will live here until the day I die. I give back to the community that which I take away. I have supported Detroit Public Schools even when they were on the verge of collapse. And I have done all these things with nothing expected back.
But don't think of me as some warm-hearted humanitarian, either. When I step into that ring, the only thing that I will give out is pain. The only thing that I want back is blood. And the only thing that I will shed is sweat.
When I step into that ring, I live and breathe wrestling. I work towards only one thing... gaining the legendary status I am destined to attain in this sport. Call me cocky. Call me self-centered. But I will prove in the ring what I have been saying... that I am simply the best damn wrestler in this fed, bar none.
I guess that brings me to the Rookie Brawl. Come March 13th, the proof will be in the ring. Every single wrestler who steps up to me will be knocked down flat on their face. And one after another, I will send them to their preeminent doom. And when it's all said and done, only one man will stand with the victory. Guess who that's gonna be?
Beyond that... well, I guess that's up to the promotors. But I hope to see title shots coming my way VERY soon. If not... well then I guess I'm just going to have to tear apart every wrestler in this fed until they deem me fit to receive one. And that is not just an idle threat... that is a promise.
Now, I think it's time that you left. And please, try calling next time you want to intrude on my premises. Otherwise, you might not particularly like the consequenses. Until next time...
*Blottie shows the cameraman to the door, and slams the door shut on his face. The scene closes with a closeup shot of the highly decorative door before the camera FADES TO BLACK*
