How to start a feud

EW University
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
How to…start a feud. In the world of literature, it is said there are only 37 possible storylines. I`m not going to cover them - this essay is about E-Wrestling, not literature! However, there are a limited number of ways that a feud can start. I don`t know how many there are (not yet!) but here are some of the simplest (and perhaps most common) ways to start a feud/build some heat/have a storyline. Not in an angle? Use one of these! The great thing about a feud, or at least when two good players are involved, is that the smallest excuse for a feud will grow and grow and continue and continue until the original reason is forgotten - just ask anyone in Eastern Europe… Insulted The other wrestler has called you a name. You don`t like it. Are you really going to sit down and let them walk all over you? You going to let them call you a jobberoni? Of course not! Go kick their butt… The other person is attacking a friend/ally of yours What are friends for if not to help each other out? Thing is, the focus of the attackers attention can turn on the friend who came to the others aid… This town ain't big enough for both of our ego's… Along similar lines to 'Insulted' but this time this guy is claiming to be the best. You`re not going to have that - you know that in fact YOU are the best. Go kick their butt.. Accidentally hit Perhaps you`ve been teamed up with someone who you didn`t know well, and in the course of the match, they accidently hit you…who the hell is going to put up with that? Perhaps it was a friend at ringside trying to help you, but they accidently hit you…but was it really an accident? Go kick their butt… Cost them a match/title shot/something important Somebody does something - intentionally or not - that causes you to lose a match/title shot/title/status/llama…whatever…you`ve got to show them not to meddle in your affairs. Go kick their butt… Jealousy Somebody has something you want, or something that used to be yours - a title, a woman, respect, the love of the fans, money…whatever the hell it is, the best way to make yourself feel better is to beat the living crap out of them. Go kick their butt… Just plain don`t like something about them Whether it was something that said, somewhere they`ve been, something they believe in, the way they treat the fans…or you just, for no reason in particular, don`t like them, you don`t want them taking up space in your fed…go kick their butt… Betrayal Your friend/stable-mate turns on you mid-match or allies with your enemy…just the sense of betrayal is enough to make you want to go kick their butt… Bad History You`ve fought before in the past, and the matter was never resolved. (Note: You don`t actually have to have really fought them in e-wrestling before - just say you have in a previous federation). Or perhaps they just beat you in a previous match, and you will not settle until you are proven the better man. Go kick their butt… Something backstage Things off-scene can be very good for a feud. Perhaps they scratched your car, ran over your cat, took your locker space, left smelly socks lying around, pissed all over the toilet seat, farted really badly…numerous things that can give you a personal grudge to go and kick their butt… WHAT ARE YOU WAITING FOR? GO FEUD!
