{FREE AGENT} "Excellent" Evan Andrews

12 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, I'm the handler of "Excellent" Evan Andrews, and I am looking for a few feds to put him in. I have only two requirements:
1. Booked feds only. 2. Good roleplayers and competition. Well here is my sample roleplay. If you are interested just email me at [detail withheld] . Thanks. Fade In.] [ The scene fades in and you can see the nicely cut green grass of a football stadium. Farther down in the distance at far end zone sprinklers are on, making the grass look even greener and more manicured. Farther off in the distance you can see a picture of " Touchdown Jesus" and you can clearly tell that this is Notre Dame's football stadium. The stadium is huge and seems like nothing but an endless sea of seats. Every Saturday on TV you see this huge place full of fans, but now it is calm and quiet. A very well built man approaches the camera, stares into it, and then turns his head and surveys the stadium. He seems to have something extra in his step, almost a hint of cockiness or pride. You can tell he is a true competitor, a man that doesn't step down from challenges. His very well built man, wearing blue jeans, with a white Abercrombie t- shirt covering his big, but not extremely huge arms. He stands at around 6'4", not huge but by no means small. A Notre Dame hat rests on his head. He stands there quietly for a moment, seemingly pondering everything around him. He thens turns his head and stares into the camera and begins to speak.]
"Excellent" Evan Andrews: This place brings back many memories for me. I gave blood, sweat and everything else I had on this football field. God, I loved this place.
[ He points to a tunnel that leads to the locker rooms.] "Excellent" Evan Andrews: There was no better feeling on Earth than running out from under that tunnel, hearing and seeing thousands of fans on there feet cheering for you. For four years I played here, giving it one hundred and ten percent every time I stepped on the field, every time I stepped into the weight room, and every time I was at a practice.
Nobody in this federation has ever been through the things I have been through. When I was a little kid growing up in Cincinnati my parents were killed when I was young. They were never there when I first rode a bike, when
I played my first football game, or anything a normal kid has there parents there for. I was sent off to live with a poor aunt that never really gave me much attention which really aggravated me. I had to take my aggression out somehow and I chose to do that on the football field. And when kids decided to say a smart comment to me, I took my aggression out on them.
I worked my up out of my aunt's poverty stricken house through the game of football. I was an All-American in high school, I could have written my ticket to any football oriented school in America. I chose Notre Dame and its rich football history. I was a work horse all through college, with more dedication than the rest of the team put together. But now my football career is over and now my aggression and dedication is going to carry over to the wrestling world.
[ He stops and takes a few steps back, so he is standing exactly in the middle of the field at the fifty yard line. He once again gets that pondering look on his face and then concisely gets back to the matter at hand.]
"Excellent" Evan Andrews: I hope the wrestling world is ready for somebody new to make an impact. Now I know some people are saying, " He's just a beginner trying to sound tough", well you couldn't be any farther from the truth. And on Against All Odds, the WWC's first card, I am going to make an example of Tom
Hyde. I amgoing to prove that "Excellent" Evan Andrews is no fluke, he is the real deal. Tom, you are going to be a small stepping stone on my path to the
International Title. Your power moves aren't going to be enough to take care of me, because pound for pound I am a stronger man than you are....not to mention more of a man. I am the greatest technician wrestler that this federation will ever see walk into the WWC ring. So everybody better be ready, because for now on wrestling is going to be the way I say it is going to be.
So, I'm warning you, get ready because this isn't going to be like anything you have ever seen before. And remember, I'm not good....I'm EXCELLENT!
[ Evan turns and starts to walk toward the tunnel that seems so precious to him. At about the twenty yard line he seems to put a juke on an imaginary defender. He then returns to his regular walk, that walk that tells you that he knows he is tough, want to flaunt it.]
[ Fade to black.]
