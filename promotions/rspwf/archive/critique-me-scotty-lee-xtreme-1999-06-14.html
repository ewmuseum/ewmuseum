[CRITIQUE ME] Scotty Lee Xtreme

14 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am a normal AOL RPer. There's a few, spelling, and punctuation mistakes in this, but you'll live. Tell Me what you Think.Thanks
Scotty =============================== Promo -"He is your Hero" [ SLX Holds the WEWF World Title Up High ] "Your
Favorite Wrestler" [ SLX Shakes His Fans Hands at a house show ] "The One You
Want To Be" [ SLX Poses for a Camera Shoot ] " The One You Want To See" [
Scotty Lee Xtreme Flex's off the Second Turnbuckle in Las Vegas ] "The
Innovator.." [ SLX Throws Someone Threw a Table ] "Xtreme." [ The Man Who went threw the Table is on the ground, bloody] "The True Xtreme Sob" [SLX Holds the
GcW World Title Up High looking down at Eric Magnus] "Your True Champion" [ SLX
Throws the CWA Hardcore Championship Over his shoulder after a match with
Bullet ] "The One That Gives "Great" a New Meaning" [ SLX Wins CWA World Title]
[Camera Shuts off] [Fade In] [ Scene ]: In the sold out UXWA Arena In Las Vegas Nevada, the fans are all still. "Living Dead Girl" By Rob Zombie Begins to bump threw the arena. [Lights
Go Out] The Fans Bust into loud cheer's. Scotty Lee Xtreme walks out threw the curtains and pause's he looks around the arena. SLX then walks further down the ramp with a bottle of water in his right hand. He makes it half way down when he stops again. He takes a sip of the water then pours it all over himself. He runs and slides into the ring, he pause's in the middle of the ring and show boats, as a Xtreme Light Fixture is lower to the ring, he poses, as fireworks and the Light Fixture egnight. All is calm as he begins to speak.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Once, a President, now a wrestler. Was I demoted? No. Do
I want to wrestle? Yea. Now, its time. It's time, I show the UXWA the "Real"
Talent. I'm sick of watching little people coming out here making a little noise, then leaving. It's sick. Another thing that has gotten to be a nuisance, this whole "Scotty Lee Chinese" Thing. Please people, get a clue would Ya? You want to piss Scotty off? Well, keep up the good work. Only, if you want to see me, mad. Well, you all know this isn't the reason I came out here tonight right? I thought so. The reason, [Chuckles], the reason is simple. The Lord Of
Darkness, The Reaper. That's all I need to start out with.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, the one who supposedly, can beat anyone thrown in front of him. Reaper, do you really think your that great? A Legend? No,
Reaper. I have to tell you now, before it's to late Reaper, that one day, when we step into the ring to face eachother. All hell will break lose, yes, I know that, what I want to tell you is, is when you look into the eye's of the
Innovator Of Xtreme, you, yes, even you Reaper show fear at one time or another, you will have fear in your eyes. That's what I'm predicting.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Was It Funny Reaper? Was it funny when you laid me out at the recent UXWA Show? [Grins] Yea Reaper, laugh all you want about that. Your time will soon enough come. This time though, I will be ready, and I will be watching your every move. Reaper, attacking me at Saturday Night Sacrifice was the wrong thing to do. You made a mistake Reaper. A Big Mistake. You don't know what could be waiting for you Dark Man. You don't know the mind game's little
Scotty can play on people. I may not be a hell razor, or a lord of darkness, or death, but I still have little games, that I can use to my advantage. Reaper, you will witness a new Scotty Lee Xtreme as time goes on. New attitude, new appearance, and a new style. Reaper, mean's jack shit to you eh? Well, listen to me closely, the way I look, and act, reflects on my wrestling. So if I am upset one day, and decide to get into the ring with you, watch your ass my friend. Cause, Mr. Xtreme will burn it up. So Reaper, ask yourself now. Was it worth Attacking me? If it was, well, cherish it Reaper, it will be your first, last, and only.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, your too depressed. I can see this when you talk. You being a depressed soul as you are Reaper. I think you will not function as well as you normally would. Reaper, you are a lost soul. You don't think like any normal person, you don't act like any normal person, and you definitely don't look like a normal person. Reaper, to sum that up a bit, your just not normal. Reaper, are you thinking that I've lost it? Reaper, you may be right. I have lost it. I have come out here, and talked the biggest load of trash to, one who might very well be the best wrestler ever to walk the face of this earth, The Reaper.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, I may be the underdog. But, Reaper, I strongly suggest, don't look past me. You might make the mistake, and lose to someone you probably shouldn't have lost to. Reaper, I am at top notch right now. I am a wolf, waiting to feast on your carcass in the ring. Reaper, you and I may have a bit of a size difference, but rest assure, one small, little mistake you make, it's going to change the outcome in the match we will soon have. Reaper, the fact that you walk around with your head down all the time should make you lose every match, but your name is to big to let any promoter let that happen.
Reaper, you win for one reason. Your Name. Your known everywhere. All promoter's think, big namers must win. Reaper, without your hugely known name, you would be nothing. I could tear you up quicker than I will when we wrestle.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, you've finally spoken. Reaper, I made my rude, harsh comments, I threw out some tough threats, and you attacked me. So maybe I didn't get away with it, but why couldn't you just be a man and meet me face to face? Reaper I may have brought this rivalry shall I say to a new level. That's because I want it to end Reaper. Why Should two of the best in the game, wrestle day in and day out?Reaper, I want this over with, and soon. Reaper, you think that I tell myself I cannot beat you. Reaper, I tell myself every damn day that I am able to defeat you. I think I have just enough skill, talent, and dignity to pull off a huge win. Reaper, you claim with the snap of your fingers you can end my career. All I want to say to that is, BULLSHIT! There's no way on god's green earth, that with the snap of_ANYONE'S_finger's it could end my career. Reaper, you may have got on the bigger end of the stick the other night, but that will not put a damper in what I plan to accomplish against you.
A "Shower Of Pain" you say Reaper? [Chuckles] Reaper, stop the bullshit. You have no clue who you are messing with. Don't play games with me Reaper, be a man. Live up to what being a Reaper Means. Telling me to think twice before I do something now I see Reaper. Well, thinking twice is something a man that knows he can't back his shit up would do.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:Reaper, I almost tend to agree with you. It's almost a match of respect, not who can win. Reaper, you are right amongst a few things you speak. One, I may still be considered a "Crash Test Dummy Of Wrestling."
Reaper, It may be eating me that I am still known as someone who can't perform well as others, but in due time, I will be known as the one who put down The
Reaper. You claim instead of me gaining other wrestler's respect, I have gained nothing but pity from them. Reaper, I have gained respect from more people you can think of. "Dangerous" Dave F'n Taylor. Reaper, that's just a start, I don't plan to continue on that. Reaper, I don't get the point where you say, I am just "toying" with you. Reaper, you have seen nothing of The Innovator Of
Xtreme. This as you say, Is just a bit off the iceberg, more is awaiting you
Reaper.
[ Scotty Lee Xtreme ]:In closing. Reaper, UXWA is the battleground, see you soon. [Laughs] Sooner Than you think!!!
-[Scotty Lee Xtreme]- -[In..]- -[IWF, UXWA]- -[NET Free Agent]-
