(free Agent) Dr. Death

6 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The cameras start and the scene opens up in a funeral parlor. The lights are turned down and the music of hurt by
NIN is playing. The lights fade on and a 7'0" 400 pound extremely muscular figure appears with a black overcoat on and a pair of latex gloves covering his hands. He has what is look like completely white eyes and a ghostly white appearance to him.
The brave interviewer finally speaks to this men of death. Interviewer: So You want to join a e-fed Right. Dr. Death grabs the Interviewer by the neck and tosses him aside with ease: Dr. Death In a deep dark voice says: Yes I want to join I want to teach all of the wrestlers in the organization what it feels like to have the devil grab you heart out and eat it in front of you face as They watch. I will destroy any wrestler that challenges me and I will take out a few that don't just for spite.
Interviewer get up gasping for air: So why do you thing you will do so good here. What makes you think you are good enough to wrestle here.
Dr. Death bitch slaps him and the Interviewer smashes against the wall: I am and Will Always be good enough in this league I have the souls of 100 of Men that I fought on the streets of NEW YORK in me. The Strength of 30 Men And
The Agility of a Leopard. So If you want me to join your league just e-mail me at [detail withheld] Then the lights go out and a blaze of fire surrounds DR. Death and you see him laugh as he seems to be consumed by the fire.
