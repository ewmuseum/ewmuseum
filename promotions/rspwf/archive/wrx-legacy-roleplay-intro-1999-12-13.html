WRX - Legacy Roleplay: Intro

13 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The really low RSPWF member posts his rp.. Everyone.. Meet.. Legacy) <The WRX camera comes on to show some woods.. There is a figure standing in the treeline.. It is dark and there is very little light.. The figure cannot be made out.. He begins to speak>
Figure: Michael Andrews, A legend in his own mind and a champion so many times over. What happened to the man? For months I have been asking myself.
Where is Michael Andrews.. Where is the Enigma that plagued my life for so long. And today I recieved the answer. Michael Andrews is in a Flea Market?
Wrestling in a two bit organization called the WRX.. What in God's name Mike? How could you fall from such grace.. But never the less.. It's all the same. I have come to destroy you. And the predator has come for you. You are my prey... You will be my greatest kill.. And I'm here for you. But who am I
Mike? Now your worst nightmare has come to pass...
<The figure steps forward... He is wearing a long white jacket.. A pair of white windpants... A white mask with the word LEGACY across the top. The wind blows and sends his hair waving in the wind>
Legacy: Yes... Michael Andrews... I have come for you. Legacy is here... Today your worst nightmare has come. The one man that you ran from.. Hid from.. and prayed that never again would you ever have to face in the ring...
I'm here. I'm in the WRX and I have a bullseye right on your fucking forehead. Everything you've done in your life Mike.. I've been right there. I was there when you were a rookie.. Always holding you down.. I was there when you were a
World Champion.. To challenge your reign.. You have defeated me a few times
Mikey.. And I have laid you down on the mat a few times myself. But the time for the final battle between us has come. Once and for all.. Everyone must know that I am the man.. In the XBWL.. I won the XBWL World Heavyweight Title..
and I put it on the line against you.. And I took you into the air.. and dropped you with Revenge De Grace and I proved that I was the best, but still you continue to parade around.. Michael Andrews.. The Enigma.. One of the most respected men in Wrestling history.. And what am I? I was the man all that time, but now you are here.. Trying to make yourself known.. So you ask yourself... Why? Why is Legacy in the WRX.. Why has the monster came for me..
Well.. It's simple.. I am retired from the XBWL.. I lost my career to Matt
Austin.. The XBWL's new Goldenboy..You remember how that was don't you Andrews?
To be the one that they wanted to be on top? To be the champion of the world? Yeah.. They always get thier way don't they? Need I talk about The Iron
Yuppy, Flyguy.. Dare I say.. Summer Sizzler.. Two years in a row? You took the falls they told you to take because you were the perfect champion.. But me? I never took anything that they wanted me to take.. I took what I wanted.. And what did it get me? A boot out the front door... Quicker than I went in...
But forget it.. The XBWL is history.. And I am here.. but alas dear Mikey.. I don't just come for you.. There are others that I want... And just as you
will pay... He will pay also.. But who else do I speak? Of which other man do I hate just as much as you Michael Andrews? <Legacy throws his head back.. Rubs his fingers through his hair and looks back forward at the camera>
Legacy: That man would be.. The Beast... Beast.. The man who call himself
EXTREME.. the man that calls himself whatever gimmick he ripped out of TNT this week.. So Beast.. How does it feel.. To know that Legacy has once again come for you.. What should I accredit to myself? I'm the man that ENDED your ONLY
World Title reign.. I took you out.. I put you on the mat and I pinned you after nailing REVENGE DE GRACE.. Until that night, You had so much over me..
You ended my Hardcore Title reign.. You hurt me.. You hurt my pride, but I got even that night? No.. I exist to make you fucking miserable.. And because of that.. I'm going to beat your ass unmercifully... You do not deserve to wear any kind of Hardcore persona.. You don't deserve to be called anything, because
I'm the man.. I am the master and I am the one that is in charge... I will destroy you.. I will beat you and I will break you.. As I have done so many times before.. I now stand before you on a ground where I can hurt you.. In the
WRX.. I will be the man.. I will make myself a legend here as I have done in other places.. I will make myself a legend among men that already know that I'm thier master.. Never have I been forgotten.. In the back of the minds of so many men, I exist forever.. Michael Andrews... Beast.. You are both victims of this almighty terror.. You will never forget me.. You will always know that as long as I live.. I'm as dangerous as ever and I am here... You've called me washed up so many times.. You've called me old.. And you've called me stale..
But guess what? I'm not.. I'm still your nightmare.. I'm still the predator of dreams and the one that everyone wishes would go away.. So throw my name into the WRX as it grows and grows in power and the crowds draw even more.. Because on this night I signed a contract that would put me in the WRX for a very long time.. I want nothing given to me.. I will earn it all.. And I will earn in all in territory of people that hate me.. Because I will prove to you all that
I'm the best.. I'll prove to you all that I'm not washed up.. I'm not stale and
I still have everything it takes to be the master.. To be the champion and to be a God! Wrestling will never be rid of Legacy because Legacy defines
Wrestling.. You can shoot me down.. Put me in the gutter.. Blow my face off with C4.. Throw me off an Arena.. Break every bone in my body.. End my career... but I'll always rise again like a phoenix from the ashes of death..
I am eternal and I am forever.. I'll always be somewhere.. Breaking down and dominating everything in my path.. You can call me anything you want.. Insult me with your words.. Beat me with your chairs, but I'll come back.. And I'll be better than before.. Because as wrestling progresses.. So do I.. As People get better.. I get better.. As people get stronger.. I get stronger... I will always be a notch higher than any man that steps foot into the ring.. Just by stepping foot into my ring.. You will fall ... You will go down to my power and you will realize that I am the one.. I am the master of all mindgames...
<Legacy lets what seems to be a smile cross the the lines in his mask.. Laughter can be heard and Legacy looks forward again...> Legacy: Michael Andrews.. I think back to the past wars that we've had... And
I remember the days when you were a mere rookie.. Looking up to the on that you idolized.. Looking up at the one that you one day hoped to confront.. And you tackled the odds.. You came out on top finally... And I look to The Beast... I remember the same of him.. How he was just a rookie.. Hoping to one day earn a shot at someone as great as myself.. But what happenend? They both became legends in thier own mind.. At my expense a little, but things happen.. There was a time when I had lost all hope.. When my wife was killed in a car accident.. When I was losing it.. A time when I just didn't have what it took to be the man anymore.. I became insane and I went crazy.. The demons inside me took over and I was under thier control.. Shifting between the personalities trapped inside me.. But then I woke up.. I realized that my life was not over.. So I arose again.. As a new man.. A man that no one could destroy.. I became immortal and I took the next step in my leap of faith.. I went out on a limb and I captured title after title.. I destroyed and decimated all that stood between me and my goals.. I used who I had to use and I destroyed who I had to destroy.. But now it's different.. I no longer need the sideline help of such bitches as Johnny Rebel and Ted Wilder.. I am my own man now.. Johnny
Rebel, you helped me through some tough times.. You helped me when I needed the help that only a friend like you could provide, and I apologize for the times that I had to hurt you.. I apologize for the times I had to fight you.. I call for a truce.. I don't call for an alliance or a WWFW rebirth.. Just a truce, as you are a friend to me.. So I ask that you don't hate me.. And you accomplish everything you could ever hope to accomplish.. The heavyweight title is not beyond your grasp and when you get it.. I'll walk down the aisle.. I'll raise your hand and I'll show you the respect that I should have shown you for so many years while you were by my side and helping me through my ambitions and goals... I held you down for a long time, and when you really could have used my help, I was off on hiatus.. Cursing you and calling you a traitor... I will make my peace with you, that I vow.. But first I have to put the end to Michael
Andrews.. I have to put the end to The Beast.. I have to face them and I have to destroy them and show them that I am the fucking master.. That I still have it... Michael Andrews... You will never forget me, but I'm giving you another opportunity to fight me.. Because once again I look to put you down.. Once again I look to destroy you and once again I aim to plant you on the mat for the 1..2..3.. Beast.. The same goes for you.. Don't think that I'm some washed up has been... I proved that to you when I took your World Title... I'm here guys.. And there ain't a damn thing you can do about except run for your lives and pray that somehow the predator doesn't find you alone.... But don't worry... In the end.. You will fall to me.. All will end that begins..
<Legacy walks to the left.. He walks toward a lake and steps into a boat.. He sits down and pulls out a paddle.. The camera moves closer and then the boat begins to float away.. Legacy looks out over the lake and the moonlight glare shines over his reflection and the camera aims at his reflection as he speaks..>
Legacy: What will I do? I step foot into unfamiliar territory knowing there are enemies here that hate me.. Knowing there are forces here that wish to destroy me once and for all.. Do I let it bother me? No.. Because in the WRX..
I will become the legend that I know I can be.. I will become the WRX's most respected athlete and I will become the champion of the World one day... I look to the future.. Knowing that I cannot just challenge a man and take it as for once being a challenge for me.. For so long I have faced men that were not up to my standards... And now I know that here I will face men who are so much
greater than me.. Tonight I embark on a new journy.. A journy into the unknown.. Will I succeed in the sport that I have claimed to dominate for so long.. Will I become the man again? Can I rebirth my career again and once again become the best.. or will I fail.. This is the test... Michael Andrews...
Beast.. I've been told that this is where you prevail.. That here you are at your greatest moments.. So here I want to face you.. Here I want to defeat you and here I want to show you that I'm the man... Time for me to earn for myself the victories that I deserve.. Here I start with a clean slate.. There are enough men here that everyone doesn't expect me to main event a ppv every week.. I'm not expected to be the best.. I'm just another man.. Here I can prevail.. Here I can become a God and here I can earn my stripes for all of eternity.
<Legacy's boat comes up to a small island... Legacy steps out onto the island and the camera man follows him as he slumps down on the ground>
Legacy: Here I will train.. Here I will ready myself to become the best.. And here I will hone my skills to be the best man in the WRX.. Here I will practice to destroy Michael Andrews.. and here I will train to destroy The Beast.. And when it's all done.. I will be the most respected man in the history of wrestling..
<The WRX camera fades out to a logo for LEGACY>
