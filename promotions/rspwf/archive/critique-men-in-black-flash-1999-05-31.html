[CRITIQUE] MEN IN BLACK FLASH

31 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in total darkness. A gravely voice is heard:] V: "Iceman" Michael Ice and "Red Hot" Alex Perkins... [A match is struck. The light from the lit match reveals the identity of the speaker. It's The Gate Keeper, manager of the Men in Black.
The emotionless look on his face is downright grim and eerie. He takes a candle out of his robe pocket and lights it with the match.]
TGK: No, they're not the newest wigger white boy rapper group sensation. As far as I know, they do not have a video playing on MTV or a singing career. But that may soon change. If Jennifer Love
Hewitt can get a recording contract, anything's possible.
[The Gate Keeper manages to let out a weak laugh.] So, until Sean "Puffy" Combs offers them a recording contract, they are merely professional wrestlers. They are LUCKY professional wrestlers at that. Along with my men, Dagger and Jayson, they survived until the very end of a recent tag team battle royal. Because of that, they have earned a match where the winners win the vacant UEW
Television Tag Team Titles. That's the good news for the young lads. The bad news? Their opponents will be Dagger and Jayson... also known as the MEN IN BLACK.
[The Gate Keeper actually expresses some emotion. He smiles.] Since I brought Jayson and Dagger to this... this... place a few months ago... they have done many things. Some of the things would lead some to believe that, while merely relative newcomers to Ultimate
E-Wrestling, the Men in Black are already certified tag team legends.
[The Gate Keeper begins to walk.] They ran UEW's resident so called "legend" tag team, The Lethal Leaders, out of the company. After their first and only match with the
Men in Black, Heatstroke and Cancer fled UEW with their overrated tails between their legs. A team that held the UEW Television Tag Team
Titles on two occasions and the Ultimate Tag Team Titles on four... was chased out of UEW because of fear... afraid of the MEN IN BLACK.
[He continues to walk through the darkness.] At the last pay-per-view, "Ides of March '99", they did the unspeakable. Along with The Gimp and myself, they did something that was way more than just DEFEATING an opposing tag team. We did something that's BEYOND injuring another tag team. During a special tag team elimination match, we had Al Robertson and Peligro Mejicano of
The Al Robertson Experience COMMITTED. That's correct. We had those two buffoons put into a mental institution. Thus The Al Robertson
Experience was no more... all because of me, The Gimp... and the MEN IN
BLACK. [He stops walking.] Are you noticing the obvious pattern here? It never really mattered WHAT TEAM faced Dagger and Jayson in the ring. They always ended up battered, bruised, and defeated. Some even found themselves too afraid to even work for UEW ever again. One particular team even found themselves wearing strait jacket locked up in a padded room afterwards.
Will the same thing happen to the team of Fire and Ice? Will they join The Lethal Leaders, The Chaos Crew, and The Al Robertson
Experience in the ever growing sea of teams that my men have demolished? Or will they actually TRIUMPH over the darkness and win the UEW Television Tag Team Titles this Sunday?
[He puts the still burning candle down on a small wooden table. A very old looking coal stove is now seen in the distance.]
Instead of just answering that question for you myself, I will be giving you a rather informative demonstration. I hope Fire and Ice are paying attention. They may learn something about their chances at winning tag team gold this Sunday.
[He takes an ice cube out of his robe pocket.] Some ice... [He walks over to the stove. He takes the cube of ice and places it on a pan, which is already on the stove. Almost immediately, the cube melts. Within seconds, it's reduced to a small puddle of water in the pan.]
. reduced to a puddle of nothing. [He picks up the pan of water and walks back over to where the candle is burning. He looks down at the lit candle.]
Some fire... [He dumps the water from the pan onto the candle. The flame is quickly put out, thanks to the water.]
. extinguished and reduced to nothing. [He places the pan on the small table.] After this Sunday night, the team of Fire and Ice will be NOTHING. My men, Dagger and Jayson, however, will be your new UEW
Television Tag Team Champions. And there will be nothing left to do except... FADE TO BLACK!
