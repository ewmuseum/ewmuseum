```````` ::"The Red Baron" Rusty Bell is behind the scenes of the WWA. The recently signed superstar is getting his picture for the broadcasting of the card line-up. (Where it shows the wrestlers who are wrestling each other, like on the WWF) He walks up to the photographer, who is a little "iffy" like all photographers are. He's wearing his Texas Rangers hat, turned around backwards. He's got on a sleeveless FSU Baseball shirt, showing his "Yankees"
tatoo on his right bicep. He's got on a pair of mesh shorts, and an earing dangling from his ear.::
oO0- Photographer -0Oo ::Rusty walks up:: Well ! Look who we have heeeere ! Mr. Rusty Bell, yessir... You're next.. Step over in front of the spotlight and we'll see if we can get you a pose... Tee-Hee... ::Rusty walks over and sits in front of the spotlight in a folding chair. :: oO0- Photographer -0Oo Now.. DId you have anything in mind ??? oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo I was just gonna stand here... Ya know, go for the that, more is less kinda thing... oO0- Photographer -0Oo No, no silly... For someone like you, you should be doing something really special ! I got it !!!! Sit Down... ::Rusty sits down:: Now, put your head resting on the palm of your hand... Now, umm... Close one eye... Flare your nostrils... and umm... Stick out your tongue ! Yeah ! You look awesome ! oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo ::Stands up:: Nah... I don't think so... I'm Rusty Bell, I gotta get me a cool pose... Ya know, something that says, "I'm gonna kick your butt"... Here... How about something like this... ::Rusty turns the chair around and sits down in it. He gives the camera a "cool" look, and gives a slanted grin.:: oO0- Photographer -0Oo No, no, no, no, no ! You silly... That's not cool... Wait a second ! I've got it... How about a karate kick !? That will be perfect ! Like some, Bruce Lee, Karate Kid thing... I don't know what says
I'm gonna kick your butt better than a good old fashion karate kick... oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Man you're nuts... I aint gettin' in no karate kick... Now, here... I got a pose... You just take the picture, ok ? ::Rusty turns the chair around and does the exact same pose... The photographer looks at him funny, and goes ahead and takes the picture...::
oO0- Photographer -0Oo Ok, whatever you say silly billy... But, I don't think that's the kind of look that's gonna tell that guy you're fighting, Dave Hardcore, that you're gonna kick his butt !
oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Trust me... I don't need a look to kick someone's butt... It's all in here brotha'... ::Flex's his right bicep:: These are the guns I use for pounding... Heh, heh, heh... ::Chuckles:: oO0- Photographer -0Oo Ewww ! Tee-Hee ! ::Sticks his finger in his mouth:: Wanna see Dave Hardcore's pose ? He let me handle it... He's got one mean lookin' stare ! ::The photographer opens up a book of the wrestler's poses they use for the display of the matches in between commercials... He opens the book to Brendan
Bowes... It shows some guy wearing a pair of skimpy blue tights with his fingers on his head like a ballarena. Above him is a picture of a unicorn and a sign that says, "I'm a pony ! I'm a pony !" He turns the pages to Harbinger
Dines... It shows Dines flexing with a growl on his face... Rusty laughs as the photographer turns the pages to an old wrestler named "PWK"::
oO0- Photographer -0Oo EWww !!!! Yippe, I love this picture ! ::Rusty looks at the picture of "The Myth Maker PWK". [name withheld] sitting on a table. He has a daffadill costume on, with the flower petals around his face. He has his head slightly tilted with a serious look on his face, as he rests his pinky finger on his bottom lip. Rusty laughs and turns the page to another old wrestler known as the Grifter. The Grifter is standing with his butt facing the camera. He's bent over and has his head between his legs, so everything looks upside down to him. He has on white shorts that reveal his briefs on underneath that say, "Talk to me, I'm sensitive !" After Rusty gets
a good chuckle, he turns the page to Dave Hardcore... Hardcore is sitting in the folding with his legs crossed like a girl. There's a sign above him that say's "I hardcore", and Dave Hardcore patting his head and rubbing his belly::
oO0- Photographer -0Oo Tee-Hee ! I did that ! I did that ! I really did ! Dave Hardcore was so fun to work with... He was just lovely... A bit uncoordinated... But, once I tought him how to pat his head and rub his belly, he was a whiz... And he got that leg crossing thing down like you wouldn't believe.
oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Ha Ha ! ::Busts out laughing hysterically:: That's the guy I gotta wrestler !? Ha Ha Ha !!! This is gonna be great !!! So, tell me... Did anything else happen while you and he were, ::Clears throat:: "taking pictures" ::Laughs hard:: oO0- Photographer -0Oo Tee-Hee... Actually, he wouldn't stop talking about his dog ,Mittens, and his cat , Flowers... He loved them so much... it almost made me cry, because of the relationship they had, ya know !?
oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Ha Ha Ha !!!! ::Laughs hard:: So, lemme take a look at this guy... Hmm... First of all... ::Chuckles:: He's crossing his legs like a freakin' fag... Ehh... I'll break'em, then he won't do that, right ? ::Laughs:: He's pattin' his head and rubbin' his belly ? What the heck !? Who does that for a stinkin' picture ? I mean, sure, I used to do it when I was, like, maybe 7... I'll be danged... Tommy' got my fighting a freakin' loser ! Man, I can't wait to tear this guy apart ! I guess we can see how coordinated he is after If blacken his eyes some... Just lookin' at this guy makes me wanna open a can...
oO0- Photographer -0Oo Hey ! Dave Hardcore is my friend ! He is sooooo sweet... I think he is a peach, so there ! oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Ahh , You would... Well... I'll tell ya what, Mr. Photographer... How bout I kick your little "peach's" butt, huh ? Because that IS what I plan on doing, I mean, after all... It is my debut and everything...
oO0- Photographer -0Oo Yeah, well... I guess we'll have to see... Wait ! Oh Goody ! The pictures are ready, the pictures are ready ! ::Skips over to the dark room:: ::Takes a deep breathe:: Oooohhhh Myyyyyy Goooollllyyy !!!! I've never seen anything like this.... You look, You look... enchanting ! ::Shows the picture to Rusty:: oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Shut up... I believe the word you're looking for is "Manly".... oO0- Photographer -0Oo Oh Well... Tell me more about your match with Dave Hardcore ???? How are you gonna handle this fight, since it is your first one here in the WWA !? Hmmmm ??? oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Lemme let ya on a li'l something we wrestler like to call, "handicap"... Now, if you're stupid, incompetant, and ya can't wrestle.. Then you have a large handicap...
If you're lucky, and ya got skills, like The Red Baron, then you got 0 handicap... So... In this case, I think we're lookin' at a... uhh... I'd say about a 7 to -3... Something like that... Which means, that this "jobber" doesn't stand a chance against Rusty Bell... Ya know'ada mean ?
oO0- Photographer -0Oo Well... I guess we'll just have to see then... Dave Harcore's a tough fellow though... Hence the name, "Hardcore"... Tee-Hee !
oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Psshht... You must'a never heard of tha Humdinger, have ya ? I'll give ya another one of Rusty's, "Pearl's of Wisdow"... Now, there's only a few, and they don't come along very much so pay attention... Number 1... Never do jumping jacks if ya got the squirts... That one's important... And Number 2... No one gets up from the Humdinger, Nobody ! There's more little Pearl's of Wisdom, but I better not let too many of'em out yet... Back to Number 2... I've been using that moves since about 8th grade on my friends... And since then, no one has ever goten up from The Humdinger... So,
Don't count on your nifty little Davey friend to get up from either... Ok ? ::Winks and gives him "the gun" while he snaps:: oO0- Photographer -0Oo Tee-Hee ! Humdinger, what a funny, funny word ! oO0- Rusty Bell -0Oo Umm... Ok, whatever you.. Uhh.. Say... I better be going now... ::Rusty runs off:: oO0- Photographer -0Oo Toodles ! ::Waves to him good-bye:: "THAT-A-BABY !" -- Rusty Bell
