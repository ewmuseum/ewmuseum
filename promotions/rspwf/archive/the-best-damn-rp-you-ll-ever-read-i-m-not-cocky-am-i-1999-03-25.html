The best damn RP you'll ever read... (I'm not cocky, am I?)

25 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
www.nacw.ohhellyeah.com Just thought I'd plug the website before I got on with it. Here it is, the one you've been waiting for... the BEST damn RP you'll ever read. If you didn't read my earlier one, go back and check it out. (I'm "The True Living Legend"
Brian J. Blottie) If you don't feel like it, then read on anyway. I have a lot of confidence behind this one. This is the one that's gonna get you guys to finally realize I'm HERE TO STAY! And you're actually getting to read it
BEFORE it comes out tomorrow! Feel priveledged, people! And now... on with the show!
___________________________________________________________ *A camera is at the door of "The True Living Legend's" estate. This is where the scene begins. The door is open, and the camerman walks into the frontroom. The pictures normally seen on the walls are strewn about all over the place. Several have been destroyed. The carpet is pulled up in places. Glassware is broken everywhere. As the camera wanders through the house it doesn't get any better. The dining room is in shambles, food covering the walls in large chunks. The kitchen is the same. A trail of blood is shown in the lens of the camera, and the camera follows it*
Cameraman: Hello? HELLO?! *The cameraman puts the camera on the kitchen counter, and pulls out a walkie talkie. He begins to speak into it* Cameraman: Yeah, Fred? You better call 911. I don't know what's going on in here, but this place is torn up, and I've found a trail of blood leading somewhere. I'm gonna try finding Blottie. Let's just hope we're not too late...
Fred?: Ok, just be careful, Mike. We don't know what's going on in there, and
Blottie seems to have lost his mind. Ten-four.
*The cameraman picks up the camera, and follows the blood. It leads into a closet in what appears to be a bedroom. As the camera peeks inside, movement is seen from in the corner. The light attached to the camera is turned on, revealing Brian J. Blottie, leaning in the corner. Blood is dried on his forehead, and more blood flows freely from cuts on his arms and chest. He wears no shirt, and the same pants from Sunday. He doesn't look good. Blottie coughs, spitting out a little blood, and then smiles at the camera*
Blottie: I thought you'd come. Wait, scratch that. I KNEW you'd come. Coats was always good about watching out for his athletes.
Cameraman: Yeah... he wants us to tell you that he wants you to see a psychologist. He thinks he'll help. The NACW will pay for all charges...
*The cameraman hands Blottie a card, and Blottie takes it. Blottie looks down, looks back up, and smiles weakly* Blottie: Tell Shawn thanks for me. I think I'll take him up on that. *Blottie puts the card in his back pocket, then turns back to the camera* Blottie: Why'd he send a camera crew to deliver a business card? Cameraman: Well, he wanted taped proof that you got it, and also wanted to see first-hand how you were doing. Blottie: Heh... how does it look like I'm doing? I'm a miserable wreck. I have all these pent up emotions, all these fears, and inner problems to deal with, and five million people watching at home get to see them aired on camera.
They probably think I'm insane... I'm not. I just have problems, just like all of them. Mine happen to be a little bit more... severe.
All I wanted, my entire life, was my father to love me. But he didn't. All he did was have spite for me. And I took that spite and tried to make it my own. Tried to emulate him. In the hopes that I could get him out of my head.
But I couldn't. No matter how well I did in life, no matter how well I did in the ring, I couldn't. Now I realize... I was a fool. I let him control me. I let him ruin my life. And I should be enjoying the life I have. Life is a beautiful thing. Friendship is the other...
*Blottie shifts a little, cringes in pain, then turns back at the camera* Blottie: Jeremy Rhodes and The Flying Scotsman... two friends. The Scotsman wanted to be my friend, but I abused him. Hurt him. Took his trust and twisted it. Just like my father would have done. Well... I want to apologize to him. And let him know that if he ever wants a friend... a true friend, and if he can ever trust me again, that I'll be there for him. As for Rhodes...
Rhodes, this Sunday, I am coming to that ring to prove something to everyone in the NACW. That just because you're the U.S. Champion, and I'm a relative newcomer to the NACW, I can still beat you within an inch of your life and pin you clean. No outside interference. No weapons. Just me and you, pounding the life out of each other until there's almost nothing left. And then, when it's all said and done, I want you to realize... that even though we just almost killed each other, we can go to the back, laugh about it, and hug each other. Pat each other on the back and let the other know that we respect him.
I respect you, Jeremy. You're the NACW United States Champion. You're one of the craziest men in the NACW. But most of all, you're one hell of a man.
Face to face, when we square off, I'm coming in there to rearrange you're facial features. But backstage... I'm hoping that you can emulate that respect
I have for you, and we can turn that into a friendship... a love for each other that few NACW superstars could understand.
*Blottie gets up, pushes the camera out of the way, and limps towards his bed. He sits down, and refaces the camera* Blottie: I want to be brothers with you and The Scotsman, Rhodes. But not just any kind of brothers. I want to be BLOOD brothers. You understand blood, don't you Rhodes? The feeling of it trickling down your forehead as you let the pain take over every emotion, every feeling you have. The taste of it as it hits your lips, the coppery taste erasing who you are... who you once were.
The smell of it... I think you know about this the best, Rhodes. The pure exhilaration of smelling the blood of your opponent... it's almost like sex, isn't it? I understand that now. I understand why you and The Flying Scotsman love spilling the substance that flows through our veins, that gives us life.
And I want a piece of it. I want a piece of you. Blood is what I want, Rhodes... yours, mine, and the Scotsman's, flowing together in a wonderful mix of pain, hate, and love. This Sunday, I will get a taste of it. And hopefully, when me, you, and The Scotsman get a chance, we can all get a taste of each other's. Get a little bit of love going on in our
"family", if you know what I mean.
*Blottie lays back in his bed. He closes his eyes, as the camera looms above him. A siren is heard approaching* Cameraman: Ummm... I think the ambulance we sent for is here for you... Blottie: Ok... I guess I'll get on. As long as I can be out by this Sunday. Cameraman: I'm sure you will... *Blottie sits up, his eyes fly open, and he seems very intent. He stares into the camera, a look of determination appearing therein* Blottie: I WILL be out this Sunday. The "Lobo" will know it, and so will the fans of the NACW. A new Blottie is coming out. No more backfighting, no more cheap shots, no more <BLEEP> around. Just me and my opponent... our blood...
and the thrill of competition. You will know what a "True Living Legend" is. And you will know that I am the ONLY one. Until next time... *Blottie gets up, and walks out of his room, towards the enterance. As he walks out the door, the ambulance pulls up, and the EMS personel hop out, opening the back doors. They try to help Blottie inside, but he shoves them aside, and gives them a look of disgust. He climbs inside and shuts the doors himself, not allowing either of the two into the back with him. They argue a little, but then digress and climb in, taking off. The camera fades to black*
-------------- "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie Member of the NACW... soon to prove the name isn't just a gimmick... just ask
Sir Smokes A Lot, Jack Daniels, and the Rookie Rumble
Thinks everyone should worship him... Except Ish... 8)
