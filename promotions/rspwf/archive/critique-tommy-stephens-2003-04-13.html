[CRITIQUE] Tommy Stephens

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just for fun and sluts... or something... Yeah, anyway, flame away! [The shot comes to the inside of the MGM Grand Casino, where one Goku
Waru is sitting down at a slot machine, inserting coins like a madman.
For over a month, Waru's been practicing and honing the art of slot-machinery as he took his sick-leave from EMWC... As we watch,
Waru dramatically pulls down the level and lets the slots go...]
GW: AHHHH! YESSSAH! [The bells go ringing as the coins flow down, with Waru scooping them up feverishly. Must be Goku's lucky day... Well, it was.]
TS: There you are! Hey Goku! What's up, my main man! [Ugh. Yeah, you can see the delight from Waru's face fall away as the
EMWC Television Champion, Tommy Stephens, strides his way into the picture.]
GW: I just won tha big plize. TS: Big prize? Well, we're just going to have to live it up a little now, aren't we? GW: "We"? TS: Damn right! I'm not going to let any of these Las Vegas crooks rip you off and steal your money.
[Tommy pats Goku on the shoulder as he takes a handful of coins from the winnings and stuffs them into his pocket.]
TS: But hey, it's expected that you win. You're the manager of the one and only Champion of Champions - you're expected to have some of my luck rub off on you.
GW: Buttah I was winning befole you came, fol many weeks. TS: Oh, really, wow. That's, uh... [As Tommy continues to nod his head, he also continues to stuff his jeans pocket full of Waru's winnings.]
TS: That's amazing. You winning all these weeks and me also winning all these weeks, just in the ring!
GW: Buttah you haven't had a match since Showtime. TS: And? GW: So how did you win? TS: What, you didn't see that beating Ego MAX put on that schlub
Rhodes? Or that thing, Bailey? Or what about that massive thumb-war?
GW: I- TS: And hey, don't forget, anyone who wins in Ego MAX is a win for everyone in Ego MAX. I think Joey won, too.
GW: I know fol sule that "Joey" did not win anything. TS: Oh, come on, he's a good kid. Hell, if you trained him like you trained me, he'd be unstoppable!
GW: I wirr not tlain "Joey". TS: Nah, sure you will. But hey, let's not talk business. I haven't seen you in... what, a three, four months?
GW: Showtime wasn't but a month ago. TS: Oh, right, yeah... [Tommy continues to nod and, again, continues to stuff more coins down his pants' pockets] TS: Yeah, now I remember. You were there. For both me and Juan. Yeah, you got knocked around silly that night, didn't you?
GW: Yes! That is why I havah been hele fol are this time! Havah you gotten dumbah whire I havah been away?!?
TS: Dumb? Naw, not this guy. I'm just a little pre-occupied, you know? What, with Ego MAX, planning to torture that theif Bailey, fighting someone... thing... whatever this week -
GW: Tha Sclaypah. TS: Right, him... it... whatever. But more importantly - GW: Mole impoltant than a Terrorvision Titre match? TS: Yeah! Don't you remember? The Champion's Corner? MY own show? GW: Oh, light... That. TS: Hey now, don't get too excited. Save some of that energy for the show! GW: Folget tha show! You don't need to plepale for some stupid show! You need to plepale fol tha Sclaypah! He is vely dangahlous! If anyone hele, he courd easiry take youl titre.
TS: Don't talk like that... You'll kill my buzz about my show. [Waru slams down his coin bucket - taking away Tommy's chances of swiping more coins.] GW: Don't you tark rike that! I havah tlied and tlied to get thlough to you to plactice, but you nevah do! Othah times, it wasn't as impoltant, but this time, against tha Sclaypah, it's vely impoltant!
If you don't, you wirr rose youl titre! [Stephens steps back a little, his face looking a little shocked... a little like he's in some deep thought, pondering what it all could mean.]
TS: You know... You're right. I shouldn't be focusing or thinking about my show this week. [Waru smiles and nods.] TS: If I do, I could... I could really over hype the thing and maybe oversell my expectations. I really wouldn't want that. If I do that, I could bomb on the show, and that wouldn't be a good thing.
[Waru buries his head in his hands.] TS: Yeah, definitely don't want to over think it cause that could throw me off to when I'm up there. And hell, we're in Las Vegas - I should take a tip from all the performers here and really be low-key about everything.
[Goku meakly lifts his head up.] GW: Wourd you at reast think about tha match against tha Sclaypah? Fol once, even plactice a rittre?
[Tommy starts to chuckle.] TS: Ha, good one Goku. Come on, in Las Vegas? Practice? What, with all these winnings just burning a whole in my pocket? Pfft, who has time for practice? Anyway, nice seeing ya, Goku, but it's craps time for me!
[Tommy walks off towards the craps tables as Waru shakes his head.] GW: Clap indeed... [The scene then fades out on the saddened Waru, who starts putting what's left over of his winnings into the slot machine... only to get some lemons and oranges... blah.]
