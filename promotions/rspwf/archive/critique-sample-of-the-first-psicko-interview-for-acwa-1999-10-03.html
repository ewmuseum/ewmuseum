The RP is aimed at Sammy Super Salsa.. See if you can find it. And what would ACWA be with out a psychopathic schizo ? So here goes everything : [9:21 PM, middle of nowhere. The cameracrew is in the backseat of a 1930 classic car, as they drive up an abandoned road on this stormy night. The rain is pouring, the wind is raging, visibility is near zero as they creep forward over the muddy forestroad. Riding shotgun is none other then the world-renowned Inspector Chopsuey. The chinese investigator is accompanied by his faithfull secretary and sultry pornstar, Asia Carrera. The private eye, in his mid 40's turns around and adresses the camera.]
Chopsuey: Old Chinese proverb say "Rain fall, wind blow, but both suck". A-ha, as long as we stuck in stormy condition, me tell you about destination, yes ? We go solve murder of Elisabeth Arden at Manson Mansion.
Drive is still long, you better turn of camera, save batteries, yes ? Cameraman: Why don't you tell us some anecdotes from your long and fruitfull career. It would be great filler material.
Chopsuey: Conversation like TV-set on honeymoon : unnecessary. And who told you about my fruitshop ?
[The cameraman reluctantly fades to black.. The next shot sees the car arriving at Manson Mansion. The rain has gone to a trickle, the wind has gone to a sigh, the clock has gone to 10:12. When the car stops all men get out, and Asia too.]
Chopsuey: We arrived at mansion, good, good.. Pull the chain, Asia, please. No, the bell, the one with the bell, next to the door..<br> [Asia pulls the chain hanging next to the door and a loud scream is heard, coming from inside. [Then..All of the sudden..Unexpected..Catching them off guard..Surprising them..Are you getting tired of this..Without warning.. Unannounced..the door creeps open. A man is standing in the doorway, dressed like a butler and carrying a white stick. He turns towards Asia and starts speaking.]
Butler: Inspector Chopsuey, I presume ? We've been expecting you. Enter, please.. Can I take your bags ?
[The blind butler bends forward and grabs Asia's breasts. She screams, startling the poor man.]
Butler: Oh, I'm sorry.. Never mind the bags, I'll get them later. I'll show you to the murderscene now. But first let me close the door.
[The butler proceeds to take the handle of the door and opens it wide. He then walks away to a corridor, beckoning the guests to follow him as he waves his stick in front of him.]
Asia: Excuse, Mr. Butlerman, but what should we call you ? Butler: Benson, mum. Asia: Okay, Benson, lead us to.. Butler: It's Bensonmum, Jamessir Bensonmum. It's my full name. Cameraman: Bensonmum, what kind of a name is that ? Jamessir Bensonmum: It was my father's name. If you would just follow me.. [The butler leads them up a flight of stairs. Chopsuey, who has been pretty silent until now, walks next to the visually challenged househelp.]
Chopsuey: Excuse me, no other staff in house ? Jamessir Bensonmum: This one I'm using is the only one I have. Chopsuey: /sighs/ I mean personnel.. Jamessir Bensonmum: Not as far as I know.. Haven't seen anyone else yet. Ah, we have arrived.
[The group has arrived at a large wooden door, leading to the family room. The butler slowly pushes the door open. The room is nicely decorated. A red couch, some bookcases, a large open fireplace, a naked body hanging in front of the fireplace.. That must be the murder victim. The murdered female is hung by her ears from the fireplace. There is no trace of shotwounds or stab bings. No blood at all. Inspector Chopsuey walks into the room, looks around for about 2 minutes and turns to the butler.]
Chopsuey: Go and fetch everybody. I know who did it. Jamessir Bensonmum: As you wish, sir. [The butler staggers out of the room, and closes the door behind him.] Cameraman: I've never seen anything like this before.. How come you allready know who did it ? How is that possible ?
Chopsuey: Good power of deduction, years of experience and reading the script thoroughly. Let's take a seat while we wait for the usual suspects to arrive.
[The cameraman take a seat opposite of Chopsuey, who has Asia on his lap, dancing around while they wait. After a minute or two the door opens, and a
50-year old corpulent man, dressed in a corporal's uniform, walks into the room..] Man: Ah, Inspector Chopsuey. It's good to finally meet you. Jamessir tells me you solved the whodunnit ?
Chopsuey: Correct, Major Tom. And you were my first suspect. Take a seat and allow myself to explain..myself. I understand you and Elisabeth Arden had a special bond. Correct ?
Major Tom: Well, Major Tom to Groundcontrol, that's true, I admit it.. Elisabeth and I have loved each other for as long as I can remember. Chopsuey: /in a stern tone of voice, one finger in the air/ But ! Major Tom: But.. but last week she, she ran away from me. After all these years.. I could've killed her right then and there. /voice going from calm to agitated/ Yes, I wanted to kill her, I was mad enough, but I didn't ! I didn't, okay !!
Chopsuey: I never said you did, Major. No, as much as you hated her for leaving you, you are not capable of killing man. What also lead to discharge from army, yes ?
Major Tom: Ay, that's is true.. Asia: I've helped many armymen discharge too. Chopsuey: Not now, Asia. No, Major Tom, you're not the killer. My next suspect was HIM ! [Chopsuey points to a scrawny, 20 year old, red-headed, snotnosed kid who just entered the room. The kid looks startled from the screaming.]
Chopsuey: Yes, you, Minor Tom. Major Tom's own son, you were my next suspect. You wanted to get Elisabeth out of house, yes ? You had to get her away from your father, to be able to fullfill your desires !
Minor Tom: /bursting out in tears/ Yes ! Yes, it's true. I had to get her away from here. I could never love the one I covet as long as she and my father lived under the same roof.. Oh daddy, I love you ! I love you !
[The young man jumps on his father and tries to kiss him, but is forcefully pushed away by the elder military officer.]
Major Tom: Damn queer ! You killed her, you killed her ! Chopsuey: /calmly interfering/ No, no.. Your son also didn't kill her. He just wanted to get her away from here. Just like his old man he doesn't have balls to kill someone.
Minor Tom: Snif, snif.. But who killed Elisabeth then ? Chopsuey: My next intuition told me it was this man. [Chopsuey walks to the corner, where there lies a pile of dirty laundry. He reaches in with one hand, and pulls out a 3'5", very hairy midget. The midget is kicking and screaming in the air, but quickmy calms down when
Chopsuey passes him to Asia.] Minor Tom: Who or better, what is that ? Chopsuey: It's your halfbrother, Aristocrates.. That's right, your father and Elsiabeth's secret lovechild.
Minor Tom: Is it true, father ? Did you and Elisabeth produce offspring ? Is this freak my kidbrother ?
Major Tom: /bowing head in shame/ Yes, yes, it's true. We've been hiding him from you in this pile of dirty laundry ever since he was born. We fed him all left-overs and all the mice he could catch.. I'm sorry I never told you,
I'm so sorry, son.. [The two Tom hug in tears, and Minor Tom takes the opportunity to fondle his father's ass.]
Chopsuey: Excuse me, me so solly to break up big family moment, but save it for therapy. Major Tom: But the case is solved right ? Aristocrates killed his own mother, didn't he ? Chopsuey: No, he was just suspect. He didn't kill her, for he saw no threat in her. She was one of only 2 he ever saw, and he loved her with passion.
No, Aristocrates didn't kill Elisabeth either. [At that moment, the door flies opens and a mermaid riding a giant pink crab shoots from the hallway, circles the room and leaves as sudden as it appeared.]
Cameraman: Did you see that ? Soundguy: No. Cameraman: Neither did I.. /mumbling/ Gotta lay of that herbal tea. [From the open door appears the blind butler as he wobbles into the room.] Chopsuey: Me know what you think. Butler did it, yes ? Wrong, my movies are good. No Hercule Perrier.
Major Tom: But no-one else is left ? Who commited this horrendous crime then ? Who ? I beg you ! Who ? [Inspector Chopsuey walks to the window, opens it and pulls in a small, yet muscly Mexican man. The strange man is wearing green flashy spandex and a cape. A bottle of Tequilla rolls onto the floor, as Chopsuey judothrows him to the ground.]
Chopsuey: Man did ! Asia, tie him up. [Asia whips out the bondage gear and hangs the man from the ceiling in leather straps. The leather mask covers his head, as we still haven't seen his face.]
Minor Tom: But why ? How ? When ? Chopsuey: One question at time.. Greeny here did kill Elisabeth. After close inspection of corpse, I concluded victim was gassed to death. With my superior powers of deduction, it was obvious that this total stranger to you, killed Elisabeth.
Major Tom: Okay, but how did he gas her ? Surely we would have noticed if a man with that kind of equipment entered our house.
Asia: Looking at him, you can clearly see his equipment is easy to be hidden.. I need a magnifying glass here.
Chopsuey: While your beloved Elisabeth was resting in that very couch, he sat down and gassed her.
[Beans bubble-bobble-bubble up from the strapped, masked man. Just be glad smellovision wasn't invented yet.]
Minor Tom: Oh no.. You surely don't mean ? Chopsuey: Yes, Beans sat down on your beloved poodle and sufficated her with his bodily odors. He's a very cruel man, Major and Minor Tom.
Cameraman: What a horrible way to go.. even if you are a poodle. Only why remains, Inspector. Why did he gas her ? What's his motive ?
Chopsuey: It's millenium. People don't need a motive.. Besides, he's one craey asshole. He's supposed to be at Marple Institute of Criminally Isane, or Mickey. He was institutionalised after he tried to smuggle rich, white
Americans across Mexican border to have them work on his melonfarm near Tijuana. Cameraman: That can mean only one thing.. and I don't know what it is. Minor Tom: The man is one demented lollipop, but I want to see his face. The face of the one who killed Elisabeth. Take of the mask. Please.
[Asia starts undoing zippers from the leather bondage mask.. Green and pink bangs fall out, and a hint of goggles is seen. This horrifying sight scares
Aristocrates so much, that they have to cover up the Red Neck's face again.] Chopsuey: Another case closed. Thank you for outrageuos pay. I have to go now. Have to solve mistery in Newark tomorrow, murder taking place 4 minutes from now. Widow will kill her 3-legged, 11-year old lover..
Major Tom: Women.. Last time I trusted a woman, was in Paris, 1940. She went out for a bottle of wine and 2 hours later the Germans invaded.

Hippie: Hey man ! Peace ! Make Love, Not Here ! Yeah ! Wacky Weed ! [The hippie runs off in to the woods and vanishes from sight. Asia starts the car and they drive away. This time taking the highway next to the house instead of the forestroad.. Once again, Inspector Chopsuey resolved another open and shut case. Until next time, I'm the narrator.]
