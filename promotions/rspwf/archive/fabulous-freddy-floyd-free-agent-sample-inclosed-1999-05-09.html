Next there should be at least two hopefully three flash packets sent out a week. if your fed meets these please E-mail me, or IM me at

Freddy Floyd - Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, can I share with you, that
I don't think that I have ever been so insulted in my life. The way that these morons think that they can treat Freddy Floyd is freggin joke. I have people banging down my door begging me to return to the International Championship
Wrestling Federation, and when I get here what happens? I have blow overs like

Freddy Floyd - ah let me see here, first up we Shawn "Scorpion" Viper. Now
Shawn, I will admit you are a somewhat average wrestler. Not that really means anything, but I thought I would strat you off with a single glimmer of hope.
But the sad truth is, that your skills don't even come close to matching up with Freddy Floyd. So in a match up where anything can happen, one thing is for certain. It won't be Shawn "Scorpion" Viper walking out with the victory, becasue he's just plain not good enough. Next
<< Freddy hands Florence back the paper, and she hands him the next sheet on the list >> Freddy Floyd - Now this one is sad sad story. We have a guy by the name of
