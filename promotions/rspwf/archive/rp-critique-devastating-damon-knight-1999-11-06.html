[RP Critique] "Devastating" Damon Knight

6 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Aight, this is a roelplay I did for Harp's fed, NWP. First roleplay. First time posting a roleplay on RSPWF, too. Anyway, get at me, tell me what you think ...
CRITIQUE! Thank you. Ryan H. Handles: "Devastating" Damon Knight [NWP : 0-0-0] The Main Attractions[FREE AGENTS] Quote: "You can run all your life and never go anywhere." [Fade In...] [The camera has faded in from a pitch black screen, to a solid white screen. At the bottom of it, a cursor begins blinking spelling out ...
"Devastating" Damon Knight The cursor blinks after the end of the name, "Knight." Soon after this a voice is heard.] VO : Damon Knight ... a lost man now found. [The sound of an commentators voice is heard screaming "TOTAL DEVASTATION!!" After this the voice is heard once more.] VO : Damon Knight ... Making moves and doing big things. [The commentators voice is heard once again, screaming, "DAMON KNIGHT DOES IT
AGAIN!"] VO : Damon Knight ... He's back, and this time, he ain't playin' no games. [Damon Knight voice is then heard saying "I'm Lots of action...Twice the satisfaction... and If i'm not the main event ... I'm DEFINITELY the MAIN
ATTRACTION!!"]
[From that, the scene opens up to a steel frame of Damon Knight. He is shown in a federation holding a title above his head. A few seconds past and that fades out, then another picture fades in showing Damon Knight standing above an unknown figure. Looking at the picture it is obvisious that he has just finished a heat rivalry and battle. In the picture he is sweating and he looks in obvisious discust, however, the referee looks to be walking his way to raise his hand in victory ...
... The scene then changes to the inside of a fancy Hummer Limosuine. The hummer limo is white, the windows are tinted, and around the windows are a silver outline. This pacific limosuine is one of the fancier ones. Though the tinted glass you are able to see that a light is on. This makes you progress.
As this happens you commence to move foward the door is swung open. At this point, you are unable to make out any figures, however, a hand is seen inviting you in. As you progress and get into the vechile you see ...
"Devastating" Damon Knight!!! ... Damon Knight is wearing hushpuppies, The type with the silver buckle. He wears a jet-black Armani slacks. Damon Knight has a tucked in coller-less shirt, under a jet-black vest. Lapping over the shirt is a twenty-two inch platinum chain. The chain is naturally glistening as it slightly sways back and fourth due to the body movement of Damon Knight. The shape of the piece is that of a pentagon, which is closed up due to the black pearl, and over that is the initials "D.K." which is in platinum, as well. As you look up at the face of
Damon Knight you see his unmistakable green eyes. You notice that Damon's ears are pierced white have diamonds in them. AS always, Damon Knight's hair is cornrolled in zig-zags, with a see though skull cap on. The camera slightly zooms out and you notice the definition of Damon Knight's body though his attire, you also notice that Damon Knight fits in well together with the colors of the vechile ...
... Damon Knight is seen in full view now as he casually lays back, getting a glass of wine out into his right hand. With Damon's left hands he rubs his chin with a natural cocky smirk on his face, and begins to speak ...]
DK : Yo, a true playa, a hero is someone who makes moves and does big thangs no matter tha time nor place. Michael Jordan wa a hero. Wayne
Gretzky was a hero. John Elway was a hero. [A slight pause as Damon Knight speaks once more with that trademark smirk.] DK : Damon Knight is a true playa. Damon Knight is a real man and Damon Knight is back and this time i'ma play by my rules. All of those in tha NWP <Grin> y'all can envy me.
Ladies wanna be wit' me. All of the fake men out there wanna be like me. But point blank, when push comes to shove ...
[Damon Knight pauses with a serious look on his face.] DK : Y'all are all my bitches! My first singles match is gon' be a match against Brian Floyd. <Smirk> The self-proclaimed "Brutal One". <Grin> Yo, peep this, from what I hear you like to "shoot tha shit". I'm gon'
advise you different, i'ma advise you to keep it shut. I'm drop a lil' knowledge for ya, Brain, "It is better to be thought an idiot then to open your mouth and remove all doubt."
[Damon Knight chuckles.] DK : You wanna talk slick, then do that. <Shrugs> Yo, if your 'bout it, <Shakes Head> i'm wit' it. I hope you was takin' notes on Spotlight ... I hope you saw tha skills ... I hope you saw tha serial thriller in action 'cause come November eighth ...
[Damon Knight grins.] DK : It's yo turn ta' have Dee dee kay carry ya to tha top, and then drop ya all in tha same night. You step up against tha Main Attraction, in a weapons match at that, well then, be prepared to be smacked down.
"Brutal One" you wanna play tha game? You wanna see if you got what it takes? [Damon Knight shrugs his shoulders curiously.] DK : Fine by me. When it comes to it, your always on the bottom rung of the ladder. I'm always higher on the totem pole than you. Next time, don't sign tha match 'cause when you look up you'll be viewing in tha light
... tha knightlight. [Damon leans foward.] DK : When your laid out, you'll know that you have been totally devastated! [Damon Knight claps his hands together as the engine of the hummer is heard turned up.] DK : "Brutal One", I'm Lots of action ...Twice the satisfaction ... and If i'm not the main event ... I'm DEFINITELY the MAIN ATTRACTION!!
[Damon Knight sets back with that scrol which is his very own smirk.] DK : Now, moving on, as I looked upon that piss-poor card I saw my name twice. Due to NWP and their lacking of high ratings they have came to me and asked me to be an "ironman."
NWPs' Knight and Shinning armor is gon' be in two matches. Brian Floyd and I will tangle and then to throw tha icing on the cake Damon Knight will step back into the ring to face off against sixteen others.
[Smirk.] DK : I'm going to make this short and sweet. On Spotlight last week, I lost my chance, this time around, I get another. I'ma break it to y'all like this ... fool me once, fool me twice I think not. So no outside interferrance, no time to fool wit' tha commoners. Going into this match i'm just another name in a hat, coming out i'm going to be the third and final man to qualify.
[Pause.] DK : People think they are gon' be tha last real man? People think they are going to be the winner, and think they are going to take the last spot?
Yo,t hink again! At the end of the night, and when the show is goin' off, tha last thing you'll see is Dee dee kay, and i'll be tha last man standing!
[Damon Knight shakes his head as he holds his hand up, victoriously. The camera fades to black as Damon Knight has a huge smirk on his face.]
