Looking For A Fed.

13 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please get back to me if you like this. Note: I can RP better ,but it`s been a couple of months since last fed. Name: Trent Rage Height: 6'1" Weight: 235 Entrance Music: "Come As You Are" by Nirvana Finisher: The Headbanger [Fisherman Buster] Physical Description: Trent Rage was a grunge rockstar. His hair is Dirty Blond and never combed covering over his blue eyes. He wears a blue torn jeans, leather jackets and cutoff gloves, occasionally wearing t-shirts and jean shorts. He has tattoos of a chain going from his shoulder to his bicep, a large serpent on his back, and FUCK written across his knuckles.
Bio: Trent Rage was a Grunge RockStar. He was always very insane and never fit in. Anger and violence fills his mind with other thoughts of madness. He was always a crazy one no one understood. He is crazed and misunderstood then he is normal. He is easily threatened and flings into an unstoppable rage of hate when confronted by another person who he doesnt like. He is never to be seen anymore he just lies in his house and thinks of what thoughts will tortue him next.
Style: Trent Rage is a street brawler, fighting, and high flying skill. 10 Moves: [1] Rage - He flings into an unstoppbale rage using of his strength. [2] T-Bone Suplex [3] German Suplex [4] Hurrican DDT [5] Running Powerbomb [6] Famouser [7] Backbreaker [8] Elbow Drop [9] Moonsault [10] 7-Year Itch Strengths [1] Confidence: He never gives up and keeps trying with all his strength to beat his opponent.
[2] Stamina: When Trent Rage is losing he keeps with it, never gives in on submissions, never gets pinned without a fight.
Flaws [1] Unconcentration: When he is set too much on his confidence he can lose concentartion and end up losing.
[2] Crazy Determination: His high flying skill can get him in trouble when he goes for Suicide Dives and other crazy moves which can go completely wrong.
Sample RP: [The scene opens up in a dark and dirty club. "Image of Grunge" is playing a crazy set, complete with frontman, Trent Rage belting out anti-pop vocals. The song goes on for a few minutes when the bassist messes up horrible and Trent calls for a stop of the music.]
Trent Rage: What the F*ck, man!!! You suck!! Bassist: Trent calm down your going crazy. Trent Rage: What the hell does that mean. Bassist: Just calm down Trent. Trent Rage: Don`t tell me what to do. Bassist: What the hell is a matter with you Trent. Trent Rage:You f*cking asshole. [Trent runs for the Bassist and throws some punches. A fight breaks out and
Trent flings into a rage and starts to beat the Bassist.The Bassist tries to defend himself, but can`t take Trents punches.The other band members break it up before it got serious.]
Bassist: Uhh *Cough Cough*. Trent Rage: You better leave me alone before I kick your ass. Drummer: Yo Trent calm dow.... Trent Rage: Shut up get out of my face. [Trent runs for the bassist , but is held down by the police. Screaming at them
Trent is taken away to jail.]
[The camera arrives at the jail cell.Trent is looking confused and is laying on the ground mumbling to himself.]
[He lies lifeless in the corner of his cell talking ot himself. He is shaking and rapping his hands around himself making faint sounds of disturbness.]
Trent Rage: Whats wrong with me damnit. I`m crazy I got to get out of here now. Let me out let me out. Help Me help me please don`t leave me. [The Police are looking to see what he is talking about. They go in and ask him whats the matter]
Trent Rage: Who are you get out of my cage. Police: Be quiet whats the matter. Trent Rage: Everything is wrong you cage me in to my death. Leave me alone go now Now!!!!!!!!!!!!!
[Trent starts to scream at the camera as its filming all of this from outside his cell. The cameraman starts to ask him questions]
Cameraman: Didn`t you know there was a big producer at that club? What got into you? Trent Rage: Hate. Now get the f*ck off. [Trent spits in the camera and tries to knock it over by sticking his hand out of his cell. The cameraman loses the camera.]
(Fade to Static) [Trent is still screaming Help Me as loud as he can. The camera is broken and loses sound.]
