[MVW Fang] Roleplay *Constructive Criticism _DEMANDED_*

4 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a uncouth avenue, in a unidentifiable part of the US. It is pooring rain, and the sky is very dim and grey; with thick clouds. The camera sluggishly moves through the rainswept streets, until it stops outside a set of barred up windows. There seems to be a store, and it's sign reads... "Dave's
Shop o' Shit." Captivating the scene, however, is the man who stands in front of the shop, leaning against the brick wall around the windows. It is none other than MVW Oneida County Champion, Fang. He wears a black trenchcoat, buttoned up in front, and a pair of casual Levi's 501 jeans. The collar of his jacket is lifted up, to protect his neck from the ice cold percipitation, and his. Finding no shelter from the rain, the wrestling superstar is drenched, his grey bandana soaked to the bone with rain and matted to his forehead, while large drops of rain bead up and cling to his face. Even though he's not in the most peaceful of settings, Fang wears an all too familiar grin of devilry. He has his Oneida County belt slung on his shoulder; being pounded with raindrops, he speaks.]
Fang: [patting the championship belt] A piece of metal... Gold, to be exact. Gold to be tarnished in the rain, and gold to be melted by the hot. But never would a piece of gold be so destroyed as if it falls in the hands of "Hotstuff"
Johnny Adonis. But this golden accessory means nothing compared to the satisfaction I'll feel after smashing your face into millions of tiny fragments.
[Sighs.] All of your asinine words put together... They mean nothing. All of your jeers, all of your degrading remarks do not insult me the least bit. But they make me mad. They get my heart pumping; they get my blood to race throughout my body in a gush of molten steel.
And then you claim that you're the _real_ champion. The man who can wear the
Oneida County Belt around his waist and be proud of it. Well, the only man capable of doing that is Fang.
Who's the one they warned you about in wrestling school; the one they said took his bumps too crazilly, too suicidal... to the extreme!? Who's the one with the fire burning in his eyes and devilry dancing in his heart; the man who'll dive to his potential doom if it gives him just one chance to take some punk out for good?! Who'll plunge this whole damn league into anarchy until he gets exactly what it is he deserves - the metaphorical crown of respect from his peers, glory, and the blood of every wrestler in existence upon his fists? Fang! That man is Fang, and until you can say the same about yourself, you obviously cannot take his Oneida County belt, nor his dignity.
[Fang takes off his bandana reveiling his slightly thin brown hair. He rings out the cloth, and slips it into his pants pockets. He continues.]
But hopefully you are a fighter. I only wish that you don't serve as my dumby; or personaly trainer for the evening. You want what is mine... You want my championship. You want the glory, respect, and honor that comes with wearing the gold. But let me remind you, Mr. Adonis, it is not the belt that makes the man, rather the man that makes the belt.
A cowardly narcissus like yourself, Mr. Adonis, would give the Oneida County
Title a name of shame. A courageous warrior like myself, on the other hand, makes the golden belt worthy of it's moniker, _championship_. And for that reason, without the weight of the many outlying factors, I shall stay
"champion" until my day is done, while you, Mr. Adonis, shall remain a lowly footsoldier for the rest of your career.
[The rain starts to let, up as rolling thudner is heard in the background. It's not finished, but the rain has slowed to a drizzle.]
Mr. Adonis, you have marched into a kingdom of my own madness. You have brought yourself into the private hell of a madman. A mad god. Fragmented, deranged, twisted beyond all recognition. How are you going to contend with all the odds being against you? How are you going to cope with the rage... the madness of the Gods that thunders from on high. I shall be coming for you, Johnny Adonis.
And all your "Hot Stuff" will not be enough to save you. [Lightning strikes, as Fang continues with no hesitation.] Yes, you are walking into the private hell of a man. You are looking to enter my world. My world of pain and try to kill the god in the middle of his kingdom. Well, in the squared circle, my private hell and chaos, I rule. You've seen that in all four of my MVW encounters. Undefeated, with the exception of a tarnished score, yet to be y fades to a look of nothingness.]
Many men in the Mohawk Valley have debts to be payed to Fang. Mr. Adonis, yours is of extreme value... Physical and mental value, however, I don't think you have enough physicallity or mentality to spare.
Now I welcome you, with open arms, to your own destruction. [The view of the screen gets narrower and narrower until all that can be seen is the Oneida County Title. As the last of the raindrops beat upon it, the screen turns black.]
