{USWP RP} "Cunning" Chris James

9 January 1999

Here was my roleplay for the new USWP! Enjoy Kate Come Visit the Home of "Cunning" Chris James & Kitti http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Gym/7142 ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ {It is a bright, sunny day out. We see seagulls flying overhead. All around, there are people with shorts on and short sleeve shirts, as well as some people that have no shirt on at all. All of the sudden, two figures come out from the side. The two figures, one of whom is a gorgeous blonde female with a half shirt and tight shorts and the other of whom is a very muscular built male with a robber's mask on, are two of the newest stars to enter the e-wrestling world. These two are known as "Cunning"
Chris James & Kitti. Kitti begins to speak to the camera.} Kitti: As you can plainly see, this is one of the most beautiful places in the world to be. I am talking about none other than Myrtle Beach, South
Carolina. This is the home of the next greatest superstar to come into e-wrestling. If you don't believe me, then let me do some "convincing" to all of you out there.
{Kitti then takes up her shirt and begins to lift it, exposing more of her breasts. As she is about to lift her shirt the whole way, Chris James takes her arms and pushes them down to stop her from lifting up her shirt.}
Kitti: It's obvious that Chris doesn't want me to convince any of you out there because he feels he can do it all by himself. But, with his recent failures in life, I'm not too sure about what he can and what he can't do. All I know is that soon, he has some sort of match coming up in the
USWP. {"Cunning" Chris James then nods his head as Kitti takes her hand and places it near his crotch. "Cunning" Chris James then takes Kitti's hand and kisses it. He looks around to see if anybody is watching him, as if he has something to hide. Then, as he notices nothing spectacular, "Cunning"
Chris James begins to speak.} "Cunning" Chris James: Don't you all just love Kitti to pieces? She is the best thing that can ever happen to a guy like me, I will tell you that from experience. But, I am not here to talk about Kitti all day long. I am here to talk about my match coming up in the USWP. It's one of my very first matches in my e-wrestling career and I get to face a guy by the name of Modern Day
Warrior. I have never heard of him and I am sure he has never really heard of me. But, as time goes along, he will not forgot me because I am the person who will the the one who gave him one hell of a match in the debut of the USWP. It appears I have someone that is a little bit more Modern
Day, so to speak. I don't have any of these older generation players to go against. I assume that is good. In a way, it's good to get a new start against some of the newer guys. However, how can I make myself a legend without even of having to face a legend? It just doesn't make sense. But,
I'm gonna try my best and give it my all. I'm gonna do it for the fans and most importantly, I am going to do this for myself.
Kitti: Chris, honey. Calm down. Don't start talking about being a legend when you haven't even of had your first match yet in your career! You know that stuff like that will eventually jinx you in the end, right?
"Cunning" Chris James: Kitti, once again, you point out the obvious, this is why I love you so much. {Chris & Kitti share a kiss} Kitti: That was nice, I think I'm gonna enjoy more of that later tonight. Chris, did you even hear the comments from the Modern Day Worry?
"Cunning" Chris James: No, why? Kitti: Well, I just happened to put it on audio casette tape and thought you should take a listen to what he was saying in his interview about you.
Here, borrow my walkman. {Kitti hands "Cunning" Chris James her walkman and then the tape of Modern
Day Warrior speaking. Chris puts the walkman on and then the tape in it.
He then presses play and listens to the comments, which won't be repeated since we already know what was said. After a few minutes, Chris stops the tape and then shakes his head in disbelief.}
"Cunning" Chris James: Normally, I'm not the type of person to respond to people's comments. I'm usually the one who will make the comments and then the challenges. I really don't care what others have to say, but this case may be differant. All I have to say is, huh? That just made no sense! I don't get it. You say I have been in this sport for a long time now? Funny, I just started in this sport. I'm a rookie! Don't you get it? You say I am very well known? Well, I'm glad to know that people know me so well considering
I have only been heard from once. But, that just proves that people listen to what I have to say and they listen to when I have to say it. I must admit, even though you are a Modern Day Warrior, you seem to be very out of date! Ok, I know I'm probably getting a little out of hand here, and this is usually not me. But now, I am home. I am back where I belong, in
South Carolina. I am happy to be here right on the Grandstrand. I don't want to talk about my hometown though, I just want to enjoy the sites and the smell of the ocean. I miss waking up in the morning and listeng to the waves crashing against the wall of rocks, it is something that I will always cherish. Modern Day Warrior, I wish you the best of luck in our match. I'm sure that if we both work hard enough, then we can put on a great show for the crowd and help out with the profits for the USWP. Just make sure you get your facts straight next time.
Kitti: Wait Chris, one more thing. This week, I will be at ringside with "Cunning" Chris James and I will be helping in any which way I can for his match. However, I don't want any of the guys out there to get any ideas. I will have a special treat for one lucky audience member next week though, and it will be a surprise that they will remember for the rest of their life! Chris, we gotta get going and do that autograph session.
"Cunning" Chris James: Oh, you mean the one of the mentally disabled children? Kitti, who got us this autograph session to do anyway? I don't mind it at all, but personally, I feel it would be better if we opened ourselves up to the public and let every single person be able to receive our autographs. I'm getting sick of making people pay to get our autographs, it just doesn't sit well with me. Hey, cam I finally take this mask off?
Kitti: Now Chris, don't let you're temper get to you. We all know that it's our agent's decision on who gets our autographs and we have no control over it. As for the mask, you have to keep it on. I don't want any of the women out there getting jealous of me because they see what type of a handsome man I am really engaged to. They probably already have seen enough with your body though. Well folkds, until next time.
{The scene then fades and we see "Cunning" Chris James & Kitti start to french kiss each other.}
