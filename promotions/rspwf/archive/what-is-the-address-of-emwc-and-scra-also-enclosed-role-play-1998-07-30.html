What is the address of EMWC and SCRA also enclosed role-play

30 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To RSPFW. I wish to join the EMWC or SCRA, what are their addresses, my wrestler is
Doomsday and is in IeWC, WOLF,CREW &HCW.
He is a great role-player also please post your comments to my mail address about the role-play which I enclose.
Anyway speak soon CROW Prez EMWO CO-Prez #NEW# IeWC VP [name withheld] Doomsday(111-17-0) -------------------------------------------------- ________ / ______ \ _______ ______ / / \ \ | _____ \ |_____\ / / ____\ \ | | \ \ || \\ / / / \\ \ | | \/ ||____// / / / \\ \ | |____// |_____/ /_____/ \___\ |______/ **The scene opens up inside the headquarters of the DAD, seen in the distance celebrating their victory last night are Mr. Menace and the DAD, Doomsday and Smoke. Mr. Menace with a champagne glass in his right hand raises it way into the air and together they all drink to their victory.**
**Mr. Menace then turns to Doomsday and Smoke and all together they put their champagne glasses down and begin to speak to the HFWA. Mr. Menace turns to the onlooking camera that he can now see before him, to show that his nice sparkling blue suit, black shoes and white derby hat begins to glisten in the view of the camera lens.**
**Mr. Menace then picks up his champagne glass raises it to the camera, the drops it on the floor and steps on it. Mr. Menace then begins to speak.** Mr. Menace: The clock has now struck, the dawning of the new horizon is soon to be complete, the era of Death and Destruction shall soon be bestowed upon you all. Now women, child or beat shall be safe to cross our paths again.
"Lionheart" Travis Smith, for you last night was just a taster of what the DAD are capable of, did you really think that Doomsday wouldn't show up to get a closer view of what his partner was doing to you. Well I would suggest that now you think real hard, is it worth having that happen all over again or should you just give it up now.
Travis Smith, you needed to be taught a lesson and now the lesson is nearly complete, come Sunday night at the Great Beyond three we shall truly see who is the greatest athlete in the HFWA. You say you will win, well last night HELL was created for you. The CELL though didn't cause you much pain, yet when Doomsday got involved that was all it took to dispose of you.
Travis Smith, I hope at end of all this rivalry, you will be able to forgive for we know and you know we respect you and you likewise with us. We do not ever want to break that respect and that is why come the
Great Beyond three, I will keep Smoke away from the ring. I will let the match be a fair one, yet if you decide to pull any stunts then be warned
I shall come down on you like a raging fire.
Therefore Travis Smith, it leads me to believe that you need to be taught those extra skills and Sunday Night, Great Beyond three, Doomsday shall teach you what is like to step into the ring with a legend, somebody who in a few years will be up their amongst the top athletes in the world of professional wrestling.
Travis Smith, we have less then 72 hours before the DAD claim their first HFWA Championship. The Unified World Championship, a title which whoever holds, that person earns the utmost respect possible. Yet we also know that that person is the best in the business and will be hounded day and night from challengers who want to be like Doomsday or you. Yet the time will come and your demise will be brought about.
**Mr. Menace then turns to Doomsday, who is wearing his clad red and black satanic outfit, Doomsday then signals to his waist, stares into the camera lens and then begins to speak.**
Doomsday: The time, the time, the time is short, in less then 72 hours the HFWA are going to be presented with the most demonic, evil creature that has ever been placed on this earth. The reign of DOOM shall be here and "Lionheart" Travis Smith, you shall be the first to feel my almighty anger. The wrath of judgement, I shall bestow upon you.
The crime of betrayal is a serious one and one which no man can live from, therefore, Travis Smith, you have betrayed me, betrayed the DAD and for that reason, you shall pay dearly. The fires which enrage my soul shall be shown, the HFWA Unified World Championship is the title which belongs to me and at the Great Beyond 3, phase one of the DAD’s plan of evil shall be complete. HFWA have you ever realised why there is nobody here who dares to accept any challenges which I make.
The reason being for everybody here, even Travis Smith knows what I am capable f doing, what I did when I went through 3 men in one night to earn this chance was nothing compared to what I shall do to you Travis
Smith. You disgrace me and I would advise you to get some extra help.
No I won’t let the DAD interfere but here are plenty of other people out there who want their chance at getting you, namely Chris Martin, the man who Smoke defied when he took his soul. Yet Travis Smith at the Hell In
The Cell, you were spared from that punishment, yet now I shall have to drive the demons out of you and there shall be no escape. The end is nigh and the time ticks down, there shall be No Way Out.
The burning sulphur of the place where you shall die will consume your entire fleshly body and nobody will be able to spare you for the soul shall die and the spirit will live on. When it comes to Sunday night, the winner shall be whoever possesses the better skills and who has been training more. I may talk the talk, yet can I walk the walk?
Well Travis Smith, soon you just like all the rest shall find out why
Doomsday and the DAD are in the HFWA. Not only will we have the Unified
World Championship, oh no, Smoke shall be the new Intercontinental
Champion and the DAD will regain the titles which were stolen from them. The DAD is going to set this federation in a blazing ball of fire and it will never stop until we are dead and buried.
**Doomsday the turns to Smoke who with his black garments then begins to speak into the camera.** Smoke: Some people will say that last night was a travesty of justice,
"Lionheart" Travis Smith had me in the Liontamer and I was going out.
Yet Smoke found new life and was born again. Smoke was born of the spirit and shall die likewise. Travis Smith, I know that Doomsday was a major part in our match, so I say this to you.
After the Great Beyond 3 is gone, I will give you a no interference, no rules, falls count anywhere 2 out of 3 falls match and then we shall determine who is the better athlete for I know that you gave it your all last night and you deserved to win and that is why I will grant you this rematch. Accept If you dare?
Now at the Great Beyond 3, Chris Martin, the "Crusher" will have to have the toughest challenge of his entire wresting career. Martin the first time we met, your soul was taken, yet now you want a real match, well it has been signed and will take place.
Chris Martin, beware for whatever you have, it just will not be enough to keep the DAD back. The DAD are he new millennium, the DAD will represent the HFWA and strike fear into all your hearts, souls and minds and worst of all, the DAD will destroy all those who oppose them with their awesome power.
So Chris Martin beware for once more your Soul shall be crushed and then if you think you are going to be able to handle us in a Tag Team Match, then I say let’s get the Tag Team Tournament underway for you all know who just like last time is going to come out on top. Eak, you are no match for us, just like you were no match for the United Forces.
Finally their is a title which I shall soon possess, that being the HFWA
Intercontinental and Barry Atmos or Air Lucha, you just can’t handle the power of the DAD.
**Smoke then turns back to Mr. Menace, the DAD then look into the camera and as a breath is blown, the scene fades to black and the DAD are no more.**  -----== Posted via Deja News, The Leader in Internet Discussion ==----- http://www.dejanews.com/rg_mkgrp.xp Create Your Own Free Member Forum
