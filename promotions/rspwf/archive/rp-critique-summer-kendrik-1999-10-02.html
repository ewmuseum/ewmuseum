RP Critique: Summer Kendrik

2 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera's yet to fade up from the long-lasting blackness...] [What we hear is the rumbling of a running engine. Loud and clear the sound is, piercing even this deep darkness and hitting us in the back of our minds.
In a way, we can feel the vibration. Up and down, side to side... heavy movements shortly follow. Then we realize... we really are moving...]
[... We really are in a car.] [Fade up.] [Where we are, is in the front seat of a car. As expected, we're moving, and the road's wide open. From the current angle, it's obvious that it's a fancy car -- yellow in color, and fast in speed. A shift to the right shows the flatlands of grass and wheat, while straight ahead, we can see for miles and miles. We hold this for a few seconds, observing every inch of the 'scape.
Deafening silence. The only sounds heard are those of the dips and bumps in the road, then the clanking of the car. Then, we shift slowly to the left.]
[And to the left, there sits a man -- expectedly. Long, flowing golden blond hair graces this man, dangling down unprotected and care-free. A pair of black sunglasses rests comfortably on the bridge of his nose. A smirk curled on both sides of his mouth. Not much else needs to be said about this man. He's cocky. And with no remorse.]
"Nice to see that you finally got enough balls to look this way." [The camera doesn't move an inch now. It stays in place on this unknown man.] "Here I am. Three-hundred, fifty miles away from my destination and a whole new environment in front of me. No more desert roads, no more hundred degree weather every morning at eight... an unreturnable bliss, to say the least. And
I'm sure as hell not going to turn back. Not much back there to miss."
[A sigh and a laugh now by the cocky man. Both low and arrogant.] "Let me introduce myself. The name's Summer Kendrik. Many of you probably haven't heard much of me, and I'd like to change that without much effort. No disrespect intended [snort]. I come from Texas. The land of Lone Star
Wrestling. A big-time promotion that just doesn't cut it for me anymore, and the stars of the show are the barkeepers in the slums. So this all might bring up the question; 'What's a man of your skill coming to AWMC?' Well. the answer's simple. I want bigger, and I want better.
So I hear that the Motor City has some great talent. My agent calls me up, tells me about the stars and studs up in Detroit, and says that I'd fit in nicely. Didn't surprise me a bit, however. There's not a place in the sky that 'The Most Agile Man in All the Land' doesn't fit in."
[A short pause takes over the thoughts. The wind continues to fly directly into the face of Kendrik. The convertible top remains down to his liking and the car engine still trucking at a steady eighty miles per hour.]
"So I say to him, 'Sure. I'll go down.' And obviously, that's all it took. Six and a half hours later, I'm on my way to Detroit and talking to you in this car -- Don't you like it? And in Detroit, I imagine it'll be much of the same things that happened in Texas. I get a match -- I win a match. The song remains the same wherever I may be. It's a rule of life, I suppose..."
[Pause. Feel the drama.] "... And one that can't change." [Abruptly, the sunglasses of Summer slide off of his face, and latch to the sun visor above Kendrik's head. Amazing how the camera can stay focused and watch three things at the same time. Purposely or not, Kendrik's giving this camera man a hard time.]
"At the present time, there isn't much I can say that sounds crazy and unreasonable. And there isn't a solitary thing I can say that you all wouldn't dispute. But I will say this... I have talent that you've never seen before.
Talent that you've only dreamed of possessing. And talent that I know how to use. Combine them and you have a daredevil that can run all over you. Front and back.
And it doesn't look pretty. [Grin] Trust me." [And then, the fans of the AWMC get a slimpse of the one thing that gets under any mans skin. The classic Kendrik smirk. From ear to ear it stretches.
Sending the watcher into a frenzy of anger and agression.] "So as I have many more miles to travel until I reach the Motor City of Detroit, you have one common thing to consider. How are you going to stop the high-flying assault of 'Lord Agileness?' And as you think hard and deep about it, you'll soon realize that no matter how hard you try? You just can't stop it. You can't win. And when you realize this, you'll crumble at my feet. Sad story, I must add.
But you see, I'm not perfect... Not at all. I'm better than that... [Pause] I'm just that damn good." [We fade to black and the same engine noise as before. But before the end of the feed, Kendrik's heard one last time...]
"You have a few days AWMC. Find some talent deep inside... Then, I'll give you a free lesson. I'll show you how to use it."
[End.] -John T. Handler of: -- Summer Kendrik [AWMC] 0-0-0 -- Tex Robinson [Free-Agent] 0-0-0 Former National Wrestling Council Tag Team Champions. National Wrestling Council J-Crown Title Contender x2
