[critique] "The Fallen Angel" Azrael

21 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Decided to give this character a go. Not going to be too long, this RP is kinda a character development thing, or intro if you will. Give me your opinions, suggestions, and whatever comments you want to throw in. Thanks...
[Begin Transmition] [The visual fades in turning in a three-hundred and sixty degree motion. What is seen is a room dimly lit by candle-light. The walls of the room are wood, cabin like.
Water seeping through the cracks in the wood. As the camera slowly comes to a stop, an object is seen. A small piece of furniture, apearing to be some sort of couch. It is covered by a tattered white bed sheet. Next to the small couch sits a man holding a candle up to his face.]
[The camera zooms in showing the man, his face, scarred, emotionless. His eyes are open wide, appearing to be teary. He opens his mouth, and he lets out a sigh.
The camera pans back showing him reaching out, petting the sheet covering the couch] [the man speaks, as if speaking to someone] MAN: Has it been so long? I remember the days of old, when I ran around this very couch, hiding away from my brother. [shrugs] Brother...please forgive me.
[The man tosses the candle onto the white sheet, setting it a blaze. He stands firm. The camera turns to the other side of the couch, showing the man in his entirety, shown through the fire]
MAN: The last piece of a past that strikes fear into my soul. No more memories of those times. No more chances to be weakened by the sadness of the past.
The shackles of love, compasion, pain...all burn in that fire. [The man, know seen in the light, is known as Azrael, The Fallen Angel. Azrael looks to the camera and speaks]
Azrael: You know me not, this time is the first time that you have seen me. The only thing I say to you now is, do not be quick to judge me. Be patient, and listen. For if you listen, then, and only then will you truly be able to see me for who
I truly am. I am Azrael, a man, no more no less. What you must know about me is that I am a man who plays no games. I will not try to blind your mind in order for me to beat you. I will beat you with my skill, and abilities, which include my mind.
[Azrael kneels down, and he closes his eyes] Azrael: The Fallen Angel [pauses] A name given to me by my fellow soldiers in the Gulf War. A name given to me because in action, I strike with a force that can only be described as "un-holy." A name given to me because of the brutality I used when fighting against any oposing force. Should any of you worry for your lives? No, for I do not kill without reason. I am not a murderer, but, you would be surprised of what you can live through. I know that first hand.
[Azrael stands to his feet, and he speaks, as if speaking to someone] Azrael: Do they not know what force stands in their view? Will their ignorance blind them from seing that I am more than meets the eye? I hope not, but they will be the ones who decide that, not me. For they will choose their paths. Wether it be the road of wisdom...or the road of destruction. The only thing I can do now, is watch. View my opposition. Also listen. Hear the voice of strength, and the voice of weakness.
[The camera slowly fades out, showing Azraels' face through the still burning flame.]
