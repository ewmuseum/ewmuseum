{RP Critique} Hannibal Carver - Flames and Criticism Are Welcome

25 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::The scene opens outside a large, rust-colored building. As the camera pans, it is revealed the building is located on Mass Avenue, in Boston,
Massachusetts. There is no sign to let anyone know the name of this business, and for good reason. The only way you would have any idea of the purpose of this building is if you knew it's owner, and it's owner is a little less than friendly. Unread newspapers pile up to the left and right of the main door, yellowed with time and neglect. Flyers for local concerts, flyers loudly proclaiming "WORK FROM HOME!", business cards guaranteeing quick weight loss, and bumper stickers asking that you vote for "Quinn" are plastered all over the wall around the main door of the building. Clearly the caretaker of this establishment is quite lax when it comes to the area of housekeeping.
The view of the main door gets closer and closer, as the cameraman approaches the building. The cameraman gets so close to the door you can read the rings on the wood to see how old the tree which gave its life for the door, and begins to knock. His hand is visibly shaking, no doubt he's spooked by the "ghost town" atmosphere of the building. The door opens, revealing a stocky man in a black kimono, with more than just a little stubble growing on his chin. For those fans at home who have been paying attention, it is quite clearly recent
Maple Championship Wrestling signee Hannibal Carver. He looks thoroughly unimpressed by the MCW camera crew. He mutters something that sunds like
"Christ, it's the canucks.", and gestures for the cameraman enters, followed by his crew. As they enter, the interior of the building is revealed to be a training facility of some sort, complete with speed bags, heavy bags, weight sets, and a wrestling ring. As the boom microphones and the camera are set in place, Hannibal Carver slides into the ring. He hooks his arms around the top rope and leans back on the ropes, and then releases. He repeats this action until he sees that the microphones and camera are in place. He walks over to the side of the ring facing the camera crew, and begins to speak as the camera zooms in.::
So, soon I'll be travelling yet again. I wasn't a good boy, wasn't willing to take one for the team in the good old U.S. of A, so I go to where wrestling truly lives, Japan. I was never asked to "put over" the boss's son, never made to wear some ridiculous costume, never forced to proclaim to an arena filled with drooling rednecks that I was "in the hizouse". Yet, I find myself back here, where it all began. Boston, Massachusetts...or as many of the more realistic residents call it, Massive Two Shits. Why? The almighty dollar. Why else? What you see around me, is my home. Some of the things I've done in my wrestling career were seen as disgusting, degrading, and shameful to my parents. In their eyes, they have no son. So, all that remains is the facility you see here. The Grinder. My good friend Weathers allowed The Grinder to slip in his absence, causing it to fall into the hands of ecstacy-addicted morons who converted it into a disco, or "rave". Disgusting. Thanks to me further degrading myself by performing in front of my fellow countrymen who ashame me so, The Grinder is restored to it's former glory. Apparently self-degradation is a good business move. But, alas, it is not a good sanity move, or a good
"Stop myself from going into Burger King with an Uzi" move. So, you maple syrup-gulping peasants up north get the pleasure of seeing Carver go to work.
Unfortunately, my line of work is rending the limbs of your disposable heroes totally useless. The question keeps running through my mind. Not the question all you might think, though. I am not wondering who my first opponent will be, or if I can work a good "program" with him. No, the question I have to ask is how much of the roster of MCW is made up of the unwanted children of that legendary whoremaster, Stu Hart?
::Suddenly, a small black phone locvated on the floor several feet away from the ring begins to ring.::
Oh, please excuse me for a moment. Unless you have more pressing matters you must attend to? Perhaps Farmer Stewey of Ottowa's prize moose is giving birth?
::Carver steps through the ropes and jumps down on to the floor from the ring apron with a smirk on his face. He flashes the camera crew a look of disgust as he walks past them. He walks over to the phone, and bends down to pick it up off the receiver. After saying hello, he seems genuinly pleased to hear from whoever is on the other line.::
That's right, Canada. Well, the Harts and Storm had some semblance of skill, so it might not be as bad as working main events in high school gymnasiums. It looks like they might have some guys with game, as a matter of fact. Who knows, has to be better than that glorified backyard fed in Washington. Yeah, that pecan pie was good, but that was about it. Well, it still remains to be seen if they make the grade. What else do you have to lose though? It's not as if you're sitting pretty now as it stands. If nothing else, we can always use some target practice, maybe they'll be nothing more than a good workout, but at least you'd get paid for it. Yeah, they're here right now. Yeah, I was half expecting the top of their heads to flap up and down when they spoke. Yeah, you know what they say, no rest for the wicked. Yes, you should, definitely. You still have the key, right? Excellent, I'll see you then. Have a good trip.
::Carver hangs the phone up on it's receiver. As he turns back to face the camera, he has a rather malicious smile spreading like the black plague on his face. The camera zooms in again as he begins to speak.::
Who was that? Vilification. Retribution. A bomb will soon drop on Maple Championship Wrestling. The aftermath will be ghastly, for everyone it affects. The roster, save for a select few, will soon have an amazing physical similarity to the victims of Hiroshima. I've seen no tag teams step up thus far. Keep it that way. Think of your familes, of your wives, of your children.
By not showing up for work, you shall save them years of grieving, years of unbearable sadness. By not stepping up to the challenge, the lives you save may be your own. You should think of them, for I will not spare a moment of my time on their pathetic lives. Why should I be the only one without as father? If anything, by punching your ticket once and for all I'm being kind. I can drive by my father's house right now, I might even catch him raking the leaves, perhaps erecting a scarecrow in the front yard in preparation of Thanksgiving.
They will be spared such an insult. They will be safe in the knowledge that their father is gone forever, that if they wish to see them, they'll need a shovel and a flashlight. Rotting bones and remnant stripes of flesh are famore preferable than a mocking, living father. No need to thank me though, I'm just doing my job. Speaking of doing my job, it is time to prepare for the move.
I'll have to break out the winter wardrobe, I suppose the frozen tundra of
Cana-Duh is a bit too chilly for the comfort of a silk kimono. Training must increase, and other preparations, which are none of your business quite frankly, must be made. In private. So, in case you hockey hicks need it spelled out for you. Get the hell out of my home.
::Carver turns his back to the camera,and walks off in the direction of a spiral rod-iron staircase at the rear of The Grinder. As he makes his way up the steps, the scene fades to black.::
