[UWF Roleplay] "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao [Era of Defiance pt.2]

9 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
---------- [Scene: A tranquil Friday morning, inside the hectic Georgia Dome Arena, in
Atlanta Georgia. Many bodies of both Male and Female gender are scurrying about inside the Georgia Dome, trying to prepare and gear up for this Saturday
Night's, 'Rampage.' Many ring-crew associates, light directors, and stage managers are within crowd, as only a few wrestlers are noticed walking about.
The scene pans from backstage, to the empty and vacant seats which surround the
UWF ring. High above the highly illuminated and tense atmosphere which is given off by the crew preparing the UWF ring, sits a strangely attired and awkward figure in a butterfly legged manner. The man sits high within the rafters of the Georgia Dome Arena, quietly and in a meditating manner.]
M: "Dear father, please, hear my calls for your knowledge. You are the one man who can help me through my inquisitive point in time. I have defeated my first opposition I was faced with. Though, am yet to pave a solid road into a home which I desire in reflecting the morals of spirituality within."
"Father, I am not strong enough as one man alone to bring an entire realm of such power, into redemption. Though, there is a man who I trust in, and a man who I believe can strengthen my conquest. Though, this man does not see fit to ally with such a novice, as myself."
[The camera pans upwards towards the seated figure high within the rafters, to get a discerning view of the man. The camera reaches full zooming capabilities as just then, we realize the mysterious figure is none other than the UWF's newest, "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao. He is attired with no upper body apparel, but with his customary green spandex wrestling pants, with the trademark Dueling dragons running down both pant legs.]
JT: "The 'Era of Defiance' Gabriel Whitecross has not yet answered my calls for a bonding between both of our compatible realms. Yet, you see father, this man understands what I believe in and uncannily seems to know why I'm here. Where I come from, and who sent me."
"You father, you're the one who sent me. And yes, Mr. Whitecross claims to be familiar and acquainted with you. What does this all mean? Father, my questions lie within your hands and only your hands. And yes father, I shall attend tomorrow evenings Saturday Night Rampage, and I 'shall' heed Mr. Whitecross'
title defense." [The facial expressions of Jin Tao are hidden behind his mask, though the tilting of his head and body expressions he makes, notes his seriousness.]
"I shall receive my answers, as well as heeding my right for being here, within the UWF. I shall bring peace and protection to the realm of the Universal
Wrestling Federation. Though, time is of the essence and the future is what holds the truth. Mr. Whitecross. Remembers these words, for they will be the last until we meet again.. "
[The camera fades from the rafters where Jin Tao sits upon, to a glimpse of an
"Era of Defiance" logo. Just as the logo appears on the screen, the screen fades to black slowly.]
