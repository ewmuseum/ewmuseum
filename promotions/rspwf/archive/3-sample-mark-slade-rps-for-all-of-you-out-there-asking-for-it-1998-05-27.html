#3 Sample Mark Slade RPs for all of you out there asking for it

27 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In the desert I saw a creature, naked, bestial, Who, squatting upon the ground, Held his heart in his hands, And ate of it. I said "Is it good friend?" "It is bitter-bitter," he answered; "But I like it Because it is bitter And because it is my heart." -Stephen Crane [The scene opens on the sandy plains of a Texan desert. The golden sand stretches out for miles as the sparse terrain is dotted by a few plants and rocks. A lone figure stands out from the bleak surroundings. The man walks across the sands slowly with a slight stagger. He is a man in his thirties with silver colored hair. A stubble of beard growth covers his face. His icy blue eyes are clear and bright as he stumbles with the effort of walking. His black jeans and shirt are covered with sand, dust and persperation. His skin has a deep tan, but some signs of sunburn can be seen. He walks on determined, but exposure to the elements, heat and lack of water have taken their toll as he stumbles and falls to his knees exhausted. He lays prone for a few minutes staring down at the ground]
Mark: [whispering] Won't give up. I'll show you all. [He tries to stand up, but cannot. After a few moments he starts to crawl. He continues his progress crawling in the sand. Eventually he slows down and falls to his stomach. He lies on the ground unable to move anymore]
Mark: So this is how it ends. I never would have thought that this is how I would go out. [As Mark lies in the sand he stares off at the horizon. Something catches his eye, but he can't make it out at the distance. Slowly the object gets closer. It appears to be a person walking towards him]
Mark: [mumbling] Who is this fool? What's doing wandering around out here? Doesn't he know how dangerous it is. He's going to get himself killed.
[Mark laughs to himself after realizing his own joke. As he laughs the figure gets closer. Know he can make it out. It's a man in faded blue jeans and a jeans jacket. The man's head is down and he is wearing a battered hat which covers his face. A voice in the back of Mark's head tells him their is something strange, but he can't figure it what it is. The man walks up to Mark and stands over him. He reaches up and slides his hat back on his head and looks down at Mark on the ground. As Mark looks up he sees the man's face. His face is worn and aged with several wrinkles. Mark thinks to himself that the man looks almost like Clint Eastwood. At this moment Mark realizes what was bothering him, when the man was walking their was no dust coming up from his feet. Mark looks down at his worn leather boots and sees that the man has left no footsteps]
Stranger: Looks like you've really gotten yourself in trouble now Mark. Mark: How do you know my name? [The stranger smiles a small mysterious grin] Stranger: Does it really matter? Mark: Not really. Who are you? The Devil? [The stranger throws back his head and laugh] Stranger: That's a good one. [He thinks to himself a second] Maybe I am the Devil, here to take you to hell. Or maybe i'm a figment of your imagination brought on by your fevered mind. Or maybe i'm just a man out for a walk in the desert who happened to stumble onto you.
Mark: It figures. I can't just die peacefully, I get to play twenty questions with the riddler.
Stranger: Your not dying...yet. You've still got a little life in you. The question is do you want it? Mark: Buddy, all I want right now is something to drink. Stranger: How about some Jim Bean. Oh I forgot your a Jack Daniels man. Here you go. [The stranger pulls a bottle of Jack Daniels out of his jacket pocket and drops it in the sand in front of Mark. Mark looks at it for a few long seconds and looks back up at the man]
Mark: No thanks. You got any water in there? [The stranger laughs again] Stranger: See you haven't given up yet, but I think your close. You want that drink worse than you ever have before. I can see it.
Mark: Go fu#@ yourself Stranger: [smiling] That's generically impossible. You do have a sense of humor though. I like that. Aren't you scared?
Mark: Why should I be. When you know your going to die soon, you really stop caring about things like fear.
Stranger: You stopped caring a long time ago didn't you Mark. Did you care when you killed those people in that car accident?
[Mark stares up at the man in disbelief] Mark: Who the hell are you? Stranger: Well get up and i'll show you. As a matter of fact i'll show you who you are too.
Mark: You think i'm laying here because it's comfortable? I can't walk anymore. Stranger: Quit being so dramatic. Get up. [The strange reaches down and pulls on Mark's arm. Mark struggles to stand up and does it easily. The stranger starts walking and Mark starts to follow him]
Mark: What the hell? Am I already dead? Stranger: [shaking his head] Would you get over this dead thing already. You sound like an old woman. I thought you were the main man. The meanest thing walking the earth.
Mark: I am...but thins have changed lately. Stranger: Oh no. If this is where you start into your little pitty routine then save it. Mark: Did Keith Kelly drop you off out here? Stranger: [chuckling] I guess i'm going to have to show you in order to make you understand. Man, are you hard headed and stubborn. I like that though.
[They walk for a minute in silence. Mark keeps looking down at the stranger's feet and it almost looks like the man is walking on air.
Mark is about to comment on it when the stranger stops. When Mark looks up he sees a small pool of water in the sand]
Stranger: You know i've been watching you for a long time. When you were a little younger you used to be a bad mo-fo.
Mark: Still am. Stranger: Nahh, not anymore. Now your like a snivelling little girl. [The stranger points at the water and Mark looks at it. As he watches the water ripple he starts to see shapes in it. A picture forms of Mark back at the bar. For the first time sober Mark gets a good look at himself in the bar. He notices that he had put on a few pounds and was dirty and unkempt]
Stranger: You see this is you now. Sure you've lost a few pounds wandering around out here, but that is still you. Broken. You gave up on wrestling. You gave up on your friends and you gave up on life. The question is why.
[Mark starts to say something, but is cut off] Stranger: Let me guess what your going to say. [The stranger points at the water again and in it Mark sees a scene which is all to familiar to him. He sees the car he was driving that night many years ago when he smashed into a woman and her daughter in their car.]
Mark: Screw you. You don't know what it's like. Knowing that you were responsible for something like that. I can't get it out of my mind.
It's like a cancer that was just eating away at me. Stranger: [yawns] Well here's a little question. If it was so bad then why did it take all this time to break you down?
[Mark opens his mouth to speak and then closes it slowly] Stranger: You know what I think? I think you just used that as an excuse. Look here. [Once again the water shimmers and the picture changes. Now we see an image of Mark Slade standing in the ring with his partners Keith and
Terry. The scene changes and we see Mark with the ICCW world title held high.]
Stranger: You see. You went on to do some big things in wrestling, but you wait until now to quit. Why? What else happened to you? Let's see, you were picked up by the NeCW after the ICCW folded.
Mark: [angrily] Those punks. They knew that the federation was going under and they still gave me a contract. I wrestled in some house shows for them and was getting ready to really make some history. I figured that finally I was going to get my shot at some of the punks who really pissed me off. Jinsei Shakanuzi, Jim Wildcat Lewis Jr, Kain and the rest of the "all-star" squad. They tried to screw me over from day one. They knew I wasn't there to play their games. They figured that since they were there for a while that they owned the fed. Getting special favors from the staff and things like that.
And out of all of them that punk Chris Hopper was the worst. All I wanted was a shot at that fool. To show the whole world that he's nothing but hype. He dodged me and...
Stranger: Yeah, i've heard this before, but you know what. The old Mark
Slade wouldn't have run off like a dog with it's tail between his legs.
He wouldn't have run half way across the country to hide in a bar drinking himself to death. He would have went after those punks. He would have hunted down each one of them to wherever they went and beat them down. You didn't though. You quit on yourself.
Mark: Bull. There were other things going on. I had money trouble. I couldn't get picked up by any federations. I couldn't do anything. It wasn't my fault.
Stranger [rubs his chin thoughtfully] You keep saying that. If it wasn't your fault then who's was it?
[Mark doesn't say anything. He just stands there staring at the stranger with his fists clenched]
Stranger: You know there's one thing you haven't brought up. The real reason why you gave up. Because you know that you couldn't handle it.
For the first time here was something that you couldn't fight. Something that made you realize that you are only human. Something that you knew could beat you.
[The strange points at the water. Mark doesn't look for a few seconds. And when he does he knows what is there before he sees it. The picture is a man in a hospital bed. He is hooked up to life support machine and apparently unconcious. His body is thin and several sores cover it. He appears to be old, but a look at his face shows that he is a younger man aged before his time. Mark looks at the image silently. His throat constricts and he swallows trying to loosen it up. He slowly turns to the stranger]
Mark: You bastard. Stranger: [smiling] I think we've got something here. Your old friend
Terry. Look at him. [shakes his head slowly] You see this is what happens in the later stages of a full blown case of Aids. That is a man who is dying. Is that what did you in? When you were sitting at home that night and got the phone call about him. What did they tell you? That he didn't have much time left and that all he wanted was to see you and Keith again. You couldn't go though. Keith went, but you skipped out. Your oldest friend and you left him hanging.
[Mark turns to face the stranger] Mark: I couldn't do it. I couldn't go see him like that. That...that thing isn't Terry. That's what is left of him. That's no way to live, hooked up to a machine. Hell, he can't even go to the bathroom on his own anymore. That's no way to live. I couldn't see him like that. No way.
Stranger: So you jumped in your car and took off. You picked up a little something to drink and turned your back on the world. You realized that no matter how tough you are that there was something out there that you couldn't fight. Something that you couldn't protect your friends from.
Mark: Yeah....I did. I mean at first I just didn't believe it. I didn't think about it. It was the only way to handle it. We kept in touch and Terry was doing all right. After a while though we fell out of touch. And then one day I got the phone call. After the call I just sat there thinking about him. Picturing what he must look like.
I couldn't take it. Stranger: And for the first time in your life you gave up. You ran off. Mark: Yes. Stranger: [Shaking his head] So here we are out in the middle of nowhere. Why did you come out here to the desert anyway?
Mark: I don't know. I thought I came out here to die, but... Stranger: [eyes widening he leans forward a little] But what? Mark: But i'm not. I'm not ready to go yet. I've still got things to do. Stranger: [smiles] This is a big change from the thing I found laying face down in the dirt. You sure of that Mark?
Mark: [Turns and looks back at the water] Yeah i'm sure of it. I... [Mark stops as he notices the water fade away. There is nothing, but sand on the ground now. He spins around to look at the stranger, but he is also gone. Mark looks around for a second and then stops. He looks up at the sun trying to get his bearings and then starts walking off]
