[Free Agent] press conference

22 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi! I'm looking for a fed and I'm not asking for too much. I would prefer an
ORIGINALs only fed that sends the roleplays out via email so I don't have to check out the homepage every time to find out if new rps are out.
What do I have to offer? I normally are an active roleplayer and I also would like to join a cruiserweight devision if there is need. So mail me at
[detail withheld] Below is a short press-conference of my character so you cab get an slight idea of what he is all about.
later Markus -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ The scene opens inside a press-conference room. The room is filled with journalists, reporters and cameramen. It is very noisy and one is hardly able to understand his own words. In fornt of the press is a podium with tables to the right and left of it. The camera zooms in on the tables. There are water and juice bottles, glasses and name plates on the tables. The camera focusses on the name plate directly to the right of the podium. It reads: "Patrick Burton". The scene abruptly cuts to a hallway of a hotel. The camera slowly follows the hallway down until to a door with two huge bodyguards in front of it. They are all dressed up in black suits with a white shirt and also wear the obligatory sunglasses. Suddenly the door opens and two more bodyguards appear, they are followed by three other men. The camera focusses on the last man first. He is tall, approximately 6'5", in his 50s, but still in shape and has long grey hair which is tied back in a tail. He is wearing cowboy boots, blue jeans and a black leather jacket. All in all he looks like a former wrestler. The next man the camera focusses on is about 6 foot, in his 40s, and definetely not a wrestler. He wears an expensive light grey suit, glasses and has well-groomed brown hair. Last but not least the camera shows the last of the three. He is 6 foot, in his 20s and similar build to the likes of Chris Benoit or Eddie Guerrero. He has wild, short dyed black hair and wears a black suit along with a white shirt and a tie. The camera zooms in on the right side of his neck.
Right above where the collar of his shirt ends, can parts of a tattoo be seen. The camera follows the group to the elevator. Let's listen to some of their talk. ]
Well-groomed man:...( he is a little nervous )...Didn't I tell you to bring a suit? Grey-haired man: Yeah. [ He is all clam and cool, chewing his gum, as if nothing on this world could bother him. ]
Well-groomed man: How can you be that calm!? It's a very important day today! Everything's got to be perfect!
Grey-haired man: Calm down. It's not that big of a dea.... Well-groomed man: Calm down!! This day will decide over the future of my man's future!!! Grey-haired man: Remember? I train him. He's good and I know it best,
'cause I've ben in that ring all my life long since I've ben a little kid. I trained him like my son. He is exellent and he has proven it already.
Well-groomed man: But this is the tough world of e-wrestling!.... [ They get on the elevator. ] Well-groomed man: ...Floor - 3.... [ One of the bodyguards pushes the button and the elevator doors close. ] Well-groomed man:...There are no presents to be given away! We need to take our chance..NOW!
Wrestler: I just gonna show them what I'm all about. They can't close their eyes to the painful truth. Patrick Burton has arrived and is taking over.
Well-groomed man: Just try to behave. No cussing, no rants about governmental oppression, and absolutely no comments about journalists who are tools of....
Burton: I'm not going to be hyprocitical. [ They exit the elevator and walk towards the press-conference room. ] Well-groomed man: Just don't start talking about it. Don't forget. This could be your big break!
Burton: But if somebody asks the wrong questions...I'm not guarantee for anything... [ They enter the press-conference room. Flashlights flare. Burton and his trainer take a seat while the well-groomed man stands behind the podium. ]
Well-groomed man: Ladies and gentlemen....Please...Could I have your attention, please....Thank you....okay
Ladies and gentlemen, I'm more than just glad to be able to present to you tonight one of the greatest young wrestlers who, without a shadow of a doubt, has a long and successful career ahead.
Mr Patrick Burton will answer to all of your questions in a moment. For those of you have would like to ask some business questions, can ask me now.
Reporter #1: What are your demands in regards of payment and working days? Well-groomed man: I can't, as you can probably understand, give exact numbers. We will evaluate every offer and then decide. As for the working days. Mr Burton is known for his high working morality and his close contact to his fans.
Reporter #2: You were just talking about the working days. I mean, Mr
Burton is involved in some other promotions already. Does he have time for another deal?
Well-groomed man: Well, definetely! Please, don't forget that numerous of his agreements are just for the big shows and that he isn't wrestling on a regular basis for all of these smaller organizations.
If there aren't anymore questions I would suggest that we go on with Mr
Patrick Burton himself.
[ The flashlights flare again as Burton gets up and walks to the podium. ] Burton: Well, what do you wanna know. Reporter #3: What are your aims? Do you want to go for Heavyweight title or some other belt?
Burton: Right now, I probably would rather fight it out for a Cruiserweight Championship, with 220lbs I still fit in most of the devisions around. Who knows, I got the abilities to win any title I want. But title reigns are not that important to me.
Reporter #1: You extensively toured in Japan and Mexico over the last two years. What did you learn being on tour in those countrys?
Burton: I improved my wrestling a lot in Japan as well as in Mexico which makes me just more dangerous. On a personal level. I learned a lot about foreign cultures and became even more tolerant.
Reporter #4: To get back to what is important to you. How would you define this? Burton: Good question...I guess, going out there every night and giving my utmost to please the fans who pay their hard earned money to see me..
Reporter #5: How do you think about the current directions wrestling is going? Burton: You know, I think it is pretty rediculous to see all of these wanna-bes out there pretending to be something. All that trash talking and put on attitude is nothing but a joke. They try to be somebody, but they are nobody. They change their opinion and attitude as often as the wind changes his direction. I really don't have any respect at all for all those want-to-be-bad-asses. The money is all they are caring about, it is not the sport or the fans as it should be.
Reporter #6: I'm going to talk real long, but Burton...I think you are the JOKE here! I mean, com'on! Who cares about this stuff anyways?
Besides, your wrestling abilities are as high as the heels of my wife's shoe...and I don't even have a wife!
[ Burton is getting a pissed look on his face. He is about to freak out, but still trying to constrain himself. His manager is already getting nervous again. ]
Burton: Let me tell you something! I am sick and tired of guys like you! Does every brainless punk get a job as a journalist in this country! It is guys like you that rip off the kids and the fans who buy all these over-priced magazines of no value or taste but full of lies. It is people like you...
Manager: Patrick, please, remember.... [ But Burton doesn't hear him. ] Reporter #6: Do you want to threaten me? You don't have the guts! I'm going to sue you! Burton: Fine! You want to sue me!? I gonna give you something to sue me for!!! [ Burton leaps over the table and rushes towards the reporter who quickly jumps up and runs through the exit.]
Manager: NO! No, stop! Ahem...I'll be right back...don't leave.....BURTON!!! [ fade to black ]
