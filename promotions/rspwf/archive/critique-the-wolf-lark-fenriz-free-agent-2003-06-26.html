[CRITIQUE] "The Wolf" Lark Fenriz (FREE AGENT)

26 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
THE BEGINNING IS ALWAYS THE MOST DIFFICULT PART I plan to start off on the right foot, what about you? PART ONE OF ONE -- "Heaven and Hell Collide Part Deux" [A sterling silver Norse wolf satanic cross lies flat against black cloth, most likely apart of a generic Hanes tee-shirt. The necklace is all that can be seen for a few seconds, but the camera quickly zooms out, revealing the creature who is wearing the necklace.]
[It's Lark Fenriz.] [He is sitting in his throne, a throne made out of welded together metal Satanic crosses. Wherever the throne sits, the room must be spacious due to the fact that no walls can be seen all around, its all pitch black. There is a glimmer of though light thrown by the two black candles burning on either side of Lark.. He is wearing a plain black tee-shirt, and black dress slacks. The shirt is even tucked into his pants. Finger less gloves, with four inch spikes on each knuckle, cover his hands, while a double row alternating five inch to three spikes cling to his neck. He has recently shaved his head, and facial hair completely off. In fact, he even decided to take his eyebrows with it. Lark shoots an evil glare towards the camera, resentment deep in his bones.]
Lark Fenriz: <Anger in his voice.> For many years, blood has been shed in the name of God. Blood has touched the Earth for many reasons.
Land. Money. Rights to plots of land. But never... <Serious anger in his voice!> Never has the banner of God been waved in such a pathetic, shallow, selfish way than now. You disgust me, Pope. <His veins will pop if he doesn't calm down soon.> Everything about you I loathe!
Everything about you makes me want to vomit! Chasing a wrestling title has become a Holy Crusade nowadays? <Shakes his head, obviously disgusted.> That is simply pathetic.
["What an ignorant fool... Soon he'll be sheered, just like the rest of the good little sheep."]
[Lark leans forward, getting really into his words--compassion and care in his stature. With eyes are focused straight on the camera, he hasn't even blinked.. Even so, his eyes seem to be hiding the evil within.]
Lark Fenriz: <No more anger. Lark is just disgusted, plain fucking disgusted.> Your priests rape little boys. Your Pope is near death.
Famine and disease has gripped the world. Violence has broke out into the streets all over. But you, the next in line, are here... in
California, trying your best to win some wrestling title? Do you understand how stupid that makes you look? Working here in SEX will do nothing to help serve your Lord! Working here in SEX trying to win some wrestling title is not going to help rid this world of evil, devil worshippers! It will do nothing to further spread the word of
Jesus Christ. Do you understand that? <He laughs, out of pure frustration.> Why do I even bother trying... The only way you
Catholics seem to learn is through bloodshed. <A strange, evil gleam comes over his face.> A love for bloodshed--one of the few things we have in common.
["Blood, blood, blood... Oh I can't get enough of that wonderful, sweet liquid!"] [His eyes. They are baby blue, and seem so angelic, a sharp contrast to everything else about Lark.]
Lark Fenriz: And bloodshed there shall be this week. <Happiness stands out in both his voice and nature. He is more relaxed, more joyful now.> I will spill your blood across the mat. I will make sure you understand the power within these bones. The fans get to choose the weapons, but it does not matter what they bring to the ring--the end result will be in the same. I will teach you a lesson this week
Pope. <Serious tone now.> You are no better than me, and I will do everything in my power to make you realize that. You are not some religious figure, but a lunatic thinking he is hearing God in his head. You and your alter boys are no real threat to me, or anyone else for that matter. Just a bunch of a nut cases parading around, trying to scare non believers into submission before they are damned.
Your words fall upon my deaf ears, and your attempts at domination make me laugh. You are a man living in a world of lies and false promises. You will be exposed. And, Pope...
[Demonic smile. Happy, pleased, satisfied demonic smile.] Lark Fenriz: It will not be painless. No, it will actually be quite painful. [He stands up, smirks and the candles go out. The darkness won.]
