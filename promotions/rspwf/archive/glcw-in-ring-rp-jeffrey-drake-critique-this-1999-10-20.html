___________________________________________________________________ [The fans are obviously enjoying themselves...so far on All-Stars, they're being treated to a great night of wrestling....and, they fall drop-dead silent when the lights dim and "Welcome To The Jungle" begins to blare, causing GLCW newcomer "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake to appear in the center of the aisle.
The fans begin to give Drake a heel pop....seeing as though Drake's match is coming up...He is dressed in his wrestling tights, and is ready for business.
He climbs in the ring, does a couple 360o spin kicks, and takes the microphone from the ring announcer. The fans are getting a special gift...THE PRIVELAGE OF
HEARING DRAKE SPEAK LIVE!]
["TC" JD] Shut your mouths and listen up. [Heel pop.] ["TC" JD] Everyone knows that I have a match coming up in which three little imbred, pompous ingrates...want to try and make a sudden impact in the GLCW...they want to get in
Andy's favor...they want to show that they're something special. Well, well, well..isn't that just too bad,
because they've forgotten one little factor that makes a world of differnce...[Points to himself with his left hand] ME! I am the one that will be the name on the tips of every single GLCW fan's tongue within the next year! [M E G A H E E L P O P !!!!!!!!] ["TC" JD] I mean....we've got some real winners in this four corners match...THE MASKED SUPERSTAR JOBBER?! Now, tell me this guy doesn't have a complex...he must be a little insecure...or maybe its the fact that if he never becomes a star...which he probably won't...no-one'll be able to insult the poor slob because he always had the "Jobber"
part of his name to fall back on...this guy must actually have some smarts...unlike some other people I can name... ...Moving along.... ...CURMUDGEON...now, is it just me, or does this guy have the hardest name to pronounce in the sport? I can barely spell it, nevermind say it right...and what does it name mean anyway? Is it some kind of card game or something? If it is, must be pretty damn hard to
play, because this guy hardly has a personality..or imaginination. Nonetheless...he's gonna have to pay the human toll ta'night....if I were him, I'd be suckin' down some nerve pills in the back, because when I get through with him, he's sure as hell gonna need em'! ...Then... ...Ahh.... ...There's my best friend in this business... ...My main man... ...THE ONE PERSON I CAN'T FREAKING STAND IN THIS ENTIRE BUSINESS...."MONEY DRIVEN" MIKE SEBASTIAN! ...How the hell have ya' been, Mikey? Long time no see...still running your big mouth as usual? [Cold, evil grin.] Probably...its too bad I don't care enough about you to ask ya how your career's been going...but, it figures..you've been doing good....you're Mr. Bigshot now...Mr. EMWC...Mr. Strictly Business...STRICTLY MY ASS! You claim that I had a little hint of an ego problem last year? MIke...you didn't know me....but I knew you...and I knew I would hate your guts as long as I live! ...And guess what?... ...That's one promise I've kept so far!... ...Revenge is sweet Mike...and no revenge would be sweeter than me pinning you right in the center of this very ring. I don't want to change the world, Mikey...I just want to change the course of your career...have all the reps from your bigs*it leagues knocking down my door..whoa oh oh..I can't wait ...oh, and as you like to say... ...The check's in the mail. ...Signed... ...Sealed... ...And Delivered!... ["Welcome To The Jungle" begins to play again as Drake leaves to a resounding chorus of boos from the audience...who feel Drake wasted their time...Drake just smile smugly to himself as he heads backstage.]
