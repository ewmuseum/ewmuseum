[RolePlay For Adam Morley] - Please critique?

18 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a little sumpin' I did for BRAWL. Please tell me what'cha think. Thanks - [The scene opens up at a local gym complex. There are different activities going on, as people walk from here to there. In one corner of the room, people are lifting weights, running on treadmills, and doing other aerobic type work- outs. The camera then pans over to see a group of middle-aged men dribbling a basketball around, and shooting towards the rim, but failing misserably. The camera then moves over to a row of three batting cages. In the first cage, an older man is swinging at softballs, and connecting pretty good. The second cage is occupied by a younger teen-aged boy, he is swinging like mad, attempting to kill the baseball. In the third, and final cage, there is a younger looking man. He appears to be about 20 or so. He is dressed in a pair of short black "nike" shorts, and a black, Chicago White
Sox, jersey. He swings at a ball, and blasts it into oblivion. The next ball, he hits a major line shot right back at the machine. He then turns around, and shuts the machine off. He takes off his helmet, revealing ... Adam
Morley! He shoots a grin into the camera, and begins speaking.] AM - "Impressive, eh? Yea, well, I figured since I was in town, I'd stop by and brush up the old skills."
[He begins to remove the batting gloves, while still looking into the camera.] AM - "I'll be accepting major league offers, so bring 'em on boys!" [He smiles once again.] AM - "Well, well, well, In the "Big-Time" Adam Morley has to go up against
"The One And Only". This punk runs his mouth, claming to be "cocky" and
"charismatic", well, he doesn't know the _first_ thing about it! Lousy independent wrestlers, they should stick to the hardcore bull[CENSORED]."
[He then shoots a big grin, and walks over to an empty basketball court, and picks up a basketball, and sinks a three-point shot.]
AM - "Damn! I'm good! Y'know, I don't even have to be in BRAWL kicking these schmucks as[CENSORED], I could be in the NBA, or the MLB, or the NFL, or hell, even the WNBA! I'm that damn good! And Mr. Lockheart is going to find out first hand what it's like to step in the ring, and go toe-to-toe with a _real_ athlete."
[He dribbles the ball towards the rim, leaps, puts the ball under his legs, brings it back up, and slams it down the basket.]
AM - "Jesus, this is _too_ simple! Y'know Lockheart, being the competitor, and athlete, that I am, I will help you out a little during out match. I won't go full force on you, untill I see you're putting up a fight, then, I'll blast ya with the "Attitude Adjustment" and pin ya, for the one, two, three!"
[He removes his jersey, to reveal a plain white "wife-beater". He wipes his sweat, and shoots another smile into the camera, then begins speaking once more.]
AM - "Lockheart, BRAWL, and the _whole_damned_world_ ya better be ready,
Mister Morley is coming for gold, and whoever steps in my way, will be removed, just as quick ..."
