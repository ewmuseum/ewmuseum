(RP, Please critique) William Craven.

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On a whim I've decided to post 3 William Craven RP's. Now, this is the music video nobody critiqued when I first put it up. Now, somebody please critique.
---- (From a blank screen springs the sound of heavy drumbeats. Pounding slow rhythms that increase in tempo slowly, they seem to be leading up to something. At the bottom left corner of the screen appears a set of credits in white.)
"The Cravening" (Pleasure, Pain) Ultimate Breakdown. To crack, to break. Darkest Horse Records. Director: Jhonen Taybeck. (Center-screen appears these words.) "Craven-A definition: To crack or break." (Both these words and the credits fade away as the rhythm continues to increase in speed for a precious few more seconds before exploding in sound.
Electric guitars and mechanical sounds accompany the drums which now beat fiercely and fast. On the screen words in white appear and dissappear, fading and flashing into and out of existance. One and two at a time, one after the other, these appear. . .)
". . .Again. . ." ". . .Sober. . ." ". . .Liquor?. . ." ". . .Consequence. . ." (Darkness gives way to light, an intense overhead light burning down on a bald figure from the shoulders up. The figure is nearly silhouetted in white, only the slightest details of a brow ridge and the undercurve of a nose are visible in black. A deep, intense, male voice begins to hammer out these words rhythmically in an angry-sounding rap.)
"You think you can? Funnyman?" "Step between those ropes and let's see where you stand." "It's just a mass of piss and *bleep*, when you're in the pit!" "Takes everything you have, all of it." "Can you imagine? Can you see?" "You are nothing but a pit to MEEE!" (Eyes appear in the black shape under the brow on the face of the figure, then the camera zooms in and the scene flashes to an instant black. Quickly fading in is a scene of chaos in an earthen pit. Inside the pit are Craven and Steve Starr at war, surrounded by twenty-five masked thugs that encircle the hole in the ground. The rapping voice changes to a high pitched, shrieking one.)
"You see the cuts? You see the breaks?" "Once you can take these, you'll be a freak." "The scars that you see, cut to the heart of man." "How can you know when you can't, even understand?" "Can you imagine? Can you see?" "Are you like others, or are you JUST LIKE MEEE!?" (The scene shakes and flashes, like lightning on the screen. A sudden cut scene shows Craven standing over Starr, a smile on his face, his mouth moving. The electric guitars raise in volume, putting the drums to the background, and a second voice sings. . .rock style.)
"Killing gives him pleasure," "living gives him pain," "and every time you think he's gone," "he'll be coming around again!!!" (The music goes instrumental for a moment as the screen goes black and a few more cryptic words flash across the screen.)
". . .Change is bad. . ." ". . .Remembrance. . ." ". . .Eternal. . ." ". . .Relaxation. . ." (Like opening an eye, a scene expands from the center of the screen out. Craven kneels in the center of the ring, seemingly entranced. Mark Farrow makes his way in, chair in hand. He looks around for a moment, seemingly confused for a moment, then he shatters the wooden folding chair over
Craven's head. Craven bleeds a little, but stands up, unaffected while
Farrow runs. Suddenly a grouping of actions plays rapid-fire. Craven is struck with a hundred weapons by a dozen different people. All the while, the hard beat and rapping lyrics play. This time, it's said by the
deeper-voiced rapper.) "You think you can? I know you can't." "Me, I know you for crap, but you think that you're all that!" "I can't believe you've come at last." "Why waste my time when you know that you're outclassed?" "What's your deal? What's your story?" "If you got the guts. . .I GOT THE GLORY!" (The rapid-fire clips continue, but now they show Craven applying his finisher, the Crucifix kneebar he calls the "Reverse legbreaker". The voice barking the lyrics changes again to the higher-pitched rapper.)
"He lays a bitch on his back," "and then he starts to attack," "snap popple crack," "bends back," "they can't take, he will make," "'CAUSE HE DON'T STOP PULLIN' 'TILL SOMETHING BREEEEAAAAAKKKSSS!!!" (Again the rhythm changes to more of a rock beat, and the chorus plays.) "Killing gives him pleasure," "living gives him pain," "and every time you think he's gone," "he'll be coming around again!!!" (Music quieting, the screen is suddenly filled with the mug of Craven. The film is grainy and bright, making Craven look like a cartoon character drawn in bright primary colors. His blue eyes are intensified to a pastel hue, his skin is yellowed and his scars are red. His mouth moves strangely out of sync with his words.)
"It's about me. . .all about me. . .it was never about you. . .only me. . ." (Craven's words trail off to a whisper, and then to silence. His lips continue to move as the credits from the beginning of the video appear again in the lower left corner.)
"The Cravening" (Pleasure, Pain) Ultimate Breakdown. To crack, to break. Darkest Horse Records. Director: Jhonen Taybeck. (All fades to black as Craven continues to mouth his claims. The credits disappear, and the music finally fades away.)
