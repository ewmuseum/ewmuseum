USCW Flash Central 5/12/98 Pt. 2

13 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Demolitionist Daniel Dare Da Lench Mob (Noumenon) Sinister (2) Scott Dakota -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (A reporter is talking to the Demolitionist) Reporter: Demolitionist, how do you feel about your future here in the USCW? Demolitionist: Well, my last few matches have been great and I feel that my future will hold many great things for me.
Reporter: Does that include any titles? Demolitionist: Yes, I beleive that I have the talent to become a title holder here in this fed and I think that sooner or later other wrestlers will find this out also.
Reporter: Now, who is your next match going to be against? Demolitionist: I don't know, that depends on who accepts this challenge. I challenge any one of the wrestlers here. Accept and be prepared to fight the new and improved Demoltionist. I want a match at the next event. Who wants to be my opponent? Just bring it on.
Reporter: So you are making an open challenge to the entire fed? Demolitonist: Your damn right. Bring it on. Reporter: Ok, good luck in the future, well folks it looks like the Demoltionist means business and to tell you the truth, I wouldn't want to be his next opponent.
(Scene fades to black) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <Daniel Dare, the GREATEST wrestler in USCW history, is at a wrestling convention in Virginia. The "Legend of Legends" is dressed up in a blue t-shirt, with black jeans, and old white Reeboks. He is listening to the conversation of a couple of local fans>
MARK FAN: "Didjya hear? Bullet joined WTF!" SMART FAN: "Another old fart to add to the extremely stale WTF lineup. Ya see, Kev. I think it is time you learned the truth about wrestling. Guys like Viper and Bullet DO NOT have talent." MARK FAN: "They don't???? What do you mean?" SMART FAN: "Look at when Viper and Bullet wrestle. Dude, it looks so fake. They can't sell moves for shit. They know 5 moves. They're boring, man."
MARK FAN: "What do you think of Daniel Dare." SMART FAN: "Dude he RULES. He knows so many moves, he's awesome on the mic, he carries the BWFE and USCW. His finisher is the BEST."
MARK FAN: "I don't like his finisher." SMART FAN: "<laughing> That's because you always wet your diaper when he does it." MARK FAN: "<In a whiny voice> I wear underwear, you asshole." MARK FAN: "I learned it from watching Viper." SMART FAN: "That is why he sucks, little bro. He can't speak properly. His interview skills are limited. Dare is the picture of eloquence on the microphone." MARK: "Eloq-WHAT!" <The camera quickly zooms up to the face of the laughing Dare> DARE: "Ah, one of the life's beautiful moments. When a true fan of this sport explains the realities of it to a younger sibbling. WTF attracts a crude, rugrat crowd, with little maturity, and no real knowledge of how this business works. I, on the other hand, am loved by those who know how it really goes."
"Viper, Bullet, WTF. You are all simply pawns in the game of life. I am the master of your reality. Rob Spicer has made me the most powerful man in the USCW. More powerful than WTF think they are, and
DEFINITELY more powerful than their new ass-end, BULLET." "Bullet, I hope you realize that you joining WTF had absolutely NO IMPACT. You do not have the name, the credentials, or the talent to add ANYTHING to ANY group. It makes me chuckle, knowing that those two lowdown yellow bellied dogs, Viper and Sinister, are just going to use you as their glorified GOFER <smirks>. I am glad you made the mistake of accepting my challenge of a NO DQ, NO COUNTOUT, NO TIME LIMIT, NO HOLDS
BARRED STREETFIGHT. That is MY kind of a match, a match you CANNOT win. Realize something, silver boy, you are just a STEPPING STONE to the SNAKE. EVERYONE in the USCW is gonna be my personal stepping stone to the serpent known as Viper!"
"Viper, the trouble that you have caused my BEST FRIEND, Rob Spicer, will NOT go unpaid. You have a piece of jewelry that I want to add to my collection of gold. The USCW World Heavyweight Title. I shocked you and the whole world at MAY DAY '98. You owe me something, boy. Something that has been ONE YEAR IN THE MAKING. STOP DUCKING ME, GOD DAMNIT. It is time to face your fears and meet me in the lion's den known as the ring.
We'll finally get to see if you have enough venom to poison a 'Legend of Legends' career. Ha! Ha! Ha!" <Dare laughs hysterically, as the camera fades to black> -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <you see noumenon> seagram: horse heads, we will fight you. we are not afraid of you and we sure as hell know we can kick your asses. dont bother flattering yourselves cause you really suck. there are several tag teams hear in the uscw that could beat you with out trying. and noumenon just happens to be one of them. wtf, dlm must give you your props. you sure as hell fooled everyone. and id also like to let one voodoo spectre know that he has made the best decision of his life by going dlm.

seagram: we sure are and we will like to let all you know that maxi and lowe had to go back to england cause queen elizabeth requested it. she needed her boytoys around and didnt like him going away so much to wrestle. but know back to that jobber that mopreme will be facing, what was his name again?
mopreme: i cant quit remember, if you said it i would remember it. seagram: is it one of them evens losers? they suck bad. mopreme: no, i believe he would take one of them. seagram: what about a swa member? mopreme: it definitely isnt one of them and if it was i wouldnt know have ever heard of him. you know any swa members?
seagram: not that i can think of. oh, i have it, is that dr. guy? mopreme: yea i believe so. oh well, if it is that will make life that much more easier. we know we can beat his sorry ass.

mopreme: we know that tse has a big decision infront of them. will they lose to us next sunday or just chose not to fight us. either way you need to let us know so that we can schedule another match for sunday in its place.

we apologize for any inconvenience to our members as a result of his comments. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (The camera fades in and we see Scott Dakota in his living room. He is laying back on a reclining chair watching the TV. His injured leg is taped up and has an icepack on it. Dakota looks to be in great pain as he sits up, he reaches over and starts to gently rub the taped part of his leg. His face is also bruised and he has a cut on his forehead which has been stiched shut. Dakota grimaces in pain and then begins to speak.)
DAKOTA: Well Matthews, what can I say? That had to be the most grueling, painful, intense match of my career. It was nothing short of awe inspiring the performance that we put on, we both went through hell this weekend. I know one thing, Matthews, after that match I have a new found respect for you. You definatley showed me that you have every single bit of determination and heart that I have, you proved to me that you are a real man. I take no shame in the fact that I gave up, you gave up as well, at the same exact time that I did. We both just could not go on any longer, and had to give in. I looked over at you, and our eyes met, and for a few seconds I knew that you had no more left, and I had no more left, I saw the pain in your eyes, as you saw the pain in mine, and then we both uttered those dreadful words, "I Quit". Matthews, we both gave it all we had this weekend, and for me it resulted in some serious injuries, which will force me to be sidelined for a couple of weeks. I suffered nerve damage in my leg, which I may need surgery on, I'm not quite sure yet, I still need to get another opinion from a nerve expert that I will see in two days time. All I know is my leg is hurt really bad.
(Dakota sits fully up and reaches over to the table by him and grabs his cigarettes and a lighter. He puts a smoke in his mouth and lights it, takes a big drag and continues.)
DAKOTA: All I know is I will be out of action until the next Pay Per View, that is certain. I am going to wrestle at the next PPV though, even if I am not 100%. Matthews, this has to come to and end, we need to do battle one more time, and this time, I challenge you to a Spin The
Wheel, Make A Deal Match, at the upcoming PPV. Now, there is no telling what match the wheel will land on, but I am game for anything. The score between us needs to be settled once and for all, I have a newfound respect for you Matthews, but I still need to prove to you that I can defeat you, and at the PPV, even if I have to crawl to the ring, I am going to wrestle you, and trust me, you will be SILENCED!
(The camera fades to black as Dakota takes a drag off his cigarette.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------
