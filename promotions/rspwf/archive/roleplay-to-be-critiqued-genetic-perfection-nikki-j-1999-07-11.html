(Roleplay to be Critiqued) "Genetic Perfection" Nikki J

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens up to Detroit city, early in the moring. The sun is just starting to show as the camera moves around and we see the motor city. Cars driving down the road for morining rush hour. It continues around until we see a small hotel. The camera slowly moves in onto it then fades out. . )
(The camera comes back now inside of the hotel moving down a hallway. Passing room after room then it stops at a door and turns in on and we see the number of the room #212. The camera zooms in on the number until the scene goes black once again. .)
"THUMP,THUMP,THUMP. ." (We hear the sound of footsteps as the camera comes back into focus now inside of the room. We see a small sofa sitting in front of a television but the sound of the footsteps continues and the camera turns to where they are comnig from as we see Nikki J come into view. Nikki stps in the doorway looking over at the camera as we move in closer to her. She has on a pair of loose grey sweat pants, and a tight white shirt on. Her hair is tied back in it's usual ponytail, and by the way her face looks sh has just woken up, along with a few bruises from the recent attack. She waits for a moment before she begins to walk across the floor and speak as she goes. .)
"GENETIC PERFECTION" NIKKI J - Jasmine, you really showed what kind of a champion you are last Sunday. You showed the world one thing, somthing I knew right from the moment I saw the look in your eyes when you first addressed me, and finally, a week ago you showed the world that one thing you have for me. .
. (Nikki stops fast and turns around, she looks right into the camera, almost burning a hole into it with her eyes.)
NIKKI J - Fear. . (Nikki turns back and walks a bit further then stops and sits down on the sofa. .) NIKKI J - Fear, somthing you're not used to having when it comes to your womens title. .but as I stated before, the times have changed. You attacked me like a coward, trying to eliminate the threat before it becomes to big, but I got bad news for you <sarcastically> Honey. .
(Nikki gets back up and stands there with her hands on her hips. .) NIKKI J - I'm still standing. You made two mistakes, the first was attacking me like you did, the second. .
(Nikki grins almost devilishly. .) NIKKI J - Was not getting the job done. You see MJ, you wanna play these games,
I can play them to, but when I'm done with them you won't be standing. .for quite some time. Summer Sizzler comes closer with each passing day, and you can fel the gold slipping through your fingers, try, try, try to stop it from happening but let's face it. You can't stop the inevitable and you can't...
(Nikki gives a little bicep flex.) NIKKI J - Fight the future. . (Nikki sits back down on the sofa, she goes to speak again but yawns. She then places her arm across the top of the sofa and goes on.)
NIKKI J - But in the meantime, before I get to Sizzler I have to continue to show all other women in the ICW who the number one woman in town is. . .and
Tuesday on Rockin' I face Tara Marshall. I teamed with her, but it wasn't exactly by choice, and personally I have nothing agaisnt you, but Tara we all know this isn't personal. You know what I'm here to do, and I'm not letting you stand in the way. So on Tuesday you're just going to have to be another little girl taken out by a. .
(Nikki pauses for a moment and grins. .) NIKKI J - Pardonme, by THE woman in the ICW and wrestlnig in general. Tuesday night, Genetication claims another victim, sorry Tara. .well <pauses> Not really, but anyway. Tuesday night, you will see first handed why I am the one who will topple the great Jasmine, and why I am beyond a shadow of a doubt.
True and absolute. . (Nikki moves her arms in a way so we see the amazing size of them as she grins. .) NIKKI J - Genetic Perfection. . . (The camera slowly pans back as it fades out. . )
