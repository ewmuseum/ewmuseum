[CRITIQUE]-[TJ Corruption]-[2]

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another little flash I just did, this time for WILD wrestling.. Positive criticism please sirs. ======================================== [ Fade in. ] [ The cameras begin rolling as you apparently open into a bar. You can tell the bar is located in Cleveland based on all the memorabilia spread throughout. You see a few people sitting upon barstools, a few empty stools, and then the back of a very familiarly built man. As the man turns over his shoulder and motions for the camera to come closer you now see the very familiar face of "God's
Gift" TJ Corruption. The camera aligns itself as almost if it is positioned in a barstool to the right of Corruption.. ]
"GOD'S GIFT" TJ CORRUPTION: WILD Wrestling, frankly I'm not very impressed thus far. I ink a deal with the promotion looking to possibly get some competition and I once more find myself a couple days away from dominating some scrub.
Don't get me wrong, I think I have finally found a place where there is quite a few kids here that can compete, but then again, I may be wrong. Only time will tell if this promotion can produce some competition for "God's Gift".
I've traveled all around the nation, all around the continent, and all around the world looking for a solid competitor and I've yet to _TRUELY_ find one. Who knows though? This may be the place, but it's highly doubtful to say the least.
[ Corruption motions for the bartender. ] Bartender: How's it going? What can I get for you? TJC: Go ahead and give me a whiskey please. [ Corruption picks up his pack of Turkish Gold cigarettes off the bar and swiftly pulls one out. He puts the cigarette in his mouth and lights it, exhaling as he places it on the ashtray. The bartender returns and places the whiskey on the counter. Corruption takes a few sips, picks back up his cigarette and looks up at a corner positioned television while speaking. ]
TJC: My search for the absolute competitor begins Tuesday as I go two to two with a complete zero known as Kadego.
[ Corruption sighs as he takes a few drags off his cigarette and then continues to speak. ]
TJC: I mean I want to believe this kid is going to give me a run for my money but it's just so hard to do so. After all, I've been doing this for years and
I've yet to find a true opponent why would some identity known as Kedego supply me with one? Nevertheless, I truly hope you have your a-game on come Tuesday because it's going to take a hell of a lot more then luck to take me out and luck is all you have to depend on when up against "God's Gift."
I'll work you over in that ring until you can't be worked anymore. I'll personally make it my mission to give you some of the most intense bumps of your life, and that's no lie. Honestly kid, you've caught me at the wrong time because I'm fed up with the trash promotions keep sending at me. I'm livid as hell Kedego and that's a very negative thing when your in the ring opposite me.
[ Corruption takes a few more sips of his whiskey and then finishes off his cigarette with a couple drags before putting it out in an ashtray. ]
TJC: I'll definently be seeing you soon Kedego, unfortunently it won't be a pleasent meeting, more of a boot to the face, suplex on the mat, Absolute
Corruption, and a one two three type of meeting.
You've still got quite a bit of time to prepare for the match kid, use the time wisely. I won't hesitate to physically and mentally break you down. It's only a matter of time before you drop a couple rungs down the ladder and notice the new notch in your loss column. WILD Wrestling,
_You've_ _Just_ _Been_ _Blessed._ Goodbye. [ Fade to black.. ] -[name withheld] ______________________ Owner and Operator - |Garden State Independent Wrestling| http://www.angelfire.com/ult/gsiw Handler - "God's Gift" TJ Corruption [WILD]-[EWC]-[S.W.T.] "Mr. Wonderful" Dallas Matthews [PW:C]
