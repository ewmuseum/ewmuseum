[ROLEPLAY, JUDGE IT DAMMIT!]Let's try this again

8 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: The screen opens up on an oil rig. You see men scattered all around working on various things. But there is one man centered on the screen who seems to stick out. He is wearing a white wife beater, faded black jeans, and black boots. His hair is black and stringy down to his shoulders. The wife beater is all dirty and covered in what looks to be dryed oil. His back is turned to the camera. Someone yells something and the camera catches it.]
Voice: Scotty! Enough on the day for you! You're done... go home! Scotty Drake: Thanks Lou. See you tommorow! [Drake walks towards a ladder. The camera follows intently right behind him. He climbs down it and the camera man follows. Drake starts walking down a covered hall. It's pretty dark. The camera mans assistant flashes on a light and the cameraman finally says something.]
Camera Man: Mr. Drake. I am Bill Wojahowski, with the L.A.W film crew. I have been asked to come here and ask for your first words in the L.A.W. Could you share some?
[Drake stops slowly. He turns around and scrathes the bridge of his nose.] Drake: Yeah sure. Follow me. [Drake turns a corner and the camera catches sparks shooting out from around the corner. He opens up a door on the left side of the room where he is and walks in. The cameraman follows.]
[The room looks to be a lockeroom. The walls are banana yellow and orange lockers line the walls. He walks to number 357 and opens it up.He pulls out a black t-shirt and some jeans.]
SD: So, you guys wanted some comments? Camerman: Yes. We were wondering if you would share some thoughts on the L.A.W and some of it's roster?
SD: Sure. Hold on one sec. . . [Drake pulls off the wife beater and throws on the black t-shirt. It reads
"Traffik Jam" in white letters on the front. He slides his boots off. Drake takes his jeans and goes behind a wall. He comes back 20 seconds later and zips his pants up.]
SD: Ok. First off, let me formally introduce myself. My name is Scotty Drake. Now, about myself. Well, there isn't much to say. You've seen my work, and you can see the enviroment I am around petty much all day. I am from Atlanta,
Georgia, but now I reside in Phoenix, Arizona. I have been training in the
L.A.W House of Wrestling for 2 years. I have been intensely working, day in, day out. Snow, rain, or sunshine.I have been there. Sure, I could have graduated 16 months ago, but I wanted to make sure my game was top notch. When
I graduated, I finally got recognized as an athlete. Now, I am ready for the big time.
[Drake pulls out some socks and a pair of Nike Air Max's. He slides his socks on, then he puts his shoes on.]
SD: Which brings me to my first L.A.W match. I will officially be added to the roster December 13th in Los Angeles. My first match, is against a man. . . that
I have _NO_ idea about. I have heard his name thrown around a few times, but it wasn't anything big. His name, is Boltz. A name that took hours and hours of thought. But I am not one to insult. I am a man of action. So Saturday, Boltz, be prepared to enter into the -
...WORLD'S... ...LARGEST... ...TRAFFIK... ...JAM...!!!! Because, like me, or hate me, I don't really give a fuck. You're going to live by the ring, die by the Drake!
[Drake finishes tieing his shoes. He shoves somethings in a little Nike duffel bag and zips it up. He tosses it over his shoulder and heads out the doorway.
