[Free Agent] - ["Da' Greatest Ever" Shawn Walsh]

8 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear RSPWF, Ya lookin' for someone? Well, then I'm the man you should be LOOKIN' for, if ya smell what I'm COOKIN'!
I, [name withheld], handler of "Da' Greatest
Ever" Shawn Walsh am looking for ONE more organzation to join up in. Maybe,
I might get one big offer. :) But probably not, cause we have Macro Cutter. [Sigh]
Anyways, here's a sample roleplay, if you like what you see, give me a ring at [detail withheld] , and no, already, I will not join the ECWP!!! Now, that I have that cleared up, here it is. -Brett P.S. Not my best work, but it's like good. :) [Fade in ...] [The nickname of Shawn Walsh, "Da' Greatest Ever" appears smack dab on the center of the screen in gold glitterly letters. A voice over then yells ...]
VO - "PLAIN and SIMPLE!" [The screen then shatters like glass into millions of itty, bitty, tiny pieces, as they then fly off the screen revealing nothing but complete silence and darkness.]
[SCENE: The camera shot opens up to a shiny, just cleaned and sparkling glass window inside a house.
Through this, we see a dazzling garden, green grass on the ground, and falling tree leaves slightly moving inch by inch from the breeze in the air.]
[The camera then turns away from this view to find none other than sitting on a blue leather couch, Shawn
Walsh.] [APPERANCE: Shawn wears a plain white T-Shirt, with the sleeves cut so you can see is well defined, but not bulging out biceps. His long sandy blonde hair hangs down tied back into it's usual ponytail, as his black and green airwalks rest peacefully on the gray carpet.]
[Without any hesitation, he speaks.] SW - "INWI ... You have felt the wrath of me once! It shall soon come again to a certain soul, a certain person, a certain "Homeless" person you can call it.
None other than "Homeless" Joe Boxcar. Sonny, you might've put Lawrence Williams out of tha' wrestling ring, but there's no way in HELL, your takin' me down, at Hot Spot, on Tuesday, October
Thirteenth! [Shawn adds a broad smile onto his face, as he whips up his resting feet now onto a oak table. Immeaditely, he speaks.]
"Your nuttin' but a born losa' Joe Boxcar! I captured my first title a few days ago in the Philly Wrestling
Union, and it's just bad luck for you that, well ... I FEEL LUCKY MYSELF! Don't you? [Chuckles] Isn't it just grand, and wonderful that you will collide with the man with the plan this Tuesday? That will knock your head clean out of your sorry "Homeless" ass?
Isn't it just grand to be me?! [Smiles] Joe Boxcar, your just another obstacle, on my path of doom, on my path till' my reign as Champion. Yet, there is another person
I must address. JAMES PILLOW ... [A serious look crosses Shawn's face.] "Es Te Bee" your in this place, huh, Pillow? I've defeated ya once, and I'd sure as hell LOVE to do it again! [Smiles]
Pillow, after I'm done with this hea' Joe Boxcar, your next in line, for the checkout man to SWIPE you out! For I'm ...
The man of the hour... The tower of power... The top of the line... The icing of the cake... Who's just too sweet to be sour! [Another smile cross good ole "D' GE's" face as he continues.] Joe Boxcar, you will be the second person to find out why I truly am ... The man ... The TRUE myth ... The living legend ... That is just ... "DA' GREATEST EVER!" _PLAIN_ and _SIMPLE!_ [Shawn places his feet on the floor again, then slowly but surely stands up. He then says ...]
SW - "Boxcar, be prepared!" [Shawn then walks away from the couch slowly, and then takes a right into another room.] [Fade.]
