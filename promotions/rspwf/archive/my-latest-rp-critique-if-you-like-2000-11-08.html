My latest RP, critique if you like...

8 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'll be honest, I haven't done to many "dark" roleplays, but this one really seemed to flow well in my mind, and I would like to hear some feedback on it, good or bad. So sit back, grab some popcorn and a beer, and hopefully
enjoy..... :-) [name withheld] ---------- (The scene opens on the campus of the University of Nebraska. You can see students walking from one building to another, and some that are standing and talking amongst each other. The camera slowly zooms in to a building that looks brand new. As the camera gets close enough, you can see
"HAMILTON HALL - ERECTED 2000 AD" engraved in the stone above the entrance. As the camera moves inside the building, you see the floors gleaming and the walls are lined with pictures. The camera moves down the hall further until it reaches the end and comes to a door marked "Chem Lab". The camera then fades to a shot inside the lab, in which the only light is from a small desk lamp, but is enough to see all the beakers and containers organized neatly on the various tables. On one side of the room, there is a picture of the remains of the Chem Lab after the bombing that took place, which serves as a reminder of the events that went down that near fateful day in June.
Sitting on the floor below the picture is none other than Jason "The Bandit" Blake, who is wearing a White Sox jacket and jeans. His is holding his knees up and has his head facing the floor. He lifts his head, and stares directly into the camera, with a look of pure ice.)
Jason Blake: The time has come. November 12th. My moment of eternal glory. This is my chance to be the big man in the NAWA, but it won't be easy. But what most don't know is there is much more motivation for me on this fateful day. But as we all know, this Saturday is a day in which we remember the ones we lost in the great wars of old. Well I am here today, remembering what I nearly lost on that sunny day in June. I was the victim of one of the most dangerous attacks ever seen in sports history. An attack that nearly saw four people losing their lives, including myself and the most important person in my life, the lovely Amy Sparks.
(Blake begins to slowly look towards the door of the lab as he continues.) Jason Blake: We escaped by a fraction of a second, and more importantly, with our lives. You see, my career, and even my life, pale in comparison of what Amy means to me. For those who didn't witness or hear about this incident, the perpitrator was none other than "The Atomic Son" Sean Malcomb.
That's right, the drunken hillbilly who wants to be looked at as a hero, is nothing more than a killer. We all know his intention was to kill the people who were in this very room. Of course I shouldn't have been in here to begin with that day, but I had an obligation to fill, and I did just that, but that still doesn't give him the right to take innocent lives away.
IT'S STILL NOT RIGHT TO TAKE AWAY MY PRIDE AND JOY FROM ME!!! (Blake stands up, and looks at the picture with an intent, but puzzled stare in his eyes, almost as if he's having a flashback.)
Jason Blake: Sean, I've put my pain on the backburner for far too long, and now I have no other choice but to bring it to the forefront. I'm sure you've moved past all of your past misdeeds to become the man you are now, but that doesn't change what happened. This is my first chance to step in the ring with you, and face you like a man. I've seen the look in your eyes when you are faced with the concept of facing me. It's the look of fear. I promise you Sean, you will be feeling my anguish that I've held down since
Memorial Massacre. You will see what it felt like from my side of that bombing. You will feel my fury like no other has before you. At first this match was only about the belt, but now my alterior motive has emerged, and I will not rest until I have satisfied my hunger for true revenge. But speaking of revenge....
Blake turns his back from the picture, and walks over to one of the tables and starts to fumble around with an empty beaker.)
Jason Blake: El Paco. You've been given the unenviable task of stepping in the ring as the first Pentagon member to face me since I was taken out by
Jimmy and Damage Inc. It's a good thing I was prepared for that backstab, or else I'd probably be crippled right now. But I will take out all my aggressions I have towards the Pentagon on you. I don't care if you have them come to take me out again, I already have my plans set out for that.
There is an ace up my sleeve, and I won't reveal it just yet. You know I've beaten you before cleanly, so I am sure that the desperation has set in on your part, and now all I have to do is wait for you to make the fatal mistake, and I will capitalize, and I will deny the Pentagon the one thing they want the most. All of Frank's beautifully laid out plans will....
(Blake drops the beaker and it smashes on the floor.) Jason Blake: Be shattered. Screwing me was the worst thing you could do
Frank. I was your biggest hopeful, and this Sunday I will prove once and for all that I am the Personification of Greatness. Who knows? Maybe one day it'll be you and I in that very ring, and we can see who's better. But
I am prepared for your reaction when I become the NAWA Champion. I am prepared to defend the title each and every week, in a futile attempt to wear me down. In fact, I'll be more than happy to defend that very title against a different member of the Pentagon each week until I've gone through them all. You know I'd love nothing more to take on each of them one by one and exact my vengeance on each of them personally.
(Blake stares briefly at the broken beaker on the floor and then heads for the door. He opens it quietly about half way, and is about to step through it.)
Jason Blake: Sunday, my destiny is fulfilled. Malcomb, prepare to suffer for your actions.
(Blake goes through the door and shuts it quietly. As you hear the click, the scene fades to a slow motion shot of the Hamilton Hall firebombing at
Memorial Massacre. The scene then fades to black.)
