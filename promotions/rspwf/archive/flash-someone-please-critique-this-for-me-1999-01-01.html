THE LEGACY It all took place the day that Xavier Belmont met wrestling superstar Deep
Thoughts. At the time Belmont was a lost young man struggling for a job. He spent days in the gym and days reading through newspapers looking for a job, until the day he met Deep Thoughts. Deep Thoughts saw that Belmont was struggling for a job and also realized that Belmont was a rather strong man.
Deep Thoughts at the time was an IWF employee. Thoughts thought that Belmont could and would be a rather good wrestler. Belmont, under the guidance of Deep
Thoughts made his debut in the Insane Wrestling Federation under the name the
"Heathen Spirit" Xavier Belmont. Belmont was signed and was put to work days later. His first match was against a much larger JoKeR. Belmont overcame the size factor and went on to his first win. At this point Soldiers of Style,
Belmont's stable consisted of two men. Deep Thoughts and Xavier Belmont.
Although Deep Thoughts wasn't exactly on a winning streak at the time Belmont kept his winning ways by beating Chris Stone hands down. The win took the rookie, Belmont to a record of two wins and zero losses. After being named number one contender for the Cruiserweight title at Halloween Hell is was
Belmont's chance to prove himself. Quantum, the Cruiserweight champion at the time thought that Belmont was just a bunch of hype as many others did. But on the up side Belmont got many who admired him and knew he had what it took to represent the name of IWF. Primarily Deep Thoughts and new found friend John
Myles. He also received confidence from Mr. No Mercy. On the night of
Halloween Hell 98 Xavier Belmont proved what he was all about and defeated
Quantum to become the new IWF Cruiserweight champion. Belmont that night left
Soldiers of Style to join FaTaL FiVe with "Elite Soul Seeker" Shades, the
"Ungrateful Soul" John Myles, RattleSnake, and David Frank. The same night
Deep Thoughts quit IWF. Through all of this time Xavier Belmont made a name for himslef, a legacy and so it was written that the legacy of the Heathen
Spirit lives on. Lives on as the IWF Cruiserweight champion of the world. And day after day a new page is written, the legacy lives on forever.
("Long Hard Road Out of Hell" by Manson blares over the TV as the "Heathen
Spirit" Xavier Belmont's logo appears on the screen. The screen fades to black and Belmont walks out of the shadows. He is wearing a long black leather jacket with a baggy white shirt underneath. He also has a pair of black sun glasses on and the IWF Cruiserweight title resting around his waist. His long black hair is tied back into a pony tail. He is also has a blackish brown goatee with blackish brown side burns that angle in at his nose. He walks forward as the camera zooms out. All that can be seen is Xavier Belmont standing in the middle of nothing. Complete darkness is all that can be seen besides Belmont. He lowers his glasses revealing his ice blue eyes, smiles and readjusts his glasses on his face. He looks up at the camera and begins to talk.)

None bad, but all good. I have acheived the goal of IWF Cruiserweight champion and now am I a founding member of FaTaL FiVe. A long with John Myles, RattleSnake, David Frank, and "Elite Soul Seeker" Shades we formed one of the most powerful stables possibly in IWF history. As well that very same night I said that I would beat Quantum and I did as my legacy goes on. Nothing is going to stop me now from staying on top. Many say that it is easier to get to the top than stay on the top. Well in my case they are wrong because on my road to the top I beat some of the best and I will continue to beat some of the best. Quantum said he was the best and I beat him. Then on my first win JoKeR said he was the best and I beat him. Then the my second win and maybe the easiest was against Chris Stone. One punch. One single punch laid him out for the three count. And then on Halloween Hell I beat Quantum.
You see my friends the cockiest guys never are able to back their mouths up. They say they are the best and they can beat everyone and they are the richest alive but where does that get you? Bragging gets you nothing except a big ego.
Then when you tell the cocky man they aren't the best they go on to say that they slept with every women in the world. Well congratulations, but it won't help. Then they get confused. Speaking of confused on Sunday night is my first title defend against the man Playne. Why did I say confused? Oh Playne my friend first you say I am a great wrestler. Then you go back on your word and say that I am not that good. As you can clearly see you are one confused man. You think you are going to be able to waltz into the ring and get any easy win? You better think twice because I am not going to lay down for you.
I didn't lay down for anyone. Not Quantum, not JoKeR and definatly not you,
Playne. You better just keep your sights set on the tag team titles because they are the only thing that will rest around your waist. You just don't seem to understand that I am not another easy win. The title that you want is mine
[points to the belt] I didn't hang at the bottom of the barrel until I got three easy wins. Now that I think about I got a title shot after two wins. It doesn't matter but the fact of the matter is we both have unblemished records and on Sunday night you will soon find out that it is you who will suffer his first loss. You will be denied of the IWF Cruiserweight title.

Under who's terms? You have three wins but let me say this, I am one to make things come to me not to wait for an oppurtunity. You see Playne the IWF isn't about waiting. It is about making things happen. By you getting a Cruiserweight title shot doesn't make you great. You see Playne as many have said to be I am going to say to you...You are living the impossible dream. You are going after two titles at once and more importantly one of mine. You see Playne no Fatal Five member is going to let you get away with an easy win. I know you aren't going to beat me and I know John Myles and Erebus won't let you get away with it. It isn't about what you think you deserve. It is about what you go out and get.
Playne, I am going to go out and get me a win. Then you go on to to say that you know what it is like to just win a title and then have it taken away from you. And you are going to take it away from me. Well, Playne I know what it is like to win a title and then defend it successfully time, after time, after time. You are going to see it first hand because you just do not have what it takes to hold the IWF Cruiserweight title. You don't have what it takes to carry the IWF flag on your shoulders and the belt around your waist. It is time that you realize that things don't always go as planned. Playne, you don't seem to understand that saying what you are going to do isn't the same as doing it. I said I'd be the Cruiserweight champ and I went out and did it.
You have yet to do anything that special my friend. Three wins...congratulations. I know I have not accoplished as much as men like the
Reaper or Drakon Hughes but I do have what you want Playne. The Cruiserweight title, but you don't seem to realize this but I do not have to beat you my friend. It is you who needs to beat me. I will come out and give you my all because that is what type of champion I am but don't except me to make any stupid mistakes. I am not ready to lost the title because I missed a suicide dive and hit my shoulder on the guardrail. You said you'd put your life on the line, well you don't even need to say that because when we get into the ring together I will hold your life in my hands. Your fate will be in my hands. I will have no mercy on your soul when we meet in the ring. I am not going to let you be the one who walks out of the ring with my Cruiserweight title. You won't rate me as a champion if I win by run in. I don't need you to look at me as a champion but think is for sure I do not intend to win by run in. I intend to pin you to the mat for the three count. One Two Three and you walk away a loser. You will not be pimped by me? I don't pimp, I just win. You see
Playne you are just like Quantum, always trying to make this "tought" image.
Well thinking you are tough and being tough are two very very different things. Saying it leaves it in your head, but being gains you respect. When I think of Playne I do not think of tough. To tell you the truth this is the first time that I actaully paid attention to you. Never thought that you would make it up to a Cruiserweight title shot. Well, death due us part because I am going to put you in your place and that is on the losing side. Trust me
Playne, I do not lie. (Xavier Belmont lowers his glasses and steps back into the darkness. He then takes off his leather jackey revealing the baggy white dress shirt underneath.

You think you know what I am all about but you are wrong. You thought you had me figured out...boy are you wrong. My ego is over size? It is time for you to look into the mirror my friend. Take a step out of your mystery world where you are the best and where everything goes your way. Walk into my world. A world of pain.

You don't know what it is like not to be appresciated for all your hard work. Day after day I would go to job interview after job interview just to be turned down because there was a corperate genious who was the "modern american" The guy who carried a briefcass everywhere he went. His wife was a buisness women and his kids got straight A's. Well, it was the complete opposite for me. I wasn't the best working man's man. I was not a corperate clone, I was outcasted but I was original. As a wrestler, now I am going to unleash my anger on you Playne, you are going to find out what it is like to be a true OutCast. When you are laid out in the center of the ring after losing your match to myself and there is no one in the locker room to congratulate you or say nice try you will then know what it is about to be outcasted. Just because you say you are an OutCast doesn't mean it is true. You are an outcast to what exstenet? An outcast cuz it sounds "cool." Maybe you think it will bring you popularity, well let me tell you this much wrestling is a popularity contest. Wrestling is about who is the better combatant and when we meet on Sunday night you will truly find out who is a true combatant. Your time has come Playne and you will be put into your place.
___________________ _________________________ ____________________ "Heathen Spirit" Xavier Belmont ___________________ _________________________ ____________________ -[~Xavier Belmont~]- You will find the true wrath, the true fury, the true anger that I unleash on my opponents match after match after match. Three wins and zero losses and a champion is an impressive resume` if I don't say so myself. Playne, you say that you are extreme. Well extremeness ends inside the ring and that is where it is going to hurt you the most. You see Playne I think I should remind you that you cannot win by a disqualification or a count out. So if I were you I'd face the facts and realize that you are going to be beaten by a better man. You don't phaze me by calling yourself extreme or any other adjective that you thing will make yourself sound promising. Now I hope you really do not think that I wouild havbe a member of FaTaL FiVe run in to help me out because that is truly wrong. What kind of a champion would I be if I had help. But assuming I do not have any help can I assume the same for you?
Just how low will you stoop to be the Cruiserweight champion? Well Shade help you out? I hope not because what kind of champion would you be if you didn't win fair and square. You see Playne I have _NEVER_ been pinned to the mat for more than two seconds. I have _NEVER_ tapped on the mat four times signaling that I give up. Because champions don't quit. It isn't about who has the best finisher. You may feel you have the finisher that is superior to everyone else's but what if you cannot execute your finisher? Can you rely on your pure wrestling skill to come up with a win? More a title? Death From Above meets

That ius my goal when we meet. Not to do some song and dance and spend the whole night trying to hit you with my finisher. I am about winning not show boating. That is what I do my friend. I will show you what it is like to get into the ring with a man like myself, a true champion is how I look at myself. Not as the biggest, or the baddest, or the cockiest, or the strongest, I look at myself as a real life person. Not as a "fan's wrestler." When I am going to hit you with a move I hit you with it...not look at the fans for approval. All I need to do is make myself happy. If the fans want to cheer for me then great. I am not going to lose matches for them. Wrestling, to me isn't about pleasing the crowd...it is a form of entertainment but I will not bust my rear for them. If a get an impressive win then a round of applause is enought recongnition for me.
