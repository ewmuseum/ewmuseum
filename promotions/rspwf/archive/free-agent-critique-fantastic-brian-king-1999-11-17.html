*Free Agent* [Critique] {"Fantastic" Brian King}

17 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Brian King is spotted in a 24 Hour Diner somewhere in northern Delaware. It's four o'clock in the morning and the diner is all but deserted. King is wearing a Philadelphia Flyers jersey. His hair is pulled back behind his ear and the scar above his right eye is visible. He is eating a ham and cheese omelet.
The omelet is half gone when King puts his fork down. King swallows and begins.] Brian King - Well, I am starting on my "Beginning of an Era" tour and today I just happen to be in the hometown of an old friend of mine. His name is not important as this interview is all about me. I have eight days until the PPV and I am going to update everyone on where I am. Today, I am in Wilmington,
Delaware. Why am I in a small city like this, when I could be in big cities like Philadelphia or Baltimore? Well, I know that Zach Morrison is trying to stay as far away from me as he possibly can. I mean, he knows that I, the man who loves to bask in all my glory, would go into the cities with the most people. So, he is trying to get to Atlanta through all the small towns, so I figured I'd follow him. Why should I even have told you Americans that? You need not know why I travel as I do. Zach Morrison, isn't he the man who continues to pester me? Isn't he the one who follows me, UWC Royalty, around?
He must not have heard Ian Aloxson's declamation, all shall call him "Greatness." I am not your master Zach, although you shall be my whipping boy at "Beginning of an Era". Zach, I want to hear you say something, anything at all. I need to hear you, a filthy American, say how you plan on defeating a man that comes from the greatest city in the world, Calgary. Just tell me
Morrison, I need to know. I need to hear you speak but a few words about how you how going to hand me my tail, as all Americans seem to saying in a more vulgar way today. Now, I must be going, my breakfast is getting cold and I need to be up early tomorrow to get out on the road.
[The scene starts to fade out as King puts his head down and picks up his fork to begin eating.]
