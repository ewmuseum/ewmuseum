[News Flash] Tony Paseno Arrested!!

13 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens with a shot of a news reporter standing in front of a restaurant. She is wearing a blue business suit with a skirt and a scarf around her neck. As she prepares to begin with her story you notice a few officers and detectives walking in and out of the restaurant. It is very cold out as you can see the breath of everyone walking around. The news reporter begins talking.]
Reporter: This is Brandy Cole reporting live in what seems to be the top news story of the day and possibly the year. Right now, I am here at Pazelli's
Restaurant here in downtown New York as FBI and local police are beginning an investigation on what seems to be a case of organized crime. I understand that professional wrestler, "The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno is said to be one of the main offenders placed under arrest earlier today. Let's show the footage of
Tony Paseno being taken into custody.
[The camera switches to a shot of Tony Paseno standing outside of Pazelli's
Restaurant with his hands cuffed behind his back. He is wearing a pair of black jeans and a white T-shirt with a black leather vest over top of it. He is resting his head against the wall with an arrogant grin on his face. A few police officers go over and proceed to put him inside of a police car as the camera gets in closer. People are standing around yelling at the cops and the camera man mutters out the question..."Did you do it Tony?"]
"The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno: This is a f[BLEEP]ing joke! [The police close the door on Tony as the police car speeds off. The scene switches back to Brandy Cole.]
Brandy Cole: Many people around the area here have very mixed emotions about the happenings here today. Many people are saying that they have the wrong man, yet some say that Paseno is guilty as charged. For those of you who are not familiar with the career of Tony Paseno, we have a small documentary of his career to show you now. You decide for yourself whether Tony Paseno is innocent or guilty.
[The scene fades to black and fades back in to a shot of Tony Paseno signing autographs for a long line of fans. He looks into the camera with a smile on his face as if he was genuinely enjoying himself.]
Narrator: At times Tony Paseno was the biggest fan favorite around. The kids loved him and all the ladies adored him. During this time Tony never once complained about doing a thing for this fans. He did numerous autograph sessions, charities, fund raisers. Some event went as far as to call this man a saint.
[The scene fades out and fades back in to a shot of Tony Paseno standing in the middle of a wrestling ring with life long friends Dusty Love, James Miller and
Justin Miller. There are bodies laying around the ring most bloody from the beating that these men just gave them.]
Narrator: But when Tony Paseno made up his mind to be a so called "bad guy" in wrestling, he can be your worst nightmare. It almost seems as if he has no conscience at all and will do whatever he wants. But no matter whether he was a good guy or a bad guy, he was always successful. Holding numerous titles in his career and carrying a great win-loss record throughout his career.
[The shot switches to a shot of Tony sitting in Central Park staring up into the sky.] Narrator: How much can we really tell from his wrestling career though. We know the character of "The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno, but do we really know the man Tony Paseno? Before we judge this man, I think that we should realize that the person you see on TV is not always the same guy in real life.
[The scene fades out and fades back in to Brandy Cole.] Brandy Cole: Tony Paseno had wrestled his last match just the other night and it will probably be his last for quite a while. I just got word that Tony has made bail and is not talking to reporters at this time. From speculation, it is rumored that Tony will not be seen around much until the court date, which is being set as we speak. Tony's uncle Max Pazelli was also placed under arrest and he is also out on bail. We will be bringing you more on the story as it happens. For now this is Brandy Cole reporting live for Channel 8 news.
[Fades to black.]
