Hey everyone, here's a sample RP, I took it from a random guy of our roster, here's the kind of quality you can be up against in DAVE.
www.e-fed.co.uk Sample RP: The BBC 1 hot air balloon appears on the screen, at present seen floating over Stoke. The voiceover guys kicks in…
VG: In half an hour we have CBBC with a Pingu double bill, that’s at 3:30. But now on BBC1 we have a special edition of “Through the Keyhole” Camera cuts to cheesy titles and dodgy music of “Through the Keyhole”. It then cuts into a studio with a capacity crowd on hand. Finally the camera focuses on the host, Sir David Frost except there is a problem, it is not David Frost but rather EWA star The Black Mamba! Crowd ceases clapping abruptly. The average age of the audience appears to be about 120 and so no one appears to know who TBM is except for a bizarre fat man with no hair on the back row who starts a “TBM” chant.
TBM: Good Afternoon ladies and gentleman and welcome to this hostile takeover edition of Through the Keyhole with me, your host, The Black Mamba!
Crowd: *silence* TBM: Before we start I think a little survey is in order. So audience types, who did you all come to see here today? Did you come to see me, the Epitome of Intensity?
Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! TBM: What? Well did you all come to see Sir David Frost? Crowd cheers loudly and starts a “David Frost” chant. Some people even “Whoo!” for seemingly no reason.
TBM: Survey says….what the hell is the matter with all you people? Don’t you realise who I am? I am The Black Mamba, the Epitome of Intensity, the former OSW Champion! I am the greatest technical wrestler in the entire EWA. Nest week I will become the EWA British champion then you people better respect me!
Crowd starts an “Aeris” chant out of nowhere! TBM runs over to the booth and turns off the audience microphone before sitting back down in his chair.
TBM: Well let’s get on with the show. First off let me introduce you to my panel for today, first a legendary chap and member of The
Insiders…Carl Heggs! Camera cuts to Carl Heggs who waves. The crowd is silent because the microphone is still turned off. The producer turns it back on but there is still silence.
TBM: Our second panellist is none other than part time colour commentator and inflatable hero…Mr Giraffe!
Camera cuts to Mr Giraffe who nods his head. Crowd cheers surprisingly loudly. TBM: And finally our third panellist tonight, the host of TV’s “Newsnight” please welcome Jeremy Paxman! Camera cuts to Paxman who remains still, looking slightly uncomfortable. Crowd claps politely. TBM: Well now we have met the panel its time for us to go to our first house here tonight and so without further a do lets go through the keyhole!
Camera cuts to the video screen where we see not regular host Lloyd Grossman but The Paralyser!
TP: Well tonight’s first house is a charming little cottage set out in the beautiful, peaceful countryside of Ireland. As we move inside we notice that the lights inside are all red for some reason, possibly suggesting that this person has an affiliation with red light districts. Notice also the erotic magazine collection on the table here. Now as we move into the living room the first thing that strikes me is the large metal pole in the middle of the room, which I believe is used by strippers. More evidence is the collection of £5 notes scattered on the floor and the chairs grouped around the pole, as if making an audience. Now lets take a look at the drinks cabinet.
TP strolls over to the drinks cabinet and removes a bottle of Vodka. He downs it quickly. TP: Hmm whoever lives here has excellent taste in alcohol, specifically
Vodka. Perhaps the owner of the house uses alcohol in every day life or maybe they just knew I was coming. Now lets move into the master bedroom.
TP runs up the stairs and into the bedroom. Inside he delivers a Frog Splash onto the bed. He then opens the main wardrobe.
TP: Now as you can see the wardrobe is filled with skimpy garments. There are also a lot of uniforms in here such as nurses, policewomen and what seems like a dominatrix. This perhaps suggests that this person likes dressing up and perhaps undressing for money. So lets look at the evidence.
The Strippers pole, the erotic clothing, the alcohol. Who lives in a house like this? TBM it’s over to you.
Camera cuts back to TBM. TBM: Thanks Teep and now for the benefit of our home and studio audience here’s who’s house it is.
Camera shows a silhouette in a darkened room. The lights come up and we see
Jimmy Nirab wearing an Aeris Lockhart mask. The subtitle says “Aeris
Lockhart” TBM: Now panel any ideas? CH: Well judging by what we saw I have to say it is a woman. Crowd claps to suggest he is correct CH: Ah right err is it Hulk Hogan? TBM: err no CH: Oh, damn it Mr G: Mok! Crowd claps very loudly Mr G: Mok! More loud clapping TBM: Yes Mr Giraffe she has been linked with prostitution and does work with alcohol. Well done, now then Jeremy any ideas?
JP: Well when TP first entered the house I saw a painting on the wall by
Renoir, which may suggest that she is someone who is heavily involved in the arts?
Crowd boos JP: Oh, well when TP when into the bedroom I noticed out the window there was a lovely field with some horses and stables in and I was just wondering if the lady is in any way involved with Horses, perhaps show jumping?
More booing, some people begin throwing various vegetables and hedgehogs at
Mr Paxman. Fearing for his life, Paxman quickly flees the stage and runs out of the studio.
TBM: Well guys I need an answer, who do you think it is? CH: Casey Chaos? TBM: I’ll forget you said that Heggs, Mr Giraffe? Mr G: Mok? Crowd roars, in fact a standing ovation TBM: That is correct Mr Giraffe and now please welcome to the studio Ms. Aeris Lockhart! Crowd applauds as Jimmy Nirab appears from backstage in an Aeris mask. He shakes TBM’s hand and then takes a seat next to him.
TBM: Nice to meet you Aeris, that is a lovely house you have there “Aeris”: Thanks ace, I designed it myself. I wanted to fit as much of my life into the house, which is why I have the strip joint in my living room.
TBM: I didn’t see much in the way of wrestling in your house, how is it that you are a 2-fold champion?
“Aeris”: Well my stripping helps me keep fit and my large bed comes in useful whenever the President comes round to discuss angles.
TBM: Ah I see, finally Aeris will you be dropping the British Title to me next week or will I have to beat it away from you?
“Aeris”: Well you can have it if you sleep with me. Otherwise you’ll have to wrestle me for it.
TBM: Hmm I see, well thanks for being on the show Aeris, here is the special
Golden key as a souvenir, ladies and gentlemen Aeris Lockhart!
Crowd cheers TBM: Right now lets go back to Teep to see house number 2. Camera cuts to TP outside a dark, poorly lit house TP: Thanks TBM well our next house is quite dark and mysterious. As we enter inside we see it is lit only be candles. If we listen closely we can hear
Staind’s “Break the Cycle” album is playing in the background. We can’t actually make anything out as it is so dark. Hang on, I have an idea.
TP pulls out his mobile and makes a call. Seconds later there is a knock at the door. TP opens it to reveal Andy “Drunken” Barlow standing there with his toolbox.
TP: Barlow! How’s it going? AB: Not bad, what’s the problem? TP: I think the electricity in this house is out, can you fix it? AB: Cause I can, I’m the world’s greatest electrician! Let me take a look Drunken Barlow heads into the basement. A lot of banging is heard as well as a small explosion. Suddenly all of the lights come back on. Barlow emerges from the basement smoking a cigar. He utters something about “King of the road” before heading back to his van. TP waves goodbye.
TP: Right now we can see the house is completely different. The walls are all painted pink, the carpet is pink in fact everything is pink. Perhaps this person has a split personality, one we see and one we don’t. If we look at the CD collection we can see the Staind album is surrounded by albums by N*SYNC, S Club 7, A1 and Destiny’s Child. This is further evidence of the double life. The walls are decorated by posters of Graham
Norton and other “Gay” comedians. Now lets look at the drinks cabinet.
TP opens the drinks cabinet and finds a great deal of alcoholic beverages. He pulls out several cans of various beers and drinks them all. TP: Now this person seems to have a great taste in alcohol, lots of variety. Lets head upstairs now shall we? TP walks into the main bedroom and delivers a Swanton Bomb onto the bed. He then hops up and begins inspecting the draws.
TP: If we take a look in the draws we find nothing but pairs of gloves. It seems this person has a fascination with gloves. We also notice that there are no bathrooms in the whole house. This may suggest that the person may have a certain smell to him, possibly focused in the area of his fingers. So let’s take a look at the evidence, the lack of bathrooms, the gloves, the gay imagery. Who lives in a house like this? TBM it’s over to you.
TBM: Thanks Teep and now for the benefit of our home and studio audience here’s who’s house it is.
Camera shows a silhouette in a darkened room. The lights come up and we see
Jimmy Nirab wearing an Jace Maverick mask. The subtitle says “Jace
Maverick ” TBM: So panel any ideas? CH: Well I’m judging that it’s man Crowd clap politely CH: Is it Sensational Sherri? TBM: Not quite Mr G: Mok Crowd get to their feet and start a Mr Giraffe chant TBM: You’re right Mr Giraffe he is a hardcore gimp, Jeremy? JP appears on the screen with a helmet and a riot shield JP: I’m judging by the record collection that he is a connoisseur of the renaissance period, possibly Andrew Lloyd Weber
The Crowd look at their fruit and realise that hurling would get them nowhere, so look to their hero Mr Giraffe to sort things out. Mr Giraffe gets out of his chair and instructs the camera to temporarily turn away.
After a couple of minuets of Paxman screams the camera comes back to see him put through the table and Mr Giraffe standing over triumphantly.
TBM: As good as that was Mr Giraffe I’m afraid I can’t give you any points for that and must ask for your final answers. Heggs?
CH: I’m afraid I'll have to guess, err is it The Fabulous Moolah? TBM: Surprisingly it’s not, Mr Giraffe? Mr G: Mok? The crowd almost riot with excitement. Someone sets off a flare while an impressive pyro goes off behind Mr Giraffe. Many people are fainting simply at Mr Giraffe
TBM: Well what can I say except bring the man himself out, Jace Maverick! The few members of the audience who have not yet fainted applaud politely for Jace who is in fact Jimmy Nirab in a Jace mask. He again shakes
TBM’s hand and takes a seat. TBM: So err Jace, are you actually gay. JM: Yes, it’s true TBM: Oh and do you have smelly fingers like I said? JM: Smelliest in the business TBM: You are quite boring really aren’t you? JM: Yeah sorry about that, I'll leave now. Jace gets up and leaves to no reaction from the crowd. TBM: Well time for our final house tonight so lets go straight over to Teep. Camera cuts to TP. TP: Well here we are in the beautiful Mediterranean climate and what is no doubt the finest house we have seen so far. Has to be at least 40 rooms set around hundreds of acres of land. Lets head inside.
TP heads inside. The walls are decorated with wrestling pictures and title belts. TP: My this is a fine house. If we head into the living room we see a large collection of WCW videos. The walls are decorated with posters of Scott Hall and Kevin Nash and there is a smell of Vodka in the air. Lets head into the drink’s cabinet.
Rather than walk over to the drinks cabinet like with the previous 3 houses,
TP is able to walk into the drink’s cabinet as it is actually a whole room. The walls are lined with bottles of Vodka. One wall is a huge fridge filled only with Red Stripe. TP begins to consume the alcohol at a ridiculous rate until he collapses. The camera immediately cuts back to TBM.
TBM: Hmm I think its quite obvious who’s house that is. I don’t think the audience even needs to see who it is, Heggs get this over with, who’s house is this?
CH: Aeris Lockhart? TBM: No you goon, we have already seen Aeris’ house CH: Oh right Mr G: Mok? Crowd starts a full fledged riot, tearing down the set and setting fire to everything in celebration of Mr Giraffe’s performance. Bodyguards rush in to remove Mr Giraffe before any harm becomes of him. Angered that Mr
Giraffe has been taken away, the fans go after Heggs and TBM. An elderly female rushes towards TBM to no doubt get a hug or something. Rather than hug an old person, TBM nails a stiff Northern Lights which shocks the crowd to the point that they all head over to Heggs instead. Being a bit weak,
Heggs is easily taken down and beaten severely.
TBM: Well I guess that is all we have time for on this special Insider’s Edition Through the Keyhole but look out for more Hostile takeovers of quizzes in the near future. Until next time, you can all Kiss
My Face!
The show ends with a blooper real showing TP and the camera flamingo talking outside Aeris’ house The Camera Flamingo appears to be talking Teep out of breaking Aeris’ back window and breaking in, yet Teep does it none the less with a big foot. Then they cut to Aeris’ bedroom with
Teep sitting with his back to the camera and The Camera Flamingo shouting
“That’s not the toilet you can’t do that their!” with that the show ends and Pingu starts, Enjoy!
