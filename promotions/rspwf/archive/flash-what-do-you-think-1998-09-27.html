[FLASH] What do you think?

27 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up in front of a country club in Toronto, Canada. It is beautiful. The garden full of flowers, the elegant decor, everything. As the camera zooms in on the entrance, you see some of the wealthiest and well known people in the World. To the left is Cindy Crawford, to the right is
Leonardo Dicaprio, and right in front is....Doomsday Dan?! With Maniacal
Mark?!
</p> "Hey, Mark, I think you gave me the wrong suit. This one's tiny!" </p> The camera zooms out as it shows their entire bodies. Mark's 5' 9" frame is covered in a huge suit. He has the pant legs and sleeves rolled up. Dan, on the other hane, is wearing a tiny suit. The seams of the jacket are ripped, the sleevs go barely past his elbows, and his pants are like shorts!
</p> "Nonsense Dan. Now here we are, the finest Country Club around. This is how we should act. You are The NIWF North American Champion, you gotta dress, talk and act like a champion!"
</p> "But Mark, I just don't fit in with this crowd. Look at me. I'm 6' 8" and I'm wearing a suit fit for a midget! I can't hang with these classy types!" </p> "Dan, your going to do this! Now go over there and get some caviar." </p> Dan scoops some caviar out of the dish with his hand, pops it into his mouth, and spits it out.
</p> "Aw, this stuff tastes like SH*T!" </p> A gasp is heard through the crowd. Sounds are going around, such as
"We'll, I never", "How rude", and "despicable behavior". 2 big security guards come up to Dan. The larger one says "You two, out now!"
</p> Dan and Mark look at each other, smile, turn away from the guards, and start to walk out of the door. They get to the door and stop. All of a sudden Dan cracks his knuckles as Mark pulls his trademark Branding Iron out from the large suit.
</p> "Never leave home without it!" </p> Dan and Mark swing around. Mark hits one of them with the Branding Iron as Dan almost takes the other one's head off with a clothesline. Another
Gasp is heard through the crowd. Dan sets up one for the Doomsday Device, and locks it on. Mark is pummeling the other with The Branding Iron. After about 2 minutes, Dan lets go of The Doomsday Device and Mark gives the
Branding Iron a rest. They both walk out of the door. As they walk away, they rip off their suits. Underneath are their street clothes. They hop on
Dan's motorcycle and speed away.
</p> They stop at an old, dirty bar. They walk inside as loud music blasts from the bar. </p> "Now this is my home! None of that pansy @$$ crap for me! Wait a second, that guy's in my seat!"
</p> The camera shows a large man sitting on the middle stool. Dan walks right up to him, taps him on the shoulder, and decks him with one punch. The man falls to the floor as 3 men playing pool throw him out.
</p> "Now, lets get down to business. Cameraman, you have followed us around for a while now, so you know what we are like. I have been accused of going soft, losing my mean streak, and being a wannabe. Oh really? Have I been going soft? Last I remember, I was undefeted for a while. Going soft? Did you just see those security guards get their @$$ whipped? Being a Wannabe?
Who am I trying to be? I don't drink beer during my matches and give everyone the finger. I don't stand in fireworks and develop nervous ticks. I don't play my belt as an air guitar, I'm not anybody's wannabe!
</p> NOKANDU, I have decided that you shall be my partner. I don't like you,
I know I can beat you, but I respect you, and if I have to, I will carry you to The Tag Team belts.
</p> Whoever is unlucky enough to win up facing me on Wednesday, you better have insurance, because ten dyas off is too much for me! I'm dying to let loose on somebody, although those security guards did ease my urge. (Dan laughs to himself)
</p> Finally, Rampage, you wanted a match, you got one. In one week, you will find out why I am called Doomsday Dan. That's all I got to say for now, so camera man, get the hell out of here!
</p> Dan pushes the camera away as the Camera Man stumbles to the door. -- [name withheld] Manager of Doomsday Dan and others NIWF North American Champ WICWF Extreme Champ (As Chris Candido) WICWF Internet Tag Team Champs (As Bam Bam Bigelow and Little Guido) SVW World Champ WIW World Champ And an all around great guy
