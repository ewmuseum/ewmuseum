A.C. Kryder needs a fed. Debut.

19 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A man is born into this world with the understanding that nothing is ever promised to him. Whether he makes it or not is solely up to the man himself. In his hometown of Mobile, Alabama is a man. An athelete who for his entire life has been a professional wrestling fan. After his learning years in school he signed with various independant wrestling leagues as a crowd security officer, as it allowed him access to a world where he felt he truly belonged. After three years of working various gigs with the independants and at the age of 26, he turned his life around by joining a fed. Two years and two American Championships later, he looks to move to another federation to learn his craft. This is the man known as:
A.C. Kryder
{Opening Scene} [At his trailer in central Mobile, A.C. Kryder is scene in his small but quaint yard. It is a hot day as the sun is steaming off his trailer, but
A.C. has always enjoyed the heat. A camera comes into view and notices a strong, muscular man with short brown hair and green eyes. He is cleanly shaved and has a tatoo of a tornado on his left bicep. Except for a pair of dingy blue jeans and a pair of worn out brown boots, he has nothing else on. He looks to the camera and begins to speak.]
A.C. - Not much to this little place, but it is my home. Allow me to introduce myself to the wrestling world. I am A.C. Kryder and I am a professional wrestler. For years I have busted my ass to find myself in this world, my true calling in life and now I know that my life is pro wrestling.
[Looking around his yard and taking note of a black cloud on the horizon, he continues.] A.C. - When I first started wrestling, I got my ass kicked nightly for almost a full year. I never gave up though and now I am a former two time American Champion. I was not raised in a rich environment, nor do I have millions of dollars stored away in some bank. What you see, is what you get. I am 5' 10" which is not very tall when you face on the average night a 6' plus man, but I have what it takes to make it.
[As the cloud gets closer, little drops of rain are now pelting the ground with much needed water.]
A.C. - I guess what I am saying is this. There are many great ones in the world of pro wrestling. But if you want to sign a guy who has the heart and the desire to make it in this buisiness, then look no further.
I have wrestled guys like Johanns Devere, Brute Force and Deathwish. I will face any and all challenges and I will win and achieve my dream.
You ask what that dream is? [Looking at the camera with an amused look.] A.C. - To get the recognition, the money and the respect to move out of the s*** hole. [He then turns from the camera's view and goes back into his trailer as the scene ends.]
