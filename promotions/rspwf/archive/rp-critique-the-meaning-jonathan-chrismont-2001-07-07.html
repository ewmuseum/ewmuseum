[RP Critique] "The Meaning" Jonathan Chrismont

7 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I don't know how my rp skills are doing lately as I haven't rp for almost a year now. So I decided to give it a try. It's probably not very good, but then again, I'm trying to improve, so advice is welcome here.
This is a new character I created for a fed that hasn't started yet. So it's an intro rp. I hope you guys enjoy it as much as I did writing this stuff. ----------------------------------------------- [The scene fades in the Kansas City airport, 11:30am] :narrator's voice: [To be more precise, inside the male bathroom. The place smells to cleaning utensils, it's a quite bright room, with the color white dominating the place. There's only one man in there, this man is the New-To-Be-Top-Star-Sensation Jonathan Chrismont, and right now he was about to...]
JC: Sh*t ! :narrator's voice: [That too. But he is now getting ready to leave
Kansas City, his home town...]
JC: Oh, hello fans, and future fans. Sorry for my language, I'm not having a very good day... Many of you don't know me, but they soon will. This little town has become too little for my talent. I need something bigger you know. I already conquered Kansas City, as you probably noticed...
:narrator's voice: [Let me give some background information... Jonathan Chrismont is a highly know wrestler in Kansas City, he claims to be one of the highest and hottest wrestlers in Kansas City, and probably, as he said himself, of the world. Strangely, nobody seem to care for Jonathan when he left... I wonder why ?]
JC: But you are wrong of course ! They practically begged me to stay. And so did my fans. :narrator's voice: [I didn't saw anybody...] [Jonathan changes his tone of voice, starting to lose his patience] JC: Oh really ? What about him ? [Jonathan opens the bathroom door, and out of it is a short little man, around his 40's, with a big chart saying "Jonathan Rulez !!!!"
upside down...] JC: It's upside down you idiot ! Short little man: What ? JC: The chart ! It's upside down ! Short little man: Oh, sorry... [The short little man flips the chart in it's correct position] JC: Jesus... Anyway, this is just one of the many fans I have. And I promise, my dear fans, you will now be able to see much more of it, soon. A whole new life awaits for me. And the way wrestling is doing,
I think there is only me and a select group of people who can save it from total destruction and failure. That man is...
[Moment of Silence] JC: Ahem... That man is... [Another Moment of Silence] JC: WAKE UP !!!!! Short little man: Ahn ? Oh, it's "The Meaning" Jonathan Chrismont ! JC: *sigh* That's right. And with me in the fed, they are only going up, to the spotlight zone. Start to understand The Meaning of my words, 'cause they are going to be like a prophecy from now on. It's all written in a book, my book... Which by the way it's being sold in the US stores nearest you. Only 18$. Buy it !
[Jonathan looks in his watch] JC: Time to leave, my flight is about to leave, and so am I. [Scene fades out with Jonathan leaving... Well... Running to get his flight. And then the scene finally fades out to a commercial of his book]
----------------------------------------------- Note: As you probably noticed (if not, I'm telling you now), this rp has much more comic situations. It's something I'm trying to put in my recent roleplays, I would like to know what you guys think about it.
Like, was it good ? Was it funny ? And so on... Useful criticism please.
