Chris Douglas/Rocko Johnson flash war

21 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Rocko dishes out some fire of his own] (Camera cuts to Germany. Rocko Johnson is walking down "main street" and is being greeted by all the nice people of Germany. Tracy Moore follows closely behind, a man runs at Rocko with a magazine in his hand, Rocko looks at it and it's the Germans version of Sports Illustrated, Rocko looks down towards the picture on the front page and it's dated March 1, 1988. Rocko thinks back and remembers that’s the day when he came to Germany and won the German boxing championship away from Francisco Freshauf the ex-German National Champion.
Rocko signs the magazine and hands it back to the man who has a huge smile on his face, Tracy speaks out to him)
Tracy: I didn't know you won that match on March 1. Rocko: Yeah, so what. Tracy: Well in less than a week it will be the ten year anniversary of that main event and your first boxing title.
Rocko: Oh yeah, we should celebrate that evening. Tracy: We can't. Rocko: Why not? Tracy: THAT'S NUCLEAR FALLOUT 2!!! Rocko: Well that's pretty ironic, my most important boxing match and wrestling match are on the same day, only ten years apart of course.
Tracy: And speaking of the match, Chris Douglas and a few comments made by him come to mind.
Rocko: Oh yes, I remember him saying something about nobody has ever shut him up. Well, the Ringmaster did a pretty damn good job of starting to shut you up because you were mute for a week, now I guess I'll have to finish the job that he started.
Tracy: Didn't you here him, wrestling had to take back seat in his life, he is such a busy man.
Rocko: Oh, yeah, my bad. Anyway, Douglas thinks that this is just another ordinary match, the same old thing he's been doing for years. Well, Mike Tyson might not get a crack at Steve Austin but I am one boxer who does get his shot at a wrestler named Chris Douglas, and boy I can't wait.
Tracy: Douglas is all ready making excuses, you've had your whole life to prepare for this match...
Rocko: Sorry Chris, I didn't know you were born yesterday ?!? Tracy: Then he just restated everything we've all ready mentioned in about fifty different ways. Rocko, feel free to take control, I've got to run to the bathroom.
Rocko: Women, anyway Chris, it looks like it's just the camera and myself to finish this one up. It is no secret that you are something special in the world of wrestling, your presence is always felt and even though you claim to be the original Degenerate, I am not one to question that remark. Whether or not your the most original man alive is well beyond me because I believe and many others will tell you the same is that Genetics are passed on from generation, nobody is original, everyone is made up of other people, aren't they? I know I will see Titanicus in your corner and that proves to me that somehow, and someway he will get involved, now, I could call for a steel cage match but I'm afraid you'll just run out the door and take an easy trip back to the top of the NEFW. So, we'll keep this one a standard match. I know
you've been involved in many main events in the NEFW, and the APW, and other feds I'm sure and it must feel pretty awkward to be out of the spotlight and not have a title match. Well, get use to it because you think that the ball is in my court well your wrong, if I lose to you I am the same wrestler I was before, however, if you lose to me all means of a comeback are on their way down the toilet. To tell you the truth
Chris, I have been waiting for this chance, and I've been waiting for a long time. The winner is a definite candidate for the World Title shot and is likely to win no matter the outcome of you or myself. We could throw in that stipulation, the winner gets a title shot but it's not our call, so bug LAD if you want the shot, my shot will come sooner or later. And
Chris, I here ....
(Tracy Moore comes back into sight) Tracy: Did you miss me? (Tracy plants a fat kiss on the lips of Rocko) Rocko: Just a minute hun, I've got to finish... (A large man walks into the picture, he holds a paper that reads managers license for one match.)
Rocko: Who are you? (The man removes his hood but Rocko blocks the camera mans sight and begins to speak) Rocko: OH MY GOSH!!! I HAVEN'T SEEN YOU SINCE, since, well, in a real long time? (The man embraces his hand towards Rocko and they each give a firm shake.) MAN: I'd love to stay and chat, but I've got a meeting with one Lord Arthur, whoever that might men.
(The man tosses his hood back on and whistles for a cab, he takes off towards
NEFW studios in Germany)
Rocko: That was quite a surprise, where was I, oh yeah, Chris, well, I forgot what I was going to mention but don't you worry you'll here from me later in the week. I have to address a few others right now. My last two matches were battled efficiently, a win over Backdraft but a disappointing loss to Badboy after the good Dr. stuck his a** where it doesn't belong, you'll here more from me later. Now, a young man by the name of Johnny Rocka is my last encounter before NF2, come armed, come dangerous, and come prepared, I've got a special treat just for you in honor of a few old friends.
(camera fades to black) ------- [Chris keeps his cool and seels, sends, and delivers] ( Chris Douglas and Titanicus are in the NEFW video room, reviewing Friday
Night Fusion. ) Smat: Chris Douglas has a difficult time finishing off The Fisherman. He better improve his skills if he expects to get a win over Rocko Johnson who had a great match previous.
( Titanicus stops the video. ) Titanicus: It seems you have a little ring rust there Chris? Chris Douglas: You have so much to learn, Titanicus. You see, I always work a decent match whether I'm up against a main eventer or indeed Johnny Nobody or anyone else they throw in my way, including this Fisherman dude. That's why everyone seems to want to get me into the ring. Because I no longer feel the need to embarass someone, but rather choose to help them look good, no matter how inept they are.
Titanicus: I see, so you could have finished him off at any time, but you choose to make it look good for your millions of admirers, and at the same time ensure you're on the TV as much as possible.
Chris Douglas: Precisely. But I'm somewhat disturbed by those comments by
Smat. Titanicus: I'm somewhat more disturbed by his name. Chris Douglas: Well anyway, I'm supposed to be improving my skills? Are you kidding me? Rhetorical question, don't bother answering that. Anyway, you think I should be flashing more huh? So this, being the fifth flash within a week, all of them close to, if not up to my usual standard and length, and you want me to work harder? Give me a goddamn break. I'll work harder as soon as all the other lazy good for nothing idiots do. When everyone else is throwing out more than one flash a day, I'll think about doing the same. But right now, as it is, I'm working my ass off for this company. So enough of this trivial garbage, and on to business. Rocko Johnson, we meet in just a few days now.
So desperate is he to give the execs a reason for him to win that he feigns having been the boxing champion of Germany. It's funny, cause Titanicus was around Germany about that time. Remember when
Rocko Johnson was the champ?
Titanicus: Who? Chris Douglas: Precisely. Rocko, I've done a lot of low-down dirty things in my time, and I admit that. I'm the original Degenerate, remember. The original Misfit. But I have NEVER, EVER claimed to be something I am not, and you can bet your ass that I never ever will. You can show up with doctored up pictures of you on the cover of magazines that don't even exist in Germany if you want. You can go right ahead and pretend you beat up some boxer and took his title. But Rocko, I'd be careful when you pretend you're a better wrestler than Chris Douglas. The bottom line is you're not. Sure, I've been beaten in the past, and I'll probably get beat up again, many times before I call it a day. But when push comes to shove, if you pardon the pun, I'm the man who can get it done. And whether it's taken me one, or even more attempts, I don't stop until I'm happy with what I've acheived. So, let's just go along with your little fictional story, and let's say you did win that little meaningless boxing title exactly ten years ago. Well, this time round, it's going the other way, pal. You'll be falling from grace,
Rocko, on the anniversary of what you seem to consider as being your biggest and best acheivement. And let's just hope I'm not in a bad mood on that day, or you could be forced out of this business and into hospital for a while.
Although that might not be such a bad idea, cause you can use some of the free time to figure out exactly what irony is. It seems that having two matches ten years apart is irony? My god, what are they teaching in schools these days. No Rocko, you faultering at this hurdle would be as close to irony as we're going to get with that kind of situation. And can you honestly say that the Ringmaster has shut me up Rocko? If I was shut up, surely I wouldn't be out here talking right now? So, I didn't say anything for a week? That was where the problems came in. At that time I wasn't flashing elsewhere either, pal. Rocko, if you can honestly stand there and say that you think you can shut me up, then you are quite frankly wasting valuable flash time that could quite easily be filled with intelligent Megacorp propoganda. So you think you can beat me in a wrestling match using your boxing skills? Man, you've got to be kidding me. What are you going to do, Rocko? While the ref is reprimanding you for trying to use closed fists, I'll be hitting you with my unparalleled technical skills, coupled with the style the Future Warrior taught me while we were taking on all comers to defend our Anthracite titles. You see Rocko, every match is a learning experience for me. I might have been around in various places for what might seem like forever, but trust me, I'm quite happy to learn something new. And if that's how to beat up a boxer, fine, so be it.
Born yesterday, Rocko, of course I wasn't. Clearly what I said went straight over your head, so I'll reiterate. For you, Rocko, this is the match you've been waiting your whole life for. For me, Rocko, it's just another match. I've had a couple of weeks to prepare for this, that's it. If you think that's an excuse, go ahead and think that, cause I really don't give a damn. Yet you accuse me of regurgitating previously gone over information, and you're the one who's guilty of it. That's irony, Rocko. You stand there giving your lectures on genetics and so on, but the fact of the matter is I've never claimed on being the most original man alive. Merely that whenever you see a heat generating angle run in your other televised federations, the chances are more often than not, I've played the angle myself. Rocko, don't give me trivial whines about me being upset about it being a title match. Let's go back to a mid-January, when John Connors decided to have something to say on the weekender...
( Titanicus hits play on the video. ) John: ...However it would appear that you have not been given the opportunity to wrestle for a singles title, so I AM giving you the chance to wrestle for any belt you want. As soon as there is a window in your calendar I will sign your name for the match.
( Titanicus stops the video. ) Chris Douglas: Rocko, that was me that John Connors was talking about. I earned my shot, and it was told that I was to get the world title shot before this pay-per-view. But, with so many other things going on, I turned down that shot, Rocko. So don't give me any crap about me shedding tears over how important this match really is to me. I'm already a definite contender for a world title run, Rocko. You're not. You see, we'll see how the result of this match affects me, Rocko. If I win, I'll be shot back to the top, but if
I lose, Rocko, as unlikely as that sounds, don't count on my career being down the toilet, because you can bet your life it won't be. Bring along your phoney manager if you like. Bring as much ammunition as you like. Bring the cavalry, anything you want. The match is a singles match, Rocko. One-on-one.
You have all the people you want in your corner, cause the fact of the matter is that I, all by myself, am going to beat you.
Titanicus: It seems he's already getting a case of the nerves. Chris Douglas: Forgetting what you were going to say, Rocko? Hmm, shaking already? Rocko, you don't need to worry, I'll make sure you look better than you really are in the match. But just you remember one thing, Rocko. I'm making you a star. That's right, pal. Since you're feuding with me, your flashing has picked up somewhat. You throw out a ton of stuff about me, and then one quick mention of everything else going on in your career at the end of it. You see Rocko, I've always flashed like this, so I'm responsible for any improvement that anyone's seen in your flashes in the last week. I'm enabling you to do better Rocko. That's just how nice I am. And I'm even going to allow Titanicus to talk about his upcoming match now since I'm in such a good mood.
Titanicus: Vell, it seems that Magma talks. Never have I seen such a big mouth of such a leetle man. You requested a rematch, I just found it too amusing to comment upon at that time. I thought you vere joking, like the joke that you are. But unbelievably you were not kidding around. The match has been signed. You must now face the music leetle man. And the music can't be too good from where you're standing, and trust me Magma, at the end of this match you vill not be standing. I am Titanicus, the biggest man in the history of the NEFW. Your petty insults did nothing, Magma, but now they're beginning to infuriate me. Do you honestly vant me mad in an extreme match?
I've beaten better than you, Magma, and in fact I've beaten you. You von't be redeeming yourself, Magma, you'll be preparing to go to your grave. The inevitable resting place of all who face Titanicus. Going back to the theory of irony, it's funny that you should be planning this as the year of your leetle team, when in fact it's the year of your ultimate demise.
Chris Douglas: Life insurance is something I'd be looking into if I were called Magma right now. Anyway, we have just a couple more situations we address before we hit send on this baby. First of all a couple of potential rematches that I'd like to see for myself. Since Magma and pretty much everyone else around here is entitled to redeem themselves, as should I be.
So I'd like to request a couple of rematches. Firstly against the Ringmaster. A tag team member. An adequate tag team wrestler, but nothing more than that. He's no singles competitor, and yet he was put over me. Ridiculous.
That's the first thing that'll change. And next on my agenda of rematches is against the Rude Dude. Rude, you're good, and you beat me, but I can beat you, and that's something I intend on proving to the whole world. Don't get me wrong, execs, I'm taking my match against Rocko Johnson seriously. And I'm not spreading myself thinly. But you know I'm better than having one match lined up. I deserve more, and you people know that too. And last, and certainly not least is the Megacorp situation. Quite why they're running a tournament to declare their champion is beyond me, since I'm their last recognised champion. But anyway, they've always done me right in the past, and it's in my belief that they will again.
Brandon Charm, I urge you to once again join me in what will undoubtably be the strongest, and most profitable force around here.

Rocko: Well Chris Douglas, your big mouth is running again. I see you've brought up an interesting point, my match against Freshauf, well, your pretty good at playing VCR's but you know what, I just happen to have a little tape in hand.

YOU THOUGHT FRESHAUG WENT DOWN HARD WAIT TILL YOU SEE HOW FAR I KNOCK YOUR ASS!!! (Tracy gets out of her chair and grabs Rocko by the shoulders, he calms down and takes a sip of a glass of water. He then continues to speak)

This anniversary will not be shot down, maybe in ten years I will be in ultimate fighting or something, but right now, I'm a wrestler with a boxing background, I have numerous weapons you don't know what to expect, I might come out and box your ass right over the top rope and let you take a ten count, but, that would be to easy, or, I could not throw a single punch and out wrestle you, yes you heard me right, out wrestle you, beat you at your own game, is it possible, damn right it is!!! Only a few more days Douglas, and you'll have a whole new outlook on life, wrestling, and me. You think you've changed me, well take a look in the mirror, I've completely changed you!!!
