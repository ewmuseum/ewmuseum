-=(SWL)=- You like Violent Rp's?

5 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(I had a picture here but some people are not accepting them things, not to mention any names KRAZE!)
(Backstage at the Supremer Awards ceremony. The Hostile Takeover is ripping every thing apart. Lockers are fallen, tables split n two and the SWL walls are criss crossed with black spray paint. Iron Man Steve Gorecki takes a cardboard cut out of Tony Arnold and gives it a devastating football tackle and also giving it a huge bend. Gravedigger takes an SWL Program and sets in on fire. Hardcore is turning over popcorn and soda venders, Da Possinater is smashing windows with a poolstick. The camera zooms into Da Possinater.)
Da Possinater - Well, it looks like the Hostile Takeover has gotten screwed again. The SWL awards ceremony was the funniest thing that I have ever seen.
All the nominees went to schmucks like Harry Heiny and all that. Manager of the year? It will be going to some chick that I have never heard of. How could I not be nominated. I am the only manager that actually did something for my man. I have given beatings out with poolsticks, hit people with chairs, and won the mWo, Midget World Order Title in the very tough and grueling battle royal. Who knows when we will get the appreciation that we deserve, who knows when we will catch our break, and who knows if we will ever stop getting screwed by the president. Tony Arnold should thank up for the ratings that we have brought him on TV. We are the real reason why people actually watch this junk. See this poolstick right here Arnold, I had it specially made for you, see, perfectly smooth for now, but soon it will be indented with your faceprint. Me and you Arnold, if you aren't that much of a chicken, I say that you get you <bleep> in the ring with me. I am just a poor little 7'2" manager!
Get out your tights, put you tutu on, and please put on a mask, and then when you are don with ballet, you can come and face me!
(Iron Man walks in) Iron Man Steve Gorecki- You heard the man, he ain't joking either. Well, it seems like every other SWL jobber got nominated for an award except me! Over there in the SWL Committees little puny minds, they have it switched around. I should be the only one who gets nominated, and why? Because I should be champ if it wasn't for them cheating me out of it in the first place. I should be holding that US Title, but nope, Hostile Takeover can't win a belt because it must be against their <bleeping> religion! Well, I am sick of working my rearend out here to get screwed every week. So when I face my opponents, they will get what they paid for. SWL members will start dropping like flies. When you are visiting them in the hospital, just remember that it was you who is at fault. Hostile Takeover will takeover your life. We will be around every corner, everyday haunting you. So I suggest you do what's right before we give you a little surprised beating! I haven't had a chance to play my role in the
SWL yet because I have had these presidents down my throat for the past one and a half months. It is time for me to break lose and take out my revenge on the rest of you <bleep> heads out there. So now lets see who I face this week!
Gravedigger- Hey guys, they are about to read off the matches for the week. Turn on ESPN! Set up the TV....grabs some beer and lets partay! (Hardcore brings out a little TV and places it on a table. Iron Man sets up four chairs and Da Possinater gives everyone a beer. They all sit down and have their eyes glued to the TV set! Jim Ross is talking at a SWL house show and the matches start to scroll in the bottom line. They wait patiently, sipping down their brew.)
-=(Opening Bout)=- -=(Gravedigger Vs. Kevin Walters)=- (They all start to laugh hysterically giving a fake beat down to Gravedigger because he drew a nobody!)
Gravedigger- I have it made in the shade fellas. Read it and weep! (Start to watch screen again) -=(Main Evevnt For SWL World Brawler Title)=- -=(Cassaday is Champion)=- -=(Hardcore Vs. Cassaday)=- (Every cheers real happily. Hardcore stands up and almost knocks over the table! He starts to speak!)
Hardcore- There we go boys! The first Hostile Takeover belt will be arriving in no time! I have to face Cassaday, a nobody in my books. He ain't what he used to be! Brawler title, appropriately named because we are the best in the business!
-=(Singles Action Non Title Match)=- -=(Kraze is Champion)=- -=(Steve Gorecki Vs. Kraze)=- (They start yelling at the TV set trying to get at Jim Ross. (Not too loaded?) The four of them start to boo and holler. Gorecki starts talking!) Iron Man Steve Gorecki - What the <bleep> is this <bleep>? I fight for my paycheck and now I get a non title match against Kraze? Supremer Strap, it means that the person who holds it must be the best that the fed has to offer.
Well, that role is given to me but I can't get it because only past champions can get a shot at it. I am the true Supremer of this federation and everyone knows it. What's the difference, I will probably get jobbed out of this match like all the others that Tony Arnold is up to. He is trying to wipe me off the face of this planet....well, I ain't leavin! I ain't leavin until I give out my share of beatings and hold a belt for a period of time. I will be in the SWL
Hall of Fame sooner or later, but I can't do it with the president out to get me! Now on to this Kraze deal! I joined the fed with a no name stable like the IHM was it? I didn't know what I was doing because never did I turn on the
TV to see what kind of antics were going on in the SWL. Kraze, the name said it all, so I joined, not because I wanted to, but I knew that it would be easy to take over, easy for me to take charge of the stable. I swept past my first opponent with ease....and you were stunned. You kept on losing so I said screw it! Kraze, you were on your own...me and Cassaday flew the coop and the loon was stuck without a friend by himself! Kraze needed support so he went to this
Harry Heiny fella and made friends with the goof! I got screwed by the president for the belt so I came to the Hostile Takeover and that is where we stand today. You say that I don't know your fighting style and all that?
Well, I was training with you at one point and that's why I left, because I was not in one way or another impressed with your wrestling ability. How you held a belt before that was beyond me! You couldn't keep up with the young 'un!
The old man better hang it up sooner or later! Kraze, this match might be your last, it ain't lookin pretty. You will be stepping in the way of a disgruntled
SWL employee, and we got a whole stable full of them! Da Possinater will be ringside, he wants a few shots at you after what you said about him! We here at Hostile Takeover are sick of your fat mouth talking about nothing. Maybe you should write programs for a new Seinfeld shows because that's what you are about....you are about nothing. You make no sense, slurred speech, and most of the time you sound loaded. That isn't any way to wrestle!
(TV goes to Kraze's interview! They watch the whole thing and laugh every few seconds at his comments. Here is the thing that Gorecki had to say between each line.)
Kraze - Well now on to Steve Gorecki. Well, this first is a NEW EPOCH for me as well as the sWo, this new era Gorecki is none of that cocky <bleep> and I am just going to tell it as is, work hard, and train right, and be prepared to win at my matches.
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - What the <bleep> is a new Epoch? See, he's loaded before he even wrestles! He must be in lala land right now! Did that line make any sense at all, anyone? Is he talking about himself or me? I do work, train and prepare hard. Look at this body, does it not look like I do all of the above? So the correct answer is D...all of the above!
Kraze - And I am on a losing streak and I will not lose 3 in a row. I have no idea what has happened to me and I am not going to have it happen again.
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Yeah you are on a huge losing streak! He can't even beat the jobbers that he has been facing, how does he expect to beat me? Kraze is right that it will never happen again because he will never be able to come out of this slump. It will keep getting worse and worse!
Kraze - So also with the rest of the new period I am just going to be the same old KRaZe as I used to be before I left the SWL for the first time. And Gorecki you weren't here for most of it and I know it was the best of times for me and the worst of times for the 7 wrestlers I beat.
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - He should have never came back! He was scared of the
Iron Man then, and now he is even more frightened, lets hope that I have a chance to give him a beating this time! 7 Wrestlers he beat, yeah, that doesn't include video games now! Take the game level off beginner and get a real challenge!
Kraze - Gorecki you dont know what is going to happen because you never faced me but I know you will be ready, so the only way for me to win is to be as or more ready than you. Gorecki I got to admit you are the only raw talent to come out of the SWL powerplant since probably Cassaday.
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Yeah, I am as pumped and ready as I will ever be! You better try to get, try being the keyword, try to get better than me. But can anyone come over the wall to that level? It has never happened and won't stop now. The next generation is here baby, my talent has overcame yours so admit it! I hated them drill sargeants in the Powerplant, so bad that I kicked the
<bleep> out of them!
Kraze - And I am going to be ready, criticize me all you want, I have 3 loses out of 12 my whole career and you have 1 lose out of 3. I have been down this road before and Ive seen people better than you and I have beaten them. It hasn't been hard Steve but I did and I am NOT going to stop after 2 loses in a row for you or anybody for that matter.
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Hes not going to stop losing he says? Fine with me! Try all you want, but I will have to pound you into the ground! Kraze - Gorecki you have not faced anyone at my caliber, sure you faced MD but he doesn't have the experience I have and doesn't have the sense I have yet.
And Gorecki, he beat you. And now as I have matured after these loses, I will beat you. You dumbt my status as Supremer Strap, well dumbt me if you will but when you see me in the ring for the first time...then you will dumbt me no longer and you will be shown first hand that KRaZe is Supremer Strap Champion for a reason!
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Isn't that special. He is holding the belt like his son! Look at the man, he has totally lost it! Give it up Kraze, you will never beat me and that's that bud! The sWo is counting on you Kraze, you are holding the whole team on your shoulders. They don't want a leader that is 1-3 and still with a belt. We call them kind of people losers in Hostile Takeover land. Still waiting for the shot "Arrogant" Anthony! You can hold the belt as long as you keep facing jobbers, but the second that you face an experienced wrestler it will all come crashing down. Now you have a match against this
Night character, another SWL pile of <bleep> they will throw at you and another win. But no need to worry because you will step in the ring with the Iron Man real soon!
Da Possinater - The Kid, who is this guy now? He wants a beating too? Lets see, that will be Tony, Kraze, and the Kid who all want some of the action. I will lay a hand on Kraze, so bring it over to my side of the ring! I was the former WCW Tag Team Champion, former WWF Television Champion and now the Midget
World Order title holder. Bring the partay to Da Possinaters house because I am the real manager of the year around here! Bring a chair if you want to play that way. The Kid better grow up and know who he is dealing with. The little man will just be like the people I faced in the Midget World Order battle royal, and that is a statistic! I will guarantee that there will be two hits between us, the first is me hitting you and the second is you hitting the ground!
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Hey Cassaday, hand over the belt to Hardcore right now because he is on a mad tear in his matches. In the Hostile Takeover warehouse he has destroyed his opponents. Through the barbwire and on tables, these men have all suffered. Former allies my <bleep>, you are nothing but a cheater and a sore loser! 2 times you should've lost that belt but you had to bring out the old metal chair and smack my buddy Gravedigger over the head with it. If I see you try anything like that, then I will come down there to give you a little delivery. Hardcore wants the belt more than anything else. He will do anything for it, which means that you could get seriously hurt. And to let you guys know out there, never count the Iron Man out because I always have something up my sleeve.....so BRING IT! Turn that camera off right now!
