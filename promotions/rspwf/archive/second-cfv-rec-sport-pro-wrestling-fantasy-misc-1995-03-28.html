CHARTER REC.SPORT.PRO-WRESTLING.FANTASY will be an unmoderated group dedicated to participents of fantasy wrestling. Its purpose is to provide a public forum for play-by-E-mail (PBeM) leagues and public fantasy leagues to post their FAQs, events, updates, interviews and statistics. The following is a rough list of what should be included in the new group: Should: - Wrestling reports (weekly taping results, "supercards", etc.) - Administrative stuff, notes to members, etc. - New league creation - Advertisement for existing leagues RATIONALE It is felt that this new group is necessary because of the current signal-to-noise ratio on the parent rec.sport.pro-wrestling group, of which one factor is the amount of fantasy posts which really deserve their own space in USEnet. REC.SPORT.PRO-WRESTLING.MISC will be a renamed form of the current parent group REC.SPORT.PRO-WRESTLING. It has long been felt that a split of the parent group was in order, and renaming the main group is a first step in accomplishing just that.
