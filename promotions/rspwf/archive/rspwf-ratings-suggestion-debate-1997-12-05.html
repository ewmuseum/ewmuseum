RSPWF High Ups... A Suggestion for the Group

5–13 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[5 December 1997 — Funklord] Hey there every body (said in real obnoxious radio guy tone)... Name's [name withheld], aka Funklord aka The Lord of Funk aka Klee the highly uncordinated... Former (And possible FUTURE) president of MCW, handler of the Super Duper Ultra Successfull/Snazzy dressing/Really neat-o tag team Crusher Tech and up and comer "Fantasy" Kevin Legend... Yadda, yadda, yadda... Let's get to the point shant we?.... I think we shant... Ok, I've been in e-wrestling for a little over a year now (Probably something like 16 months or something... anyway...), and I am a popular idler on IRC's EFNet #e-wrestling channel... Hey, If I don't idle, I lose my connection and I have to try for another half hour to get through the busy signals when I get back from work... give me a break :) Anyway, this afternoon on #e-wrestling, myself and a friend of mine [name withheld] (Roadmutt) came up with a brain storm... I'm sure this has been thought of sometime before, but we think it's a great idea, and could be fun and or informative for everyone... We propose a sort of e-wrestling ratings show... Here's how I described it simply on #e-wrestling... "<VF_Legend> I don't think it'd be to hard... Grab a handfull of people, say 10-12 to just go through and read all the cards posted on RSPW, rate them, give helpfull hints, and at the end of the week post a ranking of the cards, and the feds, (The feds due to the quality of this weeks and past weeks cards/consistancy...) I think it'd be a GREAT idea..." Ok, there's the schpiel in a nutshell... Let's break it down shall we? Oh, I think we shall... TONS of feds post on RSPWF... most of the time (Probably 90% of the time) they go un-noticed and deleted within the first few seconds that they recieve it... Well, in most of these cases... they're probably right in doing it... But why not give everyone a little help? A little constructive criticism and something to shoot for... It's all good fun... Now what I was looking for in way of "Staff" I guess you could call it... I was hoping to get about 6-7 "Readers" from here on RSPWF, Preferably regulars, preferably with some sort of e-wrestling experience and or fed head experience, to go through the cards, read them through and give me a grade for the card/fed, and probably a write up on the card/fed... Possibly some sort of article in what ever my Weekly update is going to look like... So basically another pre-requisite is going to be a little bit of free time :) Then with the 5-6 "Readers" from RSPWF I was going to employ possibly another 3-4 from #e-wrestling, to give a variety of view points... I was going to assemble all the reviews into a Weekly (Maybe bi-weekly) Update sort of thing... Sending out our "Official E-Wrestling Ratings" on cards, matches, fueds, wrestlers, tags, feds... and what ever else comes up... feds may even be able to send little blurbs in as ads... It'll basically come out looking like a e-zine when it's all said and done... So, PLEASE everyone tell me what you think about this idea... If it gets a good reaction then we'll do it, if you hate the idea... Well, I'll cry for a few days... *sniff* but I'll get over it... :) Later people... Funklord ------------------------ [13 December 1997 — [name withheld]] What!!! Get some people to read every e-fed and then publicly post constructive criticism. That sounds like a lot of work for no good reason. Here's why. First, with the abundance of e-feds, people will have to either give up their "real" lives and their e-fed lives just to read all of them. Second, there is no better way to make someone defensive than to offer public criticism. Third, lots of these feds will fold up immediately because there is a surplus of mediocre and lousy feds, so many of these people will go back to e-wrestling, learn the trade, and try again with more success if they have the time. Having said this, I would like to offer a suggestion for those who would like to improve the quality of marginal e-feds. Browse through cards when you can and offer to help (or ask the fed head to ask for help) promising feds and fed heads get on the right track. Do this by e-mailing your opinions to the fed head/booker in private so he/she may heed your advice without losing face or (Heaven Forbid!!!) credit someone else for an idea.
