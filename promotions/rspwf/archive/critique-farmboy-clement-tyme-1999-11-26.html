Critique {"Farmboy" Clement Tyme}

26 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my new character. He's supposed to be a naive twenty year old. I tried to make him into a 1980s heel, except as a cruiserweight. Tell me what you think.
[The scene fades into the view of the country roads of Florida as seen from the inside of an old beaten up Chevy pickup. The VwA Miniature Tyrant Championship has been lain on the dashboard. The camera turns from the road to the new VwA
MT champion, Clement Tyme. Tyme is wearing a pair of faded blue jeans and a stained white T-shirt. The cameraman and Tyme ride along in silence for a few minutes until out of nowhere, Tyme breaks the silence.]
Clement Tyme - Well, we're almost to my house. We can't stay long, I want to get up to Conneticut early, so my flight is in a couple of hours. I can't wait, it's the first time I've ever left the state of Florida. At least I have
Mr. Williams to watch out for me while I'm up there. I'm so excited about winning this belt and later on taking it back from that guy Carter after he stole it. Well, I guess I'll try to get me revenge on Carter, if Parkman gives me any chance against him. We're here.
[Tyme's old red pickup is turning down an old dirt road, tracks well worn into the ground, and seperated by a few short weeds which have grown over the years.
On both sides, the truck is surrounded by orange groves. The road seems to go on forever into the oranges, but the road turns off to the right and a huge three-story farmhouse can be seen towering over the groves of oranges. Several other old farm trucks can be seen lining the clearing where the house has been built. The house is extremely well taken care of, as the house looks clean and new. Several of Tyme's brothers and sisters are playing on the swings and riding bikes in the front yard. His mother, a rather stout woman, who is wearing a sundress, is laying in a hammock reading Cosmo when her first-born arrives. No father is present. When Clement Tyme gets out of the beaten up pickup, he picks up the MT Title and rushes to join his siblings. After Tyme has hugged and kissed each and every one, all of the children notice the TV camera and run over and start waving and saying hi to all of their friends.
The cameraman side-steps the children and quickly catches up to Tyme who has wandered over to his mother.]
Tyme - Hey, mom! I won! I beat that guy in the ring with the "Orange Squeezer" that Mr. Williams taught me. That Serj guy never even mounted an offensive move. I really don't have much time, so I just came home to tell you
I won and talk to all of my new fans before I leave for Conneticut.
Mrs. Tyme - You are going to Conneticut? That's an awful long way away! How are you ever going to drive there in time for your match?
Tyme - Mother, I'm not going to drive! Mr. Williams said he'd fly me up there. I'll be safe mom. Mr. Williams would never let anything happen to his number one draw. I'll be back down here shortly, as we have to come back down here to wrestle in Miami-Dade on Saturday, when I have my first title defense. Now, mom, I'm gonna go around back and do an interview.
Mrs. Tyme - Okay, I'll keep your little brothers and sisters out of your hair. [Clement Tyme walks around the corner of the house as he is followed by the cameraman. The camera is greeted by a flowerbed, carefully weeded by loving hands and sculpted by Mother Nature. Tyme sits down on a wooden bench which would overlook a flowered bed during the spring and summer. Tyme, still carrying his Miniature Tyrant Championship belt around, carefully places it down next to him on the bench. He runs his fingers through his short red hair before he says a word.]
Tyme - On Friday, I became the first ever MT Champion. During my match, I overheard a play-by-play man mention my father. A word of advice to you, do not mention my father ever again. It's very messy and I don't want to get into it, so I repeat, do not mention my father or my relationship with my father ever again. Now, onto the thing I love, wrestling. I've got a match on
Wednesday and again on Saturday, so I would say I have a very full week. On
Wednesday, I have been teamed up with a man I respect, but do not all together trust. Parkman, I am your partner for this match, and it's only because you and I both want to get back at Carter. Obviously, you and I scare Carter, he hasn't had a nationally telecasted interview for this match yet. This other guy, Blake Feldman just freaks me out. I don't know what he's been smoking as the say, but I'll bet it's the same thing that Malakian guy is. Rex, he's talking about all this stuff he's going to do to you and I, but I want Feldman to remember that I am faster, more agile and have more heart to win than he does.
[Tyme stands up and stretches a little bit, and then picks up his title and walks down a little cobblestone walkway that leads deep into the orange groves on the Tymes' property. Tyme leads the cameraman past many orange trees and onto the banks of a little pond, surrounded by many orange trees. This pond seems to be the perfect place to spend a summer afternoon, as it provides shade, food, water, and a certain privacy. Tyme squats down next to the pond and skips rocks into the pond before standing up and facing the camera.]
Tyme - After Wednesday, comes my Saturday match. I have been forced to wrestle a number. This guy must be new, because I didn't see him wrestle on Malicious
Friday Night. But I think I'm ready for him. I've heard that this One is a mat wrestler. That's good, because mat wrestling is my second favorite thing.
That's after flying of course. Well One, you seem to wrestle the same way I do, except I don't miss many of my high-flying moves. I can wrestle you on the mat, but I don't want to. I want to make you fly. I want you to try and counter me, the MT Champion. You need to come up with an answer to me, not vice versa. I don't need to beat you, but I will. I don't need to fly to win this match, as I can wrestle on the mat better than you can, I learned from the best. Although I've never met you, I've heard a lot about you. You seem to be all Mr. Williams was talking about on the phone the other day. I guess it's up to you now to try to take away something I've worked hard for. I guess I'll have to wait for you to talk to me.
[Tyme looks down at his watch and notices he has spent more time than he has planned at his home and grabs the cameraman and starts to run. The camera is bobbing up and down as the cameraman tries desperately to keep up with the fast running Tyme. The man finally realizes it would be easier to run without the camera on his shoulder and turns it off and we fade out to a Clash of the
Titans commercial.]
