[Logan Gunz (CWO, NWC, CWC)] Sample RP

20 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just thought I'd get a little of my work out for everyone to see. (The scene fades in on Logan Gunz. He stands in the middle of a large field WAY out in the boonies, behind him is a black Porsche 911 Carrera.
Logan is dressed similar to how he always is, blue bandana, blue jeans, black gloves, t-shirt with something catchy like "Logan Gunz" on the front and "Porsche Sux, Buy American" on the back. He is about shin deep in grass and weeds, why is Logan here??? Just like Porsche....it's point proving time...)
Logan: I always knew you were an arrogant S.O.B. Porsche, but with some of your recent statements, you've proved you're ignorant as well. No BIG surprise there.
(The wind picks up a little and begins to whip the grass and trees that surround Logan.) Logan: Let's see....where to begin. First the statement that you know you'll beat me....confidence is good Porsche, all great wrestlers have it, but that is sheer lunacy. You don't think so??? Let's review the facts. Porsche lost like the first five matches of his career, some names that come to mind real quick??? Porsche and Penny, I think that's enough said right there. Though I have never personally had the displeasure of stepping through the ropes with Penny.....I did have a nice encounter with Predator. The whole CWO watched in terror as I
Shotgun Pressed him from the top rope, possibly ending his career.
Perhaps you better check up on him Porsche.....reconstructive surgery on your face can be a [Beep]......
(Logan continues to stand in the middle of the field, the wind has died down a little now. A fawn can be seen at the edge of the field, staring in amazement at the commotion oon his usual feeding grounds. Such a gentle creature......)
Logan: Porsche....as far as your comments regarding the LE go...once again your stupidity shines through the darkness of fact. Check the record Porsche, have the LE ever interfered in another LE members match??? Simple answer..NO! You wonder what the LE will say if I lose the TV title?? I'll tell you what my comrades will say....plain and simple..."Logan, go kick his ass!!!" The Lethal Enforcers are about one word, and that word isn't "titles" Porsche, that's what you're all about. The LE is about "R-E-S-P-E-C-T", something you know absolutely nothing about. These titles are not the goal of the Lethal Enforcers, they're not why we were formed, we just happen to have collected a few along the way......
(Logan turns his back and begins walking toward the black automobile in the background, as he walks he continues talking)
Logan: Next thing Porsche informs the world of is that the LE hates him....."it's a proven fact". Where is this so-called proof of yours???
I hate to burst your bubble Porsche....but the LE doesn't really give a damn about you. Face doesn't care, Night doesn't care, Hunter and
Jammin' REALLY don't care.....only I do. I decided that it was time for someone to knock your head out of the clouds....Porsche screamed about the titles he was going to win....and I got sick to my stomach hearing it. The only place your skills are "unparalleled" is in the category marked "PATHETIC". Think about it Porsche, if you pin me you'll justify your existance in the CWO, if I pin you......you're just another piece of trash sent out with the rest of the garbage. You've got nothing to lose here Porsche.....
(Logan finally reaches the black Porsche, he opens the door and begins fumbling around the back seat....)
Logan: Watch the tapes Ice, see what you're getting into, see the power and devastation that goes along with the Shotgun Press.....and prepare yourself accordingly. A week from Thursday.....be ready to be blasted!!!!
(Logan comes out of the back of the Porsche with a Mossberg 12-gauge pump shotgun. He step about twenty five feet back and takes aim. The first shot goes directly through the windshield and shatters it. The second shot cracks the engine block, sending smoke billowing up into the air. The thirds and final shot pierces the gas tank.....the Porsche explodes and sends a HUGE fireball straight up in the air. The car sits there ablaze and Logan turns to the camera.)
Logan: See what a Shotgun can do to a Porsche???? Now that's sending a message!!!!! (A strange grin comes across Logan's face as he stares at the inferno.....fade to black) [name withheld] CWO Board Member Handler of Logan Gunz CWO TV Champion GRW Georgia State Champion
