Well, this has to be my best roleplay since my return, tell me whatcha think. Am I getting better or should I slip back into the shadows or retirement? heh =o) [name withheld] [Fade in.....] [SCENE: Philidelphia, Pennsylvania. Scene opens up looking out over the "City of Brotherly Love" spanned round, looking at all that Philly has to offer until it comes to "The Hammer" Alex Cote standing with his arms are his sides lookg out to the city.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Philidelphia, the town I was concieved, borned, and raised in. Everyone once and a while, when I have some free time, I like to come up to this rof and look over this wonderful city. When you travel as much as me, it's good to remember where you come from. This city loves and me and I love this city. I've been here the last few days training, visiting old friends, preparing myself mentally and physically for my match this Monday, this coming Mayhem, for my IWF/WOW North American title match!
[Alex sighs and turns toward the camera.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: I told you Redding, I told you the time would finally come when you would have to face me again, I told you we would stand across the squared circle for a second time! But this time, you have to put that belt of yours on the line! You avoiding it last time, you got yourself a nontitle match with me andlast time I buried your ass! But this time.....this time I've got you, I've got you for that belt and this time thereb is no way around it,
NO WAY OUT because I will not stop pounding, kicking, slaming, smashing, pulzerizing, KILLING you until I walk away from that ring with the gold!
[Alex runs his right hand through his hair and look down.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: YOU SEE WHAT YOU'VE DONE TO ME?! Everyone knows me as that poppish big guy who always gets a laugh BUT NO MORE! You've done something to me and I don't know what it is! You're little tactics, you're dodging me week after week after WEEK has turned me into a raging machine hell bent on your destruction!
[Alex looks up at the camera with a manical look in his eyes.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: This machine, fueled by rage, finally gets what it needs to calm itself, TO PULL ITSELF FROM THE BRINK OF INSANITY, blood, sweat, pain, and gold, ALL FROM YOU! Trevor Redding, there is no where to run this week! Not you, you little valet, or even god himself will be able to save you this week!
[Alex starts to pace back and forth.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: I've been working myself rampade these last few days, getting myself prepared because after the match, I'LL BE DAMNED if I am getting on that plane home, looking out that window, thinking to myself "You are not the North American champion, you are not the North American champion, YOU ARE
NOT THE NORTH AMERICAN CHAMPION!" So, Redding, if you want to hold onto that
North American championship, you better be able to step up to the plate and BE
READY TO KILL ME! Because you will have to drag my body kicking, screaming, bleeding, and DYING, are you ready to make that sacrifice? Because I am.
[Alex stop pacing and runs up to the camera with his finger pointing towards the lens.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Let me wrap this up, let me finish this whole tyraid! Trevor Redding, Monday, you're era ends so you better prepare to be hammered because.....I TAKE NO PRISONERS!!
[Alex walks away.] [Fade to black.....]
