Axel Schmidt a FREE AGENT (Sample RP enclosed

3 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, this is the handler of Axel Schmidt, I just got into the MVW and I am looking for another RSPWF caliber fed and would appreciate if you would post or just email me at
[detail withheld] and let me know what you think. I would hope to hear from anyone in the UEW, EMWC, WWO, or whatever......but hey I will look at all offers.
Thanks, and here is the rp (The scene opens up outside of the local YMCA in Western New York. A white stretch limo pulls up to the curb and a foot steps out. The man is wearing nice snakeskin shoes and a nice leather coat. It is none other than Axel
Schmidt. Right behind him is Adolf Eichman and a nice looking white women.
The two men begin conversing.) Adolf: Alright, Axel, now this is just something I set up to improve your image and I hope you take it serious. We need this to get you off as a guy who isn’t afraid to give a little back to the kids of America.
Axel: Well, I am not even American. Adolf: You get where I am going though. The point is, just sign a few autographs, pose for a few pictures, and kiss a few babies and it will be just what we’re looking for. I got these cameras here to get this on tape and send it to Franky for the video he is going to put out on you.
Just be cool in here alright? Axel: Alright, I can’t promise, but I can give it a go. Here goes nothing. Come here sweetie.
(The two men and the young woman walk into the youth center, Axel arm in arm with the female. A bunch of kids greet Axel at the door to the gym.
Axel starts off ok.Axel: Hey guys, how are you today? Eight year old: I’m great. I think you are the best wrestler in the MVW. You are almost as good as Stone Cold Steve Austin. Will you sign my t shirt? Axel: Hell no, I am not signing shit for any little mark Stone Cold Steve
Austin punk. You can go have Stone Cold sign your shirt. I am sure he will be happy to sign the damn thing for you. I suppose he will even give you a
Steveweiser or something, good god, you are pathetic.
(Eichman shakes his head. Axel starts looking in other directions as the Austin fan begins to cry. Axel doesn’t pay him much attention and starts signing other shirts, hats, and pictures.) The kids then go to warm up for their junior league basketball game. Axel sits in the front row with the female and attempts to coach. It doesn’t go too well.)
Axel to the female: Man, how did I let him talk me into this? I hate kids, they just aren’t my thing. Dylan Dice would never let this happen, same with Fang or Andy Simmons. These guys don’t need or care about image. Why should I? This is completely stupid.
Female: I think it is cute….it makes you look sexy…..coach……ummmm……. Axel: Let me give coaching a chance though, just so I can say I tried. (A kid throws two bad passes in a row and the score stays at zero to zero for over ten minutes. Axel gets fed up and starts a tirade.)
Axel: What is your problem!!! You kids suck. Give me that damn ball. Let me show you how it’s done. You guys are truly horrible.
(Axel gets the ball and tries a dunk that isn’t even close and he falls down.. The kids begin to laugh at him. Axel gets fed up and goes off. He starts choking one of the parents in the stands that confront him. Adolf pulls him off him and he grabs Adolf and chokes him. He begins to yell at him.)
Axel: Look, I am not changing who I am for you or anyone else. You are just some tired old man that couldn’t cut it in your day. From now on I call the shots. You are gonna listen to and do it my way. No more image change and no more “Axel, the nice guy.” I am gonna be Axel the real man.
If that’s too much for you, then hit the door. Adolf: You don’t need me, fine, but don’t come crawling back when Andy
Simmons or Fang or the Hardbody gets a hold of you. I won’t be there. I am done helping a helpless wrestler that needs me to succeed. Fuck you.(Adolf walks out and the scene fades.)
Later that evening…….. (The scene opens to show Axel Schmidt hitting a bottle of Jack Daniels pretty hard. He is already smashed out of his mind and doesn’t look like he should be doing an interview. He begins a sorry attempt at an interview.)
Axel: Hello, and welcome to the new and improved Axelvision. Tonight I wan tto talk about what happened earlier today and what is going to happen in that battle royal. First, today is behind me. Those kids didn’t deserve my company and they aren’t getting it. And then you have that run down geaser
Adolf Eichman trying to make me a nice guy. That won’t happen anytime soon.
I tried to take his force feeding any more. I am my own man and I am a damn good one at that. This message is to Adolf. If you can get yourself together then maybe I will give you a chance. God knows you need the money.
Now, I want to talk about the important matter of the night. I want to talk about the battle royal. First, I want to address Hardbody Stewart. Son, you had better start to concentrate on me. You have been touring all around and making all these statements about Fang and some others, when you ought to concentrate on me. I am your true worry punk. When you step into a battle royal, you aren’t allowed to look ahead. I have had that problem.
That is what landed me on the outside of wrestling a few months ago. I used to overlook my opponents, sit around talking how I am going to dominate and win this title and that title, but when you start doing that you forget what you have to do to even get to that goal of a title or whatever it is you are pursuing. You are what I used to be, a primetime punk. I have respect for the guys in this matchup like Dylan Dice and Havoc, but you, there is nothing at this point that gives me any reason to respect you. Even after I have had a few drinks tonight I see that you are going about preparation the wrong way. Me getting drunk is no worse then the way you have been getting ready. Spending nights on the town, sleeping with nice looking women, sure it makes you a hardbody, but you won’t be too hard to throw out of that ring come Monday night January the 4th. The national Championship and the right to win a championship are being decided on the same night. Just like FSU is going to come out a winner, so am I. You have gained my attention, but have also brought about my fury. First, the Hardbody gets slammed to the mat, then Fang gets a chunk taken out of him. Fang, you are a great champion, you know that, the people you hang around know that, the whole MVW knows that, but what you must realize is that the winner of this battle royal has the upper hand on you. While you have been sitting there and relaxing, the people in this match have been gearing up to take you out, and don’t think that we won’t go to any lengths to do it. You had better find your way out of that comfort zone and get back into your battle zone because once this match is over, whoever wins is coming right for your head. A big bullseye sitting right on top of you. Someone is bound to make a dent in it. I plan on being the one to do it, but as a newcomer to the MVW, I know it will be tough and I am prepared for what lies ahead. Franky has lined up one hell of a schedule and I plan on making him thank the good lord for the day he signed me. As you have seen today, I am not afraid to shake things up if it brings me to the top. MVW, mark my words, you have seen the dawn of a new day. Love me, hate me, but always be afraid of me, for I am always there.
Good night and thank you for watching Axelvision. I will see all of you
Monday night when I make my first steps into MVW history. Good night.Also that night…….(The scene shows Adolf Eichman picking up the late edition of the local paper. On it is a picture of Axel choking him and making a ugly scene at the YMCA. Adolf makes his way out of the snow covered streets and into his apartment. He reads the article, and then shakes his head. Then he puts the paper down and begins to speak. Just looking at him, you can see the pain in his eyes. The speech is somber, but has a purpose. Camera
Crews caught the interview and got the footage to go along with Axels interview.)
Adolf: Axel Schmidt, I raised you like my own son. I got you into the business of wrestling. I raised you into a man in a sport of men. You won titles because of me, you got rewards because of me, and you got attention because of me. I got you EVERYTHING. You fell hard Axel. Pete beat you like no one has beat you before or since. I saw that and tried to fix what was wrong. You didn’t give me the chance. Already you are going the way of the troubled soul. You have already returned to the ways that got you in trouble. What do you intend to do Axel? Are you going to
“:DOMINATE” wrestling. That worked really well for a while Axel, but there is always someone waiting to knock you off. You would be a beaten man today if I hadn’t saved you from your soul searching. Do you remember how I found you? I found you in a drunken rage, crying over what you COULD have been, or should have been. That wasn’t the Axel I knew, and neither is the one you are becoming once again. I see the light at the end of the tunnel.
Your tunnel has a very dim one just like many who have squandered their careers before you. You can still make it a big shiny light though. You have the chance to redeem yourself Axel. This is your last chance in the wrestling business. If you blow it this time you won’t get another chance.
No one is going to want to see a broken down former champion, as is proof in some other “BIG TIME” promotions. That doesn’t bring in big money there and it won’t bring in big money here. Pull yourself out of the gutter and make a man of yourself again. I ask you to please, just think more of yourself.
You have friends, you have a good career in front of you, and you have me, but you don’t seem to want that. I am going to be there for you, but only if you want me to. If not, then I guess that is it. I will bid farewell to you this one final time, because I am not changing, and you obviously won’t either. I have never asked much of you , and I don’t ask much of you know, but please, don’t blow this. This is a big opportunity for you, and I got it for you. Don’t make a fool out of me, and don’t make a fool out of yourself. That is about all I have to say. Thanks and good luck to you
Axel.
(The scene with Eichman fades out, but a new one fades in, early in the morning hours. Axel is seen talking to a man in a run down alleyway and he seems to have something on his mind. The conversation is taped and reused, but is unauthorized by Axel or any of his management. Here is a portion of that conversation.)
Axel: Hey, I have a little proposition for you. I have been having problems with that little German guy who always tagged along for me, you know who he is. Well anyways, I need to get rid of this guy before I get into this battle royal. He is trouble, and is a big distraction. I want you to…..you know, just put him in his place, and out of commission.
Man: No problem, but like anything, I have a price tag on me. Five grand up front and tw more after I get done. Axel: Alright, here, just make sure he doesn’t know what hit him.. I don’t need the trouble on this coming back later. Here is the money.
(Axel hands the man a large quanity of bills and the man gives him a firm handshake. The scene fades…..)Early morning hours…….
(Adolf Eichman is seen jogging along one of the dimly lit streets by himself. Out of no where a man jumps out of the bushes and starts to attack the man that tutored Axel. The man does a good job of taking care of him and knocks him out cold. The police arrive in a matter of minutes and begin to search the area, but find nothing. Eichman is taken to a local hospital and is later questioned by poloice about the incident. All he could tell them is that he thinks it was Axel Schmidt and that he doesn’t want to press charges and wants to deal with it in his own way. This only leads you to believe that you will see Adolf
Eichman on Monday night at Ring Wars. The scene fades one final time…..leaving everyone to wonder, just what is going to happen on Ring
Wars……)
