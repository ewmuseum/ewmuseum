Flash for critique...

13 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey...this is just sort of an experimental flash-type thing...hopefully, it'll be better than the debacle I wrote for Fury in first person. Let me know what y'all think.
BEGIN FLASH [The scene cuts in, and we see a dark apartment, with all sorts of wrestling paraphernalia strewn about. The camera shot pans about the apartment, and we are able to pick up enough scenery to verify that it is the apartment that we last saw Fury at. Except this time, the champ’s asleep…we sneak into his bedroom, and we see him tossing and turning in his sleep, mumbling something.
It appears that the champ isn’t going to give at interview…suddenly, the screen does a slight dissolve into another scene…all of a sudden, the Doors’
“Love Her Madly” plays over the audio, and we enter a strange
dream-sequence.] [In this sequence, the spotlight is on a VERY bewildered-looking Fury is the middle of an orange plain. Fury looks left and right, and whirls about several times, confused…a figure in black materializes in front of Fury…the figure looks strikingly similar to Fury’s old bodyguard Cyclone. The strange man is hugely tall, and dressed in a black hooded robe. He beckons to Fury with one thick finger.]
J. Morrison: “Don’t you love her madly…” [The figure grabs it’s collar, and throws off the robe, revealing a stunningly beautiful woman. She’s about five-foot ten, and she’s got long brown hair that extends all the way to her knees. Her skin is pale, and there are freckles dotting her nose. She is wearing a diaphanous black dress, but where the dress is thin and we can see through, we see no body…we can see through her body as easily as we can see through the dress. She rises up to kiss the champion on the cheek, and Fury looks shocked. Then, she whirls, and begins to walk away, her hair swishing and jumping around her body. Then, the WWO championship materializes around her waist, and Fury cries out. He chases after her, but then she begins to spin around in a strange pirouette…]
JM: “Don’t you love her, as she’s walkin’ out the door…” [The pirouette goes faster and faster, until the beautiful woman disappears, and in her place appears a small tornado, the funnel whipping around in the air. Fury tries to pull away, but he’s too close to the maelstrom, and gets sucked in. Fury is bouncing up and down within the clutches of the dream- storm, eyes bugged out and frightened. We get a close-up of the champion’s face, and his lips are pressed together tightly. The storm carries him away, and then drops him in the air. We get a long shot of Fury falling and screaming…]
JM: “In a deep blue dream, seven horses seem…” [As Fury falls, a set of winged horses appear in the background, hooves and wings producing a massive cloud of dust. Fury lands squarely upon the lead horse, and breathes a sigh of relief…then, looks down at the face of the horse, and yells out. The horse’s face has molded itslef to look like the face of Robert D’Artois, the man Fury beat for the WWO title. The camera pans over to look at the other horses, and they all have faces of the former
WWO champions— Tony Tobis, Vader X, Iron, Andros, Zac Schneider, and Bellamy
Albaneese. Fury struggles on the horse, but seems to be in some sort of dream-induced paralysis, and cannot move. The horses carry him on and on, until finally, rising above the horizon, is a large set of gates…they come closer and closer, until the gold lettering on top of them becomes legible.
The lettering reads WWO HALL OF FAME. Fury looks up at it with great anticipation, but the lead horse whinnies contemptuously and bucks him off…Fury falls again, and continues to fall…]
[The scene fades to black as Fury falls, and only the champion falling against a black background is seen. Suddenly, he stops, and we hear the classical strains of an orchestra. Fury himself is seated in a chair, in the string section, with a violin in hand. He begins to play furiously, and slowly, one by one, all the other players in the string section stop playing and look up.
Soon, Fury is the only one playing, and he struggles to keep up with the relentless pace of the music. As he looks around, he notices that the other players in the string section are not people at all, but ghastly hooded apparitions who stare at him with red, slanted eyes. He looks around frantically at them, and turns from side to side. All the demons point their skeletal fingers up towards the front of the stage, where we see the back of a conductor in a white wig. The conductor waves his baton to and fro, and then puts one skeletal hand to his ear, listening for music that has long since stopped. The conductor turns around slowly…]
[Fury’s breath catches in his throat as the face of the conductor becomes apparent. It is his mentor, his “teacher”, Night’s Harbinger. The lanky man turns to his protégé, red eyes burning in a pallid face like two coals tossed against new snow. He springs for Fury, pulling out two monstrous hands curved in the shape of claws…]
[Fury wakes up with a start, sweat streaming down his broad face. He rises out of the bed slowly, his hands visibly shaking. He walks over to the window of his apartment, and prys it open.]
Fury: “This cannot go on…I cannot live like this…” [Fury steps out of the window onto the fire escape. The camera fades.] -----------== Posted via Deja News, The Discussion Network ==---------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Discuss, or Start Your Own
