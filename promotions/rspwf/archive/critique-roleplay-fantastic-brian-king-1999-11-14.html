Critique Roleplay {"Fantastic" Brian King}

14 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Critique, flame, tell me what I did wrong...Please? [Interviewer expert, Danny Fitzwater is searching for soon-to-be Empire Wrestling superstar, "Fantastic" Brian King. Fitzwater brings along a cameraman on his quest to find the elusive Canadian. Finally, Brian King is spotted in a
Detroit night club. He is dancing with a nice looking young lady, about 23.
King is wearing khaki dress pants, a Barry Sanders Lions jersey, and Nike sneakers. His long dirty blond hair is hanging down over his ears. As the song ends he whispers in the girl's ear and moves to a table as she nods. King motions the cameraman and Fitzwater over to his table. Fitzwater sits down and the cameraman sets up as the first question is asked.]
Danny Fitzwater - Hello. Brian, how are you today? Brian King - Actually, I am doing quite well considering I have been hunted down by an interviewer like I was a run-away dog. You know, you could have called my hotel room here in Detroit, and we could have set this up.
DF - I'll remember that next time. Next Tuesday, on Tuesday Showdown, you have to face a fellow Canadian, Aron Rave, a man who has barely even given you a passing glance in any of his interviews since the brackets were announced. How
- BK - How do I feel? Is that what you were going to ask me? Don't ever ask me that! Of course, I am deeply insulted by the lack of recognition I have received here in EW. I think it's because I wasn't part of their little Gyeah
Wrestling "clique". It's a double standard here. Unless they have seen you in the GW, they don't give a damn. I've seen all the money in the EW pumped into guys coming from CWO and GW. But nothing about me coming in from UWC. Nothing!
Aron Rave has mentioned me but once in all of interviews. I am extremely upset at President Brock. I've seen the poll floating around in the locker room about everyone's thoughts on the first EW champ -
DF - That was my next question, how did you handle that when you didn't seen your name listed?
[Brian King stares at Fitzwater dumbfounded for a few seconds before he answered his question.]
BK - Did you just cut me off? Don't do that again! Do you hear me Fitzwater? Of course, I thought I was going to flip out on President Brock. When I came in, he said he already had an angle all lined up for me. I was going to be a star.
And now, that whole thing has bottomed out. Dont' believe everything you hear. DF - Your match on Backtalk tonight, do you think you have it well in hand? BK - Well of course! I am wrestling a man with no talent or substance and his finishing move is a clothesline...what do you expect? Him to upset...me? Ha!
Fitzwater, you are an insane man. My Backtalk match is going to be a laugher. Plus, I have a little trick up my sleeve, so Mr. Von, you better watch out. Which reminds me, I have to go soon and I'm gonna get this chick's number. DF - Any final words for your match against Aron Rave? BK - Of course, Brian King always has a mouthful. Aron Rave, your days in this tournament are numbered. Let's see...three actually. Rave, you better start recognizing I exist in your interviews, or you are going to be in for the upset of your life, in what was really no upset at all. Rave, get your act together, because I will be simply "Fantastic" and I will tear through you and the tournament. You may show me how they kick ass in Quebec, but trust me, Alberta is better than Quebec, each and every day of the week! Enough said!
[Brian King gets up from his chair, walks over to where the young woman is now sitting and talks to her. Shortly after, he gets up, walks past the still rolling camera and gives his infamous half smile. The camera then fades to a
First Blood advertisement.]
