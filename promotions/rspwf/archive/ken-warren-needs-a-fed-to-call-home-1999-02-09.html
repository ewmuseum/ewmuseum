Ken Warren needs a fed to call home.

9 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sunday, January 31, 1999: forget the Superbowl, forget the Half-Time Show, what you really want to see is the Main Event from the WWWA's "Clear The Lane."
//-//-//-// Main Event! \\-\\-\\-\\ THREE-WAY-NO-DQ-FALLS-COUNT-ANYWHERE TO DECIDE THE #16 SPOT IN THE UNIFIED WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT Trey Lawrenze -V- "Crazy" Chris Hurt -V- "Superstar" Ken Warren [At 5'10" and 235lbs nobody would ever believe that Ken Warren could ever amount to much. Today's atheletes are tall, agressive and rely on brute strength to win their wars in the squared circle, but not Ken Warren. Ken has earned the monicker "Superstar" because of his mat expertise and skill, desire to improve and his work ethic.]
[Recently, Ken Warren was in Minneapolis, Minnesota to offer his assistance to a friend who is ill. On this day, he is taking in the sights and walking along a small pond. Ken is wearing a long sleeved, black shirt, blue faded jeans and black riding boots. He is also wearing a white cowboy hat and in his left hand is a cigarette. As he follows the length of the pond, he is contemplating the upcoming match on Sunday, as he will face both "Crazy" Chris Hurt and Trey
Lawrenze in a three way dance with no disqualification and falls count anywhere to determine the number 16th participant in the WWWA Unified World Title tournament. Ken is deep in thought when he turns and sees a camera, he walks slowly to them and begins his message concerning the upcoming events.]
KW-First let me say this. It is an honor and a priveledge to have the opportunity to even get in the tournament for the vacant Unified World's Title.
Now that I have the chance, I plan to take full advantage and win the three way dance. Now, it will not be easy and I figure that before the end of the night I shall have shed some blood. Hmmm, before it is over I believe that we shall all shed some blood and I figure that the two opponents I will be facing, their blood shall be shed in vain. (laughing) But when all is over it will be me facing the number one ranked man in this federation and that man
is:"Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen. [Warren takes a draw from his smoke, then throws it into the pond and with a grin he continues speaking.]
KW-Uhmm, what was I going to say? Oh, I know. Let me talk about "Crazy" Chris Hurt. Now, Hurt I don't know who the hell you are trying to be or what it is you hope to accomplish, so let me tell you what you shall accomplish on
Sunday. Absolutely, not one damn thing. The only thing you can hope to accomplish is me beating the hell out of you and actually leaving some air in your lungs. I will not settle for anything less than you laying face first in your own pool of blood, now try as you may but to no avail you will be defeated.
[Ken begins to walk the length of the pond again, as the camera follows and with a deep breath he continues.]
KW-Trey Lawrenze? Who the hell are you? Ya know, I try to be a nice guy with etiquette and composure but with you and the "Crazy" man in my face this Sunday just makes me wanna puke. Trey, you like the other man I will be facing will taste defeat at the hands of the "Superstar."
[Warren slows down, coming to a complete stop and with a grin on his face, bends over and picks up a stone and tosses it in the pond.]
KW-Now, let me talk a bit about "Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen. Truly a gifted wrestle with some impressive moves and that "Dreambuster?" Yes, that is truly an impressive manuever Mr. Jensen. And when I win the three way dance, it will be myself and you in the title tourney. Just a quick thought on that subject
Jensen and that is this:"I am not called the Superstar because I woke up one morning and just started calling myself that, it is a name that I have earned."
If we meet, it will be my intention to pin you and to move up where I feel that in time I shall be and that is on top of the world.
[Ken turns from the camera and then...static.] Interested? Contact [detail withheld] Ken Warren "The Superstar" [WWWA] Former AIWF International Champion
