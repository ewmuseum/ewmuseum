[RSPWF] "Ministry of Pain" Roleplay. [Critique if you like.]

27 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up in a small wooden cabin somewhere in the outer mists of a realm known as Zoglanland...SilverLeaf is sitting with a black cloak with the hood covering his head, he slowly removes the hood off his head and begins too speak about his upcoming match with Drifter and Kid]
["Tower Of Pain" SilverLeaf Halfmoon] : Well not many men have taken myself and my partner into heart and we recently showed the world what we were about when we destroyed Erik and Derrick Andrews, those two lost souls walked into the ring with their heads held high in hopes of a victory and their hopes and dreams were shattered by the powers held by myself and Morpheus Tals.. Now it seems as if their are two more men in this federation who are on a mission to destroy us... Kid and Drifter, [chuckles]
The both of you need to take a step back for a brief moment and look into my eyes... Do you see fear? Do you sense and remorse from what I am about too do to you both? I personally do not care if either of you have a title, nor does it bother me one bit that you are a contender in this federation, when the
Ministy Of Pain picks their judgment day what are you going too do? We take things seriously we do not kid around about anything in life, we take one day at a time and make sure we do things right.. Drifter and Kid the both of you shall perish at some point in your life, it just so happens the day you two meet myself and Tals that day will come sooner then expected... [sigh] Kid you may have gotten lucky by capturing a victory over 'Ungrateful Soul" John Myles but do you honestly believe you can repeat history and this time capture one over myself? I think not, when I came into this federation I was put down, made fun of, and completely was told I was a nobody now that two of the top superstars have already fallen at my grasp I am starting too earn some respect....
[A loud bang on the cabin door rages out as "Darkened Soul" Morpheus Tals enters onto the scene]
["Darkened Soul" Morpheus Tals] The bitterness that we suffer, it is inevitable. The torment that we go through shall never be put to just mean. Drifter, on Sunday night, we showed you just why we are the Ministry of
Pain. We took you and that Indiguos 6Pac into the outer realms, and we tortured, we tormented your souls, just as has been done to us throughout our entire lives. We walked through your curtains, we raised our arms, and we struke down with furious thunder, and we took your souls. We do just as we promise, and when we say something, you had better believe that it shall be done. Those whom step into our path shall be dealt with in a very vicious manner. Kid, Drifter, you are the next, you are the second piece of the puzzle that we shall put together for you all here in the "IWF." As it unravvels, and you begin to see what we are all about, you shall all feel the Pain, and you will Suffer an indignent fate, just as all others that cross our path. Here in the outer realms, here in outworld, this is _OUR_ world. Anyone crossing the boarder into our realm shall feel our wrath, this we promise, and you all know, that we _NEVER_ lie. We shall strike down with vicious vengeance. Kid,
Drifter, Part 2 of a never ending saga... ["Tower Of Pain" SilverLeaf Halfmoon] : The both of you made the biggest mistake, everyman has a side of hate and darkness in their souls, mine has overcome my entire inner spirit and engulfed my insides with a entire blackness of unexplainable confusion...Drifter, Kid... we'll take the two of you out anytime, anywhere and you time shall come too pass I can assure you and where it leads you is too one place you wish you would not be...
Welcome to hell young youths, feel the heat of the fire in brimstone.. Kid and
Drifter you both were never a tag team you both paired up in hopes of getting asome tag gold in this federation and the only place it shall lead you is six feet under courtesy of the Ministry Of Pain.... You both do not know eachother very well and that is the first mistake you both made, Drifter can you really trust your partner? [laughs] do we have a surprise for you Drifter... Wait and see we always and will prevail no matter what the cost, fear us or avoid us the option is yours.....
[Morpheus and SilverLeaf leave the small cabin and walk outside, they begin to stroll down a small dirt path witch leads to a small shrine worshiping Hades,
SilverLeaf and Morpheus bow down to the "God Of The Underworld" as Morpheus begins too speak]
["Darkened Soul" Morpheus Tals] Oh Lord of the Darkness, we have taken the souls of four mortals, just as you asked. The brothers Andrew,
Drifter, and 6pac have felt our wrath. These men are very weak in heart, and soul. We shall suck the rest of the power out of Drifter, and his mortal friend, The Kid on Sunday.
[Voice] You two have done well, but words of advise, take their souls, send them back to me, but do not stop. Kill everyone whom you see, and we shall rule their megacosm very soon...
["Darkened Soul" Morpheus Tals] Lord of Darkness, we shall not let you down. These mere mortals think that because we are on _THEIR_ planet that it denies us our enernal powers and wisdom. So, Lord of Darkness, I tell you this, we shall deliver the souls of all mortals, and shall bring them back to you. These weak and pathetic men are nothing but pawns in our game, and they shall suffer the indignent fate which all whom step into our path suffer.
Drifter, we have already started on, and Kid, he shall not take much power to demolish. So, Lord of Darkness, we shall bring them to you, this I promise you. We shall not let you down...
["Tower Of Pain" SilverLeaf Halfmoon] : We have yet too fail you my lord and we do not plan on beginning anytime soon.. The mear lives the men in this federation hold are meaningless, their souls shall be for your gain and only yours... We need no assistance in this match we can deal with these pathetic mortals ourselves...
[Voice] SilverLeaf you and Morpheus are my aires you know what needs to be done, be careful but not too cautious, Kid and Drifter shall soon have a permanent home in the Underworld, see that this happens....
["Tower Of Pain" SilverLeaf Halfmoon] : Consider it done, Kid and Drifter shall be sublimed in a series of moves in which they cannot bare too handle.. They will be two more names to add into the books after we mutalate them..Kid and Drifter listen too the words I speak, write them down and remember them, understand the meaning I am saying try and comprehend just what I am telling you..The two of your souls mean nothing too me.. Your just yet another obstical too overcome that shall not be able to hold back the darkness in us...
[SilverLeaf takes a deep breath as he reaches into the side pocket of his cloak and begins too read a short quote that he believes in]
"The darkness is one thing many do not understand, they look at the darkside as a side of evil, death and eternal damnation..
If only everyone would realize that each one of us has a hidden chamber of mist in our souls and it shall be released at any given time"
["Tower Of Pain" SilverLeaf Halfmoon] : Kid and Drifter you both have that place inside you, deny it all you want, say it isn't there as much as you wish but every man posses it, you'll understand the ways of the darkside soon enough.. If your soul is not black as sin then you are simply against us, no questions asked, no guilt will cross our minds when we leave your rotting carcass left for the vultures in the ground...
[SilverLeaf looks over too Morpheus Tals and steps back as Morpheus spreads some light on the subject of Drifter and Kid once more]
["Darkened Soul" Morpheus Tals] These two men, mere pawns in a game of deception, greed, and anguish. No mere mortals possibly can step into our paths and stop us now. How can a couple of "God's" children step into the path of the darkness and think that they can stop the lethal alliance of the darkside. You see, we shall not let any of these men stand in our way. The
Lord of the Darkness has given us a mission, and when we put our evil minds together, nothing good possibly can come out of it. The only thing that these two men shall be happy about is that they will soon get to lurk on the darkside. They shall soon have the precious opportunity to walk on the outer reaches of the earth's realm, and get to know what it feels like to be one of
_US_ only for a mere moment. Then, we shall striken your soul from the inner cavity of your chest, and it shall suck strait into my loins, leaving you helpless and completely overwhelmed. When we have reached this stage, then, and only then, is when we will finish you off. And, my friends, once we decide that you shall be no more, then, and only then, will you know what it is truely like...to be _ROYALLY SCREWED_ So, my friends, you know the line, and you shall take notice and...Live in Trepidity Until You...
[Stairs deep into the camera's lense...] ["Darkened Soul" Morpheus Tals] LIVE NO MORE!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Morpheus Tals disappears deep into the fog of night...] [Ministry of Pain] ["Darkened Soul" Morpheus Tals] [Morph KiLL] ["Tower of Terror" Silverleaf Halfmoon] [RatedX187] [Together we make up the RSPWF's Lost Soul's.] [Stay on the look out.]
