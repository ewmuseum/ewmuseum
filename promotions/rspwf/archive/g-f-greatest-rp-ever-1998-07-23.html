23 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-=(reading past this point exterminates your rights to TOS)=- -=(Howard Stern is sitting in his usual seat and begins to talk. Jackie is playing the opening for the Howard Stern show and Robin is back in her cage away from everyone else.)=-
Howard Stern - Man do we have a great show for you today. We got our wrestling experts here, Big Black, a huge wrestling fan...do you watch it Jackie...Robin?
Jackie - Of course I watch it...I wore my Iron Man Steve Gorecki tee shirt today just for the show!
Robin - I don't watch that junk...to me its like a beating on 36th street. Howard Stern - In front of your ghetto house? Haha, well, lets have big teeth bring in the Iron Man and his manager Da Possinater. We have Big Black here today along with King of all Blacks and they are huge wrestling fans. Lets watch the teeth master bring them in...
-=(Scene shows the waiting room where Iron Man Steve Gorecki and Da possinater are waiting. They get up and start to follow him into the Howard Stern show, while they are walking...)=-
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Hey man, did you just grab my <bleep>? What do you think you are doing? Are you a homo or what?
Big Teeth - Ah, no, no sir...I was just trying to get in front of you to lead you.... Iron Man Steve Gorecki - That ain't no excuse, Da Possinater, take him out! -=(Da Possinater grabs a handfull of hair and whips him into a door in the hallway head first. The door swings wide open and he falls to the floor. Howard Stern sees this and starts cracking up)=- Howard Stern - Man, I have been wanting to do that for so long. Its about time someone put him in his place. Haha, He flew into that door...from now on I am watching this wrestling <bleep>, what is it the Global Wrestling Federation...I am watching this stuff more often!
-=(Da Possinater and Iron Man Steve Gorecki enter the room while Jackie is playing "Lets get ready to rumble". They sit down and put their head sets on next to Big Black and King of all Blacks.)=-
Howard Stern - Welcome to the show fellas, but before I ask you guys any questions, Stuttering John is your biggest fans ever. He's got all your action figures and this guy is just a spaz...so he wants to ask you some questions!
Stuttering John - H..h..hey Iron Man! Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Alright, get the <bleepin> questions rollin here buddy. Stuttering John - D..d...did you ever feal up another wrestler in the ring? Iron Man Steve Gorecki - No, but some guys have been getting real close to me lately...like in the Mass Destruction match...he got pretty close to the main vein! I was about to walk out after he touched my manhood.
Stuttering John - D...d....don't you think t...t...that the GWF should hold nude w...w...women wrestling?
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Of course, I would always love to see that before my match. Hey, maybe I would join them! I think women wrestling can do a lot.
Howard Stern - Maybe me and two of them can have a bed match if ya know what I mean...first one who...well, we will not get into that.
Jackie - First one who gets thrown off the bed? Is that what the match would be? Howard Stern - It goes a little further than that...its way past your experience! Stuttering John - Will you ever c..c...come out with an Iron Man condom? Iron Man Steve Gorecki - What the heck is that supposed to mean? Iron Man is written all over it or what? Does it have muscles? Man, you are one weird little guy!
Stuttering John - L..l...last question...do you think that t..t...the battle royals are one big orgy?
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Thats why I never enter myself in them. Its like a gang bang out there...can't trust anyone because any one of them could take a walk on the wild side.
Howard Stern - Alright, get out of here John, go get a new tounge or something! King of all Blacks - You know <bleep> well that you feal them men up in that ring...thats why you are a wrestler!
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - What the <bleep> are you talking about? Maybe you have had too many trips to the porno palace! You know all them tapes back and forth!
King of all Blacks - Yeah right, you wish you little sissy wrestler, you haven't been on the streets and seen the real stuff...its all fake! All fake! Iron Man Steve Gorecki - You couldn't last 2 minutes in the ring with our worst jobber. If you could see the training we go through you would be beat. Go sit your fat <bleep> down and shutup! Bow to me, yeah, get down there and bow to my feet. You little black punk!
King of all Blacks - You calling me black you little fairy wrestler? Da Possinater - C'mon King, bring it to us! Lets see what you got you little thug! I think your wife, oh no, thats wife number 10 just got picked up on the corner. And your 16 children are stealing bread down at the market!
Big Black - Hey guys, lets all just settle down here! Howard Stern - Hey, all of you just shut up and sit down! Big Black, be the referee over there!
King of all Blacks - Sorry Howard! Its just that this little white boy came here to pick a fight, I think he is racist!
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Whatever you little scrub. Howard Stern - Yo, Steve just cool it, now talk a little on your match with
Mass Destruction coming up.
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Once again he cannot beat me fairly, I had him right where I wanted him...that top rope ddt was it! He had to pay off that low life
Tony Arnold to come in there and smack me with a pool stick! I don't know if it came from Da Possinater or the Hustler, but if I find out I will beat whoever it was's <bleep>
Da Possinater - How the heck do you think it is me man? I had enough of that fool so I just walked out. I would never do that to a brotha!
Iron Man Steve Gorecki - Why the heck did you walk out then fool? You could have wooped Arnold when he smacked me, instead you just let me hang and lose my title. This match on Saturday will be a title match let me mind you, Mass
Destruction if you have any balls, then put the belt on the line. This time it will be a fair tag team match. My man Da Possinater will throw Arnold's head through car windows and smash that fools head up and I will take care of Mass
Destruction, I don't know what I will have to do but it will be done. I might have to throw him in the locker, seal it shut and throw it in the east river if
I have to! I am going all out to see it that he does not get past that night with that belt on his shoulder. Alcatraz match wouldn't be nothing without the pain and glory, you pick door number one and I will take the glory. Tony
Arnold, look for a switch so that I can manhandle you also! That match at
Summer Splash was the best match in GWF history, but this one will be even better!
Howard Stern - I have one <bleep> of a special surprise for you today Iron
Man...you know you hate Mass Destruction, well this is something that will drive him up the wall! Bring her out...BEAUTIFUL BETH! Mass Destruction's wife ladies and gentleman!
-=(Beautiful Beth walks out in a gee string bikini that doesn't leave much to the imagination. She walks over to the Iron Man and sits on his lap)=- Howard Stern - Wow, I didn't know Mass Destruction had suck a nice looking broad! What are them, C's?
Beautiful Beth - C's, yes, you wanna take a peek? -=(She flashes him)=- Howard Stern - Oh my god, that has to be one of the best sights on this show since me! Iron Man Steve Gorecki - And let me mind you that she is sitting on my lap! Mass Destruction eat your heart out bra, because she left you and wound up with yours truly!
King of all Blacks - Shutup man, I have had enough of your lip! Lets get it on right here, stealing other people's women!
Big Black - C'mon guys, I don't like fighting and you know that! I am a silent giant. Break it up now!
-=(King of all Blacks throws a punch that nails Gorecki in the jaw, a huge riot breaks out in the studio as everyone starts to get into it, Big Teeth comes from behind but Howard Stern kicks him in the groin and levels him. The studio is a complete mess...lets go to a commercial)=-
