[EWC] The Sebastian-Douglas War... Flash # 1...

31 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, I guess since Duane made a good point I thought I would put my rps on here also, so here is my first one...
-------------------------------------------------------------- [The scene opens in a room which appears to be a hotel room. The camera pans around the room showing a king size bed with purple blankets, a lamp, and an entertainment center. On the entertainment center is a Tv in which Ricki Lake is showing. The camera continues to pan until a man can be seen in the picture.
The man has shoulder length black hair, and he is wearing a white t-shirt with a pair of black denim jean shorts. The man turns around and his face is revealed to all... The man is none other then "The Crazy Man" Derek Douglas...
Douglas cracks his neck and begins to speak] Derek Douglas: So... Mike Sebastian... You my friend don't have a clue of what your up against. On November 11th you and I will face each other one on one. No more verbal warfare. It all comes down to who can do the best in the ring once the bell rings... I don't know about you Sebastian, but when that bell rings
I'm no longer that nice a kinda man you see before you. I my friend am a man that feels no pain. I am a man that will do anything and everything to get the job done... Sebastian, you are good with words, but what are you in the ring???
[Douglas's face turns red and he smiles. He looks around and begins to speak] Derek Douglas: Look at this room... This has to be the nicest room I have ever slept in. I mean usually I only sleep in my car, but the EWC has gone all out and gotten me this great room. Anyways, I don't think any of you want to hear about me and my problems... I'm here to talk to you all about a certain person that needs someone to love. Mike Sebastian, you are a man with no love... You go around with no friends saying you don't need them and you don't want them.
Well, I have a little surprise for you... I will show you the love you really need. You might think you don't want it, but you know you do... {Giant Smile}
Sebastian, one more thing... If you do have a few extra bucks I could have, it would really help me out...
[Suddenly Douglas begins to flinch and falls to the ground. His head begins to knock off the ground like a bowling ball hitting the pins. Suddenly he stops, and his eyes roll back into his head. He sits still for a moment and his eyes go back to normal. He sits up and frowns. He slowly makes his way to his feet and begins to speak]
Derek Douglas: You know something Sebastian, I don't give a s[beep] what the hell you think about me anymore. Everyone knows your full of yourself and it's not because of your wrestling skills. It's because your use to getting your own way, but if you think really good and hard you'll realize it isn't going to happen anymore. On November 11th it will be the day of reconing... On November
11th you will indeed feel the warm soft flow of the crimson mask... Learn it, love it, and for damn sure get to know it...
[Douglas stops looks at the tv and begins to laugh at the Ricki Lake Show. He looks back at the camera and begins to speak]
Derek Douglas: I love this show.. It has to be the funniest thing since bullet bras in the 60's... {Cuckles to himself} I mean where else can you find a girl that thinks her dad is her mom... Ok, well I guess I don't really have anything else to say at this point except I wish everyone piece and love.. Have a good night boyz and girlz... {Smiles and Waves}
