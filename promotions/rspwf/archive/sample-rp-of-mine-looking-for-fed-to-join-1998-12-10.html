Sample RP of mine, looking for fed to join

10 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene] Setting: Somewhere in the Carribean on an offshore yacht Time: 3:43 PM Subject: IWF 's own Lanier Riley Surroundings: Open deck, no other people around, sprawled out on a lawn chair
Attire: Navy Blue Bathrobe, Brown Boat shoes, Sunglasses, and long hair tied back
Notes: Sipping strawberry daquiri, eating crackers with a fine french cheese on them, staring out at the ocean, opera music playing from inside the cabin
Lanier "Strictly Business" Riley: Ahh.. The days I've spent out here on my lovely yacht, sailing throughout the Carribean, retired on a large sum of money provided by the Insane Wrestling Federation, my present employer. Ahh.. I suppose that is what we're here to discuss, Lanier's got a new job eh? Well, I left this federation assuming that I'd never return. They say I'm back to avenge the embarassment I suffered, ah, that is a mere rumor. Now you wanna know the real reason I decided to rejoin the IWF? Listen closely, the one reason Lanier Riley has graced you all with his prescence again, is simply because I was bored as hell. I saw the good name of Prez Kelly going down the tubes and I decided maybe I could help out. I mean living the life of luxury is great, but it gets old when you don't have a thing in the world to do except drink fine wines and smoke cuban cigars. A man needs his exercise, an occasional pounding of an unworthy foe, because as we all know, for the Biz, worthy foes are few and far between. Ahaha..
[Takes a sip of his drink] Lanier "Strictly Business" Riley: So in the past I've been known as quite the ladies man, and you will all see that side of me hasn't changed a bit. I love women, and [Tilts sunglasses down to the bridge of his nose, grins then says]
women love me. So boys, guard your girls, because if the Biz sees something he likes, he's gonna take it, and there is _ABSOLUTELY NOTHING_ you, nor anyone else can do about it. Which brings me to another topic, my wants, my needs, my
IWF hopes. Well I guess they are like any other competitors, to attain that elite status that only so few hold, to attain the IWF World Heavyweight
Championship Belt, which even fewer are honored with. I, my friends, shall be one of those chosen few, to wear the belt. I haven't been around for a while, and I'm not quite sure who the champ is right now, but I know that the Biz could take him. So Lanier's rusty, Lanier hasn't been in the ring for a while, well, he's here to show you that he's still got the stuff. My MoneyMaker is still just as strong as it was when I left. I've got this Federation under my thumb, yet nobody knows that except for Lanier and a little special surprise that all of you will find out about later on.
[Stares out into the water again then looks back at the camera and smiles] Lanier "Strictly Business" Riley: Well, I've been assigned my first match here back in the IWF, it's against a man of many faults, a man not worthy to step through those ropes and lay his hands all over my heavenly body. Kardiac, ahaha.. You've pushed me and the rest of the IWF around long enough, hell your one of the reasons my departure from this noble federation lasted so long.
Revenge shall be sweet young grasshoppa.. ahaha.. So how's a man like myself,
The Biz, to handle a guy like Kardiac??? I'll tell you, I can him with the consequence, finish him off with the MoneyMaker, cover his body, although I know it's gonna smell like shit, and listen to that ref do what he learned how to do in grade school. Then we're gonna hear that bell ring and for the thousands in attendance, a new star shall be born. You know it's just too damn bad the Insane Wrestling Federation isn't smart enough to show me terrorize
Kardiac.
[Lanier sort of spins around to sit upward in the chair, he sets his empty glass on a small table next to the lounge chairs. He folds his arms over his knees, and then leans in close.]
Lanier "Strictly Business" Riley: So, everybody wants to know who The Biz is going to associate with this time around, he hasn't spoken about SoS, maybe it's just a thing of the past. Maybe you don't give a damn who I choose to acquaint myself with, but I'm still gonna tell you what's goin on. You see,
SoS was a creation of me and a man named Ares, we hopped out of TrU CliQuE and went independent, nothin much more to that. We found ourselves becoming much more popular with the IWF fans. With the popularity, with the stardom came jealousy, greed, and envy. Not only from the other wrestlers, not only from the fans, but from each other as well. I decided that one of us better leave the infamous group before we ended up killing each other. Ares was content where he was, so I made the move. Shortly after that, I ended, or at least thought I did, my wrestling career. Without me, Ares was also hollow, he had to leave this great sport as well, and although he hasn't made a comeback yet,
I am hoping he will.
[Lays back down in his chair and slides his glasses back down over his eyes] Well, I think I've said my piece, Kardiac, maybe you'll show a little wisdom and start kissin ass now, but if you prefer getting demolished, hey, who am I to thrash another man's hobby?
[Fade to Black]
