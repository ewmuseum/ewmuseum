N.A.W. Update (3/3/98) {Flash for "The Guardian" Judas Steele}

4 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens on a darkened, empty locker room. The only light in the room comes from a single candle near a far corner. Sitting in the corner, slouched over, sits a man with long blonde hair wearing a flack jacket and cut off jeans. As the camera zooms in, the man lifts his head and brushes his hair off his face. His face his highlighted by a gotee and simple cross tatoo that covers his left eye. As the candle light refects from his eyes, the man takes a beep breath, and addresses the camera.)
Judas Steele: And so the cycle of pain is renewed. A cycle I am all to familure with. My life has been filled with pain you see. From a childhood filled with hate and discrimination, to an adult life marked by sadness ,heartache, and disappointment, I have come to know pain as few have. I have seen misery first hand in the death of brother and the raping of my wife. And I have somehow found the strength to return to the ring. But something must happen first............
(The picture fades out) JS voice over:..........an awakening must happen.............. (The pictures fades back in. Judas is no longer in the locker room, but standing at the foot of one of the stones in Stonehenge. His look has changed remarkably. He is now wearing a black unitard with a flaming heart wrapped in thorns airbrushed on its front. His hair is tyed back, and the cross tatoo is missing. Judas' expression is entirly different as well. His body language is more confident.)
JS:.......and it must be complete. In my tragety I have been reminded of everything I have, and everything I have gained. I was reminded of exactly why I became a wrestler in the first place. Now my journey brings me to North American Wrestling, the N.A.W.. I enter the N.A.W.
with a sense of purpose. Not to win titles, not to beat people, and not to be the most "hardcore". I enter the N.A.W. to be the best, period. I may not win titles, I may not win matches, but the legacy I leave behind will attest to one fact, that I gave as good as I got, and raised the mark by which all other wrestlers are judged! No one in the N.A.W. can prepare for me!
(The camera pulls away from Judas as he holds out his arms, giving the crusifix gesture. The camera fades to black on that final image.)
"Fool, pain is my friend! She is a companion, a lover, a caregiver. Allow _me_ to introduce you to her!" - J. Steele
