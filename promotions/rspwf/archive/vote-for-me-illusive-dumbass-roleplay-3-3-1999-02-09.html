(Scene opens up to a crowd. The crowd is mixed with all races, all different styles in clothing, and all different types of people in general. Yet all the people and all the culturally different backgrounds are all looking forward.
The majority of the crowd in the park area are in there 20's, 30's, 40's, and
50's. The camera pans around to see a stage with Illusive Dumbass wearing a white Nike hat, black sweat pants, white shirt that says "Buck It" and on the
back it says "Down there? No...over here." Illusive Dumbass has a microphone in his hand and is pacing back and forth. The camera fades to black and then comes back to a classroom full of 4th grade kids. The kids are all sitting
Indian style on the floor. All the children are sitting very still and quiet.
The kids have there hands folded on there lap as they wait patiently. Then the camera pans around to the front of the room. Where you can see Illusive
Dumbass and a teacher. The teacher is an elderly woman who's wearing reading glasses. She doesn't look to happy. She's wearing a light green sweater pull over and a long flower patterend skirt. Illusive Dumbass isn't wearing a hat on his bald hat. Probably because your not allowed to wear a hat in school.
He's also wearing a black shirt that say's "Mr. Buttass is gay" and on the back it says "Well at least that's what Larrita said." He's also wearing black
Adidas warm up pants. With his usual black Nike shoes. Ish is looking at the teacher. The camera fades to black and then comes back to a room full of old people. Half of them have either a cane, crutches, walking aid, and a wheel chair. The rest of them are sitting in chairs. Most of them have a look of confusion and some are just looking in front.
The camera pans around to see...who else...Illusive Dumbass. He's standing there while brushing his eye brows. He's wearing a black Nike hat, black nike warm ups, and his usual black Nike sneakers. He is also wearing a shirt that says "I hate Guru" and on the back it says "Hey Jackass...stop looking at my ass." Illusive Dumbass seems to be waiting when he suddenly looks at the camera. Fades to black and then comes back on where you once again come to the park with all the people standing there. The camera quickly pans around to see
Illusive Dumbass holding the mic and seems to be waiting. He then looks at the camera)
Illusive Dumbass: Oh...is it on? (Camera nods) Dumbass: Okay good...Okay ladies and gentleman. As you all know we came here to ask you a few questions about the EWWF Alliance Tournament.
(The majority of the crowd nods there head and a few people actually say "yes" outloud) Dumbass: Okay people well let me explain a few things to the people watching at home. (turns towards camera) Well as you can see we have gathered a group of people here today. (The camera shows a shot of the crowd...the crowd is waving at the camera and smiling) Well everybody in the crowd here today is between the ages of late 20's to late 50's. I've also gathered crowds at a elementary school and a group of elders in a retirement home. Now the reason
I've done this is to see what the effects of wrestling, the Alliance, me, and a bunch of other things have on people of different ages. I'll start off first with this group of people here. Okay first off...as a group do you like wrestling?
Group: Yes! Dumbass: Okay good. Soooo how do you guys feel about the EWWF Alliance as a whole? I mean...do you dislike it...or like it?
Group: Like it! Dumbass: Great...me too...errr except for a few things like...well I better not say. Oooookay...now I'm gonna ask you some technical questions. Do any of you know what DECWF is?
Group: YES!!!! Dumbass: And do you know what me beating the shit out of JP Blood is? Group: (Somepeople say "Yes!" but most of them are looking confused and some are pondering why the hell there here)
Dumbass: Excuse me...I couldn't hear you guys. Group: (some mumbling) Yes! Dumbass: That's what I thought. Okay so do you know how me getting cheap shotted by that son of a bitch, cock sucking, fairy tale living, Disney story loser, that's a bad boy wanna be, can get me realllllllllly fucking pissed?
(The crowd is looking at each other wondering if this guy is for real) Dumbass: Listen here you piece of shit's. That's not a fucking answer...answer me god damn mother fuckers. ANSWER ME!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
(The crowd is now starting to get a little scared, annoyed, and ticked off. In fact some people are leaving because of pure disgust)
Dumbass: You stupid PRICKS!!!!!!! You can't even answer a simple question. Fuck YOU ALL!!!!! YOU KNOW I'M THE BEST!!!!!!!!! ADMIT IT YOU STUPID BITCHES!!!!!!!!!!!! (At this time a police car is pulling up and two cops step out to the mayhem) Dumbass: AHHHH NOW YOU CALL THE COPS...EH? WELL FUCK YOU!!!!!!!! FUCK YOU
ALL!!!!!!!! YOUR ALL GOING TO HELL!!!!!!!!!! I FUCKING OWN JP BLOOD!!!!!!!!! I
FUCKING OWN JOHNNY SLEDGE!!!!!!!!!! I FUCKING OWN THIS STUPID BITCH HERE IN
THIS FRONT ROW!!!!!!!!!!! (The cops are now running to get on stage. Meanwhile the crowd...or what's left of the crowd are all egging Illusive Dumbass on. There acting like they want to fight him)
Dumbass: WHAT?!?!?!?! YOU WANT TO FIGHT ME PRICK!!!!!!!! I WILL FUCKING KICK
YOUR ASS!!!!!!!! I FUCKING OWN YOU!!!!!!!! I OWN THE WHOLE FUCKING
ALLIANCE!!!!!!!!! (The cops are on stage and stop Illusive Dumbass right before he lunges at the guy that wanted to fight with him)
Dumbass: I OWN YOU TOO!!!!!!!!! FUCK YOU COPPER!!!!!!!!!! UH OH EVERYBODY!!!!!!!!!!! IT'S THE FUCKING FUN POLICE!!!!!!!!!!! FAKE COPS FAKE
COPS!!!!!!! WHATCHA GONNA DO...WHATCHA GONNA DO WHEN THE FAKE COPS COME FOR

YOUR FUCKING TOY COPS!!!!!!!!!!!! I OWN YOU FAT BOY!!!!!!!!! DAMN DONUT EAT'IN, FAT ASS, MOTHER FUCKERS!!!!!!!!!!!!! HERE...I'LL GIVE YOU FIFTY DOLLARS AND YOU CAN CALL IT A DAY!!!!!!!!!!! I MEAN HELL...YOU SPEND WHAT
LITTLE MONEY YOU DO GET AND BUY FUCKING DONUTS!!!!!!!!!!! FUCK
YOU!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Camera turns to Static. It then comes back on in the classroom full of children. Also in the room is Illusive Dumbass and the Teacher)
Illusive Dumbass: (looking into camera) Ummm...haha...yeah...well the group in the park didn't work out to well...soooooooooooo now I'm here at this classroom. I'm in the 4th grade class of Mrs. Schneiderman here at St. Cloud elementary school.
Mrs. Schneiderman: Say hello class. Class: Heeeelllllooooo Mr. Illusive! Dumbass: It's alright class...you can call me Dumbass. (the class chuckles) Mrs. Schneiderman: I would prefer them to call you Illusive. Dumbass: No really...Dumbass would be fine lady...In fact I'm gonna have to insist Mrs...oh yes...Schneiderman.
Mrs. Schneiderman: I'm afraid that I ca... Dumbass: That's nonsense lady...now shut you mouth! Class: ewwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwww!!! (Mrs. Schneiderman is shocked. After realizing what has just happened the teacher gets scared and leaves the classroom)
Class: yaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa!!!!!! Dumbass: Hurray!!!! NO teacher!!!! Well children...let's get down to business. Chubby boy: Mr. Dumbass...why haven't we heard from you lately. Dumbass: Oh that's simple fat boy. (The class chuckles and the chubby boy get mad and sad at the same time) Dumbass: Well the reason is because I was told that there were no more interviews to happen after the first Friday...but I was seriously mistaken. In fact I had no idea about what was going on. All I new...is that there was a battle royal...that I never trained for and that's why I was knocked out of the
Battle Royal with two and a half minutes. It's pretty pathetic, but now I'm much more prepared and I know what I need to do. Well what are you gonna do...eh?
(The kids seem to be bored by how long it took him to explain that) Dumbass: Well kids...I have a few questions to ask you. (The kids start to come awake again) Dumbass: Okay kids...do you know who Guru is? Class: Boooooooooooo!!!!! Dumbass: That's right kids the Guru sucks. Girl wearing glasses: He makes me fall asleep! (The whole class erupts in agreement) Dumbass: Your telling me!!! He even puts me to sleep. I'm telling you guys that your not alone...trust me...YOU ARE NOT ALONE!!! Well anyways. Kids...I would just like to point out how that unsimplified stupid freak got lucky because I never got to touch him. I should smack the shit out of him.
Class: ewwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwwww!!!!! Dumbass: Sorry...I should watch my language. Kid with hair slicked back: Fuck Guru...that punk ass BITCH!!!!! Dumbass: (wiping his eyes)...I just got a tear from my eye...I have just met little Dumbass. Oh god...this is emotional.
Kid with hair slicked back: Hey Dumbass...stop being a fucking pussy. (Dumbass looks up and looks reeeeeally pissed) Dumbass: What did you say? Shut the fuck up little bitch. Now sit your smartass self down before I throw you out the window!
(The kid sits down and the class get's scared) Dumbass: Now...CLASS! Listen up class...I'm gonna ask you a question and you are all gonna tell me either YES...or NO! Do you understand?
(The entire class nods there head) Dumbass: Good! Now I am the best damn wrestler in History? Class: Yes! Dumbass: Okay...can I beat the shit out of the fucking german nazi...c4? Class: Yes! Dumbass: Do I own Wolverine? Class: Yes! Dumbass: Can I bitch slap the Crypt Keeper to hell? Class: Yes! Dumbass: Can I beat the shit out of JP Blood and put him in another figure four like I did before?
Class: Yes! Dumbass: Good...If I feel like smacking the shit out of Johnny Sledge for no reason besides getting pay back for what the low life punk ass reject of society did to me?
Class: Yes! Dumbass: Will I finally get my hands on Guru so I can pound the shit out of his ugly ass face.
Class: Yes! Dumbass: I hope...god know's he'll probably lose to C4 because he wants to annoy the hell out of me. Okay kids...here's the big one. My first opponent in the Battle Royal...Will I fuck up the guy who can start a fire until he fucking spits up a lung or something. Will I beat him senseless until I knock the life out of him. Will I smack the shit out of him so hard that I'll bring him within inches of his life?
Class: YES!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (adds to the effect) Dumbass: Anybody got any further thoughts...that's what I thoug... Nerd Boy: Actually I wanted to talk about Guru... Dumbass: Boy sit your ass down! Nerd Boy: Excuse ME DUMBASS!!! but I want to talk about MY bastion of cypher and my god. GURU!!!
Dumbass: What the fuck? Nerd Boy: You damn miscreant...you reclusive dumbass...you damn NON title holder. You need to whip out a TVTC Blunt relax. For my HOLY SHITBALLS!!!
Bastion of cypher and me are... (Ish throws a stapler at the kid. The kid get's nailed square in the head and get's knocked out cold!)

Fat Cop: There you are...we've been looking for you! Dumbass: Fuck off you fat ass! Obese cop: (sounds like fat albert) Hey hey hey Dumbass...were taking you in. Dumbass: Jesus Christ...have you guys ever heard of the word "diet" I'm afraid to go with you...YOU MIGHT EAT ME!!!!
