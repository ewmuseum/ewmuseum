[The Poet - WLW RP] Please Critique

30 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey. This is the first flash I've written for this character, The Poet. He's a very jaded, frustrated, unsuccessful writer who's turned to the world of wrestling as a source of income and as an outlet for releasing his aggressions. The Poet debuted himself with a very brief promo at April Reign. But this is the first time he's ever been seen by the wrestling public.
Please let me know what you think so I can improve this character who's a work in progress. Thanks in advance.
[Six legs; four old, creaking, rickety, wooden legs and two fresh, well-oiled, masculine, human legs. Listen to the sound of a typewriter clicking away frantic-mad with unrest. The desk's surface and the word-machine that sits on top of it come into view as the shot
pans up. Paper, flowing like wine from the abused typewriter, stains the desktop with unsatisfying words, not yet aged to perfection. "No, no, it's all wrong!" screams the man in the mask vehemently, as we watch him rip the stream of paper from his best friend's mouth,
crumple it in his hand and throw it behind him. The paper lands in a small pile of equally brutalized manuscripts, as the man in the mask slowly exhales, setting his best friend's cursor to the upper
left-hand corner of a clean, white page. Who is the man in the mask? He's the man who sits behind the old desk, alone with his mechincal friend. He's the man whose baby-blue mask does not match his brown bleach-stained bathrobe. He's the newest addition to the WLW roster.
He's The Poet, the man who speaks as he returns to his typing.] The Poet: "'Hello', by The Poet." [The shot switches to a full view of the typewriter, we see the words printing on the page as he creates them.]
"I said hello -- Greeted you all with warmth And gave the gift of my words Not a smile a nod a wink a word A shot of expensive whiskey in return. Unnoticed like the younger brother I am or the Lump of melanoma on your lower back But I will spread much quicker than cancer Creeping like venom through veins or like the Monster under your bed when mommy Lifts the covers and tells you 'There's nothing here!' But mommy doesn't know shit does she? The monster is definitely there There inside your head As real as the thoughts that Crossed the stream of your mind during April Reign And said: 'The Poet? Who?' Who is not important nor What where when how but WHY?" [He stops speaking the words, but continues typing.] Why? Whyyyyyyyyyyyyy? yyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyyy [The massive hand of The Poet sweeps across the screen and tears out the page, crumples it, and tosses it routinely into the pile behind him. He let's loose a frustrated: "ARRGGHHHHH!" Once again he sets the cursor to the beginning of a new page and takes a deep breath. As the camera starts its fade to black, The Poet speaks.]
The Poet: "Okay, one more try." [The blackout is soundtracked to the melody of typing keys starting yet another unsuccessful writing attempt.]
