FREE AGENT - "The Greatest Ever" Shawn Walsh

10 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alright, I can't take it anymore. The boredum.. the horror.. of having no addictive bloodsucking life-taking away hobby to particpate in anymore. =)
So, I'm announcing my character "The Greatest Ever" Shawn Walsh as a free agent to any organzation on the internet. All offers brought to my address
[ [detail withheld] ] will be considered. Here's a sample roleplay whether you want to take time out of your momentary life on this planet to e-mail the studliest one of em' all. =)
* Brett [Fade in ...] [The nickname of Shawn Walsh, "Da' Greatest Ever" fades smack dab onto the center of the screen in big glitterly gold letters. A voice over then yells...]
VO: "PLAIN and SIMPLE!" [The screen then shatters like glass into millions of itty, bitty, tiny, pieces. They stay motionless for a split second, and then fly off the screen revealing nothing but complete silence, and pitch black darkness.]
[SCENE: The camera shot opens up to a US Airways chartered flight, leveled off, 30,000 feet high in the air. The passengers are seated comfortably, as we move from the coach seating section, to first class. The seats wider, the flight attendents much nicer, as many sit there talking, drinking there champagne. We then discover sitting alone, in the corner, near the door exit, the man with the plan himself, Shawn Walsh.]
[APPERANCE: Shawn wears his usual out of the ring attire, which consists of a plain white T-Shirt, a pair of black and white nike shorts, a pair of black and white nike cross trainers, and short white socks up to his ankles. His long sandy blonde hair shines brightly, tied back into it's usual ponytail.]
[Shawn then turns his head slightly, notices the camera, straightens his posture, and then speaks.]
SW - "Do you have to follow me everywhere?! I mean, for the love of god! I'M ON A CHARTERED FLIGHT!
But, ya' got get money to feed your kids, don't cha'?" [Shawn smiles, as we hear a *Click* and the voice of the captain.] CAPTAIN - "We are approaching our destination of Chicago, Illinois. We will be landing in approximately twenty minutes. Please be prepared and remember all carry on bags must be under the seat. Thank you!"
[We hear another *Click* as Shawn speaks.] SW - "Dumbass." [All of a sudden, a guy is screaming in the background.] GUY - "PEANUTS! I HAVE PEANUT ALLEGRIES!!!" SW - "Good lord!" GUY - "FOR THE LOVE OF GOD, HELP ME! HELP ME!" SW - "The nerve of some people! I'd outta' call the captain! Oh, wait. He's flying the plane." GUY - "GIVE ME WATER!" FA - "Please sit down and your seat! Thank you." GUY - "I'VE GOT PEANUT ALLEGRIES GOD DAMNIT!" [The passengers, a bit uncomfortable, continue to stay in there chairs.] FA - "Please, do not use vulgarity on the flight. Thank you!" SW - "Vulgarity? [Mumbling] (Bleep!) You! Take that stupi ... [The flight attendant turns to Shawn.] FA - "May I help you with anything?" SW - "Ummm ... A drink! Yeah! Let's see, I'm not a cheap-o like Pillow.. GIVE ME A MR. AND MRS. T BLOODY MARY! YEAH!" FA - "Your wish is my command." SW - [Mumbling] "Wise women." FA - "Pardon?" SW - [Out loud] "Nothing! Anyways, where was I? Oh, yeah! The UKCW! I sign this stupid contract! And then they throw me into this match right away! The nerve of some people!
Stupid meanieheads." [The flight attendant hands Shawn his Bloody Mary.] FA - "Here you go." SW - "Thank you. [Mumbling] Now leave!" FA - "Excuse me?" SW - "Nothing!" [The flight attendant walks away. Shawn takes his bloody mary and slaps it on his tray.] SW - "Now .. This little four man match thing!" [Shawn pops open the can and takes a slug.] SW - "Let's take a look at the situation. Three man trying to kick my ass! Not good. [Shawn takes another sip] But
I've got a master plan! I mean, I am the man with the plan, so it makes sense doesn't it?" [Shawn smiles]
Andrew "Flash" Tucker? Your just a rip-off of Mistah Kauffman! Plus, the true "Flash" is on Superfriends! That episode where the earth was drifting towards the sun and he had to run around the world with copper wire was a classic!
Francisco de La Moyne. [in his best french accent] May I have le snails? [chuckles and goes back to his normal voice] You sound like another addition to the
Hoover Vaccum Cleaner! Prepare to be smashed to be oblivion on Tuesday. Pedro Kozaro... [chuckles] Shit. I have absoultely to say about you. Damn you Pedro! Get a more jokable name!
[Shawn takes another sip of his Bloody Mary and continues.] And two mystery opponents! You _two_ guys are still going to be _mysterys_ cause no one is even going to know who you are I'm going to knock your damn lights out so fast! Another run-on. I need another cuecard boy. Yo, cameraman?
CM - "Yeah?" SW - "Is there even two mystery opponents?" CM - "No." SW - "Damnit Cuecard Boy! Do you know the one that works for Bozo?" CM - No." SW - "Damn. I wonder if he can get me on the grand prize game!" CM - "That's super Shawn." SW - "You buddah' believe it, sonny boy!" [We hear a *Ding!* as the captain's voice is heard again.] CAPTAIN - "We are approacing O'Hare Airport. Please turn off all eletronic devices, be sure to put your tray tables in front of you, and all carry-on bags under the seat until the seat belt sign is turned off and we are parked at the gate! It's been a pleasure flying with you and we hope to see you on another US Airways flight again."
[The *Click!* is heard this time as we hear the guy again.] GUY - "HELP ME! HELP ME! MY EYES ARE WATERING!" FA - "SHUTUP AND SIT IN YOUR SEAT! I DON'T GIVE A DAMN ABOUT YOUR PEANUT ALLEGRIES!" [The passengers gasp at the flight attendant as we begin to drop slowly.] SW - "Meanie flight attendant!" [All of a sudden, we hear the *Screech!* *Screech!* of the breaks on the runway, as sound is heard as the plane slowly comes to a complete halt. It then moves across the paved rows to it's gate.]
SW - "Yes! Landed! Oh, joy to the world!" [We then immeaditely park at the gate, as the sign flicks on, and everyone grabs for there bags. Shawn picks up his Bloody Mary from his lap at chucks at the peanut allegry guy.]
GUY - "NNNOOO!" [The door then opens to exit the plane as the flight attendent says..] FA - "Bye, Bye!" [Shawn smirks and steps off the plane.] SW - "At least it isn't total bastard airlines! UKCW, be prepared for the man that is just truly..
The Meanest... Leanest... Sweetest... Fighting machine of all! Be prepared UKCW.
