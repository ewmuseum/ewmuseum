[ FLASH ] "Suicidal" Kydd Riggs [ NcWa ]

23 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sup? Anyway, this a recent RP I've done for the NcWa. It's pretty good, and only the second one I did with this character. I am not looking for flames, I really want some help so that I can improve my character. So please be serious, and give me and tips, or suggestions you might have. Thanx. Later.
[...Fades in...] [ Scene ] [...It's a warm summers day in New York City, New York. This is where the
NcWa held a recent house show, and autograph session. It is quiet in the back, while each wrestler is in their respective locker rooms, getting dressed, taking a shower, and packing up. The dim hallways are unusually empty, and clean. Suddenly, someone comes walking out from around the corner. It's a tall, skinny, but well built man, wearing nothing but long baggy shorts, and a pair of black boots. He is looking down, reading something that he holds in his left hand. He starts to shake his head and laugh. When he looks up, you see that it is "Suicidal" Kydd Riggs. He walks right past the camera, as if it was not there. He heads down the hallway, and off into his locker room. The camera man slowly moves down toward his locker room, until he reaches the half open door. Inside, you see Kydd Riggs sitting down, going over some papers.
He looks up for a brief moment and starts laughing...] [ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "I've only been here for three days, and already I can't escape you guys. Damn, it's unreal." [ Scene ] [...He looks back to the papers, and continues laughing. After a moment or two, he throws the papers onto the floor and then leans back into his seat.
With a large smile still on his face...] [ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Fury, you know, maybe you're right. But then again, maybe not. Maybe I shouldn't come in here running my mouth. Maybe I don't know what I got myself into. Maybe I don't know what the NcWa is all about. But then again, maybe I do. [ pauses ] See, there are a lot of things that you don't know about. For instance, who it was who actually brought me to the NcWa. But ya' know what?
That doesn't matter right now. Anyway, I looked at the title histories a moment ago, and I see you have held your share of gold. One time US champion, two time IC champion. Not bad, not bad at all. But I also see that you've never hit the big time. That you've been around quite a long time, and never got that World title. Now you have yet another chance. But you know what's funny about this? You've been here damn near a year, maybe more. I've been here a matter of days, and already I have this shot. Fury, that should tell you something. Maybe your not as great as you say. Maybe you are. I'll find out won't I? The bottom line is, I am just as good, if not better than you, and that can be seen already. If that was not true, I would not be in this match with you."
[ Scene ] [...Riggs chuckles, as he reaches down and picks up the papers. He starts going over them again...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Now, you say you _have_ to be intense to make it here. If your not, you won't make it here. [ blinks ] The Reaper right? Yea, one time World champion, one time Insane champion. [ smiles ] But he didn't have what it took right? Well, you know what else I see on this sheet, that there is one man that you never beat in the NcWa. [ cough ] Reaper.
[ cough ] But what does that mean to me? Nothing! Well... Very little. But enough on that. As for being intense. Not all wrestlers here in the NcWa are intense, as a matter of fact, most men here seem pretty mellow and calm. But what do I know? I am just a stupid rookie right?"
[ Scene ] [...He puts the papers down on the table in front of him, and proceeds to take off his boots...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Fury, you are already making a mistake. You are looking at me, as if I am a rookie. As if I don't know everything there is to know about, not only just you, but everyone here in the NcWa. Like I said before, there are to many things you don't know. As for me coming from the streets, that has a lot to do with our match. Although you think it has very little. But see, I have fought with men as big, if not bigger than you on the streets. Men who thought the same way you do, and think the same way you do. The only difference between the two, is that there will be people to pull me off of you, those poor men had no one. But that's fine, have them pull me off of you. Then maybe, just maybe, there will be more of you left, for a later date in time. [ pauses ]
Now you seem to think I tried to put it over to you and the rest of the NcWa, that I am insane and intense. Well Fury, I don't think you were paying enough attention. I said I'd be more than glad to show you, and that you'd be my first example. I don't expect you all to believe that just because I said that. See Fury, already you are underestimating me. Trust me on this one, I know what I have to do here. You can think whatever you wa.... Ya' know what?
Forget that. Don't think whatever you want, because you might hurt yourself. See, you have got to be one of the dumbest bastards that ever walked the face of the earth. You say that working hard doesn't mean shit, and all that matters is what you do in the ring. Does that not include working hard? If you do not work hard in the ring, will you not come out victorious?"
[ Scene ] [...Riggs starts laughing as he sets both of his boots aside. He reaches over, and grabs a pair of Vans from his bag. He then puts the boots in the bag and stands up. He starts walking, but stops suddenly and turns around. He grabs the papers on the table, and then faces the camera again...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "I wouldn't want to forget these!" [ Scene ] [...He takes a few steps toward the door, and puts his hand on the light switch...] [ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Fury, I'm glad you think you are better than me. I'm glad that you think I am going to be that easy to beat. But the bottom line is, you don't know, nor does anyone else know that you're better than me. That's something that you, along with everyone else are just going to have to find out come BloodBath. [ chuckles ] I'll see ya' there Fury. I can't wait!"
[ Scene ] [...Riggs clicks the lights off, and the room goes into total darkness. You can see the outline of Riggs as he walks out the doorway, shutting the door behind him...]
[...Fades out...]
