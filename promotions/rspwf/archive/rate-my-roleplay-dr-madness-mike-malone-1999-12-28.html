[Rate my Roleplay] "Dr. Madness" Mike Malone

28 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First off.. this sucks and I know it. I need to know whats wrong with it though. -------------------------------------------- -[ Scene ]- On the video screen an image comes out of the darkness. As it fades in you can see some sort of shadow in a laid back position. A moment later you can see who the shadow is. It is "Dr. Madness" Mike Malone. He has the synical smile that he is famous for on his face. He is laying back in a
Lazy-Boy recliner with a bottle drink in one hand, and the remote control in the other. Dr. Madness is looking ridculous: wearing a pair of sweat pants, a white sweatshirt with "Die Santa, Die", and uncombed hair. "Santa is a Fat
Bitch" by the Insane Clown Posse softly plays in the background as he begins to speak.
-[ " Dr. Madness " Mike Malone ]- [Laughs] Ha! I haven't said much in the last couple of days now have I? That's all going to change now though.. I have a lot on my mind tonight, and I intend to make everyone of you sit their a listen through it. [Changes the channel] Lifetime, Home and Garden, and the
Christian channel.... I don't understand it. TV companies are responsible for being some of the dumbest people to walk the face of the earth. Don't they know nobody watches these channels.. Just plain stupidity... [sighs deeply, takes a sip of drink, and changes the channel]
Fortunately for all you inferior beings.. your tuned into the Madness Channel. Tonight on Madness TV we will be discussing one of wrestlings worst faults, that being Bruce Blade. Also known as the Butcher. Also known as my beating post this Saturday, et cetera, et cetera. Now Bruce.. at the time I was in the shower with a prostitute so I couldn't watch your little interview live, but I had my butler, who's name is also Bruce, tape the piece of horse shit so when I was bored as hell or possibly had a case of insomnia I could watch it. Well just a few minutes ago I got tired of watching "The Christmas Story" And
"Miracle on a street that I don't give a damn about" so I popped the tape into my VCR, and I listened to it. I was shocked when I heard it. It was idiotic, but not quite as idiotic as I expected.
You went through some stuff about the Fabulous Four or something like that. Doesn't matter. Don't care. The fact is you said it. After some of that BS, you got to me. You said you remembered me, which may be true. Then you said something that isn't true; you said we were friends! That pisses me off in so many ways you'll never no. I wouldn't be your friend if you held a machine gun to my head. On another note: your not a man of respect. I don't really respect you, so ya got nothing. Ca pish? I hope so. Understand this: I'm the one in control here. The Madness [pause for effect] owns you.
I'll admit your a far cry from those guys I always beat up on at the liquor store. Their always drunk.. and your well.. to pathetic to get drunk. Thats the main difference.I can hear you saying [In a small girls voice] "But oh Madness, why is it pathetic not getting drunk" The answer is because I said so. Now I might be getting high every night but I can have a little rum on Christmas Eve to keep me warm if I want. [Holds up bottle of rum proudly and takes a big gulp] I doubt your Mommy would allow it. [laughs] HEY! Chump. You can have your interview now.
-[ Scene ]- The camara moves over to show a man tied up with an extension cord. The man also has one of Dr Madness's old socks stuffed in his mouth. He is wearing a 3 piece suit, with part of it torn. Dr. Madness just laughs at him and takes out the sock, and lets him speak.
-[ Interviewer ]- [coughs] Ugh... Don't you think you need to unwrap this... extension cord....... it.....hurts........ -[ " Dr. Madness " Mike Malone ]- Ummm.... Your a real funny guy.. shut the hell up and give me the damned interview.. k. -[ Interviewer ]- [Gives a gasp of relief as part of the moldy extension cord breaks] Well.. this being your first match in the oWa, how do you feel so far. Are you impressed or what. -[ " Dr. Madness " Mike Malone ]- Like I said before I allowed you to speak, there are some people that are just plain dumb and retarted. I think your one of them. We haven't even had our first event yet your questioning me on do I like it or not. Your a real candyass you know that. Why did you ask that
queer-ass question. -[ Interviewer ]- Well.. [looks embarrassed] I'm a -- -[ " Dr. Madness " Mike Malone ]- Don't.. DON'T answer it. everybody's knows just by looking at you. Thats why i tied you up.. don't try nothing funny. I like oWa alright, next question.
-[ Interviewer ]- I.. ugh.. are you going to be joining the Fantastic Four or anything like that. -[ " Dr. Madness " Mike Malone ]- Your really trying to show your stupidty now aren't you? Fantastic Four is dead. And why would I want to join somebody's elses little group. I'm great enough just by myself. I have no need to be with anyone else. As you'll all see in my match against Bruce Blade tommorow, I have more wrestling skills than you might think. Just because I have been labeled as a madman doesn't mean I am a brainless half-wit. I'm actually pretty damn good. Just wait, you'll see. You'll all see.
-[ Interviewer ]- Are their any challenges you'd like to make? Or is their anyone you would like to compete. -[ " Dr. Madness " Mike Malone ]- The so-called "Main Eventers" always seem to piss me off some-how. Not because their Main Eventers. Its because they think their better than everyone else. People like Blair, Born to Kill, and others who think their greatest. Donchya know I could beat 'em all at one time? Of course you do. I'm not going to go right up and challenge them this early in the game, but sooner or later, wether they like it or not their gonna fall victim to the Madness just like everyone else will. It's simply a matter of time. I'm like a panther. First I stalk out my prey... then I kill! DIE! DIE!!
DIEEE!!!!!! Muwahaaahahahahah. NEXT QUESTION! -[ Interviewer ]- O.......K....... Then..... Your scaring me... so are their any other comments. -[ " Dr. Madness " Mike Malone ]- Theirs just a few.. Blade, I have never claimed to be the best... well yes I have. BUT, I have never insulted others with no good reason.. well yes I have. BUT, I can back it up at least. And you.. your just running your mouth off with no end for NO reason. Does it piss me off? YES, it does! Am I going to do something about it? YES, I AM! I am going to beat you to a bloody pulp and then, I'm just gonna plain destroy you.
'Nuff said! I don't have to sugar-coat it, or anything like that. I'm gonna beat your ass. It's easy enough to understand. Event to your meager brain. I know your somewhere wrapping little presents that you can't even afford, and your probably cooking the Christmas ham.. but if your out their and you can hear me. Remember......
-[ Scene ]- A L L F A D E S T O B L A C K
