[Free Agent] Jose' Tortilla

17 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the brain of [name withheld]. Last spring, when I first started role-playing, I created a crazy mexican. His gimmick was an obsession with tortilla chips and sex, rather than the common stereotype of beer-drinking mexicans. He's a whitty, humurous type whose use of the english language is slightly underdeveloped. None the less, he remains as the "1 POP, Can't Stop"
condom bursting, cherry popping daddy we all shall continue to love. I wish to get some legitimate offers, as I am pondering that this may be the very last federation for Jose' to stomp on.
small note* I am a slef-proclaimed master of foreign dialects. As in the role-play below, you'll see me artly capture the jamaican dialect. *
Below is a role-play I did for EWO, [name withheld]'s fed, which I think is closed?! [The camera focuses. We see a dark tanned figure with a sombrero walking down the streets of what is a very rural ghetto. It's none other than EWO's Jose'
"Little Sexy, Midget Killer" Tortilla! He passes by a corn stalk field. He sees many african-americans working extremely hard in these fields. Most of which have their hair tied up in dredlocks. The man moves toward a small building. He opens up the shotty screen door that leads into it. As he is well with-in the building. We see its a bar. The man spots to jamaicans. He grins as he walks up to the two jamaicans. He takes a seat down at a stool along the bar, situated right next to the jamaicans.
The jamaicans pause a conversation they had going. They both turn around to see Tortilla. Tortilla smiles, exposing a front row of gold teeth. He opens his wingspan. As he hugs the jamaican closest to him.]
Jose' - [using a jamaican accent] "Brudda mon! What be happening mon?" [The two jamaicans stare at one another. One is shocked the mexican touched him. He's not sure what to think. The first jamaican talks.]
Jamaican - "Mon. You don't look a t'ing like jamaican to me. Watcha be doing in here mon?" [Jose' looks down at his hawaiian pressed t-shirt. He pats each breast-plate pcket, feeling for his wallet. He then takes the wallet out of one of the pockets. He then pulls it out. He unravels the wallet. There seems to be a ton of pictures of children.]
Jamaican - "Whoa mon. If you come to get your groove with some of da' jamaican ladies, yo' shootin' yo' stackpipe where it don't belong, mon."
Jose' - [jamaican accent] "Hey brudda mon. You be looking at da' wrong pictures mon. Look here." [Jose' pulls out a picture of a man.] "I am looking for dis'
african-american jamaican mon." [The jamaicans glance down at the picture. They see a picture of an extremely pale white man. They give each other an inquisitive look.]
Jamaican - "Brudda mon. Whot kind of tricks you tryin' tah pull here?" Jose' - "I don't know whot your talkin' 'bout brudda mon." Jamaican - "Dat boy is caw-cay-shun." Jose'- "Caucasian? He be lookin' black tah me." [The jamaicans study the picture.] Jamaican - "Just who of this is a picture of?" Jose' - "Whot? You fellas ain't ever heard of ''Da Hamma'' Alex Cote?!" Jamaican - "Who!!" Jose' - "Dat bastard. He be tellin' me you fellas always be hangin' wit' him." Jamaican - "I neva seen da' white boy in mah life!" Jose' - "White boy?! He looks pretty black to me...." [Jose' and the jamaican both look at the picture.] "Yup. Dat boy sure look black."
Jamaican - "All-right brudda mon. I've had just about as much as I can take." [The two jamaicans grab Jose' pic him up, and throw him out of the bar.] Jose' - "Yah bastards. Last time I come to a jamaican for help!" [Jose' dusts off his clothes. He begins to walk out of town as he talks aloud to himself.] Jose' - "What's up wit' 'dis Alex Cote guy. He tellin' everyone he jamaican. Lyin' like dat. I bet dey jus' call him ''da hamma''' caus' he likes to spank his monkey 'bout all night long. Wouldn't be suprise to me if he tried to spank his opponent's monkey in dat ring too!" [Jose' thinks about that.] "I betta wear a cup...hope i can find one big enough for mah choad.
"Say. I know wah Alex lied about being Jamaican. See. Jamaicans are black. Black people gos' big choads. I bet yah Alex jus' wants to fit in. He can't accept da' fact I'm bigger than him in more areas 'den one!"
[Jose' wipes the sweat off his fore-head dripping down from the top of his skull.] Jose' - "I tell yah. Dis' hamma fella ain't gonna be smackin' monkeys no more. Jose' Tortilla is against domestic violence! Sure. I can see Alex thinking abstinence leads to a better life. But..., Alex, lemme tell yah from someone
'dat knows. Sex is...alot better 'den da' real thing. Look at all mah kids." [He again pulls out his wallet. The rolls of picture holders is so long it drops down to the ground.] "Thirty-fo' kids. Dey all came from different mommas too."
[Stops walking as he reaches a run-down bus stop.] "Hamma, yah best be ready for me. Dey don't call me Little Sexy, Da' Midget Killer fo' nuttin' yah know. I've slayed massive giants too. But seein' how your earnestly a..." [holds out his hand. He points out his thumb and pointer finger, an inch apart from one another.] "well....your a little bit smaller than me. I think I'll beat your smurf, monkey smack ass."
[The bus pulls up at the bus stop. The door swings open, as Jose' takes a step onto the bus. He deposits his change. He turns back to the camera.]
"Hamma...you like to smack; well, prepare for...THE WRATH OF MY WHACK!" [The bus door closes. As it continues on out of town. The camera focuses out...]
