Looking for an e-fed

23 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I know there are about a hundred and one e-feds that post and are looking for wrestlers, but I've been searching for some time and filling out application to various feds. If you're a fed owner and looking for a wrestler and an RPer, then I'm a good one( a cheesy line but whatever works). I've been around e-wrestling for several years now, but took a leave of absents after a fed called FWG murged with another big e-fed at the time and were called BUWE, but didn't go for to long. This wrestler I has made the top 200 of RSPWF in 2001 at #95 seed. That's what I needed to say and here's my application:
================= Wrestler Information ================= Wrestler's Name: Alex Jones Date of Birth: 10/29/77 Hometown: Toronto, Ontario, Canada Height: 6'0" Weight: 225 Alignment: Tweener ======== Entrance ======== [A cloud of smoke goes over the whole ring area.....then I Hate Everything
About You by 3 Days Grace starts to play over the speakers. Once the smoke disappears, Alex Jones is standing in the ring...]
BOB ANNOUNCER: Coming down to ringside, hailing from Toronto, Ontario,
Canada, standing 6'0", and weighing in at 225, 2 time FWG Young Stars title holder,
... ALEX JONES!!!!!! [Alex waits for the match to start...] ====== Style ====== Wrestling Style: Mostly technical wrestling, but a little bit of lucha
Physical Appearance: Ear long dark brunette hair, a small scare above his left eye , blue eyes that have gone green due to contacts. Small, but a very thick body.
In-Ring Attire: Alex wears a black trenchcoat down to the ring where he takes it off before the starting of the match. Under that, he wears a black tank top and black leather pants. His boats are also leather and are also black.
Top 10 Moves Used: Belly to Black Flip, Brainbuster, Dragon Suplex Pin,
Spinning Backdrop, Hurracaranna from top rope, Reverse DDT, Chicken Wing,
Missile Drop Kick, Northlights Suplex, Ankle Lock
Marquee Moves: Impaler, Frog splash and Boston Crab Finisher: Stone Crusher Technical name / description: Senton Bomb ========== Misc. Info ========== Wrestler's Personality? Angry Young Man that's wanting to help.... in a way
Background? (briefly): Alex Jones was a normal working man with a family. He lived in the subrubs and waited for his purpose in life. Not saying he wasn't happy with his life, it's just that he thought he had a greater purpose. It came quickly for him, when his family died in a sudden car crash. He discouvered himself that day and his purpose. To ease the pain, he decided to go into wrestling, but it's still not enough to help him.
Previous wrestling history? (briefly): Work in FWG briefly for about one and a half years, where he came in 95 in the RSPWF top 200 wrestlers of the year in 2001. He also worked in AVW for about 2 months before taking a long leave.
Primary strength: 1. Is very quick and speedy 2. Larger wrestler can't keep up very well Primary weakness: 1. There are small wrestlers, which can keep up with him 2. Once the larger wrestlers catch him, it's hard for him to get away once he's been beat down =========== Character Development Info =========== Angle you wish to pursue in GSiW: An angry young man looking for a way to confort himself. Title you wish to pursue: Any, it doesn't matter. Any past injuries or anything in your character's past that can be used in storylines ect?: The death of his family and an injuried left shoulder.
=========== Sample RP =========== [It's a dark and gloomy night, with storm clouds overtop, raining apon the streets like hell was from above. An old abandon warehouse stands tall in the rain. It looks as if it hasn't been used for quite some time and may fall over.]
[As the camera slowly moves from outside in the rain to the inside of the old abandon warehouse, gun shots go off.]
[The camera is now set inside the abandon warehouse.] [The camera is looking over a small torn apart ring that has the look of a thousand men have wrestled apon it's mat. A small dark figure stands in the mist of the darkness that's within the warehouse. The figure moves slowly in some kind of motion that can't be telled because of the darkness. A voice speaks out from within the darkness.]
Voice: I was once one of you. The simple folk that had no purpose in life, just to live life out until there was no tomarrow. I had a simple life. I lived in the subrubs with a small family that I could call my own. I had a decent job that I went to everyday. I was looking for a purpose, waiting for my time. I was waiting...
[The shadowy figure that was talking paused for a moment and moved into the only light that was pointing down at the ring. The figure happens to be none other than Alex Jones.]
AJ: I was waiting for my purpose. It came faster then I ever thought possiable. One rainy, dark day, just like this day, I kissed my son and wife goodbye and headed off to work, the same as I always did. I didn't know it at first, but it would be the last time I would say goodbye to them. They went out for groceries and ended up in a car crash, where they were both killed on impact.
[Alex pauses to take time to look back on the day of his families death. When he finishes looking back, he falls to one knee in the ring and sways his head back and fourth...]
AJ: What was my purpose?... What was my purpose?.... [He stands back up and regains himself again.] AJ: I found that my purpose was really my fear... the fear of losing everything. Not being able to hear my wife's voice, helping my kid with homework. That was everything to me.
[Alex shakes his head and thinks to himself before saying anything.] AJ: I should of seen it coming. No one ever has life that good, only the ones that lose it. My fear had came to collect what was its' own property.... my life!
[Alex looks up into the light that's shining down in the middle of the ring. He opens his arms and screams, then falls to his knees.] AJ: Everybody says you must have a fear of some kind... death, not being loved or never succedding. My fear was losing my life.... everything. Now, that I have lost everything, I have no fear left.
[Alex stands up and looks straight into the camera...] AJ: Now, I'm a newly formed person, one that lives life by a day at a time. Everyone must fear something, but me. I don't fear anything, anymore. My fear has come and passed me by and took everything with it. It took my heart along with it.
[Alex pauses, looks back at the light and then looks straight back at the camera with a gleam in his eye.]
AJ: Now I am the one to fear, not fear itself. I am the shadow that lurks behind you, wherever you may go. I am the one that you're hoping not to find... the fear that's within you. I don't want anybody to go through life without getting what's their's....
[Once agian he pauses with a tear in his eye, Alex speaks...] AJ: I will be the one that gives them their dues. [Once Alex is done talking the light from the middle of the ring goes out and with a whisper, he speaks]
AJ: I am the fear. [The voice stops and nothing else is heard from within the warehouse.] *Scene Cuts out*
