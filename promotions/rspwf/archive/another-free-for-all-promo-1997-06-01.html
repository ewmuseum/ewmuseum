Another Free-for-All Promo

1 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
REPORTER: I am Diane Whaley with channel 9 news. We are live here at Blockbuster Vidio. Some "athletes" are causing some commotion in this video store. We hope to get some words from them so let's go inside. (Inside Blockbuster, Dan and Mya Special can be seen talking to customers.) WHALEY: (walking up to Dan and Mya) Excuse me Sir, may I get some words with you... DAN: OH GOODY, the local news. Well I am sure Mya and I can arrange something that will help. WHALEY: (fumbling through some cue cards) Well, I have some questions that my producer would like for me to ask you. DAN AND MYA: Ask away. WHALEY: You are obviously athletes...But in what sport? MYA: We have been trained to compete in many sports but we are currently in NCW. WHALEY: (sweating profusely) Wh-Wha-What is NCW? MYA: NCW is the wrestling organization where Dan and I currently wrestle. (Reporter fidgets with her cue cards and then accidently drops them. Diane goes to pick them but Mya puts her foot on the cue cards) MYA: Will you stop messing with those?! WHALEY: (crying) P-Pl-Please help me out, my job depends on this interview. MYA: Sorry sister, you had your chance. Now get out my sight, (grabbing the camera man) but you will stay since we actually need you... (The reporter runs out of the store wailing and crying, meanwhile everybody starts to stare at the cause of the commotion) DAN: Now that we have everybody's undivided attention...Free-for-All, NCW's first supercard is on June 9 at the PedMall here in Iowa City. NCW has plenty of action for everybody. No matter what your tastes are NCW has it. For those people that like to watch European Babes in action, NCW has what you are looking for!!! MYA: For you nature show morons, NCW will also have a match between a Lyon and a Wolf inside a steel cage which is ten times better than National Geographic wildlife shows. DAN: (walking over to a customer) Hey pal, put down the war videos and get ready to go to the war zone where international wrestlers collide to win the titles and hold on to your seats as the wars will hit the Street Level. NCW first Supercard has all of this and more action than any video you could watch. You have no excuse not to attend because Free-for-All is just that, FREE! The price is right, the action will be outstanding and you will not be disappointed. MYA: The First NCW supercard will happen June 9. If you can't get a decent seat, watch it at your favorite bar or friend's house. You will see all the action in color, not black and white. (camera fades out)
