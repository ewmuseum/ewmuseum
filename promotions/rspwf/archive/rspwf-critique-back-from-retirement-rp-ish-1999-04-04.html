[RSPWF] Critique {Back from retirement RP} [ish]

4 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
hey everybody, this is the first time I've ever asked to critique my roleplay's but I need to get into a good federation and i need to perfect my flaws. So I would like lots of people to use some constructive criticism to help me out. I would deeply appreciate it. Thanx, well here we go. If you need explanation on his gimick then contact me, I'm trying to change his gimmick because he was beginning to lose a gimmick, but a dumbass is easy to transform =)...Well enjoy.
[You go to sit down and turn on your television. You turn it on and you it's just crappy commercials...so you switch it to a channel that has a lot of wrestling on it. Including the EWWF...There are commercials on when it switches back to a black screen...it then fades into the scene. The scene opens up to a room that's dark because there are no lights on. The sun is shining brightly through the closed shades inside the room to give what little light there is. In the room there's an unfamiliar face. A lone man who sits still pondering what to say, what to do. The camera moves around to come in close...but you can't see the man...your blinded by lack of light. Your blinded by the unwilling dark. The figure sits up in his seat in the fairly dark room full of shadows. One can only ponder, who is this? Where is he from? Is this a joke? Or perhaps...it's what we've all been waiting for...the chance to finally see the man, the legend. The legend that you've never seen.
Because he's just not there. Wrestling has lost what it once had. Interviews get boring and you fall asleep while listening to constant babble. Your looking for something more...your looking for somebody to mark for. Where is this man, where is this legend? Is this the man, the shadow, the legend you've been waiting for?
Man: ummmmmmmmmmm (All of a sudden the lights in the room flash on at once...your eyes blink from the transfer of dark to bright lights. As you squint you try to see...WAIT
THAT'S...THAT'S...IT'S, OH MY GOD!!! IT'S...)
Illusive Dumbass: NO! (IT'S ILLUSIVE DUMBASS!!! ILLUSIVE DUMBASS IS OUT OF RETIREMENT...OH MY GOD!!!!) Dumbass: Hello little bitchies...the king, the god, the holy one is back. That's right...I, the Illusive Dumbas, has came out of retirement...for something I've been waiting a long time to get my hands on. That one thing is to shock the world and show that a no name, a man without a championship , a clueless man can accomplish something that only a few men have been able to succeed in doing...and that one thing is...to win the EWWF Alliance Tourney of nineteen ninety nine! I will succeed in doing so...mark my words...I will
_not_ lose. It's no longer in my vocabulary. While I was retired I learned many things...I have became a better man, a better interviewer, a better wrestler. I have traveled the world and seen many federations. I've chatted with wrestler's and made some friends...I also made some enemies. It all comes with the territory. But I am, by no means a legend...I don't even know if winning this tourney will make me a greater...people will still look down on me...like I'm inferior...like I'm still not as talented and able as they are.
No matter what I do, what I say, where I go, how I do it...I will alway's be played down by the people who think i'm not in there "league"...I'm overated,
I'm not worthy. Well it's time for a change...now the Illusive Dumbass has decided to change his life and move on. I'm sick of the beligerent babble being put out there. Such as Guru, who mindlessly babbles on and on and on. A man who thinks by no means, that I am worthy of him. A man who probably thinks he has this entire tournament in the bag. He thinks that he will walk in and walk out as the greatest man alive. Little does he know that a revelation is beginning...a revelation that cannot be stopped, a revelation cannot be contained...a revelation that _will not_ be stopped nor contained. For I am bringing something to the floor that is way beyond talent, I'm talking courage,
I'm talking smarts, I'm talking about all the little things that make a good man, great. It makes a great man, looked up to. It makes a man who's looked up to by his fellow peers a legend. Then...when I accomplish that...I will become something even more...more than a man who's looked up to. More than a legend, even more. I'll become an immortal, a man who will be considered god.
Not an almighty god...there can only be one almighty, but there are many immortals, immortals who die in technicality, but live on in many other ways.
I will become that man, I will become that immortal. First is the tourney, then it's the world! There's Guru...a man I once dispised, but I just can't any longer...I can only pitty his lost soul. He is a man, not a god...he doesn't compare. Maybe his knowledge is superior. But he can no longer take my skills. He can no longer hide from me. He's lucky DECWF closed down before
I got to face him. I will finally get to have my revenge on a man that strives to mock people. A mocking that I will no longer stand by and take. I will not let his words get inside my head. I now know better. This time, I'm on top.
I will be the one to let the words lead into the ring. Guru...your just another piece of my game.
(Illusive Dumbass brings his hands up to his face. His face has a goatee that is shortly trimmed and isn't very big. Besides that, he's sporting a bald head. The camera pulls away and you can see the surrounding. Illusive Dumbass is wearing his usual Nike warm up outfit. It's black with white trimmings.
He's also wearing his usual Nike shoes, but there gray, not black like he usually has it. The room he's in is basically empty accept for a few dressers and the chair he's been sitting on. The rooms walls are painted a yellowish white. Which just emphasizes the lights in the room. The ground is nothing but wooden panels. There is also a closed closet and a closed door in the room.
The closet is to the far left and the door is directly behind Illusive Dumbass.) Dumbass: Let's move on... (The camera focuses in on Illusive Dumbass) Dumbass: There are not just two people in this match. This battle of the best, a fight to the end to crown a deserving champion. There is also a man named Wolverine. A man that seems to be mysterious to me. I'm perplexed by this man's intentions. At first he wanted War Child, a man who has nothing to do with this tournament. Perhaps he was uninformed, or maybe he's just trying to mark for people. I really am dumbfounded when it comes to this. He also goes from talking about how he dislikes somebody to screaming like a maniac while threatening to kill somebody. This man is usually heavily intoxicated and if you ask me he's just hurting himself. As you all know, my entire family accept for my father was killed by a drunk driver. I will never drink again.
Wolverine is just a runaway train. One day he's gonna do something from accident, something he can't take back. He's gonna destroy a life, and then regret it. He'll alway's wonder what if...Well Wolverine...what if you got your life back on track and focused on wrestling and not drinking or the harm you'll force upon somebody. Then maybe, and I say just maybe, maybe you'll be able to become a champ, or somebody who's looked up to. Because when I look at you...I see a man that is lost. A man that's got nothing to strive for, nothing to lose, but you don't realize there's plenty to lose. It's truly a shame...because Wolverine...right now your nothing more than a piece of my game.
(Illusive Dumbass does a mini stretch and rubs his eyes) Illusive Dumbass: Sometimes I wonder...how can people be so ignorant, or so blind, but now I know. I know many things, with time comes knowledge, with time comes wisdom. I am a wise man now. I know how to control everything, including my thought, my mind, my actions. That is why I reign superior. That is why I will one day be a god, a god of flesh. Now I shall move on to the last man, I save him for last for many reasons. Some of which are obvious, some of which are impossible to recognize or see. Johnny, you are perhaps a strong man, a man who's got potential. However I don't believe you know how to use that potential. Your a confused man that's got everything going for him...but you still don't realize it can get better. Your living in a winter wonderland. You act as though nothing can hurt you because you study tapes and profiles on people. Your wrong. Just because you won the the battle royal...that means nothing. I here that you've told people you were to champ, and flaunt it. That's bullshit, your a damn pansy if you think the world revolves around _Johnny Sledge_...Fuck no. It revolves around the sun...I can't believe that you think that your some great champion. Now I know you'll come back with something like "I feel nothing like that, you must have a mis understanding"...or something...you'll try to do something to make this difficult...but what I said...is what it all comes down too.
It all comes down to who wants this more. To you it's just another championship, to me it's a prestigious award that I would love to put around my waist and hold high. That's why I'm gonna win...that's why I'll remain victorious. Johnny Sledge...you merely just another piece of my game.
(The lights suddenly switch off, and the room once again becomes a room of shadows...and none of those shadows resemble that of Illusive Dumbass. The room is silent and without a single movement. The room is cold, a deep cold that sends chills down your spine for some unknown reason, you can only ponder to yourself, what's next, what could possibly happen next. Then you realize that only time will tell, so you turn off your tv and go in a room and think...you just think.)
That's it, I hoped you liked it, either way, post on how you feel about it. I wasn't worried about how many lines, I was aiming at quality over quantity.
Ish...would like everbody who read the rp to have some input, thanx. -Ish- Handler of the one and only Illusive Dumbass. Never lost in GNW. A GNW legend. CDW legend and US title holder.
