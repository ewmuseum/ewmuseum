[RP] "Blitz Lightning" Matt Kreig! [Please Critique]

10 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi guys. I know, I know... You guys hate this character. You hate his name
'cause it's too much like a WCW character, so I've heard. And I know, I all ready posted his intro RP, but this is my newest character and I want to keep improving him.
This is an RP for the TWL. There's a little story behind it... There's a
Cruiserweight Dance coming up. I thought that meant like a 10-way dance, but no... It's really a dance contest. This was a roleplay I wrote before I realised that it was really a contest, and I thought it was a wrestling match.
So, since it didn't end up counting for the fed, and it was never even publicized, I figured I'd post it here, to get peoples' comments and even some pointers along the way. Any help or ideas would be appreciated. But please, don't flame. Thanks....
---- [As the tape begins rolling, we see a zoomed in view of a beautiful half moon. There seem to be no clouds in sight as the shot slowly pans back to reveal more and more of the night sky. Star after star, planet after planet twinkles into view with no distortion, the brightest colors of the cosmos shining through to meet the eyes of... "Blitz Lightning" Matt Kreig. TWL's newest cruiserweight addition is sitting in the cab of a pick-up truck, the make undistinguishable in the weak light of the moon. He seems almost oblivious to the camera... to the world, as he stares into the heavens with obvious respect and interest.
Suddenly, without the tiniest motion, he begins to speak in a calm voice.] MK - I've always loved the stars. The best Christmas gift I ever got was my first telescope, which my dad bought me when I was, oh, seven or eight years old. It didn't have the strongest lens, the shiniest case, or the heftiest price tag, but it got the job done... I didn't need to see every star, my imagination took care of that. Still, a decade and a half later, I feel the same thing I did as a child when I look at the stars. I feel focused.
[For a moment, Kreig stops talking, as if he's just shared a truth so fundamental it need not be further expressed. The silence is almost eerie, until Matt starts up again, still not moving a muscle.]
MK - The Cruiserweight challenge match, or whatever it's called, is something that has grabbed my complete focus. My first TWL encounter, and it comes against some of the best Cruiserweights the world has to offer. And we all want one thing... I believe as a debut, this could be one of the toughest wrestling matches I've fought through. And I've done 'em all... Tag matches galore, cage matches, handicap matches, Texas Tornado matches. If there's any way to see who the number one contender for the Cruiserweight belt is, this has got to be it.
Because there's _more_ than just wrestling skill involved. Sure, if you're not keen and focussed in the squared circle, you're gonna get no where, but... This one's gonna take some thought; a real game plan. Of course luck has something to do with it too.
Heh, I know if I win, I'll be... [Points up.] MK - ... thanking my lucky stars. Oh believe me, I have every intention of coming out of that match the winner, and coming out known as one of the better cruiserweights that the Tough Wrestling League has to offer, but I can't do it by myself. I need to put on a show that the fans will never forgot, 'cause, heh, I need their support.
And if the rest of you, Devon Case, Dusty Love, Duncan Shearer for example, want to be known as the most deserving challenger for El Puerco's title, then join me. Put on a show, make a gameplan, wrestle your heart out. This match is gonna take it all... It's not just about the better wrestler. This one helps determine the better man.
[Nods.] See, it's all about drive. It's about getting up in the morning, and thinking to yourself, "I'm doing stuff today". It's about pushing yourself each and every day to go past the level you set for yourself the previous day. If you're not improving, then you're stagnant, and sooner or later, you're going to find yourself with a kicked behind and some broken teeth.
["Blitz Lightning" clears his throat, and hesitates slightly before continuing.] MK - What you all must be expecting is one whirlwind of a fight. If I have to take this one out to the walkway, on the floor or keep it clean on the mat, it doesn't matter. You must come prepared for a fight this weekend, and that means all of you, because that is what you're going to get.
[Matt looks up again at the stars and admires. His jaw drops as he's awed by the countless balls of fire that illuminate the sky. Then he speaks, still gazing.]
MK - Everyone in this match... _Every_ one is a star. We all have what it takes to be great wrestlers, we've proved it. To the suits, to the referees, to the fans. Some of us heros, some villains; but nonetheless, we're all stars. Only one thing, folks... I'm the only "Blitz Lightning," and come this Monday at
Massacre, the TWL, my nine opponents, and the entire world will see for the first time, that...
[Kreig looks directly at the camera. No smile, no fun-loving character... all attitude.] MK - Lightning _does_ strike twice! [The camera strays from Kreig's face and up at the stars, then back to the gigantic moon in the sky... Then fades to black.]
