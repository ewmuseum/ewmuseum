<free agent> Extreme Machine for opinions!!!

26 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following is a roleplay I did just to point out some things. ___________________________________________________________________ ::Screen fades in on total darkness. a familiar sounding voice starts to speak.:: Voice: There was once a time when wrestling wasn't about money. There was a time when Pro wrestling was real. There was a time when wrestling was about talent. There was a time when being Extreme meant going at it with an opponent for over two hours. How do I know that??? I watch A & E. Look at the sport today. It is pathetic. I mean, idiots like Goldberg, with a repetoire of maybe
3 moves at the most, on top of the world. Newcomers are stifled by the conditions of the sport that still host relics like Randy Savage, Hulk Hogan, and "Rowdy" Roddy Piper. Time change, and with new times, come new athletes.
But in this day and age, I am sad to say, no one cares about talent. No one cares about a double leg takedown into a Boston Crab. They want to see the best wrestling move in Hogan's arsenal: the chair shot. They want to see tables broken and blood flow. Although I can provide that, it is getting old for me. I feel it is time for a new Extreme. I feel it is time for someone else to take the reins and lead the sport into the year 2000. It is time...FOR A
CHANGE!!!!!!!!! ::The lights come on, and the place is more recognizeable. It is a gym, with weights and stairmasters and all that. In the middle of the gym there is a wrestling ring. In the ring we see who the voice is, as voice now has a face.
He is 5'8" and weighs 180 lbs. He has braided brown hair, marbeled eyes, a brown goatee, and hideous scars all over his body. His left arm has a tattoo of
IEUW with a big X over it. His right arm has a tattoo of ERUW on it. He is the
Extreme Machine. He is wearing his usual ring gear, black ERUW tank top, black shorts, and black combat boots. He starts to speak again.::
Extreme Machine: I am tired of everyone b!t｢#ing and moaning about who the
"ICON" is. Piper, Savage, and Hogan fighting over everything they want. All three of them actually are ICONs, of the retirement homes in which they live.
It is time for someone who doesn't proclaim himself an "Icon" or a "Phenom" or anything like that. It is time for someone who can change the sport forever. It is time for someone to take out the impression that older is better. It is time for someone who can take the reigns and lead the sport into the 21st Century. I am saying that is time for the Extreme Machine to prove to the world that he is more than just a Cruiserweight. I am read of the "Slasher" and what he had to say and I was appalled. I am not a member of EULW, nor do I intend to be. As I look at his foul language, I am enlightened as to the problems that plague my sport. How many times did he say f*** in that pathetic excuse for a segment???
And let me tell you something, the one thing I did not want to see was that little thing he calls a penis. The EULW is one of the most racist organizations out there. A wrestler by the name of Luke Van Damm uses a move called the "Jew
Blaster," what is up with that??? Is he and "Slasher" boy members of the KKK or the WAR??? KKK stands for Killers, Kowards, and K***s, while WAR stands for We
Are Retarded. Rascism has no place in this sport, and we get enough of it from
Vince McMahon and Eric Bischoff. It is time FOR A CHANGE!!!!!
::Mid to low card wrestlers gather around the ring as EM looks on. He smiles and continues to speak.::
EM: Damn, I feel like Martin Luther King for some reason. I am here to prove to the world that talented wrestlers have a place in wrestling, and it is not to job to the likes of Hulk Hogan and Bill Goldberg. We don't need tables breaking, that isn't Extreme anymore, it has been done before. We need something more Extreme, like throwing someone through a plate glass window,
Hitting an opponent with a car, or just plain good wrestling. Good wrestling, there is nothing more Extreme than a great match with plenty of moves and countermoves. That is what made the Dean Malenko-Eddie Guerrrero ECW fued so legendary. No broken tables, no chair shots, just damn good wrestling. Let me tell you something, you can't do much better than working your @$$ off to hear the fans chant "boring" over and over again. I swear, we have the fans sooo blood thirsty, that they don't care about good wrestling anymore. I am partially guilty of that, because I want to give the fans a good show. I don't do low impact cage matches, I deliver when I give hardcore wrestling at its best, and the keyword is Wrestling. That is what the sport is called. Its not
World Championship Bar-Brawling. or the the World Fighting Federation, it is
WRESTLING!!!! I'll continue to fly, but I am gonna bring it to the big time!!!!
I am gonna cause a change that will make this sport a whole helluvalot better. So tell me, who wants a change??? ::The wrestlers erupt.:: EM: What do you want me to do??? The wrestlers: Take the sport to the EXTREME!!!!!!! EM: You got it!!!!!! ::Screen fades to all black.:: ________________________________ I may do this more often. Soon I will put up a friend's RP to get opinions. Extreme Machine
