[]The scene opens in a little white room with a couple of spotlights shining upon Eric Emerson who is sitting upon a steel folding chair. Eric is wearing a black T-shirt that says, "High Society" upon it in Green neon writing, a pair of black Tommy jeans, and Dr. Martins. Eric runs his hands through his hair as he begins to speak[]
Eric Emerson: Well, I guess the future for Eric Emerson has already been set in motion. With Johnny glamour teaching me what he knows, and with my up coming win over Candy Man... [cocky smile]... [pauses]... So, I guess with all that going down you could be looking at the next International champion.
Whatever the case may be I'm going into Spring Slams with an open mind... [pauses]... Not because I think Candy Man can beat me, but because I'll be hunting "Call Girl" down and possibly get a little lovin before my match up...
[cocky smile]... Speaking of that lucious lady I told you what I would do to your precious lover boy. I told you I would walk to the ring and get a victory and what did I do? Yes, like I called before the match started I did indeed get the victory. Anyway, I'm through with Emu, and I want you "Call Girl" I want your pretty little ass sitting upon my perfect thigh. Face is "Call Girl" you want me and that's that...
[]Eric looks around a for a moment and then cracks his neck[] Eric Emerson: Now, I have a little something to say to a few men that need a wake up call. Candy, Liam... [pauses]... You two men think your above me, and for that you have been delt with. Candy, Glamz and I have tricked you once, and for god sakes I have knocked you down to your knees numourse times.
Liam, you and I have had a few words of choice for each other and I have basically had the upper hand the entire time, but now it will be you and Candy against the IWC World Champion, and me... [long pause]... Eric Emerson. Well, I get my hands on spam Man at Spring Slams so I guess this will be a little warm up for "The Great One". You see Candy, I do have a plan going into Spring Slams and it goes a little something like this... [pauses]... I will humiliate you and hurt you until you no long think you can beat me. I know what your thinking right now Candy. I know you think your going to walk all over me, but think ...
Think about the past two week when Glamz and I tricked you. Think about the time where you humiliated yourself and dressed up like a Bear. Think about what happened to you once you hit the ring with that stupid ass costume on? I beat your ass down and handcuffed you. I hit you will the your own finishing move...
[cocky smile]... Now think what you want Candy, but the facts are set in stone. I beat you around the ring not once, but twice and it won't stop there. []Eric stops and begins to rub his chin. He looks aver his shoulder as if he hear something, but then turns back around. He runs his hands through his hair again as he begins to speak[]
Eric Emerson: That's right Candy. It's only just begun because this
Tuesday you and I will be in the ring against each other, who knows what will happen. It could end up being you and I only in the ring with Glamz knocked out along with Laim. Then everyone would be so happy because it would only be a sample of the thrashing your going to receive. Hear me once Candy, and hear me for the last time... [pauses]... You will find out why making money is harder on your body then anyone thinks... [cocky smile]...
