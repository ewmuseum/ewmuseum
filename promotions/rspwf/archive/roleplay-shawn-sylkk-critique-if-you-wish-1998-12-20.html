[ROLEPLAY] Shawn Sylkk (Critique if you wish)

20 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's the first Shawn Sylkk RP for a packet I've done in about two months. Please flame it or critique it... or just totally ignore it if you want. =) [The scene is outside Shawn Sylkk's home in Long Beach, California. He's washing down his black 1999 Acura Integra on this cloudy California day with a dirty white rag. The radio is on and blasting as "It Ain't My Fault" by Silkk the Shocker plays loudly from the car. Shawn Sylkk smiles and looks at his reflection on the car's hood.]
[Sylkk is wearing blue Levi's shorts that fall past his knees, showing off a tattoo of the Dru Hill dragon on his right calf. He's wearing black sandles with a white Nike swoosh across the top of the velcrow strap as well as white socks. He has on a plain black tank top and a black bandana tied backwards around his head.]
[Sylkk throws down the rag down in a nearby bucket full of water and walks away from his car. He admires it from a few feet back and then sits down in a nearby lawnchair. He reaches into his right front pocket and puts on dark
Armani sunglasses with a black frame. He looks at the camera and begins to speak...]
SHAWN SYLKK - I'm goin' to cut right to the chase. This Wednesday night; myself, "Top Quality" Shawn Kross, Eric Emerson, "Dangerous" Dusty Love, Doomsday, and "Smoke Fiend" Sam Attic are in a battle royal. Hell if I know how the match came about, damned if I care. For someone that hasn't wrestled for over two months, I'm bein' thrown into one hell of a lion's den, aren't I?
[Smirks] But provin' people wrong and wrestlin' against all odds is one of my strong points. Let me get down to the competition here.
[Sylkk pauses and then puts his hands behind his head. He resumes talking...] SHAWN SYLKK - "Top Quality" Shawn Kross. That sounds like some kind of defective clothin' line. Kross, at the CWC Palace of Horrors this Wednesday night, I'm goin' to make sure you feel like the southern end of a northbound jackass. I'll slap you so silly that you're goin' to be lookin' for a corner in a round room come Thursday mornin'. Kross, try and hold a candle to the
Sylkksta and watch the candle get burnt out. When the candle burns out, you'll just be lost in the dark like all the other Sylkk victims there've been in the past.
Now we have Eric Emerson. I've heard a lot about you, much of it I couldn't really care less about. The fact is, your face will look like a deformed baboon's ass if you slip up and get caught in the Sky-High Sylkk Drop, then dumped up and over the top rope. The fact is that the Sylkksta's plannin' on big things here in the CWC and I guarantee a whole lot of ya that I will be a major star here in the CWC given the proper oppertunities.
[Sylkk sits up and rotates his head, causing his neck to crack. He lies back once again and resumes talking.]
SHAWN SYLKK - Next up we have "Dangerous" Dusty Love. Finally, someone I'm familiar with. Love, I know you're friends with some chump named Tony Paseno.
So, what I want you to do is ask him how it feels to be beaten by me twice so you can get a good feel of what losin' to the Sylkksta is all about. And if talkin' to him doesn't convince ya that losin' to me is a common thing, you best get in that battle royal and step toe-to-toe with the Sylkksta so I can show you firsthand how it feels to be smacked around, beaten and humiliated by me. I know one of your catch phrases is that "I'm gonna love you to death."
Well, Love, you best better learn to love tastin' defeat because as long as you're in the Sylkksta's way, you're gonna be tastin' it over and over again and you better pray that it tastes like chicken. [Smirk]
Onto Doomsday. I've been around this business long enough to know that there are about seven dozen Doomsdays out there and I can bet my bottom dollar and your monthly wellfare check that you're as worthless and as talentless as the others. On Wednesday night, I'm walkin' into the Palace of Horrors and I doubt that you're goin' to prevent me from takin' the victory. Doomsday, your name was probably made to intimidate, but it'll serve you no purpose other than for me to write your name down in the "Shawn Sylkk Scrapbook of Defeated Rubber
Testical Spotlight Observers" which means that you're in my scrapbook of rubber testical wannbes who thought they had what it took to beat Shawn Sylkk, but ended up flat on their backs observing the spotlight while their shoulders were down on the mat and bein' counted down for a three.
[Shawn Sylkk sits up and peers at his car. He puts the aunglasses back up to cover his eyes and then sits back once again. He resumes talking.]
SHAWN SYLKK - You know, last and certainly the least is "Smoke Fiend" Sam
Attic. Attic, didn't you listen to Smoky the Bear when you were young? Stay away from fire. Attic, that means you have to stay away from me because, quite simply, I'm as hot as the sun and when you get too close to the sun, you get burned and I sincerly hope that you try to get close to me so I can smack you into your 500th gimmick. So, Brett Fury -- er Slapjack Jett-- awe damn it, I meant Hardkn... no, that ain't it. Oh yeah, Sam Attic. [Smirks] Attic, step on up to the plate and try to knock a homerun but I guarantee ya, you're not Mark
McGwire, but I damn sure can play the part of Kevin Brown and throw you strikeouts, not to mention collect about $105 million. [Smiles]
Boys, when we get right down to it, not only am I... ...SMOOTH... ...AS... ..._SYLKK_... ...but when compared to me... ...ALL FIVE OF YOU ARE ONLY _FOREPLAY_... ...AND I'M THE WHOLE DAMN _ORGASM_!!! And whether any of you like it or not, I'm walkin' out of the ring with my hand raised and it'll become an often-seen sight in the CWC, so all of you in the
Chicago area best get used to it.
[Scene fades to black.] -CJ --Bored enough to actually get AOL back Handler of: Shawn Sylkk "You're only _FOREPLAY_... ...and I'm the whole damn _ORGASM_!!!" *International Championship Wrestling *Los Angeles Wrestling *Chicago Wrestling Corporation [37-5-4] -Voted ICW's Wrestler of the Year -Voted ICW's Most Improved Wrestler of the Year -"Smooth As Sylkk!!!" voted best ICW Catch Phrase -Former ICW International Champion -Former DPW World Champion -Former JPW World Tag Team Champion -Former ICW North American Champion -Former DPW United States Champion -Former JPW Television Champion
