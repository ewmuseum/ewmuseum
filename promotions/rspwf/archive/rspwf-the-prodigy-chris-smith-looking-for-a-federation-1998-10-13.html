[RSPWF] 'The Prodigy' Chris Smith looking for a federation

13 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys Coop here. I have come up with my newest creation, 'The Prodigy' Chris Smith. He is looking for a fed and I almost _NEVER_ post here on RSPWF. But I need a new fed to try him out in. Here are a few guidelines: 1) _MUST_ be a booked federation. 2) No AOL feds(Meaning must be closed Roleplaying) 3) Must keep a rankings and win-loss record 4) Must be by Email. No message board and web BS. Send all apps to [detail withheld] Heres a sample Roleplay. ================================================== --[Fade in.]-- --[Camera fades into the outside of a city and into the sign which reads *WELCOME TO SEASIDE, NJ.* The camera fades through the town as it seems to be a wet rainy day. There are very few people seen walking through the town. The camera then fades into the almost empty boardwalk. Not one person is seen anywhere in sight. A few surfers are seen in the ocean taking advantage of the higher waves due to the storm. The camera then fades into the front of a house right on the boardwalk. The house is a two story house with a big front porch.
As the camera zooms in a man is seen sitting on the back porch on a nice beach chair. The man is seen to be just laying out looking over the ocean as the storm hits. The man then sits up and sits on the other end of the chair.]--
--[The man is seen to be dressed in a pair of baggy khaki Old Navy Cargo pants. He also has on a White Tommy Hilfiger T-Shirt. His short brown hair is combed straight down, and his red and black Michael Jordan sneakers are tied loosely on. He also has on a pair of glasses.]--
--[The man removes his glasses looks deep into the camera and he begins to speak.]-- MAN - What a crappy day here on the Jersey shore. Anyways, ladies and gentlemen I have brought you all here for one reason. You are all looking at the newest sensation to hit this sport in months! The sport of professional wrestling. A sport of pure talent. But you see, I exceed all the talented wrestlers in this business. I am going to take this business by storm. That I promise you. Oh yes this business will _CHANGE_ once I hit the rings. Now Im sure each one of you out there are just wondering who the hell I am. Well I am a man known as:
__'THE PRODIGY' CHRIS SMITH__!!! --[Stands up off of his chair.]-- 'THE PRODIGY' CHRIS SMITH - You see, I have been watching wrestling since I turned three years old. I have seen all the greats come and go. I grew up watching the likes of Rick Wright, Fang, Tommy Rifles run this sport. And even though two of the three still wrestle to this day, I think its time for them to hang up the boots. Then when Shane Slaughter was the big stuff around.
And now up until today I see men such as Shane Alexander, Evan Manchester, and a lot of other talented superstars making names for themselves in this business. And I must say they are all quite impressive but _NOTHING_ and I mean _NOTHING_ compares to _ME_!!! I am the best wrestler, and as I said before I am going to take this business and just light it up with the talent I have.
You see when I turned eighteen years old I had finally saved up enough money to go away to pro-wrestling school. And that I just did. I trained for two long grueling years to finally be able to think I can survive in the big time. I was trained by the man to this day is making a name for himself in ERIC COOPER. A man I have nothing but respect for.
Then after my two years of training I thought it was time to go into the ring for a promotion. And I just did that. I signed on the dotted line with Boardwalk Brawl Wrestling(BBW) and in my first match faced a man by the name of Brian Jacobs. And what happened?! What do you think happened?! 'The
Prodigy' defeated him with the most devistating maneuver to ever be created.
The move known as the 'Prodigy'. A move not one man will ever, and I mean
_EVER_ be able to kick out of.
After bringing myself to a record of twelve and zero in the BBW, I then finally had a match with then BBW Heavyweight Champion. The most challenging match I have ever had and who came out on top? Who do you think?
[Laughs.] _ME_!! Once again beat him with the most devistating maneuver in wrestling. I held onto that belt for 8 long months beating star after star.
And then four days before my twenty first birthday I am named WRESTLER OF THE
YEAR in BBW. I then on my twenty first Birthday announced that I am dropping the belt, why? Well because the BBW isnt big enough to hold the talent of
_ME_!!!
I am six foot 5, two-hundred and thirty-four pounds of the greatest wrestler you'll ever see. And soon enough, Chris Smith will be making a few appearences in a few organazations. And all I can tell you is this, THAT I
WILL BE A WORLD CHAMPION!!! I will win the World title in any organazation.
And you can quote me on that one. I am challenging anyone out there to sign me even if its for just one match, I will show each and everyone of you just why they call me the
'Prodigy.' I will show you all just why I AM THE BEST DAMN WRESTLER ON EARTH!!! As a man in the big time once said 'To be the Man, youve got to beat the man,' AND IM THAT MAN!!!! --[Camera fades as Chris Smith takes a seat.]-- --[Fade out.]-- ============================================== Brought to you buy: Eric Cooper Handler of: 'Five Stars' Eric Cooper [L.A.W., IWC, ESWA] 'Absolute' Aaron Anderson [GWI] 'Wild Thing' Christian Hawk [Currently inactive] and 'The Prodigy' Chris Smith [Currently looking for a fed.] And also President of the PWO [Philly Wrestling Union]
