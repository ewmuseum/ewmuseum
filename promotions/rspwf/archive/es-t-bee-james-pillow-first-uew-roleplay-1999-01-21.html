"ES T BEE" James Pillow - First UEW Roleplay. .

21 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE:The camera opens up to outside of the UEW head office buildings. It's a bright winter afternoon, a slight chill is in the air. .but not enough to tell it's even wintertime. The camera moves around as we see cars driving around the streets as well as people walking up and down the sidewalks. he camera continues moving closer to the building as we se a few people entering and a few leaving through the swiveling doors. Three people made there way out and the first two walk down the steps and out of view as the third one stands in front of the building for a moment looking up at the sky, squinting slighly. We move in closer to him as we realize that it is none other than James Pillow. .
.] [APPEARENCE:James has on a black dress shirt with white zig zag lines, a pair of khaki pants and a long black trench coat. He also has on a pair of black adidas sneakers, and a piece of paper in his hand. The camera slowly zooms in on James as he merely flashes his usual, and very much expected arrogant grin.
He rolls the paper up and taps it agaisnt his chest as he begins to speak. .] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - What possibly could this paper be? It could be many things. . .No, no, don't go hurting yourselves trying to think of an answer because I'm going to come right out and tell you. It just happens to be a contract. . .a UEW contract for yes, yours truly. Th time has finally come for me to step into _THE_ federation, into the U E _FREAKIN'_ DUBBYA! The place that seperates the men from the boys.
[James reaches into the pocket of his trench coat and pulls out a pair of black oakley sunglasses and pputs them on. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - I was here not to long ago in the managerial position for two proteges of mine. .but it's much different now. Now I am going to be lacing up the boots and stepping into the ring with the so called best in the world of wrestling. . .and yes, they have the cream of the crop here in the
UEW, but no matter how good any of them are, I'm still gonna be a cut above all the rest. From Vinny. .excuse me _V.D._ Vitrone, to Marcus Manson, Candyman, to
Liam Rogan all the way upta the Top Dawg himself, Rick Styles. The very best. .
.but let's face it, they may be the best of the ebst in the world of wrestling but there's only one man who is Simply The Best and that my friends is _ME!_
[James opens the trench coat up a bit and places the contract into his pocket then closes it back up. James looks down a bit, his sunglasses come down to the tip of his nose. . .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - And each and everyone of you are going to see just why that is. Give me your men at the bottom of the ladder, the middle of the ladder, the top contendors and the champions and I'll hand you back broken, bloody, and beaten men. So I guess this can serve as a warning to all those currently competing in the UEW because there's a new sheriff in town, and it'll only be a matter of time before gold just finds itself around my waist weither it be the TVtitle or all the way up to the Ultimate title. . .it doesn't matter because everyone is going to find out exactly why I am. . .
SIMPLY. . . THE. . . BEST!!!!! [James ips the glasses down further and grins. .] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - So you all better just get used to it, learn to live with it 'n most importantly. . .
. . .LEARN. . . . . .TO. . . . . .LOVE. . . . . .IT!!!!!! [James pushes his sunglasses back up and looks up at the sky. He then begins to walk away and down the steps to the sidewalk as the camera pans back and slowly. . .]
