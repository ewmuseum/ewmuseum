[Vote for me] Illusive Dumbass Roleplay (1/3)

9 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens up to a gym where the almighty champion of champions who has actually never won a champion. He's the king of kings without ever being a king. It's none other than Illusive Dumbass. Illusive Dumbass is sitting in a chair in front of a television. He looks to be sleeping. He's still has sweat on him from a previous work out. He suddenly starts to snore which confirms that he is indeed a sleep. The camera pans around the gym where you can see a vast amount of equipment. You might even say it's a fitness buff's heaven.
It's got everything you could possibly need and then it's got a lot of extra's. Besides all the equipment such as a leg press, bench press, free weights, assorted bars, weight's, various weight machines, and a machine for every muscle of the body there is also a ring, pool, jacuzzi, a dressing room, showers, mirrors galore, and a nicely furnished area. It's also got a waiting room which is like a living room. That's where the sleeping Illusive Dumbass is...who may I add is still sleeping. The camera pans around again and focuses in on a sign that says "Welcome to the Illusive Gym." The camera pans around again to look at Illusive Dumbass. Illusive Dumbass is sitting in a comfortable leather recliner. It's black leather and leans back pretty far.
Illusive Dumbass is wearing black and white Nike sweat suit. You can still see the sweat marks. He is also wearing a black Nike hat with a white Nike symbol.
Along with his usual black Nike shoes. The camera then walks closer to Illusive Dumbass where the Camera man takes the camera off his shoulder so that he can tap on Illusive Dumbass's shoulder. After tapping he quickly gets the camera back to ready position)
Illusive Dumbass: (Just waking up) What!!!? What the do you want?!?! Who is it?!?! Camera man: Umm I'm a camera man hired by Rob Dalton for the EWWF Alliance. I'm here so you can do an interview about the upcoming tournament. Illusive Dumbass: (Just now starting to realize what the hell's going on) Who??? Oh yeah that's right...I forgot. What time is it? I told you to come at eight o'clock.
Camera man: ummm it's eight o' clock sir. (Illusive Dumbass looks at his watch) Illusive Dumbass: Well, I'll be damned...you know what it is don't you...It's those damn Guru interviews. They'll put you out like a light. So if you have any children out there who are having trouble sleeping than buy a good old Guru interview...it's a sure way to put you to sleep. That's if you kid doesn't scream to death about turning that kind of shit off. Damn I'll tell ya Guru is a piece of work...He still hasn't learned to shut his mouth when he has nothing to talk about, but he can always seem to talk about nothing for the longest periods of time...I guess that's the annoying ass Guru for ya. In fact he could go on for hours about his "bastion of cipher," "Uncircumcised Schlong,"
and let's not forget your "son of a miscreant." Well, anyway's I glad I slept through it...I wonder if he said anything about me...damn!!! Oh well I'll have to check later...I can't believe I'm gonna have to go through his interview again. I'll just have to put it on closed captioning (Thank Cline for that one).
(Illusive Dumbass gets up and starts stretching. He yawns a few times and then sits back down. He then turns off the VCR and TV with a remote controller)
Illusive Dumbass: Well as you all know it's time for the Alliance tourney again. This is the first time for me. In fact I'm only here because I had to suck up my pride and go begging to Dalton to let me in. I'm so glad that it worked. Anyway's if you ask me a wild card is better than nothing. I mean hell...technically I'm ranked in the top 8 in the EWWF Alliance, and that ain't bad. Just to get a chance to beat the shit out some people in the greatest event of all time is good enough for me. I don't even care if I win. I just want to get my hands on Guru for the first time. Guru's nothing but an overpriced over thinking loser who believes in a different god than everybody else and let's not forget that he has a drug addiction. Damnit Guru!!!! I wish you would just shut the hell up!!!! I'm sure now I'm going to have to watch your next interview also. Which will be loads of fun! Not to mention
I'll get to see all the neat little criticism names that you throw out OH so often!! The last one you used was...was...Oh yes! Reclusive Dumbass.
Unfortunately I don't have the time to talk about my name and make fun of yours...and so on and so forth. Guru is just a big waist of time.
Then there's always everybody's favorite whiner...JP Blood. I'll get my hands on you one way or another...Whether it be for the title belt in DECWF or in the tourney...actually I guarantee I get you in the tourney. It's gonna be so nice not listening to you whine and bitch about every little thing that bothers you...it's kind of annoying...actually it's really annoying. Not that I don't respect ya...because I do, but like I've said before I would like nothing better than to start to make you bleed. In fact I'm counting on some bodily harm to be inflicted...on my part. It's gonna be a great day...I can't wait!!!
Blood your an obstacle. I think you will be my toughest opponent. Other than you there's nobody else that poses much of a threat.
(Illusive Dumbass walks over to the leg press and puts on a fourty-five pound weight weight onto the left side. He then puts three more onto it)
Illusive Dumbass: I've looked over the tourney roster. I saw Crypt Keeper...who've I've never heard of, and haven't heard an interview from yet. Then I saw c4...yup...c4. I don't understand why a damn german who can't even speak american would think that he's tough shit or something. It's kind of pathetic. c4 is just some damn freak of nature that Guru happens to like.
This c4 guy thinks that 7 other people are gonna quit just so he can win. This guy doesn't even know what this tournament is about. He say's that he was told to come here or something. I mean...com'on is this some kind of joke. I can't believe a president of there respected federation sent a german who doesn't know what he's talking about or what he is doing to represent them...what were they thinking. I dunno...and I don't care because the more the easy wrestler's the better. Hell I don't care if they pack the ring full of has beens or losers. That's just better for me...so I can get my hands on Blood and Guru.
(Illusive Dumbass walks around to the other side of the leg press and puts four fourty-five pound weights on also. He then goes to sit down in the leg press as the cameraman moves so that he can get a shot of Illusive Dumbass's face)
Illusive Dumbass: Let's not forget the Firestarter. Watch out everybody...he my start a fire!!!!!! Thank you, thank you...one of my many impersonations.
Well firestarter is a somebody...I know that much...but he's a great somebody. He's just another guy who's trying to make a name for himself...I don't blame him. Except his hopes of winning are definitely not in the picture...if you catch my drift. Then there's alway's that crying piece of shit Johnny Sledge.
What's with this guy? He's like some guy straight out of a cartoon or something. Hey...johnny...do you actually think that your the only one to have a hard life. If you do your living in Candy Land...because that's just not the way it is...half the people in this sport have never had it good. My parents passed away at the beginning of my career which halted everything...but now isn't the time nor the place. This is the best of the best...the elite of wrestler's and your wasting everybody's time about this place that you love to go when you were a kid...I'm mean...that's sick. For the first time in my life
I'm gonna have to agree with Guru...who gives a shit about your foes in your federation...can I have a show of hands...(starts to look around the room)...that's what I thought...NOBODY!!!!! So get a damn clue and talk about something important. Liiiiike the upcoming tournament for instance...DING,
DING, DING...correct answer...so wake up damnit!!!
(Illusive Dumbass begins to extend his leg and then draw them till his knees touch his chest. He does this 5 times and then he stops by putting the weights on the rest bar)
Illusive Dumbass: Lastly there's Wolverine...who's fucking went nuts...I mean what the hell is wrong with this guy...and for some reason he kept saying
Ounce...and I'm like ounce of what? OUNCE OF WHAT?!?! I still don't know...oh well perhaps it's one of those things that your never going to find the answer to. Well anyway's Wolverine is the representative of the fed in which I began my career. GNW was a good place to me. When I first started my career there I went undefeated until my whole family except me and my father who were in the car with my Mother and two brothers. A drunk driver hit us. I received broken ribs, broken collar bone and two fractured wrists. My father is paralyzed from the legs down. He's in a wheel chair and is my only reason for living in my life. After this accident I was out of GNW for 3 months. When I returned I went undefeated again until GNW closed down and became CDW. I was undefeated in GNW. I am a GNW legend if you will so Wolverine I'm your damn mentor...you should call me papa because I could teach you a few things. GNW was a great place and I'll never forget it except I don't have time to talk about GNW.
Instead I've got to be training for the Tourney. For all you wrestler's...get your panties out of a bunch and stop crying...till next time...this is Illusive
Dumbass signing off.
(Illusive Dumbass waves the camera off and tells the camera man he can leave. He then begins to do some more leg presses. Instead of doing 5 he's doing ten. The camera man walks out backwards until he walks out of the gym. Fade to a black that's got a touch of green and blue in it)
Ish...I did this roleplay
