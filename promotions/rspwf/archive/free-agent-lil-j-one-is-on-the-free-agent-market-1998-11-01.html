[Free Agent] Lil J One Is On The Free Agent Market

1 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alright, ladies & gentlemen. Lil J One is finally on the free agent market after being tossed around like a ping pong ball for a month. I'm looking for an established fed[That means if you haven't been around for 2 months leave me alone.]. Any format, webpage, e-mail, message board.
Wrestler Name: Lil J One Height: 6' Weight: 212 lbs Hometown: South Brooklyn, New York City, New York Age: 24[4/10/74] Wrestling Style: All-Rounder, but mostly an aerial assault 6 Favorite Moves: 1. New York Legsweep(Russian Legsweep.) 2. East Side Driver (Death Valley Driver.) 3. NYC Blockbuster (Leaping neckbreaker from the middle rope.) 4. Swinging Neckbreaker 5. Hurricanrana 6. Guillotine Legdrop Finishing Holds: 1. The Bronx Bomber: Corkscrew Legdrop from the top rope. 2. The Eastsault: Standing Moonsault Nationality: 1/2 Italian 1/2 Puerto Rican Skin Color: White Hair Style: Short, and Well Kept. Build: Trim Appearance: As stated above, Lil J One a young white man, with a trim build, and short well kept hair. His eyes are dark brown. He has many scars, from the street fights that he used to get into, and from some of the extremely violent matches that he has taken part in. The most notable one is on the back side of his right shoulder. It stretches from there all the way down to the middle of his back, and to the spinal cord. He also has 2 tattoos one of them is just below his knee on his left leg. It is of his name, but is vertical. The other tattoo is across the upper part of his chest, and it is the word E- Thugz. It is filled in with lines going through it, and drops of blood look like they are dripping from it. In-Ring: He wears long black tights with blue trims, and scattered across his are logos of all the New York sports teams. He also wears Wrestling
Boots. That have the initials LJO in cursive on each boot. On the trim of each boot, is the word E-Thugz. The boots are a very dark shade of blue, and the letters are in a bright red. When wrestling he also wears a light
New York Yankees jacket, with the logo on the back, and the NY insignia on the right shoulder. He wears a blue bandana as well with his name written all over it. He also wears a pair of black shades, which like the ones basketball players wear have elastic on the back of them, so they don't fall off.
Past: Lil J One has had a tough life from the beginning. When he was first born, his parents were very seriously thinking about putting him up from adoption. The only thing that kept them from this, was Lil J One's grandmother. She refused, to let them, and eventually they just decided not to do it. But as Lil J One got older, his family went into serious financial problems, there nice home was taken away from them, and they were forced to move to the slums of buildings. J One's father became so focused on finding a way to get back to where they were that he had no time to spend with his son.
And his mother, was far too busy with the things that needed to be done around the house. J One started at a new school, and it was here that his life went on an unbelievable spill. First he wasn't paying attention to the lessons, and was constantly disrupting the class, this seemed normal for a kid in a new school, so he could try, and make firends, but he soon began fighting, anyone, and everyone for no good reason. It wasn't long after that, before he just stopped showing up to school at all, and just hung outside, with a few friends. Constantly getting high. These friends of his, soon had him join a gang, but it was in this gang that J One realized who his real friends were, every time he and these men would do something, they always ended up running off before the job was completely finished, and J One became angry with their constant cowardice, and pulled a knife on them once.
They quickly ran to the police, and J One was sentenced to 5 years in Dannen
Mora Maximum Security Penetentary. Once he got out he went to his real friends. The people he hung out with before his family ran into financial problems, and he began to train he was still only 18 after prison, and had a hard time getting into college, but a man by the name of Johnathan Albello known throughout the wrestling world as Mr. Insanity, decided to give him a break, and gave him a job wrestling with the ICW. He achieved minor success here, but never enough to tell if he could make it or break it. Through the years he went throught many different gimmicks as the wrestling world calls it, but finally found one that suited him. He quickly signed on with one of the strongest independent powers of wrestling The Devils Cradle Wrestling
League. As well as a brand new organization the North American Wrestling
Council. He has so far proven, that he is a strong man, with a heart big enough to match the power of a 7 footer. So far, Lil J One hasn't had anything big happening to him in the NAWC other then defeating the
Terminator, but he has become one of the top stars in DCWL. Originally thought to be nothing more then a gangsta boy, he has proved his in- ring worth by taking out Arin Gaoluon in the unforgettable exploding rope death match. Even though he did suffer a heart breaking loss to Edward Glide, and was eliminated 3rd in a battle royal. He is certain to prove a main eventer in the near future. J One has also been climbing into the ring with the old fold of the DCWL's top talent, as he took out Lightning Levi Ash on Saturday
Night Slayings. He has also recently joined Edward Glide although the full potential of that teaming has not been seen yet. He is scheduled to face Kato
The Drunken Monk on the 8th of August, in a second round match for the DCWL
Light Heavyweight Title. If he wins he will then go on to the main event of
Saturday Slayings where he will do battle against the winner of the Latin
Lightning, Rey Autrey match. For the DCWL Light Heavyweight Title. Also Lil J
One has been ranked number the overall # 7 wrestler in the DCWL. Lil J One went into DCWL Saturday Slayings, and beat Kato, he then was ready to fight
Levi Ash, after being brutally attacked by Arin Gaoluon, and Jose Garces. He not only walked it off and fought, but ended up winning the match! And the
DCWL LIGHT HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE, this now being his first major title. Overall
