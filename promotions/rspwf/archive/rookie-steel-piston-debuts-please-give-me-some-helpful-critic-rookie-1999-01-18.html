[ROOKIE] Steel Piston Debuts.. Please give me some helpful critic.. [ROOKIE]

18 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everybody.. You may all now know me from my pervious post's about certain things but now I have finally been ready and am posting a post of my own! This is my _VERY FIRST_ ever net rp.. Please Critic or however you spell it.. I want some helpful and useful criticsm.. Thankyou..
and enjoy :) [Fade in] [In a dark, fairly illuminated gym on the corner streets of 78th and 2nd street in Atlanta, Georgia.. Swifts thuds are heard throughout an abandoned gym.. The swift thuds break the silence of the room because they are the only action and noise going around..The thuds continue as a camera zooms in closer into the gym's dark mystique..]
[As the camera focus' in a bit more into the gym, a punching bag is spotted.. The Everlast branded, red punching bag is swinging back and fourth caused by the impact that is being driven straight into it.. The creaking of the punching bag's chains colliding together is also a noise that break's the silence of the building.. As the punching bag continues to be punched rapidly, a shadow is seen throwing the swift but yet powerful blows into the punching bag.. The shadow of the mans appearance seems to be muscular yet not to tall.. Stocky yet not fat or flabby at all.. The man looks to be chiseled from granite rock as he continues to drive his blows into the punching bag...]
[As the camera focus' in a _bit_ more the impact towards the punching bag stops and all of the creaking comes to a "Hush.." Now all you can hear is the panting and gasping for air of a man who is bent over trying to catch his breath..
After a few seconds the panting and gasping stops, as the man stands straight up, and begins to walk over towards a gym bag, which is placed on a wooden bench in the far right corner of the gym.. The man who is walking over to the gym bag is wearing a white stretch v-tank which is covered with sweat, and a pair of umbro shorts.. The camera pan's over to the right side of the building as it quickly refocus' in on the man..]
[The man who apparently seems to be training for some reason, bends over to pick up something's out of his _Reebok_ gym bag.. The man pulls out a white towel and a Gatorade bottle from his bag.. The man first tilts his head back and squeezes a mouthful of Gatorade into his mouth.. Most of the Gatorade liquids get into his mouth but some splash off and onto his face.. The man then brings his head back to a normal perspective and wipes his face and head from all the sweat and Gatorade liquids.. He then throws his towel and Gatorade bottle back into his bag and takes a seat on the fine wooden bench.. He sits their in relaxation and just begins to relax... Without any warning or prefiguring the stocky, black, muscular man begins to speak out-loud with no-one listening except himself listening to his own words...]
[The man begins to speak...] [Steel Piston]: As the sweat drips off my forehead, I can already taste the bitter sweet sensation of victory and success.. I can already see my hand being raised in a flawless victory and the crowd booing me, but deep down inside, they know they like the performance they just have witnessed..
[*Smirks*] _I_, being a former I. B. F. competitor and champion, I know the rings and I know the ropes.. I might be new to the sport of professional wrestling but I am not new to the ring.. All of you _suckers_ right now are probably thinking thoughts about me and how I am, in your opinion, am just blowing smoke up my ass.. [*Chuckles*] Think what you want, but you are completely wrong.. I am a man who was born in Atlanta, Georgia in the suburbs and who was raised to take care of my own.. I was raised to take care of myself and no others... When I went to school, I was always the one to take leadership and the one to swindle those without street smarts and those without hustling skills.. I was always the hustler and not the hustlee.. I was always the one who overcame, whoever opposed.. I overcame them with something they call a
_right cross_ but something that I call "The Steel Curtains.."
[Piston pauses for a second and brushes his hand over his head.. He looks straight into the camera with yet an intimidating and serious looks.. The looks of a fighter...]
[Steel Piston]: None of you will _ever_ realize the punch and power of a true former International Boxing Federation champion until, _I_ leave, each and every one of you lying on the mat dazed with confusion.. [*Smirks*] You all are probably thinking right now.. "What will one infamous _right cross_ do.."
[*Chuckles*] That right there is the reason for me being in the D. S. W. A. and for me getting kicked out of professional boxing.. My so called _infamous_
"Steel Curtains" paralyzed a man in one of my premiere career bouts..
It Left him paralyzed from the neck down leaving him helpless.. That single incident from _My_ right cross ruined my boxing career and left me unemployed..
I then went back to the suburbs in Atlanta and realized where I was from and re- cooperated myself.. I re-established my life and that's where the phone calls begun to come.. 24/7 the phone kept ringing.. Then one day, a professional wrestling legend by the name of, Higor "The Rebel" Rosa gave me a call.. The call regarding him, wanting me, to join his new wrestling promotion.. The Dirty South Wrestling Association.. He, I guess, had been watching that premiere bout of mine and noticed a spark.. He noticed my will and still yet "The heart" that I once _had_.. He wanted me to represent him and his promotion in their run to become one of the best.. I looked over the papers, the wrestlers manuel, and the rules and I decided if I couldn't _Hurt_ people in Boxing then hell.. There are less rules in wrestling then in boxing and less limitations, so why not.. I'll give this a shot.. And with my right cross and the "Steel Curtains.." I can probably take down and drop each and every one of the D.S.W. A. superstars and one-day take home the 16 pounds of gold to add to all of my I. B. F. trophies and medals... And one day _much like_ Higor Rosa, become one of the All-time true greats and legends in this sport.. By some I will be loved.. By some I will be hated.. Hey, I guess that's just the way the ball bounces for some people...
[Piston chuckles a little and begins to walk around the dark atmospheric building.. You can sense the eagerness and tension of Steel as he continues to walk around.. He begins to speak again..]
[Steel Piston]: Now as the tension and the eagerness pumps through my veins, awaiting the arrival of the first D. S. W. A. card I can do nothing but wait and see who I will be up against.. As I skim the roster of the new D. S.
W. A. I can do nothing but laugh at who my future opponents might happen to be.. People like Great Southern Rapist, Ash, Daddy Desire, and Shyco Path to just name a few.. One man though who by all means, is the biggest wigger and faker I have ever seen, he's a _wanna-be_ me, a man by the name of Maztah
Disaster.. This _child_ was my first prospect to leave lying on the mat with, at the least, a broken jaw.. Nothing against the man at all because I do not have a slightest clue on his skills or experience but it just clicked inside of me that there would soon be controversy between the two of us.. Controversy, feuds, battles, rememberable matches and everything that comes with it.. See, I accept everyone and everything.. _Everything but_ gay's and fakes.. Two things that Maztah Disaster seems to be.. Now boy, you might not know it but you are on the brim of "Steel Curtains.." One wrong thing you say or do Maztah Disaster will leave you helpless in the ring with me.. Maztah.. to me you are nothing close to a Maztah.. You are nothing but a Maztah's petty servant.. You will bow at my feet Maztah or you will get smacked up.. Now be careful child because when in the ring with me, you are in the ring with _god_.. [*Smirks and
Chuckles*] Now till I speak with you all again I exit with these words.. "Get in my face.. [*Chuckles*] And I'm gonna put you in your place.."
[Closing Scene] Steel Piston begin to smirk as he heads towards his back to pick it up.. As he picks up his bag a sudden smile and quick snicker comes out of his mouth as he leaves abandoned gym with no trace of him ever being there...
