A couple of free agents - 'Spanish Fly' Jose Rivera and Sam Quinn

5 March 2003

A couple of short descriptions of a couple of characters I just created. Anyone want to sign them up, feel free to email me at [detail withheld] . -- 'SPANISH FLY' JOSE RIVERA Height: 5' 8" Weight: 174 pounds Hometown: Spain Finishing Move: Spanish Fly top rope splash He thinks he's God's gift to women. He thinks he knows all about them. Other than that, he doesn't think much else.
Discovered by Gerald 'G.Q.' Quinn while he was across the Atlantic, the ever-opportunistic manager had him shipped off to America because he thought he could make a buck or two off the guy. Granted, Jose is quick and exciting to watch in the ring but everyone knows he was only picked by Gerald because of the potential female following his Latin lover looks could draw.
Unfortunately, his cockiness only makes the wrestling fans, both female and male alike, hate him even more.
Currently under the charge of 'Texas Playboy' J.D. Dillinger, the Spanish
Fly is doing what has been asked of him; smile, look pretty and keep his mouth shut.
-- SAM QUINN Height: 6' 10" Weight: 380 pounds Hometown: Charlotte, NC Finishing Move: Sideshow(Sidewalk) Slam The former 'Sideshow' has been called a lot of things; a freak, the Giant
That Never Was, the Sleeping Giant. A sleeping giant indeed he is. Out of the ring, he may be a jovial character but when provoked Sam will destroy anyone in sight. Compared to his brother, Harland Quinn, Sam has been content to stay out of the spotlight along with his sister, the former Miss
Maximus, Madeline Quinn.
Question is, how long before the desire within him awakens and he begins his journey to the top of the wrestling industry. Whatever the case may be, you'
d better wish you weren't in his way when it happens. -- - Ridz Handler of: - Harland Quinn[SWT] - 'Spanish Fly' Jose Rivera[Free Agent] - Sam Quinn [Free Agent]
