[Free Agent] Jakob Venom - RP inside - Critique too~

9 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Blahblahblah.. Lookin' for a new fed, but I have some serious needs for me to join your fed. 1) I need it to be an email based. No message boards please. 2) I would prefer a quality over quantity, and angles too. Something fun. 3) I NEED an established federation. I don't want to help someone rebuild, or a place with like 10 people. I'm hopin for a group that's been around a while, and has a decent roster already.
Send me an email~ or an IM~ ============================================================ -The camera fades into the drab concrete of a sidewalk, facing down to peer into the cracks of a shadowed city. The light is abundant, showing that it's closer to noon than midnight, and there's a few people walking around as shown by the passing shadows. Suddenly, a voice breaks through the pseudo-silence of a somewhat busy city. The voice of a younger child..-
Boy Child: Hey.. Mister! -The camera swings up to look down at a small child, who is facing away from the camera. The kid points up towards a wall, where the camera follows.- Boy Child: Hey, you, mister! -The camera zooms in a little bit, following the boy as he walks to the alleyway that he is pointing too. The camera turns into it along with the child, and we see Jakob Venom squatting down in the alley. His back is against the drab concrete of the building to his left, and his eyes are closed.-
Boy Child: Hey mister! Whatcha doin? -The boy can't be more then eight years old, with the keen interest of any child at that age. He inches closer to Jakob, who suddenly opens his eyes as the boy gets less than three feet away. He doesn't move, he doesn't even f*ckin' blink, and he looks not quite alive.. almost alive, but not enough to be considered a working part of a community. More like one of those autistic kids who have been stuck in a psych ward and given double doses of their medication for roughly six months straight.-
Jakob Venom: Child.. You don't know who I am.. These are rough times, boy. People wouldn't think twice about doing things to you that your mother could never have dreamed about. I sincerely suggest you put away your child like innocence and curiosity in a deep deep well of your mind and keep it there before it gets you hurt. I know you wish to only know what I am doing... but things like that bring mothers no end of grief.
-His voice.. is dry.. parched almost. His glazed over look doesn't change, and his mouth slowly comes closed when he is done speaking. The child, though, will not be dissuaded from his pursuit of knowledge.-
Boy Child: Mister.. are you a bum? -Jakob sighs... closes his eyes once and stands up. He walks over to the kid and holds out his hand.- Jakob Venom: Here.. take my hand. -The boy pauses for a second, but fear never flashes before his face. He somehow knows that Jakob won't do anything. He takes Jakob's hand, and The
Spider slowly begins walking out of the alleyway and down the sidewalk, boy in tow.-
Boy Child: Where we goin? Jakob Venom: Don't you worry about that.. boy. Don't you worry 'bout it one bit. -The camera fades to black.- -The camera fades in. We're no longer out in the midday sun, but inside a building. Drab grey all around, nothing new to the eyes. A picture hangs on the left wall of an otherwise unfurnished room, and a man kneels before the picture in a possible prayer. The man is undoubtedly Jakob Venom.-
Jakob Venom: The more things change, the more they stay the same.. it seems. My
Goddess of Duty and Grace.. I have sinned. You know this already, so I will not speak of it any more than I should.. The void within my soul is not eating at me anymore, and it's what I fear the most. The void has consumed me.. I am forsaken. I am one of the Forsaken. There are others like me.. we have found each other and have banded together, to work towards the same goals in which we have a like mind to accomplish..
... but that's not why I pray. I pray for I have sinned.. but again, you already know this, don't you? You look at me with disappointment in your eyes, because you know what I did this afternoon. I warn.. but childhood innocence is too much to handle sometimes.. even for the void. So I kneel here and pray..
pray for the soul that I wish would have been completely demolished with all of my actions. It's not completely gone, is it? You couldn't do that to me.. but it's enough to let me accomplish what I need to be done..
Dream Cast... Fyre.. We're the same. They have their own prayers, their own sermons to atone to, yes? Maybe my shrine will help them feel their own remorse for doing that which is done when someone has a conscience. It doesn't matter..
it only matters in what we do when we're of one mind. Of one soul.. which we are now, though that soul is only a partial fragment of what could have been.
Blood flowed at Vigilante Justice.. though not all of the Forsaken saw the great quenching of the thirst of victory.
Jake Menace somehow seems to elude, Dream Cast. We brought him into a greater understand, we did. He thinks we're all out to get him for different reasons..
but he doesn't understand the greater message that's being sent out. He might deem himself an athlete of the upper echelon, but he needs to understand..
three of one mind shall always bring down the greatest of opponents. Even those who are not so great.
-Jakob sighs.. and stands. He runs a hand through his hair and shakes his head, turning to look to the camera. His eyes.. still glazed over in a drugged zombie look. He's all there, it seems, just by the way he was speaking before.. but his eyes show very little in the way of intelligence, or understanding. Maybe, he doesn't understand anymore.. and he just goes through the movements. Who knows? A short, raspy chuckle erupts from him and he walks past the camera. A quick one-eighty degree pan and we see Jakob has gone outside of the room through a wooden door and into the cool air of a summer night. The camera follows as he goes, following into a short field just outside of the room.. the door. Jakob looks up to the starless sky and shakes his head again.-
Jakob Venom: Menace.. I will not explain it, because it's beyond your ability to reason. People are all against you, yes it is true.. but why anyone is besides The Forsaken is beyond me. We have our reasons, which should be enough for you to head for the hills in just knowing that you've been targeted. You defeated Dream Cast, an unlikely event.. but you were shown the err of the ways you preach by the end of the night. Continue with your procession, and it will continue on our end. Renounce your false claims, your retarded and uneducated fight and causes.. and maybe it will cease as well.. You control your own fate.. we're only the Hand of the Creator.
And please.. don't think that you can actually stop what we do. You might want to think that you are something, because you defeated the large man.. but also remember that in all of our meetings, you and I, that you have never defeated me. I can trace it to the slim beginnings of a time long ago.. when we has just entered this phase of life we call the UWO, all the way until our last meeting.
You are nothing to me, Jake.. but if it's brought to our attention that you need to be let down.. so be it.
-He walks a bit farther.. letting the words sink in as the camera follows him. The gravel scrapes and crunches under his bare feet, but he hardly seems to notice. Another deep breath.. and he turns to look at the camera. His eyes still glaze, but stare intently into the camera lens.-
Jakob Venom: To all of those within the UWO: The Forsaken hath arrived. Does it mean head for the hills because the bad guys are in town? Nothing of the sort..
We're not the bad guys so to speak.. We are just the Hand.. the Messenger.. those who seek out the ones we've been sent to berate, belittle and demolish... and do so. Don't bring yourselves to the attention of us like our dear friend
Jake Menace, and you might not have a problem. Might..
-He sneers and looks away.- Jakob Venom: Though.. many have decided that they already want to start in.. start before they knew exactly what went on. People like.. Simon Patrick.. who seems to think that it's all right to just drop names to get a little bit of a reaction from the ones he speaks of. Oh, yes, Simon. I heard you before.. with your little ramblings of how you drove me away.
Please... Don't flatter yourself either. You did nothing of the sort.. You know as well as everyone that I was having problems before your opportune time of getting a challenge to take on me. I was sunk, before I had my epiphany, and the realization of everything just happened to coincide with a match-up that I ended up not attending..
Coincidence.. nothing more. So.. Simon, and the rest of the pastey Greys. I suggest you keep to yourself your comments.. especially to myself, Scott Fyre or Dream Cast. While keeping out of sight does not necessarily mean out of mind.. it will keep you in your working order a bit longer.
-He smirks and turns away.- Jakob Venom: Keep out of sight.. but don't forget about The Forsaken. We're always around, when you least expect it. We are the true boogeymen. Not the ones who come from under your childhood beds to snatch you in your sleep, like you wished to have done.. but the ones who lurk in the shadows, ready to slit your throat as you walk down the street, without a care of yourself, your morality or your status in life. We're the ones who will not stop to think about what we do, only know what we do to survive..
Morality.. Remorse.. Conscience.. None of these things a Forsaken possess. We give these up for the oneness of being.. to be able to pursue that which we strive for with the blind ambition and reckless might of an animal.. Just remember that. You might stop a Forsaken for the time.. you might stop him for a long time.. but you will never completely cease him from finishing that which must be done.
Never... until it is done. -He walks away, into the darkness. The shadows play tricks, making it look like he fades into them much like what an apparition might be able to do. And the camera fades to black.-
