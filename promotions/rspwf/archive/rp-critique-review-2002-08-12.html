[RP] Critique/Review

12 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, Just wondering if anybody besides my usual circle can give me a little feedback, constructive criticism, or just general ego-stroking or flaming, if you feel like taking it two one extreme or the other.
This was my RP last week for PAW, for this week's match with Alex Hawkins
(JIMMY~!). Zach ***** [Black.] V/O: When you look in the mirror, Alex, what do you see? [Fade in.] [SCENE: A darkened room. A single light bulb hovers above, giving off just enough light to make out a face. Dark, choppy hair hangs in front of deep, piercing green eyes... Circles from sleep exhaustion give the face an old, tired look... Light stubble covers the well tanned face, partially obscuring a few colorless scars. This is the face of a battle-worn man. It is the face of
Alex Reaver.]
V/O: Look closer... [Suddenly, the camera pulls back and the blur of a hand flashes by. CLICK! Suddenly, the entire room is lit up and the camera shot has changed. Now, we are staring at the back of Alex's head. The face we have been looking at is his reflection in the mirror. He is in a bathroom. White and black tile covers the floor. Alex is shirtless, dressed only in a pair of black and blue striped boxers. The bathroom sink is lined with toiletries and different bottles. Without warning, Alex takes his hand and swipes the entire sink clean.]
V/O: When I look in the mirror... I see you. [CRACK! For no apparent reason, Reaver smashes the mirror with his fist. Shards fly everywhere, slicing open his hand. The reflection is gone. Slowly, breathing hard, Reaver turns around. He looks at the camera.]
AR: And that scares me to death. [He pauses, catching his breath. Alex looks down at his right hand. It is soaked in blood. He cringes a bit, but just shakes the hand out, not seeming to mind the pain. Alex stares at the camera.]
AR: I don't want to be you. I don't want your life. The past few weeks, I've watched you... I've listened to your promos, I've seen you in the ring, I've observed you after the show... I see how you _are_, Alex. You're not like everybody else. You don't fit. You don't belong.
Kind of like me. You remind me of myself. It worries me. You sit around backstage, smoking your cigarettes, and you're so goddamn angry at the world. You sit there, alone, moping... And I wonder... Is this what I look like? I mean, I listen to your promos, and you act like you're so goddamn superior... Like you're too cool for all of this. It makes me think, Alex... Is that what I sound like? I look at you and I can't stand to look any longer, because you depress the hell out of me.
And I wonder, Alex... Is that what people think when they look at me? [Reaver pauses. He rubs at his eyes, giving a deep sigh.] AR: The past few weeks have been frustrating for me. I came here thinking I could just walk in the door and come back into the spotlight. Well, it didn't go exactly the way I planned. I didn't cope. I chose to fall back on old habits... Doing things I had promised myself... Promised Chloe I'd never do again.
I've been down that road before. The pills, the cutting, the drinking... It didn't get me anywhere. Well, that's half-true. It got me in the sanitarium... It got me in detox... It got me in rehab... It got me fired and blacklisted from half a dozen major promotions.
But it didn't get me where I wanted to go in life. [Reaver stops. This brutal honesty is hard for him. He is shaking a little, his voice without any intensity or edge, but just a hint of regret.]
AR: I've been at the crossroads of life far too many times in my twenty-four years. It's taken me some pretty harsh reality checks to wake up and try and get my shit together... Near death did it... Losing Chloe once did it... But this time, I'm not about to wait for things to fall apart. I'm not going to wait until I'm lying face down in the gutter, alone, freezing, and about to die.
All it took was you, Alex. After following you, and thinking about my life, I made a decision. A conscience choice.
I don't want to be like you when I grow up. [Reaver nods to himself. Finally, a soft smile comes across his face.] AR: I see you, Alex, and I see the man I could become if I stoped caring... The man I'd be if I lost all hope... All passion... All my desire. I see you, and I hate myself for the possibility of ending up like that. It scares me to know that I'm _that_ close to being just like you.
I don't want that life. I don't want to be miserable. [Reaver looks at his hand. The blood has dried up a little. He turns his back on the camera for a moment and turns the faucet on... Rinses off the hand...
Dries it off. Then, he turns back to the camera and shows his hand. No more blood.] AR: I owe you, Alex. You made me look in the mirror and realize I had to stop the bleeding. So, thanks... I hope you don't take it too personal when we get in that ring and things turn violent. It's _not_ personal... It's just cartharsis. After we fight... I can stop hating myself... I can stop worrying about ending up just like you.
I'll be able to look in the mirror and _smile_... And I'll be able to sleep at night. [Pause. Reaver gives a slight smirk.] AR: So, tell me something. [Alex reaches out to the wall and flicks the light switch. CLICK! Again, we find ourself left in near darkness. A chuckle is audible. And then...]
V/O: When you look in the mirror, Alex, what do _you_ see? [Fade to black.]
