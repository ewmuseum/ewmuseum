[CRITIQUE] "Hardcore Pop Star" Brandon Spears

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SETUP: Spears cut a promo where he did an "Anti-War Porno" and was foulmouthed and screamed a lot about being a POP STAR and such. His opponent (Simon Kempthorne) pointed out that being in a porno and calling women cunts was not the way for a true pop star to act. This is Spears'
reply to that. Please note two things. First, the guy's fucking nuts. Second, he has selective amnesia. He's fucking nuts. Oh. And he swears. It's his character. Deal. I rarely ever swear in roleplays, and I know there's better ways to communicate, blah blah blah. But this one swears because it fits him.
[The camera fades into Brandon Spears, sitting in what appears to be a "green room" of the type you see backstage at concerts. A large buffet table, numerous couches... there should be a party going on.
But there isn't. There's just "The Hardcore Pop Star" Brandon Spears. Sitting on a couch with a large turkey drumstick in hand.] SPEARS: Sick fuck. Simon Kempthorne called me a sick fuck. [Spears wags the drumstick at the camera.] SPEARS: WHY AM I A SICK FUCK, SIMON??? Because I'm AGAINST THE WAR??? Because I'm brave enough to state my opinions??? [Spears looks at the drumstick.] SPEARS: I love the taste of flesh. [Spears opens his mouth wide... ... and just RIPS the meat off the bone, and speaks as he chews with his mouth open.] SPEARS: I wa ooh ah oh a- [Spears puts a finger up to the camera and swallows the meat with one big gulp.] SPEARS: I want you to know that I don't hold anything against you, Simon. All these mean, mean things you're saying about me don't hurt my feelings.
But my fans? That's another story. [Spears holds the now bare drumstick bone in his hand and taps his head with it.] SPEARS: My fans are crying, Simon. Say it ain't so, Brandon! I hear it everywhere! You're not a sick fuck, are you Brandon? Why would he say such GODDAMN
FUCKING HORRIBLE SHIT ABOUT YOU, BRANDON??? AND TRY TO FUCK UP YOUR ALBUM SALES WITH NEGATIVE PRESS... BRANDON??? [Spears starts digging the drumstick bone into his forehead, and twists it along the flesh.]
SPEARS: And I say... I DON'T KNOW, KIDS! Maybe because HE'S THE SICK FUCK! SPREADING THOSE LIES!
THOSE MISTRUTHS ABOUT ME! [Spears keeps digging... and a drop of blood falls along his forehead and over his eye as he keeps digging in.]
SPEARS: And how DARE YOU DOCTOR THAT VIDEO! I would NEVER call a woman a cunt! NEVER! I would NEVER participate in a porno! [Spears bleeds more from the turkey bone digging into his forehead as his eyes start to get red with tears.]
SPEARS: WHY ME, SIMON? I'm a victim here! [Spears throws the drumstick off to the side... and touches his forehead. The blood now has a steady, thin flow, and he touches the blood.]
SPEARS: What? [Spears looks at his finger, covered in his own blood.] SPEARS: I'm... bleeding... [Spears' face turns red.] SPEARS: How... how am I bleeding? [Spears' nostrils flare.] SPEARS: Kempthorne. He attacked me when I slept. He must've. He busted me open, that dirty fucking bastard.
[Spears looks at his finger... ... and sucks the blood off of it.] SPEARS: I bleed. [Spears begins to tear at his small wound.] SPEARS: I BLEED FOR YOU! [Spears sticks his bloody fingers in his mouth and licks them wildly... ... then smiles a sickening smile.] SPEARS: My fans... I live for you. I die for you. I bleed for you. And I will kill for you. [Spears stares at the camera with a demonic glare... ... then shouts.] SPEARS: EEEEEEEEE HEEEEEEE!!! [And leaps to his feet, doing the splits and sliding his way back to a standing position, then posing for the camera with his chin up... blood running down his face as he flashes a pretty boy smile.]
SPEARS: Because I'm YOUR POP STAR~! I'm YOUR American Idol! And I'll never let you down, fans! Never let you down! I'll keep my undefeated record! I'll bust out with hit after hit!
[Spears' pretty boy smile... ... turns into a demonic glare into the camera.] SPEARS: And I will ravage Simon Kempthorne. I will crucify him to save you, my flock. And I will eat of his flesh.
[Spears bows his head.] SPEARS: All... to feed my beloved sheep. [Fade to the bloodied Spears.] -- [name withheld] "Generic 3 quote sig Part 1." "Part 2. Nothing to see here..."
