[Free Agent] Luke "Labyrinth" Longmire

1 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE: In front of Luke "Labyrinth" Longmire's home. The camera zooms out, to see reporter Dustin Dage, looking at his watch, then impatiently looking around the home. Dage is dressed properly in a black suit, holding a coordless microphone. His anger growing, he tells his camera man to pack up, just as a man dressed in wrestling atire walks out from the side gate, latching it, and walking toward them with a big smile on his face. As he walks past them and to the front door, we see it is Luke "Labyrinth" Longmire. Luke is wearing full length black tights with " Lł " in bold sky blue on the butt of the tights.
The suit also has a tanktop top, with sky blue along the trim. He locks his front door, then turns around. We see the words "L Cubed" across the stomache portion of the top. Luke has short sleaked back bleach blonde hair, bleach blonde side burns that go to the bottom of his ears, and a beard-only goatte the same color. Finaly, Luke walks back to Dage and the camera man, stops and smiles.]
[DAGE] Well, I'm looking at my watch again, Luke, and it seems you're twenty minutes late for this little chit-chat we had planned. You want to be a wrestler so bad... I'd suggest you learn a little about puncuality.
[Lł] I'd suggest you not worry about it. You see Dustman, I've been through wrestling school for the past two years. Learning everything I need to know to suceed. Now, when I finnaly make my debut, I'm confident that 'ol "L to the
Third Power" will cause an impact. Do people get angry with James Pillow when he is twenty minutes late? Hell no.. they kiss his ass some more, so he won't leave. So, don't treat me any less than you would him, Dage. As you can see,
I'm dressed for business.. ya know.. a tryout? I think that was a little more important than givin' you and all those idiots sitting at home what they want
-- a glimpse of the Lukesta. [DAGE] Wow, for a rookie you sure have a big eho. [Lł] No, it's called confidence my friend. Now, while you're here... I'd like to send out an open challenge to ANYONE. To all your Pillow's.. all your
Vars', all your Vitrone's.. or anyone who wants a piece. Luke Longmire is here, and ready to test you for what you've got. Any and all leagues who want to see a set of three letter L's on their roster.. I'm your man. Any and all leagues who want to see a rookie take their biggest mountain (vterans) and knock them down to size.. I'm your man. Now.. I really must cut this short, guy.. so I'ma mash out. One last word to all those doubters:
When the Labyrinth gets ya all mazed. . . . . .YOU'LL BE DAZED AND CONFUSED!!! [Luke Longmire climbs into his Ford Escort, and drives off as Dustin Dage shakes his head and says "What an idiot." The scene fades black.]
