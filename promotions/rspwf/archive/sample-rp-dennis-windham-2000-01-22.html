Sample RP: Dennis Windham

22 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Scene opens up with BWF Magazine host Cyberpunk sitting In his usual seat on the set of BWF Magazine ]
Cyberpunk: Folks, we apologize for Interupting TSN's "Lacrosse's Greatest
Dynasty's", But we've got word that BWF Superstar Dennis Windham has Interupted an MCWA event, and has actually taken over the ring...we take you there now..as It unfolds....
[ You now see Windham In the ring wearing street clothes, with mic in one hand and a budweiser beer In the other. ]
Windham: Is this on? [ Fans are mixed In reaction..some are booing..some are cheering. ] Windham: I'll take that As a yes. Well..We're just days away from the beginning folks. I want to take this time right now..to answer the questions that you all have..going Into the biggest PPV of the BWF's history thus far..
[ takes a drink from his adult beverage ] You see...I make no claim that I cannot back up. I've busted my ass Inside this ring..and a thousand like It during my career. I know what It's like too lose..and I know what It's like to win. In my career, I've found the biggest enemy I have..Is myself. Sometimes, It's like I sabotage myself...But Sunday
Night...It's a whole new ball game folks. You see..last night, durning one of my matchs for an Indy group based In Detroit.. I wrestled a young man named
Krimson. You all know him..he's been In the BWF for the past 2 months, paying his dues...
Well last night, that son of a bitch taught me a lesson...He busted me open, and made me bleed..It reminded me It's not who ya are In this business...It's what you do.
And as I sat In a pool of my own blood last night...as I felt my heart pumping, and the thick plasma, sliding down my face..It all came too me...Why exactly we do this. Why, everynight..250 nights a year..why we sell ourselves..
Respect. It's the same reason a Painter paints, a pianist plays, a preacher loves and a child cries.
Jason Starr...I dont respect you. And that's why this match...this 4 man match..means so much. Because, Jason..You dont respect me either. And that's not right.
Jason...how many times have you wore my blood? And In the same respect...how many times have I taken a piece of you home with me? Weather you or I like
It..we are attached In history forever. This match...should have happend along time ago..but In this business..somethings..well there just too real....But let us on not pretend, that deep down Inside..we didnt like the bleeding..the pain..the fear or the pressure that came with creating a match so full of hate..so full of anger..that It fed us to our own demons..and created something so wrong.
And what's happend now Jason? We've run out of places to hide ..the demons, they've all come back. The hatred..the hatred has created something more powerful then You or I . More powerful then The BWF or the WIWO or the NIWF or the INWI or the MCWA...More powerful then the BWF World Title or the BWF North
American title. More powerful then your blood. More powerful then my blood. It go's above and beyond.. The demons are back. This time, we MUST face them.
This Isnt the time to be a coward and run. It's time to stand up, and fight..fight for once In you goddamn life Jason. Forget the title reigns..they mean shit Sunday. Forget about all the trivial bullshit..forget about buy rates and neilsen ratings...forget about your t-shirt sales. Forget about The Lone
Star Resistance or Jimmy Cougar or Assaf Levavy or King Apples or Tony Houston or failed marriages.
Remember, that This could be the end... In all respects to the other two men..they shouldnt be there. Nelson..Carter...This Is about much more then being a champion...It's about being the man. Its about respect. It's about honor. It's about destiny. It's about reality. And it's about Jason Starr and Dennis Windham.
Sure, There's been other marquee match-up's. Starr vs. Cougar. Cougar vs. Windham, Nelson vs. Darby, Cougar vs. Nelson, Nelson vs. Windham, Windham vs. Graves, Dice vs. Starr, But sunday Night IS ABOUT DENNIS WINDHAM VS. JASON STARR. You know what the most Ironic thing about Sunday's match Is Jason....There will be NO WINNER. Because, You know, just As I do ...I'm bringing all I got..and just a bit more...And I know, Your gonna do the same....I'm not quitting,
You'll have to kill me. As I know I'll have to kill you too.
We'll be but mere shells of our own selves Monday morning. This whole damn thing Is a freight train gone away. What happens when Legend meets Legend? What happens when one Myth steps up against another Myth?
When a champion falls, and no one's there too see It...Did that champion fall
Jason? Sunday night...It doesnt matter. No one wins....No one wins.
So, I ask you all ...the fans..the reason the BWF exsists...to watch Sunday night with a heavy heart..because legends are only mere mortals..and the end comes Sunday.
