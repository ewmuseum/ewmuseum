[Free Agent] Noah Johnson

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, This is [name withheld]. I'm trying to find a fed to put my character in. Hit me up on AIM as [detail withheld] or e-mail me at
[detail withheld] or reply to this post. I'll look at all feds, but I'm really looking for a good independent fed. I like e-mail feds over board feds, but am willing to look;
however, I'll probably stick with an e-mail based fed. Thanks! [Static.] [The night sky is dark, very dark. The moon fades in and out as heavy rain clouds move in. There's not a star in sight, thus not a whole lot can be seen. Nothing can be heard either, it's like the world is standing still. As the camera pans down, a light begins to make its presence known. This light is a streetlight, and sitting under this street light is a white young kid.
He has on baggy street clothes and a CD player in hand. The kid bobs his head to the rap music that's flowing out of the headphones, but turns the CD player off as the camera pans in closer. The kid then looks into the camera with his ice blue eyes and begins to speak.]
"My name is Noah Johnson. Yeah, Noah, like Noah in the Bible. Mom always was a strong religious woman, funny how I turned out. I doubt she's proud of what I became. What mother would be proud of a professional wrestler? Okay, some mothers would, but not my mother. I was always told I was to become a preacher like my father, Reverend David C. Johnson; however, now I'm known as a wrestler and the forgotten son. No matter what anyone tells me, wrestling is my calling, and I will listen to my heart and soul. This is my life; I'll never stop living and loving wrestling until the day I die."
[Noah thrusts his head back; thus his long blond dreadlocks fly into the night.] "Go ahead, bash me, tell me I'll never make it, tell me I don't stand a chance. I really don't care what you think or what anyone else thinks. Yes,
I do respect those who have fought to make this sport what it is today, but I'm not going to sit here and kiss your legendary [bleep]. I'm here to do what I know I can do, and that is proving I'm worthy of standing face to face with any wrestler in the ring, new or old, it doesn't matter."
[Noah rubs his chin and begins to speak again, but this time with more anger.] "So this goes out to all, you can laugh at me all you want, but I'll be above you one day. I'm on my way, boys, and I have no plans of falling back because of my rookie status. True, I may get my [bleep] handed to me many times, but I'll never back down from anyone. I have worked long and hard to get where I am now; I will not let any [bleep] critic hold me down. So like
I said, I'm on my way; you can make your own opinion about me as I am before you, but what will be will be, and I shall be the greatest wrestler ever to grace a wrestling ring... one day... one day."
[The man you now know as Noah Johnson takes a deep breath, places his headphones back in his ears, turns his CD player back on, and then stands up. Noah takes a long look around him; making sure trouble is out of the way. When it's okay to begin to journey into the last night streets, Noah nods to the camera and walks into the shadows of the night.]
[Static.]
