[Free Agent] Matt Haden

16 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
All right, I'm looking for another federation to join, I currently compete in the UCWP and I am looking to join another fed with either Matt Haden or Casey
Powell.. well here's a sample roleplay. It must be a net fed, so please, send all handbooks, invites, yada yada yada to my e-mail.
P.S. - If the handlers of "The Handler" Liam Rogan, "Mr. Charisma" Christian
York, or "The Goldenboy" Jeff Keenan, please move on to the next post because you will see it later. =) If you keep reading.. you'll be a big fat cheater!!!!
-- [name withheld] [A slight noise begins to fade in as the scene is shifted to a highway, you are obviously riding in the passenger side of a truck. The camera looks over to the right as Matt Haden is driving, he's got on his brown cowboy hat, a cheap pair of sun glasses, a plain white t-shirt with stains, and a pair of blue jeans as it can only see down to his thighs. He starts to slightly chuckle as he begins to speed up the truck.]
MH - "Ah've hadda 'nuff excitement..." [Matt Haden slows down the old ratty truck, he begins driving the normal speed limit on the empty interstate as he begins feeling the bandage on his forehead.]
MH - "... Insanity was ah little tougher than I thought. Him taken that chair ta mah head an all. Don' matta a bit though. In the long run I came outta there witha' victaree.
That means I advance... Ta Jeff Keenan." [Haden stops feeling the bandage and places his hand back on the steering wheel.] MH - "This man thinks he can jus' go aroun' the U.C.W.P. demandin' evera frigin' thang he wants. Well I got news fer 'em. It ain't happenin'. I'm ah gonna putta stop ta it real quick.
With mah own bare fists. The way I see it, Keenan is ah big name, I ain't. So I'm bettin' the odds are on me. Fine. I dun' giva' rat's ass about who's ah underdog an' all that. The people o'Canada are gonna be expectin'
me ta lose. Well ain't. I got _too_ much pride ta letta match slip outta mah fingers an' I'm willin' to bet ah pretty penny that Keenan is feelin' the same way.
Whoop dee doo." [Haden raises his hand in the "Whoop dee doo" manner.] MH - "Now that we got thatta outta the way, we can really talk. Talk 'bout how I'm gonna beat the livin' crap outta Keenan... nah. That's to 'passe'. I'm goin' for ah different approach. Let's talk about how Keenan performed. Misser 'Es Te Bee Pilla got the win. Guess Keenan better start thinkin' 'bout ah new game plan, he didn' get the job done with Pilla, he ain't gonna get the job done with me, not in any form o' fashion. What Kevin Starr was doin' nosin' 'round in Keenan's 'n Pilla's business ain't mine.
But I know if he comes 'round me... I'll make sure he regrets it dearly. I'll go as far as ta say that it was ah good match, an entertaining one atleast... and then Starr stuck his nose where he shouldn't have.
One thing, amigo... ya' got lucky, I was expectin' Pilla to tear ya ah new one. This go 'round, you won't be so lucky. It's jus' me an' you, bub. An' I'm _really_ lookin' forwerd ta Tango-in' with ya.
Hope yer jus' as excited as I am. Cuz I know, as well as ya do... we're both itchin' ta get our hands on that gold strap. An' I'd say it's safe ta say, we're both gonna give it our all.
Well, I know I am atleast. Dun' know much 'boutcha. Ah guess ya can say I'm perty *keen* on the idea of you an' me goin' toe ta toe. Ya see the thing is, I ain't ah big name like ya'self. An' ah win over you, that'll put name in the contender's list.
An' that's where Ah belong. An' that's where yer leavin'. I'm lookin' ta take your spot." [Haden looks over and reaches toward the ash tray, he pulls out a half smoked cigar and puts it in his mouth, and leans over and pushes in the car lighter.]
MH - "It only seems fair. That the..." [The lighter pops signalling that it is ready, Haden makes a signal with his hands as he speaks, the signal for qoutations, he leans over.]
MH - "... That the 'Goldenboy'..." [He leans over, grabs the lighter and lights his cigar, he takes a few puffs and leans his hand out the window, along with the cigar.]
MH - "... That the 'Goldenboy' becomes the U.C.W.P.'s firs' ever World Champ, huh? Well forgetta 'bout it... it ain't happenin' in this day an' age, uh huh, not while I'm ah 'round. I'm lookin' for that gold an' if I don't get it, I'm ah gonna be sure that ya don' get yer filthy pretty boy hands on 'em.
I've seen yer type before. Ya think ya can get whatcha' want jus' cuz ya got money, well I have sum'thin ta say 'bout that.
I've worked _too_ hard, an' _too_ long ta let that title slip outta mah hands and fer me, there ain't no turnin' back, it's either all or nuttin'... an' I'm going fer it all.
That title's mine, Keenan. Ya keep yer grubby hands away from it." [Haden keeps on driving when the cigar is blown out of his hands, he looks out the window with a desperate look.]
MH - "Ah shit. That was mah last one. But it looks like it's Christian Yorks firs'. Firs' lesson in respect. Sum'thin ya jus' don' see in the rasslin' world taday.
Sum'thin I'm gonna enjoy teachin' ol' Yorky 'bout. He ah comes strollin' in the U.C.W.P. straight outta the OWF, yeah, I've seen ya in there, I know what I'm ah facin', ya come ah strollin' an' ya think ya own the world, well, bub. I got news fer ya...
Ya don' own shit... _I_ own ya, amigo. An' the way I look at it, I'm the toughest hombre you'll ever lay eyes on. I know what competition ya been facin' in the OWF, and frankly...
Ya can't even begin ta spit on it. It ain't shit. But ya think that ya won a lil' gold an' you can' do whatcha god damn well please. An' I'm here ta show ya otherwise. Snicker all ya want, bring yer skirt out and try an' impress every one, well I ain't impressed, I got three lil' ol' words fer ya...
Get ah life." [Haden begins to get real quiet as he looks down at his gas gage and notices he's below empty, he drives off to the side of the road and parks it. He gets out and reaches in the back of his truck revealing a small red tank of gas. He pops open the gas tank and begins filling up the truck with gas.]
MH - "Then ya got men like Liam Rogan. Dun' know much 'bout him, jus' that he's one o' them... " [Haden raises one hand as he holds the gas tank with one hand, and makes the qoutations symbol.]
MH - "... 'Big names' like Keenan. All the more better fer me. Jus' ah free ride ta stardom if ya catch mah drift. Ah win over him... garuantees recognition. Sum'thin I need. Sum'thin I need to start gettin' the _real_ matches... an' the mos' important...
... _RESPECT_. I gaurantee that by the time this is aw'll said 'n done, I'm gonna be respected... by aw'll o' ya. No questions asked."
[Haden finishes putting gas in his truck, and closes both of the tanks and replaces the gas can. He hops in to the truck and begins to start it as the engine turns a few times but never fully starts. You pick up a "God demmit!" just as the camera begins to fade.]
