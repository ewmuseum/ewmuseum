[ROOKIE RP] Please Critique and ...

26 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings, I am the handler of Dark Wolf. You all probably don't know him because he's a new wrestler on the scene. Below is the latest roleplay I posted for the fed I'm in, the WWA
( http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Stadium/7037/index.html ) Please critique it and email me with tips on how I can improve. I've only been in e-wrestling since early April of this month and I think this is some of my best work yet. But, how can I improve?
========================================================= ROLEPLAY ========================================================= [The scene opens and we're at a beach. But the weather is not as it should be. Dark clouds cover the sky like a blanket and rain pours down. The camera moves down to find Dark Wolf sitting on some rocks.
His feet dangling over the foamy water. He is wearing a pair of jeans, a black leather jacket and a pair of boots. His hair, dripping wet, is slicked back. His hands cover his face. Lightning cracks nearby. But
Dark Wolf doesn't flinch. The camera looks back to the sky. The tape speeds up to show the clouds rolling bye. After a moment, the clouds have gone out of sight. The camera slows to a normal pace. It appears to be night time as there is no light. The water is still wild but is constantly calming down. Little by little. We look back at Dark Wolf.
He removes his hands from his face and places them on his knees. He looks us and then quickly turns away. At this time, the sun peaks over the horizon and begins to rise. The words, "A new day ..." appears at the bottom of the screen. Dark Wolf takes the soaked leather jacket off his back and flings it into the sea. We watch as it is thrown back and forth. Dark Wolf turns to us again.]
Dark Wolf - It is a new day for the Dark Wolf. Many people believe that
I have no talent, no wrestling ability, and no ability to take the
Hardcore shots. It is true. I have been a sniveling puss that couldn't stand getting beaten on. Then, last night, at Massacre, I faced The
Machine. And to my misfortune, he beat me. Oh well. I'm not gonna cry over spilled milk. I'm going to take it like a man and move on. We all have losses in life. But we have to handle them. I've realized that now. And I look on the bright side of things. That finisher The
Machine uses, the Suplex DDT, is a combination of my two favorite moves. So I've honored to have been beaten by such a devastating move.
[Dark Wolf looks off to the horizon and continues to talk. We struggle to hear him.] Dark Wolf - Now people are going to complain that I've lost my focus. That I'm taking things too easy. And that I'm not suited to be in
Killer Force anymore. Well when I joined Killer Force, I made a commitment. I will not go against that commitment. And just because
I'm handling life a little better now, doesn't mean I won't do well in the ring. When I step through those ropes. And raise my hands before the crowd. I'm a totally different person. I'm a Deadly Force.
[Dark Wolf looks back at the camera. You can tell he is thinking hard. A minute passes before he speaks again. His face is expressionless.] Dark Wolf - I've made some enemies quickly here in the WWA. There's
Judas, the man who wants to be the only dark soul here. He tried to introduce me to his gods but failed. There's Fontana Stuart. The man wants to beat me for speaking bad of his manager Taylor. There's Scott
Dakota. The man wants to kick my ass because I whined when he made me ride the escalator. There's Innocent Sleen. The man's aware that I want a shot at the Hardcore title so he's after me too. Which reminds me of a better reason why Dakota wants to beat me. He wants a shot at the Hardcore title too so he wants to put me out of the picture. And then there's my good buddies the Metal Maniacs. I ruined their career, helped kick them out of Killer Force, and then shocked the world when myself and Crazy Horse beat them at Crash-N-Burn.
[Dark Wolf kicks off his boots and dives into the water. He swims for a short time and then climbs out.]
Dark Wolf - Judas, you failed. I hope your gods are unpleased. [Dark Wolf snickers to himself and then speaks under his breath.] Dark Wolf - If they even exist. [He then clears his throat and speaks in a normal tone of voice.] Dark Wolf - Now on to Fontana Stuart. You're right. I'm an impatient bastard. I don't have a problem with you. Yet. Let's keep it that way. Next there's Stuart's rival Scott Dakota. I may not have been ready for the Hardcore division before but I am now. Like I said earlier, today is a new day for me. A new beginning. The beginning of a fiercer, stronger, meaner, and tougher Dark Wolf. And the end of the puss that whines all the time. I'm done whining. If the Booking
Committee decides to put me in two, three, or even four matches on a card, fine! That equals more time in the spotlight for me. Bring 'em on. Dakota, we will hook it up on the next House Show. And the whole world may not see it. But a couple thousand will witness the new fighting machine that is ... Dark Wolf. And they will witness the Fangs of Fear being locked on. And big ol' Scott Dakota tapping out. And then there's Innocent Sleen and the Metal Maniacs. Three people that I fight in a huge tag team match. Well first of all, Sleen, you felt what
I am capable of. And my sights are still aimed for that Hardcore title. Watch your back. Cause I'm coming after you. And then, my buddies the Maniacs. Trash and Doom. No, I didn't say Thrash's name wrong you morons. That was on purpose. So Trash and Doom will be in this tag team battle also. Never mind the fact that they have to get beat AGAIN by myself and Crazy Horse at Riot House.
[By this time, the sun has risen into full view. Dark Wolf continues.] Dark Wolf - You all want to get at me. But I have my own list of people to take out. There's the Metal Maniacs of course. A team who I am determined to ruin permanently. Then, there's Scott Dakota. A small thorn in my side as I strive for the Hardcore title. Dakota will be defeated. And I will move on. There's Gravis. The old man thinks he's so much better than myself and the NEW members of Killer Force. Gravis, let me tell you something. Tradition has always been thrown out the window. You, the Maniacs, and whoever else ran the 'Force have been removed. You are old. The younger, faster, smarter, and more powerful generation of fighters that is now Killer Force, are the future of the
WWA. You all, are just a waste in space and valuable show time. It's time to move on. Why don't you retire and move to that house made of
Twinkies you've always been dreaming about. Next on my personal list is
Innocent Sleen. The man who claims to be "innocent" somehow latched onto the Hardcore title. Now what's wrong with that picture? Huh?!
Somewhere, someday, Innocent Sleen, it'll be you, and me. For the
Hardcore title. And you know what happens after that Sleen? I'll leave that ring. But I won't leave the loser. Oh no. I'll be leaving that ring with the Hardcore title draped over my shoulder. And the fans chanting my name. And you'll be laying face down on the canvas. You will have just experienced what it's like ... when the Fangs of Fear are upon you.
[Dark Wolf stops momentarily. He takes a deep breath and continues.] Dark Wolf - But before that ever happens, there's one thing that is more important. So count your lucky stars Sleen. Cause you'll have a little time before I strip you of that title. You see, myself and Crazy Horse joined the WWA to pursue a career as the Tag Team Champions. And we have not forgotten that. We continue to strive for that goal. And we will reach it. Now I don't care who has the titles at that time. Be it
Thugs Xtreme, be it the Metal Maniacs, it doesn't matter. Because when we get that shot. There'll be nothing to stop us.
[Dark Wolf stands up and walks out of the view of the camera. We follow and watch as he steps onto a Suzuki motorcycle and starts it up. He speeds off. The camera fades but returns a minute later. Dark Wolf has parked his bike and is walking towards us. He walks right past and goes through a door. It is obvious that we're in a WWA arena due to all the logos and pictures on the wall. Dark Wolf turns a corner and walks down a hall. He gets to a dark room where he turns and begins to head down another hall. At that time, a man wearing a balaclava and clown suit jumps out from behind some crates. He has a chair in his hand. He runs up behind an unsuspecting Dark Wolf and cracks the chair over his head.
Dark Wolf falls forward on his face. The clown continues to deliver several chair shots to the head of the unconscious Dark Wolf. Security rushes the area and we cut out.]
=================================================== Also, I am thinking of starting a new e-wrestler. He is supposed to be a kind of Kevin Nash character. I've come up with either a first name of Bryan or Max, but I couldn't think of any fitting last names. Any ideas from you all would be greatly appreciated.
