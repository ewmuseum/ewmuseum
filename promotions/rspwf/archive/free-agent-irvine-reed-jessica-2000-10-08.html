Often is afraid to do some moves, and thinks twice before flying through the air..giving the other person time to recover. 3. N/A Background: Irvine Reed grew up in east Steubenville, Ohio. The bad part of town. Basically, being the only white kid in the neighborhood, he had to constantly watch out for himself. He learned alot about himself in his grade school years.
As Irvine became a freshmen in high school, the sport of wrestling became a major interest to him. He tried out for the team and made it as the last cut but never, never got to fight in a real meet...only practice. But, the coach noticed something about Irvine in practice. The kid was VERY flexible, he could maneuver his body in strange and odd ways. The school's wrestling coach told
Irvine that he should look into gymnastics.
Irvine, not bothered by kids making fun of him for being a male gymnist, started to work on his agility more. Through his high school years, Irvine won many state championships and even placed in the nationals.
At the age of 18, Irvine was trying out for the Olympics, being selected by the state committee. At the tryouts, a few wrestling scouts noticed Irvine's amazing ability to do strange things with his body. Even though Irvine never did qualify for the Olympics, the wrestling scouts had a nice talk with him.
Irvine knew that wrestling wasn't his thing because he just wasn't strong enough. But the scouts had given him a better, more realistic view at wrestling...be a highflyer. Hence, Irvine Reed enters the wrestling world as one of the most dominate highflyers today.
