Roleplay Critique - "Top Dog" Rick Styles

14 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I thought people on this board might like this roleplay, I worked very hard on it. Let me know what you think...
======================== [A dark soundstage that slowly lights up to reveal a very simple set. A beige colored room, dimly lit by low quality lights. A chair in the center
of the room. And in that red metal folding chair, sits Rick Styles, the One Man Militia. Dressed in what is typical attire for Styles: torn blue jeans, and his beloved (if somewhat blood stained) leather jacket, no shirt underneath.
Brad sits forward, hands clasped in his lap. His long hair is loose and with his head leaned forward, his face is obscured by the wavy brown strands. The sound of exhalation can be heard, before Kilgore lifts up his head, and brushes away enough of his hair that his scarred face can be seen from behind the ebony veil. In a powerful, concentrated voice, the
muscle-bound fighter speaks.] RICK STYLES - You want to know what kind of man Brad Kilgore is? Ha... When I was in junior high, there was a group of about a half-dozen high school kids who'd come 'round. They called themselves the Ninth
Street
Spiders. A street gang, of sorts. A bunch of young bullies preparing themselves fer a life of crime. They came 'round twice a week, and demanded money, and beat the hell out of anyone who refused ta give 'em their weekly allowance. And, additionally, they beat the hell out of anyone who was different. Fat kids, skinny kids, ugly kids, pimpled kids!
... Tall kids! [Styles draws in his breath, and leans forward slightly, his hair once more cascading over his face. The breath is exhaled, and the grappler continues.]
RICK STYLES - I got my butt kicked by them kids damn near every time they came 'round. I was tough, even back then, but I can't say I've ever been tough enough to take out six guys all at the same time. I remember, one time, in particular though.
See, this morning, I just decided I wouldn't wait fer 'em ta jump me. I decided I'd take 'em out as soon as they set foot on campus. So, as soon as I saw 'em, I rushed 'em, determined to show 'em that I wasn't gonna take their crap.
So, I got the hell beat out of me early that day. [The massive man's shoulders shrug, almost uncaring about the events he is detailing.] RICK STYLES - But thing was, that beating was worse than usual. Not
'cuz I attacked 'em. But 'cuz I didn't never go down. I refused ta get on the ground, and play dead fer 'em. Every time they hit me, I told 'em to do it again. Every time they told me to give 'em _my_ money, I called 'em a different cuss word. Each and every time they tried ta put me out, _I_ let
'em know that _I_ was here to stay. And I remember, sitting in the school nurse's office, looking down, as blood dribbled from my nose to the ground, and made a puddle before my feet, what that nurse asked me. She said "why?" That nurse wanted ta know why in the hell I didn't just lay down, and spare myself the beating I took.
Wanted ta know why I wouldn't roll over, and play dead fer 'em. And, finally, after wiggling a loose tooth around with my tongue, I looked up at her, wiped some of the blood off from my lip, and I gave her the only answer
I really _could_ give her.... There wasn't nothing else _I_ could do. [Kilgore pauses for a long, long time. His hair is brushed away entirely from his face, revealing a full view of the man's visage. It is the face of a warrior. Dominated by two long, angry red scars, that run diagonally across his face. Minor scars dot the rest of his features, on his forehead, cheeks, and neck. His nose is crooked, a result of numerous breakings. His eyes are a soft green color, the shade of a cricket glistening in the sun, but hide behind them a burning, ferocious intensity.
This is a man who has been down the road numerous times, a survivor, who has outlasted a life time of battles that would have broken lesser men.
Finally, after many minutes of silence, Brad speaks again.] BRAD KILGORE - See, even back then, I was a fighter. Even at that young age, I knew that win, lose or draw, I couldn't never back down from a fight. Something in my heart wouldn't allow it. I've been fighting a long time. And now, every time I walk down the street, and someone recognizes me, every time I go to the doctor and get stitched up, every time a promoter looks at my medical bill, and every time my wife looks at bloodstained towels, I get asked the same question. Why?
I guess they just don't understand. Sure, there've been times in my life when I coulda backed down, but
That woulda been like taking a back seat to the Spiders. And that just ain't an option fer Brad Kilgore. It ain't worth it, if I don't fight fer it. Maybe it'll get me nothing more than a broken body, and a wheelchair.
But it don't matter. I've been fighting so long, I don't know any other way. And even if I did, I wouldn't take that route.
[Rick spits and watches the saliva fall between his legs and splatter on the otherwise untarnished floor.]
RICK STYLES - When its all said and done and I'm planted six feet inta the earth, I don't want no one ta remember anything 'bout me, except for one thing, and that is... I was a fighter.
All the titles I've won, all the men I've beaten, all the cheers
I've heard, it don't matter nearly as much as me staying true ta what I am,
and that's a guy who fights fer _everything._ Sure, there's an easy way ta do things, but I ain't never been one ta take the easy way out. [Another pause, as Styles looks down once more, hair sliding into its position as a mask over his marred features.]
RICK STYLES - Alex Reaver, V.D. Vitrone, Jeff Keenan... all of you
Pure American Wrestling fighters, I hope you're listenin' real close. I know ya think this "Path to the Crown" is gonna be easy fer ya. I know ya think 'cuz I ain't been in this sport fer too long, I'll just roll over fer ya. I know ya think gettin' yer hands on the National title is gonna be a cake walk. Just remember who you're dealing with though. Just remember what kinda man ya got facin' ya. Remember, 'cuz see, soon, someone's gonna ask you why. They're gonna want to know why you're in such bad shape.
They're gonna want to know why you're in the ER. And fellas, ya can look 'em square in the eye and say... Well, its real simple. I found out what kind of man Brad Kilgore is ... ... the _hard_ way. [Fade to black.] -Adam Get the picture?
