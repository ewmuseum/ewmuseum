[Sample RP] History of Myth Maker PWK and Silfverling Dines

13 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<Scene opens in the executive office of the Vice President, Myth Maker PWK. The camera focuses in on his nameplate.. "[name withheld]". Then it goes to VP
Myth next to a covered frame on his wall.>
Myth: You see, Wednesday Night was a turning point in my life. No longer will I be a follower, a new leader has been born, and that was me. Truth be told Kiely, you do suck @$$. I hired that fan! That's right.. a plant, and you, Cereal Killer, the WWA fans and wrestlers fell for all of it hook line and sinker. <Just then a man walks in, it's Jon Thompson. He walks over to Myth.> Thompson: Way to go, Mr. Vice President sir. Kiely has had that coming to him for a long time. I mean, can you believe they picked that Lyndon Keiser guy to do the color commentary, and made ME a lousy sidekick!
Myth: That is the only good judgement that Tommy Kiely has ever made. His life has been full of bad choices, bad decisions. Now he has to face the harsh
reality that there is no one he can trust without at least thinking twice. He picked me to be vice president, as you found out on Wednesday, because he trusted me, and I "trusted" him. Well, many of you may think that was an out and out lie. The fact of the matter is that I told the bold truth, I trusted..
that he was naive enough to take me up on MY offer to be the Vice President of the World Wrestling Alliance. Well, judgement day came and went.. and Silfverling Dines jumped aboard... at the expense of Cereal Killer.. who will be out of action for a few weeks.
Thompson: Yeah, why did you help Dines? I mean, he's the one who put you out of wrestling a little more than eight months ago!
Myth: Learn to check up on what I say, and not believe a word until you have factual evidence it's true. Dines didn't injure my knee. Truth be told.. he was my tag team partner in the WSW, I went down hard and then resorted to hitting one of our opponents with my world championship. I aquired that belt by beating FOUR men in one night, without much trouble. Dines and I formed the
Soldiers of Destiny and added men the likes of the Legendary "Angel Reaper", shocking the WSW!
Thompson: Wait a minute.. where have I seen that name? Soldiers of Desinty? Myth: Ringside, Wednesday Night.. yet another plant, some forshadowing to your future Kiely. But you were also too naive to pick it up! Anyway, I'll continue because I know you want to hear this. WSW Officials said that the tournament for the belt was "tainted" because Angel Reaper put up a voilent attack on the knee of a man by the name of "Executioner", in the semi-final round. After that the fat @$$ president of WWA, Jon Penney, tried to give me the title in exchange for not wrestling Executioner... but that wasn't going to happen. I injured his knee even more, then beat him again the following week.
Thompson: Wow! your relentless! I like that. Myth: Yes, but soon I learned of my next opponent.. Silfverling Dines! Thompson: The WSW president matched you up against your own tag team partner? Myth: Yes, in an effort to break apart the SoD.. the most elite force in the world of wrestling.. he put the two leaders in a match against one another..
but when he realized how we didn't care.. we had a battle of words.. but they weren't harsh, they were words of respect for one another. It was then he realized that the SoD was there to stay, and he did something very cowardly..
he shut the federation down, due to how scared of us he really was. Thompson: But.. how does that explain you turning on President Kiely? Myth: Patience.. my brown-nosing friend. Penney and Kiely slightly resemble one another.. but I decided then, 8 months ago, in WSW.. that I would never let a president push me around again. I was going to do what I want, when I wanted and where I wanted to do it, no matter what he said. So you see, Kiely just pushed a few wrong buttons.. how dare he ORDER me to do an interview with him!
It almost made me puke, the way I said all those kind words about the fans, about the suits and everyone else.. It was quite possibly the worst thing I've done in my whole life!
Thompson: Oh, I get it. Well.. what is this? <points to the frame> Myth: Oh yes, I almost forgot.. I had this made in order to remind me of what being a good guy can do to you. The downfall of a once.. fairly decent man.
It is.. a... CORPORATE ASS KICKING! <Myth Maker PWK pulls down the cover from the frame and unveils a 5 X 7 of him kicking Kiely. Dines gets up from his seat and walks to the wall, on the other side of Thompson, next to Vice President Myth>
Myth: Now, Dines and I, together once again.. will be able to take over the WWA, much like we did the WSW. The time has come for good to reign over evil once again. The age of the hero has died and it's a time where the fans cheer at blood, injuries and personal suffering. Well, once again I guess I am going to give the fans what they want. Corporate Elite will be a household name, depicting drugs, sex, violence... everything that america symbolizes. <pulls a peice of paper out of his pocket.> Now, this has to end.. you may recognize this <camera focuses in> as the letter sent by a fan to President Kiely, worshipping him... What's that I smell? I believe it's a MARK! At first he was alright, he commented on how he likes Dines here, but here's a news flash kiddo.. Dines isn't exactly out there to please the crowd anymore.. get used to it! Now, let me move on.. a few sentences down in his fan letter he mentions how Tommy Kiely should FIRE his vice president, because he does nothing for him! That's exactly right pud! I do nothing for Kiely anymore, I'm my own boss.. and he doesn't have the brass to fire me.
<Myth hands the letter to Dines who pulls out a lighter and lights the paper on fire, throwing it idly into a nearby metal wastebasket.>
Myth: Oh, and by the way Kiely.. there's more where Dines came from, and they're already in the WWA, so you better get used to having my friends with all the gold, and Dines is going to start with the World Heavyweight
Championship.. I have a place for that picture too.. hahahah!!! <Screen fades into: Hey Kiely.. Go %$#@ Yourself!!!>
