*Sixth row has Indy Wrestlers we don't know much about. Hell, they look like they were created right out of the video game, WWF Warzone! But they are probably friends of Bad-Cool, nevertheless. We see one man who is half-Mexican, half-American with a blank black with long red jean shorts on and "Sketchers" tennis shoes. He has on black gloves and black Ray-Ban sunglasses. Carnage finds this man rather interesting and decides to ask him some questions.*

F'N Serial Thriller" on the front. The final man has on a d-generation X shirt -- but it's all green. He has green dreadlocks, green leather shorts, etc.*

KFK, and Marky are all really good friends.* *Suicide comes in -- dressed in all black -- and Carnage stops him.* Carnage: Nice try, Dusty. Now, get out of here before I call the cops. *Dusty runs away... Carnage wipes the sweat from his forehead as The Apocalypse comes in the front door. As he does, everyone at the wedding boos him. He flips them all off and takes a seat in the 2nd row. He crosses his arms and puts his head down.*
*King Dave Nasty and the Demolisher come in and sit down in the 2nd row. Demolisher looks very angry, as if he's going to break something.* *Fiend and Dusty Boy come in and are met with a series of boos. As soon as they step in, Carnage picks up a hockey stick from The Goon and busts it over Dusty's head. Fiend cowers away.*
*Savage, Black Widow, Jurate, Crazy Chick, and Kali (with Shiva) come down to fill up the 2nd row and start to fill up some of the front row. They are met with many cheers.*
*Gettin' It World Order (G-Lo, Awn Top, Uncle Nelz, and that other guy) come down and fill up the front row. Carnage is sweating as more people come in. He looks again at the guest list and then shrugs. He looks over at the chairs he was going to hit Apoc with and then sets them up in the back.*
*As if it was the jWo, a ton of jobbers come in. Gilbert/Outlaw, Crackdown, JD, Nick Fury, Pandemonium, etc. About 20 jobbers come in and quickly fill the chairs up again. Carnage has to get more. Soon, "Thunder" Union Cain walks in. Carnage stops him again.*
Carnage: Dusty, that's it! Now, you're dead! *Suddenly Kozzy steps in through the front door. He is in his blue blazer with red tie. He sets up a chair and kicks "Dusty" Union Cain in the midsection and then does the Wrath on Dusty through the chair. The chair is broken to pieces. Kozzy then does the Wrath 7 more times on Dusty before
Grendel steps up and reaches into his pocket and stands over Dusty. Grendel has the "Thunder" Union Cain mask.*
Grendel: Hey, looky dare, Dusty! Looky what I got! *Dusty tries to snatch his mask back when Kozzy chokes Dusty and pounds his face so many times, he looks like hamburger. Kozzy picks Dusty up... we don't know what'll happen next, but some are saying that Kozzy is throwing
Dusty in a river or something.* *As more jobbers (and Dark Angel, who arrived late) fill the chairs up, Carnage zooms in on the front desk. Ice Cube is the DJ. Carnage looks down at his instructions and goes up to Ice Cube.*
Carnage: Hey, Ice, get out of here now! We're playing some other songs, not your rap crap from this wedding! Get out! NOW!
*Ice Cube leaves but not before doing a drive-by shooting on someone in the audience with Snoop Doggy Dogg, Master P, and his other gang members.* *Carnage takes a seat in a chair and soon, a red light flickers. That's the signal for Carnage to hit "Play" on his casette player. Carnage does so, and "Space Lord" by Monster Magnet blairs over the loud speakers. Jim
Rooster in the back appears to be jamming as Amanda and Felicity smile for the camera as Carnage zooms in on them. Suddenly, out of the shadows...
here he is.........* ...Bad-Cool comes out! He has on a bluck tuxedo with a white blazer and a black tie that goes down where we can't see the rest. He has on black form-fitting pants and black suede shoes. His fuchsia hair is fixed up like the Road Dog's normally is. He has on a black set of gloves and both the
HaRdCoRe/Bad and the International titles around his waist. Bad-Cool gets booed by everyone (especially in the back by OAD members) as he goes around and looks at Felicity.*
Bad-Cool: Come on, baby, one more time for old-times sake... come on... *Felicity looks at Grendel who nods, and Felicity gives Bad-Cool a kiss on the cheek. Bad-Cool smiles and hugs Felicity and whispers something into her ears.* Bad-Cool: This is out of my character, Felicity... I miss you so match... I miss you.... *A tear comes to Felicity's eye as she wispers something back.* Felicity: I know... *Bad-Cool squeezes her tighter than ever before.* Bad-Cool: But this will all work out for the better, you know? Laura and myself... I don't want to get married... but... it's something I have to do...
*Felicity pushes Bad-Cool away. Felicity rolls her eyes and then looks over at Grendel who's giving Bad-Cool dirty looks behind his mask. Bad-Cool nods and Felicity gives BC a wink. Bad-Cool then goes around and then looks at

Rooster gives him a dirty look as Bad-Cool smiles.* *Bad-Cool comes down and shakes Nathan/Snake's hand as well as the other guys that are there in the back. Nathan gives BC a hug and then pats him on the back.* *Bad-Cool moves along a little bit and waves to some. He stops and goes over to Marky, and Marky gives him a pat on the back.* *Bad-Cool moves up to Laura's family. He looks at Laura's fat mom (who's gasping for breath) and her wrinkled mom and shakes their hands.* *Bad-Cool then moves and gives Savage a hug. STOP! STOP IT, DAMMIT! *Bad-Cool and Laura are rolling around on the floor making out already. Juice Bag is trying to kick them off of each other.* Baggy: Stop it! STOP IT ALREADY! *Bad-Cool and Laura gets up and BC brushes off.* Juicy Juice: You know, there ARE kids who watch this! *Bad-Cool starts to slip his red silk boxers on and then looks at Carnage, the camera man, who reaches into a gym bag and gives Bad-Cool BC's black jeans shorts.
