If you think you are the best rp'er then read this ....

20 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, I am posting two of my latest rp's. They aren't my all time best rp's but I think you'll find them to be pretty darn good! :) The way that we can settle this debate is to have handlers send in rp's for there guys and show everyone _why_ they are the best! I would reccomend that you send in two rp's so that we can see what you can do for sure (i.e. you won't have a fluke and give us your greatest rp ever. We want to get what you can normally do on a regular basis). So here are two of my rp's: ------------------------------------------------------------------------ [The camera opens with Magus walking around in a vacated gym. The gym seems to be _real_ worn down, and the only thing that is in half good condition is the wrestling ring in the center of the gym. Over in the corner there are some old punching bags, and some dust covered weights, but the ring really sticks out above everything else. It is like a center piece in the mantle of gym.] [Magus is wearing a pair of grey champion shorts that go down to his knees, as well as a tang-top that says, "EWA: Yes we do know how to kick some", and on the back it shows a picture of someone's rear. Magus is also wearing a cap, which he throws off revealing his medium length brown hair, which is blowing from the open windows that are placed in the room.] [Magus looks upon the gym, and then he jumps onto the apron of the ring. He goes under the second rope, into the ring and starts to run around for a few moments. He bounces off the ropes several times, making the dust on them come up into the cool Autumn air. There is a sky light that is also covered with dust, but not so much that light if completley drawn out of the gym. Magus then comes over towards the corner of the ring and looks out into the gym. He isn't look at the camera yet, but his head quickly snaps around and looks into the wide angled lense of the camera.] Magus: HELLO! I am here, to do a little training down in the trenches if you will. I am not going to a little "down and dirty" training if you will to prepare for my 4 way match which is sure to be one hell of a WAR! I mean, I've got three tough competitors but in the end ... [smirks] there will be _no_ one left to fight! I am going to talk about a different member of that match every day. First up for today is .... [Magus goes to the outside of the ring and pulls out a dusty old ring bell. He blows off the massive amounts of dust that were almost curroding the bell. He takes a small gavel that he sees beside the bell and his the bell with it. The bell clashes twice and Magus goes back into the ring.] Magus: The first man on the list is ...... [Magus picks up an old, yes, dust covered mic. He taps it and then speaks ... but nothing can be heard.] Well, this is a piece of junk, so I'll just say it right here, right now. The first man is .... DRUM ROLL PLEASE .... "AMERICAN NIGHTMARE" GARY ........... GRAYSSSSSSSSSSSSSOOOOONNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN!!!!! [Magus makes a sound as if the crowd were cheering. He gives a little giggle and he goes over and sits on the top rope.] Magus: Grayson, Grayson, Grayson. You claim that _I_ am a jobber? [smirks] Well ... who is the jobber that is a former EWA TV Champion? Who is the jobber that is ranked number 6 in the freakin' world? Heh? Answer me that one Gary my boy, and maybe you'll realize for _once_ in your life that ... [shudders mockingly] YOU'RE WRONG!! Yes, it may be a strange though but ... it is the truth! You aren't god, you aren't omnipotent Grayson and frankly .... YOU WILL NEVER BE! Gary my boy, you go off saying that I will be easy for you to take out?! You think that I will be just another berry fresh for the taking? I am not just another jobber that is lined up for you to kick the crap out of! You call me [grins, and puts his hands up as if he was making quotation marks] "Jobber to the Stars." Have you ever tried your hand at the comedy circut Grayson? I mean ... I guess that you wouldn't do that well actually because you are full of nothing but CRAP! [Magus jumps over the top rope, landing on his feet not having to even balance himself for a seconds. He brings out an old dusted mirror from the corner of the gym. He uses the sleeve of his shirt to get all of the dust off of the mirror. He then takes off his shirt and starts to flex. It is obvious that he is mocking Gary Grayson. He flexes his abs, then his pectorials, then he does a double bicep flex.] Magus: Grayson ... I am the most clean cut ... most toned human being on the face of the planet! I will take out all of you <BLEEP> 'in JOBBERS! I am so CLEAN CUT and so well toned, and I just have a superior physique. I mean .... I am _so_ CLEAN CUT! You think that you can handle the baddest chico in the land, Chico?! You think that you can handle the most dangerous ... CLEAN CUT wrestler on the planet?! [Magus gives out a big laugh at what he just did, mocking Grayson the entire time of course.] Magus: Ah! Grayson ... you may be the world's [mockingly] clean cut, well toned, powerhouse [normal voice, very straight forward] but I .... I AM BETTER! 'Nough said Grayson ... but you can bet that my words are _far_ from over!! Gary my boy ... [grinning] LIGHT'S OUT!! [Magus goes over to the corner and puts back the mirror. Magus goes over to the front of the gym, below a "Jim's Gym" sign. Magus looks up at it and he walks by it. He flips a switch and the lights go out. The camera fades to black.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ [The camera opens in a large church, that is almost large enough to be a cathedral. Several men are infront of the giant cross in the church, and they are down on their knees praying apparently. The altar boys are walking around and doing various tasks.] [The church has several colossal stain glass windows that are multi-colored and represent an assortment of different thigns. One seems to be the crucification of their god, or one of their gods for that matter. A preist is walking around and picking up all of the bibles or other holy books off of the pews. There are seemingly 40-45 rows of pews, each a light colored oak.] [The camera comes down from a large shot of the church to a shot of someone's back who is sitting at one of the pews. The person has on black pants and a white shirt. The camera pans around infront of the man, revealing his face. It is Magus. Magus closes a book and hands it to the passing priest. Magus gets up from the pew and enters the aisle and starts to proceed towards the large doors in the front of the church. Magus gets to the large, red oak doors and opens them wide. He walks out into the crisp fall air and looks up at the towering church.] Magus: Welcome everyone! As you may have guessed I am here to talk about the one ... the only ... CALEB TEMPLE! Temple is a man that is one of the greatest up and coming stars here in the EWA. Caleb, you told me that you have a great deal of respect for me. That is a real compliment to me. I mean, with all of these guys running around here in the EWA, most of 'em never stop to think about what they are doing. You have .. that is a good virtue my friend. But I am not an up and coming star here in the EWA. I am a _true_ contender for those prestigious EWA World, NA, or TV Titles. [Magus goes over to a bench and sits down on it. He flexes his muscles, in an act of mockery toward Gary Grayson.] Magus: [in a neanderthal like voice] Me rippled! Me buff!! Me well toned! Me strong! Me masculine! Me have abs of steel! Me have muscles like gods! Me will crush you with my large biceps! Me stong! Me GRAYSON! Me INVINCIBLE! Me muscles VERY strong! Me VERY strong! [pulls out a mirror] Me very ... hot! ME GRAYSON!! [Magus coughs and gives out a little laugh. He puts his feet back and he talks normal now.] Magus: Well ... there you have one of the most _accurate_ Grayson impersinations that you will see anywhere! [grins] Well, back to Caleb, okay?! Good. Caleb, you my man are one of the top newcomers. I mean, whether anyone likes it or not you and Grayson are two of the brightest newcomers that I have _EVER_ seen! Yep, I am going to tell all here ladies and gentlemen. Caleb, you and I must work as a team to help get rid of Grayson and Mailgn. Why is this ........ [Magus runs his fingers through his medium length black hair and he sits back on the bench. He puts his hands behind his head and then lays down on the bench. He is looking up into the bright, blue sky that brings light upon the earth. Magus turns his head to the camera and look intensely into it.] Magus: ..... Temple ... it's because we are the best two wrestlers in the ring! You know it and I know, and so does the rest of the EWA. We have two damn tough competitors to take down if we can ever hope to get a good honest, true test of talent match. You and I are the only ones that will dare to fight a fair match ... and if I get you one on one Temple I will fight fair ... but don't respect _any_ remorse out of me! The same can go for you Gary "The King of Testoserone, the Sultan of egoism, the Emporer of Self Centerdness, and the Master of not being able to back up all all the crap that he says" Grayson. [Magus breaks a huge grin across his face. The wind starts to pick up a a little bit now and Magus hunches up with his coat to keep out all of the cool air that is casscading upon him. Magus now gets up from the bench and starts to stroll down the side walk that is nearest to him. There is grass that hands along the edges of the sidewalk, and it is obvious that the grass needs to be cut badly. There are many trees that inhabit several animals and birds.] [Magus walks in a not to slow, but not to fast pace. Slightly methodical, but not over emphasising it. You can hear every now and then him kicking pebbles as he walks along the off white concrete sidewalk. Several birds go flying across his path and he just smiles at the sight of playful little squirrels that leap from tree to tree in pursuit of one another. Magus stops at the corner of a street, and leans up against a sign which has, "Lima Lane" pointing forwards and leading into a small street. There is a sign that is pointing horizonilly that reads, "Freetown Drive", which continues over the horizon. Magus leans up against the pole, rather causally apparently.] Magus: Temple, I have been watching you ever since day one here in the EWA, or atleast day one for you that is. I have been through more than you Grayson, and Mailgn _combined_! I have been in a _major_ slump as of late, going on a _big_ 4 match losing streak. But I plan on ending that slump when it comes time to fight this Sunday at RARE! I have always done my best, win or lose during _every_ match that I have here in the EWA. I _never_ do something incredibly stupid which is underestimate my opponents. Grayson ... that is something that you _really_ need to work on! Respect for other's abilities can be a _great_ virtue in the ring. If you respect your opponents abilities then the possiblity of you getting surprised by his talent is _very_ slim. Grayson, you may be able to take three bums off the street in a Sealed Cage ... but you won't be able to take 4 wrestlers ... ALL gunning for you! You are in the center of a laser sight my friend ... and you are [smiles] GOING TO GET BURNT! Temple ... Grayson ... Mailgn .... see you in the ring boys! LIGHT'S OUT!! [Magus stops leaning on the cold steel of the street pole and starts to walk down the street which says, "Lima Lane." He continues for a minute or two before he reaches a white house with pillars in the front of it. He opens the door and enters the house. It is apparent that the house is the place where he resides. The camera fades to black.] ------------------------------------------------------------------------ That is the stuff that I will do on a regular basis. So, now you know what I can do! If it matters to you or not. The main idea of this is to get some people to post rp's here so we can see who the best rp'ers are even if we _aren't_ in that league. Kent
