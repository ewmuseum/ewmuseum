[RP] Not a free agent, but looking for comments cuz I'm bored. :)

17 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey Guys, Wow, long time since I've posted. If you don't recognize the addy, it's [name withheld] -- the Stuff, ex NOW President, handler of about a million guys. You know the story. Anyway, earlier today I sat down and had a killer idea to bring back one of my characters from '97/early '98... Darwin Tide, a character
I used in the LWC for the most part w/ a one night appearence in the MLWO. Anyway, here's the rp. I'm not looking for a fed, I'm not looking to get bitched at by some lamer who thinks he's the shit, I'm just bored and wondering if this RP is as good as I thought it was and on the level I should be at.
-Zach Currently Active in SCRA (Jeremy Steele) and GLCW (Alex Reaver) ----------------------- [SCENE: A dim, faint lit shot of the shore at Hilton Head, South Carolina. The waves lightly crash against the rocks... gulls overhead give a light cawwing sound to signifiy their prescence... and the faintest white sliver of the moon can be seen in the horizan, waiting to darken as the sun sets.
The colors are faint because of the poor camera lighting, but still just as majestic. In the distance, we can see the faint outline of a man walking methodically towards the shot in the sand. From seemingly thin air, a deep voice speaks over the shot as he gets closer.]
VO: They told me I'd never wrestle again. [The voice's slight chuckling is audible.] VO: Imagine that. The same man doctors thought would never be social again after seeing the bloody carnage of his own parents' death. The same man who got his shoulderblades ripped into bone fragments and still recoperated and won a shiney gold belt a few months later. All because of a few inconsistencies... a breached contract here and there... they have the gaul to say I'll never work in this business again.
[Pause. After a few seconds of silence, there's another laugh.] VO: Every last one of them's gonna eat their words, 'cause I finally found the dumb bastard with the balls to bring me home. [name withheld], bless his little heart... the poor guy probably didn't even know what kinda hell he was agreeing to when he answered my call. Pity, all his hand picked little so called superstars are going to get ripped to shreds by a wild animal... and it was their own retarded President who accidently let him loose.
[The voice pauses again. In the distance, we can see the shadow outline of a man slowly draw nearer.]
VO: WWEW. Four little letters, but for me, they stand for a whole lot more. Retribution. It's a chance to pay back all those hindsighted fools who thought I was just another nutcase without a prayer. Salvation. I can finally sleep easy at night knowing I can wake up the next morning and send one of God's little do-gooders to the hospital with one way ticket to the land of pain. Entertainment. Why? 'Cause nothing's more enjoyable than hearing the SNAP of broken bones, the CRACK of a skull meeting canvas, and the POP of joints being dislocated. Add in the everlasting fun of seeing a man laying in a pool of his own blood... why, it's a regular old
Disneyland parade! [More laughter.] VO: I almost feel sorry for them... almost. A load of unsuspecting martyrs, sacrificed to the lords of pain at my will. There's nothing they can do to stop it... nothing they can do to prevent it. They can try and run, but it's only delaying the inevitable... their pain is my destiny.
[The footsteps in the sand are almost in front of us, now.] VO: Can you feel it, children? The seagulls are singing their song, the crescent moon's rising... it's time, children. It's time.
[The figure finally arrives. Short, cropped blond hair, blue eyes, and a wicked smile make up the head of a well built man, dressed in a pair of nice khakis and a red Polo shirt. But there's something sinister about him, despite his friendly appearence.]
VO: Darwin Tide's coming home. [Fade to black over his laughter.]
