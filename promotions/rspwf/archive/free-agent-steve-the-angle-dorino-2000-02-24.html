Free Agent (Steve "The Angle" Dorino)

24 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am looking for an established federation that has a good layout, hand written cards, is booked and does not have a large roster. In return I will roleplay on a regular basis, become involved in angles and so on. I am experienced and have held a few titles in my time. I would be interested in a fed with roleplay packets.
Name:Steven "The Angle" Dorino Height: 6' 3" Weight: 240 lbs Hometown: Miami, Florida Style: Technician Apperance: Steve is very attractive and he knows it. His black hair is neck length and has a slight curl in it. His brown eyes are meaningful. He has a well toned upper body and is well oiled. He is not greatly muscular but has good sized biceps. His legs are hidden by his tights.
Ring Attire: Steve does not wear a shirt, he likes to show off his body. He wears traditional black armpads and black sunglasses, obviously he takes them off before he fights.
His pants are full length and are black. They have two silver stripes down the side. He also wears a pair of knee high black wrestling boots over the pants.
Entrance: The arena lights dim and the big screen (where applicable) shows an entrance video with Steve in action, hitting the Right Angle on a few guys while images of his face flash through these. While this is happening white pyros explode on the entrance way as Dorino appears, arms raised and sunglasses on. "Bawitdaba" by Kid Rock plays as the entrance video carries on with white and black lights flashing around the arena as Dorino walks to the ring in an arrogant fashion.
Basic Moves: Russian leg sweep, Vertical suplex, Boston Crab, Sidewalk slam, Samoan drop, DDT, Underhook suplex, Brainbuster, Arm Drag Take down, Jawbreaker, Cryonic kick Trademark Moves: 90 Degrees A tornado DDT from the top rope, often used as a set up to the finishing move. Wishful Thinking A whip into the ropes is followed with this move. Dorino catches and hoists his opponent onto his right shoulder before powerslamming them to the mat in one swift movement.
Finishing Move Right Angle Dorino tends to hit the 90 Degrees before applying this submission move. It is hard to explain, but is similar to
Steve Blackmans Gullotine. He wraps his legs around the neck of the fallen (face down) opponent and grabs their left arm, which he pulls on through his legs. Believe me, having been a victim of this it really does hurt!
Gimmick Steve claims to be "The Angle". This is true in more than one way. His flexability and precision could be linked to his name but in his eyes it is because he is always the centre of attention. He feels that any decent angle in a federation will involve him. This can upset fans but bring other to their feet. This is why I have not designated him as a heel or face. He is a highly skilled wrestler with bags of charisma.
Sample Roleplay The lights in the arena dim and their is a sudden explosion of white light from the entrance way. The light clears as
"Bawitdaba" by Kid Rock blasts through the arena. The entrance video of "The Angle" plays. Dorino is present with his arms raised. He makes his way in an arrogant manner to the ring. He has a microphone in his hand and climbs through the ring ropes. Dorino stands in the centre of the ring, waiting for his mixed reception to die down. He removes his sunglasses and smiles at his adoring female fans, who erupt in screams.
"Stop it, you're killing me!" The screams die down and Dorino rubs his chin before speaking once again. "Now, you all know that when "The Angle" comes out here, what he is going to say is important. (a few boos and cheers) Tonight is no exception. At Friday Night Flame I went one on one with Misery. You watched in awe as I hit the 90 Degrees, The Wishful Thinking and locked on the
Right Angle. (cheers outweigh the boos) That makes Steve "The Angle" Dorino, number one contender for the Television Championship. Well who gives an Elk's ass about that!? (cheers drown the boos) "The Angle" does not want to be associated with that piece of crap! I have my eyes on Gold, not Plastic! So, Jack Traylor, take your number one contender slot and get the hell out of my air space!
(cheers are immense) At March Madness, I will have a World Championship title shot and that Gold will be strapped around the waist of the only man that counts, Steve "The
Angle" Dorino!" The Music kicks in, followed by the usual video and lights as Dorino climbs back through the ring ropes and leaves for the locker room
