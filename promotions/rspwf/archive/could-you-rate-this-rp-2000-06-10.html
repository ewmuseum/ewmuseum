Now I'd like to let everyone know I've been in e-wrestling for just over a year now, and I for one don't think I am the greatest, but I am throwing myself to the wolves here, so just be brutally honest with this RP.
Thanks. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (The scene opens in front of a Northwest Airlines ticket booth. A man is seen buying a ticket for a flight. The woman behind the counter is giving him a funny look as she types on her computer. She finally prints up two tickets and hands them to the man. He picks up his suitcase and walks over to the waiting area. The next man in line begins to talk to the woman.)
Man - I need a First-Class ticket to Vancouver please. Woman - I'm sorry sir, but that man over there bought the last two. Man - But he's all by himself. What does he need two tickets for? Woman - I'm really not sure. He said he was supposed to be bringing someone, but now she can't come. So I guess he wants to save the seat in case she shows up or something.
Man - Well do you have anything in coach? It's quite imperative that I catch this next flight.
Woman - Sorry sir, but the flight is completely booked. Man - Is there anything else I can do? Woman - Well I guess you can try to convince that guy over there for his other ticket, but he seemed pretty persistent.
Man - Thanks. I'll try my luck. (The man walks over to the one sitting down. He has his head down and is wearing a Chicago White Sox hat.)
Man - Um, excuse me, but do you have a spare ticket to sell? (The man sitting down lifts his head as he notices he's being addressed. He reveals himself to be Jason Blake.) Jason Blake - Can I help you? Man - Um, yes. The lady at the counter had told me that you may have an extra tick... Jason Blake - (Cutting off the man) I'm sorry, but I bought this extra ticket for a reason. So unfortunately, there is no way I can give it to you. I guess it has sentimental value to it.
Man - How can a plane ticket have sentimental value? Jason Blake - I guess if I don't tell you, I'll never hear the end of this. Well my girlfriend always comes on my flights with me, wherever I go. But this time, she is stuck at home with a bruised spine, so I would like to be able to have the seat open. I don't think I would be comfortable sitting beside anyone else but her.
Man - I'm really sorry to hear that, but if I don't make this flight to
Vancouver, I will lose out on a half million dollar contract.
Jason Blake - I'm sorry. I really can't help you. Man - I will give you five thousand dollars for that ticket. It is that important to me. I'll even pay you in cash.
Jason Blake - I can't. Please don't make this any harder on yourself. Man - Ten Thousand. Please, I'm begging you. I can't lose this contract. Jason Blake - Fine then. Just please don't beg anymore. It looks really stupid. Man - Thank you so much. I really appreciate it. Here is for your trouble. I know how much it means to you.
(The man hands over ten thousand dollar bills over to Jason. Blake puts the money away and reluctantly hands over his extra ticket. The man hurriedly grabs his bags and heads over to the boarding gate and waits in line. Blake is now left talking to himself.)
Jason Blake - I never thought I would end up being a plane ticket scalper. This is so degrading. But ten grand will get Amy some really cool souvenirs from Canada. Airport PA - Flight 274 to Vancouver, Canada is now boarding at gate 5. I repeat, flight 274 to Vancouver is now boarding at gate 5.
Jason Blake - I guess I'd better get going. (Jason Blake walks over to the gate and hands his boarding pass to the attendant and proceeds to board the plane. He reaches his seat, and sits down in the window seat.)
Jason Blake - Well at least I have a little time by myself. (Pulls out a picture of Amy) I'm sorry this happened to you. I am going to do this for you Amy. I am going to win on Monday, and I will show everyone that
Memorial Massacre was no fluke. (At that moment, the man from earlier comes and sits down in the aisle seat right beside him. He puts his bags in the overhead compartment, and sits back.)
Man - Hey again. I can't thank you enough for this. You've saved me a bundle. Jason Blake - Yeah, no problem. All in the spirit of giving I guess. Man - Well I guess the ten grand was a little enticing, wasn't it? It's okay, everyone gives in to money once in their lives.
Jason Blake - Yeah I guess so. It is a lot of money. But I normally wouldn't give in, but I could sense your desperation.
Man - I guess I was a little obvious, wasn't I? (laughs a bit) But this really does mean a lot to me. So if you need anything on the flight, just ask and I'll be more than happy to get it for you.
Jason Blake - Thanks. I'm sure I'll be fine. (The plane begins to move and the seatbelt sign comes on. Blake buckles his seatbelt and sits back as the plane takes off.)
Man - So why you going to Vancouver? Jason Blake - Business. Man - What kind of business? Jason Blake - It's a work trip. A bunch of guys will be there. Man - So what do you do, if you don't mind me asking? Jason Blake - I'm a professional wrestler with the NWWA. Man - Very impressive. I've heard of them, but never really paid attention to wrestling. I guess it just wasn't my thing I suppose.
Jason Blake - Well it's not for everyone I guess. But it's nice knowing you're not a crazed fan.
Man - Even if I was a wrestling fan, I'd have enough courtesy to not bother you. I'd hate being harassed on a flight too.
Jason Blake - Yeah, it can be a little nerveracking at times. Man - So are you going to be wrestling there or are you only going to watch? Jason Blake - I'm scheduled for a match on Monday. I have to defend my
Canadian title in Kamloops.
Man - Awesome, so I am assuming you're really good then. Jason Blake - Well I like to think so, but I still have plenty to prove to everyone. But I really would like to not talk about it right now.
Man - Sure no problem. If you want to talk, just let me know. Jason Blake - Will do. (Blake reclines his seat back and closes his eyes as he begins to talk to himself once again.)
Jason Blake - This one is for you Amy. I refuse to let you down. I will be coming home with this belt, and Brimshore will not stop me. The poor bastard is going to bear the brunt of my anger. I promised Amy that I wouldn't retalite on DuBose, and I will keep my word. So poor Brimshore is going to feel the wrath of the Bandit.
(Thescene fades to a shot of the man nudging Blake's shoulder and Blake sits up and opens his eyes.)
Man - Hey wake up. You've been sleeping the past two hours. We'll be landing in Vancouver any minute now. You may want to get your seatbelt on. Jason Blake - Thanks. Man - So who is Amy? You were talking about her in your sleep. Jason Blake - Damn, I was talking in my sleep? Man - Yeah you were. Sorry if that bothers you. Jason Blake - Not a problem. Amy is my girlfriend. She's at home in
Chicago nursing her back after she got powerbombed through a table in
Nebraska. Also we almost got killed when a Chem Lab got firebombed during a hardcore match. It was pretty ugly.
Man - I can imagine. Stuff like that can take a toll on someone. Jason Blake - I know, but for the first time in my career, she won't be with me, and it's kind of freaky.
Man - Well I can assure you that her thoughts are with you. Just take it easy and I am sure you'll win your match.
Jason Blake - Yeah you're right. I guess I just needed some reassurance. Man - So who's this Brimshore character you were referring to? You don't seem to like him that much.
Jason Blake - Well he is the guy I have to face on Monday. I guess I'll be suing him to vent my anger. I pity him in a way, but if he wants to try and take me on, then he deserves it anyway.
Man - Damn. I can tell he's in for a rough ride on Monday. I just hope you don't hurt him too bad.
Jason Blake - Don't worry. I already got plans for him. He's to be made an example of. In fact, he is the one I have chosen to be the first to recieve something new from me. Something that has never been seen from me before. It'll be a sweet symphony hearing his head bounce off the mat.
(The plane touches down and comes to a screeching halt on the runway and when it reaches the gate, Blake and the man get up out of their seats.)
Man - Well once again, thanks, and I really enjoyed talking with you. Best of luck on Monday with that Brimshore guy.
Jason Blake - Thanks. I hope you get that contract. (The man disappears in the crowd of people getting off the plane as Jason
Blake pauses to look at the picture in his hand one last time before making his way to the door. As he gets through into the airport, he stops, looks around, and walks towards a cab.)
Jason Blake - (To himself) Here I am. It's been ages since I've been to
Canada. But unfortunately I don't have time to sightsee. It's now time to show the NWWA that I can make an immediate impact.
(The screen fades to black as Blake gets into the cab and it drives off.) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Once again, thanks for taking the time to rate this for me. -- [name withheld] Handler of...
