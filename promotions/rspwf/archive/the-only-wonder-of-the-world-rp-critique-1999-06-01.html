[The Only Wonder of the World] RP Critique

1 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well RSPWF'ers, saying as just about everybody else has posted a roleplay to critique, I figured..What the hell! My upcoming match is against Shane Austin in WoW/NBW...so critique on!!
____________________________________________________________________ T.O.W.O.T.W. Big Daddy Brooks T.O.W.O.T.W. [The scene opens at "The Only Wonder of the World" Big Daddy Brooks' house in
Las Vegas, Nevada. He is wearing a pair of Docker's shorts, along with a wife-beater shirt. Brooks is sitting down, watching TV. Subsequently, he is watching the WOW/NBW Control Center. He then watches it, then turns off the TV.
He then begins to speak as the camera zooms in.] Big Daddy Brooks: Man..after what happened last Sunday Nite, I am both confused and disappointed. First off, I appear for my match, and my opponent doesn't show. That kinda pissed me off, because I know I could've gotten that win over somebody as easy as Hybrid. Buutttt...then Peter Tiger comes out and confuses me, as well as that Rowdy Scotsman.
And how does he manage to confuse even the SMARTEST man that _I_ am? Well, he tells me that I am going to face off against the Rowdy Scotsman this Wednesday, but then, I watch this Control Center, and it says that I am facing Shane
Austin. Now, if any of you so-called *geniouses* out there can understand exactly what in the HELL is going on, please, INFORM ME!! I've heard I'm facing
1 man straight from Tiger, and I read this and it says I am facing Austin. So who am I to talk about? Might as well go with what this Control Center says..otherwise I'm kinda S.O.L.
Shane Austin...I guess you're relatively new around here, so I guess this must be your second match. Bad luck on your behalf..because you're stuck wrestling against me...The one & ONLY Wonder of the World..Big Daddy Brooks. By now, I should've had me a win, but on the other hand, Peter Tiger kinda isn't all there in his right mind..
But back to Austin, 'cuz god knows if I get sidetracked, then I'm screwed, and
I can't get another loss...Especially from a guy like Shane Austin. Now Shane,
I know a little about you from that RSPWF board, and I've seen your roleplay..Dear god, going on the _JENNY JONES_ show? Please...It's bad enough wrestling against you, but if you're gonna make some sort of guest appearance..Do something that has some respect.. I hear Rosie O'Donnell will shoot ya down if you're a gun enthusiast, but that's a whole different story.
Austin..now, where have I heard _THAT_ name from before? Perhaps, just perhaps, could it be from the World Wrestling Federation's STONE COLD Steve AUSTIN?
Perhaps it could..but then again I've heard lines that some people were "born" with last names like Nash, Hall, Hogan, and everything else that really isn't totally original. However, Big Daddy Brooks may be a SORT-OF rip-off, but at least that wrestler's long gone, and I don't believe we'll ever hear that nickname from him again..Or at least I hope so. Besides, in this business, there's only 1 man great enough to carry the name, legacy, and...well, perhaps tradition of Big Daddy, and that man IS the Only Wonder of the World.
Why call me the ONLY Wonder of the World? Andre was great, but he didn't last all that long. Chyna is a wonder, but not of the World. _I_ am the ONLY Wonder of this world worth seeing. Ask me if I care if the fans boo me or cheer me..It really makes no difference. I've yet to receive a single penny from those god-forsaken bastards. They all suck..each and every one of them. How do I know? Cause when I was a fan, I never contributed a single cent to my favorite wrestlers...and I doubt they'll ever do that for me.
Austin, I could go on and on about how I'm gonna beat your sorry carcass, but I really do have other things to do. The Only Wonder of the World is a _VERY_ busy man, and let's just say that you'll be the first person in my way of victory towards any of those WoW/NBW titles..
That's that, and son, I'm out of here. [With that said, Brooks stands up, walks out of the room, and heads for his '83
Blazer. He then starts it up, and leaves as the camera slowly fades to black.]
-Banjamin X. [NACW] [WOW] [W3O]- "The Only Wonder o/t World" Big Daddy Brooks [LOL] - Ken E. Cheznee "Say your prayers, take your vitamins, and drink your milk!" - RIP Owen Hart
And darnit...I said my prayers, took my vitamins, AND I DRANK MY MILK!
