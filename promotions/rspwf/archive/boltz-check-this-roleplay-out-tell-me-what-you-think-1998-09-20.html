Boltz- Check this roleplay out (Tell me what you think)

20 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene Starts off at a downtown Lowell, Massachusetts house show for an indy fed called the GWA- Grande Wrestling Alliance. The Tsongas arena in
Lowell is packed with about 8,000 people all cheering and yelling. This event is a television one but not national like the JPW is, this one broadcasts to New York, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Florida and some other states. In the back sits Boltz watching the taped footage from the screen.
As Koko B. Ware takes on the Iron Sheik, Boltz watches the two that he used to envy now try to stuggle to pull of this one match. Koko B. Ware ends up beating him with the Koko drop. Boltz is the special guest on this rare televised event and Jason Lives is in the ring ready to conduct the interview!
[Jason Lives] Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen and there are very few of you out there tonight!
<Boo's pour down from the crowd including a few "Fuck You's and You Suck's">
[Jason Lives] You know it's true. The man that I'm about to interview is, er was 1/2 the JPW Tag Team Champions of the World...until Ralph Julevic took them away. Here is the ExtrEmE I.C.O.N.- Booooooooltz!
["Phenomenon" by LL Cool J plays as Boltz comes out of the back to a huge pop. The crowd is going nuts for they have to watch the washed up WWF stars preform in this indy fed and this is a treat. Boltz comes down and shakes some fans hands. As he walks down he looks at a lady and gives her the thumbs up sign, then she flashes him. This brings on an even bigger pop as
Boltz steps into the ring.
[Jason Lives] Hello Boltz. I've been watching you wrestle on the JBS and you've been pretty damn good. I've noticed you're starting a little fued with Ashikari "Mystic Warrior"
[Boltz] As Ken Davison says, "God Bless" you. Well, yeah I've been pretty damn good and yes Ashikari and I have a little fued going on. He talks about stupid things that I don't know where he gets them from, but yeah
I'll talk. The C-Cup tourney? Just a mirror of some of the great tournaments that I've been in. You won't win because you're not skilled enough. You want to see who's better? Then next tourney you get send it this way and I'll fill out the app and everyhting. I'm not very bright? You take a look at your team for War Gamez, and do you see a ringer? I do and you'll all see it at the Pay-Per-View coming up in just a short time. You think that you posess some sort of power that you can take my soul and do all this shit with it. I've heard of playing into your gimmick, but hey this IS rediculous! You think that you actually have the power to do shit like that? There's where everyone makes a mockery out of you, when you speak of things this horendous. For part of your interview you come out and talk about how your going to crush me coming up at the Pay-Per-View and I really don't think that's going to happen. I am going to get you with these two massive arms of mine and strap on the Boltz Tamer, and then we'll see if you can get out of it. If you can then it's the screwdriver and then if that doesn't work it's the Hiroshima Bomb, oh wait! That might offend you because of your heritage. I don't get you and nither does anyone else in the fed and maybe that's your plan. Then you talk about how your going to smash me into the cage and make me bleed and some other shit about me going and crying. That's a pretty good dream and in reality it's not going to happen. You see, if you were even semi-intelligent you'd have seen or heard about my epic battle with Scorch in which I was brutally torn apart as well as him and then you say smashing my head into the side of the cage and making me bleed is going to make me cry? Nope, I don't think so. You want to spank me? Yeah, we all knew that and whatever way you mean it, it's sick because to think for one second that you'd actually want to spank another grown man but it's easy to see why YOU of all people like that sort of thing. You see the warriors you so think that you posess have run out.
Nothing can save your ass from this futile existence and nothing will be as punishing as me beating the shit out of you losers. Why must you run around with the Crime Boss Snake? Is it because you know deep down inside that you couldn't beat me one on one? You see, I don't have the luxary of having a manager nor do I need one. Unlike me you desperately NEED one because on your part the mistakes are flying and when you get into a jam that's where he'll help you out. You must think you're pretty clever. You want to prove to the JPW how great of a wrestler you are? I find that funny because you're such a story teller that it's not funny. You actually think that anyone buys the useless shit that you throw at them? Nope and they shouldn't either. We don't care about the warriors or the AmW. The soultaker? What the hell is that? Another useless thing with no meaning behind the phrase. When I get hit with the bumps of the business? You're one fucked up person. First you do on talking like a posessed man and then you go on talking like what you said 2 seconds earlier didn't matter. I don't cry inside, but I will when I see your ass spread out over the canvas in a heap with the rest of your team. You made a grave mistake talking shit about the other two captains because Peter Damien and I just might want to take you out before we take each other out. You will show me what it takes to be a champion in the JPW!?! I've been a JPW Tag Team Champion and let's throw this no-brainer to the audience...What titles have you held!!! This is why you need to think before you speak because you don't make sense.
You've had shot after shot and still come down like the loser that you are. You went on about the HCW..is anyone there now and/or does anyone care? All the people in this fed are just high-priced talents? Exactly and that's why you're not accepted. You make shit because you are shit. I don't like you and the more you talk the rest of the fed hates you even more. They still don't have a TRUE Champion, fighter and a true warrior? Let me ask you this almighty one...why haven't you held any titles? You claim to be such a warrior and lots of other things, but why in the hell have you never held a title here? That's something that only you can answer and when you do, it still will linger in your mind. Your very familiar with bloody battles? So am I and yet again you fail to see that. You may have Snake talking strategies with you, but you sure as hell aren't smarter than I am.
I know things that you can only fathom of. War Gamez a little hardcore? What's so hardcore about guys in a cage fighting? There isn't weapons, there ain't tables, chairs, thumb tacks or anything else in the ring that's even remotely hardcore so yet again with not even knowing you that well,
I've learned something. You think that you'll demolish me? I highly doubt that and when it's all said and done people will say "Ashikari Who" and they'll bring signs of that to all the events and you'll NEVER live it down. Oh hell yes I want a piece of Americaz Most Wanted. Bring em' on and
I'll take em each one by one! You then go on and say I should build a casket instead of spending my money on one? First off, if I had to buy a casket, I'd be for me to stuff you in so I'd never have to listen to your lame ass again, and second What difference does it make if I buy it or make it. I am going to say this now and end this damn thing, I am not dying this match, I'm not going to lose or even be demolished by you and if you actually think that, well then you're stupider than I thought! Now,
Ashikari "Mystic Spammer" I'm going to talk about your 3rd little interview. You can stop SPACING it out! What the hell are you doing trying to make it longer, and as Shawn Sylkk says, it's pure unadulterated shit!
You want me to get some sort message across to me? I really don't think so. You're like one of those annoying sportscasters on NBC, they think they know what the athelete is thinking but they have no clue! I hate when people try to tell ME!! What I'LL do! It has to be the biggest crock ever and you do it. You respect me? <spits> I spit on that respect because the respect of useless garbage such as yourself means shit to me. I am the
"franchise" of the JPW! When did the JPW start to pick up? AFTER I came here. That's gotta tell you something. I create more heat than a fernus and you're buying right into it Ashikari. You think for one second that I actually fear facing you? YEA, I do. I fear that like you said you'll spank me and that's scarrier than losing the match. You want to make fun of my name? Funny because lots of people want to and then I beat the hell outta them and they don't say shit. You want a one-on-one match with me? Right on! I won't eat for a week and I'll get down to the flyweight calibur. I am
245 right now and I'm regularly 265 but since I've had the flu I've dropped lots of weight. You won't beat Madman for the title and if you somehow do then I'll face you for the Flyweight. You look ahead for that title match as if to signal that you won't win the World and that's basically what I've been saying all along. If you're not SoV then you're Nothing and that's the motto!
[Jason Lives] Wow, I can really feel the intensity forming for that match. I've gotta order that one on Pay-Per-View. Now, you've been talking about some of the various threats to you in this ma..
<Boltz cuts him off> [Boltz] Listen you piece of trash, I've been "adressing" my opponents, not people that can have a threat to me. So as the rock says, shut your damn mouth and know your damn role which is to interview me!
<Heel Pop!> [Jason Lives] Yes, sir. As for another man in this match, David Sundblom has just spoken, anywords on this man?
[Boltz] David Sundblom man of 1000's gimmicks, you're part of Ashikari "Mystic" Spammer's team. I am going to take you out easier than anyone in this match. Last I checked it was a team match and not one man
<cough,hack::spamming:::cough,hack> to get the job done. Scorpio is busy down south and he'll be in tip-top shape for the match at hand. Going back to David Sundblom, the man that gets nauseated over the SoV meanwhile if we ever asked him to join which won't happen he's shit his pants. He's like an
Ashikari, he thinks with his will and not with his brain. Sure, everyone in this match tries to think that they are going to win it and that's fine, it's you dicks that actually talk with the same valves that control the thing that makes you want to win and that makes you look useless! David, you make me want to go and jump off a bridge. You talk alot, but you don't say anything. You talked about the teams and didn't go into much detail.
Boy, is that where you're going to screw up big time.. you can get nauseated by us and that's all and fine, but when you want to sit and think we're useless that's where your going to find out that no longer will I sit all calm and collected at the SoV headquaters with
Ironfist, Madman, Scorpio and Airbourne laughing it up. Nope, I am going to concentrate on this match at hand and it's sad that it had to come to this.
I've lost the fun side, I must prove to myself, the rest of the fed and the
SoV that I have what it takes to be a leader and if I don't win this match,
I don't even deserve that spot. I am going to come out on top so no one need there worry. David Sundblom you're going down!
[Jason Lives] Boltz, I've also noticed a war between you and Peter Damien has broken out, care to elaborate?
[Bolt] Peter Damien, no doubt a pain in the ass. He is like a thorn in a flower patch and you just cannot get rid of his lame ass. I am going to however and I know that earlier I said we might team and we still just might. I still have a hatred for Ashikari and so does he and maybe we can put our differences behind for a short time. The fact of the matter is
Peter Pumpkin, you tried to split the SoV apart and why is that? Well brother, you knew we had the best damned thing going and you couldn't handle that. You knew that if we stayed together we'd have the JPW by storm and most of all, you knew that if we had a second to be alone in the ring with you, you'd be all done. The fact is Peter, you don't seem to think when you speak, similiar to the losers on the JPW Team. You are going down in this match and you're going down hard.
[Jason Lives] Let me ask this, how do the SoV plan to own the JPW as you said? [Boltz] Well, Lyssa Wylde is going to take the Intercontinental Strap from
Shane Andrews and that's a given. Then, we're going to have the World Title because either myself, Scorpio or Davison will win that. Ironfist is clear to have some sort of title and Madman already has the Flyweight strap and so it's clear that we own this federation. There really ain't any other stable in this fed that can match us. Oh, Like what? Triple Threat? I don't think so, these people must be born deaf, blind and dumb because they don't see anything. I am going to pull off the win at War Gamez. You can count on that!
["Phenomenon" plays as Boltz walks out to the back. The camera follows him and as he steps outside with his gym bag that he picked up when he left to go, he pushes the button on his car alarm and then begins to get in. He drives a Red Ferrari GI and the licence plate is blanked out as he speeds away.
