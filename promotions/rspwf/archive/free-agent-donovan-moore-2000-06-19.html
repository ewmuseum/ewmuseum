[Free Agent] Donovan Moore

19 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings I'm looking to get back into a fed with a face character of mine named
Donovan Moore. Yes, he's a FACE :)
Anyway, here's what I'm looking for. -Original wrestler ONLY fed. No mixed feds. - E-mail based and must have RP/Flash packets. I don't like the e-mail feds where everyone sends their RPs to everyone else. Too many e-mails. - Prefer a fed that has at least held one card or close to holding one. I don't want to wait around for a fed to start. Contact me: E-mail: [detail withheld] ICQ: [detail withheld] AOL IM: [detail withheld] If your fed doesn't have what I'm looking, don't bother contacting me. Sorry, but it'll be a waste of time :) A little bit about Donovan Moore Height: 6-2 Weight: 235 From: Tallahassee, Florida Manager: Denise Background: Moore has lived a life of tragedy, but has gotten through it. He lost both his parents before the age of 10 and was raised by his uncle until he was 13. An old shop owner took him under his wing and that's when
Moore discovered wrestling.
He was two-time Florida Amateur Champion and signed a contract with the NAWC at the age of 22. Moore enjoyed great success in the NAWC, capturing the
Lightweight Title 2 times and holding the Intercontinental Championship for an extended period of time. He was the only man to hold 2 titles at the same and was voted the best Lightweight and IC Champ.
Sample RP (in preparation for NAWC March to Mayhem PPV where Moore was facing Speedy Irons and Thane Sloan in a handicap match. He won the match.)
[Scene opens to the dark interior of a car speeding down a long stretch of dark highway. The camera is positioned in the backseat tilted slightly upward. The only light comes from the dashboard lights and a digital clock that reads 3:26 am. The shadow of the driver can barely be made out as he changes lanes and takes the next exit off. The car stops at a signal light and the clear distinct shape of Donovan Moore can be made out through the street lamp illumination. Moore's usual neatly combed light brown hair looks like it hasn't been touched for days, he has grown a thin stubble beard and his eyes are bloodshot red as the camera catches him just as he looks at the rearview mirror.]
[The car finally starts to move again into the pitch-black darkness. Moore starts to tap the steering wheel ever so slightly.]
Donovan Moore: 3 letters have been running through my mind for the past 2 weeks. 3 letters that have haunted me since I saw the site I could never ever picture. W-H-Y..why..why. The only person that can answer that question is nowhere to be found. No phone calls, no one has heard from her, she doesn't even have the respect to explain her actions in a letter. The moment I saw Denise with a chair in her hand, the same chair that was used to knock me out, a part of me died right there. I could see the look in
Denise's eye. The look of fear, disbelievement, shock, call it what you will, she knew what she had done. She not only cost me the NAWC Lightweight
Championship, she cost me the bond that I thought could never be broken.
The latter is much more valuable to me than the former. [Moore runs his hand through his disheveled hair and wipes his eyes. The car suddenly accelerates and then slows down.]
DM: I haven't slept for more than 3 hours at a time and with each passing day I grow more frustrated and angry. Mere days from March to Mayhem and I have not one, but two men who are partly responsible for what I'm facing now. Finally, something else to concentrate on, but frankly it makes me just about as sick. I don't often
say.in fact.I never say that a wrestler isn't worthy of what he has achieved, but Speedy Irons is rare exception. Where I come from Speedy, we have a little thing called "sportsmanship" and
"winning with class." Yes, you beat me, you are the Lightweight Champion, but you are acting like a teenager with his first set of car keys. Don't you know how to carry yourself as a champion? You don't shove that belt in
the camera every chance you get. You don't keep reminding people who you are. You make a name for yourself by actions, not words. You don't even have the decency to give me the tiniest bit of credit and that's the biggest insult of all.
[Moore pauses again as he takes a deep breath and glances in the rearview mirror again.] DM: I'm not certain about a lot things, I'm not certain where this road evens leads to, but I am certain that I'll regain my championship and restore my name. I hope it doesn't come to this, but if I have to, I will
BEAT some respect out of you Speedy Irons. Pose for the camera and hog the spotlight while it lasts because I have never, in my entire life, had the desire to put someone in his place more than you. And that place is flat on your back in the middle of the ring at March to Mayhem. I wouldn't be surprised if you and Thane got to Denise and corrupted her. In fact, the more I think about, the more it makes sense, the angrier I get. [Moore pounds the steering wheel and the car veers to the left sharply before he regains control.] Every man has his breaking point and I'm just about there
Irons, so I go into this match not caring and when I stop caring, that's when things get dangerous. Not only for me, but also for you and Thane
Sloan.
[Moore reaches into the seat next to him and grabs a water bottle. Instead of drinking it, he pours the whole bottle on him. He doesn't flinch as the water rushes down and soaks his face, shirt and the car seat.]
DM: I'm well aware that there are two men in this match against me. All the skeptics out there already have me penciled down for a loss. After all, how many men actually win a handicap match? Yet alone, one with the stakes this high. If this were a match at another time in another place, I'd say I'd have a million to one odds, but the way I feel, I am the favorite. While
Irons worries about how he looks on camera and impressing the young ladies in the crowd, Thane Sloan "educates" the entire NAWC on various subjects that have little to do with what he's facing at March to Mayhem. I still have great respect for you Sloan, but you should be holding class with only one person..me. I could be like your friend Irons and flaunt my NAWC
Intercontinental Title in your face, but I'm above that. While you have countless other NAWC superstars on your mind, I have 2. I don't know which of you two is worse. I don't know why I continue to say to myself that I respect my fellow wrestlers when it gets thrown right back in my face. You,
Sloan, muttered the word "untalented" and frankly, I'm surprised you didn't throw in the word "fluke" considering I was the one who beat you for the IC belt about 3 weeks ago. Some call you a wrestling icon because you've been accomplished all these great things and yet you haven't given back. Instead of facing the fact that your skills are deteriorating and recognizing that young talent like myself is the future, you mutter the word "untalented."
That isn't the kind of vocabulary I expect from an "Educator," I'd expect it from a complete and utter MORON. Harsh words, yes, but deserved nonetheless. I'm tired of giving credit where it isn't due and it all stops at March to Mayhem.
[Moore suddenly slams on the brakes and makes a 180 with the wheel. He starts to speed in the opposite direction.]
DM: Some might have viewed March to Mayhem as the continued downward spiral of Donovan Moore, but instead it is going to be the rebirth. Denise or no
Denise, I have more than enough to focus on to put a "moron" and a
"prima-donna" in their place. After this match is over Irons and Sloan, I may not have your respect, I may not make you, but I will MAKE..YOU..BETTER.
[Fade out as the clock 4:16 am]
