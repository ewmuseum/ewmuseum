another RP, better than the last one...

21 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
.... but read that one first then tell me what you think of both please. I need help improving my rp's so please read both of them and tell me what you think. ******************************************************************* (A bolt of lightening strikes. The scene opens looking at a house in the middle of no where, all the surrounds it but fields and farm land so it is obviously a farm house. From what we can see the farm looks deserted and a storm is ripping through making gates slam shut and anything that isn’t nailed down blow away and go flying through the air. Rain beats down on the ground and thunder keeps roaring and banging up in the clouds as lightning tears down to the Earth from the sky.)
(The scene cuts to a room inside the farm house and we can now hear what sounds like a group of children whispering, we can’t hear what they’re saying because they are all whispering together but we can just hear there constant whispering. We can still hear the wind from outside and see flashes of light from the lightening through a distant window at the back of the room. The room looks like it has been hit by a storm itself with it being almost empty apart from a few cupboards , draws and shelves around the from and the paper and broken glass that is all over the floor The walls are cracked and damaged and there is no carpet on the floor. The walls are all covered in writing all of it abusive such as “DIE YOU CRAZY BITCH”, “WHY THEM WHAT DID THEY
DO?”, ROT IN HELL YOU MAD BASTARD!” and so on. The only light in the room is coming from candles randomly placed around the room only there’s a lot of them so we can still see quite clearly.)
The Angel: Can you hear them? (The Angel walks into the room with that crow on his shoulder, he stands in the middle of the room with his long black hair wet down again and he is wearing his shiny black trench coat.)
The Angel: Can you hear there pain? I can, I always can. No one comes here anymore you see there’s a horror story that goes with this place. A farmer used to live here with his wife, there names were Mr and Mrs. Doyle. They were well liked about the village some miles down the hill and provided the village with pork, beef and milk. Mrs. Doyle so wanted a family, a couple of kids to call her own but unfortunately for her Mr. Doyle had a slight problem and the couple could never have children. One day while on her way home a shopping trip in the village Mrs. Doyle spotted a little girl, little Beverly White who was only 6 years old, playing on her on in one of the many fields which you will pass walking up to this house. Over come with emotion and regret that she couldn’t have a girl of her own Mrs. Doyle saw it as a perfect opportunity, she approached Beverly who she knew offered her cookies and milk and took her home with her much to the horror of Mr. Doyle. In the blind panic that followed, afraid of what the villagers would say about his wife if he was to take poor little Beverly back to her parents, Mr. Doyle instead murdered the child and hid the body in the upstairs attic. But it didn’t end there, over the next ten months 8 more children went missing from the village and eventually following an extensive investigation by the local Police this house was searched and all the missing children were found murdered in the attic. The village was rocked by this and destroyed this farm a few days after the arrests of the Doyle’s, vandalising all the rooms of this house and burning all the unfortunate farm animals alive in a field not far from here. Mr. Doyle is long dead now after committing suicide in his cell while serving a life sentence in prison while Mrs. Doyle is still alive and still moving from mental institute to mental institute, she is indeed a deeply sick woman. The anger in the village has never died down, it never gone away....
(The Angel picks a candle up, blows the tiny flame out and crushes the candle in his hand.)
The Angel:... everyone still seems to be asking “how could this happen?”. All those poor children murdered by a man you thought he was protecting his wife who kept abducting them, all murdered in this very house. People keep there distance from this place now so no one comes here no more....
(The Angel walks over to the window and watches the raging storm.) The Angel:... apart from me. Where as other people keep there distance I’ve found a home here. Can you here them? Can you even begin to imagine there pain, they’re all trapped souls now, trapped in this house forever, TRAPPED. CAN YOU IMAGINE THERE
PAIN? I CAN, you see I come here because I can relate to them, I can relate to there pain because I feel it too and although they can’t hear me and I can’t do anything to help them now there voices and there pain remind me of my mission, why I was brought back, brought back to a world where children can be harmed and murdered. They always remind me what I’m here for. Can you hear them? I can....
(BOOM, Suddenly thunder roars and a lightening bolt seemingly strikes inside the room and The Angel is suddenly gone, disappeared into thin air but his crow remains. The crow flys to the floor and stands next to a photo that lays among all the broken glass. The photo is of a happy couple, it’s of Mr and Mrs.
Doyle. The voices whispering begin to phase out until we can only hear one voice on it’s own, it sounds like a little girl whispering.)
The Voice: Why couldn’t you save us? Why angel why? (The voice stops whispering and starts crying. The picture fades to black as we look at the photo of the Doyles and hear the crying of a trapped and tutored soul.)
