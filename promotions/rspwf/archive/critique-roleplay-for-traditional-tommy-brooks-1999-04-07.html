[CRITIQUE] Roleplay for "Traditional" Tommy Brooks

7 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please let me know what you think of this roleplay for my newest character, who can be seen in the GSWP, "Traditional" Tommy Brooks. ( [detail withheld] )   /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\   [Scene: Ontario, Canada, around 7:00p.m. Were inside a strict wrestling gym, as a regular 16 x 16 size ring, with black leather ropes, and metal ring posts, stands as the center of attention. Outside around the ring, the gym is scattered with various work out machines, and bench presses, some taken up by various men, others standing alone and dusty. The gym, which is not very big, though suitable for anyone, is lit dimly with tinted lights that hang from the rafters in the ceiling.]   [Soon, the scene develops onto the ring, where a man dressed in a pair of pink spandex, and wrestling boots, is sparring with a man who is obviously the main attraction, as he is dressed in black leather boots, a pair of black spandex, one side reading "Traditional", the other reading "Tommy", and a tee shirt which read "Tradition Rules". The two work around, the more attract- ive of the two taking most of advantage, while what seems to be a reporter, jumps on ringside with a microphone in hand, calling for the man....let's listen in.]   [REPORTER] Excuse me....excuse me! Mr. Brooks! I'm from GSWP...you asked for a reporter sir....correct?   [The man who was totally controlling the sparring session in the ring, turns around, and stares down the reporter....then he replies in a distinguished matter.]   [MAN] ...yea...yea just give me a second alright man....   [REPORTER] Sure thing....   [The man mumbles something to his sparring partner, and then walks over to the nearest corner, grabbing a towel, which he wipes his brow with, and he then walks over to the corner nearest where the reporter stood on the canvas at ringside.]   [REPORTER] Alright, let's do this...it's going to be a basic interview, since this is the first time the GSWP is getting to see you, alright?   [MAN] Yea..   [REPORTER] I'll just ask you some basic..   [MAN] What the hell do I look like to you? A rookie or something? I was slappin on leglocks when you were studying for your history quiz there buddy, let's just do this, tradition doesn't supply me with a lot of time alright?   [REPORTER] ....yea...yes sir.   [REPORTER] Alright, first off, who are you, where are you from, and all that stuff?   [MAN] Everybody knows who I am...well everyone, that is anyone should know who I am....I, my dear reporter, am the great- est thing to happen to professional wrestling, since Ric Flair glazed the chests with chops...I am the new hope of bringing _TRADITION_ back to this sick sport of professional wrestling. "Traditional" Tommy Brooks, is here and he's coming straight out of Ontario, Canada, where the real wrestlers of the world, are produced...   [REPORTER] Any past experience in wre...   [TOMMY BROOKS] Past experience?! How do you think I have a nickname already? How do you think I am known WORLDWIDE as the man who will bring tradition back, the man who will re-instate technical wrestling, of course I have "past ex- perience" in professional wrestling...   [REPORTER] Well...where?   [TOMMY BROOKS] All over...Canada, United States, Japan....been there done that...Stampede Championship Wrestling, now owned by the great Stu Hart, gave me my start in professional wrest- ling, and I owe everything to them, but now, Tommy Brooks is bigger then them, I'm bigger then all those little indepen- dant promotions, hell, I may be bigger then the GSWP....   [REPORTER] Any world championships? Any titles?   [TOMMY BROOKS] What's the difference? Gold or no gold, all they are, are straps of leather with one heck of an over sized belt buckle hanging on the front.   [REPORTER] So no?   [TOMMY BROOKS] .....No, I have not held any titles....   [REPORTER] Well, I dont see how you can be so known but yet have no past championships...   [TOMMY BROOKS] You know something, your a real funny guy, I bet you feel real big, talking to a man of my stature...a man of my fort- ittude, but the fact of the matter is, Tommy Brooks doesn't like you...you understand that? I dont like you, because you, my friend, are nothing but a small peon in the wrestling business. You get a little peice of paper telling you to come out to Canada, and do a little interview, and then a week later, you get a twenty-dollar paycheck in the mail, am I right?   [REPORTER] ...well...   [TOMMY BROOKS] Then you go out with the guys later that week, an brag to them what you did, who you talked to, is that how you get by in life? Yea..I thought so...get out of my face you sick peice of American trash...   [Brooks grabs the mic out of the reporters hand, and then pushes him off ringside, as he lands on some blue padding, butt first, as Brooks looks straight into the camera, and speaks with a determined tone.]   [TOMMY BROOKS] GSPW...Robbie Kincade....I dont care for either of you, in fact I dont care for nearly, nearly the entire roster. I am here for only one purpose, and that, is to bring the TRADITION back into this worthelss promotion. I, am the greatest, and most talented athlete in Garden State Wrestling Promotion today, tommorow, and forever will be.....so if any of you think you even deserve to step into the ring with Tommy Brooks, think again, before you realise the one thing, that will change your career, and life forever...           _TRADITION_                       _WILL ALWAYS_                                        _RULE!!!!_       [TOMMY BROOKS] Kincade...your only the first, but you better....fear the worst.     [The reporter slowly gets back on ringside, and snatches the microphone away from Tommy Brooks, which brings a disg- usted look to his face, as the reporter quickly runs away, as the scene slowly fades to black. Tommy Brooks and Robby Kincade, a match for the making, it should be great, be sure to check it out, this Sunday, at the GSWP Madhouse!]       =-=-==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-   Handler of Dave "too Extreme" Bean [ICW 0-0] [SPWA 0-0]   Handler of "Traditional" Tommy Brooks [GSWP 0-0]   =No Limit Records Supporter=   =Cash-Money Ha8a=
