(ACWA) "Backstage Pass" Flash show.

31 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(It's 9pm on a monday, and the RTN cable network's original comedy "Bubbles" has just ended. With no commercial breaks between the two programs, the sound of Nazareth's "Hair of the Dog" plays as the black and blue ACWA logo fades in from a black screen. With a thunderclap the words "Backstage Pass"
form beneath the logo, letting everyone know that the ACWA is alive and well on basic cable! The music plays for a few seconds more before the interior of the new and improved ACWA studio appears.
Behind a broad white desk sits Johnny Axis. On the wall behind Johnny is the ACWA logo that had just been filling the screen, blue on black. Axis himself looks quite collected, happy, almost as if he's in his own home. He wears an "ACWA Innovation" T-shirt with a sportsjacket. His shoulder-length hair pulled into a ponytail, and he still has a soft cast on his left hand.
As the camera zooms in close on Axis and the desk, axis begins to speak.) Axis: Hey hey hey, folks. It's me, Johnny Axis here with ACWA Backstage
Pass, your weekly dose of hype. Oh, but it's not just hype this week, folks. It seems that our beloved owner has recieved a few pieces of hatemail from some nimrods in high places. Y'know, the heads of some of those fringe element groups? Well, anyway, these guys have been bitching that we don't show enough wrestling, so this week, to mollify the morons, we got not one, not two, but three matches from house shows with perfectly predictable outcomes...
(Axis pauses in his speech, considering his words.) Axis: Okay, not_all predictable, but what can you expect from the Bald,
Scarred Freak? That guy who claims that everybody should be "just like"
him? He was scheduled to work a house show this last Friday, right? He has a match against some nobody who he coudl like, beat...severely beat...but he doesn't. What happens, you ask? Well now, you'll just have to stay tuned, won't you?
---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- William Craven. (Cut from the ACWA studio to the action. The camera is focused on the broadcast area where sits "the Prognosticator" Paul Jackson and "Sweetstuff"
Scott Lane call the action in one of the ACWA's small venues, a bar. These two are already in an animated discussion as they appear on the screen.)
Jackson: (Already in the middle of his sentence.) ...been awhile, but William Craven is going to be in action during a house show. Lane: Do house shows have enough insurance to cover a Craven match? I mean, honestly. . . Every *bleep* time he has a match something gets broken.
Like, oh, cameras, tables, chairs, bones, people, you get the idea. . . Jackson: Yes, yes I do. Craven is arguably the most dangerous man in the
ACWA, and I'd say that nobody would argue that he's not the most destructive. He mangled an ACWA title belt, like you said, tables, chairs, and put people in the hospital several times in his career. . .and he hasn't even been in the sport a year yet!
Lane: I don't care what he does so long as he doesn't do it to me. Jackson: I agree with that statement. Now we're going to take you to another man that I'm sure agrees with that statement, David Strubridge.
(The man the young girls like to call "Dead Sexy" climbs the ringsteps from the outside, missing his cue by a few seconds. He places a cup of coffee on the ring apron and steps between the ropes, ready to do his job.)
Sturbridge: Ladies and gentlemen... This match is scheduled for one fall and has a twenty-minute time-limit. Your referee...now...getting into the ring...
("Dead Sexy" Dave Sturbridge stops and looks in amusement at a young man with long brown hair and wearing a referee's uniform who climbs into the ring.)
Sturbridge: Now _in_ the ring is Freddie Jacobs! (A few scattered cheers come from the drunken crowd who seems to think that
Jacobs is a wrestler. Jacobs looks confused, and raises his hands in mock victory as a wrestler might. It's a very silly looking scene.)
Sturbridge: Now, introducing first... ("Superfreak" by Rick James plays over the loudspeaker and a silvery-dressed young black man emerges from the backstage area with a leap.)
Sturbridge: Hailing from Jersey City, New Jersey, he stands five-foot-six and weighs in tonight at one-hundred and fifty-two pounds...
(His long gerry-curled hair waggling side-to-side as he gyrates in what seems to be a disco dance, this young man looks like a refugee from the seventies. The crowd pops slightly, recognizing this young man. They like him, obviously.)
Sturbridge: ...He is...SSSUUUPEEERRRFFFRRREEEAAAK!!! (Dancing all the way, Superfreak makes his way down the aisle. He's just as happy as he can be to be in this tiny venue performing for about a hundred fans. He hops up to the apron, and then over the top rope to stand at the ready, awaiting his opponent.)
Jackson: Superfreak looks awfully confident for a man in his position... Lane: He probably doesn't know who he's fighting. (snickers.) Jackson: If that's the case, he's in for a rude awakening... Sturbridge: His opponent, now making his way down the aisle... (The lights in the arena lower to a sunset level of illumination. Sampled lyrics from "Prison Sex" by Tool begin to play over the loudspeakers.)
Music: You look so precious... (Long pause then again.) Music: You look so precious...now... (A blood red spotlight shines on the entranceway as the crowd begins to react. They give a strong mixed pop that leans more towards a heel than a face pop. Suddenly a large figure bursts from the back bathed in the intense red light.)
Sturbridge: Hailing from Detroit, Michigan... Music: Do unto you now what has been done to me. . . (Standing in the entrance way the figure is illuminated by the spotlight. He appears to be the stuff of nightmares. Covered in deep grooves that tell of old injuries. Not an inch of space exists on him that isn't crossed by a scar of some sort. He is shaved from head to toe and wears nothing but a pair of pants. In his right hand is what appears to be a wooden sword. The crowd recognizes the former ACWA champ and screams ever-louder, some cheering, most booing.)
Sturbridge: He stands six-foot-five and weighs in tonight at two-hundred and eighty-seven pounds. . . Music: You're breathing so I guess you're still alive... Sturbridge: He is. . .WILLIAM CRAVEN (Craven stands in the entryway for only a moment more before beginning his walk down the aisle. The spotlight follows him as he walks. The music continues on but sounds odd as certain lyrics fade out while others blast louder than normal. It seems almost as if someone is trying to convey some message.)
Music: Won't you come just a bit closer? (Louder, louder, ever louder the crowd grows. The pop from the fans is almost deafening and some articles of garbage rain on him from the crowd.
Paying it no heed, he continues on his way.) Music: I need you to feel this. (The music goes instrumental as Craven finishes making his way to the ring. He stands on the outside his weapon the wooden katana in hand and looks back at the crowd a twisted grin forming on his face exposing missing teeth. He then slides into the ring on his belly to fall to one knee leaning on his kendo stick and glaring down at the mat in a detached way.)
Music: You look so precious...now... (The lights raise again and Craven tosses his weapon to a ring attendant on the outside. Superfreak, who has been staring at Craven the entire time, takes a step back, horrified.)
Jackson: Uh-oh, it looks like you were right, Scott. Superfreak looking shocked. (Craven locks eyes with his small opponent for a second before adancing on
David Sturbridge and taking the microphone without much of a struggle.
Sturbridge beats a hasty retreat as Craven begins to speak.) Craven: Whoa now, where are you going? (Superfreak has a leg through the ropes and is about to leave, but stops in his tracks when Craven speaks to him. He just stares at the bald, scarred freak like a deer in the headlights.)
Craven: You're not...scared are you? (Craven walks slowly towards Superfreak who still doesn't move.) Craven: You shouldn't be. You shouldn't be afraid because I'm not the one who's gonna beat you up tonight.
(Superfreak doesn't believe it one bit, and hops down to the floor. Craven advances to the ropes and leans over them while Superfreak scrambles to get out from under the big man.)
Craven: No, really...I'm not... (The lights lower again and a red spotlight shines down on the entranceway, imitating Craven's entrance. This time, however "Prison Sex" by Tool does
NOT play, rather "Crazy" by Seal plays...)
Music: There are two people inside my head. One of them's got a gun, to shoot the other one...
Craven: He's five-six and one-hundred-thirty pounds. Music: Miracles will happen as we sleep... (The music continues as Craven finishes up.) Craven: He's my main man, man... He's Doctor Victor Schwartz! Music: No wer'e never gonna survive, unless...we get a little crazy... (In the entranceway a slight man in a red amateur singlet, and matching boots and pads appears. He wears thick cokebottle glasses and looks very happy to be here.)
Lane: Holy! Ho..ha..hahahaaha!!! Jackson: William Craven's psychiatrist is coming to the ring... It looks like Craven is going to give his match over to his doctor. Wait, is he
Craven's manager now too?
Lane: I don't know, he's been around a lot lately, more than your average doc anyway. (Schwartz steps lively on his way to the ring and tries to slide under the bottom rope, but stops sliding after only a few feet, and crawls the rest of the way. Once inside he hugs his favorite patient, and the two taunt
Superfreak.) Schwartz: Hey-ay, what, you afraid of a middle-aged Jewish doctor? Geez, what a pansy! Caman! Get in here!
(Craven steps aside, and dances across the ring, rolling out on the side opposite Superfreak. Schwartz waits with the microphone in hand. Freddie
Jacobs cackles in one corner while Superfreak finally begins making his way uneasily into the ring. He climbs the ringsteps this time, looking at
Craven the entire time.)
*ding**ding**ding* Jackson: Freak is really nervous, and I really can't blame him. Lay aside the fact that Craven is on the outside, just think of how we've seen
Schwartz act in the past. He may not be nearly the physical specimin that
Craven is, but he's still crazy...
Lane: Um, but Paulie, he's not a wrestler. Jackson: Crazy is crazy, Scott. I'd think you'd know that. (The good doctor gets in a "bring it on" stance, crouching down hands extended in front of him, waving Superfreak on. Freak responds by looking to the crowd. The crowd cheers, loving the chaos. They want some violence, and they don't care who does it. Superfreak begins to circle his opponent, and Schwartz follows suit.)
Lane: C'mon, he's smaller than the referee! Jackson: True, I... Wait, Scott, so is Superfreak. Lane: Oh...yeah. (In the ring the two competitors go for a collar-and-elbow tieup. Schwartz makes a quick gobehind and hits a waistlock takedown on Superfreak before standing, stepping on the man's back, and stepping off in what looks to be a very embarrassing move.)
Jackson: Well, Schwartz looks to have some amatuer skill if nothing else. I...dropkick from behind on Schwatz! Schwartz up, another dropkick sends the doctor down! Scwhartz stays there this time, he's down!
Lane: Geez, Paulie, this is just what I said would happen. Jackson: It's not over yet... Schwartz taking it on the chin though, Superfreak just pounding the hell out of his face... This is just ugly. Referee Jacobs is pulling the man off, and Superfreak lets go. He's celebrating, a bit too early for that.
(Jacobs checks on the good doctor who grabs onto him. Schwartz clings to the referee, screaming about a sprained kidney or something. It's obvious that something is up, and it only takes a second for it to take place as
Craven is back in the ring, boken in hand.)
***!!!CRACK!!!*** Jackson: GOOD GOD! Craven just shattered his boken over Superfreak's head! Superfreak is bleeding and Craven has left the ring! Schwartz has released
Jacobs and both men scramble for Superfreak, Schwartz pinning, Jacobs counting... 1, 2, 3... Not even a chance...
*ding**ding**ding* (Schwartz raises his hands in victory, ecstatic over his win. Craven in the ring again, picking his doctor up in his arms and celebrating. Jacobs checks on Superfreak who is totally unconscious. "Crazy" plays again and
Sturbridge makes the official announcement.)
Lane: That was the best match I've ever seen! Jackson: That wasn't a match at all! This young man isn't waking up, he could be seriously injured by that shot and the crazy people are leaving the winners! This is so like Craven. The ultraviolent, bald, scarred, demonic freak that he is!
Lane: Hey, you say that like it's an insult! (Cut back to studio.) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- (Leaning back in his lushly apholstered, extemely expensive office chair,
Axis has his feet up on the broadcast desk, talking to someone off camera.)
Axis: Hey now, baby, I'm telling you. You stick with me and you'll go places. (Axis continues blithely on unaware that the camera is rolling.) Axis: Meet me in an hour when this *bleep* pile of a show is over, we'll talk. Then when I move up to Lane's spot on the BIG show, maybe you can come with me. What say?
Female voice: I say you're on the air... Axis: What? I...AH!!! (With a jerk Johnny looks to camera, absolutely horrified with the fact that he's just said what he's said on the air.)
Axis: This...this isn't live, can't we edit that out? Female voice: Um, sure...if it'll make you feel better. Axis: Heh, thanks babe... Okay, tell me when... (The woman off screen sighs, then...) Female voice: Okay, we're on in three...two...one...go... Axis: Hey guys, wasn't that just incredibly *bleep*ed up? I mean honestly, a middle-aged doctor in the ring, wrestling. When will they stop letting these non-athletes in the wrestling ring? Huh? Ah, anyway, it's on to the waffleboy mister Kenneth Vanneth. He's done well in the ACWA so far, but now he's taking on Love Muscle alum Riot in what has to be referred to as a
"David versus Goliath" matchup. Oh yeah, Kenny beat Hiroshima, but so what?
Hiro's fat, out of shape, etcetera. Riot was one of the members of the greatest group ever to lace boots in this organization, the Love Muscle.
Hey, they may be gone now, but I think we all remember when they were_the in thing on Wednesday nights. Anyway, Vann has something to say, but then don't they all? Roll it...
(Cut to clip.) ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Kenny Vann. ( The scene opens to the backstage of an ACWA Houseshow. Sorry, Hiroshima, but you're twice my size.. and sorry, Riot, but damn ! The people who pull the strings think it's funny having the new guy fight wrestlers out of his category..

The crowd pops. Obviously for the word 'oaf'. ) Vann : And what can I do about this ? One simple word, challenge. That's right, beat the men in the back to it.. And the man I challenge is none other then the new Mexican Luchador, Vendetta. My age, my size, my next win..

17th, we'll see when we get to it..Now, before I leave you for the next great match, I'll just have this to say to Riot.. Riot, the bigger they are, the harder they fall.. Damn, that was corny.
( Vann hands the mic to the ringannouncer and leaves the arena, signing autographs for and highfiving the fans. )
