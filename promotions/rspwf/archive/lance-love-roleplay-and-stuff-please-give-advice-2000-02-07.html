Lance Love Roleplay and stuff: Please give advice!

7 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, Here is a small sample of a roleplay for one of my characters. It's not much...but quality over quantity right? Also, beforehand is his history and his entrance...just in case anyone wanted to give me some constructive criticism on that. Thanks for any help.
[detail withheld] [detail withheld] HISTORY:Lance was just your regular ordinary kid growing up in Minneopolis. He has always been a huge fan of professional wrestling, but never dreamed of being a wrestler until one day he was at a show watching one of the greats Brad "Love Machine" Morris and saw Morris win his first ever WHW TV Title. Lance right then and there knew where he wanted to end up in the world...in a pro wrestling ring. He was able to set up a meeting with Brad Morris almost a year later, and Brad decided to take Lance under his wing. About a year ago, Lance finally made his debut in the SCW and from there...he's been constantly on the rise being a tag champion on numerous occasions. And now it's time for Lance to become an even bigger star in the sport...and the Empire Wrestling is where he wants to prove it.
ENTRANCE: All of a sudden the lights dim as a red beam hits the ring. The red beam then transforms into numbers and letters in the middle of the ring. 100% appears to the crowd as a burst of red pyros go off at the entrance. "Pretty Fly For A White Guy" by offspring booms across the auditorium as "All Natural" Lance Love emerges wearing his long brown curly hair back in a ponytail. He has long black tights on with red lettering going down both legs that read 100% NATURAL. On the back of his tights are little red letters: LL. He then struts to the ring making a few gestures to all the beautiful women and arguing a bit with the men.
<<Camera opens up on what appears to be a living room. On a white leather sofa, with his feet propped up on a coffee table sits "All
Natural" Lance Love. In one hand is a NWWA magazine that Lance shuffles through as he drinks a few swigs every now and then from the budwesier in his other hand. Lance's long brown hair is tied back in a ponytail, and his face is cleanly shaven. As the camera approaches, Lance offers a sly smile and begins to speak.>>
[LANCE LOVE]: "You know, it hasn't been that long ago since I first ventured out into this business. Back in South Carolina is where a 19 year old kid got into the ring for the first time and got his butt kicked from one ring post to the other. Who would have though a little more than a year later, that same kid would have made an impact on the sport of proffessional wrestling that must entertainers only dream about. You're looking at the man who has willingly allowed this sport to be put upon his shoulders and stride into the new millenium. You are looking at the man who has made an impact, is making an impact, and plans on making an even bigger impact in the future...in the GPW, in the
NWWA, hell in the entire sports entertainment world. You see, they don't call me All Natural just because of what you see in front of you.
Don't get me wrong, I am one hundred percent all man, all muscle, all everything, but the truth is i give it my one hundred percent every time
I step foot out of a locker room. I don't play games out there, I do it the all natural way. The only way I know how. I do it to win, and really I do it for the pure enjoyment of watching moron after moron express their anguish and frustration all because of me. It's amazing what just one man can really do isn't it?"
<<Lance takes a swig and then opens up to a poster in the magazine. One of Vince Viper. Lance giggles a little.>>
[LANCE LOVE]: "There are a lot of new faces I see as I get ready to make my first appearences in a ring labled with the letters NWWA. There are new experiences in the future that hold who knows what. But as I look at all the new faces, I see a few familiar ones as well. Vile
Vince Viper, I've seen ya wrestle. I've seen you do your damnest to be the bad guy. I've seen you at your dirtiest, and quite frankly, I loved it. Every bit of it. Vince, if theres one thing you got, it's my respect. Hell, not just anyone can ruthlessly shatter images and dreams of young wrestlers just entering the business...but in all honestly I think you have that talent. You know, maybe I'm proposing a little of an alliance of sorts somewhere in the future. Maybe I'm proposing the best damn match between two men who don't give a damn about anyone else that will tear the roof down in the near future. That's whats fun and exciting in the business...you really never know what's right around that corner until you turn it and hit it head on, whatever it may be.
Vince, I just want you to know that I know you're here. And I'm sure, in one way or another, somethins bigs a brooin!"
[LANCE LOVE]: "You all know what Lance has done for this sport. I've been two time tag champon...I've beaten the best...I've lost to the best...I've carried countless wrestlers on my back and made them the best. In just a years time, I went from a nothing kid from the dirt holes in Minneapolis, to a damn good wrestler that is showing what it really takes to be the best. And yet, I have still not been handed a
firm contract with the NWWA. What's that mean? You can draw your own conclusions...but I guarantee one thing. If the NWWA gives me a deal...with a generous amount of money of course, because no matter what anyone else says, every person on the face of the earth is only worried about the green....then I'm gonna be in everyone's face, every night of the week. And I will not let anyone forget that I am completely natural...and I am the man to lead this business into the next millenium!!"
