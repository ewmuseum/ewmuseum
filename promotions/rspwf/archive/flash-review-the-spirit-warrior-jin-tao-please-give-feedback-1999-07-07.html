[ Flash - Review ] The "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao. [Please Give Feedback]

7 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello RSPWF' ites !! This is MD (Mario). You may know, I am the handler of the one, "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao. I haven't really posted a post of my "Own" in a couple weeks, so I decided to post another (Yes Another.) Flash
Review. I am really looking for some feedback on my character in general, and a steady critique on one of my flashes here I am about to post. I have been in eWrestling now for approximately Two Months. I am doing quite well (IMO), trying to acquire character development in the following promotions. (GLCW - GLOW - HHWO)
So anyways, please read and enjoy. Thank you all and I hope to give you all an enjoyable post. Thankyou & G'night..
________________________________________________________________ [The GLCW All Stars of Wrestling Show comes to a closure, as our camera shot picks up inside one of the GLCW Dressing Rooms, within the lustrous GLCW
Centre. As our camera shot pans around the quiet, barely illuminated, and eerie
Dressing Room, it brings a certain 'Chill to the Spine.'
The camera man uses the dim light that he has to his advantage, as he locates the light switch to the room, and flips it upward as to turn on the switch.
This elucidates the dim & eerie atmosphere, as shockingly, there in the corner of the coarse Dressing Room, sits a man on a small wooden stool. His head inclined in a downward manner, his body bent over leaning forwards, and his head; dripping with small droplets of sweat, as he sits there in a calm & collective, yet worried manner. He begins to speak with shaken emotions.]
V/O: Tonight, as I look past the previous weeks, as I do tonight as well. Tumult and Mayhem has occurred. Myself, caught in between the fireside rage of
Adrian Trust, and the possessed individual that is, Sean McKinnon.
[The man then straightens up his back & posture, and sits upright. This reveals a tattoo that was previously wrinkled from his bent over body, that is etched along the abdominal structure of this man. The tattoo reads, ' Jin Tao.'
This stirs more heavily born attention towards, the now identified, Jin Tao. He continues to sit there, in his full wrestling attire, just very calm but very shaken by tonight's previous occurrences.]
JT: I continue to undergo the battle that is the controversial triangle of
Myself, Sean McKinnon, and Adrian Trust. Though, before I continue into that, lets venture back into the beginning of this lovely evening, which I imagined to be a stepping stone for myself. But not only myself, my opponents as well.
The Tennessee Fiddler. Sean McKinnon . ' You two' were my main concern tonight. 'You two' where my focus this evening. ' You two' are who I studied & researched upon. I set forth a goal to never cheat or swindle to progress here in the GLCW. Though, this is all I have become a victim of. I set forth a goal to never Jeopardize a man 'nor woman that lies within my grasp of victory.
Though, not only has my health & career been Jeopardized, but my life, as well. [The fingers of Jin Tao, quickly brush through his jet black locks of hair, as a sudden look of concern appears on his face.]
JT: Jeopardized by men without heart. Men without feelings. Cold men, cold men who love to be hate. Men such as The Tennessee Fiddler. A man who I recently, within the evening, have shown the power of the Order of Light. A man who I have shown the true persona that is the "Spirit Warrior." I meant no harm to you Fiddler, only reverence, as do I every evening when I perform. I only meant to use what I possess as defense, not towards harsh or brutal actions, 'nor intentions. GLCW, you right now must know this, if you do not. Then you, do not know, 'Me.'
[A quick implicated snicker is heard from Tao.] JT: Now, the last two men that I have spoke about, have truly shown me only the opening chapters of an epic vendetta that involves, myself, as one of the victims. Adrian Trust & Sean McKinnon, you two quench two different possessions which only the uniqueness of yourselves, could ever desire.
Adrian Trust, you desire the fetor of my burning flesh among one of your heatseeking kindles. You desire the sight of my burning body among a toasty flame, on a cold winters evening. Am I wrong Adrian Trust? From what you have portrayed, it is highly doubtful. Though, the question lies, can you obtain this?
Sean McKinnon, you desire a man named Grey's, phone number. A most unusual desire, but again, only you desire such an original quench, in life. The day I shall decide to hand to you the phone number of such a treasury to you, will then be the day that peace is restored to us, and the GLCW Promotion. McKinnon,
I though, do not obtain such a phone number, but I believe you still feel a need to fight me. To bring me to the mat and humiliate me before my home in
Japan, before my step father and before the sacred Taoist that live among my land. But again, Sean McKinnon. Can you obtain this?
[He now has a sudden questioning like look, upon his face. ] JT: But, there is another. Yes, another man that I have my heaviest of battles
& combats with. A man that I compete with, everyday, when arise at dawn. And a man I compete with, everyday, when I sleep at dusk. Yes, another man. The man who is. .. "Myself ."
The man that I battle continuously, is myself. My mind, my body, and my soul. I urge to overpower myself and I am trying to achieve, to much to fast. I must not fail my father and I must not dishonor the name of the "Taoist." I must portray the warrior inside of me. The "Spirit Warrior," that is.
. And who is to come. For the future, and the future that is to come. And for the past, and the past that is set behind us. This is where I shall renew my venture towards the GLCW, and here it begins.
[He then quickly stands on his feet, with high pride and more dignity then he looked as if he came with.]
JT: Till then, you may try to contain me. But to contain, is like trying to kill me. Trying to kill me is like trying to penetrate an unpenetrable force.
Let it be known. For I shall be born. Learn to respect. Learn to forgive. 'Tis' the only way, I was taught to live. [He, with these words sits back down upon the wooden stool, without the slouch this time. The Camera Shot pans towards the exit door of the Dressing Room, as suddenly the camera man approaches his hand towards the Room's light switch. A sudden pause and * SWITCH *. Fade To Black.]
