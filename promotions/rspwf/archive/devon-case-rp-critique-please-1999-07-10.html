Devon Case RP [Critique Please]

10 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Pitch black is the view as nothing can be seen nor heard. Almost as though waiting for the previews at a movie theatre the suspense builds up. Suddenly, without warning or remorse, a voice is heard cutting through the dark background.]
[VO] - Two plains of human intelligence envelope the minds of not only the young at heart but also the old. These two plains conceal and, at times, reveal the truths and non- realities of human existance as we know it on a one on one level. One of them, REALITY. Reality, where truth and justice and the common goal of life can be found. Where the true "real" men of their respective professions shine while others wither in their shadows. The other, simply put, NON-REALITY. Non-reality is where the scum of the
Earth reside, reassuring themselves they are so much better than there truth entrusts.
[A flicker of light appears as quickly as it disappears, to only appear once more, constant this time, revealing it must be a small lighter of some sort. The lights brightens a hair to give a feel for the room and to show an outline of a man who holds the lighter up while seeming to light a cigarette.]
[From the brightness and shadows of the room all that can be made out is known. The room no more than ten feet by ten feet, square in shape. The man stands motionless bar the frequent drag of the cigarette ... once ... twice ...
thirdly ... then steps up closer to the camera.] [His face comes closer to view ... closer ... still unknown, short hair, dark in color is made out ... just a bit closer. A few short steps away and two drags later, the focus gets better, revealing the man wearing all black, jeans and shirt alike, is none other than Devon Case. He takes a long, deep drag of the cigarette, ashing it on the floor before speaking.]
DC - Christian Madison entrusts his way of life, his truth conception, to the non-reality side of intelligence and of existance alike. Madison is one who believes in taking without giving, of course, I guess when your mother whores you around, that conception becomes clear, Christian.
[Dev takes a log drag of him cigarette before blowing the smoke through the camera lens, he continues to speak.]
DC - IWE, I called this place my home away from home, but what so abruptly happened was I felt mistreatment of myself surpassed the good I was doing for the company at the time. Madison, let me let you in on something you already know, we know who tried to weasel his way into me and
Tucker's feud, we know who called me at home constantly wanting to get noticed through the use of me and Andrew, we know. So don't you go getting on some high horse, don't you go getting on the cheap heat bandwagon, 'cause I'm the type of bastard that brings you right back down to reality.
[A bit worked up all of a sudden, Devon steps back and looks down, holding his head in a cocky, sarcadtic slinece fashion. He spits upon the ground then looks up.]
DC - To continue with IWE, I have sat down with the head offials, I have sat down with the president, I HAVE WORKED
THINGS OUT! So, this time I have a chance, this time I have the ability to do what everybody in the world of wrestling is afraid of, become the top of the sport.
Enough said. Now, Madison, you wanna relive the past, you wanna talk International Championship Wrestling do ya?
Lets. I wouldn't suck off Pillow and I wouldn't kiss his or Bevins ass' either and without that, you don't get the respect and push you deserve. Obviously, with your talent so shady and your mic skills so piss poor perhaps you did
QUITE MUCH behind the scenes. End of the f*cking story.
DC - Now, this weekend, DC makes his in-ring debut once more, I enter that ring alone but not against only one man but a collaboration of few. The Trixxster, a man I have only heard in legends, poor the legends be but legends enough. You call me Devon Chase, well, I gotta question to ask ya buddy, "WHAT THE F*CK IS THAT! Sounds like a bad
America's Funniest Home Videos joke. Call up Bob Sagat, I think you two would do well together.
[Devon starts to chuckle a bit at the mere words he has spoken.] DC - The next contestant, a man known to be a Xena the
Princess Warrior stand-in, as well as wanted in 13 states nationwide for pedophilia, come on out, ARIES! Back to reality Aries. One, you are no God of War, if you were why the hell would you be in professional wrestling? Exactly.
Two, you have no skills, mic nor wrestling, therefore, I shall waste just this last breathe on you ....
BWAHAHAHAHAHA! DC - Allan James, honestly I have no f*cking idea who you are or where you came from. But from your name, I can reckon this. You sound as though a Family Ties reject, perhaps somebody who marched in last weeks San Francisco
Gay Pride Parade. Perhaps? I'll surely be watching my back for you this Saturday, so you may not do any "illegal"
tactics from behind, but fear not, I heard Christian Madison loves that sort of stuff. [Devon looks down once more before looking up, cocky smile glaring wide, near to bust out with laughter.]
DC - There is nothing like a little "The World's Gonna End" flare to get people riled up. I mean, isn't that what you are trying to portray Doomsday? That your the badass everyone should fear? Well, let's get back to reality, such as I have before. Doomsday you are nothing to fear or cry over, in fact, you are nothing to even sweat over. If
I'd ever thought you had one chance in a f*ck that you could pull this match off I'd acknowledge you, but till then ...
[DC looks revitalized.] DC - Then there is the odds on favorite, so to say, in
Steve Wallace. Mr. Wallace, we have never crossed paths, in fact, I think this may be the first time we've graced each other in the same promotion. So here's my rundown, take notes. I'm supreme, I am the not the leader of the next generation, I AM THE NEXT GENERATION! Steve, you are no champ, you are no man, and you definitely are no Devon
Case, so move over, the odds on favorite has spoken!
