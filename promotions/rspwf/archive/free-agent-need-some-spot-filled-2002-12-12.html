Nice apartment door staring us in the face. The dead bolt is locked, and the door is white. Years of abuse have left their mark on the door. The floor is oak, creating a nice illusion of decent flooring. To the right is another room, and to the left, a coat rack with nothing on it. [
[Voices. In the hall. Two of them.] Voice #1: [It speaks with such a light hearted voice, one without anger and depression. A happy man, probably in his forties. Doesn't seem to be too... cool. The nice, loserish type.] This is apartment
6D. One bedroom, one bath. Small kitchen, and living room.
Voice #2: [Deep voice, harsh sounding. The politeness seems forced.] Sounds... touching. How much a month? [The door jiggles. The two men are coming in.] Voice #1: 400 dollars, utilities included. [The opens, and the two men are revealed. The one on the left is holding the key, and seems to be the landlord. The overalls give it away. With an faded Cleveland Brown's hat, he seems quite normal.
The man next to him, though, is anything but normal.] [He's tall and lanky, too skinny for his own good. Shoulder length brown hair, and with the black trench coat, gloves, and slacks... He fits the image of your typical metalhead. He walks into the room. He ducks his head into the room to the right, and walks down the hall a ways, off camera. The other man just stands there, jingling the keys softly.]
Landlord: Well, take it or leave it. I can't afford to argue the rent. [The customer comes back on screen, a buck toothed smile. He takes off his trench coat, revealing a shirt with Bathory's logo, a goat's head, on it. The landlord takes a step back, and seems to be edgy.
The customer just smiles, a happy gleam in his eyes.] ["Scared, old man? Wondering what beast you've let into your lair? Foolish man. Foolish old man."] Customer: $400 is perfect. I'll stay the night. Thank you, good sir. Now good day. Landlord: [A confused looks clings to his face. A look of shock, of fear.] What about furniture? These rooms are all empty.
["Why must you ask questions, old man?"] Customer: Like I said, I'm new to the area. Brand new. I came with a suitcase, which is now sitting outside your office. That's it.
["Satisfied now, old man?"] Landlord: I'll get my boy to run it up later. I'm down on the first floor, apartment 1A, if you need anything. Good bye.
["Bye bye. And thanks for giving me an unstable mind to play with. Your boy is going to be fun, and I'm sure you'll find me to be a great influence over him."]
[The landlord shuts the door behind him, he mutters something as he walks away. The odd one remains, looking around at his new purchased, completely empty apartment.]
["And, I'm off to a great start. Wonder when that boy is dropping by."] [The man walks off camera, and we fade away.] =========
