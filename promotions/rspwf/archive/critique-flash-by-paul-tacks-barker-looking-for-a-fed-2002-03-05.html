[Critique] Flash by Paul 'Tacks' Barker (Looking for a fed)

5 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yea, I'm looking for a fed... This is the character I'm gonna use, and... he's pretty simple to understand. Anyhow, this a example of my work... If you like, talk to me via AIM, or email me. My AIM is
[detail withheld], and my email addy is
[detail withheld] . Yea.... ============= [Fade up...] [There he is -- Paul "Tacks" Barker. Decked out in his Black Sabbath cloth, Paul stands in front of the standard, inexpensive, Eastern Wrestling Alliance background. His jeans are cut off at the shins, and his boots are untied. His socks don't match, and his hair is uncombed, knotted, and a mat on top of his head. Or least that's what comes to mind from looking at him. Not many people can wear a backwards Black
Sabbath ball cap for days, and remain hat-hair free.] [So, today is just like any other day for Paul. He's feeling good, after a nice breakfast of three week old pizza. He's standing tall, and ready to speak his mind. Finally... That leaky faucet in Kade's basement was about to drive me mad.]
PB: In life, eva' day it seems some people choose to feel superior ova' someone. Eva' day someone tries to push themselves onto people, whisperin' in everyone's ear just how bad 'da otha' guy gots to be. Hell, sometimes that little cocky bitch _might_ be right...
But 'dat 'dere Hunta' Sawya' fellow... Well, he's throwing stones in a glass house, if ya catch me drift.
[Paul shifts his weight, taking his time with his thoughts.] Ya see, Hunta' is 'da type of fellow that rather enjoys pointin' out eva'one else's flaws, just so he can sit back and, at least for the moment, stand on top of 'da world. But 'da sad fact is 'dis man's got more problems than a heroin addict.
[Insert a pause here... Every good wrestler pauses.] Hunta' is just filled with hate. There is no love in 'dat 'dere man's body. No love for the life he sadly wastes away. No love for anythang. No wonda' why that little mutha'fucka' is willin' 'ta put his body on the line -- he's got nothing to lose.
[He relaxes a bit, almost smiling at the idea of wrestling Hunta' Sawya'.] Maybe that is why I'm gonna enjoy 'dis match Hunta'. Ya gonna be standin' toe 'ta toe with me, Paul "Tacks" Barker, and 'cha ain't gonna be backin' down like most. Ya gonna be fightin' 'till 'deres nuttin' left to fight with, and that means I'm in for a long haul.
[Paul smiles, but looks in straight into the camera. The happy-go-lucky smile has faded, and is replaced by a face that is beyond description.] You've got me attention Hunta', and ya have ya chance at stardom in here in 'da EWA. Let's just hope ya don't screw it up, and make me seriously hurt you.
[You expect a laugh from this fun lovin' guy, but none comes. Umph, I guess he's serious. [Fade out.]
