[Critique Free Agent] "Cold Hearted" James Morgan

21 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sup fellas... Just looking for a decent e-mail Fed. Don't care if it's the "best damn Fed on the net" or if it's the "next best thing." Just something that has been opened for a fair amount of time with a steady Fedhead. Just hit me up with an e-mail if anyone is interested in the James Morgan character.
RP is below. - Mike E. ================= [Fade in] [The view slowly fades into a small apartment. The refridgerator door is open, as the interior light pierces through the spacing between the door and the console. A draw is quickly shut along with the door, shutting the light off.]
*Click* [A second light is turning on, this time from a wall switch. The kitchen of the apartment is fairly small with only the aforementioed fridge, a sink and a chair taking up space. The man sits down on the chair, clutching a red apple in his left hand. This man is "Cold Hearted" James Morgan,
Independent hopeful for the GWC/MPW Invitational Cup. Morgan is wearing a pair of torn dark blue jeans and a white T-shirt. He takes a big bite out of the apple and then holds it away from him, eyeing it intently.]
"COLD HEARTED" JAMES MORGAN: For some odd reason, ... I draw a comparison between this apple and Chris "The American Rage" Page. Both are fairly respectable based on the outside. Chris Page seems like a fair competitor.
He embodies everything presumed "good" for Pro-Wrestling. He is in good shape, has an intimidating presence - to some and he appears athletic. Much like this Apple. Bright red skin, strong outside and it appears to be very tasty.
[James takes a second bite, this time getting to the core, exposing the seeds. His eyes squint as he places the apple down and spits the chunk into his hand.]
Fortunately, I don't judge books by their cover. Because by the same token that has Chris Page and this Apple looking good on the outside, they both have poor insides. Both tend to leave a bad taste in your both and when you get to the core of them, you realize there really isn't anything special about it.
[James re-adjusts himself in the chair, bring his right leg up underneath his left leg.] You see Christopher, ... amongst your rambling, your incoherent babbling and yes, your deviation from the actual matter at hand - one thing became painfully obvious. You, my friend, are simply out of your league. I am far from conceited. I am the biggest advocate of 'any man can be beaten, any time.' I'm an under dog. I know I am. I thank the Suits of this
Tournament for giving me, a no-name unknown a chance to shine. And that is again where we differ, Chris.
[James sighes.] You have placed yourself upon a pedestal, Christopher. But everyone can see you're in too deep. You have put yourself up to the very top and because of that, you are going to fall very long... and very hard. I may not be the man to make you fall. And the man you face after me might not be either.
But sometime soon -- a man will come along and make you fall. And I hope I am there to see you fall from grace. What a smile that would bring to my face.
[Slight pause.] You have laid down the foundation of this match quite easily, Christopher. You are the wrecking ball. You are returning to Pro-Wrestling, better than ever and nothing will stop you. I am returning to a Pro-Wrestling ring but clearly, no match for you. I am the under dog. I am the bottom of the barrel. In your eyes, I shouldn't even be fighting you. Well Christopher -
I am and because of that, you need to prepare for me. No one else. I am who you will see across the ring. Not your agent. Not your accountant.
Not your wife. [Morgan's eyes light up.] Me. James Morgan. You need to be ready to fight me. I have absolutely nothing to lose and because of that Christopher, I will do anything to win. You, though, have all but guaranteed victory. If I lose, it will be expected. If you lose?
Well, ... well then everyone would see you for who you truly are, Christopher. [Morgan picks up the apple and walks over to the garbage can, holding the fruit above the can.]
That one apple everyone in the store touches... but never buys. [Fade to black.]
