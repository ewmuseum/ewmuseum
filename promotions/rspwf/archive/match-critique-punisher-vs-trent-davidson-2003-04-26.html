[MATCH CRITIQUE] Punisher Vs Trent Davidson

26 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was recently entered in Setzer's American Wrestling Council, but he ended up closing the fed before the first card. I had finished a doozy
of an opening match with "Punisher" Dan Stein Vs Trent Davidson and decided it would be a shame if such a match would go to waste, so now it's here. So feel free to read it and critique it to your hearts content. If the handlers of the participants are reading this, give me feedback!
Stuff to know- MKC: Michael K. Cook, play by play announcer VM: "Venemous" Vance McCabe, color commentator Total time it took me to write this thing: two days. Finished it before Trent managed to give me his match strat.
________________________________________________________________________________ MKC: Alright folks, coming up next: Trent Davidson versus "The Punisher" Dan Stein! Vanny Mac, your thoughts? VM: My first thought is that I don't ever want you calling me Vanny
Mac ever again! Secondly, it'll interest you to know that I did my homework on the two promising AWC superstars; I'm a journalistic reporter, don't ya know?
MKC: Yes, Bobby Heenan just called, he wants his material back. VM: Shut your gob, I'll steal any goddamn material I WANT! It might interest you to know that Dan Stein is coming back from a difficult fight over the Wolverine over who the hardest badass is, while Trent is better known as the frontman to the wrestling stable Nine Inch
Nails!
MKC: You know jack squat about those two, and you just made anything up, right? VM: EXACTLY! MKC: We'll get to see what those two can do very soon, as they're coming down the aisle as we speak!
MC: The following match is scheduled for one fall: Introducing first...hailing from New Orleans, Louisiana...standing six feet three inches tall and weighing two hundred and sixty nine pounds, here is...
TRENT DAVIDSON! [Static fills the airwaves for a moment until "Whatever" by Godsmack roars to life. The crowd jumps to it's feet in anticipation of the Bayou
Badass
and are satisfied in moments as Trent Davidson walks out from behind the back. He stands still for a moment, methodically cracking his knuckles as he looks at the audience, a faint smirk on his face.]
VM: See? Gobsmack, Nine Inch Nails, same thing, really! MKC: Would you please shut up! #And I wonder day to day I don't like you anyway. I don't need your shit today, You're pathetic in your own way. I feel for you... Better fuckin go away I will be here... Better fuckin go away! I'm doing the best I ever did I'm doing the best that I can I'm doing the best I ever did...# [Trent has short black hair that is too short to comb, with a black mustache and grey eyes. He is dressed in blue jean shorts which go to his knees. Out from under them can be seen the bottoms of a pair of black kneepads. His boots are black with white laces, his wrists heavily wrapped in white tape. Trent wears a cutoff black t-shirt as well, with a sleeveless blue flannel shirt overtop. He walks methodically to the ring, ignoring the fans even as they reach to slap hands or even touch him.]
#I don't need to fantasize. You are my pet all the time. I don't mind if you go blind. You get what you get until you're through with mine! I feel for you... Better fuckin go away I will be here... You better go away! I'm doing the best I ever did I'm doing the best that I can I'm doing the best I ever did...# [Trent rolls into the ring and gets right back to his feet, taking off the flannel shirt and throwing it over the ropes. He stretches out in his corner, loosening up as Godsmack dies out.]
MC: And his opponent, hailing from Detroit Michigan… accompanied by
Domino, he stands at six foot seven and weighs two hundred and eighty-five pounds: "THE PUNISHER" DAN STEIN!
(The arena goes black. "More Human Than Human" by White Zombie begins to play over the sound system. Overhead, on the screen, three symbols flash slowly in progression- Alpha, then Omega, then the
Punisher skull. The graphic switches to a loop, with a three dimensional skull rotating and the graphic "Dan Stein" alternating it, as well as some cut scenes from various wrestling moves and camera shots. The lights slowly fade on as Dan Stein walks out with his manager Domino flanking him.
Stein slowly raises the Peacemaker to acknowledge the crowd, then makes his way to the ring area.)
MKC: He's a whopping six foot seven and a few shades under three hundred pounds. He's a hoss I tell you, a goddamn HOSS!
VM: Now who's stealing material here? MKC: As "The Punisher" makes his way to the ring, I can't help but wonder about his equalizer, that powerful club he likes to call the
Peacemaker… You know as well as I do how such a dangerous weapon can turn the tide in a match, hell- a weapon such as that could easily end a career!
VM: And don't forget about Dan's lady: Domino! She's as much of an equalizer as anything else, and between that and that stick of his,
Trent Davidson has a lot to worry about! But most probably, Dan might just simply use the stick on the lady, get arrested for spousal abuse and emerge as the number 1 good guy of this wrestling federation!
MKC: Will you stop it! Ref has finished checking up on both guys, and we're just about to start!
# DING - DING - DING # [Huge pop for the wild brawl!] MKC: HOT DAMN! Both men start off swinging and I'm talking about some heavy handed swings here! Trent Davidson with powerful fists across
Dan Stein's face, Dan comes back just as hard! BIIIIG right hands from the Punisher as he just soaks up the damage and offers up some more!
VM: It's a slobberknocker, a pier-six brawl, a donnybrook, a CHI-NESE FIRE DRILL! MKC: It's a brawl is what that is! Trent with an upwards chop to the throat, Dan Stein spinning with the blow and nailing a vicious backfist, causing a knockdown!
VM: But not a knockout! WOOWEE, Trent just bounced back and drove a right fist straight into his mush!
[Nice pop for the smaller man with the big punch that manages to drop the bigger man to a knee!]
MKC: Not quite enough to drop Punisher down! And as the brawl continues, the height and weight advantage of The Punisher is slowly taking it's toll on Trent Davidson. You can tell that Dan Stein is landing more blows, connecting with more punches; Trent's gonna have trouble going toe to toe with the big man from Detroit!
VM: I'll say! Trent just missed with that wild haymaker, and Dan is letting him know it with a HARD forearm shiver across the face! Simply nasty!
MKC: THE REF WILL HAVE NO PART OF THIS! Dan Stein whips his foe into the ropes, catches Davidson with a tilt-a-whirl slam! He picks up
Trent Davidson by the neck and just TOSSES him into the corner!
Powerful ham-sized fists striking the face of the pit fighter from the
N'Awleans! VM: Jesus H. Crimeny! Dan Stein is simply punishing Trent Davidson with those big fists! MKC: Why yes, that might be why they're calling him "The Punisher". VM: No, really? MKC: Really! And now The Punisher is choking Trent Davidson in the corner with that big size 36EEE boot! Dan Stein with an impressive display of superiority here!
VM: How come you don't know anything about the history of a wrestler, but you know his goddamn shoe size?
MKC: We can see Domino at ringside, cheering her man on despite his use of clearly illegal manoeuvres! Ref, however, finally getting some semblance of a backbone and decides to get himself involved, pushing for a clean break between the two brawlers!
[Serious boos from the crowd at this attempt to prevent even more brutality from the wrestlers!]
VM: Pity really. Because if Dan would've kept on choking Trent Davidson, we would end up with a fight between The Punisher and a giant Smurf. And that's just cool.
# OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOW! # MKC: What's this? Trent Davidson catches his opponent unaware with a face rake behind the refs back! Trent Davidson, building some kind of momentum here with an upwards chop to the throat! Jawbreaker! Another!
A third! Knee to the gut, and Davidson attempts for the piledriver… he's lifting that big man up… CONNECTS! Ouchies!
VM: That's right! He doesn't look like it, but Trent has major fighting experience! He's something of an underground fighting sensation in the seedier bars and drinking establishments of the
Louisiana region, don't ya know.
MKC: Why Vance, you impress me! You actually did ended up checking on our wrestler's backgrounds!
VM: You mean I was right? Hell, I was just making stuff up as we went along! Who knew I'd be right?
MKC: Well I certainly should've known better. Brutal piledriver from
Trent as he shows off what he thinks of this Illinois crowd.
# BOOOOOOOOO! # MKC: And not necessarily in any positive fashion. VM: Yeah, well I certainly can't blame him. I wouldn't want to be caught dead in Illinois either… unless I'd get paid for it. Like he is. Lucky bastard.
MKC: SCHOOL BOY BY DAN STEIN! A COVER! 1! 2! # AWWWWWWWWW... # MKC: Trent Davidson almost got too cocky out there! VM: Yeah, well "almost" only counts in horseshoe and hand grenades, which means that... Trent gets to wrestle some more. Lord, my metaphors sucks.
MKC: Indeed they do, but it's an incensed Trent Davidson that comes back with a boot and an Ace Crusher on Dan Stein! The Punisher leaves staggered by the manoeuvre and stumbles into a twisting belly-to-belly suplex! Trent Davidson, trying to make a statement here tonight!
VM: Specifically, "don't try those fancy-shmancy moves on me! I'm a pit fighter, dag yo!" MKC: I wouldn't call a school boy fancy, nor would I ever get caught alive saying "dag yo". Trent Davidson stomping the taste out of Dan
Stein! Connects with a diving headbutt! Now he's climbing the turnbuckle!
VM: Yeah, who needs this flippy-flippy cruiser stuff when you can get
REAL men to splash suckas from above!
MKC: It's looking like a top rope moonsault from here, as Domino tries to catch the attention from the New Orleans-born scrapper!
VM: And really, why? There's no telling what Trent Davidson would do to the woman if she actually succeeds in getting his attention, and I forgot to bring my camera today for all the ensuing ass-ramming goodness, so of course it would suck.
MKC: And Trent just waving off Domino away, giving her the stiff arm and proceeds to execute a picture-perfect moonsault.
VM: Of course, if Trent had paid more attention to Dan Stein rather then Domino, he might've even noticed that The Punisher rolled away to nowhere near the scheduled landing spot. Of course, it's the thought that ultimately counts.
# THUD! # VM: Or not. MKC: Nobody home! Dan Stein passes to the offensive! Sidewalk Slam! Trent tries to make it to his feet, but Dan hooks him in a front headlock. VM: No dice, Mickey K Cookie! Trent Davidson is used to fighting injured against opponents much larger then him! He's punching The
Punisher in the gut, driving him straight into the turnbuckle!
MKC: Right you are, Vanny Mac! And OOOH, once more into the corner goes "Punisher" Dan Stein! And again! Trent is repeatedly driving
Stein into the corner, now he's going to try to suplex himself out of the headlock-
[Crowd pops!] MKC: DENIED! As Dan Stein executes a big swinging neckbreaker from the front headlock! Covers!
1! 2! 2.9999… KICK OUT! MKC: Not quite there yet! VM: But not so far off! Dan Stein has this match under control! He bounces off the ropes and hits his opponent with a reverse bulldog!
Keeps it hooked for the grounded inverted side headlock! MKC: Hey! Leave the play by play for us professionals! But it's very true that Trent Davidson won't stay up very long if he stays on the receiving end of such a battery of offence! And notice if you will, that The Punisher is raising himself to one knee, still keeping the inverted side headlock hooked up but now using his raised knee to grind the back of Trent Davidson's neck, forcing the head back with his free forearm!
VM: And get this! I was just informed by the boys in the back to one of Dan Stein's finisher, it's a reverse neckbreaker that he calls the
Twillight Hammer!
MKC: Like I said before: this does not look good for Trent Davidson! Dan Stein is now standing on both feet, trying to add any extra pressure on Trent's neck as possible! Wait! Hold the phones!
VM: But I was just about to call my momma! MKC: Trent managed to reach up and grab Dan from underneath the chin, whipping him forward into some sort of reverse snapmare!
VM: And that's one way to escape the hold! MKC: Trent Davidson, taking a page out of Dan Stein, keeps the resulting front headlock applied so as to execute a brutal brainbuster!
VM: Although it's clear to see that Trent is favoring his neck at this point! MKC: Yes he is, although Trent's resiliency is shining through at this point! He whips his larger opponent into the ropes, goes for the high mafia kick!
VM: A bit too high, Dan simply ducked under it! # TWACK! # MKC: But he couldn't avoid Trent's lunging clothesline on the return! That one was just plain nasty! Dan was knocked head over heels as
Trent Davidson crawls over to his opponent's prone body and executes the pinfall!
1! 2! 3... [Crowd pop!] Not 3! SHOULDER UP! MKC: Could've been the finish, just wasn't in the cards! VM: But Dan's not gonna stay in this fight for long. So to Domino, I say: don't cry. I'll be waiting in my hotel room to console you, console you over your good friends loss. Come to me, Domino.
MKC: Hold that thought! VM: For how long? MKC: For as long as you can breath, because Dan Stein is using some dirty tricks of his own to regain the advantage! Mainly, a strange
Chinese clutch in which he wraps two fingers and his thumb around the windpipe of Trent Davidson!
VM: You know what Mick? I don't think I'm gonna hold that thought for that long a time! MKC: I thought as much! Ref warning The Punisher over the illegal hold, so he lets it drop!
VM: Correction: he lets Trent Davidson drop! MKC: Well I was gonna say that but you beat me to it. Vicious head slam from a standing position, and now Dan is straddling his opponent and crushing his neck down with a full nelson!
VM: And that's 285 pound of crush coming your way! MKC: The referee is checking to see if Trent submits, but Dan won't give him the chance, lifting his foe up and slamming him down, all while keeping the full nelson hold applied!
VM: Correction, that's 285 pounds of angry crush coming your way! MKC: Once more, Dan Stein lifts his opponent up, but now he's hooking his reverse neckbreaker!
[Sympathy pop!] MKC: But not if Trent Davidson can help it! VM: That was a VICIOUS mule kick in between the legs! BEAUTIFUL! MKC: Trent Davidson spins The Punisher around, and connects with a sharp right hand! VM: CRIKEES! He nearly cracked his jaw with that one! MKC: DAN STEIN DROPS LIKE A TON OF BRICKS! I'm just surprised by the power in Trent's right hand! That was INCREDIBLE!
VM: Well don't miss the cover! MKC: Dan Stein bleeding from the mouth! Trent hooks the leg! 1! 2! 2.999… SHOULDER UP! MKC: LITERALLY, A LAST SECOND SAVE! VM: No, a last second save would be a kickout at 2. This was a much closer to last second save. As in, a last, fraction to a second change... but how close to zero can a fraction get without being equivalent to zero?
MKC: And as Vanny waxes semantics, Trent keeps favoring his neck, forcing his opponent up and applying the double underhook. Trent lifts up The Punisher- TRENT DAVIDSON IS LIFTING UP THAT HUGE HOSS OF A
PUNISHER!!! TIGER DRIVER '91! DAN STEIN LANDS ON HIS HEAD! VM: He started off with a piledriver, moved up to a brainbuster, now we have the Tiger Driver- Man, the Tylenol corporation should be paying this guy a representatives fee!
MKC: TRENT COVERS! THIS COULD BE IT! 1! 2! 2.99999… NOT QUITE 3 BEFORE THE KICKOUT!!! MKC: Last nano-second save from Dan Stein as Domino is cheering her man at the top of her lungs! But it may be too little, too late! Trent
Davidson climbing to the top rope!
VM: With that neck? Didn't the man learn anything from his previous attempt at a top rope maneuver?
MKC: He learned that if at first you don't succeed, try try again! TOP
ROPE FAT ASS MOONSAULT! THAT'S 269 POUNDS COMING DOWN AT YA!
# SQUISHEES! # [Puroresu-like UWAAAAAAAH! reaction from the fans!] VM: Ahh yes, the moonsault: a study in paradoxes indeed. How can something so beautiful end up so messily?
MKC: YOU'LL BE ABLE TO ASK DAN AFTER THE MATCH, CUZ TRENT IS FINISHING THIS ONE! 1! 2! 2.9999999999999999! [Incredible pop from the crowd, and from one young girl in particular...] MKC: I CANNOT BELIEVE IT! FOR THE THIRD TIME IN A ROW, "PUNISHER" DAN STEIN MANAGES TO AVOID CERTAIN DEATH! VM: The good news is, if you managed to avoid death for three times in a row, then four times shouldn't be a problem either. Because look at what Trent is doing now...
MKC: A short arm clothesline... VM: Chained into a burning hammer-type maneuver. Which incidentally, is Trent Davidson's finishing maneuver.
MKC: Dear Lord, Trent forced Dan Stein to his feet and connected with that short arm clothesline, but he's not letting up on the wrist, lifting his foe up for the Torture Rack... Man oh man, Trent Davidson managed to lift "The Punisher" up on his shoulders...
VM: And in case we didn't pound the point into the ground yet, that's a LOT of Dan Stein to lift up!
MKC: A testament to the muscle strength of Trent Davidson to say the least! [The crowd looks on, worried as all get-fuck. And in the shocked silence, a lone woman makes a solitary prayer... ]
[Crowd gets on their feet to see this.] [The people gasp in unison.] [The neck does not hold up.] MKC: THE NECK DOES NOT HOLD UP! TRENT DAVIDSON'S BATTERED AND BRUISED
NECK DOESN'T HOLD UP TO THE PRESSURE! TRENT DAVIDSON CAN'T KEEP DAN
STEIN UP! VM: On the flip side, Trent didn't really drop Dan in a non-painful manner when he found he couldn't hold him up, so it's not like it's all over for Trent, now is it?
MKC: Trent Davidson's neck is really killing him, Dan Stein isn't in that much of a better condition after that brutal beating from Trent!
VM: It's a battle over whom will recover the fastest to win! MKC: Indeed it is! And once again, it's the pit fighter, the legendary underground brawler Trent Davidson that shows off his resiliency to the crowd! A short arm clothesline-
VM: He's going for his Burning Hammer combo again? MKC: Dan Stein has that move scouted! He ducks under the arm- VM: Trent Davidson ain't through with The Punisher yet! MKC: Trent not letting go of the wrist, going for another pass with that short arm clothesline!
VM: No dice! MKC: And Dan Stein manages to grab the offending arm, passing it between Trent's legs! Dan is hooking his opponent up for a pumphandle slam!
VM: A Pumphandle SITOUT slam! The Hammer of Justice! MKC: Another finisher??? [As the crowd looks on in shock, Dan Stein manages to execute a sit down pump handle slam. Trent Davidson looks dead.]
MKC: THE HAMMER OF JUSTICE CONNECTS, BUT IS THAT ENOUGH TO FINISH THIS MATCH??? 1! 2! 2.9999999999… 3! MKC: HAMMER OF JUSTICE WINS IT! VM: I CAN'T BELEIVE IT! MC: The winner, by pinfall.... "THE PUNISHER", DAAAAAAN STEIN! MKC: UN-FRAGGLE-ROCKIN'-GLAUBLISH! That was a hard fought victory if I ever saw one! VM: Three words: Lucky Fuckin' Break. MKC: I'll have to disagree with you there, Dan Stein fought HARD for his victory! And here comes Domino to rejoice with her man! Oh boy, and Domino has the Peacemaker!
VM: Correction, Now Dan Stein has the Peacemaker! MKC: And Dan Stein talking trash all over his defeated opponent! Trent
Davidson soon gets up, but discovers discretion is the better part of valor.
VM: Not to mention that you should never argue with a man armed with a very big stick. MKC: That too. We'll get back to you right after this.
