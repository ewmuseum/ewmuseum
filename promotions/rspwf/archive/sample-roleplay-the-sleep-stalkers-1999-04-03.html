[SAMPLE ROLEPLAY] The Sleep Stalkers

3 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens and we can see that there is a press conferance taking place. The Sleep Stalkers, "Dream King" Biafra Tokado and "Mr.
Nightmare" Shinya Shuhiro are seated behind the podium while their interpreter, Hakoyami is standing to the side of them. A man in a tux walks up to the podium and taps on the mic.]
Speaker: Testing,1-2-3 [A loud screaching noise is heard from the tapping.] Tokado [Translated to English]: Idiot! Speaker: I would like to welcome you all here today for this very special meeting! We have two Japanese legends that have come all the way from the Land of the Rising Sun to face the best America has to offer!
I'm glad all you representatives could make it. [A man holds up his hand.] Speaker: What is your question? Man: Ah yes, Mr. Tokado and Mr. Shuhiro, what do you think of America so far? [Hakoyami translates it for them.] Tokado [Translated to English]: This America is trash, all I see on the streets is a bunch of fat out of shape idiots. America should be used to dump Japan's trash in.
[Hakoyami gets up to the podium to translate.] Hakoyami: Mr. Tokado says that America is a great place to live. He is very proud to be competing here for the next couple years. He also says that all the Americans he has seen are very healthy looking human beings.
Man: How do you think you will fair against your American opponents? You have completly different styles.
[Hakoyami translates it.] Shuhiro [Translated to English]: Hahahaha, American wrestlers are nothing in this sport. Americans treat wrestling like trash, the
Japanese study the art of wrestling. It's going to be such an easy task to destroy you American scum. You will pay for what you have done to our benifactor.
[Hakoyami gets up to the podium to translate it.] Hakoyami: Shinya says that it will be a great experience to wrestle against people of such a different style. He says that is what makes
American wrestling so great, the way that they treat the sport.
Tokado [Translated to English]: I am sick of looking at these fat slobs. I am leaving. [Tokado and Shuhiro both get up and begin to leave.] Hakoyami: They say that it has been a great honar to be here today but they must go to the gym now and train. I thank you all for coming.
[With those words, Hakoyami heads for the exit as the sceen fades to black.]
