Eric Emerson - Sample Flash - Please CRITIQUE

8 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up in a room filled with chairs, and other furniture made up of black leather. The lighting int eh room isn't that great, but it is good enough to notice the pictures hanging on the walls. As the camera goes to a close up shot of one of the bigger pictures a few young children can be seen sitting on a wooden swinging chair in front of an old white house in which the paint is starting to peel off. In the back ground a few sounds can be heard.
Such as footsteps and a few mumbling voices. After a few moments of the close up picture shoot the camera fades back to a full room shot, but with a difference. Instead of the room being empty besides the furniture there are now two men in the room. The two men are none other then "The Elite Sensation" Eric
Emerson and "The Devilish One" Derek Douglas.
Derek is wearing a pair of black jean shirts, a white t-shirt that says,
"Heaven Forgot About Me!". And his jet black hair is hanging down to his shoulders. Eric is wearing a pair of black leather pants, a white tank top shirt, and his short blond hair is combed forward with the front spiked straight up.
After a few short moments of silence Eric points at Derek and Derek stands to his feet and moves directly behind Eric who is sitting upon a leather chair.
Eric's face goes from being one of calmness to one of seriousness.] Eric Emerson: As I sit here in this leather filled room I wonder why I even bother with the Premiere Wrestling Organization. As I read over the latest report that was put out by Dan Stevens and Tommy Grayson it read that there are now _TWO_ divisions. First there is the Mid-Atlantic Division... The division that I _AM_ in? Then there is the "Elite" division. That being the division I'm
_NOT_ in? Now what the fuck is this?
[In the back ground you can see Derek Douglas shrugs his shoulders in amazement.] EE: After reading this bogus shit I can only think of three words. What... The... Fuck...? Don't you all think that a guy such as me who is known around the world as "The Elite Sensation" would be in the "Elite" division? what do you think Derek?
[Eric turns his body to look at Derek as Derek looks down at Eric face.] DD: To see is to know, and to know is to learn. I've learned that when you are named something you are known as something...
[Eric's face goes blank because he has no clue what Derek just said. After a few short seconds of silence Eric turns his body around in the direction of the camera and begins to speak once again.]
EE: That's exactly what I was thinking Derek. Anyway, I've been labeled as an "Elite" athlete so I damn well deserve to be in the "Elite" division. Which brings me to my main point. That being the bullshit that goes on up in the PWO offices. First off you've got Dan Stevens who doesn't give a shit about anyone except for Fang, Alex Extreme, and Victor V. Hill. A guy that has his head shoved so far up their ass that he doesn't see true talent even when it is directly in front of him. Then there is the even bigger pile of crap Tommy
Gayso... I mean Tommy Grayson. The guy that felt the need to job me out to a has-been like Eric Cooper. It's guys like them that make a guy like myself feel unappreciated...
[Eric's face turns sour as he rubs his chin.] EE: Besides the fact that I'm not in the _Division_ I should be in, I've remembered something that is almost as shitty. His name is "Sweetness" Stan
Daniels.
[Derek looks down to Eric once again] DD: When you know something you learn something. I think Stan needs to know something. [Eric rolls his eyes and slightly shakes his head as he begins to speak.] EE: Okay... Anyway, like I was about to say I'm being placed in an opening bout match up against Stan Daniels. Once again three words come to mind, and I think you all know what they are. Why would I be in an opening bout? Why would
I be in a match with a guy that has two first names?
[Eric pauses for a moment and looks at Derek.] EE: Sorry, Derek... DD: You know, so you in turn have learned... [Eric turns away from Derek.] EE: Yeah, I've learned something all right. Besides the fact that I have to set foot in the ring with a guy that no one has ever heard of is the fact that this cock suckin match is for the number one contendorship for the Mid-Atlantic title. In my opinion it's actually the "Elite" title of the PWO. Now what I would like to know is where the hell did Grayson find this waste of sperm? The only thing I can think of is he opened his slut of a girlfriends panty drawer and found a little left over nut and decided to throw him into the ring against me. Because this mother fuck doesn't stand a chance. Basically he's an ice cube getting thrown into hell...
DD: Hell is without water thus he will be eaten alive. [Eric stands to his feet and walks into a different room leaving Derek behind.] EE: Okay, now that he's gone I'm going to say this. Stan, you can try to stand in my way. You can give it your all. But your not going to get the job done. I'm going to Christmas Carnage for the "Elite" championship of the PWO.
I'm going to Christmas Carnage to _BEAT_ Eric Cooper... And Grayson, well you can try yourself. You can throw people in my way. Hell, you can try and make
Cooper job his title off to someone else before Carnage, but it won't happen.
Cooper is going to Carnage, and Cooper is going to go down in an "Elite" way... Take those words to heart bitches... [Camera fades to black.] [name withheld] Handler of Eric Emerson and Derek Douglas - [Retired]
