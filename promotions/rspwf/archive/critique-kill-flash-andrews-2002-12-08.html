[CRITIQUE/KILL] "Flash" Andrews

8 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE 1: Since nobody's put a "critique" post on here in awhile, I figured I would. This is the latest OWE roleplay for my newest character, "Flash" Andrews.
NOTE 2: I am hoping for an honest critique. I mean, I started the character as a gag with a buddy of mine... but it's actually got promise.
NOTE 3: This promo is especially funny if you follow the career of either Andrew "Flash" Tucker or Strictly Business.
Get to it. Nick [A thumping bass beat is heard, the sound of "Can't Deny It" by Fabolous. On screen is a white kid with blonde hair and Oakley sunglasses on, standing in front of a beat-up brick building. He stands with white tights with black lightning bolts on them. He looks into the camera.]
"Flash" Andrews: Maaaaaaannnn... what da FUCK has I gots myself into? ["Flash" shakes his head.] "Flash" Andrews: I say it 'gain. What da FUCK has I gots myself into? Everyone gots ta have a startin'
point, 'n I guess dis be mine. 'N one day I might laugh 'bout all dis shit. "Flash" Andrews: But right now, dis shit ain' funny. ["Flash" walks up the road towards a bunch of black guys playing dice.] "Flash" Andrews: I mean, look at who I gots ta face. Sheeeeeit. Carl Blair. "Your True Life Fannasy" 'n some shit. Da fuck kinda name dat be? Sounds like some kinda gay-rights rock band er some shit. True
Life Fannasy... "Flash" Andrews: Prolly your true life fannasy t' be all alone wit' a half-dressed, buff-ass brotha like me. But it ain' gonna play like dat. I'll whup yo ass fo' I letcha touch me like some faggot.
["Flash" stands above the men playing dice, who just go about their business like he's not there.]
"Flash" Andrews: I mean, jess aks Morlock n' Brown what I can do if ya don' believe me, Carl. But don'
think those bitches is gonna come help yo ass in our match, foo. Jess t' make sure y'all ain' got no plans t' sneak attack my ass, I'm bringin' some insurance.
[A scrawny white guy stands up from the crowd of black men playing dice. He has platinum blonde hair under his pimp-style hat. His pinstripe suit is one size too big on him. "Flash" pats him on the shoulder.]
"Flash" Andrews: Dis be Money Mike. My insurance policy. Mutha ain' dat big, but he be crazy. Yo, talk at da peoples, Money Mike.
Money Mike: My man "Flash" is gonna mess you guys up! He's going to take you out! And you better not try to screw with him, or I might just have to whine and cry about it!
"Flash" Andrews: Yo. Money Mike. Keepin' it real. Money Mike: That's right, homeboy! ["Flash" whispers in Mike's ear.] "Flash" Andrews: What'd I tell yo ass 'bout talkin' ghetto, white boy? Money Mike: Sorry. "Flash" Andrews: Go on, Money Mike. Money Mike: My boy "Flash" is the real deal! He's going to take it to you! And I'm going to make all the money off of it!
"Flash" Andrews: Dat be right. Cuz yo my investor. Money Mike: Word. ["Flash" smacks Mike behind the head.] "Flash" Andrews: Hard t' git good help dese days. ["Flash" steps in front of Mike and speaks.] "Flash" Andrews: See, dis ain' no game t' me. Morlock, Brown, 'n Fannasy Boy seem to be fuckin' 'round in their careers. But me? I takes dis real serious. I gots ta feed my sista, ya heard? 'N she's a big ole fat bitch. She cain't be fed off no last place paycheck. I gots t' make da big dough.
"Flash" Andrews: So all y'all tryin' t' take food out my sista's mouf. 'N I cain't stand fo' dat. I'm gonna mow yo punk asses down. 'N wit' Money Mike in my co-nah, makin' sure ain' nothin' fucked up happenin'
but not doin' anything particularly worthwhile, y'all don' stand a chance. [Money Mike steps in front.] Money Mike: Yeah! What he said! Don't try to ride his coattails like I do! ["Flash" smacks Mike in the back of the head and looks into the camera.] "Flash" Andrews: 'Cuz threats 'r fah th' fearful. "Flash" Andrews: I'm jess statin' th' facts.
