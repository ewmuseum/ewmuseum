Charles Scheffield of the IIWF (Obviously a Free Agent)

11 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings everyone, I am the handler of Charles Scheffield... ex-member of the IIWF. Of course even if I said I was a member of the IIWF, it should be quite obvious that I am a free agent at this time.
As Scheffield was voted #5 European Wrestler by the Melville Vernon Gable,
Jr. Memorial Awards when the IIWF wasn't even notified about the awards ceremony as a federation (though I'm sure many people in the IIWF had other sources as I did) I must say that quite a few of you must already be familiar with the character. Of course, Scheffield is a United States citizen... but
I guess that it was that he had a lot of European traits which got him such a high rating.
Nevertheless, he is a free agent and I'm sure that many federations will be jumping to sign him up. Now, I'm not coming out here because I haven't been invited to any federations. Lord knows that the IIWF list has been spammed quite unsparringly since it's demise. The thing is I'm not interested in joining a fed where I am handed a form letter. I want the people who are interested in signing me up to come out like men and write to me personally to invite me to the fed. I'd also like it to be a fed that is somewhat as notorious as the IIWF. And no... to answer the question already, I will not be bringing Scheffield to the EMWC.
If anyone is interested in signing me up to their federation, I'd be more than happy to see what you have to offer. I'm not going to bring Scheffield to any federations based soley on America Online... the reason being is any federation secluded to one server, or even people on other servers who tend to flock around a single server scene does not often bring about much competition. On top of it all, many times AOL feds require that you splash color all over flashes or else they don't get noticed... something I can do but I truly wish not to pervert the Scheffield character in such a way.
So anyone who has a slightly respectable fed which is pretty much on the
RSPWF/Internet scene, I'd be happy to join. I will admit that if there is a respectable AOL federation maybe I'll bring Scheffield there... but I'd rather have him in a federation where no single ISP takes any significant majority. I like Scheffield to intermingle with handlers from around the world. I'm wording it this way so people don't start complaining that I am biased against AOL feds... I'm not. I'm simply against the idea of secluding a federation to a single ISP because it limits the talent that can be brought in. Even if it is done unintentionally it still isn't a good thing. I want to make it clear that I won't join federations such as SPWO or CNWL either.
I guess I am being rather picky, but I need a good federation to continue
Scheffield's legacy. I'd like to have a decent push as well, but I won't force that on anyone because I know how much the members hate seeing a new guy come in and dominate. However, I'm sure Scheffield can live up to any expectations.
I believe I've typed enough now. If you need to see anything I have done with the character, just let me know and I'll send something over. If you have any questions, mail me at:
