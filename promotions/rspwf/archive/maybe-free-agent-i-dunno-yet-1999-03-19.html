- Kincade was raised in a wrestling family. He's been on the mats since he was able to walk. He has 2 brothers in the sport, that go by different names. One is Devon Case, and the other is the retired "The Goldenboy" Jeff Keenan. His father used to be the promoter for an independent federation in Colorado, called the CWP(Colorado Wrestling Promotion). When Robbie was old enough to work, he swept the floors in his fathers federation, hoping to one day wrestle in that ring. Well, the day came when he got a chance. He was now wliving his life-long dream, and wrestling. Well, his father ran into some financial problems, and had to shut the fed down. On the day of the last card, MSW Main
Recruiter and President, Adam Jarmusch was there to scout for his new federation. Robbie impressed himm, and he was quick to sign him. Ever since,
Robbie has been training hard under the care of his brothers, Case and Keenan.
