(RP, please critique.) William Craven.

31 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's one I just whipped up. Lemme define a few things, just to be clear. . . Boken = Wooden kendo stick. Ro-zay = The Rose, the guy that Craven is currently feuding with. Plus this is after the cameras stopped rolling at the last ACWA card. Begin flash. . .NOW! -- (Cut to the ACWA's backstage area used during the recent card held in the
Fairless Hills Lions club. The scene is that of a hallway, cluttered with the remnants of fast food and bits of lumber from unfinished projects in the club. A long hallway, closed to the public, is empty of all people but one. Brad Marsh waits nervously for something to happen. His eyes shift back and forth and he sweats buckets. He twitches as if his cheap suit is three sizes too small. Finally after a tense silence, he speaks to the cameraman.)
Marsh: Where the hell is he? I don't even want to do this. Cameraman: Hey, don't look at me, I just hold the camera! Marsh: (Under his breath.) Ya stupid. . .you get paid an awful lot for a glorified tripod. (Suddenly, some distance down the hall, a door swings violently open. From it steps a figure, clothed in military fatigue pants, no shirt and no shoes. He carries a heavy backpack and. . .a gold decorated weightlifting belt, the ACWA championship.
Marsh and the cameraman have fallen silent as this man draws close, bald head and scars becoming clear in the close proximity, he can only be Craven.)
Cameraman: (Whispering.) Pssst. . .say something. Marsh: I will, just. . .wait for him to get here. I thought you just held the damn camera.
(Craven, who has been looking only at the ground up 'till now, raises his glance to lock eyes with Marsh, and stops about six feet from the man, cocking his head curiously at this obstacle in his path.)
Marsh: (Nervously, almost jittering.) I, uh, mr. Craven, Bill, a few words. . .please? (Craven stares at the man with his cold, ice blue eyes, in silence. This lasts for a good long time, until Brad Marsh clears his throat, at which point Craven grins big, showing the gaps between his teeth.)
Craven: Brad. . .hello. . .I've missed you. . .where have you been? (Obviously taken aback by this friendly reception, Marsh seems emboldened.) Marsh: I. . .I've been on assignment, Bill, thank you for asking. I just want to ask you, and the people at home want to know, why did you attack the Rose and Stefan before your match?
Craven: . . .What an odd question to ask, Brad. Why wouldn't I try to get an advantage like that?
Marsh: Well, it seemed to everyone like you had made a change. The way you tenaciously stuck to Steve Starr, you were the good guy. . .
(Craven cuts the man off, suddenly a bit annoyed.) Craven: . . .I've never changed, Brad. I've always, ALWAYS been this way since coming to the ACWA. I always
WANTED to attack my opponents before the match, but you see, the Lovemuscle have been very cagey up 'till recently.
Now. . .now they've broken down a bit, you see, they aren't as. . .cohesive as normal. Ro-zay left an opening, I took it.
Marsh: But, you attacked Stefan, a MINOR, you bit his neck! Craven: Why, Brad, you sound almost. . .confrontational! You're not confronting me, are you_Brad?
(Marsh is suddenly afraid again. Whether it's the inflection on that last repetition of his name, or the fact that Craven is repeating his name over and over, and getting closer and closer to him is not clear.)
Marsh: No, no, why would I do that? I'm not a wrestler after-GAK! (Without a word, Craven has dropped his bag and grabbed the throat of Brad
Marsh. Looking into Marsh's eyes and pushing him back against the wall, Brad Marsh is trapped, alone. He reaches towards camera, but true to what he said before, the cameraman only holds the camera. He is an observer, an observer to a possible tragedy.)
Craven: You, Brad? No, no, no, it isn't about you at all. Do you see me? I_am the man that it all circulates around. I took out Starr, I made him just like me, I took the ACWA belt, and made it just like me, do you see this belt, Brad?
(Craven lifts the ACWA belt, which until now has not been focused upon by the camera. Now center-screen, the belt is shown to have been scratched, nicked, cut and dented. It seems to have been worked over with an ax or hammer, and is looking like it could fall apart at any time. Marsh, both hands on Craven's wrist, squirms feebly and Craven spikes the belt like a football, and begins to stroke Marshes' hair.)
Craven: Please quit struggling, Brad, it makes the air go quicker. You need air, you have air, don't waste it! You and I, we're going to have an interview_my_way. I'll tell you what, you don't have much time, so I'll be. . .as. . .quick. . .as. . .possible. . .
(Craven pauses after his slow speech, chuckling.) Craven: First, let's cover Ro-zay's problems with me. I beat him that one time, I bit his neck, I bit his boyfriends neck, and I took "his" title, "Single Danger". This, I suppose is supported by the general press, who claimed when I returned to the ACWA that the Rose was dominant, and asked if "Craven could take the Roses'
place." The one problem with all that is that even when I beat him, I never took his place, HE TOOK MINE! Me! I was the dominant one! I was beaten out of the league by six men, and he snuck in while I was gone!
Marsh: *CHOKE!* (The hand of Craven tightens on Marsh's throat.) Craven: I'm sorry, Brad, were you still getting some air? I meant to cut it off completely. My point is that I was_always the
Single Danger, I just didn't have the words. . .he had the words, I used them. I am the single danger, he is IN single danger, and you. . .well, you have very little time left. . .
(Craven strokes Brad's hair a few more times before applying both hands to his throat, and squeezing harder than ever.)
Craven: Don't think of this as an attack, Brad, in Detroit, this happens all the time. . . (Marsh is forced to the ground by his neck, kicking and clawing the entire time, trying to free himself, when a commotion is heard behind the camera. The camera wheels to see. . .a group of wrestlers.)
Grant: What in the hell!? Get off of him! (About ten men number among the group, Hank Grant, the Pain Brothers, Lotto
Ray, the Voodoo Daddy, and others. They look uncertain, looking around to see if Cravens teammates, the Unstable are around.)
Craven: Stay out of it, Hank, we already had our confrontation, remember? Brad and I are having an interview. Grant: Yeah, well, I don't see that stick of yours, and this time I ain't alone, so let him go now!
Craven: Heh, heh, hehehehehehhah!. . .make me, bleeder. (The group of ten is instantly on Craven, pulling him up and dislodging his hands from Marsh. Even though this is only a group of "jobbers", the ten quickly overwhelm the one, getting him down and stomping him, all the while Craven laughs. . .)
Craven: HAHAHHAH, MY INNERVIEW, I ,*GLAK!* (With that, Craven stops struggling, but continues to twitch. Brad Marsh still chokes on the ground, clutching his neck. One of the ten, El Loco Gato, checks on Marsh, who tries to wave him away.
Suddenly, without warning, two large figures burst into the scene! Maxx Madhouse and "Dark Angel" Ken Androma suddenly attack the ten, Maxx with Craven's boken, and Dark
Angel with the baseball bat he used on Riot earlier in the night.) Androma: HOME RUN! Maxx: HAI-YAH!!! (Craven is quickly on his feet, the championship belt in hand, the melee is short and ugly, each man singling out one of the ten and the other seven scatter. They continue the beating as Brad Marsh looks on in terror. In the end, Craven wipes his own blood on each man, drawing a single line diagonally across each man's face, emulating the largest scar on his own face.)
Craven: Maxx, Kenny, their like me, Starr's like me, Ro-zay's gonna be like me, soon, everybody's gonna be just_like_me!!!
Hahahhaahahaahhhahaahahahahaahahahaa!!! (The slow fade of the scene begins. Craven kneels on the concrete among the three fallen wrestlers, Hank Grant and the Pain brothers, Wayne and Shane. Maxx and DA flank him, staring at the camera, grinning wide. Craven laughs and Brad Marsh cringes. . .chaos reigns supreme. . .)
