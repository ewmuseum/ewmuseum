JS: A- a-Anything you say Heidi!!.. DGHL: That's a brave little man..(giggles) Now.. I See you've assembled some of my ~adoring~ fans for my little press conference..
JS: Press conference? DGHL: ( Looks at Styles like he oughtta know by now ) JS: OH .. Yeah.. OK.. sure.. I Did all of this..just for you.. ( shakes his head and looks down )
DGHL: Thats better..(smiles) Hello SAN ANTONIO!!... WHO DO YOU LOVE!!! ( Crowd of fans starts to chant Heidi!! Heidi !! Heidi !! ) DGHL: SEE.. Mimi.. honestly.. I thought that I was WAY too good to wrestle in an opening match.. see.. I only wrestle in MAIN Events.. Hyuri knew that..
and you're a good Kid Mimi.. but just not good enough to have a Debut against moi.. JS: Heidi.. I have to ask you.. what was on your mind when you came down to ringside after Hse Han was given the title?
