-[FREE AGENT]- CHRIS PAGE

24 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I am a free agent looking for a good federatiosn out there. Now I may not be the best roleplayer out there but I sure as hell try to do my best. So if any fed head needs a good roleplayer just send me mail at
[detail withheld] . Here is a sample roleplay of mine: {Shay Peters is in the ring and walks over to get a microphone. Shay Peters grabs a mic and clears his voice.}
[SHAY PETERS]: "Hello and welcome SCW fans. Let me introduce to you my guest at this time he is a man hailing from Fort Lauderdale, Florida weighing in at 260 pounds he has the chance to be the first ever BRASS KNUX Champion he is accompained by the lovely and gorgeous Paula he is "The American Rage" Chris
Page.
{The Star Spangle Banner begins to play. The entrance way is shooting out Red,
White & Blue fireworks. The entrance way is also covered by stain glass which has The American Flag embroider in it. Then you see two shadows walking towards
The American Flag. The glass shatters and two figures walk out. Both figures look very familiar. A women is walking down in front of 'The American Rage'
Chris Page carrying an American Flag. The women begins to wave The American
Flag in the air and Chris Page is just following her down to the ring. Chris
Page is wearing a Red, White & Blue shirt with blue fubu jeans, a silver necklace and has a distinctive star across his left eye. The women has on a white mini skirt with a red, white & blue bikini top. Chris Page reaches the ring and escorts the lady up the stairs and into the ring. Chris Page then takes the American Flag and places in it in ring post as four big fireworks go off and Shay Peters then approaches them.}
[PETERS]: "Hello Chris Page. Folks I am here with a man who for the first time in a long time of his life is a reletive unknown in the SCW but he has a chance to make history. He will go into the Continental Cup to face Derek Sweger. It will originate this year from The Reunion Areana in Dallas, Texas on March,
14th. Now, Chris Page, let go back to Sunday Night Slam were the announcement was made that you would fight Derek Sweger.
{Then footage is shown on the big screen as Paula, Chris Page & Shay Peters look on.} [KAY]:"And now....for our second announcement...here is Bob Lane..." [LANE]:"Thank you Richard. As many of you know..."Dynamite" Derek Sweger is our current Cruiserweight Champion. We have felt for some time that this title limits the amount of competitors it has by specifying a weight class.
We have therefore, decided to remove the title from competition and delcare it defunct!!!" <<HUGE amount of boos go throughout the crowd....>> [LANE]:"In its place, we are putting into effect a NEW Championship to be known as the "BRASS KNUX title. A title whcih will be defended under the NO
Disqualification, NO Countout, falls count anywhere rules!!!!"
<<The crowd POPS and goes into a FRENZY!!!!!>> [LANE]:"Dynamite - Derek Sweger will be give the belt to replace his old championship. He will defend the NEW Brass Knux title against "The American
Rage" Chris Page at the Pay-Per-View!!!!"
{The tape is then stopped and the camera focuses back into the ring.} [PETERS]: "Now Chris Page what were your first thoughts when you heard this announcement?" [PAGE]: "Well I was actually shocked to hear it. I mean I just got done fighting a battle with Derek Sweger the other night and then while I was in the back watching Slam on the monitor Kay and Lane make these huge announcements and they mention me in this match. I was in awe at first but then I said this match will decide one of two things. It will either make me or break me in the
SCW. This is my chance to shine in the SCW and I have a feeling that it will be a great night on March 14th. Win, lose or draw I will prove to Sweger one thing!"
[PETERS]: "And what may that one thing be?" [PAGE]: "That I am deservable of wearing any belt around my waist and this is my first title shot and it may as well be my last for all I know so I will be focused on this match 110% I guarantee that!"
[PETERS]: "Now speaking on your last match just a few days ago you had a match with Derek Sweger at a house show. Now what do you have to say about that?"
[PAGE]: "Well to tell you the truth I went into the match having one thing on my mind and that was revenge. You see not a lot of you know this but me and
Derek were good friends behind the scenes of the SCW. But then when I transformed into Chris Page he seemed to change and so do I. I mean he didn't talk to me. So one day I approached him and he just shrugged me off. Now that was a wrong thing to do. So I went into the SCW Staff and demanaded a match with him. As always the SCW Staff agreed with me and they signed a match. We fought for fiteen grueling minutes a few nights ago. I didn't care about a win
I just wanted to hurt somebody and I needed to make a name for myself so I tried but had no luck. I thought Sweger would be a walk in the ballpark but now
I see why he is the SCW Cruiserweight Champion."
[PETERS]: "Why is that?" [PAGE]: "Cause whatever move I did to him he came right back and did a move to me. I've got caught up on the outside and we both counted out. So I now at look our upcoming match and see that it has No Disqualification, No Countouts and its a Falls Count Anywhere match. Now thats my type of match!"
[PETERS]: " So now that you have both of you men have squared off against one another how are you going to go into this match?"
[PAGE]: "I am going into this match completely in control. I won't let anyone get to me. Now I know Derek Sweger has a surprise or two in this match but I haven't show you all my tricks yet Sweger so you will be surprised. Now Sweger
I know you have friends in the back and I could name a few but I rather not ruin it for your fans. So for those of you who are Sweger's friends if you want to come down to the ring and help your friend win go ahead and come down I am giving you an open invitation but you see that means that Sweger isn't man enough to face me alone. So Sweger you can bring all you want to bring down to the ring cause I will give you a run for that belt you have around your waist.
Sweger you may have friends but don't forget I also have friends and it doesn't take a rocket scientist to make friends in the SCW.
[PETERS]: "What do you see as a key in this match?" [PAGE]: "It all comes down to one thing. Who wants to go home the winner with a brand new belt around his waist and who will go home the loser? There are so many ways this match could end and how long it could be. It comes down to determination. Now I know the only way to get out of the shadow of some many of these SCW stars is too beat you and win that BRASS KNUX Belt but I know that will be no easy task so I will train hard for this match. Now I have taken you too the limit before and I have never wrestled with you before then but now I know your style and I will be more dangerous and determined than ever."
[PETERS]: "Well Page, there is a rumor going around that Derek Sweger made you? People are saying if it wasn't for him you would still be in the SCW gutter and wouldn't be going anywhere!"
[PAGE]: "Well Sweger may have helped me with some things but for one thing he didn't make me. I have to wrestle in the ring when I have a match and win the match. Now for all I care even if he did make me its doesn't matter cause I have changed somethings and Sweger has never seen me fight in a Falls Count
Anywhere match. Sweger may have guided me and shown me some tricks in the SCW but when he left me and I had to defend for myself I became more determined than ever. Now Sweger you may walk into The Reunion Arean in Dallas, Texas on
March 14 but you will not walk out without a challenge. I will make sure you get a taste of how it feels to get a concussion and to swallow your own blood.
This match will not be for the weaken heart." [PETERS]: "Lets say you do beat Sweger and become the SCW Brass Knux Champion what are your plans?"
[PAGE]: "My plans will be to take this belt and take it to better and high levels that no one has ever done before. I mean it will be hard to do at first and I am sure that after my match with Sweger we will meet again but I will make sure by the time I lose the belt that I lose it to a worthy opponent who can follow in my footsteps. Now I will not guarantee a win but I will fight for this belt and you will see a whole new different me. Sweger get ready for the fight of your life!"
[PETERS]: "Thank you for your time Chris Page. And good luck in your match against Derek Sweger."
[PAGE]: "Thanks and Sweger even though your my opponent I wish you luck cause I know you will need it."
Chris Page hands the microphone to Shay Peters. Paula walk over to the ring post and grabs the American Flag. Paula begins to wave the flag and Chris Cage sits down on the middle rope for her to exit. Paula exits the ring while Chris
Cage slides under the ropes and escorts Paula down the steel stairs. Paula is waiving the American Flag while walking down the aisle. Chris Page and Paula reach the beginning of the aisle way and Chris Page begins to flex his 22 inch pythons while Paula continues to waive the American Flag. Chris Page and Paula then go through the curtain into the back.
