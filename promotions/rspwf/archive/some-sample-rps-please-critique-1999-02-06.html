Some Sample RPs... Please Critique...

6 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey -- not bragging here -- I'm the top dog in the AWFL, and I have the highest push, but anyway, I
want to know what you think. Give it to me straight, if they suck, they suck... Because my push can always get higher! :)
Note: I talk in 3rd person, but I'm not a rip-off of The Rock. Double Note: The top part is regular, the bottom is HTML-ized, so if you view it on your webpage, message board or whatever, it looks MUCH better!
______________________ BEGIN TRANSMISSION... Bad-Cool's Adventures at K-Mart (Regular) Bad-Cool Speaks *Bad-Cool is standing outside of a local K-Mart convenience store with a microphone. Ecstacy and Laura are not around, because Bad-Cool knows people would try to grab their breasts and ask them out if they were with him...
just to make him jealous. They are at home, getting ready for a "big night". Laura has her camera on, and as fans pass, they stare at her. Bad-Cool knew it'd be a bad idea to bring her, but he needed a camera man... or camera woman.
Bad-Cool has on finger gloves, a black "Bad-Cool is King of the World" shirt that has a dead Leonardo DiCraprio on the front with Bad-Cool standing over him, silk "Bad-Cool" pants, Bad-Cool sneaks, and red seethrough sunglasses. His hair is up like Roaddog Jesse James.
Laura has on a tight miniskirt with a matching top. She has on black high-heel shoes. Her hair is pulled down. She starts to film BC...* Bad-Cool: This is AWFL superstar Bad-Cool reporting live from outside this local K-Mart in downtown Columbus. Anybody who knows anything knows that
K-Mart sure has been the butt of many jokes over the years -- "Blue Light
Specials," "Your momma shops at K-Mart" and other timeless knee-slappers.
*Bad-Cool, standing outside a K-Mart convenience store with a microphone and Laura, starts to approach a little girl, no more than five years-old*
Bad-Cool: Hi there, Little Girl. You know what this big store is right over here?
Little Girl: K-Mart. Bad-Cool: That’s right! K-Mart! Very good! What have you heard about K-Mart, little girl? Little girl: I heard a joke. Bad-Cool: You heard a joke? Would you like to tell the camera that little joke? BC'll give you a dollar.
Little Girl: Okay. Here’s the joke. What did the little birdie say when it walked past K-Mart?
Bad-Cool: Okay, BC'll play along. What did the little birdie say when it walked past K-Mart?
Little Girl: Cheap, cheap! *Bad-Cool hands the girl 75 cents instead of a dollar, because he's a major cheapass. Bad-Cool starts to talk again.* Bad-Cool: But nowadays people are willing to take their K-Mart humor a bit further. Many people even advocate minor vandalism at "the K" for some laughs. As one couple told me, maybe it's all one can do after scarfing on a blue Icee and pretzel at the K-Mart snack bar. So here we are: Fun things to do at K-Mart, as told by the street trash that shops and works there.
*Bad-Cool is standing near the shopping carts with an ELDERLY BLACK MAN, acting as an energetic commando journalist. He shoves his microphone in their face.* Bad-Cool: So tell BC, what is your idea of fun at K-Mart? Elderly Black Man: Me and the mississ like to take shopping carts for the express purpose of filling them and stranding them at strategic locations.
*Bad-Cool moves along and stops a young punk nerdy-looking kid with thick glasses. Bad-Cool reads the name tag that says "K-Mart Employee of the
Month". Bad-Cool speaks to him.*
Bad-Cool: ... what is your idea of fun at K-Mart? Employee: I like to set all the alarm clocks to go off at 10-minute intervals throughout the day. That freaks people out. One time, I seen a couple of kids contaminate the entire auto department by sampling all the spray air fresheners. That was annoying as hell.
Bad-Cool: Who did it? Employee: Street trash. *Bad-Cool moves along and is soon standing with a YOUNG MAN who speaks with a slight working-class English accent.* Young Man: Oh, the most fun I ever had at a K-Mart was when I challenged other customers to duels with tubes of gift wrap.
Bad-Cool: Did they comply? Young Man: Every one.... Bad-Cool: Anything else? Young Man: Well, one time I left cryptic messages on the typewriters. And on another I Re-dressed the mannequins as I saw fit.
Bad-Cool: What was the MOST fun you had at K-Mart? Young Man: When there are people behind you, walk REALLY SLOW, especially in narrow aisles. Now THAT is fun.
*Bad-Cool moves along again and is soon standing alongside a couple of YOUNG BLACK KIDS from the inner city. They are all between the ages of ten and fifteen.* Bad-Cool: What's the most fun you've had, kids? Black Kid # 1: Walk up to an employee and tell him in an official tone, "I think we've got a Code 3 in Housewares." And then see what happens.
Black Kid # 2: Tune all the radios to a polka station; then turn them all off and turn the volumes to "10."
Black Kid # 3: Play with the automatic doors. Black Kid # 4: While walking through the clothing department, ask yourself loud enough for all the people to hear, "Who BUYS this crap, anyway?" That always turns some heads.
Black Kid # 3: Ride a display bicycle through the store; claim you're taking it for a "test drive." That was good...
Black Kid # 1: Or follow people through the aisles, always staying about 5 feet away. Continue to do this until they leave the department.
Black Kids # 3: Play basketball with a group of friends. Use the entire store as your court.
Black Kid # 1: Move all them "Caution: Wet Floor" signs to carpeted areas. Black Kid #2: Set up a tent in the camping department; tell others you'll only invite them in if they bring pillows from Bed and Bath.
*Bad-Cool moves along and is soon with a REDNECK, dressed inn plaid and armed with an assortment of firearms, chewing tobacco, cigarettes (Camels) and a few AWFL rasslin’ tickets protruding from his shirt pocket.* Redneck: To have fun, well... I like to test the fishing rods and see what
I can "catch" from the other aisles.
Bad-Cool: Anything else? Redneck: You mean like ask other customers if they have any Grey Poupon? Bad-Cool: Yeah, that definitely qualifies. Redneck: Also, I seen one guy who draped a blanket around his shoulders and run around saying, "I'm Batman. Come, Robin -- to the Batcave!" Personally,
I’d like to TP as much of the store as possible. My wife likes to randomly throw things over into neighboring aisles. My boy Zed he likes to play with the calculators so that they all spell "hello" upside down. When two or three people are walking ahead of you, run between them, yelling, "Red
Rover!" That always gets a reaction. I got kicked outta the store for that once. Or, while handling guns in the hunting department, suddenly ask the clerk if he knows where the anti-depressants are. Act as spastic as possible. That’s real good. When someone asks if you need help, begin to cry and ask, "Why won't you people just leave me alone?" That’s good, too.
*Bad-Cool moves along and is seen with a COLLEGE STUDENT.* College Student: Take up an entire aisle in Toys by setting up a full scale battlefield with G.I. Joes vs. the X-Men. My girlfriend nonchalantly "tests" the brushes and combs in Cosmetics. Sometimes the guys get together and we hold indoor shopping cart races. One time I saw my friend Joe dart around suspiciously while humming the theme from "Mission: Impossible." And to top it off, we attempted to fit into very large gym bags. And you can freak the employees by saying things like, "Would you be so kind as to direct me to your Twinkies?" I’d like to someday set up a "Valet Parking"
sign in front of the store. Jeff... He’s crazy.... But one time my friend
Jeff went into the auto department. This is true! In the auto department, he practiced his "Madonna" look with various funnels. I am NOT making this up! This really happened! Dude, he looked like a f*cking whore! Oh my God!
I busted up! I think the funnest thing I ever did was to wait for someone to step away from their cart to look at something, then I would quickly make off with it without saying a word. Jean relaxed in the patio furniture until he got kicked out. When an announcement would come over the loudspeaker, Jean would always assume the fetal position and scream, "No, no! It's those voices again!" He was hilarious! One other time, he dragged a lounge chair on display over to the magazines and relaxed there. My god!
Bad-Cool: The question that begs to be asked then is, why? Why do you desecrate K-Mart? College Student: Cause they had it coming, man. As soon as they sold us this Blue Light sh!t, they had it coming.
*Bad-Cool exits the store and is ready to wrap it up.* Bad-Cool: ...Because they had it coming. Indeed. Trash deserves only to get trashed. And this Sunday night, Demolisher... BC trashes YOU! You may be able to bust a rhyme, but Demolisher... tonight... BC'll bust yo' ass. End of story.
[NOTE: Oh, and in case you were wondering, I busted his ass.] ________________________ Bad-Cool's Workout (Regular) Bad-Cool Speaks *Scene opens showing Bad-Cool with a black "AWFL" tanktop on, black shirts, black finger gloves, and blue glasses. Bad-Cool has a muscular built as far as we can see (no sign of steroid abuse), and once again, Ecstacy and
Celena are nowhere to be found. Laura is actually not filming this time -- a guy named Clay is filming. Bad-Cool knew that there are too many overgrown pigs in this gym, and he didn't want his girls here.
The camera pans a bit and reveals a sign that reads "New York City's Olympic Gym". Bad-Cool starts to walk through the glass doors when the camera pans around a bit more. Suddenly the camera man is interrupted.*
Bad-Cool: Listen, "Clay"... Get inside this gym, point the camera on BC, and don't wonder off too long, got it?!
Clay: Gah... gah... gah... Bad-Cool: Good. Now stand there and look pretty and film this gym. *We open inside a vast gym. The clashing sound of metal ricochets off the gym walls as the daily assortment of bodybuilders work on attaining inhuman muscular frames.
After a few minutes of shaking of the tense shock known as "Bad-Cool", Clay moves to a weight bench where a giant man is lifting up some serious weights. A young blond-haired teenager, serving as the mountainous man’s spotter, watches the incredible feat and shakes his head in amazement. !
His giant biceps elevate the weight as if it were merely a toothpick supporting a cocktail olive at each end. An incredible display of raw power!
Several of the local bodybuilders working out nearby stop dead in their reps to watch the man's performance.
Suddenly, since Bad-Cool was out of the spotlight, BC got off the treadmill and told everyone to "Go !#%$ off" since they didn't recognize this former
AWFL International champion. They all agreed as Bad-Cool go to lift some weights, wondering if he could get any attention.*
Bad-Cool (under his breath): ... Eleven... Twelve... Thirteen... Fourteen... Fifteen!!!! Whew! *Bad-Cool sets the weight back up on the supports over the bench, carefully guiding it to a rest with grace and control. But the other body builders don't even notice as they're still watching the other young man with the blonde spotter. Bad-Cool, in utter disbelief, sits up, grabs a towel from off a nearby stool, and wipes the thin layer of sweat off his brow.
Bad-Cool, who had been doing 500 pounds but had not been noticed, gets frustrated.* Bad-Cool (to Clay): Alright, kid. Next we’re gonna do 600. Throw two more plates on there. And make sure they are fifties!
*Clay collects the weights off a nearby rack and begins to slide them onto the bar while Haggar towels his face dry. Bad-Cool takes a quick sip of water from his Gatorade sports bottle.*
Bad-Cool: Hey, Clay... Let's make a deal... you do some lifting yourself, and Bad-Cool will legally change your name with his good money... go ahead... try it.
*Clay looks down and thinks to himself. He knows he hoes a retarded name and desperately needs it changed, so he agrees. Clay tries to lift the 600 pounds Bad-Cool was about to do, but can't. After some struggling, he removes some plates and brings it down to 250 pounds. After some slight lifting, Bad-Cool notices something that bothers him.*
Bad-Cool: Hey, kid! Remember to lift with your knees and not your back, alright?
Clay: Go play with yourself... Bad-Cool: Wouldn’t want to get a hernia, would you? Pop out your intestines? That would hurt. You wouldn’t be able to lift a pencil, let alone a weight.
Clay: Yeah, yeah... I got it... *Bad-Cool eyes the kid, hoping he got his point across. Bad-Cool takes a second sip of water and looks across the gym. He notices the camera... on the floor... Bad-Cool picks it up and hands it over to somebody walking by, along with a $500 bill. Bad-Cool then looks around... He notices somebody.*
Bad-Cool (to himself): Shit... Clay:What is it? Bad-Cool: They’ve found BC... I don’t know how, but they found BC. Clay:Who? Who has found you? Bad-Cool: The parasites... *Clay looks across the gym towards Bad-Cool point of interest. A new camera crew pair of two is making its way into the building. They probe the gym, seeking out there intended target. One of them spots
Bad-Cool, and the two men start to make their way towards the
ex-International champion.* Bad-Cool: F*ck... F*ck... F*ck... F*ck... *The camera crew, led by veteran CNN reporter and commentator Christopher Coma, and Scott the camera operator, finally makes it to Bad-Cool’s position. Coma, terribly out of shape, is visibly huffing and puffing for precious oxygen. They take but a moment to start setting up their equipment. *
Bad-Cool (disgusted by Clay’s flabby build): Jesus, Chris... Think you could stand to lose a few? (changing the subject when Chris blushes) Ah, hell... It’s okay, Chris... BC needS guys like you around to compare his
Godly physique to.... So tell BC... How’d you bimbos locate BC? Thought BC lost you punks on Pennnsylvania Avenue...
Chris (catching his breath): We lost you for a while, but we found your car parked in the underground garage across the street. A little deductive thinking, and we knew you had to be in here...
Bad-Cool: You figure that out all by yourself Chris? Scott(chuckling): Chris ain’t as dumb as he looks... Chris (annoyed): Steel is gonna hear about that one, Parker! Your head is gonna roll! Bad-Cool: Want to make a bet on that, Scott? *At this comment, BC and Scott bust up, while Chris Coma turns very red.* Chris (trying to keep his composure, to Scott): Are you set up? Scott (setting up the camera): We’re ready to roll! Whenever you want,
Chris... Chris (to Scott) Roll ‘em! (to the camera, holding a microphone) Hello, wrestling fans! I’m Chris Coma coming to you from the world-famous
(not really) Olympic Gym in New York City, New York! I’m standing alongside the former AWFL International champion Bad-Cool, who is attempting a comeback bid in the AWFL... (to BC)
So, Bad, I guess the question that’s got to be on everyone’s mind is: Why now? Why are you choosing now to make your comeback? You never really retired, but you slacked off for about a week... what's wrong?
Bad-Cool: Now is as good a time as any. BC lost a lot of respect for myself on the streets. BC guesses he's just too stupid to find a job.
Chris: What did you do in your time off? Bad-Cool: Uh, BC wasn't really off... Chris: Meaning? Bad-Cool: When BC wasn’t on the road, BC spent most of my time looking for trouble. Chris: How did you go about that? Bad-Cool: Uh, BC traveled around a bit. BC visited all the local bars BC could find and BC basically harassed motorcycle gangs. BC would just stand outside the bar and kick over Harleys one by one until someone came out and was willing to start something with me.
Chris: Who won? *At this inquiry, Haggar bursts into laughter.* Bad-Cool: Hell, BC's still here, isn't he? BC's still walking. The same can’t be said for some of the other guys BC fought. Course, BC did get knicked once or twice.
*Bad-Cool turns his forearm to Clay, displaying a fresh-cut knife slash.* Bad-Cool: You should see the other guy, man. BC ain’t ever seen so much sh!t pour out of one six-foot five inch frame.
*This statement disturbs Chris, and he suddenly looks very sick.* Chris: (choking> We saw you on the pay-per-view Horrors of Haloween where you and Sampson fought quite a battle. It was a classic confrontation of brute strength and brawn versus brute strength and brawn. A hard fought contest for both of you. So tell me: What was on your mind going into that contest?
Bad-Cool: Why the hell are you bringing that up for? Geez, you must watch wrestling once every blue moon... That's old crap.
Chris: Uh... What’s next on Bad-Cool agenda? Bad-Cool: Just to get back into the thick of things. Be effective. Win some titles. Prove that BC is all BC says he is. Be the very best there is.
Chris: What is the most important thing in your life right now, Mike? Bad-Cool: BC guesses it’s staying true to himself. You know, lately BC thinks he's sort of lost my way in life. BC's broken his word on one too may occasions. When you come into this world, you’ve only got one thing, and that is your word. When BC came in the AWFL, BC gave his word to all the fans that I would be there for them, that I would stick by them and always perform for them, that I would be the very best I could be. Well, I strayed from that path one time too many. It’s time to make amends. It’s time to bring some good back into my life by doing good deeds.
*Noticed Clay has the bar on his neck, and he can't get it off. He is turning purple.* Bad-Cool: Get outta here, Chris. This impromptu interview is over. *Chris attempts to mount a protest, but BC waves him away. Dejected, Chris and Scott pack up their camera equipment and leave. BC eyes the 250 pound weight with keen anticipation. Clay slowly lays his mammoth frame down on the bench. After taking three deep breaths, he extends his arms upward, grasping the bar with his gorilla-sized paws. BC carefully lowers the weight down towards his chest, sweat literally seeming to leap off his face, forming a puddle on the cold concrete floor below him. With one mighty thrust, his huge arms push the weight skyward as he lets out a low, primitive grunt. BC holds it in place for perhaps ten seconds as the veins in his massive arms seemed ready to explode. The gladiator then lowers the weight, gently placing it back into the bench’s holding arms. BC grabs the towel and begins to wipe the sweat from his eyes.
Bad-Cool (to the spotter): See kid? A piece of cake. A piece of cake. *BC tells Clay to go "F!ck a tree", gets his camera back, still gets hardly any attention from the crowd, and leaves the gym, disgusted.* ******Like the inhuman exhibition at the gym, there haven’t been many potholes on the road to immortality since his September arrival to the
AWFL. How long will it be before he once again carries around the gold again?*******
