$$ =-_STEVE GREEDY _=- $$ _ Money isn't the Only thing _ _It's_ _
Everything_ ( The Scene fades in to a Small Bar called the " Red Dog" in the Town of Augusta ,Georgia. There is a slow song playing in the background of the Bar, but Only three people are in the Bar. That is counting the Bartender. The Two other men in the bar Are setting there Quietly Drinking there Budweiser.
Man 1 : Well How bout those Disc Golfers ? I heard they set a new course record out there to day in that Roaring wind.
Man 2: Must of been hard... I mean I think the winds where around 30-40 mph all day long. That's saying alot for those disc Golfer's.
Bartender : Bah, You know the City is going slow when the main focus of conversation is Disc Golf.
( Just then a Three Man Camera crew comes rushing into the Bar....One man is
Holding the camera, the Other has a pencil and paper as if he is gonna take
Notes and the last has a Microphone in his right hand ,with a smile which brightens the room on his face. On the mic the Letters "BFTW" stand out in Bold
Blue Letters.
Interviewer : Hello, This is the "Red Dog" Right ? ( The three men all Nod there heads as if to agree... There is a look of shock on two of the three mens faces and the Other just assumes he is drunk and takes it as a normal occurence.
Interviewer : Is it true that $teve Greedy hangs out here ? I need to tell him about his match up this Saturday Night.
( Bartender says as he is laughing Bartender : Greedy in this bar ? In _OUR_ Dreams. No way he would ever show his face around here. I think you are looking for the "Red Diner", But He wouldn't be there no more. I can give you his home address though and maybe you can catch him there .
Interviewer : Thank you My good man, [ Whispers ] make sure the man get's tipped well boy's,
( The Two men talk for a brief moment, then the crew heads out, They leave a hundred - dollar bill on the table to thank the Bartender for his services. The
People in the bar go back to the way it was before the Crew came in, While the crew heads to the Home of the Greedy one. They are riding in a Large Black Van made by the Toyota company.They are almost to the home, when they are caught by a red- light. A red truck pull's up beside the van. One man in the back, then of course the driver.
Driver of Red truck : Hey yall don't look from around here. It appears yall have shaved in the last TWO WEEKS. Can you help me find a place where I can buy whores ? I am kinda lonely and Don't feel like being with my brother again tonight.
Interviewer : If we are not from around here, How would we know where the
Whores are located man ?
Driver of Red Truck : I was trying to be Polite...... To be Blunt, How much are yall ? Interviewer : Excuse me ? Driver of Red Truck : How much for two hours....Me and my friend ? ( The Light then turns green and the van speedily speeds off towards the Home they are set to go see. They reach the Large home and are let passed security.... They set there camera up Outside the Home, Then the Doors of the home open slowly as a Man in a Nice brown Armoni suit step's out, with brown
Itallion Lassen shoes, A gold Rolex watch and some shades to add to his overall
"Hotshot" image. He Look's into the camera, then says the following.
^^^^^$TEVE GREEDY^^^^^^^ I know yall are here to see me, but I am really not in the Mood to see you. So an Interview is out of the question but a few statements shall be made of course.So you will still go away
_Blessed_ (He pauses, then whispers to the Interviewer Who do I fight on Saturday, I did not get all the Information, I was so excited
I turned the Tv off.. I was just glad to get a match.... Well you didn't need to know that.
Interviewer : First time out of a dark match eh ? ( Greedy gets an angry look on his face, The Interviewer quickly tells $teve
Greedy who he fight's On saturday and tells him the fight will take place in
Queens.
( Greedy starts to say something, but then once again whispers to the Interviewer ^^^^^^$TEVE GREEDY^^^^^^ Where is Queens ? That aint from around here is it ? ( The Interviewer chuckles Interviewer : You don't get away from here much do you Dude ? Queen's is In New
York. ^^^^^^^$TEVE GREEDY^^^^^^^ Well it seem's That The so called King of Chaos shall be my first victim, Just as THE KING is dead, This man who claims just as many others to be a king shall be dead shortly..... Anyman who think's they can beat me in the ring, must be
BRAIN DEAD to begin with. So he shouldn't be much of an Obstacle for a man Like myself.
I am not saying that I will kill him, for you Country ass hicks in the Banned from Television Wrassling program might Get me Fired or put behind bars, but the Main thing you need to know is.
_ I _ _ Do Not Plan to Lose _ That which I don't plan to do, I don't do...... By the way, since this banned from Television can I say Fuck ?
Interviewer : HEY WATCH THAT !!!!!! ^^^^^^$TEVE GREEDY^^^^^^^ Guess not ( He chuckles a bit, then continues to speak....... Back to the Subject, The so called "King" Chris Cage, is gonna feel like he is in a cage when he step's in there with me..... Why ? Cause I am the predator in charge and he is the Poor little prey....Thus he is _Trapped_....... Get it ?
Cage..... Trapped..... oh nevermind. ( Greedy pauses a moment, to re-gather his thoughts, then speaks once again. ^^^^^^$TEVE GREEDY^^^^^^^^^ This is My first match in the Big time since...... Well...... ( The Interviewer with a huge smile on his face cuts in... Interviewer : This isnt the Big time yet, Its just a normal match man.. ^^^^^^^$TEVE GREEDY^^^^^^ This Interview is over ( He says angrily and storms back into his Huge home. Once Greedy is back in his home, the Annoucer speaks.
Interview : This wasn't an Interview anyway....That conceited bastard. [ Fades ]
