IIWF was recently voted #1 e-fed. I agree. In mid-november I joined the mailing list and my wrestler J.P. Steele will soon be on the roster (1-2 months says Daniel). But he did have a few flashes in IIWF. Anyway, though
IIWF has some great roleplayers such as [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld],etc. it also boasts the stupidest roleplay ever written in my mind. Written by me. :)
For archive-ial purposes, we shall call it the B-rated move of roleplays...THE ATTACK OF THE HUNGREY MAN!
Read on and see just how bad that flash was: =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [SCENE:A gym in Detroit. It is Saturday November 30th, in mid-afternoon.J.P. Steele is training with a sparring partner, when Annie Mack calls him over from to come over to the television set]
[Annie Mack]:C'mon over here J.P. It's that Stud Stetson.... "There is another wrestler I would like to address here this fine evening and that is one J.P. Steele. You are hoping to make it here to the number one federation in the nation and you say that you have been watching it very closely lately. Well watch this [Stetson lifts up his middle finger to the camera]. Little boy, if you do get in, you will find out what the IIWF is all about because I am going to give you the beating of a lifetime. I am the next leader of the
IIWF and you, along with the rest, will find that out." [J.P. Steele]:Heh heh. Stetson, oh your REAL cool, you can stick your finger up. I could care less what you say or do. Because the second the IIWF let's me in the ring, I'm gonna kick your butt. You see, you may be more expirienced, but I'm no stranger to the ring.Four high school state championships, 3 college
NCAA championships. Stetson, this rookie is for real. I've been all around the worls in the past 6 months, winning titles and gaining knowledge. I've beaten the best...and also beaten by the best. I've been everywhere from ICE to WAR, to TCW to, hey let's just put it this way: I've wrestled so many times in the past 6 months than most people do in 2 years. And I always make time to train in the gym. Stamina, agility. Everything. How many 6 feet 9 two houndred ninety four pound men do you know that can go topside and land more aerial moves than you can ever dream off?
[Annie Mack]:Hey J.P.,look at this! [Annie has turned on her laptop computer, and found the IIWF web site. Annie searches over it, before finding a application form. She and J.P. fill it in, and send it off] [J.P. Steele]:Well, there we go. Weather Prez Dan will let me in yet is to be decided. But even if he doesn't just yet, I'll still be watching. Always, always watching. Not a card will go by that I don't attend live or watch on television. Not a pay per view left un-ordered.
Speaking of pay per views, this Snow Brawl looks awesome. Up in Alaska is it? That'll be some trip to make up there. Here's hoping that I'll be with IIWF December 21st to be apart of it.Say, a lot of these IIWF wrestlers look familiar. Armed Forces, seen 'em. Robksi, fueded with him. Randy Acorn, beat him twice, in under a minute both times. Geez, this is a deep talent roster. Only one thing missing.[smiles] My name on it. C'mon IIWF, where else will you find an excting, hard working, enthusiastic, rookie extremly intrested. Heck, I'll wrestle in the dark matches if I have to! Just give me a chance IIWF. I can really put on a show! I mean, hey I'm a rookie and unproven, but just give me one match to show my stuff. If you don't like it, bye bye. Adios, I'll be out of your hair. But just let me try! IIWF is the best wrestling has to offer. I'll never know how good I can be if
I never get the shot. Just
one match! Against a jobber even! Just one chance at glory!
Man...if I could say to a friend....'Hey, you know that IIWF. I wrestled for them once'...to say that would be the greatest. Anyhow, I'm gonna shut up now. I got a plane to catch...to Mexico City.
I got to get to my fifth row center section seats at the Sombrerro Dome! [smiles] And I'm darn hungry. Maybe 10, 15 tacos will hit the spot. Annie, got the tickets? [Annie nods, and flashes two plane tickets and two tickets for the IIWF Saturday Night card in Mexico that she bought via mail order] [J.P. Steele]:Oh hey, I guess we can leave in a bit longer. Card isn't 'till tonight anyhow. Mexico Mexico, here I come! Say, is Stetson wrestling Saturday? Hmm, where's that video I taped from the report. Ah, here we go [pops tape into VCR and watchesm pops it out] Rats, he doesn't got a match. Aw well, I'm sure he'll be somewhere around the arena. Perhaps I should get a backstage pass [laughs] maybe meet him with a
"greeting". Stetson, it may be days, weeks, or years. But as soon as IIWF signs me to a contract, we'll all see who the real 'Superstar'
is.Remember IIWF, give me one chance. One chance. Maybe I'll need to make another cameo appearence on TV like last Saturday in the crowd? Or perhaps a special
in-ring interview when you least expect it? Or maybe a special corner man for a legend like Kauffman or Shakespere? Or maybe I'll play in sugar with those kooky Alphabet Boys? Or maybe...the IIWF will give guy who cares a chance? Nah, they wouldn't dare ask a rookie fan favorite who is the biggest IIWF fan in the world to wrestle for them?
Quigly left, maybe someone else will too. Money is leaving the bank, I can put the fans in the seats as well as anyone given the chance. All it takes is one match
IIWF. When was the last time an enthusiastic rookie like me asked to join, and hounded the top brass weeks on end? Who was the last rookie you signed?
Someone who gave the fed qualtiy matches perhaps? So, why not risk it once more. It's a win/win situation, you can't lose. IIWF, you can't go wrong when signing
The Jackhammer. IIWF, remember one thing: J.P. Steele is watching you, very very carefully. Very cautiously, scouting, taking notes.
It never ends until I step into the ring for the greatest fed. IIWF...I'm always watching you. He and Annie walk out of the gym, and get into a blue convetible. They drive off. The camera goes to all static, then turns on. It is hours later, in mid-flight on a Southerwest Airlines plane headed to
Mexico City. Annie Mack is holding a camcorder, taping everything for the IIWF to see. J.P. opens a bag of peanuts, and devours it in 2 seconds flat. A flight attendent walks down the aisle, as notices J.P. pushing the
"service" buton. She walks over, looking down at her order list] [Attendent]:May I help you? [J.P. Steele]:Er, yes. It appears I'm really hungrey. Any chance I could get, oh, a dozen more peanut packages?
[Attendent]: Sure [laughs] I guess you must be hungry. [looks up and gasps] My god....you're....you're J.P. Steele! That wrestler! Oh my kids love you! They've been following your carrer since you started. I usually let them watch IIWF in before they go to sleep, and when they saw you on Saturday
Night.....they nearly fell out of bed. It took me hours to calm them down. So, have you signed a contract with IIWF yet? [J.P. Steele]:Not yet ma'm. I'm trying awfully hard to. Hopefully before Snow Brawl. Well, seeing as your kids are fans, maybe I could sign an autograph? [Attendent]:That'd be great. [J.P. Steele]: Here we go...[pulls out a Wrestling Trading Card with him on it, and signs it] there.
Hope your kids like it! [Attendent]: Thanks a lot Mr. Steele. Here are those packages of peanuts...[hands him his dozen packages]
[J.P. is in the midst of scarfing them down] [J.P. Steele]: Mmmm. Thanks. Ufff....mmm. Bye. [attendent leaves]. Want one Annie? [Camera view shakes side to side] Ah, okay. Gotta keep that awesome figure huh?
[smiles] [Camera again goes to all static. When it returns, it is hours later, just inside the Sombrerro
Dome. J.P. Steele and Annie Mack just have walked throw the ticket lines, and are walking through the arena, trying to find the stairwell to their seats. J.P. sites a McDonalds stand and rushes over]
[J.P. Steele]:They got McDonald's Annie. Wow, Taco Burgers! Burrito Shakes! Oh boy! [Annie Mack]:J.P., you just had that huge steak at the airport here but 30 minutes ago! [J.P. I'm hungry.[steps up first in line] Uh, I'd like 10 taco burgers, 2 extra value meals...the one with the supersized Big Mac and Fries and Large Coke, and um....5 orders of the 6 piece Chicken McNuggets.
