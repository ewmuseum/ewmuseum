E-mail me at [detail withheld] . I want your comments. My roleplay's all come from mood. I was kinda pissed when I wrote this....     --The scene opens up to Morpheus riding in his 1957 Plymouth Fury. The car is Cherry Red with everything else chrome. He rebuilt the car from scratch when he found it in a junkyard. He spent 2 years building it and he drives it with pride. The stereo in the car is playing “November Rain” by Guns N’ Roses. The song is at the part near the end where Slash is playing his Second Solo and its the downtrodden part of the song. Morpheus is wearing his black leather jacket, a red flannel shirt under his jacket, an old Morpheus T-shirt from his HwF days, black boots, and a pair of Black Jeans. His face is rather pale and bags hang under his eyes. His total appearance looks like he hasn’t slept in a few days. Morpheus pulls into a Hospital parking lot and parks his car. He walks into the building and right up to the front desk-- Morpheus: What room is Aaron Price in? Nurse: How are you related to him? Morpheus: Business Partner. Nurse: Oh, he’s in room 302. Morpheus: Thanks. --Morpheus gets in the Elevator and hits the button for the 3rd floor. The elevator begins to move when Morpheus notices the Muzak. The song playing is a Muzak rendition of the classic kiss song, Beth. Morpheus laughs to himself-- Morpheus: Damn...Take a good song and ruin it for some Elevator music.. Thats funny. --The elevator reaches the 3rd floor and Morpheus steps out. He looks down the hall and finds room 302. He walks in and sees Aaron Price sleeping on his bed. Morpheus reaches in his jacket and pulls out a card. He puts it on the bedside table and then leaves the room. Morpheus leaves the hospital and then jumps into his car. He pulls out of the parking lot and towards the city. Morpheus drives for a while and then hits downtown. Hookers, Whores, Sluts, whatever you wanna call them, line the streets. Morpheus is in the bad part of town. He see’s a little Chinese Resturant and parks his car. He walks inside and they escort him to a table. Morpheus is looking at his Menu when a kid about the age of 14 walks over-- Kid: Hey, Mister. Excuse me Mister. Morpheus: Yea, how can I help ya kiddo? Kid: Are you Morpheus from the UpW? Morpheus: Why, yes I am. What would ya like? An Autograph? Kid: No, not really. Why would I want an Autograph from someone as bad as you? Morpheus: Excuse me? Kid: You heard me, You suck. Morpheus: If your trying to pick a fight with me, its not gonna work. I have thing about fighting kids. I don’t wanna end up in jail and become some big bad man’s boyfriend. Kid: Punk.. --The kid walks off and Morpheus sighs. The waitress walks over and asks for his order. He orders a plate of Pepper Pork and a pitcher of beer. The waitress walks off to get his order. Morpheus begins to think. He thinks of his upcoming match against 3 other men. 2 of which he knows, and the 3rd being some what of a mystery to him. He thinks of Ken Davison’s and SirJ.O.B.’sAlot Weaknesses and how to capitolize on them. He begins to remember his Matches with Ken Davison. How they once had to call the match because both men were unable to compete any farther. He also remembers their classic cage battle where Morpheus took a Huracanrana off of the top of the cage and still got up. Morpheus also remembers how he countlessly tried to regain his HwF IC title from Ken, but he never quite could. Morpheus snaps out of his Haze when the waitress comes by with his food. He thanks her and asks if they have the local newspaper. She hands him a paper and leaves him to eat. Morpheus reads the paper and eats at the same time. He comes across an ad for Friday’s card at the Sports Arena. Morpheus can’t seem to remember much about the Sports Arena. He knows he has wrestled their before, but can’t put a face or a time to the arena. Morpheus finishes eating and gets up. He leaves a tip for the waitress and then heads to the front counter to pay. He pays and leaves. Morpheus jumps in his car and heads towards his hotel room. He gets into his hotel room and sets up a Camera for an interview. The camera is set and he turns it on-- Morpheus: War..War never changes. Whether it be a Nuclear Massacre or a Wrestling match, nothing ever changes. You go out there, you fight, and you come home a winner or a loser. Its just as simple at that..or is it? What happens when 4 sides are at war? What happens when you throw a punch, and there’s 3 more coming at you? What happens when you have to watch your back and have no one to trust. Yea, me and J.O.B.’s were a sensational tag team, but who says I ever trusted him? We won titles, we lost titles. I always knew we would one day meet in the squared circle and it would be for a greater prize then a number in the win column. This match is the chance to face Hellfyre for the International Title. I’m undefeated here in the UpW, so a chance at a title sounds just about right. And Ken Davison, J.O.B.’s, and Blitz all should know what I will do. No Holds Barred? Fuck yea, I’ll bring the bars that no one can hold. I’ll bring everything including the Kitchen Sink. Hell...I’ll take a fan and beat all 3 of you silly if I have to. And don’t think I’m afraid to put my life on the line. I’ll dive from the ceiling of the Arena and splash your ass through a table if I have to. And ya wanna know why? CAUSE I’M HARDCORE! I’M HARDCORE! Lucifer? Satan? God? Greek Gods? They all know not to fuck with this because I am the sickest, most deranged, suicidal sonofabitch this side of the galaxy and I will stop at nothing to taste gold. So in closeing...Come with me..and You will see the light.
