Hello, please read this roleplay, let me know what you think, and if your interested in signing a wrestler to your e-federation, drop me a line.   Thanks Dave   ---------   [The scene opens up, inside of a small, confined locker room. In view, is the regular scene, of lockers too the side, a white painted brick wall, while on the other side, a large mirror, with a desktop counter, featuring a faucet and cabinets. The room is carpeted with a light blue carpet, that sets off the white walls, making it a gleam to the camera.
Then, on set comes a lovely looking women, wearing white boots, a small red mini-skirt, and a lovely, red bra, which shows off the large value of cle- vage that the woman has to offer. As the camera spins a lil, tipsy topsy and side to side, the woman begins to speak....]
WOMAN: Ladies and Gentleman....the W..W...W-A. Allow me, EVE, present to you...the new ERA, the new TRADITIONAL FRANCHISE, and the FUTURE, of the W-W-W-A.....here's, none other, then my personal favorite...."TRADITIONAL" TOMMMMMYYY
BROOOOKS... [Suddenly, the brief sounds of "Man in a Box", are heard in the background as, the camera turns around, to zoom in on a man, who sits on a locker room bench, knee up, as he stretches out his body, and sits in his in-ring gear, consisting of black leather wrestling boots, a pair of
ice-blue tights, and nothing else but shear body, and muscle. His brown haired, crew cut, shines off the lens, with glistening water, as he begins to speak, with a look of determination on his face...]
TOMMY BROOKS: Well...that's right, everyone, can settle down, everyone, can take a breather, because "TRADITIONAL" TOMMY BROOKS...is here, in the
W-W-W-A. A man pure honor, a man of pure skill, a man of pure tal- ent, which is obvioulsy a rare find anywhere, let alone here, has finally made his way to the roster, and to the payroll. The ratings, the sales, and the pop- ularity of this promotion, are about to skyrocket like never before. The
W-W- W-A offices may think that they've made such an improvement, to get a couple hundred, thousand fans in the arena's, but now...now, when the three words,
TRADITIONAL, TOMMY, and BROOKS, ring out on the TV screen, on the bill- boards, and on the websites, only one thought comes to mind...and that is,
"Holy *#@$, we gotta go see
him!"...and in my mind, when you add two and two together, you get ME, and when you get ME, you get MONEY. Now that we got this across, let's talk about the state of wrestling....
[Brooks whipes his brow, and sniffs his nose...] TOMMY BROOKS: Hardcore wrestling....death style wrestling...luchador, mexican, japanese wrestling. All this stuff is just crap. The following were not even classified styles, untill losers, like MICK
FOLEY, and SUPER CRAZY made it up here, into the great continent of NORTH AMERICA. Now guys like LANCE STORM, CHRIS JERICHO, BRET HART, the late OWEN HART, DAVE BEAN, and fellow other great technical, and actually skilled wrestlers, are keeping the sport, of PROFESSIONAL
WRESTLING, alive, and I thank them for that. And now I, TOMMY BROOKS, must come forward, to the W-W-W-A, and save each, and everyone of your asses. Trust me, after the ROLLING GENERATIONS, the sweat, the blood, and the tears are over, all you guys will be thanking me.... But...for now, my time with you guys is over, so remember...the one fact, that may haunt all of you for the rest of your careers...
    _TRADITION_                     _WILL ALWAYS_                                     _RULE_...... TOMMY BROOKS: I'll see you in the ring... [The scene fades, as Brooks hops to his feet, as him, and his lovely valet EVE, leave the lockeroom, hand in hand, as the lights, and feed cuts with the shut of the door. What kind of impact will Brooks bring to the WWWA? Will
"TRADITION RULE" over the HARDCORE and HIGH
FLYING styles, which
are sure to be found in the WWWA? We'll see, stay tuned!]
