[TOP] Flash Packet - 05.05.01 [2/2]

5 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
============== LEON DE NEGRO ============== [The scene opens in what appears to a small TV studio. The show focuses on an average man, of average height and size standing in front of a black banner which reads "T-O-P on T-V-T." Oh yeah, forgot to mention. That average man...
well hes wearing a black mask with silver markings which make the mask resemble the face of a lion, with the man's long black, wavy hair serving as the "mane."
He wears a simple black t-shirt which is neatly tucked into his faded blue-jeans. The man sways/steps excitedly or nervously, maybe a combonation of both, from side to side as he speaks...]
MAN: Konichwa Japon!! My name es Leon de Negro, luchadore extrodenaire! I have traveled to many countries during my wrestling career, but none is more beautiful than Japon! I am very happy to have been invited here by mi amigo
Shishi Fujiwara. Our relationship is understandable since we are both Lions!!
[de Negro laughs nervously at his reference to the fact that "Shishi" is the
Japanese word for Lion before continuing...]
LEON: It is my honor to face Shoji Suzuki this Domingo. I hope that you'll all come out to the card, I promise that my performance will not disappoint! Feel free to tell the box office people that you're there to see Leon de Negro. Thats L-E-O-N D-E N-E-G-R-O. If you don't feel comfortable pronoucing it, I will have my manager in front of the building handing out [de Negro reaches into his back pocket and pulls out what appears to be a wallet size photograph of himself] these coolio photographs which you can present at the gate to show your appreciation of my booking.
[de Negro breaks out into a wide smile which is visable since his mask doesn't cover his chin, as he breaks out a pen and waves it gleefully...]
LEON: Kidos! If you would like, I will sign your pictures after the show. Please take two though if you would like me to sign, my name is fairly long and it is very difficult to fit it all on a single 2X3. [de Negro quickly turns serious, almost seeming upset...] But if I catch you taking more than two....
[Stops quickly and lets out a nervous laugh, and then quickly changes the subject...] LEON: Well I'm very much looking forward to seeing you all this Sunday and hopefully for many Sundays in the future. My green card is good for the next year so I would LOVE to stick around Japon! I must go now, see you later, if you are a fan, please feel free to write to me at the TOP Offices... as many times as you would like.... encourage your friends to write to!
[The shot abruptly cuts to black as Leon de Negro is still frantically making his pitch to the fans...]
============ SHOJI SUZUKI ============ [Open on opening doors. Exiting through those doors is Shoji Suzki. Suzuki is wearing black running pants, black Nike crosstrainers and a light green t-shirt with "NEON BUSHIDO FLYING ON ANGELS WINGS TOUR 2000" in bright yellow lettering on the front. His long jet black hair is pulled back into a ponytail and he is carrying a gymbag. He halts as a small crowd of reporters and photographers approach him. They all begin to ask questions. Suzuki raises his hands palms out and smiles.]
Suzuki: "Please one at a time." [He nods to someone to his right.] Reporter: "Suzuki-san, how do you feel about your match tonight?" [Suzuki rubs his arms, shoulders and legs.] Suzuki: "Sore." [The reporters politely laugh as Suzuki continues.] Suzuki: "Eiji Yatsu is a great competitor and it was a great fight. A fitting first match for me after a long period away from the ring. It is good to be back."
Reporter: "Are you disappointed by the loss? Do you feel you have hurt TOP by losing to an outsider?"
[Suzuki shakes his head and smiles slyly.] Suzuki: "Not at all. Of course, I would have liked to have won, but there is no shame in losing to a talent like Eiji. I understand many are looking to me as the one to lead the way in the junior division of TOP..."
Reporter: "You_ARE_the junior division in TOP." [More laughter from the press and Suzuki joins in.] Suzuki: "Yes...that will soon change. I hope to see the division grow to one of the best. However, I don't want to rule out facing off against the heavyweights either. There was a time that would not have been accepted, but times have changed."
Reporter: "So, are you challanging the likes of Yoshikazu Kanda, Takeshi Koyoma, Ryo Mazumi, Akira Hashimoto and Ed Dean?" Suzuki: "I am not challanging any one individually, but I would like to test myself against any of those great talents. I see no reason for my size to limit my opponents."
[The reporters buzz with excitement at this somewhat surprising statement. Then from the back someone shouts.]
Voice: "Suzuki have you heard that 'Black Hawk' Kazuya Asakino has joined TOP?" [The smile fades from Suzuki's face for a moment and he lets out a resigned sigh.] Suzuki: "Yes." Voice: "Will you team up with your 'best friend'." [There is some more laughter, this time Suzuki does not join in.] Suzuki: "Asakino is...An annoyance. I do not see the need to team up with him. We are oil and water." [The reporters buzz some more. Suzuki shifts his gym bag from one hand to the other and runs his hand through his hair.]
Reporter: "You next opponent is another 'outsider' Leon de Negro. What is your opinion of him? Will you rebound with a victory?"
Suzuki: "I always set out to win. The rest is a combination of fate and luck. As for de Negro, I know very little of him. I plan to look at video of his matches in CLLL. Regardless I look forward to a very good and differant match than the one with Yatsu. It will be another test, and one that I look forward to. I hope you are all there to watch it and write about it. Thank you."
[Suzuki moves through the group as cameras flash. Fade to black.] =============== KYOJIN YAKUMA =============== [Fade in to an awkward pair of Asian eyes. A nasty red scar bisects the left eye, which a white contact is placed in, so that you can only see the pupil.
The contact contrasts with the right eye, which is of a normal black color, giving the pair of slanted eyes a lopsided appeal. Blink. A black liquid eyeliner circles around both eyes and adds the final touches to an intimidating, yet unconventional, look. Blink, again.
As the camera pans away from the set of eyes and into a full-body shot, there sits a Japanese man of about age 22, attired in a "PENIS + VAGINA + BROKEN
CONDOM = ME" t-shirt, a pair of white on black camouflage pants, and a pair of black combat boots. He rests comfortably on a tacky black sofa, his hands motionless at each side of his body, and his feet lying across the cushioned fabric. A poster of the punk band "X Japan" hangs on the off-whitish wall behind him and a cup of some unknown liquid is placed on the coffee table in front.
He begins to speak in English, but with a confused and garbled Japanese accent.] "Follow me? Can't I be left alone to privacy!?" [He begins to shout in an unexpected rage.] "This is my dressing room, bakayarou! Who had invited you!?" [The once calm man swings his feet off the couch and onto the ground.] "With instance of Kyojin Yakuma joining TOP and already I'm hounded!? Shimatta!" [The camera zooms in a bit, focusing solely on Yakuma's face.] KY: GAH! Now my sanctuary of wonderful has been exploited to you baka's! [Yakuma lowers his head in shame.] KY: Well, continue I must guess... where would I start? [Yakuma raises his head, shoots his eyes to the ceiling as if he's trying to grasp a thought, and continues.]
KY: I am a supernatural which is referred to as "Poison Youth" Kyojin Yakuma. I've come which ways to pursue my destiny. Believe the teller when I say,
"nothing will stop destiny from grasping my awaiting hands!"
[The camera backs away to return to a full body shot of Yakuma. His hands rest in his lap and his right leg taps up and down continuously.]
KY: Anxious for victory I await no longer. I've been patient my entirety of life for this opportunity and I now grow eager. No men will be let to distract my knowledgeable of instincts for victory. No men will be allowed to disturb the plans for greatness which I have already constructed. TOP is that of my bitch! Finally, allowing to express which feelings I'm felt, my emotions are to be running freely.
[A sadistic look shines through his right eye, his left eye remains lifeless due to the white contact that disguises it.]
KY: Taking some baka's place, I show my greatness this weeks Sunday. The end of this long week shall be the weakness end of this short lived federation! I come to rip apart hero's of which you enjoy, Tokyo!
[Allowing himself a moment of laughter, Yakuma continues.] KY: Masahito Hasegawa, see fucker... I'm all of which TOP can handle. You're to be the death victim of first choice. You portray these men of weak minds, but even then, you do not contain one of your own. You can't beat me, baka!
Don't lie yourself to death. It's impossible to overcome that which exceeds your very own self.
[Yakuma lashes out with an arrogant snarl.] KY: Prepare defeat for yourself, fucker! My stronger determinations and skills will be able to reign supreme in the meeting of minds! It once was said that evil defeats any and all which oppose!
[Yakuma begins to mutter.] KY: I've said already too much... You must go. Leave me to be. Leave me to my privacy at once!
[The camera begins to back away, just as Yakuma elevates a middle finger and gestures a farewell. Fade out.]
