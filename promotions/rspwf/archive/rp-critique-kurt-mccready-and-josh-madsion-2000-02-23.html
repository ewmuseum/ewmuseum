(RP Critique) Kurt McCready and Josh Madsion

23 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My name is [name withheld], and I am the handler of Kurt McCready. Josh Madison is
Kurt's Tag Partner, and he is handled by [name withheld]. We both have been into e-federations for over a year and a half. After my federation ended after a year, we decided that RPing together could make a good name for the both of us.
My character, Kurt McCready, is probably one of the dumbest, but skillfull, wrestlers you would ever see in the world of e-wrestling. He is one of those types that you would think everyone could kill. But in the end, he comes out from no where and pulls off the win. He is also extremely tough, but not letting it out all the time. That's his main way of making people think that they can beat him, but they really can't. This guy is new, and hopefully Kurt is going to go far.
Josh's character, Josh Madison, is the smarter out of the two, and the more serious. Joshua Madison is a sophisticated type of guy, he enjoys classical music, champagne, fancy cars and ritzy parties. He grew up a good life, rather well off, an extermly smart athletic kid, alittle on the serious side though.
Eventually he met Kurt McCready, who he trained with to become a wrester, Joshua was always alittle more advanced in actual wrestling, but if McCready was smart enough, he would destroy Madison in a fist fight.
We are now in the MCW, and it takes place on an island. We just arrived there, and we are unpacking our stuff. So here it goes. Please respond. Thanks!
============================== [Kurt McCready's face suddenly appears on the screen. He has a big smile on his face and starts laughing histarically.]
Kurt McCready: "Hey Josh! Look I'm on TV!" [Josh can be heard yelling in the background. It seems like Kurt has the camera in his hand, holding it up to his face. The large smile then goes away, and
Kurt then holds the camera up to his eyes.] Kurt McCready in a shivering, and somewhat serious voice: "...Mom...Dad...I'm so sorry. I swear it was only suppose to be a three hour tour. We were going to turn the boat right back around when it started to turn dark. I swear! [A tear can be seen rolling down Kurt's face.] But now we are stuck on this...this...Island. This place...It's...It's so weird! I'M SO SCARED!!"
[The camera falls to the ground and the only thing that could be seen is the carpet and part of a deflated raft. And the only thing that could be heard was
Kurt crying and Josh cursing, telling him to shut up. The view from the camera showed that Kurt and Josh found shelter, and that they are still on the
Malacian Islands.] Josh Madison in the background: "That was the cheapest imitation of the Blair Witch Project I have ever seen in my entire life....you dipsh(bleep)." Kurt McCready: [Kurt bends over to pick up camera...ignoring Josh's comment.] "...We just got back from HEATWAVE!! Yeah, it was fun. And how do you like our SWEET appartment.... [Kurt takes the camera from his face and turns it around toward the rest of the room. It shows a 1 room appartment with two beds, and one table, which Josh is sitting at at the time.]
Kurt McCready in a voice as if he was talking to a baby: [Zooming in on Josh.] "Say 'Hi' Joshy...Don't be shy." [Josh looks at the camera. His hair is all messy, and his eyes are all bloodshot. He slowly raises his middle finger in the air and points it at the camera.]
Kurt McCready: "Rock on! Joshy drank a little too much soda." [The scene shuts off and comes back on. McCready is seen in a pair of underwear and a wife beater. He pretends he is a professional wrestler. Josh is seen in the background, still sitting in the same exact position, same facial expression. Kurt starts doing some wrestling moves on his bed's pillow. He seems to be skillful at what he does...which really doesn't mean a thing. Kurt then gets up and runs toward Josh. Josh stands up and decks Kurt right in the face. Josh then walks oever to the camera and shuts it off.....]
[The camera is then turned back on again, and Kurt's eyes are back on the screen. Kurts right eyes is tightly closed shut, and redness covers the whole area. A tear is seen rolling down his left cheek.]
Kurt McCready: "I'm not scared. But I got to be really quiet. Josh is sleeping. If he hears me, my left eye is going to match my right. But I'm not scared! MCW...here we come." [Kurt closes his left eye and shuts off the camera.] ============================= Thanks for your time. I don't have a signature quite made yet. So here is my temporary one.
- Jason W. - Handler of Kurt McCready - [detail withheld]
