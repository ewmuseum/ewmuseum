Hardcore Carey.....Updated ["The Benjamin's [Remix]" by Puff Daddy plays over the loudspeakers.] RING ANNOUNCER: Introducing first ... accompanied by Tressa, hailing from Long Beach, CA, standing 6"6' and weighing 225 pounds, here is .... THE LEADER OF THE 420 FAMILY "HARDCORE" CAREY [Hardcore Carey walks down the aisle the bad ass that he is! With his amazing manaher Tressa wearing as usual a tight ass shirt! The make it half way down the aisle, and then she kisses him good luck like usual] OPTIONAL: Hardcore Carey steps into the ring and gives the finger to the fans, then puts his arms out to the side, and slowly turns in sircles like Bret "The Hitman" Hart used to] WRESTLER PERSONALITY ==================== Gimmick: Hardcore Carey was born....John R. Carey III, in Philadelphia, PA on April 4, 1980. He was living large, as his father was one of the world's biggest drug dealers! His father "Kingpin Carey" was in charge of all the dope in the state of PA. The cops knew this, but still had no way of finding out. Hardcore's family was so rich, that they even bought of the President of the United States of America. While growing up, Hardcore enjoyed everything in life. He had the best of everything. He can remember taking long trips on his fathers jet, and now realizes that the trips were to Mexico! When Hardcore was 12, news broke out that "The Kingpin Carey" was gunned down. Hardcore's father was killed by Jose Everso. A hitman for the MAFIA. The Mafia was afraid that Hardcore's father was getting to big to fast! Hardcore then decided at the age of 12, to live in his fahters footsteps! He entered the world of drugs! Hardcore got big almost as fast as his father did! Along with his friends Steve "Buff" Adams, and A.J. "Smooth" Meloy, he ruled the streets of Philly! A local gang, Eastside Crew, hated the fact they weren't as powerfull now that Carey came along, and held a grudge against Carey's crew. The decided to do something about it! On October 30, 1994.....Buff Adams was shot in the arm. He had no serious injuries, but this sparked a flame, that to this day is still burning! Carey concerned for his life went out to the streets, and found a crew to have his back.......He found them. R.S.P. {Rough Style Production} a local gang lead by Muss~Dogg aka Bradford Thomas Musser, decided to join Carey. Carey told him how "The 420 Family" ruled the streets of Philly, but needed back up! Everything was going fine, until on November 29, 1994 a drive by shooting from the Eastside Crew claimed the life of Carey's mother! His best friend was put underground of December 2, 1994. Carey was a man on a mission. He soon caught up with the leader of the Eastside Crew, Nate~D, and shot a round in his head! On December 7, 1994, E~Dawg, the right hand man of Nate~D, shot Carey eight times! Carey was russhed to the hospital! The whole floor was marked off with the color black, the color of "The 420 Family", and "R.S.P." On December 9, 1994 Carey awoke from his coma to find his Ex~girlfriend at his side. They broke up over the safety of her life, but on that day she returned! From that day, Carey took an oath to stop what he was doing. He decided to take his power to the weight room. At the age of 14 he lifted everyday, and turned himself into a fighting machine! A local wrestling recruiter talked him into wrestling for his organization! He captured the EWF Title at the age of 16, then the WWWF title followed! At the age of 17 along with his manager Tressa. He became the HWF Champion, and left the federation. Now at the age of 17.....He's ready to become the next Champion in YOUR Federation! BUILDING YOUR WRESTLER ====================== *** Here's where we figure out how your guy wrestles.
