RP Critique (Just want to have some opinions)

21 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was just wanting some opinions on this RP I recently did. I decided to do it in third person, I think it reads very well, I just wanted some second opinion.
The scene opens up as Frank Catano is slowly pulling into his driveway. He is seated on the driver's side of his black Ford Mustang. His window is cracked open on this mid fiftees afternoon, letting out the sounds of Lit's "Ziplock".
From the outside you can only catch a quick glimpse of him through the winshield, which is the only piece of glass on the entire car that's not tinted out black. From the quick glimpse you can see his head, with dark red Arizona
State Sun Devils hat, and dark green frogskins on. He beeps the horn and the gates on his driveway part in the middle, allowing him passage into his estate.
Once halfway up the driveway Frank kills the motor and steps out. This allows a full shot of him as he stands tall at 6'6. He has his, "Il Dio" t-shirt on, a pair of jeans, and his trademark polished Italian shoes. He looks back at the foot of the driveway to make sure of what he thought, he was right. The iWWa camera crew, sitting cozily steaked out waiting for his arrival. He slowly makes his way back to the street in his cocky, arrogant manner which he's been known to use most of his existence on the Earth.
"You're here for me, correct?" Remarks the angered Catano. "Yup," a over-weight slob from inside the van states. The man opens up the passenger side door and steps out. He has the typical Toms River, NJ trash look going for him. A pair of light blue jeans with several bleech stains on it, a red flannel shirt which is equally as disgusting. It has several holes, where the pocket on the right breast once sat there now is a gaping hole, and the several white paint stains to top it off. His hair thrown to and fro in no apparent style, and he looks like he hasn't shaved in the past month as a full gray beard grows where ever it wants.
"You wanna take this interview in the back of this van, Frank?" the fat cameraman slowly remarks. "Ehh, no. Why don't we take this to my house, did you bring anyone else along with you, someone cleaner perhaps?" The cameraman takes a look at what he's wearing with a upset look on his dirty face, "nope, only me and the sound man, he's gotta come to." "Can he takes some decent camera shots? We could just take him." "Nope." "Fine, get your stuff and follow me."
The fat cameraman sticks his head back through the window into the van, "Yo
Johhny, come on, let's get the stuff, all mighty Catano's finally ready."
The driver's side door opens and slams shut in mere seconds as the sound guy steps out. He looks to be a carbon copy of the other man, just half his size.
The sound guy has a denim iWWa button down shirt on, with several bleech stains to match his counter part's. He is totally bald, and trying to make up for it with a white beard that is held together in a rubber band sort of like Captian
Lou Albano's. He has a thick pair of black glasses on to complete the look.
"Get the camera stuff, I'll get the film and the mics," the bony little sound man yells to his friend. The fat cameraman opens the back hatch to the van and they pull out a sh*t load of equipment, ranging from several cameras and lights to a few microphones and other sound equipment.
"Where we going with this sir?" asks the bony sound guy. "Umm," Frank looks over both men in disgust, "I think we should hit the backyard with this, wouldn't want to interupt my maid."
The three of them head up the driveway which is only filled by the single
Mustang, and there seems to be maid at all, just a cheap excuse to keep the
Toms River trash out of his beautiful home. Frank walks up the driveway and hangs a right along a stone path. They approach a large wooden fence, the gate protected with a padlock. Frank walks up, tries the combination once, pulls, but it doesn't open. He tries again, pulls, nothing. He steps back for a second, re-assures the two other men, and in one swift motion brings his right foot past his head and crashing down on the padlock sending it to the ground in two pieces. A response of "Oww f*ck!" by Frank comes out as he hobbles his way into the backyard of his estate.
"Ok go set up things by the porch, I'll be right back." Frank walks into his house as he pulls out his set of keys and opens the back door. The to men take their time setting up the equipment as they wait for Frank to come back out.
After several minutes Frank is back, he's got one shoe on, and a bag of ice on the other one that he kicked the lock with.
"You know, I think I hurt my foot kicking that damned lock." The other two men let out a laugh.
"Ok, Catano, we're set", the cameraman states as he settles behind his camera. "Welcome everyone, it's your personal hero here, the Appex of Athleticism, the
Personifaction of Perfection, the Pride of New Jersey, the god, il dio, Frank
Catano. Now I know it's been quite some time since you last heard from the man hey refer to as the almighty, me. Yes, Sunday night was rough, Lunatic actually got some hits on me. But don't let that shadow the fact I got the job done on that jabroni nobody. Sure, nobodys got that close to defeating the god in who knows how many years, but hey, every kid has his one minute in the sun when they have a brief advantage over me." Frank stops as he lifts up his hat and scratches his head. He pulls it back down as he continues.
"Now speaking of kids, let's go to the cartoon, the shame of New Jersey, Vagina. The biggest threat to copyright laws since eMachines. I mean I am the god, correct? Yes. I am the pride of New Jersey, right? Yes. So Vagina thinks he can just combine them and name himself the God of New Jersey.
Personally, that just brings shame to anyone who's stepped foot in Jersey, not to mention the fact he's self proclaimed. But hey, let the little kid have his fun before he gets his ass kicked at the PPV and the true Pride of New Jersey is founded. Vagina, if you can come up with a way to cheat me out of another win this time then you can claim yourself the pride of Jersey, if not, you can shut your Jersey shore trash mouth."
"Let's see, what's next on the list ta do? Ahh, something to do for Sunday night. You know, I was quite bored the two weeks before I didn't have an opponent, and last week was well...quite fun. I mean not the fact that Lunatic
'almost' upset me, according to the awards commity, even though I kicked his ass from ringpost to ringpost, but that's beside the point. So who now you ask? I don't know. That's right, don't get all upset just because the man with all the answers didn't have an answer this time, don't worry, something will come up. Ick, you wanna schedule me a match I'd appreciate it. Or I'll just go out and find a two man party to crash."
"Ok, I'm done", Frank remarks to the cameraman. "Hey nice promo, that was pure money." "Thanks, later guys."
