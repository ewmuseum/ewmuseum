[Please Critique.] Apu Nahedaposa Penapedalon Roleplay

2 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: I am NOT looking for a fed to join [Through your TV you can hear "America the Beautiful" playing through your speakers. The screen shows clips of various American monuments, such as Mount
Rushmore, the Golden Gate Bridge, Devils Tower, The Rocky Mountains, Washington
Monument, the White House and the Capitol. And finally, it ends up showing the
Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island. A man doing a voice over with a Pakistani accent can be heard...]
-=Voice Over=- America, the beautiful. What a country. The place that makes dreams come true... [You hear a voice in the back saying "You can't say that about any thing but
Disneyland!"] Oh, I am very very sorry. I would never like to do anything to anger you Americans. It is I, Apu Nahedaposa Penapedalon, and this is my story... [The scene changes to inside a Corner store. Behind the counter, a Paikistani man is standing serving the customers with great joy on his face. He is wearing a white button up shirt and Khaki pants. His hair is combed neatly. He takes money from them and gladly puts it in the register. Apu has been doing this for many years, and he's had his run ins with criminals, but nothing ever serious.
Tonight will change his life, forever. Let's watch, and find out what happens] -=Apu Nahedaposa Penapedalon(With accent)=- Here you go Mrs. Jones. I am very glad to serve you in your Kwik-E-Mart needs. Are you sure there is nothing else that you would like? Beef Jerkey? A squishee? [You hear the woman say No] -=Apu=- Very well. Come Again! [He wipes down the counter with a cloth, and just as he is about ready to lock up to go to the bathroom, the familiar ring of the door being opened sounds. A very large man dressed all in black with a black mask walks up to the register.
All this time, Apu has had his back turned.] -=Apu=- Hello [Turns around] May I help you? Could I interest you in some Beef Jerkey? Perhands an ice cold Squishee? -=Man=- Gimme the money in the register. -=Apu=- The register? To what are you referring to? I do not understand this 'register' you would like the money from. -=Man=- Don't play dumb with me. I'll blow your head off. -=Apu=- I am not playing sir. [The man takes out a gun and points it at the register] -=Apu=- Oh! That register. Now I understand. [Apu opens the drawer. All it has is two tens, a five, and two ones. With some spare change.]
-=Man=- Is that all? Wheres the rest of it? -=Apu=- There is no rest, this is all! -=Man=- I don't think so boy. [He reaches over, takes all twenty seven dollars in the drawer, steals some beef jerkey, and then shoots Apu numberous times in the legs. Then, he even has the nerve to steal... a Squishee!]
-=Apu=- Why did you do this? Someone help! Tell that man to not come again! -=Man=- Go back to your own country, you're probably illegal anyway! -=Apu=- No sir, I do have a green card! Would you like to see? -=Man=- No, I'm gettin the hell out of here! [The man leaves, and Apu was taken to the hospital] [Flash back to the present.] [It shows Apu behind the counter again wearing a black button up shirt and black pants. He is wiping the counter down again. Apu walks around to the front to straighten the gum racks. Another man in black and a mask walks in.]
-=Man=- Hey, get behind the register and get the money, ya damn Illegal Immagrant. -=Apu=- Sir, I tell you. I am not Illegal. And I will show you my Green Card. [Apu walks up to the man, who was looking over his shoulder, and when he turns around, Apu nails him with a Reverse Russian Legsweep... The Green Card!]
-=Apu=- All you had to do was ask me to see my green card. I am not illegal, I love this country, and why do you people always try to rob me? I see these wrestlers on tv claim they are hardcore, but none are as hardcore as Apu. Do you know why? Well, I will tell you my good friend. Because no one can possibly be as hardcore as Apu, because I am constantly fired at. Do you see what I just did to that man? I put him down with a move I saw on Pro-Wrestling the other day. I like to call it the Green Card! And anyone who thinks I am illegal can see my Green Card, free of charge!
[Apu walks over the phone, calls the cops, and kicks the man out of the door.] -=Apu=- Do NOT come again! Well, I was contacted recently by Arizona wrestling. They would like me to prove myself against one Jefferson Davis. I do not know this man, but I would like to become his friend, if he would like to become mine. But if not, I'll just have to show him my Green Card. I would like to be friends with all Americans, but they just do not want to be friends with me. I try to offer them service by running the Kwik-E-Mart, but they do not want to pay! They want to steal the monies and the beef jerkies. I have had enough! I came to this great land of America because I heard it was full of hope! A man could make a decent living here. But I guess not. That is why I would like to become a wrestler. To prove myself worthy of being in this great land. If I do not, I will make all these other men happy, and go back to Pakistan. But if you would like me to stay, I will. I want to stay in America, I am a citizen! I have a Visa card! Why do all you people try to hurt Apu? Well, It's time for
Apu to do some hurting, starting with Mr. Davis. It's not personal, but I just have so much pent up anger I cannot stand it any more. I hope we can still be friends afterwards Mr. Davis. I'll even offer you free Squishee after match.
But right now, I must go to the bathroom. If you would like another interview, please, COME AGAIN!
[Fade to the scenes shown at the begining of the piece and "America the Beautiful" can be heard again]
