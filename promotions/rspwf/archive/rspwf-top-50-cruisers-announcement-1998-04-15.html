During RSWPF discussions about the RSPWF "officials" awards, someone (I don't remember who) complained about the cruiserweights not being well rememberd, due to much leagues having a Cruiserweight division totally (or almost totally) independant from the heavyweight division. So, I decided to start a Top Cruiserweights list. My predictions are that it will be a list around 50 names (see "Guidelines for Voting" for the rules of this poll), but of course if the amount of votes is high enough, it can be a Top 75, 100 or (who knows?) 200! 8) ======================= OBJECTIVE OF THIS POLL ======================= This poll was not created in order to be a definitive list of the best cruiserweights in e-wrestling world... As I know many good guys may be out of this list. But my goals here are to have fun (of course) and to make this a permanent list, as well as the RSPWF Awards, the RSPWF Top 200, the RSPWF Top 200 Tag Teams... Well, you got a picture. 8) Also, I have no relation with others "RSPWF Awards" owners, despite the fact that I voted on thier polls. 8) ================================ HOW THIS POLL WILL BE CONDUCTED? ================================ Well, it starts NOW (so soon?)! Today (April 15th) the voting process begins. I'm creating a account on Mailcity for all ballots, so I will keep an archive of them, if I decide to also send the ballots along with the list in the end of the poll. The address for sending your ballots is: [detail withheld] The voting process is pretty simple: choose your 5 favorite cruiserweights, in order. The 1st choice gets 5 points, the 2nd 4 points... In the end, the wrestler who gets more points wins (Duh). Don't forget to tell something about your choices, like feds where they compete, handlers' names and addresses, title reigns, feuds... Any cruiserweight that actually competes in the e-wrestling world is apt to be voted. Wrestlers that retired will not be accepted. The deadline for the ballot to arrive is MAY 22ND, 23:59 EST. Any votes that arrive after it will be discarded. I will send a reminder each week to RSPWF, informing deadlines, amount of ballots, and other useful info. From May 22nd to June 15th the votes will be compiled, and the list prepared... I believe it's time enough (Hey, I'm a full-time student, can't spend 24 hours surfing in the Net. Maybe 23 only.) But it's not all. The Top 10 wrestlers will have the "right" to talk a little about his wrestler (you know, like it happened with the Top 25 on RSPWF 200). So after the compiling, I'll be contacting the handlers of the "winners" and asking for a "speech" about their wrestlers. The scheduled date for the list being posted in July 4th, because, well, it's holiday! (and for me, Summer vacation begins 8) ) Sugestions are welcomed. I may change something in the rules if someone raise a good point, but by now ===================== GUIDELINES FOR VOTING ===================== Sugestions are welcomed. I may change something in the rules if someone raise a good point, but by now that's what I have. Please follow all of them, and your vote will be accepted without problems. IMPORTANT NOTE: Altough many feds have different weight limits, in this poll a Cruiserweight is any wrestler that competes in the Cruiserweight (or Lightheavyweight or Junior or Middleweight or whatever name the division receives) Division OR has a weight BELOW 240 pounds. So, if the weight limit for your fed愀 Cruiser division is 250, you're in. IMPORTANT NOTE 2: For a wrestler be considered into the compilation, he must be in at least THREE different ballots, no matter in what position. Those mentioned only on TWO ballots will be put on a "Honorable Mention" list. Those mentioned in just ONE ballot will not be considered. So start make your campaign today! 8) 1. ANSWER ALL FIELDS! Altough this may seen stupid, I'm avoiding trouble by warning first. If the ballot is not complete, your vote will not be considered. Period. (not [name withheld]'s one, but a period anyway.) If you don't know 5 cruisers (or don't want to vote) explain that in the ballot. 2. RESPECT THE DEADLINE! While I'm planning to respect it, I also ask you to do the same... Please do not complain that "I had no time to vote" or "I couldn't make the speech about my wrestler." 3. ONLY ORIGINAL WRESTLERS If anyone didn't catch it... No Jerichos, TAKAs, Syxxs, Malenkos... 4. NO FREE AGENTS! It's hard to say "this wrestler is the best" if he isnt' currently wrestling in any fed... So, only ACTIVE wrestlers by now will be accepted. 5. SEND THE BALLOTS TO [detail withheld] ONLY! Ballots posted on RSPWF and/or to any of my other addresses will be deleted or ignored without remorse. ======== SCHEDULE ======== Apr 15th-May 22nd - Voting process May 23rd-Jun 14th - Compilation of the votes Jun 15th-Jul 3rd - Contact of the Top 10 (maybe 20)/Acceptance Speeches July 4th - Posting of the RSPWF Top ???
