Tricks of the Trade, Vol. 1 Issue 1 — “How to Pull Off the Fed vs. Fed Angle”

1 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to my new RSPWF column. I know that most of you may not know me, but here's your chance to find out what I'm all about and why I'm doing this. My name is Mike (call me MC). I'm now 18 and currently attending a community college outside of Washington D.C. I have been in E-Wrestling for about five years now and I have seen it all. Unfortunately, I have grown to feel that there has been a recent demise in the quality of most feds. Federations that aren't at all innovative are popping up and acting as if they are the greatest thing on this side of the world. What can one do to help bring back things to the way they were? Well, I think I have the answer to that questions. First of I'm working on bringing back my fed ACEW (which I was forced to drop due to family problems). Then I wanted to open a service much like the E-Wrestling Central (http://www.ewrestling.com) except free. This was also delayed because of my hiatus leave from E-Wrestling. So, As I get ready and prepare to open ACEW I will begin posting this column. Then, I'll start sending it out by E-Mail as people get interested in it. Then finally when ACEW is up and running I will create a web site for my version of E-Wrestling Central which I originally planned to call "The E-Wrestling Center". It will all take time, but right now my priorities are finding writers for ACEW and getting my revolutionary fed rolling. Then this column will expand into an entire service which hopefully will fix E-Wrestling and bring it back to some decent level of quality. Bad 4 Life, MC ------------------------ Tricks of the Trade Volume 1 Issue 1 "How to pull of the Fed Vs. Fed Angle" ------------------------ Well I can safely say that not ever fed has flopped at this little angle, but there are those that have. The question remains why? Well, I hope to answer that right now for you all. Let's take a look at all the steps from planning it to pulling it off and hopefully you'll get a better idea of what you need to do in order to get the angle of it's feet and make it into a success. Step 1 - Find a federation When looking for a fed there are those that go with some friends federation and those that pick the first one they run into. Well that's a mistake either way. The federation you pick depends on what your fed is like. Both in style and quality. *Style* Extreme/Hardcore - Picking a extreme or hardcore federation for your angle would work best if you run either a traditional style fed (IE: ECW and WWF) or a Japanese federation. The reason being is that there is a mixture of different attitudes and styles in those two feds where as an extreme going against an extreme would be like one giant fed having a PPV. This gives you possibilities to cause havoc in your matches and be more creative. The traditional wrestlers would not be used to the all to violent extreme style so they would be behind at first, but then they figure it out and add their own style to extreme. Or vice-versa, this can only work best in a situation like WWF and ECW. Traditional - Same as before. Japanese - This one again works like the extreme and hardcore federations and it works with all types of feds. Things to look for would be the difference in culture between the Japanese and Americans. You could really throw those out and show them off in interviews. Then you could go on and say if you are also using an extreme fed point out how the Japanese are insulted by the low down and rotten tactics of the extreme wrestlers. While if using a traditional federation it would turn into a very classic and honorable event. Lot's of handshakes and little cheating by the heels. *Quality* You need to be sure that you don't ask the wrong fed. While they might be doing a style which creates good possibilities, they might be also to good or bad for you. You want to get someone that matches your federations. That why you have the best chance of getting two equal feds. Look for the following: - Same amount of wrestlers (It doesn't have to be exact, but not a 50-30 difference). - Same quality in role-plays. Compare their flashing with your feds flashes. Try and find someone that is as good as you or at your level. - Same quality in cards. Compare your cards to theirs. You need something will blend together easily. If not one half of the card will be good while the rest will be disappointing. Step 2 - Working out the minor details Why are they considered minor details? They are the biggest part and probably where the angle flops in most situations. All I can say is to go into this knowing your wrestlers will lose some and win some. *Set a date* The first step is deciding how you will judge the winners and who picks the winners. I suggest getting an outsider for this. Ask someone reliable to pick the winners if you feel that an argument will spring up. Once you have that ironed out it's time to move on to picking a date. First check your calendar. If you need to set the angle at a later time then hold off to see what angles build up in your own feds. If not then set a date which will allow you enough time to get interactions between each other going. *Story line* The story line should bring both federations into the angle. This can be hard if you don't do the right thing than members won't be interested. To start off most angles people either attack the president of the fed or talk trash. Well the President had better have the right character or members won't care enough. If the President is cool and people like him as a character then it will get their attention. If you go with the talk trash option then your best bet is bash everyone and leave nobody out. Pick one of their cards out and bash everyone there. Then the members will want to show up and bash your wrestlers for the insult. Be sure you keep the angle working through several cards. If interactions get low then you are losing interest from the members. (NOTE: A good habit is for fed heads to demand a lot of participation right from the beginning. Demand that members give suggestions and ideas right from day one. Then this will come a lot easier.) Step 3 - More Interactions You have gotten attention from both parties what should you do now? Well to further push a big card you need to keep those interactions going, right? Have attacks going on from both feds. Push your wrestlers by setting up matches on the other feds card. Be sure to show the match to your members on your card rather than sending out a card from the other fed. Some members won't bother reading the card. So just attach the match to your own show either at the beginning or the end. Do this and get it to a point where both federations detest each other. Then announce the big one. Step 4 - The big card Planning it shouldn't be a problem. Make sure your members know who is writing what. So all plans go right to the appropriate writer without any delay. Then a week away bring the mailing lists together for simplicity in flashing. Finally put your card together and watch the heat come to an end. Step 5 - Closing the angle You have done a ban up job with everything and everyone is happy with the card. What comes next. Well one fed needs to say "forget you" in order for you to get back to the way things were. So be sure to have this in mind right from the beginning. Overall don't expect to make your fed look better than the other. Both feds should wind up as equals and leave possibilities for any future interactions. I guess the best ending is either friendly or violent. You can end it with a brawl and saying "The war isn't over" or a handshake. It's all up to you. At this point you can't go wrong. I hope you all enjoyed this first issue. I realized just how big topic this is and I know I left some things out, but I feel that I have given you all the basics in this area of E-Wrestling. ------------------------ In the next issue: I will discuss how to begin a federation properly. What best fits your needs. What to do to get recognition. How to attract members and attention. All that and more in the next issue of ...
