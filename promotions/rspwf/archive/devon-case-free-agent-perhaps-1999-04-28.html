DEVON CASE - Free Agent Perhaps?

28 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yep, its that kinda post. I am looking to put my character, Devon Case, into one more fed. Now, I usually don't set some sort of criteria for the sort of fed I would like to implement my character into but I shall for personal reasons only. Firstly, the fed must be either well established or relatively brand new as in not started up yet. Secondly, I really would like to see either a sample card or something from a past fed run, or even a good faith estimate of how well and detailed the cards will be. Thats it really, below are two sample rps to see how ya like me or not.
RP #1 [From IWE] ----- [A small sounding pitch is heard as though it is a rustling in the leaves, scurrying about. All is still black as this overheard sound grasps an eerie and yet melodic sound as though a person is setting this up as their own amusement. The camera focuses in as it slowly fades away upwards.]
[From the angled view much can be seen as though it concerns the viewer. Leaves, red and orange from the Autumn season, are in small piles upon the ground as many large obstructions, unmarked for the most point, overshadow the rest of the horizon. These obstructions look like small Roman cathedrals and temples, soon to realize, they are just mere burial sites for those deceased from years past. A quick rustle is heard from the right side off the camera viewing angle and quicker than the camera could turn, Devon Case walks into focus kicking the leaves about as he walks.]
[Case is wearing a nice black Bulls sweatshirt from Nike and a nice gold chain around his neck, barely seen is the figure of the cross on the chain. As far as his lower torso, he is clothed in nice Levi's Loose Fit Blue Jeans, semi- white washed for his pleasure. He stands center stage in the middle between the camera and the large Romanesque burial habitat, and looks up deadlocked in the cameras focus.]
DEVON CASE - Now I know many of you have got to be sitting on the couch, in a chair, even standing straight up wondering just what the hell DC is doing in an old-fashioned graveyard. To some extent, if I didn't know myself as good as I do, I would be in the same boat as you. However, I know what runs amidst my mind and I know I do everything for a reason, and soon, oh yes, very soon, you shall come to realize that as well.
[Devon looks down at the leaves and then breathes a large sigh of what appears to be relief. He then looks back up at the high set camera.]
DEVON CASE - A graveyard. A plotted land of mystical treasures and great histories, aging back to the very colonial times of our great ancestors.
Now, all that is fine and dandy, but you know something is very special when you see me walking about it and talking about it as well. You see here
[points in a universal way] this is not only a graveyard, but really a symbolic reference to more or less myself and my career here so far in the
IWE. How you must ask, how can this defiled and deathly place symbolize your career Dev, how?
[Devon lets out a quick chuckle and then quickly changes to a deviant and criminal look] DEVON CASE - Well lemme explain this as plebian as I possibly can, so you people watching this, especially the millions of southern inbreds out there, can understand. [He points directly behind him at the large structure.]
This tall glass of water here truly symbolizes and carries out the action of death and unification with Mother Earth after death. Just like what I do to each and every single wrestler that has ever stepped in the ring against me.
Should I explain further, I bet so. [Stops and just stares intently for a few moments before continuing.] DEVON CASE - You see, I have sent numerous people to there deaths, in a symbolic sense. I am no murderer, I am no criminal, I am just that damn good.
I drove Eric Cooper to his death [smiling as though very sarcastic], I made multiple people have to retire to injuries suffered at the hands of myself. I am what some may call Devious, but what I call Clever. However, one man has escaped my grasp, narrowly, but nonetheless. Yeah Andrew Tucker, that man is you. Let me first say, I am sorry. I am sorry that you and your family, yes even your family, will have to feel the wrath that comes with f'n around with the hottest and youngest commodity in professional sports, Devon Case.
[He looks up and down his body as though admiring his chiseled physique through his heavy sweatshirt.]
DEVON CASE - It's not your fault, or is it? Did you step to me, or did I make that step towards you? Do you even remember how this all started, cause frankly I don't and I don't care to recall either cause the past is the past and the present, well that is where I make things happen. So Tucker, hope you went to college and earned yourself a useful degree cause after we meet just one more time, you will be forced to retire due to the injuries not only physically but mentally that you endured in one match against Gods Gift to
Wrestling, Devon Case.
[Devon looks around and acts as though he is having trouble seeing, with arms outstretched and the such. He quickly regathers himself as though he was playing all along.]
DEVON CASE - On to my second symbolic expression, the dark. You see, the IWE head offices struck gold when they signed me to a contract only a short week ago and then they went off and made complete asses of themselves and I. Why would you put your best showman, your best technical and aerial wrestler, your hottest young star in a dark match that will never, ever be shown on television. Are you guys looking for bad ratings or whats the deal?
[Devon starts to patter his feet on the ground, somewhat in disgust, however, he always keeps that devilish smirk on his face.]
DEVON CASE - Now, I don't know who or what I'll be facing at this dark match, but the odds are that you won't see me working at 100% and you won't see me putting on the great shows that I am capable of. Why? Because I don't waste my talent against underdeserved and underdeveloped talent as such that I will face. Therefore, I leave you with a message for the IWE head offices, keep me in dark matches and you will never ever see the true gold mine that sits in your hands.
[The camera quickly erodes to nothing before any other action could take place.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- RP #2 [From IWE] [The camera slowly yet elegantly fades in to a close-up of a beautiful fire roasting upon the fireplace. The close-up only reveals the image of the fire crackling woodchips underneath, the slow color changing of red to orange to blue as the immense heat intensifies the embers for which it is burning. A solemn voice is heard in the background.]
MAN [VO] - A fire, so basic yet so complex. Embers burn, fire unites and forms into one merely indestructible force which has vanquished millions of lives and injured oh so many more. As this phenomenon occurs so naturally and so often so does the skills of one man, 22 in years but so many more in the development of his skills and physical prowess. This man is not a mere mortal, no. He is a mere mortal with a plan, a plan which can never and will never backfire under any given situation. This man not only develops the plans at hand but also carries them out with complete and utter efficiency and thoroughness, making him the hottest 22 year old on the market.
[The air is once again silent as the crackling of the embers are quietly heard from time to time in the background. The camera starts to draw back some focus as the fire gets farther and farther away and the Olde English Red
Brick wall from which it originates from can be seen in full. There is a delicate-looking mantle piece above the fireplace which is home to various medals, and pictures of Devon Case holding up his ex-wife and ex-manager
Precious and a few pictures of him holding up championship belts of various sorts. The camera then whels quickly to the left where a nice Oak foot stool lays right in front of a large Oak chair. Sitting in the chair is 22 year old superstar Devon Case.]
[Devon Case is wearing nice Black Dockers and alligator skinned Black shoes. His upper torso consists of a nice black and white art shirt which has various shapes and shaded of black mixed with white, a real sharp looking shirt. His hair is slicked forward as the small black bristles look razor sharp adding to his clean and sharp loooking image. He looks into the camera with those solid baby blue eyes and cocky smile.]
DEVON CASE - Well, for those watching tonight, be grateful, for you have been graced with being one of few to ever see my personal study. This room which you see before you houses everything and anything important in my career, from still frame moments that will last centuries or to pictures of me and my ex- wife, the one I call Money Pincher, holding up what was most important to me at one time, titles. Now you may be wondering, What Does He Mean "At One
Time"?
[Devon pushes himself up and off of the chair, the camera follows him as he heads towards that matle piece referred to earlier. He pushes aside a few of the frontal pictures and memorabilia and grabs a silver-framed picture from the back edges of the mantle piece. He holds it up to the camera.]
[The picture comes into focus and it really isn't a picture at all but rather some sort of certificate. It states simply, "#10 Most Underrated Wrestler of
1998"]
DEVON CASE - Read that, read it all, even the fine print. This is my most cherished prize, not a chmpionship belt, not some special promotional t-shirt, not some material prize. No, this is truth and a very dark one at that. Underrated, let's take that word for a second. To me it means more than just not being appreciated by those close to the sport nor does it only mean you are just not at your peak. Uh-uh, you wanna know what it truly means?
[Devon Case puts the frame back in its spot on the mantle and fixes up the order once again. His eye become deadlocked on the camera lens.]
DEVON CASE - The true meaning of Underrated as it correlates to me, Devon
Case, is that you people just haven't seen the fullest of what i have to give. And that is a shame, not for me, but a shame for those who must face me in the ring. You see, I have injured, I have maimed, I damn near killed wrestlers in the past with my lightning and ruthless ability and here in IWE, is anything gonna be different. No, it won't be, quite simple isn't it? But how will I get cast out from the underrated shadow which envelopes me?
[Devon Case sits back down in the large Oak chair and then lays his feet crossed at the ends on the fott stool.]
DEVON CASE - As I have said before, I am not a mere mortal, but a mere mortal with a plan. And my plan is quite simple at this time, and being the nice guy that I am, I'll let you boys in on a little secret of mine. Andrew Tucker, we aren't finished yet, you think just cause our feud was cutoff elsewhere the flame wouldn't continue elsewhere? Hope that wasn't the case Andrew, cause if it is, you surely have a big surprise coming your way. And boy do i love to hand out surprises.
[Devon Case raises his left hand and strokes the chin of his face as though in contemplation. He reemerges with that trademark cocky smile and once more glares into the camera.]
DEVON CASE - In fact, upon contemplation I realized a strange yet grateful occurrance. Andrew, maybe you won't be the only one with a surprise from me in your future after all. In fact, Sid Starr, Jeffery Keenan, VD Vitrone, you boys are what I have grown up to believe as legends of the Cruiserweight division. Too bad your times are up and mine is on the horizon, only one question remains in my mind.
[The camera quickly fades to black as you can distantly hear Devon Case's voice in the background]
DEVON CASE - Who will be the one to pass on the legacy?
