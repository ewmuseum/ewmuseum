[FLASH] Tommy Grayson

31 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is an RP I wrote for a fed called the EWA (no, not the one that's been posting here lately). This is for an upcoming TV title match I have at a pay-per-view, and I think this is one HELL of an RP...probably one of the best
I've written in ages. Please read it and give me any comments, constructive crticisim, etc.
PS: I'm not accepting any offers for efeds. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [SCENE: In front of Madison Square Garden in New York City. It appears to be around the hour of midnight. The camera opens up on "The Extreme
One" Tommy Grayson standing in front of the main entrance, with his arms crossed. In his right hand, he's holding an official EWA Devil's
Night program card. Grayson appears to be wearing a black leather bomber jacket, a "KoRn: Follow The Leader" t-shirt, Levis blue jeans, and white Nike sneakers, which are greyish, resulting from all the dirt that has gathered upon the leather. Grayson's short, black hair is combed neatly, and his goatee is neatly trimmed as usual. Grayson looks up from the ground, and glances at the camera.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: You know, there comes a time in a man's life when he has to admit to things he doesn't want to admit to, and say things he doesn't want to say, but he has to. No, I'm not confessing a sin...I'm admitting that I've got a few butterflies in my stomach...after all,
I'm fighting in my hometown right here. The home of the Yankees, the home of the Knicks, the home of the Rangers, and more importantly at this moment, the home of Tommy Grayson. When I step under those hot, bright lights, and I step through the ropes with the music blaring out, I usually block everything out and concentrate on one thing: My goal. And what is that goal? To beat my opponent and to gain whatever there is to gain. But on Halloween night at Devil's Night, I'm gonna be in a different frame of mind. I can't help but hear those proud New
Yorkers sitting there, chanting my name with pride, and holding up pro-Tommy Grayson signs. I feel I have to give something back. I feel that I'm not fighting just for the TV title...I'm fighting for anybody out there who supported me through all the hard times. Ronny Wonder, you can certainly expect more effort, more drive, and more desire in me than I've ever had before...and I guarantee you, this'll be a classic match. Not only is there gonna be one hell of an ass-kicking happening, but there's gonna be one man standing tall, holding a strap of leather with shiny gold slapped on it...and you're damn right, I want that man to be ME. There's nothing you or anybody else out there can do to stop me from beating you, and winning one for the Big Apple.
Forget Suicide King for that long half-hour of your life...forget your relationships, forget everything...and concentrate on doing your best to beat my ass, which is something that's gonna be quite a feat to pull off.
[The camera closes up slowly on Grayson.] TOMMY GRAYSON: We've got something in common, Wonder...we've both got something to prove. By winning, I prove that I've still got the heart.
That I've still got what took me to where I am today...and that's the top. If you win, you prove that you're gonna be a force to reckon with. You prove that you're gonna be a major contender, you prove every damn thing that you ever wanted to prove, and you get your kicks on Route six-six. Like I said, Wonder...this match isn't just about the TV title...it's about pride, respect, and seeing who's the man.
This is much bigger than Fang versus Violent Dark. This is bigger than the two three-way dances for the cruiserweight title. This is bigger than the number-one contender match between Brett Anthony and Eli
Flair. This is TRUELY the main event. This is what people came for. They came to see a man become a man and a man remain a man. Now, I know Dean McGrath and Suicide King are out there, laughing their asses off about this...but there's one thing that you don't have that I have. HONOR. Respect for anybody who risked life and limb just to "get over" like I did. Suicide King, you've got NOTHING. All you are is an asshole who's an Andrew Dice Clay-wannabe who wants to get in the sack with chicks, and tell people to suck his brass. Just try saying that to a goddamn pit bull, SK. As for Dean McGrath, you're just another version of SK, except you actually have c<BLEEP>t in your face twenty four-seven, courtesy of Dianne. McGrath, you can just continue to mock me...and I'll just continue hating your ass. You wanna antagonize me?
Go ahead and antagonize me, motherf<BLEEP>ker. It just makes me all the more pissed each time, and I just get the craving to kick your punk-ass. I've got nothing to prove by beating you, McGrath...but I'll beat you if I have to.
Ronny Wonder, I guarantee that when the time comes, you're DEFINITELY going to... ...MEET ...THE ...END!! And you know as sure as hell that you're going to be taken... ...TO ...THE ...EXTREME!! [Grayson slowly walks away towards the end of the block.] TOMMY GRAYSON: Twenty-four hours 'till the kill. Be prepared. [Fade to black.]
