This is the first flash I ever wrote, for David "The Professional" Rune. This is exactly the way it appeared when I flashed it, I haven't changed a thing. Some people in the PWU may say this was my second flash, but I wrote my first two in reverse order, so while this one posted second, I wrote it first. Here it is:
[Fade up on a gymnasium filled with weights, skipping ropes, rock climbing simulations, rowing machines, and practically any other exercise equipment you can think of. The Professional is in the middle of the gym, supporting himself absolutely motionlessly on the gymnastic rings. The camera slowly zooms in on his face, which is locked in concentration but shows no sign of strain. After several seconds of silence, he finally speaks.]
"I've put in six years in the roughest feds you'll see this side of Gehenna, boy. I've been powerbombed through tables, dropped off cages, had my head busted open by steps, chairs, and guardrails, even got impaled on a nail once. And those were my nights off."
[A faint flicker of a smile passes over his face, very briefly, before the scene cuts to a rapidfire montage of all of these things happening to him.
Then it cuts back to him, still motionless.] "Now, Floyd, I know you just want to get paid. Hell, so do I. It's nothing personal. But I won't let anyone come between me and that International title. You step into the ring with The Professional,you've just signed your own death warrant."
[The camera slowly pulls out and fades to black, leaving The Professional still there, motionless.]
