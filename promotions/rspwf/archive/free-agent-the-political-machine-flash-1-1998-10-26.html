[Free Agent] The Political Machine; Flash 1

26 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<The scene fades into a darkly lit room as a grim patriotic ballad echos hallowly through the air. Slowly, the camera pans back to see the massive
Political Machine standing upright with his head hung low... sunken into his shoulders. Wet blond hair covers his forehead, matted down to his eyes. A torn and tatterd American Flag is draped across his massive shoulders. The camera continues to pan out, bringing the mammoth man into full view. Viens wrap endlessly around his biceps... water running down his humongous chest. His tights are similar to the flag hung over his shoulders, covered with the stars and stripes of the American Flag... shreaded into pieces. The music fades out and he brings his head up, brushes the dirty blonde hair from his eyes and stares coldly into the camera. After a moments pause, he tilts his head to the left and with eyes squinted, he speaks...>
[Political Machine]: "You should've known that the system couldn't keep me down forever. I've cracked the foundations time and time again.... and now I'm back to give it one final kick to the shins and watch your society crumble before my feet. You can seal up the cracks, but you can't prevent the scent of dominance from leaking back into your diseased world. You've always had an image to place to your patriotism. Heros of war... politions... Grandpa's shotgun... Grandma's
Apple Pie. But now in a world lined with lies and filled with hate you'll have an image to place with the downfall or your society. A REAL Fuck Machine... A
Political Machine!!"
<The Political Machine brings his arms behind his neck, grasping opposite ends off the wooden flagpole and snaps it in two...>
[Political Machine]: "This is what has slipped through the system. A politically corrupt man for a politically corrupt world! Suck it in, people...
inhale their lies... let someone else pave your distorted future. It's your world. Destroy it as you see fit. Just remember your choices when you're left to left to choke on them. True & Ruthless Propaganda... you've gotta love it!!
After all... it's the `American Way`." <As the Camera slowly fades out, a message fades onto the screen...> "This announcement paid for by the communist party of Northeastern Pro Wrestling" Mark Handler of: "Your Mother" "Sorry... I don't do macros"
