[FREE AGENT] John Davies (ISSW, GLOW, ???)

21 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cut to; the scene of the living room of ISSW superstar, "No Remorse" John
Davies, the near seven foot monster. Davies is slouched in a comfortable looking blue leather chair, with his feet resting on a small matching stool in front of him. Davies glares into the camera, which soon focuses in on his clear, blue eyes. Davies now starts to speak.]
JD: "Four matches. Two wins. Two draws. Or as I prefer to put it, four matches, four maulings. When I came to this federation, I made it perfectly clear what I wanted. Sucess, and victims."
"Therefore, the draws aren't a bad result. In both those matches,
I achieved the pleasure of inflicting more than the normal amount of pain on my opponents. I've hurt the likes of Jamie Kidd, and many others in my ISSW days. And more shall follow."
"This Monday Night though, I'll get my chance for the second part of my ambitions. This Monday, I'll enter the Seeding Battle Royal for the chance to suceed in the ISSW."
"Lets look through the list of entrants, shall we, to size up my [chuckles] competition. First up, there is Christopher. What's the matter, kid? Don't have a second name? Disgarded at birth by your parents due to your incompetence? Basically, you may have won two ISSW matches, but you ain't beaten anyone. I've watched you wrestle, you couldn't tell your ass from your elbow.
One loss already, kid -- another on Monday." "Al. E. Gator. Again, pathetic sums this guy up. Basically, you can claim to be some big shot because you wrestled over in the
SCRA. You can use your "name" to get what you want. Or so you may think. Basically, in the SCRA, you did f*** all. And this week, and in the following weeks, _I_ will make sure that you do the exact same here in the ISSW. Got that? You can intimidate the rest of the federation, but I'm not scared of you."
"Jamie Kidd. I think we've been over this. Kidd, I've mauled you too many times for you to show up on Monday Night. If it wasn't at MBC SplatterNad, then it was last week in the Monday Night
Action main event, and guess what the result will be if you step in the ring with me this week, Kidd. Mauling number three."
"Mark Maleau. Never heard of you, kid. Did some checking though, and your stats don't impress me. One win over the likes of Paul Beaver. One match followed against a _real_ star, and you lost. You can enter the Seeding Battle Royal, but you won't last long -- and that's guarenteed."
"Mitsunori Takizawa. Two wins. One Loss. Nice record, kid. You are doing pretty well for yourself here in International SuperStars of Wrestling. But I want to make one thing perfectly clear for you.
Your good run, or should I say, luck, stops here. This Monday, I'll be heading straight for you, and you'll be hitting the ringside canvas earlier than most."
"Shane Bryant Jr. I've always hated people named after their parents. It's because Bryant is a shadow, pathetic, and trying to get recognized through his father. Individual talent's important
Bryant, and it's obvious you just don't have any. Again, a nice record, but your run will stop this Monday Night."
"And finally, Richard Gracie. The self-proclaimed Ultimate Fighting Machine. Gracie, if you think your tough, wait till you get a load of John Davies. I've never liked people claiming what they're not, and expecially what _I am_. So, Graice, your near the top of my hit-list, and I'll be going after you. I've got an extra special beating waiting for you."
"All sorts of names are being thrown around for who will win the Battle Royal, but your all over-looking the obvious. John Davies. Basically, this type of match is all about power, endurance. And nobody fits the bill better than me. There can only be one winner, and your listening to him now."
"The other guys can show up, but inevitably, they'll just be..." "Hurt." "Maimed." "Killed." [Fade]
