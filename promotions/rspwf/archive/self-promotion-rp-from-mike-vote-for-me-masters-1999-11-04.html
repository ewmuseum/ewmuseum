[Self-Promotion] RP from Mike 'Vote for Me' Masters

4 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Critique, suggest, brownnose, whatever. Just react and reply, that's all I ask. ************************************************************************ [On the sidewalk of a dismal and grey city street sits a man in rags, with the words "Help Me, I'm Hungry. God Bless You" written in shaky handwriting on a large chunk of cardboard that he holds in his grimy hands. A grease-stained baseball cap with an old-school Milwaukee
Brewers logo (that stupid-ass thing with the blue glove that was made up of an "M" and a "B" fused together with a white ball in the middle...you remember that one, right?) is turned upside down at his feet, this being the man's only means of storing the pittances tossed down by more fortunate members of society. What happened to this guy is a mystery.
He may be an honest, blue collar worker who lost his job when the company moved the plant out of the country, where little brown people can do his job for 7 cents a day. He may be a once-proud professional athlete, beaten down by career shortening injuries and alcholism. It doesn't really matter though; to you and everyone else who sees him, he's just another bum to be kicked at, spat on, and generally tortured for being the painful reminder that he is that capitalism doesn't really work.]
[A long black limo pulls to a stop in front of him, and the power window rolls down to reveal the smug, pale face of Narcissa gazing contemptuously at the bum.]
Narcissa: "Excuse me!" [The bum looks up, and his eyes grow wide as he sees Narcissa's hand hold out a bill of large denomination. He starts to struggle to get his weary frame on its feet and claim this gracious donation but, as you ought to expect, he might as well have stayed where he is.]
Narcissa: "You wouldn't happen to have change for a $1,000 bill, would you?" [Raucous laughter emerges from the limo as Mike Masters, Burt Clifton, and Narcissa derive great joy and amusement from their wicked joke.
Narcissa pulls her hand back inside and the window rolls up. What a bunch of assholes...] [Cut to inside the now moving limo (don't ask questions: videotape is magical and cannot be explained) where the UWC's REAL Trinity sits in all their splendour, dressed as per usual: Masters looking like a CEO in his dark blue custom-cut suit, Narcissa looking like a nouveau beatnik in her tight-fitting black turtleneck and skirt, and Clifton looking like a NKOTB reject in a pre-torn t-shirt and jeans ensemble.]
Masters: "Once again, I implore you people to feast your eyes upon opulence that you will never have and could never handle having. And while your eyes glaze over in awe, try and keep your ears open because, like everything else we have to say, this is going to be important.
First of all, Hellkid Johnny Lords. Have we learned our lesson yet, little man? Lords...Traci's baby brother...you, not content with sitting in the center of the frying pan, have decided to take a running leap into the hottest part of the gas fire underneath. We were just going to toy with you until your misery ceased to be amusing, but now that you have committed that most cardinal of sins -- putting your disease-ridden hands on my divine physique -- I'm afraid we must now subject you to much worse treatment. We are going to put you through the proverbial living hell, where the only limits on your suffering will be set by the limits of our imaginations..."
Narcissa: "And we have _VERY_ vivid imaginations, John." [Clifton nods his head vigorously in agreement.] Masters: "Now, as for the rest of you people, I have some bad news as well. Due to the interference of Traci's baby brother and his drugged-up cohort, Art Blake and Johnny Sledge were able to retain the tag team championship. And now this week the world has to be subjected to watching these unworthy louts defend said titles against a thrown together team of...who are they again?
[Narcissa and Clifton shrug their shoulders.] Masters: "Well, the who isn't really all that important in comparison to the why. The why is, of course, because they are afraid of me, and
Clifton was more of a handful than they expected him to be. Don't try to deny it, boys. Especially you Sledge. I could hear you screaming in pain when I hooked you in the glass ceiling. You were done, and you know it. If Traci's baby brother had come a second or two later, you know I'd have those belts right now. You two cannot defeat us. We know it, and most importantly YOU know it. Don't fool yourselves into thinking anything other than this. It's not a matter of if we claim those tag team titles, its a matter of when."
[Clifton again nods his head vigorously in agreement.] Masters: "Now, as a result of their collective cowardice, I do not have my deserved rematch this week. I do get to torment Traci's baby brother and embarass the self-proclaimed tag team specialist in tag team action, but I think we can all agree this isn't going to be much of a challenge for me, [turns to Clifton] or you for that matter. I think even you people watching this can deduce where I'm going with this. This is the bad news for the rest of you that I was referring to earlier. I am going to pull double duty next week. I will be in the battle royal for the TV championship."
[As Masters says this, Clifton begins to repeatedly point to himself with his index fingers and silenty mouth the words "me too" a couple of times.]
Masters: "Sorry to crush all your hopes and dreams of championship glory, but..." [Masters throws a quick glance at Clifton, who immediately stops his non-verbal announcment of his eligibility for the TV title battle royal.
Masters slowly, mistrustingly, turns back to the camera.] Masters: "...well, actually, no I'm not sorry at all. Never mind. You're looking at the next TV champion. End of story." [Narcissa, who had been looking out the window, speaks into an intercom that presumably connects to the driver's seat somehow.]
Narcissa: "Driver, stop." Masters: "What is it?" Narcissa: "I've seen another bum...and you know I never tire of this game..." [Narcissa smiles callously as she depresses the button that lowers the power window. She picks up the thousand dollar bill, and prepares to taunt another bum. Personally, I don't have the stomach to watch them do this again, so now is the time when we will fade out.]
