Ian Walsh rp: Would like any and all comments . . .

22 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a roleplay I did for S.O.W.. All comments are welcome. {--Begin Transmission--} [Camera fades in.] [Camera drop-focuses on a landscape design. The design is held up by a rock wall. The design consists of brown top soil, light brown wood chips, assorted flowers, and some driftwood. The rock wall that holds this up is made of mostly long and flat rocks. However, there are some parts of the wall that shape around bigger rocks. The wall seems to have strategic positioning for each rock. Behind the design, there is a large oak tree. It seems as it is the master of the whole design. It looks as if this whole design is made in social classes. In the background, there are numerous trees and there is a nice, grassy lawn. There is another garden at the far side of the lawn symbolizing the line where all of this ends; a boundary of sorts. On this cloudy day, everything looks unhappy. The plants look withered due to lack of rain. However, the tree seems strong. In the background, birds are heard chirping as if they are carefree. Suddenly, a prominent and deep, manly voice overtakes any other noises, saying the following:]
"Beautiful . . . It's breathtaking . . . This is definitely a work of art. It shows the dominating factor of the oak tree. It shows the superiority it has over the flowers, the dirt, the wood chips, and especially over the lowest part . . . the rock wall. The wall may be the support of the landscape, but the tree does not need that in order to stay alive;
to stay in control. The flowers are wilted; withered and need water after days of no rain. Yet, the tree stands tall and strong, not needing rain. That is a true warrior. That is me. _I_ am a _true_ warrior. I can stand by myself. When put into a group; into a crowd, I show my prominence. I show my superiority. I stick out and I am noticed. That is something that people, sometimes, fail to recognize. That is also something that leads to a person's downfall."
"There is a man . . . He goes by the name of Louis Cyphre. He is an athlete. I look at this garden and I see myself as the strong oak tree. He, at most, is one of those flowers. Y'see . . . he has the S.O.W. Welterweight Title, a title that I cherish deeply. This
Sunday, I see that I must face him in order to achieve my goal and gain that title. _I_ am Ian Walsh. _I_ will accomplish this task.
_I_ will achieve my goal." [The camera switches shots and shows a far shot of Ian Walsh. Ian is about twenty feet in the distance. His arms are crossed. The added shades from the trees makes the area he is standing in dark and dreadful. The camera zooms in very slowly and Ian continues.]
"For years, I have tried my luck everywhere I went. I have worked hard for moments like these. I have seen men like Louis Cyphre before. Men like him come around all of the time. Men like me;
legends, hardly ever make their way along the bumpy roads of the S.O.W.. I vow that I fufill my destiny of the S.O.W. and win the title that rightfully belongs to me . . . the S.O.W. Welterweight Title. It is true that I do not come out to the ring in grand fashion. It is true that
I am more of a meat and potatoes type of wrestler. However, I also know that I am a skilled and master technician. I am a submissionist;
a master of torture. Cyphre, I am destined to wear that gold. You are just the small obstacle that I must defeat in order to get it. I'll see you
in the ring, my friend. I'll see you in the ring . . ." [Camera fades out.] {--End Transmission--} All private comments send to [detail withheld] . All public comments are welcome, too. I hope that you all enjoyed the roleplay. If not, that's life. Ian Walsh Handler of: "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh (V.C.W., I.W.F./W.O.W., S.O.W., P.W.U., H.H.W.O., N.E.W., H.O.W.L.) Participating in the Zielinski Cup II President of World Vintage Wrestling #24 on the RSPWF Top 100 Cruisers V.C.W.
