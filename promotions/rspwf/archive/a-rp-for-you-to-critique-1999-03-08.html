A RP for you to critique...

8 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Usually I get no response, but what the hell, right? I'm looking for constructive criticism on the RP. Anything that you have to offer, please do.
Hell, even if you think the RP is the best thing in the world, and you wouldn't change a thing, just let me know. I doubt that, but still...
*Scene opens in what is seemingly a biker bar. Smoke fills the room, as large men in leathers coats, sporting beards and bald heads, walk around with beer in their hands. The camera moves through the crowd, some people noticing it, others ignoring it. It comes upon the back of a man, also wearing a leather jacket, but not as large as the others, and not bald. He is picking a selection from the jukebox, and then turns as Blind by KoRn begins to blare over the speakers. As he turns around, a t-shirt is revealed from behind the open jacket. It says "Simply Legendary". The man is "The True Living Legend"
Brian J. Blottie* Blottie: Good, you're here. I invited you to this... place... for a reason. I wanted to experience, with my fans, exactly the type of environment that my opponents inhabit. I wanted to prove to each and every one of them that the wrestlers, if you want to call them that, of the NACW are nothing but indolent lowgrades. Nothing for a legend, such as myself, to EVER face in the ring.
But, it looks like the more that I talk, the more of these "men" I infuriate. *Blottie turns towards the screen of the T.V. in the bar, and sees the interview with Shaun McWhirter. He smirks, and turns back to the camera*
Blottie: Like I said, more and more everyday. Isn't it amazing, how you take on a name like "The True Living Legend", and suddenly everyone wants a piece of you. Well, Mr. McWhirter. You want to tango? Wait in line. I have more people, rookies and veterans alike, wanting a piece of me I could start my own battle royal just to see who would get to challenge me next. Am I far from a title shot? Well, with the way things are looking now, definitely not. Am I afraid that too much is coming to me in too short amount of time? Why would I be afraid of that? I DESERVE everything I get. Whether it be a match with a veteran like McWhirter, or a match with that lowgrade Big O, I deserve EXACTLY what I get in those matches. If I lose, well, then I deserved to lose. But if
I win... well, then it just means that more people will finally respect me for what I really am... a legend.
If you want to question my status, talk to Sir Smokes CRACK A Lot. I destroyed that over-emphasized jobber in one of the most THRILLING debuts in pro-wrestling history. Ask him how it felt to be able to do NOTHING but look on as I technically took him apart piece by piece. Ask him how it felt to fall victim to "Simply Legendary", the move that will take me STRAIGHT to the top here. Ask him who the "TRUE" living legend is. Plain and simply, I am the man to BEAT in the Rookie Rumble, and I am the man who will be, in the monthes to come, the man to beat in the NACW in general.
*Blottie walks to the bar and orders a Bloody Mary. The bartender brings him the drink, and Blottie pays, leaving a ten dollar tip. Blottie sips on his drink* Blottie: Not bad, for a hole in the wall. I might not drink beer, but hard liquor... that's my specialty. Yeah, I like a glass of wine, and I'm quite definitely NOT a drunk, but I do enjoy something with a little... punch to it.
That's why I DON'T drink beer. It tastes like dung, and it takes at LEAST ten to even get you tipsy. I'd rather drink something that can knock you on your ass nice and fast. Which is kinda like me. Why concentrate on moves that take nine to ten hits to knock your opponent down, when you can deliver a devastating manuever straight off the bat, and take them out with relative ease?
Big or small, I will take each of you out. And like liquor, I might have to dish out a little more to take out the big boys, but trust me... everyone falls on their ass when they're done drinking from this cup. In other words, lowgrades, bring it on. Rookie Rumble first... the top of the NACW later. Now how can you argue with that? Until next time...
*Blottie gets up and downs the rest of the drink, then walks to the door. As he walks out, he turns back for half a second, smirks, then shakes his head as the camera FADES TO BLACK*
-------------- "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie Member of the NACW... soon to prove the name isn't just a gimmick... just ask
Sir Smokes A Lot.
