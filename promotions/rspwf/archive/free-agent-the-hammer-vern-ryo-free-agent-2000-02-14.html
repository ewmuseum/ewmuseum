I don't want to be Elitist, or anything, but I'm looking to join _1_ Federation. Requirements 1] E-Mail Based. I can't stand RPing over message boards. 2] Ahh...that's it. I don't care if it's just starting up, if it's been around for aeons of time, just so long as it looks good.
A Brief Bio of my Charcer, "The Hammer" Vern Ryo, is below. Wrestler Info - Wrestler's Name: "The Hammer" Vern Ryo Age: 24 Height: 6'1 Weight: 234lbs Hometown: Hurricane, Utah Birthday: 28th Feb Theme Music: "Another One Bites the Dust" by Queen Ring Entrance: [The Lights in the Arena Dim. "Another one bites The Dust" by Queen starts up over the Arena's PA System; Some fans rise, some don't. With each beat, the music gets louder.
"OH!!! Let's go!!!" With that, "The Hammer" Vern Ryo emerges from the Curtain. Decked out in his
Black ring attire, and wearing a Black T-Shirt saying 'Your bones break against the Hammer', Ryo looks the Picture of Confidence. His eyes are hidden by his
Silvered Sunglasses; his Black hair is slicked back behind him. "The Hammer"
stands just outside the Curtain, and acknowledges the crowd, despite their distaste for him. They Clearly don't like posers.]
[Ryo just srugs off the crowd's reaction; small as it is. HE makes his way down to the ring; savouring the atmosphere. His showman like attitude makes a big thing out of this little reaction, and he acts like it's Madison Square
Garden. Posing, Struting; everything. He even goes as far as to pretend to slap a few fan's hands, but to move his away at the last minute. Ryo eventually gets to the ring, and climbs the steps. he walks a couple of steps along the apron, and removes his sunglasses and T-Shirt; handing them to a nearby staff member.
He hops through the middle Ropes and goes to the turnbuckle. Ryo climbs to the second rope and raises his hands to the Crowd. The Boos are now quite audiable;
they don't like this attitude. Ryo shrugs them off, and gets down. Being, in his own mind at least, a consumated professional, he does a few warm up exercises in his corner waiting for the match to start.]
Wrestling Info - 25 Favourite Moves: 1) Cross Face Chicken Wing 2) Fireman's Carry 3) Samoan drop 4) German Suplex 5) Implant DDT 6) Reverse DDT 7) Indian Deathlock 8) Fisherman's Suplex 9) Doulbe Leg Takedown 10) Drop Toe hold 11) Hammerlock 12) Northern Lights Suplex 13) Figure Four 14) Bulldog 15) Ankle Lock Submission 16) Side BAckbreaker 17) Elbow Drop off the Second rope 18) Top Wristlock 19) Aztecian Suplex 20) Side Suplex 21) Spine Buster 22) Belly to Belly Suplex 23) Million Dollar Dream 24) Full Nelson 25) Sleeperhold Primary Style: Mat Technician Secondary Style: Submission Wrestler Finisher and Description: "The Hammer Blow" - 3 Rolling German Suplexes, with the Last one going into a Bridge.
Setup move: "Ryo Reveler" - An Airplane Spin into a Samoan Drop. Strengths: Stamina - Ryo has excellent Stamina and is at his prime 15 minutes into the match. He is able to go on for quite a while, wearing the opponent down, and using submission holds to force him into quitting or a pinning position
Wrestling Knowledge - Ryo is a student of the game; he combines an excellent choice of moves with knowledge of weak spots in the body and pressure points.
Mental Strength - Ryo is strong mentally; he dosen't worry about is opponent's comments about him or events which don't apply to him.
Weaknesses: Strength - Ryo is not a particulary strong wrestler; whilst what he has can get the job done against people his size, he struggles against big guys who take it away from a traditional wrestling match.
Arial Ability: Ryo rarley goes to the top rope, and knows that when he does there is a large chance the move won't be sucessful. This can be a problem against crusierweights.
Sensationalism: Ryo thinks everything is the bigger event, ONLY if it involves him. This attitude can lead to him posing for the crowds, and giving opponents a chance to turn the tables.
Personality - Orientation: Semi-Heel, nutreal in his interviews but not in his actions. Personality notes: Ryo is calm; and won't snap on opponents. Cheats: Frequently. Foreign Objects: Definitly. Wrestler Description - Looks: Face: Ryo has middle length black hair, which is usually slicked back behind his head with enough Grease to Sink a Ship. His eyes are blue; and his face is rather Long and thin. His noise is thin, like the rest of his face, and is what you would call a 'Roman Nose (Ken Shamrock)'. His mouth is small, and his ears are middle sized. his face is clean shaven, usually with a couple of cuts on it from regular wear and tear.
Upper Body: Ryo is not what you would call 'Buff'; instead is your average, well built for his style wrestler. HE is pretty Definied, and his skin tone is very pale. his arms are not Hogan Style 24 inch Pythons, but look pretty porportionate, and still give a definted muscle when he flexes. His chest is smooth; and not hairy at all.
Lower Body: Once again, Ryo is not Buff; but well built for his style and pretty defined. His calves are pretty big; but his feet are size 10. His legs are not overly hairy.
Ring Attire: Head: When Wrestling, Ryo wears a gold necklace with a Crucifix on it. Coming to the Ring, he wears a pair of silvered Sunglasses.
Upper Body: Ryo wears nothing on his upper body; apart from a pair of black elbow pads with 'VR' on them in white, bold type. he also wears finger gloves, which are black
Lower Body: Ryo wears short pants, much like Bob Holly. these are black, and on the Back have 'the Hammer' Embroidered on them in White Cursive. Down the Sides some Acid Green Arrows point to the floor. the Trunks have a Green Trim. He wears the Same design on his Knee Pads as he does on his Elbow Pads; 'VR' on them in White, Bold Type. His boots are black and old school' no fancy designs on them.
