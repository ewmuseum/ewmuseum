**Possible Free Agent** Tony Paseno

9 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is one of my best flashes in a while, if not ever. If you like it and you want me in your fed then write me or something. Send me some info on your fed and if i like it then i might join. No gaurantees that i will join any fed, but there is always a possibilty. If anyone see's anything wrong with the flash, or something that i should try to do another way, then please post your comments or email me or something. Here it is.
[The scene opens up with a shot of a wrestling ring in the center of a huge arena. No one is in the arena. It is completly empty and completly quiet. The only light on in the arena is the one directly over top of the ring.]
Voice: The battleground. [The scene cuts to a shot of the same arena jam packed with wrestling fans screaming their lungs out. Many are holding up signs. The camera pans through the crowd getting shots of the signs that are being held up. It goes all the way around the arena one time and then hits the center of the ring. The letters
EIWC are written in the middle of the ring. All of a sudden you can't hear the fans anymore and when the camera pans up there is no one in the arena again.]
Voice: Once a week these people come out to see the greatest stars on earth compete for the crown. Every man wants to be on top, but only one man will be able to come out victorious in every match. That leaves fifteen men that must be left on the bottom.
[ The scene immediately cuts to pictures of the wrestlers who are competing in the EIWC. In no particular order it shows Jack Vars, James Pillow, Fang,
Westside Walavan, Arthur Essex, CandyMan, Shawn Crimson, Tyrone Parker, James
Miller, Randy Acorn, Brian Farmer, Evan Manchestor, Jimmy Cerone, Damien
Cooper. After all the pictures have flashed on the screen it goes back to the ring.] Voice: These are the fifteen men who must be left on the bottom. Leaving only one man who will stand on top of the mountain.
[Bitter Peace by Slayer starts playing loudly as the scene cuts to a shot of
Tony Paseno walking out from behind the curtains of the same arena that was just shown completly empty, but the fans are there and they are cheering loudly. Tony is wearing black leather pants with "Italian" written down one side and "Stallion" written down the other leg. He has on his silver spandex tanktop and he is wearing his trademark sunglasses. His jet black hair is slicked back into a pony tail and his goatee is neatly shaved. Tony starts walking down the isle when all of a sudden the scene changes back to where the arena is empty, but Tony Paseno is still walking down the isleway, in the same spot where he was when the scene switched and the music is still playing. Now
Tony Paseno is wearing black jeans, a white t-shirt, a black leather vest, and black boots. He keeps walking to the ring and as he walks up the steps onto the ring apron the scene switches back to the jam packed arena with the fans cheering their lungs out. He steps over the ropes and walks to the center of the ring. He stands in the center of the ring while the fireworks start going off from the corner posts. The fireworks stop as the scene cuts back to Tony
Paseno standing in the middle of the ring while the arena is empty.]
Voice: This is the man who will be the last man standing. Those other men will be sitting at home watching him walk away with the crown, wishing that they were in his shoes. Wishing that they had the talent that he has. He is "The
Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno and whether you know it or not. He has already won the EIWC Tournament.
[The camera starts getting a close up of Tony Paseno.] Tony Paseno: Listen up people. You are now looking at the hottest wrestler in the world today. I am doing nothing but rising to the top no matter where I go and it ain't gonna stop here. There is a lot of talent here in the EIWC
Tournament, but that means nothing while I am here. All the talent in the world isn't gonna help any of you bums. You come out and you try your hardest everytime you step into the ring, but it ain't gonna matter in the long run.
[The scene switches to where the arena is jam packed and the fans a screaming their lungs out while Tony stands in the middle of the ring. Then the scene cuts back over to the empty arena.]
Tony Paseno: Now I see that this week I must face a man by the name of Fang. Man, this guy really scares the hell out of me. Just the name alone should make any man wanna forfeit the match, just so he wouldn't have to face this guy. For those morons out there who don't know sarcasm when they see it, i just smacked you in the face with the biggest load of sarcasm that any of you will ever see. The name Fang doesn't mean a thing to me. I really don't care what this guy wants to call himself. I am gonna advance in this tournament after i beat him and that's just something that he is gonna have to deal with. I don't care if anyone likes it or not cause it's gonna happen no matter what.
[Tony pauses and takes his sunglasses off.] Tony Paseno: Fang, you take a good look at me right now because I want you to remember the man who is gonna make a fool out of you and eliminate you from this tournament in the first round. I want you to see how much better i am than you. I'll give you a little peice of advice; after i beat you this upcoming
Monday night, go and find a tape of our match and you watch it a couple thousand times and see where you screwed the match up. I don't think that you will ever see where you actually messed up, so I'll go ahead and tell you. You screwed up when you stepped into the ring with me. I always hear people blame their losses on a move that didn't go the way they planned or some other B.S.
like that. But the truth is that the only mistake any wrestler really makes is when they sign a match against me. Another thing that I think that you will realize the first time you watch the match all the way through is that I am the greatest wrestler in the world, but I am sure that you and everyone else in this tournament already knows this.
[The scene cuts to a shot of Tony Paseno executing his finishing move, The
Brooklyn Back Breaker on some unknown wrestler. Then the scene cuts back to him standing in the middle of the ring, in the empty arena.]
Tony Paseno: Now this is what you have to look forward to Fang. I see nothing but pain in your immediate future. You know what my finisher is now. You should be trying to figure out a counter for it. There is one thing that you should remember though. I might not use that move this Monday night. I have plenty sitting in the back of my head and i will promise you that any one of them will make you wish you were anywhere but in the ring with me. Now, you sit back and you think about what you have gotten yourself into. Maybe next time you won't make such an ignorant choice.
[Fades to black.]
