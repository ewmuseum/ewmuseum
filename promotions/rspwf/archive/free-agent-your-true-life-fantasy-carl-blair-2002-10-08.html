Here's my application: -- PERSONAL INFORMATION Your Name: [name withheld] Your E-mail Address: [detail withheld] IRC Nick: AIM Nick: [detail withheld] Availability For Matchwriting: Never, actually I'll write my own victories!! :) (I run a fed of my own) -- WRESTLER INFORMATION Wrestler's Name: "Your True Life Fantasy" Carl Blair Hometown: Orlando, Florida Height: 6'3" Weight: 245 lbs. Age: 26 Wrestling Style: Technical/Flyer Alignment: Heel w/female following Theme Music: "What's yo fantasy?" Ludacris Detailed Appearance: Picture Shawn Michaels, you've got YTLF. Long ash brown hair often pulled back in a pony tail or wetted back, blue eyes, a very defined physique. His grin says it all, cocky and arrogant and his facial expression will let you know it. His initials "CB" are tatooed on his right bicep.
To the ring YTLF sports black spandex pants, with "Fantasize" embroidered on his ass in red. The pants have a striped/swirled design in gray and his knee pads also read "YTLF" in small red letters. His boots are black and have chromed out heels. Often times he wears the latest t-shirt design, then rips it off once inside the ring. Black tape covers his hands and wrists.
Background: Born Carl J. Blair Jr. to father Carl Sr. and Kate Blair. Raised in a upper middle class family, wrestling was his dream, or more. . . his fantasy. He idolized Ricky the Dragon Steamboat as a kid, and later went on to train at the Keening School of Grappling Arts from there he joined CCWF, where he never captured any gold but vied for the World title on several occasions. Following CCWF was Great Lakes Heritage
Wrestling, where Blair mustered to win the Television title from
"Iconoclast" Dietrich Jones in an iron man match. GLHW closed down leaving YTLF to emerge on the AWMC where he conquered for quite some while. Joining forces with Chris Del Rio, "Sweet" Jarred Bombay, and
Ajari Joan to create International Incident. There he won a Motor City championship before jumping ship to join Team EGO over at Global Pro
Wrestling. Team EGO was made up of Madison J. Valentine, Victor V.
Hill (who later departed) and Tommy Gunn. The trio dominated, but
Blair found no gold. Which led him back to AWMC where he found himself another Motor City title run. After AWMC closed it's doors Blair found
Deep South Wrestling, where he joined alliances with Damien Churchill and later Harley Phirestiener to dominate. Churchill had the World title and Blair had the Southeast, the secondary. This made Blair jealous and Phirestiener and him turned on Churchill ending the dominating group known as Thrillogy. Deep South Wrestling closed it's doors before the feud could be ended, Blair has still yet to snag a world championship. Which leads him to. . . WLW

Resilience - YTLF isn't a big guy, he takes a ton of punishment often times taking more than dishing out, but somehow he always finds a surge of energy and comes back. 2. Speed/Agility - Although very hated, his moves in the ring often put on a show that captures the crowd in awe and amazement. He's a true athlete. 3. Cockiness - He can get into your mind, piss you off and sucker you in. He knows what he's doing when he talking trash, he's throwing your gameplan off and trash talking is something YTLF specializes in. List three weaknesses: 1. Plays the crowd - After a beautiful manuever instead of following it up he'll play to the crowd, luring on their jeers. 2. Size - He's not the biggest or strongest to enter the ring, he often takes alot of punishment when facing larger sized foes. 3. Cowardly - As cocky as he is, YTLF is no idiot, he knows when he's outnumber or been outdone and isn't afraid to run from sticky situations. Basic Moves: 1. Left & Right Jabs 2. Dropkicks 3.
