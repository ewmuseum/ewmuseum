[AWMC] "Devastating" Higor Rosa (Critique or something like that........)

15 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey you guys, I have just done my first RP in nearly 3 weeks or so. I usually don't post RPs on RSPWF, but this is a special cause. Please feel free to throw whatever type of responses you'd like, negative or positive. Just nothing ignorant/arrogant. Thank you.
[We are on top of a three story building in New York City. To be exact, we are in Woodside, Queens. The temperature is approximately 35 degrees Fahrenheit.
The time of the morning, as shown on camera, is 8:23 AM, Eastern Time.] [Across the roof of this three story building, there is a man standing there. All we can see him wearing from the behind angle is a pair of blue jeans, and a black leather jacket. His jeans are folded up, so we can't even get a shot of what kind of shoes then man wears. The camera angle moves in closer towards the man.]
Man: I have finally made my way into the AWMC. A federation I tried to get into before, but just wasn't good enough. Now I am, and what will stop me from letting loose? Nothing. Who will stand in my way to get to the top of this federation? Nobody. Why is that? Because I am just too good for anybody to contend with me. Higor Rosa shall reign supreme in this company. I will make an impact on the wrestling world through the AWMC.
[As the man turns around, we see that it is indeed, The People's Drunk himself,
Higor "The Rebel" Rosa. Higor Rosa moves closer towards the camera. His leather jacket is closed tight to keep himself from getting cold, and he is wearing a pair of light brown work boots.]
Higor Rosa: "The Rebel" just won't do it anymore for me. That nickname has lost its touch a little. I mean, its 2 and a half years old. "Devastating"
Higor Rosa. I will hold that for as long as I feel like. I just got sick of being a rebel. That was when I was a rookie. Now I must move up from being a rebel. That is mostly why people can't take a guy like me seriously. I needed a change and it has happened.
[Higor stops for brief moment. We can see the white smoke from the very cold
New York air coming out from his nostrils as he breathes. Higor walks back towards he end he was on before and looks down the building.]
Higor Rosa: My first opponent in my AWMC debut is this man known as Bloodhound. Bloodhound, taking me lightly is the last thing that you need to do right now. You do take me lightly and you will pay in the ring in a rather violently way. If you think that I am joking, just try and take me lightly. To me, I don't really think you deserve to wrestle against someone with such high standards in the ring as me. Everything I've accomplished throughout my career I did solo without needing anyone's help. That is exactly how I will ruin you this week.
Solo, so that which I can enjoy kicking your ass. I can't wait until Explosion. That is the night that I will make my debut and start to take out the worthless crap that is always thrown at me. I don't think I'll be out of the title pictures out here for too long.
[Higor turns around.] Higor Rosa: After the way I will defeat Bloodhound, people will notice how much
I am truly worth. I don't see myself not in the title picture here for longer then two weeks. I am more then what most wrestler can take. And this week,
Bloodhound will find that out. Until next time. I'm out of here.
[Screen slowly fades to black, as Higor walks past the camera.] Higor Rosa ECWF President and damn proud Handler Of: "Devastating" Higor Rosa #141 On 1999 RSPWF Mid-Year Top 200 Poll.
