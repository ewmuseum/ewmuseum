-Iron Curtain- Sample Rp "Art of War"

28 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ivan "The Iron Curtain" Ramius Sample Segment. Title: The Art Of War The scene is a dark deserted train station. The circa 1950's lights are flickering, and the train creeks and clatters as it decends through the station. The men and women in attendance are not there because they want to be.
Rather , because this is their only means of transportation. The lights go dim...and the station starts to shake. The monster train halts before two old pesants. Communists, men and women who have been a part of the political machine...known now as the 'old guard'. The train hatch opens, and the married couple ventures inside. Inside the train is a world of amazement. The sheer beauty of the communistic engineering is all inspiring. The woman takes off her glasses, and rubs her eyes. Acrost from her, a man now sits. He is tall, slender, and very inconspicuous. His deep black hair is cut short, and a trench coat hides much of his body. She looks to her rifght..then left. Two more men sit around her, she starts to feel uneasy. Her husband, named Nikta, shares in her fright, inside this most beautyful of trains, lies the darkest element.
Though they offically disappeared with the Soviet Union..many are still there, and their power lies in the manipulation of the truth. Yes, the KGB.
Nikita moistenes the tip of his thumb with a little saliva pinched out his cigarette, and dropped the stub into his shirt pocket. Later, he would add the tobacco inside that of the other stubs, and reroll them, wasting nothing. He needed a smoke more than anything now. His nerves taunt. His concentration pushed to the limit. His role in the former Soviet Union had led him here. A
KGB escort to the Russian Capitol City of Moscow. Where he would await further orders from the Russian supreme council. In his half century of life, Nikita
Gerashinov had grown all too familiar with the monsterous things nature could do when it turned hostile. He had lost two sons in the cholera epidemic just four years ago, and his lone daughter in an earthquake two decades ago. Not to mention part of his farm in floods that had swept acrost the grasslands when the river overflowed its banks. The lines and wrinkles on his face were a record of the hard times he had weathered.The somber depth of his eyes spoke of survival dispite bitter loss.
He was not a man who had a great need for physical comfort, or believe it was anyone's due. That way of thinking was alien to him; he could not understand it. His ancestory, that of a mere Russian Farmer. Whose people had been cultivating the soil for centuries, he had an inborn belief that it was enough to work an persurve dignity. To complain, or wish for more, might only bring a curse upon oneself, and provoke the world into another cruel demonstration of it's power.
He knew what the earth could do. Nikita had felt it's wrath, and somehow lived through it. And yet today, sitting here amid the train roaring down the track toward his destiny, among the men whose job it was to keep him in check.....Today he felt angry. And scared. He had come along way in his career in Moscow. But he had retired to a better life. A life of love, and family, and peace. He didn't feel the need to come back to the stress of national politics.
But he had been summonded by the General Secretary himself. He could not turn him down. Something was in the works...something huge was happening. He knew not why he was involved, but he knew that the Mother Russian he knew and loved..would never be the same.
"What is past is prelude...." .....The Future Is Now.... [ The two Russian genuises, making the weapon, named Sergei and Igor...or code named Nikita. They were on a raised platform, hovering over the bomb. They were connecting cables, making sure every little detail was taken care of....Days earlier Igor and Ivan had used workers to lift the bomb casing out of the ground. They transported the shell of the weapon, now they were building it from the inside out. They had the tritinum. From the Soviet "black market", its amazing what 500,000 american dollars can bring. Tritinum, the most expensive material on earth, ten times more expensive than plutonium, and 100 times harder to get your hands on. They were almost finished with the weapon, the great equalizer the world's economy needs...]
Scene: Athens Greece. The Iron Curtain has been contacted by Sergei and Igor for a mission. He has no idea what he is to do....The Iron Curtain see's Igor driving a late model sedan, and gets into the car.
Time: 12:30 PM The Iron Curtain settled back into the seat. He didn't bother with the seat belt. If he had to escape the car, he didn't want to be bothered. The driver of the car was a good one, manuvering in and out of traffic, which was lively here. He didn't say a word, and neither did the other men. That was fine with the Curtain too.The big Russain moved his head to the side, and scanned forward, looking for a trap. His eyes flickerd around the inside of the car. No obvious places to hide a weapon. No visible microphones or radio equipment.That didn't mean anything, but he looked anyway. He didn't have complete trust of his "comrades".
Finally he pretended to relax and cocked his head in a direction from which he could see best. His hunter's instincts were taut and alert this morning. There was potential danger everywhere.
The diver took what seemed to be an aimless path. It was hard for Curtain to be sure, of course. The streets of this city had predated chariots, much less automobiles, and later concessions to wheeled vehicles had fallen short of making Athens a Los Angeles. Though the autos on the street were tiny ones, traffic seemed to be a constant, moving, anarchic logjam. He wanted to know where the diver was taking him, but there was no sense in asking. He was committed to this course of action, he knew. It didn't make him any more confortable, but to deny the truth was to lie to himself, and the Curtain was not that kind of man. The best thing he could do was to stay alert. And that he did....
The Iron Curtain counted a bunch of cabs. They had to cater to tourists mainly, or other people who didn't care to drive in the local traffic.....thats odd. It took him a moment to figure out why. Oh, sure , he thought, it's dome light wasn't on. Only the driver. Most of the others had passangers, but even those without had their dome lights on. It must have been an on-duty light he judged.
But that one's was out. Curtain's driver had it easy, taking the next turn to head down toward what appeared to be something akin to a real highway. Most of the cabs failed to take the turn. But the one with the light out followed them arund the corner, 50 yards back.
"Were being followed", Curtain announced quietly. "You have a friend watching your back"? "No". The drivers eyes immediately went up to the mirror. "Which one do you think it is"?said the driver.
"I don't think sport. It's the taxi fifty yards back, right side, dirty-white, without the dome light on. He's made two turns with us.You should pay more attention"Curtain added....wondering if this was the trap he feared.He figured he could kill the driver easy enough. A little guy with a skinny neck that he could wring as easily as he killed a morning dove, yeah, he thought, that wouldn't be hard.
"So I see" said the driver. Well, this changes our plans somewhat." The drivers mind was racing. Unlike the iron one's. Who now knew that he wasn't set up.Though he still didn't trust his comrades, no police or intelligence officer would have given him that warning. Well, he though so.
"It might be necessary to do something about this", said the man in the front seat, passenger side.
"I don't have a gun", said Curtain. I do, said the driver, but I prefer you don't use it. How strong are you? By way of answering the Curtain reached out his left hand and squeezed the driver's right knee.
Driver: You have made your point. If you cripple me, I cannot drive. Have you killed before? Iron Curtain: Yes. I can do that... Ready? Curtain: Yes The car { A european made '"Fiat" } stopped breifly. The Iron Curtain had already removed his jacket and tie, still wondering if this could be the final act of the trap, but he didn't really care anymore. What would happen, would happen. He flexed his arm, and walked back up the street.
Seargent Spiridon Papinicalaou, CIA--European operations division, increased the speed of his taxi. If they were heading into narrow lanes, he hoped he could maintain visual contact with the Fiat without getting any closer. Well, he thought, if they had identified him, he could always call for help. This work was dangerous...
As he approached the corner, he saw a man standing on the sidestreet, reading a paper. Neither of the men he was "shadowing", this man wasn't wearing a jacket.
But his face was turned away, and he was standing akwardly. The Officer smiled dryly at that---but that smilled disappeared at once.
As soon as the CIA officer was fully onto the sidestreet, he saw the Fiat, no more than twenty meters away from him, backing up rapidly towards him. The man stood on the brakes to stop the taxi, and started to think about reversing himself when an arm reached acrost his face. His hand came off the wheel to grab it, but the powerful hand clasped his chin,and another seized the back of his neck. His instict to turn and see what was happening was answered by the way one hand wrenched his head to the left, as he saw the face of this giant
Russian. He felt his vertebrae strain for a second, and snap with an audible sound that announced his death to the Iron Curtain as surely, and irrevocably as a bullet.
The Iron Curtain waved, and the Fiat reversed yet again, and slammed into the taxi. The driver flopped forward, leaning on the stearing wheel with his broken neck.
The Curtain smiled to himself as he leaned into the Fiat..he thought to himself, gee that wasn't so hard.
He's dead, let's get the hell outta here! Driver: Are you sure? Curtain: Ya, im sure . Broke his neck like a toothpick, he was a little pencil necked guy. Driver: Like me you mean? The driver turned and grinned. He'd have to dump the car, of course, but the joy of the escape and the satisfaction of the killing was sufficent to the moment. He had found a comrade. A worthy one. They would need such a great man for this mission. A mission of the greatest imporance.
Your name is? Iron Curtain: Call me Ivan [fade] Ivan "The Iron Curtain" Ramius CNWL, INWI, NEW, IeWC, Select feds. J-Cup hopeful.
