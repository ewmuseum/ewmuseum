[Roleplay] Opinions Wanted Please...

25 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***This is a new character. I am far better with my eerier guy, the Iceman and haven't done roleplays of this kind in awhile. Lemme know what you think. Thanks!*** -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [scene: a beautifully furnished family room. A hot, crackling fire churns within the limestone fireplace. On the mantle sit copper picture frames, with action photo's of several wrestlers, with their signatures in golden pen across the bottem. One is unmistakably, "Detroit's Own" the Iceman.
Another, is the retired daredevil, HardCastle. And last, the also retired,
Stone Fox. Other wrestling memorabillia marks would only fantisize of posessing. Out of no where, a man wearing worn, ripped jeans, and a white t-shirt that reads "HOT DAMN!" on the front in flaming lettering.
The man in obviously very young, 19, maybe 20. He kicks his feet up showing his bright white Adidas running shoes. He fishes the remote from the oak coffe table, and clicks on the 50" television. Pressing play,
WrestleMania III begins. The man begins to speak in a semi-cheerful voice...]
MAN: Tis that time. Hide your daughters, lock up your wives, and, well your mom is probably a LITTLE too old, TNT has arrived!
TNT: For those of you who actually wonder what my cute lil' nick name means, no, not the explosive, The Newage Tyrant. Yup, if you brainless blunders haven't gathered, I'm one of those annoying kids who just relishes in PISSING off the oposition. And oh how you all shall despise me, my friends, and the ground I walk on! Butt (not a typo), lets just slice the salami...I'm here to suceed, and humiliate. Sucess, of course will be my primary goal, but humiliation will be handed to anyone who crosses my glittering golden path to greatness.
[Snatches a leaflet of papers from a folder sporting the CAWF logo.] TNT: Well, skimming the list I've been given, I just couldn't help but notice this yuppy. MANABEING. Now just for my entertainment, explain to me in detail what the HELL THAT MEANS!?! What, you couldn't think up anything creative, so you drew some crayon letters, cut 'em out, then moved them around untill they made something that ALMOST resembled a name?
While your searching Websters good book, get the definition for this one too...STUPIDITY! YOU'RE DRIPPING OF IT PAL! But seriously, if you can drag your blood crusted ass out of bed after your World title match, I'd like to dismantle you in front of the world. I advise you to fit me in your schedule, or I'll fit you with a new hat that will strangely resemble a folding
chair. Get the idea. Buuutttt, since I just know the CAWF suits won't book us for a week or two, I'll fit you with a cute nickname...
[drumroll...] TNT: Manibite! That's great! Certainly more entertaining than Manabeing. It certainly suits a putz like yourself better also. Well kiddies, as much as I'd like to tear a new asshole for all the other CAWF "athletes" who are presantly sitting at home on their second hand couches drinking a sun faded can of Old Millwauke, I gotta zoom. I shall return, I'm just enjoying myself to leave this soon. HOT DAAAAAMN!!!
[camera slowly backs to the side of the couch showing Hulk Hogan body slam Andre the Giant under the roof of the Silverdome. As 90,000 fans go completely berzerk, the Hulkster becomes the first man in 15 years to defeat the 8th wonder of the World.]
