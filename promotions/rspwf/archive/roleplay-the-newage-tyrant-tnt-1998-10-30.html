ROLEPLAY - The Newage Tyrant (TNT)

30 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(screen fades into a television studio. There's a desert backdrop, and sand on the floor. The camera shifts to the left, and sees a camel, being groomed by an Arab looking man. The camera then slowly moves to the right, as stage hands and grips shuffle back and forth. The camera comes upon a makeup table. You see the back of a man's head, wearing a huge headress, and Middle Eastern clothing. He's squirming in the chair, as a woman struggles to apply the makeup. She finally conceeds to the man's stubborness, as the man let's out a high pitched,
"WHOOOHOOO!" He then hops up from the chair, removing the bib, and turns around to be startled by the presance of the camera crew.
It's none other than TNT. His white and green head dress has the Sprite logo on it, as he begins to explain...)
TNT- See....ummm......errrrr......uhhhh.....hmmmm.....damn.... is that thing on??? (An stage hand walks over and whispers in TNT's ear, and he flops over to the set. The director says action and he says his lines....)
TNT (in a nervous, mechanic voice) I-drink-Sprite-after-I-am-done-body -slamming-my-opponents-through-the-mat. And.....this shirt is chafing the HELL outta me!!! (The director yelps "CUT!", and begins scolding TNT.) Director- That's the FIFTH damn time you moron!!! Your costing us money here! TNT- What'd you call me you dungeon and dragons playing, scooby doo underwear wearing, chess club leading, slick hair parting, blue sock wearing, BASTERD!?!
Director-Uhh... TNT- THAT'S IT! ........I'M SNAPPING YOUR SUSPENDERS! Director- NOOOOO! (They begin chasing each other around the set, and finally two burly men wearing black snatch TNT up by the arms.)
TNT- Hi boys. You remember me? I'm Foster's buddy! (wry smile) Security- Yeah, we remember. (sinnical grin) TNT- DAAAMMNNN! (The screen goes black. A red lettered caption reads, "AFTER 1 HOUR AND 45 MINUTES OF CHASING, SCREAMING, NAME CALLING, AND CATFIGHTING...") (The screen then fades into an alley scene. On the beige brick wall, two silver doors fling open, and a man comes flying out on the ground, as if being thrown. Its TNT.)
TNT- SILLY BASTERDS!!! I'LL START DRINKING MOONMIST!!! I SWEAR!!!! Oh, hello again. They promised me a liftime supply of Sprite. Wouldn't you do it!?! Anyways, let's change the subject.
Budden, after a 12-pack of Sprite, I'll be ready to maim you, dismantle you, and leave you in a heap. This Saturday will mark the downfall of PoE. At the hands of who you ask??? THE NEWAGE
TYRANT!!! THAT'S WHO! It's my turn to burn baby, and inside the confines of the cage, you will feel the unrelentless wrath of TNT!
(Pulls a Sprite out of his head dress, unscrews the cap, and takes a long swig, then begins to walk.)
TNT- Budden, you're are just like the lots of other men I've left lying on their backs. You say the same things, throwout the same empty threats, and expect me to be intimidated by them. Well the bullshit ends now. I'm sick of this superficial crap. It's time to get it done in the ring. And I hope you aren't planning to exercise your technical skill, because this isn't going to be a wrestling match.
This is going to be me kicking your ass untill it reaches your throat and you choke on it. (smiles) I hope you're ready, because Saturday,
I'm fixin' to do the hookie-pookie on your face.
HHHHHHOOOOOTTTTTT DDDDAAAAAAMMMMMMNNNNNN!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (TNT slams the remainder of the Sprite, then tosses in to the ground. He adjusts his head dress, and flops off into the city...)
