[Critique] Blake Carter's Roleplay

5 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Time: 5:30 P.M] [Place: Restaurant] [Scene: The scene opens up in front of a table in a restaurant. Sitting at the table is The Extreme Wrestling Federations very own, superstar Blake Carter.
Sitting across from Blake is Steven Spence, an interviewer and ring announcer for the EWF. They are both waiting for the waiter in the restaurant to come and give them menus. While they are waiting Blake begins to speak.]
[Appearance Description: Blake wears a pair of nice khakis and a buttoned, long sleeved white shirt. Blake weighs about 225 pounds, and stands at 5 foot 9 inches. He has a strong muscular build, and is rather thin individual. To most women Blake is considered an extremely handsome individual, with black short hair. That is highlighted blond. Blake also has dark blue eyes.]
[Blake Carter Speaking: Hey, Spence if you want to start this interview now, before the waiter gets here, that would be fine. It doesn't matter to me either way. Because one way or another I will get what I need to across.]
[Scene: Blake, then stops talking and waits for a response from Steven Spence. Blake then coughs right before Steven Spence replies.] [Steven Spence Responding: Sure that would be great Blake. Well I'll get started; First off, I'm sure every one would like to know just exactly what your intentions are in the Extreme Wrestling Federation?]
[Scene: Blake sighs, and shifts his body to face Steven Spence, then he answers] [Blake Carter Speaking: Well Spence, that question I can answer easily. My intentions are to make my name known. That where ever I go after the EWF my name is known. And that's what I plan to accomplish, with many other goals.
One of my intentions now is to defeat Guru in an upcoming match. And you can be assured that I will also defeat Paul "The Bear" Zane also. Both men will be defeated. No matter their physical attributes. They can try as hard as they like, but they should know I am at %100 percent, and am not in any condition to be defeated. After they are defeated, I will move on, to harder and greater challenges. One by one, I will work my way to the top. Until I am the highest in the game of the EWF. That being the greatest champion in EWF's industrious history.]
[Scene: The waiter walks up to the two of them and them both menus. Blake looks lover the menu, obviously thinking of what he shall order. He then sighs, and tells the wait what he would like for his meal.]
[Blake Carter Speaking: Well, I guess I will have the rib eye, and a glass of milk. What about you Spence?]
[Scene: Steven Spence looks up from the menu and says "oh", then gives his order to the waiter.]
[Steven Spence Speaking: I think I'll have... how about just a salad for me. With ranch dressing and, a soda.] [Scene: Then the waiter rights down both orders and walks away. Next Steven
Spence starts off where he left off earlier in the interview.]
[Steven Spence Speaking: So Blake, we all listened to you yesterday, and I've got to tell you, you had a lot to say. A lot of goals, and dreams I would think. And today the EWF received thousands of letters in the main and online asking one question, Is Blake delusional? I think I will let you answer that yourself.]
[Blake Carter Speaking: No, I am not delusional. The problem is that you do not see the difference between the future I tell, and the dreams you hope for.
What I said earlier is a dream, a dream that will become reality. There is a difference because the fact is I will accomplish it. You can quote me on that.
My dreams will become a reality, trust me. Soon I will have defeated the competition, and become the top of the game in the EWF. Nothing's going to stop me. I've been quoted saying it before, I have nothing to lose, and everything to gain!"]
[Scene: Once Blake finishes speaking, the waiter comes to the table with a glass full of milk that will go to Blake, and a glass full of soda that will go to Steven Spence. Steven Spence then in return says thank you, and begins the next question, while Blake takes a drink from his milk.]
[Steven Spence Speaking: I know you've talked about Guru before, but before our food gets here do you think you could make a few last comments on your upcoming match?]
[Blake Carter Speaking: Well, you know it's the same as I've said before. I have nothing against my opponent, Guru. But there is a job to be done. In the end there has to be a victor. And that outcome must be in my favor. I will do anything and everything in my power to guarantee my victory over Guru. This is my chance to prove my worth, and I will not fail. Here, now. This is my time, my time to become the champion, the victor, the icon. Nothing will stop me from reaching my goals; I have the will power, and the determination. Believe me when I say that I will defeat Guru.]
[Steven Spence: Well, thank you very much Blake, but if you have any time would you like to comment on your other match coming up? I believe you will be competing against Paul "The Bear" Zane.]
[Scene: Blake sighs, and takes another drink out of his glass of milk, as
Steven Spence takes a drink of his soda. Then Blake answers.]
[Blake Carter Speaking: Spence, there's no difference. I will upset his victory, and defeat Paul "The Bear" Zane the same as I will to Guru. I am prepared for any opponent they put me up against. It really doesn't matter to me, because I have the determination. I can defeat any one if I work hard enough for it. And that's exactly what I plan to do. When everything is all said and done you will see my hand raised at the end of both the matches. I will defeat them both with a single maneuver. A maneuver I would like to call
"The Plummet" and once it is supplied, that will be the end of the match.]
[Steven Spence: Thank you for your time Blake, I appreciated it. And I'm sure all the fans of the EWF will to. Hey it looks like your foods here匽
[Scene: The waiter comes to the table once again, but this time with a tray of food. The waiter sits the plate with the rib eye down next to Blake, and the salad next to Spence. The waiter then leaves the area, as Blake and Steven
Spence begin to eat. Next the camera fades out slowly until everything is pitch black.
