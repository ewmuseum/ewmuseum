"Dangerous" Dusty Love RP

15 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Dusty Love, Givin' Some Lovin'" >>The scene shows a very large and beautiful hotel. This hotel is in Detroit, Michigan. It is known as the "Wilson World" hotel. This hotel has everything that anyone would ever want. The camera scans over just a few of the magnificent things that you can do while you stay there, such as: an arcade room, an inside heated swimming pool and hot tub, an inside tennis court, and the last place the camera shows is the huge bar. The camera continues to move on as it passes many well-dressed people and many, many, rooms. Some of the rooms have signs on the door that read, "Do Not Disturb". In front of some doors, there's trays with champaigne on ice. This is a very HIGH CLASSED hotel as you can see. The camera continues to move, up the stairs, around corners, and through hall-ways until it finally comes to a halt. The camera stops at a room marked... "69". Someone that's with the camera man knocks at the door.
There's no answer. Then the camera looks on the door knob and it has a sign hanging that says, "Do Not Disturb". We hear a voice say the word, "Ooops."
And then the door cracks open. A voice is heard... "Who is it? Can't you ready?" The reply from the man with the camera man is, "Sorry to disturb you, we're from the IWC. You wanted an interview?" The other voice then says,
"Sure. Just gimme a minute." The door closes once again and then a man stands in view with a microphone. He is an unrecognized worker for the IWC. He now begins to speak.<<
Interviewer: Hello everybody, I'm outside the hotel room of the IWC superstar,
"Dangerous" Dusty Love. He requested an interview so we're here. I'm hoping to get a few answers from him myself. I've followed his career throughout the
UHW days and now here in the IWC days. He's a brilliant wrestler as we all know, but he'll be getting in the ring with a lot of other guys that are just as good as he is. He's been working hard and training for this BIG Battle
Royal, and I think he could come away with a win and the North Eastern
Heavyweight Championship Title. He could very well do it, but he'll have to go through numerous other stars such as... Hardcore Champion, Al Robertson, Robby
Blaze, Harry, James Pillow, Candyman, and many, many, more. He says he's ready, but we'll find out on Monday night. This will be his first match in the
IWC, but he's been hanging around for a few weeks now. Two big moves that the other wrestlers will have to watch out for are the "Love You To Death" and the
"Lover's Leap". These are two of Dusty Love's finishers and can be hit in various positions. But when it hits you, you'll be feeling it for a long, long, time...
>>The door opens up all the way now and interrupts the man while he's talking. Dusty Love stands in the door-way wearing nothing but a robe. He's got his sexy long blondish-brown hair slicked back and he has a big smirk on his face.
He motions for the crew to walk on in. They do so and they're amazed by the huge, and fancy hotel room. Dusty Love then begins to speak.<<
"Dangerous" Dusty Love: Well, well, well, guys. I'm glad you like it. I stay in this hotel everytime I'm here in Detroit. I just "LoVe" this place. But man, you really need to work on your timing, pal. You kinda interrupted something. I got a hot blonde babe in the bed room over there that's waiting on me to return to her. I gotta make this kinda quick if you don't mind.
Interviewer: No problem, Mr. Love. I just have a few questions that I have to ask and then I'll leave you alone with your "babe" back there. First, can you give me your thoughts on the North Eastern Championship Battle Royal at Monday
Madness?
"Dangerous" Dusty Love: I've been talking about this thing for days upon days now. I'm kinda sick of talking. I'm ready to actually climb in that ring and take care of business. I'm ready to get in there and take what's rightfully mine, and that's the IWC North Eastern Heavyweight Championship Title. I've got the money, I've got the cars, I've got a REALLY nice house, the hottest chicks... I've got everything I could ever want. But there's only one thing missin' and that's that Championship belt. But come Monday Night, it won't be missin'. It'll be sittin' over this shoulder right here. >>Points to his right shoulder.<< It's just a matter of time, bro. Dusty Love's bound to get it, and this Monday, it'll finally happen. I've been sittin' back doin'
nothin' the last few weeks I've been here, but I'm sick of that crap. I'm ready to get in the mix of things and it all starts this week. The IWC has seen stars come and go, I'm sure of it. But none like me. There's only one
Dusty Love. People might try to talk like me or act like me, but go back in that bedroom and ask her how many of me there are. She'll tell ya straight UP that there's only one, and that's yours truley. IWC, Dusty Love's here, so ya better watch out.
Interviewer: What do you think of what the other's have said about... >>Dusty
Love interrupts.<<
"Dangerous" Dusty Love: Blah, blah, blah... They can talk all they want to. Talk's cheap, but it's a whole 'nother story when you step between those ropes and square off in the squared circle. They can say whatever they feel like sayin', but the fact of the matter is, when they get in that Battle Royal and step face to face with the "Love Man", they won't be able to back a damn thing up. Just like Robby Blaze flaps his jaw about how he "CAN and WILL" beat me on
Monday. Robby, I've known you for a while now, and you've ALWAYS been second to me. And that WON'T change here in the IWC. I CAN beat you but you CAN'T beat me. That's the way it is, was, and ALWAYS will be. And Monday, I'll prove it to you once again. I'm a former Champion, but the funny thing is,
I've never seen you holding ANY gold except for the watch on your hand, and that's FAKE anyway. Or we can go back to the Candyman talking about how I
"have no chance." Or when he said that he should be the Champion because he
BEAT Nevermind. You might have beaten him but you shouldn't be focusing on that. You should be focusing on what you see right here. >>Dusty points to himself.<< Focus on this because this is YOUR problem. You'll have to go through ME to get that Championship title and to tell you the gospel truth...
I don't think you can. All these guys come out and blad on and on about how they're the best and this and that... But Monday night, only one man will survive and reign supreme over the rest... And you're lookin' at him.
Interviewer: As you've said before, you're just now returning to the ring after a few months break. Do you think you'll have any ring rust?
"Dangerous" Dusty Love: Of coarse NOT. I've been wrestling my whole life and once you start, you never stop. I don't think the lay off will have ANY effect on my skills because I am the best at what I do. And when you're the best, you can do whatever you want, whenever you want. Plus, I've been training hard and working out the entire time. I never stop workin'. I have to keep this body in tip top shape for the ladies, bro. And trust me, you don't look like I do over night. And I'm in the best shape of my life. I won't be rusty, I'll be at my BEST for this Battle Royal, and I'm not comin' to finish second. I'm second to NONE. You, I, and everyone else knows exactly what I mean.
Interview: Well, that's all I have for you today, Mr. Love. I wish you the best of luck in the North Eastern Championship Battle Royal. And all the rest of your matches here in the IWC as well.
"Dangerous" Dusty Love: Thank you very much, but I won't need luck to win. And one last thing to the guys in the Battle Royal... Bring your best, because
I don't want any excuses to be used when I DO kick all your asses. Now, if you'd excuse me, I have a little business to tend to in the bedroom.
Interviewer: Thank you for your time, Mr. Love. See you at the Joe Louis
Arena. >>The camera man backs up as Dusty Love walks into the bed\room. You see Dusty Love open the rope as he walks in, but you can't see anything because he has his back towards us. The door shuts and the camera leaves the room. The camera then fades out.<<
