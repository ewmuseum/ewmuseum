[Free Agent] The Political Machine; Flash 2

26 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<The Scene opens to see the Political Machine standing in the lockeroom in full costume. A mixture of water and sweat runs down his body as he shakes the dampness from his hair. A row of lockers lines the wall behind him, each plastered with political campaign stickers from the years past. Memoribilia of past presidents including Nixon, Carter, and Reagan fill the background. The
Political Machine raises one tremebling hand and runs it through his blond hair. He takes a step to the left, revealing a black and white banner carrying the likeness of the father of Communism, Karl Marx, pressed against the wall where he stood moments before. He glares onto to banner with a slight grin, then he speaks...>
[Political Machine]: "There's the man... the filthiest philosopher of our time -- rolling in his grave as we speak. Rolling with LAUGHTER as our government falls apart piece by piece... crumbling under the Democratic spotlight. I can't help but think that if he were standing here now, he'd piss in all your faces..."
<PM tilts his head back, letting out a long overdramatic sigh. His head violently snaps forwards and the slight grin increases... stretching from ear to ear...>
[Political Machine]: "... but he can't. He's a little busy resting in piece. It looks like someone is gonna have to do it for him. someone is gonna have to piss in the face of YOUR leaders... The Mike Legacy's... The Tru Souljah's...
The JT Savage's... the whores lining the street corners of NPW. Any voulenteers?" <PM's grin fades away, replaced by a sudden look of seriousness, and he whispers...> [Political Machine]: "Yeah... Pick me, Karl... Pick me." <The grin slowly finds its way back to his face, as he smirks into the camera>
[Political Machine]: "The lines were drawn Sunday... and that bitch Wright was the first to cross it. In the future, maybe it won't come down to using 150 pound announcers as ragdolls. Maybe in the future, one of this promotions `Big` stars will be down there to peel your ring announcer's ass off the mat. Or better yet, maybe next time, one of these `stars` with their pretty long hair and `cool, flashy tights` will step into that ring themselves and let me chew their heads off. Instead, you fools let some skinny bastard with a mic. take a fall when it *should* have been some grunting ape with gold around his waste.
Don't worry... I'm not losing sleep over it. Either way, I got my point across. Line up your talent. Hell... line up the whole damn faculty. Stick that Tru
Souljah in the ring with me and afterwards you can ask him if a 40 oz. tastes just as good alongside the head!!"
<PM holds up a piece of paper and a pen> [Political Machine]: "Here's your legal binding document, Mr. Souljah... as if you're educated enough to read it. And here's the pen I'll put through your eye if you too fucking blind to see this as a challenge."
<PM signs the bottom of the paper and uses the pen to tack it into a nearby bulletin board>
[Political Machine]: "Ya see... unlike America... Communism is an equal opportunity employer. And unlike The Legacy's, the Savage's, and the
Souljah's... The Political Machine is an equal opportunity Destroyer! It's the way of the world, people... and even if it means driving EVERY wrestler in this promotion through the mat and straight to hell, you're leaving with a greater respect for *my* politics. The politics of NPW and the politics of the WORLD!
Make the choice... or just like the political machines, you'll be CRUSHED by the opposing forces!!"
<PM turns to the banner behind him and drops to his knees in prayer as the camera fades out...>
