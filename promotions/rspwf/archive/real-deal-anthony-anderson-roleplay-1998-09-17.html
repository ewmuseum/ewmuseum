I see RSPWF Polls are coming around yet i havent even been posted on not one of them. So i guess I gotta do something to get votes right? So check out the rp and enjoy. Also I'm a free agent and is acceptin apps, enough room for 2 more feds!!
[The scene opens inside the home of Anthony Anderson. A cameraman is wondering around inside. The camera is on, and captures multiple views of the foundation.
The cameraman is struck with amazement with the beautiness of the building.As they wonder around he hears screams and noises from upstairs. He hears the sounds as he creeps and tip toes on up the steps trying to following from where he heard the noise. He then travels up to the third floor as he comes to a room with the door partially cracked and a glimpse of light that peeks out. He slowly pushes open the white door as he sees...]
[...Anthony Anderson laying next to Miss Ashley Dawson with his hand stretched out around her shoulders. He then turns in the other direction and sees the television on as he hears girl emotional screams, sounds. It hits him, that it's a porno movie that he heard earlier downstairs, he walks into the room and
Anderson quickly gets up and get into his face arguing. The cameraman immediately apologizes and sees can get a few words with him. Anderson is in a pair of blue jeans and a white shirt that has Tommy Hilfiger written in blue and red and nothing else but his white socks. Anthony then escorts him down stairs to the living room as they sit down and he clicks on the tv.]
[name withheld] - Hey, I'm from ICW Studios. We're putting together a special edition the day before and we thought we'd come to you first. And we was just wondering can we get a few words with you on this coming Friday Night Fallout?
"REAL DEAL" ANTHONY ANDERSON - Ahh, it seems everyone is bothering me these days. What kind of show is it? Some kind of dicumentary or something?
[name withheld] - No. It's a special show, showing the appreciation for the ICW fans. More like an interview show. You know, like that Barbra Walters show.
"REAL DEAL" ANTHONY ANDERSON - Fans are special aren't they? They need so much attention. Sorry, cant help ya.
. ..WAIT.... "REAL DEAL" ANTHONY ANDERSON - Alright already, I'll do it. So what is it that you want to know?
[name withheld] - Well so far, you're doing so good in the ICW, and we see that you're placed in the _BIG_ battle royal coming up this Friday Night. We the fans are wondering, what possessed you to participate in this BR?
"REAL DEAL" ANTHONY ANDERSON - I took it as a _GREAT_ opportunity. An opportunity to get known as well as getting my shot at the pride and joy or what some would say...a shot at the International Championship. Um..if you didn't know that's what everyone is here for, to strive for that one particular title. But some people are here to set there own goals but I guess thats them.
It would be an pleasure, an honor for me to go up against the top notch superstar in the ICW, which would be Tony Paseno at the moment. It's every man's dream to become champion. Now I figure that I backed myself up into a corner with 29 other competitors, count, twenty-nine. That's like be a pack of
RIBS thrown into a wolves. But the consequences a man will go through to claim, to latch, to capture a dream or goal of his. I consider myself the lucky one, because right now at this point...I feel sorry for those other twenty-nine other superstars who has to step into that squared circle. In the state that
I'm in, I'm more dangerous then _EVER_ and you know how a crazy man acts when he's pushed to fight! This is for all the marbles...for instance... Mark
McGwire has broken the HomeRun Record, thats something he has strived for, fought through and conquered. Do you expect him to leave it at that? No...he's trying to go further and deeper and become Home Run King. It's no different from wrestling!!! It's something he took in as pride to be the only man to break the record in what, over thirty years? And that exactley what I'm trying to do...push myself until I cant go no more. And i tell ya this, I will walk out going onto face the man himself, the International Champion.
[name withheld] - You know you have some hard competition in here. This isnt some easy task. This will take everything out of you...
[Anderson interrupts him] "REAL DEAL" ANTHONY ANDERSON - Do I look stupid to you? Huh? Dont you know that I know it'll take every thing out of me? Just shut up dumbass and let me do all the talking alright. What is a match with no competiton? Then the match is simply boring...its an easy win and Anderson dont accept no easy nothing. I worked too hard to take an easy route. If I known that...dont you think I would've done that my whole life. All I can do is walk into that ring and give it all that i got. And hopefully I end up still walking at that. [chuckles] No one can predict my future but me and thats exactly what I'm doing...leading into my future, future to be champion that is! I'm not in this Battle Royal to just get a mark in my win column, but to hurt people. Hmph...and I wont leave until I've done that. I dont just speak so you can listen andjust pass up the words thats I'm trying to get clear to you...but soon enough you'll feel what
I'm saying. Frankly, I'm looking forward to this thing, day after day I continue to wake up in broken down cold sweats of dreaming of what may go down this Friday Night, some may be good, and some may be bad. And just for some...it might be worse then you could possibly dreamed! I promised myself that when in fact I do win this thing that I will not settle for being second best. Hell no. I wont stop until I'm at the top! You only live once is what they told me, and I damn sure plan on living this life I have to the fullest, you can count on it!! Some people out there run their mouth alot...it's like checks you cant cash. But thats how some people are, just talk. Like they say,
"if you can talk the talk, you damn sure can walk the walk". We'll find out on Fallout wont we? [At this moment, Ashley Dawson comes down the stairs wondering whats the hell going on in a white silk like robe. Shawn turns his head in attention to miss
Dawson. Anderson turns his head back in the other direction. She smiles and then sits next to Anthony. Miller speaks with a HIGH TONE]
[name withheld] - Hi Ashley! I was just asking Double A here a few questions. What do you think of these other 29 other wrestlers entering the ring with you.
Recently, Kevin Kopeland spoke a few words, what do you say about that? "REAL DEAL" ANTHONY ANDERSON - As i said, I can think more or less of these people. But right now, I'm thinking much low of them. i can't speak for
Kopeland, he'll just be one of the many sorry people who walk out empty handed. He ran his mouth so much, even for him. The only thing that'll be laying down is your dumbass Kevin, yeah, on the outside mat wondering what happened. Well lemmie tell you, I'm what happened, cause I threw your sorry ass out. Look around you son, does it look like I'm playing around? No, this is as serious as that stank feet problem of ya old lady!! For you and all the rest of the bunch of losers, you all will fall, at the hands of...
... _ANTHONY ANDERSON_ ... ...I will not leave until you all are, broken, bloody and battered!! Once that is done, there's only one thing for Anthony Anderson...and that is TONY PASENO!
And that my friend, is the final chapter. Night after night, all i can think of is this Battle Royal, and when I do walk into that ring, you'll see ANTHONY
ANDERSON throw in every last ounce of sweat, heart and soul in to that match. Never, I mean _NEVER_ will you see the REAL DEAL lay down for any one. This is for all the nuts as a squrriel would say.Kopeland, Tim Beck and all the rest of you...beware because the clock is still tickin and it aint over til the final the fat lady sings.And as the bell sounds as if its your calling card to complete destruction. Beware and Bewarned...because once the Blue Light Special is slap on any of you, you'll know the true meaning the REAL DEAL.
[name withheld] - Thank you MR. ANDERSON, i really appreciate it! See you folks around some time. Onc again, Thanks.
...ANTHONY.... ...F'N... ...ANDERSON... ...LIVE... ...WITH... ...IT... "REAL DEAL" ANTHONY ANDERSON - Alright...it's been real kidd. Peace, catch ya around one of these days.
[Shawn then exits the foundation and takes off in his `97 Expedition as the scene then fades out to black]
