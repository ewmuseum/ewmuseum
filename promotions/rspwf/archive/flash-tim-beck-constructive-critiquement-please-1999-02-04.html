[Flash] [Tim Beck] [Constructive Critiquement Please]

4 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in.] [We see the inside of a mansion. A luxorious room. A leather couch sits in the middle of the room, with miniature oak tables on each side of the couch. A lamp sits on one of the oak tables, and a TV sits in front of the couch, where we here a rewind of IMWA Preview, more specifically the
Tim Beck\Scott Richards match. We now pan to the left slightly, more head on with the TV. A single leather recliner, sitting in it the one, the only, Tim Beck.]
[Beck is attired as usual, in a blue Structure Sport T-Shirt, and a pair of white cargo khakies. His brown hair is wetted-down to perfection, and he smirks slightly as the IMWA camera focuses on him for the first time.
A wink.] TB: IMWA, 'ay? Another place for "The God Damn Show" to dominate. Another place for Tim Beck to destroy the rankings. Another place for Tim Beck to look
_damn good_, but then again, for me, that ain't too hard.
[A smirk.] Scott Richards. Ya' think you're better then me, kid? Ya' think ya' can honestly beat Tim Beck in the wrestling ring, hoss? Ya' think you're gonna get the one, two, three on Tim Beck this Friday, the sixth of Febuary?.....
Ya' _think_ wrong....... Ya' say good ole' 'TGDS' is over-rated and that you can defeat him? Ya' say that one day mah' mouth is gonna catch up tah' me and I'm gonna get a big ass kickin'?
[Another wink.] Ya' say _wrong_....... Richards, that's simply all you are. Wrong, wrong, wrong. A mistake. A has-been. A nothing. An absolute flaw. A bug under Tim Beck's rug.
Richards, ya' think you're the greatest thing going today. But Richards, you know what happens after tha' bug comes out from under the rug?
[Beck slams his foot down.] Squash. You will be sqashed this Friday, and I will defeat you as if you were nothing. You will be the first of many victims Tim claims here in the
IMWA. The legacy of Tim Beck in the IMWA begins this Friday, Richards, and ya'
sure as hell be ready. 'Cuz if ya' ain't, I'm gonna stroll to that squared-circle. I'm gonna be the better man, and I'm gonna get a big, _fat_, dubbelya......
Nevermind.... [Beck winks.] I'm gonna get a big, _faaaat_ dubbelya anways. [A smirk.] And Richards, that's just the way it is, whether you like it, or not, Tim Beck is a better man. Tim Beck will win this Friday, and Tim Beck is......
THE..... GOD..... DAMN...... SHOW! [Beck continues his smirk as we fade out.]
