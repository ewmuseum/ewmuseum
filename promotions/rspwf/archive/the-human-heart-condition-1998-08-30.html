***THE HUMAN HEART CONDITION***

30 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Appearance:A muscular and somewhat tall man with long, coal colored hair and black eyes. His pupils (eyes) are black in color.
Ring Attire:Wears a Long black trenchcoat, black trunks w/mathcing knee and arm gear. Black motorcycle boots with a silver spur on the back left heel.
ENTRANCE: Arena lights go off, the theme music begins to play while the lights remain off. Minutes later <BOOM> the lights are back on and "HHC" Shane
Sexton is standing in the ring.
Theme Music:"Storm Trooper" by Ted Nugent Wrestling Style:Powerhouse/brawler When in trouble: Powerhouse Cheating: (If so, Weapon?)The silver spur on the back of the left boot. He will take the boot off while the ref is down or distracted and then hit opponent in the face with the silver spur.
Brawling Style:Win at ANY and ALL costs. III. MANEUVERS ============== 1.Reverse STF 11.arm bar 2.vertical backbreaker 12.neck ringer 3.Suplex 13.head butt 4.step over toe hold 14.sleeper 5.Choke hold 15.belly to back suplex 6.standing neck breaker 16.shoulder breaker 7.atomic drop 17.chest breaker 8.eye gouge 18.elbow smash 9.power slam 19.spine buster 10.pile driver 20.boston crab Finishing Move: (Up to Two)Cardial Pulminary Breakdown Description:Heart punch Signature Maneuvers:Standing Neck Breaker, slapping of the victims face repeatedly. Setup Maneuvers:Power bomb, power slam String Moves:Belly to Belly (3x) these require a lot of flipping or lifting 1.Multiple Power slams 2.Multiple Pile Drivers 3.Multiple suplex's Real Wrestler Relations:The younger, sexier version of Kevin Nash and Scott
Hall combined. Background Information on Wrestler: CPW United States Champion (Former), APCW World Champion (2x, former) SAMPLE ROLEPLAY: (Important. It doesn't have to be a new one. One previously done is ok.) ***Sample Roleplay*** ###Setting### {In a church, near downtown Peoria, Illinois sits a man. He is watching a preiest perform his sacred acts during a mass, he is thinking of life and what people will do to try and escape fate.}
######Begin Transmission###### ###Scene### {As the priest continues his sacred rituals, the man clad in black attire with his cold, piercing black eyes begins to rise to his feet. He turns away from the priest and looks to the camera, then begins his walk towards the front alter. Once their he grabs the priest and is set to deliver the man of God's fate. However, he lets him go and turns to the audience.}
"HHC" Shane Sexton: You poor fools. You spend what precious time you have in hopes that their will be a God that will grant you more time on this hell called earth. While their may be a God, this I do not doubt, it is I who tell you that not anything that you do will save you from your fate in life. It is called destiny. You pray to your priests and your blessed virgin Mary for absolution and while you are told that you are receiving that absolution, you still do not believe.
{Turning in disgust from the audience, he goes to a holding tank that houses a quantity of "Holy water." Putting his left hand in it, he brings it back out and let's it drip and then turns once more to his congregation.}
"HHC" Shane Sexton: If God is love and love is God, then why do you people insist on breaking his commandments and then each holy day, beg for absolution only to return to your vile ways? I care not for you or for your religion. I am my own religion and my own destiny. I make and create my own breaks in this world and I am not nor will I ever care to help anyone but myself. Not long ago, I spoke of people trying so desperately to play God with people's lives.
Doctors, lawyers and even law enforcement officials try to do this. {He stares down at the ground, then quickly looks up to the congregation and replies.} "HHC" Shane Sexton: I am a man of pain. I am a man that brings pain. I believe in putting fear into the hearts of every man, woman and child on this planet. And, I intend to inflict as much as possible for my express enjoyment.
{Suddenly the lights go out in the church and then....<BOOM> An explosion is heard, people are screaming and then the lights come back on and the man is gone.}
######End Transmission###### Shane Sexton Handler of "THE HUMAN HEART CONDITION" Shane Sexton ^^^^^-----------
