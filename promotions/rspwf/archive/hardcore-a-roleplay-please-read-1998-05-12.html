This is a roleplay that I have just done nad it shoots about a lot of people...please tell me how the roleplay is...please tell me any improvements I can make...it would be greatly appreciated...THANKS...
-------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------- [SCENE] [The scene opens up to a shot of HaRdcOrE outside of the EWA Control Center...yes the EWA Control Center...he is with Steve Gorecki...Gravedigger...and...a Masked Man...HaRdcOrE is wearing a pair of black oversized ripped jeans...he has a pair of black Timberland boots on and a black shirt that says..."DON'T F*CK WIT ME" in red bloodlike letters on it...he has a black Boston Red Sox hat on with the "B" colored in white...he also has a pair of Oakly sunglasses on...Steve Gorecki has a pair of old ripped jeans on with a black shirt that says..."DON'T SCREW ME AGAIN" in white letters...he as always looks pissed off...Gravedigger has a black shirt on that says
"BLOODBATH" in red bloodlike letters and a pair of oversized black jeans on...the masked man has a pair of black jeans on with a bloodbath shirt...his mask is black and has red blood dripping in the front...they are standing outside of the EWA Control Center arguing with a security guard dressed in a blue rent a cop suit...we will go to the conversation]
[HaRdcOrE] But we are on the appointment list...check right there...yeah there...we are uhhh...John Morrus's butt buddies...
[SCENE] [HaRdcOrE almost throws up when he says that...the security officer looks at the list...he looks up and speaks]
[Security Officer] Ok...you are in...but there is a note on here that says..."IF YOU ARE IN THE
MOOD...GO TO STEVE ROCK'S ROOM"...aside from that here are your passes]
[SCENE] [The Security Officer hands the 4 men EWA passes...then the men walk through the doors...they look around...then they stroll over to the map under a waterfall...it reads..."STEVE SICA'S OFFICE 3rd FLOOR"...as soon as the men read this they start up the escalator...when Steve Gorecki starts to talk]
[Steve Gorecki] HaRdcOrE why are we here again?...This place is not exactly the best place to be...I mean Tony Arnold will probably get pissed at me for leaving the state...
[HaRdcOrE] Well Steve we are here because I have a little business to take care of...and it starts and ends here...
[SCENE] [The men reach the third floor...then they ask a security guard where the office of Steve Sica is...he point down the hall...the 4 men start to walk down the hall...when they get to the outside of the door they huddle up and the camera can not make out what they are saying...then they kick the door in...when the camera gets a shot of what is happening it automattically cuts out...but what they got a glimpse of was Steve Rock and John Morrus
69ing...when the camera turns back off...it shows the 2 men laying on the ground...the Hostile Takeover is standing above them...they all of a sudden take a can of black and red spray paint out of there pockets...they tag SWL and
Hostile Takeover all over the walls...then they run down the hall and down the escalator...HaRdcOrE knocks an old lady over...then they run out the door and jump in the 1998 Ford Explorer that is parked out front...the camera man is in the backseat with them...HaRdcOrE starts to talk]
[HaRdcOrE] Well EWA...we have started the war all over again...we do not give a fuck what you people want to do or say...but we will always be one step ahead...this is no DX scam...this is a Hostile Takeover mutha fuckin
JOB...anyways...Sica...Morrus...Rock...Reality...and whoever the hell else...if you want some...come get some...anyways on to the match I have with a bunch of no life scum bag bitches...KrAzE...Mislan...Cassaday...all of you are the epitemy of the word SHIT...Today I am gonna focus on Anthony Mislan...do not think that the rest of yous are off the hook...cause you sure as hell are not...anyways...Gravedigger...you wanted to ask me a few questions about
Mislan...go ahead... [Gravedigger] Okay HaRdcOrE...we all know that the last time you and Mislan hooked up the result was you loosing the Heavyweight Title to him...what seems to be your response to this ludicrest event?
[HaRdcOrE] Well Digger...the first time we met you are right...I lost the belt...but guess what...this time there is no belt for me to worry about...there is just 3 guys...and the one that I am gonna get the most REVENGE on is Mislan...he took the biggest thing that I have had since the XWA Heavyweight Title...so
Mislan...ecpect me to release all of my fury on you... [Gravedigger] Okay...now onto the title reign that he has had so far...what do you think about it? [HaRdcOrE] Well to tell you the truth...how does a man with the heavyweight title only fight 3 matches?...He has fought you and PrimeTime...and I think there was one other...but he has had the belt for 1 month almost...what the hell is that all about...then he calls himself a fighting champion?...That is some straight up
BULLSHIT...if I were the champ i would request that I fight every night...so in my books he is no champ...unless it is the HOGAN champ
[Gravedigger] Well what do you think about the match on Monday? [HaRdcOrE] Well I know that Mislan is gonna get an ass kicking...but who is it gonna be from?...it will definately be from me...what about Cassaday...I know he wants the Heavyweight title somewhere along the road...and then we have KrAzE...what will happen when these 2 sWo members are in the same ring and they must fight?...I think that that will be worth the price of admission...So
Mislan...consider yourself a marked man...When you expect the expected...it is gonna be a suprise...PEACE
[SCENE] [The EXPLORER pulls up to a red light...then the Hostile Takeoverthrows the camera man out of the car and peels out with him behind...the camera cuts out]
