DSMWF Promo

27 April 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[CG] Hello everyone! I'm Chimp Gale along with the Guy with Pocket Change, Theo de Biased, here to announce the lineup for next week's sensational DSMWF T.V. Broadcast!
[TdB] You Pawns better listen up 'cause memers of my Pocket Change company are going to be in action!
[CG] Would you please cease and desist! My apologies for the remarks of
Mr. de Biased, folks!
In our first matchup we will have the debut of the Texas Parrot Man,
Franky the Widowmaker as he faces the mulitcoloured, multitalented superstar, Man Hole Sewer!
[TdB] Oh, no, Gale! Everytime I look at those tie-dyed outfits of Man
Hole, it reminds me of raw sewage!
[CG] You're kind of similar to that statement! [TdB] What did you say!!!! [CG] Be quite of I'll have you flung off the premisses! In our feature matchup, we have the battle of the acronyms! [TdB] Yeah, and you can bet that my Company member, EPA is going to bowl right over Mr. KFC! Hahahahahahahahahahah!
[CG] I wouldn't wager much on that one, de Biased! Neither of these two competitors exactly fan favourites, yet this could be a main event in any local hockey rink! I'm really looking forward to that matchup!
Our third matchup features the first appearance of the Heartburn Guy, Ron
Mitchell since his brutal beating at the hands of YOUR man, de Biased,
Sociopath Sam!
[TdB] I told you NEVER call him "sociopath"! Are you hard of hearing or something, Gale?! [CG] Anyway, Mitchell will be taking on the Rhode Island Raider. This should be an excellent test to see if Mitchell is 100%. Few people would be after FIVE Sociopath slams off the top rope!
Plus we'll have two additional exciting matches scheuled for you folks! Don't venture to miss this one!
