Lamby isn't a wrestler, really.. all he is is a stuffed animal that seems to have a odd cult following among some wrestlers in the MLWO. 24) Tommy Rifles - Sure, he was the one to put the first loss in Shane Slaughter's win/loss column, but apparently that wasn't good enough to keep him out of the top 25. 23) "Cold And Calculating" Tony Houston - INWI - Last year he nearly cracked the top 10 in the RSPWF TOP 200 awards, this year some people weren't too happy with his and King Apples' rankings and voted for this rank as they just simply weren't too impressed. 22) Wrestlingman - It seems like it's a really lame superhero of some sort. Odd name, probably really, really bad e-wrestler 21) Atomic Bomb - Born when the bombs stuck Japan during World War II, this guy was really freaky and stuff and pretty much unrealistic enough to be on this list. 20) The Rude Dude - The small campaign wasn't enough to get him the number one spot he wanted, but it was good enough to get a "coveted" Top 25 spot. 19) Killer - A really, really original name. One of the members of a really originally named stable(Death Row), Killer just had a really plain name, and a really horrible career. 18) "Franchise Suicide Blonde" Kevin Douglass - Most famous for throwing a poor, unfortunate, defensless SCW booker off of a cliff in a flash. Apparently nobody found humor in that at all. 17) Knick Man - One of the top wrestlers from the AICW. AICW recently scored a lot of awards at the recent RSPWF snapshots, and some people weren't too happy or too impressed so they took it out on Knick Man. 16) Brass Knuckles - Some people put him down simply because he writes long, long flashes, and enough people thought it really wasn't a good thing as they placed him at #16 15) "Ravishing Crippler" Ric Studd - Apparently a three way cross between Ric Flair, Rick Rude, and Chris Benoit and one other wrestler(Diamond Studd? Big John Studd? Scott Studd?). Wasn't very original, eh? From the looks of it, he took the worst qualifites of all four wrestlers and rolled it all into one. 14) H20 - The symbol for water, also the symbol for bad e-wrestler according to some of you! 13) Bill Bob Woijicowski - recently retired from the "sport" of e-wrestling, and apparently not too missed by some of you people. 12) Mark Cutter - The "lovable" e-wrestling personality who owns the MLWO and dons the tights on occasion. Did well in the recent D-Cup, but it just quite wasn't impressive, and his ego doesn't help his case. 11) "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh - Didn't get too much comments about this guy to be honest. It does seem that a few people either don't like this guy, or he has some sort of bad reputation, and that earned him #11. 10) FREEK - Had flashes that consisted of almost nothing BUT CAPITAL LETTERS! STOP YELLING IT IS HURTING OUR EYES!! FREEK OUT! 9) Sabin Rene Figaro - Was in over 50 feds at one time.. successful in just about zero. He had his share of titles in feds where he was active.. too bad his competition wasn't 8) Cobra - GEWA - This guy had a pretty strong drive to get himself to the top of the list. Weird... well, he fell short. The handler admitted to me this guy was horrible with his snake lisp, so he felt he deserved a high spot. 7) King Apples - INWI - Last years #13 in the RSPWF Top 200 had people scratching their heads and saying "who is this guy??". Apparently some of those people thought there should've been better wrestlers in that position, and some of them just wasn't impressed, as they voted him #7 6) "Playboy" Ronnie D - Self promoter extraordinare. So self absorbed it either pissed off a lot of people, while some others were mesmerized. Apparently the angered people outnumbered the impressed people and voted for Ronnie D to earn #6 5) Raekwon - UWF - Horrible e-wrestler named after a rap star. Apparently this handler had a fetish for naming wrestlers after rap stars, as there apparently was more guys just like this one. Oh well... three words for Mr. Raekwon. ay yo, sup? 4) American Patriot - A lot of people just don't like this guy simply because he won the tournament that he runs once, and made it to the finals. They also say this guy has no charisma whatsoever and is a disgrace to the good ol' USA. The validity of this guy's tournament might be restored since he won't be entered in any anytime soon, but what about the validity of this guy's reputation? 3) Shane Slaughter - Started off his career something like 64-0 or 65-0 before Tommy Rifles finally beat him. Many people question the validity of his streak, saying he's either exaggerating, or never beat any really stiff competition to amass such an amazing won-loss record. This questionable streak probably what earned Slaughter #3. 2) Jade the Blade - Just when it looked like this would be a one horse race, here comes Jade the Blade. With a goofy theme song, plus the reputation of quitting and coming back at will and making enemies wherever he goes, a lot of people just couldn't stand this guy. He really thought he was very good, others... just couldn't agree with him. And now.. Number ONE! 1) Casey Stanfield - He barely eked out number one by a couple of points over the surging Jade the Blade. Nobody really was impressed with this guy right from the start. Many people call this guy a "mediocre wrestler or worse". Once, which probably earned this guy a share of votes, he was torn apart during a match and was pronounced dead. A few months later he came back like nothing happened.
