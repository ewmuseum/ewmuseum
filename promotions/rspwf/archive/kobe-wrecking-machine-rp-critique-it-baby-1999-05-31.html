"Kobe Wrecking Machine" RP. Critique it, baby!

31 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, folks, here's a pair of RP's from my G-pro guy, "Kobe Wrecking Machine" Takaharu Watinabe. The first is the intro RP, and the second is for his first match. Let me know what you think. He's a new character, so there should be lots to criticize. :)
Intro RP. A "teaser" that doesn't really show the character. ---------- Fade in on a gray, grainy image seemingly shot from an elevated position. The scene is one of urban decay. A dilapidated highrise building stands center screen. After a moment a detonation occurs, and in slow motion, the building begins to collapse, straight down. Suddenly, detached voice comes.
Voice: HEY GUNRYYYYYOOOO!!! Woo-hah! You was gone for awhile, but now you're back, and SO AM I! Oh, yeah, you don't know me, but I've been around, watching, waiting, wishing for the chance to show my stuff. Oh, yeah, it's a fact, you got some tough guys here. That ain't no issue. Now to take this soup and add me into the pot. Now, now it gets more interesting. . .
A huge cloud of dust billows out from the sides of the falling building, which is not nearly fallen. The slowdown of time is mesmerising, the wreckage barely moves at all.
Voice: What we got here? We got colorful characters, lots of 'em. We got the old ZS, Zokugan Sangai, oooh, real impressive, they're a bunch of guys after my own mold. We got the big, soft, ugly, (Searching for adjective.) STOOPID
American swine known as Jar-head Jones. Oooh, real impressive, you're like a sumo without the muscle. You're kinda like this building. Y'know, kinda tall, kinda weak, SPLAT! All it takes is a little EXPLOSIVE, and a WRECKING
MACHINE, and you go down like a TON OF BRICKS!
The building is most of the way down, covered almost entirely by the cloud of dust. A clapping noise is heard, seemingly from the disembodied voice.
Voice: Whoopita, I never get tired of watching this crap. It's a little like watching a train wreck. Hell, it's a LOT like watching a train wreck. It's like watching. . .A KOBE WRECKING MACHINE MATCH!!!
The building is finally leveled, it is deathly still as the dust begins to settle. The silence of the visuals gives them an eerie quality, which is broken again by the voice.
Voice: BO-RING! Calm is boring! You gotta keep moving! You gotta keep moving in life, you gotta keep moving in the ring, and in general KEEP MOVING!!!
The scene suddenly fragments, like glass, and the pieces fall from the screen, leaving an inky blackness. A spotlight suddenly appears, highlighting a lone figure hunched down and seated cross legged on a concrete floor. Beneath the spotlight he seems as a radiant sun as his metallic clothing reflects the luminescence in all directions. His back is turned to the camera. As the camera zooms in on him, the only darkness is his hair, short cropped and black, typically asian. He continues his rant without turning around.
Voice: Oh, yeah, it's time for the instant impact. It's time for everyone to get to know the one-man wrecking crew. It's not a matter of pride, it's a matter of like and dislike. There's only about four guys here that I like, and several that I really, REALLY hate. Watching these guys on TV I had to wonder, 'why are they stars and I'm not'. Oh, but I've found my answer, boys and girls. The answer is that I'm a star too. The single_most_destructive force that you've ever felt, I'm the TNT, I'm the earthquake, I'm THE
TSUNAMI!!! RIDE THE WAVE PEOPLE!!! Suddenly the light dies, signifying the end. All is black again. After a moment of silence the voice is heard one more time.
Voice: Hey, Japeater, I got something for you to munch on, RIGHT HERE! He does not speak again. . . ------- Follow up RP. It was just against a jobber, but I still tried to make it good. ------- A scene of urban decay opens. Demolished buildings and shredded roads form a dismal background. Rats scurry from place to place, and crows fly overhead. An odd static is heard, like running water or rushing wind. It is the only sound.
In the distance there is a glint, a small gleam of reflected light that stands atop a pile of rubble. In a dramatic motion the camera angle shifts from a wide-angle long-distance shot to a close up of a man dressed in metallic tights. The sudden movement makes it seem more like the figure has flown at the camera than the other way around.
Turning to camera, this figure, a japanese man, grins wide, seemingly happy at the attention that he is getting. He removes a pair of sunglasses, showing a pair of close-set eyes, scratches the stubble on his chin, coughs, and then speaks.
Machine: Focus, focus people. You know why you're here? All who watch me tonight should know that they watch the future of the G-pro organization.
About to make his debut, about to demolish from beginning to end the pale pretender Serei Ketsueki, you are watching The "Kobe Wrecking Machine"
Takaharu Watinabe! With a snap on the final word, the self-proclaimed wrecking machine moves smoothly down the pile of wreckage, sliding quickly down the rough jagged hill of concrete and metal.
Machine: As the future of the G-pro, I feel I have a duty to inform the past, that's you Ketsueki, that it's time to make way. I know, you've probably had a long and illustrious career in wrestling, don't tell me. No matter how much you try to remind me of who you are, I don't know you at all. I've made it a point to ignore non-entities like yourself. I see the big names like the Zokugun Sangai, and the fat fury of Jar-head Jones. I don't know you, don't know of you because I've made it a point not to.
Turning back towards the heap of rubbish that he stood on, Takaharu begins running in place. The purpose of this is not clear as the chaotic young man begins to sprint up the hill. Within a few seconds he is at the top of the manmade mini-mountain.
Machine: Imagine! Me! Top of the heap! King of the hill! Here I am, looking down on all of you! You, imagining yourself as my equals! You,
Serei Ketsueki, even trying to step in the ring to derail this locomotive!
Me on top is to be the reality, your imaginings and actions are the joke. Serei, when the walls of your career cave in, and your world comes tumbling down, I want you to know that your sacrifice has not been in vain. The end of your career shall begin the greatest legacy of all. The legacy of
Takaharu Watinabe. From your ashes a phoenix shall spring, that phoenix is me, and my flames shall burn for all time!
Takaharu raises his hands high overhead, eyes wide he screams. . . Machine: BURN, BABY, BURN!!! The sounds of explosions and metal tearing rip through the air, disrupting the static and eliminating all else. Takaharu becomes the flashpoint for a great luminescance. From him springs a light that envelops all. The screen goes white, as with an explosion, and all is still. Silence, nothingness, the serenity of oblivion. Fade to black. . .
