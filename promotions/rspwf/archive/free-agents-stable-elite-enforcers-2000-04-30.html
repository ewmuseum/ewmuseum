[Free Agents] Stable:Elite Enforcers

30 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We are a Stable which became unhappy with the fed we were in. I posted earlier on this ng on what happened.
All our Stable members decided that we were going to leave. The kind of criteria we are looking for in a fed are: 1 or 2 cards a week Role-playing board active relatively new or established fed Web site based Several titles Un biased prez who has determination and dedication Relatively large roster 20+ Fake wrestlers only Here are our applications: Stable: Elite Enforcers Number of members: 3 (4 soon-another member will be entering) Leader: Avenger AVENGER Height: 6'3" Weight: 254 Disposition: Face Style: Technical/High Flyer Appearance He is of average size and build with tanned skin and brown eyes. He has black, short hair that is spiky at the front and wears red and black facepaint that is in the style as the old Sting and Ultimate Warrior. When wrestling he wears black wrestling tights with red trim. The red on the tights makes it look like someone has torn and slashed the tights similar to
Chris Benoit's design. With that he also wears knee high black wrestling boots. Out of the ring and entering it he wears a black leather trenchcoat.
Personality He is a very honourable wrestler believing you should not cheat to win. Because of this he is very popular with the fans and a rolemodel to many young children. He will always help those in need and will always try to put to an end any injustice that will go on. Believing in ‘not forgiving and not forgetting just getting even’ makes this character one not to mess with.
Entrance He enters with the arena tinged in red. A circle of fire appears from the stage and he rises from the ashes of the flames. He enters the ring and does
Kane's in ring taunt with coming Fire from the ring posts. When helping others the ring will fill with smoke from the ringposts and he will appear in the ring
Regularly used moves 1) Greetings from Ashbury Park/Fire Thunder/Rikishi Driver 2) Texas Cloverleaf 3) Flatliner/Downward Spiral 4) Somersault Legdrop(360 Leg Drop on the Top rope) [Trademark Move] 5) Juvi/Michinoku Driver/Nail in the coffin 6) Sambo Suplex 7) Samoan Neckbreaker (Samoan Drop position into Diamond Cutter) 8) Brainbuster 9) Tornado DDT on the top Rope 10) Bulldog Finisher: Vengeance Drop A reverse DDT/Brainbuster Sample Roleplay The scene opens in the boiler room of the arena. Pitched in darkness the camera man can hardly see a thing. A mysterious voice had called on the telephone him ten minutes ago to meet him in the boiler room with a camera.
It was cold in the boiler room, there was not a sound. The camera man felt a shiver down his back, he wished he took a jumper with. It seemed too quiet, almost creepy. All the camera man could hear was the dripping of water on the floor and a constant tapping.
Unknown man: Hey you, over here. The voice came from one of the isolated dark corners of the room. It was very familiar as if the camera man had heard the voice somewhere before.
Slowly, he walked along the floor of the boiler room, each step making an echo. Looking hard, the camera man could just make out a silhouette of the man in the corner. The man was crouching down against the cold boiler room wall. Getting even closer he started to recognise the man as he made out his features. On the man’s face was red and black facepaint on he was wearing a black leather trenchcoat almost camouflaging him from human sight.
Unknown man: I said you, over here, now. With that voice he now knew who this mysterious man was. The camera man had filmed him many times before in and out of the wrestling ring it was the wrestler known only as Avenger.
Avenger: I want you start filming now. I have a message for the Dogg pound. Camera man: First, can I ask you a question. Avenger: Yeah, shoot. Camera man: Why are you here? A week ago you were around the locker rooms. Now you’re in the boiler room underneath the arena. Avenger: I’m just dealing with my anger and frustration in my own way. LJ, my tag team partner, is dealing with his frustration in a different way to mine. It’s just my way of dealing with things.
Camera man: Thanks. He immediately followed Avenger’s command and turned on the camera. Avenger: So, the Dogg pound, or whatever you call yourselves, you think you’ re veterans. Well it doesn't’t really bother me. As far as I see it, this my analysis of you two, diamonds are forever, you two, though, are not. To coin a phrase, ‘In with revolution out with evolution.’ This Saturday me and LJ will do just that. I may be what you call a ‘rookie’ but new stars have to come and go and established wrestlers have to ‘pass the torch.’ I want that torch and I will take it. Team Impact’s destiny is the Tag team titles. Your destiny is one of you in the middle of the ring, lying there, with Impact getting the 1, 2, 3.
Avenger: I see a little bit of you two in me. Like me, you desire to work hard to gain a good record by destroying people one, by one. Unlike me, you cheat . You beat people from behind, you interrupt in scheduled matches and you stick your nose into other people’s business. On Saturday After hours I will put to an end all your unjust actions. You will pay for evil and unjust ways and that is how I will get my first taste of Vengeance in this federation.
Avenger pauses. Avenger: There is no way you can beat me Dogg pound. You can’t hurt and torture me. My soul has been tortured a long time ago so that what remains of it is insignificant. Look at these eyes Dogg pound there is no fear, only anger and vengeance. You may have heard the phrase forgive and forget. Well,
I don’t forgive and I don’t forget, I just get even. Dogg pound you messed with the wrong people this time. So get ready for an impact because
Vengeance approaches. A disturbing smirk appears on the face of Avenger and the camera slowly faded to black. LJ Name: [name withheld] Wrestlers Name: LJ HT: 6' 4" WT: 245lbs Disposition: Face Physical Description: LJ has should length reddish-brown, kinky hair which he keeps tied into a loose pony tail. He has a small nose and well defined cheek bones and small chin. Into the ring LJ was a black muscle shirt, blue jeans, and rattlesnake skin boots.
Personality: LJ has a complex personality. To strangers he is shy. Once he becomes aquatinted, LJ can be very friendly and outgoing. But LJ is like
Clint Eastwood. IF someone screws him over, then LJ turns mean and vindictive toward that person. LJ tends to hold a grudge for a very long time.
Style: Hardcore Manager: None Name of Finisher: Rodeo Finisher Description: LJ sets his opponent facing away from the highest point that is close to him(ie. Turnbuckle, stage, security fence). LJ then jumps off and grabs the back of the opponents head and slams his opponents face into the ground.
Five Favourite Moves: 1) Double Underhook DDT 2) Suplex 3) Powerbomb 4) Power slam 5) Slaming his opponents head into the hardest object that is close by. Sample Role Play: LJ is LaCrosse. The action is fast and curious. The camera follows LJ around the field as he 'desctely' takes out a few opponents with his stick. Finally the ref spots LJ and calls him for a penalty. LJ heads toward the penalty box were the camera is waiting for him.
LJ: "Yeah, I do whatever it takes to win. I make enemies some times, but I don't care. I know who my friends are. Take this Avenger for example, he's a good man, I like him a lot. So you can be damn sure that I'll watch his back wherever he goes. Like I was saying before when I am competing, I do whatever it takes to win. That's how I am and thats how I will always be.
Another thing I don't care for is loud mouth arrogant wrestlers who shoot off their mouth. The last thing people want to here is some jack ass bragging about how big and strong and talented he is, then tell everyone else that they are pieces of dog cr@p. When I go out and wrestle, I don't yell, scream or brag. I simply tell my opponent how I fell, then go out and wrestle like a true competitor. Now, I have to get back to the game."
LJ leaves the penalty box and joins the game. Stryke Name: [name withheld] Wrestlers name: Styrke Disposition: Face Height: 5’8" Weight: 204lb Appearance: Short, spiky blonde hair. Blue eyes. Yellow and black bandanna. Average build. Black leather jacket with a yellow lightening bolt on the back and the word "Styrke" written in jagged red writing underneath. Black wrestling pants with the same lightening bolt down the right leg and the word "styrke"written in the same writing down the left leg. Black fingerless leather gloves. Knee high wrestling boots.
Entrance: The whole arena goes black. Then simultaneous a flash of light appears and the sound of thunder is heard. Then after a couple of seconds, it happens again but this time the lights flicker on and Styrke is at the top of the ramp and the song "Floyd the Barber" by Nirvana begins. As Styrke walks down the ramp, fireworks spray up as he passes each one and a thunderclap is heard each time another firework is ignited. Styrke then enters the ring and just soaks up the applause.
Personality: A real fan favourite. He loves to keeping the fans in suspense before he does his finisher or one of his specials. He hates all those full of themselves wrestlers who think the are amazing and that everyone loves them. He hates cheating unless the opponent is winning by cheating. Helps those in need.
Wrestling style: U.S.A style high flyer. Finishing move: The Roll of Thunder Description: Top rope 360 tornado headlock DDT. 5 Best non-finishers: Samoan neckbreaker, Frankensteiner/Top rope Hurracanrana, head scissors take over, strong lariat/Clothesline, Bulldog. Sample role-play: The Georgia Dome, Atlanta, Georgia. The crowd had gathered and were waiting in suspense to verify a rumour, a rumour of a new power in wrestling. The crowd could but speculate amongst themselves.
Suddenly, the whole arena went black. Every light in the auditorium went out. There was a flash of light and a huge thunderclap, and then darkness.
There was yet another flash and a thunderclap, but this time it was different. There was a man at the top of the ramp and a grunge tune was heard all around the Dome. The man was dressed in almost all black with only a few significant features. He had the word "Styrke" written on parts of his clothing and some lightening bolts decorating his clothes also.
He walked forward in a strong and confident way. He had only a smirk on his face. As he walked, huge columns of fireworks sprayed up, each accompanied by it’s own thunderclap. The music was now on full blast.
He stepped up and into the ring. He grabbed the mike and began to speak: "Now that’s a flashy entrance", he said jokingly. The crowd laughed at the joke. "I have a number of things to discuss with you all. Firstly, I mean business. I ain’t here to mess around and talk all stupid and macho and become everyone’s friend, I’m here to win and win is what I’m going to do.
All those people who don’t like it know where they can stick it. Secondly, I
’d like to give a message to Techno. Toughen up a bit, man, ‘cuse you’re acting like a goddamn homo. It’s not like we’re here ‘cause we don’t want to be, just live with it and go with the flow or you’re gonna get an extra good kick in the ass from me on behalf of all the people tired of your "hard man"
act. I can see through you like a sheet of glass. You shouldn’t get too slack though, now. D’you know why? ‘Cause if you do, and I’m near by, and I will be, you will get a good kicking on behalf of me". At this point the crowd erupts into a cheer.
"One last thing before I go. I’d like to remind you all that I’m no phoney,
I’m the real deal, and when L.J, Avenger and myself get together, we make a real mean team. Now, let’s stop all this ranting and raving and get on with the show". Styrke holds up the mike to the crowd to get them cheering and whooping. It works.
The lights go out again and after a few seconds a thunderclap is heard and the lights are back on again. There is one difference, Styrke is gone. The crowd is amazed and begins cheering and whistling as if their lives depended upon it.
