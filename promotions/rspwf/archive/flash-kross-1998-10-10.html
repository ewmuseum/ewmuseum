[Flash]Kross

10 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now I know you all will probably flame this but I really don't care. I just want honest opinions. I know I can improve and I want your tips to help me.
Because one can only get better from experience and others help. Please lend a hand and give me some advice on how to be a better rper. I only spent 10 minutes on this but here's an average one of my rps. I just started this character so bear with me.
[The Screen slowly fades into a gigantic arena. The Richmond coliseum. All around fans are up and yelling. They can't wait to witness some of the NEW'S great stars. The fans are uncontrollable with their enthusiasm. As the sold out arena looks on, From the back of the arena, "Electric Funeral" By Black
Sabbath begins to play and smoke begins to sizzle from the arena entrance.
Fire shoots off in short bursts as the fans come up with an uproar of cheers. They don't know who the hell it is, but they love that music. On the Big
Screen, A Big Kross Is Shown as it Fades into the name Shawn "Top Quality"
Kross. The video kicks in as the ramp entrance area catches ablaze. There is nothing but the little square in front of the entrance. Out from the back of the arena steps a mid-size man with a mean look on his face. He sports a Black
Goatee, A pair of oakleys, and Long Brown hair. He isn't gigantic but he definitely has a 6-pac. He's wearing a pair of pants with lightning going down the side and Top Quality on the butt. He smiles as he hears the fans reaction.
He steps through the fire and heads toward the ring. He jumps up on the ring,
And springboards into the ring off the top rope. He looks around as he lowers his shades, gets a microphone and begins to speak.]
Shawn "Top Quality" Kross - Yes, NEW, you, yes all of you will soon witness the birth of a new legacy. The Beginning of a new dynasty. The
Starting of something bigger than big, that it's (O)HUGE(O). [Takes a moment...as the fans roar with cheers...a few boos here and there but over 2/3 of the fans are definitely Qualityholics.]
Shawn "Top Quality" Kross - Ladies and gentleman, Friends and neighbors alike. Lend Me Your ears as I tell you of a ghastly fate that awaits one certain man named by attrition. Yes, folks he will suffer the fate that awaits many of the NEW superstars and that is the Top Quality Drop because brother and sister alike all know that when Top Quality is pumped then he just can't be stopped!!
[The Fans Go Wild] Shawn "Top Quality" Kross - It's time. I've been waiting all week and yes tomorrow night at Saturday Night Slam Dance it will be time for Mr.
Attrition to go through the grinder because The Quality one is waiting for him with anticipation and there is not a damn thing that he can do about it.
Because no matter what he does, I will always be better than him, more gifted than him, more talented then him, and ya damn for sure know that I'm more
Sexier than him. Mr. Attrition you have a thing or two to learn in this world and I think it's time for you to meet the Teacher!
[Kross takes a pause in his dialogue] Shawn "Top Quality" Kross - There's just one thing I don't understand. And that's why don't I get any respect around here. You got attrition coming out here and ranting and raving about me. For christ sakes why doesn't he look in the mirror and see the filthy piece of trash that lies within himself. Before he goes and sees other people flaws he needs to see his own. [Pause] [Fast] Saturday Night Slam Dance, marks the night that my debut takes place and that my fist finds it's mark because this is gonna be nothing less than a SlobbeRKnocker. We both hate each other's guts and we're not afraid to express that. I understand that. And that's why I'm not gonna have any regrets when I unleash a flurry of wrestling moves that you never knew existed. I am the most talented technical wrestler to ever walk the earth and
I sure as hell don't see why i shouldn't prove it to you and the whole entire world tomorrow night. [Normal Speed] This is my time to shine and you know for a fact that I'll take a hold of this oppurtunity. This is the chance I've been waiting for. Your gonna look like one of those picaso pictures when I get done with you. Your face is gonna be in a thousand different slabs and pieces.
[Chuckles to hisself] Shawn "Top Quality" Kross - I always loved fine art. And now ya think about it ya know why now don't ya. When your the hottest thing on the planet. And all the guys wanna be ya and all the girls wanna meet ya, then why wouldn't you love art, considering I am the greatest single piece of art ever created. Because Top Quality is truly a one of a kind. And attrition you will sooooooooon find that out as the bell will ring, the fans will cheer, The
lights will dim, and you will be dropped. And oh yeah, call me crap, call me trash, call me whatever you want. But don't forget to call me ....TOP QUALITY!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [The screen fades as as the fans are going nuts and he slides into the back.] King Of All Who Smells Funny... - Shawn "Top Quality" Kross(0-0-0) Currently Competes in: INWI, N.E.W, L.A.W
