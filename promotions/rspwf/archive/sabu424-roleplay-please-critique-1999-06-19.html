Clack.. Clack.. Clack... [The Sound slowly grows louder and louder...] Clack.. Clack.. Clack. [A dark figure emerges from the shadows, soaked in what appears to be water. A dim light shins on his face, which is half hidden beneath a mask. The part of the face which isn't hidden is pale white with huge red gashes. The dim light flickers, as the Wind roars.]
-The Messiah- Since the Dawn of Time there are those people who have striven for Success, while others dive into Sorrow, right now Wrestling and the World is in a state of misconception.
Misconception, in Man, Misconception in Myth and Misconception the Lord. For those who chose the right path, Success and Virtue will fallow them. For those who chose the wrong, Grief and Sorrow will surcom them in a Life of SIN. I died for your SINS, I traveled down the path of God and I lived a life of SIN. But I percervered. Why.. Why.. Why.. Why Must I come back and reclaim a crown, that was always mine.. Why.. I did not ask for this life, it was trust upon me. But I understand it is my Calling, My Destiny. I left the World too Soon, he says. I say I didn't leave soon enough. I good man I was, a bad man I am.. But I'm the chosen one,
They Say. But I no longer question why, because Questions, lead to answers, and that Answer may be one that I don't want to hear, but yet I already know. The Legacy of the Man, knows too well.. Too well... But I ask why.. Friendship out the door and you broke one of the Commandment's, for a Night.. Why.. But yet your actions bare my name.. That's a disgrace.. I have been sent from the Heavens to show you the Way. The way to REDEMPTION. As I stand in the heavens and I peer down, I see nothing but Chaos and Anarchy. I chaos in which I lived Threw, beaten abandoned and on my own since I was a child, I survived. My Father died before I turned 9, my mother remarried and was addicted to drugs. My step father beat me to a bloody pulp, using my soft skin as an ash tray. I never cried. My mother Overdosed when I was 13, my step father abandoned me. Winding up in Foster care for a year only brought more pain, as I was an outsider looking in, There was no love, I was nothing more than an extra pay check a month, the abuse continued just not as often and not as bad, so I went on my own. Living in shelters or any place I could I went on, until the faithful day I walked into a church. As I opened those big
Doors and
walked in, I felt the Warmth, I felt the Spirit and I knew I would survive. I was almost 15, the local Minister at the Church, found my Grandmother, and I moved across state to live with her and my dying Grandfather. My life was perfect, school, sports, women and work consumed my life. Some say I was an
Over Achiever, but in my heart I never achieved Much. I won trophies and titles in the various sports in which I competed, boxing, wrestling and football. I earned a college scholarship in football and wrestling, and on the way
I won a
NCAA Wrestling Championship and an Olympic Gold Medal. I went pro in both sports, Wrestling and Football. But that's where my Perfect life would
Turn
Sour. Traveling.. Leads you to many different lands and adventures, for some those are fruitful for others they are dangerous. As I traveled across the country, sleeping in hotels and motels, drinking in a different bar ever night, going home with a different woman ever night, I was introduced to something that would haunt me for the rest of my Life.. DRUGS.. Drugs is what has led to my Demise, it took a Once Promising Young Man with all the Ability in the
World, down a dirty road of Violence and Abuse. How was I supposed to know, I was never aware of the Warning signs. But they were there, after a short stint in jail, I was let go, it was natural for a young man abused as a child to snap. So I went threw some counciling and back to work I went. Work...
Oh god, I lived to wrestle, and Wrestle I did, I captured titles in almost every single federation I was in, but that wasn't good enough. I raked in millions of
Dollars, but that didn't matter to me. I loved what I was doing, but that loved was transformed over into hate.
The Time is upon us and the APCW and The World will realize that The Resurrection has taken place, Lead by one Soulless man.. Who is the Chosen one.. This man is The Messiah, and I am this man. You will all bow at my feet upon entering my Domain, my domain of a Hellish brutal pain, that will plague you through out eternity if you go against my Commands. This time I will not be
Crucified, those who disobey will. Disobey my Commands and Suffer my Wrath. For I am the Seeker of Truth, for I am Not a Man, For I am Not a Myth. For it was written, so it will come to pass... In the last year of the Millennium, I will rise and regain my Throne. For I am not dead in Mind and Body. Just Soul. Without my Soul I cannot roam the Heavens, yet I cannot burn in the fire pit of hell. I just sit in between the too watching from a distance. Watching and waiting. I've been to both, but yet I never stayed, all I want is rest but yet
I must do this first. Why... Why. Why. I ask. Do what I love. Nonsense. But it is his Wish, my wish, but yet I do not command it. For it is my Will. and I am The MESSIAH.
