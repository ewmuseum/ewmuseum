[FREE AGENT, MAYBE] "Superstar" Sean Holloway

22 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, guys, I may be a free agent in about a weeks. If you would like me in your fed, email me the handbook, and I will make a decision. Also, I wouldnt mind some *constructive* criticism. Here goes .. not my best, but it'll do. ;-)
[Fade In] [The scene fades into a locker room equipped with weight sets, benches, lockers, and other things. The camera moves forward into the room and pans around. A water fountain is located at the end of a long line of lockers. The cooler on the fountain kicks in and makes a loud buzzing sound. The camera swings around and moves around the room looking at the furnishings. A weight bench with some weights on the bar sits right in the middle of the floor. Next to it sits a weight holder with many assorted weights on it. Attached to the wall is a mirror. The mirror extends from one corner of the room to the other.
The mirror is the same exact height of the wall.] [Suddenly, a door bangs open. In steps "Superstar" Sean Holloway. Clad in a
Nike jogging suit and Oakley sunglasses, he seems not to notice the camera. He tosses his gym bag to the side and sits down on the weight bench. He starts to lift the weight, but then notices the mirror. He stands up and poses in front of the mirror.]
[He takes his shirt off and tosses it off the camera. He stands in front of the mirror, and flexes his muscles. He smiles at his reflection then turns around.
It is now that he notices the camera. He smiles and begins to talk.] SEAN HOLLOWAY: Damn, I look good! And I mean *good!* Everybody wishes they could be like .. *ME!*
[Sean Holloway turns back toward the mirror and runs a hand through his blonde hair. He takes the sunglasses off and peers into the mirror. From the reflection you can see the intensity in his eyes.]
SEAN HOLLOWAY: Well, it looks like I've done it again. Hell, sometimes even I suprise myself. I've done beaten the odds *once* again and defeated Danny
Vicious. I hate to brag .. *but damn I'm good!* [Laughs] Jax knows it, Pillow knocks it, *everyone* knows that I'm the best thing going today. And that is evident in the fact that Pillow won't let me rest. Every week I take a look at the schedule, and I see my name on there going against some wannabe. Hell, if there is a wannabe in International Championship Wrestling I've took him on.
Not only took him on, but *beat* his ass. It seems all Pillow can do is throw wannabe after wannabe at me. He won't give me a challenge. Probably 'cause he knows I'll straight up kick their ass and show 'em the most underrated wrestler today.
[Sean Holloway lays down on the bench and grasps the weight bar. He picks it up into the air and lowers it to his chest. He does this eight or so times, then puts the bar up. He sets up and begins to speak.]
SEAN HOLLOWAY: Looks like I'm on tha big time again, eh? Yup, sho' 'nuff .. Pillow knows everyone that is gonna be at Friday Fallout next week wants tuh see moi. So what does Pillow do? Like the money suckin' fool he is, he gives
'em what they want. He gives tha "Superstar" a match with the number one contender for the Juniorweight title, Roby Blaze. But what I wanna know is?
[Pauses] How does that dude become number one contender after one match? Huh? Is he on Pillow's "list"? I mean, c'mon, I'm the hottest commodity going today! Everyone knows that! But it seems I been gettin' tha shaft while people like
Blaze come in here and become number one contender like [Snaps fingers] that!
Well, I'll show Pillow who tha real man is. Roby Blaze is gonna be sorry he every tried to play at Sean Holloway's level.
[Sean Holloway lays back down and grabs the bar again. He picks it up and pumps it eight more times before setting it back down. He takes a deep breath and remains laying.]
SEAN HOLLOWAY: "Die Hard" Roby Blaze .. haven't I fought a "Die Hard" before? [Thinks to himself] Yeah, that's it! "Die Hard" Darren Shaw .. the only man that I didn't pin for the three count. The only man that could put a "blemish"
on my record. The only man that didn't go straight to Grady Memorial when I was done with him. Mr. Blaze, that won't happen again.
[Sean Holloway sits up and streches his arms. He then goes over to the punching bag and takes a few swings. After that, he sits down on a bench and sighs to himself.]
SEAN HOLLOWAY: When I take a look at the rankings here in tha ICW I see myself residing at number eight. When I look at them, I fail to see Roby Blaze anywhere. Yet, he is still the number one contender for the Juniorweight title.
Hell, keep it. I got bigger fish to fry. The Juniorweight title is chump change compared to the haul I'm gonna be making. And by the way, I won't be number eight for long. Pretty soon, I'll be at the top of the mountain .. while Roby
Blaze is spitting up teeth down at the bottom.
.. Where he belongs. Roby Blaze, you probably think you da bomb or something just 'cause you beat
Kevin Anderson.
.. *News flash!* Kevin Anderson ain't shit. He's just some little small time chump that thinks he's "goregous." And if Kevin Anderson has a problem with that ..
.. he can step in line with the rest of the fools that think they can take me down. But Anderson ain't my main priority. "Die Hard" is .. and Blaze .. you can ask anyone. That ain't a very good place tuh be. But, if ya want, you can just lay there, let me pin ya for tha one-two-three, and we can all go home. It saves you an ass whipping, and I can get to tha ladies quicker. 'Cause you know when we go to Cali .. the ladies *love* me.
Roby, I would stay here longer, but all this is giving me a headache. Besides, this is taking time away from my "hard" training schedule. [Laughs] Blaze, with that said .. there is only one thing left tuh do ..
.. *knock you the *FUCK* out!* [Sean Holloway breaks out in a fit of laughter.] SEAN HOLLOWAY: Oh yeah .. and that's the way it's gonna be .. .. *because that's the way it's gonna be!* [Sean Holloway laughs once again and stands to his full six foot, two inch frame. He stretches his arms over his head and exits through the door he came.
The camera slowly pulls back the way it came .. and we slowly ..] [Fade Out]
