[Blackness. Silence. Suddenly, a shrill whine punctures the void with a loud, screaming air guitar riff. The chords grow higher and higher until a maddening drum solo bursts through. Just as the sound came, it is gone. Replaced on the screen now, are flaming letters:]
U I W C The leader in professional wrestling [The screen then cuts to multiple clips. First, we see Alex Fury, in his aged state, clotheslining another wrestler. Another image; an un-named wrestler hitting a doctor bomb. Flashes of suplexes, piledrivers, and maddening, high-impact maneuvers, come in rapid succession. The roar of thunder can be heard above the images, which explode on the screen. Lightning screeches, and illuminates each picture. And just as quickly as the barrage came, it subsides.
Replaced with another line of words:] Coming Soon [Fade.] - - - Email [detail withheld] to join the new revolutionary force in e-wrestling. Guarenteed, it will be the greatest e-wrestling experience you will ever have.
