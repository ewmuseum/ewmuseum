Bored? Critique this... It's something to do, at least...

21 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In.] [There he is--Ryan Delaney, leaning back on that old blue recliner he lugs to every apartment he keeps. He's sipping on some sort of drink.
It looks to be a Sobe beverage, probably Sobe Dragon... That's Delaney's favorite. Delaney is wearing plain black jean shorts, with a red Heinz ketchup shirt. Yea, red... an odd idea for Delaney.]
[He takes a sip of the Sobe, and after the bottle is removed from his lips, his face goes all weird, like he's cringing. He takes a few deep breathes, acting like something potent is in that drink. But what? It looks like it was recently open, and Delaney looks pretty sober, for Delaney that is.]
RD: Fuck the System... An event that showcases the philosophy that when you do things your _own_ way, it normally ends up _better_ than expected. That when you listen to the voices _inside_ your own head, and _not_ the ones that surround you, the ones that _suffocate_ you and choke the _life_ right out of you, the end result is so grand its
_mystifiying_.
[He frowns, and then takes another drink of the Sobe. After taking a drink, the smile comes back, as he licks his lips.]
I _wish_ I could practice what I preach. I _wish_ I had _never_ listened to that little _hissing_ in my ear. I _wish_ I had _never_ let it inside my mind. I _wish_ it's _poison_ had _never_ spread. I
_wish_ it's eggs _never_ hatched. I wish I _never_ met you, Mr. Snake
Man.
I wish you _never_ hatched. [Apparently feeling good from whatever is in his system, he takes another sip of the wonderful drink. He closes his eyes, letting the fluid running through him. He continues speaking, his eyes shut.]
But wishing _doesn't_ make the problem go away. No, Mr. Snake Man is
_too_ slippery for _simple_ wishing to get _rid_ of him. I _just_ have to grab him by the _neck_ and squeeze real, _real_ hard, paralyzing the little _bastard_, allowing his _eliminaton_ to be
_alot_ easier. Yes, that _is_ what I'll do to you, Mr. Snake Man.
I'll _choke_ the life out you like you've done to _me_. I'll slowly chip away at your _existence_, like you've _done_ to me.
I'll get you back for _all_ the shit you've done, times _three_. [His eyes shoot wide open, creating a weird scary look on Delaney's pale face. He laughs, and takes another swig of his beloved Sobe. He starts to sway slightly back and forth, as he speech becomes even more crazy.]
You _thought _it was funny, eh, Mr. Snake Man when you _cracked_ me last week, _giving_ us the win over those two _hacks_? You _enjoyed_ that little clubbing _didn't_ you? I _bet_ you are happy we won, Mr.
Snake Man. I _bet_ you're overflowing with _joy_ now that the _alore_ of title gold is _dancing_ in front of my _very_ eyes. I _bet_ you are so fucking _proud_ of yourself because maybe... _just_ maybe you created a reason for me _not_ to break you into a million pieces, and
_show_ the world what the _insides_ of a snake look like.
[He pauses, taking a even bigger swig of the potent Sobe.] Well, looks like I _need_ a pair of new shoes, and I think you are the
_perfect_ size, Mr. Snake Man.
[He smiles, a devilish, drunken smile.] I'm _done_, Mr. Snake Man. I'm _done_ being your _slave_. I'm _done_ being your _help_. I'm _done_ being your little _mouse_. I'm _done_ being played with as if I was your _dinner_. And, trust me, I'm
_prepared_ to bite the hand that feeds me. I'm prepared to _destory_ the snake I _once_ respected, the snake I _once_ looked up too, and the snake that _once_ lived by a certain code of morals. But _what_ should I really have expected?
I was dealing with a _snake_, after all. [He shakes his head, trying to understand what drug made him say yes to Mr. Snake Man's wishes. Too hard to think, so he takes another swig and continues on his obviously Sobe induced rant.]
I _thought_ there was something _underneath_ your skin, Mr. Snake Man. I _thought_ somewhere in the _cold_ blooded body of yours there was a
_heart_. I _thought_ underneath the skin and behind the fangs was a
_man_ inside the snake exterior... a _man_ that I _could_ respect and learn _great_ things from!
Too bad I was _dead_ wrong. [The Sobe... It's now about three quarters of the way finished.] See you Saturday, Mr. Snake Man. Until then, [Looks at the rest of the Sobe. That almost forgotten about, Sobe induced smile returns.] I'll just sit back, and picture my new loafers. [Fade Out.]
