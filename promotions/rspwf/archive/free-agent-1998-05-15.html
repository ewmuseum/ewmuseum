**FREE AGENT**

15 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a sample of our work...the ICWA is looking for you! [We return to ICWA Monday Night Heat with a scene of Lucky Chance laying on a stretcher and being loaded into an ambulance in the backstage area. The Doll is standing over the stretcher, crying. The scene changes to show Nick Klien and "Big Nasty" Paul Agren sitting at the announce position with a near- silent crowd in the arena]
Nick: [in a serious, low tone] Ladies and gentlemen, apparently Lucky Chance was injured by this move here...
[The scene shows a slow-motion replay of Axon Blade reversing the Jackpot. This shows, more clearly than before, Lucky Chance landing head first on the mat, and his neck compressing]
Nick: There you see, Lucky Chance coming down on his neck. You hate to see these things happen, especially here at our first show... All we can do now is just hope that Lucky
Chance will be OK, Paul.
Paul: You know... I, I just don't know. Nick: Fans, you never expect anything like this to happen, but... But nonetheless, the show must go on.
Right now we're schedueled for our main event, the last match of the evening between The Knick Man and The Preacher... So, uh, let's get it to Jason Garrison.
[DING DING DING] Garrison: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our main event of the evening, schedueled for one fall, it is the final First Round contest in the ICWA World Heavyweight Title Tournament! The winner of this match will advance to the Semi-Finals, where they will face "The Punisher" Mike Wick!
And now... ["O Fortuna" by Carl Orff begins to play throughout the arena] Coming down the aisle, from Godsville, Ontario, Canada, weighing in at 327 pounds... THEEEEEEEEEEEEE PREEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAAAACHERRRRRRRRRRRRRRR!!!!!!!!!!! [The lights in the arena dim and only several ice blue laser lights shine throuhgout as The Preacher walks slowly down the aisle wearing a white robe, embroidered with gold at sleeves, neck, and hem. He makes the sign of the cross on his chest, kisses his two fingers, and extends them out to the camera]
The Preacher: LC... Nick: The Preacher obviously showing affection towards Lucky Chance. Garrison: And his opponent... ["Go NY Go NY Go" plays loudly throughout the arena] Garrison: From New York City, weighing in at 250 pounds... THE KNICK MAAAAAAAAAAAAN!!!!!!!! [The Knick Man makes his way down the aisle, dressed in an all blue Knick Man
Championship Jersey,
as well as a pair of blue wrestling trunks with the Knick Man Championship logo on the side of them.
Accompanying The Knick Man to the ring is an short, old negro preacher. He is dressed in a long black robe and is holding in his right hand a bible. The Knick Man climbs up to the apron and waves at the crowd who responds with loud boos and jeers. The Knick Man then holds the ropes open for the preacher who steps inside of the ring. The Knick Man then climbs inside of the ring himself and takes the microphone away from Jason Garrison, who was just exiting the ring]
Knick Man: Thank you! Thank you all! I love you all! Thank you! Anyway, I guess everyone is wondering why The Knick Man has brought this man of God down here tonight. Well, it's pretty simple! You see,
The Preacher [points towards his opponent] isn't the only righteous man around here. I'm a strong believer in the faith myself! <crowd boos> I'm serious! I go to church EVERY SUNDAY! So before we start this athletic competition, I would like everyone to bow their heads and close their eyes as the man of God prays a blessing for us. Go ahead Reverend!
[The Preacher bows his head and closes his eyes] Reverend: Father God, we come to you on this fine evening asking for your blessings and mercies! We pray that you might bless these two wrestlers tonight, especially The Preacher!
We pray Lord that The Knick Man won't hurt him too badly! We pray that The Knick Man won't kill him, beat him = to a miserable pulp...
[The Preacher looks up and realizes this is a trick just as The Knick man attacks him with a clothesline, and the reverend that Knick Man brought to the ring with him exits the ring as quickly as he can]
[DING DING DING] Nick: What a disgrace! The Knick Man attacking The Preacher before the bell, and during a prayer!
Paul: Hey, that Preacher's just a big dummy! Nick: Stop it! Knick Man kicking and stomping away at The Preacher, down on the mat. Knick Man now hoisting himself onto the second turnbuckle... He leaps half-way across the ring and comes down across the back of The Preacher will a double axhandle blow! Knick Man picking
Preacher up, and now sending him into the ropes... STANDING DROPKICK BY THE KNICK MAN SENDS THE PREACHER BACK DOWN TO THE MAT!!! Cover now by the Knick Man... ONE!! That's all he got! Paul: Look at the Knick Man, just totally outsmarting this big dummy! Nick: Knick Man once again picking up The Preacher, and backs him into the corner... Chop on the chest of the big man! And another! And another! Wait a second, it looks like he's just firing The Preacher up!
The Preache r is burning a hole right through Knick Man's skull with that look! Knick Man continuing to chop away on the big man... NO EFFECT! Preacher grabs Knick Man... And throws him right into the corner! Right hand by the big man! And anothe roundhouse right! And another...
SENDS KNICK MAN OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TO THE FLOOR! Paul: This guy sure ain't no Saint! Nick: The Preacher deciding not go out after The Knick Man, and both men are taking a breather. The
Knick Man gets back up on the ring apron, and he's telling referee Rich Dengler to keep The Preacher back as he enters the ring. Knick Man's in, and both men are circling the ring... They lock-up! And
Preacher just shoves Knick Man down, and now Knick Man is backing himself into the corner, once again for a timeout.
Paul: I don't think there's any question that The Knick Man is gonna wrestle the smarter match!
Nick: Why's that? Paul: I told you! The Preacher ain't nothin' but a big dummy! Nick: You wouldn't say that if he was right here! Anyway, Knick Man back to his feet after some stalling, and they're locked-up again... No, Knick Man just kicked the knee of The
Preacher! And he kicks it again, and that brings Preacher down to one knee! Knick Man kicking his other knee, and he's got the big man down on the mat! Nice strategy being impliemented by the Knick Man!
Paul: Ya see, I told ya! Nick: Knick Man now taking the big man's leg and... Slamming it down to the mat! Knick Man driving the knee of The Preacher right down into the canvas! And another one! Knick Man now locking in a leg lace on the right leg of The Preacher, and referee Rich Dengler checks in...
Paul: I can hear 'im yellin' from here! "I give, I give!" Nick: The Preacher NOT GIVING UP, despite what my colleague says, and the crowd is getting behind
The Preacher! The Preacher now trying to gain momentum from the crowd, reaching for the ropes! Knick
Man trying to apply more pressure, but The Preacher is fighting... He's almost there, aaaaaaa and YES!!
The Preacher reaches the ropes, and Knick Man will be forced to release the hold! C'mon, let 'im go!
Dengler: ONE! TWO! THREE! FOUR! Paul: He broke on the count of four, it's legal Klien! Nick: The Preacher trying to get up, but The Knick Man is right on top of him with more kicks and stomps on the right knee! Knick Man bringing The Preacher back to the middle of the ring, picking him up, and slamming him right down to the mat with that bodyslam! Knick Man now gains some momentum from the ropes, and comes crashing down with a kneedrop right on the kneecap of
The Preacher... Knick
Man holding the right leg in place, and comes crashing down on it again with a kneedrop!
Paul: HA! The Preacher ain't even gonna be able to walk when The Knick Man's done with him!
Nick: Knick Man dragging The Preacher over to the ringpost, and putting him in a very comprimisable position! Knick Man goes to the outside and...OOOOOOOO!!! Rams The Preacher's leg right into that steel ringpost! Referee Richard Dengler trying to back the Knick Man away on the outside, but... BAM!!!
AGAIN THE KNICK MAN RAMS THAT LEG OF THE PREACHER INTO THE POST! Paul: This is great Klien... A masterpiece! Nick: Dengler finally getting the Knick Man back into the ring, but the damage has been done! Knick
Man just standing in the corner as The Preacher tries to fight to his feet... Ugh! The Preacher now getting up, NO! He was up, he was up, but he couldn't support the weight, and just fell back to the mat! The
Preacher is in trouble here tonight... Knick Man now approaching The Preacher, and Preacher is trying to fight his way, pulling himself up and using the Knick Man as a support! Knick
Man just laughing at him!
Knick Man: YOU ARE A SINNNER!!!!!!!!! Nick: Oh no, The Knick Man just hit The Preacher with a backhanded slap that sent him back down to the canvas! How humiliating! BUT WAIT! I think he lit a fire inside The
Preacher once again! The Preacher now finally fighting to his feet, hobling over to the Knick Man... Knick Man has his back turned, and he didn't see him coming! Clothesline by The Preacher sends The
Knick Man down to the
mat! Knick Man gets back up and charges at The Preacher... No, The Preacher catches him with a forearm shot right to the head, and now Preacher is setting him up... CHOKE
SL... NO! HIS LEG GAVE OUT! HIS LEG GAVE OUT! THE PREACHER TRIED A CHOKE SLAM ON THE KNICK MAN, BUT HE COULDN'T DO IT! Paul: C'mon, get it over with! Finish 'im off Knick Man! Nick: The Preacher back down on the mat, clutching at his knee... We may have another injury here tonight. Knick Man now finally back up on his feet, and he's capitalizing on the injury! Knick Man pulling on that bad right leg, and dragging The Preacher back to the center of the ring...
Paul: It's over, right here! Nick: Knick man has The Preacher in the middle of the ring... And he's laughing at the fallen man! Knick
Man now going for a figure four leglock! NO!! THE PREACHER ROLLS KNICK MAN
UP!!! SMALL PACKAGE!!! ONE!!! TWO!!!! THREE!!!!!! NO!!!! Rich Dengler says it was only two, and that was close! And
The Preacher is still
down on the mat... I think he may have further injured that knee in that small package! Knick Man back in control, stomping like a madman on that knee! The Knick Man tries to pick up
The Preacher... But the
man can't even stand!
Paul: Just tell the ref to ring the bell! Nick: I'd hate to admit it, but that's what Richard Dengler may want to do at this point. The man can't even stand for God's sake! The Knick Man isn't even sure what to do! No, wait... Aw, come on now!
Knick Man back on The Preacher, hooking him in a single-leg Boston crab, applying the pressure right on that right knee!
Paul: Ha! Listen to The Preacher scream for mercy! Nick: The Preacher screaming out, and there's nowhere to go! Dengler checking in with The Preacher... I think he's out! Rich Dengler calling for the bell, and this match is over!
Garrison: Ladies and gentlemen, your winner of the match... THEEEEEEEE KNIIIIIIIIIICK MAAAAAAAAN!!!!!!!!!!! Nick: The Knick Man has won the match, but... C'mon, break the hold! BREAK THE HOLD!!! Paul: Alright, uh... I think enough is enough here. Nick: The Knick Man refusing to break the hold, and look at that reverend that accomapnied the Knick

Referees have come out here, trying to get The Knick Man to let go and... Finally! Finally, Knick Man has broken the hold! And he's laughing in the ring... How dispicable! Oh yea, he's real proud of himself now!
Oh no, he's got a microphone! Knick Man: You see Preacher, you committed a grave sin! You disobeyed one of the ten commandments...
[The Knick Man opens the bible and begins to read] Knick Man: Thou shall not take the name of THE KNICK MAN, your WRESTLING GOD, in vain! Now I must cast judgement upon thee, FOOL! [The Knick Man takes the bible, and cracks it right across the head of The

Disgusting... [The scene fades to black as The Knick Man walks back to the locker room, laughing, and medics try to help The Preacher back inside the ring]
