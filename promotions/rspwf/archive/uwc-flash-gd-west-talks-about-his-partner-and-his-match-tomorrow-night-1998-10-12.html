[UWC] FLASH: GD West talks about his partner, and his match tomorrow night

12 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's the latest GD West flash for the UWC, which can also be found on the
UWC's Role Play Board:
http://www.cgiforme.com/cgibin/8351/wwwboard.html Remember, UWC Monday Night Madness is tomorrow night at 9pm EST, http://www.geocities.com/Hollywood/Bungalow/2153/mnm.htm As always, please post or EMail me with comments: ------------------ [SCENE: GD West's apartment. GD sits in his recliner, watching THE X-FILES, alternating his attention between that and an account of Gav-Dawg's time with the Towering Inferno. He seems a bit angered, as his feelings for the Inferno are known. Suddenly the phone rings]
WEST: [picking up phone] Gav?! If that's you, you got... MANGLOR: Ah, no GD, it's Joseph. You know, Joseph Manglor? WEST: Yeah yeah yeah...lucky you're NOT Gav-Dawg then... MANGLOR: Um, what's this about Gav-Dawg? And why are you still in Indianapolis?! WEST: Well if you thought I should be in Champaign by now, why are you goddamn CALLING ME in Indy, then, Joe?! And as for Gav-Dawg...he can make his friends with whatever worthless trash that runs around in the UWC. Hell, he can still associate with the iTg for all I friggin' care! But the fact is, he has one partner in that ring, in Champaign, against the Mercenary: GD West, the Circle City Rottweiler himself. And so what if we've been trainin'
together, the fact is he's off doin' his own thing, like he's gonna take on the Mercenary himself. And both you and I watched Hangover this morning...
MANGLOR: yeah... WEST: And what happened? He got his ass handed to him. I'm not so sure of this, Joe...maybe GD West hasn't made the right choice here. But the fact is,
GD West made a deal with Commissioner Sissen, he signed his name to teh dotted line for that match, where I tag with Gav-Dawg, and I fully plan on honoring that agreement, since GD West always lives up to his end, even if nobody else in the UWC thinks so. Mercenary, and everyone else who has said
I'm "without honor", let me tell you this: It will be an HONOR to
Westerminate you tomorrow night! And GD West doesn't do jack diddly unless he knows he's got a shot. And Joseph, I've ALWAYS got a shot, because I'm ready to rip the Mercenary and his Mystery Partner a brand new one tomorrow night, in Champaign, on Madness, and that's what it is!
MANGLOR: Any ideas on your end on who the Mercenary has chosen for his Mystery
Partner? WEST: I haven't got the slightest idea, Joe, but it don't matter, because he's gonna step up to his taste of the Real Thing as well! This match is the closest thing GD West has gotten to gold in the UWC _yet_., and although it's not The Belt, it's *a* belt, and I ain't gonna turn that down. I still have over 4 months to get that belt, so consider this a side trip along the scenic route of the UWC, if you will. And GD West is full on, ragin' to win, I'm at
100%, and Gav-Dawg better be as well, cuz otherwise there's gonna be some serious Hell to pay, and that's what it is!
MANGLOR: Gotcha. Thanks GD, see you in Champaign tomorrow! WEST: Yeah. (West hangs up the phone, and crumples the transcript of the
Inferno and Gav-Dawg up into a ball, and tosses it over his shoulder) Goddamn right his ass better be ready tomorrow...
