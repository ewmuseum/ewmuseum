Critique This. Rp for my wrestler

31 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"The scene opens and The Franchise is seen sitting in his living room watching some TV waiting for a reporter to come knocking on his door waiting to give the first interview of the BOB. He hears the doorbell and gets up.
He walks out into the hallway and he opens the door. A reporter about 5'7 comes in. He is wearing khaki shorts and a nice white golf t-shirt. Kaos is in ripped blue jeans and a wifebeater. Kaos leads the way into his rec room.
There is a bar over in the corner with a TV set up and a Pool tournament is on. Kaos walks up to his Pool table and brings all the balls out of the pocket and sets them down into the rack. Kaos racks and hands a hand carved wooden pool stick with a woman standing in a short dress on it to the reporter. Kaos grabs his black and gray graphite stick. The reporter breaks knocking a stripe in. He shoots again and doesn't make the shot. Alex takes his shot and sinks the 4 ball leaving him an open shot to sink the 3 ball in the left corner pocket. He makes the shot. Walks over to the chalk and chalks his stick"
Reporter: Well Alex, you do know why I am here right? "The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Yeah I know, you want your damn interview on my involvement in being in the Best of the Best tournament. So I am going to give it to you, about some of my favorites to win this tournament and some people I would like to meet in this. Also some people I would like to see be beat in this.
Reporter: Well I see you picture with a lot of the greats here. The Beast,
Eric Draven, Mr. Efx, Triple F, Nails Payne, Darkstar. Also amongst people is the ever mysterious Saoirse. A lot of famous people, Syko Sid from one of the Independant circuits you met him at. Also some of the people from the
EWCI tournament. The last tournament and first by that matter you were in.
You are here with Paladin and Iceberg in your Training Area. "The Franchise" Alex Kaos: I met a lot of people and a lot of people know who I am. They just don't recognize my real name. But they will soon recognize my style and I will talk about the name change and the major weight loss I put on.
"During this time Alex has been sinking shots and he is on the 8-ball at this point. He calls it in the right side pocket. Sinks it in with ease and then walks over to the bar. Alex goes behind"
"The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Would you like something to drink? Reporter: Umm, I will take scotch on the rocks. "Kaos gets out a glass and a frozen mug and he takes out a Budweiser and a bottle of scotch. He puts some ice in the small glass and pours in the scotch and slides down a coaster and puts the drink on top. He then pours the beer in the mug. Jackie Hard comes in the room and the camera spots a white leather couch. She takes a seat and grabs the channel changer. She flicks it onto her soap opera. Kaos puts another coaster down and puts his beer on it and then walks over to her and lets his hand down. She puts her hand in his and then they walk to the bar together. She takes a seat on one of the stool's and then Kaos proceeds to introduce the 2"
"The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Jackie meet Larry, Larry meet Jackie. "Larry grabs her hand and kisses her hand" Larry: Nice to meet you. Jackie: The same. I take it you are the reporter who is taking care of the interview with my fiancee about the upcoming BOB tournament.
Larry: That would be me. "Larry turns and sips his drink and he is now facing Alex. He then puts down a tape recorder on the bar and and proceeds to ask Alex questions and Alex starts walking around the room picking it up a little. The pool sticks and he is clearing the table of all the balls and putting them in the pockets.
He takes the pool sticks behind the counter and puts the graphite one up in the pool stick rack. He then untwists the wooden one and puts that in a glass case and places that under the bar"
Larry: Well Alex, how did you lose all that weight? You went from 287 pounds to 228 pounds. What caused the sudden weight drop?
"The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Well I was wrestling in the HCW where I was the
Insane Champion. During a match my back started aching really bad. I didn't know what it was. So I went to the doctor and it turned out I had an eating disorder. The pain was really in my stomach but with all my action and moving around so much it hurt my back. So I lost all this weight. Doctor's say I could put it back on at anytime. As of now, I have made a turn to do all these high flying moves. I still have most of my strength and I am still
6'7". So I don't want all that weight back right now.
Larry: And your former Identity, we all know and love you as him. Why the name change and how does it feel to be out of the asylum?
"The Franchise" Alex Kaos: You all know and love me as the Candyman. That's right I am the Candyman and most of you wouldn't remember me talking about my real name. Which is what I am using now. But it feels so damn good to be out of the asylum. I wrestled the whole EWCI in there and now I am out and I am going to enjoy it while it lasts because one day when I am ready I will go back to my old ways and I will back back on top of the Insane Game. But for now, I am going to take a turn at the Cruiserweight division. And for now I will be doing more hardcore things than ever now that I can move my body better, So be prepared for an all out aerial attack when your in the ring with me.
Larry: How about your first round match with "Devastating" Dean Perry? He is
3 inches smalls and 17 pounds heavier. This could be one very good match between you 2. We guess his friend is Lunatikk Crippler. So we are basing his style similar to him. The match should go on and be one to go down in the record books. Hopefully in your favor here.
"The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Yes, I am basing his style on the Crippler's and yes it should be a very good match. I am only here to make my presence felt.
It doesn't matter if I make it to the end and win the damn thing. I am just here to prove to the world that yes, I can hang with the elite. I have brought in some friends and I am sure they will prove to be very very good.
For now though, I am taking the Devastating one on my top priority list. I am coming at him first and I will prove to be the better man this Friday when our match takes place. I hope the president comes to watch my defeat this opponent of mine. I must say I have never heard of Dean Perry before but then again I have only heard of most of the opponents here once before.
So I am pulling out all stops to prove to the world why I should win this match. I am a one year veteran, I know most of the people here have a little more experience but now that I am a little lighter. Or I should say a lot I should move a lot faster and I will be putting more than my heart into each match. My heart can only put out 100% but with my body ready to go the distance 115% is going at him. I have been training and I am ready to redeem myself and I will with the hopes and dreams that I carry of my family and friends, I should make it to at least the third round. Dean Perry stands in my way and he will be the one who is devastated when I pull out all these fantastic moves out of my bag of tricks and I will show why I am called "The
Franchise". Maybe not the Franchise of this tournament but I will prove to become the man and the legend. There is no myth about me yet but there will be many stories to come. On all the way of when I could have defeated Dean
Perry and why I didn't. If I am going to win I am going to injure and hurt the person who I am fighting. Sorry to say it must be this man named Dean. I will have the fans support, even though I don't really need it, but I will have it on my way to victory. This world is not big enough for all the men in this tournament and we will see the Crash of '99. Meaning the crash landing of many superstars that stand in the ways of the elite. I am one who may be called Too Sweet for the Sweet 16 so I will move onto the Elite 8.
That is my goal and no man not even Perry or Smirtdogg, will stop in my way to the top. I am bringing all that I have and Perry you better do the same.
I won't hold back on you, not even if you were my best friend. I am too focused to lose this early. I could care less about moving on right now. I am just focusing on my win against you on this Friday. It will prove to be on of the best matches in the history of this tournament and I will prove that it will be one of my best victories. I am not going to guarantee the win but I am going to carry out my goal and I will become the winner of our match. Nothing will hold me down for this match and I will prove to everyone, even the president of the US if he shows up. I have 2 kids that want me to win. My 2 kids. Alex Jr. and Lyssie. So I am dedicating this match to them. They will be with their mother during the tournament but I know they are rooting for me on TV. Devastation will be seen and it will be
Perry who sees it first hand.
Larry: Wow, I will get more comments a little later. Right now, who are your favorites to win this tournament?
"The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Don't stop you little "Alex starts mumbling to himself a little. Jackie looks on and she is like amazed at all that he had to say"
Larry: I am sorry Alex. My fault. "Larry who has been sipping on his scotch while Kaos talked finally finished it. Kaos with half a mug left downs the whole thing and then shakes his head and looks more peaceful into the camera"
"The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Alright well now that you have stopped me I may as well answer your question. My favorite is probably my good friend Eric
Dane. Others would have to be Jack Daniels, Haywood, Ray Wilmott, and the
Dark Prince. As you noticed none from the top half because I plan on taking that. Meeting up with someone who I just mentioned in the finals. Haha, no I don't even plan on making it to the finals myself. But I still will say it.
Larry: How about, who you would like to see be beat in the first or second round. "The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Well I would like to see Dean Perry lose his first match sending him packing because of my aerial skills. But I am just as... sorry trailing off the subject. I will get back to that. I would like to see Thorne Richards because he is fighting a friend and one of my favorites to win. I would also like to see even though he is one of my favorites to win, Haywood Jublome. HBK Shawn Michaels is another I would like to see fall. Mainly because I don't like real wrestlers, I don't care who you are all that you are is a ripoff of the real thing and that is why I don't like you. Thats all for the people I would like to see fall.
Larry: Now who would you like to fight in this tournament? "The Franchise" Alex Kaos: I would like to meet none other than Haywood
Jublome. I would like to see him fall at my hands, he beat me last time we met and now I think things could be a little different only if I were to be more focused. The other person I would like to meet is Eric Dane, one of my best friends. Never fought before and this would be a first. Never have found out who the better man was and we have known each other since the UCW back in October and November. But I will only prove to be the better man in all of this. Hopefully. Haha.
Larry: Alright now you can continue about your upcoming match. "The Franchise" Alex Kaos: I need another beer before I can do anything. You need another Larry. How about you Jackie.
"Jackie had Larry's cup and was rubbing her fingers around it. She looks up and shakes her head"
Jackie: Yeah, give me a White Russian. Thanks Alex. Larry: Yeah I will take another. "A knock at the door. We here a few footsteps and Cadbury, the doorman, walks in the room" Cadbury: Ms. Jackie. Your friends have arrived. They have requested you come with them to the mall. They have also requested to take your car. What shall
I tell them Ms.
Jackie: Tell them I am going to get the keys. Be out in minute. Meet me in the garage. Cadbury: As you wish Madam. "Cadbury leaves the room and Jackie gets up and runs around the bar and kisses Alex on the cheek and whispers something to him. She reaches in his pocket and pulls out the keys to the Hummer"
Jackie: Thanks, be back later. Umm, I forgot money. Can I have some Alex? "The Franchise" Alex Kaos: Yeah $200 should be good for what you want. I take it I have to cancel your drink?
Jackie: Yeah, and are you sure $200. Don't you want it really sexy? "Alex nods his head and then throws down another Benjamin on the bar. Jackie grabs the money and kisses him again and leaves the room shaking her ass.
The camera goes back to Alex and he just has this smile on his face. He brings out a bottle of Love Lotion Cherry Flavored"
"The Franchise" Alex Kaos: This was my idea of foreplay. She wants to go out and buy all this nice lingerie to tease me with. I just want it going right into it. Ya know what I mean?
Larry: Yeah I know what you mean. "Alex pours another Budweiser and brings the bottle of scotch back out. He puts some ice in the glass and pours it. They both have a drink and Larry is the first person to say something"
Larry: Alright, add anything else you need to your first part of Devastating
Dean Perry. "The Franchise" Alex Kaos: You see I mentioned all of this high flying crap and now I am going to say that I still have all of my hardcore brawling moves with me. I am very technically skilled wrestler and I will prove these skills as well. There is much I am going to prove and and this is only a few of them. Proving my greatness is the other and I will be the man for the best of this tournament. I was brought here not to become the best, but to bring the best here. Now Dean Perry will be fighting for his very life against the best of this tournament. I am creating the dynasty of the
Candyman and I will be the Greatest if not the World's Best wrestler. I didn't focus nearly enough on the EWCI as I am focusing on the BOB. The EWCI was only to get my name out. Now the BOB is to prove to Dean Perry and all my other opponents why I am the best. Larry it was nice having you here and it is time for you to leave. I leave with a phrase. Bring your best because your best isn't good enough Perry.
"Kaos gets up and they walk away Alex leads the way out of the rec room. Cadbury sees them and he leaves the top the garage. Kaos opens the front door and it breaks it sweat on both of them. We see the tree skyline and it is still burning hot and the sun is setting. The sky looks beautiful and then Cadbury drives up in a little volvo. He gets out and hands the keys to
Larry. Larry shakes his hand and leaves down the long driveway. Kaos keeps the door open for Cadbury they walk back into the rec room and Cadbury cleans up as Alex racks another game"
Handler of "The Franchise" Alex Kaos. At this moment only active in this tournament but my book is full.
