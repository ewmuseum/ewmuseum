The Aerial Daredevils (Name Subject to Change) is a newly created tag team consisting of "The Case Dawg" Casey Stanfield and "Blue Lightning" Brian Maher.
They are both Cruiserweights. Here is some more info on them. Wrestler's Name: "The Case Dawg" Casey Stanfield Height: 6'2 Weight: 245 pounds Age: 24 Hometown: Schenectady, New York Allignment: Face Appearance: Hispanic male. Very 'handsome'. No facial scars or blemishes. He has light brown eyes, and dark brown hair. His hair is short, faded on the sides, and curly on top.
Ring Attire: He wears white tights. They say Case Dawg in black letters on the back, and have neon red lighting bolts over the rest of the pants. He also wears a dog collar on ocassion, And black boots. He also has Red ones with white letters, and neon black lighting bolts and white boots. And black ones with red letters and white neon lightning bolts and red boots.
Theme Music: "Around the World" by Daft Punk Wrestling Style: Methodical and technical with some aerial Finishing Move: The Lockjaw Description: Red shorts with black stiching and black sneakers. Finisher #2: Frog Splash Signature Maneuvers: Flying Kneedrop, Flying Elbow Drop Setup Maneuvers: Bulldog --> Lockjaw String Moves (done in sucession when Stanfield is on a roll) these require a lot of flipping or lifting 1. Backbreaker --> shoulder breaker --> tombstone piledriver 2. Inverted Atomic Drop --> Powerbomb --> Diamond Cutter --> Elbowdrop To The Back Of their Head 3. Spinebuster --> Boston Crab 4. Back Suplex --> German Suplex --> Backdrop Driver Wrestler's Name: "Blue Lightning" Brian Maher Height: 6' Weight: 229 Age: 23 Hometown: Saratoga, New York Allignment: Face Appearance: Korean Male. Dark Skin. He has short jet black hair that is combed forward so it hangs over his face
Ring Attire: He wears black jean shorts that have white stiching. He also wears black sneakers.
Theme Music: "Around the World" by Daft Punk Wrestling Style: High Flying Daredevil Finishing Move: Blue Lightning Special Description: Firebird Special (450 Splash) Finisher 2: Second Strike Description: He starts outside the ring on the apron. He runs towards the corner, and runs up the cornerpost does and does a springboard carwheel Senton.
It looks like something someone would do off a diving board. Signature Maneuvers: Lightning Star Press - Shooting Star Press Setup Maneuvers: Rollover Samoan Drop (Ala Saturn) String Moves (Moves Chained Together Like Benoit's Three Rolling German Suplexes) these require a lot of flipping or lifting 1. Manhattan Drop --> Spinebuster --> Boston Crab 2. Powerbomb --> Giant Swing 3. Shoulderbreaker --> Tombstone V.
