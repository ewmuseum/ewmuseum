After we beat the hell out of you four losers, we get to fight in some specail match where the winners get title shots. We you better fucking believe I am winning that match. I don't give a damn who I have to beat the hell out of.
Weither its that pansy Lord Lionheart who is too damn scared of me to even acknowledge me kicking his ass on Fallout with a baseball bat, or Diamond,
Jasmine, Floyd or any other piece of American Trash I don't give a damn. I will be the last man standing after that match, and Jax when the time comes to step into the ring, and beat the hell out of whatever champion you put be for me. I want you to hand me the belt after I beat the crap out of your worthless champion.
Look at these champions, Tripple X is holding onto a title everyone knows should be mine. Jasmine is holding onto a title that she knows I beat her for but thanks to that dipshit Lionheart i'm stuck here in blackball country.

If you think my war path has ended now that Warfare is upon on us, you got anther thing coming. Jax....if I were you, i'd be afriad, afriad for the your life and the lives of every American that plans to walk out to the ring at Warfare. You have ignored me in the past and you've seen what has happened. Do it at Warfare, and something bad will happen.
