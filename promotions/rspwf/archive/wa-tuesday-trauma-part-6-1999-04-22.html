WA Tuesday Trauma - Part 6

22 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bealzebub [Champion] vs. "Bad Boy" Marcus T. [Challenger] IWA Intercontinental Heavyweight Title Match Tony Black: As the evening draws on, I am in total suspense! What exactly IS the BIG surprise for the end of the card? What is it? A returning superstar?
You know, there is talk in the locker rooms and at the WWC that Heartbreak
Meyers is looking for a fed to join.
Chris Clay: Wake up Tony! The guy won four world titles! What would he have to prove? Tony Black: Well, I for one would still love to see him return. Chris Clay: Focus Tony! We have a title match coming up! Tony Black: Indeed we do. Bealzebub, the sickest man in wrestling, ad the reigning IWA Intercontinental heavyweight champion, takes on the IWA TV champion, "Bad Boy" Marcus T.!
Chris Clay: This is a rematch, Tony. When Bealzebub made his shocking return to the IWA some time ago, it was against Marcus T.!
Tony Black: That was a victory for Bealzebub! He went on to capture the vacant IC title by cooking his brother at the IWA Pay Per View "Fade to
Black."
Chris Clay: Let's go to the ring, Tony! This match is set to begin! Tony Black: Keep in mind, wrestling fans, only Bealzebub's IC title is up for grabs in this one! Marcus has nothing to lose!
Chris Clay: He already assaulted Bealzebub at the top of the show! Well, referee Mike Pulver is ready to call this one! Bealzebub is in the ring, but he looks distracted somewhat. Marcus looks focused. That could mean a lot in this contest's outcome!
Tony Black: The bell has been rung! Marcus starts quickly, jumping Bealzebub even before the bell went! Marcus hurts Bealzebub with hard fists ad a stunning headbutt!
Chris Clay: And you know Marcus T.'s skull is bulletproof! Tony Black: Marcus drops Bealzebub with a drop toe hold and slams a fist into the back of the Intercontinental champion's head! BBMT tries for a figure four, but Bealzebub craftily reverses the attempt into a small package! 1-2-BBMT escapes!
Chris Clay: Bealzebub pelts BBMT with a quick inverted atomic drop and drives him down with a bulldog! Bealzebub picks BBMT up and powerbombs him!
Tony Black: Bealzebub with a cover! 1-2-The TV champ escapes! Chris Clay: Bealzebub is storming on the offensive now! He scoops BBMT up and buries him with a face-first powerbomb!
Tony Black: BBMT is reeling in anguish! Bealzebub picks him up in a fireman' s carry and squishes BBMT with a Samoan drop! Bealzebub with a cover! 1-2-BBMT escapes! Chris Clay: Both men get up at about the same time. Bealzebub retaliates with a low blow and snaps the IC champ down with a snap suplex!
Tony Black: BBMT backs into the corner and mounts the middle rope. He leaps. Nice elbowdrop! Chris Clay: BBMT with the cover! 1-2-Bealzebub escapes! They get up simultaneously and hit the ropes. BBMT ducks a clothesline from the IC champion and nails Bealzebub with a drop-kick! Bealzebub jumps right back to his feet and rakes BBMT's eyes!
Tony Black: The IC champ nails the TV champ with a side Russian legsweep! Bealzebub scoops BBMT up and plats him in the center of the ring with a side suplex! He covers BBMT! 1-2-The TV titlist escapes yet once again!
Chris Clay: Bealzebub picks BBMT up and tries a DDT, but BBMT reverse and nails the IC champ with a belly-to-belly suplex! BBMT picks Bealzebub up and locks him in a painful abdominal stretch! Bealzebub's guts are gonna spill out all over the canvas!
Tony Black: Oh, Bealzebub uses a hiptoss to escape! But BBMT is right back on the attack! He scratches and claws at Bealzebub's face, tearing his flesh apart! BBMT whips Bealzebub to the ropes. High back body drop! BBMT goes to the top rope. He jumps and nails a guillotine legdrop! BBMT covers
Bealzebub! 1-2-Beal escapes! BBMT picks Bealzebub up and hits a swinging neckbreaker followed by three consecutive elbowdrops!!!
Chris Clay: Bealzebub fights back! He leaps up, early decapitates his opponent with a lariat and hurts BBMT with a big boot! He grabs BBMT by the throat and chokeslams his ass!
Tony Black: How did he grab him by the throat and end up chokeslamming his ass? Chris Clay: Nevermind! Bealzebub sends BBMT to the ropes and snaps his back with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker! Bealzebub picks BBMT up again and piledrives him!
Tony Black: Bealzebub hurts BBMT with a shoulderbreaker and then a brainbuster! BBMT is nearly done for! Bealzebub plants BBMT on the top rope and goes up with him! Bealzebub wants to finish him off with a superplex!
Bealzebub goes for it. NO! BBMT hooks Bealzebub's leg and comes down right on him! Bealzebub is pinned! Referee Pulver administers the count! 1-2-3!
BBMT is now the IWA IC AND TV champion!!!!! What an upset this was!!! Winner: "Bad Boy" Marcus T. (Marcus becomes the new IWA Intercontinental
Heavyweight Champion!!!)
