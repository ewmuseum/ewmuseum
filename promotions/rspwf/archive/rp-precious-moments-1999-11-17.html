<RP> Precious Moments

17 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We open where we left off, with The Binky Fairy following The Winky Fairy through the streets of Detroit, as Winky is in a trance-like state, muttering
"Wafflehouse" over and over again. Winky doesn't seem to know where he's going, nor does he care, as he runs directly in a police officer. Both men go down, and as they get up, Winky is still staring straight ahead)
The Winky Fairy: (In a monotone voice) Excuse me, officer. I was wondering if you could direct me to the nearest Wafflehouse.
Police Officer: Wafflehouse? What are you talking about? There are no Wafflehouse's in Michigan. You're not from around here, are you? And... WHAT THE HELL ARE YOU WEARING?! TWF: I am wearing what is the normal attire for residents of Fairyland. If you don't like it, I will take my wand and...
(Binky's face turns to that of fright, as he runs over and covers Winky's mouth) The Binky Fairy: You must excuse him. He's not been feeling well lately. PO: Um... ok. Whatever you say there, buddy. Just keep your "wand" away from me. I'm not that way.
TBF: Of course you're not, silly. You're a human, not a fairy. PO: Damn straight. (With that, Binky and Winky continue walking, Winky in the same trance) TBF: What's wrong with you, Winky? You almost threatened a human in uniform! TWF: We must find the Wafflehouse. TBF: Didn't you hear him? He said there were none in Michigan. TWF: We must find the Wafflehouse. TBF: That's it! (Binky runs in front of Winky, and reaches deep into his leotard. He pulls out... a tire iron. He looks at it, shakes his head, and tosses it away. He pulls out... a monkey wrench. Another shake of his head, and another discarded tool. Again he reaches in, so deep the his arm disappears into his tights, and he pulls out... a wand. He nods, smiles, and then looks directly at Wink)
TBF: I didn't want to do this, Winky. But you've left me no choice. (With that, Binky touches Winky lightly on his forehead. Winky collapses to the ground, twitching, as Binky looks on sadly. Finally, Winky regains his bearings, and pulls himself to his feet)
TWF: What... what happened? TBF: You were going on and on about some kind of wafflehouse. I had to use my magic fairy wand to knock the evil out of your head.
TWF: Evil was in me? (Winky's eyes well up, turning a slight shade of red, and then he bursts into tears, grabbing onto Binky tight)
TWF: I'M SO SORRY, BINKY! TBF: It's ok, it's ok... everything's alright now. (As the two embrace each other tightly, three Asian men walk by, cameras in their hands, and start taking pictures of Binky and Winky. Then they turn to a building and start taking pictures of that)
Asian Man #1: Ahhh... we now own dat building. Very, very good. Asian Man #2: Ahhh... we also own dat building. Slowly but surely, we are buying America. Asian Man #3: Ahhh... soon we will own the world. All three: Oh ho ho ho ho ho ho... (The Asian men walk away, as Binky and Winky stare, in disbelief) TBF: Own the... (gulp) world? TWF: They... they must be evil. TBF: Thusly, they must be stopped! TWF: We'll start Wednesday. TBF: What's Wednesday? TWF: You know... Motor City Madness. The AWMC card. TBF: Huh? TWF: AWMC... it's wrestling. TBF: OH! What will we do there? TWF: We will take on The China Connection. TBF: Yeah! That's it! We'll take those pesky slanty-eyed humans down from the very top! (Binky and Winky start walking again, with no particular destination) TWF: Did you know that China has the largest per capita rate in the world? TBF: Ummm... no. What are you talking about? TWF: It means they have the most humans. And it keeps getting bigger because they keep breeding.
TBF: Breeding? TWF: Reproducing. TBF: Repro-what? (Winky bends over, and whispers something into Binky's ear) TBF: AHHHH! That's disgusting! They must be stopped at all costs! TWF: And they will. Fairy help us, they will. TBF: Hey, plus those Hate guys are gonna be there! Maybe we can get that tape from them then!
TWF: Good idea! We'll show them that evil doesn't pay. (Binky and Winky keep walking, as we pan down to a sewer grate. We pan down further, then into it. The water rushes by and as it does, it forms three distict words. TO BE CONTINUED. We fade to black)
