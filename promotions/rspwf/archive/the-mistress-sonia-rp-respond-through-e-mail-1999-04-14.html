The Mistress Sonia RP {Respond through E-mail}

14 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[[The scene fades in at a hotel where there are men in suits playing the violins with skill, grace and elegance. In the middle is a fountain with the sparkle of a thousand stars shining brightly as the light of the ceiling lights bounces off. The people walk in quietly telling stories of their past. And then an asian woman in a leather tanktop and pants walk in. Her hair was as black as a raven's darkest feather. Her skin tan to put other women to shame. Her eyes as blue as a ocean after a hurricane passed through it. Her beauty seemed to attract the attention of even the most married men and she loved it. ]]
[Lady] What good reactions these people seem to have. Now, where is the camera. [[She looks around the fountain, the men in suits, and the people and to find the camera at the T.V.]] Aw, there it is. [[She sits down slowly as if to shout out her elegance and beauty to all in the room.]] It is an honor for you to hear my voice to speak to you. But also a foreshadow that your doom is coming.
I am The Mistress Sonia, one of the soon to be most feared women in wrestling. I'm sure some of you already heard of me. I am the master of the most devastating tag team known to earth. Pain & Suffering.
[[She sees a teapot and a cup. She picks it up and pours some tea into her cup and pours very little suger and slowly and carefully stirs the tea. She picks it up and takes a sip and sighs. She slowly places it back down on a saucer.]]
[Mistress Sonia] But, for you men, I will not bring my pets into this federation. I shall do the work myself. Even a king must sometimes go out into battle to face it's foe.... to crush....to elimate him from the ranks. That's what I'm going to show you people. That I shall get off my throne in my kingdom of torture and walk on the battlefield to destroy the enemy. And my enemy are all who are not under my control. People who are in chaos, people who are in despair. People who are in need of someone to look up to and follow. I have placed that responsiblity upon my shoulders to go against all odds to bring back peace and law to chaos and anarchy.
[[A man walks up to her]] [Man] Excuse me, would you care to go out with me? [[Mistress Sonia breaks out her six foot long whip and lashes him across the face giving him a deep cut across the cheek]]
[Man] Aw, you &^$%#! [[He raises his arm to slap her, but she quickly jumps out of her chair and grabs his arm and throws him onto the table and wraps her whip around his neck]]
[Mistress Sonia] The price for insubordination is death. [[She squeezes the whip tighter but then releases him]] But you are not worth it. Get out of here and learn to fight. Thanks to that tasteless display that brings me to another topic for you men. I am not someone to come after unless you can put up a remarkable fight. Even if you do, you still shall lose. I am a Mistress and I have no master. I will not be dominated. Only the weak can be dominated which is why I came. To dominate the weak and to make them a strong army to serve me in completing my vision. A world where there shall be no war, and no hatred.
These people will serve me for that cause. A better world, and better place. Where there is only one throne. One government. One leader. And from that throne... _I_ shall watch over you. I shall be that government. I shall be that leader. I shall crush every rebellion with my iron fist but I grant you that I shall rule with an open mind.
[[She takes another sip of her tea]] [Mistress Sonia] Now, I'm sure you ladies will rebel. I mean, your jealous of my brilliance, my elegance, and my beauty. You want to eliminate me for you do not understand me. I understand it's human nature. "Destroy what you don't know" is mostly the first rule of the human race. I don't die, I don't get destroyed. You can only surpress me, but you can only surpress someone for so long. And then they come back... WITH A VENGEANCE! But I am above vengeance...
I come back with DOMINANCE! Domination is the only way to come back. To take over not just the body...but the mind as well...and more importantly....domination of the soul! You ladies....you amazon women....you barbarians...you uncultured people. Your primative ways will become useful one day when I dominate your ass. You will become a loyal army of my slaves. A army that will continue to grow and continue to conquer. I am a storm which can not dissipate, that will not just go away, that will not blow over. I am a virus that spreads quicker than the black plague. Only this disease can't be cured.
It can only be accepted and obeyed. Nothing else is tolerable. You can not resist it.... and you can not fight it. You can only bend to it. Only bow before it as it walks above you. But if you care to fight out how strong I am.
Step up and challange my power. I'm sure you'll be pleasently surprised.
