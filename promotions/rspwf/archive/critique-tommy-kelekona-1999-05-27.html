[CRITIQUE] Tommy Kelekona

27 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all... this is my first roleplay I've done for this character in well over
2 years... please tell me what your thoughts...
====================== <<The scene opens up in a cemetary... the weather has taken a turn for the worse as the dark grey sponges above look prime for a good squeezing. Walking alone in this cemetary, wearing a pair of tan khaki shorts, a pair of sandals, and a floral hawaiian print shirt is Tommy Kelekona. This young man of obvious polynesian heritage keeps track of the gravestones as he walks past them, twirling a single white rose in his left hand as he notes the names engraved on the tombstones untill he comes to a complete stop before a white marble headstone.>>
Tommy Kelekona [under his breath, reading the engraving]: "Here lies Jo
Jo..." [Kelekona kneels down on the grave, places a kiss on the headstone as he sets the single white rose down, leaning it against the marble.]
Tommy Kelekona: Happy birthday, old friend. [Kelekona stands up to his full 6'0", 225 pound frame. The young man takes a few steps backwards as backs up against the nearest gravestone, sits down leaning up against the back of the marker as he stares out onto the grave of his longtime friend.]
Tommy Kelekona: Jo Jo... remember the time when we were in the IWA? [laughs to himself] Yeah... we had some good times, buddy.
[Tears begin to swell in the eyes of Tommy Kelekona... the scene begins to fade to black and white as a flashback ensues....]
<<Sometime in the Spring of 1997, IWA event in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.>>
[Kelekona is in the middle of his match with Prince Fajita. Tommy appears to have the match in control as the action spills to the outside where things begin to turn ugly. Tommy goes for a piledriver on the concrete floor, which is blocked by Fajita as he backdrops the native Hawaiian hard onto the cold floor. Fajita then picks up Tommy and delivers a devastating brainbuster to the young man, knocking him out as all of a sudden a monkey jumps on the
Prince Fajita and proceeds to beat him over the head with a banana. Irate over the foolishness... Prince Fajita grabs the monkey off his back as he proceeds to powerbomb it onto a steel ladder which was used earlier on in the match. The concussion blow from the ladder knocks the monkey unconscious as
Kelekona slowly comes to his senses...]
<< Fade back to the present, Tommy Kelekona is wiping the tears from his eyes as replays the event over, and over in his mind.>>
Tommy Kelekona: I miss you so much, Jo Jo... everyday I think of you. Everyday I wonder how you're doing up there... everyday I wonder why you had to go so soon.
[Tommy stands up, stretches out his arms as he begins to feel a few rain drops on his arms.]
Tommy Kelekona: Jo Jo... I've decided to once again step into the sport that took your life... I'll be traveling to Mid-South, where I will once again continue our fight for respect in this business... I will carry on our revolution Jo Jo... I will not let your dream die. Our exceptance into society will one day come, but before that happens... blood will be spilled, and vengeance will be made. This, I promise you, Jo Jo.
[With that said, Tommy Kelekona casually begins to walk back to a cab parked at entrance to the cemetary. As Kelekona approaches the cab, he turns around and gives one last look at the graveyard.]
Tommy Kelekona: Shaka... brah. [Tommy enters the cab, motions for him to take him to the airport as the light rain turns into a heavy downpour.]
<< Screen fades to White >>
