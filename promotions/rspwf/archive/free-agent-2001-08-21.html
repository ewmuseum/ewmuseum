Hey all, I'm looking for an e-fed with a fairly decent level of competition where I can handle my tag team, Los Dos Bros, and have some fun doing it. I will fill in a more detailed application on request, as I will sample RP's.
Cheers! Their stats are: Wrestler Name: Los Conquistador Magnifico From: Gijon, Spain (alledgedly) Height: 6'3 Weight: 227lbs Entrance Music: "Rendez-Vu" by Basement Jaxx Attire: A selection of gaudy tights (neon green and black being recurring favourites) and a selection of even more ludicrous luchadore masks.
Gimmick: Claims to be a Spanish luchadore, and speaks in an attrocious
Spanish accent, which laspes frequently into an American accent. The
LCM gimmick has been used by several wrestlers (notably the original
LCM Edward De Villiers and his tag team partner Nick Wright for some screwjob wins). But a new man has taken up the mantle. The only thing he has in common with is predecessors is his high flying recklessness and taste for violence.
Wrestling Information: Primary Style: Reckless Highflyer Secondary Style: Hardcore/Brawler Favourite Moves: 1. Tornado DDT 2. Missile Drop Kick 3. Flying Clothesline 4. Airplane Spin 5. Swinging Neckbreaker 6. Abdominal Stretch into a DDT 7. Figure Four Sleeperhold 8. Asai Moonsault 9. Hurracanrana 10. Senton Bomb (not a bloody Swanton either.) Finshing Move: Conquistador Press Description: 450 Splash Wrestler Name: The Hooded Hatstand From: Unknown Height: 6' Weight: 208lbs Entrance Music: "Come back Johnny" by DEVO Attire: A circa 1991 Hulk Hogan "HULK RULES" tank top, black cotton shorts, trianers, a purple luchadore mask (lacking a mouth) and a pink cape.
Gimmick: Probably the most pathetic wrestler to have entered the squared circle, the Hooded Hatstand is rumoured to have learned to wrestle from the 1986 NES classic "Pro-Wrestling." Having never won a match in his life, the Hooded Hatstand has taken to tag team wrestling with his "brother" Los Conquistador Magnifico.
Wrestling Information: Primary Style: Dirty rotten, low down cheater Secondary Style: Hit and Run (that being run before he gets hit.) Favourite Moves: 1. A sloppy form of suplex 2. Pimp Slap 3. Eye gouge 4. Low blow 5. DDT 6. Foot on throat choke 7. Bionic Elbow 8. Knee Lift 9. Powder in the eyes 10. Small package (which he always screws up and pins himself with.) Finshing Move: The Hooded Hatstand has no finishing move. Instead he usually goes for a brass knuckle laden right hand smash to get the job done. Or another illegal object from within his tights.
Description: N/A Tag Team Name: Los Dos Bros Entrance Music: The theme from "Bonanza" Team Finisher: Rocket Launcher Description: The Hooded Hatstand climbs the top rope and is slammed down onto a prostate opponent by LCM.
If anyone willing to have me on their roster could e-mail me on [detail withheld] I would be very much obliged.
