[DEBUT FLASH] 'Youth Uprising' [DEBUT FLASH]

9 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey! Just wanted a few comments on my debut RP for my new team, 'Youth Uprising'. I was kinda unsure of how to roleplay them, so I'd really like to know what people think. For a quick bit of background, I thought I'd go for something along the lines of the 'young' gimmick people were talking about a coupla days ago.
So, these two are 19-year-old indy stars, and this is, like, their last indy show, or something. Before they move on to CCW.
So... get to readin'! Enjoy! New York. The staff, apparently, have had some success in brightening up an otherwise dingy room; the walls are washed a cheerful, summery yellow colour, and the cracks in the plasterwork have been covered over with posters promoting works of classic literature.]
[Sat, by himself, in the middle of the empty room, is Patrick Milan. Propped against one of the desks, he slips a charcoal-grey pocket T-shirt over his sculptured upper body, his jaw rough with stubble, his dark hair closely cropped. He picks up a pair of white wrestling boots, from the floor, and drops them into his black, canvas hold-all.]
[From this angle, we can just see through the open door, as, in the corridor outside, Milan's life-long friend Eddie Casper, an ice-pack pressed to his temple, slaps his hand into the open palm of another, shadowy figure, and shakes it.]
[JEFF]: "See ya' around, Casp. It was great havin' ya' on board..." [CASPER]: "Cheers, Jeff." [JEFF]: "And, uh... good luck, and sh[bleep]. With CCW. They' ain't two more desrvin' guys I can think of than than you and 'Trick."
[Eddie laughs.] [CASPER]: "Thanks, man. Take care." [He slaps Jeff on his bare shoulder, and they part. The man's footfall echoes, and fades. Casper enters the classroom, bathing himself in the white, strip lighting. Now, more clearly, we can take in every aspect of his person.]
[A compact individual, Casper looks to stand at a height of no more than, say, five-eleven. Washboard muscles glisten with perspiration, classic, black matt wrestling trunks adorn his impressive frame. His hair, like
Milan's, is shaved to within an inch of his scalp, a three-day-old growth of stubble shadowing his jaw. The bag of ice held to his forehead is stained crimson at the edges as he scans over the room with his eyes, and sighs.]
[CASPER]: "Well... this is one thing I _won't_ miss..." [MILAN]: "What?" [CASPER]: "Having to get ready in places like this. High school classrooms. Toilet cubicles. Sh[bleep]t like that. Y'know?" [MILAN]: "Heh. Yeah." [Patrick pulls a plain, black leather jacket around his shoulders, as Casper begins to unlace his boots.]
[CASPER]: "Oh yeah. The pay. Of course. How could I forget? God knows I won't be missing _that._"
[He laughs again, and Milan joins in. Eddie pulls off his boot, and tosses it into a hold-all of his own. Leaving the ice pack on the table at his side, he dabs at the open gash now visible on his forehead with a towel, smearing it pink slightly.]
[MILAN]: "Sheesh. How's your head?" [CASPER]: "Oh... 'be alright. Nothing I haven't had before." [He steps into a pair of black, Nike running pants, throwing the towel round his shoulders. Milan stands, cleaned up, ready to go, as Casper dresses himself in an off-white, hooded sweater.]
[MILAN]: "So... you think you're ready?" [CASPER]: "For what?" [MILAN]: "The big time. The big show. National TV... _every week._" [Eddie looks right at his best friend, and smirks.] [CASPER]: "I am if you are, partner." [Milan nods.] [MILAN]: "Well, it's gonna be tough. No doubt about that. There's a lot of people been where we've been, and not made it. We can't shut ourselves off from that, bud."
[He pauses.] [MILAN]: "But you and me, Eddie... we've got what it takes. I'm sure of it. Sampson's said it more times than I can remember. Heh. And, thank God, CCW thinks so too." [CASPER]: "Well - it won't be a walk in the park, 'Trick. Not by a long shot. I've been watching these CCW tapes for the past _week,_ and these are the big leagues. Some of the best talent in the country."
[MILAN]: "Oh yeah. No doubt." [CASPER]: "But I'm not gonna let this slip away. I mean - look at me. Look at _us._ Nineteen years old. Three-year pros. There's guys go their whole f[bleep]in' _career_ and never make it somewhere like CCW. No way I'm gonna let myself make it this far, just so I can throw it all away.
Not a _chance,_ 'Trick." [He throws the rest of his gear in the bag.] [MILAN]: "You're right. It's only up from here... if we _want_ it to be." [CASPER]: "Uh-huh. And we can do it, bud. We can pull this off. I _know_ it!" [Slinging the bag over his shoulder, Casper nods at his partner, and they head for the door, side-by-side.]
[MILAN]: "So... you wanna hit the gym tomorrow? 'Cause I'm running _real_ short of time, with all this packing crap."
[CASPER]: "Sure. I pro'lly won't be able to make any training any time next week, so I'd better do what I can."
[MILAN]: "Eleven sound good to you?" [CASPER]: "Fine. I've got this _great_ new move that I wanna show ya'..." [MILAN]: "Oh yeah. Here we go..." [Reaching the door, Casper steps out into the corridor. Milan flicks off the lights, closes the door, leaving us in total blackness. With the sound of the men's voices, footsteps ringing out, the screen fades...]
