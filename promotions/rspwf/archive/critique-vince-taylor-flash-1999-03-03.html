[Critique] Vince Taylor [Flash]

3 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please give me some feedback on this flash, becaue I really want to see what people think about this character, and the way I flash. Also please tell me some things to work on that would make my roleplays better. Thanks
*************** [Scene: Los Angeles, California. Time: 3:30 P.M. The scene opens to the view of a few roads. The traffic is light at this time, but a few cars are passing through. The weather is nice, and the sun is shining down, causing a glare for just a second. The camera turns to the right and starts to move toward a parking lot that is all but deserted, except for a lone car. The camera starts to move toward it at a brisk pace...]
[As the camera moves toward it, you notice that there is a man sitting on the hood of the car. You also see that the car is a jet black '98 Chevrolet
Corvette. You move even closer, and you finally get a view of the man. It is none other than "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor....]
[Vince's cocoa brown skin is gleaming in the sunlight. He is wearing a pair of
Tommy Hilfiger jeans, that are a little baggy and slightly come over his
Timberland boots. He has on a gray, short sleeved, Duke basketball t-shirt. Dangling around his wrist is a Rolex watch. Also around his neck is a gold chain. His gold framed Rayban sunglasses are on his face, keeping the sun out of his eyes. In his left hand is an unlit cigar. He looks into the camera and begins to speak...]
"THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: It looks like I'll be packin' up and headin' to
Canada for a little while. The Ulitmate Canadian Wrestling Promotion has showed that they got some type'a sense, and they went out and signed the hottest commidity in the wrestling world today... Vince Taylor.
[Vince pauses for a second, gathering his thoughts, and then continues...] "THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: There are many men who are in the top echelon of the wrestling world, and have come to the UCWP. I look at this promotion, and
I see nothin' but the best. Most people would turn an' run if they had to step into the ring with guys like these... but then again, I'm not most people. El
Tee Em'll face 'em head on.
Everybody starts at rock bottom, at the bottom of the food chain. As impressive as I'll be in the ring in my debut, I won't be considered up there with the Matt Haden's, Tyrone Parker's, Randy Turner's, or James Pillows' right away. And that's perfectly understandable. But I just want to deliver one message to them and anybody else that doesn't believe in me.... Give me time!
The day will come, and that day will come soon, when everybody will be looking up to me, and I'll be on top of the UCWP.
[Vince stops and puts the thin cigar in his mouth. He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a lighter, and lights the cigar. Vince takes a hit off of it and exhales, as the smoke blows out. He smiles his approval. An intriguing smell then starts to surface from the cigar. He moves his hand away from his mouth and continues...]
"THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: <Breathes in.> But lets not get ahead of ourselves. This Friday, at Warfare, I step into a UCWP ring for the very first time. I'm bein' put up against The Agent. <Laughs to himself.> Come on, The
Agent? Not exactly the best this federation has to offer, but like I said I haven't proved that I deserve anything better yet. But don't look for me play down to my competition, 'cause I bring my "A" game every night. And that's just too bad for The Agent. <Puffs his cigar.> But I don't look for this to be much of'a match. I mean, not many people can match up to El Tee Em's wrestling ability, let alone The Agent. But if you aren't a believer now, I guarantee you will be after Friday Night.
[Vince stuffs the cigar between his teeth and holds it in his mouth. He then jumps up from the hood of the car, and walks toward the driver's side. He opens the door, puts one foot in the car, with the rest of his body still out of the car he speaks...]
"THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: Soon enough, The Agent and the rest of the UCWP will know what it feels like to be beaten by...
THE. . . LADIES. . . MAN!! [The scene starts to fade away as Vince Taylor gets in the car, and speeds away.] [name withheld] Handler of "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor [WCWA, UCWP] Handler of Simply Irresistible [WCWA, ICW]
