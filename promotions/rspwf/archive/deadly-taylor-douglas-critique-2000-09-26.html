["Deadly" Taylor Douglas] [critique]

26 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Comments; bad, good...send em in. This is a new character for me, and I am looking for all of the feedback I can get before "moving in" on this character.
Any word(s) are appreciated. Before I go on, I will let you know that I will be RPing with him as if he has been through this game more than once. He may be a new character, but I don't want you to think he is any sort of...rookie to the game.
you may notice that I am speaking as if in the DWF, but this is all under assumption that Nick lets me in ;)
::begin transmission:: ...In a single brilliant flash of light, the word "Deadly" is seen, but could have easily been missed if one were to blink their eyes. In the same fashion, the letters "DTD" are seen and in the manner they were shown, the word "Deadly"
and the letters "DTD" become etched into our minds. [fade in] [His grips a wooden match tightly with his fingertips as he drags it across the rough, rugged cement sparking the match a flame. The flame burns brilliantly in the darkness which surrounds. All perceptual vision is focussed on the match as it is raised to the end of a cigarette. Slowly the flame loses intensity, and at the same time the tip of the cigarette begins to glow a bright cherry red. A bluish gray cloud of smoke passes the tip of the cigarette exstinguishing the once vivacious flame. He tosses the match to the ground and brings the cigarette from his lips]
...He is the unmistakable Taylor Douglas in trademark fashion; wearing a cream colored turtle neck shirt with a pair of black khaki pants sitting amungst a plethora of lifelessness. This scene, seemingly a familiar place for Douglas who calmly smokes of his cigarette. Acting as if this is so familiar, not unlike a ritual before each match.
He rests his hand to his side, and speaks with his eyes half shut, and a relaxed but focussed tone of voice
[DTD]: "This week, unlike every other, I have been preparing myself for an event that could surely turn the tide of things for me. [grins] I know, your probably saying to yourself right now...Taylor Douglas? Preparing himself more than he already is and improving an already VAST arsonal of moves and tactics?
[sighs] Yes, I have. I have been pushing myself to best my best, and to top everything which I have accomplished up to this point of my life. So far, I have succeeded in doing so, but I am far from finished."
[Douglas pulls his cigarette from his side back to his lips, and he takes a slow drag. He speaks again as he exhales the smoke]
[DTD]: "Now, I walk on. Stepping onto an unfamilair ground called the DWF. Although there is nothing I know about the DWF, I must say that what I have seen so far...has not impressed me. [shakes his head] Does that mean I am going to blaspheme the name of every superstar in the DWF? No, but as always, there will come a time when all I can help to do is look down upon those who are below me. "
[A smirk grows on Douglas's face as he takes one last drag from his cigarette. He closes his eyes and flicks the cigarette into the air. After a breif pause, he releases the smoke through his nostrills and for the first time, looks into the camera. He speaks]
[DTD]: "As always, I will break the breaking points and best the best that there is. Night after night proving that no matter how good any of you THINK you are, I will always be better. So begins the legacy of deadly Taylor
Douglas in the DWF, and so begins the slow agonizing slaughter of the DWF talent. [smirks] Until next time kids...Suck It Easy.
[The visual begins to fade to black as Taylor steps out of the view of the camera]
