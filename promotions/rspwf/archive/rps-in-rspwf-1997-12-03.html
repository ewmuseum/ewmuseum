Hey everyone... I've looked at this part on how people like to read about characters in e-wrestling... well everyone has seen stuff from Dan Kauffman, but not too many have seen Craig Anderson, aka my other guy. Here is some of his most recent stuff, starting with an attack from the GFWA PPV, and following with the events of the past week. Enjoy... ----- From Friday Slam Masters, 11/21/97 [Reed:] Hey... what the hell?! We've got another disturbance in the locker room! [Adams:] We've got a cameraman still back there... we're in Craig Anderson's locker room... OH GOD! [Reed:] Victor Vendetta just nailed him across the back with a baseball bat! It's Victor Vendetta! He's the one attacking people! Vendetta is stomping away at Anderson, after already attacking Andy Vanderpool! He's picking him up... and slapping him around a bit... _V-LINE INTO THE METAL LOCKERS_! [Adams:] OH MAN! Vendetta is asking for trouble! [Reed:] JESUS! ANOTHER FIREBALL TO THE FACE! Anderson is down! [Adams:] Now he's ripping off Anderson's t-shirt... and he's spray-painted a red "V" on his chest as well! This is sick! Victor Vendetta is a sick, sick individual. ----- Craig Anderson Flash, Two Days Later... [Blackness. There are no lights, no movements, no sounds. Just total, complete blackness, encompassing all senses and vision Then a voice... a very familiar one...] If you try to kill someone, you make damn sure you finish the job. I remember my time in the Gulf War. The troops, of which I was a part... 232nd infantry, west Saudi Arabia... were scared shitless at first. War was such an ugly thing. Lots of blood. Lots of shit. Lots of brain matter and bones and debris lying all over the place. None of us wanted to be there, but it was the right thing to do. Serve our country, stand tall, all of that. Then came the real killings. The real battles. And we found out first hand what war was really like. It's dust in your eyes, cannon smoke in your nose, and blood on your shoes. Only much much worse. There's a strange phenomenon that occurs in the midst of such carnage. It happens when you are looking through your gun, see a target, just standing there, probably as scared as you are. You get all nervous inside, like you ate a bad oyster or such. Then, you fire. It takes effort. It takes a lot of emotion. But you fire. And you see the guy's matter fly all over the place. You realize that you killed a fellow being. You start thinking in all kinds of crazy fashions... You have mercy on his family... maybe his wife and kids... you feel pity for the man. Maybe you even try to write to the people he knew, to assure them that what you did was in an act of war. You try to lesson the guilt that somehow racks you inside. And you see the next guy in your gunsight. No different than the first, save maybe 10 yards closer. And you refocus. And you steele your nerves. You fire again. You kill again. You go through many of the same emotions again. And then you realize that killing men isn't that hard to do. In fact, it is one of the easiest things to do in the world. Men are fragile. They break, they crack, they scream, they bleed, they splatter, they crush just like that deer that came too close to the Mack Truck. And somewhere in you, you find the ability to throw out the emotions and the fear and the courage and the honor and the country. You throw it all away. because you've killed your own flesh. And you've done it more than once. And you know you can do it again. And you do it. And again. And again. And again. After so long, the equation of life becomes simple. You live. They die. It becomes a game, this killing of people you don't know, have never met. You only know they are omn the other side, so you kill them. You start to lose track of the number. It happens so many times that you fall into your own world. One more down. Another. Brain shot. Flesh and blood and organs and tissue and gore everywhere. You live, eat, sleep, and breathe this fun new game that is your world. Then the war ends. But not _your_ war. You keep killing. You find more of the enemy, who pleads with you to allow them to live. They pray and beg and plead. And you kill them anyways. Because it's a game. It's your world. You live, they die. Then you lose sight of the enemy. You see someone... maybe someone you are friends with... and he's looking at a woman you admire. And you kill him. And then you get taken away. You get locked up. And your life seems to end. You can't kill anymore. They tell you that. You refuse to believe. You try and try and try as you might to get away and kill all the asses. And fnally, you slip away into a coma. And you wake up. It takes you 5 long years to become normal again, to accept your fellow beings. That's what war does. It destroys reality. You become what mankind once was... a savage machine. I promised myself those days were over. And then someone lit the fire. And became the enemy. And he will not survive. If you are going to kill a man, you best make sure you get the job done. If you don't... Then you are on the wrong side of the gunsight. Vic, you won't here the bang. I promise. [The black screen lasts still, but no sound is heard further.] ----- Craig Anderson RP, Two Days Later... [The fire consumes more of the screen, slowly filling up 30 percent of it... then 50 percent... growing and building without caution or retreat, becoming a living thing...] We weren't made to be animals. But in the game of life, sometimes we have no other choice. In war, you become the hunted. You can't survive as the hunted, so you have two options. Die in the middle of a jungle or a desert or a field or some wintry grave... Or become the hunter instead. A hunter can only kill... it can not be killed... [The fire builds and builds and builds until now the entire screen is filled with nothing but the illuminescense of this living orange and yellow apparition...] When you take the step to being the hunter, you make it your life to end someone elses. You decide to BECOME one with that other man. You watch him for days on end, find out what he's like. How he sleeps at night. Which weapon is his favorive. You find out personal things. Does he like sex in the morning or after lunch. Does he kill men with a knife, or a gun? With his hands? Does he shave with a razor blade or with electric razors? Then you start to live his own life... you throw away your old self and you become the hunted's own flesh. The hunted's own brain. You start to thuink, act, talk, sleep, and eat as he. You covince yourself that it is YOU, and not HIM, that is real. And then, you pick a night, you prepare... You eliminate the one who is not needed anymore. Because you are him, and he is no longer needed in your world. [The fire rages on, brighter and, presumably, hotter than just seconds before...] All it takes is a spark. Then the spark becomes a fire. And then it becomes a blaze. And suppousedly, it creates light, an ability to see. But fire does not. For in fire, you only get... [he fire dies immediately, and there is total...] Blackness. Vic, you shall see it for an eternity. I promise. [The blackness is total and complete, and no more words are heard...] ----- Scenes Involving Craig Anderson, Sunday Night Slam Masters, 11/30/97 [The following is a GFWA production.] [Black.] [Adams:] Folks, we were going to have GFWA Sunday Night Slam Masters, but the power has failed in Bosnia... [Reed:] Figures. Hey, what the hell?! There's a fire in the entranceway! [Adams:] It must be an electrical fire, get security over there! [Reed:] It went out... by itself??? [Voice:] "It starts with an ignition... It will end in blackness." ----- Later that Night, during Victor [The following is a GFWA production.] [Black.] [Adams:] Folks, we were going to have GFWA Sunday Night Slam Masters, but the power has failed in Bosnia... [Reed:] Figures. Hey, what the hell?! There's a fire in the entranceway! [Adams:] It must be an electrical fire, get security over there! [Reed:] It went out... by itself??? [Voice:] "It starts with an ignition... It will end in blackness." ----- Later that night during Vendetta/Hazard Match... [Adams:] Well, some paramedics have just brought a stretcher out for Victor Vendetta.. But they're going to need another one.. [Reed:] This has been total mayhem.. I just don't feel right calling either of these men "winners".. Those referees now trying to move Hazard out of the ring.. [Adams:] I'll have to agree with you there.. This h.. [The lights go completely out AGAIN] [Big Crowd Pop!] [Adams:] Not something ELSE?! [Reed:] WHAT?! I can't hear you?! [Adams:] WHAT?! [The Pop starts to die-down, but there is still a loud buzz of noise coming from the crowd, since everyone is in pitch-black] [Reed:] I said I couldn't hear you! [Adams:] Oh.. Well, folks.. I'm not sure what is going on here.. It might be yet another attack on either Hazard or Vendetta, but I just don't know.. [Reed:] It may just be a technical hitch this time. [Adams:] That's true. We just won't know until the lights come back on.. It's been about 30 seconds now.. [Almost on cue, a spotlight shines down onto the centre of the ring] [Reed:] What the... [Dallas' voice is drowned-out by the incredibly shocked, confused and downright terrified reaction from the crowd as they each look into the ring and see the __thing__.. We can barely hear the sound of Adams and Reed chattering away excitedly, but we can only guess as to what they are saying....] [Mounted on a tripod in the centre of the ring is a fixed bayonet... And impaled on the bayonet is the ___head___] [Off-the-Scale Crowd Pop!!!] [The head... of Victor Vendetta...] [The thunderous sounds of people yelling and screaming at the top of their voices almost bursts our eardrums] [We cut to another camera where we can see Adams and Reed pointing and panicking at the ring, illuminated by the nearby spotlight.. Ringside officials scurry around outside the ring, and fans at ringside can be seen with a variety of facial expressions..] [Just before the camera cuts back to the ring, a very large person stumbles past us.. It looked somewhat like Victor Vendetta, head intact. We cut back to what we know hope as well as pray to be a dummy head.. Suddenly, a voice is heard over the public address, even over the incredibly loud din of 55,000 panic-stricken people] "Welcome to your future, Vic. It was really quite painless..." [And then, the lights go out again, and the Mammoth Crowd Pop gets even louder, if that is possible. Complete and utter pitch-blackness covers everything for another 30 seconds before the general lights suddenly switch back on. Those who aren't blinded by the sudden flash of light notice several things.. The head, tripod and bayonet are _gone_ without a trace. The charred, but undecapitated Victor Vendetta stands outside the ring with an almost delirious expression on his face. He looks in the crowd, around the ring for the _thing_ or the responsible party. A paramedic follows him around, trying to attend to his burns, but keeps getting shoved aside by the big man for his troubles. Finally, the noise dies down enough for us to hear the commentators] [Reed:] Man, oh man.. Vendetta must've thought he had died and gone to HELL for a moment there.. [Adams:] ... [Reed:] You okay? [Adams:] Fine.. Let's just hope we can clear all this mess up. [Reed:] Jesus... we're gonna go to Pike Rose now... god, Craig Anderson has _really_ scared Victor Vendetta. ----- Reaction? - Dan Kauffman
