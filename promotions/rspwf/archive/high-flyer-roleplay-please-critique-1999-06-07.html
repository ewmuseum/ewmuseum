High Flyer roleplay(Please critique)

7 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, here's an rp, and some background. This is for a fed called IWO, www.fwlnet.com . High Flyer has just recently lost the television title, after the referee was knocked out, he had Fugite, his opponent beat, and then lost when the referee woke up. Then, later in the night, (which was on a thursday), he formed a stable called the Cult, with Jax Stone, Al
Coholic, Justin Shack, and Ken War, beating down on Era of Our
Kind(Phantom and Master Mage), Tony Davis, Chris Anthony, and a few others. On Monday, he faces Phantom in a singles matchup.
(The scene opens inside a gym area, but it's not the dungeon gym. Some guys are working out, lifting some weights, as well as a few females.
Bench presses, treadmills, curling, everything is being done. You can almost smell the sweat as the entire gym is dead silent except for the groans of lifting too much weight at once. Then, a door opens and you can hear that bell ring, and it's High Flyer. He's dressed in khaki shorts and a brand new Cult T-shirt. It has The Cult in huge letters, just like the logo on the web page, in the front, and on the back it has
Each of their logos, with a small IWO logo and a copyright of 1999 on it. He has a gym bag in his right hand, and a white towel in his left.
He goes up to the desk, and sees a clerk there dressed in white. He has one a white button up shirt with a jacket on, as well as a tie. With this discription, it may seem as if he is very well dressed, but he's not, as there are stains all over his shirt, and his shirt is half tucked in and not. His slacks are bright blue as well. He is missing a few teeth, the "Common" southerner, at least, what most people picture them.)
Southern Ascent Man:What can I do for you boy? Flyer:Well, I was wondering if I could use your gym? I'm away from home right now and I need to get ready for my matchup against Phantom on
Monday Night Meltdown...
SAM:Ohhhh, Phantom, isn't he on of those IWO folk? Flyer:Yes, he is. SAM:IC tag Cheamp, right am I? Flyer:Uhhhh, yeah, but I didn't come here to discuss his ackalades, if anything, mine, but I'm not here to do that either. I want to get ready.
What do you charge for a one time service? SAM:One time? NAAAWWW. We don't do tat round hier. Only membership, naw on time. Flyer:What did you say? I got the NAAAWWWW part, but I didn't understand any of the rest. SAM:Hey, you, over hier. (He is calling one of the workers over, as he is dressed more conventionally. He has on the same as the Southern acsent man, but there are no stains, and looks very presentable.)
Worker:Yes? SAM:Tis man hier wats on time work, bu we use membership. He don't know tat. Flyer:What did he say? Worker:Yeah, his acsent is hard to understand, but if you live down south all your life you learn it. Sometimes it rubs off on ya.
Flyer:There's a prime example. Ok, here's a person that speaks english. I want to know... Worker:You want to know if you can work he for one time only? Flyer:Yeah, that's what I want. Worker:I'm sorry Mister, but only on very big occasions do we give one time only workouts. Boxers, football players, Wrestlers, other sports athletes. Otherwise, we can not give out memberships, or let you work here for one night.
Flyer:Well, I am a wrestler. I wrestle for the IWO. Former T.v. champ, damn Fugite... Worker:Ohhh, you're High Flyer, am I right? Flyer:Yeah, I'm facing Phantom on Monday, and I need to prepare a little bit. Worker:Well, walk this way. SAM:Wat r you doin'? Worker:Hold on(Speaks to SAM.)Tis man is High Flyer. He is an Wrestler. He want to use our facite. SAM(Talking to Flyer):Why don't you say tat in thie firt place? Flyer:What? Worker:Just follow me sir. (The Worker directs Flyer where to go, but Flyer is still looking back with a weird look on his face. He doesn't get their custom. Flyer turns back around and follows the worker.)
Worker:What would you like to do. There's weight over there, a television over there with a vcr. We've got endurance training to your left, as well as a sparing partner on your right. The sparing partner is an additional fifty dollars...
Flyer:That's ok, I won't need a sparing partner. How much for the one time show? Worker:Ummm, eleven ninty five. Flyer:You guys are expensive in Arkansas. Worker:Well, we don't get many athlete's down here. The owner over there seems to think if we raise prices, we will attract more people...
Flyer:He's the owner? Worker:Yes, he's the owner... why, you don't think he deserves to be the owner? Flyer:No, no... do you guys take credit? Worker:No, I'm sorry, we do not accept credit cards, except visa? Flyer:So those commercials lie. American Express doesn't work everywhere! (Flyer takes out his wallet and looks through it. He has an American
Express card, a Blockbuster rewards card, a mac card, a summit bank card, and a coke card. He has about 18 dollars in his wallet, and reluctanly hands the man 13 dollars. He has five dollars left.)
Flyer:Keep the change. (The man walks away to the register, and rings him up. Flyer snaps his fingers) Flyer:Damn, that was my dinner money. Oh well, I'll get something from
Shop rite. (Flyer rubs his hands together giving him heat, even though it does seem like a sweaty day. There is no air conditioner in the gym though. Then, the bell rings again, and in walks a reporter from the IWO. He goes over to the desk, and asks the man where a Mr. High Flyer is. The man points to where Flyer is, telling him "that is where Mr Fly...". He stops when he sees Flyer's hands telling him not to, but it's too late, as the reporter walks over.)
IWO Reporter(IR):Hello Mr. Flyer. Glad to see you. Flyer:Not as glad as I am to see you... leave! IR:Now I know you don't mean that. Flyer:Yeah I do. IR:Well, you were abruptly put back in your(quotes using hands) place by a Kate Young earlier on today. What is going on between the two of you.
Flyer:Don't worry about it. I smoothed things over. We are back on the same leval we were. We are good.
IR:That is good. I've got to say that, but if you heard Phantom's comments earlier, well, he said a few choice words to you.
Flyer:A few? We pratically killed him and his partner's career, and he is down my throat! He wants me in the ring. He thinks I'm the lower part of the Cult... WELL I'LL SHOW HIM! Look what happened to Billy Larson when he won the t.v. belt. Two months later he's had three different title reigns! I see a good future for me, and for Stone. If you think that I'm the lowest end of the Cult, I could care less. If you face ANY of us, well, it's hard to beat us, plain and simply put. We may be still midcarders, but we haven't had the shot to be in the main event. At least not a decent shot. Billy Larson is crawling his way up to the top, as well as I am. Don't think it will be long until you see my name as champion again.
I may not have had anything but the television title, but I held that for a month. I have capitalized on all of my title shots, and at Beach
Party, I am going to capitalize... with Jax Stone, on the
Intercontinental tag titles. IR:Well, you are going to have to beat Phantom first to get the shot. Flyer:You didn't do your research, did you? I don't have to beat Phantom to get the shot. It's been signed, sealed, and WILL be delivered.
Whether I win, lose, or draw to Phantom, it doesn't matter, because
Stone and I are taking some more gold home to the Cult.
IR:What about Erik Cambell. He has been let go by the IWO. Flyer:What about him? IR:He was threatening you for legal action had he not gotten the television title shot that he was promised. Do you feel him as a problem?
Flyer:Problem no, nuicense, yes. He always seemed to have those lawyers of him backing him up. He only talked about the television title, and refused to wrestle otherwise. I'm glad Jamie let him go.
IR:Back to relavent matters, Monday Night Meltdown. It will be you and
Phantom, one on one. Any comments?
Flyer:Are you brain dead. I always have comments on this matchup. I always have comments. You should know that by now. Let's start off on the background to this matchup. On thursday at Hostile Takeover, we started our stable, by beating the crap out of Chris Anthony, Reaper,
Tony Davis, Era of Our Kind, and RaynMan. Well, what do you know, we took out a LOT of people on HT, with only me and Stone helping at the begining. We started it, and we are going to finish it. We took Era of
Our Kind to our... oops, sorry, their limits, and destroyed them in the ring. Whatever you see, you have to know that we came out on top when the commercial started. Check the web site with the movies, you can see it yourself. We took them out, and now they are pissed at us for using them to jump start the stable. Well, GET USED TO IT! The Cult is here to stay, and it's staying for a LONG time. We may lose people, we may gain people, but we WILL stay here. The Cult is here to stay, like I said, and we are starting off with the SECOND best tag team, and the SECOND best Stable, and taking them out. The second best tag team, Era of Our
Kind. The first best, Hostile Youth, just because they have the better title. THe second best stable, X4, the best??? Are you brain dead too?
The Cult, no questions asked! I'm still pissed off at Fugite, and on Monday, Phantom, you are the lucky contestant to have my frustrations taken out on. It will be a day you will NEVER forget, for it will be the day which you are taken down, and out. Singles action, or tag, it doesn't matter. Why don't we just make this the tag team title matchup right now? Don't wait till Beach
Party. Stone will wrestle, and Master Mage, why not? I.C. Titles on the line? How does that sound? I know it's always been said, "WE DON'T HAVE
TO WAIT FOR THE PPV", but this time, it's the truth. Let's see if you guys have the guts. I know Stone would LOVE to have a crack at the gold on Monday. He may not be ready for you, but that won't matter. I'm ready for BOTH of you!
And what was that mark about me being too green to remember your first singles match? IR:I believe he meant you are too much of a newbie to remember it. Flyer:I know that. But does he mean that I'm bad because I'm new. And
Phantom, I'm not that new, I've been here the ENTIRE year. You just don't seem to listen to me, or look at me. I guess you have ignored me, and now, you see me, and I whooped you from pillar to post, and now, you want a piece of me. Only because I hit you with a baseball bat or whatever I did, you finally listen to me.
Then I look at some more of your little interview, and I see somethings that I don't like. I don't have a losing record. You must REALLY not have paid attention to me. I mean, I've only lost four times, and now, you think I have this wrong record or something. I'm eleven and four, and I haven't lost in about two months before I lost to Fugite on
Hostile Takeover. I took him for granted, and THAT'S WHY I LOST! No other reason. I thought he was out, but he wasn't, and KERPLAT, there is that Lost time.
You feel sorry for me Phantom? Ha, that makes me laugh. I feel sorry for you! I feel sorry because I'm going to stick this foot down your mouth, and make it come out the other side, if you catch MY DRIFT! A scared little boy? Far from it. I am a PISSED OFF BIG MAN, and if you want me to show... no, hold on, I won't go that far. Phantom, you see here, I am a huge threat. Stone and I are a huge threat to your Intercontinental tag team titles, and I am a huge threat to putting you out to pasture.
You better realize that I am the greatest crusier weight champion in
Wrestling today, and no Phantom is going to take me down and out for the count!
Now, I have that entire interview memorized, so this might take a while. Phantom, twenty minute match? You won't last twenty minutes! Plain and simple, YOU WON'T SURVIVE TWENTY MINUTES! You won't survive twenty minutes of pain, and twenty minutes of destruction. Being a tag team competitor means that you only face six people over and over. There are only four tag teams if you realize that. The singles world is much more tougher than you think, and I'm just going to have to show you this on
Monday. Being a singles wrestler, and a former champion, I know that everyone in the world, tag team and singles, wants a shot at you. I just gave too many people shots! I was worn down as well when I faced Fugite.
Phantom, I'm using you as a rebound, a rebound to stardom. If you beat me, you might get a title shot, but you won't take mine, that's all I know. You're extreme side? You have yet to show that? I wonder what Desperate Measures was, when you killed those two up and coming tag teams? What was that all about? I don't know what their names were, but may the rest in peace(snicker). If only they could have done that to the O'Neil Brothers.(Another snicker) Well, this interview is getting pretty long. I mean, it's almost been an hour.
IR:Yeah, it has. Time flies... Ok, I believe that's it. Thank you for your time. Flyer:No problem, now let me start my workout. (The worker comes over to him.) Worker:Excuse me Mr. Flyer, but you have to leave. Flyer:WHAT! I didn't even do anything yet! Worker:I am sorry, but that eleven dollars was for an hour only. To stay, you have to pay another elven dollars.
(The bell rings, the small one, showing that the reporter has left.) Flyer:WHAT! That's bs! Are you trying to get more money off of me! I refuse to pay... do you take a summit card?
Worker:No sorry sir. You must pay in cash. (Flyer gets up quickly and grabs his bag. He looks the man in the face, and looks to hit him, but just storms out of the gym. The bell rings as the scene fades to the MNM logo, as well as (Again) Flyer vs. Phantom, with Phantom having the belt on his shoulder. The scene then fades completely.)
