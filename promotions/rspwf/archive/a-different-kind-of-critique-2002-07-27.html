Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've seen plenty of RP critiques on here and some of them are really good ones. Now, I'm actually trying to get my match writing skills up. I like writing matches (and yes, I know alot of you are gonna be hounding me to write now, just because I like to write them). But, as said, I want to get better at it. Below is what I think to be one of my best matches written. Tear it up boys, rip it to shreads. If it's a detailed analysis, then so-be-it. Just do whatever to see if I got what it takes. Thanks!
