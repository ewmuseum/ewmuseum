The end of flashes

EW University
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I can safely say that I`ve had enough of flashes. They say pretty much the same thing, time and time again, from the same perspective, and no matter how well they`re written, they stop being interesting to read. I`ll tell you why. It`s because it`s just one person slagging off another (usually). It provides no analysis, speculation, update, rumour, innuendo, review...nothing of any real substance...nothing that really develops characters further. I can think of a few exceptions, but mostly, flashes are boring, repetitive, and really don`t appeal to be read. They`re also too long, usually. Heck, they`re supposed to be 'FLASHES'. If lightning lasted as long as some flashes, we`d have no darkness. So what am I saying? I`m saying vary your ways of flashing. I`m saying go read a magazine like Pro-Wrestling Illustrated, or go to Scoops, or whatever. They may not be classy, but just take a look at them. Usually they manage to recap the situation (keeping readers up to date with the current angle, which flashes rarely do. In fact, they can confuse), they manage to get some words from the wrestler. They get some comments from fans or other sources offering another viewpoint. They give analysis. They offer speculation. They give insight, into the wrestlers deep down emotions and possible underlying motives. And they`re interesting to read. There is more scope for creativity. Doesn`t it make more sense to leave 'flashes' for in-rings or pre-match, or pre-view show, and basically have a magazine approach to flash-packets? I wouldn`t suggest it for all feds, just those with mature players. But try it, see what happens. I`d guess people would read it. I`d guess other players will become more interested in the other angles in the fed. I`d guess you`d get more variety. I`d guess it`d be better. I`m only guessing, but I know that I, personally, find it much easier to write magazine style articles than flashes. And the feds that go this route could collaborate together, using their best articles to make an interfed magazine. This is just an idea. But it`s one based on the idea that E-Wrestling is a text based forum, yet most of the time we try trying to give everyone visuals through those words. Try something else for a change. For example, the film 'Howards End' is infinitely more understandable if you have read the book, because the book goes into the thoughts and underlying feelings. Equally, Bram Stoker`s Dracula explains the history and emotions much more deeply than any film has. Flashes are very much 'What you see is what you get.' They`re one dimensional, with one viewpoint. Articles can give extra dimensions, more viewpoints, and as such, they are more interesting to read. Further example. If you take the script of a film, it has the dialogue and the camera angles, and perhaps a few other angles. The book of the film can have all that, PLUS more insight into the situation, effectively making it more real.
