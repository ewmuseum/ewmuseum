(((ECWF))) The Best Rp fed around. Talented RPers needed!

8 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We are low on talent, but here are some of the rps from the ECWF! Read Away! Do you want the challenge? If so, send me a sample rp! ------------------------ Scene: Awesome Incorporated sit inside their home in Venice Beach, California. Shane yet smoking another cigarette, and Shawn reading through a EHWF magazine. A camera man knocks on the door and requests an interview with the superstars. They ponder it for a minute then agree to the interview. Shane puts out his cigarette and Shawn goes and gets some bud's. They are generous enough to offer the camera man one, he accepts. After he is finished setting up, they start the interview.
************************************************************************** ************************************************ AWESOME INCORPORATED **Next Tag Team Champions** ************************************************************************** ************************************************ Shane: Well Sting and Luger. You think you know the beach huh? Well, I'll bet you do. But you know, who doesn't know the beach. What is there to know about the beach?? Answer that for me. And you say that you forgot more about wrestling than we'll ever know? Well, you got it kinda wrong there. You see, we know as much.. as you forgot about wrestling, and thats everything. You forgot every thing about wrestling. And thats how much we know about it, everything!! And if you're not sure, you'll definetely find out on Monday
Night Madness. Because we're bringing it all baby, we're bringing it all ! !
And Triple H. You lucked out again. Because I am giving up the shot that I want at the TV title to my dear friend Mission Man. He didn't lose the title,
White Knight did. But, as you said, you forgive him. And that seems like what
I have to do now. But don't worry guys, me and Shawn will replace that title with the Extreme Hardcore Wrestling Federation World Tag Team Titles. And after that, we're coming for you Ric Flair and Chris Benoit. The 4 Horseman needed the Elite. And now with out it, your nothing but four jerk offs that want to retire but need the money. So at the pay-per-view, Elite's; Awesome
Incorporated VS the Horsemen's; Chris Benoit and Ric Flair. Easy win and onto the next challenger. LOD2000. The men who think they have it all. Including a #1 contender's spot for the tag team titles. Now guys, who did you beat to get that spot? Let me rephrase that. Who are you going to beat to get that spot. Two cruiserweights. Juvy, and Aguila. Hey guys, if you need any help at all beating those two Chicago idiots let us know. We'll watch your back.
Hey LOD, too many lead pipes to the back of the head I suppose?? Probably. You ought to lay off those street fights for a while, your getting too tangled up in your work, which really sucks. Now, back to our opponents for this
Monday. Sting and Lex. Now here are to guys that think they have the stuff to beat us. Listen you dick heads!! Let me get it through your thick heads. We are the best tag team duo, IN THE WORLD!!!! We can beat anyone who lays out a challenge to us. Like LOD. We can beat them, frankly, because they can't rp.
We can beat Kurrgan and Golga. Frankly because, all the Jackyl does is do that
STUPID Jackyl Show. You could at least make it the roleplay line limit. Ten lines isn't very hard to do. But I guess in your case we really don't have a choice. So this Monday, we're off for about a week since we'll be waiting for the other side of the braquet to have a finalist. Well, as of now, its late.
This interview is over. Scene: The camera man packs up his things kind of slowly and leaves the home of Shane and Shawn Awesome. The home of Awesome Incorporated.
---------------------------- Scene: Dan Severn is in the locker room preparing to leave from the arena after a UcW event. Just before he leaves, a television crew enters and asks for a short interview with him. He ponders the request for a moment before nodding his head "yes" to the crew. A UcW announcer appears from behind them and walks through the small group with a microphone in his right hand. Lights are set up as well as a camera or two at seperate angles, both pointed toward the spot where Severn is going to take the interview. The lights are turned on, and some photographers from wrestling magazines and newsletters start taking picture after picture while Dan comes into view of the TV camera. Just before the interview begins, Dan learns that the announcer's name is Jim Ross
(Dan is new to this federation and isn't familiar with some names around here).
The interview starts, and JR asks his first question. +-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+ THE SHOOTERS :::::DAN SEVERN::::: +-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+ Jim Ross: Well, Dan, can I call you Dan? Dan: No. JR: Alright then. Well, Mr. Severn, what are your plans now that you've moved from the EHWF to UcW?
Dan: You see JR, I'm kinda glad you asked that, and I'm kinda not. I am because I have a h•ll of a lot to say, and I'm not because that's about the most asked question by announcers today. And I'm already starting to think that many of the announcers in the UcW are as average as you morons are. I mean this is a GREAT fed, but with people like you, JR, you'll just end up bringing the fed down… in the long run. Now every sane man on the planet knows that I'm new in this fed, and if they don't know that after what JR here just said, than you have to be absolutely insane. I have a lot of expectations for me in the UcW, but I'm not gonna run at my hopes full-blast… yet. Just like any professional wrestler I have to build a reputation for me and the stable I represent. And just like any pro, that's exactly what I plan to do. My good friend Ken Shamrock and I started The SHOOTERS because… well frankly I don't know exactly WHY we started it, but I do know that this stable can rise to the top over the best of the best in this fed. And I have a feeling that we can do that here. That's about all we can hope for right now, actually, but there's always more to come.
JR: Ok, next question. It's quite early in your career here, but is there anyone that you'd like to fight in the near future?
Dan: As a matter of fact, JR, there is. Ryan Hart. He's the only one I've seen here so far, and to work my way to the top I'll have to fight people like him. So, Ryan Hart, that's why I'm challenging YOU to a match on Summerslam
'98. But I'd like to add a few stipulations to this match, just to show what
I'm made of. Here I'll show you a list of what they are.
(He pulls a piece of paper out of his pocket.) Dan Severn vs. Ryan Hart Stipulations No Disqualifications All Falls Will Count Barbed Wire Ropes Surrounding the Ring (He puts the paper back into his pocket and begins to speak again.) Dan: I especially like the barbed wire. It gives matches a unique excitement that makes them twice as fun. Hart, if you accept, get ready for a beating on
August 5th, because that's exactly what's gonna come to you, wether you like it or not.
JR: Well I think we've about finished here, so well leave Dan… Dan: I'm not finished yet! Shamrock and I are looking for new members into the SHOOTERS, but we aren't gonna accept just anybody. Not a chance. We're looking for the most elite, most extreme wrestlers here. We'll let you know.
+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+ THE SHOOTERS :::::DAN SEVERN::::: +-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+-=-+ Scene: Jim Ross unplugs his microphone and leaves the locker room. The cameras are then packed away along with the lights, which are beginning to feel quite hot on the hands of the crew. While all this is happening, Dan leaves and drives away to his hotel and prepare for another day in the UcW.
