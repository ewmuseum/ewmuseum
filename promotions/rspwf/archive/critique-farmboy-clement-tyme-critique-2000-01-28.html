[Critique] "Farmboy" Clement Tyme [Critique]

28 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I really don't care what you do with this rp. You can flame it, critique it, or praise it. Just give me some feedback on it.
[The scene fades into the store front of a Hallmark Gold Crown store in the
Minooka Mall. Christmas carols are being sung in the background. All of the stores are decorated for Christmas. Clement Tyme is coming out of the store with a few shopping bags in his hand. Clement is all bundled up in a
Buccaneers Starter jacket and Devil Rays sweat pants. He has a blue scarf keeping his neck warm. He then runs into a mountain of a man, at least compared to Tyme. The man Tyme has run into has to be six feet, five inches.
The man is dressed in a Toronto Maple Leafs hockey jersey on as well as some real tight jeans. The big man helps Clement up, seemingly pulling Clement arm out of it's socket.]
Big Man - Hey! Clement Tyme? Clement Tyme - Brian King..."Fantastic" Brian King? Brian King - Hey Clement! How have you been? CT - Great how about yourself? BK - Oh, same as always. Getting into feds and having them shut down. You know the deal. CT - All too well. But I really wish you wouldn't talk about wrestling right now though Brian.
BK - How come? Something wrong? CT - How about we go over to the food court and we'll talk over lunch? BK - Okay that sounds like fun. [The two wrestlers walk together to the food court of the Minooka Mall. Tyme gets in line for pizza and King stands in line at a Mexican restaurant. When both men receive their food, they meet at an empty table in the middle of the food court. Tyme has ordered a slice of cheese pizza and a slice of mushroom pizza. King has ordered three burritos and a taco.]
BK - So, Clement, what's this about you, of all people, not wanting to talk about wrestling?
CT - Well, Brian, you are my oldest friend in the business. You know how I easily I get hurt but I always play through it.
BK - It figures though, the way you wrestle. All of those springboard moves are bound to get you stuck in a hospital one day.
CT - I know. But that's not what I'm upset about. It's my next match in mWa. It's...a...um - BK - Spit it out now! [Clement starts rubbing his head and rubbing his fingers through his hair. He cracks his knuckles several times before answering his friend.]
CT - Well, it's just this match. Against Pico Flush. I'm totally unprepared for it. BK - Well that's not bad. When's the match? CT - The thirty-first of December. BK - That's over a week away. What's the big deal? CT - What's the big deal?!? What's the big deal?!? I could get killed! Pico
Flush is the "Hardcore Icon!" Someone said I was talking trash about him. You know me! I don't talk trash about anyone! Let alone someone as well-respected as Pico Flush!
BK - You still haven't told me...WHAT IS THE MATCH? CT - A Pico's Playground Match. BK - You're as dead as a doornail. CT - Thanks for your support! I appreciate it! I feel like I'm on Death Row. Washo said I'm going to die Pico himself said I'm gonna die. Everybody keeps saying bye to me. I don't understand. Why is the match gonna be so bad?
BK - Well, from what I understand, first of all you start off thirty feet in the air. Then you wrestle up there until on of you falls through 20 tables to the ring.
CT - Thirty feet? To the ring? That's gonna hurt! That's like 100 mes! BK - Well, Clement, you were the one parading around in the back saying you could beat him. You have to try to back your words up now.
CT - I never said that! That was Solomon bragging about how he already beat
Pico Flush. I would never say that! I was sad! I lost to Morrison of all people! You said you beat the crap out of him.
BK - Well...I did. But Clement, I don't want to send you over the edge but their is another stipulation...
CT - WHAT?!?! [Clement stands up and starts pacing and yelling out toned down cuss words like darn, crap, gosh, screw, and butt. All eyes in the food court turn to Clement.
Clement then sits back down in embarrassment and buries his head in his hands and later his arms. Finally, a few muffled words are uttered.]
CT - Vwat is da clipoovation? BK - Well, some of it doesn't necessarily happen. But the ring engulfed in fire and the ropes turned into barbed wire will happen.
[Clement lifts his head from his arms. His hair is messed up and he looks as if he has just seen a ghost.]
CT - FIRE? BARBED WIRE? [Tyme's voice seems to get higher and angrier with every word.] CT - Tell me Brian...what is the stipulation that may not happen? I want to know! I have to know!
BK - Well, Clement, I said this may not happen, but if what I've heard about
Pico is true, he'll probably do this to you...
[Clement's face goes red. His eyes get bloodshot. He seems pretty pissed off. He says his words as if King were deaf.] CT - Brian King, I want you to tell me what the gosh darned stipulation is or I am going to rip your head off.
BK - That's not a very safe thing to be saying right now, especially to a man eight inches taller than you. But, anyway, other than by submission or pinfall, you can win by throwing your opponent into the doggie dumpster.
[Clement's face has lost coloration now. He has gone from beet red to a very pale white.] CT - Doggy dumpster, what is that? BK - A trash dumpster containing a 200 pound pitbull... CT - Two...Hundred...Pounds? Is there like a separate weight for dogs or something? BK - No, that's in American weight. CT - But...but...but...that's like nine pounds less than me. I mean, that's absurd...surely no dog can be that big...
BK - Yes it can Clement, yes it can, and yes it is. [All of a sudden, Clement's head starts to move from side-to-side until he falls off his chair, seemingly fainted. As soon as he falls, King leaps up from his chair and rushes to Tyme's aid. Then, the camera is placed on a nearby table as the cameraman is sent for help. After several minutes of the camera being on the ground, and lots of little kids asking their parents what happened to the guy on the ground, help arrives in the form of three women who were shopping who happened to be EMTs. About ten minutes later, uniformed paramedics lean over Clement. Soon after, they revive Clement with smelling salts. Tyme sits up, somewhat dazed, somewhat confused. After several consciousness tests, Tyme sits back down in his chair. Tyme quickly polishes off his food.]
CT - Now, Brian, is there anything else that you want to tell me about this match? Please, don't make me faint again.
BK - Well...there isn't much else I can tell you about the match that won't make you faint. At least you learned most of the worst part...
CT - What do you mean most of? BK - Well, Pico claims that he has never been thrown into the doggy dumpster. It is his match you know... CT - Yes I know. But still, hasn't he ever come across anyone who has beat him in a Pico's Playground Match before?
BK - Nope. Not a single person. But, Clement, I've yet to tell you the rest of the stipulations for the match. The only other rule is that anything goes.
No DQ. You can do anything to him that you want. Even throw him into the doggy dumpster. CT - Oh God, Brian, I'm dead meat. I've never wrestled a hardcore match in my life. Oh why couldn't have someone challenged me for the Cruiserweight Title?
Oh why? Why did maybe the biggest guy in the whole mWa have to challenge me, a little tiny guy, the smallest in mWa?
BK - I don't know, but Clement I think you will do okay. It's no big deal. It's just a match. I mean, if you go into the doggy dumpster, just hold on. Pitbulls are built low to the ground. If you hold onto the top, he won't be able to get you. Just use your quickness and speed.
[After Brian finishes his pep talk, Clement bursts out in tears. After attracting a few wondering eyes, Clement stops.]
BK - What's the matter with you? Are you turning into a big weenie all of a sudden? Everybody is staring at you. You need to learn to control your emotions.
CT - I forgot to buy my Mom a Christmas present! She's going to be pissed! And I never said good bye to Claire. Do you realize she will never see me again? It's so sad. At least I'm going home on Wednesday. Oh well. Wait a minute...did you say that anything goes?
BK - Anything goes. No DQ. CT - Yes! I have a plan! I need you to leave now, Mr. Cameraman.
