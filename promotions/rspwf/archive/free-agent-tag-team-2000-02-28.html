Free Agent Tag Team

28 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Looking for a e-mail based RPing fed, regional is preferable, to place my tag team...for a sample RP see below.
<<The scene opens with the trio of "The Rocket" Jonny Zoom, "Superstar" Shawn
Sommers, and Miss Misty seated around the dining room table of their downtown apartment. Ever since they came to the GRW they've been considered the underdogs, they've been called too small, not experienced enough, too young, and too flamboyant...but one thing that they've never been called is dull.>>
<<Obviously in the middle of a strategy session, the three know what they're up against. They've got to do what no one has given them a chance to do, they've got to beat Domination for a second time. This is a task that they take seriously, perhaps moreso than anything that they've done before. Misty rests her head in her left hand on the table as she listens to Zoom. She's dressed in her usual pair of jeans and a t-shirt that reads simply "ZG" on the front and "100% Excitement" on the back. She's stunning as usual, her brown hair pulled back into a pony-tail. "The First Lady of the GRW" as she has been dubbed by countless magazines and "insider" shows, her brilliant managing abilities have led Zero Gravity straight to the top...>>
<<Seated to her left is "Superstar" Shawn Sommers, the All-American Boy. His deep blue eyes have wooed more than their share of the fairer sex. Perhaps the best true athlete in all the GRW, Sommers has built a reputation for breaking noses with his "Hurrikick" inside the squared circle...and an even bigger reputation for breaking hearts outside the ring. His long, wavy blonde hair rests on his shoulders as he leans back in his chair with his arms crossed.
His slender, yet muscular, build is quite evident as he wears only a pair of jeans and allows his chest to breath. A pendant on his gold necklace lays against his chest and reads "ZG".>>
<<Finally, seated across the table from Miss Misty is "The Rocket" Jonny Zoom,
Mr. Arrogance himself. Despite the sound being muted, it's obvious that he's holding court with his partners as they both look upon him intently. As always, he's wearing a pair of Ray-Ban sunglasses with mirrored lenses that obscure his eyes from view. He has jet black hair which is trimmed neatly into a crew-cut. His chiseled features move expressively as he continues to speak.
He wears a baby-blue shirt that reads "The Rocket" on the front, and "ZERO
GRAVITY" on the back...>>
<<It's a rare treat that the average fan gets to sit in on such an intense strategy session. There's no doubt that their preparations for Domination have been the sole focus of everything that they've done for the last few weeks. In fact, if we listen closely, we may get some insight on how the two "Funky
Floridians" plan on going after their arch-nemesis....>>
[ZOOM]: "So, then the chick says to me..." <<Or, maybe not...>> [ZOOM]: "...something about how her boyfriend is a pro-wrestler, and about how mad he's going to get when he finds out about me."
[SOMMERS]: "So...what'd you do?" [ZOOM]: "I did what any red-blooded American would do, I told her my name was
Van Palmer and to tell her boyfriend that if he wanted some he could come get some."
<<Zoom and Sommers erupt in laughter...>> [SOMMERS]: "Smooth move, Daddy-O. Very smooth..." <<Misty, however, does not seem to be so amused by Zoom's story. She continues to sit with her head in her hand...>>
[SOMMERS]: "What's wrong, babe?" <<Both Zoom and Sommers turn their attention to their manager, Misty raises her head up and folds her hands together.>>
[MISTY]: "Sorry to break up the "lockeroom chat" guys, but we've gotta start getting ready for Domination."
<<Zoom looks across at her, puzzledly>> [ZOOM]: "Uh...why?" [SOMMERS]: "For real, those two low talent, low class, don't stand a chance of derailing the ZG X-press...as a matter of fact, I'm getting kinda bored with them."
<<Sommers bulks himself up to do his best impersonation of Volcano.>> [SOMMERS as VOLCANO]: "Cause we're tough, and you're afraid of us...yeah, that's the ticket. You're scared!!! Cause we're tough, tough I tell you!!!
It doesn't matter if ZG whips our ass, we're tough...tough...tough. Even though I got pinned by a girl, I'm tough...tough...tough."
<<Zoom catches on and begins to impersonate Wick>> [ZOOM as WICK]: "Yeah!! Me too...me too!!! I'm tough!!!" [SOMMERS as VOLCANO]: "And we're going to win those Georgia Tag Team titles because we're tough...tough...tough."
[ZOOM as WICK]: "Yeah, boss...and don't forget mean!!! We're tough and mean!!!" [SOMMERS as VOLCANO]: "Good point, we're mean too!! We're so mean...we...we...we uhm.." [ZOOM as WICK]: "We're so mean that we even scare ourselves!!!" [SOMMERS as VOLCANO]: "Yeah!!! We're tough, mean, and scary!! Be afraid of us...because....because we said so!! Yeah, that's it...do what we say and be afraid because we're tough and mean..."
[ZOOM as WICK]: "And as soon as this camera goes off, we're going to drop our drawers and LET THE BEATING BEGIN!!!!"
<<Misty can no longer contain herself, she begins to burst with laughter.>>
[MISTY]: "Okay, you two have proven your point!!! But, it doesn't change the fact that we've gotta defend against those two on Sunday. Yeah, they smell bad, they have no in-ring skills, and between them they have the IQ of a ring-post...but, they're still the number one contenders to us. We've got to take them seriously..."
[ZOOM]: "Take it easy, Misty! We're "The Mighty, Mighty ZG"!!!" [SOMMERS]: "For real, Daddy-O, those two don't have what it takes to stay in the ring with the most exciting tag team of all time for 15 seconds. The only way that we're going to drop the belts is if they put one of us in a headlock and we pass out from the smell..."
<<All three members of ZG cringe at the thought of Domination's "unmistakable odor".>> [MISTY]: "Still, I want you two to be ready for anything. They're tricky, and they're going to do whatever they can to get the advantage over you guys. I'll do whatever I can to help from ringside, but there's no guarantees..."
<<Zoom interrupts...>> [ZOOM]: "Don't worry, "Oh Babe-ish One". Sommers and I have got it all figured out! First...we're gonna out-class them, then we're gonna out-think them, then we're gonna steal their women..."
[SOMMERS]: "Dude...I think you've gone too far. You ever seen their women?" <<Zoom pauses for a second to think...>> [ZOOM]: "You're right, forget that part of it. What we're gonna do after we out-think them is we're gonna make fun of their women!!!"
[SOMMERS]: "Yeah, I'm sure that they'd like to step into Zero Gravity...I mean, they're only human...but ZG don't deal with the fat chicks."
[MISTY]: "So...do you guys plan on winning the match?" <<Zoom and Sommers look at each other...then back at Miss Misty.>> [ZOOM & SOMMERS]: "Well....DUH!!!" [SOMMERS]: "That's a foregone conclusion." [ZOOM]: "Without a doubt, we're ZG! Everyone else is just fighting for second best." [MISTY]: "Okay, but...I still want to make sure that we've got all the bases covered." [SOMMERS]: "Chill out, Misty. You act like we're Youth Gone Wild or something..." [ZOOM]: "We're telling ya, we've got it covered! You worry too much!" <<Misty cracks a smile...>> [MISTY]: "Okay, maybe I do. But, I love you guys and I only want what's best for you." [SOMMERS]: "Don't worry, we've got this one in the bag, babe!" [ZOOM]: "Dude, look at the time, we've gotta jam!" <<Misty looks a bit befuddled...>> [MISTY]: "Where are you two going?" <<Sommers and Zoom look at each other and grin...>> [SOMMERS]: "Out cruising the malls for chicks, where else?" <<Misty shakes her head, half in disgust and half in amusement. Zoom and
Sommers get up from the table and head to their rooms to change. Misty continues sitting at the table, simply staring at the wall...she's obviously trying to come up with some sort of strategy for Domination...>>
<<Misty sighs>> [MISTY]: "I've said it before, sure...they're unfocused and juvenile...but, they're the best tag team I've ever seen. And, they're my boys!"
<<Misty gets up from the table and walks to her room to change to go with her charges. The scene fades to black and the following words appear..>>
THANKSGIVING THUNDER ZERO GRAVITY vs. DOMINATION "Second Verse, Same As The First..." <<END>> -Shawn B.
