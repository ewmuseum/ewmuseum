[Critique] "Magnificent" Mike Douglas Roleplay

1 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, This is the latest roleplay I've done. Someone just critique it and tell me what you think.
=================================================== [Scene: The IWF/WOW HQ. The IWF/WOW's Anthony Edwards has invited "Magnificent" Mike Douglas to the HQ to conduct an interveiw with him. The camera opens up to see a small room with an interveiw setup. There are 2 chairs along with a small table in the middle of them set up on a platform. In one chair sits the
IWF/WOW's own Anthony Edwards. And the other chair is empty. Anthony Edwards is holding a set of index cards in his hands. He files through the cards for a moment and then begins to speak......]
[Edwards] Hello Everyone, And welcome to this very special interveiw with one of the IWF/WOW's top rising stars. Tonight, We will hear from the so-called
"Changed Man" of the IWF/WOW on a number of topics. So folks, I don't want to keep you waiting so............My guest at this time is the man who claims to be...The greatest wrestler.........The best looking wrestler........And without a doubt Magnificent....Here is "Magnificent" Mike Douglas..
[Mike Douglas enters the room. He walks up onto the platform and sits in the seat next to Anthony Edwards. Mike is wearing a pair of Khaki pants with a white sleevless shirt that says "Mike Douglas Training Center". He has his hair slicked back in a pony tail and he is also wearing a pair of black sunglasses.
After a few moments Mike takes off his sunglasses and Anthony Edwards begins to conduct the interveiw....]
[Edwards] Good to have you here Mike.. [Douglas] Of course it is. [Edwards] Okay lets not waist any time. Lets get right into it. Mike we all saw you lose your US National Title this past week on Mayhem to the Iceman. Do you have any plans on re-claiming that title or do you have any plans to get back at the Iceman?
[Douglas] Hmmmm....Lets see.......Well Iceman I bet your feeling pretty good. You just come off a big win against a man who is undoubtably the greatest wrestler in this sport and you took my title. Well, let me just say this.........
[he pauses] [Douglas] IT WAS NOTHING BUT A FLUKE!!!! Iceman, You had aldy luck on your side that night but the next time we meet, Your luck runs out. I had the spotlight I had the gold. And then some loser with the name Iceman comes along and with one cheap shot victory takes my gold away. Tony, Your a smart man...
[Edwards] Um...Yeah... [Douglas] Let me ask you a question. How did Iceman get that title shot? [Edwards] Well..Uh.....He talked to president Jurkschat about it. [Douglas] He what? [Edwards] Well, he kind of....Uh......Complained to Jurkschat. [Douglas] Ahhhh, Yes. Now you've got it. He complained to the president. Oh poor Iceman, he never gets any tv time. Did you ever wonder why? It because
President Jurkschat doesn't want the IWF/WOW to go out of buisness because your on tv. He wants to keep this federation running strong and with you on all of the shows, thats pretty hard.
[Edwards] He did win the title from you. [Douglas] Just read the cards and shut up. I know and my good friend Iceman knows it that he caught me at a bad time. And Iceman we both know one on one me vs you, I can take you any day of the week. You got lucky, It will never happen again. And as for re claiming the IWF/WOW US National Title. Hey, If it comes my way I'll be glad to take it back. But as everyone knows a week from today I aim for a higher goal. The IWF/WOW North American Championship. Which is currently around the waist of Hightower.
[Edwards] Oaky, Well get to the North American Title situation a little later but first, lets talk about your match this week against Richard James on
Battlezone. Richard James has teamed with the likes of Christopher Stonebreaker in his time. What are your comments on this match?
[Douglas] Well, Dick..... [Edwards] His name is Richard.. [Douglas] Okay. Like I said. Well, Dick. Like I said before, You are the first man to feel the wrath of the _NEW_ Mike Douglas. And all I can say is, I feel bad for you. You see Dick, On Battelzone you will see what it's like to go one on one with Mike Douglas. I don't care who you have teamed with. It will make no difference in the result of this match.
[he pauses] [Douglas] You beat up Brian O'Neil and that's supposed to scare me? Obbviously you haven't paying attention the last couple of months, I don't back down from a challenge. So if you want to get the beating of a lifetime, Show up and I'll give it to you. But the smart move is the stay away from Battlezone. Because pissing me off is not a healthy move.
[Edwards] Does the fact that Richard James is the friend of Cooper Concrete effect this match at all?
[Douglas] Not one bit. Cooper Concrete is a man who back in the day was something. He could actually wrestle/ But now, Like all the other has beens, he has faded into non existance. And personally, I don't really care. I think it's good for this industry. People like Cooper Concrete just take up tv time and it's just one big waist. So Coop and Dick, Why don't you try Golf? Its a lot safer than this.
[Edwards] Okay Now, Next week on Battlezone you get your first crack at the
IWF/WOW North American Title and Hightower. What are your comments on this?
[Douglas] Well,Well,Well, What do we have here? A North American Title shot? Now Tony, You know I can't pass up a chance to become NA champ. US National
Champion was Okay, But now I'm aiming Higher, Now I'm aiming at Hightower.
Hightower, In your short reign as NA Champion so far, You've had it easy. So far, You have never _REALLY_ been challenged for that belt. Until, _NOW_.
Hightower, the free ride ends now. It's time for you to step up to the plate and lets see what you've got. I can tell you this, No matter what, After our match you will remember the name of Mike Douglas.
[he pauses] [Douglas] And this win would look great for me on paper. Hightower, The NA champion, A man who came close to winning the World Championship. And if I defeat him on Battlezone, That would prepell me straight up. So Hightower, I'm taking this match seriously. I hope you are. Because if you don't, then I think you'll find yourself walking to the ring about 15 pounds lighter.
[Edwards] Okay, Mike you say your a changeg man. How have you changed? [Douglas] Well Tony, I'm still the same great wrestler. It's just that now, all the dumb mistakes I made are over. Every single man who steps in my way will know they have been in a fight with Mike Douglas. I'm still the greatest wrestler, I'm still the greatest looking wrestler and I am without a doubt
_MAGNIFICENT_. So from now on, we are playing by Mike Douglas rules.
[Edwards] Well Mike, Thanks for sitting down with me for this very special interveiw......So for Antjony Edwards and for Mike Douglas I am saying.......
[Mike grabs the mic from Anthony Edwards] [Douglas] Wait a second. I can't close an interveiw without saying the famous words. You can think your Good, You can think your Great, But.........
YOU WILL NEVER BE MAGNIFICENT!!!!! [End of scene]
