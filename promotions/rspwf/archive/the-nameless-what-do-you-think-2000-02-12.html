<The Nameless> What do you think?

12 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before I start, I want to let ya'll know that I haven't really rped in a year. The main question I want to ask is: Do I still got it? ___________________________________________________________________ ::Screen fades in on a man perched on a wooden stool looking outside of a window. His black sleeveless t-shirt and black shorts extentuate his toned frame, as well as the sleeves of tattoos on his arms. His hair is brown and shoulder length, and the light from the room turns the window into something of a mirror, revealing a pair of black eyes with red pupils, a brown goatee, a rather pointed nose, and high cheekbones. His pointed chin is almost a pillow for his lips,a corner of which are turned up in a slight sneer. The weather looks to be a downpour, and his creepy eyes seem to notice the cameraman behind him. He addresses him without turning around, giving him chills with hopeful, yet hopeless monotone.::
Man: So, you have come to hear my voice, the words of the voiceless? Harken to the sounds of silence. It is a beautiful thing, and it is broken only by the song of the water goddess Aqua. To think, I had planned a seven mile run today.
Yes, Aqua can be a bitch sometimes, but it doesn't matter. She is aiding her mother, Gaia, the Goddes of Earth, and renewing life for her friend
Christopher, the God of the Living. But enough of my regilous beliefs, what brings you here?
Cameraman: Well, sir, I am here to ask you about your wanting to wrestle for
Runoff Wrestling Alliance*, and President Rowdy Randolph needs to know a few things. First of all, why did you not fill in the blank for your name?
Man: I have yet to make a name for myself, so therefore I am but a nameless face in the wrestling world. Being the Nameless, I don't feel that I had to put my name on the paper. Hmmm...The Nameless, has a certain ring to it, does it not?
Cameraman: Yes it does, as a matter of fact. So is that your name? Man: It is not, but does it matter? I believe a certain man would scream out unintelligibly that it doesn't. So, to appease Mr. Randolph, I shall be the
Nameless.
Cameraman: Ok, Mr. Nameless, I looked at some of your credentials, Trained at the ERUW Gym where you were taught by Wayne Owens, better known to some fans as the Extreme Machine. The question is, will we some of the psychotic offerings that he brought to the sport?
The Nameless: It depends, will I put my all into every match? Yes. Will I send a severed goat's head to an opponent? No. Do I base my style on some Lucha
Libre? Yes. Do I base part of my style on some midget from Brooklyn? No. Am I aggressive? Very. Will I be successful? Only if I want to be.
Cameraman: It also says that you are a second-degree blackbelt in Tae Kwon Do. How will that fit into your style? The Nameless: Martial artists have a very difficult time in the ring. For one, they are often pigeon holed into wearing a gi or a ninja costume, neither of which I intend to do. Secondly, they want to rely solely on their martial arts state-side, which makes for a very dull match. Martial Arts is kind of like
Social Security. As a supplement, it is good. But as a primary source, it is not. I may be a second degree blackbelt, but you may or may not notice that fact when I am in the ring.
Cameraman: Your eyes are very creepy, kind of like the Extreme Machine's marbeled eyes. What is the point of them?
::The Nameless turns around and faces the camera, his eyes narrowed. As he answers the question, the camera starts visibly shaking.::
The Nameless: Do me a favor and try not to compare me to the Extreme Machine, ok? However, I will answer your pathetic little question. The eyes are the doorway to the soul. You can tell alot about a man just by looking into his eyes. My eyes are no exception. They reflect on a man who has spent alot of time suffering. They bring about alot of fear from those who don't understand the man who bears them. Fear breeds hatred, hatred breeds suffering, suffering breeds pain, pain breeds fear. Mate. Feed. Kill. Repeat. Perhaps my eyes are the greatest weapon I possess. For a man with fear in his heart is already defeated. However, even my greatest weapon has to have supplements, that is where the rest of my body comes in.
::He pauses and there is an uncomfortable silence. He sizes up the man behind the camera, birthing a cruel smile as he stands. He turns his back again to the camera and lifts his left leg high, almost 90 degrees off the floor. He holds himself there for a moment, displaying superb balance before violently bringing down the appendage, turning the stool into several splinters. With his back still turned he continues.::
I go through many stools like that. My eyes may scare the hell out of those who may face me, but it is the rest of me that beats the infinitesmal remains of it from the body. Is there anything else you would like to ask?
Cameraman: N-n-n-no, there isn't. The Nameless: So then, I guess this is the point where I spout out my catchphrase? Don't concern yourself with who I am. Don't ask yourself why I am.
The only thing that matters is that I am... The Nameless. ::The Nameless gets farther away as the camera shakes more violently and the screen fades to discource.::
