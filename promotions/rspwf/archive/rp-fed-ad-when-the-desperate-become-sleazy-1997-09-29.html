[RP fed Ad] When the desperate become sleazy

29 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LOOK BELOW FOR YOUR GATEWAY INTO 3.2 REAL YEARS OF TRADITION. ============================================================ Listen guys, I hate junk mail and adverts just as most of you do, and I have enough pride not to send junk about crap or crap about junk. Anyways, I've run into a little snag in my fed and am going to try as hard as I can to overcome this snag. Please try to help me by reading far enough to decide whether or not you want to keep reading, because what I'm trying to do is offer you something good in return for your membership. If you want to call this an advertisement, then I guess it's an advertisement for the International Wrestling Association (IWA). The name is really simple, isn't it? Doesn't sound too creative.. and with due cause. The IWA (first called the W.W.W.) was created 3.2 years ago. That's right- it's a fact that I'm very proud of - the IWA is very old, very much based on tradition, and as many people and most fine wines, it has improved through time. I've been with the IWA for 2+ years and have learned quiet a bit from my mistakes-- enough to run a decent league. Let me just address the snag and then make my shameless plea. You see, the IWA has had rocky and smooth times (DIE, ROCKY, DIE!) and most of the bad days are caused by my stupid resistance to advertising and bringing in new handlers, thus causing shortages in the roster. I'm going try to resist my resistance, so please think over your possible allegiance with the IWA and if you decide that you're interested, fill out the application. I'm looking for as many handlers as I can get. I have twelve right now (including myself), perhaps more on the way, and hopefully membership from you folks. In the last week and a half I cut the membership down to the members who participate and do a great job. Thus, I can promise you roleplaying competition and vast enjoyment-- and the cards are fantastically fantastic, as long as I'm already tooting our horn. Well, I'll shut up now. If you're reading this, then you haven't skipped the message and must be a bit interested (I hope) so here comes the groveling: Please join.. look over the application and sign up. Please. Thanks so much, [name withheld] [detail withheld] =============================== I'm sending the application in this post, but I have an extra item called an Application Guide which I can send to you upon request. It explains some parts of the application and will make sure that you don't make a mistake in the app. I really recommend using it. --- + I don't have an FAQ.. for several reasons, their leader being the fact that I don't want to write one. So what can I tell you? Well..... = The IWA is fairly booked. Matches are fully written. The PPVs (which are usually monthly) are just beyond description (in a good way.) = The IWA has an Open Flash System. You can send your roleplays to a mailing list any time you want. BUT I prefer that you send roleplays to me. I will forward them ASAP. This is done so that some members know which messages to delete right away :) = Challenges. Challenges should be sent to me. My email address is [detail withheld] OR Make a challenge via a roleplay. Cards are booked according to challenges sent to me and feuds through roleplaying and previous cards. = My policy on roleplaying. The IWA is a booked fed. You roleplay and participate, and in return we write the best darn cards you'll ever find. If you do a good job roleplaying, you will WIN. If you don't, then I figure you either need some help or you don't want to WIN. AND In my opinion, a roleplay can be as long as needed as long as some chunk of it makes a point. Interviews, flashes, situation flashes (flashes not just taped in a recording room of some sort), interviews at cards (usually given by a card writer- going back and forth through email), commercials are all roleplays. There are plenty of other types of roleplays, but I'll leave it to you to try them out. send roleplays to me ( [detail withheld] ) or to the mailing list which I use. AND... There's such a thing as STRATS. A short word for strategies, and pretty much self-explanatory. Explain what your wrestler will do before, during, and after a match. Send STRATS to me -- they will often determine a winner. STRATS = BIG WIN! Why? STRATS help the writer of a match tremendously! = I will field any other questions. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ INFO ON THE STAT SHEET ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ + All questions marked "optional" are strictly optional.
