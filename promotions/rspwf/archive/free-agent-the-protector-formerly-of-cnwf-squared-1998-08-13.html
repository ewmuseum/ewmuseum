[Free Agent] The Protector, formerly of CNWF Squared

13 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up inside a large park, somewhere in Richmond, Virginia. It is a quiet, peaceful day, and locals are trying to make the most of the beautiful sun which shines from a cloudless sky. The camera shows a number of elderly couples, just sitting on park benches, talking to one another and just relaxing in the sun. We also see a group of children kicking a ball about to one another. There are young couples strolling around the park, arm in arm, enjoying the day. The camera then focuses on a man nearby. The man is a tall man, around six feet five inches and he looks as though he weighs around two hundred and sixty pounds. The man has short brown hair which he has got combed forward. He is dressed in a smart looking blue sweater and a pair of black trousers. The man is smiling and looking up into the sky. The camera pans up to him as he seems to be ready to speak.)
Man: What a day. (The camera now focuses on the green eyes of the man.) Man: It makes you stop and think, doesn't it, when you see a day like this. There may be so much suffering in the world today, so much violence, so much pain, but there are still occasions when everything seems perfect. Well. . .
almost everything. (The man then leans down and picks up a crumpled up white paper bag, takes a few steps forwards and places it carefully in a rubbish bin.)
Man: Still, there are some _idiotic_ people who take it upon themselves to make the earth just that little bit worse. There are those who try to destroy this wondeful planet. I, thankfully, did not turn into one of those people. I, am the exact opposite. I work hard to keep the earth in the shape that it should be. I try to keep it in good shape. I. . . am the Protector!
(The man smiles as he says that.) Protector: Yes, I help make this earth a worthwhile place. I pick up trash, I try to get the word through to young people what they should and shouldn't do.
I do everything I can do help people. Although, I punish those who deserves to be punished.
(The Protector's smile leaves his face as he says that last sentence and is replaced by a long, cold stare.)
Protector: Yes, those who take drugs, those who cause unnecessary violence, they all deserve to be punished. And _I_ am the man who shall do that, and nobody else! I saw violence in wrestling. I saw drugs in wrestling. Those two things are the things I hate most. So I set out to punish those who have sinned and I shall make them beg for my forgiveness.
(The man smiles again.) Protector: More than anywhere, I see sinning in the CyberNetic Wrestling
Federation, squared. Take, for example, Jason Agony, one of the top contenders in the federation. Here is a man who mocks God, the creator of the world, the creator of Agony himself. Jason Agony is the exact opposite of what a role model should be. He has been sending the wrong message across to young people today, about the government for example. For that, he shall be punished. Yes, he shall be punished.
(The Protector smiles once more as he says this. Then, he stops in front of a couple of boys, who are leaning against a wall, smoking.)
Protector: It may be too late to save you boys, although you _really_ should stop smoking. If only I had gotten my message across sooner. . .
Smallish boy: Listen, _dork_. We can do whatever the hell we want and we don't need some posh [CENSORED] like you coming in here, telling us what we can and can't do.
(The boy and a large, fattish boy then move towards the Protector.) Protector: Step backwards, I don't want to punish you. It is the fault of your parents, and partly my fault that you have taken the road that you have taken.
For that, I will not punish you. (The Protector then continues on.) Protector: Back to the CyberNetic Wrestling Federation, squared. however. I have decided to sign with Lex Victor's little promotion and try to tidy things up a little bit. I understand that there is a tough task at hand, a long road ahead of me, but it is a path I am willing to go down because I see a much better world once this part of my mission is complete. The CNWF² has got a large roster, full of those who have sinned in one way or another. Those people shall be taken care of in the appropriate manner.
Already, I have been assigned a federation debut match. Wednesday afternoon is the date for the next federation house show and I have got my chance to take out and punish the first of a long list of names. This man is the Headhunter. I have done some research on his past and I have found out that he has a long list of sins against his name. I saw drugs, cursing and betrayal in his past, three of the things that I hate most. For that, the Headhunter must be punished, and he will be punished on Wednesday afternoon, when he steps in the ring with me.
Although I am against violence, the appropriate measures must be taken to punish those who really deserve it. I can't say that I am too concerned about taking on the Headhunter. God knows that I can protect myself, or else he would not have choosen to give me this mission. I am a big boy. I can handle the
Headhunter, a smaller figure than myself. I know what I am doing in a wrestling ring. The Headhunter though, shall just be the first of a long list of those who will be punished.
Two days later, I will be back in action. The second man who I must take out is a man who's life is filled with sex, drugs and violence. Triple X, a man who defines the word degenerate. I haven't got the time of day for low-lifes like him because basically, the man is a pig. Straight out, he is a pig. His lifestyle leaves a lot to be desired and because of the badness that he has gotten himself into, an early death is destined to strike him. Unfortunately, I was not able to save him from the path that he took, and because of that I sympathise with him. Triple X, you have done some bad things though, and you continue to do them and unfortunately, that cannot be forgot.
You must be punished. And you will be punished. (The Protector sighs.) The Protector: There are a lot of people who deserved to be punished. They know who they are. They know what they did. I know who they are. I know what they did. And I'm going to get them.
(Fade to Black.)
