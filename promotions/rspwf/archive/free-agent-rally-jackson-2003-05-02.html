[Free Agent] Rally Jackson

2 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm thinking about entering a new fed with my character, Rally Jackson, when I get out of school. It doesn't end for a couple weeks, but my time frees up in a few days so anyway... yeah. I usually check out cards for the feds out there and contact fedheads for openings when I'm looking to join a new fed. But this time I think I'll try it this way. I'm going to post a sample rp and if any fed is interested either email
[detail withheld] or simply respond to this. This is one I did a few weeks ago in I-Slash. [Fade in to I-Slash newcomer, "Tigerheart" Rally Jackson and his roommate, Tito, outside of their apartment in Los Angeles. I think they're moving. Either that or the real owner of that Uhaul's going to be pissed to see them sitting on the hood of it.]
Rally Jackson: Well here we are again. I just got settled in to the west coast. Just got used to all the time changes. And now we have to move again. To Canada of all places!
It seemed like just yesterday. Signed with Pacific Coast Wrestling. Had to learn all the gangs signs to survive on the street. Learned to stay (far) away from any hookers in San Francisco or Palm Springs.
Tito: Long surprises, huh Rall? Rally Jackson: To say the very least. Now here I have to move again. [Rally slides off the hood of the Uhaul. He walks around checking to see if the half dozen kids he gave five dollars and a cigarette to are moving for him.]
Rally Jackson: And it's not just the moving. I was getting used to PCW and now they have passed.... like Rain on a mountain. Like wind in a meadow. Tito: Okay, no more taking you to see "The Two Towers". Rally Jackson: I built up an impressive win/loss record of 2-0 with victories over some of PCW's biggest talents.
"Handyman" Stanley Toole, with his toolbelt filled of wrestling wizardry. And Craig Jay-hawff, the mohegan sun owner who displays his wrestling talents on weekends. Yeah, I really shook up the world over there by defeating those living breathing icons.
And I would've went on to beat Dan "The Man", but....... here we are. It ended before I had that opportunity to kick his teeth in. Luckily, though, there was something in my dark matches that scouts must've really liked. Maybe it was Jim Russ hyping me up. Maybe it was that drunk guy in the front row that kept asking me to sign his breasts. I don't know. But I was offered a contract for the 'big league'. The mother league of PCW, I-Slash!
Tito: I think they just felt sorry for you. Rally Jackson: Now this was all great, but the down side is I have to move to the land where they call ham bacon and stretch their football fields out all the farther just to make the games a LITTLE bit more boring. I hope you brought your skully cap with the little fuzzy ball on top, Tee, because it's gonna be a cold one!!
[At this Rally notices one of the kids drop one side of his couch and it gets dirty. Rally runs over to the kid and all you can hear are church bells going off in order to censor his swears. When he's done, he regains his composure and continues.]
Rally Jackson: Now Tito, it's not going to be easy up there. If a guy named Stu approaches you and asks you to go down to his dungeon, don't do it. A man has never returned from that place the same. And television? From what I hear, all Canadians ever watch are "Kids in the Hall" all day long. Day and night. Something about guys dressing up as gals. And plus we need to learn how to talk like them.
Tito: Talk like them... Rally Jackson: Yeah so Tito, what say we get going soon, eh? Tito: Eh? Rally Jackson: You got it. How aboat that. My first victim will be a man on the undercard before the ppv. They call him A Mystery Opponent.
Tito: Rally, I don't think that's his na... Rally Jackson: Hush, Tito. Go make sure little Rico isn't knicking up that DVD player. So what's the big mystery about you, Mr. Opponent? Are you one of those guys that wrestles with a big question mark on his costume?
Y'know, it's just like some of you wrestlers out there. Disguising your names so I won't know who you are. Afraid to let us see the real you. _Mystery Opponent_... good one!! Did your fifth grade social studies teacher ever tell you... you're reallyl clever!?
Well I don't care what your name is. When I step into that ring, I'll be stepping in there as the best wrestler here. So whether your name is Mystery or Jesus our lord and savior, I'm still smokin' ya!
Me, my name's Rally Jackson. I've got nothing to hide. Brazil, born and raised. It's a long way from Canada, but it's like grandpappy
Jackson used to always say... sometimes you have to look long and far if you want to find a good fight!
[At this, Rally gets in the truck and drives off forgetting all about Tito and the scene fades to black.]
