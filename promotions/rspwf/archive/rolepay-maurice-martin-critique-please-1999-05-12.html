Well, this is my new guy I've recently created, Madling Maniac Maurice Martin. So far he's just in GSWP and TWL. Out of many posts I've posted on this board
I've only gotton 2 responses, so please critique rp if any. I know the scene sems weird in a way but he goes for the bad guy type look. Thanks
[The scene quickly fades into the dark demented streets of Harlem,New York. The camera captures the fog that lumes around in the air and steam arising from the street sewers, as you see a man walk along the pavement in a hooded Tommy
Hilfiger sweat shirt and his hands confirmed in sweat shirt pocket. You can not make out a good picture of this figure that walks the street as the camera fades in even closer capturing the movement of his eyes, you can now make out a clear face as he steps into the light. Next to him is a huge building. He raises his head and then reads the sign above him. The camera slowly zooms in as you see a sign dangling above as a shingle hanging from a ceiling. He walks into the creepy burned warehouse and looks around. He opens his arms and begins to speak.]
4M: Tough Wrestling League... [Martins slightly scoffs.] 4M: A nice, but suttle home for the "so called" maniac in this here world of sports entertainment, this world of madling entities, this world of wrestling so they say. There's a nice world outside, a world of which society has ruined for me, a world of peace for some, a world of freedom for others but for me, it's nothing but a pathetic polluting society which only strives on killing it's only race. And now that I have come to this league which feeds on itself, TWL, I find to get caught up in on all this drama that's going around for a mere World Championship.
Hmph, a belt of which represents society's ultimate force...now as I sit in this hell that I live, and think to myself, my whole heritage fights againsts the society of which killed not only my father, but also my sister. And as much as my father loved this sport, I can honestly say...I don't. And everytime I step into that ring, that squared circle...I can't help but think of the glory which was captured in the eyes of my father, the love he had for this diabolical sport. And everytime I see some worthless dumbass, an opponent, I can't help but strike with anger, rage, bristle, and ire. In the eyes of the outside world, I'm known to you as an felon.
A thug, you might say...but who cares what you murderous sorry excuse of a human thinks? I honestly don't, so go ahead and speak your truth.
[Maurice paces around the lose wooden floors as you hear the eerie sounds of the wood squeaking underneath him, and a smile appears on his face.]
4M: Enough with my past and your petty outside world. I have a job to do so to speak...it's first come, first serve and Alex Cote you _are_, my first reckless victim, believe me, I won't stop there. <diabolical laugh>
Cote, you've selected the perfect name, son. In the near future, obsolete is what you shall be. I want you to tell me something Cote, what does it feel like when I have my chilling palms around your breathless throat? I bet that won't feel to good would it? You see, I don't care, about you, this whole friggen society, the TWL, nothing. That's the thing with me Alex, I feel no remorse, no sorrow, no guilt -- not a solitary ounce and that you can believe my friend. But you, you live and strive for the arrogant polluted air outside to swell your head, you feel remorse and I know for a fact that you definately feel, pain.
But the remaining factor is, how much of an pain treshold do you have? It's certainly not unbearable, but I can positively guarantee you that the pain that I embrace on you will be deep and complete laceration.
[Maurice trademark sardonical smirk appears on his face as he walks towards the camera as it focuses.]
4M: Cote, I somewhat find you as an idiotic imbecile much like most of society out there...but the surprising thing is your lack of brain cells, you better come prepare Monday Night because it will be nothing less then a Massacre! And that's my truth.
[Martin pauses for a breif second.] 4M: Society has labelled me the "Madling Maniac" but as stated, I don't care. Call me god if ya want, you can all me Michael Jordan for all I care. But one thing, I will take that TWL World Championship belt and melt that son ofa' bitch down, not only will I tear into your madling soul Alex Cote but bring down this hell hole of a foundation. Cote, look into these eyes and you take a good look at yourself and you tell me, who's the Maniac!
[Maurice then proceeds to leave out of the abandoned warehouse and into the streets as he places his hood over his head as the scenes slowly...]
