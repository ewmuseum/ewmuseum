[Free Agent] "The True Contender" Jeremy Odessa

3 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's another sample RP for my wrestler. ~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v "The True Contender" Jeremy Odessa Current OSWA TV Champion (EWA, CiA, NCWA, ICWF, OSWA, etc...) ~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v~~^v The Dungeon E-Wrestling Training Center: http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/arena/7859/index.html Jeremy Odessa's Homepage: http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/arena/7859/odessahome.html ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (The scene opens as OSWA reporter Terrence Phillips makes his way down the halls of the United Center in Chicago. He comes to a door that says
"Private -- Superstar Locker Rooms" and pushes the door open and walks into a smaller hallway that has several doors along it. He walks up to the first one and turns to the camera...)
Terrence Phillips: Ladies and Gentlemen, as usuall the other reporters are affraid to get the tough intervues but I will bring you exclusive comments from the OSWA's newest superstar Jeremy Odessa. He doesn't know it yet but i'm about to bust into his private locker room and corner him for a few questions. I'm sure he won't mind.
(He pushes the door open and walks into the room...) Terrence Phillips: Mr. Odessa, I'm here to get some exclusive comments..... (Suddenly, Holly walks around the corner wearing nothing but a towel and a very surprised look on her face...)
Holly: What the...what are you doing in here?!?! (The camera turns away as a very large thump is heard in the background and the screen fades to dark...)
COMMERCIAL BREAK (The image returns to Jeremy Odessa standing over a dazed Terrence Phillips as Holly is heard cursing and walking away in the background...)
Odessa: Dude, are you ok? She hit you really hard. Terrence Phillips: I'm ok, just let me sit down. Odessa: Uh, you're already laying on the floor:-) (Jeremy Odessa pulls him back to his feet and puts the mic back in his hand. A very shaken Terrence Phillips starts over...)
Terrence Phillips: Like I was saying, Mr Odessa you're facing Rattlesnake tonight in your first match here in the OSWA. Do you feel that the pressure is on tonight?
Odessa: In professional wrestling the pressure is always on! I'm here for one reason and that is to destroy whoever the prez puts in the ring with me. Just because i'm new here doesn't mean that I can slack off. I'm on a mission that starts with Rattlesnake and ends with the World Title wrapped around my waist. I've worked for ten years to get where I am now, I've fought men that had no mercy or pitty for anyone, I've even fought men that would be behind bars for a long time in a perfect world! I've gone from night to night, card to card, making a name for myself to get a real shot at the bigtime. I did all that and still never lost my smile! I guess if that means the pressure's on...then so be it! Tonight, i'm starting my quest and nobody, especially not Rattlesnake will stand in my way!
Terrence Phillips: There's been talk around the locker room that there will be a battle royal to determine the new TV Champion. If you're given a chance to compete will you vi for the title as well as your match with
Rattlesnake?
Odessa: If there's on thing i've learned in this life it's that if fate gives you an opportunity you take it! If I get the chance I will be the next TV Champion!
Terrence Phillips: Self confidence is one thing but how can you be certain you will win? Odessa: I have all the respect in the world for the other wrestlers but when I want something bad enough, nobody can stand against me! And beleive me...I want OSWA Gold!!!!! Tonight at Onslaught...I WILL MAKE HISTORY!!!!!
(Holly comes back in the room fully dressed with a very upset look on her face and Terrence Phillips quickly disapears back out the door...)
