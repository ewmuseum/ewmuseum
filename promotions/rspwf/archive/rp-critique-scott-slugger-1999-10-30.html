:::RP Critique::: Scott Slugger...

30 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, everybody. I'm new to this whole RSPWF thing, but I've been in e- wrestling off and on since around '95 or so. In '98, I joined a fed called the fWo with a character called Scott Slugger. Below you will find one of his RPs from a little over a month ago. September 28, to be exact. It does help if you visit the site of the fWo
( http://www.fwowrestling.com ) to get a little background, but it's not completely necessary. Anyway, I was wondering what the population of the rspwf thinks of my character/roleplaying skills. Let me know.
----- :::The scene opens with Scott Slugger, without the World Title, standing in front of the Harris County Domed Stadium, or as it's better known, the Astrodome. As the stadium is currently closed, fans of both the Astros and Reds mill about in the background, as vendors, both
Astro-licenced and independent, hawk their wares. Slugger, in shorts and an fWo logo shirt, takes a moment to savor it all, then turns to the ever-present and oft-omnipotent fWo camera.:::
Slugger: Welcome to Houston, fWo fans, and welcome to perhaps the ugliest stadium in the Major Leagues. What am I doing here? Well, with the fWo taking a week off, it's only fitting that the heart and soul of the fWo take a week off too. So here I am, taking in one of the biggest
baseball games of the year- Houston vs. Cincinnati. Both teams are trying to win the NL Central, both teams are 94-63. Casey and Bagwell,
Wagner and Williamson, Biggio and Larkin, it doesn't get much bigger than this. You see, the team that loses this game could very well be out of the playoffs. Not mathematically, but mentally. It'd be a crushing blow knowing that they choked at such an important time. You see, some people just can't handle the spotlight. In fact, 5, count
'em, 5 fWo'ers come to mind with that statement. In no particular order- Primetime, The Flying Frenchie, Black Panther, Death, and Lance
Star. Sure, you've got a couple titles between these guys, and even a couple World Titles. But really, take a look at those names and tell me they're at the same level- that they *belong* at the same level- as the
WGA. You just plain can't. In fact, let's look through there one at a time.
First, Primetime. You, my friend, have completely blown your shot at the big time. Sure, you managed to soil Meltdown's reputation by winning it, but what have you done since then? You've won the
Cruiserweight Title and the Women's Title. Big fat hairy deal. To win the Cruiserweight belt- which has never been the same since I moved on, by the way, you beat Wretch Kidman Jr. The same guy who could not cut it in the PS. He's nothing, a nobody, and always will be. The Women's
Title? Ooh, tough competition. I don't know how you stopped Nikki The
Valley Girl's 100-0 Winning Streak, but you managed to pull it out.
Please. And then, as if that weren't enough, you handed the title to the Frenchie. Now, how am I supposed to take you seriously, Strangler or no
Strangler, when you can't even hold on to the *Women's Title*? Whenever you get your shot, you can rest assured that it'll just be another day, another squash for the WGA.
Now, on to Frenchie. :::Slugger begins to walk over to a nearby hot dog vendor.::: You keep whining about how I'm ducking you and all that crap. TFF- excuse me for a second here.
:::Slugger quickly turns to the dog vendor, and orders one. As it is put together, Slugger turns back to the camera.:::
TFF, do you have any idea what falls into an industrial sausage press? :::Slugger, without looking, reaches back and grab the ready hot dog from the vendor. He reaches into his pocket and flips a 20 back to the
vendor.::: Keep the change. Now, I'll tell you what falls into an industrial sausage press. Try rodent hairs and bug excrement, among other things.
Coincidentally, they have about as much claim to a title shot- not to mention as much talent- as you.
:::Slugger tosses the hot dog into a nearby trash can.::: So keep on whining, frogman, but it won't get you anywhere. Black Panther, I've only got a few words of warning for you. B.P., you may have won some B.S. tournament to call yourself "Wrestler Of The
Year", but everybody knows two things about that. Number one, Scott
Slugger is the Wrestler Of The Year, no two ways about it. I won that honor the night before CyberSlam, and by my count, it ain't been a year yet. Number two, the winner gets a World Title shot. Panther, I've beaten you before, I'l beat you again, and I will once again fail to break a sweat while doing so. Oh, and by the way, it ain't Halloween yet, so I think I might just take that mask with me before I'm done.
Speaking of Halloween, now we come to Death. Deathy boy, the fact of the matter is that you can walk around acting like you're the King Of
Evil or some such crap and wear dark glasses all you want, but I think those glasses may have made you blind to the facts. Fact number one, which is no real surprise, you've never proven that you can beat me without using a foreign object. I, being a fair and honest person, would never cheat to win. Fact number two, you and me are a lot like
Larry Dierker and Bob Lillis. Who's Bob Lillis, you say? Why, he's the guy who Dierker just passed on the Astros' all-time manager win list.
You see, Bob Lillis is nothing, a nobody, and nobody but his family knows who the hell he is. Lary Dierker, on the other hand, is an incredibly respected and successful manager, and was a hell of a pitcher too. Plus, he's won more games than Lillis. You figure out who is who, and when you're done doing that- which knowing your mental capacity will be some time next year- you get back to me, we can sign a match, and I'll kick your ass again just for the fun of it.
:::With this said, Slugger makes his way towards another nearby vendor, this one apparently independent and selling t-shirts. Slugger checks over the racks of shirts, and see one he likes.:::
Slugger (to cashier): One Billy Wagner jersey, please. :::The jersey is quickly fetched, and brought back to Slugger.::: Cashier: That'll be $20 please, sir. Slugger: No problem. :::Slugger nonchalantly pulls out a 50 and slaps it down in front of the cashier. After staring wide-eyed at the money for a moment, the cashier grabs it and puts it in the register, and begins to look for
change.::: Slugger: Keep..the change. :::Slugger walks off, leaving the cashier stunned at the purchase. After reaching a good distance from the cashier, Slugger turns to the camera again.::: Slugger: Well, it looks like that just leaves one person. Lance "I'll
Never Be A" Star. Lance, do you know who this jersey is of? Billy
Wagner, the Astros' closer. Wagner has been untouchable this year. :::Slugger quickly pulls on the jersey over the fWo logo shirt.::: And quite frankly, so have I. You see, what Billy Wagner does is come in in the ninth inning, and close the book on the game. Once the other team sees him, they know they don't stand a chance. And in that respect, Lance, I'm a bit like him as well. On a whim, I can close the book on anybody in the fWo's career, and once my opponents see me- they know *they* don't stand a chance. Lance, if you're not too busy running your mouth about me, let me ask you a question. Do you want me to close the book on your career? If you don't, you better get your act together, and shut the hell up about your fluke win. But whether you do that or whether you don't, I'm still going to teach you a lesson you'll never forget- assuming you've got the little Lancies to take me up on my offer. There's a tag title tournament going on, and from what I hear, it starts Sunday. How about me and Ice...versus you and Universal for a Round 1 match? If- and only if- you're man enough to accept- I'll show you just how much of a fluke..your win was. And it most certainly will not...be your pleasure.
Now, if you'll excuse me, I've got a ballgame to watch. :::As Slugger finishes speaking, the gates of the Astrodome begin to open, and the crowds excitedly start to pour into the stadium for this huge ballgame. Slugger, still in the Wagner jersey, turns around and heads for the ballpark- pausing to throw a last, trademark smirk over his shoulder- as we fade to black.:::
