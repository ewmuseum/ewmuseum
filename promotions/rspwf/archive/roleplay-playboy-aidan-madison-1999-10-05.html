-[Roleplay]- 'Playboy' Aidan Madison

5 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-X Scene X- The scene opens up inside of the sold out West Virginia Mountainer Stadium. At the time being, the West Virginia University Marching Band is on the field summing up their pre-game show. As the band marches off, the scene cuts down to field level showing cops walking along with a un-identified person. As the camera gets closer, the cops spread letting you see who the person it. It is
'Playboy' Aidan Madison. Aidan Madison is wearing a pair of khaki shorts along with a 'ADIDAS' shirt. His hair is spiked up with a new frost-tip job done on it. He looks into the camera and leans up againest the concrete wall by the stands. Aidan Madison begins to talk.
-X 'Playboy' Aidan Madison X- So, WAR!, long time no see. I bet you were all saddened by my abscence. But now, I'm back. No need to worry, your savior is here...the person that gave your sister the best time of her life last night has showed up once again. I've been in contact though, I know what is going on around WAR! Although, it really doesn't bother me. I see all these people going around saying how good they are, but in all reality....can they live up to their words? I don't know, but
I'm not about to find out. Right now, I have a hand of cards that have Greg
Allocca writen all over them.
So, it is set. 'Playboy' Aidan Madison facing off against 'Sexual Blueberries' Greg Allocca...for the second time. Granted, the first time was a Triple Threat match which made it different, but Greg and I were the only to guys to really fight. Unfortunatly though, Greg was the one that had to come out on top. Yeah,
Yeah! I admit it....Greg did beat me. But, that was then and this is now. I've matured since then. I'm starting to learn the make of the ring and I'm gaining more and better knowledge everyday. It is a great feeling knowing that you are only a rookie and you are able to run with the big dogs for awhile.
Greg, I saw what you had to say. Frankly, I'm saddened. What the hell do you think your doing? Are you crazy or something? You have to get rid of the dye!
Gasp! Whatever shall you do? The funny thing is, you blame a loss on it. Just because you have a allergic reaction to hair dye, you say that you lose a match. Now that is something for the ol' book o' funny. It was Dustmite, how hard could that be. I could put my little sister on Dustmite....no, hold on...I don't have a sister. Scratch that! Is this being taped?!? DAMN!!
Anyways, with all that aside. Greg, I'm so proud of you. You finally realize that I'm not an alien...but you still do call me a bit obnormal. I agree with
Toxic Shock when I say...'You consider yourself normal?!?' Geeze, what is WAR!
coming to? Hold on, don't answer that! If people like Greg Allocca are going to be in WAR!, I'm sure as hell afraid to look into the future. Geeze, image ten sets of blue hair running out...not just one.
Greg, this time is going to be different. I'm ready for you this time. I've been better than you from the beginning and I'll always be better than you! And to take it even farther..... my little 'Playboys' will always be better than your little 'Blue Hair FREAKS' years from now! All I have to say is that the
'Playboy' is back...and back with a vegance.
-X Scene X- 'Playboy' Aidan Madsion climbs back into the stands before the kickoff. A few fans ask for his autograph, but he simply pushes them aside as he makes his way to his seat.
