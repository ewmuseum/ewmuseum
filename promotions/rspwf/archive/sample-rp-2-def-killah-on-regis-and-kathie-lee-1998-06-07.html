[SAMPLE RP] 2 Def Killah on Regis and Kathie Lee

7 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up with Regis and Kathie Lee in their chairs on the everyday set of their show. There is a coffe table with three mugs of coffe on it, the new issue of BBCW Magazine, and 2DK's new CD entitled; I'm Baaaack.)
Regis: Ok, we're back from the commercial break. Hello everyone, I'm Regis. Kathie Lee: And I'm Kathie Lee. Today we have a guest that many people have probably heard of, he is one of the most controversial people in the pro-wrestling world, and one of Cody's favorite's. He is, the multi-talented, 2
Def Killah!!!
("Make Em Say Uh" By Master P plays as 2DK walks out raisin' the roof. 2 Def
Killah is a young white male. He has blond hair and blue eyes. He's extremely attractive. He is wearing dark blue BOSS jeans about 15 sizes too big and a baje and blue denim shirt. He is average size with a large build. He walks over to them, and hugs hugs both of them, then sits in his seat.)
Regis: Well, 2 Def Killah, welcome to the show. 2DK: Well, I'm extremely happy to be hea, ya know? I mean, I gotta get myself back out there somehow. BBCW closing, ruined my career. I was so close to being at the top. And it all came crumbling down. I'm makin' sure that don't happen this time.
Kathie Lee: Well, let's talk about your brand new CD out, it's called "I'm
Baaaack". What's the meaning of that?
2DK: Come on Kathie, are ya just gonna waste my time? You know wut it's about. It's about a return to the top. I mean, this disc is phat. It's got 31 rockin' tracks wit cameo's from all of Amerikaz Most Wanted. And remember everyone,
Sunday the 21st, get it in stores.
KL: Well, I heard rumors a few months ago about you signing with Bad Boy Records? Is there any truth in this? 2DK: Naa, none at all. I mean, I'll give mad props to Puffy, Mase, the Lox, Lil
Kim and all of 'em. I love em all. But this CD and all my other one's to come are produced by a label I started up, it's called BeatHouse Records. I'm signing some new talent, so expect to see more from BeatHouse.
Reg: Well, let's get to the hard questions. We all have the race question. I know that you were in an independent wrestling card, and you faced someone named PimpDaddy. And he didn't like you becuase you were a white rapper. This is a very controversial thing. Can you please explain to us your opinions on the race issue?
2DK: Look, I dont give a sh{bleep} if you're white, black, red, green, yellow, blue, or even f{bleep}ing pink. But other people do. Look, cuz I'm a rapper, they don't like the fact that I'm white. It don't really matter, I don't need people like that. PimpDaddy was a different story though, he was just a playa-hata. The bitch wuz mad cuz I make more G's then him, and my rhymes are tighter than his. If you people think I'm gonna disappear, you're wrong.
(pointing to himself) This is the 2DK. This aint no Vanilla Ice. I'm gonna be hea weather you like it, or not. You aint driving me out like that. Everybody, you betta not mess wit me. Everyone know's I'll flip on thos that choose to use disrespectfull views on the King of LI.
Reg: Hmm, King of LI? Don't you think that's a little bit cocky? 2DK: Yea, and your point is? Kathie Lee: Ok, let's look at the new issue of BBCW Magazine. It has the new president on the cover, wut do you think of him?
2DK: I don't know, I don't talk s{bleep} about {bleep}s that I don't even know. Reg: What about this new BBCW thing? They went banckrupt and are opening up for another month? Are you involved in this?
2DK: Hell no, why would I be? There's nothing in it for me. BBCW screwed me once, they aint gonna screw me again. I'm looking for big time. WORLD EXPOSURE.
I'm a world class athelete, why would I go back to BBCW? They screwed me out of my lightweight title, and almost screwed me out of my career. They sent these two masked men to jump me at every match I had. President Davis was a big ol'
playa-hata, and I aint takin my chances with the new president. KL: So, do you have any wrestling contracts right now? 2DK: No, but don't expect that to be for long. I'm coming back, and coming back wit a vengeance. All of da peeps betta watch out.
Reg: So, what song are you gonna perform tonight from your new album. 2DK: None, I wanna take this time to pay tribute to 2 influences in my life. I'm gonna perform the 2Pac song, "Life Goes On". It's dedicated to DA Mack, one of my best homies, the late-great 2Pac, the late-great Notorious B.I.G., and any otha young men that died in the struggle. I'm also gonna perform one of my other favorite songs, 2Pac's "Only God Can Judge Me".
