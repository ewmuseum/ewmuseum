[FLASH] FBK....Positive Criticism Welcome...

18 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is my latest flash for my character FBK (Full Blood Killah) in ICW... [ SCENE: A luxurious mansion off the coast of Virginia Beach. The sun is setting and there is a slight breeze. Its about sixty degrees, very comfortable weather. The camera looks at the Atlantic Ocean and it paints a beautiful picture, being the sky is red-orange due to it setting. The camera then walks toward the front door of the mansion. The front yard is landscaped nicely, the hedges are trimmed in different shapes, the grass is finely cut, etc. The man rings the door bell. A few seconds pass, no answer. The man rings again. A few seconds pass, and just as he's about to ring the bell again, the door swings open and in the doorway stands the six foot five inch, two hundred seventy five pound Killah. ]
[ APPEARANCE: FBK has on a pair of teal and white And 1 basketball shorts along with a Duke basketball t-shirt. His hair is a mess and he has on no shoes, just low cut socks. He looks like he just woke up. ]
[ He invites the camera man in and orders him to take a seat on the couch. FBK walks into what looks like the kitchen and comes out moments later with a glass of milk. He sits in a chair, across from the camera. He takes a few sips of his milk and then begins to talk...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: [In a groggy voice] Jus' woke up man, can't even get ma' sleep anymore. [Clears his throat and begins to talk in his normal voice] Ah, thats better. What can I say, [holds up his glass] Milk, does a body good!
Well, all of you that don't know, welcome to the epicurean Full Blood Killah estate. Just got through with Rockin' Da World in Pittsburgh so decided to spend the night here at my home, in Virginia. Here, I _ALWAYS_ get treated with respect, twenty four-seven. But in ICW, can't say the same. Things will change though, over time. Well, I had my Rockin' Da World debut just the other night and I wasn't very pleased with my showing. Sure I kicked Tommy Boy's scrawny ass up and down the very ICW ring. But it ended in a Double DQ. No biggie though, I remain undefeated in ICW. Kain, you surprised me Tuesday night, I guess I underestimated you. But ya know the old saying "always learn from your mistakes"...well I'm going to have to use that in my pursuit to winning this battle royal Friday. Kain, I was watching a video tape of our match last night and I found some of my flaws that I intend to fix in the gym later on. Kain, you're nothing special in the ring, and I think the same way about every other guy that is going to participate in the battle royal Friday. I mean, you may think I'm opinionated when I say that, but I'm not...
_JUST_... ..._ALWAYS_... ..._RIGHT_! FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Kain, you're toast come Friday evening. But moving on...as I listen to interviews of ICW wrestlers, I always listen to what my opponents have to say because I usually have to set them straight. Well concerning this
Friday's battle royal, that really isn't the case because the only guys talking smack are Tommy Kain, and a guy I think is yellow, Jason Williams. Williams,
I've heard good things about you, how you've been great in other federations and I respect that. But now you're in ICW, a federation where FBK can run with tha big dawgs. You might be as good as everyone says, or maybe a little overrated. We'll find out Friday evening when FBK and yourself brawl in the ring, to win a battle royal and to make our name in ICW bigger than it already is. Williams, Friday we may be the last two left, but I _PROMISE_ to win this thing. Its my ticket to super stardom and I'm ready to cash in. I'm gonna make your scrawny ass famous Friday night Knux, and there ain't _NOTHING_ you can do about it. Knux, I'll make it plain and simple...when you mess with da Killah...
_YOU_... ..._GET_... ..._KILLED_! [ FBK finishes his glass of milk. He then looks into the camera with a milk mustache. The camera man begins to chuckle and FBK looks confused...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: What? Whats so funny you piece of camel crap? VOICE: Sir, wipe your mouth... [ FBK looks in a mirror next to him and then turns his head. He doesn't look too pleased. He proceeds...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Shut the hell up...don't make me come over there and shove that camera straight up uranus! But that would arouse you so forget that idea.
Fallout is going to be big once again and I'm looking forward to it. But it looks like my good ole' buddy Tommy Grayson doesn't have a match I can interfere in this week. Damn...takes the wind outta my sail. But I'm sure he'll be in the building so maybe I can get his attention in another way. Grayson, you seem to be a little too preoccupied with your little bitch, Samantha. Well
Grayson, I may not be International Champ or recognized as a great wrestling in
ICW _YET_...but I'm no jobber here either. So you better clean the poo-poo out of your ears and listen to what I have to say. Stay out of my business from here on out, or else your ICW career will take a turn for the worse...courtesy of da Killah. Grayson, you just don't get it. Well you better realize that
I'm...
_THE MAN_.... ...._THE MYTH_.... ...._THE LEGNED_.... ...._DA KILLAH_!!! [Brief pause...] FULL BLOOD KILLAH: You better worship the ground I walk on Grayson or else I'll give you a helluva lot of trouble! I don't care what you did in the past or what you plan to do in the future. The time at hand is the present and you better worry about da Killah. And if you decide to not listen to me, well, then its easy...I'll make your life a living hell...
_FROM_ _BELL_ _TO_ _BELL_!!! And that goes for _EVERY_ single man participating in the battle royal Friday. Kain, Knux, Nyghtstorm, and whoever the hell else. I'm gonna walk all over you and there ain't _NOTHING_ you can do to say yourself.
[ FBK then flashes an arrogant grin to the camera. He then puts his hand over the camera lens and nothing can be seen. The camera then gets even darker....FADE ]
