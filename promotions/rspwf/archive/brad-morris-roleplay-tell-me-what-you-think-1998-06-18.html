Brad Morris roleplay. Tell me what you think!!!!!

18 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens up on a building that reads MORRIS BROTHERS GYM. There are many people that look like body builders walking into the building. The camera then enters the building and focuses on one man benching at least 250 pounds with ease while an older man spots him. As the camera comes closer, the man shows to be none other than Brad Morris. He is wearing nothing but a tight pair of biking short with a black heart on each leg.)
Brad Morris: Hey hey hey. I've been expecting you guys, considering I am your next United States Champion. But let me first introduce you to my father, a true living legend in his own right, "The Rock" Tony Morris.
Tony Morris: Thanks Brad. I tought this boy everything he knows, and I have faith he will be your next US Champion, no matter who likes it. Brad, take it easy now. Don't talk to much, you still have a lot of training left to do before tomorrow's big match.
Brad Morris: All right pops. (Tony Morris walks over to a desk and begins doing some paper work.) Brad Morris: That's the man that tought me everything I know. He tought me to be the best, he tought me my own Heartbreaker, and he tought me how to be a real man. But he never tought me about friends that live to stab you in the back. He never tought me how hard it truly is to get the title
"champion". These things I've had to learn on my own. These things I've had to use my own living instinct on. And these things have truly made me what I am now.
(A very beautiful blonde wearing hardly anything walks up and whispers something in Brad's ear.)
Brad Morris (smiling): Well, I shaved my hair. I grew a goatee. I took out my name. I've changed my style. But you know, this is living. This is what it's all about. Women still love me, and I take no prisoners. I never knew I could have all at once. But it sure is nice. Now, what's next? That little belt that ole Mr. Killer has around his waist. That title, which soon will be mine.
Brad Morris: You know Killer, go on. Tell everyone just how big and tough you really are. Go on, and tell them how I have failed time and time again. That was the past Ken. It's time to look towards the future. It's time to look towards tomorrow night. I think you are underestimating me an awfull lot. I've been two time TV champion and a Hardcore champion here in the WHW. And you say I have yet to prove myself? You say I have gotten nowhere? You're wrong. Look at what the WHW has gotten me. Or better yet, look what I have gotten for the WHW.
(Brad walks over to his closet and opens it up. He pulls out various Love
Machine shirts. He pulls out a hat that reads The Real Love Machine. He pulls out a styrophome finger shaped insead as a heart with the words
HEARTS OF DESIRE on it. On the side of the door is a poster of Brad, Steve
Morris, and the Kiss of Death. Next to it is a picture of Josh Cidadao.
There is many other Brad Morris WHW merchandise hanging in the closet.) Brad Morris: Look at this stuff. This is what has made the WHW rich. I have changed the face of professional wrestling more than any other superstar has ever done, in any federation. And that's all because I am the greatest, and you all know it!
Brad Morris: Let's take ole Josh Cidadao for instance. A man with not many talents when he first entered the WHW. But what happened? He joined the old Hearts of Desire. He took his place right behind me, and he became famous. And now look what I have gotten for him. He is now known as one of the greatest cruserweight champions in the history of WHW, all because I allowed him to be great. Another sample. Old Steve Bundy. Yes, your one and only fat ass commentator who goes on and rambles about, I don't know,
ABSOLUTELY NOTHING. But yet, people love him. Why? Because. Interesting story. Back in the days of the PFWL, I allowed Steve to face me one on one. We were great friends, but I felt that Steve wasn't getting over with the crowd as well as he wanted to. And so, I set it all up. Bundy would defeat me in front of one of the biggest audiences the PFWL and the WHW has ever had. And that's exactly what Bundy needed. After that win, which was all played on, he became hip. He became popular. And what thanks did I get? Nothing, but that's all right. I've proven my own fame on my own.
The way it ought to be. Brad Morris: Now, we've got "Killer" Ken Wilson. A man just beggin to get the fame and fortune every wrestler hopes for. And Ken, you've come to the wrong place. To quote ole Billy The Kid, I'm gonna make you famous pal.
No no no. (Brad begins to laugh.) Brad Morris: Wipe that grin off your face right now Ken. I'm not going to let you beat me. No way pal. I'm just too hot nowadays to give you a win.
You're gonna loose. You can bet on that. But it's up to you how you're gonna loose. It can be easy, or it can be awfully hard. And personally, I have chossen the hard, because that's how I'm going to make you famous.
When I break your heart in front of thousands, that's when you become famous. When i pin your shoulders down for the three count, that's when you become famous.
Brad Morris: Ken, we all know you will be bragging about your loss for months. I can just here it now. "YEA!! I GOT BEAT BY THE GREATEST SOB IN
THE WORLD!!" Yea, I know. But I'm modest. Anyways, your title, that gold strap you have around your waist now, is headed straight towards my waist.
You know what, I really don't care about a little US title. The world knows I am World Title material. But Jeff won't give it to me. So to show what I am, I'm gonna beat you. And then, maybe you will shut your mouth.
Brad Morris: And the little No Holds Barred stipulation, not gonna work against me there Kenny. I've been former Hardcore champion if you've forgotten already. FOR CHRIST SAKES MAN!!! I KILLED A MAN. Now if that isn't hardcore, I really don't know what is. So Ken, bottem line is I'm walking out with my hand held high and that US title around my waist. And you, well, you're gonna wake up famous.
(Brad begins to laugh as camera fades out.)
