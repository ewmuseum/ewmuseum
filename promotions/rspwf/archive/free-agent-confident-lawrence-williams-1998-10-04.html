[Free Agent] 'Confident' Lawrence Williams

4 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade-in to a small room, where dozens of reporters and other prominent people in the wrestling world have gathered. A soap- box rests in the front of the row, topped with a wooden podium and a small microphone. Behind the podium, three chairs sit, all of them blue and with leather padding. All the clamor in the room comes to a cease as a tall black man steps from behind the curtains that are to the right of the podium. The cameras quickly begin to flash as the room realizes that it's none other than Daniel Rushing. Daniel, wearing a black, two-piece Armani suit, steps to the podium confidently.
He puts a fist to his mouth and clears his throat. He then begins.] DR - "Ladies and gentlemen of the press, I have come before you today to drop a huge announcement on the professional wrestling world. Yes, this announcement will top any other made this whole week, and that's a guarantee.
Over the past week, a few people have made themselves free agents, claiming to the biggest and the best free agents on the market. And while that _may_ have been true, today that all changes."
[A few murmurs go up through the press. Daniel patiently waits for the noise to cease. He then continues.]
DR - "It is my duty to introduce to you the best thing since the death of Ian Walsh . . . Yes, I introduce to you
. . . the conquerer . . . the cocksure . . . the confident 'Confident' Lawrence Williams." [Now, clamor fills the room as all attention is put on the curtains, where Lawrence Williams is supposed to come from. And then, none other than Mr. Williams himself steps through the curtains, sporting his trademarked smirk of sanguine and a black two-piece suit. Lawrence walks up to the podium and shakes Daniel's hand.
Both men exchange a few words and then laugh at whatever they said. After this, Williams adjusts the micrphone to his heigth and begins.]
LW - "Well, well, well . . . betcha' didn't think I'd be up here 'gain, did ya'. Well, I'm tryin' my best to speak [Lawrence throws up the quote sign with his fingers] "proper English" fo' y'all so that you can understand what I say."
[A few laughs go up through the crowd.] LW - "Shut up. I didn't give you pa'mission t'speak, boy. Anyway,
I came out here today 'cause I was disturbed by some comments made by some dude who calls himself "the Showstopper." He said that _he_ was the biggest free agent. Well . . . I got a few words fo' ya', pal . . . _I AM!_
So, who should it be? CNWL? They've expressed numerous times 'bout how they'd love t'have me. UIWF? I heard that rat-faced bastard LOCO, who has _MY_ ending quote, left, so its chances of signing me are slim. UEW? Well, I'd sho'
'nuff like t'kick Creed's ass fo' takin' _MY_ HHWO World Title. Well, outta all them choices, I guess I'mma choose--" [Lawrence is then interrupted by his agent, Daniel Rushing, who comes from behind him and puts his hand over the microphone.
Daniel then looks at Lawrence and begins to shake his head.] DR - "The league that he will be joining can not be revealed, but if you want Lawrence, give us your best offer and you just win get 'em."
[Lawrence then pushes Rushing out of the way.] LW - "Gimme this damned mic, boy. Look . . . all of you leagues out there had better listen up. I'm the biggest, the best, the baddest, and sho' 'nuff the most confident free agent on the market right now!!! Fo'get Simon Lebec, Lawrence Williams is lookin' fo' uh fed!"
[Rushing now leans over into the microphone.] DR - "Thank you, everyone, for coming out, but that's all the time we have for you today. Lawrence and I will be back on October
14, 1998, to discuss which league we decided on and why.
Once again, thank you, everyone, and have a nice day." [The camera fades-out with shots of the reports flashing their cameras and shots of Lawrence Williams and Daniel Rushing getting into yet another dispute.]
[Black.] Well, there you have it. Let the invitations begin . . . -Daniel "I will say of the Lord, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him I will trust." - Proverbs 91:2
