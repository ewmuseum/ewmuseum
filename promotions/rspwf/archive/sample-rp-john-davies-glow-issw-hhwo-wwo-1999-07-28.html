[SAMPLE RP] John Davies [GLOW, ISSW, HHWO, WWO]

28 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cut to; the scene of the living room of HHWO superstar, "No Remorse" John Davies, the near seven foot monster. Davies is slouched in a comfortable looking blue leather chair, with his feet resting on a small matching stool in front of him. Davies glares into the camera, which soon focuses in on his clear, blue eyes. Davies now starts to speak...]
JD : Hometown Heroes Wrestling Organization; a federation whose
'stars' must now live in fear, must always watch their backs, must be wary of the latest addition to the roster of their beloved federation.
When I signed a deal with the HHWO, I made it perfectly clear that I wasn't here to play. I made it a known fact that I was not coming to the HHWO to mess around with the pit of the
HHWO superstars, competing in meaningless matches and just being used as someone to make up the numbers.
I made a pledge, not only to myself, but to Wayne Michaels, that the name John Davies would turn into the most feared one in the history of the HHWO. This is a federation which boasts names like Setzer van Strife, but in time, people will soon realize that John Davies is the name to fear.
[Pause...] JD : Already, "Magnificent" Mike Douglas has felt the wrath of John Davies. Last week, when watching his match on Warpath, I was so disgusted by his lack of talent that I decided it was time for me to go on just that - the Warpath.
Suffice to say, Douglas is not scheduled to compete this week and I wouldn't expect to see him back in the ring in the near future. Basically, Mike, that lead pipe I used on you wasn't the most brutal thing that the HHWO will see from me. Neither was the Powerbomb I performed on you through a table.
As time wears on in the HHWO, things are only going to get more and more brutal from me. I am on a mission to establish myself as the most dangerous superstar around - I want the name John Davies to be feared - and I will stop at nothing to ensure that my goal is achieved.
[Pause...] JD : This week on HHWO: Warpath, I make my singles debut here in the HHWO. Boltz is the fool that I am scheduled to face, so Boltz is now set to be my next victim. He is the man who
I will go out and destroy this week, he is the man whose career shall be destroyed. I want to make it clear that when I pummel you into oblivion come August 1st, Boltz, it will be nothing personal against you. It will just be me doing what I must do, what makes me tick in life. From this moment onward, I am setting out on a rage mission against you, Boltz, and come Warpath, I will end your career.
There is nothing I won't do, Boltz, nothing that is too dangerous for me to try. I will harm you in each and every way possible, and I would be grateful to Wayne Michaels if he would make this into a no disqualification match, for I want to hurt you with style, Boltz.
You see, when I step into the ring, Boltz, I have only three goals, and they are to: Hurt. Maim. Kill. [Fade...]
