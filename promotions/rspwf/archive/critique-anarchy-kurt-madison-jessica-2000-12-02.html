[CRITIQUE] "Anarchy" Kurt Madison - Jessica

2 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up inside of a bus, apparently a Gray Hound, with pratically every seat on the bus packed. The camera's view is very blurry and is going in and out of focus as it bounces up and down. The camera slowly pans around right onto the face none other than "Anarchy" Kurt Madison.]
[Kurt Madison's green mohawk has been combed out for this self-shot interview. His green hair is spiked out in all sorts of different directions. The light illuminating out from the home video camera is making Kurt squint his blue eyes. Madison, very obviously, has a defining smirk on his face, as if he is up to no good. All you can see is the top of the seat behind Madison and a very close up shot of his face.]
[KM]: "Who let the dogs out?" [Kurt Madison raises his eyebrows as if he is waiting for the camera to answer.] [KM]: "No, really. Who let the dogs out? Could it be that the [beep]ing security system at the dog pound went down again? Nah, I don't think so. Could it be that Rocky Mountain Wrestling has issued me in a dog collar match? DING!
You are right! Johnny, tell them what they have won!" [Kurt Madison's face turns cold as he takes a quick glance out the window. As Madison looks back into the camera, his face is etched with carelessness.] [KM]: "Let's take a look at the facts, shall we? I'm in the main event at the first ever RMW card. I could care less. I'm in the tournament for the RMW
Heavyweight Championship. I don't care. And, last but not least, I'm fighting in a dog collar match."
[Madison takes his free index finger and twirls it in the air.] [KM]: "Whoopie [beep]ing Do! Seriously, does the RMW think that I am a _DOG_ of some sorts? They want to tie me by my neck to one end of a dog collar while the other end is tied to Stephen Bourdo's neck. I mean, I could be wrong, but the law of physics says that one neck is going to have to give way. And it ain't going to be mine."
[KM]: "So, what do you want me to do? Speak about Stephen Bourdo and tell you what I think of him? Yeah, well, it is sort of hard when I know nothing of the guy. He could be a winner. He could be a loser. He could be a dork. He could be a [beeping]ing elephant for all I know. But, I know, for a fact, that Stephen
Bourdo will be looking at the ceiling for the three count." [KM]: "You may say I'm a little over confident, Bourdo. But to tell you the truth, I'm not over confident at all. I'm merely at the point where I don't beat around the bush. I tell you how I feel, I tell you exactly what I think is going to happen. And what do I think is going to happen?"
[A dramatic pause.] [KM]: "Only I shall know."
