Well people, I've used this e-wrestler before, and people liked him. I'd like to hear some feed-back on if he'd be a pretty good character to use, or if anyone wants him in their fed. (Prolly not, but oh well.) Well, here is Corbin
King's bio ...
-- Name: Corbin King Height: 6'2" Weight: 231 lbs. Hometown: Walla Walla, Washington Finisher(s): "The Finishing Move" - Springboard Bulldog Date Of
Birth: 7/18/67 Entrance Theme: "Rappers Delight" by The Sugarhill Gang
Face/Neutral/Heel: Face Style Of Fighting/Wrestling: Brawler/U.S. Worker
Titles Held: Stupid, Idiot, Moron. =o) - Ring Attire: Corbin wears a mid-theigh length singlet that is two different colors. The top part of the singlet is usually covered by a t-shirt that will have various quotes on it, but underneath, it is usually a neon-green color. The bottom part of his singlet is black, that comes down to mid-theigh. He will usually wear plain white boots to the ring. - Attitude: Corbin King is plain and simply put, a moron. He does stupid things, as well as says stupid things. He really never knows what is going on, and he is always messing something up. He will often go out of his way to make people like him, because he doesn't have too many friends. Most of his opponents think that it's a trick, and that he's fooling them into thinking that he is really a good wrestler. But people, he's not kidding you, he's a moron. - Background Information: Corbin grew up in Walla
Walla, Washington. He was really close to his parents, Nicholas and Sandra, who are still living today. While growing up, he started to take a liking to the sport of wrestling. He excelled very well, but couldn't keep up, because his grades were way too low. He eventually quit school, and lived at home with his parents, untill a local wrestling show came to town. During this show, he seen how "cool" it was, and he began to work with the fed setting up rings, and occasionally doing the "JOB". He then began to work on his skills, really impressing promoters, and working his way up to main event status. He now travels all over, looking for work. - Corbins Favorite Moves: 1) Running
Savate Kick 2) Legsweep Takedown 3) Moonsault 4) Frankensteiner 5) DDT

(Super, Front Lay-Out, Sitting) 15) Headbutt 16) Rocker Dropper 17) Spinning
Spinebuster 18) Swinging Neckbreaker 19) Chinbreaker 20) Michinoku Driver II
