FREE AGENT W/SAMPLE ROLEPLAY

4 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, I'm looking to get back into e-wrestling and I'm looking for a league to join. A sample roleplay is below and right before my roleplay is a few things that I am looking for in a league.
--- Booked league with all original wrestlers - no Austins or Goldbergs --- Cards that are on time --- Web page --- Roleplays come in the form of flash packets - don't like feds that use boards --- Been open for at least 3 months --- Roster size of at least 15 wrestlers - also don't want to join a league that has too many wrestlers in it SAMPLE ROLEPLAY "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- Fades in to a large conference room where wrestling reporters from around the world have gathered to interview the newest member to the wrestling world, a man who calls himself "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase. A table is set up at the front of the room with a chair in the middle, a bottle of water awaits the man of the moment. A door to the side of the room opens and in walks a man standing about 6'4" in height and appears to be in very good physical shape. He has long blondish brown hair that is pulled back into a ponytail and is wearing a black t-shirt, black leather jacket, blue jeans and black Army combat boots.
The man takes a seat at the center of the table and grabs the bottle of water. He looks around at the reporters, takes a drink of water and motions that he is ready to begin the interview. ---
--- Reporter #1 --- Hi, I'm a reporter with 1wrestling.com and I'd like to ask you a few questions. First though, how would you like to be addressed today? --- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- You can just call me by my first name, Sebastian. --- Reporter #1 --- Ok then Sebastian. Most of us know that you have been training for a long time and are ready to make your anticipated debut in the pro wrestling world. For those that have never heard of you and don't know about you, can you please take a few minutes to fill them in on your background?
--- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- You really think these people give a (censored) about my life? If you think the pro wrestling fans want to know about my childhood you are dumber than you look.
--- Reporter #1 --- Some people out there could care less that's true, but there are a few people out there that are curious as to why a man would turn to pro wrestling as a profession. So for those people will you please answer the question?
--- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- Very well then, if that's what you guys want. My parents were people who tried to be good parents but were anything but the sort. My mother grew up in an abusive house where her father would go out and get drunk then come home and beat her, her mother, and her sisters. My dad grew up in a more high class family but due to a very high fever he had when he was young his body stopped making a certain chemical in his brain which caused him to become mentally unstable. Most of the time my father was fine as long as he was taking his medicine, but on occasion he would fly off the handle and need to be hospitalized so they could adjust his medication. It's said that subconsciously, people in abusive households grow up and seek people out that are much like the abusive people they grew up with in their life. My mother did exactly that and married my father, who was not physically abusive to her, but very mentally abusive. You see, instead of physically abusing my mother he abused me. I was the one who took all the hits, my mother was the one who took all the mental abuse. We suffered together, each taking a piece of the abuse, it was the only way to survive. Luckily for me my grandparents lived near by and I was able to escape three times a week to stay with them. My grandparents were the ones that kept me sane, but it was my father that made me strong. I took the beatings and after a while, you don't feel them anymore. I grew up a bitter and angry person, but my grandparents tried their best to get that part of my past to be nothing more than a bad memory. I moved in with my grandparents during my high school years and my life turned around, I was on my way to becoming a successful young man. Then I graduated and hooked up with the wrong type of friends. I grew up in a very strict house because of my parents so when I was old enough to be out on my own I had a lot of catching up to do, a lot of partying. I wasted a lot of years partying with my friends, and all that became apparent when the worst thing happened. I lost my grandmother. It woke me up and I tried to better myself, I wanted to make my grandfather proud of me. I was going to college and trying to make a better life for myself when my grandfather passed away, tragedy had struck a second time. I was devestated.
I never got the chance to make my grandfather proud, and to this day it haunts me. I know now that I am not the college ty-e of person, I'm a blue collar guy that believes that hard work is the only way to get ahead in this world. No one in this world is going to hand you anything, this is a hard world to get by in.
I learned the hard way, if you want something you have to take it. Pro wrestling is my way of letting out my pain and at the same time, making a name for myself in this world. I have worked my ass off while I was training, knowing that my only goal is to hurt and maim men inside that ring.
--- Reporter #2 --- Sebastian, I'm a reporter for ESPN and I have a question for you. --- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- A question for me? I guess that's what makes you a reporter huh? Ask your freaking question genius.
--- Reporter #2 --- It sounds like you did indeed have a very troublesome childhood. There are lots of people that have hard lives growing up though, so do you expect people to feel sorry for you?
--- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- Did I ask you to feel sorry for me? I told that first reporter that no one would give a (censored) about my childhood, but I answered the question because he asked me to. I don't want anyone to feel sorry for me, that's not my goal.
My goal is to use my past as a motivational tool to hurt people, put them in the hospital. I don't like the way you just came off with that question and I have half a mind to come out there and twist you into a pretzel. I know you are a reporter though because you are the type of guy that would rather report on a sport that you like rather than take a chance at breaking one of your manicured nails. Guys like you are pansies while guys like me feast on your souls as we beat you and humiliate you. You are done for the night, I will not accept any more questions from you so you can just sit down now.
--- Reporter #3 --- Sebastian, do you know what league you will sign with yet? Have you recieved any offers? --- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- I have not recieved any offers as of yet but then again, this is the first day that I am announcing that I am accepting offers. I have no idea where I will sign, and I really have no preference where I compete. I will be very selective as to where I sign as I do want to sign with a federation that suits my individual tastes. I will not just sign with the first league that sends me an offer, they will have to prove to me that I would be a valued asset to their federation and that I would not just become another man on their roster. I know there are too many leagues out there where the owner or president hires their friends and pushes them over more talented wrestlers. I am not the type to take a back seat to anyone, I am one of the big dogs and I expect to be treated as so.
--- Reporter #1 --- Sebastian, can you explain to us your goals? I mean what do you want to accomplish in this sport? Are you out to just become a champion or do you have any other goals in mind?
--- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- When someone enters the sport of professional wrestling their goal is always to become the world champion, to gain the spotlight for their own personal glory.
If you are not a competitor and don't want to accomplish that goal, well, you won't last long in this sport. I do want to become a world champion, but that is my secondary goal. My primary goal is to become a legend in this sport. When people hear my name I want them to quiver in their boots. I want to be feared, respected, and hunted. When you are the top dog in this sport, there are men whose goal is to take you out to make a name for themselves. If I can become that man that people want to defeat, then I will have accomplished my goals.
--- Reporter #2 --- Sebastian, my next question.... --- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- Shut up! I already told you that I would not accept any other questions from you. You try to ask me a question one more time and I will come out there and beat the (censored) out of you! That's your last warning.
--- Reporter #4 --- Sebastian, I like many others are wondering why you have taken on the moniker of "The Bounty Hunter"?
--- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- I use that name because I feel is suits my character. I don't like anyone, I'm not out to make friends. This is a business where everyone is out for their own good. There is backstabbing galore, you can trust no one. I will go after who ever I want to, or who ever I am paid to go after. That's right, I will hire myself out to other wrestlers or league presidents. This is a business first and foremost, and if someone wants to pay me to take out their opponent then that is what I will do. If a president wants to pay me to protect his pansy ass, then that is what I will do. Don't think for a minute though that money will buy my loyalty, it only buys you time.
--- The Organizer of the Press Conference --- Gentlemen, we are running out of time. I will allow two more questions and then we must call it a day.
--- Reporter #4 --- Sebastian, would you consider yourself to be a technical wrestler? A brawler? What style do you prefer to utilize? --- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- I don't consider myself to be any particular style of wrestler. I do what I have to, when I have to, to win a match. If I had to say which type of moves or style I use mostly I would have to say that I am a brawler, but I am a technically sound wrestler. You don't go through wrestling training to learn how to fight, you learn how to wrestle and I like everyone else, know how to wrestle. I'm a fighter though and I'm not out to wow you with technically sound moves or impress you with my ring knowledge. I'm out to show everyone in this world, and in this sport that I am the most dangerous rookie to come into this profession in a long time.
--- Reporter #1 ---- Sebastian, for the last question I'll make it sweet and simple. In just a few words, how would you describe yourself
--- "The Bounty Hunter" Sebastian Chase --- Eager to cause pain and sufferring, as I establish myself as the most dangerous man in this sport.
--- Sebastian Chase gets up and throws his water bottle at the one reporter he did not like as he exits the room and the press conference ends. --
