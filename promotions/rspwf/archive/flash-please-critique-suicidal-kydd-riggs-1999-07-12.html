[ FLASH ] Please Critique [ "Suicidal" Kydd Riggs ]

12 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, here is an RP I did for the NcWa. Please give me some help, and some tips. I am trying really hard to become a better RPer, and I need some ideas, and ways to get better. So please critique professionally.

You see men like Carnage, Jimmy Davidson, Midnight Killer, Kool Kid and others getting hit over the head with chairs. Shot after shot, after shot. Until the screen goes blank. Then it slowly fades back in, and there are words across the bottom, which read 'The most extreme match in NcWa history.' You see three men standing in the ring, surrounded by a cage, with electrical wire wrapped around it. The men are Freak of Nature, War Spirit, and The Reaper. Suddenly clips of that match start flashing. Men flying everywhere. Chairs, chains, men getting electrocuted. And again the scene goes blank. A voice is the heard...]
[ Voice ] "Is that all that the NcWa has to offer? Is that there limit? This is extreme." [ Promo scene ] [...The scene fades back into more clips. This time from different organizations, and of different people. "Suicidal" Kydd Riggs is one of these men. You watch him getting suplexed thru a glass cage, and body slammed thru the ring. It then shows clips of him stand alone in the middle of the ring, blood pouring from an open cut on his forehead. And another of him laying on the ground, with a bloodstained title around his waste. The scene again fades out to the crowds cheers...]
[ Voice ] "Is that intense enough for you, Fury?" (¯`·.¸.·­» End ProMo «­·.¸.·´¯) [...Fades in...] [ Scene ] [...The locker rooms. It's dim and quiet now, because all have left the arena. Outside you hear a bit of a drizzle, which slowly gets heavier.
Inside, all but one light is on, and it is at the end of a long, dark, cold, hallway. Which is completely empty. The room is secluded. Put there to purposely be away from everything, and everyone. This is the locker room of
"Suicidal" Kydd Riggs. Inside Riggs sits alone at a table, wearing nothing more than a pair of cut off, black shorts. He is leaning up on his hands, and his shoulder lengths black hair hangs over his face. Very slowly and with a deep breath, he leans back into the chair, and begins to speak...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Ya' know, I've learned a lot in my first four days here in the NcWa. First off, don't listen to what anyone says. Because no one here really knows what they are talking about. I heard that the NcWa was the place of the stars.
Only the greats can make it in the NcWa. But that's not all true is it? [ pauses ] Yesterday I saw what the NcWa is really all about. And that is games and jokes. Everyone is always messing around, and no one takes this seriously inside or outside of the ring. Was that the intensity of the NcWa Fury has spoken about? Is that what makes the NcWa tick? If that is what Fury was talking about, then I see that this is useless, and will get me no where, and will help my career very little, if that at all. I have been in small promotions, and unnamed indy organizations that are better run, and more serious than this. I am tempted to return to Woodstock, and continue my career there. _BUT..._ I am not going to. Oh no. Not after the things that have been said here. I have to show you all what wrestling is all about, and what it's like to know a real star."
[ Scene ] [...Riggs flips his hair back, and looks up at the camera...] [ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "And so it would seem, as of right now I have no friends. No allies. None what so ever. Well, one or two people. But that's fine with me. That's the way it's been my whole life. So I can easily deal with that. The thing is, how fast you all grew a dislike for me. It seems as if you are all intimidated by what you see. Jealous even. Although your egos are all to big to admit such a thing. See, each and every one of you think you are god. Like you each have no flaws. As if none of you ever lose, or never will lose. But what's the case when you do lose? Now I know you are going to turn it around on me, and say I said the same thing. But I can take a lose, and admit I was not the better man that day. See, what I have is confidence, you all have egos. There is a difference. Fury, your case is the worst one I have seen yet. You know you are going to beat me? Yea, right. [ chuckles ] You knew that the NcWa was to good for me, and that it was intense to, right? I guess yesterday everyone here proved you wrong, didn't they? You are all nothing more than children, playing a game of let's pretend I am the greatest. Well, you're about to get a reality check. You're not the greatest, none of you. See, no matter what you do, or where you go, there will always be someone better than you. _ALWAYS!_"
[ Scene ] [...Riggs shakes his head in disgust. He puts his legs up on the table and then crosses them, as if he were home. He gets a strange smile over his face, as he continues talking...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "But don't take my word for it. You'll all find out for yourselves. Sooner or later. Fury, you'll be the first to be taught a lesson. See, one thing I learned out there on the streets, was to never prejudge a person, which you have already done with me. And although I see you have a rather large ego, as well as a little brain, I will still not take you lightly. I still expect much from you, although I feel I will get very little. But that's fine. You do whatever it is you see fit. And I will do that as well. [ pauses ] Oh, and seeing as how everyone here in the NcWa dislikes me, and I am such an outcast,
I have a feeling people are going to be out for me. So, I've come up with a plan to stop that. So Fury, if you think that's your way out, you are dead wrong."
[ Scene ] [...Riggs stands up, and walk toward the camera. He takes his hand and pushes the camera man back out the doorway...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Good-bye now." [ Scene ] [...He smiles and then slams the door shut. It echo's throughout the empty arena. You hear some chairs move inside his locker room, then all is quiet...]
[...Fades out...]
