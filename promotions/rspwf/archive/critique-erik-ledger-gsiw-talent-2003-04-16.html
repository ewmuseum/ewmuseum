[Critique]Erik Ledger - GSIW talent

16 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Seriously, no one posted jack last time. The character's new, and I could use the feedback.
=== [A voice pierces the darkness.] "…and that's the run-down for the first edition of GSIW Live, airing
Thursday Night on local networks all across the Tri-State area."
[The voice belongs to Slammer, Disk Jockey of the Wrestle War Radio
Show. And with that, we fade up.] Slammer – "Now, coming into the studio now is a kid who's making his break into the business with the Jersey based GSIW, and he happens to be
one-third of the Main Event for this week's show. Ladies and Gentlemen, please welcome Erik Ledger to the show." [Canned applause is piped through the audio system as the Trenton native makes his way into the cramped studio and takes a seat across from
"The
Slammer" at the table with all of the equipment you need to put on a radio show. Erik is handed a headset from Slammer's producer, and the overweight
DJ adds a bit more filler.] Slammer – "Now, I made it out to Atlantic City for last week's house show, and I've got to say, the tag match that Erik and "Gang Related"
Vincent Green had against The Broncos was easily the highlight of the night! Especially with the aerial skills that Erik was showing off!" [Slammer is just what you'd expect of a wrestling radio show host. His hair is thinning, his waist is expanding, and he's got a face that was absolutely
_made_ for radio.] Erik – [embarrassed] "Heh, thanks." Slammer – "Speak up, kid, the World is listening! Well, at least the
North East." [Erik shifts uncomfortably in his chair.] Erik – "Sorry, I'm not used to this radio thing." Slammer – "Right, my bad." Erik – "Don't worry about it, let's roll with this." Slammer – "Alright then! Well, first thing's first, what do you think of GSIW?" Erik – "It's my dream come true, so of course I'm stoked about it! There's a few weirdoes running around, but I guess that's anywhere…" Slammer – "Weirdoes?" Erik – "Well, take this Cyn guy for example." [The Slammer nods.] Erik – "He goes on about how he's going to win this match based solely on his crappy life. He's got this whole ‘my life sucks so I must be hardcore'
thing going on, and it's just weird." Slammer – "So, you think otherwise?" Erik – "Honestly, I could care less about his past, or his pothead friends! None of that garbage matters Thursday night, what matters is who's gonna be able to take all of the punishment, and get back up and ask for more!"
Slammer – "I'll agree with you there!" Erik – "And this other guy, I think his name is Demonstone, I mean, what's the deal with that?"
[The pudgy host cracks up in laughter.] Slammer [catching his breath] – "So, back to the ‘weirdoes' of the
GSIW! I'm
interested in this!"
Erik – "Well, you've got this one guy calling himself the ‘One Man
Restraining Order,' and I'd just love to ask him what the heck that even means! I mean, it doesn't even make sense!"
[This breaks the Slammer up again. With a signal from the producer, some canned theme music starts up and Slammer segues into break.]
Slammer [between laughs] – "We've gotta take a break! But we'll be back for one more segment with GSIW's Erik Ledger!
[And with that, we fade out, rest assured that the next segment is filled with more of the same, Erik cracking jokes, and Slammer laughing like an idiot.]
