[ RP CRITIQUE] "Magnificent" Mike Douglas

3 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, This is a recent RP I did for the GSWP. I'm just looking for some feedback and what people think of it. Here it is.....
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [The scene opens up to the home of "Magnificent" Mike Douglas in Tampa, Florida. The camera opens up inside his house in his living room. The living room is decorated very nice. A big screen TV, A giant leather couch which Mike sits on and pictures and trophys all around the room. The camera pans around to see framed pictures of him on magazine covers, Title belts under glass cases and awards won by him. The camera then pans over to Mike's Black leather couch.
Their sits Mike Douglas. A very muscular and good looking man. His long brown hair pulled back into a pony tail goes very nice with his tan colored skin.
Mike is wearing a pair of baige Khaki pants with a black sleeveless shirt. Mike sits their hunched over holding an Icepack on the back of his head. After a few moments Mike notices the camera and begins to speak......]
DOUGLAS: Hmmm......Last week, GSWP Impact was supposed to be a night to remember. It was supposed to be the night when Mike Douglas made his
"Magnificent" comeback and become _YOUR_ television champion. Well, It what supposed to be but things didn't happen that way.
[He takes the Icepack off his head and throws it onto the ground, He then continues.....] DOUGLAS: The world was supposed to witness the return of the greatest wrestler as well as the greatest looking wrestler in the sport. But thanks to some young nobody trying to make a name for himself they didn't. Adam Morley, Who do you think you are? Do you think you can just lay your hands on my pretty face and get away with it. Well, That proves how stupid you really are.
[He pauses] DOUGLAS: Adam, You could have been somebody. You could have rose to the top of this federation. Yes, You heard me right, I said _COULD_. Because now, You career is only going to go downhill. Your first mistake was ever even laying a hand on me, Your second is signing the dotted line and stepping into the ring with me next week.
[He pauses] DOUGLAS: Adam, You and every other superstar have to wake up to reality. And your reality is that no matter what you try, No matter how hard you try, Mike
Douglas will always have the last laugh. So I have 1 loss in GSWP. Let me tell you this, That will not only be my _FIRST_ loss in the GSWP but it will be my
_LAST_ loss is GSWP. And that include this week. I face Adam Morley for the third time in my GSWP career. The first match, I won. [sarcasticly] Gee, Who would have thought. The second match, By some _FLUKE_, Adam Morley scores a
_CHEAP_ victory over me. It won't happen again. This Sunday, It's the third and deciding match.
[He pauses] DOUGLAS: This match was supposed to happen at the last GSWP Pay-Per Veiw, But I had some personal matters to take care of, So I wasn't able to give Adam Morley the beating of his life. But _NOW_ Mike Douglas is back and _NOW_ I am ready to dominate the GSWP like I usually do everywhere else. And It starts with Adam
Morley. So I'm just going to warn you from now Adam. If you don't want to be beaten and humiliated at the hands of Mike Douglas, Stay away from the
Madhouse. Because once I see your sorry excuse for a body walk into the GSWP
Madhouse, You belong to me.
And thats just the way it is. [Mike sits back on his couch, Runs his finger through his hair and then continues.....] DOUGLAS: Enough about Adam Morley, I will take care of him ni time. Lets talk about the man who gave my my _FIRST_ and _LAST_ loss in GSWP, Chris Winters.
Chris, Is there something wrong with you? Were you dropped on your head as a child? You must be to do what you did to me on Impact. You knew once you heard my music cue that you had no chance in beating Mike Douglas. So what did you do? You called on your friend Adam Morley for a little help. Adam came out gots his shots in and then you hit a Freezer Burn onto a steel chair. Hmmm......Not a good move. A rather stupid one if I can say. And now just like your friend
Adam Morley, You will have to suffer the consequences. You think just because your the television champion, You can get away with robbery? Well, You can't.
Hmm....."Ice Storm" that kind of fits you Chris. Because from now on, Your hot streak turns cold. Undefeated, Not as long as Mike Douglas is around. Nobody is undefeated. Soon you'll find that out. Next time we meet and I may be no psycic but I can tell you it's gonna be soon. You will realize why, I'm Magnificent"
and your not. [He pauses] DOUGLAS: Chris Winters, You want competition? Look no further than the man who speaks before you know. You want to be challenged? Well, I have a little challenge for you. After I get done with Adam Morley on Impact this week. After
I dispose of him like yesterdays trash. I want another match with _YOU_. That's right Chris, A rematch with you. And you know what, Since your so scared of making this a match for the Television Title. We can even make it NON-TITLE.
Just as long as I get to show the world that your victory over me was never meant to be. And it will never turn out that way again.
[Mike takes a deep breath and then continues.....] DOUGLAS: Now for the GSWP in general. Back about a month ago when I first came into the GSWP, I dominated. Until some personal things came up which caused me to leave. Now I'm back and I expect to dominate the same way I did the last time that I was here. Because I have one goal here in the GSWP. And that goal is to be the top dog of the GSWP as _YOUR_ World Champion. I don't care if I have to beat every single superstar in the GSWP because I am the _ONLY_ superstar in the GSWP. I don't care if I have to win every single championship in the GSWP because it's not like I haven't done it before. No matter what I have to do, No matter what it takes, I will eventually become champion. And there is nobody who can stop me.
[he pauses] DOUGLAS: Eric Cooper, The president of GSWP. This is what I want you to do. I want you to take everyone of your so-called "Superstars" and I want you to put them against me. It doesn't matter who.
How about Dylan Ryder? How about Fang? How about James Pillow? And even how about Johhny Glamour? I want you to put everyone in front of me and I want you to sit back and watch me run through each and everyone of them like only I could. Do you know why it's that easy? Because I'm just that good.
[Mike stands up and then continues.....] DOUGLAS: So Adam Morley and Chris Winters, You are both marked men. First comes
Morley then comes Winters. All leading up to my way to the top of the GSWP. So to put this whole interveiw into just one sentence, there is only one that comes to mind. You can think your Good, You can think your Great, But.....You will never be.....
MAGNIFICENT!!!!!! [End of scene] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [Chris] [Handler of:] ["Magnificent" Mike Douglas] [IWF/WOW] [11-9-1] [GSWP] [0-1-0] *Rookie of the Year Canidate
