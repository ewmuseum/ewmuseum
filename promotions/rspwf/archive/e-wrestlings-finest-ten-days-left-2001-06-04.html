Ten Days left...

For you suckas to vote.. we already have 40+ for the opening round, and if you want yours favorites to win, then fill out the damn ballots.
I. Intro II. Format III. The Voting Process IV. The Categories V. The Participants VI. The Deadlines I. INTRO Hola. This is [name withheld], longtime e-wrestling handler who decided to call it quits for a while some time last year, and as been lurking around ever since then. As you may or may not know I once handled V.D. Vitrone, the most adored character in the history of e-Wrestling. Yeah whatever, anyways, I was looking through the old archive of e-wrestling cards on my hard drive (which totals over 300 MBs) and I thought to myself,
"Wow, there really were some great characters out there."
Well I was drunk, so I doubt it made that much sense, but I'm sure I said something along those lines.
To make a long story short, I thought it would be really cool to see who all the handlers and fedheads thought that the greatest character of all time was.
The criteria is simple: vote for who you like. Whether it be you like the character's name , gimmick, enjoy their roleplays, whatever floats your boat.
The purpose of this poll is to get a general consensus of who is "Mr. Popular" so to speak. This little tournament that I've thrown together is called E-Wrestling's Finest, and has gotten its name, but its general purpose is to find out exactly who is the best in the eyes of the fans -- whether you, the voters, be fedheads, handlers, or just RSPWF trolls. All votes are equal in this case.
As for determining the participants, I would like to think that I have a semi-wide range of e-wrestling knowledge, and well, on a bored Sunday night, in #New_Chat, I was able to get some help from [name withheld] and [name withheld] in filling the categories. Good little servants, they are. Anyways.. here's how it goes. II. FORMAT With help from others, I've come up with who I feel to be 64 of the greatest characters in e-wrestling's history. From these 64, they were divided into eight categories, based primarily by gimmick type.
Your job, as voters, is to determine which one of these 64 is truly the best. I tried to make some interesting categories, and I think I did a good job, in that any given person could garner enough votes to win their respective categories.
III. THE VOTING PROCESS How the votes are going to be calculated is quite simple. THE FIRST ROUND (QUALIFYING ROUND): The voters vote for one person from each category which, for you mathematically challenged, comes down to eight people.
The amount of votes each participants receives will then be calculated and the TOP TWO wrestlers from each category will move on to a 16 MAN TOURNAMENT (KoTR style). THE SECOND ROUND: All matchups for the Tournament shall be randomly selected by yours truly, with each participant being given a number between 1-16, and then numbers being drawn from a hat..
Blah blah blah, it comes down to 8 matches, where you, the voter, have a chance to determine the winner by mailing in your Round 2 ballots.
THE THIRD ROUND: The 8 winners move on to the quarterfinals. Again, the winners are determined by the same format of the second round.
THE FOURTH ROUND: The 4 winners move on to the semi-finals. Same format as the second round.
THE FINAL ROUND: The 2 winners face off in a one-on-one for the E-Wrestling's Finest Reader's Tournament Championship. ALL VOTES ARE TO BE SENT TO: [detail withheld] WITH THE SUBJECT: [EWF] VOTES IV. THE CATEGORIES~! Here's a brief description of what each category means. BADASS - Those scary bastards who's sole purpose is to beat people up. They never run from away from a fight and foam at the thought of a bloodbath.
FOREIGN: Your typical wrestling immigrant. They're of a culture other than American and have come here to spread their evil ways. BOY SCOUTS: Those who seem to always have the fans backing them, no matter what they do. They have an honor code and are sportsman, rather getting things done the clean way.
ANTAGONIST: See V.D. Vitrone. Seriously, these are the guys whom you hate, whether they're sneaky, evil, or just downright annoying. Whatever the case, these are the guys who draw the major heel heat and are always looking to cheat their way out of things.
JUNIOR-HEAVYWEIGHTS: These are the guys who put on the sleeper matches of the cards, and sometimes don't get as much respect as their heavyweight counterparts. But JHWs make up some the more interesting characters in e-Wrestling and often move on to make their mark in the HW division as well.
VILLAIN: SUPAH-HEEL~! These guys are kind of a cross between a Badass and an
Antagonist. They're the big, nasty bastards that steal candy from babies, and rob old ladies. Move over Saddam, these are some bad men.
ALL-ROUNDERS: They can play the face. They can play the heel. These are the versatile guys who sort of go with the flow and select their alignment depending on the given feud.
LEGENDS: Come on. Just look at the members of this category, and you tell me what it means.
