[Critique] Eric Forbes

6 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm currently in WAO, and IWF/WOW as Andrew Belar but I'm having a hard time roleplaying for him, and I'm considering returning to my original character. I wrote this flash fast, because it always seems to be easy for me to flash with
Eric and his manager, so if you could take a look at it I'd appreciate it.
Note: The reason the wrestler that they are referring to is not named, is because there is no wrestler. This is a sample flash I am looking for feedback on.
[Fade in. Eric Forbes and his manager Chris Stevens are standing outside of a
Super Wal-Mart, next to a set of red carts, used for collecting clothing and such.]
EF: "So, Chris you see what I mean?" CS: "Naw. We, well you, don't have a chance. Whether you say you're going to beat him down with a rubber ducky or not, I don't think it'll phase him. From what he's said he's been scouting us, and he knows all about the rubber ducky trick."
EF: "Non-sense! That rubber ducky trick always works, and if it doesn't I'll just be forced to do some actual wrestling."
CS: "I dunno Eric, that guy looks pretty strong. You may be in shape for a cruiser-weight, but you're no 300 pounds." EF: "300 pounds, 400 pounds, no matter. With the rubber ducky, and my ability to speak latin, well what can stop me? He certainly won't, he can scout me all he wants, but he'll never know that I can speak spanish."
CS: "You don't speak spanish." EF: "How do ya know? What if I do and I just didn't tell you? Ever think of that one? Yeah, maybe I don't speak spanish, but maybe I do. Nah-nah-nah!"
CS: "Anyway Eric, like I've been saying, you need to get your head in the game. Spanish, Latin, Rubber Ducky's, there not going to save you from him." EF: "Eh, ya never know. He may have a phobia of spanish speaking, latin rubber duckys." CS: "Just keep hoping.." [Eric grabs one of the carts and heads towards the doors of the Super Wal-Mart. His manager, Chris Stevens follows him as the scene fades away.]
