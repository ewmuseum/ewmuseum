Roleplay to Critique

1 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens, inside a dark and glommy basement type room. There are brick walls surrounding the musty place, with several pillars holding up the ceiling. Spread across the floor, are several blue mats, wrestling mats used for technical wrestling practice. On the bricked wall, hang posters of great legends, including Darrin Macey, Bret Hart, Owen Hart, and even one of Brian Pillman is seen to the side. A litten up staircase, is shown to the side, where seemingly a dark figure sits...]
  [As the camera man grows closer to the figure, this man is seemingly noticable, and known to the WWA already. He's wearing a hooded sweatshirt, and a pair of blue jeans, followed by some black boots. His waivey brown/blonde hair is a little damp, hanging over his ears. His clean shaven face, and baby blue eyes, set's off his hair, as he stares into the camera. His sweatshirt reading, "Technical Survivor", as he muscles his hands together around in cirlces.]
  [As the scene centers on him, he continues to look into the camera, and then begins to speak...]
  TOMMY BROOKS: VV...VV...A....a forsaken place of unsanctioned animals, with nothing better to do then crack some other animal's skull wide open, pick up their pay check, and invest in some American Smut for the weekend...a place where the traditional name of wrestling has gone to the toilet, and has been flushed repeadatly week after week...a place where the fans, not only promote, but keep these terrible actions to their heart...the
WWA... a place, where at Riot House...Unsanctioned...the most up and coming superstar in professional wrestling to date, the most feared technical wrestler in the World...."Traditional" Tommy Brooks...is going to show, not only Mike Angel, and Michael Moore...but the entire WWA, and the World...that
TRADITION is still in the sport, and is still effective today, as a winning strategy....I dont need a chair...I dont need a hammer...I dont need an
"Unsanctioned" match, to show off my skills...All I need is an opponent, and the oppurtunity to beat down the professional dissapointment, and you will see what being the MAIN DRAW is all about....Being that me, drawing millions and millions of fans, among ratings, is nothing new...
  [Tommy Brooks, now get's up, and begins to walk across the floor...]   [He continues to stare straight center at the camera...]   TOMMY BROOKS: When I came here, to the VV VV A....I knew what went on. The bloody matches....the barbed wire....the raking chair shots....but that didn't phase me. Because my goals were to strictly set the mood of this federation totally different, to turn this place into a dominant dungeon of technical wrestling. And that, to this day, is STILL what I plan on doing. I'll beat the
TRADITION out of Angel and Moore if I have to...I will beat it out of every professional athlete in that lockeroom as we speak...And if anyone obliges, please, just leave me, or the guys upstairs a note, and I'd be more then glad, to settle things out with you. Understood?
  [A small smirk sneaks up on Tommy's face, as he continues to pace...looking around, heading again for the staircase...]
  TOMMY BROOKS: So many legends have come through this sport, in such a small amount of time. And what your seeing right now...is thee NEXT legend of this plagued sport. TOMMY BROOKS....a man of TRADITION...a man of TECHNICAL ethics...a man...of the DUNGEON. "TRADITIONAL" TOMMY BROOKS, is, and will be thee number one man in the sport today, no question about it. And the
WWA...has the divine opportunity of sponsoring the birth of me, the biggest draw in wrestling....Out of all this happening, the fame, the gold, the money, oh and the fans....one thing will never change....
   [The camera now zooms in on a straight edged face of Tommy Brooks...]                           _T R A D I T I O N_                                                                 _W I L L  A L W A Y S_                                                                                                                           _!!! R U L E !!!_   [Tommy Brooks now heads up the stairs, his feet clamping against each metallic slab. As he hits the last step, the sound of a light switch is heard, as the basement lights flicker, then close to darkness, leaving the camera useless to it's compatability. The scene closes...]
