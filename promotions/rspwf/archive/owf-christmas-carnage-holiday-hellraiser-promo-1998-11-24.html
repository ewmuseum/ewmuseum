[OWF Christmas Carnage: Holiday Hellraiser] Promo

24 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Your screen is pitch-black. Nothing can be seen; nothing can be heard. Suddenly a spine-chilling voice-over is heard; the jet-blackness remains.] VO: There comes that time a year when the family comes together. [The screen then becomes verdant, slowly fading-in on a heartwarming Christmas scene. There are two parents and two little boys. The two boys are opening up their presents, while the mother and father sit on the couch, smiling as the kids become excited with glee. The voice- over continues.] VO: There comes that time a year when all your problems are put aside, and peace reigns supreme. [One of the little boys becomes exuberant, as he has just realized he's been bought a new Power Ranger toy. The little boy rushes his mother, giving her a hug and a kiss.] VO: There comes that time a year when bad turns to good. [The voice-over then laughs in a goosebump-raising fashion. We then switch scenes to another location: one mother and three children--all of them considerably different in age and skin tone. One of the children, a dirty-looking teen, reaches under the skimpy tree, picking out a coupon for a free Personal Pan Pizza at Roscoe's Pizza. A scowl appears on the boy's face. The boy then gets up and rushes the cigarette-smoking mother, bringing her down to the ground with a double-legged tackle, otherwise known as a spear.] VO: But _this_ year is going to be different. _This_ year, violence will reign supreme. [New scene: a shot of "The Disciple" Eric Wayne hitting "Mr. Charisma" Christian York with the Teardrop Powerbomb is seen. That quickly turns into a shot of "The Onslaught" Jakob Andrews cocking back to nail Nick "The Bulldog" Demola with the chair... Into a shot of "The Real Paramount Picture" Tyrone Parker sunset flipping off the top rope with a chair, but only to be drop kicked by "Sudden Death" Shane Matthews.] VO: This year, there's going to be destruction at its fullest extent. [All shots fade-out, leaving a faint OWF logo seen on the center of the screen, set ablaze on the blackened background.] VO: The question is, Will you be there to see it?
