USCW Flash Central 5/8/98

11 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jacob Yoder Viper The Bishop "The Marauder" Chris Evans Abyss Daniel Stephens Daniel Dare w/Bullet Sinister -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- **Jacob Yoder is sitting in the horse stable that use to be Saul's. There is only lantern light in this dark barn. So you cannot see most of Jacob's face. The part of his face that you do see looks haggard and depressed. ** Jacob: Oh, what fun times did we have, Saul. Till that evil man known as Abyss took yer life. The pain that I do suffer is one that I cannot ever get rid of.
Abyss! Thou hast taken my best friends life, and have stolen my happiness. I have not forgotten. I will takist that which thou hold so dearly. The coveted
TV title. I will make thou pay for what ye have done. Makest no mistake about it. The Butter Churner will finally be yours for the experience.
**Jacob begins to cry as the camera fades out. ** -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Camera fades in on Viper sitting on a bench outside of the Spectrum in Philadelphia, around Viper people are crowding in tryin to hear what he's going to say to the camera... Strangely, no one asks him for any autographs, nor does anyone speak to him... Viper is dressed in a Yankees jacket, a Yankees baseball cap, Black jeans and his usual SnakeSkin boots... On the bench next to him are the USCW World HeavyWeight Title, and the UWF Unified Title... Viper looks around at all of the people and grins...)
Viper: Ya know, seems like EVERYONE wants to talk about The Snake like I'm a "Hated Heel" Yet look, EVERYWHERE I go, people crowd around me and want to touch or talk to Da Showstopa... I see these interviews with Spicey, with Marauder, and with others, and everyone boos when they say my name...
Heh, guys, it MIGHT make ya feel good to hear that, but the fact is, those
SAME people who are booing while you're standing there... THEY are the ones buying all the Viper and What the Fuck merchandise...
Viper: Look around ya USCW, Viper is THE MOST popular and prolific champion this fed has EVER seen... But Spicey wants me gone... Spicey, you DO realize that without ME as yer fuckin champion, yer merchandise sales will go down(hehe, unless people REALLY like the GDDC shirts)... Yer show attendance will go down... Yer RECOGNITION will go down... Robby boy, I AM the USCW right now, without me, yer just ANOTHER fed... Remember that durin yer next "plot" to take me down... Spicey, you WILL be crushed by me, sooner or later, all yer little "surprises", "tricks", and
"Defenses" will be stripped away, and you'll ber left standing face to face with yer greatest fuckin fear....
Viper: Now, Shining Path... You DO have a WHOLE lotta ego dontcha?? YOU will be the next USCW champion?? Please, you'll be the next VICTIM OF the
USCW champion maybe, thatz about ALL you can claim fer our title bout...
Damn boy, USCW's been re-activated for all of what, a month... 2 maybe, and YOU are already burnt out and gettin stale??? Thatz a pretty pathetic track record boyo... And Against ME, it sure as hell won't cut it... So unless you decide you still want wrestling to be a CAREER for ya, instead of just one long, job... Maybe you should think about yer efforts around here, and the chance that Spicey has given ya...
Viper: Now, Marauder, ya know, I was HOPING it wouldn't come to this, but since YOU think throwin cheap shots around is cool, guess I will too...
You say that when I fight YOU I'll be fighting someone of MY caliber??? HAHAHA, there is ONE other man in this fed who is MY caliber, and itz NOT you... Boyo, yer so proud of yer little win in the Battle Bowl, that yer ego's overridden yer common sense... What you did at that battle bowl is prove yerself King of the Mid Carders... EVERYONE in that Battle
Royal(With the exception of Sin, who got thrown out by luck and surprise) was just another piece of flotsam in the USCW... Viper: So what you DID Marauder, was show yer better than the jobbers and
Mid-Carders around here... I've done THAT time and again... Yea, you proved yerself ready for "main-event" Status, unfortunately for YOU...
That proving ground is against the USCW HeavyWeight Champion of the World... WHich is, of course, ME!!! Now Marauder, I don't wanna turn this match in to a personal hatred/feud... I have enuff of that with Nut grabber... But I think you had better realize yer place in the scheme of things, and quickly... In other words, heh "KNOW YOUR ROLE!!!!" Damn, I been wanting to say that to someone fer a long time...
Viper: Anyhow Marauder, today, tomorrow, next month... WHENEVER our match is, you'll be facing a TRUE "Superstar", not some wanna-be like Storm, not some loser like Danny Dare... Nope, you'll be fighting the EPITOME of
"Superstardom"... Damn, did that sound as pompous to you guys as it did to me??? Ahh well, anyhow Marauder, we'll see each other in the ring REAL soon... Don't rush it, ya WON'T like what happens when it finally DOES occur...
Viper: Now, NO rant would be complete without mentioning everyone's favorite Ball Hugger... Danny Boy, Howz life boyo??? Ya seem to have gotten scared... Didja run fer yer fuckin life already??? I know what it is... I've seen this all before, itz the Nut Grabber syndrome.. See Danny doesn't like being embarassed, so when the truth comes out, and he's made to look like a fuckin idiot... He keeps quiet fer a while HOPING people will forget whatz been said about him...
Viper: Well, I'm here to make SURE no one forgets Danny Boy... EVERYONE will know what a fraud and a loser you are... Hell even yer "Buddy" Spicey will know by the time I'm done with you... Yer a little worm on a BIG fuckin hook(tm) Danny Boy, and yer ABOUT to get thrown to the fuckin fish!!
You revolve yer wrestling career around making "Friends" with the presidents of the companies you work for, kissin their asses, and TRYING to analyze people's interviews backstage... HOPING that yer president "Buddy"
will notice and realize that yer right... Viper: Well guess what Nut Boy, yer just another victim, a victim of what
I like to call... The ROB STOCK DISEASE!!! See Most(Not all), but Most of the BIG names in the BWFE, came from Rob Stock's habit of taking losers who he LIKES personally, and making them in to HUGE stars... Then, when they go somewhere else, where Stock can't protect them... They revert to TRUE form... Loserdom... And thatz what YOU did in the UWF, and thatz what you'll do here Buddy Squirrel... You made the worst mistake of yer life when you showed up here Danny Boy...
Viper: And I, SURE AS HELL, am gonna make sure you regret it... See Danny, YOU made all of this personal, so I will be mroe than happy to finish it... This has been in the making for a year, and when we do finally have our match(and all it'll take is one)... Yer gonna feel ALL of the rage, all of the hatred, and all of the poison as itz unleashed on yer sorry ass... Ya know Danny Boy, in SOME ways you actually are right... In
SOME ways you DID make me...
Viper: See, I was a pretty nice guy in the UWF... I helped people out, I was an upstanding, loyal champion... THEN, Danny Dare did what he did, and now ya see what ya have before ya... So yea Danny, maybe what you did, DID help me a little bit... Made me MUCH more fearless and MUCH more ruthless... But believe me when I tell you ONE thing... What you did was a SMALL part of Viper's success, a VERY small part, don't ever overestimate yerself in THAT respect...
Viper: Anyhow, I look around the roster TRYING to find some good contenders for this title, and I see ONE, who's ranked enuff to actually contend... So Yoder, whnever you want the rematch Amish Boy, I'll be HAPPY to show ya why it is I'm champion... and YOU aren't!!!
'Nuff Said -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- From the Church of the Holy Alliance: Bishop: Sin man, are you ready to pay for your sins? Don't fool yourself, you will pay, and you will pay my way. You will be crusified.
The USCW magazine asked who should be prayed for. You or me. The answer is neither of us. I Sinister am the Bishop, I need no one to pray for me. You Sinister, are unworthy of being prayed for at all. You are a worthless sinner just like everyone else in this federation. I will make you confess and do my pennance, and then you will be
CRUSIFIED!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ( Scene is in " The Marauder " Chris Evans hotel, just outside of where Weekend Edition is going to be held. People are yelling outside his window for an autograph. He is by himself, and is wearing
A Marauders cap, the new Marauders t-shirt, and his Battle Bowl Ring. ) Chris : ( Sees Cameraman ) Hey, how'd you get in here? Oh well, might as well make the best of it.
Chris : You know what makes me mad? I'm not sure if I'm going to face Max this Weekend Edition. The Magazine said he was injured or something, but I still wanna inflict some pain on him. For some STUPID reason, Mr. Spicer won't let any information out. I've been waiting some time to face you " Sir " Maxwell ; our paths will cross someday once again.
Chris : So I guess I can take out any frustration I have on Storm. Ya know, Storm. I don't know what you're talking about when you say I have a " big mouth ". As the world can tell by whatever you say on TV, you're the one with the motor mouth. But yapping ain't gonna win matches ; it's the hard work, training, and dedication that's gonna win.
And, as far as I can see, all you're doing is running your big mouth at the media, saying how you're gonna beat me. Well, this ( screen shows
Chris lifting his Battle Bowl Ring ) is no fluke. And it isn't gonna be a fluke when I beat you 1, 2, 3 in front of all those people.
Cameraman : Mr. Evans, can I ask... Chris : I've had enough interviews for today. Talk to me later. I got to train now. ( Picks up gym bag, puts on his shades. ) I'll talk to ya later people. ( Pushes cameraman out the door, then walks out himself ). Here we go again....
( FADE TO BLACK ) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <Abyss is seen walking down a long dirt road. A large sign in front of him says
"Idahoin Amish Convention". The camera fallows Abyss as he makes his way down the road and you can now see that Abyss is carrying a large satchel in front of him.>
Abyss: Yoder, you said not too long ago that a new age of Amish is coming. An age that doesn't give in to the English. You know what I think, pal? GREAT!
Yessirie, I'm all about taking out a WHOLE generation if need be! Abyss: I didn't know you punks were like Star Trek freaks and had conventions. <Abyss enters a large red barn. Hay bells are seen everywhere being used as seats and the livestock still accompanies the people. Abyss stacks 2 hay bells up an ascends them. The crowd goes quiet and focuses on Abyss>
Abyss: I bring modern tools for all! From pens to TV's come and get it! <The crowd goes back to chatting and Abyss looks angry> Abyss: Dumb pricks. <Abyss reaches in the satchel and pulls out a handful of pens and mechanicle pencils. He throws them one by one at the Amish folk who cawer in fear.>
Abyss: Hahahaha! See Yoder. You Amish folk are nothing short of push-overs. It's time I put you in your place again, but this time, you will stay there! Hades Bomb's galore. Last time you tried to surprise me with that poor helpless horse, Saul. This time I bet you're gonna bring out your new tag partner. I'll use Uncle Zeke against you too! It's amazing Yoder, you lose to me, then to
Vipe, and you'll lose to me again, but you don't stop. That's one if not your only GOOD quality.
<Abyss takes a serious tone to his voice> Abyss: Say your prayers Jake, you'll want to be ready for judgement when I'm through with you this time.

Bullet: No Dare, ya see that's where you are wrong, I have been USCW Heavyweight Champ, and that's something you will NEVER be Dare. That is something which I can promise...So just sit there and answer my questions!!
DARE: I don't need your lip, mother fucker. Since I probably have some time to wait before I get to the snake man, how about ME AND YOU MEET AT

I am looking very forward to teaching your power mad ass a lesson in RESPECT,
Daniel Dare style. Bullet: Now you're just pushing the limits little man, I HAVE THE POWER, I can make things happen you just watch things happen...Everybody knows I'm the man, and always will be, we won't play by YOUR rules we'll play by OUR rules!! Got that little man??
