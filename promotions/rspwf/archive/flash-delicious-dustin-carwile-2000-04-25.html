[Flash] "Delicious" Dustin Carwile

25 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a flash that I just did a few minutes ago for GSWP. I haven't posted in a long time. And some guys don't know about the change of Dusty Love to
Dustin Carwile. Critique if you please. :) But here goes nothin'...
[The camera opens up from darkness. The first thing we see is a pair of pearly white, smiling teeth. The owner of the set of teeth begins laughing, as the camera slowly pulls back into a wider shot. As the camera pulls away, we see the rest of the smiling face. The face is all too familiar to the fans around the world... and especially to the former GSWP World Heavyweight Champion,
Fang. It's the smiling face of the one and only...
..."Delicious" Dustin Carwile. Dustin Carwile sits in a chair in what appears to be a bathroom. Behind him, we see the contents of a bathroom. A Jacuzzi tub is seen directly behind "The
Delicious One." The tub is filled with bubbles, as the Jacuzzi is turned on full blast. Carwile continues to laugh, as he runs his hand through his long, shiny, brown hair. Some of his hair falls back down to the side of his face, as the rest stays back. Now, we notice that he is wearing a white, house robe.
It appears that he's about to take a dip in the Jacuzzi... After a few seconds, "Delicious" Dustin Carwile stops laughing... and just keeps the smirk on his face. Now, we see a head pop up from the bubbling water. It's a beautiful redhead. After the appearance of her head and face, we almost get a glimpse of a little more, but the bubbles make the save by covering the cleavage. After a giggle, the gorgeous redhead speaks in a loving voice...]
[???]: "I'm waaaiiitiiing..." [Carwile turns his eyes, but not his head, as if he's looking at the woman. Carwile replies, as he is still wearing his patented million dollar smile...] [DC]: "Patience, my dear. Just remember, good things come to those who wait. But I understand. I completely understand. If I were you, I wouldn't be able to wait to get a piece of the only man in the world that can be called... 'Delicious.'"
[The young lady pokes her bottom lip out, as if she's pouting, but in a teasing manner. Carwile just smiles, looks in the camera, and continues to speak...]
[DC]: "Now, down to a little business. And I do mean _little_ business." [Smirk...] [DC]: "Fang..." [Chuckles for a second...] [DC]: "...you were greeted, treated, and cheered for like a World Champion, Sunday night. But Fang, I took you out, Sunday night... like a World Champion. And there's one simple reason why... because I _am_ a World
Champion."
"Fang... _if_ you ever return to the ring again, I'll be waiting to whipe the mat with you one more time. But I _highly_ doubt you'll ever be the same again. I _highly_ doubt that you'll ever be _able_ to get back in the ring again. And last and definitely not least, I doubt you'll ever _wanna_ get back in the same ring with 'The Delicious One' again."
[Carwile combs his hand through his hair again, and continues...] [DC]: "Enough of Fang. He's history. Next up... Duke Putrid." [The grin turns. Carwile kinda snarls... then, turns back into a smile...] [DC]: "Duke... good work. You proved to the world that you were capable of winning the big one. You proved to the world that you're definitely World Champion material. But one thing you _didn't_ do... is prove to _me_ that you're World Champion material. When you beat _me_... then, and
_only then_, will you be World Champion material."
"You people just don't seem to get it. There were two people that made the
GSWP what it was before. _Two._ One's still here... and the other suffered a serious injury on Sunday night."
[Grin of conceit...] [DC]: "Duke, you beat me, _then_ you'll be World Champion. And I have a feeling that you'll be gettin' your chance very, very soon. Good luck... you'll need it."
"Now, one more thing before I get down to the _real_ business at hand." [Carwile turns his eyes again, but not his head, as if he's looking at the beauty behind him. With that said, we see the redhead in the background blowing bubbles off her hand. Carwile continues with a smile on his face...]
[DC]: "I'm booked in a 'double debut' match against... Rick Stevens?" [Carwile squints his eyebrows, as if he's confused...] [DC]: "Mr. Cooper, is this some sort of a joke? 'Double debut?' Now, I'm a _newcomer?_"
[Carwile laughs...] [DC]: "Eric, you can try to hold me down for as long as you want. But there's no stoppin' me this time around. I promise... no... I
_garuntee_ you one thing. I'm gonna mow down every opponent you throw at me
'til I reach the top. And if I have to, Eric...
...I'll mow you down in the process." [Carwile has a deceiving grin come across his face...] [DC]: "Now, sweetie... tell the people what me and milk have in common. And if you answer correctly, you'll get the most 'delicious' thing in the world..."
[Carwile grins, as the camera turns. Now, we see a shot of Dustin Carwile, face to face, as we now see that we were looking at Carwile through a mirror.
The young redhead smiles and answers...] [???]: "You both do _my_ body _goooood._" [Now, she puts her finger on her lip and smiles a sexy smile. Carwile stands up, takes his robe off... and before we can see any of the goods, the robs is thrown over the camera and all we hear is giggling. Now, we fade to black.]
