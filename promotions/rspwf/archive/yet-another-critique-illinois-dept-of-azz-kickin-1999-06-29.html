Yet another [Critique] Illinois Dept. of Azz-Kickin'

29 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, this is an RP for a tag team that me and my brother just started up. Chris Hurte is in it, and one my brothers many characters. This is a sample RP to get us in WWO (World Wrestling Organization), so I wanted to know if it was good enough. We might have made it too long I think. Anyway, it's just an information RP. If you want more info on the tag team, just ask.
(( The scene opens up on a normal afternoon at the World Wrestling Organization
Offices. The view is on the parking lot, where there are a variety of cars filling up half the lot. A black Ford F-150 (truck) drives down the road and swerves violently into the parking lot, with a man in the truck bed looking over the hood of the cabin and hanging on as the driver drives crazily. The camera zooms out to show most of the lot, and the black truck swerving in and out of the lanes, nearly hitting several automobiles and pedestrians who shout at the truck. The man in the bed shouts back, waving a closed fist. The truck turns into the lane lined up with the camera, and speeds right for the camera.
The camera man moves to the left and pivots, showing the truck bounce onto the curb and skid to a stop right in front of the tall glass doors of the WWO
Offices, the man in the bed barely hanging on. You can now hear "Born to be
Wild" blasting from the truck's speakers.
The music goes off as the engine goes off, and a door on the drivers side opens and slams closed. The now noticeably short but muscular man in the bed hops out the truck, wearing a Ghost Busters shirt and a pair of slightly baggy jeans, his long black hair slicked back in a pony tail. A pair of boots can be seen under the car, walking around towards the back of the truck. A man appears. He is a well built caucasian, wearing a black Chris Hurte t-shirt and slightly baggy jean shorts. He brushes his long blonde hair out of his blue eyes and face, and over his head. They begin to walk over in front of the camera man. ))
Chris Hurte: Most of you don't know me, but some of you probably do. My name is
Chris Hurte. And if you don't know me, you better get to know me, because you're gonna be seeing a lot more of me. And trust me it's gonna be hard getting to know me in the ring when I'm kicking your ass.
J.C. Hayden: Yeah, and I'm J.C. Hayden. I'm another guy none of you will know. I'll let my ring skills speak for themselves however, though I must say things don't look bright for the WWO and we are here to pick up the status of this federation by coming here and bringing our large following of indy fans along with us. So looks as if we are gonna be making the WWO some money!!
Chris Hurte: Yeah. And not only that, we're gonna give the Tag Team Title ranks a little help by taking them off of the hands of whoever the champs may be by the time we work our way up. But if it was up to me, we'd be fighting for the
Tag Team Titles at the very next card. Of course, it isn't, and nobody knows that we're better than Search and Destroy yet. They will though.
(( A flashlight cop walks into the background, looking at the truck in front of the door. He looks over to J.C. and Chris and calls to them. ))
Flashlight Cop: Hey, you guys! Is this your truck?! Chris: Yeah, why? F.C.: (( He begins to walk over to Chris and the camera, hand on flashlight and ready to illuminate if neccessary. )) This isn't a designated parking zone.
There's a bunch of open spaces. Get one o- Chris: ((sarcastically)) Oh, I don't mind staying there. It's fine where it is. (( He goes in his pocket, and produces keys. )) Hey, since it needs to be moved, why don't you put it in a designated parking zone for me? (( He tosses the keys to the cop, and he fumbles them and catches them. )) Thanks. We're going inside. We'll only be a second.
J.C.: Guess you ain't above the law after all Chris. CH:What did you say?? J.C.: Nothin..... (( Chris and J.C. walk off, with the cop putting the keys in his pocket and going to the truck to write a ticket. Chris opens the door, and they enter the lobby followed by the camera crew, who films the duo making their first steps of their future in wrestling. J.C. walks up to a man wearing a suit who is headed towards the door, and extends his hand. ))
J.C.: ((shaking hands)) Hello, my name is J.C. Hayden. I'll be kicking ass around here from now on.
Man: Oh, gee. That's nice. Chris: ((joining the conversation)) Yep, thats right. Any asses kicked or to be kicked are either because of us, or they have to go through us. Not only thatm but we're gonna get those Tag Titles too.
Man: Oh. Well, there's a lot of talent here. Especially with the tag teams. It's gonna be tough to accomplish your goals. Good luck. Chris: Thanks, but we don't need luck. (( The man walks past Chris, J.C., and the camera, and the two continue to walk around the lobby telling people what they told the man. They finally take a seat at a couch, and the camera sits across from them. The camera zooms in on
Chris stern face, and firery eyes. ))
Chris: Well, lets talk about us a little more. Me, I'm not a "bad ass," and most of those guys are sissies pretending they're tough anyway. I don't drink much beer. I'm not a little goody two shoes. I'm not the most hardcore guy ever, although I will draw your blood every now and again. I'm not a movie star, rock star, rap star, or any other star for that matter. I'm not a gangster, or any kind of so-called "tough guy." I'm not a pretty boy or a ladies man either. I'm married, so I can't be a ladies man. Not one of those guys that goes around, doing whatever he wants. I know my place in each fed I'm in. I don't wear masks and sparklers, and I don't have extravagant entrances to the ring. Hell, I don't even have theme music! So what am I?, you ask. Well, quite simply put, I'm a wrestler. All that other stuff isn't needed. I'm just a wrestler. And more importantly, an extension of my profession, I'm an ass kicker.
(( The camera then turns to J.C. Hayden. )) J.C.: Well, since everybody is making a speech then I guess I better make one too, before some guy comes up to us asking questions. But before I expound I gotta say, Chris, that was inspiring man. I don't agree with your opinions or whatever, but you said it so well.((wipes away a tear)) Anyway, looking on this roster, I don't see many _GOOD_ cruiserweights. Maybe that's because there is no divisioin for cruisers, but what the hay, I figure in such a high class fed, there'd be at least one or two to spark the crowd and add a little life to the show. But nobody is real exciting. So I decided to add the excitement to all the WWO cards and make the show an actually good and enjoyable one for all the fans out there who pay good money to see WWO shows. Now, despite Chris' laid back ways, I'm flamboyant, showy, and I'm standing for everything that is good and right about wrestling. So that's me, like it or love it.
(( A voice comes from behind the camera. )) Voice: So how'd you guys get teamed up? I mean, we know you're both from Illinois, and... Well, you tell us the story. (( The camera zooms out to show both wrestlers, who smile almost simultaneously. )) Chris: It's really kinda funny, but spooky. Ya see, we both go to this gym and wrestling school ran by a couple of brothers who wrestled before we were born.
Their names are Madd Joe and Bobby. Anyway, the old bastards have wrestling rings where you can fight freely or spar or whatever you want. And J.C. would go up there on Wednesdays and have open challenges.
J.C.: Yeah, and I would kick everyones ass too. That is until Chris came along. Then everything changed. He was the only one I couldn't take out like before. I actually had some trouble. And it was almost eerie too, because it was like we both knew eachothers moves, even though we never met before. Of course, I kicked his ass for about an hour and then they stopped the match.
Chris: But that wasn't it. Then we got to talkin, and we decided to tag up since we were practically "wrestling soulmates" and all. So we started tagging up and sparring, and we did pretty good.
J.C.: We beat just about everyone we faced. And with the help of Madd Joe and
Bobby, we got that much better.
(( The cop enters the lobby, and interrupts the conversation as they both look at him.)) Chris: Not this guy again. J.C.: Hey, it's your truck. (( Chris gets up, and walks over to him as J.C. relaxes on the couch. The cop has a peice of paper in his hand. ))
Chris: ((looking at the badge)) What now, Mike? Did you move my truck? Mike: No. I'm here to personally hand you your ticket for a parking violation. ((going in his pocket, and taking out the keys.)) And take your keys. You still gotta move your truck. ((handing Chris the keys and the ticket.))
Chris: ((taking the keys and ticket, and speaking sarcastically.)) No problem,
Officer Mike. I'll move right away. I hope you don't haul me off to jail, or flash me to death with that flashlight. You're real good at what you do too.
Hopefully one day you'll be a real pig. Good luck, and godspeed. Mike: Ha ha ha. Very funny. Just move the damn truck buddy. And it's Officer
Balboa to you. ((mumbling under his breath.)) Hot shot wrestlers think they can do anything they want.
Chris: Sure thing, Mike. (( Chris waits for Mike to walk out the door, and Mike exits. J.C. gets up and walks over next to Chris. Chris looks at the ticket and laughs. ))
J.C.: You're gonna pay that, aren't you? You look like you're not going to. Chris: Nope. I have no intentions of paying it. Everyone's not good like you, man. (( They then head towards the door, with Chris folding up the ticket and putting it in his back pocket. ))
Chris: Well, we're gonna get outta here. But before we go, and end this interview, we got somethin to say. We need to get noticed here by kicking someone's ass and doing some damage. J.C.?
J.C.: So, we've decided to challenge NEW GENERATION to a match. Anytime, anywhere. Chris? Chris: We've been looking at you guys, and we think you're the perfect team to challenge and fight. And you also need to get a chance to get some wins on that point board Search and Destroy have. So what do you say?
J.C.: Yeah. You want a match, or are you too afraid? I would fully understand, seeing as we're so intimidating and everything.
(( They reach the door, and open it and exit. They walk to Chris' truck, which is in the same place. Chris opens the driver side door, and J.C. prepares to hop into the bed. ))
Chris: If you're not too afraid let us know if you accept or not. If you don't, we can move on and fight someone else. It doesn't matter to me. You wanna know why? Because we're The Illinois Dept. of Azz-Kickin', and we're kickin' ass,
{BLEEP} the names!
(( He hops into the truck and starts it up, as J.C. hops into the bed. "Born to be Wild" begins to play again, as Chris pulls off and drops off the curb and
J.C. pounds on the roof of the cab. They're driving out the parking lot as a balled up peice of paper exits the driver side window. The truck speeds off as the screen fades out. ))
