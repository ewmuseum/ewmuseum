GOOD ROLEPLAYER LOOKING FOR ANOTHER DECENT FED - SAMPLE RP

13 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a little something I wrote for the Worlds Only Legitimate Federation
[ http://center.simplenet.com/wolf/ ] [Scene fades in on the charred, broken chassis of an old car. From the surrounding it would appear we are in a junkyard. The sound of heavy machinery lingers in the background. The camera zooms in on the old car. In the back seat, leaning back on the worn leather seat is none other than Eddie
"Bloody Arrow" Chavez, the insane drunken Native American. He is wearing a studded leather Harley jacket, torn blue jeans, a pair of spurred cowboy boots and a blue bandanna. In one hand he has a bottle of Jack Daniels and in the other he is holding the sacred necklace. You know he's in a bad mood because he is vigorously fingering the beads and taking huge swigs of whiskey. He pauses and turns to the camera]
"Toussi!" [He pauses to finger the necklace some more] "You've insulted my dignity! You have insulted my people! My ancestors! For it is said that brave who loses prized trophy in battle is destined for greatness. But he who does not lose his trophy in battle has been greatly shamed!"
[He takes another swig of whiskey then contemplates what he just said] "Ah bullshit! That belt wasn't worth anything! It was only keeping me from reaching the higher echelons of WOLF! It's been keeping me from scalping the
NIGHTBLADES while I toy around with the OMEGAS and BBKINGS! So nothing is lost, nothing gained......."
[He continues to finger the beads of the necklace as he thinks about what he will do to his future victims]
"But I think I'm going to put you on the top of my list. Why? Because you remind me of fat buffalo who cannot run from pride of lions. You impose many sanctions and penalties on the worthless roster of WOLF while you sit in your oversized office and smoke cheap cigars! So keep looking over your shoulder........."
[He pauses and laughs at the thought. He puts down the worry beads, seemingly in a better mood]
"Enough about that.........." [He starts laughing and is drowned out by the sound of a bulldozer. He takes yet another swig from the bottle]
"Let's talk about a real idiot! Ha! Ha!" [He leans forward in his seat, spilling out mouthfuls of whiskey. He regains his composure but barely]
Maple Leaf! Ha! Ha! Well that's all I have to say about him!" [Chavez leans on the door of the old car. The door falls off and he falls out of the car into the dirt. He manages to grab a hold of the bottle as he lays face up on the ground. He continues to speak as if nothing happened]
"By the way, just in case you pale faces are wondering. Why is Bloody Arrow teaming up with Chris Tiberia? Well all you have to do is sign the dotted line. And find out for yourselves....."
[He tosses the bottle aside since it's now empty and laughs] "And pale face JT Damon, I haven't forgotten about you! Troll, we've never met in the ring before. But I predict a swift and painful demise for the former......
[Somebody is walking through the broken glass and metal in the foreground. We can't see who it is but we can hear footsteps]
"And I emphasize, FORMER, EWF Hardcore Champion! Ha! Ha! Troll you haven't done anything since then! And you will be doing nothing this weekend except suffering in the Navajo Death Grip! Ha! Ha!"
[Suddenly, Chavez looks serious. He gets up off the ground. We can hear the footsteps approaching. He sits on the edge of the car, on the hood]
"JT Damon........." [He starts to tap on the hood. Then bang on it. Then smash it with his bare fists. He starts mumbling]
"JT......JT......JT.......JT!" [He then starts smashing his head into the metal until it's bloody. He keeps repeating to himself]
"JT.......JT.......JT.......JT.......JT.........JT.........JT" [Then the footsteps get closer. A man appears on camera. It's Chris Tiberia. He is heard muttering "Save it for the ring Chavez" as he pulls him away from the car] [Fade out] Big Dawg Handler of: Eddie "Bloody Arrow" Chavez 3-0-0 Former WOLF Television Champion [stripped of title] "Who's next?" -- Can u smell what da DAWG is cooking? =P -----------== Posted via Deja News, The Discussion Network ==---------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Discuss, or Start Your Own
