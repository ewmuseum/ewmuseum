[RP] Alex Hawthorne [Critique]

9 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, here's my second roleplay done so far for the highly up and coming, A1
Pro. *Ass kissing grin*
Whatever criticism or comments you feel need to be shared or said, please, share with us. This roleplay was done for a no-holds-barred match-up with the
"Sadistic Serpent" Keiji Zasaki. Continue forth and educate your mind. Thank you and enjoy.
----------- [Darkness. The screen fades in onto the ocean shore beaches of Redondo Beach,
California. The murky evening sky is enveloped in thick, gray puffs of clouds, masking away the late evening moonshine, which settles behind. The ocean just below is at rest, no major tides or waves clash up against the shore, causing for a calm evening on the beachfront. There on the vacant beach, sitting in front of a small kindled flame, is an obscure figure. His legs, tucked up against his chest, his right arm resting on his right knee and his other arm planted in the ground for support. Seconds go bye as the main just sits there, quiet as can be, staring into the small flame which dances in front of his face.
A tattoo is noticed running along the man's right inner-forearm. The tattoo reads the phrase, "Pleasure in Pain," in some sort of gothic print, signifying that this individual is indeed Alex Hawthorne. Gradually, he begins to break the said silence with a calm, yet potent, tone of voice.]
AH: A1 Pro and a serpent. This is what I've been left to deal with. [The man shakes his head.] AH: Keiji, do you think that you've impressed me? That you've shaken me up with your mindless stunts, or is that what your manager tells you? Is that what he tells you to calm you and soothe your uneasiness, or is this just all in my head?
[Alex inches his head up a bit more, achieving eye contact with the camera. Slowly, he begins to grin.] AH: Have I been telling myself the same lies over and over again, or is it your manager who tells the lies? Which one of you exactly is it that claims to be the "Sadistic Serpent?" Is it you, Zasaki, or your manager?
[Silent pause. He leans over to his side and begins to write slowly in the white grains of sand with his index finger.]
AH: You, Keiji, can only be as successful as your manager. Another man who makes decisions for you. How does that feel? Does he also help you going to the bathroom in the late evening hours? Inside the ring this week, Keiji, will it be you that I'm destroying, or him?
[He leans back over, sitting again in an upright position. A slow chuckle sneaks out from behind his locked lips.]
AH: Your tool of destruction lies within my grasp. Through each passing hour, inevitability grows closer until we find out just how much the truth actually hurts.
[The once seated Alex Hawthorne begins to get up and walks away slowly into the murky evening atmosphere. The camera focuses once again on the area where Alex sat. Just next to where he sat is the words he etched into the beach sand.
The words read, "FEEL MY PAIN" in a large writing. We fade out to the image of the still flickering flame, which lights the etched ground lettering.
