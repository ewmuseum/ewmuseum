[PLEASE CRITIQUE] John Rambin Speaks....

23 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera fades into a Press Conference at the Y.M.C.A., and there are press people everywhere snapping all kinds of pictures. There are kids ranging from 8 to 10 standing around as well, and they're all wearing T-shirts that say
"Rambin's Kids". At the podium stands the big man himself, John Rambin. John's wearing a USC sweatshirt with a pair of blue jeans on. You can't see his eyes due to the Oakley sun glasses he's wearing, but his hair is flowing across his shoulders and his back. He's right in the middle of talking about a new program he's started for kids....]
John: ......so I give to you the "After-School Slam Fest". Now kids will be able to wrestle each other in a safe and fun way. We've got trained professionals at each YMCA here in Southern California, and regulated wrestling rings. This way the kids don't have to go and wrestle in their backyard hitting each other with everything but the kitchen sink. I've seen too many kids get hurt because they're doing things the wrong way, or they think wrestling is all about hitting someone with a chair.
(John clears his throat and continues.) John: I feel that kids need to know what wrestling is all about. Throwing a chair and someone or even putting someone through a table isn't wrestling.
Wrestling is about telling a story and getting people interested in the sport. I feel it's a good way to build friendships, good clean competition, and it will keep kids off the streets and they'll be doing something they love. Now
I'll take any questions right now...I have to go in a little bit, but I'll answer as many as I can.
Reporter #1: John you've done so much for the community, and today you come to us with wrestling. We all know you're a wrestler, and a lot of us enjoy watching you....but don't you think that this is promoting violence?
John: That's a good question, but let me answer it with this question. Is amateur wrestling violent or what about Football? We have after school programs for both of those, but for some reason people love to come down on wrestling.
As a matter of fact I think Pro-Wrestling will structure a kid more than Football or Amateur style will. In Pro-Wrestling you have your own identity, you're out there being yourself or creating a character. You're not just going out and catching a ball or trying to get some points on a blue mat....you're creating a story. People also said the same thing about boxing, but we continue to teach that at the Y.M.C.A, and it's safe and fun.
Reporter #2: Is it true that you have just signed with the NCWA and you're going back to wrestling full time?
(The crowd reacts with a big gasp and start talking as though they thought this day would never come again. John tries to hush the crowd with his hands, but a lot of people seem very uneasy about the announcement that was just made.)
John: People.... (The noise level doesn't decrease.) John: People please...let me explain. (Finally the noise lowers...) John: I wanted to wait and make it a suprise to everyone here in Huntington
Beach, California....but YES I am going back to the wrestling ring.
(Parents are obviously upset, but the kids are cheering.) John: Now before all of the parents get down on me just know that I'm not going anywhere. I will still be apart of your kids lives, and I will still be around to get "After School Slam Fest" off the ground. The NCWA has contacted me, and
I am very interested in wrestling for them. They seem to be a top notch wrestling organization, and they will steer my career in the right direction.
(One of the mothers shouts....) Mom: This isn't fair...you're going back to wrestling and you're going to start hitting people with chairs, you're going to bleed....you're not practicing what you preach!!! You're nothing but a liar!!!
Other Parents: Yeah!!! Liar!!! Cheat!!! Scam Artist!!! (John has a smile on his face as he is trying to calm them down. He takes a little sigh and removes his glasses to show his knock out Blue eyes. The women in the room go quiet, and John lights up with a huge smile.)
John: Ladies you have my word that I won't use a chair, put someone through a table...or even make someone bleed. You know me better than that women! You know that I love all these kids like my own, and the reason that I'm going to the NCWA is because other kids need a role model too. You can't just have me all to yourself. I'm gonna teach the bad guys a lesson...
Kids: YEAH!!!! John: I'm gonna show them that John Rambin from Huntington Beach, California is not to be messed with!
Kids: YEAH!!!! John: I'm gonna show all the wrestlers, and all the people at home watching that they can stand behind someone that is all about truth and justice. Those who oppose me...well I'll have to show them "No Love"!!!!
(NOTE: "No Love" happens to be Johns finishing move...) KIDS: YAY!!!!!! JOHN!!! JOHN!!!! JOHN!!!! JOHN!!!!! JOHN!!!! John: Now if you'll excuse me...I've got some training to do!!! (John steps off the podium and signs a few autographs. Kids are all over him as the camera fades on him giving a huge smile.)
