[Free Agent] Jeff "Madfox" Matthews addresses Simon Lebec

17 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, I was bored again... [Camera opens up to a shot of an angry looking Lawrence Williams. Williams is reading his latest posts on his Pentium II Acer. Williams is wearing a black shirt entitled, "Shut the F*** up."]
Williams: Okay... everyone, shut the hell up! I'm sick an' tired of this
Lebec character runnin' 'round *my* domain talkin' 'bout bein' the biggest free agent. You ain't nobody, Lebec, face it! Ya' might be the Stuff That Dreams
Are Made Of, but when ya' compared ta' me, you're the Stuff That Comes Out The
Crack Of Muh Ass! That's right... you uh pile uh s<bleep>t.
Oh an', Colby, do us all uh fava', an' shut the hell up. Ain't nobody wanna hear 'bout yo lil' UEW ova' dere... UEW ain't *nobody!* 'Specially when ya'
got lil' punks like Creed runnin' around, thinkin' they bikers an' stuff 'cause they got some leather jackets an' Harleys.
So, in uh nutshell, I'm basically tellin' ev'ryone ta' shut up... 'cause ya' not on muh level. An' that's the truf, 'cause the truf hurts! [Fade.] -Daniel "He that is void of wisdom despiseth his neighbour: but a man of understanding holdeth his peace." - Proverbs 11:12
