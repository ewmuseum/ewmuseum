Personal Info: Handlers Name: Kenneth Morlock E-Mail Address: [detail withheld] AIM: [detail withheld] ICQ#: IRC: Wrestlers Info[For Tag Teams, fill out TWICE] Wrestlers Name: "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock Height: 6'4 Weight:245 Heel/Face: heel Hometown:Memphis Tennessee Appearence:Kenneth is very stocky build, he has no facial hair except for the occasional Goatee. He is light tanned with a very nice six pack and well defined arms and shoulders. He has short brown hair which is usually in a part to the side. He also has hazel eyes.
Ring Attire: He wears no shirt. He has a pair of dark blue leg tights that read "REAL" down the left leg and "DEAL" down the right leg. He also wears a pair of black wrestling boots that read "SSS" on both of them.

Wrestling Style: Brawler/technical 15-20 Moves: 1) Gutwrench Suplex 2) Belly-to-Belly Suplex 3) Spinning Reverse DDT 4) Rolling Clothesline 5) Reverse Russian Leg Sweep 6) Full-Nelson Slam 7) Neckbreaker 8) Back Breaker 9) Front Suplex 10) Shoulder Breaker 11) Sidewalk Slam 12) Lariat 16) Brain Buster 17) Fall-Away Slam Finisher: "The Lock-Down" Set-up: Double Arm Underhook DDT List your characters Strengths and Weaknesses (They must be equal. If you have 3 strengths than you must also have three weaknesses)
Three Strengths: 1)Not Afraid of Anyone! 2) Very strong for his smaller frame
Three Weaknesses:
1)Not Afraid of Anyone! 2) Slow (speed wise) Profile(Charecters History)Kenneth grew up in inner city Chicago till the age of fifteen. He saw many horrible things while he stayed in chicago. At the age of fifteen his mother was few up with all the violence and senselessness going around so she decided to move her and her child to the small atmosphere of
Arkansas. Kenneth joined the wrestling team in high school and loved it. He became the state champion in his junior year and was third in his senior year.
Kenneth went off to college at the University of Tennessee. During his freshmen year his mother told him that she was not going to be able to keep paying for his schooling and that he would have to take on a job of his own also. So
Kenneth jumped from crappy job to crappy job for about half of a year until he decided to give wrestling a try. He saw that they were paying decent money and he rolled with it. Morlock became an automatic success in his college day in the Tennessee and Arkansas Indy feds as his face character "The Real Deal"
Kenneth Morlock. After he graduated college, he decided to go try some other markets, some bigger markets. Kenneth was picked up by James Pillow who ran the
ICW. For a few months Kenneth couldnt even score a victory. Kenneth decided to change his gimmick and come out as a heel. "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock had now arrived. Kenneth turned his back on the friends he had, and turned his back on the fans. No one understood the change except him, at first he hated the change and was going to go back until finally things started rolling his way.
Pillow and Diamond were sending title shots his way, and Morlock was not going to mess these things up. Morlock became the ICW Continental champion and the man to break the Mike Masters winning streak and undefeated record in ICW. When
ICW closed Morlock jumped around from fed to fed until now.
Gimmick/Attitude: He is considered as a pretty boy heel in most aspects. But he is more than that. Kenneth can overwhelm you with his looks and his ring smarts. He knows how to play the pretty boy role to a "T" to get what he wants and he knows how to outsmart you to get what he wants. He has no feelings for anyone in the ring or anyone working any company. He see's everyone as someone who may stand in his way one day.
