\Janis, I've read a few of your posts on RSPWF (obviously if I'm writing this...) and I decided that I should write my response to you with out repeating everything. You talk about how a person wants to create a better wrestelr through the simulator but should feel NO obligation to RolePlay his character.... that Roleplaying has nothing to do with Professional wreslting? Hmm, let's look at that assumption, shall we? Professional wrestling is based upon a steady balance of athletics, and entyertainment. Now, wrestling depends greatly on flashes... whether you believe it or not. Characters tend to fall into obscurity without them, whenther they're bad at it or not. (ie. Luger, Backlund, et al.) You tend to think that a player should be able to create any wrestler he wants, and then just see how he works... well, for those people who do work they're butt off flashing, they deserve more credit. They try to Role Play, and e-wrestling is a ROLE-playing game. A fed is boring without the Role playing. If you just want to see how your wrestler does in a simulator, buy the simulator. I agree with past posters who say that a Non-RolePlayer should be penalized. I'd rather watch a great feud rather than a silent champion. And I know that great feuds don't revolve around belts, believe me, I know. But the fact is, sometimes they do. And you can't feud with someone if they don't DO anything more than create a character. The champion is supposed to be the pinnacle of the league, and that pinnacle should have a nice mixture of skill and talent in ALL areas, not just creating a TNM monster. -mark
