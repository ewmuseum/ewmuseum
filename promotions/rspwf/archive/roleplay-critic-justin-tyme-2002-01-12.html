Roleplay Critic - Justin Tyme

12 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Could someone help me out and tell me how I can make my rp's alot better, by reading below and maybe breaking it down and finding mistakes?
Scene - The Camera Crew is in one of the top notch hotel suites in Venture,
California. They go up to room ' 423 ' Where Justin Tyme and Angel are spending most of their time while they are in California. A Camera Crew knocks on the door and Justin yells for them to come in. He is drinking a cola and looking threw some old magazines when he looks up. He smiles and turns his head back to the magazine.. The Camera Man tries to say something but Justin holds up a finger, signafying for them to be quiet for a moment while he finishes reading something. He closes the magazine and looks up.
Justin - Sorry about that, just checkin up on some stocks.. and lookin at some restraunts they have in this area. Did you know they don't have a single ' Fast
Eddies ' in this whole fucking city? That's a crock of shit.
Camera Man - Well you know, if you go up I-4 about a mile and a half, swing a right to onto Chestnut.. they have a Fast Eddies.
Justin - Thanks man..Can I do something for you guys though? Camera Man - Well we wanted to get a word with you.. Justin - On what.. Camera Man - Well.. your next match at California Collision next week. Justin - What!? It's already been sent out.. Scene - He scatters through some mail thats lying on the bed.. He pulls out an unmarked letter with only the letters NCW on it. He rips it open.. Reads a little message that was on the top. He then looks down and reads over the matches. He signs it, and puts some little message on it. Puts it back into the envelope and signs his name on the envelope.
Justin - Well, I'm in a match against some guy named Tommy Dutch? I don't think
I've heard of him, much less from him. So theres not much to say. I fought
Butch Mccaull last week. And he never came out and said anything.. Am I really this intimidating? All I can say right now is that I will beat Tommy Dutch. He will feel the wrath of the king of extreme.
Camera Man - Oh, Ok. Scene - The Camera Man starts walking towards the door. Justin - Whoa, Whoa.. I'm not done yet. I won't be done until Angel comes through that door (he points to the bathroom door) half naked in a thong and some lingerie. What I can say is that I am getting tired of the way Jason Cage is running things around here. And if things continue, my career here won't be long. You see Jason Cage is a very self-centered person. If you have noticed last night. It showed Jason Cage with all of his titles around him. No one cares Jason.. Give it up already... It's getting old fast. Another thing I don't like is this Too Pretty Character.. This guy talks alot of trash for someone who I have yet to see has any talent whatsoever. I guess you could say he is exactly like that Somebody Special Jordan Kelly Guy. Both these guys'
egos are entirely to big for their reputation. They are both self-centered, damn.. you know now they sound like Jason Cage. Jesus.. These three should for some kind of cult. You have to think very highly of yourself to get in. I would love to have a match with either of these three characters. I would give my king of extreme title to show them a thing or two. Bust up their ego's a bit maybe. But, oh well..
Angel - Justin.... Scene - The Camera shows her half naked sticking her barely closed chest out of the door.. Justin - Ahh, time for you guys to leave.. hey take this to Jason Cage will you.. Tell him theres a note for him at the bottom. Scene fades to black.
------------------------------------ Note Reads: Dear Jason Cage, I just wanted to let you know, I am not happy with the way things are running currently. I would like to see some of the following changes made.. Really the only thing I want to see changed is your attitude. Stop thinking so highly of yourself. Your not as good as you act. I am getting sick of it and if you don't stop.. well let's just say I don't want the headlines to read.. ' ANGRY NCW
SUPERSTAR ATTACKS OWNER '. Do what you can to prevent those headlines.
Signed Justin Tyme
