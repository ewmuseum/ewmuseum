Free Agent E-Wrestler

22 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is the intro RP I used for my last fed. Give it a once over, critique it, roast it, whatever, then give me a shout.
<<<--- --->>> The air around you is thick with dust, and the bright light hurts your eyes. You hear something in the background, it sounds like a hundred freight trains flying by. As your eyes get used to the light and the dust you can make out where you are.
Rows upon rows of seats rise up in a circle around you, and those seats are filled with hundreds of screaming people. They all are chanting a single word that grows louder every time it is said.
"Blood!" "BLood!" "BLOod!" "BLOOd!" "BLOOD!" The roar is deafening, fear washes over you as you wait for something to happen. The chant suddenly stops and turns into a roar of applause. You look around and see a statuesque woman, dressed in a shift of pure white with a gilet of bright silver chain mail, walk into the arena.
The beauty motions the crowd for quiet before she begins to speak. Her voice thunders with the acoustics of the arena.
"LADIES AND GENTLEMAN, we all know why you are here. You want to see..." The crowd screams out as one. "BLOOD!" She waits for the noise of the crowd to subside before she speaks again. "Yes, and you shall have it. Today, my master, Mars begins to train for the battles to come."
Great double doors in the back of the arena begin to open, and disgorges a dozen men. Some large, some small but they all have the look of killers. The men begin to pace the sand and moving to the opposite side of the arena waiting for something to happen.
As the trumpets sound from far overhead, the fear begins to twist your stomach into knots. As the great doors open all the way, you find yourself trying to sink back into the wall. Trying to make yourself as small and unnoticeable as you can.
The crowd surges to life with a terrible crescendo of inhuman sounds, as a very large man enters the arena. He is dressed in black leather pants and boots; a vest of chain mail covers his far to massive chest. Heavy black gauntlets cover his fists, and an executioner's hood covers his face concealing his identity.
A bright flash draws your attention back to the goddess, how else could she be described you think to yourself. She begins to walk to a dais located above the great doors. As she climbs the stairs to the top, the killers begin to prepare themselves something, something you're still not sure of. Once at the top the goddess begins to speak, her voice full of lust and hunger.
"WARRIORS, before you stands my master, Mars." The crowd roars in approval. "You have been brought here because you are the most depraved, sadistic fighters in the…" "SILENCE!" You leap back, awestruck by the power of the voice. Your gaze turns to Mars, who has removed his vest and hood. Showing a wolf-lean visage of hate. Salt and pepper black hair falls to his shoulders, a short, trim, graying beard that surrounds the face. Yet the most frightening things are his eyes, an ice blue that seems to drill into your soul.
Mars glares up at the goddess and then at the killers. "Athena, enough of your mindless chatter. These whore-sons are here for one reason, TO SUFFER!"
Angered the warriors begin to advance. Waiting for the right time to attack, but the decision has been taken out of their hands. Mars rushes forward attacking all within reach while bellowing his challenge.
"CRY HAVOC AND LET SLIP THE DOGS OF WAR!" The level of violence you witness in that attack, shocks your sensibilities. The sounds of men screaming in pain and rage, as flesh is tore and bones broken. The sight of warm blood splashed on other men and the walls and spilled in the dried sand. Your stomach knots as you watch and wait for the outcome. An outcome you can't be sure of.
Now only four remain, Mars and three of the warriors. Mars is covered in blood and gore, much of it his own. Two of the warriors charge Mars and begin to lay in to him with a fury of punches and kicks, but he refuses to fall. Mars grabs the first of the two and tosses him into the other, knocking them both to the ground. As the warriors rise to their feet Mars lifts one of them to his feet, then drives him back to the ground with a short-arm clothesline. The other warrior begins to attack again only to be met by a big boot from Mars, sending him hurtling backwards in the sand. Now only the largest of warriors remain.
The last warrior is as big if not bigger than Mars, outweighing him by forty or fifty pounds. You don't know how Mars can survive this last attacker, and you hope he doesn't. It would serve the bastard right for bringing you here.
Mars stands somewhat shakily, bleeding from several cuts. The warrior laughs, and then speaks.
"What is the matter, old man? Do you tire so easily? I am Typhon, and I am your executioner!"
Mars sinks down to one knee with his hand resting on the sand. The warrior charges, sensing an easy kill.
Before the warrior can even lay a hand on Mars, Mars shoots to his feet and has him by the throat. A move so frighteningly quick, that you're not sure how it was done. Mars lifts the warrior high into the air before choke-slamming him hard upon the sandy arena floor.
With the battle complete, Mars stands straight and tall and bellows out to the crowd, which sits in stunned silence.
"NOW, DAWNS THE AGE OF WAR!" Mars begins to turn towards the great double doors, when he notices you. He changes direction and begins to walk right at you. He begins to speak, as he approaches.
"Xander Lake, I will accept your invitation to join the AWC. Be warned though nothing can stop me from getting what I want."
His tone changes now that he is right in your face, his voice full of menace and hatred and rage.
"All of you out there will learn to fear me, for this is just a taste of the violence of War. The fog of War is soon going to roll into AWC. So, AWC, be warned Mars is coming and with me dawns a new age. AN AGE OF WAR!"
Mars turns from you and walks towards the double doors as the crowd begins to chant. "Blood" "BLood" "BLOod" "BLOOd" "BLOOD" Fade to Black. "BLOOD" "BLOOD" "BLOOD" Fade to silence. <<<--- --->>>
