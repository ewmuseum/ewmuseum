The 1996 R.S.P-W.F Award Winners

24 December 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Award Winners from the 1996 R.S.P-W.F Awards: 1. Best Wrestler of the Year Winner: "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor Runners-Up: "Iceman" Brian Knight & "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley 2. Best Tag Team of the Year Winner: Damage Incorporated Runners-Up: The Good Knights & The Maloney Brothers 3. Best Face of the Year Winner: Dan "Flash" Kauffman Runners-Up: "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley & Drac "The Raven" Dravin 4. Best Heel of the Year Winner: Super Scott Runners-Up: Serge Annis & "Iceman" Brian Knight 5. Best Worker (Roleplayer) Winner: Dan "Flash" Kauffman Runners-Up: Robski & Christian Kingsworth 6. Best Flyer Winner: Youth Gone Wild Runners-Up: Physcrobat & Blitz Krieg 7. Best Technical Wrestler Winner: Chris Degeau Runners-Up: Dan "Flash" Kauffman & Christian Kingsworth 8. Best Brawler Winner: Otto "The Butcher" Verhoveon Runners-Up: Mark Destructo & Raekwon 9. Most Popular Wrestler Winner: "Dreamlover" Trey Porter Runners-Up: "Quickstrike" Chris Quigley & "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor 10. Most Overrated Wrestler Winner: Super Scott Runners-Up: Chris "Hitman" Thomas & The Jammer 11. Best Wrestling Gimmick Winner: The Awesome Alliance Runners-Up: The Universal Powers & Robski 12. Best Wrestling Move Winner: Alex Kidd's Summersault Bulldog Runners-Up: Hellshock's Hellfire CLAW Hold & Dokuhebi's Venom Lock 13. Best Match of The Year Winner: "Offensive" Alex Adams vs. "Dreamlover" Trey Porter (EWA's Offensive Dreams) Runners-Up: "Iceman" Brian Knight vs. "Hot Stuff" Chris Taylor (FoPEW) & Ladder Match: Serge Annis vs. Youth Gone Wild (UWF's Pushin' Up Daisies '96) 14. Best Feud of The Year Winner: "Offensive" Alex Adams vs. "Dreamlover" Trey Porter (EWA) Runners-Up: The Awesome Alliance vs. The Rest of E-Wrestling & Serge Annis vs. Youth Gone Wild 15. Most Disappointing News Item of The Year Winner: "Dreamlover" Trey Porter leaving the EWA Runners-Up: GWF shuts down & John Moore & Jayson Stancil leave the NCW bookers team 16. Best Manager of The Year Winner: Cherry Cheesecake Runners-Up: Brian Lau & "Iceman" Brian Knight 17. Best Announcer or Colour Commentator Winner: Mr. Flawless Runners-Up: "Slick" Rick Baptist & Thomas N. Tyler 18. Best Interviewee Winner: "Spotlight" Billy Shakespeare Runners-Up: The Trailer Park Assassins & Hollywood 19. Best Angle of The Year Winner: [name withheld]'s shoots to make Super Scott the megaheel of all time Runners-Up: Chris Taylor joins the Awesome Alliance & Drac "The Raven" Dravin's near death 20. Best Wrestling Show Winner: EWA Sunday Night RARE Runners-Up: IIWF Saturday Night & The World Cup of e-Wrestling 21. Best E-Wrestling Federation of the Year Winner: IIWF: International Internet Wrestling Federation Runners-Up: FoPEW: Federation of Professional e-Wrestling & EWA: Extreme Wrestling Association
