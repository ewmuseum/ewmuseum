To All Fed-Heads: Hi, my name is [name withheld], and I've been doing this e-wrestling gig for about 2 years now. Perhaps some of you know me, most I'm sure do not. I have a large number of characters, listed below. However, most are only concepts and will never see the light of day.
Allow me to get down to business. I'm looking for another fantasy wrestling league. The league I was currently in, the Ohio Wrestling Federation, closed (Gee, thanks Dan...hehe. just kiddin'). However, before I am flooded with applications and email messages, I have a few rules I would like to make perfectly clear:
1.) The league must be booked. I will not respond to any TNM league offers. No offense, but I'm not a big TNM fan. I know they can have great RP (I
*AM* in a TNM league, but it's basically the league I've been in since I started RP'ing!). So, if you run a TNM league, please look elsewhere for handlers. 2.) The league must use a flash packet or mailing list (preferably packet, due to the amount of mail I get anyway). I detest message boards. Some love
'em, I don't. I don't have time to go to the website when I want to roleplay. I'd much rather just open my email program and type it up there. Sure, message boards offer the use of
HTML or images, but that can be as much a curse as a blessing. 3.) The league must not have more than two cards per week. More than that tends to lead to overkill, plus, as a former fedhead, I know that kind of workload can burn a fedhead out easily. At least one of the cards should be fully written.
4.) I would like a league with a comparitively small roster. This, I think, allows more room for angles and character development. This isn't set in stone, however.
5.) I would like a "classic" league, not an X-TREME HARDCORE DEATH LEAGUE! This is great if you like that sort of thing, but I find it boring after awhile. Wrestling should focus more on WRESTLING and ANGLES. A league with a hardcore title would be okay, it would add spice. Just not a league where *EVERY* single match is hardcore. Think modern day WWF for a good mix of classic wrestling with the occasional hardcore match.
Well, I think that's it. Sorry if this sounds "stuck up" or anything. I just know I haven't had good experiences with certain aspects of leagues. Anyway, on to the wrestlers!

His gimmick is a bit too complex to explain here, but that's not his main focus anyway. However, his wife is currently pregnant, so he is on leave of absence from the sport. Expect a return in June when they have their child.

He was created to help Sabbath out in the UCWF around May or June 1997, and has been here and there. However, I've never been his biggest fan.
Leagues: UCWF (twice), IPWF, UWA, UKCW The Hatebreeders: Jackal and Asylum, the e-wrestling world's resident tricksters. They were created to have fun with. Always over the top, and just plain
WEIRD. Can be over just about anywhere, as opposed to the rest of my characters, because they always wind up pissing someone off.
Leagues: WOWA, UCWF, HLWA, OWF Titles: (Jackal) 1-Time UCWF TV Champion, (As a Team) 1-Time HLWA Tag Team Champions. (NOTE: The following wrestlers may or may not wrestle again. They were mainly created for my amusement)
