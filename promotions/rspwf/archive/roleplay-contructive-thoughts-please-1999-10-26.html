[Roleplay] Contructive thoughts please

26 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Subj: [LVPWA] "The Hammer" Alex Cote flash Well, this is the first roleplay I made for Cote in almost two months, please tell me what you think honestly.
[Fade in.....] [The scene opens up in the desolate and barren landscape of the Nevada desert, outside the lights and glamour of the big city. The camera turns to see nothing but mountains in the distance, few small plants, and miles upon miles of sand leading to the horizon. The camera continues to turn until it finally hits something other than sand. The car sees the back of a car. A red car, as the camera continues to turn, it recieves a better view of the car. When it is halfway done, we see the car is a '69 Chevy Camero convertable with chrome rims, spoiler, and flames on the side. The top is back and when is continues to turn, we the beginning of a man who is apparently sitting on the hood of the car. The camera turns the rest of the way, stops moving, and reveals the man is none other than "The Hammer" Alex Cote.]
[Alex's attire consist of a pair of black stonewashed jeans with black boots. Also, a navy blue tang top with "I Am Your Lord And Savior" printed on the front, a pair of black leather gloves cut at the knuckles, and a silver chain with a pentagram hanging from around his neck. His goatee is neatly trimmed and his hair is done neatly exceot for part of his bangs hang in his face.]
[Alex slowly looks up at the camera with a cold stare. He then looks back down to the ground and hows his hand out.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Out there, the Nevada desert, a barren land with nothing more than sand. And endless wasteland where the only creatures here live by one rule, survival of the fittest, [pause] the way it should be.
Mother nature had the right idea by making this rule, and this is how life should be. The EL VEE PEE DUBBA YA AYE, a land almost as desolate as this, this right here is only the beginning, soon it shall bloom into something to be admired, but now, it is nothing more than the basics......nothing. The land of wrestling is like the wilderness, survival of the fittest, but there are always those who are able to survive, but the strong conquer, and thats, simply, is my plan.
[Alex runs his hand through his hair pushing the bangs from out of his face.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Let me start at the beginning so everyone can get a feel for what I am saying. I have been out of action for almost two months now, and over that time I have thought of a lot of things. When I left the world of wrestling, I was the most hated man in the land, I lost what is really meant to Alex Cote. When my career began, I used to feed off the roar of the crowd, the cheers of the fans, it made me feel stronger, more powerful. But then, like many others have in the past, I lost focus on what was important, I strayed from the path and let my own arrogance possess my very essence. I was on top of the world and that in turn helped me do a lot of things I never did before.
[Alex crosses his arms slowly.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: But I wasn't happy, I wanted more and more until I just snapped. Ignorance may be bliss but arrogance is hell. And one by one I let my opportunies.....
[Alex bends down, picks up a handful of sand, and lets is falls between his fingers.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: .....slip through my fingers. [Alex stands back up and leans against the car.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: But now, I have gotten back on the path and with the helps of all the fans out there I plan to once again get myself to the top of the ladder. Slowly, but surely, my plans are once again falling into play and the EL VEE PEE DUBBA YA AYE is going to be my playing ground.
[Alex gets a cocky grin on his face.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Monday night, millions of people watched me take out Nick Madison and help David Velmont get a win. Now, I don't know Velmont, nor do I want to know him, and I also wasn't out to get Nick Madison. I was just sitting there scouting the competition when Madison, trying to play the tough guy, came over to me and tries to act tough, so I made sure I brought him down from cloud nine and back to the cold feel of the Earth's ground. Madison, a word of advice from a man that knows, don't mess with forces you have no control over because they always come back and bite you in the ass!!
[Alex begins to walk away from the car slowly.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Now, November first, a day that will live in infamy in the EL VEE PEE DUBBA YA AYE, it is the debute of "The Hammer". I have not stepped into the squared circle in almost two months but I've still be working out and doing what I've done for years. But this is not just a match, it is a battle royal, a type of match I never lose. When it come to tossing pieces of trash over the top and to the floor, I am the master of the art, hell, I created the art.
[Alex stops, reaches into his pocket, removes a pair of sunglasses from a case, puts them on, and continues walking in a slow pace.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Twenty three men, but only four can move on to the Pay-Per-View for the gold. I've looked at who is my competition, and I have to be honest in saying [chuckles] I'm less than impressed. I see a list of men who even in their prime, have never come close to being as great as this man who stands before you. I stand here and think about men like Tommy Lionheart,
Kenneth Morlock, Marcus Blaq, Shawn Viper, men from the now defunct ICW. They all worked hard to be the best, but none of them were ever above me.
[Alex turns and looks into the sun.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: But then, I see men like David Velmont, Jeffrey Drake, Mike Barlow, Pecker, men I have never seen or stepped into the ring with before and are as mysterious to me as the surface of the sun. But, like me, we all have a common goal, and that is the gold. They may have achieved many different things in their careers and so have I, I don't plan to underestimate them, but as people can tell, confidence, like many things, are strong points when it comes to me.
[Alex walks to where the car once again comes into view. He stops right in front of the car with his arms crossed.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: But there is only one man who I am looking at as a threat, a man who is world renown for his feats, a man who is feared within the wrestling circuit, and that is Ess Vee Ess, of Setzer Van Strife. Strife, I seen you before and I know what you are capable of and if any man can send me for a loop, it would be you, so you have to expect I plan to go right after you and make sure I send you to the floor. A man I can admire, a man who has almost a bigger ego than myself, but unlike you, my ego doesn't flood my judgement. Now, if I was paying attention, you challenged the wrestlers of this federation, a move that could get you making a deposit from the local blood bank, but I admire that. But you have to see that I am coming straight for you and I will personally make sure you see the top rope from an aerial view and get a formal meeting with the floor.
[Alex opens the car door, step inside, turns the engine over, and looks at the camera.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: The EL VEE PEE DUBBA YA AYE, the new playground for "The Hammer", I plan to walk into that ring on Heavy Metal Monday with a plan, and walking out with a victory. Like the ICW, and many other fed before it, I shall be the man who walks away the most feared and resented man in the game and with all the million and millions of people behind me, I will show each and everyone that this is "The Hammer"s land and that you all better simply prepare to be hammered because, like always, _I TAKE NO PRISIONERS_!
