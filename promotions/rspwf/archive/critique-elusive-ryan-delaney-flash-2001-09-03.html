[Critique] "Elusive" Ryan Delaney - FLASH

3 September 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Trees, woods, and a fence. Dogs and their masters keeping them at bay. Sun shining, wind blowing, and grass flush green. It is a beautiful day in the park, a beautiful day indeed. Dogs of all shapes and sizes are running.
Golden labs to poodles to even the occasional mutt. And there are masters of all shapes and sizes too.
But, there is someone else, not in the center of it all, watching from the outside. His face seems so familiar, almost as if he were a ghost. The long, black hair; the leather pants; the frightening face... Ah yes, it is him... It is... "Elusive" Ryan Delaney.]
"ELUSIVE" RYAN DELANEY: There they are.. The primitive beasts running around, barking, growling. Feeling as if they were free, and as if they owned this land, like it once was. Though their masters, the people to which they are bound watch carefully, they are lost in the feelings they feel.
[Two dogs, a German shepherd and a mutt, begin a stand off. Growling and staring -- the masters become worried, worried of a fight ensuing.]
"ELUSIVE" RYAN DELANEY: However, when the things get to hot to handle; when the matter at hand gets too big for them and their minds to handle
[And they were right, a fight breaks out between the two dogs. Snarling and biting, the dogs claw for each other's throat, as the masters rush in -- trying to do something. One lady falls, in immediate danger. Others just watch in horror as the dogs begin to move closer to the fallen master. But, another
one, rushes in, and like the first, falls flat on her ass. As her eyes wide open with terror of dying right then and there, Delaney speaks, but now, with a different, commanding tone of voice.]
"ELUSIVE" RYAN DELANEY: all hell breaks loose! [The camera switches positions, as the melee continues in the middle of this dog run. It is now focused on Delaney, who sits in the shade, out of the lime light, on a rock, Indian style. His eyes hidden behind the hair, his face hidden behind the paint, and his opinions being shared.]
"ELUSIVE" RYAN DELANEY: On the edge they do sit, unsure of what to do. They're scared, frightened and worried. They sit at the brink, the edge at which they could gain everything, or loose it all.
[With a shake of the head, the hair is gone, and the eyes, the eyes are visible. The melee has stopped, the growls intertwined with the shrieks have ceased to occur.]
"ELUSIVE" RYAN DELANEY: That is where Jake Morgan, Buddy Lee and even Angel
Rotten sit. Mr. Morgan is afraid of fighting Buddy Lee, he is scared of losing, of his name being tarnished. Mr. Buddy Lee is frightened by the aspect of wrestling Morgan, of wrestling for something even more valuable for gold -- pride. And Angel Rotten...
[A compassionate tone of voice is now tucked away in his voice. His eyes begin to wonder off, almost as if he, Ryan Delaney, is imagining something, or even, recalling what happened days ago.]
"ELUSIVE" RYAN DELANEY: She's lies in the midst of it all. Dating a power hungry, self indulged maniac, and being assaulted by a man caught in the moment
-- she hangs in the balance, worried. She is worried of Morgan throwing all that he holds dear. But worried even more that she, she has placed herself into a situation even she can't escape.
[And with a smile on his face, showing love, not hate.] Such a shame, too... I thought she was smarter than that... [The camera then switches, showing on the of the ladies walking away, holding her arm, and her other holding a towel to a cut on her forehead. The towel is soaked in blood, as the camera fades.]
