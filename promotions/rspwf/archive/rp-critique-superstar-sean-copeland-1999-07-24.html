Stupid" Danny Copeland, Jessica Stevens, and an unknown person. Sean Copeland is wearing his ring attire, consisting of long black tights with Superstar on his butt in white writing, black boots, black Rayban sunglasses, and a plain backwards black cap. His loop ear ring is also shown on his left ear. He has on a shirt that says "I almost F*cked my mother" on the front and on the back it says "Superstar 4:20 says I just smoked your ass out." "Big Stupid" Danny
Copeland is wearing long black jeans, some brown hiking shoes, a white shirt that says on the front "Sean Copeland is my idol" and on the back "Danny
Copeland 9:11 says I almost called the cops on your ass but I forgot the number." His long, black goatee and mustache give him that biker type look.
Jessica Stevens is wearing a pink bikini top, really short blue jean shorts, and some sandals. Her long brown hair with streaks of blonde give her that
Jenny McCarthy type looks and figure. The camera overhears the conversation going on.]
"Superstar" Sean Copeland: So do you understand the plan? Unknown Person: Yes....... "Superstar" Sean Copeland: Repeat it to me one more time. Unknown Person: OK. You'll be in the front row, right? "Superstar" Sean Copeland (in Dr. Evil type voice) : Right! Unknown Person: Well I'll be fighting Basher when your brother comes down and kicks both our asses, as you jump into the ring giving Basher your finisher and
Jessica just mocks me and throw me out of the ring. And how much do I get?
"Superstar" Sean Copeland: $15,000 Unknown Person: Cool, that's about ½ as much as I make all year. I want it now though. "Superstar" Sean Copeland: My policy is half before and half after the job is completed. OK! Now get going, your match is starting soon.
Unknown Person: OK [The Unknown person leaves. As he is leaving, on his small tights: Bobby Stone is written. "Superstar" Sean Copeland, "Big Stupid" Danny Copeland, and Jessica
Stevens begin to chuckle as he leaves.]
"Superstar" Sean Copeland: Stupid retard. [Sean Copeland notices the cameraman and shoves the cameraman down as the camera turns to black.]
[The camera cuts back on into a XPW house show. The crowd is jam packed as they go to the ring announcer for the next match.]
["Welcome To Miami" by Will Smith plays over the PA.] Announcer: Making his way to the ring at this time.........standing in at 6'2 inches tall...........weighing in at 234 lbs...........he is Bobby
Stooooooooooooone! [The crowd goes into small mixed reactions as out comes Bobby Stone wearing small red tights with Bobby Stone written on the back. He has no shirt and red boots on. He heads off to the ring showing off his muscles. As he heads down, he looks towards "Superstar" Sean Copeland and nods but continues on. He gets into the ring as his music dies down.]
Announcer: And his opponent.................... ["Back in Black" by AC/DC plays over the PA.] Announcer: Making his way to the ring at this time, hailing from the great state of Utah..........standing in at 6 feet tall and weighing in at 252 lbs............he is the Basheeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeer!!!!!
[Another bit of small mixed reactions occur as out comes Basher in red and black overall tights. Basher has a mask on with crazy shapes in red and black.
He makes his way to the ring, as the crowd laughs at this horrible looking man. He gets into the ring and warms up on the ropes a little as the match gets underway.] Commentator #1: They lock up and out comes Basher with some aggressive forearms to the head of Bobby Stone, sending Bobby down.
Commentator #2: Hahaha, they guys suck! I could kick both there asses with my hands tied behind my back.
Commentator #1: Ok....Basher pulls Bobby up and sends him into the ropes and hits him with a vicious clothesline sending Bobby back down to the mat.
Commentator #2: BORING BORING BORING! I'm going to take a shit, I'll be right back! [The camera turn towards the ring as Basher gets Bobby into a headlock. The crowd begins a chant of "Boring" as about only 1 minute of the match has gotten underway. Bobby begins getting to his feet. He hits Basher with a few elbows then sends him into the ropes, only to be getting a knee into the low chest area sending Bobby back down to his feet. Out of the back comes stomping, "Big
Stupid" Danny Copeland and Jessica Stevens in the same attire from before.
Basher sees this and lets go of Bobby Stone. Basher begins talking trash to
"Big Stupid" as Bobby Stone comes from behind with a rollup.]
Commentator #1: 1...........2...........3.....Bobby Stone wins. The guy fellow wins the match with help from the manager and valet of XPW's franchise and future, "Big Stupid" Danny Copeland and Jessica Stevens.
Commentator #2: Haha! I love it! ["Big Stupid" Danny Copeland hops into the ring and lifts up Basher 7 feet high and drops him with a chokeslam. Jessica Stevens then rakes Bobby Stone in the eyes, runs to the ropes and does a hurricana rana, sending Bobby Stone over the ropes and out of the ring. "Big Stupid" Danny Copeland then lifts up Basher and powerbombs him. Jessica Stevens then slides out of the ring and brings in at chair as "Pretty Fly For A White Guy" by Offspring plays over the PA and from the front row of the crowd comes "Superstar" Sean Copeland. He walks cockily into the ring with a smirk on his face. He picks up Basher and double underhooks him into a DDT type position and hits him with his 6 Star DDT on the chair.]
Commentator #1: That's the 6 Star DDT! Oh my god! Commentator #2: The best signature move in wrestling today! ["Superstar" Sean Copeland asks for a mic and gets his wish granted as he speaks.] "Superstar" Sean Copeland: War of the Worlds is coming soon, so i thought i'd show my appreciation for all you fans by warming up a little and giving u guys a good time but all you do is boo me. All you fans can suck my cock for all i care.
[Tremendous heat comes from the crowd as Sean Copeland continues to speak.] "Superstar" Sean Copeland: Shut The Hell Up!! [More boos erupt from the crowd. "Superstar" Sean Copeland lets them die down before he speaks again.]
"Superstar" Sean Copeland: You know, i'm been sitting in my home, sitting in the crowd, sitting in the gym..........sitting, sitting, sitting, and finally i'm aloud to get up and fight and well, i'm fighting for 15 pounds of tin. Am I a lucky guy. Hellz no! Its the US Title I'm fighting for and well, that title means little but nothing to me. My eyes are focused on the most prestigious title there is to offer and thats the XPW Heavywieght Title. I'm go home with the XPW US Title but i won't be satisfied. I'm going into the match and kicking serious ass but who gives a flying rats ass. I bet Rob Dalton and Great
Southern Rapist do. I bet those guys are in there seats right now, picking there nose, thinking, "oh my god, oh my god, "Superstar" Sean Copeland is my idol. I don't want to fight him. I'm gonna get my ass kick." Because that is all there is to think and that is what will happen.
Commentator #2: This guy is the future of wrestling. "Superstar" Sean Copeland: I am the future, and the present of wrestling. Rob
Dalton and GSR are the past, but the past is the past and well, who gives a damn about the past. All anyone cares about is the New Aged
Icon.................the showstoppa...............the main eventer....................the high-priced ticket and all that represents myself. I'm the one that headlines. I'm the one that everyone talks about because i am the most controversial wrestlers to hit the ring hands down. Rob
Dalton, I canned your ass with that chair last War of the Worlds and I damn will do it again. Great Southern Rapist, I may not know anything about you but i know one thing about you. And well, your not me, so therefore, you aren't as good as me! On Friday, July 30, i'm coming to the XPW arena to kick ass the only way i know how to and thats hardcore style. And well Dalton, GSR all that matters is:
__ME__ __MYSELF__ __$__ __I!!!!!!__ "Big Stupid" Danny Copeland: Now hit his music!!! "Superstar" Sean Copeland: Wait!!! Danny, you will not talk unless spoken too!! I forgot one thing and that's: "H is for Hardcore, E is for Extreme, W is for
Wrestling but Sean Copeland stands for them all!"
Jessica Stevens: Now hit his music! "Superstar' Sean Copeland: Oh yeah!!!!! ["Pretty Fly For A White Guy" by Offspring plays over that PA as out leaves
Sean Copeland, Danny Copeland, and Jessica Stevens to the chorus of boos erupting throughout the crowd. The camera then fades to black.]
[The camera then comes on in the back as you see Sean Copeland, Danny Copeland, and Jessica Stevens beating the crap out of some guy. They leave as the guy is shown as Bobby Stone. The camera overhears Copeland.]
"Superstar" Sean Copeland: And you thought i would pay you dumb retard!!! Jessica Stevens: Hahaha!! Bastard. Sean, you are a genius!
