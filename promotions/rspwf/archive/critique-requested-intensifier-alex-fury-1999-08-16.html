[Critique Requested] "Intensifier" Alex Fury

16 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade into a scene in the back of a recent AWMC show. The camera moves through a relatively empty hallway, the lights dimmed. A single janitor sweeps the floor, as the camera turns down another alcove.
Finally, the camera reaches its intended destination. The locker room door. The camera man's hand comes into view, and the door slides open into a changing area. Crouched over a bench is none other than
"Intensifier" Alex Fury. His handsome face is graced with enigmatic topaz eyes, giving his embochure a certain light-heartedness. His square jaw is locked in a wry grin, the small scar upon it barely noticeable through the crimson light. His brown hair is cut short, slicked forward. The streams of golden locks running through his brown tendrils reflect off the glare of the lights. Graced upon his physique is his simplistic attire. Black wrestling tights, with matching elbow pads and knee pads is adorned upon his musculature, with wrist length black gloves covering his hands. The camera rotates around Fury, giving it better lighting. A few muffled tones can be heard, most reminiscent of a microphone being scratched. Then we hear the camera man's voice.]
CM: Mr. Fury... excuse me, Mr. Fury? [Fury looks up, staring straight at the camera. His gaze unwavering. Suddenly, unexpectedly, his face forms into a beaming smile.] AF: Well don't just stand there ya nut! Get your scrawny little behind in here. [The camera angle moves from the edge of the doorway up to a shot of
Fury's torso, from the waist up.]
AF: I'm expecting you didn't come in here to watch me undress, didja? [Fury moves his arm over his forehead, wiping off some of the perspiration eminating from his crown.]
CM: No, of course not. AF: You seem a bit nervous. CM: Sorry, I'm brand new here. [Alex cracks another smile, his pearly white teeth rebounding off what little light source is available in the diminutive room]
AF: Don't worry, I'm new here too. So what did they send you in here for? [The camera man clears his throat.] CM: I've actually been sent here to ask a few questions. Apparently you're the hottest thing since sliced bread in the AWMC. Everyone wants a piece of you, so they gave me the assignment to interview you.
AF: Well. Do you plan on asking me any questions? CM: Oooh, yes, sorry. So, what's your opinion on the talent roster in the AWMC? AF: In all honesty, and no disrespect to the good ones out there, I think the AWMC needs to see some new, fresh talent coming onto its roster. Of course, we have the great Hiroyoshi Takada who I actually met with while training in Japan. There are a couple other notables out there, but you must understand. I'm one of the greatest technical wrestlers in this business, and I just want to let everyone know, that there's a new source of energy in town. It's funny, because some of you actually expect to beat me! Now I love a good clean fight, but when it comes down to it, I'm one of the greatest professional wrestlers to ever grace the United States. Ask the AWMC why they hired me? Because I am "the" man. I can rip anyone to shreds, and tape them back together if they so wished to be.
CM: Those are some pretty strong words. Isn't that just being a little bit cocky? AF: Look, when it comes right down to it, in professional wrestling, you have to have attitude. You have to have an ego. I'm not saying
I'm egotistical, I'm just point out what I believe to be true! I've only been defeated once in my professional wrestling career. I don't plan to add any more numbers in the losses column. Oooh. And especially you "bad dudes." The guys who think it's "cool" to come in and take someone out from behind. Who think it's "cool" to break the rules. Let me tell you something. That isn't going to work on me, so you might as well back your bags and head on home. I know my way around the ring. I know how to avoid stuff like that. I don't let myself become a victim, I turn the tables and make you the victims.
Just remember, you cross the "Intesifier" and you're going to learn just how intense it can get.
CM: So what are your plans in the AWMC? AF: There's a couple things I'm lookin for. First things first, I need to owe some dedication to the fans all across the world. You guys make this sport what it really is. Major props to all of you sitting at home. And all of you know, especially if you're a fan of the Fury, that if there's something the Fury sets his mind on, the
Fury gets it. Right now, in fact, at this very second in time, there is one thing the Fury sees in these blue eyes. That thing is gold.
Particulary the type attached to a belt, which states AWMC Heavyweight
Title Belt. Now I plan to win my matches, I plan to work for it, and
I plan... No... In fact, I'll make a guarentee to everyone who is listening right now, that "Intesifier" Alex Fury, will come out on top. I'll do anything I have to get to that epitome of progression, the top of the mountain. When I reach it, and I will reach it, I'm going to show the world how a kid straight from the bottom can reach the top. I'm going to show everyone that anything is possible, as long as you put your mind to it.
CM: It sounds like you're ready for competition in the AWMC. AF: That's right. Everyone knows I am. So get ready, because when the Fury steps inside that ring, there's no turning back.
[Fade to black.] - Ryan Z [detail withheld]
