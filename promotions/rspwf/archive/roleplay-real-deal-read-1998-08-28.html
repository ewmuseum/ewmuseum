[Roleplay..."REAL DEAL"...Read!!!]

28 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is one of my latest flashes from ASCW [Nugmans fed] I just broke this character in and I got offers from GREAT feds before. Like the ICW and EWC before and a few moe apps. I just broke him like i said, and i think i did pretty good for my first Flash with him. Hell, might be next Anthony Gold!!
LoL!! Tell me what ya think...I join one fed...and looking for one or two more feds...so if ya like...drop me line or somethin' and I might join the fed. Most likely a good fed. Thanks and hope ya like...tell me what ya think...
[*Darkness. Television scenes are blank with complete darkness. Then arises with beams of light distancing from variety of angles maxium of 15 angleing into a round type loosened star. Also blaring out in many of multiple colors.
The star shape figure twirls clock-wise in a circle rotating so fast you're nearly color blind by the sight of color it's producing.It then rotates even faster and then the scene burst open with superstars fighting or shall i say brawling in a ring with with trash containers on fire with the ASCW logo in the middle. You see superstars such as "SIMPLY THE BEST" James Pillow brawling with Steve "MEGABUCKS" Woodthorpe...and Jack Vars battling with Headhunter.
Then scene then fades out into the ASCW Studio where you see Tim Beck and Brian
Schmaranzer at an rounded cut-off commentators booth and behind a large screen with footage of wrestlers sqquaring off with one another. The scene then fades to the back where capturing a few words from one Anthony Anderson...*]
[...Anderson stands in a darkened alley way with a sweat suit with the hood enclosed over his head. He stands there pressed up against the brick wall foot leant back and a cigarette dangling off the roots of his lips as he pulls out an lighter. He cuffs his hand blocking the wind as he bends himself a little to light the cigarette. It lights. And he follows by removing his lighter and despoiting it into his right pocket, he then takes a quick puff, exhales, turns his head to the left and looks down as he then begins to speak in a few words of wisdom*]
"REAL DEAL" Anthony Anderson: Ahhh, defeat! One of the many things in life that irritates me the most. I know I don't like defeat, as for everyone else i would image that you wouldn't like iit either. It doesn't matter what kind of defeat, defeat in losing a match or game, or defeat in self-confidence, or whatever the situation is. Although I did lose to Steve Woodthorpe, it was nothing but a giving victory to his satisfactory. I mean, I might have let that one slip away from under me, but now I have another chance, a chance to revenge my depth, my defeat as I even it with win aside it. But there's one way to accomplish that, and yet I say it won't be an easy victory. As I face someone similiar to me, someone who's wits are in compare to mine, and caliber is closing in on. A man of Lethal Injection, Drakon Hughes. I know this man personally and in a matter of sportsmanship. I know the whole ordeal he has been through in his lifetime.
I know everything if you want to say. I dont want to lie, but in my mind it's the truth. As i walk into that ring i see myself in control like a child in control of playing a video game, as a pilot in control of an Airplane. I lay my life on the line every now and then and I expect to get something out of it..a thrill, a championship, a moment of sucess...anything. I enjoy the pleasures of pain and winning. And it will be great enjoyment if I got an victory of my opponent, Hughes. Now one thing I know what I'm doing in the ring and I know what you're capable of. Of running your mouth that is...don't forget who your dealing with. Because this is the REAL DEAL ANTHONY ANDERSON that you speak about. Monday Night will feel like a ton of Nitro exploding and impacting upon the scene as I have you tap out with the Blue Light Special. Now thats one move that no one has mastered an escape from, except the man that stands before you.
My every intention is to drive you mad, get you a little pissed off. Thats my game plan, because when your distracted with all that anger it leaves an openning that drives you from what you've intend on doing or your gameplan. I know what you're thinking..that I'm some rookie that has no talent, well thats in your eyes and I'll be happy to clear that up for you. I'm the dream of
America and tha life of the ASCW. Things damn sure will heat up Monday night.
There's not a barrier in tha world that can hold me back next week. And Hughes, be prepared for something you can handle!!!
[*He then looks around and takes another puff of his cigarette and then tips the ashes down to flaske them off. And then take another puff. He pauses and starts again*]
"REAL DEAL" Anthony Anderson: You see, it seems tha more you open your mouth tha more it makes you look like a dumbass!! I mean every other thing you say is nothing but a lie. Why? because thats just you, you live a life more of deceit which got you imprisom with all those "so called" greatness impressions. You see, myself, more of an fighter who will fight what is right. Because I'm the
Real Deal and that speaks for itself. You getting a victory over me is like...umm...ummm, is like Satan hugging GOD...and that'll _NEVER_ happen, well maybe in a different lifetime. It's just impossible!!! And it's impossible for you to overcome my power. Look at me, a slim 233 pounds and standing a SeXy
6'2! Sure you might over weigh me but thats alright with me, like the odds against me. It wont make no difference....when Monday swings around it'll be like taking a look around in K-mart cause tha Blue Light Special will be ringing in and out of the arena. Well there not much else to say except to see you Monday Night at Heat...so til then I'm out!
[*He take yet another puff and exhales...he flips whats lefted of the cigarette on the ground and swashes it out with the tip of his shoe and then walks away.
the scene then starts to fade out*] --Handlers-- Mystikal [CNWL,NXW, IWF] REAL DEAL Anderson [ASCW, SooN ICW]
