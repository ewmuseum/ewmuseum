"The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie (Roleplay to critique)

27 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I don't know what you guys will think of it, but I think this is one of the better Roleplay's I've done in a long time. Send your opinions, rants, and other assorted goodies to
[detail withheld] This RP was done for the WWEF, for "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie's debut match. *Scene opens in a dark corridor. The camera casts light into the hallway, illuminating the only door in the hallway, at the far end of it. The camera makes it's way to the door, and you see the cameraman's hand reach out and grab the handle. He slowly opens it, and light suddenly floods the scene.
A huge ballroom, lit by hundreds of beautiful, crystal ceiling fans, is set in front of the camera. At the far end of the ballroom, sitting in a single chair, is the only living being, besides the cameraman, that is in the room.
Music plays over the speakers, System of a Down, as the man in the chair beckons the camera forward.
The man is wearing a black KoRn t-shirt, black jeans, and a silver anhk, which hangs from his neck, swinging back and forth as the man shifts. The man runs a single hand through his light brown hair, over his streak of blonde hair. His hair is cropped short, and his eyes dance in the light, changing from brown to hazel as the light catches his eyes. He smiles, shifting the brown goatee that adorns his face upwards, and begins to speak*
Man: Fans of the WWEF... let me introduce myself. I am "The TRUE Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie. And I have just signed with this wrestling organization. I hope I bring you years of enjoyment, and my opponents years of punishment, for I plan on staying here for quite a while. So get used to me.
Eventually, you're going to have to. You may be asking yourself, "Where did this guy come from?" Let me explain. I am orginally from Detroit, Michigan. I now reside in a much more... suitable living space in Grosse Pointe Farms, a very high class section of Michigan.
You see, I own my own business, which happens to have become quite successful, so I can afford myself "certain" luxuries. A glass of wine here. A new car there. And an estate that makes the White House look like low budget housing on the lower eastside.
So I splurge a little. I deserve it. I've been through more than any of you low grades will ever complain to your psychiatrists about. Pain. Abuse.
Mockery. I grew up in Detroit, one of the few whites in the entire city. I know more about survival than a tribal African nation. So I deserve everything
I get. Including the title that I have given myself.
You see, "The True Living Legend"... that's not some catchy name to hook you low grades onto my "gimmick". That is the plain and simple truth. I AM "The
True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie. And if you don't believe it, wait until this Monday. For this Monday, I make my WORLD professional wrestling debut.
Sure, I'm in the NACW, and the IWA, but they have yet to put me on a card yet. They're saving my talent for a later date, at which point they will exploit it for the highest rating possible. Whereas here, I am just another name. That's going to change. Quickly.
This Monday, I will compete in the ring with a man by the name of Aaron
Farner. First of all, I have never heard of this Farner character, nor do I really care who he is. My mission is solely the destruction of him, then my next opponent, and then the next. I don't care who you are, I have your number. 'Cause I'm just too damn good for any of you to handle alone.
By this point, the wrestling fans are probably wondering where I come off acting all high and mighty. Well, it's for the simple fact that I AM high and mighty. I deserve to OWN the world, not just exist in it. I deserve a world title shot THIS Monday, not a Monday down the road. But do I get it? No.
Instead I have to tear apart a LOW GRADE who only WISHES I could carry his ASS. Trust me, Mr. Outlaw. I have your number. And you're just a stepping stone that I have to walk over to achieve the legendary status in this sport that I so deserve. And Mr. Farner... go to the gym, work out, and TRY giving me a decent match.
*Blottie gets up and walks over to a booth. The music stops, there is silence, and then the sound of cymbals tapping starts to permeate the room. A few seconds pass, and a the chunky guitar riff that shatters the light tapping of the cymbals obliterates the room. Blind by KoRn plays loudly as Blottie walks out of the booth*
Blottie: As this music hits, Mr. Farner, know that the last few minutes of you calling yourself a "professional" wrestler will be over. I will take you to school. A school where pain is the subject. And you are the flunking student.
We shall see if you can hang with a legend, Mr. Farner. And I want you to watch carefully, Outlaw 3:16. Aaron Farner's doom is the same as your own. I will claim the WWEF world title as my own. I am "The TRUE Living Legend". And
I am aiming to prove it. Until next time...
*Blottie turns around and walks away from the camera, out another door on the far side of the room. The music is still blaring over the speakers as the camera FADES TO BLACK*
