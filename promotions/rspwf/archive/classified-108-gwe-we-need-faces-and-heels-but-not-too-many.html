[GWE] We Need Faces!!! (And heels, but not too many)

Feds Seeking Wrestlers · July 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Feds Seeking Wrestlers: [GWE] We Need Faces!!! (And heels, but not too many) My name is Thomas, and I am involved with the Global Wrestling Entertainment, which has just opened. It is apart of a new "interfed" alliance with Underground Championship Wrestling called Electric Wrestling Entertainment. And guess what? We would love to have you guys involved in GWE. We have just gotten off the ground, and need guys and gals fast to make GWE the number one. I've scouted the efeds of RSPWF (because I know the talent in RSPWF feds are top-class), and you guys have what we're looking for. If you need a description of what GWE is about, then I'd have to say it's all about good old wrestling entertainment like WWE (except without the necrophillia and stuff). We have interpromotional shows every month, and we also run our own show on a Wednesday called GWE Loaded. We are an Original Role-Play fed, meaning ORIGINAL CHARACTERS ONLY! We do not want The Rock or anyone like that. We want original, innovative, cool and awesome characters, backed up by wonderful, innovative, awesome rpers. If you reckon you want a piece of our action, then come to see us! We especially would love for some of you to team up and fight for the Tag Titles. Click the link below and you'll be directed to Global Wrestling Entertainment - Pure Wrestling Entertainment at it's best! PS: We need specifically more faces. Well, we'll accept heels as well, but we need faces sooner rather than later. Please, if you want a good EFED, join us!!!
