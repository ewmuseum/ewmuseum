[RP] Critique Requested -- "The Cobra" Ryan Sharp

3 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is an example of my work. I've used this roleplay as an introductory one in a number of federations. Tell me what you think.
[Darkness. A sound begins. Starting softly, then growing in strength. "Engel" by Rammstein is heard through the blackness. Then, a voice.]
VO: It's like a storm that you know is coming... [The screen explodes. Replacing it is a shot of a man. Short brown hair flows into his caucasian, bronzed skin. Massive, rippling muscles outline his back. The screen then explodes again, going to black. "Engel" continues to rumble.]
VO: He knows no fear... [We see the man we saw previously, hooking the arms of an opponent in a standing bodyscissors. The screen is then interspliced with another image, so the screen is cut vertically. The man now has his opponent's arms locked in a double-underhook.]
VO: He only knows how to give it... [The opponent is hoisted into the air, the image in slow motion now. Lightning, added for efect, takes shape around the man, who raises his opponent, and leaps into the air. The opponent is tucked in between the thighs of the man.]
VO: His opponents remember him by it... [The sound of thunder erupts as we see a slow motion replay of the man driving his opponent into the ground with a deadly, high impact
double-underhook piledriver.] VO: With fear comes pain... [The man is seen clutching another opponent, and delivering a huge punch to the jaw of his opponent. A quick close-up of his opponent's face reveals blood practically exploding from his nose.]
VO: With pain, comes anguish... [Another scene. The man, standing over a broken opponent, covered with blood. The man raises his foot to deliver a stomp to his opponent's face once again, but the screen goes to blackness again.]
VO: With anguish, comes defeat... [We hear the ringing of a bell, signaling the end of the match. We see multiple clips flying by the screen, all of them involving this man's hand being held up by an official.]
VO: And when you make him angry, there is no stopping him... [More clips. We see the man delivering a Uarange chokeslam through a table. Another scene, involving a chair shot to the head. And finally, and low blow followed by a huge, high impact Ace Crusher.]
VO: Take a good look at him, because you'll never forget him... [The camera begins a shot, directed at the boots of the man. The camera begins moving upwards, revealing a stylistic pair of Jushin
"Thunder" Liger style boots, reaching to his knees, bathed in blackness. The camera shot continues upwards, now showing massive, rippling thighs, and finally a pair of red and black tights. The camera continues to move upwars until we see a torso, stacked with well defined abs. A quick cut shows the hands of the man, covered in wrist length wrappings. Back to the body. We continue moving farther upwards, until we reach the chest. Massive pecs, and huge biceps and arms give him the look of a body builder. A quick cut to his face.
Deeply set, icy blue eyes. The remenants of a few days of not shaving. A five o'clock shadow. Brown hair, short, slicked back, but slightly messy.]
VO: This is a portrait of brutality. This is a portrait of your nightmare. His name... [Lettering suddenly appears over a black background.] VO: "The Cobra" [The lettering is replaced by another set.] VO: Ryan Sharp. ["Engel" dies down.] VO: Get ready. His poison is going to flow deep into your veins. The storm is coming. [Fade.]
