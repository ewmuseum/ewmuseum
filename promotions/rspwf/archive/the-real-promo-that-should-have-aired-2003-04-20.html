20 April 2003

NOTE: I gave that fed one last try... and they just fucked up. What you saw on that card was NOT representative of Frank and my work. It was cut severely short. Just like Frank's initial promo was completely butchered to start our angle. That promo being cut was the reason Frank and
I didn't work for our angle. We made that clear. That's what led to the
"shoot". That shoot then led to this angle... that just got fucked up
AGAIN. ANOTHER dropped ball.
THIS is the promo that was meant to air. I'm not quitting over it right this second... but I'm fucking close to. <After the match...> Carter: WHAT A MATCH! WHAT A DAMN MATCH! Diamond: That wasn't a match! That was a DOUBLE SUICIDE!!! Carter: And the cage is going up!  Diamond: My God... this may be the end of BOTH careers!  Carter: And down the aisle comes Junya Kontani to check on his partner, Frank Anthony.  Diamond: There's nothing left to check on! He's dead!  Carter: He's not DEAD!  Diamond: And now there's Nick Demola holding onto the bottom rope!  Carter: He's getting to his feet!  Diamond: He's on a knee... and CALLING FOR A MIC???  Carter: Nick Demola is calling for a mic! What the HELL FOR???  Diamond: To tell the fans to kiss his ass one last time!  Carter: Well, Demola has the mic. Do we have the delay on?  DEMOLA: YOU MOTHERF<bleep>ER!  [Kontani shoves Demola backwards and grabs the microphone from him.]  KONTANI: Nick, you big fat slut!!! [HUUUUUUUUUUUUUUGE POP!!!!]  KONTANI: GO HO YOUSELF OUT TO SOME OTHA FRED!!! [TITANIC, EPIC, HUUUUUUUUUUUUGE POP!!!]  Carter: YES! TELL HIM, JUNYA!  [Kontani throws the mic down and kneels down next to Frank.]  Diamond: Who the hell does this guy think he is???  [Demola wipes some blood off his face, and picks up the microphone.]  DEMOLA: You think I'm an <bleep>hole, huh?  [Demola points at the crowd.]  DEMOLA: AND ALL OF YOU DO TOO, HUH??? [HUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUGE POP!!!]  DEMOLA: Well... don't believe everything you read.  Carter: Don't believe everything you read? I think Demola just showed his true colors in this match! He just tried to KILL HIS OWN BROTHER, FOR GOD'S SAKES!
[Demola shakes his head.]  DEMOLA: You wanna fight me? You wanna defend your partner's honor?  [Demola steps up, his steps shaky, and gets in Junya's face.]  DEMOLA: YOU WANNA FIGHT ME? BRING IT, SLOPE!  Carter: Now THAT'S going too far!  DEMOLA: BRING IT, YOU MENTALLY RETARDED SLOPE!  [Demola stands in Junya's face.]  Carter: NAIL HIM, JUNYA! HIT HIM!  [Demola looks back to Frank, who is just starting to get to a knee.]  DEMOLA: It's time to stop the bullsh<bleep>!  Carter: Time to what? Who's he talking to?  Diamond: He's talking to Junya! He's telling him to fight!  Carter: I think he's talking to Frank! DEMOLA: Frank... it's time to stop the bullsh<bleep>. [Junya looks over to Frank, who just shrugs and points at Demola as he gets to his feet.] DEMOLA: Come on, Frank! For years you've called yourself "Frank Anthony". You've denied our name.
It's time to show the world what you're made of. [HEEEEEEEEEEEEL POP!!!!] Carter: What? DEMOLA: It's time to show that you're my flesh and blood... Carter: What? DEMOLA: It's time to show, once and for all, that you... ... are a Demola. [HEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEL POP!!!] CROWD: KICK HIS ASS, FRANK!!! KICK HIS ASS!!! Carter: Demola's delusional! That fall must have jarred his brain! [Nick throws the mic to Frank... and smirks.] Carter: What IS this? [Frank shakes his head as Junya looks at him with a confused glare.] ANTHONY: Don't buh'leave't Junyah...ya've done heard dis sawng an' dance befo'. [HUUUUUUUUUGE FACE POP!!!] Carter: Come on! Frank and Junya can end it all right now! [Junya leans into the mic.] KONTANI: WE GO KICK ASS NOW!!! [HUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUGE FACE POP!!!] ANTHONY: Yes, Junya. Carter: Wait. Where'd the accent go? ANTHONY: Yes, Junya. We kick ass. [Demola charges... ... HUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUGE SHOCKED HEEL POP!!!!!! ...] Carter: OH MY GOD!!! DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE BY FRANK AND NICK ON JUNYA KONTANI!!! Diamond: YES! YES! Carter: What the HELL IS THIS??? [Kontani lays on the mat as Frank and Nick put the boots to him!] Carter: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON HERE?????? Diamond: I don't know! [Junya lays on the mat as the fans give a HUUUUUUUUUUUUUGE HEEL POP!!!! Nick and Frank share a smirk... and lift Junya to his feet.]
Carter: What the HELL??? Diamond: THEY'RE WORKING TOGETHER! THEY'RE WORKING TOGETHER! Carter: Now Nick and Frank have Junya over their heads... and are CLIMBING THE TOP TURNBUCKLE WITH
HIM!!! Diamond: YES! YES! END THAT RETARD'S CAREER!!! Carter: Nick and Frank on the top rope with Junya! Diamond: THIS IS IT, STAN! THIS IS IT! Carter: They lift Junya... ... CRRRRRRRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAASH!!!!!!!!! ... HUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUGE HEEL POP!!!!!! Carter: JESUS GOD ALMIGHTY!!! THEY JUST THREW JUNYA KONTANI OFF THE TOP ROPE AND INTO THE CROWD!!! Diamond: AND HE LANDED ON THE CONCRETE!!! JUNYA LANDED NECK FIRST ON THE CONCRETE!!! Carter: THIS IS SICK! THIS IS SICK! WHAT KIND OF AN EXPLANATION CAN THESE MEN POSSIBLY HAVE FOR THIS??? Diamond: WE'RE ABOUT TO FIND OUT!!! [Demola grabs the microphone as the crowd just throws a BARRAGE of cups and trash into the ring at Nick and
Frank!] N. DEMOLA: If there's one thing I've learned in this game... ... it's that the IDIOTS will believe anything you tell them! [HUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUGE HEEL POP!!!!!] N. DEMOLA: Consider yourselves f<bleep>cked with. [Nick hands the mic to Frank.] F. DEMOLA: No more hokey promos. No more stupid accents. No more gimmicks. Cause I've got the only gimmick I _need_. My last name. I... am Demola! "BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" Diamond: Look at Frank take in all those boos! Can you hear that, Carter? Carter: This...this is CRAP! F. DEMOLA: I AM A _DEMOLA_... AND I'M PROUD OF IT! "BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" F. DEMOLA: I AM A DEMOLA...AND YOU'RE JUST JEALOUS! [Nick smirks and takes the mic back as the crowd gives a HUUUUUUUUUGE HEEL POP and chants "YOU SOLD OUT!" at
Frank.] N. DEMOLA: What does it mean to be a Demola? It means being smarter than everyone one of you IGNORANT SH<bleep>TS IN THE CROWD! It means getting you to buy a show, just so you can see what would happen... then
SCREWING YOU ALL OVER! Just for sh<bleep>ts and giggles. [Frank takes the mic.] F. DEMOLA: It means being the DEFINITION of greatness! Only LEGENDS can carry this last name. AND BY "LEGEND"...I DON'T MEAN CALLING YOURSELF A "LEGEND" AFTER BEATING SOME OVER THE HILL HERMIT COWBOY! [Nick takes the mic.] N. DEMOLA: It means being the most evil sons of b<bleep>ches to ever set foot in a ring. More evil than Mike Bilodeau. More evil than Mark Rosendorf.
[Frank takes the mic.] F. DEMOLA: And in the coming weeks, we're going to RANSACK the tag teams of the WLW...Sound familiar? Well, we're DEE...MOE...LAS. We _will_ own this place! 'Cause NOBODY...NONE OF YOU PEOPLE IN THIS
F<bleep>ING PLACE _DESERVES_ TO DRAG YOUR DOUBLE A, BUSH LEAGUE ASSES DOWN THE SAME _AISLE_ THAT WE DO...BE YOU A CAMERAMAN, A "WRESTLER," OR THE PRESIDENT OF THIS COMPANY! [Nick takes the mic.] N. DEMOLA: Because we are... The Men who just put ALL OF YOUR ASSES IN THOSE SEATS. We are The Myth, the epitome of greatness that all other tag teams in this place will look at with fear. The Legends... the men who have done everything in this business, except dominate it together.
[Frank winks as he speaks.] F. DEMOLA: Dominate? Come on Nick...this is "Dubbya-El-Dubbya"...huh yuck...huh yuck. "Dominating" isn't a factor here. Cause Nick...when you're a DEMOLA... You're bigger than Mike Bilodeau. You're bigger than WLW. And you...and I... [Frank points to Nick, and then himself repetitively and aggravatingly.] F. DEMOLA: _WE_ ARE THE ALPHA AND OMEGA OF THIS _DAMN_ SPORT! Because Kings...they come and go. But WE'RE DE-MO-LA...and there is just ONE word to describe being a Demola... "BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!" F. DEMOLA: GODLY. [Frank throws the mic at the crowd as they CONTINUE TO BARRAGE THE RING WITH GARBAGE!
