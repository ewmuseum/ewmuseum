Roleplay Critique

8 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens up at a local Wendy's Fast Food Resturant. We see the dining room, surrounded by brick walls, and large windows, which shows off the urban world out- side of it. The sun shining, as a commercial road is filled with traffic, the Wendy's sign standing tall, calling in customers. Inside the dining room, sits numerous tables, maybe twenty in all, as they sit there only three or four feet apart from each other, at least half of them, filled with customers. The red carpet, sets off the room's colors, as the wall clock comes into view, showing that it's near 2:30 in the afternoon.  Then we head over to the front counter, by two registers, a drink machine, and where a customer stands, talking with one of the employee's....The employee is wearing the manditory black dress pants, and a Wendy's tee shirt, with a Wendy's hat, containing several pins and stickers on it. He is also wearing a gold chain, containing a golden emblem of a Pita, which are availible at your local Wendy's. The employee is partic- ulary in good shape, with large shoulders, biceps, and arms in all.]   Customer: Okay I'll take a...   [The employee inturupts the customer...]   Employee: Is that for HERE, or to GO sir?   Customer: .....for here....I'll have a....   [The employee once again inturupts!]   Employee: Would you like to try are VEGGIE GARDEN PITA today sir? You can get the combo for a mear $3.15.   Customer: NO...I will have a number six, biggie size, with a.....   Employee: Would you like Coke with that sir?   Customer: .........with a Dr. Pepper....please.....   Employee: Would you like to Biggie Size tha....   Customer: YES...I already stated I would like to biggie size that.   Employee: ......Sir.....I dont appreciate you getting an attitude with me,
I'm attempting to provide you with the best meal possible, and would appreciate some respect from someone like yourself. So....that's a Spicy Chicken combo meal, Biggie
Fries, and a Biggie Dr. Pepper correct?   [The Customer then nods, as he is searching through his wallet]   Employee: Your total comes to $4.56.   [The Customer looks up with a shocked look]   Customer: Four fifty six!? Are you friggin nuts?   Employee: Sir...one more outburst, and I'm going to have to ask you to leave. Wendy's provides you with top quality food, and service. Now if you want to get a meal for a buck fifty, with food that's been urinated, and has contained baul movements from Africa, please
there's a McDonalds about two miles from here. But if you want food that is close to per- fection, then just cough up the cash, okay?   [The Customer reluctantly hands over the money, giving away a crisp five dollar bill.]   Employee: Alright...forty four cents is your change.   [The employee hands over the change....]   Employee: It will be up in a minute sir...   Customer: What's you name son?!   Employee: My name....Dave....Dave Bean, why?   Customer: I've never been so insulted before in my life. I just want you to know, that if this meal isn't thee best damn meal I've ever had, that I'm coming back, and your going to hear it, okay?   [Dave Bean gives a discouraged, yet mean look to the customer. He then leans over the counter, and whispers into the customers ear.]   Dave Bean: *Whispering* Sir, if I wasn't "thee best damn" Wendy's employee, and if I wasn't on the clock, I would completely beat you to the little chicken nugget that you are, so please refrain from ever tempting me again, while I'm at work...   [Another employee brings the food to the tray, as Dave Bean then hands it over.]   Dave Bean: There you go...sir....have a great day, and ENJOY your meal.   [Dave Bean then see's that he's clear up front, and heads to the back for a short break so he can cool down. Once in the back, he looks at the bulletin board, and yet notices a short memo to him from the entire Wendy's crew, wishing him luck in the EWA, and at his upcoming matchup.]   Dave Bean: I wonder if I'm doing the right thing. This is my home...Wendy's is my home. But, wow...professional wrestling. What a neat thing to do. I thought getting to work drive-
thru was heavenly, but now....getting to run in the squared circle, getting to do the same thing that people like Stone Cold Austin Goldberg, and Hollywood Savage, and who can forget "The Best there is, The Best there was, the Best there ever will be", Trent "The Fatman" Tart....or something like that. Ohh... King Hardcore Hamed...you just dont know what your going up against. I'm a three time, state wide, EMPLOYEE OF THE MONTH SON. You aint never seen nothing like this before.....I'm a mean green, Pita stuffin, hamb urger making, meat flipping machine.   [The manager calls up for Bean to get back on line...]   Dave Bean: Untill then Hamed....Prepare to get Driven, straight into the
Veggie Garden, courtesy...of the WENDY'S WORKAH, DAVE BEAN.   [Bean then hops up, and runs back to his position.]   [Scene fades.]
