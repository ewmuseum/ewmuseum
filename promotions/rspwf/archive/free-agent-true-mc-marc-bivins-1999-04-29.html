Well folks it's time for me to go on the FA Market again. My favorite character of all time, Marc Bivins needs a new home. I took to long posting this the first time, and I was disconnected from the newsgroup for inactivity, because
I wrote-up most of his history. Well, here it is. All I ask is that you send me your fed's application and FAQ, and not to reply to this message simply with a link.
Thanks, Casey Handler of "True MC" Marc Bivins Part I: Personal info ===================== •Your Name: [name withheld] •E-Mail Address: [detail withheld] Part II: Basic Wrestler Info ============================ •Wrestler Name: "True MC" Marc Bivins •Real Name: Marcus Bivins •Hometown: New York City, New York •Date of Birth: 5-20-75 •Height: 6'3 •Weight: 225 •Build: Athletic •Heel/Face/Neutral: Extreme Baby Face •Gimmick: Former Rapper, turned wrestler. After his Sophmore Album entitled
"Hit Em Up" went Double Platinum, he claimed he needed nothing more to prove in the rap game. He finished up a 24 City Tour. After that he seperated all ties with rap, save a few guest performances on several rappers albums. Wanting more that what he had accomplished, he searched for a new career to pursue. After watching some vintage and newer wrestling tapes, he knew what he wanted to go after. He searched for a school that would train him. And after a month or two of searching, he found it. The World of Hurt Wrestling School in Upstate NY.
After training, he spent a while in the indy leagues, and grew a huge fan base since he was already a Superstar in Rap. Finally, he got his big break and was approached by Bradley Waubash of BRAWL. He was signed for a deal, and wrestled exclusively with the big name fed. When BRAWL went under, he went back and wrestled a few stints in Indy leagues, but couldn't get the feel back since he had a taste of the big leagues. Now he searches for that big league to wrestle in once again.
•Basic Appearance Description: He is a black male, with fairly light skin. He has an athletic build. His hair is very short in the faded style. In his left ear he has a gold hoop earring. On his left bicep, he has a microphone tatoo.
On his right, MC is tatooed in elabotate lettering. He also has a basic thorn tatoo going all the way around his right mid forearm.
•Attire: He has a few outfits. #1(Most Common): He wears a somewhat baggy yellow t-shirt with the sleeves cut off (Where it's cut is not raggedy though). On the shirt it has his name on the front in black lettering(The same lettering as his tatoo). On the back of the shirt it says "Hit 'Em Up Tour". Madison Square Garden, Friday April 26th 1996.
He also wears baggy yellow jeans. On the jeans it has Bivins down both sides in black. He wears plain black Nike Sneakers on his feet. To the ring, he wears his famous thick gold chain with a charm that reads "MC" (Also in that same lettering) He also wears yellow head fitting Oakley Sunglasses, and a backwards
NY Yankees Cap. (Everything after the sneakers he removes in the ring)
#2(Less Common, Wears Every Few Weeks): He wears a black t-shirt with the sleeves cut off. It has MC in white covering the whole front of the T-Shirt. On the back, it too says "Hit 'Em Up Tour". But this time it says Pepsi Arena,
Albany, New York. Friday, February 23rd, 1996, all in white. He has baggy black jeans with Bivins down both sides in white, and his black Nike's. He has on his gold chain with charm, black Oakleys and a black backwards NY Yankees Cap.
#3(PPV's ONLY): He wears a baggy orange T-Shirt with his name on the front in black. (Similar to #1). On the back, however, it says the PPV Name, it's date,
City and Arena (Like his other shirts, just customized for the PPV at Hand).
His jeans are yellow with Bivins down the sides in black, and his black sneakers. He wears his gold chain and charm, Orange Oakleys, and a backwards
Orange NY Yankees Cap.

Similar to the Superkick, just to the chest. And also, he will sometimes use the Platinum Hit to pin opponents Part V: Ring Entrance ===================== •Entrence Music: "Watch for the Hook" by Cool Breeze •Entrance Description): I like to send in introductions for every match he wrestles in, so you'll most likely get a different one before every card.
