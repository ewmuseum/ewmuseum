[New Character] "The Imperil" Janus King (Feedback Wanted)

I created a new character and was looking for some feedback on him... [WRESTLERS INFO] -Wrestlers Name: "The Imperil" Janus King -Height: 6'4 -Weight [230 is CW Limit]: 280 -Hometown: Washington, D.C -Hair Color: Black (Long and stringy) -Eye Color: Brown -Ring Attire: Full length black latex pants with the word "Imperil" written down each leg in navy-blue writing. He wears black boots with the initials "JK" on them in navy blue writing as well. Around his left arm, he wears a black "Body-Glove" arm-pad. (To the ring, he wears a chrome half-face mask. He takes it off to wrestle though.) -Build: Good build. Like that of Kanyon. -Ring Style: Submission - Technical -Gimmick/Attitude/Personality: He digs up dirt on people, then makes them pay. He is a nice rouded neutral, so he can dig up either heels or faces. -Wrestlers Background: His background remains as a mystery to all of us. The only thing we know is he has inside information on _everyone_! - Theme Song]: "Sad But True" by Metallica -Describe Your Wrestlers Entrance to the ring in full: [The lights fade out as "Sad But True" by Metallica begins to blare over the P.A.] ROBERT BOWEN- About to come down the aisle ... He hails from the rolling hills of Washington, D.C... He stands at 6'4" and weighs in at 280 lbs... Here is... "THE IMPERIL" JANUS KINNNNNNNNNG!!!!!!!!! [A dark blue spotlight suddenly hits the entrance as an eery looking King is already standing there. He is wearing black boots, black latex pants, a black arm-pad and around the left portion of his face is his trade-mark chrome half-mask. He begins to make his way down the aisle as white smoke shoots out from boths side of the ramp in front of him. He walks through all of the smoke with fans reaching out to touch him. He disregards all of the fans' reactions to him. As he gets to the ring, he slides under the ropes. Smoke shoots up from every ring-post as King removes his mask and gets ready for his match.] Then remove all the ___ above and then you are done! -Taunts: He stands over his opponent and looks directally into the camera. -Set-Up Move: Kick to the back off the knee-cap -Description of Set-Up Move: --- -Finisher: The Last Impression -Description of Finisher: Dragon sleeper with a body scissors -Please list 10-15 moves your wrestler performs: 1 - Sharpshooter 2 - Spike DDT(Gangrel's finisher) 3 - Tornado DDT 4 - Piledriver 5 - Powerbomb 6 - Half nelson slam 7 - Northern Lights Bomb 8 - Boston Crab 9 - Gut-wrench Piledriver 10 - Sleeper 11 - Hangman's Neckbreaker 12 - Double-underhook suplex 13 - Superkick (Usually hits it on the bigger guys) 14 - Brainbuster 15 - STF -Las Handles: Robbie Kincade - 4/2/0 - VRW "Judge" Eric Justice - 0/2/0 - VSW Scotty Drake - 7/2/0 - Free Agent - L.A.W Pacific Coast Champion Forever
