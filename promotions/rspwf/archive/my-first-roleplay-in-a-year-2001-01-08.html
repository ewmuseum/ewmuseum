My first roleplay in a year. .

8 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[-The scene opens up in front of Woodland Hills Correctional felicity. It seems to be a Level four Lock Down prison. The walls are about 15 ft high draped with barb-wire.-]
[-The camera switches angles and you see a Black stretch limo pull up to the front gate. The gate opens up and the limo drives through. The Limo parks and the driver gets out and runs around to the back of the car and opens the door and none other than Michael D. Taylor steps out with a black leather briefcase in his hand. He is wearing a Black harmony suit with some black leather cowboy boots and a brown cowboy hat.-]
[-Taylor begins to walk across the parking lot into the main building. He enters a little room with a secretary in it. She is behind what looks like 6ft of Plexiglas.
Taylor walks up to the glass and begins to talk-] Michael D. Taylor: "Yeah I have a meeting with Bill the warden. We have some serious business to discuss."
The Secretary: Is your name Michael Taylor. Michael D. Taylor: Yes Mam it is. The Secretary: Well then come right in. He has been waiting on you. [-The Secretary Picks up a two way radio and pushes the side button.-] The Secretary: Umm Bill Michael D. Taylor is here to see you should I just send him in. [-She lets go of the button and through some fuzzy discussion you here send him on in. The Secretary pushes a button and a large slab of the Plebe glass slides open and Michael steps inside. She takes Michael through an old beaten hall way. The walls are all worn and you can see water stains on the cement walls until they get to the end of the hall where there is a huge wooden door. The name plate on the door has
Warden written across it in big bold letters. the secretary opens the door and Michael D. Taylor walks right in as she shuts the door behind him.-] [-You see a large a wooden desk and all kinds of plaques on the walls behind it. There is a large man sitting in a white leather chair behind the desk. He is wearing Cowboy hat, button down shirt blue jeans and a belt buckle that almost covers his whole stomach. The mans name tag says Bill and below that it says warden.-]
[-Taylor slings the brief case on the table and opens it up. Inside it has rolls and rolls of money. All the top bills are nothing but hundreds. On top of the cash is a contract.-]
Michael D. Taylor: "One Hundred Thousand dollars. That is what you asked for ain't it." Bill: "Well, son I would imagine it is all there so let's get down to business. You want one of my inmates to come and wrestle in your federation and you are paying me for the lowlife."
Michael D. Taylor: "Sir he is one of the original greats. He has been locked up for well over a year now and he only has a few months left. You can hold him in your felicity until he gets done with his time I just would like for you to bring him to my shows and let him perform. You also have to also allow my reporters to interview him from time to time."
Bill: "Hmmm, Well sir you my friend have a deal?" [-Michael picks up the contract and hands it to bill. Bill takes the contract and signs it.-] Michael D. Taylor: "When can I speak to him?" Bill: "Anytime you feel like it." Michael D. Taylor: "Can you set me up a place to meet with him." [-Bill picks up the two way radio. He presses the button and say's-] Bill: "Can you please bring prisoner 4860-468 to holding cell 3 to meet with a friend of mine."
[-Through a very distorted fuzz you here yes sir right away.-] Bill: "Michael he is not the same person as you remember him as. This place changes people. But anyway come with me."
[-Bill gets out of his seat. Bill and Michael begin to walk through the prison the head through three or four security gates. Down a small hall way. Until they reach huge locked steel door.
Bill unlocks the door and Michael opens it and walks in.-] [-The room is extremely small. In the center of the room there is a small wooden table with a pack of cigarettes sitting in the center of the table.
Michael takes a seat and shortly after you here the door being unlocked and it swings in and in walks a man who is about 6'2" 235 pounds. He has long black hair. It seems to be in poor condition. He has a scar across his left cheek right below his eye. He is dressed in all orange.
The man looks at Michael and lets off a slight smirk on his face. The man takes seat.-] Michael D. Taylor: "Well, if it is none other than my favorite, Mitch "Sure
Fire" Gatlin. Look what this place has done to you."
Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin: "Now Michael I have been locked up for well over a year now and you have not even came to visit me one time I know you are not here to be my friend what in the hell are you up to."
Michael D. Taylor: "Mitch you know me all to well. I am here to get you back into the business. I have everything set up. I have it arranged for you to be able to come to my shows and perform and they are allowing me to send camera crews to see you. The C.W.A needs someone like you. Don't get me wrong we have some exceptional talent. But we have not had someone like you come around are parts. So what do you say."
Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin: "You my friend have a deal? I have been in my cell dreaming about when I can step back into the game. I am better than I have ever been. All I do is train and fight. You have to fight to survive in here.
Fight to eat, fight to take a shower, fight to even take a shit. If you are looking for someone who is going to hurt somebody. Then I am your man. I am ready for someone to test me. Michael I want you to pick out the biggest baddest wrestler in your ranks. In a no DQ match."
Michael D. Taylor: "Mitch you got it, You will face Joe Snow in a no D.Q. match up. That is if you can handle it." Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin: "I can handle anything. So this goes out to the entire C.W.A Especially Jow Snow Step up and get your knees dirty because there is a new top dog in town."
[-The Scene Fades as the door opens and a guard takes Mitch back to his cell.-]
