[XBWL - Critque/Roleplay] Jack Diamond

10 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Since he's kinda pissed at me (see Monday Mayhem card), what better time to publicize him in RSPWF? Hey, he could be your World Title contender instead of our misused talent soon...
Jack Diamond, [detail withheld] , was the reigning International champion at this time of this interview, heading to a matchup versus Brandon Thornton
(in a match that ended up delayed till Monday.)
Feel free to critique on the group...I'll pass it on to him.) ---- Scene: (The fans of the Xtremely Brutal Wrestling League roar as they await a returning interview of the XBWL International Champion "Sexy Boy" Jack
Diamond. Diamond has been enjoying a lucious vacation in his home away from home New York City. Diamond is going to have a tougher match against
Thornton due to his lack of training. Diamond is ready though. He is going to give the fans a huge show in the Chain Match against Brandon Thornton on
Sunday at Summer Sizzler 99'. The fans have signs and tee-shirts for both
Jack Diamond and Brandon Thornton and they are ready for a top quality interview like only Jack Diamond can give. The fans erupt when "That Don't
Impress Me" by Shania Twain blares over the loudspeakers as "Sexy Boy" Jack
Diamond enters the ring with Lexy as his arm trophy. The fans boo once he comes into view. Diamond has on a "Jack Diamond:Egotistical?" tee-shirt, a pair of his customized wrestling tights, a pair of black wrestling boots, a solid gold necklace, a pair of black leather fingerless gloves, elbowpads, and a pair of Oakley sunglasses. He has the XBWL International Championship
Title around his waist. The fans boo as he enters the ring with Lexy in tow.
He grabs a microphone and begins to speak.) "Sexy Boy" Jack Diamond:"The Future" Brandon Thornton, you really SUCK!!! I mean, you suck baddddd. You think that just because I take a little vacation that I am going to be thrown off my game for our match up? Bull(CENSORED). I am as ready as ever for our chain match. In fact I use the chain a lot at home with Lexy. I am ready and trust me, I know how to use a chain. You call yourself "The Future", well how far into the future will this name come into effect? The last time you did something important was when you won this belt from SeVeRe Pain in February. As far as I am concerned you are "The Past"
Brandon Thornton. While J.B.Destiny is "The Has Been". I am the true "Futresent". Does that make sense? It should because I am the future and the present. I am making a huge impact on XBWL competition now and I will make an even bigger one in the future. I am the XBWL's very own Triple Crown
Winner. The crown is over my head. I am the king of XBWL. Brandon, you say I was a pitiful North American Champion. Well then why am I the longest reigning North American Champion of all-time? Why I am the second man to capture the North American Title three times? As far as I, and the rest of these people, I am the greatest North American Champion there ever was. You held that thing for about three seconds. Thornton, I am the greatest
International Champion of all time, with or without my defenses. It's only a matter of time before I humble you. Because this is not YOUR belt. It is my belt. The nameplate says "Jack Diamond". Does it say Brandon Thornton? No. I rest my case.
(Fans boo. Some renegade fans cheer. Diamond continues.) "Sexy Boy" Jack Diamond:Being the best is great Brandon. Maybe someday in your dreams or fantasies you'll know how it feels. How it feels to be the greatest thing going on God's green earth. However you'll never know how it feels to be God's gift to women. Not even in your dreams. As far as you becoming the International Champion after Summer Sizzler. That my friend is a dream in it's own right. There is no way I would ever lose to you. The degrading thought makes me shiver. The greatest International Champion of all time could lose to a poor excuse for mid-card talent like Brandon
Thornton? Thank God, it could never happen. You see Brandon, the things that seperate you and I, money, power, sex appeal, fame, ya know, all the things
I have and you don't. Really cramp your image, your ability, and your resemblance of Al Gore. Now ya see, if you were to beat me at Summer
Sizzler, I wouldn't respect you. I would never respect a peice of dirt like you. A man who changes loyalties every five seconds and a man who constantly relies on insulting The Beast to fill up his interview space. It's time a real man, such as myself, shut you up. Your forgetting that I have done a lot more in XBWL than you ever will. You have forgotten that I am the XBWL.
You have forgotten that I am the most talked about superstar of all time. You have forgotten that I am truly unbeatable when I am at one hundred percent, you have forgotten all those things and they will cost you more than the match at Summer Sizzler. It's gonna be payback and everyone in this arena knows that payback is certainly a (CENSOREd)itch.
(Diamond pauses and then continues to speak.) "Sexy Boy" Jack Diamond:Matt Austin. Matt, you've really got an oppurtunity the night after Summer Sizzler. I am wishing you a lot of luck against
Beast. Ya know why? Because if you win the XBWL Hardcore Championship at
Summer Sizzler, you can't compete for the XBWL World Heavyweight Title the next night. Simply saying that you can't hold two belts at once. Unless you want the embarassment of forfeiting your title and become the most disgracfull champion of all time. I would like to see how this pans out. But until then. I am just the greatest thing going on God's green earth. God's gift to women. Hit the music that reminds me of me.
("That Don't Impress Me Much" by Shania Twain blares as "Sexy Boy" Jack
Diamond and Lexy leave.)
