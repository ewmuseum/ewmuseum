16 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have posted a message here a couple days ago and got tons of feedback. Thank you all for your interest in my character. But I am still undecided on which one to join. I had picked out two that I was very interested in. Since then, I have been scouting out both federations. I realized that one was way above my roleplaying abilities. So I decided not to join it. I asked one of the staff members of the other if its members roleplayed a great deal. And to this minute, I have not gotten an answer. So I repost this message to you all.
I am the handler of Dark Wolf. I just quit Fantasy Pro Wrestling as I am interested in making a permanent commitment to a federation. I have some demands that MUST ALL be met before I join your federation. Please read these before emailing me.
The federation MUST be booked. I won't accept any simmed federations. The current roster of the federation MUST be at the same level of role playing level as myself. A sample roleplay will be included below.
There must be a MAXIMUM of 50 members, including myself, in the federation. Tag teams controled by one person MUST be allowed. This is important as
I may choose to run a tag team.
The federation MUST have been around for at LEAST six months before I join. Roleplays MUST be done on message boards. I will not join any federations that roleplay using email and mailing lists.
Minor swearing such as that used by Stone Cold Steve Austin IS ALLOWED. There is one card weekly. Preferably on the weekend. Also, one PPV per month. The federation MUST have all original wrestlers. No ripoffs either. Meaning, I don't want to see a roster that has wrestlers that use parts of a real wrestler's name.
SAMPLE ROLEPLAYS Here is a sequence of roleplays my character Dark Wolf has done for a dying federation. These roleplays, and their introductions, can also be found The Official Dark Wolf Web Site at
http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/lodge/9866 FIRST ROLEPLAY Here I was just starting out in the PCW. I wanted to introduce myself and make a challenge at the same time. So that's basically what I did.
---------------------- Camera rolls showing a graphic of a wolf. It looks very similar to the
1998 Turner Cup championship Chicago Wolves team logo. Below it is the caption, "Dark Wolf." Slowly the graphic fades out. Dark Wolf replaces it. He stands at 6'7" and weighs about 280 lbs. He wears his traditional black pants and boots. A tattoo can barely be seen on his left shoulder.
Dark Wolf speaks. Let me introduce myself. I ... am Dark Wolf. You may be wondering why I am giving this interview but am not yet on the roster. Well, let me clarify that for you. I recently sent in my application to join this federation. And not long after, it was acknowledged by the President of this company. And he said I should give an interview introducing myself.
A couple of months ago, I was on the payroll of a federation known as
Windy City Wrestling. And I wasn't just any old Joe looking for a fight.
I earned the status of Hardcore Extreme champion by defeating the champ who, until facing me, was undefeated. I believe his record was 6-0.
Later, I gave him a rematch. And won that as well. Then, a couple weeks later, myself and a buddy of mine, Crazy Horse, won the Tag Team titles after defeating all opposition in a tag tournament.
Why am I telling you this you say. Because I want to warn you never to underestimate me. I was champion until Windy City Wrestling folded. I proved myself there. And now, I've come here to prove myself again. You can count on it that I'll be going for that PCW Extreme title. And I'll win it too. As soon as Crazy Horse joins up (he's still undecided byt I think he'll join), the two of us are going for that Tag Team title.
But until then, I'm issuing an open challenge to anyone in this federation. Anyone. But not just some boring old traditional match.
Let's make it a Street Fight. So what do you all say? You up for the challenge? Then I'll see you Monday.
Dark Wolf turns and walks out of the view of the camera's view. The camera fades out. Another screen takes it's place. The wolf image is there but in front of it is a caption. "Copyright, 1999, Dark Wolf
Productions." Cut to commercial.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECOND ROLEPLAY With this one, I had just checked the updated roster to see if I had been added yet. I was and so was someone else called Moon Wolf. I decided to make a feud with him and that's what sparked this roleplay.
------------------------ Camera rolls at the back of the arena in the parking lot. A red Dodge
Ram pulls up. The engine is cut. The drivers door opens and Dark Wolf steps out. A group of fans run over to meet him along with a handful of reporters. A fan gets to him first.
FAN - Dark Wolf! Dark Wolf! Could you sign my shirt? I just bought it today! DARK WOLF - Sure. What kind of message do you want on it? FAN - How about, "To Adam. From Dark Wolf." DARK WOLF - That's it? FAN - Yeah! DARK WOLF - Okay. Sure. No problem. The fan hands him a marker. Dark Wolf takes the pen and signs the shirt. He hands the shirt and marker back to the fan. FAN - Thanks so much! Wait until my friends see this! DARK WOLF - Your welcome. Camera focus in on the shirt as the fan raises it up. The fan runs off as a reporter makes his way through the crowd towards Dark Wolf who is signing other fans autographs.
REPORTER - Excuse me. Mr. Dark Wolf? Might I have a word with you? DARK WOLF - Sure. But let me finish this autograph first. REPORTER - Alright. DARK WOLF - Okay. I'm done. So what did you want to talk to me about? REPORTER - Have you seen the roster yet tonight? DARK WOLF - No. Why? Is my name added to it yet? REPORTER - Yes. But that's not what I'm here for. There's also another new employee who goes by the name Moon Wolf.
Dark Wolf interrupts him. DARK WOLF - Moon Wolf? Hah hah! What kind of name is that? You couldn't think of anything else so you decided to use part of my name huh? It's stuff like that that really ticks me off. You knew I here because I have already done an interview. So you liked my name so much you thought you'd use part of it.
Well Moon Wolf, since no one else has replied to my last interview. And you decided to use part of my name. How about the two of us hook it up
Monday in a Street Fight. Because like I said before, all I'm interested in is destroying the opposition. And winning the Extreme title. So Moon
Wolf, you're the first on my hitlist. And I'll see you Monday.
Dark Wolf turns from the camera. He goes back to the fans who keep asking for autographs. The camera keeps watching as another fan asks for an autograph of a Dark Wolf baseball cap. Camera slowly fades out.
Blackness covers the screen. -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THIRD ROLEPLAY This was written on the day that the fed's first card was supposed to be. I didn't think there would be a card because very few matches were made. I had never got an answer from Moon. And another wrestler called
Big Wreck made an open challenge to anybody which was also unanswered.
So what I did was request a 3-way dance between me, Big Wreck, and Moon
Wolf. --------------------- [The usual Dark Wolf logo appears on screen. It is the same as the logo used on the Dark Wolf merchandise.
Slowly, the screen fades out, revealing Dark Wolf in its place. He stands in the parking lot of an arena next to his red Dodge Intrepid.
Dark Wolf wears the usual black pants and shoes. Although, this time, he also wears a Chicago Wolves jersey.]
I've been receiving a lot of crap from PCW fans about the Dark Wolf logo. They've been saying that I ripped off the Chicago Wolves logo. I disagree. I am good friends with the Wolves owner. He is also a huge wrestling fan. So when I told him I would be using the name Dark Wolf, in honor of the Chicago Wolves, he suggested I use the logo of the
Wolves. At first, I politely said "no thanks." But it wasn't long until
I was persuaded. So that's the story behind that.
[Dark Wolf leans into the open window of the Intrepid and pulls out a sheet of paper. He quickly glances at it and then looks back at the camera.]
Oh yeah, I made a challenge to Moon Wolf a little while back for a match for today. That coward Moon Wolf never accepted nor refused the match.
So let's change the match a little. Big Wreck, I've got nothin but respect for you. I noticed that you made an open challenge earlier as well. But nobody responded. I would have accepted except I wanted the match with Moon Wolf. So, how about, me, you, and Moon Wolf hook it up for Saturday? In a Weapons match. Let's get some action going here.
After that, Moon Wolf, I'm coming back after you. [Dark Wolf puts the sheet of paper back in the car. He walks around to the driver's door and gets in. Moments later the Intrepid backs out of the arena parking lot. The Dark Wolf logo returns to the screen. With the caption underneath, "Copyright, 1999, Dark Wolf Productions." The screen fades out to black.]
