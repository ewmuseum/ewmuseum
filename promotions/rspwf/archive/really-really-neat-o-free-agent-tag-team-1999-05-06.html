REALLY REALLY NEAT-O FREE AGENT TAG TEAM!!

6 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 OK, sorry. I know I only post to this group every blue moon, and when I do, it's because I'm looking to freeagent myself to other feds. Here's the deal.
I've got a tag team that's never done anything outside of [name withheld]'s WWO, but I'd like to try them out with different feds. The team name is Uncivil
Disobedience, and they are composed of a mean skinny misfit named Rax Justice and his defense lawyer, The Firm. They won the WWO tag titles about a month or so after entering the fed, and would be willing to wrestle for food. Or women. Or, preferably both. Here's a sample flash. This flash takes place right before a card--UCD has a match on that card with Almost Better, and the flash is a spoof on kung-fu movies. Enjoy, and if you'd like UCD in your fed, drop me a line at
[detail withheld] . [name withheld] handler of Uncivil Disobedience (10-2-1--WWO) Former WWO tag champions former handler of Fury, who won many belts & ate many babies. BEGIN FLASH The scene opens up to a fresh college campus somewhere in the upper lower Midwest. The students are green, the grass is young and fresh, and everything seems just lovely. We pan about to a large building smack in the middle of the campus; it's an unadorned building, except for the words
`International Local Midwestern College of TV, Radio, and Stuff' which are hung from a small plague near the double doors. Speaking of the double-doors, the five active members of Uncivil Disobedience burst through them happily, wearing graduation caps and gowns, and clutching rolls of paper in their hands. Rax Justice, flushed with the excitement, begins to speak.]
Rax: `Wow, guys, they aren't just whistling Dixie when they say that getting a diploma from this fine school was tough. It's kind of like the Peace
Corps--'it's the toughest job that we'll ever love.' Firm: `Yeah, it took a good, solid week of training and studying to get that thing. I've never been so academically stressed in my life.'
Rax: `But Firm, I thought you went to law school?' Firm: `Yeah, in Minnesota.' Rax: `Oh. Well, that's different, I guess.' [Rax shakes his head, strangely, and beckons to the other members of UCD.] [The five of them make their way through the campus with that traditional UCD swagger. Tracy Hudson (another member of the stable) pauses to wink at a chubby coed, and Rax cuffs him in the back of the head.]
Rax: `Tracy, quit it. We've got more important things to worry about now. Like Almost Better. Boy, are we going to beat the living snot out of those `foo's' on Superstar Showcase. I don't know who you princesses think that you're going to punk, but it won't be me, it damn sure won't be Firm, and that's really all we've got to say about that.'
Firm: [shrugs] `Look, I don't give a rat's ass about those crazy pieces of poop. We're going to beat Vanilla Stalka until there's nothing left but
EXTRACT of Vanilla Stalka, but that's a given. We're going to further solidify our positions as number-one contenders for the tag belts, but THAT's a given. We're going to have lots of fun at everybody else's expense, but hey, that's what we do best. Almost Better, we'll have to show you what your station in life is. It'll be hard and painful, but if there's one thing we here at UCD pride ourselves on, it's taking down idols. I mean, just look at us. We've beaten every member of the Hall of Fame except Outhouse, we ran the Ultimates clear out of the WWO, and we retired Almost Better.'
Rax: `What about Battle of the Titans? {tm}' Firm: `Well, Rax, at Battle of the Titans {tm}, there's going to be a tag team title match. Now, I know that Search and Destroy are hell-bent on defiling the titles by giving the shot to Blunderstorm, but I don't freakin'
think so. We're going to have that title shot, no matter what.' Rax: `Hey, let's just roll our graduating project;It's a nice little spoof on the kung-fu movies that we all know and love; let's hit it!'
[The scene segues into one of the old black and white silent movies. We see the words flash on the screen...]
`Once upon a time, in the hood.' [The scene goes to Rax and Firm, in VERY ugly kimonos, behind the counter of a Chinese restaurant.] `There was a Chinese restaurant named `Sum Yung Gai's''run by our two heroes, Wang Chang and Bei Lang' [Firm and Rax begin to speak to each other. Since this is a SILENT movie, we wait for them to finish talking, and then the words appear.]
`Ah, it is a fine day in the hood for two fine restaurant owners such as ourselves.' `Agreed, it is truly fine.' [Narration] `But, then;' [We see two large men enter the restaurant. They are Loaf and Benny (two other members of the stable), but they've obviously doctored themselves up to look like Almost Better. Loaf is wearing a skullcap and a hoodie, and Benny is wearing Techno Nic. B's outfit. The two start talking.]
`Yo, what be up foo's? We is in da hizzy like tin lizzy, and y'all better recognize.' `Aw yeah, Stalka word. Anyway, Chang, hook deez up wit some beef fried rices. And some chicken wings without the little leg on dem.' [The camera pans back to Rax and Firm. The discuss the matter, obviously concerned.] `These young men come into our domain like the fierce hyena to the lion's den, oh great Bei Lang.'
`It is so, Wang. But we must pride ourselves on serving the people, so we must give these uncouth ruffians what they want.'
[`Almost Better' talk amongst themselves.] `Hah, G, I knew deez foo's was wimps. See, we's just gonna get us some grub befo' we take on UCD at da `case. See, cuz UCD be actin' all uppity, like us bruddahs can't eat like dey eat, and we gone prove dat we can, aw yeah.'
[Rax and Firm jump over the counter.] 'You want beef?' `Yeah, G, we want some beef fried rice and summa dem chicken wings fried HARD, loc-dawg!' `OK, fine.' [Rax and Firm get into their fighting stances.] `You want beef NOW, chumps?' `NO, no;we want beef to EAT, man, we ain't got no beef! AAAHHH!' [Rax and Firm land a few swift kicks, and then `Almost Better' hightails it out of the Chinese restaurant.]
`With their honor now indelibly besmirched by the foul runnings away of Almost Better, Wang and Bei pursue them to the ends of the earth in order to maintain their stolen honor.'
[The scene shows a map quickly, and it shows the path of Almost Better in red. Every now and then, we come to them running. After a while of the running and the pursuing, the screen has a message for us.]
`Meanwhile, in another part of China, the great master Monk Xio Mulan pondered the fate of his protegees, whom we had sent into the city to prosper long ago.'
[We pan to a shot of Tracy Hudson in a field, in a monk outfit with his legs crossed in meditation position. His lips move.]
`I ponder the fate of my protegees, whom I had sent into the city to prosper long ago.' [We pan back to Rax and Firm chasing down Almost Better. Rax's lips move.] `You think we open up a Chinese restaurant in the middle of the hood and don't know what's going on? We friggin' represent! We will represent by whupping your asses--word is born!'
[The shot shows that the AB group is fast coming up on the great monk. Upon noticing the monk, they stop dead in their tracks. The monk speaks.]
`My friends, do not worry.' [Rax and Firm run into the picture, about to whip some heavy-duty booty. But then, they stop upon seeing their mentor. The monk looks upset.]
`My proteges, have you forgotten all I have taught you? Does not the fire need its embers stoked in order to remain? Does not the mountain need its storms? Do Almost Better's scrotums not need kicking?'
[Following that speech, the monk gives a double nut shot to the members of Almost Better, and the three administer a serious beating.] `The End' [The scene fades back to reveal all five members of UCD in a makeshift movie theater, eating popcorn and cheering loudly.]
Rax: `Hey, guess what, Almost Better. There's a hidden message in that one. Go on, see if you can figure it out. While your trying, we'll be busy preparing to rule the frickin' roost on Superstar Showcase. Have a nice day, girls...'
[The scene fades.] END FLASH -----------== Posted via Deja News, The Discussion Network ==---------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Discuss, or Start Your Own
