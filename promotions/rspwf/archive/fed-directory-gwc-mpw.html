GWC/MPW (GWC/MPW) Website: http://www.efeduniverse.com/gwc/ Owner: Kiz Description: Planning of the Global Wrestling Connection began in mid-2000 when business tycoon Colin Fox got in touch with wrestling star Ben Tuckwell to discuss his ideas. They began planning every intricate detail of the league throughout the months leading up to the grand opening date, which was March 1, 2001. Two weeks later, administration signed a deal with American Wrestling Promotions to run a talent exchange deal. In May 2001, only 2 months after the original opening, Ben Tuckwell stepped down from his position as president of the GWC. Taking his position was former AWP president Sebastian Manning. He was more than qualified for the job, but his appointment in this role led to some conflict between AWP and GWC, signaling the end of their mutual agreement. Anyway, soon after taking the job as GWC President, Manning signed “Elite Sensation” Eric Emerson as commissioner, a move that provided the league with another edge in the admin department. However, ratings slowly dropped and despite the hard work they had put in to the fed, they were fired on July 21, 2001. Looking to improve ratings and the executive committee, owner Colin Fox threw a lifeline to a small fed out of Des Moines, Iowa. This fed was Midwest Pro Wrestling. The two promotions merged together to form GWC/MPW. Rob Loney took on the role as President and brought his own MPW faction into the league, leading to some real differences of opinion between him and Colin Fox. However, ratings were higher than ever and Loney’s contract was iron clad. Going into the inaugural Invitational Cup in November 2001, Rob Loney’s MPW faction fell apart and he was left alone. He was also a clear target for attacks, so he decided to take a vacation and never returned after that. It seemed the executive turmoil in GWC/MPW was set to continue as they went without a president for the rest of the year. The appointment of Mike Mills as GWC/MPW President on New Year’s Day 2002 indicated the start of something even more special and it’s clear that GWC/MPW is a fed to look out for in the future. The roster is as solid as ever with some real top class talent there and their shows are becoming better every week. With Mills at the helm and Colin Fox’s continued financial backing, the league is destined for great things in 2002 and beyond. Keynote Members: Featuring names such as Sinister, Brandon Franklin, DDI, Corey Hart, Justin Cage, Brian Taylor, Team Extreme and Forever Unchained. Federation Champion: Rosco Riggins Flash Show Descripition: THE RANT: The Rant is shown on Monday Nights and the main purpose of this show is to air all the flashes from the wrestlers on the GWC/MPW talent roster. Regular Show Description: COLLISION COURSE: Replacing Saturday Night Fury as the fed’s weekly show, Collision Course is held on Thursday Nights between 8:30 PM and 10:30 PM. PPV Description: GWC/MPW holds a Pay Per View every two months with the signature Pay Per View being Glory Daze which is held in February.
