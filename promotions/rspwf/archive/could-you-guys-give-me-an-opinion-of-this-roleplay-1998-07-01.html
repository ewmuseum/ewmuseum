Could you guys give me an opinion of this Roleplay?

1 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thanks this a new combo I'm working on and I'd like any feedback ------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Camera fades in to a dark room where you see the out line of three people.In the background you can hear the song "Shotgun" by Moist.The first man is sitting on the ground with his head down and his arm around the shoulders of the woman.The third person is a man who is much smaller than the first.He is sitting on a table indian style with his hands holding up his chin.He's actually pretty good looking..He has brown hair He is wearing a black "No Code" Pearl Jam t-shirt with jean shorts.He has a smirk on his face.He begains to talk]
Jason Drake[sing softly]:Everybody is all right,everything is automatic,everybody is outright everything is skin deep.
[He looks up and breaks into a wide grin] J.D.:Eric Charisma look they sent a cameraman to talk to us isn't that awesome! Before we begain though I wanna sent somthing straight.Eric and
Charisma's friends call them Eric and Charisma everyone else can call them OutKast and Sympathy.That means I call them Eric and Charisma and everyone else...well you heard what I just said.Anyway Eric man come and talk to the camera it's cool I bet this'll be on Tuesday Titans or somthing.
[The bigger man stands up and you can now see what he looks like.He has long blonde hair and has on pretty much the same outfit as Jason Drake except his shirt that says "Feel My Pain".He looks at the woman before he speaks]
OutKast[softly almost whispering]:I never did any thing to deserve this...I just was always punished.I didn't eat my potatoes...I was punished...I didn't get straight A's I was punished...If I stood up for myself I was punished
[As he lists things his voice gets louder and angrier until finally he's yelling] OutKast:WHY! WHAT DID I DO! WHY AM I FORSAKEN WITH THESE BURDENS!! [At this point the woman stands up and you get a good look at her.She has brown hair that falls on her shoulders and she is very beautiful.Her clothes are in stark contrast to the men's as they are quite fashionable and they only make her look more beautiful.She is holding OutKast's head in her arms like a mother would a childs.She is trying to soothe him as
Jason is shaking his head.He hops down from his seat]
J.D[patting OutKast on the back]:Calm down Eric man not in front of the camera. [He turns towards the camera with a grim look on his face] J.D.:See you what people have done?This is a good man who has had to live through years of abuse.If wasn't for myself and Charisma I shudder to think where he'd be.So now as we leave I'll share two things with you.
One if your looking for a name to label us with call us The Misfits
Secondly don't think this is all of us.There are more they two will arrive.Good Night children.
[Camera fades as Jason sings] J.D.:I can't live here anymore but it's hard when you reach for that floor there's somthing that tears me inside about I can't live here anymore anymore...
[whispering] ..anymorrre [Camera fades out]
