[NWC] Andrew "The Hurricane" Pierce post....please evaluate/critique.

23 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens inside Pierce' club, Le Discotheque Orlando, once again. It is a number of hours before the club opens for the busy Friday night crowd. A few people are walking around the periphery, getting things ready.]
[Off to the side, in a dim corner, Andrew Pierce sits quietly, watching the well-oiled machine, the intricate dance of his employees preparing the club for the evening. Dressed in khaki pants, a rust-colored shirt with it's sleeves rolled up, and a plain black tie, Pierce just sits there, drinking occasionally from the glass of lemonade before him. Off to one side, a cigar sits smoldering in a crystal ash tray.]
So Ice has decided to grace us with an interview. Showing off her so-called body in her jacuzzi, trying to cast the illusion that she is calm. How commendable.
It might have been more interesting if she actually had a body to look at, but hey, she doesn't. She's trying to play a man's game, and all she has faced so far are wimps. Wusses. The inane and the unable.
[Pierce pauses to take a drag off of his cigar, and following it with a sip of lemonade.] Poor, poor Ice. Surrounding herself with delusions of grandeur. She is so high on her steroids that she actually believes what she says. She had one word for me: Whatever. To that, I have only this to say. Ice, I got two words for you.
Estrogen and Electrolysis. From what I have heard, Pride has been hanging around your locker room, and we know what gender he really goes for. Here's a hint...they won't be found in Penthouse.
And for your poor, whipped Johnny Blaze. It's funny that you had him serving you. I guess we know who wears the cup in that relationship. He may have been wearing pants, but I am sure he had some frilly things on underneath.
[Shaking his head in disgust, Pierce sips from his glass once more] Ice, in all reality, you have already powdered yourself. Self-doubt and fear have already ingrained themselves inside you, and are at this moment eroding your confidence. You fear the Hurricane, as well you should. I'm going to pick up the shattered pieces of your career and use them against whoever comes next, be it Golden Shower Tremain or Brandywine Smitty.
This Hurricane doesn't slow down. Not for land, not for glaciers, not for diamonds, and sure as hell not for wanna be transsexuals that think they can wrestle.
But I digress. [Again, Pierce pauses, puffing at his cigar and shooting shrewd looks at the employees just coming in late.]
After Sunday night, it doesn't matter, Whether I have to crush Ice, cube Ice, or just plain powder the Diamond, I will move into the finals. And I will get the damn belt. Cause, dammit, I want it more, I am more talented, and darn it, people hate me. With good reason. It's easy to hate those you can't ever defeat. And none of you, be you in the CCW or the NWC, have a snowball's chance in hell of quelling the Hurricane.
Hmmm. Snowball? Maybe that should be Ice Cube. Or, Ice Diamond. It's not how pretty you look in the ring, Girlie. It's not what you've done before. It's what you are going to do now. And the only thing you are going to do, the only thing you CAN do, is stare up at the Arena lights in Montreal after crying out in pain and shame, saying I Give before all those in attendance.
And you will prostrate yourself, like a blade of grass in gale-force winds. You will bow down before the Hurricane. So batten down the hatches, whatever ones you have after all the drugs.
[Pierce grins, leaning back from the table and the light, picking up his cigar. Puffing on it and watching the smoke twirl in the light, the camera slowly fades.]
