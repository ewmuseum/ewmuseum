[RP] What's wrong with this flash???

5 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I read it over and over again, and I'm just not happy with it. I can't figure out what's wrong with it? Please read it, and give me your thoughts...
====================== Title: "The Answers" "Homeless" Joe Boxcar 5/4/99 ====================== [the scene opens up in a familiar alley located in the friendliest city in the United States, New York. An instrumental version of New York by Frank
Sinatra begins to play. In this alley there is trash. Also in this alley, there are dumpsters, but, by far, the most important thing in this alley are the bums. The camera slowly moves towards the back of the alley passing bum after bum. One group of our economically challenged friends are huddle together under a pile of newspapers, trying to keep out the cold provided by the cold New York night. Another group of our homeless companions our standing around a barrel that contains a flame giving off much needed heat.
Finally, we come to the dumpster in the back. It's closed, so the cameraman knocks on it..]
(KLANK KLANK KLANK) [the top creaks as it opens. After a few seconds of creaking the dumpster is open! The camera man steps back as a figure sits up, the figure being
"Homeless" Joe Boxcar. He has obviously been sleeping. He rubs his eyes, scratches the back of his neck, and brushes off the banana peel sitting on top of his red beenie. He realizes who his visitors are...]
JB- "I'm guessin' you guys are here to get me to talk about MOTR, right?" Cameraman- "Yes sir, Mr. Boxcar" JB(stopping him)- "Please...call me Joe" Cameraman- "You got it Joe" [the cameraman and Joe shake hands] JB- "Well...I guess I should start by tellin' you folks where I've been for the past 6 months. You remember the N.E.W?"
Cameraman- "Yeah" JB- "Well, after messing up some of there quote-un-quote important plans....J.R Hollis, the President at the time, had a bunch of his goons beat me up and ship me off to an island in the South Pacific inside of a little crate. By the time I came to, I was already on a plane headin'
towards Asia. It wasn't until they got to the island that they realized I was in that crate, and even when they did...there was nothin' they could do...."
Cameraman- "Why's that?" JB- "Heh...cause not a single one of them spoke a lick of English. All they could say was things like 'ching chow cha' and stuff like that..."
[the camera man laughs] Cameraman- "..so how'd ya get back?" JB- "Well...it took me a while, but eventually I saved up 5 dollars and sixty three cents in change and was able to get a hold of my agent, Aaron
Kozak. You guys know him....KNI and all that jazz"
Cameraman- "Yeah" JB- "He paid for my flight home and here I am...you know....eventually I DO plan to get J.R back, but for now....I got other things to worry about."
[we hear the cameraman sniff] Cameraman- "...MAN! What is that rank smell" JB- "Oh..that's just Larry...I share the dumpster with him." [a tall bum, bigger and even more dirty than Joe walks towards the dumpster] Cameraman- "BLAH...*HACK*...I can't..*HACK*..breath" JB- "Look mister, being a bum ain't all that easy ya know. It's not like we get to shower every month like you 'privileged' people."
Cameraman- "I'M SORRY...*COUGH*...just ask him to leave! *COUGH*" [Joe signals Larry to leave, and he does. The camera man takes a deep breath] Cameraman- "Thanks....boy you guys do have it rough" JB- "I've lived this way all my life and to be honest...I can't really think of any other way I'd rather live. Here...we have the satisfaction of being able to survive alone in harsh conditions. I've never been fed by a silver spoon in life..."
[scratches his head] JB- "Although..once I ate some left over macaroni and cheese with a plastic one....mmmm...man I love macaroni and cheese"
[Joe licks his lips] JB(to the cameraman)- "Do you have any macaroni and cheese?" Cameraman- "Um...no..but I suppose I could get you some after the interview" JB- "Really? That'd be great!" Cameraman- "I just have a few questions first." JB- "Shoot." Cameraman- "Are you ready to come back?" JB- "Hmmm...that's a good question. I don't know if I can really answer that. Master of the Ring Tournament will kinda decide it all for me. If, while beating the life out of Pedro Kozaro, I find that I don't have the same feelings for the sport like I once had, I may retire. However, if I realize I love the sport more than ever...you may see me stick around for a while. We'll see how it goes"
Cameraman- "Speaking of Kozaro, what are you feelings going into your upcoming match up?" JB- "Guys like Kozaro....they under estimate me. They think because I don't get to train in nice facilities, eat good food, or even shower ever week....I can't fight. Let me tell you right now....I can fight. I can fight..and I WILL fight. Kozaro, Prince Judife, Rip Fury...I DON'T CARE WHO
IT IS! When I have to go through several of the top wrestlers in the game to reach MY GOAL....I'm gonna fight, and damnit, they better hope to God, that
I'm feeling good. Otherwise they're gonna get a taste of my New York Style
Knuckle Sandwich....my specialty.
[Joe rubs his fist] JB- "Anything else you wanna know?" Cameraman- "No...that's great!" JB- "Good...then let's go get the macaroni" [camera fades]
