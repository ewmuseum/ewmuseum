I need a fed to join( Sample Interview)

19 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello Presidents my name is Rick and I am out looking for a good e-fed to join. I handle Jake Stone and I've been involved in e-wrestling for over a year and I've also been a President in my own fed, so I do know what it takes to make a good fed. I will roleplay often and I love great angles, so if you have a good fed let me know, but here is a list of requirements your fed must meet before I even look at it. If your fed doesn't meet these requirements do not send something to me because I will not join.
1. Orginal Wrestlers ONLY 2. Guestbook Roleplaying Only 3. No Hardcore or Extreme feds 4. Atleast 2 cards a week 5. Good Roleplayers Ok that's it if you have a fed like that or know of one give me a call. Here is a sample roleplay to see if you would let me in your fed. <i><b>( Jake is sitting in the locker room after an exhausting workout. He is drinking a beer and sitting back relaxing in a chair. Suddenly Jake jumps to his feet and slams the chair across the room breaking it into pieces. He then walks right up to the camera and puts his face real close and begins to speak in a harsh tone.</b></i>)<p>Barnes you lowlife scum I told you I would find you and make you pay and now I hope you rot in hell you piece of garbage. Your lucky they stopped me because I would of killed you. Hope your hospital bed is comfy because from what I hear you'll be sucking food through a straw for a good month. Well lets just say thats I warning to the SUWO don't mess with Jake Stone or you'll get your A** kicked. You see Barnes could never beat me, HELL as a matter of fact
NOONE in this pathetic Hell Hole can beat me. Next Tuesday some loser named Checo will be the next victim on Jake's list.<p>( <i><b>Jake gets up and walks away from the camera then swiftly turns around and throws his beer can into the wall. Jake then slams his fist into a locker busting his knuckles open. As Blood is going everywhere Jake continues to speak.</b></i>)<p>
<b>Checo</b> I don't know much about you, but I don't really need to because no matter what I will beat your Checo A** from one side of the arena to the other side. You see Checo I love to fight and I love pain and
I promise you this I will be the toughest opponent you've ever gotten in the ring with and when they carry your broken body out on a stretcher just remember I warned you. Also I suggest you not have your family in attendance that night because I always hate when some Fat Wife or some ugly
Mom is crying there eyes out and yelling at me because I destroyed there son or husband. That sickens me and just ruins my wonderful night.<p><i><b>( Jake grabs a towel and wraps it around his hand. He then grabs some ice and sticks it in the towel. jake grabs another Beer pops the top and continues to speak.</b></i>)<p><b>Checo</b> if you haven't realize by now I'm not doing this because I don't like you, Hell I hate everyone not just you, but the real reason I'm going to hurt everyone in this fed who faces me is because I Can and nobody can stop me. I am the
Baddest Man ever to breathe air and Checo you'll be praying for lightning to strike that night and kill me dead because that will be the only way they'll get my foot out of your a**.<p><i><b>( Jake laughs out loud then goes over and begins to put on his blue jeans and his shirt. As he's putting on his tennis shoes Jake looks back up and speaks.</b></i>)<p>As for the rest of the So called Superstars of the SUWO.<b> Addict, Slugger,
Butt Master, Venom, Nuetron</b> and all the rest who walk around here thinking they can't be beat I have a message for your sorry a**es, <b>Dr.
Rick and Myself will rule this fed and we will fight anyone who gets in our way and I'll promise you this I we will come out on top and you will leave on a stretcher and there's nothing you can do about it.<p><i><b>( Jake slams his locker shut then walks out of the room and heads towards his truck and the scene slowly fades away.</b></i>)<p>
