[Critique] - [Jesse Madrid]

3 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am wanting to get into this character a little more than I have. I am trying to remold his attitude, and I have been having a bit of success. The scenes might not be too...well thought out but everything that Madrid will say will give you and idea of the type of guy he is...anyway, feeback appreciated.
[[The word "Notorious" flashes across your screen in a brilliant show of light. Not two seconds later does the name "Jesse" flash across your screen in the same manner. And a final word, "Madrid" flashes across the screen leaving us in total silence.]]
[Fade in] [The visual fades in showing an unforgiving sun glaring in a cloudless sky above a large ware-house building surrounded by railroad tracks and abandoned train cars.  There is nothing familiar about the building.  It appears to be the same as the many warehouses around it.  A graveyard of sorts you could call it.  Each building and each car characterized by their rust and fading paint.
 This building though, seems a little different from the rest.  Other than it's seemingly new windows, it is the only one with an open door.]
"Not too impressive, am I right? If you only knew the kind of talent that this warehouse held at one point."
[The dank stench of dried blood and sweat fill the air as we walk further into the warehouse in question.  Everywhere around us, broken tables, shattered glass, and mutulated folding chairs.  This all seems so foreign, untill we see a ring set in the center of it all.  The ring is still standing with it's ropes clinging on by a thin line of threaded barbed-wire.  A loud buzz is heard and we can almost feel the electricity flowing through the ceiling above us.  A single light shines above us triggering the rest of the lights in the ware-house.  After the comotion, we look to the ring and see a single man sitting in the center in wrestling gear, wearing a black shirt and sitting backwards on a folding chair with his arms layed on the top of the chair and his legs spread around the legs of it.]
"To think; if this had been two years ago you would have been thrown through one, if not all of the windows in this place for trying to bring a camera in here."
[His voice is deep and clear.  Filled with confidence.  The kind of confidence that comes from a man who knows what is what.  He isn't an extremely large man, but he is physically built and is bigger than the average man.  His long brown hair hangs down below his neck line, and he sports a goatee of the same color.
 He stands from the chair and indeed...he is an impressive sight.  Our attention is distracted from his body to the logo which we can plainly see on his shirt: Jesse Madrid .]
"There is a time, and a place for everything. What better place than this? [looks around at the warehouse] and what better time than now?   [Brushes off his chest with his hands...almost purposely motioning to the crimson red logo on his shirt]  
"What you see in front of you is eight years of history. This...ring, is the place where I broke my first bone. This...ring is the first place that I won a title. [nods his head approvingly] This...ring is the first place that I pinned the man who taught me everything I know about this sport: "The Loose
Cannon" Chris Champion. I, am the man who turns heads in this bussiness. I am the notorious, Jesse Madrid. And while none of this may make much sense to you, it will in the days to come. This...ring will be the place where you get to find out what countless numbers of other men like yourselves found out the day they stepped into the ring with Jesse Madrid...I'm no joke."
[Slowly, this unknown man begins pacing back and forth in the center of the ring.  He pulls his hands in front of them and rests his finger tips together.
 He stops pacing and looks back to the camera and speaks.] "Fear me, love me, loathe me. It's all the same. I don't need to sit here and give you a grocery list of reasons why I am better than you. I'll leave that up to my actions..[nods his head] in the ring. I'll leave it up to my hands, to make a good grapple every chance I can get. I'll leave it up to my legs, to keep me up and moving through thick and thin. I'll leave it up to my knowledge of the ring, to put me in the right place at the right time. And in the end, I will either walk out the winner, or the loser. Either way, I will bust my ass to make sure that you bust yours because when I win, I want you to know what level you have to compete at. And if I lose, I want you to remember that I am still coming for you, and I wont let up untill I see your eyes staring to the ceiling and I hear those numbers that I love the most."
[He walks to the rings ropes and leans on the top rope while placing his right foot on the lowest rope.]
"Do what you can do when it counts, because if you don't...you might find yourself in a_DEADLY_situation."
[Fade]
