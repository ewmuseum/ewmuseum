Second thing, I recently joined the TWL since I needed a second federation but as soon as I joined, it is going to close(think it was my fault?!) So I now need a new fed to join.

[thanx] Date: 4/29/99 9:23:31 PM EST From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] [Fade in.] [SCENE: Anaheim, California. TIME: 7:45 P.M. The scene opens up outside a five star hotel in the upper class part of Anaheim. The cameraman stands outside. Suddenly, a black stretch limo pulls forward and stops in from of a red carpet which is laid at the base of the curb. The valet opens the door.
Suddenly, "The Hammer" Alex Cote steps out.] [APPEARENCE: Alex is dressed in a pair of blue jeans with a tight black t-shirt. He also has on a pair of Nike Airs, a gold chain around his neck, and a high school ring on his right hand.]
[Alex steps out and sees the camera. He smiles and stands on the red carpet with his hands in his pockets.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Well, I'm glad you showed up, despite the fact I knew you would show up anyways. Being the _ICON_ of the ICW has it advantages, and one of them is when I want something, I get it A-S-A-P!! Now, as soon as I grab my bag from inside the limo, we shall get goin.]
[Alex reaches inside and pulls out a black duffle bag. He stands up and begins to walk toward the front door.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Well, I finally showed up here in Cali. I stayed a few extra days in my hometown, of Philly. I visited my family and a friend of my friends. It was hard to leave. But I have a job to do and tommorow it shall once again be fulfilled. Tommorow, I face the infamous Kenneth Morlock.
And yet, I do not mean "infamous" as to say he is known for being a hard ass or anything of that nature, I mean it as to say that he is known for being a loser and just being your everyday punk. Ya see, when people look at "The Hammer", they "Oh" and "Ah", but when they look at Morlock they can only do one thing which lots of us have done when dicussing him in the past, laugh! So Morlock, as you can tell, the fans and the employees of the ICW think you are nothing but a....
~-.._JOKE_..-~!! [A man in a red jacket opens the door for Alex. The cameraman stays in front of him, walking backwards, to make sure to keep a clears shot on him. Alex walks up to the front desk. Another man walks up to him and smiles.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Hey, sup? MAN: Hello, how may I help you sir? "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Yeah, I have a room reserved for me. MAN[looks over Alex]: Ummm...sir...I highly doubt by the look of you that you have the prestige or the money to afford a room in this hotel. This is a five star hotel and it is only for the finacially proficient.
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE[gives the man a dark stare]: Excuse me?! Listen to me little man, I make at least ten times as much money a moth as you make a year!
I am a well known athlete and respected person around the world so you will treat with respect!
MAN: Whatever! Just give me your _FULL_ name, I will check the computer, and when it says that you on there I will have sercurity remove you, ok?!
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Ok, but when it is I am going to laugh in your god damn face! Now, look up Alexander Montague Cote.
[The man behind the counter punches the name into the computer. He has a demonic, snobby smirk on his face until the screen changes. His face drops and he slowly looks up at Alex.]
MAN[shocked]: Umm....yes Mr. Cote, you are all set for our presidential suite on the penthouse floor.
[The man holds out the key which Alex takes.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Well, thank you! [Alex begins to walk away. He stops and walks back to the counter.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Oh wait, I forgot something] [Alex bends over the counter and gets right into the man's face.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE[yells]: HA!!!! [Alex turns around and walks over to the elevator. It opens and he and the cameraman enter.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Bunch of snobby bastards here in Cali! I hate this town! Oh well, before I leave I am going to beat that guys ass! But before I do that I have to save my strength for tommorow with Morlock! Morlock, I have listened to you run your mouth and on talking meaningless jibberish but you have not yet realized no one cares what you have to say. I know you want a piece of my man [name withheld] but as it specifies on the contract for our match, you will not recieve that shot unless you beat me and as wee all know that is something that you just can't do! But, you, unlike that guy, will not be so lucky because IU am just going to hurt him, you, I am going to stomp a mudhole....
~-.._IN_..-~ ~-.._YOUR_..-~ ~-.._ASS_..-~!!! [The doors opens and Alex walks up to the only door and looks at the camera.] "THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: Now, Morlock, before I go inside, I want to just let you realize that the money I have recieved for this room and this respect has been earned by beating sorry pieces of trash like you from pillar to post every week! Rookies have come and gone in the life of Alex Cote and they have all left losers and you will not be an exception to that tradition. Tommorow is the day when I do what I do best, and that teachs punk the meaning of respect!!
[Alex unlocks the door and walks into the room. The room is _HUGE_!! There is a full living room with a complete entertainment system. There is a large bathroom and a bedroom with a kingsize bed.]
"THE HAMMER" ALEX COTE: You see all this?! This has all came from what I have accomplished! And I know that you are jealous of a man like me and you just hope that one day you can have all this but I will make a foreshadow right now, you will never be this big! Compared to me you are Freddy Floyd! Or explictly, nothing but a punk! I am the "Real McCoy" and I am the _ICON_ and you are just a low carder! So, Morlock, I hope you open your eyes and see what you have gotten yourself into because if you think you have what it takes to beat me you better think again because you have though _DEAD_ wrong! To,
Kenneth Morlock, you better..... ~-.._PREPARE_..-~ ~-.._TO_..-~ ~-.._BE_..-~ ~-.._HAMMERED_..-~ Because...... ~-.._I_..-~ ~-.._TAKE_..-~ ~-.._NO_..-~ ~-.._PRISONERS_..-~!!! [Alex opens the door and lets the cameraman out.] [Fades to black.]
