As people may know the DWO has just closed, which leaves my tag team as free agents - if you want a sample roleplay have a look at the board, the link has been taken off the main page but can still be accessed through
http://www.insidetheweb.com/mbs.cgi/mb386975 , there's about 4 up for what would have been the tag title shot today. Before I continue, my requirements - Fed must be stable and open 6+ months Roster of at least 25 with some tag team competition Roleplay board based with easy to use web site - preferably without 94 pop up windows PPV monthly, at least one card a week You won't have heard of them because I've only been around with these characters since November 99 but here are the details:
The two of them together are known as the Northern Lights. For full bios look at http://www.[detail withheld].freeserve.co.uk/bowenmoyles.html
Basically the two guys are from the north of England and are primarily technical wrestlers, Bowen tending towards brawling style and Moyles more of a high flyer. Moyles has a dumb ass of a sidekick called Comedy
Dave's who's a short arse, not quite midget size but short, who is addicted to burgers and other junk food.
Technical info: Jim Bowen: 6'4", 243 lbs. From Leeds, England. Finisher - Bowen Bomb (standard powerbomb, if it fails a top rope variety is used). Music - Hello (Oasis) Chris Moyles: 6'1", 231 lbs. From Newcastle, England. Finisher - Guffalator (somersault butt drop to the head, either from top rope or springboard). Music - Avengers movie theme Northern Lights: Music - Foggy Mountain Breakdown (Cuban Boys). Finisher - Lights Out (same as the Dudley Death Drop - how did they lose at WM2K???) Send replies by E-MAIL to [detail withheld] - don't just reply to this post on the newsgroup. Send me a web addy and I'll have a look. If you only want one of the guys I am willing to split the team up but it seems as if every other fed is crying out for tag teams.
