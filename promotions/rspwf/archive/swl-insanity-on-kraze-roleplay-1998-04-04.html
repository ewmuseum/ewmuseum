-=(SWL)=- iNSANiTY...on KRAZE [ Roleplay ]

4 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene=-The scene is showing you a shot of iNSANiTY and Barbed Wire inside of the Hawaii iNSANiTY has just bought...iNSANiTY is sitting in the parlor with
Barbed Wire on his lap...He has the brand new NBK T-Shirt on...(which will be released at a later time)...Barbed Wire is wearing a pink bikini top and some short boom-boom shorts...her long wavy blonde hair is in a ponytail...iNSANiTY has his hair slicked back and is wearing a pair of Navy Blue oversized
Southpole jeans...iNSANiTY is watching old video tapes of Kraze....he is about to talk to the camera crew...
iNSANiTY=-Well Kraze I have been sitting here for 10 minutes and I have reviewed your history of wins 6 times...do you know why?...Cause you have no wrestling history...You are a nobody...let me tell you what wrestling history is...it is when you first come on the scene in 96 under the handle of
SABU...yeah it sounds corny...but I did it...I started in the original NSWO...I was there along with some of my personal friends...there I became the NSWO Tag
Team champs along with Kronus of the SMF...Sick Mutha Fuckas...an unknown stable...after I left the NSWO I went on to the IWA...(Not the original
IWA)...There I became the IC and Heavyweight Champ in the same week...I single handedly took out a stable by the sWo..Sick World Order...I had wrestled under the handle of HaRdCoRe in that fed for 4 months...for nothing...no recognition...nothing....then I became Vice Prez and I tried to turn the fed in for the better but I was kicked out when the prez got scared and could not take the pain that I was a better man than him...So then it was off to the EWA...Yes the EWA...I was recruited by my friend Voodoo...In the EWA I really started to shine...I was on 3 ocassions almost a champ...There I wrestled under the handle of HaRdCoRe...But I was screwed by the "suits"...People in the EWA knew that I was the better man and that I should have been a champ...Well I left the EWA and went to the fed that everyone was talking about...and that was the
SWL...There I started a whole new beggining...I emerged as the handler of iNSANiTY...this is when I really became a star...I was first greeted by Mr.
Justice..he beat me at the SWL PPV:Sharp Turn...But I vowed vengence and got it when I beat him the next night to become the SWL World Heavyweight
Champion...From then on I was undefeated..until that night when Anthony Mislan beat me...but now I am on a rampage..one that has come to you Kraze...I will beat you for the title...I know that you have given the title to Ruiner..but that is cause you are scared to fight me...Be a man and grow some balls...I am the man that you fear...the man that will be undefeated from this day on...The next champ...
iNSANiTY=-As for you Gravedigger you have abandoned the NBK..that is okay but now you know that you will feel the wrath of iNSANiTY...I rememba Friday...in the UNWF...I will have revenge!..Suck this...
Scene=-The scene fades to black as iNSANiTY plays a tape of his days of HaRdCoRe...
