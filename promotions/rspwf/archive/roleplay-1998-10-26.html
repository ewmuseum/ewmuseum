Roleplay.

26 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Bored. Should be studying or writing my current li'l story. But I'm not. So, without further crap... O=========================================================================O
[SCENE: Deep, static shadow. Unrelenting, ungiving, unmovable. The stillness of the grave. The dear, dirty confines of eternity.
From deep within the silence, cutting the surrounding void like steel through flesh, a cold voice rasps. The voice of Ethan de Sade.]
EdS - "Problems have solutions, Sean." [An array of track lighting explodes to offer into view Ethan de Sade. Cracked, yellow light breathed from overhead, Ethan stands stolid like a malevolent archangel in the center of an aged, beaten, worn wrestling ring. The familar dust-pounded gymnasium Ethan de Sade regularly inhabits stands in weary repose -- faded, yellowed wrestling adverts stapled over each other in cascading fashion, frayed tarmac extending between the ring's four posts. High-tiered, boarded windows offer minimal lighting, the irregular light from overhead offering the lone source of illumination.
Clad in plain jeans and a white t-shirt enscripted with the phrase "Nice
Enough S.O.B.", Ethan looks dead on into the camera with a direct gaze.]
EdS - "You don't like me. I don't like ya'. Well, Sean..." [Juts a thumb back at himself.] EdS - "You're lookin' at _solution_. I ain't gonna offer no 'bad-ass' insults or promises of 'injury'... I'm jus' gonna say two li'l words for your ears, 'Iconoclast'. Ya' wanna match, Sean? Ya' want _me_, Sean? Well, Sean, here goes those two li'l words... I accept. No gimmick crap, no help from all the other sumbitches involved in this..." [Points a forefinger into the camera.] EdS - "Jus' _you_..." [Redirects the finger to himself.] EdS - "An' _me_. Problem solved, Sean. Ya' think I misjudged ya'? Tha' ya' better than 'good?' Well, kid... show me. See, Sean, I've seen a _whole_ lotta promise outta you since I hooked up wit' the Ess-Cee. I've seen skill and moves that'll make any man a superstar. A World Champ even. But Sean, that's _promise_ for you. Sometimes... hell, _most_ of the time, tha' promise jus' don't equal realization. An' Sean, all I see in your face now is a ruined boy. A good 'nuff wres'ler... but not good _enough_ to be more.
A failure. You can run around wit' your li'l hack belt an' talk about bein' 'good'... but all I see in tha' is a few pounds of worthless gold strapped around your waist. See, Sean, you've never beat _me_. The way history sees it..."
[Holds up a finger.] EdS - "One, walked out of a match with me." [Holds up a second finger.] EdS - "Two, only confronted me with shots to the _back_." [Holds up a third, and final, finger.] EdS - "An' three, ran away from Evolution when times got rough an' I was gearin' to kick your ass.
Was Evolution a good idea, Sean? Sure, why not. But so was punchin' holes in ya' head to let out evil spirits. Same thing, really... they're both _really_ logical at first glance. But after awhile, you jus' have to ask what in the _HELL_ you're thinkin'!
But Evolution's dead now. Next Monday, me an' Chris hold the funeral. About time we start lookin' ahead. I had a talk with a good friend of mine... Alex Grey. Next available Scraps, Sean, after we take care of problems _this_ Monday. You an' me. Jus' a wrestlin' match.
Have I got it in me to put up my li'l title shot? Sure. But do _you_ have it in you to raise the stakes, Sean? Do you have it in you to add your li'l belt to the prize?"
[Smiles a slanted, cruel grin.] EdS - "But our date is still a good week off, Sean. Tomorrow I go up against our pal Rick Williams. R'member Rick, Sean? Sure you do, he was the one tha' tried to take us out a few months back... but then kinda faded into the void that is Syndicate Oblivion.
Well, Rick, you an' me ain't all focused on each other goin' into
Monday. At least, I'm sure _you_ aren't. Your li'l war with 'Cheap
Heat' Derek has kinda side-tracked ya', Williams. You've lost focus, kid. But I'll beat some into you Monday...
Daddy didn't raise no fool, Rick... I'm ready for you. I survived you once. That's fact. Monday, I take ya' ass out again. That'll jus' be pleasure.
Big plans Monday, Ess-Cee. The New Evolution makes it's presence
_known_. Me an' Chris... we here to shake things up few times, then kick it around for good measure.
We're The Upstarts. We're the Next Evolution. We're The _New_ Evolution. Hi."
