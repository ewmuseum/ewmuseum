this is just a sample roleplay from a free agent

16 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I save most of roleplays, so this is just one I picked out randomly to show my ability. I am looking for a federation that has a spot for a top group of heels. We do not want to go to a new federation, because they are a waste of time generally so please do not respond if you are just trying to get your fed off the ground.
[scene opens with Furious Steele sitting on a chair in the middle of an empty room. A lights hangs from the ceiling right above him and has a shade on it that funnels the light only on Furious Steele while the rest of the room is pitch black. Steele is wearing his black sleeveless "I Hate You"
T-shirt, blue jean cut-off shorts, and black army boots. His black straggly hair is soaking wet and covers his eyes, but the scowl on his face is very clear. He is staring at The Imperial which is laying across his lap.]
Furious: This evening I faced many forces which attempted to dethrone the
Furious One. I faced a great warrior who has the strength to fight his own fights while facing his fears. I faced the lord wish he follows who would like nothing more then a heathen such as the Furious One to fall to the feet of one of his solders. I faced the curse of the Imperial Belt which kept others from ever holding the belt for more then a moment. The difference between the Furious One and the other past champions is that they saw the
Imperial title as the end of an epic quest while I see it as the beginning of a new era. An era where the opinions of the fans don't matter, where the ideals of the other wrestlers are of no concern.
[Scene flashes of the Wrestler awards for November being displayed. Award after Award goes by only the name of Furious Steele is called once, but the
Regulators are called for award after award. Time after time their names are called. ]
Furious: As you can plainly see the fans complete disdain for the Furious
One has blinded them of giving me the awards I deserve. It is nothing new to the Furious One. The fans have tried to keep me down since the day I arrived in the FWA. The Wrestlers of the FWA have an overwhelming jealousy for the
Furious One that prevents them from giving him the due he is deserved. I will get the recognition that I deserve yet though. I will continue to face anybody who dares oppose the Furious One, and I shall continue to rack of victories until every last soul in the stands has no chose but to accept the
Furious One as the Greatest WRESTLER ever to enter the squared circle..
[Scene flashes of Kidd Alec's latest comments about how he has faced many people much larger then him and even beat the all mighty Titan.]
Furious: When I began at the Warehouse at the age of sixteen I only weighed about 160 pounds and took severe beatings from men much larger. At that time
I was able to overcome the size difference in the men who were less talented to gain victory. As I grew older I filled out and though I still lack great physical size others have been blessed with I understand how to take their disadvantages and make them my advantages. When I get my hands on you Alec,
I am not going to let go until I have claimed victory. I am going to get you on the mat and choke you out until you can not go any longer.
[Furious begins to move his hands like he was actually choking somebody. His faces has an intent expression of almost pleasure.]
Furious: Your high flying tactics may dazzle the crowds while your martial art ability makes your mentor proud but they shall not save you Alec. You will make one mistake and that shall be your downfall. You shall make that mistake and then you shall feel the Hand Full of Hate. As the ref counts one two three as your limp body stares at the lights you shall know why I am the greatest warrior ever, and you are one of the greatest light weights.
[scene flashes of TJ telling the world about his daddy was in the mob and has connections, anther flash of TJ stating how is Daddy died a couple of years ago, flash of TJ making excuses why he did not win at the last PPV
[did not train etc.] and yet another flash of TJ talking about how his Daddy would buy the FWA since he could not win on his own.]
Furious: TJ, I don't know which grows faster. Your Lies, or your excuses. Your Daddy was a Mobster, long since dead, and now he is resurrected as a potential buyer of the FWA. Your Daddy can buy ever soul in the federation, but he can not buy the Furious One. He can not offer me anything that would allow me to let a fool like yourself defeat me. As for your training methods, that is your own fault. Your vast wealth deters you from spending the hours of physical and mental preparation required to compete at this level. Come Wednesday you are going to feel pain, sweet blood, and at the end you are going to beg for mercy. I live, and I die for every match..
Every match is just another chance for me to show the world why they shouldn't have sold me out.
[scene fades with Steele standing and turning the light out and the room goes black.]
