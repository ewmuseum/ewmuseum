16 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Nick Kray and Christopher Carter introduced themselves in RMW's previous interview show: "In Your Face"]
[The black screen switches to a shot of their past interview segment.] [Kray is seen conversing with the principle of a school, at a graduation speech where he was last seen by RMW cameras. Focusing in on the conversation the following is heard...
[PRINCIPLE]: "What the hell do you think your doing. This is suppose to be a graduation speech, your suppose to be setting an example. We here don't tolerate that type of language."
[NICK KRAY]: "Sorry... I uhh.. I thought.." [PRINCIPLE]: "That's just the problem you thought." ...The scenery fades away and the screen turns back to black, the Narration continues.] [After Kray and Carter's interesting introduction, RMW President Josh Orloski decided it time to test his tag team talent, thusly he signed a match between
Disgruntled and Deadly and Nick Kray and Christopher Carter. In response Kray and Carter have compiled the following interview for the RMW fans, so without further ado...]
[The camera fades into the new scenery, obviously the present time. The focus of the camera is concentrated on Nick Kray and Christopher Carter. Kray wears a pair of blue jeans and a white t-shirt partially covered by his silver jacket, while Carter sports a Nautica overcoat, and a pair of jeans. They appear to be in an airport, somewhere in the rest area.]
[NICK KRAY] - "Ha... That flight attendant, she was quite the lady. You wouldn't know though, because she turned you down. What was it you said again?
Oh.. Oh yeah I remember now, "I heard milk does a body good, but damn how much milk have you been drinking?" Now if my memory serves me correct, she slapped you didn't she?"
[CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "Shaddup Nick! It's worked before, she just wasn't my type. She obviously wasn't sophisticated like me."
[NICK KRAY] - "Buddy, sophisticated? I doubt you even know what that word means." [CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "It aint like you haven't been strikin out with the ladies. Your pick up line was so retarded I can't even remember it. So there!"
[NICK KRAY] - "Uh huh, looks like you got me. Speaking of sophisticated, what about Disaster and Deadly. They're quite the bunch."
[CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "Hmm... sophisticated?" [NICK KRAY] - "From what I haven't heard about them they are... well you know." [CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "What?" [NICK KRAY] - "Well, Chris from the name I've gathered they are hired guns. RMW's President is out to get us, so he hired the Disgruntled Assassins of
Death to get us."
[CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "Really? I feel proud. To think, they hired Assassins of
Disgruntled Wrestlers to put us out of commission. That really says alot.
Seriously though Nick, you're off your rocker." [NICK KRAY] - "We must've made quite the impression the other night. Just think, the Rocky Mountain's Hired Guns are after us."
[CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "Wait a second. How again do you know that the RMW
President hired the Disgruntled Death Bringers to defeat and retire us?"
[NICK KRAY] - "Well... umm, see... Chris you just have to read between the lines. And I was readin' between those lines and discovered RMW's evil secret.
They want to get rid of us, and they hired the Death Disgruntals to do it." [CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "You sure you just aren't paranoid? Maybe it's a legit match so that the RMW can chock up some rankings for the tag team division...
ya know no ploy to defeat us." [NICK KRAY] - "Can't be... I'm tellin you they are hired guns! And this whole idea was masterminded by Josh Orloski, President of RMW. He hired them because they're worse than disgruntled post office employees, and when you find people like that they are always hired guns. Obviously they're after us. Why else would we have a match with the Disgruntled Death Duo? I mean think about it, it makes so much sense."
[CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "Nick, I see the logic there. You definately have a point." [NICK KRAY] - "Damn straight I do!" [CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "Well as to not completely waste the RMW fan's time, I think I'll discuss our match a bit. Maybe we can stray away from Nick's paranoid theories that RMW President Josh Orloski is out to get him."
[NICK KRAY] - "He's out to get us I tell you!" [CHRISTOPHER CARTER] - "Like I was saying...We're anxiously awaiting our match with the Discontent Deadly Force from the Future, and we're well prepared. At least phsyically. I'm not so sure Nick is ready mentally anymore... but hey we'll give it our best shot."
[NICK KRAY] - "We better give it our best shot, they might have guns. After all they're hired guns working for Josh Orloski!"
[The scene of the airport slowly fades to darkness as the fans are left wondering if there is any truth to Nick's Theory, and whether or not Kray and
Carter will be able to fend against Disgruntled and Deadly, after all they can't even seem to remember their name.]
