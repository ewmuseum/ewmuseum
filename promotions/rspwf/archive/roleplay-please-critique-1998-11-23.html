Roleplay, Please Critique

23 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens on a cool night. Light has not yet been shown as the sun has yet to rise. The grass is moist from morning dew and the air is crisp with a certain coldness to it. Frost sits upon windows and such and the streets are asleep. Not a single light is on in the city of Detroit, all is quiet. The camera takes us down a dark alley, past a slew of garbage, scattered along the ground. As we travel farther, we see a steel door to the right. Noises are heard on the inside and it is apparent that not all are asleep in the city. The door is unlocked so we open it slowly, a thick smoke pours out of the room. The door is opened completely and we see hundreds of chairs with hundreds of people to occupy them. People are screaming and cheering as we observe a large stage with a podium on it, in the front of the room. A banner hangs above the stage that reads "Sik".
Suddenly as the clock strikes midnight, all turns silent and everyone takes their seats. A red spot light comes on and out walks Sik to stand beneath it. The people cheer loudly as he makes his way to the podium. He motions for the crowd to quiet and they cooperate. The light is turned off and another set of lights in front of the stage come on and shine on Sik. He begins to speak.)
I must now, begin a ruthless attack on all, for now I am pitted against every member of this fed. Not one man will be able to defeat me. I will hold the Heavyweight Championship as well as my many other trophies.
Blood will spill by the gallon and flesh will be ripped and torn from the bone and the opposers of the Sik one will be exterminated. Every man for himself in a battle, trying their hardest not to let themselves perish like the rest. The Sikness will spread rapidly and break down the immune and nervous systems of the so called competition. I feel as if I have eaten from the plant of Henbane, a plant thought to cause insanity and hallucinations. My mind is filled with violent prophetic visions and I will wreak havoc and strike terror in the eyes of everyone as I have done many times before.
Two truths are told, as happy prologues to the swelling act of the imperial theme. I will reign as king and defend my title honorably and I shall never fall. No repentance will be taken lightly. The force of all hell and all that is evil will come crashing down like thunder on a dark, stormy night. My purpose here is simply to conquer and destroy all who stand in my path to glory and success. I will prove myself once more and be named victorious as my journey to being labeled the greatest soon comes nearer. Many people wish to see me fall but their wishes shall never be granted for I will come in fighting and I will fight to the very end. My prophetic visions have forecasted your dooms and by the hands of the most violent man on earth you will surely all see a bloody gruesome end to your lives.
I have fought in matches of the most extreme nature and always exited the ring with my hand raised in victory. The mold was broken when I was created because there is not a man alive that could ever compare to my intensity or tenacity. Come you spirits that tend on mortal thoughts, unsex me here, fill me, from the crown to the toe, top full of the direst cruelty!
Make thick my blood, stop up the access and passage to remorse. I will make a graveyard of the ring and your corpses will rot in the heat from the fires of hell burning deep within my soul. It is too late for me to stop now, I have been driven to the limit. You are all in for a catastrophic change of events, a cataclysm if you will. Your existence will be a thing of the past. Now as my blood runs through my veins even more rapidly I begin to feel the anger, swelling inside my mind. I must let all this pain and aggression out on each and every one of you. Prepare for a showing of gruesome violence to the extremity. The force of nature itself, in all its glory is less powerful than I am by far. My fury will be matched only by that of a ruthless army, fighting for their lives.
A new beginning and a new king has come forth. From beneath the streets, in a place unseen by man himself, the Sik one roams. An underground prophet, the bringer of provisions and revelations. The Sik one will conquer all others who oppose him. With a love for violence and a hate for all who are weak, the Sikness will run rampant through this fed like the plague. You are no match for me. You will be crushed like all the rest.
Death has sent you to me so that I may release your soul from it’s frame to start it’s long journey to hell. You are an unnecessary waste of space and
I shall dispose of you quite brutally and forcefully. Your corpses will be piled on top of all the others. You will see how it feels to have your teeth knocked out of place and into your brain. You will be a bloody mess after our grueling battle. I, too will be drenched with blood, with not a drop of it being my own. The era of the Sikness has begun and soon the championship gold will be mine. I will go down in the history books as being the greatest champion who ever lived. I will continue to reign as champion as long as humanly possible. The name Sik will be known all across the globe by every man, woman and child alive. All I need is but 5 minutes and the opposition as a whole will be desecrated.
I once had a dream that I was the leader of a great army in the medieval days. What a great misfortune I would witness when I saw that my entire army lay slain. There I was, Cornered by the whole opposing army in full. I knew I had no choice but to fight each and everyone of them until they stand no more. Suddenly some great surge of energy and inspiration flowed throughout my blood and my eyes burned with a bright fire. I raised my metal blade high in the air and began striking at the enemy with all my might. Blood flew and metal crashed as the sounds of war could be heard for miles. Before I knew it I stood alone in a field of bloody bodies, the sole survivor. Is it more to be a man and face your fears, even though you may be slain in doing so? Or is it more of a man to know when you have no chance of surviving and run for your life, therefore admitting your fear?
Maybe now that ultimate fame and success is within my reach, my conquest comes nearer to an end. It also might be that it has just begun.
I must now work harder than ever to achieve what I have already worked so hard for. This will be my most astounding victory in my wrestling history.
No other man will take my glory or take my throne. Now I become champion and begin to reign as such. My prize will be untouchable, as will I. I will defend my title against all who wish to see an early demise. Friends and foes from the past will be seen through my eyes only as victims to the
Sikness. Many people now seek revenge on the Sik one and I must keep my guard up at all times. It will be difficult for me to trust another, for trust is blind in war. Friends will be sorted from amongst the heap of dead bodies, the casualties of war. All personal issues shall be set aside and I will tend to these people as soon as they are released from the hospital.
Since the closing of this fed I have journeyed to other places, finding success there as well. I have captured new gold, crushed many opponents, and in the 3 places I have ventured, I have been undefeated, as I will be here as well. Like a flame, burning in the cold wind, you will all be extinguished soon. My thirst for violence has now reached a new high.
Blood is the sight I desire most! I guess I could be labeled insane for a number of reasons. One could be that people thought I was crazy for living on the streets for so long. I always told those people though, that these were my roots, and just like a plant, if you don’t have roots, you will surely perish. I do not think I am insane, but rather permanently scarred. I can not shake the visions of blood and death from my mind. I have seen them too many times. These violent tendencies had become a part of me. I needed that aroma of death.
I crave that which is hard-core or extremely gruesome. I trained my mind hard for these competitions. I learned to control my violent rages. I learned to focus that energy towards my opponent and obliterate him. I shall let no human being stand in the way of what I desire. I have survived for 17 years on the streets, alone, with no help.
I have the power to conquer anyone I so please. Oppose me if you will but you will get nothing but death as a reward for your stupidity. All others will watch in awe as I dismantle every limb on your bodies and tie your spinal columns in knots.
I speak only of the truth and how I feel. The Sikness will run rampant through this fed and spread like wildfire. Everyone tries to ignore me, they hope I will just fade away. It will take much more that that to get rid of the greatest ever, the Sik one. This ring will look like a god damn graveyard when I am done. No more will I be looked down upon. No more will
I settle for being anything but the best. I will reign supreme on my own.
The gold will shine around my waist as bright as the morning sun. Make your hospital reservations now, and I will prepare your gravesite, for you will all die a slow, painful, suffering death and your journey into the flames of hell will be twice as bad!
I want to hurt each and everyone of you! The Sikness will invade your body like a troop of the most powerful soldiers invading a weak country to conquer and destroy it. I have a vaulting ambition to become champion of this fed. All those who wish to get their revenge upon me, let me hear you now. None will be able to hide from the most violent man on earth. I came here alone and I will rule this fed alone and no in is going to stop me. I do not plan to fight each and everyone of you individually in this battle royal, no, I am going to take you all out as a whole, I see you all as the opposition, I know no names.
Into battle you go, unknowingly sent on a suicide mission against the
Sikkest man on the face of this earth. A flame burning at the highest possible temperature could not match the wrath of the anger that resides inside my evil heart. Not a day goes by that I don’t think about how much I hate and how much I feel the need for blood of another human being. These thoughts are what drive me to be the greatest and to be the most violent man on earth. I have accomplished everything I have earned by working harder than anyone else on this planet. I have sacrificed myself many times to succeed further, and my blood, sweat and tears have been poured into every single thing I have ever done.
After a million times the sun rises, and a million times it sets, I will still remain standing as the greatest ever. Untouched and unscathed I will be until the end of time. The legacy of my name will be one which will be etched in stone and remembered by all for years and years. My accomplishments will not go unnoticed for no man has ever been capable of doing what I have in my entire career. I have only been in this business for a short time but I have risen to the top with astonishing quickness. I can not be moved no matter who I face. Many say every man is unbeatable, but they do not realize that I am not every man. My home is here, in the fields of violence, and I will fulfill my destiny here, on this night.
I believe that all events are not pre-decided and each day is taken as it comes. I do not believe that my path has already been traveled down by somebody else. I have created my own destiny and I will travel the path that I have cleared MYSELF! You shall all perish without warning and that much is true. How you shall perish is a question I am still pondering. I plan to make it slow and painful, and so far I have only decided on a handful of ways to destroy you all. Whichever way you go, is fine with me as long as you do not live anymore. Do not humor me with your futile threats and actions. Come meet the bringer of death face to face and accept your mission of destruction like a true warrior would.
Now dark consumes the light and the warmth of day is overwhelmed by the cold of night. I plan now to attack without mercy, to shatter dreams as well as bones. I will be victor, there is no doubt, but I will have to work like never before. I will be inside a ring of people who despise me, perhaps even hate me. I will show an upheaval of strength and power as I continually beat on every single one of you. This match is mine! I have it won! Now I can not be stopped, the apocalypse is now. I told you all that
I would return and wreak more havoc than anyone has ever witnessed. I am not to be taken lightly. You might also like to know that I have a major secret for this fed that will be announced after I win my championship belt.
This secret will have a major impact on this fed and will change wrestling history forever. The sik one has made his return, the end begins now.
To all those who are thinking about stepping into the ring with me, I would like to share with you a prayer you might like to say before engaging in battle with me.
"The Prayer To The Sik One" Sik 1:87 reads: I pray now to thee, thy god of thy Sik, have mercy on my soul. Thou art a noble man and my leader. I call to thy begging for forgiveness of my sins against you and wish upon my life that you will not taketh it from me. My soul cries out in fear for I have angered you, god. I hope thou doth hear this prayer and have mercy on my weak soul. Never before hath there been a god as wonderful as you, I worship you, Amen.
"The love of violence is deeper than any love imaginable. The skies will rain down with blood from my victim’s corpses and I will be drenched with blood, but not a drop of it being my own. A new king will rise and the flames of hell will reach the surface of the earth and all it’s inhabitants will be engulfed in it’s devilish inferno. The Sikkest man on earth has foreseen these happenings and will be the sole survivor of this near
apocalypse" -Sik So says the Sik. He who has opposed me in the past will see a dark end to his bright future.
(The crowd goes insane as Sik finishes a brilliant speech. Cheers and chants fill the room noisily and the Sik one raises his hands high in the air and the stage lights go off and on comes the red spot light once again.
The crowd is cheering louder than ever now as Sik disappears into the back and all is dark again. Loud chanting starts and the camera man makes his way out of the room. He shuts the metal door behind him and is now back in the alley. A large shadow is seen underneath a street light. The camera goes over to see who it is. Standing before the camera, underneath the light stands Sik himself, cigarette in his mouth with his head down. We walk over to him and he stares deep into the camera)
I have spoken many words tonight and I am confident that now my title will again be in my clutches. I have trained harder than ever and I am ready to recapture my throne and rule this fed with an iron fist. I have said before that the true king will rise again and indeed he will. My return will be a moment to remember. Enemies of the Sik one beware, and friends of the Sik one, don’t prove yourselves to be enemies for you will suffer the same consequences. I must go now, I have no more to say besides this one thing… my time has come, I can not fail now.
(He turns and walks off into the distance without another word. Puffs of smoke rise up above his head as he vanishes into the darkness of the streets. The camera fades to black, the audio cuts and the program ends.)
