*Critique* Dan "Phat Man" Falk

9 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene fades in to the backstage area of a local XPW show in lower California. Exodus walks past the camera, coming off his last interview to the packed arena. His gold strap is layed across his left shoulder and he slaps hands with a backstage worker, with a "Staff" shirt on. He walks off into the background, until he's not noticeable anymore. Another door is heard opening, then closing, over to the right of the camera. It scrolls over to see a caucasian man walking down a hallway. In his right hand, hangs a black gym bag with the word "Spalding" on the outside. Covering his chest is a black t-shirt with "P-H-A-T" printed across in red. He's got long white denim shorts on, that hang low to his knees. The man, OBVIOUSLY known as Dan "Phat Man" Falk, walks towards the camera, whistling "This Time Around" from that new Tommy commercial. His hair is tied back into a tail behind him, as his newly grown in goatee is very noticeable on his chin. His silver earring shines off the lights above him, with the camera watching his left side, walking past. Falk lifts up his left hand to his eyes, and covers them with a pair of sunglasses.
He motions for the camera to follow him, as he smiles, with his ever-so-famous smirk. He looks over to the left and says "See ya lata" to an unknown person, as the man yells something back. Falk looks back to the camera, making sure it's following, as he pushes open a door and you're exposed to the bright lights of Southern CA. Falk walks over to a brand new blue '99 Dodge Ram, as he unlocks the front door, throws his bag in the trailer bed, opens the door, and steps in. He unlocks the passenger door, as the cameraman steps in himself. Falk starts up the truck and backs up out of the spot and speeds out of the arena lot. He looks over to the camera and smiles as he takes a right turn.]
Dan "Phat Man" Falk: "You want music?" [Falk reaches over to the radio and Lenny Kravitz's "American Woman" has just begun, as Falk smiles and turns it up slightly. He puts his left arm out the window and drives with his other hand on top of the window. Around his neck is a leather strap, with the WWF's Brood© symbol hanging from it. He smiles and looks over at the camera again.]
Dan Falk: "Well well well....back on XPW TV. Yeah yeah....I know....you've all missed me. Who couldn't? Haa haa...well, falk. I've been gone for _1_ goddamn month, and look at the shit we have now? Let's see.....Guru...HAAA
HAA...Guru is the extreme champion....now there's the funniest shit I've ever seen in my life. Jimmy Bowels....oops, I mean ahh....falk, what's his name?
Jame....John....ahhh, who cares? Buttkid, why'd you have GURU as the extreme champion? He's like....the Diet Coke of extreme. Just one calorie....not extreme enough. Get what I mean? Good. Oh, and Guru....ya damn fool....you can say all your big 18-letter words back at me for this. But I don't give a falk. Because you're still a fool. And if you DO decide to be a hardass, and sing your TVTC songs, or hell....even make a.....69 LIST ABOUT ME!! I wouldn't....really, give a shit. Because we all know you're just a little bitch, in a little boy's body."
[The song fades out, as commercials start to play. Falk reaches forward, as he rolls to a stop at a stoplight. He presses a button on the radio and a moment of silence again, as "Deepest Blueist" by LL Cool J begins to play. Falk leans back into his seat and takes off through the green light. He reaches up and adjusts his rear-view mirror, then brings his hand back to the wheel.]
Dan Falk: "And next up on the hit list....Exodus. No, wait, in fact....I'll leave him for later. Just because he's the world champ. I'll move on to "The
Prodigy" Jason Cline. Oh, and just one thing.....just because I was
MIA...doesn't mean I wasn't paying attention. I saw this guy. He came in, has a big name.....everyone knows this falker. And he talked a bunch of shit....and LOST to the #1 nobody, Whiplash. Even though he deserved it.....and then, proving the maturity level of the 'big boys', he decided to resign from X-rated Pro Wrestling. Tsk tsk tsk....Cline, I've said it before....and I'll say it again. I respect you.....but you're one, dumb ass mother falker. Shiiitt....that's all the time I wanna spend on him."
[Falk reaches into his armrest, between the seats, and pulls out a piece of paper. On the white paper, is some black handwriting, that makes Dan smile and laugh to himself.]
Dan Falk: "Heh heh....yeah, this is XPW's next War of the World's card." [The song fades out as the disk jockey begins a rant. Falk leans forward and pushes another button, to change the station, as "What's it gonna be" by Busta
Rhymes and Janet, begins to play. Falk sighs, and he looks back at the paper and laughs.]
Dan Falk (in a redneck voice): "Wal, whaddya know? We's gonna haf ourselves a fine old fashioned tag team match! Fry mah hide!"
[Falk laughs again at himself, and puts the paper back where he got it.] Dan Falk: "At the next, War of the Worlds....August 13th, to make a plug....I will be participating in a tag team match. Fresh off a world title match....I get into a tag team match. With whom, you may ask? Chandler Lewis....against
"Good Ol'" Jarred Matthews and "Hey, I just beat Jason Cline" Whiplash. Haa haa....NONE of these men know me....know anything about me....and to them, I'm simply a name. Well now, I'm a challenge. And I'm a power....and....I'M YO
DADDY! Chandler Lewis....lemme think back....shit, I can't remember one thing you've ever done. Oh wait, now that I think back a couple weeks....yeah, I remember seeing a spot from you. Not bad, not bad at all.....'sides, I like you in the ring...you've got the moves. But you're not as good as me. Don't get me wrong.....I like you....you're a good kid. But ya can't kick _MY_ ass.
And as for "I can talk for 20 seconds and beat Cline" Whiplash...don't even try it, son." [The song switches over to the disc jockey again, as Falk reaches for the radio. But before he gets to it, "Tell Me It's Real" by K-Ci and JoJo, begins to play, and Falk leans back into his seat.]
Dan Falk: "And not to forget none other than JaRrEd 'Cline is my hero' MaTtHeWs. Yeah, you're a big shit-talker. You seem like a young Cline, anyhow. But you suffer from the same dilemma that both Whiplash and my good partner, Chandler Lewis suffer from...you're good.....but ya can't beat me.
I'm not sayin' you won't go far....cuz you'll get places. But as far as you know...I'd kick the livin' shit out of you."
[Falk stops at another stoplight. He reached under the armrest again and looks into it, trying to find something, but the light turns green and Falk takes his hand out, and drives off. He switches over to the next lane and takes a left onto a dirt road. He starts speeding up, as he flies down the dirt.]
Dan Falk: "Hell yeah, this is _MY_ kinda drivin'!" [He gets to the end of the road and turns right, onto a paved road, and he takes a normal speed, going down the smooth road. Falk changes the station, as the song fades down, and "All Star" by Smashmouth begins to play throughout the truck. Falk leans back and puts his arm back out the window.]
Dan Falk: "Now, the moment you've all been waiting for....my rant on the XPW
World Heavyweight Champion....Exodus. Hell, falk callin' him Exodus, he's
MeccA. Plain and simple, never been anything else, whether he calls himself
Adam Irons, Exodus....he'll always be MeccA. Mecc, why the hell are you hiding behind another name, bro? You're a falkin' legend, my man. And don't tell me you don't remember me. You my nigga! Guess that takes care of that.....so now
I talk about MeccA, the XPW World Champ. You deserve that belt. I dunno if you deserved the shot....being in XPW ohh....a week, maybe 2. But you deserve the belt. Maybe not now....but ya know. I dunno if I could take you. You've got alot of talent, and you obviously haven't had any rust from your run, years ago. I have to say, MeccA....of any wrestler, I have every seen in my entire life....all of my upmost respect, goes straight to you. Because of you, I was developed into what I am today. Yeah, sounds like I'm puttin' my lips to your ass, but I truly mean this. MeccA....if I got in the ring against you....I'm sure it would be one hell of a match. But I don't want that to happen.....not just yet anyway. We're takin' how we took it in the old days. You go your way....I'll go mine. But when we meet, this time.....it'll be a helluva lot different than last time. Keep in touch...and I'll see ya when I get there."
[Falk pulls off down a backstreet and turns onto a main road. The street is lit up with numerous hotels, restaurants, and other businesses. Falk pulls in at a Holiday Inn and parks the car. The cameraman gets out of his side as Falk slams his door shut. He reaches in the trunkbed and takes his bag out. He reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a brown leather wallet. He takes a few bills out and gives them to the cameraman.]
Dan Falk: Here, call a cab, get wherever the falk you need. 's this thing still on? Yeah? Well ahh....Jarred Matthews....and Whiplash.....watch out....War of the worlds....you're both gonna get falked up. That's a promise, suckas.
[Falk walks up into the hotel as the camera fades away into nothingness.] -Dan "Phat Man" Falk [XPW] 1-1
