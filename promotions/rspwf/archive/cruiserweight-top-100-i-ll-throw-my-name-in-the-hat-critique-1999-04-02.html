Cruiserweight Top 100, I'll Throw My Name In The Hat, Critique

2 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera pans in to a place not unlike a desert however by the cheap wallpaper you can notice it is a set of some sort. As you look across many people are shown shuffling around and about but in nearly a matter of three to four seconds the set clears with one person standing in front of a camera]
[The man is Devon Case and he is dressed in a football uniform and is holding a football in his left hand. There is a kid who looks unaware of what is going on over to his right, about 40 feet from Case. The director is by the camera]
DIRECTOR - Action!!! [Devon is looking into the camera] DEVON CASE - Hi, i am Devon Case, professional wrestler and one handsome man, but right now there is something I would like to talk about that doesn't concern wrestling in the least. It is that which we call Parapeligesy and it plagues thousands of men, women, and children worldwide.
[Devon walks back a few feet] DEVON CASE - See that child over there, Kenny has been plagued with this disease since birth and knows no better, but through research and development we can help people like Kenny. It's just that simple, it's just like a hot night in the desert and a football.
[Devon throws the football tight spiraled to the kid. The kid, obviously unaware, gets nailed square in the face with the ball. Devon looks pissed]
DIRECTOR - CUT!!! Damn it. Devon I am so sorry. DEVON CASE - [walking off the set] F**k this, I am outta here. [to the director] You tellin me you can't get another kid out there, sure he looks retarded but does he have to act like it for Christ's sake! Damn, the catch line doesn't even make any sense, "It's like a hot night in the desert and a football?" WHAT THE HELL DOES THAT MEAN?! And why do I look like a football player when, when, I am not a football player. This is damn ridiculous. Bye and good f**king riddance.
DIRECTOR - Come on Dev, come back, COME BACK! [Case walks off the set, grabs his clothes and jacket off a nearby stage area and walks out of the door. Precious, sensing this had already gone to get the car and was waiting outside in their black '99 Lexus. He hops in the passenger seat as the director is too late getting outside.]
PRECIOUS - [driving] That may very well be the stupidest thing I have ever seen, how many times did you bounce that damn football off that kids head today?
DEVON CASE - At least five times. Goddamn ridiculous how they set me up for a sh*t gig like that, mustbe the DSWA head office tryin to mess with me again.
First off I get a five on one match for the pay-per-view in addition to my two other matches, and then I get booked for a retarded, literally, commercial.
What next? PRECIOUS - [laughing] Maybe they will get you a gay porn gig. DEVON CASE - Thanks but no thanks dear, that is damn near frightening though. This is very, very annoying, I am so angry i could just punch this window, goddamn. PRECIOUS - Whats on your mind dear, and don't just tell me the commercial, I see right through you babe.
DEVON CASE - [serious yet distern] Yeah yeah, its the pay-per-view, too many damn people I hate and only so much energy a man has. Thank god the five on one will be a piece of cake, that sets my mind at ease.
PRECIOUS - Piece of cake? How you figure? DEVON CASE - Well I watched Sycho Path's interview and he said that he is gonna make it one on one after he rips his teammates up and spits them out.
And if he can accomplish that, then I can just destroy him in under two minutes tops and go about my other business, but to actually think Sycho can defeat anybody is a challenge all unto itself. But we will see. However, that is not the match that bothers me.
PRECIOUS - Then which one is dear, you know I am here for you. Come on, give me a clue for Christ's sake.
DEVON CASE - [subdued and ready to tell] Fine, fine its just the titles all unto themselves. I have a chance at the Lightweight and Southern titles this
Sunday, which I will no doubt win both. That is about 40 to 50 pounds around my waist each and everytime I walk. But then, the next Saturday I grab the
Universal title with ease from Westside and the chump whomever that will be and that belt weighs, I say, at least 30 pounds. Do you know how much weight
I have to carry on my waist after next Saturday? It's absurd.
PRECIOUS - [angry at the worry] Wait, let me get this straight. You are worried not about your matches, as fierce and as streneous as they are, but you ARE worried about the weight of the belts on your waist?!
DEVON CASE - Your damn right, haven't you heard of people with curved spines because they carry too much weight? I don't want that to happen to me, I don't want to be helpless like that stupid kid back there, I don't want---
[Precious cuts him off]
PRECIOUS - Here Dev, I will hold one of them for you, how about that? Does that suffice you at all? Damn stubborn man.
DEVON CASE - [smile on his face] That makes me feel soooo much better really. Now I don't have to worry, I am free as a bird hehe. Except for the fact that
Westside Walavan got a victory along with me.
PRECIOUS - [eyes wide open] That's what is bothering you isn't it? Westside getting a cheap victory at your expense right? I knew it.
DEVON CASE - You knew it my ass. I just don't find it fair that he gets the title shot along with me, like he deserves the shot? He deserves a swift ass kickin Case style, he knows it, and more importantly I know it. So good ole
WW, next Saturday and furthermore this Sunday be ready to be closed down via
Case-closed and hope your trip to the hospital is long and painful.
[McDonald's is seen coming up] Precious, could we stop there really quick, I am hungry for a fat Big Mac.
