Feds Seeking Wrestlers: Rampage World Wrestling Then apply to Rampage World Wrestling. You want a federation that has continually graced it's members for nearly 3 years? You want a federation that bases its results on only your roleplaying ability and dedication? You want a federation that has great storylines but doesn't have to betray a hard-working wrestler to put one over? You want a federation that breaks all known barriers to provide fun? Then you really want to apply to Rampage World Wrestling[URL=http://www.geocities.com/rampageworldwrestling]Rampage World Wrestling is a place where you'll get treated fairly. Superstars such as Legend Inc. have gone on record as saying it was the place that gave them the chance to shine because they were treated fairly and judged on performance. I'd hasten to say it has one of the lowest turnover of performers in the leagues, people WANT to stay. It's creative, it's different, but its always fair and straight down the middle. Yes, we have heinous heels, but matches go to the deserving recipient. So take the step to the New Frontier. Rampage World Wrestling is professional, up front, honest and creative. Owners who between them have a decade of experience at the highest levels. People who are constantly having fun and putting out creative material. Come on, make the click. But don't wait, click now and receive a free Jinitsu Cutterly Set. If you click within the next 15 minutes receive a free Steak Cutter, a knife that can cut through your ex-girlfriend's boyfriend's muffler and fuel tank!
