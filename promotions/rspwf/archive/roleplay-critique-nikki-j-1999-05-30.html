Roleplay(Critique?) - Nikki J

30 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera opens up to inside of the head office building of the ICW. Phones are ringing. Secretaries answer them while doing other things as well. The camera moves down the main hallway as workers walk back and forth down and across. . and even some are standing around not doing anything. The camera continues down the hallway until we reach a set of double doors. The camera moves in on them and the knob turns and it opens up and a figure steps out, that figure is of the genetic freak herself, Nikki J. Nikki closes the door behind her with a slight grin on her face. She then begins to walk down the hallway looking around at the people buzz around, some stopping and looking at her.)
(Nikki is wearing a pair of tight cut bluejean shorts, and a blue denim button up shirt. She also has on a pair of brown timberland workboots, and a gold necklace. A pair of sunglasses are poking out of her shirt pocket as well. Her hair is hanging down instead of in it's usual ponytail. Nikki stops a bit ways down the hall and turns and looks at herself in a mirror. She knocks an out of place hair back into it's place then turns and continues down the hall. As she walks she begins to speak. . .)
"GENETIC PERFECTION" NIKKI J - I just got the news. . .Mistress Sonia, I see you decided to step away from the ICW for a while, and just as our match was coming closer. (Shakes head) Pity. . such a pity. (Grins) Now poor, poor
Artista is put in your place, it's bad enough she has to face me on Tuesday but now. . (Nikki stops and chuckles almost evilly .) NIKKI J - Now she has to face me at Showcase as well. . . (Nikki pauses for a moment. .) NIKKI J - Well, that is if she is able to make it to Showcase after Rockin' Da
World. (Nikki continues to walk once again, an elevator dings and a few people step off before the doors shut again as Nikki looks down there then continues on. .)
NIKKI J - Artista you accepted the challenge. I stated a few times that if you had any brains in that little head of yours you would stay as far away from me as possible, but it appears as if you chose not to heed my warning. That's a shame, a real shame, for you that is. Artista, Tuesday we will headline the card. .or should I say, *I* will headline the card, while you will be nothing more than another preverbal lamb sent to the slaughter. . .
(Nikki reaches the elevators as the doors slide open, She steps on as a man is in it. He looks at Nikki and moves over to the corner, almost sheepishly. Nikki turns back to the camera as the doors shut. . )
NIKKI J - Artista, you are a fool. You quote a popular saying "The Bigger they are the harder they fall." Well you forgot the second half of that infamous quote which reads "But they don't fall that often." and Tuesday night the only time I will fall is when I crash down onto you for the pinfall (Nikki grins)
Sorry, but that's the way it's going to go down. . . (The doors open once more and Nikki steps off as we see we're now in the main lobby. Nikki walks down towards the door and walks out of the building. She then reaches into her shirt pocket and pulls out a the sunglasses and places them on. . )
NIKKI J - Artista, Tuesday night, it will make what I did to you before seem like nothing, but don't worry.
(Tips the sunglasses to the bridge of her nose and winks. .) NIKKI J - I'll make sure there's enough of you left for Showcase so I can bat you around for a little while longer before taking my spot as the number one contender on my way to becoming the new champion and beginning a new era in women's wrestling, the era of. .
(Nikki winks then pushes the sunglasses back up her nose. .) NIKKI J - Genetic Perfection. . . (Nikki walks off as the camera follows then slowly fades out. . )
