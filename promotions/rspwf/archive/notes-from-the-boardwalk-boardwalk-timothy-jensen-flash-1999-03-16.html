Notes from the Boardwalk ("Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen Flash)

16 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An Enigma? (A voice. Calm, cool and collected resonates across the black void that fills your screen) A mystery? (This time the voice seems to be lost in thought as it keeps the same tone) A puzzle? (Another question to add to your confusion) Not to me you aren't! (At those last words the screen goes from dark to fuzzy to focused in on the face of one "Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen. His blue eyes sparkle in the camera's light as you can only see his face against what appears to be dark sand. Jensen has a relaxed look on his face as he looks directly into the camera.)
BTJ: No Joe Hutchings, you're easy to figure out, unlike your nickname suggests. You're no enigma, no puzzle, no mystery. You're just like everyone else in the WWWA. You're only after one thing. The gold. And at Genesis I guess it's going to be you and me to see who gets to wear that gold. But you see there's something that makes me different from the rest of the lot here in the
WWWA. I don't just fight for the gold, I fight for respect. Now don't get me wrong, the gold title belt means a lot to me, but I have to say that I'd live without that title as long as I had the respect from everyone else back there in the lockeroom.
(He stops for a second and you can see his hands go behind his head as he settles in for a nice long monolgue on R-E-S-P-E-C-T Find out what it means to me! err okay just read on and you'll find out :) )
BTJ: Respect. A word that I'm sure you know it's meaning Hutchings, but I'm also sure you don't know what it means to have respect. You see Hutch, I've been watching over the weeks and it seems that you've built up quite a reputation for putting people out of the game. You put MacGregor out, Green and
St. Clair. And I bet you're happy with yourself for doing that. Hell I think
I've even heard you brag that you're glad you've done this. Well what all this has shown me is that you have no respect for these men whose lives you've seemingly ruined. And that bothers me. Because it's people like you that stomped on me when I was down and out and living on the streets. People like you that had pockets lined with money, but were too damn stingy to respect your fellow man and spare a measly dollar so that he could find a decent meal.
(By this time Jensen has a scowl on his face as memories from Under the
Boardwalk come flooding back to him. He looks to the left once and continues speaking as he turns back to the camera)
BTJ: Respect. Anyway you say it, it amounts to appreciating what your fellow man has to contribute to the world. Hutch, by injuring those men you've taken away what they can contribute and now you would attempt to do the same with me.
Well I've fought too damn hard and long for a nobody like you to try and disrespect me. Hutch, if you think that you'll have an easy go of it, that you'll put Boardwalk out of action for a long time, and that you'll get away with it well you've got another thing coming. If that's what you think then you're showing no respect for my abilities and for what I've accomplished.
Hutch dear boy you've landed on Boardwalk and for you not to respect your surroundings when that happens is for you to commit a fatal mistake. Respect
I've earned it, you want to take it from me. Well if you're man enough to talk the talk then step to the plate and face the 'Walk!
(Jensen removes his hands from behind his head and sits up. From the surroundings behind him you can see that he was lying down underneath a boardwalk that he probably used to call home. He gets up, not bothering to dust himself off as he looks at the camera again)
BTJ: An enigma? I hardly think so. You're not hard to figure out Hutch. You're just a fool with delusions of grandeur and heart full of disrespect. Well,
Hutch if you don't respect me, if you don't appreciate my talents and if you don't think I have what it takes to earn your respect then think again fool and know that Boardwalk hands out expensive fees for ignorance gone mad.
(Jensen walks from beneath the boardwalk and out into the crisp cool night air, his hawaiian shirt blowing in the land breeze and his mind set on continuing a winning streak at Genesis
