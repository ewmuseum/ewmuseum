[Critique] IWA Carnage Flash

1 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the first time that I have posted any of my roleplays here but I'd like to know what you think.
---------------------------------------------------------- [Scene opens in a dimly lit room, there is a faint smell of mould in the air. There is a single ray of moonlight shining through a small window in the wall to the left which is cutting through the room. At the end of the moonlight ray, where the floor and walls meet, we see a figure sitting against the wall with one leg bent and the other stretched out in front of him. The figure leans forward so his head is visible in the moonlight, he has his head bowed low but it is obvious that the figure is Carnage]
Carnage: I have noticed on the Internet and in the general media people asking where is Carnage? Well the answer is that I have been taking it easy and resting a few minor cuts and scrapes that I received in my tag team match at Holiday Hell. Yes, that's the match where Blade and myself lost the
IWA Tag Titles, the titles that have been ours since the creation of this company, they had become like another member of the family and then they were taken from us. Losing a title is not simply losing the strap of gold that you carry around to show everyone that you are the best, the crème-de-la-crème in what you do, no, it goes a lot deeper than that. It hurts your pride deep down inside, it makes you wonder if you still have what it takes, the ability to carry on, if you are still able to cut it in the only thing that has become your life since you care to remember.
[Carnage reaches down and picks up a stone from the floor and begins to roll it between his finger and thumb]
It has caused me to do a lot of digging deep, a lot of soul searching to figure out exactly what to do and the best way forward. I started to look at the big picture and realised that although the tag titles will come home to their rightful place, sometime soon, losing those titles may not be such a bad thing as it means that I can start to concentrate on my singles career.
Now, I have been a two-time tag team champion and also the TV champ so I realised the next logical step in my IWA career is the American title held at the moment by ACE.
ACE, I have a great deal of respect for you not just for your in ring ability but for the way in which you have been conducting yourself in recent weeks which culminated in you coming to the aid of my good friend and fellow
WOMD member, Jared Grimm.
[Carnage throws the stone away that he was holding and picks up another] I'll admit that in the beginning I had may doubts about you, what with your disturbing behaviour towards your fellow professionals and the blatant disregard for the welfare of anyone who was in any way remotely connected to or came into contact with you, but ever since you rid yourself of the
NETwork you have become a changed man, a change for the better, and with that you have received the respect you deserve, not just from the fans but from what really matters, your piers. This new found respect that I have for you doesn't mean however that when the time comes I won't be ready to disrobe you of your title, far from it but for now you enjoy your new found glory, as somewhere down this long winding path that we are all treading, life, we will collide in the squared circle and it will be one of the greatest matches of all time.
[Carnage again throws the stone away and finally lifts his head although only half his face is visible in the moonlight]
But for now I will continue plugging away and laying waste to however dares step in my path and wait with anticipation for our date with destiny.
Which brings me nicely onto the next hopeful who is trying to obstruct my path to the title, a newcomer by the name of Armageddon. Armageddon in the dictionary is depicted as being the scene of the final conflict between the forces of good and evil at the end of the world, but we will not have to wait until the end of the world as we will see the final conflict between good and evil this Friday night in the great state of San Jose, California, where you, my friend will have the shortest known career of any professional sportsman ever, as I return you to the insipid nothingness from where ever it is you call home. It will be the greatest ever scene of debauchery every witnessed, I guaran-dam-tee it.
So, Armageddon, you had better be physically and mentally prepared for it will not only be your body that I commit to oblivion but also your soul.
[Carnage lets out a little laugh to himself and allows a smirk to come across his face as he leans back against the wall so that his face is no longer visible]
