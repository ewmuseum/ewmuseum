[AWA Roleplay]Tony Lattanzio

3 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fade In [Camera pans over an office room, a den in a very luxurious house. There is a bookshelf with many books on them, including the works of Edgar
Allen Poe and William Shakespeare. Seated at a vast desk is Tony Lattanzio.
One single light is on, on small lamp that is bearly penetrating through that darkness. Tony is wearing a buttoned undershirt, with the top few buttons unbuttoned, as he looks tired and weary. His silky coal hair is tangled up, looking as if he just got out of bed. In front of him is many papers, with numbers on them which probably represent his financial status. He looks up, and sees the camera.]
Tony: Finally, the time has come. No, this is not my first appearence with the Apex Wrestling Association, I do have a record of 2 wins and 1 loss, which will certainly get better as the weeks tick by. But this is my very first official interview with the AWA. Due to personal obligations, I wasn't able to get in front of a camera and say what is on my bright mind. Finally. Time to set the law straight and address everyone in the entire world of Tony Lattanzio's status. My two wins are against totally different men. I beat X, a formidable opponett. His record speaks for himself. Then I beat Tony Flames soundly, totally dominating that match.
Both of those men were dealt the Royal Flush. The Royalty of Royal Flushes. [pause] Tony: I say that because there is one man that seems to think he is worthy enough of carrying my very finisher name. BlackJack. Jack, me and you have many things in common. We both like to play cards, preferablly five card stud. We certainly both like to hustle others. We even have the same finisher name, Royal Flush. There is one thing, though, that I am not certain about which side you take . . . . . . . . . . .
_I WANT TO BEAT THE LIVING PISS OUT OF YOU_ Tony: You may be the "Five Card Stud," but I am the "Ace of Spades," and no one, and I mean _NO ONE_ gets away with trying to hustle the King of Kings, the _ACE OF SPADES_ You my man, are in for the fight of your life, and expect to be dealt the ruling monarch, the _ROYAL FLUSH_
