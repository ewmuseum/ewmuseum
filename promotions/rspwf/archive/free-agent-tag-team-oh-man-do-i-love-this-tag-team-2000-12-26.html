[Free Agent Tag Team] Oh man do I love this tag team!

26 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
So, I'm coming up on almost my third year of e-w now. And I hate myself for not coming up with a tag team like this two years ago... I haven't had this much fun since way back in the days of AOL shoot-em up feds :)
Here's the website... I haven't done much with them yet (one in-ring, and one more thing this week)
http://sammy-online.com/reddhooks/ So.. I'm going to see what other feds out there are looking for a tag team. Yes, Shawn Redd and Mark Hooks are looking for a second federation! Whooooooo! [copyright: [name withheld]] Here are their basic apps: ------------------------ - Tag Team Info - * Team Name: No name as of yet... but for now they are going by "Redd and Hooks" (I'll let you know when I think of one) * Team Members: Shawn Redd and Mark Hooks * Combined Weight: 505lbs. * Team Finisher: (most of the time it's one of the individual finishers, however on the rare occasion they'll do): Top Rope Bulldog/Lay Out... Hooks puts the opponent on his shoulders, and Redd climbs to the top ropes. Redd comes off after grabbing a handfuls of hair and leaps off going for a bulldog, while Hooks drops the guy forward for a lay out facebuster
- #1 - * Wrestler's Name: Shawn Redd * Hometown: Pittsburgh, PA * Height: 6'3 * Weight: 240lbs. * Age: 25 * Alignment: Heel * Wrestler Appearance: Shawn has short black hair, which is always styled as best as he can get it. He is in great physical shape, and doesn't mind to remind the fans about it. Shawn is never far from having a smile on his face, one of his trademarks.
* Ring Attire: To the ring Shawn wears a pair of long black pants, which are loose fitting, and a plain white sleevless t-shirt. Also, he wears a large elbow brace/pad on his right arm... to 'prevent injury' of course.
But, it mostly used for an extra pop on some of his moves. * Wrestler's Gimmick: Arrogant and cocky, just what your typical heel is these days. However, he and his partner are very calculated no matter how badly they seem to be out matched/ out gunned in the ring... there's always a gameplan and they stick to it perfectly.
* Wrestling Style: Techincal * 15-20 Moves (or more) your wrestler performs: backbreaker swinging neckbreaker hangman's neckbreaker snap suplex vertical suplex falcon arrow suplex belly to back suplex german suplex running clothesline armbar takedown figure four leg lock drop toe hold into a single leg boston crab sleeper hold into a reverse DDT fisherman's suplex jumping elbow smash (with the padded elbow) elbow smash from the middle rope (with the padded elbow) flying elbow smash from the top rope (with the padded elbow) * Setup Move To Finisher (If Any): one of the padded elbow moves or shots * Finisher Name: "Seeing Redd" * Finisher Description: Catches opponent as if he were to go with a fallaway slam, however he pushes his opponent legs first in the air and hits a sideways DDT
----- - #2 - * Wrestler's Name: Mark Hooks * Hometown: Las Vegas, NV * Height: 6'4 * Weight: 265lbs. * Age: 26 * Alignment: Heel * Wrestler Appearance: Mark simply looks like the pumped up version of Shawn Redd. He is extremely well built, and takes pride in always having a perfect tan on. He has shoulder length brown hair, which he normally wears back into a ponytail. Mark will actually stop in the middle of the match just to flex for the fans/opponents/managers/announcers. He's a heck of a guy.
* Ring Attire: To the ring Hooks wears a pair of black wrestling tights, which have a Superman "S" logo on them, in gold of course. He has a pair of black knee pads which have a golden outline on them, and wears a pair of black boots with gold laces.
* Wrestler's Gimmick: Mark is just like Shawn in the fact that he'll act arrogant and cocky on the outside of the ring, and during breaks in the match. However, Mark has more of a darker side than Shawn. He'll go that extra step that Shawn won't to make sure that they will win.
* Wrestling Style: Technical/Power * 15-20 Moves (or more) your wrestler performs. vertical suplex reverse suplex (falls forward instead of backwards) exploder suplex (belly to back with an extra pop) cobra clutch suplex release german suplex running powerslam superplex belly to belly suplex powerbomb sit-down powerbomb knee drop to the face knee drop to the ribs elbow to the groin (holds legs in a wishbone right before) body press drop (lifts opponent over his head, and then just drops them) running clothesline running kneelift * Setup Move To Finisher (If Any): any * Finisher Name: Hook Shot Driver * Finisher Description: it is a spinning thunder fire driver, which more times than not will put a guy down for good. Email me, IM me, love me, just don't tease me... wait strike those last two. -- - Duff - AIM: [detail withheld] "I rule!"
