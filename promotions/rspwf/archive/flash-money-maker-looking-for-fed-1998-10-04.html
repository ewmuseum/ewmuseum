[Flash] Money Maker Looking for fed...

4 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In...] [The scene opens to a jam packed EW F'n A house show. In the ring, we see two of the mid card stars of the EWA fighting it out, tearing each other limb from limb. The match ends, with a stunning victory for a young Mexican Superstar.
The two men clear out the ring. An EWA Announcer steps in the ring with a microphone, and begins to speak...]
[Ring Announcer] Ladies and gentlemen of the EWA... Tonight, right here in Youngstown Ohio, at the Beagly Center House show... we have a very special treat for each and every one of you. [Small Pop... in anticipation of the suprise...] Tonight Ladies and Gentlemen... We bring you the _brand_spankin'_new_ EWA European Champion, the one and only "Bad Boy" Jason Money!
[The crowd lets out a _huge_ pop, as "In My Bed" the So So Def remix by Dru
Hill featuring Jermain Dupris begins to play... The lights flicker on and off, as if there was all strobe lights in the arena. Fireworks erupt, as the Bad
Boy walks out from behind the curtain. "Bad Boy" Jason Money is wearing a pair of Jeans and a black t-shirt with the words "Bad Boy" written in red. He has on a long black overcoat, with a pair of black shades on. His black hair is parted slightly to the side, and his gotea is as tight as ever. Over his left shoulder is draped the EWA's European Championship Belt... Money slowly makes his way to the ring, making sure to sign every autograph... He hops in the ring, and takes the microphone from the ring announcer. He orders the man to get out of the ring, and bring him a chair. The announcer does so, and then retires to the back room... The crowd is hushed as Money lifts his hands in the air, and has a seat on the chia in the middle of the ring... Jason Money begins to speak...]
["Bad Boy" Jason Money] Yes Yes Yes! EW F'n A! [Pop] It's me again, and I'm back in full effect! The Extreme Wrestling Alliance's one and only European
Champion!I got to give all the fools that stepped in the ring with me some credit, they posed some real threats, Especially Marcus Nyghtwulf. Nyghtwulf, you are one hell of a wrestler. You and I had what I considered to be the match of the night, and I am looking forward to a rematch... but this belt is mine now. You wanted it, and I beat you. I beat you _bad_! Now you will have to wait in like with every other Tom, Dick, and Harry to get a shot at the most coveted belt in the EWA.
[Money adjusts himself to a better angle on the chair, and continues...] Why is this the most coveted belt, you ask? It's a fairly simple answer... It's because I OWN IT! I am the number one gun in the EWA, Hense this belt pulls the most weight.
_A_belt_does_not_make_a_Champion_ _A_Champion_Makes_a_belt_! Remember those words... I have the strap, and I am the man. Look at the world heavyweight belt.. Two men are fighting for it, but in reality, neither one has the skills to pay _my_ bills! I will face Beck and Torch down the road, and I look forward to it. Both are struggling late in their carreers, and can't seem to get back on track. This is the perfect time for a Super Star like me to make my run for the big gold. I look forward to the challenge...
[Money gets up and has a seat on top of the turn buckle...] My next matchup is against a man named Agua. [Money begins to laugh...] WHO? Oh well, the guy is obviously no good, and the President is looking for some way to hurt him... ya know? Make him _not want to come back for another match. Good call, Pres. I know how to take care of [CeNsOrEd]
like this. Agua, You will feel the wrath of my "Bad Boy Drop" just like Nyghtwulf did. I will walk away from another match... another night... another
Win under my belt, and I will _still_ be the EWA European Champion. Youngstown
Ohio... I'm out!
[Money drops the mic, and walks out of the ring, and exits through the crowd... Slapping hands with all his fans...] [Fade Out...]
