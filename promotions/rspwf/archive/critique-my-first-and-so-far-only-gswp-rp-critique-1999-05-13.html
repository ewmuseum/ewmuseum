[Critique] My First, and so far only GSWP RP [Critique]

13 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In] [The scene comes into a very large, nice house. The person who has just let the camera man in goes up stairs, talking can be heard, and then someone walks down the stairs. The isn't wearing a shirt. He is wearing a pair of Adidas
Tear-Away Pants, a pair of Adidas Sandals, a chain with a money sign on it, and the give away, a blue bandanna. The man is "Simply Perfect" TJ Masters. He walks into the living room where the camera man is, the camera man has just finished setting everything up, and sits down in a chair right across from where TJ Masters sits.]
[TJ Masters sits down in the chair, opens a bottle of water, takes a drink, and sits it down on the table in front of him, the camera man begins to talk.]
Camera Man: Thank you for allowing us into your home for this interview Mr. Masters, I hope we're not too much of a bother.........
TJ Masters: Nah, you ain't got on my nerves yet, just don't press your luck or you'll piss me off, and well, you just don't want that, get on with the interview.
Camera Man: No problem. Well, what do you think about the GSWP, there has been talk between you and President Coop for quite some time now, what was the dealy with bringing you here?
TJ Masters: Me and the prez been talkin' for a while that's true. First off, I wanted to come here, then got tied up with other stuff, and ain't have a chance to do it, so we stayed in touch. I got back in touch with him and the fed was full, but he kept in touch with me till a spot opened up, and here I am.
Camera Man: Interesting, well, Sunday Night you square off against another new-comer here in the GSWP, Vince Taylor, he has quite a reputation, are you worried about suffering from ring rust due to your excessive time off?
TJ Masters: Yo, I ain't "Simply Perfect" for nothin'!! Yeah, it's been a while since I've been in the ring, and Vince Taylor is a good wrestler and all that, but I ain't even sweatin' it. I still got my skillz, my stuff is gonna come back to me once I get back in the ring and the competition starts up.
Camera Man: So what are you over-all thoughts on Vince Taylor? You're in for a tough match...... TJ Masters: It's all good, I want a touch match, there's no better way to get back into a groove then to start off facin' someone who is supposed to be one of the best. As far as Taylor goes, I don't know too much about him 'cept what I've heard here in the past few days. I had heard of him before this match was signed, but nothin' to really pay attention to, we've never been in the same federation before, but the time comes now that we gonna step into the ring against each other and see whos got what. Vince, you call yourself the Ladies Man, and that may be true, but unless you want them to see you gettin' your ass beat, ya better leave em at home. I really got nothin'
against ya Vince, but I have to beat your ass to get goin' here in the GSWP, so that's what I'm gonna do.
[TJ leans forward and grabs his bottle of water and takes another drink, this time laying it down in this lap.]
Camera Man: So you're confident as always going into this match. How do you think you will fare over-all in GSWP competition? TJ Masters: Same way I do everywhere else. I'm going to make the people here bump they games up a notch or two. If you don't come to fight me with your A game, you're going to get embarassed. Vince Taylor is going to find that out Sunday night. Vince, you may not have heard of me, I'm not very experienced in the sport of wrestling, but I've gone through a lot of changes. When I first came into this sport, I was trying to be something that
I couldn't be, a technical/high-flying wrestler, and as a result, I lost my first match, but since then.............I've gone on tear, not losing since that very first night in the industry. I've found my style Vince, and you and everyone else in the GSWP better take a close look at some old film of me or something, and try to get some kinda clue of what you're in for, cause ain't nothin' gonna be the same.
Camera Man: That brought me to my next point.......How do you explain this more extreme turn you've taken?? You're still cocky, and style kinda raw and rugged, but very extreme.
TJ Masters: Its just what I feel the most comfortable doin', that's what I grew up in. Out where I'm from, it ain't all pretty when ya fight, its whatever you wanna do, anything goes. So I grew up in all reality basically training for the sport of professional wrestling. So, I came here, and I tried to do what the big wigs wanted me to do, and I tried to work the fans and be a high flyer, but nah, to hell with that. I'm me!! Y'all ain't gonna turn me into some corporate toy. You get what you see when you get me. I got a few moves I took in along the way that's more flashy than my style is, but they still hurt like hell! If you're scared to get a lil extreme, and you're scared to bleed, don't even come to the ring to fight me, cause you're up against the wrong damn guy!
Camera Man: OK, back to Vince Taylor. How do you think he will fare against your style of wrestling? It can't really be pinned to just one facet of the sport.
TJ Masters: Well, he ain't gonna be a ladies man after I'm done with him, that much I can tell you. Vince, I'm gonna get you into that ring, and I'm just gonna kick the hell out of you. All around the arena if need be Vince. Its simple, I'm going to use you to send a message to the rest of the GSWP, don't mess with me or you're gonna get hurt. You're not going to have the same opportunity that everyone else will. The other people, they're more or less going to be able to pick if they face me or not, you were just thrown into this match cause me and you was the newbies that needed a match, so you're gettin' the piss beat out of you just cause you're new here, cool eh?
Vince, plain and simple, I'm comin' to the ring Sunday Night for one reason, and one reason only, to walk away with a win after proving to you, and the world that I ain't no one to mess with!
Camera Man: Strong words, well, I guess just give us your final thoughts on "Ladies Man" Vince Taylor.....
TJ Masters: Well Vince, me and you right now buddy. Lookin' each other dead in the eye, what's gonna happen next? Whose gonna win the verbal battle? Does any of that really matter? Hell nah!! The only thing that matters, is what's going to happen on Sunday Night, when me and you come down to the ring, and lock up in our first match here in the GSWP. Only one of us is going to get our GSWP career started off with a bang, unfortunately for you, it ain't gonna be you Vince. But cheer up, atleast you'll still have them thingz you call women right? Don't sweat it Vince, we all gotta lose sometime,
I've already lost mine, so now, its your turn!! Vince, one way or another, your'e not going to win this match. I ain't really sure what you're going to come down to the ring and do Sunday Night...But one thing is for damn sure.........Sunday Night................
Vince you find out why I'm.................... _SIMPLY_PERFECT_!!!!!!!!! [TJ Masters smirks at the camera, and gets up, the camera man turns off the camera.] [Fade Out]
