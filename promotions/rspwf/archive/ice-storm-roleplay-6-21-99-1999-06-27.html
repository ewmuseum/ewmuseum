[ICE STORM] Roleplay [6.21.99]

27 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens up to shot Chris Winters standing in his kitchen on a cordless phone. A long tiled island of a table is set in the center, and large windows overlook a beautiful lake. The ceiling fan swings, but Chris perspires anyway as the burning orange Florida sun seeps in the form of light through his window. Chris bids his farewell, and then looks at the cameraman.]
CHRIS WINTERS: I'm not going to pretend I didn't know you were coming, like most guys. Relax and take a seat, I'll grab you a Heineken from the fridge.
[Winters wears a pair of mesh white shorts and a white wifebeater t-shirt. His spiked brown hair sparkles from the sun's rays, as does his hoop earring. He turns his head and makes his way to the refrigerator, grabbing a green beer bottle and sliding it on the table towards the camera. He pulls up a seat as well, and begins talking.]
CHRIS WINTERS: Dusty Love? Call me unfair. Call me lucky. Call me cheap. But what ever you do, fat man, don't go and call me talentless. Just because you had to deal with failure for once in your life, don't go barking up my tree, because I'm going to be the man to kick you right back down and make you hit every branch on your way to rock bottom. Your complaints are falling to deaf ears. The truth is, nobody wants to listen to what you have to say. So why don't you take a deep breath, suck it up, and pray to god that you'll have enough power to overcome _all_ the obstacles next time. The truth is, Dusty, I was as angry about the finish of that match as you were. I'm not concerned with an undefeated record. It's nice for bragging rights, but it's not a necessity. I would rather have been pinned fair and square in the center of the ring than to have not gotten the chance to finally shut your mouth once and for all. What happened was one of those things. Too late to go back and change it man, but you only live once so your forget about it.
... so shut up. I still haven't heard anything from this mysterious bandit that incited this whole situation, but I'm sure when he speaks, his comments will fall to deaf ears as well.
Nothing special. A mystery man in a black mask who's after Chris Winters. That's happened, what -- [thinks] -- seven or eight times before in my career? The fact of the matter is: I don't back down from anybody, so if you have a problem with the "Ice Storm", you can confront me to my face about it.
And now I've got a comment for not only the president of GSWP, but also all fifteen men in the battle royal next week. I don't understand the logic of a _new_ title being ranked higher than an existing title which has a well developed history and a strong, fighting champion. If this organization made any sense at all,
GSWP's titles would rank in order of importance: GSWP Television Championship, GSWP Hardcore Championship, GSWP World strap and _then_ the Garden State Championship. Ask the fans, and I think they'll agree. Nobody wants to watch a dried up Johnny
Glamour taking on some main eventer from the independent circuit. They'd rather watch "Ice Storm" Chris Winters technically embrace with a man of technical qualities. Putting on a great show for the fans to enjoy, kicking ass JUST enough, and then railing him with a Freezer Burn and catching a win, just under fifteen minutes.
[The green beer bottle, now empty, slides across the table and into Winters' hand. He walks to the sink, and rinses it out, still speaking.] CHRIS WINTERS: One of the cardinal sins of the wrestling suits is overlooking _just_ the right talent. And I feel that if
GSWP's Television Title is _not_ ranked higher than the GSWP Garden State title, than a title vs. title match should be in order. After all I've done, if my title is not worth a new one, then there should be a fight over it. I'll take on _any_ of the
15 men in that battle royal, and if I'm not crowned victorious, then I'll be humbled and the titles will stay the same, and I will have a loss on my record. If I _win_, then the Garden State champion hangs on to my good old TV belt [pats the strap] I'll be headed to higher hunting grounds.
After all, wouldn't that be the _fair_ thing to do? I don't mean to sound like a Dusty Love, or anything, but I deserve that much after all I've contributed to this organization in the past three months. I think I'm well within my boundaries on this one. I don't ask for too much, do I? I hold on to the Television Title,
I defend every week in front of a jam packed audience that loves me, I work for low pay because I don't need money, and I am yet to punch the president in the mouth.
[Puts his arms out to his sides in a questioning manner as he makes his way back to the table.]
CHRIS WINTERS: Really, how many guys are there like me out there? I think I'm pretty much one of a kind. Unlike Eddie Rue. Rue, all I've got to say to you is that you'd better tuck your tail between your legs and run away before you start a problem you can't solve. I don't know what problem you have with me, or why you would want to fight me, but I'm giving you a warning for now. If you don't heed that warning, you're going to get Freezer Burned through the god damn mat.
And another thing. I heard I'm going to be defending my title against TJ Masters for the second time in a couple of weeks. All
I have to say about that, is "TJ, more power to you." You actually worked your way up the ranks and _earned_ another shot, and
I'll actually wish you luck on conquering the unconquerable. I've always been a fan of the underdog, and lets face it, brother:
that's you. I know you're all upset that this interview's over, but I've got some VICIOUS business to attend to.
[The black cordless phone on the table rings, Chris picks it up as he shoes the cameraman away.]
CHRIS WINTERS: Adam? What's up, buddy? I told you it'd only take a couple of minutes ...
