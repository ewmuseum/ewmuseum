[Flash] Please Critique...

31 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first roleplay for FBK in a while since he took a sabbatical. So please give me some _CONSTRUCTIVE_ critism or good remarks about the flash...
[ SCENE: DSWA Headquarters in Little Rock, Arkansas. Many cars fill the parking lot showing that DSWA employees are working hard to build a wrestling empire.
The DSWA Headquarters are located right next to a big expressway called "Rocky
Road Express." The camera gets a good view of the gigantic headquarters, with the DSWA logo on top the building. Not many people are seen in the parking lot except a man that just walked out of the building. The camera approaches the man to recognize wrestling superstar, Full Blood Killah, FBK. FBK has on a pair of navy blue, Structure Carpenter Jeans, a red, black, and white Aeropostale
Sport sweatshirt, a shiny pair of brown, suade Tims, and a fitted red New York
Yankees baseball cap rested on his head. FBK notices the camera and signals for the man to take a walk with him. They walk up to a black, new 1999 BMW Z3
Roadster. FBK leans up against the car and begins to speak...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Well, Dirty South Wrestling Association just signed a huge wrestling icon, none other than da F-B-K! The money wasn't important, but I still made out like a bandit but the expectations are running high, which is no problem what so ever. I mean, they did everything in their power to get me, and
I can't blame them. Now I gotta show them what a good investment it was for them so they throw me into the tournament for the biggest title in the biz....the DSWA Universal Title. I have no problem with that because when I step up to the plate and swing, I _ALWAYS_ go yard. And going yard this time means taking the Universal Title home.
[ FBK slides from his trunk, to the ground. He then pulls his key chain out of his pocket and turns his car alarm off, and then opens the driver side door of his car. He sits down and turns back to look at the camera...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Presence of Persecution...February sixth...Round One of the
DSWA Universal Title tournament with FBK competiting for the most coveted price in the good ol' Dirty South...the stakes don't get any higher. Steel
Piston...you're the first piece of crap to go up against FBK...and believe you me, going one on one with FBK makes into a super-fucking-star! But don't start going around thinking you're the greatest thing since slice bread, or maybe even as great as FBK, because just because I'm gonna make you look like a superstar, doesn't mean you are one...it just means Da Killah is just that great a wrestler. I've shed my blood day in and day out...broke bones time and time again...and every damn time I've given a match a hundred and ten percent...and every time I give a hundred and ten percent, I come out the victor and on February sixth at the Alamo Dome in San Antonio, things aren't about to change. Nothing is permanent except change...and its time for a change, its the beginning of a new era...its the time when FBK begins his dominance on the Dirty South...and Pistol...unfortunately for you, you're gonna be the first to walk the aisle, face FBK, get your ass kicked and realize how great I really am!
[ FBK puts his key in the ignition of the car and starts the car engine. As he does, rap music begins to blast and FBK shakes his head to the beat. He then turns down the volume so he can continue to speak...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: After I live an imprint of my foot on Steel Piston's ass due to the biggest ass kicking the Dirty South has ever seen, I'm gonna advance in the tournament, and in the pursuit of FBK taking home the Universal Title. I've done some scouting, I've looked at who are big names in the tournament. Take
Alex Cote for example...the self proclaimed "Hammer" of wrestling. Well Alex, if me and you do meet up in the tournament and the Universal Title is on the line, you can bet your bottom dollar that you'll no longer be known as the
"Hammer" of wrestling because you'll be too hammered to ever wrestle again courtesy of FBK. And Double Down...I've seen you wrestle in WWO before, you held some gold over there, not a bad wrestler...but when you step in the ring with the King of Kicking Ass...you'll just be another face in the crowd, and you can take _THAT_ to the bank!
[ FBK then slams the door of his car and presses the gas pedal to rev up the engine of the BMW and it purrs like a kitten. FBK then rolls down his window...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: DSWA has a new icon as of today and I guarantee anyone I'm gonna take the Universal Title and dominate. Steel Piston, you're going down first. Cote, Double Down, I'll see you in later rounds and I promise it won't be very pleasant for you. So Piston, and any other participant that plans on facing me in the Universal Title tourney...FBK equals great and if you can't understand that concept fully, I'm gonna have to beat it into your thick skull and thats the way it'll _ALWAYS_ be!
[ FBK then rolls up the window and backs out of his parking space. He then shifts the car into drive and speeds out of the parking lot, onto the freeway...FADE ]
