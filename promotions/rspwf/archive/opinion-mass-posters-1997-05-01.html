[OPINION] Mass Posters

1 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You know, I have to tend to agree with these people that are posting against these new fed heads that are posting a ton a day. These people clutter the board with the same advertisement and claim how great their league is. Then I ALWAYS go to their homepage and it is usually a joke. I mean, if you are going to run a classy fed with Quality and stuff, you have to have, IMO, these certain criteria. 1. Quality - If you can't produce a web site and a sample card or even match, I personally, won't consider the league because it's a risk. When I first started, I was in a couple of leagues that folded within a month. I consider myself a little better than those leagues now and I look out for them. A web site is important for the fact that you can put stuff there (Rankings, news, updates etc.) and cut down on mail. 2. Players/Roleplaying - I think that you need to be in a fed where all of the handlers are active. I am personally against the use of simulators, but I can understand why fed heads use them. I think that roleplaying should be a must in ALL feds. I am sick and tired of seeing the roleplays that are one line or one paragraph that claim his guy will beat mine. Those roleplays show usually that a person is in either 5 or more leagues or that they have no clue about roleplaying. Roleplaying should be an essential part of a push. 3. Your editing skills - I know I personally hate getting cards where everything is spelled wrong and is all out of place. Have good grammar and spelling or don't write the cards. They are the two main things I look for in a fed and I have others, but everyone should have their own guidelines. I will say this though, people that post 17 messages in a row, I personally would not consider that fed. I come into the boards to read the different feds and see what's going on. I like to compare them and angles to the two leagues I am in. Hey, maybe there's an angle or a small idea that you can use yourself. The best is when you sift through the older messages and see that the people are either trying to run two feds or change the name of their feds to try and make it sound better. Take for example, the person that posts as "[name withheld]." Take a look back in the RSPWF Folder and see what he was up to. He was recruiting people for the enWo. Now he has the MWF. I checked them both out and they both still have a web site, so maybe he runs two feds. That is definitley what I am not looking for. And the best is, this is the FED and WE ARE BACK!! I never knew they we here before, so why should I care that they are back? They were probably one of the feds that lasted a month and quit. Those type of feds are starting to bug me as well. You see them on the board and never again. You go to their web site and there is nothing there. If you're going to do it, do it for more than a month. I do want to take this time to say Bravo to Fletch for going against one of these fed heads and stating what he thinks is right. Way to go fletch and you're fed, the NeCW looks like a real good fed. Well, this was just my 2 cents and I figured I would get if off my shoulders. P.J.
