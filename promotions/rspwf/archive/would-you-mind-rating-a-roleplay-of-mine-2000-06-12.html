You see Bruce and I don't have that much of a past. But for those of you who are interested here is a run down on the "past" that me and Bruce, or Bruce and I have together.
Mitchell and Preacher. For those of you who care MadMan McGuinness wrestles as Bruce Preacher these days.
Soon Scott Mitchell got upset with the management of this federation and resigned. Preacher went on to be a star there however.

Then one of his friends asked him to work a few dates for a federation that he had just started, Bruce agreed.
His friend had one other request however and asked if Bruce could bring in another NWC guy and work as a tag team with him.
Now Bruce hasn't got many friends as he is an assh*le, so he asked me to come in with him. I refused as we don't like him. He however said that I could make an awful lot of money and got his friend to give a big contract and lots of promises.
So about a week and a half ago Bruce and me worked a first show for the
Superior Wrestling Federation. We captured the their World Tag Team titles.
Three days later I became annoyed at the crapness of the federation and the federation in general and quit. Taking the tag title belt with me. Bruce's friend was upset, the end.
So you see Bruce and I don't really have a history together, unless you consider beating him in nine seconds and being a world tag team champion with him for three days in a federation that epitomises the description of bullshit indy fed as a history. I don't.
(Scott sighs) Now why am I here in "XXX Video"? Well I wanted to upset Bruce Preacher because it's fun. Before I go I want to say one more thing to Bruce.
