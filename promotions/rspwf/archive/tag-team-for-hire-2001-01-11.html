Tag Team For Hire

11 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tag Team Name: Simply Flawless Credentials: LVPWA Tag Team Champions, Finals of AGW Tag Team Titles. Past Federations: AGW, LVPWA Wrestlers: "Mr. Attitude" Kenny Kopecky & Hunter Douglas KENNY KOPECKY - Well Hunter, it looks as if we are on the Free Agency block. HUNTER DOUGLAS - It's quite funny if you think about it Kenny. One day we were
_THE_ team to beat, and now...we're seeking employment. So I guess wherever we go, we will still be the LVPWA Tag Team Champions until someone beats us for those titles.
KENNY KOPECKY - You know...maybe I've been reading too much baseball news. Manny Ramirez signing with the Red Sox, Alex Rodriquez signing with the Texas
Rangers, Johnny Damon being traded to the Athletics. I guess we consider ourselves an Alex Rodriquez. We're not too flashy, but we get the job done.
HUNTER DOUGLAS - Actually, we are very flashy, and we also get the job done. KENNY KOPECKY - The total package all in one. You've got the best technical wrestler in wrestling today, Hunter Douglas. And the most charismatic, flamboyant, superstar today. For those of you out there who don't think we are quite perfect...you're right...because we're _SIMPLY FLAWLESS!_
