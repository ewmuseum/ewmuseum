[Flash] "Rated G" Eric Bischoff [CRITIQUE!]

23 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Most of you know me from such characters as: Peter Damien, Jose' Tortilla, and [name withheld](That's my real name also)...I have recently created a outlandish wrestler,...using my brains..I plan to break every RP rule imagineable ONE WAY
OR ANOTHER! So far...my character is the _BIGGEST_ Kayfaybe in history. [People clutter about an office building conghested with people. A secretary srambles across her desk, picking up phone call after phone call. People try to sneak by the double doors that lead to the office of 'The Man' behind it all.
Each time, the door attendant, a stout, husky, terribly strong fella, grabs them, and throws them out. From the outside, we he're yelling, one man with a seriously hot temper is argueing with another man back and forth.]
V/o: I don't care if your you know what give's a yippity about my hahaha. So there! Now get your foul, rated 'X' language out of my room. I do not appreciate that kind of language on my show! You here me....WOMAN?! "Rated G"
Eric Bischoff does not promote swearing. If you can't play by my moral facalities, then don't play at all. Ya' here?
[Pause] Now then. What is your stance? V/o #2: God damn it you fucking gay prong. Don't feed me your redneck crap, I came here to this company to drink some beer, smoke some weed, and maybe get laid once a month. Is that so much to ask? No, it fuckin' isn't. You'd be a god damn lunatic not to let a little "hahaha" go around this federation!
V/o: Well, take a look at this name tag, it says "Rated G" Eric Bischoff pal. it means i am all about fairplay. You break my rules, there are prices to pay. V/o #2: Whoa, I ain't that kind of guy...if that's what your ' leaning into.' Eric Bischoff: [In disgust] That's it...Henry....get in here and take this guy out. I would do it myself but you know how deadly my "hee-yah" sounds are when
I do my karate chops. [In walks the bouncer, Henry, he takes one look at the other man...the other man has on a "Beer, Sex, And Violence" T-shirt, a small pot belly, and very light-blonde hair. Big Henry grabs him by the shirt, and throws him outside the office. Where the other man grabs a can off beer, and downs it, throwing the beer on Bischoff's desk.]
Eric Bischoff: Close the door on your way out Henry,...and make sure that _MORON_ stays out of my office. [Bischoff lays his head down on his desk, moaning....finally, after a minute, he picks it up, and burshes his hands on his face, getting a slight stressed out.]
Eric Bischoff: Jiminy Crickets! Do these people not know who I am? Who I had to _SLEEP_ with to get this position? Sheesh. These Yippity blankity blanks with there "uncensored" hahas. Well, they can kiss my you know what.
[Picks up a pencil, and breaks it in _HALF_!!!] Natural Born Killer, Barry Blades, bring your you know what to the ring, and I'll be sure to introduce you to the 1-2-3. Because when you actually run the whole show... you determine what people who actually have mic skills ever get a chance to talk, and you got to make sure that the people who can't spell words out of aplhabet soup touch the mic _AS_ _MUCH_ _AS_ _POSSIBLE_!!!
See, Barry, I know you have talent, but...alas, in this business...with this company, the less talent you got the more chances you have to suceed.
So I hereby pronounce you a _JOB_ to me. This Monday Night Madness represents the newest era in bad entertainment...I _HOPE_ you plan to help me proceed into the next millenium as _THE WORST_ organization in history!
[Throws a pencil at the camera] Eric Bischoff: That's it, I've had about enough...turn that fu...wait...GOT TO KEEP IT _RATED G_!!! [Fade out as we don't get to hear Bischoff finish off the other end of "Fu..."]
