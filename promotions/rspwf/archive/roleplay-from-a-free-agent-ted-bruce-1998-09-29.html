[ROLEPLAY FROM A FREE AGENT] Ted Bruce

29 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*Hi. This is a recent roleplay I wrote in Global Championship Wrestling. It may be harder to understand since it is a bit out of context... but here it is...* -=- Eye-Opener -=- "Perhaps you should rethink your options... try to get a good grip on things... take a vacation maybe -- above all, Ted... I'm concerned about you."
[Ted looks up from his plate. He raises an eyebrow...] "When was the last time you actually took a shower, Ted? I mean... Jesus... you look like shit-ran-over, man." [Ted shrugs and goes back to cutting up peices of his meat. Once seperating a chunk of beef from the slab, he devours it. Then looks back up...]
"Did someone steal your razor? Jesus... you look like Grizzly Adams! Ha! No man... seriously though..."
[Ted scratches the thick stuble on his chin... as if a finally a bit concerned about how thick it's gotten. Then picks up a small plastic salt shaker, spreading it around his plate.]
"... you... my friend... are in _no_ shape to wrestle." Ted Bruce: "And what makes you say that, Dave?" [Finally... as if it couldn't hold any longer... the camera turns to the man who's been consulting Ted ever since they arrived in the small diner. His hair is cut short... probably buzzed and then jelled down... but very stylish.
He's tan... very tan... but not "tan-in-a-bottle" tan... more like naturally tan... as if he has some Hispanic or black in him. Dark, brown eyes remain sunken in his face -- he has a very sharp appearance.
This man is very familiar to Ted Bruce. This man is Dave Flamingo. Flamingo was fired from Global Championship Wrestling for reasons of misconduct behind the scenes along with lack of participating (Flamingo felt that he should wrestle on his terms, and his terms only). Now, a participant in a much smaller federation that has yet to start, Flamingo has had much more time to do things that he wishes. One of which was to come to Albany to help out a friend from the past.]
Dave Flamingo: (sitting back in his seat... groaning a bit) "Jesus man... I keep up with the "old fed." I even saw the as-yet-unreleased tape of you going ballistic after your match...."
Ted Bruce: (squinting his eyes in a bit of dissapointment at his friend... then sort of barking at him) "...ballistic? I was attacked, you jackass! I had all the right in the world to go ballistic."
Dave Flamingo: "Ted... you just don't lose your temper like you did -- why do you think I get ejected?"
Ted Bruce: (sitting back in his seat some) "...I don't know, Dave... because you aren't half as good as I am?"
[The coldness in which Ted said that is utterly chilling... Flamingo bites his lip... but holds back a temper of his own.]
Dave Flamingo: "...I'm _not_ going to get mad at you... Ted. Because ever since '89, you've known I've been here for you. And you also know damn well that I won't just sit aside and let you be a fucking jerk..."
Ted Bruce: (suprised at the comment) "...me being a fucking jerk? _Me?_ I'm not telling you how to live _your_ life. I'm not telling you that you should get the hell out of that peice of shit federation you are in right now..."
Dave Flamingo: (interruping) "...I hardly think that it's a peice of shit fed..." Ted Bruce: (not even listening) "...I'm not telling you anything. You're the jackass who wanted me to come home -- a place, if you remember correctly, I
_don't_ like coming back to. None of that was me. Why should I sit here and listen to _you_, of all people, _lecture_ me? Why? Tell me? Why? COME ON,
DAMMIT!" [A swift fist to the table does the trick -- a fist slammed down by Flamingo. The salt-shaker spills over... the silverware rattles... and Ted's eyes light up -- his attention clearly gotten. Flamingo leans over... gets right in the face of Ted... and locks eyes dead on with him.] Dave Flamingo: (now trading some rather... bleak words) "...listen here... Ted. You want to know _why?_ It's all about 'why?' with you? Here is the answer to your question: because, believe it or not, there _are_ those you can trust. And when you get that stick out of your ass... quit whining and crying about everyone attacking you... live up to what you say you are... and just quit being a stupid little bitch... maybe then... and only then... you'll know..."
[He pays... ...he puts on his jacket... ...and then Dave Flamingo is off to _his_ life again. _His_ goals... _his_ motives. Ted sits there... still tearing at his stake with a knife and fork.
He let's out a sigh... runs his hand through his hair... and says the one thing that often makes him feel better...]
Ted Bruce: "Dammit." [But why doesn't he feel better?] T E D B R U C E *Pretty much, Ted is a brawler/match worker. He's got a bit of an attitude, and a sort of hidden past. You can definitely get to know him fairly easily. I'm looking for a closed-style roleplaying system with only the best of talent and the best of roleplays. Thanks for your time. :)*
