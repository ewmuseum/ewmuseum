[RP Please Critique] Zach Morrison in Time Square?

22 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera cuts to Time Square where there are carolers, little kids ice skating, and people looking at the huge Christmas Tree. There are two cops standing next to a toy drive. People are dropping in gifts that are $10 or more. Somebody drops in a bag of Pokemon' cards into the box, and all of a sudden Stinky Pete runs up....]
Stinky: Help...help...somebody stole my baby! Help me! [Stinky Pete leads the cops away from the box of toys, and who should appear but Zach Morrison. He sneaks up and he's going through all the toys. He finds a
PC Talking Trent Cole doll, and he throws it on the ground. All of a sudden this cute little 11 year old comes up and picks up the toy.]
Little Boy: Hey Mr.....you dropped this toy. Zach: Yeah so? Little Boy: But it's Trent Cole....he's my hero. Zach: Ooooo how sweet. Get out of the way before I puke on you. Little Boy: Are you supposed to be picking up the toys like that? Zach: Uhhh...yeah of course. Don't you know who I am? Little Boy: Aren't you that guy from "Saved By The Bell"? Zach: WHAT!? No what the hell is wrong with you, you stupid boy? Get out of here before I tell Santa to give you nothing but cole in your stocking, and I'm not talking about Michael Cole.
Little Boy: You know Santa? Zach: Of course I know Santa. He and I hang out at the nudie bars together all the time. He always spots me the $40 dances, because that's when the girls take off all....th...nevermind.
Little Boy: What's a nudie bar? Zach: It's Disneyland for bigger boys. Little Boy: What do they do there? Zach: They over-charge you for watered down drinks, and put on a show that makes you smile from ear to ear. Now go away kid, you bother me.
Little Boy: Can you tell Santa something? Zach: If I do will you go away? Little Boy: Yes. Zach: What is it then? Little Boy: I want Santa to give me two gifts. One is something I've always wanted since I was tiny. I mea...
Zach: Just get on with it you lit...cute boy. Little Boy: I want a daddy. Zach: Oh shit. Little Boy: WHat did you say? Zach: I said....oh this is it. This toy is for you. [Zach pulls out a football and gives it to the little boy. The boys heart lights up, and so does his smile. For a minute Zach forgets about himself, and thinks about how happy he's made the boy. He kneels downs to the kid with a huge smile on his face and embraces him with a hug. All of a sudden "Oh Come
All Ye Faithfull" starts up and Zach has a tear rolling down his cheek.]
Zach: What's your second wish little boy? Little Boy: Well it's that I wan... Zach: Shhh no need to say it. It's going to be a lot fo hard work, and I know deep down inside I have it in me to be your daddy. I will be the best father you could ever have.
Little Boy: WHAT? Zach: Ummm...isn't that what your second wish was? Little Boy: No...it was for Trent Cole to win his match against that loser Joe
Morrusy! Zach: Why you little piece of.... [Zach goes to grab the boy, and he trips over the box of toys. The boy sticks out his tongue and spits at him, and runs away. Zach tries to make it to his feet and he trips over the toys. He grabs the little wagon and throws it down breaking it into a million pieces. All of a sudden the two cops come back, and stop him.]
Cop #1: What the hell do you think you're doing? Zach: Get that little boy...do you know what the hell he did? Cop #1: What? Zach: He told me that he wanted Trent Cole to win his match this week. Cop #1: I want him to win too. Zach: You pig! Cop #2: Alright that be enough of ya's. We're gonna have to take you in. Stinky Pete: My baby...my baby! Cop #2: Oh jumpin' jahoseafat......we have to help this man find his baby again. Don't you go anywhere.
Zach: Uhhh okay. [The cops totally take off and Zach makes a break for it. He's talking to the camera man as he runs.]
Zach: Trent Cole...see if I don't beat the living hell out of him this week. The guy is nothing more than a flash in the pan, but you know what's going to be in that pan soon enough? Me, and I'm the big bad eggs that take up the whole damn frying pan. I've beaten two men already within an inch of their lives, and
I don't stop tonight nor do I stop when I get into the ring. Trent Cole can go on and on about how he's the Nature Boy of wrestling, and how he likes to kiss babies on the forehead....well Trent I ain't no baby, and you ain't kissin' me with those chongo lips of yours. I'm running all the way back to Mashpotato
Wrestling to beat the living day lights out of you, and when I do beat you...when I do show you who the boss is...NO not Tony Danza....it's me "Sweet"
Zach Morrison....after I am done beating you I'm going to look into that camera...turn to that little boy and say....HA HA....I could've been your father. Now you're a little orphan like Annie.
Sure the Sun will come out tomorrow...but you'll see it as you're scrubbin the damn floors. WHOOO I JUST LOVE CHRISTMAS!!!!
[Morrison takes off onto a sprint into the darkness. The camera fades.]
