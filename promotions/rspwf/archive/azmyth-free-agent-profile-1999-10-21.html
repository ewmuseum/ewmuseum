Azmyth.....free agent....profile

21 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi I am the handler of a fantasy wrestler named Azmyth. As of now he is a semi free agent.....which means he is still actively participating in a fed but will leave for one more suitable..anyway here is a profile and sample roleplay....
Azmyth Azmyth is a virtually unknown that not many people have heard of. Those who have seen Azmyth compete or wrestle were startled by his dramatic and unexpected entrances. He has this aura about him that makes the fans wonder who's side he's on....his words are often times riddles that you must dissect to understand his intentions...other times vivid descriptions of events make his actions all that more startling. Not much is known about him except for the fact that he has a liking for blue flames.....its supposedly what he stands for.....his symbol for justice....or whatever cause it is he supports. He will do whatever is neccesary to win a match as long as it is fair....he has never cheated to win.....
Title History 3 time ICWA World Champion 2 time ICWA Us Champion TCW Hardcore Champion EFWO Continental Champion EFWO Bad ass Champion IWA Atlantic Champion ACW Inner City Champion ACW Tag team champion w/ Hell Knight current fed: WWA (World Wrestling Association) Sample roleplay (this is a roleplay for a the WWA in a feud with a wrestler named Abyss....I use a descriptive roleplay instead of the interview type since Azmyth is a loner and doesn't really talk to anyone....
(Scene begins......with a dark room....a small room..the aura is rank and everything is covered in complete darkness....We see that someone has set up a strange looking diagram in the floor with candles making a large circle....all that is clearly visible is the white wax, all else is dark.
Suddenly, the candles set fire one by one....in a strange pattern one candle lights at one side of the circle while another starts on the other side crisscrossing making a strange and mysterious symbol appear..yet the strangest thing of all is the fact that the flames are blue in color!!
Slowly the room begins to illuminate and slowly we can make out a shadowy figure standing inside the circle of fire...as the flames get brighter the identity of the figure becomes more apparent....wait...is it who I think it is? Yes.......the fans in the arena recognize the man....and they start to cheer.....Azmyth, Azmyth, Azmyth.
Now he is fully visible and we can see the expressionless look on his face....his eyes dark and piercing....he turns to the back wall slowly.....now we see a large body mirror.....we can barely see
Azmyth's reflection in the large mirror. Azmyth starts to speak....the fans listen in anticipation in what he has to say..... "The children have gone to the other side of the gates...waiting for there souls to be freed from its imprisonment. The flight takes hold of the so-called war....I look into the pond of lost hearts and see a face...A face of a well known expression.....a face of someone who I hear standing......knocking...at my gates...wishing to travel down my pathway....He has the heart of darkness and the mind of evil itself....I look slowly his aura fills the view...his thoughts and wishes flood the souls of the righteous...he is though only a thought, a reflection in the pond of lost souls.....the time has come to shatter the darkness and burn down the walls.....the man called Abyss!!!"
Suddenly....Azmyth turns around slowly and raises his hands slowly and lowers his head.....suddenly all walls burst violently in blue flames and the mirror now clearly shows....not Azmyth but the man he spoke of
Abyss!!! Without warning the mirror suddenly shatters....on the pieces left on the floor you can still make out abyss's reflection in the broken shards!!!!
The when we go back to the main view..we see that the room is burning and Azmyth is gone!!!! but strangely the name Abyss is emblazened in flames across the wall where the mirror stood.....the scene fades to black....
Any that are interested in my wrestler please contact me!!
