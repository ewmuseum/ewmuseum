[critique] "The Fallen Angel" Jacob Azrael

11 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is an old RP I found while looking for an old application. I read it over, and I liked it. Wanted to get some feedback on it. It is a bit lengthy, but if you read the scene alone, I am sure you will continue on reading. I definately like the scene I wrote for this one. Give me honest opinions, and point out certain things you like if you wish. At any rate...enjoy :)
-Kade *likes the scene...A LOT. [[...A harsh shrill cry pierces the air like a gunshot and the air carries the cry through the empty void that is night. Visual perception becomes keen as we look at what surrounds us. There are many trees all set in sequence. Each tree with it's leaves turning brown and it's bark drying up. The beginnings of a beautiful time of the year...autumn. Behind the wet, green grass lies a small concrete trail leading to an area which is covered in sand. The wind picks up, blowing a bit more harsh than before. A sudden screech is heard, catching all attention to a set of swings set inside of the sandy area. There are three different swings all characterized by the paint falling off of them, and the beginnings of rust. Each tells a different story of it's lifetime in which it has seen many childhood's pass. A single street light dangles from a loose hanging chord. As each swing sways in the wind, it's rusty bolts and chains sing a dissonant sound. Suddenly, a familiar figure steps from the darkness and sets down on the center swing. Closer to the figure we go realizing who it is; none other than the fallen angel, Jacob Azrael. Azrael peers into the darkness focused as if he is looking at a certain object. His lips part and he speaks with a voice that is barely audible. Almost as faint as a whisper, but still heard.]]
"The Fallen Angel" Azrael: What is defeat? Nothing but education, nothing but the first step to something better. [Wendell Phillips] If this is so, then why do men concern themselves with such a trivial thing as defeat? Obviously, there is some sort of knowledge gained from defeat in experience, so in essence...aren't both parties, in this case, the winner and the loser, both the benefactor of something deeper than a difference in record? If not, then to what point do men fight for? No man fights to lose, but he who does lose often gains more knowledge than the victor. Because it is he who has lost who must see what his wrongs were and to what extent that he needs to better himself.
Whereas the victor looks upon the win as a reassuring victory. Technically speaking, there are no losers to any battle. From the blind eye, hungry for attention there is though. And it are those ignorant eyes which will be blinded the worst when they are stricken to see something that they did not think they would ever see...defeat.
[[...Azrael extends his legs straight pushing himself backwards a small bit. He moves his legs and he begins to swing slowly, back and forth in small amounts of space. His eyes filled with wonderment. Suddenly, his mood changes as he takes notice to a dog walking through the night. The dog strides proudly with it's head held high, stepping almost majestically. Azrael then looks on behind the dog, and he sees a mutilated cat. Obviously a victim of the cocky, pompous dog. A small smirk grows on his face and he speaks. ]]
"The Fallen Angel" Azrael: Like the hunter who eliminates all opposition,
I wait. And although I wait, I remain on the offensive. Although it seems like all have seen my true intent, they have only begun to see just how easily minds are clouded.
[[...Azrael lets out a deep breath and then pauses for a moment. He stands to his feet and he steps away from the swings and out of the sand and he sets himself down with his legs folded and he begins speaking. Not looking at anything. Eyes closed. ]]
"The Fallen Angel" Azrael: Although it may seem that the games have ended, they have only just begun. Stalker, for your eyes have remained open, but they have also allowed something to enter. Chameleon I think you called me.
Mimicking all that I see, adapting to my surroundings. Who is to say that isn't exactly what I wanted you to see? Although that sounds more like an excuse than a truth, allow me to dwell further into it my friend. You see, coming into this battle one thought stuck out in the minds of not only others, but you as well. Jacob Azrael is on the search to distance himself from The
Stalker. When in all reality, the day I stepped into the WRX I was easily seen as distanced. You though Stalker, it is you who wanted this. Am I wrong? Did
I play exactly how you predicted? You say yes.
[a voice is heard although no one speaks] -["The Psycho" Stalker]- I tried to warn you Jacob. I tried to stop you before it was too late. [stops abruptly] .......... [Begins to look around and smile.] Poor little Jacob, my child....... IT IS TOO LATE!!!
"The Fallen Angel" Azrael: Indeed I am angered that you, of all people think that my mind would fall so easily. You see, with the man James...All that needed to be done was to mirror his ways, and to do something that was thought to be impossible...beat him at his own game. And as you said...that is something that you were not able to do. Yet you still think that my mind has already fallen don't you? And although it would appear to be a much harder feat...Anthony Kutler fell easier than any man I have ever done battle with.
So I thought to myself...in what way will this Stalker, a man whose mind is as great if not greater than mine, allow himself to fall into something that he would not with any other man? [smirks] It took a moment of thought, but I came to the conclusion that in order to seep into your mind, and to do something that not many have done...over rule the Stalker...I merely gave myself to you. I gave you what you wanted. It was your mistake to warn me of what not to do Stalker, because it was those things which I was not supposed to do which allowed me to determine what will be needed to defeat you. I knew the one thing that would ease you would be my explanation of Perdition. When you of all people should know.."Understanding Perdition" is a paradox in its own.
-["The Psycho" Stalker]- you now attempt to explain Perdition. But it takes more than a dictionary and some well educated guesses to truly get inside perdition. "The Fallen Angel" Azrael: I am not wrapped in your words Stalker. Nor am
I wrapped inside of anything that you have opened to me. It is you who is wrapped inside of my actions, judging them and looking upon them as if they
were my only chance of defeating you. As you said yourself...I'm only playing what you have allowed me to play. Remember what I said to you? I said I will play no games with you, because you are less than willing to do so. I guess it is just my luck that games...you were willing to play. And like you, many men's minds are my play thing, and I am not one to be fragile with my toys.
You may think you have me figured out, but the truth is, you only saw what I wanted you to see. And that is yourself. In essence, I did not fully complete showing you what you wanted. I did though succeed in showing you enough. I knew that coming into this battle you had seen my battles with Anthony Kutler and James Saint Vyolence. I knew that you would be expecting me to come to you as I did them. Unfortunately for you though, you were wrong. Perdition to me, means nothing and will forever mean nothing. My mind dwells on much more important things than personal hell. I know who I am and what my purpose is and I accept who I am. Unlike some people in this world who give themselves to their pain and accept that they can only live through it. You have not learned from anything Stalker. Although you think you hold the knowledge needed to defeat me, you only hold a deeper understanding of yourself. Why portray this act? To play with you Stalker. And much like the young Anthony Kutler, when the ball was placed in your court, you dropped the ball. You can try to show my words and actions to be false, and come up with a likely excuse. Because that is the only thing you will be able to do...backpedal away from your words and make up excuses. Have I ruled you out completely yet? No, because there is still the factor of the battle that must insue in the ring. There we shall see who the true master of the mind is. I have not begun to dwell into the chasms of my mind yet Stalker. I have only made it appear so. So that you would rest a bit easier and lay your guard down for a mere second.
Right now, I want to ask you a question...How does it feel to be the defeated
Stalker? How does it feel to know that you lost in your own game...not only to someone you claimed weaker in the mind, but someone who was only showing you what you wanted...and that was a cheap imitation of yourself? If you have indeed been defeated Stalker, what will you do to rise from that defeat? Will you allow it to break you, like Anthony Kutler? Will you allow it to shatter you? Like the Saint? Or will you take something from this much more? Defeat should never be a source of discouragement, but rather a fresh stimulus. Maybe this is just what you needed upon your return. A young "chameleon" to hand you a defeat. A young "chameleon" to jump start your mind. What will they say
Stalker? What will they say when they look upon you lying on the mat in a pool of your own blood?
Do you see now Stalker? Do you see how easy it is? There may be a strong difference between us Stalker, but inevitably all things alike are meant to become one. Do you still need more understanding? Why fight something that is so close to yourself when together, your force could be much more powerful?
Open your eyes Stalker, sleep no more. Stop trying to fight something that you cannot oppose. Yourself. Stand strong. As far as you know, all of what I have said can be a lie. As far as you know, this is another act. As far as you know, you may not be facing Jacob Azrael...you may be facing yourself.
[[...Azrael shakes his head and lets out a massive laughter. Birds are heard flying away. A sudden breeze blows by and the visual fades to black.. ]]
