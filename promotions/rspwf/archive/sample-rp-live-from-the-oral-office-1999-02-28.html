[SAMPLE RP] LIVE From The Oral Office

28 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in the posh Philadelphia office of Fred Scuttle. However, despite what you may think, it isn't Fred Scuttle who's sitting behind the mahogany desk in the center of the room. It's Gambit, AKA "The Giant Lovah".
He's sitting behind the desk, sorting through a small pile of documents. There's a huge smile on his face as he reads...] Gambit: Paperwork, paperwork, paperwork. Aw yeah. The Giant Lovah is getting worked to the bone.
[Gambit smiles wickedly as he looks underneath the desk...] Hear that, Monica? I'm getting worked to the _bone_. [A muffled "Mmm-hmmm" is heard coming from underneath the desk. Meanwhile,
Gambit sighs happily and holds up one of the documents...]
A signed and finalized contract to face "The Servant" William O'Leary at next weekend's episode of "Sunday Night Heat".
[He puts down that document and picks up another ... flashing it to the camera...] A yet to be signed contract to face "Screaming Drillbit" Jason Keening at "Ides of March '99".
[He puts that document back in the pile. The smile on his face gets even bigger as he picks up another piece of paper. But, this time it isn't a document. Even though the camera can't pick up the words written, it appears to be a handwritten letter.]
A letter from a very special lady who's rather smitten with me. Aw yeah. Who's The Giant Lovah kiddin'? She's beyond smitten and into smut, judging from these letters. Aw well. You'll be seeing her very soon enough in UEW if
Smooth G has his way...
[Gambit laughs...] But the first piece of business that I'd like to get out of the way is "The
Servant" William O'Leary, my unfortunate opponent next Sunday.
[Gambit laughs even harder ... slapping the desk hysterically...] That little sniveling runt has been calling the Oral Office nonstop ever since our match was announced. He's been asking me not to show up. He wants The
Giant Lovah to throw the match and lay down for him. He's scared. He's upset.
He's downright cowardly when it comes to the prospect of going one on one with the... aw yeah ... professional wrestler with the most sexual magnetism in the business today.
[He folds his hands on the desk ... holds back the laughter ... and looks into the camera, seriously, but with a giddy smile on his face.]
O'Leary ... you amuse me terribly with your nonstop calling. Don't get me wrong. But you've also angered Smooth G with the never-ending phone calls to the Oral Office.
[Gambit picks up the receiver of the telephone sitting ontop of the desk. He holds it up to the camera...]
This phone is the main phone of the Oral Office. It serves two purposes. One, it's for me and Freddie to conduct business. Two, it's for me... aw yeah ...
to talk to all of the lovely ladies who find Smooth G... aw yeah, man ... simply irresistible. [Gambit slams his hand on the desk furiously...] IT'S NOT... I REPEAT __NOT__... FOR WHINEY LITTLE COWARDS TO CALL AND HARASS SMOOTH G 24-7!! [He leans forward on the desk. His eyes are blazing with rage...] Don't you realize how many lovely hoochie fans out there were kept from getting through to me because of your annoying phone calls??? Don't you realize that
Erin McCoys... Veronica... Tempest ... all of those professional wrestling babes ... are all probably crying now because they couldn't get through to the
Oral Office to talk to Smooth G... ALL BECAUSE OF _YOU_!!
[He points to the camera and sneers...] William O'Leary ... on Sunday night, March 7th... I will be releasing all of my frustration on you. Aw yeah, brotha. You'll be feeling the violent wrath of a man who's libido was stunted all this week because of your stupidity and your cowardice.
[The anger on Gambit's face soon fades as he picks up the contract for the
Gambit/Jason Keening "Ides of March" match off his desk. He chuckles...]
As for you, "Screaming Mimi"... aw yeah ... as I sit here contemplating adding my signature to what I've been told is the finalized contract for our match at
"Ides of March", Smooth G wonders. Aw yeah. Is there anything missing from this match? I've already heard that you may insist on Scientific Rules. UEW officials will soon decide the location of where our match takes place. I just gotta ponder. Aw yeah. Is there a piece of the puzzle missing?
[He scans the contract briefly and then looks up at the camera again...] I think so. Aw yeah, Keening. I think there's a very important piece missing to the jigsaw that is our "Ides" epic.
[Gambit licks his lips sensually...] Let me tell you a fact of life, Keening. The women love The Giant Lovah. Everybody knows that. What if one of my millions and millions female followers sees you put a headlock on Smooth G and worries that you may mess his perfect head of hair? She may very well climb into the ring and take you down herself.
Under Scientific Rules, that'd cost me that match and... aw yeah ... that's something Smooth G does _not_ want to see.
[He scratches at his goatee ... smiling like a Cheshire Cat. It's obvious that he has something on his mind.]
So, what I propose... aw yeah ... is that we add one more stipulation to this match before I sign the dotted line. Why not make our match a match that's contested under ... mmm yeah... Mano a Mano rules?
[He smirks at the camera sarcastically...] Now ... mmm aw yeah... I know that since you _are_ my opponent, we're a man short for anything to be Mano a Mano but I think UEW officials will overlook your obvious inner femininity and let the match be contested under Mano a Mano rules if you agree. The ball is in your court, Mimi.
[Gambit stands up and walks over to the door of the office. He opens the door and looks towards the desk.]
As for now... I'm gonna finish my little business meeting here... [He puts his fingers in his mouth and whistles loudly. Almost immediately, a sorta pudgy yet sexy brunette woman in a beret with unknown yet visible stains all over her dress crawls out from underneath the desk. She briskly sprints out the door ... patting Gambit on the behind and giving him a cigar before she leaves. Gambit looks at the cigar...]
Aw yeah... [He puts the cigar in his back pocket and smiles...] A souvenir for Smooth G. How presidential of her. [He walks back over to the desk and picks up the telephone.] And now I'm gonna call the number one lady in my life. But, before I do... aw yeah... [He begins to dial...] I'm gonna leave William O'Leary... Jason Keening ... and whoever else plans on getting in the way of The Giant Lovah... you can pursue me with all of the confidence in the world, each and every one of you ... but the end result will always be the same. You're all gonna go down at the hands of the smooth talkin' giant with all the style ... and, let me tell you...
I T ' S A L O N G W A Y D O W N ! ! ! [Gambit smiles as he begins to talk into the telephone...] Hey baby... aw yeah ... it's me, boo. So, when are you gonna show your face to all of my fans? Aw yeah ... then? Perfect. I'm sure you'll make a _SMASHING_ entrance...
[Gambit looks at the camera and smiles devilishly as we fade to black.]
