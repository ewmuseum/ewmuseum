[Critique of Roleplay] Edgar Noyd

22 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The view slowly fades in to a simple setting. Edgar Noyd is sitting on a plain, blue steel chair with his upper body hanging forward. Both elbows rest on his knees as Noyd is focused on the floor. A dim light hangs over his head as a large LCW logo remains in the background. Noyd slowly looks up, both eyes glassy and surrounded by redness.]
EDGAR NOYD- Literally, ... minutes now seperate Edgar Noyd from Shawn Ryder and Gordon Venta. In a very, very short while - Plymouth Rock'D will hit the
Pay-Per-View satellites. Millions of Lost Conscious Wrestling fans will see an event top to bottom full of Wrestlers wanting to stretch the limits of what is excepted. The only problem is - <shakes head> only three men are truly willing to stretch the limits.
[Noyd puts his head into his hands, noticeably upset.] I have tried. Bane has tried. Willie _[BEEP]ing_ Blanco has tried. We have all tried. But it's obviously rather futile. We wanted to set a new standard.
We wanted to take the LCW... a rinky dink cookie cutter upstart Promotion and make it something great. Bring it to a level to compete with the X8oD's, HIW's and ALW's of the past year. Make it into something all want to see. But a month later, ... I can tell it's been a waste of time. A waste of ABUSE's time.
[Noyd's eyes light up with fire and anger.] A WASTE OF MY TIME! [His angry visage slowly turns into a glaring look of regret, apathy and complete and utter dissatisfaction.]
I hate wasting time. I finally get the opportunity to make something of my self. To make people look at me different. To get an OUNCE of respect from this filthy, [BEEP]ed up Society and what happens!? YOU RUIN IT! YOU GO AND
RUIN MY ONLY CHANCE AT SHOVING IT UP THEIR ASS FOR PISSING ON ME! YOU TAKE THE
ONE THING I WANTED MORE THAN ANYTHING ELSE IN THE WORLD AND YOU TURN IT INTO
[BEEP]! YOU TOOK IT FROM ME!
[Noyd stands up, folds the chair and lifts it up with his left hand.] SINCE THE PUBLIC KNEW OF THE PLYMOUTH ROCK'D PAY-PER-VIEW... I KNEW I HAD A
CHANCE. A TIME TO RECTIFTY ALL OF THE RIDICULE I'VE ENDURED. I KNEW I NOW HAD
THE ABILITY TO CORRECT ALL THAT HAS BEEN WRONG IN MY LIFE. BUT NOW IT'S
LOST... FOREVER! I'LL NEVER HAVE ANOTHER CHANCE AND IT'S ALL YOUR FAULT! YOU
RUINED IT FOR ME, YOU SELFISH BASTARDS. YOU TOOK THE ONE THING I WANTED MORE
THAN ANYTHING ELSE IN THE WORLD AND YOU RUINED IT. [Noyd winds up and cracks himself in the head with the chair.] YOU RAPED ME OF MY GOD DAMN RIGHT TO PROVE EVERYONE WRONG. [Again, Noyd rears back and cracks himself directly in the face. A cut between his eyes begins to bleed as a small trail of blood runs out his left nostril.
His shoulders begin to pump up and down feverishly.] That is fine... THAT IS FINE, I SAID GOD DAMMIT! Because tonight... in minutes... just minutes, YOU EGOCENTRIC LITTLE BASTARDS WILL PAY. YOU WILL
SUFFER LIKE I HAVE SUFFERED. I WILL MAKE YOU FEEL THE YEARS OF PAIN I'VE HAD
TO LIVE THROUGH. I WILL MAKE YOU FEEL LIKE YOU'RE IN A BATHROOM STALL AT THE
LOCAL GYM AND FOUR PRICKS MUCH LIKE YOURSELVES ARE RIPPING OFF YOUR CLOTHES AND
KICKING THE PISS OUT OF YOU. [Noyd grabs both sides of the chair and begins slamming it into his head, ... shot after shot after shot after shot. He reels back and drops to one knee.] Tonight is your end, god dammit. I will leave the Georgia Dome with one thing... maybe a win, maybe a loss. Maybe as the Empire Champion or maybe as a downed challenger... but no matter what happens -- I will leave as one thing.
[Noyd cracks himself once for good measure and collapses to the floor.] A FIGHTER! [Fade to black.]
