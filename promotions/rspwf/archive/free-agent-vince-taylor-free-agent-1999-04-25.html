[FREE AGENT] Vince Taylor [FREE AGENT]

25 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well Vince has been out of action for a while, and I'm starting to want to get him back. So here is a sample roleplay, send me any offers.
---------------- [Scene: Tempe, Arizona. Time: 2:45. The camera opens to the view of water. The camera is rocking back and forth a little bit, and when it looks down you realize that you are in a boat. The camera looks out at the cool blue water and you notice that it is nothing but a little pond. It then looks in the boat and you see different things. A cooler, fishing poles, and then it looks up and you see none other than Vince Taylor lying on his back resting. . .]
[Vince is wearing a pair of tan, baggy cargo shorts. Under those are a pair of white Adidas shoes, that are left untied. He has no shirt on and you can see his cocoa brown skin. This also exposes his incredibly built chest and upper body. On his wrist is his usually gleaming Rolex watch. His eyes are concealed by his trademard gold framed Rayban sunglasses, because it is a sunny day. He must have felt the presence of the camera, because he looks up. He smiles when he sees it, and begins to speak. . .]
"THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: It's gonna be a big week for me out here in
Arizona. <Smiling.> As usual I got some women lined up that I'm gonna get with, and as usual I'm gonna pick up another victory in Aye Es Pee competiton.
Oh but this week it's not just your usual match, this week it's one of the biggest matches in the spectacular career of Tee El Em.
[Vince pauses and collects his thoughts. . .] "THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: Let's do a little recollecting shall we? Well last week I stepped into the squared circle with <Twirls his finger in the air.> "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd... No, no, let me rephrase that, "Less Than
Fabulous" Freddy Floyd. Well I was beatin' his ass from post to pillar, but fortunately for him we ran out of time. And since the winner was goin' to advance to the finals of the World Title tournament, Kincade decided that both men should advance, making it a Triple Threat match at this week's Tuesday
Night Tailgate for the World Title. And when he announced that it really didn't bother me 'cause I figured I could either finish whoopin' his ass right then, or just postpone it 'til next week.
<Pause.> It's really no surprise though, we all knew that I would be in the finals, only difference is that I get to whip up on two guys instead of one.
No biggie, maybe it'll give a little extra challenge. I know I won't get one from Floyd, but maybe Williams can make it some sort of a match.
[Vince sits up from his lying position. . .] "THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: Floyd, you gave it everything you possibly could last week, yet you still couldn't come away with a victory. Yet I on the other hand, fought a mediocre match and still cruised to an easy draw. You've got nothing left that you didn't already show me last week. I know what to expect from you, and I know how to counter it. So to put it in simple terms for 'ya
Freddy, it'll be a cold day in hell before you win _MY_ World Title. I on the other hand, will pull out all the stops this week. I showed you very little in our last contest, and I still have my ace left in the hole. I still have a few things up my sleeve that I guarantee will amaze not only the fans, but you yourself.
[Vince reaches into his pocket and pulls out a cigar and a lighter. He puts the cigar in his mouth and lights. He takes a few drags, nods his head in appreciation, and continues. . .]
"THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: Then the other man that is under the illusion that he has a chance to win _MY_ title is "Mr. Wonderful" Chuck Williams.
<Shakes his head.> Keep dreamin' you big lug. The bigger they are, the harder they fall. And you'll find that out all to soon Chuckster. Now I'll admit it, you're much, much, much more talented than Floyd. But like Floyd and all the others, you'll crumble under the pressure. But don't feel bad Chuck, it's perfectly normal not to be able to measure up to "The Ladies Man." And you'll feel the same way. We've been in many federations, but have never found our paths crossing. But this week you'll know what it means to "Make Money", when you get nailed with a Money Maker!
[Vince finds a nearby ash tray and lays his cigar in it. He the continues. . .] "THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: So I guess what we all knew was inevitable will happen on Tuesday. I'll dominate the Aye Es Pee, and become the first ever
World Champion.
[Vince turns away likes he is done, but then suddenly turn his head back around. . .] "THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: And one more thing... somebody that is "Fabulous" and somebody that is "Wonderful" is no match for... THE. . . LADIES. . . MAN!! [The scene fades to black as Vince lays back down and starts his napping again. . .] -[name withheld]- -Handler of Dave Hicks- -[IFU 0-0-0]- -Handler of "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor- -Free Agent-
