RP tell me what ya'll think

21 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OK this guy is a blatant rip off of The Crow so instead of commenting on the character just tell me what you think of the actual rp. Thank You.
*************************************************************** (The scene opens with a crow flying through the night air. The sky is filled with black clouds that can just be seen against the black night sky. There is only a half moon tonight as the other half of the bright white moon is hidden behind a cloud. The crow drops out of the sky and lands perched headstone in a misty graveyard. The graveyard is full and some of the headstones are crumbling and leaning forward because of ageing. A dark figure walks out of the mist to stand behind the headstone that the crow is perched on and stands still in some dim light. He is wearing a black leather trench coat and has long black hair that has been yet down in typical wrestler style. He is not a big man and could probably be compared to the Hardy Boyz. The man puts his hand out for the crow to walk on.)
The Man: “Some people believe that when someone dies a crow carries their soul to the land of the dead but sometimes something so bad happens that a terrible sadness is carried with it and sometimes just sometimes the bird can bring that soul back to put the bad thing right.” That was a quote from a film I once saw, you may know it you may not but that doesn’t really matter, the film has no relevance to me, just the quote. Since I saw that film and heard the little girls voice at the start saying that quote, I’ve had her voice stuck in my head just repeating it over and over and over because this was when I realised this must have been what happened to me. This must be why I walk the Earth once more but why? Why am I back here, in this world, this hell, MY
OWN PERSONAL HELL. Why? Why was I brought back? but wait I know why, I know what I’m here for, I KNOW and soon everyone else will too. But all you need to know for now is that I’m on my way to the IWA and someone... naming no names but someone will feel the same hell I’ve been through and the hell I’m still going to this day.
(The crow suddenly bolts it’s long wings out and flys off) The man: Are you scared? you should be. I’ve talked about hell a lot in the last few minutes or so and hell is something I can talk about at great length because Hell is something I know a lot about. I’ve known hell for my whole life from being a young boy not knowing who he is not knowing my true identity and having no family to look after me to being a confused young man wondering the Earth alone after thinking it was all over. That is hell but
I also know another hell, you see I’ve been to hell and felt the intense heat pressed against my face, I’ve felt the burn from being so close to the flames that they slowly slowly burn away at your skin without even touching. I’VE BEEN FACE TO FACE THE DEVIL
HIMSELF, oh yeah I’ve been to hell but now I’m back and I’m still living my own personal hell so it doesn’t matter what I do I can’t seem to escape it.
(The wind has now picked up and we hear a few small pats of rain drops hitting the ground and it begins to rain heavily.)
The Man: I’M BACK, but what does that make me? what am I? I could be a soldier for the Devil sent back to fight for Evil and wipe out all that is good but it could also make me an Angel. But no it doesn’t make me either of those things I’m not an Angel I’m
THE Angel. What am I hear for?.... you’ll find out soon very soon. (Suddenly flames shoot up seemingly from underneath the dirt of the grave The Angel is standing in front of and stay burning despite the now heavy rain that is dropping like bombs from the sky. The Man turns around and starts walking away as the picture fades to black and we are left looking at the small fire from the grave before the picture is completely blacked out.)
