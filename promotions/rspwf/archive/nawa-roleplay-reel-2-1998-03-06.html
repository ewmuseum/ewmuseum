(Scene starts with the Flamboyant Paul Dare and a sizable sparring partner in a wrestling ring at a unkown location. The only thing that is lit is the wrestling ring and about five feet around the ring. The rest of the surrounding area is in pitch darkness. At ring side can be seen Master Ito,
Rob the Rock, and again the unknown giant.)
Master Ito- Begin!! ( The two start in the ring by circling each other. Dare shows a little smile to his opponent. Then they grab each others hands, interlocking fingers for a test of strength. Dare uses this to his advantage by kicking his opponent in the gut. Doubling him over, Dare sets him up for a swinging neck breaker. After that, Dare picks him up and pokes him the eye. From there
Dare does a bodyslam and follows with a falling knee to the nose. Dare from there again picks him up; and flings his opponent against the ropes. When his opponent returns. Dare gives him a clothesline, flattening his opponent on his back. Now the Flamboyant One goes for his finishing move- The Market
Crash. A version of a reverse neck breaker. He holds him, then looks to
Master Ito for a signal.)
Master Ito- Finish him!!! ( Bamm!! Down goes Paul Dare to the mat with him and his sparring partner in his finishing manuever. The unfortunate opponent feeling the jarring pain of the Market Crash in the neck. Dare rises to his feet. The opponent in the middle of the ring flat on his back unconscious. Rob the
Rock
claps in approval. Master Ito bows in respect. The mysterious figure does not react at all.)
Rob the Rock- Good job, boss. Paul Dare- Thanks Rock. (now looking at the camera) So you see. I just haven't been watching wrestling myself. I have been training for many months for this opportunity to wrestle in the IMWL. So again beware because;......
something wicked this way comes, freakboys. ( Dare crawls under the bottom rope to meet the rest of Rising Sun
Incorporated. They turn to look at the still unconscious sparring partner in the squared circle. Then one by one they walk into the darkened surrounding area.)
