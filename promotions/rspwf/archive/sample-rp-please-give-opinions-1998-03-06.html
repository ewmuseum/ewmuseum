Anywho, here is the RP. This was made for the EESW ( [detail withheld] ) on March 3rd, 1998: ——————————————————————————— ~The screen is pitch black. The letters "GcG" fade into the screen, and when that finishes, the words "Gang-Greene Productions, 3-2-98" are printed on the bottom in small white text. The theme music for "Gorgeous" Cyrus Greene, Sex
Type Thing by Stone Temple Pilots, starts playing quietly in the background, and the screen fades over to a well lit interview room. Sitting in different chairs, facing eachother, is an interviewer and non other than the wonderful, the amazing, the "Gorgeous" Cyrus Greene. Cyrus Greene is wearing a black
T-Shirt that reads "I dO wHat thE vOicEs iN My hEAd tELL mE tOo." He is also wearing an Oakland Raiders cap which is turned backwards, a pair of blue Levi jeans, and some silver-tinted Rayban sunglasses. His shoulder legnth blonde hair is brushed behind his hat, and it shows on the back of the head. Since it is a side-view, you can't see the ENTIRE back of the head, but who really cares? The interviewer is a little on the chubby side, and is wearing a grey suit with some really cheap tie. He looks like a mix between Jim Ross and Jim
Cornett. He is, wearing glasses. The chairs that they are sitting in are pretty big, black chairs, and they look very comfortable. The back setting is just a blueish-black wall. In between them is a coffiee table, with an assortment of snacks are placed on. In front of each of them is a glass filled with water and ice. Cyrus's music stops playing in the background, and the interview starts.~
Interviewer: Hello everyone, I am Jim Rosnet, and with me here is Extreme Elite Supreme Wrestling superstar, Gorgeous Cyrus Greene. Glad you could make here today, Greene.
Cyrus Greene: You better be, it's not too often that I allow overweight horrid figures like yourself interview someone with as much class and beuity as
MYself. The only way you got me here was the amounts of digets on that check, fat boy. ~Jim Rosnet is obviously irritated by Cyrus Greene allready with those remarks being made, but just brushes his fealings off to the side and takes a sip of water. Jim Rosnet places the cup back down, and gets back to the interview.~
Jim Rosnet: Ok, well, with that aside, lets cut right to the beef, shall we? As every EESW fan knows, "Gorgeous" Cyrus Greene is one of the people that are predicted to win the Battle Royal for the EESW World Heavyweight Championship.
Along with you, the people that are also rated high on the predictions are Rattlesnake, Death, Coolest Kid, and Big Fat Pimp. They are all highly expirenced wrestlers, and if my information serves me correctly, you are not.
Cyrus Greene: Allright, I guess I'll give you some credit that your sources are correct. No, I am not a highly expirenced wrestler. I am only twenty-two years old, and the only other federation I have ever participated in other than the EESW is a federation called the SCW, which unfortunetly had folded a little over a week it was founded. But I do have ring expirence, coming from the
Hart's Wrestling School, one of the most highly regarded wrestling school in the WORLD. In that school I had beaten some men that weighed almost 500 pounds when I was twenty, not by over-powering them, but by using my superior high flying methods to ground them. Same with technical wrestlers, brawlers, and other high flyers. It all comes down to one thing, keep them on the ground, and you got the match made. And my stunning good looks help too, because we all know that ugly fat people, such as yourself, never win any matches. And that good looking, high-class people, such as myself, always win their matches.
~Cyrus Greene smirks over this coment, grabs a couple Cheetos from the coffiee table, and scafs them down. Jim Rosnet prepares for his next question to Cyrus
Greene, which are all neatly organizaed in notecards in his hand.~ Jim Rosnet: Ok. Now, there is a "stable" if you will, known as Death Row. You and Terror were getting in each others face yesterday at a wrestler's pub. What are your comments on him, if any? Cyrus Greene: You know, it's probably not that smart to start picking fights with people when you have a title shot coming up. And i'ts downright stupid to start a fight with an entire stable before one. But, I seriously could care less because I could beat the hell out of each and every one of them, and they know it. Terror is not much of a wrestler, plain and simple. I've seen him in action before on television. But he has one thing over me, and that is that he has a large alliance. Sure, I could beat them all, even at the same time. The reason they would be able to get an advatage over me is that when they attack me, I am semi-worn out from the fight I just had! But I don't want to get into more s(bleep) before my Battle Royal, considering a member of them is in it, and I don't need them all coming down to gang-bang me. So, whats the next question?
Jim Rosnet: Rumors are running wild that you may have a surprise for the Battle Royal. Could you possibly give us more information? ~Cyrus laughs over this comment, and grabs a few cheetos. He has been snacking on cheetos through this entire interview.~
Cyrus Greene: Fat boy, I don't have a "Surprise" in store for that battle royal. And even if I did, what in gods name would make you think that an intellegent person like myself would spill the beans to someone who is obviously of lower mental status. I sure as hell don't know. Now, you said something about replying to fan letters? Well, we all know I got about half a trillion fans in the world, so your only gonna get to tell me a few, I don't got all day like you. I got places to go, people to see, things to d..
Jim Rosnet: Sure you.. ~Cyrus Greene snaps back at Rosnet before he even gets to finish his third word in the sentence. He gets pissed off at people who do this kind of crap to him.~
Cyrus Greene: Hey you obeesse mothe(bleeeep) bladarent redneck bastard! I don't interupt you when your f(bleep)ng your sister, or your donkey, or your self! So show me just a LITTLE bit of respect and have a nice, cold glass of
SHUT THE HELL UP. Now get to the f(bleep)ng letters! ~This had caught Jim Rosnet off gaurd, and he looks very upset by the coments made by Cyrus Greene. Cyrus Greene drinks from his glass of water, and takes a few more Cheetos. Rosnet pulls out a couple letters off the coffie table and reads the first one on the list aloud.~
Jim Rosnet: This one is from Porker Morrise of Diablo, California. Mr. Greene, why are you always such an ass hole to everyone? Everywhere you go you disrespect the people you meet. I'm sure you have allready constantly poked fun at the size of Jim Rosnet, who is a very fine indivisual. Now I'm su..
~Cyrus Greene looks disgusted by this letter and interupts Jim Rosnet before he had the chance to finish.~
Cyrus Greene: Allright, I've heard all I need to hear. Now I know for a fact that this guy has a severe mental illness. Porker, get some help. The reason
I do this is to point at a well known fact to the "un-believers" of the world. I do what everyone wants to do, speak their mind. If you think what I think is all pesimest and insulting, thats what you think. I think it's the truth, because Jim here IS fat and we all want to tell him that in his face! I got the power to do that, and because of the money he paid me, he can't afford to lose me as a guest! Thats the beuity of being a celeb, you can do what you want, when you want.
~Jim turns to the next letter.~ Jim Rosnet: This one is from Jeremy Johnson of Seattle Washington. Hey Cyrus, even though I have only seen you two days, you have became one of my favorite personalities. Growing up in Seattle, what were some of your favorite music artists?
Cyrus Greene: Now this kid has an education. Well Jeremy, my #1 favorite all-time band is Nirvana. No question about that, they are the best. I grew up listening to stuff like Pearl Jam, and other bands popular in the early
90's. Also, just for general info, I ran a club band for three years before decieding to become a professional wrestler, because the money involved in doing music for clubs ain't that great.
~Like last time, Jim turns to the next letter (Bet you didn't see that coming!)~ Jim Rosnet: This one comes from Al Issacs of...Scoopscentral.com. Cyrus, unfortunetly, you forgot to answer my questions at the press conference, but don't worry, I got a whole new batch for you. Are you in anyways related to
Bret Hart, and if so are you his brother..and what is your favorite ice cream flavor... ~Cyrus Greene mumbles something to himself before deciding to answer the questions.~ Cyrus Greene: Fatty, can you give me Al Issacs address after this program? I don't think the security gaurds did a very good job of shutting him up.
Jim Rosnet: Yes, I can. Last question...This comes from Annoymus. That person states YOU SUCK CYRUS! I CUOLD BEAT U ANE DY OF TEH WEKE, TWCE ON
SNDAY! CAUS STNE CLD SAID SO SON! ADN THTS TEH BOTOM LIEN CUSE $TONECOLD SAID SO BICH! ~Cyrus Greene spits out the water he was drinking all over the coffie table and starts cracking up. Once he stops, about 10 seconds later, he decides to respond to the letter.~
Cyrus Greene: How many times do I have to tell you guys, that you are all idiots! If that letter is true, not only is a man that sucks going to be the first, and last, EESW champion, but he is gonna kick the crap out of Stone Cold
Steve Austin just for starting this all, SON. Now fat boy, according to my $15,000 Rolex.. ~Jim Rosnet glares at the watch for a second.~ Jim Rosnet: That's a digital watch...Timex if I'm not mistaken. Probably worth around $25.
Cyrus Greene: I didn't ask you! According to my WATCH, I'm outa here you piece of redneck trash.
~Cyrus Greene picks up the bag of Cheetos and walks off the camera. The camera is still on and Jim Rosnet looks at his watch.~
Jim Rosnet: Sh(bleep)..he still had ten minutes left... ~Camera fades out back to whatever the hell is on next. I'll let you all guess whats on next.~
