If manager will interfere, if so with what weapon: Playmaker's cane and Mama's belt. Wrestlers appearance: Asswhupper is a well built bald black. Unlike his manager he is strictly focused on wrestling. He comes to the ring in black tights that have Asswhupper writen on them in red. He also has a black rag on his head.
Wrestlers hometown: Wherever he damn well fells like living Height: 6'2 Weight: (Playmaker does the introduction) a slim trim, cut, ripped, chisled,buff, jacked, dangerous 245lbs
Age: 23 Nationality: American Race:Black Gimmick and/or special info: Asswhupper's father used to be a wrestler he was also abusive to his family. Asswhupper got interested in wrestling by watching his dad. Asswhupper loved his fatther even though he was abusive however
Asswhupper wanted to one day become a wrestler and inflict the same pain to his father that his father had inflicted on to Asswhuper and the family.
Asswhupper was going to start wrestling as soon as he turned 21. Asswhupper's father died a couple weeks after Asswhuper's 21st birthday. His death left many holes in the Asswhupper. The Asswhupper would never have the opportunity to beat his dad.
Playmaker came along and convinced Asswhupper that it was wrestling that turned his father abusive. He told him that he was abusive becasue he was frustrated that he never won a world heavyweight title Playmaker also told him the only way to let Asswhupper's father's soul rest and the emotional scars of the abuse to go away was to win a world's title. Asswhupper became convinced of this and now is out to abuse every wrestler who stands in his way of the world title.
Asswhupper's girlfriend (not mother)Mama brings to the ring the belt that
Asswhupper was beaten with as a child. Mama uses the belt on opponents and its a constant reminder of Aswhupper's torment. When Asswhupper wins a World title the belt will be buried next to his father's grave.
