Please critique my returning to e-wrestling RP

15 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have just recently gotten back into e-wrestling after about a two year absence. I played for about 2 years mostly in some smaller feds, but I was in the old UEW for the last like 4-5 months. I also ran a fed Global
Championship Wrestling, way back before there were like 6 of them. I just recently got the itch back and joined up with GPW and this is the first RP that I have actually worked on to see what I can still do, so please, tear it apart, tell me whats good and whats bad. I know I still need to sharpen my skills a lot, but I'm looking for some honest critiques.
Thank You. ( P.S - This is my debut RP for GPW ) [ The scene opens on a sidewalk in the "wrong part of town." The street is littered with plastic bags, cups and soda cans blowing around everywhere. The buildings lining this street appear to have been abandoned a long time ago as no evidence of upkeep can be seen. In the area a few bums are scattered about here and there, some bundled up asleep on old stoops, some are huddled around fires burning in trash barrels, while some are just wandering, seemingly aimlessly. Standing on the sidewalk under a flickering street lamp is GPW's own Shay Peters. He is dressed in casual clothes of jeans and a black GPW polo shirt. He looks around nervously, obviously out of his element. ]
SP : " I can't _believe_ they sent me out here! Of all the places to catch an interview .... "
[ Peters is standing directly infront of a large red door to a large warehouse. On the door a sign is prominently posted "No Trespassing." ]
SP : [ To the camera man ] " Well ... here we go. Stay close, and if anything happens ... well ... I'll meet you at the car. "
Camera Man : " If there still _is_ a car. " [ Peters looks back at the camera man with a terrified expression as he reluctantly swings open the door. Once inside we see a large room where a group of about 200 men are gathered. The cement floor and walls have seen better days and show the signs of not being maintained for possibly years. Through the dim lighting we can see that the men are forming a large circle where there is apparently action taking place in the center. The camera man and Peters, clearly intimidated, are able to make their way through the crowd to the edge of the "ring." Inside we can see that this is some sort of fighting competition. Two men are grappling with each other, bare knuckled, throwing wild lefts and rights. The men in the crowd are shouting encouragement to the fighters, each with wads of money in their hands, no doubt betting on the results. One of the fighters is a big man, looking to be about 30 years old, 6' 7 and about
280 pounds. The other fighter is a much smaller man, looking to only be about 6' 5 and 235 pounds. He looks very young though, no more than 24 years old. This man is set apart from the crowd mostly by 8 inch, skinny, bleached white dread locks that bounce around his head as he battles. As the camera continues to watch the men, we see the smaller man with the white hair, showing amazing strength, grab the larger man by his waist and hoist him into the air and slam the man down to the ground. The smaller man, with no hesitation, begins to lay vicious looking left and right fists into the larger man's face until a loud buzzer goes off and the fight is called. The crowd erupts in cheers and boos as the bet money is exchanged hands. A rather large man, atleast
400 pounds, in a cheap suit, the only suit in the crowd, steps to the center and raises the arm of the smaller man and hands him a wad of cash as the man leaves the circle and two other men step in as the beaten opponent is carried to a near-by corner. ]
[ As the winner makes his way out of the circle he grabs a T-shirt and a tattered green duffle bag off the floor. Peters and the camera man make their way through the crowd to follow the man as he goes through the door to the outside. Once outside, the man bundles up his T-shirt and places it to his face to stop a cut from bleeding. ]
SP : [ To the man ] " Excuse me ... excuse me ... are you Clay Michaels? " [ The man turns around to see Shay ] Clay Michaels : " Who's askin? " SP : " Well I'm Shay Peters of the GPW. Head Quarters sent me down here to pick you up for your meeting with Mr. Blain. "
Clay Michaels : " Then yeah, I'm Clay. What's with the camera? [ Without waiting for a response Clay puts his hand up to the lens and says in a raised, stern voice ... ] I don't like cameras! "
SP : " All right, its ok. Joe, put the camera down. [ The camera lowers but still has the two men in shot ] Its just that we tape everything.
You know, if you wanted to say something to the rest of the GPW, this is your chance. " Clay Michaels : " Just keep it outta my face. Now are we going or what? " [ Peters and Clay begin to walk down the block infront of the camera man, Peters has an " what an ass**** look on his face." ]
SP : " So ... Lemme ask you something, what just happened in, well, what the hell was that? Is that legal? "
Clay Michaels : " Legal? Hell ... legal? Do you think it matters out here what the hell is legal or not? What you just saw in there is the extreme underbelly of this country. Its where men compete in the most intense sport possible, beating each other until one can not continue.
You know damn well what is was, how the hell you gonna ask a question like that? " SP : " Ohh ... sorry. Excuse me for saying, but you sound a little bitter, even though you did win. "
Clay Michaels : " Well, I guess after doing this for that past four years of my life, I'm allowed to be a little bitter. Working for scum that piece of lard, living out of this here bag, and having to spend my nights in hell holes like this, yeah, maybe I'm just a little bitter.
But, that's gonna change real soon. As soon as I sign that contract, this'll all be behind me. You can count on that little man . [ Michaels pokes Peters in the chest with two fingers. ] I've been training for the past two years every chance I got. For the past two years I've either been here in the fights, or been in the ring training, every damn day!
This is no joke, I am tired of working this trash, the competition is too weak. There is noone left for me to beat, I've taken them all down, one by one, year after year. No man has ever stepped up to my level and threw me off. Thats why I'm coming to the GPW. I need the bigger, the stronger, the smarter wrestlers to take me even further up the ladder.
You don't survive here without knowing how to fight, and how to survive, and when I make my impact on the GPW, everyone there is going to find out just what a "Street Shooter" is, in Clay Micheals. "
[ The trio arrives at the car, only to see that it is sitting on blocks with completely stripped. Peters and the camera man looked to have messed themselves as Clay busts out laughing at the two. Peters stunned for a moment, reluctantly pulls out his cell phone and tries to call for help, only to see they are out of range. ]
[ Fade to black ]
