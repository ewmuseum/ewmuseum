[Free Agent] Nigel "The Steel Beast" Payton

17 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi guys. If you're interested in recruiting Nigel "The Steel Beast" Payton, infamous for ending the winning streaks of numerous overconfident wrestlers ;^) then e-mail me with information on your fed. (
[detail withheld] ) His stats: Nigel Payton 6' 10" 372lbs Los Angeles, California Ex-police officer on the LAPD, given early retirement for his outstanding record. Finisher: The Steel Bomb Sample roleplay: (The scene opens in front of Nigel Payton's Los Angeles house. As he pulls up to the three-story Los Angeles house which could be considered a mansion in some parts, but is normal here in Los Angeles, he opens his garage door using a remote (We assume. This is Nigel's house we're talking about, here, not Bill
Gates) and pulls the car in the garage. After turning the car off, he opens the door and gets out, then walks out of the garage, down his driveway, and towards his mailbox which is standing at the end of the driveway. As he approaches it, a man dressed in a white dress shirt and khaki dress pants goes up to him, holding a recorder in one hand. Nigel halts his journey to his mailbox temporarily to speak to the man, who appears to be a reporter.)
Nigel: Can I help you? Reporter: Mr. Payton, my name is Douglas Emerson, I'm with the Wrestling World
Watcher of California newsletter... we're doing a special on yourself.
Nigel: Oh, really? Emerson: Yes sir. We've already done background research. What I want from you, if it's ok with you, is some comments on your upcoming triangle match between you, Dr. Pain of the Legendary Physicians, and Zach "The Shooting Star"
Bradley. Nigel: Of course... on one condition. (The reporter cocks his head slightly, unexpecting of a 'condition'.) Emerson: What, uh, would that be, Mr. Payton? (Nigel smiles.) Nigel: Why, send me a copy of the newsletter I'm in, of course! (Emerson laughs.) Emerson: Mr. Payton, we'll offer you a free subscription, if you'd like! Nigel: Sure! If you don't know my address... (Nigel motions towards his mailbox.) Nigel: Just look here. (The reporter laughs.) Emerson: I'll remember that. Now, first of all, what are your feelings towards the method in which you received your place in the triangle match?
Nigel: Well, I'm not ashamed of it. Sure, maybe Dr. Pain and Bradley had to win one more match than I did, but I took the 'bye', just as any sane wrestler would do. Neither Dr. Pain nor Zach Bradley I'm sure would turn the offer down.
Emerson: I agree. Nigel: Besides... what good would it do if I turned it down? Someone else who would most likely stand less of a chance of winning it than I would would get the shot just the same way I did. I honestly feel that if anybody has a chance at beating Pain, it's me.
Emerson: Which brings me to my next question... you show a great deal of respect towards the larger half of the Physicians.
Nigel: That I certainly do. Emerson: Despite the respect between the two of you, you two have never been on the best of terms... why?
Nigel: Well, that's very simple, and I don't think it's anything you need me to answer, but I will anyway. You see, the Physicians are loners. Now, don't take this wrong, Pain and MID-Nite, but just how many stables have you been in?
How many alliances? Very few to none... at least none I can remember. The
Physicians work so much better when it's simply the two of them, they don't need anybody to be their "friend", quote unquote. They've proven that you don't have to have an army backing you up to succeed. Neither do I, at that.
I'll be out there by myself. Emerson: What about your long-time friend, Hunter Cole? Nigel: Mr. Emerson, Hunter is one of my best friends... but he knows and I know that there's no reason for him to be out there. Just in case, though...
Emerson: Though? Nigel: Just in case something were to happen, outside interference in the match that would NOT increase my chances of winning, Hunter will be in the back to try to straighten things out. Trust me, he's good at breaking up riots.
You've never seen anybody break up a barfight until you've seen this guy do it. It's simply amazing. He might not be as large as me, but he can be just as intimidating, and as soon as he earns more of a name here in the CCW, he'll be a force to deal with. I can see it now.
Emerson: Sounds good. Now, your thoughts on Zach Bradley. Nigel: What about him? Emerson: Well, uh... what do you think about him? Nigel: Emerson, I really don't know what to think. I don't see how I can say much about him. To my recollection, the man has yet to even acknowledge my presence in the triangle match in which he WILL be facing me! But don't worry,
I'll make sure that he knows I'm there when we get in the ring. He won't be able not to.
Emerson: Any other thoughts? Perhaps anymore on Dr. Pain? (Nigel scratches his chin.) Nigel: Pain, it's gonna be tough. If I wasn't in the match, my money would be on you. But since I am (and I'm not a gamblin' man!) then your chances aren't as great. Trust me, Pain. I won't be as easy as you may think.
Emerson: Alright, that should be good. Thank you very much Nigel, and look for your article within a few days.
Nigel: Anytime. (Emerson starts to stop the recorder. Nigel puts his hand on Emerson's arm.) Nigel: Wait, one more thing. (Emerson looks confused.) Emerson: What's that? Nigel: Well, I've always wanted to end one of my interviews like this, and this seems the best way to do so...
Emerson: Well, how is that? (Nigel leans over and speaks into the microphone...) Nigel: YEAH BABY!!! [Side note: This is how most of the interviews of Dr. Pain, my opponent, ended, so that explains the odd ending.]
