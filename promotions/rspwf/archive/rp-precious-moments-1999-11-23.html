<RP> Precious Moments

23 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The streets of Detroit at night are usually quiet, bar the random gunshots and screams that break this tranquility for a moment or two, but then are gone.
But on this night, two voices have broken the silence. We enter the scene on two men, covered in black tape, and wearing pink and blue tu-tus, respectively.
At this point, I think it's painfully clear whom is disturbing the peace on this night. The Binky and Winky Fairies, also known as Precious Moments)
The Binky Fairy: Boy oh boy... I never knew those Hatebreeders were this nice. Look at us, Wink. We're decked out in this beautiful black ribbon, compliments of The Hatebreeders. We've discovered a new way to buy ribbon, and a much funner way to use it, compliments of The Hatebreeders...
The Winky Fairy: It's more enjoyable, Bink. TBF: I know it is! Isn't that what I said? TWF: No, no, no. You said it's much "funner". Funner isn't a word, Bink. It would be "much more enjoyable", not "much funner".
TBF: You're way too smart, Wink. Anyway, back to what I was saying... what was
I saying? TWF: You were talking about The Hatebreeders, and how nice they are. TBF: OH YEAH! They're just really nice guys. So, instead of calling themselves "The Hatebreeders", I think they should change their name. From now on, they will be called "The Happybreeders".
TWF: So, Happybreeders... do you have any more presents?! TBF: Wink! Don't be so greedy! TWF: But... but... I want a new toy! TBF: Hush. That's no way to go about asking for one. You have to be kind and courteous. (Suddenly, from up above their heads, a voice is heard) Voice: KEEP IT DOWN! THERE ARE PEOPLE TRYING TO SLEEP, MOTHER<BLEEP>ER! TWF: I know I have to be kind and courteous, but... I REALLY WANT A NEW TOY! Voice: IF YOU DON'T SHUT THE <BLEEP> UP, I'LL GIVE YOU A MOTHER<BLEEP>ING TOY,
MOTHER<BLEEP>ER!
TBF: Shhh... we're angering the natives. We have to be quiet. Humans sleep at night. They're not like us.
TWF: Oh... ok. SORRY UP THERE! Voice: IF I HEAR ONE MORE WORD OUT OF YOUR GOD<BLEEP> MOUTH, I'M GONNA KILL
YOU, MOTHER<BLEEP>ERS!
TBF: I think we should move from here. Lets go, Wink. TWF: Ok, Bink. (Winky and Binky start skipping, arm-in-arm, down the street, while singing the theme from "The Smurfs")
TBF and TWF: La la lalalala la lalalala... la la lalalala la lalalala... Voice: WHAT THE <BLEEP>!? YOU'RE MOTHER<BLEEP>ING SINGING THE MOTHER<BLEEP>ING
SMURF'S SONG!? THAT'S IT, YOU BITCHASS MOTHER<BLEEP>ERS!
TBF: Uh oh... TWF: I think we're in trouble, Binky. TBF: Let's run! (Binky and Winky start running down the street, as a large black man bursts out of the house Binky and Winky were singing under just moments ago)
Large Black Man: YOU BETTA RUN! IF I SEE YOUR ASSES AROUND HERE AGAIN, I'M
CAPPING YOUR ASSES! KEEP RUNNING, YOU BITCHASS MOTHER<BLEEP>ERS!
(As Binky and Winky run, the tape wrapped around their bodies flapping in the wind, they flap their arms, as if they were trying to fly)
TBF: Huff... huff... I don't think... huff... huff... we should go over there... huff... huff... anymore.
TWF: Huff... huff... agreed... huff... huff... (Finally, Binky and Winky stop, and attempt to catch their breath) TBF: Woo... that was a good workout! TWF: Yep, yep! We'll be ready for whomever the AWMC deems evil enough for us to face next!
TBF: And that evil will be defeated... oh yes... yes it will. (Both Binky and Winky look at each other, and start giggling insanely. After several seconds of this, they stop)
TBF: What were you laughing at, Wink? TWF: I was laughing because you were laughing. TBF: I was laughing because... oh yeah! I was thinking... maybe we should ask the AWMC to book us in a match.
TWF: But against who? TBF: How about... ummm... I KNOW! THE THREAT! TWF: Why The Threat? TBF: Because they tried beating us up, and that's not very nice. In fact, that's downright evil!
TWF: How so? TBF: Well... we were in the middle of fighting evil. Then, they attacked us when we were about to vanquish the evil. That means...
TWF: THEY SAVED THE EVIL?! TBF: And thusly, they are evil themselves. TWF: Evil is bad. TBF: Evil is very bad. TWF: Evil is very, very bad. (After several minutes of Binky and Winky one-uping each other, they finally stop) TWF: Evil is very, very, very, very, very, very, very, very, very, very bad. TBF: What were we talking about? (Winky shrugs) TBF: Oh well. Let's go see if we can find some more of this ribbon in a box! TWF: WOO HOO! (With that, we leave Binky and Winky skipping down the streets of Detroit, in search of "ribbon in a box". And since taping a brick wall isn't exactly entertaining, we fade to black)
