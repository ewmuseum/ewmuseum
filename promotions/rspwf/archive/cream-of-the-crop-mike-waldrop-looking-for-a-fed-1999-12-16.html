"Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop looking for a fed?

This is a new character I've created and I'm looking for a league to put him in. Here are the criteria I require [yeah, it might sound picky, but oh well].
-Must be a roleplay original league [no simulators]. -Must have quality matches, no summarized matches on major shows. -Web page isn't a must, but I do want to see some of the cards. Here's a sample roleplay. If you want his stats, you can contact me at
[detail withheld] . [Scene opens up to a smoke filled pool hall. A TV plays loudly reciting the sports scores from the day as loud 80s rock music plays as well. The camera catches a glimpse of a man with long blonde hair surrounded by three blonde busty beautiful women. All wearing a black bare mid-drift and black mini-skirt.
The man, who is rather attractive, is wearing a white turtleneck and a pair of blue jeans, grabs a pool cue. One of the busty blonde beauties racks the balls as this man with blonde locks and boyish good looks catches a glimpse of the camera and leans up against the table posing with cue in hand.]
Man: Why hello there? For all of you who don't know me, I the newest force in the NWWA, Mike Waldrop. I'm 19 years old and oozing with wrestling talent. Not to mention I am so damned sexy.
[One of the women interlocks her arm with Waldrop's.] Woman: [seductively speaking] Mike, let's play. MW: Alright, one second, babe. [She gives Waldrop a kiss on the cheek and Waldrop returns the favor with a lip lock. The busty blonde leaves the view and Waldrop continues to speak to the camera.]
MW: Uhh, where was I? Ahh yes, describing myself. Let's see, I've mentioned being damned sexy, 19 years old and gifted with wrestling talent, what else is there?
[Waldrop taps his foot on the ground posing as if he were thinking.] MW: I know there was one more thing. [Waldrop snaps his fingers.] MW: I've got it! I'm also, not to mention gifted with talent, damned sexy and 19 years old, but I'm the Cream of the Crop! I've followed wrestling for practically all of my life and I've seen some great performers come and go, but these guys are no match compared to my astonishing ability that I posess. Many of you out there might be saying, "Big deal, another one of those cocky bastards running his mouth again. Wait until he steps into the ring with a veteren." Well, to put all of you nay sayers to rest, I'm 6'1 and weigh in at a buff, cut, slim, trim two hundred and twenty and one half pounds. I just make the cruiser ranks and heavyweight ranks. And to prove that I am just as great as I say I am, I will be holding a special challenge to any wrestler in the NWWA. If you wish to take on the "Cream of the Crop"
then by all means step forth and make it known. [Waldrop holds his cue in his right hand and walks over to the pool table to break. He chalks up the cue and then sets up for his shot.]
MW: Some claim that they're great, then they turn out to flop. But no one can claim to be as great as the "Cream of the Crop," Mike Waldrop.
[Waldrop shoots the cue ball and breaks the balls apart as he and the three other blonde beauties pose in front of the camera.]
