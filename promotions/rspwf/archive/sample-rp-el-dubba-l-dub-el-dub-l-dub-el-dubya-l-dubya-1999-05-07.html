(Sample RP) El Dubba, L Dub, El Dub, L Dub, El Dubya, L Dubya...

7 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Haven't posted one in a while. If you critique it, fine; if not, fine. This is one I did for EMWC, and it's one of the best since El Dubba, L Dub, El
Dub, L Dub, El Dubya, L Dubya's return a couple of months ago.
(The camera fades in on the living room of EMWC newcomer "Confident" Lawrence Williams. Williams, who is layed back on the couch, with his legs spread out and his arms across both sides of the couch, just looks at some Monday night wrestling program. The camera pans around to catch a glimpse to see some old guy with a World title across his waist walk down the rampway. It's at this moment that Lawrence turns off the television and gives the camera the smirk of sanguine. Lawrence begins to speak:)
LW: There's somethin' that has really been pissin' me off. An' yeah, a lot of things piss El Dub off, but this one has really latched onto my nerves and hung on... It's the fact that this _bum_ VD Vitrone is gettin' his _second_ title shot within a month.
Now you're sayin' that he payed his dues by winnin' that little five-way cruiserweight match we had las' week, but I say he didn't. I say he beat one guy. Chris Van
Ryan was the one who did all the work an' only to be eliminated by Vitrone. If anybody should be gettin' that shot at Youth Gone Wild, it should be Ryan. Seems t'me that Vitrone is gettin' some luck nowadays. But all know that luck doesn't last forever - have fun next week,
Vitrone. (Small chuckle is given by Lawrence.) LW: But to more important items: Chris Van Ryan. I must say, Chris: y'surprised me. Of course, I respect you, because I respect any man who is able to compete in this game, but y'really surprised me. Y'eliminated two guys, when I couldn't elimimate one. You were able t'stay until the end, when I was the second man out, not including that Tokyo Fubu wrestling reject.
Ryan, y'surprised me. An' when you surprise Lawrence Williams... When you give something to Lawrence Williams that he wasn't expectin'... (Close-up.) LW: Y'get respected. But... (Even closer close-up, with the smirk of sanguine.) LW: Respect is as far as it goes, kid. You could do a triple back flip off the Golden Gate Bridge and make no splash at all, an' I still wouldn't give uh damn 'bout you. The fact uh the matter is that while I might respect you, respect only gets _you_ a determined Lawrence Williams in the ring - but that won't and _can't_ stop me from spittin' on you, or kickin' the crap outta' your... testicular vicinity.
Chris, you handed me my first loss in about 5 o' 6 months. You also gave me my official welcoming to the
Extreme Mail Wrestling Council. In other words, you let me in the house, an' showed me the ways of the house, but y'know what they say:
Give 'em an' inch... They'll take uh mile. An' I plan t'run miles up an' down y'face on Noise Pollution, Chris. I plan t'show you what happens when you manifest bad manners to your company. I plan,
Chris.... to basically beat the <bleep!> out of ya'. An' when all uh that's over. You'll know not t'mess with the conqueror... the cocksure.... the - an' I quote -
"_sexiest_ black man you have ever seen"... "Confident" Lawrence Williams. (Smirk.) An' if that ain't the truth, y'don't want to be lied to, boy. See you in the ring. (Gradual fade-out.) Thank you. Dan.
