Rookie o'yr Opinion Request

24 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay...this is going to be the only plug I'm gonna do for myself in the rookie of the year competition...So, if you're tired of reading this kind of self promoting stuff, just go to the next message. But I really wouldn't mind -some- opinions on how my roleplays are. So if you feel like sticking around for -that-....:)
This roleplay is from the MAPW, a branch of the CWC, which has now taken its old name, Southern Championship Wrestling...I wrote it as a reaction to a match I had with one of the showcase talents...*coughjobbercough* and also raised some of the issues Caliban was dealing with at the time.
================================================================== <<fade in to the rear exit of the Mid-Atlantic venue. It is late in the evening, and another spectacular energy charged night of wrestling has just electrified the crowds. The wrestlers are making their way to their limos, tour buses, either heading for a good night's sleep or a late night party. From the poorly lit exit steps
Caliban. He is dressed in a leather jacket, jeans, and running shoes. A young woman approaches him. She is dressed in a navy blazer and matching miniskirt, with black high heels. She is also wearing fashionable dark sunglasses, quite unusual for night wear. Her long dark hair is flowing free. Knowledgeable Mid-Atlantic wrestling fans would easily recognize her as Victoria, Von Johnson's attractive
"valet". Caliban sees the woman and tips his head in a questioning glance. He is obviously not expecting her.>>
[VICTORIA]: "Caliban? I come in peace...Can I talk with you a moment?" [CALIBAN]: <<Stopping a moment, one can hear the bristling in his voice>> "What is it you want to talk with me about?"
[VICTORIA]: "I haven't seen you in person since..." [CALIBAN]: "My match with Von. I know." [VICTORIA]: "I want to apologize for kicking you while you were down...I got caught up in the match...and I was trying to help Vonnie win...he works so hard, you know..."
[CALIBAN]: "He's a good wrestler, Victoria, but he needs to push himself more. As for your apology, I accept it."
[VICTORIA]: "Thanks...I felt really bad about it...I was hoping I could make it up to you...can I take you out to dinner?"
<<A slight, hesitant smile crosses Caliban's face at the offer from the very attractive lady before him, but it fades to his usual neutral expression>>
[CALIBAN]: "I'll agree...on one condition. Will you take off your sunglasses for me?" <<Victoria, suddenly caught in a corner, slowly removes her sunglasses. We see her pretty face marred by a black eye. She puts them back on nervously>>
[CALIBAN]: "I thought so...after all...the last person to wear sunglasses at night was Corey Hart...a present from Von, right?"
[VICTORIA]: "He just gets so frustrated when he doesn't win matches...I...I wish there was something I could do..."
[CALIBAN]: "Well, you and I can talk about it over dinner. All right?" [VICTORIA]: "That would be wonderful. Shall we take my car or yours?" <<The scene fades as Caliban and Victoria walk off...then fades back in to the interior of a well known bistro. We can see Caliban and
Victoria sitting at a quiet table "talking shop".>> [CALIBAN]: "Really, Vic...Von's got awesome upper body strength...but
I think for him to be formidable, he needs to work on his legs. I will do up a regimen of stretches and excercises the two of you can do together to work his legs up. If he gets them limber and strong, he'll be unstoppable."
[VICTORIA]: <taken slightly aback>> "You want me to work on MY legs too?" [CALIBAN]: <<With his wry smile>> "No...not what I meant..., your legs are fabulous just the way they are...<<she smiles at the compliment>>...but with this new workout, he'll need a good spotter when he's stretching his legs, someone he knows and trusts...and it will give you something you can do with him...like a
"quality time" thing..."
<<suddenly, we hear a loud voice yell out a phrase familiar to fans of television comedy>> [VOICE]: "NO SOUP FOR YOU!!!!" <<A frustrated chef walks by Caliban and Victoria. He is carrying a small bowl on a tray. Caliban motions to the chef who, as he re- enters the shot, bears a surprising resemblance to Al Pacino. It is the infamous Soup Nazi.>>
[CALIBAN]: "You're working HERE now?" [SOUP NAZI]: "What can I do? The show is over, and they would NOT listen to my ideas for a spinoff..."
<<The Soup Nazi shakes his head and marches off in a huff>> [VICTORIA]: "How do you know HIM?" [CALIBAN]: "We met a while back in Anchorage. He was looking for fresh Snow Crab for his bisque. I have friends in the fishing business up there, and I keep him stocked."
<<Victoria smiles and looks at Caliban.>> [VICTORIA]: "You know, Caliban...I had an alterior motive for offering you dinner..." [CALIBAN]: "Oh? And what was that?" [VICTORIA]: "Well, aside from my valet duties, I run a small wrestling fanzine...we've been getting a lot of mail about you, and I was hoping you could answer a few questions..."
[CALIBAN]: "I'll do what I can to answer..." [VICTORIA]: "Great! Okay, let's see...what is the biggest problem you see in your career right now?"
[CALIBAN]: "If my challenge match to Rick Donovan is accepted, and I lose...<<Caliban chuckles>>...all kidding aside, though...I think the big problem I have is that as an unusual kind of guy, people like to make me a target...they like to blame me when things go wrong...<<Caliban shrugs>>...not much I can do about it though..."
[VICTORIA]: "Hmm...and what do you feel is your biggest strength? Your martial arts background?" [CALIBAN]: "Actually no...my biggest strength is that people underestimate me." [VICTORIA]: "How so?" [CALIBAN]: "Well, think about it. Everyone thinks I'm some sort of superfreak chopsocky wrestler, right? Well, sure I'm a student of
Kung-Fu, but most wrestlers have some form of fighting art under their belt aside from pure wrestling. I mean Von is a Golden Gloves champ, right? <<Vic nods>> My martial skills are one side of what I can do in the ring. I am a skilled technical wrestler, I can moonsault with the best of them, and brawling? Hell, Drexus WISHES he could mudstomp like me. <<Caliban and Victoria share a round of laughter>>... But, what people expect when they face me is a lot of chinatown razzmattaz and lunacy...but I have more than a black sash under my belt, if you know what I mean..."
[VICTORIA]: "And what are your plans for Drexus and his challenge, by the way?" [CALIBAN]: "I think when I beat Drexus, one on one, I should get my match against Rick Donovan....the way I want it. That's what I think. Drexus is a skilled wrestler, but I can wrap him around my little finger in a heartbeat. And after I do, I'll be taking that title shot for the Chesapeake Bay Title. I'm already one of the top ranked wrestlers in this league after a short stay. And when you look at the MAPW roster, whose is the one name on top? Caliban."
[VICTORIA]: "I caught the finals match of that tournament you were in, Caliban. I think it was way out of line for Rick to do that to your shoulder...I saw it on the backstage monitors, and I was horrified...but what I couldn't understand was your weeklong absence...I saw the footage from the Shady Pines Hospital where you were 'detained'. Any comment on that?"
[CALIBAN]: "Well, the Mid-Atlantic Heads got a look at my old medical files when I had to get my shoulder checked. It had an old note in there from a previous doctor who thought I had some sort of 'fully functioning homicidal disorder' or something...<<Caliban lets this shocking comment roll off as if he were talking about having a headache>>...They let me fight my scheduled match, but after that, and my unusual behavior in the interview, they set up a psychiatric evaluation and short-term 'rest' for me...they thought I wasn't firing on all eight cylinders, if you know what I mean...but I got clearance from my shrink, and was declared fit to wrestle."
[VICTORIA]: "There are some people who say you intimidated your doctor into releasing you...the same way you intimidated your
'alternate' the night you tagged up with Dan Starr..." <<Caliban says nothing...he just smiles>> [CALIBAN]: "Opinions...everyone's got 'em. We about done here, Vic?" [VICTORIA]: "Just one last question...<<Victoria slides her hand across the table and runs it lightly over Caliban's. She looks at him, and one can tell by her honey-sweet voice what she's up to>>...would you like to take me back to my room?"
[CALIBAN]: "Two things." [VICTORIA]: <<still in flirtatious 'come-on' mode>> "And what are they?" [CALIBAN]: "First is the mascara and blush you used to paint that shiner on your face. I can tell a make-up job from the real thing,
Victoria."
<<Victoria visibly shivers, and mutters an "oh damn">> [CALIBAN]: "Second is that I'm sure Von Johnson is waiting at your hotel room with a two-by-four in hand, waiting for me to come through the door first."
[VICTORIA]: <<very miffed>> "It's a tire-iron actually. We figured we'd be able to beat you in a match once you were nice and tenderized..."
<<Caliban grins>> [CALIBAN]: "Not a bad plan...<<Caliban gets up from the table, and smiles in a sarcastically cordial fashion>> it was a lovely dinner,
Victoria. I'll have those exercises sent over to your room later. I meant what I said about Von. If you two work on his legs together, he'll be even better than he is now. You won't have to resort to such desperate measures to win. Be glad I'm leaving you with the check, and not with a broken back. I'm a bastard, but I'm not an evil bastard. Take care. <<Caliban exits the shot, leaving Victoria stewing in her own frustrations as the scene fades to black>>
