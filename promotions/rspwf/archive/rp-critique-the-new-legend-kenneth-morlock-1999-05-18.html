[RP Critique] "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock

18 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The ICW cameras begin to roll. The scene is a barber shop in Memphis Tennessee. It is a small old time barbershop. Inside the barber shop there is an old barber with gray hair and a small white beard. He is standing over a man sitting in a barber chair. The man in the chair is getting his hair cut. There is also another man sitting in another barber chair with a steaming towel over his face. The man with the towel on his face is completely silent. The barber is laughing and carrying on with the man that he is giving the hair cut to. The barber finishes the man and collects seven dollars from him. The barber walks over to the man with the towel on his face and takes it off slowly. The man under the towel is none other than "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock. Kenneth
Morlock is wearing a pair of navy blue Dockers khakies and a pair of white
Tommy gym shoes. He also has on the barbers gown so the hair doesnt get on him.
The barber walks over to him smiling.] [Barber] And how would you like your haircut today son? ["The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock] I want just a regular men's cut. And dont call me son again. You aint never been up in my momma.
[Barber] So sorry, didnt mean to offend you. ["The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock] I know your sorry now I think you should apologize to me. [Barber] Ok, I apologize. ["The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock] Much better. Now could you start cutting already I got places to see and people to do. [The barber begins to cut Kenneth Morlocks hair as Kenneth Morlock looks into the mirror at the camera.]
["The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock] One word came describe the way I feel. Finally!! Thats right Finally! Finally
Shane Diamond has came to realize that I am the hottest and newest sensation in
ICW and he is finally granting me a shot against Mr. Not So Wonderful Chuck
Williams. So on Sunday June 13th it is going to be a big night for "The New
Legend" Kenneth Morlock. Not only will I be making my pay per view debut in ICW but I will also be fighting for the TV, or should I say, WINNING the TV TITLE.
I have proven myself over and over again already in ICW. I took Alex Cote to limits and boundarys that the man has never even seen before and never knew existed. I have given Chuck Williams, Lock-Down after Lock-Down in attempt to make this wanna be punk step in the ring with me. But all he could do was make up excuse after excuse to stay away from me. Well, my friend, at ICW Showcase there will be no more excuses, there will be no more running for you , you little punk. It will just be me and you mano-e-mano, face to face and me kicking your head into the mat and taking the ICW TV title and wearing it the way it deserves to be worn.
[The barber walks right in the way of the camera and Kenneth Morlock and Kenneth Morlock gives the barber a dirty look as the barber scoots out of the way very hurrily.]
["The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock] I promise some people are just STUPID! But anyways, now onto the present tense. Dante Calvaria. We know each other pretty good, from past organizations when we were both just breaking into the biz. Well, my friend a lot has changed since then. Some things good, somethings bad. Like a good, I have become a household name all over the country. Now a bad, you have become a resident loser in ICW. Now another good. I am the hottest and newest sensation in ICW and I have the power to do as I feel. Now another bad. You have turned into a wanna be punk who doesnt deserve to be a part of anything, let alone DI. You see, a few weeks ago I came out and gave your buddy, Kuba Rico a hand in beating Chuck Williams ago. But for you, I aint gonna give you no kind of hand in surviving. I am gonna destroy you from the opening ding till I am standing over you with my hand being raised high in victory. I am gonna dominate you in a way that you will nevr forget. And of course a Lock-Down is inevitable. SO you, my friend, Dante Calvaria, are in for one hell of a beat down at the hands of "The New Legend" Kenneth Morlock. SO I am letting you know now, you had better get prepared real fast because Kenneth Morlock is looking for blood. No more playing around with anyone, now is the time that Kenneth Morlock has been waiting for pounce and take what I want to take and no one to stop me. Oh yes, the world is a very good place. But you all better remember this one small thing...
PRETTY...PLUS...POWER...EQUALS...MEEEE!!! [The camera fades out to black as the barber finishes cutting Kenneth Morlocks hair and Kenneth Morlock gets up and pays the barber.]
