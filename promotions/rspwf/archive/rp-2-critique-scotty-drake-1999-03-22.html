[RP 2 Critique] Scotty Drake

22 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: We open up inside of what looks to be a penthouse bedroom. Around the room, you see a lot of what looks to be expensive furnishings. Straight ahead in front of the camera is an Arcadia door. Both of the doors are open and the wind seems to be blowing the branches on the tree outside. The camera begins to pan forward as we pass a bed, a love-seat, and a coffee table. As we enter the balcony, you notice a man laying on a lounge chair. He is wearing a pair of black board shorts, no top, a phat silver necklace from around his neck, 2 silver hoop earrings through each of his ears, and some black Oakleys. His hair is black and shoulder-length, and dangling freely like string. He notices the camera as he turns his head. He cracks a devilish smirk, and then begins to speak.]
Man - Why, hello Vintage society. Welcome to the home, of the best damn wrestler to ever grace a squared circle. If ya' don't know who that is, than I don't know where you've been for the past 2 years, but I am Scotty
Drake. The man, the myth, the fu- ... the _ONLY_ icon! I've been waiting for my big break, waiting for the right promoter to notice what skills I have. I've been training, saying my prayers and all of that! I even ate a few vitamins ...
but that didn't help any! The only thing that helped me, was the fact that I wanted to be the only man in professional wrestling to kick everyone's asses, in every promotion. I guess the other organizations didn't realize what a commodity I was, and decided to pass up the greatest entertainer on the face of this maggot ridden earth! I've got the skills, the speed, the stamina, the endurance, the knowledge, and the plain out drive to keep me at the top of the
Vee Dubbya Aye. I've watched guys from "Money Driven" Mike Sebastian, right down to "GQ" Elan Desire ... and I'll tell ya', those guys couldn't carry a match to save their children's lives. Me, on the other hand ... I can carry whomever is in the ring with me at the time into one of the greatest matches of all time.
[Drake scratches his chin, and adjusts in his chair.] SD - Starting with the man known as "Sizzling" Scott Sexton. Triple Ess ... the man who proclaims that he doesn't have to say how great he is. This guy came out here, and said " ... why talk the talk, when you can walk the walk?" Well buddy, let me let you in a few things. First off, I can do any damn thing I please. If I want to come out in front of the world, and say what a pu- ... pansy you are, I _WILL_! I _AM_ great, and I can walk any walk that your ass can lay down for me. I will run right over your like a two dollar whore in front of Donald Trumpt!
You claim that noone in this business will leave a mark on it like you will? Well, no sh- ... duh Sherlock. We all remember to wipe our asses after goin' to the bathroom! Let me put it simply for ya' Sexton: Noone will ever be as great as me, noone will ever look as good as me, and Scotty ...
[Drake gives off a devilish grin.] SD - ... noone will ever get the one, two, three on me! [Drake cracks a smirk as wide as the pacific. He then leans his head back as the screen fades out.]
