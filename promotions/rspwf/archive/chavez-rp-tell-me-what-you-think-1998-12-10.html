CHAVEZ RP - TELL ME WHAT YOU THINK

10 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene fades in on a huge WOLF logo against a black back-drop. The camera zooms out to reveal a set. It looks vaguely like the Slamzone set. There is a couple of leather couches and a short table on the set and behind it, there is the WOLF logo. One of the guys sitting on one of the couches is Chuck
Bell. We cannot see the face of the other guy since his head is currently off-camera. Bell is shifting nervously about in his seat as the camera zooms out further to reveal who the other guy is. It's Chavez! The insane, drunken
Native American is wearing his infamous F*** U t-shirt, which has been censored by the cameras, a pair of bell-bottoms, cowboy boots and a buffalo hide sequined jacket. He is also wearing a blue bandanna, neck chokers and about a half ton of gold chains. And yes, he has the WOLF North American
Championship belt around his waist. He looks solemnly at the camera while
Bell adjusts his tie]
"Well, they have chosen yours truely to conduct this very intimate, very personal interview with the man who would like to be known merely as Chavez.
The man who is sitting here right next to me. Well I suppose a little bit of background info would be...appropriate. [adjusting his tie] Well, let's see here. Chavez was born Eduardo Chavez in a Navaho reservation outside Truth or
Consquences in New Mexico. His father was a Mexican coke dealer and his mother was a Native American activist. Well, as a child Chavez had a erm....tough childhood. His father left him and his fifteen......"
[Chavez off-camera] "Sixteen Chuck......." "Sixteen brothers and sisters and his mother were living in a small, unventilated, unheated house with no food and running water and let's see here......Then you left home at fifteen to pursue your Native American heritage? And you never saw your father again is that correct?"
[The camera pans over to Chavez. He looks more disturbed than usual if that's possible. He looks over at Chuck and says]
"Daddy?" [Chuck starts to nervously fumble through his notes] "Uhm, alright let's move swiftly on. So when did you, uhm, decide to pursue a career in pro wrestling? And when did you get your start?"
[Chavez ponders the question as he reaches into his pocket. He finds nothing then curses under his breath]
"I need a drink. But back to your question, Chucky. I got started in the...Well I shouldn't say the name because then some of the young buffalo will think I got this here [pointing at the championship belt] because I was in the.....Well you know THAT fed.....But then I moved on to the ICW where I perfected the dreaded Navaho Death Grip......"
[Cut to a clip of Chavez taking Ravishing Rob Ray down with the Navaho Death
Grip and getting a quick win and the #1 contender spot for the ICW Foreign
Nations title. Then back to the studio......"
"And Chucky, the rest is, as they say, history......" "And then you joined WOLF. And you are currently unbeaten in active competition in WOLF. And you are the North American champion. Chavez, you've been on a roll lately......"
[Chavez is smiling from ear to ear but he still looks like a maniac] "Well, I couldn't disagree with that statement Chucky. But you know what? There is something that has been bothering Chavez lately.....But I'll get back to that!" "Well, the question that everybody has been asking, who came up with the
Chavez character? Did it come from you?"
"Character? Chucky, what you are looking at right here is no [making quotation signs with his fingers] gimmick. This is no character. Baby, this is the real thing. What you see is what you get. Next question....."
"Well I was wondering about - " "You know what really bothers me Chucky? You know what really jerks my chain? You know what that is Chucky? Huh Chucky?" [Chuck looks really nervous right now] "What's that Eddie? Can I call you Eddie?" "I need a drink! It says in my contract, Chucky, I'm a raging alcoholic and I need to be provided with a sufficient quantity of hard liqour whenever I'm on
WOLF TV........" "You mean.....that's not part of your gimmick?" [At this point Chavez looks like he might go postal. Then he seems to cool down. He sits back in his chair looking normal. Moments pass by. Chuck looks relieved. Then he goes postal anyway. He leaps out of his chair, slaps the
Navaho Death Grip on Chuck Bell]
"Ahhmmmmffffff helpppfffff me!" [Stage hands are standing by but they dare not go anywhere near the two of them. Chavez finally let's go of the submission hold]
"Chucky? Don't piss me off Chucky......" [Chuck pulls himself off the floor and makes a run for it. Likewise, so does everybody else who is around. Chavez sits down in the middle of the set then smiles into the closest camera]
"Look mommy. I'm on television!" [Fade to black....] -- Can u smell what da DAWG is cooking? :P -----------== Posted via Deja News, The Discussion Network ==---------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Discuss, or Start Your Own
