Icicle Sample RP

2 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is Icicle's Sample RP (The scene opens in the middle of Manhattan. You see hundreds of people running across the streets. Cars pulling up, and people saying the F word in background. The camera turns to the big screen in Times Square. Where someone is about to commit suicide, and people are shouting "JUMP." Then the camera turns back around, and a dark blue limo pulls up to the sidewalk. On the front liscense plate is reads "ICICLE", and below it in smaller letters it reads
"Next FSW Champ." Then a chaufeur comes out of the car, and opens the door.
Icicle steps out of the car. He sees the camera man there, and walks up to him. Icicle is wearing a blue tuxedo with a bow tie.)
Icicle: So It's been 24 hours, and I haven't heard anything from Stewart. So I guess he took the cowardly road. Yes he was smart, but cowardly none the less.
Doesn't really matter. I don't really care who I have to face. OR when I have to face them. To me it doesn't really matter. Because the song says 5 to
1 and those are the odds of winning anyone has if they step in the ring with me. Now that has been said, and done. I've got to address someone. That fat out of shape sweathog named Barry Soltzer. He comes here, and tries to build himself a fantasy. That he has girls crawling all over him.
NEWSFLASH!!!!!!!!!!!!! Soltzer. The Only thing you've got crawling all over you is ants. You say the girls call you Dr. Gyrate. Well maybe that was the nickname your mom gave you, but it don't cut it around here. From now on Barry
Soltzer your nickname is Dr. Blubber. You say your gonna steamroll the competition. Yeah you might be able to steamroll a lot of the competition here in the FSW, but there is no way in hell that you can beat me. You my friend on your best day couldn't beat me on my worst. I hope they serve Twinkies in the hospital boy because that is exactly where your going. Let me just ask a lady here what she thinks of you.
(A beautiful blonde woman walks up to him.) Icicle: What is your opinion of Barry Soltzer Dr. Blubber as he is more commonly known. Woman: I went out with him one time it was the worst night of my life. He smelt worse then limburger cheese. Then when he went to kiss me he pushed me against the wall, and crushed me breaking almost every bone in my body.
The Handler of the Single Most Hated Stable in all of Wrestling ICE Incredible Champions Extreme
