[RP] "SuperStar" Sean Copeland, please critique

16 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens up into a Dominos Pizza shop. It seems to be about 12:00 AM from the clock. The cameraman watches the pizzaman make the pizza. Then you hear a slam of a door as the cameraman turns around and a white '99 Honda Civic is there as out from the car walks "SuperStar" Sean Copeland and "Sensatonal"
Jeff Montgomery walk out of the car and into the the Pizza shop and they sit down on a counter. "SuperStar" Sean Copeland is wearing some long light brown khakis, a white undershit, his loop ear ring on his left ear, some casual brown shoes, and a blue, green, and yellow tight plad button up shirt. "Sensational"
Jeff Montgomery is wearing blue jeans, a white undershirt, some nike shoes, and a LA Lakers hat.]
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Hey yo, padre, get us a jumbo pizza with Peperoni, sausage, canadian bacon, and extra cheese, hehe. I'm a little hungry after that party. Whadya say, Jeff?
"Sensational" Jeff Montgomery: Hellz yeah man. But I'm tired as hell. Lets finish this pizza and get the hell out of here.
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Fine, fine, fine! I have a good time with Kristi and
Jamie. Those girls are gorgious!!!
"Sensational" Jeff Montgomery: Yep, you were in that back room for awhile. But you know Heather and Fracine were fine as hell too man. I had a good time with them.
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: You got with Heather!! Thats tight. She's sooooooooo fine. I wish I could of gotten on her but I had my hands full, hehe!! Did you get into the closet with them?
"Sensational" Jeff Montgomery: Dammit, I wish I could have but they that stupid hoe, Jon, who was doing the drawing, didn't pick my name!!! Is really sucks?
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Ya it does.......................... Pizzaman: Your orders ready. Pick it up. "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Don't you ever interrupt me again in front of a mic. Just for that!! ["SuperStar" Sean Copeland gets up and hits the Pizza man in the groin then follows with a chair shot and a off the table leg drop on the back of the
Pizzaman's head. The pizzaman seems to be unconsious as "SuperStar" Sean
Copeland comes back to his seat, taking hihs pizza with him.] "Sensational" Jeff Montgomery: Nice job man. Now to me, right there, that proves that your going to beat the living crap out of "Gemini" Todd Phelps.
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: [While munching on his pizza] Of course!! Anywayz, while we are on the subject, I guess the camerman and the whole IWA wants me to talk about this Gemini character. Anywayz, I heard him talking about his matchup on that mini jobber card, Shakin da Foundation. He kept saying how's he's not looking ahead at me on Monday Night Magnificant and will be keeping an eye out on Wonder but not me. Right there is a big mistake man because i'm the living legend. I put the W in wrestling I'll put a 1 in your L collumn of your record because that is all I do man. I'm used to getting picked around in high school because I was tall and clumsy but I matured over the time and this man near me was to thank for all this. Now I'm muscular as hell [flexes], really good looking, and can wrestle like a mother ucker, with a F in front of the u.
"Sensational" Jeff Montgomery: Hellz yeah man!!! Hey, I was reading the IWA bulliten this morning and I was looking at the IWA World Heavywieght Title
Tournament and your in it. Your first round opponent is James "Simply The Best"
Pillow!!! "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: James "Simply The Best" Pillow. This man could be
Simply the Best but I'm EASILY THE GREATEST!! I've really never watched him wrestle nor do I care because he's a man that I really don't look for in wrestler. He's a man that just slips my mind but I guess I can't now because
I'm fighting in a heavywieght tournament and that is something. But Pillow, I can worry about you later because I"m into my match against Mr. Tod Phelps and really that is all I could worry about.
"Sensational" Jeff Montgomery: Yep, Todd, just back out while you can because this man near me is what Hardcore is all about. He's used to jumping off roofs of houses. Thats what kinda Hardcore training I gave this man. This man puts the H in Hardcore and E in Extreme and when I say this I mean it because I know
Hardcore and I know Extreme and I know just plain old techinical and mat wrestling. And I wrestled with the greatest so I am teaching this man as much as I know and I know great skills.
[The pizzaman starts to get up but Sensational Jeff Montgomery kicks him down with his feet then DDTs him onto a chair on the ground as the pizzaman lays is down and out.]
"Sensational" Jeff Montgomery: And stay down fool!!! Anywayz, I only manage people that I know will have talent and this man does!!!
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Thanks Jeff, but Phelps, just watch out because
Copeland has been let off his leash. Phelps, I am da high priced ticket. I am da ratings buster. I am da main eventer not some mid carder like you. And
Phelps, I will show you why. And James Pillow, the man I know nothing about. I here your some sort of a great thing in wrestling but after I handle you in the ring and give you the fight of your life I wish show everyone why I'm the god of wrestling, the future of wrestling, and the icon of wrestling.
["Sensational" Jeff Montgomery picks up the pizzaman as "SuperStar" Sean Copeland slaps him a few times, grabs the pizza, and leaves as the camera fades to black.]
