'; `'ท::: ::::`'/` `'ท;:/ Fuck My Enemys, Fuck My Foes Scene: Your television fades into a commerical, you see "Premium" Taj Nyquest walking a long dark corridor, he looks behind his back once and then continues walking he walks through two glass doors and then vanishes behind the wall to the left of him. The camera-man continues to walk out through the doors as he takes his first step outside of the arena Taj Nyquest appears again and pushes the camera out of his face and then the camera falls to the ground. It then fuzzes to black and the words "Be Premium, Just Like Me" appear on the screen in big bold white letters. You then see two black t-shirt's on a table one up and one face down and the front reads "Fuck Off" and on the back you see the words "Premium" Taj Nyquest..And bellow it "God it's good to be premium" and the words "Calvary Wrestling Alliance Attitude". Then scene then returns inside of a CWA arena and you see "Premium" Taj Nyquest standing in the center of the ring a microphone up to his mouth and he looks around the arena. To his right and then to his left. He then begins circling the ring. He thin scratches his chin and then walks to the center of the ring holds his left hand up and then his right hand brings the microphone up to his mouth as he begins to speak.
"The Premium One" Taj Nyquest: I don't know...what you people think of me. I don't know what you people think I can acomplish, I simply don't know what I am in the Calvary Wrestling Alliance. But what I do know is that I am Premium. I know that to be premium you must be the best, Premium is simply defined as..Someone who exceeds another. Someone who is superior in quality. And you know, as I look back in my past wrestling federations, I see myself defeating many great names in the sport of proffesional wrestling yet I do not see myself being ruined, wrecked, annihilated, killed, lost, devastated, wasted, done away with, abolished, demolished, or overturned. No, none of this has happened to "The Premium One" no, I have only done this to my fellow opponents in the ring. They have never had the will to do something like this to me, for they know if they intrude on the "Premium One's" territory they will be ruined.
Their reputation and dignity will be stripped away from them like the snap of my fingers. The snap of my fingers could be the snap of your neck. Tell me I suck, tell me I can't do it, tell me my goals are abnormaly wrong, tell me I am the worst talent you have ever seen. I will tell you to "Watch." Watch how I prove my mad skills in the ring to the entire world. To the entire Calvary
Wrestling Alliance and beyond. I am pit in the ring against two men you might actualy do good in the CwA...Well, one might have a future, the other...has a past and a bright future. The first, Dime Johnson..A man know one quite knows, just..came out of nowhere. He calls himself the "Punks Champion" I call him the
"Punk". He is a punk and that is all there is to it. Calling himself the ultimate fighter, calling himself the best wrestler to set foot on the earth.
Well you got your head messed up Dime, cause the man who is talking to you at this very momment is the best wrestler to ever set foot in a wrestling ring.
And he is no punk..he is simply..."Premium". He is the only man who can talk in third person and be good at it..See I am doing it now. ::chuckle:: Now you see, Dime Johnson isn't what you would call a "Talented Wrestler" this kid is just a brawler. He will go into the ring and punch you up and down a couple times, but while the match moves on he will become weakened and vulnerable. And that is my chance..my chance to take him out using skills. Not just simple brawling but wrestling tactics and skills. I will have him on the mat crying out in agonizing pain hoping and wishing he gets out of the ring. Dime Johnson you are no obstical, you are no friend of mine..and frankly you do not belong in my house. My house is the CwA, My whole life in wrestling evolves around the
CwA and you need not be a part of it. You must face your greatest fear imaginable. You must face me. I will leave it at that Dime Johnson, for I have yet another who needs to be reckoned with. Now, who I consider "The Man." a all-around great wrestler, Dr. Death. Everyone knows about our past together, but no one knows or can know the future until the future occurs. What is Dr.
Death's future? Is his future to win the television title in the CWA? Is it to beat me? Or is it to simply lose to me. All those questions are asked, and only one will be answered. I don't know how I can devise a plan to destroy Dr.
Death, there is no planning with this man. He can not be dealt with easily. But he can not be placed in my mind as a great wrestler for I must face this man and must think of myself as the best, why then do I continue to ponder upon the fact that he just might be great? And that he just might be able to defeat me?
Is it because I am scared of this man? Is it because I don't feel I need to hurt him? Is it because everyone has respect for him? I do not know. But what I do know is simple, I will not lay down for this man, I will not give up at any one time. You can call me Jack, and no matter how high that beanstalk is, I will climb it and reign supreme in the Calvary Wrestling Alliance, come hell or high water. The CwA needs me, the action tends to strive off of me. I don't know if it is my love for war and blood. I don't know if it is my will to win, or simply my talent. I think it is the talent though, for I have destroyed the best, whether it be Tj Masters, Bullet, or even Dave Sanders destroy them I have, and reign supreme I shall continue to do so. I believe the CwA is my calling, my common-place in the world of wrestling. I know walk upon the hardcore zone of Dr. Death, trying to stay adept trying to not let him see my frailty's. For if he sees any of these, I know that he will strike me down. He will strike with hells power backing him. But will the Infamous Antidote be his key to sucess? Or will I block him from using such tactics against me? Only time will tell before I know that I am the best. And now I leave u.
