I'd like a critique of a character I've been thinking of making

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Simply put, he's the Devil. Lucifer come to the world of pro wrestling because he sees so much opportunity for his "business to pick up", if
I may quote Jim Ross. He wouldn't be red with horns and a pitch fork, weilding all sorts of black magic; If anything, I see him as a nice combination of AL Pacino from "The Devil's Advocate", John Glover's portrayal from the TV series "Brimstone", and just enough Jake Roberts to make him truly sinister. Supremely confident in his role as a corruptor, he lets his silver tongue, movie star looks, and ability to convincingly rationalize any action - no matter how atrocious - do most of his work, playing all sides equally well. If he finds his latest source of amusement resistant to his persuasion, he takes it to the ring. That's where his true, demonic self comes out, relentlessly beating them into a boody pulp and smiling wickedly as he paints the canvas red with their blood. To that extent, wins and losses aren't that important, as long as he ultimately gets what he wants. He can still be foiled, because the belief in his own superiority sometimes causes him to underestimate humans. However, woe unto those who can beat him at his game ... because the Devil never forgets.
The only downside to this kind of character is the tremendous amount of RPing involved to make him work. Both on the part of myself and my opponent, and more for the continuation of the angle than for any actual match we may have. I think I could pull it off, but I'd need a good fed to do it in. Therefore, if any fed-heads read this and would like to help me give this thing a whirl, let me know.
Thanks.
