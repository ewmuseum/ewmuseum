*Free Agent/Rookie* Clement Tang

26 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Time: 3:26 PM Location: A kitchen in Titusville, Florida [The camera fades into a black and white tiled kitchen. There is a bowl of oranges on the wooden table in the middle of the room. A red haired, freckled-faced young kid is sitting on the counter. He is wearing jeans and a white T-shirt. Orange groves can be seen through the two kitchen windows. The kid begins to speak.]
Clement Tang - Hello, wrestling world. My name is Clement Tang. I know that none of you have ever heard of me before. I just got out of training. And now, I'm looking for a fed that will be able to showcase my talent. As you can see, I am not a very big guy. But, I can wrestle. Boy, can I! Dang it, if I was just a lil' bit bigger, I could really have been good, but now, I am forced to become a cruiserweight wrestler. I came into this wanting to throw people around, but when I found out I couldn't bench press my own weight, well, I had to refine my style. My trainer said that fed heads are always looking for new cruiserweight talent. And now, I'm it. I can't wait to show off my stuff. I can really fly. It's like I was born an air plane or somethin' like that! I'm really willing to work hard and beef up your cruiserweight ranks. So if you give me ring down here in Florida, I'm sure my mom'll let me come up to wrestle for ya! I gotta go, it's just about time me to pick some more oranges.
[Clement Tang grabs an orange and a little basket to and rushes out the back door. The camera goes over to the window and watches Tang run back into the orange groves. As he disappears down the long lines of orange trees, the camera fades to black,]
