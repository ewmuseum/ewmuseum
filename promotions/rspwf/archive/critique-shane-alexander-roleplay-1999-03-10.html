[Critique] Shane Alexander Roleplay...

10 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWFers, I have not posted a roleplay in a while, so please tell me what you think of my latest work. I hope you like it. Well here it is...

A deep voice over begins to be heard.] VO - In a match which saw some of the most extreme action that the CROW has seen in a long time, we also saw a match practically get changed from a one on one to a three way dance. This third man came in and dominated the match. [the screen shows the masked man nailing Levi Ash with a few chair shots to the head. It also shows the masked man nailing Shawn Jamison with his own move, "the Brainscrambler."] But then this man took the match in a whole new direction, this direction was a _DEADLY_ one. He began to beat Levi Ash up the rampway and then as he nailed Levi with a DDT, he got a microphone, he began to preach about innovating the CROW. Then he unmasked himself and revealed to the world that he was indeed... ... "Sadistic" Shane Alexander!!! [the screen shows a shot of the masked man unmasking himself.] After unmasking himself, Shane poured a liquid on top of a table that was descended 20 feet below the rampway. Then lit the table on fire, then he did the unthinkable. He threw Levi Ash through the flaming table. [the screen shows Shane throwing the bloodied Levi Ash off the rampway and through the flaming table.] Only one type of man could do something like this, that is a man that has... [two words illuminate a now black screen, the words are in red lettering.] __ _ _____ _ | \| | ___ | _ \___ _ __ ___ ___ _ __ ___ ___ | | | . ` |/ _ \ | |_) / _ \ '_ ` _ \ / _ \| '__/ __/ _ \ |_| | |\ | (_) | | _ < __/ | | | | | (_) | | \__ \ __/ _ |_| \_|\___/ |_| \_\___|_| |_| |_|\___/|_| |___/___| |_| [the screen then switches to a shot of Shane Alexander standing in an aisle in a local hardware store. Shane is wearing a pair of old ripped, black jeans and a shirt that has the words, "_UCK OFF" on it. Shane's hair is hanging in front of his face and the camera can not see his facial expressions behind it. Shane has a small orange carriage next to him with some things already in the cariage. Some things that are visible are pieces of long wood, a hose, and a kitchen sink. The camera man begins to ask Shane some questions.] CM - Hello Shane, I was wondering if it would be okay if I got some words from you on your actions at Bring Da House Down and your match comming up against Levi Ash at this week's Bring Da House Down? [Shane turns to the camera man and looks at him with a cold and pissed off look, he speaks in a low voice.] SSA - Who the hell are you? I have never been interviewed. I am my own intervier. Been that way for about a year now. But then again, I have not had a title since I have been in the business, so it looks like there is always room for change. [Shane begins to walk down the aisle, and the camera man follows, speaking while walking.] CM - Well then, I guess we will start off with what happened at Bring Da House Down in the CROW Extreme Title match-up between Levi Ash and the champion, Shawn Jamison. Why did you come out during the match and interupt the match? [Shane stops in the middlw of an aisle that has ll types of nails and thumbtacks. He speaks as he grabs an assortment of nails and places them in the carriage.] SSA - Well, the main reason for my interuption was to show the world that I am back in the CROW, and to show that no matter who the best is in the extreme division, I will beat them. Levi, you have been hand picked by me. I wanted to fight you simply because me and you have tried to go down these roads many times. We have been scheduled to fight many times, but we never ended up fighting, for many reasons. Well this time Levi, I made sure that me and you were going to fight, and not only to find out who is the better man but to find out who is the most EXTREME man in the CROW. So you better shine up that belt because when it is presented to me, I will be happy for the first time in 13 years. [Shane walks down another aisle and sees a place that sells long chains, Shane grabs a chain that is about 8 feet in length. The chain is pretty thick and is placed in the carriage.] CM - That explains a lot Shane. But another question that pops up in everybody's mind is, what is the rules in a Sadistic Strap Match? It sounds like a match that has never happened before. What is it? SSA - Now, I told you at the CROW Awards Banquet that I was to face someone in this match, but I never said that I would release the exact stipulations in this match. I will tell you some of the stipulations however. The first thing is that pinfalls count anywhere, there are no disqualifications, and no countouts. Weapons are legal, and Levi Ash and myself will be strapped together, in a way that has never been imagined. I can not wait for this match. It will truely be one of the bloodiest match in the history of wrestling. Why? Because I am the... ... _INNOVATOR_ ... ... OF ... ... _PAIN_ ...
