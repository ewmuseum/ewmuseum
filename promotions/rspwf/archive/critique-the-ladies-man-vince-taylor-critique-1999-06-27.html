[Critique] "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor [Critique]

27 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: West Keys, Florida. Time: 6:30 P.M. The camera opens the view that is seen is beautiful. Endless water sparkling water, and many different yachts.
The sun is beating down on them, and the camera focuses in on a specific yacht that catches the eye. The boat is white, with a sleek gold stripe running down the side of it. It is incredibly glean, causing it to appear glossy. The camera moves onto it and right up toward a man that is sitting back in a lounge, enjoying the rays the sun is putting out. The black man in front is none other than "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor. . .]
[Vince has a baggy pair of black mesh shorts that come down to around his knees. He has no shirt on, exposing not only his cocoa brown skin but his exquisitely built chest. His trademark Rayban sunglasses are on his face. The diamond stud earring in his left ear gleams. He smiles and begins to speak. .
.] VINCE TAYLOR: Well "The Ladies Man" has resurfaced on American soil once again. After taking my short hiatus from the wrestling world to go out and see the world with some women, I've returned. But not without a path. You see there is only one thing that has made me come back from my cruises around the world.
It's wrestling, and to be more specific, the APWI. American Productions decided that they want to get into the wrestling business. Being a mega corporation, they had the cash, but all they needed was talent. So they went out and recruited the best wrestlers money could buy. And seeing that they snagged me, they accomplished their goal.
[Vince sits up and removes his glasses. He twirls them in his right hand and continues. . .]
VINCE TAYLOR: So after they waved all those bills in my face and showed me some of the men that will be bidding against me in the promotion, I made my return. And what am I greated by? [Waves his arms around.] This great land that I left is the first thing. But the other is this... I'm given a match within two weeks of my return. Now I know that most people would complain about this, saying they needed time to train or get ready. But guess what, I'm not most people. You see I've been staying in shape, preparing for this time to come. You think I would just go away and forget about this beautiful body?
No, no, no, think again. So for all of you wrestlers in the APWI that were hoping that Vince Taylor would lose a step in his absence, dream on.
But on to more important things, like who I'll be facing in my return match. Well it seems that I don't get to beat just one person at APWI's first card, but I get to beat the nine other APWI competitors in a battle royal. The stakes are big for a first card. The four best finishers go into a tournament for the World Title. While the fifth and sixth finishers battle it out for the
Television Titles. And you know where "The Ladies Man"'s gonna end up? Yep, you guessed it, in that tournament for the World Title.
[Vince pauses, as if collecting his thoughts. He then stares deep into the camera and continues. . .]
VINCE TAYLOR: Now don't get me wrong, I'm not saying that the competition in this battle royal won't be stiff, I'm just saying that none of these men that will face me can measure up to my ability. You've got guys who are trying to come out and quickly make names for themselve. Mark Winters, Jeff Dallas,
Rupert Amoruso and Dylan Ryder are all the epitome of this. Well maybe one of you rookies will get lucky and make it into the tournament, but just forget about winning the battle royal. Because winning it would mean you'd beat me, and that's just out of the questions.
Then you have your veterans, guys that have made it big and are trying to stay on top. "Keen" Kurt Orr, in my opinion overrated, but I've seen him around.
Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin. Supposedly he's violent, but violence is nothing compared to a good wrestling, which is what I am. Then "Showtime" Shawn
Sylkk... he's successful, and pretty damn good. But after sitting back and watching him run his mouth for the last year or so, I'm sick of it. At the battle royal I'm gonna be gunning for you Sylkk, and I'm gonna prove that I am the APWI's true "Ladies Man", and the foremost African American sports entertainer. You better watch yourself Sylkk, because before you know it you'll be thrown over the top ropes by the man that is going to becom the first ever APWI World Heavyweight Champion...
THE... LADIES... MAN!! [Vince lays back down and once again starts to soak up the sun's rays. . .] [Fade to black.] -[name withheld]- "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor [APWI] [0-0-0] [ICW] [0-0-0]
