A RP by Stevie Franklin, if you have comments please tell me.

17 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Wolfpac music hits the PA and out walks Buff Bagwell. He has on his nWo
Red and Black overalls, and the red Wolfpac t-shirt on underneath. He goes to the ring and walks to Ross.)
Jim: Buff, I heard Goldbergs interview and I know that you have something to say. (Buff grabs the mic.) Buff: J.R. get your fat a$$ out of the ring.(Ross leaves.) Goldberg you say that I am the stepping stone, well look here dumb a$$. I am already in the
Pac, and you will sure as h*ll not run over me. You have the spear and you will never in your life have the chance to put it on me. I will snap your big a$$ in my Blockbuster so fast that all you will see is Red and Black. If you think that you even have the qualifications to join the Pac, you are sadly mistaken. To be in the Pac, you have to be able to do what it takes when asked to do it. And frankly Goldberg you will never have what it takes. The Pac is elite, not DX, not the Flock but the Pac is elite. If you wanna join up with jobbers, because that is all you are qualified for well then go talk to Triple
H. Now Goldberg you will be my stepping stone and you will become my b*tch when we step in the ring. Now Steve I will get my hands on you pretty soon. And once I do it will be all fun for me and none for you. But Triple H I got a friend that wants to talk to you, let me introduce to you the next European
Champion, Stevie "The Crippler" Franklin!!!
(The Wolfpac's music blares over the PA and out walks Stevie. He has on his black nWo wrestling tights, and he has on his Red and Black t-shirt. He walks in the ring while getting a standing ovation from the crowd.)
Buff: Stevie it is your time to speak. Stevie: Buff, I thank you for getting rid of old fat a$$ Ross, all he can do is ask the same question over and over. Now onto somebody who needs his a$$ kicked, and that somebody is Triple H. Hunter you know that now the Wolfpac has a title that DX used to own. When you brought your a$$ down to the match, you were asking for DX to lose that belt. So we answered and we made sure that
DX lost it. Austin got the best of you, Rod,Sabu, and especially Crazy Craig.
I told you, run with the Pac or get beaten by the Pac. We showed you and the rest of the WRF how a team works. I got all I needed in so I passed a little on to Buff, he got what he needed so he gave some to Austin, and Austin finished the rest off. Hunter like I said you got a nice shot in on me on
Wenesday but the Pac still one. And that is the way it is going to be this
Saturday, you can get a nice shot in, but you will never win, because the Pac is on top and we will stay that way. Triple H since you agreed to have one man or b*tch out their, I will tell you right now you can bring out Steve, or Chyna but the Pac will always have a stronger member. Look at the Pac, I could bring out Buff, and we all know that he could kick your a$$ and anyone else's in DX.
Now I could go with Big Sexy because we all know that he is the biggest and strongest in the WRF. I could bring out the Undertaker who did beat Craigs a$$ last night and took the gold from him. Now there is Curt Henning and excellent wrestler who has beaten many of you before. Then we have Rick Rude, and he proved that he can still take out DX when he helped the Undertaker. And then we have Stone Cold Steve Austin. The man you claim "doesn't trust anybody,"
and the man you claim "only has one move." Well he trusts the Pac and we trust him, and he is the cause for the Taker to have the belt because of his "One
Move." But Triple H your choices plain out SUCK!!! The Icon in his own mind, and the champ who if Buff gets his hands on will never wrestle again. Ryan the
Jobber, who got lucky against Nash. Then you have Crazy Craig who "Dropped the
Ball." You said that anyone who loses to any member of the Pac wasn't worthy of being in DX, well he Lost. Now let's go to that half man half b*tch Chyna.
Oh I better not call her any names the b*tch might get p*ssed at me, and I don't want that. Then you have Skanky, who spreads her legs for a dollar a night. So Hunter you talk about DX being "Elite," show me one person other than Psycho Steve that is worthy of being elite then come talk to me. Now look at the Pac we are elite, we handle business then party like no one else can.
Hunter this Saturday you say that they will need a stretcher for me, well the ambulance will be there for you and the ride will be courtesy of "The
Crippler." H*ll if you think that I won't be able to wrestle again after your match, you need to see a shrink because there is no way in h*lll that you will ever end my career. Hey dumba$$, my muscles are all natural unlike your big b*tch Chyna. Hey maybe those steroids she is taking are making her chin look like Slaughters. I have wasted to much time talking about a pathetic peice of sh*t like you and your big b*tch Chyna. So the bottom line is this, on
Saturday when your gold is up for grabs you will be another victim of "The
Crippler" and I will bring even more gold to the Wolfpac. So Hunter make sure that the belt is nice and polished when you bring it because I want it to shine when it goes around my waist. (Stevie begins to walk out of the ring with Buff, but he comes back in and grabs the mic.)
Stevie: Oh, I will personally make sure that your b*tch Chyna gets to the hospital to see your broke, crippled a$$.
(The Music begins to play and the crowd erupts as Buff and Stevie leave through the crowd.)
