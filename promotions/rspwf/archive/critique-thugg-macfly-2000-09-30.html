(CRITIQUE) Thugg MaCfly

30 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay just tell me what ya'll think of this roleplay. I know there's problems with it and I know what they are but I don't want to tell you what they are I want you to tell me so read on.
(Fades in.) WWWWWHHHHHHHHHHHHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK! (The pop from the crowd in this packed arena is deafening as a big wrestler who is clearly of oriental origin wipes out a white wrestler with a steel chair. Instead of ropes there is barb wire, the apron around the ring is black with something written in red
Japanese writing on it, a good majority of the crowd look to be
Japanese, scattered in and around the ring there are chairs, broken glass, thumb tacks, a bed of barb wire, a baseball bat with you[guessed it] barb wire coiled around it and other various bits of debris and goodies as we've joined what looks like a brutal battle nearing it's end. The Japanese wrestler who is wearing your normal light blue wrestling trunks scrapes the white guy who is wearing a pair of jeans instead of orthodox wrestling tights up from the blood stained canvas. Both wrestlers are around the same height but the Japanese guy looks a lot bigger built and could probably eat your fat ass lazy uncle bob for breakfast if he wanted to. There is a lot of blood on the canvas as both guys look to have bled pretty heavily from various part of there anatomy during the course of this match. The Japanese guy throws the white guy to the outside then tries to follow up with a suicide dive over the barb wire and.....
TTTTTTTTTHHHHHHHHUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUD! ..... the white guys moved out of the way and the Japanese guy has gone crashing into the floor. The white guy gets his opponent to his feet, locks him into side head lock and starts to lead to the backstage area of the arena, the crowd booing him as he goes.
When they get to the back the Japanese guy frees himself from the headlock by pushing the white guy face first into a wall. He starts to really lay some punches in on the white guy before scooping him up onto his shoulder. He charges to a nearby concession stand as fast as his tired legs will allow and throws the white guy over the counter. He now climbs onto the counter but before he can do anything the white guy stands up and punches him in a place that is only ever meant to be treated nicely. The white guy climbs up so that both guys are standing on the counter now and the white guy hits a sickening suplex from the counter to the concrete floor. Both men get to there feet at about the same time but the Japanese guy seems the worse for wear as the white guy locks him into another side headlock and once again leads him to a different part of the arena. They are now heading into the parking lot. The white guy starts to run then leaps into the air with the bulldog making the crowd in the arena groan as the
Japanese guys face hits the floor. The White guy gets the japanese guy to his feet then whips him hard into one of the parked cars. He now climbs on to the top of the car and pulls the
Japanese guy up with him then piledrives him from the roof of that car to the floor. The white guy climbs onto the small concrete barrier that at the end of the parking lot like he’s going to do a small turnbuckle type move onto the Japanese guy but the Japanese guy manages to quickly pick himself up from the floor and stick a foot into his opponents groin. First the white guy jolts forward and grunts in pain but then he loses his balance and starts to wobble backwards and he really doesn't want to fall that way because it's a big drop if he does. The crowd start to cheer louder and louder AND IT LOOKS LIKE HE'S ABOUT TO
FALL.... but suddenly there's a stunned silence come over crowd as the Japanese guy has crabbed his opponents hand and stopped him from falling. The crowd being to boo but their mood quickly changes again as the Japanese wrestler lets go and nails a perfect crescent kick to the chin of his opponent and.....
CCCCCCCCCCCCCRRRRRRRRRRRRRRRAAAAAAAAAAAAAAASH! ......THE CROWD GOES WILD AS THE WHITE WRESTLER CRASHES INTO ONE
OF THE CARS PARKED DOWN ON THE LEVEL BELOW! The ref of this match, who is standing next the the Japanese, makes a ten count then raises the hand of the Japanese wrestler in victory and THE
CROWD POPS LIKE MAD. As the crowd continue cheering all we see now is the face of the white wrestler who is still laying flat on his back with blood covering his face. We here a deep voice come over the image we are seeing.)
Voice: That was me three years ago and believe me I aint ever letting that happen to me again!
(Fade to black) (The picture fades back up with that same white wrestler standing in front of an American flag. Instead of white stars on this
American flag the stars are black and it has “DETROIT BABY!” spray painted in black over the stripes. The white wrestler is wearing a white vest, a blue pair of jeans and a red Nike cap which he is wearing backwards. He seems to have changed a bit since the the match he wrestled in the video package we saw earlier. He looks to have built himself up more he noticeably has more meat on his upper body. But the other change that is instantly noticeable is his tattoo's which he didn't have before.
Tattooed around the arm and bicep on his right arm is barb wire while tattooed around the same place on his left arm is a chain and his third tattoo on his right shoulder simply says “THUGG”.)
White man: Well for those of you who don't know me first of all why the hell not and second of all on the Indy circuit I've become known as Thugg MaCfly. That's a name you all better get used to hearing because from this point on I'm gonna be around for a little bit. I'm gonna be around for a little bit because you see there's something I want. What I want is a job, I'm getting sick of wrestling in all those piddly little Indy federation and I want one of them big money contracts you big boys are on. Thing is I know I can run with the big boys so I know I should already be signed to one of them contracts but hey if I've got to come on here like this and alert all you talent scouts and suits that I do still exist then that's what I'll do.
Three years ago I was moving up the ladder and setting the foundations for my career over in Japan but that was all before that match. I wrestled some brutal matches over there and I held my own pretty good in those matches the names of those matches are still a bit of a mystery to me because the Japanese always had this habit of giving the matches names that were so long by the time they'd finished telling me what they were I’d already forgot the start of them but as long as most of them names had the words barb wire, death, bomb and hardcore in them they still seemed to sell to the Japanese audience so that was okay. As much as the names baffled me the rules of those matches never I just went out there and wrestled to the same rules I’d wrestled to since I started wrestling, BEAT THE OTHER GUY TO A BLOODY PULP
BEFORE HE BEATS YOU TO A BLOODY PULP! And I know that match was basically the same as what we'd call a Last Man Standing match only with more barb wire and a longer name and I also know it almost ended my career. After that match I had broken ribs, broken bones and a severe back and neck injury that could have stopped me wrestling ever again but it didn't. I picked myself back up again, I hit the gym again and I climbed back in the ring again and now I'm ready to start climbing that ladder again. Right now
I'm in great shape and I more extreme than I've ever been before.
So what I'm saying to you all now is take notice of me because I'm ready for my career to take off and if you don't take any notice me I’ll just keep coming back until you do, kind like them inside wrestling news reporters who get on your nerves when they follow you into the gym or a restaurant in search of a red hot exclusive to go and put on the Internet. My name is Thugg, my nature is extreme and if you find me opposite the ring to you and
I will knock you down BITCH!
(Thugg MaCfly walks out of shot and the picture fades to black.)
