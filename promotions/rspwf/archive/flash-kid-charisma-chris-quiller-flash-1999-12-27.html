*FLASH* 'KID CHARISMA' CHRIS QUILLER *FLASH*

27 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before you read this, please look at my comment on the BWA. I mean everything I say. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- --- ****************************** 'KID CHARISMA' CHRIS QUILLER ****************************** TO: GPW FROM: 'KC' Chris Quiller <<The scene opens with a shot of complete darkness. A sound can be heard through the black - a familiar 'thump, thump' on a metal floor, much like footsteps. The 'footsteps' continue for several seconds and then stop abruptly. Silence is prominent for a split-second before a new sound occurs in the darkness.>>
BEVAN: WHAT THE HELL?! WHY THE HELL IS IT SO DARK IN HERE?! TURN THE DAMN LIGHTS ON SOMEONE! <<A New York accent shouts out from somewhere in the darkness. It has the familiar ring of Jake Bevan, manager of GPW's 'Kid Charisma'.>>
QUILLER: YEAH, WHY THE HELL AREN'T ANY LIGHTS ON?! HOW THE HELL DO THEY EXPECT ME TO SEE MY DAMN WAY?! <<After a few moments, bright white lights fill the area exposing the two figures of Bevan and Quiller standing at the top of a metal ramp.>>
QUILLER: I thought you'd have had the whole tour-thing sorted out by now
Jake - you've had, what...well, at least a couple of hours to sort it out.
Don't you want the hottest new sensation in GPW to have a proper tour - WITH LIGHTS?! <<Quiller speaks in a harsh Canadian/American voice, challenging his manager. Bevan struggles for an excuse.>>
BEVAN: Sorry KC, like ya' said, I only had a couple of hours and they said they didn't have time to arrange a tour for us. Sorry - but I DID try...
QUILLER: ...but you didn't try enough DID you? I'm new talent, just signed up. Surely they should have some loser employee that could show me around?
Surely...? Do they not know who I am? They mustn't if they think they can just leave me to my own devices?
BEVAN: They DID say however that we could show ourselves around the building and we can go anywhere? How's that?
<<Chris Quiller looks slightly annoyed with his manager's feeble attempt to arrange a tour. He cringes as he replies.>>
QUILLER: I suppose that will have to do. I'm not pleased with this though,
Jake. Remember that your post as my manager is permanently under review.
Don't step out of line or you're out mister. <<With that, the two men begin to look around the expansive arena. The ramp that they are still standing on appears to lead to the ringside of the huge wrestling hall. They look around at the now visible surroundings. At the very top of the ramp is 'The Global View' - a massive screen for projecting images on. Often wrestler's entrance videos are played on this sizeable white canvas, yet the screen is at the moment, blank.>>
<<Next to the top of the ramp is an announcing booth fully equipped with microphones and television monitors. These are the very seats which every week are filled by the two Global Pro-Wrestling play-by-play commentators.>>
<<At the foot of the ramp is the ring - currently naked of it's usual canvas and curtains. It is still a vaguely impressive structure with various wooden platforms and several VERY large springs. The four ringposts stick up vertically at the corners as per usual but no turnbuckles or ropes are present.>>
<<Quiller and Bevan begin to look around at the expansive walls of the arena. The room is filled with row upon row of plastic seating. They all have lost their shine and are not a dull matt shade of blue. Due to the number of seats, the estimated maximum attendance must be in the high tens of thousands.>>
<<Quiller looks toward Bevan and shrugs his strong shoulders.>> QUILLER: I've had enough of this now Jake. I've seen basically all there is to see now. All I need to see now are all these seats, filled with fans, watching the debut match of 'Kid Charisma' Chris Quiller. Let's hope that day will come soon, eh Jake?
BEVAN: I think I get what you're getting at Quiller. I'll see what I can do. QUILLER: You'd better Jake, I don't want to fobbed off with some excuse like today. You'll have more time to sort this out but I want to know soon. Don't fail me again Jake, you remember what I said earlier about your position being under review...?
BEVAN: Uh huh... QUILLER: I wasn't joking. <<With that, Quiller spins around on his heels and walks back through the curtain and off screen. Bevan soon follows quickly.>>
