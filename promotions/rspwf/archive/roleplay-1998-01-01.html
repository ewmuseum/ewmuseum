Roleplay...

1 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
("Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh is again in the locker room where he is sitting with more comments for the Admiral.)
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh: "Admiral! I told you what I wanted, yet I see no response. You seem to have a beef with a lot of people. I am sure that a lot of people don't really like you either. I, for one, am not a fan of yours and I couldn't care less about who is a fan of mine. This sport takes a lot of effort and hard workd in order to get your fair share. I have worked hard at this. I have tried. Now, I am going for the bigger challenges. The Admiral is said to be one of the best in the business. I think that it is time for me to show all of you people out there just what I can do in the ring. Admiral!
You will be the first to find out what I am about. You see, since May, I have gotten a lot of practice with this. It is time to go to the top. You see,
Admiral, I don't like you for the simple fact that you put hard working people down because you think that you are ten times better than they are. It is not necessarily true. Just because people are not well-known, that doesn't mean that they are absolutely no good. I am sure that people agree with you when you say those things, but we are all entitled to our own opinions. I may not care for your opinions, but hey, nobody is perfect. So I say, let's do it.
Let's have a match that will settle the score. Let's have a match that will show my skills. They say that this is the big time? Then, I will make it bigger. I am sure that this sport has seen enough people like myself, but I am one who tends to back up what I say and do what it takes to reach my goals and to get to the top. This is a sport built on blood, sweat, tears, and the hard work and effort of people like me. So why would I challenge somebody with the credentials and the status of Admiral? What would be a better way to become a top star? Admiral thinks that I am just a joke. Maybe I am... Then again, you can ask the people who I have faced in my career and they will tell you differently. I understand that I just came out of the woodwork and that I am just an unknown. I should at least get my shot to prove myself in the major circuits of the wrestling business. If I become a star, good. If I don't, I can always try again. I can tell you one thing Admiral. I will fight with all that I have and do what it takes in this match up in order to beat you. You may have certain advantages, but I will overcome them. You may beat me around, but I will come back and take more pain. You may humiliate me, but I will make you respect me. You see, Admiral, this is no longer a game. This is a reality. If you beat me, you won't shatter any dreams. I am ready for a beating. The question is... Are you? You can think of all of the possibilities of what you may do and what might just happen. While you are thinking, ask yourself a couple of questions. Will you win this match? More importantly, will you survive? That is the question that you should be focusing on right now. I have felt pain at it's highest forms. I still came back, and I beat my opponents. I have been humiliated. I have lost. Yeah, sure, they were bias losses, but they were blemishes on my record. Now, I face you. Do you think that you will make me submit? It is very unlikely that I will give up in a match. My tolerance for pain is so high that there is just no use in trying to beat me that way. Pinfalls... Well, you will have to knock me into a coma in order to pin me. You see, Admiral, I am more than you think. This isn't some eight foot tall monster talking and doing crazy stuff.
This is someone who is five feet, nine inches tall. I weigh in at one hundred and ninety pounds. I still do what I have to do to win. I may be small, but I am determined. I am pumped and ready for any match at any time. You have to wonder why I am so persistent about this. Oh well, that can be left to your imagination. I want the respect that I deserve. Is that too much to ask for?
You just remember that I am the 'Human Torture Rack' Ian Walsh. I will inflict pain onto your body. I will be the one that tortures you. Win if you can!
Survive if I let you!" (Camera fades to black.)
