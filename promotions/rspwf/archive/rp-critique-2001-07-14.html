Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Did this spt a bit ago for my character, "Homicide" Dutch Blackheart. I've been RPing this character fr almost a decade in this fed and that fed. Just wantedto get a sense of what you experts thought of my handiwork. I'm thick skinned, so enjoy yourself...be honest, and hoefully constructive. Give me an idea what the strengths and weaknesses are as well as what could be done to improve it overall!
------------------------------------------------------------------ Scene opens up as a paperboy is seen riding his bike down a familiar street. He stops briefly, checks a scrap of paper, looks to the hosuse he has stopped at, and throws the paper on the porch. The front door opens, and
Jordan Michaels stands...looks curiously at the boy on the bicycle. The boy shrugs hs shoulders, peddles off, leaving Michaels alone on his porch.
MIchaels looks down to a morning paper...a paper he has never subscribed to. He pulls off the rubberband, opens the papers, and leafs through it quickly. On the Forth page he sees something circled in Yellow Highlighter, it is a small column. Looking to the header, he notices he is in the obituary section...the highlighted region simply says, Jordan Michaels....promisiing career comes to a screeching halt in the ring...cause of death:
HOMICIDE. The camera pans back slowly as Michaels drops to his knees, screaming...the viewing audience is greeted to a screen, upon which what they have just seen was broadcast. The screen goes blank leaving a white light on a backdrop...a backdrop that goes black as a man steps i front of it: That man is none other than Dutch Blackheart.
Dutch: A cold wind is blowing through the mHWO..its origins...my heart. Michaels, the day of reckoning is upon you my friend. In mere hours you will face me in and mostly likely out of the ring. YOur prize, the TV title. My friend, you boast of your accomplishments, but they mean nothing to Dutch
Blackheart. YOu see Michaels, I too am undefeated. In my brief stint in the mHWO I have defeated men alone, two at once, and a whole ring full of eager and foolish men who aspire gretness. But you see Jordan,. things like the TV
Title, my record, and even the fans mean absolutely nothing to me. And your pitiful story about lows and depths...it means nothing to me.
YOu speak of pain and anguish..highs and lows, my friend....you have felt nothing yet. For the Valley of Suffering and Pain is without bottom, just as my heart is without pity. YOu seek to impress the fans, impress your family, impress yourself...but you've impressed only one person so far..ME. You've impressed me as a feable, weak, pathetic little man and I plan to show the world that as always, I am a man of my word. YOu broken and bloody body shall be a fitting sacrifice, I shall release you for good from your torment
Michaels. YOu need not worry any longer about dispair, about self-pity, about highs and lows. For I have the answer to all of your lifes questions.
I shall redefine your sense of pain, your sense of agony, your sense of fear, and most assuredly your sense of despair. YOur mortal period of strife and discontent is but an open door, a door to the Valley my friend. The
Valley greets you my friend, and I as its overseer promise you WILL be coming for a one way trip, for surely what is left of you when I am done will be unfit for the mortal realm.
YOu speak of my evil God...my friend, you know nothing...I have no God. Your puny religions, let's understand something...Heaven won't take me and hell can't hold me. I live and exist in a world you cannot comprehend, your small mind will not allow it. But, rest assured, your moment in the spotlight will glean a bit of understanding. Your small glimpse into my world shall leave you insane, a petrified and trembling fool. The rest, words cannot describe the suffering your body, mind, and soul will soon be exposed to. YOur realm is the ring, the lights, the crowd, the cameras. MY realm is one beyond your senses, somewhere in the recesses of your mind, in places so warped and dark you fear to face them. In your sleep you shall see my face, feel my breath, see my smile and know once and for all that your quest for glory was nothing more then a patch to your finals moments. It is a path you walk, but a path
DUTCH BLACKHEART tred....tred it, paved it, and posted the signs. The last sign you will EVER see:
Destination HOMICIDE Arrival time: NOW Prepare your afairs my friend, for I am the Harvester of Soulds, the Bringer of Pain, and He who walks before the Grim Reaper. I do the work, he takes the credit, and in the end we are happy, but surely you shall never be again.
