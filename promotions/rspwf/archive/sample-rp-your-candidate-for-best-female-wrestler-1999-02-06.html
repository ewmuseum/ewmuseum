[SAMPLE RP] Your Candidate For Best FEMALE Wrestler :)

6 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in the Shady Acres Cemetery in the rural, peaceful looking country town of Whitbury, England. It's a damp and dreary Sunday afternoon.
The camera pans around to get shots of the miles and miles of headstones and graves ... before finally coming to a shot of Kieran Erotikka, dressed in a long trenchcoat concealing most of her body, standing before a beautiful marble tombstone. The tombstone reads, "Sarah Jane Spencer-Lane... Born on January
10, 1943 in Wales... Died on December 25, 1992 in London, England" with an epitaph which reads, "Wife - Mother - Friend".]
Kieran Erotikka: [deep sigh, holding the tears back] I'm home now, Mum... for now, at least. I thought I'd come by and see you on your birthday before going on the road.
[Kieran kneels down by the tombstone. She tries to hold back the tears as she speaks. Fortunately, for her sake, she's successful, for now.]
Yeah, I know. I know. It's been awhile since we last got the chance to talk. Alot's happened since we last spent any time together here. Bob's out of my life. He really changed after a while. I couldn't take it anymore. I know
I'd be lying if I said that I didn't missed him ... but I've been well off without him. I have moved on.
[Kieran takes a deep breath and smiles slightly...] To make a really long and complicated story short, I met another guy this past summer. His name is Liam. While he's not what you'd call a "decent chap", he isn't really a bad guy either. At least he's not a bad guy to me.
Unconventional, yes, but not bad. He's been rather good to me so far. I don't see it changing any time soon, either.
[Kieran runs her hand over the lettering on the tombstone gently.] There's been another rather significant change in my life ... and I'm not sure if you'll like it. You weren't terribly pleased when I showed interest in becoming a professional wrestling manager right before you died. I think you'd be downright livid as hell if you knew about this decision that I've made recently.
[Kieran takes another deep breath and smiles.] Well, enough of my babbling brook impression. Here it goes. See, recently, I got this urge inside me to do something with my life ... something worthwhile
... something that I enjoy. As I struggled with this urge burning inside me, I looked around me. I looked at Liam wrestling inside that ring ... and saw me standing their on the sidelines. Then, I looked back in my life ... my life with Bob ... those three years ... and there I was sitting on the sidelines watching _him_ compete in the ring. Never once have I been able to shoot for some glory myself. That is, until now.
[She looks up at the sky ... smiling a half-smile. She looks back down at the tombstone. There's a tear rolling down her cheek but she's still smiling.]
After three years of watching other _guys_ do it themselves, I've become a professional wrestler, Mum. That's right. You heard that one correctly. I know that right about now, if you were still alive, we'd be having quite the row about my decision but this is what I want to do. This is what I feel is right for _me_ at this point in my life.
[Kieran runs her fingers through her dirty blonde hair and shrugs.] I really got sick and bloody tired of sitting on the sidelines like some kind of bubble-headed high school cheerleader while someone else got to enjoy the fame ... the glory ... the cheers and boos of the fans ... and yes, the titles...
[A strong wind blows through the cemetery. Kieran's dirty blonde locks whip in the wind...]
... So I signed with an organization called ICW... International Championship
Wrestling. I've already competed in two matches and I'm undefeated! How about that? I know you hate wrestling but I'm sure this would make you proud. At least, I hope it would.
[Kieran looks around the cemetery and then back at the tombstone. Her body shivers as she continues to speak...]
This Friday night, I'm in for what will probably be the toughest fight in my young career to date. I have to face not one ... but _two_ other women for the
ICW Women's Title... a title that is said to be one of the greatest honors a woman wrestler can achieve in the business. One of my opponents is Florence
Floyd. She's not really a huge worry to me because I already defeated her a week prior to when this event takes place. She's nothing more than some empty-headed, disrespectful little _girl_ who thinks she can get to me by making fun of where I'm from and calling me such original names as "whore" and
"slut".
[Kieran laughs softly ... but quickly composes herself in order to continue speaking...] However, my other opponent is the ICW Women's Champion herself, Miss Jasmine. She's like _the_ woman to beat in ICW. She's had a stronghold on this title since midsummer. She's even defeated _men_ in singles competition. Everyone in the locker room ... as well as the fans who see me on the street ... they tell me that no one can beat Miss Jasmine. I plan to prove them wrong.
[Kieran smiles a warm smile at the tombstone.] If there's one thing that I learned from you, it's that anything can happen as long as I put my mind to it ... and believe in myself. I've carried that advice in my heart ever since you gave it to me. I will be taking that advice with me when I go to possibly achieve greatness at the Richmond Coliseum this
Friday. I can't make any promises to you for I am afraid that I'll let you down. But I can guarantee you ... with every ounce of my being ... that I will give it my all when I step into the ring to face Floyd and Jasmine. I will use every ounce of energy that I have that night in hopes that it will win me the
ICW Women's Title. I will do it in hopes that it will help me achieve something ... something that will finally make you proud of me.
[Kieran stands up ... still looking at the Tombstone ... as the tears begin to fall...] Mum... I know you can't watch me up there. Hell, you never even got to see me as a valet. But, please be rest assured. On Friday, January 15th... I will be stepping into that ring without an ounce of fear in my body and I _will_ be giving one hundred and ten percent and, hopefully, I _will_ be coming out of that match as the NEW ICW WOMEN'S CHAMPION. Mum, this match is dedicated to you.
[Kieran takes a single red rose out of her trenchcoat and places right by the tombstone.] I love you. [Kieran kisses her finger ... and touches the grave with it ... as we fade to black.] -[name withheld] Handler of "The Extreme Goddess" Kieran Erotikka Vote for Kieran and make Sensuous Samantha smile :) LOL!
