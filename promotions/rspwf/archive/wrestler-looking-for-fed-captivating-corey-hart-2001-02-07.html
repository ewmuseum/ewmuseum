(Wrestler Looking For Fed) "Captivating" Corey Hart

7 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi guys. My wrestler currently competes in the JPA and GLCW but I was looking to add another fed, as well. I'm preferably looking for a fed that only requires one rp a week. Anyway, a sample rp is posted below to give you an idea of my work.
It ain't the best. It ain't the worst. It just is.:-) Thanks. =========================================================== (We find ourselves at Mario's Pizza Palace, a small restaurant near the campus of MSU. Seated at a lone table is the GLCW's newest cruiserweight sensation,
"Captivating" Corey Hart, and his valet and best friend, "America's Sweetheart"
Lolita Love. The two lithe youngsters are seated across from one another, a large, vegetarian pizza situated between them. Corey wears a baby blue, turtle neck sweater and baggy, blue, denim jeans, completing the look with tan boots.
His short, curly, blond hair frames his handsome face. Lolita wears a formfitting, pink, cardigan sweater and low-cut, denim jeans, decorated with a sprinkling of rhinestones. She finishes the look with pink and white, Nike, tennis shoes and her long, blonde hair is tied in a ponytail. The GLCW has never seen a cuter pair of wrestlers. Honestly. The dapper duo each grab a glass of soda, raising them high.)
Lolita: (grins) To victory! Corey: (equally cheesy grin) To victory! (They clink their glasses together in a toast before each taking a sip.) Corey: I gotta admit that I never thought I would earn a win over Mikhail
Therbenko. I mean, the guy is friggin' huge! When I first looked across the ring at him, I was so certain that I was going to get my keister kicked.
Lolita: (smiles) Corey, I knew that you could do it. I had complete and utter faith in you. Plus, Therbenko seemed really out of it. It was obvious that he his mind was anywhere but your match. And you definitely had the fans rooting for you which is a big plus. They love that whole "under dog" thing. From the looks of things, you're doing really good in the GLCW. I won't be surprised if they ask you to stay past your little tour of the fed.
Corey: You really think so? (Lolita nods her approval) Thanks, 'Lita, but the challenges have only just started. My next opponent is one of the GLCW's stalwarts and a former champion, Tim Turner. I have a feeling that this one won't go as easy as my match with Therbenko.
Lolita: Hmmmm. Tim Turner is definitely /not/ going to be a cake walk. He beat
Eddie Jacobs at the last American Freakshow.
Corey: (sighs) Don't remind me. I'm already nervous enough. Lolita: Hey, you've got nothing to worry about. Sure, he may have you on experience but I think that you can more than hold your own in the ring. You know, you really shouldn't doubt yourself or your abilities. Just look at how easily you handled Therbenko.
Corey: Yeah, but as you mentioned, he wasn't exactly focused throughout the match. Lolita: So? Who's to say that the outcome would have been different had Therbenko been giving you his complete and undivided attention? Personally, I think you would have kicked his butt either way. Corey, you're a very talented wrestler and you shouldn't let others intimidate you.
Corey: I know. I know. I guess it's the rookie in me. I sometimes find myself intimidated by those that have been in the sport longer and have accomplished more than me. I guess it's just a phase that all rookie's go through. Didn't you feel the same way when you first started wrestling?
Lolita: I suppose. But you should never allow these GLCW wrestlers to scare you. After all, you've got something that they'll never have.
Corey: My speed and agility? Lolita: No, silly! (beams with pride) I'm talking about having me in your corner. Corey: (with a sly grin) Remind me again. Is that supposed to be a good thing or a bad thing?
Lolita: (smiling despite herself) Keep it up, Mister, and Tim Turner will be the least of your worries! Speaking of which, you two have a lot in common. I mean, you've both got that whole gay thing going on.
Corey: (groans) I just knew you were going to go there. Lolita: (in a teasing tone) Hey, during your match, why don't you try to distract him with a little Livin' La Vida Loca? You know, shake your booty a bit, give him a little show....
Corey: (puts his face in his hands) Please, make it stop! Make it stop! Lolita: (With a playful smile) I'm only trying to help. A boy's gotta do what a boy's gotta do to get a win around here. Besides, it's obvious that /I/ can't distract him for you.
Corey: (shakes his head) While I appreciate the advice, I don't think I'm quite ready to start playing "G-string Diva". When I'm facing Turner, "booty" will be the last thing on my mind and I can bet that it's the last thing on his, as well. We're serious athletes and, most importantly, professionals. Although I shudder to think what some of the other wrestlers and fans are going to say when they see the GLCW's two, resident, gay guys go at it. I get enough snickers and strange looks in the locker room as is.
Lolita: Don't worry about what a few morons have to say. Just go out there and do your best. Besides, from the looks of the GLCW roster, you and Tim aren't the only two batting for the other team, if you know what I mean.
Corey: (laughs) "Lita, you are too bad! Lolita: I try. (smiles) Hey, how about another toast. (She lifts her glass) To another victory for the GLCW's newest employee.
Corey: I'll certainly drink to that one! (The camera fades as we hear that familiar clink again.)
