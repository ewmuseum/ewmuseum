2.Brown Pants [BBW begins to narrate a story.] Long ago, when wrestling was a new sport, a wrestler and his valet (Brass
Knuckles and Poison Kiss) were about to wrestle jWo. As the valet became frantic, BK bellowed to his valet, "Bring me my red shirt!"
Poison Kiss quickly retrieved BK's red shirt, which BK put on and then went to wrestle Jonathan Sunset. He suffered some cuts, but won the match.
Later that day, the Valet screamed that next he would have to face Dog with
Rabies. The valet cowered in fear but BK, calm as ever, bellowed, "Bring me my red shirt!"
Once again, the battle was on but he survived with only a few more scrapes than before. Weary from the battles, BK sat around the house that night recounting the day's occurrences when Poison Kiss looked to him.
Poison Kissed asked BK, why he call for his red shirt before the matches? BK's reply was that if he am wounded in the ring, the red shirt does not show the wound and thus he could continue to fight without his opponent knowing he has an advantage.
Poison Kiss sat in silence marveling at the courage of BK. On Hot Spot the following monday Poison Kiss screamed that she saw BBW on his way. And he would be his next opponent. Poison Kiss became silent and looked to
BK, for his usual command. BK was as calm as ever. But this time he said,
"Bring me my brown pants!"]
