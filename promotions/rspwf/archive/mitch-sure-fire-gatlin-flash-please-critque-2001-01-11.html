Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin [Flash] Please Critque

11 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[-The scene opens up in Atlanta, Ga. The camera is focused in on a couple of bums standing around a fire barrel. You can see a few colored guys standing on the corner a little ways down the street. The camera begins to slowly move to the right. You see a brick wall with all kind of tags on it. Then you see all kind of exspensive cars next to the guys on the corner. It appeares that they are shooting dice. The you begin to see a chain linked fence. It appears to be a basketball court.-]
[-The camera zooms in on a man sitting by him self on the bleachers. There is no one else around him. The camera begins to move closer and closer in on him. He is wearing a white hooded sweatshirt.
Snow camouflage pants and a pair of black work boots. He has the hood on his sweatshirt on, so you cannot see his face.-]
Man: "Let me tell you about my self. I started wrestling about four years ago. I was an apprentice of the late great Boltz. He taught me everything he new. From an arm bar take down to a DDT. He taught me. Since he had a good history behind him he managed to get me into a decent fed. It was called the
Extreme Wrestling Council. "My first match was against a man they called The Rebel. He was a good old southern boy. He talked a lot of trash but when it came down to it he just could not cut it. We fought for about 45 min and then he just gave up. You see I am not to big on wrestling. I am more of the brawler type. I will punch you in the jaw before I suplex you. Anyway that is besides the point. I won the E.W.C World championship just one year later. After I won the title I held on to it until the owner of the federation got into something with a loan shark and got killed. The
E.W.C shortly died out after that." "I had no other choice but to move on. I roamed around from place to place. I just could not find anywhere that met my standards. They were all soft and had no heart for the sport. It was all about the money for them. Not for me. I thrive on give and receiving pain. If I come out of a match and there is no blood on me or my opponent
I feel like I have done something all wrong, but I ended up in a small town in Portico. In the P.C.W.
There I started to make a name for myself. I won the
Southern Portican Title. I finally ended up getting sent back to the states because my mother had gotten sick. I came back to the US and I have never made it back to P.C.W. When I got here I joined up with
the A. P. W. A. Were I met up with the guy who taught me everything Boltz. Boltz was getting to be a little over the hill but he put up a hell of a fight. In the long run he just could not hang. I got the pin and ended up having to carry his ass back to the locker room. Boy them were the days."
"Then I got a few aggravated assault charges put on me when I got striped of a title. I got sent to Woodland Hills Correctional Felicity. That place just struck the spark that started the fire. I had to fight to eat, fight to sleep, fight to even take a shit. I just got out two weeks ago and I am still not all the way readjusted. Prison life is a hard life, but it just made me that much tougher, meaner, and gave me a whole new out look on life. I am so used to Fighting to survive I do not know how not to."
[-The man pulls his hood down from over his head and it reveals a dark complicated face. This man has a long black hair that is up in a pony tail. He has a scar right below his right eye. It start's from his lower right cheek and stretches across the right side of his face just missing his eye.-]
Man: "My name is Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin. Some of you may have herd of me and some of you may not have herd of me. It really does not matter to me because you all will get to know me all too well.
That is for sure." "I have looked at the A.A.W.C roster and I see a few impressive names throwed in with a bunch of guys I myself have never herd of. Think about these guys are guying soft. They are used to sleeping in Six hundred dollar hotel rooms. I am used to sleeping on slab of mettle. We did not even have mattresses. These people did not have to live with Drug Dealers, Low Life's, and people who killed people for fun.
"The people in prison will kill you for anything, or try. Look at my face. I got that in prison.
While I was sleeping. Imagine waking up and having blood dripping all over your chest. A guy sitting in the corner rocking back and forth just mumbling to him self. If you are not crazy before you go in you are crazy when you come out. I was crazy before
I went into prison, think of what it has done to me now."
[-Mitch reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pack of Marlboro Lights and black zippo. He lights him a cigarette and continues to talk.-] Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin: "I have seen the A.A.W.C card and I am not on it. Just because I am booked does not mean I am not going to let my _presence_ be known. Everyone will know that _I_ am in the building.
You can Quote me on that." "You like me, love me, or hate me, I don't give a damn but you will respect me. So anyone who wants to Step up and get there knees _dirty_ then come with it! I am not a hard person to find. That is for damn sure."
