[name withheld] E-mail Address: [detail withheld] Back Up E-mail Address: [detail withheld] My wrestlers: Thanatos "the Reaper of Souls" Height: 7' 0" Weight: 420 lbs. Home Town: Parts Unknown Style: Technical/Submissions Alignment: Tweener Theme Music: Guitar solo played by eric Draven in the Crow (I have a .wav file for you if you'd like . . .)
Gimmick: Filled with rage by an unknown event in his mysterious past Thanatos considers himself a righter of wrongs and dispenser of "justice" (or more apropriatly vengeance) to those who, as he puts it "Have blackened, shriveled souls filled only with darkness and hate."
Description: Coming down to the ring in a long black robe with a hood pulled over his face Thanatos is an imposing sight striking fear into the hearts of his opponents, and some of the fans as well. When he enters the ring
(stepping OVER the top rope) he removes the robe to reveal a chiseled form covered by black spandex and a white, skull like mask that compleatly covers his face and head. Quick and agile (for a 420 pounder at least, he doesn't
LUMBER like the Giant but he's obviously no Rey Mysterio Jr.) for his size
Thanatos is more than a powerhouse, he's a ring technition on the par of
Benoit or Malinko. Based on comments he's made in other feds he considers himself a fighter for "justice" and will never harm an innocent, however the heels of the fed often find themselves the targets of rather brutal attacks.
Ten Moves: Suplex (any variation), Reverse DDT, "Reaper's Harvest" (Dimond
Cutter), Bearhug, Power bomb, Torture Rack, Atomic Drop, Nerve Holds (various types), Sleeper variations, "Interment" (this is a brutal move where he grabs his opponent in a face claw, and if they're small enough picks them up and power slams them maintaing the claw the whole time sort of like a choke slam.
If they're too large to be lifted he will sweep their legs.) Set Up for Finisher: Short arm cloths line (set up for Hangmans sleeper),
Reverse DDT (set up for Harvest of Souls), No set up for "Touch of Death"
nerve hold. Finishers: Thanatos has developed three finishers for use in different situations. His favored move is the Hangmans Sleeper, a sleeper where he lifts his opponent off his feet while it is applied. Unfortunately he finds it difficult to lift opponents his size or larger by their necks so he developed the Harvest of Souls. To apply the Harvest of Souls he sends his opponent into the ropes and on the rebound jumps up and delivers a vicious
Bull Dog. Lastly he uses the Touch of Death, a double trapezius nerve hold used when he desires a submission.
Role Play: (The camera opens in the Training Grounds, the dungeon like training center of Thanatos. In the flickering torchlight one can make out the makeshift ring in the center of the room, with in this ring Thanatos and an unknown Mexican wrestler are engaged in a little friendly sparring. The smaller, but quicker and more agile, man spring boards off the bottom rope and lands a brutal drop kick to Thanatos' left leg, Thanatos drops to one knee with a grunt of pain. Thanatos responds by grabbing the smaller man in his arms like a small child and, struggling to his feet, lifts him and delivers a Tilt-a-whirl back breaker that nearly snaps his opponent in half.)
Thanatos: "Did I get it right this time?" (His opponent nods with a grimace of pain on his face. He begins to struggle to his feet for more "sparring" when Thanatos notices the camera man.)
Thanatos: "What are you doing here? This is our private sanctum, none may enter without our permission!"
Camera Man: "I-I th-that is . . ." Thanatos: "Cerubus! Hell Hound!" (Two large dogs lope menacingly out of the shadows and begin growling at the camera man.) CM: "S-sir, I-I brought a note th-that I thought might interest you . . . p-please call off your dogs . . ." Thanatos: "They won't attack with out my command. Give me the note and we'll decide whether or not I give that command . . ."
(The camera man gulps audibly and hands Thanatos a large manila envelope. Thanatos opens it and reads it over.) Thanatos: "It's a new fed. Apparently they want the best, me. Give me a pen, I'll sign." (The camera man hand Thanatos a pen and Thanatos signs his contract. As
Thanatos hands the contract back to the camera man he speaks one last time.)
Thanatos: "Your trespass is forgiven this once. I warn you, do not return here with out permission or Cerubus and Hell Hound will enjoy their new chew toy. And tell the other wrestlers in your fed that . . . THEIR DOOM IS
SEALED!" (Fade to black.) ************************************************************************************ Contractual demands: Posting board or Open Flash system are prefered, Flash packets sent out by the fed head daily are aceptable as well but I need the other handlers RP's to respond to if you expect quality work from me . . .
Please send a roster or a link to one at least. I don't want another fed that allows such gimmicks as "The Amazing Superstar Jobber" who spent most of his flashes talking to an invisable gorrila and breaking Kayfabe. . . and the man WON.
Speaking of which, at least ATTEMPT to pretend this is real wrestling, the anouncers on the card refering to the fed as "E-wrestling" is jarring, so is hearing someone refered to as a "jobber" by someone in charicter.
No "Hard Core" or "X-tream" feds, a hard core belt is one thing but an entire fed devoted to ridiculous "barbed wire-flameing-cage" matches is not something I want to be involved in (guess what nine out of ten feds I found on my own were?)
Two match per month MINIMUM (more is prefered) I don't want to waste my time roleplaying if I'm not even going to have a match that month.
Apearances at at least one third of the pay-per-veiws your fed holds. These are the most important events in a fed, I'm not asking to main event them just be involved even if it's only as guest referee or something.
I'm not asking to be considered the "baddest dude" in the fed or for a world title right off the bat, just a little reward for my work, thank you.
