Alex Martinez Rp...

26 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
well... I've just spent eight or ten hours trying to slog my way through Kant (a rather unsuccessful slogging, I might add) and my brain is sufficiently fried, and my ego is sufficiently battered. So, I thought I would post an rp here. its nothing special, just an rp for
Alex Martinez. I do this periodically, just for the hell of it. People invite me to their feds, other people tell me I suck, most people ignore me, and a generally fun time is had by all.
Btw, don't bother to tell me which real wrestlers I am ripping off, I am well aware of all my "influences" :)
enjoy. "Victory is my destiny... violence is my legacy" [These words, spoken in a familiar whispery, intense voice, come from darkness. They resonate through the black screen for a moment's time, before light is brought into the scene.
The scene itself is nothing spectacular, nothing at all elaborate. Merely a white sound stage, the only prop a red "UEW" banner hanging on the far wall. The scene matters not for its form, but for its content, namely, one
"Black Knight" Alex Martinez, the current UEW Ultimate Champion. The man, who, for better or worse, represents Ultimate E-Wrestling. No longer just the Black Knight, no longer a secondary man who serves a higher power, now
Martinez is the man who stands over the UEW as its Dark Prince... demented, disturbed, and yet... nearly unstoppable. Martinez has chosen black clothing to contrast against the stiflingly sterile white environment: a black leather jacket over a plain black
T-shirt, a pair of dirty black jeans, and black leather boots. The UEW
Ultimate title belt is draped over his shoulder, and it is only the sparkling gold plates of that title belt, resting snugly upon a black leather strap, that breaks the monotony of Martinez's outfit. His dark hair is pulled back into a severe pony tail, revealing a severe widow's
peak that seems to plunge all the way to his nose. Gone are the sunglasses he usually favors, instead, his chocolate brown eyes look out over the world, unhindered by the mirrored lenses. The red scar that runs diagonally across his face, from temple to jaw, seems to be even more prominent than usual.
Arms cross over his chest, and Martinez speaks] Through the years... many have tried to understand me. Many have tried to comprehend my actions, many have attempted to even second guess me...
thinking they might be able to outsmart me. As of yet... none have succeeded, and I hold little doubt that everyone who tries in the future will meet with the same failure.
Bryan Sullivan thought he could, as he put it, claim my soul. He thought that with a few silly tricks, he could weasel his way into my mind, and lay claim to my power. Bryan Sullivan discovered that the inside of my soul is a dark place... and it proved to be more terrifying than he bargained for.
In the end, I became the first and only person to pin Bryan Sullivan in the UEW. Nevermind, for all of his much vaunted ability to manipulate his opponents, found himself constantly playing into my hands... found himself giving me everything I wanted. Mannifred Lester? What happened to the most sadistic force in UEW? What happened to that dark follower of Nod?
He too fell into my web... and when I was done with him, he could not even remember who he was.
There are others of course... Magnus Colby, and Alex Extreme just to name two. People who thought they were finally the one who could put an end to my reign of terror. People who thought they held the advantage over me...
and yet, each of them failed, and wound up seriously injured for their efforts. One by one... they have stood up to me, and one by one, they have each fallen victim to the Living Terror.
But now, they line up again. A new rogues gallery stands arrayed before me. Each of them hoping that they can be the one who finally destroys me.
There are many, as there always are. Many more than I shall ever deal with... because most of them are sheep, many of them are nothing more than opportunists who will never pose a threat to me. At best, they are tiny dogs, sitting out on the porch, barking and begging to be let in from the cold.
And yet... there are three who stand out most prominently. First, there is Jason Keening. The much famed Screaming Drillbit, who steps forward to challenge me. Keening is the first among what I am sure will be many to call me "paper champion." And yet, Keening, like many before him, is very quick to beg off when actually faced with the prospect of fighting me.
Keening... you show your wisdom through your cowardice. You do not want to fight me... you are willing to leave that task to another. That shows a great deal of intelligence on your part. But I can see that your intelligence does not extend far enough. Do not attempt to rile me, do not try and badger me. For the time being, I will let your comments go by, but speak words of that kind again, and you will find that the yellow stripe down your back is a meager shield against my wrath.
[Martinez pauses, and draws a breath. As he does so, he head inclines forward, his ponytail falling over his shoulder. When at last his head raises, there is something extra in his face. The rage is joined by a certain melancholy, almost like mourning.]
And then there is Rick Styles. A man who would fashion himself my mentor. A man who I considered to be my best friend... a man who was more a brother to me than the one of my own blood. Such a shame Rick... I always thought you could be spared my wrath. I always thought that our friendship would be enough to ensure your survival. I even let you take credit for acts you never performed. I stood behind you... crippled your enemies, and swallowed my pride. Because, more than anyone or anything else, I actually believed in you... believed in the goals of the New Alliance. But now
Rick, now you have overplayed your hand. Now you have let your arrogance blind you. If it weren't so funny, it would be tragic. You had the beast at your command, and now, that beast will destroy you... for something you
have done. What is it you have done? The same thing that all others have done... you misunderstood me. You see Rick, all that I ever wanted was to be a part of the New Alliance. I never wished to lead the group, I do not even wish that now, when I am the leader. I was content being your enforcer. I did not step up to the leadership of the NA because I wanted to... I took it because you had left.
I took the position Rick, because you abdicated it. I was comitted to the ideals of the NA, and I still am. But let's face it... after the loss of
J'ster and the PPD, you and I were the NA. The Stallions and Kamikaze have long since ceased to be effective at all. And even in their heyday...
you and I both understood their use was primarily as cannon fodder. But you left Styles... you allowed your pride to be your downfall. I'll not fault you for losing to Chris Tyler... he beat me twice. But at least I stayed
Rick. At least I never abandoned you. Even in Japan, after fighting both Extreme and Nevermind... I came out to help you. I was always in your corner... I never, ever let you down as enforcer... you must admit this.
And yes, you aided me in the ring as well... for that I am grateful. But when I needed you most... you were gone. There has only ever been one time in my life when I felt truly reliant upon someone, and you were not there.
And now you have constructed a myth for yourself. You are telling the whole world that my ambition killed the NA... you misunderstand Rick.
It is _only_ my ambition that has kept the NA from fading completely into oblivion. Is the NA staggering? Yes it is... I will admit that. But you were the one who struck the blow Rick... so do not blame me for your own blundering. I am not another pawn for you to manipulate...
You never did understand me though, did you Rick? You never quite could get your mind around the reality of Alex Martinez. You tried a thousand times to pigeon hole me into a neat category, because once you understood me, you could manipulate me, as you manipulate everyone and everything else around you. But you never could quite grasp that I defy simple explanation. The reality that I was more than another pawn has always eluded you. And now Rick... you have once again allowed yourself to say things which will bring sorrow to you.
You say that I tried to make the NA into my own image and failed... but again, you do not understand. The NA _is_ in my image. It is shattered, fractured, incomplete... and yet it thrives. Just like I am Rick. I am not whole... I am not sane, I am not even healthy. My body has been broken a thousand times. My entire body is covered in scars, all of my bones have been broken. And yet look at me... I am unstoppable, nearly an elemental
force, as I stand over the UEW, the unconquerable juggernaut. It is a paradox... and yet, it rings true. You do not see that though... and it will be to your detriment.
[An evil grin crosses Martinez's lips, and a faint snicker can be heard issuing forth.] But I'll not call the Stallions back from Japan.. let them rot there. They did nothing for me... but rest assured, I do accept your challenge
Rick. I
will bring the New Alliance roaring back into life. It will be filled with people who are like me... fractured, and yet still thriving. Not only will the NA be fashioned after my image... the whole of UEW shall as well.
Now that I am the Ultimate Champion, this federation will be plunged into the abyss. It will be a bloody experience... and one fraught with peril. I hope that you enjoy it Rick... perhaps, once your body has been broken and you have fallen victim to _my_ NA, in _my_ UEW, you will, at long last, understand me.
But Keening and Styles... you two are for another time. You two will be dealt with after I deal with another. Who? None other than Nick
Leshenn, the fabled NightHawk... If _anyone_ does understand me... even partially, it is Nick Leshenn. After all, it has been two years and three federations, and here we are, still at one another's throat. Its been a long, bloody road between us, hasn't it Nicky? You and I, constantly battling, and there has never been a clear victor for more than a fleeting moment...
I wish I could say that this is the final battle Nicky. I wish I could build this up as the last climactic battle between two bitter enemies...
but what would be the point? You and I will never be done with this war
I
fear. It will not end until one or both of us are no longer physically capable of movement. The war between us has always been one of attrition, nothing short of our enemy's demise will sate our bloodlust. I have resigned myself to this Nicky. Once, I thought it could end... but now
I
know better.
Another might look at this and blow off our upcoming battle. Another might simply dismiss the fight we will have on Sunday Night Heat. But not
I... once, a long time ago, I lost a title to you. Nicky, that will _never_ happen again. I will never allow you to dethrone me in that fashion again.
I look forward to the next fight Nicky... and I look forward to seeing you get... BURNED!!!!!!! [Fade to Black]
