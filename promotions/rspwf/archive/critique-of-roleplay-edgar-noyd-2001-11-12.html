[Critique of Roleplay] Edgar Noyd

12 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just looking for some feedback. New character I've been using for maybe the past month. I know a few people might flame the usage of caps during 2 or 3 blocks of speech, but at that point I hope you can see Noyd is just flipping out.
And oh, yeah. To explain his random flip out.. he is pretty unstable, mentally. But the Fed already knows this so it isn't really mentioned in this
Flash or gone into any great detail.
- [name withheld] ======= [The visibility gradually increases upon a man sitting alone in the middle of an empty ring. One white, dim spotlight shines on the man casting an illumination upon one half of his body. The other half remains in the shadows, unseen. That man is Edgar Noyd. In typical fashion, Noyd is wearing a tight white T-shirt with a pair of blue jeans and black boots. Edgar is actually sitting in the new LCW Arena in Memphis, Tennesee.
Noyd seems to be spacing out in this somber and lonely moment. As he sits on the cold, blue steel chair he raises his head up as his face twists into a hybrid of a smirk and a frown. His bright, blue eyes stick out even more in comparison to his pale, bald head. Looking out into the darkness, Noyd continues to show no acknowledgement to the Camera as he begins to talk to himself.]
EDGAR NOYD- Maybe I should take the wheel and paint it orange and make it into a baseball cap. <stares around aimlessly> If a tree falls, do you hear it?
How many times does it take to for a boy to burn himself with a lighter to learn not to play with fire?
[And with that last comment, Edgar Noyd stands up and throws the chair across the ring, sending it over the ropes. The chair makes a loud clang against the cement floor as Noyd becomes completely enraged, out of absolutely nowhere.]
AND THAT IS WHAT IT ALL BOILS DOWN TO, GOD DAMMIT! LITTLE TOMMY, PLAYING WITH
MOMMY'S LIGHTER! HE DIDN'T KNOW WHAT COULD HAPPEN! HE HAD NO GOD DAMN CLUE!
HELL, HOW COULD HE!? HE WAS JUST A LITTLE FIVE YEAR OLD! HE DIDN'T KNOW HIS ELBOW FROM HIS ASSHOLE! [Noyd begins to press down on his own skull, reminiscent to the way he presses down on the head of his rabbit.]
NO ONE TOLD HIM WHAT MIGHT HAPPEN! IT ISN'T HIS GOD DAMNED FAULT! WHO KNEW
THE CURTAINS WOULD LIGHT UP!? WHO KNEW THE RUG WOULD GO UP IN FLAMES LIKE A
TANK OF NATURAL GAS?! WHO KNEW!? HE DID IT ONE TIME! EVERYONE THOUGHT HE'D LEARN HIS [BEEP]ING LESSON! BUT
THEN .. HE DID IT AGAIN! HE BURNT THE HOUSE UP AGAIN! AND THE SECOND TIME, IT
ALL WENT UP IN FIRE! AND THEY HAD NOTHING! DAYS BEFORE CHRISTMAS! DECEMBER
17TH, 1990. THE ENTIRE THREE FAMILY HOME WENT UP IN A BALL OF FLAMES! AND IT
WAS ALL LITTLE TOMMY'S FAULT! HE DIDN'T LEARN FROM HIS MISTAKES!
[Long pause as Noyd falls to his knees, pulling down on his cheeks, pressing on his eyeballs.]
AND HOW [BEEP]ING CONVENIENT FOR ME TO RE-TELL THIS TALE! BECAUSE LITTLE TOMMY HAS A COMPARISON! HE HAS A TWIN! AND HIS GOD DAMN NAME .. [Quick pause as Noyd suddenly becomes calm.] Jake Fiend. [Noyd remains seated on the mat, his chest and shoulders pounding up and down with adrenaline.]
I thought I taught him a week ago what happens. I thought he learned. Prior to our match, I had a small ounce of compassion for him. He didn't know Edgar
Noyd. He didn't know me.. he didn't know the power of ABUSE! .. And he was
IGNORANT to it all!! And I had mercy for the lonely, miserable fruck! .. And so I taught him a lesson! Never, .. _ever_ in your right mind approach Edgar
Noyd with the thoughts of destroying him! You can pummel him. You can whip his freaking ass.. and like a certain someone says.. You can drag his ass from bell to bell and straight through hell..
[Noyd flashes a grim, psychotic smile.] But you can _NEVER_ kill him. He'll come back like the Red Death. Stronger, angrier and more poised to rape you of each and every shred of decency, normalcy and pleasure you ever endured in your measely little [BEEP]ing life.
Because that is how the cookie crumbles. And all my life, the cookies have crumbled. So because of that, it's time for the cookies to start falling on someone else. It is time for someone elses Wheaties to get pissed on. So Jake
Fiend, the first time was a Lesson I hoped you'd learn.
[Pause.] The second time? [A maddening smirk.] Well Jacob... the second time is the price you should've paid for the first one. And seeing as the payment is late.. just like everything else in life, ..
the second time around - the payment is double. And I'm only taking one form of payment...
[Noyd's eyes light up.] YOUR LIFE! [Fade to black.]
