Sample rp...

29 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm just posting this becuz none of you guys know me yet. I figger this will sorta be my intro to RSPWF. Anyway, if ya like it let me know. If you think it sux, let me know that too. I'm always willing to improve and am open to CONSTRUCTIVE criticism. ------ [Scene opens on a crowded metropoltian city. It's a dark and windy day. Most people are hurrying along just trying to get on with their normal little lives. Traffic whizzes by making crossing the street a perilous journey at best. Throngs of nameless individuals surge across the street as the "walk" light activates. As the masses moves to the adjacent corner, you notice the crowd seems to be parting around some obstacle. The crowd thins, showing not an object, but a man. That man is Jeru The Damager. A feeling of cold dread washes over the crowd, thick and solid like an invisible quilt, as Jeru walks past. Looking back over his shoulder, he sees their quick hidden looks and outright stares; he smiles. Reaching his destination, Jeru enters the building. Walking down the hall, he pauses to admire his reflection in the mirror. Standing at just over 6'4" and a solid 265 pounds, Jeru is a model of physical perfection. His physique looked to be scuplted from onyx by the very hands of Mother Nature herself. His jet black skin and blad skin gave him a fearsome look. But his eyes...Jeru's eyes were right from a Stephen King novel. A freak birthdefect had given him completely clear irises. Staring into those white pools of ice was like looking into death itself. Jeru knew this, and used it to his advantage.] Receptionist: Ahhh...Mr.?? Jeru: Just Jeru honey, just Jeru. Receptionist: Ok..uh..[flustered] Mr..uh Jeru, they're waiting for you in room 6B. Just up the hall, to the left. Jeru: Thanks...and uh, when I'm finished here...you want to get a bite? Receptionist: Oh..uh..sure...I [Jeru waves his hand] Jeru: No prob...pick a place and I'll be back in a bit. [Leaving the flustered and blushing receptionst, Jeru heads down the hall.] Jeru: 6b...6b...6b....ahh, here it is. [Opens door and enters.] Jeru: Hello folks. [Already in the room is a small camera team, and the interviewer, former wrestler "Whiz" Wizinkowski. Whiz stands and shakes Jerus hand.] Whiz: Hey there! Go ahead and take a seat and we'll get this thing rolling. I know you're a busy man. Jeru: Not really. I'm sorta in-between feds right now. Whiz: Okay let's start there, why haven't you signed with anybody yet? [Jeru fishes a long thick cigar out of his pocket and lights it. He takes a nice long draw off it.] Jeru: Well, it's like this. I've wrestled in my share of feds. Some were pretty decent outfits. Most were bushleague, half-assed crapshoots. I've worn plenty of gold. I figure it's time for me to take my talent to the next level. The next fed I sign with will be one of the best around! Whiz: The money wont be bad either with a top fed eh? Jeru: True. But money has never been the main motivator for me. What I want it top tier competition! I've beaten enough jobbers. I want to test myself against other champions. Whiz: For our veiwers who don't know you [chuckles] tell us about your past. [Jeru stretches and takes another drag from the cigar, then sits it in the ashtray.] Jeru: I've had to fight for everything I have. I came up hard. Born and raised in the Calliope Projects in Louisiana, 3rd ward. Even tho my mother had instilled a good work ethic into my brothers and I, it became obvious that I wasn't going to college. Trying to provide a good example to my younger brothers, I joined JobCore to earn some money and futher my education. WHile I was away, my brother Ali was killed in a gang dispute. I quit JobCore to take care of my mother and my youngest brother Kimal. When my mother died of angina, Kimal took it hard. He started hanging out at this gym all the time. I should went with him...I should suspected something. [Jeru pauses and looks off in space for a moment.] Jeru: Anyway, turns out he was doing some underground shoot fighting. One night he was killed in a brutal match with a guy named 2 Big. I snapped...I found the people who organized the fights and challenged 2 Big. They refused until I threatened to go to the police with the whole shebang. I don't know why they just didn't kill me. Whiz: Maybe they saw potential in you? Jeru: I doubt it...anyway the night of the match, 2 Big showed up with his entourage of groupies. I guess he figured I'd be an easy win. Well, I wasn't. By the time they pulled me off of him, he wasn't quite human anymore. He died later that night. I'd avenged my brother, but lost something of myself. I was never the same after that. Somehow, the police got wind of it. I was charged with manslaughter and ended up doing 5 years. Whiz: Really? I wasn't aware you were an ex-con. Jeru: There's probably alot about me you aren't aware of Whiz. Whiz: True! But that's why I'm here... So what are you doing now? Jeru: Just training. That and I teach a self defense course over at Central Park. Whiz: Self defense in Central Park? Whoda thunk it? [laughing] Well, thank you for coming down Jeru. Jeru: No problem. Anytime for a legend like yourself! Whiz: Ahhh, stop that talk kid. I'm no legend. Is there any last thing you wanna say? [Jeru puts out his cigar and stands. Turning to face the camera, he points directly at you.] Jeru: I am ready. Ready for anybody who thinks they have the talent to best me! No boasting, just facts. Remember, I am The Damager! [The scene fades out as Whiz and Jeru leave the room chatting and laughing...] +++++++++++++++++++++++ Adonis Handler of - Jeru The Damager "The orginator" Damien Luft aka Deviant "Domination Extreme"
