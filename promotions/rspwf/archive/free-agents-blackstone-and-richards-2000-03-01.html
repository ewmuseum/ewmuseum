[Free Agents] Blackstone and Richards

1 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera fades in to the "Old School Wrestling Academy" somewhere in Ohio. The place is packed for a Saturday afternoon, as wrestlers from the age of 17 to 36 are either lifting weights, sitting and watching matches, or trying to work on their gimmicks. The camera swerves around the room, as it's being filmed by a total amateur. There are about 12 different guys sitting ringside and watching two small (by wrestling's standards) men wrestle in the ring, and they are doing the springboard moves, the top rope backflips and everything, but then the camera focuses on two really big men at ringside, snickering at what's going on in the ring. One man is a huge black man, about 6'6, while the other is a white guy around 6'8, but with a little bit more fat on his body.
They both have their t-shirts off, and while their bodies aren't great, they would make a triple XL shirt look tight. The camera stops with the closeup, and actually walks up to the two men sitting ringside. The black man has blue jeans and white sneakers, while the white guy has black khakis and black Timberland boots.]
Cameraman/Patch: Blackstone and Richards, you think this match is funny or somethin'? [Patch Morrison is the owner of Old School Wrestling Academy, and indy pro-wrestler. Richards looks at the camera, and takes a pack of cigarettes out of his pocket, but Blackstone talks.]
Blackstone: Yeah, it's funny. It's damn funny, Patch. Everytime we come here, we got to see these small-ass <blank>kers jump around like some acrobats or some sh<blank>. I'm tired of seeing these people in wrestlin', seriously.
Richards: Whatever happened to the age of the big guy, where the 5'11 dude was the guy who always got squashed?
Patch: It's a new era, boys! You gotta live it with it! Richards: That is bull<blank>. If one of those little assholes jumps off the top rope at me, I'm gonna turn him around and powerslam his ass through the ring.
Blackstone: You ain't been to any highschools lately. Don't you know that the smaller asshole is ALWAYS the one to lose?
Richards: Hold on, if the small asshole picks somethin' up, then he can win the fight. Blackstone: But I've already got somethin' in my hand though...I could kick his damn ass. These small ol' assholes in the ring now, I could beat them down, too.
Richards: The big men always win. You see guys like them in the ring, they got to have some gimmick to get over. They got to jump off some turnbuckle or spin some dude's head around in their legs, or get on the mic and talk some <blank>.
All we gotta do is powerbomb some dickhead and look at the crowd the wrong way, and we're over.
Blackstone: Y'see, back when I was playin' football, the other teams always had some smaller assholes that had some serious heart.
Richards: Y'eah...godbless the <blank>ers. They had some heart, they had some speed, but they ain't have a bit of power.
[Richards smacks his fist in his palm.] Richards: Raw power, like me and Stone Daddy over here. Blackstone: Heart ain't sh<blank> against strength. They kept gettin' up and gettin' up, but we kept puttin' them down. In this sport...in any sport, you don't need that damn logic. You don't need heart, you don't even need to know the rules all that well.
Richards: Now Patch-o, ain't we the best two bastards you got in this whole school? Patch: You ain't the best wrestlin'... Richards: Has anyone ever beat us in the ring, though? Patch: ...Nah. Blackstone: There you go. Bigga is Betta. Richards: The good big man will always...and I, Big Richie, mean ALWAYS will beat the good little man. It's the rules of nature, baby.
Blackstone: You wanna see how Blackstone and Richards get stuff done? Richards: Let me allow you a demonstration, Patch. Patch: Oh no, the last time y'all had a demonstration you pushed two lightweights outta this school.
Richards: What the hell can I say? Only the strong survive... Blackstone: So me and Big Rich are at the top of this damn food chain. We're the lions...and these little bastards right in there...
[Richards points in the ring, still holding his cigarette.] Richards: They are those little deers with the big antlers that hang around in
Africa. Blackstone: Yeah...them Gazillos. Patch: It's not Gazillos, you fu<blank>in' momo. It's Gazillesos! Richards: Yeah... [Richards and Blackstone climb in the ring, over the top of the ropes. The lightweights get a terrified look on their face.]
Blackstone: Hey! You ain't scared to get in the ring wit' your friends Wade and
Rob, are ya?
Richards: We're just gonna do a little demonstration! Lightweight 1: N...no. That's alright! We're cool. Richards: We've trained like...what? Twice ever! But we could still kick your asses! You boys train everyday though!
Blackstone[Bouncing off the ropes]: Let's see that hard work pay off! [Blackstone does a 180 clothesline on the first lightweight, then lifts him off and bodypress drops him on the mat,while Richards kicks another lightweight in the stomach, and powerbombs him on the turnbuckle. Patch makes the camera move around, because he's climbing into the ring. This was a massacre.]
Patch: You didn't have to do that, you frickin' bastards! Blackstone: Nah...we didn't. But we had to make a point, man. Richards: To all you 180 pound pro-wrestlers, stay the hell at home and dream on. Cause Big Blackstone, the Stone Daddy...
[Blackstone points at Richards.] Blackstone: And Big Rich, Rob Richards...we're gonna make wrestling belong to the big men again. Two big bastards with arms like tree-trunks are gonna take it all back...
You small bastards, get out now while you still can. Richards: Work hard all y'all want...it'll just make you feel bad when we powerbomb you through the ring, though. We're a pair of hustlers, baby.
And as any hustler will tell ya...hustling is heaven if you're a hustler... Blackstone: And hell if you ain't! [Camera fades out.]
