USCW Flash Central 5/17/98 Pt. 2

19 May 1998

Dylan. You two will find out first hand that "The Machine" takes no prisoners.
That "The Machine" doesn't give a damn about the opponents he faces. Syske,
Dylan, you two have an honor bistowed upon you. That honor is to face the team of "The Machine" and Storm. Two members of the SWA that have come to the USCW for pure dominance. And if need be, the army known as the Supreme Wrestling
Alliance, will call upon our forces. The SWA is deep in talent and it's talent that you will never forget.

Ron Matthews: And Scott Dakota. A man, an enemy of mine. Dakota, a three way cage match can be very dangerous. Especially to a man that's had some nerve damage to a leg. As far as I'm concerned, your leg has a bull's eye on it.

SUPREME WRESTLING ALLIANCE What The World Is Watching!! ©1998 -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <you see a camera going down the hall backstage at the weekend edition. the camera arrives at a door with the future legend on it and the camera man knocks on the door, the door opens and you see stephens> camera man: hello mr stephens, can i come in.
stephens: stephens is a very busy man, what do you want? camera man: i would like to come in and have a word with the you about your match tonight against shawn evens and kid sharpe for the light weight title.
stephens: come on in. <as the camera man makes his way into the locker room, the first thing you notice is a fifty three inch projection tv surrounded by an entertainment center with surround sound, a couch and a lazy boy. stephens motions for the camera man to have a seat on the couch as stephens kicks back in the lazy boy.
the camera pans around the room and on the walls you see several awards that stephens has won in other organizations and then you notice a bunch of autographed pictures on the wall in a pyramid shape at the bottom of the pyramid you see wrestlers such as piper, abdul the butcher , flair and hennen, as you look further up the pictures you see bret owen and his dad, along with pictures of andre the giant and hogan. but the picture at the top of the pyramid is that of daniel stephens. you also see a window that overlooks the arena>
camera man: you sure have a nice setup here. stephens: thanks! stephens takes pride in himself and his possessions. this is his favorite place to be when waiting for a match to take place.
camera man: i have several questions for you, some fans also gave me some questions to ask you.
stephens: ask away, there is no question that stephens dont have an answer for. camera man: ok, truthfully, what do you think will be the result of the match tonight? stephens: stephens knows that he could beat either of the two men in a match one on one. as far as stephens winning the handicap match will be a lot harder. you see stephens will be put into the ring with two of the best lightweight wrestlers here in the uscw. the betting man would bet go against him. but stephens believes that the match will not be totally a handicap match. stephens knows that kid sharpe wants the title back and shawn evens wants to keep it. they should start out attacking and double teaming stephens, but midway threw the match, one of the two will see the opertunity to attack the other when his back is turned.
camera man: you saying kid or shawn will attack the other when he has his back turned? stephens: stephens knows it will happen. stephens has studied wrestling longer then he has wrestled. he knows that the light weight belt is what really maters to both of them men. at some point during that match, when they are double teaming stephens, you will see one of them hit the other from behind and try to go for the easy win. as to wether it will be kid or shotgun who tries to take advantage of the situation, stephens dont know.
camera man: i doubt that will happen. stephens: of corse you doubt it, but remember, stephens told you how bullet was and no one believed him then either.
camera man: you do have a point. stephens: of corse, stephens sees stuff happening before anyone tells him a thing and he is real quick to pick up on stuff. i know something was up with bullet when he helped sharpe win the title match against stephens.
camera man: and you believe you will win the cruiser weight title tonight. stephens: stephens will win the title tonight, he hasnt won it yet do to rare circumstances. stephens will surprise you all as he gets his hand raised and the belt handed to him after he fights of the double team assault.
camera man: and the question that everyone wants to know, who will be your tag partners in the 4 on 4 match that you are trying to get scheduled at the pay per view?
stephens: stephens was waiting for you to ask that. at this time he is not exactly sure who he will select. he will though select a team that will beat the opponents.
camera man: why do you call yourself the future legend? stephens: stephens didnt call himself that at first. he used to be known and the shock. camera man: the shock? stephens: yea, he shocked every one when he first came into the wrestling seen at the age of eighteen with his very impressive win streak. people then came to expect him to win and no one was shocked anymore. after a while many wrestlers and announcers started to take notice of how great he was and how far he will go in the sport so the nicknamed him the future legend.
camera man: speaking of the future, what are your plans for the future? stephens: daniel stephens is know on a diet and exercise program that will take five years to complete. over the next five years, stephens will be gaining about fifty pounds of pure muscle but still maintain all of his quickness and skills. at the end of this period he will move up into the heavy weight division and win titles all over the world. and prove to everyone that he is the future legend.
camera man: thanks for your time, i need to get down to the arena, the show is about to start.
stephens: no problem. <stephens gets out of his lazy boy and shows the camera man to the door. on the way out you see a desk with a book that is being written and the title is wrestling history of daniel "the future legend" stephens. and then the camera man exits>
