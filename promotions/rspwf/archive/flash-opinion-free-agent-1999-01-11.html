[Flash] Opinion/Free Agent

11 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: We are inside an "ACE Hardware" store, in the outskirts of Cleveland Ohio, at about 7:30pm. It's cold, brisk, and yet beautiful outside, with the weather picking up greatly, snow falling down to the freezing ground like mad.]
[The cameraman stands in one lonely aisle, with hardware for outside snow work on one side, for example shoves, ice picks, winshield scrapers, and even little hand warmers. On the other side, it must be a section for leftovers as hammers, small axes, and very small amounts of barbed wire sets in a box to the corner.]
[Then chatter is heard, as two men walk in to camera veiw, one being an employee, the other, is fellow ICW employee, Dave "too Extreme" Bean.
The employee of course, is wearing a standard "Ace Hardware " uniform, while Bean, stands wearing a "Nike" winter coat, and a pair of black FUBU jeans, while sporting the annual Timberland boots. As they walk in closer and soon stop by one aisle close, the camerman cuts into the conversation for are listening pleasure.]
EMPLOYEE- Well what exactly are you looking for sir? DAVE BEAN- Hmm...Have you ever seen...say....Monday Night Raw? EMPLOYEE- WWF? Yeah been watching that stuff since I was a small kid...never miss it sir! DAVE BEAN- Well...if you would see any of this stuff ,in this aisle, that would be on the show.......then I dont want it, aint hardcore enough....
EMPLOYEE- Is this for a wrestling match?! DAVE BEAN- Yeah...ICW....I got a date with Kenneth Morlock, and I want to set the standards, cross the line of extreme and hardcore with him...can you help me pick out a couple toys to bring him tommorow night?
EMPLOYEE- Yeah....yes sir! Wow...what's your ring name? DAVE BEAN- Dave "too Extreme" Bean... EMPLOYEE- Hmm...heard of you being in independant places and stuff.... glad to meet ya... [Employee extends hand for a regular shake] DAVE BEAN- Nice to meet you son.... [Bean slaps the hand of the Employee] EMPLOYEE- ..........what can we get for ya......ummm......what about this? [The employee walks over towards the area of snow shovels, which makes it seem that another stock must have recently came in, for there are many shovels left. Salt for melting ice stands above the shovels, as does several small shovels. The two stop right by the metallic, and some wooden shovels, and re-start there discussion....]
EMPLOYEE- I can't think of a time I have seen shovels on television....easy to handle, easy to swing, and...easy to hurt somebody!
DAVE BEAN- Hmmm....yeah.....I can see a double axehandle, with that thing....sounds good.....
[Bean grabs the shovel, and tosses it into a cart, that he see's nearby...] DAVE BEAN- What about that crap over there? [Bean points near the barbed wire, small axes, and even different sizing hammers, and get's an anxious look on his face...while the two now make their way to the section. They stop near the "toys", and begin to talk about making yet, another deal.]
DAVE BEAN- Now this is the shit I'm talking about son...this is hardcore.... EMPLOYEE- Well...I think using a small axe, would be...attempted murder... [Bean does not enjoy the smart ass comments....] DAVE BEAN- Pack it... EMPLOYEE- What sir?! DAVE BEAN- Throw it in that cart over there son....cmon.... EMPLOYEE- Yes sir...your not...err....nevermind... [The employee takes the small axe over to the cart...] DAVE BEAN- You uh...got any larger packages of barbed wire? EMPLOYEE- That's as large as they come sir... DAVE BEAN- Serve me up two.... [The employee grabs two boxed packages of barbed wire, and tosses them into the cart, as Bean goes over and takes a look at the ice scrapers...picks one up..
and takes it over to the cart...] DAVE BEAN- This too...ah I think that is enough...thanks my good man...what by chance is your name, so I can let your boss know your one helluva kid...
EMPLOYEE- Steve sir....Steve Worchester...... DAVE BEAN- Well Steve.... [Dave pulls out a twenty....and hands it to the kid....] DAVE BEAN- Thanks for your help son...... STEVE WORCHESTER- Thanks....thanks alot! [Steve exits the scene, holding the crisp twenty dollar bill in his hands, and walks over to another aisle out of camera veiw. The camera now centers on the cart that
Dave Bean has in hand, which now holds a small axe, two boxes of barbed wire, an ice scraper, and what looks like a singapore cane also stands out. He begins to push the cart towards the cash registers, that are not to far away.......]
DAVE BEAN- Well Kenny boy....are you ready? Hell, I've got my stuff packed up, and I'm ready to get it on....but did you prepare? Did you study my wrestling, my pschology? Did you learn my strategy? No? Then I'll tell you....I have none...I come to the ring, with one intention in mind....I am going to whoop some ass...and I'm going to do it as hardcore, and as extreme as I can....ask yourself
Kenneth.....
_ARE_ _READY_ _TO_ _GET_ _EXTREME_?!?! [Scene: The camera slowly fades to a scribbled screen, as Dave Bean makes his way to the registers, with his cart of toys right in front of him.....preparing for his small matchup coming up in...I...C....W.....]
