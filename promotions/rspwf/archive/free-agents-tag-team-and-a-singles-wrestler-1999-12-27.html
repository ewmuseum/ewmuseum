FREE AGENTS! Tag Team and a Singles wrestler!

27 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am now officially announcing my free agency for my three e-wrestlers,
Scorpion, and Greg and Johnny Harris, known as The Harris Brothers or The
New Era.(Note: I came up with these names about 2 or 3 months ago, before the WWF started their New Era, and before Creative Control went back to being called The Harris Boys.) I have held many titles with these three, and usually wherever I go I draw a lot of heat and set up a lot of matches/feuds. I send in a lot of flashes/strategies for the cards to help out with the feuds, and usually roleplay whenever I have enough material to respond to. I have enclosed an old roleplay from my last fed. This was when
The New Era became The New Era by splitting from Scorpion and proclaiming themselves The New Era of stars in the AWA.
----- *The scene opens up inside of a sold out arena where reporters, cameramen and photographers are surrounding the ring in anticipation of Scorpion's announcement. All of a sudden "Hell's Bells" by AC/DC hits the speakers and the crowd erupts. Scorpion walks out getting a good reaction, then walks down to the ring. He steps through the ropes, then goes and grabs a mic from a reporter.*
Scorpion: Well, there has been a lot of speculation about this announcement that I'm going to make, but before I make the big announcement, let me go over a few details of my career. I began my career here in the AWA last year, winning the European title a month after I joined, and the World title a month after that. I went on to hold the World title two more times, as well as numerous other titles, then I decided it was time for me to move on.
I went to SCWF where I competed for all of it's two month history. In that two month fed I was their only World champion and beat everyone. From there
I signed with the WWWA, a fed that had just started when I won the SCWF
World title. I came in an immediate top contender, feuding for the World title and winning it after two months. After becoming a two time champ there
I left for one of my biggest mistakes. I joined ICW as a top contender, but was never treated as one, always held back for the less talented people who had been there since the start of that fed. After awhile of this I left wrestling for awhile to train my brother, The Enforcer, and my cousin Greg
Harris. After getting their careers off the ground I rejoined the AWA. The rest was a struggle with Peter Lowry, then when he left becoming a top contender. I never got to win the AWA title again, but I really have nothing to ask for. I've gotten all that I need in my career, except for one thing.
I want a retirement match. The last fight I had with Jagpreet Ghuman will not be how I go out. Instead I'm suggesting that we take the AWA's top three stars ever. Myself against Bob Peil and Guy Gagne. This match will be a match I have wanted for awhile, to see who the true icon of the AWA is. So
I'm saying that at this month's PPV, we go at it in a three way match, and see...
*Scorpion is interrupted as Welcome to the Jungle by Guns and Roses hits the speakers. His cousin Cyclone Greg Harris walks out from the back and walks to the ring. As he reaches the ring he grabs a mic, but before he can speak the lights go off. More Human Than Human by White Zombie hits the speakers and Diablo walks down to the ring surrounded by fog. he steps in the ring and grabs a mic as well, as Scorpion stares at the two men.*
Scorpion: What is this all about? Cyclone: First of all, I want to know what you're doing? It's wrestling tradition to pass the torch when you retire. Now I have had my ups and downs in the AWa, but I've never gotten my breakthrough shot at being a star.
Instead I've been in your shadow. Now you're leaving and you're telling me that in your last match you're gonna put over two of the most over people in the fed? That means that you'll go out famous, and you'll disgrace your family by ruining my career.
Scorpion: Listen, this is the match I want. Now I've done enough for you. If it weren't for me, you wouldn't have been able to sign up in the AWA, much less be recognized as a good competitor from a good stable. It's not your place to tell me what to do for my last match. I'll fight who I want to fight. Now what about you new guy. What are you here for? You want a fast jump to the top too?
Diablo: First of all, there is nothing new about me. *Diablo removes his mask to reveal himself as The Enforcer.* Diablo: So you made me a big star huh? You got me over and started my career? Well the only thing I've been doing is hiding in your shadow, just like Greg. Look what I've done. I won the World title in the BWO with a horrible gimmick, and the only reason I won was because of who I was, your brother. No one has ever considered me a good competitor, they consider me your brother and nothing more. Look where you got me. I went from your protector in SCWF, to a horrible gimmick in the BWO, back to your protector, and now I'm back with a similar horrible gimmick. I'm the wrestling devil, the person who has all the power in the world but uses it to win wrestling titles. No, I'm not a devil, nor the leader of the dead. That's not what I became. I am a wrestler, just like you Scorp. I became a wrestler, not a devil. I am Johnny Harris, not Diablo. All your career you were a wrestler, while I was thrown around with gimmicks. I agree with Greg, you should think about your family first and stop thinking about your career when it's over.
Scorpion: And just like Greg, it's not your place to tell me what to do with my career. Greg: Well Tim, there's only one thing left to do for us, and i'm sorry it came to this. *Greg and Johnny attack Scorpion at once, beating him into a corner. Greg picks him up and places him on the turnbuckle while Johnny still pounds him in the chest and face. Johnny then picks him off the turnbuckle and executes a running sit-down powerbomb, causing Scorpion to bounce off the mat. Greg follows with a somersault leg drop to the throat as Johnny gets a table in the ring. Johnny sets the table up as Greg goes to the top. Johnny picks his brother up and sets him up for a powerbomb as Greg flies off the top with a legdrop. Johnny powerbombs his brother through the table all while Greg comes down on him with a flying legdrop, adding more force. Both men pin
Scorpion and they both count the three count, then celebrate as the crowd boos them both.*
Greg: We've had enough of this crap! From now on we call the shots and we're gonna be the stars. We are the New Era of the AWA! As for this tag team tournament, we want in. The Harris Boys are gonna be the next tag champions, and this will be step one to our stairway to the top of this fed. The New
Era has arrived, and now Dream King, Bob Peil and even you Scorp, it's gonna be out with the old, and in with the new.
*With that Johnny and Greg Harris leave the ring, as medics attend to their relative Scorpion. * ----- Just a note: I don't make a habit of attacking people in my roleplays. I do it in flashes for cards, but the only reason I did it roleplay was because all three characters were mine. I will join one fed, that being the best fed
I get an offer from, and I will choose this fed Wednesday. I already have a few ideas as to how I could enter the fed with my gimmick and with an impact, so if you have any other questions or you are interested, please
e-mail me at [detail withheld] Thanks! -- [name withheld] [detail withheld]
