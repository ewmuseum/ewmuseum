I WANT TO JOIN A FED

4 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I WILL JOIN ANY FED THAT DOESN'T PLA ON A WEB SITE.SAMPLE ROLE PLAY BELOW. You are in a very dark room.You can barely make out a desk.Suddenly a voice speaks) Voice:I've been sitting in here for awhile now.I'm trying to decide if I made the right choice in signing a contract with thw UCW.Well anyways that's not what I want to talk about now.You see I've been in other federations before and I haven't been satisfied with my experiences.First I'm in the finals of their tounament and it looks like they folded.Then I team up in another fed..I amke it to the finals and get beat out by somebody that just shows up.Then they got the nerve to make another new team the number one contenders.Well by the time I get through with them they'll be folding to because they won't have anybody capable of wrestling for them.So,you see I'm not a happy camper.Now I'm new to this federation so the wrestlers will underesimate me.That's good.Looks like my first big match is the battle royal.Well,I'll be scouting the rest of the men entered in it.Maybe I'll be in front row.Maybe I'll be up in the rafters.Maybe Ill be under the ring.either way,I'll be watching...And learning.Then I have Hulk Hogan.I used to watch you on T.V.I used to think you were great.See I said used to.Now I think you are a washed up has been.Hogan,I am new breed in wrestling.You, old man are a dinosaur and you know what happened to them.THEY WENT EXTINCT AND THAT WHAT'S GOING TO HAPPEN TO YOU ON THURSDAY.
