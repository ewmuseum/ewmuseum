Hey Hey! What's up all RSPWF'ites?! Yep. It's me again, the 'Spirit Warrior' Jin Tao.. Or to my friends, M.D. [Wink] Anyways, I am here on the boards 'cause I'm bored and I don't know how to start off my 3 Day Weekend. So, I decided to post one of my recent flashes for The GLOW!
I got some good thoughts about this flash from people like V.D. Vitrone & My cousin, [name withheld] and they seemed to like it.. Now, I want you, the RSPWF heads to let me know what you think.. And please, people like Mike Sebastian or anyone else who I have 'beef' with.. (Not to many of you though, huh?)
Don't critiuqe this the worst flash you've ever seen because of some stupid 'beef'.. Just please, don't. Oh and, I don't want 50 FAQ's or APP's in my mailbox either please.. As you can see I am in four feds already. I am not looking for anything lower than the callibar of an IWE or an GLCW.. So please,
Enjoy my flash, let me know what you think, but remember.. I am a rookie, I am trying to still develop my character, and YES, I do have some things to work on.. =-)
ENJOY! ___________________________________________________________________ [Japanese shrine of morality. Friday evening, 8:05 P.M. located in Fukushima, Japan. Through the misty sky's of the Fukushima, Japan night, the Historical Japanese shrine of morality, etched from stone, stands in high honor amongst the open lands. No movement among the night is seen, except for that coming from one mystical figure standing in front of the shrine. The man stands, with his hands
& fingers weaved together, his eye lids tightly shut, and his body relaxed from within..
Suddenly, the man releases the strain from his eyes and opens his the lid's widely. This is now clear, the man standing before the shrine is analyzed to be the 'Spirit Warrior'... Jin Tao.
Tao, attired in a silk black suit, which collar is the clearest of the whitish shade. The man's pants come to about only his ankles, as some view of his white tinted socks, can be seen. Jin Tao, also wearing a pair of black Zori's. Zori as in, a flat thonged sandal made of leather.
The short slicked back black hair of Tao glows in the night essence, as he slowly begins to speak. His enthusiasm lacks, hence while he speaks.]
JT :Dylan Rapture's actions two weeks ago were ghastly disliked and unhonored.. I would have never seen you to be a deceiver,
Rapture.. But, many have warned me, "Your eyes can deceive you." I should have listened and token this to heart. My eagerness for success though, blocked this out.
I though, did not appreciate the swift kick into a chair, which was targeted successfully onto my face...
Rapture, I may have judged you wrong the first time around, but if I get another chance at you, 'friend'. I just might not be so venial.. And just might be a little more cautious..
[Tao, hangs his head in disgust.] After a week of hiatus & further training, the 'Spirit Warrior' will come back to achieving my goal of success, honor, pride & respect.
This time though, I do not seem to be within my own nature, as I did when I first entered the GLOW.. The smaller, the quicker, & the smarter of the GLOW Bunch. Oh no, this time around, I walk myself into a new level of competition..
Tomorrow evening, Saturday Night Hot Spot, Jin Tao returns in full.. [Happiness appears on the face of Tao.] My heart, re-locked in floating emotions. My mind, refocused on success. My body, reconditioned for battle. I will now reveal a higher level of 'Tai Chi,' and this time around...You see the same 'Spirit
Warrior' of old, with just _that_ much more of a drive. This, I had been searching for. And finally, have found. [Sudden Smirk.] JT :As I come to recuperate myself from such a harsh loss against the deceiving one himself, Dylan Rapture. I face a man who seems to be out for blood and vengeance.. Seemingly, looking to destroy anything in his instant path..
Westside Walavan. A man of brutality and of fascism. Sir, may I warn you, that size does not make you better than myself. You, are a towering Six Foot Eight, but my heart will always be bigger.. You are a menacing Three Hundred & Three pounds, but my mind will always be stronger. No matter how strong, how big, or how tough you may be, The 'Spirit Warrior'
will always have that drive, that capacity, and the eagerness for victory... We will just see this Saturday, who will become victor in our intriguing battle. If yet again, Jin Tao foretells the future, or if my forgiveness and kindness is just not cutting it, anymore..
[A look of Taoism appears on the face of Jin.] In the past I have been correct Walavan, I sense success for the 'Spirited One.' The future of Walavan though, remains unclear to me. You are shrouded in mystery, my friend, your life long goal seems to be unclear to everyone who seems to search, even yourself,
Walavan. Mine though has been told, has been noticed, and will be successfully managed.. A search for peace and an end for hatred is becoming clearer. I can see this now..
[A Sudden Pause by, Tao.] JT :Till then my friend, Westside Walavan, you may try to contain me. But to contain, is like trying to kill me.. Trying to kill me is like trying to penetrate an unpenetrable force..
Walavan, let it be known.. For I shall be born.. Learn to respect. Learn to forgive. 'Tis' the only way, I was taught to live.
