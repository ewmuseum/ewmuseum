Numba One Mobsta

Wrestlers Seeking Feds · July 2003

Wrestlers Seeking Feds: Numba One Mobsta Sample Roleplay: “Please keep close everyone, this is one place you do not want to get lost in.” Informs the tour guide as he leads the group of tourists deeper into one of the famous Pyramids of Giza. “Please watch your step as well.” Along with these group of tourists are the recently new tag team of Armond Julius and Spider also known as Arachnophobia. While the other tourists are all decked out their explorer gear Armond and Spider have gone with a more casual look. Armond is wearing a pair of blue jeans with a tight whit t-shirt revealing his bulging muscles. Spider is wearing a pair of khakis with a white muscle shirt and a pair of dark sunglasses. “Damn man this is boring as fuck.” Armond tells Spider as they trudge behind the rest of the tour group. “ How did we ever get stuck in this crap tour anyways?” “Hell if I know.” Spider answers equally bored. “I thought it would be interesting to check out all the mummies and shit but with this fairy boy tour guide leading the way all I have seen is lame ass drawings on the wall.” The fairy boy tour guide that Spider was just talking about looks back and notices Arachnophobia dropping behind. He glares at them with his hands on his hips in a girlish fashion. “Hurry up boys, you don’t want to miss what is ahead!” The tour guide shouts towards Spider and Armond. “Oh no we wouldn’t want to miss even more sand!” Armond says in a sarcastic tone. The tour guide just shakes his head and continues to lead the group into the pyramid. Armond and Spider wished they were back at the bar celebrating their dominance over the Innovators of Hell who they gave a severe beating to on Livewire. After that night they went out and partied until the sun rose the next day. Now they would do anything to relive that night again instead of enduring this boring tour. Spider then stops in his tracks and smiles a mischievous smile. Armond stops soon after noticing Spider stopped. “Yo, what’s up?” Armond asks nearly bored out of his mind. “Hey, lets ditch these guys and find us some real sites.” Spider whispers to Armond with the smile still on his face. “God knows if I follow that queen anymore I am going to die of boredom.” “Now that’s what I call a great idea.” Armond whispers back excitedly. Armond and Spider break from their two man huddle and continue to follow the group. They keep their eyes open for a possible ditching situation and it shortly comes as the tour group goes off to the right in a fork in the middle of the pyramid. Armond and Spider following closely behind but as soon as they reach the fork they slip off to the left and on to a brand new path. Armond and Spider slap hands. “Hell that was easy.” Armond says laughing to himself. “Now lets go and see if we can find something interesting.” Spider suggests as he looks down the long passage way in front of them. Spider and Armond slowly make their way down the passage which is lighten up by torches on either side of the pyramid walls. A scarab scurries across the floor and disappears into a hole in the wall. Armond and Spider get this eerie feeling as they make their way through the passage. “Hey Spider, does something feel strange to you?” Armond asks shivering as a cold chill goes down his spine. “Yeah, now that you mention it.” Spider replies looking around at his surroundings with a confused look on his face.” Arachnophobia continue walking but they are definitely spooked by something. They both jump as a loud crashing sound is heard behind them. They look and see that part of the wall had just fallen off and they let out a deep breath in relief. “So did ya hear we are going to be facing Cash and Kula this Sunday at Slamfest?” Spider speaks up trying to change the subject. “Yeah, I am sure we can beat those two though.” Armond answers as he analyzes his surroundings. “I mean look at how much damage we gave to them on Livewire, how could they stop us?” “Yeah,” Spider laughs. “Just because Cash claims to be the best rookie in the SAWF always bragging about his damn record. I wonder why I haven’t stomped his ass sooner.� �What’s up with that little bitch Kula?” Armond asks laughing. “Is he strange or what? Always hanging out with that Ben guy. I don’t know how I ever got caught up with freaks like this.” Spider grabs one of the torches from off the wall as the next part of the passage is very dark. Armond follows behind so Spider can light up the passage. “Well those two may be convinced that they have us beat but I am sure we can kick there ass.” Spider says as he angles the torch to get the best lighting. “We are facing one guy who cares about nothing but money and his damn record and we have another who has to be the most bizarre person on the planet.” “Now look at us.” Armond adds on as he gestures to himself and Spider. “I was born to a mob family and I have practically fought my entire life. Then there is you, a guy who trained in the most run down of places and just look at how big you are! Damn you will crush them!” Spider laughs at the comment from Armond as the two continue down the passage. Spider then feels a soft patch below his feet and stops but Armond isn’t paying attention and he continues to walk. “I tell ya, they are going to be wetting themselves when we get in that ring!” Armond remarks as he laughs out loud. “Armond stop!” Spider shouts as Armond steps on the soft patch but it is too late. The floor gives way and Armond and Spider fall a few feet before hitting the hard ground below with a thud. Spider drops the torch and it goes out leaving Arachnophobia lying on the ground in the dark. “Ah my head!” Armond shouts as he holds his head in pain. “Damn that wasn’t very fun.” Spider sits up and looks around but since it is so dark he can’t see a thing.. �Yo Armond!” Spider hollers looking for Armond. “Where are ya?” “Hell I am wondering that myself.” Armond answers back through the darkness. “It is so damn dark down here.” Armond gets to his feet and searches along the walls for may an opening or something. Spider soon gets to his feet and just tries to find Armond. “Armond, where ya at!” Spider shouts out to Armond. “Keep talking so I can find ya!� �I’m over here!” Armond shouts back from behind Spider. “I am near the wall!” Spider turns around and heads into the direction of where he heard the voice from. As Spider walks he feels a part of the floor sink down under his feet and the next thing he knows fire erupts out of two torches at the front of the room. Armond flies back as he was hanging on to one of the torches. The room now has some light and Spider spots Armond lying on the floor. “Hey what are ya doing down there?” Spider asks as he helps Armond up to his feet. “Nevermind that, at least we got some light now.” Armond says as he looks around the room. The room looks to be some kind of tomb as a giant sarcophagus sits in between the two flaming torches. Armond and Spider slowly approach the sarcophagus. The sarcophagus it self looks to be solid gold and may belong to some old Egyptian royalty. A chill falls upon the spines of Armond and Spider as they look the sarcophagus over. “This thing is giving me some bad feelings man.” Armond tells Spider as the hairs on the back of his neck stick up. “It feels like we are being watched.” “Yeah, I know how ya feel.” Spider answers agreeing with Armond. “Maybe we should get out of here.� �Don’t tell me your scared man.” Armond looks at Spider disappointed. “Oh and like your not!” Spider replies calling Armond’s bluff. Suddenly the torches go out and Arachnophobia are one again plunged in darkness. Armond and Spider then hear a faint whisper. “Get out…get out now” Armond and Spider knees start to shake. “W..Whose there?” Armond asks in a shaky voice. “Show yourself.” Spider and Armond dart their heads every which way looking for the source of that voice. Then Armond and Spider feel a hand each drop on their shoulder. “Armond…is that you?” Spider asks. “Yeah, why are you touching me?” Armond asks Spider. ��But I’m not…” Spider says then trails off. Armond swallows hard and then grabs the hand on his shoulder and Spider does the same. “Hi fellas.” Both Armond’s and Spider’s eyes go wide. They quickly turn around and attack the mystery man who had grabbed them. Armond kicks him in the gut while Spider punches him the face. A shout of pain is heard and then a thud as the stranger falls to the floor. The torches then flare up again and Arachnophobia are surrounded by the tour group. The group look at them strangely. “Heh..hi everybody.” Armond says sheepishly. Spider looks down and sees the tour guide out cold at their feet. Spider tugs on the collar of his shirt as he face goes red. Armond then looks down and sees the unconscious tour guide. “Whoops.” Spider remarks as he steps over the lifeless body of the tour guide. “Umm, sorry about that.” “Geeze, we got him good didn’t we?” Armond asks Spider as he too steps over the body. The group stares at Armond and Spider with angry looks on their faces. Armond and Spider make their way past the angry tourists with bright red faces. They then see a opening to the outside world where the tour group and guide entered from. Outside was the bus that they took to get to the pyramid. Armond and Spider head out to the bus while the group try to revive the tour guide. “Well…that was sure interesting.” Spider sarcastically remarks. “At least we took out that little fairy boy.” “I bet you thought that he was a mummy, didn’t ya.” Armond says smiling. “You were scared stiff!” “You did too!” Spider shouts back. “You were just as afraid as I was!� �Whatever.” Armond says rolling his eyes. “Well we both know there is no such things as mummies.” Spider nods and he and Armond board the bus and take a seat right behind the driver. They sink in their seats relieved after a long day. “To think we thought it was a mummy.” Spider says laughing at such a thought. “Yeah, man was that stupid or what?” Armond asks Spider laughing as well. “Like mummies actually exist!” The bus driver turns around in his seat and the faces of Armond and Spider go white. “Yeah…that is kind of stupid.” the bus driver says and begins to laugh evilly. Armond and Spider stare at the bus driver whose face is nothing but rotting flesh on crusty bones. A scarab crawls out of his eye socket and into his mouth. Arachnophobia scream at the top of their lungs as the door to the bus shuts and it drives off down the dusty path to god knows where…
