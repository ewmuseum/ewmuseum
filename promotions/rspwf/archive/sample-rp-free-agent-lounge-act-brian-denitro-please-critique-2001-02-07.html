[Sample RP] [Free Agent] Lounge Act Brian DeNitro. Please Critique

7 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(<Close your eyes. When you open them you find yourself in the lounge of the Golden Nugget in Las Vegas, Nevada.
It is here that part time wrestler, part time lounge singer Brian
DeNitro plys his trade and you about to find yourself enjoying it, as he is introduced by Nick Wesson, his booking agent, in addition to you, there are about 50 others drinking, smoking and having a good time, just as you are about to..>)
NW: Ladies and Gentlemen, the Golden Nugget of Las Vegas, Nevada is proud to present... BRIAN DENITRO AND THE BIG BAND ORCHESTRA!!! (<Mild Crowd Applause as DeNitro, dressed in a black tuxedo, with a red jacket comes out.>)
BD: Heyyo. Boys and Girls. And all you hep cats. Get ready for the lounge experience of a lifetime. But before we get into our first song, let me talk to you people a little.
(<Makes his way to a man near the front, sitting alone at a table.>) BD: I see that you're alone, I know all about lonely my friend. We all do, don't we folks?
"I'm LOOOOOONLEY...Mr. LOOOOOONLEY, Wish I had someone...to ca-a-a-a-l my Oh-oh-oh-oh-oooown."
Thank you, all you're very kind. Now what most of you don't know is that I make my living not only doing this, but dabbling a bit in the pro wrestling business.
(<More mild applause>) BD: And I've got a little bit of a match this week. And I'm glad that you people are here with me tonight, so that I can share my thoughts on this one baby. I've got this guy, Ironman Scotty Force. And I'm not going to lie to you folks, he's a bit of a heavy. But I'm going to liven this thing up a little, with some Ironman, one of my favorites, and I know it's one of yours. By Ozzy. He only needs one name, unlike
Force who needs 3.
"I....I....I am I...I...I...Iron Ma-a-a-a-a-n." THANK YOU AGAIN, you're too kind. (<One man gets up to leave.>) BD: Sir, where are you going? Man: Listen buddy, no offense, but, well, you SUCK! BD: Did you hear that ladies and gentlemen? We've got a heckler, I love it. This guy reminds me of Scotty. Telling me I suck. Like I've never heard that before. I've been hated, I've been loved, I've even had my heart broken once or twice. But haven't we all? Listen babe, you go on, get yourself out of here, you're driving me crazy.
"You...You...YOU DRIVE ME CRAZY! I just can't sleep. I'm so excited,
I'm in too deep. Cra-a-a-a-a-azy, but, IT feels, alright, baby thinking of you, keeps me up, all night. Thank you, thank you. (<The man walks out shaking his head.>) BD: Don't get me wrong folks, I'm not crazy in the head like our friend there and Scotty Force, I'm just here to have a good time, just like all of you. But yesterday, I find myself watching MFW Television, and BAM, right in my face I see this guy Scotty Force telling all kinds of lies about yours truly, and folks, it hurt. He not only said that my mother was a whore, he said my father sold drugs, and that I was a wanker who flashed people on the corner.
Well old Brian DeNitro, well, he just doesn't handle slander like that very well. So I've got a little somethng to say to Mr. Scotty Force.
"I...I...I...I'm Not, no I'm not, YEAH, I'm not your Steppin' Stone St-ooooo-ooooo-ne. Not your Steppin Stone." Thanks again folks. (<DeNitro makes his way over to another table sitting down across from a lovely young lass.>)
BD: I see you're here, and you look like you're the kind of woman who likes a guy who gets things done. Am I right?
Woman: Absolutely. BD: Well let me tell you honey, I get the job done. Every time I'm out here in the ring. I'm a hard working man. And that's why I'm going to take Mr. Scotty Force and drop him on his head at Violent
Outbreak. Because I work hard...
"I work HARD, I work HARD, I work HARD FOR THE MONEY-Y-Y-Y-Y-Y...so you'd better treat me right. Yeah, you, that's right Scotty Force,
YOU'D Better treat me right."
Thank you. And thank you, you are a lovely young woman, and thanks for taking in the show tonight doll.
(<DeNitro kisses her hand and makes his way over to his orchestra.>) BD: These guys here, let me introduce you to these guys, on drums Mr. Joe Nicholson! On Bass Guitar Mr. Johnny Sample! And on Sax Mr. Freddie Yarro. The Big Band Orchestra. And let me tell you something about these guys. Not one of them has wrestled a day in their lives, and from what I've seen of Scotty Force, I'd be willing to bet that any one of my good friends here can do to him what I'm about to do at
Violent Outbreak. They can knock him down just like I will, and put him down for a 3 count. And if by some chance Scotty Force knocks me down, well...
"I get knocked down, but I, ohh yeah, I get up agaaaaaain. You, yeah you Ironman, you're never, ever, gonna keep me down! I...I...I get knocked down, but I get up again, you are never gonna keep me
Doooooooooown." Thank you very much, you know I've said alot here about Scotty tonight, and let me make this real clear. I'm not being cocky or arrogant.
Because that's not what I'm about. I'm about peace, love, and good music underneath those Las Vegas lights, just like I know that all you people out there are.
(<More mild applause>) BD: So don't get me wrong, as much as I suppose Scotty has some talent, when it comes to who's gonna win this match...
"You might been hurt, BAAAAAABE...That ain't no lie. You seem to mark up and GO. I remember you told me, that HE, made YOU believe in no man, NO CRY. MAYBE THAT'S Wh-y-y-y-y-y.
Every little thing I do, never seems enough for you. No, it's never enough. You don't want to lose it again. No you don't. But I'm not like them. Baby, when YOU finally get to love somebody, guess what...
It's Gonna be me." Thank you, thank you. Now I don't mean that Scotty is going to love me. I realize that, but
I do realize that when he gets to FACE somebody, the winner, it's gonna be me. So Scotty Force, babe, buddy, you need to cut all the theatrics, you need to stop the lying, and maybe, just maybe, get a little bit of love in your life. Because after you face The Lounge
Act, you're not going to have anything but love to look forward to.
Because I'll tell you kiddo, as much as I want to help you out here, a win just isn't in the cards, you're a gambler...but sometimes...
"You gotta know...YEAH, you gotta know, when to hold 'em. You gotta know when to fold 'em..."
And Scotty, this isn't the time to hold 'em. This is the time to take a walk into the sunset. Because baby, for you, it's all over. You're just not cutting the mustard. And now it's time to take this one home, because...
"Oh no, O-o-o-oh No, I've said too much...But really, Scotty, for you,
I haven't said enough. That's me in the corner. Yeah, it's me.
That's me in the spotlight. Where I belong. Losing my religion." But I won't be losing this match. Good night everyone, you've been a lovely audience, thank you, thank you, thank you! (<With that, you close your eyes again, open them and you're back home. Better off for your free trip to Vegas, and hopefully a fan of "The Lounge Act">)
