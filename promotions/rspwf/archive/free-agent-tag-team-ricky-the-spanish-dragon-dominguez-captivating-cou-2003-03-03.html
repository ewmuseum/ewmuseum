Part I: Personal info ===================== •Your Name: [name withheld] •E-Mail Address: [detail withheld] Part II: Basic Wrestler Info ============================ •Wrestler Name: Ricky "The Spanish Dragon" Dominguez •Real Name: Ricky Dominguez •Hometown: Brooklyn, New York •Date of Birth: 2/23/1978 •Height: 6' •Weight: 230 •Build: Good build, lack of definition •Heel/Face/Neutral: Heel •Gimmick: Ricky is a former cocky, pretty boy heel. He used to be everything his partner is now, confident, arrogant and over-zealous. Now he's cold, calculating and to the point. When he lost his father late last year, something inside him snapped. He became a vicious striker and focused attacks on the neck, looking to do serious injury most of the time. He also felt that he wasn't getting the proper respect he deserved, and that's what fuels him now.
He spent weekends helping train new students and the local wrestling gym, teaching them to grab the respect they deserved and get straight to the point.
One student however, stood out as the best of the bunch and became a shining light. That was :Captivating" Courtney Vendrell. He took Vendrell under his wing because he was almost a carbon copy of what he USED to be, and he wanted to make sure Vendrell got the respect he deserved.
•Basic Appearance Description: Ricky is a good looking male, with short black hair that is held back by a baby blue bandana, and some of it hangs over the front. He has two hoop earrings in each ear. On his left arm is an R.I.P.
tattoo to his father, and on his right is a tribal band and a japanese character which means "Dragon".
•Attire: Ricky wears baby blue short tights that have the same japanese character on black on each side and "Dragon" across the rear. He also wears black wrist tape and black kneepads, with baby blue kickpads.
