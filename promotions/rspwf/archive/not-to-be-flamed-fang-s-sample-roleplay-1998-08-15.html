[Not To Be Flamed] Fang's Sample Roleplay

15 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up to a EWC superstar Fang, sitting in a rocking chair on a porch. We are in Glenn Falls, New York in the back of Fang's lovely home. The porch is on top of a very steep hill and is over-looking the streets of Glenn
Falls. It's a beautifult sight. The camera swings back to the view of the deck.
Fang, sitting still in the rocking chair, is wearing a pair of Levi's 501 blue jeans. On his head, of course, is his grey bandana, while a pair of black sunglasses rests on his nose. It's an extremely hot day, so Fang's shirt is off.Sweat is beading on his forehead and torso, while an expression of elation is marking his face. The shot wavers as the camera's operator runs up to Fang, prompting him for a few comments on his upcoming and past events. Fang turns around and faces the camera directly.]
Fang: I just can't get over it... Tomorrow Never Dies. It was a battle ground of epic proportions! What a grand event for the wrestling's most courageous warrior to make his return! The thrill of combat; the surge of adrenaline rushing through the bloodstream; the roar of the fans as they reveled in the spectacle of violence - these are senses and feelings that have eluded me for too long now!
[A devilish grin appears on his face.] Three months: It is almost as if several life times have been lived in my absence. The world of professional wrestling has grown mightier and more wicked while I convalesced and nurtured his withering spirit; but since my return, it has been overflowing with conflict and the glory such conflict breeds. The battle between good and evil no longer gives rise to the eternal striving us warriors have made our calling; now, the ruthless man sets himself against the still more ruthless man, and calls it his right and honor. It is an environment that suits the tastes of the returning Fang well - I have arisen from my inferno of madness with a new heart of steel. Ha ha...
[Fang rubs his sweat moistened hands against his jeans as he begins speaking again.] I can contain my anticipation no longer - to charge back into the midst of the fray, to embrace the blood and the sweat, the joy and the pain of battle - that is what I long for. James Pillow, our battle, though a great one, was a brief skirmish, and was but a taste of things to come... a little spirited action for
Fang, and a little portion of punishment for a wayward spirit...
This Wednesday night, I shall have the opportunity to test my new strength to it's fullest - how it will cleanse my mind to step back into the ring! But first - the "Enigma" has been absent for a long time, and mystery surrounds his name. The time has come for me to offer you all an explanation, to shine a brief ray of enlightenment over the man who is called Fang...
[Fang scratches his head as if to think of something. Finally a look of satisfaction appears on his face as he speaks.]
Ya see... There's something called patience. They say it's a virtue. I say it's a vexation. That's what I've been -- patient and vexed. It's been a three months since I was really in the pure habit of cracking skulls and drawing blood. After that three months I've had two matches, both in one night. But it's not enough. I thought this was the toughest federation around, one where my grappling excellence would be postulated. But here I am.... waiting.
Waiting for you Andru Edwards. [He starts to rock in the chair.] Patience. They say it's a saintly quality. Well Fang is _not_ a saint. I'm the man who's put fear into the EWC. What other reason could there possibly be for others' trepidation? Well I'm here now and one man all ready fell beneath the legend that never dies. Andru Edwards... You're about to find out why some men just ain't cut out for this line of work. Wrestling is not everyone's calling.
Coming your way is a plethora of pain, agony, and pure mayhem. [He continues rocking, as more sweat drips from his brow.] Patience. Some say it's a blessing. I say it's what my opponents become, Mr
Edwards. You're gonna get your own personal tour of Hell. Bring a lunch because you may _think_ you know Fang, but the truth is sometimes warriors aren't always what they seem to be...
[Fang takes his sunglasses off, and rests them on the arm of the chair. Slowly, he stands] ...sometimes they're worse. Edwards, I feel as though you're a total optimist. One who refuses to accept the view that mankind is so tragically bound to the starless midnight of racism and war that the bright daybreak of peace and brotherhood can never become a reality. Well my friend... You know why we have this match, right? If not, I'll remind you... Our run-in last week on Wreckage. I all ready told you that there is no such thing as an injustice. When I spoke of you last, I knew you'd want to get some type of revenge. I warned you... And I welcomed you to your own destruction... Now it's too late. I don't _want_ to wrestle you, but I must.
For a warrior who does not venture into combat, cannot venture into voctory. I'll use you as a notch on my climb to the Elite Title. For now, so long... Our eternal conflict begins now, prepare... [Fang's eyes open widely.] FOR COMBAT!!!! [The screen fades to black as Fang's lit-up face becomes engulfed more and more with smile.]
