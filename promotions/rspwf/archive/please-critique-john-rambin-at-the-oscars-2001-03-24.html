[Please Critique] John Rambin at the Oscars!?!?!?

24 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera fades onto the E! LIVE at the Oscars. Joan Rivers is standing by with a microphone in her hand and a horrible smile on her fake face. She is puckering up at the camera...she doesn't know the camera is on just yet...]
Joan: Oh fuck, I just hate dealing with these pompous jerks who call themselves movie stars. Do I look okay? How's my make-up? Damnit...if I have to interview
Angelina Jolie and her husband tonight I'm gonna snap. That girl is just an odd ball. Wh...we're live?
(Camera goes up and down.) Joan: RIGHT NOW!?!? (Camera goes up and down once again.) Joan: Thanks for telling me you idiots! Geez, we're live back at the Oscars and
I was just doing some acting of my own. You know I love all of the actors here tonight, and I think Angelina Jolie is just amazing. As a matter of fact I'd love to have those big juicy lips of hers. I bet it keeps Billy Bob happy. HA
HA...ho gee I'm funny tonight.
(In the back we see Tom Hanks and his wife Rita Wilson run by Joan so they don't have to be interviewed by her. Russell Crowe is also in the back, but you can barely see him due to all the FBI agents covering him in case the
"Kidnapper" tries anything funny.) Joan: So who do we have next? HUH? A WRESTLER? Who is this? Can he even complete a sentence?
(All of a sudden John Rambin walks over to her and he just towers her. John has on a very stylish tux with his trademark Oakleys. On his arm is the famous actress Mira Sorvino, and both of them are waving to the fans. Joan is in awe of the size of John, and can't seem to get the words out of her mouth.)
Joan Rivers: You're a big guy. John: Yes I am, Joan. Joan Rivers: I bet you please the women in the sack. John: Hah! Well I haven't had any complaints.... Joan Rivers: I bet you're hung like an Elephant. (Obviously John is getting a little uncomfortable with this kind of talk, but he continues and doesn't lose his cool. Mira Sorvino is laughing really hard as
Joan is egging Rambin on.)
Joan Rivers: So what are you doing here tonight? I mean why is a wrestler here of all places? Shouldn't you be at a bar drinking some Jack with some biker chick?
John: Heh, well actually I was invited here tonight by the Academy. I made a movie earlier this year called "Mr. Thunder Goes To Egypt", and in just a few short weeks I'm going to be making another film.
Joan Rivers: Now this "Mr. Thunder" movie...was it any good? John: Actually to be honest it did a lot better than I thought it was going to. You know, as I'm sure because of your reaction....many people think that wrestlers can't speak, or write, or even act. I think the success of my movie proved a lot of people wrong. Critics liked it, fans liked it, and even Mira liked it.
Joan Rivers: And this is your date? Mira, how long have you been going out with this man? Mira: I've been with him for about 4 months now. Joan Rivers: Ever thought you'd be with a wrestler? Mira: You know I love wrestling. My father always used to take me to the events when I was a little girl, and I grew up watching the classics. I think what they do is almost like the movies, but it's live. That's a hard thing to do, and you're looking at the best of them right here.
(John blushes.) Joan Rivers: Well what's in store for you now? You're filming a movie...are you going to do any wrestling any time soon?
John: Well it's funny that you should ask that Joan. My first match is coming up with the NCWA, and I'm really excited about it.
Joan Rivers: And who do you face, Crusher McGee or something like that? John: Actually his name is ______. I don't know much about the guy, and to be honest with you I'm not really worried. A lot of people try to make a name for themselves by going up against me, but when it boils right down to it the chances of them beating me are so slim.
Joan: Don't get to cocky there bucko. Oh I said cocky....the FCC is going to kill me. John I know you're not like the other wrestlers, but can you do me a favor and pretend to be one for a second. We have to go to a commercial can you take it away....
John: Sure....Listen up brother!!! Coming up, this broad right here Joan Rivers is going to have none other than that little puke, candy ass jabroni Bruce
Willis here. He did that lousy movie "Unbreakable" and I'll prove to him how just unbreakable he really is...WHOOOOOO!!!!
(John smiles and shakes hands with Joan and walks away. Camera fades to a commercial.)
