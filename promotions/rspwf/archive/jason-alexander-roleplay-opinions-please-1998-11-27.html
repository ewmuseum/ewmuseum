Jason Alexander Roleplay (Opinions please)

27 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first roleplay, please give me your opinions on it and things I can do to improve as the character develops and progresses.....
[ž]The scene is inside Gold's Gym in Brooklyn Heights, New York. The clinks and clanks of barbells, dumb bells, and other workout equipment is heard as the gym comes into full camera view. Muscular men and toned women are shown working out and doing various excercises. One man is leaving the gym and is wearing a mask. He looks to be around 6'0%22 - 6'3%22 and looks to weigh around anywhere from 220 to 240 pounds. The camera focuses its attention on that one man who leaves the gym, wearing the mask. The man is wearing a black mask with various blue and white designs. On the top of the mask, it reads %22Pure Attitude%22 on it. He also has on baggy black Levi jeans that fall past his ankles. He's also wearing black shoes, but are unrecognizable because of the pants. He also has on a black t-shirt with a white Nike swoosh across the abdominal area. To top off his wardrobe, he also has on a black leather trench coat that falls to his thigh area. The man notices the camera following him. He stops, glares at the cameraman, and begins to speak.[ž] MAN - %22Excuse me, are you looking for something?%22 CAMERAMAN - %22As a matter of fact, I am. The ICW sent me over here to look for a masked man by the name of Jason Alexander.%22 MAN - %22Then you're looking in the right direction.%22 CAMERAMAN - %22There have been words going around that you're the newest superstar to enter the ranks here in the ICW.....%22 %22PURE ATTITUDE%22 JASON ALEXANDER - %22I'm the newest and the best.
