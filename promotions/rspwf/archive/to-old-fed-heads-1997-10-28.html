To 'old' fed heads...

28 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey . . . watch who you are calling old! I am not a fed-head but just prior to my 34th birthday I became involved with e-wrestling and the BsWo. Four months later and I am still having a blast! I guess I am a true greybeard when it comes to this. My only complaint to date is the fact that there are so many kids involved. Too many in my opinion. I shoudn't say that they are all bad but some are very hard to talk to when trying to explain angles and such to them or trying to get them to be more involved with their fed. I do have to say that many, ranging from 14 to 17 are relatively mature and are open to suggestions. I found out about e-wrestling purely by accident and thought that I would give it a try even though I am not a wrestling fan. I was a long-time gamer, running many a Champions campaign. After it was explained to me what I had to do, I thought of this as a way to get my role playing fix. And I have not been disappointed yet! Sometimes, I do wish there was a fed that catered to more adults but for now I'll take what I can get. Looking for a fed to try out? Why not try the BsWo? Contact the fed head at: [detail withheld] - and tell him Skeletone sent you! BJPerris Skeletone, current holder of the BsWo Rising Sun Title and the BsWo Excellence Title
