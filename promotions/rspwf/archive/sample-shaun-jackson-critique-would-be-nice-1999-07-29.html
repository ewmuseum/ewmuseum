[Sample] Shaun Jackson (critique would be nice)

29 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWFers, Alright, here's the first role play for Shaun Jackson. Please note that this is my first time role-playing with him, so there will be some discrepancies. I would like a critique on this.
Basically, the point of this was to establish the fact that he isn't a wrestler hiding behind a gimmick, but that he's all real and all wrestling. I didn't want to drag it out, but I'll let you guys be the judge.
Thanks! Dan. [detail withheld] -------- [SCENE: Inside El Dub Towers's press conference wing in Los
Angeles, California, after the press conference that announced Lawrence Williams's newest protege into the world of professional wrestling.
The press conference wing is a simple construction. The members of the press are sitting in chestnut-colored steel folding chairs, while an elevated soapbox is found at the posterior of the room. Three adjacent chairs are strung together at the back of the soapbox, with a wooden podium at the front.
The protege is sitting on the edge of the soapbox, looking out towards the camera as it zooms right back in on him.
The man is an intimidating figure, with a tight-fitted white shirt, black and green fatigues, and black steel-toe boots. After a little bit more zooming of the camera, the man is ready to speak.]
SJ: Shaun Jackson. Yeah, that's my name. I'm not wasting any time using some metaphorical approach to discuss my background and where I intend to end up in life. My name is Shaun
Jackson, and it was announced today that I have entered the world of professional wrestling under my agent Daniel Rushing.
Shaun Jackson. That's it. It isn't "Mean" Shaun Jackson. It isn't "Superstar" Shaun Jackson -- it's
Shaun Jackson; no explanation necessary. What you look at right here is what you get. I don't wear masks... I don't wear make up... I don't drop from arenas on cables.
I just wrestle. I wrestle for the love of pro wrestling, not for the money, definitely not for the
fame -- but for the love. But it seems as if there aren't too many men out there like me. It seems like the majority of these so-called men run out spandex and makeup, trying to please the crowd or purposely trying to piss them off... all of this at attempt at some kind of recognition... some kind of heat.
I don't need that. I don't want that. I'm not going to give you that. These fatigues, boots, and this [points to his heart] are all I need to get _my_ recognition. And while I might not get on the cover of People... while I might not be on the cover of TV Guide or starring in the latest movie -- I'll get the job done.
Because I'm Shaun Jackson, and that's what I do. [Gradual fade-out.]
