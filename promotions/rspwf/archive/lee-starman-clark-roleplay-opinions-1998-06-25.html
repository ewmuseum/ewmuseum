Lee "Starman" Clark Roleplay. Opinions?

25 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
for those of you that are keeping up with the KNI, you will recognise this. But here it is for those of you who haven't seen it. I'm looking for an opinion. I wonder how good you think this actually is?
------------------------------------------------------------------------ "A star, waiting to shine" by Lee "Starman" Clark :: The scene opens up to show a corridor, with just dim lighting all the way down. Somewhere down the corridor, a shadow begins to move. The shadow is that of a man, his frame seems muscular, but the thing that 
stands out most is a slight reflection of the light on the man's shoulder, coming from the belt he carries there. As the man walks towards the camera, he speaks, and his voice echos down the corridor.
It is a deep voice, and the accent is unmistakable, only likely to be from the northern part of England. ::
Voice: A darkness reigns over the world of e-wrestling. A darkness in which there are many stars, some brighter than others, but all bring a small amount of light to this great sport. Each one has it's own place in the night sky.
 :: He passes through the focus of one of the dim lights, showing up the shoulder length black hair, and a muscular figure, but before you get chance to gety a good look, he has moved forward again. ::
Voice: People seem to be happy with the darkness in which they are in, looking to the stars, and admiring them. But although each star is special and different in it's own way, many wish for a star that will set the world of e-wrestling alight, a star that will shine bright for all to see, bringing the world of e-wrestling to brighter, better days.
:: The man passes through another light, this time you are more prepared, and you make out that the man is about 6'3 to 6'5 in height, and that he is carrying a championship belt over his shoulder, which he looks at before moving out of the light once more. ::
Voice: People now look to the KNI Tournament, where a new breed of wrestler awaits to show the world just what they can do. Is this new bright star to emerge from this tournament? Maybe. They look at the list of athletes, and they see a few names that they may already recognise. Idol Austin, and Eugene Robinson, are both well known for their exploits in the NWC. But what about the other men from the NWC?
Who has heard of these men? :: The man now steps into the brightest light in the corridor, and stops walking, he is wearing blue jeans, and on the T-Shirt he wears, a name is written, it says Lee "Starman" Clark. ::
Clark: Yes, the man from the MCW, has come from Detroit for one reason and one reason alone, to bring in a new era, a new light if you will, to the world of e-wrestling. The KNI Tournament is my chance to bring my unique light to the world. Many have heard of Lee Clark, but few realise just who he is.
 :: Clark takes the belt off his shoulder, and straps it around his waist, the camera quickly zooms in on the belt, which holds the writing,
MCW Television Champion. Before the camera moves back to Clark, who is now looking directly at you through the screen. ::
 Clark: First and foremost, I have been a champion already, on many different levels, in many different forms. I have held Tag, Television,
European and World Titles in the six months I have been in this sport, but has anyone ever looked? Has anyone ever paid attention to Lee
Clark? No, but I don't hold this against opponents, it is something they hold against themselves after they face me.
:: Clark brushes the long black hair out of his eyes with his right hand, his eyes now staring into yours deeply through the screen. ::
Clark: Any good wrestler always knows what is lying in wait, but through their ignorance, they have all taken me on, and many have fallen by the wayside. I am only now beginning to get recognition in my own federation, the NWC, where I beat off eleven other men to earn a World title shot. That champion was, and still is a very wise man, and through hard work, and learning who I truly was, managed to defeat me.
I have respect for that man.  Clark: Through the KNI Tournament, the whole world will begin to realise the brightness this star holds, and with no restraints placed on me, this Star will rise into the sky, and light up a new day for the world of e-wrestling. But for me to do so, another star stands in my way, a man by the name of Lonestar.
 :: The camera now focuses on Clark's eyes, showing them to be grey, but in the middle, there is a burst of the colour blue, almost like a starburst in his eyes. ::
 Lonestar, for this star's light to shine, it must first extinguish your own. You stand in my way, and your light must be set to rest in the first round of the tournament, but as they say Lonestar...
:: The screen zooms out to show Clark's whole face, where there is now a wry smile. :: Clark: One man's sunset, is another man's dawn. In the KNI Tournament, my dawn begins. As does a new era for e-wrestling.
:: Fade to Black :: ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Well? What do you think? Let me hear your opinions. Lee "Starman" Clark MCW TV Champion KNI Participant ToC Participant Preparing for some serious flaming or maybe(Fingers crossed, knowing it'll never happen) Constructive critisism? maybe (Now this is pushing it) even praise.
