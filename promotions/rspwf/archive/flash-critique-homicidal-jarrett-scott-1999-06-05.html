Hey, My name is Craig, aka CKW. I'm a rookie to the Net Fed's but I've been in e-Wrestling for almost 2 years, AOL fed's of course. Damn, the time flew!
Anyhow, here is my 2 Flashes I did against Chris Johnson in which I beat him. They aren't my best. I've been trained by; Jay Ralston, SyKic X, Joe Yanock, Chris Hopper, Ronnie D, [name withheld], and Creed. I've been told that I am one of the best rookies in e-Wrestling. Well, I'd like to see what the RSPWF has to say, rather or not RSPWF sucks now. Anyway, here it goes. Oh yeah, I am not a free agent YET, but if you think I'm good and want me in your Fed, e-Mail me. I'll always look into it, especially if your name is Chris Blue
{:-) Thanks, Goodbye, Farwell, Ahem! ----------------------------------------------------- [Fade in the old spectrum in Philadelphia. It's dark out and no one is around. The stars from above lite up the building as in for a perfect. The shot then show's the statue of Rocky Balboa. In front of the statue is no other then "Homicidal"
Jarrett Scott! Jarrett wearing a blue T-shirt that reads "Philly Sucks" on the front and on the back it reads "Curt Schilling, Scott Rolen, Doug Glanville, Bobby Abreu,
John LeClair, and That other Wrestling promotion are the only good things around here!" in white. He has on black boots, and blue jeans as well. His hair is pushed back in front of his face, as his two usual chains are seen over his T-shirt.
Rocky has his hands up, Jarrett has his arms in a crucifix position. He stand's there with his icy cold eyes viewing Philadelphia. Finally, he puts his arm's down and walks a few spits. He turns around and look's at the statue.]
"HOMICIDAL" JARRETT SCOTT - Right here is a great champion. Rocky, true it was a movie but the in the movie, he fought with all he had. Rocky, like
myself was a good guy. He isn't like King Snake or Sid Starr. He always had tons of weight on his shoulders and in the end he came out a winner. Just like myself. Now, I haven't had anything on my shoulders. The wrestling world had shut it's mouth on Jarrett Scott. . .Nah, not yet. You see, I've defeated every opponent they put in front of me. I lost only when I had a goon attack. Now, besides fighting THE stars. . .what is left to do? [Jarrett smiles and signals a belt around his waist.] That's what. Sid Starr, at the Survival of the Fittest we fight with 3 other men on our teams, but it isn't gonna stop me from showing you that I can beat you and win that Title! No sir. King Snake, Haden, and Aries will get taken care of by a few friends of mine while I once again get my hands on you. It isn't gonna be pretty Sid, it'll be the Stray Cat Vs. The kid who has been on the streets since he was 12. . .get ready, pretty boy! Before, I fight at Survival of the Fittest I've FINALLY got a match on Explosion, again Chris Johnson. Chris, if I'm not mistaken this is your debut match in Explosion, and the IWE has set you up to fight one of the best Cruiserweights in the World. . .Jarrett Scott. You haven't
loss yet in the IWE Chris, but after our match is over and done with, you'll have your first loss. Because I still haven't been defeated cleanly in the world of Wrestling. On Saturday Christopher, I'm gonna be full of energy. I'm gonna be running circles around ya, that's how much energy I'm gonna have. Why? Because I am back doing what I love and that's Wrestling. Your another Lightweight in the IWE, and another Lightweight I'm gonna respectfully beat to show everyone that I am the best lightweight that there will ever be. But you see Christopher, your gonna be a stepping stone for me to do it. I know, that I haven't proved that I am the best Lightweight, but ya see many and I mean many years to come when you look at the almanacs, and the Wrestling magazines your gonna see what I have accomplished and that is gonna be a whole lot of stuff, let me tell ya. Mark
my words Brah. I have been on a little hiatus and now as the time ticks, the time is closer for the IWE to get a little bit more Homicidal, and on Saturday the
whole damn arena is gonna cheer their heads off when I return and I am gonna show Chris Johnson why I am "The Most Homicidal Man In The World" and if you got something to say about that, all I have to say is bring it and I'll show you why they call me
H O M I C I D A L ! [Fade out.] ============================================================= [Fade in a dark room. An organ playing in the background. Nothing is seen except a throne-like chair, and a man sitting on the chair. The shot gets a closer look to the man in the chair and you see the mans blonde hair with and black streaks. . it is no other the "Homicidal" Jarrett Scott!
Scott wearing a "Liars Inc." T-shirt and a blue patch on his arm that reads "Colorado." He has on black jeans that can barley be seen and black boots, and his 2 usual chains. Jarrett's hair just stays right in front of his face.
Jarrett sits in the chair staring at the camera with his icy cold blue eyes. They are very cold and piercing. He closes his eyes for a second and puts his head down, quickly his raises his head as his hair is out of his face.]
"HOMICIDAL" JARRETT SCOTT - Chris Johnson, the IWE has said he is real good. But is he good enough to tame "The Most Homicidal Man In The World?" That is what I wanna, and that is what I'm gonna find out on Saturday. No one yet has been able to tame me and Chris your gonna be just another victim, rather you wanna be or not because when you step in the ring with me you aren't gonna be safe and one way or another. . .the Homicidal One is gonna hit you with the Homicide!!! The Catalystic, eh? Chris I hope you actually are what they say you are because that only means I'm gonna be in for a good match and everyone knows I love a fast pace match. The IWE hasn't been giving me any matches recently and I come back and fight Chris Johnson, known as The Catalystic. . .this could be what the new IWE is all about. Johnson, rather you are what they say you are or not this match is gonna have
a bunch of highflying moves. . .rather it's me hitting you with them or us doing it doing one way or another. . .when Jarrett Scott is Wrestling your gonna get your moneys worth and if not I'll pay you!!!! Now, I got something to say to Aries. On 23rd Chris Hopper is gonna get you for what ya did to that family. Aries, I'm gonna warn ya from now. . .when you leave San Antonio, Texas you will never be the same after the beating your gonna get from us. Because as piss as Chris is, is as piss at I am. And that isn't good. Aries, you crossed the line you jackoff,
and your gonna pay big time when we step in the ring. Beware. [Jarrett then stands up and pauses for a second and thinks.] Johnson, your a great Wrestler but your attitude is what I don't like about you. Your just like "Stray Cat" Sid Starr and The Puppet Master"
Nicholas Christian. That is why I am trying to keep the IWE, and all of Wrestling clean of. When I won my first match and the WPCW Cruiserweight Title I put Jamie Love in his place, as I defeated him and Chaos Kid. Since then I've been doing it all over, mostly in the IWE and Saturday you'll be another man that was in my way, and another man who felt The Homicide!!!!
Nothing more has to be said Chris, because on the 8th we are gonna be going out it till one of us can't continue. I know I've got the hart to
defeat you. Chris, you've gotta have the hart to win and you don't Chris. That is why I am gonna defeat you on Saturday, laugh it off if ya want but it's a fact. Watch me do it, because after you are hit with the Homicide and pinned. . .remember. . . "HOMICIDAL" JARRETT SCOTT!
