[RP Critique] Shotgun Messiah

7 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, here's a recent RP I did against 'Mr. Wonderful' Chuck Williams in the
IWF. Please look at it and give me some feedback. It's not long, and it wasn't meant to be. Please look it over.
---- [ Running throughout one's mind, a faint voice can be heard off the coast of the Castle of the Lost Souls. ]
V/O: The reluctant gentleman with the sizeable mouth is Charles Williams. He has all the drive of a broken camshaft and the aggresive vinegar of a corpse. As you have, no doubt, gathered, his big stock in competing is the tall tale. Now, what he does not know is that the visitors out front are a very special breed, destined to change his life beyond anything even his fertile imagination could manufacture. The place is Buffalo, New York; the time is present. But Mr. Williams' on the first leg of a rather fanciful journey into the place we call the Castle of Lost Souls.
[ Fade in. ] [ The thunder rings from the heavens and the lightning streaks through the clouds. The rain pelters down upon the Castle of the Lost Souls. The Caslte of Lost Souls; a place that has been left with nothing but mere emptiness and depletion for quite sometime. It has recently been open to a select few, however; a few that were hand-picked by a certain deprived individual. ]
[ Within the Castle of Lost Souls has been nothing but a great and short running conquest to show the world that the inhabitants within are those that are the ones that one should not be to be underestimated. Within are a group of unknown individuals whom were known to be dead but now are once again a thriving unit. Four men greatly underestimated and underappreciated. These four men are all veterans, men whom have breached upon all opposing souls throughout their careers. It has come time, now, however, that they must once again run all oppositions. To begin that journey, they are seated at the
Castle of Lost Souls. ]
[ In a dark corridor the sounds of footsteps can be heard as there is nothing to be seen as the Darkness holds the light out of the corridor. The red carpet lays on the ground as the light of the camera lights up the surrounding areas but does not extend that far. The steps come closer but yet none are seen, and a voice booms through the corridor. ]
[Voice] I wander all night in my vision, stepping with light feet, swiftly and noiselessly stepping and stopping, bending with open eyes over the shut eyes over those that are employed in this very federation.
Wandering and confused, lost to themselves, ill-assorted, contradictory, pausing, gazing, bending, and stopping. This other soul wanders around not knowing what is coming next or the last thing that occured. Charles, it was you that I once trusted and it is you that once deprived me of what I hoped to get in return to my trust. Though the rather large events and most important are remembered but those small ones are easily forgotten. You attempted to defeat me and win the battle, but the battle, Charles, continues to remain far from over. You see, it was me who laid you down to rest with the rest of the souls that treated me wrong. How solemn you look there, strectched and still, the body state of rest is what you are in and the easiness for me to take your soul. But that is too easy, for I like to battle for your soul. Take him when he is awake and seeing what is about to happen, now when he lay helpless as I stand above him while he sleeps. The battle remains, for with the re-birth comes battles from the past, and how far you may have forgotten, yet I continue to recollect. How quiet his breath, this little child in his safe cradle. The wretched features of ennuyes, the pale features of corpses, the livid faces of drunkards, the sick-gray faces of the dead that these men look like in the eyes of the Darkness. You have looked into these eyes before, Charles. These eyes of tiredness, of pain, of distrust, and of sickness. The gashed bodies on the battle-fields are what I plan to leave behind as I move up towards the accomplishment of my new quest. The insane in their strong door rooms, the scared idiots that think that they can grace this federation with their presence alone, and the new borns emerging from the gates in search to take their place amoung those dying that were just coming through the gates, the
Darkness prevades them and infolds them. You, Charles, fit into this category from now until forever, for I will not rest until your souls lies within this
Castle. For now, Charles, may you rest within your small confinments, for no one shall be held responsible for your soul ...
[ The camera fades out, and although the identity was hidden, the true self-form was brought back to life. ]
