Thirteen would leave the family home at the age of seven to pursue the life of a crime fighter. What he became instead was a ninja working for an organization hell-bent on world domination. He would've become one of it's most merciless agents, if it weren't for one of El Santo's movie hits he found during training. He would later renounce their ways and follow his true path through lucha libre; however, the family he left behind would always stay in his heart.
To that end, he hired his sister to act as road agent, only to realise what we all knew all along… that all little sisters are a pain in the neck.

In his bag are various small fruits and vegetables that he picked up from a local vendor, and the camera moves follows along as he enters a department store. Some punk kid laid out
Neige Thirteen from behind using a steel chair.]

Jersey title! You know, just like everyone else, because that's how things work out in the GSIW, right? Neige Thirteen's sign: "DEUS EX MACHINAS SUCK!" [And at this point the flash could very well end right there and it would be the cleverest piece of video on air. Behold the ninja: although more often then not touted as an empty-headed spaz, Neige's clear-headedness can be seen as a gift that allows him to reduce the most complex issues and strip them of their superficial aspects, revealing them at their simplest form; written for all to see on a piece of 20 by 30 inch cardboard. Doesn't do a damn thing for headaches though, so Neige buys an extra bottle of Advil and storms out of the market with groceries in hand. Cue the voiceover again.]
Voiceover: It's a given that Neige is not a happy camper. After the example set by last week's show, and the PPV before that, Neige's life has been a continuous nightmare of endless chair shots and surprise attacks from untalented but over-eager wrestling fans that have understood what it takes to be in the GSIW main event. Neige's daily strolls along the Jersey parks have been a veritable pain lately, what with the ninja having to dodge all sorts of errant attacks from everyone from passing joggers to little old ladies walking their dogs. And Neige Thirteen is sick of it all! But what can he do about it?
Nothing, because I'm about to shoot him in the neck with a nerve paralysing dart. [Cue the dart. Neige Thirteen is hit squarely in the neck and quickly falls on his back while his oranges roll out on the street. Passers-by would stand and gawk at the fallen ninja, if it weren't for the fact that they had something else to stand and gawk at. Namely, a shapely female with raven hair dressed in an all-black leather outfit sashaying this way. Coming from the voiceover booth behind the camera, she soon walks over to the fallen body of everybody's favourite ninja.]
MF: Neige dah-link! We really haf to stop meetink like dis! Miss Friday: Back to business: it would appear as if the only way to keep the #1 contenders spot you haf earned for yourself is by draggink an inept American along with you to victory. But even then you LOSE the spot which you have fought for, as you will have to face the very man you've helped attain the top spot. Tell me Neige, can you explain to me the point to all this?
[Close-up on Neige Thirteen's face. Even though his body seems paralyzed, his eyes registers the shock at the question. Miss Friday continues.]
Miss Friday: It is becomink painfully evident that your trust in the Mr. Kutler has been misplaced. He has screwed you, my dah-link! Stolen your #1 contendership even before the match begun! MY suggestion is that you screw him right back: miss the match! Think about it: If you fight, all the efforts you would throw in the battle would only serve to get your enemy closer to your own goal. By not appearink, you guarantee a victory to the Misery and the Outlaw.
And who will be the ninja that will not have been pinned? YOU! If Outlaw defeats Misery, you remain the #1 contender simply because you are the only one whom he has faced and not cleanly defeated! If Misery defeats the Outlaw, then
Misery owes you for his chance at gainink the belt, not to mention the fact that Keenan has been shamed twice and Outlaw soundly defeated! Now do you understand, my ninja? Farewell Ninja, we shall meet again...
[Miss Friday leaves Neige Thirteen to his trance and sashays her way out of sight. Neige eventually recovers from the mild poison, and attempts to recoup what groceries he can muster before going home.]
