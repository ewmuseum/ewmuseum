USCW Flash Central 5/5/98 Pt. 1

6 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sinister The Commonwealth of Pain Viper Jacob Yoder The Demolitionist -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (The scene opens up showing a group of homeless men gathered around a fire which has been built in a back alley next to the USCW building. The men surrounding the fire cover themselves in old sheets and blankets to keep warm and discuss useless stuff. Five bums are present around the fire, one who has just come to the alley this day and his features are hidden behind his coat and hat.)
Bum 1: ...... that's when I took her dog and pumped that ass! Bum 2: What a riot! You tells the best stories pal! Bum3: Yep. Did you guys get a sneak peek at that wrasslin show through the hole in the wall the other day? Them security guards always be watching it. If ya look real hard you gets to see most of the TV.
Bum 4: How about you new comer? You like wrasslin? (The fourth bum lifts his head up just enough to reveal his mouth, which is stuck in a wicked smiles. He merely laughs and looks back down.)
Bum 3: Well now, that ain't a very polite. You respect your elders young man. We been in these holes for a long time and we......
Man: Shut the fuck up and go back to your stories old man. Bum 2: What did you say? it seems you need a learning of the worst kind! (The second bum smacks at the mans head and knocks his hat off. The man look sup revealing himself to be Sinister. he snarls and glares at the
Bum.) Bum 1: Hey ain't you....... Sinister: Hell on earth, you got it. (Sinister grabs the second bum by the face and slams his head into the fire. The man lets out a muffled scream and the other three men try to pull him away.)
Bum 4: Gods! Someone get some help! He's burning alive!! Sinister: Charbroiled Bum, yummy. (Sinister lets the man go and he rolls over and goes limp. Sinister reaches out and grabs the third bum and slams his head into the brick wall so hard the wall cracks down the middle. He grabs the fourth bum as the third falls to the ground with a thud and stiffs him into a trash can and seals the lid on tight. he then kicks the can over and rolls it into the street where it is struck by a car.)
Sinister: Well now, what to do with you... (Sinister grabs the last Bum by his hair and drags him into the street. He points to a pile of dog shit someone forgot to scoop up.) Sinister: Eat it old man. Your always bitching about being hungary. Bum 1: Please! I beg of you, don't........(Sinister slams the mans face into the crap and forces the man to eat. Sinister looks up at the camera and grins.)
Sinister: Well now kiddies, you had to come and interrupt my fun. nothing better than picking on the defenseless. Call it being a bully. Call it cowardly. I call it tons o' fun and a level 8 on the Sin-o -meter of carnage. (Sinister kicks the man in the head knocking him out and begins walking down the street to the USCW building entrance.)
Sinister: Well now, everyone seems to be in an uproar over the recent happenings int he USCW courtesy of myself and the undisputed Champion of the WOOOOOORRRLLLDD, the snake man, Viper. I told each and every one of you kids at the start that this was the way things were going to go down.
Unlike the SWA we do not seek to control a federation, no, we seek to destroy it. We are like a cancer, we kill everything we touch, destroy dreams, and crush lives. Now that we have the World title, what's next?
We are at the very top with so very few to stand against us. Storm will try. He will call the SWA all around the world to come help, but the SWA has never amounted to much against us. The Marauders are gloating about their chance at the belt, but Chrissy poo will end up taking the rest of his Marauders right where Homicide took the hWo. Did you see what we did to the first stable that opposed us Chris? Your fight is not only with
Viper, but me as well. People in this federation seem to be taking me for granted. They are overlooking me, looking past me to Viper, and I would have it no other way. What better way to plot and scheme the downfall and destruction of every member of this federation than to have them all ignoring you? Of course Storm will never ignore me, I have done far too much to his sorry ass and his family as well over the past couple of months. If the hWo ever gets out of the hospital, i doubt they will be so eager to jump at me again. Now I still have a bit of pay-back to deal with in this federation. I never got to finish off the Bishops annoying ass and Danny Dare is about, and we all now I got no love for him.
(Sinister turns to enter the building. Workers look at him and turn quickly to move away. Sinister moves into the elevator and heads to the top floor.)
Sinister: Now it is time for a few challenges. the big May Day event is through,a nd it is time for the Sin man to make his way to the very top again. So this is what it comes down to kids. I got a little score to settle with Ron Matthews for putting his hands on me in the Battle Royal, and he is associated with Chris Carter, and that's enough reason to bloody him right there. Then there is the Delinquent, who seems to be a thorn in the side of the members of WTF, most notably Viper. Last time he got involved with our business he ended up on the receiving end of a bat to the skull, and if not for that bastard the Saint, he would be out for good. If the kid still wants to paly, I'm always game if Viper is too busy. I got a lot of time on my hands as I wait for my chance at the
World title, and I am willing to wait for Viper to get done having fun at the top. The thing is, I am willing to take on ANYONE at anytime. I really don't give a damn who it is. If Storm wants to finally settle our differences, fine with me. By next PPV I plan on having him at the brink of madness. By then I will be at the top of the list and he and I can fight it out for a shot at the title. Keep insulting me Storm. See where it got you last time? You have never beaten em and you never will.
not only to i beat you twice, I beat you CLEAN twice. Even the Sin man doesn't have to cheat all the time. As for your little whore woman, I have not forgotten about my threats, so be sure to take them to heart little girl. Remember what happened to your sister........
(Sinister laughs at the top of his lungs and the elevator door opens up slowly. he moves into the hallway and towards Mr. Spicer's door. Seeing it still smashed to pieces, he peers into the room and notices a note on the wall next to the door saying "Mr. Spicers office has been moved down the street till repairs are finished." Sinister swears under his breath and turns and disappears down the stairs. The camera fades out as he exits the USCW building and heads down the street.)
