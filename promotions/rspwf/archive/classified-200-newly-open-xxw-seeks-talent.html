XXW Application --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- 1. Kayfabe Story (Fake Story) An elderly owner of a bingo hall made the biggest business decision of his life when he sold his bingo hall to a 34-year-old sharply dress businessman for a lump sum of cash. This man’s name “The Outlaw” Ty Houston. Back by his fathers wealth Ty gets what he wants when he wants it even if means he has to lay down a lump sum of cash. He took the old bingo hall and decided to turn it into a strip club. But he decide he wanted to expand and live his life long dream of running a wrestling federation. He decide to expand his strip club, “X-rated,” into the abandon building next door. So he knocked the wall down in between the two buildings and connected the two to form his life long dream federation, “X-tremely X-rated Wrestling.” Ty figured that he would give the men their top three needs…Tits, Beer, and Wrestling. 2. OOC Story My name is [name withheld]. I roleplay a lot and have been in this “business” for eight years. I decide to try my luck with opening up a fed and I present to you X-tremely X-rated Wrestling. I have had success handling characters in many different federations and you might have seen my work as Hell on Earth {The Rebel & Blacktop} or some of my recent work with The Rebel. 3. XXW Staff A. The Cartel (Top Officials) I. “The Outlaw” Ty Houston {President/Owner} Born to a wealth oilman and his wife on September 23, 1968 in a small town in Texas. Ty is former SPW World and Tag Team Champion but during the prime of his career he suffered a serious neck injury which forced him to retire and relinquish the SPW World Heavyweight Championship before he realized his full potential as a wrestler. Ty fell back on his business degree, which he obtained at the University of Sam Houston. He tried his luck as a regular business man but he felt something was missing that his life was not complete just working 9 to 5, sitting behind some desk in some office building in a three piece suit. So he quit his 9 to 5 and decided to venture out on his own. After a huge business deal with an elderly owner of a bingo hall Ty had a place to build his dream. Ty opened “X-rated” a strip club and did well. Ty obtained the abandon building next door and knocked the wall down in between the two buildings and connected them expanding “X-rated” to create his life long dream, X-tremely X-rated Wrestling. Ty figured he would give the men their top three needs, Tits, beer, and wrestling. Ty rarely gets involved with his wrestlers but if he feels threaten that someone is going to kill his life long dream then he will do what he has to do to preserve his dream. II. Christina Love {VP of Operations} Fiancée of Ty Houston. Christina and Ty met while they both were attending the University of Sam Houston. They shared a common interest, wrestling. When Ty told Christina he wanted to go pro she supported him and told him to go capture his dream. When Ty won his first World Championship he asked Christina to be his life long partner, of course she said yes. After that Christina went on the road with Ty. Through his ups and downs Christina was right there. But during Ty’s last and violent feud he ask Christina to please return home because he did not want her to see the man he was about to become. A little worried Christina returned home and about two weeks after being home she received a phone call telling her Ty had broke his neck and was going to have to have surgery and that he might not be able to wrestle again. Christina was flown to Atlanta from Florida on Ty’s Lear jet and was at Ty’s bedside as he woke up from surgery and heard the horrible news. Now she stands beside Ty in his latest endeavor as his Vice President. She has a limited on camera role but is very busy behind the scenes. She believes everyone deserves a fair shake of things but if pushed to far she can be on nasty bitch. III. “Dirty” Doug Stone {XXW Commissioner} Doug is a childhood friend of Ty. Doug was at one time Ty’s tag team partner. They had a very successful run as The Dirty South capturing the SPW Tag Team Championships. But shortly there after Doug disappeared out of the wrestling scene leaving Ty high and dry. Years later after a phone call from Ty confirmed the rumors that Doug was an alcoholic and a drug user and that is why he left the wrestling scene. Now with Doug a devoted family man and clean Ty has vowed to give him one more chance and has appointed him as XXW Commissioner. Doug is in charge of making matches and likes to keep the wrestlers in surprise. Has somewhat of an on camera role but if a match needs to be made expect Doug to show up. IV. Antonio and Sergi Marcelo {XXW Security/Bouncers} They are twins of Italian blood. Both standing 6’6 and weighing in around 275-295lbs. High school friends of Ty. Hired as bouncers for Ty’s strip club, “X-rated,” now are working for XXW as the security staff. These two take no shit from anyone. If you so happen to look at the two of them funny they are likely to punch your lights out. They are there to make sure and guard Ty from enraged wrestlers. They have limited roll on camera but were you find Ty you will usually will find these two. B. Commentators and Backstage Personnel I. Ken Lynch {Face Commentator} Ken graduated with a degree in broadcasting. Ken is a wrestling nut and broke into the independent circuit down in Texas as a commentator. This is were Ty first saw Ken’s talent. Ty lured Ken away from the independent circuit with a lump sum of cash and promised him the number one commentator spot on X-tremely X-rated Wrestling flagship program, Wednesday Night Violence. Ken has studied the art of wrestling since the age of eleven so he really knows his stuff but has a tendency to be a company kiss ass. II. “The Genuine High Roller” Seth James {Heel Commentator} Las Vegas born. This native of the city of sin fell in love with wrestling at the age of eight. Worked his way up through the wrestling business from the bottom. He thinks he deserves more than he has been given. Ty met him in Vegas at a poker game and pitch him the idea of being a commentator for X-tremely X-rated Wrestling. Seth tried of being bounced around from promotion to promotion jumped and promised Ty that he would take the bull by the horns and inject some of his lethal poison of wrestling acknowledge into X-tremely X-rated Wrestling. Seth pulls for the heels no matter how bad the situation is. III. “Handsome” Mark Hanson {Ring Announcer} Little is know about Mark Hanson. He has no relation to Ty or any of the other staff. Some say that he just happen to stumble into “X-Rated” and pick up a microphone and hasn’t put it down. Some say he is a little on the “FUNNY” side if you know what I mean. Some say he is mentally handicap. But the question on everyone’s mind is does he even speak? IV. Chevy Love {Main Backstage Interviewer} Chevy is the younger sister of Christina. Compared to Goldylocks of NWA-TNA fame or Terri of WWF{E} fame. XXW superstars seem to trust her. If she wants an exclusive with a certain superstar who is unwilling to give an exclusive she works here “MAGIC” until the superstar caves in. V. “Wild” Adam Wyatt {Backstage Interviewer} Fresh out of college. Not liked by many of the XXW Superstars because he tends to run his mouth to damn much and thinks he knows all about the sport when he knows very little. Gets beat up more by XXW Superstars than he interviews. 4. Rules I am not going to spend a lot of time of this section because I figure most of you are not new to this “Business” and have general knowledge of the basic rules. Don’t use other wrestlers in your roleplays/promo without permission and running it by me first. Don’t do anything that can not be done in real life. Keep RP’s/Promo’s realistic. Don’t come yell at me about an outcome of a match. Take a couple of breaths and then come talk to me. I am only human and I do make mistakes. I will be willing to discuss the issues involved and try very best to be fair to all. 5. Roleplay’s/Promo’s & Strats A. Roleplay’s/Promo’s Roleplay’s are to be sent to [detail withheld] and will be sent out in bi-weekly form in a flash packet still yet to be named. I believe in quality over quantity, but this does not justify throwing something together and rambling on about nothing in three minutes and expect to win. I look at the mount of time and hard work put into the roleplay/promo. Few things I do not want to see in roleplays: 1. A lot of swearing. Swearing is allowed but must be used sparingly. You cannot use words that you would not hear on TV. 2. XXW will not allow any prejudice against any minority, whether it be racial or against someone’s sexual preference. A deadline will be sent when I sent he matches out for the card. Also you can find the deadline on the site as well. I expect everyone to follow it. I am very strict about the deadline because once the deadline is up I can judge the roleplay’s and decide the winners and begin writing the card. B. Strats I urge you to write strats, but I do not require them. If you do one or not will not effect the outcome of the match. It just gives you the chance to have your wrestler represented the way you want them to be. 6. XXW Events A. Wednesday Night Violence This is the flagship program of “X-tremely X-Rated Wrestling.” It can be seen on Florida Sports Network {FSN} throughout Florida. It’s run bi-weekly and in the late/early morning hours. At press time Ty was in heavy talks with a Pay-Per View provide to carry XXW PPV's. B. Pay-Per Views/Supercards “X-tremely X-Rated Wrestling” holds one every two months. This way it gives us enough time to build up angles and storylines. January: Crime Scene March: High Stakes May: Suicidal Tendencies July: Red, White, Black, and Blue September: Resurrection November: X-treme Warfare Descriptions of the PPV’s/Supercards can be found on the webpage. 7. XXW Titles A. XXW World Heavyweight Championship If you hold this singles championship you are the king of the mountain. But being king is not easy because you have a bulleye on your back and everyone is gunning for you. This championship must be defended once a month, usually at the monthly PPV. B. XXW Southeastern Championship Southeastern Championship is like the Intercontinental Championship. The secondary major championship. If they cannot get the World Heavyweight Championship you better believe they are coming after this one. But are you man enough to hold onto it. Usually defended twice a month. C. XXW X-treme Championship You have to be one tough hombre and pretty sick and twisted to hold this championship. All matches for this championship are gimmick matches. This championship is usually defended whenever Ty or one of his staff members see fit. D. XXW Cruiserweight Championship Little guys need some love too. This championship is for the wrestlers that way two hundred twenty pounds or less. If you hold this championship you are the top cruiserweight in XXW. Usually defended twice a month. E. XXW Tag Team Championship If you handle a tag team and you hold these championships you are the best tag team the XXW has to offer but be very leery because other tag teams are right on your heels. These championships are usually defended once a month at the monthly PPV. 8.
