[Flash] Shawn Parker...

10 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
***Disclaimer*** When you skim through this, please keep aware of something. You are going to notice my opponent is The Rock. Pleas keep in mind this a league where Flash
Tucker is the World Champion, it is the wWw. I say that lends credibility to the wWw. The wWw has been around for I'd say ten months, and has been going through some changes as of late. We have been doing our best to weed out these
"real name wrestlers" (Want to read up on those annoyances about them, go read the [Rants] thread. I have the same annoyance _now._ E-mail [name withheld]
(Flash of course) and he will also tell you about how the owner is trying to make many changes, and is up against someone wanting to keep their real wrestler (The Rock). If he does not, he takes away the webpage for the league.
(He is the webmaster.) wWw is now a BCC fed, and _will_ be all fake, regardless if the leftovers agree or not. I've always wanted some of the people on RSPWF to join wWw. Competition is great, and the sooner we get the people who post here here, the better.
[Takes deep breath] All of that just to say someone named The Rock is mentioned in here. By the way, in theory that could be considered a fake name, considering that is just a nickname, right? I'd appreciate comments, because that is the only way I can get better. [Note: I'd post a CNWL flash, but hey, with all the anti-CNWL sentiment, I want to save you the site of something from CNWL.]
If you want information on wWw, please e-mail or instant message [detail withheld] . Its basically a rebuilding project, and it can only be done with some people like those on RSPWF.
************************************************************************** ******************** [detail withheld] [The scene fades into a shot of the wWw interview area, which has been set up especially for Shawn Parker at a recent house show. A banner is behind Shawn, which reads "SHAWN PARKER: The Present and Future of wWw." Shawn Parker is wearing blue jeans, a tanktop, and sandals. Along side, of Shawn is his manager and lover, Maria. Shawn Parker looks intense as he begins to speak]
SP: We are more than a week away from my premiere match here in the wWw. In a week, or more time, the wWw will see talent personified. When I walk down the aisle of Oakland Coliseum, in front of thousands of my adoring fans, the new era of wWw shall begin.
The man who gets to be loser number one? [shakes his head] ...The Rock. SP: 'nuff said, as they say. You definitely were _not_ what I had in mind to start off my wWw stint. I'd much rather wrestle against someone who is going bring my game to an entirely new level. You just do not fit the bill, Rocky.
Rocky, you might not fit my glorious plans I had set myself, but you will do just fine. Because Rock, it is time for me to teach you a lesson. A lesson in respecting those who are better than you!
[Parker begins to frown quite noticeably] SP: At Monday Night Jericho, you put me in a position I had not wanted to be in. Costing Jack Vars a match has angered me all week. Rock, Vars is better than you. But it will not go in the books as saying that. It will just say, "The Rock d.
Jack Vars. So not only will I teach you a lesson in respect, I will also seek revenge for Jack Vars. Its the _right_ thing to do!
And dammit I am going to do it! [ Shawn looks up to the ceiling, and takes a deep breath] SP: I know for a fact that you are going to come out here, and talk all original about how you are going to do this, and you are going to do that. Smack talk, right? I've heard it all before, Rocky, you are nothing special.
[Shawn makes a motion with his hands, a motion that shows chattering] SP: Blah blah blah, what is it this time Rocky! Is it going to be, "Know your Role, and shut your mouth?" Or could it be, "Shawn Parker is nothing but a candy ass jabrone?" With the little amount of dignity you have left, I offer you the chance to redeem yourself.
I dare you to go without saying any of those "catchy" phrases you came up with. :::cough::: Sorry, must be the damn flu. I know its going to be hard. I know its going to be tough, but Rocky, thats life for you. Enough of you Rock, you sicken me.
[Shawn clears his throat before speaking again] SP: In closing, I have to extend an olive branch to one, Jack Vars. I hope, and I pray there is no tension between us, after that incident on
Monday Night Jericho. If there is anyone in this world you can trust, its
_me!_ Not only that, but I _respect_ you. To drive this point home, Jack,
I will even go as far as to mimic your wrestling style, and beat the living crap out of The Rock. I entered this promotion, and looked down the roster, and at past matches of these superstars. I can't trust anyone here.
Everyone is out for their own personal gain. Greed is running rampant here. All except for two people... myself, and... ...Jack Vars. SP: Jack Vars, we have to band together. The virtuous must overwhelm the masses here. I'd be honored if you would accept my apology for my mistake from Monday Night Jericho. I am man enough to do so. Anytime you need assistance here in wWw, dammit, you know you can call on
Shawn Parker. Maria, you've been silent today, care to share any comments to The Rock? [Maria raisies her middle finger to the camera, and walks off angrily. Shawn, equally as angry moves his head following Maria walk off. Shawn Parker looks back at the camera.]
SP: I couldn't have said it better myself! [Fade out] ************************************************************************** ******************** Mike D. Handler of Shawn Parker (CNWL, wWw, ESWA) "I mark for Jack Vars"
