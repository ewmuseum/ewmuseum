[Critique Repost] "Couch Potato" Carl Lumski

7 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Outlook fucked up... or I should say my news server fucked up by bypassing my post's content to attach some friggin' attachment... so here I am reposting on good ol' AOL...
Anyway, Carl is just a new little character that I've been toying around with. I currently have him in the IWF/WOW, where I'm having a ball. This is my most
recent roleplay with him. I just thought I'd post it to see if I could get any input from anyone on the board... so compliment me, flame me, or ignore me. Do whatever you want and enjoy! :)
[Fade in. Tuesday night, March 6th, 9:23pm. The scene opens in the living room of "Couch Potato" Carl Lumski's Scranton apartment. As the camera pans around the cluttered room, we see that the normal Tuesday night festivities are underway. We see Carl sitting back in his lounger, remote control in hand, enjoying a peanut butter and tuna fish sandwich as he watches his
Tuesday night addiction - Fox's newest hit series "Dark Angel". The camera gets a quick shot of the television. We see a leather clad Max, played by the exotically attractive Jessica Alba, beat up a trio of guys in less than a minute. When the camera reverts back to its previous shot of Carl, we see that he's practically drooling. A look of sheer astonishment comes over his face as he shakes his head...]
CL: I honestly don't think there has ever been a woman on television as gorgeous as Jessica Alba. Man alive, she's hot! That leather outfit looks like it was painted on her! Whew... geez... what a body... but I digress.
[He shakes his head again, heaving a sigh...] I'm not here to talk about TV Land hotties. I'm here to discuss business. Ever since I won the Four Corners match at "Mayhem" last week, I've been on
Cloud Nine. I've felt just like Fonzie after he jumped over the shark on water-skis in that episode of "Happy Days". I'm all keyed up... swollen with pride... and proud of my newfound success.
[Carl points to himself...] Undefeated for over two weeks now, I'm eager to reach even bigger heights here in the IWF/WOW. I have my eyes firmly fixated on a prize - the North
American Title.
[He pauses for a moment to finish his sandwich. Once he finishes, he resumes speaking...] That title has had its share of ups and downs in the last few weeks. When I first came here, it was held by that ill-mannered, degenerate dumbass Trevor
Redding. Considering the fact that Redding is nothing more than a childish, chauvinistic, and cowardly chowder head, it wasn't worth much back then. As far as titles went, it was worth less than the plastic IWF/WOW World
Heavyweight Title replica that Toys R Us has been selling. Yes, my friends,
Redding sucks _that much_.
Fortunately, Redding's supreme reign of sucktitude didn't last too long. It ended a few nights ago at WrestleFest V, thanks to one Alex Cote. "The
Hammer" bested both Redding and "Countdown" Chris Carter to become the _brand new_ North American Champion. The title finally _means something_ again. [Carl looks directly into the camera, cracking a pleased grin...] Mr. Cote, I thank you for bringing some prestige back to the title and I congratulate you on your pay-per-view win. You gave it your all and you came out on top. I think highly of you for doing that. I have no doubt in my mind that you'll be more of a fighting champion than Redding ever was.
[The grin slowly fades from Carl's face. He turns off the television with a click of the remote and continues...]
With that said, I want to make something perfectly clear. I respect you,
Cote... but I'm not about to let that reverence distract me from what's really important in the whole scheme of things. You see, you bring a lot to the North American Title. I admit that. But you don't bring as much to the table as I would.
[He motions to his waist, miming a belt...] If I had that title around my waist, it would _really_ mean something... even _more_ than it does now. I'd take it to the highest point of worth it's ever experienced.
[Carl cocks his head to the side, staring into the camera with an inquiring glaze...] I know you're probably wondering why I'd say such a thing, huh Cote? I'm just some new guy... someone who really hasn't even established himself in the IWF/WOW yet. Well, it's simple. I don't just have the aspirations to be a modern day superhero to. I have the _heart_ of a hero. I believe that
I can do _anything_. I have _that much confidence_ in myself.
[He shakes his head...] But I'm not going to use that confidence to leap over a tall building in a single bound like "The Man of Steel" or to take on a gang of rowdy ruffians single-handedly like Texas Ranger Cordell Walker. I'm going to use it to wrest the North American Championship from your grasp. I truly _believe_ that I can defeat you for that title, Cote.
[Carl holds up a single finger...] All I need is _one chance_... just a single match with you. I want you one-on-one without any possible chance of the Franchise Players or some other kind of low-life troublemaker interfering. I want to _show you_ that
I have what it takes to humble you. Above all, I want that North American
Title... and I want it _right now_, Cote.
[He shakes his head...] I'm not going to sit on my hands ands wait for you to grant me a title match. I'm going to nip things in the bud and challenge you here and now.
I don't care what type of match it is or where and when it takes place. I just want you in the ring with the IWF/WOW North American Title on the line as soon as the powers that be can make it so.
[Carl folds his arms across his chest, nodding...] I have a pretty good feeling that you'll accept my challenge. You strike me as an honorable man. However, in that slim chance that you reject my challenge or, worse yet, ignore me, I will take matters into my own hands.
Much like nerdy Steven Q. Urkel in his pursuit of the lovely Laura Winslow on "Family Matters", _nothing_ or _no one_ is going to keep me away from what I desire. I hunger for your title, Cote. Keep that in mind as you decide what you're going to do next.
[He takes the remote and turns the television back on...] Now, if you don't mind, I'd like to get back to watching "Dark Angel". I'll be looking forward to hearing your response.
[Carl eases back in his chair and watches the television. He rolls his eyes...] Jessica oh sweet Jessica... I can't believe you're really with that dweeb co-star of yours. Can't you see that he's only using you as a beard? Oh well. Sooner or later, you'll realize it... and then maybe just maybe then you'll drop that zero and get with a hero. Dare to dream...
[He sighs as we fade to black...]
