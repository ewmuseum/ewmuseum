[SAMPLE RP] Liam Rogan: I Have Sinned!!!

16 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, and children of all ages... I'm bored :) So I thought I'd post yet another oh so fascinating RP on RSPWF... this one being the follow-up to oft talked about "Liam Rogan/Erin
McCoys" incident in UEW...
All compliments, critiques, flames, insults... they're all welcome and encouraged, too! [The scene opens with a clip from the J.D. Carter/ Alexander Patrick main event of "UEW Anarchy" two weeks ago. Music begins to play in the background of the clip as it runs.] # Hey where did we go, days when the rain came. Smith: J.D. Carter victorious, makes his way back up the ramp as Erin McCoys tends to the fallen Alexander
Patrick. OH NO!!! NOT AGAIN!!!
# Down in the hollow, playin' a new game. Colby: IT'S EL PUERCO!! LIAM ROGAN'S BACK IN THE RING AND HE'S MAKING HIS WAY OVER TO PATRICK! Smith: He just shoved McCoys to the mat and continues to beat away on Alexander!! # Laughin' and a runnin' , hey hey, skippin' and a jumpin' n the misty mornin' fog with our hearts a thumpin' at you... Colby: McCoys gets up and tries to shove Puerco off her man. Oh God no....she just made him angry!!
[BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!] # The Brown-Eyed Girl! Smith: NO!!! ROGAN JUST GOT UP AND SHOVED MCCOYS TO THE MAT!! MCCOYS TRIES TO ESCAPE, BUT ROGAN POUNCES ON HER AND BEGINS TO PUNCH AWAY!!! [BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!] # You're my Brown-Eyed Girl! Colby: THIS IS DISGUSTING!! LIAM ROGAN'S DONE SOME PRETTY LOW THINGS IN HIS UEW CAREER, BUT BEATING UP ON A WOMAN IS THE LOWEST HE CAN GO!!! I GOTTA STOP THIS!!! [Freeze from on a shot of "Erotic" Erin McCoys cowering in the corner as Liam Rogan starts to unload on her with a series of punches. The scene then cuts to a shot of an unmarked and empty auditorium. Eerie church-like organ music plays throughout the place. There isn't a soul stirring in the seats or in the rafters. All several hundred seats are totally bare. However, a lone man stands behind the podium in the center of the stage. It's "The Handler" Liam Rogan. Rogan is standing on-stage with a solemn look on his face. He looks down at the floor of the auditorium with a look that's a mixture of disgust and sadness. He gives a disgusted look to the camera as he begins to speak...]
Liam Rogan: Friends, this last week has been one rocky road for me. I've said some things that I shouldn't have said. I've done some things that...
[Rogan sighs to himself before continuing. It looks as if he's holding back tears.] ... That I shouldn't have done. Sure, at War Games, I went and disrespected the deceased relative of Man... Shim ... oh whatever the hell his name is this week. But that's not what I'm talking about. Sure, I turned my back on my teammates at War Games, too, but that's not what I'm referring to either. I'm talking about something that I did more recently than that ... as recent as last week ... specifically during Alexander
Patrick's match with J.D. Carter.
[Rogan appears to be getting a little bit more worked up. He clutches at his forehead with his one hand while he runs the other hand through his bushy hair. As he tries to speak, he appears to get choked up.]
I went out there ... and ... well, I did the most heinous act that any UEW fan or employee has ever seen in quite some time. I went out there and _cost_ Alexander Patrick his shot at the Colby Cup ... but it doesn't end there. No no no, unfortunately, it doesn't. You see, "Erotic"
Erin McCoys got into my face for doing what I did ... and ... and ... well,
I'm not exactly proud of this. I did something rather ... awful.
[Rogan hangs his head down seemingly in shame. He pulls a mask reminiscent of the one worn by his recent luche libre alter ego, El Puerco, out of his jacket pocket. He looks at it in disgust.]
I put on this mask here and ... and ... after costing Alexander Patrick his match ... well, as Erin McCoys crouched in the corner of that ring, I raised my right hand and...
[Rogan throws the mask down onto the stage floor and spits on it.] God almighty have mercy on my soul... I proceeded to beat away at McCoys like a madman. Punch ... after punch ... after damn punch ... not stopping until Revolution came out there to stop me themselves. Not even McCoys' terror filled cries of "Please stop! No!" could stop me from inflicting the swift and blinding pain that I put forth against her that night.
Ladies and gentlemen... [The camera cuts away from Rogan for a brief second to get a shot of the empty seats. As the camera cuts back to Rogan, he can be seen visibly crying now.]
I come before you tonight to make a confession... a confession for the wrongs that I showcased in UEW during the last few weeks, especially the savage beating of Erin McCoys. I've been spiteful ... sadistic ...
unmerciful ... ladies and gentlemen... I . . . H A V E . . . _S I N N E D_ ! ! ! [Rogan buries his head in his hands and slumps onto the podium. After a few seconds, he rises back up again ... but instead of the original solemn look upon his person, there is a new look on Rogan's face... a look of
_extreme joy_, Rogan laughs to himself for a brief second before approaching the podium microphone once again ... all smiles while he does so...]
Yes, I sinned, ladies and gentlemen. When I repeatedly cold-cocked Erin McCoys in the face last week, I sinned something fierce ... and you know what? I wouldn't have it any other way. If someone were to tell me I had the night to do over, I wouldn't have changed it one bit ... except for maybe a few more crushing blows to the lovely Miss McCoys' angelic face.
See, Erin ... and you too, Butch Patrick of Eddie Munster fame ... you're not messing with your average every day wrestling Joe here. Not at all. Did you honestly think you could go on national television, turn on me and then _badmouth_ me and expect me not to do something about it in retaliation?
[Rogan smacks his hands down on the podium a few times, laughing. He shakes his head and laughs some more.]
Well, if you honestly believed that either you haven't been paying attention to me here in UEW or you're the type who finds the
"Teletubbies" television program too intellectually complicated in content.
Either way, you let your guard down and you got what was coming to you.... BOTH OF YOU. Patrick got his precious Colby Cup opportunity taken away from him and you, Erin ... you lucky little girl you ... you got one hell of a shiner and a dislocated jaw to boot. Ha ha ha.
With Patrick and McCoys sent for a loop, I got to thinking. What's next for Liam Rogan? I mean, look at what he's done so far. In my short time here, I've done everything short of taking a leak on President Steele's head. What will I do? What _will_ I...
the man who crossed the line oh so many times ... do?? Should I bring Ike Turner, O.J. Simpson, and Tommy Lee into UEW and form a Women Lovers Of
Wrestling stable? Should I toss a defenseless dwarf into the crowd before every one of my matches? Or, better yet, maybe an elderly man? That would work. "Ladies and gentlemen... LIAM ROGAN!!!" and then, moments later, someone's senile old Grandpa gets picked up, taken into the ring, and then tossed into the crowd like a sack of potatoes.
No wait... I know what Rogan should do next. This would top any of his previous actions because it's virtually impossible. To the shock of the crowd in the UEW Gardens.... as well as the fans watching at home... Rogan's going to sit Erin McCoys down in the center of the ring ... take off his jacket ... clench his fists ... take a deep breath ... and make Erin McCoys form a complete and coherent thought!
Now that would be really crossing the line!! Imagine the blood squirting out of her perky little ears as she strains and strains to form a complete thought beyond the "right ... left ... right ... left" walking instructions that circulate in her head. That would truly be hardcore ... and way over the line of decency. It would be sooooooo __ME__.
[Still leaning on the podium, Rogan turns around and looks around him ... at all the empty seats and then back at the parts of the stage behind him.]
However, for now, that's going to have to wait. Alexander Patrick and his faithful Cocker Span
... umm... I mean manager, "Erotic" Erin McCoys, will have to linger on the backburner for a little bit because a more important opportunity is coming my way in a few weeks. I'm getting my third and possibly final shot at the UEW
Television Title. Yes, it's still a multi-participant affair but there's a twist to it this time. It's a "Pick Your Poison" match where, somewhere during the match, each competitor involved gets to select a stipulation that will be carried out for a certain interval in the match. With all of that long winded detail stuff said, all I have to say is, "Finally! My type of match!".
[Rogan strikes a ponderous pose as he continues to speak from behind the podium.] Mannifred "Glitz" Lester... Steve Spector ... and the honorable UEW Television Champion himself, "What? Me Worry? What? Me Have
Talent?" Johnny Glamour ... the three monkeys of UEW: used to be frighteningly evil, insane in the membrane, and talentless flake ... three totally different men when it comes to personality, wrestling style, and the stipulations that they will probably choose for this match. Three stipulations that are probably very unpredictable and unique in their own individual rights. That is ... if you're not a quick thinker like I am.
When the time comes I will probably have each of your stipulations narrowed down. Hell, I already have a few somewhat figured out already. Especially with you, LesterGlitz and you, Spector. See, that's the difference between me and you. You guys started out in UEW unpredictable and blazing hot and ended up getting molded into something totally predictable and bland. I, on the other hand, am the most unpredictable man in UEW. I always have been and
I always will be. I'm sorry to disappoint you, guys, but that gives me the advantage in a match like this.
I leave you with this thought. Do you honestly want to step into the ring with a madman like myself? Listen to the fans and how they jeer, "He's gone too far! He's gone over the line! He's one sick puppy!". Do you honestly want to be on the receiving end of what
I'm capable of dishing out? Do you seriously want to risk your well being like that? Look at Alexander Patrick. Look at ... hehe ... look at the bruised and battered Erin McCoys. Do you want to be my next example of terror here in UEW? Well, I guess it's all academic now, really. You signed the dotted line ... you're going to show up at "Holiday Horror 3" ... you're going to step into the ring with that craziest son of a bitch that ever was
... it's only fitting that ... when the time comes...
YOU'RE ERASED. [Fade to blood red.] -- -[name withheld] Quote of the Day: "Good gravy, Marie!", Brett Somers -----------== Posted via Deja News, The Discussion Network ==---------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Discuss, or Start Your Own
