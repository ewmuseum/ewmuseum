~SHADOW OF DEATH! sample rp

20 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[I dream of a world with no power, no light, the smoky haze only pierced by a tiny hole in the sky, just enough to see a little in front of you and see that shifty fellow who was following you duck into the shadows in a stealthy manner, perhaps waiting for the opportunity to pounce upon your unsuspecting body; although, now that you suspect it, perhaps he knows, perhaps he doesn't, and maybe he'll leap from the shadows anyway, so it is imperative that one learns to protect one's self, with whatever force be necessary. Perhaps it's good to be evil.]
[The sky is gone, and the light bulbs don't work. You attempt to light your cigarette, but the lighter won't even spark. It is so dark. Not complete darkness once your eyes adjusted to it, but a darkness so limiting vision that one must rely on other senses for any direction. Up a head, a source of light, like any source of light in this godforsaken world, blinds with holy might. Of course, it is holy might, as it is the glowing cross atop a beautiful cathedral. The centuries-old church is in better shape than the rest of the city, and the denizens flock to it like sheep; like vultures toward a dying man.]
[You approach, too, hypnotized by this solace from the evil world around. This is your salvation; this is the sign the city's waited for. You quicken your step. There's a bit of a cacophony coming from the church. There's a sucking sound, a chewing sound, the din of worshippers all saying different prayers, and when straining your ears, you hear screams.]
DE: "They say the difference of heaven and hell is perception." [The man leading the prayer, Dead Ed, is in heaven. He preaches with earnest passion and a gleeful look on his face despite the suffering of his parish. Food is being gorged upon among the pews, and wounds are healing at a rapid pace; this is a holy place. What further proof do you need than the gigantic crucifix above the altar, the one that looks as if a human Jesus hangs from the cross instead of a porcelain one. And the twin cups underneath him, to catch those bloody tear drops.]
[Finally, the din is quieted, those eating pause, mouths full, as the man turns to the Jesus. All is quiet, and the man listens intently. He turns to the throng, which includes you, and begins a holy proclamation:]
DE: "All sins you commit will be revisited upon you tenfold. This town will be pure for the Second Coming of the Lord!" [You sit, but no sooner do you when --] A child: You stole my seat! He was going to steal my food! [Silence once again reigns in the once noisy church. A hooded man tending to teh teardrop cups turns to face you, but you can't see his face. He slowly walks to you, and all you can dare to look at are his blindingly green eyes that seem to glow. He's much larger than you, and he moves with purpose; he spits in your face. The child sees and spits too. Soon the congregation joins, covering you with saliva and undigested food. Dead Ed sighs at the altar, and you look up. The hooded man produces a knife and takes advantage of your exposed neck and rips a hole in your throat.]
[As you struggle to breathe, Dead Ed stands above you.] Dead Ed: All sins will be revisited upon you tenfold. [you are in darkness. There is nothing but two figures: Dead Ed and the hooded man.] Dead Ed: As you can see, we just don't care. You will bow to us. You will bow to Shadow of Death. With Shadow Warrior by my side (the hooded figure), this joke news group is nothing more than Satan's Plaything. But please, do try to defeat us. I would enjoy the spectacle of your doom.
[Game over. You lose.] * Sent from RemarQ http://www.remarq.com The Internet's Discussion Network * The fastest and easiest way to search and participate in Usenet - Free!
