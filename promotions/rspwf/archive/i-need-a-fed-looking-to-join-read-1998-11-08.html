I need a Fed ! Looking to join......read.......

8 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Nov 8th, 1998 2:37am EST Fade from black. The last Greyhound to Metropolis comes to a noisy halt.... on a empty road eight miles from the city. The cold night air rushes in as the doors swing open. Bus driver: "You sure you want to get out here? I know it's a stop and all, but..." Petra: "Did we ask you to stop or not?" Bus Driver: "Sure..." Petra: "Then mind your business." Bus Driver: "Ok, ok. No need to get all bent out of shape." (Under his breath) "You try to do some people a favor..."
Snatching the driver by his collar and stiring the sleeping passengers: Eric: "What? What did you say!! The last time some one tried to do me a 'favor', my partner ended up in a wheelchair and my wife almost died."
Bus Driver: "Hey! Hey! I'm sorry....." Petra: " Baby, baby... relax! Please! You promised me!" She reaches out her hand to his. Letting go of the driver and and taking her hand: Eric: "Your right. I'm sorry" Together they step off the bus and into the cold night air. Before the doors slam shut the driver yells out to the young couple:
Bus Driver: "With an attitude like that shithead, your not long for this city or this world!"
The bus flies off towards the city. Its taillights iluminating the desolate road. The two begin to walk down the road towards Metropolis. The wind and the creatures of the night their only companions.
Petra: "It won't be easy." Eric: "It never is." Petra: "The've gotten stronger since we left." Eric: "No, not left. Ran. Ran away with my tail between my legs." Petra: "You did it for me. I was afraid... for both of us. I didn't want to see you end up like Robert..... with wheels for legs. I just didn't want to hurt anymore. It all seemed to be a waste, but now we're back. Like we promised each other."
Eric: "We're stronger." Petra: " And smarter." Eric: "Together we'll make it work right this time." Petra: "And you'll win." Eric: "No, we'll win ." Petra: " I told them they wouldn't see the last of 'Eric the Red'. As my mother use to say: 'It's always best to take care of..."
Eric: "Unfinished business." Unfinished Business! December 31st! Live!! And only on Pay Per View! Call your local cable or satellite company. I want to roleplay and Petra and Eric want to join me. Interested? [detail withheld]
