Roleplay inside

30 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, I would like people to evaluate my roleplay honestly for one. Two, any suggestions? Send all e-mails to
[detail withheld] The set-up: I'm the Heavyweight champion and half the tag team champions. Bloody Revolution is coming up and that is a PPV on July 4th. My opponent is Iron
Horse, he is supposed to be a heel in a heel group. He once left the fed after i challenged him and came back when the fed really got going.
The Eternal Outlaws Dhamon ShadowHawk (The scene is an academy in San Antonio, TX. Dhamon throws a guy off the ropes and hits him with a high knee. A whistle blows and the action stops. Dhamon goes over to talk with his teacher and mentor. He tells Dhamon what he could do better on that move and then says to take a short break to address the reporter that is standing in the corner. Dhamon gives the guys a hand signal to come over to talk to him. The cameraman sets up while the reporter fixes his tie. The cameraman gives the signal to go.)<p>
Reporter: In your intense training, I don't know if you seen the last <b>Iron
Horse</b> interviews, but I would to see your comments on some of the same questions put to him. Do you have respect for him and do you think he has respect for you?<p>
Dhamon: Come on. It's obvious he doesn't respect me. It calls me a horrible champ and thinks I'm the worst champion ever. If I am the worst ever, that would put me below <b>Freak</b> who never even stepped in the ring after he won it. Saying I'm worse than that is as low a blow as you can get. <p>
Do I have respect for him? I have respect for anybody who can step into the ring and become the number one contender and get people to think they deserve a title shot at a PPV. However, I don't respect runners. So, I have a mixed reaction with him. He has done both. As he points out, the running part happened a long time ago but should people forget? They don't forget World War
I or II. They don't forget the American Revolution or the Civil War. The history of POW is short and even though this is in the past, it is recorded in
POW's history. <p>
So, do I respect him? Like I said, it gives me mixed reactions. He has been a proven liar and has only fought limited fights against mostly crappy opponents.<p>
Reporter: <b>Iron Horse</b> has stated that you, <b>Big Red</b>, <b>Magnus</b>, and <b>Kooky</b> are the only quality players in this league. What is your response to this?<p> Dhamon: Well that surprises me. He named me, the worst champ ever. No doubt he means that only because I have won two different belts. A win over a champ is always good. About the rest? <b>Kooky</b> is a great showman and has lots of charisma. Whatever he lacks in skill, he can use the rest to make up for it. He was also the runner-up of the championship tournament.<p>
<b>Magnus</b> is good, but not a main eventer. He named him because it is his only good win. He wanted to make himself look good by naming the guy he beat a few weeks ago.<p>
<b>Big Red</b> is big and stupid. He got named because they are stablemates. I could name my tag team partner, <b>Blade</b>, who is had more success in singles than <b>Big Red</b>, but I won't. <p>
The only person left off from the rankings if <b>Brax</b> and he wasn't picked because <b>Horse</b> hasn't beat him. Like <b>Magnus</b>, he could become a main eventer but isn't yet. However, <b>Horse</b> doesn't own a victory over the number three ranked guy so he was left off even though <b>Magnus</b> is ranked fifth. <p>
Reporter: He states you said that you would win just because you have wrestled more.<p> Dhamon: That's funny because I never said that. I never said it guarantees me a win. In fact, I believe he was trying to say he had the experience advantage over me so I proved him wrong. That's the reason I stated that.<p>
Reporter: He has accepted your Ladder match proposal. Any thoughts?<p> Dhamon: I've been waiting for this match to happen. Not only the ladder match, but a ladder match against him. So far in POW, this is my dream match. The
<b>Iron Horse</b> against me in a ladder match for all the ante.<p>
Reporter: He states his advantages over you are size, ring savvy, and ability plus he ties you in quickness and finesse. <p>
Dhamon: Wow, I guess he is hands down the winner. Beats me in everything. <p>
Hmm, size, well, he is three inches taller than I with a pound advantage, but I would say that is not a great advantage. That means I'm built more. In those extra three inches of body, he only has one more pound of weight whether it be fat or muscle. I'm more put together with no loose ends or inches.<p>
Ring savvy? What, he can show off better? Is that what he is saying. He can have that because I have the ring awareness factor over him. No matter what the situation is, I know where I am in the ring and know exactly where to go and when. <p>
Ahh, ability. You don't become champion in two divisions without any ability. No, I'm not saying he said I have none but he is underrating me. His ability is overrated and he shows very few moves. But, some dogs just can't learn new tricks.<p>
Quickness!? How can a train tie anybody in quickness? Or anything? Compared to him, I'm a jet fighter ready to blow him away. But I suppose this train can stick with jets. <p>
He has finesse? Mister power train can be a technician too? Please. Has he ever been to the top ropes? <p>
Reporter: <b>Iron Horse</b> says he loves his job and he has you reeling, what about you? Ever think of a career change?<p>
Dhamon: Oh heck no. I love my job too. I'm more interested in the reeling part though. If that is what he thinks, then he has another think coming. I'd like to know why he thinks that but he doesn't need to take the time to tell me. I'll be fine without knowing. Actually, I thought it was the other way around. I just got him to admit he was wrong and lying and also, I got my match.<p>
Reporter: Do you think it is true that everybody in POW except Blade wants the title out of your hands?<p>
Dhamon: If they got the title instead, then yes. But, would they rather have the <b>Iron Horse</b> as champion? No. He has <b>ID</b> back-up and in
<b>ID</b> are the biggest cheaters ever. It would be easier to beat a honorable wrestler like me than a no good wrestler like anybody from <b>ID</b>.
<p> Reporter: Who is the belt for in your mind?<p> Dhamon: I've said it before and I'll say it again, its for the fans. We won the tag belts, we dedicated it for the fans. I won the Heavyweight title, and dedicated it to the fans. <b>Iron Horse</b> is selfish and wants it for himself and his pals.<p>
Reporter: <b>Iron Horse</b> was seen in a school and all the kids were cheering for him, what do you think of this side of him?<p>
Dhamon: A face? He's trying to be a good guy now? Hope he didn't spend his whole paycheck on getting those kids to cheer for him. I have never heard a
POW crowd cheer for him. And when did he care. Wasn't it him who said that I was trying to win a popularity contest and he didn't care about winning it? If he buys kids to do acting, well, they get some money and experience for a drama club in the future, but he is not a good guy. <p>
Reporter: He says you are distracted, and not focused on him but on <b>CIA</b> and <b>Payne and Misaree</b> instead.<p>
Dhamon: Well, unlike him, I can have more than one thought on my mind. I don't need to think about one topic 24-7 to stay totally focused. He needs to keep thinking about me for two reasons. One, he doesn't have the intellect to think about two things at once. Two, he is obsessed with me. He's a loon.
<p> Reporter: I have a sound sample I want to play and get your reaction to it. Here it is: <I>The Iron Horse: So, there's really no doubt who's the best wrestler, record or not. There's no doubt who the fan favorite is. There's no doubt who the POW wrestlers want to win. There's no doubt who wants it more. There's no doubt who's stronger. There's no doubt who's smarter. And there's no doubt who's head is free of distraction. So there's no doubt who gonna win.</I>
Dhamon: Well, we agree on something. There is no doubt I will win. <p> Reporter: Any response to <b>Payne and Misaree</b> who have decided not to face you Friday and want to go through that tournament you proposed awhile back?<p>
Dhamon: Well, it proves they are scared and huge talkers. We give them a shot and they knew they couldn't win a fair fight. If they survive the tourney, we will face them and still beat them. And it will still be on our terms. <p>
Teacher: Hey Dhamon! I said a short break!<p>
Dhamon: Well, interview is over. Can't stop the training and the training will make it impossible for the <b>Iron Horse</b> to stop me.<p>
Stop me?<p> <font color=teal> You can't even slow me down!<p> <font color=black> (Camera fades to black. Dhamon goes back to training and the cameraman packs his things up. The reporter takes a few minutes to watch Dhamon train while thinking, this guy will also become a legend in wrestling.)
