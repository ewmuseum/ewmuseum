RP- PLEASE CRITIQUE!

8 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
okay heres whats happening. Theres a really obnoxious jackass in my fed who is badmouthing everyone and is a major Val Venis wannabe. He's the same guy
Easyide was referring to in his sample RP. I am usually in a Tag Team called No Remorse (tag champs)with my partener Whiplash but this is a singles match I'm requesting to the prez. This is an interview with me asking the prez to allow this match to go on the PPV. Please tell me what you think and keep in mind that I'm only 13. People tell me I'm good but I want to hear from the pros. Thanks. Later! By the way. I'm Lynxx.
_________________________________________________________ ***Lynxx is standing infront of a giant backdrop with black and red clouds as the pattern. The camera is doing somewhat of a closeup, from his head to his chest. He is wearing a sleevless Garbage shirt and his short black hair is slicked back as usual. He has some stubble on his chin from not shaving for the last two days. He stands there, thinking about something.
Something seems to be bothering him. He looks mad. Whiplash is not with him today. Only Lynxx. Standing alone infront of the backdrop. He reaches up, scratches his neck and then his head. "Could you start the camera rolling now?" says Lynxx. The camera fades in slowly, with Lynxx standing there looking mad and a bit confused. He starts to speak...***
Lynxx> I would like to ask the president of the HWF to allow me to have a one on one match against The Irish Heartbreaker at the Pay Per View. Pay
Per View's are usually mega events that have IMPORTANT matches on the card.
Everyone may think that this match would not be a very good match since there was no feuding before the match, and that No Remorse should fight
Battery again. Well as good as that match against Battery was, and Battery and No Remorse usually have good matches, this stupid perverted Irishman made a BIG mistake by calling the talent of the HWF a bunch of Dino Bravo wannabe's. Well you're one to talk about wannabe's, Mr. Irish Val Venis.
You sick little poor excuse for a lucky charms guy. What I want you to do, is to leave the cockyness and the girls at home, and I want you to show me what kind of wrestling talent you really have. I don't think you do have a lot of talent seeing as how you spend more time making sick video's and calling people fudgepackers than you do training for a match. I didn't get to be one half of the Tag Team champions of the world by sitting on my ass.
I worked hard to be where I am today, and that talent is the same talent that you will learn about in a match with me at the PPV. You may not know much right now, you don't strike me as the brightest guy in town, but there is one thing you will learn at the PPV. I'm gonna teach you how to get beat up. I want to dedicate this match to ALL of the talent in the HWF and everyone who thinks IHB deserves to get tuned by one of the best. I bet you're scared now aren't you. You failed against a wrestler just making his debut. But now. But now you are going to step into the ring, and go toe to toe with one of the BEST wrestlers in the HWF. I am the man who little kids watching HWF on TV want to be like. I am the man who nobody wants to be caught in a dark alley with. There's a reason. There are so many ways I can hurt you. I earn my fanbase by showing them my talent. I don't have to be perverted and sick to get ratings. Just remember who you're fighting. I am no Dino Bravo. I am not one of those losers who are all muscle and can't wrestle. I know more technical moves than almost anyone. I am a mixture of technical wrestling and better than average strength. I never claimed to be the strongest, but I do claim to be one of the best. I also reserve the right to cripple you at the PPV. Prez. Please sign this match. As you can see I am extremely excited about getting in the ring and making IHB scream for his mommy in the Crossface Chicken Wing. I beg of you. Please allow this match at the PPV. I will defend my titles on the next card after.
***Lynxx walks away and the camera fades out on the spot where he was sitting. Another camera fades in and follows Lynxx down a quiet hall. It has white walls and a grey carpet. He walks through a double door into a training center. He bypasses all the workout machines right away and heads straight for the ring. He faces a few trainers in the ring before going to the machines. The camera man decides to cut the tape so Lynxx can do his workout in piece. The camera fades in again quickly to a closeup of Lynxx on the rowing machine. The camera man asks, "What happened to Whiplash?
Will he be in the PPV?" Lynxx replies, "He hurt his knee really bad against
Battery at the last card. It's none of you're buisness anyway so piss off."***
