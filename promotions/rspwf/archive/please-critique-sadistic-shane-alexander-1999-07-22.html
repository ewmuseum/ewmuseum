[Please Critique] "Sadistic" Shane Alexander.

22 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Posters of the RSPWF, Well, I have not done this in awhile so please read my roleplay and give me the worst you got. :) Not really, I get hurt easily. But seriously please tell me what ya think and pick the parts that need help. I would appreciate it very much.
Thank you! --- [The scene opens up to a shot of darkness. Then the darkness is overseen by a shot of a bar in Albany, New York. The bar is very dirty, dusty, and musky. In the front is a long bar stool. Behind the counter is an old man with a long white beard. The bar is almost empty except for a few drunks sitting at tables watching ESPN. Shane walks up to the bar stool, sits down, and begins to speak.]
SA : "Wha'sup. Slow night?" [The bartender answers Shane in a low voice.] B : "No, this is actually a pretty good night. I mean we have alot of customers. Usually we would have no-one by now."
SA : "That is not right, a place like this should be gettin' some good business. You are like the only bar open in this part of town, this late at night."
"Let me try a drink, umm... gimme a Heineken and a shot of whisky." [The bartender does so, he sits the tall glass of beer on a napkin along with a shot glass of whiskey on the stool in front of Shane. Shane looks into the camera, and starts to speak.]
SA : "GSWP... Ever since I have joined the GSWP, it seems as if it has gotten a little bit more extreme. It seems that the
Extreme Division of the GSWP has picked up the ball a lil'." "So what does that mean to you Tood Phelps? Simple. How are you going to size up against the man who started extreme wrestling in GSWP? You can not come close to the Messiah of
Hard-Core Wrestling." "Here is to you Todd, this one is for you." [Shane hold the glass of beer up to the camera, motioning a tip o' the glass, if you will. Then he takes a sips and spits it out. He has a facial expression that shows that the beer must have been skunked for over three years. Shane turns toward the bartender and speaks.]
"What the hell is this? Piss? I mean I come in here looking for a nice cold beer and a shot of whisky to go down with it and I get this piss."
"Anyways, Phelps, that warm skunked beer remids me of you. Why? Simple, you claim to be "ice cold" but in reality, you are nothing but a warm pillow-biter. Always running away from the competition, afraid of what will happen if you stick around. You guarantee me pain and defeat? Well the defeat I can handle, I mean hell, who is to say I am better than you?"
"Wait... no... I _am_ better than you. Sorry. So, a defeat I can _not_ handle. And the part where you say, you will inflict pain onto me? I think you must have fell on your head too many times as a baby, 'cause when you get in the ring with me, the only pain allowed, is what _I_ allow."
"You see Phelps, people talk about a rose among thorns. But if you wanna describe me, you better reverse the words. 'Cause I am a _thorn_ among all you _roses_! Phelps, you think you will use me as a step-stool to your rise to the top of the GSWP ladder. I have news for you. The only way my name and yours are in the same sentence as a ladder, is if I am beating the hell out of you with a damned ladder."
"Phelps, you claim to be "ice cold" but when you get in the ring with me, you _will_ be _melted_...
..._ONE_ WAY OR _ANOTHER_ !!!" [Shane tosses a fifty dollar bill on the table and yells to the bar-tender.] "Here, this is for my drinks. And try to buy a thirty rack and some ice, it might sell better."
