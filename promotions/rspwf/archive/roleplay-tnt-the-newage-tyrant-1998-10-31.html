ROLEPLAY - TNT (The Newage Tyrant)...

31 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(screen fades into a gorgeous fall day, the sun gleeming in the ocean blue sky. The grass seems greener, the leaves crisp, and the air fresh. The camera slowly reviews the scene, and comes upon kids enjoying a skate park. You can hear the Beastie
Boys "Body Movin'" blaring out of a nearby car, as the kids defy gravity like it's their job. A familiar face stands at the top of a half pipe, wearing tan cargo pants, blades, a "Bret Hart - Talent =
Jason Budden" t-shirt, and a backwards tan Mossimo had. He starts his run on the pipe, executing several death defying tricks, and breath taking stunts. All the kids seem impressed, but not in awe, for most of them can do the same. He finishes with an inverted 540, and exits the ramp. He sees the CAWF camera crew, and skates cheerfully up to them.)
TNT- WHO WANTS TO BE ON TV!?! (kids rush over) Hey, CAWF'ers, TV land, this is where I come on weekends. Do you guys ever get vacations? And how come I never get one of those sexy reporters?
CONSPIRACY VICTEM! Isn't that right! (kids scream "DAMN STRAIGHT!" in harmony) I love these hoodrats. But really. This Monday night, I meet a bird, who I have seemingly ruffled the feathers of. Well Talon...I am feelin' froggy, and I just may...no, I think you can pretty well bet the farm that I'll pounce all over your carcuss and hand you off to some homeless hillbilly to pluck, cook, and eat right up.
You see, while you all sit at home on your second hand couches, counting the roaches that roam aimlessly throughout your rotting shack of a home...I enjoy the finer parts of life. Such as this...
(motions twoards the skate park, and notices a sign that reads "Tyrant's Terrace".) Yep, that's right boys and girls, I own the place. I also owned that fancy lil' resturant too. Basterds can't kick me outta my own pad, now can they! But back to our roadkill friend. Talon, you sure ramble alot for a guy who can barely keep up while you trot behind PoE, holding onto their coatails. But, don't fret, in less than two days, you'll be put out of your misery. Although you'll have to spend a few days in the metro hospital, I'm sure you'll fully recover, because I like your attitude. But your pals, well they gotta GO! And as I'm sure everyone will be sad to see them perish (kids scream
"BULLSHIT!")...It's just something I have to do. (fake sniffle). (A kid skates up and tosses TNT a Sprite, and a five dollar bill.) TNT- Wait, I should only have like 4 bucks change... Kid- The dick didn't speak english, so I just borrowed it for awhile. But man, his shotty spoke volumes! I've never skated faster in my life! TNT-......HA! Abu, you silly basterd. When I want my Sprite...I WANT
MY SPRITE DAMMIT! Budden...Budden...Budden...you can't come up with a color scheme...hell, you can't even come up with an insulting t-shirt! I mean you could have at least stolen one of my lines, or mocked me...something!!! But, "I can kick my own ass!"
What the HELL is that supposed to mean! Did you think of that one ALL BY YOUR LONESOME!? Did you lose SLEEP over creating that hilarious line!? I CAN'T EVEN MAKE YOU LOOK LIKE A BIGGER
ASS THAN YOU DO ON A DAILY BASIS!!! And that is just sad! Well ...kiddies..., this is my weekend off...so I'm gonna enjoy. FLEE! (motioning for the camera crew to disperse...and they do) (the camera crew backs away, and watches as TNT skates back to the half pipe, watching for a moment or two, then decides to take to some other ramps and grind rails. The camera fades out.)
