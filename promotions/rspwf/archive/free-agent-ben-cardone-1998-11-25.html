[FREE AGENT] Ben Cardone

25 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera cuts pool side to a beautiful mansion set back in the hills of
California. The horizon shines brightly in the background, luminated by the radiant orange California sun. An abundance of professional looking aristocrats seem to be enjoying the pleasantries of their somewhat carefree lives. The pool is octagon in shape, lined with elegant marble tiles. On these tiles, towels are outstretched allowing many stunning vixens to sun-bathe by the cool breeze of the water. The men seem to be gathering at tables around the pool under flowered sun-umbrellas. Many of them are wearing expensive pinstriped suits, and not suprisingly, Rolex watches and Versace shoes and slacks.]
[One of these men takes exception to the camera's presence, and excuses himself from his table to get up and find out what the fuss is about. As he approaches the camera, he runs his fingers through his formerly short, black hair.
It has a ruffled and cuddly 'just woke up' look, to it. On his arm is a diamond studded gold watch and he wears a pair of tan lacks and a white silk button up shirt. On the top of his forehead is a pair of Gucci mirror shades. He looks at the camera intently and begins to speak.]
And you sir, are you an invited guest, I presume? [He says this with a sarcastic tone, but the serious expression on his face leads you to believe that he just may not be kidding.]
Sir, you'll have to speak up, I have business to tend to, if you're not of any importance to me.
[The camera man promptly replies that he's a veteran ring cameraman for the Hometown Heroes Wrestling Organization. A wrestling promotion which has recently gone on a spending spree, dishing out an ample amount of cash to some new, but unproven, wrestling talent.]
I take it I can interest you in a cigarette? [The camera shakes 'yes', the man takes out a pack of Benson & Hedges
100's
and hands one to the cameraman, also lighting it with a solid gold Zippo lighter.
Suprisingly, he seems like a nice guy. How often does that come about in
HHWO?] Of course, I'll have you know that I don't smoke these myself, I wouldn't dare put 100's of carcinogens in such a physically gifted body as my own. I'd be a mortal sin.
[And just like that, his true pompous colors are shown.] You know, there's a tremendous weight on my shoulders every waking hour of my life, being that I'm Benjiman Cardone. And that weight is a tremendous pressure to me, and somewhat of a driving force that works as a motivation to my success. When you're so gifted and technically skilled in a wrestling ring as myself, every promotion is breathing down your neck, just itching to get a piece of your talent. And every guy with long hair who stands at least 6 feet tall wants to ride your coattails and call himself a wrestler.
But it's a good thing that I see right through these farces and blunders. If I didn't, my wrestling career would most certainly be a whole lot more complicated. If I wasn't so educated, I might allow a wrestler who's winning as of late, such as Billy Kane or Solitude, into the New Dynasty. But because of my superior intellect, it's as clear as glass to me that their lack of scientific wrestling skills will eventually lead them down the road to nowhere, the road of which there is no return and no remorse. I've picked out a group of wrestlers in the HHWO and brand them with the word 'mediocre'. The way I perceive it, anybody mediocre to semi-talented can be a thriving success in an organization such as HHWO, because, well, where's the competition? When you have half-witted buffoons like Akira Yamoto and Lawrence Williams having spots on the roster, it makes you wonder just how much of a privilege it is to be the
HHWO World Champion.
Is there any prestige that goes with that title? [The camera nods yes.] Well if so, I'm yet to be exposed to it. I've only been on ship for three days, and I must say, I'm already bored. What will a gentleman like myself have to do to have some fun around here? What will it take for people to recognize that the guys with all the heart aren't the necessarily the talented men of the World, and that those guys are just second best compared to the emotionless masterpiece known as Ben Cardone? These questions and more will be answered once I make me ring debut, and I sincerely promise that it'll be one for the ages. Ben Cardone is something to take a look at. And the New Dynasty will follow in my footsteps, as difficult as that may be.
The New Dynasty is a group of men, not boys, who are blessed with the gift of technical wrestling. Guys like me, are not only able to put you away with something as simple as an german suplex, but also something as complex as a slingshot swinging vertical suplex into a brainbuster DDT. Not only can I powerbomb a man through the mat down into the depths of Hell, but I can also twist his arms and legs into a such a pretzel that Jesus H. Christ himself couldn't untie. And I don't just do this because I like to be an jerk, I do it because there's money involved. And when money and Ben Cardone mix, it's not like oil and water, it's like gin and tonic. We're perfect for each other. So if you'll excuse me for now, I'd like to get back to my social gathering, god knows it's more important to me than ranting about the HHWO.
[The camera begins to fade out, but then comes back as it's interrupted by --] And next time you plan on coming to my estate, give me a call, first. You know my cell phone number. I wouldn't want you to interrupt something truly important ..
[With these words, Cardone begins to fixate his eyes on a busty brunette with a small black bikini laying on a pink towel. He slowly swaggers over to her as the camera now, finally, fades to black.]
