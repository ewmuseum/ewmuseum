"The Great One" Anthony Gold Flash.. [who wants Gold!]

28 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I'm the handler of "The Great One" Anthony Gold. I'm currently in the
CNWL and I have the big world title shot at the pay per view. I'm kinda wanting to join another federation, I just haven't found the right one. ICW,
EWC and a few others have asked me, but I'm not sure which one to choose. If you like my work maybe give me a call and I'll get back to you all. Thanks..
[Fade in.] [The scene, is in the home of Anthony Gold. The back yard of the 26 year old
Gold. As the camera continues to pan around the area, you can see how beautiful
Anthony Gold's backyard is. Surrounded by flowers, blooming around the area. As the camera is done surrounding the flowers it shoots right back at Anthony
Gold. The next CNWL World Heavyweight Champion is laying back on a long chair.
He has on his blue shades, with swim trunks, and no t-shirt. Anthony Gold is just relaxing laying down looking up at the sky as small spots of sweat surround his muscular body. As the birds being to chrip around his household
Anthony Gold stays lookin' up at the sky as he speaks.]
"The Great One" Anthony Gold: "One week exactly... just one week untill I get to prove myself. Not to anyone, but to prove to myself, why I am the next CNWL
World Champ. Ya' see, I have nothing to prove to anyone, 'cause everyone knows
I'm great. That's why I'm Anthony Gold, and your not, now aren't you lucky?
"The time has flew by already, I can't believe only a week seperates me from stepping into the Chicago United Center, into that ring to put on a show. Guru, the pressure is all on you my friend, ya' know Guru, the reason the pressure is all on you is 'cause your the World Champion. Ya' see, your expected to get into that ring and defend your title, which I know you shall. But the pressure is on you, I know your thinking in your mind "Can I beat Anthony Gold", or
"Will Gold win the World title." I know all sorts of stuff is running throughout your mind, what will happen though? Can you beat Anthony Gold? It's just a simple answer.. No you can't beat me, no one is capable of standing up to Anthony Gold.
[With that, "The Great One" stops lookin' up at the sky and looks into the camera, with a small glare coming from Anthony Gold's shades he speaks yet again looking right into the camera.]
'cause if they try, they'll end up like the rest of 'em lookin' up at the sky hearing the three count. Just like Guru will be."
"Guru, I gotta admit one thing, you did beat one hell of an athlete to capture that title, I admit you did your best, but you see I'm not Rally Jackson pal.
I'm Anthony Gold, let it be known, you don't know much about me, but I know all about you. I've been watching your wrestling tapes and studing you hard. Ya'
see, I'm not the type just to get into the ring and just fight ya'. I study my opponents, that's why I'm so great. Guru, I know what your capable of doing, now by the looks of it..
[The World Champion on August 30th leans his shades down, your able to see his light colored eyes.]
You can't beat me." "Every single day goes by, and people are always telling me, "Hey Mr. Gold, I can't wait till you take that title away from Guru." Guru, every kiddo alive knows I'm winning that title. But you don't seem to think so."
[Gold pauses for a while.] "Guru, as I remember you saying your four wins and zero losses in CNWL. Guess what big man.. The Next CNWL World Champ, which is myself I'm five and "O", now buddy, one of us is bound to lose at Battlezone '98. Now the man that will lose isn't on the camera right now, so we all know who that is. But since I'm bringing up stuff from the past, let me bring up the little song that Guru sang. "Truly Madly Deeply" was that little queer song you sang. Now, I'm wonderin' this, but if I'm wrong you can shoot me, but why does David Cameron let this guy have the world title? He's such a little homo, he acts like such a girl, it suprises me that he doesn't act like one in the ring, even though he's one outside the ring."
[Gold laughs.] "But I'll be doing you all a huge favor on August 30th, I will beat the Guru. I'll do all you fans a favor and beat him to death, so you will _never_ have to hear that homo sing ever again! Guru, I kinda feel sorry for you though. I honestly do. I can't say why, wait yes I can. It's 'cause your dreaming kid.
Ya' see your always dreaming about how you will keep your title, and how you will still be champ after Battlezone. Guru, like they all say "wake up, and smell the coffee." 'Cause what your dreaming about won't become a reality, but a nightmare. It's sad that you think you'll walk out with my title, but you'll hear the words "new champion"...
[The pay per view main eventer pauses for a short while.] "trust me. 'Cause when Anthony Gold says he's walking out of the arena with a win, you better believe I will. 'Cause I've said I would beat Jamison, I said
I'd beat Fletcher, I said I'd beat whoever I would fight in the ring. What ended up happening? I won, just like I said. That's why I'm telling you all right here as I stand. I, Anthony Gold, will win that title, you can bet your house on that. All you fools in Las Vegas that bet for this stuff, put your life on me.. you'll see why."
"All you fat Internet nerds that sit on your ass all day talking about Battlezone '98, and Anthony Gold, remember what I said, I'll walk away with that title.. like I've said before..
'Cause I'm Anthony Gold.. ...... "The Great One." [With that, Anthony Gold suddenly gets up from is lawn chair and walks into his door way into his house, but before he enters his house.. he looks at the camera again.]
"Now ladies, Anthony Gold has to take care of business, just remember one thing.. remember how great I will look with that World title around my waist, start picturing it now.. 'cause it'll happen on the 30th."
[Fade.]
