Random Roleplay -- Nick "The Bulldogg" Demola

12 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: I got bored so I decided to do a random rp for everyone to read, just for fun........Okay. Fine. It's also so that people will notice that
I exist. :-) So sue me for publicizing myself!
(The camera fades into a crowded press conference room in Boston, Massachussets. The world has just witnessed a tremendous win (again) for the self-proclaimed God of Wrestling......Nick "The Bulldogg" Demola. He has added the XWF Xtreme Title to his resume. The buzz in the press room, though, was not about the match itself. It was about the big announcement that Nick Demola said he had for the world after the match. The writers all get quiet as a big Mexican man with jeans, sunglasses, and a t-shirt that reads "Demola 3:15" on the front and "My way or no way" on the back walks up to the podium.)
Juan Vasquez: Hello ladies and gentlemen. As you all know, I am the bodyguard for Nick Demola, "El Rey" Juan Vasquez. Before we get this press conference underway let me suggest that we give a big hand to The Man, The
Myth, The Legend.......The GOD of Wrestling, Nick "The Bulldogg" Demola on his impressive debut in the XWF tonight.
(A couple of fans clap.) Vasquez: Did I mumble? I said CLAP! (Everyone in the room erupts into a frenzied standing ovation.) Vasquez: Okay. Okay. That's enough. I knew you guys heard me correctly. Now, onto the business at hand. The entire world has been awaiting this press conference for a very long time. Everyone wants to know what Nick
Demola has to announce that is so big that every major newspaper, internet writer, and television station has a representative at this press conference. Well, you won't have to wait much longer. Because here he comes......The Man, The Myth, The Legend......The GOD of
Wrestling..........NICK "THE BULLDOGG" DEMOLA!!!!!!!!!!! (Cameras click and fans hoot and holler as Nick Demola walks into the room in typical Demola style. He wears a white cape with a crown on his head.
He also has sunglasses on to cover up the black eye he recieved earlier in the night.) Nick Demola: Alright, alright. Let's get this show on the road. I have many souls to save, so there's no use wasting time on petty small talk. I have a prepared statement to read and then I will field questions from anyone and everyone in the congregation today.
(Nick pulls out a piece of paper and begins reading.) Demola (clears his throat): Ahem. I, Nick "The Bulldogg" Demola.....the
Lord of the Ring......the Master of the Squared Circle......have done many things in my career. In the years before I hit the big time, I was a six time world champion in a California regional federation. Since I have hit the big time, I have been defeated a total of seven times in my career, and been pinned cleanly once. I have also never lost on any pay per view card.
I have taken on men who have been listed high in the esteemed Top 200 list and defeated them all. I have had bloody contests with terrifying opponents. I have had technical wrestling gems against skilled athletes.
I have held titles in every federation I have stepped into. I have done an awful lot in my day. But I am bored and tired. I have done all I can do.....
(Nick flips over the page as the fans of Nick Demola all groan and whisper about this being a possible retirement speech.)
Demola: ......I don't know what is left for me. I have been denied entry into the exclusive federations, probably because they don't want a "newbie"
to come in and dominate, which is exactly what I would do. They don't need another great wrestler, they feel they have too many to manage already.
And it is with a sad heart that I say these things. I so wanted to be accepted into their fraternity. But no more.....
(Nick crumples up the paper and tosses it behind him.) Demola: ......I am not a second rate citizen. I am the Dominator. I am the Original Headsmasher. I have been called by my Lord God to live up to my full talents. But the only way I will do that is by getting into every major federation that I can. So it is with that that I announce my new battle plan. IIWF, MLWO, all of you federations....no longer am I asking to join your feds.....I am DEMANDING it! Why? Because I am Nick Demola and I know that I am the best in the world. I'm just looking for a chance to prove it. Now, are there any questions?
(Nick points to a random reporter.) Reporter: So, Nick, if you join these federations will keep treading the line of blasphemy and downright self-deitation that you have in every federation you've been in?
Demola: Nick Demola's gonna do what Nick Demola's gonna do. (Nick points to another random reporter.) Reporter 2: Are you going to keep being the most controversial hero in wrestling history? Or are you going to tone it down to fit in with the crowd?
Demola: Nick Demola's gonna be what Nick Demola's gonna be. There ain't no hand up Nick Demola's rear end! Nick Demola's way is Nick Demola's way.
Reporter 1: Do you always refer to yourself in the third person? Demola: Nick Demola doesn't get what you're getting at. Reporter 2: Well......you never answer a question with the word "I". For example.....I think.....I know......I hate you Nick......
Reporter 1 (interrupting): You can't go through life without saying "I". Demola: Nick Demola is missing your point. Reporter 2: Okay. When you got married to your now ex-wife Shannon, what did you say in your vows?
Demola: Nick Demola do. Reporter 2: No STUPID! You said I DO! Reporter 1 (interrupting): Alright. What was the name of Bill Cosby's first television show?
Demola: Nick Demola Spy. Reporter 2: No you IDIOT! It's I SPY!!!!! I SPY!!!!!!!! Demola: Listen here. Nobody talks to The Bulldogg like that. Juan, my good buddy, show this man the gate.
(Juan Vasquez gets up, lifts the man up with one arm, and throws him out the door.) Demola: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the beginning of my assault on wrestling as we know it. With the Lord's backing I will bring the Good
Word to all of you heathens out there that do not believe in me. Feel free to jump on board. Become a Disciple of Demola. I will attain superstardom.....with you or without you.
Demola 3:15 -- My way or no way.
