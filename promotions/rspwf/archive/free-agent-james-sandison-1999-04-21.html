[FREE AGENT] James Sandison

21 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[FADE IN INSIDE THE LUXURY FRONT LIVING ROOM IN THE HOME OF CROW new-comer James Sandison, in Dallas, Texas. The room is well-furnished, brightly lit, and very spacious. The camera pans low across the light blue carpet, then it comes to rest on a dark green couch at the other end of the room.
Sitting on the couch is James Sandison himself. Judging by him sitting down,
Sandison is of medium height and a Cruiserweight build. He has got long, blonde hair and piercing blue eyes. He has no visible facial scars or tatoos.
Sandison's face is clean shaven. He is dressed in a light blue CROW tracksuit. The camera focuses in as he starts to speak.] JS: "So, at last, it's almost time. Time for me to make my debut here in Cyber Realm of Wrestling. It's been a long road over from the great city of Newcastle, England, but already I can say that it's been worth the wait."
[Pause] JS: "It's fitting that I came from Newcastle, the greatest city in the whole of England. Because, as you'll no doubt have heard by now, it is very, very true that James Sandison is the greatest. Ever since I took my first steps, at an extraordinary early age, it was clear to all those who knew me that I was destined for fame -- for greatness."
"Here, I hath come to CROW, looking to make my name in the world of wrestling here in the States. Back home, in the *real* country of the world, I'm a hero. Now, it's time for me to show all you American scum exactly who the real master of proffesional wrestling really is."
[Pause] JS: "This Sunday, April 25th, I make my debut here in CROW against 'Superstar' Shawn Saxton. It's fitting that this is the first ever Pay-Per
View event that Joel Tennuchi decides to beam LIVE throughout the entire world for FREE, because it's the event on which a long and highly successful era shall begin."
"I make my debut against another debutant here in CROW, a highly incompetent fool of a man, Shawn Saxton. His up-bringing has been thoroughly checked by yours truly, and he is established as single blade of grass in a large field."
"Basically, Shawn, there are tribes of Indians in Southern Venezuala, as yet to have been touched by civilization, who have established more impressive wrestling skills than you have."
"And this weekend, Saxton, when you step in the ring with the greatest man alive, the greatest wrestler the CROW has ever and will ever see, your only heading in one direction..."
"And that's down." [Fade.]
