Blake Carter [Roleplay] Please read!

5 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Time: 5:15 A.M ] [ Place: Blake Carter's Bedroom ] [ Appearence Description: Blake wears a pair of black shorts reaching down to his knees and no further, Blake has no shirt on, revealing a strong, musculear build. Blake has short black hair, which has blond highlights in it. Blake is
22 years old. ]
[ Scene: The camera begins filiming, as Blake Carter lays in his master bed, fast asleep. The camera zooms in on Blake for a while, until the buzzer on his alarm clock goes off. Blake wakes up startled, and reaches for the button on the alarm clock. Blake then crawls out of bed. He stumbles out of his room. It is well notice-able that Blake isnt an early morning person. ]
[ Time: 5:16 A.M ] [ Place: Blake Carter's Kitchen ] [ Scene: Blake opens one of the cabinits in his kitchen, and reaches for a bowl of cereal. Then Blake closes the cabinit and opens another where he grabs a bowl and sits it on the table next to the bowl of cereal. He then walks over to his table and poors the cereal into the bowl. Next he opens the refrigirator and grabs a 2% milk carton. He then poors the milk into the cereal and grabs a spoon from one of the droors. Next which is quite obvious, Blake begins to eat his cereal. Every so often he speaks to the camera when he does not have cereal in his mouth. ]
[ Time: 5:18 A.M ] [ Place: Blake Carters Kitchen ] [ Scene: Blake is still eating his cereal, but now he is half way done. Blake reaches for a napkin and wipes the milk off of his mouth, then blake pauses from eating and looks up at the camera. After a few seconds Blake begins to talk. ]
[ Blake Carter Speaking: Now, If I understand it correct I have an upcoming dubut with an athelete named Paul "The Bear" Zane. Is that correct? ]
[ Scene: The camera man shrugs, wrinkles his forehead and responds to the question, asked by Blake. ]
[ Camera Man Responding: Yes, I believe you do, Blake. ] [ Blake Carter Speaking: Is that so, well I guess it will be my chance to prove myself in this industry. You know, I may be knew to the business, but you can expect alot from me. I'm not a push over, and that's what I'm here to make known. I'm in this industry to show every one, that I can, succeed. And succeed
I will. I will make my name known. Known as the greatest preformer and superstar in wrestling today. There is nothing that will stop me, I will go to the edge, and back. What ever it takes I am willing to do. I have everything to prove, and nothing to lose. The only thing that I will do is succeed. You can gaurentee it. ]
[ Time: 5:23 A.M ] [ Place: Blake Carter's Kitchen ] [ Scene: Blake stops talking, and finishes his cereal. Once done he throws the napkin away into the trash, and puts his bowl, and spoon in the sink. Blake then puts the cereal back in the cabinit, and rinses out his bowl. Next blake opens up his dishwasher and puts his bowl, and spoon in there, along with various other items. He then starts the dish washer up. Once everything is done; Blake leaves the kitchen ]
[ Time: 5:25 A.M ] [ Place: Blake Carter's Living Room ] [ Scene: Blake walks into his living room and sits on his couch. The couch is navy blue. Blake then turns on the television and turns it to channel nine, to watch the early morning news. After the commercial on the television ends the news segment starts. Blake watches it for about 5 minutes then turns it off and turns towards the camera, and begins to speak. ]
[ Blake Carter Speaking: Well, my first opponent. Paul "The Bear" Zane is it? It seems you will be my first test. A test that I will pass. I have nothing against my opponent, yet I plan to injure Guru permanately physically, and mentally. When that match ends I will gaurentee that my hand is raised. Nothing will stop me, from having that happen. My debut will not be a defeat, but a victory. More than just a win, it will be much more than a win. I have the will, I have the power, I have everything I need to make a name for myself. And you can trust me I will use everything to make a name for myself. ]
[ Scene: Blake then smiles, and chuckles a little bit. He then turns the television back on to watch some more of the news. After about 10 minutes Blake turns off the television and leaves the room. ]
[ Time: 5:43 A.M ] [ Place: Blake Carter's Bedroom ] [ Scene: Blake enters his bedroom to make his bed. Once in his room, Blake notices that his alarm clock has fallen off the table in his bedroom. Blake bends over to pick it up, and puts it back where it belongs. He then continues to put the sheets on his bed. While making his bed he talks to the camera. ]
[ Blake Carter Speaking: My future in the wrestling industry is known. My future is to be a champion. The greatest champion ever. I plan to defeat any one who comes across my path. And my opponent Paul "The Bear" Zane, has now crossed my path. Trust me, I will do what ever is needed to defeat him. No matter the consiquences, I will gaurentee my victory. It will soon be known, that I cannot be beaten. Prepair yourself Paul "The Bear" Zane, you must be in top condition even to consider upseting my victory, which will not, and can not happen. ]
[ Scene: Blake stops talking and finishes making his bed. He then leaves the room, and heads towards the computer room. ]
[ Time: 5:50 A.M ] [ Place: Blake Carter's Computer Room ] [ Scene: Blake sits in his swirl chair in front of his computer. His computer is a Gateway 2000, which was a gift he recieved from his parents, along with this entire condo. Blake turns on his computer, and waits for it to load. Once it is done loading Blake clicks on the America Online Version 4.0 icon in windows. Then Blake enters into the login screen, and signs on. Once he's online he begins to check his e-mail, during the time Blake speaks to the camera. ]
[ Blake Carter Speaking: You know, I've been thinking and I'm pretty sure that every one watching probably thinks I'm over zealous, too cocky, or that I don't have what it takes. That I'm just kidding myself. But, that's fine. They can think what they wish to think. But the truth is that I'm not cocky, I just speak what I believe. And what I believe will soon become the truth. And trust me, I have what it takes. I've prepaired for my debut every day. Ever sense I was born I have been prepairing myself. Becoming mentally, and physically stronger. Every day I become more experienced. Soon the time will come. The time for me to prove myself. Whatever you think, it doesn't matter. The only thing that does is what I think. Because soon what I'm telling you now will become the truth. You can mark every word I say. But trust me, I will defeat
Paul "The Bear" Zane. He is the first step in my journey . . . ]
[ Scene: Blake then finishes checking his e-mail, which takes him about 8 minutes. Blake closes the screen of the mail box. And chooses the optioin, sign off. Next Blake closes the entire program and selects shut down on the start menu. Once the computer is completely shut down, Blake sits up from the swirl chair and leaves the room, and heads for the door to leave his condo. ]
[ Time: 6:00 A.M ] [ Place: Blake Carter's Yukon ] [ Scene: Blake then closes the door to his condo. He then walks across the street to his car. Which is a black, gmc, yukon. Blake opens the door and sits in the drivers seat. He starts the car, and puts in a cd, entitled Hellibully
Deluxe by Rob Zombie. Blake then turns up the sound and begins to drive the car. As the camera man sets in the passanger seat. While driving blake starts to talk to the camera. ]
[ Blake Carter Speaking: This is almost the end of this segment, but the beginning of my career. Soon my career will be sky rocketed. I will become the
Extreme Wrestling Federation champion in time. But first I must defeat Paul
"The Bear" Zane. But then I will move on to bigger and better things. Once I am the EWF Champion, I will have proven myself. Then I will seak new goals. But for now my main goal is to defeat Paul "The Bear" Zane, and that I will. You can gaurentee it ... But, right now this interview has to end because were at the gym. "
[ Scene: Then Blake stops talking, and the camera begins to fade out, until everything is completely black. ]
