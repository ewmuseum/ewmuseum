Personality: Van has always been the underdog. He isn't the biggest, the strongest or even the most talented but he without a doubt has the biggest heart.
Van is what you would call a "pseudo face", meaning he gives his all for the fans but does it with an edge.
Appearance: Van weighs in at 240lbs. and stands at roughly 6'1". He has an athletic build and a golden skin-tone. His dishwater blonde hair is usually kept short and he is almost never seen without a goatee.
Van has a tattoo of a red and orange sun on his upper right arm. Ring Attire: Van's ring attire consists simply of a pair of baggy, black knee length shorts with gold trim and matching black/gold wrestling boots. Van also has an obsession with painting his finger nails black before a match.
