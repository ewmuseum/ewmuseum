[name withheld] ( [detail withheld] ) wrote: : On Sat, 8 Feb 1997 [detail withheld] wrote: : : > : > My questions to all of you: If you have a roleplay based fed, how do you : > decide who wins? : Personally, 80% of the winners are based on the roleplays. Occasionally, I have had to force a rp'er to lose to get a great angle going. For instance, we're having a tag team tournament in our fed. Well, this handler has an additional wrestler he controls, whom he wanted to started a feud with a member of a second team that was in teh tournament. So, to get the fued started. One team ended up eliminating the second via interference in the match (we had a really complicated stable war brewing) and the second got revenge in a later match. Anyway (god i just realized how complicated this got)..because of the various angles involved (there are about 4.) just involving these two teams/handlers (and one of the winning teams via DQ), I decided to write a loss to a team which IS a great role playing team.... They were goign to get a title shot in the near future, but they send the president and myself very annoying letters (almost qualifying as flame bait). Anyway ... I got off track I think.... 80% of our wins are based on rp's and about 20% are on angles....(give or take a few for the "impossible" wins/losses adn the "upsets") Once inn a while, we give a random wrestler a solid push to see if their roleplaying gets better, and sometimes you'd be surprised... we had one wrestler, we got teh occasional 5 line role play from, we gave him a small push, and now, he can put out some really good ones....(He doesn't do it often, but enough to take him off the jobber list). So I guess it varies by fed-heads...but that's ours.. : : Do you ever keep someone from winning because you don't : > particularly care for their gimmick? : Nope! Absolutely not...if it's a dumb gimmick and they can rp, they'll get wins...or at least a lot of heat. : : Do you let someone win because you have : > met them online (ie. chat room, bulletin board, etc.)? : Absolutely not... : : And do you think I am : > right in quitting a fed where I am obviously getting nowhere? : > : If you think you're not getting respect, there are lots of other federations out there, that i fyou're as good as you say you are, would be glad to snap you up. -- ******************************************************************* [detail withheld] Go Sea Wolves!!!!
