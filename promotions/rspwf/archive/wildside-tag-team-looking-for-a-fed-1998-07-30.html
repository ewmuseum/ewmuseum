*WildSide* Tag Team looking for a fed.

30 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*WildSide* walk out to the sound of the song "Wildside" by Motley Crew and slowly walk to the ring. They don't care how long it takes and are in no real hurry. If there is a jumbotron, the screen only says "WildSide Rules" and nothing else.
They slowly step into the ring and either sit down in the corner or stand up, leaning against the corner. The fans are not impressed.)
Ring Announcer - Ladies and Gentlmen, from Wildside, USA, at a combined weight of 471 pounds....Flame and Heat....."Wildside"!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
<Begin Transmission> (Scene opens to Hellfire,the manager of the "WildSide", who is walking through his personal training gym for he and his team. He reaches up on a shelf of wrestling tapes and pulls one
out. He reads the label which is marked "Greatest Wins". The tape has various matches of "WildSide" including the 3 they had with the "Funky Boys" in the EIWF.
Hellfire: Well, Da' Funky Boys, Trash & Compost. Once again, fate has brought us to each other's door steps. Once again, we have to face each other in the ring, only this time it's in a tag team, loser leave town match.
You with your "shocking" surprise (yeah, right) partner and my with my choice of partners. Kind of fitting it does come down to us, don't you think, boys? We both such interesting roads that led us to this match during the Dream Partner tournament. However, you never thought that my team
*WildSide* Flame & Heat would show up out of nowhere and win your tag belts, now did you? (Hellfire places the tape back where he found it and turns back to the camera) Hellfire: No need to watch this, I've seen these matches and studied them plenty of times already. Of course, for myself and the *WildSide*, it's all about business. Right now, it's in all of our best interests for us to walk away the winner of the Dream Partner Tourney.
I'm going to do whatever it takes to put you down, but just know, it's all personal. (Flame & Heat arrive and together with Hellfire, walk to the center of the library where there is a large table with many seats going across both sides with one chair at the head of the table. Behind that seat is the TV/VCR combination which are both currently turned off. Flame begins his message of mayhem.)
Flame: Compost,Trash recommending YOU as his replacement for the Saturday Night Special title match came as a surprise to all of us. Our manager actually seriously considering this came as a bigger shock...to ME, at least. After all, the point of this upcoming SNS is to settle some dispute from Summer Dreams, which just shows that Dumpster, you guys' manager dosen't have faith nor does he stand by the decisions of the referees. So, he tried to get our title victory over you declared vacant. Why
YOU should be
involved in settling the title dispute is beyond me? What have YOU done recently to deserve to take on us for the titles? Certainly beating
Rock & Todd, who apparantly had ODed on Sleeping pills before the match, dosen't warrant a claim on your part to the tag title. No, I think not.
<Flame ceases his message and we here from Heat> Heat: Whether or not, we face at SNS in August, Funky Boys, we WILL be facing each other next week, where the one who hates you the most teams up with the one who knows you the best. What can you expect, jerkoffs? What should you expect? (Heat smiles) Expect the unexpected. For the record, my partner is very much looking forward to this, and damn if I can't say that I am too. We WILL win the Dream Partner Tournament and not even the
"No Worries" Express will be enough to prevent that from happening!!
(Hellfire stands up and begins to walk towards the door and motions for *WildSide* to follow.) Hellfire: See you next Sunday, boys. And may the best, (US)...PEOPLE win. (Hellfire & *WildSide* exit the room as the camera follows them out the door and fades to black) [name withheld] [detail withheld]
