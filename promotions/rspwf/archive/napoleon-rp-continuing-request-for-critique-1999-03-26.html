[Napoleon RP] Continuing request for CRITIQUE!

26 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes! That's right folks! Nassir is so desperate for an actual critique of his recent work that he will KILL WORLD LEADERS to get it! Have bullet, WILL SHOOT! Don't miss this limited offer, CRITIQUE NOW!
Has the catchphrase "And that's an order" been used in RL before? **************** [SCENE: Simply... Napoleon, the 4'4" 115pound Militant Mini himself! Napoleon is, shockingly, sitting in a cloth folding chair, behind which is a grey wall. He is dressed in his full military uniform, with his legs crossed and a large unlit Cuban cigar chomped between his teeth. He removes the cigar and gestures with it.]
Napoleon: Well I can say one thing about this Boltz character. He's not stupid. It seems that the five entire minutes he's spent in a classroom during his pointless little existence must have been during a history lesson. This has proven to be really quite fortunate for him, because now he is able to tell us, with absolute 100 percent certainty, that the original Napoleon is _DEAD_!
[Napoleon jams the cigar back between his teeth long enough to clap slowly and deliberately a few times.]
Napoleon [sarcastically]: Oh well done Boltz. Very well done indeed. Now, on Sunday the 28 when you climb in the ring with me, you can amaze the _entire_ crowd with your exhaustive knowledge of _DEAD_ people. Meanwhile, I, the present _Napoleon_, will be very much alive and kicking.
Kicking your oversized ass from ringpost to ringpost, that is. [Napoleon leans back and smiles rather darkly.] Napoleon: Yes, I really must give Boltz some credit. He also knows all too well that if he should lose to me, be pinned for the 1-2-3 by a
_mini_, that he'll be a complete laughing_stock_. For the rest of his life people will point to him and whisper, that's the guy that lost to the mini. That's the guy Napoleon pinned. You know, 4 foot 4 inch 115 pound Napoleon. You'll never be able to walk down to the ring with your head up _ever again_! You've got a _damn_ lot riding on this match, and you _know_ it.
But for some reason, for some _ODD_ reason, I find it very _very_ difficult indeed to feel sympathy for you. Maybe it's because, oh I don't know, I'M A MIDGET! Maybe it's because, due entirely to the efforts of the oppressive and elitist pro-wrestling establishment, I simply am unable to go any lower. You're afraid of hitting the bottom of the barrel, Boltz? I've _been_ there, and I've _done_ that, and to be perfectly honest with you,
I'm kind of sick of it. So I'm going to stop it. Starting, with _YOU_! I'm going to beat you, and I'm going to pin you, and I'm going to become the _FIRST_ mini in the history of this great sport to defeat a _full-sized_ wrestler.
[Napoleon gestures with the cigar and El Pyrato dashes onto the screen waving a Zippo lighter. He lights the cigar, and then continues flicking the lighter on and off, on and off. Napoleon waves him away in annoyance. He takes a few pensive puffs from the cigar, and blows a tidy smoke ring. He watches it dissipate in the air.]
Napoleon: So, Boltz, you're afraid of losing your lofty reputation? You're afraid of being humiliated like you've never been humiliated before? You're afraid of being laughed at? Well you should be. It's time to stop worrying about the _DEAD_ and start worrying about _ME_, because come Sunday March 28, OWF Sunday Inferno,
_YOU'RE_ going to make me famous. [Napoleon grins, from ear to undersized ear.] Napoleon: And _THAT'S_, an order. [Napoleon throws his head back and laughs uproariously. The cigar smokes in his hand. Fade to black.] -- [name withheld] AKA: Rantai (IRC/AIM) Tequila!
