[Free Agents] The B.A.M. & "New York" Donny Broadway

1 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey there, I am the former fedhead of the now defunct SSCW, and since that rave reviewed fed went under, I haven't done much of anything in e-wrestling. I'm looking to change that, however, with your help :-)
I have a singles wrestler and a somewhat famous tag-team that I'm digging out of retirement. The Tag-Team is known as B.A.M. Or, The Bad Ass
MuthaF*kas, they were #13 on the 1998 RSPWF Tag-Team 200, and #11 on the 1999
RSPWF Tag-Team 200. They had most of their success in the AWMC (or now known as AGW).
My singles wrestler is a newer creation with a lot of attitude and humor involved in his RPs that I've written thus far.
What type of fed am I looking for? I don't have many real needs, but overall, I'd like the fed to be ran on e-mail, hopefully with a site.
Exceptions can be made, of course. For B.A.M. I would like an established fed that has been around for at least a month. The following RP is the only one I can find in my box. Please note that this was done in mid 1999, and I like to think I've gotten _much_ better since
then :-) [The screen is totally black. Nothing is on your screen. Suddenly, an intimdating voice comes of your speakers.]
Voice: You know somethin', AWMC is in fo' somethin'. It's in fo' somethin' _beeg_. [The screen suddenly shows a clip of a black man doing a triple jump moonsault, while embracing a chair over his chest. It returns to black.]
Voice: And yo, I don't mean this in just the figer-aye-tive sense. [ It now opens to a clip of a larger black man powerbombing a poor fool onto a stack of chairs. The man then swings up his arms in a fit of rage and excitement. Despite his girth, the man is rippling with muscles. ]
Voice: Yas sees, AWMC, a little ole tag-team has just reared it's head.You may have heard of us, you may have not... but one thang is fo' sure, you aint
_NEVER_ gonna forget us...
["CRSSSSSSHHHHHH." "Natural Born Killaz" by Dr. Dre and Ice Cube begin to bump through your speakers. As the man says his last words, we see a sudden caloge of various clips of the same two men. Most noticeable, the bigger man holds a man in his clutches while the smaller kicks a chair in the opponents face, a legdrop/powerbomb combination through a table. And finally, the bigger man lifts an opponent up into a powerbomb position as the smaller comes off the top rope and delivers a viscous clothesline. The opponent is flung into the air, and flips straight to the ground, lucky that he didn't land on his neck. ]
[A huge gunshot is heard.] [The scene now fades into the back of an arena, where apparently the AWMC has just finished doing a house show as we can see the AWMC trucks and ring crew putting things away. The sky is a darker blue, just before sunset. A door swings open and two men come bustling out. One is bald, black and smaller then the other. He has a dirty torn shirt on, a pair of black jeans, a pair of red nikes, and a bloody bandage barely sticking to his head. He is currently unwrapping the red tape from his right fist and rist. The bigger man is putting on a red t-shirt and is wearing a pair of black pants and black boots.
He notices the camera and grimaces at it. The two both have their bags hanging over their shoulders.]
Smaller Man: AYE-DUBBBAYA -Em- See, take a ganda, take a peek, take a look at what it is you seek.
Ya sees, AWMC has apparently been lookin' for some assistance in it's eva-present quest for tag-teams. While tourin' Jay-pan, we got a phone call from an old TWL buddy of ours that this Aye-Merican fed outta Detroit was lookin' for some tag-teams.
We said what the hell and we bounced ourselves out of Jay-Pan and all the way to Daye-Trizoit. America aint nothin' new to us, we've been blastin' holes in this continent fo' far too longer then I care to speak, and now AWMC is the newest addition to our resume... Or should I say hitlist...Tell 'em Lance.
Lance : (In a much more intimadating, and slightly raspier voice) YA DAMN
STRAIGHT! THERE AIN'T _NO_ FU[BLEEP]IN AROUND WITH THE B.A.M. THERE AINT NO
FU[BLEEP]IN AROUND WITH MICHAEL SPEARS... AND THERE SURE AS HELL AINT NO
FU[BLEEP]IN AROUND WIT LANCE JORDAN. See, We've been bustin our asses, _EVERY_ night and day for the past two years, and we've enjoyed every bit of it. From the losses, to the wins, and back on down to the losses, we kept on fightin'.
Michael Spears: And our trademark isn't any wishy washy fireworks, it isn't any kiddy lightshows, or bright tights... Our trademark is that _EVERY_ opponent we ever face, regrets the day that they ever decided to get in our way. In _our_ quest.
Lance Jordan: OUR QUEST TO BECOME THE CHAMPS! Michael Spears: Ya dayum right. Lance Jordan: And the quest starts this Wednesday, when we take our 500 and something pounds of whoop assin' selves, into the ring wit' two CHUMPS, who don't know the meaning of B.A.M... but soona or later, _everybody_ learns what
The Bad Ass MuthaF*kas are all about.
[The two come towards a beaten down station wagon. This vehicle, no doubt a rental from the AWMC.]
Michael Spears: God dammit. [Opens the passenger side and throws his bag in the back, as does Lance Jordan. Spears decides to sit in the seat while Jordan hops up on the hood of the car, substantially lowering it to the ground.]
Michael Spears: Damn man, have you been losin' weight like I told ya. Lance Jordan: If I told ya once, I told ya a thousand times. I'm big boned. You know, all that genitical sh[bleep]t.
Michael Spears: [pauses and looks at Jordan.] ANYWAY, let's get this shindig started. Who we facin'?
Lance Jordan: Brian Corlet and "El Legacy" Shawn Johnson. Michael Spears: Ah yeah! I thought of this last night... Watch dis', ya ready? Lance Jordan: Yeah, yeah, yeah, man I'm ready. Michael Spears: Alright then. *Ahem*! "The Legacy" Shawn Johnson, ooooh whoop-dee-doo. You know somethin' MAN, I couldn't give two sh[BLEEPS] from Friday what two-cent ToysRUs titles you've held in the past. Because I know when you step into that squared circle, manos y manos, with you' littlle butt buddy slaggin behind...When you can barely stand cuz ya've pissed yo' pants and you're knees are a rattlin' like theres no tomorrow, you aint got no chance.
Lance Jordan: CUZ _THIS_, BITCH, is the new era in AWMC tag-team 'rasslin. This is what it's all ABOUT! And as far as I'm concerend, you and your punk ASS friend and got much but a lot of hope.
Michael Spears: You're so lucky that you don't compete in the tag-team scene on a regular basis, otherwise, those pathetic lil comments you dropped would've had you in a whole _SHI[BLEEP]_ load a trouble!
Lance Jordan: Wednesday Night, consider it Judgement Day, biatches. Michael Spears: The Bad Ass MuthaF*kas are here... [The scene slowly fades as both men look into the camera.]
