RSPWF Card Archive

introductory roleplay from The High Flyer.

6 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OK guys, I would like you to tell me if this next role-play is any good, good enough to compete in the big feds and RSPWF. Of course, criticisms are welcome.
-Start of role-play- (The camera shows a shiny colour of black. It zooms away to show the black is a part of a leather sofa in the middle of a well furnished, modern apartment in Connecticut. A man is sitting on the sofa, he is wearing rectangular sunglasses with a silver rim which sparkles in the light. He is wearing short denim pants and a sports jacket which is open, revealing a tanned muscular chest.
The man has a gold belt over his shoulder, the camera zooms in on the belt. It reads: JECW Television Champion. The camera zooms pout again and a caption appears at the bottom of the screen:
<"The High Flyer" 2 time Television Champion, Intercontinental Champion and number 1 contender for JECW World Heavyweight Title.>
Above The High Flyers head are his achievements, all in a glass case mounted on the wall. The High Flyer looks towards the camera and opens his mouth to address an awaiting public.)
The High Flyer: "To all those pieces of not-me crap who don't yet know you I am, let The High Flyer introduce himself and give you people some knowledge and background information about wrestlings next big thing."
(A captions flashes on screen saying "The Early Days".) "Early last year The High Flyer joined a federation named the GWA. Upon joining The High Flyers potential was recognised and he was put straight to the top of the card. In his first match though, to some masked freak named
X, The High Flyer somehow lost. Then The High Flyer entered a feud with a certain Chris Brick. Chris Brick was, if you'll allow The High Flyer to be so frank, a rip-off of some dude named Jericho and was also close with the boss of the company, which meant The High Flyer was screwed out of numerous victories against him. Soon after Brick defeated him, the GWA closed down and Flyer, perhaps still a little green, moved onto a federation named
Johnny Extreme Championship Wrestling."
(A caption flashes on screen saying "JECW") "Within days of joining the damn fed The High Flyer, after being drawn number 1, simultaneously won the Royal Rumble match at JECW's first PPV entitled 'Nuclear Winter'. This earned The High Flyer a shot at the
Television title the next night on Monday Heat against the other two winners, Dario 'Don' Giovanni and PWW legend Mav 'Playboy' Richards.
Unfortunately due to an injury suffered at the hands of a freak named Body
Count, The High Flyer made the triple threat main event into a singles match at the end of the show.
Weeks passed and The High Flyers popularity and in ring skills grew immensely and on January 9th 1999 The High Flyer was scheduled to fight then
Intercontinental Champion Body Count in a 'Buried Alive' match."
(A caption flashes on screen saying "January 9th 1999") (A clip is shown of a man named GALACTUS powerbombing Body Count into the grave in the midst of a face versus heel brawl during the 'Buried Alive'
match. The High Flyer, being the loveable rogue he is, gets up and sees the action, refuses offers from the faces to join the fight and takes a shovel to win his first singles championship.)
"So basically in that clip you saw how The High Flyer oh-so cleanly beat
Body Count with no help whatsoever from anybody. After that win The High
Flyer was once again propelled into superstardom but a very damaged right knee was instrumental in The High Flyer dropping the Intercontinental title to Mad Dog McGrill in a ladder match and his elimination from a Battle Royal to crown a new JECW World Heavyweight Champion."
(A caption flashes on screen saying "The Moment Of Madness") (A clip is shown of a press conference in early February in which The High
Flyer finds out that Body Count is the number 1 contender for the JECW World title. The High Flyer becomes enraged by this and tips his table over the edge of the stage, it lands on a young woman who is later diagnosed as crippled for life.)
"Damn tables, don't make them like they used to. Now if it wouldn't have been for that stupid woman The High Flyer would be a lot further than he is right now as for some reason Flyer was arrested at the 'SuperBrawl'
pay-per-view and forfeited an Intercontinental title shot. Luckily though that piece of not-me crap Body Count didn't take the title and died days after SuperBrawl- or so everybody thought..."
(A caption flashes on screen saying "The Return") (A clip is own three weeks after the event showing JECW 'Corporate Commish' Dario Giovanni is in the ring shooting a promo when all of a sudden The High
Flyers theme music starts up and the crowd goes wild. Flyer comes out and to everybodies surprise turns Corporate and sides with JECW owner Johnny
MacMaHon and Dario Giovanni and JECW World Champion Mad Dog McGrill.) "Sure the fans went crazy for Flyer but jeez- who doesn't? Anyway Johnny
MacMaHon asked Flyer if he wanted to be the special guest referee for the
Wrestlemania main event- JECW World champ and monster corporate heel Mad Dog
McGrill versus Ghost Face Killer, a newcomer who suspiciously rose to World title status quickly. The High One met Johnnys demand of sorts and The High
Flyer booked his place in the biggest JECW match of all time.
Between then and Wrestlemania The High Flyer captured Television gold for the first time but lost it before Wrestlemania because of that fruitcake
Ghost Face Killer."
(A caption flashes on screen saying "Wrestlemania- The 2nd Coming Of The
Peoples Flyer!") (A clip shows The High Flyer in his refereeing role at Wrestlemania and once again obeying Johnny MacMaHons actions by turning on Mad Dog McGrill. Then all of a sudden, Ghost Face Killer rips off his mask and everybody discovered......)
"The High Flyer hates this bit of film... yeah it was Body Count, but you know what? Flyer had just about enough of being MacMaHons lackey and decided to turn good guy again......."
(A clip shows Body Count pinning Mad Dog at Wrestlemania and MacMaHon demanding High Flyer doesn't make the count, High Flyer however makes the quickest count in JECW history and as Body Count celebrates in the ring
Flyer blanks an irate MacMaHon and walks to the back.)
"That was last week. Since then The High Flyer has regained his rightful place as number......1......... contender and his Television title to boot and tonight on Monday Mayhem, he has a non-title match with his arch-rival
Body Count. Watch and learn people as The High Flyer will prove to you time after time after time that he was is and always will be the biggest, the baddest and the best damn wrestler in the world of wrestling today and you know it, I know it and e-v-e-r-y-b-o-d-y with a damn pulse....... knows it."
(Camera fades to black.) -End of role-play- So what did you think of that? It was hard to write because I wasn't sure what to write about. Anyway tell me what you guys think OK? If I'm good enough I'd love a trial in a big e-fed and wrestle people who were actually in the RSPWF 200.
Thanks for reading Michael [detail withheld]
