10 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{SCENE: It is in the backyard of a mansion.... Ric Ice is sitting pool side with a robe on and some chick is feeding him grapes. The camera man is back for a interview.... He walks up to Ric Ice and begins to speak}
Camera Man: So Ric.... rough lost last night huh? Ric Ice: Well... I had him.... But I let him go and live another day. Camera Man: What??? he wiped the floor with your ass.... you know it..... I know it... and the fans that watched it know it.
Ric Ice: Hey.... You want me to send you on a first class trip to the unemployment office? Because one call to daddy warbucks will have you sent packing.
Camera Man: All right Ric..... You "Let him go and live" {The Camera Man smirks} So Ric.... whatcha doin'? Ric Ice: Well.... I'm just catchin' some rays and hangin out with one of my flock of ladies! This here is.... What was your name again sweets?
Lady: Whatever you want it to be Mr. Ice. Ric Ice: Hehe.... you see.... Money is power and I have the _ABSOLUTE POWER!_
Camera Man: Exactly how much money is it that you have Ric?
Ric Ice: Hmmmmm.... You know Bill Gates? Camera Man: Yeah... Ric Ice: Well.... I don't have quite as much as him but I'm gettin' there. Camera Man: Well ... It really isn't YOUR money... it is your Father's.... Rich oil tycoon Steve Ice... Am I right?
Ric Ice: His money is my money! Camera Man: Hey... I saw some guy walk down the isle with you on Monday... who was this man?
Ric Ice: Oh.... I haven't introduced you to him yet.... WINCHESTER! Winchester: You rang sir? Ric Ice: Meet my personal "servant" Winchester.... He comes highly recommended from the Queen.
Camera Man: Of England? Ric Ice: No.... of Jersey you dumb ass.... of course England.... Geez... it's like I'm talkin' my myself. Winchester is here to make sure that Ric Ice doesn't get "hurt" in any way whatsoever. You can say he is a bodyguard....
but I like the word "servant" better.... it degrades him even more. Winchester.... Go find a paper bag and some dog shit and put the shit in the bag and throw the bag at David Letterman's house.
Winchester: Yes sir. Camera Man: Well.... coming up on Thursday at a "Shakin' Da Foundation" show you will be facing a man that has been pretty big in the wrestling news of late attacking people left and right on Monday.... FBK... The Full Blooded Killah.
Ric Ice: Killah?.... what is this chump some sort of "gangsta"? Geez.... don't we got enough of these kind of morons running around the wrestling scene? It's like the promoters and bookers of all the wrestling federations just give people a pair of baggy jeans and a FUBU shirt and they have some new kind of
"gangsta" attitude... it makes me sick... Why don't you people just be yourselves. Let me ask you something oh great holder of the camera.... what do you see when you see me?
Camera Man: You want me to be honest? Ric Ice: As honest as possible. {Ric smiles} Camera Man: Well.... you are kind of greedy and self-centered. {Ric loses the smile} Camera Man: And..... a very kind hearted and..... one hell of an athlete... hands down the best wrestler in all the world..... Now Ric... is that what you wanted to hear?
Ric Ice: Yeah.... you pretty much hit it right on the nose.... except the greedy and self-centered part.... I'm not greedy.... I was in L.A. a few days ago and there was this bum on the street askin' for change.... I gave him a dollar... of course with my money problems I really couldn't spare too much more.... I'm kinda strapped for cash these days.... can you beleive I only bought two BMW's with this weeks allowance?
Camera Man: Thats amazing Ric.... I really gotta get goin'..... is there anything else you would like to say?
Ric Ice: Not really.... Except that Ric Ice will be stickin' his size 13 boot up that "gangsta" FBK's ass! And I would also like to plead my case the the
IWA staff... Mr. Cote please put me in the world title tourney.... it really isn't fair... I let Jackie Vars beat me... didn't you guys ever hear of the expression "Be kind to your elders"? Hehe... see you all Thursday!
Brian S. Handler of: "Swingin" Ric Ice Currently in IWA (0-1)
