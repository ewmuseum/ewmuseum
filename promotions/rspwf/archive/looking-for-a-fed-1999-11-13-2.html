Looking for a fed

13 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am currently looking for another fed to join. I'm not sure yet as which
'alter ego' of mine I'll be using, but that's not important. What I am looking for is a fed with some stiff competition. One based soley on quality, were two roleplays a week can get you the win. I want a web based fed, only fake wrestlers. A good site, and one with punctual results...so I am not always waiting on them. Email me and we'll talk.
Here's my sample RP (The Natural and White Chocolate pulls up to a small taco bell. They pulls up in a black hummer, White Chocolate driving, and The Natural on the passenger's side. They come around the side of the building and pulls towards the
drive-through window.) White Chocolate "I'd like 3 Gorditta's, two supreme and one baja. 2 Chalupas, and a large nachos."
Voice "Yes, pull up to the next window for your order." (They pulls up but comes to a stop as there is a balcony blocking the way. The hummer is too wide for the driveway to the window. White Chocolate becomes upset as the pimply teenager keeps telling him to pull in.)
White Chocolate "Hey jack ass, I can't my car's too wide." Teenager "Excuse me sir, do not call me a jack ass. I'm not the stupid one who bought a car too big to fit anywere."
(White Chocolate was about to yell something back but pulls his head back in the window. He steps out of the door and up to the window. The kid hands him the bag with a shitty look on his face. White Chocolate grabs him by his standard issue taco bell shirt and pulls his head oput of the window. He is about to hit him when The Natural calls out.)
Natural "Hey, WC, hold up on the beat down. We got to get to the interview in
20 minutes. And don't forget the mild sauce."
(White Chocolate let's him go and walks back to the hummer.) White Chocolate "Can you believe that shit? A punk like him talking to me like that? I should've tought him the lesson he'll never forget."
Natural "Yeah yeah, common, step on it, we're going to be late." White Chocolate "Fine." *** (We rejoin them as they're pulling into the parking lot of the Continental
Airlines Arena. They come flying in and hault to a stop as they pile out. White
Chocolate's got his White Chocolate T shirt on, and The Natural has his Yankees jersey. They hop into the elevator. As it stops they bolt out.)
Ring Announcer "Please welcome, the East Coast wrestling tag team champions. Top Choice!" (The crowd engulphs them in boos as their music hits. They walk out with the belts around their waiste's. They begin down the entrance ramp as the fans show their appreciation for them screwing over B&W, and the recent insults on
Chapel, aCID, and Body Bag. They flip the crowd off and continue on their merry way.) White Chocolate "Thank you old guy with a comb over. (Fans boo to respond.) Now
I'm not here to make you're day worth while, I'm here for a few select reasons.
One, I'm the best and it is maditory that we headline all house shows. Two, you can't live without us. And three, we have some responses to our fellow partner."
The Natural "Shogun, you come out here thinking you have the upper hand. Thinking that you can boss us around, insult us. Say what the f--- you want because we choose you? Well you're f---ing wrong! So now how about we alter this little orgy of blood and sweat we're having. We'll do it this way. Top
Choice vs aCID and Shogun vs Chapel and Body Bag. How's that sound. And you know what? To make it more apealing to everyone, we'll put these hunks of gold on the line as well. How about dem apples?"
White Chocolate "So you guys agree? I haven't heard from any of you guys, or is it that you are just so damn scared to open your mouthes because you'll enrage us even more? As for then...good bye ladies."
(White Chocolate drops the mic and they each step out of the ring. The fans still with a constant boo, but as of late the 'asshole' chant has become prominant.)
(This league will not be my main one, that's why I want a fed were you are ussually limited to about two a week.) Dont respond to this, email me.thanks
