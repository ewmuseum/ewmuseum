[I WANT YOUR OPINION] Idol Austin speaks about White Tiger

24 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let me know what you think. Thanks in advance. [The scene opens in the gym of “Incredible” Idol Austin’s Beverly Hills mansion, where Austin is training for his upcoming NWC World Title match against White Tiger. As the camera zooms in on Austin’s face while he is working out, there is a look of determination on his face that Hans’t been there since before he won the GSW Heavyweight Title. Austin sees the cameras and stops his workout. He picks up a towel and wipes the sweat from his face. When he finishes, he looks to the camera and speaks.]
Every once in awhile, I find that I come across people both in and out of this business that I can’t talk about enough. I have once again found such a person in the form of the current NWC World Champion, White Tiger. This man’s inherent goodness sets my teeth on edge. He panders to the fans and sells them his own natural brand of garbage as if anyone really cares about him. Wait, I amend that. I am sure that there are some people out there that care, but they probably live in boxes in alleys and are so drunk or shot up on heroin that they would probably cheer a sewer rat if someone put a mask and a belt on him and called him Champion. Well, I will tell you what Tiger, I don’t buy any of your hype one bit. And that’s all you really are you know, hype.
Let’s examine this for a bit shall we? Picture this if you will. The NWC, looking to add a little excitement to the World Title scene after two disastrous World Title reigns by Soul Shadow and having endured short reigns by Mr. Sinister and Keith Stein, are now saddled with a waste of sperm named
Vic Valari. At the same time, the founding members of The Powers of
Arrogance, “V.I.P” Eddie Zam and Big Hurt, were reviving the stagnant tag team scene with their special brand of greatness as the NWC World Tag Team
Champions. The sudden revival of the tag team scene made the NWC Region
Heads sit up and take notice of the brilliant opportunity that was unfolding before their very eyes. They thought, “Hmm. What if we gave a member of
The Powers of Arrogance a shot at the World Title? If they can do that for the tag team scene, just imagine what they could do for the singles scene if one of them was World Champion. The possibilities are endless.” So it was decided that when Valari went to the MWA for his first title defense, they were going to recommend that either Zam or Hurt get the title shot.
But MWA Commissioner 911 thought otherwise. It was only at the urging of the other NWC Region Heads that Big Hurt was involved in the three way match to determine a challenger to begin with. It is rumored that 911 was going to give the slot to the then newly crowned MWA TV Champion Toronoff. And of course, we covered how YOU were included the other day. And we also covered what happened in that match and the matches that followed. But here is a little bit of truth for you Tiger old boy, the only reason why you had yet to defend your World Title against a member of the PoA until now had more to do with the dislike that 911 and Ken Nemesis, as well as a few minor officials, had for us than because there were no members that deserved it.
[Austin stops for a moment and picks up a jug of ice water. After taking a few deep gulps from the jug, he puts it down and speaks again.]
Sorry. Anyway, to continue my story, enter GSW Owner Matt Guzzetta and GSW
President Mike Tower. They were in the midst of planning the second GSW Pay
Per View, which was to be headlined by an NWC World Title match, and were discussing the list of potential candidates to be the GSW representative.
Tower named people such as Dan Johnson, Derek Douglas, Steve Houston, Mark
McCormick, The Duke, and Lawrence Williams. While Tower was rattling off these names, Guzzetta suddenly placed my name into consideration. As expected, Tower violently objected since he was still angry at me for the beating I laid down on him the night I made my association with The Powers of Arrogance a matter of public record. He said that I of all people should not get the shot because of what I did to him. But Guzzetta disagreed. He pointed out that since day one, I have been the most dominant and publicized wrestler in GSW, and while he was at it, he pointed out the fact that I went through all of the GSW’s wrestlers en route to winning the GSW’s Royal
Rumble, staying in the ring the longest in the process. On a personal note,
I’d like to add that it was one of those Battle Royals that was won by a quality wrestler and not some two bit hack. EVERYONE in GSW wanted me in their stables after that.
But Tower was unmoved. He stated that the only loss on my record came at the hands of Dan Johnson, and that fact alone should warrant a World Title shot for him. I, of course, took care of that problem. Guzzetta countered
Tower’s statement by stating that although Johnson beat me, when it was down to me and him in the finals of the four way elimination match to determine the first ever GSW Champion, I was the one who won, AND that I won that match cleanly. He then went on to point out that ALL of victories were in matches in which I won cleanly. They argued back and forth about it and it was finally decided that I would get the World Title shot. The announcement was then made.
[Clip of the press conference where GSW President Mike Tower and GSW Owner
Matt Guzzetta are announcing the main event of International Intensity.]
Mike Tower: We are pleased to announce that the main event at International
Intensity on May 23rd, will feature NWC World Heavyweight Champion White
Tiger against GSW Heavyweight Champion “Incredible” Idol Austin with the NWC
World Heavyweight Title on the line.
[Scene shifts back to Idol Austin.] The announcement as I recall, was met with much protest and a lot of chagrin. The loudest of all of the protesters was your very own partner in
The Four Corners of Weakness, Lawrence Williams. Old Lawrence there sure didn’t look too cool, or confident for that matter as he cried about it.
Hell, he even went so far as to suggest that a conspiracy between myself and
Mike Tower was involved in the decision to give me the title shot.
Another personal note if I may. White Tiger, you are a liar. You said that you and the other members of The Four Corners of Weakness begged the GSW to give me the World Title shot. The complaints of Lawrence Williams however, suggest that that is not true. I know you’re too stupid to believe me
Tiger, so go look it up. His comments are a matter of public record, and were made recently.
So now, we move to the present. The NWC’s grand vision of what The Powers of Arrogance can do to their World singles Title scene is about to be realized. After being pushed back because of politics, personal preferences, contractual technicalities, and a miscarriage of justice or two, a member of The Powers of Arrogance is finally going to get a shot at the NWC World Title. I am the PoA’s Chosen One, which is only natural. The
NWC World Heavyweight Title was intended for the greatest wrestler in the world, and it is time for the greatest wrestler in the world to hold it.
So go ahead and continue to spew your garbage that my view on documented history is distorted. Keep telling yourself that just because a bunch of homeless drunks and junkies think you’re “cool”, that you are loved by the fans. Keep telling yourself that because you have the belt, that means you are the champion, even though there are some who say that having the belt and being “the champion” are two different things. And keep playing with your bubbles. While you are doing all of that, I will be using my status as the instrument of destiny to get what both I and The Powers of Arrogance so richly deserve, the NWC World Heavyweight Title. Come International
Intensity on May 23rd, a bubble WILL be burst, and I strongly suspect it will be yours. I will be the one who makes sure it happens, because I can.
How can anyone expect any less of me? [Austin smiles and then goes back to the weights to continue his workout as the scene fades to black.]
“Incredible” Idol Austin GSW Heavyweight Champion Member of The Powers of Arrogance Next NWC World Heavyweight Champion
