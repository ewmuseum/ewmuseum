(flash) Holly "The Toughest Woman Alive"

14 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to the parking lot of the Dungeon in Jacksonville, NC. An
OSWA van pulls up and an intervuer and cameraman jump out and head towards the front door. As they walk through the door the office is empty but the sound of someone lifting weights is coming from the weight room. They walk through the second door and find Holly curling mid-weights. She sets them down and turn towards the intervuer...)
Holly: If you're looking for Jeremy Odessa he's not here. Intervuer: Actually, we came to intervue you. This Wednesday you have a shot at the OSWA Women's World Title. From what I'm hearing, you're the odds on favorite.
Holly: It's about time I got to step into the ring again! After I got hurt they said that I would never fight again but I'll show them!
Intervuer: Let's talk about that. We all know that you were involved in some kind of accident that ended your kickboxing career but nobody seems to know exactly what happened? What's the story?
Holly: Three years ago I was the reigning ISKA Women's Kickboxing Champion. I could out kick, punch, or elbow anybody that stepped into the ring with me! One illegal rabbit punch to the kidney and five operations later my kickboxing career was over! I was one of the best kickboxers in the world and a great submission wrestler but they said that I would never fight again. That's when my best friend Jeremy Odessa gave me a call. A few weeks later I was managing him to more titles than I could drape over my shoulder. But I've always wanted to get back in the ring.
Intervuer: If the injury was so bad, how are you able to wrestle now? Isn't there a danger?
Holly: No, they repaired the damage and I've had time to heal. I've been working out constantly for three years to rehab this injury and they cleared my for competition several months ago. You see this...
(She turns to the side revealing a pad straped over he kidney...) Holly: That and a nasty scar is all that's left! Odessa's been teaching me some very nice wrestling moves here at the Dungeon and I pitty anybody, man or woman, that gets in my way! I AM the toughest woman on earth and the
OSWA is in for one hell of a ride!
Intervuer: Thanks and good luck Wednesday. (She picks the weights back up as the intervuer walks back out of the door...)
