Hey all... Before I go ahead and commit 100% to the current offer on the table, I thought it would only be fair to make sure that everyone out there had an equal chance... As I'm sure you all figured out by now, I'm looking for a new home for Mike Justice.
For those of you who are unfamiliar with him, Justice is a former SCRA North
American and World Champion, and received an honorable mention for face of the year & wrestler of the year in Jacob Kane's Year End Awards for 1998.
And, up until last night, he competed in Wayne Michaels' HHWO under a mask as the enigmatic The End.
If anyone is interested in adding Mike Justice to their roster full-time, please contact [detail withheld] ASAP. Thanks, Andj
