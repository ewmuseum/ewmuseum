[Free Agent] "The Finisher" Don Phillips

13 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello....I'm [name withheld], handler of Don Phillips. Below are some info on him and things I am looking for in a fed.
Wrestler info: ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯ -Very successful in past federations, such as EWF, GRW (region of CWC), and many others. -former 3x EWF World Heavyweight Champion, former CWC Southern States Champion.... -submission, technical wrestler -gimmick: believes he is one of the greatest ever...does whatever it takes to win. Confidence level is through the roof, but sometimes mistaken for cockiness. Fed Requirements: ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯ -email roleplaying based feds -quality website -cards that come out on time regularly -booked feds, NO simulator use -good competition, decently sized roster Sample Roleplay: ¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯¯ (Scene opens to a large room, filled with dozens of local reporters. There is a large podium set up at the head of the room. There is a slight murmur in the room, from the reporters conversing, most likely about the press conference they have come to see. After a few seconds, a short man wearing a dark grey suit and a pair of reading glasses walks up the podium holding a few peices of paper. He steps in front of the podium and begins to speak.)
Man: Hello everyone, my name is Jack Groves, and I would like to introduce someone right now who has been very important to the wrestling world in the past. He has been a former CWC Southern States Champion, a former 3 time EWF World Heavyweight Champion, and also a former EWF
World Tag Team Champion. Without further adeu, please welcome "The One and Only" Don Phillips!
(The room filles with applause as Don Phillips steps out from the back room and walks up to the podium. He is wearing a plain black t-shirt a pair of blue jeans. As he stands in front of the podium, he waits until everyone quiets down and then begins to speak.)
Phillips: Well, thanks for everyone for being here, because this is sort of a big day for me. After not actively competing professional in wrestling for about 6 or 7 months, I've been thinking about my future.
Now I'm not too old of a guy...hell, I'm only 32. I've stayed in shape by training in my gym, and I've been helping out in various wrestling schools across the country, but I've been kind of bored lately...and this training stuff just isn't getting the job done for me. I miss the days where'd I go out in front of a jam-packed arena and kick somebody's teeth down their throat. I want that back. So, because of this, I'm now announcing my return to professional wrestling! I'd be very intersted in various federations, and if you're looking for a new World Champ, you just might be looking at him. Now, does anybody have any questions?
(Don points to a bald reporter in the second row.) Reporter: Mr. Phillips, welcome back to professional wrestling. Now I'm sure alot of federations will be making offers towards you, are there any in particular that you would like to sign with in the near future?
And if so, which ones? Phillips: There isn't really a certain fed that I've been looking at....I'll just have to see where my offers come from. I doesn't really matter where I go, because I guaruntee I'll be succesful. I've wrestled in Canada, all over America, and even in Europe for a short period of time, and the results have been through the roof everytime. Next question...
Reporter #2: Mr. Phillips, what have you been doing to stay in shape over the months while you were off? And do you still believe that you could be a top contender in alot of federations?
Phillips: Like I've said, I've been working out in my personal gym regularly, and have been helping out in various wrestling schools. I'm still in great shape, and if anyone thinks that I'm too old...well they can come and try me.
But for your question about me believing I could be a top contender, I think I sure as hell can be a top contender anywhere i go. Just because
I've been off for a few months doesn't mean much, because I'm still one of the greatest wrestlers around. I think I'd be a wise investments for alot of feds out there needing some star-power. Anybody else?
Reporter #3: Don, do you actually think you could jump right into a fed after 6 months off and compete as well as you have in the past?
Phillips: Well unless you have a hearing problem, I think I've stated clearly just a few seconds ago that I can! And if you don't believe me, just watch and see, because I'd bet my life that I'll be just as great, if not better than I was! I've only got time for one more questions....anybody else?
Reporter #4: Mr. Phillips, what was the main reason why you wanted to compete again in the world of professional wrestling?
Phillips: Well let me tell you, talking to you people was not one of the reasons why I wanted to come back. There were a ton of reasons why I wanted to come back....one of them was because I was bored stupid.
Another was because I want to show everybody that Don Phillips still has what it takes to be the greatest wrestler around. That's all the questions I'll be taking for now, so next time I see you, I might have nice gold title around my waist. Later...
(Phillips then walks away from the podium as the crowd begins to applaud. He walks out throught the door as the scene ends...)
