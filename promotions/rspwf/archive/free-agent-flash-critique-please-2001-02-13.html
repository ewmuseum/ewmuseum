[FREE AGENT] FLASH! Critique please!

13 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Keep in mind, i'm only 11 years old :) [Utica, New York 10:28 AM] Scene takes place at a regular old coffee shop early in the morning, Nick
Carter sits across from an old friend Jeremy. Nick Carter has on green Nike wind pants with a black T-shirt. He has two 24 carrot gold necklaces. He also has a Yankee hat on. He has a pair of green and white
Adidas. Nick Carter has dark brown eyes, bushy eye brows, and black hair that's slicked back. When Nick smiles you can see his shinny white teeth. Jeremy has black pants on, and a white T-shirt on that says GWA in big gold letters, he also has a pair of Nike's on.
He wears a New York Nicks hat on backwards but you can still see some of his long blond hair come out of the back he has sparkly blue eyes and a small nose. Jeremy and Carter are drinking coffee while Carter has a blue mug and Jeremy has a brown mug. All of the sudden the subject of the GWA comes up [Utica, New York 10:30 AM]
[NICK] I'm getting so sick of these other GWA wrestlers. [JEREMY] How come? [NICK] They keep on saying that I'm my cousin, you know the Back Street boy, the real g@* one that I can't stand.
[JEREMY] Yea, that must get annoying. So who do you face for your next match up? Lance Storm right?
[NICK] Yea, Lance thinks he can beat me <laughs a little> How can a Canadian like him think he can beat such a great wrestler such as my self?
[JEREMY] I don't know man, what can I say? You're underrated. Guys like him think just because there veterans in the GWA somehow means there good when there really terrible.
[NICK] Good point. I think that Mr. Storm had one to many boos I mean how can one be so stupid? After the performance I put up in my last two matches you'd think he wouldn't use the 'P' word in his promo when he's speaking of my name. I mean I'm sure he saw how I took care of those to morons ... what's there names,
Stone Cold and Benoit, yet he still thinks he can become victorious against me? [JEREMY] I guess so... [NICK] You know what? I'm going to beat him more brutally then I did SCSA and The Crippler. Just to prove a point, I am and will continue to be the most
UNDERRATED yet one of the greatest GWA wrestlers ever. He also called me pathetic? What the hell is that all about? How can anybody force themselves to say something like that to such a great wrestler?
[JEREMY] I don't know. [NICK] And plus I'm UNDEFEATED not to mention, Don't you think UNDEFEATED wrestlers should get treated with A little more respect? [JEREMY] Yes. [NICK] Pathetic eh? We'll see how pathetic I am after I screw his face up even worse then Stone Cold's and Benoit's.
[JEREMY] Oh. [NICK] He wants to know where I was when he was in the sinister seven, well, I was watching in the sixth row because my career in the GWA hadn't quite started yet.
[JEREMY] Do you have any doubts? [NICK] No doubts, because I know, and many other INTELLIGENT people know that when guys like Nick Carter and
Lance Storm cross paths that Nick Carter is going to bet he obvious victor.
[JEREMY] OK, that's what I wanted to here. [NICK] I seriously have a strong belief that this guy is BLIND, how can he not see that I'm not Nick
Carter? It's strange really. [JEREMY] I know. [NICK] Well, enough talking, lets leave. [JEREMY] Ok. [Nick takes the last sip of his drink, Carter and Jeremy walk out of the coffee shop, and the scene fades]
