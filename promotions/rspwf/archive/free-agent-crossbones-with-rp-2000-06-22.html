Free Agent - Crossbones (with RP)

22 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am the handler of Crossbones, a character that I began using again a few months ago. He was involved for three months with the CPW, which recently folded. He set the record for length of a title reign with the television championship.
I am now looking for a new federation, but I do ask for a few specifications for the federations that want to have me compete. I would like them to...
*Be booked federations, with no simulations. *Be conventional (no extreme feds, please) *Have a website and bulletin board for RP's Sorry for being picky, but I feel that federations with these features would be better suited than those without them. Please e-mail me at
[detail withheld] Thanks Rick THIS IS A RP THAT I USED A COUPLE OF MONTHS AGO. {Scene opens in a rather run-down section of a city, late into the evening hours. The buildings are covered by graffiti; not one building is unscathed.
Most of the streetlights are burnt out, and many of the remaining ones constantly dim and blink, a clear sign that they are on the fritz as well. The sound of yelling between several men comes through an open window in a brick apartment complex, breaking the silence. An older man, dressed in tattered clothing, seeks refuge from the frigid temperatures and the accompanying wind.
Heavy footsteps can be heard in the distance, causing the cameraman to turn the camera in the direction that they emanated from. Though the image is a bit fuzzy at first, the lens clearly shows Crossbones as he walks down a crumbling sidewalk, held together mostly by coldpatch. He seems to be walking at a more deliberate pace tonight, although it is far from being a full-blown sprint. Once he is about fifteen feet away, he suddenly stops. After standing stoically for a moment, Crossbones raises his gloved right hand from his side, and gestures towards an alleyway, hardly visible in the darkness. The cameraman stands pat, until Crossbones starts walking towards it. Both men enter the alleyway, and are enshrouded in darkness.}
Before I start to talk about April Reign, I have to first reflect on my first championship defense last night, against Bobby Brown. Maybe I was overlooking the match a bit, concentrating on the PPV, because he kind of suprised me. He more than held his own and he did have himself situated quite well during many junctures of the contest. But when it comes down to it, he did not have a chance to legitimately win, and it isn't because of his wrestling ability. It was because of Garrett, the big man who decided to interject himself in the last place he should have, and that was in my match. I felt the chairshot that you gave; I still feel it right now. But that is nothing compared to what you will feel when I get my revenge. There is an old cliche that goes something like "Payback's a bitch, and you will find out soon enough just how much truth there is to that statement.
But for now, my focus is on the "Modern Day Warrior". You have been looking for a piece of me for a long time, practically since you first laid ink on your contract. I should be flattered in that, as I was listed among the elite like Dan Stein, Crowbar, and
Bret Masters. But you know what, I'm not. I see you as someone that considers me a steppingstone into the upper echelon of the federation, nothing more than a pawn on a proverbial chessboard. Well maybe I'm not the king piece, towering over the rest of the pieces, but I am not one of the little pieces either.
You are a pretty good competitor, which was evident in your match against Dan Stein. While you failed to win that match, I must say that your performance was still commendable, especially for a debut. But, the fact remains that you had your shoulders pressed against the mat, as I plan to do to you on the
Pay Per View. Whether you call yourself the "Modern Day Warrior" or "Modern Day Woman", the nickname does not matter, as long as you get the win in the long run. Still, you have hyped yourself good with the Warrior persona, hype that you will have to live up to during our match.
{It is evident either Crossbones or the cameraman trip over some trash in the alleyway, because the interview is interrupted by the sound of cans being clinked across concrete.}
Just like this fool cameraman here, you have been making a lot of noise, Chris Thunder. That is how you got yourself an opportunity at the coveted TV title, by making a lot of noise about me during your vignettes. I already said that you helped prove yourself against Stein, but you still have to do it against me.
While I expect us to have a phenomenal contest at April Reign, I will tell you right now that you can't avoid the same fate that several superstars have already endured. And that is the "Cross Check"
superplex, probably the most lethal finisher in the federation. So you can try to back up your statements on Sunday, but you will get rebuffed just as anyone else in line will.
{The cameraman emerges from the black alley, but Crossbones does not. As the cameraman looks in, the screen fades to black.}
