To all top RSPWers, Please Grade This Roleplay

12 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I recently joined yet another fed to make my name in, I left this as a begining roleplay and I was wondering what you guys think of it. Please give me suggestions/comments/criticisms.... constructive ones only please, thanks.
The references to "Stone Cold" Sean Oustina and Mr. Perfection are just abouple of newbies I saw in there blatantly copying real gimmicks.
================================== [The view pops into a blurry white as the lens begins to focus. Slowly the digitized bits of scenery sharpen in to show us a view best described as "completely beutifull". In front of us lies a huge lake spanning most of the distance to both sides, it's sparkling clear water reflects nicely a picture of the camera man looking down on it. Three dark blue fish quickly swim across the view and the camera tries to follow them as they shoot past it's view. The camera man looks to the far left of the lake to see the mountains in the distance. Too far away to get a good view thier impression hangs in the back ground. The sun shines upon the water sending a near blinding ray of light at the camera. It turns quickly to look behind it where a forest lies ahead.
Tall trees spreading too far up to see with branches full of live outside it's own leaves. A light brown squirrel suddenly shoots across one branch and onto another tree. The camera looks upon another one as a flying squirrell amazes us and "flies" three trees over. The camera man starts to walk forward noticing all the plant life on the ground...breaking twigs and rustling leaves as he goes he nearly steps on a fire ant hill. With luck he hops onto it and keeps walking.
Faintly we hear some talking and chattering. Two guys up ahead are talking about something as the camera man travels closer to them. After walking several dozen feet the camera man stops and leans against a tree...obviously out of shape from the big gut we caught a glimpse of when the camera stared into his reflection on the water. He squeals and looks at the tree he was leaning on to see hundreds of ants climbing it.
Like a boof he drops the camera and starts to smack his own forarm and bisceps. As the camera focuses only on his knees we hear him whining and squeeling around as he brushes and bats off the ants probably covering his arm. After a few moments he picks the camera back up and gives a big sigh of relief, and probable exhaustion. He checks to make sure everything is in tune and keeps ahead. The voices now really getting close to us. He walks for about 15 feet and stops. He stares straight ahead to see nothing. He turns the camera to the right and catches a view of the two men we heard.
About 20 feet ahead growing closer is a small campfire with two guys sitting on a log next to it. Alittle bit to thier right is a big brown camping tent, one of the new aged hectagon style ones. Big and Fancy the fire projects it's continueing flashing lights onto it. One of the men, the bigger man is making hand shapes using the fire and the tent to make shadown puppets. The camera man approaches them now only 8 feet away.]
Big Guy: Allright now what's this? Other Man: Um...um....um....uh...that's a..... Camera Man: Crocodile [Both guys look at the camera surpised a bit too see it. The Big Man drops his hand. Both are wearing light white t-shirts each with it's own individual South Park Logo. The Bigger man has on jean shorts that aren't ripped at all and some nice big boots. The other man has nike tenishooes on with slightly baggy jeans coming down to meet them.]
Big Guy: Your right. Well look what we have here...I wonder who told them...the city sherriff or that bar owner.
Other Guy: Probably both. Big Guy: Damn locals gotta run thier mouth off every time they see a big star. Other: Two big stars. Big Guy: Yup, at least they gave us a good bar fight--- [The camera man clears his throat loudly and both of the men look back at him instead of each other.]
Other: Oh...yeah. So...which fed head sent you? Camera Man: Mr. Paul Pitts, owner of GCW, Global Cham--- Big Guy: Ya ya ya we know what that is. We know who he is too. Other: Yah...Paul Pitts, The Crippler, Chuck Thorn, Sammy something or other...yah we know who he is. So...let me see...you would like a few words from us right? Everybody does...same old story each time. Well, my name is Vince. This [motions to the big guy] is Steve Buellve. We're the
"Bad Mouth Boyz" a new tag team come to join yet another fed.
Steve: Yah, we've seen 'em all. We heard from a couple of connections of ours that GCW was startin' up and lookin' for some talent. Well here you are...we're the talent. Look no further, future tag champs and singles champiion galore.
Vince: Don't get ahead of yourself there. Steve: [laughing alittle]Why not? I've seen the roster they've got so far. It's pretty damn pathetic. They've got..."Stone Cold" Sean Oustin and Mr. Perfection, damn those must be the most original guys around. I mean....I don't think I've ever seen anybody like them before.
Vince: I think your right on that one...I mean when I saw an intervie by
Mr. Perfection, the originality just shot through the roof! He must be some kinda genius to think up a gimmick like that. And Stone Cold, Stone
Cold Stone Cold Stone Cold, I'd never think of something so new and unheard of before.
Steve: [laughing alittle] But seriously...heh heh...this is pathetic. This is gonna be an easy picking for the both of us. I mean...we've tried the gimmicks, we've tried the superhero crap...
Vince: We've tried the good guy crap...we've tried the bad guy crap... Steve: But when it comes down to winning matches and getting the gold... Vince: We're best at doing that just as ourselves. No "perfection"... Steve: No 3:16... Vince: And certainly no bottom line. We are what we are....and s---this is sounding like a WWF Attitude Commercial!
Steve: Ya it is! It's even worse when everytime I try to talk you finish my sentence you little bitch!
Vince: Me finish your sentence? Your finishing mine! You rude Son of Gun quit interupting me!
Steve: Sun of a gun? Ha! Did your mommy teach you how to say that? Vince: ...hey wait a second? Did you just say something about my mom? Steve: Not yet...but now I will...damn fat cow. Vince: Son of a....bitch! [Vince suddenly rushes Steve and they start to roll around struggling. Vince gets the advantage and gets on top of Steve. Steve pushes him over and switches positions. He punches straight at his face but Vince moves his neck to the side and Steve punches the ground. Vince pushes him off and Steve brushes the fire. They start to wrestle again in the dirt and suddenly a bit of the fire shoots out to a near bye patch of brush. It quickly catches fire and so does the bush next to it. The two stop fighting immedeately.]
Vince and Steve: Oh shit! [Immedeately Vince goes inside the tent for something and Steve reaches into a cooler. He gets out a beer, pops it open and takes a small sip before spraying it onto the fire. Vince rushes out with a blanket.]
Vince: What are you doing? That damn beer can won't work! Get another blanket! [Steve rushes back to the tent as Vince starts batting at the fire with the blanket. It starts to die out and Steve comes back out of the tent with another blanket. The two do away with the fire fairly quickly and then look at the blankets. Both have huge burnt spots and holes in the middle.]
Steve: Shit! Those were are only blankets! We're gonna freeze tonight! Vince: No no no we'll just have to make another trip into town...and into the bar... Steve: Another bar brawl? Vince: [smiles]Yah... Steve: [looking back at the camera.] Hmm...well...this isn't exactly one of our best moments. What's important is, even though he's a kiss ass
Vince: [mumbles]and your a jack ass Steve: We're a hellova tag team. We do get along with each other most of the time but in the ring all the time. We're unstopable to face and amazing to watch. We're the greatest tag team this sport has seen in years and we're now here in GCW to show all you losers what talent is.
We may not have much to say now but we will in the future. To you loser
Mr Perfection and Sean Oustin we got two words for you.
Vince: They ain't what you think because although you probably would like to suck it we aren't that kinda guys....
Steve: Two words...YOU SUCK Vince: JACK ASS!!! Steve: [looking at Vince] Hey! That wasn't two words! You idiot! Vince: Actually I wasn't finishing your sentence...I was calling you a jack ass. Steve: What? Why you li... [Cameraman not willing to see another fight cuts the transmission. Screen fades to black]
