22 December 2000

Looking for an established fed, which is well known. Here is a sample RP.. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- <> We open to the UWO axxes stage in the center of all the axxes entertainment. The same spot that Commissioner Crawford and various other
UWO superstars have talked to the fans and the people watching at home on.
On this Sunday Night the axxes area outside the Ice Palace is packed because doors to come into the arena are opening soon. As the camera switches to one on stage filming the sea of fans they cheer and hold there signs up for the hope of the camera to pick them up. We switch back to a camera filming the stage as the lights around the metal frame turn to a crimson red and "Why
Me" begins to blast through the speakers around the stage as the crowd erupt into a cheer. After a few moments the challenger competing in tonight's elimination triple threat steps from behind the curtain. He walks out his hands extended to the side. He walks around the stage in some white cargo pants and one of his many merchandise tee-shirts which are on sale all around the axxes area. Layz looks into the fans with a wide smile on his face soaking up the crowds response. He then reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a microphone. <>
"Finally we are here. Jakob's had the longest wait. He won the spot at UWO's last pay per view and I earned it by the impact I've been making since coming back..interestingly enough....coming back from an injury I got at the last pay per view. Not in a match...but by a car. There won't be any cars running me over or any other sort of bullshit happening before the match.
All the bullshit goes on inside the ring... (Layz smiles as the camera pans in.) the thing is all the bullshit in in my favor. (The camera switches to another angle picking up Layz's whole body as he goes on.) The shits on Jakob and Jake..and what is the shit ?..driving there heads into the mat and pinning them for the one...the two..and the three. Then taking that world title and damn..it needs work on..it's the world title held by many many great athletes. Hell before it's downfall it had took a long and prosperous run around tigerclaws waist. But then it fell to Menace and with that the world title fell down a lot...a lot...as low as it has ever been. To the level where the world title doesn't even represent what it really is..."
<> Layz pulls out an dictionary from his pocket and opens it. He places on some "Dudley Boys" style glasses and begins to read. But then stops and laughs throwing the glasses into the crowd.<>
"WORLD; The earth. The universe. CHAMPION One that wins first place or first prize in a competition. One that is clearly superior or has the attributes of a winner: a champion at teaching. An ardent defender or supporter of a cause or another person: One who fights; a warrior.
Look at the last definition for Champion...one who fights; a warrior. Well damn I've been doing that since I've came to the UWO last year. Sometimes loosing and of course winning. But none the less I've fought with heart, determination, one hundred and ten percent and sometimes even a foreign objects too..So when I beat Menace and then Venom the rebirth of the title comes again. It is on it's way back up...and with the world title around my waist I do attend to defending it as a champion..something Menace has...not done. But all of that starts right here tonight and damn I am ready..
I'm sure Tampa Bay is ready..." <> Crowd pop. <> "And the millions of viewers watching around the world are ready for the title to be taken away from Menace. But hell come on...any-one could do that. Even good old Charlie Brown...otherwise known as Ricky Knight could do it. So Menace is obviously going down first then Venom. The real match for the world title..
Venom and Layz. The win over Venom won't be easy we'll both shed a hell of a lot of blood. But blood coming from my head hasn't ever stopped me before, shit Venom you'll need to hit the Venom's Kiss to...or will you even be able to hit the famous Venoms Kiss ?. Shit, I don't think so.
So Jakob your travels through a city all in one night where ever so interesting. It was nice to see that Jakob has a slight human side. Samantha
?...nice to see you are getting action somewhere because with the thought of your other buddies traveling around in that one bus. Just all those guys...the driver the camera men and the three wrestlers..maybe they will start getting bored...and then in one of these nights that Slade just can't get to sleep he will begin to see Shane Rodgers in a new light. Interesting new worlds to go with RO...
Awww Hell the gay shit has been done so much...yeah Slade I saw your little song. But back to Venom while your buddies have been doing...whatever that they do on that bus I hope you've been getting in some training time. I'm sure you have even said that this was the match of your life. Well it's mine to bucko and I don't attend to loose it. Shit, it's been a looooooooong time hasn't it ?. Remember when you first joined the fed Jako ?...we talked sometimes..discussed some stuff. I was currently tag team champion with...(Laughs) Dean Malenko and not enjoying it...then up came an intercontinental shot for me. Against Xavier Blue..an elevator match and on that same pay per view a four way ladder match for the second spot in the tag team...teaming with me. It was you that volunteered yourself into that match Venom...
and it was you who won it. Me in my elevator match ?...well the damn elevator blew up..neither Xavier or I were left uninjured and other shit happened in my personnel life..you know out of UWO. Well that ended..but on the return I found you engaged in a feud with our good friend Thomas
Slade..it seemed like you two wanted to kill each other..and you did during your match at the next pay per view...the only thing is once you tore each other up...you embraced...and the fang was born. Then bada-bing-bada-boom we find ourselves a couple of months down the line from there and find ourselves a week away from Christmas Eve battling in a match for the world title.
What will happen when we do meet in the ring Venom ?. Well we don't have to wait too long but heres a tip off I got off the internet...
there's going to be a lot of ASS KICKING... your ass and ME kicking.. Menace doesn't even come into the picture we all know it. But I think it was his promo which I saw just before I came out here. Let's see...slurred speech..making no sense...repeating thins over and over...YEAH it was a
Menace promo. I believe our..champ is still going on about the cage match.
Fans the only cage match is the inferno hell in a cell...don't believe our champion. He doesn't even know what type of match he is competing in...
geez...that really..is a shame... Menace, Venom bring all you can to this match. Get as hyped up as you can for this match..think Venom...the world title...how nice it would go in your.....web and Menace...little...little Menace think about keeping that title...
wait that's so far from really happening there isn't much point in even hyping yourself up for. Aww hell menace save the embarrassment and just don't come down to the ring. There is no point going out as a fighting champion when..you never ever where a fighting champ.
One week away will be Christmas Eve..the stocking hung up by the fireplace. Children sleeping waiting for Santa...well Santa is coming early for Layz...he is coming in the form of my foot being shoved down Venoms throat. It's going to be a merry Christmas for Layz.." <> Suddenly a light snow begins to fall over the axxes area. It hasn't snowed in Florida for over ten years now and the fans gasp as they hold out there hands and open there mouths to catch the snow flakes. <>
"Oh....guys...I wouldn't eat the....eh...snow I don't know it ifs toxic or not...awwwww screw the fake snow...tonight we are gonna see some.....
ICE...... and one hell of a Blaze.." <> "Why Me" begins to play once again as Layz holds his fist in the air for a brief moment and then holds his hand out to catch some of the "snow" and then turns around and walks through the curtain with the crowd cheering. <>
