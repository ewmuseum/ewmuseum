::GDWA:: Diamond Girl Heidi Leick RP

24 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: The camera pans down and shows The Diamond Girl Heidi Leick, who is sitting in a semi dark room.... , she's wearing a Iron Maiden "Killers" T Shirt, and torn up, skintight, stonewashed jeans... the way the light is cast, the shadow covers half her face... her hair falls down over the half that is in shadow...
the other gorgeous brown eye gleams an insane stare... she's rocking slightly from side to side..., in a manner which is almost hypnotizing... ::
DGHL: Some call me ~strange~ .... ::giggle:: Some say I'm ~crazy~ ::psychotic laugh:: Some say I'm Eccentric.... or just straight out INSANE.... ::grins evilly:: And then... some just say ~it's just an act~... ::looks totally sane for the moment, then grins again::
Well.. I hear One person Think's she knows me pretty well.. My Next opponnent.. ~Sarah Summers~... Sarah.. think what you want.., call me what you want... I hear the whipsers in the locker room of things that you say about me.. and have said about me.... But Sarah... I really don't care.... All I know is it was ME who rescued you and your little girlfriends from taking the beatings of your lives at the hands of the Syndicate this last Summer... what did it get me?...
A little respect?,An Invitation to your party? A Phone call to see how I was healing from the lumps I took to save the 'pretty girls' of the GDWA? A
Friendly "Hello" in the locker room? ...Hmm?.. NO!!... Not one of you had the heart to come to me, befriend me, shake my hand, or check on me when I took the bumps for you ... :: pauses and looks down::
But ~you~ ~all~ had the spineless cowardice to ride MY talent to main event status... diddn't you?
Especially when I was taking on the Syndicate ALONE..... you JUMP on the
Diamond Girl bandwagon, get a free ride to stardom...
:: laughs and looks up a bit:: OH, Sarah, and How Ironic is it that we meet , for the first time against each other.... In a match I was BORN to win... An Iron Woman Match.. 30 Minutes of
No Rules... Most falls wins... Not~By~DQ... Not~by~Countout~...
~Just~Most~Falls~... for the GDWA North American Heritage Title at Grand Dragon
Glory... Of course.. there will be two others there Sarah... but we won't know who they are until next week... and right now... ::giggles::
That means you're the # 1 single on the Heidi's Greatest Hits... See... The
GDWA WANTS me to be thier North American Champion Sooooo baaaaad.... that they're handpicking the opponnents for me.. ::smiles conceitedly:: I'm not the
GDWA Extreme Television Champion for nothing you know... and NO.. this belt isn't on the line.. you greedy little wench... See.. the GDWA wants me to have
TWO Belts... as everyone knows... ~Gold~ does go very well with ~diamonds~...
Sarah Summers... You may think you know me.... But realize this .. You don't know me... NO one knows me..... :;looks down::
Not even I.... ::looks up:: But.. I'll admit.. You may know OF me... :: giggles and smiles:: How could you not? ~EVERYONE~ knows of me... The Queen of Extreme, The GDWA's EXTREME ICON... The most recognized Woman Wrestler in the History of the world.. The one.. the only Diamond Girl Heidi Leick...... and Diamonds... ::giggles and whispers the next line :: Diamonds... and Heidi Leick, are FOREVER!.... ::The camera fades out with Heidi's Insane laughter continuing on in the darkness :: O.G.
