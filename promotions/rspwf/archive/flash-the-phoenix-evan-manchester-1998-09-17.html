[FLASH] 'The Phoenix' Evan Manchester

17 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's an interview I wrote a few days back. I just wanted to post a couple of flashes to 'get my name' out on the RSPWF. Well, here it is:
+-+-+-+-+-+ [Fade into an old, beat up classroom. It seems to be around dawn, and fog fills the room. Rays of light shoot in through the open windows set high up on the walls, and faint laughter is heard. There is an old teachers desk, along with rows of old desks and an old chalkboard in the classroom, which is concrete and painted a light shade of blue. Suddenly, the classroom door opens and a figure steps in.]
[This man stands at a bit over six foot and has a stocky build. He has on a pair of blue jeans, black boots, a black 'Pearl Jam' concert tee-shirt, and a black, leather motorcycle jacket. We now recognize this man as 'The Phoenix'
Evan Manchester. His long black hair is damp, and hanging down touching his shoulders.] [He closes the door behind him, and goes and sits down at the teachers desk. He buries his head in his hands. Suddenly, he slams both of his hands down on the desk very violently and screams: "AHH", seeminly in frusteration. He than regains his composure and looks up into the camera which is set in the back of the classroom. He begins to speak in his usual, monotoned voice.]
'THE PHOENIX' EVAN MANCHESTER: For five long years I sat in this room, hoping, and praying that I would soon be released from this hell, more commenly refered to as a place of education, or a school. I remember it well. I was a mear ten years old and Mrs. Windberry taught American Literature. No, she didn't like me, she hated me. And her hatred for me plague me for five long years. No, she wouldn't pass me; she wouldn't let me move on; she had to keep me here, tormenting and torturing me. No, not physical torture, but the mental pain was almost unbearable. She would sit, and let the other kids pick at me, not saying anything. She kept me here for five long years, for her own personal entertainment. I was brought back four times, to serve as her jester. And I have resented her, and everyone else, every day since.
You see, I was not always like this. I used to be just a normal kid, playing baseball, and eating apple pie. It was Mrs. Windberry who pushed me over the line, and turned me into the dark man who you see sitting before you today. And for that, I thank her. If it were not for those years of torment, I don't think
I would've been able to live on the streets without going mad. I still believe, to this day, that it was that torture which has made me strong enough to survive through all the hardships of my life. And for that, I must appreciate her.
As I child, I was even more resentful than I appear to be now. I hated anything and everything, including my parents, my brother, and most importantly, myself.
Now I am not going to get into a discussion about my parents, because that is a whole other story. And my brother, or should I say, my parents son, was a boy who I didn't always hate. The man of whom I speak is not my blood, for I didn't know I had a real brother until 5 years ago. The man whom I talk about is my foster parents' son. Atfirst, I did love him like a real brother, but he was one of the many who tortured and tormented me as a child, and for that, I cannot forgive.
I am usually a forgiving man, but some acts just don't deserve forgivness. Hate. Hate is a strong word. Many people use it as it is mearly another word in our language, but I know. I know what it means to hate. And that is why you will not hear me say that I hate another human being for no reason, such as many others do. I know the pain that the word hate can cause, and I feel that very few people should be chosen to suffer, by being truely hated.
[Evan pauses, and pushes the chair back. He stands up, and walks over to the chalkboard. He grabs a piece of chalk, and writes: "BLOCKBUSTER BRIAN FURY" on the blackboard. He than goes back over, and sits down in the desk.]
'THE PHOENIX' EVAN MANCHESTER: Brian Fury, I would like to apologize ahead of time for what I will be forced to do to you. As I said to Trent Young-Blair, you were just in the wrong place at the wrong time. You have been forced to get in the ring with me, and you have been forced.. to feel my wrath. Brian Fury, you and I haven't met before, so you don't know what it feels like to be locked in the SunSet Drop. So I will tell you. Imagine this. Your head is pounding from the prevous attacks, I pick up your battered body, and I grab your neck. I squeeze tightly, and you feel as though your eyes will pop out of their sockets. As you stare at the light resting high above, you can't help but wonder if you will even remember what happened to you; how you got yourself into this predicament, and how you ended up in a hospital bed. And suddenly, you feel me squeeze a bit tighter, and you know that the end is near. I kick my feet up into the air, and your head slams against the mat. You see only black now, and you hear nothing but the roar of the fans and the referee pounding on the mat right next to your throbbing head. You hear my name announced in victory, and it is than that you realize that you have just been beaten by a force stronger than any man alive. And that is the power above. No, not God, but the power of The Phoenix. This power, is stronger than any mortal man, and
I will showcase my abilities when you and I step between the ropes. So Brian
Fury. . . Fall Before Me.
[Evan now gets up again. He goes to the chalkboard and scribbles something else. It reads: "JACK VARS". We now know Evan's next topic of discussion. Evan sits back down and continues.]
'THE PHOENIX' EVAN MANCHESTER: Jack Vars. After I dispose of Brian Fury, you are the next man on my hitlist. Vars, I have reason to be mad at you. As I said before, you left, just before I could get my shot at you in the EWC. You took my chance at being the most respected man in the EWC away from me, and for that, you must pay. No, not in the form of money, but in the form of pain.
Redemption can be hell Jack Vars, especially when the one who is after you is
Evan Manchester. Vars, for now, you can rest easy, but after I dispose of Fury, be afraid. I will be coming after you. You have been warned, now take that, and run with it. Stay out of my path until we meet in the ring, because if you chose to attack 'The Phoenix', you truely will feel... my... wrath. And the
SunSet Drop is the most devistating move in the world of wrestling today. Vars, until we meet. . .
. . .Sweet . . . . . . Dreams . . . [Evan takes a deep breath, and than buries his head in his hands once again. The camera fades to black as we watch this troubled soul sit and think.] +-+-+-+-+-+-+ Well, that'd ya'll think? Not too bad, eh? I hope not. Anyways, that's about all I gotta say for now. Another rp will be posted behind this one.
