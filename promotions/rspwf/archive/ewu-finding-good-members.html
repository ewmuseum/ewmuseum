FINDING GOOD MEMBERS by [name withheld] How do you find good members for your federation? This is just a little something that I decided to write, to help out all of the presidents, or as some people call them, "fed-heads", out there. So, if you're in need of some advice, or my opinion at least, please read on. One of the toughest things to do when putting together an advanced federation, is to find good members. Before you go looking around for good members, you've got to ask yourself the question; What is a good member? A good member, or e-wrestler, is someone who is willing to make a commitment to the federation. A good member is someone who is willing to role-play on a consistent basis. A good member recognizes the rules of the federation, and abides by them. He, or she, will help out with things. A good member is someone who doesn't just sit back and role-play for his matches. A good member will actually get ACTIVE. He or she will try to set up good angles and fueds for his or her character. That, in my opinion, is a good member to a federation. So, the problem of putting together a good e-federation, is finding those good members! Where do you find them? They aren't just going to pop up and BEG to join your federation! I've run two federations before, and I am currently running my third, the East Coast Wrestling League(ECWL). One thing that I _DO NOT_ suggest doing, is looking for advanced members in chat rooms. The chances of you finding a good member in a chat room is VERY slim, although, there are exceptions. So, if you aren't looking in chat rooms, where ARE you going to get your members? That's a good question, so let me explain. Any good president has been an e-wrestler himself at one time or another. I'm sure that all of you president's out there have been in a couple of advanced federations, am I right? Well, let's say that the federation you're in shuts down...there's a FREE mailing list! Once that federation shuts down, there's atleast ten possibilites to add to your roster! That's only one way to get members to join your federation. If you have a website for your federation, put it on as many Top 10, 20, 50, etc. lists as you can. This will bring a lot of attention to your federation. But, not all of the people that visit your site are going to be the kind that you are looking for. So, what I suggest, is that on your application, REQUIRE a sample role-play. This will give you some idea of the talent they have. But then, you run into yet another problem! E-fedding's pretty complicated, isn't it? How can you be sure that the Sample RP that they send you is their's? Well, you can't. That's another big problem, but, I suggest putting them in a match right off the bat, to see if they can actually role-play well! You never know how good they're going to be until you see them actually role-play for a match. Well, that's all for now! I hope it helps you out, and good luck on finding those GOOD MEMBERS! Written By: Michael E.
