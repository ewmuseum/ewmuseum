[Flash] Christian Madison [Flash] Check it!

13 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This aint really a flash but more of a segment I did for Pillow's ICW! Check it, Free agent and can rp for one or two more feds. if you like it reply, email whatever. Constructive Criticism...Flame at will...let the show begins.
( "Can I Get A" BY Jay-Z begins to play throughout the bulding as you hear an rather large amount of boos from the crowd. You then see Carl Grant walk out from the entrance with a pair gold framed shades, blue jeans and a black ICW
Countdown shirt, and a rather huge cigar positioned betwen two fingers as he struts to the ring and pulls out a mic from his back pocket. )
CARL GRANT - Ladies and Gentle, Boys and Girls, children of all ages. . .I'd like to present to you, your _NEXT_ ICW Juniorweight Champion, the one, the only. . .and the WONDERFUL. . . .
!!! C H R I S I T I A N "S P O T L I G H T" M A D I S O N !!! ( "Way It's Going Down" By Shaq begins to blare throughout the PA system as you hear an rather large thunderous explosion in back near the entrance. Cut up pieces of Paper glitter begins to fall from the air and into the crowd as the lights goes out, and Christian "Spotlight" Madison steps out in black jeans and his "Spotlight" Jersey. Green clouds of smoke arose from underneath the entrance and spotlight shaped spotlights beams down onto the floor leading him to the ring. He has his right arm throttled into the air and a huge smile on his face as he leads into the ring with a mic in his hand. )
CHRISTIAN MADISON: Now tonight I face off against Judgment Corp with my newly found partner Chuck Williams, wait...but before I get to that, lets take a moment and go back to Countdown `98! Now, it seemed to me that I had this damn thing won. I mean I broke down and took a beating from all over them and elminated damn near half of the battle royal single handed with no ones help!
And at the end I get ambushed by one Tommy Grayson. Which pissed me off pretty much because i was looking forward to faing D.B. BUGG later on that night, but that wasn't the case. Now if it wasn't got Tommy Boy here you'd be looking at your new International Champion. If not the least Grayson, you could've fought me one on one and let the better one go on but you had to take the easy way out and sneak up behind me. . .but it seemed that night your balls wasn't in the right place. Everyone in this damn arena know that i should've rightfully won that battle royal, and Grayson. . .you've got hell to pay because you haven't seen the last of me kidd. This ain't over, not by a long shot!
CARL GRANT: My boy Madison was a shoe-in for that battle royal until you robbed him of his 15 minutes of fame. But soon enough the day of the dawning light will shine upon my young prospect. Grayson, the way you knock Madison out was a cowardly yet a stupid mistake on your behalf because now you have an upset
Madison to look upon soon. . .very soon!
CHRISTIAN MADISON: Very soon indeed my friend. Whether yoy people like me or not, you have to agree that i had that rumble won. But you know, I'm not going to soil over rotten apples so to speak, but get EVEN!! Grayson you better watch your back because now you've earned the Spotlight's Affection. You're a marked man Grayson! And now Judgment Corporation. . .my next opponents or should I say our. Whether the Spotlight is in singles or tag action, the Spotlight aways shines on the occasion. I can tell you right now that this won't be pretty because when you put to men together in an match such as Chuck Williams and myself there's kaos that'll always be lurkin' around. Warren Frost and Matthew
Jared you're ours for the pickin', I mean beating you two clutchs will be as easy as 1-2-3!
( Pauses for a few moments as he then begin to slowly wipe the pouring sweat from his forehead. )
CHRISTIAN MADISON: I'm going to make this plain and simple. . .me and my partner as good as it gets and once we get our shots at the tag titles you'll be lookin new International Tag Champions, where I go. . .I'm always _CENTER_ of attention and tonight will be no different and for you two idiots back there, don't sing it. . .BRING IT!!!
( Madison drops the mic as he warms up and Grant gets out of the ring. ) -Christian "Spotlight" Madison [LAW, ICW, INWI, TOP] 6-0-1 -The Great Kamikaze [SJPW] 0-0-0 "I've said it once...and I'll say it again...God I love being a turtle" --Michaelangelo from Ninja Turtles.
