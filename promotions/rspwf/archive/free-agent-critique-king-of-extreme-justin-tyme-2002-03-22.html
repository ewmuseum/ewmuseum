[Free Agent Critique] 'King of Extreme' Justin Tyme

22 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I have attached two roleplays, critique on both please. feds - please email me if you are interested in my character, or post back here. people - critique all you want.. I could use the extra help. ------------------------------------------- RP #1 Scene - Scene flashes over and you see the entrance way, All of a sudden the lights go out, and a baby blue search light searches the crowd... a bomb is shown up on the screen, the fuse is lit, and then it explodes, as it explodes..
Blood Brother's by Papa Roach plays. The Video shows Justin Tyme performing at his best, it shows his Tyme Bomb, his death flip, it shows him winning the King of Extreme tournament, and many other things..Justin Tyme walks out from behind the curtain, he walks over to one side of the stage and yells at the crowd, stirring them up.. he does the same to the other side, then he hops and runs down to the ring, he slides in and immediately runs over to one of the turnbuckles, jumps up and does the gun salute thing. and then jumps off and goes to the other, and so on for the third and fourth. The Lights come back on and you see Justin Tyme walking over to one corner and grabs a microphone from a main event wrestling ring tendant. He goes back towards the middle of the ring and stops for a moment and listens to the cheers of the crowd... he then begins to speak.
Justin Tyme - Evil one, oh Evil one.. where for art thou Evil one? Baphomet.. you and your little butt *bleep* buddy Justin Considerate can come out two, three, four times... and start shit with me.. but it isn't going to effect the outcome of our match. You see, I was put on this earth to beat the shit out of people like you so hard that they take a step into reality. Because in reality
Baphomet, you stand no chance against me. You can call me a fucking joke, you can say I hide behind people like Carnage, Butch McCaul, and Masked Man 2000, but in truth, I don't.. I made those men what they are today, without me, they wouldn't have gotten as big of a leap as they did.. I put them into the spot, I took them under my wing and helped them be better.. You think I get my ass kicked my mid-carders? Who's a mid-carder that has kicked my ass.. I have one lost, and that is against Morbid Wolf, and that was a lucky win. I had gotten distracted with Butch.. and Morbid Wolf took advantage of that. Let me be the first to tell you Baphomet, eternal, beast, devil.. what ever the fuck your god forsaken name is.. we are not all talk.. and come next Tuesday you will find that out. You see fruit cup, you are not in my league.. you got a lucky win over Morbid Wolf, and that is only because of that Knocked-Up slut they called
'Just in Considerate'. A man with all talk and no walk.. Let's make a deal
Baphomet.. this is a title match.. and I really, really don't want to lose the match by DQ or anything of that sort, I want to beat you fair and square, to where I win and I get the title.. so here's what I am proposing.. you leave Mr.
Badd Ass back there in the back, and you make sure none of those Darkness of
Day Night Fucks don't come down to the ring, and I will make sure no one from the MeW's most powerful group, aka the Leap of Faith comes down to the ring.
That way it's straight up fair, and whoever wins is the better man... Also.. your damn right you have something to lose... you have two things to lose, maybe more.. you have that title for one.. and my respect for you as well..
because if I kick your ass on Tuesday, then I know.. that you are also nothing but talk.. you may not think that my respect for you is worth as much as a hooker on Broadway.. but I don't respect many people, and when I respect someone.. I know that they could possibly kick my ass, and I know that if they were to team up with me, we would own every son-bitch back in the back.. now..
whooaaa.. don't get your panties in a bundle Baphomet.. I'm not asking you to join Leap of Faith, we already have all we need right now, but I want to be on good grounds you, and so we know that when the time comes for Leap of Faith to put that Darkess fucks to their graves.. that you are there to make sure we get done what we say we get done. Now back to our match on Tuesday..Your right
Baphomet.. it is like a chess game.. except one thing you forgot to mention.. I knew one day the time would come for me and you to go one on one.. so like a wise man once said.. the better man always knows his opponents next move.. and
I have been studying your game, your strategy, and you can best believe I know your every move. You see, I live the extreme life.. so pain doesn't bother me to much, I will put my life on the line day after day for something as measly as the TV Title.. so you better plan on turning this match from the second closest match on the card.. to the first, because I guarantee that for every sweat that drips from your forhead, for every deap breath you take, I will be taking 2 more breaths, and sweating twice as much, because I am a hard ass worker, and when I have my mind set to something.. I get it.. and I have my mind set on that Intercontinental title you have there. Let me tell you something Baphomet, I deserve a shot at the World Title, I am one of the best god damn wrestlers in this federation.. but lucky me.. I get stuck facing trash like you. I will go into Tuesday the number one contender, and walk out of Rage the Intercontinental Champion.. you know why? Because it's about damn TYME!
He drops the mic, Blood Brother's plays again and he walks back towards the locker rooms -------------------------------------------------------------------------- RP #2 Scene - Scene opens to the University of Tennessee, Justin Tyme walks into view, he has on a Leap of Faith shirt, and black pants. He's walking with his hands in his pockets, in the background a new college students can be seen waving at the camera. Justin Tyme begins to talk.
Justin Tyme - Rage.. Steve Anderson and Odin challenge us once again for the tag titles.. we beat them already,, why should they get another shot.. They don't deserve to be the tag team champions, they haven't worked as hard as
Flying Tyme has. So these big boys come out and challenge us... and then they don''t plan on showing until the match.. they think they are going to win?
Steve.. Member of the hard-working Anderson family.. your family has been around wrestling for generations.. and you think you can just show up Tuesday
Night and win? You're disgracing the Anderson name Steve.. you never would see
Uncle Arn not talk about his match, and just show up expecting a big win.. the only thing that will be big.. is the upset lost compliments of Flying Tyme. So now Odin.. where have you been Odin? We regret to inform you... that your time here in the MeW has been cut short.. as of this Tuesday you will not be able to compete in wrestling ever again.. Please, do not worry about renewing your contract, as it has been terminated.. Thank You and Sincerely... The MeW Staff.
That is your letter of resignation.. you will be getting it soon.. when me and
Masked Man 2000 put you and your famous partner out of action for a while
Fury...Kimo Tiger.. What? Kimo Tiger Mask? A new guy.. why is Justin Tyme facing a new guy.. I have had title shots already.. and they wanna place me against a new guy? The MeW has always been screwing Leap of Faith.. you see the
MeW is afraid of my talent.. they know I have what it takes to be the Galaxy
Champion.. and they are afraid.. they knew regardless of what they did.. I would end up with that title.. So what do they do? Call in reinforcements..
Vice-President Aaron Fields.. and they start putting me in matches against people like Kimo Tiger Mask.. Well.. Kimo.. you don't know me.. I don't know you... but you better know one thing come Friday.. and that is I will be the next Galaxy Champion, because I have what it takes.. and you don't.. so you need to step out of my way, or at least make this match easy for me... Because regardless of what you do.. Justin Tyme will get past you.. and go on and beat
Shane Shame, Sabbertooth, Jack.. or whatever two-bit son of a bitch the MeW throws at me.. you can count on that.. Anderson..Odin..Kimo.. See you soon
Scene - He walks off into the distance
