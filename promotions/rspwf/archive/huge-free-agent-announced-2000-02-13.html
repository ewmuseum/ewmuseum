HUGE FREE AGENT ANNOUNCED!

13 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 That's right ladies and gentlemen, the longest reigning Global Pro Wrestling World Champion, the Current New England Wrestling Syndicate Headline Champion, the Runner Up at the National Wrestling Council Road to Purgatory Tournament, the one, the only, Eugene "Sparks/High
Society" Robinson. Feds competed in: Lone Star Wrestling(Under National Wrestling Council) Golden State Wrestling/New England Wrestling Syndicate (Under NWC) and Global Pro Wrestling Titles Won: LSW Regional Championship LSW Television Championship GSW Bay Area Title NEWS Headline Title GPW World Title Looking For: Email Fed (UEW, UWF, GLOW, EMWC, etc.) FACTS for your INFORMATION Weights usually in between 225-230 pounds thus is elgible to spice up your cruiserweight division.
Can run as a face under the nickname "Sparks" or as a heel under the nickname "High Society." Preferably at this time I'd like to be a heel.
SAMPLE Role-play From "Road to Purgatory" Tournament. ----------------- [Fade up.] [We've seen it all. Damon Hayes and Ben Van Iten sending us into a state of humor with their amusing comic escapade, Hyuk Suh Kim being stalked by the neighborhood florist, and even Hunter Sabuani's symphonic melody that sums his ego in the Key of E. Yet, one man, one man who's driven by the blue blood racing threw his veins, seems to once again take us to a place of such magnitude. Forget about the plane rides, we are now witnessing a "work of art."]
[The Sistine Chapel, filled with illuminated musical manusripts and decorated by some of the most artistic and classical masterpiece's the world has ever seen. Perugino's, Botticelli's, and Ghirlandaio's fill the sacred walls while the Michelangelo's ceiling art is one of a kind. A place with dimensions of the Temple of Solomon certainly is suited for a man with such class. What better personal tour guide could we be blessed with than the NWC's very own Eugene "High Society"
Robinson.] [Eugene, clad with a navy DKNY leather bomber jacket with a tan cotton roll neck sweater underneath. As he peacefully walks into the chapel his casual Ralph Lauren jeans squeeze his muscle filled quads and glutes as he holds a clip board in front of his face. We can hear slight pencils sketchings being made as he comes to a rest inches inside the building. To his left, images of the Testimonial and Death of Moses, to his right, a painting of Saint Sixtus II and Saint Felix
I.] [Eugene]: "Intriguing..... Take a look around everyone, what we have before us is more than a work_of_art, it's a masterpiece that will live on forever. Eight years alone it took to construct a place of this caliber, eight years...."
[Pulling the clip board away from his face we get a glimpse of the golden locks slicked back around ears. His baby blues pierce the minds of a dozen or so folks who have gathered around him for the official tour of nobility.]
[Eugene]: "Yet, that isn't even what gets to me the most. The thought of one man alone dedicating himself for four years to please the higher power, the pope. This man, Michelangelo reminds me a lot of someone I know. That someone is me...."
[Egotistical, very..... It's as though his words don't even factor into his mind, he just spits out these comments with no emotion what so ever. Black hearted, a cold soul, call it what you want, but change you will for never do.]
[Eugene]: "For it will not take four years for me to accomplish the feat at hand but four matches if you may. You see the first round is dedicated to sketching out what lies ahead. My opponent, Lee Clark, is nothing but an outline to my future. I look at him and see a soul who once was a man with promise. But Lee Clark was never able to achieve his goal, he fell at the hands of greatness on more than one occasion.
You see folks, unlike the great Michelangelo, Lee Clark failed to draft out his career in advance."
[Several hands raise from tourists in the crowd that is increasingly growing. However, the careless Robinson choose to ignore them and continue his tour.]
[Eugene]: "To our left you will notice a thoughtful panel dedicated to the Testimonial of Moses. This painting reminds me of the very same
Lee Clark for he too gracefully pleasured us with a sobbing story of his past failures. His dreams, his desires, his faults.... But like
Moses, Lee Clark's testimonial will soon be followed by his the dismantling of his existence."
[The camera pans down from the first panel to a painting labeled, the "Death of Moses." Dull colors fill the piece of art as seven weeping men surround the fallen body while one other man stands above him with a sword. Seven soldiers, seven gladiators, seven followers sacrificing for a cause in which they believe in. One man, sword in hand, the leader, the [name withheld] of the bunch.]
[Eugene]: "Much like this painting, Lee Clark too is surrounded by seven men, seven men who are the true believers in there cause. The other man, Lee Clark, he was the outsider who failed his mission for he wasn't a believer. He was an outcast, a lone soul, a man who had his chance to shine and failed. For his own beliefs became the boulder to his liveliness and eventually lead to his death. Lee Clark, may he forever rest in peace."
[Several more hands shoot out from the crowd but Eugene neglects to recognize them once again. Instead, he turns away from them and begins to walk across the wide floor of the Sistine Chapel. Making his way across the now bare grounds of the chapel . His professor like voice echoes threw the nearly empty tourist attraction.]
[Eugene]: "Next up we have the a very unique mural. A rare look at what we believe represents the "Last Supper." Sixteen people, dressed in robes as they sit along side the dinner table. One man, our Lord and savior, sits alone on the other side. Jesus, a man of such perfection, would stand out wherever he went. It wasn't because he was ten feet tall or weighed hundreds of pounds but because of the way he represented himself. Such poise, such finesse, such class...."
[Most of us would disagree as Jesus would represent himself as a humble man dressed like everyone else. Simple robes and garnishes, nothing fancy. Yet, perfection, he hit that one on the dot.]
[Eugene]: "For you see much like myself Jesus stood far and beyond the rest. His god given power is far superior to any other man that you would ever have met in your day. Fifteen other people, sitting around clinging to every word that would leave his mouth, drooling over the preachings that he made. Jesus, well he's everywhere, and he's everything...."
[Comparing himself to Jesus is quite a mouthful. Now, surrounded by nearly thirty people Eugene stands, clip board still in tact, preaching out to those wishing to learn.]
[Eugene]: "But unlike Jesus, a man by the name of Tommy Hardbody believes he truly is Mr. Everything. Tommy, a man from the ranks of the Hawaiian Islands. Such power in his words and confidence in his mind. Shouting towards the big dog [name withheld] to lay down all his cards on the table. But Tommy Hardbody, for once in your life take a second and think to yourself, do I belong at the table with such men as
Eugene Robinson, do I belong at the table of Jesus Christ?" [Some people towards the back who have just walked in on this once in a life time tour from Mister High Society can be seen scratching their heads. They are a bit confused to the ongoing comparisons Eugene continues to speak of. However, they move in towards the speaker of the house listening to his every word.]
[Eugene]: "You see Tommy, chances are you will decide to take a seat at our table, you will place your arms down upon the table cloth and join in on our conversations. You will judge others, even go as far to call them names from time to time. But Tommy my friend, like so many others have learned you can only sit for so long. For if you so happen to find your way dancing into the second round of the tournament you will stand face to face with me, the icon of what's to come. Tommy, if you do decide to sit out our table, I hope you enjoy the supper I bring to you, for it will be your last...."
[Eager tourists begin raising their hands at a rate that practically forces Eugene to finally acknowledge their presence. He rolls his crystalline eyes into his head as he neglectfully waves his hands towards a young female several feet away from him.]
[Female Tourist]: "Mister Robinson? What does this all have to do with the Sistine Chapel?"
[Several spectators nod their heads while a few agreeing voices can be heard saying the smallest of insignificant words. Eugene, feeling disrespected, brushes his fingers threw his hair as he lets out a soft sigh.]
[Eugene]: "People, people, people.... Have you failed to grasp the significance before you. It's as if hundreds of years ago the most religious men in the world built this place knowing it would stand for something so powerful, so spiritual, so significant. When Pope Sixtus
IV della Rovere reigned as the head of the Catholic religion during 1475 he asked Gionvannino de' Dolci to supervise the construction of what you see today. Knowing that the future would hold the key to our well beings he persistently kept on top of this task. For he knew once the religious era of the world was completely over taken by the corrupt necessities that people much like ourselves would crave there would need to be a place to look back on it all. Today is that day. Today we are on the brink of Purgatory."
[Wishing to ignore the crowd before him Eugene simply turns away again. He peers around the Sistine Chapel till his eyes lock on a certain painting. There, only inches away from where he stands is another painting that catches his eye. The camera attempts to zoom in but the hands of viewers block his view. However, we are able to get a quick glimpse of several crowds of people gathering around two men.]
[Eugene]: "Awe! A brilliant piece by Pietro Perugino entitled "Handing over the keys." If you take a minute to focus your attention to the elder man down upon his knees his eyes are that of a young child looking up to another man in envy. This man, he has had his day as the master of the house. This man has had his day in the spotlight, he has been the center of attention, he has his moment of glory. This man, his time has come to hand over the keys to his successor. This man, on his knees, well he reminds me of someone I know of, but have never met face to face. Hunter Sabunai....."
[Here we go.... Two simple words that roll of the tongue of Mister High Society could possibly stir an uproar larger then life itself. A former NWC World Champion, a man that on any given day couple topple the likes of Eugene Robinson.]
[Eugene]: "Hunter, a former Georgia State Heavyweight Champion, a former NWC World Champion. This dog definitely has had his day.
Hunter Sabunai has seen his time in the spotlight and definitely had his fair share of Glory. Hunter has held the keys to not only the GCW but the entire National Wrestling Council. Indeed he has seen his dark days such in a rare loss to Hyuk Suh Kim but the brighter days clearly outshine the rest. There was a day when Hunter Sabunai was just another face in the crowd but the day he defeated Joshua Lee for the
NWC World Title he enlisted himself into the picture of being the next holder of the much coveted keys."
[It seems as though the questions have faded, all eyes are like ice on Robinson as there devoted attention belongs to him. The people, putty in his hands, grasp onto every word that he speaks of, every analysis that he makes.]
[Eugene]: "But it is the day were you place yourself in a position to step away from the shadows of your successor that you are handed down the fortune that you crave for. When Hunter Sabunai turned on Vatch
Indesson he removed himself from the shadows of the men like "Sexy" Steve Sire and White Tiger. The day he became his own person and began to show his true colors was the day that the keys were handed to him.
Ironically enough I too have turned on what you people wanted me to be, something I wasn't. Now, I stand before you as what I truly am, a man of High Society."
[I think we all know where this is going. Eugene, standing as if were on top of the world with his hands at his side and no arch in his back. Top to bottom he represents a class that truly is one of a kind.]
[Eugene]: "But my time has come, much like Hunters, to have the keys handed to me. Hunter, like the man that kneels down in this picture, you are old, you are weak, your time is finished. If you can over step such men as Ben Van Iten, Ahmed Reed, and Blade, you will be forced to stand face to face with me, the successor to your throne, the man that has come to take the keys from you put you in a rest home next to Steve
Sire and Mr. Sinister. Moving on...." [Speechless, the crowd follows Eugene Robinson as he passes up the dozens of pictures along the walls, not even glaring towards the ceiling filled with passionate work above him. Instead, Eugene, as if he knew exactly where he was makes his way towards the North end of the room, to the golden altar.]
[Eugene]: "Here we are, the last painting added to the Sistine Chapel. Fittingly enough, it is entitled the "Last Judgment." Michelangelo revisited the chapel dozens of years later to finish of this amazing piece overlooking the altar that we stand before. Filled with angels carrying everything from columns to the most symbolizing object in all religious beliefs, a cross. But take your minds away from the angels, take your eyes away from the trumpeters spread throughout, if I may direct your attention away from all the cluttered commotion and have you look at something else. Please, take a look in the far right corner, what do you see?"
[The now grown crowd of nearly sixty people move in as close as they possibly can. Hanging their bodies over the red ropes that act as a road block to touching anything worth value. Everyone in the chapel, every soul, mind, body, thought, and eye is locked onto the bottom right corner of the extravagant picture.]
[Eugene]: "What do you see? Yes, you...." [Eugene calls upon a man, middle age, dressed fairly warm. The man, balding head and all, clears his throat as he licks his dry lips in anticipation of speaking. The chatter filled crowd dies down as the man's voice graces them all.]
[Middle aged man.]: "A bunch of naked people..." [Laughter breaks out throughout the crowd as even the arrogant Eugene Robinson can't help but to smile. He shakes his head showing signs of his old self for the first time in over a month. However, his grinning lips quickly fade away as he calls upon another person.]
[Eugene]: "You! Miss, tell me what you see.?" [The women pauses regaining her focus as she too was filled with laughter. She regains her posture as she takes another step closer as she squints her eyes towards the picture.]
[Women]: "I see a bunch of men, fighting, tearing each other apart." [Eugene]: Very good. For those of you who lack the perceptiveness of this young lady I would like for all of you to take look towards what she spoke of."
[Sure enough all eyes are directed towards the bare bodies whose hands are around one anothers throat. A small boat, preparing itself to tip over, a man with a serpent lingering around his chest. Blood, overlapping the skin of every person while deceased bodies lay at some of their feet. This portion of Michelangelo's piece carries the title "The Damned Dragged Into Hell."]
[Eugene]: "These were the men who thought they were above the sins that carry within each and everyone of us. These were the others who thought they were above and beyond the low life that Satan may be.
These warriors, they too fell at the hands of hate, scorn, and death. These men represent who we all have come to know as the "Pierce Bracket." [As people look on confused they restrict themselves from asking questions as Eugene time and time again has found a way to explain everything to them. As several people scratch their heads other nod as if they actually knew what was going on.]
[Eugene]: "These are the men that think they are better then us. These are the men who walk life trying to play by the rules. But the truth is there are no rules, anything goes. These brave men will be forced to place their iron will on the line to see if they can out last the creatures that they shunned for so many years. But like the men who stand behind one Alex Pierce, they too will fall at the feet of
Purgatory...." [Eugene presses the clip board against his chest as he steps threw the gawking crowd. Several people shout towards the NWC U.S. Champion but like the beginning of the tour, he will have nothing of it. The camera pans around the Sistine Chapel one last time it finally comes back with one final shot of Eugene looking back towards the "Last Judgment" and then finally fades to black.]
