  Later, [name withheld] Handler of:  "The Icon" [name withheld](MWA, EWA) *_=MiSERY=_*(EWA) Chris DuBois(Coming Soon!) ----------------------------------   Name: [name withheld]   E-Mail: [detail withheld] or [detail withheld] Wrestler: "Killer" Chris DuBois Height/Weight: 6'1" 220 pounds Face/Heel Neutral:Heel Hometown: Sacramento, California Music: "Mo' Murda" by Bone Thugs -n- Harmony Appearance: Well Built.  Medium Length, Shaggy black hair, stubbled beard.  Wears black tights with the word "Killer" in blood font across the butt in red Blood font. 
Background: Born on March 29th, 1971 to a well-off family in Jacksonville,
Florida.  He grew up in Ashtabula as a happy kid.  He had alot of friends and got almost anything he wanted from his parents.  He was not very religious,but that all changed on his birthday in 1986.  Him, his family and his parents were celebrating his birthday in their living room when a big rig came through their front wall, running over and killing several of his family members.  His father was killed in the freak accident, and his mother was badly injured.  She was hospitalized in serious condition. 
With no other recourse, he turned to God for help.  He prayed every night that his mother would pull through.  But to no avail.  On June 1st, his mother passed away, leaving him with nobody.  Chris was enraged. 
When he heard his mother had dies, he trashed his home, which he had been sharing with his grandparents for the past months, and went on a rampage. 
Over the next 5 years, he managed to spend all of the money his parents had earned on alcohol and drugs.  During that span he was jailed 8 times for drug abuse, attempted murder(found not guilty) and assault.  In 1992, he was found guilty of assault with a deadly weapon, and sentenced to 6 years in prison.  Ehile i prison, it's said that he tried killing himself several times, unsuccesfully.  In 1997 he was let out, claiming to be a new man.  He quickly proved himself wrong immediately, going on the booze again and being arrested for drug dealing.  He was put in rehab, and was released in late May of 1998.  He has been looking for a job, by the advice of his doctors, where he can take out his aggression, and has found his was into the world of professional wrestling.  Now living in Sacramento, his first choice for emplyment is the GSW...
Attitude/Gimmick: He is a crazed man who hates everyone.  He's liable to go off at any second.
Manager/description:NONE(He hates everyone, remember?) Finisher and Description: Serial Splash--An american moonasult from the top rope--Climbs up top, faces away from the ring, jumps and SPINS(not a flip) and lands stomach down on the opponent(or the mat...but hopefully not...).
Favorite Moves: DDT, Piledriver, snap suplex, flying elbow, frog splash, clothesline, huracanrana, drop kick, body slam, fisherman suplex, suicide plancha, baseball slide, legdrag takedown, football tackle, barrage of punches, low blow, kick to the back of the knee, kick to the stomach, swinging neckbreaker...
Other: Often, about five minutes into the match, if he's losing, he will start to go off.  He starts with a kick to the stomach or back of the knee and just goes off, hitting his opponent with a barrage of punches. 
If he wins the match, he will pick up his opponent and throw or push him over the top rope.
He likes to take the match outside, using tables, chairs, the guardrail, the ringpost, the announcers(hehe), and anything else he can get his hands on.
