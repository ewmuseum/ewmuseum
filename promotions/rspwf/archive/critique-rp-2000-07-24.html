Critique RP

24 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens in a busy Japaneese airport. J.B. Destiny has just purchased a ticket and is now waiting for his flight in the lobby. He notices the camera and begins to speak.)
J.B. Destiny: Wow an XBWL camera has come all the way to Japan just so I can get a few words out to my fans back home. I didn't think I would ever see the day. But I also didn't think I would ever be stepping back into an XBWL ring again, I guess is just goes to show you that anything is possible in this crazy business. The reason I'm returning is basically because I don't want to see
The XBWL die. This organazation is a place where legends are made and people become emortalized and when I heard it was going through a hard time I knew I had to do whatever I could to help. Now even though the last time I was part of The XBWL I complained every time I would loose a match, I said that I wasn't treated right and that I deserved more than what was given to me, I said
Micheal Wilkins didn't know what he was doing and he would be sorry when I was gone. It's kinda funny, now that I look back on my actions I can see how very foolish I must have looked. I was complaining about loosing my matches. I mean what did the front office have to do with that??? I'll tell you what...absolutley nothing. I now realize that if I want to win I have to do it myself, and if I loose I can't blame it on other people, I have to take responsibility for my own actions only I can control my destiny. Anyway I knew
I had to help this organazation someway, and there was really only one way I could I had to come back and be a team player. I can no longer be one of the people complaining about every little thing to the other guys in the back. I have to help set the example, you have to show the younger guys who don't care about anything except personal gain that to get something back from The XBWL you first have to put something in to The XBWL. And that is why I'm back. I feel I can be a big part of this organazation and that I can help set The XBWL back on track. And I do look forward to this oppurtunity that I have been given. You see, The XBWL is going through a re-building process right now and that is the perfect oppurtunity to be noticed. The front office are going to be looking for people to step up and become new leaders and like I said I really feel I can be one of thoose leaders.
(J.B. Destiny looks down at his watch to see if his plane should be there soon.) J.B. Destiny: Well it looks like I still have some time left. So I can adress a few more things that I feel are very important. First off, for the guys who were here when I last was you might be asking yourself who this man your watching is, you might be saying this isn't the J.B. Destiny that I remember.
As a matter of fact your proably wondering what happened to the arrogant guy who could care less about what people thought of him??? Well to anwer that I can only say since I've been here in Japan I've been wrestling some men who have made idiots out of themselves by coming out and berating the fans something that I used to do on a daily basis. Anyway theese men, one inpartiular are spreading Nazi propaganda and they just don't know how ignorant they sound and I decided that the fans needed a role model, I thought to myself what kind of message are we sending to theese young and impressionable children who just want to watch wrestling??? I mean if they wanted to hear racist comments and explicit acts they would be watching Jerry Springer not us. So I guess you could basically say I had some sort of epifanee and I realized that to "get over" you don't have to insult the fans all you have to do is go out to the ring and give them what they want and what they want is wrestling. So the answer to the question of what happened to J.B. Destiny is I woke up. I'm sure your going to notice other things that have changed about me too. For one I don't feel the need to cut interviews in which I just drag out subjects and say the same things in different ways, I guess it's all part of the same thing. I just don't feel like I need as much attention anymore. For example before I used to come out and blab on about how I was going to beat my opponent in our upcoming match and I would talk forever. Honestly I don't even know how I did it. And I'm serious when I say that. I mean how many ways can you say your going to beat someone??? Anyway like I was saying the new J.B. Destiny only talks about things that I feel need to be adressed. So you wont see me coming out every other day and talking about how I will win my match. I mean I obviously still have to adress my opponents but I'm not going to drag out the subject I'm only going to talk about things I need to get off my chest. But would you take a look at me, I feel like I'm dragging out this subject right now so I guess it's time to adress my opponents. Umm...
(J.B. Destiny is fishing through his pockets.) J.B. Destiny: Come on I know I have it. Ahh here it is. (J.B. Destiny pulls out a crumpled piece of papper from his pocket.) J.B. Destiny: Well it says here it's The New Icons taking on Slash and Ronnie
Brown. Now I really don't know much about theese two guys. As a matter of fact I know virtually nothing Ronnie Brown and even less about Slash. But what
I do know is that Ronnie Brown at one point claimed to be "The NEW Future".
Reminds me of myself. Oh and by the way that reminds me Thorton I'm not here to start anything back up with you about the name. You say your "The Future"
and I say I am let's just leave it at that for now. But Ronnie Brown you on the other hand have done nothing in this business as far as I know, and you definatley havn't done anything for The XBWL. I on the other hand am a former two time XBWL Tag-Team Champion and my partner is a former Tag and Hardcore champ here, and you and your buddy Slash are victims of bad booking because your going to be stepping into the ring with The New Icons in a tag match.
That's when we're at are most dangerous. And I feel sorry for you I really do as I have been booked to JOB before. Don't feel bad though you and Slash wont be the first team to fall to us and you certaintly wont be the last. Because
Steve Edwards and J.B. Destiny do plan on wearing thoose Unified Tag Titles once again and we wont be denied. As a matter of fact I believe it should be
The New Icons and not The Kleman Brothers who are getting a shot at Sean Dudley for his Tag Titles because if anyone remembers it was The New Icons who beat
High Intensity to be named the number one contenders before we left for Japan and we never got our shot. It doesn't matter though I'm sure we'll have our oppurtunity in know time. But until then Slash and Ronnie Brown you can consider yourselves DESTINED FOR DESTRUCTION!!!
(The Scene fades to black as J.B. Destiny continues to wait for his plane to arrive.)
