At long last... and a roleplay

31 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
At long last, I, [name withheld] have returned to RSPWF! I've been reading, but actually, i believe this is my first post since my John Rocker reply that started all the racism stuff A LONG TIME AGO. Nonetheless, for the newbies, I've been around here for two years, if it matters. Anyway, I felt like putting one of my newest roleplays on here to get some of the critiques going again - I used to post roleplays a lot. Maybe I've gotten a little bit better...
====== [Scene fades in.] [We see a large metal surface with an overhang over it. The overhang and sides of the metal surface are all made out of wood, with weird engravings in the side of them - some symbols, dragons, etc, etc. Also, behind the metal surface is a bench, and a small table located behind that. Suddenly, the metal surafec, which we now know as a grill, lights up in flames right in front of the camera. The camera backs up, and then a medium height man walks into the picture. He has black hair and is dressed all in white with a nice little chefs hat to top it off. He sretches out his arm as if to seat someone at the bench, and "Roman" Thomas Lawrence walks into the picture, and takes a seat on the bench. he says something back to the
Japanese chef, who then walks off.] [Thomas Lawrence is dressed quite nicely tonight, wearing a full Armani suit. The suit is black, with charcoal stripes - Lawrence's favorite.
Underneath the suit, a white dress suit and "Icon" tie is visible. As always, Lawrence has his medium lengthed black hai slicked back and a gold chain hanging from his neck... and a smile on his face as always.]
[Lawrence brushes something off of his suit and straightens his tie. He clears his throat before making a sarcastic first statement to the camera.]
"ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: Greetings ladies and gentlemen... I'd like to introduce you to myself... "Roman" Thomas Lawrence!
[Lawrence laughs at his own, pitiful, joke before becoming serious again.] "ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: That's right folks.... again, the true great, Thomas Lawrence comes through. Making me two and oh in NEO, giving me a 1000 winning percentage, having zero losses..... I believe that deserves some recognition. Am I right? Of course I am. I mean, NEO has finally found themselves a Savior.... a man that's willing to save NEO from total destruction and chaos.... a man that's just _GREAT._ But, even as Jesus performed miracles without an recognition, so do I. Not only did I beat one of NEO's top draws, I beat him senseless. One thing I have to ask is this:
How in God's name can a fifty year old man be a top draw and a "superstar?" Needless to say, he is a superstar no more, for I am the only superstar in NEO... not even Steve Dumars excels more than I, for I am NEO's Savior. But again, I am not here to brag, but here to speak to you people.
[Lawrence laughs.] "ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: And well, I am speaking.... speaking to you saying I am who I say I am, your Savior. But I guess the only way you Canucks are going to believe me is if I prove it, and that I will do. But enough of me telling you Canucks what I am to you, since you don't believe me - just wait and see. And my friends, _WE_ will dominate - keyword there being we. You see, in the English language, we is the subjective plural pronoun....
meaning more than one. The other being my good friend, "The Hitman" Ray Owens. NEO, _WE_ are together, _WE_ are a team, _WE_ are _THE_ team... _WE_ are icons. [Lawrence looks dead into the camera as he says it.] "ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: In th sport of professional wrestling, there are things called stables. Stables, as defined by me, are alliances. These alliances could be between two wrestlers or twenty wrestlers - the number is unlimited. And in these alliances, you may be involved in tag matches with your stable-mates, three-way tag matches, or even Survibor Series. And in a stable, you watch out for friends... and if you don't, then you've probably got a problem. And Ray Owens, I'm telling you right now... if you don't watch my back, I've got your _ASS._
[Lawrence nods.] "ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: But enough talk about Ray Owens and our "stable," per se. Instead, lets talk a little bit about the Pay Per View this Sunday... NEO's first since Thomas Lawrence has arrived, and easily their best to date. Why will it be the best? Well, it's no coincidence that I am on the card, is it? No. I may not be in the Heavyweight Title match... I may not even be in one of the headlining matches, but I guarantee you that my match will be the highlight of the night. Or wait, I take that back...
not my match, but _ME._ I will be the highlight of the night... I guess you could say all spotlights will be on me. I'll make my presence in Canuck
Land known... [On that, the Japanese chef walks back and sits down a foreign beer in front of Lawrence. Lawrence takes a drink, and hands it back to the chef. He then says a few things to the chef, who with the beer in hand, turns and walks out of the picture.]
"ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: Foreign alcohol.... why was I stupid enough to order that crap? Anything foreign sucks... including beverages, most food, and even the people. I mean... Canada for example. Canadian food...
whatever it is, it sucks. Canadian people, Canucks, suck. I guess Canada is "Land of Suck." I mean, everyone in NEO blows, except for myself and Ray
Owens. And all of those Chinese and Japanese people on the roster, you suck too. The only good foreign thing is Chinese and Japanese food - they can cook. Everything else sucks...
[The Japanese chef walks back into the picture and sits a glass of water in front of Lawrence. Lawrence nods at him, and the chef walks off.]
"ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: There's one Japanese food I dislike... Sushi. Who in their right mind would eat raw fish? Well, other than dumbass people, no one. And apparently, someone in NEO likes it so much that he decided to give himself the name "Sushi" - what a dumbass. Sushi Tsunami, Raw Fish
Tidal Wave, you're one sick person. Not only are you stupid enough to have a dumb name, but you're so disrespectful that you come to this country, be in Canuck Land or the United States, and you don't even know how to talk the language. Not, it's understadable here in Canada, because half the people here are illiterate, but in the United States, it's totally disrespectful.
Sushi, I'm your daddy... [Lawrence shakes his head in despair.] "ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: And you, John Adams, have the nerve to use the same name as a former president of the United States of America. And you can't use the excuse that you're a Canuck, because you're from the United
States... but then again, you're from San Francisco, home of the Giants. But, like I said, you have the nerve to use the same name as one of our early forefathers.... a great man. And then, you're an ego-driven maniac.
What kind of person would be like that?! [Lawrence smiles and laughs.] "ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: Certainly not I, not Mr. Modesty.... [awrence straightens his suit and tie again. He stretches, and then becomes very serious.]
"ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: Sushi Tsunami, John Adams... you two better be ready come Sunday. My friends, you can work together for all I care - it doesn't really matter. If you do, it'll just be less work for me, because
I'm sure I can use each of you on each other in some way. But, you see, whatever you do, you won't win, because I am "Roman" Thomas Lawrence, your
Savior. And NEO, I hope after Sunday you finally realize that I am your Savior... I am the man I claim to be - I'm not a "false prophet." NEO, you have alot to learn, and I'm willing to be your leader, because leadership is what you lack.
[Lawrence takes a drink of water.] "ROMAN" THOMAS LAWRENCE: NEO, I will win... I will impress... and I will take the _WHOLE_ show because...
______I'M JUST GOOD LIKE THAT______ [Scene fades to black.]
