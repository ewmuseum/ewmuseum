[FLASH] Tommy Grayson - Constructive Criticism, Comments...

30 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I think this is a pretty damn good RP. Probably one of the best I've written in a while, so, I want you guys to give me some constructive criticism on it.
Thanks. BTW...excuse the excessive cursing...or, at least I THINK it's excessive. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [SCENE: Riverside Park in New York City. It's morning and the sun is just coming up. Birds are chirping and there are people jogging in the park. The camera pans from a shot of the buildings nearby to the pathway in the park, where a man is doing some warm-ups. The man appears to be Tommy Grayson. Grayson is wearing a black hooded sweatshirt, a pair of tear-away Adidas pants with three stripes on each side, and a pair of black Nike sneakers. Grayson is also sporting a red bandana with a pattern of a skull and crossbones over his crewcut black hair and he's also wearing his signature mirror-shaded sunglasses. He appears to be clean-shaven. He stops doing his warm-ups and looks straight at the camera and begins walking towards it. As he strides towards the camera, he pulls off the sunglasses and hangs them on the collar of the t-shirt that he's wearing under his sweatshirt. He glances into the camera with his absolutely
stone-cold brown eyes.] TOMMY GRAYSON: Well, I think it's appropiate to say that it's gonna be "crunch time" come next Sunday. Before I talk about the two punks I'm going to be facing at Countdown, I wanna talk about my rival, Tommy "Lord"
Lionheart. "Lord"? {chuckles} Lionheart, it's in my belief that you should be called "The Sore" because you're just a God damn pain that'll never go away no matter what the hell I do. I could beat your ass, I could berate you with insults, I could throw sticks and stones at you, and you still wouldn't haul ass. I'm just plain sick and tired of you, Lionheart. I'm sick of your insipid whining and your pathetic verbal masturbation. First, you sit there on your ass telling the whole world that I'm a piece of crap and I'm nothing but scum all because you were so naive and all because you couldn't stand the pain of getting the crap beaten outta you. Can't you accept responsibility for your own shortcomings? You're just like a little boy, Lionheart...a little boy who runs to his room and starts crying and whining on his God damn pillow. You're a little boy who needs a good old traditional, American-style beating. {chuckles} And in my opinion, I think I should be the one to give you that beating. So, Tommy Lionheart, this BS ends as soon as damn possible.
I want you in a match on Friday Night Fallout. I don't give a damn when and I don't give a damn where...and I want it in a steel cage. No interference from my boys in Devastation Inc....no interference from those maggots in Heaven N'
Hell. Especially that has-been loser, Lord Humongus. I'm gonna make you into what you wanna be, Lionheart. I'm gonna lay you out like Jesus, kid. I'm gonna leave you shivering and I'm gonna leave you for dead with everybody staring. The vultures are gonna be circling around your ass, Lionheart. I guarantee that. Tommy Grayson will put an end to ALL the whiny bitches out there, whether you LIKE IT...or NOT.
[Grayson begins to walk down the pathway of Riverside Park. The camera follows him, and he looks at the camera as he's walking.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: Now, let's get to my match at Countdown. As a lot of people know out there, that rat-bastard James Pillow decided to put me up against
Matty Dee and Arthur Essex. These guys are nothing but a pair of God damn jabronies. Matty Dee hasn't said a damn thing, and I'll tell you why: Because he's afraid of Tommy Grayson. Let's face it...when he hears the name Tommy
Grayson, his underwear is yellow one side and brown the other. I don't really give a damn about you anymore, Dee. Why? Because you're nothing but a piece of crap who hits and runs. At Countdown, when you go to hit, I'm gonna hit you as hard as I've hit every-damn-body else. I'm gonna gut you like a fish, kid. You better believe that, because Tommy Grayson is undoubtedly the most dangerous man in International Championship Wrestling right now. Now, Arthur
Essex...this is a kid with spirit. This is a kid with integrity. But when I look into his eyes, I see that he doesn't have much resiliance as I do. I can beat you. I know I can. God damn, Essex...if you truely think in your heart that you can beat my ass...well, you've got another thing coming. If you also think that Evan Manchester is gonna be there to save your ass from total devastation, well, you've got another thing coming. If he comes down, my associates from Devastation Inc. will come down and, well, DEVASTATE him. I guarantee you, Arthur Essex and Matty Dee...your blood will be in my hands when the match is over. I will be standing above two unconcious carcasses, and I will go on to face either Shane Diamond or Westside Walaven. It doesn't matter which. They'll still get the absolute crap beaten outta them by Tommy
Grayson.
[Grayson pauses for a few moments and then continues.] TOMMY GRAYSON: {chuckles} Somebody told the press I was once again attained by the authorities, except this time in St. Louis. I'll tell you...the food in their jail is great. It's better than the rest of the food in that two-bit city. The fact is, some jabronie came up to me and stuck their middle finger up at me...I didn't give a damn at first...but then I said to myself, "What kind of example would I be making to the fans if I didn't...{pauses} BREAK the man's fingers"?
[Grayson stops walking.] TOMMY GRAYSON: So, that's what I did. I broke his God damn fingers...and then, as an added bonus, I broke his nose. Speaking of breaking noses, there's a guy out there who needs a broken nose. That would be the Klansman of ICW...L-Mo. This guy went to Kuba's Club Violator and insulted a bunch of
Latinos. Well, L- Mo was already expecting a beating from Kuba Rico after that. Then, I heard that he went and said that Devastation Inc. was a bunch of morons. {smacks his teeth} How stupider and more moronic can you get,
L-Mo? First you're about to get the crap beaten outta you by one of the most dangerous men from the most dangerous group, and then, you wanna have the crap beaten outta you by the WHOLE damn group? Well, you'll have your wish granted. Things can be done. People can be spoken to. Your ass will be done for when D.I. gets to you, boy. You and your little bitch, Jason Creed. I guarantee you...Devastation Inc. has things in store for the ICW. Very, very, very destructive and quite Devastating things. {chuckles} All of you jabronies out there will find out in due time.
[Grayson puts back on his mirror-shaded sunglasses.] TOMMY GRAYSON: You pieces of crap in ICW are on line. On line for a one-way ticket to Grayson-ville...that would be another name for HELL. Tommy Grayson has a dog-eat-dog sly smile, he's got a molotov cocktail with a match to go, he smokes his cigars in style, and I can tell you, Kennedy Chambers...you can make my money ANY night.
Live if you dare, die because you must...{brief pause} BITCHES!! [Grayson walks away from the camera, and begins jogging down the pathway. FADE.] -- Handler of: Tommy Grayson [EWA, ICW, CWC, EFW] 13 Championship Titles (more to come!) "Live if you dare, die because you must." "They're out ta get me! They won't catch me!
