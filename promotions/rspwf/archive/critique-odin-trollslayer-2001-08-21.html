Hatebreeder (Running Underhook Powerbomb) ************************* Strengths and Weaknesses ************************* Strengths: 1. Veteran: Has been around the sport for many years, and has traveled all over the world. Because of this he can easily outwit a mat technician or know when to get out of dodge when a Luchador is ready to fly.
2. Powerhouse: He's nothing short of a walking brick wall. Pushes himself every day to train his hardest, and as a result has acquired one of the more impressive physiques in the sport. This allows him to pummel most opponents into submission before they've gotten a chance to mount any offense at all.
3. Sadist: There's nothing Odin loves more than to see the blood of the enemy. Will go that extra mile just to punish an opponent. This works for him as well, as the sight of his own blood will only get him to go to insane heights to beat his opponent bloody and lifeless.
Weaknesses: 1. Ego: While Odin will readily admit that anyone can beat anyone given the right circumstances, one has to wonder if he truly believes this rule applies to him. He truly believes that the end of his every match
is merely a foregone conclusion, and will often set himself up for a roll up pin from an opponent he believes to not be in his league.
2. Showboating: Odin has gotten the nickname of "The Most Over Man Alive" quite honestly. When he feels he has an opponent sufficiently winded, he will not hesitate to showboat for the fans. A wily opponent could use this against Odin, playing possum and getting the roll up pin while Odin was posing.
3. Sadist: This can also be a flaw, as often Odin's thirst for carnage will supercede his need for a victory. He has been disqualified many times because of this, even in matches that he could have easily won.
*********************** Character's Personality *********************** Character's Gimmick[Be unique, creative, and descriptive]: Odin has retained much of his old "Deathcow" gimmick. Deathcow was often described as a "Homosexual Kane". Meaning a very dark character with very over the top homosexual overtones. Odin has gone so far as to retain some of the Deathcow ring attire, and many of the same wrestling maneuvers he used as Deathcow. Odin is very charismatic, yet intimidating. He keeps tight-lipped about his true sexual preference, as he knows it creates a mystique that keeps the fans coming in droves.
Character's Personal History [Be descriptive]: Odin's parents, like many of their fellow countrymen, had a great love for Norse and Viking mythology. Such was their love for it that not only did they have their last names legally changed to "Trollslayer", when they're firstborn son was born they placed the name of "Odin"
upon him. Just as the mythical Odin was king of the Gods, Odin Trollslayer was king of the playground, using his superior size and strength to pummel his unwitting classmates to a bloody pulp. Rather than punish him, his parents rewarded him with love and affection every time he was sent home from school for nearly crippling a fellow classmate. The school system, however, felt differently. Odin was expelled from public school and sent home where his parents, in the fashion of parents many millions of years ago, sold Odin into slavery.
It was at this point in his life that he went from a loving, if bizarre, home to one of ridicule and beatings. When he was old enough, he was sold to a man who ran pit fighting tournaments. While using nothing but his bare hands and brute strength was usually enough to subdue an opponent, Odin quickly took a liking to fighting with a simple wooden staff, which he still brings to him in wrestling arenas all over the world. After a while, his great success caught the attention of Henry Garfield, an american wrestling promoter on vacation in Norway. He signed Trollslayer as soon as he saw this brute of a man in action. Now a free man, Odin was completely loyal to
Henry. Upon arriving in the states, Odin was unhappy with the gimmick given to him. He was dressed in cow tights and a leather mask, given the gimmick of a "Homosexual Kane" as Henry put it. Even though he felt humiliated having to pretend to be an over the top gay character, he stayed loyal to the man that made his freedom possible. That is, until the day that Henry ran off with all that the promotion Odin had worked for made, never to be heard from again. Odin then wandered the country, getting any independent wrestling bookings he could get, as well as taking on countless odd jobs. That all stopped when he got a telegram from Taurus, a wrestler he was friendly with backstage in the
Henry-owned wresting promotion. Taurus had a job for him. A job, and a new home. Unfortunately, Odin had a hard time getting back in the swing of things in the new promotion, mostly due to the fact that he had to make up for the show-boating nonsense that "Hot Shit" Rick
Sturgis, who he was now teamed with, perpetrated. The result was a less than sparkling debut, and Odin happily being let out of his contract a short time later by the promotion's president. Odin then went back to making money from several independent bookings as
Deathcow, the gimmick that gave him a rather comfortable living. He finally settled down for a time in the Revolutionary Wrestling Order, where he instantly captured the Hardcore Title. Unimpressed with the level of competition there, he dropped the title, thanked Steven Bunn, the owner of RWO for the opportunity, and returned to his new home of
Boston to reflect on his life and decide what to do next. His timing couldn't have been better. After a few days back home, he received a call from BSCW President / Owner Richard Vail. Vail had an opportunity of a lifetime, a chance to be Commissioner of the fed that gave Odin his biggest break, and on top of that, to share duties of the commissioner with his long-time friend, Hannibal Carver. Needless to say, Odin took the job. In time, Odin saw that life as a higher up wasn't for him. Along with Carver, they relinquished the commissionership to Philippe LaFleur. Since then, the wrestling bug has bit Odin yet again, causing him to leave his comfy job in search for new competition.
