God's Justice Role-Play please comment on

4 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings RSPWF'ers, I am posting below a copy of _ONE_ of God's Justice's role-plays, please tell me what you think of it. Just no recruits as I shall ignore them all. Comments either on the board or mail them to me.
========================= God's Justice Role-Play ========================== [Scene: Fades into the Church of Salvation. A banner which reads "JUSTICE IS SALVATION!", is then seen hanging from the rafters. The banner then continues to sway, back and forth. A light is then switched on and then the cameras just begin to fade downwards onto the benches.
Three figures are then seen seated on these solid oak benches. The camera then passes over the figures. It then fades out as a dark yet, serene voice comes on.]
V: "This is the time, our time. This is the place, our place. Justice, Our Justice. God, Our God. Worries, no not ours but they shall be yours." [A loud laughter is then heard as the following then just hits the screens.] _______ / /\ / / / / / / __________/ /_/________ / / / /G O D S J U S T I C E / / /__________ _________/ / \_________/ / _______/ / / / /_______\/ / / / /______/ / \______\/ "JUSTICE IS SALVATION!" [The display then changes once more as the camera begins to close in, listening to a soft voice.]
V: "Sin..Remorse.. Hatred..Repent Evil..Be calm Justice.. God's Justice Falsehoods.. Mallice ALL BE STILL! FOR THE LORD SHALL COME AND HE SHALL HEAL!!" [Fades out as the light in the Church then just begins to flicker on and off. The lights then dim down and then with a sudden outcry, all that is heard are the loud footsteps of people walking across the old stone floor. The camera then fades back down a little to see somebody realling around on the floor. A loud voice then cries out.]
V: "And I heard a loud voice in Heaven say, Now is come SALVATION, and Strength, and the Kingdom of our God and the power of his
Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which is accused them before our God, day and night(Rev 12:10)."
[The cameras then fade downwards towards the Alter of Grace. Father Prior is seen standing by.] [Appearance: Father Prior props himself up against the sweet savour of the Alter. The elixer which is given off bringing nothing but kindred hearts and minds to God. The Father then just turns and you can see he is wearing an everyday pair of black trousers, black dress shoes and over his shoulder he has on a vestment. Father Prior then begins to speak in a humble serene manner.]
FP: "Once more my children have slain. The evil of the place they go to. This place, the TWL. They claimed they were tough, yet nothing is greater then the Justice which God grants. Now come children follow me, hinder me not for the quest is near. God's Justice is coming and people of the world, understand that nothing can seperate us from the
Love which the _ALMIGHTY_ shows us.. Thus once again as I render forth my Disciples, they shall not be slain. For the lamb was slain and that was that, this is this and now
TWL. Once more, they call out in battle to deal with the realms of wickedness and thus now they shall be answered.."
[Appearance: Two figures then stand by the right hand side of Father Prior. The two figures can be seen wearing distinctive black leather jackets with a golden cross enblazoned on the back with the slogan which reads "JUSTICE IS SALVATION!"]
[The cameras then turn towards Great Jackson who then just stands there with his hands down by his side, then in a sweet, yet humble voice begins to speak forth the Words of the Lord.]
GJ: "The TWL. They weren't so tough. We sure showed them what God's Justice is all about. And now I hear we are being called the #1 Tag Team on the planet, I think not. For nothing, no riches, no titles, nothing shall seperate us from the Love of GOD! Thus it came to pass that on that evening, Big Tyrone and Harold, just couldn't meet the criteria needed to defy the Willpower of the Disciples of Christ..
They came at us with all they had. Yet still, only God prevailed. Yet The Historian, he thinks Harold was in trouble, so he leaves us to deal with Big Tyrone. What kind of man is that..Well there shall come a time, one day that you and Harold meet us, and then you shall be taught a lesson in Justice. Our Justice, not Injustice, but God's
Justice.." [Crusader Richard Justice then turns to Father Prior and then sheds a tear.] FP: "What my child is the meaning of this sorrow?" [The Crusader then just sighs.] CRJ: "Oh Father. I am in remorse for what we did to Tyrone and Harold. If only they had have understood us, then maybe things would have been a little bit different. But on the contrary, they kept quiet and thus God opened their hearts and minds to our wills and ways. Yet this Historian, he teaches them nothing but facts. Why Historian? Why don't you teach them morals?
[Pauses] It's because you don't have any. And as Great Jackson clearly said, some time down the road we shall end up facing off with each other, but for now we have smaller fish to fry. For the Assassins, they go about their ways. Yet what of them? Assassins, are you really going to try to assassinate us? Well you can try, but as the Word of the
Lord clearly speaks "Without understanding Covanentbreakers, without natural affection, implacable, unmericifal: Who knowing the judgement of GOD that they which commit such things are worthy of death, not only do the same, but have pleasure in them that do them
(Romans 1: 31-32)." [Great Jackson then turns to the Father and then speaks.] GJ: "Thus as The Crusader has just read. Assassins, thou commit crimes which are pleasureable to thine own self. And the penalty inflicted by the Lord GOD himself, thus come March 5th, thou shalt surely know why shou shalt surely die! Yet not an eartly die, but a spiritual die. All ties that you have or have ever had for God's Kingdom shall be erased before your eyes, for Assassins, all you know of is complete evil and it's our time now, the Calling of the Lord God to jugde you all, for you will know what Justice is all about, for JUSTICE IS SALVATION!!"
[The cameras then fade out as the following words just hit the screen.] "..The Promise shall come, the Angelic Promise and thus then all shall know why Justice, our Justice, God's Justice is the only way for
Faith can firmly trust him come what may.." [Fades out as the cameras close up as God's Justice then just walk up the aisle. Then fades to black.]
