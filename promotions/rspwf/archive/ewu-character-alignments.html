This one comes from [name withheld] originally --------- Early this morning, with some of the WWO handlers (and some Cafe di Ameretto.....), a discussion came up, that considering that most people believe the Heel-face dividing line is pretty much gone, there had to be a way to successfully classify characters and their actions. There are the people who are downright mean, there are people who are "goody- two-shoes", and yet there are faces who will cheat, steal, backstab if the purpose is good. You've got people who write up characters who do despicable things, yet the fans love them for it. why? If they're doing rotten, evil things, shouldn't they be classed as heels and booed? So with the help of the handlers of Tyrone "Purple" Hayes ([name withheld]) and "The Prodigy" Tracy Hudson (Tracy Hudson), we used a game system that the three of us knew, and felt had a pretty good availability o "alignments". You've still got your good, your evil, and your selfish, but you can get a little closer to identifying where your character fit in the overall scheme of things.. So wihtout further ado, I'd like to introduce you to the new WWO alignment scale. (It has been officially put in place as of 6AM CST, on 3 December 1999..so those of you who are in teh WWO who view it..take a look and see if our ideas for your character match where you think they belong? ====================================================================== "Face" Alignments Principled A principled character lives by a strict code of ethics. There is a definate right and wrong in the world for this type of character, and they attempt to right those wrongs. There are certain lines that cannot be crossed though, and these sorts of wrestlers will do all that they can within the rules to bring wrong do'ers to justice. A principled character is the "ultimate" face. Example: Fang Scrupulous People of this nature know right and wrong, and believe that those in the wrong should be brought to justice... but if the rules are preventing this, they will bend the rules so that justice can be served. The adage of fight fire with fire can be brought to light with this sort of character. Example: Apex "Neutral" Alignments Unprincipled The unprincipled wrestler understands that there are rights and wrongs in the wrestling world. However, as long as they can stretch a "few" of those rules, and don't get caught, they're still okay. As long as the damage done isn't devastating, and the repercussions don't have a long-term negative effect. An unprincipled character will be a nearly "facish" character, as he knows the rules, and will tend to abide within them, with only the occasional 'line crossing'. Example: "Money Driven' Mike Sebastian, Thunderstorm Taoist Whatever is fun or enjoyable is what is right. A person of this sort seeks out what they like and strives for that above other things. It doesn't matter where something came from or how they got it, as long as they can have fun using it. If someone doesn't understand the humour of a situation, they're missing out on the purpose of the whole thing. Insults and "fun scenes" are more important to a Taoist than an assault or sneak attack on an opponent. Example: Tyrone "Purple" Hayes Anarchist To the anarchist style of wrestler, the social norms are simply roadblocks to allowing someone to be their best. The anarchist wrestler may or may not be a "me, me, me" type of person, as they may or may not care about their own best interests, as long as they're allowed to push the boundaries of standards and practices. Example: Roundhouse Selfish Much like an unprincipled person, only one who works within a system, and as long as the system benefits the selfish wrestler. These people are usually quite aware that their actions can cause misery, but it doesn't bother them. The rules apply only when they are are beneficial. When the laws in place begin to become a burden, they are discarded. The only thing that matters is how things can best serve the person, not the other way around. Example: "White Hot" Darren Morgan "Heel" Alignments Aberrant Honor among theives. People of this nature have a code of ethics that governs their lives. They abide by this code, and usually the actual "law". Above what the accepted social norms are. They will not break their code, and have honor. That's not to say they won't cheat and use shortcuts. On the contrary, the nature of the sport clearly allows it, and they know it. Example: "Mr. Ratings" Warren Hayes Miscreant A person of this sort is someone who causes grief for kicks. Someone who seems to enjoy making life miserable for other people. They do have some lines that they won't cross... but not many. Most "heelish" e-wrestlers would probably fall in this category if they are true heels. Example: Tracy Hudson, The Outlaws Diabolic Those few that fall into this catagory are evil, plain and simple. There is no level to which these people will go to achieve their goal. Backstabbing, trickery, or whatever else is necessary to get what they want are tasks done without guilt. And it may not be for any natural goal. The opportunity is there, hurt your opponent, and maim him/her intentionally. Example: Latex Lex, Fury ====================================================================== Now granted, some of you won't recognize the WWO names, but I think you could find a spot to fit your own characters in, or spots where you know other characters now go? The three of us, Dave, Tracy, and myself, being Palladium gaming fans (Robotech, Mystic China, TNMT, Rifts, etc.. for those of you who don't recognize the name), decided to use the 9 available alignments since we know the game system fairly well, and tried to apply them to the wrestling world. Now granted, everyone won't fit in all alignments, but you could have a character of one alignment leaning towards another, and it would help in a character's turn, if it's a gradual turn. And it would be useful i you were turning a character from heel to face, as now you don't have to go from the most "corrupt creature in wrestling" to a "pure- goody goody". I mean, you want to turn someone such as Craven for instance, since most people know his name. Well, right now IMO, Craven is as diabolic as you can get, although he may have a twisted code of reality, that makes him a potential aberrant.. Moze, is there a code of reality that Craven does have? If there is, then he'd be diabolic w/ aberrant leanings. If not, he's strictly diabolic. There is no way, IMO, Craven can turn o a top of the line face without losing credibility. So instead of turning him from face to heel, you've got a series of steps that you could watch/work Craven through.. Starts as diabolic... could work his way up to Taoist, and even possible, after some more time, could work his way to Unprincipled. Could Craven get higher? Not likely, but even at Taoist, according to the above chart, he could actually get some "legitimate face pops" and be believable by "average John Q. Public" watching the shows. For instance, Jerod (otherwise known as KrazyAce on the newsgroup), runs the tag team of fire and ice. For their entire stint in the World Wrestling Organization, the duo was Scrupulous. They knew the laws, and only broke them when they had to. Well, one of his characters got into a bitter feud with a singles character, and Ice began to change. He would attack Lord alexander Bennington all the time. He usually had a reason for it, but there were a number of additional unprovoked attacks by Michael Ice. So Ice began sliding down the chart, pushed far enough, he became Unprincipled in his actions. Then. Michael Ice and ALex Perkins turned (probably teh biggest story in the WWO tag ranks when it happened), and became Anarchistic. Because he dropped so far (3 ranks), he started getting boos. Perkins, on teh other hand while not as hated, turned Anarchistic as well, as his name was attached to Ice. So when the final turn came, thanks to the slide, it was a simple matter of going to the heel ranking of Miscreant for the duo. A progressive natural turn for a tag team who was very popular, and now became hated...all of which was satisfactorily "stepped" through on the above chart... Anyway... that's the general idea... I was wondering what people thought about it? Are there classifications that we missed? Are there people who simply cannot be classfied by the above list? And if so, why not? WHere do you see your character fitting in? Where do you see other characters fitting? Does it help/hurt in the way of character creation? Does it help/hurt in character growth? What do you think?
