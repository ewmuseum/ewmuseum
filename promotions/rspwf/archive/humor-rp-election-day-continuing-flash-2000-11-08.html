[ CuMM reporter Monica Blewklinski , who you can see is holding a cigar, wiping her lip and looking at the stain on her dress comes on air, and gets her cue from the cameraman, she nervously ditches the cigar ( we won't say where it went for public purposes ) and stares at the camera.. she stands outside of an office door,... the letters behind her read G.D.W.A., 2 Secret Service Agents stand there , guarding the door ]
Monica Blewklinski : Monica Blewklinski, reporting for CuMM here, with some
Breaking News on the Presidental Election.
It seems that with the fact being we've got political gridlock here, and
Congress, after deciding they did not know what to do, makes what appears to be the most ~unconventional~, ~unprecedented~, ~unbeliveable~, ~unconcieveable~ political move in history... has decided to let the Wrestlers and Officials of the "Grand Dragon Wrestling Alliance" *confused look* decide who *actually* won this election, I'm told that the Secret Service has secured all the Officials and Wrestlers from GDWA, and they are all behind this door.. *pointing to the
GDWA offices* casting their ballots at this very moment... even though SOME of these wrestlers and officials are not American Citizens...
Like I said before, this ruling came down from Congress, as they could not decide what to do... I'm told they came to the decision to let these "women wrestlers" decide who becomes president when The Speaker of the House made a comment, in jest I'm told,.. and the rest of Congress agreed to his surprise..
his comment was, and I quote; "Hell,Since we can't ever agree on anything, except for the fact we all like to watch GDWA Tuesday Night Catfight , why not let those babes decide.. they'd probably do a better job of it than us,.. plus the fact that they're all hotties too doesn't hurt... "
*She looks quizzically at the camera and wipes her lip* And after that comment, Congress decided to round up these women, thier officials and let them decide the future of this country... And I , Monica
Blewklinski, *smiling like she was the Cheshire Cat who just swallowed a bird, or maybe "swallowed Clinton's load would be a better metaphor " have recieved exclusive coverage rights for this event from The President, for my network..
CuMM. I'll be polling these men and women as they leave this door, since, the only condition that they said they'd do this "favor" for the country is that each and every one of them that wanted got a few minutes of exclusive interview time on CuMM.
Oh.. the doors are opening.. and here comes someone.... Excuse me.. Miss.. MISS!!.. [ A puzzled Heidi Leick comes walking into view, dressed in a Leather MC
Jacket, Jeans, Combat boots, and a "SCREW THE PMRC" T Shirt on, she stops when she sees the camera ]
Heidi Leick: Oh.. ~you've~ got the camera.. is this thingy on? Monica Blewklinski: It sure is... that's what the little red light means, or so they tell me... Sometimes I think the guys black it out when they leave it in my room though, I mean it always SEEMS like it's running when we're partying..
and I always black out and wind up naked in bed with sticky stuff in my hair and on my dresses... but that's not why we're here today..
*Heidi, looking at her like she's insane, rolls her eyes * Heidi Leick: Well? You wanted something? Monica Blewklinski : Yes, I've been granted the exclusive post Presidential vote interviews with you GDWA employees, and in return, you get to say whatever you want in a few minutes of ~LIVE~ National Airtime, of course..
Heidi Leick: Live? Hmm.. *smiling evilly* Well go on woman.. ask away... Monica Blewinski: Monica Blewklinski here.. Heidi Leick: * stepping back and looking at the floor, disgustedly..* She did? Umm, befroe we start this.. can you tell me ~Where~... ~Exactly~, so I know what spot to avoid standing on... it could be evidence you know..
Monica Blewklinski: Huh? *looking really confused now, then suddenly realizes what Heidi means*... no,...NO!.. Oh my.. Monica Blewklinski that's ~my~
~name!~ Pleased to meet you.. And You're name is? *Monica sticking out her hand to shake Heidi's, Heidi declines* Heidi Leick: Wait.. who do you work for? Monica Blewklinski: CuMM! *smiling larger than life* ( Heidi looks at the camera , shaking her head ) Heidi Leick: No woman!.. I said WHO do you work for, not WHAT! They send you here, to ask ME questions, and you don't know who I am? ( Monica Blewklinski shakes her head no ) Heidi Leick: Well Dingbat, consider yourself lucky enough to be in the presence of a SUPERSTAR.. and one who's in a good enough mood to tell you who she is..
I'm famous all over the world, .. a movie star... an actress... a WRESTLING
LEGEND.. I'm Heidi Leick.. now what in the world do you want..
Monica Blewklinski: Well Miss Leick.. what I, and the people of America want to know,.. is Who did you cast your ballot for; Bush, or Gore?
Heidi Leick: Neither.. of course... Monica Blewklinski: Huh? Wait.. I was told you all were deciding who should be
The President in there, and the choices were Bush and Gore?
Heidi Leick: Oh yeah, that's what they ~said~ we had to do... choose the President.. and I did.. *smiling psychotically*... Monica Blewklinski: Well then, who did you choose? Heidi Leick: The most capable, most qualified person to run the Country.. the one to make everyone happy..
Monica Blewklinski: And that is? Heidi Leick: LEICK you Don't know.. It's ME of course! Monica Blewklinski: But?... But?... I Don't understand.. it's supposed to be
Bush or Gore..

Monica Blewklinski: I don't know.. Heidi Leick: Come on Dingbat, that would be a natural for someone with a name
LEICK yours..
Monica Blewklinski: my name? Monica Blewklinski? Heidi Leick: STOP saying that.. P~L~EASE.. it gives me the creeps... anyways
.. it's natural.. BUSH and DICK... I LEICK that combination.. it just seems..
natural.. not out of my way.. I have a plane to catch and a movie to make... ( Heidi storms off ) Monica Blewklinski: Well there you have it.. One Ballot cast for BUSH and
DICK... Monica Blewklinski here, waiting for our next interview...
