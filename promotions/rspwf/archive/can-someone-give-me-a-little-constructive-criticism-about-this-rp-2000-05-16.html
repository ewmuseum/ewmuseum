Can someone give me a little constructive criticism about this rp....

16 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
...but just a little?! :) First and this will probably upset some people, but I am involved in an
E-fed that uses real wrestlers, but encourages us to develop our characters so that they aren't total carbon copies. Like right now, my wrestler is the
Rock (please, keep the boos to a minimum! :-p) and I sort of have him being a little on the schizophrenic side. I know, where's the development, but anyway! This is my latest response to my opponent for Wednesday, Mankind.
Please feel free to tell me what I can do to improve my rp style and content, as I have only been doing this for about 5 months. All constructive comments will be appreciated. Here it is....entitled "InRing Promo at
Tuesday Terror" Thanks! [Fade in to a shot of a sold out Secret Cove in Alexandria, Virginia, where
Tuesday Terror is originating from. As the camera pans the crowd, Jim Ross'
voice can be heard] JR : Welcome to Alexandria, Virginia for the EiW's Tuesday Terror. I'm Jim
Ross and beside me is my co-host tonight, filling in for Jerry Lawler,
Michael Cole. Before we get to the action, there's a bit of videotape that we'd like to run.
[As JR says that, a tape showing the last few minutes of Mankind's remarks about the Rock plays on screen. As the tape ends and the camera comes back to a shot of JR & Michael Cole, the Rock's music hits, causing the arena to fairly come alive.]
MC : That's the Rock's music, JR. Obviously he has a few things left to say about Mankinds' remarks from last night.
JR : You're right Michael, but what could the Rock say after his scathing remarks last night?
MC : We're about to find out JR! [Rock steps out through the curtain, clad in an electric blue colored silk shirt, black slacks, black dress shoes, and his trademark shades. He makes his way down to the ring, ignoring the fans, and steps through the ropes, motioning for a mic as he does. The ring announcer Lillian Garcia hands one to him and walks away as the Rock lifts the mic to his lips to speak]
Rock : You know Mankind, when the Rock saw your promo calling him disloyal, at first it pi**ed him off. But as the People's champ sat back and thought about it, in a way, you're absolutely right. The Rock's sole loyalty is to himself. Not Ric Flair, not Dean Malenko, not the Road Dogg, not the EiW, but himself. And if anybody else says they won't look out for themselves first in this business, Mankind, they're either lying or they are incredibly naive. As far as Reckless Youth was concerned, it was a spur of the moment thing. In hindsight, it wasn't the best of ideas. Would the Rock align himself with them again if he had the chance? Probably not, but that's in the past. The Rock doesn't care about the past. He cares about the present and the future. What gives the Rock the best chance of succeeding in the
EiW. Now, Mankind, if you are too busy worrying about being loyal to someone, what's the chances you'll do what's best for you? Slim to none.
[Rock walks towards the ringside camera] Rock : You see, the Internet title is an opportunity for the Rock. Just as it was an opportunity on Sunday for you. The main difference being that the
Rock will take that belt and use it as a steppingstone to bigger and better things. You Mankind, you will use it as a barometer for your career. You will be sitting outside on a rock, next to a bubbling brook, reminiscing about how you used to be a champion, how you used to be one of the men that got the adulation and cheers. And it drives you crazy doesn't it Mick? You know how your career here in the EiW should have gone, but you have to look at how it went instead, and it makes you miserable. Missed opportunity after missed opportunity and the knowledge is eating at you, gnawing at your insides. And it should, Mick, it should. But your career is of no concern to the Rock. The mistakes and failures that you have caused and endured,
Mankind, it's obvious to the Rock and everyone else in the EiW that you are doomed to repeat them, because you simply can't learn. That's a shame Mick, it truly is.
[Rock pauses for a second, looking out into crowd, before continuing] Rock : Once this Wednesday's show is over, you will be revealed as the imposter that you are, Mankind. And the Rock will take his next step up the ladder to the top of the EiW and take that Internet belt. Get ready to hit
RockBottom, Mankind, courtesy of the Rock!
[Rock flips the mic away and makes his way out of the ring, once again ignoring the fans, making the fans more than a bit curious, as JR speaks]
JR : Well, Michael, the Rock wasn't his usual fan friendly self tonight. Why do you think that is?
MC : I have no idea, Jim, but he seems very focused on the task in front of him, winning the Internet title from Mankind.
JR : Once we get back from commercials we'll have a special matchup between
Disco Inferno and Kid Romeo. We'll be right back!
[The shot switches to a commercial for Wednesday Night Heat before the scene fades out]
