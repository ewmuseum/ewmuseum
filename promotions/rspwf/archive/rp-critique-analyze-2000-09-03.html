[RP] Critique/Analyze

3 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, I do this once in a while. Typically, the responce is low. I'm hoping to get more feedback this time. First of all, no, this character is not a free agent looking for any feds. Secondly, yes, this RP is long, but it needed to be, imo, because it was the most important RP of the character's existance thus far. A lot of things came together. Thirdly, a lot of it won't make sense unless you know the background...
1) This is for the GFWA, Alex Reaver vs. Alexander Creed Hell or High Water Death Match that took place on August 24th. 2) The supercard is Terror in the Triangle 2000. At the '97 version, Reaver had attempted suicide. Throughout the years, he has gone from troubled soul to manic depressive addict. This RP kind of shows that degeneration at its fullest.
3) A lot of the comments at the end are refrencing important lines in GFWA history. Fate 101 was sort of a kayfabe code for the idea that Reaver, then a babyface, was destined to go over the heel to win the title. It was also one of his more well known interviews from the time. (Like Austin's KOTR speech on a smaller scale.) The line "Deal with it" was almost a catchphrase/closure for him a long time ago. And the final words he speaks were the final words he said to Shawn Adams before their epic A Nightmare in Newton Schoolhouse Brawl in October 1997, the climax of his first major feud.
Now, for your reading (dis)pleasure... the roleplay. ***** [Black.] VO: We may be through with the past, but the past ain't through with us. [Fade in: As the haunting epigram from P.T. Anderson's "Magnolia" fades into silence, the scene opens to a remote location on Elbow Beach in Bermuda. It is early morning, just past sunrise, and the sky is still a mix of colors, blending together to form a post card perfect view. The calm waves caress the shoreline, rocking back slowly back and forth. The sand rustles a bit from a light wind, making the faintest sound. As the camera pans along the shore, we hear the discontent sigh of a man off camera. Slowly, the shot circles... and there stands Alex Reaver, slouched against a palm tree, looking off past the waters, a glazed look in his eyes. He is barely dressed in a pair of black flip flops and knee length black board shorts with a red border. His hair is dry for once, uncombed, hanging down in his eyes, but also not quite laying flat on the sides and back. His chest and arms sport visible scars and cuts, some looked to be freshly made within the past day.
Reaver slowly takes his attention away from the beyond, glancing at the camera hesitantly. Normally at ease, almost condescending to the camera, he seems nervous, fidgety.]
AR: Thanks for coming on such short notice. I know I wasn't scheduled now, but... I don't know... I woke up early and couldn't get back to sleep. I just had to come out here, finally, walk around... it's been so long, you know? I just... wanted to come out here... and I figured... I thought it'd be nice, you know, if I could just say whatever and have it filmed. I don't want to sit here and cut a promo, I just... I just wanted to just speak off the top of my head, just capture whatever I'm thinking... feeling right now, being here again.
[He pauses, abruptly. Alex sniffs in.] AR: I thought the hardest thing would be the first time I was back in Newton, but I guess I was only kidding myself. I guess I tried to block it from my mind, the fact that I'd have to come here... 'cause this...
[Alex pauses, breathing harder.] AR: Damn. [He pauses, bracing himself against the palm tree with one hand. The other hand covers his face for a moment. The hand against the palm tree is visibly shaking. Reaver's breathing is also unsteady, almost erratic. There is a long, uncomfortable silence. A voice behind the camera speaks hesitantly, trying to not be heard.]
CM: Do you want me to stop rolling, Alex? [Alex looks up, his eyes reddened. He bites his lower lip, looking a little embarrassed. He stumbles, trying to speak.]
AR: Ah, no... no, just give me a second. [Reaver stands up straight, runs a hand over his face, bringing it down to his chin. He rubs at his stubble for a bit, sighing. Beads of sweat are visible on his forehead. Alex tries to steady himself, to stop his hands from shaking.
Finally, by bringing them around his chest tightly, he is able to. He tilts his head up towards the camera, his voice still a little shaky. His typical bitter, edgy tone has been replaced by a softer, saddened one.]
AR: It's been three years, you know... but I guess sometimes, no matter how hard you try, it's not easy to cope with the memories. I've tried to block it all out... tried to move on with my life... but it's like some higher force won't let me walk away. So I guess it's kind of fitting, then, that right when
I had some semblance of control in my life, I'm sent to the site of the most horrible moment in my entire life. Some wounds will never heal... not like this.
I've done a lot of things in my life I'm not proud of, but that night... that night surpasses anything else I did before or did since.
I don't think I'll ever life down the shame... of what I tried to do... what I could've done. It gives me nightmares just thinking about it. July 7th,
1997... that was probably the lowest moment of my entire life, and considering all the shit I've been through, that says a lot.
[He pauses, gazing out at the ocean. Reaver bites his lip, contemplating something. Then, he slowly shifts his weight from one foot to the other as the expression on his face changes.]
AR: Sometimes... when I think too much... I wonder how things could've been. Like, for instance, if I had never become a wrestler... if I had just stayed on the streets with Jules, what would it have been like? The depression and the poverty probably would've driven me to suicide anyway, but then again, Jules never would've gotten star stuck, and I'd still have my support system to fall back on. So maybe things would've been better that way. [shrugs] I can't rewrite the past, though... so I'm left to deal with the mess I've made.
[shakes his head] God, what a mess it is. I shouldn't be worrying myself over ancient tragedies, though. I've got a big match to prepare for. This is an important event in my life, and I know I should be completely focused on my match, but...
[Alex sighs.] AR: I've just got too much baggage... too much shit wracking my brain to really focus. I mean, really... what the hell am I doing? When I came to the
GFWA three years ago, I was a suicidal, manic depressive, attention starved kid with low self esteem... since then, I've become even more physically and emotionally damaged. I'm lonely, I'm bitter, and I feel the need to inflict pain upon myself or I don't feel right. I'm a goddamn mess, and sometimes I wonder if I would've been better off slitting my throat those three years ago and saving myself the heartache.
[Suddenly, he turns, facing the camera. There is a desperate, vulnerable look on his face.]
AR: Alexander Creed... there's something you need to know. It's something I've never fully admitted to anybody. I tell you this, and by proxy, I'm telling the world... but I wouldn't do it if I didn't think it's gotten to the point where it's absolutely necessary. You see, Creed, our differences are pretty obvious... I'm a sinner, you're a saint. But the excess of my sins...
it's too much to handle sometimes. I mean, seriously, Creed, the things I've done... Ran away from home and turned my back on my family... Attempted suicide... Took too many painkillers and too many dosages of morphine... Tried pot, cocaine, speed, heroin... Resorted to self-mutilation to make myself happy... Tried to make everybody else suffer as much as me... I'm sick, Creed. I am physically and mentally sick. It's gone too far. All of this just keeps building up, and I can't go on like this... something's got to change. I'm not a good person. I'm flawed. My soul... shit, I don't even know how much of the naive, innocent, pureness that we're born with remains.
My body is a mess, my mind is racing, and my heart is heavy... I'm so fucked up that it even hurts to breathe. I'm on the verge of another crash and burn.
[He pauses, looking deeper into the camera.] AR: So you see, Creed, this match... I guess it's my cry for help. I'm asking you... no, I'm begging you...
Save me. Maybe I'm asking for too much here, like a miracle or something, but you walk around like with a wave of your hand, you can make all the bad memories go away. So many people look up to you... respect you... and I wonder what ever happened to my respect... my self worth... but I guess it's my own fault. I can blame [name withheld] and the world as long as I want, but I'm only living in denial.
You see, Creed, some nights... when I go to bed... I dream of waking up and everything being different. I dream of being with my family... with a girl I can love... and with a job, a real job that I enjoy... and all these scars...
all these reminders of the hell I've been through... they're not there in my dreams, Creed. It's like all I have to do is close my eyes and they vanish.
The sad thing is, I've got to wake up eventually. But you... you can help me. [Slowly, Reaver drops to his knees, the weakness showing in his face.] AR: I'm down on my knees here, Creed... I'm begging you to help me. Clense my soul... exorcise my demons... make me a good person... please, Creed, I'm begging you to save me... before it's...
[Alex trails off, his head dropping. For a few momements, all we hear is him struggling with the word "it's" and unable to finish his sentence. Alex begins to rock back and forth, stammering the same word over and over, barely audible... and then, suddenly, he rises to his feet, snarling, with all the look of weakness and regret erased from his face as if he never said a word otherwise. His hands are flailing everywhere, and his voice is raised, dripping with hate.]
AR: It's too late, Creed! It's too fucking late! [He slowly gets closer to the camera, the veins in his forehead beginning to show as he grits his teeth. Alex's hands are shaking violently, almost throbbing as he comes closer to the camera, looking resentful.]
AR: I am what I am and there's not a goddamn thing you can to about it, Creed! Do you understand me? My fate is already sealed! I came to terms with that a long time ago... but you... you... you sit there and you act as if you've got some magical healing power... [spits] you make me sick! I fucking hate your guts for your ignorance! You can't change me, Creed... you think I'm sick and weak, but I'm not... I'm strong, Creed, I'm stronger than you can ever be...
you think my mind is clouded and I'm too screwed up to think? Well, fuck you, Creed, because the past few months, I've been thinking clearer than I ever have... and you... look at you! Judge yourself, you fucking walking fallacy!
Your life... is a lie... you can't save anybody, Creed. You can't even save yourself! Your blind faith is ignorant... and one of these days, Creed, you're gonna wake up and realize that you're nothing but a sham... and that you're a fucking failure, too. Yeah, that's right, Creed... you're a failure. These sheep look at you with their puppy dog eyes, begging you to be a hero... asking you to be their savior...
Your no fucking savior, Creed! Because no matter how hard you try, you will never be able to save them or me. [Alex pauses, trying to catch himself... slowly, he backs up against the palm tree he was first leaning against, and slowly begins to rest himself against it. He sighs, the faintest of a smirk curling on his lips.]
AR: So, you see, Creed... even if you win, you lose. You look down on me like some sort of pity case, but guess what? I look down on you like a blind fool.
Few things in this life bring me joy, but when I toss your wretched body into the Atlantic Ocean and set that motherfucker ablaze... [inhales] oh, I get wet just thinking about it. You can stand on your soapbox all you want and preach to the sheep, but your words are meaningless to me... just hypocritical lies from an ignorant man.
So screw you, Creed. Screw your beliefs. Whether you like it or not, there's something you're going to have to accept. I already know I'm damned to a Downward Spiral straight to hell, but I've got a reality check for you... I'm gonna take you down with me! And nothing, and I mean nothing, not even a divine intervention, is going to change that.
Fate 101, Creed. [smirks] Deal with it. [He smiles a satisfied, malevolent grin, but then pauses.] AR: Oh, wait, I almost forgot... one more thing. [Alex's scratches his temple for a moment, eyes closed, as if trying to remember a line he heard long, long ago. Then, it apparently comes to him, as he suddenly opens his eyes, grits his teeth, and snarls a final warning.]
AR: Prepare... to burn. [Fade to black.] *****
