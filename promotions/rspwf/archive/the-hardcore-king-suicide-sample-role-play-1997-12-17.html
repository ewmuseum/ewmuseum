"The Hardcore King" Suicide sample role-play

17 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a role-play I did a while ago as a mystery man in the WOWA my first match back I won and became IC Champion. It isn't that good but it's the only one I had around so I thought I would share it with everyone. http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/3663 http://members.tripod.com/~ECWSFPW/ Warning: The following contains graphic language and adult content, which may not be suitable for children under the age of 14. Parental advisor is advised! The views in the following interview are not necessarily those of the WOWA or its wrestlers, they are simply the views of the man stating them. (Taped 2 AM last night. Scene opens with the camera man outside of a local night club, called "The Rave!" The camera man walks up to the door and is stopped by a large bouncer. The bouncer sees the WOWA identification card and lets him in. The camera man comes through the doors only to be met with flashing lights, loud music, and people dancing all over the place, the camera man starts walking through trying not to bump into people, he sees the Mystery Man sitting at a bar. The Mystery Man isn't wearing his mask, but his face it black out by the WOWA Production Crew. The Mystery Man sees the camera but pays no attention to it. The bar tender then hands him a large glass of beer; he takes it and chugs it down.) MM: Do you guys have to follow me ever where I go? (He then gets up and motions for the camera man to follow him. MM??- starts walking toward the door, he gets there then goes outside. The camera man is a few steps behind him, when the camera man reaches outside the MM??- is leaning against the wall. The MM??-starts clapping.) MM: FINALLY! Shades you're not as dumb as you look! You finally figured out who I am! About fucking time! I only through out about like ten hints! So I took my mask off, but I still can not say who I am. Because some people still don't know. You said I am not big deal? Well the WOWA President thinks other wise. This idiot offered me about ten contracts these last couple of months. He wanted to make me the highest paid WOWA wrestler buy giving me not 1 million, but 2 million dollars for two months! I told him ok. But then I had to take it back, as I became caught up in other business. So you know what your President did? He had a nervous break down! He started throwing a little girl hissy fit. The man even stated to me and a couple of others that he was going to shut the WOWA down, all because I wouldn't sign! So who's not the big deal now? Shade's you think you are so good, then tell me who you ever beat that was worth mentioning? NOBODY...! I have done more in my short career then you will ever do in your whole life time! Now I know I may have said some of this shit before, but I don't care, I am saying it again! Shades you a nothing! You named yourself after sunglasses, you are a rip-off of Razor Ramon! You don't know the correct English; never mind your Spanish! The only reason why you are consider one of the best wrestler in this federation is because everyone else sucks! The President keeps on hiring these no names, and he thinks the WOWA is the greatest thing in the world, when in reality this fed has no talent and it never will...I take that back, there is an exception to that, me, Loco, and Vile Vince Viper. And that is it! The rest of you are a bunch of pussy little bitches who don't know a clothesline from a piledriver! The most "hardcore" thing around here is a saying the word stupid. I said I would never come back to this federation, but I guess I lied. I told that idiot Malibu Mike to send a message to all you WOWA'ers. But he failed to do so! In this message I challenged all the WOWA Wrestlers to come over to another federation so I could kick there asses. He didn't send it because he said you were all too busy, but we all know that you are all afraid of me! So instead of you pieces of shit coming to me I came to you! But when I get here the only think I hear is the word cool, bad grammar, and horrible Spanish every five seconds. All you WOWA morons are living in a fantasy world. You all set your selves on a thrown and think your all high and mighty, but if you ever stepped into a real fed your asses would be kick the first five seconds you got there. Now here a challenge for all of you! A little tournament if you will. One night the WOWA's best against me! One by one I will kick the face in of all you WOWA chuckle heads... But you all know the WOWA Officials won't let this tournament happen, because they know I will make the WOWA Wrestlers all look like idiots! Hey Rey Jeran why don't you mind your own damn business before that mouth of yours gets you an ass kicking that you can't handle! I have heard from the WOWA VP and President about how damn great you are, and you are going to be the next World Champion, but I am sick of it! You are a no nothing fairy. You might be considered the best now, but after I kick Shade's ass then have my way with you at the PPV, people will be singing a different tune! So after you win the World Championship, I am going to come down and challenge you! Not for your little belt, because I really could care less. So if you are man enough and think that you can go another match then wrestle me! After I get down with ya, you'll be confessing about how much you suck and how great I really am! Now onto my next topic, and that being Tarheel! How clever you named yourself after a school. You are another one who doesn't know how to mind his own business! Go ahead and support Shades, because he is gonna need it! After I kick Shade's and Jeran's ass you come down and I'll kick yours! Who should I talk about next? Hmmm. How about my little buddy Buzzkill. Here is another genius, naming himself after a television show! Well Buzzy I really never liked you and I don't like you now. So I am asking the WOWA Officials to sign me a match with Buzzy. Just so I can teach this little idiot a wrestling lesson! Now a message to all the rest of the WOWA mid carders don't be dumb like Jeran and Tarheel, keep your noses out of my business! (Suddenly a drunk guy comes out of the night club. He starts walking around, then he stops and throws up all over the place. Some of it splatters and gets on the Mystery Man's shoe. So the Mystery Man takes the drunk by the neck and starts punching him directly in the face. Blood pours out from the man's nose, but the Mystery Man keeps on punching him. He then stops and the drunk guy flask to the sidewalk motionless. But the Mystery then starts kicking him in the ribs. The drunk guy starts to cough of blood. The Mystery Man then starts to walk away with the camera man following him, people can be heard screaming for help. The Mystery Man turns the corner and a black limo is waiting for him. He steps in, so does the camera man. The limo then pulls away, as sirens can be heard in the background.) Sorry about that, it just had to be done! Now you're all gonna sarcastically say I am such a tough guy because I can beat up a drunk guy and use bad language. But I don't care, say what you must, but just remember what goes around comes around, and it may come back around in a different form, such as my fist! Now where was I? Let see I already bashed the WOWA President, I bashed Jeran, Tarheel, and Buzzkill. Now I think it is time for me to talk about the Golden Alliance! These guys think they are the greatest stable ever, when in fact that are a bunch of mixed up morons, who sit in the back plugging each other up the ass all day! After my good friends who make their WOWA return, show up and cost Man Power their gold, and after I get down with Shades, they won't be so golden! Halloween Holocaust I am going to show the WOWA that there is only one "ALLIANCE!" Now on to my unworthy opponent Cool Shades. This boy is just lost in his oversized ego! He goes around calling himself the "Royal Coolness", he claims he is the best, and he starts a fan club. A bit fond of yourself there Shades... For your viewing pleasure I will now to my impression of Shades. (He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a pair of sunglasses. He takes them and puts them on.) Hey I am cool! I am the best, oh yeah I am cool to! Tarheel I am cool and your not! Don't you wish you were as cool as me? Join my cool fan club, it is real cool. Rey Jeran the Royal Coolness is going to beat you in a real cool way with my cool Shade's Blade. The move is very cool, so cool you won't be able to get out of it! Hey El Whatever, I Don't know correct English or Spanish! But I think it is real cool sounding like an ass all the time! Here is some more cool names I came up for you! El Mysterosis, El Enigma, and El Puzzelo! Hey Wait I just came up with a name for myself, El Cool! Wow that was real cool of me thinking up all those cool names. I am really, really, really, really COOL! (The Mystery Man stops, he then takes the glasses off and puts them back in his pocket.) And once again for you those just tuning in, that was me pretending to be Shades. But enough of the games, lets get serious! (Suddenly the limo goes over a large pot whole and the Mystery Man and Camera Man nearly hit their heads on the roof of the limo.) Hey driver, watch where you're fucking going! As I was saying let's get down to business! As I have stated many times before, Shades you can not compete with me! Your a two move nothing, while I am a skillful, aerial skilled technical wrestler! Don't get me wrong I can brawl with the best of them! Just two weeks ago I was in anything goes match, with a truly great wrestler, who will go nameless. We went up and down the aisle, into the food stands, back in the locker room, we went everywhere! I was hit with chairs and tables, thrown off a balcony, and dumped over a pile of thumbtacks! Sure I was a bloody mess, but my opponent was even messier! When the bones stopped breaking, and the blood stopped pouring, I was the one standing victorious! The same will be said at the PPV! Shades you may be bigger, but you are far from being better, win or lose that's what I am going to prove! Wait a second, what am I saying? Win or lose, did that just come out of my mouth? I meant to say when I win and I will win! Right now I am riding on an undefeated streak in another fed and it is going to carry over here, because you my friend, Shades, are not going to beat me! Well we have established the fact that you have no chance of beating me; so the question now is how fast will I beat you? That is a tough one. Do I want to make it short and sweet, or do I want you to make it long and have you suffer a hard agonizing pain? Well I think we know what the answer is, hard an agonizing pain! Anything goes, pin-fall anywhere! Sure is my kind of match! No one can stop it, so it's not over until I feel like ending it! Shades for all you know I can beat your ass in five minutes or five hours! Your little Golden Alliance can come down and help ya if you like. But if they get in my way they'll all be getting a backhand across their faces! (The limo stops.) Well it looks like I am home! (He stretches out his arms and yawns. He then opens the door and steps out of the limo.) Shades you will only have that belt for a few more days, then it will me mine! And could you please do me a big favor? Shine that belt up real nice, I don't want to have any grease spots or scratches on it while it is around my waist! Now let us all just wait and see how much of this interview the "Copy Boy" Shades copies, because we all know he has no originality! I just have one more thing to do before I go and that is… (The Mystery Man jokingly starts doing the "Cabbage Patch", a dance, and starts going around in circles as he starts chanting…) I am going to be IC Champion! I am going to be IC Champion! I am going to be IC Champion! I am going to be IC Champion! (Screen fades to black.)
