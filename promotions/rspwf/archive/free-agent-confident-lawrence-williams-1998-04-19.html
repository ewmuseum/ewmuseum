[Free Agent] "Confident" Lawrence Williams

19 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Presidents... My name is [name withheld], the handler of "Confident" Lawrence Williams. I'm in High School, and the end of it is coming up soon. June 16th to be exact.
Well, now I'm only in 2 federations, simply because I have to run my own league, the NAWA. --Don't worry, I'm not plugging now.-- I'm not looking to join a federation, but I would like to be on the mailing list so that I can get a feel for what I want. Maybe have in-ring appearances, and all of that good stuff.
Now, I'm not talking about these abhorent leagues, because I'll know if you're an abhorent league by the first contact.
I'm going to leave you a roleplay I just did for the IeWC (great league:) a few minutes ago.
============== IeWC, 4/19/98 Sub: "Confident" Lawrence Williams finds a feud ============== ::"Confident" Lawrence Williams and J-Dogg are seen sitting down in one of the chairs in the United Center, Chicago, Illinois. The lights from the top of the arena gleam down on Lawrence's dark, bald head, making a million beams of light go in different direction. Williams is wearing an old, black Rugid XXL Wear t-shirt. It has some lettering on the top and bottom, and the face of a
"rugid" man in the middle. Lawrence is also wearing navy blue Fubu jeans that are faded at both of the knees. On his feet are a new pair of blue
Timberlands; no dirt, dust, or scuff marks. J-Dogg is wearing the usual manager wear. He has on a blue Versace sport jacket, and blue Gucci slacks.
He's also wearing black Rockport shoes that he's waxed up many times. Lawrence takes off his black, Ray Bans off, and puts them in his right pocket. He stands up, steps over J-Dogg, and starts walking down the aile. J-Dogg soon
follows:: Lawrence: Well, I haven't been in the Internation e-Wrestling Conference but 24 hours, and I've already found out somebody I want to feud with.
::Every other second as Lawrence and J-Dogg are walking down the aile they make a 'klop' sound::
Lawrence: I decided that since I'm not in any of the IeWC Title tournaments, that I might as well get in some action with a wrestler.
::Klop:: Lawrence: I mean, I've got to find_some_thing to do while in this federation. I'm not going to sit here and be a slacker. I'm going right to the punch. I'm not backing down, b'cuz I'm sure as hell not no punk!
::Klop:: Lawrence: Well, I'm sure you all are_dying_to see who I'm going to feud with, and that's natural. Everyone wants to know Lawrence's business. I'll get to that in a few seconds. But, I'll give you like a umm....
::Lawrence pauses on the second to last step in the aile, and starts scratching his bald head::
Lawrence: Preamble! Yeah, I'll give you like a preamble sort of thing before I give you the wrestler. Well, here we are.
::Lawrence and J-Dogg hop over the security railing with ease. When they get there, they walk up the ring steps, and walk on to the apron. J-Dogg goes ahead and hops into the ring. He then looks up and around, and starts nodding his head. Lawrence is still on the apron looking around, and he starts nodding his head. He then gets into the ring and continues::
Lawrence: Man, I love the United Center. It signifies what Chicago is all about, eh, J-Dogg?
::J-Dogg has taken a seat on the top turnbuckle:: J-Dogg: Yeh, man. This is type bangin'...that's what this is. Lawrence: Now, back to the preamble. Throughout my illustrious professional wrestling career, I have seen_many_of these so-called "hunks" of wrestling.
The ones with the_huge_egos. The ones that are so over-confident that they think they are god's gift to women when they're not. I've seen the "Pretty
Boy" Jeff Kings, the "Superstar" Devon Starrs, the "Playboy" Ronnie Ds, and now a wrestler in this league.
Some of these wrestlers I can respect, because they can hold it down in the ring. The others I can't, because I know they're nothing but punks who talk the talk, but can't walk the walk. And that's where "Confident" Lawrence
Williams comes in. I smash the egos, the confidence, and the cockiness. I smash all of that, and more. People go into a match with me thinking that they're the next greatest thing since Jerry Springer, but they go out thinking that they're a failure! A flunky!
Well, if you can hold it down in the ring with "Confident" Lawrence Williams, you're not a flunky. If you can't, that doesn't mean you're a flunky, because
Lawrence Williams is not an easy opponent. ::Lawrence takes a step back to clear his throat 2 times:: You'll have to excuse me. Coming off this flu I've had a few days. Anyway, you already know why I don't like these Hollywood, glamour and glitz, sexy boy type characters, and here's why. If you know "Confident" Lawrence Williams, you know that I don't like unoriginal bastards and what I just said. I just don't. So now it comes down to this....the announcement of who Lawrence
Williams will feud with. J, the drums.
::J-Dog begins to smack the turnbuckles in a drum-like fashion:: Lawrence: The winner; and feuding with Lawrence Williams.....TA DAAAAAAAAAAAA.....JOHNNY GLAAAAAAAMOOOOOOOOOUR!!! ::Now Lawrence begins to imitate one of those guys who tell you what you've won on a game show::
Lawrence: Johnny, here's what you've won. ::Images of "Breaker" Dane, Havoc, Mr. Extreme, and a few other wrestlers in a graveyard are shown as a Lawrence Williams voice over is heard::
Lawrence VO: An all expenses paid trip to EGO SHATTERS, USA! This great, big place lay deep in the heart of America. Here you'll get to meet the people that have gotten their asses kicked by Lawrence! Get tips on how to overcome this tragic event, get a free, "I got my ego shattered by Lawrence Williams
Club" membership, and most importantly at EGO SHATTERS, USA, you can restore the confidence you_ONCE_had!
::Images of Lawrence applying the Clutch of Confidence on GFW's Havoc are shown:: Lawrence VO: You've also won a free attempt to break out of the Clutch of Confidence. If you can do this, Lawrence will give you a free Confidence College pamplet, with details on how_you_could go to Confidence College and learn how to have true confidence.
::Images of Lawrence Williams, Steve Houston, and Derek Douglas kicking the crap out of Havoc are shown::
Lawrence VO: But most importantly, you've won an all expenses paid trip to ass thrashed land! The land where thou's ass, gets tapped, and there's nothing that you can do about it!
::The Images fade out, and we're back to real-time with Lawrence and J-Dogg:: Lawrence: You know, that felt so good. I found that very gelastic. Very much so. It feels so good to get all of that out of my system.
J-Dogg: I know what you're feeling, man. I went through the same thang when I was wrestlin', and you're gonna go through the same thang when you're wrestlin'.
::Lawrence has a look of axtasia on his face:: Lawrence: J, what are you talkin' 'bout, man? You're trippin' off all that sherm you be smokin'. Well, anyway. Glamour Boy, you've heard it. Now it's time for_you_to be a man and respond! You might have never heard of me before, but you'll remember the Clutch of Confidence when you can't get out! I'm OUT!
::Lawrence jumps over the ropes, and lands flat-footed on the outside. J-Dogg takes his time by stepping through the ropes and onto the apron as the screen fades to black::
============= Now, I can do better than that, simply because I don't have a firm angle with this character yet. But when I do, I can make more quality roleplays than that. Be it 60 lines, or 200. It all depends on the situation.
Here's something that I look for in a federation: - Quality cards. I don't care if the card comes out late, I just want it to be quality. - Competition I'd rather get beat by "Playboy" Ronnie D. then defeat the Brooklyn Brawler. - Not a PPV_every_month. I know this is eliminating_many_federations right now. I feel as if a PPV every month is_way_too much. It doesn't give angle assembling time.
- A booker(s) who judges the winner on roleplaying and angles. Roleplaying includes in-ring appearances, flashes, strategies, commercials, promos, and all those goods things. I don't mind losing to a wrestler if this is what the angle calls for.
Well, if you can provide this for a brother, then I'd be tempted to join your federation. Remember, not_every_league will get me. I'm not trying to say that I'm the good God Almighty, but I don't want a garbage federation.
Thank you for your time, and if you wish to flame me for whatever reason, do it! And that's the bottom line, because Oprah Said So!
