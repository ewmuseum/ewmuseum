The crowd slowly leaves the beaten an battered Majix laying in the desert sand~~~Majix lays motionless in the sand~~~The holy land has become the final resting place for the son of
SATAN~~~ The heat grows hotter an hotter as the day moves along~~~Majix has yet to move~~~The holy people continue to watch to see if the evil man will arise~~~But the heat has yet to move him~~~So he must be dead an gone~~~~
Night falls an the people take one last look~~~~Majix lies motionless face down in the sand~~~The evil has ended~~~The holy people rejoice in their feat~~~Majix rest in peace in the land of ALLAH~~~In the land of the
GREAT JEHOVAH~~~The wicked is put to rest~~~Let all people rejoice in their feat~~~ The night passes by quietly~~~~Not a sound~~~Its the first night in year these holy people slept in comfort~~~~The root of all evil Majix has been taken care of in the old way~~~The way of the HOLY LAND~~~~The sun slowly rises over the hillside~~~The men of these holy people come out to a great horror~~~All the women an children lay in a circle~~~A rock enbeded in each skull of every woman an child~~~~Fathers an husbands run out to each family member~~~~They fall to their knees in tears~~~They beg an pray for it to be a dream~~~They each look in the center of the circle~~~majix sits cross legged~~~His head bowed~~~He looks up~~~The blood of the mens wives an children drips from his mouth~~~~The men stare in horror~~~~They slowly creep backwards to escape this sight~~~

But before you each get elated with the words~~~I no longer wish to due the bidding of SATAN~~~Is because I am the FALLEN ANGEL LUCIFER~~~Yes I now know that I am the true leader of the SEVENTH REALM OF HELL~~~~I am the man called SATAN~~~But on earth known to each of you as Majix~~~I have walked into your lives an changed everyone of you~~~~I have come to the HOLY LAND to walk the path of your LORD an SAVIOUR JESUS
