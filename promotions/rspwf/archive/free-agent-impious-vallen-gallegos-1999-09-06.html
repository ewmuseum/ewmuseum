(FREE AGENT) "Impious" Vallen Gallegos

6 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings... My name is [name withheld], and this is my first time posting to
RSPWF. I've been e-wrestling since 1995 (off-and-on, mind you), and I'm just returing after a year or so of a break. Don't call it a retirement; just call it "The league I was on closed." Moving on ...
I'd like to tell you about my current character, "Impious" Vallen Gallegos. His nickname describes him ... I'll post a sample role-play and his application that I used for WwCT, which I'm assuming is done (e- mail me if you know!). E-mail me at
[detail withheld] if you're interested in this character, and I'll respond regardless if I'll have time to join your league or not!
Cheers! Dylan THE ROLEPLAY- (Open to traffic. Cars honk their horns at intersections and people attempt to cross the street without getting hit. Tourists seem to be abound, and most seem like they're in the center of all of the mayhem.
One man emerges amongst all of this - one man that seems like he actually knows his way around. He appears on the opposite side of the street than the one the camera is shooting from, and seems not to even notice it. He begins walking, shoving all that's in his way, including a car or two. Once he makes his way over, the man with chin length green hair and a black goatee smiles to the camera and then gets up close in the camera. He wears a long black trench coat with black shorts, sandals and a shirt underneath. The man - who appears to have just awoken, either that or he hasn't moved at all until now - stares in to the camera, trying to catch a glimpse of himself. He messes with his hair a bit, and then grabs his crotch. He begins giggling before he sighs and speaks in a rough voice.)
"Welcome to _my_ territory, participants in the World Wide Cruiserweight Tournament! Welcome to the streets of Phoenix, Arizona." (As the man grins and begins walking again - he is frozen in-place, and a graphic appears under him. It reads in bold black letters: '"Impious"
Vallen Gallegos." The graphic remains for half a minute or so until the man...Vallen Gallegos, resumes walking. He stops next to a brick wall, and leans up against it as he pulls out a cigarette. Vallen puts his hand on a stranger walking down the street, and holds out his cigarette, indicating he wants a light. The stranger shrugs and Vallen raises his fist to the person, sending them in a scurry. He turns around to the cameraman who is holding a lighter out. Vallen smiles and holds his cigarette out, but the cameraman is trembling so much he can't get it lit. Gallegos rolls his eyes and grabs the lighter and lights his cigarette, rolling his eyes. Once he puts it in his mouth and takes a long drag, he lets out nothing but a sigh of relief, dropping the lighter on the sidewalk in the process.)
Vallen Gallegos: "Some people just can't trust the name, can they? I mean, check-it, the other day...(Changes tone of voice) This is before
I signed with the Cruisey tourney...anyway... (Back to the old tone) I was walking down the street wearing my big "IMPIOUS" T-shirt and expecting to be feared, y'know? So I go up to this old lady and I say,
"Hey, lady, got some spare change?" and she has the nerve to call _me_ a slob who needs to get a job. Heh, I don't know if it's just that people can't understand such a big word as "impious" or if people are just brain-dead in Phoenix now. It beats the hell out of me...I just hope the jerks in Alaska have somewhat of a higher form of intelligence. Really. Oh, to end the story, ladies and gentlemen - I slapped the [bleep] out of that old bag...needless to say I got the money. Hey - how else was I to get in to this tourney?"
(Gallegos grins at his story and slides down the brick wall, relaxing. He takes another drag on the cigarette - already halfway smoked by now. When he exhales, he takes a look at the cigarette and grins.) Vallen Gallegos: "I've become dependent on these things...ha, oh well, I doubt it'll [bleep] me up any in the tournament, I'm in the shape of my life even if I smoke two packs a day.
Anyway, to the tournament. To be quite honest? I'm not really taking it seriously. I'm being paid regardless -and- I know for a fact that none of these wrestlers are worth a _damn._ The only wrestler that I've even
_heard_ of is Setzer Van Strife - and boy, if that doesn't speak a thousand words for you, I don't know what will. Needless to say I have no trash talking and no insults to speak - especially for that Scott
Jamison guy that I have to face in the first round. Your wrestling ability is trash talk enough - eh? (Vallen looks off camera with a grin.) Hey - here's someone who knows what I'm talking about.
(A large bald African-American man emerges from behind the camera. He wears black sunglasses and a nice suit. He extends his hand to Vallen, expecting a handshake, but Vallen grins and slaps him a high-five in return. The man takes off his glasses and pulls out a cigarette. Vallen hands him his butt to light it and goes back to the wall.)
Vallen Gallegos: "Hey, guys, this is Beef." (Beef shyly backs away from the camera, but Vallen pulls him back.) Vallen Gallegos: "I set this guy up yesterday...heh...speaking of yesterday, it seemed just like yesterday that we were stealing each others money each night when we slept next to this brick wall. Hard to imagine we were best friends. But, hey - we knew what we were up to, and as Oscar Wilde says: "A true friend stabs you in the front."
Well, Beef, my main man, you know what's going on, right? I'm going to pro-wrestle." Beef: "Yeah, yeah, yeah, brotha', I know all about it. I know all about 'dose scrubs you gettin' in the ring wit', and I know you gonna rip 'em a new one." Vallen Gallegos: "Um, thanks, I'm glad you did your research. I surely did - and, heh...I wasn't impressed. I don't know what this Scott Stein guy thinks he's doing - putting me into some tournament full of cruiserweight losers and boozers...I've never wrestled before or anything, but I think I know what I'm capable of, and it's far superior to that of these...scrubs."
Beef: "Yeah, quite a vocab ya' got goin' there." Vallen Gallegos: "Thanks, I learned it all from you, big brother. The fact is, vocabulary doesn't cut it in the wrestling biz. That's for damn sure - I pulled some tapes out from the past of all of these guys...geez...it seems like they only had 6 years of school - which, mind you, I did, too. Anyhow - they lack in skill as well, so I can only wonder why they were hired and why they're so Goddamned respected."
Beef: "Their looks?" (Vallen ponders for a minute and scratches his goatee. He rolls his eyes and then stands, putting his hand on Beef's back.)
Vallen Gallegos: "Beef - you may have gotten something...if I'm going to be successful in the WwCT, I have to get a look going. We'll work on
it - I already have my first paycheck! Now, eh...I gotta finish off this interview. I think I've made my point clear on what I think of this tournament and its wrestlers. I've found my niche in how to win, I'm set. I just need to finish this off with something witty..."
(Vallen ponders once again - but the voice of reasoning seems to be Beef as he leans in to Gallegos and whispers something into his ear. Gallegos giggles and then gives a straight face to the camera. Beef counts down from 3 - but we only see his lips moving. On "three," they both squeeze their crotches at the camera, crack up, and begin walking down the street again. Fade out.)
THE APPLICATION- ------------------- HANDLER INFORMATION ------------------- Your Name : Dylan [name withheld] Your E-mail : [detail withheld] Your AIM/ICQ : [detail withheld] - AIM name -------------------- WRESTLER INFORMATION -------------------- Wrestler's Name : "Impious" Vallen Gallegos Wrestler's Weight : 6'2" Wrestler's Height : 236 Wrestler's Age : 19 Wrestler's Hometown : Phoenix, Arizona ---------------------------- WRESTLER GIMMICK INFORMATION ---------------------------- Wrestler's Story : Vallen was born from rich parents in Phoenix, Arizona. He was spoiled all of his life despite being a troublemaker from the first day he was around other children in kindergarten. His parents just shrugged off teachers and officials telling them to discipline their child and spoiled Vallen even more...until an incident occurred that totally ticked off his parents. Vallen burned one of their two Phoenix houses down - and it was at that point that his parents realized that they had a nuisance for a son. They immediately decided to send their child to military school...but Vallen didn't like this. He threw a hissy-fit, hoping to get at least his Mother's praise.
Failure. At that, Vallen decided he had to do what he had to do: run away. And he did. He ran away and never came back. It was tough for Vallen to go from being filthy spoiled rich to being homeless, but he eventually learned to fight for himself on the street and steal. Finally Vallen has saved up enough money 7 years after he ran away from home to find a job. A job that he'll like. A job that doesn't frown upon those who are as impious as Vallen Gallegos. A job that Vallen doesn't even consider a job...mere fun to him. And it's wrestling.
Wrestler's Attitude : Vallen is impious, bottom line. Not only does he lack respect for authority; but he lacks respect for his fellow wrestler and anyone else who gets in his way. You could be the president of the United States and "smudge his puma" and he would jump you. This may be because of his spoiled upbringing...or maybe he was just born impious.
Wrestler's Enemies : Vallen doesn't like _anyone._ He has less respect for authority (hence his nickname) than wrestlers he works with...but still has hardly _any_ respect for them. He works by himself, and if he was to pair up with somebody, it'd be because a common hatred for another man. Vallen would still turn his back on the man when all was done.
------------------------- WRESTLER MOVE INFORMATION ------------------------- Wrestler's Style : Brawling / Ariel tactics Wrestler's Strengths : (1) His wrestling skill. He knows his moves...he's been watching wrestling his whole life and has just taken all of that in...and added a few unique moves of his own. (2) He's lived on the streets for 7 years now - not only is he pumped/ready for this tourney because he's ready to indulge the good life as much as he can, but he knows how to fight it out and not just wrestle. That
_could_ come in use. Wrestler's Weaknesses : (1) His blatant disregard for the rules. He can be easily disqualified, but he doesn't care. There is no limit for this man. (2) His inexperience. The WwCT is his (known) debut in
pro-wrestling - and what a debut it is. Wrestler's Favorite Moves : (1) Asai moonsault (2) Rana from top rope sending them into corner -> bronco buster (3) Tornado DDT (4) Frog splash (5) Cannonball (totally signature move): jumps off of top-rope and onto opponent like a cannonball onto their gut. (6) Flying clothesline (7) Rana to the outside of the ring -> suicide plancha onto them (8) Spinning heel kick (9) 450 splash (10) MAJOR REVERSAL: When Vallen is lifted up for a powerbomb, his signature reversal is quickly spinning around and pushing the wrestlers head into the mat. Wrestler's Finishing Move : The Impious Ending Description : Low blow, which kneels them over -> missle dropkick ---------------------------------- WRESTLER RING ENTRANCE INFORMATION ---------------------------------- Theme Music : "Isreal's Son" by Silverchair Wrestler Entrance (apparel and attire included) : (Cue "Isreal's Son" by Silverchair. Immediately, the fans explode with a mix between jeers and cheers - regardless, they're crazy to see the man emerging from the back.)
Ring Announcer: Introducing first - weighing in tonight at two-hundred and thirty-six pounds and hailing from Phoenix, Arizona... "IMPIOUS"
VALLEN GALLEGOS! (Almost immediately after the ring announcer's words ring throughout the arena, "Impious" Vallen Gallegos begins pacing down the aisle in leather pants, black boots, and an "anarchy" T-shirt. Halfway down the aisle, the green haired young man rips his shirt off and tosses it at a fan. He begins laughing as he looks at the fans side-by-side and spits on one that yells at Vallen. He then sprints down the aisle and slides into the ring, where he shoves the ring announcer over and puts the microphone in his leather pants after taking off his belt. Vallen acts even younger than his ripe age of 19 - and looks younger, too. He has baby blue eyes and green hair, down to the bottom of his ears. He runs around the ring as the fans now boo him more than cheer. His music fades out and Gallegos tosses the mic right on to the fallen ring announcer. Vallen holds his leather belt high before stuffing it into his pants - preparing to use it when needed.)
