NEW ROLEPLAY?

15 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 SV110MHH wrote: > > I am interested in joining some efed, but I have one problem, I don't know > what roleplay is. If you can tell me please email me. Thanx in advance. > > Shawn Essentially, a roleplay is a transcript of an interview. You can have your wrestler in a special scene, his home, whatever. In roleplays you can talk about upcoming matches, opponents, or whatever your wrestler wants to talk about. :) You start out an rp describing the scene. This doesn't have to be large, but some people make it large (like myself). You can rp in a variety of ways. Anything from just your wrestler talking, to a wrestler roaming the streets talking to people, to a press conference. I'll post a sample roleplay here, done by myself, so you can see what I am talking about. Kent --------- [3-15-97] [Anaheim, California. The locker room of the EWArena.] [The camera enters the locker room, and the door closes behind them. The camera man continues to walk through the locker room, which is vacated completley. He passes by several gym lockers, all painted with the blue and white EWA colors. The floor of the locker room has a few puddles of water near the shows, left of the cameraman. There are no windows in the locker room, to keep wandering eyes from seeing the dressing wrestlers.] [The camera man continues, and as he turns the corner he sees Magus entering the far locker room door. He is wearing shades, blue jeans, and an all blue EWA gym bag. Magus comes up to the camera, drops his bag on the bench, and sits down. He starts to un-tie his shoes, and he opens his bag, taking out his wrestling tights, and wrestling boots. Not by his side is the EWA NA Title.] Magus: You guys are allowed in here? Not very good security .. but that's alright. [smiles and takes off one of his shoes] Why am I here? As you know, EWA RARE is tommorrow, and I already made the trip to the arena, so I decided that I would come down here and do a little bit of physical preperation for my upcoming handicapped match, as well as some mental preperation. By the way, if you were wondering why I didn't bring my title along with me .. I'll tell you this: I don't think I need it to train, do you? Now onto ... Crossfire and myself vs. The 'Awesome' Ones. You guys, the Great One and Suitz, seem to act like you aren't afraid at all that we can beat you. _Very_ confident of your skills, eh? It's more than just confidence, it's a _large_ ego. Both of you are arrogant, obscene, over optomistic wrestlers. Both of you don't seem to understand about the fine-line beween arrogance, and confidence. I think I'll clear that up for you, eh? [Magus pulls out a towel from his gym bag, and lays it down across the bench. He then looks back up to the camera.] Confidence is a positive attitude that you can perform at levels that were never expected of you, and that you never expected of yourself. It is when you go out there, each and every day with the eye of the tiger. But arrogance is when you say you _can't_ lose, that you are 'immortal' in the ring, when the truth is, you aren't. You act nonschalant about everything you do, when you should _really_ be acting serious about it. From what I have said, you two are the epitome of arrogance and your self proclaimed 'immortality' is going to be crushed, courtesty of Crossfire and myself. You want to throw the rules out of this match, boys? I'd be glad to oblige that request. I have taken it to the extreme a number of times, more than you could count, boys. You want to throw everything at me? Well, while you are throwing everything at me, I'll dodge everything, then Crossfire will hit you with twice as much as you threw at me. I am very _confident_ in the abilities of my partners, and I'm sure that they will hold theirs, with ease in this match up. [Magus smiles, then looks up at the ceiling, with a grin gracing his face. He then folds his arms and returns his look at the camera.] So, you want to end my career, Golden One? Hell, you are so _arrogant_ that you claim to _know_ you will end my career! I have gone through a lot tougher matches then the one tommorrow, but don't think I am underestimating you two. I know that if I act liek this will be an incredibly easy win, then it will be an incredibly tough win, or no win at all. Take every match seriously; a motto that I felt you have to live or die by in this sport. Crossfire and myself plan on showing you just how true this is, boys. [Magus rubs his slightly scruffy chin.] Guess I better shave, eh? [smiles] Golden Boy, you think that you are gonna lock on the Irish Coffin? You think that you will even get the CHANCE to lock on the Irish Coffin? I beg to differ, Suitz. I know that the Irish Coffin is a powerful hold. But .. do you know that the Executor, my finishing manuever is equally, if not more deadly? I've locked it on you before, Golden One, so by now you should be aware that if I lock the Executor on it won't be a pretty ride. EWA Sunday Night RARE: the card, the night that it all goes down. You have made your return to the EWA, and you are trying to make a big impact here. _This_ is the chance you need to prove yourselves, by taking out one of the EWA's best tag teams, and one of the EWA's best singles wrestlers. It isn't an easy task, by far. Are you up to the challenge, boys? You talk like you are, but can you 'walk the walk'. [smiles] All those questions, and SO much more will be answered on RARE! [Magus takes off his shirt, and puts on a sleevless t-shirt.] It's gonna be a war, boys. BANK ON IT! LIGHTS OUT! [fade to black.]
