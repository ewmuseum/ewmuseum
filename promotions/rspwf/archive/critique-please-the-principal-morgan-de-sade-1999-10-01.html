[Critique Please] "The Principal" Morgan De Sade

1 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A little weasel-like man walks down an apartment complex hallway. He glances ever so slightly behind himself and notices a large man following him. The man quickens his pace and comes to an elevator and pushes the button. The large man steps behind him as the elevator opens. The man attempts to rush inside but standing in his way is "The Principal" Morgan De Sade. De Sade pulls his dark round sunglasses to the edge of his nose and gives a look disappoint to the man. The larger man standing behind De Sade's disappointment pushes the little man inside the elevator as De Sade keeps his stoney gaze forward. The elevator doors close.
The scene cuts to the inside of the elevator. The little weasel-like man shakes subtlety as Karl, De Sade's bodyguard, pushes him against the wall as the camera pans down showing only half of De Sade's face and the large German intimidating the little man]
MDS: You disappoint me Gavin.... you said you were the best source of information money could buy. I put my faith in you and you have failed me miserably. You know Gavin, life is full of misery, loneliness, and suffering.... and it's all over much too soon. Karl, give him a demonstration of misery please.
[Karl smiles ever so slightly as the camera continues to hold the angle of De
Sade's half face. Karl punches Gavin in the stomach. Gavin doubles over quickly as Karl reaches down and grabs the scruff of his neck and picks him back up. Gavin strains to gain air as he speaks in a raspy voice]
G: I.... tried.... [gulps in a large breath of air and wipes his brow with one hand as he clutches his stomach with the other] Really Mr. De Sade, I tried. I could find any information on this Kraven Smith person. I followed him from the ACWA offices and he didn't do anything out of the ordinary!
[De Sade's half face still fills the left side of the screen as Karl lets go of Gavin. Gavin straightens his jacket and tries to step towards De Sade, but is pushed back by Karl. Suddenly the lights flicker as the elevator comes to a screeching halt]
MDS: What did he do then to appear ordinary? G: Well.... [catches his breath]... he walked down the street and caught a cab and then went to lunch at this little deli. He ordered a pastrami on rye and a glass milk. He sat down and ate it. Left a tip then picked it up as the waiter come over to clean the table and collect. He laughed at the waiter and flipped him a dime then told him to get a life.
[De Sade raises an eyebrow as he continues to gaze forward, his eyes never blinking and his voice steady as rock]
MDS: I would say he is out of the ordinary Gavin. Nobody realizes that some people expend tremendous energy merely to be normal. It sounds to me as if you have neglected to note this fact of life.
G: I'm not sure I understand what you mean? [De Sade sighs and closes his eyes for a moment. He then opens his eyes and speaks with paced enunciation]
MDS: Gavin..... Gavin..... Gavin.... [sighs again] You truly are a dolt. Everyone is entitled to be stupid, but you Gavin, like Rupert Shark, abuse the privilege. [The camera pulls quickly up overhead as De Sade turns around and faces Gavin. Gavin looks cautiously at the gothic figure standing before him. De Sade simply smiles] MDS: Gavin, sometimes I need what only you can provide.... your absence. [Looks to Karl} Karl... [Karl reaches in back of De Sade and releases the emergency stop button. The elevator starts on its trek back up the long dark shaft then suddenly comes to a stop]
MDS: If there is one thing that I am Gavin, and you will learn it now, is that
I am consistent. And consistency requires you to be as ignorant today as you were before you met me. Karl, dispose of this trash.
[Karl grabs Gavin by the back of the coat and leads him out on to the roof top] G: No! Wait! There is something I didn't tell you! Please! MDS: Too little, too late. You had your chance to gain my trust but you have failed me for the last and only time. Good day to you sir.
[Morgan nods to Karl as the elevator doors close. De Sade pushes the "B" button and the elevator begins its slow descent from the 13th floor. The camera pans downward and again, the left side of the screen is filled with half of De Sade's face. The elevator stops a few seconds later. Light shines into the elevator as the doors are heard opening. A very voluptuous woman enters and stands in the back. She looks at De Sade for an instant and smiles to herself]
MDS; What floor do you wish milady? Woman: Fifth floor please. Thank you. MDS: You are most welcome my dear..... [The elevator again stops a few seconds later. The doors open and the woman exits. De Sade's eye follows the woman's sultry form as her reflection is seen within his cruel gaze. The door closes and the elevator descends]
MDS: Ahhh.... the scent of a beautiful woman. Anyway, back to the matter at hand. It looks as if I will have to rely on wit and instinct if I am to defeat this Kraven Smith person. This should pose no threat. Kraven Smith, hear me, and hear me well..... The human mind treats a new idea the same way the body treats a strange protein; it rejects it. So, if you have any ideas as to what you are going to do to me in our upcoming match, forget it now. I have the edge. I may not know much about you, but any man stupid enough not to leave a simpleminded waiter a tip is asking for spit in his next sandwich. You pick up your tip before he can savor the gentle clinking of coins. But, I guess at this rate you'll save a little money each month and at the end of the year you'll be surprised at how little you really have.
[The elevator comes to a stop and the doors open to darkness. De Sade steps through and past the camera. The camera turns swiftly towards De Sade who stands in the darkness wearing his gothic black clothing. De Sade seems to almost sink into the darkness that is slowly enveloping him]
MDS: Kraven Smith, you, like Rupert Shark, will learn something of politics from the Principal. You will learn that politics is the skilled use of blunt objects. I shall introduce you to the Board of Education. Once you have been
"educated" by me and with the ample opportunities I will provided you at my school it shouldn't be hard to find employment at any fast food restaurant you wish. I think you overestimate your abilities as a wrestler.... but we shall soon see.... we shall soon see........
[De Sade steps back and becomes one with the darkness and the elevator door closes. Suddenly a loud crash is heard as something heavy hits the topside of the elevator. Glass from the lights overhead shatter and send splinters everywhere as De Sade's voice is heard from outside]
MDS: It seems that Gavin has arrived.....
