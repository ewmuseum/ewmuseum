[ROLEPLAY] Robie123. Critique or comments wanted, constructive or otherwise.

24 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there! This guy is brand new but I must say it's the best idea I've ever come up with. He's basically a guy in a robot suit who hates humans.
What I really want are opinions on the speech. I needed some way to illustrate his synthesised robot voice, and this was what I came up with. Is it too much?
I'm also looking for a fed to run it in. Something that doesn't expect more than one or two roleplays of this sort of length a week. Anyone want him?
******************** [SCENE: A strange looking laboratory. Large banks of flickering lights and buttons are all around. There is a large viewscreen in one wall, flashing on and off. Huge supercomputers line the walls. These walls are of bare hewn rock. It all looks like something right out of a poorly funded sci-fi flick.
In the centre of the room there is a large silver metal box, rather like a filing cabinet in many ways. There is silence except for a few buzzing and bleeping noises, and the camera moves slightly closer to the box.
Suddenly, the box starts banging and vibrating! The door swings open and a strange being walks out. It appears to be a man dressed in some sort of badly made robot suit! It is a very blocky gray robot suit at that, made out of some sort of cardboard. It had some twisted coathangers stuck out of the head for aerials, blinking lights make up its face, and metal cones constitute its ears. All sorts of dials and button are painted or glued onto his chest. Yes, this really is some guy in a robot suit. He waves his claw-hand stiffly in greeting, and speaks in a computer generated voice (if you've heard Steven Hawking talk, you'll know what I'm on about). The lights of his mouth oscillate wildly.]
