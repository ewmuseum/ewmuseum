[Critique] "Country Club" Chris Cameron [Flash]

27 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well this is my new character, and this is the first flash that I have done with him. Tell me what you think of it, and some things to improve on. I am also looking for one or two more feds to join with him. --- [Scene: Bel Air, California. Time: 4:30 P.M. The camera opens to the view of endless green grass. It is freshly cut, with different lengths all around. you see flags sticking out of the ground, and you realize that you are looking out at a golf course. The camera moves and starts to move toward a large building. When it is close enough you see a sign that reads "Bel Air Country Club." You enter through the large double doors and move toward a table that has two men sitting down playing cards. You can hear them making small talk as they play. You approach them even closer until you notice that one of them is none other than WOW superstar "Country Club" Chris Cameron. . .] [Cameron is sitting there with a pair of Polo khaki shorts that come down to around his knees. He has a dark green and white striped golf shirt on that is also made by Polo. His shoes are simply black and white golf shoes. His blonde hair is gelled and combed over the side. A silver watch rests on the left wrist of Cameron's. Chris looks over and sees the camera and shows a sly smile. . .] CC: [Lays down a card, followed by laying down the rest of his hand.] Gin! Damn, I'm good. That'd be two hundred dollars you owe me, you card shark. [Fully focuses his attention to the camera.] I'm a man of many talents and things. I've got all the luxuries I need, and as you just saw I can hold my own on the card scene. But unfortunately for one Will Sears, I'm better in the squared circle than I am at gin. [Chris then pauses as the man lays his money down on the table. Chris grabs, pulls a huge wad of money from his pocket, and adds the new money to it. He then stuffs it back into his pocket and continues. . .] CC: The WOW has gone out and recruited and signed all of the best wrestling talent that this world has to offer. They snagged Tommy Lionheart, Alex Cote, and the best and most exhilerating man, myself, Chris Cameron. And then they also had to sign some midcarders for people like me to get a tune up every now and then. Guys like Chris Barden, John South, and yep you guessed it, Will Sears. This Sunday at Live and Hardcore, that's all you'll be Sears, a tune up. You'll be like a practice round would be in golf. Simply a stepping stone to get you ready for something bigger and better. [Pause.] So many people are anticipating seeing "Country Club" step into the ring for the first time. And let me tell everybody, you won't be disappointed. What's the word I'm looking for to discribe it.... wonderful! That's it. [Showing sarcasm.] Oh wait, isn't that what Will Sears claims to be? Yeah, but judging from his wrestling ability he wouldn't know "wonderful" if it bit him in the ass. "Wonderful" would be a specimen of a body like I have. "Wonderful" would be having the ability to join a place like this. And "Wonderful" would definitely be what you'll call my match after I absolutley _dominate_ the self proclaimed "Wonderful" Will Sears. [Cameron gets up and starts to walk to the outside of the clubhouse. Once outside he stops and takes a big breath of fresh air. Chris then heads to the first tee where a caddy and set of clubs are waiting for him. . .] CC: [Looking out at the first hole.] What a beautiful site. It's just too bad that all of you underclass wrestling fans won't ever be able to see it. Why you ask? Because only a certain kind of people hand around here. You gotta have money, you gotta have style, you gotta have talent, and you gotta have class. And after the first qualification most of you WOW fans out there lost your chance. And besides, why would most of you people want to come here? [Smiling.] If I didn't take your money in the clubhouse playing gin, I'd sure as hell take it out here on the course. [Chris stops and goes to the tee box and tess his ball up. He then steps back, takes a few practice swings, and continues to speak. . .] CC: But I guess that I can't take your money on Sunday, huh Will. I guess I'll just have to settle for pride... But that's cool, because all that really matters is that I pick up my debut victory which is as sure a bet as you will ever get. Will, it will be a wonderful match for both of us now that I think about it. A wonderful victory for me, and the most wonderful match you will ever be and lose in. I mean, when you step into the ring with me, it's automatically wonderful. But for all you that hate me because you can't be like me, you can just wait and see. On Sunday, Sears' ass is mine and you might as well go ahead and chalk up one in the win column for the man they call... COUNTRY. . . CLUB!! [The scene starts to fade to black as Cameron steps up to the ball and blasts a drive right down the middle. . .] [name withheld] -- "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor [AMCW] "Country Club" Chris Cameron [WOW, FREE AGENT]
