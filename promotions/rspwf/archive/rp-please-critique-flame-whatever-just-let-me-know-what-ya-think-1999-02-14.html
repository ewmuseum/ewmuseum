RP Please Critique, Flame, whatever, just let me know what ya think

14 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my latest RP for the TWL. I'd really like for some people to tell me what I'm doing wrong, right, or whatever, Thanks. It's really not all that great until the end which I love.
***Dave is driving through the streets of Indianapolis in his 1980 Chevrolet Station Wagon. The car isn’t very nice at all with some kind of torn up red interior and no rear view mirror. Dave however looks pretty nicely dressed today as he is wearing a pair of loose khaki pants and a blue and white checked button up long sleeved shirt tucked in with a thick black belt on. He is also wearing his normal black Oakleys with his hair tied back, he appears to have not shaved in a day or two but looks well maintained.***
Dave:Alright now I know all the great TWL fans out there are wondering, where is Dave going? Well in response to that I’ll tell ya where I’m going,
I’m going to a fortune teller! Her name is Madam Esmerelda and she’s apparently pretty good. You see I’m gonna go in there and find out how many more days it’s gonna be before I get a title in the TWL, or at least my first win. Where better is there to go than Madam Esmerelda’s? Apparently she’s from Hungary where she lived with a group of traveling fortune tellers living in constant torment when one day she sold a magical bean to a young prince and was granted freedom to the U S of A. Now I don’t know how valid the story is but that’s what I heard from Crazy Bob, and he’s pretty reliable.....
***Dave is cut off by another driver and is forced to slam on the brakes.*** Dave:Dammit, I hate drivers. Anyway as I was saying the best thing about the peanut butter cup isn’t so much the peanut butter, I particulary like the chocolate cup that the peanut butter is in. You see if it were’t for the chocolate cup it wouldn’t be a peanut butter cup, it’d be peanut butter. I don’t really understand why it’s called a peanut butter cup it seems to me it should be called a chocolate cup filled with peanut butter.
I mean it’s just common sense people**Dave pauses** No wait this is for the
TWL not that anti-Peanut butter cup campaign I’ve been working on, sorry.
Anyway I guess I should probably discuss wrestling or something. Now I know
I talk about wrestling a lot, but hey, it’s my job. Well let’s see where should I begin, possibly The Dentist Marvin Davis.... Possibly that gangster wannabe Raymond Knight....Maybe even that loser arrogant bastard
Delicious Dave Davidson **Dave pauses** Wait, edit that last part out cameraboy.
***Dave sops for a minute and waits.*** Dave:Well, let’s start again after the editing. There are a lot of people I could talk about, but I think I’ll talk about The Dentist Marvin Davis. Now
Marvin I know it’s gonna be tough to follow the little poem I told about our match in my last interview, but hey what the hell. Now Dentist I hope your well aware of what getting your teeth pulled feels like, because when you fight me it’s like that, except ten times worse. Not many people as pretty as me can cause so much pain. Dentist I don’t know who you’re trying to fool, I don’t know why you’re even gonna show up. Remember the battle royal at A Cold Day In Hell, how I beat the hell out of you. See Dentist
I’m gonna show everybody that, while you might be 6 or so inches taller, 80 pounds heavier, and a lot uglier, I’m still a far superior athlete. I’m still the greatest athlete to ever step into the ring. Not only do I have some of the fastest feet this side of the Mississippi, I’m also the greatest high flying, and technical wrestler around. Dentist when you walk out to the ring with the fans booing and calling you names such as
Delicious dork and **Dave starts to look sad** The Gayve, and Pussyicious
Dave, and......**Dave wipes something from his eye** Nevermind. But Marvin when you hear those boo’s for you I want you to say to yourself, “Wow, Dave really is loved, he’s the greatest alive.” Yo know why I want you to say that? Because it’s true. I’ve proven myself to be the Rave again and again.
I’ve shown the world I’m the best alive, and now I’ll show the TWL.....Wait the TWL is in the world so I guess I’ve shown them already.......Anyway
Dentist stepping into the ring with the great one is the dumbest career move you’ve ever made. It’s a career ending move in fact.
***Dave looks to the side of the road.*** Dave:Ahhh, there it is. ***Dave pulls the station wagon to the side of the road and parks. He then proceeds to get out and walk up to the door of the house. It’s a very big mansion and is obviously not in the heart of Indianapolis, it is in a very nice area. It’s huge and is all white in Victorian design. It looks like mansion’s you see on movies. Dave walks up to the porch where the front door is located. It is a big wooden door with a gold door knocker which
Dave uses three times. He then stands to wait when a beautiful woman in lingerie walks to the door. She is wearing a white teddy and has a perfect body. She has long blonde hair and looks like a movie star Dave’s jaw drops when he sees her.***
Girl:Hello, can I help you. Dave:Uh....du.....la....my......ummmm.... Girl:Are you ok? Dave:Uhhh...Yeah. Is this Madam Esmerelda’s the fortune telling place. Girl:No, this is Madam Heidi’s the bordello. Madam Esmerelda’s is down the street. Dave:Well um....maybe I could come in. Girl:Is that your car? Dave:Yep it sure is. Girl:Sorry, you couldn’t afford me. Dave:Bu..... ***The girl slams the door in Dave’s face. He stands there for a second before turning around and walking back to his car he re-enters the car and looks at the camera.***
Dave:WOW! Did you see her eyes, she’s obviously in love. Ya know the girls like to play hard to get around big stars like the rave. Many of them are intimidated by my physique and good looks. They feel that I might be too good for them or something. I mean when you’re not some little jobronie like Raymond Knight you get this kind of publicity, however with publicity comes problems. Why just yesterday I walk outside and I’m mobbed by three young woman. They wanted me to sign these things called Sepeeners or something. I’d never heard of em so I just threw the things away. Or another time last week I caught some kid trying to take my paper. The kid was smart though, he had a whole bag of papers with him and was riding a bike, I think he was stealing a lot of my papers or something.
***Dave turns the car back on and starts driving down the street.*** Dave:Now, I heard myself mention Raymond Knight so hey I’ll talk about his no good, Dave wannabe, wishes he was delicious, or the rave, booty. Now
Raymond we all knoe you go home every night get in bed and dream of being a huge mega-star like myself. We know that in your little fantasy world you are the rave, and your an icon. In that world you can do my amazing aerial techniques and lightning kicks. But Raymond Knight the population of that world is one and that one person is Damon, damn, sorry I mispronounced your name like the rest of the world, I meant Raymond Knight. Raymond at night you can retreat to the world where your on Dave’s level but when the morning hits so does reality. The cold truth that you so fear creeps upon you and you once again realize that you’re just another person Dave’s gonna beat on his climb to the top. Raymond do you truly in your heart believe you are better than the rave? Do you actually think for one nanosecond that your no good arrogant behind can go toe to toe with the greatest professional athlete alive today. Raymond it may not be today or tomorrow or the twenty-second of March, or the fifteenth of May, or the first of
April, but one day Raymond you’ll face your greatest fear a fear that is a dream to every woman alive, a fear known simply as Delicious Dave. DAMN!!!
***Dave slams on the brakes and comes to a screeching halt.*** Dave:Whoo, I almost passed Madam Esmerelda’s, let’s check this out. ***Dave steps out of his now parked car and walks up a sidewalk to a small house. It is just a normal white house with nothing fancy about it. Dave steps up to the door, which is blue with a crystal ball on it, and proceeds to knock. Soon a woman comes and answers the door. She has on a tag that says Esmerelda on her long flowing purple robe. She has coal black hair and isn’t very attractive with moles on her face. Dave flinches when she first appears but then relaxes.***
Esmerelda:**With New York accent** Whaddya want? **In Hungrian accent.** I mean what would you like.
Dave:Are you the fortune teller Madam Esmerelda? Esmerelda:Yes, can’t you read the nametag dumbass? Dave:Oh, sorry. I’m here to get my fortune read. Esmerelda:What else would you be here for a moon pie? Dave:Hmmmmm, good point. Esmerelda:Alright, come in. ***Dave steps in and sees a very strange room. It has purple carpet, black walls, and a table set p in the middle of the room with a crystal ball, and a book on how to read palms. Dave walks up and sits down as does
Esmerelda.*** Esmerelda:Ok, what would you like a palm reading or the crystal ball. Dave:How about the crystal ball. Esmerelda:Alright, it is done. Hmmmm, I see you are a banker of some sort. Dave:No. Esmerelda:A Businessman? Dave:Nope. Esmerelda:Accountant? Dave:Wrong again. Esmerelda:How about you tell me? Dave:I’m a wrestler. Esmerelda:Ahhhh, I see it now. Dave:Wow, you are good! So what do you see, me with gold around my waist? Esmerelda:No, you’re in a wheelchair. Dave:**Scared**WHAT? I’m a star I can’t be hurt. Esmerelda:I’m just messing with you. You are getting pinned by a man. Dave:First of all you got a sick sense of humor. Secondly I’ll never be pinned again I’m the great one.
Esmerelda:Yes, I see them raising his hand I believe the man’s name is
Damon. Dave:No, no, no this must be some kind of mistake, maybe that thing shows the opposite of the future. I’ll fight Raymond Knight someday but on that day I’ll be victorious. I’m idolized by millions. I’m the perfect role model to children. I’m the greatest athlete alive today! That thing must be broke, I can’t lose. Let’s look at something else **Dave stops** How about my love life, what women are in my future.
Esmerelda:Let me see. ***Esmerelda looks into the ball and whispers chants.*** Esmerelda:All I see is a hand. Dave:A HAND!!? This damn ball doesn’t work I.....I’m a champion a great athlete, a hero, a leader to all. This has to be one of three things.
Either you’re no good at this, you got paid off by Knight or the Dentist. Or you are upset at the fact that you can never have me so you are trying to scare me, then you’ll come back to me and try to get me to love you, but guess what you twisted psycho I’m leaving so just take your hundred bucks
**Dave pulls out a bill and throws it down.** And get the hell outtta my house, I mean your house, I mean...... AHHHHHHHH! ***Dave runs from the house frightened and screaming as Esmerelda laughs. Dave jumps in his car and takes off.***
