[RP] Eddie Rue [Critique If you Dare]

22 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 [A man is seen sitting in a rocking chair. He turns, giving off a cold, calculating, face of death. Humbled only by a slight grin.
He refuses to release his expression, not speaking other than his ever so calm, passive bodily movements.
Rocking back slightly in his chair. Steadily observing the figure before him. After minutes of deliberation, he exhales as a cold mist comes forth from his mouth. His hand moves slightly, his eye deterred to focus on the figure before him.
Trying to maintain his ominous standpoint. He slowly brings his hand to his chest. Patting it with coven witness to his own multiple-persona behavior.
At first -- he looked at the figure before him with bewilderment and unsure of it's cause. Slowly, he comes under the realization it is a cameraman. Then, taking his other hand, lifting his middle and index finger up, and ushering the figure before him to come closer...]
The bolstering spirit that is in every human soul annoys me. [Blanking the expression on his face. Then he grows weary, his eyes turn into a whirlpool of despair.]
Such confidence. It's a lost relic of one's soul to be disappointed. Tell me, Tony Paseno -- Do you ever lose your confidence? I'd bet you'd say no. No?! WHY...?!?!
[The man frowns.] I think your confidence drops continuously. Every second of each passing day you suffer a down and wait for something to lift you back up into high spirits once more.
[He sits and watches the sweep hand on his watch.] And hey, you know what? I am not going to become the thing you use to jumper that confidence.
I know -- if you don't put me out this time -- you will be frustrated. After all, you must have been pretty *frustrated* when I kicked out of two
PasenoBombs. I would have kicked out of the third ... but ... my body told me
'no'.
[Looks at himself. His arm is in a sling, his stomach is heavily rapped in medical tape and gauze pads. And a patch on his right eye. It would be imagined he is in great pain and displeasure, however, he could tell you otherwise.]
If there was such a thing as happiness -- I, Eddie Rue, am experiencing it right now. [Eddie splashes a crooked smile on his face.] Pain is bliss, Tony Paseno. And I will enjoy every minute of it. But you want to know why I consider it bliss?
[He laughs menacingly.] It's because I know it *FRUSTRATES* a big mauling animal like you to know
... a mere average-sized mortal ... like me will take your beatings and still come back for more.
Week in and week out, Paseno, I cautiously mind my opponent that my purpose is to bind their carcass to the grave, yet at the same time releasing their soul. But this week -- my purpose changes.
This week ... I *cremate* your body on the altar of god ... But until then
... [Rue focuses his attention at the camera. Getting up. Walking forward.] It's time for you to . . . [He takes his right hand, ushering it left to right over camera's lens ever-so coaxingly. Then, in a faint voice.] Sleeeeeeeeeep. [Eddie slinks away, limping. And the segment does the same; limping into the darkness.] ~ John B. * JABronie Handler of: Eddie Rue [G.S.W.P., W.E.T.] "Luke ... The Lag will be with you ... Always." ~ Star Wars Internet Humour
