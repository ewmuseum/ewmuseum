[FLASH] Mister Yuck Killers [FLASH]

17 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yup, this is the latest effort from the MYK, the Mister Yuck Killers. This baby, originally "aired" on EMWC's "Noise Pollution"
Opinions? Insults? MARKOUTS... It's all welcome. Let me know what you all think by replying to this post. ==================== [The Las Vegas strip, a monument to materialism and frivolously wasteful human self-indulgence. In all of its artificially lit glory, a clear view of the Strip is provided by the window of another needlessly extravagant toy -- a large and sleek black limousine. The camera pans to allow a better view of the inside of the limousine. Sitting at opposite ends of a standard leather seat in back of the limousine are the Mr. Yuck Killers, Devil Elvis and William Craven.
Elvis stares at the Las Vegas Strip outside his window, the wild and bright lights reflected somewhat in his dark eyes. Devil's black cherry hair looks slightly different from the last time it graced an EMWC camera, having grown longer. The young man's preference for simple attire remains the same, however, as he wears only nondescript black jeans, black shoes, and a black t-shirt with the words "YOU BORE ME" written in a snaking white script.
Craven, on the other hand, has himself all dressed up for his visit to the Sin City. Or, perhaps in Craven's twisted mind, he is dressed up. In reality, the elder Mr. Yuck Killer wears a rather tattered navy blue blazer, a white button-up shirt with the faintest of pinstripes, and pants that just aren't quite the right shade of blue to match the blazer. William seems content, however, nursing a bottled drink of some sort in one hand while looking with mild interest at the various casinos, shopping centers, and other circus-style attractions that the Strip provides.
For a moment, the soft noises of the limousine are all that accompany the shot of the Yuck Killers. Something that bears resemblance to a laugh comes from Devil Elvis, though his face remains devoid of expression. The camera zooms in somewhat on Devil's face, which looks youthful yet timeless, all at once. Elvis turns his head, slowly, to face the camera. He begins speaking rapidly before he's even entirely facing the camera.]
Devil Elvis: Though several of my points have already been proven, physically and otherwise, it seems that the very dregs of the EMWC roster persist. They stand firm in common ignorance and denial of their predestination to failure.
[The very beginnings of a scowl form on Devil's face. His eyes are ablaze, though it seems that the intensity these "windows to the soul" contain has only begun to shine through.]
DE: The rise of a new force in EMWC signals the arrival of certain things, though. The "Mister Yuck Killers", as we have been dubbed, will leave a lasting effect on the EMWC. With the...
[Devil appears reluctant.] DE: Mister... Yuck... Killers... Come a variety of revelations. [The camera cuts to a shot of both Mr. Yuck Killers.] DE: Judgment. [Cut to only Devil Elvis.] DE: Damnation. [Cut to William Craven, who jerks himself violently toward the camera. A grin that conveys no happiness whatsoever forms on his worn face, and he leers at the camera.]
William Craven: Salvation. DE: _Driver_. [The word is snapped in such a manner that it is painfully obvious that Devil considers the limousine driver beneath him. The camera does a 180 degree pan,] DE: We'll be staying at the Luxor, in case you've forgotten. [With a look of slight annoyance, and just a touch of fear, the driver nods to the rearview mirror, and hence to Elvis, before turning his attention back to the road. As the camera pans back around, catching glimpses of casinos and hookers and pimps, William Craven is already speaking.]
WC: Salvation of the mind...salvation of the soul... Destruction of the physical form... [Raggedly exhaling, Craven runs his fingers through his hair, which now looks to be a white buzzcut. Set in stark contrast to his ever disturbing translucent blue eyes, it provides a striking visual effect.
Glancing up, glancing down, sipping at his nondescript brown bottle, then words...] WC: Said it once...said it again, and again, again...againagainagain... "It gets worse"... The image of the villain of yesteryear has become the image of the hero of today. The villain of today becomes the hero of tomorrow.
You hate us today, you worship us tomorrow. Imagine for a moment the Las Vegas strip... The very heart of sin... Drunken debauchery, sex, drugs, rock and roll, gambling... Murder... [With a chuckle, Craven holds his drink up to the camera, showing that the brown bottle bears a crimson label. It reads "Little Devil's Red Rum"...]
WC: Do you get it...yet? Look at where we are...the city of sin and the site of salvation. In twenty years more kids will be coming here than Disneyworld. Then, finally...it will be so tame in comparison to the rest of the world, that this "city of sin" will cease to be... Suddenly, it's nothing but another community.
Today's hell is tomorrow's paradise. [Glancing at his demonic compatriot now...] WC: Do you know what "Las Vegas" means? [A blank stare and an annoyed sneer from Elvis tells Craven clearly that the answer is "no".]
WC: Neither do I... But Los Angeles is "the Angels"...so...maybe...Las Vegas means "the" something... "The Sin" perhaps...? [Devil Elvis shoots Craven an annoyed glance before pulling a bottle of water out of a cooler next to him and gulping it down.]
Driver: "The Fertile Valleys". [Craven lifts his eyes to the front of the limo.] WC: What? [The camera pans about to look at the driver.] D: Las Vegas... It means "The Fertile Valleys"... WC: My...how...disappointing... [Craven falls silent as the camera pans back around the limousine. Devil Elvis gives Craven a quick once-over, before speaking in a tone less harsh than that which he has a tendency to use when speaking to people.]
DE: William... When you go looking for evil like that, it usually isn't there. You wanted Las Vegas to translate to something "sinister", just to amuse yourself. But that...
[Devil pauses, and almost appears to be reflecting.] DE: That is not how things work. You don't go looking for bad things to happen, they just...
[Elvis, in a blur of motion, reaches toward the camera lens. Though the camera is moved back some, likely in fear of Devil striking it, Elvis barely touches it. And when he pulls his hand away, all that's left for the camera to film is a round bright green face, tongue protruding from its mouth. All that's left... Is Mr. Yuck.]
DE: Happen. [And with that, the shot makes one final transition. From green...to black.] ==================== -- Every last damn thing I've typed in this post is Š and Ž 1999/Y2K [name withheld], by god! Whoo-hah!!!
