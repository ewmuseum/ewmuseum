4 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my latest flash for ICW...please give constructive criticism... [ Camera opens to the locker room area after a blockbuster Friday Night Fallout. The media floods the locker room hallways, interviewing wrestlers and staff. Many reporters surround new ICW President, Shane Diamond, asking him questions about his plans in office. Tyler Rich sees the camera and leads him through the masses toward an empty hallway in the arena. Rich knocks on a door, but no answer so he turns the knob and the door opens and Rich walks in . . . ]
[ The room has clothes thrown all over, tables broken into pieces, a broken television set, and a locker laying flat on the ground. FBK sits in a corner across the room with a towel over his head. He has on a pair of mesh "And 1"
green and white shorts with no shirt and no shoes. The camera can hear him breathing heavily all the way across the room . . . ]
[ Rich moves toward FBK, but the camera man keeps his distance. Rich has to hurdle over a few objects but finally stands just a few feet from FBK. Rich speaks . . . ]
TYLER RICH - Umm...FBK? Are you alive? [ FBK doesn't respond . . . ] TYLER RICH - Its me...your buddy Tyler. I guess you're kinda bummed out about the International Title match you had tonig--
[ FBK suddenly springs to his feet and grabs Tyler Rich by the collar of his shirt and lifts him up off his feet and up against a wall . . . ]
FBK - Seems like you got everthything figured out, Ty. Life must be tough for you...wearing the fancy suits, asking a few guys questions, lookin' pretty.
Well you're right, da Killah is bummed out and he don't need your stylin' and profilin' ass, bitchin' to him for another interview. Yeah, so da Killah got screwed like a ten dollar crack whore...what else is there to be said?
TR - There's plenty more to be said, Killah! [ FBK drops Rich to the ground and Rich lands on his rear. Rich then gets up and rubs his behind . . . ]
FBK - What else can be said? TR - Plenty...let it all out, talk to Ty! FBK - So in other words...it'll just be another interview for you... TR - You see right through me! But it'll work out for you as well, just tell me what you feel...let all the anger and hostility out, come on my man!
FBK - Ugh...alright, lets get this show on the road. TR - Great! Now, tonight, I'll have to admit...you did get screwed. Christian
Madison struck Alex Cote first, therefore you should've won. But the odd thing is, you would've won, but not have taken home the ICW International Title. Did
Madison do that purposely to make sure he held onto the belt? Do you think he was ducking you?
FBK - Probably. Ty, look at me...what do you see? TR - One big guy that could kick a lot of a--I mean behind! FBK - Heh, exactly. Madison hitting Cote to stop the match was another way for him to put his tail in between his legs and run. He knows that coveted title of his would've became property of da Killah if we fought out that last fall.
Madison...you cost da Killah the ICW International Title. Da Killah _IS_ the uncrowned ICW International Champion. You sure as hell better duck me in the upcoming weeks because you've got hell to pay. No one cheats FBK out of something and gets a way with it...and you can take that to the _BANK_!
TR - I wouldn't want to be on your bad side, thats for sure! You do hold a pinfall over the champion...as he holds one over you. The third pinfall was thrown out, but it should've belonged to you by disqualification. FBK, you hold a pinfall over the ICW International Champion! You've got to be excited about that...aren't you?!
FBK - Why? Da Killah doesn't have anything to show for it. Look at my waist...[Camera soons in at FBK's wash-board stomach] Its bare if you're blind...nothing on it. Sure, da Killah went to a stalemate with Christian
Madison and he sure as hell could've beat him...but he ran, like the sweet
_PU-TANG_ he is! Da Killah would love to step in that ring at Spring Slamathon and beat the living hell out of Christian Madison and Alex Cote to become ICW
International Champion...but lets be realistic, the ICW Front Office doesn't want it.
TR - Whether they want it or not...with your ability, some day its going to happen! FBK - You can bet your scrawny, overpaid ass it will! TR - Yeah! I mean, hey! Anyway, the main reason you don't have that International Title around your waist, or even a disqualification win around your belt is because of one man...Shane Diamond. The Diamond has taken over as
ICW President and boy did he screw you. Have anything in store for Shane
Diamond, payback? FBK - Definitely. The bastard cost me the match and the title, what the hell do you think?! Shane, you little piece of crap. You messed with the wrong man, and now your presidency is gon' take a turn for the worse. Call me Houdini because da Killah has a whole bunch of magic up his friggin' sleeve in store for you!
Being the president of ICW is gon' make you a marked man, Shane. And unfortunately for you, da Killah's a sniper and when he aims...he hits his target. You're the bullseye for me, Shane...so consider yourself in a helluva lot of trouble. Da Killah knows you'll be at Rockin' da World as well as
Fallout this upcoming week...hell, if you're not then you're not doing your job. Just be prepared..._FOR ANYTHING_!
TR - Ah, I wouldn't like those words being addressed towards me. But thats not all, Killah. The Diamond has booked you in a match against "The Hammer" Alex
Cote in the main event of this upcoming Fallout. His record in ICW is practically immaculate at sixteen wins, two losses, and two ties. He doesn't lose very often...and I doubt he plans to lose to you. Your comments on that...
FBK - Well that bastard better change his plans because he's gon' lose to da
Killah! Cote, you can ramble on and on about how you're gon' take the ICW
International Title at Spring Slamathon, but da Killah don't give a damn because da Killah ain't fighting you then. He's fighting you at this upcoming
Fallout with something as big as a title is on the line...respect. Cote, you're probably one of the most, if not _THE_ most established wrestler in tha eye-see-dubbya. Our battle on Fallout should entertain the fans. We're both big, powerful men and have similar styles. It should be interesting, but
Cote...you're _LONG_ due for a loss...and da Killah's gonna be honored to make it three losses on your record. Its gon' suck goin' into Slamathon after suffering a defeat...but its something you're gon' have to get used to because on Friday, April ninth, da Killah's gon' take your big ass on a roller coster ride with some absolute _EXTREME_ action thats gon' leave you puking your brains out once its all said and down. Because when it comes down to the truth, da Killah's gon' bitch slap yo' ass up like a . . .
. . . _NEW YORK CITY PIMP_ . . . smackin' some skanky . . . . . . _TEN DOLLAR CRACK WHORE_ ! ! ! TR - Now you mean business! One more man I'd like to address...Westside Walavan. He says he's the best from the west, and its kinda hard to argue with him! He just suffered a controversial loss to Mike Masters tonight on Fallout.
You're scheduled to lock up with him at Spring Slamathon, April eighteenth, with a Hardcore Title shot on the line for him. Are you confident going into that match?
FBK - First off, da Killah will be _VERY_ confident after he beats Alex Cote the Fallout prior to Slamathon. Second off, sure, he may be the best in the west...but what sucks for him is that da Killah's da best from da east,
_BIZZNITCH_! Walavan, da Killah's gon' chew you up and spit you out like Lenny
Dykstra did with his tobacco. Your chances of beating the most exhilarating, breath-taking wrestler in the biz today are _SLIM-TO-NONE_! You're can't wear da Killah's jock and you sure as hell can't lace his boots. You lost to Mike
Masters, which makes you a Grade A _JAY-BRONE_ in my book. You come to Caesar's
Palace in Las Vegas, Nevada and da Killah'll give you a clinic on _EXTREME_ wrestling, something you know nothing about. You wanting to be ICW Hardcore
Champion is no more than a lame joke. Take a number and wait your place in line...because as long as FBK is in the wrestling biz, he'll always be the
_HARDCORE LEGEND_! Walavan, da Killah hopes that chair shot he gave you on
Fallout knocked some sense into you because in a hardcore match, you ain't nothin' but _MA' BITCH_ compared to da Killah. Walavan, da Killah opened up a hospital for all the guys he plans on beating the other day, its called "The
Spanked Monkey." Your reservations have been confirmed Walavan, the only thing left for da Killah to do is...
. . . _KNOCK_ . . . . . . _YOU_ . . . . . . _OUT_ ! ! ! TR - Well, aren't we a little determined to be the best?! FBK - Damn straight, rich boy. TR - Excuse me? Anyway, FBK, I'm sure you're ready to get this over with. Any closing comments?
FBK - Nothin' da Killah hasn't said already. Madison...you have somethin' that belongs to da Killah and he's comin' to get it...and thats the ICW
International Title. Diamond...heh, the best advice da Killah could give you is watch your back. Cote and Walavan...your lives are about to be made a living hell...
. . . _FROM_ . . . . . . _BELL_ . . . . . . _TO_ . . . . . . _BELL_ . . . [ FBK then tosses the towel that was previously on his head, at Tyler Rich and storms out of the room. The door slams behind him as the camera stares at Tyler
Rich... FADE ]
