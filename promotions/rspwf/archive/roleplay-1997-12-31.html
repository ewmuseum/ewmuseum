Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In my opinion that wasn't a roleplay, it was just something he said. A roleplay includes gestures, emotions, description of the background, and so forth. Compare these two short roleplays and you'll see what I mean. ------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Fade in: the Underduck is swimming in a small lake in a park. It looks into the camera and says:) "IEWA duckracing division! You are all doomed because the Underduck is coming for you!" (The Underduck then scares a few swans away) "Quack in piece!" (Fade out) ------------------------------------------------------------------------ (Fade in: a small lake in a park. It seems to be very early in the morning as there is a red coloring in the sky and dew is dripping from the grass. A soft rustle of movement, perhaps a squirrel. The scenery of the golden rays of the sun reflected by the surface of the water is calming and relaxing. A swan family is sunbathing, the sunrays are dancing gently on the feathers of the majestic birds. However, as the camera zooms in closer, we can clearly see that one of the birds is not a swan but a duck. It's features are hideous and ugly compared to the noble swans, and with a glimpse of intensity in it's eyes it opens it's crooked bill and croaks:) "IEWA... the duckracing division... you are all doomed because the Underduck is coming for you..." (The duck then throws it's neck back and hisses at the swans. A sound full of hate and cruetly the swans panic and flee in every direction, causing the Underduck to throw it's head back and cackle in insane glee:) "Quack... in... piece..." (Face out) ------------------------------------------------------------------------ Do you see the difference? Hope I could help you, tell me what you think! Easy G
