(FREE AGENT FLASH) 'The Icon' Shawn Kearns

29 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The Camera fades into focus, and standing in the middle of a dark alley is
'The Icon' Shawn Kearns. He is 6' 2", 245 pounds, and is carrying a chair. For some odd reason, he has a microphone, let's listen in.)
'The Icon' Shawn Kearns: Everyone out there in Wrestling land, my name is Shawn
Kearns. I have been in many successful federations. I have not been on the
R.S.P.W.F Top 200 yet, but I am climbing the ranks. I want every last one of you pieces of trash to know that I am in the house, and I am looking for a fed that wants me. My current federation, the UWF
( http://www.geocities.com/Broadway/5327/body.htm ) is on hiatus right now, so I need a good fed that is half way worthy of my incredible wrestling skills. I am a simple man, and I have got a simple philosophy, I am the best, the rest suck.
To give you a little insite on my life, I have held three major titles, two secondary titles, and two tag titles in my carrer. But, for some strange and odd reason, some jobber from way back when I first started keeps stalking me, of course I get rid of him, but, it is annoying.
(From behind a dumpster, some guy in wrestling tights jumps out and starts running at 'The Icon')
'The Icon' Shawn Kearns: See what I mean. (As the jobber approaches, Shawn picks him up and nails him with a Cradle DDT, the setup for his finisher. He picks him up off the ground and nails him with
The Superkick. He lays the chair on his head, pics up the mic, and starts talking again)
'The Icon' Shawn Kearns: I know what you are all thinking, that I am just a copy of the best superstar in wrestling, Shawn Michaels. You are wrong, see my controllers real name is Shawn Kearns, so this is just his dream, being a wrestler. It also just so happens that his favorite move is the Superkick, so he chose it as his finisher. However, back to me. I am willing to go by rules, even though I don't totally agree with authority (this is where the I am the
Best, the Rest suck thing comes into play). I want to be the best, so therefore
I must destroy the rest. But most important, I need to be in a good fed. So, anyone who wants a REAL Wrestler, my e-mail is
[detail withheld] . I am ready for the challenge. ('The Icon' leaves the alley, the camera fades to black.)
