You never critique my rps before, so you won't now but... here's a roleplay nevertheless...

8 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[NOTES] This is, like, the worst rp I've ever done... But look at it anyway. NOW. [BEGIN TRANSMISSION] (Cameras open up to the narrow hallway of what looks to be a very expensive condo building. It zooms around as you hear an elevator door chime and then open. Footsteps descend upon the camera. A woman speaks, just simply saying
"This way!" She has a gentle voice. As the cameras zoom back even more, it shows someone carrying a few shopping bags -- quite a bit of shopping bags, actually. It gets a closer glimpse of the man being Bad-Coool and the woman guiding him as Ecstacy. Ecstacy opens a door and enters as Bad-Cool follows behind her. Ecstacy is wearing a purple satin skirt and a purple velvet blouse with white lace outline the sleeves and neckline. She also has on a pair of silver sandals and has her blonde hair scooped into a twist.
Bad-Cool is wearing a pair of pressed khaki pants and a nice dark blue sweater. He has about six packages... from Bloomingdale's, to Macy's,
Sach's Fifth Avenue to Gucci. Ecstacy flips on the lights to show a beautiful penthouse living room. She smiles and speaks...)
Ecstacy: Put them down on the couch. Easy, though... I've got clothes, very exspensive clothes in there.
(Bad-Cool does as he's told and sets the clothes down on the floor very easily. Bad-Cool then goes over and sits on a couch and is gasping for air)
Bad-Cool: Okay, could BC get his gold card back any time soon? (Ecstacy laughs and smiles and then goes and sits on a long blue sofa and crosses her legs and lays down. Her long legs are beautiful as she lays her head down. She kicks her shoes off and her little toes wiggle a bit)
Ecstacy: You can if you're a good boy... But fetch me a water, okay, dear? (Bad-Cool gets up and goes into the ketchen and pulls out a glass from a cabinet and turns on the tap water and waits for the water to get cold)
Ecstacy: Bottled water... I don't drink that nasty New York City water. (Bad-Cool nods and turns the water off. He then puts the glass back into the cabinet) Bad-Cool: Sorry.... BC forgot, yeah, you don't know what they put in this water. Heck, the whole town smells like urine.
(Ecstacy stretches out her whole body and lets out an Ahhhhh! sound as
Bad-Cool can see her in the front room -- she is very beautiful)
Ecstacy: Exactly, and I'm the best... which means I deserve the best, right, Bad-Cool? (Bad-Cool nods as Ecstacy gets up from the couch and joins BC in the kitchen. As she walks in, she can see BC nodding)
Bad-Cool: And nothing but the best is what I am going to get you. Now if only BC could get some more belts, then he'd have it made...
Ecstacy: You're with the great Ecstacy LeCain... they can't overlook someone like me; you'll get your shot.. BC, where's my water?
(Bad-Cool hands Ecstacy reaches into the fridge and reaches in to pull out some bottled water. BC hands the bottled water to Ecstacy. She uncaps it and takes a few sips)
Bad-Cool: Sorry about that, baby, let's hope BC gets it right next time... BC would do anything for you... you're just so great.. (Ecstacy nods and then lets out yet another yawn -- all the shopping she was doing wore her out. Bad-Cool in turn yawns as well)
Ecstacy: You've got that straight. I look around at all these other managers and people in the AWFL that all try to be like Ecstacy... who are they fooling? I have an attitude, I'm out to get what I want and I'm even called cocky... that's fine by me. No woman and let me say no woman in this sport ever has been or ever will be like me. There's only one, and
Bad-Cool, you've got her. (Bad-Cool's expression gets wide-eyed and he looks as if he's angry. Ecstacy takes another sip of her water while listening to BC) Bad-Cool: You cocky?!? Who dares to call you cocky? What you say should be considered modest to what you are truly capable of. Speaking of other managers, they seem to be dropping like flies, must be afraid to face this unstoppable duo of you and BC... Amanda is leaving with Rooster, for example... And Big Mac? Shiva? Azrael? Hmmm...
(Ecstacy looks at her water bottle and sees that it's half-way empty. Ecstacy is surprised that she's this thirsty -- especially for water, which she hardly cares for unless she's hot... but she is always hot...)
Ecstacy: As always, who can blame them? The others bailed out while they could, I will mention no other names... I will say, though, one was from my past and well... she just couldn't cook up the spotlight that I have gotten. It's all the way to the moon and back if I wanted, Bad-Cool...
We've had our ups and downs, but in theend, we have made a dynamic pair... better than that old bitch, Laura and you... (Bad-Cool nods and picks Ecstacy up over his shoulder. As he does this, she drops the water bottle on the floor and the remains of the water pours over the floor. Bad-Cool carries her into the front room and they start to make passionate love, standing up)
Bad-Cool: Damn, BC loves you! (Bad-Cool and Ecstacy move unto the sofa and start to make out. Soon,
Bad-Cool is on top of Laura as they continue making out. Mike, the camera guy, decides it's time to shut the camera off as we fade to black)
