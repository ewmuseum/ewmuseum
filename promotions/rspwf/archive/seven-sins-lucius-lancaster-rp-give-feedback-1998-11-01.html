"Seven Sins" Lucius Lancaster RP--give feedback

1 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a sample roleplay from one of my characters, "Seven Sins" Lucius Lancaster. Your feedback is appreciated. (1:00 A.M. October 31, 1998. The first hours of All Hallow's Eve have begun, and it is a time for the cold reign of the moon to envelope the earth, not in the warm embrace of a lover, but in the deceptive brace of a mercenary, for it is in the moment when the brace is most sincere where betrayal, a silent and malevolent stalker, lurks. The camera pans the night sky, dotted with tiny white stars, individualistic and independent of one another, not a mass swirl of galaxies and constellations. It is a night meant for thinking, when the stars are simple, steadfast, and as romantic as blood-stained daggers. The camera slowly pans level with the ground, and we see that we are on the stoop of a small building, location unknown. A man is hunched on the stoop, a man clad in simple black sackcloth, his face shadowed by a black hood. His head is rested on his right arm, which is in turn rested on his leg, and he seems to be staring at both heaven and earth, contemplating all that has become his existance: life, death, joy, sorrow, comfort, pain, trust, betrayal... an endless cycle of contradictions. Rested beside of the man is a dull orange pumpkin, into which is carved the face of a crow, glowing with a fearful malevolence. Slowly, the man speaks, and we recognize his voice: it is indeed the voice of "Seven Sins" Lucius Lancaster, unusually quiet and contained, yet full of myriads of emotions.)
Lancaster: He has returned....... He has brought me back to my former glory............... He has brought me my gold back. Who is He? The Almighty,
The One and Only, The Shepherd of Lost Souls................
However, his identity is no concern of yours at this time. For now, the main matter at hand is one Vincent Crow. (sigh) Vincent Crow. The man I offered the world. The man to whom I gave the riches and treasures of the world on a silver platter. I gave him treasures that he had only owned in his dreams: trust, purpose, belonging, friendship...... I gave him his soul. You dare say I poisoned you, Crow? I saved you from yourself! The demons of your world were closing in, and you know exactly who I am talking about. When you were trapped in a corner, and who swooped in to save you? When you were lost at sea, who pulled you into his boat? When you were about to fall to your death, who offered a hand to pull you up?
I did, Crow, and you damn well know it's true. I didn't turn your soul to nothingness... YOU did that yourself! What about the mysterious man who you followed before me? What about Jennifer? What about your losing the
World Title to Eric Aberle? It would seem to me that the only man who destroyed Vincent Crow was Vincent Crow. I pulled you together, Crow, I gave you a second chance..... and THIS is how you repay me? (shaking his head)
No..... no, this will not do at all. You took my name, and you took His name, and you disgraced them both. Crow, the World Title which I wear means nothing for my own glory; it is for Him. However, for you to win it, for you to take it back, to disgrace both He and the title once again is something I cannot allow. What I must do, I must do for His good and for the good of humanity.
I'm not a merciless man, Crow; I do feel compassion. So Crow, one final warning: you're not a dumb man; make the right choice..... this is your ultimatum....... come back to me.....
(Suddenly, a black shadow falls over the already-dimmed figure of Lancaster. Lancaster looks up at the figure, and we can at last see the face of Seven
Sins, and what we see is a mixture of fear and reverence. We hear the voice of the figure, of the man who Lancaster once called, "Master!".... whoever he may be.)
???: The traitor.... he must pay for the desicration of my name. Lancaster: But Master... perhaps he deserves a second chance. (The enigmatic figure hands Lancaster a large wooden sledge hammer. Lancaster eyes the hammer with a mixture of uncertainty and pure terror.)
???: No more second chances. The time for second chances are over. (Lancaster nods, then slowly stands up. The mysterious master of Lancaster still remains unidentified as Seven Sins rises to his feet, clutching the sledge hammer tightly and staring down at the crow jack-o'-lantern with a look of uncertainty. All of a sudden, his eyes narrow and fill with hate, pure contempt for the crow he sees. The rage the uWa has come to know Lucius
Lancaster for returns, and from the mystery figure's silhouette, we can see that he is nodding in approval.)
Lancaster: ....then let the beatings begin! (Lancaster swings back the hammer high over his head, and the camera cuts to slow motion as we zoom in closely on the crow-o'-lantern. The fire within it still burns, sending out an eerie glow throughout its surroundings. Then, from the top of the screen, the hammer of judgement, in slow motion, comes swinging down within the hands of vengeance. The hammer goes crashing through the crow-shaped pumpkin, shattering it into pieces and extinguishing the flame within. The camera simply zooms in on the wreckage, the crow crushed, the flame smothered.......... fade out.)
