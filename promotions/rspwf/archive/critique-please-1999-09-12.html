<Critique> Please........

12 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, [name withheld] again and I am asking someone to please look at this roleplay and give me their honest opinions. I will be honest in saying that is honestly my first ever roleplay! I have always been good at doing storylines but I got aALOT of help from an elite roleplayer everyone around here knows. He helped me set up everything and I just hope this roleplay lives up to the standards of all you guys out there. Thanks for your time.
[name withheld] /\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\/\ <The scene opens up in the forest just outside Grand Choulee, Washington. It is mid day at a small camp ground. The camera is locked on a small campsite.
There is a fire going with it s nice size. A tent is set up and there is a chair between the tent and fire. A cooler sits by the chair and on it is a beer which looks cold due the to condensation dripping off the sides. By the chair a small pile of wood and a tree with a large ax leaning up against it.
The camera turn back to the tent as the zipper can be heard. The canvas opening opens and ECWF's new prospect, "Hardrock" Bruce Spencer walks out.>
<"Hardrock" is wearing a pair of blue jeans with a pair of brown work boots and he has a plaid shirt in his hand.>
<Bruce steps out with the shirt in his hand.> "HARDROCK" BRUCE SPENCER<monotone voice> - Don't you people ever let someone when you are coming or do you love to get your kicks from sneaking up on people and showing up unexpectly? Normally, since my time in the woods is my personal time, I would get up and show you why I hate to have unexpect guest but today
I'm not really in the mood so just stand there and listen to what I have to say. <Bruce put on the plaid over shirt and buttons it only to below his rib cage.> "HARDROCK" BRUCE SPENCER<monotone voice> - This Saturday night, I debute in the pro circuit and I have to say that I am very anxious to begin. I've spent the last few years working in small indy feds and I have finally moved up and I have to prove myself. I came out here to clear my head and prepare for the task which lies ahead of me.
<Bruce walks over and sit on the chair next to the fire.> "HARDROCK" BRUCE SPENCER<monotone voice> - I see my match has been finally anounced and I have to admit that I am pleased. I don't get pleased often but this makes me very happy because with a win in this match I will show everyone
I am serious and that I am the real deal. I face Eric Emerson in my debute. Emerson, I've heard alot about you, you infamous in the wrestling world. I've seen you in action and you impressive and I am sure I will have one hell of a fight ahead of me.
<Bruce begins to roll up his sleeves.> "HARDROCK" BRUCE SPENCER<monotone voice> - But everytime you step into the ring things and conditions are different which makes the outcome different. In the ring, there are a million different possibilities and you have to prepare for each and everyone of them. I study who I face, I watch then, I find out what they are strong at and what their weaknesses are. I anticipate all cicumstances and that is what I will have over you.
<Bruce takes a long sip of his beer then begin to poke at the fire with a stick.> "HARDROCK" BRUCE SPENCER<monotone voice> - Mr. Emerson, out here in the forest I am a calm, docile and right now is an example of this, but don't be fooled by the calm shell because in the ring is my Mr. Hyde. Out here I stop and listen to the forest and it makes me feel one with nature and that brings me to inner peace, not to sound like a corny movie with shaolin monks. But when I am in the ring I have one agenda, and that is to be the man that walks out of the ring with the win on my belt.
<Bruce gets up and walks over and throws a small log on the fire.> "HARDROCK" BRUCE SPENCER<monotone voice> - Time to cut some more wood. <Bruce grabs his ax and walks over to a tree.> "HARDROCK" BRUCE SPENCER<monotone voice> - I am mentally and physically ready for not only what you have to offer but what you have that I'm not aware off.
You are looking at determination in the flesh and I have a good feeling about this Saturday and that is something I don't have that much. Emerson, I want you to stop, take a look at the man that stands before you, and think, think hard, and ponder what exactly you have gotten yourself into because no matter how great your reputation may be, reputations don't win matches. Saturday night draws closer and closer with each passing second and soon you will be caught in a web of self deception you can't escape and I will walk back to the woods the ECWF Television champion.
<Bruce begins to wildly hack at the tree with keen precision using the ax as the cameraman backs up and the screen fades to black.>
