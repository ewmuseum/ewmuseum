Hey People, It's me again, posting me latest Eddie 'Machismo' Lopez flash...check it out and give me a reply to tell me how I am going with this guy if you will, I am in the process of getting used to handling this guy still, so any remarks good or bad will be just fine for me! So please read!
Thankyou! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [The scene opens up in a park, it is full of trees and places for people to enjoy...Eddie sits on a bench looking around as suddently a man dressed in a trenchcoat comes up to him and sits beside him, the man then hands Eddie some money as he walks of quickly and Eddie quickly puts the money away...the camera then zooms up on Eddie who looks shocked as to see the camera filiming..]
Eddie 'Machismo' Lopez: "Oh...what are you doing here...[In state of
Paniac..] Oh, you think that was something going on with that guy in the trench coat...[Really paniacing now..] Well, he is my......[Confused as to what to say..] Uncle!! That's who he is, Yeah my Uncle...[Looking up at the sun..] Sun is really getting to me I think...but yeah, it was my uncle and he owed me some money from when I was five, a long time ago...but yeah he owed me it! [Relief as he gets himself out of trouble..] Now enought about my Uncle, he is a old man and needs to be rested....let's talk about NCWA Japan because I know that is what you want to hear!"
[Eddie puts his hand on his head and get's rid of some sweat on his forehead from the paniac as he continues to talk..]
Eddie 'Machismo' Lopez: "Now let me begin with Nieba, the man who I beat last Saturday! [Grins..] I heard what you had to say Nieba...You still want a piece of me Nieba? I thought I taught you a good lesson the first time around, but I can make sure to teach you a second lesson...that is if you really want one? A win is a win and a loss is a lost...I won! You
Lost! [CandyMan smirks..] But then again, you always _Lose_ and _I_ always _Win_!..I see why you are so angry, _Jealousy_ is a curse...he don't worry Nieba, you where beaten by the _Better_ man...[Laughs..] But come to think about it _Everyone_ is better then You!..
Hey you can keep on talking about your myths, legends, fantasies, dillusions or whatever the heck you was going on about....because
_Noone_ is listening!!" [Eddie enjoys making fun of Nieba, as he then proceeds to talk about someone else..] Eddie 'Machismo' Lopez: "Now Dogmeat, I heard what you had to say the other day....you seem to be having a hard time making the decision as to who you will face in the next round of the tournament...well I got the solution! _Noone_!!! [Smirks...] Because you ain't even going to make it there! You see if you had any sense about even thinking of who you would face in the next wrong.._IF_ you made it, the obvious answer would be..[points to himself..] _Me_!!..
DogMeat, we are goin to play a little game...and it's called _Burry The
Bone_, because you see...your like a piece of DogMeat and I am the
_Pitbull_..[Eddie smirks as he continues..] This is the way things are going to get done...Feed the dog, Eat the Meat, Chew on the Bone, Dig a
Hole, Burry the Bone...and then...[Eddie goes to unbutton his fly..]
_Piss_ on it!!!!" [Eddie laughs to himself as he nearly falls of the bench laughing at what he just said about DogMeat, he then controls himself as he continues to talk..]
Eddie 'Machismo' Lopez: "Moving along...to the man who stroke fear into the hearts of many people during his reign as the _Ripper_...Jack the
Ripper..[Eddie scratches his head for a second..] Oh wrong guy, the guy we are talking about...is the man that everyone _Laughs_ at, not are scared of...What might have came over me just then! [Eddie smirks..]
Jack Johnson...you call me the man tha is wasting everyone's time, ya know ya right!!.. I am doing exactly what you said, wasting _Everybody's_ time...You see! Everyone knows who is the greatest, everyone knows who is going to win the title, everyone knows who the man who is going to beat them silly everytime..[Eddie points to himself..] Everyone knows that man is _Me_!
So I prove your point, I am the man wasting everyones time..because there is no way in hell _Anybody_ is going to get past me...
I knew you was a fan Ripper...but to even promote me in your interview's, you are great! [Eddie laughs..] Your your matches are boring, because we know what is going to happen...[Smirks..] You get a beating and then your lover and partner in crime helps you get the win!
You know those things..._Get Old_ kind of like _You_!!! You see I could come out and tell the world how much you suck and how much of a waste of time you are...but you told me to tell the world something we don't know so I guess..[Grins..] No can do there!"
[Eddie grins to the camera as he finishes up saying bad things about
Ripper, and moves onto a different subject..]
Eddie 'Machismo' Lopez: "Now there is one man, who things he is the man who strikes fear into the people in NCWA Japan, that man is Steel!
[Eddie looks with anger into the camera..] You called me second best to you? Who ever said you where the best!! I don't see your name in big writing on any wall claiming that you are the best! [Taps his head..]
Fragment of your imagination...as I see it!.. I am disrespectful? Disguestd by my culture that I can't even recall the city of whereabouts I was born in? [Eddie looks confused..] Either your a bit _Dumb_ in the head, or all those hard hits you have taken during your life have finally caught up to you! How could I not know where I was born you Fool!! I was born and raised in the capital of
Mexico..._Mexico City_! Does it look like I don't know where I am from!!! _No_!!!!... I call myself Machismo for no evident reason? [Eddie looks angered..] It's about the past! It's about what I am made of! It's about the man I am! Look at yourself! You name yourself after a earth resource...Let me guess it's cause you are as hard as Steel? Or is it becaue your as thick as Steel in the head! [Smirks..] Steel I have said it once and I will say it again....[Grins..] Stell is useful for a period of time, but in the end you will do what all steel does...
...RUST!!!!!" [Eddie points his finger into the camera as if to say Steel you are next. He then looks around at the park, as people continue to walk past and he goes on with his words..]
Eddie 'Machismo' Lopez: "Finally I come to my opponent for this Saturday, the man who will be comming in the second round a winner and leaving it a _Loser_! [Grins..] Chane Steel who did you beat to make it into the second round? Ronin?? Need I say more...I could beat this guy with my eyes closed, both hands tied behind my back and my leg's tied together!!! [Smiles..] But that would be way to _Easy_!!..
The other day you stated that you would put on a show better then the
Pamela/ Tommy video!! [Eddie laughs at this comment..] If you call that video entertaining, then you really need some help....cause if I was in
Tommy's situation, I would work with Pamela much better and smother..and she would be calling _Machismo_ back for _More_ everyday...So you go on and put a better show then that video, because it really wasn't anything special...but I guess you _Ain't_ anything special either..
Chane Steel by the end of the night, you will be siting in your hospital bed trying to remember one word which has slipped out of your mind because of the beating you took at my hands....That word, is simple...that word is Me...that word is..
....._MACHISMO_!!!!!!!"
