<RP> "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie

23 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Give some words of hatred, encouragement, or overall uncaring please! It'd be greatly appreciated. But first, a little background. Blottie is a rich man, who competes in wrestling for the glory. He's only 23 years old, and is considered the "next big thing" in the NACW. This is following a loss Blottie suffered in a Triple Threat match, in which he, The Flying Scotsman, and "The
Scottish Monster" Shaun McWhirter battled. The Scotsman won when Blottie was knocked out by "Lobo" Jeremy Rhodes, and The Scotsman made McWhirter submit to the Scottish Breaker. Scotsman thinks Blottie is his friend, but Blottie has been using him up to this point. This is a major turning point for my character, as this will define what he's like in the monthes to come. And now... onto the RP
__________________________________________________________________ *Scene opens backstage, in the locker room of "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie. Blottie sits, rubbing the back of his neck, with a towel around his waist. His hair is wet, and it is apparent that either he just got out of the shower or he REALLY likes the Val Venis look. Blottie looks up at the camera and smiles*
Blottie: Come in, come in. Gloat in the fact that I didn't win. Take it ALL in. "The True Living Legend" in his moment of defeat... but wait a minute. I was never pinned. So you CAN'T gloat at my defeat. All you can do is say I didn't win. And even legends can't win everyday.
*Blottie stands up, walks over to his locker, and drops the towel. A blur comes up over his buttocks, as he grabs a pair of underwear, and then a pair of black jeans. He puts them on, then sits back down, shirtless*
Blottie: Congratulations, Flying Scotsman. You got the big win. But remember that you never beat me. You never pinned me in the middle of that ring. And in the meantime, your other "friend" decked me upside the head with a chair, collectively taking me out of the match at the key moment when you put the
Scottish Breaker on Shaun McWhirter. And now comes the true moment of confusion. The cherry on top of the cake. McWhirter gets a WORLD title shot for his defeat. And I get a non-title match with "Lobo" Jeremy Rhodes. And this is a just decision in what way?
*Blottie shakes his head, stands up, and begins to pace. As he speaks, the pacing increases in speed* Blottie: But I'm not going to complain. I'm not going to question Shawn Coat's decision. Instead I'm going to lay it down like it is. This Sunday, at Sunday
Night Slam Masters, I'm going to take on, and take apart, the NACW United
States Champion. But I'm not just going to beat Jeremy Rhodes. Oh no. I'm going to make him realize, once and for all, who the TRUE living legend is.
I'm going to make him WISH he was in a Crimson Rain match. There is no word in the English dictionary that describes the pain I will put him through. And in the end, I hope to God, I hope to Jesus Christ himself, that Jeremy Rhodes realizes just what I am... his "friend".
*Blottie stops pacing and turns to the camera. A wild look is in his eyes, a look never before seen from Blottie. It is a look of pure glee and pure hatred all rolled up into one. It is a look of insanity that Wayne, Rhodes, and The
Scotsman COMBINED could never emulate. He looks downright commitable. And the smile on his face doesn't make him look any saner*
Blottie: Oh yes, we WILL be "friends". The bestest friends there ever has been. Hell, we're gonna be like brothers. BLOOD brothers. We're gonna let the blood flow like it never has before. Just me and you, Rhodes. Beating on each other until one of us stops moving. And then, one of us is going to win.
And that man... well, he won't look like a winner. He won't feel like a winner. But he will have the win over the other. And he will be able to take that win... into the match I'm proposing now.
*Blottie's smile, as impossible as it may seem, gets wider. The look in his eyes gets wilder. And what little semblence of sanity remained in the face of
Blottie is gone*
Blottie: Three way dance. No count-outs. No disqualification. No rules. Just me and my "friends", Jeremy Rhodes and The Flying Scotsman. Getting down and dirty... and bloody. I will prove to the both of you that I am a legend.
I'm gonna prove to EVERYONE that I'm a legend. I'm gonna prove it to you, daddy. So long ago, you told me that I could be a legend... if only I applied myself. If I wasn't such a wuss. If I wasn't such a little boy. I'm not a little boy anymore. I have everything... except you. I hate you... and I love you... and I... I...
*Blottie breaks down, weeping outloud. Tears stream down his cheeks, he sobs uncontrollably. And inbetween the sobs, he keeps talking* Blottie: I... wish you were here... to see... me... succeed. To see... me... make something of myself. To have... your... love. Why didn't you... ever... love me? Why?! WHY!?!?!? *Blottie puts his head in his hands, and sobs. He gets up, walks over to his locker, and leans his head against it. He suddenly begins to slam his head into the locker, over and over*
Blottie: THE PAIN... IS THE... ONLY WAY... OUT!!!! YOU NEVER... LOVED ME!!!! ALL... I EVER... WANTED... WAS YOU TO... LOVE ME!!!! THE PAIN... THE PAIN YOU MADE... ME FEEL!!!!! I DON'T UNDERSTAND... I... *Blottie collapses, his back against the locker. The locker is dented where he just got through slamming his head, and blood is trickling down his forehead. He looks up at the camera, tears and blood mixing as they both pour down his face* Blottie: I don't understand. Hopefully... I can find love with my new "friends". I never found it... with you. I have to move on. That's... what mom told me. When... I was... younger. But now she's gone... too. And you...
won't get out... of my head. Your voice... lives on... through me. I'm going to make you proud. This Sunday... I'll make you... proud. Rhodes... I want to be... friends. I want you... to love me... like you love... The Scotsman. I want the Scotsman... to love me... like he loves you. All I want is love...
and your blood. On my hands. *Blottie smiles, though blood and tears still stream down his cheeks* Blottie: I will beat you... this Sunday. We'll be... Blood Brothers. Me, you, and The Scotsman. We'll love... each other... through the hate. And I'll still be... "The True... Living...
*Blottie sobs and laughs at the same time, the sanity slipping back into his voice* Blottie: Legend". Until next time... *Blottie weeps loudly, as the camera backs up. Blottie cries loudly into his hands, and the camera, finally, fades to black* -------------- "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie Member of the NACW... soon to prove the name isn't just a gimmick... just ask
Sir Smokes A Lot, Jack Daniels, and the Rookie Rumble
Thinks everyone should worship him... Except Ish... 8)
