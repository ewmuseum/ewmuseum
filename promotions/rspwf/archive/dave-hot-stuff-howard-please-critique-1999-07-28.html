Dave "Hot Stuff" Howard please CRITIQUE

28 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene] The scene opens up in the locker room of the All Star Wrestling Federation. Kelly Price is searching for Dave Howards locker room. See seems to find it. She knocks on the door one time. No one answers. Then she knocks again. A blond woman in a green rood on gets the door. Kelly Price looking mad at the girl because she wants to be her says where's Mr. Howard? All of a sudden Kelly
Price sees David walking out of the back room with nothing on but some blue jeans. Her mouth is wide open. She begins to talk as Dave tells the girl to leave.
Kelly Price: Mr. Howard the All Star Wrestling Federation sent me down here to interview you. Can I interview you?
Dave "Hot Stuff" Howard: Go ahead baby. Kelly Price: Ok now...what are your goals for the All Star Wrestling Federation? I'm sure someone with your athletic ability and the way you look...I'm sure you'll be going places in the All Star Wrestling Federation, but what do you want to be known for?
Dave "Hot Stuff" Howard: Well Kelly...I plan on tearing through the so called
"competition" in the All Star Wrestling Federation and rise straight to the top. Now, I've climbed the ladder more than once, and to tell you the truth...when your as sexy and as good of a wrestler as I am...it isn't that hard. I'll probably be reigning supreme in this fed in...hmm, about two weeks or so. I mean, who's going to stop me? Glory?? Hahaha. He only wishes he looks at good as me. I'll be on top of this biz, it may take some work, but hey...when you look as good as I do, it won't be that hard. Matter a fact you can call me Dave "Hot Stuff" Howard because I am just too hot for the woman.
Kelly Price: Ok Dave..Now the world knows that the All Star Wrestling Federation or better known as the ASWF is the wrestling federation to be right now. But do you have a manger or anything, some wrestlers think a manger is the only way to win.
Dave "Hot Stuff" Howard: Well Kelly...To tell you the truth I don't need a manger to win. I mean I have the talent..And I have the looks to win. I don't need a tagalong to just slow me down. If I needed a manger it would be a female because me and males just don't mix. Who knows maybe you will be my manger some day. Anything can happen when your with the sexiest wrestler in the world.
Mangers only get in the way. Kelly Price: Hmmmm..We shall see what happens. But Dave what do you think of the talent the ASWF has. It looks to have a lot of talent in my eyes. I mean not as much as the IWF did but its way up there.
Dave "Hot Stuff" Howard: Well there is a lot of great talent here in the All
Star Wrestling Federation, but rightfully I think I could take any one of them on. Well theirs a few people who can't cut it here in the All Star Wrestling
Federation. But really....There is no one in the world who can beat me. I mean
Kelly look at me. {Pauses for a second while Kelly takes a look at Dave} I am the next All Star Wrestling Federation Crusierweight Champion with out a second thought in my mind. I can very well compete with this competition and if any of these wrestlers thinks that they can beat me, well im not backing down, so bring it on because we both know who will win and that will be me. I mean I could take out half of the All Star Wrestling Federation roster right now. I mean look at the roster. About the only people I can see that have talent are me,James Paxton and maybe James Kingsly.
Kelly Price: Well Dave "Hot Stuff" Howard you sure do look good. It seems you are very touchy on the fact that your the best in the All Star Wrestling
Federation, I wonder why? But we'll find that out on a later day. Now lately you have been talking about wanting that World. I mean some of All Star
Wrestling Federation's top athletes are also gunning for this prestigeus title, how are you going to keep up?
Dave "Hot Stuff" Howard: What do you mean. I am one of the best wrestlers in the All Star Wrestling Federation, I already said I could probably take out half of the current roster. If you don't think so bring anyone out right now and I'll personally show you a *** kicking or something eles if you want me to show you that. But since its not good TV to attack someone during an interview
I will do it this Sunday if i really want to or when ever they have a houseshow. You see I could take out anyone in this fed but since Im a new comer im stuck in the Mid-Card status FOR NOW. I mean I don't have the Main Event spot. But believe me when the pay per view comes I will be in the main event.
When I reach that Main Event level theres no stopping me from finally following my dream, which is to become World Champion, Which (Ha Ha) I will. I think that is everyone if its in Baseball or even Tennis they all want to me the Champion and I am no different. Maybe the only thing different from Baseball,Tennis and
Wrestling is that my opponent never gets up and if they do its in a hospital bed the next mourning with a broken neck or back. Haha..... Watch my next match-up and you will see someone walk out with a broken bone and I can garutee you it will not be me.
[Scene] Kelly gives Dave a notes and he responds with a sure baby. He askes now? She responds with a yes. Then you see Dave Howard exiting the camera man from the room. As the camera man is left on the outside you can hear screaming coming from Howard's room. The scene then fades away with the Words The sexiest man in
Wrestling is here to stay
