[Free Agent/RP] The NightCrawler

27 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've had a little taste of life in an efed, (W4), and enjoyed it. Following the W4's demise I took a break, originally intending to not bother ewrestling again. However I've now changed my mind and so I'm looking for a contract.
Below is a RP I did for W4, (a triple threat match). I'd prefer to work an
RPing efed, but I'm open to all offers and also any general comments on the RP. Cheers, Mark. Handler of: The NightCrawler - WWWW [3-1-0] ------------------------------------------- (As The NightCrawler’s music plays he stands in the middle of the ring, dripping with sweat and a little blood, looking down at the lifeless bodies of Gangrel and Benoit. He looks up to the audience before smiling and raising his fist in victory. Da Rassler’ enters the ring with a mic and approaches The NightCrawler.)
DR: “What an incredible match! You must be happy with that victory NightCrawler…” (The NightCrawler grabs the mic off him.) TNC: “You know… I said it on the last Monday Madness and I've said it all along… this is where the legend that is The NightCrawler begins. It doesn’t matter whether its one-on-one, two-on-one or twenty-on-one… I will prevail and I have proven that here tonight.”
(He looks down once more at Gangrel and Benoit who are now stirring slightly. With another smile he looks back up.)
TNC: “In fact if any one else back there wants to go at it, they should drag their flea bitten carcasses down that aisle and we’ll go at it right now.”
(The crowd cheers madly as The NightCrawler turns to the aisle and beckons for them to come. After waiting a few moments no-one appears. The
NightCrawler throws his head back and laughs.) TNC : “As I thought… they’ve seen the power of The NightCrawler and no-one wants any part of me… no-one wants to feel The Painkiller dulling their senses. Well… at the end of the next Monday Madness, I’ll stand here in the middle of the ring… victorious once more, holding a lot more than just a microphone. And that…”
(Again he throws his head back and laughs before scowling right into the camera.) “I promise you!” (He throws the mic back to Da Rassler.) DR: “Ladies and gentlemen… The NightCrawler!” (Again he raises his fist in victory, turning slowly on the spot taking in his adulation. Gangrel and Benoit are now on their hands and knees trying to shake the cobwebs free. Smiling, The NightCrawler grabs Gangrel by the hair, getting him up to his feet. Keeping one handful of hair, he grabs the seat of his pants and throws Gangrel over the top rope. There are huge cheers from the crowd. He turns to see Benoit stagger towards him, attempting to throw a punch. The NightCrawler steps aside it and uses
Benoit’s momentum to throw him over the top rope too, landing on Gangrel.
Again there are huge cheers from the crowd. The NightCrawler just smiles and slides out of the ring under the bottom rope and makes his way back up the aisle whilst his music plays.)
[The Painkiller - a Power Bomb from the middle turnbuckle.]
