Critique me...."The Dream" Rick Taylor

27 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Gentlemen, and ladies, since it appears that the next obvious step in my career is to step up and join an email fed, I have decided to put out one of my roleplays for you to criticize (well I no that I'm not perfect, yet). So please take a look and critique me, tell me what you think, and if I should pursue a career in an email fed. So buckle up and prepare for a ride cause here he comes, the man we all love, "The
Dream" Rick Taylor!!!!!
[Rick Taylor is being rolled out of the hospital in a wheel chair. He neck brace on and from his slow movements and the looks of pain in his eyes, the fall that he took off of the top of the second cage last night was an extremely hard fall. The orderly rolls The Dream over to a beat up rust-colored Volkswagon Beetle where The Dream's long-time friend and wrestling reporter Jimmy Irons is waiting with the door open. The orderly approaches Jimmy with a set of specific directions for taking care of The Dream over the next few days]
Orderly: Ok this man will need a lot of rest, his spinal cord is bruised and he is suffering from whiplash. From what I hear he is very lucky to still be alive, but this man should not wrestle for two weeks, not with his back in that condition.
Jimmy: [looking at the orderly in disdain] But what about training, when can he return to training?
Orderly: [looking over his papers] Says here no strenuous activities for two weeks. [The Dream looks up at the orderly as if he was suddenly awakened. The pain has melted from his face and it is now filled with anger]
Dream: [with anger in his voice] You mean to tell me that The Dream can't get his revenge on Sebastian Caine for kicking me off the top of the cage for two weeks??? That S.O.B. is gonna get it before then, nothing will stop me from ripping him a new a**-Hole!!!
[The orderly looks down at The Dream and smiles, obviously trying to hide his frustration] Orderly: look, I am a fan of RedneX, and I want to see you get your revenge, but I also want to see you wrestle for long time. This is why you gotta take it easy and give yourself time to heal. Everyone is rooting for ya, we want you to make it, but we all want you to last a long time and not become a flash in the pan.
Dream: [looking up at the orderly] Ok, I'll do what it takes to get back to one hundred percent before I step in the ring, I want the best therapist and trainers available. With your help, I will return to the ring and regain that world title.
Orderly: [smiling a real smile now] Now that is what we all want to hear. Before long you will be back on top of the world. But now you really should go home, sleep in your own bed, and eat some real food.
We'll send our best therapist in the morning. Dream: Ok now help me get into the car!! [Jimmy and the orderly reach around The Dream and help pull him up off the wheelchair. Both men support him as they all walk up to the passenger door of the Volkswagon. The Dream is lowered into his seat as
Jimmy sets The Dream's legs in the car. Dream leans back and rolls his head towards the door looking out at the sun and then glancing back at the orderly]
Dream: If I aint better in two weeks, I'll be back, and the treatment won't involve pills or therapists, got it?
Orderly: [with a look of serious fear] Yes sir, we will get you back in top form, maybe better.
[Jimmy climbs in the driver's side and closes his door, the orderly approaches the passenger door and begins to close it]
Dream: One more thing. Orderly: What's that? Dream: I want to hear you say it!!! [The orderley begins to smile and he straightens himself up, then he bends down and in his best "The Dream" imitation growls out the catch phrase]
Orderly: Reality is Just a Dream!!!!!! [Dream smiles as the orderly closes the door] Dream: Music to my ears, thank you. [Jimmy starts the engine and both men drive off as the orderly rolls the wheelchair back inside the hospital]
