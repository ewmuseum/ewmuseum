Free Agent-Darun (inc. sample rp)

15 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, Free agent here looking for a NEWLY started fed. I've also included a sample rp if that helps. I would also greatly appreciate feedback on it as im relatively new.
Gget in touch w/ me at [detail withheld] ***Trenton, NJ. The camera fades in from black to an empty park. It is evening time, around 8:30 PM and a slide and seesaw can clearly be seen several feet away from the camera as a mild, rusty-like swinging sound can be lightly heard. The camera pans round in a semi-circle where a set of swings can be seen, a figure sitting on one of them, gently swaying himself to and fro. The camera moves several feet closer until an almost clear shot can be seen helped slightly by the moonlight shining down onto the left side of the figure's body. He sits, head facing down with a hood cover, wearing a black jean jacket, torn blue jeans and black, army style boots. While continuing to swing himself slightly with the toes of his feet, he begins to talk.***
Darun: (snarls) I remember these days (looking around at the play-set surroundings). The days that were so few and far between. It's a shame, it really is. I had a lot of fun here with my brother, Jamie he was called. I remember how we used to seesaw together, how we used to race down the slide and how we always knew that it might have been our only chance to escape the devil's clutches. We knew what awaited us when we got home. The bullying, the abuse, the ridicule, the intimidation. We were always nice kids, but where did being nice ever get anybody?
***Darun digs his heels into the dirt to stop the swaying. He then stands and rests against the swing's inside support. He looks into the camera.***
Darun: You see, the point is, no matter how hard you try, no matter how much blood, sweat and tears you have to shed, your thanks for working hard and being nice to people is a swift kick in the balls. So what happens next?
Well it's like the story of the tenants in Darun's yard. ***As Darun walks forward, the camera moves backward in motion with Darun's movements*** Darun: Darun was a nice, friendly kid who always believed in loving, caring and peace. So one day he decided to let his 'friend' borrow one of his toys, but the next day, when the friend came to give the toy back, Darun noticed that it was slightly broken. However, because Darun was a friendly boy, he forgave his 'friend'. Next, another 'friend' wanted to borrow one of Darun's video games. Darun was more than happy to oblige but when it came to his
'friend' giving the videogame back, it no longer worked. However, because
Darun was a friendly boy, he forgave his 'friend'. Then, the next day, yet another 'friend' wanted to borrow one of Darun's books but when it came to giving the book back, the front cover was messy, pages were drawn and scribbled on and were ripped out. So finally Darun had enough. He went round to each of his 'friends' houses and asked to borrow their toys. But when they gave them to Darun, he immediately took them home and threw them on the damn fire.
***Darun stops' walking as does the cameraman. He once again looks directly at the camera*** Darun: I can hear the words on the tip of your tongues now: "What the hell is my point?" My point is that every dog has his day and God knows I've been treated like a bitch. My 'action plan', if you will, is to prove that I will no longer be anybody's bitch, slave, and victim, call me whatever you want.
Just don't underestimate me. I'm not here for the money, the gold. I'm here to prove my point. The point that two wrongs CAN make a right and God knows that I'm here to achieve a few wrongs. I don't care how many bones I have to break; I couldn't care less how many pints of blood I have to spill. You people, being the insensitive pricks that you are, don't have to appreciate my hard work. But to me, if I can terrorize your mind, your body and your soul, I would have achieved more than anything you ever could, and believe me, I will. Oh and by the way, just for the record, I'm making this personal, not business.
***Darun smears at the camera as it fades to black***
