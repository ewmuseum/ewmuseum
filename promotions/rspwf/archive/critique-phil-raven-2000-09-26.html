(Critique) Phil Raven

Good RPs Brian and Phil. What do you think about my roleplay? (Note: at the time, this was for a PPV match between Mojo and Hamilton fighting Mark Rock and Jason Sensation and Mojo and Hamilton were bitter enemies) ------------ {{ The scene is outside on the island, where it is very dark. Faint bongo drum sounds can be heard in the background. A big fire is burning, and there are torches hanging from the trees. Woodchips cover the ground. Lord Mojo of the Quaganites walks into the scene. He is wearing a shark tooth necklace, and torn up track pants. The camera looks into the fire, and through the fire we see shots of Mojo hitting the Cerebral Boar and attacking Nick Hamilton. The camera focuses back on Mojo, who is now sitting in front of the fire on a fallen tree log. He speaks. }} I have returned after another short absence. And what I have done lots is I have been thinking "How can I brutally attack someone at MCW Uprising?". I pondered that for a while and now the card has been released. While I am happy with my match, I can tell you I'm not impressed with the choice of partner: Nick Hamilton. First of my opponents on the list is this Rock guy, who hasn't dared to even show his face in the MCW as he is obviously not able to take on the task that awaits him. And only annihlation awaits him. I cannot say too much about him, as he hasn't even appeared, but I can say that when I go into the ring, you will not know the boundaries of how much I can snap, of how far off the edge I will jump... yes, Rock, the end is coming for you. And now for my second opponent. One who calls himself "Jason Sensation". Well Jason, I do not see why you think you are so spectacular. All I have to say is this: enjoy NOT being savagely beaten while you can, because when MCW Uprising hits the Pay-Per-View channel, when it hits the movie theatres... you will say "Hey, I'm Jason Sensation! Who needs to care about Lord Mojo?"... but when we step in that ring, I will give you a look of rage, a look of fear and a look of evil. And looks alone will make you shake and want to throw the match, because looks are a minimal version of what action I can do in the ring. It is your time to enter the Hall of the Damned, where you will be sentenced to life in hell right beside the others who have felt my evil wrath. But I'm not all a crazy savage living on a near-deserted island with my people: I believe in setting realistic goals just like you stupid mainland people do. And I've set goals such as no opponent will leave the ring without scars, stitches and one whole lot of blood loss. I should be able to fill two 4 litre milk jugs with the blood and guts you will see flying around the ring. And then, there is my other opponent, and that is Nick Hamilton. Nick, you may be my partner, but I refuse to fight with you. You can attempt to be a "good sport" all you want, I will toss your sportsmanship in the trash can because I do not want you as a partner and I will not take you as a tag team partner. I will attack you throughout the match, and I will enjoy beating you just as I will beat Rock and Sensation. Hey, wait, how about we settle this then, Nick? You and me, in a match right after the Rock/Sensation .vs. MOJO without Hamilton match, not sanctioned, just an all out brawl. We shall see who is the real lightning. The only lightning strike you'll ever be able to pull off on me is when you pick up a sign and go on strike due to the weather. AND THAT'S IT! And Hamilton, I will not tag you, I will punch you. Go in after me, I could not care less. You will join the Hall of the Damned as Rock and Sensation will. Now you can make up questions asking why won't I fight with you. "Does he not like my shirt? Am I a stupid jerk? Do I have cooties?" I could not care what formula you attempt to make, you had betrayed the Quaganites and now Hamilton, it's time for you to rot in hell and for all three of you, it's time I outwit, outlast and outfight you three because I AM THE ALMIGHTY LEADER OF THE QUAGANITES AND EACH AND EVERY ONE OF YOU CAN PREPARE TO BE QUAGANIZED... TO THE EXTREME!!! {{ He pauses. }} OK, I'm done now. Get the hell off my island or see this torch? See how it's lit on fire? It'll be shoved up your ass in 20 seconds if you don't get out. {{ The camera is seen running away from Mojo, then the cameraman hops onto a ship and goes back toward the mainland as the camera goes black. }} -- Jordan ================================ Proud Handler of: Lord Mojo of the Quaganites (MCW) --------------------------------------------------------- - Maple Championship Wrestling - First-Ever MCW Canadian Champion - Almighty Leader of the Quaganites - The savage warrior... the SURVIVOR... - All-Time Record: Unknown Scott Samson (MCW) ---------------------------------- - Maple Championship Wrestling - Just making his debut in e-wrestling - Great Wrestler, Even Better Soccer Player - All-Time Record: 0-0-0 Nick "Lightning" Hamilton (MCW) ----------------------------------------------------- - Retired, formerly of MCW - MCW InterProvincial Champion - All-Time Record: Unknown Team eXtreme (MCW) ----------------------------------- - Tag Team, Retired, formerly of MCW - All-Time Record: Unknown - 2 Time MCW World Tag Champs Mo and Carnage (PWF) --------------------------------- - PWF Hardcore Champ (Mo) - PWF Hollywood Champ (Mo) - Leaders of the "EskiMOs" - Retired  "[name withheld]" <[detail withheld]> wrote in message news:[detail withheld]... > [The scene opens with pure blackness, nothing in sight.
