You don't even understand [Imagination sequence 1 ] : Picture yourself alone in a dark room around 2 o clock in the morning..Just walking threw the door from a movie.. Candles are lit and the only thing on your mind is treating your lady like she is a queen by giving her all she need's.There is soft music in the background. The kind you make love to....Just then a door creaps open and there stands your beautiful brown haired lady in the sexiest outfit you have ever seen in your life...She struts over to you slowly....
Ok enough of that....let's focus on some reality...None of you reading this have that happen.Cause you can't have girls over this late at night yet. Cept for maybe you Rattlesnake.
[Imagination sequence 2 ] : Think of This situation for me...A man who is great...He could be great at anything...Ranging from Baseball, to boxing, to race car driving ,to sex.....He was one of the best at his time. He then takes a break.The man decides he is good, but still huiman.He comes back and people think he is "washed up". As you imagine this...Think of the effect this has on a normal persons self esteem....What would you do to rebuddle ? What could you do ?...As you imagine that, try to put yourself in the shoes of the
Outlaw....His reality fits this imagination sequence to a "T". What will the
Outlaw do to prove those eyes who decide to turn from the truth wrong ? I don't even think he knows.

1 [ Scene ] The scene fades into a view of a bath tub...The tub is filled with
_WATER_.....Just then a man comes into the scene with a towel wrapped around his waste. The man reaches into the water and pulls the plug on it....The water all slowly drains out.Once all the water is gone the camera view freezes and a voice is heard with the picture still being seen
[Voice ] : " Soon it shall happen to you....All water can be put Out. There is always a flame out there somewhere to strong for the water "
[ Scene ] : Just then the scene fades to black.....Nothing is seen or heard....Then you hear two gun shots from a shotgun of some kind...Still nothing being seen....Then there is a laugh...A sick one like you hear in the movies..The camera then fades back into color...You see Outlaw standing in a shallow stream laughing...Outlaw is not wearing a shirt, has his normal gold necklace, a shotgun, some baggy millitary pants....and his feet are in the water so we don't know if he is wearing his normal combat boots....Outlaw looks over to the camera and smirks.....He then tosses the shotgun over onto land and motions for the camera to come closer....It does....Outlaw's smile gets bigger and bigger.Finally when the camera crew gets real close Outlaw tosses water at the camera [ Moment of fuzz due to the water ] And were back....Outlaw then smiles again....then speaks.The shotgun can be seen in the background setting on the land.
[ Outlaw ] : " Mother fuckers.......Been along time since a federation heard from me about a match I was actually in. I mean shit...This is a face you were all ethier missing really bad or wishing would stay away forever....Ethier way
Like Chucky says in all his movies " I'm Back !!!!!" Feels good to...Uxwa a place where I have won more titles than Sleep Stalker has done hardcore moves.That is saying something to folks incase you don't know the myth of the
Stalker....Ya he never got his world title, but he made a name for himself without that title.Like I have told people who talk to me from day one.It's the champion who makes the belt, not the belt which makes the champion. Uxwa has had people like Hustler, Malik, Scotty Lee Extreme, DDT, Sleep Stalker and Rob
Stylz compete in it.The place doesn't have a rich history, but it has a nice history to it....Ya know what I mean ? [ Wink ] Of course you do. Those names which i said are all gone gone now except for Me...I am one big name who is trying to stay in the sport...Some say I am just trying to hang on.I am scared to let go.Some say I am the only true legend..The only one "Standing the test of time"....Well fuck what you heard...This is what I am saying [ Pauses ] I know I have heard that from somewhere [ Rubs chin ] Well anywayz......I am saying I am here to fuck some people up...Make the ride bumpy for the new guys...Win, lose or draw I will put up a good fight...and for the old guys....Well you want to be called a legend don't cha.....Bring it.See if you still got what it takes to take a barbwire bat upside the head, or a deathwarrant spinebuster....One of the overall best finishers the game has ever seen...Fuck the test of time....Fuck the cocky mother fuckers.....I am an Icon of this shit no matter what you want to say. I put in my hours.....But I would like to let yall know.My goal...Yes a goal...Which I usually do good when I set goals...Is to come back just as good if not better than before...Might take me a lil time...Like a few weeks or maybe months, but I want to be back full steem ahead with no looking over my shoulder very soon....Can I do it ? "
[ Outlaw then slowly walks out of the water while shaking his head up and down....as if to say Damn right I can do it. Once he gets out of the water you see he is wearing his combat boots...He reaches down on the ground and puts a reebock hat on backwards....then grabs the Shotgun....He shoots a shot in the air and it echoes over the area....He then puts the Shotgun on his shoulders and walks down a path....We continue to walk down this path till we reach the infamous 1994 corvet zr1 which Outlaw has owned for ages now. He puts the
Shotgun in the back of the Corvet, then turns around and talks to the camera again.
" All the shit I just said starts off very soon...Thursday Night to be exact [
Outlaw breathes deeply in and out a few times....then continues with a stern tone in his voice ] OH YES...Thursday Night is the day a man who has been beaten by Sleep Stalker so many times...Stalker has legal rights to call him a bitch steps into the squared circle with Yours truly...Kinda my warm up match or practice match before I get a shot at the one title in the UXWA i have never owned...I have stole it, but never earned it.....Water Boy you want to play with fire....Momma should of told you how bad that was when you were young, cause now your gonna get _BURNT_...Water can put out most flames, but not the one which lurks on the inside. H2o you think you can stand in the way of my
Universial title...I know you do. I can't blame you for thinking that.You have to have confidence in yourself to be able to win matches in this shit...But the thing is.That your gonna get some bruises, broken bones and concussions for your efforts. Damn....What a kid gets for thinking huh.....H2o its not personal...Ya in NAWP there was some personal bull shit in a royal rumble we both competed in, but those times have been buried under the bring at least on my side...If you want to bring that shit up fine.But realize I will fight fire with fire.I am can be nice, I can be mean, or I can plain out fuck you up with no words just actions.....The Outlaw is coming "
Outlaw then pauses, a voice comes over the screen...We assume its a cheap rip off of the "British Our coming" Voice you hear on TV when they try to act like
Paul Revere.....It says "Outlaw is coming, Outlaw is coming"...It stops and
Outlaw looks around....As if to say "Where the fuck did that come from"....He then shrugs his shoulders at the camera, and with a devilish smirk continues to speak.
" Water Boy....Whomever wins out of us Thursday Night gets that universial title shot against ethier Brandon Ledford or Shadow of Style.Now I don't know bout you , but I have beat them both and wouldn't have a problem doing it for an Oh so sweet third time..... Ledford and Shadow both know they would rather face you than me H2o...They Might even be so scared of me they try and help you pull this win out of the bag, but Don't count on that...There is no way I am letting my comeback to the game, and my lifetime record be emberassed by someone like you. No offence of course [ Wink ] Water Boy I could tell you eight hundred different ways I could beat you, but I won't. I would much prefer to show you....This isnt the WRX water bitch....Hear your gonna have to earn your win, your lucky your even considered high enough up on the rankings to be able to step into the ring with me.I am down right now so you got one card in ya deck, but other than your shit out of luck.No ring rust, still top notch shape.....Much more experenced, Wiser....Prolly faster Only different I am a little nicer. Which is good and bad...Good cause now I am more in control.Fewer mistakes happen this way...and Bad cause I used to love to beat people till near death....Now I stop at hospital wounds [ Shrugs ] Go Figure....."
"See...It's funny to me..I mean almost to the point that I can't stop laughing when people hear the name Outlaw and automatically think the bitch on the down hill....Well boys....You can say that all you want, but till you prove it to me and the rest of the world.I wouldn't voice your opinion....Just keep it to yourself. Cause if you speak out and be proven wrong....You will be a fool, Not just assumed to be a fool.....Take your pick though...Ethier way you look at it your below me and the real wrestling fans.I say real wrestling fans cause anybody who has followed the history of this sport threw good and bad....at least know's I deserve respect at all times.You might not like me, but you at least respect my in ring talent....Which for years has stood up among the best in the game....In all sorts of crazy ass matches. This of course don't mean shit though cause We have a normal match...At least for now we do..Knowing UXWA they will change it right before match to a no dq pinfalls count anywhere match or some bull shit like that...Just prepare and beware Kid...I saw in WRX you lost to Rob Stylz...No shame there...but losing two matches in a row ? That's got to be hard for the self esteem of a person [ Smile ] So there you have it
H2o...You are at a fork in the road when it comes to your wrestling carrear...Don't choose the wrong path."
Outlaw then gets in his corvet...and turns on the engine. Outlaw by 2pac his tradtional theme song is playing very loudly...He turns it down a bit where he can talk over it and says some last words for now.But I am sure there will be more....
" Simple H2o....If you ignored everything else I said just hear this one Okay kid.....Come with your Best game, or don't come playing the game at all. This isn't what you think it is...You Just don't Understand. My comeback is not something to fuck around with Water kid.Take that seriously ......Quote the
Outlaw No More"
