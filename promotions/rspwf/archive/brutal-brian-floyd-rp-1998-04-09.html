"Brutal" Brian Floyd RP

9 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** Email me and tell me what you think. It's not one of my better ones, but I saw everybody putting there's on here so I figured I'd show my newest one. ***
( The scene opens on a busy highway in New York, New York. There are thousands of cars moving ever so slowly down the busy road. The camera films the traffic build-up from a helicopter-like view, and then slowly descends until it is gliding about 5 ft. from all the vehicle's tops. It continues to "fly" low until it gets to a brand new '98 Red Corvette. It then goes even lower until it is in the car. Driving this fine piece of machinery is none other than
"Brutal" Brian Floyd. He has on a black shirt with "THE CULT" in white block lettering, JNCO Jeans, and black Doc Martens. His blondish brown hair is curly and ever so slightly combed. He has his right hand resting on the steering wheel and his other rubbing his chin. It seems as if he's tryint to go for the sexy, yet sophisticated driver's look. After a few moments, he finally notices the camera and gives it a slight smirk. After a brief silence, he begins to speak. )
BBF: Well, hello there Mr. Camera. Fancy meeting you here. How do ya like my new car? I stole it from that McDonald's Monopoly Game. The person wins this baby is gonna be PREEEEETTY upset when they find out it's missing. But...oh well. I can always just buy them another one. It's not like I'm short on cash or anything. But anyways, enough about the car. I'd like to say in the direction of Sterling Mitchell.
( Brian pauses and gives the camera a stern look. ) Sterling, I suppose a chair to my skull is now in the process. Why? Because every single time I help you out you thank me by bashing me on the head or finding some other way to rip me. If you haven't figured it out by now
Mitchell, I'm trying to be your friend! ( Brian says that in a childish, goofy tone ) And all you do is sh*t on me every time! Let's see, the first time I helped you out was when I gave you the TV Title. What did you do? You smacked me on the head with a chair. The next time was at the PPV when I saved you again. What did you do? You beat me with a 2 count. THEEEEEEN...on Fever I just fought off Knick Man's Finest so you wouldn't get the living hell kicked out of you. So, it's only appropriate that you hurt me again. ( Brian puts his cheek up to the camera and points to it ) Come Sterling, hit me, you know you want to.
( Brian pauses as the traffic moves about 10 ft. and then stops again. ) But ya know what Sterling? I really don't blame you one bit. I mean, if I had the TV Title then I'd be as cautious as you are too. Anybody can stab you in the back at any given time. Hell, I don't even know if I'd trust my good buddy
Frank Crow ( pauses to let it sink in ) or his mentor Sephiroth ( pauses again ). I use their names to prove to you how skeptical I would be if I had gold around my waist. So I understand where you're coming from Sterling. That's why I respect you. Or half way respect you anyways. To tell you the truth, I don't even know why I help you out. I guess it's just something to do.
Without showing my face once in a while, I'd be lost in the ASW shuffle. Hell,
I've fallen from being the top TV Title contender to a nobody in two weeks.
That just goes to show fast your glory can last in this federation. If I've learned anything in the last 6 months, it's to always have something up your sleeve and to be unpredictable.
( Brian pauses as a fly buzzes into the car and lands on his face. He swats at it and it flies around in front of him. He continues to battle it until he fights it out of the car. He then smiles a goofy smile and continues to talk.
) So anyways, as I was saying before I was so rudely interrupted by that fly. You can become a hero to a zero in no time here in the ASW. One PPV, I'm wrestling for the TV Title...and the next I'm wrestling Darren Shaw in a
Bathroom Match. ( Brian pauses for a second and then continues ) What the hell is that? Do I look like the type of person who enjoys rolling around in human waste for fun?
Huh? Well, I don't think I do. You other nimrods out there might have a different view. Buta that's beside the point. The main point is that I'll wrestle in this match, and I'll shut this Darren Shaw kid up once and for all.
I'm gonna introduce him to my NEW finisher....THE SWIRLY!!! ( Brian laughs hysterically for a while and then his laughter subsides. ) Gosh I kill myself! Man, I need to do a stand up comedy show or something. People must know how funny I am! But anyways.....( gains seriousness ) back to my situation in the ASW. As I was saying before, I'll wrestle this Bathroom
Match. Every wrestler is handed crap ( snickers ) at one point in their career. What you have to do is squeeze it out and flush it down the pot. (
Snickers again ) Because eventually you're going to get your title shot again, and eventually you're going to win. Because like I always said...if life hands you a lemon, make some lemonade!
( Brian pauses at his stupid comment. He looks at the road as traffic FINALLY begins to move fluently again. He shows a happy smile and then talks once more. )
So anyways, Mr. Shaw, I hope you're ready for one hell of a match. I'm gonna throw everything at you, including the bathroom toilet! After this massacre, you'll cringe every time you hear the name..."BRUTAL" BRIAN FLOYD. You're come to respect the kind of wrestler I am, and you'll except the fact that I'm just plain better than you. So, Darren Shaw, if you wanna get brutal...then LET'S
GET BRUTAL!!! ( The camera zooms out as you hear Brian yelling "Whooooooo-Hooooooooo!!!" in a high pitched yell like he's riding a bucking bronco. The camera goes back up in the air, shows the traffic moving, and then fades to black. )
