(Note: I came up with this gimmick BEFORE WWF introduced Edge. He is sort of similar appearance wise, but not much further than that)
I just have a few requirements. -Booked Federation -Closed Flash System (BCC) -I'd like a fed just starting out, or a Well Established One Thats basically it. If interested, just email me with offers. Please, don't make me part of a mass mailing. I'd like a Personal Invitation, makes it seem like you are more willing to do the work involved with a fed.
Part I: Personal info •Your screen name: [detail withheld] •Your first name (optional): Casey Part II: Basic Wrestler Info •Wrestler Name: "Nytemare" Josh Blayde •Real Name: Josh Fairfax •Hometown: Toronto, Canada •Date of Birth: 2-23-80 Appearance- •Height: 6' •Weight: 220 •Build: Atletic •Heel/Face: Heel •Basic Appearance Description (Run down on how he would look, wrestling gear, coming down to the ring, to the fans): Josh has short black hair that is always wet and hanging in his face. He is caucasion. He has dark eyes as well. He wears a black trench coat to the ring. He isn't large in muscle mass, but everything is well defined. He wears no shirt. He wears tights with a gothic style silver cross on both sides. Within the cross it says Nytemare in red letters. His tights are either Black, Dark Blue or Red. He also wears black boots with Blayde written on them veritcally in red letters. His laces are the color of his tights. He has the same gothic cross on his tights tatooed on his left bicep.
•Gimmick Be descritptive: It's almost like a Tortured Soul Edge Style gimmick, but with the Dreams added. He loves to talk about the fears of humanity, and what goes on in the Human Brain after one goes to sleep. He plays
Mindgames like Mankind used to.
