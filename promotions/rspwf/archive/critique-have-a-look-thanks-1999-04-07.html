Hi i would be gratefull if you have a read and tell me what you want at [detail withheld] This is one for many first Roleplays also look for another fed to join with a Tag team !!1 <I> ECW cameras are outside some gates in sunny Miami. The gates open and the camera crew run to their van and drive up the drive way. Passing well kept lawns and gardens once past the swimming pool their see the house.
White walls with ivy growing up one side of the wall, the van pulls up next to several golf carts outside the house. The camera crew gets out and are met by the Butler who shows them in to the house and then into the what seems to be the main room of the house. The room is well furnished on the wall infront of the crew is a glass cabinet with what looks like a old championship belt the camera moves in on it but can’t make what it say. The
Butler then specks to the camera crew </I>
Butler: - Master Tyke will be with you in a minute. Make your self at home. <I> Jordan walks past the door making her way down to the pool. You hear the sound of an electric motor pulling away from the house on the gravel.
The camera crew sits down on leather chairs in the room. Tyke enters the room rubbing himself down with an towel </I>
Tyke: - Sorry about this just came from training and was not expecting you this early. Well since you’re here might as well start.
<I> Tyke sits down on one of his leather chairs, the butler walks in with some food </I> Help yourself. ECW is only Three weeks old and already it has had some great fights. The inferno match up between X-Plosin and ODB is just one that stick out in my mind. Well The Great One enjoyed his little photo shoot with Jordan the other day. Excuses me a minute.
<I> Tyke takes a sip of water and leaves the room. While Tyke is gone the
TV at the far end of the room turns on showing flashes of Tyke and many
Others Wrestlers in ECW, but mainly Tykes fight against Nick Matthews of DX.
Tyke comes back in wearing his all Black suit and black tie. Once again you hear the sound of an electric motor and the sound of gravel outside, Then
Jordan walks pass again. Tyke takes another sip of water and sits down again.</I>
Well I surpose you saw the TV?? Well let tell you all something you see so far all the greatest moments in ECW have not involved the GREAT ONE it seem
I’am getting held back in my quest to get gold here. So starting this week on Fusion when I destroy who ever the Prez decides to put in my path you will see The Tyke Express calling at ever station until I get what I want and that is GOLD!!! But not any old gold but the championship. You see The
Good looking one in ECW does not want to smash his face by getting involved in The Extreme belt. As you see The Great one is after much bigger Gold than that and has much more respect for my body than to throw myself through tables and jump from 40ft onto the mat. God You lot make me sick!! As for
GWO trying as hard as they can trying to bring some class to their stable my bring Vincent the PIMP to bring to talent in “wOw” keep trying. As the only time that stable will ever have any CLASS and that is when TYKE joins you and that ain’t going to happen I can tell ye.
<I>Jordan enters the room wearing an all black dress. The camera crew is some what confused to why both Jordan and Tyke are dressed like this so one of them asks</I>
Soundman: - May I Tyke: - Go on then Soundman: - Why are Jordan and your self dressed all in black are you attending someone funeral??
Jordan: - You’re quite right we are attending a funeral. We are going to show our respect to Ian Paul Freely who died the other day in the lift accident
Tyke: - I don’t really want to go but Jordan talked me into it. As we has a member of ECW.
<I> The Butler enters the room he begins to talk to Tyke </I> Butler: - Sir the car is ready for you shall I bring it round to you?? Tyke: - Yes James bring the car round. <I> The butler than leaves </I> Tyke: - Before we leave just a quick message to a Pal as I know he will be watching this at home. ODB I’ve not seen or heard of you since last Fusion when I introduced you to the world as The Untouchables newest member. What up buddy cat got ye tongue. You have charged since you became the champ, you seem to be hiding. There is no reason for you to hide as
1. You are ECW World champion 2. You’re a member of The Untouchables You should be out in the public eye taking what you want and making the most of bring the Champ…
Jordan: - Come on Honey will be late. <I> You hear the gravel moving again and Tyke black convertible Porsche 911 pulls up outside the house. The butler enter the room again </I>
Butler: - The car is ready sir Tyke: - Ok thanks James can you see that this gentleman are shown off the grounds. Thanks Gentleman but if you may excuse me I have an funeral to attend.
<I>Tyke and Jordan leave the room and get into the car. Tyke than drives the car and is now out of sight. James than attends to the removal of the
Camera crew. The camera fades to black. </I>
