"Superstar" Brad Hawk roleplay

Hey guys, could you please take a moment and read this new roleplay of a new character I have come up with. And if anyone would like for him to sign a contract, please get a hold of me at
[detail withheld] Thank you! [detail withheld] (Camera opens up on a white limosine pulling up to a resteraunt with a big sign that reads HAWK'S PLACE. There are armed gaurds standing around keeping the people away. The driver quickly exits the car and runs to the passengers back door. As he opens the door, a man wearing a black suit and tie exits. He has long dark brown hair tied back in a ponytail. He looks well built under his suit, and he stands about six and a half feet tall. He has dark Oaklees sunglasses on and no expression is on his face whatsoever.
After he gets out, he puts his hand out and a very beautiful blonde comes out of the limo. She is about 5'7'' and slim. Her breasts about pop out of the top of her blue sparkling dress as the couple is lead into the resteraunt by security. The man motions for the camera man to follow. The cameraman follows them into a back room that is deserted and they are seated in a table for two as the cameraman remains standing.)
Man: Well, I'm sure you are being paid by the EDF to get a few words from me, their newest aquisition, so we might as well get this over with so you end up with a check to cash at the end of the week. Let me introduce myself and my beautiful wife.
Woman: Ahem. Brad, I believe I am capable of introducing myslef. Man: SOrry. Go for it. Woman: I am Jessica Hawk. For all of you managers, valets, and bimbos out there I want you to pay attention. I'm not gonna be one of those women who just goes out there for the kick of it. I'm his support, and if it means I have to get physical out there, so be it.
Man: Whoa Jess. My name, and it's a name all of you in the EDF will find hard to foget soon enough, is Brad Hawk. They call me the Superstar, and that's exactly what I'm going to become around here.
(ALl of a sudden a waitorcomes over to the table and Brad seems to become furious. He stand up and grabs the waitor's pad.)
Brad Hawk: Does it look like we're ready for you punk? If it wasn't for me, you wouldn't even have a job. SO take this pad, and I'll call for you when I want you. And if I get one more interuption it will mean your job!
Do you understand? (The waitor nods yes as Brad whips the pad across the room.) Brad Hawk: You see, I own this resteraunt. When my father died a few years back, he left me so much money we've been rolling in it for the last three years. My beautiful wife and I have gotten anything and everything we want.
That's just the way we live, and I love it. I could own the whole town of
Los Angeles if I wished. And for every one of you in the EDF, I could own you with just the snap of my fingers as well. Everything in life is about money...and I've got it. I've got the looks, the money, the perfect woman,
I've got everything, and I'm bringing it all with me to the EDF.
Brad Hawk: Now, of course there is this title and that title. That's not what I'm here for. There's fame and fortune...big deal. I've got the money, and I can easily buy my way into fame if I want to. I grew up on the streets of L A. A neighborhood where I hate to fight and brawl just to get to my doorstep. The gangs and bullies were always out there, just waiting to strike. But then I soon realized something. It's much more for to be the bullie than be bullied, and so I'd be the one standing in front of doorsteps blockin little punks like I see today in the EDF. I enjoyed being the hunter opposed to the hunted. And I realized that yes, the one thing that runs through my blood, the one hobby that I find the most fun...beating people up. That's why I'm here. That's the statement that will be made on my behalf. And if I have to start at the bottem of the ladder, so be it.
Cause soon the Superstar will be at top, with a heap load of loosers laying at the bottem begging me for a chance to be in my shoes.
Jessica Hawk: And myself, I'm gonna be standing there right next to Brad. If he has a title, so be it. But we're here to take over...and to get to the top..we really don't have to buy cause it's going to be just that easy.
(Jessica snaps her fingers.) Brad Hawk: So, EDF, you now know my full intentions. I'm not gonna sit back and watch the action...I AM THE ACTION!! So just sit back, and take notes...cause I'm HERE!! WAITOR!!!
(Camera fades out.)
