(RP, Please critique) William Craven.

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
On a whim I've decided to post 3 William Craven RP's. This one is a nutty little comedy bit I decided to throw together I'd like to call "Uncle Bill".
. . ---- (Fade in on an odd scene. Uncle Sam lays on a couch in a psychiatrist's office. A bookcase behind him and ratty old diplomas and degrees litter the dirty wall. The office itself isn't in the best state of repair, but it's still obviously the office of a psychiatrist. Everything in the room points to the stereotypical, movie-scene type of a doctor's office, and the couch is the type used in a mental physician's workspace.
The identity of "Uncle Sam" is in question because the costume covers almost all of his body. Wearing a mask of an old man's face, a wig, a red, white and blue polyester jumpsuit, flaglike tophat, and even white gloves and long socks, he is 99% covered. Only the slightest bit of flesh peeks out between the segments of the man's bodily covering. His identity is truly a mystery.
. .until the psychiatrist speaks off camera.) Dr. Schwartz: Bill. . .why are you dressed like Uncle Sam? SamCraven: I. . .I am Uncle Sam. . . Dr. Schwartz: Of course, of course you are! (The camera swivels to face the good doctor. Dr Schwartz is shown to be sitting in a chair to Craven's right. Schwartz is, like his office, shabbily dressed and ratty in appearance. He looks as if he's missed a few meals, forgotten to shave, and is addicted to some sort of drug. Oddest of all, he wears a "Craven: Bloody Scream" T-shirt under his open suitjacket.)
Dr. Schwartz: Um. . .Bill. . .do you actually believe that? (A tense moment of silence as the camera pans back and forth between the doctor and his patient. Neither man moves much. Schwartz grabs a clipboard and begins to take notes and Craven's hand reaches down under the psychiatrist's couch.)
Dr. Schwartz: Now. . .why, why are you dressed as Uncle Sam? I don't understand. SamCraven: I have to defend the coast, Vic. . .the Dane's are invading. Dr. Schwartz: So_this is how you deal with the. . .um. . .invasion? Receding into a fantasy where you are America's symbol of. . . (Schwartz is cut off as Craven's hand comes up from under the couch. In it
Craven holds his favorite weapon, his pride, his joy, his center, his_boken.
. . This is the only movement he's made since the tapes started rolling. The doctor continues to scribble on his clipboard.) SamCraven: It's like Teddy Roosevelt said. . ."speak softly and carry a big boken". Dr. Schwartz: I think that's stick, Bill. . . SamCraven: Don't contradict me, son, a boken is a stick!!! (On hearing the word "son" the doctor scribbles on his clipboard in a hurried manner. He is seemingly_undisturbed by Craven's increasing agitation.)
SamCraven: They're coming. . .the Danes. . .the POD people. . .the Rose
Buds. . . I have to protect the Atlantic Coast from foreign insurrection!
They're coming to take America's gold! Dr. Schwartz: Gold, I see that you've brought a cameraman from the ACWA, but where is your gold, Bill? Er. . .Sam?
SamCraven: Oh. . .I buried it. . . It's in good ol' American soil now. Those nasty Nordic flowers will never_ever find it. Dr. Schwartz: (Looking alarmed, scribbling madly on his clipboard.) You buried the title!? That's hard to believe!
(Craven is silent again as the camera swivels once again between the two men. The doctor just scribbles on his clipboard while glancing at Craven.)
Cameraman: Hey, I'm new around here. Is he usually like this? Dr. Schwartz: (Stops scribbling, looks to camera.) Hm? No, no, usually he's either manic or depressive. . .sometimes silly. He tends to get delusional under stress sometimes.
SamCraven: I'm not delusional. . .I'M UNCLE SAM! Dr. Schwartz: I know you are. Now hold still! (Starts scribbling again.) Cameraman: (Under his breath.) Hold still? What the *bleep*? SamCraven: Oh, I'm a yan-kee doo-dle dan-dee, yank-ee doo-dle do or die! (Trails off into mumbling. . .as if he can't remember the words.) Dr. Schwartz: It's because today's the fourth, and he's fighting a foreigner on the seventh. Understand?
Cameraman: I. . .I think so. Dr. Schwartz: GOOD! SamCraven: Did I tell you when my army fought against the Danes? Well, just one Dane and a few other guys. My army won!
Dr. Schwartz: Hm. . .thinks that the Unstable and Love Muscle were armies. . .interesting. . . SamCraven: Aren't you done yet? Dr. Schwartz: No, HOLD STILL! Cameraman: (Again, under his breath.) This don't look like any headshrinkin' session I ever seen. . . SamCraven: Y'know. . .the son of Jesse Love is the last remnant of the
Lovely army. . . If I can wipe him out, I'll achieve my objective!
Total_war brings total_annihiliation! Dr. Schwartz: That's right, scorched earth. . .very nice. . . Cameraman: (Still under his breath.) Wha'? I don't get it. . . Dr. Schwartz: Now, Sam. What will you do if you can't_beat the Dane? What if he beats you?
SamCraven: I. . .I dunno. I never thought of that. Dr. Schwartz: Well, try. What would you do? SamCraven: I'd. . .I'd have to kill him. Kill. . .k. . .kill. . .him. . . (The camera itself twitches as if the Cameraman had flinched at the inflection in Craven's words. The pure, unadulturated hatred in his voice, the disbelief at the possibility of losing. Craven tenses, confused. .
.angry. . .violent. . .) Dr. Schwartz: I'M DONE! SamCraven: Really!? Lemme see! (The doctor shows what he's scribbled to Craven, and Craven squiggles in his seat, overjoyed at what he sees.)
SamCraven: IT LOOKS JUST LIKE ME! Cameraman: What? SamCraven: Yeah! Look! (Craven's massive bulk lifts quickly from the psychiatrist's couch as he holds the clipboard up to the camera. On the clipboard is a sloppy sketch of Uncle Sam laying on a psychiatrist's couch with a stick in his hands.
It's rough, it's somewhat flat, but it's a decent sketch.) SamCraven: Vic's a real artist, ain't he!? Cameraman: Yeah. . .artist. . . (The camera begins a slow fade to black as the patriotically dressed bald, scarred freak pulls back to discuss his portrait with his psychiatrist.
Craven seems highly animated as they whisper together, possibly planning something for the ring or an art gallery. Then the nonsensical scene finally disappears. All is black. . .)
