Heidi Leick RP for to you Rate/Enjoy (2/3)

14 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ It's late at night and Heidi Leick is walking down the street towards the camera , it's dimly lit... and from the looks of the abandoned buildings and
Hurricane fencing in the background, it's somewhere near the parking lot where she cut her last interview, in Brooklyn, NYC, She's wearing the same clothes,... she stops a few feet before the camera.. on the stoop of an abandoned brownstone, full of debris, needles, drug paraphernalia, litter, & other vile filth associated with the streets.. a fire burns in a garbage can halfway down the block, a few of NYC's homeless are hovering around it for warmth, as for the music .. this time.. "Toy Soldiers" by Martika plays in the background ]
Heidi Leick : I remember growing up.. watching Wendy Richter , Misty Blue
Simms & Madusa Micelli on TV.. Those women.. were Hero's to all the little girls who were wrestling fans... There was a time I wanted to be Leick them..
loved.. respected.. cherished by the fans... in fact, at the time.. I... I was one of the ~biggest~ fans.. and I ~loved~ my ~heroes~.
[ turns towards the side of the stoop and stares at the city ] Right there, across the river.. lies Madison Square Garden... Oh, how I waited overnight on line for tickets, there in the 80's.. wanting to sit in the front rown to cheer them on.. and then waited near the workers entrance to get a glimpse and maybe an autograph of my ~heros'~
[ shakes her head and turns towards the camera again, sitting on the stone handrails of the stoop ]
But the scalpers had a hold on the best tickets.. And as for My Heroes. my ~Heroes~ turned their backs on me . that 15 year old girl that waited in the freezing cold for a glimpse of recognition from them...
for a smile... hoping for a hello... begging for an autograph... Yes.. my heroes broke my heart.. and made me into the ~woman~ you see before you today... GD Dub.. My heroes then.. just like yours now.. do the same things to you when the cameras aren't on them.. Yes Fans.. Your GD Dub Heroes, just like my former heroes, have a lot in common with The Title of the song you hear.. and the words.. how ironic.. yes GD Dub.. your pathetic heroes. they are all fallen.. or maybe better to say.. They all fall down Leick ~Toy Soldiers~..
[giggles] Butterfly... Manson.. Zaranna... Tiger.. Grier... Sakkura.. where are you when the fans need a hero?
[ Heidi looks to the west, towards the city, and sighs ] Probably out selling more merchandise.. ripping off the fans by doing paid appearances for autographs ... just Leick you Officer Order..
[ smiles and pats the back of her neck as she turns towards the camera ] Let me ask you.. How did it feel to have to count the 1, 2, 3 for me.. and raise my hand as winner.. [ smiles evilly ] after I nailed that waste of life
Zaranna ... ~with the same move that put you out~
Double O.. The Biggest sellout of them all.. you dont care about your fans.. you should just get up off your "daydreaming cause ~you~ deep down inside really ~want~ to be ~me~" ass and tell them... tell them the ~truth~
[grabs that apple out of her pocket again.. and stares at it ] But.. alas.. you do not have the courage.... [ Heidi snickers a bit, tosses the apple in the gutter, and folds her arms..as clouds of her breath form in the cold night air ]
That's OK.. They can handle it Double O.. Double Nothing..., just ~tell~ them.. Tell them.. Leick I told ECW.. NO F'n Way.. No way in the god damn world was
I going to become Ravens follower.. the new Ravens Nest.. Heidi Leick saving
Scott Levy's pathetic ass from beating after beating, and taking the abuse that
He should...
and cant. No Womens title there.. and no chance to take one of the men's titles.. No, no no.. [ giggles and continues ] I'll take my chances in GD
Dubbya A... ~thank~ ~you~ ~very~ ~much~...
[ Heidi stares into the sky, laughs smugly, and speaks again ] But Double O, you cant abandon the facade.. can you? [ looks back towards the camera, gets up and starts to walk ] And while I'm on the subject of Double O.. let me tell you something.. If it wasnt for you.. Zaranna would have never had a chance in hell to compete vs Andrea Chandler.. Andy had that bitch in her finisher and You Double O, were more preoccupied with ME..
Double O, you didn't enforce the rules, you tried to stack the deck against
Andrea and I.. You tell me Double O... Miss Play by the rules. Miss Ref breaks the rules and drop-kicks a competitor..
Now who's playing out of her character? Hmm? Allison De La Cruz.. Double O Should be FINED! She was there to ENFORCE rules, not get physically involved!! She HAS to be Fined!! Fined for: Crimes against Heidi Leick and Andrea Chandler!.. Crimes against the fans of the GDWA!.. Crimes like breaking her character!... Show her, just Leick you showed Andrea and I , that you wont take this blatant disregard for the rules!
[ Heidi Leick smiles, laughs and bows her head ] But you wont Allison.. will you? Just Leick you wont fine Rekka Sakura or Lady
Tiger... [shakes her head negatively ] Thank the Goddess for sending Miki
Duran.
GD Dubbya fans.. You want to see how much I care about the people? I'll show you... I care about the people, Just as much as Double O does.
[ Heidi approaches the garbage can where several bums keep warm around the garbage that is on fire in it ]
HL: How are you all doing? Keeping warm? Bum # 1: Yezza Maam.. as wawm as wawm can be's on dis fwozen nite.. can ya spares sum change fo da needy?
HL: Change?.. Sure.. I can give you change.. [ Heidi kicks the can over,.. and the garbage that was on fire spills into the street.. and dies out in a few moments, the bums scatter ]
HL: HOW'S THAT FOR CHANGE!!! SCUM!!! [ Heidi laughs and smiles ] Change.. Change will do you good.. !~ HA!! [ Heidi turns , and stares into the camera , evilly.. viciously, maliciously ] All you ~Heroes~ of the GDWA.. hear me.. and listen well.. Not ONE of you can stand up to me one on one.. Not one of you can take me on, in a match with MY RULES, and win. I Challenge.. No wait.. GD Dub.. I DARE your Heros.. to ~TRY~ and stop me.. And for you uninformed.. The Book of Heidi Leick reads this following passage.. from the new testamet.. Leick 13:69 says .. and by the 8th day.. God decided he was lonely..bored and tired of all things good.. leick his right hand.. so
.. he created something better to watch..
THE SHOW [ Heidi smiles, then looks down, hair hanging in her face ] GDWA.. [ holds her arms apart crosses her legs and tilter her head, mocking like she's being crucified ]
~I~ ~feel~ ~your~ ~pain~ [ The scene fades out on Heidi.. and the music continues with the chorus on.. "We all fall down... like toy soldiers.........
