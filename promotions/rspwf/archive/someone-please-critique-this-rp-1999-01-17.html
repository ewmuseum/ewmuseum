I'm trying to make my rp's better. Can someone please rate this one for me and tell me how to fix it.
{Scene} It's a bright, sunny day in early January, in North Wildwood, New Jersey. Later today there is an HWA house show in the Wildwood Convention Center. T.C. Ice is not scheduled for anything but he went along to have some time to himself to reminisce in the place he spent most of his youth. There was a convention at the convention center so T.C. walked the boardwalk thinking about his past. He got to Morey's Pier, which to his surprise was open. All the rides are running there are people running around having fun. T.C. walks up to one of the ticket booths to get a bracelet to go on the rides. A few hours later, the sky turns overcast. T.C. is getting bored of the rides and it is almost time for the HWA event. He starts walking toward the convention center.
All of a sudden, it begins to rain. T.C.'s mood begins to change. He starts thinking about the HWA and the fact that he is no longer the HWA Extreme
Champion. Ice starts running toward the convention center. He passes the tram car and many fans that tried to stop him for an autograph, which he would normally give but doesn't because he is filled with rage. Finally, T.C. Ice reaches the Wildwood Convention Center. He walks in the back entrance and into the president's office. When he enters, the president appears shocked to see him. T.C. has an angry and disturbed look on his face.
T.C. Ice: Mr. President, sorry to bother you but could I have some time on the show for an interview?
[detail withheld]: Sure T.C., but why? T.C. Ice: You'll have to wait and see. Just tell me when to go out. [detail withheld]: You can have the beginning of the card. This seems really important. T.C. leaves the office and goes to the locker rooms. He waits there until the show begins. All his friends try to find out what is bothering him but he won't talk, not even to the rest of the Total Threat. The announcers begin speaking. T.C. hears them over the speakers and prepares to walk to the ring.
As he reaches the entry way "The Kids Aren't Alright" by The Offspring begins playing and white flames shoot up from the floor. He enters the ringside area and walks toward the ring. When he gets there he grabs a microphone and begins speaking.
T.C. Ice: Hey everyone. This is kind of last minute, but I requested this time to say a few things. This is very important, so everyone in the back, you better listen up.
First off, I want to say good luck to The Rock on his upcoming title shot against Sting. I hope you regain your title Rock. Secondly, I want to wish
HBK luck on his number one contender match against Rob Van Dam. And you know what, if both my stablemates win, they'll be facing each other at the next pay per view for the title. If that happens, I hope the best man wins. Next, I wasn't going to do this until someone else took the title first, but I'm taking my rematch for the Extreme Title. Rattlesnake, this is not because I don't like you or want you out of the Total Threat. It is because I feel that I should have never lost the title. You see, you didn't beat me and I feel that
I lost a lot of respect from everyone here when I gave up the title, especially from you. You know, you talk a lot of shit, but I don't really think you can back it up, especially against me. You think you are better than everyone in the HWA. You aren't nearly as good as you think and I'm going to prove it at the ppv. I don't want the Total Threat to break up so if this is going to be a problem then I think you'll need to deal with it because, as I said, this is not because I don't like you, it's to regain the title that I shouldn't have lost and to bring your ego back to earth. Finally, Ace, I have defeated you three times. You have absolutely no chance at beating me this time. You might as well just stay at home on Thursday. And just in case you decide to show up,
I'll be prepared to beat your ass. BEWARE FALLING ICE!!!! T.C. Ice throws down the mic and jumps over the ropes to the floor. He slaps all the fans hands in reach as he goes down the aisle to the locker rooms.
