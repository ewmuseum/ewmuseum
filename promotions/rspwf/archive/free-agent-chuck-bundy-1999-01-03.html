[Free Agent] Chuck Bundy

3 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:: Scene opens up to a dark, cold jail room cell. There is a slightly overweight man sitting on the jail cell bed. This man is wearing a black and white pant, and a shirt with barcode numbers on it. ::
Mike Bundy: Tony Wayne, you are the reason why I am behind bars. You were the one who pushed me into hitting you repeatedly with a crow bar. It was ALL you
Tony Wayne. Bu...
:: Mike Bundy's jail cell mate interrupts him:: Cell Mate: Hey buddy, can you please shutup, I am trying to sleep here. :: Mike Bundy gets up from his bed and walks over to his cell mate. Mike then grabs his cell mate by the collar of his shirt ::
Mike Bundy: Boy, if you keep on interrupting me, I will make sure you will sleep forever. Do you understand me?
Cell Mate: .... :: Mike Bundy pushes the Cell Mate back down to his bed :: Mike Bundy:: Now Tony Wayne, as I was saying. The sight of your red blood gushing down from your forehead, traveling down slowly over your nose, and finally. Tony Wayne, trust me, I had front row seat, you looked like you were about to pass out. Then when you sticked out your hand for a tag, pleading me to make the tag. And I took out the brass knuckles and nail you across the forehead, leving you for dead.
:: Mike Bundy slowly walks around the jail cell with an evil smile on his face
:: Mike Bundy: Oh yes, Tony Wayne, it is true. What happened last Sunday on the
GWA was all a ploy, SET by your "supposedly" best friend, Mike Bundy. Now you might ask yourself, why did I do it? Well Tony Wayne, I want to recap you.
Remember when I got slammed across the head with a chair two weeks ago by
Travis McKay? Where were you at? I soaked many towels from wiping off the blood that was flowing down from my forehead. So you see, Tony, I had to give you a taste of what I went through. The taste of blood dripping down from my head?
No, that is what I crave for in wrestling. But the taste of being abandoned. The taste of being abandoned by a friend that you trusted. Tony, it will only be days before the GWA bail me out. I'll see you soon.
:: Camera fades away as Chuck Bundy sits down on the floor, next to his bed. ::
