Erotic Erin McCoys.. SAMPLE RP

29 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: We see a photography shoot in the AICW Magazine office. Erin McCoys is seen sitting on a desk with her legs crossed and a cigar in her left hand.
She's wearing an aqua colored pant suit with ivory buttons and a pearl choker. Her hair is down and curled lightly. The photographer snaps pictures furiously. The photographer stops and begins to reload] Photographer: That was fantastic. You practically eat the lense. Ever done modeling professionally?
Erin McCoys: Not at all. I was never into that whole scene. Everyone knows the stereo type of a model and I didn't want anything to do with that.
Photographer: Tell me about it. I see alot of female managers and valets here and they are TOTAL airheads. You are the first one in a long time who actually can put sentences together. It's really been a pleasure working with you.
Erin McCoys: I must admit I didn't want to do this shoot today but it teaches me to read the fine print of the contracts I sign more carefully. It looks like
I have a series of endorsements I must fufill each month or I won't get paid.
The list goes on and on. Photographer: I'm sorry to hear that but I must confess, these pictures are going to make a fortune! The suits are already counting the dollar signs in there head! I think this will be highest bought issue ever! How about you take five while I reload. There's a couple more outfits I want to catch you in before we close for today.
[The photographer walks off as Erin leans back in the chair behind the desk. She pulls out a bottle of Evian and takes a long sip] Erin McCoys: It seems like my job is never done. The AICW has found a way to plaster my face on everything from tshirts and poster to magazines and TV apperances. And the pathetic thing is is that I'm not getting anything extra for it. I'm expected to be at fifteen different places at once and still have time to manage the best wrestlers the AICW has to offer. It's a tough job but someone has to do it. It's a job I brought on myself and it's something I enjoy doing. It's hard carrying the weight of the federation on your shoulders. I've seen big bad men buckle under the pressure and I see other women resort to cheap shots and low blows. But not me. I've always aquired a higher standard from people. I take people to a higher level because that's the only way anyone stands a slight chance of beating me. That is what I bring everytime I step into an arena. That is the aura that surronds Erin McCoys. It's an aura that has yet to be dublicated and never will. While other managers are having a wonderful time sleeping with their clients, I have my priorities on bigger and better things. The AICW PPV is fastly upon us and it's a night where I capture what's been rightfully mine since Day One. I capture the AICW and hold it as my own. First victims are Sam's Own Personal bleach blonde play things Bad Boy
Inc and then onto Vic Gibraldi and the AICW World Title. And then the world.
The world will be delievered to me on a silver platter like the days of the
Bible foretold. The most devestating thing of it all is that nothing can stop it. Nothing can stop what I have already started. No way out and hope for anyone. I've always gotten what I wanted out of life and the AICW has been no different. I want the multi million dollar contract and I got it. I don't have to rely on the past to get myself over. I look towards the future because that's where everything is. Those who live in the past are doomed to repeat.
Sam, you've made a moutain of mistakes in your past and you've already repeated several of them. Keep up your ways and you'll be doomed to repeat them all.
Billy Kane, don't make threats that you don't intend on keeping. The Karate kid wants to challenge the Stone? I bid you good luck because you sure as hell will need it. Looks like you've been watching Jerry Springer alittle too much because you are starting to believe Beastality is acceptable in this society.
Gives new meaning to Pig in a Blanket doesn't it Kane? I can see why you'd go after her though. She's so brainless that she won't fight back. Just another little girl who has been brainwashed to follow a man's every word and not think one her own. But that's just her style. You can't blame a dog for a being a dog. Steel City Meltdown will write a new chapter in my career and that of the careers of T3.. It will mark that once again, Erin McCoys out did Sensuous
Samantha and did not have to rely on backhanded attempts. Sam you remind me of that one girl in high school who would always try out for the cheerleading squad, not make it and then bad mouth all the girls who did. You try so hard to be good, fall short and then bad mouth everyone else. Well I'm prepared to finally insert your foot in your mouth. I just hope you don't eat yourself.
Shane Diamond and James Pillow are nothing more then shy little lambs who are being led to the slaughter by Sam herself. Diamond has already tasted defeat before.. Now it's time for Pillow to pertake in it's bitter taste. It's a taste everyone will savor when they step into the ring with the New Age Predetors.
Vic Girbarlidi will have his share of it too. Vic, you will not only lose that match but also at your chance for glory. The Stone will be the next AICW
Champion. Learn to love it cause there's no stopping it. There's no stopping us. [The photographer walks back into the room. He's carrying his camera, a rolled up magazine and two hangers with clothes on them]
Photographer: Here are the next two outfits I want to get you in [Photographer shows Erin a pair of black flared lyrca pants and a black halter top with white trim and a pair of jean shorts and a T3 tshirt]
Photographer: Here...[hands Erin the magazine] Here's the cover of the issue.. tell me what you think [Erin takes the magazine and the camera zooms in on it. We see Erin McCoys standing in front of an AICW Backdrop wearing a long silver strapless dress and smoking a cigar. Underneath her, we see the words "ERIN MCCOYS: FIRST LADY OF
THE AICW]
Erin McCoys: First Lady? Photographer: Arge's orders. You are now the First Lady of the AICW. Erin McCoys: More weight on my shoulders. Photographer: Let's get started [Erin lets out a sigh and throws the magazine on the table. She walks off screen. FADE]
