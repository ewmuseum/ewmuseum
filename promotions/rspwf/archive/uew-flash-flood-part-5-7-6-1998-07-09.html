UEW Flash Flood Part 5 (7/6)

9 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1) Explosion 2) You Can Trust Me Johnny Glamour 3) Real Deal Shawn Harrison 4) Widow Maker Magnus Colby 5 A further word from the editor ------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------- From: Explosion Subject: Zach's leaving home Setting: As the camera pans up, we find that we are at the front gate of the Rose family compound. A sign to the right reveals that it is next to the Kennedy family compound in Florida. The camera sweeps through the front gate and zooms right in on the front porch. Zach Rose walks out on the front porch and throws a rather large duffel onto the ground.
Zach Rose: Who knew I had this much crap on me? (Fire and Nitro exit both carrying large boxes) Fire: If you're so rich Zach how come we have to move you out ourselves??? Zach Rose: Knowing my uncle, he'd probably go through my things before he sent them. If he didn't, you can bet that punk Styles would have pawn my crap to pay for his Geritol addiction. New generation my ass!!!!
Nitro: Hey kid, where'd you get that nifty Rubik's cube??? (Before Zach can answer, Zach's 98 year old great grandmother comes out on the porch and begins to cry and babble in Yiddish)
Zach Rose: I told you grandma, as long as this family associates with Uncle Mark I am out of here. But don't worry, you can come over to my place and make halushkis for us any time.
Nitro: I don't know what a halushki is, but if it tastes anywhere as good as her sauerkraut did last night she's welcome at our place anytime.
Fire: As long as Styles and Rose don't show up. I hear they've been known to show up on your doorstep practically every night begging for a free meal.
Zach Rose: That's not too suprising. Ever since Uncle Mark started hanging out with Rick Styles he's become quite a scumbag. And lets be honest,we all know that Styles is always out for a free lunch.
Nitro: Tell me about it!!!! Everyone knows those guys in the NA have never done anything on their own. They are too busy doing Styles job for him. The NA made Styles a legend by making sure he never had to do a job by himself.
Fire: Anybody who needs an example should look at the Stallions. These guys were the rookies of the year and were making a name for themselves around the wrestling world. Then they join the NA and lose the titles they worked their ass off to gain. Their ranking has slipped ever since.
(Zach's mom comes out on the porch) Mom: Here son, I made you a lunch to take with you. Zach Rose: Ooooohhhhh Mom!!!!!! Mom: Your father and I are behind you. In fact, he'd be here seeing you off but he's busy writing that bastard uncle of yours out of your great grandmothers will.
Zach Rose: Don't worry mom. I will restore glory to the Rose name. And while I am at it, I will teach Uncle Mark and Rick Styles a thing or two.
Mom: Will I see you later son????? Zach Rose: Of course!!!!! You are coming over to do my laundry in a couple of days. (Fire and Nitro finish putting the boxes in the Uhaul truck while Zach kisses his mom goodbye. As Mrs. Rose watches with a tear in her eye, the truck pulls away and goes down the driveway. The last thing we hear is the truck horn beeping as the scene fades to black.)
========================================== From: You Can Trust Me Johnny Glamour Subject: Glamour for the Gold [Scene opens up in Hollywood California inside a low budget movie studio. Sitting in front of the camera dressed in a black tuxedo with a gold bow tie is none other than the resident huckster of UEW, Johnny "YOU CAN TRUST ME!!"
Glamour. Sitting alongside Glamour is his airhead valet extrordinaire Bambi, who is dressed in a pearl white dress and diamond necklace. Glamour who is reading some sort of cue cards looks up to the camera and begins to speak in his used car salesman style voice...]
JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- Well, I'd like to welcome all of my wonderful "Glamour
Groupies" out there in TV Land to the first ever edition of my new television show entitled...
"...GLAMOUR FOR THE GOLD!!" [A rousing round of applause, obviously a taped one, is heard. Glamour smiles and continues...]
Since today is the first ever edition, I thought we'd have it be a really super show and talk about everybody's favorite wrestler/entertainer/business man in the world today. That's right... ME! Before I begin though, I'd like to update all you fools, [hesitates] I mean fans, on my steal of a deal hotline 1-900-4-RIPOFF. Call today and select option #9 to find out exactly what happened between Dark Vanity and Doug Milne after a 12 pack of beer and a game of truth and dare that UEW DOES NOT want you to know. People, Johnny
Glamour has the scoop and at only $4.99 a minute, how can you lose?
Pick up that phone and let me start stealing your money today. [Glamour realizes what he just said begins to stutter] Di...D...D...Did I just say stealing your money? I _meant_ to say you will start reeling in the money.
That's right, because when you dial 1-900-4-RIPOFF and select option #8,
Johnny Glamour the certified psychic will read your fortune right over the phone! Maybe you want to know the outcome of all the matches at Sunday Night
Heat. Well, to prove to you all that I am a _real_ psychic, I'll let you all in on the outcome of the Johnny Glamour vs. Doug Milne match... Johnny
Glamour by pinfall after a "Glamour Jammer!" Since we're already on the subject of my next opponent, "Dangerous" Doug
Milne, I'd like to take this time to actually talk about what makes this guy so dangerous... Ummm... Well... [Utter silence breaks through the recording studio.] Let's see.... He's dangerous because...
[Glamour begins to scratch his head, still trying to think what makes Doug
Milne dangerous. The room is now so quiet that you can hear bugs chirping...]
I guess trying to hype up my next so called opponent is harder than I thought. You know, I can't even find one interesting tid bit to say about this guy. And the sad thing is he has had 16 matches not one of which is memorable... Well Doug Milne, that all changes on July 12th at Anarchy... You will be the next victim to cross my path and feel my glamorous wrath, the next of the many who will take a one way ticket to humbling humiliation in "GLAMOURVILLE!" And after it is all said and done and your looking at the lights on the ceiling while laying flat on your back you can finally say that you are famous...
Afterall, getting beat by the games fastest rising star is news worthy indeed. That brings me to my next subject... "Just where do Johnny Glamour's loyalties lie?" Everyone seems to want to know if I will end up with my supposed "mentor" Magnus Colby and his Widow Makers... If I will end up with those rugged and tough hillbillies who seem to have a similar love for conning, I mean earning a buck, the Outlaws and their stable, Organized
Chaos... Or does my biggest fan, in more than one way, Calista Fairchild, have some crafty mastermind plot laid out with her hero, her inspiration, her life, Johnny Glamour. Well, being that I love each and every one of you fans out there, I cannot keep you in the dark any longer. Simply pick up the phone and dial 1-900-4-RIPOFF and select option #7.... Be the first on your block to hear where Johnny Glamour's loyalties lie! [Glamour turns to Bambi and begins to talk...] Hey toots, I've said about all I have to say for the evening, would you like to add anything?
BAMBI-- [In her squeaky and annoying valley girl voice] Like for sure,
Johnny... Like oh my god, I just like wanted to say to like call
1-900-4-RIPOFF and select option #1 if you want to hear all about what like turns me on and stuff... Like I'll be waiting naked for your call and like you can bet like we can talk about anything that like "pops up."
JOHNNY GLAMOUR-- [With a wise guy smile] Well on that note, I guess it is about time to wrap this thing up so we can get to our hotline. People, the
Glamour for the Gold" era in UEW is upon us... And believe you me, it is more than just this talk show... Johnny Glamour is declaring war on every title holder and before too long, will be wearing _all_ the gold in UEW. You can trust me on that. Until the next time, this is your hero, your life, your inspiration, this is Johnny Glamour signing off.
================================================== From: Real Deal Shawn Harrison Subject: none given [ Someone whistling "Mack the Knife" is heard. "Real Deal" Shawn Harrison steps into the scene swinging his car keys off a long keychain. He's wearing a backwards Princeton cap, an Absolut wrestling t-shirt, black mesh shorts and nike sandals. Oh yeah, forgot to tell you people he's still in Hawaii. He's in the hotel lobby of the Honolulu Hilton. ]
HARRISON- I must say, this little island is one helluva spot. Ya got your women, your nightlife and your sun. It's almost as good as Venice Beach... but then again, nothing is as good as Venice Beach. Ya just might be wonderin', why is Shawn still in Hawaii?
[nods] Well, ya see, I kinda didn't want to leave yet. Hell, there's supposed to be this federation opening up here and I want to see those losers in action before I fly back to the mainland. Plus, I wanna stay as far away from that mentally unstable person we all love to call Jeff Matthews. He's a bit miffed if you hadn't noticed. I just got off the phone with him and he's a wreck.
Can someone please tell him that his life ain't over? Then again, some people take this way too seriously. Hey Jeff, if you lose.. you lose. Just get over it and we can like go on with stuff. I've talked to Nick and he feels the same way. Yeah, you were screwed royally once again, but aren't ya used to it yet?
[smirks] I mean, somehow, I managed to accept the fact that Chris Tyler got a win against me. Well, seeing as it took Malloy and his damn bodyguard to take me out of the match and you so conviniently fell on top of me for the pin. But that's that.. I can care less. You see, I have my eyes on something else. Oh yeah, but I ain't telling you people a damn thing. You can put two and two together and figure it out. I'm sure you rocket scientists can do it.
[Suddenly that same brunette Harrison met on the Springer show pops into view. She's wearing a white tanktop and really short shorts and those damn flip-flop slippers. She locks arms with Shawn.]
Take a look at this piece of ass, UEW. [An embarassed look spreads on her face as Shawn smacks her in the butt.] This is what I've been _doing_ in Hawaii. I betcha no other wrestler had this much fun. They all had matches to take care of. Not me. I was up in the Prince
Takealeake suite of the Honolulu Hilton gettin' jiggy wit' it. Doing things that would get me arrested in Utah. I mean, I don't understand how those mormons do it. It's like illegal to do anything in that state. Ah well...
[ Harrison grabs her by the hand and starts to walk off away.] People, it's been a blast I suppose. I'll see ya when I see ya. And if not.. [ Winks towards the camera.] ...well, you know where to find me. Because I'm the real deal... and you're not.. [ Flashes the grin ] Deal with it! [fade] =============================================== From: Widow Maker Magnus Colby Subject: Colby struggles with defeat (fade up to Colby on the familiar ledge where he did his flash that Tyler parodied into a video. Colby is standing looking over the edge. The camera pans up to the ledge a little further up the mountain. Colby however is _not_ looking upward. He is just staring off into the distance.)
Camera Man: Mr. Colby. Mr. Colby. Magnus. (At the sound of his name, Colby turns and gives an icy stare in the camera man's direction.)
Camera Man: Mag.... er Mr. Colby, the UEW sent me to get a few words from you about the new UEW American champion Chris Tyler. Can you say a few words about him and talk about your match from Dreams?
(Colby turns and ignores the question. He again starts staring off into the distance.) Camera Man: Um... ok. You don't want to talk about that huh? Well how about the new NA? What about Alex Martinez?
(Colby turns to face the camera man. A cold hatred is visibile on the face of
Colby. He glares at the camera for a long time. Then he starts to turn again.)
Camera Man: Please sir... Colby: Would you please quit running your mouth? I am sick of all you damn camera men and reporters following me around. Do you know why they call me the Widow Maker?
(Camera shakes up and down as the camera man anxiously nods his head yes.) Colby: Are you aware that I could end your miserable existance? (Camera shakes up and down again.) Colby: I am sick and tired of being followed. This place is supposed to be where I go to think about things.... to clear my head. I promise the UEW will hear from me. Just not from here. Shut that thing off and get out of my face.
Camera Man: But... (Colby grabs the camera man and the camera. He throws the camera over the edge. Fade to snow.)
