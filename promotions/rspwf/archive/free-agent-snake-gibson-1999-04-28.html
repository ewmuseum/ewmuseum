[FREE AGENT] Snake Gibson

28 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SNAKE GIBSON, the self-proclaimed "wrestling's original party animal," is now available (non-exclusive). A sample introductory RP is attached. Interested fedheads should contact Snake at
[detail withheld] . _______ <I>[The opening shot is a long view down a deserted beach somewhere in the
Caribbean, bathed in shades of orange and red as the mammoth red sun sinks into the water. Crystal green waters lick at the sand in slow, steady waves, and a slight breeze whispers through a stand of palm trees. The camera closes on the stand of palms, revealing a hammock strung between two trees. A half-empty margarita rests on a bamboo table, along with a copy of Mark Twain's FOLLOWING
THE EQUATOR. A red-and-yellow flowery shirt is draped across the table, too.
Lying in his hammock, barefooted and tanned in cut-off jean shorts and an
Atlanta Braves baseball cap, is SNAKE GIBSON. He has a tiny color TV on his belly, the orange extension cord and cable spilling down to the sand and winding behind one of the palms. He turns to look at the camera.]</I>
SNAKE: Is this livin'...or what? <I>[He reaches over and flips the TV's power button OFF. He places the TV on the bamboo table and rolls over, sitting up in the hammock.]</I>
SNAKE: Braves were losing anyway. You know, nothing refreshes a man quite like packing a few choice belongings and a pair of flip-flops into a sack and running away to someplace warm. It's invigorating, nature's way of re-charging the human batteries. Fill 'er up with the sun, the company of a few oil-slathered honeys, and whatever high-octane beverage you can get your hands on.
<I>[Picks up the margarita and drains the glass.]</I> Yep - a little R&R makes me ready to head on back up to the States and start kicking a little ass. It's not all baseball, sun-tanned cuties and booze around here, you know. I've been giving some serious thought to what's been happening in the life of ol' Snake here lately. Seems like everywhere I go, the competition gets jittery and starts dropping like flies; I'm left challenging the ring crew's Mamas just to get a match. Or the promoters spend a little too much money out in Vegas, their checks start bouncing, and one day, the arena doors are bolted. Damn shame...
<I>[Steps down out of the hammock, slips his feet into a pair of flip-flops and grabs his red-and-yellow flowered shirt, slips it on and starts buttoning it.
He looks into the camera.]</I> Now I'm taking it easy...and I'm still getting a call every few hours, begging me to come back...again...and join up with this outfit or that outfit. Every fed says they've got a bunch of bad-asses up there, stomping around, puffing out their chests and calling themselves champions. Whatever...
I debated it, really had a heart-to-heart with myself. I said,"Self...do you really want to go all the way back up there, only to discover you'd have more competition leg-wrestling the 72-year-old Jamaican lady who cleans your cabana?" And Self said, "Is the money green?" So...I decided, what the hell?
After all, you're looking at the Sun God, baby! The Sultan of Surf, and the
Wizard of Whoop-ass! This is my turf, and I don't like leaving it unless it's worth my while. I'm the original sandbox brawler; I don't know how stomping around some dollar-a-holler indy that's lucky to half-fill a middle school cafeteria is going to sit with me. But if the money's right...as long as the checks clear...I'll give it a shot.
<I>[Grabs the TV, unhooks the cable, snatches the extension cord out of an unseen outlet, and picks up the book and the margarita glass.]</I>
So, all you quote-unquote champions, battan down the hatches. I'm catching the next flight out. No plane on Sunday; maybe be one come Monday. But when I get up to the States, I expect a little competition to be waiting for me. Think you boys can handle that?
<I>[SNAKE turns and walks up the beach, into the setting sun, dragging the long orange extension cord behind him…as the shot FADES TO BLACK.]</I>
