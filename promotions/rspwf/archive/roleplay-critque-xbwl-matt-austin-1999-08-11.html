[ROLEPLAY/CRITQUE/XBWL] Matt Austin

11 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
One guy featured a day is probably the best idea to do this...we do an open flash system, so I can just pick out who I want from those who roleplay played that day, and display on them on RSPW.
Today, it's Hardcore Champion (the #2 singles title), and perhaps future
World Champ, Matt Austin. Matt just got punked by ex-tag team partner, and current enemy the Beast (who he beat for the Hardcore Title), after defending his title in a barbed wire match. Both guys are acting like tweeners, although the Beast presents himself as a straight face.

>> Matt "Da Mac" Austin: Hahaha... you know, some people just don't know when to quit. Some people just don't know when to shut their god damned mouths.
And some people just don't know when they've been beaten, plain and simple. Brandon Thornton, you know, you might not be so annoying, if maybe your information was correct. You boast about beating me in a six man tag match?
...ok, does anyone else already know where he's wrong? Brandon, go back, and
VERY carefully read, listen to, or brail that fucking statement. You beat me, in a tag team matchup. Do you know what that means? It means you haven't beaten me in singles action, nor will you ever. You see, the biggest difference between me and you is... when I find loopholes, they work, but when you find loopholes, I always shoot them strait down to hell, because
90% of the time, they are wrong. Then we have you saying i'm more annoying than the Rock. Ok, god damn Brandon, do you want to be in another federation that bad? I mean, you mentioned the Rock, HBK, and DDP in your interview, the other day you spouted off about 5 catch phrases from the WWF and WCW, and what's next? I mean, that's really sad, when you have to steal material day in and day out to get along. I mean, it's cool once or twice, but you are WAAAY out there my friend. ... You think, that just because you're having a bad interval with reality that you're undeserving? Oh no, that's not all. You are, without a doubt, one of the saddist sons of bitches that i've ever seen. When you don't get things handed to you, just the way you want, you whine. You whine, you threaten to quit, but you end up staying and hating every minute of it. Which brings me to another point. My threatened quit. You think that was because I was told to lay down in that tag match?
Oh no my friend, that was only a contributing factor. Everyone that talked to me that week, INCLUDING YOU... knows that I wanted to quit for
984789572345 reasons, but no, you've got to put yourself in the spotlight, and think you're actually worth a damn, well, you picked the wrong person to say that to, because I will bitch you out like the stupid whore you are, with enough time left to pin Flyguy and realize my dream, but that's just my opinion, I can't be wrong.... Now... Brandon Thornton has offered a challenge to ANYONE in the XBWL for a shot at his belt... Brandon, normally
I wouldn't do this, but since you decided to run your mouth at me.... _I_ will take that challenge. That is, unless you're too much of a god damned pussy to face me, which i'll bet you are. But no, I couldn't do that. I couldn't stand having to face you AGAIN, and pin you, AGAIN, in the center of the ring, which SO seems to be common fashion today for me. I've got better things to worry about, mainly Beast, and the XBWL World Heavyweight
Champion, Flyguy.
<< Austin scratches his nose. >> Matt "Da Mac" Austin: Beast... I think I made it clear last night, that I don't like you. After a bullshit attack, after one helluva match with Claw, and after being put through hell in a barbed wire match, you felt the need to attack me, and to try and re-establish yourself back into the XBWL field.
Well, it's not that easy Beast. If you want the World Heavyweight Title, and if you're any kind of man that you say you are, you'll face me for the number one contendership. The XBWL needs to understand what they have in me.
Hell, i'm not in the World Title chase yet, but let's look at the facts here. I sell the most merchandise, I get the most cheers, I get talked about the most on the hotline, and I have more yelling and screaming fans waiting for me in the parking deck two hours after the show just to see me walk out.
I'm not a face... i'm a WRESTLER! The fans cheer me, because I don't fuck with them. The fans cheer me because I give them what they want. I'm not a
"tweener", i'm Matt "Da Mac" fucking Austin. When you look at me, that's what you get, and if you don't like it, you can go fuck yourself. The fans seem to like that for some reason.... All I know is, that the XBWL never thought i'd make it. They said "You've got no gimmick, you're never going to get anywhere". I heard the same things from Jack Diamond, Brandon Thornton, and many others. Well, I defeated the odds, I beat all of the assholes who said I couldn't make it, and look who's standing here in front of you, a 2 time XBWL North American Champ, a record-setting International Champion, a tag team champion, and the current Hardcore champion, who beat, arguably one of the best Hardcore champs ever. I'm not bragging, i'm showing my assests in the XBWL. I've been here for 7 months, and i've grown to be more respected than some have over their whole careers. Me, i'm no Hogan wannabe, i'm no Steve Austin wannabe. I'm a Matt Austin wannabe, and i'm doing that one damn good if you ask me. But enough about how the XBWL is stupid, and has no mind, and onto hopefully my opponent this week. Beast, you attacked me, I called you out, what you gonna do now big boy? What's the big bad
Beast gonna do, when bigger and badder Matt Austin comes to whip his ass?
Beast, I beat you for this Hardcore Title, not because you were not 100%, not because you were in jail, not because you were off your game, and not because you, like Andrews showed up one night before the show... no, I beat you for this Hardcore Title, because i'm the better man. And if you want proof. You seem to be alive and well now, so how about a match this week?
How about a match on Prime Time, pending there is one. Or hell, if we have to, we can wait until Monday Mayhem. I could care less. All I know is, I want your ass, and by far i'm not talking about your "bend-over butt plugs".
I'm talking about planting your ass through a table in a hardcore match for this belt. How would a fifth title reign for Beast look? We'll never know, because there won't be one as long as i'm wearing this belt. Beast, the challenge is out there, now it's your turn.

It's a cry for help, from a badly beaten, and badly humiliated chump, who for once in his life realized... that he doesn't have what it takes anymore. Andrews the great, the saviour of the XBWL. Fuck that, Michael Andrews brought the XBWL down.
I mean, HE is supposed to be the best you've ever had? I'll tell you one thing, he's making you look pretty bad. Andrews, why did you run off? Have you lost your mind? Have you actually found a woman? No, not Mike, he couldn't do that. To Andrews, it's like this... Wrestling is money, money is time, and time... time is wasted trying to think of new things to say in his interviews. Andrews.. why? Why why WHY do you keep trying? The plain and simple fact is, that you can't hang. You don't have what it takes anymore.
Your "legacy" has been tarnished by the likes of Beast and Flyguy. How could you? The great Michael Andrews that only lost to Iron Yuppy and Hawk
Henshaw. You lost to Beast, Flyguy, and when you come back you'll probobly be a jobber anyway. Michael Andrews used to wow and whoo the fans with his great speeches that made no sense, but now, now they're just annoying. The fans boo you out of arenas, and you wondered why. You thought it was because you "turned your back" to them. Well, in some ways it was. When not looking at you as a hero, they looked at you in a different light, in the RIGHT light, and they saw what you truly are, and that is a loser. You can't get it done. You couldn't then, and you can't now. The great Andrews will once again come back, he will try to make a big shabang, but it will end in disgust, entirely because he's involved. Andrews, this isn't the XBWL of yesteryear. You can't just come out here, make long speeches about coffee houses and how great you are, to get over. I'm sorry, but that just doesn't keep the ball rolling. Your in ring talent. Some could contribute that as to why you have such an illustrious career. Well, your talent is, by far, supremely lackluster compared to mine. Andrews, when you come back, don't make any plans... except ME.
