#1 Sample Mark Slade RPs for all of you out there asking for it

27 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Recently a couple of people have had wrestlers who have just won a title for the first time have been posting here at proclaiming that their feds title prove that they are the best. When I point out their flaws they say "well why don't you post an rp to back up your talk" so here they are. Mark Slade's first 4 rp's for the I-Crown tournament are here. These will give you a good idea of his character.
===================================================== Job 17:1 "My spirit is broken, My days are extinguished, The grave is ready for me." [The scene opens outside a ramshackle bar on the outskirts of a small Texas town. The sun beats down as a soft breeze blows stirring up dust clouds and pushing tumbleweeds along. The parking lot of the bar is deserted except for a dusty red porsche with a smashed front end. The porsche is parked at a crooked angle taking up two parking spaces directly in front of the bar. It looks like it's been parked in the same spot for a couple of weeks. Empty alcohol bottles are scattered on the floorboards of the car. Inside the bar the air is a stale combination of dust and alcohol. The smell of cigarette smoke permeates the air. An old jukebox sits in the corner playing an old rock song. Tables and chairs are spread around the room, all empty. An older heavyset man stands behind the bar wearing a red flannel shirt, blue jeans and a Dallas Cowboys hat. He is slowly washing glasses and humming along with the music. Sitting across from him at the bar is another man. He is wearing a dirty black t-shirt and worn black jeans.
His silver colored hair is greasy and matted to his head. He sits on his stool slumped over with a bottle of Jack Daniels in front of him and a glass in his right hand. His head rests on his left hand as he sits motionless with his eyes closed. He slowly raises the glass to his lips and drains it in a long drink. He reaches over for the bottle to pour another glass, but the bottle is empty. He slowly opens his eyes and stares at the bottle. His eyes are bleary and bloodshot, but underneath they are an icy blue color. He mumbles to himself incoherently and slowly straightens himself up.
He reaches into his back pocket and pulls out a few crumpled bills. He slowly fumbles through them and tosses a few on the counter. When he starts to speak his speech is slurred and drunken]
Mark: Hey Jesse, let me get another bottle of Jack here. Jesse: Geez, Mark it's not even four o'clock yet. Don't you think you ought to slow it down. The way you've been going lately your going to kill yourself.
Mark: [Mumbles under his breath] Maybe that's what I need. [Out loud] Just give me another bottle alright? Jesse: Okay, you got it. [Jesse picks up a bottle of Jack Daniels and places it on the counter in front of Mark. He picks up the money off the counter and walks over to the register. As he rings it up he stares at Mark in the mirror behind the bar. Mark slowly unscrews the top of the bottle and fills his glass. Jesse shakes his head slowly and turns toward Mark.]
Jesse: Why are you doing this to yourself? Mark slowly turns his head looking around the room with a confused look on his face. Mark: [In a sarcastic voice] Oh i'm sorry. I thought I was in a bar. My mistake. I'll just... Jesse: Oh save it. You show up here out of the blue for the first time in five years. Haven't I been letting you crash in the room upstairs for the last couple of weeks no questions asked? Did I give you any crap about you drinking again?
Mark: I'm sorry Jesse, it's just that...I don't know what happened. One minute everything is going great and the next my whole world is crashing down around me and there was nothing I could do.
Jesse: What happened? The last I heard you were wrestling with Keith Kelly and Terry Jenkins. [Mark gets a faraway look on his face and is silent for a few seconds. When he speaks it's in a slow and deliberate tone of voice.] Mark: That was a long time ago when we were in the ICCW as The Cadre. The three of us were something back then. No one could touch us. Until
Keith injured his knee and Terry...[Mark's voice wavers] well Terry had to retire. I was the only one left. It didn't stop me though. I went on to win the heavyweight title a couple of times. I was at the top and then the whole thing folded. The ICCW closed and left me out in the cold. I started looking for a new federation, but it was hard. Everyone knows that I don't kiss @$$ for anyone. I guess word had gotten out about how I bumped heads with the staff of the ICCW, because no one wanted to hire me right off. They wanted me to jump through hoops and play their games, but I don't go for that.
Then came my big break. At least that's what I thought at the time. The
NeCW had decided to hire me. I figured that this was it. I was finally getting my shot at the big time.
[Mark clenches his fist and his face starts to form into a scowl.] Mark: I remember the first time I stepped into the NeCW locker rooms. Everyone there knew who I was and they were scared. You could smell their fear in the air. They knew that I wasn't going to play the games there. I was coming to fight and I wasn't going to take any rookie crap or just stay in the background silently. I started hearing them whining and crying about newcomers having to "pay their dues", but I didn't give a damn. I said from the start that I was looking forward to beating on all the so called "superstars" there. Kain, Jinsei Shakanuzi,Jim Lewis
Jr. and the rest of those fools.
Most of all I was looking forward to getting ahold of that punk "Too Cool" Chris Hopper. I called him out from day one and he ducked me. He tried to hide behind his title and pull strings with the president so that he wouldn't have to face me. I was ready to show the world what the main man could do and then suddenly the NeCW closed.
[Mark slams a fist on the bar.] Mark: I never asked for anything from anyone. I don't need any favors. The only thing I wanted was a chance, but I couldn't even get that. The
NeCW said that they would give me a chance. They said that they would guarantee me a contract. Then they shut down right away. They knew I was depending on the money they promised me. They knew that they were going to be closing soon, but they lied to me and screwed me over. I went into debt and had bills I couldn't pay. I couldn't get signed by any other feds because I told them all to screw themselves when it looked like the NeCW was going to pick me up. I was already signed to face John Smith at the WCoW so I figured that my last chance would be to make an example out of him and show the world what I could do. If I did that then I would be picked up by some fed and get things back together, but that whole match bombed and Smith bailed on me. Then I got the phone call about Terry.
[Mark stops talking and takes a long drink. When he starts talking again his voice is quieter.]
Mark: They said he was in the hospital. You see the reason he retired was because he had aids. It got worse and now they say he hasn't got to much time left. I was going to see him, but I...I couldn't do it. I just sat on my couch and thought about everything. It all hit me at once and I thought that I really wanted a drink.
Mark stops and stares down at the bar. Mark: Do you know that I hadn't drank in five years. Ever since that night when...when I got in that accident and that mother and her little girl was...well you remember.
Jesse: Yeah, I remember. [Jesse pauses for a second thinking] That was all settled back then though. You went to court and were found not guilty.
Mark: Come on Jesse you saw me when I left that night. I was drunk as hell. It was all my fault. I was driving and I didn't see the stop sign. I didn't even see the other car until it was too late. I had money back then and was able to call in some favors and got off, but I always knew it was my fault. I quit drinking after that, until I got that phone call about Terry. So I gave in. I got drunk that night and the next night and so on. One night I decided to just leave. I just wanted to get away from everything. I got in the porsche and took off.
I don't know what I was thinking, but I just had to get away from it all. I just drove off, not even paying attention to where I was going. Of course deep down inside I knew that I would wind up back here. This is the only place left where I was really happy at.
Isn't that sad? Now that i've been here though it's just the same thing. I'm empty inside. I don't know what i'm going to do now. It's over.
[Mark drops his head into his hands and shakes his head slowly] Mark: [whispering] I didn't have anywhere else to go. I have nothing left. I just can't go on anymore.
Mark stops speaking and sits at the bar not moving. Jesse opens his mouth to say something but can't find the words. He just shakes his head sadly and walks off.
