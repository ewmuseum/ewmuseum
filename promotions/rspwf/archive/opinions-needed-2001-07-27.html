Opinions Needed...

27 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am in the process of creating a new character to run with. The problem is, I have too many damn ideas in my head to pick just one. Let me cut to the chase,
I'm going to put down my ideas and I would like any opinions I could get on each character and which would be the best.
Here we go... "The Pinnacle" Brody Anderson: I first thought of this character in [name withheld]'s HWA. I was never able to get started with the character due to other time consuming activities.
Brody Anderson is a Shane Douglas style character. Think of Douglas ECW time. The dirty blonde curly hair hanging down past the neck line. He is filled with confidence and is the type of character that knows who the hell he is. And he knows that he is more than you can handle.
Society 1: I am currently using this character in OLW. And I am having remotely good comments about the character and I am starting to like what I am producing with him. Society 1, or S 1 for short, is a mind rapist. He toys with your mind by almost mimmicking your actions. He is comparable to a chameleon. Ever adapting to his surroundings, allong the way mawling whatever steps in his path to his ultimate goal...to die.
Gabriel Cash: I have always wanted to run a cowboy style character. And Cash is the man who fits the bill for me. Texas native, Gabe Cash is the epitome of the popeye complex "All I am is all I am, all I am is me." Fist in your face attitude type of guy who ALWAYS shoots from the hip'.
Santino Martinez: More of a personal character. Santino is a suave, shoot style fighter. He is quick to act and even quicker to shoot you down where you stand. Born in Los Angeles, native to the Oakland/San Francisco bay area.. Basically, he has stood up against the odds his entire life and he'll be damned if he will let anyone get in his way of climbing the ladder in the sport. His main attribute?
Heart. Fights every fight as if it were his last, and stands his ground no matter what the circumstances may be.
Well, only four. But after writing all that stuff I've come up with two more. Any reply will be appreciated -Kade *God, hurry before I think of 6 more...
