[Flash] "Snake Eyes" Eddie Jacobs --> Critque, Please

8 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jawohl boards, Its that Obscure guy. op, I'm not fishing for votes. In fact, Don't vote for me. I suck! Rushing even said I did :) But please tell me what parts of this flash did suck, I'm open to improving my character whenever I can.
Thanks. ================================ [The camera opens on total darkness. The only reason we know it's recording is that we hear the noise of people talking loudly, with faint music playing in the background.]
[As the music begins to pick up, te talking dies down, and eventaully the music dies down, too. Finally, someone pulls his hand from the camera lens, and we can see what's going on.]
[The hand belongs to Little Brandon. We appear to be in a small concert hall in Lexington, Tennessee. And Brandon is guiding the camera man through some people in the small backstage area, to a door down the hall with a star taped to it.]
[Little Brandon stops in front of the door, and we hear a mattress squeaking loudly. Little Brandon knocks softly on the door, and calls out.]
LB: "Eddie!! The camera crew from INWI is here. They want some comments about Tony Houston to air on 'Inside The Ropes'."
[The squeaking stops momentarily. Jacobs calls out.] EJ: "Tell 'im ta go shove his thumb where the sun don't shine. Ah haven't done Inside The Ropes in months. A World Champ doesn't belong on
Inside The Ropes." LB: "Eddie, he has a slip from DeLuise saying it's a five thousand dollar fine to you if you don't talk."
EJ: "Which DeLuise?" LB: "Both." [We hear a loud sigh come from inside the room. Finally, after hearing some more noises from the room, we here from inside.]
EJ: "Come in." [With that, Little Brandon slowly twists the knob, and stumbles into the room along with the camera man. The camera man spends a few seconds adjusting to the scene.]
[Clothes and bottles are everywhere in the small room. Eddie is laying back in a bed, with one hand behind his head and the other grasping for a bottle on the nearby nightstand. Jacobs has a smirk on his face, but no shirt on.]
[Also with the blanket pulled relatively high to her neck is Sandra Apples, sitting up in the bed. Sandra has a box of Camels in her lap, and is lighting one as Eddie finally reaches the bottle. He pulls the bottle over
to him, resting it on his chest, before he begins in a low, Southern drawl.] EJ: "Well, Ace, ya gone an' done it. Ruined a private moment wit' me an' 'Mommy' here. Whatta ya got ta say fer yerself?" LB: "I'm sorry, Eddie. Really, Dad, I am. But I just figured... big match ahead... plus five thousand dollars on the line...."
EJ: "Jus' shut yer trap an' leave it at that, Ace." [Eddie adjusts himself slightly, turning towards the camera.] EJ: "Now. Mr. Camera Man. Bustin' in on me wit' somethin' that a low life like Houston could never attain; somethin' that a crack head like
Apples could never hold onto. Normally, this is where Ah'd tell ya that this better be good, but Ah guess Ah should be the one makin' this good."
"After all . . . " [Eddie nods to Sandra, chuckling. Sandra glances back quickly, but goes back to reading to cigarette pack.]
EJ: "After all . . . Ah'm the one who got interrupted, eh, Ace?" [Eddie grins again as he unscrews the bottle of what appears to be burboun. Evan Williams.]
EJ: "Now, Ah don't want ta be tellin' ya how its done or nothin', but Ah've had more'n my fair share 'a gold thrown my way. Hell, Ah'm the current INWI World Champ. My idea is, 'till Van Strife beats ME, that belt is mine."
"Like Ah was sayin', Ah've been up an' down this road an' that, an' Ah've run wit', Ah guess some 'a the bigger dogs 'a the sport. But Ah tell ya what really burns my ass, Ace. Guys who like ta talk tough, wit' their fancy Tommy Hill clothes, an' their big puffy Fubu jackets, tryin' ta out style the guy to death."
"An' most 'a the time is, these guys are the talkers. The guys content ta yammer on 'bout this, that, an' the other thing, but when they step 'tween the ropes an' get their ass handed to 'im, they jus' sit back an' bitch an' moan."
"Well, they can do all 'a that, but my Tony Houston Decoder Ring tells me that this l'il kid is all talk an' no walk."
"Tony, yer the longest INWI Champ in history. Ya went seven months solid. Congrats, Tony my boy, but there comes a time when ya gotta put the old out ta pasture. An' then shoot 'em in the head."
[Eddie chuckles at the thought.] EJ: "Ya know, if ya don't mind, Ah'd like ta quote my most _favourite_ American Poet, Jim Morrison, when Ah say . . . "The old get old, And the young get stronger." "See, Tony, the old in that l'il notion is you. While yer agin' up in New
Yawk City, beggin' for quarters and bummin' rides offa the Central Park
Junkies, Ah stay down South. Where it's warm all year."
"Ah train, an' Ah think 'bout INWI's current state of affairs, an' frankly, Ah jus' can't help but laugh. 'Cause the INWI has been goin' downhill ever since Setz Strife pinned Apples in that Triangle Match an' took
_ MY _ belt."
"An' Ah jus' wanna make sure Ah'm perfectly clear, Setz, when Ah say
_ my _ belt. Yer jus' loanin' it, an' pretty soon Ah'll have ya pay for it
wit' blood." [Eddie gets another grin on his face, and then tilts his head back, taking a few gulps from the burboun bottle.]
EJ: "Tony, Ah jus' want ya to know that no matter how rough, how tough, or how full 'a stuff ya think ya are, Tony, there's always someone better.
An' you can take that to the bank, Ace." [Eddie thumps his fingers rythmatically on the neck of the bottle.] EJ: "All Ah'm sayin' is this, Tony. Gamlin' man. Yer rollin' some deadly dice, Ace, an' pretty soon they'll be comin' up . . . "
" S N A K E E Y E S . " " D E A L E R T A K E A L L . " [With that, the camera turns to static for a second, and then abruptly goes black.] The preceeding words of wisdom were spewed out from your friendly neighbourhood superhero: ~ Joseph B. * Obscure Handler of 'Snake Eyes' Eddie Jacobs [INWI, IWE] * Former INWI World Champ * Former NEW World Champ
