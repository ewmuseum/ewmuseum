Blatant Self-Promotion: Masters posts an old RP

8 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I dunno...just thought I'd throw one of my oldies out there and see what people think of my work. Critique if you will, flatter me if you can, chastise me if you must...whatever:
[The sound of a basketball being dribbled on an asphalt playground assaults the ear as the camera fades in on an elementary school playground. A young lad in the 8 - 10 year old range (can't tell exactly with kids these days, they grow up so fast) is dribbling a basketball with the amount of skill you'd expect from an average, nondescript boy of his age. The boy is wearing denim shorts, a San
Antonio Spurs Jersey (that his parents no doubt bought for him the day after they won the NBA championship), little kid-sized FILA shoes, and a
Yankees hat on his head.]
[Suddenly, as though this were some scene out of a movie, the theme from the film "The Natural" strikes up. The boy gets an inspired look on his face as he looks up at the basket and begins to dribble around imaginary defenders...]
KID: NBA finals...game seven...Spurs down by 2 with 4 seconds left on the clock. [Kid tosses the ball into the air and catches it.] KID: Duncan inbounds it to young rookie sensation John Wolverbeck...he dribbles outside the three point line... 3... 2... 1... HE SHOOTS AT THE
BUZZER! [Clang.] [The theme to "The Natural" cuts off abruptly.] [The ball bounces square off the front side of the rim. The kid has missed the "game winning shot." Undaunted, he picks up the rebound...]
KID: But he gets the rebound with time remaining...dribbles outside the line and lines up the shot... 3... 2... 1... HE SHOOTS! [Not even close. The kid has just uncorked the biggest air ball in the history of this great sport. He is, however, not discouraged. He chases down the ball, scoops it up, runs back to the court, inbounds it to himself, and tries again.]
KID: Wait, hold on.... John Wolverbeck gets the pass from David Robinson...he lines up the winning 3 point shot... 3... 2... 1... [Not surprisingly, the shot misses as it pounds off the side of the backboard and falls to the ground harmlessly.]
KID: Wait a minute...the referee has blown his whistle.... HE IS CALLING FOR A DO-OVER! INCREDIBLE! You don't see that call very often, but considering the viciousness of the foul that Grant Hill put on John
Wolverbeck you can't really blame the referee for making this call.
Robinson will inbound it again, and you can bet he's going to be looking to Wolverbeck... Wolverbeck breaks free of the double team! He gets the pass! Clock ticking down... 3... 2... 1... HE SHOOTS AT THE BUZZER!
[Surprise, surprise. Once again the shot is off and it bounces off the side of the rim. However, this time the kid doesn't have a chance to run down the rebound, as it is scooped up by a leaping large man dressed in dark blue jeans and an ICW T-shirt commemorating the 2nd annual
Summer Sizzler Pay Per View Event. It is quickly recognizable as Mike
Masters, the Fightingest Champion in ICW today. The kid is understandably surprised and, after Mike trots out and easily hits the three point shot himself, the kid is quite impressed as well.]
KID: Wow mister, how'd you do that? MIKE MASTERS: You really ought not to talk to strangers, kid. Just play your little game and I'm sure that if you give yourself enough chances that you'll eventually get lucky and hit that shot.
KID: Really? Hey, you look familiar... MIKE MASTERS: I should. Gaze in breathless wonder child, for you are in the presence of Wrestling Superstar Mike Masters.
KID: Wow! A real live professional wrestler! Hey, is D.B. Bugg or the
Masked Superstar Jobber here too? They're my favorite and I'd really like their autograph.
[Masters, with an exasperated look on his face, glares at the kid.] MIKE MASTERS: [More than a little annoyed.] No, they aren't. I happen to be here by myself, filming a segment for ICW Television...note the camera person over there.
[Masters points to the camera, which the kid didn't seem to notice before judging by the awestruck look of surprise on his face.]
MIKE MASTERS: I'm here to discuss the merits...or lack thereof...of
Kenneth Morlock and explain to him _ONCE AGAIN_ that he just simply isn't good enough to win this match we've got coming up. And in the process, I've just ensured that you'll be on TV, kid. How does that make you feel?
KID: WOW! [The kid starts jumping up and down and practically soiling himself with joy.] MIKE MASTERS: So now who's your favorite ICW superstar, huh? KID: [Calming down briefly] You, Mike! I want to be like you when I grow up! I wanna be like Mike!
MIKE MASTERS: [To the camera:] An excellent marketing tag line if I ever heard one. Be like Mike...buy his shirt. Maybe now that some 10 year old kid has done their thinking for them, the ICW marketing wing can finally getting around to producing a Mike Masters T-shirt for all the trend-following sheep to plunk down their hard-earned for. [To the kid:] So, who do you think is going to win the big title match this
Friday...Me, or Kenneth Morlock?
KID: Oh, you're going to lose! I mean, the guys fighting in his hometown for one... and look at the skill the guy has! I mean, he's got that saying... PRETTY PLUS POWER EQUALS ME! You? You don't even have a
catch-phrase! He's got you beat there! [It's hard to tell whether the child's ungrateful insolence or his naive foolishness is the source of the disgusted look on the face of Mike
Masters, but he certainly is very upset.]
MIKE MASTERS: A catch phrase? You think he's going to win because he has the better catch phrase?
KID: How else do you win matches? [Masters buries his head in his hands and weeps for the future of America, for with uneducated children like these destined to run the country one day, there truly is no future.]
MIKE MASTERS: Come on Riggins, we're leaving. KID: What did I say? Am I still going to be on TV? [Masters ignores the kid and walks away, followed by the camera man. The kid resumes his imaginary Game 7 and begins to take shot after failed shot in an attempt to win the game at the buzzer. When Mike has walked a good distance away, he looks into the camera, disgusted frown on his face, and he speaks:]
MIKE MASTERS: How else does one win a match, indeed! [Masters turns his head back to look at the kid, as if trying to verify that yes, there really is a misinformed boy playing basketball back there and he did just tell Mike Masters that catch-phrases win matches.]
MIKE MASTERS: The sad thing is that many more people in this nation, and indeed this world, have been conditioned to think along similar lines.
Born of ignorance, raised on illusions, and living in a world of nurturing fantasy that makes it comfortable to remain blind and deaf to the simple, uncomfortable truths of our world.
You're a lot like that child, Kenneth Morlock. For starters, you have the mind of a child. You're a cretin. You're an imbecile. You're "a little slow." You are, after all, from Tennessee. I'm smarter than you, and will outsmart you during the course of our match this Friday, and I'll do it again at Summer Sizzler, leading myself to victory in both cases...but then, you already know all that, or at least, you should.
You've been living in a fantasy world all week. Longer than that, actually, but especially this week. Again, just like that boy. This
Title match...it is to you what that Game 7 with its 3 seconds that last forever are to that child. It's his dream, it's his fantasy. As it is his fantasy, he is able to shape its physical laws. He can play those three seconds over as often as he wants. He can take as many tries at that buzzer beating shot as he wants. It is, after all, only in his head that that game exists.
[Camera pans over to and zooms in on the kid making more attempts to sink that game winning three pointer. It lingers on him for several seconds before focusing on Masters once again.]
That's you, Morlock. This is your dream match, your chance to make the home town crowd cheer and chant your name...but there is one crucial difference. And, even though I think it's fairly obvious what that difference is, I remember that you come from Tennessee, so I'll have to spell it out for you anyway:
[Masters speaks in a slower, deeper voice. The kind of voice you'd use when trying to explain something to a senile old man, or the functionally retarded guy employed by your local McDonalds, or in this case a person from Memphis, Tennessee.]
This is no fantasy. [Masters drops the Moron-Friendly voice and continues normally.] This match is, pardon the pun, "The Real Deal." This is no figment of your imagination. You really have this match, and you really can get really lucky and become Continental Champion for real instead of just pretending that you've won a championship, like that kid over there.
The downside is, though, that as this match is real, it is governed not by the laws of your fantasy world, but by the physical laws of the real world. There are no do-overs here, Morlock. When you go for the winning shot and miss...I will not stop the clock, hand you the ball, and let you shoot again. I will punish you. I will hurt you. I will grab hold of your arm or leg and I will torture you. Only in your dreams will you have the chance to win this match, and when the bell rings I will pull you out of your dreamlike slumber and leave you to suddenly wake up in the midst of a real-life nightmare...the nightmare in which
Mike Masters, the greatest mat-wrestler of them all, has a hold of a weakened part of your body and is about to do something horrible to it that will make it hurt like nothing has ever hurt you before. And then, you'll look down helplessly at your separated shoulder, or your wrenched knee, or if you're really unlucky, your broken leg...and you'll know what I and everyone else in the ICW knows in the bottom of their hearts...
...Here's a nice little catchprase for all you Philistines in TV land...Against Mike Masters
YOU... CAN'T... WIN! [Camera zooms in on Masters' face and the wicked grin that adorns it, just before fading to black.]
