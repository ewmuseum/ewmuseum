"The Reaper" Daniel Death rp. Read and tell me what ya think.

25 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is not so good. It is a pretty bad rp of mine actually. But the only one
I have right now.
[...Fades in...] [^v^v^]...Day after day, I come out and give the world a show. Each time doing my absolute best at what I do. The man I once was, the man known only as the
Reaper, did what he could, and yet was not good enough. But now, the man known as Daniel Death, will do his best, and this time the outcome shall be different. The NcWa is a changed place. What is was, is no more. The feuds, the champions, the real men. They are all gone, or have long time changed.
Now I have set the differences aside, and me and the Reaper have become a team. And with the help of Tim Beck, have become an unstoppable force. And we now begin the long road, that is the destruction of the NcWa and all it's members.
And in the time it took me, to lose the NcWa title the last time, I have yet another chance to regain it. Only this time, I shall not miss my chance...[^v^v^]
[...The camera comes on, and you see a bright orange sky. The sun is setting behind the large mountains, far off in the distance. There is a slight chill in the air. And a low breeze, with makes it nice and cold outside. As the camera pulls back from focus on the sun set, you see you are in a forest filled with trees. Filled with many, many colors. It seems as if, it is fall. Each leave, on each tree, has a different texture to it. Some of the leaves are falling off, and forming a small sea of colors on the dirt ground. You hear as a few birds chirp, and watch as some small squirrels run along the ground, frantically looking for nuts to bring home. For winter is on it's way. The sky now becomes a bit darker, and a few stars can be seen in the sky. The camera turns a bit to the side, and you see a series of colorful trees. As the camera focuses in on them, you see that there are pilled with holes, from birds, and covered in long white spider web. From beyond the tree, you hear a rustle in the leaves. Then comes a deep, dark voice...]
Voice: "The time have changed. As have all the men I once new. Each man a different attitude, each man a new ability. I have watched for many weeks now as you have all tried to destroy one another, now I have come to destroy you all. Not so long ago, I was your leader. The one who stood above you all. But my time had come to an end. So I was brought down, and therefor I had disappeared.
Only to return, and because of problems was forced to leave yet again. The man you knew at that time is no more, the man I am now, is much different."
[...The voice gets closer, then you hear rustle in the bushes. As the camera focuses in, you see a man walk out from behind the series of trees, and trough the thick spider web. He walks over, in front of the camera, and stares directly at it...]
[...He is wearing black pants, with black boots. A black and red shirt, with a leather jacket over it. His brown eyes stare dead into the camera, and there is but one man who stares like that, and that is Daniel Death.... The Reaper,
Daniel Death...]
"The Reaper" Daniel Death: "I have now made my return. You have born witness to yet another rebirth. This one much different from the last. See, as I lost my title to Carnage, in my last battle, I realized, I was not what I thought I was. I have now realized that no matter where you go, no matter what you do, there is always someone better than you are. Soon, a few men shall find this all at. But let me get back to that. Now, with the help of some friends, I am out to destroy the men, that have caused me so much trouble. With the help of Tim Beck and
The Reaper, I intend to destroy WoA. I get my first shot at that, when
Revolution rolls around. This coming against Armageddon. But not only Armageddon. I also must face Deathevn and Gemini at the same time. Great names, yes they are. But I feel I am just as good, if not better. This shall be a match for the ages, this I already know. You need not think twice about this. You need nothing more than to look at the names."
[...Daniel Death pauses, as a heavy wind whips trough the trees. His hair flies up into the air and in front of his face. As leaves spin in circles. He flips his hair back and starts talking again...]
"Sure, I may not have been the best champion the last time. I look at what I was, and do nothing more than shake my head. I admit, I was not fit to be the champion. But things are a little different now. I am able to hold that title, and I will be able to hold that title. Granted, there are great names.
But as it is, I stand at number two. With but Gemini ahead of me. Well,
Gemini has seen what I think of him already. I should have taken the chance when I had it. I could have easily put you out Gemini, I just simply choose not to. For what reason? I do not know. My instinct told me to leave things be. For I had a chance coming. Now I do. The cheap shot would have been nice, but I enjoy the sweet taste of fair victory much, much better. This is a taste I have not had in a long while. Someone must always interfere. I hope no one does in this match, either. I am give you my promise, that none of my friends shall step foot, even on the platform for this match. I want no excuses, I want no cries. This time, I shall walk out fair and square, and with knowing I had beat some of the greatest names in the business."
[...Daniel Death stops, and takes a big deep breath of the cold air. The night is almost upon you. The light slowly goes away, and a few stars can be seen in the sky. There is another low breeze, and a few birds chirp. Death looks back to the camera and starts talking....]
"The night comes slowly upon us, yet so fast it was unnoticeable. Much like myself. I come in slow, and quiet. Unnoticed. And as if nothing, I am all over you. And without knowing, all that you had come for, all the fight, all the stardom, it is all gone. This is what the NcWa is in for. I am a new man, going for the same old title. Something that means much to me. So for all those in my way, and all those who wants be in my way, it is a big mistake.
This time it is going be different. This time, I will be the center of attention, and this time, I will truly be the best."
[...A high breeze picks up, and the leaves circle around Daniel Death. When everything clams down, and the colorful leaves fall to the ground, Daniel Death is no more. All you can hear is the howling of the wind, and the rustle of leaves. Then you hear the familiar sound of Death's voice...]
"I have done many things in my career, and held may titles. Non so great as the NcWa titles. I have tasted that, but once. And I fully intend to taste it again. I am out for the same thing as the others, and plan to fight, much like the others. Gemini, and Armageddon, I feel you two will be on the top of your games, Deathevn, good luck. This will be a war, and the last man standing will deserve nothing more than the right to be called the NcWa champion. All be ready, the revolution has begun!"
[...It is completely black now, and as the Reapers voice slowly fades away, as does the wind and all else. The beautiful forest becomes as quiet as can be...]
[...Fades out...] [^v^v^]...Again I have my chance at greatness. Perhaps this time I will not take it for granted. Many good men will walk into the ring on this day, only one man will walk out. Who this man will be? Only time will tell, my guess would be me. I have nothing to lose, and all to gain. So what's more to be expected? My very best of course...[^v^v^]
