[CRITIQUE] "Blaq Power" Marcus Blaq...tell me what you think...

28 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This one's from IWF/WOW... [The scene fades into "Blaq Power" Marcus Blaq's locker room in ACE. A slightly sweaty Marcus Blaq, his younger brother Shawn, and his entourage are sitting in the locker room, drinking champagne and celebrating Marcus' first win over "X-Man" Dee Brown. Marcus is sitting down on a bench in the locker room wearing his usual ring attire of black pants with "BLAQ POWER" and "BLAQ
ATTACK" written on each leg in white, white knee pads, white boots, and white wrist tape. Marcus also has a white towel draped across his broad shoulders.
Shawn is wearing a pair of khakis, black loafers, and a black turtleneck. Marcus is receiving several pats on the back from his entourage as he takes a bottle of champagne and chugs some of it down. The cameraman looks on as
Marcus refuses to acknowledge his presence. Marcus closes his eyes before finishing his chug and smiling with a satisfied look.]
SHAWN BLAQ - Great job, Marc! You really kicked Brown's booty tonight! "BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Was there any doubt? Brown didn't stand a chance! [Marcus' friends and entourage all nod or say something with a chuckle. Marcus looks around, smiles, and chugs some more of the champagne. Meanwhile, Shawn pulls out a small piece of paper.]
SHAWN BLAQ - Oh yeah...Marc, I just got this fax from IWF/WOW...It's the card for Monday Mayhem....
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Well, give it here, bro.... [Shawn hands Marcus the folded piece of computer paper. The cameraman watches as Marcus unfolds it, looks through it with eyebrows turned down, and folds it up after reading it.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - All right! Boys, I am about to make my I/W debut on MONDAY MAYHEM! [Marcus' entourage say something in response. Marcus receives more pats on the back from his friends and younger brother.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - One question....Who's Instant Karma? Do you know who he is, Shawn?
SHAWN BLAQ - Sorry, Marc. I've never heard of 'im. "BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Huh. Have you guys heard of 'im? [Marcus looks towards his friends who surround him. His entourage responds with a chorus of "No" or "I don't know who he is." Marcus finally looks at the camera with a confident look.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - You fed heads need to start givin' me better matches against better competitors. I don't know why y'all keep stickin' me in matches against guys who I've never heard of...To me, if I ain't heard of a guy, he ain't any good. I hope that I get better opposition soon.
SHAWN BLAQ - Actually, Marc, that fax also has the cards for the rest of the week. You're gonna be facin' "Crippler" Kyle Combs on the 7th at BattleZone and you're gonna be in a Young Guns match on the 9th at Mayhem.
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Kyle Combs? Isn't he retired? SHAWN BLAQ - Apparently not. He's coming out and he's gonna be facing you first, bro. "BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Humph. I get to give him his first loss since comin' back from retirement, huh?
SHAWN BLAQ - Yep. "BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Well, Shawn, I couldn't care less whether or not he's comin' outta retirement or semiretirement or whatever you wanna call it.
All I care about is the win... SHAWN BLAQ - And you ARE gonna win, Marc... "BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Yeah, 'course I am. Do you guys think I'm gonna win against Kyle Combs on BattleZone?
[Marcus turns back to his entourage awaiting a response. His entourage all says "Yes" at one point or another. Marcus turns back to the camera with that same confident look on his face.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Ya see Kyle, I've seen you in action. You're pretty good....Yeah...you're pretty good...but you're NOT great. In fact...you're not even close...It takes a lot to be great...You have to be in the best shape, the best mind set, and the best overall condition out of anyone else in the world.....You've got the shape down cold...but that's ALL you got....I remember you saying, "Greatness isn't a measurement, it's an accomplishment." Well
Kyle, you've got a helluva long way to go...You say that you're gonna devote the rest of your stay in I/W to "rippin' and tearin' your way through the roster"...You say that you're gonna "cripple anyone and everyone that stands in your path"....Well Kyle....I'm first in your path and I ain't gettin ripped, I ain't gettin' torn, and I ain't gettin' crippled....
SHANW BLAQ - You tell 'im, Marc! [Marcus' entourage erupts into a chorus of "YEAH!"s in the locker room. Marcus nods his head at his friends then at the cameraman before speaking once again.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Instant Karma. You're gonna be the first to feel real BLAQ POWER. And you're gonna be the first to feel the BLAQ ATTACK
...Combs, you're next to feel 'em. I'll see both of your asses in the ring soon... [With that Marcus, Shawn, and their entourage walk away from the cameraman and through the locker room door, energy still pumping through the air as his entourage still pats Marcus on the back and screaming in appreciation. The scene then fades to black.]
-The Wush "'D.A.R.E. doesn't stand for 'Drug Awareness Resistance Education.' It stands for 'Drugs Are Really Excellent.'"
