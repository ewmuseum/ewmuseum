Djinn of Ernham looking for a home.

1 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello. I am [name withheld]. I handle a character called Djinn of Ernham. I am looking for a home for my character. Here is a sample of my work.
[Fade in . . .] [Desert area. Somewhere in Turkey . . . Sunny afternoon, a slight breeze blows the Turkish flag to the east. The land is barren. A man stands in the distance, with a sword in hand, looking toward the horizon. The camera revolves around the man to show his arms crossed, right, holding sword and rests the sword over the left.
The man looks to be Arabic. Wearing a red turban that drapes down to mid-back, the wind blows it out a bit. The man stands, emotionless and alert to his surroundings. He is well-built and thick. His chest has some hair, but it isn't prevalent, just noticeable. His face is square-like and emotionless.
His long black hair is tied in a tail and reaches down to shoulder blade. Razor stubble is noticeable on the sides of his face. He has a trademark Arabic beard and a curly mustache. His brown eye is stern. His other eye, his left eye, is covered with a patch.
The man is wearing no shirt, but is wearing baggy red pants with boots to match. The camera shows a front view of him . . . With an Arabic accent, he
speaks:] Man: "I am . . . the master of my palace. Tyranny runs strong in the Arabic countries. Dare I make my stand and show my force? Dare I start a conquest to become a king?" [The camera moves around the man again. The man's attention, not being driven from the horizon.]
Man: "As I dwell deeper into this sport, I find my challenges get greater. People see me, they see they appearance. They see the patch over my left eye seeing that I have no apparent vision from that side. But as it works as a disadvantage, I did not cause it. I did not ask to be attacked with an awl. I did not ask to be blinded forever, in my left eye."
[The camera stops showing the front view of the man, his shadow, large and superior, behind him.]
Man: "When I entered the sport of wrestling, I saw nothing less than opportunity. I wrestled in Asia and dominated the competition. In
SCRA, I see my work enhancing greatly. I am the Djinn . . . the Djinn . . . of Ernham." [Nods] DoE: "In the SCRA, I plan to make an impact, a strong impact. In my years of growing up, I have always dreamed of dominance. I always wanted to go and dominate me challenges, obstacles, and opposition. I always wanted to have fame . . . as much fame as I have fortune.
Ah . . . But it takes work. I realize that. I hope that all people realize that. And therefore, I have readied myself for this work. Now . . . I am prepared. And the SCRA . . . has fallen under the invasion of the
Djinn." [With his head down, he relaxes his arms. He turns away from the camera and heads back toward what looks to be a large palace. As he walks away, he speaks these last few words.]
DoE: "Welcome to my domain." [Fade.] -John M. Handler of: Djinn of Ernham
