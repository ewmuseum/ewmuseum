Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Bastard sits on a toilet in a mall bathroom. He is trying to shit but the turd may be stuck.]
  The Bastard- Damn fuckin' turd!! Fall out of my ass already!   [At that moment "WAR" by Edwin Starr plays and The Bastard bits his lip and grips the bowl.]
  The Bastard- Huh! Ugh! Ah! Ho ho!!! What is it good for?! Absolutely nothing! Huh!   [We here a loud KERSPLASH as the beast of a turd slips out of
The Bastards ass and into the commode.]
  [The Bastard rips his shirt off and whipes his ass with it. Then he stripes down bucked bare ass naked and exits the stall. He goes into the mall and "The Named Mall People" starts to play as everybody in the mall stripes down naked and starts dancing and singing.]
  The Naked Mall People- We are the people!! The people of the maaalll!!! We are so naked!!1 We love to break the lllaaww!!!!
  [A guy takes a shit in the corner of the mall and a fat women laughs her ass off.]   The Naked Mall People- Fuck you Dirty Dick Johnson!!!   The Bastard- That's right, Monday your going down Dirty Dick
Johnson!!!!   [The Naked Mall People back off while The Bastard gets a solo] [The camera fades away as The Bastard is dancing and doing slick moves]
