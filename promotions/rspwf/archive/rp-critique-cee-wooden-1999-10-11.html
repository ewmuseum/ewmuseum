[RP Critique] Cee Wooden

11 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::The Wooden Files V1.0::: - Cee Wooden at a bar in NYC- - Time 8:00- - Sunday- ::: Cee Wooden is sitting at the bar. People are all around watching Football games. Recaps, Sports centers, ect... A commercial for Fox Sports Net GLHW every Monday and Thrusday nights. Some fans notice others just veiw it as another dumb wrestling commercial. Cee has a Beer in hand and the Packers game is on. The bar tender comes over and starts talking to Cee. :::
[ Bar Tender ] Hey Cee, wasn’t that commercial for GLHW. That’s the same wrestling company that you joined up with, shoot man I didn’t it was on cable.
I thought it was one of those small feds. [ Cee ] Yeah, I don’t know how it’s gonna work out though. Man I am just wondering how my life took a turn to this. Working in wrestling, I know I can wrestle alright, I did it in highschool. But that was like 15 years ago, this 30 year old body, I don’t know how its gonna hold up with all this young guys that are in this business now a days. Chris is just not like the old days, where you did a couple of punches a head but then hit a leg drop for a
1..2..3 Its alot more complicated then that. I’ve been trying and training hard, but you and I both now this is not what I would prefer to be doing. I’d rather be back on the force, just beating the crap out of NYC trash.
[ Bar Tender ] Sh*t Cee, half these guys in this bar would kill for a shot that you have right now. On TV wrestling, its a dream come true. You’re telling me you rather be out on the streets back in the blue dealing with drug dealers and criminals risking your life everyday? You’d rather be doing that then traveling the US and wrestling for all the fans out there. Man just look at yourself, just case you were the scape goat on a deal gone wrong doesnt mean that your life is done.
:::There is a shot glass full of tequila infront of Cee, he picks it up and slams it back. He motions for one more, Chris(bartender) pours him another shot and Cee slams that back down too. :::
[ Cee ] (in a grimicing voice) Man being a cop was all I ever knew, my father was a cop, It’s in my blood. I alot has happened from being a cop. Boxing tournements, not to mention the fact that I met my wife through it. Its hard to just walk away from the one thing you have known all your life. Sh*t I haven’t ever even been infront of the cameras yet, who knows if im gonna be any good.
[ Chris ] Man you think the people aren’t going to like you? Your a great boxer, and you played football and wrestling in highschool. Not to mention the fact when you walk down that aisle with that knock of a wife you have, every guy in the audience is gonna mark out for you. You have it to make it big, all you have to do is take this opportunity and turn it into the best descision you have ever made. I mean your a natrual, fans are gonna love ya.
[ Cee ] Maybe your right, guess we will just find out monday night. My first ever match aginst some Drake stevens guy. I’ve talked to him a couple of times, and I think he is a arrogant prick. He thinks just cause I’ve never wrestled before that he is going to walk all over me.
[ Chris ] Well he has another thing coming. Whats your finisher anyways? Something elaborate? Powerbomb? Sh*t i’ve always loved that move. [ Cee ] Nah, just a plain and simple football tackle. Drive my shoulder into their gut, lift them high into the air and plant them on their back. I figure it’s something I can do to anyone at anytime. And you know me, I was a pretty good football player, 7 sacks in one game in highschool. So my tackles sure pack a punch.
[ Chris ] You know Goldberg uses that move, not as his finisher though. [ Cee ] Yeah I know, but not as his finisher, so Im in the clear. :::Cee drops a 20 spot onto the counter, and gets up.::: [ Cee ] Well later chris, watch tomorrow to find out how I did. Man a long ride ahead of me too, up to Ohio area. They don’t even pay for plane tickets thats how cheap they are.
[ Chris ] Can’t expect everything though, good luck man lata. ::: Fade Out :::
