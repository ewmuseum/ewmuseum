Roleplay Critique - "The Professional" James Masters

17 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pretty simple setup here. James Masters is in Pro Wrestling: Complete. He has a match against Alex Oshima. James is a rookie in pro-wrestling, so... yeah.
Read and let me know what you guys think/what I can do to improve. ----------------------------------------------- [Open ... inside a candy store? The hell?] ???: What in the world are we doing here? [Because of the quality of the footage, you have to assume that the camera taping this is pretty crappy. From off-camera, "The Professional" James
Masters strolls in, his face staring blankly.] JM: Just tape, man. ???: I am. JM: What?! The red light isn't even on! ???: Must be burnt out. Would you just get on with this? [James looks disappointed, and backs away a bit.] JM: C'mon, guys. You have to admit, this is a cool ass setting. You don't see the "big boys" in California and Michigan running around doing skits in a candy shop!
???: There's probably a good reason for that. JM: Jesus. And they call you professionals. I'm surprised you two ever got passed the bush leagues.
???: Ahem. You're still on. JM: Oh ... right. [Backing away further, James adjusts his shirt, a button down floral print that only tourists and jackasses would ever wear.]
JM: Hey, folks. James Masters here, but I'm sure you already know that. I
_am_ the most identifiable star this rinky dink organization has going for it.
[James reaches over into a bin full of gumballs and pulls several out, popping them into his mouth one by one.]
JM: Mmmm ... rrrrrrr... [Chewing on five gumballs at once is a chore and a half, and James, while having a big mouth, isn't exactly up to the challenge.]
JM: Rowr. Roo ruch rum! [An audible "slap" can be heard, more than likely by the cameraman. Suddenly, an Asian man stomps into the scene, hands on his hips.]
AM: I HOPE YOU GONNA PAY FOR THAT! [James reaches into his mouth and pulls all the gum out in a giant wad.] JM: You're ruining my shot! Back up, grandpa! Don't you know your store is being used as the setting for a major promotional video? Hell, this could bring in _thousands_ of more customers, overnight!
[The Asian man perks up.] AM: Really? [A big, goofy smile appears on the Asian's face, as he shuts his eyes tightly and makes a peace sign.]
AM: HI MOM! [James rolls his eyes.] JM: Now if you'll excuse me... AM: Oh yes! Very sorry! Continue, sir! [Getting out of the scene, James is left to his lonesome.] JM: Anyway, as I was saying, I'm here in... AM: CHUNG YO'S CANDY CORNER! SECOND AND MAIN! JM: ... ahem. I'm hanging out here at the local candy store, checking out the fly honeys and the totally dope dudes.
???: We're in a candy store. If you're trolling for "honeys", I think we're going to have to call the police.
[James goes several shades redder.] JM: Shut up, B. You're ruining everything! ???: Yeah, James doesn't need the help! JM: YOU TOO, W! ???: Awww, man... JM: Dammit. [Flustered, James walks over to a little girl.] JM: We're running out of freakin' tape, so... Alex Oshima! You see this girl! [James points down at the girl, who is in a pink dress and a pink bow, sucking on a sucker.]
???: I like her dress. That's my favorite color! JM: SHUT UP, B! ???: Grumpy ass... JM: This girl is just like you! [Suddenly, James grabs the sucker right out of the little girl's mouth, shoving it into his own.]
JM: Beating you? Like taking candy from a baby! ???: You're... kidding, right? JM: What? [The little girl has finally overcome her shock, and goes running off, screaming like a banshee.]
???: That's why we're here? To use a stupid cliche? JM: It's not stupid! I'm a professional. I know these things. [A heavyset woman walks into the shot, with the little girl clinging to her leg.] LG: That's him, mommy! HE STOLE MY CANDY! HSW: It's OK, baby. I'll take care of this... [The woman rolls up her sleeves.] HSW: Want to steal from _my_ little girl?! JM: Now ma'am, wait... I can explain... _SLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLAP!_ [James drops to the ground and covers up, crying like a little baby, as the woman starts slapping the shit out of him.]
JM: AHHHH! AHHHHH! TURN OFF THE CAMERA AND HELP ME! [A sigh.] ???: Jesus flippity Christ... [Cut out to black.]
