[FLASH] Critique if you dare.... For this is BeaR!! (that rymes!!)

13 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
$EliTe Establishment$ We have money, power, success, women, happiness, and we all look damn good. That's why we are all 'EliTe' ________________________________________________________________ [^v^v^] Scene opens up with hugh G. Rection sitting down in a chair watching a
TV monitor. He seems to be in a little booth, or room. He has headphones on and everything. He looks up, and sees the camera. He then smiles, and begins to speak. [^v^v^]
Hugh G. Rection: Hello Everyone. I am here at the Boston Fleet Center, where the long lost New Kids on The Block are performing tonight! All of the ladies love them, Joey Macentyre, Jordan Knight, Jon Knight, Danny McCloud , Donnie
Whalberg will all be performing tonight live, on pay per view. For only 420$.
Many celebrities will be in attendance as well. Jennifer Love Hewitt. Sarah
Michelle Gellar. Pamela Anderson Lee. Many wrestling superstars will be in attendence as well. BeaR, KooL Kid, and Fury. Speaking of NcWa Superstars, hopefully Midnight Killer will not be following around Sarah tonight. Damn he is annoying.... Wait I have just gotten word that a big black limo just pulled up to the side entrance of the arena. Let's get a camera back there...
[^v^v^] Scene is changed. We are now looking at the side of the building. The camera is wandering around in different directions. It focuses. You can now see a limo pulling into a garage. The camera man, as well as many news reporters and fans, all chase after the limo. But the garage door slowly shuts. By the time the first person gets to it, it is completely shut. Everyone sighs and walks back up to the front. Scene quickly changes again. You now see three men, wearing EliTe Establishment T-Shirts, sun glasses, and Black jeans. They are all walking towards the camera. The first man takes off his sunglasses. It is
BeaR. The other two men are obviously Fury and KooL Kid. BeaR pushes the camera down, and walks by. the other two are right behind him. The camera man gets up, and tries to follow them, but they walk into a room, and slam the door. Scene changes once more. This time it is back to Hugh G. Rection. [^v^v^]
Hugh G. Rection: I don't know what to get from that. But I would like to know a few things. First off, why is BeaR and the $EE$ here? What business do they have being here? At a New Kids on The Block concert? This isn't there kind of music! I happened to watch NcWa television, and they listen to Songs like
"Pretty Fly for a White guy". Well, I believe that the concert is about to officially begin. Let's see whats happening out on the arena floor....
[^v^v^] Scene goes to the arena floor. There, there is a wrestling ring. Michael Buffer is standing in the middle of it, with a microphone. He begins to announce the New Kids On The Block. They come out one by one. All of them are out except for one man. Jordan Knight. Lights go out. Then They start flashing.
Pretty Fly For a white guy starts blaring over the speakers. the fans' cheers turn quickly to boos. the three men from earlier in the night, walk out to the ring, carrying Jordan Knight over there shoulders. They get into the ring, drop
Knight, and grab a mic. The rest of the New Kids on The Block start giving
Jordan Knight CPR. BeaR begins to speak. [^v^v^]
BeaR: You gay assholes.. He isn't dead. We just severly beat the crap out of him. Why? I'll tell you why. Because his name is pretty damn close to my next opponent's. Jordan McKnight, you New Kid on The Block ripoff... Why would you want to copy this loser's name? ::Kicks Knight:: You see, you Unoriginal bastard, you will always be one step behind me...
[^v^v^] Jon Knight, Jordan Knight's brother, yells at BeaR. Bear then begins to laugh, and speak again. [^v^v^]
BeaR: What? did I hurt your brother? Well Too fucking bad!! It's not my fault he has a gay person looking up to him, Jordan McKnight. Oh, and for yelling at me.... ::Kool Kid, Fury, and Bear all beat the shit out of the New Kids on The
Block:: You see everyone, the real reason I came here was to conduct an interview, about the $EliTe Establishment$. But the Fleet Center owners had to go and rent out the place, the day I was going to use it. To who? The New Kids on the Block? Fuck that. But then I heard about my next opponent being Jordan
McKnight, and then hearing about Jordan Knight being here, and all of the pieces came together. Now, Jordan McKnight, I won't take away from you, your great wrestling skills. I know you are good. ut one question. Are you as good as me? NO! Now McKnight, you have been one of the most annoying men in the NcWa as of late. Demanding title shots. Beaint jobbers, I mean, you have done everything. but now, you got your title shot. what are you going to do with it?
Are you going to fuck up, and get pinned? Are you going to fall victim to Total
Destruction? Or are you going to be the next Intercontinental Champion? You see, The Intercontinental belt means nothing to me. What I really want is the
United States Championship. This will not be the last time you see me before the match, Jordan McKnight. So beware. Because you never know when a BeaR could attack!
[^v^v^] BeaR drops the mic on the floor. Him, Fury, and Kool Kid all walk out of the building. the New Kids on The Block are sitting unconcious in the ring.
Fans are crying and screaming. Screen fades to black. [^v^v^] Ok, there is my first rp for this match, my second one will be out next!!
