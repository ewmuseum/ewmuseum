NAW Roleplay Showcase 1

18 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen to... NAW Roleplay Showcase =================================== "Playboy" Tony Flint =================================== <The camera opens inside of a $3 million wrestling training facility in Beverly
Hills. A muscular bleach blonde is seen working on a few moves with a sparring partner in the black and purple ring. Two big guys (nearly 7 foot and each weighing over 300 lbs) are seen lifting weights in the corner of the building.
A beautiful red haired lady is seen filing her nails in a Gunchfield Co. office chair at her desk. The camera man pans in closer to the wrestling ring where
Playboy Tony Flint has just executed his patented Playboy's Dream on his sparring partner. Flint pins him and then walks away. You can barely hear the sparring parnter mumbling in the background...."Is very good move. Hurt much badly." Tony Flint approaches the camera.>
Tony Flint: You have just gotten a shot at the best looking thing in professional wrestling-the Playboy in action. Alex here just witnessed the power of the Playboy's Dream. True, I've been on the shelf for a couple of months...It takes a while to get over a beating by the Extreme King Evil K. It seems like lately I've been wasting my time. I get tired of lame gimmicks and angles that fed heads are trying to place on me. For example, before I went on vacation I was wrestling on a Riverboat....not the life of a real playboy, I'll tell you that much. And there was another guy named Playboy in that fed.
Imagine that-the pres wanted a Playboy vs. Playboy match for the rights to call yourself playboy. Everyone knows there's only one playboy. And I am he. Enough about me though. I have a couple of guys you should meet. They aren't much for talking now, but they'll beat the crap out of anyone you want to throw at them.
Meet John and James Harper-the Dallas Connection. <the two giants get up from their weightbench. You can see their muscles rippling under their tight purple and black Palace of Power t-shirts. John picks up the barbell, with 300 lbs on it easily, and swings it like a baseball bat towards the camera.>
<snow> <fade to black> =================================== Jacob Yoder =================================== **Jacob Yoder is seen sitting in his chair in his living room. He is wearing a clean white shirt, suspenders, and black pants. The white shirt says "Better
Living Through Butter" on it. He is holding a glass of lemonaid. He looks at the camera and speaks.**
Jacob: There tis nothing in God's green earth that is better after a hard days work than sipping on Mary's lemonaid.
**Jacob pulls a deep gulp from his glass.** Jacob: So, it seems I have my first battle in the NAW. It doesn't really matter, I will not be in the "Minor Leagues" long. But for the time that I am to be here, I will prove my point to the whole wrestling world. No more will the Amish be the play things to the English. People will walk on the other side of the street when they see the Amish coming. A new generation of Amish art being raised! And of them, I will be the first.
**Jacob takes another sip from his glass and sets it down on the table next to him.** Jacob: Soon NAW. Soon, I will show thou what true power is. After you feel the
Butter Churner, you will never be the same. I am the Loose Bull. And I am charging after you!
**Jacob sits in his chair and laughs a laughter that is not quite sane. The camera fades as Jacob gets up out of his chair.** =================================== Ian Walsh =================================== {--Begin Transmission--} [Camera fades in.] [The camera drop-focuses on the bright noonday sun from the middle of a grass-covered valley. Faint noises of birds chirping can be heard. There isn't a cloud in the sky. A voice speaks out in a somewhat low, but stern tone of voice.]
"Tis a mirage that sets over this pit in disguise. That's all that this is . . . a pit." [The camera slowly pans down to show Ian Walsh standing at the bottom of the valley.] "This pit resembles the wrestling world. Everybody starts out at the bottom . . ." [Ian begins to run up the far side of the valley. After some quick running, he reaches the top. He takes a moment to catch his breath.]
". . . and they work their way to the top." [Ian puts his hands on his hips and bends slightly backward as if to crack his back. The camera zooms in at a good speed and stops at a good distance from
Ian. Ian is wearing a plain white T-shirt, blue "X-AM" shorts, white socks, and black and white striped reebok sneakers. He continues.]
"Some people just can't cut it in this sport and they _fail_ to reach the top." [Ian takes a deep breath as if to relax.] "I have been to the top of the _pit_. I have made myself a prominent member of this industry. I have earned my _spot_ in the history books. However, there is still a lot more to be accomplished. I still have to do what it takes to keep my
_spot_ in the wrestling history books." [Ian looks at the sun and points to it.] "Let's pretend that the sun is what we are shooting for. We'll pretend that the sun is our ultimate goal. The furthest you can get isn't as far as you want to get. That is what the ultimate _problem_ is."
[Ian looks at the camera.] "I know that I . . ." [Ian points to himself with both index fingers.] ". . . have not accomplished every goal that I have set out to do." [Ian puts his hands on his hips and takes a good look at the ground before him.] "That makes me think about my purpose in this life. It makes me wonder about what I am supposed to do. I set out to accomplish these goals and I know that I won't be able to complete all of those goals."
"So what do I do?" [Ian looks up at the camera with an emotionless face.] "I am supposed to go out there and do as much as I can and then some. As long as I do that, I will know that my life was indeed a successful one."
[Ian crosses his arms.] "This Tuesday, I face John Anderson in a title match. This man is obviously one of perseverence. I bet that he would not miss this match for the world."
"I'll tell ya, this man has a lot of guts. However, the man is just plain stupid." [A devilish grin appears on Ian's face. The grin turns into an angered look. He points at the camera and continues.] "John Anderson . . . . . . you have made the wrong choice. You have decided to not only put your career on the line . . .
. . . but your life, as well." [Ian pauses as he puts his hands on his hips.] "Beating me . . . is no easy task, Johnny boy. Facing me in the ring is not what many people consider _fun_.
You have made it known that you want _my_ title. Well, that is just fine and dandy. However, you need to beat me.
You need to take all risks and think out a fool-proof plan. You need to ready yourself for this match and prepare to feel pain that you have never felt in your entire life."
[Ian wipes his brow with his right hand.] "You can say that I have proven nothing to you. You can say that beating you twice means nothing. If beating you a third time is what it takes to get you to respect me, I will do it."
"You may not fear me, but you will have to respect me. I will take you out, boy. _My_ title will remain _my_ title." [Ian crosses his arms and looks into the camera.] "Our match, will be your last match . . . . . . Prepare to meet . . . . . . your demise." [Ian turns around and heads off into the seemingly endless field at the top of the valley.]
[Camera fades out.] {--End Transmission--} =================================== Kristina "the Weather Channel Drag Queen" =================================== (Kristina - The Weather Channel Drag Queen and his manager Jim Cantore are on a stage. Jim is holding a guitar and is standing in front of a mic and wearing a black suit, white shirt, and red tie. Kristina is holding a mic and is wearing a fringed leather skirt and a matching fringed leather halter top)
JIM: Well, all of us but Executioner know that Kristina is going to kick his ass. But to get Executioner to figure that out here's a song. Ready Kris?
KRISTINA (MALE VOICE): Ready. (Jim starts playing some chords) KRISTINA (FEMALE VOICE): Hey there Executioner, I'm gonna whoop your ass on TTAAANNNGGOO. JIM: Whoa Whoa, Yeah Yeah. KRISTINA (FEMALE VOICE): Hey there Executioner, I'm gonna get medieval on your AAASSSSSS... JIM: Yeah. (Guitar solo) KRISTINA (FEMALE VOICE): Hey there Executioner, You don't stand a CCCHHANNCCEE... JIM: Whoa. KRISTINA (FEMALE VOICE): Hey there Executioner, don't think that your ass. JIM: Has a chance! KRISTINA (FEMALE VOICE): YEAH EXECUTIONER! YOU'RE GONNA GET YOUR ASS WHOOPED!!! YOU'RE GOIN DOWN! JIM: You're going, down. KRISTINA (FEMALE VOICE): Hey there Executioner, you know you're going to... (Jim stops playing) KRISTINA & JIM: Die. (Jim and Kristina clap for themselves) =================================== Sir White Collar =================================== The scene flashes before the camera men. They have arrived at Sir White Collar's mansion in Pleasanton, California. Being led through the spacious halls and up two flights of stairs, the enter a door. There before them is Sir
White Collar, soaking in a tub. He has a cuban cigar in his mouth and is puffing away as he relaxes in the very warm, refreshing water. Seeing them, he speaks his wisdom.
Sir White Collar: Gentlemen, please! I have only just arrived in the NAW and you are making quite a fuss. However, since I do have some things on my mind and they are not really important, however I will speak. There are two men that I see here in this fed that I definitely want. First there is a champion here and he is the Human Torture Crack, Ian Walsh and Ian, we all know how much
I hate you and who you are. So, with this I issue this challenge. You and I for your title. Are you a champion? Or are you a coward? I know you and you are aware that I do not like you, so what about it chump? Bring it and let's do this thing, for the Mindset Empire shall rule the NAW. Now, there is a little matter of this Jade "The Blade". What a name? Last time I saw you, you were running around the CHO trying to wash my Rolls Royce and you could never be the man that I am. So, Jade listen up. The Mindset Empire needs a butler, apply for this position and I may let you live.
(Laughing) Sir White Collar: Now, as I sit here in this warm tub and I am thinking about my debut, one name comes to mind. John Q. Denver. What a name? You know, I never liked John Denver the singer, and with a name like you have you must have taken on his name in memoriam. What's the matter? Can not come up with a more original name? Ha, ha, ha, ha. I am the ruler of the Mindset Empire and I shall not lose to some pathetic low life, such as yourself Mr. Denver. When I put my patented "Blue Blood" on you, you will be but the first to know and to understand my intentions. Finally, I want to give a big hello to my friend the
Perpetrator and let everyone know that the Mindset will definitely see more members joining its premier ranks very soon. With that in mind I must bid you all adieu and say that, none of you matter to Mindset.
Scene ends. ===================================
