[Flash] Josh Battle -- *please* give me your honest opinions

24 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To give you some background, Hentai molested Battle's escort, Sympathy, and this is the beginning of Battle's retribution.
If I suck, I want to know. Don't hold back! [The scene opens on a beautiful suburban house, with a white picket fence around it. The sun is shining, birds are singing in the trees, and an older
Japanese woman is on her hands and knees, doing some gardening in front of the house.
The camera pulls back slightly to reveal Josh Battle, in blue jeans, a white golf shirt and expensive sunglasses, walking up the path towards the
Japanese woman, a small photo album in his hands. He stops a few feet away, and she looks up at him.]
Woman: May I help you, young man? [Battle gives her his most charming smile and takes off his sunglasses out of politeness.] JB: Yes, ma'am, I think you might. Are you Mrs. Hideki? [The woman starts to get to her feet, taking off her gardening gloves and walking towards Josh.]
Woman: Yes. [Josh smiles and bows slightly.] JB: I have a package for you. From your son. [Josh hands her the photo album.] Mrs. Hideki: My son? Have you spoken to him? Is he alright? Why hasn't he spoken to me in four years?
[Josh looks apologetic.] JB: I don't know, Mrs. Hideki. I spoke to him only briefly -- he told me to give you this album. I haven't looked inside it, as I wouldn't want to pry into personal matters.
[Mrs. Hideki begins to run her fingers along the outside of the still unopened photo album.]
Mrs. Hideki: Where did you speak to him? [Josh's face saddens.] JB: He approached me on the street, Mrs. Hideki. At first I thought he was going to cause me trouble -- his clothing was a little worn. But then he explained to me that he wanted me to give his mother this photo album. He said it would show you what he had been doing with his life. That he hadn't wasted the education you and his father had worked so hard to provide for him.
I couldn't say no, Mrs. Hideki. He looked so...so desperate. [Mrs. Hideki stops stroking the cover of the album and, tentatively, delicately opens it.] Mrs. Hideki: Oh my... [Inside, the album is filled with photographs. The nude photos of Sunshine that Hentai took; the photos of Sympathy in the shower; and other photos, photos that the censors hastily try to scramble out of the picture lest they face charges.]
Mrs. Hideki: He....he...did this? [She is quickly turning through the pages, her face half turned to the side in an expression of disgust that worsens with each passing page.]
JB: I don't know, Mrs. Hideki...maybe there's some mistake, maybe he gave me the wrong album...but this is definitely the album he gave me. It hasn't left my side for a minute.
[Weakly, Mrs. Hideki closes the photo album and drops it onto the path. She appears shaky, so Josh puts his hand on her elbow to steady her.]
Mrs. Hideki: I...I need to lie down. [Mrs. Hideki, her heart in pieces, slowly walks into her house. Battle turns the other way and walks back to his car, dropping a small business card next to the photo album. As his car pulls away, the camera moves into a close-up on the business card, which reads in plain black block letters on the white paper: "Surprise! You're Dead"]
