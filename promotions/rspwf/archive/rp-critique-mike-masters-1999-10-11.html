[RP Critique] Mike Masters

11 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
MIKE MASTERS IN: Buggs, Amish, and Other Out-of-Touch People [The camera's initial blackness melts away to reveal a town that seems to be frozen in time for over 300 years. Unpaved dirt roads, horse-drawn carraiges, watertanks built from wood...and not a single sign of any of the recent technological advances of modern society. The presence of several people in simple and archaic clothing confirms the suspicions: this is an Amish community. Of course, one has to wonder what a camera would be doing in an Amish community...]
[The answer soon comes in the form of two familiar forms, walking towards the camera down a dirt road stretching from the site of a series of outhouses. Mike Masters and Narcissa, showing flagrant disregard for the Amish way of life walk proudly towards the camera, flaunting their flamboyant modern attire. Masters' brightly colored T-shirt
(commemorating the upcoming second annual ICW Shockfest) seems to spit on the humble white button-up shirts of the male Amish, while Narcissa's black turtleneck and skirt cling to her voluptuous form with a fervor that Amish women are far too modest to permit their clothes to do.
Their lighthearted, stuck up smiles, symbols of their spoiled and easy upbringing, contrast sharply with the determined countenances of the locals, working hard as they have had to throughout their lives.]
[Masters adresses the camera as he walks towards it:] MASTERS: "Look around you, and you see the People That Time Forgot. Our world has long since passed these people by. Their entire villiage is better suited to be a museum than a place to live. Everywhere around us lie monuments to the obsolete technology of the old world."
NARCISSA: "D.B. Bugg would love it here." [Masters laughs as he nods his head in agreement. The locals don't seem to be the slightest bit aware of the presence of Masters, or Narcissa, or the cameraman. They just continue to go about their business, ignorant of those who stand in their presence...the evidence of their society's obsolete status.]
[Masters looks around, taking note of the Amish failing to take note of him and his company.]
MASTERS: "Yes...the parallels between Bugg and...[a belittling look creeps onto Masters' face to match the condescending tone of voice as he says]...these people."
NARCISSA: "Look at them toiling on, blissfully unaware of how backward they are, of how the rest of us have left them far behind. And yet, to themselves, they are the pinnacle."
[Narcissa finishes her sentance with an exhale of disbelief that sounds like the begining of laughter.]
MASTERS: "Now, for you stupid little people at home watching this on your glowing, mind-numbing box...[Masters smirks pompously]...do you see how D.B. Bugg is like these isolated wretches, or do I have to spell out the metaphor for you people once again? Wait, what kind of question is that? Of course I do..."
[Asshole.] MASTERS: "You see, once, long ago, the technology imployed by the Amish actually WAS the best in the world. That was a long time ago...before they got this silly notion that they were somehow superior to the rest of the world, and cut themselves off from it. Before they began to live in stagnant isolation...before they decided to refuse to advance. That world that they foolishly shut out passed them by without a second thought. And now look at them! They are nothing more than a curiousity, an anomaly, a joke for the amusement of us with our superior modern ways."
[Despite Masters' rising tone, the amish continue to go about their business of fixing the wooden wheels to their carraiges, or churning their butter, or whatever. They still do not admit to themselves who is in their midst and what they represent.]
MASTERS: "Bugg, you too have cut yourself off from the wrestling world, thinking that you were best that could ever be. And maybe once, long ago, B.M. (Before Masters), you were...just like these people were. But just like these people Bugg, you failed to realize that when you stop progressing, you effectively regress. The rest of the wrestling world has passed you by, Bugg. While you've sat still in your cloistered biosphere, facing only those who were big back in the bygone days when you were big...people like me have been cropping up, advancing the world of wrestling beyond the level it was at when you left it. You, you just keep using the same tired old techniques you were using a year ago, in spite of the slight problem that they no longer work. Your pitiful win loss record this year bears this out."
[Masters smiles wickedly.] MASTERS: "The truth hurts when forced to face it, doesn't it Bugg...you stubborn simpleton."
NARCISSA: "He's lived in denial so long...ignoring the outside world as these people have...what makes you think he'll allow himself to see his numerous flaws now?"
MASTERS: [sighing] "Call it a naive hope..." [laughing derisively once again] "Bugg, you can live in denial if you want. Hey, if that helps you get to sleep at night, go ahead...but whether you admit it or not, I
_AM_ better than you in every way imaginable. I had a better upbringing...I have a sharper mind...and I have superior wrestling ability. Part of that is because I'm me and I'm better than everyone just because...but part of the vast gap between us is that you stopped trying to improve yourself long ago. You got sloppy. You got lazy.
You stopped pushing yourself. So now its up to me to push you..." NARCISSA: "Push you out of the way." MASTERS: "Bugg, your hollow threats fall on deaf ears. You see, one of the things we modern types realize is that you can't intimidate people with a bunch of tough talk unless...well...you know...you actually BACK
IT UP once in a while. That's something you just can't do anymore, what with you going 0 for Forever in singles matches. That's why it was so incredibly easy for me to run down to the ring and spray you in the eyes with a can of Raid. Because I know I could do it without repurcussions;
you can't stop me from doing whatever I want to you. Just like Westside
Walavan couldn't stop me from bouncing him from the International title picture down to the bottom of the totem pole where people feud over belts nobody cares about like the Hardcore title. Just like Tha Dragon couldn't stop me from beating him up in every encounter we ever had, exposing him for the joke he was, draining him of his will to compete, reducing him to a worthless shell and effectively ending his career."
[Masters pauses and looks at a slightly upward angle into the sky, remembering the worthless piece of crap that was Tha Dragon.]
MASTERS: "Unlike you Bugg, I can still back up my words. When we finally meet in the ring one on one you will realize the truth of this along with everything else I have I have said today. You will also realize what everyone except you already knows: that you are
... ALL ... ... WASHED ... ... UP !!! [Camera zooms in on the haughty faces of Masters and Narcissa as it fades to black.] -- handler of Mike Masters Available for your viewing pleasure in: [ICW] 7-1-1 "This is America! Rock stars don't go to jail here! Hell, Vince Neil only did 30 days, and he killed someone."
