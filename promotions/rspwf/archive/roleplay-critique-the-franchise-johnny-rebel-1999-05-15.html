[Roleplay, Critique] "The Franchise" Johnny Rebel

15 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens to a broken down shack, around a junkyard. Thing's are seen lying all over the ground, mostly junk. The moon is set over the junkyard, as the night air flows through the stench of the garbage reeks through the night-air. The broken-down shack, begins to rattle as the cool rain flows down from the star-lit sky. Someone walks out from the shack, and sits down on the repulsive curve, and pulls his hair back in the rain. The man is seen to be,
"The Franchise" Johnny Rebel as his hair has seemed to grow out in the absense he's taken from wrestling. Johnny Rebel is seen taking a drink out of a bottle in a paper bag. Rebel coughs and spits it out, as he notices the camera and begins to speak.
-= Johnny Rebel =- It's been so long, and it's been so short. I came out to the ring, and I told the world that I was done with professional wrestling, and that nobody would ever see me again. I had mixed-emotions. I had Legacy come searching after me, and everybody begging me to come back. Why? Why should I come back to an organazation that hated me, they didn't want me. I'm not sure now, if they even want me anymore as it is. But, I came back. Just to see what people would say, and I was put in my place by the disgruntled members of the
X-Tremly Brutal Wrestling League. It seems, I've adopted the motto, "As many things change, some of them stay the same." I watched people come and go, and they waltzed past everybody and they just left and watched me in the dust.
People always told me I was crazy, and that I could do anything that I wanted to do. And that kind of pissed me off. People came into the X-Tremly Brutal
Wrestling League, and then they automatically got the XBWL Heavywieght
Championship.. why? I don't have a clue.. people came and people go, I couldn't figure out what was going on. Was it a conspiracy, I guess so. Was it a reason to hold, Jobber Rebel from staying at top of his game, probly. I'm done whining, Now it's time to put everything to one final push to the top of the ladder. Everybody is on the hitlist, now. I'm going to start with a couple important names on the list.
Rebel gets up and walks over, and sits down in a old 54 Chevy Pickup. The camera man follows and sits down in the passengers side, as Rebel flips on the radio, "Rosa Parks" by OutKast is playing. Rebel smiles a little bit, and looks back at the camera.
-= Johnny Rebel =- Legacy and Mad Jester. The goof-balls that are running the X-Tremly Brutal Wrestling League. Thats a scary thought. Legacy, we've had our diffrences and we've had our friendships. Everything has been thrown out of the game, except for the ounce of respect that drains out of my body for your wrestling career. Not the respect for you as my boss, but as a friend that we once had. Your theone that told me, I wasn't good enough for the XBWL and went through one ear out the other. You left me hanging in the dry, in the WWFW. You pushed me and I wen't for in my career. I owe it all to you, I'm not offering anything but my respect. Wether, you take it or not is up to you. But it's on, as you walk through the Kronos Klan. The most respected stable in the history of the XBWL and USWL. A stable that Grizzle and Deathstroke, built an empire out of. You remeber those days Legacy, when me and you faught off against the
Kronos Klan all by ourselves. It was me and you, through and through. When everybody slacked off, it was just me and you fighting against an army. Thing's have changed now, I'm leading the battle and your on the opposite side. We're no longer together as team and as one unit. It seems that the tables have changed, and you've got power in the XBWL and you've got control over me. Thats fine with me, because things won't change. You know me better than anybody in the world today. My mother and father, left when I was 10 years old. I was dumped on the porch of an old-lady and then you stepped up and helped me in life. You tought me everything I needed to know, and now your leading my carreer in another way. This time, you hold my destiny in your hands. Should I be feared? I guess so. The crazed man, that I threw off a fifteen foot scaffold, that man has my life in his hand. With one snap of his fingers he can screw me over, just like that. You know, when I wen't to find my "smile" if you will. I came back to find you being the president of the XBWL, you and Mad
Jester who would run rampid on the XBWL for years now. We've been here since the begging, and now I see that your giving and taking just because you've got power. The annoys me to no end. DeAndre Colemen, running around as the World
Champion. Why? You've got people that have been in this league for years, and your one of them and you let a rookie run around as the World Champion? Not a smart move. Legacy, we've been through it together as the thick was thin, and when everything was crumbling. You and I picked up the pieces and run together as pack of wolves. You were leading the hungry pack, and I was following. Then one day, you got what you deserved. Taking control of everybody, and everything in your way, the WWFW just stepped out of your shadow. Micheal Andrews, walked away from your grasp and went on to do his own stuff. He became the XBWL
Heavywieght Championship, over me. Which was not an easy task, I guess, if thats what you want to say. He left us in the pits, and told us that he was better than us. Then there was Scorpion, who just physically combusted in the center of the ring causing all sorts of trouble. He left us, and by his means he told us that he was to good for us. Then there was Robert the Demolisher who was almost their for the whole ride, but left on us to. Legacy, we've been through everything together. Everybody who told us, that we weren't good enough to fight with them. Or we weren't good enough to hang with them, and even compete with them. There you go Legacy, just remeber that before you go and make anything out of proportion. And then there was Mad Jester. The head-master of the Kronos Klan leading the way for all of these young, XBWL-ites. When you have a madman running a company, you've got a scary and big problem. Mad
Jester, you've got serious problems that god knows who can help you with. I think, you shouldn't even have gotten out of your retirement home. Now, I have to listen to you in the TXW and around this league somemore. Thats something I can't stand. We haven't had the greatest of historys, and we're not headed towards a bright future. Things' are going to happen, and they're going to be bad for you Jester. Wether or not, you stay in the league as a co-owner or not, your going to get yours. I don't know how, or I don't know when.. but when it does happen, be prepared to get your (CENSORD) kicked, once and for all. Ending anything, or any dreams that you had of once being a World Champion again. The more, I think about it. The more it comes true, things have changed. Will they ever be the same? In this case, it's a Do or Die situation. You've got to do something Jester, or your going to die..
Rebel flips the station on the radio as "Anywhere" by 112 plays. Rebel leaves the station their as a loud lightning crack is heard, and the camera men gets kinda jumpy. Rebel laughs, as He begins to speak again.
-= Johnny Rebel =- DeAndre Colemen. The XBWL Heavywieght Champion. Climbed a mountian to become the World Champion. Colemen, you did the unthinkable and the unimagainable. You were the quickest ever to be World Chmpion, beside Micheal
Andrews. Now, you climb around the league and pretend to be the top dawg. You prance around, wearing that title belt snug around your waist. I don't blame you, I know what it's like to be a champion. I watch you in APCW, and I watch you in the XBWL. You've got something, that nobody else has and thats charisma.
Nobody can perform like you, the last person that was like you was Micheal
Andrews. And when you compare, Colemen and Andrews you get the same product.
You see Colemen, ever since I lost to Micheal Andrews, life has been one big exclamation point. Now, it seems that your the answer to the question. Ever since, that one night where me and you, took on Legacy and "Dangerous" Dave
Taylor, things have never been the same. You went on to bigger and better things, and where did I go? To the end of the line, and I stayed in the same place, I had always been. Just like always, I would get a break and then slide right back into my place in time. Colemen, as I watched you slip your way into the top of the XBWL against people like Frost and Beast, you didn't let anybody stand in your way. You wen't to a stable that would help you, in the Kronos
Klan. Just like me, when I controlled the New Breed. I wen't from rags to riches in a matter of time. Now, what's going to happen? Colemen, you think that just because your in the Kronos Klan, you think your going to be top dawg for ages? Let me ask you something Colemen, you think that the people in the
Kronos Klan don't want that Heavywieght Title? Butch, the hungriest of everybody in the XBWL, want's that title as bad as anybody. He just doesn't show it much anymore, as people have singled him out and told him that he couldn't make it as World Champion. Now, He's backed down and hasn't said a word in ages, because people got him down. You think, that Legacy doesn't want to wear that belt anymore? Your crazy. He's got you in the corner of his eye, waiting for yo to step out of line and then he can strike when your not looking. I'm not going to say much more about my past, It's going to be on with the future. And in that future lies a young and expierenced, Ring Troll. The
Unified XBWL Heavywieght Champion, I've got a couple words for you. DeAndre
Colemen, I know your a man of honor and respect. And you'll do anything for that XBWL Heavywieght Championship and I know that you'll defend it against people that deserve it and people who think they deserve it. So, I'm offering you a contract. One night only, just me and you. Inside a steel cage, so no one can interfere. And nobody can stand in my way or your way. No exscuses, just one steel cage, two people fighting for their chance at supremecy. And one belt, that shimmies in the night and glimmers in the light. Ever since, I lost that championship belt, things have never been the same. And I doubt they'll ever be the same again. I feel empty inside, and thats the remedy to my drought. I'm not going to sit her and bullshit with you for along time. I'm simply going to give you everything that I've got and thats all I can produce.
If you've got a problem with that, and that leads me to one conclusion, that your not the dreamer that everybody thinks and wishes you are. I've beat around the bush for along time, and I can't do it any longer. I've ran from my fears, and I've hid my emotions for four years. Who would have thought, that one day
"Jobber" Rebel would have been the Champion of a league. Nobody could have ever said that, and anybody would believe them. It's only a threory of man, that nobody could conquer. When I was in the USWL, and I first came in.. everybody says, that he wouldn't make it. Just look at him, He's a jobber. Yeah, I was a f*ckin jobber. I don't care anymore.. I've been through everything, anybody could ever think of. I've been thrown off balcony's and I almost ended my f*ckin carreer, when I was dropped on my neck through 30-40 tables. I've headlined my share of matches and cards. Now, It's time to explode deep. Like everybody said I could.. I watched Johnny Macaroni run this company from scratch, and he told me that I was nothing. He was the one that always told me, if I didn't put in any effort, then I would be stuck with getting my a** kicked everynight.. This one's for you, if your listening Macaroni..
Rebel shoves the camera man out of the car, and turns down the radio. Rebel lays down in the seat, and slams the door shut. The camera men leave, and the scene closes with Johnny Rebel's name up on the screen, dripping down in red lettering.
