RP, please critique.

12 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, let's see if anyone critiques me this time.  [detail withheld] said he'd do it, so here you are.  It's a different RP than the one I asked you to critique, though.  Just tell me your opinion.
-- Fade in. Dueling Banjos plays softly in the background. The same rustic cabin that always precedes words by Tobias Rodland is in the background. This time we see the back of the cabin. The day is sunny and the birds sing. A beat up old ford pickup that looks to be from the turn of the century occupies a spot behind the cabin stripped of grass. From stage right steps Tobias Rodland, his massive form threatening to blot out the camera view.
As he passes, a cane pokes him in the back. . . Daddy: C'mon, Stupid. The world wants to see you train! Tobias: Buh, buh, Daddy! The camera pans to show the two men pass. Daddy Dearest prodding the huge man along with his cane, Tobias cringing from his father and slowly, very slowly making his way towards an object in the distance.
. . Daddy: Don' *bleep* Daddy me, boah! Yer gonna show the world how you prepare for a match! Tobias: Duh, duh I godda match? Daddy: You_will_get a match, boah, now just move! The view continues to follow the pair as they make their way towards a cage on the side of the cabin. It is unclear what is inside the cage, which is made from railroad ties, but whatever it is, it's growling and sounds very big. Suddenly Tobias turns to his father, looking panicked. . .
Tobias: Nuh, no daddy! I DO' WANNA! Daddy: You_just_open that *bleep*'n cage Stupid, before i fix ta brain ya! Tobias turns back to the heavy, huge cage and goes about moving a tie that holds it closed. Daddy turns back towards the camera.
Daddy: Y' can turn that thing on, now. We're gonna give ya' what ya' came ta see! Cameraman: Um, it's already on, has been for some time. Daddy seems confused for a moment, but just shrugs and looks back to the cage. . . Daddy: C'mon, boy, y'coulda opened that ten times b'now! Quit stalling. Tobias finally heaves the heavy railroad tie aside, and from the cage comes a_huge_grizzly_bear! The bear is muzzled and it's paws are mittened, but it is still an angry bear and it advances on Tobias. .
. Tobias: Duh, DADDY! Daddy: C'mon, boy, you wanted a pet, play wi' 'im! Tobias: I wanned a bunny, notta BAYUH!!! Tobias backs away from the bear, fear in his eyes. The cameraman begins to back away as well.
Daddy: Heyheyhey! Where y' goin'!? Cameraman: What are you, nuts? That's a *bleep*'ing BEAR! I'm getting out of here before he gets his muzzle off! The bear has begun boxing at Tobias, who dodges with a speed inspired by fear. Daddy: (Laughing) What, you kiddin'? It's just the boy playin' wit' his pet! I mean, lookit' that, the bear ain' even as big as
Toby!
The camera focuses on Tobias and the bear. Although the bear is about the same height as Tobias when standing on it's hind legs, it doesn't seem to have quite the same bulk. The bear at that time connects with a haymaker, which staggers Tobias, but seems to more anger him than hurt him.
Bear: Grrr. Tobias: Grrraaahhh!!! Cameraman: So he can handle the bear? Daddy: Oh, yeah. It's a pet bear. I jus' bought him yesterday. Got a nice package. . . After being struck quite a few times, Tobias has begun to fight back. He punches the bear in the stomach, with little affect.
Daddy: . . .a muzzle, some mitts, a tranq rifle. Tobias grapples the bears head, and is grappled in turn. It looks odd, a collar-and-elbow tieup between species. Oddly, Tobias seems to have the edge in this contest, forcing the bear back.
Cameraman: Tranq rifle? I thought you said the bear was tame? Daddy: Oh, no, I never said he was tame! I said he was a PET! There's a difference. Cameraman: Like what difference? Suddenly the muzzle comes loose, and the bears' jaws are free. Tobias takes little notice, and continues to force the bear back by his head. Daddy Dearest notices not at all.
Cameraman: JEEZUS!!! Daddy: What? (Turns around) Oh, that. (Turns back) Well, y'see. . .(Turns around again.) Jeezus! Mah meal ticket! Suddenly panicked, Daddy runs to grab a small bundle on a back porch of the cabin. From it he pulls a rifle, and he empties three shots into the bear. The cameraman doesn't move during all of this. He just stands still, paralyzed. After a moment, Tobias forces the big animal down, and it passes out. Toby chokes the animal on the ground. . .
Daddy: Let go boy! (Beating Tobias with the rifle) LET_THE_*BLEEP*'N BEAR_GO! After many blows, Tobias finally releases the bears' throat. . . Daddy: Now, you shove that *bleep*'n thing back in the cage! Tobias: (Whimpering) I jus' wanned a buh, a bunny. Without a word, Toby pulls the bear up by the head, gets a shoulder under it, and after several seconds of struggling, manages to carry it to the cage, open the door, and put it inside. Toby then locks the cage, and the pair begin to walk away, as they do, Daddy's words quiet and then fade away completely.
Daddy: When I tell ya' ta' let go, you let go, you got that!? All is very still for a moment. The cameraman hasn't moved, the bear is unconscious, Tobias and Daddy are gone. 30 seconds have passed when he finally speaks, quietly, slowly. . .
Cameraman: *bleep*, I wish I'd brought a change of boxers. . . The scene fades away to the sound of a bear snoring. -- [name withheld].   Handler of various guys in various feds.   ACWA= William Craven and Salvatore Cassanini.
