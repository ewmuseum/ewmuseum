[ Chris King ] Grade RP

19 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Comments are appreciated, I am taking King to be more of a mega heel, instead of the heel/tweener he was before..
thanks to everyone who seriously takes the time to help me out here tim [ "Wherever I May Roam" by Metallica blares over the PA. The fans rise to their feet and a quiet murmur slowly swells to an overwhelming pop from the crowd. While the majority of the pop is a "heel" pop, there are a faint few who can be heard chanting "King!, King!, King!" ]
LF: THE KING OF EXTREME!!!!!! RA: ( takes a deep breath ) Yes, fans, that music could mean only one thing as the self proclaimed King of Ext...
LF: Wait a minute! He is not just "Self" proclaimed, he is the _Neck_ proclaimed King of Extreme Andj. And don't you forget it!
RA: How could I? LF: Wait a minute! Is _that_ Chrissy? RA: Why I do believe it is. [ The man, who steps out from behind the curtain with "Prince Louie" in his left hand, and the battered form of the King's Cup in his other is indeed
Chris King however, he is not so easily reconizeable as usual. Usually he would walk out, his long, wavy, dark, brown hair flowing down over his shoulders with some sort of T Shirt and a pair of pants or shorts on. But this man, well he paints an all together different picture. The Chris King we all see, stands with his head bowed as the fireworks shoot off behind him in beat with the grinding guitars in the song. His hair is tightly pulled back in a ponytail, he is wearing a very expensive looking dark grey Armani suit and tie. There is a folded up piece of paper in the breast pocket of his jacket.
As he raises his head and walks down the aisle towards the ring We do notice one thing that is always a constant with King. He still has his smartass cocky grin slapped on his face. ]
LF: Damn! Chris cleans up nicely! RA: Yes he does, maybe we are in store for an attitude change as well. LF: Heheh..don't bet on it Andj. RA: Well we can hope. LF: Well at least he has good taste. He looks Like Glitter Boy himself helped him pick that out.
[ As King steps into the ring he pauses for a moment to soak in the jeers of the crowd. He calls for a mic, and is shortly supplied with one ]
King: Cut the music! [ Wherever I May Roam quickly fades out. ] King: Thats better. Now all of you ignorant pieces of trash out there rise to your feet and bow down before _your_ King! Your King of Extreme! Your King of
T C god-<beep> W!
[ The boos, along with various debris fly at King whose grin just gets bigger. ] King: Well, I can tell all you inbred pieces of trash aren't going to let me get through this any quicker so I'll get right to it, casue quite frankly, most of you smell like <beep>!
[ Another HUGE heel pop ] LF: Well, Chrissy seems a bit pissed Andj.! Even meaner than usual! RA: I noticed..... LF: Well you wanted an attitude change Andj. There you go! RA: _This_ is _not_ what I had in mind! LF: Well, who cares? I like it! RA: Well I don't. LF: Yeah, and I bet America and Voodoo won't like it much either! [ King reaches into the breast pocket of his jacket. He pulls out the folded piece of paper and unfolds it and holds it high for all to see. Although the print is MUCH to small to be read by anyone there or at home ]
King: In my hand I hold a piece of paper. And, depending on how you look at it, it is one of two things.
[ King pauses and looks out at the crowd and basks in the jeers ] King: You _might_ look at it and say....Hey that is a contract for Jim America and Voodoo and Chris King to go at it for The Kings Cup later on tonight.
[ We assume that it is indeed a contract as it is impossible to tell. After this first suggestion of "how to look at it" King just shrugs his shoulders and kinda half rolls his eyes ]
King: Now that is how all you out there with your fourth grade education levels are probably looking at it!
[ Just as the crowd was beginning to die down a bit they erupt again in a nearly 100% heel pop ]
King: Well, if you would shut the hell up I am going to do you a favor. See I went to college full ride on a football scholarship at Ohio State, and compared to you guys, that makes me god <beep> Albert Einstein! So I tell you what I'm gonna do....
[ King reaches into his pants pocket and pulls out a pair of eyeglasses, he puts them on and continues, now ignoring the boos. He continues in a very fake, and calm voice, trying to "teach" the crowd. ]
King: You all can call me Professor King for now...Now today's lesson will be that things are not always what they seem. Take this contract for example.
[ King once again holds it up for all to see, a worthless gesture ] King: Now, to the common, uneducated man, whose brother is also his uncle and cousin.....that would be most of you out there...
[ another huge heel pop King continues ignoring it ] King: Yes to you, it seems as though we have a contract for a match, between myself, and then two other no names. Well, at a first glance that is enough.
_BUT_ upon closer examination..... { King holds the paper close to his face for a "closer" examination ] King: Upon closer examination it seems to me that what we have here is none other than a dual death warrant for Jim America and Voodoo!!!!!! Signed by none other than Xavier Lee Jackson!!!!!!
[ Another huge heel pop as the flying debris is coming ever so closer to striking King. King once again goes back to basking in the boos ]
King: And, from what I understand, the funeral is to be held right here tonight, immediately after the match. And, _that_ is the answer to the question all you numbskulls out there were wondering, in why the hell am I so dressed up. I mean, it is just not right to attend a funeral of your co workers in wrestling pants and no shirt. I just hope they can keep their damn blood of the jacket!
King: Yes folks, your lesson for today was free of charge, only because I want to see your fat asses use the money to buy some more clothes to cover your repulsive bodies the next time I am forced to come here! So class dismissed, and Jimbo, Voodoo, see you in a minute, and my condolences to your families...Long live the King......
[ Wherever I May Roam begins to play again as King quickly makes his way back to the dressing room, somehow managing to dodge all the trash thrown at him.
The deafening chorus off boos shows no signs of dying out even after King disappears behind the curtain..... ]
RA: Well...... LF: hehehehehhehehe.....Chrissy's ready to rock! RA: Yes....he....is.....and as Chris King changes into his battle gear, let's hear from Voodoo then head up to the ring!
