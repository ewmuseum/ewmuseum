[Critique - Roleplay - XBWL] IA2 - Home

22 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey (to whoever actually reads this) This is a roleplay I did recently...about a week ago...that got a lot of praise from people in the XBWL, but I wanted to know what people who were a little more indepenent have to say. (I make a few inside refernces...one very noticable, which may give people a different impression if they know what's going on with the league.)
The backstory from the work perspective, as best I can sum it up, is that former prez of EVCW, Ed Payne, is now in charge of the XBWL, and the tag team International Army 2 (abbreviated IA2, Belgian Sensation and Assassin)
is not happy about it, given the changes that have been made since Payne has been in charge. The XBWL is a long time home to them, and they went into
EVCW to fight them in the past, so for them, Payne being in charge is something that doesn't agree to them. The fact that they've been pushed in the background (okay, so that's not much work), also is another complaint.
They're pretty much sick of the state things are in, and aren't sure if the fights' worth it anymore.
To sum that up: They're not happy with Payne. :) Any comments would be appericated. ----- (Scene: An empty arena. Quite likely the XBWL Dome, as it's set up for a wrestling event and seems to be familar. Only a few lights are on, around the ring area. As the camera pans around, nothing more than the floor seats, and the close ones at that, are visible. The camera (apparently in the ring itself) stops as it comes upon the Belgian Sensation, sitting at the timekeepers desk. Sensation starts talking to the camera, as the camera man (quite likely his partner Assassin) exits the ring, while still facing the camera at him.)
Sensation: You know who sits here? Yea, the timekeeper...but do you know his name? He's sat here and watched and made some noise for over 2 years, and I don't think any of you know his name.
I'll be truthful...for a while, I didn't know it either. But, time passes, and you meet people. The veterans, in and out of the ring, start to hang out together, like surivivors of some battle, remembering their former comrades and discussing the new guys chances. As time goes on, the group got smaller...some times, people popped back around, some times, the youngers hung with us, but the inner circle gets smaller and smaller...till only a few are left...
Wednesday, it got smaller still. The man who sits here, I saw him yesterday, and we talked. He'd been here since the beginning...not the beginning of the XBWL, but back in the USWL. He'd seen a lot of champs, and a lot of presidents. But, on Wednesday, he'd seen enough. It wasn't anything more than a little thing that set him off...it was the effect of months and months of frustration. He told me he was leaving the XBWL...he told me that the dream when the XBWL started has long passed. He'd stayed loyal to the XBWL all this time...turning down offers from other leagues, because this place felt like home. But...it doesn't feel like home anymore, he said, so it was time to move.
I've been thinking about what he said...ever since he walked out that door for the last time and drove off. I've rewatched a tape of Prime Time...and it hit me...he's totally right.
This ain't home. This ain't the XBWL. The XBWL is dead. You're asking yourself...well then Sensation, what am I watching right now? It's the ghost...it's the remains of a bombed out city, with new residents. It's a lot of EVCW and a little bit of CCW and maybe no bit of IA2... Are we next to leave? I don't know...there's been signs lately that we...we after giving everything we had to this league...that we aren't wanted around here anymore.
We're never told that we were scheduled to face Destiny and Edwards, and only get lucked out when the match is bumped.
We're taken out of the loop...given no information after being the supplier off all info in the past...
Our matches...like the cage match with Brandon Thorton...get conviently forgeton. Is this the end of the IA2 here...whatever this place is? We've got offers from other places...and for the first time ever, we're considering leaving...
I don't know...I really don't anymore...but...if this is the last time we're allowed to speak out over the XBWL airways...then we've got some words to say...
Destiny and Edwards...I assume we're facing you two sometime this weekend, 'though it'd be nice if the XBWL would let us in on the schedule. Doesn't really matter, 'cause we've already figured out what they to happen...IA2's always the team to call on, when you want their opponents to look good. We make matches great with people who ain't, and we make classics with people who are (ain't that right, TGT-TE?)...we'll do our jobs, like we always do...
Andrews...You know to hang with a better person. Nope, not talking about
Mandrake, 'cause we don't have a problem with him, or Maximium, 'cause we respect him. Death Warrent. You're talking about how you're the 'XBWL',
Andrews, but right now, you're just a stand-in....Johnny Douglas'
replacement in this enternal war between Flyguy and Warrent. If you're really the XBWL, then you don't need any EVCW guys to get yourself some heat. And figure out how to make a point, and some sense.
Maximium...keep reaching for the top, man. You're trying to do something we never were allowed to do...get respect as singles. I'd tell you to keep speaking your mind, but I don't know if you can and reach the top. We got your back, if you ever need it.
Legacy...I don't remember if I'm supposed to hate you or like you this month. Let me know if you remember. But, either way, we respect you too...for dealing with all the crap that doesn't stop coming your way, and probably won't as long as you stick around here. You probably will, to...'cause you got the same sick attachement we have...or had...
Brandon Thornton...since the XBWL seemed to forget we don't like each other, let's forget it too, 'kay? In fact, I'm going give you a make-up gift, right now...Brandon Thornton is the new XBWL Hardcore Champion! Since it's no longer defended and all, it couldn't hurt. Congralations an-
(The camera shakes, as the cameraman/Assassin waves his head sideways) Oh yeah, I almost forget, I HAVE NO POWER...so I can't do that. Gee, I forget all about that...although, I'm sure someone would have pointed that out to me again, sooner or later.
Max Profit$...hey, you want to hit me agian? 'cause we all know, being a manager and all, we gotta make sure we put you over actual wrestlers like me, 'cause you need your heat!
Flyguy...yea, you did deserve a chance at the title...but last summer. Now...is the 'glory' of becoming champion worth it? Ed Payne...didn't it work out nice that you didn't have to worry about rebuilding ECVW, having a league fall into your lap (or grabbing it out of someone's hands, something like that) so you can have your cake and allow some else to clean up when your done?
Well...I'm pretty sure I"m not going be on TV again, after that. But I don't really care too much...this ain't home, this ain't the XBWL.
By the way...his name was Jim...the timekeepers, that is. I'm sure Payne can dig up someone else from EVCW to fill the gap, but not really...
