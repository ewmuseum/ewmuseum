Roleplay... grade it... or not...

21 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Damn, I'm bored. Bored as hell, really. How bored am I? Bored enough to post a rp for no real reason whatsoever. Oh, I suppose give me feedback on what you think of it... if you want... I can't make you.
You probably haven't heard o' me, or my character, "Excellence" Jeff Easthope... so I'm kinda interested to see what the regulars of RSPW think of my stuff... Here we go. ___________________________________________________________________ (Scene opens to a gym.) (No... Scratch that. While it is a gym; no ordinary, run-of-the-mill Bally's is this. No aerobics classes are to be found, no groups of fat slobs sweating buckets while trying to move to the rhythm of 80's dance music.)
(Let's be thankful for that.) (Instead, what can be found here is a *real* gym. A place where people who are serious about keeping their bodies in immaculate condition can be found.
A place where a man who desires to bring out his potential can go to. And a place, where one's skill can be tested. What skill? Wrestling skill. For situated in the center of it all, is a wrestling ring.)
(It doesn't appear new, or in the type of shape that a first-class league would use, but that could be taken as a testament to the abuse men have put each other through between those ropes over the years. The rest of the gym doesn't appear in that great of shape either. But as the chiseled men and women who pump iron within these walls knows, you don't need flash to work out. You don't need the peppy trainers, you don't need the cheesy music.
All you need is yourself, and the weight.) (The men who test their prowess in the ring know this too. They don't care about expensive entrances, theme music, or multi-million dollar contracts.
They just want to wrestle. And wrestle they do; for although most everyone in the vicinity looks like the kind of people that would merely swing their fists and hope for the best, such a person wouldn't be found here. The patrons know how to handle themselves in the ring, anyone who doesn't would find themselves being laughed out of the building.)
(This is indeed a place where a serious wrestler would go to train. To try to better himself. And so we find, to the probable surprise to many watching on TV, the GCW World Heavyweight Champion. Jeff Easthope. To see him in such a... shall we say, less than clean environment, is quite a change from his usual stomping grounds. But despite that, the champ appears at ease, reclining on a steel folding chair, watching the two men who are currently grappling in the ring.)
(Judging by his get-up; his wrestling tights, boots, complimented by a Triumvirate t-shirt; it would appear that Easthope has arrived to test his own ability against those of the regulars. This has not gone unnoticed, as people who walk by him look at him unsurely. They know who he is, but would not expect a GCW superstar to train at their gym.)
(Easthope is looking on at the actions of the two men in the ring. His expression is unreadable; neither intense, nor jovial, nor angry. He simply watches. As the seconds tick by, Easthope finally deigns to address the camera, talking to it without taking his eyes off the ring.)
"An insult is what it is. To take the finest men in this sport, both in terms of skill and class, and to stick them in a fiasco like the one on
Superfights... it's just disgraceful."
"An act of disrespect by Thomas Hale, Jeremy Enigk, and the GCW as a whole; directed toward the men who rule here. That can sum up Superfights.
A slap in the face that was borne out of jealousy. For what else could it be?" (Easthope pauses as the two men in the ring exchange a quick combination of chain-wrestling holds, neither man really getting the upper hand.)
"Everyone here is jealous of us... jealous because we're on top of the world. We're the big draws, the main events, whatever you want to call it. We know how to get to the top, and how to stay on top, and that just drives everybody crazy!"
"So, what happens? What the the product of all this anger? Well, apparantly the result is being hated by the entire organization... being made out for fools by the front office... and gaining absolutely no respect whatsoever."
(Easthope stops, as if to think, while he taps a finger against the metal chair.) "This will not be tolerated. GCW, you want us mad? You want the *Tri*umvirate angry? You saw what John did to Jordan Thorn not too long ago... *that* is the result of getting under our skin, folks. If you people want it to happen again, just keep it up."
(Easthope, for the first time, takes his attention off the ring to focus it on the camera.)
"Why do you annoy us so? What would you have us do, anyway? Act like we like the fans? Act like low-income rednecks so they can relate to us? Fat chance. As far as being a nice guy... they say nice guys finish last, and if you don't believe that, well... Thorn had to prove that he was a lying, back-stabbing coward before he was signed for a one-on-one title shot. What does that tell you?"
"GCW, get it through your heads. We are *not* nice people. We are not so-called 'good guys'. What we *are*, is the best at what we do.
Perfection. If you don't like it, too bad. We don't change who we are for anyone, not even Thomas Hale."
(Easthope momentarily turns his attention to the wrestling ring, before facing the camera again.)
"Speaking of which, I think Thomas Hale had better consider who he's dealing with. After all, should our most incompetent president suffer some sort of harm, what could he do? Set a fine? Isn't it a good thing that John and I are quite rich..."
"But that isn't for now. The future perhaps, so Hale... just watch who's toes you step on."
(Easthope, who it would seem has been sitting in the chair for quite a while, cracks his knuckles, and yawns.)
"There is quite a lengthy line to use that ring... but why do I wish to use it, you might ask? Why, when it is common knowledge that I myself own much higher quality... and much cleaner... equipment? Simply because I feel it's time for a change of pace. The travesty that happened last week, it won't happen again. I can assure you of that."
"To that end, I've come here. Here, to where men like Jeremy Enigk train. For I have sparred against men aplenty who abide by the rules, and use pure skill... but I must admit that perhaps I should train for Enigk, by taking on men like him. Men who won't hesitate to break the rules... or your neck. Men who would consider a boot to the nether regions to be a submission hold. It is men like these that I shall contest myself against, and it is men like these that I shall conquer. For how can they stand against the
'Excellence'? The master of the squared circle?" (One of the men in the ring hits his opponent with a vicious german suplex that bends the victim's neck at an unnatural angle. The three count is administered, and the winner checks to see if the loser is hurt. Easthope cracks a smile.)
"Cardinal mistake, right there. When your opponent's down, don't let him get back up. Break him. Because if you don't, if that guy there doesn't, then it might be *his* neck that is snapped the next time they meet."
"Mercy is a virtue that cannot be afforded in this buisness. Remember that, Enigk. Next time we meet; one sign of weakness, one indication of an injury, and I'll exploit it like you wouldn't believe. For we *will* meet again. I will not allow this matter to be closed in such a ridiculous way. We'll meet, and when that happens, you'll find out why
'Excellence' is not just a nickname, it's a literal truth." (Easthope turns his attention back to the two wrestlers, the winner now helping the loser out of the ring. Easthope looks around, and notices that several men are converging on him, apparantly to challenge the champ.
Easthope smiles.) ___________________________________________________________________ There ya have it. Tell me what you think. ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Jeff Easthope - He's man enough to eat salsa made in New York City "Whaddya mean Barq's has bite?" -Johnny
