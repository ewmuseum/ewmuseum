My First Ever RP....

19 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, after hours of searching my hard drive, I have finally run across HADES's FIRST EVER roleplay. This is all the way back from January of 1997, and it was for a little fed called the IWCW, which was run by [name withheld], who handles Jeremy Thranton of IWF/WOW fame.... Keep in mind that before I wrote this roleplay, I had absolutely no clue what the hell "flashing" was! I had to ask Derek, I felt so stupid. Needless to say, I think I've improved a lot.....
=========================================================== (The setting is an old run-down steel mill along the Ohio River. The camera is shaking as if someone is physically moving or pushing the cameraman)
CAMERAMAN: C'mon Hades, I'm doing what you told me to, don't be so rough. (Hades comes on camera dressed in faded blue jeans, black boots and a black t-shirt. He is carrying a baseball bat. And as always, he looks pissed.)
HADES: NO, you are NOT doing what I told you to. I told you to film and all I hear is your mouth flapping. Now shut the hell up!!! I don't think I told you to talk, DID I??? Or did that slip my mind? Well?
CAMERAMAN: No, you didn't ask me to talk. HADES: The why in the hell are you still talking?? No one wants to hear you, you're nothing but a prop. You keep your trap shut and let me do the talking.
Now this is only my first interview that I've done for IWCW so I'll try to keep it short because you'll be hearing A LOT more out of me in the months to come. Especially, when I clean up on whatever 4 pieces of crap I draw in the heavyweight title tournament, but I'll get to that in a second.
First off, I don't know who the hell President Kilne thinks he's fooling giving these bullcrap excuses why he doesn't have enough wrestlers in his little federation here. The reason is simple. It's because I'm here. I'm sure there was a lot of guys considering entering the IWCW until they heard ol'
Hades was here, then they ran away with their tail between their legs. Not that I blame them, I wouldn't want to step in the ring with a bad-ass mother like me either. Fortunately, I never had to and I never will have to because there's no one as good as me.
Now, about the 16 man tourney. That is, IF it ends up being a 16 man tourney. I doubt there's 15 guys man enough to enter because they're all scared they might end up having to get in the ring with me one of the rounds.
Dark Bishop....hahaha....you're a brave man accepting this match, either that or just STUPID! I prefer the latter. So, are you into chess, Bishop? Well, in my eyes, son, you ain't a bishop, you're a queen! And I'm gonna whoop your ass, little girl, and any other moron that's stupid enough to step his foot in my ring! I'm making an example of you Dark Bishop, so maybe the other wrestlers here will think twice before asking for an ass-whoopin' from me. So, make sure you eat well these next couple weeks Bishop, because once February
Fury is over, you're gonna be eating through a straw! You and anyone else who gets in my way!!! THIS IS MY FEDERATION!!! And everyone's gonna have to learn that real quick or I'll have to beat that fact into all of your carcasses!
It's a new era in IWCW.....the Hades era!!! Welcome to my world!!! ............now I'm tired of looking at this damn camera..... (Hades takes his bat to the side of the camera. The screen goes black......) =========================================================== Yeah, I've definitely improved in the last two years..... :-) [name withheld] Handler of HADES......God of Wrestling #29 1997 RSPWF 200 #132 1998 RSPWF 200 IWF/WOW, UCWF Current Title(s): None
