[Flash#2] "Homeless" Joe

30 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWFites, Here is another flash I did for MTTM. This is a rebuttal to a flash that Mad Goat did, the one where he went around hitting homeless people with rocks. This has quite a bit of jokes involving the Adam Sander cd, so be looking out for those. I was really happy with this one, and I hope you will be too
:-) Enjoy, [name withheld] (The following is entitled "Joe's Revenge") [Random Beginning Mode: On] [Trumpets Sounds. A snare drum taps lightly. On the black screen, several words in white letters appear...]
B.U.M Productions proudly presents... [the words fade out. The trumpets sound again, and the snare drum continues to tap] a Joe Boxcar film... [an orchestra begins playing the Star Wars theme. There is a flash and we find ourselves in outer space. A bright object comes towards the screen. As it moves closer, we are able to tell that it's a meteor with the title of the film engraved on it...]
BUM WARS [The film stops. Leaving the screen black, a notice appears..] Dear Viewers, We were only able to bum enough money to pay for the opening credits, so that's it for the movie. It was a great opening don't you think?
Thank you, The Staff [the notice fades out] [Random Beginning mode: OFF] [The screen fills with static. The static stops, revealing a black and white shot of some type of farm. It's night. The camera is quite unsteady, the cameraman is obviously an amateur. The cameraman pans around, eventually stopping at a sign. The camera zooms in allowing us to read the words
"Goats, Sheep, and other stinky animals." Next to the sign, stands the
Bummiest of all bums himself, "Homeless" Joe. The camera shakes some more]
Joe- "Damnit Smelly Carl, hold the camera still." Smelly Carl- "Sorry Joe.." Joe- "Oh...and don't even get so much as a scratch on that camera, I gotta take it back to Radio Shack before it opens."
Smelly Carl- "Ok Joe.." [Joe clears his throat and looks at the camera] Joe- "Well...Goat...I hope you had a good time. Whackin' the less fortunate on the head with a rock....well...that tells me a lot about the kinda goat you are."
[Joe laughs] Joe- "Well....KID....Ole 'Homeless' Joe can play that game too...oh yes....I love games." [Joe pauses] Joe- "but before we play, let me just remind you In case you've forgotten, of our last encounter. It was my first match...EVER...both you and myself entered the ring with four other men, and that night, you tasted my New York
Style Knuckle Sandwich. Well, Goat, I'm sure you and those raisins you call testicles remember how badly that hurt...Be ready Goat, cause you will more than likely be feeling it again....Oh, and by the way Goat, if I were you,
I'd invest a couple of those bucks you made from whoring out Mother Nature to Damian Bruno's dead beat daddy...in a cup."
[Joe laughs] Joe- "Now on with the games...I'm sure you know well what _THIS_ is" [He pulls an object out from underneath his shirt. We hear the goats shriek.] Joe- "That's right Goat...it's a.... __HICKORY STICK__ [Ominous music plays] Joe- "Can you guess what I'm going to use this stick for?" [pause] Joe- "That's right..." [Joe lets out a sinister laugh] Joe- "GOAT WHACKIN'!" [Joe hops the fence, followed by Smelly Carl who is video taping the whole thing. Joe runs around crazy, chasing the Goats, who are attempting to get away. Joe runs up behind one of the unsuspecting goats]
(WHACK!!! WHACK!!! WHACK!!!) Goat- "BBBBBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!!!" Joe(laughing madly)- "BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH!" [The goat, who has just been whacked right on the arse, runs around crazy, rubbing is ass against the ground. Joe continues chasing all the goats]
(WHACK!) Goat2- "BBBBBAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHH!!!!!" Joe- "COME ON GOATEES....IT'S WHACKITY WHACK TIME!!!! BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH!!!!!" (WHACK!) (BAH!) (WHACK!) (BAAAAHHHH!) (WHACK!) [the whacking continues until nearly all the goats have been whacked! Joe laughs as he watches all the poor defenseless goat's rubbing their backsides on the ground]
Joe- "See goat...games are fun...and at MTTM, we'll play a new game...it's called the 'me kickin' you ass game'"
[Joe pauses] Joe- "So stinky nuts...remember this..." [Joe pauses and prepares to deliver the greatest catch phrase a homeless wrestler has ever though up, in a Pillowesque style none the less.]
YOU ARE TRASH~! [pause] And I eat trash for breakfast... [we hear a door open and a man yell..] Man- "Hey...whaddya doin' ta mah Goats!" (BOOM) [A shotgun is fired numerous times] Man- "AHHH! Mah GOATS! ETHEL-MAY...GET OUT HERE...WE GOT TRESPASSERS!!!!" Joe- "UH OH....SMELLY CARL!! LET'S GET OUT OF HERE!!!" Smelly Carl- "Uh...ok Joe" [The camera cuts off, filling the screen with static. The scene fades out] [name withheld] The ORIGINAL 5 star General of the RSPWF Army RSPWF Army Founder KNI Founder [detail withheld] Handler of: "Homeless" Joe Boxcar [NAW] Record: 24-2-0 #76 on the 1998 RSPWF 200 #2 on the 1999 Top 50 Brawlers poll #12 on the RSPWF Mid-Year 200 #22 on the EW-Zone top 100 #1 on your Mom's poll MTTM Participant B.U.M- "Homeless" Joe and Larry [NWP] Record: 0-0-0 Quote: "NOW for even more great news on the UWF and [name withheld]!
