Hey!!! Here's my roleplay...I guarantee you like...GNW Vice...I'm so damn good

8 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 (Scene opens up to a corridor that's pretty dark inside but you can see quite well. While walking down a hall you see doors upon doors. Suddenly the Camera jerks and then points towards a door to it's left. A white man who you can't make out comes into view. He has a key ring with a group of keys in his hand.
There's probably about 20 keys on the ring. The camera pans up towards the door. The door is a darkish mahoghony color with 5 locks on it. One of the locks is on the doorknob. All of the locks including the doorknob, are all made of shiny silver...and the actual door had cutting in it that remind you of the 17 hundreds colonial style doors. It might even be an authentic because of it's realism. He then takes the very next key and places it in the second lock. He then twists and opens it. The man with the keys is searching through the keys savagely. He's taken a key and tried fitting it in the top lock about
5 times already. He takes another key and places in the lock. This key fits but it won't open the lock. He moves one key over and places it in the top lock. He twists and unlocks the first lock does the same thing for the next three locks. When he's finally unlocked all the doors he motions for the camera to come near. He quickly opens the door and then goes in. The camera quickly follows. Once in the room, which is dark, The man closes the door.
Which makes it completely black? You can't see a thing. As you hear the keys that the man had clanking against each other, another door opens. You can see the door because of it's shadow. You can see the shadow because of a light that's flickering in the next room. The camera walks into the next room where you can see a television that's on. The television is fairly small and doesn't give off much light. You still can't see anything. The camera takes a few steps towards the television when suddenly the lights go on. You can see a bed with somebody in it and under the covers. Except for his feet...he's wearing white Nike socks with a black Nike symbol. The camera pans around the room where you can see a fairly small room that packed with things. There's a stove, refrigerator, television with a VCR, tapes all over the floor. The television is now visible where you can now see everybody's favorite wrestler...The Guru. The camera moves closer to the bed when you see the man walk into view again. You can now see that it's Mr. Buttass. He walks over to the bed and taps on the man that's obviously sleeping. The sleeping man does not move so Mr. Buttass taps on his shoulders again. Suddenly the sleeping man leaps forward in the bed as if he was just waking from a nightmare...he desperately looks around trying to figure out what the hell is going on. The man is none other than Illusive Dumbass. He's still bald and he looks like hell. His eye's are blood shot and he looks like he just woke up from a coma)
Mr. Buttass: Wake up man...the DECWF camera's are here... Dumbass: Huuuhhhh what?...Who's here (Squinting because his eye's still haven't come into focus)
Mr. Buttass: Hellllloooo!!! Any body there...I said the DECWF camera's are here. Dumbass: Ooooohh!!! (He now peers up towards the camera's) Mr. Buttass: Yeah...so say something... Dumbass: Uhhh oooh okay... (Illusive Dumbass looks at the camera again and then looks at the television. He then looks at his arm, probably expecting a watch to be there...which there wasn't. So he looks back at the television)
Dumbass: What the hell is this shit...I must've fell a sleep over an hour again and this damn Guru character is still talking...Will somebody make him shut up!!!!!!!
(The camera focuses in on the television where you see Guru still)  Guru on TV: Shut up!! You son of a miscreant...my bastion of cypher and I are here to kick some HOLY SHITBALLS!!!! Blah blah blah blah blah...All of a sudden you see remote control fly by and hit the television)
 Dumbass: SOMEBODY SHUT THAT SON OF A BITCH OFF!!!!!!!!!!!!!!  (Mr. Buttass runs over to the television and shuts it off)  Dumbass: That's more like it...uhh where was I...Oh yeah!...as you all know
I've been gone from DECWF for quite sometime now...sooooo let me explain where
I've been. Well it all started a few weeks ago. I finally got out of the hospital so I took my Jagged Edge momento...the movie projector...with me. I noticed that the
X-treme film was still in there so I decided I should get it to him so I went out to see him. Well when I finally got to him...I gave it to him...he said thanx...and somehow or someway he persuaded me into taking that acid that he calls god. So I took it and well...all I have to say is DAMN!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
That's the sheeeeeeit. It's powerful as all hell. I mean I thought nothing of it at first but when I went into a nearby Jewish store (Illusive Dumbass is not
Jewish...he's just high) I saw a dradel. I could've sworn that that dradel was a mexican god that told me that I had to go kill all fat black women who were pregnant between the months of June and July last year. So I went off onto my crusade...Thank god Mr. Buttass found me before I did something really bad. I mean I truly know what it means to go to Planet Motha Fucker. It's definitely a treat. Unfortunately I must never do it again...and that's why I'm here.
I'm in denial...I told Mr. Buttass to lock me up and give me everything I need for a whole week...of course him and Larrita came and gave me company but it wasn't enough...so I asked for all the interviews. While watching about 40 interviews I noticed a lot of things. First off some mystery guy starts to talk shit about me and how my vocabulary is revolved around "fuck". Well tough guy...I got two words for you...Fuck...and FUCK!!!! So shut the hell up you pansy ass momma's boy. Don't talk shit about somebody you just don't know because they might have to beat the shit out of you. Then there was this Flash guy or Flash man...or something like that...well anyway's the comic bite off said something about how I had a contract with ICW...
(Looks at Mr. Buttass) Dumbass: Was that the company? (Mr. Buttass shrugs) Dumbass: Well anyways that's not really that important...what's really important is that this guy challenges me...Yeah you heard it right...HE challenged ME! Now usually I back down from jobbers who bite off comic book hero's names, but I wouldn't dare back down from the first person in DECWF to actually challenge me. So it's on now buddy. I want a match with fast man at the next match...that's if there's anybody that disagrees. So it's settled tough guy. Now as far as this ICW stuff...well I joined and then quit when I realized how sucky it was...and that was a long time ago...I'm surprised you even knew about it...in fact I had forgotten until you brought it up...which reminds me, How did you find that out? I guess that's not to important though...at least I got a challenge.
(Illusive Dumbass takes in a deep breath) Illusive Dumbass: Lastly I would just like to shed my words about the Guru...What is your major malfunction? Huh...You go on jabbering all day and all night...I have a question...Do you ever shut up? I would just like to know...I mean is there ever a time in which you pause for like maybe a minute or so? If there is I would like to know about it...so that I can be there to share in the momentous occasion in which the Guru actually shuts his mouth.
Now that I've spoken my word about you your probably going to insert me into your next interview...and god knows you got the time to do it...but I'm begging you...Please don't...Try to keep your interviews as short as possible. Short and to the point if you will. Which is exactly what you don't do, because when
I do actually manage to stay awake for your interviews I try to find a point to it all...that's all I'm looking for? A FREAKING POINT!!!!!!! Your enough to drive a group of monks nuts. May I offer you what you really need. That's to enjoy a stay at Planet Motha Fucka...in fact you should take so much that I'll never have to listen to you again...instead you'll be permanently stuck in
Planet Motha Fucka...I feel sorry for everybody that has to get stuck with you though. Well that's all from my humble abode...I must catch some sleep...cuz I think there's another Guru interview and those interviews will knock you out...there worse than a lullaby.
(Illusive Dumbass lays back down angrily mumbling things like "miscreant" and
 "bastion of cypher" and "blah blah blah blah blah". The camera turns and exits the room. Fade to a purplish green)
Ish...Damn I'm good Controller of the Illusive Dumbass...A CDW and GNW legend. A CDW US title holder. Ranked 5th in DECWF. Vice President of the new GNW.
