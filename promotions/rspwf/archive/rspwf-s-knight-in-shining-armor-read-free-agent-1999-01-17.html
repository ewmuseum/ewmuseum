Here's a sample rp I did for the INWI. Pretty good rp. I'm a free agent and lookiing for a fed. Judge and critiqur please...thanks..The RSPWF's Knight in
Shining Armor....
["Can I Get A" BY Jay-Z begins to play throughout the bulding as you hear an rather large amount of boos from the crowd. You then see Carl Grant walk out from the entrance with a pair gold framed shades, blue jeans and a black INWI
HotSpot shirt, and a rather huge cigar positioned betwen two fingers as he struts to the ring and pulls out a mic from his back pocket.]
CG - "Ladies and Gents, Boys and Girls, children of all ages. . .I'd like to present to you, your _NEXT_ INWI Lightweight Champion, the one, the only. . .and the WONDERFUL. . . .
!!! C H R I S I T I A N M A D I S O N !!! ["Way It's Going Down" By Shaq begins to blare throughout the PA system as you hear an rather large thunderous explosion in back near the entrance.
Cut up pieces of Paper glitter begins to fall from the air and into the crowd as the lights goes out, and Christian "Spotlight" Madison steps out in black jeans and his "Spotlight" Jersey. Green clouds of smoke arose from underneath the entrance and spotlight shaped spotlights beams down onto the floor leading him to the ring. He has his right arm throttled into the air and a huge smile on his face as he leads into the ring with a mic in his hand.]
CM - "Keenan...Keenan...Keenan, when will you ever learn? You jus' can't match the speed and ability of which this one man holds. [Smiles] Y'see, as a young child a man once told me that, "Money talks...and bullshit...
walks". And right now, in my mind, you're jus' bullshittin'! You claim to the INWI Lightweight Champion? [Chuckles] Well think again chump, because as far as I'm concerned that belt of yours is comin' home with his majesty, a _TRUE_ champion. Remember all the good ol' time that you held that belt `cause good memories is all you're goin' to have left."
[Christian pauses for a few moments...] CM - "Keenan, I'm goin' to take what means the very most to you...and that's
_MY_ title thangs around your waist. [Grins] You and your friends can sit back and relax all ya want...but when it everything boils down, we'll know for damn sure, who is actually king, or shall I say champion of the lightweights! Let this be an a lesson to you and all the rest of the losers in the back, yea...that's right. Vitrone, Deegan, Jacobs, Apples and who
- eva' else I left out. This is truly one of those moments when you could jus' die for. And Jeffrey, remember, everywhere you go..I'll be one step right behind until I finally get my hands around ya scrawny little ass."
[Madison passes over the mic to Grant as he then begins to pace.] CG - "Now, I've been in this sport for many of years, and never have I anticip - ated a match such as this stature! Not only will this be a classic...but yet my boy Madison's first crack at championship gold, and I can guarantee ya that we, will come out on top...as always! Now, there's a point in every man's life that he finds himself upon a time and period of poetic justice, an'
unfortunately for you...that time of reckoning has arisen upon ya. Keenan, your fate and title now lays in our hands my friend." [Christian Madison looks around the arena with an angered look upon his face as he then stops and heads out of the ring. He climbs outside and walks round the ring a few steps and then picks up an chair and slides back into the ring as he takes the microphone away from Mr. Grant.]
CM - "This chair here, represents victory and pain as well. Pain of which I've endured for the past 23 years,,,and victory, from what use it can bring.
There's only one thing on my pretty little mind, and that's winning, winning at the cost of anything! My passion to wrestle and become champion is now reality...everythin' a man could dream of..is about to come true."
[Madison sets up the chair and sits...] CM - "It's time for a lesson in defeat...because Keenan that's all you'll ever experience my friend. There's nothin' else in the world that could bring me more joy then to capture my first championship in none other then the
INWI. Keenan, you're my first victim...my ingenuity of this sport is jus' simply remarkable. Goldenboy, or whateva' you wanna call ya'self, the only way you'd be able to get rid of me is to literally kill me. And believe me,
I won't
leave that ring until I hear your bones snap...or til i hear the words
_I QUIT_
and that'll be as soon as I hit the ring. There's only one spotlight here, and that's _me_...not Vinny Vitrone...he's a damn has been for god's sake, and if he has a problem with it then he can stick it. Because I don't have time for jabronies who feast off their own ejaculation. Vitrone, ywanna go around and steal my thunder? By callin' ya'self the Spotlight? As I said, I'm the only true spotlight around here chump...and ya got live wit it. Keenan,
Vitrone...your numbers up, the _true_ spotlight waits for _no_ man."
[Madison gets up and folds up the chair and then begins to head out of the ring and heads back toward backstage as the scene fades out.]
