=========================================================== Your Name: [name withheld] E-mail Address: [detail withheld] Wrestler Information ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Wrestler Name: Brass Knux (Brass Knuckles, but likes Brass Knux much more.) Height: 6'3" Weight: 249 lbs. Age: 26 Hometown: Philadelphia, Pennsylvania Alignment: Tweener Appearance: Caucasian male; dark brown hair closely cut to the scalp, grayish eyes, small nose, pearly white teeth, cleanly shaven face;
Tall figure, muscular neck, well toned upper body, solid mid-section, muscular legs, rather hairy shins, buff biceps and triceps, tattoo on right arm with letters "BK" wrapped in barbed wire, strong back, two long scars on his left arm.
Ring Attire: Black T-shirt with a design, blue jeans shorts, Nike knee pads, black Nike street sneakers.
Background Information on Wrestler: Brass Knuckles was born under the name of
Jason M. Williams in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania on April 27th. Being a child in
Philly, he was automatically marked as a "bad boy", a "street punk". Since he was four years of age, people bullied him, treated him like a nothing. The only person he could look up to was his older brother, Michael Williams. The two brothers did everything together. Michael taught Jason how to fight the World.
Little Jason loved his older brother. As the boys got older, they grow closer to each other. At age fifteen, Jason and his twenty year old brother joined a nearby Philadelphia street gang known as "The Advocates" . The two felt like they belonged, felt wanted. But, this gang was devoted to trouble. Stealing, fighting, and weapons were their style.
Therefore, this become Jason and Michaels's style. The two brothers became full blown criminals, and they liked it.
After spending over a year in the gang, Jason earned his official gang nick name. His fellow gang members got to choose the name. Since they saw Jason as a pure brawler, they called him Brass Knuckles. Jason loved the name and thought it fit him well. His brother, known as Da' Slick, loved the gang, too. It was their new home, and their new life.
When Jason turned eighteen, the gang was still going. But, at this time, Jason and Michael began to mature. They wanted a real life. Michael wanted to be an architect, and Jason wanted to be a professional wrestler and be like his idol,
"the Eraser" Steve Bronco. They informed the gang face to face that they wanted to leave. Of coarse, the gang didn't take this the right way. The gang considered it a degrading insult. Somehow, Jason and Michael got out, but the gang promised the two a painful pay back.
About a week later, Jason Williams (Brass Knuckles) found his brother shot in an alley. On top of his forehead, a note was placed. The note was labeled: The
Advocates. Knuckles was furious. He let out his rage by mauling every single kid he saw with an Advocate jacket. Night after night, he destroyed another member. Until one day, when Knuckles was caught by a police officer. After that, he was sentenced to one year in prison. While in jail, Knuckles was involved with many prison riots and started many fights.
Nevertheless, Knuckles was released one year later. Then, a man known as "Hardcore" Scottie Russell heard about Knuckles. He read of his great fighting skill. Some how, Russell got a hold of Knuckles. He introduced him to the wrestling ring. He taught him the ropes.
Every day and night, Russell would train Knuckles. He pushed Knuckles until
Knuckles could go no more. He whipped this young man with no direction in life into a strong, confinent, skillful young wrestler. Knuckles was in the best shape of his life.
Russell started to send out resumes to every wrestling organization alive. Knuckles received a surplus of try outs. Within a couple of months, Knuckles was hired by a independent organization known as Hardcore Championship
Wrestling. Knuckles wrestled about a year in this organization. Knuckles then advanced to Online Championship Wrestling [OCW]. There, he became a champion. Loved by millions, Knuckles won the World Championship Title, defeating Eddie Jacobs.
Within time, OCW folded and Knuckles was out of work. In about a week, though,
Knuckles was hired by one of eWrestling's finest organizations, National
Extreme Wrestling [N.E.W.] In N.E.W., Knuckles had a history making feud with
"Desire" Brandon Bloom. This helped him win the N.E.W. North American Title. He later would win the N.E.W. World Tag Team Championship with Brandon Bloom.
Knuckles was doing great. He was a top superstar and a world-class athlete. But, Knuckles's bad side came out again. He began to raise hell all over the
R.S.P-W.F! He was running rampid all over ewrestling. He was hated by many.
Knuckles's made lots of enemies in an organization known as INWI, and rival organization of N.E.W.'s.
Nonetheless, Knuckles continues to do great in N.E.W., as he recently won the
N.E.W. Intercontinental Championship. He also competes in IeWC, G-Pro, and
HHWO. He looks to rise farther up the charts in the near future.
Also, recently, Brass Knuckles entered a Newcomers tournament known as KNI. He advanced to the finals after defeating RSPWF Newcomer of the Half-Year Gino
Valentini, Lee "Starman" Clark, and the #1 Cruiserweight in eWrestling "Iron
Curtain" Ivan Ramius. He then lost in the finals against Steve Wallace.
Previous/Current Titles: OCW World Heavyweight Championship GwA International Championship N.E.W. North American Championship N.E.W. World Tag Team Championship N.E.W.
