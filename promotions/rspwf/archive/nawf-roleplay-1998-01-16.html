NAWF roleplay

16 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(In his locker room, "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh sits in a metal chair. He is wearing baggy jeans and a T-shirt. Bishop is standing in the back of the room wearing an Axis Powers T-shirt with an ICON patch and he is also wearing jeans and a trench coat. "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh gets a glass of water from the table that is beside him, takes a sip, and then begins to speak.)
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh: "Tommy Shades, I look around my locker room and see a boring room with barely anything going on. I look and see Bishop and wonder why he stands by me. I think and ponder about this for a moment and I soon realize that the reasons are simple. I am the man that this sport revolves around. I raise my glass and toast you all in petty fear. I see by the evil looks in the people's eyes that I am a marked man and a man who will be entering many battles. I have been around the world and I have seen many abhorrible situations. Hell, I have caused many of those situations. This
Sunday, we will face off in a showdown of the ages. Who knew that a rookie, such as myself, would be involved in such an important battle so early in his tenure with the NAWF? I am no rookie, but I will still have to prove myself worthy to receive the recognition that I think I deserve. I look at you responding to what I said and I say that this man wants a war. Well, Tommy
Shades, war is not a problem with me. As a matter of fact, I thrive on war. I thrive on pain. Soon, I will bring your demise. You decided to take it upon yourself to challenge me when you knew that you would be in for the fight of your life. I don't care what kind of match this is, but this is something that needs to be settled. So, I accepted your challenge, Tommy Shades. I had no hesitation in doing so. I went out and said yes to your challenge. Now, it is set. The match will take place on Sunday. I can't wait. You say that you are offended by what I had to say and what I stand for. I don't care if you read the Bible to your daughter, beat your daughter, love your daughter, or molest your daughter. Your daughter has nothing to do with this. This all has to do with you and me. In the ring, we will face off in a match between two gladiators. One man will leave walking and the winner, and one man will leave on a stretcher and as the loser. Tommy Shades, I do not plan on losing this or any other match in my career. I will win this match at any cost and do whatever it takes to show these people and to prove to my fellow wrestlers that
I am no pushover. I am as true to my words as I say I am. Pain won't stop me.
Pressure won't stop me. Most of all, you won't stop me!" Bishop: "Tommy Shades... I have seen you around and I have to say that you don't impress me the least. I see that you have talent, but you cannot match up with the likes of the 'Human Torture Rack' Ian Walsh. You take offense to what he says and so you challenge him. You see, that is where you made your mistake. I have seen people come and go. People have said that they can beat
Ian and show the world that he is just talk. Those people have failed to do what they said. Some are alive and some are dead. This man has been in many federations and has won many titles. Sure, there is some punk in the TEW who claims to be better. That guy cannot hold his own in this federation. That is why he is in the bush leagues. I would like to see him come here and say what
Tommy Shades has been saying. TEW happens to be a bias federation, and they lost the 'Human Torture Rack' Ian Walsh because of that. NAWF isn't bias.
They also have the talent and competition that Ian wants. Tommy Shades, you are the competition that Ian wants. He is anxious to get into the ring and tear you limb from limb and show you just who the master is. When Sunday comes around, I want you to remember that you aare facing the elite. I also want you to prepare to meet your demise!"
("Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh stands up and puts the glass of water back on the table. He walks around the room and looks at the bare walls.)
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh: "These walls are baron. All that is seen is the cynder. The concrete blocks that make up these walls... Tommy Shades, can you see these walls? They are stationary and do nothing else besides standing there. You will be like these walls. The difference is, is that you will fall when I hit you. Look at yourself. You see a clean man. You see a loving and caring man. I am a determined man. I am a man full of pride and destruction.
My rampage is your downfall. I go out there to win and to destroy. You like to fight for the good guys. I pity you, Tommy Shades. You make me sick to my stomach. I look at the crowd and spit upon them. Those fiends and fools.
Cheer for the meek! I will destroy your heroes and show the world that I am the true master! Tommy Shades, you are a petty man. You have good intentions, but your motives point you into the wrong direction. There is a time and a place to do the right things. I never do anything for the crowds or for the good of mankind. I have killed in cold blood. I have maimed with evil intent.
I have run many out of this sport. Now, I will face you. You will not be a difficult opponent, but you will be tough enough for a good workout. I will use you as a stepping stone for my success, and your carcus will be the victim of my path of rage. Win if you can! Survive if I let you!"
(The camera zooms in a little to show an evil grin form on the face of the
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh. Bishop laughs a little and sighs. "Human
Torture Rack" Ian Walsh looks down at the ground and shakes his head. He laughs. Bishop walks to the door, looks back, opens the door and leaves.
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh walks to the chair and sits down again. Camera fades out.)
