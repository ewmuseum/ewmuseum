[Critique please] VLAD DRACULA X FIRST ROLEPLAY

20 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you have the time Critique for me please. Thanks in advance... (We open up in downtown Seattle, Washington. We are in the "goth" section where all the Goth clubs are. The camera man walks up the left side of the street until they go into a club dubiously named "The Raven". We walk through the front double doors and once we get inside we see multi-colored lights going on and off, people dancing, Rob Zombie's "Dragula" is blaring through the speakers. Most of the people are dressed in black and red.
Most of it leather, some silk. We continue to our right and through a huge crowd at the bar. We finally make our way to a room thats has no doors. We walk in and the whole place is lit up with a black lava lamp,making the whole room darker and more creepier than it is,sitting on a end table in the center of the room. On the far end we see movement. Then a smooth, silky voice comes out of the darkness.)
?:...Please....sit down on the chair over there.... (The camera man looks around and notices a chair which seems to have been specifically placed there for him.)
?:.....and dont turn on your light I have had enough light for one day.... (It is obviously a mans voice but we also hear a hint of some womensvoices.) ?:....My name is Vlad Dracula the tenth.....I hail from England..... (Long pause as to increase anticipation.) X:...I assume you wanted to talk to me here about my upcoming match with
Uchrin....hmmm...indeed...quite a character as I recall from his interview earlier today....curious hair style...but he lacks on power....and intelligence.....hmmmm....
(The silence comes again....this time much longer.) X:....I have been chosen to fight in the first card ever here in Hammerlock....a honor I am sure....but with more dire consequences then you know of my good man....I am not at liberty to suggest or even hint at what I am talking about....for through the natural course of time you will see what i mean....
(Vlad giggles a little before continuing.) X:....What many things you mere mortals take for granted....and yet you say you love life...and all that encompasses it...but yet you berate it...curse it....believe me my good camera boy....you will look back on this day sometime in the future and say to yourself that you wished you kissed the ground you walked on....but of course that again must come through the natural course of time...now go away little bird....fly fly away...fly fly away....
(The camera fades out as we continue to hear Vlads voice. We cut to a commercial for Hammerlock merchandise.)
**** I have just started using this character in Hammerlock. I believe I can take him a long way as long as I work out his character right....so anyways critique away....
