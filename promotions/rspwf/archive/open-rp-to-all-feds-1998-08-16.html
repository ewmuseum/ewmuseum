(Scene opens in a small town church. The walls, once white are now gray and black from a fire that ruined this place long ago. All of the stained glass windows are shattered allowing the moonlight to sep through and brighten up the abandoned place. There are three rows of pews, covered in black soot and pushed around so they are no longer lined up with each other. The air has a sickening smell of fire. The floor of the church is carpeted with a scarlet rug that has patches of burn marks all the way down its path. Looking up, the roof has caved in partially letting even more of the night sky illuminate the inside. At the altar, there is a white pulpit. It too has been burned somewhat, but still it stands with a bible on top of it. Standing behind it is
Reverend LaVey. He is shrouded in black robes, from head to foot. Around his neck is a silver cross, which is flipped upside down. His pale, bony hands are leaning on the pulpit, just peeking out of his robe. His voice is calm, but loud enough to hear.)
Reverend LaVey-"I am tired. So...very tired. Tired of watching the world go on with it's peace and happiness. Tired of being placed in situations where good is assumed to defeat evil. Tired of allowing all that is considered holy to continue to reign. That is why I must put a stop to these things. I must return to a world of violence and madness. In order to avenge the devil, I must act in his place. The wrestling world has been graced by my presence before...and just as I did then, I want to create sacrifices in the name of Satan."
(The Reverend sighs. He puts his head down almost as if in despair for th future.) Reverend LaVey-"Throughout my time on this petty earth, I have scoured the land for a place to have my work recognized. I have walked a seemingly long road from the pits of Hell itself to find a place to be accepted and have my work commended as well. Yes, there have been smaller places that have been ransacked by my presence. Many an enemy hath been burned on a cross or had a laceration to their throat from my foot. Yet, these were smaller places...much like the small towns that get no exposure. I am prepared to participate in the larger federations and completely walk through and destroy every man on their roster. I posess the single most painful move in creation...the Last Rites. With it, the biggest men have fallen, the srongest beings taken down and sent to the blackest portion of hell conceivable. Yet on the flipside, neither the frailest woman nor the smallest child has ever been spared.
When the Devil himself compels me to do his work, noone is safe...their souls are weak and meager and must be put to rest."
(The Reverend grips the cross on his neck with both of his pale hands.) Reverend LaVey-"I know there are bigger places out there, where sinners are running around praying to a God that they'll never meet. There fate is doomed to be in the underworld, a place where nightmares are grim realities. Everyone must be taken to meet their maker someday...and I walk along as that messenger. I offer no condolensces, only fear. Fr whence you see the Reverend coming along, know that it may very well be the last breath you are ever going to take, the last drops of blood that shall ever pump through your beating heart. I have seen the face of
Satan...and I shall deliver anyone to him as long as they are willing to face me. I am the keeper of souls and when the Reverend begins his sermon...not even God can save you. I implore all of the federation presidents...make a place on your roster...and watch as the Reverend eliminates your sinners."
(The Reverend takes his hands off the cross. He picks up his bible and turns from the camera. The camera focuses through the stained glass and fades out on the moon.)
