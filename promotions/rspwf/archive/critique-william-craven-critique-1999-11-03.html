[CRITIQUE] William Craven [CRITIQUE]

3 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've been experimenting... This series of flashes is something of a departure for me. Production videos with a "hidden camera" kind of setup, the characters not acknowledging the existence of the man behind the lens.
I've always tried to be ultra-realistic with that sort of thing. Sometimes succeeding, sometimes not.
Also, you'll notice a concrete, unvarying formula to the flashes. While limiting, I found that the consistent structure allowed me to focus more on the content and less on the setup.
That is to say, Craven's in essentially the same place each flash, a set opening, and a set closing.
Please, do read and render an opinion in this thread. I'm quite curious how this series will go over with the people at large.
----- [Blackness reigns upon the screen. The faintest of sounds is heard...breathing. Just enough noise to inform the viewer that there is an audio feed. A few seconds of this, then a slight buzz as words in white appear emblazoned upon the screen...
"Hello I must be going. I cannot stay, I came to say I must be going. I'm glad I came, but just the same, I must be going."
--Groucho Marx. A few seconds more, and all is black once again. Virtual silence, only the faint sound of breath...and finally, fading in...
Light and shadow set the scene. Dimly shown is a barren room, devoid of all material. The floor is hardwood, and the walls are white. Our view rotates, showing all four walls, and an open door, no windows...turning full circle, and coming to rest on a seated figure who was not there only a few seconds before.
Nearly a silhouette, this man hides in the most shadowed corner of this place, almost hidden right there in plain view. He sits, back pressed against the walls, knees to his chest, clutching his legs with both arms, hands locked... All in all, he looks to be in a seated fetal position.
Nearing this corner, the camera finally is able to discern details. The figure's head, ducked down, face concealed behind legs covered in shredded blue denim. The top of the head is covered in a pale, short hair, barely more than stubble. Shirtless, shoeless, lined with creases and scars...this is William Craven.
Finally, the head rears back, ice blue eyes exposed for the merest of moments before the head itself is concealed completely by a deep shadow that forms where the walls meet. The focus of his view seems to be the doorway, the only source of light in the room.]
WC: Clear... [One simple word, and the usually talkative Craven falls silent. He seems to think that, for the time being, this statement should suffice. His chest sucks breath in, and puffs it out as he remains inactive for a moment more...]
WC: So blind for so long...even when I saw clearly, it didn't register... [Silence again. For all his scattered monologue, Craven seems oddly sure of his words. Unfocused, unintelligable, and yet somehow fiercely intelligent...]
WC: I made the mistake...me... The mirror showed me...look at _me_... [Although his words don't even seem to be directed at anyone, let alone the cameraman, the camera view zooms in tight on Craven's head. Even in the low light, at this proximity his features are seen clearly. A slight grin and a tightening of the face...and Craven works the words through his clenched teeth...]
WC: I...I let it go, I let it grow, in hopes that raven locks would grow... But no raven locks for the Craven man...only downy...white...tufts... My hair's gone white... White... Now...I'm just like my father... Father... [With that, Craven picks a topic, never before addressed, out of the air and places it on the proverbial table. As sudden as it is, the wild-eyed, bald, scarred freak tries his best to shoehorn it into the monologue, speaking haltingly and yet somehow never missing a beat...]
WC: William Senior...the fighting preacher, the man who broke bones for
_GOD_!!! The white-haired menace to the papacy...and the one that made grown men wet their beds at night... I look in the mirror now, and he's lau ghing at me. He's looking down on me... He's telling me that for all this money I'm getting now, the money that supports my extended family, I've lost my dignity.
Lost it because I left a place where I was dominant to come to a place where anything can happen.
Lost it because I let a man like Grayson catch me unawares. Lost it because I let broken bones stop me from winning not once, not twice, but three weeks in a row. The first time in a _church_ of all places. The second time because I just had to brutalize some oversized walking corpse...
The third time...because a hick dropped me on my head... [Another pause, and Craven cocks his head to one side, just staring at the doorway...as if someone was there. He blinks several times, as if absorbing something...]
WC: He...he thinks he's above me... He thinks I'm a joke... He thinks I'm a shootfighter?... If he thinks any of these...then I think he's a fool... The pits, the
Kumite's...they're so far from...from that...that's what I am...
[Again he stops, trailing off into some other place. What seems to be a monologue to everyone else seems to be a dialogue to William as he listens intently to nothing at all.]
WC: Don't worry...don't worry. I'm healed now... I've got something for
Gary Grayson...I've got something for the hick...and I've got something for anyone else who gets in the way. I'll get my dignity back...and I'll burn the bridges. I'll burn them all. I'll up the ante... I've got a special toy, a new routine...
[A flicker of shadow crosses the doorway, disrupting the flow of light from it's only source. Although only momentary, it seems to have alarmed
Craven.] WC: Honey? [Craven stiffens, clumsily flopping forward onto his hands and knees...] WC: Lydia!? [Shivering terror rocks the body of this huge, battle-scarred gladiator as he staggers his way towards the doorway. The camera pans to follow him.
Craven stops in the doorway, leaning heavily upon the wall...] WC: LLLYYYDIIIAAA!!! [The screen goes instantly black, and the breath from the beginning is heard once again, only louder this time. It sucks and whooshes, and plays as musical accompaniment as the golden threads weave upon the screen.
Predictably, formulaically, they form the word... "Craven" ...upon the screen, gold on black. Glittering and glinting, this is all that is seen, the breath all that is heard...three...two...one...fade to black...]
----- [Like unto a cave in the depths of the earth, darkness sits astride the television screen. The harried beating of a hideous heart echoes throughout this nothingness, leading the mind to any number of conclusions while giving no information whatsoever... We've stepped into the void, as it were...
*THUMP* *THUMP* *THUMP* Breaking through the ether are symbols, glittering into existence as rippling water. In white they appear, reading...
"Ah, little Tommy Daggett, how I loved to hear your prayers at night, and then, you'd JUMP into bed, afraid I was waiting underneath....and I was!"
-Lucifer in the movie "Prophecy" Then the shimmering again...and all is black once more. *THUMP* *THUMP* *THUMP* Accompanying the heart now is wheezing breath. In and out it breathes... This distinct opening, the patent segueway leading to what is sure to be a view into madness... A slow fade, and a room, barren of all is shown in stark view from a doorway. White walls, brown carpet, and a single naked lightbulb dangling from the low ceiling. All goes silent...the heartbeat ceases, feeling death and sparing everyone it's ill effects and fearsome sound. The breathing stifles, and all is silent for the merest of moments when...]
Voice: Make it shut up... [Whipping about violently, the camera nearly loses it's lens bumping into the doorframe as it's holder spins to look into an empty hallway.
Empty...no one, nothing to make a noise, much less utter a word.] Voice: I...can't take much more, woman... Make...the brat...SHUT UP!!! [A frustrated hiss becomes a growling bellow at the termination of the sentence. Both cameraman and camera wheel about to show the room again.
The same room...yet subtly changed... The lightbulb on it's cord swings wildly to and fro and about in circles. The shadows cast flit about with a rapidity that seems to simulate the movement of bat wings... Again the cave analogy shines through.
Panning down ever so slightly, a large man kneels before a rocking cradle, staring into it's bedclothes and speaking. His hands are clasped to his ears, framing a head with only the slightest downy coating of white hair.
Upper body bare, scars lining the knotted muscles, wearing only a pair of ripped jeans...this is William Craven.]
WC: Wha...wha...his soul's sprung a leak and his pain's pouring out... [Eyes wild, Craven turns 3/4 to camera.] WC: He's trying to put it on me... [Clumsily whipping back, Craven looms over the cradle, nearly falling facefirst into it.] WC: It's not my fault... [Whispering.] WC: Make him shut up... Lydia... [Shrieking, starting to panic.] WC: Make the BRAT SHUT UP!!! [The camera pans about, coming to rest in a position that shows better
Craven's expression. Eyes spring wide, and Craven gapes, slackjawed at what he sees. Recoiling back, he nearly falls as he stands up to his full height.]
WC: No...it ain't fair... Shut him up... MAKE IT SHUT UP, BITCH!!! MAKE IT SHUT UP!!! [Looking at nothing, screaming at nothing, somehow it seems that Craven sees something, hears something. Yet he seems oblivious to the camera.
Obvlivious to all that is real, and entranced by that which lurks in the ether...the imaginary.]
WC: CRUSH IT'S HEAD AND MAKE IT AN ASHTRAY!!! [To punctuate his point, Craven aims a heavy handed overhand right at a spot in the plain white wall furthest from the cradle. Then a scream, impotent and anguished...]
WC: AAAAAAAEYAHPEICAHATE!!! *tshc* [With that, the naked lightbulb dangling from the low ceiling smashes on
Craven's head...] *THUMP* *THUMP* *THUMP* [The heartbeat returns...] WC: Sssshhhh... [...and a much calmer voice emerges from the darkness. Breathing heavily from his panic, trying to sound soothing...Craven speaks to nothing, and no one...]
WC: Sshhh...I'm sorry... Don't cry, my boy... Daddy's sorry... [Yet...he seems to be speaking to a baby.] WC: Hush little baby...don't say a word... [Raising in tone, trying desperately to hold in sobs...Craven sings in raspy tones to an infant that does not exist.]
WC: Papa's gonna buy you a...mock-king-bird... [The heartbeat growing in volume...] *THUMP* *THUMP* *THUMP* [...and with his voice lowering in tone, Craven hums through the rest of the song. Seeming to not know the remaining lyrics.]
*THUMP* *THUMP* *THUMP* [Against the black now threads are sewn. Forming up into clumps, yellow, golden strands of bone weave together. From this, letters form...coming together to spell a single word.
"Craven". Formulaically, we have come to the close. The heart continues, the breathing rasps.] WC: That's my boy...that's my boy... [Fade away. All is still, all is silent, all is at an end.] ----- [The formula begins anew as a cold, dark void spreads across the screen. Heavy breathing and the beating of a horrible heart are heard, night sounds to this twilight dream.]
*THUMP* *THUMP* *THUMP* V (Voice): They didn't believe... [Haltingly, hauntingly, raspy and rough. There can be no question who the voice belongs to.]
V: I made them believe... [It's William Craven...] WC (William Craven): Lydia...I've done it...I've won... [His voice detached, he speaks to someone. Be they real or fake. In the dark nothing is known, nor does it matter.]
WC: I won...for you... [And the blackness is broken by words set in white, a bible quote... "When a man hath taken a wife, and married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes, because he hath found some uncleanness in her:
then let him write her a bill of divorcement, and give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.
Deuteronomy 24:1" Back to black, then fade in slow.] WC: Maybe now...now we can... [His words cut off by nothing, Craven is nonetheless silenced as the scene comes into view slowly. The camera looks down on a hardwood floor, well kept and sterile. Bare, empty, it's just wood, nothing more.
Thump, thump, thump...the heartbeat ceases as the scene comes to life. Panning slowly, the camera looks upon a gold weightlifting belt, the EMWC
North American Championship title belt. The strap is strangely disfigured, chisel marks marr it's surface, a "Mr Yuck" symbol having been dented into the frontplate.
An establishing shot has begun as the camera moves about more. Panning up,
Craven's back is viewed, bare to the waist. Cut all to hell, stitched up and encrusted with blood from where the stitches pulled free, he looks a mess, but doesn't care.
He kneels before an altar of sorts. It's a closet alcove, common in homes that don't have laundry rooms. The folding doors are wide open, the washer and dryer have been removed, the pipes are unconnected and tied to them are the sleeves of a dress...
A wedding dress...] WC: You remember, don't you? Remember when I told you that I'd hit the bigtime...and you made that joke... You'd heard that "the big time hits back", thinking that I couldn't handle the fame...
Well, I hit the bigtime, and the bigtime hit back in a big way. I hit back harder... I won, Lydia. I won like I always do. You know me...you should know better. [A pause and chuckle from Craven as the camera continues to pan. Up, up, to where a framed photograph hangs from an old, rusty nail. It's a couple, both with black hair and pale skin. The woman with her shining green eyes, the man's translucent blue. Garbed in tuxedo and wedding dress, the same dress that hangs now in the alcove, they look quite happy, Wwords in gold read at the bottom, "Our happiest moment, Bill and Lydia".]
WC: Surely my intentions are clear now. The money in hand, I've won back our dream... See the house? Our old house? They took it from us once, the bank...they took it from us but I bought it back. Look, Lydia...three bedrooms. One for us, one for junior, one for guests...
Six figures for mom...six figures for you...six figures for me...six...six...six... I won the titles, I won the belt, I won the trophy,
I won the money...
All for you... All for you. [Moving, moving, ever moving. The camera never pauses to focus on one object or life form, rather moving about to show a signed photograph of Gary
Grayson laying on the floor. The "American Nightmare" is posing like the son of a bitch that he is, just as he always does. Move again to show the
Bunkhouse Stampede Silver Boot Trophy that Craven got for winning the
Bunkhouse Stampede finals at Blood, Sweat, and Tears. It sits on the photo, perhaps acting as a paperweight.
A shadow flits over the room, and the camera pans about to look at the entrance to the room.
Walking in pensively, glancing suspiciously around the room, Devil Elvis has entered behind Bill. Looking curious, he walks a bit nearer to Craven, stopping a few feet short of the Motor City Madman, and staring at the odd display in the alcove. Wearing his orange jumpsuit and barcode, he looks ready for a wrestling match right now.]
WC: I...I know you said it's over. I know...I know...everything, but couldn't we just give it one more try?
One more?... [Staring up at the dress, listening intently to nothing, Craven spontaneously grasps at the flowing white lacey train attached to the back of it. He fiddles with this, lifting it to his face, trying to smell the long evaporated scent of his one lost love. His eyes shut tightly as he brushes it against his cheek...and Elvis makes his move.
He deftly lifts the Silver Boot Trophy a fraction of an inch and takes it, quickly ripping the bottom off, and puts the rest back under the trophy.]
WC: Lydia...I...I can't hear you... [Devil tears at his fraction of the photo, reducing tearing away everything that isn't Gary Grayson's signature, and shoving that into a pocket.
Craven's head lifts from the dress, and he stares straight ahead into the white lace.] WC: Now...now it's clear... I think I understand... You're...not really here... You're not real...not really Lydia... [A pause for thought, and Craven glances up at his demonic partner in crime, acknowledging his presence for the first time, then back to the spectre of his ex-wife.]
WC: ...and I don't care... [Keeping with the formula, the heartbeat starts anew as the scene fades to black. The barren home with all it's strange rooms and veiled secrets disappears from view...
Golden strands of bone weave upon the screen, reminding the world, if they had forgotten, who they've just seen.
"Craven"... The 'bone shard' logo disappears from the screen, and all is black...the cycle has completed, we've come full circle. We now return you to your regularly scheduled violence.]
