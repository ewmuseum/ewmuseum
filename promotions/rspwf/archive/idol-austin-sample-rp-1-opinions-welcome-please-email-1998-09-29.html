Idol Austin Sample RP#1, opinions welcome, please email

29 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This one is from the GWA [The scene opens in the Bellevue, WA condominium of "Incredible" Idol Austin. As the cameramen arrive in Austin's den, they see he is on the phone talking. As he talks on the phone, Austin can be seen smiling at some times, frowning at others, and just out and out laughing at still others. Finally, the telephone conversation ends and Austin hangs up the telephone. He gets up from around his desk and moves to a couch where he can be more comfortable before beginning to speak.]
So, here we are again. You, to hear the words of truth that are The Gospel
According to Idol, and me once again to tell them. The cycle continues, the cycle is complete, and it is good. Because all things associated with me are good.
So, what should be on the agenda today? Do you know? [shrugs] No, I don't suppose you would. That would imply an intelligence that I already know none of you possess. How do you live with yourselves knowing that you share the same brain?
[Austin stops and looks at the confused looks on the faces of the cameramen and shakes his head slowly in disgust.]
Don't bother answering that question. I don't have the time nor the patience to sit here and wait while you try to come up with something that remotely resembles an intelligent and articulate answer.
So, since I do not have the time to wait for you to give me an answer to my question concerning what I am going to talk about, I am going to show you.
Make sure to keep those cameras rolling gentlemen, this could get good. [Austin reaches over to the end table next to the chair he is sitting in and picks up a remote control. He points the remote to the television on the other side of the room and switches it on. As the TV comes on, we see that it is playing a tape of this past Saturday's edition of CWF Total Combat. More specifically, the interview segment between LudiCris and Keith Schiano. Austin looks back to the camera and begins to speak again.]
Don't look so surprised. As if you didn't know I was going to talk about this. Sometimes I seriously worry about you. So, anyway, let's see what we have here. [Points to TV] OK, over here, we have LudiCris, known lunatic, most famous for kidnapping a commentator and forcing him to wrestle as his tag team partner in the NWC. He won the SWF Tag Team Title there, a title I currently hold there myself, and was within a hair's breadth of winning the NWC World Tag
Team Title. I would mention his GWA/CWF accomplishments, but I could care less about them. They are secondary at best.
[Points to TV again.] And over here, we have everybody's favorite village idiot, the reigning GWA/CWF
Unified World Heavyweight and Tag Team Champion, Keith "Rockshade" Schiano. So here we have Schiano, self proclaimed leader of the wrestling world, so trusting, so self effacing so [Makes quotation marks with fingers, mocking his old adversary White Tiger.] "pure". Everybody loves him, everybody worships him, he is the end all be all of wrestling. No one except that "despicable"
Idol Austin and his "cowardly" partner Shawn Arrows would dare to attack him. Or so he continues to delude himself. Let's fast forward a bit shall we? [Austin presses the fast forward button and then after a minute or so, hits play again.] And what do we have here? Why, I do believe that is Mr. Maniac. Another notorious lunatic. What is he doing? Hmm. Looks like he is grabbing the microphone from LudiCris, totally interrupting his interview with the "great"
Keith Schiano. Hardly a very nice thing to do. [Austin fast forwards again to the part of the video where LudiCris has regained possession of the microphone. He then presses play.]
I am not too fond of listening to the ravings of certified madmen. But for this one clip, I will make an exception. Let's see. Here we have LudiCris, he has the microphone, he is talking, and OH MY GOD! He just attacked Keith
Schiano! [Laughs] Good Show! I know you are thinking "You were there when it happened, what's with the replay?" And the answer is simple, I love a good show, and this was as good as it got.
It is so refreshing to someone like me who hates Keith Schiano and everything he stands for to see that others are following my lead. When I got here, I would easily have described the GWA as being a bunch of Schiano Disciples. But that is no longer the case.
First, Alex Quick realizes that Schiano is not the man he thought he was, and whether or not it was by my influence doesn't matter, and now LudiCris and Mr.
Maniac have also seen it. Because of me? Maybe not. Likely? Sure. But it is irrelevant.
How does it feel Schiano? How does it feel to see your "Empire" your "Dominion" begin to fall down around you? I don't imagine it feels good, but I am sure you will soon learn to adjust to the feeling because it is something you will be doomed to experience again and again until there is nothing left for you to call your own.
And when it happens, and believe me it will, you will finally see that what I have been saying all along is true. I am everything. You are nothing.
Nothing that is, except fodder for my greatness. [The telephone rings and Austin gets up to answer it. As he begins to talk on the phone, he waves the cameramen out, signifying the end of the interview.
The screen fades slowly to black.] [name withheld] Handler of "Incredible" Idol Austin [NWC]SWF http://members.aol.com/IdolAustin/ "The Original Starman" Andrew Powell [NWC]MCW Co-Commissioner of [NWC] SCCW - Sin City Championship Wrestling http://sccw.hypermart.net
