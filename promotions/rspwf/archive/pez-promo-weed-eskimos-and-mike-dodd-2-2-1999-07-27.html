PEZ Promo - Weed, Eskimos, and Mike Dodd. (2/2)

27 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Also availble at the PEZ homepage, http://pez.itgo.com Mr C: Hey Dan! Come on man, you can take this guy! (Dan just stands there, seemingly a bit frightened by this biogenetically created mutant.) Mr C: Come on! He's nothing! He's just a stupid hunk of wood! (Dan stands still.) Mr C: Oh FINE. See if *I* help you make an impact in the PEZ ever again. Hmph. (Mr. C turns away from Dan, crossing his arms pouting.) Shrimp Scampini: Also, what roster would be complete without a guy in a gimmick crisis! I bring to you dudes, Philbert Magweed III!
(A pregnant clown walks through the door. Yes, you heard me right, a pregnant clown. *HE* has globs of makeup on, multi-colored afro hair, a huge plastic nose, and a large child-bearing stomache.)
(HONK HONK! Philbert Magweed honks his horn in Dan "The Dan" Dan's ear. Squirt! He sprays Dan Dan with water.)
Philbert Magweed: Hey! Wanna see a trick! Dan "The Dan" Dan: Uh... Philbert Magweed: GREAT! Now, see this? (Philbert points to his clenched fist.) Philbert Magweed: Keep your eye on it! (POW! Philbert punches Dan Dan right in the eye.) Philbert Magweed: I didn't mean LITERALLY! Har har! (HONK! HONK!) (Without saying a word, the irritated Dan Dan charges at the unexpecting Magweed, and slugs him right in his pillow-stomache.
Mr. C: YEAH! Philbert Magweed: MY BABY! YOU KILLED MY BABY! (Before Magweed can throw any more punches, Sean Cox is down there seperating the two.) Sean Cox: Okay, that's enough out of you two! If I see any more of this fighting, you're gonna have to answer to ME!
(Even though Sean Cox is about half the weight and a foot smaller than either of the two, Dan Dan and Philbert Magweed comply.)
Philbert Magweed: DON'T YOU THINK THIS IS OVER DAN! YOU KILLED MY FIRST BABY! I WON'T REST UNTIL YOU DIE! Shrimp Scampini: Uh, alright... next on the PEZ roster is... uh man,
I'm hungry... Marshal, you do the introduction.
(Shrimp Scampini walks off stage to satisfy is craving for junk food, as Dean Marshal takes the microphone.)
Dean Marshal: Alright, the next wrestler on the PEZ roster is known simply as Charly. Come on out, you.
(One of those Buckingham Palace guards enters the fray. You know what they are, they wear those red suits, big black fuzzy hats, and never move or speak? Well, Charly marches about five feet into the room and stands still. Uh oh! Looks as if Eskimo Joe has taken notice to this
British chump.)
Eskimo Joe: (From across the room) HEY YOU! RED FAG! (Charly takes no notice.) Eskimo Joe: (Approaching Charly) HEY! I'm talking to YOU! (Charly takes no notice.) Eskimo Joe: Oh I see how it is *hick*! You don't wanna talk to me cause I'm a Eskimo, isn't it you racist pig?!
(Charly takes no notice, and Eskimo Joe takes another swig from his bottle of Jack Daniels.
Eskimo Joe: You stupid asshole! Eat shit and die! Ahhh! (Eskimo Joe attempts to nail Charly over the head with his bottle of
Jack, but before he can connect Charly grabs his wrist and twists.
Looks very painful. Eskimo Joe drops his alcohol on the ground... prompting Philbert Magweed to come and snatch it.) Eskimo Joe: (Still in pain and being held by Charly) Give me back my booze you dumbass clown!
Philbert Magweed: Ha, not likely! (HONK HONK! SWIG SWIG!) (Charly throws Eskimo Joe about six feet across the room, into Fat
Bastard.) Eskimo Joe: Ahh! I think you broke my wrist you bastard! (Charly gives no responce, but Fat Bastard does and starts knawing on
Joe's arm.) Eskimo Joe: (struggling) GOD DAMNIT! STOP IT YOU FAT BASTARD IDIOT! (Eskimo Joe is able to escape, and wobbles his way back to the sled with Eskimo Mo.) Fat Bastard: DAMN LADDY! I HATE IT WHEN THE FOOD RUNS AWAY! (Shrimp Scampini returns with a bag of Skittles.) Shrimp Scampini: (chomping away) Alright man. Man, this stuff is fruuu-tay! And here's our next wrestler dude, we caught him auditioning to be a General Hospitol cast member... "Young and
Restless" Doctor Jax Jasberby! (A stereotypical 20 year old soap opera cast member enters the room. It doesn't take long before he accidently steps on a small puddle of
Eskimo Joe's spillt alcohol.)
Jax Jasberby: Oh why! Why did this have to happen to me? Of all people in the world, why me!
(Some mellodramatic background music starts to play, as the camera slowly closes in on Jax.)
Jax Jasberby: Why me! What is the meaning to this illconcieved fate! I know that there must be a meaning behind my shoes being tainted with the smell of hard liquore! A meaning that is far out of grasp by normal reasoning...
Doctor Zoloft B. Toxique: My, where is that music coming from? I see no audio system in this room.
Big Bird: Uhh... I'm cold. Jax Jasberby: Maybe, that my shoe being tainted with the smell of alcohol means that Rick will soon be leaving Dana!
*gasp* Jax Jasberby: I must tell Dana imm... (Jax is cut off by Philbert Magweed.) Philbert Magweed: Oh for the love of God, you stepped into a puddle of booze, that doesn't mean jack diddly-squat.
Jax Jasberby: Maybe there is a meaning behind a pregnant male clown confronting me about my reasoning?
*gasp* Jax Jasberby: JULIE IS CHEATING ON STEVE! Philbert Magweed: I give up... Shrimp Scampini: Alright man, Stanly Stevenson, how about you announce the next group of wrestlers. You were the one who was able to hoax them into signing contracts.
Stanly Stevenson: Y-y-y-yes sir. PEZ p-p-proudly introduces to y-you, f-f-former champions of th-th-the League of L-L-Lusers... THE EX-X-
EXPRESS CLUB! (If you were ever a fan of the LOL, four familure faces enter the room. First, the mammoth-sized GI Slow who is chomping away on some freshly grown Walla Walla onions [he has a whole bucket of them]; GI
Ho, their skantly dressed slutty manager; Sgt. Slumber, who is only half awake; and Bubbolicious Bob, the sales clerk-turned wrestler who is still wearing his half of the LOL Tag Team championship title.)
(Bubbolicious Bob glares over at the bald Big Bird.) Bubbolicious Bob: YOU! You're friends with the Cookie Monster, aren't you?! Big Bird: Cookie Monster is my friend. Bubbolicious Bob: That bastard has been taking advantage of the free samples at the pastery section in Big Y!
Big Bird: Uhh... Bubbolicious Bob: And I bet *YOU* steal the eggs too! Big Bird: I like eggs. Bubbolicious Bob: Hey you, want an apple? (Bob hands an apple to Fat Bastard, who takes no time devouring it whole.) Bubbloicious Bob: THAT WAS THE MOST AMAZING THING I EVER SAW! GI
Slow, take a look at this! Here's another apple!
(Bob hands Fat Bastard another apple, and he grabs it and eats it whole.) GI Slow: That's nothing, watch this. (GI Slow pops two onions into his mouth, not even shedding the slightest tear.) Fat Bastard: Oh laddy, think you can outdo me eh?! (GI Slow takes a seat next to the already seated Fat Bastard, who have an onion eating contest between each other. GI Ho seems to be eyeing the unknowing Jax Jasberby.)
Jax Jasberby: But WHY did that pregnant clown say that he gives up? Certainly there has to be more reason behind it than just his laziness... Shrimp Scampini: Man, the PEZ just wouldn't be complete without some sort of cereal character... I bring to ya! CAPTAIN CRUNCH!
(And in walks a downright lunatic. Weilding some fierce looking blue boxing gloves and funky looking blue goggles, the Cap walks towards Dan
"The Dan" Dan's manager.)
Captain Crunch: ARR! YOU LITTLE PEANUT BUTTER BASTARD! Mr. C: What the hell?! Captain Crunch: I'M THE ONLY MR. C AROUND HERE! I GOT TWO Cs IN ME NAME! (POW! The captain knocks Mr. C one right in the face. Dan Dan comes to the rescue and tries to tackle the Captain down, but alas, here is
Sean Cox once again to save the day.)
Sean Cox: HEY! I SAID I DIDN'T WANT ANY FIGHTING GOING ON AROUND
HERE! Dan, this is your SECOND warning! One more and I'm going to have to open up a major can of whoop-ass!
(Note again: Sean Cox is half the weight of Dan and about a foot shorter. But still, he sure SOUNDS intimidating, so Dan turns around and grumbles to himself.)
Shrimp Scampini: And also on the PEZ roster... SILENT ASSASSIN! (Silent Assassin marches his way into the room, and stands still. Not a man for introductions, that's for sure.)
Shrimp Scampini: And there ya have it Mike Dodd, the entire PEZ roster! "StunnaTime" Mike Dodd: (In the middle of all the wrestlers) Okay, now, uh, how about you tell us about the first card.
Shrimp Scampini: I would most certainly dude! Our first card, PEZ is
ZEP, will be held exactly seven days from now at this exact time. But the most important part about that grooby show is gonna be content.
For the first card, the entire PEZ roster will be split into teams of three. The teamates and captains will be decided by me. Now, why am I going to be splitting the PEZ roster into five teams? That brings me to my next point dude: the first PEZ title!
The winning team of the PEZ DODGEBALL TOURNY will be declared the first ever PEZ Dodgeball Champions! Yes, that's right, Dodgeball. Cause man, I just don't dig that wrestling stuff. Details on the teams and how it works will be availble the day of the first PEZ Super Happy Show, in two days.
Once I declare the teams and stuff, man, I want to see you doin' your interview segments WITH your teamates! I don't want no half-assed stuff man, if you guys are a team, you guys gotta be hangin' out together.
(Shrimp stops speaking... and silence hits the room. Maybe they are shocked about his decision to hold a Dodge Ball tourny instead of wrestling? Either way, nobody is speaking, and the only sound being made is by GI Slow and Fat Bastard eating onions.
Silent Assassin breaks the silence by walking towards Eskimo Joe, who is still tending to his arm wound that Fat Bastard knawed into him.)
Eskimo Joe: What do YOU want? (Silent Assassin opens his mouth as if he is about to say something.) Eskimo Joe: You dumbass, if you're gonna say something about me, SAY
IT! (Silent Assassin just stands there with his mouth open.) Eskimo Joe: Oh for fuck's sake! (CRACK! The sound of Eskimo Joe picking up an empty bottle from his sled and shattering it over the cranium of the Silent Assassin, who falls to the ground unconsious.)
The Binky Fairy: HEY! What did you do that for?! He didn't say anything to you! Eskimo Joe: That's the damn point you queer! The Binky Fairy: (obviously fuming) BIIIIINNNKKK!! Eskimo Joe: What the hell did you just say?.. ACK! (Binky Fairy bashes his foot right into the chin of Eskimo Joe, knocking the already wounded little Eskimo out cold. Also, it might be noted that Sgt. Slumber is now resting quitely on the floor.)
Doctor Zoloft B. Toxique: Hmm, maybe this is a good time to test my new expiramental drug.
(Zoloft whips out another syrnge filled with glowing green liquid. He spots Charlie... who is just standing there.)
Zoloft B. Toxique: Hello, young one. Have you ever been curious what it would be like having purple blood?
(Charlie just stands there.) Zoloft B. Toxique: I'll take that as a yes. (Zoloft punctures the skin of Charlie and injects the glowing green fluid. Within moments, Charlie's skin turns purple. Not purple like loss of blood purple, but purple like Barney the Dinosaur purple.)
Zoloft B. Toxique: Oh my! I can't believe it worked! (Charlie, knowing full well what is going on, swings around and grabs
Zoloft by the throat.)
Zoloft B. Toxique: Aaaahhgggg lettt goo offff meee!! (Ugor the Hunchback and the Wood-Faced Gremlin, seeing their master in pain, stand confused. OH! They know what they need to do! BASH
EVERYONE IN SITE!) Ugor The Hunchback: *forceful grunt* (Ugor and the Gremlin start going wild on everyone, and to put it losely, all hell breaks lose. Dan "The Dan" Dan takes no time at going towards Sean Cox.)
Shrimp Scampini: WHOA! WHOA! STOP! I DON'T WANT ANY KIND OF FIGHTIN GOING ON MAN! Ren Hoek: Oh you imbecile, it's helpless now! They're all brawling, and we don't have any officials to break them up! They're going to be fighting till they pass out.
(Speaking of passing out, let it be noted that both Fat Bastard and GI
Slow have done just that. The two men have tied each other in the Onion eating contest, and we all know eating too many of those damned things just isn't good for ya.)
Shrimp Scampini: STOP IT! STOP IT! STOP FIGHTING! (All the sudden, some rather phat rap beats start playing from out of nowhere.) Luigi: (Mike Tyson voice) Weeeerrrddd! (That phat rap beats continue to play, as none other than the Super
Mario Bros from 1st Gen PEZ bust their way into the press conference room and raise some midgety hell. But heck, it's not JUST two midgets! They got their whole posse with em, the Koopa-Troopas, their
DJ Sir-Spins-Alot, and Ghetto-Slut Toadstool. Mario takes center stage.)
Mario: So?! You bizatches think you can run dis' mothafuckin joint without the SMBs?! Shrimp Scampini: Now what the hell dude! This ain't groovy at all! I didn't hire you two squares, and I didn't plan on it either! We're runnin this company on love, and man, we ain't got any love for a couple gangster wannabes who think they run this company.
Mario: Yo! Shut ya face, you hippy-ass mofo. Suck on this blunt I'm about to spew at ya bitch!
Luigi: Weeeeerrrdd! Mario: This bitchass redneck hookah-fuckin Scampini wouldn't let us back in the PEZ like everyone else, so, we gonna be gettin in this hizouse on our own!
Luigi: Yo, dats damn straight! You ain't gonna be gettin rid of us bitch, and that's all there is to it!
Shrimp Scampini: Sean Cox! Get these two ghetto-cheese jerks out of here! (Sean Cox jumps down off the stage, only to be mugged by three or four
Koopa-Troopas. Man, maybe PEZ should have spent a little money beefing up the security?
Mario: Yo, you thinkin that this little bitch-ass is gonna be able to stop us?! HA! Bitch, you ain't seen nothin yet.
(Scampini, while still being a total loveing hippy, is still downright fuming over these two ghetto-assholes invading his company of love.
But, he has nothing more to say on it and just marches into the back... probably to reunite himself with something whose effects have worn off during the press conference.)
