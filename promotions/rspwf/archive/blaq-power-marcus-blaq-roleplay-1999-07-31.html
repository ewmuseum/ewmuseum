"Blaq Power" Marcus Blaq Roleplay

31 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The place: Smoky Joe's Bar-n-BBQ, Columbus, Ohio, USA. The time: 1:42. The scene: outside the bar at an autograph signing. The cameraman walks into the session noticing the yellow tent in front of the old, run-down grill. There's somewhat of a line in front of the yellow tent, consisting of anxious fans hoping to get something of their's signed by the man in the tent. The cameraman looks up at the large white, canvas sign above the tent and examines its writing. The sign says, "Meet "BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ of IWF/WOW and ACE!
TODAY AT SMOKY JOE'S!"] [The scene cuts to the inside of the tent where more fans are gathered to get their autographs signed. At the head of the line, sitting in a fold-up chair in front of a poker table, is the one and only "Blaq Power" Marcus Blaq.
Marcus is sitting down, signing autographs and making sure each person who enters leaves with a smile. Marcus signs three more autographs before finally noticing the cameraman. He looks at the cameraman and holds out his index finger. He then stands up about to speak.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Attention, please. Keep your line in one place and
I'll be back in about 5 minutes. I'll be right back to sign more autographs...
[Marcus leaves the table and gestures to the cameraman to go through the tent's second exit in the back. The cameraman follows Marcus through and heads into the restaurant's front yard area where he finally stops.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Ya know, it amazes me how people are already looking for me to sign their autographs after one match...I'm glad I put on a good show for 'em...
[Marcus looks at the tent area for a moment. This mometn allows the cameraman to see what Marcus is wearing. He's wearing a blank white t-shirt untucked with a pair of black jeans. He's also wearing a pair of black Nike basketball sneakers. He turns back to the cameraman and speaks again.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Well, onto more pressing matters....You're from
IWF/WOW, right?...Thought so....I've heard a thing or two from Instant Karma, finally. It looks like he's focused more on Alex Cote than he is on me....That's understandable....Cote's the next man he's facin', right? He should focus on him....but he still has to watch out. After Cote, Karma gets the chance to feel some BLAQ POWER *after* he fights Alex Cote...
[Marcus scratches the back of his next and looks around. He then turns his attention back on the camera.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Karma, like me, you're a rookie in I/W. We're both out to make a name for ourselves around here...To prove our worth amongst the big dogs...Ya see, Karma, we're both lookin' to be one of the movers and shakers around I/W soon....You wanna believe that you're one of the top new guys around I/W, don't you? You wanna believe that you have what it takes to make a big impact around here. You wanna believe that you have what it takes to beat me....Well, I got news for ya, Karma. You don't have what it takes to beat me...Few people have beat me and fewer still can say that they wiped the floor with me...Karma, you *won't* be one of those few...Instead, you'll be one of the many who has been beaten by MARCUS BLAQ. You'll join the ranks of jokes like "X-Man" Dee Brown, who's probably one of the sorriest excuses for a wrestler I've ever seen, and "Nytemare" Josh Blayde, who's even worse than
Brown...You'll also be joining the ranks of some of my bigger rivals such as
Sex Rooster, the perverted bird, and Sestren, a man who I hold a lot of respect for...Karma, soon enough, you'll feel BLAQ POWER for the first time...and it'll be the *last time* you *ever* feel it again....
[Marcus blinks at the cameraman once or twice before sighing. He closes his eyes and shakes his head retaining the passiveness he began the interview with.]
"BLAQ POWER" MARCUS BLAQ - Now, if you'll excuse, I have autographs to sign... [The cameraman looks on as Marcus makes his way down to the yellow tent. The cameraman continues to watch until Marcus is a good distance away from him before turning away. The scene finally fades to black.]
-The Wush "'D.A.R.E. doesn't stand for 'Drug Awareness Resistance Education.' It stands for 'Drugs Are Really Excellent.'"
