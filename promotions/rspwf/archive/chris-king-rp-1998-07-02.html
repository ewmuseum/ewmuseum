Chris King RP

2 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Comments and suggestions are appreciated...... this is my latest RP for him in TCW............ [ Screen fades to silver. As the camera pans back, it becomes apparent that it was in an EXTREME close up of the TCW's "extreme title", The King's Cup. As the camera continues to pan back, we also realize that the setting is one that has become familiar to King lately, the hospital room. The Cup sits on a night table beside the bed that King lies in, his head even more heavily bandaged than the last time we saw him. The King's Cup shimmers as the lights reflect off of it. Beside King's bed there is a doctor. He is a balding man, who looks to be about 50. What hair he has left on his head is a dark brown, as is his bushy mustache. His wire frame glasses complement his wiry frame body perfectly. He has a clipboard in his left hand, and he is talking to King. He does not appear to be very happy. King lies in the bed, with his cocky, arrogant smile on his face, not listening to a word that comes out of the doctors mouth. The camera moves closer to the bed and we can hear what the doctor is saying. ]
DOC: Mr. King, your injuries seem to be healing nicely, and _despite_ your blatant disregard for my orders for you _not_ to wrestle when you did, you were lucky that you did not further injure yourself.
[ King raises his eyebrows and rolls his eyes, looking at the camera, making you wonder if he is paying a little bit more attention that he appears to be.
] DOC: I see no reason to keep you here much longer, after all you don't listen anyway, and you sneak out in the middle of the night and disobey our instructions.
King: Heh....so you are saying I am out of this hell hole finally? DOC: Not exactly my words, but yes, we would like to keep you here for a few more days, then you are free to leave.
[ A huge, devious smile crosses Kings face. ] King: Thanks doc. [ The man looks as though he is going to answer but King doesn't give him a chance as he just continues to speak. ]
King: Well you heard the man, in a few days I'm out of here!...Good for me, bad for TCW. Really bad for a few TCW "stars" in particular! Oh, I guess I could name names, but...well...that would just take all the fun out of it now wouldn't it?
[ King's smile returns to his trademark grin. ] King: And all I have to say is one thing.... [ King gestures toward the King's Cup ] King: Long live the King.....Long Live the <beep>'n King! [ King's grin disappears as he suddenly looks to be angry. ] King: Yeah, All those other guys in the Stairway to Hell match, _my_ match, had been taking turns the last two weeks taking Kipling out for drinks, thanking him for his attacking me, and knocking me out of the match. They all thought they had a chance, they thought since I wasn't there, there might be a chance for them to leave a permanent mark on the TCW.
[ King's grin returns. ] King: But they were wrong...The King's Cup....nice ring to it wouldn't you agree? For the most part of TCW, the name King has been associated with
Extreme. You heard King, you thought Extreme. Now...Long after I hang up my boots, the name King will _still_ be associated with TCW and extreme. No, actually it will be better than associated with it, the name King will
_define_ extreme in the TCW, from here on out! Jackson, I will leave it up to you just who the unlucky man is that has to face me when I get out of this bed. Because, at Stairway to Hell, I was still a little groggy from all the medicine they have been pumping into me, thus not at one hundred percent. Maybe that is what made me think it was a good idea to deliver the crowning like I did. But the only thing that matters is, it worked. Pretty damn well if you ask me! Now TCW will never forget the name of
King....Long live the King.
