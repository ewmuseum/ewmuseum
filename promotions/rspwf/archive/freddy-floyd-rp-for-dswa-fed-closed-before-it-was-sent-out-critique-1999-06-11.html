Rico? Man - Kuba . . . . oh yeah, i heard of him. Florence Floyd - you have, damn. Man - Yeah, he's that gay guy who used to be on the sitcom about the dogs right? Florence Floyd - (Trying not to blurt out in laughter) . . . Yup thats the one. (Her face instantly becomes stonecold) Now beat it dirtbag!!
[ The man flips her off and the continues down the street ] Freddy Floyd - You were right, no one has heard of this loser. I would say its mission acomplished. Lets go, the limo is makeing its round now, I'll hail it down.
[ Freddy hails the limo who quickly stops off. The driver gets out and opens the back door and the two get inside, along with the cameraman. The driver runs back up to the front as Freddy and Florence peel the stocking caps off of their heads ]
Florence Floyd - how do these pieces of trash wear cloths like this all the time, it makes me sick.
Freddy Floyd - Me to, but what it did was prove a point. It shows the entire
Dirty South Wrestling Alliance exactly what Kuba Rico is all about, and thats a big fat nothing. This guy comes out here using his slang that no one can understand anyway, and jabbers off about how tough he is, and how he came from the streets. Well Kuba, your nothing impressive. You, your stinking mother and father, and all thirteen of your ratty little brothers and sisters and nothing more than waste of space. And this Friday, on the very first ever Presence of Persicution, I am going to show you exactly how much it pisses me off when I pin yoru shoulders to the mat for the one, two, three, and move on just one step closer to the Universal Championship, the belt that is practically already mine!

Freddy Floyd - Your absolutly right Florence, once I am done withj Kuba there are plenty of names on the roster that I plan on single handedly takeing out.
Some of those monrons include Alex Cote, Ethan Love, Devon Case, and Eric Van
Steele. These four men all know what the Fabulous One is all about, and sooner or later theyt will once again feel my wraith. So Dirty South, get ready because things are looking to get a . . . .
LOT MORE DIRTY !! Freddy Floyd - Now you can take that and show on any DSWA show you want. [ Scene fades to black as Freddy lets out an evil laugh ]
