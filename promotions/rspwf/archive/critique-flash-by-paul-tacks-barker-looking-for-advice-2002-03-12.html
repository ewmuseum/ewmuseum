[Critique] Flash by Paul 'Tacks' Barker (Looking for advice)

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yea, looking for some advice.... ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^ Paul "Tacks" Barker (Official Member of DAMM) ^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^*^ [Fade up...] [There he is -- Paul "Tacks" Barker. His wounds have basically healed, but he looks scraggly as ever sitting in that steel chair. No backwards Ozzy cap today, just Paul's shoulder length hair hanging free, covering the drunks face from view.]
[His Skinlab cloth reeks of alcohol, as does Paul's breathe. He's breathing in and out slow, thinking long and hard. He leans forward, resting on his knees, staring out form behind the hair. He sighs, adjusts his legs, and then begins.]
PB: When y'ins look inta' the mirror, do y'all see sumptin' yer proud of bein'? Champion... ya got 'da looks, 'da moves, and 'dat 'dere talent. Yer a top draw, a big name, and a rasslin' champion.
Winter... ya gots the skill, the knowledge, and 'dat damn charisma. Yer a up 'n' comer, just waitin' 'ta bust out. Yer winnin' left and right, turnin' heads at every corner... But me...
[Paul sighs, thinking about everything he's ever done.] Nah, Ah haven't gots a single thing 'ta be proud of. No championships, no big matches... nuttin. Ah been fired from just about eva' promotion Ah've ever worked for and Ah been turned away from countless more. Ah be a alcoholic bum livin' in the back of car, livin' day 'ta day... doin' nuttin important at all.
[And then, then that goofy 'Cole Paul Barker smile comes back.] And 'dat is why Ah am gonna win dis week fellows 'cause I'm the man with everything to gain, but yet everything to lose!
Chris, yer been a champion many timez over, winnin' here, and there. Yer one of 'dose men 'dat can come back from yer losses. Yer one of the lucky guys who can regain everything ya lost. Yer one of the few that can take back the cow hide he loses on his unlucky nights.
Guy, yer basically the same way. If ya suffer a defeat, ya can regain
'da territory ya lost. If yer makin' mistakes, ya can come back, and pull out 'dat 'damn victa'ree.
But fellows, Ah can't... [Paul laughs, his smile fading.] This is 'ma one and only shot. 'Dis is the one chance ah have to be like y'ins all, and Ah'm not gonna let it slip away wit'out a fight..
When yer backin' a dog inta' a corner, he's likely to bite yer damn hand off. But when yer backin' a violent drunk inta' a corner...
you're basically a dead man, and fellows, [Paul brushes his hair out of his eyes, as he stares right into the camera, almost making a attempt at staring down Champion and Winter.]
I'm not a happy drunk ana'more! So basically, whether Ah win or lose, yer going 'ta have 'ta hurt real bad for yer victa'ree. Ah will leave a scar, a mark, anything that will remind you of the night you wrestled Paul "Tacks" Barker! Ah might not win, but yer neva' gonna forget me! Yes boys, it's time 'ta pay up for all yer wise crackin', for all yer insults, and most of all...
[And now with a almost violent tone...] not giving 'ma drunken arse 'da respect 'dat Ah've deserve, and fuckin'! earned! [He smiles one last time, as the camera fades.]
