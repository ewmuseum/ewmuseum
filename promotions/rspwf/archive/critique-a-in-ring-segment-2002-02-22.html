Critique: A in-ring segment

22 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Smoove is the challenger to the SEWF World Title. He's also a "ally" of the SEWF World Champion, Adam Mitchell. This comes at the end of the show. Smoove has just won his match, and Adam Mitchell is about to say some things that Smoove probably won't agree with.
Smoove-Heel, going to turn face. Mitchell- Heel, about to stay heel. Hope you enjoy. And yes, I know it's long. It's a 12 minute segment at the end of the show.
SSC: Smoove retains the SEWF United States Title in a grueling match just one week away from Alarm...
[That familiar guitar rifts] Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO MC: YAAAAAAAAYYYYY #UGH# [Your Disease, by Saliva starts to play over the PA, and the SEWF
World Champion comes out to a ringing endorsement of "We hate your guts and hope you die" type of heat. Mitchell walks towards the ramp as Smoove looks on, wondering what the man that has the biggest title in the game wants.]
SSC: Two friends, two allies, staring at each other, one week away from Alarm.... MC: I like Smoove. He's a good guy. But he's nothing compared to, or without Adam Mitchell...
[Mitchell walks up the ring steps and around the ring on the apron. Not looking directly at Smoove, Mitchell walks around the ring, before pausing and entering.]
SSC: Anything could happen here. [Mitchell grabs the microphone from Ryan Vaughn and takes the SEWF title off from around his shoulder]
Mitchell: Smoove. [Smoove nods as if to say Adam] MItchell: What are you doing? You want my SEWF title? Do you really? [Smoove, seems indecisive, as if not knowing the right answer at this moment.[ Mitchell: Unsure now that I'm standing right in front of you? Unsure of yourself now that you are standing....
[Mitchell moves in and is an eyelash away from Smoove's face] Toe to toe with the Undisputed, Undefeated, Unbeatable, SEWF World
Champion... Standing toe to toe with the most destructive force the wrestling world has ever seen? Are your knees shaking? Heart racing? You breathing seems rush,
Smoove. Feeling dizzy? Lightheaded?
[Moves in even closer, down to a faint whisper in the mic] Going to pass out? Faint, Get rushed to the hospital? Are you thinking
Smoove? Thinking of ways out of this match?
[Long pause....] You Should. [Mitchell turns and storms off, and Smoove and most of the arena lets out a sigh] Mitchell: My Hammerlock, Smoove, isn't with Matt Hammer....It...is with you. [Suddenly, from the crowd, hops Ryan Sanders and Axel Reed, the two cronies of Adam Mitchell. They throw down Smoove and beat on him a little, before grabbing his arms and pulling him up.]
Mitchell: Steve.... MC: Who is Steve.. SSC: Magnus, I think Smoove is Steve. Mitchell: Steve, I"m very disappointed in you. Very. I've always looked at you as a protege. Someone to carry the mantle, the trophy, the flag of this company after I was gone. I've always tried to impart on you, the knowledge, and the secrets that my experience in this sport has afforded me. I've tried to be your surrogate father...
And how do you repay me? By coming after the only thing in this world that truly matters.... [Holds up the SEWF World Title] This title..... you have seen what I am willing to do to people who come at me from the front and take the most valuable prize in our business today...
Just imagine, Steven, what I will do to you. A man so cowardly, so despicable, as to weasel his way into a match with me, a match for my title, a title you know Steve, YOU KNOW, to mean more to me than your life.
Remember back in September, Remember Smoove, when you first started here. You came to Solkoff, you came to me, you came to Ray Hagan, and you begged, you pleaded,
[Mitchell gets on his knees in front of a restrained and visibly angry
Smoove] "let me in the authority. I promise I won't betray you. I promise I'll stay loyal to you. I'll stay loyal to Solkoff, to Hagan. I'll do what you say, I'll do what you ask. I need to belong..''
It was *I* who rescued you Smoove. The abyss of the SFW. Remember that? Run by Art Blake. Run by Thomas Casey. Remember?
You and Violence. Going in front of high school gyms. Being a minor leaguer for the SEWF, for Shaw.
Remember how after it was shut down, you wondered the indy circuit, looking for work, begging for a job...
You came here, because of me. You got work here, because of me. You became a star here, because of me. And you repay me by attempting to take the SEWF World Title that belongs to me.... [Mitchell nails Smoove with a right to the jaw.] You ungrateful sonofabitch. I ought to end you now, so you can't make it to Alarm, and fulfill your dreams.
[Mitchell with another right to the jaw of Smoove] Mitchell: [Rubbing his fingers along the cheek of a bruised Smoove] I enjoy shattering dreams, Steve. I enjoy it oh so very much. I've repeatedly shattered Tommy Kain's dreams. And with each time I beat him, I get happier. I enjoy it more. It's a sickness, a disease. I shatter these people's hopes, their dreams, everytime I successfully put away one of their favorites...
I shattered Jamie Roberts dream... I shattered Jeff Kane's dream... Burt Clifton's.... Larry Gionet's.... The Authority? That was all about descerating on David Briceland. Shattering his dream of a perfect utopia for wrestlers... And now Steve, At Alarm....I get to shatter you dreams. [Mitchell stands up] Steve, Steve, Steve, tsk tsk tsk, [Mitchell with a sidekick that comes perilously close to taking off the head of Smoove] Mitchell: You made a career riding my coattails. You made a name hanging with the World Champion. Now you want to be a World Champion.
[Mitchell, brings his foot back, and walks towards Smoove] Do you know the history of this belt, Steve? The lineage of this title? From Hollywood Phil, to Big Daddy Cool, to the man called Warrior, JP Blood, Texas Assassin, Caladan Moore, Fraizer Fury, Rob Dalton, Rick Wallace... Ray Hagan... Jay Lange.. David Briceland... and none of them.... NONE OF THEM, Steve... was as dominate...as intimdating...as commanding...as unbeatable... as the man that is looking you dead in the eye... [Mitchell kneels right in front of Smoove] Steve....Let's be honest..Let's pretend we're token black guys, and let's keep it real.... Do you honestly think, in your heart, that you are as legendary, as iconic, as great as those men?
[Mitchell doesn't let Smoove answer, and instead shakes his head no] I don't think so. And Steve, do you honestly think, that in some small recess of your egomaniac brain, you are half the man, half the champion, of the man standing right in front of you?
[Mitchell shakes his head again] I brought you into this company. I gave you an identity. I offered you wisdom, intelligence. I gave you a personality, and a place to fit. I kept the title on you, Smoove, so you wouldn't feel left out. When I came back, I brought you back into the fold. I made you a player........ I made you what you are today... IN no small part, I blame myself for this match. I can't punish myself, Steve....so I punish you even more. I'll make you bleed more than you ever thought possible... I'll make you scream in agony, without Steve, you knowing the thrill of victory... I'll toy with you, revisit history, and make you to be the little bitch you were last summer, when me and Solkoff found you .
Everything you are, is owed to me, Steve. Everything you will ever be, is owed to me. At alarm, you won't be able to pay me back. At Alarm, the teacher, teaches one......final.....lesson.
A lesson in respect. A lesson in integrity. A lesson born out of love, that has reverted to unbridle distaste, hatred. Smoove, you crossed a line you shouldn't have crossed, and tested a man that you need not test........
I saw the results of my work a couple of weeks ago.... At Alarm, you will see the results of yours. [Sanders and Reed let go of Smoove and push him forward, and exit the ring] Your free, your standing toe to toe, in the same ring, with the World
Champion. The man you want to beat, the man you NEED to beat, to validate to yourself, and to these people the greatness you boast about..
What are you prepared to do Steve? [Back up in Smoove's face] Mitchell:[whispering] You going to hit me, Steve? Nah, you couldn't hit me. You won't hit me. You know what will happen, and it scares you to death, doesn't it...
Steve? [Mitchell circles Smoove, as Smoove is just standing there] Mitchell: You know I am right. One hundred percent. You know, deep down, that you are an ungrateful little bitch...
You know you are everything I say you are, and you know that you hope, you dream, to be everything....I am.
You dream to be a champion. You lay in bed at night....tears running down your face, and you shake, violently you shake.
You shake because you know, deep down, that you will never be able to reach your dreams. You will never fulfill that fantasy of yours. The fantasy to ride the nice cars, date the finest women, to be the new-age wheelin, dealin, kiss stealin, limo riding, jet flyin, sonofagun.
You dream to be able to walk down that aisle.. [Mitchell points down the entrance way of the SEWF] and have millions of people go "There goes Smoove. He's the Champion." You fantasize about little kids wanting your autograph. You sit at night, and you look away, and you wish you lived the champions life. And as your head is turning, and you are dreaming, you look in the mirror. It happens everytime. You see yourself in the big match, and you look in the mirror, and the self-realization hits you, Steve...
You aren't quite good enough... And you never will be. The famous line is "To be the man, you got to beat the man" Steve.....I'm the man. I'm the man, here, I'm the man......period. Everywhere I go, I"m the Main Attraction. I'm the Star of the show. The headliner, the Main Event. Top of the Card, last of the show, best of the broadcast. I make people come here, I make people buy the Pay per views, the merchandise. They boo and hiss at me, in a wrestling ring, and beg, plead for my autograph outside.
I"m Everything You want to be you, Steve. You want my life? It requires one task, one simple task... To be the Man, you got to beat the man. [Mitchell whispers in Smoove's ear] Beat Me Smoove. Beat me and your dreams come true. Beat me, and you are the Man. The absolute final in the SEWF.
[Mitchell steps out of the ring] Smoove....You didn't get me and you had the chance. The war was over, before it actually begun.....
[Mitchell steps down as Smoove stands there, his fist clenched, his body straight, and tense. The crowd continues hissing the SEWF World
Champion]
SSC: I can't believe Smoove stood there... MC: I can. Hell, I was paralyze in fear of Mitchell. Adam Mitchell is more sadistic, more evil, than anything I've ever imagined. The man is coldhearted, and ruthless. I"m honestly afraid of him..
SSC: This explains why you mark out so much for him.. SSC: Folks, we're out of time. The World Champion has left an indelible mark on the challenger for Alarm. My God, what a match that is going to be.
