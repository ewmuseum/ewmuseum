NAW Roleplay Showcase 2

18 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome Ladies and Gentlemen to hour two of... NAW Roleplay Showcase =================================== Jade =================================== (It had to happen. In fact it was going to happen. Then it did not happen. Once upon a time in the not so distant past, Jade was the North American Champion and the first ever one at that. A cage match was set up for Jade to defend the title against The Dog With Rabies, but it never happened and with
Jade's mind in a state of depression, he gave up the North American title and even though he had not been defeated for it by DWR, The Dog won the title.
That never set well with Jade, so he contacted the new champ to arrange a title shot and it was promised that DWR would defend against Jade, but that never happened either and now, almost two months later, Jade seized the chance to vent his frustration out on DWR. After hitting DWR with a chair on TNT and causing him to lose his chance at becoming a two time champion, a match was ordered for the two to face in a cage match.)
<At Jade's palacial home in Tampa, Florida there is an aura of excitement in the air as Jade prepares to face DWR in the long anticipated cage match. Jade is found sitting in his private den, watching bouts with him and DWR and also viewing other matches that DWR has had, he has a notebook in his hand and appears inquisitive at DWR's performance. He is wearing light, tan slacks and a white Polo shirt with a pair of loafers on, his hair is shoulder length and he is deep in thought when he notices the cameras. Putting down his notebook he stands and after greeting the crew, he begins to speak.>
Jade: First off, I want to address this Sir White Garbage. White trash, you have no right in this fed and the only thing I have to say is that if you step out of line, then it is your ass. Now, DWR we have some things to air out. I will do so at TNT and I will tell you somethings now that you must hear again, even though you already know it. DWR, you screwed me royally and it is not about win/loss records anymore. In fact, I am not out to pin you at TNT. I am out to totally and completely kick your ass and to once and for all let you know that I am the man. I am one half of the tag team champions, so that means two things. One, I got a belt and where is yours? Two, I have proven time and time again that I am totally a superior athelete than you are.
{Laughing quietly and then adding} Jade: The chair I hit you with was but merely a warning, because I want you to know just how much I hate you. Now, Dog with Worms I must say that even though you have no talent and that you can not teach an old dog new tricks....
{That remark brings a snicker from the camera men) Jade: Shut up! Did I tell you to interupt me? As I was saying. I thoroughly plan to teach you a new trick or two at TNT, I plan to humuliate you like the stray that you are and then when I get finished kicking your pathetic ass all over the caged ring, I am going to call a vet to give you your parvo shots and then have him worm you. You see DWR, just because I am a tag team wrestler right now, does not mean that I do not and can no kick your ass and I will show you once more, why I am the man and you, well you are simply a dog with fleas.
<Jade struts around the den for a second and then clicks on the video. The part where he destroyed DWR with a chair.>
Jade: You see that. The funny thing is, is that BBW or Bill Bob whatever his last name is could not even beat you. He has a title that he could not even defeat you for, so BBW why don't you bring that belt over to my home and just hand it over to me this instant. I know you are watching Bill Bob Whatever and
I know you compete in the INWI, you have to be elite to have been there and I will say this to you right now. "If you got the guts and I think you may, come on down and put that North American Title on the line against me." Be the first man to pin me? Not likely. Sure I can be beat, but not by you.
{Jade walks over to the VCR and shuts it off. Smiling he replies.} Jade: I ran Rick "The Trick" Taylor off from the NAW. I beat the hell out of
DWR at will. J.W. Storm and I beat the Big Bad Buff Stryke Force, not so buff now huh? I scared that homo Jim Vickens away and to add to the list, I am going to mess you up real bad DWR and then? Well then, I am coming for the belt that I never lost, the North American Heavyweight Title. It is mine and
BBW, you are on borrowed time. In conclusion, DWR if you do not like what I am saying that you can just....
{Pointing to his bazooka} Jade: Suck It!!!! That is all gentlemen, be gone. (The scene ends) =================================== "Best of the Best" =================================== [The camara opens up to outside of the Marriot Hotel in New York. Suddenly we see the headlights of a car coming up the driveway, it comes closer in view and we see it's a White Stretch Limo. The Limo comes to a stop and the engine turns off, the camara then zooms in on the license plte which reads -BOTB- A moment later the driver steps out then opens the back door and out steps Shaun and
Jonathan Riche. Shaun has on khaki pants, a black silk shirt, and a pair of silver ray ban sunglasses. Jonathan has on black jeans, a navy blue blazer with a white button down shirt, and a pair of black armani sunglasses. The three walk into the hotel and the camara fades out. A few seconds later it turns back on as we're now inside of an elevator. Shaun pushes the button for the penthouse then turns towards the camara and begins to speak. . .]
SHAUN RICHE - Well, wel, well NAW that's one down, and several to go. The
Booger Brothers came into that match thinking these newbies to the NAW would be no trouble at all. . .but it was US who had no trouble disposing of those two sweaty mongaloids. They were just the first of many in the NAW that shall find themselves getting a taste of the SPIKE! It's a bitter sweet pill to swallow, but once it hits you. . .You don't feel anything after it because you're lying flat on your back and out COLD!
JONATHAN RICHE - Just like we said we would, we decimated and demolished those
Booger Brothers in the ring at Tuesday Night Tango. We lifted Snot Boy up off his feet and SPIKED his little head into the mat snapping his neck like so many dry twigs before pinning him for the easy 1-2-3. I mean lets face it, did anyone doubt that we would indeed beat them. . .and beat them easily? I should hope not, because anyone who has ever seen us wrestle before knows that we're the best thing since sliced bread. . .and now for those NAW fans who haven't seen us, now have seen us therefor they know it as well.
SHAUN RICHE - And this Thursday Night those INWI fans will find out that fact as well. . .but we won't get into that right now. Now we're gonna talk about another team in the NAW that's days are numbered. Yes, Yes I'm talking about the Bladeliners. The NAW Tag Team Champions. They've beaten so many teams in the NAW to get those belts, yes they did. . .but they've never faced us. Ohh no. . .well not yet at least. <grins> But they shall face us sooner or later. .
.much sooner I hope though. And when they do those NAW Tag Team Titles will be coming around our waists where they belong. We look great normally, but with the gold, WOO BOY! We look a whole lot better.
JONATHAN RICHE - OHH YEAH BROTHA! PREACH ON!! Bladeliners I hope you boys enjoy your time as the NAW Tag Team Champions because now that we're here you're on borrowed time. Those tag titles are on borrowed time around your waists and it will only be a matter of time until we tear them right from around your waists.
You can try to avoid us all you want and make your reign as champs a bit longer, but lets face it. You can't run from us forever, sooner or later we're gonna catch you and drag you into that ring where we will decimate and destroy you. We will beat you within an inch of your pathetic existances and when we're through doing that we'll end you once and for all by SPIKEing your heads into the mat, get the 1-2-3 and walk out the NEW NAW Tag Team Champions of the
WORLD! SHAUN RICHE - Becuase we are, without a shadow of a doubt. . . SHAUN/JONATHAN - THE BEST OF THE BEST!!!! [The elevator stops and the two step off as the camara fades to black. . . .] =================================== Raging Infernos =================================== (The camera drop-focuses on a dark and dreary forest. The camera man has a coat and a hat on because it is raining. The camera has a little bag to keep it dry. The camera man walks down the path to find the Raging Infernos. The camera man is stepping on brances and stepping in mud. All of a sudden a voice comes out of no where.)
"Who is the one who steps in our enviroment?" "Tell us who you are and why you have come." (The camera man starts to speak.) "I am the camera man, and my name is Bryan Young." (The mysterious voice speaks again.) "Ah yes we have been expecting you." "Come over here and sit, so that we can talk." (The voice is coming over from a couple of trees over on the south side of the forest. The camera man walks over, and sees three wrestlers talking about ther thoughts. The camera man starts to talk and then turns on his camera.)
"Ok what are your guys names?" (All three reply in unison.) "Rage, Fury, and the Angel of Death." (The camera man asks one more question then they start.) "Who wants to talk first?" (Fury signals for him and he starts to talk. Fury is wearing baggy jeans, and a shirt that says FURY 4:20. He is also wearing a black trench coat over him and black shades.)
"I will talk........ First I have to say that the raging infernos will be active in competition soon. When we return we will go back to winning and hopefully win the tag team belts that we deserve. Rage and I are two young superstars that have their future to look forward too. I am also not going to let up once in any match. What happened when we lost was a discrace to ourselves and to this fed. The fun is over when we come back we are playing for everything. The pieces are placed all we need is to get a check mate.
When we win the belts our side will have victory. There will be many battles, and many wins and losses, also many casualties. But we will come out ahead and win the titles. For we are The Raging Infernos, and we play for keeps.
Because Fury 4:20 says, those you face the best get burned like the rest" (Rage pushes the camera over to his side and starts to talk. Rage is wearing black cut up jeans, and a black cut up shirt. The back of the shirt says,
"Pain is just part of the game." He is also wearing a black trench coat and he bleached his hair.)
"Fury put the words perfectly. When we come back we have a new everything. We have done it before, and we can do it again. We are the new generation of wrestlers. We are not the old or those &*#$ing degenerates. We are the new brand of wrestlers that will not take any %^&* from anybody. We will destroy what comes before us, and then when the smoke clears we will have won the title. When we come back everybody will know us once again. So I say to all the tag teams out there be prepared to be challenged. For when you face the
Raging Infernos you will get burned. As Rage 3:23 says, Your @#$ is grass, and you are a prick you piece of $%^&."
(The camera man is still looking at Rage until, the Angel of Death moves the camera to him. He is wearing white jeans and a white shirt, and his hair is white.)
"I hope the world is taking a big look at the soon to be tag champs. We all have been training like mad, and we will come back as a new fighting force.
The pain and suffering indored by us will come to a stop as we go on our long journey towards the belts. When we get those belts, we will have dominated the
N.A.W. This tag team will have its revenge against Rob Stryker and that other
%^&*ing prick for getting a win on us. I hope you guys know that when you beat the Raging Infernos we had to leave a week before that match. So as the Raging
Infernos manager and friend, WE CHALLEGE YOU TWO AGAIN. You will feel the wrath and feel the pain. If we do get this match we will decide the date, and the place. As the Angel of Death says, UP YOURS."
(The camera turns off slowly, and all four of them leave.) =================================== Enigma =================================== <A man has arrived here in the NAW. Nothing really special about him. No special ring entrance, no flash and not even a wealthy individual. This man, a man born on the streets knows what hard work is and he intends to show each and every opponent why he is an enigma.>
The scenery is pleasant. In a park in San Antonio, Texas is a man, his wife and little daughter and they are walking through a park hand in hand. The father is a simple man, wearing faded blue jean shorts with a red t-shirt and no shoes. The wife and daughter have matching blue shorts with white t-shirts, these people are not rich and they are not poor, they come from a basic middle class town and know what family means to each other. The man is called Enigma and with his wife Marissa, they enjoy life with their daughter Nicole. Enigma begins to speak.
Enigma: After months of honing my skills for my trade, I have entered the wild and wooly world of pro wrestling. I do not intend to brag about my past, only that I am happy to have my family and I love them very much. I refuse to comment about what I plan on doing, because my actions will speak loudly for me in the ring. This coming TNT, I have a match against Morgue. Well, I have never heard of this opponent of mine but I can only tell you this Morgue. I call myself Enigma because I feel that there is no one quite like myself, I do not boast this fact for it is just the way that I feel and your name disturbs me. How can anyone call himself a place where deceased individuals are buried?
Very disturbing, indeed. However, when we meet at Tuesday Night Tango I plan of beating you very soundly to advance in the rankings here in the NAW, I am a very hungry man and I thrive on competition. I may not meet and defeat everyone like other wrestlers, but I always wrestle my best. So when the time comes, and you are flat on your back and I deliver my finisher to you, we will see if I can put you to rest and move on up that ladder. I expect to do very well here.
<The scene closes with Enigma and his family sitting on the ground of the park to enjoy some quality time together and the scene ends.>
=================================== Sir White Collar =================================== <Truly a man of greatness, Sir White collar is indeed all that he says that he is. This man took over the Mindest Empire and had taken it to a better level, no one can truly compare to Mindset.>
{Sir White Collar is in his Pleasanton, California home. He is wearing absolutely nothing and is relaxing in his jacuzzi, reading the evening paper and smoking the finest cuban cigar ever made and he talks about himself.}
Sir White Collar: Yes, it is indeed obvious that not only am I the best grappler known to man, I am also the most sexiest and coming up at TNT I shall face a commoner calling himself, John Q. Denver. Denver, you should know that you do not matter to me or my Mindset Empire, for you are nothing but a Sir
White Collar slave and that is the truth. When I apply my "Blue Blood"
finisher on you at Tuesday Tango, you will know that I am King, ruler of the
NAW and the monarch of all wrestlers. Ian Walsh, the East Coast Champion.
What a terd he truly is. I have never faced you fool, because you simply are not in my league, you are trash and you certainly do not matter to Mindset.
However, your title does. If you are the man that you truly claim that you are, you will put that title on the line against me and then I shall prove to you why I am far superior than you. So, in a final gesture of my greatness, I will say this to the president of the NAW. Sign the match between me and THE
HUMAN TORTURE CRACK for the title and I shall forever rid you and all of these other low classed idiots of him, once and for all.
<Scene ends> ===================================
