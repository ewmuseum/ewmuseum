[Rp Critique] Lazarus Blight

5 July 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This was just a E-wrestler I just created an hour ago, tell me what you think of this intro RP
[[The scene fades in with a large library. Many books of many sizes fill the shelves and the room. Candlelight was used to light the place other than the normal lightbulb. In the middle of the room sat a man reading a book. He seemed very tall and built. He had a young handsome face with short cut hair. He continued to read the book when he looked up and saw the camera. He took a bookmarker and placed it in the book. He took off his glasses and looked up]]
[Man] 1984... a classic, but I find it dreary and dull. The thought that someone is watching you of every moment of every day seems interesting since this was made in 1948, but how it was done was simply boring.
[[Stands up]] [Man] I do believe I haven't been kind enough to tell you my name. Lazarus
Blight. Lazarus as the whom Jesus rose from the dead and Blight as a blight that destroyes fields of crops. I believe my name suits me quite well for me and this business. Shall I tell the tale?
[[Goes to a window and looks out]] [Lazarus] When I was a child, I was blessed with both superiority of intelliect and in atheletics. When I was in the tender age of 8 I decided that I must use both of these gifts to the fullest advantage. I ate right, trained my body well, and did many sports. My favorite was rugby. But not only did I train my body, but I trained my mind. I read books, kepted up in school, and made sure that everything I got was nothing more than the peak of excellence. I graduated top of my high school and at graduation I heard nothing but praised for my speech. I went to college and I decided to major in psychology. Why? I find the human mind interesting. [[Looks to the camera]] Not the brain, the mind. The brain can be given to the doctors, but the mind I wanted to know. I wanted to know what made everyone so similiar...[[looks back to the window]]
yet so different. I graduated, top of my class and field. [[He walked to his chair and sat down]] [Lazarus] I then went to medical school to further my study in my career in choice. I studied more than I did in college because I couldn't find many sports to partake in. Again I graduated top of my class and finally was able to practice psychology. I could find figure out what made people act how they do. I was an aggressive psychologist. I didn't want to deal with petty things about how they had glimpsed at another woman and now think they are unfaithful.
I went to fears, secrets, and dark fantasies. Somehow someone thought I was abusing the patients trust and one reported me to the state.
[[He looked down and took out his glasses and cleaned them]] [Lazarus] They brought in a spy disguising as a patient who believed that the world was against her... yes it was a woman. When they got enough "evidence"
they took me to the board where they took away my right to practice. I tried everything to do what I loved. Criminal profiler, councilor, even went far as school councilor... no one would take me for I had my trade stripped away from me. I was at a pub drowning my troubles away when I saw a wrestling show....
from this federation and I decided that if I was to do what I loved again, I would do it in the ring.... and I was born again and hence the name Lazarus....
and what about Blight? [[Leaned forward revealing his Brown eyes, yet behind them burned brightly a sadistic flame that seemed that it will never be extinguished. ]]
[Lazarus] I will be a blight upon everyone of your minds and bodies.... and everything I touch will wither.... and die.... and nothing that could be concieved in your mind will survive.... Ta-ta.
[[Puts back on his reading glasses and opened up 1984 again as the scene faded to black]]
