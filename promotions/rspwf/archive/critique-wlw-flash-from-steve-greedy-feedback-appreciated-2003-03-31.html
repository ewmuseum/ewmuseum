[Critique] - [ WLW flash from Steve Greedy ] - [Feedback appreciated]

31 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Black Screen ] Voice : " If a picture typically says a thousand words . .. Then . ." [ The screen changes from blackness, to a clear picture of Steve Greedy sitting behind a desk, with his feet propped up onto the desk looking at a photo with an evil smirk on his face.]
[ Behind Greedy you can see several books which come together on a huge bookshelf. On the table beside Greedy's Italian made shoes you can see a water bottle, his cell phone, a rolex watch, and a few more photos turned face down.]
Steve Greedy : " . .. this photo is spitting out enough words to form a novel." [ Greedy's evil smirk turns into a huge smile, as Greedy lowers his feet from the table, and leans up in his chair focusing in on the picture.]
SG : " Maybe even a two part saga . . ." [ Greedy tosses the picture down on the table, and turns some of the other's over looking at them.]
SG : " And there is more where that came from . . .Are you curious yet Madman as to what I have my hands on here ? I saw you running around a week or so ago like a chicken without it's head. Is it starting to bother you a bit ? Is it starting to consume your life, and the air you breathe ? "
[ Greedy looks back up at the camera, as his face has turned into an evil snarl.] SG : " I bet it has . .. I bet your setting at home right now wandering if it's going to be emberassing, tragic, life changing or horrid at the pay per view when these photos are shown to the whole roster of the WLW, and there many fans.
I'm usually pretty good at bets . .. Did I win this one Madman ? " [ Greedy chuckles a bit, as he once again leans back in his chair.] SG : " Of course you won't tell me if I did or not, you don't want me to get an added advantage or anything like that by seeing that you truly are _ WORRIED_ to death about what is coming your way at April Reign . .
That's okay . .. I have plenty other advantages, besides playing with your fragile head. There is the obvious difference in skill level, As I am a much superior wrestling technican than yourself . .. Definitely a big difference in fashion, and most importantly a big difference in classes. Yes classes . .. "
[ Greedy reaches out grabbing his water bottle, then kicks his feet back up onto the desk.]
SG : " In life there is classes . .. Some live good . . Some live bad . . Some live in between. . . " [ Greedy takes a sip of his water, pausing briefly.] SG : " You are an in between at best, and I am definitely one of the people out there living good . .That puts us in another social class, a different tax class, in a different wrestling class, and an overall different class when it comes to life in general.
Your just outclassed here my friend . . Simple as that." [ Greedy shakes his head in disgust.] SG : " It's really a shame that you wouldn't join Greedy N' Services so I could help you change all that.
Now your on your own against a man who eats people like you for breakfest, lunch and dinner on a regular basis. You can talk to your motor heads .. . Or even pray to your God's if you like, but at the pay per view . . .
You, along with your pathetic life will go _BANKRUPT_ " [ Greedy takes another sip of his water, as he winks to the camera.] SG : " Take that to the bank . . My bank . ." [ Greedy sharply sits back up in the chair, putting his feet on the ground, and leaning towards the camera.]
SG : " If your a betting man .. . This is one of those deals they call a ' for sure thing " . .. Be sure to do the right thing, and place your money on the right man. Don't know what they use for money over in England, but I guarantee you the same goes true over there, as it is here in the states.
Money Isn't everything . . . It's the only thing ." [ Greedy chuckles a bit, as he puts his hand up over the lens causing the screen to go just as dark as it was before.]
[ Fade Out ]
