[CRITIQUE] "Hot Foot" Samuel Tinsleton

15 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Lol, I'm not sure where I'm going with this character, but I was wondering what you fellas' thought about him thus far.. This is the first flash I've done with him, critique, flame, and bash away. =0
========================================= [ Snap! ] VOICE OVER: God am I good looking.. [ Snap! ] VO: This is amazing.. Work with me, come on.. Your doing it.. Good job.. Nice, you look great.. God your sexy.. Look at that body! [ Snap! ] [ Snap! ] VO: If I looked as good as you.. [ The camera fades in from black and you see a man taking pictures of himself with a polaroid camera while looking into a mirror.. ]
MAN: Oh wait! I am you! [ The man pulls the polaroid camera off from around his neck, and dangles it down by his side in his right hand.. The man sports a blonde crew cut, small goatee, a dark tan, and a physique that would make the god's happy. ]
MAN: Forgive me, for sometimes I get caught up with myself.. It simply amazes me how great I look. But then again,
I'm not the only one that's frequently amazed.. [ The camera follows the man clad in his khaki colored cargo pants and white muscle shirt that reads
"Hot Foot." As the camera continues to follow him, you watch intently as he struts like a schoolgirl to what appears to be the kitchen of his home.. The man leans up against a counter, strikes a quick pose, and then throws his hands outwards as he begins to speak.. ]
MAN: Well, if you didn't know already, I'm sure your mother or sister does.. The names Tinsleton, not just any Tinsleton, the _"HOT FOOT"_... _SAMUEL_
Tinsleton. [ A sly smirk comes on the face of Tinsleton as he begins to bust out in some weak mexican Tejano dance..
Tinsleton then stops, gets a serious look on his face, and holds his right index finger up in the air. ]
"HOT FOOT" SAMUEL TINSLETON: Now that I've been properly introduced, I'd like to let _ALL_ the
Tinslefanatics out there know, that you can catch the "Hot Foot".. _IN ACTION_ this week in none other than Edmonton, Alberta, Canada..
[ A confused look comes on Tinsleton's face as he hops to his right leg and begins balancing while holding his crotch with his left hand, and his right index finger still raised in front of his face.. ]
"HF" ST: Wait.. Did I just say Canada?! Holy schnikeys Tinslefanatics, how did I get myself into such a predicament?!
[ Tinsleton pops out of the balancing position and stands with two feet spread out, and his hands on his waist.. ]
"HF" ST: As if the ugly cows of Canada could suffice to be graced with the "Hot Foot".. _BLASPHEMY_ I tell you! Utter blasphemy! No worries Tinslefanatics, I, the Tinsleton with the most tinsle, will _NOT_, I repeat, will _NOT_ perform any less than fantinsilating!
[ Tinsleton begins to hop around his kitchen and then begins to skip out of it, into what appears to be his living room.. Tinsleton hops onto a couch, and strikes a pose.. Looking into the camera.. ]
"HF" ST: Battle Royal eh!? [ Tinsleton hops onto his feet, standing on the leather couch, pointing into the camera with his right arm, holding his crotch with his left hand.. ]
"HF" ST: Well good thing It's a battle royal, for battle royals are none other than the "Hot Foot's"
_SPECIALTY_! Never has the "Hot Foot" been known to lose a Rumble.. Especially with these looks!
[ Tinsleton suddenly holds both hands out in front of his face, striking a weak excuse for a vogue pose.. ]
"HF" ST: Look at the names that threaten my beautiful face.. Trance Extacy?! Such a man has been known to be very reckless in the ring, what if he screws up!?
[ A concerned look comes over Tinsleton's face.. Then it quickly turns into a stern one as he looks with one eye open.. ] "HF" ST: No worries, it's inevitable that my shockingly great looks will remain the same.. There is no possible way that this gift that I have recieved from god can be altered in any way..
Especially by any of these third-rate, un-handsome gentleman! I've seen bums that look better then some of these guys!
[ The "Hot Foot" suddenly looks like he's seen a ghost.. He shakes his head quickly and then looks back into the camera ]
"HF" ST: Ah.. Bums.. Yuck. Just the mentioning of them makes me feel ill.. So dirty, so.. _MALODOROUS_!
[ Tinsleton hops off the couch and stands in the middle of his living room, striking a weak example of a SuperMan pose.. ]
"HF" ST: Tinslefanatics! Stay tuned! Your about to witness something.. [ Tinsleton spreads his arms out in the air and lifts up his left knee.. ] "HF" ST: _TINSILATINGLY_ ..... _TINSTACULAR_! [ Fade To Black as the "Hot Foot" begins to dance around his living room, hopping from one foot to the next.. ]
