[SAMPLE] 'Spotlight' V.D. Vitrone - What do you think?

1 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well.... I'm rather bored here tonight, having just come home from going bombing with my friends. (No, I'm not a terrorist)
Anyhow, with all this publicity I've been getting on the bored - I figured I might as well show up a sample flash. Read if you like, but if you do read -
I'd appreciate some comments - whether it be negative or positive.
So.....before I bore you all to death...... Here goes. [The camera fades in to the site of a run-down old town - somewhere in the middle of New York City. It pans past one street - capturing all the headlight lit cars, and the shaggy old houses. But then, all capturing the actions of this street, it moves to the next and continues the same pattern.]
[The camera then quickly fades out, blending into the next scene, which is still a run-down section of the Big Apple. The camera continues its view of this neighborhood by capturing the cars, the litter-filled streets, and the grafitti infested buildings. The camera picks up a voice while going.]
VO : Sometimes I wonder how these people who populate areas such as these continue to live a life of poverty.
Waking up each day, not knowing if they're going to be able to walk down the street without getting savagely attacked. How they drive to work, not knowing if their parked cars will get stolen. Going to the stores without being robbed.
But then, when I think of this, I think of the rough- edged people who live in these neighborhoods. Knowing how they're stronger than the majority of the world. . .because they don't let _anything_ get them down.
But, to put it simple, the reason why these people don't get weakened and upset is the sole moral which they possess. These people have *hope*.
They believe that if they keep on the `down low`, and continue to work hard ; that they'll be able to move to a better place. And that *faith* is why you must respect these people.
[The camera continues to pan down and sitting, on a cracked porch is none other than V.D. Vitrone. `Spotlight` V.D. Vitrone, clad in a "Get Real" t-shirty, a pair of saggy stonewashed blue jeans, and finally a pair of white-blue Adidas sneakers.]
VDV : An' well, I share the philosophy that sometimes you _do_ hafta stick through the hard times. And hopefully, my U.E.W. career will share this philosophy.
I think to myself that hopefully, if I keep working hard - that I _will_ be successful. I think that in the end, it'll all work out. . .
[Pause] But then, I realize that what I'm saying is a crock of sh[BLEEP]. The whole thoughts that go through these people's mind is nothing more than some deprived man's hidden dream. But beyond these lovely fantasies stands something which is reality. The _truth._
And the truth is that for the majority of these people ; they're gonna live the remainder of their lives in this *chock full of poverty*. Why's this? Because they listen to the lies of others. They let *nature take its course*, an' well - they're gonna die a buncha poor bastards.
[Vitrone stands up and begins to walk around a bit.] VDV : But, of course - there is the exception to the rule. There is always the one man who goes out and takes life by the horns. He puts _his_ fate into _his_
hands - and he enjoys the luxuries of life. Fans of You Ea Dubba, I'd like you to meet *the exception*. Me, V.D. Vitrone. I've had alot of time to think lately, and I've come to a conclusion that, as much as I love to blame the heads of U.E.W., my lack of success is my own fault. I've been bitching a bit too much, an' by concentrating on excuses to shed, I haven't been doing my job *in the ring.* An' fer that, I apologize. . .
I apologize to all the fans who've supported me. I apologize to the people who've supported me my whole life. . . And, more importantly, I apologize to _myself_. I should be enjoying what it is to be a star - not this slouchy loser
I've become. And now, my reformation starts. No more screwjob losses for me. 'Cuz from this day forward, I'm gonna be the `Top Gun`.
[Vitrone smirks.] Now, I'm sure you're all wondering how I plan on doing all this but, it's quite simple. If anyone of you've been paying attention, you'd notice something about all the stars of U.E.W. But, one thing you'll immediately notice is that their success comes from playing by their _own_ rules. They would never would up with what I put up with.
And now, from this day forward, I'm not going to put up with it either. Y'see, Al Robertson, when you hit me with that bat, you've caused me to notice something if what've took me a long time to notice. I haven't been playing an aggressive game like I should have. Instead, I've been keeping myself open, waiting for someone to attack. And, well no shit, you attacked.
But, now it's my turn. From this day forward, I'm going to put on one hell of a show, no matter what happens. I'm going to make people choke on their own teeth, dance over their faces, and all the other shi_at Sex Machines like myself do. Quite frankly,
I'm gonna make U.E.W. my _bitch._
[Vitrone has a demented look plastered across his face.] But all good things have their downfalls. And the downfall of this is that there's one camper not going to be happy.
This unfortunate soul is none other than Ivan Ramius. `Iron Drapes` as I prefer to call him. Without a doubt an incredible athlete. . .but everyone already knows that.
However, what they don't know is this. Ole' Ramius and I have hooked 'em up in the ring before, and two outta three times, I've came up on top. And if Ivan couldn't beat me when I was just fixin' to compete. . .
"what makes you think he's gonna beat me when I'm fixin' to... KICK_HIS_ASS ." Robertson, Ramius. . . STAY OUTTA MY SPOTLIGHT! [Camera fades to black.] -M.J. M. Handler of "Spotlight" V.D.
