Crimson Carnage: Look at this Gravedigger ( points at belt ). I told you to enjoy it while you had it. It was only a matter of time before I got it back.

Vars. You said you wanted a shot at the winner of that triangle match. I will give you a shot at the title. You seem to be the self-proclaimed best. Here is your chance to prove it. I said when I got this belt I would not back away from any challenge. I don't intend to stop now. You name the time and the place. Me and you will get it on in the squared circle. Crossbones: I bet you expect me to be all bent out of shape Douglas. That is not going to be the case. The Order failed to deliver the decisive blow to
Fatal 4 in the PPV. That won't be the case down the road. This was a bump in a long road. Don't think the war is over Douglas. I promise it is about to get uglier. I did not lose this match because of the way I wrestled. I lost it because somebody was there to bail your sorry ass out of the fire. We will meet again down the line Douglas this is far from over. If you think it is you are sorely mistaken. Down the line I will ask for a rematch. In that I will make the following stipulation. If I lose, I give up rights to the title as long as you have it plain and simple. I have somebody I have to take care of first.
That would be you Steve Houston. You cost me something that I wore as a symbol of honor. I don't forgive or forget. I want you to take the corporate suit off and get it on in the ring. We should have fought a long time ago. You know what
I am talking about to. The president there though did not sign the match. That for the reason the president there had his head stuck up his ass. Let's get it on at the next Thrashin. I don't care what style you want the match. If you want a street fight, so be it. If you want to fight on top of the arena, so be it. I don't care what it is Houston. I will make you pay which ever way it is.
You operate in the shadows. It is those very shadows I will pull you out of. I will tell you one thing too in the meantime. If I see you anywhere in a arena, you better be prepared to put your fists up. I don't care if I get a fine slapped on me too. It does not matter. I will rip the suit off you and drag your pampus ass to the ring. Fatal 4, watch yourselves. You never know when we might appear. This fed is not yours yet by a longshot.
