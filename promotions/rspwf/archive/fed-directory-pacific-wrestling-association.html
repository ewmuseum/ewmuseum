Pacific Wrestling Association (PWA) Website: http://pacificwrestling.fateback.com Owner: [name withheld] Description: The PWA stands for the Pacific Wrestling Association, and was conceived on August Twenty-Fifth, Two Thousand and Two. Justin Taylor and James Nourse are the original founders. PWA is something I've thought about for a while, and wish to put a bit of work into, while having a bit of fun at the same time. I don't expect anyone to solely handle in the PWA, but rather have a bit of fun, and maybe fill up an hour or two per week of free time. The most important part of PWA is fun, if you're looking for squash matches and title shots, then save us both the time by applying somewhere else. Most of the players in the PWA would much rather see a well executed angle come off than squash a load of Jobbers and be a seventeen time Champion. I've dealt with my share of egos in the past, and currently, I have absolutely no patience for it. The PWA will run on a two week schedule, with cards every other week, and anywhere between one and three House Shows in the off weeks. The results of the bi-weekly shows will be summarized in a way that you get the feel of the whole match, but you don't have to tread through a thousand words to do it. It has been my experience that the vast majority of handlers rarely read more than their own matches/segments anyway. This could conceivably change is I get a staff of four to seven match writers, but I severely doubt that will ever happen. On the event of a Supercard, matches will be fully written, and I will require help, but hey, no one can do it all. Supercards will be huge events, signifying the closing of one cycle, and the beginning of another; therefore, more will be expected of not only myself, but you the handler as well. Keynote Members: 'Violent' Evan Hurley, Joey Tesauro, Terrance 'Twister' Thompson, 'The Tsunami' Nakamoto Hamaguri, Elvis Sunday, Donovan Cross Federation Champion: Vacant Flash Show Descripition: http://pub32.ezboard.com/fwfwatelevisionnetwork70278frm29 Regular Show Description: Eruption! Originating most weeks from The Volcano, this is where all of PWA's televised action will happen. With anywhere between four and eight matches per card, expect Eruption! to be overflowing with Hawaiian Wrestling Action! PPV Description: PWA will hold two annual tournaments, the Super Juniors Grand Prix, and Rage in the Cage.
