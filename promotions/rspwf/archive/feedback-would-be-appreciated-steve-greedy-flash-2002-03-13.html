Feedback would be appreciated....Steve Greedy Flash

13 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I would appreciate some feed back on this Flash....I am just looking for consistancy really in Greedy's character, and I know Most of you really don't follow him closely....but still...I could use some "In general" advice...
[ The IWF/WOW Logo fades into our view...As Suddenly we hear sounds in the
Background...] " Oh Jennifer....Uhhhhh....Oh Yes..." [ The Logo slowly fades out of our view, and we see Steve Greedy sitting on the couch to his lovely home with a remote control in hand...He has some relaxed type clothing on, and infront of him on the coffe table you can see a glass of
Some sort of mixed drink, and Popcorn...Greedy while smiling begins to speak...]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Some of you people thought I would be upset about how the Last two or so weeks played out here in this glorious federation..."
[ In the background, Coming from the TV we assume we hear the sounds again..."Oh Jennifer...Uhhh...Oh Yes.." Greedy's face turns a bit red as he stops in mid sentance, He then turns the tv off, and tosses the remote control on the table infront of him...]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Sorry about that, I was watching tapes of my
Honeymoon...My wife hated it when I called her Jennifer..But anways, Where was
I..." " Oh yes....Some of you thought I would be upset, but why would I be such ? I finally solved my drinking problem, My life is getting back to how it used to be one step at a time.. I got my revenge on Leviathan at Rage, Then tossed him out of the royal rumble to add to the pleasure I had already received from attacking him with a Pipe from behind...That was my main goal in all reality,
To completly make sure the monster Leviathan realized he was my downfall briefly, but in the end I will be his downfall forever, and I also managed to stay in the rumble up until the final four so to speak...I am a small man in size...Rumble's are not really meant for me, but I do get a chance to revenge my rumble loss by facing the man who tossed me out on Mayhem this week...That man being Ceaser Riley..."
[ Greedy pauses, and Kinda Sighs...] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Before we worry about him though, I must remember I face Markus Chrome on Rage..This week the IWF/WOW must want to follow the Pay per view up with some good ratings, Cause they have me booked twice....Once on each show, That way people watch both shows...Smart thinking there by the bookers, but what they forgot to realize was....The people they put me against can't really help me much at all when it comes to carrying a good five star type match up which is usually what I try to compete in...Sure,
Ceaser has a few good spots..He is a spotty wrestler, but that's about it..His consistancy level is very low, and as far as Chrome..Well, Let's just say I think Glovie and the Maverick could wrestle a smarter match than him...If you know what I am saying.."
[ Greedy reaches down towards the table and grabs his mixed drink..He takes a small sip, then continues speaking..]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " I have been threw some hard times here in the
IWF/WOW....Losing to Leviathan in a match I thought I should of had, Tapping out in a match with Jacen Canadi and Cooper Concrete, Having problems in my marriage with my wife Ginger Dupree, A drinking problem, and not to mention The lack of the spotlight...I am used to the spotlight, and here I am just not getting it...But with all that said...Let's go back to what I said about the last two weeks...Steve Greedy is starting to become a player...I have learned what button's to push and what cards to play...My money is starting to help me zig and zag threw the rest of the IWF/WOW Crop...Like I said earlier, This week
I am booked twice, and both should be wins.."
" Not just win's though...Win's over people who the fans like, Wins over people who are in line for title shots, Win's which should move Steve Greedy towards my goal of being rich, Famous and Infamous...I am not sure what my gameplans will be quite yet for the matches, but I do promise you all I will cheat, Bribe and Manipulate if nessecary to win these matches...I will have trick's up my sleeve and aces in the hole come match time.."
" Markus Chrome is a man who has battled with The current North American Champion quite often threw his carrear and usually came up short..The good news is though, He is almost a carbon copy of the champion...So if I take it to him,
Like I think I can....With a few minor adjustments...I should be able to take it to Jacen Canadi...Which is something I really want to do here in the IWF/WOW
Since he did make me tap out and he did win the Royal Rumble in which I entered...I have never spoke to Markus and he has never spoke to me...I don't even know if I have seen him wrestle live yet....I am sure he has me, Since I am Steve Greedy, but I don't think I ever stuck around for any of his matches...None of that matters though...I don't need to do homework, or study tapes...I know that on Rage, I will walk out the winner..Why do I know this
?...Well let's just say I had a vision while I was sleeping, and the vision said "Your NEW North American Champion Steve Greedy "....And sure...Markus isn't the champion, but any man who ever loses to Chrome will never be a champion in any federation around the Globe..That is a fact, So I am positive that the vision means I will come out with a win one way or another at Rage this weekend..."
[ Greedy pauses and takes another sip of his drink...He clears his throat and continues...]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " Rage is just a stepping stone though...Cause the real chance for me to continue my earlier mentioned goal is on Mayhem...The
Rich One versus the Next Generation...."
[ Greedy Chuckles...] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " I am not sure why he is dubbed the "Next Generation"....Maybe it's cause he won some Title in NEO..The land of the underachievers...But this is the IWF/WOW and it just so happens...both me and
Riley are smaller men, Quicker than your average type men and both of us are young....So what makes him the "Next Generation" I ask ?...I will tell you what..It's not that he is the actual in reality Next Generation of this sport....He got the name cause the fans like him, Since he plays by the rules and sucks up to them every chance they get...Thus the cute lil nickname...He can have the nickname though...Cause anybody who has ever seen me wrestle knows...One on One...Nobody can beat the Rich One...Nobody..."
[ Greedy sits the mixed drink back on the table infront of him and stands up...The camera pans up a bit...]
"The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " So beleive you are the next generation if you want kid....But once we are done maybe you will look at that and change it to "
Part of the next Generation" Ceaser Riley....That's how good I plan on being from the first bell to the last once it is time for us to compete...Money is one thing that will be in your generation, The last generation and every generation...The other two things are Pussy and Ego's....I have been known to have or to be able to get all those things quickly in life Riley....I am prepared for your Generation, The One after, and the current one...The question now is are you ?...Or are you being set up to go...
_BANKRUPT_...." [ Greedy walks out of the room casually, as his words are left to sink into our heads as we fade to black.]
