RP to Critque

13 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
2 Different RPs from 2 different Characters. I'm trying to decide which one to stick with, what do you think? The top one is 'Gravedigger' John Johnson and the bottom one is 'The Holy Flame' John Johnson, both are currently in feds but I'm going to drop one soon.
'Gravedigger' John Johnson: [The cameras somwhere in a desert, the yellow ocean of sand stretches out into the horizon, the barren landscape is empty except for a few drifting tumbleweeds. This is perhaps the closest thing to true devestation you can see this side of the Armaggedon. As the camera pans around a column of smoke comes into shot perhaps 200ft away, the camera starts flying over the dunes, despite the brief period of action the scene remains errily quite. As the camera approaches the source of the smoke you can see that it is being caused by a giant bonfire, a sand storm is blowing up but it seemss to blow around the bonfire which remains unmoved by the chaos surrounding it. You can see a man standing by the fire, he's dressed in a full length black leather jacket which is fully buttoned up, despite the searing heat in the desert the man appears to be perfectly calm and cool. His lips are moving but no sound comes out, as he speaks silently the flames grow higher and taller and the sandstorm grows more and more intense. Just as you think the camera will be destroyed by the intensity of the storm it comes to an abrupt halt, as do the flames of the bonfire. The camera lowers to the ground and begins to move towards the man, he turns to the camera.]
'Gravedigger' John Johnson: Your late....... [A secound man comes into shot he's holding a mic out to the John Johnson in a shaking hand, the camera pans around briefly to show a helicopter resting on the sand a few metres away from them.]
Secound man: Sorry Mr Johnson, something strange happened, a storm came out of no where, we almost crashed. Sorry. [The man seems afraid of GJJ, he's voice is hollow and weak and his muscles are tensed, as if prepared to run.]
GJJ: Yes I saw it, strange things happen out here in the desert, it's a dangerous place. Secount man: We noticed, anyway are you ready to go ahead with the ASWA interview? GJJ: Yes. Interviewer: [Regaining composure] So what brings you too ASWA? You've been working the Indy feds for a few years now but why choose ASWA as the next step?
GJJ: I will do well here, that is guarenteed, success is all I want. Interviewer: Would you care to elaborate? GJJ: No. Interviewer:{Slightly annoyed but GJJs lack of enthusiasm] What are your goals here in ASWA?
GJJ: To win Gold, but then again I know I will do that. Interviewer: Errr ok then, so...... [The helicopter pilot jumps from his vehicle and runs over to the interviewer, the 2 men turn their backs and whisper for a few secounds.]
Interviewer: We've got a radio report, another storms brewing, we need to go now. We can finish the interview back at the airport.
GJJ: No, I will make my own way back. Interviewer: What? It's atleast a 100 miles to anywhere! You'll die. GJJ: I will be ok. Interviewer: Look..... [The pilot tugs at is sleeve and gives him an urgent looks] Screw you, you wanna die then feel free, I ain't stayin' here to watch.
[The interviewer and the pilot run to the chopper and take off, as the camera fades out GJJ can bee seen walking towards the storm.]
'The Holy Flame' John Johnson: *The arena lights cut out, church bells start chiming and on the sixth toll four white fireworks go off on the four corners of the ring illuminating the arena. As the fireworks fade out the lights come back on and the Holy Flame is standing in the middle of the ring, clad in a full length white suede jacket, white shoes, white trousers and a white shirt. Thanks to clever lighting it almost looks as if a heavenly glow surrounds him, all he's missing is a halo and the wings. Someone at ring side throws a mic up to him.*
John 'Holy Flame' Johnson: *Deep southern accent* I am your salvation! This federation has become rotten, rotten to the core! Tainted by corruption and evil it's wrestlers breed within it, I am the clensing light which will destroy the darkness! The good Lord has enpowered me to destroy all evil which stands in my way, I will not be merciful, I will not rest nor stop till I have made this federation a better place for all it's millions of fans. I am the chosen one the messiah sent down from heaven to save this federation from the pits of sin it has decended into! But you mere mortals may know me by a different name, the name of your salvation, I am The Holy
Flame John Johnson!
*A choir starts singing over the speaker system which has been rigged around the arena, the lights illuminating the Holy Flame grow brighter.* JHFJ: As it says in Johnson 3:16, The chosen one will come forth from heaven and save the people from the pits of damnation! I am the Chosen One! I am the Holy Flame! You boy come here
*A boy limps up from out of the crowd on crutches, unsurprisingly he's wearing a Johnson 3:16 t-shirt, as the boy comes up to the Holy Flame, John
Johnson puts his hand on his head and starts whispering something to himself. The ultra sensitive mic just manages to pick up a few words like
'pay' and 'later'. After a few secounds he removes his hand and choirs voice grows louder, the music suddenly cuts out as the boy throws his crutches down.*
JHFJ: ALLELUA! I have healed the cripple! I the Holy Flame John Johnson have mended this boys bones, I am truly the chosen one!
Boy: Can I have my money now? JHFJ: *Whispers* Shut up unless you really want me to cripple you. *JHFJ Throws down the mic and the lights cut out again, when the flash back into life the ring is empty except for a bible which lays open on a page titled 'Johnson 3:16'. The crowd seems confused by what happened, a few people boo and a few cheer, most just stand around scratching their heads.*
