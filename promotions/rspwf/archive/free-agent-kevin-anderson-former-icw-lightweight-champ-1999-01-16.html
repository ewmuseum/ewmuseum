[FREE AGENT] Kevin Anderson (Former ICW Lightweight Champ)

16 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up at an ICW house show. The fans are pretty lackadaisical. Two wrestlers just finished their match and are walking back to the locker room. All of a sudden the theme music of the _real_ ICW Juniorweight champion, ''Gorgeous'' Kevin Anderson blasts through the arena. The fans jump to their feet and start cheering emphatically. Anderson emerges from behind the curtains. He is wearing a pair of black wrestling pants that say "THE CHAMP" up the right leg. He has a huge physique. His long blonde hair is tied in a pony tail.
He walks down the aisle slapping five with some of the fans. The fans pat him on the back as well. He makes his way up on the apron and steps through the ropes. He gets up on the turnbuckle and puts his fists in the air. Fireworks explode from all four corners of the ring. The music fades out as Anderson gets the mic and waits in the center of the ring for the fans to quiet down.]
GKA : "Well, well, well. Look who's back in International Championship Wrestling. Hell, I thought I could stay away from this buisness, I thought I could go out quietly, knowing I am the _true_
International Championship Wrestling Juniorweight champion. But there were a couple of things that forced me to change my mind.
First of all, a poll was recently conducted, to determine the ICW's greatest thirty wrestlers of all time. Now, whilst I was delighted to
make the poll, I was only ranked down at number thirty, and that told me that I just hadn't cut it with the ICW fans. Whilst Pillow knew I was the best, someone obviously disagreed.
So now, I'm back in International Championship Wrestling, solely to prove to the world what _I_ and the other superstars already know.
That Kevin Anderson is the _real_ ICON." [Pause.] GKA : "I don't care what anyone else thinks. All I want right now is for
Pillow to get off his fat, _retired_ ass and sanction me a match for the earliest opportunity. I've let that ageing jackass stand in my way for too long, so now it's time for me to take things into my own hands. If Pillow won't give me a match for _this_ weeks
Friday Fallout, then I'm just going to have to take matters into my own hands."
[Pause.] GKA : "The truth is that the ICW is filled with nothing more than a long list of over-rated, incompetent, no-talented hacks! Guys like
Shane Diamond carry the ICW banner.. and that is a worrying thought. Diamond, I've never liked you, or your style, and sooner than later, we _will_ meet in the ring _right_ here in
International Championship, whether you like it, whether _Pillow_ likes it or not. I'm sick and tired of seeing you taking _my_ spotlight, and soon I'm going to teach you who the real ICON really is.
And as for guys like the Westside Walveran and Tommy Grayson, your days are numbered too. Because my career is turning round, your going to be seeing a new Kevin Anderson, and your days for bragging to the world about how great you two chumps are will soon be over... long over!
In time, I'm going to destroy all the ICW, so that _each_ and
_every_ superstar is quite clear on who the real 'number one
ICW wrestler of all time' really is. Triple X, you are another man who's days are numbered. You've been getting some nice little publicity with the wrestling 'big boys', and I don't like that one bit. So inevitably, soon, we will meet... and I shall _humiliate_ you like you've never been humiliated before... just for the hell of it!"
[Pause.] GKA : "But Pillow, the ball is in your hands. You either get off your ass and do something... or the ICW does things my way!"
[Fade out.]
