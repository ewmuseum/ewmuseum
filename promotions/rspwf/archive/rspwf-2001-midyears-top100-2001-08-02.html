"The Screaming Drillbit" Jason Keening [UEW] #14. Tommy James [GLCW] #13. Doc Holliday [UEW] #12. Eric Emerson [AWP] #11. Madison J. Valentine [NEW, KCW] And the Top 10 of the Mid Year.. #10. Casey James [EMWC, GLCW] - Still growing strong after all of these years. The Blackheart and his partners in crime, the Syndicate, have left a trail of disaster so far this year. James was a part of one of the best known feuds of the mid-year, and faces #3 on our list in the Killing Box at Showtime later this month. #9. Scott "Hotspot" Daniels [UWF] - Scott "Hotspot" Daniels has made a name for himself as a career member of the UWF. A former multiple time tag team champion, Daniels finally broke through in late 2000 to capture his first UWF Heavyweight Championship. Daniels held the strap firmly until April of 2001, and is currently on hiatus from wrestling. #8. Max Pain [AVW] - AVW's cornerstone name. Was always a key part of AVW's continued success. Sadly, retired recently. Pain is one of those names that will be always brought up by the many fans of AVW, he was good... real good and will be missed. #7. "Pimplicious" Leevon Parks [AVW] - Two-time AVW Appalachian Champion. Took the reigns from Max Pain as AVW's top face. Can Parks carry this torch and lead AVW to bigger and better things for the next half of this year? AVW is counting on it, and is counting on Park supplying them with women as well. #6. "Brattitude" Michelle Bailey [EMWC, KCW] - One of the most original characters to come along in a long time. The 17 year old girl trapped in a man's body has risen to become one of the most known wrestlers in all of our sport. And is part of the foundation that has helped build KCW. Held the KCW Volunteer Heavyweight Championship, and is currently EMWC's Juniorweight Champion. #5. "Hot Shot" Stevie Scott [KCW] - Your current KCW Volunteer Heavyweight Champion, and the master of STEVIETAINMENT and the STEVIEGRIN~! He can be also considered one of the top heels in all of wrestling, I mean the guy really gets under your skin. I'm getting annoyed just writing this... #4. Devon Case [EMWC, RCW] - After scratching and crawling for an ounce of respect for the past few years, Devon Case made a name for himself this year after a great run in NEW and now is enjoying the life in EMWC. His match at Eternally Extreme won top billing this year, and one of main reasons is because of all his hard work in the past. #3. Caleb Temple [EMWC] - Who better than Mr. Temple to bring a family... er wait, no. Who better than Mr. Temple to turn your family into the Mansons? Not only did Caleb Temple have one of the top matches of the mid-year, but also took best feud and best heel. #2. The Gremlin [EMWC, KCW] - Taking top gold in both EMWC and GLCW, and being one of the cornerstones on which KCW was built on helps you in these rankings. Really. So, in honor of the Gremlin, may I say "Fuck, shit, asshole, cocksucker, motherfucker, piss, and tits." We all love the seven dirty words. #1. "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade [EMWC, UWF, RCW] - DA MAN! Well at least at the halfway point of the year. We'll see if pressure makes this UWF Champ crack or make stronger. But most likely it will be the latter, even though he did suffer a broken arm... don't think a quick swig of JD won't make him ready to kick some more ass. Hell, he's number one, let's crack open the Crown Royal.
