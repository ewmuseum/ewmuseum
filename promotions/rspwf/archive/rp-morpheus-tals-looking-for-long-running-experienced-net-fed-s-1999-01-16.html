RP [Morpheus Tals] Looking for Long Running Experienced NET Fed's.

16 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Morpheus Tals is standing in an old cathedral. He is wearing a long black robe and a black hat. His hair is hanging down over his face. He is reading out of a "Book" screaming the words out in a thunderous manner. He throws his arms into the air and the roof blows right off the top of the cathedral. He begins to shake abruptly and stairs deep into the camera.]
["Darkened Soul" MT] These powers that I speak of are inevitable, and of trifling power and grace. These words I speak are not those of a fable, but those of authenticity. I do not play games, for this I do not know how to do. I preach my Godlyhood to all that I decide to bless with my presence, and you are no different. But, the thing that is on my mind more than anything is a so called "Match" that I am scheduled to be a part of in the USWP. This man, a man whom the desire I have to fight is unbarring, and this man is The Modern
Day Warrior. Warrior, as I said before, you try to live in the present, but your lifestyle is that of the past, so I say to you, my harmless companion, if you know what is best for you, you shall step down before your soul belongs to the God's...
[Morpheus sets the book down on the ground and the ground begins to shake. It seems almost as if an earthquake has just struck and Morpheus is laughing hysterically. As he picks the book off of the ground the shaking commenses, and Morpheus looks engulfed with joy.]
["Darkened Soul" MT] So this is the "House of God?" Well, my powers seem to overwhelm this place for the "good." All of whom step into this place believe that this so called "God" can help them, but in actuality, he shall do nothing but watch your soul be stricken from the cavity inside your chest, and into my body. He is to afraid to help you mere mortals, and with just cause.
The power that we bring from the depths of hell are much to powerful for "God" to do anything about. This treacherous being, that you all believe to be full of good, is really just a weak and pathetic mastermind of your demise. So I say, weak mortals..._COME WITH US_ We shall take over the world, and this so called "God" shall sit back and watch, because his powers are limited, as ours are infinite in the glory that we bask in. So, I say this to you, Modern Day
Warrior, will you join us...or will your demise be as rechid as the rest of this weak and pathetic universe?
[Morpheus steps from the alter onto the podium and looks up at the cross as he merely laughs in it's face. He stairsdeep into the camera as all of the sudden his eyes become overwhelmingly large and he _SHOOTS_ from the podium, through the wall, and out of the cathedral.]
[Voice] Do not enter this place again Morpheus, if you know what is good for you! This devilry that you speak of is luticris and shall not be tolerated! Remember this and do not cross our line again!
[Morpheus picks himself up off the ground as the voice fades out. He looks up into the sky and his eyes turn bright red.]
["Darkened Soul" MT] Why must you make me do this you weak and pathetic imbecile?!?! What you have caused now is an even more calamitous ending for this planet that you place your so called "Children" on! You had better be prepared old man, for we will not go away. This is the beginning, and the end shall not mark until all of Lucifer's warriors come to earth and destroy you pitiful mortals. I hope that you understand your fate you old worthless imbecile! You and the rest of this magacosm shall live in
_TREPIDITY_ until you... [Screams into the camera.] ["Darkened Soul" MT] LIVE NO MORE!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
