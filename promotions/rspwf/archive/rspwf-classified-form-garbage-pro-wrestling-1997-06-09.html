Garbage Pro Wrestling Announces Itself, 1997

9 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you sick of all of those fantasy wrestling leagues that claim to be EXTREME? If you've been to reading here on RSPWF lately, then you know that one seems to spring up every single day. Most of them are never to be seen again. Well, I'm starting a league that goes beyond extreme, and it WILL be heard from again, I guarantee you. Some of you might be familiar with FMW, Big Japan, or IWA Japan. They have cards full of wild, bloody gimmick matches with names like Barbed Wire Baseball Bat Glass Hell Death Match. In these matches, pretty much anything goes. This is commonly called garbage wrestling. Well, I'm starting the first (to my knowledge) e mail wrestling league based on the premise of garbage wrestling. The league is called Garbage Pro Wrestling (ooh, original). It will be a booked league, with matches decided by the quality of strategies and role plays. The will be one card, Monday Massacre, per week. The league will run like this: The matches for each card will be announced aproximately two weeks in advance. If you have a match, then you are required to send in a strategy for how you intend to win. The strategy is due on the Friday before the card takes place. If you wish to write an interview, it is also due at that time. Interviews aren't required, but they increase your chances of winning, and are helpful when it comes to starting feuds and angles. If you don't send in an a strategy, then quite simply, you get massacred. Stipulations will added to matches at the request of one or both parties in the match. They must both agree on what they will be. All requests are to be sent to ME, and ME alone. Garbage Pro Wrestling has four titles. They are, in order of rank: King Of Death Match Title-defended the first week of every month Television Title-defended the second week of every month International Tag Team Titles-defended the third week of every month Lightheavyweight Title (Weight limit is 220 lbs.)-defended the fourth week of every month. All title matches are automatically a NORMAL match, except the King of Death Title. The KD champ can pick the stipulations for his defense (Subject to approval, of course). All titles are automatically defended on any card taking place the fifth week of the month. Those are Garbage Pro Wrestling's PPV cards. Here's the application to fill out on your wrestlers. Each owner can have three wrestlers maximum. Name: Height: Weight: Finishing Maneuver: Five other moves: Foreign Object: If applicable Type of wrestler: Appearance: Attitude: Origin: Manager: If applicable Manager's Interference: If applicable Tag Team: If applicable Tag Team Finisher: If applicable I'm also looking for a couple people who would like to serve on the Garbage Pro Wrestling championship commitee. They would help determine title shots, keep track of wrestler's records, and other such stuff. Email me at [detail withheld]@aol.com to join! Our first card should be June 30th!
