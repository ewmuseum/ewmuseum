Character's History (Be descriptive) : Ever since he was a child, Henry had a love for wrestling as an art form. His father was a wrestling coach at his local high school, so he was introduced to greco-roman wrestling at an early age. He would often beg his father to let him tag along as the home team had away meets with other schools. Like a himan sponge, Henry soaked up everything he saw. Around this time he caught an episode of the weekly NWA television show on TBS. It was everything he loved about the high school wrestling meets, and so much more. After witnessing Ric Flair take on Harley Race in a steel cage, he knew what he wanted to do with his life. The two biggest names in the sport, locked in combat, both men wearing a mask of crimson on his face held such a lure for him. He only wanted a chance to prove to the world that he could be at their level. He convinced his father to ler him join the wrestling team after weeks of begging and pleading. At first he had quite a bit of trouble from his fellow team mates. With his limited ability, it was obvious that he got on the team because of his father. The final straw came when crouched on all fours, a malicious team mate dropped his knee on Henry's ankle, nearly shattering it.
Remembering the backyard lessons given to him by his father, he hit a leg sweep on his tormentor, dropping the young man on his face. He then quickly crawled over to him, and applied an Arm Bar, refusing to let go until several coaches, his father included, pulled him off the boy. It was later revealed that he had almost pulled the young man's shoulder out of it's socket. The repercussions of this incident followed him until graduation day, where he was constantly getting into fistfights with members of the team seeking retribution.
After graduation, Carver found his goal, his dream of being a wrestler at a standstill. He worked local independant shows whenever possible, but due to his reputation he more often was asked to leave. Until one day, when his father came home with exciting news. Due to an exchange student program at the high school, his father had been in contact with a promoter from Japan, who helped run the Nippon Pro federation. This was the chance of a lifetime. A chance at a clean slate in Japan, where all his heroes had waged epic battles. His embracing of classic wrestling was seen as golden in the eyes of the trainers at the Nippon Pro dojo, and he was quickly put into shows for Nippon Pro. It was at this time that he met Rick Sturgis, otherwise known as Leo, and Timothy
Weathers, also known as Ichiban Gaijin (Best Foreigner). Rick, like Henry, was from the states, Timothy was born in America, but lived his entire life in
Osaka, Japan. The three became fast friends. His first bout was met with much trepidation, for the simply reason that the promoter didn't feel "Henry
Garfield" was quite exciting enough. At this point Henry had become a great fan of horror movies, punk rock music, and serial killer books. A favorite book at the time was The Silence of the Lambs by Thomas Harris. He took the name from the main character of the book, altering the last name. Hannibal Carver was born. All was going well for quite some time, as the japanese crowds seemed to be embracing the newly named Carver's mix of brawling and submission moves.
This would all come to a halt when Hannibal and Leo received what seemed like good news. An american wrestling promotion based in Carver's hometown was interested in signing them both. The two jumped at the chance to wrestle in front of American crowds, and were on a plane to Logan Airport in Boston,
Massachusetts two weeks later. Rick was almost immediately pushed into main event status, still wrestling under the name Leo. Hannibal was another matter.
Two of the prmotions most wealthy backers had sons that wanted to be wrestling stars. They also wanted the gimmick of street thugs. The promotion chose
Hannibal as a partner for them, and was given the ridiculous name of "Da Masta of Disasta". Gone were the suplexes and arm bars, and in came forty ounce bottles of malt liquor and kissing up the the fans. Hannibal was very succesful in his new role, but he knew it was because he was with the two golden boys, not on his own merits. To make matters worse, the two regularly botched the simplest of moves, causing several injuries to other, more competent workers.
Because no one dared upset the promotiont two golden eggs, all the hostility was thrown Carver's way. It looked as things might look up when Leo was able to bring in Weathers, now known as Taurus, but that was short-lived as the now egocentric Leo used Taurus as a stepping stone, and Taurus was soon shelved with a laughable "amnesia" storyline. Before thigns could get much worse, the promotion folded, leaving Leo rich, Taurus homeless, and Carver with a plane ticket back to Japan. Upon his return a new side to Carver was evident. Having a good bout was no longer important. Injuring his opponent became paramount. He now snapped at the fans that he once only worked to impress. Every match turned into a shoot fight, and he began brawling with anyone who dared to speak to him backstage. He was soon given his letter of resignation, but he was not upset.
