[Free Agent] Atomic Blonde; Flash 5

20 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<The camera pans across a deserted baseball field, positioned at the far end of a run down park, scattered with leaves & trash. The wind picks up, blowing derbies and dust across the surface of the field. The camera pans over to a dugout, tagged with graffiti and half-peeled paint. Behind the cage, the Atomic
Blonde sits on the wooden bench wearing a flannel overcoat and black leather pants. He takes a drag from his cigarette, while softly humming `Take Me Out To
The Ball Game` in monotone beneath his breath. He tosses the cigarette to the dirt and addresses the camera...>
"Billy Reel... don't you listen? I already told you who the bully of this playground was. Now, I don't know how they do things on *your* playground, but around here, when I wrap someone's busted ass up in barbed wire & bitch slap across the pavement, they quickly loose that whatever status they had prior to stepping into my park. Reel, you're one night away from losing your status, your dignity, and your pretty little face. I'm gonna carve you like a f<censored>ing jigsaw puzzle... and not even the worlds finest doctor will be able to put the pieces back together."
<Blonde reaches under the bench, pulling out a steel baseball bat wrapped tightly in barbed wire. The light catches the coils, reflecting back into the camera, as does the gleam in his eye.>
"You wanna play hardball, Billy Reel? Well, now you're on deck, bitch! You haven't seen nasty yet, Reel. You haven't seen nasty until The Atomic Blonde is standing over you, pissing down on your pride and sh<censored>ing on everything you live for. All you are is piss & sh-t to me, Reel. Maybe I just wasn't up to it before, but now... now I'm in a pissing mood. I hope you're ready for a mouthful, Reel."
<Blonde begins unbuttoning his flannel overcoat, and tosses it to the ground. He pulls the white undershirt over his head, revealing a body tattooed with scars & bruises. He pushes off the brick wall behind him and rises to his feet, standing face to face with the camera...>
"Reel... I bet you thought the last match was hell. Well, December 24th, you were just outside Hell's city limits. This time, I'm taking you across the border. I just hope to God you've got a better strategy than driving shards of barbed wire though my already numbed and calloused skin. Torture me, Reel..."
<Blonde extends the barbed wire bat and draws it diagonally across his chest, leaving a trail of blood and torn flesh. Trembling slightly, he again pulls the bat across his chest, forming a large X-Shaped cut. Blonde slams the bat into his left hand, gripping the face of the bat tightly and stares deeply into the camera with watery eyes...>
"Billy Reel.. you can't break an already broken man. I've got nothing to lose but the match and a quart of blood. You on the other hand, are risking it all.
That's the beauty of having nothing, I guess. Kinda f<censored>ed up, huh? Yeah... I like it that way. Saturday night, Reel, we play hardball... and your ass is outtttttttta here."
