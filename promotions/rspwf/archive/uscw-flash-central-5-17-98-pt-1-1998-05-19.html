USCW Flash Central 5/17/98 Pt. 1

Sinister (2) Kid Sharpe The hWo The Delinquent Daniel Stephens Da Lench Mob (Noumenon) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Sinister is seen entering a local 7-11 near the USCW building. He walks past the clerks that look on in awe and begin eating things as he goes.
he grabs candy bars and drinks and gulps them down without thinking twice. Finally the man behind the counter begins yelling at Sinister:
Clerk: Don't be eating my food! You pay for everything you take! Sinister: Bite me Habib. Clerk: You no be dis respecting me like that! I teach you a thing or two! (The Clerk pulls out a shot gun and points it at Sinister. Sinister walks up casually and rips the gun from the mans hands.)
Sinister: Listen you little reject burnt turd from the third world. Before I take your dot headed ass and shove this shot gun up where the sun don't fucking shine! How bought I take you and.....
The Management: The remainder of this video has been cancelled due to content. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (The scene comes into focus in one of the technical centers in the lower levels of the USCW building. The camera crews leave for the night and a shadow passes in front of the camera as they shut the door. Sinister walks out in front of the camera and looks around to see if anyone is left. When he is sure he is alone he grins at the camera.)
Sinister: Well now I can finally get a few fucking words out of my god damn mouth without Spicy trying to censor my ass. I got a lot of shit to comment on too, so stick around. it seems I have over looked a few shits in this federation that are beginning to get on my nerves. So before I get into my match on Sunday and Bitchops annoying ass.
(Sinister begins pacing around the room. He turns his head towards the camera and wicked smiles crosses his lips.)
Sinister: First we got Shining Path. normally this asshole would not catch my interest, but I can not just sit back while his worthless stable speaks so poorly of my brother from a different mother, Viper. I personally could beat every fucking one of you at the same fucking time.
There is not a single one of you that could stand up to me, much less my main bro Viper. See, there are wrestlers, and there is WTF. We are beyond anything you have ever seen. We are power, skill, attitude, and nastiness all molded into two of the cruelest things mother nature ever came up with. What are you Shining Path? The terrorist! Ohhh! I am so fucking scared! I a pissing my pants right now thinking of how TERRIFIED
I am of you! Please don't terrorize me! Please, that no talent Bishop could give you a run for your money. You will never be World champion.
you can't compete with us. Viper and I got it all planned out you see. We are going to hold on to that World title till the end. If one of WTF is champion, we are all the champion. We all work to make sure that belt stays where it is, if you catch my meaning kiddies. There is no stable in this federation that can stand up to us! The SWA is nothing more than a shell of it's former self. Old Man Storm can't buy a real win and Ron
Matthews is nothing more than Chris Carter boot licker. the fact that Scott Dakota can't beat him just lowers my opinion of Scotty, and that's hard to do. Now lets see, I don't think i forgot to mention any one I needed to bitch out so moving along....
(Sinister stops pacing and holds his hands up in the air and praises the lord.) Sinister: Oh lord I am going to be crucified by Bishop! Oh no I fear him! He is the son of god himself! What a load of trash! Look Bishop,
I don't want to fight you again, but if you must have me I think i can find time to show up, but sure as hell not at the PPV. You are nothing
Bishop! You are a fucking loser! Less than a fucking ass stain on a pair of week old underoos!! But you want to know something? I will get back in the ring with you. Wanna know why? Cause I got a problem with people talking about me in such a harsh manner in their interviews. I got this little problem with low card scum challenging me. I will sure as hell not fight you at the next PPV, but how does next fucking
Wednesday sound? You and me God boy! You want that fucking cage match? You got it! I am tired of your god damn mouth! You should have settled with surviving our last encounter but NOOO, you had to come and PISS ME
OFF! Get ready for you final prayers to your fake ass lord, cause it's time to go meet his ass. The Sin man can only take so much annoyance before he snaps, and you went and did just that.
(Sinister spots something and goes over to retrieve it. It's the Snake cam from Wednesday night.)
Sinister: Damn, I knew I forgot this thing after I smashed Shining Path over the head with it! Damn, I thought Viper would explode he was so pissed it was gone. Well now, besides the head mark in the side from
Shining Paths thick skull, it's as good as new! (Sinister tosses the snake cam to the side and begins going over the recent rankings in his head.)
Sinister: I am number four??? My ass should be number fucking one! Yoder can't buy a win over that loser Abyss, and he is ranked above me? that's what I get for being locked in worthless feuds with losers like Bishop. Anyhoo, I got another Kiddie asking for the master of evil to come calling. Delinquent, Yoders little boy toy, has been mouthing off about the two masters of this federation more than even Shining Ass has.
Delinquent went and did the on thing that is way uncalled for. it even makes the Sin man shy away. he went and insulted my style of interview.
Just cause I don't like to sit here and bore the fuck out of everyone every fucking night like your ass does not mean my interviews suck. My style is the best style, and yours comes up a far, far, last. I entertain the populous, you put them to sleep. Am I not PROFESSIONAL enough for you? Heaven forbid I should not do things the way Delinquent wants them done. I guess Viper and i just don't know what the hell we are doing. maybe we should both become Delinquent marks and just copy everything he does! Will that make us cool Delinquent? Will we gain your respect then? I got a better idea, I will gain your respect while I am kicking your scrawny little ass! We all know the only reason you joined up with Yoder is because you could not cut it on your own. The little big mouthed kid got all scared of big bad Viper and Sin so he went running for help. The best you could come up with was the animal rapist?
Give me a fucking break! Your ass wasn't even considered for the tag match this Sunday. We thought about it and we said, "Damn, Yoder's only friend REALLY sucks! Lets try and talk him into joining up with Dare so we can at least break a sweat." it's a low thing when Daniel Dare is considered better than you, and you have hit that low kid. Come back when you grow some balls and maybe Viper and I will consider blessing you with an unholy WTF skull crushing.
(Sinister laughs as he thinks of Delinquent mouthing off at him and shakes it off so he can continue.)
Sinister: Now I been saving the best for last. Last and surely the least is our huge tag team match this Sunday against Buddy Squirrel and the animal rapist. We got Daniel Dare, who was so damn afraid of Viper that he ran like a little girl to the BWFE when Viper finally got a chance for revenge and we got Jacob Yoder, whose ideal mate is a fucking horse. I will give Dare credit, the boys got a few brain in his head, but he has not skill worth mentioning and, well, he grabs people's balls for a living. I heard they pay little boys like you big money for that down town, so when your career goes belly up after a few more rounds with the snake man and me. As far as Yoder goes, the man buddies up with little boys like Delinquent and spends too damn much time with horses names Saul. You an figure out my meaning, nuff said kids. It is only a matter of time before Robbie is forced to change the name of this federation to the WTFCW, or the famed What the Fuck Championship
Wrestling. See you Sunday gents. (Sinister grabs the snake cam and heads out of the filming studio. He disappears into the hall and the cameras go black.)

Kid Sharpe: Daniel Stephens. Our battle has been more strategy, mind games if you will. {He holds up a sign with a picture of a chess board on it.} Kid: We have split our series so far but it is far from over. {A sign with an odometer that reads 00000001} Kid: The three way match, then another one to see who gets a title shot, if

{He holds up a picture of Bill Clinton.} Kid: This guy tried to be a role model, but he sadly failed. {A picture of King Louis the 15th.} Kid: Well I guess it doesn't matter, they're dead anyway. This is Kid Sharpe, from TSE of the USCW, signing off.
