Tim Beck : Roleplay : Tell Me What You Think

23 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yo fellas, I really want you to tell me what you think, if you think I suck so say, if you think i'm great, say so!
[Fade in....] [We are taken to a very fancy club. There is a party happening there, and many celeberities are out on the middle of the dance floor. But the man we want, is sitting off on a couch to the side, with a beautiful woman.]
[Tim Beck sits on a well-taken care of green couch. He is in his usual, pair of baggy beigie corduroys, a pique polo which is navy blue, and his brown hair is wetted-down. His body is in shape and looking better then ever.]
[To the side of the couch is a table, where Tim Beck sets his drink down after taking a sip of it. He looks up at the camera.]
Tim "Nugget" Beck: "God, I love being me. See, when your Tim Beck, nothing matters. You can be yourself, throws fits, hit people, hell, for all I care you could piss in the middle of the street. But those are all things that do not go along with Tim Beck, except being yourself, but one thing that does go along with Tim Beck, is killing the Killa. Plain and simple."
"You see, this Friday, the twenty-fourth of July, Killa makes his debut. How unfortante for him, that he makes his debut against me. You see Killa, in case you didn't know, I am simply _THE GREATEST_. And to spell it out for you, you can't beat me now, you couldn't beat me five years ago, and you won't be able to beat me five years from now, because, hell, I'm just too good."
"Killa, I don't care if your a young rookie. I don't care if this is your first chance to beat a star, because, simply......
you can't, couldn't, wouldn't, and won't." [Beck takes another sip of his drink, then smiles.] "Talk, talk, talk. I know the NEW has some great wrestlers, but a lot of them are flat out talkers. Dog with Rabies, your lucky I didn't get my shot at you, because if I did, you would still be foaming at the mouth. Jim Whitten, your flat out nothing, and if we ever meet eachother in the squared-circle, I will crush you."
"Now, call me a talker, because technically, I am. But it don't mean shit because I can back it up, and all of you Tim Beck wanna-be's in NEW can't....."
[Beck points a finger at himself.] "You see this man?..... He's not your ordinary wrestler...... He's Tim Beck..... Plain and simple...." [Beck smiles as the beautiful lady on the couch uts her hands on his chest.] "Me, I'm great. I was born to be great, always have been great, and always will be great. I have more heart than anyone in this sport, hell, not me out one-hundred times, I'll get back up like it's nothing."
"So, Killa, be prepared. Be prepared for the teaching, and beating, of your
_LIFE_ this Friday at Global Warfare, slash, Slam Dance, because that's exactly what I'm going to give you.....
AND THAT'S THE WAY IT IS, LIKE IT, OR NOT!" [Beck takes a sip of his drink and smiles...] [Fade out.]
