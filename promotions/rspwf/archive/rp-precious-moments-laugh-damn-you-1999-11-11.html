<RP> Precious Moments... LAUGH, DAMN YOU!

11 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a new flash for everyone's favorite misunderstood fairies, Binky and Winky, collectively known as Precious Moments. I know you gonna dig this... -------------------- (We open in darkness. Total and utter darkness. From within the darkness movement is heard, as is weeping. Suddenly, a voice cuts through the darkness like a knife. A voice that is unlike anything heard before. The voice says...)
VOICE: I'm scared, Binky. (Without warning, a flame ignites, revealing that the darkness was inhabited by The Binky Fairy and The Winky Fairy, better known as Precious Moments. They are both wearing their tu-tus and leotards, and Binky has the source of the flame in his hand; a lit lighter)
The Binky Fairy: Is this better, Wink? The Winky Fairy: Yes! Thankie, Bink! You're my hero. (Wink suddenly grabs onto Bink, in a huge hug, causing the flame to go out) TWF: AHHHHH! It's dark again! I'm scared of the dark! Ma... ma... mommy! (The flame again ignites, as a relieved Winky sighs) TWF: I thought we were goners! TBF: Don't worry, Winky. There's no reason to be scared of the dark. Well, except for Beezleweezlebutt.
TWF: (Gulp) Beezleweezlebutt? Wha... what's that? TBF: It's a giant fairy eating monster. It only comes out in the dark, and it can see everything with it's one big fairy-tracking eye.
TWF: AHHHHH! PROTECT ME, BINK! (Winky again grabs onto Binky, causing the flame to again go out) TWF: NOOOOOOOOO! BEEZLEWEEZLEBUTT IS GONNA GET ME! (Again, the lighter flares up, casting light in the room) TBF: Stop worrying so much! He'll never find us here. Anyway, we have other things to worry about. Like that tag match we're going to be in, against The
Wild Cards. TWF: Who? TBF: I don't know. I haven't heard anything about them, nor anything from them. TWF: Maybe they're... (gulp) ghosts. TBF: Hee hee. We could only be so lucky. No, they're human. TWF: Oh... then it should be easy. TBF: What do you mean, Wink? TWF: Well... when a human doesn't talk, it means that he's hiding or afraid. If these two guys are hiding or afraid, then we'll be able to walk in and defeat their evil ways.
TBF: How do you know that they're evil, Wink? TWF: Their name is a gambling reference. TBF: Gambling? TWF: It's when two or more things, usually humans, bet material objects against each other in a competition. And it's EVIL.
TBF: Evil? TWF: (Nodding) Evil. TBF: THEY MUST GO DOWN! TWF: They must. TBF: How are we gonna do it? TWF: Easy. We're going to... (With that Winky starts whispering into the ear of Binky. Binky is listening, when a strange look comes over his face. He looks over at his finger, which is still holding down the trigger, keeping the lighter lit. His eyes get big, and then we are again plunged into darkness)
TBF: OW! That hurts! TWF: AHHHHHHHH! IT'S DARK AGAIN! TBF: Hold on, Winky. I'll relight the fire thingy. (The room illuminates again, only this time there is a huge, one-eyed monster thing behind Binky and Winky. Smoke pours out of his nose as he breathes)
TWF: You're breathing awfully hard, Binky. And you're blowing smoke! TBF: Ummm... that's not me. (Binky and Winky turn around, and their bodies tighten with fear. They spin around, their eyes wide)
TBF and TWF: AHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH! BEEZLEWEEZLEBUTT! (Binky and Winky run away, as quickly as possible. The room is dropped back into darkness, as a deep, growlly voice is heard)
Voice: What did I say? (With this, we... well, we can't fade to black, because it already is black. So... I guess we end it here. Until next time, that is) -------------- Your personal Jesus, [name withheld] He handles... Precious Moments (4-0, AWMC) (Yes, I'm counting house shows... 8)) Brian J. Blottie (5-0, ICPW) He also likes cheese. And he marks for Jenn... Oh, and he's the founding member of the RSPWF SECRET SOCIETY! Moze, Jenn, Pato, Ish, [name withheld], CJ, [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], and Las Post are just 10 of it's global members.
