-Jack McLavel -=Quotes=- -=Intro=- A Life Of Hell!(Thanks to Johnny V of IwF) Back in the day on the streets of Pensacola, Florida a bad ass was born. He was born in the back seat of an old shitty Ford Pinto, and was then taken to the hospital and was close to death. When the doctor came to the woman who was in a drunken stupor he told her the baby had died on the way to the hospital.
The baby was still alive and as soon as it was healthy it was put up for adoption. When the child became the age of 13 he knew that the people who had raised him were not really his parents. He looked at his skin and saw it was so tan and white. He looked at the people who raised him and their skin was as black as ash. He eventually ran away from home to seek his parents. He looked long and hard. But the people who adopted him lived in Upstate, New York. He hitch hiked, walked, and ran. And he somehow found his way to Brooklyn. He started walking around the slums and had no idea where to go. Some girl ran up to him in the street and said he looked like her father. He followed the girl to an old run down apartment building. When they walked into the apartment he saw an old man sitting in a recliner flicking through the channels on the television. When he looked at the man it was like he was looking at himself many years from the time. The old man took his attention away from the television and looked at the boy that stood in front of him. The old man muttered something that nobody could hear. He then shouted out "Mike" and the old man ran up to him and hugged him. The old man took him and sat him down and ask how he found his way to Brooklyn. Mike told him the whole story and the old man told Mike that he knew somehow his only born son would find his way home. Mike decided to live with the old man, and Mike enrolled in a local school in the area. Mike's adopted parents began to look for him but he always kept himself well hidden. They searched for a couple of months then they gave up the search. During school Mike made some friends, but little did he know that these friends would turn on him. After living their for a year he thought these people he hung out with would never fuck him over. But it came down one day and they all jumped Mike and took every thing that was on him. Mike crawled all the way home. When he crawled in through the door his real father looked at him and drug him into his room, cleaned him up, and got him dressed.
Mike could not under stand why this happened. Because he never knew what a double cross was. His real father taught him every thing there was to know about the streets and he taught him a few moves on how to fight. The next day when Mike was coming out of school he saw one of the people who helped jump him. He went up to the person and hit them across the jaw literally shattering it. He walked from school to a boxing gym and he walked in. An old trainer walked up to him and immediately assumed he wanted to learn how to fight. He looked at the bruises on Mike's face and he simply said "Someone kicked your ass and you wanna learn how to fight." Mike just shook his head and the old trainer immediately started training Mike. For 2 weeks Mike did not go to school. But during the two weeks Mike was being taught how to fight and defend himself. When he finally went back to school the kid's jaw who he had shattered brought his older brother to school to beat up Mike. Mike dropped his book bag and put up his guard. The kid's older brother took a swing and
Mike dodged it and nailed him in the gut. He automatically bent over and Mike knee'd him in the nose and broke it knocking him out. Mike went on for weeks fighting people and not losing. But he knew when he got hit that it was time to end it quick. This went on for about two years. And on Mike's 15th birthday he started running the streets and selling drugs. One day he walked into a martial arts dog and said he wanted a fight. Mike, got the fight he wanted but he got his ass kicked. So he wanted lessons and he took them until he saw he was good enough and he left. He continued running the streets but he also stayed in school CUs he did not want to become another statistic. His senior year at his High School he went out for the wrestling team working his way to a state championship. Once he graduated and sat on his ass at home he saw pro wrestling on TV and he saw that they were coming to his area so he ran out and bought a ticket as soon as they went on sale. He went to see this and he looked at it. And he thought he could do that. A job where you get to beat someone up. That was his dream job. So he tried to get in the back and knocked out security guards just to get to the back to get a contract or a one time match. The commissioner of the federation told him to come on in. They discussed about the whole thing. Within 3 hours the contract was signed and
Mike was ready to go. Mike went back to his home and told his father and sister the good news and then he packed and said he would be back. He wrestled for about 3 years when he found out his father was shot. He came back home as soon as he heard of this news. Only two people showed up for the funeral, him and his sister. After the funeral they went back to the apartment and he helped sort through their father's things. They packed everything in boxes and moved it to the house that Mike had bought for the family. They set up a room for his father and he let his sister move in. He then went back to wrestling and started working his way to the World title. When he got to the top he dedicated it to his father. After that Johnny just dropped the title and went from fed to fed. He noticed if he could make it there he could make it any where. He has come back on an amazing rampage. He ran through 2 opponents to get a shot at the World Title in the UcW and Wensday he will be in the fight of his live to get it!
