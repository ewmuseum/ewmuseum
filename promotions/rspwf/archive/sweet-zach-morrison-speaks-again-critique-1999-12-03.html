"Sweet" Zach Morrison Speaks again [Critique]

3 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera fades onto the Motel 6 where Stinky Pete, and Zach Morrison are staying the night. They are in their respective beds mind you, and they just got done watching Clement Tymes little interview that he gave. Both men are in shock at what they had just witnessed. Camera cuts to Stinky Pete who is wearing an old
Guns & Roses shirt, and he hasn't shaved in about 8 days....he just stares at the television with his mouth open, and his eyes real wide. When the camera cuts over to Zach he's just got a smile on his face, and is laughing in shock.]
Zach: Wha....what the hell was that? Stinky: Whoa? Zach: Dude that guy is like straight out of the Ozarks. This is horrible!!! HAHAHAHA!!! They sign a guy like me, and now they're going to put me in the ring against some no talent hick from Timbuck Two? Look if I wanted that kind of fight I would've stayed in Texas. There are a million guys who look like
Opie there, and I don't need to travel all the way to Moomooka to fight another. Oh my gosh that was just plain pathetic.
Stinky: Whoa? Zach: Where you watching the same thing I was? Hello?!?! Pete go take a damn shower....you smell almost as bad as that guy at the front desk tonight.
Geez...I can't have you looking bad on Millionaire. Tomorrow is our big day, and I'm not about to let you ruin it for me. If I have to use my lifeline....you'd better get it right when I call you.
[Stinky Pete gets up and walks into the bathroom.] Zach: That guy...how did I ever get hooked up with him? So the "Farmboy" thinks
I'm all talk? Well, we're just going to have to show him won't we? I guess it's going to be the so-called "Modest" Clement Tyme against the "Evil and Caniving"
Zach Morrison. Which is fine by me, because I'm anything but modest. I will beat your overall wearing, tabacco chewing, straw eating, cow milking, tractor truck driving, monster truck watching, bare footed, inbred, ho-down ass anytime of the day, because I live off of people like you. I thrive off of the stupidity that you give....you think that you're going to make your momma proud? Well, let me tell you something boy...you momma will never ever be proud of you, because once she sees Zach Morrison on TV....once she sees "Sir
Sweetness"...she's going to be a fan of mine for life. That is why...on Saturday I'm dedicating my match to _your mom_. I will win that match against you and I will do it for that thing you call a mother. You know and I know that she is going to want me to win. You saw her trying to persuade you not to go out and risk your life against me. Didn't you? She was telling you not to go out there and get your Jean wearin', bacon eatin', corn pickin', one tooth, dirty red headed ass kicked....because she knows that
"Sweetness" is much more than just a name. Oh no...I'm not conceited, I am just convinced that I am the greatest damn thing to ever walk this Earth. So Ma
Tyme....this match is for you because I know that your son is bound to let you down this Saturday. He along with all the other losers in this fed are going to let you down, but you shouldn't worry. You should have no fear, because MA you're looking at the new Mamooma World Champion, and I will be it for a long time and until I see fit that I should retire the belt.
Clement Tyme you call me Cotton Candy? Well I just might be Cotton Candy...you know why? I'm like the Cotton Candy you eat when there is just not enough water to go around. I will stick to you, and dry you out so bad that you're going to have a hard time breathing. I'm that Cotton Candy that is all over your fingers, and hard to shake off. You shake and shake and shake, and all you get is tired. Oh no Mr. Hee Haw....the Cotton Candy never gets tired. It'll never say stop, and it'll never say I quit. Although when that Cotton Candy has run it's course on you...you're going to give up. You're going to wish that you never even opened it up, and started to eat it. Once that bell rings I will be all over you like Cotton Candy on a pair of dirty disgusting fingers.
Nobody will hold me back from my dream of becoming the Motorola World Champion. I can remember when I was a little boy, and I was trying to find a station that carried it. When I was looking all over the place for a sign or magazine that said anything about it...I would stop and imagine what it would be like. Wouldn't life be grand? Wouldn't everything be great if I were the
Marginal World Champion? I mean then people might even start watching the shows we put out there. Then maybe we wouldn't get hicks for wrestlers, but we'd start getting actual guys who are talented. Clement Tyme you're going to be an example and a start of what I need to do to clean this fed up. I know it hasn't opened yet, but soon it will and there is so much trash walking around. There is so much scum on the bottom of this Trash Can we call the MWA...that I need to clean it up. I need to make sure that Saturday is your first and last match.
So when you go home to the Ozarks...and you go up to dear old paw, and tell him to stop playing the bottle or his knee....and then you break the news that you just couldn't cut it as a wrestler. I hope he looks at you and says..."Son I knew that you were never going to be good at anything in your life. I knew that you were only good for one thing, and that's bailing hay. Now go have sex with your mother because she misses you!"
Ugh Inbreeding...that's a whole other subject that I'll get into some other time. Now if you'll excuse me there is a movie that I want to watch. I need to turn this camera off. Okay there we go...
[Zach walks over to the camera and turns off the picture. Although you can still hear sound coming from the room.]
Zach: Ahhh with Stinky Pete in the shower, and the viewers not watching anymore
I can finally watch a movie in peace.
[All of a sudden the sound of the movie is heard...] Man: Oh yeah baby! Women: You're so hard! Man: This fish stick is for you baby! [Camera fades]
