[Roleplay-Critique] "Black Flagg" Vincent Lockheart

14 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is basically a charecter introductory type of deal..anyway, read it, critique it, whatevere...
[The camera opens up to the streets of New York City at Midnight. It slowly makes it's way around as we see we're not in the best part of the city as we see women standing on street corners dressed in tight and revealing clothing.
Some of them are very beautiful while others have the worn look of a long career in this lifestyle. The camera continues around as we see a building with a sign that reads "XXX" on the front. In front of the building two men are talking, they then shake hands and one of them walks away as he puts something in his pocket while the other man takes the money he was handed and adds it to his roll. In the distance we hear the sound of a police siren, it grows louder before it slowly fades away as it moves further away. The sound of the siren causes quite a few people on the street to stop what they were doing, some even going into the nearest building. The camera does a 180 turn around as we see a dark alleyway. A light fixture hangs above the entrance but it is smashed so with the lights outside the alley we can merely see the very edge of the entranceway where a trash can sits with a rat sitting on top, clawing through the garbage looking for food. The camera goes to turn once more when suddenly we hear the sound of heavy footsteps...]
"THUMP, THUMP, THUMP..." [The thumping sound is then drowned out as we hear the sound of broken glass underfoot. Then, just as quickly as they began the footsteps cease. Then as if the world had been put on pauses everything becomes a sickening silence. It doesn't last more than thirty seconds yet it feels like an eternity. It is then broken by the sound of a match striking the wall, then the small light of the match illuminates a small area by the wall then slowly moves upwards as we see the dim shadow of a mans face. The match is then brought to his mouth as it lights a cigarette perched between his lips. The match is then shook out as now the only thing seen is the dim spark of the tip of the cigarette. We hear the sound of the man inhale then see the smoke escape into the air as he laughs releasing the smoke into the air. The laugh tears through the night air like a sound you'd imagine to hear coming from the mad locked inside of the psycho ward of a mental institution. The laughter slowly trails off as he begins to speak. The sound of his voice deep in bass, his words full of conviction yet conflicted at the same time...]
MAN - New York City, it doesn't hide what it is. it's not sugar coated so people can see what the want to see. It's reality...the painful truth so many try to keep from facing. The thing the world can't deal with, I grew up in this city and I am like it. It is what it is and...
[The man steps forward into the dim lighting as we get our first look at the man who is known as "Black" Flagg, Vincent Lockheart. Vincent is around six foot eight inches tall, around three hundred pounds. He has long, stringy jet black hair. The bangs hang down covering most of his face. A mustache on his upper lip which curves down around his mouth connecting to a goatee covering his chin. Two major scars are visible, one of which is under his bottom lip.
The other runs from under his left eye curving downwards ending under his left nostril. He also has another scar running down his left forearm. He is dressed in a pair of torn and tattered black jeans, a dirty black shirt with the left sleeve torn off, and a hole over the right kidney area...]
"BLACK FLAGG" VINCENT LOCKHEART - I am who I am... [Lockheart takes another draw off the cigarette then flicks the butt away. A quick gleam from the tip is seen as it flies through the air for a brief moment before disappearing..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - No one understands that concept. The world is filled with fakes and phonies wearing masks hoping no one will see who they truly are. They spend there time analyzing the few like me. .trying to find out what makes them do the things that they do. Why do they <grins devilishly..> enjoy inflicting mental and physical anguish on others. I've give the answer to that many times, but no on wants to hear it because it shakes the foundation of everything they believe in, but the fact of the matter is I do what I do...
[Lockheart moves his hand up to his face and pushes his hair out of his face exposing a pair of almost crimson red eyes buried deep into there sockets, there gaze looking more towards the ground than the camera...]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - Because I enjoy it. Nothing more... [Lockheart raises his eyes so they gaze directly into the camera, and even though you know he can't see you through the camera you feel as if he is gazing directly into you, piercing your soul with his eyes..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - Nothing less. I spent my entire adult life doing it...Most of it in the pits, the underground where the rich businessmen came to watch senseless bloodbaths without fear of the world seeing what they really are. .
.but now, it's not illegal anymore, now the brutality will be broadcast all over the country, even the world. March to the Millennium is going to be the place where the thing society has attempted to keep in the dark corner for so long has the chance to stand in the spotlight and wash away the sugar coated sense of well being and give everyone a taste of reality.
[Lockheart makes a fist with his right fist, resting it in his left hand as we see a tattoo of a pentagram on his right fist..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - March to the Millennium, the carnage begins. Eddie Jacobs and Victor Hill, the first lambs to be led to the slaughter. The first examples of what this world is truly about..
[Lockheart turns around and moves his hand to the back of his neck pushing his hair out of the way as we see an Anarchy Symbol tattooed on the back of his neck. He then turns back around as his hair once again falls over his eyes..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - March to thw Millennium will be the first showing of true
Anarchy among men. Society can label me, they can analyze me but deep down inside they know the words I speak and the things I do are the truth, and this
Sugar Coated fantasy everyone is living will be exposed for what it truly is when the Black Flagg is dropped....
[Lockheart once again pushes his hair out of his eyes exposing his eyes then slowly steps backwards moving back into the shadows. His red eyes seemingly glow in the darkness, but it disappears as you shake it off realizing it's merely a figment of your imagination...]
[Fades out....] James Pillow Handles a couple guys competing in a couple feds..no biggie
