[Flash] Tommy Grayson

20 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tell me whut'cha think, guys. =) Give me any constructive criticism or compliments. ==================================== [Fade in. The scene opens in an empty basketball court. Suddenly, we hear the sound of a basketball bouncing off the floor. More dribbling is heard, and then a shot is made...it goes into the basket with a "Swish." The cameras pan down from the basketball hoop to the man who shot the ball. We recognize it to be
"Good Ol' Tee Gee" Tommy Grayson. He catches the ball and dribbles it once again. He takes another shot, and he just barely gets it in. He takes the ball again and dribbles it.
Tommy is wearing a sleeveless, black Nike shirt with a white Nike swoosh on the front of it. He is also sporting a pair of black Adidas shorts with white stripes and white Nike sneakers with black trim. His short, closely cropped black hair is gelled back slightly and spiked. Tommy shoots the ball once again, but this time, he misses. He catches the rebound and turns to look at the camera. He speaks as he is chewing some gum.]
TG - Well, it's only a couple of days away from IWA Magnificent Monday Night. Yet another night where yours truely gets ta' show off his talents once again...'cept we ain't talkin' about "hardcore" this time, and obviously, you ain't that jabron-hole, Jose' Tortilla.
Now, I'm dealing with Brian "Thunder" Storm. Storm, you think that you're gonna have 'nuff talent to beat a man like me? I certainly don't think so, and I'll even tell ya' why. It's 'cause you can't shift your styles. It's because you can't wrestle at _my_ pace...and I when I'm in the ring, I pick the damn pace, and I decide what goes on.
[He chuckles] And ya' know what I've decided? To beat your pathetic bee- hind around that ring until you realize that you should've bowed out of this match when ya'
had the chance to. Either way ya' look at it, Storm, everybody's supporting me, and _God_, along with everybody else, knows that I'm gonna come out on top...[smirks] like I _always_ do.
Now, Brian Storm, I realize that you're a man of big words. I know that ya' like to talk the trash talk, but....ya' like to come off as a
"humble guy." Well, my definition of a humble guy ain't some punk who goes around putting some ignoramus in sumbission holds just to impress a bunch of mindless, blonde groupie chicks.
[Tommy walks to the foul line, dribbles the ball a little bit, and shoots. It goes right through the net. He turns back to the camera.]
But come Magnificent Monday Night, you're gonna know what being humble is all about when I shove all that B-S you were spewin' out back in your damn mouth! Tee Gee's gonna show ya' that he's the man. The myth. And yes, the legend. And I'm gonna show you that you're nothin' but just another
_wannabe_ ...another pretender to the throne. Brian Storm.....
....Tee Gee's gonna paint the town red.... .....With _your_ blood...
