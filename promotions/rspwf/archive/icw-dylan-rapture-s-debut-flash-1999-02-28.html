[ICW] Dylan Rapture's debut flash.

28 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene - A small convenience store in Chicago, Illinois. It is almost 2:00
Central Standard Time. A man is working behind the counter, reading the latest edition of The National Enquirer. As he flips through the pages he repeatedly glances up to keep an eye on his store. The only clothes you can see are his
"Uni-Mart" hat. He has a full beard and mustache combonation. He looks almost like Santa Claus. He looks by following his eyes you can tell that someone is approaching the counter. The man folds up his paper revealing his name tag, labeled "Chris". He puts his paper down on the counter and greets the man.]
CHRIS - Is that everything? [The camera shifts over to the man at the counter. It is "The Incomparable" Dylan Rapture. He is dressed in a navy P-Coat with a green silk shirt on underneath it and a pair of black dress pants that fit just a little baggier than typical dress pants. He looks as though he has just been out clubbing.
The camera shows a bottle of Lipton Brisk Iced Tea and a pack of Winterfresh on the counter. The camera shifts back up to Dylan who starts speaking.]
DYLAN RAPTURE - No, I'm taking one of everything, I just didn't feel like carrying it all up here.
[Chris starts punching a lot of numbers into the register.] DYLAN RAPTURE - I was kiddin' dude. [Chris stops typing.] CHRIS - Oh, sorry. Your total comes to $1.36. DYLAN RAPTURE - I only got one pack of gum. CHRIS - Oh, once again, sorry. [Chris presses three buttons on the register.] CHRIS - Does $1.11 sound better? DYLAN RAPTURE - Yes it does. [Dylan reaches into his pocket and pulls out a one. He then reaches into his other pocket and you can hear change jingling around. He pulls out a quarter and flips it to Chris.]
DYLAN RAPTURE - Give the change to the American Heart Association dish or somethin'. CHRIS - Do you need a bag? DYLAN RAPTURE - It's two things, one of which is pocket size. Do you think I need a bag? CHRIS - Not really. DYLAN RAPTURE - Then why'd you ask? [Dylan smirks at him looking disappointed in the man. Dylan grabs his drink and gum and starts to head out the door.]
CHRIS - Say, I knew you looked familiar. [Dylan stops and turns around.] CHRIS - You're that new guy in the ICW... Dylan Raptor is it? DYLAN RAPTURE - Actually, Dr. Grant, it's Rapture. This isn't Jurassic Park. But anyway, I'm going to get going now. [Dylan starts to head out the door.] CHRIS - Why'd you attack Tommy Kain last night? [Dylan sighs, realizing that he's not getting away anytime soon.] DYLAN RAPTURE - Because, he looked like the easiest target on the card last night, that's why.
CHRIS - Really? DYLAN RAPTURE - No, not really, but I can't tell you the real reason. CHRIS - Why not? DYLAN RAPTURE - ....Well... because I don't have one. I just kinda wanted to get in the spotlight.
CHRIS - Well you did a good job. DYLAN RAPTURE - Yeah, I think you've got something on your nose. CHRIS - What's that? DYLAN RAPTURE - I don't know, why don't you keep it out of my ass. CHRIS - .... DYLAN RAPTURE - Not too bright are ya? Maybe that's why you're working night shift at Uni-Mart. Oh well, bye.
[Dylan turns to walk out the door.] CHRIS - Wait. What brings you here? DYLAN RAPTURE - [sighs] We're in town this week. CHRIS - Chicago? DYLAN RAPTURE - No Miami. YES CHICAGO!! You have one of the damn flyers on your door. CHRIS - Oh... really? DYLAN RAPTURE - Yes! CHRIS - Are you on the card? DYLAN RAPTURE - Yeah, I'll be taking on some guy named Underground Quicksilver. CHRIS - Who? DYLAN RAPTURE - Good point. CHRIS - What? DYLAN RAPTURE - Nothing. CHRIS - Okay, how are you gonna fight him if you don't even know who he is? DYLAN RAPTURE - I don't know. When you're as good as I am you don't really need to plan out a strategy. I just kinda get in the ring, scout my opponent for the first couple minutes and take it from there. I mean, I never have lost a professional wrestling match, and I don't really plan on doing it in my debut match in the ICW. I finally made it to the big leagues and I'm not going to fuck it up.
CHRIS - Watch the swearing, this is a public environment. DYLAN RAPTURE - Yeah, and I'm the only guy in here besides you, moron! Anyway... back to what I was saying before your morality kicked in. Underground Quicksilver is nothing more than one of those guys who comes to the
ICW, loses his first two matches and quits. He'll be off the roster in no time at all, trust me on that. And his manager... Jesus... what kinda sac-relig'
guys am I dealing with here? That's not even close to being funny. CHRIS - What if it's the real Jesus? DYLAN RAPTURE - Shut up. It's not, and even if it were, you'd be telling my mom and dad to get ready to disown me then, because this Tuesday night, I'll knock out the lord of I must to win. Underground Quicksilver has about the same chance of beating me that every other opponent I've faced in this sport has had... and that's not a very good chance at all. Well, of course it's not, because I've never been beat.
CHRIS - You're really proud of that aren't you? DYLAN RAPTURE - Of course I'm proud of it. Wouldn't you be? CHRIS - Probably. DYLAN RAPTURE - Probably? Of coure you would, but unfortunatelly, you'll be working for fifteen cents over minimum wage, until you die, right here. So you'll never have to be proud of anything.
CHRIS - Hey, that's a little harsh. DYLAN RAPTURE - You're like the poster boy for the "Stay In School" campaign. CHRIS - Come on now... DYLAN RAPTURE - Okay, I'll stop. CHRIS - Thank you. So what are you doing Friday on Fallout? DYLAN RAPTURE - Don't you check the ICW website? CHRIS - I can't afford a computer on my salary. DYLAN RAPTURE - Good point. Well, since you asked so nicely, I'll tell ya. Friday, I'll be competiting in a Mini Battle Royal. A nine man Battle Royal. And why I'm in it? I just don't know. It seems like every week ICW has a
Battle Royal for all of the newcomers. Well, I look at it like this... it's just another way for Dylan Rapture to show the world how much better he is than everyone. But not just one guy this time, but eight other guys will have to fall at the hands of Dylan Rapture. We've got "Simply X-Sessive" John Bridges in the match, he has yet to wrestle so I don't really know what to expect from him, nor do I really care. Then in the match is James Drake. Oooh that's a real catchy name. "James Drake." It just sort of leaps right out at you. This guy needs to get a nickname and he'll need to get a couple bandages after he is eliminated from the Battle Royal by "The Incomparable One." Then my next opponent is Underground Quicksilver, and I think I've said enough about him for one night. My fourth opponent is Darren Henry, he's actually wrestled an ICW match. In fact, he's got five under his belt. What's he doing in the "Newbie
Battle Royal"? Point is, I've seen him before, and he just don't got tha stuff to keep up with the fastest man in the ICW. On to my next opponent, we have Spencer Young. Currently undefeated in the ICW after five matches. Well, his nickname is the "Natural Born Loser" and I think it's time for someone to make a loser out of him so it might as well be me. Next to go over the top rope will be Mystic, the virgin beverage. Oh wait, wrong Mystic there. This
Mystic has nothing mystical about him. And even if he did, incomparable means beyond comparison so he doesn't stand a chance anyway. Seventh opponent is
Jack Daniels. Get it? J.D. Number Seven? Come on that was funny.
CHRIS - [sarcastic tone] Oh yeah, it was a riot. DYLAN RAPTURE - Let's not get smart with me, okay? CHRIS - Sure thing. DYLAN RAPTURE - Anyway, back to Jack. J.D. better be ready to drink his troubles and sorrows away after I had him his ass in the Battle Royal this
Friday on Fallout. And last but not... well okay, last and least on the list is "Brass Knux" Jason Williams. I think I've seen this guy in some other federations before, but it's hard to tell since he changes his name so often.
Jason, I hope you bring your woman to the ring with you so after I toss you out of the ring, I can take Tank Girl Tara to the back and unload my turret in her.
CHRIS - Oooh, nice one. DYLAN RAPTURE - Thanks. [He looks down at his watch.] DYLAN RAPTURE - I've waisted the better part of an hour sitting here talking to you. CHRIS - I know, pretty sad huh? DYLAN RAPTURE - Extremely. I better get going. It's late. CHRIS - What's your quote? DYLAN RAPTURE - Excuse me? CHRIS - Your quote... everyone has to have a quote, it practically makes or breaks the wrestler.
DYLAN RAPTURE - Oh my quote. Come here and I'll tell ya. [Dylan steps out onto the first step of the three steps leading into the store. Chris walks over to him.] CHRIS - Why'd I have to come over here. DYLAN RAPTURE - So I could show... I mean tell ya my quote. [Dylan places his hand on Chris' back and shoves him out the door, tripping him on the way down. Chris lands right on his face on the sidewalk.]
DYLAN RAPTURE - Hey Chris... [Chris looks up, dazed.] CHRIS - Huh? DYLAN RAPTURE - ...Watch... THE!!! FIRST!!! STEP!!! CHRIS - What's that supposed to mean anyway? DYLAN RAPTURE - You'll find out Tuesday pal, right before I get the three count on Underground Quicksilver. Now I'll be seein' ya.
[Dylan steps over top of Chris on his way down the sidelwalk. Chris stands up and closes the store, almost an hour later than scheduled. The camera shows
Dylan walking into the distance down the road, eventually he becomes nothing more than a shadow.]
[Fade to black.] Presented to you by... [name withheld] Handler of "The Incomparable" Dylan Rapture. Formerly the handler of Triple X who is apparently gone. Dylan Rapture's ICW Record - 0-0-0 His career record - 8-0-0.
