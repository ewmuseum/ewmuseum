[Free Agent] Summer Kendrik (RP Inside)

28 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[It's black.] "They say I'm hiding." [The voice is gruff and loud. Noticeable for a second, then it echoes back to the darkness from where it hence came.]
"They make up fancy little catch phrases to make me look like a fool. To mock -my- own words." [A long pause from the darkness. Enough time to soak in every last word. A few times over.]
"They say that I'm a has-been and that my time to shine is over." [Pause.] "Yet, I beg to differ." [Another pause. A heavy breathing sensation picks up. Anger building on the inside and just presses to come out. But it doesn't.]
"But still... It remains the same that indeed the 'J' in J-Crown stands for jealousy." [The breathing returns to normal and we're left in complete silence, for the time being.] [Fade up gently to the streets of Detroit, Michigan. The Motor City to the common folk, and a bustling city this time of day. The sunshine shines with a harsh degree of heat down upon the landscape, and many people can be seen walking to their jobs or errands; simply avoiding all of this rush hour traffic. People of all races, classes, and genders roam these busy streets.
All going to a particular place... ... Except for this man.] [In the midst of all these people is a man with no common end point. Simply resting upon a corner store's wall and thinking ahead a few days is Summer
Kendrik. Not seeming himself today. His trademarked smirk isn't seen, and his classic jeans and a tee combo isn't worn. On this day, Summer wears jogging pants. A lazy man is he. Jogging pants and a rolled up sleeved sweat shirt.
Of course, they are yellow in color... But that shouldn't mean a thing.] [Then, the camera comes closer to the streets. Leaving it's birds-eye view, only to shift to a ground level shot. The hundred and thousands of people flying past him without notice of a camera. Slowly, we walk to towards the
Kendrik. And he, too, doesn't bother to address the camera. Unusually so, he's always the first to do so.]
[But from this stance, and staring directly at the ground, Summer speaks in the voice that is distinguishing and audible. The people distorting it a bit, but it's still all intact. It always is.]
"You know, right now, I really don't have a whole lot to say. I'm pissed, and that isn't the best time to be around me. In case you haven't picked that up yet."
[The cameraman gives no response. Instead, he bravely stands three feet away from Summer. The camera right in his face, expecting answers to unasked questions. A brave man, indeed.]
"Look pal. I know what you want. You want me to comment on Scott Sloane and
Doman Schwahling. You want me to smack talk them to the best of my ability and make them feel like the soles of my boots. As easy at that is, I don't feel like wasting my breath."
[The head of Summer slowly raises to face the camera. His blue eyes focusing in to the center of it all.]
"I'm not hiding, if that's what you're wondering. It is indeed four days until
Civil War '99, and I'm right here. I'm in Detroit. I'm damn close to the
Silverdome. And I'm not running anywhere."
[The many people persistently walk in front of the camera. Every now and again distorting the image to the dismay of Kendrik. Several angle switches by the camera man. Just trying to find the one that will remain undisturbed. But good luck, there.]
"But instead of making Sloane and Schwang feel like dirt, I'm going to tell you about how boring my week has been. First thing, I had to listen to a goddamn boring-as-hell Scott Sloane segment. Something about how I'm such a has-been or some crock of the likes. He talked about how he's going to rip Doman apart, and how much of a non-factor that I'm going to be. But the magnanimity of all that, is the fact that Sloane's wasting his breath. It isn't going to be me that's the ill non-factor. It'll be him. Please play again, Scotty."
[Kendrik simply continues without thinking too much.] "Then, Schwahling. Then he comes out and disgraces the J-Crown name with the garbage I saw. He directly attacks Sloane, which is my job, then he tries to insult me and my abilities. Not smart, Doman. Not smart at all.
"If you feel like keeping that title of yours, I suggest you keep to your gameplan and stay the fuck away from me. It'll be better for you, and your career. And that's a promise."
[A wide eyed stare into the camera now by Kendrik. The camera at an angle now where the sun hits down hard against the back of the cameraman. Unfortunately for him, it's probably the only place where the people won't bother him.]
"And you know. Even though all that was painful to watch... That wasn't it. I had to endure more Sloane garbage accompanied by the pointless talk of Doman.
it was all pointless. All saying the same old stuff. 'Summer, you're a has-been.' 'Summer, you'll never be anything.' 'Kendrik, just stay the fuck out of my way.' Gimme a break."
[Summer sighs a long distressful one. He then returns.] "Let me take a moment to add a few of my own. Please respond with, 'Lord here our prayer.' Sloane, you're a babbling moron. Say something different and worthwhile every once in a while? Then I'll respect you. Schwahling, you suck. I can't repeat myself enough time on that simple known fact. You've fought the best second rate cruiserweights across the nation, and you call yourself a champion. I have a little wake up call for you both..."
"... The 'J' in J-Crown? It stands for jealously." [Finally, the smirk.] "I know that you both idolize me. You want to be able to fly like me. Wrestler like me. Talk like me. Hell, even dress like me. But the truth is, there's only one man who can even equate into all these facts. And that's me.
Many men will try, like yourself, but it'll all be a wasted effort. You see,
Doman. That belt that you're wearing around your waist was made by me."
[Summer nods agreeingly to himself.] "I made the J-Crown what it is today. I fought my hard out for LSW's first shot at it. I fought Lightning until we both had nothing left. It truly was epic. But, to make a long sob story short, in the end, I gave respect to the
J-Crown. Soon after that, all the cruiserweights wanted a piece of me, even though I had lost. And just the mere purpose that I fought for that title, gave it such a name."
"So it is true that the 'J' stands for jealousy. Scotty feels it, and you feel it. When I made the title that you're wearing, I guess that'll make any champion jealous. Jealous knowing that you can't beat me. But you both are warriors. You both are damn good fighters. And you won't go down without a fight."
"But fella's, save your energy. You... can't... beat... me. Now that that's off my chest, I feel much better."
[Pause.] "So I guess I lied. I guess I did make Sloane and Schwahling feel like the bottoms of my shoe. But that's all right. I digress. Come Sunday, I'll be in that ring. Sloane, Schwahling... If you can manage to dig up some talent in the bottom of your toy closets... You go ahead and bring it."
"I'll show you what real talent looks like." [Fade to black. Then these blue letters.] <center>Monday, September the 6th Kendrik vs. The Dynamic Duo "He'll Definitely Show You Real Talent"</center> [End!]
