Glee Club RP - Please Give Feedback

6 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** Hey, everyone. I just thought I'd post this here and see what everyone thinks. If you all would tell me what you like/dislike, what needs improving in your eyes, I'd appreciate it.
Thanks! ** [A faint sound is heard in the background. There's nothing but the sound. You can't quite make it out, so, obviously, it annoys the heck out of you. You think and think, to the point where you begin to get a headache. All there is, that incessant sound. Then... you realize you're surrounded. Surrounded by a constant in life.
Everything around you. All there is at the beginning and the end...] [Darkness.] *BOOM!* [There is an explosion, followed quickly by the sound of air raid sirens wailing. The sirens continue screaming for a few more moments before slowly fading out. With the explosion came the opening of the scene. Neat, huh? What a wonderful world of advancing technology we live in.
Anywho... Ahh, yes... The scene. We're in the bedroom that belongs to one ICON of the BJWC, namely the MSW. This is the same bedroom of the same mansion of the same wrestling superstar that lives in Miami, FL. The look of the room has changed a little... He still has the big screen
TV across from his bed. His bed is now covered by a BJWC bed spread, his walls covered with posters of various BJWC superstars. Suddenly, we hear a loud flushing sound...]
'Ahhhh, yeeeaaahhhh...' [Suddenly, from off-camera, walks onto the screen our favorite ICON, "The ICON" Tyler Lee. He is wearing an Orlando Magic jersey with a question mark as a number. It says "The ICON" on the back for his name. He is also wearing a pair of khaki cargo shorts. And, don't forget, mirrored sunglasses. As we follow him across the room, we notice something we didn't see before. Seated on the bed, sitting all pretty like he owns the world, is none other than Bunny. ICON walks over to him and takes a seat. He looks very relaxed.]
ICON: Ahh... nothing like clearing out your system before getting down to business, right Bunny? Bunny: .... ICON: What what?! I am not an idiot! What are you talkin' about?! Bunny: .... ICON: [ignoring Bunny] Let's see... What is on the agenda of business for today..? Bunny: .... ICON: Ack!! You're right!! I have to get things to the way they should be before the first event.
So, what not go ahead and do it NOW! So, without further adieu -- Bunny: [interrupting him] .... [Lee just stops and stares at Bunny. After a few moments, he once again starts.] ICON: Alright... as I was saying before that [glares at Bunny] RUDE interruption... [he makes a face at Bunny, then continues] I would now like to introduce you to the FUTURE of the MSW, PSW, and BJWC! Ladies and gentlemen... may I present to you the rest of my entourage...
[He hops up and runs over to his stereo. He picks up a small microphone used for karaoke purposes and hits play on the cassette deck. "Thunder Kiss
'65" by White Zombie begins playing in the room.] ICON: First, a man who is new to the BJWC... He is the best at what he does! Of course... I'm not sure exactly what that is... but he's really cool! Here is... CHAHUAHUA!! [Note: pronounced
CHA-HOO-A-HOO-A] [Out of ICON's closet walks a man, about six feet tall, wearing a suit of black pants and a black and white striped shirt. He has a very average build. His dark hair is covered by a black derby hat. And, his face is painted white, with red lips and a black tear under his right eye. Ladies and gentlemen... it's a mime.]
ICON: Yeah!! Woo hoo!! That's what I'm talkin' about. Now, nonono, I'm not done YET. I've got one more intro to do. And, let me say, you're all in for a REAL treat... Girlies and guy-guys...
The Underground Franchise... The Paragon of Pain... The RETURNING... The Gomo!! [That's right! From the closet steps the returning BJWC superstar! The Gomo! The midget comes out dressed just like The ICON, except on his jersey the number is a 1/2 and the name is
"The Gomo." He walks over and sits next to the mime.] Gomo: Grr! [The mime turns towards his slowly with a look of fright.] ICON: Don't worry, Chahuahua, he's harmless. [The Gomo looks at Chahuahua and nods while smiling. He then licks his lips and rubs his hands together.]
Gomo: Mime like chicken... Mmmmm... ICON: [looking at Gomo] Riiiiight. [ICON shakes out of his little daze and shrugs, then grins.] ICON: Anywho, all of you may be wondering just what these people are. Well, they're all my managers. That's right. Bunny, The Gomo, and
Chahuahua are my managers. And, well, we're our own little group. What are we called..? [He stands up, grinning as usual, then...] ICON: [shouting] THE GLEE CLUB! [He then sits back down on the bed next to Bunny, across from Gomo and the mime. Gomo is still eyeing Chahuahua closely, licking his lips.]
ICON: Now, it has come to my attention that one wanna-be ICON punk hasn't cut a promo yet. I mean, hey, that could be smart. He might be trying to keep me guessing. He might be throwing me off my game, making me wonder what he's thinking about. And then, he knows that I know that the more I know about his game plan, the more he knows he has to keep changing it to keep me from knowing what to know.
[He pauses... thinking about what he just said. He then beams as if he remembered something.] ICON: Then, -I- remembered something. JAKE BLOOD IS A MORON! He can't figure any of that stuff out. So, all I have to worry about is nothing at all. He's probably off somewhere hiding in a corner, eating pie, stuffing himself with cupcakes, having dreams of grandeur that will end when he steps into the ring with the GREATEST,
BESTEST 'RASSLER in the entire WORLD! That's right... ME! [The ICON chuckles.] Gomo: Er... Who got game?? ICON: I... GOT... GAME!! [The mime stands up, turns, and moonwalks off the screen. He then returns, via the moonwalk, holding some cue cards. He holds one up, it reads, "Jake Blood." He drops it. He then looks straight in the camera, with a very pissed off look on his face, and gives the screen a double bird!]
ICON: Ha! The "Chahuahua Finger"! I love that guy! [He chuckles as Gomo launches himself off the bed, diving onto the unsuspecting mime.] ICON: So, Blood, you should either speak up, put up, or something, cause you already shut up. But, it doesn't matter. Promos or no promos, you're mine. I own you, and I'm The ICON, so I own you good. Blood, remember... When you step into the ring with The ICON, you WILL be staring at the lights!!
Bunny: .... ICON: Please! Don't talk about my momma' while I'm cutting promos! Good gosh... [ICON shakes his head. Suddenly, Gomo and Chahuahua roll across the floor off the screen, scuffling.] ICON: [smiling] Silly boys... [What the...] [ICON looks off-screen.] ICON: Hmm... Never seen that before. A mime doing a 'silent scream'... [Lee shrugs, then looks back at the camera.] ICON: Forget superstars. Forget heroes. Forget legends. There's only two things that matters, and it ain't wanna-bes like Blood. Nope...
it's... The Glee Club and The ICON!! [Suddenly, after a low growl, Gomo flies through the air, crashing into the camera, sending it into static. Blackness follows.]
