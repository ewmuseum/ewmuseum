[Scotty Drake] Not Really a Free Agent...

15 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
But searchin for another federation to join. I am doing well in the L.A.W, and
I need 1 more federation to fulfill some time. =) And I swear to god if you own the JWO, the BMWF, or any other mark ass feds, don't bother E-Mailing me. I am interested in top feds(MVW,CWC,INWI). Here's a sample RP:
[NOTE: My guy is drunk. Keep that in mind.](MAXIMIZE FOR BEST VIEWING!) [Scene: A ghost town somewhere out in New Mexico. The camera is placed at one end of the road that leads into town. On either side of the camera, you see shops, bars, and hotels. The doors are blowing about the endless wind as tumbleweeds roll by. The camera starts moving towards one of the bars at a medium pace. As it comes upon the doors, you can hear voices coming from inside. The camera passes by the old oak doors and into the bar. You see a man sitting at the bar with about 8 shot-glasses around his spot. You also see a bartendor. The bartendor is wearing a white dress t-shirt with an apron over it. The man is wearing a black shirt that reads "L.A.W Crew" on the back in yellow writing, and jeans. The camera stays at a good distance about 8 feet away from the two. Neither of them notice the camera crew.]
Bartendor: So Scotty, what's up with your career these days? How's the ah... wrestling circuit coming? Scotty Drake(Talkin with a slur, because he's kind of drunk): Ah Jay, it's pretty good. For the past 2 years I have been doing some training for a federation called Los Angeles Wrestling. They're a pretty good promotion, solid roster, good staff, and most of all. . . competition.
Jay: That's good. You got anybody lined up right now? SD: Yeah, I got a few men in mind. But I got a match tommorow against a guy named Boltz. He's some "Tough guy" who is the self-proclaimed most extreme guy in the world. I mean noone really cares how extreme he is. He wants to already claim the win over me, but I'll tell ya. . . I ain't gonna go out like a chump.
I might win, but I might lose. No matter what happens though, I am gonna be damn proud because I at least have a job. You know what I'm saying?
Jay: Yeah, I hear ya. Tell me more about this Boltz guy? SD: Sure. He calls me a nobody and says that he isn't gonna lose, especially on television. But I'll be damned if he is going to rag on me, then get the win. I may be a man of action, but I know areas not to go into with words. He came on
"Shooting From The Edge", the L.A.W's interview show and was talking 'bout
"I'll do anything for the win. What will you do Scotty?" I'd wouldn't do
_ANYTHING_, I'd do _EVERYTHING_. Boltz likes talkin' 'bout being extreme, sayin' he'll use tables, chairs, and anything else he can find. I'll use them too! He says I am not high on his priority list, well I should be. I am a dangerous man! I may be a punching bag, because I will admit, I don't control every match I am in, but this punching bag can throw some punches back. I might even be able to throw a couple of moves out here and there. Yeah, I think I will throw some moves, maybe a greco roman low blow, an eye gouge, then I'll hit him with the 'Traffik Jam' and that's the end my friend.
Jay: That's down. Anything else he said about you? SD: Yeah, he said that my name is 'kiddish'. Just adding a 'Y' to my name makes it kiddish? Don't think so. I mean, c'mon Jay, does Sammy Sosa's name sound like a kid?
Jay: Not really. SD: NO SH*T! What about Scottie Pippen!? What about Roddy Piper!? Ricky Steamboat?! Huh, what about Billy Gunn!? Those names are all attached to grown men and they sound perfectlly fine to me. Plus, I have had this name stuck to me since birth, I ain't about to give it up because some Nutz guy thinks it doesn't sound right to him. Clinton gettin' a good mouth job from some fat chick doesn't sound right with the whole country yet he's still in office. The point I am trying to make here Jay, is, you can't just rag on someone about something and expect no bullsh*t back. I am gonna come back at him harder, and faster. Well Jay, it's been cool brotha, but I gotta get home and rest for tommorow. See you later man. . .
[Drake trys getting up, but as he is standing, he stumbles and knowcks all of the shotglasses over. He starts laughing hystrically as the camera fades out.]
