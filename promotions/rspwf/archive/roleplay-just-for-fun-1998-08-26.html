Roleplay -- Just for fun . . .

26 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I decided to post my humor roleplay since I just wanted some opinions from all of you.
This roleplay is from the IWF/WoW e-wrestling federation, and if you want info on it, simply goto their excellent webpage at
http://members.xoom.com/IWFWOW/ (How's that for a plug, Jon? :)) Anyway, I decided to do something different with Robertson for once, and have him do a parody sketch -- he's usually just a straightforward kind of guy.
This probably makes more sense if you know Brian O'Neil's character, but it might still be funny anyway :)
On with the flash! -=-=-=-=- (The scene opens up in an extremely bright room. There is a figure sitting at the table, but all the light is blinding the viewer. Suddenly the figure reaches out and hits something, causing the lights to return to a normal lighted area. It is Al Robertson, sitting at a desk with a wall of books behind him. He is wearing an Armani suit. His shoulder length blonde hair is hidden under a short black wig.)
Al Robertson: Hello, and good evening. Welcome to another edition of . . . ANNOUNCER: "ANGEL OF DARKNESS" Neil O'Brien and his SPECIAL EFFECTS PARADE!!! (Music begins to play ... BOWN ... da da da da da da ... BONG! BOWN ... da da da da da da ... BONG! BOWN ... da da da da da da ... BONG! BOWN ... da da da da da da ... BONG! BOWN BOWN da da da BOWN BOWN da da DA da da ... DO do do do ... __DONG!__ Cartoon clips of "Angel of Darkness" Neil O'Brien are shown. A cartoon Neil
O'Brien being beaten by a cartoon Al Robertson, a cartoon Neil O'Brien getting his with a chair by a cartoon Al Robertson, and a cartoon Neil O'Brien running around with his gleuteus maximus on fire.)
Robertson: Yes, ladies and gentlemen, welcome to another edition of AODNOBSEP, or "Angel of Darkness" Neil O'Brien and his Special Effects Parade. Tonight, we will bring you the NEWCOMER'S BATTLE ROYAL!! Neil O'Brien is being introduced first. (Fades into a mock arena. The arena grows dark. Suddenly, producers are heard yelling as "Angel of Darkness" Neil O'Brien walks out from backstage.
It is Al Robertson with a long curly wig on. He is wearing overly large baggy jeans, in which the butt of the pants is dragging on the floor. "Neil"
is also wearing large work boots which have extra long untied laces which must be dragging 7 feet out from the floor.
Neil is also wearing a black t-shirt that says "dA ToOtH iZ oUt DeRe ..." He is carrying a book of matches. As he gets to the ring posts, he sets off firecrackers from the ringposts, one by one. He climbs into the ring and grabs the mic.)
Neil O'Brien: I point to the ringposts and flames come out of them! I am an enigma ... I am not in a gimmick. I am totally real. Now, cue the fake
fireworks -- I mean ... POW! (Neil points to the far left ringpost and the far right one goes off in flames.) Neil O'Brien: HA HA -- What?? Hey, guys in the truck -- FIX THAT!! I mean
... I MEANT TO DO THAT!! I mean -- I MEAN, YES! GRR! MY POWERS ARE ULTRA-
STRONG! I DO NOT KNOW MY OWN STRENGTH! HA HA HA! (Neil points to the far right ringpost, but instead pieces of paper fall from the ceiling.)
Neil O'Brien: NO! What the hell is going on? PRODUCERS!! YOU'RE ALL FIRED!! ARGH!! (A booming voice enters the arena.) Voice: Introducing second ... AL ROBERTSON AND THE REST OF THE FIELD. (A bunch of extras enter the arena.) Neil O'Brien: AGH! (The words "FAST FORWARD" come onto the screen.) Announcer (It's actually Al Robertson's voice again): I DON'T BELIEVE IT! Al Robertson has eliminated everyone in this batte royale except for Bad Boy
McCade and "Angel of Darkness" Neil O'Brien. O'Brien was eliminated 17 times actually, but the refs didn't see any of them. Bad Boy McCade just showed up
2 minutes ago, and conveniently snuck into the ring.
(Scene flashes to the ring. Al Robertson is standing there in blue jean shorts and white wrestling boots. There are cardboard cutouts of Bad Boy
McCade and Brian O'Neil [Neil O'Brien] in the ring.)
Al Robertson: Hey McCade! Al Robertson impersonating Bad Boy McCade: Hey man! I'm a prankster! BETTER
WATCH OUT ... or i'll ... i'll ... i'll ... GLUE YOUR LOCKER SHUT!
Al Robertson: Then I'll open it with your head. Al Robertson impersonating Bad Boy McCade: AAAAAAIIIIIIIIIIEEEE!!!!!! (The Bad Boy McCade cutout is pulled out by a string as he eliminates himself.) Al Robertson: NOW! O'Neil! Neil O'Brien (Al Robertson impersonating): I am an enigma. I am an angel of death. Al Robertson: Shut up, you're not Travers! Neil O'Brien: Da tooth is out dere ... Al Robertson: Take this! (Al delivers his "super cutter," in which he superkicks his opponent to the ropes and then hits him with a diamond cutter on the way down.)
Neil O'Brien: Oh! Oh, please no more! Al Robertson: Oh yeah! (Al Roberson hits his "Philly Death Drop" on the Neil O'Brien cutout. It is a tiger bomb variant. The cutout falls in half. Al tosses both halves over the top rope.)
Announcer: The winner of the newcomer battle royal ... ___AL ROBERTSON!!!___
Al Robertson: Yes! Yes!
(Fades back to the original room. Al Robertson is sitting there, still in the
Armani suit and black wig.)
Al Robertson: That's all the time we have for the episode. Tune in next week, when Neil O'Brien faces a rock in a one on one street fight.
Goodnight, everybody. (Fades into the credits ...)
