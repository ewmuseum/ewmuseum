RP Crit., another Devonshire RP...

20 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all. Another "Dangerous" William Devonshire roleplay for you all to tear apart. This one was written after the previous one. I like it a bit better because some of the problem areas pointed out in the last one are a bit stronger here, and just because I do. :) It's better in the area of character development I think. But, that's me...please critique, your comments are wanted and welcomed...
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[...screen fades into the interior of small piano bar. The air is filled with lingering smoke, seemingly made to dance by the slow, methodic melodies coming from the grand black piano in the corner. The booths are all full, the bar as well, bustling with activity and conversation. The camera weaves in between the shoulder to shoulder people on the dance floor, peeking inside a booth here and a table there. Finally, focusing in on the pianist, the camera crew find what they are searching for...]
[..."Dangerous" William Devonshire is dressed unusually casual for his surroundings. He wears a red and black beanie, coving the majority of his long, red-streaked hair; bits poking out the sides and rear. He also wears a black t-shirt which hugs his massive torso as well as old, worn blue jeans and gym shoes. Devonshire's fingers run up and down the black and white keys with amazing ease and precision. A pair of stunning ladies lean on the far end of the piano and simply gaze in amazement and the man-mountain punching out the beautiful ditty. Devonshire glances at the camera momentarily, then, as if dismissing it, returns to his playing...]
DEVONSHIRE: Well...once again...no gold around my waist. A shame really. But what to do? Justin Arcola. One thing happened this past week at Mayhem. Respect was earned; hopefully mutual. My frustration with you kept me from accepting your offered hand-shake, I hope you can understand. However, if in your mind you are beginning to think that one day we'll be sharing pleasant conversation over flavored mud at
Starbucks, you have a rude awakening in store for your, sir. I will keep coming, Justin. I'll keep coming at you, a thorn in your side until I put you down. It doesn't end, I don't go away. You will need to be glancing over your shoulder at every turn, peeking around every corner. The one time you don't Arcola, I'll be there.
[...Devonshire comes to the end of his tune. He stretches his arms, cracking the knuckles in the same gesture. Reaching for a tall glass on a cocktail napkin, Devonshire takes a sip of something red, possibly wine, scratching his temple. He then sparks up another unknown song...]
DEVONSHIRE: One more thing before I leave you to your paranoia, Justin. That gold around your waste...won't be there long. You foolishly handi-cap yourself willingly, week in and week out. Though I respect your cause, I laugh at your methods.
They will catch up with you and a clever opponent will take that gold from you; most likely very soon.
[...pause. Devonshire shifts a bit, a break in the music. He then quickly resumes his playing...]
DEVONSHIRE: And now...we come right back to the same place we seem to have left moments ago. TREVOR STORM! Poor chap. I pray that you are not under the mistaken impression that I have forgot about your cameo at my first go at our 'fearless' World
Champion. Not to mention, the fact that I have a very high dosage of medicine for that confused, damaged head of yours. You don't...WON'T! learn Storm! I can't fathom what is going on in that skull of yours, and will no longer attempt to. I come into the Halloween
Mayhem with the same intentions of previous meetings. There is a lesson Storm...one you must learn, soon. Your reckless tactics in this business will lead to your demise, as you have indicated they have in the past. You are on a hot-streak, which I'm sure isn't assisting your already out-of-control ego. Not only will I end your precious 'streak', I will also end your hopes of defeating me. There will be that mark in the loss column for you Trevor, a constant reminder. A constant remnant of your inferiority to keep you grounded, to make the lesson you need so direly stick in your twisted memories. While I am here Trevor...while
I am within these ranks, you WILL BE SECOND BEST! IT'S THAT SIMPLE!
[...a small man in a tuxedo, carrying an empty serving tray under his left arm slowly approaches the piano. In a high, nasal tone he speaks slowly...]
WAITER: 'Scuse me sir, we're going to have to ask you to...keep your voice down. Thank You. DEVONSHIRE: (yelling) WHAT!?! PISS OFF JUNIOR!!! [...the waiter, taken aback, is speechless. He just staggers back, digesting what was said to him and storms off. He shoves his way through double doors into what is assumed to be the kitchen...]
DEVONSHIRE: Now...Storm...like I was saying. You sit in your rat infested hole, in YOUR little 'section of reality' where Storm is God, and you genuinely believe that you HAVE
A CHANCE at defeating me. The fact is, you don't and somewhere in your mental you know this. I will make sure you leave the ring knowing this. I will teach you a lesson,
Trevor. I will teach you one in humility as well as other things. I will _EMBARASS YOU_ and I will _MAKE YOU SAY 'I QUIT'!_ . This I promise you Storm.
I PROMISE YOU!!! [Two moderately built men in black suits come from the kitchen with the same, skinny waiter. This time, with an angry look on his face he comes back to the piano where
Devonshire is playing, flanked by the two men...] WAITER: EXSCUSE ME, SIR! I am going to have to ask you to LEAVE. We don't appreciate your TYPE around here.
[...with a straight face and a cool head, Devonshire halts his song. With a contemplative moment passing, he then rises slowly to his full height, turning to the small boy and his two bodyguards. Devonshire, in one swift motion catching all three men off guard, grabs the boy by his collar and raises him off the ground by a good six inches. With one arm...]
DEVONSHIRE: I'm sorry old chap, I'm a bit hard of hearing. Could you possibly repeat that for the benefit of this poor man?
WAITER: Uhh...umm...well...nothing really sir...I just...well... DEVONSHIRE: Large pianist got your tongue? [...the two men begin to make their way towards Devonshire...] DEVONSHIRE: I wouldn't do that boys. Not unless junior here wants to see the guts of this piano without my opening the lid...get me?
[Devonshire tosses the poor boy at the two suited men with force, knocking all three to the floor. He casually grabs his denim jacket from the piano lid, and pulls it on. As the two men rise, Devonshire finishes his drink in one swallow, replacing the glass.
The two men now, on their feet, come at Devonshire once again. He strolls towards them, one man advances. Devonshire grabs his extended right arm, wrapping it up in his own and plants a stiff foot behind his leg, folding it inward. Down to one knee, the man is put to the ground by a sickening blow to the back of his head. The other suited man makes eye contact with Devonshire and simply stands there in shock as
