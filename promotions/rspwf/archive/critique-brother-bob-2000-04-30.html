[Critique] Brother Bob

30 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The interview begins, appropriately enough, on the entrance ramp. The crowd is a little rowdy, having just been informed that the foam fingers that they've spent fifteen dollars on are a class-one carcinogen, and are being confiscated by the World Health Organization. That'll teach the cheapskates in marketing to buy their stuff from WCW... As the cameras pan over the crowd, the lights dim and, after a moment to be sure that everyone is paying attention, "Blood,
Milk, & Sky" by White Zombie fires up on the PA. The boos are, if possible, even louder. They reach a crescendo as the lights brighten just enough to see a figure standing on the ramp. It is dressed in pure white monk's robes, and it has its head bowed, keeping its face hidden.)
Bob: "Ah, my children, hearing your voices, lifted in praise, makes the grave burden on my soul lighter."
(The boos grow louder. Bob raises his head, letting the cowl fall back to reveal his face, a beatific smile on his lips and a sinister gleam in his eye.)
Bob: "Yes, though my heart is full of bliss, there is a dark stain on the tapestry of my soul. For despite all the things I have given him, my friend
Pike still refuses to see the error of his ways."
(At the mention of Pike's name, the crowd lets out a brief cheer.) Bob: "And yet, still, I think of him as my friend. We've been through so much. Spending our formative years in the Orient getting kicked in the head and thrown through table after table, all the way up to winning the SEWF tag team titles. There was no one I trusted more."
(Bob pauses, seemingly overcome with emotion. The crowd isn't buying it, and starts to chant "asshole".)
Bob: "And all that came to an end, just because I won the Hardcore title. Next thing I know, you're challenging ME to a match? Attacking ME after I give up the belt to go after the World title? YOU UNGRATEFUL PUNK!"
(Bob begins to pace, like a caged animal. His smile is gone, and he's practically foaming at the mouth.)
Bob: "I GAVE YOU EVERYTHING! Who spent a month in the hospital after the barbed-wire landmine match and let you go after the TV belt? Who had to get twenty stitches from a chairshot so your precious girlfriend would be OK? Me, that's who."
(Bob gets very close to the camera, and almost whispers the next line...) Bob: "And who, after five years of blood, sweat, and severe skull trauma, is still the better man? I know the answer. And next week, so will the rest of the world. First one's free, Pike... After that, there'll be hell to pay."
(Bob, after a moment, collects himself and begins to smile. He lowers the cowl of the robe over his face and makes his exit as "Blood, Milk, & Sky" starts up again.)
