Brad Morris roleplay: Please read

12 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just recently "Love Machine" Brad Morris, the most know e wrestler on the planet, changed his gimmick. Please read this interview and tell me what you think about everything!! Thanks!!
(All of a sudden "I'm Too Sexy" by Right Said Fred begins to play. From the back, Brad "Love Machine" Morris comes out wearing his black tights and wearing a white tee shirt with a black heart on the front. In his hand is an old Hearts of Desire tee shirt. He has shaved his head and has a goatee still. He stomps his way to the ring very angrilly and roll under the ropes and demands a mic.)
Brad Morris: TURN THAT SHIT OFF!!! NOW!!!!! (The music stops.) Brad Morris: I'm Too Sexy, bull shit! It's all bullshit! And now the truth is really gonna come out because I've been sitting on my ass at home for the last month. And all I had to do was think. Think about the WHW.
Think about the wrestlers. Think about the Love Machine. Think about my career. And now, the truth finally is gonna come out once and for all.
(Brad holds out the shirt he has in his hand. It was the old Hearts of
Desire shirt and he begins to rip it savagely apart. Then he throws it down towards Jeff Runyan.)
Brad Morris: Jeff, you can shove that shirt and the whole Love Machine crap right up your ass!!
(Huge pop!) Brad Morris: I'm sick of it all. I went through hell for you Runyan. I stuck up for you when those PFWL goons came in to invade. I stood beside you through ever decision you've ever made. I've dropped titles, and I've pretty much flushed my whole damn career down the tubes, all for you!! But that's all dead. And so is the Love Machine!! Runyan, my low card status, my constant losses, my being screwed each and every week, it's all done.
Things are gonna start changing now! NOW!! And you better believe it. I sat at home for the last month. Why? Because of you Runyan. You can shove that little month vacation right up your ass too!
(Brad walks over to the ropes and invites Runyan to get into the ring with him but Runyan declines as he shakes his head.)
Brad Morris: Yea, that's what I thought. Now I come back to face the man who was half heartadly the cause of my one month vacation. Bob Thurmand.
Thurmand, you can outspart my little brother. You can outspart all the other superstars in the WHW. But pal, tonight, you're not going to outsmart me. As I sat at home and thought, and by god I did a lot of thinking, I decided you were going down. Not just for a pinfall. But you are going down in flames tonight Thurmand. Because of you, the people no longer see that TV Title where it belongs, right around my waist. But
Thurmand, soon enough pal, I'll have that title again. Soon enough.
Brad Morris: Which brings me to Kurt Castle and to the Best of What's
Around. You boys have repeatadly come out and cried and whined and cheated until you all had a piece of trash gold around your waists. Kurt, my thinking has led me to a conclusion. You and I, yea, we've been the worst of enemies. You've betrayed me not once, but twice. But ya know, I hold nothing against you anymore. I suppose I would have done the same damn thing if I were in your shoes. And Kurt, the battles are over and the war is done. You have come out the victor. I'll give you all the credit in the world for that pal. Because you deserve credit for what you have accomplished in the WHW. Nobody can possibly deny that. But you beat the
Love Machine. The Love Machine has been layed to rest for the final time
Kurt. Brad Morris is here in his place. The real Brad Morris. But Kurt, it will be a while before I put myself through hell again. For now, our war is done. But someday down the road, the flame will ignite again and I will kick you ass all over the country.
(Brad takes his shirt off and throws it to the crowd. The fans go nuts.) Brad Morris: Yea, sure, I still have the great physique and body that the
Love Machine had. What can I say? But now my attention focuses to one,
Ken Wilson. Ohhhh, the Killer. You know Wilson, you can complain and whine about my success all you want. You can say anything you wish, it is a free country. But Wilson, you will never get me to sign a contract for a retirement match. NO NO NO NO NO!! You see, my friends here in the WHW are virtually non existant. I have none, and to tell you the truth I like it better that way. But my enemies, boy the list goes on and on. And each one of these wrestlers here in the WHW would love to see nothing else but me leaving the WHW. So you see Ken, if I were to sign that contract, I guarantee I would have three hundred wrestlers doing their damnest that night to help you beat me. And the odds aren't too great I could withstand the WHOLE WHW. Well, to tell you the truth, i probobly could kick all your asses in one night, but there is a risk and I'll be damned if you're gonna lure me into that one. Look what happened last month. I had Bob Thurmand come out and whack me upside the head to get rid of me. No way, uh uh. No how Killer. But if you want a street fight you got it. If you want a cage match you got it. You want no holds barred, well brother, you got it.
Just step up, and I'll put you our of your career the old fashioned way. With my own two hands. Brad Morris: Now, Thurmand, I want you to get your scrawny little ass out here. Bring the Dark Riders. Bring the whole friking WHW for all I care.
I feel pretty damn good, and I'm prepared to take all my misery out on your ass pal!! Get out here!!! NOW!!!!!
