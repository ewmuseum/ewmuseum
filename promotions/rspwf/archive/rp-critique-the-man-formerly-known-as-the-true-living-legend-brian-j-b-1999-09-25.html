"The Devil's Advocate" Damian (The red corvette screams down the highway. Like a flash of red lightning, it passes trucks, econo cars, and rental vans, all in it's path. The headlights bear down on old ladies driving big cadillacs in front of it.
Hell, even Harley riders move over to the side of the road as the roar of the V12 engine approaches, burning at speeds up to 125mph and up. The driver laughs to himself, and turns up the music. Just a few tunes from
Metallica's "Ride the Lightning" album. He's feelin' a little crazy, and it can only get better from here. Looking up, he zooms past the sign telling him that his exit is approaching. Moving over to the right lane, he screeches on to the ramp, and hugs the turn like stink on a skunk, and with the grace of an eagle, comes to a screeching halt at the stop sign. All the kids now at the arcade across the street practically jump at the sight of the screeching corvette and just watch, amazed. The car then roars again, and peels into a wicked left, heading down the main road of yet another unknown town. Zooming down the road, cars pull off to the side at his approach. He smiles. Sh*t. Red light. No chance he is stopping. Hope there aren't any handicap people or anything trying to cross the street. Right through the light now...and with that, the familiar whirl of a siren is heard. The Po Po...the 5-0...the cops. Damn it. Ah well, he might as well have his fun now. He's only done what he's about to do once before, and he got into a sh*tload of trouble for it, but it's worth it, isn't it? Hmm, he was beginning to have second thoughts...no, wait. It was worth it. And with that, he accellerated.
Too late now, he was going to out run those doughnut eatin bastards in their cheesy caprice classic squad cars that couldn't out race a corolla.
Without signaling...Hell, what was the point of signaling, he makes a hard right onto a side street, and tears down the neighborhood, honking his horn like a mad man. The cops miss the turn, screech to a halt, back up, and turn down the side road too. No doubt they called for reinforcements, so he would have to keep them guessing, make sure that they couldn't set up a road block anywhere. Now if he were sober, he might not have done this. But damn, he had his share of drinks tonight at the bar, and that just fueled the fire. And boy did he love it. He just better not get caught, or else....well, he's pretty f*cked. Coming around the corner of an intersecting road he just passed, was yet another police cruiser. Two of 'em now. Damn, he must have passed a pizza place or something, they got here pretty quick, f*ckin' pigs. Sirens roared, louder than the engine of the red Corvette with the 666 licsence plates. The whole town was probably up by now. These hicks though, they are all probably bangin' their sisters or something. For a moment, he felt as if he was in the Dukes of Hazzard. He hated that show, but now more than ever, he hated this little sh*tty backcountry redneck town he was in. But damn, they had some good whiskey. Throwin' her into high gear, Damian accelerated even more. He was actually being careful now, he didn't want to hurt his car at all. Hah, those pig bastards can't even keep up. Looking around, he saw another major road coming up. He decided on a hard left, but he wouldn't stop, so hopefully, there won't be any cars coming....hopefully. First he made like he was going right, then he pulled hard to the left, and made a wicked turn that only one could make with a corvette, but that isn't much of a turn at all. The two cruisers flew around the turn as well, and were joined by a third coming down the road he just turned on. This was beginning to look like that Blues Brother's movie he saw on his cable in the hotel this morning. Damn, things were really boiling up. Looking into his rearview mirror, he could see the look on the officer's faces in the first car behind him. They were pissed, and he just kind of stared at them for a moment. They stared back. He laughed, then winked. With that, he hit the brakes and cut the wheel all the way around in a wicked 180. The cops screeched to a halt, and he accelerated again, flooring it, and now going in the opposite direction. The cops all rear ended each other. He just laughed and continued to accelerate, making a left onto some side street, and completely disappearing from the pigs' view. He could see them now.
Cursing under their breath. Sending out a warrant for his arrest, blah, blah, blah. They will check the plates though. Hmm, that should be interesting. He doesn't really have a permanent address though. He made on up on his registration. So, he's untouchable. He loved it. After 30 minutes went by of driving around, getting as far away from that town as possible, he approached a small strip mall, filled with all sorts of stores, and pizza places, and....a bar. Hmmm. Joe's Bar? Looked like a sh*thole. His kind of place. He pulled in, and parked around the back, hopefully, so his car wouldn't be recognized or anything. Damian stepped out of the car. He walked around the building to the front and headed on in to Joe's Bar.
It was filled with three old men. On behind the bar, serving the other two sitting on the other side. None of them recognized Damian, nor seemed to care. Good, that was actually pretty welcome, considering all he ever has to deal with is the usual bullsh*t with people asking him things. He sat down inside and ordered himself a whiskey sour. One guy to his left had been taking shots of SoCo, while the guy on his right had been downing what must have been beer, after beer. Both very depressed men. It was pretty sad, but what can you do? Life was rough. Damian asked the bartender for the remote, and he flipped around the tv a little bit. He found the network he was looking for, and there it was. That stupid little ICPW show, filled with segements and vignettes. Damian had one taped earlier today, and it better have gotten on tv damn it. Speak of the Devil, laughing to himself, thinking of the term, there he was. On tv. The beginning of his segment, about to be shown...Damian turned the tv up, and watched. The segment showed Damian at a local arena for an Indy event, doing interviews...) "Brian J. Blottie? Yeah, I heard what he said, but let me just go over a few things huh? You have no idea, how many times, I have heard, 'Yeah, he's good, but he hasn't stepped into the ring with me.' Please, Bri, get over yourself. I always get that little extra joy of beating the crap out of someone who says that before a match. I don't care if I've never been in the ring with your scrawny ass, the end result is going to be the same with you it has been all my other opponents. You will be pinned. Plain and simple. Then you go on to talk about a damn conscious I don't have. This is the main event, you better believe that whatever conscious I have in me somewhere, it will not come into play. And as for fear? Hey, I know you aren't afraid, and you can bet your ass I sure as Hell ain't afraid either. I'm not going to beat up cameraman, reporters, and production staff to prove my point. My point will be proven on Saturday. Hey, you think you're hot sh*t here in the ICPW, don't you? Guess what, bitch, I'm hotter 'n Hell. And your ass is going to get burned, 666 style. If I have to go through you to move up in the ranks of the ICPW, so be it. I'm already the damn Main Event, baby. That's right. Me. The Main Event. I like the sound of that, I'll have to see if anybody copyrighted that name yet... "Damian - The Main Event." I like it. Anyway, the Devil's Advocate is going to toss you like the salad you are Blottie. And Mr. TMFKATLLDKEHSKSJKSHSLEKSHIE,CNMM,WUMDM,SUKDKKKE-0DKSKSKKSK#%$&KDK*&%&(((* #!!GH SKSKSKSKKSKHJJS, you better believe in miracles, because buddy, that's all ou got left. Now if you will excuse me, I have a long hard night of drinking ahead of me, and thinking about kicking your ass into next week has made me thirsty. The 666 is out." (That was it. End segment. Wasn't too bad of a little cut there. He would have to improve on it though. Maybe later though. Right now, he ordered himself another whiskey sour. The other three men in the bar didn't even notice that that was him on the tv. Good. All the better. He could concentrate on better things...grandaddy's cough syrup that was just laid out right in front of him. I wonder how he got this shot against Blottie anyway? And the main event no less. That's pretty impressive, but the reputation proceeds him I suppose. Looking at his watch, he realized that he can't pass out in his car or anything tonight. For two reasons moslty. A, he had a long day of hitting the gym tomorrow. And B, if he passed out, he would probably wake up to hand cuffs around his wrist and his car being towed.
Hmmm, he definitely did not like that idea on bit. He downed his drink, and ordered a Killian's Irish Red beer to wash it all down. He wasn't as drunk as he was before, when he was driving. Now he was just tired, and realized what an idiot he was being, running from the cops like that. But he did lose 'em, and he laughed his ass off in the bar, out of nowhere. The thought of all three of those bastards in their cars slamming into each other was just two much. And after thinking about the wreck, Damian then thought about
Blottie. That bastard better hope his game is on key, because if Blottie didn't watch himself in the ring on Saturday, he was going to wind up looking a helluva lot worse than any accident. Getting up now, Damian tossed a few bills on the bar, and walked out. He went around back to his car, and looking around a corner, he saw a police officer standing outside his car, writing down the plate numbers, and chatting on his walkie talkie. Sh*t. Not good news. He had to think quick. How in the Hell would he get his attention away from that car. Just then, he did the only thing he could do.
Walk up to the officer, hopefully he wouldn't recognize Damian by his description that the precint must have already sent him by now.)
Damian: Excuse me, officer? I believe I'm a bit lost (I hope this works.) Officer: Damian! Damian the Devil's Advocate, well hot damn, I'll be a monkey's uncle if I didn't recognize you! Hey man, I hear you sign up with the ICPW! Great move! I used to drive all the way up to New York just to take my son to NWF shows, and now you'll be local! Damian: (sh*t, a wrestling fan.) Haha...uh, yeah, that's great. You know, maybe I should give your son an autograph or something... Officer: Hey you know what? That would be great, but first...(the officer pulled out his cuffs) You're under arrest. Damian: (F*CK) What do you mean officer? What did I do? Officer: You had yourself a little joy ride here, Mr. Advocate...(speaking into his walkie talkie) All units, officer needs assistance, the devil has been nabbed. (Just like that, Damian held out his wrists for the officer to cuff. When the officer went to put them on, Damian kicked him in the gut! He then went up behind him, grabbed him, spun him around...HELL'S ELBOW! Geez, what am I doing?? I just used my finisher on a police officer. The officer was out cold. Damian stood over him for a second. Here he stands...a wanted man.
Damn, this was bad. But he smiled. Not only that...he began laughing. He loved this kind of stuff. Grabbing his keys, he hopped into his corvette, revved the engine, and decided to get the Hell on out of there before all his buddies arrived. He wondered how he was going to explain this to the ICPW President, Justin Russell. Should be interesting to see his face, when he realizes what kind of man he signed to a contract. He kept thinking about the officer lying there on the ground, out cold. Damian laughed, but he was having trouble remembering the police officer's face. Everytime he thought about it...the body he was seeing there...lying there, out cold...was Brian J. Blottie, The Artist Still Known As A Dumb Jackass. He liked the thought, actually, he couldn't wait till he got to nail a nice ol' elbow from Hell on Blottie's corpse of a body The mental preparation for Saturday was now beginning. Let's see, it's only Tuesday, and so far, Damian is a wanted man, with a warrant out for his arrest with a various number of charges against him. He loved it. He decided though that he was going to have to stake out somewhere for the night, he should be driving his Corvette around at all. And he was going to have to hide it...good this time. Let's see, let's see...driving by an auto repair shop, he saw a sign that said "Police
Impound
Lot." Bingo. Pulling in, Damian got out of his car for a sec, and broke the chain. Swinging open the barb wire fence doors, Damian pulled into the lot, parked his car, got out, and closed the fence gate now. What better place to hide his car for the night? Would the police honestly look for a car in an impound lot full of cars they already had towed due to violations? Not likely. Damian would just have to wake up early enough tomorrow before the mechanic's got there, so he could leave unnoticed...well, except for that little broken chain leaving a clue. Getting back in his car now, he put the seat down, and grabbed a flask of SoCo out of his glove compartment. Taking a few drinks, he began to think about his day tomorrow...outside of hiding from the police, he had a long day at the gym ahead of him...getting ready to whoop Blottie's ass into the next millenium...) ---------- Handler of...
