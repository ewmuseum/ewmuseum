Bayou Wrestling Association (BWA) Website: http://www.angelfire.com/wrestling/BSW/ Owner: [name withheld] Description: The BWA is a regional fed based out of Louisianna. It's built on the old NWA tradition with a little hardcore from the bayou in it. It's a booked fed with a very talented roster. We represent the Louisiana region of the National e-Wrestling Alliance. Keynote Members: With such talent as 'The Vagabond' R J Harris, 'The Dawg' Paul Blake, 'Sinner' Deke Carter, Brad 'Buff' Baggs, Logan Cain, Steve Arezzo...this fed is full of talent that's burning for competition. Federation Champion: 'The Dawg' Paul Blake Flash Show Descripition: We utilize a rp board system for rps and they are judged on quality. Regular Show Description: We have a show every 10 days entitled BWA: Cajun Rage Live which takes place in different cities and towns over the state of Louisianna. PPV Description: Supercards are being done on a bi-monthly basis to help build story lines and angles.
