Hey Moze, Is this a decent flash?

14 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If it is, post the link to your fed after it and I will most definitely join ---------------------------------------- [Darkness..... The camera sees the veiw from behind someone's eyes. The eyes blink a few times and the man sits up, trying to remember where he is. The camera pans away and it shows a view of the man sitting on a metal table in an all white room. He pushes his silvery-black hair away from his eyes and he begins to mumble to himself.]
Man: Ughh.. Where am I? [The man stands up and looks around, running his fingers through his hair. He begins to panic, realizing he has no idea where he is.]
Man: What is this?!?!?! Where am I!?!?!?!?! [The man looks around and suddenly a door swings open. About a dozen men all in white walk in. They begin to talk to the man.]
Man in White #1: It looks like he is doing good.. Man: Where am I?!?!?! Man in White #1: Don't worry you are safe here.... [The Man in White flashs an evil smile and starts to walk closer to the unknown man. The man backs away into the corner of the room and closes his eyes.]
[Darkness once again, the veiw is once again behind a man's eyes. Blinks.. Stands up.. The man looks around at his surroundings. He sees what appears as a
1600's home, complete with open fire, armor, swords and the tradition clothes from the time period. The man climbs out of bed, as a woman walks towards him.]
Woman: Aye, Weigraf.. Thou has been sleeping for days.. Do ya feel ill? Man: Weigraf? Woman: Aye, tis you! Weigraf: Where am I? Woman: Why, Ya be in Ireland... Weigraf: Where are the men? Woman: What men, Tis just I.. Weigraf: What's.. What's wrong with me.... Woman: Weigraf?.... Weigraf!!!!!!!! [Weigraf falls over, fainting.. Darkness........ Weigraf's eyes blink once more. He awakes wearing the same clothes he was wearing in Ireland, a puffy shirt and a a large grey overcoat, sitting on a bench as if he was a bum. A girl, around 18, sits down next to him.]
Girl: Hey, nice duds man... hehe... Weigraf: Uhh, thanks.. Can you tell me were I am? Girl: Uhh, Your in New York... You musta had a rough night last night heh... Weigraf: [Stareing at the girl] What do you mean... Girl: [The girl starts to talk but Weigraf doesn't hear her.. He looks at her and sees her lips moving but no words, suddenly he hears a voice.. Not her voice but a voice of a man.]
Voice: Yes, Weigraf.. Your in New York.. Do you know how you got here? Weigraf: No, please tell me... Girl: Tell you what? Weigraf: [Talking to the voice and not hearing the girl] Tell you what? Voice: Sleep Weigraf.. Think about the dream with the doctors.. Think about what they did to you..
Weigraf: I will.. [Weigraf snaps out of his trance and begins hearing the girl again] Girl: Your crazy man.. I'll see ya later.. Weigraf: Ehh.. [The girl gives Weigraf a dirty look as she walks away.] Weigraf: I guess I'll just sleep here... [Weigraf closes his eyes and quickly falls asleep. He awakes backed into corner like he was before.. But he is floating about the people looking down at what is about to happen. Weigraf sees the fear in his very own eyes and again the voice speaks to him.]
Voice: Weigraf, do you know who those men are? Weigraf: No.. Voice: Weigraf, do you know what's going on? Weigraf: No.. Voice: Weigraf.. You were born to fight.. You have a power.. Weigraf: I do? Voice: Watch yourself... Watch what you do.. [The men back Weigraf into a corner.. Weigraf looks into his own eyes again.. He sees fear... One of the men try to grab Weigraf.. Weigraf looks at himself.. The eyes change from fear to rage.. From a calm blue to a violent red... He sees himself smile...]
Weigraf: What's going on? Where is that? Voice: The place doesn't matter.. This is what matters... [The moment he says this Weigraf sees himself scream in a of war as he rushs at the doctors and utterly destroys them... He looks up to the sky and screams once more.. Then he snaps out of his rage... He looks around and see the men's bodies, lieing in
unconsciousness, and walks out of the room. Darkness once again...] Voice: Now you have to wake up.. Weigraf: Wait.. I have questions!!! [Weigraf opens his eyes and is sitting on the bench.] Weigraf: I don't understand....... [He slowlys stands up and walks into the distance.] Josh Handles- "24 Karat" Kerry Powell [UWC ~ 4 - 0 - UWC World Champion] Weigraf Tiamat [UPWA ~ 0 - 0 - Currently Looking to join more] "mWa rebirth.... November 22nd.... Be prepared...." - Prez Washo
