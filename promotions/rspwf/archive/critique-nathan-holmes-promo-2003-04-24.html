[Critique] Nathan Holmes promo

24 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alright, if it sucks, and it probably does;) just let it out. (Blackness is all you see, sheer blackness. A deep hollow voice is heard from it.) Man: People fear what they do not understand. (A scene appears, filling up the upper right corner of your screen, of a newswoman sitting at a gray desk speaking muted words, with words behind her in big red letters that say "Is Saddam Dead?" You see a mute sign in the top right of the picture.)
Man: Throughout history it has been proven. (The upper left corner of the screen is taken up by stunning photography of Venito Musallini being torn apart and being adorned on the city square. A mute sign also is here, but in the top left corner.
The newswoman to the right of it still speaks.) Man: But why do they fear? They don't know what the person... (In the bottom right hand corner appears a scene of the rioting Somalians surrounding the black hawk helecopter. Another mute sign in the bottom right corner. The 2 scene above continue with it.)
Man:..Are capable of. So they press the mute button and opt not to listen to any of it, and continue with their lives of ignorance...
(In the bottom right hand corner appears black and white film of
Hitler infront of his masses, raising his arm, the hundreds and hundreds of soldiers following suit. The mute button here is in the botton left. All this while the other muted videos continue.)
Man: As society leads them by a short chain. (The woman with the sign behind her continues talking, Mussalini continues recieving his brutal punishment, the Somalians continue rioting, and the nazis keep saluting.)
Man: But who is the true villain in this reality television show called life? (An American flag appears in the center of it all, it blocks Hitler out of the bottom left picture, so it looks as if the Nazi soldiers are saluting the flag.)
Man: You are. All of you the ignorant trash. The ones who step on less fortunate people for your own gain.
Congratulations, you're the top of the filth. (The Somalians remove the dead bodies from the black hawk helecopter, and appear to be sending him towards the flag.)
Man: You are the cause of all the trouble. But I am not here to lecture and show you the footage that you know exists, you will simply hit your mute buttons and whine about your thrones not being shiney enough.
(A short pause to let his thoughts sink in.) Man: I'm here to show you what happens when you listen to the cries of the people. (All at once the mute signs dissapear, and your speakers are filled with the noise of the newswoman speaking about Saddam, Mussalini's captures screaming, the rioting Somalians yelling, and the Nazis shouting their praises.)
Man: Soon, Freedom shall spread beyond your fries and toast and come to you wheather you accept it or not.
(The pictures fade and you see 2 cold, hateful, and world weary eyes outlined in red and black war paint staring at you.)
Man: Soon, the battle for the truth... (A bloody broad sword is lifted, it covers the eyes.) Man: Lead by Nathan Holmes shall begin and... (The edge of the sword slowly turns towards you, revieling the hateful eyes once again.) Nathan: "If I must die, I will encounter darkness as a bride, and hug it in mine arms" (Fade to black.)
