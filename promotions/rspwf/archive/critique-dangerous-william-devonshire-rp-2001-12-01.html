[CRITIQUE] "Dangerous" William Devonshire RP...

1 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Anyone Who Is Kind Enough-- Another one for ya. It's a departure from my normal Devonshire roleplays. The situation in the I/W is an unusual one and I'm attempting to adapt. Anyway, the actual roleplay is directed at an alliance of "old timers" that really aren't that great, don't roleplay and when they do it's not the best work ever. They are basically where they are because of politics. So, it's directed at them and just reinforces my
"the thorn isn't going away" mentality while changing the direction of my character somewhat. Let me know what you all think...thanks in advance...
------------------------------------------------ [..screen fades into a dark room. A man sits in the middle. He rocks slightly...back... forth...again. You cannot see his face, because it's bent towards the ground, chin touching his chest. His long, red-streaked dark hair falls...reaching for the floor.
Remnants of a white cloth bandage dangle from the man's head...with his hair...towards the floor. No sound, barely a sight...then he speaks...]
MAN-- It's time. Before I sensed it...I felt it coming. Now it is upon me. It surrounds me...envelops me. I am certain of it. You were the messenger. You remnants of the past brought me the future. You brought it to me. I will thank you. I will thank you in the way you thank others. I bring myself here; to you. And you thank me through violence and cowardice. But what other ways do you know? None, I would suppose. You were once "great", they say. They say you were, but were you truly? Greatness is attained when power is tempered with conscience. Through your actions you illustrate your lack of conscience...and therefore greatness. So...
remnants...what is your purpose? Why do you drag yourself from bed every morning and pretend? Pretend to be something you constantly show that you are not...
[...the man shifts, a phase of moonlight plowing through the lines of his face, flooding his features...]
MAN-- The lot of you, the group of pretenders you are. You run together because alone you are nothing. You are wasted space. For each one of you, the other creates the illusion of greatness. You create your illusions so that you may sleep at night.
However now that it is time, I will take your illusions. Then...what will you have? A group of nothing. I'll thrust you into the light so that you are shown. Thrust you in front of a mirror so that you can see. So that they...and you see that you are indeed nothing as I have known all along...
[...the red and brown hair intertwines with each other dances in the silver light of the night...his face aches, radiates the feeling etched on his face and in his glittering blue eyes...]
MAN-- A few short days and someone will lose them. The first. Lose the illusions of course. You feel you have control. You have convinced yourself that these things you create, the greatness and the constant thoughts of it are truth. But in your
MIND! ...in your MIND! you know it to be false. I know this just by seeing you. Just by laying my eyes upon what is you I see it. So what makes you think the people that you are trying to fool are not seeing the same? YOU FOOLS! Your ignorance shines like the moon upon my FACE! I will OPEN your eyes. I will SHOW the world that this ignorance that is your bliss is false and that you are indeed what I have known all along!!!...NOTHING.
...I will show you...so that we may all...have a Happy Ending... [...the face, now masked in a silvery-white light is that of William Devonshire. He is battered quite badly, the white bandage stained with blood and sweat twirls in a slight breeze. He is sitting, ever so calmly in the middle of the room...no sound, no action. Just sitting, waiting and pondering...]
