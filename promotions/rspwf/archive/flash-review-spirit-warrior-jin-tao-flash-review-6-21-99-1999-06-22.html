[Flash - Review] "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao [Flash - Review - 6/21/99]

22 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello RSP-WF'ites and friends, This is MD (Mario) handler of the "Spirit Warroir" Jin
Tao here speaking to you all. I am here with a flash I did for GLCW about four days ago. I thought it wasn't that bad, not one of my best, but hey; it's exceptional. So I want you guys here to critique it and give me all of your thoughts.
Thanks.. Have a nice & most enjoyable evening. Sincerely, [name withheld]. ___________________________________________________________________ V/O : Doctor, the burns are only first degree, once we got past the blistering and did a clean wash, the damage was found to be very minimal.
We've dressed the burns, and at this time there is no need to keep Mr. Tao here any longer.
V/O 2 : Thank you, nurse. Tell Mr. Tao I'll be signing his release forms shortly and faxing them to the GLCW Head Administration Offices. Show him how to apply the protective dressing for his burn, on his own and wish him the best of luck and a speedy recovery. Also, could you ask him for an autograph? It's for my son.
V/O : Very well doctor. Thank you for your time. V/O 2 : Also, do not forget to give him the protective mask. That will keep all risk of infection at bay. That is very important, nurse. Do not forget..
[This conversation fades away as our scene silently fades in on the University of Michigan Hospital located in Ann Arbor, Michigan. The hospital is clearly a towering ten stories tall, the impressive exterior matched only by the welcoming and soothing interior. Slowly, the focus is on the hospital's interior.
As the camera pans the hallway, we notice a brown clip board resting in an upright position inside a clear holder, which mounted on the door of Private
Room One-Twenty-one. Focusing on the clip board, we notice the name "Jin Tao"
labeled across its top. Our camera now proceeds inside room number One-Twenty-One as we quickly notice
Jin Tao, who's lying on his hospital bed with an opened book lying on his chest. The head and face of Jin Tao have been completely wrapped in bandages which cover all gestures and facial features of the "Spirit Warrior," except his mouth and his shut eye lids. A wheelchair waits by the door.
Tao, attired in a blue hospital Johnny gown which reaches past his knees, still lies there without any movement.
Suddenly, the door of the room opens, and a tall, slender nurse walks in. As the nurse approaches the bed, she begins to speak to him quietly.]
N : Mr. Tao. Mr. Tao. Please wake up. I have some splendid news for you ... [Jin Tao, who was just seconds ago lying without any sudden movement, now begins to squirm above his sheets. A yawn from Tao is heard as his attention is drawn to the nurse. Our focus though, is upon the man himself.]
JT : I must have dosed off while I was reading.. Though, I don't remember ever really being tired. Peculiar, do you know anything about this, nurse?
N : Mr. Tao. It must have just been your medication taking effect. You've been asleep for a few hours. [she smiles] Ready for some good news? [Tao now steadies both his arms on the bed, lifting himself into an upright position until he is sitting on the bed. The nurse continues.]
N : Mr. Tao, your burns were superficial, and have begun healing rather quickly. The bandages are ready to come off. And ... the doctor has signed your release forms. [she smiles even more now] Isn't that great news? Though your injuries aren't completely healed, you can go home. Congratulations, Mr.
Tao. JT : Thank you. You do not know how happy this news makes me feel. I will be able to compete this Monday Night, after all.
N : How so? Other than the obvious reasons? JT : I worried that I would not heal in time for this Monday's All-stars of
Wrestling exposition. I wrestle for the GLCW. I suppose I was mistaken, though. Your news is most gratifying.
[Both Tao and the nurse begin to laugh lightly, although Tao's laughter is a bit strained; mostly because of the bandages covering his face.]
N : We'll Mr. Tao, before you do leave, I will help you with your bandages. The doctor says they can be removed as long as you wear a protective facial mask when outside. Your skin is still sensitive from the burns you received and will have to stay dressed and covered for a few more days.
JT : All right, but while you do so, could I ask you a question? Do you watch wrestling? More importantly, do you watch Great Lakes Championship
Wrestling?
[The two continue to talk, as the nurse very carefully begins to remove the bandages from Tao's face and head.]
N : Oh yes, Mr. Tao. I am most interested in my home state's wrestling promotion. I've been a fan of wrestling since I was a little girl. Every chance
I can get, I tune into All-stars of Wrestling. Overdrive and Latenight, too.
[placing the discarded bandages on a small tray near his bed, she continues to talk] ... And yes, Mr. Tao. I've noticed you wrestle there yourself. You face Joey
Cocktails this Monday, right?
JT : Well Ms... [he strains to read her name tag, and fails] You're correct, nurse ... and might I say, you sure do know your wrestling.
N : Yes, I do. I also know how you got your facial injury as well. The
"Firestarter" ... a man by the name Adrian Trust. Correct?
[A grin appears on Jin Tao's face as the last set of bandages come off. Tao releases a sudden sigh of relief as the nurse steps away from the bed, nodding to the small bathroom. He nods to her, then walks over to the mirror in his restroom.]
JT : Yes, you are correct. The "Firestarter" Adrian Trust. The man who did this to my face. A man who I respected and trusted. A man who I thought of as a respected opponent as well as a friend.
But look at my face, nurse. Look at what he has done to me. [Tao sharply turns around to the nurse, who's sitting on a wooden chair in front of the hospital bed. A large blister runs down the right cheek of Jin
Tao, as another large blister just above his left eye brow and forehead is seen. Tao, obviously not in the best of spirits now, continues to speak.]
JT : Adrian Trust is not a warrior. He is not a fighter ... nor is he a man who can be trusted. Adrian Trust is a miscreant and a fiend. I could have never though I would use those words, but the damage has been done.
Adrian Trust, you have lost all the respect the "Spirit Warrior" ever had for you. You are no longer considered just an "opponent" of Jin Tao. You are considered an adversary.
N : ... [The anger from Jin Tao now boils within his blood.] JT : Know this, Adrian Trust ... "Flames do not always destroy .. sometimes they illuminate." I, Jin Tao, have "seen the light," so to speak. I know I must teach you the error of your ways.
But that lesson will have to wait, Mr. Trust ... as you are not what occupies my mind at the moment. On Monday night I face a man who goes by the name of
Joey Cocktails. A somewhat respectable man, from what I have seen. While a bit of a braggart, he is loyal to his friends.
Listen well, Adrian Trust and Joey Cocktails - The lesson I wish to impart on the both of you is a positive one. I was brought to the GLCW and wrestling world in general, in order to restore peace and show you all that hatred is not the way.
I shall continue my conquest ... all the while, keeping one eye on you Adrian
Trust. I will show you how good triumphs over evil, and soon my friend ...
soon you will learn. This I promise you ... In due time. [Tao quickly pauses and brings his hand to one of his blisters. As he touches it, he again squirms in pain and drops his hand to his side. With a slight wince, he continues speaking.]
JT : Joey Cocktails, you are a rookie -- as I am. You know little about this game, as do I. You know little about me, and vice versa. Oh, I've seen your work ... and if you have done your homework, you will have studied me as well. I've heard it said, that you - Joey Cocktails - are "One To Be Watched."
Guess that comes from running about with the likes of Eddie Van Gibson. As the future for Joey Cocktails comes into focus, the only obstacle you may encounter on your road to fame and fortune, is myself. Our match shall be a classic, and it shall be chiseled in the record books. This Monday night, destiny will be met and both our futures focused upon.
Respectable combat shall commence this Monday, Joey Cocktails. We enter into battle and fight cleanly, fairly ... and if all goes well, we shall both emerge victorious. Regardless the final decision.
[Jin Tao walks over to his bead and pulls out his stuff from under the bed. Tao then slips into the bathroom for a few minutes, emerging from it dressed in a simple suit suite. He seems ready to leave as he continues to speak.]
JT : Cocktails, Trust ... and the entire GLCW roster. The order of 'light and good' shall prevail once again, I know it will.
[Tao pauses.] JT : Now that all has been said and my feelings have been released ... I am one within my spirit again. I leave you all with this...
Cocktails and Trust, you may try to contain me. But to attempt that would be like trying to kill me. [he sighs] And trying to kill me is much like trying to penetrate an impenetrable force.
Gentlemen, let it be known, for as I was born ... I did so learn to respect and forgive. 'Tis the only way, I was taught to live.
[As Jin Tao begins to head towards the door of his room with his duffel bag, the nurse walks up to him with a mask-like object in her hand.]
N : Mr. Tao. I forgot to give this to you. It's your mask. The doctor told me not to forget to give this to you. You must wear this to protect your wounded flesh, to prevent infection and possible scarring. It shouldn't be for
more than a week at most. [again, she smiles tenderly] Let me help you with it. Please. [She moves to him, applying a light salve -- then helps him guide the thin, membrane-like mask over his wounded face.] N : That is all Mr. Tao. Now, if you'd please sit in the wheelchair, I'll take you to the lobby and you can be on your way. [she indicates the wheelchair]
Hospital policy. JT : Of course ... [Jin Tao sits and is wheeled out of the door and through the hallway, to the lobby with his mask on. Reaching the sliding glass entrance doors, Jin Tao rises and with a nod to the young woman, disappears into the sunshine outside. As the nurse turns back with the wheelchair, she stamps her foot and runs back to the doors ... hurrying outside. Looking both ways, she pouts and returns to the lobby, realizing that she's forgotten to get Tao's autograph for the doctor's son.]
