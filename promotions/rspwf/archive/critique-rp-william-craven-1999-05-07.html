(Critique) RP, William Craven.

7 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, people. I'm gonna try this again. I JUST want a critique. Please no offers to join your fed as I am already involved in a bunch of feds. I'm sorry, but I just want to know how the world at large views my writing style. Here's the flash that won me the ACWA world title. It's over 1700 words, so keep that in mind before you read it. It's rrreeeaaalll long.
[name withheld]. ____________________________________________ Jackson: Now we have a video for you folks. It comes to you from the man known as Craven. Lane: Doesn't this nut get enough airtime? Jackson: Apparently not, roll that footage. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- . . .Descent. . . Glowing words: Craven-a definition: To crack or break. Glowing words: In the beginning. . . A scene forms from the darkness. A grainy picture-the interior of an apartment. The cake is ready to be cut, people wear party hats, a few children and a large man. The man wears a simple button-up shirt, and a priest's collar. His face is oddly familiar. The video itself is silent, nothing said in the old home movie can be heard. Fade to black.
Glowing words: It's little Billy Craven's birthday. Craven: I remember. I forget many things, but this is etched into my memory. Fade in: The preacher man cuts the cake. Craven: It wasn't a bad present. I liked it! The scene changes. A bit later in the evening, the preacher man gives a long, thin package to one of the children. The little boy happily opens the package, but seems disappointed. Fade to black.
Glowing words: During a psychotic break, his father strikes him once in the neck with a baseball bat.
Craven: It just wasn't a Louisville Slugger. Fade in: The grown man's face twists at the boy's disappointment. Fade to black. Glowing words: William Craven's is paralyzed for the first time. Craven: Every kid in my neighborhood had a Louisville Slugger back then. Glowing words: It is his ninth birthday, his tenth will pass before he walks again. Craven: It was then that I discovered the pit. . . Prison Sex by Tool begins to play in the background. It fades in and out, soft, then loud, then soft, then loud. It skips like an old vinyl record repeating itself at times. Some lyrics invade the words spoken by
Craven. Craven's words stop when the music takes over, never missing a syllable. As the music plays, still pictures flash across the screen.
Music: I'm breathing so I guess I'm still alive. Picture-The boy, laid out on a hospital bed, a black bruise peeks out on the side of his neck.
Craven: When I was in a coma the first time, I became aware of the pit. Music: I've got my hands bound. Picture-The back of the boy's neck, still blackened with cuts from surgery sutured. Craven: It was always there, I knew that, I just hadn't seen it. Music: I'm treading water. Picture-The boy, his neck in a hard neck brace. Craven: Realizing that day that I was on the edge, and had always been on the edge. I was looking down.
Music: close enough so I can smell you. Picture-The boy, awake for the first time. The brace is still there, but he is obviously alive.
Music: You look so precious. Craven: Down. I knew what waited there, I longed to leave it behind, but something was calling me back, God, Satan. . .Mother. . .
Music: You look so precious, now. The music stops for a minute, then dominates as the still images fade to black. Then in white come the words.
. . .Into the pit. . . Glowing words: Craven-a definition: To crack or break. Craven: Standing on the edge. . . Glowing words: July 18, 1992. William Craven has journeyed to Asia to compete in the deathmatches there.
The scene fades in. A grainy image looking down into a pit. It seems artificial and is about six feet deep. Two men face off. Though he looks quite different than he does at age 32, it is obviously Craven. He has fewer scars, most of his teeth, and also hair on his head. Grinning fiercely, he charges at his opponent, missing with a kick. Still the music plays.
Voice: C'mon, Billy, Music: I want what I want. The opponent attempts a backhand, missing as well. Voice: do it for mom! Music: I need this to make me whole. The scene cuts ahead. Both men seem weak. Craven's opponent has him by one foot, and is trying to pull him towards the center of the pit.
Voice: Aw, *bleep* c'mon, Bill! Fight! Suddenly, Craven has something. From it's hiding place in the loosely-packed earth, Craven takes the bokken. He turns to the man holding his foot, and with a cracking sound, hits him in the head with the bokken.
Voice: Holy, he's got the stick! He's using my stick! Go, Bill, go! Craven chokes the man with the bokken then jerks the bokken to one side. The man falls limp.
Voice: Yes! Yes, we got it! Ten G's! Craven looks down as a few figures run towards the downed man. They examine Craven's opponent. He stares horrified for a moment, and then runs towards the camera.
Bill: Let's get out of here! Voice: But, but, you won! Bill: I heard a break! The *bleep*'s gonna hit the fan, let's go, Bro! Let's go! Music: Do unto you now what has been done to me. Craven: (voiceover) He didn't die. He lived through it. He_was_just_like_me. Glowing words: Craven would compete in Asia for years. He would win titles as a kickboxer, and money in the pits.
The scene changes, showing a closeup of Craven. He is bloodied, battered and holds a large trophy over his head. He screams victory. . .
. . .Into insanity. . . Craven: It wasn't difficult to come into wrestling. My only problem was keeping my head. Glowing words: Craven-a definition: To crack or break. Fade in video footage from the ACWA showing Craven with the reverse legbreaker on Dark Angel.
Craven: Although I had the ability, my mind wasn't always in the match. Glowing words: September 2nd, 1998. The inaugural card of the ACWA. Craven is not yet signed to the ACWA.
Another scene. This time, Craven, Wildfire, Rapier, and Flipstyle have been drawn ouside the ring during a six man tag. They brawl with four other men on the outside, while in the ring, Chad Shakespeare is given the superstar spike and pinned.
Craven: To this day, those first few months are just a blur. Glowing words: November 4th, 1998. Craven is forcibly expelled from the
ACWA, and injured at the hands of six men.
Footage again, Craven is bloodied, senseless, swinging wildly at everything that moves. Suddenly Talon Zahn attacks him, placing Craven in his finisher, the Red Alert.
Craven: Which_is_just_as_well. Glowing words: March 3rd, 1999. Craven makes his return to the ACWA, helping to cost Steve Starr the ACWA world title.
Craven: . . .because now I've been able to make a fresh start. A mass of video clips and sound bites plays. The sound and video don't go together. It is a mass of confusion. Everything happens to the sound of
Prison Sex by Tool.
. . .Into hell. . . Mark Farrow shatters a chair over the head of Craven, who is unaffected. Craven: It's not over, Starr, it's never over until I win! Craven hits Starr with his bokken. Craven: I will beat you. Glowing text: What crawls on it's belly. . . Craven: No fear, Glowing text: and lays all men low. . . Craven: The sky is falling! Craven: All I have!!! Craven: I'll beat you, hurt you if I can, kill you if I must. . . Glowing text: in time?. . . Craven sits in his weapons room, a sword in his hand. Craven: I have to settle with killing. . . Glowing text: Call it a casket, Craven on his way to the ring, covered in blood. Craven: No pain, Glowing text: Call it a corpse, Craven: If you got the guts, I got the glory! Glowing text: I'm in 'till the end dear, Craven: . . .but I will beat you! Craven: All I have!!! Glowing text: Call it a fun'ral, Craven: No, no, of course I don't mean kill. Craven on his stone slab, bleeding. Craven: Not die, Glowing text: Call it a fire, Craven: You. . .can't do that in a wrestling match, can you?" Craven is his with his own bokken, once, Craven: Never die. twice, Craven: There are only two ways out, Starr! three times, and again. Craven: All I have!!! Craven bites the neck of the Rose. Craven: You know the one, can you guess the other? Craven bites the head of the Rose. Craven: It's not over, I haven't won yet! Craven eating flowers. Craven: It's taxtime, Starr, do you know what goes with taxes, Starr? Craven twisting young Stefan Rose in the Spreading Blossom. Craven: Just like after I'm done drinkin' my beer, I always break the bottle!!!" Craven: Think about it!" Craven chokes Starr with his bokken. Craven: All I have!!! Now Craven's voice sounds different. Not mixed, not corrupted by recording and re-recording. The screen goes stark black and the sounds of
Prison sex fade to the background as he speaks, Clear as a bell.
Craven: I think I have made myself clear now. Glowing text:What has come before shall be again. Craven: Do you get it yet, Starr? Glowing text: So it is written, so it is done Craven: Do you get it? Glowing text: In the end, there can be only one. Craven: Do you? Glowing text: Descent -into the pit -into insanity -into hell. . . Music: You look so precious, now. . . End video. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---- Jackson: Do. . .do you know what we've just seen here, Scott? Lane: I know, I know what that means! I gotta call Steve! Jackson: Wait! You can't just walk away, we gotta broadcast! Voice: That's right, Lane, now sit_down! Lane: I, oh, no. . . Voice: Oh, yes. You knew I was in the building. I'm very close by, so don't you go warning Stevie. I want him to find out just like everyone else. I want him to know exactly what's going down if I don't get what I want. . .
Jackson: Craven, listen to me, you can't do this, you'll go to jail, maybe worse! They could put you_back_in_the asylum.
Voice: (Craven) I've said nothing, Paul. Just hints. I just want Steve to know. Remember, I've done nothing illegal, remember that.
Jackson: Craven? Craven, hello? Lane: He's gone. Oh, god. Jackson: Oh, god is right. ACWA homepage: http://www.1wwf.com/acwa/Home.html
