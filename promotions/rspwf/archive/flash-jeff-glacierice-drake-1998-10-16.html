[FLASH!] Jeff "GlacierIce" Drake

16 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jeff "GlacierIce" Drake is attending a very quick INWI press conference concerning the INWI holding this week's Hot Spot in Sydney, Australia. Drake is at the podium wearing his John Lennon style sunglasses, blue Route 66 jeans, and a Doors t-shirt that says "Day Destroys The Night" on the front, and "Try
To Run, Try To Hide" with the infamous Doors logo on the back. Ice has already begun taking a few questions from the press in Sydney, but they were all simple questions....let the harder ones begin!}
REPORTER #1: Mr. Drake....are you upset that you were not granted a match here in Sydney for the INWI's Hot Spot?
JEFF "GLACIERICE" DRAKE: Well....it depends. My former partner gave me all I could handle last week, and I'm still feeling a little sore from that contest, even though I came away victorious....I am gonna be backstage however, since
Sydney is a great place.
REPORTER #2: Jeff...as you mentioned, you took on your former tag partner, Tim
Warren....do you feel as though he really wanted to fight you, regarding it appeared as though his mind was somewhere else?
JEFF "GLACIERICE" DRAKE: I couldn't tell you for sure. I was too busy concentrating on winning...if he didn't want to fight me, he should have spoken up _BEFORE_ the match took place, it's a little too late for anything of that sorts now. It's all said and done.
REPORTER #3: Ice...do you feel as though your former employer down south is at a loss since they lost you to the INWI just a few short weeks ago?
JEFF "GLACIERICE" DRAKE: Yes. I was destined for greatness down there, and I had to make a stand for myself due to "GF" being a ***-****! I wasn't bound to degrade myself and lose my dignity, just because of him. And, I have made it very clear that a few days after my apparent "firing", he tried to re-sign me, however, I felt then, and I still feel now, that I am just too good to work as a active competitor in National Extreme Wrestling. So, yes, NEW made a very big mistake in letting one of their future stars go.
REPORTER #4: Mr. Drake....you try and come across a a "fan-favorite", yet you seem to have a very big ego for one.
JEFF "GLACIERICE" DRAKE: Perhaps, but you have to see just where I'm coming from on this one. I was let go by a company that I have been loyal to for _ONE
WHOLE YEAR_, and in return for my services, I got a blatant slap in the face, and a pink slip for all of my hard work...so I have an ego, big deal, I have just one thing on my mind, and that is showing National Extreme Wrestling just where they'll be going without having me in tow for the rest of their existance!
REPORTER #5: So..you feel as though NEW is a sinking ship? JEFF "GLACIERICE" DRAKE: Sinking ship? Nah...more like the Titanic...._A DISASTER WAITING TO HAPPEN!_ REPORTER #6: I've heard that you seem to be disappointed with some of your
"former friends"?
JEFF "GLACIERICE" DRAKE: Yes. Some people whom I considered "friends" have let me know what they really think of me lately, most namely Jim Whitten, Ivan
Ramius, and Brass Knuckles...they have all degraded me just because I have ventured to an organization where my talent will be appreciated for once!
Sorry...but I only have time for one more question. REPORTER #7: Your feelings towards the insults that Head Hunter has been throwing at you ever since he beat you a while ago?
JEFF "GLACIERICE" DRAKE: Well, he never actually _BEAT_ me....considering eh has to hold my trunks to get the win, but I'll give the guy credit, he got the one, two, three...but he'll get his due very soon...THANK YOU AND GOODNIGHT!
{"Back In The Saddle" by Aerosmith begins to blare over the PA system of the conference room, and all of the reporters as well as INWI Commisioner Jerry
DeLouise begin to cheer, as Drake flashes a peace sign and then exits....STAGE
RIGHT!}
-Jeff "The DeGenerate" DX 4 Life "You make the rules, we'll break em'!"
