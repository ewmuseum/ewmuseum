Soviet Machine -[ Empire Wrestling RP ]-

3 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Soviet Machine - Empire Wrestling Tag Team www.nemesis-productions.com/empirewrestling/ December 3rd, 1999 [The Scene: Just outside of the White House in Washington DC, the political center of the lone Superpower left in today's world, the United States of
America. We see the view of the White House we've all seen before, the iron gates separating the bustling street from the lawn of this symbol of
Americanism. Perhaps just the opposite, however, is the commotion now going on here in the open public. We quickly spot the culprits of an anti-American protest, garnering very little support from passerbyers, but deterring the two individuals, none the less. Zooming in, the cameras quickly reveal exactly who it is behind this outcry, it is the Vladimir
Khruschev and the Kiev Krippler, collectively known to the world of professional wrestling as the Soviet Machine...]
Vladimir: Say no to capitalism! Say yes to socialism! [Try as he might, Vladimir seems unable to urge a following, despite the combined efforts of Krippler and him, as they both wave signs declaring old
USSR propaganda, as the two Russians circle around in your basic protest pattern...]
Vladimir: Down with Clinton...eh, look at dis, Krippler, Brock has sent us a public medium to transmit our all-important message! We know, deep down in his black heart, he is a Marxist, just like us!
[The Kiev Krippler nods in agreement, his broad shoulders covered by a long coat, the kind issued by the KGB for harsh Russian winters. They warm perfectly for the climate here in Washington, D.C. this time of year as well...]
Vladimir: As you can see, we are in ze midst of bringing down the evil capitalist regime that has, for hundreds upon hundreds of years, destroyed society, as we know it. We, the Soviet Machine, will not stand by and watch as the multitudes of sheep are led to the slaughter...
[Vladimir abruptly stops, as it seems the cameraman and crew are informing the Russian a few things concerning the topic of the interview...we can't be for sure, not being able to hear the muted voices of the cameracrew, instead, relying on the facial expressions of Vladimir, which now slowly contort to those associated with anger and frustration...]
Vladimir: What!? Talk ze about wrestling, not our great revolution? That is the greatest of insults leveled upon us by a foul American as of yet; it is a good thing that I am able to restrain the Kiev Krippler here, less I let him loose here and leave a pile of broken bodies upon this very sidewalk!!
[Camera swings over to the Kiev Krippler, standing still with no emotion. Vladimir sees this and nudges him in the ribs, causing the massive Russian to take on a more menacing posture, motioning to the camera crews with threatening gestures, among those being a snapping in half with both of his hands...]
Vladimir: The way ze Soviet Machine sees things, it is ze own fault of this country that idiots like the Ex-Cons run rampant on capitalist sponsored airwaves. It is zis same system that peddles the Backstreet Boys to ze brainwashed public, but most important of all, prevents us, the Soviet
Machine from doing the noble thing and rally the people of zis corrupt country against the Great Evil!
[The Kiev Krippler leans over and whispers something to Vladimir, all the while, sunlight gleams off the shiny red and yellow mask that hides the identity of the huge Russian wrestler...]
Vladimir: Krippler here would like me to... [Suddenly, the Kiev Krippler again intercedes...] Krippler: I will do the talking, comrade, if you do not mind, for I must directly address the two vagabonds who call themselves the Ex-Cons!
[Vladimir stands beside the Krippler, looking in awe...] Krippler: Come now, comrade, be not surprised I hold my tongue this long before I speak my mind on these Amerikan scumbags who parade around with our belts, thinking themselves to be the tag team champions. I got news for you, Ex-Cons, you may be recognized as such in this puny country, but in the
Motherland, you are merely chumps starved for the spotlight when measured up against the Soviet Machine. You may think of yourselves as being hardened criminals...but just because you have 10 counts of jaywalking to your rap sheet, it does not impress the Soviet Machine one bit!
Vladimir: (Still surprised at the sudden talkability of his comrade) By the powers of the mighty Kremlin, what has gotten into you? I thought it was I who did the talking around here.
Krippler: You know how it goes in dis business, comrade, if it doesn't work at first, repackage and push it down the fans' throats. In this case, just in time for Christmas for these stupid Amerikans to realize that the Soviet
Machine is indeed, the real deal! That no time, not the Ex-Cons nor the
Latino lunatics, the Vilanos, can stand in the way of us!
Vladimir: Well said, Krippler! But we mustn't let the idiocy of yet another typical Amerikan go by without notice!
Krippler: Then, by all means, enlighten the dim-witted Amerikan public to the truths we, the Soviet Machine, can only know!
Vladimir: What can one truly say of The Goc? We saw this rail of bones show his hide at First Blood and announce that his worthless belt is up for grabs. Now, this nutrient-deprived loudmouthed Amerikan know we could take his little belt for our own, seeing the Soviet Machine have no competition.
Not even The Goc's hired muscle that he so often hides behind in fear could prevent the Kiev Krippler here from manipulating his spinal columns in such a way that would leave The Goc singing an entirely new tune!
[Vladimir and Krippler share a couple moments of chuckling...but that moment of levity is quickly ended when the Krippler captures the attention of
Vladimir, pointing to a black, unmarked car parked across the street, much like the ones you would see a government agent driving around in. Standing outside of the car appears to be it's driver, a gentlemen dressed in a black suit and black shades, smoking a cigarrette while eyeing the Soviet Machine intently...]
Kiev: Comrade Vladimir, we are being watched! Vladimir: Ah...there are forces that conspire to silence the voice of the
Soviet Machine, but never will they succeed for...
WE ARE THE SOVIET MACHINE...AND THEY ARE NOT!!! [And in closing, our enigmatic communist duo gather up their protest material and storm off down the sidewalk, to a destination as of yet unknown...]
[Fade to a promotional ad for the latest release by Nine Inch Nails, "The
Fragile"...]
