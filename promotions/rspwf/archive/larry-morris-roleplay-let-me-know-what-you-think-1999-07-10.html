Larry Morris roleplay...let me know what you think!!

10 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens to a ring in the middle of an empty auditorium. All the lights all of a sudden go off and very quietly "I'm Too Sexy" by Right Said
Fred comes from the loudspeakers. Then, from nowhere, a big spotlight hits the ring as the music gets louder. Standing in the middle of the ring is a very well built man standing alone. He has short blonde hair, and a neatly trimmed goatee. He is wearing blue jeans, and a red and black tye dye tee shirt tucked in. Both his hands are in his pocket as he slowly turns around. He then pulls a hand from his pocket and reaches towards his back and grabs a microphone from his jeans. The camera quickly pans in for a closeup of the man as he stares striaght at the camera.)
Man: Finally, my time has come. It's time to step out of the shadows of the rest of my family, and begin a career of my own. And it looks as though the IWA is the place it's all going to start. The beginning....the end...it's something new for each and every other performer, entertainer, athlete, and wrestler in this business. It's time to step up to the plate, and aim for the stars. It's time....
(The man rips off this shirt and throws it to the ground. He is a very well built athlete...dark well tanned skin, and very big muscles for a man his size.)
Man: The women love me...the men love to hate me. That's how life goes...and that's what I'm bringing into the IWA. "Lucious" Larry Morris is here, and now is the time to make my mark on this earth.
Larry Morris: You see, from day one, I knew I would be in this business. Like my father, like my two older brothers, I knew this was the business for me. I didn't know when, I didn't know why...but I knew. And now here I am...ready to take on anything and everything the promotions attempt to throw at me. I'm ready for it all.
(Larry paces the ring a couple times and then looks out to the empty bleechers.) Larry Morris: I've dremt of some day wrestling in front of crowds of thousands. And now it's my chance to shine. It's my day in the sun. And each and every one of you will realize exactly what I'm talking about soon enough. You know, you always get the talk. Those same ole guys who sit there and run and run there mouths..but yet sit and home and watch the action from there little 10 inch black and white. Let me clear something up right now...so there is no confusion whatsoever. I AM...THE...ACTION! Pure and simple. No sitting watching people at home...I'm gonna be there to issue out the black eyes and the broken noses. And in the process..if it means grabbing a bit o gold here and there...so be it.
Larry Morris: So many men come to get the fame...they come to get the money. Those fools just don't understand. I'm not here for money or fame.
God knows I could give a rats but about what those lousy people out there think of me. If they hate me...so what. If they love me...good for them.
I'm here for myself...and eventually...you'll undertand so much better what
I'm about. It's about glory and honor really. And it doesn't hurt when you're the sexiest man alive...like myself. I'll be around...each and every week...strutting my stuff. Showing the women what I'm made of...and making each man out there extremely jealous. What can I say...it's tough being the best.
Larry Morris: Don't hate me because I'm beautiful. Don't hate me because
I'm the best. Don't hate me because I'm the champion. Just love me like all the rest. There's my motto...each and every one of you morons out there shall live and breath by eat...or it may end up being your demise and abrupt halt to your careers. But one thing is certain...from this point on....the professional wrestling business is gonna change. It's gonna get a lot hotter...if you know what I mean. And you will all LOVE...ME!!!
(Camera fades out as Larry laughs.)
