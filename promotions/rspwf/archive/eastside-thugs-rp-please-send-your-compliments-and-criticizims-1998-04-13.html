Eastside Thugs RP- Please send your compliments and criticizims.

13 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, this is a rp for my stable, The Eastside Thugs. They are all white rappers. Also, they are free agents, looking for a good fed. (The scene opens up with ET standing on the corner of 12th and 12th in 2 Def
Killah's hometown, W. Babylon, New York. It's a great sunny day. 2 Deff Killah is wearing a pair of light blue BOSS Jeans sagging down to his knees, and a white BOSS shirt. Sean G and Da Mack are wearing almost identical outfits of black BOSS jeans and red and white No Rules shirts. Da Mack is wearing dark blue BOSS jeans and a Remember Notorious B.I.G. T-shirt. Da Great Craig T is wearing a pair of Jenco jeans and a 2Pac T-shirt. Goliath is towering in back of all of them, not fitting in exactly. He is wearing a huge pair of BOSS jeans that dont fit him, and a XXL T-shirt with 2 Def Killah's picture on it. They are all holding hockey sticks, and there are hockey nets on the street. It looks like they are starting a neighborhood hockey game.)
"2 Def Killah" Mike Dippi: Hey, whud up peeps? I just figured I'd tell all my adoring fans whats up. I know you all were wondering where I was. Ya see, I was in BBCW, but it folded when I was gonna get my lightweight title shot. Then, I was in HWF, and it folded the night that my man Goliath was going to get a shot at "Maniac" Mike Slater and his US Title. They just wanna hold us down, but you know wut?
Sean G: Can't noboddy hold us down. We'll fly. Ya see, I was trying to get into the rap game, but all the main producers wouldn't listen to me. Puffy, Master
P, Suge Knight, noboddy. Then I got wit 2DK. Now, I just got a call from Dre.
You all know, one of the greatest of all time, Dr. Dre. Too bad I'm hea wit ET. Coulda been good Dre. But see, I aint mad at you all. Cuz, "It's the same cats that wouldn't listen to my demo, now want their ass in my limo. Cuz now I'm a sex symbol. Intercontinental, LA airplay just like Jay Leno. Now all the labels out wanna send a memo to do a remix y'all but I say N-O. Figure once I make it, they'll fake it. All the girls show me love when I'm naked. And then the threw my tape in the trash I laugh, cuz now they got my tape on blast at last.
Da Mack: Same thing hea, "a year ago I couldn't go to a show, was standing on the corner selling dope for dough".
2DK: But see, we had to take some time off. Because we got the playa hatas out there. "Wit all dis money that we can make, why y'all cats wanna playa hate?"
We coulda made the money together, you know who we talking bout DP. You and
Westside Ridahs. You chose against us, instead of with us. Too bad.
Da Mack: But we back, we bad, and we oh so good. But the thing is, we dont got anyplace to fight.
(Goliath takes a slapshot that must have gone at least 90 mph. It hits the crossbar and breaks the net, but it went in.)
Goliath: GOAL!!! Da Great Craig T: Come on big man, now we gotta go get the other net. Ya dont have to hit that fast.
Da Mack: Well, back to this now. Barely anythings for sure, but there is one thing that I do know. Whatever fed we enter, we will dominate. Goliath is still undefeated. So am I and the rest of ET except for Mike. Cuz he's the vet of us, he's 52-9 with four World Heavyweight Titles, two World Tag Team Titles, one US
Title, and one World Lightweight Title.
2DK: Umm, Mack thats 55-8. Da Mack: Oh sorry. 2DK: No problem. See that's not important. The important thing is this gold around my neck.
Easy C: "Yo, I be that young fly fellow, got dero, die for Dinero. Hit everything in the Tri-Borough. And I'm the newest member of the ET team, and
I'm gonna give this nigga Mike mad more cream. Wit hooks galore, leave this city shook for sure. And I'm a take em back wut Biggie took before." You see,
I'm here in ET for one reason. To bring victory. We got gold
(shows the camera his gold necklaces, rings, and his prized 14k gold switchblade with "Easy C" engraved on it)
Easy C: But we want more. "Before it's all over alot of blood gonna be spilled. We aint discriminating even thugs gonna be killed. We early inhibators, going into cream. If one of our niggas gets shot we form as a team. Bullshit if want it go for this cream, the weatherman dont even know the storm imma bring."
2DK: Look, we got the most talented lightweight in the world, the 2DK. We got the Bronx's most infamous balla, Da Mack. We got Easy C representing Brooklyn.
We have Sean G representing the brawlers in Harlem World. One of Amerikaz Most
Wanted, Craig T from Staten Island. And the eight wonder of the world, Goliath.
We unstopable. Craig T: We gonna do dis shit the playa way. Any hatas in our way will get crushed. It's as simple as that. We dont care what fed it is, BBCW, NOW, NEW, or anything else. We can take on anyone you throw at us, even the famed
"Playboy" Ronnie D. You aint nutting but a punk. Brody Thunder? Why do I hear so much bout him, who has he beaten? Make way for the greatest force in wrestling, ET.
2DK: You see, we gonna just play some hockey and wait for the big money offers from the big feds to role in. Cuz "Everything we do be all about the loot, we be kicking up dust in our polo boots. Getting blowed in the dro, getting smoke up my nose. I give the world to apartment but I dont love ho's. I'm a playa baby, and dont you forget it. You need to get wit it, let me hit it to spit it.
In the bed on the floor, hot tub every day.The Playaway, the Playaway." (about ten more people show up to play hockey and ET turn away from the corner as they start picking teams. The camera fades out just as they start playing.)
"The 2 Def Killah" MikeD THUG TILL I DIE!! Amerikaz Most Wanted Baby!! "The first amendment gave us freedom of speech, so what you saying it didn't include me? I like to party and have a good time, there's nothing but pleasuring in our rhymes."
