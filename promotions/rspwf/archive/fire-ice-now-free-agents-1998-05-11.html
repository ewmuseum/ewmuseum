Fire & Ice now Free agents

11 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That is right the best Tag Team around Fire & Ice are free agents. We have just left the IIWF because of management. The management didn't like the way we RPed so we left. So to let you see how we RP here is a sample. And if you think that your fed can handle us, then send us an app.
(The scene opens with Fire & Ice in thier locker room, Mene Gene has just enterted the door. And he asks them for an interview. Here is what happened.)
Gene: Fire & Ice I want to get your comments on the things that Axl and Balls had to say. Fire: Balls, you think that you are a man. And that I will know your name, well the only thing that I call balls are in my pants right now. Yeah only a complete idiot would call themselves Balls, and not have any. Cause you don't.
You see I told you what all champions we were just to let you know that you were facing the best tag team ever. And after this Sunday when we put you two down for the one-two-three, you will know for a fact that we are the best tag team.
Gene: Well those two men have said that they will defeat you, your thoughts on that Ice? Ice: Well it seems to me that we have a little more competition in the IIWA then anywehre else. Since you two bone heads have the balls to take us on, then maybe you guys need your heads examined. Too many of them days of falling through tables and sh*t. You see we don't need chairs, we use our own knowledge of how to beat morons like you. We come to compete each and every day that we wrestle. We have fought and worked hard to become the best tag team around. We have proven time and time again that we back up what we say and this Sunday we will show you two idiots that we do what we say and we do it going 110%.
Gene: Now gentlemen I have just one more question that I want each of you to answer. Balls and Axl say that they will bring chairs, and you two said you would bring Power and finess, but what do es all that mean. Can you withstand chairshots?
Fire: Hell Yeah!!! See I have been through anything goes matches before and I have been beaten with chairs, so I know what it feels like to get nailed with a chair. And if I get hit with one this Sunday, I will just do what I always have and that is keep on going and kicking a$$. And as far as the titles I have won in the past, yeah we lost them when we left the fed. We have left every fed as champions. Most feds close down after we win, but hey we will show you two what real men do in the ring.
Ice: Axl, I am begging you to bring a chair, cause once you hit me with it, I am gonna look at you, laugh and then kick your a$$ even more. So Axl, Balls, you two are just another stepping stone.
(Suddenly the locker room door opens and in walks a man who Gene can not tell who it is....)
Gene: Who is this? Fire: Just another friend. Gene: Well one more question what about the Extremeists? Will you join up with them? Ice: We have talked to them and your looking at the newest members of The
Extremists. So now not only are we the best tag team but we are in the best stable as well. And to let you in on a secret this man you see right here behind me is the man who will stop all the chair shots, to give you a clue he is from Canada. So Axl, Balls not only will you get your a$$ kicked by us, but any of your chair shots will be thrown right back at ya. Boys put your Balls to the walls cause in two days your get your a$$es kicked!!!!
