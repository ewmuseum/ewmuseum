Reading Material - Nemesis RP - Gyeah Wrestling

27 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The Scene: The camera opens up inside the confines of a radio studio room, one of the many located inside One on One Sports headquarters which will provide the backdrop for this latest in Brotherhood interviews. Inside this room, two desks are located in the center of the room, and at one is located the former GW Heavyweight Champion, Nemesis, being seen placing a headset on, as across the way sits the host of the show, Art Zandelli. Throughout the room, sports memorabilia of every type lines the wall, a New York
Yankees pennant here, a Green Bay jersey there, and numerous posters. Autographed baseball bats and footballs hang on the wall, while a few hockeysticks are propped up in the corner of the room. A person walks into the room, looking of the producer type and flashes a signal to Mr. Zandelli, as a red bulb outside of the studio flashes, and a catchy theme song signals the start of the interview segment...]
Zandelli: This is the Art Zandelli show, I'm your host for the evening, and we've got a great show lined up for you tonight, folks. The ArtMan's got one of the great superstars of wrestling here tonight, as well as expert opinion in all the latest going on in the world of sports. Callers feel free to express your opinion, whether it be about the seemingly invincible
New York Yankees, or the state of baseball that leaves the high payroll teams in contention and the small market teams that are left on the outside looking in.
[A sound of a baseball striking a baseball bat is heard...] Zandelli: Also, we can talk about a little NBA basketball, as the playoff races are heating up as we start to wind down to the end of the NBA regular season. Who's in, and who's staying home? Share yoru thoughts, as well any hockey fans out there, call me and tell me what you think about the Detroit
Redwings, can they do it again?
[A hockey goal siren now blasts away, hey, this is one hell of an interactive radio show...]
Zandelli: But now, we've a special guest here tonight on the Art Zandelli show, and you wrestling fans are gonna love the ArtMan for this. One of
Gyeah's wrestling's finest, the man is a two time heavyweight champion of the sport, and one of the most well known faces, or masks anyways, recognized by fans and kids alike. He's all Brotherhood, and he's here tonight on the Art Zandelli show, let me introduce to you tonight, NEMESIS!
[A recorded standing ovation from some unknown sporting event now plays, as the view switches from the host to the guest of the show...Nemesis. Nemesis wears his signature black mask, but his wardrobe for tonight consists of a
Chicago Cubs jersey as well as black pants. His long black hair is tied back, while he leans upon the desk before him, and we can see a glass of water provided to him to the left of him. Nemesis makes a few final adjustments to the headset before introducing himself to the radio audience...]
Nemesis: How's it going, Zandelli? I would say it's great to be here and all the usual I bet you get from your special guests, but then I would be lying...
[Nemesis speaks those words with a grin, with Zandelli feeling obligated to respond....] Zandelli: Hey hey! I can see why all the fans hate you. Plus, just how many rules have you broken in your career?
Nemesis: That's not relevant, what is, is the number of times I've walked out of the ring the victor. As for the fans, all they are good for is paying for my salary that befits a superstar of my status. But that's all they're good for.
Zandelli: No you didn't! How can you say that and keep a straight face, speaking of which, what's up with that mask? I mean, c'mon, did you take a bad spill off a motorcycle for cryin' out loud or what?
Nemesis: Now, Art, let's be serious. The mask, it's a symbol of the power and prestige the Brotherhood and myself now represent in wrestling. I mean, everywhere you go, what's the thing you hear the so called wrestling fans talking about? It's what the Brotherhood did the other night on TNT, or whatnot. And it's nothing to see half of the bloated morons in the grandstands wearing Brotherhood merchandise. I guess you could say, we're the straw that stirs the drink.
Zandelli: Nemesis, my man, you're killing me. My initial thought of you was that you were some sort of one of these brooding, mysterious types that you always see spouting off the lip on these wrestling shows. But I gotta say, you've got a sense of humor I like.
Nemesis: Art, when you're in the business with some the colleagues I, unfortunately, must have to work with, you're kind of forced to adapt a humorous appraisal of what's going on around you.
Zandelli: Funny as it may seem to you, Nemesis, but I go through the same deal here on the show. I mean, look at the mongrels I surround myself with.
Nemesis: Art, you don't even know the half of it. *chuckles* Zandelli: Now, Nemesis, you mention somewhat in vague terms something about your peers in the GW, let me run off a list of some names here and see what you've got to say about these guys. This should be real interesting...
Nemesis: I bet. Zandelli: First, let's start at the top of the organization, what's your thoughts on Jon Forte, the man largely responsible for building the GW into the multi-billion dollar business that it is today. And recently, he's been revealed as in league with you guys in the Brotherhood, what's up with that?
Nemesis: Like you said, Jon Forte is the man responsible for the GW Empire, with my help, as well. Jon did a fantastic job leading people to believe that he had a vendatta against myself and the Brotherhood, and you can always count on a feud with the president to reel in the ratings. With myself as the marquee of the league, it was a cinch to acquire the necessary financial backing to...get the show on the road, so to speak.
Zandelli: I was gonna mention something about that, in the beginning, it was Forte against you guys, the guy should get an Oscar...
Nemesis: Oh, you said it, not me. And the thing between him and the Quebeccan Nightmare himself, Aron Rave, it was a masterpiece of a plot worked to the perfection that you come to associate with the good name of the Brotherhood. And if you ask Jaamal Jammer, he'll tell you that Forte has one hell of a right hook...
Zandelli: I heard all about that, my God, I couldn't believe it. I don't know how I could live with myself if I was knocked out cold in front of thousands and thousands of people...by my boss! What's this world coming to these days, sheesh. I mean, if my boss took a swing at me, the hell with the job, we're throwing down, for crying out loud. But more on the subject, it looks like you'll have to watch Forte fight Jammer now...
Nemesis: Nothing to it, the President's is in the company of some of the most skilled performers to ever step foot in the ring.
Zandelli: So what are you trying to say, you're giving pointers on your boss on how to wrestle?
Nemesis: In a word...Yes. But seeing as how he'll be going up against
Jaamal Jammer, he won't be needing that much help.
Zandelli: Take it easy on Jammer, will ya? [The two men laugh, Nemesis then taking a sip of water...] Zandelli: Now, Nemesis, what's your take on Jaamal Jammer. I know you two have been at it a couple times...
Nemesis: And with myself coming out with the advantage each time, I might add. Jammer, you've got to feel sorry for the guy. He's quick to acknowledge the fact that he was born a piece of trash. But as far as him compared to the level of myself, in the ring? He's not even close. As I was saying, the poor chap got knocked out by the Boss, and suffered another defeat to the Brotherhood, all in one night. But setbacks is something
Jammer is used to, especially when dealing with the Brotherhood, and I just hope he's prepared for another one at the hands of Jon Forte.
Zandelli: So you're going out on a limb and saying Jon Forte will beat
Jammer? I've got my doubts, it's going to take more than a crash course in wrestling to get Forte even up to half the speed of a professional wrestler like Jaamal Jammer.
Nemesis: What you underestimate, Art, is the killer instinct, and if there's one thing Forte has, it's the willingness to put the competition out of commission. I mean, just count all the leagues who've tried to compete with the GW unsuccessfully, and you'll get an idea of what the situation really is.
Zandelli: Alright...on to the next subject, let's talk a little about the group of guys right now you don't have much of a fondness for...let's talk about the Kindred.
[Nemesis sneers in contempt at the mere mention of the name, Kindred...] Nemesis: What about them? Zandelli: I believe it was Above The Law that saw Viper come out on top of the GW, taking the World Title from your hands. You can't be a happy man when I mention the man's name. What's your thought on the champion as well as the rest of the Kindred group that has a cult like following.
Nemesis: First things first, concerning Viper and Above The Law, the man experienced a phenomenal display of luck in walking away from our match with the Brotherhood World Strap. The fact that he went to such extremes to conceal his identity, it just goes to show how much of a coward this man really is. I mean, it's no small secret that if we were ever to square off in the ring again, I would make him look like the complete and utter joke that he is. The man is a fluke, and his little band of flunkies will soon suffer the ill effects of a showdown with the Brotherhood.
Zandelli: That's right, I'm reading this paper here, it has the card for
April Anarchy and here's a match between the Kindred and the Brotherhood, and it looks like a dandy!
Nemesis: Well, after we're done with the Kindred, you'll say they look anything but pretty after the Brotherhood has systematically demolished them in the way only the Brotherhood can.
Zandellie: Damien King will be there... Nemesis: Who gives a damn about Damien King? Zandelli: There's plenty of fans that do... Nemesis: To hell with the fans, Damien King knows the deal. The deal being that he knows I can and will kick his ass, along with the rest of his
Kindred cohorts. It's guys like the Kindred that make me sick. They get together, throw a little party, announce they are a team and immediately think they are able to lock horns with the Brotherhood? The Brotherhood, we've made more than a name for ourselves, it's safe to say. We've put guys on the shelf, and ended careers. But when a ragtag team of pukes such as the Kindred come together and immediately think they're nipping on the heels of greatness in the Brotherhood? Guys, let's be real about this. We are the Brotherhood, you are the Kindred. WE kick your ass, you deal with it.
Plain and simple. Zandelli: C'mon, you're not even giving these guys an ounce of respect. Nemesis: The only thing I'm going to give these guys is a fist to the jaw, at the very least. Damien King, he's got his nose in every other person's business in the GW, these days. His punk friend, Morphine, he's worse then
King. If you got some gold on you, you can be assured you'll see Morphine's shadow nearby. And is it me, or has the former United States Champion taken a few hundred steps backward, now reducing himself to fighting for the...*gasp* Television Title? What was the Kindred thinking of when they let this chump in?
Zandelli: Not everyone can wear the World Strap like you, Nemesis. [Nemesis grins a smile, as if to say..."yeah, i know..."] Nemesis: You're right about that, Art. We haven't seen the last of each other's company, you can be sure of that.
Zandelli: Now, Nemesis, let's go to the phones and see what the people have to say, you ok with that?
Nemesis: ....why not. [A brief moment of static, as a caller is introduced on the show...] Caller1: Hello? Zandelli: Caller, you're on the air... Caller1: Ah, yes, I just have a question for Nemesis, don't you feel any amount of guilt for what you did to Billy Larson all those months back?
Zandelli: Billy Larson? Get this guy off the air, he must be some wacknut from South Carolina. Next caller...
Nemesis: Well, Art, in response to this moron's question, the answer is No, and actually, every time I think about it, it brings a smile to my lips.
Zandelli: Yeah, I'll bet. Caller, your on the air with Art Zandelli and special guest, Nemesis!
Caller2: Yeah, I just want to ask Nemesis where in the hell did he dig up this Leviticus? Zandelli: Pretty good question, Nemesis, just WHAT is the deal with Leviticus? Nevermind who is this guy, the guy's downright strange. Nemesis: Give Levi a break, he's coming into his own as a wrestler, thanks in large part to me. Given that the match at April Anarchy will be his first real big match in the GW, I think Leviticus will do just nicely.
Zandelli: Well, that's nice to know, Nemesis, but you still haven't answered the question, where did you find this guy?
Nemesis: It's not important where I found Levi, what is, is that Leviticus is prepared to step up to the levels of competition that the Brotherhood faces each and every night. I'm most confident that he is.
Zandelli: Geez, and I thought Bill Clinton gave the runaround on answering questions. Nemesis: Bill Clinton doesn't drop people on their heads for a living. Zandelli: Good point. Let's go to our next caller... [The next caller comes on the air, and immediately, by the drawl in his voice, you can tell this caller calls the south his home...]
Caller3: Yeah, I was a wanderin', Mr. Nemisis...have ya recovahed from da beatin' Psycho Soup gave ya a few days back??
[Nemesis simmers upon the border of losing his temper while Art Zandelli orders the caller cut off...Nemesis shakes his head in disgust, one would expect him to angrily announce this caller as a Psycho Soup mark...]
Zandelli: Something about you, Nemesis, that just brings out the best in people it seems. I haven't had this many losers call in since Dennis Rodman was on the show. We'll hold off on callers, in favor of some more questions from myself. What do you think of other sports compared to that of the one you perform in?
Nemesis: They sure have it a lot easier than my opponents, you could say, *laughs*. But as far as other sports go, I don't think we get the same respect as those group of losers in baseball, say for instance. They get paid 10 million to smack a baseball, we, as wrestlers, we just smack each other up.
Zandelli: Good way of looking at it. Being the franchise of the GW, how do you view yourself in the sport? Some people have already labeled you a legend, isn't that a bit premature?
Nemesis: Of course not, we are talking about me, afterall. Hockey has
Wayne Gretzky, basketball had Michael Jordan, and professional wrestling has it's Nemesis.
Zandelli: I got one more question before you leave, Nemesis. Which person do you most respect in sports other than wrestling? Which person do you identify, can you say?
Nemesis: Well, I'd have to go with Albert Belle with that one. You have to like this guy, he tells the fans what's on his minds, and he's never afraid to show his biceps to the opposing team. He's got style.
Zandelli: Well, we're coming up on the break here, and I'd just like to thank you for appearing tonight on the Art Zandelli show, it was great having you here.
Nemesis: The pleasure was mine. Zandelli: We wish you good luck. Coming up on the next segment...Roger
Clemens gets shelled by the Orioles, plus the Great One retires. We'll be taking callers, so get on the phonelines while they're still open. The Art
Zandelli show will return after these messages...
[Nemesis removes his headset, while shaking hands with Art Zandelli. Taking a final sip of water, Nemesis then departs for the door, as the scene begins a slow fade to blaque...]
