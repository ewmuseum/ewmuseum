Top Turnbuckle Wrestling (TTW) Website: www.topturnbuckle.com Owner: [name withheld] Description: Brought to you by the people responsible for Vegas Championship Wrestling (late 90s) and High Impact Wrestling (2000-01), Top Turnbuckle Wrestling is an attempt to resurrect the golden era, glory years of e-wrestling. Angles and character development are the most important aspects of the fed. Two championships to compete for - TTW World Championship, where all matches are best 2 out of 3 falls, and the TTW Impact Championship, one fall to the finish. A large writing staff insures that cards are delivered on time. Website is constantly updated. Fed magazine hosts articles, news, rumors, interviews, and opinion pieces by TTW staff and wrestlers. Keynote Members: 'Golden Boy' Troy Armstrong, Fu Jow, Lord Monstar, Torrance DeLarge, Konrad, 'The Hand of God' Tyson Priest, Federation Champion: John Messier Flash Show Descripition: RPs are done on our message board, accessible through our website. One RP a week is customary. In addition to the RP board, there is also an annoucements board, feedback board and an out of character discussion board. Regular Show Description: TTW Evolution airs every Monday night. 4 - 5 matches per week, plus in-ring and backstage interviews. PPV Description: PPVs are typically held once every three months.
