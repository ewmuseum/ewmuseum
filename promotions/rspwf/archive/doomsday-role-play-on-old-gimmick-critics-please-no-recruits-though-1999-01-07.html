[Doomsday Role-Play on Old Gimmick]Critics Please, No recruits Though!

7 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fades into a nearby location. An avid reporter is seen parading outside the room of "The Torturer" Doomsday. He seems to be pretty annoyed about something. The locker room door then swings wide open and then standing there wearing a red and black attire with black wrestling boots with the IIWA
Extreme Championship seen on his shoulder. The reporter then goes to ask a question, but is stopped by a harsh voice of Doomsday.]
DD: So I make my return to the MLW, get screwed out of the TRUE North Lake
Championship and now get screwed by not even getting a chance, what is going on. All this politics, Chris Herbert, I hope you have a Good explanation for this, for you don’t want to make me mad, especially not in the state I am in.
And what is it with you avid reporters can’t you learn to leave me alone. Anyway you’re here now, so you might as well here what I have to say and listen real closely for I shall say this only _ONCE_
[The Reporter then begins to jot something down in his notebook, Doomsday then continues.] DD: Did I say you could write anything down? I haven’t even said anything yet. That’s all I need an incompetent fool like you. Well don’t test my patience for it can only be tried for so much and all the things that have happened so far in the MLW, it has be tested far enough. Now let’s get down to business. You see I got so tired before, I just up and left, yet then I had the call to return and I did so, but what for, well that’s exactly what I am getting at. I spoke to Eddie Gibson, he said they needed good talent once again and thus I guess he persuaded me to rejoin, but so far nothing has happened apart from me being screwed out of Steve Lake’s Championship.
Steve Lake, you think you are real good, well I got the victory over you, that proves I should be the #1 contender and not some strange Freakazoid who never even pinned my shoulders to the canvass. Hashmoto Asui, you know something, go ahead, beat Steve Lake, that is if I let you, I may just come out there and cause a riot, yet again I might just sit in the back and watch the match from the monitors backstage or I could even show up and sit down at ringside and watch it, but you can bet your bottom dollar that I shall be there, maybe you will see me, maybe not, but whatever happens, you want
Extreme, you want Blood, well step into the ring with the Extreme Champ of the MLW and then we shall find out what the meaning "Extreme" is all about.
[Doomsday then looks at the IIWA Extreme Championship, he then continues.] DD: You see this belt here, when the IIWA closed down. It was left to me to carry, I took the belt with pride, just like I shall take the MLW North Lake
Championship with pride, Regardless of who the Champion is, soon that
Champion shall meet their Doomsday as the Extreme Nature of "The Torturer" Doomsday, a great legend from the IIWA makes his long awaited return to claim and to take by any means necessary the MLW North Lake Championship. You see
Hashmoto Asui, if you ever saw me in the days of the extreme world, then you would realise just what lengths I would go to. Just go ask Del Juico Final or
"Great American Nightmare" Mike Capri, just what the meaning of "Torture" is all about. That is if you can find them.
DJF, he ran before he could leap. I showed him the way out of the MLW and now cry Baby, for the MLW’s Most Extreme Force is back and raring to go. Chris
King, you call yourself Extreme, well find out the meaning of the Word next week. Are you up for the challenge or is Chis Herbert still your keeper. You claim to be the "King of Extreme", well it seems now as if there’s a new Guy in town and one which the MLW shall never forget. Just like I did to the IIWA and the UCW, I shall take you under my wing, spit you out and make you know why YOU ARE TO BE TORTURED!
[Doomsday then turns towards the reporter and then he says these final words.] DD: Mr. Reporter, you can get out of here now. The lesson for today is over, but I shall leave you with the final words of thought. There used to be a time when all said and done, I did what I was there to do, I broke people with just one click and now it seems as if the "Torturer" Doomsday is about to make a close return to battle. Hashmoto Asui, Steve Lake, one of you shall be leaving town with the MLW North Lake Championship, _MY_ MLW North Lake
Championship and if you think this was it, well think twice, for the road to the Gold starts here. The Twist in the tale gets underway and battles shall take place where blood shall boil, blood shall be shed and most of all as he comes riding back into town, thick twice, for the Torture is about to begin.
Now get the hell out of here or you may just know what the dreaded Corkscrew is all about! [The Reporter then leaves the area as the cameras fade out, just as Doomsday puts his boot into it, signalling the end of the scene.]
