[Critique]First RP ever for me-Miles Flannigan

29 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, this is is my first EVER roleplay, I have run feds but never ever even thought of writing one, I wrote this up to see how good it is. I did it pretty quick so I could watch the beginning of RAW but it is still long and pretty good I think. Anyway the character is Miles Flannigan and he is a former gator wrestler from Australia that moved to Canada to wrestle in EFW because of the money.
HERE IS THE ROLEPLAY: [Scene opens on a Range Rover pulling into a free parking space at a large
Independent Grocer. 3 people are in the vehicle, 2 men in their 30's enthralled in a deep conversation and a young boy happily playing with a GI
Joe. The men don't exit the car right away, they carry on their conversation.]
Man 1: For the last frickin time mate, I am not wrestling with those chumps. They belong in the dunny *under breath* along with you, ya bloke. I feel like a pick-pocket at a nudist camp (Australian slang for: feeling out of place)
Man 2: I heard that! Man 1: (In an English tone) Oh, your a genius, your a bat baby, you've got good hearing! Damn I love that new Austin Powers movie, it's smashing baby yeah!
Man 2: AAARGHH! You fool! Can you be serious for once? How many times do I have to tell you; YOU HAVE TO WRESTLE SOMEBODY! You can't just sit around and wrestle with gators anymore you signed a contract! And Rock Solid on
Canada Day is a GREAT opportunity, especially with those jobbers who challenged you!
Man 1: Ok, ok, your right I mean I am going to have to wrestle sometime beside the road eh mate? BWAAH! Yeah right, I'm still not convinced that I should wrestle these guys, besides I have to get my groceries for my wife so she can cook us some food before we have to wear a belt with our pants! Plus its that time of the month if you know what I mean. I want to lie doggo
(Australian slang for: to remain hidden to avoid work.) in this business.
Young boy:(in a whiny voice) Daddy can't we just go in the store so I can get my popsicle?!
Man 1:[He cracks a nice smile and chuckles] Right kid lets go! [The man driving the car gets out and bumps his head on the roof of the car, he shouts profanities out loud while grabbing his head as an elderly woman waddles by. Its blistering hot out and he wipes some sweat off of his forehead with his shirt sleeve. He opens to door for the young boy and puts him on his shoulders, softly. He is wearing khaki cargo pants and a white t-shirt with long brown overcoat and a leather outback hat. The other man gets out of the car carefully as to not hit his head. They all begin to walk towards the store, the first man walks proudly with his head high. The second man walks swift and unsure of his steps trying to keep up with the other man.]
Man 2: See that's what I'm talking about! You can't just go about screaming profanities all over the place, SPECIFICALLY infront of old women. Oh and by the way its not beside the road its DOWN the road. You have to create a good image for yourself, a fan favorite, a good guy, a HERO! That's were the big bucks are.
Man 1: [Mimicking man 2] And the idiots like you right? Can someone tell me why I hired a stupid manager like you, Rich Randall?
Man 2: [Mimicking man 1] And can someone tell me why I am working for a jerk like you, Miles Flannigan??
Miles: Because I am paying you more money then you got while you were working at McDonalds, hehe!
[They reach the entrance and Miles sets his son down to walk beside him, they continue walking. Rich stops, lights up a cigarette and hurries to catch up.]
Rich: Do you always have to leave me behind like that, really? Ok, lets talk business here; you want a title shot right?
Miles: Correct. Rich: A SuperFight title shot? Miles: Correct again, oh boy that's two in a row you're on a roll, continue. [As they stop to get a cart a the manager approaches Rich and asks him to put his cigarette out.]
Rich: Stupid b@!ch. Well smart one, nobody even has that title yet! I have the perfect plan you steal it! Take it from the commissioner like he can do anything about it!
Miles: Can somebody explain to me why I hired you as a manager? Oh man, major déja vu! If you can come up with a good way to do it I'll think about it. I think I have to start lifting waits before I wrestle though man, my muscles are tiny!
[While strolling down the vegetable isle Miles picks up a melon and it breaks in his hand. Melon guts splatters everywhere and a teenage worker runs up to see what is happening.]
Rich: Bullshit man! Do you see what you just did, you busted that melon with your bare hands. It takes superhuman strength to do something like that!
Miles: It was bloody nothing good bloke! Teenage worker: (voice cracking) Hi, Mr. Sir. Ummm....what happened here? Miles: I broke that melon there with my.. Rich: Can't you see he broke the friggin melon with his bare hands you numbskull. He's a star! Clean this mess up and get his boy a popsicle! I have big plans for you Miles!
Son: Woohoo! Miles: (Sarcasm) Oh great! I guess we are going to work on my (sarcastic tone) MAT skills right?
Rich: Exactly my man! You are also a genius, oh boy are we going to be RIIIIIIIIIICH! [Scene fades out with the teenager cleaning up the melon, Miles putting his son back on his shoulder, his son licking contently on his popsicle, and
Rich with a mischievous grin on his face scheming a plan to get the
SuperFight title in his head.]
