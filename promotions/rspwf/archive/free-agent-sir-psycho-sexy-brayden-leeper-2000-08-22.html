I've been away from e-wrestling for about six months now, and I think It's time to get back into it. This is a new character I just came up with, thus, no sample RP from him. Just a few preferences about the fed I'd like to get into.
1 - No "Reply To All" Feds. I like the BCC fashion much better. 2 - No Real Wrestlers allowed in the fed. 3 - No "Hardcore Only" Feds. I'd like a fed that has Classic Style Matches with
Countout and DQ. Gimmick Matches should be saved to End Feuds.

He makes sexual jokes pushing the envelope, but never completely breaking the rules.
He's a pretty boy, and he knows it. His cocky/arrogant attitude is never comprimised for anyone in any situation, unless he seriously knows his ass is about to be handed to him.
•Basic Appearance Description (I want his Physical apperance, detailed. Any scars, tatoos, etc): Brayden is caucasion, but he is tanned well. He keeps his hair cut short. His hair is combed forward and up. He has BL tatooed on his left bicep.
•Attire (What does he wear to the ring and inside. Be VERY descriptive. I mean elbowpads, kneepads, wrist tape, EVERYTHING! I want any designs he has on any part of his clothing words written, etc): Brayden wears full length white tights. On the tights legs are the same BL, written in blue, that he has tatooed on his arm. On the rear of the tights is "SPS" in blue and gold.
