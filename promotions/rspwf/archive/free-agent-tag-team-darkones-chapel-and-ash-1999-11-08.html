Free Agent Tag Team: Darkones (Chapel and Ash)

8 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The lights go out in the Arena. Smog begins to fill the floor and myst falls from the raptors. A flash of light strikes and quickly lightens up the arena for a brief second. "Cakes and Sodomy" begins to play. A huge strobe light is turned on and is flashing on and off at the entrance of the Arena. You see a dark figure standing on the Entrance ramp. He raises his hands in some type of symbol. You can't hear what he is yelling but you know he is. He begins to walk down the aisle. He looks left and right and then enters the ring. The strobe light stops flashing and the lights come back on. You can see a tall dark man.
In a sleeveless black trenchcoat. Black pants. Black boots with silver blades. One on the left side and one on the right side. A silver cross necklace and
Black Gloves. He has white face paint covering his forehead eyes and nose. He has black hair in the shape of a flat top. He raises his hands one more time in the symbol and this time you can hear him yell Sanctuary!!
::Chapel:: EFW. I make my presence again here. I hope you have enjoyed my stay. I have given everyone entertainment from time and time again. ive gone through so many damn matches its hard to count. Ive lose some ive won some. But my highest achievement is being 4 time Tag Team Champ and keeping it the longest rein in the best damn fed ive been in. but my life has not been fulfilled. Ive gave everyone 110% of my abilities. And there is just one thing i have never gotten and that is the World heavyweight Title. Ive gone through thick and thin. Ive had just about every title. And i ask my self every day. When will i get my world title shot. In my wrestling days ive seen new comers get a title shot. Just to get ratings cause the prez thinks that if a jobber wins a title match people will start watching more and cheering more. Too me its not that.
If you want the most out of wrestling get the experienced. The ones who put them selves in danger of losing there life's or pride. But you know something i guess i can say that ive given everything ive got. The only thing that i have for me now is the tag team championship. And right now i would like to introduce one of my best friends and tag team partner Chris Steele!!!!!!!!!!
King nothing plays over the Pa system. Red and yellow lights stream through the crowd. All of a sudden a bomb explodes and Chris steele walks out rapped in his barb wire. He is wearing a cut up shirt that Says death wish on the front wrapped in barbwire and blood running down the words. On the back it has the
Name Chris Steele Written across. Chris Steele looks at the crowd and begins walking down the aisle. He sees chapel and then walks up the steele steps to the ring. CHris steele goes to the corner post and climbs up it and raises his hands. The crowd cheers and boos. Chris Steele does that to the other ring posts and then meets chapel in the Center of the ring. Chapel hands Chris steele the mic and Chris Steele begins to Speak.
:::Chris Steele::: EFW, Chris Steele is back, He is here making his present felt, Im not gonna be someone you can just walk all over, Me and Chapel we have held the Tag Team Titles 3 times here and Soon it will be 4 times[Ash's start to fall down from the top of the arena, They land on Chris Steele and Chapel and start to fill the ring]Now what the fuck is that, Ash is falling from the
Arena well, EFW , The Darkones have been on Vacation a little bit but The
Darkones are back an there as hot as they always have been.
:::Chapel::: Chris Steele. Who knows what these ashes are for. Only god knows. And you are right We will be the greates Tag Team Champs in all of history. Weve gone through many things in the past. We have fought each other where both times we have had a draw or double dq. But the point is When i make history and hold these Tag Titles for a long time it won't be with you. Iam so sick and tired of acting that you make all the wins. I am the one who does all the damn work. You might of done something. i just dont know what. We come out here and have our matches and i beat the bloody hell out of them and then you take the
Credit cuase i let you. and you know that. And you know that i have carried the team for ever.
::::Steele::::What the fuck are you talking about. Ive carrie most of the team and you have set back in the corner doing shit. We had a match against outlaw and HbGay. You were beat up in the corner while i was beating the living hell out of them. Then i got the win. So you shouldnt even be talking.
::::Chapel:::: Steele. you know very well about that match and you are living off a big factor. Ya i was in the corner but taking a break and ya you beat them up will bad and got the pin. But before that happend you know that i gave both of them the Sanctuary on the table. You know something Dont even talk. Im sick of your shit and im sick of your whole crap. I dont even care right now
Chapel knocks Chris Steele in the back of the head and chris stumbles through the ropes and falls to the outside, A fan wearing a Black shirt that says "Ash"
on the front and "Ashes to Ashes and Dust to Dust" on the back With blue short jeans with out hems hops over the railing, He picks up Chris Steele and delivers a Wicked DDT. Ash throws Steele in the ring, Chapel grabs Steele and throws him agianst the ropes Ash bounces off the ropes and Drop Kicks his face.
Chapel picks up Steele and does a Sanctuary. They lay him out on his back, Ash stands over him.
:::Ash::: Chris Steele it looks like your Carear is coming to a complete stop, I Ash am taking the place that you once roled in, Im taking the place in a Tag Team you was once apart of, Together Chapel and Ash the Darkones, The New
Darkones are gonna take the Tag Team Titles and were not gonna lose them like you did, Were not gonna desgrace them like you once did. I will not take the
Glammer and Credit like you once did, I will renounce with pain, I will inflicted it like you couldnt, I will do what Chapel is doing I will even the odds I will bring Respect to each side of the team.
Chapel poors gasoline on steele, Ash ligts a match and steps away, He drops the match on Steele.
:::Ash::: Chris Steele as you sit there and you Rott and You burn, Listen to what I have to say somthing right now, The last words you will ever hear,
Chris Steele,.. Ashes to Ashes and Dust to Dust!
:::Chapel::: CHrise Steele. Your soul is nothing more on this wrethced earth. In a way i helped you out. If you dont know what it is. I geuss you never will know. The question is right now. Did you go to heaven or hell? I know the answer and the answer is niether. Your soul has embraced the earth's life stream and is very far away from your burnt and depicted body. Your soul roams around in disgrace. Your soul roams around not knowing what it did to deserve such monstrosity. Steele, I hope your thanking me wherver you are. I did you a big favor. You were on this wretched earth and now your gone on to a bigger and better place. But right nowi need to talk about other things. Chris Steele is
the least of my worries. As you can see The darkones are taking on a new turn of events. My new partner Ash. we will take the Wrestling league by storm and no one will be able to stop us. Are souls are unpenitratable. We absorb pain and we have no mercy!!!

Shoots and Ladders by KoRn plays and The Darkones stare at eachother, Chapel slides out the on the right side and Ash one the left, The meet togther at the ramp, The stare each other as they walk up it then when they reach the top they turn around and raise there hands. The camre fades to black
