[Tag Team RP] A lil' feedback please

12 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, I'm trying to get back into the game now, and I want to handle this tag team of two Japanese brothers. I've never handled a tag team before, nor do I know anything at all about Japanese wrestling or the language.. but, eh, what the hell.
I'm just interested in what others think of my writing, that's all. So, I'd enjoy some feedback.. anything really... So here's my rp... thanks beforehand.. and sorry if the format is screwed up. I'm bad with that type of stuff
*Will === [Small white letters appear at the bottom righthand portion of the screen.] 06.12.01, 3:00 PM GMT Ashikaga, Japan Tashahiro's Gym [The sun creeps through the bare windows of Sensei Tashahiro's gym. The room is bare apart from a wrestling ring centering it, canvas yellowed with age, and a desk off to the side of the room with a small black and white television sitting atop it.]
[Leaning back restlessly in a chair crafted of intertwining bamboo is the Sensei himself, golden sun shimmering off his tonsured head. His eyes are plastered to the television which is playing Japanese soap operas.]
[Suddenly, his attention is grasped by a creak in the wooden paneling of the floor. He wheels his head around slowly to see who or what stirred to floor to a whine.]
[At the old gym's entrance stands Kiyoi Danshi, shrouded in shadows, almost just a silhouette, the infamous rising sun palpitating on his back. He dons merely a pair of black sweatpants, a bare chest, and bare feet.]
KD : Konnichiha, Sensei... ST : Your brother is late for shinen again. Where is he? KD : I don't know, Sensei. I haven't seen him. ST : This is the third day in a row he's been late. Find out where he is, Rei. I have little patience today. KD : Yes, Sensei. [Sensei Tashahiro directs Kiyoi with is knurly finger to the phone which rests on the wall behind him. Kiyoi nods obediently, turns around and picks up the phone. He dials several numbers and waits.]
[..........] KD : Moshimoshi, Mother... is Sihung there? [..........] [Suddenly, Kiyoi steps back from the phone, wincing and clearing his ear with his index finger. Barely audible is the irate squeal of Kiyoi's mother through the phone.]
KD : Please, Mother... don't scream. [..........] KD : What? That's impossible... where is it you say he is? [Cut.] SP : PAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAATTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTTIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIII! [We are suddenly thrown into a completely different scene. It's a mile high airplane. And smack dab in the middle of the aisle is Suupaasaizn
Ponpiki
himself, a wild look in his eyes and his tongue hanging out of his mouth, swinging about. Surrounding him are women, girls, children, businessmen who have loosened their ties and untucked their shirts... all sorts of people.]
[In each hand, Ponpiki has a miniature bottle of liquor from the air bar. The crowd (CRWD) around him is chanting.]
CRWD : IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! IKKI! SP : I'M A PAAAATTTTTIIIIIII ANNNNIIIIIMAAAAAAAAAL! AAAAAAHHHHHHHHH! [And in the midst of the chant, Ponpiki downs the bottle in his right hand, and then moves onto the bottle in his left hand, guzzling it down, his adam's apple bobbing up and down like a buoy in the middle of the ocean.]
[After finishing off the last few drops, Ponpiki throws the bottles down as the crowd roars in approval.]
CRWD : PON! PI! KI! PON! PI! KI! PON! PI! KI! [Cut.] [We resume footage back in Sensei Tashahiro's gym. Kiyoi is just getting off the phone, face flush, and eyes unable to blink. Tashahiro sees the distress and worry in his student's eyes, and inquires about it.]
ST : Well, Rei-son, where is he? KD : He's,,, he's... ST : Well? KD : Mother said he just decided to pack up, and go to... to... [eyebrows furrow quizzically] ...the United Kingdom? ST : The... United Kingdom? Why would he go to the United Kingdom? [Cut.] [We're on the plane again. Ponpiki is now sitting in his assigned seat, in between two women, one with a wet tee shirt, and another dry, but also scantily clad. Ponpiki smiles at them. They smile back. Their teeth are horribly disfigured. They must be Brits.]
SP : So... do you two foxy ladies like Mickey Mouse? [Smiles.] It's been a life-long dream of mine to meet him. We've never had the money to go before though... now that I've been wrestling and have some extra cash,
I decided to take a little trip myself, you know? [Chuckles.]
If my Sensei found out that I'm missing our training sessions to meet
Mickey,
he'd kill me! [Cut.] [Back in the gym.] ST : I'll kill him! BAKAYAROU! [Cut.] [Back in the plane, Ponpiki continues to look the ladies up and down through his glazed eyes. He continues to smile at them. Finally, he places his hands on their knees.]
SP : So, you chicks like Mickey too? Or you're more down with that chicken Minnie? GRL1 : Um, I guess so... SP : Oh, so you two are going to the United Kingdom just for the rides? GRL2 : No, we live there? SP : Wow! I'd kill to live in the States... especially in the United Kingdom. It'd be super if I could live in America... I'd watch the Yankees, eat pizza, get fat, and mack to all the fine honeys... GRL1 : The States? America? And what rides were you talking about? SP : Yeah, the States... and you know, the Tea Cups, Space Mountain... I read all about them on the internet. [The two girls suddenly burst into peals of shrill laughter. Ponpiko begins to laugh along, despite not having the slightest idea as to why.]
SP : Hehe... what? GRL2 : You're not thinking of the United Kingdom, sweetie. You're thinking of the MAGIC kingdom...
SP : The Magic Kingdom? GRL2 : Yup. [Ponpiko ponders over this bit of information he's been given.] SP : KUSO! FAKKU! What have I gotten myself into! [Cut.] KD : What has he gotten himself into now! [In the gym, Kiyoi is pacing around, while Sensei Tashahiro remains static in his chair, watching Japanese soap operas. Under his breath, we can see
Kiyoi might be muttering some fakku's and kuso's himself.] KD : BAKAYAROU! What's wrong with him! He knows we have a match in two days. His stupidity infuriates me sometimes, Sensei! ST : I know, Rei... I know. I myself question the things he does sometimes as well. Sihung will be Sihung. At some point, you have to accept that fact and instead of being surprised by Sihung's actions, respond to them, and right them. Without you Rei-son, Sihung would crumble. He relies on you whether he or you know it.
KD : Yes, thank you, Sensei... So, what should I do now? [Cut,] [Ponpiki is curled up in his seat, wailing, tears rolling down his eyes. The two girls beside him are rubbing and massaging his back with looks of concern on their faces.]
GRL2 : Oh, don't worry, Ponpiki. Anyone can make that mistake. We'll take care of you in Britain.
GRL1 : Yeah, don't worry, Ponpiki... [Ponpiki raises his face, cheeks covered with a thin film of tears, and smiles at the two girls.]
SP : Really? You promise? GRL1 : Of course. You're our Suupaasaizn Ponpiki! GRL2 : Yeah! [Thinks.] Say, what does that mean anyway? SP : Oh... don't worry about it. You two can call me Sihung. That's what my friends and family call me. GRL1 : Awwwwwwww... GRL2 : That's sooooo sweet, Sihung! SP : [Grins.] Yeah, I know... [Grin broadens.] You two wanna see what else about me is "hung"? GRL1 : What? SP : Oh, don't play, bitches... You know you want to touch Suupaasaizn Ponpiki's wang... go ahead, ladies... touch Ponpiki's wang... but just one at a time, sweet things... we don't want Ponpiki's rising sun to set too quickly! GRL1 : EW! ["SLLLLLLLLAAAAAAAAAAAAP!"] GRL2 : GROSS! ["SLLLLLLLLAAAAAAAAAAAAP!"] [The two girls stand up and storm away down the aisle. Ponpiki stands up and calls after the two fleeing girls.]
SP : What?? Did Suupaasaizn Ponpiki say something wrong? No bitches for Ponpiki? Don't be dissin' me! ...So you don't wanna touch
Ponpiki's Wang? Not for a little?! GRL1 : GO TO HELL! SP : Ponpiki likes a little hard-to-get! [And Ponpiki leaps from the chair and trails the two girls.] [Cut.] [In the gym, Sensei Tashahiro and Kiyoi Danshi are thinking. Kiyoi suddenly scoffs in disgust.]
KD : I bet he's getting into trouble this very second. [Turns to Tashahiro.] Sensei... what are we going to do? [Tashahiro doesn't answer immediately. Instead, he continues to focus his attention to the Japanese soap opera before him. After several seconds, sappy music oozes out from the small black and white television. A small smirk manifests itself on his face as he languidly reaches over and flicks the television off.]
[He slowly turns around to Kiyoi.] ST : Let's go get your brother, Rei-son. [Tashahiro nods at Kiyoi and walks off screen with the assistance of his bamboo cane. Kiyoi throws his hands up.]
KD : What? Go to the... leave Japan? The United Kingdom? But... but, Sensei... there isn't a better way? [After receiving no answer, Kiyoi follows Sensei Tashahiro off screen.] KD : Sensei! But Sensei! [Fade out.] "To be continued..."
