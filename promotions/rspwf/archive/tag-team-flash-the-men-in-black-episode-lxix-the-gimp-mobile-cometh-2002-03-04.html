[Tag Team Flash] The Men in Black: Episode LXIX - The Gimp Mobile Cometh

4 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in. An enormous, magnificently beautiful sea of stars engulfs the entire camera shot. War drums echo through the heavens as a rollup of white text begins to crawl into infinity, a la George Lucas' "Star Wars" films...]
Not too long ago, in a rather well known wrestling organization somewhere in Canada... The mysterious Men in Black drew closer and closer to becoming full-blown SUPERSTARS in the business. Together, they were on the verge of taking the tag team division by storm. Individually, they were making strides as well. Jayson, a gifted ring technician in his own right, was one step away from earning his first ever singles title shot whilst his partner, the eccentric "Atomic Dog" Dagger, was on his way to becoming one of the most popular competitors in the organization.
Meanwhile, the team's mentor, the curmudgeonly Gate Keeper, and his impish right hand man, The Gimp, were experiencing their own bit of good fortune as well. The Gimp was on his way to successfully getting over his crush on domestic guru Martha Stewart. Plus, after an astounding FIFTY years of celibacy, The Gate Keeper finally got his groove back, thanks to some smooth operating by his little buddy.
Sensing that his boss was feeling lonesome, The Gimp went ahead and acquired a mail-order bride for The Gate Keeper. After a couple of weeks of preparation, Who-Li Quon, a docile twenty-eight year old dame from Peking,
China, was flown in. At first, The Gate Keeper was hesitant (and angry) at
The Gimp over his altruistic deed. However, time (and a couple dozen bottles of Absolut Vodka), he warmed up to his soon-to-be bride.
Regrettably, their happiness didn't last for very long. Hell came to Hoboken in the form of one of the co-presidents of the wrestling organization where the Men in Black competed, a noted moose-loving alcoholic with a superiority complex. He began to make life miserable for our heroes. Some say he did it just to flex his presidential muscles.
Others say that he did it because The Gimp mistakenly drank all of his booze after a show late one night. Either way, the Men in Black soon grew tired of his simple-minded shenanigans and left the organization.
No job, no security, and no source of income for The Gimp and the boys to spend on pornography. It was a dark time for the Men in Black and
Company. Luckily, some light was shed on the situation a week ago... when they were contacted to work for Outlaw Championship Wrestling, a fledgling promotion and offshoot of the now defunct RAW wrestling federation.
It's now Sunday, March 3rd, and The Gate Keeper, The Gimp, and both Men in Black are en route to Dallas, Texas, the locale of OCW's first ever show in just ten days time. It'll be on that card that Jayson and Dagger will compete in the first round of the World Tag Team Title tournament. Will the
Men in Black's luck change or will their new venture lead to even more disappointment? Only time will tell...
[The scrolling text fades from view and the camera pans down. A windswept road meanders through the outskirts of the Louisiana/Texas border. We see a lone white station wagon driving along the aforesaid road. Upon closer inspection, we see that it isn't just any ol' station wagon. It's the aptly named Gimp Mobile, The Gate Keeper and The Gimp's rickety old jalopy. We cut to an exterior shot of the car where we see the strangest assembly of people since the last Jackson family reunion. We see The Gate Keeper in the driver's seat, his fiancée Who-Li Quon alongside him in the front passenger's seat, and The Gimp and both Men in Black (who happen to be sound asleep at the moment) in the back-seat. There's a somewhat distressed look on The Gate Keeper's face. We soon find out why. His significant other is gleefully singing along with a Kylie Minogue song that's playing on the radio... rather badly... in broken English. Let the good times roll!]
Who-Li Quon: I should be so rucky! Rucky, rucky, rucky! I should be so rucky in ruv! [The Gate Keeper reaches over and taps Who-Li on the shoulder...] The Gate Keeper: Hey, Who-Li. Uhhhh... errr... dear? Can you stop it with the singing for a little while? Please?
[The Gate Keeper's plea goes unnoticed as Who-Li persists with her crooning, bobbing her head back and forth to the music...]
Who-Li Quon: I should be so rucky! Rucky, rucky, rucky! I should be so rucky in ruuuuuuuuv!
[The Gate Keeper can't take it anymore. He turns the radio off!] The Gate Keeper: Okay! That'll be enough of that! We're going to take a little break from listening to the radio! Effective immediately!
[The Gimp quickly leans forward in between the two front seats and glares at
The Gate Keeper...]
The Gimp: Boss! What in the HECK did you do that for??? Who-Li was lulling me to sleep with her singing!
[The Gate Keeper shudders...] The Gate Keeper: Well, her singing had me about one second away from having a big-time seizure, Gimp! So, unless you want my ears to start bleeding, it needs to STOP for a little bit!
[Who-Li sighs...] Who-Li Quon: Oh no! I aporogize, Crate Greeter. [The Gate Keeper groans...] The Gate Keeper: First of all, it's GATE KEEPER, not Crate Greeter. Secondly, I thought I told you to call me by my first name. [Who-Li looks over and smiles meekly at her future husband...] Who-Li Quon: Okay, Skippy. [Who-Li proceeds to rest her head against the window, quickly falling asleep in a matter of seconds. The Gimp chuckles...]
The Gimp: SKIPPY! You share your name with a brand of peanut butter! That's funny! Hee-hee-hee! [The Gate Keeper briefly turns his head to give The Gimp a dirty look...] The Gate Keeper: Oh, is it now? Hmm, MARMADUKE??? [He wrinkles his brow...] The Gate Keeper: How the hell did you get that name anyway? The Gimp: My father told me that he and my mother decided to name me that after the doctor mistook me for a Great Dane puppy when I was born! What a crazy doctor, huh?
[The Gate Keeper nods...] The Gate Keeper: Yeah. [Pause.] The Gate Keeper: You look more like a Schnauzer. [Fleeting close-up of The Gate Keeper glancing directly into the camera, grinning, a la Mr. Roper from "Three's Company...]
The Gimp: Say, boss, do you know anything about the boys' first round opponents? I think they're called Debonair. From what I've heard, they have quite a reputation. They call themselves "the most entertaining tandem in sports entertainment today".
[The Gate Keeper rolls his eyes...] The Gate Keeper: Ah geez. Not another pair of misfits with delusions of grandeur! They always seem to find us! When will it end???
The Gimp: They've already started making fun of Jayson and Dagger. Nothing too damaging though. Just the usual C.M.R.
[The Gate Keeper is now sporting a confused "deer caught in the headlights of an incoming Buick" expression on his face...]
The Gate Keeper: C.M.R.? The Gimp: Yeah. Crappy Movie References. [The Gate Keeper rolls his eyes some more...] The Gate Keeper: Oh dear! When will they learn? Petty insults about an inane five year old film doesn't bother them one bit! Jayson and Dagger are grown men, for crying out loud!
[The Gimp shakes his head...] The Gimp: Yeah! You can't affect grown men like that! Now, if these guys went and hid the boys' collection of Ashlyn Gere movies, THAT would be an
ENTIRELY DIFFERENT STORY! [_WHACK_! The Gate Keeper swings his hand back, smacking The Gimp on the forehead!] The Gate Keeper: Enough! The Gimp: What do you think the future has in store for us? Do you think things will be different now that we're working for this new promotion? Do you think that things will FINALLY go our way?
[The Gate Keeper snarls at The Gimp...] The Gimp Keeper: Who the HELL do I look like? Miss Cleo??? We'll just have to wait and see.
[The Gimp grins...] The Gimp: Okay, boss. I don't know about you but I have high hopes. Can I turn the radio back on? I'm getting a little antsy.
[Exasperated nod from The Gate Keeper.] The Gate Keeper: Sure. Anything to spare me from your incessant babbling! [The Gimp reaches forward and turns the radio back on. The Talking Heads' trippy 1984 hit "Once in a Lifetime" is soon heard playing...] #And you may find yourself living in a shotgun shack# #And you may find yourself in another part of the world# [Much to The Gate Keeper's dismay, The Gimp proceeds to sing along with the radio, gesturing towards The Gate Keeper...]
The Gimp: And you may find yourself behind the wheel of a large automobile! [He points over at a sleeping Who-Li and grins at The Gate Keeper...] The Gimp: And you may find yourself in a beautiful house... with a beautiful wife! [He throws his arms in the air...] The Gimp: And you may ask yourself... well... how did I get here? [The Gate Keeper heaves a deep, "What the hell did I do to deserve this?" sigh as The Gimp starts to do the Robot. The song plays on and the camera pans back upwards, getting a final shot of the night sky...]
