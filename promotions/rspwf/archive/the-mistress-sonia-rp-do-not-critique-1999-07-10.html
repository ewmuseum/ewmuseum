The Mistress Sonia RP [Do NOT critique]

10 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a note here: This is not a opening RP, this is from a fed I already am in. So please don't be wondering what the hell I am talking about [The scene comes in at the gate of a castle. The Cameraman waits as the gate is lowered so he can cross the moat. At the end of the castle stands a tall muscular man waiting for the cameraman to enter. Hesitantly he steps across the moat looking at the murky moat not able to have any visibility of what waits for him under the water to feast of his flesh and marrow. He gets to the entrance and the gate is risen and the servant directs the cameraman to follow him downstairs. He follows him getting the statues of demented demons and pictures of lost souls. He goes down stairs and sees Mistress Sonia wearing her ring attire and along with it a leather cape. Behind her is the pit of fire and not around her this time. The torture equipment is precisely in the same location as it been in the basement of her house's basement. Mistress Sonia stares at the cameraman and gives him a chilling smile. She walks towards the guard]]
[Mistress Sonia] You are dismissed. Be sure no one bothers us. [[The guard bows graciously and leaves closing the door]] [Mistress Sonia] Welcome, to the improved Kingdom of Torture. I do hope you enjoy your stay and do watch your step. This is an OLD castle, and some traps I still haven't found.
[[Suddenly a scream of pure agony is heard upstairs]] [Mistress Sonia] Ah, another has been found. Poor fool... oh well That's life. Please, won't you join be for dinner? [[She reveals a dinner table with a roasted chicken, salad, and more food fit for a feast among gods. She goes to the table and takes a piece of the chicken and gently places it in her mouth, chews then drinks it down]]
[Mistress Sonia] This is a good meal. I must get the chef to cook more. Well, anyway, I've read the article on the women's fed and how it was going so well.
Then... I came to this.. [[she folds the paper and turns it around revealing the part about her]] This idiot thinking that I was here only to make these people my SLAVES? No, no, no. This man has got it all wrong. Maybe I should complain to the company. They only got half of my good side. The only thing I'm pissed about is how this man describes me. I am here not to enslave people. But to bring order into chaos. That is all I am here. To be their much needed leader. That these poor ignored children of the damned have been acknowledged by someone like myself. I'm surprised they didn't humbly give me the women's title. So, I guessed I was FORCED into showing my power. That I and I alone is the rightful ruler of the woman's fed and I'll go to the men's if they deemed worthy of needing a leader such as I, but until they do I'll humbly allow them to go on and destroy each other as for now. But until then I shall face the women. And speaking of GIRLS, I have taken noticed that I have a match against
"Wyld Fyre" Tiffani Blayze.
[[She takes another bite of the chicken]] [Mistress Sonia] Yes Blayze. The sister of Arista and Tara Marshall. Well
Blayze, it's a sure victory for me right now. Don't forget I did defeat your sister Arista AND Marshall. And.... how do they saw it? "The apple doesn't fall far from the tree." I know they are not your parents, I HOPE they are not your parents, but she is your sister and you come from a whole damn FOREST of failures. Your sister failed to defeat me, your other "sister" has failed to defeat me, and so shall you. And when I do defeat you and you receive your punishment for insuborbination accordingly you too shall humbly accept that I am the only one acceptable to be champion. But you might be saying.... "But I have the midnight sisters. I have an ace up my sleeve. I will win with that ace." Bull****. You honestly think that a Mistress would come on to the battle unprepared for your cowardly attempt to cheat? [[she shakes her head]]
Ignorance. Nikki J ignorance to be on the mark. You see I have servants who would walk into a mindfield to make sure I don't get a splinter on a single inch of my skin. I will bring a servant to guard me and to watch your sisters.
These people here love order and leadership. [[She claps her hands and very man stands in attention]] [Mistress Sonia]] They THRIVE on it. Can't you see it Tiffiani? Can you hear it? It's the sound of your freedom being stripped away like the other two. You along with your "sisters" will be nothing more than another stone of the building blocks of my Utopia.
[[She takes another bite of chicken]] [Mistress Sonia] This is truely excellent... You sure you don't want a bite? [[The cameraman shakes his head along with the camera]] [Mistress Sonia] Suit yourself. Now, let's get on to the Missing link Nikki J. You've seemed to have been ambushed by the now imfamous Jasmine. I must say
Jasmine you have shown deceit, dishonor, and low down cheating.... I love it.
You must show me where you get these marvolous ideas. I saw it being nothing less than picture perfect. Seeing Nikki J, being humilated like that with her own equipment. But, something did displease me. You acted unaccordingly as a champion would and for this I must punish you. You will not expect it. You will not see it. You will not be prepared no matter how hard you try to be. And most of all, you won't suspect it to be me. [[She gives a icy grin]] I would act in harsh manners if needed, but I wouldn't do something behind someone's back. I remember when I chained Gina to a post how you said I would regret it if I would do that to you. Believe me, when my punishment has reign over your soul, the only one whom shall feel regret will be you as it feasts off your dying spirit. And when it's all said and done, you shall know why I am the only
Mistress and that there will be no master above me or equal to me. Nikki J, I wish for your unpleasent bitter victory, as for the rest of you. Put your ass in line. It belongs to me.
[[A scream of agony is heard again through the castle's stone walls.]] [Mistress Sonia] Oh good. Another trap has been found. [[Scene fades to black]]
