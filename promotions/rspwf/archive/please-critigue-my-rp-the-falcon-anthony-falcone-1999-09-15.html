PLEASE CRITIGUE MY RP..."THE FALCON" ANTHONY FALCONE

15 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
PLEASE be honest! I won't cry And here it is... (((SCENE OPENS AT THE JERSEY SHORE. A CAMERA FINDS A SIGN THAT READS, "WELCOME
TO PT. PLEASANT." ANOTHER CAMERA FADES INTO THE BEACH. HUNDREDS OF PEOPLE ARE
LAYING ON THE SAND, SWIMMING IN THE WATER, AND JUST RUNNING AROUND. THE CAMERA
TURNS TOWARDS THE BOARDWALK. THERE ARE HUGE CROWDS WALKING AROUND. ICE CREAM
AND FOOD VENDORS ARE CROWDED WITH PEOPLE. THE ARCADE IS OVERFLOWING WITH
BODIES. SUDDENLY, THE SCREEN TURNS BLACK. THE CALL OF A FALCON CAN BE HEARD.
SUDDENLY THE SCREEN COMES BACK ON AND WE ARE IN A ROOM. THERE IS A SHORT,
CHUBBY MAN SITTING ON THE SOFA. A TALL, SLENDER GIRL WITH SHOULDER LENGTH HAIR
FACES AWAY FROM THE CAMERA. THE SOUND OF RUNNING WATER IS HEARD FROM THE
BATHROOM. THE SHORT, CHUBBY MAN GETS UP AND WALKS TOWARDS THE CAMERA.)))
MAN: Hey Nicole, grab me a beer? NICOLE: Pop, don't you think you've had enough beer? You're going to get sick. MAN: Ahhh, just one more. I'll be alright. NICOLE: If you say so. (((NICOLE LEAVES THE ROOM AND GOES TO THE KITCHEN TO GET A BEER. THE MAN SITS
ON A CHAIR. HE GRABS A WRESTLING MAGAZINE AND BEGINS READING. AFTER A FEW
SECONDS OF READING, HE REMEMBERS THE CAMERA.)))
MAN: Oh, hello there. I almost forgot about you. Before we get things started, let me introduce myself. My name is Paulo Falcone. I am the father of "The Falcon" Anthony Falcone. Anthony is taking a shower right now. We will be going to the gym shortly. I am a wrestling legend from the country of
Italy. I wrestled for five years in the Italian Wrestling Federation, part of the European Wrestling Council. In the IWF, I was the champion. In the EWC, the champion from each country came and wrestled in a tournament. There were
32 countries represented in this prestigious tournament. My first round match was against "The Spanish Bull." I defeated him in an easy match. Next was the second round, and Joshua Steinburg from Israel. He was a tough opponent, but I was fortunate enough to finish him off in 15 minutes. In the quarterfinals I faced "The Assassin" representing Austria. I defeated him in 30 minutes after his valet turned on him, and gave him a low blow. Next was the Semifinals. I fought the huge German Machine by the name of Hans Pike. In another 30 minute match, he defeated me by the use of brass knuckles. He went on to win the
European Championship.
I have been around wrestling for a while, this story, possibly boring to some, shows how great of a wrestler I am, and I am showing this to my son Anthony.
Yes, he is in his rookie year, but he has already been a World Champion. He is not to be messed with my friends, and if you get on his bad side, you get on my bad side as well!
NICOLE:(((SHOUTING FROM THE KITCHEN.)))Pop, do you want a Coors or Micalob? PAULO: No Budweiser? Um...get me a Coors. NICOLE: Alright.(((NICOLE COMES OUT WITH A GLASS OF BEER.))) PAULO: Thanks sweetheart. NICOLE: No problem. PAULO: Everyone, this is Nicole Albertini. She is Anthony's girlfriend. Say hell Nicole. NICOLE: Hello everybody. My boyfriend, Anthony has been in EnWa since the beginning. And don't tell him I said this, but he is a little mad that he was taken out of the World Title Tournament. For all you newcomers, the EnWa started up, and then the first card was announced. There would be a tournament for the World Title. Anthony was to face Jarred Matthews in the first round.
However, people started to quit, and the EnWa shortly shut down. When it restarted a few days later, he was then put into a match for the TV title. He said that he has been completely ignored and should be in that triple threat match for the title. He also said that it is not fair for the other wrestlers that were originally in the tournament and now are fighting for belts like the
US and TV titles. He just doesn't understand it. But at this point, he said he doesn't care anymore, he still gets a title. Whether it be World or TV, he still gets his named recognized.
(((ANTHONY WALKS OUT OF THE BATHROOM WEARING BLACK WARM-UP PANTS AND A WHITE
T-SHIRT. HE IS WEARING NIKE CROSSTRANING SHOES. HE STEPS IN FRONT OF THE
MIRROR AND COMBS HIS HAIR FORWARD. HE WALKS OVER TO NICOLE, AND KISSES HER ON
THE CHEEK.)))
ANTHONY: Hey guys. Dad, get the car ready, I'll be out there in a sec. I have to talk to my fans here.
PAULO: OK.(((PAULO PUTS ON HIS SHOES, AND LEAVES THE ROOM.))) ANTHONY: Alright, where should I start? First off, I would like to say hello to all of my fans out there who have supported me. Second, I think I should start off on the right foot and tell everybody about my past, and what my future will be like. I came to America from Italy when I was eleven years old.
For six years we lived in a little town in New Jersey called Moorestown. We then moved here, and we've been here for the past for years. Let me talk about my short wrestling career. I started my wrestling career on July twentieth, over a month ago. I signed I deal with ACE wrestling. My first match was a tag team match a week later. I got the pinfall in that match and won. I then went on to win two more matches. The following week, I went to ACE's first
Pay-Per-View, Wildcard '99. It was a one night tournament to declare the first ever World Heavyweight Champion. I went 4-0, and won the World Title. A few days later, the fed crumbled. I then traveled to the WWA. I quickly went 5-0.
That's when I got an offer from the EnWa. I knew that the competition in the
EnWa was much better than that of the WWA. I quit the WWA, and now I'm here in the EnWa. Enough about how I got here, lets talk about where I'm going. I plan to win the TV title on Thursday. I will then successfully defend it for a couple of weeks. But then, I will move onto better things. I will gradually make my way to the top. First the US title, and then hopefully the World
Title. I will defend that successfully, and gain the respect that I need from the wrestling world.
(((SUDDENLY, A KNOCK CAN BE HEARD ON THE DOOR. ANTHONY WALKS OVER AND OPENS
THE DOOR. AND ENWA REPRESENTATIVE IS AT THE DOOR.)))
ANTHONY: What do you want? ENWA REP: Mr. Falcone, these are the EnWa interview tapes you wanted. Here is a list of them.
ANTHONY: Oh, thanks pal. If you want, you can stay and watch my interview. Lets see, The Assassins, James Taylor, "Vigilante" Bill Thomas, ahh, here it is...Dragon Warrior Number One, Dragon Warrior Number Two, and J.Andrews/Y2FU.
This Thursday, I have a Triple Threat Match against both Dragon Warrior, and
John Andrews. Lets talk about Dragon Warrior first.
My name, Dragon Warrior, is Anthony Falcone. Get it straight. You should be worrying about more things than my name and alias. You should be worrying about my overwhelming skill, not my name. You say that my skills are just up to par? Dragon Warrior, you probably never seen me wrestle before, have you?
Because up to par isn't even half way there. I take my opponents by storm. And you, will be one of them. You think that my garbage is wrestling? I don't have much to say about that, I guess that my skills will show you up on
Thursday. Yes, "Sure Thing" Brian Goldstein, "Heetseaker" Ace Dowling, &
"Sensational" Johnny Winters will most likely bring back gold. But Dragon
Warrior, as long as you are wrestling me, you won't be in any title scene.
It's as simple as that. I am the toughest, most resilient man ever to step into a wrestling ring. And it will take a lot to take the TV title away from me. Dragon, you've decided to ignore me, huh? Well, that's fine with me. But remember, you ignorance will be your downfall. You ignoring me will be your biggest mistake. You'll see, you'll see.
Next up, is "The Fallen Angel" John Andrews. The Prince of Pain, The Soul
Survivor. Soul Survivor of what? Your past twenty feds that you were in? All you're hype before you're interview was more impressive than your interview itself. Thursday Night, two "No-Names" will get their Thrashings? Well, I don't speak for Dragon Warrior, but I know he has something to say about that, as well as myself. John Andrews, I don't like the way you talk. Now I know that Dragon Warrior is a quality wrestler. He isn't a no-name. But to call me a no-name, that's wrong. And you'll see why very soon. I will be the name in a month or so, after I climb the ladder to the top. The TV Title is not the lowest belt in this fed. You defend it every card against whoever wants a shot. It's a hard belt to defend, but you don't realize that. John Andrews,
I've never tried flying with a boot up my-
NICOLE: Tony, you shouldn't let him talk to you like that. I hope you kill him this Thursday.
ANTHONY: I'll be fine. And I don't intend to fly with a boot up my- NICOLE: Are you sure? ANTHONY: Yes, I'm sure. Now where was I. Oh yes, Andrews, you're right, I'm not fighting an average man, nor am I facing an above average man. I am facing a man who isn't ready to take his step into the big leagues yet. He's still waiting for his chance, but as long as I am around, he'll never get that chance. I don't really care where you went, and where you're coming from. You may be thirsty for blood, but there is no way you're getting mine. I'm thirsty as well, thirsty for some gold! You want to talk about skill, talent, and ambition? Well, then let me add some things about myself. Skills, I have more than enough the skills. I have the skill to beat anyone I want. Talent, like
I said about a second ago, I have skills, but I lack the ability to use those skills to my full potential. That is where my father steps in and helps me.
AMBITION, you want to talk about ambition? You're talking to the wrong man. My ambition blew a huge hole in the roof, and keeps on going. I am the most ambitious son of a gun you'll ever see. The reason why I'm not going for a higher title is not because I'm a bad wrestler. I wanted to be in this match.
Why? Because if I win, I will defend the title on every card successfully. I will prove that I am a top contender in the EnWa.
Ya know, since "The Fallen Angel" John Andrews was talking about the World
Title Match, I think I might as well put my two cents in too. The match is a three way dance between "Sure Thing" Brian Goldstein, "The Immortal" Outlaw, and Jarred Matthews. I was originally part of the World Title Tournament. My opponent, Jarred Matthews. The fed then shut down and then restarted a few days later. And now I'm left in a match for the TV title, and my scheduled opponent is going for the World Title. Problem? Yes. But I'm over it. I don't need a World Title to prove that I am good. I have my hands full this
Thursday already. I like my odds Thursday, and more than likely I will be the
Television Champion. After that will be US and World gold. But for now, I have to get past Dragon Warrior, and "The Fallen Angel" John Andrews. Oh, and by the way Dragon and Angel...
I AM THE FALCON, AND YOU ARE MY PREY. I WILL FEAST!!! (((ANTHONY STARES AT THE CAMERA. HE THEN GETS UP AND GRABS HIS GYM BAG. HE
ESCORTS THE ENWA REP OUT OF THE DOOR. HE KISSES NICOLE AND THEN LEAVES. SCENE
FADES TO BLACK.)))
PLEASE let me know how yo feel about this roleplay, and please be honest! "The Falcon" Anthony Falcone ::Free Agent:: Career Record: 18-0 Titles Held: ACE World & North American Belt, EPWA North American & European
Belt, EnWa TV Belt
