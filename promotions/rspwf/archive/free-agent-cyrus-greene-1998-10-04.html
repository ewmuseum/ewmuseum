[FREE AGENT] Cyrus Greene

4 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, it's me again, and while sitting in my default state of mind, which is boredom, I decided I need a new fed to rip it up in. Here is some background on me.
X One time I ate two pounds of cookie dough. Ended up with a ruptured stomach lining. X I've been ewrestling for damn near two years. X I actively participate in the HHWO and LAW. And, here is a sample roleplay. Knock yourselves out. [ Enter the streets of Seattle, Washington. No, it's not that dark alley that seems to be the regular place for baboons that call themselves wrestlers to perform what they like to call, a flash. Nope, we ain't there, never gonna be either. We are standing in the busy streets of downtown Seattle, Washington.
The cars are honking their horns, and the sidewalk is filled with people trying to get to x destination. Oh, but not one man, enter Cyrus Greene, standing smack in front of the camera with a microphone in his hands. Even though he's trying to act PROFESSIONAL, he is still wearing an obscene shirt and torn at jeans. ]
CG: "Welcome folks, we are here standing in downtown Seattle. And you know what I'm going to do? I'm going to question some of the local folks around here about the issues concerning _ME_," Cyrus speeds up while saying the last two names, "Knick Man and Scott Wright. Oh yeah, and camera, get a shot of my belt, would ya?"
[ The camera obeys and gets a close up of Cyrus Greene's foam belt. After a brief look at it, the camera moves back up to see his arrogant mug smiling with his eyes closed. His expression quickly changes, as he begins to talk once again. ]
CG: "_FIRST_ of all, issue numero uno. Me. Since I am of utmost importance to myself and everyone else, first we are going to talk about me. Ya know, as the official HHWO Champion of Cyrus Greene, I have a lot of responsibilities!
That's right, some people think that being the only champion in the HHWO isn't nothing but a joke, but everyone who says that are so stupid they couldn't pour piss out a boot if the instructions were written on the heel. Anyway, as a champion, I have to interact with the people. And that is what I am going to do while that little red light is in my face."
[ Cyrus turns around and starts yelling for a man to come here. The man responds, and comes towards the camera, with a "What the hell is this all about" expression on his face. ]
CG: "You, guy, are you a professional wrestling fan?" [ The man laughs. ] Man: "Are you kidding m…" CG: "Great! Then I take it you're a fan of the HHWO, and therefore a fan of
Cyrus Greene. Tell me, what do you think of Cyrus Greene?"
Man: Laughing, "I don't have time for this…" CG: "Like hell you don't! Now tell me, do you think Cyrus Greene is the greatest wrestler to ever grace foot on he planet?"
Man: "Uh…" CG: "Thanks, I knew it, you are my biggest fan!" Cyrus smiles, "Now get out of my way you ugly cuss."
[ Cyrus pushes the man off to the side and continues walking. ] CG: "Issue two, Scott Wright. This man has the NERVE to hit ME with a CHAIR during MY match! This man should be locked up and fed to hungry squirrels for the sake of humanity. And you know what all my Cyrus-o-holics, I am going to be the one that puts that rat bastard to rest. Come August 30th, I get to beat the living crap out of this egotistical loser. Scott Wright, if I were you I'd hope that my face ended up on a milk carton before this match, cause once I get in there with you, I'm going to smack you around ever corner of the ring."
[ Cyrus changes subjects. ] CG: "And last, and MOST DEFINETLY least, Knick Man. He says he's going to shove ME TITLE down my throat?! Listen here, you poster-boy for abortion, if you even DARE to put your grubby paws on me, I swear to god I'm gonna shove my boot so far up your ass you're going to start gagging it out. With that in mind, let's ask the people another question. YOU! Taco man!"
[ Cyrus Greene points to the man, a small Mexican. Cyrus approaches the man who has a very uneasy look on his face. ]
CG: "You look like a semi-bright man, how old are you?" [ Cyrus puts the microphone in his face, but just as fast, takes it back. ] CG: "Seriously, I couldn't give a flying piece of monkey crap about that. What I want to know is, what do you think of Knick Man." Mexican Man: "Listen, I have got to go to work, I am already late." CG: "SHUT UP! This is not Burger King, you DO NOT get it your way! YOU GET
IT MY WAY DAMNIT! Tell me what you think of him NOW!"
MM: "I've never heard.." [ Cyrus cuts him off. ] CG: "See Knick Man! No one knows about you!" [ The man quickly scampers off. ] CG: "And no one cares about you! But I'll say it again, if you even DARE to put your nose into my business, or even go as far as MENTIONING my name EVER
AGAIN, I will put you on a damn respirator!
[ The scene gets abruptly cut off. ] Tony S. <()> Ewrestler par pathetic "REHAB IS FOR QUITTERS"
