Roleplay OWWA Dimension of Fear

9 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
((The stadium is packed and fans are getting rowdy . Jethro Tull's Aqua Lung ,an old 1970's acid rock hit blasts in the speakers. The walkway to the ring ad the ring esxplodes in a hot ,blazing and continous bonfire. Two figures appear in the ring out of nowhere. One is tall and thin wearing all black except for red boots .He has his face covered by an executioner's hood ad he is holding a long handled double bladed axe,The other charachter is a little taller and wider more burley.He is wearing a maroon and brown snake skin wrestling tights from neck to boots.His boots are red like The Decapitator's.Tatoos of a rattle snake extend down each of his arms ending at the knuckles with a human skull head and long fangs on each.
The fire and the music dies simultaniously and the sound of rattlesnakes eminate from the ring .
On the ring floor a dozen diamond back rattlers are slithering about.The fans cheer as The Grim Ripper grabs the mic.))
GR:Asssss you may allready know The Dimensssionss of Fear are ssstriking terror in all we have faced. One thing I musst set sstraight,my name isss not Grim Reaper it isss THE GRIM RIPPER.Hahahahahahahahaha"
Decapitator:"Hahahahaha I hear something unusual these past tag team excursions in blood letting and mayhem.Cheering !!
Could it be the mass of nitwits out there are actually 'devil's advocates as it were? GR:"It way very well be that the multitude of fanssss are eager to see thier heroes crussssshed by usss.Make no missstake we are out to injure and ruin livessss.
It matterssss not weather we win or losssse ,whether we are cheered or jeered we will draw asss much blood and inflict asss much pain asss inhumanly posssible.
Decapitator:"Indeed we hope more nitwits will come out and watch us brutalize wrestling's so called best.
There are no images we cant marr,no brightness we cant blacken and absolutely no grappler so good as we can't drag them through the dark funk of pain ,torture and humiliation."
GR:"Yesss we will sssssstrike(as he said strike all snakes struck both men in the boots as they kicked them gently aside)fear and loathig in heartssss everywhere !"
((Both men laugh and an explosion of dark smoke engulfs the ring then clears leaving behind an empty ring))
