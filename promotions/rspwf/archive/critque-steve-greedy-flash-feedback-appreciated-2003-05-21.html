Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
V/O : Kick. [ Tap, tap, tap . .. tap.] V/O : Punch. [ Tap, tap, tap . .. tap.] V/O : Slam. [ Tap, tap, tap . .. tap.] V/O : WOOOOOOO [ Fade In.] [ The camera fades into a view of a shirt less Steve Greedy sitting on the couch in his rather large living room, playing the playstation 2. It is of course a wrestling game which he is playing as we can see two generic wrestlers going at it on the screen.]
" The Rich One " Steve Greedy : WLW needs a game like this. [ Greedy quiets down once again concentrating on his game. We set in silence for minutes watching only Greedy's facial expressions, and body language.]
SG : << Out of Nowhere >> THATS A THREE COUNT BABYYYYYY . .. [ Greedy tosses the controller down happily, and begins to pump his fist.] SG : I wish it was that easy in real life. [ Greedy pauses.] SG : Wait a second . .. It usually is. [ Greedy laughs as he leans forward turning off the playstation and the television set. He then once again gets comfortable on the couch.]
SG : As you can tell my training for my up - coming match has been superb. I am in top notch shape. . . .
GINGER BRING ME A BEER PLEASE ........ I have been eating right, drinking right, running miles, and hitting the gym everyday." [ Ginger Dupree enters the room bringing her husband a beer, and a plate of fried chicken.]
Ginger Dupree : Here you go .. [ Steve takes both items, and sets it down on his lap.] SG : As I was saying . .I am in top notch shape for this lil steel cage match coming up. I am taking this thing _VERY SERIOUS_. Just as serious as last time
I beat him, which was only a few weeks ago at April's Reign if my memory serves me correct.
[ Ginger breaks out with laughter behind Steve, as he looks back up at her smiling. She then blows him a kiss, and exits stage left.]
SG : Madman is a great wrestler, and will be a handful in the ring. I can't wait to be challenged like never before.
[ Greedy takes a sip of his beer, and a bite out of his chicken.] SG : Okay enough of that bull. Madman will be as easy as taking candy from a baby to beat again in that ring. That ring is my domain, and not many people can beat me in it. Not very many people at all. I'm not trying to crack on Motor City Madman's skill, but it is an honest fact that in the ring I am one of the best in the business.
Sure, I have been poking fun, having a few drinks and eating whatever, but all the knowledge is still there. All the talent is still there. All the intensity is still there.
[ Greedy sets his stuff to the side and stands up revealing his well toned and tanned body.]
SG : And obviously I am not in that bad of shape. I still look _ DAMN GOOD_. I will be ready one way or another to defeat my old buddy once again. I will make him my personal slave, and will be more than happy to have him on my side to help myself reach the next level of dominance in the WLW.
[ Greedy sits back down on the couch grabbing his beer.] SG : Believe that. [ Greedy takes a rather large drink of his beer.] SG : I do alot of things that most people don't agree with, but in that ring most would admit I do get the job done. I think Madman can vouch for me on that one.
[ Greedy finishes his drink and drops the can on the ground.] SG : And my maid would do the same cause I pay her bills quite easily with the paychecks I make in this sport.
[ Greedy pops his trademark smirk.] SG : The sport has treated me good, and I know I promised I would never wrestle in a cage again after my match in 1999, but the time has come to give a little back as they say.
Madman you challenged me . .. Now what ? [ Greedy shakes his head.] SG : I'll tell you what . . .. Prepare to go _BANKRUPT_ Simple as that old friend, simple as that. [ Fade ]
