[Fade in.] [the screen is engulfed by a mansion. The mansion seems to be bigger then the camera lens's angle. You see the front door which is made of wood and the gold knocker. There are beautiful plants all over the front yard. In the driveway, there are two 1999 Lexus GS's. The reflection of Christmas tree lights is in one of the windows. The camera man begins to walk torwards the front door.]
[He reaches the front door. Looks around and finds a door bell. He pushes the door bell. A man's voice is heard from the intercom underneath the bell.]
"Who is this?" [the camera man stands there and then answers.] "This is a reporter from the NGW, I am here requesting an interview sir." [the man on the other side of the door pauses then answers.] "How long will it take?" [the camera man quickly answers.] "About 5 minutes sir." [the man answers quickly.] "Exactly what your wife said. C'mon in." [the camera man pauses then opens the door. He walks in and the inside of the house is shown. The front room is beautiful. There is a huge chandelear hanging from the ceiling. A black piano is in the corner behind a half circle of black leather sofas. There is nobody in the room so the camera man walks down a hallway which leads to a door. The door is shut and the camera man knocks three times. The same voice from before answers.]
"It took your ass long enough." [Camera man answers.] "Sorry, can I come in?" [the man answers.] "Sure, hold on." [Some ruffling is heard from beyond the door, then the man answers with . . .] "Allright, c'mon in." [the camera man walks inside. There is a huge room with a beautiful bed in the center. The bed is shaped like a set of lips. On the bed is Jason "the Playa"
Geribo and a blonde haired woman.] [Jason is on top of the covers in a pair of black silk boxer shorts. The woman is under the covers with god knows what on. Jason starts to speak.]
"So, you have finally made it to my room, how did you like the rest of the house?" [the camera man answers with a nod.] "Good, but I bet that is not why you have come here today. You are here to get my opinions on the man I am facing Saturday. The man known as Jamal Jordan." [Jason stands up and walks torward the television in the corner. He grabs an unmarked tape and pops it in the VCR. He is blocking the screen. He pushes play on the remote control. You hear moaning and screaming comming from the television, but Jason is blocking the television screen from view. He quickly takes the tape out.]
"Oops, that could have been very bad for the little ones, and the older ones. That is the advanced workout tape by Kathy Ireland, guest starring myself."
[Jason then pops in another tape and presses play. This time it is a voice unfamiliar to us. Jason steps back as the voice speaks.]
"Cages are where animals that can not be controlled are thrown into. weapons are things that man has invented in order to protect themselves. What happens when uncontrollable animals are placed in a cage with weapons?"

Pure mayhem." [Jason shuts the television off and starts back to the bed.] [When he gets to the bed, he sits next to the woman again and starts to speak.] "So the NGW wants to put me in a match that only animals should be displayed. A weapons filled cage match. I am far from hardcore, or far from extreme, but I am an animal, at least that is what the ladies tell me."
[smiles.] "Jamal, if you think that anytime in the match you will hit that finisher, what is it called again?"
[the woman answers.] "THE PLAYA HATA" [Jason laughs then speaks.] "The playa hata, sounds like all the men whose ladies have been with me. Anyways, if you think that you will land that move on me in the match. You are dead wrong."
[Jason pauses.] "'Cause when you come to my neighborhood, or any neighborhood... ...You find out exactly why ... ... I AM THE ONLY TRUE PLAYA IN DA GAME TODAY! ... [the scene fades to black as Jason pops the same video in and then mounts the woman and starts kissing her passionately.]
