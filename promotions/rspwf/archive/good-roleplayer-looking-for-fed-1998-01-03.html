Good Roleplayer looking for fed

3 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone. I am the handler of The Devastator Kurt Castle, currently competing in World Hardcore Wrestling. I am looking for a fed. Here is what I want. If you can provide this email me at
[detail withheld] -A webpage -At least 1 play by play card per week -Booked........I dont want some fed where someone will win because they are friends with the prez -Running at least 4 months -Cards out on time for the most part Here is one of my better Roleplays that I did -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------- (Scene begins to total blackness. Then you see the words "The Devastator" Kurt
Castle show up in the distance in white. It then gets focused in and comes closer and closer until it fills up the screen. It then scrolls off to the right. It then shows Kurt Castle sticking a series of "Total Devastaions" on
Dan Maddux at November Carving. After it shows that it shows Kurt Castle have the figure four on Dan Maddux at November Carving and shows Maddux tapping out.
After that the scene goes black. Then the TV Rating pops up on the screen. Only it is large and covers the full screen. It says "TV MA" under it in parenthesis it has "V" for vulgar language. Again the scene goes black. It then goes to a still of Castle holding of the WHW World Television Title Belt right after he won in on Monday Night Funeral on December the 8th. Scene then shoots to a live audience in a show room where a sign is posted in sparkling red letters which read "Castle's Kingdom" Scene fades in on a TV host sitting in one of two plush chairs on the set)
Man: Welcome everyone to the first edition on Castle's Kingdom. Hello everyone, my name is Michael Moyer and I will be the host for this weekly TV show which each week will feature WHW Television Champion Kurt Castle. This will be a program in which he will express his feelings on certain subjects around the world of WHW or the world of wrestling period. Also, we will be taking questions from you! The TV audience. We'll give you the number for that later.
(Moyer takes a drink of water from a china cup and then swallows) MM: Well without any further ado, lets bring in the star of the show. Reining
World Hardcore Wrestling Television Champion. "The Devastator" Kurt Castle!!! ("Tubthumping" by Chumbawumba begins to play as Kurt Castle comes walking onto the scene. he is wearing blue jeans and a Kurt Castle T-shirt. On the front of the T-shirt it reads "WHW has been Devastated" As the scene goes to a back shot of Kurt the Tshirt reads "Devastation Tour '97. Kurt is also wearing a pair of black Oakley's and has the TV title belt strapped over his shoulder. Moyer stands up and the two shake hands and then both sit down. They begin to speak)
MM: Welcome to.......Well, your show. (both men laugh) Its a pleasure to be a part of this.
KC: Same here. Though it was about damn time I got my own talk show. Im a big enough superstar you know.
MM: Of course you are. Well lets start right off. And a congratulations is in store for your big win this past Monday for the WHW TV Title
KC: Well all I have to say was it was one hell of a match. I was looking forward to it every since Dan Madden ruined my first shot. I told everyone for weeks that I had the credentials to hold a title in WHW and I didn’t disappoint. Now as far as Demonic went, he was a great champ. He had the second longest TV title reign in WHW history next to Vega Dread. But like I said my time had come and nothing was going to stop me. Thats all I have to say about that!
MM: All right. Next, what are your plans for the TV title in the near of distant future? KC: Well I hope it gets me what it has got other people that held the title. The chance to be noticed and move on up the rankings in WHW. I mean, not to harp on Vega Dread but hell hes a great wrestler. Anyway, Vega Dread held the
TV for just over two months and look where that led him. Straight up the totem pole to the top. And I hope that will happen to me too!
MM: Now coming up Friday you have your first WHW TV title defense. Its against a man by the name of Stalker. He seems to have made a quick impact on WHW, and hes looking for you
KC: Well, let me get a few things straight now. He may have made a quick impact on WHW but not on the Devastator. He hasn’t shown me a damn thing since the day he has walked into the doors of WHW. Until you bleed, sweat , get screwed by the committee, and pay the price to be here then you dont deserve to be a champion. And Ive been through all of those and Im still standing today. And thats why Im champ.
MM: Not to interrupt you, but what do you mean by getting screwed by the committee? KC: Mike, I like you man. Thats why I hired you, but for Christ's sake dont play dumb. You know that theres been some instability behind the scenes of WHW between myself and Mr. Runyan. But thats not really something that needs to be brought out in the open. That will just cause more controversy then Im willing to be a part of. Anyway, back to Stalker. I mean, Ive mentioned this right after I won the Title last Monday but that is almost the most unoriginal gimmick that Ive ever seen. I mean, correct me if Im wrong but wasn’t there a guy in the WWF that went by "Stalker"
MM: Thats correct. His real name was Barry Windham. Quite a veteran! KC: You hit the nail on the head. Quite a veteran. So why the world of wrestling allowed this to go on is beond me. Not only is degrading to the World
Wrestling Federation and Barry Windham but its degrading to me. I want some real competition. Not some fly by night , no talent mid card jabrony called
Stalker. But hell, if thats what my task is then thats what Im going to do.
Thats the one thing I haven’t done since Ive joined WHW, and thats complain about my opponents and put them down. Now granted , in mine and about 1million other minds Im going to successfully defend my title Friday and move on to bigger and better things!
MM: Speaking of bigger things. Rumor has it that someone has accepted the open challenge for this Monday at Monday Night Funeral. Can you give us any confirmation on that?
KC: I can and I will. The man I will be defending against next Monday is none other than former WHW TV champion "Sycho" Steve Morris!!
MM: Steve Morris. Now thats some competition. I think this will be your chance to prove to everyone in WHW what the "new generation" of WHW that you talked about before is all about.
KC: Indeed! This man has more credentials than we have time to talk about it. Of course an esteemed member of the Hearts of Desire. He has been here since the beginning and chances are he'll be here till the end. But when youre talking about me then youre stepping into a different league!!
MM: Well you know I back you 100% but one other credential worth mentioning is that the man is currently the number 1 a contender for the WHW Cruiserweight
Championship currently help by Atari Allia.........err......Beck Backster!
KC: Now, Im going to stop you right there because what you said brings up another point. Hes the number one contender for the CRUISERWEIGHT championship.
Now I know everyone believes that a cruiserweight can beat a heavyweight, hell so do I, and its happened many times. But when you have a 6 foot 8 ,305 pounder that knows how to use his weight in the ring , youre talking about a different thing. But, I think Im going to put more emphasis on this match than any other.
This is the ultimate chance to prove myself, and for that fact, so is the
Stalker match. And I intend to be prepared for it like never before!
MM: Well Im sure you will. But right now, Id like to ask you how you feel about
WHW outside of the TV title division. Im talking on the lines of The World
Heavyweight Title and the feuds arising in it!
KC: Well hell, where should I begin. I guess Ill start out addressing the World
Champ himself. "Extreme" Roger Lambert. The man is awsome. Im going to admit it, the man has done more in his short time in WHW then I could even do. He came in, didn’t worry about feuds, which was my biggest mistake, and went to work and went to the top. Thats how he became World Champion so fast. Now other people that have to be considered contenders have to be, Ryan Decker. But
Decker has caught himself up in the feud with the Iron Curtain. Then we have
Mike Austin, Vega Dread, and of course you have to look at current US champ Dan
Maddux, and hell I cant overlook myself!
MM: I think that you have overlooked one huge monster. The nearly eight foot,
500 pound Monstrosity!! KC: I left him off on purpose. I was planning to talk about him separately. So
I shall. I think that Monstrosity is an awesome athlete for as big as he is.
And he made a huge impact on WHW as he quickly rose to the top and won the
International championship. But I think after November Carving hes lost an edge of sorts. I mean hell, he lost his manager, his friends, and his International title. But as facts would have it he gets a shot to lead WHW. He gets his shot at Robert Lambert. Now I dont want him or anyone else to take this the wrong way, but I dont like the fact that he might lead WHW into 1998.
(interrupting) MM: Wait a second Kurt. Now are you trying to tell me that Monstrosity isn’t good enough to be the champ. You just sat there 5 minutes ago and talked about how much you were FOR the new generation..
KC: Well you know I look at this way. Extreme Roger Lambert is just as much as a newbie as Monstrosity. I just feel that Roger Lambert is a better man to have the job than Monstrosity. Remember, size isn’t EVERYTHING. Now, Lambert has went through hell and back more than once in these 45 and 50 minute matches.
Now when it come straight down to it, I dot care how in shape you are, if you were almost eight feet tall and 500 pounds you wouldn’t be able to go that long. The only thing I meant by all of this was Lambert has more stability and more moves. hes a more rounded athlete that Monstrosity. And I dont think
Monstrosity can pull it off. END OF SUBJECT!
MM: All right then. Let me ask you one more thing and then we'll go to phone calls. By the way, if you want to call in , its a toll free number at
1-800-KINGDOM. Ok, well what I was going to ask you is how do you feel about the setup that was exicuted on Ryan Decker at the hands of the Iron Curtain back at November Carving?
(Castle laughs a little and then shakes his head) KC: If there was one thing that I thought that Ryan Decker would know after all this time in the sport its this........DTA buddy , DTA. DONT TRUST ANYONE. I mean come on Ryan, what were you thinking. I mean a blind person could have saw this one coming. Mike Austin and Roger Lambert teaming against you and Curt X.
Granted Curt wasn’t exactly a "bad guy" I dont think he would have accepted just like that if he didn’t have a plan. I feel for you man. I really do. I was just in a war with a stable, the only difference is noone stabbed me in the back. All I can say is choose your battles wisely and always, and I repeat
ALWAYS have a plan!!!!
MM: Well, thats what you always have to do in professional wrestling. Anyway, lets get a question from our first caller.
(Moyer Talks into a speaker phone) MM: Hello. Youre live on Castle's Kingdom Caller: Yes, my name is Tony, and I live in North Carolina and have been a big fan of Kurt since he debuted in WHW
Kurt: How about that?!?! Caller: Anyway, I was wondering , do you have any future plans in renuing your feud with Dan Maddux and the Northeast Alliance? Kurt: At this point no. I feel that the only thing that feud did was hold both of us down. And Ive already beat him THREE times. I have nothing to prove to him.
MM: Thanks for calling!!.........Hello, you are live on Castle's Kingdom! Caller2: Yes, I was wondering how Jeff Runyan and the rest of the athletes of
WHW felt about you having a talk show.
Kurt: Well , I think Jeff is scared. He now knows that I dont need for money ever. And that if he screws me Im gone and I wont look back. And the other wrestlers? Jealous. They want to be as big as myself but just cant be!!
MM: Thanks for calling!!.........Hello , youre live on Castle's Kingdom! Caller3: Yes. I was just wondering, who would be the man that you would like to face most in WHW right now and why?
Kurt: Id have to say Brad Morris. The guy is like a fly. He eats shit and he wont go away. If I met him in the ring Id shut him up once and for all!!!
MM: Thanks for calling. Well I think we went a little to long on the interview session because we are fresh out of time. Damn Kurt, if you got so much money, you should buy another half hour!
Kurt: Thats not a half bad idea. Ill think about it! MM: Well for Kurt Castle, Im Michael Moyer, see you next time on Castle's
Kingdom!! (Scene fades out as Tubthumping by Chumbawumba again begins to play. Kurt stands up and him and Michael shake hands again. Then both men walk off the scene. Scene goes to a wide angle then goes black)
