Flash - "Black Knight" Alex Martinez

16 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
what the hell? I have no self esteem, tell me why this sucks, or, if you're feeling especially benevolent, tell me why I am so damn awesome
-Dave- [The camera opens to only a thick, silvery mist. This heavy fog swirls around, obscuring anything from sight. But, from beyond the thick wall of mist, comes a sound. The heavy, staccato like rhythm of footsteps, pounding against hard concrete pavement. As the clip-clop cadences continues, the swirling, dense fog slowly begins to lift, almost as if afraid to stand in opposition to whoever it is that approaches. As the fog departs, a dark city scene emerges. A skyline, dominated by looming skyscrapers, almost gothic in their design, dirty streets upon which debris sails along guided by a chilling wind, almost in mockery of tumbleweeds in the classic western setting. And from this new frontier scene, this dark, gritty urban wilderness, the source of footsteps, the harbringer of doom, comes more clearly into focus.]
[The first, and most distinguishing characteristic of this figure is his height and build. Fully seven feet tall, the figure's well developed body, one that appears to have been chiseled from granite, is undeniably menacing. He is dressed in a tight black t-shirt, the short sleeves revealing a pair of well muscled arms. He also wears form fitting leather pants, and a pair of black leather boots, the toes shod in steel complete the ensemble. The figure approaches with his head bowed, until he stands directly in front of the camera, and only then does his head raise.] [Despite the omnipresent darkness, the man wears a pair of silver and black mirrored sunglasses, and wears his long, black hair pulled into a severe pony tail. But the glasses do nothing to lessen the look of intensity, that barely contained sadistic rage that dwells deep within this man's heart. A long, angry red scar runs diagonally down his face, and his nose
is partially crooked, a remnant of a badly healed broken nose. Only after all of this has been taken in, does the figure begin to speak.] AM: In the movie Taxi Driver, the character played by Robert Deniro says that one day, a great rain will come to the sprawling cities, a rain that will wash away all the filth, that will purify the tainted earth, and that some day the darkness will pass, leaving only sunshine, light, and ultimately, the good that supposedly lay within every man's soul. [The figure shakes his head several times, as a small smirk curls the edges of his lips.] AM: The character was right in one thing... a rain will come, in fact, that rain has come. But it is not to cleanse the world of its sins. It is a destructive torrent, a violent force that will bring about eternal night, not purification. And that rain has come... heralded by me, by the man known as Alex Martinez, the Black Knight.
Mark my face well, all of you, for when the nightmares come, I want my name, my face, because years later, when people ask you why you cower in fear now, why you jump at shadows, you will be able to describe the terror that I instilled within you accurately. I am the man that all of you fear, I am the demon that haunts your dreams, and I am the monster that shall destroy you. I am the Black Knight…
I am Alex Martinez. Let my name echo in your memory, for as I have said, it portends the destruction of any who stand in my path. I am seven feet tall, three hundred and fifteen pounds, of sheer rage and destruction. My mission is simple, I seek only one thing, to cut a bloody swath through the entire wrestling world, and to leave a trail of broken bodies and shattered dreams in my wake. Make no mistake, I want what every other wrestler wants, gold, fame, and prestige, but these are secondary goals. Let me explain this to you in another way. Some join this sport for money, others for fame, others have childhood dreams of winning a gold belt, there am here for only one reason… the sheer pleasure of hearing a man's bones snap between my fingers, for the thrill I get when I split a man's head opening. Winning is good, but there are far better goals. Chaos, anarchy, and mayhem, these are the elements I bring into the ring with me. Fear and pain are what remains within the broken bodies of my opponents. Make no doubt, someday, I shall take my place among the so-called
"legends" of wrestling, I will sit upon the pedestal of "icon" status, this is inevitable. But my ascension will come only after everyone else has been destroyed. I will not be one of many legends, I will be the last man standing. [A smirk crosses Martinez's lips once more] AM: "Black Knight" Alex Martinez… remember the name, because very soon, the darkness falls upon this federation, and when it does, you are all going to get… BURNED!!!!!! [Martinez spins around on his heel, and walks from the camera, and with each step, the silvery fog encroaches once more, until all visibility has fled, and the scene fades to black.]
