[RP Critique] - Chris da Costa -

6 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Haven't done this in a while.. This is an introduction RP I did for the Great Lakes Championship Wrestling. I'm looking for a couple critiques on this bad boy right here. This is the second RP I've ever done with CDC, so.. Let's hope you like it! :) ----------------------------- [The camera opens inside a small, beat-up pickup truck streaming alongside a scarcely occupied highway late in the evening hours. The inside of the pickup truck is decorated in torn brown seats, a littered dashboard, and a pair of occupied cup holders. To the left of the cameraman sits the driver of this trashy vehicle. He has greased, stringy brown hair which hangs just below his shoulders, a deep golden brown tan, and is sporting a pair of blue denim jeans and a black T-shirt which reads "Fuerza" across the front. This Hispanic looking man is quite the handsome fellow but is letting off a somewhat uneasy feeling. His eyes are heavily concentrated on the highway in front of him and his hands are clenched tightly onto the steering wheel in front of him. Trying to calm the Hispanic man, the camera guy attempts to start conversation.] CG: So.. Why're ya looking so tense there, buddy? ??: Silencio, mamón! Yo no tensión, okei?! [Sub-title translation: Quiet, punk! I'm not tense, okay?!] [The camera guy chuckles behind the camera and continues to record the driver. He spots a small gym bag lying next to him with the name "Chris da Costa" stitched along the side of the suede material.] CG: Chris, give the GLCW fans a few words. I mean, that's why I'm here, right? CDC: Puto, silencio! [Sub-title translation: Bi-ch, keep quiet!] CG: Listen, I don't have to be here, Chris. You told me to come.. CDC: Vaya por dios.. Tu bien. [Sub-title translation: Well, I'll be damned.. You're right.] [Christopher grins and continues to speak.] CDC: Qué usted necesitan saber? [Sub-title translation: What do you need to know?] CG: I don't NEED to know anything. Just tell us a little about yourself? CDC: Bien, le diré que todo que usted necesita saber; Soy un mejicano, soy un Luchador, y soy luchador mejor que cualquiera de usted soñaba siempre con ser. Crecí para arriba con él, yo pago mis cuentas con él, él soy mi vida, él soy lo que lo hago. [Sub-title translation: Well, I'll tell you everything you need to know; I'm a Mexican, I'm a Luchador, and I'm a better wrestler than any of you ever dreamed of being. I grew up with it, I pay my bills with it, it's my life, it's what I do..] CG: Isn't that being a little conceded, slick? Seriously, you're headed for the Great Lakes Championship Wrestling. We have some of the best wrestling talent in the world. Can you hang? [Chris laughs and begins to turn his head away from the road in front of him to look straight at the camera. He shakes his head, directs his attention back to the road, and speaks.] CDC: Usted nunca ha visto la lucha mejicana, tiene usted? los latinos de clase baja de Us tienen más talento entonces bastante mucho todo de la población de GLCW. Most todo de la sociedad de lucha americana, para esa materia. I era apenas afortunado bastante ser recogido y ser lanzado abajo aquí en la " corriente principal. " Ím no aquí a permanecer, no todavía de todos modos. Ím apenas aquí para darle un poco gusto del americano sobre de qué Lucha está la lucha todo. [Sub-title translation: You have never seen Mexican wrestling, have you? Us lower-class latinos have more talent than pretty much all of the GLCW population. Most all of the American wrestling society, for that matter. I was just lucky enough to be picked up and thrown down here into the "mainstream." I'm not here to stay, not yet anyway. I'm just here to give you American's a little taste of what Lucha wrestling is all about.] CG: You're right, I haven't. But what you're saying hardly seems realistic. I mean, we have CUCO Travieso touring the lakes right now and he's one of your kind. Yeah, he's talented, but he's hardly anything to gape in awe at. CDC: Bien, ése es CUCO Travieso para usted. Él no es la misma casta que mí. Convengo, él entre tiene definitivamente talento, pero hay una diferencia él e I. Utilicemos su máscara como ejemplo -- no hay necesidad de ninguna máscara de ocultar detrás. Necesidad del dont I cualquier máscara de desfilar mi tradición. Heche una ojeada mí, heche una ojeada buen la tradición. Tome una ojeada en ella en la CARNE. [Sub-title translation: Well, that's CUCO Travieso for you. He's not the same breed as me. I agree, he definitely has talent, but there's a difference between him and I. Let's use his mask as an example -- There's no need for any mask to hide behind. I dont need any mask to parade my tradition. Take a look at me, take a good look at tradition. Take a peek at it in the FLESH.] [Christopher takes one hand off the wheel and uses it to adjust his crotch. The cameraguy extends his hand out to Chris and taps him on the shoulder.] CG: Uhm, we're still rolling here, Chris. CDC: Sé, tan qué? [Sub-title translation: I know, so what?] CG: Well, forget it. Is that all you'd like to share with us? CDC: No, de que no es él -- tengo una cosa a la izquierda a decir. Es un millenium nuevo, es un Año Nuevo, y es un GLCW nuevo. Puede ser que no esté aquí para de largo pero estaré aquí, no obstante. Reloj usted mismo, GLCW. Mire sus partes posterioras y prepárese para un cambio. [Sub-title translation: No, that's not it -- I have one thing left to say. It's a new millennium, it's a new year, and it's a new GLCW. I might not be here for long but I'll be here, nonetheless. Watch yourself, GLCW. Watch your backs and prepare for a change..] [The truck pulls into a parking lot and parks in front of a medium sized building with the words "Great Lakes Championship Wrestling" emblonized on the side. Just before stepping out of the truck Christopher turns to the camera for the last time. He winks his right eye and cockily grins just as he steps out and closes his car door.
