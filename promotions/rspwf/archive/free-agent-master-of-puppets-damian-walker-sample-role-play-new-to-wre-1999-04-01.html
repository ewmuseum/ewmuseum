[FREE AGENT] "Master Of Puppets" Damian Walker[SAMPLE ROLE-PLAY][NEW TO WRESTLING, WAS WRESTLING IN GUESTBOOK FEDERATIONS, YET NOW WISHING TO JOIN A BOOKED FEDERATION]

1 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(SCENE: The cameras open as the sign of the Doll's House just fades out. A soft pindrop sound is then heard as tiny little feet scampering across the floor is then heard. The steps of the Doll's house then begin to creak. A rather short figure is then seen walking down the winding steps. He then pauses, looks out of the window and then just with an evil smile grins. The figure then walks over to a cupboard, he then opens one of the cupboard doors, peers in and then lets out a shriek. The camera then sees that he has blood dripping from his hand. The figure then quickly removes his hand and then begins to speak.)
MOP: "..Ouch. They are not supposed to do that. I guess they are aggitated that nobody is listening to them..So now, it's time for Master Of Puppets to be heard. Starting right here next Friday. [Master Of Puppets then carves into a block of wood using his blood, the word SANDMAN, he then just grins, before continuing.] Johnny Sandman, I came into the TWO to gain the true understanding of what it's like to be in control. For, you can say all you want about this and that, for that is all it is, Words..Words mean nothing to me, for it is my actions which breed your inner most thoughts. For it is through these very deeds that the TWO will begin to realise why I am the
MASTER and why I PULL YOUR STRINGS.."
["Master Of Puppets" Damian Walker then just this time carefully opens the cupboard and then seen seated on the top shelf is Blade. Blade has on a little black trenchcoat and a little knife is placed onto his arm instead of a hand.
Blade then makes a whirring sound and then begins ina soft tiny voice to speak.] BLADE: "..Master, is it time yet? Can I once again be made alive? Oh Master, this TWO, it can show us nothing. Why are we here? We need flesh, flesh to be torn, is the place where we shall tear apart their flesh, their hearts and then make them like us, make them into us, make them Puppets? [Blade then with an evil snarl then just make his eyes show their full redness. The
Puppet Master then just stares at Blade, his eyes focused on the red parts of
Blade's eyes. The Puppet Master then just makes a strange whirring sound and then begins to speak.]
MOP: "..Now Blade, now is not the time. Soon, yes very soon, the TWO shall realise just what the powers of the minds eye are all about. Yet for now, we shall leave them in peace, eternal peace. Johnny Sandman, all you say is that which you know not to be true.
There is no way that you shall stop me on my quest to show the TWO what the meaning of "pulling people's strings" is all about. For Sandman, once the order is given, the knife shall be made sharp. Your flesh shall be torn and then Johnny, nothing shall be able to stop the power of The Master Of
Puppets[Evil grin.] You can only try to imagine what I am feeling and what I am bringing right now, but you shall never be free, for you can't fool me
..As you were raised from the sands of the earth. The living vessel, your vessel, your mind, come Friday, all I can do is screw it up so bad that you have no choice but to call me by my title. You shall call me The Puppet
Master, for that is exactly what I am. Now Johnny, you shall be the first who I make claim to having control of their vessels. All your thoughts, words and doings. Nothing I shall not know of.[A soft cry is heard from the cupboard, The Puppet Master then just opens up the door and then seen seated wearing a tiny pair of checked trousers, having a solid china face is Janus. He then looks on and then just fires one of his guns in the air.]
Janus: "..Master, Master, is it time for the TWO to get burned. Is it time for the takeover ceremony?Oh Master let it be known that The Puppets and The
Master of Puppets form only what the mind can handle. The mind being the channel which we all communicate through. Let me take Sandman, for I know I can do it.[The eys of Janus then just begin to show blood. The Puppet Master then pats him on the head and then sits him on his shoulder, saying these last few words as he does so.]
MOP: "..Janus, Janus, Janus, as much as I would like for that to be the case. I see no real reason why Johnny Sandman poses a real threat yet. But Friday
Night, Johnny Sandman shall now just what its like to be manipulated by the master of all manipulation. I shall take him under my wing, shed him a little light, carve him into an image of Blade and then I shall be triumphent, ruling all over this excuse of a domain..All of you shall know, all of you shall fear, for now you shall heat the dumbbells ringing..Listening deeply to the Master's voice and then you Johnny Sandman, you shall BE PREAPRED FOR THE
TAKEOVER.." ["Master Of Puppets" Damian Walker then just kneels down and then picks up
Blade. Blade then just with an evil smile then just closes his eyes.
Janus then does the same."Master Of Puppets" Damian Walker then just opens the door of the Doll's House, where then he puts back the
Puppets, saying goodnight. It then fades to black as the streaky shadow of his disappears back up the creaky winding stairs.]
