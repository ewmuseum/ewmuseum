[FLASH] Brad "The Marksman" McNeil - Lemme know what'ch think!

2 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my new character... Lemme know what'cha think of 'im. ;-) --- [The scene opens up... We see nothing but complete darkness... After a few seconds... An extremely bright light comes on... It shines right into the camera... Telling by the brightness and size of the light... It looks like a spotlight... It shines right in our faces... But all of a sudden, we see an image walking inside it... It's a man, but we can't quite make out who it is... We just see his dark outline... The light then begins to move... It is now glowing above the man, shining down upon him... We can now see who he is... It's none other than BRAD "THE MARKSMAN" McNEIL... He's wearing a black leather jacket... A "CWC SATURDAY NIGHT LIVE" T-shirt... Hilfiger blue jeans... Himberland hiking boots... And a pair of round lense Mossimo sunglasses covering his eyes... His long, brown hair is slicked back and not in it's neatest form... McNeil stands there... He appears to be seirous...
The glare of the spotlight is seen in his glasses and shadows on his face are clearly visisble... After a few seconds... He begins to speak calmly and convinsingly...]
BRAD McNEIL: As the days go by, Saturday gets closer... Closer by teh second, as a matter of fact. And the closer it gets, the more you worry...
Christian Madison. And worry you should... Because in less than a week you'll have to climb in the ring with a man that's not only _BETTER_ than you are...
But you'll be climbing in the ring with a man that's _SMARTER_ than you are. When I beat you Saturday night, it won't be a surprise to anyone... Not even to you. And I'll tell you why... Because it'll be the second time in one week. Tuesday... And then again Saturday... Sorry to have to single-handedly destroy your win-loss record.
[McNeil smirks... He pauses for a few seconds and continues on...] BRAD McNEIL: Madison... Whether it be in the CWC or the ICW... You don't stand a chance against me, and you know it. I'm not tryin' to be cocky and I'm not tryin' to be big-headed... Just stating the truth. We'll be in the House of Horrors in Chicago, Illinois... It's perfect. Not only is Chicago a great city to wrestle in... But the arena is perfect, as well. Because Madison...
When I'm done with you, it _WILL_be your House of Horrors... And in that house... I'll be the ringmaster.
[Again... McNeil grins... But remains serious and calm... He continues...] BRAD McNEIL: Like I've said before, "Spotlight"... "THE MARKSMAN" has you in his sights... And when he aims and fires... He's gonna put _YOUR_
"Spotlight"... _OUT!_ [The spotlight goes out at that very second... We see nothing... But can hear Brad McNeil talk...]
BRAD McNEIL: And I _WON'T_ miss, Madison. Because... . . . I ' M A S U R E S H O T ! ! ! And... . . . I N E V E R M I S S ! ! ! [The dark screen fades out and into teh CWC logo... Then it fades to black.] "Dangerous" Dusty Love -IWC [20-5-3] Brad "The Marksman" McNeil -ICW [0-0-0] President of the IEWC E-Mail me to join! "Believe me sweety... I got enough down \HERE/ to feed the needy!" - Dusty
