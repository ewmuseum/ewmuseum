{Free agenT} SledgE

15 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Screen fades in on a biker bar. In walks two people, a male and a female. The female looks dazzling, in ripped blue jeans, biker boots, and a red halter top.
She has long blonde hair and blue eyes. She is that girl that you wouldn't expect to be with the male she is with. The male also sticks out, but more like a sore thumb than anything else. He has black horn-rimmed glasses, a white button down with pocket protector, black slacks and black street shoes.
Attached to the pocket protector is a name tag that says "Hi, my name is Orville." Some laughs are heard from all directions at this male, this Orville. Suddenly, a much larger man, the stereotypical biker, walks up to the female and starts to speak...::
Biker: Hey, baby! Want to dance? Female: Sure! Orville, I am gonna have fun, be good now, ok hun? Orville: Um, ok. ::the female and the biker go off and start dancing. Orville just watches them. She does it all the time, she runs off with some guy, they wind up screwing, then she comes back to him. All because she knows he will take her back. He watches them dance as the biker's hand starts to fondle the female's rear. He seen this before, he never liked it, but what can he do? At 5'9", 190 lbs, he wasn't much able to do anything. But this time, he decides to walk up to the biker. He is tired of this garbage, he doesn't seem to care that the biker is about 6'8" and weighs a rock solid 290 lbs. He pushes the big man, who turns and looks at him, almost amused. Orville decides to show backbone for what may be the first time in his life...::
Orville: Alright, you big musclehead, don't touch her there, dammit! ::The biker pushes Orville back, almost throwing him into a nearby table. He broke the poor guy's glasses, the big bully! He walks over to the little guy and bends over him.::
Biker: Do you know who you are talking to? I am Sludge, the baddest son of a b*tch in this bar. I do and say whatever the hell I feel like doing and saying.
And no one, especially not you, will tell me what to do, you got that shrimp? Now, if you don't have anything else to say, Me and your hoochie have some unfinished business to take care of...
::Sludge turns around and starts back to where the female is. Meanwhile, Orville kickstands and throws down his glasses. He walks right behind Sledge and delivers a Rabbit Punch to the base of his skull. The big man staggers, then he turns to see who hit him, but he can't, as he was temporarily blinded by the shot.
Orville: NO ONE CALLS ME SHRIMP!!! ::He uses the opportunity to unleash a good ol' fashioned kick to tha balls, making Sludge drop to his knees in agony! Orville then dims his running lights with a three inch punch to the temple. The crowd sits in a hushed silence as
Orville walks over to the female, who is sceaming at him like he killed a
Backstreet Boy. He just looks at her, angrily.::
Orville: Shut up, Bitch! I'm tired of your crap! ::He throws his name tag and pocket protector to the ground as he slowly walks out the door and turns, hearing a request to wait up. A guy his size walks up to him, beaming.::
Guy: Thanks, man! That dude stole my girl from me a month ago! It was only a matter of time before someone stood up to him. So, what's your name?
Orville: O-o-o- ::he suddenly hears a Peter Gabriel song from a passing Tempo. A better name comes to mind...::
Orville: Sledge... Guy: Sledge? the name's Herring, nice to meet ya! How did you learn to fight like that? Orville: My girl wasn't paying attention to me, so I started hitting the weights. One day, some muscle-head called me out to a fight, a much bigger guy.
We fought, who am I kidding? He beat the tar out of me until some guy smaller than me walked up and knocked him out with a sock. Of course, the sock had a brick in it, but that is not the point. The little guy showed me the easiest way to survive was to use any means necessary to hurt them. I used it the next day, when some weight lifter called me a shrimp. He left me aside to attempt a world record powerlift. I was so irritated, I waited until he had the bar over his head and then slammed an 80-lb. weight into his leg. he crumbled under the shot, the bar just fell on him and crushed his ribs. Folks called me a p*ssy, but I didn't care. This p*ssy wasn't gonna get pushed around anymore! And after an incident with some hard-ass bodybuilder, which I dealt with by kicking him as hard as I could in the elbow while he was benchpressing 400 lbs, no one was willing to do so. It was around then I started Pit fighting. There I learned many different holds and strikes. I may look like a dork, but I can take care of anyone that gets in my way...
Herring: Cool! Have you ever considered Pro-Wrestling? Orville: Me, a Pro-Wrestler? Don't I need to be that Sludge guys size? Herring: Not at all, man! I Wrestle, and I am pretty good! Of course, I am not as vicious as you are, Sledge. C'mon, let me show ya how it is done...
::Herring entered a grappling stance as he approached him.:: The first thing you do is this... ::he attempts to do a collar and elbow, but as his arms are stretched out a bit, Orville just slams his forearm between the guy's eyes. Herring is rocked, he drops to his knee. Orville pulls him up, and the guy throws him in a front-face lock, a bit angry. Orville responds in kind. Then Herring attempts to knee him in the ribs, but Orville catches his leg. He then lifts him up for what looks like a fisherman's suplex, but instead drops him on his skull, knocking him out with a nasty Fisherman's Buster. Orville stands, his eyes are cold. Something in him was awakened, something he usually only felt when Pit fighting. He kicked Herring over, who was bleeding from his forehead and convulsing from the impact. He looked down on him, face devoid of emotion.::
Orville: I know all about wrestling, kid. I see them all the time, acting like they are the baddest in the Pit. Those are the one's I like the best. I actually break a sweat against them, until I throw an ankle lock on them and almost rip their foot off. I'll do your wrestling thing, or my name's not
Sledge...
::Orville walks out into the distance, as the screen fades to black.:: ___________________________________________________________________ It's been a while, so I appologize if this tidbit totally sucks!
