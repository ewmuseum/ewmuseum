Thoughts?

23 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since I find myself in need of a Fed, I thought I better bone up before applying elsewhere. Below is a copy of one of the flashes I've done. Actually it was more like a show I've done. For a time I was doing a weekly review show for a Fed and here is one of them.
I was going to ask for a critique, but was worried the [name withheld] monster may swallow me whole. SO I asked for thoughts instead.
Ahh, to hell with it. Go ahead do your worst, I have big shoulders. Anyone who wishes to tear it apart go ahead (this includes you Nick). I hope someone gets some enjoyment out of this. A little background really quick. The character's name is Nightmare. He began as a man who could invade your dreams and discover your greatest fear and use it against you. After about a month I decided that gimmick sucked and publicly changed the character. I stated
(with permission) how the Prez had given me this stupid gimmick and how I didn't need it anymore. We then started a feud that lasted until the Fed closed. I have since considered changing the name but
I'm worried that would be like creating a new character and I'm reasonably comfortable with this one. Anyway on with the story...
*****The studio darkens and the prompt begins flashing applause.***** Announcer: "We're coming to you live from WOLF Headquarters!" *****The NightmareTron lowers over the stage as 'Legs' begins playing over the studio speakers.***** Announcer: "Please give a warm welcome to the CO-host of the Howling, the lovely Miss Buffy!"
*****As Buffy walks through the curtains her video begins playing. The guys in the audience cheer wildly. A flattered Buffy begins dancing as the cheers last several minutes.*****
Buffy: "Thank you so much, you're all wonderful. I've only been around a couple of weeks, but from what I've seen we have the sexiest fans around."
*****The guys roar their appreciation.***** Buffy: "And I think The Howling may be the best damn program on television, thanks to that wonderful hunk of a host we have. What do you say?"
*****Scattered applause from the audience, except for a young girl up front who screams "I love you Nightmare."***** Buffy: "You can do better than that. Now, come on! I want to hear some cheering for the man who set the standard in professional wrestling."
*****Nightmare struts out on stage to a deafening reception. He tries to speak, but is cut off as the cheers grow louder. Jake Firestarter's head has just slammed into the mat. And the audience loves it!***** Studio Audience: "Firestarter sucks! Firestarter sucks!" Nightmare: "Firestarter sucks! Firestarter sucks!" [Chuckles.] "Okay, that's enough Kenny." *****Nightmare walks from the stage over to his desk and has a seat. Buffy is beside him in her chair. She is grinning wildly.***** Buffy: "Nightmaremania sweeps the nation!" Nightmare: "More like Buffymania." Buffy: "But you're so cute." Nightmare: [Smiling.] It was a good week for WOLF, wasn't it?" Buffy: "Another successful week for the World Tour. We were in Mongolia this week and it was quite a show. I doubt though, that we'll ever top Hong Kong."
Nightmare: "Ah yes, the night Exile ruled the roost." Buffy: "Love that Exile." Nightmare: "Hopefully then, you'll love this as well. I've prepared a little Off-Broadway production in honor of the return of a certain wrestler we could all do without. And unfortunately for those fans who aren't deaf, dumb and blind, he's brought a friend."
Buffy: "Off-Broadway? I didn't know you were a writer." Nightmare: "Writer and Director! And I make a guest appearance near the end. I've titled my little production 'Bran Flake, his buddy
Pooper and the dreaded Flush!' Buffy: "Don't keep us waiting." Nightmare: "Kenny, the disclaimer if you will." Announcer: "The following production expresses the opinions of Nightmare and is not in any way a reflection of the opinions of WOLF or the crew of The Howling. The participants in the following production are all actors and should not be mistaken for the real people."
Nightmare: "Lights, camera...Action! *****The studio grows dark and a spotlight shines on the center of the stage. Music begins to play softly, and grows slowly louder as the curtain parts.***** Bran Flake: [In Falsetto.] "I'm just a poor boy, nobody loves me." [Moves into the spotlight.] Pooper: [In Tenor.] "He's just a poor boy from a poor family. Spare him his life from this monstrosity!" [Moves into the spotlight.]
*****Bran Flake and Pooper are dressed in matching pink tights and ballet shoes. They are without shirts, revealing potbellies and hairy backs. Both men are bald.*****
Nightmare: [Narrating while the song continues softly in the background.] "This monstrosity they speak of is the evil, dreaded...Nightmare! Long has the Bran feared this mighty being.
This all began during their days in the little Indy Fed known as the OWA." Bran Flake: [In Falsetto.] "...Anyone can see." Pooper: [In Tenor.] "Nothing really matters..." Bran Flake: [In Falsetto.] "Nothing really matters..." Together: [Harmonizing.] "...To meeeeee!" *****They begin dancing wildly across the stage; Jumping and twirling to and fro. The stage grows dark, and crew members begin moving props and setting a new scene.*****
Nightmare: [Narrating.] "During his early days in the OWA, Bran Flake was green with envy. Nightmare was the World Champion and the Bran was not. Bran Flake moaned, whined and complained but was never good enough to earn a title shot."
*****The stage brightens revealing a sad faced Bran Flake sitting alone deep in thought.***** Nightmare: [Narrating.] "Nightmare was eventually stripped of his title by the evil President, but the Bran was not happy. He was scared, and wanted the all powerful Nightmare gone."
Bran Flake: [Visibly shaking.] "The Bran fears Nightmare!" Nightmare: [Narrating.] "The time came when the two were to meet in the ring. In the days leading up to the match Bran Flake whined and complained, and then whined some more."
*****Bran Flake stands and the music for Matchbox Twenty's 'If You're Gone' starts.***** Bran Flake: [In Falsetto.] "I think I've already lost this. I think you've already won. I think I'm finally scared now. I know I'm weak...Please pinch my bum!"
Nightmare: [Narrating.] "The Bran had issues. Everyone knew. Then came the day of the match and controversy raged. Bran Flake pinned
Nightmare inciting riots. Religious groups all over the world demanded answers. It was revealed weeks later that the evil President had fixed the match. The referee had been bribed and the three count was lightening fast."
*****During the narration Bran Flake had been dancing, jumping and cheering.***** Bran Flake: "I won! I won! I won...I won...I won!" *****Until it was revealed the match had been fixed.***** Bran Flake: [Sitting back down in his chair.] "I still suck." *****The stage grows dark as the scene changes again.***** Nightmare: [Narrating.] Here in WOLF, it has been much the same. More complaining, more whining...More Bran!" Bran Flake: "Even in a new federation, nobody likes me." Nightmare: [Narrating.] "An evil plot is hatched and the Bran recruits someone dumb enough to follow him. Bran Flake has the idea to train this idiot savant and unleash him upon an unsuspecting WOLF."
*****As the stage grows dark, Bran Flake holds a cardboard cut out of a light bulb above his head.***** Nightmare: [Narrating.] "They train in secret for a month before revealing themselves with a sneak attack on a dopey Ground Nothing.
During an interview later that week they had some harsh words." Bran Flake: "My secret weapon is now revealed." *****Pooper emerges from the shadows.***** Pooper: "I am the one called Pooper. All will fall prey to the strength of my body odor! Behold the power that is Pooper, and behold my feared Flush!"
*****From the side a man walks out wearing no clothes. [Television censors do not allow certain views.] He holds a sign up that reads 'Zero'.***** Ground Nothing: "I am the World Champion here. How dare you attack me! How dare you challenge my place!"
Pooper: "You too will fall victim to my feared Flush!" Nightmare: [Narrating.] "During this exchange Bran Flake sneaks up from behind and nails Ground Nothing with his patented Cheek Squeeze."
Ground Nothing: [Screaming in agony before collapsing.] "AAAHHHHHHHH!!!" *****Bran Flake and Pooper begin to pound on the helpless Ground Nothing. Nightmare appears on stage and overwhelms the Bran and Pooper, knocking them out with his Pleasant Dreams. He helps Ground Nothing to his feet.***** Ground Nothing: "Thank you mighty Nightmare." Nightmare: "Yea sure, but first point that thing somewhere else, and second why aren't wearing clothes?"
Ground Nothing: [Confused.] "I am Ground Nothing?" Nightmare: "Uhh...Okay?" *****Nightmare knocks Ground Nothing out with Pleasant Dreams as the curtain closes and the stage grows dark. Moments later, a spotlight shines on the stage as Nightmare walks out from behind the curtains.
The music from Rudolph The Red Nosed Reindeer begins to play.***** Nightmare: "You know Exile and Seizure and Taco and Phrost Bite..." *****The curtain opens, the lights brighten and a group of men walk single file on to the stage wearing pink tights, ballet shoes and signs with their names.*****
Nightmare: "...Pooper and Bran Flake, Counselor and Nothing..." *****The line of men on stage roughly equals the roster of WOLF. They begin to sing and dance, linked arm in arm and kicking like a chorus line.***** Group: "...But do you recall, the most famous wrestler of all?" *****Nightmare walks to the center of the stage and bows. The music changes.***** Group: "You've got to know when to hold 'em. Know when to fold 'em. Know when to walk away...Know when to run." *****The music stops again as the group walks to the front of the stage and begin to yank at their pink tights. Off come the tights, revealing blue tights and red shorts. Then, in one choreographed movement the group tear open their shirts displaying blue shirts with a big 'S' on the middle. The theme from Superman starts.*****
Group: "Look, up in the sky...It's a bird...It's a plane...It's...It's..."
*****Nightmare had disappeared, but right on cue he lowers from the rafters wearing a red cape.***** Group: [Bowing.] "...It's Nightmare!" *****The stage turns dark as most of the group moves backstage. Two spotlights turn on and shine on the two that remain...Bran Flake and Pooper.***** Bran Flake: [In Falsetto.] "...Anyone can see." Pooper: [In Tenor.] "We don't really matter..." Bran Flake: [In Falsetto.] "We don't really matter..." Together: [Harmonizing.] "...Do weeeeeeee!" *****The curtain closes and Nightmare hops up on to stage and begins to bow. The studio Audience gives him a standing ovation.***** Buffy: [Over the thunderous applause.] "I hate to say it, but I think we're out of time. If you don't yet know the results of this week's matches, please head over to the WOLF website."
*****The Howling goes off the air as the NightmareTron lowers playing Nightmare's video. Buffy is crying.*****
