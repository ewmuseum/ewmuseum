[Critique] Hushia Montaki... #2 RSPWF RP ...

23 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
-X Scene X- The scene opens up inside of the Russian Airport where people are rushing back and forth during their own things. The camera man turns to his side to show
Hushia Montaki sitting there. Hushia Montaki is working at a piece of pizza.
Montaki is wearing a Chinese Dragon Flag of 1890 t-shirt, along with a pair of black, silk shirts. He has his long black hair pulled back in a pony tail and doesn't have his face paint on...for the very first time. Hushia Montaki wipes his mouth off and looks into the camera and begins to speak.
-X Hushia Montaki -X Well, well, well....look who has showed up again. The man none other than
ThaDawg187. Dawg, I'm happy you got out of jail. I'm just so overjoyed that I can finally wrestle you at nWc's 'Boiling Point.' I mean, when I think of it I get a tingle feeling all through my body. Why? Mostly because I know I'm going to win the nWc Cruiserweight Championship. It can't be that hard to beat you anyways. Now, Dawg. I saw your interview. You shouldn't have had it, it only made you look foolish. But, I guess I'm going to have to respond to it. Here goes nothing.
ThaDawg187, your right. I want that Cruiserweight Championship just as much as you. And I will stop at no limits to make sure that I get it. I don't care what you say about how you are still going to be Champion. Dawg, it would have been alot better if you would have just stayed in the dark. First off, I know that you aren't going to rip me to shreads. There is no doubt about that. So far, nobody in the nWc shows the potential to rip me to shreads....well, maybe other than Shane Michaels. But, Dawg, you know Michaels can tear you to pieces too!
Dawg, after this match. I may just talk about how horrible it was to step into the ring with you. I may just talk about how hardcore you are. But, when I do all of that takling you can bet that I'll do it...WITH STYLE...No, that isn't it! HOLD ON! You can bet that I'll do it as the.....CRUISERWEIGHT CHAMPION!! I can see it now. We are both going to be in hositpital beds across from each other, mouthing off to each other while I flash the Title to you! How sweet would that be? Very sweet on my part.....a cold day in hell for you! Dawg, saying that you have more talent then me is something that you can't really say truthfully right now. Yeah, maybe when I wrestled Jason Deegan or Josh Ritter it looked like I had less talent then you, but that is because Jasson Deegan and Josh Ritter were both terrible wrestlers. I couldn't show much talent on them until they were no longer able to move. You, on the other hand, I'll be able to show my talent on you. I know that you have massive amounts of talent, and I know I do too. We will just have to see who has the most. Dawg, you can try to keep that title around your waist as long as you can...but this Saturday it is going to be coming off of you. And when I get it, I expect it to be polished 'till it shines! Dawg, you are just making your self like stupider than shit. You know that? Saying that I'm the worst wrestler in the nWc? Damn, if I'm the worst wrestler in the nWc...why am I 2-0 and why am I getting a
Cruiserweight Title shot? Answer that without making same lame excuse up. You can't!
Dawg, saying that the United States is ten times better then China is something
I could care less about. China is in my heart, the United States is in yours.
Talk trash to China all you want...see if I care. It isn't like I'm going to break down and cry like you are after I beat you Saturday. As for people being on my side. They may want me to beat you, but that isn't saying that they like me. For all we know, they probably hate both of us. They just want me to win because they hate you. Simple enough, or is your skull too thick to comprehend that? Ah, screw it! Dawg, when you talk about the Great Buda in that manner, you know he is going to deal you a great amount of pain. It may not be from me, although I'm going to beat you...but the Great Buda will only make your life that much more miserable. As for the United States nuking China...they will only do that if they want every country in the World blowing YOU up!
Dawg, granted...maybe you set the challenge, but I MADE the match. I'm the one that agreed to it, the Board only signed it. But, then again, why the hell are we fighting over this? Does it really matter who made the match or not? But
Dawg, I have to respect what you said next. You said that you have given me the respect I deserve. Thank You! Atleast somebody realizes that I do have good potential here in the nWc. And Dawg....ah, nevermind. In China, we are known for our sumo wrestling. But, we are often under-noted for our wrestling skills.
Although, not too many people from China make it into the big time like me, but when we do...we put a show on! Dawg187, I drung your ass to Rome because in
Rome, like I said, would be neutral ground. Equal amount of our own heritage.
Get where I'm coming from? Probably not, but I'm sure everybody else in the nWc understands it.
Dawg, since people in the Battle Royal are saying that they are going to face you, you have gotten a big head. But now, people are starting to realize. I think I re-call Rain saying that the 'New Chinese person is going to kick
ThaDawg187's ass.' Rain, you are exactly right when you said that. So, Dawg, don't get your hopes to hight because I'm pretty sure in the next few days you will see Rain and Hushia Montaki wrestling in the ring for the nWc
Cruiserweight Championship. Dawg, you say you respect me even thought that isn't your kind of thing. Hmmm....I wonder if you will still respect me after I beat you? I wonder if you are a sore-loser in other words. Respect is a fertile thing...you gain it and lose it. I have gained it from you, so now I'm just waiting for the moment where I lose it from you. Simple enough.
Dawg, you are exactly right....you are the biggest challenge I have had so far. Look at the guys I have had to face before you. They were Industrial Loser. People that can't wrestle worth anything. You on the other hand, I'm going to like going up againest you. I will actually be able to let the world see me wrestle for real. I will do everything I wished I could have done againest
Jason Deegan and Josh Ritter. Hey, I have the time, don't I? I mean, this is a best out of three falls match. I can do whatever I what and still have time to do more! Dawg, you ramble to much. You know that? You talk about all this stuff about how you are going to put me six foot under when, in all reality, none of that is going to happen. Granted, you may get one pin fall on me...but I'm going to get two on you to become the nWc Cruiserweight Champion. I'm done!
-X Scene X- Hushia Montaki stands up and throws his plate away. He looks at his watch and begins to walk towards his plane entrance to make the final flight to
Rome....the time has come!
