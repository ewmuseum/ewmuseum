{-} [DreadZ] {-} ANOTHER ROLEPLAY...PLEASE CRITIQUE

7 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The scene opens up with the camara gliding across the lobby of a hotel, then goes through some double doors, and finally outside. Then
  we see the famous hummer limo of DreadZ, as the driver opens the door and shuts it after the camera gets in. Now it is total darkness.
The   camara view turns right and we notice the desk of DreadZ with the refrigerator right next to it. The camra view then turns to the left and
  notices that there seems to be some light, behind the hotbath curtains. We then notice the shadow outlines of two individuals, a male and
  a female. The camera zooms in and before it reaches there it stops to hear a talk with what sounds like N-Tice and DreadZ
  N-Tice: Hehehe.......that Greave character is such a loser...   DreadZ: Yeah, and the prez wants me to waste my time on him. I mean I don't know why I fight matches with no talent p*ssies, when people   know that I have better things to do with my life, life wrestle with you....   N-Tice:Hmmmmmmm.....why don't we get star....   DreadZ:Wait, hold up...   N-Tice:What's wro...   N-Tice gets interrupted when DreadZ swings the curtains open to reveal the camara that had been spying on them and listening to every
  word they hear. DreadZ reaches to the platform on his right and takes his cup of martini...
  DreadZ: You know...the one thing in my life that I really, really despise...it would have to be GOD DAMN SPIES!!!!
  DreadZ then takes a sip, and swallows...   DreadZ: Ironic isn't it...you little nosy bastard, bring your crack @$$ into my joint, zone into my meeting, and listen to every damn word I
  have to say. But you see, the UCW World Champ is a very lenient guy, and understands. And I understand your situation. Just like every
  one of the Dread One's fans, you want a little piece of me, and it is all natural. So I am going to let you off the hook. In fact, you just came in
  at the right time, because just like you have heard, that words need to get out and straight up Greave's.....you know the rest....
  DreadZ takes one last sip of his martini and rests it back on the platform next to him...   DreadZ: Now, let's get onto business. first my little tidbits, and then onto the grand tamale. And my first so called, "tidbit", goes out to a
  certain man...or should I say boy. Yes, a certain boy by the man of SoRrOw. Yes, SoRrOw. I would like to ask you just one question, and a
  followup....Who in the hell do you think you are, and how dare you try to put yourself in the business of the World Champ. My theory says
  that, when you stick your nose in the wrong business, especially my business, especially my business, it will, and I repeat, it will get tossed
  straight into the Dread End, first class nonstop stop flight. And let me guarantee you that you will deal with me, and I promise you, that you   will be so filled with regret that you ever messed with me, that you will be hearing the name....DreadZ...DreadZ...haunt you all over until you
  come to pass. It's a guarantee...   And now onto my the big business, the grand tamale...Good ol' Greave. Yes the man with a broken house. The abondoned boy. The guy with   his so called "lords", and finally the wannabe who thinks he has what it takes at winning my belt, which indeed he will most likely fail,
  taking all the past experience. And he wants to take stats...well here's how it looks....I'm on top, Greave on the bottom. And here he goes,
  dropping...and dropping...until finally, the Dread End. Greave, you got two choices, you drop by yourself, or we do it the fast way, via the
  handy-crafty work of the Dread Master.   Now onto our match. You say that you sign a "Crucifixion Match" with me, well I turn back and say step up to the plate and bring it on if you   got any balls in that single brain cell body of yours. And when I say single brain that means one brain cell. I say that because you are the   dumbest guy to ever walk in the face of DreadZ. First of, by taking the challenge to face me, the champ may I add; and then you sign some
  dumb crucifixion match, which just doesn't really do anything, but tempt me more to kick the living hell out of you. All the hell of you.
Then   you go on to tell me to thank my buddy Alex Kaos...and that is exactly what I am going to do. I thank him for signing this match because he
  was smart. Knowing that I would eventually beat you with the 1...2...3 count, he brought up the Crucifixion Match, which now I would be able   to drop you to the ground via Dread End, then raise you up on the cross, bowing down to your master....the Dread One. So Greave, get ready
  because at UCW Blood & Bones, it's my night once again to bring to the live audience and the first time for you to experience
  Dread-ageddon...   DreadZ then closes the curtains as the screen fades to black
