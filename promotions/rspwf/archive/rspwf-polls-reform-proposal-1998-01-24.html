[RSPWF Polls] SERIOUS IDEAS...PLEASE READ.

24 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Jan 24, 1998 — HooHaa29] My ideas.....for a way to make RSPWF "official" polls." The RSPWF has all of these polls. Why don't they have 3 or 4 official polls. And these "official" polls won't over ride each other. Here's my idea. 1. Keep Gib's Top 200. Gib does a tremendous job and should be commended on it. He was super fast this year and it was a class A production. 2. Keep the Top 200 Tag Team (not sure who did it this). As for these two. Keep them the way they are. They are perfect and they are well respected. I think they should stay the way they are and not be changed....As for the other ideas. 3. Brock's RSPWF Awards. Great concept, just IMO, need a little bit of change. Like personally, I never saw a nomination ballot or the actual ballot until it was to late. And then his computer crash and not a lot of time to re vote. So, the idea is there...but it needs some work IMO. Now, here's what I think. Come up with 15-20 (25-27 is to many for one tabulator, IMO) Categories (plus the original awards...) BUT, in these categories, I would say don't have Wrestler and tag team of the year. This way, these awards don't step on the others awards. I think that the categories, other than the wrestler and tag team of the year, should be kept. Here would be my suggestions for the categories... 1. Best Face of the Year 2. Best Heel of the Year 3. Best Brawler 4. Best Worker 5. Best Technical Wrestler 6. Most Popular Wrestler 7. Most Improved Wrestler 8. Best Rookie 9. Best finishing Move 10. Best Match of the Year 11. Best Feud of the Year 12. Best Angle of the Year 13. Best Weekly Wrestling Show 14. Best Supercard or Pay Per View 15. Best Federation Head of the Year 16. Best E-Wrestling Federation of the Year Now those categories could stay with the RSPWF "Awards." So, now you have The RSPWF Top 200, RSPWF Tag Team Top 200 and The RSPWF Awards. Now, hmmm....we still have some big categories to go. So, let's think of a name. The RSPWF Front Office Awards. These awards will topple certain "inside" things of eWrestling. Here's the idea of which categories this will cover: 1. Jobber of the Year 2. Referee of the year 3. Ring Announcer of the Year 4. Interviewer of the year 5. Commissioner of the year 6. Manager of the Year 7. Match Announcer of the year 8. Color Commentator of the year AND.... 9. The RSPWF Achievement Award. Awarded to someone who has come forward to help out the sport of eWrestling. Someone like Daniel Spreadsbury and the IIWF. Someone who has really made eWrestling better. This can never be awarded to the same person and write in votes for this will NEVER be accepted. And Finally......seeing as they are all but ignored.... The RSPWF Light Heavyweight Top 50. This would be the ''official'' RSPWF awards for the people who wrestle at UNDER 240 pounds. This way, they may get shafted in the top 200, but they are big men in this award! So, there would be these "official" RSPWF Award shows. Done by different tabulators. Obviously, whoever Gib would keep the Top 200, whoever did the topp 200 Tags would keep that and Brock would keep the awards. Someone would have to step in and do the Front Office Awards and the Light Heavyweight Top 50. I myself would do one, so maybe someone else could do the other. The thing is, it would take all to agree. It would take Gib, Brock and all involved to say maybe this is the way to go "official." Then maybe the RSPWF awards could be like PWI or something. Where people reckognize these awards as the top of the line. So the "Officially reckognized RSPWF Awards" would be: RSPWF Top 200 (Gib) RSPWF Tag Top 200 (whoever does it...) RSPWF Awards (Brock) RSPWF Front Office Awards (open) RSPWF Light Heavyweight Top 50 (open) So that's my plan. Bash it or go with it. I would however, like people to give their thoughts. I think if we all get together and integrate and make all the polls so they don't step on each other, we could have some solid awards. Have them run from November to November. You have a month to vote (December) and all the Awards should come out around the same time as each other (early-mid January.) HOPEFULLY, we can all agree with this or maybe hash it out a little and come up with something all like. It would be an interesting topic. Please send feed back to me or to the newsgroup. Thanks for your time!
