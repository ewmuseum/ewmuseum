Free Agent - Sample RP's enclosed

29 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey below are 2 sample rp's.. I'm looking for one or two feds to join. I'm really looking for a fed with rp packets sent via e-mail, not message boards.
At least 2 cards a month, preferably done in wordpad or something similiar. (Posted on a webpage is fine too.) I can handle a late card if its a day or two, if your cards are weeks late then I won't be interested. I'm really just looking for a fed with play by play cards and handlers that get along and work well with each other. (Aren't we all?)
If interested e-mail me at [detail withheld] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ BOOM BOOM BRATZKE ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ CLANK..... CLANK..... CLANK..... [The sound of metal hitting metal is heard over and over again for a few seconds, the sounds of a man grunting to lift something heavy is heard in the background. The scene then fades in to show a small messy apartment, a local reporter is on the television talking about the local baseball team that is competing in the Little League World Series. Off to the side is a fake tree used mainly for decoration, hanging on that tree is an old dirty t-shirt that needs to be washed with a pile of dirty clothes underneath the tree. The camera pans to the side and you can see in to the kitchen, empty water bottles are on the counter and a few dishes are stacked up next to the sink ready to be washed. The camera then starts to pan back around the opposite way and zooms in on some papers laying on the floor in front of a couch. There is a bill for the phone, a cable bill, and a few other bills, all with delinquent notices on them.]
CLANK..... CLANK..... CLANK..... [Another grunt is heard and the camera pans around just in time to see a very strong looking but stocky man sit up on the bench. He has a goatee and his long black hair is pulled back into a ponytail. The man is wearing a black t-shirt that reads "Boom Boom" on it, sweat has drenched the shirt and he looks like he has just put in a good workout.
The man is known as Boom Boom Bratzke, a young wrestler who has been training for a while now and is looking to break into the business. Boom
Boom gets up from his weight bench and makes his way over to the couch and takes a seat. Boom Boom looks down at the bills laying on the floor and then leans his head back onto the couch as if he is very frustrated.
Thinking to himself.....] Boom Boom : I'm so tired of living in this shit hole. The bills are past due, I have people calling my house all the time, everyone trying to get money out of me, I'm really tired of this crap.
[Boom Boom sits up on the couch, resting his elbows on his knee's and looking at his bills.]
Boom Boom: I've busted my ass during training, I've learned all that the wrestling schools can teach me. Now is the time that I need to catch on somewhere, now is the time that I should be paying my dues. I send in my tapes,
I follow up by calling the offices, I do everything that is neccessary, yet I don't get picked up. Everywhere I go it's the same thing, they tell me "we loved your tape but we already have too many people on our roster." This sucks!
[Boom Boom gets up off the couch and walks over to the refridgerator. He opens the door and grabs a water bottle and walks back over and sits on the couch.]
Boom Boom: Wrestling sure has changed quickly over the past year. It went from a sport that was really popular publicly, to almost non existant. There are a ton of wrestlers out here trying to catch on somewhere, all hoping that today will be the day they hear something and get picked up. There are only a handful of quality federations out there, but loads of bush league promotions. I'm not opposed to starting out in the bush league's and paying my dues, but where are the quality indy promotions?
[Boom Boom reaches over and grabs a stack of sealed manilla envelopes, each one of them with "Return to Sender" and "No Longer Doing Business" stamped on them.]
Boom Boom: I send my tapes to some of the indy league's and they get returned to me because those promotions have already gone under. Too many people thinking they know what it takes to run a successful promotion and then once they get into it they realize it's much tougher than they thought, then they bail out. I guess I'm lucky in that sense, at least I haven't been picked up by a promotion only for it to close down. Talk about a roller coaster ride of excitement and disappointment.
[Boom Boom opens one of the returned envelopes and his tape slides out, he picks it up and sets it to the side as he begins to open the others to retrieve his tapes.]
Boom Boom: I guess I'll have to find some other promotions to send these tapes to in hopes of getting signed some where. Where are all of these developmental contracts that I hear so much about?
[The phone begins to ring, Boom Boom looks over at the caller ID feature on his phone and can see that it is the cable company calling about the money he owes them.]
Boom Boom: I need to call Barry and see when I'm getting paid for that house show we did last week. Thank god the locals and their kids will pay to see a small time show with a bunch of unknown wrestlers trying to learn the ropes.
That money isn't much but it at least it pays the bills. Guys gripe about only getting a hundred dollars a show but if you work a few shows it adds up. Sure we have to have a part time job somewhere else, but who said this was going to be easy?
[Boom Boom sits back on the couch and grabs his remote, flipping through the channels until he runs across a small time wrestling promotion show.]
Boom Boom: Look at these guys, I would mop the floor with them. All I need is a chance, just one chance at proving myself. Once I get that chance these guys are gonna pay. I'm not just just gonna beat them, I'm gonna hurt them. I'll prove that I'm the better man, and that all of these promotions were fools for not hiring me sooner.
=============== 2nd rp =============== [Scene opens to a run down apartment, a pile of dirty clothes is on the floor and dirty dishes are stacked up over by the kitchen sink. The camera pans around until is stops on a television, on the screen is none other than the RAW
World Champion, Saul Akiva. The camera then begins to pan around some more and stops on a coffee table where a RAWNewYork contract is seen laying there with an ink pen on top of it. The camera zooms in to reveal a signature, Boom Boom
Bratzke.
Suddenly you hear the sound of someone clearing their throat and the camera pans upward to show a very large, stocky but definately solid, man. The man has long black hair that is pulled back into a ponytail, his long black goatee is also pulled into a mini ponytail giving the impression that he is not your typical wrestler. The man is wearing a black t-shirt with a flaming logo centered, the logo is spelling out the name Boom Boom Bratzke.]
Boom Boom Bratzke: Raw New York, home of the great Saul Akiva. Well, Saul you better get ready because Raw New York is also not the home of Boom Boom
Bratzke. [Boom Boom pauses fora minute before beginning again.] Boom Boom Bratzke: I know my name means nothing to you, I know you have never heard of me and that's fine because I'm new to this business. That's right champ, I'm a rookie. But this is one rookie that you will see in the future, you can take that to the bank. I have been a fan of wrestling since I was a little kid, and in that time I have seen a lot of guys come into this business only to disappear a short time later. I am not going to be one of those guys.
I'm here for the long haul, I'm here to dominate. Now I know what you will say Saul, I know what everyone will say. Here is another rookie coming in shooting off at the mouth trying to pick on the top dog to make a name for himself. Well, I guess that would be true as of right this second because I am trying to make a name for myself. The difference though is that I am not picking a fight with you Saul, I'm just introducing myself. You see, I know I have to work my way up the ladder, and that's only something I am all too eager to do. I will go through each and every man that the suits put in front of me until I am sitting on top of the world.
I do want to point something out though Saul, you are the World Champion. You did _defeat_ everyone put in front of you, and you do _deserve_ to be champion.
I wanted to say that right now for a very important reason. I want people to give you credit, I want people to view you as a dominating champion. I also want you to hold on to that title for quite a while, so I wish you all the best of luck in retaining _your_ World title. Why do I want these things to be know and why am I wishing you luck? Because when all is said and done, when I work my way up that ladder to face you for that title, I want everyone to know that
I _defeated_ a real champion!
A lot of people are probably thinking that I am getting way ahead of myself, and that I need to prove something inside the ring first. Well, they are right and I will do just that. I will not be denied though and I can not be stopped.
I have a desire to be the best, and desire is what this business is all about. There have been rumors about fellow wrestlers being unhappy that you are champion, those same rumors also say that you are unworthy. I hope you squash those rumors by proving you are worthy but you know why those people think you are unworthy Saul? Desire. They don't see that desire that makes up a wrestler's character, and that is where all this dissention is beginning. When
I become the World Champion some day, those rumors won't exist. You know why
Saul? Because between now and then, people will see me battle with other wrestlers, they will see me defeat my opponents one after the other, and when all is said and done, they will know that my desire to be the best is why I am at the top. It will be that desire that will force people to know that I am the real deal, and that no one will deserve it more than I.
Rest up Saul, rest up. Boom Boom is in New York, and Boom Boom is here to make a namefor himself!
[Scene fades out as Boom Boom looks back at the television.]
