(Critique) Sample Roleplay for Phil Raven in MCW

24 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(To wrestlers looking to work in MCW, this is the quality we are looking for. Anything else will be sent down to MCWX, in the farm team. And oh yeah, what do you think ?)
http://mcw.icewolf.net (The Network's Locker Room...) Phil Raven is standing alone in the middle of The Network's locker room. It's just a few hours before Hurricane. He dresses up, he's now mid way in his pre show preparation. He puts on his black shorts and short black boots.
He then removes his white shirt to put on his wrestling tanktop for the night: The Lucky SFW Jersey, because he wants the show to go smoothly. He puts his elbowpad on, then go in front of the mirror and fix his hair. Then he goes back in his bag to pick up his MCW Championship belt....ooops...he remembers he's doesn't have a belt anymore. But he remembers a lot of things at the same time...he remembers when he won the World Championship in
Tournament at the start of the MCW's history, he remembers when he felt sick and had to forfeit the championship to T.Read, then, he remembers how he won it back from T.Read and how he defended against all comers thereafter. And now, all the MCW Championships have been made vacant. Phil wants the belt he never lost back, he does not want it around the waist of somebody that will not give it the proper respect or care or wear it with as much proud as he does. He gets out of the locker room, a lot is on his mind.
He gets out and starts walking toward the entrance area, where he will receive his final instructions from Paul and Rob.
All of a sudden, he feels something pulling down on his tanktop. He turns around and sees a kid with a piece of paper and a pen in hand.
Kid: "Can I have your autograph, Phil ?" Phil knows heels don't sign autographs. Even less when you are a member of the despicable Network. He looks down at the kid, he have a huge smile on his face. He looks past the kid and sees his mother who smile at him. Phil looks down at the kid and smiles. He takes the paper and pen and signs:
"Phil Raven, The Freebird". He give the paper to the kid and shakes his little hand. The mom comes forward:
Kid's Mom: "I am surprised you signed ! Aren't you with these evil guys ?" Phil: "Yeah...but I still care for the kids. What's his name ?" Kid's Mom: "He's named Kevin" Phil: "Kids like Kevin are my best fans. They believe in Phil Raven more than I do. I am sure he does not understand that I am not a good guy anymore. But I never turned my back on my fans, I turned my back on a group of wrestlers."
Kid's Mom: "Thank God, guys like you still are around ! I met with Alysian
Scottsfield earlier and he made fun at Kevin"
Phil: "I would never do that...(Looking down at the kid) Hey, Kev ! You like
MCW Wrestling ?"
Kevin: "Yes !!! It's The Best !!!" Phil: "Would you like to bring something back home so that you can show all your friends that you truly are a Phil Raven and MCW Fan ?"
Kevin: "Yes !!!" Phil: "Well you 2 come with me and I'll get you a thing or 2." (Phil, The Mother and The Kid walk in the hallway backstage, all the way back to the souvenir booth. Many fans are there and many come and ask for autographs, while some just walk away because they hate The Network. After he signed everything in sight, he went to the booth and bought the kid all he wanted.)
Kid's Mom: "If Kevin's father was nice like you, my son would be feeling so much better !" Phil: "What's wrong with the dad ?" Kid's Mom: "He left when Kevin was 2, I never had a boyfriend thereafter.....you are his male role model."
(Phil is shaken up. He never knew he could be that important for a fan.) Phil: "I...I...I don't know what to say..." Kid's Mom: "Don't say anything...just continue to act in a way that Kevin will be able to look up to you and idolize you..."
(Phil promised to do that to his 2 new friends. After taking the kid's phone number and promising to call when he had time. He left and went back toward the "Gorilla Position" where he would receive his instructions for the night's event. On the way, he meets with Alysian Scottsfield.Phil continues to walk and Alysian walks alongside him)
Al: "Hey, Phil. I saw you getting down to the fans' level ! Are you turning back on The Network."
Phil: "That fan was a kid, Alysian" Al: "Yeah, exactly. It was a nosy kid who just sucked up to you to get an autograph you'll probably see on E-Bay in a few days ! Come on ! Don't trust the fans !"
Phil: "I am important for that kid, Alysian" Al: "Yeah, right. The cash he'll make on E-Bay with your bad hand writting will probably help his father buying a new car or something, ha ha ha...."
(Phil stops walking, Alysian's stops walking. Phil grabs by Alysian by the collar and puts his back to the wall)
Phil: "LOOK......That kid's father have left home when he was 2 years old of age....SO HE CAN'T BUY A NEW CAR !!!! If you would think with what you have your head rather than thinking with your damn Network headset on, maybe you would have somekind of respect for kids who had problems.....ESPECIALLY
ORPHANS, EVEN IF THEY ARE NOT COMPLETELY ORPHANS !!!!!" Al: "Phil, I acknowledge that my statement was not appopriate, I forgot you too were orphan but as a member of The Network, I..."
Phil: "SHUT UP !!! Let me ask you 3 questions and then we'll know if you should continue to talk. Is the thing you were about to say positive ?
Al: "No..." Phil: "Is the thing you were about to say useful ?" Al: "Not really.." Phil: "And finally....Is the thing you were about to say true ?" Al: "..." Phil: "Stop pissing in your pants and say it..." Al: "Not...Really...I...played with the words....a little..... ............." Phil: "So if it wasn't positive, it wasn't useful and it wasn't true, why would you want to say it ? ..... Hey, what's that smell ?
Al: "It's....It's..... ....." Phil: (He shakes Alysian) HEY.... WHAT THE HECK ??? (He shakes him a bit more) THE SMELL IS GETTING WORSE !!!!!!!
Al: "It's...It's...." (Alysian looks down toward the floor under him....) (Phil looks down where Alysian was looking) Phil: "Was that yellow and funny smelling liquid there before I started scaring the hell out of you ?"
(Alysian swings his head "No") Phil: "So you put that liquid there, don't you ?" (Alysian swings his head "Yes") Phil: "So....you know what this is, don't you ?" (Alysian shakes his head "Yes") Phil: "Then...tell me..." Al: "It's....Orange Juice...." Phil: (Squeezing Alysian's neck a bit more) "I Think I know what that is......" Al: "It's....Urine........" Phil: "So you pissed in your pants ?" Al: ".... you scared me to death....." Phil: " UMPH !" (Phil then walks away. He finally meets with Rob and Paul and they give him his final instructions. He now goes toward the ring. As he walks toward the squared circle, he sees the mom and his kid, happy and cheering him, while everybody else boos. He gets in the ring and takes the microphone)
Phil: "I'd like to start by saying hi to my friends Kevin and ... (Phil stops, realizing he never knew the mother's name) ... my friend Kevin and his mom, for being so great with me. Kev, I'll keep my promise and I'll be a good model for you, kids and teenagers who look up to me as their role model. But now, let's get down to business. I was recently stripped of my
MCW World Heavyweight Championship belt....You people ever realised that the only way they can the belt away from the.....
FUCKER !!!! AND !!!! SHITTER !!!! OF THIS WORLD !!!! ...is by pulling the belt from him ? First, the belt was vacated and then again today, the belt was vacated. I never lost the belt 1-2-3 in the ring.
As a matter of fact, I am gonna make sure nobody ever gets the chance of being defeated in the ring and lose that belt, because at the tournament, I will prevail, I will thrive, I got a new motivation: I know some people care about me now. And I won't let these people down !!!"
(Crowd Cheers !!!!) Phil: "At the tournament, and I want all of you backstage to open your ears and listen, I will become a 9 Times World Champion, a 3 Time MCW Champion and most of all, I'll remain champion in my true fans' eyes. I am coming in there ready to fight the devil in hell, I am ready to take out the entire locker room, bring me the entire world in there in a gauntlet, I'll take them on. Now, let's see who's brave and who will step forward to face to wrath of the exalted, ever popular, quintessential carreer killer Phil
Raven."
(Phil points to the Mother and her kid in the crowd. They cheer heavilly. Phil signals them to come backstage.) (The mom and kid arrive backstage and Phil is waiting for them) Kid's Mom: "I am sorry ! I did not tell you my name and it made you look like you had a memory blank !"
Phil: "And that's why I called you !" Kid's Mom: "Well, my name is Laura" Phil: "I am happy to know you, Laura" Laura: "..." Phil: "..." (The scene fades to black as Laura and Phil smiles at each other with Kevin holding to his mom's leg.)
