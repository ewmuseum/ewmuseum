[Free Agent] Shawn "NBK" Rosenthal

29 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm looking for a good efed to join. Please no "hardcore" federations. Must have a web page, and roleplays must be done by email. Absolutely no simmed feds, I won't even consider them. So what I'm looking for is a booked fed, original style, with flashes done by email. Preferably not a brand new fed, I'd like an established one, but if your fed looks convincing, and has some members, I might join.
Ok, here's what you're waiting for. My sample roleplay: (We are brought a view of the ocean from a hotel room on Daytona Beach,
FL. The sun shines down on the water as it sets, giving the water a golden color. The sky is pink and orange, and as the camera turns, we see children and adults alike running along the beach. A door can be heard shutting in the background, and the camera spins around quickly.
An average size man with a muscular build is standing in front of the door, wearing jean shorts without a shirt. His abs shine as if covered with oil, and his long, dark brown hair is pushed back out of his face.
He is cleanely shaven, and his dark complexion gives him a mysterious look. The camera focuses on the man's eyes, which are entirely white, lacking both pupils as well as all color. The man walks to the window, and peers out over the ocean. After a moment, he turns to the camera, and begins to speak slowly and confidently.)
Shawn "NBK" Rosenthal - They said that I couldn't do it, but I did. I showed all of them what it means to have heart. I wanted the WOW
Extreme Title more than anyone else, and that's why I won it. I tried the hardest, and when I won this belt (pats the gold belt which he has just picked up off the hotel table) I promised myself that I would never lose it. But WOW collapsed. The president went bankrupt, and now I'm looking for a new place to destroy the competition. I urge all of the wrestlers out there to take me seriously. Don't take my words lightly, as I speak nothing but truth. I may not be the biggest or strongest man out there, but my wrestling ability surpasses all of those who have stepped in the ring to test their skill against mine, and all of them felt the pain of the Crescent Moonsault. Federation owners beware! No federation is safe from the power of the Natural Born Killer, Shawn
Rosenthal.
(Shawn Rosenthal turns back toward the window, the ocean, and stares. Finally, he turns around.) Shawn "NBK" Rosenthal - What the hell are you still doing here?! Get out!! (The screen fades to black as the cameraman makes his way quickly out the door of the hotel.)
This isn't the best that I can do, but as you all know, it's a hell of a lot easier writing a roleplay when you have upcoming matches, feuds, etc.
~Joe ( http://www.concentric.net/~Eckx666/index.html )
