[Free Agent] Tha Dragon

2 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: Inside the Key Arena, Seattle, Washington. Tha Dragon sits high up in the seating of the upcoming venue for the next ICW card. The camera view pans to the side slightly, and begins to move upward in the seating. As it does so,
Tha Dragon becomes aware of the camera's presence, and slightly shifts from his dead still position in the chair. He then stands up, and moves from his mid row seat to the isle, and starts walking downward tward the arena floor. He is wearing full length black tights, with a green stripe down the left side, and a red stripe down the right. He wears no top, but the small tatoo of a green dragon on the upper right hand part of his chest, almost to his shoulder, is apperent. Also, he has mid-back length jet black hair, and of course his half steel mask which covers the left portion of his face. As he reaches the floor, he climbs into the half-way setup ring, and begins shaking the ropes, and kicking the turnbuckles to check sturdiness. He immediatly looks tward the camera outside the ring, leans on the rops, and prepares to speak.]
Tha Dragon: Ya know... my wrestling career has been pretty damn good in it's short time. In 6 months time, I have won 3 World Heavyweight Titles. I have beat people from pillar to post, from turnbuckle, to mat. I've had tremendous wins... I've had slim alomst-lost-it wins, but the one thing that bugs me is.... I have one loss. My record is 12-1-0 ... one loss... and it bugs me.
Why does it bug me? Well.. for two reasons. The first of which is Tha Dragon hates losing.... at ANYTHING. And the second is, my opponent at the next ICW show is the only man to ever hold my shoulders to the mat for the 1-2-3. Okay, so people say... "Dragon, you beat the guy for a World Title just a few days ago.. why are you worried about him?" Why is Tha Dragon worried about him...
because he's a no good, schemeing, blob of crap. "Trillionaire" Thad Thorton... the only man to hand Tha Dragon a loss in his career. Yeah, I could whine and complain, and say that he won because his valet hit me with a purse... yeah, I can go ahead and say that he has OPENLY admited to never winning a match cleanly.. but it just aint worth my breath. The thing that people need to understand about me and 'ol Thad is... we have both beat each other once... it was for the same World Title... and now we each have a single
"1" in our loss collums. So, this is the rubber match, if you will. The one to decide it all... the one to PROVE who is the better E-Wrestler. Who is the better wrestler? Who is the better World Champ? Who is the best damn thing to hit God's grean earth?!
[Tha Dragon moves around the ring. Uncertin as to what to do... as he stops, climbs up, and sits on the top turnbuckle facing the camera. He slicks his long black hair out of his face, and back behind his head. After a short period of adjusting his mask, he once again looks to the camera, almost in a state of dissapointment.]
Tha Dragon: Last week, in my ICW debut, I continued my streak of winning my debut match in EVERY fed I have ever been in. Hell, let's talk about debuts, and understudying your opponents, shall we? I have three World Titles in my bag... TWO of which came from winning in my debut for that fed. Okay... I got blasted, and flamed... yelled at and cursed.. cuz I "didn't deserve it." So, I just want the ICW to know, that when I excell to the top of this fed, and I dominate it with brute force in a small amount of time.... DON'T BE SUPRISED!
Hell, I sure shocked World Champion The Natural Born Killer when I walked in and won a 30-Man Battle Royal in my debut, last eliminating him... and where is
NBK now? He's at Shadey Acres retirement home, sippin' his lemondae, and playing shuffle board with all the other geezers. And here I am, sittin' on toppa the world! The next thing I hear is... "Dragon, you claim to be on top of the world... but.. uhh.. I aint even ever heard of you!" That's an easy one, my friends. The world all of you live in, is far.... far.... far away from my world, and pales in comparison to it. Imagine a world... of hurt and pain... suffering and agony... hate and crime... a passion for destruction.
When you have taken that description, crammed it into your head, and imagined what you think I am talking about... multiply it by somethin' like 30. Then you're startin' to get into the correct neighborhood.
[Tha Dragon hops down, and returns to leaning on the ropes.] Tha Dragon: So, now what? What does Tha Dragon do? Well, first off, he gets rid of this little monkey on his back.. which is worse than the 49ers losin' to the Packers 6 times in a row. Ya take that monkey, and ya stick him in a blender. Feet first. This way you get to see his reaction. I'm just plain sick and tired of him... talking about him... wrestling him... knowing he's alive. So, I'll remedy all of those problems in one match. Thad Thorton...
goodbye, and good riddence. I'm going to end this silly fued once and for all. Irish Devil... you saw me stomp your head in at the last ICW show I was at, and you saw what I thought of your World Title, too, pal. And now, we have yet
ANOTHER match! One more time for 'ol Irish Devil. No problem, punk. The pleasure will be all mine.... so, after I wipe my butt with Thad's face, and he runs like a little baby back to the amature feds, I'll see YOU at the
Pay-Per-View! THIS IS THE BEGINNING... ...AND THIS IS... ....THE END!!! [Tha Dragon lets his hair fall over his face, as he drops over the ropes, and out of the ring. As he heads back up into the stands, the scene fades black.]
(This Roleplay used for ICW on November 2nd, 1998)
