Free Agent looking for serious fed

3 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys. I'm new to this newsboard, but not Efed wrestling. I've been doing this for three years now, and looking for a stable fed, as in not new and not dying. I'll I really want is one with steady roleplaying, and cards that are reasonably on time. Give my sample roleplay a look, and drop me a line at
[detail withheld]
if you are interested in signing me. I know the rp is a little on the short side, only 80 lines or so, but it's a relativly new character, with no opponent to talk about. Later.
(The scene opens with a beautiful Los Angeles sunset as the camera zooms in on a beautiful three story house on a hill. A deck sticks out the of the house, overlooking an exsquisite pool surrounding by a few tropical trees. The colored light of the sunset reflects off of the cold water, creating the most beautiful reflections. Upon that deck a man stands. He's wearing a pair of silk shorts and a tanktop, with a robe thrown over his shoulders. He has a drink in his hand, and takes a few sips from it now and then, completely immersed in the beauty of the sunset. As the sun finally goes down, the man steps into his house, and waves the cameraman to come to the front door. The cameraman walks down the stone pathway, leading to two large oaken doors. Just before the cameraman goes to knock, the door swings open to see an older man in a suit. He welcomes the cameraman in and leads him to the lounge. A projection TV is on the far wall, and one of the bowl games is playing.
Several leather couches are dispersed throughout the room. The room is adorned with paintings and tasteful sculptures gathered from around the world. A sound system is in the corner, with speakers bigger than the cameraman himself.
Godsmack is playing at a moderate level as a man walks down the spiral stairs. The man is the one we saw from the balcony. He has changed, putting on a pair of Dockers and a silk shirt. His brown hair is pulled back, showing his deep green eyes. He smiles at the camera, and goes to the wet bar to make himself a drink. He goes to the nearest sofa and sits down. He checks the score of the game and laughs. The phone rings and he picks it up. A short conversation follows, with both men laughing about something. We hear something about a bet and paying up, and the man hangs up in good spirits. He takes a sip of his drink and turns to the camera, flashing his smile once again.)
Solo: Why hello to all you people out there. The man you all see before you is none other than Solo, the new man in town. I've been around the world more times than I can remember, stopping by feds every now and then, just to win matches and break hearts. But after a while, a man begins to get homesick, so it was back home to LA for me. I was hanging out in a Hard Rock Cafe with a few lady friends of mine when I this man came up to me and asked if I was interesed in a business proposition. Now, I may be a man of high living, but deep down, I'm a businessman. So I sat down with the guy and turned out to be a recruiter for WXWF. He kept going on and on about how this is a place for the best, and I suppose my ego got the best of me. We worked out a contract in a few days, and I signed on the dotted line. Now I know what all you halfwits in the WXWF are thinking, and that is that this guy is a pansy who can't handle hardcore. Sure, I have a million dollar body that has little to no flaws, but
I've seen my share of bloodshed. I've just been better than the rest, stayed once step ahead, and been the one with the weapon in his hand, and the belt around my waist. I don't see any reason why that pattern won't continue here in WXWF. I looked down the roster, and didn't recognize a single name. The way I see it, if I don't recognize you, you have no business being here. This is my show now. I'm the headliner, I'm the showstopper, I'm the main event.
If you have any problems with that, address them to Solo, your future World
Champion. I'll be sure to get back to you at my earliest convienence.
(smirks) (Solo gets up and walks over the picture glass window that overlooks his most impressive property. Flower gardens, stone pathways, lavish pools and fountains. He sips from his glass and turns up his stereo a notch. He walks down the hall and signals for the camera to follow. We come to a game room, filled with the latest arcade games, a few pool tables, a wall of dart boards, and various other amusements. A few beautiful girls are playing pool, as Solo goes to say hello. He comes back a few minutes later, and invites the camera to take a seat. Solo sits down on the pool table, and continues to talk.)
Solo: An athlete such as myself doesn't come along to often, so thank your lucky stars WXWF that you could sign a guy like myself. I don't know who they signed up as my first victim, but rest assured it will be mearly a warmup. You see, I can afford to look into the future, past all you midcarders, and concentrate on that Battle Royal at the PPV. You see, I am the only one here qualified to have that belt around my waist, and second place would just be disgraceful to the sport, and to myself. I bet that right now, some of you guys want to rip my heart out and shove it down my throat, and the rest of you are so brain damaged that you have problems following intellectual conversation. To those of you who do understand what I'm saying, there's nothing you can do to make me shutup. What are you going to do? Challenge me to a match? That's like agreeing to go to an early grave. Face it boys, the future, the present, and the champ is here, and Solo is his name. I've tangled with the big men, wrestled with the technical ones, and broke the small guys.
I've left a path of losers and dreamers behind me, and all I did was laugh. Do you actually think you can stop me? If you do, please tell me. A laugh is worth so much these days!
(One of the girls come over to Solo and starts running her hand through his hair. She whispers something into his ear, and Solo smiles. He says something back and she walks out of the room with a seductive look in her eyes. Solo looks out the door for a moment, and then suddenly remembers the interview. He quickly turns his head to the camera and continues talking.)
Solo: You'll have to excuse that little distraction. She was getting lonely and wanted some company, company that I'll soon give her. But lets forget that for a moment. You see fellas, I'm the best thing going right now. I'm the peak, the pinnicle, the cream of the crop. I'm strong, quick, smart, rich, and handsome. Sure, some of you may have one or two of those qualities, but all five? I think not. Prepare to lose all hope in being the best, because soon you'll realize that when you're in the same league with me, second best is the best you can get. I hope you boys can handle a man of my caliber, because I've been bored lately. I hope you can at least give me a slight challenge, eh?
Tammy seems to be getting restless, so I'd better go. I just stopped by to say hello fellows, and to let all you know that the sheriff is in town. Ciao.
(Solo walks to the door, and womans hand comes into view, grabbing his arm, and pulling him off screen. The butler shows us to the door as we here laughing coming from a room on the second story. We leave the building with the doors being shut tightly behind us. We walk the pathway, fading out to a beautiful night.)
You wish you could be me, and I don't blame you! End roleplay.
