This is a RP, Please Critique, Diss, or Whatever floats your Boat to it!

10 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up with "Deadly" Drew Marris sitting in a recliner, leather chair inside huge living room. There is a huge, bug screen TV in the room, a small love seat, a large couch, and a few separate tables in the room. On the television is a tape from the IWA Magnificent Monday. "Deadly" Drew Marris is wearing a pair of dark black jeans, and a dark navy blue sweatshirt. He has the remote control in his right hand. He has his long dirty blonde hair in a single braid behind his head. He looks as though he is falling asleep.)
(Camera Man)::Mr. Marris!?? ("Deadly" Drew Marris is startled and stands up. His huge seven foot one, 345 pound frame towers over the camera man. Drew Marris sits back down and stares into the camera.)
("Deadly" Drew Marris)::Man! Don't you ever do no stuff like that again! If I am sleeping don't mess with me! Never a damn moments peace. If it isn't a camera man, it is a damn poperatzy, if it aint them it is some agency calling wanting me to be the man on there bad boy magazines! I am getting about tired.
I am a wrestler! Plain and Simple! A wrestler that is ready to kick some ass here in IWA. Thats right, I said it, and there isn't a person here in IWA that can say anything about it.
(All of the sudden there is a knock on the door.) ("Deadly" Drew Marris)::Now what the hell is this? WHO THE HELL IS IT! (Voice from behind the Door)::It is me dumbass! ("Deadly" Drew Marris)::Oh, Well get your happy ass in here already. Dude from
IWA is already here.
(The door opens up and "The High Roller" Brock Dupont walks in. Brock Dupont is wearing a pair of light colored khaki pants and a t-shirt that reads "I'm With
Stupid" and then an arrow under it. Brock Dupont walks over hurrily to the couch and sits down.)
("Deadly" Drew Marris)::What the hell took you so long? ("The High Roller" Brock Dupont)::The genius with the camera has his van blocking the drive way. I had to park on the damn street!
("Deadly" Drew Marris)::Leave it up to these guys to send us a retarded camera man. I dont know how he got in, but this dude woke me up while I was sleeping, and almost caught a DDT on my hardwood floor.
("The High Roller" Brock Dupont)::Anyway, down this fool. Did you watch that
IWA tape I sent you? I was laughing my ass off at there so called talent in this fed. I have to say I saw some decent competition on that card, but nothing to speak of. I mean the guys are slow, weak, and just out of it. And these guys think they are ready for the likes of "The High Roller" Brock Dupont and
"Deadly" Drew Marris, Money and Power?!? I think not. ("Deadly" Drew Marris)::Thats for damn sure. These IWA wrestlers arent gonna know what the hell hit them when we drop them off. Right here you got the power, the strength, and determination. And that man right there, Brock Dupont, has the speed, the finesse, and the heart. Together we are ready for all comers and all opponents. Big or small, we can take them all. I am 7'1, 345 pounds of pure man that is ready to put someone here in IWA OUT!
("The High Roller" Brock Dupont)::Down to earth or to the total extreme. It is all the same to us. We will wrestle in a cage, or a back alley! Anywhere we can get our hands on our opponents is fine with us. We show no prejuidice to anyone. You got a problem with these hard bad boys, sign the dotted line so we can get paid for kicking your ass's. I am The High Roller! The chance taker, the money maker, the familiar finisher. I can deal you hand after hand in this game, but when you got Money and Power looking for you to step in the ring with them, not even four aces could save your ass.
("Deadly" Drew Marris)::The first victims here in IWA have been announced and they go by the name Sex N' Love. "Ladies Love Me" Doug Richards and "Hottbod"
Tony Richards. You can be as much of players as you guys wanna be. But you better have your head right when you step in the ring with us. We are not some of these little fifteen year old ho's that you talk to outside the arena's. We are two twenty-six year old men that are looking to kick a whole so far through your foreheads that we can see the mat, when we lay on you for the one, two, three.
("The High Roller" Brock Dupont)::Being pretty boys might help you out once in a while on a Friday night, but this Thursday night, the only thing that is gonna help you two is a miracle. You better wish on every star you see, and pray to every God you know. Because we are taking no prisoners. We are coming for glory and there is no one, not even you two who can stop us! You can call yourself whatever the hell you wanna but I am warning you both, a name, what you have done in the past, and anything else will not save you from this beat down you will be receiving. I guess you could call it March Madness! But this time you definitly wont be advance to any other round. The only place you two will be advancing is a body bag, or a hospital bed.
("Deadly" Drew Marris and "The High Roller" Brock Dupont)::Enjoy it while it last's because the new boys are now running the show!
(The camera fades out to black as "Deadly" Drew Marris plays the tape of Magnificent Monday as both Brock Dupont and Drew Marris begin to laugh together at the tape.)
