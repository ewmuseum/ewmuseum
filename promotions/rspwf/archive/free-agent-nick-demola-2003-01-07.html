Background : Nicolas Enrique Demola was born in Rahway, New Jersey on September 27, 1966. A rough and parentless childhood helped to shape his life, as he has had to fight the battle between his dreams and his better judgement for the better part of his life.
He grew up and was never a great athlete. But he took home Junior Golden Gloves trophies in
Northern California from the ages of 11 to 13 for his great boxing skills. He then took classes on amateur wrestling and American
Kickboxing, and excelled in both through sheer guts and an overwhelming arrogance. He then moved into professional wrestling in 1997 and finally hit the big time 10 years afterwards, making a mockery of the regional federations in the southern United States and winning titles left and right. Until his neck was broken in a match in November of 1997. His career was deemed over, but he returned to defeat "Cowboy" Ken Curtis in the spring of
1998 for the IIWA World Title. He would then move to WAR!, NEW, and the INWI, winning the World Title in that vaunted federation by defeating Setzer Van Strife. He would also take the top gold in UKCW in early 2000 by brutally bashing in the knee of Andrew Tucker until he passed out from excessive pain. This began the
"mean streak" that ran through him as he tore through legends such as Valtharius the Mad, LOCO, and Brody Thunder in vicious attacks. Now he's back in a couple of federations at the age of 40, going on one last ride before he goes off into the sunset. Still as much of a fucking bastard as ever.
