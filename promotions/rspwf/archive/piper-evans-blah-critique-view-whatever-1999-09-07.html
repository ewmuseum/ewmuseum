[Piper Evans] Blah..critique/view, whatever. :)

7 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heya. This is something I did for AWS '99 that I found on a disk earlier today... it never got sent out to the list, cause Ryan stopped running it.
:) Basically, this is the third roleplay I had ever done with Piper Evans, before using her in UWF...so...blah. Critique if you like, tear it to shreds...just want it seen somewhere. :)
--------- [Open the scene with a view of one Miss Piper Evans. Perhaps she just woke up, or perhaps it's just a pajamas kind of day.but, kitty by her side and her fully-braided hair in disarray, Piper is once again sprawled across the floor of her apartment, in a
pair of red checkered boxers and a Hello Kitty midriff tanktop. She's laying on her back, transfixed with delight by the spinning fan for several minutes before
rolling up and getting to her feet with a suprising agility.] Piper: *looking down at her cat* What do you think, Ush-ee? Shall we watch it again? [Piper stares down at the cat for a time, as if seriously expecting some kind of answer before she spins and makes her way over to the TV/VCR.
Turning them on and pressing "Play", Piper settles down, a rapt expression on her face. What our viewers don't realize is that this is the third time
Piper has watched Vanessa Bellucini's "instructional video" on AWS.today.
She nods in satisfaction as each wrestler is mentioned and each point is made until she hears the other woman's wry, drawling voice declare: "Piper Evans is crazy.", whereupon she collapses into giggles and turns off the TV. Hopping up yet again like a bunny on crack, she scoops Ushkins up in her arms and goes to the kitchen to hop on on the counter. She sits on it Indian-style as she speaks.]
Piper: Silly Miss Vanessa! Saying I'm crazy. Butterflies are crazy 'coz they flitter-flatter around. I'm just...happy. I maybe get sometimes a little lost. *to Ushkins* You know, I don't kno why she helped me all those times. But... *contemplating*... it was very nice. And.and I think I would help her, too. If. If she, you know. Wanted me to. I could bring her a cookie if she felt bad, say. Tell her about the flowers that are all shifty colored and that word that means something between blue and hot chocolate.
[She hugs the kitty happily before continuing, eliciting a yowl from the over-loved beast and ignoring it without comment.]
Piper: Oooorrr... I could take anybody who's mean to her and rip their shoulders out of socket. Or kick their faces into a bloody little mass of flesh and crushed bones or tell her how to make a man wish he'd never been born to feel the pain. But... you know... only... if she wants. It's just a...
[She pauses, waiting for the word to come to her.] Piper: ...thought. But first. I'm. I'm forgetting something. [Her gaze falls on the refrigerator, on which a large, green "5" has been painted.] Piper: Number five? ...Chanel. *giggle* I should change my name to a perfume. So then I could run down the street and people could say, "Hey, there goes Esteé Lauder's Beautiful." And I could wave and smile. I'd like that. I'd also like to break
Chanel's legs so she can't stand up. She's FAR too big when she stands up. Big stuff is scary. Scary stuff is gotten rid of. By hurting it. By making it bleed and scream and cry and break like when my head goes apart on the insides late at night and I bleed.and scream...and cr-
[She shakes her head, and then stops, a perky smile affixing itself to her features.] Piper: Ushkies. I can't wait to play with Chanel. We'll...I'll...have such
FUN! Now, how 'bout mommy gets you some Jell-O for lunch?
*commercial* ----------- Jenn (Nada) :)
