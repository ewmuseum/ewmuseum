[FREE AGENT] "Diabolical" Daimon

25 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Presidents... My name is [name withheld], and you may know me from handling the character, 'Confident' Lawrence Williams. Well, I'm still keeping Lawrence, because I like the positive and sometimes negative feedback that I get from him. But I felt as if I wanted a new character, and after weeks of thinking,
I've decided to make another heel, but this time a little darker. "Diabolical" Daimon's story - Born Damon Jackson in Allentown, Pennsylvania, on May 29th, 1972, Damon Jackson was an athletic and talented boy. His parents, Cindy and Jim Jackson were firm believers in God, and the family went to church every Sunday. Damon was a standout in Bible studies, excelling in learning all the books, faster than any of the other children.
One day while going to church, the Jacksons crossed a defunct railroad. The warning system was not working, and the Jacksons did not see any harm in crossing the tracks.
The car was totalled. It was a freak accident. All three members of the family were brutally injured, Damon's parents the worst. In the hospital, Damon recovered quickly. The only reminiscents of the accident, a long scar that stretches down the right side of his face, and his right ear was completely severed. The doctors said Damon’s recovery was a miracle, a true product of God.
Damon’s parents, however, weren’t doing so good. Damon came in everyday and prayed next to Cindy and Jim’s bed. Damon stayed by his parents’ side night in and night out, praying to God, hoping that he’d be listening. But it didn’t work.
On May 29th, 1983, Damon’s 13th birthday, both Cindy and Jim died. Damon took it very hard. Damon soon locked himself inside his room for five years, reading books about satanism, cursing God for letting his parents die. Damon came out five years later, ready to live his life for the devil.
Damon soon got his name legally changed to Daimon, meaning demon. Daimon burned churches, ripped Bibles apart, and blasphemized for the next ten years. Daimon had sold his soul to the devil.
Daimon then went looking for work. He saw many advertisements of wrestling in local pictures, and wanted to try-out for a local league called the QSW [Quaker State
Wrestling] Daimon joined under the named DIABLO, and his hardcore/brawling type wrestling were soon noticed, and he was called up to a big fed called (league’s name).
Now known as “Diabolical” Daimon, Daimon intends on wreaking havoc in (the)(league’s name). Sent on a mission from the devil, “Diabolical” Daimon will accomplish his goals, or he’d rather burn in hell. "Diabolical" Daimon's stats - Age: 26 Height: 6'9" Weight: 293 Style: Hardcore/Brawler Orientation: Duh! Super Heel Finisher: Demonic DDT - Double Arm DDT Manager: To this day, nobody knows his name Manager Description: A 7'0" man with pale skin. He says nothing, does nothing. If somebody sneak attacks Daimon, he does nothing. Some say he's the devil's spy.
Appearance: Daimon is a white male with contacts that make his eyes black. Daimon has natural blonde hair, but has obviously "died" his hair black. He has shoulder length hair, which gritty and very dirty. Daimon has a scar that stretches down the right side of his face. Daimon's right ear is completely severed. Shaved in the back of Daimon's head are the numbers: 666. Daimon has no beard or mustache, but he has little stubs of hair, making his face like sandpaper. Daimon has many scars on his chest, making his chest look like a chest of many hills and mountains. He also has 666 carved into his chest.
Ring Apparel: Daimon has black jeans that are cut off at the knee to make shorts. Daimon has black knee pads on both knees, and black wrestling boots that come up to his calves, like Steve Austin's. When coming to the ring, Daimon wears a stain-ridden black shirt with "Daimon
6:66" in a white, bold font. Build: Daimon has the build of the Undertaker. Signature Moves: Satan Splash - Throws opponent into the corner, and then runs and nails opponent with a big splash
Hell's Fury - A flurry of stomps on opponent while in the corner. Hell's Fury usually comes right after the Satan Splash. Ring Entrance The lights turn off and "Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson blares over the speaker system, so loud that you can barely hear the words, just the drums. The lights them come on, and all of a sudden "Diabolical"
Daimon appears in the center of the ring. Daimon then raises his hands slowly, and then all of a sudden a black mist shoots out of each of the ring posts, and "Beautiful People" comes to a close.
Well, that's all I could think of. I will be taking requests from each fed for about a week, so if you want Daimon, send me some cards, stuff. I'll be posting a sample roleplay in a few minutes.
SPECIAL NOTE: In no way do I regard this character to disrespect Jesus Christ or God. I'm a true believer in God, and therefore I'm using this character for fun! I show any and all disrespect to the
Devil by not capitalizing his name! -Daniel R. Handler of 'Confident' Lawrence Williams [CoW, IeWC, and IIWA] and now.... "Diabolical" Daimon [????] President of EMWO [E-Mail Wrestling Organization] http://EMWOnet.hypermart.net
