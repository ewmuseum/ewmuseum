My rps...hopefully backing up some of my crap! I want input!

2 March 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's me again...the man, the myth, the whinner...[name withheld]...here's a sample of one of my rps...which I think is pretty darn good. Please give me some input on why I should or shouldn't be on the 200...I will take all comments with ease and respect opinions...read and enter the dreaming...
~Dream~ I, AM ONE THEY CALL "CHAMPION." TO MANY, I AM MORE THAN A LEGEND. SOME CALL ME EVIL, BUT CANNOT ARGUE WITH WHAT I DO. I AM THE BLACK NIGHT IN ARMOR OF DARKNESS, RIDING ON MY HORSE OF VENGEANCE. I AM THE ONE WHO THEY FEAR. I AM THE GREAT THEY PRAY TO. I NEED NO EXPLANATION, FOR THERE IS NONE. THIS IS DREAM... <Camera fades to the empty, shutdown, useless, old, dusty, BBW arena where Dream stands in the middle of the ring rejoicing in his final destruction of the federation he carried for so long. He wears a white "BBW, A long dream to the top that became a reality, but ended in all it's glory." His hair is unusually wet and slicked back, he wears black sunglasses, and his wrestling gear preparing for the neXT task at hand. There is a distinct odor of gasoline in the air, to the eXTent of irritating his sinuses. He thinks, and he brings it all back, taking in the fumes. All the tears, the tragedy, and most of all, the triumph. All of the cronies he had destroyed, all of the titles he hung around his shoulder, and all of the blood he spilled to be the best. Of course, there were many times when he wasn't on top and when he stood in darkness, alone with nowhere to go. When [name withheld] held him, and many other great talents back, he realized what was happening and claimed to be the leader of the neXT generation. And so, he lead the BBW...lead the BBW to their death. The
XT had plans in mind, but now there is no need, for the job was done now. When Dream, and the legacy he followed departed, leaving [name withheld] at the mercy of the black angel, he knew then and there that, as he said before, "Death is only a short ride away." He spray painted that on the BBW arena wall long ago, and he was right now there is nothing left but memories. With great power comes great responsibility, Dan, you weren't ready to step up to the neXT level of excellence. But now we move on to bigger and better things, not to disregard the great moments of the BBW, but nevertheless, Dream moves on once again. He has changed over the past few months, from a withered soul to a man who has seen the light, a man who does whatever it takes to get through the bad times, the worst of times. A man with many faces and a man with many ideas...but it doesn't matter how many outlooks he's had, it whether the gold is band around his waste. The fans still don't matter, the people he couldn't care less about, but now he has figured since [name withheld] is gone, he has only one thing on his mind...this little title tournament coming up. Now, he won the last won pretty easily, but it looks to be there is actually c-o-m-p-e-t-i-t-o-n this time around.
This time is just like any other time, "rejoicing in hope, patient in tribulation." It's something he's done before, and with the BBW world belt still in his hands, he will prove there is a reason why he owns the BBW and why it was him whom was the downfall as well. He comes to this league, knowing what to expect, for he knows people like Rel Rel, Tom B. Stone, Matty Dee...and others. All will vie for the same title this one night, just like BBW's One
Night...their last night. As he stands about the arena, he remembers it all. [name withheld] was a man in control, or so he thought. Taking Dream to the eXTreme in hope of that this man would crack once again. Those times are bitter memories as of now and Dream swears never to be so blind, blinded by empty promises and stories of men bigger than life. We all know that XT is no more, but it still lives in all of us. The rebellion inside, the will to be in command of your own. Knowing that others' dreams are in your hands, your bloody hands. XT was the awakening of the revolution to the neXT era, it was what we all knew was right, but never saw it in such a way before. XT is the force others wanted to be a part of, but [name withheld] had them so brain washed they didn't even know it.
Only the strong survived, and many were not that strong. Zach Xtreme wasn't that strong, and that was evident when he came crawling back to Dan, then we knew that it's more than just an idea...it became a reality. The true heroes stepped to the forefront and stood proud not for the fans or their cheers, but what the believed in. Xero Tolerance is something that flows in our veins, the taste in our mouths of freedom, the torch being passed to a new generation of heroes, born this century, tempered by a life long war, disciplined by a hard and bitter idea that their time to shine would come soon. XT will live on by the dreams of [name withheld], and he will carry that legacy further than ever before. He now knows what to do, this time will be more than the destruction of a federation, but the restraints will be broken and he will refuse to be held back by some corporate division or some fairy tale that the good guys always win. This is the real world, it doesn't matter if you're good or bad, villain or hero, you are capable of what you can do and it is you who makes the decision to take control. This is more than you'd like to know, this is reality, this is the destruction of walls, this is something more powerful than us all, this is the hope we breath...this is XT...
Dream starts to rise as he is dressed in his wrestling gear. He walks out of the ring and proceeds to observe the many halls in the arena with many of the memories...First he comes to the banners that line the rafters high above and he begins to read them; "BBW-Where The Bad Boyz Play!" "Owner and President,
[name withheld]" (Showing a picture of Dan.) "The Demons!" (Also showing a group picture of the four greatest athletes in BBW history.) "BBW Wednesday Night
Warfare! The Most Watched Wrestling Program on the Planet!" (Shows the WNW logo and the announce team.) And many more that were hung before he had started in the league. Finally he comes to one last banner hung in the middle of the arena, "BBW 1997-1998." Dream stands in the asileway and stands, staring at that banner for a while, it looks like the flag of what he dreamed before, his greatest defeat...the betrayal of the BBW. He thinks of the chance he gave him, all the great things Dan did for him...he does not fell remorse, he has no regrets. Just emotions, the emotions that held him back long ago. He continues to walk down the corridors and stops at a glass case holding gold plaques with the BBW former world champions engraved in them. Greats such as Mr. Man, Havoc,
Scorpion, etc...as he quickly skims the list he stops when he reaches the name "Silver Bullet, Two-time BBW World Heavyweight Champion." He starts to laugh at the sight of the name and pictures in his mind the great BBW legend...the hero he once was. This once again is with no remorse, no regrets. He is stronger now, stronger than ever before and he mumbles a few things to himself, moving on through the hallways.
He goes past the announcing position, the monitor screen that hung high on the wall, and through all of the empty seats. All of the seats were filled by people, people who came to watch him die, to watch him bleed. These people were the true evil, but now he has forgotten what they had meant to him. It's now about the revolution he leads and not about the people who hate him. He continues on to the back of the arena where he glances by all of the locker room doors..."Robby Blaze," "Brian Farmer," "Metal Hedz," etc. He finds the door to his locker room and opens it slowly as in caution. He steps in, looking around. Seeing the XT shirts hanging from the walls, a leather couch with a black coffee table in front of it, an empty bottle of mineral water opened on the table with a folded piece of white paper laying beside it, and a Gatorade stain on the rug. He walks to the couch and slowly sits down, kind of uncomfortably as in fear of what was in that room with him. Dream gazes about the room for a few minutes and reaches over to the paper, curious what it is.
He opens the paper and slowly reads its contents. He smirks, tearing the piece of paper in half and throwing it on the ground. "I won't need that anymore...these walls are silent now" he says, reassuring himself looking at the ceiling for apparent approval. The paper was his now void BBW contract. He gets up from the couch, taking one last look around, and walks out of the locker room, closing the door behind him. Dream stares at the name "Dream" on the front of the door briefly and moves on. He walks briskly past other locker rooms, until he sees "[name withheld]" marked on a door near the end of the hallway.
He opens the door and slides into the room. The office is completely bare except for one paper lying on the floor. He picks up the paper and begins to read...>
"It was a long and hard journey to legendary status. We had our good times, we had our great times, but we were plagued by the hard times. Trying so hard to keep the federation running and with all of the problems we faced, I'd have to say we made a proud stand for a long cause. But in the end, it was more the burden then we could all bare. It was something stronger than us all. It's something I never wanted to do, but I have done it...this time for real. I'm proud to say I was once the president of the BBW and I hope everyone else is proud to say they were proud to be a part of it as well. I leave you a torn man. I just wish we could have had better times, greater times. But that was then, and this is now...this is the future...this is the neXT generation and the BBW was destined not to be a part of it. And so I bid you farewell to whoever would be the one to read it.
"The True BBW ICON," [name withheld] [name withheld] <As Dream finishes the letter, many thoughts cross his mind, but he thinks about what he has done and laughs...not a laugh of mockery, but a laugh of respect and virtue. He leaves Dan's office slowly and walks down through the maze of halls to the outdoors eXiT. Carrying the piece of paper, Dream pulls out a lighter and lights the paper afire. He then throws the flaming paper behind him...and as he walks away we see Dream with the BBW championship belt around his shoulder and in the background the BBW arena in violent flames.
"Rest in piece, never to be disturbed," he says as he walks slowly, remorsefully away from what remains of the BBW arena. A prelude of things to come...>
"Dream a little dream for me..."
