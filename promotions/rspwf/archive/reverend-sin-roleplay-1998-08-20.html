Reverend Sin Roleplay

20 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Hello, I'm [name withheld], handler of "Brutal" Brian Floyd (EWC, PHW, LAW) and Reverend Sin (EMWO). I don't know if many of you know BBF or not, but I'm pretty sure the only people that DO know of Reverend Sin are from AICW (from late last year) and the EMWO (where he currently competing). Now, this isn't a promo RP to where I'm looking for a federation to join, it's mainly just a plug for him in any of those award shows that go on. So, without further adieu, here is my latest work (written this morning) from Reverend Sin.**
[The scene opens in the pughs of a large church; location unknown. It is late at night and it appears that nobody is inside of this massive church. The camera pans slowly to the left as you get a view of the entire front of the chuch; including the altar, the seats for the church choir, the baptism pool, and the big cross above it. Suddenly a red light begins to flicker near the back of the church and the camera turns abruptly. The back doors open and a foggish mist begins to pour inside of the church as the lights begin to flash more violently. Two figures slowly enter the church, looking as if they may be
Donovan And Reverend Sin. The camera zooms in closer and you see that it is indeed the two men. Donovan has on his usual black suit and tie with his long brown hair pulled back in a ponytail. Reverend Sin follows behind him wearing a red suit and tie with his black hair hanging down in his face. They walk slowly down the aisle gap through the pughs and make their way to the altar, where the preacher speaks to the audience. The red lights continue to flicker and the smoke follows them the entire way until they reach the altar. There,
Donovan grabs the mic and it is somehow on. Reverend Sin stands behind him looking on, almost as if he's watching somebody in the back of the church.
Donovan waits a few moments before he finally begins to speak...] DONOVAN - The Reverend has arrived...church may now begin. Hello all you pathetic worshipers of Satan, we've come to speak to you today in order to inform you of some new demonic activity that has been going on in the EMWO.
[Donovan pauses as he strokes his thin chin beard and looks out onto the pughs, as if he has a real audience. Reverend Sin keeps his ice cold gaze set on the same fixed position in the back of the church.]
DONOVAN - Now...you are all aware of Ramacees and his alliance with the devil. We proved to you that he IS possessed by Satan...I mean, look at how he snapped when he wrestled Reverend Sin. He kicked, he used chair, he bit...he did everything that your typical man of God isn't supposed to do. He got lucky and avoided the wrath of God for then, but come Saturday, August 29th...he will finally be baptized. The EMWO president has signed a No Disqualifications match between Reverend Sin and Ramacees, which is EXACTLY what we need. This means that Reverend Sin can use ANY means necessary to beat the devil...(snickers)...no pun intended, out of Ramacees. Maybe the Reverend will give him a couple of chairshots, a few piledrivers onto the entrance way, or even choke him out with a mic cord. Now I know this is going to contradict what I just said about men of God not using these sort of methods to win, but if you recall...that was a regular match. In a regular match you must ABIDE by the rules and not cheat. However...this match is a No Disqualifications Match, so I find it perfectly legal to use any means needed to win.
(Pauses for a second and smiles) I'm sure God will agree with me on that. [Donovan quits speaking as he turns back to Reverend Sin, who appears to be telling him something. He covers the mic and leans towards the Reverend with a listening ear to the side. He nods a few times and then says a whispering "Ok"
as he finally turns away from Reverend Sin. Donovan then looks deep into the camera with a new, serious expression on his face.]
DONOVAN - The Reverend has just informed me to tell you something I almost forgot to talk about. This being the issue of a NEW man of Satan. Yes, I know you might find this a *little* hard to believe, but you're going to have to try the best of your ability to look through his seemingly untainted exterior. He currently holds the North American Title and has said a few harsh words about
Reverend Sin and I in the past. "The Disciple" Eric Wayne may seem to have an alliance with God, but don't let his talk deceive you. We have received word from a VERY reliable source that he has been doing the devil's bidding for a while now, he just hasn't slipped up in the slightest bit to give any sort of hint. Now, I know all of you are sitting there on your couches in TV land shaking your heads in disbelief, but that is only natural. You are trying to convince yourself that a man that speaks so highly of the Lord couldn't
POSSIBLY have pledged an allegience to Satan, but it appears it has happened. And as if you can recall, Satan appeared heavenly just seconds before his dark plunge into the fiery depths of hell. This man, Eric Wayne, holds a very important piece of gold here in the EMWO, and it is up to us to take it back.
Speaking of which, you don't think he ACTUALLY won that Battle Royal for the
North American Title fair and square, do you? If you recall, Hannibal Stone jumped out of the ring when it was just him and Eric Wayne, which seemed awfully peculiar. The reason being? Eric Wayne, a man of Satan, got into
Hannibal Stone's head and FORCED him out of the ring, thus causing Eric Wayne to be named champion. THAT is why Mr. Stone left the ring and the title to
Eric Wayne...it was all the work of Satan. (Donovan now looks deep into the camera as if he's trying to talk to Eric Wayne himself) So Eric Wayne, you need to start watching your back. That attact last Saturday wasn't just a blind shot from my man Reverend Sin, it was a message. Either rid your soul of the devil or pay the price. If we continue to see devilish acts from you we
WILL continue to beat you senseless. So Eric Wayne, either repent...OR
FEEL... THE... WRATH... OF... GOD! [Donovan drops the mic on top of the altar and begins to walk towards the doors they entered in. Reverend Sin follows behind him as the red lights begin to flicker once more. They walk down the aisle and as they near the doors a foggish mist begins to seep through the bottom crack. As Donovan opens the doors the mist pours from the exit, and both men vanish into it. The camera films the doors until the smoke clears and there is no sign of the two men.
The scene then fades out...] [name withheld] Handler of: "Brutal" Brian Floyd {EWC, PWH, LAW, HHWO - Soon} Reverend Sin {EMWO} ***BBF...Jobber into the next millennium!***
