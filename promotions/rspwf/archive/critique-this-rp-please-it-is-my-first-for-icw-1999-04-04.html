Critique This RP Please. It is my first for ICW!

4 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The ICW cameras begin to roll. The scene is a very nice high establishement restraunt in Memphis Tennessee. There is a man with a violin playing and people listening and eating all over the restraunt. Everyone there is in nice leisure suits or nice three piece suits and dresses. The camera spans the room and goes to a private corner booth where Kenneth Morlock is sitting. Morlock is wearing a black leisure suit with a red Polo undershirt. Kenneth Morlock is sitting there reading on the menu what he wants. All of the sudden the camera spans to the front door of the restraunt where the door man is standing there talking to
Matt Allen(Kenneth Morlock's Manager). Matt Allen is wearing a pair of blue
Tommy jeans and a black and red stripped Tommy shirt. The door man seems to be trying to restrain Matt Allen from entering the restraunt.]
[Matt Allen] Dude, I am serious, I am here to see Kenneth Morlock, I come in here all the time. He is in the private booth over there in the corner.
[Door Man] If you are here all the time then you would know that you are supposed to be on the reservation list if you are gonna be here. And I look on the list and I see no Matt Allen anywhere.
[Matt Allen] Dude, just go over and ask him and he will tell you I am supposed to be here with him! I have something very important to tell him!
[Door Man] I will go over and ask him if he knows a Matthew Allen, and if he does not I will have security to escort you out.
[The door man walks over to the corner booth where Kenneth Morlock is sitting. The door man and Kenneth Morlock speak for a few seconds and the door man walks back over to Matt Allen.]
[Door Man] Sir, I am sorry but he says that he does not know you and that he is tired of you stalking him so now I shall call security, if you do not leave right now!
[Matt Allen] WHAT???!!! [All of the sudden Kenneth Morlock gets up from the corner booth laughing as he walks over to Matt Allen and the door man.]
[Kenneth Morlock] Dude, I was just kidding, yeah I know him. Let him through. [The door man pulls the red velvet string and Matt Allen walks in giving the door man a dirty look. Matt Allen and Kenneth Morlock both walk over to the booth and Morlock reads the menu as Allen speaks.]
[Matt Allen] Damn, I hate new guys! But anyways! Kenneth, I have great news. You have gotten a match in ICW! The return has begun.
[Kenneth Morlock] (From behind the menu) Who is the poor sorry sap? [Matt Allen] "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor! [Kenneth Morlock puts the menu down and has a deadly looking smirk on his face as the waitress walks up.]
[Waitress] What would you like today Mr. Morlock. [Kenneth Morlock] Lobster! [Waitress] And anything for you Mr. Allen. [Matt goes to order but Morlock interrupts him.] [Kenneth Morlock] He cant eat he has to talk to me right now babe! [The waitress walks away smiling as Matt Allen goes on speaking] [Matt Allen] Anyways, your first opponent in ICW this time is "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor. You have seen him around in other feds, you know his skills. [Kenneth Morlock] SKILLS???? SKILLS?? PLEASE!! You have got to be kidding me. Your right we have seen him around and we have all seen that he cant fight his way out of a wet paper bag. The guy is just a plain loser. I mean come on "The Ladies Man" Yes,
Vince Taylor I agree with you, you are a Ladies Man but I think over the years you have gotten confused by what that means. Everyone else thinks it is a guy that can get the chicks, right?
[Matt Allen] Right! [Kenneth Morlock] Then why is Vince Taylor using it out of context. He is prancing around, and yes I do mean he is prancing around thinking that he is a lady trapped in a man's body. But on the serious side. I have seen you around and frankly I am just not impressed. That is the reason why on...What day is this match, Matt?
[Matt Allen] The match is scheduled for Tuesday. [Kenneth Morlock] That is why on Tuesday I am seriosuly going to look forward to stomping my foot into your forehead. I am gonna take you into that ring and show you every inch of it as I drag your face across it. You will be the man leaving this match, not wondering, but asking yourself why you were so stupid, as to step in the ring with the New Legend here in ICW, Kenneth Morlock. But I dont expect for you to ask yourself anything for at least a few days, because that is how long it is gonna take you to wake up from the coma I am gonna put you in. See the games ended a long time ago, and the real fun for me is just now starting.
And it looks like you get to be the first piece of this fun. MY FUN! I am advising you to eat as many Wheaties as you can between now and then because you are gonna need anything and everything you can get to survive the beat down you will suffer when you step through the ropes. They say the only safe sex is abstenince, well punk, the only way for you to stay safe is for you to be abstinant from the ring on Tuesday night, because if you step in, I will show you not a bit of mercy. From the time the bell rings till I have my hand raised in victory because I nailed you with The Lock-Down it will be pure hell Mr.
"Ladies Man" Vince Taylor. [Matt Allen] I heard rumors in the locker rooms and offices that the people in ICW are not taking you serious. They think you will just go back into retirement again when you lose.
[Kenneth Morlock] You know I am getting tired of just about everyone. ICW, you boys wanna know if I am serious? Watch what I do to "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor on Tuesday.
And then FBK, Christain Madison, Hidden Essence, Kick Ass Chris Carter, and everyone else in ICW will all begin to realize that Kenneth Morlock is no joke, and deserves to be taken very serious!
[The waitress walks up and sets the lobster in front of Kenneth Morlock. Morlock takes a bite of the lobster and smiles as the waitress walks away. Morlock swallows the food then looks back up at the camera.] [Kenneth Morlock] But always remember this ICW... PRETTY.... PLUS.... POWER... EQUALS... MEEE!!!!!!!! [The camera fades out to black as Kenneth Morlock continues to eat the Lobster and waive over the waitress for Matt Allen to order something.]
Kenneth Morlock handler of Kenneth Morlock Feds::[ICW 0-0-0] [ASP 1-1-0] Titles::None yet Having FUN again:::HELL YEAH!!
