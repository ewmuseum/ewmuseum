[Critique] The Drifter [Critique]

26 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, this is my first Rp in three months. I don't think that it's that bad, really. Well, go ahead and tear it apart...
=========================================================== [] [Scene] The scene opens up on a big island off the coast of San Francisco. It is raining really hard. It's just full of trees and paths and all sorts of random things. There is a man that looks like an ant from the aerial shot that is currently given. The camera zooms in showing that the man is running in a jogging outfit. The view then switches to a ground camera which gives a ground level view. It is now obvious that the man is The
Drifter. He is very wet and is in a gray jogging outfit. He then stops and starts to do push ups. The camera then turns away to the rest of the island showing the many trees and wildlife. After about 60 seconds the camera turns back to The Drifter who is now standing. He jogs over to a drinking fountain and takes a drink. He is pretty muddy and out of breath. He sits down on a nearby bench and catches his breath. He then lays back and looks directly into the camera. He then smiles into the camera and begins to speak...
[The Drifter] The Drifter has finally came to the UXWA. There are a lot of old faces here. It's been a long time, a real long time. I've been on hiatus for about 3 months now, just thinking things over. About 3 weeks ago,
I got a call from a friend, Scotty Lee Xtreme, and he told me all about the
UXWA coming back. I didn't want to rush it, knowing how Taj's past feds went, but after sitting back and seeing what's been going on, I can't wait any longer. I thought to myself, this is it...this is finally my big break. You know, I've busted my ass for years and years to get the respect I deserve and
I'm now finally getting it. In the IWF, I was good. Sure, I could have been better but I was happy. Extreme Champion, Toughman Champion, Tag Champion. It was good. But I know I could have gone for the Television or United States
Championships. The only thing holding me back was the front office. They never gave me my due. Let's see, Chris Iverson, I think I could take him. It would have been a great match but I think I could have come out winner with that
Television Title. And then there's Mr. Trash Talk, "Buff" Eric Johnson. I know
I could have taken him. He was just a crybaby. Always whining and always talking trash. But he had it easy. He got lucky when he got that US Title and I know that I could have taken it away from him if I got a shot. But that was then, and this is now. Anyway, after the IWF...I went to the Calvary Wrestling
Alliance. A great fed. That's where I achieved greatness. I kicked everybody's ass and got the World Title. That's what made me great. But there's only one step left for me. And that's legendary status. I may be viewed as elite by some, but I don't buy it. And I think that this is where I will become a legend. My time has come. And I am a legend in the making.
[The Drifter] This match, this is what's gonna do it for me. We have such big names as former CWA Hardcore Champion Scotty Lee Xtreme, a guy that I've heard a lot about but never had the honor to meet, Rob Stylz, and an all around nice guy Lazarious Throxide. Now this is where I belong. The only guy that I have a problem with in that ring is Scotty Lee Xtreme. Scotty, why are you such a bitch? To me, you're just some jobber off the street. Sure, you can be a nice guy...but you can also big a major prick. I don't care about all your talk, I've taken out bigger names than you can ever dream of even meeting.
Sure, you've done some pretty big things and you have already marked your ground here in the UXWA but I'm taking over. I remember you from the IWF, jobber, then the CWA, you got a little better but nothing big...until the end.
You got a shot at me, the World Champion. You were all talk. It closed down before our match happened but you were whining about that until you lost your voice. Everything always has to go your way, doesn't it? Well, I got news for you buddy...I'm taking your spot. Everything you've worked for, forget it. I'm gonna take your place. It's time for you to go back home Scotty.
[Scene] The Drifter gets up and does a couple stretches. He then starts jogging a little bit. He stops and takes a deep breath. He then runs his fingers through his wet hair and looks out to the horizon. He doesn't turn his attention to the camera just yet. He then continues to speak...
[The Drifter] You see that, that's my future. Endless. I'm gonna be around forever. That horizon is like my career. Nothing will stop me.
Maybe there will be some breaks but I'll keep going. I've been out for three months. Just thinking things over. And now I think I'm ready. I think I'm ready to finally claim my spot as an elite wrestler. Scotty, you have no chance against me. I'm like a run away train. You can dive in front of me, but I'll just run you over. I'm taking this match as seriously as a heart attack. This is what's gonna prove if I'm ready to become a legend. I believe in my heart that I am more ready than ever. But talk is cheap, so I'll show all of you that
I'm ready...heart, body, & soul...this Thursday.
[The Drifter] Now I want to talk about the two underdogs, well, at least from my point of view. First, there's Rob Stylz. Rob, I've heard a lot about you. But this will be the first time I'll ever see you in action.
But I know I can take you. Like I said earlier, nothing's gonna stop me. Not you, not Scotty, not anybody. Because even though I haven't seen you in action yet, you haven't seen me. And I think you're better off not knowing. That way you won't be as scared, you won't be as cautious, and you won't be as prepared as the others. You'll come in courageous, clumsy, and stupid. You will see that
I'm 6'10" and you will try to take advantage of that. But it's not gonna work.
I'm just too eager to get through you that you will just be thrown to the wolves. [The Drifter] Now, the last man in this free for all is Lazarious Throxide. Laz, I remember you from the IWF. You were an up and coming star but never got your chance to shine. Well, I'm guessing that your making yourself known here. Well, this is my killing ground. I don't care if you were here first. In wrestling it's Survival of the Fittest. And you, my friend, are not gonna make it as long as I'm standing in your way. See, what everybody has looked past is the fact that this match is a number one contendership match for the Universal Title. Since The Reaper is hanging them up, Millionaire Mike needs an opponent. And since Scotty Lee Xtreme is the current number one contender, the winner will take his place. And I plan on being that man. Laz, you're still making your name while I'm a very established wrestler. So I get the right of way, of course. And well, you'll be sent out to pasture with poor
Rob Stylz. You just don't have what it takes to make it in this business.
You'll be stuck as a mid carder for the rest of your career. I'm sorry but somebody had to tell you. But me, I'm a legend in the making. I'm the one that's meant to win that Universal Title. And whatever you say doesn't mean shit! Scotty has a lot of promise and ever Rob over there might have a snowballs chance in hell, but you Laz, your destined to roam the ranks of the
North American and Television titles for the rest of your pitiful career. What you're currently at right now is the burn out of your career. This is as far as the train goes for you Laz, but I'm going straight to the top because I'm Just
Simply Untouchable...
[Scene] The Drifter then lays down on the bench and starts to do some sit-ups as the camera fades out to the focus of the horizon.
[] [name withheld] The Drifter - [I\\'F] Scotty Lee Xtreme's Bodyguard - [UX\\'A] Lord of The Ring [LOTR] Confederation X Enforcer Drifter - yes, Alan. I've heard a lot about him, and maybe will go one on one with him sometime down the road. - Zach P
