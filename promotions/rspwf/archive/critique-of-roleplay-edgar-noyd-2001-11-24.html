[Critique of Roleplay] Edgar Noyd

24 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The view slowly fades in to the Locker Room of the now LCW Empire Champion,
Edgar Noyd. Across the room, Noyd is leaning up against the wall with both legs spread out infront of him on a wooden bench. His naked upper body is sweaty and red with welts. His blue jeans, however, are worn and blood stained. Noyd unties his scuffed black boots and lets out a relaxing sigh between each boot.
He tosses both to the floor as he leans back against the wall, as he drapes the
LCW Empire title over his waist. Noyd lets out a deep sigh and shuts his eyes while he rolls his neck, trying to rid himself of it's stiffness.]
EDGAR NOYD- Tonight... Lost Concious Wrestling witnessed something mystical. Something rare. Something powerful. <deep sigh> Something special. Tonight at Plymouth Rock'D, A.B.U.S.E. walked in as three men. One the LCW Iconalized
Champion... one the Homocide Champion. By night's end, we walked out as three men. One the LCW Iconalized Champion... Co-Artillery Champion.. one the
Homocide champion.. Co-Artillery Champion and one... <rare smirk> Empire
Champion. [Noyd rubs his chest as he continues.] On a smaller scale the LCW itself had a great night. It ventured into the world of Pay-Per-View and came off successful. Wrestlers are getting exposure.
People are seeing what is going on. The problem with that? More people might want to be a part of it. More might want to also see this crumble. Kacey Ash and Erik Andrews want fame... want fortune... and want acclaim. Are they willing to endure what comes with those three things? Are they willing to endure the stress? The flops? The criticism? Remember ... when more eyes can see what you do... that also means more tongues are there to flame you for doing so.
[Noyd looks down at the Belt and slowly runs his right hand over the gold. It shimmers underneath the light as the words 'EDGAR NOYD' are plated beneath a larger LCW plate.]
Tonight I reached to the Top of the Mountain. When I got there, I did the only thing I know how to do. I took the man sitting there and threw his ass out. I took his throne.. his crown... and his robe. And I must say Gordon, they are a much better fit on me. The only way I see them falling off me is if I allow them to or I suddenly lose weight in order to fill them. I hope you can see I am speaking methaphorically. You came in brash, arrogant and rather lax. I came in angry, driven and destined. All the want in your heart to remain
Champion could not stop the will of Edgar Noyd nor could it derail the path in which my destiny flows on. Desire is powerful. But nothing can stop a strong will.
[Noyd slowly sits up, sitting entirely on the bench now resting his elbows on his knees.] And to Shawn Ryder... I say this. You, my friend, were like a steam boat in the early days of LCW's existance. But when some competition showed up, you've apparently capsized in a sea of reality. Don't be too upset. Gordon Venta is no different than you. Both of you were pale pretenders. When real Wrestlers showed up with some real emotion... you caved. LCW is caving. Three men are causing the very foundation of this organization to buckle, if not fall. But there is still time to save it. And therein lies my point of all this.
Someone can stop the LCW from falling. ABUSE is on a war path. We will stop at nothing. We are here for simple and utter dominance. I am not here for friends. I am here to destroy, maim and mutilate any challenge. Anyone.
Winning this Title will not quench my thirst. It will only make my cravings last longer and be more violent. That is the problem of the LCW, not mine. I will bring honor to this Title. I will make this Title mean something.
[Noyd stands up, clutching the Empire Title in his right hand.] I will make Edgar Noyd mean something. Here is an open challenge to the LCW. I'm knocking... who's going to answer? [Fade to black.]
