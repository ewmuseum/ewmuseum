[Tricks of the Trade] Fed Networks, why do they fail?

2 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
------------------------ Tricks of the Trade Volume 1 Issue 3 "Fed Networks, why do they fail?" ------------------------ Well it's my third issue and I'm already booming with tons of interested subscribers, but this isn't good enough. With the next few months I hope to take Tricks of the Trade to new heights. Once I get my fed ACEW equipped with some staff members to help out, I intend on putting out offer for writers in Tricks of the Trade. I will then develop a website for it, and work on bringing the absolute best ideas to new and even experienced handlers. So please help me make Tricks of the Trade even better by telling all your friends to subscribe as soon as possible. ------------------------ What's a network? ------------------------ A network (or umbrella) is when several federations decide to get together and work as a team. This would seem like the perfect thing to do to better your fed, but it almost never works for the better. Why? Several reason can be pointed to for the failure of most fed networks. Difference in quality between the feds, and a fed folding are just two of many possible reasons, but the REAL reason behind the failure is the lack of preparation on the behalf of the federations involved. ------------------------ What they should do ------------------------ Well when you are talking about a Network then you are definitely going to hold cards together. So the first and biggest step would be to get your rules straight. You should all agree on the same things. How lenient are you going to be when accepting new members that resemble real wrestlers? Things like these all need to match. As for the ring rules? Well these can vary, simply because you might have an extreme federation and a traditional federation together. This can be simply taken care of by having extreme matches on the cards. The next step is your writing style. It would be a good thing to get straight because it will make your cards match. If you use Play-by-Play and another federation uses the IIWF "descriptive" style then you'll definitely encounter some problems on the super cards. Make sure you agree on a style that will be simple and easy for everyone to use. The next step is in this process is making a universal application. You need an application for all the feds in your network so it needs to match. This will help when you want a wrestlers from one fed to appear at another fed's card. Make sure EVERYTHING matches and can easily be put together in the future. ------------------------ The next step ------------------------ Your next step is the websites. Every fed in your network should meet together at one main site. Thus creating one huge web for the leagues in the site. The main page should include links to all the feds with info about them. It should also include events, and other related material. Believe it or not your website will be a major part of of your success. Your site most look good. If you make it look neat then you'll catch a surfers attention and he'll look around, but if it looks bad he'll leave so make sure you have plenty of eye-catching graphics on it. ------------------------ Keep it going ------------------------ Make sure every fed pushes the existence of the network on every card. Have say one match from the other feds on your cards every week at least. Hold Supercards even if not competing against each other at least once a month. Then advertise, you most likely won't get any feds interested in joining. So you'll need to bring them to you. Invite a fed to attend and fight at the supercards and make then feel good about it. If they like it then they will join. ------------------------ Bring it together ------------------------ The last step is to have a universal mailing list. Everyone in every fed should get a weekly update which should include rankings, upcoming matches, and so forth. Then should all also receive all the cards. This will give the members interest in the other feds. ------------------------ Good Luck ------------------------ With even my five years of experience I found this subject hard to write about. I haven't ever really dealt with the networked feds, but I hope to do it in this coming new year. When I get ACEW up and running (Should I get myself four experienced writers and a VP), I will definitely look into forming a network between other feds that hold my same ideals on E-Wrestling, but that remains the hardest part. Everyone thinks different and so they expect different things. This is definitely the hardest thing to pull of in E-Wrestling, but I hope to do it and when I do I'll give you all the information. Sorry if this issue stunk, but I think I bit of a bit too much. Bad For Life, MC ------------------------ In the next issue: I will talk about the first crucial element in running a fed. That being starting one. I will discuss the right way and the wrong way to do this and throw out tips to those seeking to better their feds. ------------------------ - If you wish to subscribe by E-Mail please contact [detail withheld]@aol.com .
