[Free Agent] "The Natural Born Killer" Shawn Rosenthal

21 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys. Free Agent here. I've been looking around the net all day for a federation, but couldn't find one that fit what I wanted. What do
I want? Here goes:
Fakes wrestlers only. Closed-flash roleplays. Must have website (w/ no popups) Booked. Fed must be established with at least 15 wrestlers. If your fed doesn't have one of these things, don't contact me, because this is what I'm looking for (I could slide on the closed-flash roleplays, but the rest is a must). A sample roleplay is attached below, so check it out and see if you want me in your federation.
~Joe (The scene opens to a dark, abandoned warehouse. The sound of claws scrambling across the cold, damp floor can be heard, as well as a low-pitched growling. Slowly the camera pulls back, and we see that we are watching a television through the camera. The camera pans across the room, which is dimly lit, with a large leather chair and a long leather couch. There is a figure sitting on the couch, but the room is too dark to make out the figure clearly. Suddenly the door to the room flies open, and the lights flash on. The figure on the couch shields his eyes from the sudden illumination as the camera focuses on the figure at the door. It is a young woman, probably in her early twenties, wearing a tight white blouse and a very short navy blue miniskirt. Her dark brown hair hangs down over her shoulders, and her baby-blue eyes twinkle as the light reflects off of them.)
Woman: (in a soft, seductive voice) Shawn, I thought you said you were going to the gym. (The camera them moves to the figure on the couch, who we can now clearly see. He is wearing a pair of black Adidas windbreakers and a white tank-top shirt. His face is tanned, and as he pushes his dark hair out of his face, we see his emerald green eyes.)
"The Natural Born Killer" Shawn Rosenthal: I was there and back in less than an hour...man, you look great!
Woman: Thanks honey. Look, I'm gonna go upstairs and take a shower. I hope you'll be in bed by the time I get out.
(She gives him a seductive smile, and then slowly walks up the stairs, giving us a quick glimpse of the flimsy red thong resting beneath her skirt. The camera then turns back to Rosenthal, who has turned off the television and is staring into the camera.)
"The Natural Born Killer" Shawn Rosenthal: The doctors said I would never walk again. They said that an average man would be dead. But I survived, and after 18 months of harsh physical therapy, I'm ready to step into the ring again to prove why I'm still the best wrestler on the face of the planet. You see, last November I was overseas in Japan, fighting for the title of wrestler of the universe. My opponent botched a top-rope DDT, breaking my neck in 3 places, leaving me barely alive in the ring. After three weeks in intensive care, I was moved into a private complex, where I began physical therapy. Everyone I encountered gave me a zero percent chance of complete recovery, but here I am today, in the best shape of my life. As I said, I'm now ready to step in the ring again, but need a place to showcase my talent. I'm not looking for some rinky-dink independant federation, I'm going for the big time. I can with the rest of 'em, and wrestle better than all, so if you think you've got what it takes to provide some competition for the pinnacle of the sport, contact me so I can prove you wrong.
Woman: Shawn! Come on baby! "The Natural Born Killer" Shawn Rosenthal: Now if you'll excuse me, my other duties call. (Shawn Rosenthal jumps up off the bed, and bounds up the stairs three stairs at a time. The camera turns to the now dark television, and slowly zooms in until the screen in filled with darkness.)
Of course this isn't the best I can do, but it's easier once I've got an angle to work with.
