The NAEC Is a New Federation, 1997

28 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello everyone, This is a quick summary of the NAEC for your convenience. We have had our first two cards with Lord Byron and Steve "The Fury" Kowalski both ranked joint number one in the federation. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The North American E-wrestling Corporation ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ The North American E-wrestling Corporation (or NAEC) is a federation started by a group of friends who were intent on providing an interesting enviroment for people to use their characters. The six of us are all experienced e-wrestlers and wrestling fans. We found that most posts made to RSPWF are for new feds. These posts never say anything about the fed except whether or not it is simmed or booked. Our federation is small with 20 heavyweight and 30 cruiserweight spots open at the moment. We have tag teams with the Siberian Express as the seeming team on top though the Wild Mustangs are ready to debut. We have great plans to expand as we have enough writers for a womens, juniors and maybe even a midget division (no offence or rudeness is meant by the later.) So as you can see we are a great federation to join. Few people have joined our federation although three brand new players are beginning to look very promising. ~Wrestler standards~ We only accept fantasy wrestlers although say one clone of Shawn Michaels , Ultimate Warrior or a crazy guy would be welcome as long as they differ somewhat from the characters who in real life use those gimmicks. No wrestlers that are unimaginably tall or 900 lbs are welcome at all, we believe that it is people like that who spoil e-wrestling for others. I mean how many 900 lbs guys can walk and how many 9'0 people actually have the co-ordination to walk? ~The Cards~ The NAEC has managed to get its schedule to two cards per week, the warm-up card of the week Tuesday night Xcite and the main card, Saturday Night Smash. Both cards certainly have lived up to their names as on Tuesday there wasn't even a main event due to a sneak attack on one of the participants and as soon as Bob is fit the match will be re-scheduled. The two cards are followed by an interview show with arena interviews, clips and flashes and line-ups for the next card are shown on the two Control Centres. If one of the NAEC wrestlers offers to do it, a live interview segment like the Jam Session, The rose garden and The Terrordome will be conducted by a wrestler. ~The New Divisions and the Belts~ The three divisions planned are going to come into the NAEC very soon with the womens division planned for July the juniors for August and the first of its kind in e-wrestling (I think) the midget division planned for November. There will be quite a few belts in total once the roster has expanded sufficiently with the World Heavy weight Title, Global Tag Team Titles, United States Title, North American Tag Titles and the Pacific Cruiserweight title scheduled to come sooner or later until August. The reason that ammount of belts are offered is because a large federation needs a few titles to help it succeed. The planned ammount of wrestlers is big but in my experience you need a large core of active players to help your federation. ~The Pay-per Views~ The NAEC is not like every other e-fed being run by a twelve year old and having three cards, no roleplaying and a monthly or even bi-monthly PPV. It only has five pay-per-views during the year to simulate the feel of a real federation. The PPV are meant to be sold to an imaginary audience so it is worth making them that little bit special, the first PPV War Zone '97: Goldrush is scheduled for the middle of March and the titles will be decided through a number of different events such as, a battle royal, elimination tournaments and a gauntlet match. ~How To Join~ The NAEC is a great federation and most applications are accepted unless the wrestlers name is something like "Psycho" Bam Bam Michaels or Dieselkind. All players who have the time to send out a quality interview AT LEAST once a week. Everyone is encouraged to get involved in an angle or feud. This is the creation form, remember that when giving a tag team fill out one for each of the wrestlers. Information about you Your Real Name: Your E-mail address: Wrestler information Height: Weight: Hometown: Entrance Music: Alignment (heel,face or neutral): Cheating (never,only in retaliation,when opportune or always): Attitude (short fuse,calculating,cocky,normal): Style: Choose one of the following; Mat Wrestler Purist Technician All-rounder Oriental High flyer Brawler Latin wrestler Amateur wrestler Power wrestler Daredevil Cheater Ringriders (e.g bodyguard, manager, valet): A Long Description of your wrestler's appearence (3+ lines): A description of the ringriders\ringrider: The wrestler's background (i.e experience, former occupation etc.): The wrestler's age: Titles held in other leagues (if any): Finishing maneuver (up to three moves, please no somersault chokeslams etc.): Set up maneuver (performed before finisher): Signal for finisher (if any): Will the wrestler use chairs and tables etc.: Will the ringriders interfere, if so how often: Tag team information Tag Team Name: Tag Team Consists of: Tag Team Information (background etc.): Tag Team Finisher (if any): ~Interviewers~ If you want to send in an interview then send in a clip or pre-recorded comments. Or you can send in an interview with one of our four interviewers they are: Henry Lawrence: The main interviewer and the guy who runs the show, he conducts in-ring interviews. He is a face favouring guy who really knows his stuff, hey why else would he be the only person ever to interview President [name withheld]? Scott Hall: A rookie interviewer, he is unbias and a little wet behind the ears, he conducts off stage interviews. Arnus Gargoyle: Arnus is an interviewer of varying experience, he tends to favour faces and was on hand at the Thurmon/Loki incident on Tuesday. He conducts locker room and off stage interviews. Gary Miller: Gary is the only heel favouring interviewer, in fact he has grown to dislike goodie goodies with their bum kissing ways that he never interviews them. He conducts in-ring and locker room area interviews with guys he can relate to, i.e crazy slobbering psychopaths. ~The President~ This league is run by the President [name withheld] and his three commisioners and the championship commitee. More staff would be nice once the other divisions open up but at the moment it is also needed. ~Where to send your stuff~ If you have filled out your application form or have a question for me then e-mail me at [detail withheld]@new.co.za and I will try to respond by the end of the day. ~Final Word~ I really hope that lots of quality e-wrestling will come out of this federation and no, we don't use TNM, WLS or Rampage. from [name withheld] [detail withheld]@new.co.za _ _ | | | | | | | | | |_| | \ / |___| "Peace Man" The North American E-wrestling Corporation Established 1997 For over a whole week one of the emerging forces of e-wrestling President [name withheld] NAEC on-line at http://www.geocities.com/TimesSquare/Alley/1816/naec.html
