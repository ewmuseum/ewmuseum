[Roleplay to Critique] "Hollywood Gigolo" Shaun Riche

16 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[SCENE: California, just outside of Hollywood! The camera moves around as we movie studios in the distance, luxury cars driving through the streets. Off we see the sign to Hollywood Blvd.
The camera then swings around until we see the letters that spell out HOLLYWOOD up in the hills. The camera slowly zooms in on them as we can see a couple figures. .
very small from this distance. We slowly zoom in as they get a little larger but the camera fades out. . ]
[It fades back in as we find ourselves up in the hills and looking at the backs of two people as they look down on Hollywood. One of them turns around and it is Jonathan
Riche. Jonathan is
wearing a pair of hiking boots, a pair of slightly dusty black jeans, a camouflage army jacket, and a Nike hat turned backwards, and a walking cane in his left hand. . He taps the other man on the shoulder as we see that it is obviously The "Hollywood Gigolo"
himself, Shaun Riche. Shaun is garbed in a pair of skin tight leather pants, leaving absolutely nothing to the imagination. He also has on a purple silk shirt which is almost see through a pair of snake skin boots as well as a pair of black oakley sunglasses, the light of the sun gleaming off of them. Shaun looks at the camera and grins before he begins to speak.]
"HOLLYWOOD GIGOLO" SHAUN RICHE - The RIPWF. Rhode Island Professional Wrestling
Federation,
the lucky promotion that gets to have the first taste...
[Shaun sticks his tongue out in a crude fashion, wiggling it around a bit before pulling it back into his mouth. .]
SHAUN RICHE - ...of the Hollywood Gigolo himself! The man that is Gods gift to women. . [Shaun steps forward pointing out to Hollywood. .] SHAUN RICHE - The silver screen and of course <grins> Wrestling. Me and my brother... [Shaun points over at Jonathan..] SHAUN RICHE - Dominated and destroyed every tag division we ever became a part of, but alas..
[Shaun breathes in and lets it out in a sigh..] SHAUN RICHE - Jonathan injured himself and will be out of action for who knows how long. Now while he recoups I am going to add the name Shaun Riche to a new wrestling dynasty. I am coming into the RIPWF as a singles competitor and I am going to do what no other tag team wrestler has ever done and dominate with absolutely no singles wrestling experience.
The "Hollywood Gigolo", the man who has the right touch for every occasion is going to walk into
In The Beginning, and in the end I will walk away with something to make me look even better than I already do...
[Shaun grins arrogantly and winks. .] SHAUN RICHE - If that's humanly possible, by walking out of the Providence
Civic Center with
the RIPWF World Title strapped firmly around my flawless waist. .
[Shaun runs his hand down his chest to his stomach down to his waist...] SHAUN RICHE - 16 man tournament, that means I have to win four times in one night, if my match is correct, it never was my best subject. <smirks> But it doesn't matter how many I face, when you're this good, this great, this perfectly flawless you can do anything you want!
[Shaun walks over next to the "H" in Hollywood and yells out Steppenwulf as we hear it Echo over and over until it completely fades out. .] SHAUN RICHE - He's the first. The first who will get a taste. . [Shaun moves his hand up to his mouth and sticks his tongue out again, licking his fingers. .]
SHAUN RICHE - The first to feel the Gigolo Driver then get a front row seat to my Big Premiere. In the beginning. .
[Shaun chuckles then flashes his infamous Million $ smile...] SHAUN RICHE - A suiting name as it will mark the beginning of a new era in wrestling for the Riche brothers. Jonathan may be out of action, but he will be the man to lead me to the gold.
[Shaun looks over his shoulder at Jonathan then turns back to the camera, speaking a bit softer now. .]
SHAUN RICHE - Of course we all know The Hollywood Gigolo doesn't need anyone else. I have exactly what it takes to go all the way to the top...
[Shaun pauses and flashes his Hollywood grin..] SHAUN RICHE - And a whole lot more. [Shaun walks around to the middle of the H and hoists himself onto the middle then reaches up and pulls himself up on top of it. He stands up atop, looking down at the land of Hollywood below.
Shaun holds his arms up in the air then gives a little gyration for the camera. . .] SHAUN RICHE - Standing atop Hollywood much like I will stand up on top of the RIPWF. There have been three things that have always come to me over my life. Fame, women and championships.
In the RIPWF I will attain more fame, and even more women will flock to me, but most important of all, I will gain the thing that guarantees the other two, the Championship putting that gold belt...
[Shaun rubs his hand across his waist, a few fingers go a bit lower than the waist. The camera quickly moves back up to Shaun's face..]
SHAUN RICHE - Around my waist. .Four men will go up, and four men will go down on my way to superstardom in singles wrestling. Then the fame, fortune and women will all cling to me like a magnet because The Hollywood Gigolo is God's gift to women...
[Shaun sticks his tongue out yet again and wiggles it in the air...] SHAUN RICHE - ...The silver screen... [Shaun points out to Hollywood...] SHAUN RICHE - And wrestling... [The camera slowly pulls back as we see Jonathan still standing down on the ground as Shaun remains up on the H in the Hollywood sign as the screen then fades out...]
