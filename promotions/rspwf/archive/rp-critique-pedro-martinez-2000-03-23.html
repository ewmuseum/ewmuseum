RP Critique Pedro Martinez

23 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pedro has a big match coming up against William Robert, the pig- farmer turned millionaire. I wondered what y'all would you think of his rp here.
.. (The scene opens up outside of an anonymous baseball field, anonymous if you don't know where the Boston Red Sox play, otherwise...it's not so anonymous. Anyway, the camera is approaching the massive stadium but on the way in...we see a small boy. The boy has overalls on, the boy has a blade of grass in the corner of his mouth. The boy even has overalls and cowboy boots. And yes the boy has a mesh 4-H hat on his crop of dirty blond hair. As the boy draws nearer we can see him counting bills, large bills and whistling a tune...)
Little Boy - It's all about the Benjamins...babay! (The camera quickly moves inside, passing no one but emerging up the stairs, out through the dug out and onto the field. As they move into position we overhear Fasero, one of Pedro
Martinez's "Heavy Hitters".) Fasero - Damn. Those 4-H kids are heartless. (He's shaking his head sadly and then the camera turns to the mound, just in time to see Pedro shake off a few signs and then hurl a trademark fastball right down the pipe...)
Disembodied Guttural Utterance - SQQQUUUUUUUEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE! Pedro - STRIKE ONE! (My God, he's whipping 90 mile an hour plus fastballs at a gigantic sow [pig]) Pedro - Willy, thas you. A FAT, SLOBBERING, STINKING, PIG! Lissen! He sound jess like you! (Pedro pops a ball out of a nearby bucket and fires another king size fastball right at the pig! This one thuds into the pig's soft under belly and produces more of a snorting grunt from the heaving beast. The pig recoils momentarily but immediately dives back into the slop trough, greedily eating, ignoring the danger.)
Pedro - So busy eating. Stuffing hiss face. Willy you es a fat excuse for a wrestler and the Pedro is the BES pitcher in the big leagues. Who you think you fooling? Maybe fat Katie?
Should have bought a second pig so you two can be together, HA! The Pedro is going out west, to remind that coast they will never have my pitching skills...Angels, HA! Dodgers, HA!
Padres, HA! The Pedro is going west too also take a pig...to slaughterhouse. (Pedro turns, procures another ball, and fires harder than the first two. This ball catches the pig right upside the noggin.
Down goes the pig, deader than dead. From off camera the voice of Nomar Garciaparra is heard)
Nomar - Mother of heaven! You killed the pig! Dead!~ Pedro - Willy, The Pedro es a bad man...but I do bad things better than anyone else. On PPV....you are the pig. YOU are pig! YOU....ARE....PIG!!!!
