[GCW] The Suicidal High Flyers Latest Flash

28 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Suicidal High Flyers [You turn back to the channel providing "From The Core" because deep inside you just knew that The Suicidal High Flyers would be just about ready to speak. You were right. Ken Stanley and Tommy Kain are standing in the normal recording stuidos that the GCW provides for promo's just like these. Ken Stanley is wearing a black open vest, and a pair of blue jeans on. Tommy Kain has no shirt on, and is wearing a pair of white basketball type shorts. Enough of this crap, let them speak.]
TK: Yes, yes it's true. I fooled you all. You really thought for one minute, for even a second that I got injured by that car? Don't make me laugh. You see, the whole thing with The Flying Spaniards ...
it was all an act. You see from the beginning we were paying them in large assortments on monopoly money just to help us pull this entire thing off. When we heard that Viktor CarryABucketOfPiss, was just about ready to retire, and that Ken would be his last big match ...
well we just had to ruin it somehow. So we thank the future World
Champion, Derek Sky Walker for that lovely attack on Viktor.
KS: That's right, because Viktor ... you needed to be taught a lesson. You sit in the back, and you try and get everybody's attention by talking about your great promo's and your great matches.
It made me so sick, that I just had to do something about it. So instead of letting you leave the GCW with your head held high, I ruined your departure, and now just like the rest of us ... you gotta stay in this living hellhole they call the GCW.
TK: Now Viktor you tried to pull something this passed Tuesday that wasn't to our pleasing. You brought that talentless piece of crap,
Tim Dyson into the GCW. Tim, you think we forgot about you? How could we forget? Ken's father tried to give you every skill possible inside the ring to make you into a champion, but you were clumsier than Screech. Sure we were once friends, but that was only because
Ken's father asked us to be nice to you, so that you'd feel comfortable inside the ring with the people you were training with.
And then, when Ken and myself got the call from the GCW, you like your new partner cried your eyes out because it wasn't fair. You were
"supposed" to be in the spotlight.
KS: Take another thing from your partner, and DEAL WITH IT. Tim, you make me so sick. And Viktor, did you think we were afraid of Tim? Is that why you called him to GCW?
TK: That brings up another point, how did Timmy boy get into GCW? I mean, he obviously wasn't given a tryout because they would've seen what a clumsy mess he is.
KS: Not only that, but the boy was stupid too. Remember the time that idiot wanted to be like us so bad when we had our t-shirts made up back home?
TK: Oh yea, and he painted "SHF 4 Life" on his chest, and tried to make it off to be a t-shirt?
KS: Yea, what a moron. You see Tim, it's real easy to understand. I was reading through my contract and it said The Suicidal High Flyers do not have to step in the ring with somebody who can't pass the score of 200 on the SAT's. So Timmy boy, I guess that makes you [beep] out of luck.
TK: Viktor, you lame ass bag of cow piss. You listen to the words that come out of my mouth real carefully. The Suicidal High Flyers are the tag team of the past, the tag team of the present, and the tag team of the future. You ... you are just a poor champion of the past.
Are you going to cry about that too Viktor? You going to extend your contract in GCW just a little bit longer now, just so I can eat my words? Hell, why don't you just print out a new contract every damn day? It sure would make it look nicer instead of having all those eraser marks on it.
KS: And Viktor, I want to you think real hard about something. Do you think you have friends in GCW? Do you? Of course not. Where were those Steve Martin and Dan Akroyd rip-offs when I was beating the snot out of you with Derek in the middle of the ring? And, Where were they when Tommy and myself were making you beg for a dumb moron to hop the railing to save your ass? You no where they were? They were sitting in the back, drinking their FRUITopia, and eating sausage sandwhiches ... laughing about the tears coming out of your eyes. You see, we have real friends in Derek and Destiny.
TK: So Timmy and VK, here's what we'll do for you. You want a match with The Suicidal High Flyers? Well ... that's fine, we'll set up an
SAT exam for Timmy over there, and if he actually gets above a 200 then we'll have the match. And sorry VK, there will be no questions on Greek Mythology so it looks to me like you won't be able to help
Tim study for this one.
KS: Sorry Viktor. So you guys get back to us about that exam, and we'll think about granting you a match.
[The camera fades out as they walk off camera. The screen goes black]
