"The Natural" Justin Miller

13 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I never thought I'd be reduced to this. I'm looking for a fed to join, and I frankly don't have a clue which one to join. So, I figured I'd toss this up here for everyone's consideration. Now, let me just lay down a few
guidelines. No simmed feds. Booked only. Closed flash system. No "AOL FEDS." I don't care if the fed is run on AOL, but not one of those weird ones with colors and fonts. Also, please, save yourself the trouble of flaming me.
If you have something intelligent to say, I greatly appreciate it, but if not, please just keep it inside. And don't even bother telling me I ripped off
Brody Thunder, JW Hardin, or Ethan de Sade, because A) my flashes are nothing like theirs, and B) I really talk like I flash, so I didn't steal it from anyone but me. Oh, and sorry about the alignment, there's a few mistakes.
I've had some problems with the tab key. Plus, posting it to RSPWF screws it up worse for some reason. Oh well. Thanks for your time, I appreciate it.
--- [ A Trophy Room. The lights are dimmed to the point that it's hard to make much of the room out. The walls are filled with plaques, belts, and pictures of a career filled with its high spots, and its low's. Momentoes of a lifetime of fighting- night in, night out. The camera pans around the room, revealing a man seated precisely in the center. The man has intense, bright blue eyes.
His hair is cut short in a buzz-cut, and is Raven-Black. The man is wearing a royal blue suit. Underneath his jacket is a bright gold henley-collared t-shirt. On his feet are a brand new pair of gold Clark's Wallabees. The man is seated behind an immense chessboard. The pieces are elaborately carved in fantastic figures, and the board is constructed of what appears to be gold and silver. Seated in the corner of the chessboard is a blue deck of cards. The camera continues to pan up until the man is looking directly into the lens, his intense blue eyes betraying no emotion. That man, is Justin Miller. ]
NJM: Top 'a' the World, Ma. King 'a' Inferno. Pretty simple concept. Ya' win, ya' th' king. Ya' lose, well, that's th' name 'a' the game. Ten competitors. One King. Nine very disappointed men. [ Miller, looks around the room, smiles slightly, then returns to speaking. ] NJM: People've called me all kinds'a things. But one thing, in all my years 'a' wrestlin', that I've ne'er been called is stupid. Hell, you think I don't _KNOW_ I'm th' underdog? Th' new kid on the proverbial block, tryin' ta' make a name fa' hi'self. The rel'tive unknown, steppin' in the ring with all'a th' "tried 'n' true" vet'rans.
I'm even 'bout intimidated. 'Cept fa' one small thing. I ain't quite so new ta' this as some'a ya' might think. I've been 'round th' block quite a few times, t'be honest. I think 'a' all'a' th' men I'll step int'a the ring with, and I see a few men who's chops I've busted, and just as many who've busted mine right back. But more importantly, I see th' same ol' stuff. I see stuff I did 5 years ago. I see all th'
catchy little names. I see th' "Simply The Best's," and I see th' "Flamboyant's." Point is, I've seen all'a' it befo'. Matta'fact, I've
_BEEN_ all'a' it befo'. I've done that whole "pretty-boy" image. I've spent far more time that I'd like t'admit tryin' ta' convince everyone that I was better'n' them. But, ev'ryone moves on, and it's time fa'
all'a' ya' ta' get ta' movin'. Yeah, I'm th' underdog, alright. I may not be "Simply The Best." Matta'fact, I know I'm not. I've been beat befo', and I'm gon' be beat again. 'S'All beside th' point, in th' long run. Maybe I'll win. Maybe not. Don't matter much ta' me.
[Miller looks down at the chessboard, then looks back into the camera. ] NJM: In th' end, ain't 't'all just a game, anyway? One big glor'fied popularity contest? Nice guys really don't finish first, not in th' real world, 't'least. Ah, but all'a' that'll soon change. See, I rec'nize that guys like me don' gen'rally make it very far in this "dog eat dog"
bis'niss. Guys who come out, say it how they see it, an' don't depend on some gimmick or nickname to make the people love 'em, or hate
'em, depending. I've done beaten 'round _THAT_ partic'lar bush one time too many. I don't come out here an' "bite th' hand that feeds me." I know who th' lifeblood 'a' this bis'niss is, and tha's where my allegiance lies. Some 'a' ya's foolin' ya'selves, comin' out here with tha' whole "screw th' world" attitude, actin' like y'don't need anybody ta' back y'up. Take it from a man who's been there, an' most def'nitely done that. Until ya' come out here as ya'self, and rec'nize th' _REAL_ reason any 'a' us have a job, y'all're simply runnin' round in circles, chasin' ya' tails. In the end, it comes ta' all'a' us. It's just a matta' 'a' if it comes ta' ya' soon 'nuff. I hope, fa' alla y'all's sake, that it does. I'm a nice guy, really. But don't mistake that fa'
sof'ness. I don't like to lose any more'n' th' nex' guy, and I'll be a son 'a' a gun if I lose to all'a' y'all.
[With this, Miller picks the deck of cards up off the chesboard and begins to finger it absently as he continues to speak. ]
NJM: I know y'all're sittin' pretty in ya' locker rooms, thinkin' to ya'selves, "What's this joker talkin' 'bout?" I don't expect any 'a' ya' ta' take me seriously. Fa' all I care, ya' can sit back an' laugh a' me.
[Miller sits the deck back down, then flicks his wrist, like a magician, causing the Joker Card to appear in the palm of his left hand. ]
NJM: But, be warned, th' joke's on _YOU._ [Miller sits the Joker card down, then flicks his wrist again, this time producing the Ace of Spades in his left palm. ]
NJM: 'Cause th' En Jay Em's _ALWAYS_ got an Ace Up His Sleeve. [ With this, Miller begins to rise as the camera fades... to... black... ] ---- "The Natural" Handler of Justin Miller -OWF- 266-71-29 Career Record I Support Mike Sebastian!
