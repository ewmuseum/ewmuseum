[Critique] Another Christian Madison RP! FED HEADS READ!

15 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
As stated before. .I'm open for about one or two more feds since the closure of INWI is among us, and IWC shedding towards a different owner. I'm currently in 5 feds and juggling two more on the side you can say. .but looking for another steady well based fed!
Critique, I'm always looking to improve in certain areas so, here I am. Fed heads get your handbooks ready because you just might sign another great superstar. Right now I'm weighing my options as
I got offers before from many foundations, namingly CROW, and a few others...read on... not my best but yet again damn GOOD!
[The scene opens on a beautiful sunny day, somewhere in Tucson, Arizona. Glamorous clouds glazing across the early orange skies as the camera then fades down into a more field level position...the camera comes across of what seems to be an Carnival. The camera then angles around the carnival capturing it's huge banner spreaded along a parking lot. Meanwhile, camera shots shoot through the audience which was drew and finds it's way behind a couple holding hands...the couple turns around and the camera then fades into another location of the carnival. It then picks up a quick facial shot of one Christian Madison and
Jessica Steele. Madison looks back and smirks as he puts his arm around his girlfriend and walks away from an booth.]
[Madison is wearing a pair of brown slax, a white suit shirt, a brown vest and a pair a shiny black Gucci shoes. He has on a pair of Perry Ellis sunglasses nicely placed on the top of his head. Madison has an nice long gold chain resting around his neck as well. Although, Steele is dressed in a checkerboard skirt with a white long sleeve top merely cut below the cleavage barrier along with a pair of diamond earrings. They're bothed picked up heading over towards the dunking booth but
Steele points in the direction of horseshoe tossing. Christian then calling for the horseshoes as he begins to tos them.]
THE SPOTLIGHT - A game of horseshoes? Much like wrestling...never knowing where you'll end up. You can end up at the top or a long way from your target, but trying is the key to everything they say. Not only am I one of the most spectacular athletes that was placed upon this earth...though I am compassionate, in terms of capturing and placing gold around my waist. I play a game of horseshoe the same as I wrestle. I take a man for all that he got and then let out a bit of reserve that's been stored in me the whole time. You never let a man know that you're weak, nor tired for that matter because as I would do...I'd capitalize on it big time. I throw these horseshoes to win a prize and in International Championship Wrestling I do the same, but not for a prize, but as an ambition, a dream to win the
International title...winning a prize is one thing but capturing a life long dream is definately another. And D.B. Bugg is just like the booth controller, he has the prize or shall I say title in his possession but come March 20th your whole game wil be put to test! Meaning, what you have today could easily be gone tomorrow.
All I can say now is, coming soon, one of these horseshoes will lock around one of those polls as I will hold the International belt in my hands. Although, some people get lucky, but luck has absolutely nothin' to do with this matter. And although lady luck was on your side once before Bugg, doesn't mean it sticks with ya'. The similarities of a game of horseshoes and wrestling are remarkably alike, because hoping that the horseshoe wrap around a pole is the same as hoping to win the title at The Last Hoorah. But still, no matter how much you hope and pray...the effort and concentration you put in it just might determine the outcome. As ya' can see...I'm not out working out or training because I spent all week doing so already...I'm not going to stress myself out, I'm gonna pace myself and chill down but as for you...I bet you're working your ass off to stay in shape with the trim little youngster such as The Spotlight.
[Madison then tosses a horseshoe as it hits and wraps around the pole. Christian then picks out an prize and chooses a giant Lion. He then gives it to Steele as he begins to speak.]
THE SPOTLIGHT - See? Nothin' but focus and concentration my friend...to put it in other words Bugg, you have that one prize that I'm trying to get and noone else can get but me. I throw the horseshow around the pole and I win! As simple as that but knowing you're as illerate as a blind man walking into traffic. You can clown around, drink at titty bars, or just eat twinkies while sitting on your remote at home but when it's time to step into that cage, I hope you're damn ready and prepared because you're about to feel the lethal warfare arsenal of the one, the only, The Spotlight!
[Madison tosses another horseshoe but misses as Steele hops in excitement and leans againsts Christian.]
THE CHEERLEADER - That's alright bay-bee. Maybe next time...you can't always win you know. THE SPOTLIGHT - My point exactly, Bugg. Even though you're champion...it doesn't limit you from losing or anything else for that matter. You're champion, so what...you think just because you hold that belt means you can do whatever you want? Well, it doesn't work that way buddy. It doesn't mean the world owes you a great debt in society my friend. It doesn't always happen the way you plan things you know...and if everything's on schedule then I say about this time in a few days, I'll be walking around carrying your...err.._MY_ International belt.
However you want to slice it D-Man. .you're walking away with nothin' but space and opportunity because you'll be looking and feeling a little lonely without that strap across your shoulders. And you can guarantee this time, is the charm, third time they it is and by god...have i been eating my Lucky Charms!
[Christian begins to snicker as Jessica then steps in.] THE CHEERLEADER - And also...this returning star will be a major role played into this hand. I mean, coming from nowhere and knowing nothin' is what possibly could be the major playing factor here. Here's a guy who's probably watched you on television and studied you from the start, so he practically knows you and your moves meanwhile you're stuck with nothin' but a meanless thought in your head.
These type of things could be dangerous, especially how the match is already. Promise me you won't do anything stupid or life threatening. . THE SPOTLIGHT - Yeah. .Yeah! THE CHEERLEADER - Do it! [Madison holds his hand behind his back and crosses his fingers.] THE SPOTLIGHT - Alright. .alright! I promise. Satisfied? THE CHEERLEADER - Pretty much, but in my mind...this returning mystery man is just a bunch of crap and I know you can take him. This is to just throw your mind off your set gameplan, that's all. Don't even worry about it...once he steps into the ring you''ll adjust to his style as you do to everyone. I guess you just grow on people like that, [Chuckles] eh?
[Steele pauses.] THE SPOTLIGHT - I guess you're right. You know, it could be Tony Paseno... after all he was a former International champ, I think. Or even "Varsity" JacK
Vars. It could be any one of these superstars...but I guess time will only tell.
Mystery Man, Returning Star, whatever you want to be called...if I were you I'd knowledge up a little bit because you're dealing with an sensational athlete my friend born with talents you could never imagine or dream about...so I recommend you go out and find D-Man and start revolution and help find his fuckin remote.
Cause you plan on hopping on The Spotlight express bandwagon then you can somewhere else with that bullshit because next thing you know in that caged ring is The Spotlight standing on the outside with his hands raised and the belt being strapped around my waist. Both of you jabronies will be sent back to hell,
I'm pretty sure you have someone awaiting your arrival. D-Man, you and your ass munching pal who remains a Mystery can both go chew on a nutt...The Spotlight has just shined on another parade. When you're dealin' with The Spotlight, he's no ametur flashlight and once again The Spotlight will be left. .
..."CENTER"... ..."OF"... ..."_ATTENTION_"... [Christian then throws his final horseshoe wrapping it around the pole as
