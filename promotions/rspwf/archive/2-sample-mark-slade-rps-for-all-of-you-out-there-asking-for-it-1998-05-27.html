#2 Sample Mark Slade RPs for all of you out there asking for it

27 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
================================================= "Now prepare yourself like a man; I will question you, and you shall answer Me." Job 38:3 [The scene opens inside a run down bar. A heavyset man wearing a white t-shirt and blue jeans stands behind the bar washing glasses. Two men are playing darts and arguing over a basketball game that's being shown on a television that is hanging over the bar. At a table in the corner is a man slumped over his table apparently asleep. Several empty bottles are sitting on the table around him. The only thing visible is his silver colored hair. Suddenly the front door opens as a figure stands in the doorway slowly looking around. He is wearing khaki pants and a white polo shirt and his long black hair is slicked back in a ponytail.
He pulls off his sunglasses and slides them in his shirt pocket as he walks slowly over to the bartender]
Keith: So the board of health still hasn't closed this place down yet, eh. [The bartender looks up at the man and stares for a second before his face breaks into a huge smile. He leans over the bar and shakes Keith's hand]
Jesse: I'll be damned if it isn't "Kool" Keith Kelly. How the hell you been? Keith: I've been doing pretty good. After my knee injury I retired from wrestling and i've been relaxing. My business is doing pretty good. [He stops smiling] So where's Mark at?
Jesse: In the corner over there. He's been drinking like a fish. He's really hurting Keith. After I found out how bad it really was I didn't know what to do. You were the only person I could think of that might be able to talk some sense into him.
Keith: You did the right thing Jesse. I'll take it from here. [Keith walks across the room to the table in the corner. Mark still is asleep at the table. Keith stands in front of the table and slams his fist on it. Mark bolts up and stares at Keith drunkenly.]
Keith: Never thought that when we met up again it would be back in here. So you decided to start hitting the bottle again?
[Mark glares at Keith for a few silent moments as his face slowly contorts into a scowl] Mark: Well isn't this something? What are you doing here...slumming? [Keith sits down at the table across from Mark] Keith: Let's cut the sh#%. What the hell are you doing here? The last
I saw you were supposed to have a match at "A Night to Remember" with that psycho John Smith. Isn't that in a couple of days? Look at you.
Your in no shape to fight. And your drinking again. You swore not to drink after what happened last time.
Mark: [Angrily] Yeah, so what? I'm a grown man. If I want to have a drink then i'll have one. And since when are you so concerned about my life. I haven't heard from you for almost two years and suddenly you pop up and start trying to tell me what's what. Go to hell.
Keith: Don't give me that. I kept in touch, but I had a life too. Just because i'm not talking to you every day doesn't mean we aren't like family anymore. We've been through too much together. I got a business to run and a new wife. I don't have time to raise hell like we used to. Things change Mark, but obviously you haven't. Your still an selfish, egotistical drunk. Your trying to pull this pity routine, but i'm not buying it. Don't try to push this off on everyone else. I might not have kept in contact, but how hard did you try to reach me?
Mark: Listen to the big man. You got your little business going and you got a woman. Your just hot sh#% aren't you. Well don't think your better than everyone else. I knew you before all of that. I remember when you and Terry used to be right here at the table with me drinking and partying. Before you hurt your knee and quit wrestling.
Before you changed into a little yuppie. Keith: And I remember when you said you were going to quit drinking. It was about 20 miles down the road from here. You were drunk and decided you could drive. Do you remember that night? The night that you killed those people. You swore to me that you were through drinking. You said...
Mark [Lowering his voice] Of course I remember. Not a night goes by where I don't think about it. Every time I lie down to go to sleep I remember it. And sometimes if I concentrate I can see it like i'm there again. I can smell the burnt rubber from the tires skidding. I can smell the liquor on my breath as I scream out. I can see the looks on that woman's face as she slams on her brakes trying to stop.
And I can see that girl asleep in the front seat. Sometimes I dream about it. I dream that i'm back there again driving the car home. Only this time I know what's going to happen, but I can't stop it. I'm trapped in the car and it's driving like it has a mind of it's own. I try to hit the brakes, but they don't work. I try the wheel, but it just spins uselessly. I can't unbuckle my seat belt and jump out. All I can do is sit and watch as the car heads toward that intersection. Everytime it's the same thing. The same result. The crash...and the blood.
Keith: Then why are you doing this now? Why are you drinking again. Mark: You don't understand Keith. Every day I go on with this in the back of my mind. At it didn't really hit me. Not for a while. I didn't accept it though. I kept going on with my life. Then after a while things started coming apart. The ICCW folded. I started having all these financial problems. The punks in the NeCW screwed me over right before they folded. And when I got that phone call about
Terry...well I couldn't take it anymore. Keith: So you quit. Mark: No I didn't quit. You did. You quit when you left the ICCW. You went off and changed into some suit wearing a$$%#*&. Now you think you can show up and judge me.
Keith: [Raising his voice] You are so messed up it's ridiculous. You try and blame everyone else for your problems except the one person who's fault it is...yours. No one made you get in the car that night.
No one made you go to the NeCW. No one made you spend money you didn't have. You think I quit? I had to stop wrestling or I might have really messed up my knee. And don't even try to bring up Terry as an excuse. Have you even been to see him since he went into the hospital? No you haven't. You've been right here crawling into a bottle trying to blame the world for everything. And now you've given up on your match at the WCoW. So you sit here and think about that.
If you want to roll over and die then go ahead, but at least be man enough to take responsibility for your own screw ups.
[Keith stands up and walks to the door] Keith: Out of the three of us you always were the best in the ring. When
Terry and I had to quit we still got some satisfaction watching you go on. You see we had no choice, but to quit. You had a choice though.
You had the potential to go all the way to the top, but you pissed it off. You had a chance to do what you always wanted with your life. A chance that we didn't have. A chance that woman and her daughter had before you took it away from them.
[Keith walks out of the bar. Mark sits at the table staring after him for a second before he jumps up and runs after him. Mark runs out the door and catches up to Keith outside in the parking lot]
Mark: Come back here! You think you can judge me? [Mark throws a clumsy punch at Keith which he easily ducks. Keith punches Mark in the stomach doubling him over. He then hits him in the face with two quick punches which send Mark down to the ground]
Keith: Look at you. Your so drunk that you can't even take a punch. Get up! [Mark slowly struggles to get up from his hands and knees. Keith gives him a kick in the stomach that knocks Mark over onto his back. He stares up at Keith from the ground]
Keith: just what I thought, you really have given up. Your worthless. [Keith walks to a black eclipse and climbs in. He starts up the car and drives off leaving a cloud of dust behind him. Mark stays laying on the ground for a few long minutes staring up at the sky. Slowly he starts to get up. He stands up and walks back to the door of the bar. When he gets there he raises a hand to open it, but stops with his hand inches from the door. He slowly drops his hand and turns around]
Mark: [whispering to himself] I haven't given up yet you bastards. Not yet [He slowly walks over to a battered porsche and takes the keys out of his pocket. He stares down at the keys for a few moments before tossing them on the hood. Mark turns and starts walking out of the parking lot into the desert.
