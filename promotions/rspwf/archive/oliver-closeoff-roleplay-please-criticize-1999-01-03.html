Oliver Closeoff Roleplay ... please criticize.

3 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is one of my Oliver Closeoff roleplays. Please help me out here with some constructive critism or bashing , or whatever.
*The scene opens up to a beautiful view of dark night , lit up by the half-moon and sky full of stars. You can hear the sound of the tide crashing in on the rocky shore down below.*
*The camera fades back on to the back of a huge penthouse , owned by Oliver Closeoff. As the camera zooms in onto a deck off the back of this penthouse , Oliver Closeoff is shown in a hot tub , with 2 lovely ladies ... one on each arm. As the camera gets closer , it reveals that both girls are topless , with the bubbles of the hot-tub just above their chests. Oliver , with his
million-dollar grin , begins to talk.* Oliver: Scott Johnson? Call yourself "Sexy" ... {Oliver gives a "wondering" stare} ... I sign up with the AWF and get put in the ring with Scott Johnson? Scott , the only problem I have with beating the hell out of you , is the fact that ... you are obviously one of my fans. I cherish all the fans out there of Oliver Closeoff and I understand where you can't help getting thrown in the ring with your idol ... but brother , that's life. I expect what I expect out of all of my fans out of yah' ... {Oliver shrugs his shoulder} ... I expect you to go into that squared circle ... {Oliver points his index finger} ... and take your loss like a man. It's that simple
Scotty ... you go in there , with a good head , lie down for the KING of the
AWF and get pinned , 1 ... 2 ... 3 , I will promote you to president of the
Oliver Closeoff fan club. And Scott ... you know as well as I , how big of an honor that is.
*Oliver takes his hands and puts them on the two girls heads. He pushes them down below the surface of the bubbly hot tub. Oliver begins to smile.*
Oliver: So ... Scott Johnson , come Sunday night , next Sunday night ...
{Oliver takes a deep breath} ... you will become more famous than you have imagined as you go down by the hands of ... {Another deep breath} ... Oliver
Closeoff. When I defeat you , Scott , 1 , 2 , 3 ... {emmm} ... Your name will be heard_world wide_as the coverage of Oliver Closeoff is found in every continent on this beautiful planet. Consider it a favor Scott ... a big_ONE_!
*The girls heads pop up above the water as the push their hair back out of their eyes.* Oliver: Before I go finish what was just started , I want the AWF and you
Scott Johnson , to remember one saying ...
Oliver Closeoff ... in no time! *The scene begins to move out as the girls and Oliver get up out of the water. Their backs are facing the camera to reveal that they did have no top on but nothing is shown. The three grab their towels off the towel rack as they walk inside the house.*
*The scene sets back to the stars and moonlit sky as the camera fades away.* Oliver Closeoff [name withheld] Handler of: (Dillion "Shades" Galloway - AIWA) (Oliver Closeoff - USX and AWF)
