[FLASH] Vince Taylor [CRITIQUE] [FREE AGENT]

24 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: Los Angeles, California. Time: 3:30 P.M. The camera opens in the back of a limo. You see a quick view of the outside surroundings and you see other cars on the road, and a few buildings. The camera then turns to the inside of the limosuine and you see "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor...]
[Vince is wearing white Armani suit, that has black pinstripes on it. Covering his head is a fashionable white hat, that matches his suit. You see his gleaming Rolex, over his cocoa brown wrist. On his face are his trademark gold framed Rayban sunglasses. In his left hand is a fresh, unlit cigar. While in his right hand is a glass of white wine. Vince sets the glass down, and begins to speak...]
"THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: Well, well, well, would you believe it... Vince
Taylor is a perfect 2-0 in UCWP competition, just like I said I would be.
Let's recap a little, shall we? First I waltzed into the UCWP and took out The
Agent with flying colors. Then this past Sunday, I was put up against Henry
Martin. I'll give him some credit, he did put up a fight, but like they all do... he couldn't handle me for the whole match, and he eventually felt the
Money Maker. After that it was lights out, as I picked up victory number two.
[Vince pauses and reaches into his pocket. He pulls out a lighter, and lights his cigar. He takes a few drags off of it, and exhales the smoke with appreciation. He then continues to speak...]
"THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: And finally it looks like I'm turnin' a few heads. People are looking over and sayin', "Who is this fine lookin' black man? He sure can wrestle." It should come as no surprise to any of you though, 'cause I told you all that from the beginning. But I guess some people have to see to believe. Well I showed everybody, and I'm finally gettin' the respect that I deserve. And it looks like I will be really be tested in the ring for the first time, 'cause at Fireworks I'm steppin' into the ring with
Christian York.
<Sarcastically.> "Mr. Charisma." Man, you got it tough. Last week you were in the main event and got your ass kicked by James Pillow, and this week you have the unfortunate task of havin' to step into the squared circle with me. Which is worse, I don't know, but I can guarantee you that our encounter on Sunday won't be very
enjoyable for you. [Vince pauses and takes another puff off of his cigar, and then he begins to speak once more...]
"THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: Christian buddy, you're good, and you're definitely one of the best this federation has to offer. You're accomplished here, but then again, you haven't faced a "Ladies Man" yet either. I've come too damn far to lay down to you. So this Sunday when you step into the ring, you are going to receive the beating of your lifetime, courtesy of me.
[Vince puts his cigar in an ashtray, as if he is already done with it.] "THE LADIES MAN" VINCE TAYLOR: You see "The Ladies Man" is gonna walk into the
UCWP headquarters, and grab the attention of every woman in attenedence on
Sunday. And Christian, when the ladies go wild when I step out, you're gonna wish you had half the charisma that I do. Then I'm gonna step into the ring with York. And we all know what will happen next. I'll wrestle with him for a little while, showing off my various wrestling abilities. But then the time will come when it'll be time to end it, and I'll haul Christian's frail ass up to the top rope. And then I will hit the most breathtaking move in the sport of wrestling, the Money Maker. Then it's the 1..2..3, and I pick up yet another victory.
Then I guess I'll head into the the pay per view as the odds on favorite to win the Lightweight Title. And after I pick up this victory, why shouldn't I be?
Bring on Pillow, bring on York, bring on Wallace, I ain't gonna back down from any of 'em. I hope it's a four way dance, that way I can kick four asses in one night. And to top if all of, I'll walk away fifteen pounds heavier as the
Lightweight Champion.
But I don't want to get ahead of myself, so right now the only thing I am concentrating on is Christian York. Everybody better be ready for a total dominant performance from on Sunday. And just a little food for though for
Christian York... everybody knows that somebody with "Charisma" is no match for....
THE. . . LADIES. . . MAN!! [The scene fades to black as Vince finishes off the remaining wine in the glass...] =-[name withheld]-= Handler of: "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor [ASP, BFTW, UCWP] Maybe a Free Agent
