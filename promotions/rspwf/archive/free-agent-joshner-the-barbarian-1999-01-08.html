(FREE AGENT) Joshner the Barbarian

8 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Joshner The Barbarian is sitting on a park bench in Chicago, Illinois, the sight of the next Sunday Night Savage. He is eating from a bag of potato chips. He finishes off the bag and tosses it into a trash can. People are riding by on bikes, roller blades, and skate boards. Joshner gets up and walks down the sidewalk. He is shaking from the cold, he notices a coffee house and he walks in and takes a seat at a back table.)
Joshner The Barbarian: As I look over the AWA roster I notice some familiar faces. Big Bully Brandon, the world's most predictable backstabber. You betrayed me twice before in the FRW and the EAW. I got my eyes on you.
Phoenix X, the only man in this business that has my respect. We have fought on 4 occassions. I am 2-2 against you. Everytime we get together in the ring it has been an epic battle for the ages. Our epic battles will be talked about years and years down the road. I also notice the enigma that is Czar. Czar, I have never really figured you out. Everytime you get mad at someone you dawn a mask and try to ruin them. It's never worked, and I have never let you get under my skin and I never will.
Waiter: Sir would you like some coffee, maybe a cappucino? Joshner The Barbarian: No. Waiter: Well, sir if you don't order something I'm afraid you will have to leave. (Joshner stands up and his big frame is more than intimidating towards the smaller waiter. Joshne shoves him out of the way and through one of the tables. Joshner walks out of the coffe shop, he goes across the street to a pizza pub.)
Joshner The Barbarian: I have been laying back and watching the AWA over the past few weeks. I know about the AWA's loss of Ed The Enforcer and
Clockwerx. I knew both guys. Me and Clockwerx never fought but we both took shots at each other, and I never knew Ed too well. I also have witnessed the formation of The Elite Extremists, as well as their crumble, and the formation of The Shooters. Well, to all of the fallen heroes of the AWA, I am hear to annihilate and destroy all of you. I know that alot of AWA's
"stars" think they are the greatest. Guys such as Czar. Czar you are now just the shadow of the legend you used to be. I have your rise, your fall, your demise, your destruction, your resurrection, and I am now here to watch you crumble again. But see I don't just live in the past. Looking over the roster, I see a man I have never heard of. I know nothing of him, and Jack Payne I want you to be my first victim. I want to destroy you first. You should feel appreciated that I want to decimate before anyone else. You will see what Barbaric Pride is as I beat you beyond recognition.
As your life passes by your eyes, realize that it was me that caused it. AWA and Jack Payne, PREPARE TO BE CONQUERED!!!
