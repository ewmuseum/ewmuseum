Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My wrestler TOUGH TOMMY CHAOS currently competes in the WWA. I am looking to join a few more feds (and yes I roleplay). Here are the requirements a fed must meet:
(1) NO REAL WRESTLERS (2) ROLE-PLAYS MANDATORY (3) MORE THAN 12 MEMBERS (4) SEND INFO TO: [detail withheld] This is a submission form I used for another fed, lmk if you need any other info... ["You Shook Me (All Night Long)" by AC/DC plays over the loudspeakers.] BOONE: Introducing first ... accompanied by Big Ed, hailing from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, standing 6 feet 10 inches tall and weighing 456 pounds, here is .... "TOUGH" TOMMY CHAOS!!!! ["Tough" Tommy Chaos walks down the aisle. He is led by Big Ed. Big Ed is wearing a black three-piece suit and he is on his cell phone. "Tough" Tommy
Chaos follows behind, wearing a black trenchcoat. Underneath the coat, is a grey "CHAOS" t-shirt and black pants. He has grey boots to match his shirt. He has a neatly trimmed brown goatee and looks to be in great shape. The fans are going nuts. He walks very slowly to the ring and the camera focuses on a female fan. She faints after he passes by her and paramedics are running towards the stands.]
[As Chaos gets to the ring, the lights go out except for a spotlight in the middle of the ring where he stands. He drops to one knee and gives the finger to the direction of his opponent. His theme music fades, and the bell rings.]
