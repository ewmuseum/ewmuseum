I need a Competitive FED ( Free Agent )

8 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok I have no e-feds as of now so I am looking to join one, I want a few with TOP ROLEPLAYERS no 5 line roleplays. It has to do the roleplaying on the Webpage also i don't want any e-mail feds. If you have one that you think fits my demands send it over. Here is a Sample Roleplay for my character Jake Stone just so you know how I roleplay.....
<b><font color= red>( The scene focuses in on a log cabin in the middle of the woods with a lake out in the back. Then a reporter comes walking into the camera's view and starts to walk gingerly around the house towards the back. He calls out Jake Stone's name a couple times and no one answers.
The reporter looks back and says something to the camera man and then turns around and continues to walk. Suddenly an Owl lets out a screech and the reporter jumps into the air and lands flat on his butt. He gets up looking embarrassed and takes a step when suddenly a rope catches his feet and he is strung up from a tree hanging by his feet. Within seconds a man comes diving out of the tree does a flip when he hits the ground and quickly gets on his feet and when he looks over towards the camera you see its Jake
Stone wearing a torn M.I.B. shirt, faded out torn blue jeans, a full beard, and a knife in his right hand. Jake grabs the rope and quickly cuts the reporter down then walks over to a tree leans up against it and begins to carve into the tree as he speaks in a real low serious tone.)</b></font red>
Well you finally decided to show up. Its about damn time your lucky I didn't leave you hanging there all day and let the wolves eat you. I called Edwards and had him send someone out here because since the PPV everyone knows I was on leave and while I was away I've had alot of time to think and see what was really going on and now that I'm back I have alot on my mind so sit down grab a chair, shut your mouth, and keep the camera rolling because you will want to hear what I have to say.
<b><font color=red>( Jake walks over to a table reaches into a cooler and grabs two beers. He flips one to the reporter then pops one open for himself. He walks over to a lawn chair sits down, takes a swig of beer then begins to speak.)</b></font red>
Let me go back and talk about what happened at the PPV in front of millions of people. I was beating Simon's a$$ just like I said I would and he was begging for me to stop and crying like a baby and just when I was going to
Spike him through the mat, his butt buddy <b>Golden Eye</b> dressed as a f***ing weiner salesman hit me from behind and cost me the World Title.
First off Goldy Locks keep your private life out of the EWA we could care less if you play with weiners in your spare time just don't bring it to the
EWA because don't want to know that kind of stuff. Now Goldy I know what happened your buddy Simon was so scared he told you if you attacked me at the PPV and made sure he kept the title he would give you a spot in
Brutality Inc. and protect you from everyone, well your friend has made a big mistake and threw you right into a situation you can't get out of. The fact he failed to mention when he was begging for your help is he is scared to death of M.I.B and he couldn't protect you from the Girl Scout troops much less the baddest stable ever assembled, so you may of enjoyed what you did and where your at right now, but Goldy trust me no matter where you run or where you hide Simon and your little friends can't protect you from the a$$ beating your going to receive.
<b><font color=red>( Jake slices off a piece of apple and chews it up then takes another drink of beer. He wipes the blade of his knife off and continues to speak.)</b></font red>
Ok now lets speak some more about<b> Simon</b>. Hey Champ you come out after the PPV and swore you had nothing to do with the attack and then we all find out it was your plan the whole time to keep me from taking the title, well thats cool I like it when they play hard to get, but that beating you took at the PPV when you was begging for mercy will be a cake walk compared to what I do to you the next time we meet in the ring and if your sorry a$$ won't fight me again I'll wait all night long outside your house and beat you up in the street either way Brain your going down.
Oh smart guy how did you like that little trip Mercenary and the Boys took you on last night? All I can say is you seen where the power is M.I.B.
does what they want when they want and all Brutality Inc. and Revolution are is a couple of two bit stables that give us someone to beat up whenever
were bored. Simon go ahead and keep talking all your trash, but you proved to the world what kind of champion you were at the PPV when you had someone save your a$$, so keep ducking me and hiding away, but like I told
Goldy I'll eventually catch up to you and when I do your going to pay.
Now we were going to meet in this Lottery Carnage, but you and your lawyers went and cried to Edwards wanting to change things up because you knew if you got in the ring with Stone again you would never live to defend your
title again. Your a worthless, gutless coward who has skated by on thin ice for awhile now, but trust me its heating up Champ and soon the Ice will break and Stone will be there to catch you.
<b>Jupiter</b> you barely deserve a comment after the way you cried last night when I was pounding away on your head, but if you ever step out of line and insult one of M.I.B. again you'll have to answer to Jake Stone and little man your just not ready for that.
<b><Font color=red>( Jake eats the last part of the apple then throws the knife and sticks it in the middle of the tree where he was carving.)</b></font red>
Ok enough about those losers I've wasted enough breath on them its time to address some other people and first off its going to be <b>Highlander</b>.
Well Highlander you made a very big mistake when you crossed M.I.B. we take are rules serious Highlander and you broke a very serious rule never ever show mercy and you did, so now you bailed out and your going to do the honorable thing, well you have skills or you would of never been a member of the elite group, but your best bet would be stay with your Bird Friend
Falcon live in the rafter because punk if you come down and start messing with M.I.B your a$$ will be in a box on the first train back to Scotland and trust me it will be a painful experience.
<b>Fury</b> i put your a$$ into place once before and I'll tell you this only once more if you EVER mention M.I.B again in one of your interviews I personally come over and beat your whole stable up and turn you guys into the EWA cheerleaders. You boys don't belong in the big league so stay down fighting mid carders and keep your mouth shut. Thats your last warning I suggest you do what i say.
<b>Killer Cowboy Masterson</b> the same goes for you if you ever talk about the M.I.B. again your worst nightmare will become a reality because you will get a first hand look at why were the best. I don't know who you think you are talking about M.I.B. like your John Wayne, but if you have any intentions of making wrestling a long career for you then shut your damn mouth and sit your a$$ down because punk you couldn't even be the
M.I.B.'s shoe shine man much less beat us in the ring. So shut up and and enjoy that money that comes in every so often from wrestling, but if you ever make the mistake and mention us again, well trust me it won't be a pretty site for your family to witness.
<b><font color=red>( Jake gets up and walks over to where he carved into the tree. He continues to speak)</b></font red>
Well lastly let me address my secret admirer some loser by the name of
<b>Parasite</b> or <b>Paragon</b>. Hell I don't know its some loser trying to become famous the quick way. Well Parasite you got M.I.B.'s attention with that stunt you pulled last night DDTing my buddy Merc, so you want a match Hell yeah I'll give you a match and trust me your going to get alot more than you can handle. You may of thought the best way to come into the
EWA and make a name was fight the baddest man walking today, but what you failed to realize is Jake Stone takes no man lightly and Jake Stone lets no man off easy. Now I know what you were thinking i'll fight Jake Stone and he'll be easy on me and when he beats me I won't get hurt and then everyone will know me and I'll be a big star, well Parasite you'll be well known, but not the way you want to be, you'll be known as the first EWA competitor to be crippled by Jake Stone. I don't like your kind and I don't like your style, so Quit sending me Love Letters and telling everyone I'm Next because Big Man I've got an open contract already signed in the Prez's office all you have to do is sign your name. Jake Stone runs from nothing in this world and Parasite your going to realize why I am the most feared man walking today when I make an example out of you in front of the whole world.
This interview is over get off my property before I throw you off. <b><font color=red>( Jake walks away from the tree and as the reporter starts the leave the camera focuses in on the carving on the tree and carved into the tree were the words <u>EWA the fury has been unleashed</u> and the scene slowly fades away )</b></font red>
