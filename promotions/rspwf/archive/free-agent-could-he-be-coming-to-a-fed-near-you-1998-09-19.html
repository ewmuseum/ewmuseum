[Free Agent] Could he be coming to a fed near you?

19 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SuP all, Well, I am thinking about bringing back my character, The Gambler, because after I did an RP for the JRMC, I started getting back into handling. Now, I can not promise anything, but I wanna see what kind of offers I get. Thanx for your time.. here's a sample RP. Not my best, but not too bad considering its my second RP in the last five months. Keep in touch.
BTW, sorry bout the cursing, but my characters a well educated dirtbag .. what can I say? =)
~~~~~~ [SCENE: An allie behind the Hoosier Dome. darkness envelopes the camera. a muffled voice drone's on and on for what seems like an eternity.]
VOICE: What the fuck? Why do I do this to myself? I hate volenteering for this
_shit_. [kicks a can] Damn Jay .. caught me off guard .. convinces me to do this, and sign that fucken contract [pause] _piss drunk_.
[silence] [the sound of a zippo thrusting open ends the silence, as well as the darkness. while lighting his cigarette we can bearly make out his desheveled face: eyes blood shot, unshaven, nostrils bright red, black hair greeced to his head.
already we know this ICON, he is none other then, the Gambler. darkness.] [kicks the can again] THE GAMBLER: Haven't wrestled in months .. fat and out of shape .. A'int got a clue whose even in this darn thing. Its almost criminal, what this guy does ..
I swear to fucken god. [spits down] Probably get killed .. these guys have all been wrestling for years, but not I
.. NOOO, I sign this contract out of the blue .. must have been delutional or something. Who is even in this?
[lights his lighter, then digs deep into the warn pocket of his black jacket. slowly withdraws a crubbled piece of paper, then holds the lighter near. begins to read] Memorial Card? [stops] What? [face pail] Jay's Dying? God damn .. who would have thought? [continue's to read] Verhoeven, Van Strife, Creed, Mathews, Joe Reed, Blah, blah blah .. bunch of guys who are used to having smoke blown up their ass .. what else is new?
Haven't I seen these same few guys in 20 other tournements? Originally Jay .. don't you want to go out with a bang? [lights the paper on fire, illuminating his face] Same old, same old .. Same guy will win, same shit will go down, can't anyone be original anymore? I mean seriously ..
[begins walking] Wrestling needs something new .. a new face .. with fresh idea's .. and enough talent to carry him to the moon and beyond .. not these old momuments holding us up!!
[stops] People ask me to join federation after federation, but do not understand why I refuse.. they do not understand, why I go against them .. but my reasons, are as bright as day. When I look around, I see the same things being done, that were originated with the "LOOP" a few years back. Same matches, same kinds of jumping, same issues with the presidents.
[stares coldly into the camera] It _sickens_ me people, honestly. [begins walking again] I guess that is what I will use this tournement for then .. Forget the winning, the egos, and the like .. forget superstars and the sub par athletes .. this is about developing a new generation of not only wrestlers, but federations as well .. maybe Jay can even help a few people with this thing .. and I will do what it takes to _make sure_ this all goes through .. Why?
[stops] Cause hell, [pauses] Ya only live once .. [with that, he continue's walking out of the allie, leaving the camera in the darkness] [fade] ~~~ -[name withheld] Classic On-Line Wrestling President The Gambler ( 27-2-3 ) *Retired* http://www.notupyet.com =)
