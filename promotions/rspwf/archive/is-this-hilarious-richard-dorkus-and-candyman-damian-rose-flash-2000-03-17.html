[IS THIS HILARIOUS?] Richard Dorkus and "Candyman" Damian Rose Flash

17 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, my name is Richard and I handle "Disgusting" Richard Dorkus. Me and Damian aka "Candyman" Damian Rose worked on a joint flash for the upcoming supercard ran by [name withheld] called "Wrestlebowl". I'm posting this because I'd like to see y'all opinions on this.
Do you think it's good? ... Funny ... Hilarious... Boring ... Crap... Or Useless! I wanna hear from ya.. Reply back to this post... Sit back, scroll down and enjoy... _______________________________________________________ [We open up in a local food court. The common food outlets surround the court such as McDonalds, Taco Bell and so on. People are crowded around the entire complex, though our attention is directed towards a familiar face.
Dressed in a pair of long black pants, a white business shirt and a pair of sun-glasses.. this man looks around the food court in search of something.
After a few seconds, he raises his glasses above his eyes.. revealing it to be none other then "The Candyman" Damian Rose. Beside him stands a elder professional man who is apparently guiding Candyman to some place..]
MAN: Okay, see on that table there.. "THE CANDYMAN" DAMIAN ROSE: The guy seated next to the blonde? MAN: More to the left. "TC" DR: The guy in the suit? MAN: Keep on going. "TC" DR: The guy opposite the table, with that man who is eating.. 10 BURGERS AT ONCE!??! MAN: Well, that's getting closer. [There is a large pause, something just isn't right here...] "TC" DR: HIM!? MAN: Yes, him. "TC" DR: Are you sure? MAN: They told me look for the guys eating the most burgers at once and well, I don't see ANYONE else eating THAT much. I'll be on my way now..
"TC" DR: So that's it? I'm left with this guy and .. have to wrestle with him? MAN: That was my job Damian, I'm sorry if you were disappointed. Now, good luck at the Rosebowl..
[The man begins to walk away, but we can catch a few words he says under his breathe..] MAN: .. You'll be needing it. [With that he is gone and Candyman is left to only.. well, approach this man.. if you can call him that. Slowly, he begins to pace towards him..
step by step getting closer.. until, he is standing right in front of.. "Disgusting" Richard Dorkus. All 425 pounds of him. Richard is wearing a round-necked t-shirt, with a picture of a hamburger in the center. His huge belly can be seen bulging out, over his trousers]
"TC" DR: Um, are.. you.. Dorkus? [Without even stopping eating, Dorkus looks up at Candyman and begins to speak. Did we forget to mention, the food falling out of his mouth while doing so?]
"D" RD: Yeah I'm Dorkus... Can't you see I'm busy, doing what I do best? "TC" DR: You have something.. coming out of your nose.. I just thought I'd tell you that. [He has exactly that, a HUGE piece of boogie sliding down his nose.. well, that was until he picks it with his hand, places it on his burger and well.. continues to eat.]
"TC" DR: ... "D" RD: Yum Yum. That is delicious. You should try it one time, it adds extra flavor to the meal. Here... [Points to an empty seat] Make yourself at home, you look like you need to put on a few pounds.
"TC" DR: Urgh, I'll be alright. I'm fine the way I am. Do you even know who I am? "D" RD: OF COURSE! [A big grin comes across Dorkus' face] You are the KING! "TC" DR: The king? "D" RD: Yeah... The King of Candy! Speaking of candy, got any handy? [Be begins drooling] I _LURVE_ CANDY!!
"TC" DR: Well.. yeah, I GUESS you can say that and I guess I'm your partner for Rosebowl. "D" RD: [Still day dreaming about candy] I love candy. I haven't eaten candy for almost.... [Looks at his watch] twenty minutes. I can't believe that. Hey... [Turns to Damian] Seeing as though we are partners for
Rosebowl, does that mean you can give me FREE candy? [Another pause.. something just isn't right once again..] "TC" DR: Listen, I'll make you a deal. Are you up for a deal? "D" RD: Sure... You mean a Big Mac Meal? [He grins] "TC" DR: No, no, no. A deal. Meaning, if you do what I want you to do.. then after it's done, I'll give you something in return. "D" RD: Oh, you mean if give me a dollar, I go and buy another bag of chips? "TC" DR: Um, yeah. If you say so. So here is the deal. You and me, we train for the Rosebowl Rumble, we get into shape..
"D" RD: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!! [The entire shopping mall goes quiet..] "TC" DR: What's wrong!?!? "D" RD: You said the word "shape". Don't EVER mention that word to me again, EVER!! I am allergic to that word.
"TC" DR: But listen on. You work out, you lose a few pounds. So we are fit going into the match against Trixxster and Bod.
"D" RD: I don't need to train and work out for that match. I will EAT those guys alive. "TC" DR: Yeah but now, once that's done. We go into our match and we advance. Then we go our separate ways.. BUT, and I'll know you will like this.
"D" RD: You know me, I like ANYTHING, especially if it has extra chilli sauce. [Licks his lips]
"TC" DR: How about, I give you a FREE.. truck load of Candy. "D" RD: FREE? CANDY? FREE? "TC" DR: Yes, free. All you got to do is, go with my deal. "D" RD: OH BOY! Yes sir. We have a deal.... [Day dreams again] Candy... Candy.... Candy! "TC" DR: Well, there it's done. Next week, come to MY shop.. [Wait, mistake.. pause for a slight moment.. and think things over..] "TC" DR: Wait, you come to Chicago gym, down town. 11 o'clock. "D" RD: I'll be there, as long as you bring some candy with you, don't forget... "TC" DR: I'll see you there.. [With that, "The Candyman" Damian Rose turns around and begins to walk away. In the background we see a really "Disgusting" Richard Dorkus still seated there.. with an awkward smile on his face. Just like the man before.. Rose says something under his breathe as the scene fades to black..]
"TC" DR: .. thank god I got the over the top rope match. NOBODY is going to throw this guy anywhere, let alone move him one inch..
