LOCO--sample roleplay

16 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<LOCO stood there moving into a position over a worn maple floor in an obscure warehouse studio in upper New York. He sits and takes the classic pose. LOCO; at once both terrifying and benovlent, the king of cosmic dance, both deystroyer and creater. LOCO bends his right leg, lowering his center of gravity, and then lifts and slightly crosses his left leg over the right. His arms are loosley spread towards the raging fires that blaze around him in a circle. For one hand holds the power to deytroy a federation.The other the power to create from destruction. LOCO is in a room with a Priest wearing a long black cloak and a hood that covers his face. The priest is walking counter clockwise around LOCO...."> Priest: Did you give up your faith my son ? LOCO: I heard a lot of talk about the power of god. I hear how he cures the sick and brings the dead back to life. I hear how people praise him. To me it is just a lot of talk father. <The priest lays his hand on LOCO's left shoulder and speeks with great emotion> Priest: My son the lord Jesus Christ is your savior he is with you at all times. During your darkest journey's he has carried you into the light my child. He is all good and he shall be prasied. Please my son know Jesus loves you. LOCO: Jesus? A man of love? What he WAS may have been beautiful. Let's face it. He only lives inside our prayers. He is a myth from a forgotten time. Where was he when I needed him. Where was he when I needed someone? <A tear drops from LOCO's left eye and rolls down his cheek> Priest: My son I know you've been through stuggles and hardship. I know you lost your family in a terrible tornado. God works in mysterious ways. His will is your fate, Blessed oh the path Jesus lays before us. These demons they still haunt you so? These demons are they the reason you must hurt people? LOCO: I'm not hurting people.....it's more like.......saving myself. Preist: From who ? LOCO: From...<his voice gargled>.....myself. <The priest gets down upon his knees...and holds LOCO's head in his hands....the fires blaze in a circle around the pair....yet a feeling of utter darkness compels the two> Priest: Tell me your demons my child. Release them for all time. Tell me? LOCO: Where do I hide my pain. I run but it's always there. You ask me to tell you. You ask me to share MY DEMONS. They scratch and tear from inside my body. They tear my flesh from my bone. Bury their heads in my soul....my mind......my heart....MY BEING....YOU WANT THIS ???.....there are things inside me that....hate.....kill.....it breeds. So tear me open but know......there are things inside that have no concious....no regret. The demons are......deadly.....frightning.....IT HATES.....THEY FEED IT...THEY PUSH ME.. ...THEY HAVE NO FUCKING CLUE..........I CAN'T CONTROL THEM......I DON'T WANT THEM...... BUT THEY ARE ME.....MY HANDS ARE STAINED WITH............ <LOCO's voice tails off into a muffled sobbing.....> Priest: Stained with what my child ??? <LOCO gets up to his feet and walks through the circle of fire....the priest follows... LOCO walks towards the door...opens it up....> Priest: YOUR HANDS ARE STAINED WITH WHAT MY SON ????????? <LOCO stops....turns around and stares at the priest with his hands raised up from his sides......LOCO looks down to the ground....pauses> Priest: WHAT ARE YOUR HANDS STAINED WITH ??? <LOCO slowly raises his head and looks at the priest....tears rolling down from his eyes ....pain and intence agony gripping his face.....>> LOCO: The blood of the non believers. The blood of the weak. My hands are covered in the blood of the damned. I am the reaper of crimes not my own. The writer of wrongs. I must set the record straight...and remove this blood from my hands. Priest: Go with God my son. LOCO: Even God can't save them now father. Priest: Who is this THEM you speak of? <LOCO wipes the tears from his face> LOCO: The WOWA. <LOCO walks into the dark cold night closing the warehouse door behind him....the priest walks along the base of the warehouse over to his alter and podium.....takes the cloak off from his head.....seeing him from behind......the priest takes out a cigarette from under his cloak...and lights it up......it appears he takes a drag....smoke rises to into the air....raising to the roof...> Priest: Lord help the *WOWA*. <camera fades to black>
