Pink Passion Roleplay ... Read please!

23 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Hello, I'm [name withheld] and I have just done a roleplay with my resurrected character "The Pink Passion". I would appreciate any feedback on this character as I am curious to what you people think about him.**
[The scene opens at an abnormally colored pink house. The camera slowly zooms up and the door automatically opens. Now "inside" of the house you see that pink is in abundance. There is pink carpet, pink wall paper, pink furniture
... pink on pink on pink. The camera view goes slowly around the corner to a bedroom, where you find The Pink Passion and his "mistress" Rebekka lounging on a pink bed. The Pink Passion is busy puffing on a cigar and reading the newspaper while Rebekka is painting her nails ... you guessed it ... pink.
After a few moments The Pink Passion notices the camera and smiles at it with a solid mouth of strong, sparkling white teeth. He motions for it to come closer and sit down in his pink love seat by the bed. After it does The Pink Passion sits up and stares into the camera with a provacotive, appealing look and begins to speak ...]
THE PINK PASSION - Oh, you're hear early. Rebekka, would you be so kind as to fetch this man a drink. He looks awfully thirsty. You'd like a drink, wouldn't you?
[The camera shakes side to side in a "no" gesture but nonetheless Rebekka still vanishes into another room to get the camera man a drink.]
THE PINK PASSION - How do you like my fabulous house? I find it to be very ... cozy. But anyway, aside from that, I have a few things to say to the wrestlers of the HHWO. First and foremost, the competition looks to be rather tough, but it's nothing that I can't handle. For starters there is Cyrus Greene, a man who just seems to always find a way to get me hot. I mean, the guy is an absolute stud. He has the perfect body, the perfect sense of humor ... Cyrus
Greene is a god to put it rather bluntly. In addition, there's Creed ... that rebellious barroom brawler. I don't know what it is about him, but he's definitely got something that drives me crazy. I suppose it's that bad boy attitude that makes me squeal for more. Then ... THEN there's the HHWO World
Champion, King Snake. Just the name "snake" makes me wonder about him. I can just imagine what's lurking in those tight pants of his. Yum, yum, yum ...
[Rebekka appears with a glass of pink wine. She hands it to the camera man who takes it.] THE PINK PASSION - And now I've come to understand that my first match is against some man by the name of "The Blackflagg" Vincent Lockheart. Oh, tsk, tsk, tsk, Vincent. Black is NOT your color. I think you'd look MUCH better in pink! Yes, I really do! So after I dispose of you with my "Pink Dreams" DDT
I'll just have to fix that little problem for you. I mean, don't get me wrong,
Vinny, you're a VERY attractive guy. You just need something ... oh, what's the word I'm looking for ... exquisite. That's it. You need to have a
"makeover" of sorts, and I plan on doing just that. So Vinny, come next Sunday just sit back and enjoy the ride because The Pink Passion is going to make you look "purty"!
[The Pink Passion now lays back down again Rebekka's legs and she begins to give him a massage. He closes his eyes in pleasure as he continues to speak
...]
THE PINK PASSION - But you know, aside from all the good looking men here in
HHWO, I'd like to be blunt with everybody when I say that I have other things on my mind as well. No, not THAT. I'm talking about gold, baby! Now pink is the best color in the world, but gold is the next best thing. I mean, look at it this way. Couldn't you just see me with gold on pink? Sounds marvelous, doesn't it? Now I know I have to "work my way up the rankings," but that's fine and dandy with me. I'm not one to complain much ... because hey, that means I'll get to see each wrestler of the HHWO up close and personal!
[The Pink Passion now sits up and takes another puff off of his cigar. He blows the smoke out seductively and speaks once more ...]
THE PINK PASSION - So get ready, HHWO, because The Pink Passion is here and ready to get it on! Don't let this beautiful, sexually appealing exterior get to you ... because I'm about as tough as they come! The Pink Passion is big, bad ... and most importantly, I'm the only man who looks ...
PRETTY .... IN .... PINK!!! [The Pink Passion smiles and takes another puff from his cigar as the scene fades out ...] [name withheld] Handler of: "Brutal" Brian Floyd {LAW} & The Pink Passion {HHWO}
