Mistress Sonia RP [Critque] I won't say it's the best but it may make you laugh

14 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thinks I already know: Mickey doesn't talk [[The sun is shining and the children are smiling. Parents laughing, and birds are singing. And on a bench is Mistress Sonia. She has a huge disgusted look.
She's wearing her leather tank top and pants, but the unusual part is she doesn't have her whip on her. She has that inner torment look like she's actually suffering]]
[Mistress Sonia] I'm in hell. Disney World is the center of all hells. I don't know what the hell I've must of been thinking when I agreed to make an apperance in another theme park. And they even had the NERVE to hold off my whip until I leave. I can't think what is going through those owners heads.
It's my whip, I need it to defend myself. Here I am where everyone is smiling, we having walking talking mice who hug everyone in damn sight, a duck who will forever refuse to wear pants, a tiger who does nothing but bounce and give people headaches, two chipmunks who fight with each other more than the
Midknight sisters and I do, and the world's most stupid bear who can't get his nose out of a dumb jar. If this isn't hell, I don't want to know what it really is. It'll be just too scary. The only place where I find some comfort in this hell is the exit.
[[Suddenly Mickey Mouse comes and gives her a big hug and she seemed to have her eyes now lit on fire]]
[Mistress Sonia] Are you touching me mouse? [[Mickey nods]] [Mistress Sonia] You shall remove your arms off at once.... let go.... or suffer the price of insubordination.
[Mickey Mouse] But, you look like you need a hug. [Mistress Sonia] And you look like you need a doctor. [Micky Mouse] Why? [[Mistress Sonia grabs Mickey from the head rips off the costume, kids around screamed and ran to their parents as she grabs the real head and throws him to a wall and knocks him out]]
[Mistress Sonia] Any other cartoon character wants to play lovey dovey with me now? I dare you, bring on the pirates maybe they'll provide a suitable entertainment display for the little children here. Or maybe Donald Duck, I'll finally make him put on some pants for crying out loud!
[[Donald Duck runs towards Adventure Land away from Mistress Sonia. Suddenly some guards come and tap on her shoulder put she just kicked one between the legs and clawed the other one at his eyes]]
[Mistress Sonia] [[With a cocky look]] Don't bother getting up, I know my way out. I'll just take my whip and go.
[Parent] Now there goes a wrestler who can be a role model.... god bless her. [Child] But Mommy, she beat up Mickey. [Parent] I know, do you realize how long I wanted to do that as well, God Bless
Her. [[The scene fades out and into a black limousine her pulling on the whip to its full extent. She squeezes it so hard that it started to leave a print on the palm of her hand and could give her a burn. She is giving an icy gaze towards the camera. Her gaze is cold enough to freeze the pits of hell for all eternity.]]
[Mistress Sonia] That's it. I am never going back to Florida again. I want to go back to my Kingdom where the world makes sense. Where I make the rules.
Where I am judge jury and executioner. [[gives a chilling smile]] I like being the executioner the most. I would love to throw the switch on Micky. [[She screams]] I'm not really in the mood to speak. So I have here is my agent, the asshole who suggested that I go to Disney World for an apperance. I've wrote down for him everything he's to say. Take it Mike Williams.
[[There is a man who looks like a 98 pound weakling who is holding a brief case. He opens it and takes out a few papers and straightens his glasses]]
[Mike Williams] [In a high-pitched monotone voice] Greetings Simplitons. I am
MIstress Sonia. Really I'm her agent as she said.
[Mistress Sonia] Can you TRY to sound more threatening? That would be a great help if you could.
[Mike Williams] {In a wicked witch of the west voice] And when I get you in the ring, my pretties, I'm going to destroy you. And you little b****es too.
[[the scene fades to black and fades into a busy highway where the limo drives by with Mike Williams being pushed out rolling into heavy traffic. He runs to the sidewalk and yells at Mistress Sonia]]
[Mike Williams] I'LL GET YOU FOR THIS YOU DOM-BOY! [[Fades to black and back into the car with Mistress Sonia smiling]] [Mistress Sonia] Taking out the garbage made me suddenly want to talk again. So lets start from the top. Jamie. The girl who has her eye on Roadkill for more than she's giving. I'm telling you Jamie, you are on a highway with cars at you in full blast and they are NOT going to even FLINCH at the break. You better stop looking at Roadkill or that is what you'll turn out to be. Now, you've also insulted me as well. You commited the crime "Insubordination". Penalty is death, but for you I'll make it cruel and painful punishment. I don't go unusual, not painful enough. But as I said before, if you're going to go one on one against me, you're going to be way pasted YOUR bedtime girl, so quit before you'll be too tired to even walk towards the ring. If you can't be the
"Repelling" Starr then I don't think you'll be able to defeat me. And speaking or the "Repelling" Starr lets talk about her.
[[She takes out a Low-down]] [Mistress Sonia] You had the nerve to call yourself MY Mistress. Big mistake girl. I'm the only Mistress in this fed, and if you want to give a heated debate on the issue I suggest you bring more than yourself if you want to survive my wrath, because no one has survived it in the ring as of yet, if ever. You come here, TRY to make a big scene, and when no one noticed you.
You've tried to go for their ego. Well, you haven't scratched my ego but you got my attention by calling yourself a Mistress. I don't know the crime is called when pretending to be above your real rank is called, but I'm sure the punishment is harsh, and I'll make it more harsh than needed to be. All you've done so far was beat up Jamie and that wasn't much of a task to be reconized for. You've got to do a lot better than that. Beat someone like Crystal, or
Arista, or Tara, THEN you'll get some attention that you deserve.... although it'll still be very little. Beat someone like ME then you'll get all people's attention. But of course it's nothing but a miserable fading PIPE dream.
Because all you little insects who try to sound as threatening as my former agent just try to be will be as handled as he was: thrown out. But, unlike him you'll be carrying some bumps and bruised, and maybe a broken bone or two when you finally get thrown out of here. I don't care who throws you pathetic girls out the door, my motto is "Let them kill each other and take their credit." so
I still win who ever does it. [[gives a smile]] Now speaking of garbage let's talk about the Midknight sisters. If you ask me I'm beginning to see some fat building up on them from all that eating they are doing, and all that lounging around. And to think one of them is the one who I have to fight in the in
Sizzler. Thank goodness for the restraining order. Then I don't have to worry about three tubbies. Only one. You guys consider me weak for getting that restraining order don't you? Well, I've got news for you. I'm stronger than all three of you. Like the violent femms two tag team girls said. I would like to say thanks for the compliment girls. And I'm sorry that when I kick Tara's ass and put it in line that I have to go and kick Jasmine's. But I'll tell you this now, it'll be nothing personal against you two. And I hope you two won't be as wimpy as the sisters and use numbers as an advantage. I have a higher piece of respect for you two seperately than does three COMBINED. But now lets get back to Tara. KM and Williams think you're vastly underestimating me. I think they are right. Me I am going to fight you like you're the toughtest opponent I've ever met. Like my very title is on the line. Screw my life I prefer to keep my title as Mistress more than my life. Will you say the same for your title as
Tara? No, I think not. You're not going to be facing just me, but the fates as well. And we all know, fate always gets its way. So, I suggest you kneel before me now, cringe on my leather boots, and BEG for my forgiveness and I shall give it to you and punish you with a quick match. If you refuse to beg for my forgiveness then I shall punish you as I would if one of my minions commited treason. Throw you into the pit of fire and let you burn in my hell..... put your ass in line.... it belongs to me.
[[Fade.]]
