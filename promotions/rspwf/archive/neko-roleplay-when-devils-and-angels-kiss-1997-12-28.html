Neko roleplay "when devils and angels kiss"

28 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Remember too those angels who were not content to maintain the dominion assigned to them, but abandoned their proper dwelling place; God is holding them bound in darkness with everlasting chains, for judgement on the great day." -Letter of Jude: 6 The camera slips slowly through the dark concrete back-corridors of small south Asian arena. Blood trails along the dank floor, some dried black, more still wet and slick. The chants of the tightly packed crowd echoes through the halls as fighters of all sizes and both genders move to and fro, either preparing, or recovering from their matches. They all share one locker room, however the lewd gestures and remarks one might expect are absent. The fighters respect one another and their abilities. They are all brothers and sisters of the Fist. This is the Kumate (koo-mah-tay), the most intense, ruthless, and illegal fighting tournament in the world. Very often the losers of these matches must be carried out on a stretcher with broken bones and worse. Each fighter here today knows the grim reality of this tournament, as well as the great rewards bestowed upon the winners. It is a great honor to be here today, for only the best may compete. One fighter stands apart from the rest. She is small, only 5'3", and her slim compact body disguises well her intense strength. Strength from the heart, from the soul. She stands alone, looking deep into the mirror, face devoid of expression. Her features are still delicate, beautiful despite the thin scar that runs down the left side, from eyebrow to chin and across her left eye. The prosthetic eye is painted with the image of a black angel as an iris. "Why are you here, sister?" a voice says from behind. The woman does not react, continuing to examine her own image. "You aren't like us. You don't take pleasure from this combat. The blood and pain does not fuel your desire. So why are you here?" the man says. He is heavy-set, but strong, in his late 40s. "Neko..." Neko turns to look at the man. She knows him as Gereimion; his fighting name. She does not know his real name, but trusts him nonetheless. "You care to much Gereimon." Neko says coldly. Gereimion laughs, but his eyes remain steely cold. "Don't evade my question. When you first came here, I knew you weren't one of us. You never were. You're an outsider. Sure you fight as good, perhaps even better than the rest of us, but you don't fight for the same resons as us. It's not about money, or inflicting pain, or glory for you. What is it, huh? Why do you fight, Neko?" "When I first came here, everyone shied away from me, shunned me. They believed the rumors about me, all those lies that I had passed away. And seeing me alive frightened them. They called me the 'Angel of Death'...they said for every word someone spoke to me, they'd lose a year of their life." Neko says stoically. "But you didn't belive a word of it. You talked to me, you watched my back, you were...a friend." Gereimion starts to say something but Neko stops him. "I have not had many friends in this life, and those I have had always suffer in my wake. I'm a bad luck charm. Chaos follows me wherever I go. I don't know why. All I know is that when I am in that ring fighting, all my pain, all the chaos inside goes away. Everything is so clear, if only for a moment. When I am fighting, I find peace. And right now, that's the only blessing I have." "You'll destroy yourself here, Neko. Don't do it this way. You are young, and beautiful." Gereimion says, causing Neko to smile briefly. "Those are blessings as well. Go back to the wrestling ring, back to your fans and your friends. Yes..you have friends Neko, more than you could ever imagine. This dungeon, this dog-fight is not yours to call home. Your home is out there." "They all say I'm dead, or a coward for leaving the wresling world." Neko says. "I am nothing to them now." "No, you are something to them, sister. You are a legend, you are a dream, you are a HERO. They used to look up to you, and they mourned when you left. If they did not care about you, would they have missed you? Of course not." Gereimion says, placing his hands on Neko's shoulders, looking deep into her eyes. "If they say that you are dead, or a coward go back there and prove them wrong! Stop running from whatever demons are haunting you. Stop hiding in the shadows. Reclaim your honor, and your happiness." Neko looks up at Gereimion, silent for several moments. "You're right. I'll go back. But come with me...you don't seem to belong here either." Gereimion simply shakes his head. "No. I've allready had my glory and my money out on the professional wrestling scene. But that's not why I fight. I only have one good fight left in me, Neko. This cancer inside me is growing faster than ever, so I only have one last shot." "But why fight? Why not live your last days in peace?" Neko asks. He grins wryly. "Why don't I stay home,? Sitting on the couch watching Talk Shows and eating Cheesey-poofs? No..that's not living." Gereimion turns, pointing down the hall, toward the fighting pit. "THAT is living! The arena has always been my home, and I'm too old for the pro-wrestling promotions. So I've come here to fight with the best; those too violent, too proud, or too maimed to be in the wrestlign ring. We are the last true Gladiators. I'm not here for fame or fortune." "Then just why do you fight?" Neko asks again. "For the fun of it." he says, grinning wide. The two touch fists as Neko departs. "I'll be watching the TV...do me proud little sister." Neko reaches down to grab her bag and sword-cane. A drop of water falls from a leaky pipe above Neko's head. Just as it crosses her line of sight, she spins, her movement fluid. Drawing the blade from the shaft of the cane, she slices the large droplet of water clean in half. "I'll do you proud...friend."
