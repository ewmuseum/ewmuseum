[Antawn Carter Roleplay] Please give me feedback.... [This is kinda short, but sweet]

25 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in. We see a zoom in of an empty ring. A light blue mat is the only thing visible to us right now, but as the camera begins to pull up we see three ropes, one red, one white, and one blue, with a black steel post on each corner.
The arena, is silent, and appears to be completely empty, until we hear the very slight sound of footsteps, and the camera begins searching for the source.
The first thing we see is a black BOSS skully cap, covering the head of a bald
African American. As the camera moves down, this is without a doubt Antawn
Carter, attired in a black BOSS shirt, with a pair of baggy, deep blue jeans, and of course, his trademark black Lugz shoes.
The echoing sounds of footsteps continue, as they are joined by the voice of one man, "Ain't Nuttin' Personal" Antawn Carter.]
"ANP" AC: "The wrestling ring. Where many people make their living, and some lose their living. Without a doubt, one of the most feared places on earth, especially when you have to debut. When you have to get up in front of one thousand screaming fans and win, or lose. You win, they accept you. You lose, they don't. Pitiful. Just what the fans are. They are just more fakes tryin' to make livings off of kids like me by deciding not to like us. Well, come November thirtieth, it isn't gonna be about fan support, it's gonna be about the wrestler who is stronger in mind.....
Me.... .... Or Jay Cal. I think the answer is obvious, and, Cal, I'm not really too concerned with you to be honest. I think you are just another fake that has to give himself a name like 'Superstar' to get some recognition in this world. Well, kid, I ain't in this sport for the recognition, or the money. I'm in this sport for one reason and one reason only......
..... respect. You fools in the MVW gotta show me some respect, that's all I'm asking. And with me, it's, you got respect, you get respect, and my respect is something you wanna have, no doubt. Jay Cal, you don't respect me, yet I respect the man you could be if you didn't name yourself the best, but, kid, you did, and November thirtieth, it's my time. My time to be great. It's _my_ turn to show the world I am for real.....
And, Cal..... ..... it's my turn to prove...... ..... It Ain't Nuttin' Personal..... ..... It's the way I _live_....... [Fade out.]
