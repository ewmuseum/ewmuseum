[RP Critique/Rookie] 'Faithful' Justin Hvizdzak (2nd RP I Have Ever Done)

7 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Note: This is only the second RP I have ever done, so tell me what you think. Thank You! --------------------------------------------- [ A bright light, one such of those described when you enter the Holy Land. You hear music that hits your nerve and calms you into a relaxed state of mine. You can imagine the smell of roses, and your favroite smells. Your senses are running wild, you don't want this to stop. Suddenly, the bright light disappears and in front of you is a wooden podium with a Cross carved in front of it. A mist of some sort rolls inches off the ground as beams of light shines through it, giving it an awe inspiring appearance. A door opens behind the podium, and you can see a figure walking through thick fog. As the figure steps out from behind the fog, you can see that it is 'Faithful' Justin Hvizdzak. ]
[ 'Faithful' Justin Hvizdzak is wearing a long white robe. A necklave with a cross on the end is dangling off of his neck. Justin's face is cold as stone, his eye gleam right through you. 'Faithful' Justin Hvizdzak steps up to the podium and clears his throat. After a short pause, 'Faithful' Justin Hvizdzak speaks. ]
['Faithful' Justin Hvizdzak]: "From the day that I stepped foot onto God's green earth, I knew my mission, I knew my goal from God. I was deemed to be a professional wrestler. God had given me the build to be a cruiserweight, and had given me the ability to fly. But, one thing was wrong. How? When? Here I was, little Justin Hvizdzak sitting over on a couch in Poland eating a bag of potato chips. I had no way to get to America, and my parents would refuse to let me leave them for a better land to chase my mission statement from God. I tryed and tryed, but to no avail. But, on day, something amazing happened. My parents, out of the blue, just told me that I could pack up and haul myself to
America. And that is where my career started. As you can see, I'm a rookie in the GRW. But, I have established myself already. Let's take a flashback for a moment, shall we?"
"On Sunday Night Slam, I beat Tony Wright, if you already did not know. But, not only did I beat him, I put him out of action for a while by injuring his right knee. I'm not bragging or ranting on about that, but that just goes to proove that I do know what I'm doing inside the wrestling ring. Tony, I'm giving you an open invitation. When or if you heal, I'm here for a re-match. I wouldn't think twice about stepping into the ring with you again. The first time, it is was good and I won. The second time around, it will just be an open book that we will have to write."
"Now, let me explain a little something to you all. Everybody thinks that Marc
LeRoux is going to be cheating for me in my matches. That is total non-sense.
With God as my witness, Marc LeRoux is only helping me out. If I lose a match, he is going to tell me what I did wrong. If I win a match, he is going to tell me how to improve something that didn't look to flashy or could have cost me the match. It is as simple as that. And this Friday at Showdown, you can bet anything that I'll be standing along side the cage as Marc takes on Phillip
Starks. This is the way I'm going to learn, and I think I have a great teacher to show me how everything works here in the GRW."
"And now, the moment you have all been waiting for. Once again, I have a match on Sunday Night Slam. This is a foreigner facing a foreigner here, as I, Justin
Hvizdzak, take on Toshio Nakemura. Poland againest Japan. Sound interesting.
Toshio, let me ask you this. Do you believe in your God? Do you believe that He is the reason that you are in this wrestling ring today? If so, good for you.
If not, you have a problem on your hands. Apparently, Tony Wright didn't believe and look at what He did to him. God put him out for a while. Do you want the same to happen to you? I don't think so."
"Toshio, this is going to be, I think, a big match for me. A tough one at the least. So, I'm going to be giving it my all...everything I have. But, for now,
I'm done. You'll hear from me later. May God be with you all!"
[ 'Faithful' Justin Hvizdzak raises his arms as fog billows into the room. Justin seems to float backwards into the open door as it slams shut. Total silence for a moment, then everything goes black. ]
