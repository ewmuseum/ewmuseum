The RP the MCW didn't want you to see....

8 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera fades onto a dark alley way. Fog is coming out from the sidewalks, and it's covering everything in it's path. All of a sudden Zach Morrison steps out from the fog with a very serious look on his face. Zach is dressed in a black trench coat, and has his hair slicked back like Pat Rieley. He walks towards the camera....]
Zach: Money, Midgets, and Homosexuality...there are just some of the big things that have almost destroyed the life of "Simply-Golden" Jeff Keenan. Today for the next 20 minutes or so we're going to take you behind the scenes of what almost "Simply-Destroyed" Jeff Keenan. Take a walk with me as we enter...Wrestling's Secrets and Lies.
[All of a sudden this strange flute music starts to play and the camera shows rapid clips of different wrestlers. The clips are of people like Legion who is taking shots with a bearded lady, and even a clip of the Malacian President
Salvador Malacia the time he picked up that Singapore Hooker with no legs, and
1 arm. Then a huge bolt of lightning strikes and the screen says
"Simply-Golden"...."Simply Damned." Zach Morrison shows up once again...] Zach: MCW has had it's share of losers in the past. People like Clement Tyme,
Robert Mula, and even Stinky Pete. None compare to the one man who almost ruined the MCW as well as his own life. That man is Jeff Keenan. We've taken the biggest moments of his life and we're going to let you decide for yourself....if this man is "Simply Damned". Take a look at this...
[Camera cuts to a midget who is nothing but a shadow. This is to protect his identity of course. The midget has a voice altering machine as well so his voice is really deep.]
Midget "Moe": Jeff always used to come into this bar...I remember it clearly. The bar was called "Blue Balls" and he would always order this drink called
"Low Blow". Anyway he came up to me one time, and I remember how excited I was.
I mean at this time Jeff was a big ICON in wrestling, and I was a huge fan. Well he and I started talking and getting to know each other, and he kept putting his hand on my knee. He looked straight into my eyes and said..."Cut the crap lets screw", and it wasn't until then when I found out he was a homosexual midget chaser.
[Camera cuts back to Zach Morrison] Zach: A Homosexual Midget Chaser is something that Jeff Keenan was. His obsession for midgets controlled him night after night. The mental anguish that he went through, his denial was weighing on him day after day. Could this affect his wrestling ability?
[Cuts back to "Moe"] Midget "Moe": I remember one day he had lost to someone. I can't remember who it was, but it was a bad loss. Jeff came into the bar that night and we had been talking for a while, but I never went back to his house. Well, Jeff came up to me and started crying....I mean this was pure balling. So I consoled him, and took him back to my trailer park home. Then all of a sudden it happened....he closed the door and the tears stopped.
[Cut back to Zach.] Zach: The tears stopped and the boner began. Jeff Keenan was planning this all along. He didn't even lose on accident...he told everyone he was going to do the job. Now it was time for "Moe" to do his...
[Back to "Moe" again...] Midget "Moe" (Crying) I....I....*sniff*....I remember that he closed the door and said "It's time for you to wrestle with my Golden Rod
(bleep).....*weep*.....*snif*....and he picked me up by the armpits and just tossed me onto the bed like a ball. Then he.....
....*Snif*...he took his pants off and....OH I CAN'T TALK ABOUT THIS ANYMORE!!! ["Moe" goes to get off the chair but ends up falling. The camera ONCE again cuts back to Zach Morrison.]
Zach: Jeff couldn't control himself anymore and he made "Moe" a MAN that night. A disgusting act by a disgusting person, and the fact is that Jeff needed some help. Instead of getting his help though...he arrived in the MCW. They welcomed him with open arms, and that's where he took a wrong turn. We're out of time for tonight, but I want to finish by saying something. Jeff Keenan, you're a sick man...and when we meet later this week or whenever, I'm going to make sure that you never do something like what you did to "Moe" again. I reared my ugly face...well the truth about you is coming. It's time to let the people know what you're really all about, and I will do just that! Stay tuned for Jeff's obsession with Yo-Yo's and Lizards. On the next "Wrestling's Secrets and
Lies!!" [Zach smiles, winks, and fades into the fog again. The camera fades...]
