[Critique] PrimeTime Masters

8 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please Critique This..First Roleplay For This Team.. Prime Time [It's Not A Catchy Gimmick] It's Two Men.. [Simply Re-Inventing..] The Whole Fuckin' Game. [Two men..standing square in the middle of your television set..dark surrounds their every being..The camera pans from the bottom of their feet to the tips of their faces..What stories could they hold? What legacy may follow them? Are they men of great power..Men of total dominance? Men with the power to dictate the world? Or are they..an idle pawn, in the world of big stars, money, and women..? These unanswered questions that lay before you, hold the key to your fear. Why are you scared? Because you simply..Don't know..Not knowing is a key phobia in this world..Not knowing where you step, and where you tread, may very well lead to one man's downfall..The ancient floor planks make a sound, which is easily heard from all corners of this abandoned home. Why do you step forward, when you fear what lays ahead? Because a tingling sensation in the back of your head, leads your curiosity to run wild. You gradually, continue to step forth, without knowing of the tainted measures that could be ahead..You are one step in front of the room where the unknown men stand still..You spit on the ground, as a salty taste fills your mouth, the sweat becomes unbearable, as you hope the small sound doesn't make these figures turn to you.. For they are only a few feet in front of you..You take a quick step back, as the men turn and face you, their menacing grin, is illuminated by the fire..The first and smaller man's facial features now become visisble.. male, brown eyes, dread locks, and a small scarr above his right eye, and on his chin. The second man, much taller, goatee, crew cut, and a huge build..They beckons for you to come closer, leading you forth with their left index finger's.. You think about it, you want to leave, to run, to hide..But the tingling sensation returns, and your curiosity is outrageously beckoning you closer..
You can now see what each man is clad in.. the smaller man has a white t-shirt that reads "PrimeTime" in bold red letters, cargo pants, and etnies shoes..The taller man is wearing a white polo shirt, khaki pants, and nice shoes..As the smaller man's mouth slowly begins to open, you become frightened, for you do not know what is within these men of mystery..]
Cyrus Masters: What? You thought we were some generic _DARK_ wrestlers? Yeah, I wouldn't stoop that low if my life depended on it. [chuckles] ALW? What a un-talented promotion. Sure, Emery can go out and claim he is the number one promotion in the whole circuit, but everyone knows Emery simply has a big lot of washed-out untalented wrestlers. This is when all things change in the ALW.
The rules stripped, the icons created. Ajax, and myself have finally brought
_PrimeTime_ to this promotion. No more old wrestlers who call themselves legends..We are the leaders of the new revolution in wrestling. The New Breed you may call it..Above The Law eh? What really is Above The Law though? A few blunts, a couple 40's..is that Above The Law? Extreme measures taken in the wrestling ring..broken bones, broken rules, is that Above The Law? Simply put, being this good..Is _CLEARLY_ Above The Law. Take my words ot the bank, they are as good as gold. You will soon be able to see what the PrimeTime Masters can actually conjure in the ring come Sunday...
[Cyrus takes a small step back and pats the larger man Ajax on the back, who now steps forward..]
Ajax Masters: Outlaw, you've found yourself teamed with Shawn Hawke..I bet you had no idea what you were getting into. You want to know what your getting into
Outlaw? Your getting in the way of a 6'10", 310 pound man, and the ALW World
Tag-Team Titles. But honestly, do you think you will be standing for much longer? No, that is entirely not precise. Come Sunday, you will be thrown down the ladder of greatness you currently stand upon. Thrown down to the depths of which you aren't custom to..For as long as I can remember Outlaw, you've been the man on top of things, you've been the man never scared of anything, or anyone...Sure you were great, sure you were a leader, but come a couple days, you won't excel for much longer. As soon as I come into that ring Outlaw, I'm coming right for you..No mercy, no regret, no sorrow, just Cyrus and I gunning after your ass. Your my target Outlaw..and if your wondering, I'm the equivalent of a homing missile..Alright, that sounded pretty fuckin' sad..But nevertheless, your my target Outlaw, and I never miss.
Cyrus Masters: Many a wrestler in this promotion base themselves on trying to be number one. Number one is the portion of greatness that the PrimeTime
Masters possess. I'm going to lay it out there for you.. Simply put, we ARE number one..No doubt about it. Once you see our athletic skills, you haven't seen a wrestling bout. My agility out-does anyone's in this promotion. You have guys out there, Mr. Tough Guy, Millionaire Mike, Randall Brock.. All of these men believe one thing..That they are the best. Are they the best? Fuck no. They are men who believe that they are the best because asshole Emery judges them to be the best before he even see's their talent. You think for a second a guy like H2O would have been allowed to be in the main event? Fuck no..Fuck no.
Emery is all about the so called Stars, who could care less about the talent that is trying to make their way to the top. The talent that actually works their ass off to become something, the talent that shows their athletic skills in the ring night after fuckin' night. And for what? Do we ever get a fuckin'
push? Hell no..._HELL_ no. Emery you need to open your eyes man, you need to see what's taking place around you. You need to realize that PrimeTime Master's aren't two people to fuck with. We don't play games, we go out there night after night to win. Nothing more, nothing less. When we are booked, and trust me it will never be in the high-card because of Emery's antics, but when we
_ARE_ booked. We won't slack off, we won't sit by and hope our past wins us a matchup like your so-called star's Emery. We will work our ass off night after night until we have proved everything there is to prove, and we sit a-top your promotion Emery. It may be us sitting on top without your recognition, hell, maybe the fans will see us as the top performers, maybe the other people in the talent roster will recognize us as star's. God knows, maybe one day even you
Emery will see that we are the future of this god damned promotion, whether you like it or not. I've come to the ALW for one reason..That reason is to prove to each and everyone of you, that PrimeTime is NOW!
Cyrus Masters: Our destiny begins with Shawn Hawke and Outlaw. Hawke, I don't know much about you my friend. But what I do know, is you won't stand a chance in the ring with Ajax and I. We are physically and mentally built up for our opening bout. No one, not you Outlaw, nor you Hawke will stop us from reigning on top of this game. You just don't get it do you Outlaw? Starting as of now, we will bomb your ass, night after fuckin' night. Day after fuckin' day. When you can't take it anymore Outlaw..We will bomb your fuckin ass once more..no..twice more. You just don't realize that we aren't 100% dedicated to the game, we are 250% dedicated. We want the world to take in the new-breed, and destroy all that is old-school about the game. Revolutionize..that's the key word...Ajax?
Ajax Masters: Outlaw, Shawn Hawke, stepping into our domain is like stepping into abomination from a higher-archy of power. We are the dictators, your the idle surfs, or peon's of this thing we call the game..How will feel to be crushed by a man of my stature? You can clearly look at me, and fall into awe.
I'm not your average man, i'm above all standards of man, i'm a giant, plain and simply put. How will you defeat a giant? Let's compare this to a brick wall
Outlaw..Will you throw yourself into this brick wall until it decimates? But what if this brick wall is unlike any other, what if this brick wall can not fall. I am telling you right now Outlaw, I _WILL_ not fall, or will I suffice to your mind-games. You speak of the honor code Outlaw, a code I do not follow.
Honor is not in my book, destruction, abomination..these are things I hold to be true in _MY_ book. PrimeTime isn't an idle tag-team to be thrown about into various matchups..no..PrimeTime is a force to be reckoned with..A force that will re-write all record books and say.._WE ARE THE NEW FUCKIN' GAME_. This force, is what will take _YOU_ Outlaw and make your life in the ALW, a living hell. How will it feel when all that you have known, all that you have fought for over the years, was taken away from you..out from under you..and displayed by the PrimeTime Masters? I know your game Outlaw, I know your a ticking time bomb ready to set off at any moment, but Outlaw..I have the knowledge, the key to defusing your time bomb, and with time..All that you have ever gained in this game, will mean nothing. When your standing square in the center oif the ring, and your looking up at all six foot ten of me, you will realize that you have finally found your match..That wrestling, will never be the same again..
Ajax Masters: You Outlaw, your a man of discipline..Cyrus, I.. We are the thing that creeps upon you, the thing in the back of your head telling you that you don't have much time left in this game, we are the ones who lead the revolution in the ALW. The new breed revolution that in which will take over the game...
[Ajax takes a seat on a wooden bench to the right side of your television. Cyrus steps forward, as Ajax looks down..] Cyrus Masters: Basically Ajax, you pretty much scared the living shit out of me, and probably the whole fuckin' audience. You've got alot of backing to your words, hell you don't even have to prove anything, nor say anything, looking at you freaks the shit out of me. Nevertheless..Indictment. A pay-per-view for the ages says Emery, a card that could not be booked better than this..Please, give me a fuckin' break. Millionaire Mike, and Mr. Tough Guy headlining..and you think it's the best booking job you've ever done? [chuckles] You must not comprehend what the _BEST_ really is. You could book Ajax..You could book me..
you could book both of us together, and we will still turn your show from, a washed-out Milly Mike, and Mr. Tough Guy main event, to a fuckin' PrimeTime display of utter talent. You want to see head's beaten? Men's honor stripped from them within a matter of moments? Blood? Suicidal moves? Broken bones?
Broken rules? Then watch this Sunday, as the PrimeTime Masters take two men, and turn them into boys. Shawn Hawke, the "Ideal Assassin"..Being Ideal isn't being a no-talent pussy like yourself Mr. Hawke.. Being Ideal is being differant than the rest, being out of control, being out of the ordinary, re-inventing one show, and turning it into a whole new show. That's what I do..That's what..
[Turns to Ajax and points at him..turns back to the camera smirking..] Cyrus Masters: That's what.._WE_ do. Ajax, and I..We are a family. A family of two young wrestler's out there to prove to the world that we actually have what it takes. Shawn Hawke, Outlaw..Your no family..Your two men who have brash ego's and wish you were still as good as you both once were. That's not saying much for you Hawke, you never were anything to begin with. But Outlaw, you were something...keyword.._WERE_. Hawke, I don't plan on hearing much from you, basically because you shouldn't have much to talk about. No one knows you, nor does anyone care about you. I've seen some of your past work Hawke, some of your past interviews, to tell the truth, it reminds me of an irritating buzzing sound that can't be avoided..hmph..I'll just try my best to listen to your meanless words come time you do speak your thoughts, fuck..maybe your to scared to speak once you saw my big friend speak his mind. Hell, I would be scared, and I am just lucky he's on my side. Hawke, I'd watch your ass kid. We've selected you as our first target's..What happens next? Well, Sunday PrimeTime get's it's first chance to display our skills. We won't sit by as other wrestler's try and put on a better show than us..No one, I mean _NO ONE_ will put on a better show than the PrimeTime Masters. Ajax, and I, are coming out there Sunday, gunning at one thing, the ALW World Tag-Team Titles. No one has them as of yet, therefore they are vacant. Vacant they currently stand, vacant they will not be for much longer. PrimeTime holds the key to the future of not only wrestling, but the future of Tag-Team Wrestling. Many a man believes they are the best, many a man believes they are champions. But until you've stepped in the ring with Ajax or I..Your not a champion, one of the best, nor are you even a man. Your an idle pawn to be thrown about, made fun of, and even laughed at. All of you speak harsh, brash, words..Not many of you can back up those words. Sure, call us cocky, brash, arrogant..but know this...we _WILL_ back up all words we preach. Say we can't do it, say we aren't good enough, say we are rookies..Good. We will use all of that in the front of our minds, to help us push that much more. Defeat Outlaw? Many say it can't be done..Some say it won't be..Some say it's an impossible task. Fuck that, we are about to do it anyways...
[Ajax steps up once more, and looks into the camera, as Cyrus prancess around in a circle, and looks back smiling..]
Ajax Masters: Outlaw, This game isn't about talking the best, or looking the best, its presenting yourself in the ring the best. In this sport the game simply doesn't wait. If your not out there night after night, day after fuckin'
day, then your going to be dropped to the bottom faster than you can count to 10. I know firsthand man..I've been in that fuckin' position and trust me it ain't great Outlaw. Is that honestly where you want to be man? Right now hell, your around the top of the ladder..but what happens when I beat the living shit out of you this weekend? You'll be going down the ladder so hard, you'll hit your nuts on every fucking rung on it. This is our chance for the revolution to begin, our chance for the spotlight starts Sunday. We've been sitting here for
2 fuckin' months, and I'm not taking it anymore. I love this game, this game loves me..This is my life. Do you honestly think I'm going to let you take away my life Outlaw? If you do, then I'd like to ask if anyone has ever come up to you and simply stated..Just how fuckin' dumb are you? You don't understand how much I want this Outlaw. You don't understand how badly I can defeat you. You still think your the best, but open your eyes man! Come on! Your not the best anymore! New people have come, new talent, your old-school antics are not alive anymore, their dead.. soon how your career will be. Take heed to my words
Outlaw, because Sunday will be oh one fuck of a bloody sunday. Shawn Hawke, I honestly, don't know who tha' fuck ya' are kid. But you know, that's a good thing, because basically, that means you don't know who I am. And oh god, there isn't anything better than stepping into a ring, with a man who knows nothing about me. You, I can clearly predict, are just another one of those jackasses that run around in circles, talking shit, and trying to take names. You see
Hawke, I don't try and take names, I _DO_ take names...Shawn Hawke, Outlaw.
Your not a team, your not even two men who want to try and work together, your two men that will get ego-busted some Sunday. I'm not here to speak misguided phrases, I'm hear..simply put, to tell of your destiny. Outlaw, your going to come out, speak a few words, go in your little _POLISH_ library that you've found yourself in many a time, then your going to give me a little phrase from some novel that you have supposedly read, by this time, your already speaking a mile a minute, trying to show the world that it's philosophy that runs the game. Your going to come in Sunday, confident with your idiotic partner Shawn
Hawke. By this time, you know, your ready, your all pumped up and you think this will be one more mark in the win column for you. You step into the ring through the middle rope, and you bump into something, what's this? A 6'10"
giant of a man? You will see Cyrus using high-flying tactics you've never even visioned before, and when you think it's all over, and you think you've lost, but you still have your honor..I'm stepping back into the ring, and beating your ass until your so brusied up, you can't even see the light of day..
[Cyrus steps up to the camera, as both men look sternly into it. Cyrus bends over looking into the camera, he flips up his dreadlocks behind his head, and holds up his middle finger to the camera..]
Cyrus Masters: You see this finger? It means fuck you emery, fuck what you have, fuck the alw, fuck your honor, fuck outlaw's philosophy's on life, and fuck everyone who doesn't think we can handle the ring..
[Ajax puts on a goofy face and then turns and holds out his hands and looks at
Cyrus..] Ajax Masters: Cyrus! There is no time for this! [Cyrus smirks, puts on a goofy look, as he looks at Ajax. Cyrus turns back sternly to the camera as Ajax holds up his index finger indicating "one"..]
Cyrus Masters: Your right! It's PRIMETIME! [Both men shake their heads, and walk off as the camera stays on the scene for awhile, and then fades to black..As a INDICTMENT PPV commercial comes to your screen.]
