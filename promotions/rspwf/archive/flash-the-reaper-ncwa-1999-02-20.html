[FLASH] The Reaper [NcWa]

20 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys. It's been a while since I posted an RP here. So I might as well. I am sure I am going to get destroyed like always. Anyway, at the begining this
RP really sux. But as you read it, it gets better. Plus, its against a guy who is doing the JOB.. Well, not really. He just sux. So it didnt really matter. Anyway, here ya' go.
[xXx] ... It has finally happened, the Reaper has won another title, and again stands above the rest. He beat Freak of Nature in one of the hardest matches of his life, and one that will forever change the way he looks at the world.
Next on the list, is man I've known for a very, very long time. And his name... Outlaw! ... [xXx]
[...Fades in...] ((Scene)) [...It's after the PPV, and the fans are leaving the arena. All is quiet now, after a brutal night. New champions have crowned and the losers, sent home.
The camera opens inside of the Reaper dressing room. It is dark, and cold as usual, there is one single light, lighting the room, and in the background you hear footsteps. Suddenly the door bursts open, as the Reapers size thirteen foot kicks it open. He is wearing his ring attire, and the Insane title is draped over his shoulder. It is covered in red blood, as is his face. His cloths are also soaked in blood, as a large cut on his forehead seems to still be bleeding, but is someone dried up. He flips his hair back, and a mixture of sweat and water fly out and hit the wall behind him. He throws down the now red title on the table, pulls out a seat and sits down. He places his foot up on the table and starts to untie his boots as he talks...]
The Reaper: "I have done the near impossible. I have beaten an almost unbeatable man. And I have taken the Insane title with me, to prove it. Freak, you put up the greatest fight of my life, but I told you, you weren't going to beat me again.
Now I proved to all of you, just have extreme, hardcore and Insane I am. Now
Freak, I decided to spare your life, as well as your soul. Although you shall never again be the same man... ::: Chuckles ::: Now, it just may be that by brain is a little rattled right now, but on my way back here, I heard that I already have a match signed for Global Mayhem. I just can't seem to get a break. But that's fine. I am on a roll lately. Then I find out the man I must meet is none other than Outlaw. An old, old rival of mine. This goes all the way back to the EWF. Now, I have never liked this man nor will I ever. He is a former member of God, and he might be able to bring it back. Now I have the chance for many things. To end God for good. There will no longer be a threat of them coming back. I also can end a long rivalry and lastly, claim a poor, useless soul that now belongs to me. Outlaw, you have done two things by signing that contract. One, you have signed your soul over to me, and there is no escape. You have to face me now. And not only that, it has been made a last man standing match. So I get to beat on you until you can no longer stand. Until you can no longer walk... Until you can no longer breath."
((Scene)) [...The Reaper places both of his now untied boots on the ground and proceeds to walk toward the shower....]
((The Reaper)) "It will be a rough match for sure, and given all due credit to you. It will not be near as bad as the match I just walked away from. And until you can put up a fight like that, you should not even bother to fight me. But I know you, and you will not walk away from this. But look at what Freak went through, and multiply that by one hundred. That's you Outlaw. Oh, and there was other thing that happened by you signing this match Outlaw. Yea, you guessed it.
I'm gonna make _YOU_ famous!" ((Scene)) [...The Reaper laughs a little and then looks at the camera in a face he has never made before and continues speaking...]
((The Reaper)) "I know this isn't like me. But it seems I am starting to feel a little
Insane. This title, as well as that match have messed with my mind. My head isn't right. And I realize as long as I am going to hold this title, I figure
I should live up to the name. And I see the perfect time to prove my new self, is in this last man standing match against you Outlaw. Everything that has happened between the two of us, shall be brought back to the surface, all bad blood will begin to boil, and all the pain and suffering that I have had over the long period in which is my life, will again be felt. And then I will focus it all on you. And I will beat you until you can no longer stand.. No, I will beat you until you can no longer breath. So bring what you will to this match, all the weapons you can find, and all the courage you can find. Because you will need all you can get. But much like Freak, you shall leave with nothing.
And unlike him, I will not spare you. I will take your useless weak soul, and do what I shall with it. And the man known as Outlaw will be no more. And I plan to make you cry worse than you did after your loss in the European tournament. Now, you can look at it this way, and be happy for a moment. For the first time in your career, you have made it to a main event. For the first time in your career, you are a star. Outlaw, I say... Thank the Reaper."
((Scene)) [...The Reaper walks around the corner wall, and toward the showers. The camera pans left and focuses in on the blood stained belt that lies on the table. One single drop of still wet blood rolls off and hits the floor. As it hits the ground, the bead of blood does not burst, but stays together, right there on the floor...]
[...Fades out...] [xXx] ... I feel like I never have before. I now see myself as one of the best. In two weeks I have beaten two of the NcWa's greatest. And now onto a man who is good in his own sense. This is something I have done only once before, but stopped after a short while. I plan that it shall not happen that way... this time. So I say to you all. Get used to it, I am here to stay, as is this title! ... [xXx]
