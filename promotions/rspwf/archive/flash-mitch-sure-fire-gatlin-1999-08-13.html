[FLASH] Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin

13 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I did this in 30 min.'s which is fast for me. I Had to beat the deadline. But it is a flash. I hope you like it? You can critique if you want.
[The scene opens up with complete darkness. You cannot see anything. You can here faint drops of water hitting the ground making a very disturbing noise.
You then see a lighter flash up and a cigarette light up. You catch a faint glimpse of a man siting in black leather chair. For the little time you saw the man you seen he has black silky hair but you could not make out his face all to well.]
[You see the bright red cherry on the cigarette light up and then move away. Which appears the man is resting his arm on the arm rest on the chair. There is a disturbing silence in the room. The silence is finally broken by a familiar voice. A ruff and jagged voice with a little southern accent to it.]
Voice: "Well, this is the N.A.A.W Huh?" "It was only a matter of time before a place with this caliber of talent seen the likes of me. They have all the "superstars" and all the up and coming big names. I mean look around here you have people like Shane Alexander, Andy
Thomas, the list goes on and on but I do not have all day to sit around talking about them. I have more important business to talk about."
[Then you see a lamp turn on. None other than Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin is sitting in the black leather chair. He is wearing a black shirt that say's
"Sure Fire" across it in red letters and some Tommy Hilfiger carpenter jeans with some white K-Swiss.]
[There is a little table next to the Black leather chair Mitch is reclined back in. You can see awards hanging on the wall behind him. You can also see water leaking from the ceiling to the floor. The place is a total wreck.]
M.G: "I have herd I am booked this Thursday in a match against "Legacy" Arthur
Gybertson. I really do not know to much about this guy but if he want's to be made example of that is fine by me. I do not know many people willing to just hop into the ring with me but if he thinks he has what it takes I will just have to show him a thing or two."
"Arthur, my dear buddy 'ol pal. This Thursday is just not going to be your day. You have picked the wrong person to step into the ring with.
Things are going to go my way. The Only Way!" "To tell you the truth Arthur I think somebody on the booking comity made a mistake. I mean think about it. You fighting me. There is not really that much you can do now is there. When I looked at the ranking's I seen this is your first match in N.A.A.W and that's why they put you against someone like me."
"Arthur you have been warned. This Thursday when you step into the ring with me I will _Crush_ you. Son I will _CRUSH_ YOU."
"Now get this camera out of my face!!" [Scene Fades to a promo of this Thursday's card.] ##################### [-[name withheld]-] [ -Handles-] [-Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin-] [- #20 - RSPWF 1999 50 Top Brawlers-] [-Was Ranked 15th on E-Wrestling Heaven.-] [L.A.W] - [V.R.W] - [N.A.A.W] - [RIPWF]
