Any good e-feds looking for a very good roleplayer? (my own opinion.)

26 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.

[detail withheld] . My aol instant message is [detail withheld] if you use that. I would also be interested in opinions you all may have on the sample flash.
Name: Alexander Dreamwalker Age: 25 Height: 6'2" Weight: 256 Hometown: Belfast, Northern Ireland Alignment: Heel Entrance Music: He has pa Skin Color: le white skin Hair: He has long shoulder length black hair. Eyes: He has extremely dark brown eyes. Face: He has a regal face with hawk-like features that frame an intimidating and cutting stare.
Build: He is a ripped 256 pounds with hardly an ounce of fat on him. Scars, Tattoos, Etc: He has a mystical looking sun tattoo covering his left shoulder, a mystical looking moon tattoo on his right shoulder and across his chest is a tattoo of the gods of moon and sun locked in immortal combat. The tattoos are very elaborate and detailed with many bold and striking colors.
They are very obviously not the work of your standard tattoo parlor. Attire: To the ring he wears long legged loose fitting ring trunks with a design matching the current mood he is in. If he is in good spirits he wears a sun motif while if in bad spirits he sports the moon design. Both the sun and moon motifs feature aspects of cultures that have worshipped sun and moon deities in the past.
Finisher: Shattered Dreams Finisher Description: Shattered Dreams is a nerve hold designed to affect a pressure point on the neck that causes an extreme burning pain to rip through his opponent's body.
Wrestling Style: Once again this is a product of his mood. If he is in a good mood he will fight a merciless, but scientific style featuring submission holds and sup lexes. If he happens to be in a bad mood he simply tries to tear his opponent apart by any means necessary. He will use chairs, tables, ring bells, guardrails, knives, swords, guns, whatever he can get his hands on. In short he is either a straight up scientific master or he can be the dirtiest most violent brawler you would ever want to tangle with. All in all a technical brawler.
Biography: Alexander grew up the only son of a very powerful family of Irish entrepreneurs. He has all the privilege of wealth and status at a very early age and chose to make the most of it over the years. He has been to all corners of the globe and has studied under many teachers. However martial arts and wrestling are not the only things he has learned. In his travels he has studied under masters of philosophy, religious thinking, political science, and even the dark beliefs and rituals of forgotten organizations best left to the winds of history. In those travels he has developed a philosophy all his own that he has chosen to call The Art of the Three Dominations. This philosophy places all combatants of any type into three categories that allow the practitioner of the
Three Arts to dominate the opponent he faces. Now he simply waits for an opportunity to put the knowledge he has gained to the test of combat in the squared circle.
Sample Role-play: The Atlantic Ocean is churning under the force of wind and rain brought on by a raging storm. White-hot lightning is flashing in the sky with wicked intensity causing spectacular lightshows over a black starless canvas. The one hundred yards of coastline that would normally be visible on a clear night has been consumed by the merciless ocean waves that pound against the base of a one thousand foot cliff. At the top of that cliff sits a castle with a single light visible through the uppermost window of the structure. The light comes from a lamp hanging from the ceiling of an old English style den.
You have just been brought into this den by a properly clad English butler with impeccable manners. Bookshelves line one wall of the den and the other three walls have original works of art from both artists of today and the past.
Sitting in a leather armchair at the center of the room is a man dressed in a black silk shirt and pants. He is drinking a glass of wine and eating a bit of cheese. He has long black hair and very pale white skin. His eyes are dark and penetrating and he appears to be very deep into whatever thoughts he has chosen to occupy himself with. After taking a last drink from the wine glass he sighs deeply and shuts his eyes.
Alexander Dreamwalker: I see you have finally arrived. I have been waiting for you rather impatiently. The servants will hear of this upon the morn. But that is my concern and nothing with which to concern you over. I have more important reasons for requesting the pleasure of your company. I have much I would discuss with you before my entrance into this world of popular combat. Since you are to be my benefactor in this new trade I have embarked upon I consider it your due to know something of why I chose to come into your fold. So I will give you a brief glimpse into the mind that drives me. I sincerely hope you find my insights to be enlightening.
Alexander stands and walks over to the bookshelf on the wall. He grabs a black leather bound volume with gold lettering on the front. The lettering, upon closer inspection would read "The Dreamwalker Legacy". He opens to a particular page and shows you a very old picture of a group of people that obviously enjoyed the wealth and splendor of their time.
Alexander Dreamwalker: This is a picture of my family. I come from a very old family, very old and powerful. We have controlled most of the important business enterprises in Ireland for quite some time and I dare say many in
England as well. This wealth has allowed me to pursue many aspects of life most would never have had the chance to encounter. It has also forced upon me challenges that so called less fortunate can avoid. It is through those challenges that I have become the man I am. It is through those challenges that
I have determined the truth of a new philosophy I call the Art of the Three Dominations. I believe that all combatants can be placed into one of three rules that make up the Art of the Three Dominations. Through wrestling I intend to prove my theories to the world at large and to you. I am sure that the lessons I will teach on this new path will be useful to you and any allies you may have. After all, you are my benefactor and I owe you some small debt for allowing me entrance into your grand organization, but know this, the benefits you and your company will reap are far more than any risk you may be taking in allowing my entrance into your hallowed halls.
He places the book back upon the shelf and returns to the chair sits down and pours himself another glass of wine. He appears to have relaxed somewhat. He takes a healthy drink of the wine and another bite of the cheese. A napkin sitting on the table is grabbed and used to wipe a drop of stray wine from his lips.
Alexander Dreamwalker: As for my physical qualifications to join the organization I direct you to my resume and accompanying video. I have been taught by and bested the most accomplished martial artists in Europe. I have learned wrestling from some of the best world class amateur wrestlers in the world. I have learned to fight in fewer sportsmen like styles and have found teachers in some of Dublin's darker corners that have given me insights into those arts. With all of this skill and background you might ask me why I would need you? The answer to that question is simple. Of all the influence my family has in industry and other areas I must admit that my family has not a business sense for mass media and has let opportunities in that area slip away. That is why I need you to bring my message to the masses. Allowing me to compete in your organization will give me the audience I need to display the superiority of the Art of Dominance over other so called schools of thought. I beseech you to take my request into serious consideration and wish you the best of luck regardless of the decision you come to. For now however I wish to be left alone. My servants will show you out. That is all.
With that a servant promptly enters the room and respectfully escorts you to the front door and your waiting chauffeured vehicle. The car is extremely luxurious, and since your arrival has been graced with a new passenger. A beautiful woman with white skin and long blonde hair sits in the back of the limousine. She is wearing a beautiful form fitting green dress and a stunning diamond necklace easily worth the price of the limousine. She has a very noticeable European accent.
Alhandra: Hello, I am Alhandra and I have been asked to bring your attention to some of the benefits that my benefactor presented to you. Would you care to go for a ride?
