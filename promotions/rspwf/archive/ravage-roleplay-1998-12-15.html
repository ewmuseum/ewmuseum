{Ravage Roleplay}

15 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The screen is nothing save a black, eternal void. No movement is witnessed in the darkness. Only a voice is heard. The voice is calm and mellow, devoid of feeling. It is the voice of Ravage}
"Can you hear that? The heavens are screaming." {Only four words appear on the screen in Ravage's patented shade of green}
They Read: "The Heavens Are Screaming"
{The voice continues through the blackness} "They are screaming of the dark times that befall us. They are screaming of the coming of the Ravager. And...they are screaming of the fall of the loathsome coward who calls himself the enemy."
{Words appear on the screen again. This time, mist begins to rise like smoke, and the viewer can nearly smell the sulfuric aroma that permeates the atmosphere, choking the black sky like a merciless hand. The words simply read in that shade of green:}
"Dark Times Befall Us" {Then, the camera finally finds a sign of movement as the mist becomes thicker. Ravage suddenly appears from the darkness, and the camera focuses on him. He is covered in a green shade of light. Once again he wears a black T-shirt with the Biohazard symbol on the front in green and dark blue jeans. The camera affords no more detail to his form - only from the thighs up. This time, he has his slightly curly hair pulled back tightly in a ponytail. He looks directly into the camera before words rush forth from his mouth with the presence of a dragon's breath.}
"Time is passing by. Time leaves us standing still. Time gives us no chance to run....and ....Time punishes us for what we've done. The Ravager cometh. I will leave the wrestling world in the smouldering ruins with my wrath that it has incurred upon itself. But, enough about wrath and ruin. I shall quote another page from the book of the Ravager."
{Ravage takes a step back and crosses his arms nonchalantly} "I'm confident in saying my prayers until the end, i am - i hope you understand, my friend. You're quick to grip the fence closely when you face defeat. Face the mirror once again, close your eyes and try to visualize yourself, my friend. You'll see the same thing i do. A frail little girl in the shadow of the Ravager."
{What is assumed to be Ravage's theme music - "Save Yourself" by Stabbing
Westward begins to play in the background. Ravage offers the camera his rougish smirk once more before his visage fades again into the darkness. The only thing that remains, aside from the music, is a sentence that slowly appears on the screen...}
"A Ravager Cometh"
