Tell me if This Rp is any Good.......Please

10 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens in front of Gravestone's boneyard. After a few moments, Gravestone appears over a small hill on his '69 Sporty and pulls to a stop in front of the crew. He climbs off the bike and hoists the FWF World Title over his shoulder. He gives a glance toward the camera then motions for them to follow. As he approaches the rot-iron gates they seem to slide open on their own. Gravestone enters the boneyard with the cameraman close behind. They walk a darkened trail, andyou hear the gates close behind them. They walk up on a tomb, climd the aging steps and enter the tomb. They walk down a corridor and enter a room with light spilling into the hallway. The room has 13 black candles burning on a mantle; above the mantle is the EEWF World Heavyweight title. To the right of the mantle sits Gravestone's throne. He takes the FWF title off his shoulder and raps it around his waist. He climbs the steps up to the throne, turns toward the camera and sits slowly...the candles shine brilliantly on the gold title around his waist. He looks deep into the camera. He motions with his right hand, and the candles seems to dim, somehow. The tomb darkens, and only Gravestone features can be made out. When he speaks, it seems to come from the heart of
Destruction.)
Gravestone: Today, a new era in the FWF begins. There's no more room for the "nice guy." The place for the "honorable champion" has fallen to the wayside. An Era of Destruction has taken its place, and I will drag the FWF by the hand into a new age of dominance! I am now the FWF Heavyweight Champion of the World...it took far too long to aquire this strap, and it'll be a lot longer before I let it go! Men have stood before me in the past, and I have discarded them, and tossed them back among the minions of meaningless faces trying to obtain the power of
Gravestone; I done so without regard, for I cared not what their personal goals were. I did not care about their future, nor thier dreams, nor did I care about their desires. I simply destroyed them and moved on to the next pathetic soul the FWF saw fit to place on my path. But all that is changed. A new time has come. And a new Gravestone comes with it! I will no longer simply
"defeat" those idiots who dare to step in the ring with me. From now on, I will destroy the wills to survive! Because simply planning on taking
Gravestone's property means a lack of respect! And I will not tolerate those who have no respect for Destruction! I will no longer consider the source! I've been around long enough for each and every one of you to realize that when Gravestone says something...it rings true! And the bell tolls for you! I walk hand in hand with the Gods of War, and I rule there as I rule everywhere! I have the eyes the
Gods look into when seeking permission to devistate! I have the heart evil hears when seeking another soul to steal! I am where all things begin...and in being so, I am where all things end!
EMpire has only just begun! Do not allow yourselves to think that my sudden distant from the Brotherhood had anything to do with my feelings toward the group. For EMpire will lead this charge into the next generation! EMpire will rule and control, the next step will soon be seen...and will
never be forgotten! My friend and comrade Dark Leaf has left the FWF for good. I wish him luck in his future quests. And he will always have a place with Gravestone! No matter where the path leads, or where I end up, I will always have room for Dark Leaf! Busta
BYRD, our immediate fueds are over! And there's still a place in EMpire, if you so choose! I recently heard that you think EMpire belongs to you, and I also heard you were making plans for its future! Forget no fear, BYRD, for the future of EMpire rests in the hands of your King! You can walk with us, or you can walk against us! Jeff Jackson, he walks the path of Destruction, and he destroys as a true warrior should! There are also a few surprises, they will be coming to light soon...very soon. Be it known, as of here and now, that I and the EMpire will soon BE the FWF! Walk with us, or be destroyed by us!
Now, I'm told that I must return to an old stomping. I've been given word that I must once again beat the Hell out of Bary Sparks. It would seem that some men never learn! This man is so in love with himself that he refuses to accept the fact that he cannot, and WILL not defeat Gravestone! I wish someone would whisper in his ear. Explain to him the cold hard facts! Explain to him that no matter what he does, how often he does, or how loud he screams it, he'll never beat
Gravestone! Bary Sparks, I thought I put you out of wrestling? I thought that you couldn't take the humiliation of the last beating I gave you! I thought I sent you back to the Sparks Family ranch, where you and your brother sat side by side telling each other the stories of how Gravestone tore the both of you apart! I thought that you would've learned, Boy! How many tears did you have to shed to get this shot? How many phone calls did you have to make before the
Chairman finally got tired of your whining and gave in to your feeble demands?
How many boo-hoos did you cry before you finally got your way? You didn't need to do all that, Sparks, all ya had to do was ask...Gravestone would've been happy to practice on you. Practice for a real challenge, that is! I look at you and find humor in you! You are truly a pathetic man! You stromed around the FWF in reletive iniquity, not being noticed, and doing your job week after week like a man. Then you got a World title shot, and your ego took over! Since then, you've made a habit of making yourself look foolish! You think you're a destroyer, when in truth you're no more then a useless hunk of flesh! Since the day you tasted stardom, you felt you deserved it! You felt you no longer had to fight your way to the top, but that it should be handed to you! You felt that the FWF owed you something, and therefor you shouldn't have to do the things the rest of us have done! You claim victory over Gravestone, but you could NEVER defeat me! Somehow you've convinced yourself that you beat me, and the funny thing is you belive it! The funny thing is that no matter how the facts are laid out before you, you still believe, with child-like glee, in your own ability to rule and control! I have news, Sparks, the day you defeat Gravestone, will be the day Gravestone ceases to exist! Because if I can't beat you, I don't belong in the FWF! And if I can't beat a sad sack like Bary Sparks, I'll pack my bags and leave the FWF! Because I wouldn't want to be a part of something where you gained a victory over me, because you would live off that single win for the rest of your pathetic career!
Look into my eyes, Sparks...do you see any fear? Do you think I should fear you? What is it about you that should imtimidate, not only me but anyone? You've become a joke! The more you become a legend in your own mind, the more you become a joke in everyone elses! You, Bary
Sparks, will fell one...more...time, what it is to taste Destruction resting heavily on your mind! I will destroy you, and then never give you another thought! You have annoyed me for the very last time, Sparks. Last week when you said the words: "I QUIT!" You should've meant them! And you should've lived by them! You should've realized that you'll NEVER beat Gravestone! And you should've just moved on to other things! You've showed us how truly pathetic you are, and now I'll show the world how truly easy you are to beat! Understand this, Bary Sparks, you'll never again get in the same ring with Gravestone...no matter how much whining or crying you do behind closed doors! You've grown into a woman, or child who pitches a fit when he doesn't get his way! For that, you fall at my feet! You're now an animal looking helplessly for a home, seeking comfort in the arms of anyone who will pay any attention at all to you. For that, you'll ROT at my feet! You've grown into a joke, a farce with no hope to ever become the man, the wrestler you once were! And for that, you'll forever remian rotting at my feet, until the day I have no more use for a useless remnant of a once stellar wrestler! You will be Destroyed, Bary Saprks...have you ever heard me say something I didn't mean?....I didn't think so!
That's the sound of the reign...! (Gravestone motions, and the lights from the candles seems to raise again. He rises and walks over to the mantle where the
EEWF strap sits. He pulls the FWF from around his waist, hangs it over his shoulder. He looks into the camera with half a smile then walks outta of range.
The scene fades to black.) -- ------------------------------------------------------------ Saying/Quote of the Moment:    Live life 2 the max, it's over before you noticed. ------------------------------------------------------------ [name withheld] FWF President http://www.fwfwrestling.com http://www.fwfwrestling.com/service.html Developer Logo Designer ICQ - [detail withheld] ------------------------------------------------------------
