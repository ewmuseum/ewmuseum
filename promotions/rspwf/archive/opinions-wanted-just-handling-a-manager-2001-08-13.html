In case you missed the topic, what I am asking here is what RSPWF would think of someone just handling a manager. (I do realize that at first I will need to handle someone, I'm thinking a monsterous non-speaking character, just to get interest in the group, then add other handlers and phase out my wrestler.)
I've been thinking about giving this a go, and I'm hoping it would really boost my e-w situation (bored with the same old crap.). Either way, I'm going to put the apps up for a managing "group," which is tenativly(sp?) called "Team
Success." Any feedback, opinions, flames, money, sexual favors are welcomed. --------------- Member 1 --------------- Name: Shawn Avery Height: 5'11" Weight: 205 Information: Shawn is a flamboyant, youthful, rich brat. He's only 22 years old, and you can tell. He has short black hair, with the ever popular pushed up in the front style. Just like all youths striving for popularity, he likes Nu
Metal, and dresses as if he is a member of the "band" Limp Bizkit. He usually has a little patch of hair under his lower lip, and generally wears stylish glasses. He is rich by his parents, and sometimes acts like a child. Of course, because of these traits he is not above throwing a tantrum to get his way. He is not against getting his hands dirty, as he isn't really that small a guy, and he could have probably been a wrestler if he wasn't such a whiner. Holding the almighty dollar above everything else, he is the money behind "Team
Success." --------------- Member 2 --------------- Name: "Perfection" Melissa Palisimo Height: 5'9" Weight: 129 Information: Melissa is drop dead geogrous, and she is a behind the scenes genius, AND she has the personality of a seductive serial killer. He is usually dressed in a (very) short skirt, a coat top, and glasses. She will do anything to get her charge a win, even if it means seducing his oppenent. She cheats at every opportunity, and is very, very, good at pulling that off. Like said before, she has the cunning of a serial killer, and the brains of a genius,..
She is the real force behind "Team Succes." -- Josh - It seems like a cool idea.
