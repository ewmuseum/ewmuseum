Well folks, I finally came up with a suitible name/gimmick to fall back in sometime in the past. Right now, he's going to be working for [name withheld]
in the OWF, for now. Seeing as how I'm mostly going to be concentrating on the OWF since my "other fed" is having problems.. Another time, another story. Anyways, Here is an OWF application/roleplay. I'd like for people to critique this if they have the time, thanks.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=--=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Wrestler's Name: "The Saint" Dante Alexander Wrestler's Hometown: An Unknown Haven Wrestler's Age: 28 Wrestler's Height: Six-Foot-Seven Wrestler's Weight: Two-Hundred And Eighty-Six Pounds Wrestler's Alignment: Heel Wrestler's Entrance Music & Artist: "Gregorian Chants" by Unkown Artists Wrestler's Wrestling Style: Powerhouse/Brawler Wrestler's Ten Favorite Moves: Chokeslam, Brainbuster, Urange Suplex, Double Underhook Powerbomb, DDT, Rocker Dropper, Gorilla Press Slam, Pumphandle Slam (Meltdown), Doctor Bomb, and Short Arm Clothesline. Wrestler's Favorite Chain Moves: Short Arm Clothesline/Elbow Drop. Wrestler's Finisher: The Salvation Finisher Description: Reverse Russian Legsweep Finisher's set up: Rocker Dropper Will your wrestler do anything to signal the finisher? Yes If so, what? Dante drops his head to the side, and gives a wicked/devlish grin to the crowd, and extends his arms in the "human crusifix" pose.
Wrestler's Apparel: Dante usually dresses very "deep". To the ring, he let's his four inch black hair hang down, covering his clean shaved face.He also sports a long black cloak. Inside the ring, he wears a pair of vinyl black tights, that are covered upto the knee by a pair of black boots. Over his back, he wears a tight black t-shirt, that reads, "ESSEX.AEON.FLAZE" on the back of it, which represents something that no one knows about. (Be descriptive. Tell about what his hair is like all the way down to the boots when he steps into the ring.)
Wrestler's Description: Dante is a tan skinned white man. He has the "model" look going for him, although he isn't a poster boy. He has a four inches of black hair, that hangs down into his shaved face. He has very broad shoulders, and a fairly built torso.Covering his back is a large tattoo of a cross.

Wrestler's Story: Nothing really is known about Dante's past. People believe he was grown up fed on a silver spoon, but ended up turning out into one of the cruelest beings to ever grace the earth.What went wrong? No-one really knows. (Be descriptive. Wrestler's Manager Name: No-one Manager's Description: N/A (Be descriptive. Is he tall? Is he short? Is he a former wrestler? Race?) Will your manager interfere? N/A If yes, how? DW: Ladies and gentlemen, coming to ringside now weighing in at a total of two- hundred and eighty six pounds, and coming from an Unknown Haven, here's
"T H E S A I N T" D A N T E A L E X A N D E R!!!!!!!!] [A big heel pop goes through the air as darkness fills the arena. The Gregorian Chants are still wailing out as the fog filled entranceway soon becomes filled with a massive structure. The camera closes in and Dante
Alexander is seen standing, not moving. Suddenly, the wails pick up and Dante takes his first steps to the ring. He walks slowly, but in a dominant fashion. He finally reaches the ring, and begins to ascend the steps. He stands on the top step, and does the human crusifix as light begins to fill the arena. He then steps through the ropes, and removes his cloak, as he awaits his opponent.]
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [The scene opens with the faint sound of a low medative chants. The only light that peers into the old church, comes from the stain-glassed windows that hang on the walls. The camera begins to pan around, untill it reaches the altar. An old book sits upon the wooden stand, and looks to be very dirty. Out of no- where a tall man, dressed in a black pastor suit appears on the screen. He appears to be over six foot six inches in height and has a massive set of shoulders. His black hair, which seems to be about four to five inches long, is hanging down over his head. His green eyes pierce the screen as he gazes into it. He finally sets his hand upon the old book, and begins speaking.]
[MAN]: "Why hello. It seems the OWF has sent a cameraman, all by his lonesome, to get the words of "The Saint". Tsk, tsk, tsk. You see Mr. Cameraman, this is my haven. I reside here. I can do what I please in this church. First and foremost, I'm guessing you would like to know who I am. Am I correct?"
[The Man shakes his head up and down.] [MAN]: "I am Dante Alexander. Called a "Saint" by some, an outcast by most. You see, when I first began telling people I could help them, and heal their sicknessness, I was laughed at, and shuned upon by society. But now, many from all over this planet, come to me. But I am forced to reject these people. The same people who laughed at me, now come to me, crippled, asking to be saved. But, I cannot help these people. But there are those, who believed in the "Saint", there are those who still consider me their messiah.
And these people, are welcomed into my haven." [He begins to pace around on the stage, pondering his last words. He then looks up, and says something silently, and then turns back to the camera.]
[DANTE ALEXANDER]: "For now, I will make my new Haven in the OWF. I will reside here, untill my doings are done. Right now I know what I am destined to do, and I will do it. Not a person in the OWF will dare cross my path, or they shall be sent into eternal damnation. Soon, many people will be
Salvaged. And when I'm done here, with the people that are needed to fill the spots, and the things that need to be done, I shall leave. And never return.
But things have to be accomplished, and soon... they will..."
