Shootclub April Promo of the Month

8 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://shootclub.com Micah "Lone Wolf" King [All the lights in the arena go out, accompanied by the sound of a single, mournful wolf howl. The crowd rises, almost as one, to its feet. This time, however, the majority of the crowd is actually cheering as "Last Caress,"
the Lone Wolf's entrance music, hits the loudspeakers. Then Micah "Lone
Wolf" King takes a few steps into the arena, surrounded by a single spotlight, and the crowd pops even louder. Raising one hand in an acknowledging gesture, he heads towards the ring, wearing, as always, his now-familiar "Ain't No Rules / But The Lone Wolf's Rules" shirt. He slides into the ring, heads for a corner, and leaps onto the second rope with both arms raised high above his head. A moment later, he leaps back out into the middle of the ring, bringing both arms down as he lands. In that instant, his music is cut off and the arena lights are brought back up. He takes up a microphone and positions himself comfortably against a ringpost to begin speaking, but is forced to wait a bit longer, until the cheers finally die down.]
KING: "I haven't been out here to address you, the crowd, for quite some time. There's a reason for that. For some damn reason, which is still completely inexplicable to me, you started to actually cheer for me. I've never experienced that before. Took me a while to come to terms with what that means to me. But now I have, and I'm out here again. See, what your applause and affection means to me, is just about nothing."
[The crowd falls almost silent, though with a few scattered boos, wondering almost as one if Micah King is about to once again insult them.]
KING: "Now, hold on just a second. That don't mean that I'm against you, and it don't mean that you have to start booing me again, though you're all certainly free to do so. The deal is, you didn't start cheering me 'cause I suddenly appeared out here and started kissing babies and waving an American flag. You, the fans, chose to cheer me, not because you actually LIKE me, but just because I've done what I always said I would. I come out here, night after night, and give this ring and my opponent everything I have. I fight, I sweat, and I bleed, not necessarily for you, but for this sport and this profession that I love. And I'll continue to do so. So, if you all can live with that, then we appear to have a mutually agreeable basis to work from here."
[The crowd again cheers, though perhaps not quite as fanatically as before. Micah waves one meaty paw to urge them to silence again.] KING: "Good deal. Now, as for Drop Jaw. Congratulations on your victory. Wear YOUR belt well. But, that being said, could you be any more PISSY about it? Really. To claim that I intentionally DQd myself in our first matchup to retain the title? Looky here, buddy."
[Micah models his shirt to the crowd for a moment, pointing to the slogan.] KING: "The miracle is that I don't get disqualified in every damn match I have. Beyond that, I got no more comment for you. For the rest of you, sensing blood and wanting a piece of a weakened Lone Wolf. Forget it. It just ain't gonna happen. Just bring yourselves to the ring and let me prove, once and for all, that the man makes the belt and not the other way around.
'Cause when Micah damn King gets in that ring, you better believe he'll do." [The crowd shouts out, "ANYTHING!" Micah smiles, shakes his head slowly in disbelief, and makes his exit from ringside.]
