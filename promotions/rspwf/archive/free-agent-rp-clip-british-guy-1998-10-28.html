Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a sample clip from an RP I was in the midst of writing, but I figured
I'd throw it on this page so that "Mr. Main Event" could critique it. If anyone else feels the need to do so...be my guest. Remember...this is only a quarter of the RP...
Sample Roleplay: "VAINGLORIOUS" Vincent Sterling [British] (Tuesday - 10am) (Multiple lorries zoom across the lens of the camera, that fixated on the exterior facade of the Ashmolean museum in Oxford, just at the turning of
Beaumont St. and St. Giles, across from the illustrious Randolph Hotel. Here we see a man of average stature and form dressed in a beige exquisite tweed suit, dress shoes, and a bowler hat to top it off; all shown in a rather ostentatious manner near the kerb. Swinging idly from his hand in a pendelum motion is an oak cane, the golden hilt carved into the letter 'V'. The man floods the monitor with the eerie coldness of his ice blue eyes before penetrating the silence with his heavily accented British tongue.)
Vincent: I see you have taken time out of your schedule to waste my time with one of your prosaic interviews concerning my relations with the uncultured and uncivilized Yank ruffians of professional "wrestling".
(Vincent Sterling's eyes scour far beyond the reaches of the camera as he stares past Beaumont St., lightly rapping his cane on the pavement just off the
Ashmolean.)
Vincent: They do not understand the art of this profession, nor do they indulge in the finer etiquettes of true sportsmanship. By nature I believe they are ignorant of their surroundings...and after all, they are not of the aristocracy. But I digress. Let us not dwell on the misfortunes of a country that is in the "back of beyond" and discuss my resource pool, shall we?
(Sterling slowly clacks down the pavement, a few automobiles along with a removal van passing him by, the wind picking up in a small flurry. A discarded newspaper flips up from the ground a few meters away, swirling through gracefully through the air. In one fluid motion, the oak cane slices through the air, the spiked 'V' jabbing through the newspaper and catching it within its grasp. Pulling the torn, crumpled article off of his cane with one of his finely manicured hands, he scans the headline of the section.)
--------------------- You can't beat city hall, but you can goddamned sure blow it up! -- LT
