[Free Agent Manager] "Wild Animal" Sarah Mayson

11 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heya all. I've handled Sarah since I started in e-w back in '99. Recently, she's
"managed" her brother, "The Guardian" Tyler Mayson in NEO. But with NEO's closing, and because I've decided to separate the two, Sarah needs someplace else to work in.
First of all, let me point out that she will _not_ wrestle. Why? She's a schizophrenic. And granted, there are some schizo chars that wrestle, I've handled Sarah to be very weak, as if on the verge of permanently letting her mind take over. In that kind of condition, in my opinion, she's in no condition to wrestle.
So what's Sarah's deal? As I said, she's schizo. But it's not just another gimmick. She has history explaining what caused the condition. Several years ago, she was thrown over the side of a bridge into a quarry, putting her into a coma.
Upon her recovery from the coma several months later, she revealed to doctors that she had strange dreams, dreams that her fans were out to get/kill her, and that the only way to defeat them was by bringing about their jeers.
Then, last year, while in the middle of a feud, she took several hard head shots, reviving those dreams, making them a prominent part of her life, causing her to even believe them to be true. And so the schizophrenia began.
That's the role I'd like her to play. She trusts very few people, and vows to help a small few wrestlers win matches by cheating, again causing the fans to jeer at her.
Sound interesting? I'd really appreciate if some of you guys get back to me. I'd appreciate if you give me a rundown of your own characters, and how you could see the two "working" together.
Neal
