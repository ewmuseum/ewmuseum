[ICE STORM] GSWP Roleplay [6/29/99]

30 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens up to a shot of Chris Winters standing on his porch in sunny and gorgeous Merritt Island, Florida. He lives in a nicely sized white suburban house. It has two large pillars in the front, supporting the house, and there is a large balcony above the porch. Winters stands on the porch, with his black Honda Civic LX Coupe in the driveway to the left. Chris hops up onto the railing of his porch steps, sits on it and slides down onto the sidewalk. He gives a nice broad smile to the camera.]
CHRIS WINTERS: Yup, I'm still here. Nope, I haven't quit yet. I have been considering it lately, but for now I'm going to stay put. It seems like all of a sudden I have some business to attend to here. Vicious business.
[Winters wears a pair of white mesh Nike shorts and light blue running shoes. He wears a white t-shirt and his gold chain dangles in front of it. Winters has a small slender nose and a slightly squared jaw. His ice blue eyes seem to gaze a hole into the camera. His short brown hair is sparkling under the skylights. He has a small amount of light brown scruff on his face from a day or so of not shaving.]
CHRIS WINTERS: You know, it's strange how things come full circle. While I've been tied up with other affairs, it seems that the third man I ever pinned, here in GSWP, has developed himself into being a talented young wrestler ... or so he says.
I haven't gotten too much of a chance to watch him compete, lately, but he has said that he's been on a tare that not even
_I_ could match. Funny, his tare has put him at four and one. My tare has put me at eight and oh. Now, I've never been one to put too much of an emphasis on win-loss records, but I must say, mine has amassed quite a sum on the 'win' side. I try not to let egos get in the way of my wrestling, but being as good as I've been lately, it's hard not to. Especially when I'm used to competition like Tha Dragon and Mike Wilson, around here.
But I _will_ give respect where respect is due, and TJ Masters, you've improved a bit. [Winters smiles, as he stares a hole into the camera with his icy blue eyes. Winters gives a thoughtful look, and then continues.] CHRIS WINTERS: But can your talent truly match up to mine? Does your experience truly match mine? I've been in more promotions than you have matches. I've held more titles than you held women. And what makes me laugh the most, is the fact that your confidence is not beginning to go to your head. It's nice to get on a winning streak, but when you once again meet the man you had no chance of beating before, doesn't that stall your momentum a bit? Is your head so gassed up that you're not at all worrying about the man you could not _pin_? TJ,
I've been in some real battles in my time. I've been in some fights that left me thinking that I would never be able to walk again. Can you say the same?
[Winters gazes into the camera again.] CHRIS WINTERS: When it comes to wars in the ring, I'm a battle hardened veteran and you're an enthusiastic private. And when
I crush your ego and your motivation into a little ball and free throw it into the proverbial GSWP garbage can, you're going to have to go back to square one, and once again attempt to build yourself up to title shot status. I seem to be a master at bringing people down to Earth the hard way, but I like playing the role of the teacher. Do you like playing the role of the student?
[Winters paces around on his sidewalk, now, and begins speaking in a more serious tone.] CHRIS WINTERS: But enough about you, young one, it's time to talk about more pressing matters. "Heartless" Eddie Rue. I've told you before and I'm going to tell you again. Stay out of my way. You gave me a nice sized cut on my forehead, and that's something not many men have done to me and gotten away with.
You keep referring to phone calls you've been receiving, but I have more important things to do than to play petty games on the phone. If it's a title shot you want, there's more civilized ways to get one. Try working your way back up the rankings. It's not hard to do in GSWP. I don't have much more to say about GSWP, right now, except to watch your television screen closely on GSWP. You're not going to want to miss what
I have to say and what I have to do. GSWP's next Sunday Night Impact is going to be something to remember. And something to catch on video tape. For now, this has been your GSWP Television Champion, "Mr. Tenacity", "Ice
Storm" Chris Winters. And TJ Masters? I'll see you on Sunday. I'll be expecting to hear from you soon. Goodnight, everybody. [Fade to black, as the cell phone in Winters' pocket rings. He answers, and begins chattering as he walks into his house and slams the door shut.]
