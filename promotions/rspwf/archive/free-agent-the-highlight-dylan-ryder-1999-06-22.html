Agent Zero finishes it off by hitting the both of them eith a dropkick. He grabs one of the guns they dropped and starts sneaking around behind the Head Thug. Head Thug: Well. You must be Agent Zero. I was told to expect you. GET HIM! Agent Zero: OK, I guess we're skipping the hero/villain prefight witty dialogue. I like that part too!

Agent Zero tosses the broken pieces to the sides. Zero and the Thug trade blows, until eventually Zero strikes a blow to the head. Agent Zero: I've got to find that Head Thug! He has the information I need. (Agent Zero heads off in the last direction he saw the Head Thug go.
