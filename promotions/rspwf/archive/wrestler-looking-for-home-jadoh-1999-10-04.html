Lucha Terms Most Likley used in a ASAKA match Patadas voladoras:- (Flying kicks) A dropkick. It's used in plural (patadas) because they're delivered with both feet. Plancha:- (an iron board) There are two kinds of planchas: a flying cross bodypress and a splash (it could be a top rope splash, but it could be a regular splash). If the plancha (cross bodypress) is delivered from the top rope to the floor outside the ring it's called a plancha suicida.
Tope:-(a dive) A head-first blow. It can be a tope suicida (including his multiple variations) that is a fly between the 2nd and the 3rd floor in which the wrestler dives onto his opponent outside the ring, or a regular tope (i.e. a head butt to the stomach).
Un mortal:- (a high-risk maneuver) Any move that has a 180 or 360 degrees flip or a somersault. Examples of mortales would be a moonsault, a 2 Cold
Splash or a Asai moonsault.
Small Biograhy He was born in Tamana City, Kumamoto Prefecture on Tuesday, October
25, 1976. He will be 23 years old this fall. Tamana City is centrally located in Kyushu island in Japan and has a Kumamoto Prefectural National
Park, the well-known Tamana Hot Spring Spa, which boasts a 1,300 year history, and Rengei-In, a temple widely known for the largest bell in the world, as well as Mitsui Greenland, the largest theme park in western Japan.
Tamana City also concluded a sister-city relationship with Clarinda, Iowa
(the birthplace of Big Band-leader Glenn Miller).
Jinsei learned kick-boxing in high school. When he was a college student, he was captivated by Lucha Libre style pro wrestling. He went abroad to

DREAM STAGE DOJO, ran by UCF,UCWF, HCW, and MWWF star Shane Dreamer and his brother Stevie, there he learned his Hybrid of American and Japanese style.
