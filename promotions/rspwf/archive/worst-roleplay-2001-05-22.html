Worst Roleplay.....

22 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://shootclub.com It's long, it's boring, it's a guy on a phone..... ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------- TUESDAY, MAY 22, 2001, 11:57 AM [All that the camara sees is a chroma key backdrop and then from the right hand side of the screen, Paul Pratt emerges and continues walking accross the screen until he is out of the shot. He appeared to be looking at a phone. Beeping is heard and Pratt emerges from the left hand side and it is apparent he is dialing. But he is gone again.
He reappears with the phone to his ear but continues to pace in and out of the shot. It is quite apparent that is paying no attention to the camara.]
Paul: "Melissa? Put Joel on the line..." [Paul is again out of the shot. A small squeak is heard.] Paul: "What do you mean Mr. Tennuchi is not in yet? Was he in yesterday? ... How about the weekend? ... (pause) ... Does the man know that we have a show to put together for Friday night? ... (pause)
... Does the man know that he is on thin ice? ... (pause) ... With me! He is on thin ice with me! Now, don't you get your pretty little head confused with all the memos being passed around by Colossal. I own damn near 50 percent of this company and I still have some pull in this dump. Tennuchi may be a figure head but he is a figure head with
SOME responsibilities. Is the dude still on London time or what? It's friggin noon and he hasn't stumbled in there yet? ... (pause) ... I'm sure he will be and when he is, be SURE he calls me. I have some ideas for the show. ... (pause) ... He will be thrilled. He better be."
[Another squeak is heard and Paul re-emerges from the right hand side. He is again dialing the phone and pacing in and out of the shot. He leaves the shot and a tiny but long squeak emits.]
Paul: "Hey buddy, how's it going? ...(pause)... Not bad, not bad. Hey, have you been watching Fury? ...(pause)... I know, I know. I told
Rodeo that it's like a house show with pyro. ...(laughs)... Oh man, you're cruel! JR will love that one. ...(pause)... Anyhoo, that is not why I am calling. I wanted to see what your schedule was like for the next few months. ...(pause)... Shootcamp? Oh yeah? How's that going?
Any talent down there? ...(pause)... That green, huh? ...(pause)... At least now they are inflicting injuries on each other instead of the boys. ...(laughs)... Well, make sure that you tell the one's with actual talent that the ESW is where they want to go. ...(pause)...
GSW? L.A. doesn't even know how to support a football team, how are they going to do with wrestling. You tell me. ....(pause)... (in a bad southern-accent) Texas? Georgia? Why don't they just go to (bad Fargo impersonation) Minn-ah-so-ta. Sure, ya know. Or Ore-ree- gone...(laughs)... Well, just be sure to tell the good ones to come to
New York. ...(pause)... Well, Max and Alex better mind their manners and their borders. Becuase if they don't, I may have to go on the road and blow the doors off of Philly and Chi-cah-go. ...(laughs).. Hey, enough of that. Hey how about you? You got any ring rust?
...(pause)... Hey I gotta ask! ...(laughs)... Seriously, I think I might need you to come up here for a little bit and teach some of the punks around here a lesson. Whaddya say?"
(**squeak**) [There's Paul and he has a perplexed look on his face. He has the phone in his hand and a remote control in the other. He is now standing dead center of the camera looking right at it.]
Paul: "Doc and Larry? Oh man, you know Larry. ...(pause)... Yep, great in the ring. Solid worker. He is just serious, man. Very serious. And then there is Doc. Complete opposite. And he puts us to shame.
...(laughs)... Alright, me. He puts me to shame. I don't know when the dude sleeps. ...(pause)... Of course he is a little better these days.
He straightened himself right up once I went to him with an offer. ...(pause)... You're right, some people just need focus. It's paying off well and it looks as though I might be able to get Doc back in the gold hunt. ...(pause)... That depends, Larry has Hellion this week and he slipped a little. I had him earmarked for the ESW belt and Doc for the TV, but with Doc coming on strong I may switch that. Hopefully,
Larry will be able to take down Hellion this week and make my life easy. If you know what I am saying. ...(pause)... Back to my question... do you think..."
[Paul has a pained look on his face.] Paul: "Golden and Douglas? Fine. Fine. All the boys are having a good time. Can't hold a strap for more than a day. But otherwise, fun, we have good laughs. Ya know. Mostly at Tennuchi's expense.
...(laughs)... I just got off the phone with his secretary. I don't know if I want to smack her or ...(laughs)... I know, I know. So yeah,
G and D are doing good. ...(pause)... Larry and Doc together?
...(pause)... Oil and water my friend. Oil and water. We'll see. I am trying to work something out right now but we'll see. ...(pause)... So what was I saying before... do you think..."
[Paul rolls his eyes and shakes the remote in his fist. He sighs and turns his back to the camara. He shoulders are hunched over and he is looking at the floor.]
Paul: "Hellion and Edwards? ... (pause)... I know, I know. ...(pause)... They are good. They are. Which is what ticks me off even more. They have no respect. No honor. We... we are honorable me. These two [bleep]-ants are trash. ...(pause)... Skills, yeah. ...(pause) Out the wazoo. ...(pause)... Definitely. ...(pause)... Right now, they are but they're not ...(pause)... They definitely know their stuff, I am not arguing... (pause)... All I am saying is ...(pause)... Let me say this. I told them that you were thinking of doing a couple of tours with us. ...(pause)... Yeah, I did. ...(pause)... Oh he did.
...(pause)... Not the kindest. ...(pause)... Not kind at all. ...(pause)... He said he thought you would be good to have for the pre- intermission match. ...(pause)... Yeah, yeah. ...(pause)... He said that once you got rid of the ring rust that a quick run with you would do good business. ...(pause)... The nerve... I know. ...(pause)... I know, I know. ...(pause)... That's what I have been trying to say.
...(pause)... I think you know what I am getting at here. You think about it. Alight? (pause)"
[Paul turns around with a smile on his face. Paul is holding the remote control up and pointing it at the camera.]
Paul: "Yeah, yeah. I got it right here. It's very good quality. Smooth zoom and everything."
[The camera pans back and you see that Paul is standing in a office that could be in a basement of a house. There is a desk with a chair on the right hand side and some file cabinets to the left of the chroma key. Paul goes over to the desk and sits down. The chair squeaks.]
Paul: "I'll have my girl send one down to you. ...(pause)... No problem, No problem. As long as you make me a compilation tape of the worst ones so I can have a good laugh. Nothing funnier than bad promos. ...(laughs).. Well, you think about my offer and get back to me. ...(pause)... Bye."
[Paul hangs up the phone and then looks at the remote and then the camera and then the remote again.]
Paul: "Aw crap. I left this thing on. Colossal will have a fit. (goofy voice) Wasting tape Paul, wasting tape. (regular voice) Friggin moron."
[Paul clicks the remote and the screen goes blank.] ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- - For more promos where nobody is on a phone go check out: http://shootclub.com
