[Critique] Jesse Madrid

9 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, my last post drew a lot of productive comments, so I decided to give it another go. I am subjecting my RP here to be criticized(spell check) to the fullest.
Any comments are appreciated! And again, not my best work. (note: This was an RP I did when fighting a guy named Tommy Stylz) [fade in] [The camera slowly fades up upon the starry night sky of Miami, Florida. The camera pans down to show the city, full of lights and motion. It is warm out, but there is a breeze to keep the heat in check. The camera fades to the front of a night club in the center of downtown Miami. A bright neon sign lights the sky in front of the club. The neon lights form to spell out a word,
"Sandunga", a word that encompasses a variety of meanings including: groove, sensuality, rhythm, and above all, the splendid movement of a woman's waist, as she dances to an erotic rhythm the camera pans down to show a large group of people waiting outside of this "Sandunga" club, a mixture of women and men who can not stand still as the music from the club rings outside. A white stretch slowly makes its stop in the front of the club. The camera fades closer. The masses have gathered around the limo, just to get a glimpse of who it is.
Celebrity? King? Queen? A built Latino man steps to the door at the last of the limo, and opens it. From out of the limo, steps Jesse Madrid. *Flash*
Pictures are being taken. Madrid gains his footing and stands with composure on the sidewalk. His attire for this evening, Madrid is sporting a bright white Versace suit, the slacks are flowing, with a perfect crease down the center of the pant legs. His jacket, smooth, and looks as sweet as cream. He is not wearing a shirt under the jacket, exposing his well defined body. Two gold necklaces dangle from his neck, one, a cross, hanging down into the center of his chest, the other, a gold link chain that is short, and wrapped around his neck. His face, expressionless. Not a single blink. He stands firmly looking at the entrance of the club, and he begins to walk. Madrid walks into the entrance of the club with the body guards, and people holding their heads high. Madrid enters. Inside, the fresh, intoxicating sound of Latin Jazz fills the air. The camera fades to the bartender, he notices Jesse and he begins to clean vigorously. Fade back to Madrid. He walks with confidence towards a set of stairs, and upon arrival, he slowly steps up. Upon reaching the top, he looks to the floor of the club, noticing that every single person is dancing. He cracks a smile and he continues his walk through the inner parts of the club. As the camera lifts above his shoulder, we can see a large set of black double doors. The doors become larger and larger as the camera draws near them. A name, burned into the door, with white paint filling it, says, Jesse Madrid, Owner. Madrid pushes open the double doors to reveal a large office. It is plush, very plush. There is a book shelf on the left side of the room, with many books. The camera pans right to show various trophies and awards. The camera shows the center of the room, a desk, a massive, black marble desk in front of windows that span across the entire area of the wall.
Behind the desk, a large white leather chair. Madrid stops for a moment, and speaks] [In a powerful voice, the kind that carries through a large room.] Jesse Madrid: What you have just witnessed, was over ten years of planning that worked to perfection. A plan to make a club where the rhythms of our world could be heard, and could be danced to. An of course [smiles] Money, and lots of it.
[Madrid proceeds to walk behind the desk. He fiddles around in a drawer and he pulls out a single cigar, and sets it on the top of his desk]
Jesse Madrid: You see, I have always dreamed of having a place in which I could look to see people enjoying themselves. Women dancing, beautiful women. My dreams came true a little over 4 years ago.
[Madrid walks to the window and peers out, looking at the luminous city of
Miami.] Jesse Madrid: Nineteen, I was nineteen when I opened Sandunga. The years, they have passed fast. The knowledge that I have gained from just a small time in this business is great. Knowledge that one, Tommy Stylz, chooses to disregard.
[shakes his head] As great as you think you are Stylz, one thing that you lack is class. Sure, your opening a restaurant where the finest people, and the finest food will be served. Frankly, it doesn't mean a damn thing. [begins pacing] This, so-called "elite" group that you speak of are as scandalous and as devious as any human being on this Earth. Which in some cases, makes them the lesser beings. And sorry to say Stylz, but you are just that, a lesser being.
[Madrid takes a seat in his chair and he rolls his neck as to pop it. He looks to the ceiling, and he laughs, obviously he has thought of a private joke]
Jesse Madrid: [smiling] Can you honestly tell me that you are not ignorant
Stylz! You refer to yourself as a God? [laughs loudly] PLEASE! The first thing I noticed about you Stylz, was that you had the ability to show that you were the better man. Obviously, I was wrong. So far you have shown me nothing except for the fact that you_THINK_that you are unbeatable.
[Madrid leans over, resting his elbows on his legs, and clasps his hands together. He looks to the floor and then back into the camera lens. He speaks with fortitude behind his voice]
Jesse Madrid: I will not waste your time by replying to every single thing you said, no. Rather, I will tell_YOU_what is what, and who is who. [grins] As simple as it is to understand, you obviously do not grasp the concept that this federation does not revolve around you. [chuckles] Hell, your just another dead weight on the payroll! You see, the things you say might count, if you took a little bit of your sweet ass time to address the very federation you claim to be "The Franchise" of. Honestly [sarcastically] I don't remember the last time "The Franchise" blessed us with his words. [laughs] In all reality, no one cares who you are. While you have been busy "rubbing elbows" with the rich and the famous, I have been here, the same place I have always been, busting my ass to get to where I am today. [angered] It is time someone pushed the fuckin pedistool you stand on from under you!
[Madrid calms and he takes a drink from a glass of water on his desk. He looks out of the window, and the camera fades to show him from a profile view.]
Jesse Madrid: It is not a question of why I challenged you, because it was only a matter of time before we met in the ring. You know that as well as I do. I challenged you because I know I can beat you. There are no if's, and's or but's about it. Your reign as this federations champion has been by far, too long. [rubs chin] I have realized, and others have realized that I am the better man. Why don't you step into the light? [grins] I'll tell you why, because your too busy kissing the asses of corporate sponsors and the Four
Fathers, that's why. [chuckles]
[Madrid stands from the chair and walks over to the book shelf. There, he opens a cabinet, revealing security cameras. Jesse watches them, and he speaks.]
Jesse Madrid: Honestly, fight wise, I doubt that you have much to offer. From the way you talk, act, and display yourself, you probably shutter at the thought of being driven into the mat. [grins] Well Tommy boy, that is something that your going to have to get used to. Because I will be breaking you down come the eighteenth. I will test your abilities Stylz. I will test you from your weakest move, to your most powerful. Then, when I can sense a weakness, I will strike. [motions hands] Blow after blow you will begin to weaken. Devastating moves, one after the other. And when you are down to the smallest amount of fire left in your engine, I will blow it out like a candle.
[clenches fists] I will not stop Tommy, for no reason. I will pull the stick from out of your ass and beat you with it if I have to. When all is said and done, you will be_DROPPED_From_MADRID_just like all of my opponents have! [in serious tone]
I will beat you. I am the one, the one that you fear. You have already shown me your weakness. I will use that weakness against you, again, and again.
Prepare yourself "Franchise," because for the next week, I will be your world. [Jesse looks into the small TV screens watching, surveilling the bartender, who has just slipped a twenty into his pocket]
[fade out] ((XxBadCompanyxX)) [Jesse Madrid] 20-0 [UwF IC, UwF World Champ](former) [GxWp Tag Champ](former) [Current fed-WRX] [Awaiting re-opening of UwF] "If everyone decided to follow you...where would you take them?"-Victor Wooten
