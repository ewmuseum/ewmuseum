TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - Critique....

6 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Critizise and tell me what you think of this roleplay I did for the ICW.... =-=-=-=-=-=--==-=- {Scene opens outside the Pyramid in Memphis Tennessee at approximately 12:33PM where Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart, the ICW Juniorweight champion is sitting along the banks of the Mississippi river. He is wearing a 'Worship the Lionheart'
t-shirt and navy blue jogging bottoms. The Juniorweight title is around his waist. He picks up small stones and pebbles and throws them into the water. He then acknologies the camera and gets to his feet and speaks}
TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - Alright? CAMERA MAN - Fine... {Tommy walks along the banks as the camera follows} TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - You know, I have done a lot of things here in the
ICW. For instance, I've ''Allegedly'' attacked Miss Jasmine in her locker room.
In one of my matches, the lights have gone off and I have become the ICW Juniorweight champion. Now, Miss Jasmine in recent weeks has ran her lousy patethic womanly voice of hers and she's been squeeking about how she deserves a rematch and how she should be the Juniorweight champion as we speak but you know something Jasmine, the only thing you deserve is another Tommy Lionheart
ASS-KICKING!!! {Lionheart takes off the Juniorweight belt from around his waist and puts it over his shoulder}
TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - Jasmine, lately you have been thinking you are some sort of ''Hot Shot'' here in the ICW when the fact is, you're nothing but a
SCRAWNY WOMAN'S CHAMPION!! That's it..The WOMAN'S CHAMPION!! We all know that the only competiton you have in that division is Mystique and she almost whooped your ass at Winter Warfare. You know, I dunno what it is with you but you damn sure can't think of your OWN things to say or your OWN little schemes.
I mean, you challenged me to a LIGHTS OUT match at Fallout which has now been signed..How IRONIC!! Then, this Friday night you and your lover, 'Ruby' Shane
Diamond attacked me from behind in my locker room, now isn't that a little formiliar? Jasmine, the fact that you keep copying me everything I say, do and act is because you wanna be the 'ICON' of ALL Juniorweights worldwide. You wanna be the Lord of the ICW. You wanna carry this belt {Points to the belt}
Around your waist but you see, your reign is over, we are now in the era of the
LORD!! Jasmine, this Friday will be your LAST shot at MY title. Jasmine, I just hope you can take having a two in your loss column better than you did when you got the first..
{Lionheart smiles and walks away from the banks up to the pavement, he then starts to walk towards the arena}
TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - You see Jasmine, this belt that I carry around my shoulder, represents every single juniorweight in the world. It proves that I am the chosen one. I am the only man to wear this title and look GOOD!!
Jasmine, this title goes EVERYWHERE with me. So, yes this Friday night from the
Ice Palace in Tampa Bay, I'll bring my Juniorweight title and I guarantee, I'll walk take the Juniorweight title back with me when the match is ALL said and done. Jasmine, last time we met it was a Worship Match, which is my kinda match...AND I WON!! Now you've challenged me to a Light's Match which is
ANOTHER my kinda match and the outcome will be the same....I'll walk into the
Ice Palace as the Juniorweight champion...AND I'LL WALK OUT AS THE JUNIORWEIGHT
CHAMPION!!!! You know why?...Because I am TOOO SUPREME.....FOR ANY DREAM
TEAM!!! Jasmine, I'll give you a little advice before I waste anymore of my time on you by saying, you better...
WORSHIP..... .....THE....... ......LIONHEART.... {Tommy walks back into the arena followed by the camera man. He looks around and see's many ICW officials leaving}
TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - Why am I always one of the last guys to go? CAMERA MAN - Search me Mr.Lionheart.. TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - Please...Just call me ICON!! CAMERA MAN - Ok...ICON!! TOMMY ''Lord'' Lionheart - That's better... {They reach Tommy's locker room. He opens the door and then walks in and opens his locker and pulls out his sports bag with his wrestling attire in}
TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - Oh yeah, I almost forgot... CAMERA MAN - You almost forgot what? TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - I almost forgot to ask Jasmine, whether or not she
ENJOYED getting branded with, with that hot iron..{Laughs} Jasmine, you thought you were smart attacking me in this very locker room, well who got the last laugh? As always it was...MOI. Jasmine, you can expect a whole lot more surprises come this Friday night, because whatever it is, every 2 minutes, every minute or even thirty seconds when the lights go off, you'll find out why
I have the powers of a Lord and the strength of a Lion. I've already beaten your ass up and down the ring before at Winter Warfare, now it's time to do it for one final time and maybe...Just maybe, you will leave the ICON alone. I've said it once and I'll say it a thousand times, Jasmine you are a WOMAN!! You don't deserve to be in the same division let alone the same ring as Tommy
''Lord'' Lionheart and just like last time, I'm gonna prove that fact once and for all for count this a SECOND TIME!! Jasmine, I am the chosen one and you are the unlucky one. Just remember, stay alert because you'll never know where I'm coming from. See ya in the ring...IF YOU MAKE IT THAT IS!!!
{Tommy laughs and then out of the locker comes the ICW magazine which falls on the floor and lands on the page of Winter Warfare and shows a picture of Devon
Case brawling with Tommy Lionheart. Tommy grins as he picks the magazine up and puts it in his sports bag}
TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - Oh yes, Cornwall Case...Oops, sorry Devon, I got your name a little mixed up there. Devon, my main man...NOT!! Well I suppose he is, because Miss Jasmine isn't so I suppose this guy is. Devon, you are the number one contender for the ICW Juniorweight championship that is currently held by the Lord, that of course being me!! Now Devon, I saw some of your comments recently stating that I was right when I called you a nobody before the Pay per view, Winter Warfare but in my eyes Devon...YOU STILL ARE!! Sure, you may have pulled a few victories here and there lately but that doesn't mean you are a top star in the ICW let alone championship material, oh no. You see
Devon, you may be one fine young athlete but there;s a lot of difference between being a fine young athlete and championship material. I mean, as it so co-incidentally goes, I am not only a fine young athlete but also championship material, now isn't that good? Anyway, Devon you want a shot at MY title you got it. You know, I'd rather fight Devon because he is the rightful number one contender unlike one female I know who's getting another shot at the gold.
Devon, the time will come for your shot at the Juniorweight title, just let me
KICK JASMINE'S ASS first. So Devon, be a little bit more patient because after
Jasmine, I'm coming after YOU!!
CAMERA MAN - Are you that confident you will beat the women's champion, Miss
Jasmine who has only been defeated once in her ICW career?
TOMMY ''Lord'' LIONHEART - Are you mad? Of course I am confident for god's sake. She is the WOMEN's champion, she hasn't got the bulls to stay and survive in the men's division and she knows it. Miss Jasmine, instead of challenging me, you should be thanking me that I didn't BURN you to death at Fallout.
Jasmine, you've been defeated only once in your ICW career and I'd like to remind you that was by ME!! I'm on a major role as of late and it aint gonna be stopped by some 180lb woman known as Miss Jasmine so get that through your ulgy big head. Devon, Jasmine and anyone else who fancies sticking their nose in my business, I have a few words of advice for them and that is to...
WORSHIP..... ....THE.... ...LIONHEART.... Until next time, this has been your ICW Juniorweight champion saying...GOODNIGHT!!!! Now turn that damn camera off, I have to get my beauty sleep, we can't all be as ugly as Miss Jasmine...
{As he says that the camera begins to fade and then into darkness} =-=-=-=--==-=-=-=-=-=--==-=--==- [name withheld] Handler of... Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart {ICW - 12-4-1} Named #70 on the 1998 RSPWF Top 200 List
