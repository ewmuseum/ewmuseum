[Free Agent} "Superstar" Peter Tiger

12 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Superstar" Peter Tiger [ As the camera's flash from all over the place, a man in a black pinstriped suit stands up, and requests the attention of the crowd. At that, a man who looks to be built right of stone steps into the large conference room, and takes a seat at the head table. He stands in front of his seats and the cameras once again go crazy. He raises his hand as if to silence the crowd, he gestures toward a giant screen on the left wall, where a A Glowing Star appears, and the words "Superstar" Peter Tiger come across the screen ]
Tiger: Now is the time that the wrestling world has been waiting for. This is the day that world of profecional wrestling will change forever. I tell you that never before has there been anything like it, and there will never, ever, be anything like it again. I have trained for ten years across seas, first in japan honing my martial arts, and combat skills, then onto Europe to perfect my technical wrestling skills. The simple fact is, that I am the most well rounded athelete that this sport has ever produced. I have called this conference to announce that I am ready to go into contract negotiations here in the United States. You see, when you dominate Japan, when you dominate the whole continent of Europe, you are in need of a new challenge. After seeing the sucsess of my friend, and some times tag partner "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd, I have come back to America for the first time since I was six years old to look for a new challenge. That is why I ask any of these so called great American wrestling federations to get in touch with me, and let the negotiations begin.
Reporter: Excuse me Mr. Tiger, but how are we all to be so sure that you will fit into this much different style, and be as sucsessful as you claim you were in the past.
Tiger: The ignorance which is pouring out of your mouth, is enough to make me puke. You don't question anything that Peter Tiger says. To quickly reference your idiotic statement, the fact is, here in America, it is a bunch of hillbilly's who couldn't wrestle to save there life, or it is the extreme oppisite of highschool wrestlers who couldn't take a punch if there life depended on it. That is why I am differenet. As I have stated before I am the perfect blend of every kind of style that there is. Belive me when I tell you, after taking a few European upercuts to the jaw, you get hard real quick. And if there is still questions, then please feel free to watch me in the ring, it won't take long for you to see why I am truley the best.
Reporter: Well then, if you are in fact the best, and you have done everything possible, then what on earth are you looking for?
Tiger: This will be the last ignorant outburst that I will tolerate from you. As for your stupid question I am looking for more. I will never get enough of winning, I will never get enough of the glory, I will never get enough of being the best. That is the only reason I am here. Myself and my agent are ready to negoiate with any of the major US Federations. Then I will show all of you moronic Americans just what a real man is all about. Like my nickname proclaims, I am a true superstar, and now I am off, but this is not the last you will see of "Superstar" Peter Tiger, oh no, I am now a big part of this industry and am here to stay, but if you don't like it, you can always come and get in my face, and then see what the deal is. Now all of you losuy American journalists can get the hell out of here, and get back to your trashy tabloids, and magazines, because this conference is over!
[ Tiger stands up, and along with the glow of all of the flashes, he leaves the conference room, and the building. He steps into his black limo and begins to dial he cell phone while the screen fades to black ]
