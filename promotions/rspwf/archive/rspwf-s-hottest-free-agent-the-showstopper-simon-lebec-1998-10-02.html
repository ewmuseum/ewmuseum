RSPWF's Hottest Free Agent: "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec

2 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
 [Camera opens to the scene of "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec, sitting at his computer terminal, viewing the 90+ messages he has received from various fed heads within the last day, asking him to join their respective feds. As Lebec scrolls down, he grins, before looking into the camera]
Lebec: Well, daddy-o's.......The Stuff That dreams Are Made Of has now become.......The Free Agent That Makes Fed Heads Cream Themselves!!!
[Lebec laughs] Lebec: 'Cause I put my name out on the free market.....unsigned.....unrestricted..... and knowin' the fact that I'm what makes the buyrates go through the roof!
[Lebec points to the terminal] Lebec: And they came in droves, mostly begging and pleading, offering The
Stuff titles, titles, and more titles! Well, you AOL losers! It's gonna take a lot more than titles to put all this on the payroll!
[Lebec shrugs] Lebec: Quite simply, I've been there and done that, more times over than
I'd like to remember! I've beaten the best that there is in this sport.
I've found wins, I've found titles......Hell, I even found God! But one thing I ain't never found......is satisfaction!
[Lebec points to the screen] Lebec: What is up with all these Jap feds? If I wanted to beat on a bunch of Chinamen, I'd fire an American dollar bill into their circulation.......watch 'em lose their fuckin' minds! Heh!
[Lebec laughs] Lebec: And look at this dingleberry! Calls himself "The Showstopper" Johnny Gates! Johnny boy, they say that imitation is the greatest form of flattery, but joker, you ain't got the junk in your trunk to be The Stuff!
[Lebec shakes his head] Lebec: Nope, through it all, ya let The Free Agent That Dreams Are Made
Of down people. [Lebec leans into the camera] Lebec: Now, who can grease my wheels and really turn my crank? [Camera fades as Lebec winks at the camera]
