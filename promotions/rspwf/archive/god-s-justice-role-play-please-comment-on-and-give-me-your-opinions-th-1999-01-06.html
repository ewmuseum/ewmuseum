[Fade into a remote Location, actually, the camera opens up in the locker room area of the IWE Tag Team Champions, God’s Justice. The Great
Jackson and The Crusader are seen in the distance celebrating their great victory at WrestleKade. Great Jackson then turns towards the camera and begins to speak in a rather harsh tone.]
GJ - "What the.. This is our moment of Glory, so what do you think you are doing invading our locker room. You saw we did to The IWE last night, so don’t annoy either one of us, for we are hungry for Justice,
God’s Justice!"
[The avid reporter B.J Young is then seen in the distance wearing a black tuxedo and a pair of black dress shoes. He then turns to God’s
Justice and begins to speak.] B.J Young - "God’s Justice, I have to hand it to you, you sure showed the _WORLD_, why you are a _FORCE_ to be reckoned with and what about it, the ending of the Gospel Preachers and the rebirth of God’s
Justice." [The Great Jackson then turns to the Crusader, who just nods his head in agreement.] GJ - "First of all, get this down in your note-book. Time after Time, I made the Gospel Preachers. I took them to the status which they are at now and you know something, I don’t give a damn what you or anybody else thinks. And now on with the Show, for the Champs have engagements to attend, so go with the flow or end up on the wrong side of JUSTICE!"
[B.J Young then smartens himself up and then continues.] B.J Young - "First of all what made you turn your back on your partner after 10 years of being together?"
GJ - "Hey Crusader, you know something. He still doesn’t understand." C - "The time is not yet. Soon the World shall understand what Justice means to us both, but for now, we shall triumph in our moment of glory and by doing so, we shall make sure by the Word of God, that there is
Justice."
B.J Young - "Excuse me, you never answered my question." GJ - "Oh. You want an Answer, well God taught us the way to go on and it seemed that if the Gospel Preachers, just weren’t being noticed. This meant that because of this problem, some changes had to be made and the changes came about when the SCRA forced us to retire. You see, this was our real chance at the _TOP_, showing them what it is like to represent
God, but Oh No! They didn’t like it, so instead of us getting challenges, we were forced to retire and thus seek out greener pastures, the IWE and then it struck me."
C - "Don’t say any more." B.J Young - "What is so secretive that you can’t mention it?" C - "Well if we told you that, then it wouldn’t be a secret, would it. Now think what you are saying. You are asking God’s Justice, the IWE Tag
Team Champions to divulge secret information, well "Thou shall not lie."
This even abides with us, so I dare say to you that come next week, watch out for the bullet train to _GLORY_ shall once more get underway."
B.J Young - "Okay then, forget that question. But answer me this, what do you see of your future as God’s Justice now?"
GJ - "If you would be so good to turn to the scriptures then we shall see just what God’s Justice has in store for the Human race.
"Seek me daily and delight to know my ways, as a nation that did righteousness, and forsakes not the ordinance of their God, for they shall ask of me the ordinances of Justice(Isiah 58:2)."
Likewise, God’s Justice shall do exactly that, seek out to bring Justice in God’s name. Promoting that there is a God of the system of things and most of all to make the people of the IWE understand that there is way to receive what we do have and that my friend can be found as we turn to the Gospel of John.
"Jesus Saith unto them; I am the way, the Truth and the life, no man can come to the father, except through the Son."(John 14:6)
Likewise, the people of the IWE shall come to an understanding of the
Truth and what a glorious moment that shall be."
[The Great Jackson then goes over to the refrigerator and then he proceeds to take out some bottles of Soda water. He then opens up one and then takes a sip.]
GJ - "Nothing like the refreshing taste of water, just like the taste, people receive when they are refreshed with the elixir of God’s word, the Holy Spirit."
C - "Now Mr. Young, is there any more you wish to ask as we have rather urgent matters to attend to and we could not possibly get down to them with you here."
B.J Young - "I suppose there is one final question, if that’s okay with you guys?" GJ - "I speak for this team. It’s okay with me." B.J Young - "Very well then. Is there any doubt as to who you think should get a Title Opportunity in the near future?
GJ - "That my friend is simple. These belts we hold aren’t ours, they belong to God. God’s Bible Belts have to be placed into situations where ragged edges are met with and what better then to declare an open challenge to Teen Angst. You see, right from day one, Teen Angst, said that they would defy the Gospel Preachers and come away with the Gold, well it wasn’t to be. Gospel Preaches died and God’s Justice was placed into commission."
C - "Teen Angst, our final words of Wonder go out to you, no matter how hard you try. You will never be rid of the love of God. Instead, the day shall come as soon as the Jesus makes his return that the world as we know it shall change and on that note, we care not for you, the fans or anybody. God’s Justice is about seeking what we want and if it means upsetting people in the process, then go cry, for shall se Justice
Prevail, GODS JUSTICE!" [Fades as B.J Young then leaves God’s Justice to celebrate in their locker room. The camera then fades outwards.]
