Free Agent Stable: Bone Crusher Industries

5 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Members "The Baptiser" L.J. Mendez  6'4" 261 lbs. Bio: "The Baptiser" L.J. Mendez was a decorated amateur wrestler who took the bronze medal in Greco Roman wrestling at the
2000 Sydney Olympics for Israel.  He was born in the United States, before moving to Israel along with his family when he was 14 years old.  He loves competing as a tag team with his twin brother, Rocky Mendez.  Together they make up the team known as "Wrath."  He has a ritual of praying in his corner before each match.  His finisher, "Baptism" is a reverse DDT, dropping the opponent's back on his knee.  L.J. is attached to the Bible that his sister Sarah Jo brings to the ring with her, much like the Undertaker with the urn.
  Rocky Mendez 6'3" 253 lbs. Bio: Rocky was also moved during his adolecent years to Israel.  While living in the holy land, he disappeared from the ages of 21-24 before emerging on the scene in Europe wrestling professionally.  He is responsible for recruiting his twin brother and now tag team partner L.J. Mendez into pro wrestling.  He has some conflicts with his partner though over religious convictions, as he dispises his parents for moving away from California and his friends right before high school. 
He totally denies anything to do with the spiritual side of things.  Rocky's finisher, "wanderings" is an airplane spin into a Death Valley Driver.   Sarah Jo 5'2" 130 lbs. Bio: The sister of L.J. and Rocky, she is the peacemaker between the two.  Without her at ringside, this family alliance could explode.  She tends not to get involved in the matches, but does carry a Bible with her.  Rocky sometimes uses it as a weapon in his matches.  She never gets physically involved in matches though. Even though she matches up more with L.J., she refuses to take sides between the two, and always attempts to be the peacemaker.  She also accompanies Brute and Bone
Crusher to the ring.
  Bone Crusher 6'9" 321 lbs. Bio: This giant of a man is the founder of Bone Crusher Industries.  He is tag team partners with a lightweight named
Brute, and together they have won titles in two different federations. 
Bone Crusher himself was the first heavyweight champion for the now folded
IWA.  His experience and knowledge of professional wrestling is used to mentor L.J. and Rocky, though he tends to take the side of L.J. more often when it comes to internal disputes.  When in the ring, intensity is the name of his game, much like Prince Albert in the WWF.  Lots of power moves and brawling tactics are how he earns his victories.  Bone Crusher's finisher is a jack-knife power bomb called "Revelations."
  Brute 5'11" 217 lbs. Bio: Brute is the extreme wrestler of this federation.  With a mantality in the ring comparable to Sabu, and out of the ring, like Rob Van Dam, he meshes his harcore style with his charismatic attitude together to bring havok upon all divisions.  He will never back down from a fight and is notorious for dragging the other members of BCI into confrontations with anyone who opposes Brute, no matter who he or she is. 
Brute's finishing move is a diving DDT off the top rope while the opponent is stunned, standing in the middle of the ring.  He likes the attitude of
Rocky and has taken him under his wing to teach him about the hardcore side of the sport.  Brute is a former 2x tag champion with Bone
Crusher.   Introductory Role Play   :::The camera pans in on a man drilling amateur wrestling moves on a wrestling mat.  There are two people, a man and a woman, on the outside of the mat watching what is happening.  As the camera begins to focus in, the man stops wrestling and walks over to get a drink from a sports bottle he has sitting next to the other two observers.  He is handed a towel by the woman and he begins to wipe the persperation from his
face.:::   L.J. Mendez: How was that guys?  Think I am ready for the squared circle yet?   Bone Crusher: You still have a ways to go to reach the top L.J., but it's time to get you in the ring and start building up your in ring experiences.  Stick with me kid and I can show you what it takes.
  Sarah Jo: You mean...we are going to be on TV?  Woo hoo!!  What an evangelistic opportunity this could be L.J.!!    L.J.: You said it sis.  This could be what God is calling us to.   :::Two other men enter the room.  These two have a smaller build than the other two, but look like they have street toughness on their side by the looks of their clothes.  Both are wearing pitch black sunglasses and black leather jackets trenchcoats that have the letters BCI written diagnolly across them.  They both remove the
sunglasses.:::   Rocky Mendez: It's about time you're ready brother.  I was getting impatient at waiting for your Greco-Roman butt to get up to par.  Just don't hold me back, do you understand me?!?  This is my shot.  You all are just back up for my ultimate goal.  To get some gold around this waist.
  Sarah Jo: Don't rain on our parade Rocky.  We are in this...   Rocky: (Interupting his sister) Was I talking to you Sarah.  And as far as you are concerned, daddy's little angel, don't even try to use this for some spiritual awakening.  This isn't God's will.  It's mine!!  Your job is to just keep L.J. in line.
  L.J.: (Stepping up into Rocky's face and standing nose to nose with him) Watch how you talk to her, and especially how you talk about God!!
  Rocky: (Laughing) So are you mad L.J.?  Are you gonna get mad??  HAHAHA!!  Just what I wanted.  Now we can go for some gold.   :::Rocky turns and walks out of the room laughing as puts his sunglasses back on.  From outside the room, you can hear the sound of a Harley Davidson start up and zoom away.  The other man turns towards the door smiling.:::
  L.J.: What is his problem.  I mean he chose to leave us while in Israel.  He better clean up his attitude.
  Brute:  What do you mean clean up his attitude?  I don't know if you can see it, but I sure as heck can.  This is becoming a great team, just like you and I, eh BC? (smiling)   :::Bone Crusher slowly walks towards Brute and smiles.:::   Bone Crusher: Yep, brings back memories, huh little bud?  We were just like that.  This team is not going to be that way!  We will co-exist and reach the upper tier of professional wrestling.  You and I had problems.  These two are headed there and we need to stop them.  So L.J. is right about this one.  Rocky needs to ship up.
  Brute: Well, you take care of your disciple there and I will take care of mine.   :::Brute puts his shades back down and exits the room before you hear yet another Harley start up and ride away.  Bone
Crusher picks up a couple of coats and hands one to Sarah Jo and one to
L.J.  He then puts his on.:::
  Bone Crusher: You guys are in.  And by the way, welcome to professional wrestling.   :::They shake hands and walk out the door with their coats on.  The cameraman follows them and videotapes them jumping into a red 1968 Ford Mustang.  The licence plate reads
