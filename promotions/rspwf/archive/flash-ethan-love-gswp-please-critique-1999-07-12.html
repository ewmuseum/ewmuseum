[Flash] Ethan Love - GSWP [Please Critique]

12 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Any honest ideas and criticism is welcome. I _REALLY_ would like some people to help me out. I'm in the stage of being pushed, and I need to know if this is the right way to go.
Now, for the flash ---- [The scene opens up inside of a comedy club. A young comedian is up on stage. He is wearing a purple silk shirt tucked into a pair of black Jordache jeans. He has a stool to his left with a glass of water setting on it. The comedian is sweating a bit. In front of him is the audience. A decent sized crowd. Tables are spaced out and people are eating and drinking as they watch and listen. A cloud of smoke is above the tables. About four tables back, sits Ethan Love. He is staring at the comedian and smoking a Marlboro Light cigarette. Every now and then, Ethan chuckles at the comedian. Finally, the comedian ends his spot.
All the audience claps, except Ethan. The cameraman then joins Ethan at the table. Ethan looks at the camera and puffs on his cigarette. He holds the smoke in and then release it into the view of the camera.]
EL: "Didn't impress me very much. He has no talent, charisma, or intelligence. And the comedian was bad, also." [Ethan lets out a brief chuckle and a smirk. He then goes back to his normal look and puffs at his cigarette.]
EL: "Derek Douglas, my boy, I don't understand you. You cry and whine about not being in the main event, but what do have to back that up with? You complain about not being pushed, but you lose to _ETHAN LOVE_?!"
[Ethan again smirks.] EL: "I'm not main event status just yet, but when you whine about it, YOU BEST
BE ABLE TO SHOW IT! I'm a rookie to this business, and a "deserving main-eventer" like you should be able to pin me no problem. But the fact of the matter is that I PINNED YOU ... IN THE MIDDLE OF THE RING!"
[Ethan takes out last puff off of his cigarette and smashes it in the ashtray. He then scratches his goatee and continues.] EL: "Ya' see Douglas, when you lost to me on Impact, it showed the world that you don't deserve to be in the main events. If you can't beat me ... you'll be destroyed by the upper ranks of Gee Ess Dubbya' Pee. Just thought I'd point that out."
[Ethan situates himself in his seat. He rubs his hands together as he speaks.] EL: "Now, a very difficult task is ahead of me this week. Possibly the best talent in GSWP ... Chris Winters ... defends _HIS_ TV Title against lil' ol'
me." [Sighs. Ethan shakes his head "no".] EL: "What shall I do? [smiles] I'm sure I can figure something out. Chris isn't a hard man to figure out. He's a plain man ... nothing surprising. What he does is ... he goes out week after week and does what he promises: kicks some ass.
Albeit sometimes Robinson doesn't give him the best talent, he still showcases his skills."
[Ethan then rubs his hands along his face, as if he's frustrated.] EL: "In all honesty, I don't know how this match will comeout. Winters is top notch ... I'm still learning. *BUT* ... I have this feeling in my hear... no
... my gut that tells me that I can run with him. Yeah, he's good, so what? I'm gettin' there. But one day ... some David will slay that Goliath. Now, I have to find my slingshot."
[Ethan turns back to the stage as the camera fades.] ------ Thank ya' --Doug Handles: Sex and Love (ICW) -- 7-3-1 (Former ICW Tag Team Champions) "Hate Child" Ethan Love (ICW, GSWP) 2-2-0 (Nothing Important Yet)
