[RP for Bach] Oh what the hell....

11 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tell me what you think.... [The scene opens up in the living room of your average family... The father has got a beer in hand, and his arm around his wife, who is just dazing off somewhere in La-La Land. The kids are both lying on the ground with their hands propping up their heads, as they stare into the TV screen. The camera cuts to the T.V and a flashing "ICW Special Announcement" logo pops up a few times. And then the screen cuts to Simon Bach, who's upperbody is just shown. He has a huge smile on his face.]
"Now before you begin spinning the wheels in those pea brains and change the channel, looking for some good porno or Baywatch. But what kinda loser
"does it" to Baywatch? Geez, get a job and pay for Skinamax, why don't you.
I want you to put down that remote and listen to what the Bachman has to say, because Lord knows, you have nothing better to do, and besides, YOU
LOVE ME!" "So, you have decided to order ICW's Summer Sizzler, and even though just one of the matches will include, yours truly, you still found a way to pay for the whole thing... good for you [golf claps]. Now, let's do the checklist so we know that you ARE prepared for MY match. First, do you have
your very own "Worship Simon Bach" flag? Good. Number two, did you buy the official Simon Bach t-shirt, even better! And finally, have you been practicing your Shane Diamond laugh? Let's try...
[Bach takes off his sunglasses, cocks his head backwards and lets out a long
Shane Diamond laugh.]
Ah, you might want to practice that a little more, but it sounds pretty good, for a chump! Now get up and get a life because you are frying your brain watching T.V!"
[The scene cuts to a small, darkened room, in which Bach sits on a stool. He has a huge smile on his face and the "Shane Diamond Memorial Championship" hoisted up on his shoulder. He calls for his towelboy and his waterboy to get over there, and do their jobs... which of course they do.]
"And next time don't make this SUPER IMPORTANT SUPERSTAR wait five seconds for water!" [He raises his hand as if he was going to give the two "five across the eyes", but they scurry off.]
"Now.. where was I at? Oh yeah, Summer Sizzler. The place where dreams are made and the place where the dreams of one Todd Phelps are going to be crushed, by ME! Let's face it Todd, you don't have a chance against the man who would be the MAN, if it wasn't for Shane Diamond [laughs]. Now, I know that can't resist it Todd, I know that it eats you inside, and I am sure you are just going to get down on your hands and knees and bow before the greatest thing since sliced bread to hit the Juniorweight circuit, but I won't respect you any less if you give into these temptations. Because, since you aren't me, nor Shane Diamond, I have NO respect for you!"
[Simon Bach once again claps his hands together. And asks for his waterboy. Who as always hands Bach a bottle of water, then scampers off.] "Now before you go on your way, let me address one last thing. To all the pretenders to my throne, a throne which I busted my ass of for, you might think that you can just say a few things, or grovel at the knees of Shane
Diamond and he'll just hand you a title shot! HA! You have to climb the
ICW ladder just like me, you have to go through all of the other cruisers, just like me, and then, maybe, just then, Simon Bach will look down from Mt.
Olympus, point his finger and say 'Yes, you may be next.' But, don't hold your breath!" "And as for this Friday Night, well, me and Shane Diamond are going to have a very special announcement for Xavier Packson and D.B Bugg, who think that they have out-smarted us! You see, no man, will ever out-smart me, let alone out-smart Shane Diamond, so won't you all watch Friday, and see how exactly the foxes think!"
[Simon lets out another Shane Diamond style laugh, and then gives a huge smile to the camera as it fades out, he also starts to rub the emblem on the
ICW "Shane Diamond Memorial Title".]
