Critique [Black Bishop] Welcome to the US of A

5 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a sample RP for a new character I'm making, the RP pretty much explains his gimmick which is pretty basic right now but I've got lots planned in the future, plenty of angles and RPs stocked up already. The name is pretty crappy I know but like I said this is just a sample to see how the gimmick works, if it gets a good reception I'll work on a new name and add to the RP. I'm also gonna post this on the No Name Wrestling application board in the vauge hope I get in, how do you all rate my chances? Thanks in advance for any comments (good or bad).
[The camera shows the NNW sky scraper, 2 tough looking guards are standing at the door keeping any would be intruders at bay. A few fanboys stand around the entrance clad in the traditional dress of fanboys around the world, black t-shirts and shorts, all eagerly awaiting the arival of NNWs new signing the Black Bishop. A chill wind blows around the building and the fanboys huddle closer to the building seeking the meagre protection it offers from the elements. All eyes turn skyward as the 'whuk whuk whuk' of helicopter blades fills the area, a sleek black helicopter comes into shot with the NNW logo emblazoned on the side, it slowly lowers down onto the deserted road outside the massive NNW building. One of the guards runs forward to greet the helicopter, as it touches down the door opens and the fanboys crane their necks in an effort to see the new star. A well dressed man jumps out, he's perhaps 6ft with gelled back hair and a pair of Armani shades making him look like some sort of rock star, the gathered crowd gasp at the mans expensive looking designer suit, he flashes a dazzling white grin at the crowd and gestures to the helicopter. A secound man climbs out as the helicopter takes off, the secound man is the complete opposite of the first, he's wearing a long black robe which covers his giant frame, standing at almost 7ft he makes an intimidating figure, the robe drapes around his head leaving only his eyes visible. He leans on a massive sledgehammer as if it was a walking stick, dozens of cameras go off as the fanboys get another photo for their album. An NNW interviewer runs forward and thrusts a mic into the Black Bishops face but the smaller suit wearing man pulls it towards him.]
Man in suit: Hi, I'm Cliff Rockman and I'm the Black Bishops spokesman, any questions for him go through me ok?
Interviewer: Sure, you wanna go inside? Get out of the cold? Cliff: We'll stay out here for the interview, the cameras love the urban look. Interviewer: Errr ok, whatever you want. So could you tell us a little about the Black Bishops history?
Cliff: I found the Bishop in a small village in Eastern Russia, he was a basically just a slave there, he did alot of pit fighting and he was the best. A local legend really, all the villagers supported him, even if they never accepted his as one of their own he was like this big monster who was wheeled out whenever they needed to satisfy their bloodlust. So I brought his freedom, the bloke who managed him before was a fool, a few rubels and he was offering to throw his daughter in as well. And here we are, the
Bishops free to do whatever he wants and he chose to work as a Wrestler, with me as his manager.
Interviewer: Wow, some story what's with the robes? Cliff: When he was a pit fighter he got some pretty bad wounds, dozens of scars on his face. But that's not the real reason, he's ashamed of what he does. Wrestling is a barbaric past time and he knows it, he only does it for the money but he never wants to be truly famous for it.
Interviewer: What does the Bishop hope to achieve in NNW? Cliff: Gold of course, belts equal money in the world of Wrestling something we both want more than anything in Wrestling.
Interviewer: Heh, don't we all want money? What about you, are you just a manager or do you fight?
Cliff: Hell no, I'm not stupid the Bishops a natural he could destroy any one in this Fed before they even know what hit them. Me I'm just good with money, not fighting. Besides should a face this pretty ever be damaged.
Interviewer: [Laughs] Yeah of course, one last question, what's the Bishops fighting style. Hardcore? Powerful? Technical?
Cliff: Depends on the opponent but he isn't affraid of getting a little bloody in a fight and that sledge hammer isn't just for show.
Interviewer: Well, best of luck in NNW, thanks for the interview. [The camera fades out as the Black Bishop and Cliff Rockman head inside the buiding followed by the interviewer. The fans thrust out autograph books to the Bishop but he just walks past them his eyes staying deadly cold. Cliff smiles at the crowd and shrugs.]
