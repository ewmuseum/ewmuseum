[Free Agent] Shawn "NBK" Rosenthal

29 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, I'll admit that the sample roleplay I put here did suck. Like I said, it's easier once involved in a fed, and involved in some angles, or at least having some upcoming matches. I wrote another roleplay, and took a bit longer on it, so if you didn't like the other one, check this one out.
(The screen opens in a hotel room in Daytona Beach, FL. We are brought a few of the Atlantic Ocean through the window of the hotel room. The sun is setting, and the reflection off the water makes the water look like a vast ocean of gold. The sky is pink and orange, and there are people running about on the soft sand. A noise is heard from behind us, and then Kids of the K-Hole by NOFX begins to fill the room. The camera turns around just in time to see the bathroom door close. The lights flicks on, and we hear running water from the sink. The water stops, there is a moment of silence, and then feet shuffling. The light flicks off, and the door opens. Out walks a young man, no older than 19 or 20, wearing only jean shorts and black sandals. A gold belt is draped over his shoulder, and as the camera zooms in, we see that it reads "WOW Extreme Championship of the World". The belt is throw carelessly onto the bed, and then the unknown man takes a seat at a table in the corner. The camera inspects him, giving us a good view of his dark complexion, long, dark brown hair, and totally white eyes. His muscles, although not extremely large, give him a powerful look. He pushes his hair out of his eyes and begins to speak, slowly and confidently.)
Shawn "NBK" Rosenthal - Daytona Beach. I grew up here, under the sun. I lived with my parents until I was 16. That’s when it happened. My parents were killed in a car accident by a drunk driver, and I lost the only people
I ever loved. I was left without a family, and without any idea of how I could survive in the world. They tried to take me away to live with another family, but I ran away from home. I found an old, abandoned gym in one part of town, and I decided that I would make it my new home. I made myself a bed of old punching bags, and that is where I sleep to this day. Sure, I could live somewhere else besides the gym now, but it fits my lifestyle rather well. You see, when I felt lost and confused after I ran away, I had a lot of emotions bottled up inside me, and I needed a way to let them out.
So I trained there. When it hurt, I’d train harder, trying to prevent myself from realizing the truth, that my parents were gone and I was alone.
Finally, I had a plan for my life. I wanted to be a professional athlete. Picture this. A scrawny, 16 year old kid lifting weights day in and day out, his only hopes to become a professional athlete. I looked in the newspaper, and Way Out Wrestling, an organization based in my hometown, was looking for wrestlers to compete in their new league. So I figured, why not? By this time, I was 17, and I figured that as long as I kept working,
I could make my dream come true. And what do you know, they hired me!
Sure, they figured they’d just let me get beat up by the big guys there, but
I showed them how hard I worked, and they told me I had real potential. I didn’t make any appearances for a while, because they said that I still wasn’t big enough. They actually paid me to train, and to get bigger. They even helped me buy the gym, and fix it up so that I could train properly.
Finally, I made my WOW debut, and shocked the world when I won the WOW
Extreme Title. But WOW fell apart, and that left me in a bad position. I still own the gym, but since I don’t have a job, I don’t know how long it’ll last. That’s why I’m looking for another federation to join. One where I can make my presence known. I’m willing to work my way to the top, from the ground up, like I’ve done for my entire life. Please, if anyone out there has any openings, don’t hesitate to let me know, as I’ll try my hardest to rise to the level of competition wherever I go, and I’m very easy to please. Federation owners, please help a wrestler in need.
(Shawn Rosenthal turns to the window, and looks down on the many families scattered throughout the beach. A tear forms in the corner of his eye, and he covers his face with his hands. Finally, he looks up and uncovers his face. He pushes his hair out of his eyes, and escorts the camera man to the door, where he hands him a twenty. The cameraman walks away, and Rosenthal closes the door behind him.)
