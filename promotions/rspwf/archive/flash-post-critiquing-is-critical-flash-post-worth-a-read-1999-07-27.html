------------------------------------------------------------ ------------------------------- RSP-WF'ites, we speak again .. This is [name withheld], handler of the "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao, and .. [Yes, there's an and.] My newest creation .. "L'Amoureux" Sebastien Dupré .. Acquired you say? We'll .. Here the run down on the gimmick of Dupré . A Cajun decendent. Cajun's, which were a group of French Canadians who moved down to Lousiana about 200 years ago. Now, my character, "L'Amoureux" Sebastien Dupré, is a total Full Fleshed lady lover. This is what he thrives for, and this is what generates his soul & spirit, etc. To continue on in this life. Though, Sebastien is a very rugged and rough villian type, this disguises his handsome looks, thus' the women reject him. Sebastien will do anything to get the ladies attention, no matter the consequences. Sebastien is a wrestler, who really just isn't a wrestler. He taught himself everything he knew in the basement of his own house. He is very unskilled within the departments of Technical wrestling, thus' he likes to strike with his power and his brutality. Standing 6'4 and weighing in at a fine 253 Pounds. He is definatley the Cajun "L'Amoureux."
Ok, so there is the run down on the character, and right now, I am going to put a flash on here. My first and only flash with this new character so far, so go lightly on the critiquing. So here we go, Yah !! (-;
------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------ [The camera shot dissolves from black into the interior of a mildly illuminated, French Restaurant in the middle parts of Florida, upon the water washed beaches of Fort Lahderdale. This restaurant is slow in business in these afternoon hours. Few customers sit before our camera shot, just as suddenly we notice a nicely and slickly dressed man, sitting in the far corner of beach shore restaurant.
The lamp above the man brings light to his complexion and figure, which gives us the advantage to identifying the man. He sits there, with his light brown hair parted to one side of his head in sort of a wave like pattern. His lightly tinted skin is nothing to impress, but his muscular physique takes all of that attention. The man sits there, picking at his French Cuisine and suddenly begins to speak.]
V/O [In a French Type Accent.] : " Bienvenue, bienvenue à Mon restaurant. Ah yes, my investment as well as my money maker. That was, until I founded the sport of professional wrestling. And yes, what a grand profession it shall be. Everything I should want, need, cherish. Lies within the parallel world that is .. Professional Wrestling. "
" Le Fortune, Publicity, and .. Le Women. " [The last words that he speaks, he speaks with a certain happy and desiring tone. The camera attention thinks nothing of it, as this man continues.]
V/O [In a French Type Accent.] : " The Women. Since the days of my primitive teen years. The Women, have blessed my soul with their presence, their touch, and their love. Though, the last of the three, is not obtainable for a man like me. I am the French "L'Amoureux" and I search for one day, to obtain this. I am "L'Amoureux" Sebastien Dupré, and I seek ..
Love. " " No matter the costs, no matter the battles, no matter the worries it may cause. I "will" obtain this .. and whoever shall stand against this, then faites comme vous devez, but I swear to you all and to hell with me if it doesn't happen. "
[The man, now known as "L'Amoureux" Sebastien Dupré smashes his hand on the table in anger, as some food tosses off of the table onto his finely designed silk T-shirt.]
SD [In a French Type Accent.] : " Eh bien, enough of that. Until I can achieve what is rightfully 'mine,' their are aligned men in the path who are waiting to stop me. To stop the Cajun Catastrophe, "L'Amoureux" .. [Sadistic
Grin]
Yes, it's not long until I pummel the first innocent compétiteur that has been arranged. [Shakes head in disgust] "Magnificent" Mike Douglas .. "
" Le Magnifique, eh' ? A great competitor I have been told, by President
Wayne. A 'homme' that can someday reach the peak of the ladder ? [Smirk]
But, who here is to stop this ? Le "L'Amoureux" .. And who won't stop me from reaching my goal, and to sexing vers le haut de my soon to be love, Jennifer Miller .. "
[Sebastien brings his hand to his mouth, in a lightning quick gesture.] SD [In a French Type Accent.] : " Uh OH, look what I gone and did. My secret has been revealed to you all. [Smirking Grin] Yes, I, Sebastien Dupré have fallen in love with your HHWO News Reporter. Le Beau is to much to resist and she has caught the likes of myself. "
" But now, my reason for wrestling has been revealed, though it was bound to happen, so now was as best time as any. [Nod] Eh bien, Mike Douglas,
Jennifer Miller, the Hometown Heroes de' Wrestling, "L'Amoureux" has arrived .. Though the question is, how long will I stay .. ? "
[The camera pans away from Sebastien who continues to eat his meal, just as our
HHWO television shot turns to a fading black picture. ] <font face="courier new">
