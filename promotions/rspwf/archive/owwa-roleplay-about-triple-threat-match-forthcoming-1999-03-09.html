Wallace is in his office Wallace- Trainer you have the go to do what ever you want in that 4 corners match. And Puke you got it to. Now Dogg Pound I see you have figured out you are not in the Corporation by asking those people to join you. We were going to give you one more chance but you asked for it. So Masked Dawgs you are getting exactly what you wanted. But you aren't just getting the dogg Pound you are getting them in a Cage Match.
On friday the Dogg Pound is going down. Now since this will be a brawl of course it isn't a tag tournament match. Now Anaconda I give it to you for a nice win so if you want to, I will give you next week in a tag match. And Offspring you are in big trouble because you are going to get a hardcore match. You VS UNKNOWN. But of course I need the trainer, what kind of DEATH MATCH do you want offspring and unknown in. I trust you to pick a good one. Now Goliath you will be in a triple threat match with
Decapitator and Grim Reaper. And since they are a tag team I guess you are in trouble. I am sure they will work out who gets the win. So next
Friday will be a very big card. Now I have some buisness to attend so I would appreciate it if you would leave my office. (CAMERA FADES)
