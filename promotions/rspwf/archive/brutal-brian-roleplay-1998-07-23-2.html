'Brutal' Brian Roleplay

23 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** I'd like to know what you all think of this. I'm starting to get out of my roleplaying block and I feel that better roleplays are indeed on the way. But still, I'd like some imput on how you view me right now. Emails are more than welcome. **
[The scene opens in a huge, vacant room. The walls are made of red brick and the floor is solid, gray cement. The camera pans slowly until a huge clock comes into view. Sitting in front of the clock is a man in solid black clothing. The camera zooms in and you see that he has on a black leather jacket, black pants, and black Doc Martens. He also has curly brown hair with blonde streaks in it that hangs down beyond his the back of his neck. The camera zooms in closer and you know are right behind the man, and you realize that it's none other than "The Prince of Lost Souls" 'Brutal' Brian. He is staring vacantly up at the clock as each second ticks by. The pendellum at the bottom of the face of the clock swing back and forth in a fluent, yet slow motion. Brian waits a few moments and then begins to speak in a low, hard voice...]
'BRUTAL' BRIAN - Some say that time is a state of mind, and some say it's a period of life. I, for one, have the opinion that time is what you make of it.
You see, there are intervals of time in which we encounter obstacles, things that require a strong state of mind and an undaunted heart. We must -OVERCOME- these obstacles in order to better ourselves, emotionally as well as psychically. These intervals of time can be both short and long, it just depends on how quickly you defeat your "opponent". Time, for me, has been going by rather slowly. -SOMETHING- is eating me alive and I have no idea what it is. This "something" has embedded itself upon my soul and hinders my every emotion...my every thought. I have no clue on how to defeat it, for I know not what it is.
['Brutal' Brian stands up and comes face to face with the clock. He stares right into it, almost as if he's looking through it.]
'BRUTAL' BRIAN - But I am determined to be victorious. I have searched my soul in and out and I am beginning to gain some ground on what I call "my ordeal".
This may take me the rest of my life, and it may take a few, mere weeks. Nothing is for certain in my pathetic life. Well, let me take that back. -ONE- thing is for certain and that is -ME- winning the World Title here in World Vintage Wrestling. To be honest, I have never been certain of anything in my life. Everything has had no meaning or reward until now. You see, I have -NEVER- held a title in my entire career of wrestling. At this point in my life holding a title is all that matters. Friends, fans, and everything else can go to hell as far as I'm concerned. I don't need any support because
I have -MYSELF-. And, if I understand correctly, the person who wins a title gains it by winning it on his own. And -I-, 'Brutal' Brian, will do just that.
[Brian suddenly punches the clock and shatters the glass covering the face of it. His hand begins to bleed but he pays no mind to it. He then grabs the pendellum and pulls it from the clock, throwing it across the room. You hear it land with a dull, metallic clank off in the distance.]
'BRUTAL' BRIAN - Well, I guess you can now say that time has halted. This goes for me, Hardcore, and the rest of the WVW. Speaking of Hardcore, you escaped defeat narrowly. You will get yours in time, or should I say in this pause of nothingness. You right now are my obstacle here in the WVW, and you -WILL- be cleared. I want there to be a clear winner between you and I, and I want the world to know exactly who the winner really is. The real winner is -ME-,
'Brutal' Brian. Now you might be talented, you might have held titles in the past, but I doubt you have -EVER- encountered something quite like me. And I honestly think that if you say you have you'd be lying between your rotten teeth. I want us to meet again, Hardcore, and I want to end this once and for all. I'm not the kind of man to stretch a meaningless fight out, so I want to make this short and sweet. So, Mr. Hardcore, if you wanna get brutal...then
-LET'S GET BRUTAL-!!! [Brian walks away from the clock with his hand still bleeding. The camera zooms in on the shattered glass around the clock and the indention in the face of it. It films this shot for a few seconds and then slowly fades out...]
Brian F. Handler of 'Brutal' Brian {IeWC, EWC, WVW} ...and Reverend Sin {EMWO}
