The Amazing Dipsticks Introduction Flash

11 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**Outside the MCW offices** Janitor Man *exiting building*: Say, what kind of crud league is this, I. M.? They had a moron in a ninja suit giving the eye test. BTW, just say "O". I.M. Sightless *reading the braille edition of Playboy*: It's Mentally Challenged Wrestling. The last federation on the planet that'll hire us, The Amazing Dipsticks. By the way, I guessed "O". I'm already hired. Janitor Man: Well, if you hadn't drank that Aqua Velva and went blind, we would be working for the WWF. We're better than the Godwinns, or at least we were until you screwed up. I.M. Sightless *still reading*: It's a job, isn't it? Besides, it's better than mopping the floor at elementary schools. Janitor Man: Hell, no!! At least there were some babes at that job. Here, the closest thing to a babe is a 300-pound guy named Dolores or something who manages a pansy called Holden McGroin. I.M. Sightless: They let freaks like that in here? I thought this was a high-class federation. Janitor Man: Let's see, they've got inbred hillbillies, goldfish, nancy-boys, toilets, dumbos in ninja suits. We have more class than this whole organization . . . I.M. Sightless: Then let's leave. I can always be a masked jobber in Hong Kong. Janitor Man: Let me finish, I.M. We don't have more class than their six-figure paychecks. I.M. Sightless: That's six in front of the dot, right? Mommy, we are rich!!!!!! Janitor Man: Don't call me that, man. Just call me Janitor, please. I.M. Sightless: Hey, it's not my fault your mother only knew one word when she named you. Besides, you said they have wrestling goldfish. That says one thing to me, free sushi!!!!! Janitor Man: You're right, let's go. I need to stop by 7/11 and get a fifth of Sterno. I'm thirsty as hell. **They leave. I.M. Sightless bumps into a tree.** I.M. Sightless: They're bumpy here, all right. Just like trees at home. **faints**
