============================== [Fade in. The scene opens in a seedy porno store. The lights are dimmed just enough to get the right atmosphere. There are sky blue neon lights along the walls as well. We see a man wearing a black trench coat, taking a look at some of the videos they have available. The man turns around.
He is a caucasian man with short, spikey brown hair and green eyes. He is clean shaven and he looks quite handsome.
The trench coat is undone, and we see that he is dressed in a plain, black Tommy Hilfiger t-shirt, a pair of moderately baggy Nautica jeans, and Doc Martens. We recognize the man as "Sexually Driven" Brandon
Straker. He puts the porno video back on the shelf and grins at the camera. He begins to speak.]
"SD" BS - Welcome everybody to Anthony's Kinky World of Porn! Over ten thousand videos to chose from...we have the classics such as "Putting Your Ass On The Line," to the newest of releases, "Low Blow"! [chuckles] Oh, and for those of you folks who don't know who I am...I'm the newest Banned From Television Wrestling supa'sta', "Sexually Driven"
Brandon Straker. I think my nickname says it all about a man like myself. [smirks] Now, a lotta people have been sayin' to me,
"Brandon, why are you such a freak? You love porn, you love sex, heck, you'd stick your stick shift in any thing that moves! Why is that?" Well,
I'll tell you exactly why, freaks and freakazoids...
[He pauses and chuckles wickedly.] "SD" BS - 'Cause it gives me athletic prowless. It gives me the energy to beat my opponents in the squared circle...and it looks like I'm gonna be puttin' my skills to the test when I meet Jack Vars in the ring on FANatic at the St. John's Alumni Hall in Queens, New York. Too bad
'bout St. John's, eh? Friggin' Ohio State...but anyway, Jack Vars, I know that you're a great wrestler. I know that you''ve one an S-load of titles almost everywhere...
Hell, Vars, you're a professional wrestling _whore_! But what about Brandon Straker, huh? What about this "new kid"? Well, Vars, you've got the edge in experience, but when it comes to lightning fast maneuvers, high-flying action, and showing those BFTW fans what wrestling is all about...
[He smirks and folds his arms.] "SD" BS - I'm your man. Now, Vars, take a look at the initials in my name..."B-S." Not only does it stand for bull[No audio], but it stands for [points at himself] Brandon Straker. So, Jacqueline -- er,
Jack, our match is gonna be... ....._All B-S_..... I'll be sure to make our match a _pleasurable_ experience, Jack...and when the time comes, I'm gonna make ya' feel The
Climax. You'll never know what hit ya'.
Seeing you suffer.... ....Just puts me in near _orgasmic pleasure_..... [Fade out.] ==================== -Jim -[Handler of] "Good Ol' Tee Gee" Tommy Grayson [Currently Competing In] DSWA, ICW, IWA, JPW, EFW [Current Titles Held] EFW Extreme North American, ICW North American,
