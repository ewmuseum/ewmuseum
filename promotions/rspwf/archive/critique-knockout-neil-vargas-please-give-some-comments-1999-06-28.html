[CRITIQUE] "Knockout" Neil Vargas... Please give some comments!

28 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys... I never really ask for an RP critique. Just once in a while. This is a new character of mine. This is his first RP, I wrote a couple after this one as well, but I still think this is the best one so far. I sent it to both EWHeaven and EWZone over a week ago, and neither has critiqued it, so I thought I'd bring it here. Let me know what ya think.
Thanks, and enjoy... ---- [Fade in on the elegant hotel bar of the Hilton in Memphis. A jazz band plays an instrumental version of "Moon River". The guests, clad in expensive suits and evening gowns, enjoy themselves with stomach- and heart-warming drinks and everybody seems to be in a rather cheery mood, although a few throw irritated glances at the camera.
The camera zooms in on a particular patron at the bar, sitting on a stool, who salutes towards it. It is a caucasian in his mid-twenties, gifted with a powerful, muscular frame. His blond hair is closely cropped and he has a five o'clock shadow on his otherwise rather handsome face. He wears an elegant black suit with a turtleneck pullover under it. In his right hand is a cigarette, in his left a drink that looks like Cuba Libre. A confident smile is on his face as he waves the camera over.]
NV: Ah, the people from Volunteer State Wrestling. Good to see you finally. [He gestures towards his chest with the cigarette.] NV: Let me introduce myself to the unfortunate few who have yet to see me in action. I am "Knockout" Neil Vargas, _your_ next
World Champion. [He sips his drink.] NV: Most of you should have seen me before, crackin' heads in Hawaii, in
Iowa, Boston and even here, in Tennessee and you _know_ that I am the perfect choice to be the first champion of an up-and-coming promotion.
A first champion can make or break a promotion. He will represent it in the public and he will be the image the federation sends out to the fans worldwide.
I have the whole package to offer ... skills, strength, wit and almost illegally good looks. The big belt would look very good around my waist, indeed and President Carwile knows it, or he wouldn't have hired me.
[Vargas stubs the rest of his cigarette into an ash tray.] NV: Although some of the suits at VSW headquarters told me I should stop smoking in the public. "Bad for a role model", they said. Can you believe it?
[He shakes his head, chuckling.] NV: Here you have one of the most fit athletes of our time with refined manners and boxer shorts that cost more than the usual income of one of those guttersnipes and they want to change my behavior.
That's one thing I hate about this country... the witch hunt on us peaceful people who worship the great god Tobacco. Do you want to live forever or what? Next thing you know they'll outlaw vodka and
cocai- ah, already did the latter, right? [An obnoxious grin appears on his face.] NV: Did I mention where I come from? No? The beautiful nation of Germany. Sorry that I don't have that oh-so popular accent. I haff hud a very gud education, you know. Ve Germans haff de brains to learn a simple language like yours. Ve don't need no stinkin'
accent. [He pulls out a new cigarette and lights it with a match, staring at the flame for a moment, than taking a deep breath. Neil produces a not so perfect smoke ring.]
NV: Don't worry, I am not one of those flag-weaving nazi-Germans who screams at the top of his lungs. I don't have any "major" problems with the USA, quite to the contrary.
This is the place on Earth where Pro-Wrestling flourishes ... and where the top draws can earn multi-million dollar contracts. There aren't opportunities like that in Europe, where you can very well be the champion of a promotion, yet you don't earn as much money as a decent plumber.
[He drops some ash on the carpet without even looking.] NV: That is why I am here. For the big, big money. I bet there are some schmucks out there who think they fight for a higher purpose or just for the athletic competition, but not me. I want the "bucks", as you people put it, and what better way would there be to support my claim to a top contract than winning the big one on my first night out.
Like one of my trainers said, motivation is the key. [He inhales again, then draws the exhaled smoke back in through his nose.] NV: Mike Carwile may have made some strange moves in the past, with his Insane ... [A mock shiver.] NV: ... based promotion but looking at the brackets right now it looks like he has paved the way for me straight to the finals.
My first opponent is someone named Scottie Extreme, a loyal little trooper who worked for Carwile already in that Insane promotion that went straight to bankruptcy; hell because of stiffs like him that could not put an ass in the seat even if their life depended on it. I guess we need guys like him to get a bigger roster and a larger than life tournament but I hope after the next card this sorry little man is on his way to the end of the unemployment line again. We do not need to pay losers like that money that could as well find its way to my pocket.
[He rubs his left temple with his index finger.] NV: Fighting for the right to lose to me in the next round are the "One Man Riot" ... I guess the smart names were all taken, heh ... and
Arhur Essex. Can you say ... filler? [He nods.] NV: I thought so. Let me fast-forward two weeks or so to the final where you will see moi, of course, fighting ... Eric Cooper.
Eric Cooper is one of the few guys in the biz who have "made it". Money hanging out ... you know ... the anus, married to a beatiful piece of ... you know ... anus ... and having the rep to be a real ... you know ... an _ass_. You could call me a fan. I have seen him in such classics as "Five Stars, Part XIV" or "Livin'
Legend ... Washed up but still making Money". Great stuff, really, almost as good as the things he did in '95 when he could, like, still fight.
[He winks at the camera.] NV: I hope you are in good shape these days, Coop. I want my first major title win to look good, and saying "I beat Eric Cooper for it" sounds so much better than "I defeated Scottie Extreme, but, honestly, there were a couple of good men in the tourney as well".
[He turns on the stool towards the barkeeper.] NV: Another Cuba Libre Supreme, Jack. These interview things make me thirsty. [He looks over the shoulder.] NV: This is over, take the tape to the boss. They don't pay me enough to tell epics ... yet.
[Fade to black as Vargas stubs the cigarette into the ashtray and pulls out another one.] [name withheld] - All around good guy, and -
