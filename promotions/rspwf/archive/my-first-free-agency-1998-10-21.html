My first free agency...

21 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After taking some cool advice from [name withheld], I decided to do a little
RSPWF history searching myself. No, I'm not doing a flashback for everyone else, I'm doing a flashback for myself! Damn straight I'm doin' it. For those who've been on the RSPWF since about February, you might remember my first ever free agency post. For those who don't, here it is.
-Daniel To join [name withheld]'s new federation, e-mail him at [detail withheld] . ------------ I want a booked/ fantasy wrestler league. Please get a good fed. Here's a sample roleplay:
e-mail me at [detail withheld] [Scene fades into a small arena in Chiago, Illinois. There are a few hundred fans of NACW here to hear and see Lawrence Williams speak. "Scoops" Williams in the ring. He's wearing nice, all blue suit. He has a microphone in his hand...]
Scoops: Ladies and gentlemen. We're here today to hear from a man who is a few matches away from stardom here in NACW. We're here to see a man known to you all as, Lawrence Williams!!
*The crowd cheers only because he's their hometown hero...* Scoops: So without further adue. Here's Lawrence "I DON'T NEED A GIMMICK" Wiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiilliiiiiiiiiams!!!!!! [Fog, which is really dry ice being blown all over the place fills the entrance to the ring. The crowd is cheering. Mind of a Souljah is playing throught the arena. The whole place is rocking. Then a leg presumed to be Lawrence's steps through the fog. He has on black dress shoes and what seems to be an all black suit. He then puts the rest of his body through the entrance way. He steps about 3 feet from the entrance and stops and looks around him. The crowd is cheering. Confetti is everywhere. He starts nodding his head repeatively.
Then "Scoops" motions for Lawrence to come down to ringside. Lawrence starts walking at a steady pace shaking the fans hands, alternating from the right side to the left side. At one moment Lawrence is pulled back by a fan that just won't let go. Then the ring side security has to release the grip, and
Lawrence continues. Lawrence walks to the ringsteps, stops and looks back. Lights are flashing everywhere from the cameras going off. He walks up the ringsteps, and steps through the middle and top rope. He then tells "Scoops"
to move out of the way as he walks towards the center of the ring. He then throws his hands in the air for a few seconds and fireworks from each of the ringposts set off, and the crowd cheers even louder. "Scoops" then whispers a few things in Lawrence's ear, and then Lawrence motions a "cut it" sign to the crowd and the crowd dies down...]
Scoops: Lawrence, you seem to be a sensation here in Chicago! Lawrence: Yes---- [The crowd is screaming so loud that Lawrence can't continue. He breaks a smile, but the crowd dies down after a little bit...]
Lawrence: Yes, I guess I am. I'll always have love for the windy city. This is the best city in the world!!!!
*Huge Pop...* Scoops: Well, atleast somebody likes you!! *A few boos emerge from the audience as Lawrence gives "Scoops" a weird look.* Lawrence: And just what does that mean........ Scoops: Well, it simply means that a few NACW wrestlers don't really like you, nor respect you!
*Lawrence looks shocked, and points to himself, and lips the words "Who, me?"...* Lawrence: Who, me? Scoops: Yes, you! Lawrence: Couldn't be. Crowd: Then, who? Lawrence: Scoops, stole the cookies from the cookie jar! Scoops: **Silent** *A few boos from the crowd erupt again..* Scoops: Can we pleeeeeease get serious here? Lawrence: Okay, okay. Yes, I know that "Breaker" Dane, and DT Richman don't like me. Mr. All-Serious, 1998!
Scoops: It's my job, Lawrence. Lawrence: I know. So, what if they don't like me. Scoops: Well, let's talk about your match with, Dane. [Lawrence steps back, rubs his chin, and shakes his head in disbelief...] Lawrence: Yeah, I remember that match. That match I had won. The match I should have won. The match where NACW Officials screwed me over. A match where that bimbo, Lianne stuck here nose into my business. **Hostile voice**
That match? Is that the match you're talking about, Scoops? Scoops: Yes, Lawrence. That match. How come you payed attention to Lianne? Lawrence: Well, a lot of Lawrence-haters have said that it is because I was mesmerized by the beauty of Lianne. What I would like to ask first to the crowd is, "Do you think Lianne looks beautiful?".
[Crowd boos...] Lawrence: Exactly my point. Some people say that she reminds them of Sunny. [Crowd laughs...] Lawrence: Well, in my book, she looks like, Cloudy!!!! [Crowd cheers and laughs at the same time...] Lawrence: I wasn't mesmerized by her beauty, I was nausiated by her....what's that ugly as hell woman on that movie Clash of The Titans?
[Scoops shakes his head...] Lawrence: You know, when you look at here your face turns to stone... Scoops: Madusa? Lawrence: Yeeeeeeah, that's here. I was nausiated by here Madusa complex!!! Scoops: **Vince McMahon voice** Aw, c'mon------ Lawrence: Shut up! I don't give a damn about what you think, say, or want. It's time to get nasty, and nasty is what I'm going to get. Scoops: What bit your neck? Lawrence: What? Scoops: What's wrong with you? Lawrence: What's wrong with me???? What's wrong with me??? Scoops: Yeah. Lawrence: Well, if you really want to know, even though it's none of your got damned business, I'm pissed. I'm pissed that this lousy jackass, Dane can get away with crap like that. I mean, it's like Jerry Springer. When the title of the show is "Stop sleeping with my man", you know damn well there's going to be a fight. So why don't they stop the fight before it happens? For got damned ratings!!! When Dane had the ugly as hell smile on his face, and was rubbing his forearm, what the hell were you thinking? Yeah, I'm talking to that Earl
Hebner want to be referee that was reffing my match! What were you think? Did you think he was going to go through the match-----
Scoops: Well, how come you didn't do anything?
[Lawrence gives him an evil grin...] Lawrence: Don't ever interrupt me,again, boy! Did you think he was going to go through the match without pulling something out of his sleeve? Literally???
What the hell are you doing? **SCREAMING** WHAT ARE YOU DOING??? I have enough of this. This is the 3rd fed in a row that I've been that has had this done to me. I thought when I came here that you guys weren't going to have this problem. Now it's bigger than ever. And to his Big O Stevie Wonder Boy, whatever the hell your name is. Don't you ever say anything like that again.
You understand me. Because I don't give a damn how big you are, I'll knock you the hell out!! I don't care if you like my style or you don't. I don't like you, and that's the way it's gotta be!
[pause...] [Lawrence grows impatient...] Lawrence: Well, ask a question, would ya? Scoops: Well, DT Richman made some comments the other day about you and Lianne. Can you respond?? Lawrence: Yeah, I'll respond. DT Richman, I didn't understand a damn word that you said in the beginning. Something about your opinion and Breaker showing me how to get it done? Well, if that's the way to get it done, then I don't want to know the way how NOT to get it done. Yeah, I'll blame Lianne. I'll insult here. But I'm insulting you wife. Get it? Not your beautiful wife. Because as I explained earlier, she ain't beautiful. I don't give a damn if it's wrestling, insulting your ugly as hell wife, or whooping the crap out of
Legion. I'll be you in up there, down here, over there, right here, in your home, in my home, in the sun, in the rain, in a blizzard, in the ice. Hell,
I'll whoop you right now. I don't give a damn about you or your wife, and you shouldn't make promises you don't keep. And give this message to Legion.
[Lawrence gets in front of the camera and starts yelling...] Lawrence: I'll mutter out two words that you can understand. Screw and you!!!! Well, you know I had to edit that a little bit!
[Now back to the center of the ring...]
