[FLASH] The Reaper [[detail withheld]]

13 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is one a my latest RP's and one of my best ever. Given credit for some help by TheBoeMan1. Don't ask about the [xXx] thing! Please!
[xXx] ... I have had my well earned break, and now it's time to go back to work. My wounds have healed fairly well, and my mind is back to where it belongs. After a DQ, loss to Outlaw, I now must head back to the ring, to face none other than the man they call Carnage. And on top of that, Xander Khan will be his tag team partner. Two of the greatest the NcWa has to offer. But their opponent... Midnight Killer and The Reaper. A very familiar tag team here in the NcWa. Who missed being the tag team champions by an inch. Is this a true return of one of the greatest tag teams ever? ... [xXx]
[...Fades in...] ((Scene)) [...The fire burns out, the cinders of the wood collapse under the weight of the carbon that is now left. The smoke now arises in place of the fire, being whisked away into the night sky. Taken away by the passing wind to be blown across the world, the distance not a matter of functionality, the smoke will take itself around the world. Until that time when the oxygen has taken the carbon from the smoke, it will no longer exist...]
[...The Reaper continues to sit in the chair, looking into the fireplace. Though no fire exists, The Reaper's concentration is now directed past the fireplace into a place that is unknown to others. A place that brings The
Reaper closer to his forefathers...]
[...Not thinking of anything but the matter at hand, The Reaper speaks short words to himself in the form of what his forefathers would have said to him if they were caught within the situation Trinity currently resides in...]
The Reaper: "One time, a long time ago, a very wise man once said that I''d rather die on my feet then live my life on my knees.' That man was none other then Emiliano
Zapata. People like you, Carnage and you Xander Khan, know very little about a man of such stature. That is because you are on your knees, dying a thousand deaths. Listening to you speak, I gained a new revelation, a new way of looking into my heart and my soul. Look at it, for it is blackened and scarred. Why does it appear this way? Because of all the pain that I have felt my entire life. Each time somebody says something negative about me, I take it to my heart. This is not a negative thing, it is a memory thing. I take it, and I reflect upon that sentiment. I take the knowledge that I have strove for and try to make sense of it, make it practical. But after that is said and done, I return to the darkness for which I have came from. Let me discuss this darkness for the time being. Darkness, in my usage does not mean that I come from hell. Or does it? What exactly is hell? I believe that I am able to make my own definition of hell, so maybe I actually am in hell? :::
Smirks ::: Who knows. For I do not. But my darkness comes from my own choice. For I could be the one who became like everyone else, joining the light that shines upon many individuals, but I have chosen not to."
[...The Reaper looks around the room, and a slight smirk can be seen. A tear drops from the corner of his eye, as his head lowers, and his thoughts continue...]
The Reaper: "Why? Because I would like to accomplish things my way. Maybe they will be more difficult, maybe they will be more time consuming, but I will accomplish those things. In my last brief interview, I touched upon some things that I would like to expand on a little more for this next match. The battle with my team as well as Carnage and Xander Khan. A warrior can be defined as a person engaged or experienced in warfare, a person who shows great vigor, courage and aggressive. These are the words that describe one man. A man that has journeyed from one end of the federation to the other and his quest stopping at every check point along the way. Claiming soul after soul, though he is not invincible he has fallen himself, yet he has gotten up and trudged forward.
Again stopping at all of his points of interest. Doing his business and moving on. There is now only one stop on this road to Ultimacy. That road being that of one match that will show a great deal of insanity on the part of this man.
Let's now see how this applies to myself, and also my forefathers, whom all follow within my path. You wonder about the experience of myself? Yes, I am experienced in the ranks of the warriors, but as for the life ranks? We are very experienced. We have also engaged in various forms of battle in many different federations. But unlike some people on the battlefield, we do not relish in the past, talking about our past battles. It is certain that we remember these battles, our losses, what knowledge we have gained, but we are not the type of people to just forget about them. We are going to remember them but we are not going to discuss them in order to bring up our egos. That is the difference between us being warriors and you, Carnage. And you Xander
Khan being warriors. Carnage, Xander Khan, we have you defeated before this match takes place."
[...The Reaper raises his head and stares into total damnation...] The Reaper: "You put too much relevance on the past when you need to be looking from your soul at the current path and the future of your ever so weak destiny. Do you understand? Probably not. My forefathers and I are very similar. We have both realized that we may not be the best. That we may not be the ones to be invincible, but yet, whenever we get knocked down, we keep a forward progress.
They use the phrase 'Still I Rise.' I believe in that sentiment as well. No matter what somebody tries to do to my soul, it will continue to move on.
Continue to fight through all of the unnecessary battles. Carnage, Xander
Khan, many times we have used the word 'war.' As mentioned earlier, let me redefine what a war truly is. A war is a conflict carried on by force of arms, as between nations or between parties within a nation. This is a topic that is well versed within the mind of this great man as one would call. Some of his peers have called him the man, at one point he was called unstoppable. Now over the months those names have faded away and the time is now to start and end a new war all in the same night. If victorious this man will go back into history and again once become what they had called him before, the man. Some already call him a legend for his past performances. He now comes from his training to bring forth his training and to fulfill his destiny. This is not a war between us. This is just one small step for a party of mankind. Carnage, you put way too much faith into this match, too much heart, and have too much riding on it. We on the other hand are going to take it as it comes. That is why we are different, and you have nothing to show for your meager attempt to dominate. My name has grown closer to the ranks of the champions, of the legends. I know, as my blackened soul has told me that I am not a legend yet.
My forefathers know that they are not legends, yet Carnage, you live your life as if you were a legend. That is not real. That is merely a mockery of your weak and lame existence. My heart is empty for people like you, you do not have anything that my soul wants. But I know that I am still going to scream when I wake up. Each day I enter my existence with a haunting pain, one that shall never go away. I am sure you are familiar with the Silence of the Lambs.
The absolute haunting that occurred then shall occur now. Prepare yourself,
Carnage and Xander Khan. You shall confess yourselves to my soul in due time.
. . [...The Reaper lays his head back down, as to face the floor...] The Reaper: "Carnage, you and I have done battle before, and on that day, you had come out on top. But as I have stated earlier, I know of my many mistakes, and my loses. But I take that with me, when I go to the ring. And it is revenge I am out for. Now true it is, you are one of the greatest of all time. But a legend? No. I will show you why you have not yet ranked their. On the other hand we have Xander Khan. One of the greatest as well. He has but a few steps left to go before he reaches the stature of legend. But those few steps will be the hardest, and they will be hurt filled. He may never reach them, but perhaps he will. This is a test to see how bad he wants it. But in the end, it will be myself taking one more step toward ultimacy. ::: long pause :::
Midnight Killer as well will take a step. A step closer to greatness. We together will show you, that it is just not that easy, and we will prove that much like us, you are beatable..."
[...The Reaper lays his eyes to rest...] [...Fades out...] [xXx] ... Do not forget all. I may seem calm and collective now. But forget not what I carry over my shoulder. The Insane title. Just because this is a tag team match, does not mean I will not wrestle that way. The insanity will be their. I guarantee it! ... [xXx]
