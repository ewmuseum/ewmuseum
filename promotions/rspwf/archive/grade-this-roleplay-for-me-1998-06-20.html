Grade this Roleplay For Me

20 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, Alright, this here roleplay is the first work of any sort related to e-wrestling that I have attempted since November of 1997. Therefore, I believe
I could have accumalated a great amount of roleplaying rust and I wish for some of you to judge this roleplay for me. Now, this is an introductory roleplay, therefore you will not see any flashy language, nor the heat that is present in the roleplays that are related to big fueds so please take that into account.
I am simply trying to get the points that are made clear in the roleplay across and I believe that I have been successful in doing just that. In judging it myself, I would say I did a pretty solid roleplay that began off strong but that weakened as it progressed which makes me give my own work a grade of a B+ at the moment. I am interested in all comments and _constructive_ criticism.
Thank you very much. ¤RP¤ [Inch by inch, the image of absolute blackness burned onto the screen begins to take on a more meaningful shape. The form of one, Lord Gunthar the Protector spills over the monitor, his body standing firmly _erect_ within the confines of room that is seemingly bare and barren. No furnishings of any kind can be spotted, all that surrounds him is emptiness. His body garbed simply in a gray hooded sweatshirt and torn bluejeans. A look of deep determination seems forever engraved upon his deadly features, that burning desire deeply evident within his eerie green orbs that make up his eyes, a slight twinkle that had been long gone from those eyes now returned emanating a myriad of little sparkles. A lone bead of sweat slithers its way down his pale covering, reaching the end of its journey lingering its drop to the floor as it comes to a halt at the base of his chin. The ends of his lips curl upwards into a devilish grin as he gazes confidently back into the camera, never blinking nor swaying even an inch, his long black strands of hair remaining totally still.
Finally showing signs of life, he takes a tiny little step backwards, his eyes shutting for a mere moment before popping open once more. His lips part as he utters his first on camera words in a long while.]
GUNTHAR: "So, looks like the spotlight shines upon me once again. Seemed like this day would never come. I remember a moment like this as if it were yesterday. A moment long ago where I first made my return to the sport of wrestling upon coming off of a long hiatus. Only, things were much different then..."
[His head slowly bobs up and down in the motion of a slight nod, letting out a deep sigh, he finally lifts his fingers up to brush away the little bead of sweat that seems glued to the base of his chin. Taking in a deep breath, the words flow freely from his mouth once more.]
GUNTHAR: "Oh yes, the situation seems much more grim on this occasion than it did in this past occurrence. For at that time, I was simply making my return after taking a break from this rigorous sport. The only problem I had was the factor that ring rust played in my in ring ability. How did I do? Pretty good
I think as I had a terrific short stint within the rings of the _IWF/WOW_.
Things were looking up for me and a bright future was to be expected. Oh God, how things would _change_. A motorcycle accident ended my rise to the top within the ranks of that organization, as I had badly damaged my right knee and was forced to leave the sport. It was the end of my career I was told, but I,
Lord Gunthar knew _better_."
[His greenish orbs seem to give off an eerie little glow as a look of pride sweeps across his face for just a moment.]
GUNTHAR: "No one and nothing can possibly keep me down for long and this is why I am here today. Standing before the ranks of a new up and coming organization in CoW, which in my mind has unlimited potential. You see, I have beaten the odds and I have now made it back to the top. Certainly it was hard work rehabilitating my injured body, but it was most definitely worth it and as you look upon this rock solid body, it has paid off in a big way. Now, before you start blabbering about this and that. I am well aware of the fact that this fed has some strong competition. And you may be asking, "Why has this man chosen to return to a high scale organization with both ring rust and a severe injury working against him?" Well, quite frankly. I have worked my butt off simply far too hard for me to accept anything else. Even in this supposedly weakened condition, I must shoot for the top for I believe it is what I deserve and what I've been working for my whole life. Spending time fooling around with some jabronies in a little rinky dink regional federation somewhere is just a big waste of time in my opinion. Oh no, that is not what I am all about. I am merely here to please the people who love and cherish this great sport, by giving them the best show possible within that squared circle.
Coming back to action in some bingo hall somewhere would certainly be a major travesty to all those loyal hordes of fans that have followed me through thick and thin. Everyone deserves a chance at seeing me in the ring, always giving
100% whether in victory or defeat."
[Some more droplets of sweat begin to form upon the warm flesh of his cheeks, tiny little shivers cascading down his body in never-ending anticipation for the moment where he shall finally be able to once again compete in front of the fans that have made him the man he is today.]
GUNTHAR: "I am a changed man. This is no longer about winning or losing to me, this is about one thing, _competition_. Proving to all the athletes that make up this federation and to all the people around the globe that shall see my battles, that I can still kick it with the best of them. However, talk does indeed mean nothing, and I had better let myself prove all of this where it should be proven, in the middle of the ring. I can feel it in my veins, the urge to make my in ring return is very strong right now. It has kept me up every night since the accident and I am sure it will continue to do that, until
I fulfill my goal. And that goal is stepping once more into the ring of this great federation. As I look through the great names that make up the CoW, I wonder just who it is that shall be the first to enter the ring with me in battle. Until then, my eyes shall be watching and studying while I dream of the night that I may once again walk that aisle... and return to my home.
[A flicker of excitement dances through his eyes for a split-second as he remains, staring intently into the camera with a sly grin still etched firmly into his remarkable features. He parts his lips slightly, sucking some air into the inner recesses of his mouth and then with one deep breath, the screen is once again black, his work having been done for the moment.]
