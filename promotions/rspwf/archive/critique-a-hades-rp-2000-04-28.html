Critique a Hades RP!

28 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys... I've never done this before, so I figure, what the hell? This is my roleplay going into the next IWF/WOW PPV where I'll be fighting for the World title.
This isn't my best roleplay ever by far, but it isn't nearly my worst either
:-) There's a lot of inside jokes that someone in the fed probably won't get, and I'm responding directly to my opponent, Razor Ron Jeremy's roleplay, so some of it is hard to understand without seeing that one. But, do your best anyhow :-) I'll give you a little background on the angle. Ron Jeremy is a
Mankind/Al Snow type character. He writes comedy type roleplays, and Hades is kind of playing the "straight man" in this angle who thinks that Jeremy is a mockery to the title and wrestling in general. And if anyone is wondering who
Bootie is, that's Razor Ron's sidekick, a boot that he talks to (ala Mr. Socko or Head) :-) And Mike Marconi and Dark Bishop are members of my stable.
There, that's enough background! Witness the long-windedness of Hades! :-) Enjoy and let me know your comments, criticisms, concerns, hopes, fears...whatever :-)
=========================================================== [The scene opens to a dressing room somewhere in the IWF/WOW HQ. Hades is seen in the corner of the room standing in front of a mirror, getting ready for his press conference which is about to begin. He is wearing black boots, blue jeans, and a black "End of the World" t-shirt with the arms cut off. The camera zooms in as Hades musses with his hair. To Hades's right is a small television which is currently airing Razor Ron Jeremy's latest promo. Hades's eyes occasionally avert to the television to listen to it. A disgusted look covers his face each time he turns his head to the television. Hades looks back in the mirror and then gently touches the huge bruise on his forehead which is in the shape of boot tread. He gives an angry scowl and notices the cameraman's reflection in the mirror filming him. He gets an even angrier scowl on his face as he turns around to the camera.]
HADES: My press conference isn't for another 5 minutes! Don't ever start that damn camera again until I tell you to! We start my promos when _I_ give the word! I'm the star here, you insignificant nothing! You got it?
[The cameraman obviously nodded his head 'yes' off camera as a satisfied look crosses Hades's face. Hades walks over to a bench in the center of the room, takes a seat, and watches the Razor Ron Jeremy promo for a few minutes.]
Television: "SQUIRREL!!! GET OVER HERE YOU LITTLE RASCAL!!" [A look of utter disgust crosses Hades's face as he watches Razor Ron Jeremy and Bootie running their bumper car over the Cuban woman's wig. Hades shakes his head in a look that could best be described as "a sickened disbelief".
Hades grabs the remote control and aims it at the television.] Television: "RRJ, that wasn't a squirrel, it was Lu....." [The television shuts off. Hades stands and looks at the cameraman. He is about to speak, but then stops and gives a confused look as if contemplating what he just saw and the words are escaping him. He then looks back up at the camera with the same disgusted look, and again bites his tongue, looking frustrated as he grasps for the words to express his disgust. He looks up one last time...]
HADES: That was more painful to watch than a Steve West match! What in the hell IS this crap? Bumper cars? Carnival games? BALD CUBAN WOMEN??? Do you need any MORE proof that this federation is turning into a damn circus?? I mean, what in the hell does this have to do with wrestling? It makes me......
*KNOCK KNOCK KNOCK* [Hades's dressing room door opens up. Dark Bishop and Mike Marconi stroll through the door. Both are wearing HOA shirts.]
(V)arconi: What's up, Hades? You about ready? HADES: Yeah, I was just taking another look at Ron Jeremy's latest degradation of the World title....
BISHOP: Yeah, I saw that. It's amazing what kind of crap they put on television these days, isn't it?
HADES: It's amazing what crap gets a PUSH around here! That's what's amazing! Two unbridled talents like you two guys are forced into fighting Cooper and
"SuperFreak" Rick James on the PPV when complete fools like RRJ are pushed to the World title!
(V)arconi: Yeah, maybe I should give my elbowpads a gimmick and start talking to them. HADES: Mike, that's not even funny! (V)arconi: Relax! I was only joking! BISHOP: Anyways, Hades, we're supposed to tell you that they're ready for you out there. HADES: *sigh*, allright....let's get this over with. Damn Ishikawa making me plug this PPV. I hate press conferences!
[Hades continues mumbling to himself as he exits the room. Dark Bishop and
Marconi remain behind in the dressing room. The cameraman follows close behind
Hades. He follows Hades as he makes his way down a long hallway and into a huge conference room. Various reporters and cameramen are seen scattered around the room. The flashbulbs immediately begin going off as Hades enters the room and approaches the podium at the front of the room. The cameraman takes his place with the rest of the media. Various reporters are heard shouting questions at Hades. Hades takes a deep sigh before yelling into the mic.]
HADES: SHUT THE HELL UP!! [The room immediately dies down.] HADES: This is going to be like kindergarten, kids. We're going to have a few simple rules. You raise your hand, I call on you. Simple as that. Got it?
Good. Allright...... [Hades begins looking around at the room. Various reporters have their hands raised. He spots one.]
HADES: Allright, you, with the mustache....what's your question, ma'am? [An older Italian woman slowly rises from her seat.] Reporter 1: Mr. Hades, I was wondering, with all the commotion surrounding the title match and Razor Ron Jeremy, do you feel that you've been neglecting the other athletes in this league, such as the newly formed Team 3:B??
[Hades gets a dumbfounded look on his face.] HADES: That's your question?? I'm going into a World title match on the 1st and you want to ask me about SuperFreak and the boys? Let me get something straight. The fact that I have a World title match is not the reason I am neglecting them. World title match or no World title match, I will NEVER give those losers more than a fraction of my attention. Because franklly, they're just not worth it! Mike Marconi and Dark Bishop, while they should not be subjected to such sub-par competition, WILL be fighting the so-called "Boston
Bad Boys" at End of the World. So, as you can see, they're not totally being neglected. I got people on it...and they'll be more than enough to finish off the "Rick James-Village People 2000 Tour" come PPV time... Now if you want to talk about those nothings, Mike and Bish are in my dressing room, so feel free to head on over there after this and I'm sure they'll have more than a few words for you. Next question....
[Hades points to an older, gray-haired man.] Reporter 2: Hades, exactly how far do you plan to go to get that World title back? It seems to me that you will stoop to any level. If the "pinning" of opponents wasn't enough, you attacked an IWF/WOW interviewer a few weeks back just for the sake of getting a shot at that belt. When will this end?
HADES: You keep getting a derogatory tone with me, and I'll continue my attack of interviewer/reporter's right now! Anthony Edwards got what he had coming to him. I knew the man is nothing short of a friend of Ron Jeremy's. You think I didn't see that? But I let that slide because I didn't mind Tony to tell you the truth. I was under the false impression that he was a truly objective reporter. The mistake he made was showing me his cards and proving just exactly WHOSE side he was on when he was interviewing me two weeks ago. He's nothing but a Ron Jeremy ass-kisser, and he's promoting that FILTH that Jeremy puts out on the air-waves! I won't stand for it. Anyone that promotes a mockery of this sport is going to get the same treatment that Jeremy is going to get come PPV-time!
HADES: And you ask how far will I go to get my title back? I will go to ANY lengths! ANY....to get that title back around my waist. I was held down in this federation for years. Every person along the way told me that "you'll never make it, Hades, you'll never be big in the IWF/WOW. This is the big leagues." And for the longest time, I didn't. But throughout that time, I did one thing constantly. And that was BUST MY ASS for this company! And I did what they said couldn't be done. I made it to the top, I made it to the World title, and I made the I/W great in my time on top! And do you think that once
I've gotten to the top of the IWF/WOW...after I've busted my ass for THREE
YEARS to make this one of the top promotions in the world and make me the most recognizable face in it. Do you think that after all of that, that I'm going to let some 2-bit joke of a wrestler like Razor Ron Jeremy tarnish everything and anything that I ever worked for by turning this federation into a damn fun-house?? I don't think so! The fact that he is holding a belt that _I_ once held puts a blemish on MY name! So, to answer your question again....I will do absolutely anything to get that title back around a deserving
WRESTLER'S waist! And off of a circus act... Next question.... [A young, blonde-haired girl stands up.] Reporter 3: Mr. Hades, in your last promo on last week's Battlecentre, you had a few disparaging words for Mr. Steve West. I'm wondering, where do.......
HADES (interrupting): SHUT UP!! I'm not wasting my interview time on Steve
West questions!! And just for everyone here's future reference, if any of you have any more useless, waste-of-my-time questions about Steve West, Cooper
Concrete, Hightower, or any of the other washed up has-beens of this federation.....do yourself a favor and save it! I'm here to talk about EotW, not people who are lucky to be employed. I'm headlining the card, people, not opening it! Next question...
[A middle-aged, dark haired man gets to his feet.] Reporter 4: Mr. Hades, your criticisms against Ron Jeremy's title reign are well documented. However, you say that he in no way brings anything substantially positive to IWF/WOW programming. I must disagree with you, Razor
Ron Jeremy has a very large fan base.
[Hades rolls his eyes.] HADES: Yeah? And Corky on "Life Goes On" was a big star too. Does that make him any less a retard? That 1-800-COLLECT guy probably has a lot of fans.
Does that mean we should put a World title on him too? The fact is, if I want to see mongoloids like those two or Ron Jeremy run around and droll on themselves, I'd go over to the Neary Center for Exceptional People and laugh at their pathetic lives there. I wouldn't turn on my Monday night wrestling program and expect to see crap like that there! That's insane! And if that's what "wrestling" has come to, maybe I SHOULD find a new profession! The problem is, I refuse to let that happen to my sport whether these fans like it or not. Let's face it, the majority of fans are in-bred morons anyhow. And
Ron Jeremy as flag-bearer probably doesn't help matters much. If we upset and lose some of those idiot fans by getting that belt off of Jeremy, what have we really lost? Nothing worth hanging on to! There'll be more fans and better fans watching our program when _I_ win that belt! This sport won't be a complete joke any longer... Next question.
[Hades points at a younger man who stands up with a cocky, almost mocking smirk on his face.]
Reporter 5: How's the head?? [Hades gives an angered look as he slowly rubs the bruise on his head that he received from Bootie on Monday. Hades's face turns red for a few moments and he looks as if he's about to explode. But then a smile suddenly crosses
Hades's face.] HADES: The head? The head's pretty good, boy. You must have taught your mother well. Next question!
[The reporter's smirk quickly turns into an embarrassed half-smile. Hades now has a proud smile on his face.]
HADES: You got something else to ask, smart-ass? No? Good, have a seat now and don't even think of opening your mouth and making more of a fool of yourself. And if you really want to know about my little bruise here, it's nothing. But I'll be happy to give your homo-looking ass one just like it, you arrogant little prick. Now sit the f*ck down!
[The reporter's face turns red and he slowly sinks back into his seat. Hades points at another reporter.]
Reporter 6: Mr. Hades, I first must say that I am very excited about the
"Battle of the Gods" coming up on the....
HADES (interrupting): Whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa, whoa......stop right there! You know, I know Ronnie-boy there somewhere along the line dubbed this match the
"Battle of the Gods" to get himself some free publicity and to push his own worth higher than it should be, but please.....I don't want the media putting this loser on the same level as me as well! The only person that deserves the title of "god" in this match is yours truly, the God of Wrestling. Normally,
I'd say that Ron there could take the title of the God of Suck which DOES fit him, but I'm afraid your colleague there already has that title among the boys down by the docks.
[Hades points back at the reporter who made the "how's the head?" comment from before. The reporter slumps lower in his seat out of embarrassment.]
HADES: That term "God" has been thrown around a little bit too loosely for my tastes the last few months or so. Ron Jeremy, I'm afraid, is far from that type of distinction!
Reporter 6: All of that aside.....and I'm not asking this in a mocking fashion like my colleague by any means, but in addition to the shot to the head from
Bootie that you took on Monday, you also took a Jack Daniels bottle to the back of your skull as well. Do you think this will affect your match in any way?
HADES: I'm still standing here breathing. That's mistake number one that RRJ made. All that those two shots served to do is give me two more reasons to knock Jeremy's teeth down his throat for him. It pissed me off, yeah. Is that a good thing? Not for Ron. Whatever doesn't kill me makes me stronger, and
I'M far from dead. Ron Jeremy, he might only be about 2 days away...
[Hades gives a sadistic grin.] HADES: I'm not taking any more questions, but I'll give you a few closing comments. I'm not going to lie to you, the End of the World match isn't going to be pretty. It won't be a classic wrestling match for two reasons. The first reason is that Ron Jeremy can't wrestle his way out of a wet paper bag, and even _I_ am not good enough to carry that waste onto a good match. And the second reason is that I don't want to "wrestle" Ron Jeremy. I want to BEAT him to a bloody pulp. He represents everything that is wrong about this sport. He represents everything I hate. I hate absurdity and stupidity. And stupidity is Ron and Ron is stupidity! What can I say? He's already sat atop this fed for much longer than he has deserved to. He's already made my work that much harder to bring this fed back into some form of credibility. At End of the
World, MY Pay-Per-View, it's time to set things right. It's time to bring some.....sanity back into the IWF/WOW. It's time to put an end to the most ridiculous few months that this fed has ever seen. It's time for you, Ron, to be pushed back down to the depths of this federation where you've always belonged. Because, it's time for Hades, the God of Wrestling, to once again undisputedly RULE the IWF/WOW! And no matter what you do, no matter how hard you resist, Ron......you can't stop it! You can only delay the inevitable.
Because at End of the World, I will, for the second year in a row on that
PPV......become the IWF/WOW World Heavyweight Champion!
[Hades gets a serious, determined look in his eye as he stares directly into the camera.] HADES: The fun's over, Ron. Because the Hades era begins anew on Monday whether you like it or not! And you, you're just the bitch that's getting run over in the process. Now this press conference is over......
[Hades storms off stage with a fire in his eye as various reporters begins yelling Hades's name trying to get one last question in. The screen slowly fades to black....]
