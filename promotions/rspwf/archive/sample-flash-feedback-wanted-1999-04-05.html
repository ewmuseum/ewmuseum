[Sample Flash] Feedback Wanted

5 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm posting this roleplay just to get some friendly advice. This RP was for a 3 way dance in ICW with Sex and Love (me) against the Island Rats and Motor City
Maniacs. Give me some feedback. Thanks
[The scene opens up inside of a hallway in a hotel in Tampa, Florida. It is about 2:00 A.M. Friday Night Fallout ended about four hours ago. The hallway lights are on, and at the other end of the hallway, you see some sort of object. The cameraman begins walking towards it. As you get closer, you notice that it is a tent. A Fisher Price tent. It is zipped up, but you see a flashlight is on inside. The cameraman gets closer, and unzips the tent. He looks inside, and you see none other than Tony Richards sitting there, in his pajamas, reading the latest ICW magazine. The cameraman coughs, and Tony turns around. He is startled at first, then gets his wits.]
"HB" TONY R: [whispering] SHHHHHHHHH! Cameraman: What in God's name are you doing? "HB" TONY R: [whispering] SHHHHH! I'm camping outside of Kennedy's room! She'll be so happy when she wakes up and sees me.
Cameraman: I'm not so sure about that. "HB" TONY R: [whispering] You heard her, she told me she loved me. Why can't anyone understand that me and Kennedy are in LOVE!
Cameraman: We know, we know. But what we really would like to know is what you and Doug's plan is to beat the Motor City Maniacs and Island Rats on Friday.
They are both very good teams. "HB" TONY R: [whispering] Dude, you're too loud. Keep it down. Well, I'll agree that the Island Rats are a good team. They beat us. It was a fluke, but they beat us. The Maniacs though, well, I wouldn't quite say good. The Maniacs aren't the greatest team in the world. Honestly, if they wrestled Small
Package, I'd have my money on Small Package. The Island Rats though, boy,
T'Chamunga is a big boy!
Cameraman: The Motor City Maniacs almost beat the Perfectly Perfect Duo! How are they not good?
"HB" TONY R: [whispering] Man, be quiet, you're gonna' wake Kennedy up. Now, the Duo had a bad day the last time they faced the Maniacs. That's all there is to it. The Maniacs can't beat us. We're what ... 4-0 against 'em? I think that's right.
Cameraman: You've never wrestled the Mani... "HB" TONY R: [whispering] Ya' know, I was thinking the other day, if me and
Kennedy had kids, they'd be the most beautiful kids alive.
Cameraman: I think you have Attention Deficit Disorder. You can't keep your mind one thing for longer than five minutes.
"HB" TONY R: [whispering] Dude, I'm hungry. Let's go to the lobby and get some snackies! [The cameraman gets out of the tent, and you see Tony crawl out. They walk down the hallway and stop at the elevator. Tony pushes the down button, and the elevator opens with a ding. They walk in and proceed down to the lobby. They walk out of the elevator, and turn right. As they do, you Doug Richards walking in with a girl on his arm.]
"LLM" DOUG R: TONY! What are you doin' in your pajamas? "HB" TONY R: I'm campin' man! "LLM" DOUG R: Camping? Where? "HB" TONY R: Outside of Kennedy's room! "LLM" DOUG R: [sighs] You're a freak, ya' know that? "HB" TONY R: Doug, you heard her say it. She said she loves me! Why can't you support me? "LLM" DOUG R: [sighs] Tony, can you please, please concentrate on the Island
Rats and Motor City Maniacs?
"HB" TONY R: I always concentrate on our matches. I concentrated on the Rats last week. "LLM" DOUG R: WHAT?! You're the whole reason we lost. You couldn't keep your eyes off of Kennedy, so I got my brains bashed in. Now this week, you must keep up with your "NO Kennedy Diet." You cannot see Kennedy in any way, shape, or form.
"HB" TONY R: [whining] Dougggggggggg! "LLM" DOUG R: Tony, we need this win Friday night. This is our way to the Duo. You want that right? "HB" TONY R: Yes... "LLM" DOUG R: LOUDER! "HB" TONY R: YEs... "LLM" DOUG R: LOUDER! "HB" TONY R: YES... "LLM: DOUG R: LOOOOOOUDER! "HB" TONY R: YESSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSSS! I WANT THIS MATCH!!!!!! I WANT THE DUO! .......... [sad] I want Kennedy. [Doug walks over to Tony and slaps him in the back of the head.] "LLM" DOUG R: After we beat these two teams, and then after we beat the Duo, you can think of Kennedy all ya' want. As long as you concentrate on our matches. Ok?
"HB" TONY R: I can do that.... I CAN DO THAT! "LLM" DOUG R: Now, what are we gonna' do to the Rats? "HB" TONY R: WE'RE GONNA' KILL 'EM! "LLM" DOUG R: Um ... well ... no, we're gonna' pin 'em. "HB" TONY R: WE'RE GONNA' KILL 'EM! "LLM" DOUG R: Ok, enough Ton... "HB" TONY R: WE'REEEEEEEEEE GONNNNNNNNA' KILLLLLLLL 'EMMMMMMM! "LLM" DOUG R: Ok, settle down. TONY! What about the Maniacs? "HB" TONY R: WE'REEEEEEE GONNNNA MURDER 'EMMMMMMMM! "LLM" DOUG R: Ok, well, man, you're scarin' me! [Doug shakes Tony.] "LLM" DOUG R: Settle down. Maniacs, Rats, let me go ahead and tell ya'. Tony and I are the hottest tag team in the world right now. We get phone calls, we get faxes, we get everything. And unlike the Duo, our mail is about wrestling.
Not being like the Champ! Wrigley, if I see you, I'll send you to hell and back. But this Friday night, every single ICW fan, promoter, wrestler, staff member, and manager will be wanting my...._DOUGGIESTYLE_!!!!
"HB" TONY R: WE'RRRRRRRE GONNNNNNA' SLICE 'EM IN LITTLE PIECES AND PUT THEM IN OUR TRUNK! "LLM" DOUG R: NO! WE'RE THE INNOVATORS OF HARDCORE ....... SEX!!!
