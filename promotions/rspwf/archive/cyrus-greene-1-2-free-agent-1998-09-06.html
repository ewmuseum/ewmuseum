[CYRUS GREENE] 1/2* Free Agent

6 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, just dropped a couple feds, so I have room for one more. For all f those who don't know, Cyrus is a cruiserweight, blah blah blah, and here is a spiffy little sample roleplay for the all of you.
[ The scene opens up, Cyrus Greene and a young lady are talking to each other, inside a school hallway. Cyrus Greene is wearing one of those D.A.R.E shirts, and some mirror-tinted shades. The lady looks to be in her twenties, and is wearing a shiny neon green dress. That alone can tell you she is the teacher of a first grade class. She is standing outside her classroom door talking to
Cyrus. ]
Teacher: Okay, so you're going to come in here, and talk to the kids about all the stuff I read in that handbook? Like how drugs kill brain cells, and the whole deal?
Cyrus: Oh yeah, just let me get a crack at the little insignificant imps. I'll scare them so bad they'll run home and get their crack-head moms to stop. [ The teacher, raising a brow, questions herself in her mind if Cyrus even knows what he is doing when it comes to talking kids out of drugs. ]
Teacher: Cyrus, do you even know anything about kids and drugs? [ Cyrus snaps back quickly. ] Cyrus: Listen here, I got this shirt (Points to DARE shirt) cause I was trained by those fools involved with the DARE program to spread my knowledge to the willing ears. As the greatest pro wrestler to ever liv...
[ The teacher cuts him off. ] Teacher: Oh! So you're a professional wrestler! I know a lot of my students are wrestling fans, they might know you!
Cyrus: Trust me teach, if they are wrestling fans, they know me. I'm the biggest thing to hit the sport since… since the sport started.
Teacher: Well that is great! [ The teach walks back into her classroom to prepare the kids, and give them a basic intro to drugs. ]
Teacher: (From inside the classroom) And here to teach us more about drugs and the HORRABLE things they can do to your body, he is a wrestler, Cyrus Greene!
[ Cyrus walks into the room, expecting a standing ovation. All he sees is the dropping jaws of about twenty five kids, cause they realize that the man who attacked a twelve year old girl on national television, and a man who has been accused of being prejudice against ALL ethnic groups and minorities, will be teaching them about drugs. ]
Teacher: Okay, well, I'm going to leave you guys alone now, I got some stuff I need to do in… other rooms. Play nice!
[ The kids still can't believe that Cyrus Greene is their drug teacher. ] Cyrus: So…what am I supposed to talk about. [ A small girl sitting up in the very front replies. ] Girl: (Very nasal sounding) Your supposed to be talking about dru… Cyrus: Screw that, we are going to talk about me and my matches in the HHWO. Now, who here is a wrestling fan? [ Not a single kid raises their hand. They are still starring in shock. ] Cyrus: So, I guess you are all a bunch of IDIOTS are you? Okay then, for those of you that don't know, I rule, and I kicked the ass of some [Beep]er named Frazer Fury. I kicked his ass hard to. Want to se it? I knew you did!
I got a tape of it! [ Cyrus reaches somewhere and pulls a tape out. ] [ An isolated voice from the pack of kids blurts out "YOU SUCK!" ] Cyrus: Which one of you snobby little crackheads said that? [ No one responds. ] Cyrus: You guys are about as smart as your skank ass moms. I think all you kids are just a bunch of future whores and sweat shop workers, none of you little dweebs will ever amount to anything.
You know what? I think it would be better if you all got on drugs, then you would be too stoned to get hired by Burger King, so I wouldn't have to listen to your god damned voices asking me if I would like fries with my order. You all can GO TO HELL.
[ Cyrus doesn't realize it, but he is just about screaming now. The teacher is standing at the doorway too. ]
Cyrus: You all got the intelligence of a damn toilet! YOU THINK I SUCK?! YOU
THINK I [Beep]ing SUCK?!
[ Cyrus is now throwing a fit, and he starts throwing crap at the chalkboard. The teacher decides to interrupt right before Cyrus throws a meter stick at the kids. ] Teacher: (Furious) YOU GET OUT OF MY CLASSROOM NOW YOU PSYCHO! GET OUT! Cyrus: Shut up! Teacher: GET OUT!!! Cyrus: Geez... [Cyrus starts to leave just as the camera fades back to the shot of all the commentators sitting at the table in the HHWO Television Studios. Lance looks disgusted as he begins to speak...]
