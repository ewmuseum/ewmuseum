[FREE AGENT #1] Smith & Wesson

23 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: I'm looking for an active fed. Preferably closed roleplay, but if it is open roleplay I don't want a board. Weekly cards would rule. Doesn't even have to be an "established" fed. Any one will do. But only one for this team. Here's the app. I'll take a look at any fed that writes me. Write me at [detail withheld] and if you need to see proof of my work... uh... write me and I'll give you some old roleplays I've done. Thanks to G/M for the app. I really like the format. I've adapted it a bit, but it's an excellent app. APPLICATION ----------- SECTION ONE - HANDLER INFORMATION Your Name: [name withheld] Your eMail Address: [detail withheld] SECTION TWO - TAG TEAM INFORMATION Tag Team Name: Smith & Wesson Tag Team Members: Widowmaker Smith & Buzzsaw Wesson Tag Team Finisher: Double Shot (One man hits a running lariat on an opponent, then lifts the opponent and throws him into his partner, who hits a final lariat) Tag Team Moves: 1. Double backbreaker 2. Double team boots and punches 3. Double boot to the face 4. Double elbowdrop Tag Team Gimmick/Psychology: Smith & Wesson are big, drunk hicks. They like to fight, and they will fight anyone, anytime. They only want titles if it will bring them money to buy a new ranch. Wesson is the more experienced wrestler, and therefore has more moves. Smith has basic moves only, because he's a novice. Tag Team Entrance: # They judge a boy by his friends # # And all of mine are trash # RA: Ladies and gentlemen... entering the ring now, hailing from the Straight Shot Gulch and weighing in at a combined weight of 596 pounds... here are... .. WIDOWMAKER SMITH AND BUZZSAW WESSON... [A tall, bulky man in a baseball cap, vest, and blue jeans appears behind the curtain... one fist raised lazily in the air. The vest reads B.W. on it. Behind him steps an unshaven man with long, ponytailed hair, blue jeans, and a vest reading W.S.] RA: ... SMITH AND WESSON!!! [Buzzsaw Wesson rolls his head and stretches his arms as he walks to the ring with a cool, almost lackadaisical swagger. He sighs a bit as he reaches ringside, his baseball cap reading "Marty Lunde's Bar-B-Que" clearly visible. Widowmaker Smith cracks his knuckles as he stalks to the ring. He reaches ringside and gives a dirty look to the crowd.] # Cheap... thrills # # Don't you know I like # # Cheap... thrills # # Night after night # # Cheap... thrills # # Well if you ain't supposed ta do it I will # [Wesson steps onto the ring apron and between the ropes, where he stands in his corner stretching, waiting for the action to start, looking uninterested and even a bit lost. Smith just glares down the aisle, waiting for the action to start as he cracks his knuckles.] SECTION THREE - WRESTLER INFORMATION WIDOWMAKER SMITH Wrestler Name: John "Widowmaker" Smith (never uses "John", only on official forms) Gender: Male Hometown: Straight Shot Gulch, Texas Height: 6'10 Weight: 299 Birthdate (MM-DD-YYYY): 12/25/71 Alignment (Face/Neutral/Heel): Heel Gimmick (detailed): A cold-blooded killer. And drunk. He likes to get into fights, and not only likes to fight... but to destroy. Not a lighthearted drunk at all. Not a comically mean drunk. Just cruel. Theme Music (Song and Artist): "Cheap Thrills" by David Allen Coe Physical Appearance: Skin tone - Caucasian. Head - Smith has a long, dark ponytail. His eyes are a hardened dark brown, almost black. He has sharp facial features, and a flattened nose. Torso - Bulky. Thick and scarred. Tattoos all over. Legs - Extremely thick and strong. Ring Attire: Black boots, black leather vest with "W.S." on it in white. Blue jeans. Three Strengths: 1. Tough as shit - period 2. Brutal - Yep 3. Vicious - Ditto Three Weaknesses: 1. Lack of ring knowledge - Doesn't know, or care to know, wrestling holds 2. Viciousness - Gets him DQ'd sometimes 3. Slow - yep SECTION FOUR - MOVE INFORMATION Primary Wrestling Style: Brawler Secondary Wrestling Style: Weapon user Move List (10-25 Moves): 1. Punches 2. Kicks 3. Elbowdrop 4. Choke on the ropes 5. Choke hold 6. Press slam 7. Choke with the boot 8. Neck wrench 9. Chinlock 10. Weapon use Signature Moves (3-5 Moves): 1. Big Boot 2. Diving headbutt 3. Choke slam Setup Move and Description: None needed, comes out of nowhere Finisher Name: Lariat Finisher Description: A tremendous, devastating lariat SECTION FIVE - BACKGROUND AND SAMPLE ROLEPLAY Background/History: Widowmaker Smith has been seen fighting in bars for better than 15 years, since he was 15 years old. The guy's just a fighter who was found by George Wesson and picked to fight in this tag team. He just likes the idea of getting paid to fight and one day wants to not have to work for a living, buy a ranch, and sit on his ass forever. SECTION THREE - WRESTLER INFORMATION BUZZSAW WESSON Wrestler Name: George "Buzzsaw" Wesson (never uses "George", only on official forms. Real last name is Stevens, btw...) Gender: Male Hometown: Straight Shot Gulch, Texas Height: 6'8 Weight: 297 Birthdate (MM-DD-YYYY): 12/25/75 Alignment (Face/Neutral/Heel): Heel Gimmick (detailed): A laid-back, smooth talking man. He offers some comedic adventures and doesn't even know it. Theme Music (Song and Artist): "Cheap Thrills" by David Allan Coe Physical Appearance: Skin tone - Caucasian. Needs time in the sun, frankly. Head - Wesson has short, cropped light brown hair. His eyes are a soft, somewhat glazed, blue. He has a fu-manchu mustache and very defined facial features. Torso - Bulky. Not much muscle to be seen, but he's not fat either. A couple scars on his arms and stomach. Legs - Extremely thick and strong. Ring Attire: Black leather vest, blue jeans, black cowboy boots with spurs, and a baseball cap (with various teams or messages on it, depending on the week. Usually throw in insider references for e-w marks to spooge over on those hats). Three Strengths: 1. Toughness 2. Courage 3. Uncaring attitude Three Weaknesses: 1. Slow 2. Courage gets him in some tough situations 3. Years of wear and tear from bar fights SECTION FOUR - MOVE INFORMATION Primary Wrestling Style: Brawler Secondary Wrestling Style: Tough, Japanese Strong Style Move List (10-25 Moves): 1. Boston Crab 2. Piledriver 3. Stomping a mudhole in someone's ass in the corner 4. Superplex 5. Standard Backbreaker 6. Running Bulldog 7. Swinging Full Nelson (meaning that he lifts the opponent's feet onto the ropes and hangs on to the full nelson) 8. Low Blow 9. Stiff Openhanded slaps to the chest 10. Front Layout Suplex 11. Hammerlock Slam 12. Russian Leg Sweep 13. Belly to Back Suplex 14. Running Shoulderbreaker Signature Moves (3-5 Moves): 1. Rocky Gulch Bomb (snow plow) 2. STIFF right cross - his signature punch. He usually shakes his fist after hitting it, as if he's trying to get feeling in his hand 3. Hard Stuff - Leaping Leghook DDT 4. Thunderbird Bomb - Release Crucifix Powerbomb 5. Down In Rocky Gulch - Lifting Full Nelson Faceslam Setup Move and Description: None needed Finisher Name: Lariat Finisher Description: Lariat SECTION FIVE - BACKGROUND AND SAMPLE ROLEPLAY Background/History: George Wesson is a graduate of Bulldogg University, the school that Nick Demola has trained wrestlers such as Anti-Social Sublife, The Kings of Hardcore, and others. Immediately after leaving the school, at age 25, Stevens joined a puroresu fed in Japan. He stayed there for nine months, getting tired of sushi and returning to the states. But not before earning a reputation as a stiff worker and a man the crowds clamor to see... even if he can be something of a jerk. He moved on to AVW for a very brief stint, making his presence felt at the AVW mid-year awards and on a pay per view, before the apparent shutdown of the fed led to his departure. He's since been hanging out in his tiny shack in Straight Shot Gulch, located in Texas. Nobody really knows what he's been up to... except for drinking. Everyone knows he's been drinking. That's a given.
