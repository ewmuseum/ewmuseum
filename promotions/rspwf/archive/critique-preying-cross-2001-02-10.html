[CRITIQUE] Preying..... (CROSS)

10 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
lemme know what ya'll think, my character's name is cross, he's dark, evil satanic -- you know, the regular.. lol i'm not a "lengthy" roleplayer.. i don't take too long on intros and whatnot.. i'm straight, to the point, yet effective (or @ least i liek ot think so).. lol anyways, i wanna know what u RSPWF'ers think of me..
don't sugar coat anything for me.. -- spit the real.. .thanx ~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* ? [Silence..] [CREEEEEEEAAAKK] [The loud, startling, sudden creak of wood echoes throughout the entire viscinity of the abandoned 16th century chapel, which much like, Cross has endured eveyrthing from fires to floods, and remains standing, in one, peice..]
[The camera pans around the mass area..] [Weaving in and out of the half-decayed pews, the camera finds our "hero" in the first row, off to the right, kneeling in the praying position..]
[Cross]: "Oh naiive one.." "I pray for you -- the one they call Arson.." "I pray for the man that has convinced himself to believe that he is better than me, smarter than me.."
[Pause.] "I pray for the man that thinks somewhere in his mind that he can actually walk out of this match..."
[He lets the words sink in..] "..for he won't. Only one can survive. Only one can be the true champion. Only one can prevail. Firebomb will not win, and neither will you." "Prepare.." [Long, deep silence.] [Cross scoffs..] "Arson; Firebomb; Azrael/Lord Demous; Hellion --- the list goes on of spineless posers that act as though they they know of the fiery depths of hell; the hell that I'm destined to rule.. "
"You know nothing gentlemen, and never will.." [Long, deep silence.] [Cross opens his mouth and the portentous, almost unspecifiable significance in his tone envelopes his words.]
"...I warned you child tomorow night I feast on your soul. You spoke earlier on how you were such a strong man and how you fear nothing.. It does not matter though child, what I have in store for you will change your mind."
[In one unified slowly motion he unpresses his hands, lifts and turns his head. He then begins advancing towards the aisle, where the camera is..]
[His voice grows dark, and goes back to it's level of low dryness..] "..... When I hold your limp body in front of your peers as an example of what is to come, pray that I take mercy on your soul, although... I will not listen -- for you shall suffer for all of your kind."
"..You will be crucified, your blood shall rain upon your peers as warning to their coming doom. Do you think I honestly care about you child? You are but a chew toy for me; nothing more than a blood pet brought here for my pleasure. You and this fool named Firebomb.."
"..the words you two spoke at your interview are nothing but fuel to the fire your inane wordings, and thoughtless banter -- for will only bring your destruction that much faster."
"Do you see?" "Do you hear?" "Do you understand children -- you will only die as a martyr? Your sniveling and crying will serve you only the loss of face with your peers. They know though that they are next. After I have completely drained you, and as you float aimlessly down the river Styxx remember these words that I have spoken for the only thing that you will learn tomorrow night is the sound of your lungs filling with fluid as you gasp for your last breath..."
[With his arms outstretched and head slightly cocked to the side, mocking the prophet Jesus Christ.]
"Embrace the Darkness Children................." [The now-familiar demoniacal grin escaspes his lips, sending chills down the spines of the millions viewing....]
"....... for 'tis only then will you be free .. " [Slowly fade to black..] ?
