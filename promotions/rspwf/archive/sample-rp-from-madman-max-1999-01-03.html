Sample RP from "Madman" Max

3 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I am a very hard working, dedicated roleplayer. Roleplaying is something that I enjoy doing. Anyways, I am looking to expand my membership in E-Feds. So if any of you fed heads are looking for some new players, drop me an e-mail.
Below is a sample rp of mine. Thanks. Sample RP: :: Scene opens up to a quiet open field. The sky is full of tiny shining stars, and the rounded yellow moon is out. The wind is blowing through the grass of the fields, making a whistle sound. The camera then spots a figure, sitting under a tree. The camera zooms closer to the figure, identifying the figure as none other than "Madman" Max. "Madman" Max has a gloomy look on his face. On his right shoulder is the MWA's Regional Title belt, stained with blood. ::
"Madman" Max: Franky, you know, thanks to you, I have achieved my dream of being a champion. Ever since I was little, I always wanted to feel how heavy a wrestling belt felt. Now thanks to you, I have achieved it. Many nights, I pray, hoping one day I will be a champion. As the months went by and years went by. I felt like nobody from Heaven is listening to me. And yesterday, my prayers were answered. We went back and fourth, like two Japanese fighting fish locked in a water tank. But at the end, after we stained the ring, the ropes, the turnbuckles with our blood, I was the winner. You cracked open up my head with a steel chair shot that would guarantee to make any hardcore fans go
"oohhh". And when you slammed me through that table, I could have sworn I heard the table screamed in pain. But even that was not enough to put an end to my desire of becoming a wrestling champion. I finally got to see and feel the glory of becoming a champion. Yes, along with many of my fans, I even shed tears. Tears of joy were streaming down my face from my eyes, as I held the MWA
Regional Title up high, knowing that I have finally accomplished my dreams. So in closing Franky, I would like to thank you. I would like to thank you for putting me through a table, hitting me across the head with chairs, and MOST of all, for losing the belt to me.
:: "Madman" Max gets up on his feet. He looks at the belt on his right shoulder, he then looks at the camera. ::
"Madman" Max: I realized that all good things will come to an end. There is always a time limit on a champion's title reign. But I have a message to the fans and the MWA locker room. As long as I am champ, I will try my best to make this belt have good credibility. I take pride in being champ, whether it is a
$.50 bingo hall fed or a mulit-million, well known fed. In the past months, I have seen wrestlers bragging how they are the sixth time champion of this belt.
And how they are the eighth time champion of this belt. Sure they won it six or eight times, but that also meant they lost it eight or six times. All I want is to become a Champion that is known for having the longest title reign, not the
MOST. Lastly, Franky, in closing, you are a hero for many fans. With all my strength, I will carry on the legacy that you had put into the MWA Regional belt.
:: "Madman" Max walks away from the camera:: :: Scene then fades away from "Madman" Max to the scene from the "Doomsday in
Detroit PPV". Clips of "Madman" Max holding the belt in the ring, all covered with dry red blood is being shown. The camera then fades away into complete
blackness. ::
