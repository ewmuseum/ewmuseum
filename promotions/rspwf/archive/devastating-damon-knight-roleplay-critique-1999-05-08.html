["Devastating" Damon Knight Roleplay] Critique

8 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In . . .] [SCENE: Baltimore, Maryland. The picture on your screen opens up to a wide view of water, and restaurants. As the camera paces back you can see many restaurants. you see a mob of mainly children and females walk by the camera, obvisiously following someone. The camera then follows that
"mob" of people. As they continue to walk you see a man in the middle. You can not make out who this man is, but you see that he is signing autographs. He continues to walk and makes his way over to a restuarant.
You can see that it is Planet Hollywood. The man who is signing autographs, signs one more and then walks away saying, "Thats all. I'll catch y'all later."
As the man walks away you can see that it is none other than "Devastating" Damon Knight. Damon Knight walks into Planet Hollywood, and the camera fades to black..] [The picture on your screen them begins to fade in as you hear people talking. Tha camera is panning thoughout the restuarant. The camera then focuses in on Damon Knight who is talking to an waiter, and instructs
Damon to his table. You follow as Damon sets down, and begins to look at a menu.] [APPEARANCE: Damon Knight is african american. Damon Knight has braded hair down to his sholder's which is worn in a corn-roll style. He has deep dark brown eyes, he has very thin side-burns. His left ear is piereced which he wears a black pearl in. Damon Knight is wearing black timberland boots, and baggy black IceBerg jeans. Damon Knight is also wearing a white and black IceBerg shirt, with a shull cap. Damon Knight is wearing a gold chain around his neck, and a sparkling rolex watch.
Damon looks up noticing the camera. He smiles, and begins to speak . . .] DDK : It's 'bout tiem you 'den came to see tha knight and shinning armor of GLOW. An yo, would GLOW, even be "GLOW" if it wasn't for tha
KnightLight? I think not, and it is wise for y'all to think like tha serial thriller. [Laughs.] DDK : Anyway it is time for me, to introduce myself. I know that you all know who I am, but for the few of you piss poor drunks out there, i'm
"Devastating" Damon Knight. Hold your applause. Save 'em. I got plans to make big moves in GLOW, and do even bigger things, ya heard?
First, I got a match against some guy named, Death Incarnate, and from there, I just wanna beat on someone else. That is tha plan, from tha man. That is in tha future though, so we can chill on that. Back to tha introductions. See fellas I am tha man, who makes your ladies panties wet. I am tha man that'll make a grandmother catch an orgaism looking at me. I am the man that will make you do-doo in your whitey-tighties, and I am nothing less then simply devastating.
[Smiles.] DDK : Y'all love me already, right? [Looks around the place] Well if y'all don't like me bet against me. Y'all know that jealousy and envy will get you nowhere. The first bet that you can make against tha serial thriller will be on, May the second. Who will I be facing? [Pause] A
man nearly twice my size, a guy who needs a ray of blacklight in his life, Death Incarnate. See I know that Death Incarnate is going to ova look good ol' DDK . . . LITERALLY ! ! No matter though, i'm gonna play my position. What is that you ask? It is tha winners position. [Damon Knight scratches his head as he calls the waitress over to his table.] WAITRESS : Hello sir, how may I help you today? DDK : Yo, I just want some service. WAITRESS : Sure thing, sir. So what would you like to order? DDK : Anything that you can suggest? WAITRESS : Lobster and shrimp is excellent. I'm sure i'll fix it up just to your liking. DDK : Aight den. Lobster and Scrimp it is. You can be on your marry way now, Mr. Knight will holla at you in a lil' bit.
[Damon Knight watches the waitress walk away with her face lite up, with a huge smile.] DDK : Damn, she looks kinda good. I should have told her to write her phone number down, before I don't want it no more. Doesn't matter now.
[Damon Knight strugs his shoulders with a blank expression on his face.] DDK : Now, back to tha big, dark, gloomy guy who i'll have to smack, wit' tha palm of my hand. [Smiles] Yo, Death on tha real, I don't know you. I have hard of you, just like you have heard of me. I have witnessed you perform once, and if you have seen television then you have seen tha ghetto supastar, turn international supaster, on
TV. That is besides tha point though. Tha fact of the devastating matter is that DDK is ready to take that step. I have been chillin', like tha
villian, for a while but it is 'bout that time to make big moves, and do big things. Like they say, Death Incarnate, first impressions last a lifetime. My first in GLOW is going to last just that, a life time.
A Death Incarnate life time. [Damon Knight snickers and grins.] DDK : When I talked to tha owner of GLOW, he seemed to be happy. He has an all star lineup, and some real players that are just waiting to do their thing. Death Incarnate you are going against the double D, single K, "Devastating" Damon Knight. You are going against that pure playa, and nothing less then the total package. I know that you are gonna wanna brawl for it all, an knock this man right here out real quick. It ain't happenin' like that.
[Damon Knight waives his finger in the air.] DDK : Nah, not like that. See the match is all planned out. DDK takes Death Incarnate off his feet, smacks him around, pleases the fans, always please the fans, and then get tha one, two, three. I know it is easier said then done, but i'll have to turn up tha heat and make it, hot like fire. Death Incarnate, no more babble . . . May the second, it'll be you and me. I'll be walking out a victor. Until then always keep your KnightLight on . . .
[The waitress walks back into the picture with a glass of wine in her hand. She sets it down and walks away as the cmaera zooms in on Damon and he gets a devious look on his face and begins to talk . . .]
DDK : Oh yeah, Death Incarnate, GLOW, hell tha entire world. If y'all ain't know . . . i'm Lots of action...Twice the satisfaction... and if i'm the main event... i'm DEFINITELY the MAIN ATTRACTION!! See ya soon . . .
[Damon Knight looks deep into the screen as the camera fades to black.]
