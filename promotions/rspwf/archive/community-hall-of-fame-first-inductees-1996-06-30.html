The E-Wrestling Hall of Fame and Memorial: First Inductees

30 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
About two months ago, [name withheld] proposed creating an e-wrestling Hall of Fame. He wrote: I think it is about time RSPWF had a Hall of Fame to celebrate and remember the great players, fedheads, characters and general contributions of RSPWF, so that in ten years time, there will be an anchor to what we have now. Jacob also recommended that r.s.p-w.f keep a memorial section in the Hall of Fame, so that we could commemorate the late [name withheld]. However, we learned just a week or two before voting that [name withheld] is still alive. Thus the "Memorial" wing of the Hall of Fame will remain empty for the time being. After months of building a committee, making and seconding nominations, and finally voting on the nominations, we have eleven inductees to the Hall of Fame and Memorial. Before listing the lucky eleven, I should explain the mechanics of the process. All nominees must have at least two years experience, and people cannot nominate, second, or vote for themselves or their own characters. After a list of seconded nominees has been made, the voting committee (in this case [name withheld], myself, [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld] and [name withheld]) each selects half of the nomineess (rounded up) who they think belong in the Hall of Fame. Nominees must be named on a majority of the ballots to be inducted. In this case we had twenty-one seconded nominees, so each of the seven judges cast eleven votes. Everyone who received at least four votes will be inducted. Shawn ([detail withheld]@qabalah.cac.psu.edu) will be holding an official induction ceremony soon, sponsored by his fed, the KWF. [name withheld] ([detail withheld]@ulix.net) will be maintaining the Hall of Fame web page as part of his E-Wrestling Museum, currently under construction. And now, without further ado... Hall of Fame and Memorial Inductees Players In this section, there will be only one inductee. [name withheld] received five out of a possible six votes, and was an easy choice. Ray created many memorable characters (four of whom were nominated), and was one of the top players in just about every league from 1991 to 1995. Characters Brewster Cogburn was a unanimous choice in this department, receiving all seven votes. Cogburn got his start in the EMWA, the first e-wrestling federation, and remained a star for several years. He held titles in the EMWA and UFWS, won the first Slick Ric Tournament (at Summit Two) and reached the semi-finals at Summit Six. He started out as a heel, but later became a "road warrior-esque" face. Bobby "The Cavalier" Windsor was also a unanimous choice in this department, receiving six out of a possible six votes. Windsor was a star in the UFWS and ESW, and was in several prominent feuds in each. He was named "Best Male Face" at the 1994 This Week in e-Wrestling (TWiW) awards, won the Tiger Mask Invitational, won the Slick Ric Tournament at Summit Six, and held the UFWS Junior Heavyweight and ESW Lightheavyweight titles multiple times. The tag team of Wild Hazard also received six out of seven votes. Wild Hazard were a very entertaining face team, often imitated but never duplicated. They were named "Best Face Tag Team" at both interfederational awards shows (1993 and 1994) and won the Road Warriors Cup at Summit Five. The Headbangers, former UeWF and MIW stars, received five out of a possible six votes. They were named "Male Tag Team of the Year" in 1994, reached the Road Warriors Cup semifinals at Summit Five, and won it at Summit Six. In addition to their success as a tag team, Mosh and Thrash also won multiple titles in the UeWF individually. [name withheld] remarked, "Every time a tag team gets to a level of dominance in the e-feds nowadays, this is the team that they would sell their souls to face in the ring." The only manager to be inducted is Ace Diamond, who received five votes. Ace managed the face stable TNT Enterprises in the UeWF, UFWS, ESW and a few other feds. [name withheld] called him "the best manager character ever created." Ace is currently active in the Keystone Wrestling Federation. Fedheads [name withheld], creator of the UFWS (originally called PWGeA) received six votes. The UFWS made big changes in e-wrestling, introducing play-by-play commentary and popularizing mid-week updates and full-length match descriptions. The UFWS was a top-notch fed for over a year. [name withheld], creator of the EMWA, received four votes. The EMWA was the very first e-fed, and got its start shortly after the creation of r.s.p-w in November 1990. [name withheld], who ran the UeWF during its heyday, received four votes. The UeWF was originally created by [name withheld] to be largely a clone of the WeWF. Rick built the UeWF into the flagship fed of e-wrestling, and it lasted for two-and-a-half years. Contributors [name withheld] was named on every ballot, and is one of three unanimous selections to the Hall of Fame. Scott made a lot of contributions as a player in the WeWF, creating the first announcers and the magazine This Week in e-Wrestling (which lasted for about three years). Scott created the OWCH rules set, one of the most popular in e-wrestling, is the one who suggested the Summit of e-wrestling and, in fact, coined the term "e-wrestling". [name withheld] received four out of a possible six votes, for wide-ranging accomplishments including creating and maintaining the FAQ for r.s.p-w.f and the e-wrestling home page, creating the FastMatch! rules and writing a history of e-wrestling. The ten who were nominated and seconded but did not receive the necessary four votes are: Players: [name withheld] [2 votes], [name withheld] [3] Characters: Barry Bromowitz [1], Daedalus the Destroyer [0], the Fabulous Cochon Brothers [2], Frank "the Assassin" Spinelli [0], Roker Showtime [2], Satan's Little Helpers [3] Fedheads: [name withheld] & [name withheld] [2] Contributors: [name withheld] [3] Also, there were a number of people nominated but not seconded, and thus not on the ballot: Players: [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld], [name withheld] Characters: Baron Von Klaw, Flint & Steel, The General, The Four Corners, Club Anarchy, the Dangerous Emotions, Grim Weibaq, The Law, [name withheld] (manager), Fender Marshall Fedheads: [name withheld] Contributors: none The next HoFaM election will probably take place in January. All players, characters, contributors and fedheads who began before January 1995 will be eligible. This means that EWC, EWF, NCW, JCWF and I think VCW stars will be on the ballot, as well as any old-timers worthy of a second shot (I will certainly push Flint and Steel and Club Anarchy). See you then!
