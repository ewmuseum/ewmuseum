[My First RP: C.F.W.] Constructive Feedback Welcomed!

10 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some of you may know by now that I am new to e-wrestling and I recently made my first character Neo Zorbey. I signed him to The Squared Circle and last weekend was when my first ever roleplay was seen to the dozens and dozens.
What I am looking for right now is some feedback, preferably constructive. Be brash and rude if you want, but more than anything, make sure it is constructive or you probably are wasting your time... as I won't learn from my mistakes... and even capitalize on my good parts.
I think it would also help if I told what I was aiming for. I was looking for a very solid and original nature behind the playwright. Aiming to reitterate opinions and most importantly as I ended it... A yearn from the reader for a sequel.
----- [A darkness stretches the width and height of the camera lens. A series of still photos of people are shown:
A woman whose facial expression matches the harrowing cry of death. A child crying and bleeding profusely. One man lying on the ground with blood slowly pouring down out of his nose and eyelids. A set of pale feet and legs lying out of an open locker within a morgue.
The screen flashes between white and black until returning to utter darkness. A calm, hypnotizing voice entrances over. The voice withholds a soothing, godlike manner.]
"Your world right now, since you came to this place -- has been writhen with the sign of darkness. Your world right now is ending. You can feel it slipping! Away it goes and now you don't breathe. No more. You have lost your chance to do everything you had wanted to accomplish by the time you where thirty. How does it feel? To have that taken away?"
[The camera flashes once more with various shots: A body bag being zipped up. Police tape being strung around a crime scene as the dashes of blue and red lights blink in the background. White chalk expressing the body outline of a since removed dead body. A persons body jiggling wildly in the seat of an electric chair. Thick white vapors fume off his body.
The ax of a guillotine comes crashing down on a victim's head; consequently separating the body from the head.]
"Your world right now -- since you came to this place ... 4 Human beings and 1 child under five have died. All done in by murder. And 12 babies born
... for the taking. To be done in by murder! You can't stop the sins."
"Your world right now -- since you came to this place. This place where the Reign of Fear is precedent. This place where I control. I control your world."
"I can take your world away. Giving you a cardboard box along some alleyway. I can reward you with the flourishes of a rich millionaire. You are; the way I create you. Consequently, you can be nothing else but what my imagination endows you to be."
[The black screen then flashes to an unidentifiable man standing inside a small, black, dimly lit warehouse. A young and masculine man is standing atop a bunch of crates, well above a group of forty or so men. He stands a solid six feet tall. With broad shoulders and a dark face highlighted by a black whiskered chin and calm, godlike eyes that peer through dark sunglasses. Head looking down in an ominous slant. His upper body is overlapped by a black leather mini-jacket that has a 'ring of fire' motif emblazoned across the back.
He pumps his left fisted gauntlet into the air.] "ARE YOU WITH ME?!" [The men clutter about looking at each other. Depending on each other for answers. Each unable to think for themselves. Even together as a collective whole, still unable to think. Seemingly in need of someone to guide them.]
"ARE YOU WITH ME?!" [A few of the men give hesitant nods until they all form under one continuos roar of agreement. A wide, ear-to-ear grin impasses the mans face.]
"From this day forth, you pledge allegiance to the legion. There is no leaving, for I possess your prohibitions. IS THAT CLEAR?!"
[The men scramble about for answers, biting their lips as a way to prolong an answer from coming out.]
"IS THAT MUCH CLEAR?!" [The men grumble amongst themselves. Offering such acknowledgments as: "We can be rich!" — "We can be poor!" — "But ... we can be rich." These voices of uncertainty encourage the spokesman to talk further.]
"All of which I say can be true. It is only until you disendow your faith with me, shall you suffer the utmost punishment within my ... rein to power."
[The group huddle together, speaking as a unit, nodding behind but one old mans statements of, "We agree."]
"Then so be my reign this gauntlet I clench tightly. Anyone to question my reign shall be incarcerated and striped of embodiment. From this day forth, the Reign of Fear is never dead."
"HEIL THE REIGN OF FEAR! HEIL I, YOUR LEADER! NEO ZORBEY!" [The lead speaker is now identified as a stranger by the name of Neo Zorbey. Neo slams his iron gauntlet onto a table before him, breaking it down the center. His men resounding in a cheering rouse of "Heil Neo!"]
"That is what I like to hear. That is what I like to hear from my men." [Neo laughs joyously with a certain bold pride distinguished as his alone.] "Together, my men, we shall shall wreak the world. The place known as The
Squared Circle. It is there we shall gain riches together if we succeed. If we do not succeed, we shall not gain riches, rather gain pauper rags and ...
those cardboard box makeshift houses I have spoken of." "Most importantly, recognize that once you fail ... you can loose your chance to succeed. Your life pretty much ended. The last chapter written.
You die short ... a man of unfulfilled dreams and desire." [Neo punches his left gauntlet into an open right hand. A grimace buoys his face as the camera scrambles and weathers into a snowy reception, until the transmission completely closes out.]
