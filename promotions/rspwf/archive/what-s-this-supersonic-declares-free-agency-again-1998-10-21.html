What's this? Supersonic declares free-agency AGAIN?

21 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yeah, you read it right. So let's not waste any time, and hear from the man himself. [The scene opens on a press conference. The room is buzzing with excitement for what is about to happen. Just then Supersonic walks out to the podium in the center of the room to applause. Supersonic is wearing a black Armani suit. His hair is brushed back. He stands in front of the podium.]
Supersonic:After my return to wrestling, I've realized that for sure I belong in the ring. Well, after several successful months of my return, most of my feds have closed. So therefore, I am now looking for a new fed to join, declaring myself a free agent once again.
Remember the stir I created several months back? Well, as the creater of this free agent craze, I figured that I'd go for it again. But remember, I have a few limitations, I will probably only join 3 or four federations. So if you're looking for a star to fill out your roster, I'm your man.
[Supersonic walks off as reporters begin to talk and write things one their clipboards. The scene fades.]
[The scene reopens on a cheesy news-set. A corny-looking announcer sits at a desk with a I-have-big-news-and-you-don't-nah-nah-nah look on his face. He sorts papers on the desk and begins to speak.]
Reporter:Well, you heard it here first folks, Supersonic is looking for a fed. However, he has limitations. [The following things appear written out on the screen.] It must be a closed RPing fed Established feds are not a must, but preferred No guestbook roleplaying Preferably everything done through e-mail [The scene fades back to the newsroom] Reporter:Well there you have it folks!
