[RSPWF 200 Feedback] From Ian

14 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, my name is the "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh and I read the top 200. I may not be all that well known, but I have won many titles and almost all of my matches. I lost only 2 matches by DQ and 2 by pinfall because the people running the organizations were not content with me winning all of the time. I ran a few people out of online wrestling. Titles held: OCW Light-Heavyweight Title EHW Extreme Title (2) EHW World Title EHW American Tag Titles (2) *OHW World Title (OHW titles were won by me in one night.) *OHW European Title *OHW Intercontinental Title ECWF World Title WIW World Tag Team Titles **For the past 4 months, I have been the leader of a powerful group known as the Axis Powers and went interpromotional. Before the Axis Powers, the nWo was the name of the interpromotional group. Eddie Jacobs was in it and still is (As far as I know.) And Brass Knuckles left on a controversial note. (Complicated, but I fired him.)** Ask Eddie Jacobs if I am as good as I say and ask Brass Knuckles and he will say it reluctantly... Other Federations: WOW (Closed while I was heading for the finals of the Intercontinental Title Tourney.) CWF (I went there, won a match, challenged everyone, nobody answrered my challenged so I left the federation.) SWA (I left the promotion half-way through the tournament because the prez and I were not seeing eye to eye.) HOW (All of the Axis Powers were fired because of becoming too powerful of a force.) EOWA (My first federation... I won all of my matches. Federation closed down in a month.) XSW (I made my return here after a couple of months off. I won both of my matches before the federation closed down.) **Most of the federations that I was in were closed down. CWF was just frozen in it's tracks. HOW fired the Axis Powers. I am currently in a federation called WIW where I recently won the World Tag Team Titles **Undefeated in Tag Team Competition**) (I have been recomended by many for other federations. I have had at least a dozen presidents from federations contact me and said that so and so recomended me for their federation and to contact me to see if I wanted to join. I started online wrestling in May and was a star from the start. My two real losses came in two different federations. 1 loss was in OCW because the president was not happy with me winning so much with the Light-Heavyweight Title and had better plans for me as becoming the world champion. I was thrown offline and came back in time to see the federation come to it's demise. I returned in XSW and soon went to EHW. My second pinfall loss was after I won the Extreme Title. One week after I won the title, I lost a controversial match because the president was mad at me. After that, the master of wrestling and the czar of extreme went on and destroyed everyone beyond all belief.) **A reason for my success is that my wrestler is me and I come up with more things for my arsenal all of the time. Thank you for your time, "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh
