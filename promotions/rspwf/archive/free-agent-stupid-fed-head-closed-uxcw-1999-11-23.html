[Free Agent] Stupid fed-head closed UXCW...

23 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just did this nice little RP for some fed I was in. I joined last weekend, and did two RPs... one yesterday and this one tonight. Then the damn Prez closed the fed and said everyone in it sucked at RPing... most of them did,
though :-) Anyway, if anyone wants me to join their fed with KryptonicSonic, KniQuest,
Kid Kaos... or possibly all three, give me the address to your site.
Message board-based feds only, please Either only made-up wrestlers, or a mixed fed is fine... doesn't really matter to me! Must have a cool looking design and an in depth fed (meaning you must have individual wrestler bio pages and junk like that)
1-2 cards per week... preferrably only one, because I am in two other feds as well I would have joined Empire Wrestling, but the Prez wasn't too nice to me recently on here... heh
My other prospect is the one that The Cubs Fan runs... forgot the name, but it is a killer fed...
Anyone else want to try and get me to sign? If so, send me an e-mail of post to the group. --- KryptonicSonic ///Handles\\\ KryptonicSonic {SWF} [0-0] http://www.geocities.com/violator60/index.html {THO} [0-0] http://www.angelfire.com/mo2/hardcorefed/ Kid Kaos {Free Agent} KniQuest {Free Agent} -----------Roleplay------------ ::The scene is in some lounge room area. Lizzie Williams is sitting on a couch as the camera moves inside the room. When she notices the camera coming toward her she gets up out of her seat and greats the viewers.::
Lizzie: "Hello there, fans. KryptonicSonic, Kid Kaos and KniQuest are out of the building at the moment, and they left something for me to show you all.
Hold on a second while I put it on." ::Lizzie then walks across the room and opens a door on the wall, revealing a cabinet imbedded into the wall of the room. Inside is a large television and a stereo rack. Lizzie reaches toward the stereo, and turns on the reciever and one of the components. Next, she hits the 'on' switch on the television and grabs a remote.::
Lizzie: "Before the left, 4-K taped an interview and put it on a DVD. They told me to show it to you when the camera got here, so here we go."
::Lizzie hits a button on the remote and the camera turns to face the television. The scene of the interview is inside the kitchen area of the 4-K home in Northern California. This is a different home than the vacation place they were at when last interviewed for UXCW. KryptonicSonic grabs a set of keys and walks away from the camera. It follows him as he goes through a doorway and out into a garage area. One side of the garage has a large tool bench running down the wall. On the bench are all sorts of tools and car parts. On the other side of the garage are three car lifts, each holding two automobiles. The first has a red Lamborghini Diablo on top with a blue and white Shelby Cobra below. The lift right next to it houses a yellow Ital-Design Cala on top and a silver BMW M3 below. The final lift has a dark-purple Nissan Xtera on top and a silver Mercedes-Benz SLK500 below.
KryptonicSonic makes his way toward the Mercedes-Benz.:: KS: "Welcome to my toy room. As you can see, I have quite a collection in here, and I have only just begun. Many of my fans do not know this, but I went to the Universal Technical Institute and was trained to become a
Mercedes technician. I did that for six years, at which point I started my own wrestling promotion here in Northern California. Because of my past, my prize possession in this room is this silver SLK500. Sure, it may not be as expensive as the Diablo or as nice looking as the Cobra, but it is definatly my favorite. For some reason I have liked Mercedes' cars since my high school years. While everyone else dreamed of BMWs, I liked the Mercedes-Benz product line. Anyway, that is not of much importance now, because I now have wrestling matters at hand. Kid Kaos and KniQuest will be joining me shortly, but I have a few words to say until then."
::KS then walks to the side of his Mercedes, opens the door, and climbs inside. He gribs the wheel and sits there before he continues on.::
KS: "The Sandman has offered myself and the rest of 4-K to join him in a stable. Right now we already are a stable, but he apparently wants to propose a larger one. Sandman, at this point in time I have yet to even meet more than a half-dozen UXCW wrestlers. Although you do propose a great offer, I am going to have to decline at this moment. That's not to say that our two stables can't be friendly with each other and work together, but I don't think I want to be under the wing of a higher man... at least not yet."
::Once KS finishes talking, a Toyota Supra and a Nissan 300ZX roll into the garage. They both come to a stop, and from them step their passengers. Out of the Supra's driver side steps Kid Kaos, and another man steps from the passanger side. From the drivers side of the 300ZX steps KniQuest, and some woman steps out of the passanger's side.::
KK: "What's up KS? Look who it is... Katlil!" ::Kid Kaos points to the man who had been riding with him in his car, now identified as Katlil. Katlil walks over to KS and gives him a high five.::
KS: "Haha... what's up man? Long time no see! How do you like my toy room? Nice huh?" ::KS and Katlil then walk away from the Mercedes and all four men stand in a circle. The woman who had been riding in KniQuest's car makes her way toward the door to go inside the house.::
KQ: "So, what's up? You ready to go?" KS: "Nah, not yet. I'm doing a UXCW interview. I'm almost done though." KQ: "Perfect! I have to talk about my upcoming match. Let's see... who am I facing? Drop Zone, that's right. Drop Zone, man, I have no idea who you are, but I'm pretty positive that does not matter. You see, I have skills beyond what anybody in UXCW can imagine. I am ever so close to being able to compete in the biggest promotions in the world, but I am keeping it small for now so I can concentrate on more important things in my young age. Plain and simple, Drop Zone, you better start training and you better start real quick!"
KK: "From what I can imagine, KQ, your match is gonna be as easy as pie. Mine, however, won't be so. The best guy in the fed outside the 4-K, and our only true allie so far is The Sandman. Sure enough, he's the guy I have to face in the World Title tourney. It's all good, though, because I have been in training for months now, and am in the best shape of my life. Sandman, either way our match goes, there will be no bad feelings from me. I need to prove myself to you and the rest of UXCW, and our match will give me the perfect opportunity to do just that. As much as I hate to fight you, I will be there and will be ready when the time for our match comes."
KS: "I almost forgot about my upcoming match in the tourney! I believe I am fighting Shane McMahon... yeah, I'm fighting him. Shanie-boy, I am 150 percent positive that your ass is going to get kicked in the World Title tourney. So far 4-K respects everyone in UXCW with two exceptions. One is
Chris Jericho, who is just an idiot, and the second is you, who is nothing but a wuss. Normally I would be nice and polite, especially since I am new here, but you are such a joke to me that I can do nothing but laugh. Have a nice life, Shane."
::At this point, Lizzie Williams and the woman with KniQuest have come back outside and joined the four men. The camera pans back, and the six of them say something to each other... something that the camera does not pick up.
KinQuest and his female companion then climb into his 3000ZX and drive away, and Kid Kaos and Katlil climb into the Supra and follow. Lizzie Williams and
KS then climb into the SLK500 and KS starts up the engine. He slowly pulls out of the parking bay and waves Lizzie waves to the camera as they speed
away.::
