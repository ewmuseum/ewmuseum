-[Free Agents]- Press Conference - Tag Team Makes Their Debut -[Free Agents]-

20 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
^^The Scene Opens up, you see A stage With a rostrum on it, Preparing for a
Press Conference
"Come As You Are" By Nirvana Start Playing on the System, out comes the
Stunning Dora dressed up in a Black Dress, with a very low neckline, she gets to the rostrum and start speak, in a very low voice^^ Dora: Ladies And Gentlemen, For Those of you who don't quite know who am
I,The Name is Dora, Stunning Dora, at this time I Would like to introduce, making their debut in to the wrestling world, they are the Hardcore Sensation - "Hard Impact" Kurt Insanity &
"Radical" Kirk Madness!
^^ "Come As You Are" Start Playing Again, out comes two men dressed up in
Black Tights, on their side it says "Hardcore Sensation" and on the other side each got his nick name, "Radical" takes the lead steps, as he goes in a hurry to the ring, Kurt got some big muscles although he is in the cruiserweight division by all standards, he also got a crewcut in his black hair, "The Radical" Kirk Madness has long black hair, and also he wears
Sunglasses, Both of them wear wrestling boots, they finally get to the ring, Kurt & Kirk gets to the rostrum, Dora comes and give each a kiss,
Radical Smiles as "Hard Impact" takes the Microphone^^
Kurt Insanity: Well, Hardcore Sensation is now officially starting, after hard work for few months now, training hard for this moment exactly we feel we are ready to burst into the World Of E-Wrestling Tag Team Action, we know its kind of dry but we are going to make it HOT, Cause the
Hard Impact and the Radical, Along with Dora the Stunning One, will take the Tag Team Division into another dimension, you can say we are inexperience but we say we are young, you can say we don't belong as a team but we call it different styles, But if you will say we are no challenge to you, then We got a news for you, set a time and set a date cause As of right now we are free agents and we will go with the best offer we will get, and we aren't talking just money but Talents, cause we aren't going into a federation with no Action just to wait until someone kindly enough challenge us, we are going to make a Hard IMPACT! Take it away Kirk... Kirk Madness: "The Night Has Come, And The Land Is Dark..." That is what I
Feel about the Tag Team Wrestling Division, We Feel that Tag Team Wrestling is being over looked, while Single Wrestling is on top of the business, we come in The Purpose of being on top of the tag team world, and of being The best and the Most Hardcore we can be, as our name states We are the
Hardcore Sensation, We Are Great in Wrestling but if you challenge us to a
Tag Team HARDCORE Match it will take the show from a good one to the best,
Kurt & I Were On Separated Promotions & was each very good in his own promotion then with the assistant of the Lovely Dora we became a team, a
Hardcore team, a team with soul & spirit, we are here to fight for the fans, cause with out the fans there would be no business, remember that The
Radical shall never submit, if you enter the ring with the Hardcore
Sensation you are entering our zone, you are entering an insane and a mad zone, and most important of all you are entering the RADICAL ZONE!
Dora: And To All Of You Out there, Remember that if anything can go wrong...if we're around...IT WILL!
^^Dora Gets up, and hold hands with both wrestlers as they go to the back, on the Screen behind them the name "Hardcore Sensation" appears, As the
Camera Fades Out...^^
