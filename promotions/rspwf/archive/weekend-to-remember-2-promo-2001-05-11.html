Weekend to Remember 2 Promo

11 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
TO RECEIEVE THE CARD, SEND AN E-MAIL TO [detail withheld] [Darkness. Suddenly, a voice-over fills the audio...] VO: In the fall of 1998...over 75 of the sport's hottest stars gathered for one history making weekend...
[Gold letters which read _Weekend to Remember_ flash accross the black background.] VO: But these were not just any stars. These stars were men of courage... [Several rapid fire shots cross over the screen. Creed hitting the Triangle Creedplex on Simon Lebec...Chris
Myers planting Deathbringer with the Rattler..."Rajun Cajun" Christopher Stonebreaker spearing Brody Thunder, and finally a shot of Otto Verhoeven hitting Robert
Donovan with a Slaughterhouse off the second rope passes over the screen before we once again are taken to black.]
VO: Men of...dignity... [Slow motion shot of Dan Kauffman locking the Peacemaker on
Rick Styles in their classic One Hour Iron Man Match. This shot is followed up with one of Brody Thunder hitting a
Cattlebuster DDT on a stunned Creed. And finally, we see a shot of Joe Reed Powerbombing "Cowboy" Ken Curtis.]
VO: Honor... [Shots of Steve Spector hitting a shooting star press on
Tiger Claw..."Cowboy" Ken Curtis hitting the Cowboy Plex on a groggy Jeff Matthews...Simon Lebec laying Serge Annis out with a Tornado DDT...and Doc Holliday hitting Sho
Satusma with a hurricanrana, flash accross the screen.] VO: And pride... [Slow motion shot of Brody Thunder hitting a Widow Maker
DDT on Ken Curtis fills the screen...This image is replaced with a shot of a bloody Casey James Powerslamming Christopher
Stonebreaker. Finally, a still shot of Rick Styles with his hands raised in victory fills the screen.]
VO: Yes...it truly _was_ a Weekend to Remember. [This time a slow motion shot of "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder thrusting the R-Cup Trophy in to the air flashes across the screen...and stays there.]
VO: Now... [A faint classical music tune plays in the background, picking up the suspense level.] VO: Nearly two and a half years later, the stars will gather one more time. [We see a still shot of the Citrus Bowl in Orlando, Florida. The gathering point for Weekend to Remember 2.] VO: Many of the stars of yesterday have moved on...drifted to a different lifestyle. [Rapid fire shots of Otto Verhoeven...Christopher Stonebreaker... Simon Lebec...and yes, Brody Thunder whiz across the screen.] VO: This time...new stars will step up to fill the roles of hero. [Various shots of The Warlock, Apex, and Luke Kinsey in action flash across the screen. This segment is finished up with a slow motion shot of Eddie Jacobs hitting a Tranquilizer DDT on a weary opponent.]
VO: Villian. [The evil eyes of one Eugene Robinson glare in to the camera. This shot is repalced with one of Johnny Axis holding the RCW
National strap while he yaps away on the mic. This image transforms in to one of Terry Sloane standing directly in front of the camera with his arms crossed...]
VO: Victor. [Once again, we see more live action shots. This time of Maxwell Creed...Demon Boy Ishrinku...and UEW star Xavier Feyr. These clips are followed by a still shot of a bloodied Nick Demola's hand being raised by an official.]
VO: And legend... [We now see shots of Ken Curtis in action with Brody Thunder in the finals of the first R-Cup Tournament. This is replaced by a still shot of Rick Styles holding the UEW
Ultimate Title in the air in front of a sold out Yankee Stadium. Now this image is replaced with one final image, an image of Alex Martinez thrusting the EMWC Heavyweight
Title in to the air as the crowd erupts.] VO: Witness history repeat itself... [The gold words Weekend to Remember 2 flash across the screen once again.] VO: Featuring... [The words Weekend to Remember 2 are now replaced by "The R-Cup
Tournament."] VO: Weekend to Remember 2... [Darkness.] VO: ...coming Summer 2001. [Fade to Black.] TO RECEIVE THE CARD SEND AN E-MAIL TO [detail withheld] -Jay
