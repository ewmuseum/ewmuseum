UEW Flash Flood Part III (10/20)

20 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(I) Best of the Best (II) Marcus Manson (III) Marcus "The Misery Machine" Manson (IV) Prince Judife (V) "Lightbearer" Doomsday ~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~ --~~ From: Best of the Best Subject: None Given [SCENE:Camera opens up to inside of a large mansion in Hollywood,CA! The camera moves around the mansion until we hear the sound of women giggleing.
The camera moves towards a pair of double brass dorrs which are open. The camera mmoves through as we see a large pool,jacuzzi area. The camera moves around until we see Shaun and Jonathan Riche sitting in the jacuzzi surrounded by women also in the hot tub. The camera moves in on them. Jonathan has on a pair of black calvin kline shorts, and no shirt showing off his muscular upper toros. Shaun is next to him with a girl under each arm. He has on a pair of gold swimming trunks, no shirt showing his well defined chest and stomache, a gold chain around his neck with the letters "SR" made in diamonds hanging on it. Just then a man comes from behind the camera dressed in a horrid outfit I won't even describe and a mic in hand, he goes near Shaun and Jonathan. .]
AL HUNTER - Hell I am Al Hunter from the UEW to get an interview with you two. First off, congrats on your debut match. . SHAUN RICHE - Can the BS Hunter, we don't need you sucking our asses, telling us how great we are. .we already know how great we are and so does anyone with a brain in there heads.
AL HUNTER - Yes, of coruse. Well what I wanted to know is what's this with you guys and Candyman? First he came out during your match then you two did the same during his match with Matthew Tucker, any comments on this?
JONATHAN RICHE - What? He likes what he sees I guess, and I can't blame him, we're the best g'damn tag team in the world today. .as for us being at ringside during his match, just doing the same as he did, taking a little looksee at his match. .I was impressed, how 'bout you Bro?
SHAUN RICHE - Quite impressed, he's a damn good wrestler. AL HUNTER - What about James Pillow? SHAUN RICHE - What about him? AL HUNTER -Well he was also seen in the audience during your match as well as
Candymans match, any relation between you guys?
SHAUN RICHE - Yeah, James is a great friend of ours. He's the one that broke us into the professional wrestling business, we were trained by the same man, he saw us wrestling,liked what he saw and helped us break into the big time, we ow him alot for that. . .as for him being ringside at our match, well he was probably just coming to check out his investment, making sure we were still kicking ass and taking names so to speak. .and I'm sure he was more than satisfied.
AL HUNTER - That doesn't explain what he was doing at Candymans match. . JONATHAN RICHE - Why can't a guy watch a wrestling show without everyone thinking he's there for a reason? Him and Candyman know eah other, in case you didn't know him,Candyman and two others re-formed 4-D over in the SWP, he was obviously just coming to see his friend wrestle in the UEW.
SHAUN RICHE - Yeah, so stop making these assanine rumors Hunter. Now get on with things. [Shaun took a glass of champaigne from the floor and took a sip from it before kissing the blonde on his right full on the mouth as Hunter cleared his throat and went on. .]
AL HUNTER - Yes, well in a few weeks on Anarchy you guys have your next match agaisnt Thunder and Lightening. Any comments for them?
JONATHAN RICHE - What about them? Just the next team on a long list of teams in the UEW for us to beat, they just happen to be closer to the top of the list and will be the next ones taking out as we make our way up until we will inevitably take hold of the Ultimate Tag Team titles, weither we win em from
The Outlaws, Suicicadl Stallions or even Lance and Max Stank. .notice I didn't call them PPD,'cause if ya ask me there isn't a damn thing perfect about either one of them. . 'cept for how perfectly they lose to us each and everytime we step into the ring with em.
[Hunter clears his throat again and Shaun breaks his kiss from the blonde then begins to speak. .]
SHAUN RICHE - Yeah,yeah. .boy you sure know how to ruin a good time. Well
Thunder and Lightening you two baws are next on the list. Fell special? Well you should, you get to be the next team we beat in the UEW as we make our way to the gold which we _WILL_ take, and there's nothing anyone can do to stop it from happening, 'cause as it was said in the X-Files movie, you can't fight the future and _WE_ are the future of the UEW Tag team circuit and we will soon be the tag team champions, why? Because we truly are. .
SHAUN/JONATHAN - THE BEST OF THE BEST!!! [Jonathan looks over at Shaun and grins. Shaun nods and a second later Jonathan gets up out of the jacuzzii and grabs Al Hunter by the colar then flings him into the pool. Shaun then begins to laugh as the camera ooms in on
Hunter as he tries to get out of the pool but slips back in as the camera slowly. . .]
[Fades To Black. . ] ~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~ --~~ From: Marcus Manson Subject: Chapter 12: "Corin" [CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT] (Fade up . . . the scene is hazy, almost as if we are in the middle of a cloud. Several indiscernible voices join together as one with no understandable tounge. Figures can be seen moving through the mist, but it is impossible to make out what they actually are. One of the shapes starts moving towards us . . . as it gets closer it is evident that it is a female. She has long wavy dark brown hair, which cascades down to near her waist. She wears a sheer blue nightgown, and nothing else except a silver heart pendant around her neck. She raises her hand, reaching it out towards the us, speaking one solitary word.)
Female: (in an other worldly voice) Marcus . . . (Suddenly the scene shatters and reveals another underneath. Two men are struggling, one wields a knife, in what looks to be a hallway. Nearby is the same woman. She is a bit away as the two men struggle. The one with the knife draws back and swings, ging towards the woman. The other man jumps in the way, and the knife is drawn down from above the middle of the victim's right eye and down to his chin, leaving a large, bloody gash there. The knife weilder flees through an open window as the wounded man falls to his knees in front of the woman, his face in his hands.)
Female: (Sobbing) Marc, honey, are you okay? (Again the scene changes, a close up of a pair of eyes, a notable scar running over the right eyelid. Suddenly the eyes snap open, and the the scene pulls back to show Marcus Manson lying in bed. Thunder cracks over head as he lies there blinking, and he utters only one word.)
Marcus: Corin . . . ? (Fade.) ~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~ --~~ From: Marcus "The Misery Machine" Manson Subject: Chapter 13: Sojourn (Fade up. It is a hazy day, thin sheets of gray rain clouds hang over the corner house of Marcus Manson. Drizzle falls as Manson walks off of his porch, putting two suitcases and a pick axe on the curb next to his black Ford F-150.
As the camera approaches Manson, he visably sighs, not really wanting to speak to anyone right now.)
Manson: Comments? (Camera nods) Manson: Since I've been so silent recently? (Nod) Manson: Ok . . . lets make this quick. I've got places to be. (Camera focuses on Manson as he begins to speak.) Manson: Styles, it's good that you started preparing early, because I'm going to be unlike any challenge you've ever had. Go ahead and make your cheap shots, cus they won't hurt Styles, they're words. Your petty little insults, like calling me Marcie, et al., don't affect me.
(Manson tosses a suitcase into the bed of his truck.) Manson: Now Styles, you misunderstood what I said when I stated that you could very well beat me. I said that it was *possible*, I didn;t say it was definatly going to happen.
(Manson walks to the porch, retrieving a few envelopes from the mailbox.) Manson: Also Dick, this isn't quite my first main event match in UEW. The CoD and Colby went up against Payne and Guts and Glory in an SNH Main Event, and I also faced Payne in a singles match in a triple main event on SNH. I've also been in a few Main Events on Anarchy, Ricardo, so don't tell me what I need to do going into a main event.
(Manson reaches inside the cab of his truck and puts the envelopes underneath the sun visor.)
Manson: I may have lost the Ultimate Tag titles Styles, but getting rid of that insufferable puke Regnigh was the best thing that happened to me since
Summer Dreams. And my "coffin" really doesn't have any nails yet Ricky, so keep dreaming.
(Manson reaches down, picking up the pick ax and examining it before tossing it into the bed.)
Manson: Styles, as you so adequetly stated over your little sushi dinner with
Samantha, a monkey can be taught to throw a heart punch. However, i doubt a
Monkey has enough strength to thro the kind of HP I do. You know what happens when you get Heart Punched Styles? Your heart skips a beat . . . for one split second it stops beating. Stunning you long enough to let me get the win.
(Manson walks up to the porch and picks up a box.) Manson: The last of my critics is Mr. Payne. Nickedemus, my boy, your logic is very interesting. You believe that when you face me after I beat Styles and become UEW's first holder of both Ultimate Championships . . . Somehow
I'm going to be easier to beat than him? Explain that Payne, you're the knowledgable one, you apparently know everything. Enlighten me, how *exactly* will I be easier to beat than Styles after I end his fourth title reign? I'll let you think about that for a bit, get back to me after Saturday Night
Fright.
(Manson puts the box on the curb, with the trash. and gets inside the truck. As he does a cell phone rings. Manson answers it) Manson: Manson here, talk. (Over the phone) Magnus Colby: Marcus, do you have any idea what time it is? Manson: Oh about 2:30. Colby: Good to see your watch still works. Now then, do you remember something about an autograph session we were going to this afternoon?
Manson: Sorry Magnus, I got more important things to do. Colby: Like what? You know, I don't get a lot of oppurtunities to do these things anymore. I would hope, at least, that you are training.
Manson: Sorry Magnus, Like I said it's important. That's all I can tell ya. Colby: God damnit. Marcus, if you are going to be the next UEW Ultimate champion, you have obligations. You have got to learn to be more responsible then this. And you sure as hell ain't going to win it if you don't start listening to me.
(Manson shuts off the cell phone and turns the egnition of the F-150. It roars to life as Manson speeds away. Fade.)
~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~ --~~ From: Prince Judife Subject: Oh, so much on my mind [Scene opens outside a large, white mansion - a very familiar picture. In front, there are numerous sprinklers going off, watering the large lawn in front of the larger mansion. It is a picturesque image, with a long, driveway, which comes around the front of the two main doors. There’s a white stretch limo parked in front, the one that Prince Judife usually gets transported in.
The camera just looks around the large home, zooming in here and there to various parts of the house. The scene slowly starts to fade...
It opens up again in a large room, with dark green walls and low lights. In the center is a large pool table, with Prince Judife hunched over it, pool stick in hand, wearing casual clothes - khakis (with a bandaged knee) and a
Tommy T-shirt. His blonde hair has been cut, and is much shorter. His face is clean-shaven and you can see him clearly grinning. The camera continues to look around slowly before zooming into Prince Judife]
PRINCE JUDIFE: _THIS_is the life. [Judife looks around for a while, at the exquisite surroundings he is in.] PRINCE JUDIFE: You see... I am a powerful and respected man. And powerful and respected men GET_WHAT_THEY_WANT! And this past week on Anarchy... PRINCE
JUDIFE_GOT_WHAT_HE_WANTED. Did I have a match? [Pauses...] NO! So did I win a match? [Pauses again...] NO! So what did I get?
[He stares up and starts almost laughing menically...] PRINCE JUDIFE: I got the chance to beat up not ONE... BUT_TWO_WORTHLESS PIECES OF GARBAGE! [He looks around self-consciously, not realizing how he was acting, and shakes his head, as if trying to clear it]
PRINCE JUDIFE: Well, now that I got that out of my system, I can speak like a civilized person... something many of the UEW wrestlers can not claim to ever be able to do. Where to start? So many things to discuss...
[Prince Judife glances around, looking around the room, appearing to be in deep thought] PRINCE JUDIFE: Ah yes, let’s start off with Charles "The serial killer" Manson. Charles... [The cameraman begins to whisper to Prince Judife. The camera can’t pick it up however...] PRINCE JUDIFE: Oh be quiet! You don’t know what you’re talking about... Marcus
Manson? Sheesh... I know enough about him to know his name! Anyway, Charles, what I did to you at UEW Anarchy doesn’t compare to what I_COULD_DO. You’re lucky you got away with only some kicks Manson, because YOU, ME, and the ALL of the UEW knows that if I want to, I can destroy your serial killing ASS!
[The cameraman continues to whisper to Prince Judife. Judife tries to ignore him...] PRINCE JUDIFE: Leave me alone! YOUR job is to hold the camera so that all MY fans can see and hear me. It’s not YOUR job to tell me what to say... GOT
THAT?? [Prince Judife then starts to squint at something which the cameraman seems to be holding up...]
PRINCE JUDIFE: What are the odds of that? The illiterate man was right. Anyway, MARCUS... you think you’re strong? Did you lay a hand on ME? No sir. Did you lay a hand on my bodyguards? Yes sir. Do I care about my bodyguards? [Judife pauses before snapping his head back to the camera] PRINCE JUDIFE: NO WAY IN [BLEEP] HELL! You could have KILLED my bodyguards and
I would be laughing right now. "Misery Machine"... if you think that you feel misery now... just wait until I_FINISH_WITH_YOU!
[Prince Judife picks up the pool stick and takes a shot... putting the white ball in the corner pocket...]
PRINCE JUDIFE: Damn this foolish game. Grown men... holding a small stick and trying to knock little balls into small holes...
[Prince Judife grins, not realizing what he just said..] PRINCE JUDIFE: Anyway, why am I playing this game? I’m just doing a small re- enactment of what I did to Johnny "Sexy" Bod. Now... of all the weapons available to me... what did I choose to give Mr. Bod a beating with? And why did I choose it? I chose a stick... you see, Bod is a... slow and simple man.
Quite honestly, there is no reason for me to go out there with anything that requires thinking up, because no matter what I take, Bod will always get knocked down. Heck, if you give Bod a gun and me a spoon, I’d still manage to get on top of him. Knowing him, he’ll either forget how to use it, or not know what a gun is. Am I lying about this? Why would I? Johnny Bod is a mentally and physically challenged retard, and he’ll just continue to prove it to the world and the UEW. I don’t think he deserves any more of my valuable time, so
I think I’ll move on.
[Prince Judife glances around the room, and picks up a small bag that is resting on a chair behind Judife. It contains various small edible snacks...
chips, cookies and one very small piece of candy...] PRINCE JUDIFE: Ah... a single piece of candy. To many people, it is exactly like that. But to those more intelligent and who have more than half a brain, this signifies MUCH MUCH MORE. To me, this shows the single man standing out, as a softy. CandyMan, that SINGLE piece of candy is YOU. And YOU are amidst all the other type of men, standing out by yourself. CandyMan, what you did to me on Anarchy was totally uncalled for! You knew that if I got in the ring with you, I’d knock some sense into that soft head of yours, so you used dirty tactics. You make me fall on a piece of candy. Not only that, but...
[Points to his knee, which is heavily bandaged...] PRINCE JUDIFE: You injured me severely! I can’t even walk! CandyMan, you’ll pay for what you did to me and my knee...
[He looks down, very sadly at his knee, and tries, unsuccessfully, to move it] PRINCE JUDIFE: YOU’LL PAY FOR THIS!! YOU WILL!! [Prince Judife calms down from his VERY loud screaming. The screaming attracts attention, as a small dog comes running into the room, barking towards Prince
Judife. He heads towards Judife, scaring off the Prince who begins to run around, to the other side of the pool table. His stride is perfect, and he doesn’t appear to be injured at all. After he runs around for a bit, he looks at the camera and grins sheepishly before yelling at the cameraman]
PRINCE JUDIFE: TURN THAT THING OFF THIS INSTANT!! AND WHO’S FILTHY DOG IS THIS? GET HIM OUT OF HERE!! [He looks back at the cameraman] PRINCE JUDIFE: ARE YOU DEAF? TURN IT OF--- [Scene abruptly goes black] ~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~--~~~ --~~ From: "Lightbearer" Doomsday Subject: Who shall come up trumps? [Scene: The view opens up inside a casino, seen round about are various gambling icons, such as roulette wheels and poker tables. The camera then passes over a table and then it stoops as a hand comes down onto the table. The camera then looks on as the hand moves away. A voice is then heard as the dealer begins to speak.]
D - "Place your bets please, all bets please." [A figure then lurks within the shadows and then he motions over towards the table, the camera then transfixes itself as it cautiously pans over the figure. The figure who stands with blue eyes which are as cold as ice then begins to chuckle, then he smirks and begins to speak.]
F - "What kind of place is this. I wonder what business transactions are going to go on here? Oh well, whatever happens somebody shall come up trumps."
[The dealer then with a look of sheer amazement, then just shrugs. The camera then passes over the figure and you can then see that he has boots which are as black as soot and he is wearing a perfected blue as the sky set of wrestling tights. The camera then pans over the face of the figure and then it stops. The figure then with a somewhat look into the view of the lens casts a shadow over the entire table. The camera then motions in and then it just makes out the facial features of the figure. The figure has scars across his left side of his face and he has a red and black patch over his right eye. The figure then turns and then you can see that it is Doomsday or the "Lightbearer." The camera then positions itself and then it goes back to the dealer.]
D - "All bets are now in, so let’s see what the stakes are going to be?" [The dealer then spins the wheel of fate and then it lands with a black ball placed deep in the heart of one of the pictures. The dealer then looks at the picture and then looks back at Doomsday and then starts up.]
D - "I’m sorry, it looks like we are going to have a winner, and it is not going to be you. Alex Martinez is going to take this from you."
[Doomsday then gets angry and then he turns the entire table upside down and then in a rage of fury, begins to speak.]
DD - "Who art thou to say that he is going to win. He may have the support behind him, yet what he doesn’t have is a pure, yet solid heart, no all he knows of is the ways of darkness and who knows the darkness greater then any man, well I once did, but that was the past, now is the future and now what lies in store is a chance for me to show the UEW that I do have what it takes to defy Alex
Martinez and if it is to be the case then so be it, yet if not, then so be it. What I will say though is that I will never give up and that is one of my key factors. A factor which has led me to being with the best and one day will lead me to being with the extra special ones, but for now I know that I have a sheer determination level which no man hath seen before and this is what makes me go on and thus come October 21st, it shall not be different."
[Doomsday then looks on at the evil which surrounds him in the place which he is in, Doomsday just smirks and then continues.]
DD - "Alex Martinez, all you say all the time, is this and that. Well the way I see it, we both have never met before, we both don’t have any rivalries with each other, yet for me to get somewhere, then I am going to have to defy you and hopefully it shall be done. Martinez, you may have the support of Rick Styles, he may be the UEW World Champion, yet what you don’t know is that he and I , well you just don’t know. I won’t say me and Styles are friends, for I know that is not the case, but what I do know is that he along with a few others are out there watching my back, making sure that none of your so called goons interfere. Martinez, if I am to be burned, then I have to say whatever happens, is going to happen to you as well and you know why, well because I Lay down the law and that is that. You think, you are real tough because you have some friends, well that don’t mean a thing, for without them, where art thou going to be?
Martinez, I suggest, that you think real hard about your decisions in the UEW, for I know what with the undying attention of them all, the determination factor which I have and most of all, the will to lie down for nobody. With these three combined, I know that what lies in store will be my ultimate glory and thus then you shall know why "Darkness covers the earth no more." Martinez, you are the Darkness and as the cards are laid down, the spades are drawn and a Blackheart is pulled forth, yet what happens next, well that is completely up to you, for now there is only one way and that is come October 21st a place where is nowhere to run nor hide, no there is only one place and that is No Way Out."
[The scene then fades out as Doomsday looks on at the dealer of death and then in a flash of light, the scene erupts and then as clear as crystal, a ray of white light is omitted into the room and then it fades. The scene then fades to black.]
