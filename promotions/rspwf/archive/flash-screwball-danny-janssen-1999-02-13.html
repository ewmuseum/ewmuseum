[FLASH] "Screwball" Danny Janssen

13 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a sample flash I wrote for one of my characters. I know that in some areas, the spelling is off, and it doesn't have the best conclusion. However, I did find it pretty amusing, and I get to unabashedly plug my other fed (the
WWO) and my other characters. If you have any comments or whatever, please feel free to respond!
"Screwball" Danny Janssen: [Danny Janssen and his brother Andy are standing in a men's room. Each are in identical stalls, obviously doing their thing. Both of the men arefacing the wall, as that seems to be the rule in the men's room. Danny is wearinghis normal attire, while his brother is clad in a worn out sweatsuit.
Occasionally, you can see other people come in to use the bathroom, theothers pass in front of Danny and his brother. Finally, after a couple ofminutes, the bathroom is empty, with the exception of Andy and Danny.]
DANNY: "I'm not sure, Andy. I don't think I'm doing so hot in the PCWF.What do you think?" ANDY: "Well, bro, I think you should stick it out. I mean, how manymatches have you fought in the PCWF anyway? Two? Three?"
DANNY: "It was two matches, and to be honest with you, they both sucked.I did pretty good in my first match, against that one guy, 'Pudbuddy' KrisStone, but I got screwed out of that one by that chick, Lice. And...hey wait a second! Where the hell were you in that match anyway? You saw her cominginto the ring with the chair! Why didn't you come in to help me?"
ANDY: "Well, um...I was...ah...Alright, I'll admit it: I was too busystaring at her tits to notice the chair."[Danny nods in obvious agreement]
DANNY: "Yeah, so was I, until she smacked me upside the head with thechair. No, I'm serious though! What the hell am I doing in the PCWF? Hell, Iwas the
Pacific Northwest Wrestling Association's People's Champion, forChrist's sake! I wrestled against guys like Tracy Hudson and Lord AlexanderBennington!
Now those dudes could kick serious ass! And where are they? " ANDY: [Shaking his head] "Well, they made it into the World Wrestling Organisation. From what I hear, they both signed multi-million dollar contracts with incentives."
DANNY: "See? Look at them! I beat both of them in the PNWA. And they get broadcast into 75 percent of the market. And look at me! Why can't I geta deal with the WWO?"
ANDY: "Look, you know why. I talked to Mark Stone myself. He said that there was no way in hell their federation was going to try to market awrestler who's so touched with Attention Deficit Disorder that he needs his older brother ringside to keep him concentrating on his matches. Besides, they couldn't quite figure out how to market a wrestler who's most known accomplishment to date has been winning a match after ingesting a ten-strip of blotter acid."
[Danny shrugs his shoulders] DANNY: "Oh well. So, I guess I'm stuck here. At least I get to look a tLice though...Man, what a hotty!"
ANDY: "But, Danny...Well, um...Mom and Dad, and I always thought thatyou were...well, you know."DANNY: "No. What?"
ANDY: "We always thought that you were gay." DANNY: "What? How could you think that? Jesus! Look, just because I'mtwenty- six, and still a virgin, doesn't mean I'm gay! It's not my fault! Imean, I like women and all. They just scare me. That's normal, right?"
ANDY: "Uh, no Danny, it isn't." DANNY: "Oh, did I ever tell you that dream I keep having about Martha Stewart? It's pretty cool, actually. In this dream, I'm sleeping in my bed,right? So,
I'm just laying there asleep, when I feel something whipping me across my butt..."
ANDY: [Has a look of horror on his face] "NO! Do not tell me anymore! Do you..." [Danny continues anyway] DANNY: "ANYWAY, I feel this whipping on my butt, and I wake up. When Iwake up, I see Martha Stewart standing over me, wearing a Wonder Woman costume.
Turns out she's whipping me with that golden lasso that Wonder Woman carries around. After I wake up, Martha starts wrapping that lasso around myneck, and licking my ear. Then, she starts asking me all these questions abouttable settings. Of course, I can't answer her. I mean, what do I know abouttable arrangements, right? Every time I tell her that I don't know, she says,'oh, that's alright. I'll teach you!'. Then, she..."
ANDY: "Stop! Oh dear God! I can't take this anymore! This is too sick! Hey, wait a second: Are you finished pissing yet?"
DANNY: " I finished pissing about five minutes ago." ANDY: [Looks a bit frustrated] "Oh, shit! I can't believe that you keptme in this stupid bathroom, talking about your sick bondage fantasies of Martha
Stewart, while we should have been talking about your next opponent!"
DANNY: "Dude, who cares about my next opponent? I don't even know who it is. And why does it matter to you anyway? Even if I win or lose, I still getpaid, and we both get to be on TV, right?"
ANDY: "Goodbye, Danny. I'm leaving now." DANNY: "Wait, I haven't told you about my idea for the Wolverine comic!" ANDY: [Is already through the front door] "GOODBYE!!!" DANNY: "Hey, wait! I'm coming! Give me a sec...FUCK! I have to piss again!Damn!" [Suddenly, Andy's voice is heard screaming behind the bathroom door.] ANDY: "BY THE WAY, YOU'RE FIGHTING JACK TWIST THIS SATURDAY." DANNY: "Oh...Okay." [Camera fades out here. This flash is over.]
