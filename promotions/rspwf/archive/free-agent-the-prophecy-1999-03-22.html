[Free Agent] "THE PROPHECY"

22 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The lights slowly fade down onto a single man resting against a locker door. His face is covered by his sweaty hands which are still taped up from his last match. With his body wrapped up in white towels and his feet sitting gently in his unlaced wrestling boots the man removes his hands to reveal his identity. Brushing his wavy brown hair out of his eyes he slowly stands up to acknowledge the reporters scattering around him. His locker reads Karma
Demise but he will soon become more well known to the world as the
"Prophecy". Karma begins to push his way through the reporters and he grabs a cold bottle of water from a shelf and takes a quick swig before addressing the crowd.]
Karma: Another victory in this pathetic sport of wrestling. How many times and how many different ways can I continue to pound on the same individuals over and over again. I'm growing sick and tired of the same faces pressed against the canvas under my massive biceps and I'm tired of beating on worthless opponent after worthless opponent. When will a new challenge arise to the occasion and step forth like a loyal and brave knight, when will a new federation be able to maintain such honor, strength, and beauty as myself.
Who will open their doors and take me inside, and when? [The hands of many reporters shoot up anticipating Karma to call on them but he ignores them all and continues to speak.]
Karma: What will it take to get myself recognized, who do I have to beat to become a so called contender in this world. I come with an open mind of suggestions and invitations welcoming all offers of any kind. But, who will put the biggest stakes on the table, who has the offer I can not and shall not refuse, what president has the federation and money to tie me down to wrestle for him and only him, who will it be and when?
[Again, hands from the reporters shoot to the sky and Karma finally acknowledges one of them.]
Karma: You, yeah you in the back, how may my knowledge be useful to you? [The reporter looks back with a blank look on his face.] Reporter: Ah, Karma, it is Karma isn't it... That's what I thought. How did you come by the name "The Prophecy", and what resemblence does your name have...
[Karma shakes his head at the stupidity of the question from the reporter.] Karma: You call yourselves professional yet you can not figure out the most simple things in life. Obviously a prophecy in this case is a prediction of some type, but mostly it means I am a sort of missionary sent out from someone. My name, Karma, to most meaning The total effect of a person's actions and conduct during the successive phases of the person's existence, regarded as determining the person's destiny. To simplify that for all of you morons it means your destiny. And finally, Demise, meaning The end of existence or activity, or to most, death! So to sum it for you, I'm basically here to takeover and destroy, and who will stand in my way. Yeah, you in the red shirt, what's your beef?
[The man looks down at his red shirt and speaks out loud.] Reporter: What federation would most interest you at this time? Karma: Mmmm, scratching his chin, to be honest it doesn't really matter. We've all heard of feds like the great IIWF that had talent beyond beliefs but since they closed their doors federations such as the SCRA have stepped up to takeover. If you are asking yourselves if I think I am top contender quality than ask yourselves no further because I know for a fact that I can hang with the best of them and defeat almost all of them. Am I cocky, no, just confident, and why shouldn't I be, just look at me!
[Karma displays his perfect body and bows to the crowd as he steps back away from the microphones. He looks back to the center camera and leaves us with these last words.]
