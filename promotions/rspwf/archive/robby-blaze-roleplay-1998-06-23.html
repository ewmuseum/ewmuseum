Robby Blaze RolePlay

23 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<You see a black and white screen pop up. Then, the sound comes in, it is rain drops hitting the cement and builds. On the streets of downtown Jersey. Then from the left of the screen. Robby Blaze walks by, and the camera follows him.
He is wearing a black trench coat, and his black IWC Robby Blaze t-shirt. "The
Shortest Straw" by Metallica starts to play lowly, and the camera still is following Blaze. Then Blaze comes to a rusty warehouse. He opens the big doors, and there sets a local indy feds wrestling ring. Then "The Shortest Straw"
stops playing, and that same indy fed's top prospect's music starts to play. That guy comes to the ring. Then Blaze takes his trench coat off, and gets inside the ring. They fight. It is a good match, but Blaze gets the victory, via "Blaze Bomber". Then Blaze sits in the middle of the ring, sweating heavily, and begins to speak.>>
Robby Blaze: Hello Dusty. Thats right, its your old friend Blaze. And when I say old, I mean old, because we are sertinitly not friends anymore, not after the way you turned on me! You remember that, dont you Dusty? If not, i'll refresh your memory. It was in the battle royal last Monday night. You and I were cleaning house, and then you hit me in the back of the head. Then you eliminate me. Now, what I wished you would have done, is tried to hit me, while
I was looking, becasue if you would have connected then, then we all would know that your not a little wezel, but since you insist on hitting people, while there not looking, then you are a wezel. Now that I got that out of the way, let me get on with what I am here to say.
Ya know something Dusty? When I was thinking of signing the contract to fight you, I thought to myself,"Should I really fight Dusty Love? He is really good."
Well, Dusty, I am glad that I sighned that papers, becaues I cant wait to beat you, and then the IWC is going to see how much of a great wrestler that I am, and how much of a looser that you are. Dusty, Danger Zone '98 isnt thta far away, and if I were you, then I would start praying to God, because once you get into the ring with me, you are going to see what I was talking about, when
I said that you lit a fuse in me. A fuse that I didnt know I had, and you sure as hell didnt know about. That fuse is what is going to keep me going, because every time that you are taking control in our match, I am just going to remember the fuse you lit, because that is going to be enugh motivation that I need to fight back, and eventually win the match. Dusty, I am going to lay it out like it is. You dont like me, and I dont like you. But there is one thing that we have in common, and that is, that we both cant stand each other. And when we fight, we are going to rip each others ears off, and hair out. But you know what? I love those types of matches, because it really shows who is the better man at the end of the match. It shows, who can take the most pain, and still have enugh energy to get up and walk out of the arena. It shows, how much guts and heart a person has for this business, and for thereselves. I have the heart, because without heart, you wouldnt make it in this industry. And I dont know if me, or you has the guts yet, I guess that we will have to Danger Zone to find that out.
There has never been a more anticipated match in my life time. Sure the first time I was in a main event, I was thrilled. And the first time I was in a title match, as a pro, I was happy too. But you know what Love? This isnt a title match, and this isnt a Main Event. So what do I have to be thrilled about? Well
I will tell you. When I pin Dusty Love...that is going to make me happy. When I hit every move that I have studied...that is going to make me thrilled. And when I counter every move that you throw at me...that is going to make me jump for joy. But hwen I get my hand raised, and the announcer says," The winner of this match, is Robby Blaze." Well, that is going to make me enthusiastic. That is going to make me touch the sky. That is going to make my day, and it is going to happen soon, as soon as Danger Zone '98.
You know Dusty. I work hard for every match that I am in, and it shows when I am in the ring. Studing tapes of my opponents, working out, fighting into exibition matches with young, old, and even wreslters as old as me. I do everyhting that is humanly possible to get ready for a match. And it all pays off. I love wrestling, and I love beating guys like you Dusty. Basically what im trying to say here Dusty, is that you need to come prepared for me...because
I am always prepared with my opponents.
<<Then Blaze pics up his trench coat, puts it on, and walks out of the warehouse. He shuts the door behind him, and starts walking.>>
