{Caide The Free Agent} A Brutal Rampage

16 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{ The screen remains black a few moments longer. Only the sound of a handheld bell echoes through the impenetrable blackness. Centered at the bottom of the screen, in red letters, the words "Seasons Greetings 1998" materialize. The camera escapes the darkness and comes to focus on a man dressed in a Santa
Claus suit. In his hand, he grasps the handheld bell - the source of the ringing noise. Santa stands in front of a gazeebo, complete with plastic elves, reindeer and a chair for him to sit and go over his list with the hundreds of children that will visit him this morn.}
"Ho ho ho...Merry Christmas....Merry Christmas!!" {Santa exclaims a multitude of times. He seems to never get tired of this monotonous fasaud. The bell never loses its pace, each ring keeping time with the last, like a fluid ballet.}
{A man dressed in a suit and tie, obviously a mall bigshot, strides over to
Santa and begins to speak to him in hushed tones.}
"The big man is coming today. Word around the campfire is that Mr. Caide knows about the money that we've...borrowed. At least you're safe...you'll have the kids to protect you...he'd never fire Santa in front of all these snot-nosed little bastards."
{Santa continues to ring his bell, still in the holiday spirit as he greets the customers, who walk to and frow like worker ants on a mission. He looks around for listening ears, then finally speaks.}
"Well, we were going to pay it back at the end of the week. I thought you said that no one would ever notice! It's not like we were putting Caide Plaza in the poorhouse with our little 'investment'."
"The point is, we could lose our jobs over this. I may be the manager, but i can still be replaced. And you...well...you're Santa, you're always replacable."
"Oh, ha ha...you're a barrel of laughs, you know that? I'm sure that Mr. Caide will be fully understandable, besides, he probably doesn't even notice. It's
Christmas, for God's sake. He always comes at Christmas time to check up on things and see what's going on."
{Some kids have began to line up waiting to have their picture taken with Santa during his conversation. He nods to the kids and then looks at the manager.
The manager nods to Santa and then makes his exit. Santa finally stops ringing his bell and takes a seat in his large, comfortable-looking chair.}
{An attractive young brunette lady makes her way to Santa. She is dressed as a helper, displaying all her curves in a skintight green outfit. Who ever said that Christmas wasn't sexual? She arrives to where Santa is sat with a concerned look on her face.}
"Sorry," she whispers, "I know i'm late, but my clock didn't go off. I must have set it for the wrong time or something. Anyway, i hear that Mr. Caide is coming today....this can't be good."
"Don't worry about it. Just start bringing up the kids." {The helper turns to the line of children, which has grown a substantial amount. She smiles to them.}
"Who's first?" she asks cheerfully. {One of the children makes his way to the helper. She puts her hand on his shoulder, guiding him along to Santa. The kid sits on Santa's lap and proceeds to tell him all the things he wants for Christmas as the helper takes a picture.}
{This event continues for another hour or so, before things fall apart...}
{Santa looks up to see four men...three are dressed in business suits, and a taller one - maybe about six feet eight inches tall. He wears only jeans,
T-shirt and a brown leather coat. There is a shorter man leading the group, a man with thinning hair. Glasses adorn a long nose that appears as if it had been borrowed from an anteater. The short man wipes his brow with a white handkercheif. Santa guessed that this must be Mr. Caide. He went through the speech that he had prepared while countless children told him of the "hot"
items this year. He wondered though why Mr. Caide needed a bodyguard.} {The men made their way through the crowd, and once they got to the front of the line where the helper was standing, the shorter man began to speak.}
"There will be a slight delay in photographs with Santa. We apologize, ladies and gentlemen, boys and girls, for the inconvenience."
{Then, the men made their way by the helper and up to Santa's gazeebo. Santa sat up straight and began to speak as the men looked at him with inquisitive looks. Santa looked directly into the eyes of the man with glasses.}
"Mr. Caide, i know that you're dissapointed in us. Its just that we needed to borrow the money to pay off some debts that we had obtained. There is no reason, though to be alarmed. We are certainly going to pay back the money that we owe you with this weeks wages. I'm sure that you can understand how things are during the holidays..."
{The tall man stepped forward and put his hand on the chest of the small man with glasses, as if holding words in his lungs. The tall man had short, curly blonde hair. His icy blue eyes wore that wild look that always forces other people to lose when it came to staring contests. Then, unexpectedly, the tall man spoke.}
"I'M Mr. Caide." {Then, without warning, Caide began to hammer away on the side of the head of
Santa. Rights followed by rights, followed by rights. Santa soon fell from his chair and onto the floor of the gazeebo. Then, Caide stomped and kicked
Santa until it seemed as if he would move no longer. The people in the mall began to scream and yell with surprise and excitement. Children turned away from the onslaught before them.}
{Caide pulled Santa to his feet and through him through the plywood wall of the gazeebo, sending him onto the cold mall floor. Santa started to get up, but
Caide hammered him in the head again with rights. Eventually Santa's beard, glasses and hat fell off....and Santa moved no more. Caide then made his way back through the hole in the gazeebo that he had made. He arrived where
Santa's chair sat, and picked up a gift from the pile of gifts on the gazeebo floor. It was a small box, wrapped with green and red paper. He made his way to where the men in suits and the helper stood.}
{The helper looked up at Caide with wide eyes, fearful of what would happen to her. Caide only stared back, with wide blue eyes. Then, he slowly handed the small box to the helper.}
"Merry Christmas....Emily, was it?" "Yes....yes sir.." {Emily carefully took the box and unwrapped it, trying to keep an eye on Caide. Once she was finished, her heart skipped a beat. Emily drew forth a diamond necklace from the box. She guessed it to be valued at about 1200 dollars or so. She looked up, her eyes wide, at Caide.}
"Do you like it?" "Oh yes!! Very much, sir." "Well, its yours. Why don't you come with us. We have further business to discuss. Anker, get another Santa. Today."
{Anker, the man with the glasses nodded his head.} {The camera focuses on the destroyed gazeebo, with Santa lying off to the side on his back. Then it fades into darkness once again. The crimson words appear again on the bottom of the screen. "Seasons Greetings 1998"}
