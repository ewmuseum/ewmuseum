[Knick Man Heel Roleplay] Comments...Constructive Criticism appreciated...

3 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens high above the beautiful ocean waters of the Caribbean Islands, located about 100 miles south of The United States. We slowly get an aerial view of a large beach front as the beach begins to come closer to the moving camera view. Slowly and slowly we begin to get a better and better view of the beach until we finally get a glimpse of the shore itself. On the shore, we see a large mountain area providing the background for this area of seclusion and luxury. However, what catches our interest is a huge, castle-sized house which stands alone, high above the coastal area. We also see a long road which leads from the house to the beach area. At the end of the road, there is a black range-rover jeep parked towards the beginning of the beach. The camera continues to turn along the beach until we come to glimpse upon the owner of all of these magnificent items. We see a tall, bald black man, a man that WWO fans have come to know...and HATE! He is The One, The Only, THE KNICK MAN!
The Knick Man lays silently on an official "Knick Man Championship
Beach Chair", the only clothing on his body being a pair of black
"Knick Man Championship Swimming Trunks" He is reading a book entitled "The Winner Within" by Pat Riley. The Knick Man turns a page and continues to read until he lifts his eyes up off the page and looks upon the camera. The Knick Man slowly closes the book, places it down at his side and begins to speak.)
Knick Man: The Winner Within! Pat Riley couldn't describe it better! That's exactly what I'm all about folks. Winning! Some people think
I'm about World Domination and Glorification. They think that I want everyone to get down on their hands and knees and worship me. While that might be all fine and dandy, that's not what the entire story is about. WWOites, my only purpose in this federation is to win matches.
That's all I give a damn about. That's all I know how to do. I'm a winner, ladies and gentlemen! And I'm DAMN GOOD AT IT!
(The Knick Man pauses, a look of contemplation on his face, then continues.) Knick Man: And you know, there's another man in this federation who could have also been a winner. You people may know him best as the
WWO Television Champion, Double Down! This kid had all the tools necessary to be a great individual in this sport. All he had to do was to HUMBLE himself. Submit himself unto MY wishes, MY desires, and
I would have lead this kid to the top. My only request? The WWO
Television Championship!
That's all I wanted Double Down. All you had to do was give me the TV belt, and you would have been made a instant superstar in this sport.
I could have provided you with all the finest things in life. Money! Clothes! Hoes! Bankrolls! Whatever your heart desired, it could have been fulfilled. With my large financial background, I could have given you "the kingdoms of this world" And all you had to do was recognize me as the man that gave it to you!
But NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO...... You couldn't do things MY WAY! You couldn't follow MY ORDERS! You had to ATTACK me on National Television! Try to take the SPOTLIGHT that RIGHTFULLY belongs to me! Now that's a sin that I could have forgiven Double Down. That's something that I could have let slide....
(The Knick Man stands up off the chair and begins to slowly walk along the sand. The camera follows obediently as he continues.)
Knick Man: But when you had the AUDACITY to come out of your face and tell these WWO fans that......
(points to the camera) YOU!!!!!!!!!!! (points to himself) COULD BEAT ME?????????? (laughs loudly) Knick Man: That's where you crossed the point of no return my friend! So now, at Title Wave! On a beach very much like this private playground of mine, you will get the opportunity to back up your words with ACTIONS! Unfortunately, you have written a check with your mouth that your BODY cannot cash!
Ya see Double D..... NOBODY ON THIS PLANET CAN OUTWRESTLE ME! Ya might say to yourself, "heck, Iron Man got a win over this guy. I beat Iron Man. Therefore, I can beat The Knick Man!" And it's that kind of STINKIN THINKING that's going to get a young man like yourself
SERIOUSLY HURT when it's all said and done. Iron Man's suppose "win"
was a FLUKE! He didn't "outwrestle me" He pourd coffee on me! He did not earn it on his own skills, and it is something that he could
NEVER accomplish again! He knows it, I know it! And....
(smiles at the camera) Knick Man: Deep down in your heart, YOU KNOW IT! So I hope your satisfied with what you did. I hope you enjoy your little 15 minutes of fame. Enjoy it now kid.....
(stares intently at the camera) Knick Man: Because on August 30th, I'm going to bring you back down to reality. MY REALITY! The reality that states that I am The
Greatest Basketball Player, The Greatest Wrestler, and The Greatest
Man on the face of the earth! And the reality that you are going to have to deal with, when I give you a DOUBLE portion of BEATDOWN and
TAKE what should have been rightfully mines a long time ago....
The WWO Television Championship! You know the name kid. The One, The Only..... (smiles that classic Knick Man Championship Smile) Knick Man: THE KNICK MAN!!!!!!! (The camera gets one final glimpse of The Knick Man standing on the beach, arms folded, in that confident, superior manner, before the scene fades to black.)
The Knick Man [WWO, HHWO] (1998 Record: 29-9-3) WWO World Television Champion #74 1997 RSPWF Top 200 RSPWF Snapshots (1998) #7 Top Wrestler, #3 Top Face MVGJ (1998) #10 Top African-American Wrestler "I'm not arrogant, I'm just THAT DAMN GOOD"
