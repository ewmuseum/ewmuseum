|<FREE AGENT>|=====> Shade

17 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I am posting this for a friend of mine, Dave. Many of you may know him from IRC as "[detail withheld]." Anyhow, he is unable to post on RPSWF at the moment, therefore, I'm posting one of his flashes for him. Because of his inaccessiblilty to the board, please send him any and all comments regarding his work, and he is willing to join a federation, as well. He can be reached at...
[detail withheld] Please contact him if interested in picking him up for your federation, or if you think he would be a great addition to a federation you are currently participating in. Thank you for your time. Below is the flash...
=========================================== ...SCENE... - The clouds have fallen, and the dark skies hang above. The camera pans infront of an old church, where a man is just sitting down. As the camera zooms in, black boots are shown, followed by torn ripped jeans, and then the man is identified when his "Shade, Tormented and loving it"
t-shirt is shown. Finally his face is shown, and you can see no facial expression on his face, as his hair is tied back, and his shades are covering his eyes. He looks to the camera and begins to speak. -
Shade: So shall it be written, so shall it be done. [Shade Pauses.] Shade: So shall it be written, so shall it be done. Written words, are merely actions.. which can not be expressed, expressed in a manner so
unrevealing, that it pains one to see. So finally, you spoke, truer words never been spoken, yet I sense a fear, from a time of words not spoken, and actions not seen. You seem, to persist that your a blue blood, yet we all know, that the blood which runs in your veins, is not a color of choice, but a clear image of who you are. Illlusions leads to tears, yet those tears are clear, so is the site of pain clear? No, it's a justification of what is pure, and what is not. Sleep takes the soul's consciousness, while falling into the world of black, nothing is seen or heard. Is sleep black?
No. It's clear, just not knowing where are who you are, puts you into a world of darkness, but the shade, is as clear as the skies are grey.
Shade: You might not be from around here Earl, but realize that around here, things are done differently, your blood may not be blue, but when you bleed, it will be the same color, as my blood, when I was in the streets being beaten, being attacked. Our worlds will clash, and you see, I don't
understand, social grace, the human race, colors, shapes or sizes. I do see the different shades though, and if your black or white, I can still see the lighter or darker side of you. So you call yourself a blue blood, but after you look into my eyes, you will see what is true, you will see yourself.
Shade: There was once a time, that I cared, I woke from sleep, ready to face the day. Context from life. But, now I count the days, to my death. I pray that it will come soon and swiftly. Why, for I don't know. It's now a quest, that I seek life, and in life I seek redemption, and in redemption I seek you. Earl, it is not that I hate you, or have anything against you, it is that you are standing on a path, which leads to the promise land, a path that I must take.
Shade: Maybe the words I speak, you do not understand, but understand this. ALOW, when all the lights are turned on, and we both enter into that ring, you must realize that, on that day, your final journey has begun, and my quest was just shortened, as I took a shortcut, into the promise land, So shall it be written, so shall it be done. So it was said, so we shall see the outcome.. and so the skies, darkened, and so this is the truth... Don't hide behind a shadow, for there is only one place, you must seek... home.
- Camera fades to black. - =================================== Once again, if you like this enjoyed this segment, please contact [detail withheld] with questions or comments. Thank you.
