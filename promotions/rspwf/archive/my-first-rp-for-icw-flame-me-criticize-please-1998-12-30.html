My first RP for ICW...Flame Me, Criticize Please!!!!

30 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The camera pans in as it reveals a busy Las Vegas strip, from the positioning of the sun and the fact that it is light out any normal human being can tell it is the afternoon, 2:43PM to be exact. People in suits, men with fur-laced pimp coats, women with hardly any clothing at all, all these types of people crowd the street waiting for taxis, "clients", etc..
The camera starts to focus in as it comes to a screeching left pivot to reveal a guy standing at the outside corner of an alley. From far away, and quite frankly even from close up it appears to be a normal guy, but it really is newcomer Devon Case. As people walk past him one man stops, notebook in hand.)
Reporter: (walks up to the man) Excuse me, sir? Oh my God, you are Devon
Case! Devon Case:Hey, shut the hell up, keep your voice down, we don't need a Case riot right now, that thing went out of style after the Beatles separated.
What the hell do you want? Reporter:An interview, I got a pen and paper, an exclusive for the Vegas
Sun. We can--- (Devon Case cuts in.) Devon Case:Uh-uh, hell no, Devon Case doesn't do interviews for lowly scum like yourself, get that? Now get out of here before you wind up in the hospital.
(The reporter takes a look around at the busy streets and smirks.) Reporter:An interview, with me, or I yell Devon Case and everybody and there grandmother will be here and everywhere you go for the rest of the day. I didn't want this to become a bribe, but you made it that way.
Devon Case:A bribe, huh? (looks around at all the people who are, ok, all Case fans) Devon Case:This must be your lucky day, but we do it somewhere quieter, like farther down the alley.
Reporter:Cool by me, lets go. (They walk down the alley, and find a spot where nobody can overhear their conversation. Devon breaks the silence.)
Devon Case:Let's get on with this, Precious is supposed to pick me up out on the street in about 5 minutes, so you best be quick and nimble or your story will be nothing more than exclusive garbage.
Reporter:Ok, I get your drift. We here at the Vegas Sun have gotten reports that you, Devon Case, has recently signed a contract with the ICW, a place where legends are truly born. Is this speculation or truth?
Devon Case:Is this speculation or truth? You are a horrid reporter you know that. Damn, I know Vietnam vets with Shellshock that could do btter than you.
Reporter:Truth or speculation? Devon Case:Well, since you really want the answer....yes I have signed a contract with the ICW. In fact out of all the contract negotiations I have had with past organizations, none has treated me with the respect I deserved better than the ICW. They are a real class act, too bad they don't know exactly what they just signed to.
(The reporter's smile changes to an odd grin. He is silent for a few seconds while Devon Case has a gloating smirk then speaks.)
Reporter:I am not quite sure that I understand what you just said. Can you do a little clarification of sorts to help me out here?
Devon Case:Damn! What is wrong with you. I am trying to give you a meaty interview and you keep screwing it up like a complete moron, should I spell it for you?
(The reporter backs off a bit and then lowers his voice in content.) Reporter:Please, could you just tell me exactly what you meant, Mr.Case? Devon Case:There you go, Mr.Case, I like the sound of that. What I meant there tough guy is this. The ICW has no idea what kind of star they have on there hands. Like past feds, they see this youngster, a 22 year old coming out of the woodwork and think to themselves....well, he's good-looking, has good mic skills, and can handle himself in the ring, maybe we could use him to stir up the pot.
(Devon Case gets an accomplished, determined, confident look upon his face) Devon Case:What they don't say because they don't understand is this. Devon
Case is the future of not only our federation, but the sport of professional wrestling. It is that simple. Plain, direct, to the point, and 100% truthful and honest, isn't that what sports lack nowadays anyway, a true legend of his time. That will be me, whether I have to beat every single b-rate ICW wrestler in the ring 1, 2, 3 or whether I injure their skinny twigs one by one, the result will come around ten-fold, with the same result.
(The reporter walks over towards a dumpster and plops a seat onto it, a funny smirk tattooed across his face.)
Reporter:And what's that, let me guess Devon Case, the new ICW International
Champion blah blah blah.
(Devon Case's head whiplashes with a devilish grin towards the reporter, as he starts to slowly walk towards him.)
Devon Case:Are you trying to put words in my mouth? Huh? I hate it when people put words in my mouth!
(The reporter gets a bit frightened and starts to shudder) Reporter: Naa-, No Mm..mm.m.m..Mr.Case, sorry. Devon Case:Better be you little punk. I never, ever mentioned a title did
I? Nope. I am here to take this fed by storm and that doesn't always come through title reigns, although those are in the future of one Devon Case, but it comes through beating the crap out of people thought bigger and better than you, whether in the ring or in the parking lot. It doesn't really matter, its all the same. There will be a day when the elite of ICW think of me as an equal, but until that day comes I must do my gruntwork and work my way up, although I will do it swiftly and precisely.
Reporter:Any clues about who your first victim will be? (Devon Case cracks the reporter with a left hook to the jaw, knocking the man unconscious) Devon Case:No comment. (The camera pans down the alley to show a car pull up and Devon gingerly walks into the passenger side and it hightails off.)
