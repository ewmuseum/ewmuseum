Please Critique my RP

22 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please critique this RP anyway you'd like. I'm pretty resilient. It's not directed in anyone in particular since I'm not in a fed, but, whatever.
--- [Simon Snide, half-moon spectacles balancing at the end of his knurly nose, sat; his wiry frame poised in an antique armchair behind a bedraggled desk, perusing a book titled Things to do when you’re Evil. Only a sole desktop lamp illuminated the words and his vile countenance, like a horrid little spotlight.]
[After a few seemingly prolonged seconds, Snide’s ebon dominated eyes peer up and noticed the camera. An acrid scowl came across his face. He blandly placed the book atop the desk, and swung the cover over, closing it with an audible poof and a cloud of grime. He removed his spectacles and folded them up, then placing them atop the book, the sneer never abandoning his face.]
SS: Something brought to my attention of late, has, how do you say, pinched a nerve of mine. I was in the midst of my weekly excursion through the local market, purchasing every villain’s favorite cereal, Count Chocula, when through the bastion of stacked breakfast treats, I heard two lowly housewives griping to each other.
[Snide’s face grew darker. His lips thinned, and his eyebrows contorted, slowly taking the form of the letter V.]
SS: ‘My son’s been running around with those hoodlums again. He’s coming in late every night. His grades are slipping.’ Blah, blah, blah. I immediately grinned at the notion of some obsequious mother shoveling down Prozac for desert each night, when my unusual sense of content was shattered by what the other woman squeaked. ‘It’s those evil boys, they’re a bad influence.’
[Snide clapped both his tortuous, moldy-nailed hands to his rounded mouth, and shuddered as if someone just told him evil was going out of style.]
SS: ‘Evil boys’? EVIL BOYS? The adjective I have held in such high esteem was being used in descriptions of juvenile, prepubescent boys and their meek pranks? Pish posh! Only a society so daft as this, which I so reluctantly dwell in, can have construed the meaning of such a brilliant word! I instantaneously dashed homeward to scour my books for the origin of such ineptness. That’s when I came across a handy little book my some bloke by the name of
Webster. ‘Morally bad’! ‘Wicked’! That’s how he defined evil! [Snide walloped the desk, arousing a dusty mist, which danced back down like pepper. He scoffed.]
SS: It swiftly became quite obvious that this Webster’s only experience of so-called evil was a timid peek into the girl’s bathroom in his elementary school days. Otherwise, it would be wholly lucid to this man that evil in indefinable. It’s an ineffable brew of vehemence and humor that only those whom have been overcome with it can comprehend. It’s not the snatching of a baby’s lollipop, which I must had, was surprisingly amusing. It’s not the donning of black gowns, ill-looking face paintings, or imbecilic piercing. Never let yourself mistake a confused teenager with elementary household quandaries for an evil heart. Evil is much greater.
[The grimace lurches back upon his face.] SS: I’m evil.. It’s a state of mind, really, one I possess. It’s the thinking that no one on this unpleasant little planet deserves your courtesy but yourself—not you mother, your father, you sibling, your slutty girlfriend who would pleasure you within the walls of a convent if you egged her on enough, or in my case, your wife.
[A clang rattled off camera, and Snide’s eyes darted off to the side. With the sound of feet growing behind him, he wheeled his moaning chair around to take a look. In trudged in his butterball-like wife. Her plump face was speckled with puss emitting scratches. Her legs moved along slowly like thick, rolled-up carpets, and her breasts hung over her belly button like two full Christmas stockings.]
SS: Oh, you.. what do you want, Malifarious.. WENCH! You’re interrupting my lecture on society’s foolishness! M: I reckoned you called for me, sweetness. SS: No! No! No! Does it appear like I’m about to test some horrible flesh-eating potion and am in need of a guinea pig? Does it look like I’m amidst a hail of bullets and need human armor?
No! So, be gone, ugliness, before I spew whatever nastiness lurks in my bowels. [With that, the hideous woman slogged out of the room with a shrug. Snide rolled his apathetic eyes.]
SS: That’s it. Cut. Damn my wife and her grave timing. Until next time, students. Adieu. [Blackness usurps the screen as Snide resumes the reading of his book.] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
