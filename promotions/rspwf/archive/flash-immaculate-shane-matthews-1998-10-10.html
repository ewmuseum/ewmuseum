[Flash] "Immaculate" Shane Matthews

10 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
If you read the "[Flash] Shawn Parker" post from StunnaTime, you saw his little soliloquy on why wWw still has 2 or 3 real wrestlers. Thes jobrones won't be around for another week, and me and StunnaTime are taking it into our own hands to eliminate these losers. This RP is me kicking the ass of Rob Van Dam. It's from wWw, for the whole run-down and explanation on wWw, read "[Flash] Shawn
Parker" Also, I was told there is another Shane Matthews roaming around somewhere, and he happens to have my name. Big deal, I don't know who used it first, but Shane Matthews is my real name, so no apologies, because it isn't copying, it's my damn name. Thank you.
^^^^^^^^^^"Immaculate" Shane Matthews^^^^^^^^^^ [The screen opens to a backstage wWw interview area. On the wall is a banner thasays, "wWw: If You Don't Like It, We Don't Care!" Shane Matthews stands against a wall. He has his blond-ish hair tied back in a ponytail. He has a black "Poison" T-shirt on. He is limping somewhat as he paces around with an evil smile on his face. The camera pans down, and you can see a small ankle cast on his right leg. He is somewhat out of breath, and in the background you can hear the crowd roaring. A graphic flashes on the screen, saying, "Taped
Immediately After "Immaculate" Shane Matthews' attack on Rob Van Dam at Monday
Night Jericho, 10/5.".......]
SM: You all saw what just happened, Rob Van Dam saw what happened. He thinks he can one-up me? Last week, when I was lying there one the ground, pain searing through my leg. I had to even stifle a chuckle.
[Matthews grins, rubbing his chin.] "A chuckle?" You ask. Yeah, a chuckle. And no, I'm not some sadistic oddball who enjoys pain. Fact of the matter is, I had to stifle a chuckle because when I saw that greenhorn Rob Van Dam trying to pull a fast one on the master, well, you could say it, tickled my Funny Bone.
[Matthews pats his elbow, or "Funny Bone."] You see, Al Pacino said in The Godfather III, "Everytime I think I'm out, they pull me back in." Well, if I may take the basic premise of that quote, and restructure it a tad to fit to Rob Van Dam, it'd go a little like this,
"Whenever I think I'm in control, I'm more out of control than I've ever been." You see, "Mr. Monday Night", last Monday, just when YOU thought that YOU were in control, the master showed you that you're really just a pawn in an
Immaculate Game of Chess. [He laughs.] You do not, I repeat, DO NOT, f*ck with Shane Matthews and turn and walk away struttin' like you're the Royal damn Rooster. Ever heard of the Golden Rule,
Robby? "Do unto others as you would have them do unto you", is what I believe it says. Well, now I'm gonna teach you the Immaculate Rule, and this is the creed which I conduct my career according to. And it goes like this, "Only do unto others, when you're too good for them to do anything to you." Obviously, you weren't good enough to prevent me from exacting my revenge. Oviously, you or your walking asscrack of a manager weren't smart enough to stop me from getting a little payback. And right now as I speak this, you're out lying in center ring, gripping your knee, screaming for your mommy.
[Matthews raises one finger in the air.] You broke rule #1, Robby! Sammy Hagar said it best when he wrote the lyrics to Van Halen's "Finish What Ya Started." You didn't, and when you don't tie up all the loose ends, one of them is gonna come back and trip you up sooner or later.
[He crosses his arms.] In your case, it was sooner......But it's far from finished. See, I started something on Monday, that being me self-delivering your realization that you aren't the end-all of wrestling superstars. So, you bring eveything you've got to Oakland, California, you bring your little manager, you bring your mystery cornerman, and I'll have my own.
[He scowls menacingly.] But when you chip away all the excess, it's me and you. I've proven that I'm smarter than you, I've proven that I am the Master, and at Autumn Extreme, I prove that I'm perfect, I prove that I'm playing on a higher level, or in other words, that I'm....
[Runs his thumb across his throat.] F'N Immaculate. [Fade to black.] ~~~~~~~~~ [name withheld] Handler of "Immaculate" Shane Matthews Former and Future wWw World Champion If ya likes tha dubbaya dubbaya dubbaya(wWw) get in touch with me or StunnaTime. wWw rocks, it has some awesome, underrated talent, but damn, are there some hots feuds and cool matches, so, email me, IM me, whatever, and I'll make sure I hook you up with an app from the Prez, ICONisCJ. Later.
