Critique (sorta) - XHCW On-Carder

11 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I should give some background on my char. His name is Jeremy Cross, but people only know him as the person who attacked Chris Jacobson(my old char) at XHCW Counterstrike and threw him off the entrance stage.
If possible, I'd like a critique of the actual rp then whether you think he is a Nazi or not. Then I'll give an answer of how I wrote him to be and why I _absolutely love_ this character.
======== #Today is gonna be the day that I‘m gonna swing it back to you #By now, you shoulda somehow realized what‘cha gotta do. #I don‘t believe that anybody feels the way I do about‘cha now.. (The arena plunges into darkness as Wonderwall by Oasis continues. The fans, in a state of confusion look interestingly at the big screen. It lights up a gray color, with an orange cross pulsating from the middle to the edges of the screen. Suddenly one lonely spotlight illuminates the ring, then travels up the ramp, stopping on a figure standing on the stage. The figure, arms spread wide, in the shape of a cross keeps his bald head down, before quickly flicking it up and spitting water out of his mouth simultaneously. He then, head raised walks down the ramp, and hops into the ring, before grabbing a microphone. The fans stand in shock at this new face in front of them. He lifts the microphone to his mouth, and opens his mouth, but before any utterance comes from his lips he drops his arm. The man, clad in an orange boiler suit, undone upto the waist, showing his huge pecs, biceps and abs to a perfection. He picks his right arm up, and raises it above his head, calling for silence. He then drops it to his mouth, as he speaks.)
Man: Shut up. (The fans immediately start booing this monster, who speaks with a linger of a German accent, as Wonderwall fades slowly out of the fans minds. The arena, still dark, is now brimming with heel heat for this unknown man.)
Man: I have destroyed a former XHCW champion in my fleeting appearance a month or so ago, and I am back to inflict more pain.
(The big screen cuts, leaving no flashing cross but quickly, in it’s place fades in a movie, with the text “XHCW
Counterstrike” in the bottom right corner. The camera focuses in on this as the scene is set. It is the long-gone Chris Jacobson delivering his comeback promo to the huge crowd.)
++Jacobson: It's great to be back Jim. Bungalo Jim Bob: As the fans at home know, you've been on hiatus with an injury from your last match in XHCW. Now you're back. What are you going to do? Jacobson: Well Jim, I had a hell of a time here, and it's not stopping. Not by a long sh- (Suddenly the voice cuts off as a man runs up from behind Jacobson, and hurls him off the stage. Jacobson falls from the stage, flipping and landing hard back-first on a bunch of electrical equipment. The arena goes quiet as the power cuts. The camera shows nothing but darkness!)
Zack Michaels: What the- Todd Anderson: IT JUST THREW JACOBSON OFF THE STAGE!! Zach Michaels: How the- Todd Anderson: Fans, we'll get the electricity restored as soon as we can. For now, lets go to a promo...something...
(A shot is seen for the next XHCW Pay Per View. The cameras cut back to the backstage area, where Jacobson is being loaded into an ambulance.)++ Man: There I am, finishing his career. Anyone want to doubt me on that? Thought not. My name is Jeremy Cross, and you will see more of me in the future. I will take this federations stars out one by one, until I dominate. I will be the supreme power!
(Cross basks in the “ASSHOLE” chant rounding the arena.) Cross: Yes, you puny Americans, with your stupid chants...I can use your weakness too....What?
(The fans start throwing drinks containers and all around crap in the ring at the belittling of their favorite chant.)
Cross: Oh, you don’t like that do you? You see in Germany, we are content on one thing and one thing only. And NO it is not watching pornography or making love to our bulls on our ranch, it is reaching perfection. And perfect I am. But away from telling you the truth about your Home Land, you must be weeping to see Sweet America being challenged but please, move on. It will only be a small amount of time before your country will be ruled by the almighty, but for now you will have to see XHCW rot like an apple, bending down backwards as I strain your “greatest”. I will be the dominant force in
XHCW. I will see that you live by the Cross! (Cross drops the mic and leaves the ring to more boos as the show gets underway.)
