He is sort of buff but not really he does have muscles though * Ring attire: Black Trunks with the Words Champ going down the leg in Green * Attitude: Very cocky and arrogant he thinks he can whoop everyone and he probly can * Wrestler History: He was Intericertonal Champ, United States Champ, North American champ, Hall of Famer of the w3o. * Experience?: He was once part of the greatest stable ever The EXecutioners * Titles held: Intericertioal Champ, United States, North American * Finishing move: Rolling German Suplex * Describe: he does a German Suplex 3 times and on the last one he bridges it * Do you have a manager or valet? yes * If so, will he\she interfere? yes * Describe manager\valet: It is a male who is about 6'0 and he can take up for him self * What are your advantages and/or disadvantages? Europen Uppercut [Talon sits between two gorgeous blonds with his long blond hair waiving in the wind. He starts to stare at the sunset in west Bel Air where his estate is located. He begins to speak.]
Talon: Everywhere I look all I see is dirt and grime. There is a man who walks up to me every day and this man asks me for change. Being the kind of man I am I have to say NO to this hopeless man before me. People ask me "how can you do this?" "How can you be so indescribably horrible to the people who are less fortunate then yourself?" Well I have one thing to say to these people. I have worked my entire life for what I have and what I have accopleached. This man that comes up to me is not always the same man but to me these people might as well be. They all look, act, smell alike just
like everyone else who has ever came up and begged me for help. [Talon still sitting in his chair begins to speak again.] Talon: All of these people who don't have any money or don't have a house brought it upon themselves. And these people who believe that I should give these poor people my money. They are crazy. These people have never worked a day in there lives or they are stupid and go blow there money on cheap booz and even cheaper women. I have no compassion for these people. They walk up to me and their faces are covered with mud, dirt, and grime. There closes have the rank of urine and sweat from the Bel Air heat. I look in these people's hair and I see maggots eating there flesh like it was a piece of cow manure that had just been laid. You sit there and say that I should have compassion for these "poor lost souls." Well i say that I am a man full of hate a deceit for everything or everyone who gets in my way. "Hate, some say that hate is a worthless emotion that keeps people from reaching there highest potential. But for a man like Talon, A man whos accopleshments are far reached those of a normal man HATE IS EVERYTHING!"
Talon: I walk down teh street and I see these very people that I just told to get lost. I saw there house. It is not a house it is a shack. It is a dumpster, it is a cardboard box. I ponder to myself as i walk by how can these people live like this? When I wake up and I am not feeling my best I can always think about my possessions but these people can't believe in anything hey have nothing to believe in. I again start to wonder how these people, if they do at all, how they have any faith? Expecally these people who have been delt such a crappy hand for life. Then suddlenly I sort of slap myself in the face and say "You don't care about these people. You don't give a damn what they do." That is exactly how i feel. That is why I am so much better than everyone else I have see what it is like to be poor and I hate it and I will work harder then anyone else to keep what i have and that is why I am so much better then everyone else.
[Talon now quits talking and begins to stare at the sunset in west Bel Air.]
