Basically here's the deal...I was in a good small fed, it died, and now I can't find a fed worth anything. I'd love to join a fed with smaller numbers, I dont want to join a fed with 900 members, something around 15-20 would be great, but if it's a good fed I would go for more. As far as roleplaying is concerned, I won't do board feds, I don't have the time nor do I want to put in the effort of posting and going there everyday to read this and that, so it must be an e-mail fed. I don't particularly care how the RP's are presented, either from the RPer themself, sent in a packet, or how ever else you want to do it, but I don't want to have to do more than two rp's per week due to time constraints.
Thats about it, I'm big on being in a fed with good angles and knows where it's going, And in closing, here is my application...
Character Name: Bishop Real Name: Derrian Morris Height: 5'8" Weight: 175 Ring Attire: White latex pants, all white boots, occasionally an XXL white t-shirt and a pristine white plastic mask that covers his whole head, no hair can be seen. He comes to the ring in a large white robe.
Gimmick: Instead of being a dark, satanic guy, he's a semi-dark, non-satanic guy. Finishing Move: Salvation - Matt Hardy's "Side Effect" Other Moves: Sit Down Jaw Breaker (Like Jeff Hardy's), Cradle Piledriver,

History: Unknown. Not much to be known, or found out about this wrestler, it's obvious he's had a vast array of training, but nobody really knows where he came from.
Also, I have my own special entrance for him, but I've found that most feds have their own special way of doing it or whatever, but if you want it, i have it, i also have a _FEW_ samples, i didn't get the opportunity to use this character for a long time, but I am really looking for a place that I can use him, i'm not the greatest, but I definately know what I am doing. So if you're fed is looking for somebody new, then holla at ya boy!
