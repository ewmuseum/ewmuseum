[Flash] "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor

21 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please give me some feedback on the flash. I could really use the constructive criticism. Thanks.
[Scene: Hartford, Connecticut. Time: 11:00 P.M. THe scene fades into the locker room of "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor, right after the latest WCWA
Saturday Supreme. There are a few reporters that are in the room, and are just waiting to get to ask their questions to the Light Heavyweight Champion.]
[Vince is sitting in a chair, facing the reporters. His Light Heavyweight
Title sits on a nearby table. He is now in a pair of black mesh shorts. They are a little baggy, and come down just past his knees. On his upper body is a gray t-shirt that on the front in black says, "Ladies Man." The shirt fits rather tight, so it shows off his incredibly built upper body. A small white towel is resting on his shoulder, and he pauses and wipes off his forehead.
Concealing his eyes are his trademark, gold framed Rayban sunglasses. He looks out at the camera and the reporters, and then begins to speak...]
"The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor: Laides and gentlemen, lets get this show on the road. I know that you all don't want to be here at this time of the night, and
I have some women to meet in a little while.... and I definitely don't wanna be late for that. So anybody that has some intelligent questions, go ahead and shoot.
[A reporter steps forward and begins to ask a question...] Reporter: Well Vince, you were impressive tonight in your match up. But you looked as if you started off slow. Do you care to comment on what that was, or if it will affect you in the future? And do you have any more overall comments on your match?
[Vince cracks a small, sly grin. He then begins to answer the question...] "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor: Yeah, I guess I did come out a little slow. But it happens to the best of 'em, [Smiling.] and that couldn't be more true if it happened to me. And I guarantee that it would happend to almost everybody in this federation if they were stepping into the ring with a man like Chris
Carter. He came with everything he had, right from the start.. and I have to give him credit for that. But will it affect me in the future? No, not a chance. It happens to everybody, and I will definitely learn from what I did tonight.
[He pauses, and it looks as if he isn't going to answer the second part of the question. So the reporter urges him...]
Reporter: And any overall thoughts on the match with Carter? [Vince shakes his head and continues...] "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor: Well, you saw the match. It might have been the best match this federation has ever seen. After stepping into the ring with both Carter and Kidd, I honestly don't see how Carter lost his belt to Kidd in the first place. But he did, and I had to beat both of their a$$es, which I probably would have had to done anyway, just in a different order.
Carter came out hot, right from the start. He honestly had control of the match at the beginning. But you can't stop me, you can only hope to contain me. And as usual, the inevitable happened. I started to shine. I took control of the match, and control of who the title would be leaving with. It happens every time.... You may look like your gonna beat me, but you never do.
Then I just got sick of messing with him, so I took Carter up to the top rope. And then its lights out for anybody that gets hit with a "Money Maker." And thats exactly what hit him, and I guarantee you that he's in his locker room right now still feeling the effects of that move. And then it was ended by me getting the 1..2..3. Next question
[Vince stops and wipes his forehead with the towel from his shoulder. He then adjusts how he is sitting in his seat, and looks more comfortable. Another reporter steps forward and begins to ask the next question...]
Reporter: Well as I'm sure you already know, "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd is now the number one contender for your belt. And this Wednesday at In Da House, you will be defending your belt against him. Would you comment on anything involving this?
[Vince removes his sunglasses and starts to slightly twirl them in his left hand. He then starts to speak...]
"The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor: Well Floyd pulled off the victory against Kidd. Which I am glad he did, because we all know that it wouldn't be much of a match if I had to wrestle Kidd. But somehow Freddy got the job done.
Floyd has had some success in the WCWA. He made it to the finals of the World
Title Tournament, where he was dessimated by Westside Walavan. And now he is the number one contender for The Ladies Man's belt.
The only way Floyd gets his wins though, is when those two big goons of his come out. The "Fabulous" Floyd Brothers. Every time Freddy steps into the ring, those two take it upon themselves to make sure that he comes out on top of things. Well it isn't going to work on Thursday. Because come hell or high water, I'm gonna kick Freddy Flyd's a$$ all over the damn arena. Freddy has some talent, but he can't hold a candle to my wrestling ability. And I will prove this on Wednesday in Warwick, Rhode Island. Plain and simply, its gonna be a night for a "Ladies Man." And as for the "Fabulous" Floyd Brothers, you better watch yourselves because if you try to interfere, "The Ladies Man" has something up his sleeve for you two.
[Vince puts his sunglasses back on his face, as he rises from his seat. He stretches a little and then smiles...]
"The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor: Well, thats all the questions that I'm gonna answer. I gotta meet my women, and I gotta get ready for my upcoming match.
So to everybody, especially Freddy Floyd, remember this... I'M THE BEST.. DAMN LIGHTWEIGHT.. TO EVER.. WALK THIS EARTH! [Vince picks up his Light Heavyweight Title and walks out of the room as the scene fades out.]
[name withheld] Handler of "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor [WCWA, NDW] WCWA Light Heavyweight Champion Handler of Simply Irresistible [ICW, WCWA] WCWA Tag Team Champions
