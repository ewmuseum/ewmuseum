[Sample RP] - Eric Emerson... Opinions?

7 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[]The scene opens in a room filled with trophies, and pictures of Eric Emerson wrestling in high school. Sitting on the couch is none other then Eric Emerson.
He is wearing a blue and red Tommy shirt, Tommy jeans, and a pair of Dr. Martin shoes. His hair is slightly covering his face, but his cocky smile can still be seen. Emerson runs his hands through his hair revealing his face and he begins to speak[]
Eric Emerson: You know I have heard some stupid things in my short life but the things that come out of Liam Rogans mouth make me laugh. Liam, you go around saying you made an impact in the IWC, but you fail to realize a few things. You tried to make an impact in the IWC by attacking and challenging
Johnny Bod... [Cocky Smile]... Think about your actions Liam. You did nothing to prove yourself. You challenged a no body and what’s even more funny about that is you’ll probably get beat. But before you humiliated yourself by doing that business you went out and got your freak show to throw poop upon a man? No call me crazy but isn’t that a little childish? Are you that pathetic that you need to throw human feces upon a man? Whatever the case in my opinion your a joke.
[]Emerson’s face turns serious and his eyes grow large[] Eric Emerson: Now, I can stand you doing and saying whatever about whoever you want, but there is one thing I can’t stand. You entered the ring during my match at New Years Revolution and clipped my knee. Yes, I had iced it down, but guess what Laim... According to my doctors I will not be able to wrestle in the next week or so. BUT_ if you have a match while I am in the locker rooms don’t be surprised to see me at ring side. No, I don’t plan of attacking you or anything like that because I’m above that. In fact I’m above you in every category you can imagine. I’m a better wrestler then you. I’m a better man then you. And for god damn sakes I can get more women then you. Now I guess I’m just saying this to you Laim... Watch out because you never know when
_THE_GREATEST_WRESTLER_ will strike next... I’ve come, I’ve wrestled, and I have won each and every match I have been involved with. Laim, you were a victim once, don’t make me do it again... [Cocky Grin]...
[]Emerson looks at his leg and begins to rub it[] Eric Emerson: Now that I’ve warned Liam about his future I think I should address the man that is really responsible for my knee injury. Jimmy McDaniel, you are the reason I have a limp in my step. You are the reason I am out of action for a week or so. You Jimmy are the cause for my pain. What am I talking about you say, well it goes a little something like this. If you had a clue as to how to run the IWC you’d realize that Rogan is nothing but trouble. If you would have opened your eyes with the poop incident you would realize Laim is a piece of... [Emerson pauses] Well, a piece of poop... But you didn’t think about what he could do. Well, I think I’m going to have to do something about that so you better be on the look out... Usually I’m not a man that gets pissed but Jimmy your lack of brain cells has cost me some ring time. You will regret this McDaniel, but you’ll just have to wait and find out how... I think that’s all I have to say because none of you people watching are worth my time.. Later
Boys...
[]Camera fades to black[] Rob L., Handler of: | \ | \ | |\ \ | |\ \ | | | | | | | | | |/ / | |/ / |___/ |___/ Derek Douglas[Retired] Eric Emerson - [IWC] Macro by D. R.
