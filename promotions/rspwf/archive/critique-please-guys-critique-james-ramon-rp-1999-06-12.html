Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::It's a commercial for the Insane Wrestling Federation. The commercial is mostly about the re-opening of the IWF. The commercial is showing clips of some of the biggest moments in the IWF such as Chuck Williams winning the IWF World belt, Dr. Death Insane Title matches, and other high light matches. As they are showing a clip of the Insane Wrestling Federation...a voice over begins talking in a low voice.::
Voice Over: June 12th...the I W F reopens. The greatest federation ever established. The IWF is back. And they are more I N S A N E then they have ever been! Chucksterr and Kelly are finally bringing back the Insane Wrestling Fe...
::Suddenly the voice over is cut off and the screen goes to static. A different voice over begins speaking. It's the voice of a girl.::
Voice Over: The only thing bringing the IWF together is the Sexiest Man Alive. Everyone knows who he is, he's "SexyBoy" James Ramon. The "SexyBoy" is the greatest technical wrestler of all time. He wrestled amateur wrestling in high school, and wrestled in the CWF. He became known as one of the CWF's most hardcore wrestlers. But his techical wrestling skills were never shined on. So in the IWF he plans on out wrestling his opponents untill they cry. "SexyBoy"
James Ramon has joined the infamous TrU CLiQuE. James Ramon and Millionaire
Mike had been planning that for months. Now, like I was saying, James Ramon is truly...the perfect man........
::The voice fades and the screen fades to black. Then James Ramon's logo slowly appears on the screen. Then the screen turns black again, and his logo quickly fades. The screen reappears to the shores of Venice Beach, California. The camera begins showing buff men running in the sand throwing frizbies, and people surfing on the beach. It shows women in their little bikinis walking down the beach. And then the camera man hears some giggling and kissing. So he runs over to this umbrella and underneath it, is "SexyBoy" James Ramon and "The
Queen" Debbie Love. They are sitting underneath their umbrella looking at the sunset. James Ramon is wearing Nike swimshorts, a James Ramon hat, no shirt, some black sunshades, and a pair of Nike sandals. Debbie Love is wearing a
James Ramon hat with her read headed pony-tail coming out of the back, a white tanktop, some white shorts, and some white Nike sandals. James Ramon has his arm around Debbie Love and Debbie has her arm around James. James Ramon is drinking some water, then The camera gets an upclose of the two, and James lowers his glasses. Then he begins speaking.::
"SexyBoy" James Ramon: Ah, the IWF. The self proclaimed "greatest federation" in the United States. Well, it may be the greatest federation, only because they have...the greatest looking wrestler of all time. You see the IWF was
"OK", but now that the sexiest man alive has been signed in the IWF, the popularity has suddenly gone through the roof. But you should expect it, anywhere I am is a crowd of all of my tons of fans. But I really don't care for my fans...sure, it's nice to have groupies, and all of these women fantasising over me, but I don't need them. I hate people who act as if the fans screaming for them gives them such a great feeling. I think it's annoying.
::James Ramon takes a sip of his water and talks again.:: "SexyBoy" James Ramon: Now, lately...I've heard these rumors going around about all of these new and upcoming stables. Well, in this sport it's too hard to trust anyone. That's because everyone wants to get the same goal. Everyone wants to get to the IWF World Heavyweight Title. And first off...I'd like to thank Chuck for putting "The SexyBoy" in the IWF World Heavyweight tournament.
But let me get back to this stable thing. As I look through the roster of wrestlers in the IWF...I don't see too many people that I can trust. But one man I can trust...he is coming to the IWF. You know, what's James Ramon without a suprise? Something that will have all of you old schoolers in shock. But all
I can say is that I'll be bringing in a very big suprise. Could it be a wrestler? Maybe. Probably. Yea...
::James Ramon pauses. He smiles, then begins talking.:: "SexyBoy" James Ramon: Now, the IWF has finally released it's first card, and
James Ramon...thanks to Chuck...is going to win that IWF World belt. I know this tournament is full of the most elite wrestlers IWF has to offer, but James
Ramon is more than elite. I'm ravishing, sexy, cute, and then comes elite. But
IWF Madness is sure to be one of the biggest nights in wrestling. Sure, just a regular card...but this is IWF's first card since it's closing. And you know everyone will try their hardest. But you know I am going to give it my all.
That's one thing about me, everyone knows that when James Ramon is on the card, that I will give it one hundred percent, and I will make my opponent give one hundred percent...therefore giving my fans the best match on the card. And I think when I go one on one against with Chris Iverson...this will be one of the best matches on the card.
"The Queen" Debbie Love: But lets be honest here James...the only reason it will be the best matches on the card is because your in it. Not only will you prove yourself as the best wrestler in the IWF, but when you get that IWF World
Heavyweight belt...you WILL be the best wrestler that wrestling has to offer.
We've been doing this thing for three years, and I think this is finally our big break. Sure we've won belts before James, but they didn't mean anything. If we win this IWF World Heavyweight belt.
"SexyBoy" James Ramon: Your right. That is why I am so determined to win. Starting with former member of Inner Circle, Chris Iverson. Now, Chris...I've heard a lot about you lately. Being a former member of the Inner Circle, and I know that you were in the IWF last time before it closed down. Well, as I remember...you made some semi-good accomplishments. But now Chris...it's my turn, and there's nothing you can do about it. If you know me like some of the guys in the IWF do...then you know that I will not give up. But the way I see it...you must be good enough for this tournament or Chuck wouldn't have picked you. But I feel Chuck made a big mistake thinking you deserve to be in the same ring with someone as sexy as I am. Sure your good...although I don't respect you, you are good. Although I am better. But to put you on the same line up as me is absolutely...stupid. You should have to kiss my feet to even step into my league. There are a lot of people in the IWF who are good enough. But Chris
Iverson...in my view, even though your good, your not good enough. Your under me, and will always be second when it comes to competing with the best. The best would be me. Well, hell...basically everyone is under someone with a bod like mine. Now...Madison Square Garden in New York is already selled-out. So you know what that means for you? That means that thousands of people at
Madison Square Garden and millions of people all over the world will witness
James Ramon throwing you around like a ragdoll. From pillar to post. But I can already predict that I'll put you away, with ease. But wait...I have a little something to say about the crap you talked on the sexiest man alive. First of all...it's something I figured a stupid mental midget like you would sit here and brag about your accomplishments in the "Old" IWF. Ok...you were a former
IWF Jobb..I mean Telivision champion. And your point is? You fought four nobodies in one month? Is that it? Sure...I guess winning some kind of belt is something to be proud of, but the IWF Telivision belt is nothing to go around bragging about. Now, that "James Ramon...who?" comment crossed the line. You may not know me, but I sure as hell, BARELY, know you. And because you don't know me, doesn't mean I'm a newbie, or that I don't deserve a shot. Because
I've been in this sport for about three years and I think I should get my dues.
You think it's bullshit I got into the world title tourny? Well, I guess that's too bad. Because your going to have to deal with it. Then you call me your tune up match? I guess this "tune up" is just going to beat the hell out of you then. You stupid piece of trash from that giant landfill called Queens, New
York.
::James Ramon takes a deep breath. He begins talking again.:: "SexyBoy" James Ramon: You call Gods gift to women...a punk? The audacity of a man with such little size, more ways than one. You see Chris...it's people like you, in my eyes, that screw the IWF. Getting all cocky...when you know, you can't back it up. But me...you've never met me, yet you seem to think since you don't know me...I'm a jobber. Well, first of all...let me say one thing, if
Chuck did not think I was worthy of being in the tournament...then do you think he would have put me in it? Ok, you were in the IWF before me...but I really don't care. I don't care what you did in the past IWF. But I do care what you do right now in the IWF. And what your going to do is do the JOB. Seeing people like me in the World Belt tourny pisses you off? Is that supposed to scare me?
But you may be pissed off now, but after I crush you at Madness...I might as well piss on the pathetic piece of Queens, New York white trash in the ring.
Which would be you. I'm disgracing this federation? First of all...not only are you disgracing ME, but I find you coming out here thinking your the greatest...when it is so obvious that your not, disgraces me. Now, I admit...I'm not the best, yet. But...James Ramon has made a respected name for himself in the CWA, but now it's time to come to the current "big one".
::James Ramon stops and laughs. Then begins talking again.:: "SexyBoy" James Ramon: "I'm Chris Iverson and your not" you say Chris? Well, trust me...I'm very happy I'm not Chris Iverson. I sure wouldn't want to be you for many reasons. First of all...I would be dumber, a worse wrestler, poorer, and uglier. Those are just some of the things I would have to hate about being you. What you've done in this sport? I could never attempt? That's a laugh. You talk about being the leader of that group called Inner Circle.......WHO?
Ohhh...that group with that stupid logo and that stupid leader. Wait, that was you...well, that explains everything. Me...the future jobber? That's a joke
Chris. Lets get real here...take a look in the mirror man. You are not anything to brag about. You make me ashamed I'm a mammal. Trust me...a lot of the people in the IWF know that I am no jobber and Chris...you will fall down for the one, two, three. If I get good? Hell, little boy, I already am good. I'm great.
Better than you...A LOT better than you. ::James Ramon pauses, then begins talking again.:: "SexyBoy" James Ramon: Your "chilling" in your backyard? How poor are you? I'm glad the camera man didn't get a view of that entire landfill you call your house. Probably has cigarette buds and food left all over the place and everything. Oh well, I don't care. You want Chuck to give you a bye? You'd like that wouldn't you? You'd like to get off scott free, but I don't think so. You think it would regularly take you five minutes to beat me? Man, Chris, your way in over your head pal. The cops must have hit you up side the head too many times, eh? Well, you brag about using your entire arsenal on me? Well, that's fine. I could sit here and list every move I like to do when I'm in the ring, but unlike you....I'm not boring. You want to make this match go on for a long time? We can be fighting all day for all I care, all I know is I will not let you come on top. I will get my shoulder up, kick out, foot on the rope, whatever I have to do. But seeing how important it is that I shut you up...I will defeat you at all costs. You sit here and talk about telling me what your about to do to me? First of all...we're not cybering out there, and second of all....what kind of dork sits in his backyard and talks about telling his opponent what he is going to do. Man, your an idiot. Sure, you say that you just woke up and realized being a nice guy only got you a TV belt....but you can admit that you were tired of being made fun of everytime you would walk down that bronx called Queens, New York. And let me tell you...the world champions you listed...they may not have been nice guys. But they all have one thing in common with me, and not with you. They were GOOD. They actually tried their best. They weren't running around with some dumb Circle of Life group, and definately weren't sitting on their butt in their backyard. Now, I may be here in the beach right now, but hell...I live at the beach man. But when I usually get free time, I go straight to that gym and I train to beat punks like you. Your sick of being a mid-carder, eh? Well, isn't that cute...Mr. Tough Guy is fed up with being a mid-carder. You know...earlier you were bragging about your IWF Telivision belt, and now your mad about it. Idiot. And what the hell do you expect to do about it? You've got to get through your hardest challenge in the IWF yet. And that is me, the most ravishing man in the IWF, James Ramon.
And let me inform you on one more thing...it's people like me who will get ahead. And white trash like you, who just get kicked back in the gutter. You talk to me as if you are the greatest wrestler in the IWF. But wake up call man...your not. Your below me and EVERYONE knows it. Everyone knows I can beat you. In the locker room everyone is saying hey to me and telling me how easy I got it. Part one of your quest? Quest....heh, oh brother.
::A fan comes up to James Ramon and asks James for an autograph. James signs it, sighs as the kid goes away, and then speaks.::
SexyBoy" James Ramon: ANYWAY...you talk about the death of an late great wrestler, Owen Hart. One of the greatest technical wrestlers wrestling had to offer. But you know what? It's over. I was choked up just as much as you were when I found out about Owen. But it's been about three weeks since this happened. And your still crying. You don't even have the right too. You barely know the guy. You just know him from being in the WWF and from doing what your doing now. Sitting on your butt. But you know what...I bet your a big joke.
Your just trying to hit a nerve, talking about Owen. Well, your not going to get James Ramon to sit here and weep over his death. It was sad, it was tradgic, but it is not to be brought up every time you have to talk. It isn't funny that he died. And I'm sure since you act like Owen is this good pal of yours, he wouldn't want you to keep crying about it. He died doing what he did best. And it was an accident. Now, that it's been three weeks...I've gotten over it. The first week was rough, because everyone was just in shock that a great man could die like that. But it's been three weeks, and a lot of people have gotten over it. I have. So take my advice....SHUT UP!!! And dedicating this match to Owen....why do you have to bring him into your matches? You weren't his friend, yet you talk to him as if he was a high school buddy, an
Army friend. He wasn't. So Chris Poser, you might as well, shut your mouth, because I am sick of hearing you. And after I'm done with you....YOU WILL HAVE
A BROKEN HEART!!!!!!! ::James Ramon sips water from his water bottle again.:: "SexyBoy" James Ramon: Yet...I'll take it upon myself to make some more predictions. I'm predicting it's me winning that world belt at the end of this tournament. Whoever I might be fighting...Drifter, Taj? Who knows, but it certainly won't be Chris Iverson taking my place. I'll make sure of that!
"The Queen" Debbie Love: That's right baby, we're going to get that gold, that we deserve! ::James Ramon and Debbie Love begin kissing again as the screen fades to the rest of the commercial.::
