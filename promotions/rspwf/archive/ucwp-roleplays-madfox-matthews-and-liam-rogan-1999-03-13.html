[RSPWF] [name withheld] hits back at <spits> Pyle

13 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well well well... the people have spoken. Here are the UCWP RPs... First we have the Matthews RP... [ Scene opens up with a shot of an empty locker room. For the few who pay close attention, it is a very common scene, and a very familiar scene. A solitary locker stands in this room, wide open...revealing a basic history of wrestling in pictures inside. A poster of John Matthews Sr. adorns the inside door....pictures of the past greats of wrestling. A hand reaches into the scene and tosses a towel into the locker. Camera pans out to reveal that the arm belongs to none other than Jeff "Madfox" Matthews. Jeff is shirtless, revealing a silver crucifix that hangs around his neck. He wears black mesh shorts and his amateur wrestling shoes. Jeff slowly sits down and leans back against the wall.]
Matthews: Human emotions are easily flared up. Patriotism can cause a a man to stand up and bare arms against a hostile enemy, and lay himself on the line for his pride and freedom. When others mock that patriotic sentiment, they get bombarded with a wave of hatred. One man can cause an entire nation to hate him...or love him.
[ smirks ] What you did Liam, was try and take away one of the most important things in my life...my fan following. Tapes of lies, played throughout the arena...orchestrated by your sick mind...pulled the fans to _your_ side for mere moments, before you decided to stick it to the fans and burn down their beloved flag.
The momentum shifted back Rogan. Back to where it belongs, as the fooled masses came to their senses and realized their genuine mistake. They realized that their patrioticism had blinded them for a mere second, enough to allow you to get to them...and make them believe your lies.
[ brief pause ] But you _failed_ Rogan. You can fool some people some of the time, but you can't fool all the people, all of the time. In your jaded reality, you thought that the fans would flock to you like sheep being led to the slaughter. You thought that by burning their flag, you would show an universal sign of disrespect that would etch you into their minds forever. You wanted to be remembered...
[ Jeff motions for the camera to come closer ] ...but the only thing you'll be remembered as, is the man who made a fool out of himself on national television when he decided to cross the "Madfox". That little display just added more fuel to my fire Rogan. I've got an agenda that I plan on fulfilling... _step_ _by_ _step_! You won't be spared my wrath...you won't be spared a lesson. Come Sunday night....you'll know why they call me the
"Madfox" and why my bite is far worse than your bark.
You'll learn your lesson... [arches his left eyebrow] ...and that's a promise. [fade] Then, we have mine... keep in mind, I also had an angle starting a very big stable as well as an anti-Canada angle going... as well as major mind games involving Matthews' wife and his best friend. This is also coming off of an
in-ring from the previous week :) [Wednesday afternoon, February 24th, 3:55pm. The scene opens in the living room of Liam Rogan's country residence. As a tape of last Sunday's
"Fireworks" episode plays on the television/VCR, "The Handler" Liam Rogan and his fiancee, "The Extreme Goddess" Kieran Erotikka, are seen resting on the velvet couch in the center of the room, Rogan's favorite resting place. They both look up at the television and laugh in unison. On the television, Rogan can be seen telling everyone about Jeff "Madfox" Matthews...]
Liam Rogan: You know, I could watch this episode of "Fireworks" over and over and over again. I could watch it a million times and it'd still make me smile.
Kieran Erotikka: You certainly were in all your glory that night, sweetie. Liam Rogan: What did YOU think about what I did on Sunday? Kieran Erotikka: I've followed Jeff Matthews' career for a long time ... even longer than you. I remember him when he was the UEW American Champion over a year ago. I don't think I've ever seen the fans turn on him like that before.
[He looks over at Kieran laying his arms. A sarcastic expression finds its way onto his face.]
Liam Rogan: It's about time those Canadian fans found out the truth about him, huh? If there are any anti-Canadian athletes in the UCWP, the fans SHOULD know about them. It's their right as UCWP wrestling fans and as Canadian citizens.
[Kieran smiles at Rogan...] Kieran Erotikka: Yeah. Not like _you_ disgraced their country or anything. Liam Rogan: Who? Me? Kieran Erotikka: Yes, you, Mr. Flag Burner. Liam Rogan: Ah, I see what you mean. I wasn't burning that flag out of disrespect. I'd never disrespect the fine people of Canada like that. I was just burning it for ... umm ... well... I figured the flames would entertain the simple minded Canucks while Parker and Martin recovered.
[Kieran laughs...] Kieran Erotikka: Canucks? And you said that you'd never disrespect Canadians! HA! Liam Rogan: D'OH! Did I say Canucks?! I meant ... well... ummm... sweetie, you got me there. I meant Canucks! Actually...
Kieran Erotikka: Yes, Hon? Liam Rogan: ... I meant those filthy, slimy, idiotic, empty headed, rude, and illiterate Canuck FOOLS!
Kieran Erotikka: Now THAT sounds like the man I'm gonna marry! [Both Rogan and Kieran laugh. They kiss eachother playfully...] Liam Rogan: Seriously, I did everything I wanted to do this past Sunday. I exposed Jeff Matthews to the fans. They know who the REAL "Madfox" is now.
They know he's nothing more than an overrated hack with a fake fan friendly image. Kieran Erotikka: Something that I thought'd _never_ happen ever in this bloody business!! Liam Rogan: And then, in my grand finale ... in the proverbial big bombshell of the evening... I shocked those stupid pasty faced Canadian fools ... the same people who I got to cheer me fifteen minutes earlier ... when I burnt their Godforsaken flag in the center of the ring.
Kieran Erotikka: Job well done all 'round, puddin'. Liam Rogan: And now I get the pleasure of going one on one with Matthews this
Sunday. Oh happy day!
Kieran Erotikka: You know, I'm bloody shocked at your behavior. In wrestling circles, whether you like him or not ... whether he deserves it or not... Jeff
Matthews is considered a big name.
Liam Rogan: Being a "big name" just means that you have a lot of hype going for you. It relates to your marketability" and your recognition with the fans.
It has nothing to do with talent or the ability to withstand punishment. Kieran Erotikka: But he's held the UEW American Title ... the UEW Ulti---
Liam Rogan: I don't care what titles he's held. I've done my homework on Mr.
Matthews. He's not the honorable champion that everyone says he is. Kieran Erotikka: How so? Liam Rogan: Upon doing some research on his career, I found out that 80% of the titles that Matthews has won in his career have been won due to bribery to either the president of the organization or the opponent he was facing.
Kieran Erotikka: Oh my God... Liam Rogan: The other 20% was due to outside interference. It's shocking but true. The UEW Ultimate Title is probably the most well known title Matthews has ever held. In my honest opinion, winning that title is what thrust him into the spotlight of the sport.
Kieran Erotikka: I agree. After he won that title, Jeff Matthews was the only name that you ever heard in wrestling magazines, on those 900 number hotlines, and even on mainstream talk shows for weeks and weeks.
Liam Rogan: Yeah ... his career making victory ... but little does anybody know that he only won that title because he threatened then UEW President Jay
Steele's life. Steele, in turn, forced the UEW Ultimate Champion at that time to throw the match or risk being fired from the company.
Kieran Erotikka: I'm speechless. I don't know what to say. I was never a big
Matthews fan but ... bloody 'ell ... this is shocking information. Are you sure that it's totally true?
Liam Rogan: Yes. I have a very reliable source. You could say my source was once in the Matthews ass kissing camp at one time in UEW. You wanna hear the funniest thing of the whole Matthews career in UEW?
Kieran Erotikka: What's that? Liam Rogan: As soon as he got into a match where he wasn't protected... a match where he had no possible way to con Steele into granting him a quick
"thrown" victory ... he lost the UEW Ultimate Title to "Top Dog" Rick Styles.
Kieran Erotikka: Ha ha! Liam Rogan: You haven't heard the funniest bit. As soon as he lost the title to Styles, he left the UEW in a huff with his "foxy" tail between his legs.
He really is a pitiful and cowardly specimen. Kieran Erotikka: But he is so unpredictable. Liam Rogan: That's what _you_ think. Again, I talked to one of his more well known past opponents ... someone who will remain nameless at this point in time. I also watched a few videotapes of his past matches. He may _seem_ unpredictable to you but, in reality, he's about as unpredictable as a "special episode" of "Full House".
Kieran Erotikka: You really got every-bloody-thing covered, don't ya, dear? Liam Rogan: I've got all the bases covered for this match. It's not that hard to do, though. I'm serious. You can predict a Jeff Matthews match from the very beginning to the very end. He makes a big entrance ... an entrance that usually looks like it was reserved for the likes of Elvis Presley, Prince, or
Jesus Christ himself.
Kieran Erotikka: Mmm-hmmm. I'm following you so far, babe. Liam Rogan: His opponent then kicks the living crap out of him for about ten or fifteen minutes. Some friends of Matthews interfere in the match. Matthews wins. It's a tried and true formula that he's used ever since he first came into the wrestling business.
Kieran Erotikka: Unfortunately, it's brought him plenty of success. You can't deny that. Liam Rogan: It only brought him success when he was facing guys who didn't know what to expect from him. I know just about _EVERYTHING_ to expect from
Matthews. I'm going into this match more prepared than I've ever been for a single match in my whole career.
[Kieran pauses ... sighing to herself...] Kieran Erotikka: I'm glad you said that 'cause ... well, I have some bad news to tell you about this Sunday.
Liam Rogan: ... Kieran Erotikka: I'm not going to be able to come with you to Canada. [Rogan looks on at Kieran ... shocked and frowning...] Liam Rogan: Wha? Why not? Kieran Erotikka: I just can't. I need to fly out to England to clear up some personal matters ... some family matters ... before our wedding. I'll be back by Monday night, don't fret. I sure hope you're not mad. I don't feel like getting into a row with you.
Liam Rogan: I wanted you to be there. It's probably the biggest match in my career. [Rogan pauses dramatically. He kisses Kieran softly and smiles...] But ... if you need to do what you need to do, so be it. If the end result is me marrying you, there's no way I can complain about you missing one match of mine. You'll be present in spirit when I face Matthews.
Kieran Erotikka: Don't worry. I've gotten a suitable replacement for me for that night. They'll take my place in your corner. Wait until you see who it is.
Liam Rogan: Who is it? Kieran Erotikka: Let's just say it's someone that even _I_ have come to respect through a few occurrences with them in the past. You'll see who it is when the time is right. You'll be shocked and maybe a little [BLEEP] off at first but this person will show you that they're on our side this time.
Liam Rogan: But dear ... to quote that chrome domed anti-American no talent early 90's Irish crooner Sinead O'Connor ... nothing compares ... no, nothing compares to yooouuu.
Kieran Erotikka: Trust me on this one. This person is possibly one of the biggest and most brilliant masterminds in all of professional wrestling. Hell, they may even become an important part of your big "plan" for UCWP.
Liam Rogan: Whoa. That reminds me. Hold on one second. I need to do something today before I forget.
[Rogan kisses Kieran again and gets up off the couch.] Kieran Erotikka: Where are you going? [Rogan takes the portable phone off the coffee table...] Liam Rogan: I'm not going anywhere. I just need to make a quick phone call. [Rogan dials...] Kieran Erotikka: Who are you calling? Liam Rogan: I'm just checking up on a former friend of mine ... someone who's doing a huge favor for me on Sunday.
[Rogan begins to talk into the phone...] Yeah. Hey there, buddy. Yeah, it's me. It's Liam. You got the video production stuff set up in the hotel room?
[He smiles ... nodding at Kieran...] Great. Got everything set up with _her_, too? Is she ready for her role on
Sunday? [Rogan smiles an even bigger smile. He's obviously extremely pleased about something...] Wonderful. Matthews is gonna have a heart attack. Thanks so much for agreeing to do this for me. Take care. I guess me ... and the whole world ... will see you make your UCWP debut on "Fireworks" this Sunday. I can't wait. Until then. Good-bye.
[Rogan turns the phone off and puts it back on the coffee table. Meanwhile,
Kieran sits up...]
Kieran Erotikka: So, love, what's this big suprise that you have planned. It'll cause Matthews to have a heart attack? It sounds earth shattering. Liam Rogan: Let's just say that I'll be doing something that'll hit Jeff
Matthews very close to home. _VERY_ close to home.
Kieran Erotikka: This doesn't seem to sound like it'll bode well for Matthews. Liam Rogan: Matthews is in for the fight ... and a few shocks ... of his life. This time his opponent won't be someone who isn't prepared ... or someone told to take a dive by the company bigwigs. This time, his opponent will be the meanest ... the most vicious ... most hardcore
... and the most violent son of a bitch in the UCWP and the sport today. You wanna know the best part about it?
Kieran Erotikka: Tell me. Liam Rogan: It's sorta ironic really. I get to put wrestling's biggest overrated piece of [BLEEP} to rest in the biggest piece of [BLEEP] country in the world.
Kieran Erotikka: I don't think Jeff Matthews is ready for this. [Rogan looks directly into the camera, smirking and laughing devilishly to himself.] Liam Rogan: I don't think he's ready either. I don't think _ANYBODY_ could be ready for the carnage I'm planning to unleash this Sunday. Ready or not,
Matthews' career will be taking a huge turn for the worse on Sunday night.
He's not winning any undeserved titles or showboating in the spotlight. No ma'am. On Sunday night ... on February 28th, 1999...
"THE MADFOX" IS GETTING __ERASED__.
