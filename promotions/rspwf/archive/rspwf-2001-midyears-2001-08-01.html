TORA Wanizame 6-man Tribute Match [RCW] - After the tragic death of TORA Wanizame, former G-Pro owner and current RCW owner [name withheld] paid tribute to the former star the best way he knew how. He organized a thrilling G-Pro-esque trios match, featuring Raya Oscura, Michael Keening and the original Jet Jaguar, Shoji Suzuki against Nijikon, Danger Boy and Kashan Akuma. The emotional tribute match ended with Oscura's team victorious, and Raya *finally* unmasking himself as Callan Greenway, Wanizame's old nemesis. This contest is widely considered as the best match in RCW's history. 4. The Gremlin vs. Mark Langseth [EMWC] - The most dangerous match in ECW history was Born To Be Wired. The match was so dangerous, it ended flatly, with both participants being too near serious injury to continue. Well, luckily in the world of e-wrestling, we don't have to worry about that. As a result, the EMWC's version of Born To Be Wired was brutal, bloody, savage, and in the end...as extreme as it gets. 3. Jeff Matthews vs. Caleb Temple [EMWC] NIA - The final showdown to end this chapter of the most personal of blood feuds. When Temple and Matthews locked horns this time around, Temple went straight for the Madfox's mind...taunting his prey. He locked Debbie Matthews inside an exploding cage....he tricked Matthews into assaulting his own children with a sledge hammer. This was a very personal issue...and the match that capped it off was a fitting end to this very dramatic saga. 2. Jake Davids vs. Max Pain vs. Chris Collins [AVW] - Six months in the making, six months of hostility...all cluminated in one final match. "Flawless" Jake Davids had his plate full, as he took on the two men he'd atagonized since day one in AVW. Davids, being his usual self, baited Pain and Collins for the first few minutes. When they'd get close to him, he'd duck through the ropes...and the ref would call for a break. David's continued these mind games, until Pain wised up, and leveled David's with a clothesline from hell...officially starting the match. 1. Devon Case vs. Jake Shaw vs. Ribera Kid [EMWC] - What do you get when you take a feud that has already had one PPV match end in a time limit draw, add some more heat behind it, and stick it on one of the biggest PPVs ever? A great match. What do you get when everyone involved puts their all into the match in hopes it'll pay fitting tribute to the company that inspired us all? A Match of the Year candidate. ----- 4.)
