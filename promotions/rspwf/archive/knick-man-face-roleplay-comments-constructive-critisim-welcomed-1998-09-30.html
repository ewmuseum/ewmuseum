[Knick Man Face Roleplay] ...Comments, Constructive Critisim welcomed

30 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens up showing a huge logo of the NaWF/AeWF for a few moments. We can hear the sounds of many voices conversating in the background. The camera slowly pans back to reveal the scene of a press conference set up. The camera pans around the room in a circular motion to reveal the various reporters representing a variety of newspapers. We then see Mr Chris Hopper, President of the NaWF/AeWF slowly make his way out to the stage as the reporters begin to quiet down. Chris Hopper is dressed in a nice black 3 piece suit with a pin of a smaller version of the NaWF/AeWF logo. The camera closes in on Mr Hopper.)
President Hopper: (clears throat) Good Afternoon ladies and gentlemen and I thank you in advance for attending this Press Conference!
(pauses) PH: Today is a proud day in the short history of the NaWF/AeWF. For over the past week, I have been engaging in contract negotiations with one of the hottest commodities in our sport today. This man has wrestled in many federations and has won numerous titles throughout his career. He is a true superstar in our sport and a very talented individual! And now, after intense negotiations and competition from a variety of federations, I am proud to announce that the NaWF/AeWF has signed.....
THE KNICK MAN!!!!! (We see many flashes from cameras as a tall black man, with a bald head and a goatee makes his way out. He is dressed in a very sharp 3-piece Aramni suit, as well as a pair of black sunglasses. Many basketball fans recognize him as
John Williams, the point guard that led The Knicks to 10 straight NBA
Championships. [Author Note: This IS Fantasy Wrestling you know!] Wrestling fans of various federations recognize him as The Knick Man! The Knick Man makes his way up to the podium and shakes the hand of President Hopper. He then turns towards the reporters.)
President Hopper: We will be taking a few questions.... (Many of the reporters begin to shout out questions all at one time.) PH: Please, everyone, one at a time! (points to a reporter) You! Reporter 1: Mike Lupica, New York Daily News! Knick Man, you have received many offers from many federations. What made you decide to join the NaWF/AeWF?
Knick Man: Well, President Hopper is a VERY generous man! (laughter from everyone) But seriously though, many of the presidents of the other federations. They offered me money. They offered me titles. They told me about all the "big names" they got there and how they can increase my superstardom. But all that stuff really doesn't matter to me. I've already got all the money I could ever want. I've won titles in just about every league I wrestled in. I've wrestled with the "big names" as well. All I was looking for is a good organization with a bright future where I can compete to the best of my abilities. And the NaWF/AeWF is exactly what I was looking for!
PH: Next question? (points to another reporter) Reporter 2: Grahame L. Jones, Los Angeles Times! What do you hope to accomplish in the NaWF/AeWF?
Knick Man: I WANT TO RULE THE WORLD!!! (more laughter) I just want to compete with some of the top athletes of the world, and give all the NaWF/AeWF fans an athlete and competitior that they can be proud of! Of course, winning a title would be nice too! (smiles)
PH: We'll take one more question... (points to another reproter) You! Go ahead! Reporter 3: Barry Jackson, Miami Herald. What do you think of the competitors of the NaWF/AeWF?
Knick Man: Well, I've seen some of the competitiors in the NaWF/AeWF. They are without question great athletes! However, some of them have some serious
BAD ATTITUDES! (grins) You know, now a days it seems like everybody wants to be the man! Everybody wants to be the legend! Everybody wants to be the icon!
But what a lot of people fail to realize is that it takes a lot more than just good wrestling ability and an arrogant attitude. Being the icon is a way of life! You have to be a great MAN as well as a great wrestler. Now, I'm no icon. But you can guarantee that when I step into the ring, I'm going to do my best to treat these fans to some good, solid wrestling! And I'm going to give every single match 100% settling only for victory! And I'm goign to give them something to be proud of!
Now to those of you in the NaWF/AeWF that think more highly of yourselves then you ought....
(grins) I got a special SLAM DUNK prepared just for you! Because you see boys, The Knick Man is raising the athletical standard here in the NaWF/AeWF! Can you measure up?
(smiles) I guess we're just going to have to find out SOON ENOUGH! President Hopper: Alright, thank you very much for your participation in this afternoon's events! The Knick Man will be making his debut very soon, and I see nothing but great things in store for him! Good day!
(President Hopper and The Knick Man turn around and make their way towards the back as the reporters once again begin to yell out questions at the same time.
The scene fades to black.) [name withheld] Handler of The Knick Man [WWO, HHWO, NaWF/AeWF] http://members.tripod.com/~knick_man Illegal Tactics [WWO]
