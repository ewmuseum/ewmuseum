Free Agent - Grade my RP too(Pretty short)

15 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Whut up this is _the_ Marcus Taylor. # 1 Free Agent on the RSP-WF boards. Been around eRasslin'™ for 3 years now and I wouldn't want to brag but I think I am quite good. Here is a sample roleplay: *HINT* I am looking for _competetitive_ federations. Fake only. Aiite,
[Scene: A local bar in Detroit, Michigan. The camera starts on the outside of the bar. In neon red, a sign reads "Jetz and Stix." The camera fades back down and starts moving towards the door. When it gets about 3 feet from the door, the door swings open. The camera fades inside slowly. From what you can tell, there is a only one couple dancing on the dance floor. There are 4 men at the bar betting on who can chug a beer the fastest. Off in the back, there are three pool tables. One pool table is occupied by two men. Another pool table is open. And the last is occupied by a single man. You can't tell who it is because of all the cigarette smoke in the bar. The camera then starts moving in on the table where the single man is. You start making out features on him:
Short brown hair, yellow lensed glasses, a tee-shirt that says "Game Over" on the front, loose fitting black jeans, and a pair of boots(Not shitkicking boots.). He has 3 balls left on the table. He glances at the camera and begins to speak.]
"Welcome, to the sort of "Second home" of Marcus Taylor. It seems that _everywhere_ I go, cameras are sure to follow. But I guess that's what happens when you become a free agent in the world of wrestling. Now, just because I am a free agent doesn't mean that I am not a good wrestler. The terms that I was let out of my contract with the EHW(Fake fed.If someone owns a fed named EHW, its not yours), is because of the atmosphere. I couldn't work with wrestlers who complained about the way a president ran his _own_ fed. So, with a little help from my lawyers, I took off. Now that I am a free agent, I choose my next move. NEW? IGPWA? DPW? Maybe, some top talent wrestle with these federations.
And that is _just_ what I am looking for. I have wrestled with top talent, from
James Pillow, to Jack Vars, to Triple X, to Shawn Sylkk. And I am still around.
They are _great_ professional wrestlers. That is what I am looking for. I don't want to be affiliated with a federation where the top name is "Johnny
Sampson"(Fake name. If you own this wrestler, of forget it. Dont take it personally).! I want, wait a minute, scratch that. I _need_ a federation where
I can make a name for myself. Most of you don't know who Marcus Taylor is. But trust me, if he comes to a federation near you, his name will forever be etched in your mind."
[He hits the 3 ball into the 15 ball and they both go into seperate pockets.] "So I will leave you with these final words. Sit back, relax, and enjoy your last days of reign over whatever federation you may be in. Because when Marus
Taylor get's there, time to take over. And when I _do_ take over any federation, no matter what fed it is, it's _GAME OVER!_
{He hits the 8 ball in. The camer fades out.}
