LIFE STORY On April 17, 1979, a man who is very different from others was born with the name [name withheld]. As a little boy he looked somewhat demonic and he was put in the hospital for psychological analysis. A very quite boy that you could read his words from his eyes. That is the quote a doctor made when Mike was only five years old. A few years later Mike started school which it was obvious was totally wrong for him. The first few years he was quite and always sat in the back row. Then mysteriously something happened. At only nine years of age Mike ran away from home and onto the streets of New York City. While there he met an older man named Crazy J who was a pimp and a drug runner as well. When Mike turned eleven, Crazy J learned how sneaky Mike was and gave him his first job as a cocaine runner. This lasted for several years and by the time he was sixteen Mike was Crazy J's main drug runner and had made quite a bit of money for himself. Mike started getting into drugs himself and started carrying a weapon with him. On his seventeenth birthday Mike was doing a common drug run when a man he was dealing with pulled a gun on him. Mike had already claimed the name Wolf and this is when he gained the name Psycho. With the man holding the gun to Mike's face, Mike just started to laugh and saying that the man could not hurt him no matter what he did. Then Mike pulled his gun out and shot the man in the chest. As the man was dying he told Mike that he was one psycho son of a bitch. Mike simply looked at him and said, " I know, I am Psycho Wolf," and preceded to shoot the man right between the eyes.
When he returned to Crazy J and told him the story, Crazy J totally flipped out and shot Mike in the leg. Mike turned around and looked at him with a demonic look on his face. Mike took out his own gun and blasted Crazy J in the face six times. As Crazy J's henchmen entered the room, Mike just simply blasted every one of them. With a smile on his face Mike just walked out of the room and was not heard of until just about a week ago when he entered a wrestling promotion called the World Wrestling Alliance. However the WWA by itself is not enough to contain this man, so he has officially entered the Insane
Wrestling Federation. PROMO The face of professional wrestling has changed forever. Times are changing for the Insane Wrestling Federation. A new man has come. A different man from the normal wrestler. A sick and in a sense Psychotic wrestler has emerged. Nothing will ever be safe now. Not with the man who don't give a damn. Nobody, not even the World Champion can stop fate from happening. Its time for a change, and that change is PSYCHO WOLF! Welcome to my world!!! Scene New York city at around five in the afternoon. A camara focuses in at Madison Square Gardens. The Insane Wrestling Federation has announced that they will be holding a special press conference for one of their fine young rookies.
This comming just days after President's Kelly and Chuck have reopened the federation. The arena is set up in the exact same way that it is for press conferences is boxing, with the stage and a podium in the middle. Seated at the main table are both Presidents Kelly and Chuck along with a few other IWF officials that are busy looking through papers and trying to make sure the conference runs smoothly. Also in attendance is the Madison Square Gardens booking man that makes they press conferences go, and also about one hundred reporters and newscasters. This conference was announced only in the morning hours so nobody has really had the time to reflect on who will be talking or even what it is about for that matter. There is one empty chair that it can be guessed is for the wrestler that is supposed to make his Insane Wrestling
Federation debut here today. Now President Kelly is making her way to the podium. [President Kelly] Good afternoon ladies and gentlemen, and welcome to this exclusive Insane Wrestling Federation press conference. Thank all of you for comming out here on such short notice, I know it was sort of a pain in the ass.
However I am pretty sure that you will not be disappointed with what will occur here today. I know that there are many changes going on in the Insane
Wrestling Federation, but I can guarentee that they are all for the good. That is a promise. One of the good changes is that there are many promising new additions to the IWF. Many of these wrestlers are from other promotions and a few are rookies here in the sport of professional wrestling. Today you will be introduced for the first time to a nineteen year old rookie that plans on making an immediate impact here in the IWF. My hopes run so high on this wrestler that his first match will not even be a dark match. He will be fighting for the entire Insane Wrestling Federation and all of their fans to see. Ladies and gentlemen, may I be the first to introduce to you one of the mainframes for the future of the IWF, PSYCHO WOLF!!!!!!! This Sunday night he will be making his IWF debut from Hartford, Connecticut against Tony "The
Dream" Morris!!!!! Scene The lights in Madison Square Gardens are turned off and "The beautiful people" by Marylin Manson starts playing from the arena sound system. Strobe lights that are blue and gray start moving around the arena, giving it a Heavyweight
Title fight look. Cameras from fans are flashing everywhere and the news crews are starting to roll the tape. The jumbotron turns on with a gray background.
Words start filling the jumbotron. First it says "The face of professional wrestling has changed forever." After it says this there is a picture of a man in shadows that quickly flashes across the screen. Then the words, "A new man has come, a different man. Nothing is ever safe again" comes across the screen. After that the man in the shadows has a little bit of light on half his face. All that can be seen is long black hair and short sideburns and a small glimer from his white teeth. This picture also goes by real quick.
"This man don't give a damn, not even the World Champion can stop him. It's time for a change now," comes across the screen next. A shadow is still on the face but most of it can be seen now. The long straight black hair, the short sideburns, the evil smile, and even the blood red eyes can be seen. Then one
by one the letters come on the jumbotron that spell out W E L C O M E T O M Y W O R L D ! ! ! Then the letters have an exploding effect and all the jumbotron says is "Psycho Wolf." Not only does it say this but it says it in dripping blood. The curtain from the back opens up and Psycho Wolf appears.
Even though this is only a press conference the crowd of a few thousand start cheering for the man that they know nothing about. He stands at 6'6" with his long hair held back in a pony-tail. He has on a long, black, leather trenchcoat that says "Psycho Wolf" on the back of it with a picture of a blood hungry wolf in the middle of the back. He has black pants on that say Psycho
Wolf in gray lettering down the side, and basic black boots. The most standing out feature is the red eyes that give him a little bit of a satanic look. He smirks a little bit, but for his first press conference he does not look nervous at all. He comes up to the podium and the music cuts and the lights are back on. Then he puts his mouth up to the microphone.
[Psycho Wolf] Good afternoon morons of the Insane Wrestling Federation. Hello president Kelly, you are looking rather fine this morning for an idiot.
President Chuck I am not even going to go into what I want to say about you, I would be thrown out of the federation immediately. Well I am here in the
Insane Wrestling Federation, the place I have heard so much about. Frankly I have walked around and talked to several of the wrestlers here and I am not very impressed by what I have seen. IWF officials and fans are calling this the great rebirth of the IWF. I say that is bullsh*t, it is much more than that. It is the birth of Psycho Wolf, the man that will single handedly take the federation by storm and mark his place as one of the true legends of the
IWF. Not only as one of the true legends, but the best World Champion that will ever be seen in the history of professional wrestling. [smirk] Before I start answering you a$sholes questions I would like to make a few points here just so President's Kelly and Chuck are on the same page length as me. First of all President Kelly, you make it sound like I should be down on my knees thanking you for the oppertunity to fight in a regular match in front of a cable television audience instead of in a dark match. It is not going to happen little lady. You should be down on your knees thanking me for deciding to come to your organization and saying I will wrestle on your so called re-birth card Sunday night. You see Kelly, a lot of people are disappointed with what has transpired in the IWF over the last month and a half. How can you blame them, it is just like the baseball strike of 1995. It took time and players like Mark McGwire to brng baseball back where it is today. The same will be the case with the IWF except instead of Mark McGwire it will be me,
Psycho Wolf. Do not ever forget this moment Mrs. President because years from now when you are saying "I can not believe the IWF has came back to be the best wrestling federation ever," you think of me. No matter if the fans love me or hate me, it is a promise that I will bring the highest ratings on cable to the
IWF. So no Kelly, I will not thank you for putting me in a regular television event against Tony "The Dream" Morris. [laughs] Now for the other loser that calls himself a president, that would be Chuck. I know that you call a lot of the shots here in the IWF, just make sure that you are extremely carefull of what you decide to do. Screwing me would be the biggest mistake that you ever make. After I breeze through these first few opponents you better give me a shot at one of the titles. I am not warning you, I am threatining you that I better not get screwed in any aspect in this federation. [looks at President
Chuck with a serious face] Now that I have ran across how things will work no in the Insane Wrestling Federation I guess I will let you worthless pieces of sh*t ask me a few stupid questions, since that is all you products of the system know how to do. Do a pretty sh*tty job of I might add.
[Reporter #1] Hello Psycho Wolf I am Trent Williams from the New York Times. First of all before the question I would like to be the first to come out and say that you are a jerk. Now onto the question. You come in here and talk like you own the world. Don't you think that you are a little, actually way to inexperienced to come here and talk like this? I have personally known both presidents for a long time and they do not take crap from anyone. Secondly I was just wondering how old you are because you do not even look old enough to legally drink yet?
[Psycho Wolf] First of all you got it all wrong you fu▎ing moron. I am far from a jerk, I am what you call an arogant a$shole. Jerk sounds a little to nice for a man like me. It is pretty funny coming from scum like a reporter to talk negatively about anyone. You guys are what is wrong with society, only reporting what is wrong in the world. I do not care how well you know either one of the presidents. I have known them for three days, and that is plenty of time to judge their character. I mean for crying out loud you already judged me and have not even known me for an hour. [smirks] Now I am not telling
President Chuck or President Kelly that they have to take crap from me. I am simpley stateing what is going to happen around here, nothing more. It is not like I am telling them to rig the matches so I can win, or give me a title shot right now. I am just warning them of things that are to come in the near future and the real reason I am on the main card my first fight. I do not like either one of them and I am sure the feelings are mutual. Now about the inexperienced part which is pretty true. Yes I have never fought professionaly in a match in my short life. People say that wrestling in front of thousands of people with them booing you is when you will really find out if you have what it takes to make it in wrestling. My whole life has been a complete hell and I am sure that beating the hell out of someone in front of thousands of people will be an easy task. Also there are big name wrestlers here like for example TK, "Mr Wonderful" Chuck Williams, Reaper, John Myles, and Drakon
Hughes. [laughs] People are warning me that these are all experienced wrestlers that know how to win in the big matches and that I should not fu▎ with them.
Well every one of them can bl0w me. I will be the first to admit talk is cheap. These people that warn me lose to these so called greats because they go into the match with the notion that they will lose. It is all about confidence. I know I can win and if I wrestle like I am capable of, there is no way in hell I will ever lose a match. If, which I doubt, I ever lose a match it will be nobodys fault but my own. Of course that is unless somebody has another wrestler interfere to cost me a match. In my opinion though that is still a win for me because I know the other person realized that they can not beat me on their own. Next question!

Metal poles, baseball bats, anything that resembles anything that could be used in a Hardcore Match will be used to beat the living hell out of Tony "The Dream" Morris. Well this Sunday you will see that you should leave the federation when I make a complete fool out of you. [smirks] You see Tony

Even though I have my mind thinking about other, better wrestlers here in the IWF, when it comes Sunday you will be my only concern. This name you have, "The Dream", what kind of sick joke is this.
