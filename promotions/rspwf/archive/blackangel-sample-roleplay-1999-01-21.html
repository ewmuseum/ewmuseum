Blackangel Sample Roleplay

21 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens on Hamilton street in Allentown, PA. Leaning against a wall is a
Hispanic american with a faded haircut and a neatly trimmed goatee. It is none other then the now free agent known as Blackangel. He is wearing a pair of black jeans, a black BLACKANGEL shirt with the name Blackangel on the front and a picture of a tiger's face on the back with the words "Look into my Eyes" on the back. He is also wearing a pair a black bandana, a pair of Nike sneakers, and a pair of black Oakley sunglasses. Do to the chilly weather, he is also wearing a black Eddie Baeur leather trench coat. It is mid day and cars are passing along the street at a steady pace as people walk by, entering and exiting the different stores of the street. The cameraman approaches
Blackangel and with a hand gesture, Blackangel signals for the cameraman to follow as he begins walking down the street at a slow and steady pace)
BLACKANGEL: So once again I'm a free agent looking for an organization that can satisfy my craving for competition. It seems everyone wants to run their own wrestling promotion, but their lack of preparation and below average televised events only cause their promotions to crumble in a short time. I just got done terminating my contract with some alliance that was supposed to be one of the best in the business. I beg to differ on that I was forced to sit around and wait to get put in a match. Then I find that none of the events of a show are even televised. That to me is complete bullsh*t. I am looking for promotions of world wide exposure. I have wrestled in Japan, Canada, all over Europe and the United States, so global exposure is nothing new to me.
(Blackangel turns a corner and begins walking up 9th street and continues to speak) BLACKANGEL: I have wrestled the biggest men, and I brought them to their knees. I have wrestled the toughest men, and made them cry like babies. I stopped those who claimed to be unstoppable and beat those who claimed to be unbeatable. I was even faced with those who claimed to have supernatural powers, but when they faced me, they were soundly defeated and forced to leave in disgrace. I am 6' 2" and 252 lbs of wrestling mastery. Submission holds are my specialty and I know them all. I have mastered every escape and counter to every hold in the book. In an age where it seems big, lumbering, giant men want to rule the wrestling world, I prove time and time again that it is not the strong, but rather the smart that survive.
(Blackangel stops next to a red Mitsubishi Eclipse. He opens the driver's side door and gets in, turning on the car. Blackangel takes off his sunglasses and stares deep into the camera with a silent determination that could be spotted a mile away)
BLACKANGEL: Look into my eyes and see the burning fire of my soul. I crave competition and love the thrill of combat. Whether it is traditional or hardcore wrestling, regular or gimmick matches, it doesn't matter because I do it all. Brutal Taipei Death matches, vicious barbed wire matches, deadly scorpion tank matches, and even career threatening scaffold matches have all been a part of my career. When one steps into the ring with me, they step into a world where their worst fears and nightmares become a horrifying reality right before their eyes. The fear of being defeated and having their reputation trampled on is the fear I bring to life. In the end, no matter who faces me, they are usually faced with a decision. It has two choices, but no matter which is chosen, they both lead to victory for me. The choice is to either..............
(Blackangel remains silent as he puts his glasses back on and sets the car to first gear, then he speaks in a chilling tone)
BLACKANGEL: Tap out......................or pass out. (Blackangel pulls out and drives off as the scene fades to black) *******If interested in my character contact me by email ( [detail withheld] ) or by AOL Instant Message ([detail withheld])
