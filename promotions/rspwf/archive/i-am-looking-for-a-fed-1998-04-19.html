I am looking for a fed.

19 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello all... Since the N.E.W. just closed down, I am in search for a "big-time" fed. I am currently in many feds, but I can use a "big-time" one. Some of the feds I am in are: INWI, IeWC, IWC, PTWF, GNW... I am in three, minor feds, as well. I have a sample flash in case if anyone is interested. I am searching preferably for a BCC mail fed.
Sample roleplay: <Camera fades in.> <The camera drop-focuses on what looks to be a dimly lit family room inside of a house. In the room, there are there brown stained, wooden end tables. All three end tables have wooden lamps on top of them that match in color. There is one lamp in three of four corners of the room. All three lamps have green shades on them. Between the furthest lamp and the middle lamp, there is a love seat. The love seat is covered in yellow blankets. At a 90 degree angle to the left of the love seat, there is a sofa with matching coverings. About two feet from the front of the sofa, there is a brown stained, wooden, coffee table. In the middle of the coffee table, there is a red candle encase in a clear glass globe. The candle is lit. About six inches from the candle, there is a pink, crystal dish with foil-covered chocolates. On the other side of the candle, there is a copy of "The Holy Bible." The book has a yellow cover and a cross on the front that is colored red. Next to the book, there is a clear glass dish filled with foil-covered chocolates.> <The camera slowly moves to its left. It shows a book case filled with a set of books, but the angle the camera shows makes the names on the bindings hard to make out. The camera continues to move to it's left and it shows an empty fireplace and a black, tiled hearth. The walls of the room are wooden. The wall with the fireplace is made of a brownish stone. Above the fireplace, there is a split, concrete mantle. The mantle has long, yellow candles on it in red, glass candle holders. Above the mantle, there are three pictures of
Edward James Kuss-Walsh, Ian's older brother. The pictures are of when he was a young boy. The background in the pictures is a dark, red sky and bare trees.
The pictures show Edward with mid-length, brown hair. In the picture, he is wearing a baby's sailor type of outfit.>
<The camera wonders up the wall and eventually shows a ceiling with white, sheetrock, tile and brown, wooden beams that go across the ceiling. Hanging from the ceiling is a brown, wooden ceiling fan with three lights and two draw strings. The lights are covered by while, translucent globes. The draw strings are tan and knotted. The fan is stopped. The camera lens slowly fades.>
<The camera slowly fades back in and drop-focuses on "Human Torture Rack" Ian
Walsh who is sitting in a brown, wooden, rocking chair in the one corner of the room. He hands are folded and his legs are stretched out. He has a devilish grin upon his face. He twiddles his thumbs. He begins to speak.>
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh: "Hmm... I guess the time is coming near. <He let's out a small laugh.>
You people can no longer hold me back from my dreams... My goals...
<sighs> Here, in the I.e-W.C., I will become a major star. The first person who will be used as a stepping stone to my success will be Steve Wallace.
Ya know... This guy make me laugh. He comes into this federation thinking that he is the best when he obviously isn't. He makes a fool out of himself by staying silent after signing to face me. I guess some people are just not cut out for this sport."
<He pauses for a few seconds to think. His eyes look at nothing in particular. He unfolds his hand and lays his chin on his fist. He continues.> "So, Steve Wallace... You want to play with the big boys? You want to make a name for yourself at my expense? <Pointing at the camera and giving the signal to come close> I have some information for ya. I don't play."
<Again, Ian pauses to think. He looks at the camera in disgust.> "If anybody is going to be making a name at somebody else's expense, it will be me making one at your expense. <He breaks a devilish grin.> So...
You have that winning mindset. You have that happy dream in your head. And you just can't wait to make that dream come true. Well... <Shakes his head> It's not happening here, pal. You need to realize who you are messing with. You need to look at my credentials and see what I can do in the ring before you can make _any_ assumptions. You want to make a name for yourself. <Nods> I can respect that. As a matter of fact. I respect the fact that you even have the balls to be in the same ring with a man such as myself. Hey... How else do you expect to get recognized?"
<Ian pauses as he starts to rock the chair back and forth. The camera slowly zooms out. After a moment, it stops zooming out. Ian looks into the camera and smiles.>
"You know, man... This match isn't going to be pretty. I am not going to be nice to you and go easy. Hell no... I am not the kind of guy who would take another guy lightly. You can be the worst wrestler in the world and I would still go in there with focus and determination. Steve Wallace... You and I both know that you are not the worst wrestler in the world. I have already stated that you are not the best. So where does that leave you? <He chuckles.> Well, that leaves you just somewhere in the middle. However, I would put you around the fifty percent mark. Until you can prove to me that you are something better or something worse, that is where I will place you.
<Folds his hands again> Steve Wallace... <Pointing at the camera> You
<Folds his hands again> do not impress me. You have not shown me anything and I am not the one that likes to wait for what I want to see.
So, Steve Wallace... You better start showing me what you have, or I will get mad. And if there is anything that people have learned while facing me, it is the fact that he who makes me mad, pays for it. And Wallace... You don't want to pay the price."
<He pauses and takes a good, long look into the camera.> "Steve Wallace... You are looking at the man who will defeat you in the first round of the I.e-W.C. World Heavyweight Title Tournament. So what are my final words to you? That's easy... <His face becomes one of stone.>
Prepare to meet your demise."
<Ian starts rocking the chair back and forth. He breaks another devilish grin as the camera slowly loses focus.>
<Camera fades out.>
