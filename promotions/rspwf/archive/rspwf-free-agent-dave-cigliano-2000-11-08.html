[RSPWF] Free Agent: Dave Cigliano

8 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
With the closing of LWC, I decided to see if I could find a fed for the character this way. I've looked, but haven't found anything yet. With not much time to actually fed hunt well, I thought this may be the better solution. Don't email me simply because I deal with a good amount of email as it is and don't want more. Just post it on here with a link to your site or a FAQ. I'll take a look.
Here's the stuff you need. Application ========= Wrestler Information -------------------- Name: Dave Cigliano Height: 6'02" Weight: 248lbs. Age: 29 Alignment: [Heel, Neutral, Face] Tweener Hometown: Norfolk, Virginia Appearance And Ring Attire: Dave has short blonde hair and blue eyes. He doesn't have a stellar physique and sports some modest love handles. His ring attire consists of generic red trunks, red kneepads with white trim, and white boots. He also wears white wrist tape.
Basic Entrance, Include Theme Music: ["Fortunate Son" by CCR plays as the fans give a mixed-reaction. Dave
Cigliano emerges from the entrance portal, but doesn't stop to soak in the crowd's reaction. He walks to the ring, messing with his wrist tape a bit.
He slides in under the bottom rope and tests the ropes as his music stops playing.] 20 Or More Favorite Moves: 1: Boston crab 2: sleeperhold 3: armbar submission 4: double reverse chinlock 5: armlock hammerlock submission 6: armlock leglock submission 7: grapevine 8: spinning toe-hold 9: kneelock submission 10: front facelock 11: piledriver 12: bodyslam (regular and the hammerlock variant that the Anderson's used) 13: armbar DDT 14: vertical suplex 15: back suplex 16: shoulderbreaker 17: backbreaker 18: short clothesline 19: second turnbuckle axe-handle 20: gutwrench suplex Signature Moves: 1: quick left jab (out of nowhere) 2: spinebuster slam 3: DDT Finishing Move: 1: Bulldog 2: Reverse Armbar Description: Bulldog headlock performed as if out of nowhere or in the old
Michael Hayes fashion from his Freebird days.
For the Reverse Armbar, it's a Fujiawra armbar. Any Real Wrestlers He Wrestles Like: Arn Anderson Background Information: Not much information is available on Dave's background for a lack of him not discussing it. He feels that his history isn't relevant to his wrestling career. Therefore, he's not going to talk about it unless he deems it neccessary.
It has been noted that Dave's been wrestling for almost ten years. He's been in a lot of regional feds in the South and has even won past titles.
This information comes second hand since Dave doesn't talk much about his past fed experience believing it to hold no relevance to the LWC.
Roleplay ======= [Earlier in the day before the actual Stampede event started taking place. We see the ring crew setting up the ring and a few fans who've come early in hopes of getting a chance to see or meet some of their favorite stars. The camera pans left to show Dave Cigliano leaned up against a wooden post. There's a bit of sweat on his brow from the heat. His jeans have a nice film of dirt and dust from an earlier activity. His once-white t-shirt has a few waves of brown dirt covering it as well. Lines of dirt line his hands and arms. The Italian's blonde locks are unkempt and damp with sweat. He looks to the camera and nods towards the ring.]
Dave Cigliano: Just a few short hours until we step into that ring, McBaine. Then we find out if we're both cut from the same cloth or not. You know what? I'm predicting we're not for quite a variety of reasons; the top one being the simple fact of how we choose to look at things.
[Dave pauses, wiping under his nose with the arm he's leaned on. He looks back towards the ring.]
Dave Cigliano: See, McBaine? You walk to the ring. Like any oportunist, you peer around to see what's not nailed down, to see what you can use in order to gain an advantage that night. You look at the dozen chairs in the first row and think, "twelve chairs for later." You look at the tables around the ring and think, "Two tables to put him through. And a ring bell for giggles afterwards." And don't forget what's conviently put under the ring by a few ring rats given an extra twenty to make sure they "forget"
they left a wrench or a cookie sheet there. [Dave looks back at the camera.] Dave Cigliano: You tell me what I see, McBaine. Tell me. Tell me because I want to hear you say it. You're going to call me the oportunist as well.
You're right, McBaine. Surprised? Probably not, but do you see the difference between us? No. You think just because I refuse to look at the ample weapons around and under the ring that maybe I look to see what else I can use for a weapon. Perhaps the cheers of the fans?
[Dave scoffs.] Dave Cigliano: Hardly. You want to know what I look for? It's simple,
McBaine. It's probably the same thing you scan over, but don't even notice.
A slight limp. A slight grimace at the twist of an arm. The slightest wince because a limb feels a bit of pain. That's where my weapon is.
Looking for a slight weakness pre-match that I can exploit during the match to have my hand raised in victory post-match. Before you try to save yourself saying you look for all those things, stop the lie now. You simply look for the means to do such things.
[Dave nods.] Dave Cigliano: That's what makes us different. You hurt as much as you can out of a neccessity to do it. I hurt the needed amount in order to accomplish my goals. That broken arm months ago? Accident. Had it been you? On purpose. You can tell because I have remorse for it; you wouldn't.
[Dave smirks mockingly towards the camera.] Dave Cigliano: But forgive me for not following the latest fads from the trendy promotions you work for, McBaine. You act like Bobby Taylor's wronged everyone this side of Dallas by throwing a tantrum like a spoiled brat on a playground who's not the center of attention. Give him his attention he wants so desperately. But just don't act surprised the next time he throws you a telegraphed curveball. While he throws his tantrum, and you scold him like a mother, you two share a common bond. Each week, you look to see what your current image is; to see what numbers you're pulling in just to see if the thousands who think they know wrestling are paying attention. That's our other big difference, McBaine.
[He points to himself.] Dave Cigliano: I get paid no matter what. My only bonus is in winning. Then I get more. For you? Well. Your whole career out in Los Angeles speaks for itself. The bigger the numbers get, the softer you get. Word of advice, McBaine. You may think about staying in LA permanently...
[Dave's face goes serious.] Dave Cigliano: ... before you get hurt. [Fade. Cut to the ring.] -- -- Ahab the Arab Queen of the newsgroup --Bestowed upon me by Wug and Jenn. Handler of some guys you've never heard of. Handles in feds you've heard of. Run a fed you may've heard of. Won some awards you probably don't care about. I'm a famous rock star who's in the Mafia. No, really. I am. -- Founder of RSPWF Boyz in da Hood. *throws up the RSPWF hand sign* RSPWF Siiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiide! Smokin' da chronic; sippin' da Colt 45.
