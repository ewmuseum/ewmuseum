RSPWF Card Posting...Regulations?

29 November – 12 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[29 November 1997 — The Saint (NIFW)] Hello. I was wondering if anyone else thinks that there should be a standard set of rules for people who post their federations' cards on this newsgroup. I am just getting sick and tired of the garbage that people are posting, claiming that they are the best federation, when basically, their card quality sucks! If you would like to be involved in setting up a set of rules, I have a couple of suggestions: 1) All cards and PPV's posted MUST be from federations that contain ORIGINAL WRESTLERS AND TAG TEAMS ONLY! I was just looking at one card that had Sean Waltman (1-2-3 Kid, Syxx of nWo), not to mention the tastelessness of the card and its quality. Bottom line, if you have real wrestlers in your federation, don't post the card to RSPWF! 2) Don't post your cards or PPV's in 10 or 15 different parts. If it is a good card, It should be divided into hours. Have a part for hour 1, and another for hour 2, and, if possible, a part for hour 3. This way, you don't have to post as manny messages, and your readers don't loose interest by the time they are done reading the 8 part. these are only a couple of suggestions that we can make regulations for posting on RSPWF. Let's keep the garbage out, and let the good stuff in! ------------------------------------------- "The Saint" [name withheld] President/Owner of New Internet Fantasy Wrestling! http://ewrestling.com/nifw/ *Remove 'nospam.' from the e-mail address to respond. ------------------------ [29 November 1997 — SJPW's handler] Just ignore the ones you don't like.
