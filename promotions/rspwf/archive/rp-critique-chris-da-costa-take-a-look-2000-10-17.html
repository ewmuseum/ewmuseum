[RP Critique] Chris da Costa [Take a Look?]

17 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I'm looking for some feedback and possibly a few critiques on this roleplay right here.. This is the second roleplay I've done with Christopher in HLWA and the third roleplay I've done for him all together. It's pretty much a "heatseeking" roleplay and just adding more fuel to an already kindling flame..
Enjoy! ------------------------------------------ [From an enveloping darkness, the scene dissolves into a small, candle lit motel room. The interior of the room is furnished in a typical fashion: a bed, a couch, a TV, and a wooden coffee table which's littered in a sloppy ill-kept disorder. At the couch in the far corner of the room sits an unidentified man.
His back is arched forward as his head rests gently across the wooden tabletop in front of him. His left arm is tucked cushioning his forehead and is also shielding his face as his right arm is stretched outward toward a bottle of empty tequila. This man is dressed in a pair of denim jeans, some Adidas athletic shoes, and a black T-shirt which reads "Coma Mierda" across the back in a green, white, and red striped print.
He begins to open his right hand to grab the tequila bottle and take another swig of it. As he does so, he raises his head to take a sip but notices that the bottle is completely empty. The now identified face of Christopher da
Costa begins to show disgust, his eyes widen, and his hands grip around the bottle tightly. Chris pitches the bottle toward the wall in front of him, watching it shatter on impact. The Latinos's eyes now begin to swell with a look of curiosity as he begins to speak in a Hispanic slur.]
CDC: Where the hell am I? [A look of unsureness b egins to beam across the face of CDC. Chris begins to get to his feet, holding his lower back in pain, and heads over to the window curtain just to the right of him. He pulls back the curtain and is blinded by a ray of sunlight that shoots through the window. He rubs his eyes gently to regain his vision and attempts to try and look through the window once more.]
CDC: What am I doing here? [Chris shrugs and closes the blinds. He begins to walk back over to the couch in a slow pace trying to think to himself slowly..]
CDC: Hmm.. I don't remember renting this room? Actually, I don't even remember what happened to me last night? Last I remember I was..
[He collapses down onto the couch and reaches out for a piece of fax paper that lays atop the wooden coffee table. Across the top of the fax it reads "HLWA
CARD LINE-UP FOR 31st OCTOBER, 2000 : "HALLOWEEN PHOENIX."" Christopher stares at the piece of paper, eyebrow arched, with his right hand massaging his forehead.]
CDC: El Pretendiente, Poly Schanks, and Wolf? Fatal Four way? [Christopher pulls the thin piece of paper closer to his face trying to get a better look at it.]
CDC: El Pretendiente? Hmmm.. How's that neck feeling, pal? [Chris egotistically winks.] CDC: Poly Schanks? Uhmm.. I'm damn sure glad I have no clue who you are. But how about.. [A grin begins to glow across the face of da Costa. His mouth opens with a chuckle as he drops the paper back down onto the coffee table.]
CDC: ..Wolf? The prepubescent teen who got his fair share on Phoenix? The very same Wolf that I left broken and humiliated last week? The kid who..
[Christopher's grin begins to fade as a look of "seriousness" arises.] CDC: did absolutely nothing to deserve what I force fed him on Flashback?! Look, Wolf, it's nothing personal but you're just another punk in my road to whatever it is I desire. I mean, sure, you're undefeated.. Yeah, you've got talent. But let's face it -- you aren't no _me_.
[He slowly shakes his head and waves his right index finger at the camera. He then kicks up his feet onto the coffee table and continues to speak, accented and all.]
CDC: Wolf, come and let me put you through the things I've been through. Live what I've lived, feel what I've felt. Then you can come talk to me. Come talk to me when you grow some hair on your genitalia. Come talk to me when your sack drops. Prove to me that you're worth more than the seventeen year old poseur you're made out to be.
[Yet again, the same beaming grin shines across da Costa's face. His ego is now starting to get the better of him as he drops his feet from the wooden tabletop to the floor.]
CDC: Forget the fame, glamour, and girls. Get those thoughts out of your head, Wolf. Stop masturbating to what you thought your future in wrestling would turn out to be. Remember, this is a sport.. Actually, no, forget being a sport. This is work, this is pain. Wolf..
[Chris begins to get up on his feet, grabbing his car keys on the way up, nodding with all seriousness.]
CDC: This is life. This is a life that not a lot of men _could_ live.. This is a life, however, that every man _dreams_ of livning. There's a difference between reality and the dream world, though. And right now, Wolf, your living the latter. You're riding high with your two wins, Wolf, but soon that ride will get bumpy. Soon, your ride will get a flat. And soon, Wolf, that ride will run out of gas..
[He begins to blow out the last of the still burning candles that are lined up along one of the windowsills. As the last of the candles are blown out, the room turns a pitch-black and silent. Fade out.]
