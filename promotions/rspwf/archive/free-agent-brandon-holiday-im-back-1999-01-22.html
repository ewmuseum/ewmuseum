Hi, I posted this about a month ago but didnt find the perfect league for me. If you are interested in my wrestler see the bottom of this profile. Name: Brandon Holiday Hometown: Los Angeles, CA Age: 31 Height: 6'2" Weight: 274 lbs Alignment: Heel Entrance Music and Artist: "Holiday" by Madonna Style: Extreme Brawler Five Favorite Moves: Vertical Suplex,DDT,Tombstone,Larat and Drop Toe Hold
Favorite Chain Moves:15 jabs to stomach
Finisher: The Holiday Express Finisher Description: Crucifix off Top Rope Set-up Move: Anything Does he signal for the finisher? If yes, how? No Wrestler's Apparel: Well My wrestler changes his gimmick for the nearest holiday So: Oct.Halloween:The Grim Reaper Nov.Thanksgiving:A Turkey Dec.Christmas:Santa Claus Jan.New Years: Party Animal Feb.Valentine's Day:Cupid March.St. Patrick's Day:Leperchaun April.Easter:Easter Bunny May.Armed Forces Day: Solider June.Flag Day: Dresses as an American Flag 4th of July:Uncle Sam August + September: A Tree Wrestler's Physical Description: Big, Muscular, short black hair and little goatee. Wrestler's Story: Brandon Holiday loved holidays as a child. He would love to help his mom and dad decorate and he just loved to celebrate. As he grew older, as all kids do, he kinda grew out of it. He really didn't care and just let them pass him by.
At the age of 15, Brandon Holiday was involved in fatal crash that killed both his parents. From there his obsession took over. All he could think about was holidays. He got in a drunken brawl at 16 in a bar. He was in there illegally but no one cared. He beat up 3 guys for saying, "Holidays Blow". Just a matter of luck a talent scout was there after a tough day at work. He needed a sensation. He saw Brandon and from there training started. Brandon worked 12 hours a day training and became an instant superstar. He went to PCW which was closed down for personal reasons. Now he wonders the street in search of a place to call home. It could be your league.
This is my wrestler. If you are interested to adding him to your roster please dont hesitate to email me. Some things I rather have in a league but am flexible are:
-email roleplays-no newsboards to post -Roleplay only around once a week -Webpage is required -Nice owner willing to chat Thats it. Just email me. [detail withheld]
