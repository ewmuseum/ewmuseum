[18 September 1997 — Jakester] Hey, Let`s have a President of RSPWF. Let`s have a constitution. Let`s set up RSPWFLand. I`m not joking. Let`s talk RSPWF Presidential candidates. Let`s have campaigns. Let`s have it all. One man, one vote. No votes for women. What do they know about wrestling, eh? Look what happened to the world when women got into power, eh? [ Sound of Jaketer getting into flame-retardant clothing and smiling an anarchistic "I`m going to die" grin ] It may be a bad idea, but it might just work.
