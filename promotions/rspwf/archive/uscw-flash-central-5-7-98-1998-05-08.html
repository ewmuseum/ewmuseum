USCW Flash Central 5/7/98

8 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Storm w/Venus Viper Moral Majority The Marauders The Horsemen -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- Storm: Oh, its so god damn funny isn't it Punk? Some son of a bitch decides to get revenge on me by getting my disqualified. Lets all have a good laugh.
Well Punk, you can thank that bastard for saving your worthless hide. Venus: Is everyone that jealous of your success? I mean, it seems everyone is out to cause you grief in one form or another.
Storm: Its the price you pay for being so damn good I guess. But Punk, now listen, I came into last nights match with the firm intention of breaking your neck. And I didn't sleep very well knowing that you were still walking and breathing. So what do I do about it? Thats simple, I want your ass in that ring one more time....and this time I there will be no one to save your ass!
Venus: Same goes for you Chris Evans. Now lately you have been relatively silent about the match next week, which from what I hear is so unlike you. I guess you are known for having quiet a big mouth. Maybe some people here are impressed with the little ginger toy you won, but the SWA couldn't give two shits about it. See, Storm already holds a pinfall win over you so we know that you're not that great. Your battle royal win was more of a fluke then anything.
Storm: Chrissy, I'm not going to waste my time commenting on how I'm going to beat you. Hell, you already know what I'm capable of. So basically I'm here to remind you to show up and make sure your insurance is paid up because
Chrissy, you is a going to receive one hell of an SWA ass kicking!!! And on that note, we are out of here!!!
Venus: The SWA isn't just a an attitude, its.......4 LIFE!!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Scene opens to Viper sitting backstage after tonight's Mid-Week madness show... Viper is wearing a "GDDC--I'M the new 4th member of the ONLY
Daniel Dare fan club!!!" T-Shirt, a "Marauder" Baseball cap, white jeans, and his SnakeSkin boots... Viper has the USCW World HeavyWeight Title strapped around his waist, and the UWF Unified Title strung over his right shoulder... Both belts are sporting their brand new White SnakeSkin straps... Viper looks around the locker room, then stands up and walks towards the camera)
Viper: Spicey, havin fun with those lawyers boy??? I KNOW you are, I hope ya have fun in court boy, cause you deserve EVERY fuckin thing you might get from all of this! So Spicey, what ya gonna do now, those three lawsuits are JUST the beggining, I'm seeing that you have a free wrestleathon comin up next week... GUESS WHAT Spicey, I'LL BE THERE!!
Better make sure the EMT's are closer to the action this time than they were last night... Cause the lawsuits WILL be flying, and I wanna make sure ALL of the potential plaintiffs stay alive for it... Now Spicey, I'm betting that yer sitting back in yer office feeling safe 'cause I seem to have "Given up" on USCW Headquarters... WRONG Spicey, just had other things to do... I WILL get in to the HQ whether you think I can or not...
Viper: So Spicey, yer troubles are JUST beggining, and it sure as hell doesn't look like Shining Path will be the one to stop them now does it???
SP LOOKED like he had a great potential, but all of a sudden., he's died out!! WHERE YA AT SP???? I was lookin forward to a GREAT match for MY
USCW HeavyWeight title... But I guess it'll just be another squash match for The Snake... Viper: So Spicey, why dontcha book me a REAL match, this little peon bullshit is pretty sad... I wanna defend this title against CONTENDERS not people who got lucky a few times, and now are in the top 5 rankings for the
USCW World HeavyWeight Title... Shining Path, next Wednesday night, you're gonna feel the pain of stepping in the ring with The Snake, and believe me when I tell you you WON'T be stepping out the USCW Champ... So Shining
Path, ALL of that potential wasted, all of that hard work ya did before, and look at ya now... Sheesh, one little loss and you get upset and quit??? Thatz pretty pathetic boyo...
Viper: Then there's The Marauder... Evans, I NEVER underestimate an opponent, if they are good enuff to step in the ring with The Champ, then they should NEVER be underestimated... So stop countin yer chickens early boy, you WILL be stepping in the ring with a FULLY ready USCW HeavyWeight
Champion... So Marauder, don't feel slighted, don't feel left out, as the time for our match draws closer, you'll hear MORE AND MORE from me... But fer now, I'll keep it brief, you haven't been forgotten NOR have ya been underestimated...
Viper: Now, Nut Boy... Tell me, are ya proud ya beat Jacques Strap??? I bet you feel on top of the world eh??? You beat a man named after yer favorite article of clothing... The one that holds yer favorite part of a man's body... Now THAT is sumthin to be proud of, hehe, Dare YOU are DA
MAN!!! HAHAHAHAHA, next up, maybe you can beat yourself, should be just as easy, and prolly just as quick...
Viper: Dare, that little face to face last night was the LAST time you'll see me up close, unless I'm kickin yer ass all over a ring... Spicey's lucky I was feelin belligerent enuff to do that last night, or you'd have been face to face with the cameraman... Wouldn't THAT have been fun...
Dare, yer time in the USCW is SLOWLY getting less and less... It won't be long until you finally step in the ring with me again, and then, when I pin yer ass and send ya runnin from here too... You'll know that you made a
HUGE mistake messin with The Snake... Viper: USCW, yer champ is tellin ya now, ya BETTER start to show some respect... Hell, What the Fuck is the BEST damn thing ever to happen to this piss ant fed, and if WE leave, you guys will be left with nothing...
Of course, after I'm done with Spicey, you'll be left with nothing anyhow... So USCW the reign of The Snake is just beginning, and it WON'T be ending til the USCW has closed down!!!
'Nuff Said -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <<Camera comes up to show Shining Path"the Terrorist", Chip Syske, and Tony Dylan in their office at the Moral Majority Health and Fittness Center in Texarkana, TX>> SP: Razor Sharp, getting in the cage and attacking me was one thing. Attacking my wife however was unexcusable. I'm really suprised Abyss didn't kick your ass for that. As obssesed as he is with my wife I would have thought that would have upset him. I'm glad he hasn't though I'm looking forward to doing it myself. Yes Sharp that is a challenge. Abyss
I really should have had you arrested when I was in L.A. I could have retained my TV belt the easy way. But now I am moving on to Viper. I will be the next World Heavyweight Champion.
TD: Now we have a challenge for the SWA. A tag match. Matthews and Storm vs. Chip Syske and myself. We are ready to enter Tag team competition and this is an excelent way to start. CS: The SWA needs to be put in it's proper place and we are going to do just that. <<Camera fades out>> -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ( Scene is outside where the next Weekend Edition is going to be. " The Marauder " Chris Evans, " Shotgun " Shawn Evans, and " The Enforcer " Mike Adams are all on hand signing autographs, and wearing their new shirts which have the Marauders logo on the front and says
" It ain't over yet! " on the back. Shawn is also wearing a baseball cap which has the Marauders emblem on it. )
Kid : Can I have an autograph Mr. Evans? Chris : No problem? What's your name? Kid : John. ( Chris signs the shirt ( The new Marauders Shirt ) To John : and then puts his signature on it. )
Chris : Listen up. Everybody waiting in line. I got a few words to say for the USCW, so just hold on for a couple of minutes.
( Crowd Cheers ) Chris : First of all, how's it going Max? ( BOOS ) I see you've been on a losing streak as of late. Well, when I face you, the losing streak ain't gonna stop. I've been on a winning streak, and I'm gonna wear you down step by step till I've got you down on the mat, 1, 2, 3.
( CHEER ) Chris : Second of all, Storm, you're right after Max. You can be as cocky as you want, you can say you've beaten me before ( LOUD BOOS ), but the bottom line is that me and you are gonna square off in the center of the ring with millions of fans watching. And those millions of fans will see " The Excellence " take on the likes of you.
( CHEER ) And I'll show you where I got that name. Chris : Thirdly, Viper ( BOOS ). I've got a lot of respect for you. ( BOOS ) You are a fighting champion, and you can start preparing whenever you want. I could fight you RIGHT NOW, and I'd be more prepared than ever ( CHEERS ). You seemed to be pretty proud when you got to Mr. Spicer. But now, you're taking on somebody your own caliber, so get ready.
Chris : Now, finally, I got a challenge to make to Scott Dakota. ( LOUD CHEERS ) You are one of the greatest to ever step into the squared circle. And ya never learn from facing the worst. So I'm challenging you to a match NEXT Weekend Edition. This isn't just any ordinary match. I want this to be a 45 minute Iron Man Match.
When I first went into the Battle Bowl, I saw you as my top competitor. Unfortunately for you, the others did too. So I wanna face you to PROVE I can last in the ring with you. And we'll see who's silenced. Mike : Speaking of challenges, me and Shawn have challenges for any card USCW wants to put us in. ( LOUD CHEERS ). You see, our friend Kid
Sharpe has been plagued by Daniel Stephens, and his buddies, Da Lench Mob. So I'm challenging Kid Sharpe ; ( BOOS ) to join with me and Shawn in a Wargames style match, whenever ( POP ). 4 on 4. If Kid Sharpe doesn't want to join the party, then I guess we'll have to find somebody else. Or The Marauders 'll go the extra mile. And that's what we're all about.
Shawn : Mike's said it. Kid Sharpe, I have a fairly good idea of who you want to join the party. Razor Sharpe. This'll be a perfect opportunity for you two to work as a team. I suspect we'll be facing, if they're brave enough ( CHEERS ) a team of Daniel Stephens ; Oh, I wanna hurt him... Seagram and Mopreme, who we still have unfinished business with, and probably the Deliquent, who is NOTHING more than a juvenile. So step into the cage. ( POP )
Shawn : ( Lifts Lighweight Belt ) Lightweights, if you think you deserve this, then come and prove it.
Shawn : Until then... ( Line starts moving forward, as people of all ages are getting autographs from the Marauders. )
FADE TO BLACK -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Scene: "Detroit" Diesel's garage.) "Detroit" Diesel Jones: My, oh, my! Da Lench Mob, you just don't know when to quit! How many times do we have to pound you into the mat in defeat before you learn were the best?
"Little" Joe Mountain: We do it as many times as need be to get a chance at those belts, because we natural champions.
"Big Man" Mountain: That's all you need to know! (The screen fades to black.)
