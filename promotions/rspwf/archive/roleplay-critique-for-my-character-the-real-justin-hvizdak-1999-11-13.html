roleplay Critique for my character "The Real" Justin Hvizdak

13 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ As it is told, Heaven is a mystical place. Streets of gold, reunions with late family members, no problems what-so-ever. In a single word, Heaven is...perfect. And, as the scenes opens up, you see white. Plain white. Not much can be seen right away. A nice harmony is being played. A deep, constant sound of people humming different pinches can be heard. As the clouds begin to seperate, 'Faithful' Justin Hvizdzak is standing there, motionless. ]
[ 'The Real' Justin Hvizdzak is wearing a black suit, along with a black tie. He stares into the camera without emotion, with a slight smirk on his face. Justin Hvizdzak opens up the Bible and begins to speak. ] [Justin Hvizdzak]: "Sitting here in front of me is, what I think, the best book ever written. I live my life by this book, I get through problems by this book.
If I do what this book tells me, I shall make into a peaceful afterlife in
Heaven. Everybody knows Heaven is a perfect life. But, when it comes to Toshio
Nakemura, life is about to become anything but perfect. I, Justtin
Hvizdzak...the follower of God will be going up againest the boy from the
Rising Sun, Toshio Nakemura. By the looks of it right now, it is going to be a good match. I stated the first words, and Toshio followed suit. So now, here I am, in a battle of words. In the Bible it says, 'Sticks and Stone Shall Hurt
Me, but Words Never Will.' Yes, I know what you are going to say. That is a childish saying but, even as adults, we have to realize that the world we live on is anything but the world God created it to be. So, with that said, let me to get to Toshio."
"Something I noticed is that Toshio did no talking. Well, he did but it was in
Japanese or something like that. So, it doesn't count. Throughout the whole interview, Jacob Moore Jr. did all of the talking. I'm not sure if Toshio can speak English or not, so I'm not going to hold this againest him as a big note.
But, what I am going to do is enroll him into a American School where he'll learn to speak English. See Toshio, once you learn to speak English...you will be able to make yourself look like a fool, not having Jacob Moore make you look like a fool. Get my point?"
"Ahh...here we go again. More on 'perfection.' Toshio, I have to look up to you though, you actually think that if you practice enough you will achieve perfection. And that is far from the truth. There is only one person that is perfect. And he is...God. But, Toshio, if you wish, continue to think that your perfect. I guess I'll look down on you in Hell. Thank You very much. Toshio, sure, you came into America and used some hold that is now banned in the United
States because of you. I see nothing wrong with that. But, saying that federations misused you because wrestlers would be afraid to wrestle you is totally out of this world. To tell you the truth, for this match, they could allow the Royal Lotus hold and I would still step into the ring with you. It may be risking my life, but it is worth it. I'm not afraid to leave this world, for when I do, I know I'm going to a far better place. You on the other hand...."
"Toshio, I do feel somewhat bad for you. I mean, you have the crazed lunatic,
Jacob Moore, running your career. It is no surprize that your career has pratically gone nowhere. If I were you, I would ditch the guy. No, nevermind. I rather enter the evil of Hell before I become you. Geeze, what am I thinking?
<<Hvizdzak slightly slaps himself in humor.>> Okay, now that everything like that is taken care of, I can get back to bussiness. Toshio, you should be happy you are facing a non-American oppenent. Think about it, would you rather get beat by a Polish guy or an American. It is obvious that either way you go, you're going to lose."
"Toshio, you need to tell your little butt-buddy to calm down. I'm sure Bob
Lane doesn't hate all un-American objects. If that was true, you and I wouldn't be here right now. Look at the facts. You are Japanese and I'm Polish. We are not Americans, so does Bobby Boy hate us? I don't think so. Well, he may hate you because you seem to have quite a reputation here. But, that is getting off the topic at hand. Toshio, it is true. Poland isn't known for it's wrestling ability. But, every now and then, you get one of those bright stars who find a way to become big. Surprize, Surprize! I'm one of those bright stars! By the way, just because I'm a flyweight and have an aerial finisher doesn't mean that this is going to be a slaughter. How do you figure?"
"Toshio, you are not a true wrestler. You must insult wrestler's because of where they come from. That just isn't right. I'm not even going to get into it.
But, just think about this. What comes around, goes around. Career Ending
Injury..I highly doubt that. And Toshio, once again, learn English you Japanese
Speaking Devil-Worshipper."
"And as for the rest of you, May God Be With You!" [ Once again, clouds begin to float into the scene making it nothing but white again. The humming and the state of peace come over you again. After a few moments, nothing happens. Stillness. The scene ends. ]
"The Real" Justin Hvizdak AMCW 6206 - 7 -13 LIVIN LA VIDA LOCA
