{Critique] I'm beggin ya, give honest opinions. Please,

29 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a little background before we start. This was an rp for the week leading up to a SEWF PPV, where Syko and a
Mystery Partner (later revealed to be Vic Valence), were taking on the SEWF
Tag Champions, Tommy Kain and Burt Clifton w/ Narcissa. Lots of bad blood between Syko and them. Thus, the match was announced to be a first blood match. Have fun, and critique the shit out of this rp. If you can rate it on whatever scale, that'd be nice too.
Thanks ahead of time. And now, our feature presentation. ------------------------------ << The Shawplex. In and of itself, it's just a building. It's also the place that various wrestling events are held. Including this upcoming Pay Per
View. Of course, you knew that already. Inside the Shawplex, however, 4 days before the big event, we can see Syko, sitting on the top turnbuckle in the ring that noone ever takes down. He looks around, almost lost in thought. Of course, it's the perfect opportunity for resident interviewer, Matt Hammer, to get in an interview with one half of the next tag team champions. >>
Matt Hammer: "Syko.... mind if we get a few words?"
<< Syko looks over at Matt, then hops off his perch, and opens the ropes for
Matt Hammer. >>
Syko: "Come on in." << Once Hammer is through the ropes, Syko rolls out to the floor, and grabs
2 folding chairs.... After tossing them back into the ring, Syko gets in, opens one up, and has a seat. Hammer does likewise. >>
Syko: "There will be no cute chicks in this interview. There will be no mention of drugs, booze, or cum-guzzling whores. There will be no cheese, no celebrity appearnces, nothing special. Just me, you, and that camera."
Matt Hammer: "Since when do you do serious interviews?" Syko: "Since this is a match I'm taking very very seriously. I mean, hell,
I'm achieving a dream that eluded me and my former tag partner for YEARS."
Matt: "The tag titles?" Syko: "Yeah.... You know.... it was the one thing Droo and I had totally in common in this business. He was in it for the work... I was in it for the fame.... He wrestled to achieve top honors. I wrestled to get paid. He trained harder than anyone I had ever seen..... and until recently, I hadn't really trained for a while.... But that was always our goal.... To get some tag gold.... so this way, when people years from now talk about some great tag teams, our name would be sure to come up. Now he's not here to share in this. And it really pisses me off."
Matt: "Tommy Kain has said he was sorry on a few occasions, as per his actions on your partner."
Syko: "So? What the hell does that mean to me? Andrew was wrestling hurt for the last 6 months. You think Tommy Kain and Burt Clifton had any more to do with that than Guru? The Outpatients? Mike Masters? Luni? Even myself? All they did was finish it off. But the fact of what's been going on in that camp has been eating at me ever since that bitch in black set her hooved foot into the SEWF. She turned TK into something he didn't wanna be. She turned Clifton from a top contender into a stooge, AGAIN. Maybe TK's wising up. Maybe he's seeing that he's been going down that same road Clifton's already miles down. And that's good for him. I'm happy he can think for himself like other NORMAL people. Because if he does as he's told in this match, he's gonna bleed heavily, as is his partner. If he's sorry, I can say on behalf of Andrew, apology accepted. But it's all going down the same in this ring come Critical Mass. 2 teams enter, and one team is leavin' bloody, and it sure as hell ain't mine."
Matt: "And your partner--" Syko: "Before you start grilling me about who I chose to carry the other 15 pound strap afterr we win, I'm gonna stop you right here, and tell you what you need to know. He's not a very nice guy once he gets to the arena.
Especially in first blood matches. I mean, if anyone knows how to make people bleed and scream in pain, it's him. Of course, this could be a number of people in the SEWF, and a number of people have come to me since the
Hammerlock interview, wanting to be that mystery man. But I knew who I wanted in my corner. And believe me, the names that came to me, you wouldn't turn down. But it wouldn't be right without this particular man. He'll definitely be helping the cause."
Matt: "Is it Andrew Krumm? Is he coming back for this match?" Syko: "I plead the 5th. You wanna know who it is, order the damn PPV." Matt: "And about Guru..." Syko: "Guru is the king of cheap wins. I will get him back, but not now. I have bigger fish to fry."
Matt: "Okay.... now... Why did you actually, really decide to piss off probably the biggest bitch in wrestling, and declare war on the tag team champs? Besides the obvious."
Syko: "Because that bitch.... has been a thorn in my side since I first met her. She's stuck up, arrogant, and needs to get laid badly, because if she doesn't get some soon, she may end up in an even more severe case of
Permanent PMS. She was part of hte reason I was held back in UWC. She was part of the reason I wasn't really used to my full potential here in the
SEWF, and dammit, she just don't know when to drop something. Coming after me, because I wanted to bang her. What kind of stupid shit is that? She wants my blood. Well, good luck getting it. Lets see you get within 5 feet of me with that pizza cutter."
<< Syko pulls out a long boxcutter. >> Syko: "Bitch, I'll slit your throat. You think I spent my whole life in the gritty streets and didn't learn a thing about spilling blood? Dumb ho. I know more ways to make you, and your two pawns... wait... one pawn and his partner bleed, than you know lesbian S&M sex positions. Step on the apron,
I'll SPIT a razor at you. You keep your ass out of this match, or it won't just be two wrestlers that'll need blood after this match. And you know blue blood bleeds best, bitch. I've spilled too much blood in my life already over so much stupid shit that I'm not about to bleed for some woman who doesn't understand the term 'come-on'. I plan to humble you. When I take your gold, I TAKE YOUR STROKE. And I bet you'll come at me with another team. Or another wrestler. Or a fuckin' hitman or something. And you know what? I'll take it all. And I'll come out on top. Because that's what I do.
I take what you people throw at me, and I come right back with some shit of my own. I drop bombshells. And I drop them on people like you, Narci."
Matt: "Ok.... you've talked about Narci, Kain, Guru, and even your partners, new and old.... What about Burt Clifton?"
Syko: "What about him? He's more of a bitch than his owner. I've already made my feelings clear as far as he goes. If he can't see the forest from the trees, then that's his problem now. I tried helping him before. I went out of my way for him. Now, if he wants to be noting but a slave, that's his own fucking problem. Maybe another Sykokick to the head will straighten his ass out. I'm not holding my breath though."
Matt Hammer: "A little word association, then i'll get your final thoughts...." Syko: "Go ahead." Matt: "Andrew Krumm" Syko: "The Hardest Working Wrestling School teacher today." Matt: "Guru." Syko: "Soon to be a victim of SIPD" Matt: "SIPD? Is that like his TVTC?" Syko: "Yeah. Syko Is Pissed Disorder. Victims are normally found bloody and beaten. Seems to be catching all over SEWF."
Matt: "Mystery Partner" Syko: "One half of the next tag team champs, guaranteed." Matt: "Burt Clifton." Syko: "More lost than Fang." Matt: "Tommy Kain." Syko: "On the wrong side of the ring." Matt: "Narcissa Vanderwal." Syko: "Cum-guzzling whore. Okay, so I lied before." Matt: "Dave Shaw" Syko: "About to shit his pants when he sees the buyrate skyrocket." Matt: "Matt Hammer." Syko: "Schill." *smiles for possibly the first time in the interview.*
Matt: "And anything you feel like saying, now is the time."
Syko: "I have just one more thing to say. Narci, Burt, Tommy.... it's gonna be a long, violent, bloody pay per view for you 3. You can't fuck with
Perfection. And you sure as hell can't make it bleed. You haven't done it for the past month. You're not doing it on Pay Per View. Or anywhere. COUNT
ON IT." Matt: "Thanks for your time, man." Syko: "It's my pleasure. It always is." << Fade. Yes, that's it. Bye. >>
