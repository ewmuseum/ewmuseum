[Flash] Scott Jamison (Flame if ya got time! )

12 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a Roleplay from the N.E.W. It is kinda stupid, but the end makes me laugh a little... So tell me what ya think!
-Kydd Handler of -Scott Jamison [N.E.W.] [Fade in...] [We see an overview of the Jack Delver Memorial Hospital located in down town
Las Vegas, Nevada. There are ambulances running in and out, the place looks to be crawling with people.]
[We see a shot of the outside of one of the rooms, Room 112. The door slowly slides open, and the camera crew files into the room. We see a man totally asleep in the bed, hooked up to many machines. An IV in his arm, and an oxygen mask and many many bandages cover his face. The man looks very familiar, but with all the bumps and bruises on his face, it is rather hard to tell one man from another.]
[In walks Miss Tammi Winters, manager of the N.E.W. rising star, Scott Jamison. She has a seat next to the bed, and she glares at the camera with an icy cold stare. She looks to be _very_ pissed off. She runs her hands through the hospitalized man's blond hair. She then speaks softly...]
Miss Tammi Winters - Look what you jackals have done! Look what you have done to _my_ Scott! All he ever wanted was to see the world... To travel the globe, and what better way to do it then join the National Extreme Wrestling organization. Well, he sure wasn't disappointed... He sure did get to see the world! This is the 17th Hospital that my Scott has been in this year!
[Miss Winters begins to cry a little... Holding back, she continues!] TW - He try'd to be the best, but he is still young! He is still a pawn in this game of Chess. And look what you fools did! You ruined your finest young star. This masked man ruined the N.E.W.'s finest young Star! Scott had a definite win lined up against some bum named Bret Fury. Now Bret Fury can't hold Scott Jamison's _jock_, but we didn't get to prove that, cause of what happened!
[Jamison, still in bed, begins to tremble in an attempt to rise. He sits up, and looks at the camera. Miss Winters begins to sob uncontrollably, and runs out of the room. Jamison is left, staring into the camera through the bandages that cover the better part of his face. He cracks a smile ... as he begins to speak.
Scott Jamison - [Softly ... but with a hint of gittyness in his voice] How's ... it... _Hangin'_... [The camera crew begins to chuckle, as even in a time like this, Scott Jamison is able to crack a joke and make people smile. Jamison was obviously poking fun at the incident that happened on last week Slam Dance... He continues slower than before...]
SJ - In all seriousness, I am in a _LOT_ of pain right now. This Friday on
Slam Dance, I am entered in a lethal lottery match. Now as I run down through the list of combatants, I see quite a few names that I recognize. John
Smith... Attrition... Scott Richards... Marcus Nyghtwulf... The list goes on and on. Right now, I am not too sure if I will be able to fight in this greatest of matches due to the extent of my injuries. I will try my best to get back into the swing of things ... and maybe turn some heads this Friday night. I may even have a few tricks up my sleeve ... who knows, that's my game.
I want to take a minute and congratulate a _VERY_ Close friend of mine named Tyrone Parker on his exceptional performance in the National Extreme
Wrestling organization. He is putting on quite a show, and has definitely improved since the day I took him under my wing back in the day. Due to a
_HOSE_ Call, much like the umpiring job in the American League Championship
Series ... you are not the N.E.W. Cruiserweight Champion, but in all reality, I saw his shoulders pinned to the mat, and I saw you win the belt fair and square... From this point on, don't worry about the shady calls, and don't worry about guys hitting you from behind ... as soon as I am back on my feet, I got yer' back!
Well, it is almost time for my sponge bath ... which happens to be the high light of my day. It is the only reason I put up enough effort to get up in the morning! My nurse's name is Bambi, and she is one hot number! 5'3"
blonde, and legs for MILES! [Jamison rings the call button ... and a voice is heard...] Voice - [In a _Strong_ Southern Accent...] Just a moment Mr. Jamison! [Jamison looks puzzled...] SJ - Wait a minute... that doesn't sound like Bamb... [Jamison is cut off with the sound on three _loud_ knocks, and the entering of a 300 pound woman with an all white outfit on! Jamison begins to squeal like a baby, as the screen cuts to a commercial...]
[Fade Out...]
