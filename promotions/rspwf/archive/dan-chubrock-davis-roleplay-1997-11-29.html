Dan "Chubrock" Davis GOING SOLO Part 1. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Preface: The following is a roleplay about Dan "Chubrock" Davis. This is more of a story than a roleplay. but, it is a roleplay. Please take it as it is. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ "Damnit" I said to myself. "What the hell am I going to do now?" as I walked into the locker room throwing stuff around. "Dan, it's ok." Lisa said to me. "Don't worry, we will go home and rest." "No, I just can't stop right here, I am at the peak in my career and I am not going to let myself down." I yelled at her. " Now, either you stay by myside or leave right now!" "Please Dan, don't make me do this. I love you, but, I can't stay in the wrestling business anymore....." Lisa walked over to me, kissed me, then broke down in tears as she left. I too must admint, that I felt the overcoming of hurt in my heart. I knew I had to let it go though. Then, I though, what am I going to do now. There is no one for me anymore. My best friend, the man I made, Bret Farrow, betrayed me. Now, he is off with other men, men that didn't make him. I helped so many people in the bussiness, and what do I get, NOTHING, no repesct, no nothing! I just lost my championship match, with help from Bret. Hell, I almost won a world title. Now, I come to the conclusion, where do I go from here. What do I do. Just as I am thinking this, Michael Bellard, the owner of this BJW comes bursting through my locker room door. "Dan, I have talked it over and, I am realising you from the BJW" Mike said. "Why?" I said. "Bussiness, Dan." I shook my head and thought, the world is crashing down, right infront of me. God, My best friend, my wife, and now my fed. Where the hell do I go from here? I was now overcomed by tears, and sorrow, I could not beleive this. I got in the shower. I could barley wash because of what just happened, hell, I could barley stand. I got out of the shower. Threw on a pair of jeans and a shirt. Put on my sunglasses, and jacket, picked up my bag, and precced to walk out the door. As, I was leaving the arena, I saw wrestlers, Eric Cooper, came up to me and went to shake my hand, I simply looked up and him then put my head down and started to walk away. I got into my car and drove off to a local motel. I checked in and got a room. I got to my room and fell right in bed. Although, I couldn't sleep all night. I was up crying and pre-occupied. I knew, there would be another day, for tommorow, I would have to once again, find myself. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Dan "Chubrock" Davis GOING SOLO Part 2 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ My alarm starts to sound for me to get up. I reach over to turn it off, then I get up. I try to keep what happened last night in the back of my head. I finally decide to get in the shower. About an hour later, I am ready to leave my room. I grab my car keys, and walks out the door. I get into my car, and go to a diner for some breakfast. Still, very upset, I sit down and eat some breakfast. Durin my breakfast, a fan, about 11 or maybe 12 years old comes up to me. He very kindly says, "Sir, can I please have your autograph." I gave the kid my autograph, thinking that may be my last one I give out. I tip the waitress, pay for my meal and leave. I then go back to my hotel room. I sit around all morning and afternoon, just waiting, waiting for nothing. Then, the phone rings. "Hello" I said. "Hi, Dan Davis?" the man said "Yes, who is this?" I said "Hi, this is Anothy Feeds, I run the IEUW" "Oh, yes, I have heard of the IEUW" " Well, I have heard you need employment, would you like to come fly to the IEUW headquaters for an interview?" Feeds said. I was so excited. "YES!" I said. "Well, I can see you tommorow if you could be here." "Certainly" I mumbled. "Hope to see you soon." Feeds, then hung up. I was so pleased someone actaully called me for employment, my self-esteem rised, as I never thought it would. I called a airline for a ticket to New York. I barley slept that night, just waiting for my alarm to buzz. Finally, it did. I popped up out of bed and jumped on the shower. I shaved, quickly threw on my clothes, and I got in my car and I was off too the airport. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Dan "Chubrock" Davis GOING SOLO Part 3 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I guess I arrived at the airport around 11:00 and my flight was to leave at 12:54, I wanted to make sure I was there in enough time. I basically sat around the airport just waiting. Finally, it came time to board the plane. I got my seating ticket and preceded to get on the plane. The flight was a few hours, but god, did it go by slow. My career was resting on the shoulders of the IEUW and Anothony Feeds. I got off the plane around 3:30 at JFk airport in New York City. I got a cab and went to my hotel. It was about 4:30 when I got to my hotel room and I had to be at IEUW offices at 5:45. I checked in very quickly and didn't even go to my room, I asked the bell boy to please bring my stuff up and put it in my room for me. I gave him a tip and he did so. I then got another cab and left for IEUW headquarters out on the southern tip of Long Island. I got there around 5:40. I told the secratary who I was and she said, "Yes, Mr. Feeds has been waiting. You can go right in." I walked into Feeds office and he stood up, "Hello, Mr. Davis." Feeds said. "Hello" I responded. "How was your flight and your trip here" Of course I was going to say good, "It was very nice, not bad at all" I quickly said. " You can have a seat" Feeds said, as he pointed to the chair infront of his desk. "Well, Mr. Davis. I don't really have a lot to say, but, I am very glad to have you here. I have with me a very short term contract. I would like you to look it over," Feeds softly stated. I read the contract very carefully as I always do. It was basically about a two month contract. With no clauses, except for an injury clause were it said if you are injured, you can be realased from your contract. "Everything looks fine with me." I impatiently said. "First, you must have a tryout with us. Luckily, we are having our next show here in New York. I would like to see you come down and have a match. If everything goes well, we can sign the contract." I was so happy. "Sure!" I replied. Mr. Feeds stood up and shook my hand. "Hope to see you Saturday" As I left the office I had a HUGE smile on my face. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Dan "Chubrock" Davis GOING SOLO Part 4 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I just had my meeting two days ago with Anothony Feeds. It is now Saturday afternoon and I am really starting to get nervous about my match. I got up out of my hotel room and caught a cab to a local gym. I wanted to work out a bit. I figured I would work out until about 3:30 then go get something to eat, then head on over to MSG, where the card was taking place. I ended up working out until 4:00. Then I decided to stop by a pizza joint and grab a few slices a peices. I could barley eat anything because I was so nervous. After the pizza, I headed over to the arena. I was supposed to be at the arena around 6:30 and I got there about 5:45. I was greeted by Anthony Feeds at the arena. Mr. Feeds introduced me to John Dewie. John was a booker for the IEUW and was the man that stayed backstage. John was very nice. Anothony said he had to go, but he would see me after the show. John stayed with me and showed me to my locker room. At this point I couldn't beleive how good the IEUW was treating me. I actaully got my own personal dressing room. I got to my room about 6:15. At about 6:30 Bill knocked and told me to have my wrestling warmup gear on in 15 minutes and be out at the ring. I got my stuff on and left towards the ring. I walked out to the ring and the arena was still being setup for the show. The ring was ready and I could see my opponent in the ring with his gear on too. John said " This is your warm up guys" John then stated what he wanted to happen in the match, and I was chosen the winner. Me and my opponent, practiced the end a few times until we had it down, then we left to go backstage. I had no idea who my opponent was. I just supposed he was a local indie wrestler. I got back to my dressing room about 7:00 and I was to to be ready by 7:45. I got into my regular wrestling gear and did some warm-ups. By that time it was 7:35. I did my prayer and left my room. I think I had the longest walk of my life down the hall to the aisle to the ring. I finally got there and my oppenet, was waiting to be announced. Finally the announcer said his name. My opponent left. Then, I heard my music start, I was really nervous now and umping up and down. The announcer then called my name and I went through the curtain. I got a huge crowd response. Since, I am popular on the east coast. I made my way to the ring and got in. I took my gear off and worked the crowd a little bit. The bell rang and my opponent went to work on me. I thought he was a pretty good wrestler and sold my moves well. I controlled most of the match. Then as I was about to take the win. He whipped me into the ropes, just as we had planned, then he put me into a tilt-a-whirl tombstone piledriver, and then, the lights went out. It was supposed to happen like that, he put me in a tilt-a-whirl tombstone then, i kicked out and rolled him up. But it didnt. He hit the tilt-a-whirl and the lights just went off. I was knocked uncounsious, I couldn't remeber a thing after that. Suddenly, I woke up. I was in a hospital. A nurse was right there and said, "hi Mr Davis. " I was so dazed I couldn't respond. The nurse told me the date and it was Sunday afternoon. I slept fine that night, although I still didn't know what was going on. On monday morining, I got a call from John Dewie. He told me everything that happened. He also said the contract had been cancelled. So once again, I was unemployed. On monday, I got some test done. The doctor then said I could leave the hospital. I got my stuff together on Tuesday and walked out the door. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Dan "Chubrock" Davis GOING SOLO Part 5 ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ So, here I am. Going low on money. Unemployed, no friends. What do I do? I am to scared to go to my family because they will think I am a falure. I can't let this bother me. So, I caught a cab and went to the bus station. I got to the bus station on Tuesday in the afternoon. I purchased a ticket to Trenton, NJ. This was my hometown, this was where I lived. What would happen when I returened. Would Lisa be there. What? I got to Trenton around 7 pm that night. I then got a taxi to my house which is in the outskirts of Trenton. The car pulled into my driveway and I saw Lisa's car parked there. I paided the cabby, got my bags and started to walk up to the door. I stuck my key in and slowly opened the door. Lisa, was watching T.V. and she just stared at me while I was standing at the doorway. She then got up, ran over to me and hugged me, crying. That night we talked it over. I told her everything that had happened, and I told her wanted us to work things out. She agreed. We then stayed up all night just talking. The next morning, the phone rang while I was eating breakfast. "Hello?" I said "Hi, Mr. Davis home?" the man replied. "Speaking" I said "Hi, this is Adam Payne from the ICW. I was wondering if you would like to come work for us here?" "I...I..." I simply coulnt speak. Then i yelled "YES!!" I was off........I was off to a great wrestling company. I place I could show my skills. Could I be on the top of the world once again. Could this work for me. Well, the future can only tell. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ ©1997 Stunzeed Productions Please tell me what you guys think. thanx
