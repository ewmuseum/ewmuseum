[Free Agent] Experienced Handler, Looking for a Top Notch Fed.

27 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've handled this character for the last 2 years, and he's been with the National Wrestling Council since March '98, and it's about time that he moved on. Here's what I'm looking for....
- Established, preferably a high profile fed. - RPs done in packets or shows, no BCC...no messageboard. - One / Two shows a week. - Either an updated webpage, or a weekly report sent by e-mail. That's about it, and...here's a sample RP: (A wise man once said, "The More Things Change, The More They Stay The Same..". An argument can be made that he was thinking of the life of Logan Gunz when that statement was made.)
(At one time, Gunz was at the forefront of a young crop of NWC stars...just waiting to get their shots at the big time. There was a time when he was the young lion, the newcomer, the rookie with the hunger for success...it seems ever so far away now.)
(Over the last year and a half, Logan Gunz has been forced to endure things that would have destroyed the career of a lesser man, the kidnapping of his younger sister by Ray Pierce, twice being double-crossed by his own brother, passed up time and time again for shots at the gold...not because his performance in the ring is lacking, he's only lost one time in his NWC career...but because he's always insisted on being his own man.)
(However, there's always been one thing that the promoters, the Executive
Council, Madison, nor anyone else has ever been able to supress...the way that the common man relates to Logan Gunz. He's never been the one that graced the main event, he's never been the one with the beautiful technical skills, he's never even been the one that the guys in the locker room took out for beers after a hard night's work...in fact, his list of NWC accolades is quite short.
However, despite all the dissapointments in his career, and in his personal life, Logan Gunz has always been one thing...beyond "The Lone Enforcer", past
"The Panther", and something that the word "Annihilator" does not quite touch...above and beyond everything, Logan Gunz has always been a "Warrior".)
(The scene fades in to the now familiar surroundings of the small children's shelter, "Stick To Your Gunz, Georgia", that the Gunz family operates to help young people through the tough times that they're all too familiar with.
Nestled comfortably on the outskirts of Atlanta, Georgia, it serves not only as a refuge to the children who find the family that they've never had here...it serves as a sanctuary for the man who started it all.)
(Logan is seated on "the rock". A large granite boulder that stands stoically in the center of a large field just adjacent to the "STYGG" complex. It was while seated upon this rock that Logan made the decision to accept the FWF offer and enter pro-wrestling, it was on this rock that he sat and mulled over the offers of Ken Nemesis and his fledgling SWF, and it was while seated on this rock that he first had the vision for the children's home that his family now operates. Indeed, no matter how much things may change for Logan Gunz, they inevitably end where they began.)
(A hulk of a man, almost indistinguishable from the rock itself due to his chiseled features and physique. The broad expanse of his chest and back are covered by a black t-shirt that reads "100% GUNZ" in deep blue lettering across the front. His powerful arms are folded in front of him, each sporting the now familiar tattoo of a panther. Atop his head is the ever-present blue bandanna, tied tightly in the back and reading "GUNZ" across the forehead. Sullenly he sits atop the rock, his steel green eyes...legendary in their own right...fixated upon the western sky where the sun is gently setting over the horizon. The vibrant oranges and reds dance and collide with the green in his eyes...creating an almost "inferno-like" effect. Is it the lighting of the sunset, or the flames that burn within the abyss of his soul that we see?)
Logan: So...it all comes down to this. (Gunz speaks methodically, not so much towards the camera as towards himself.) Logan: For over a year, I've lived with the questions... "What if?" "Could you have?" "How might your career had been different, only if?" And, after all this time...I'm finally going to get the answers that I seek. (Gunz balls his fist, and raises it before his emotionless face.) Logan: That's right...after a year of questions, Dread Saxon, it's finally time. Ever since that ill-fated night in Seattle, Washington...the night that altered the course of my career...the question has eaten away at the very fiber of my being.
"Could I have beaten Dread Saxon?" Would you have been able to step into the reaches of the forest with your NWC
World Championship, and walked out unscaithed? I was at the top of my game,
Saxon, the panther had streamlined and become a predatory machine. These steel green eyes had peered down upon you from high up in the trees, and they had chosen you to be the next victim of "The Annihilator".
(Logan now unclenches his fist and gingerly touches the back of his head, almost as if he is feeling for a fresh wound.)
Logan: But, with one swing of the Emerald City Championship belt...all that changed. Two forces on a collision course were suddenly diverted, and two men's lives were changed forever.
You see, it was Logan Gunz versus Shooting Star in a lumberjack match for the
Emerald City Title...and a shot at your NWC title, Saxon. You had just begun your first title reign, and the name Dread Saxon was not yet known throughout the civilized world as it is now.
I had nailed Shooting Star with the Shotgun Press, the lights were out and the
Emerald City title was soon to be consumed by the forest. That's when
"Hurricane" Andrew struck, one time to the back of my head with that belt...and a career sent into limbo. You would go on to defeat Star, but instead of being launched into the cosmos as you were...you should have been lost in the depths of the forest.
(Gunz now reaches down and slowly strokes his goateef. He is pensive, very much so, as he looks back over what might have been.)
Logan: That, Saxon, was a long time ago...and we are now different men. But, I have been given a second chance...
(The thought sparks a twinkle in his eye, his pupils begin to contract almost into nothing more than slits set deeply within his green iris.)
Logan: Not a chance at the NWC title, but a chance to reintroduce the Gunz to the NWC. A chance to stand amidst the trees of the forest and announce to the fans of the National Wrestling Council that the predator is back.
Not since that fateful night in Washington have these steel green eyes peered at a man of your ability...not since then have they been confronted with as deadly an advisary...and never have they turned towards a man that I respected as much as I used to respect you.
But, that was then... You indeed were "The Franchise", Dread Saxon. You were indeed the man that the people turned to when they needed a hero, and you were the man that the people knew would never back down from a challenge. What you are now is an indignity to the man that you used to be.
(Finally the stoicism of Gunz's face begins to subside, it's replaced by a look of pure disgust.)
Logan: You not only turned your back on every wrestler in the NWC that respected you, but you turned your back on the very people that raised you to the level that you competed at...the fans. You turned your back on the young boy in Detroit with the Dread Saxon t-shirts in his closet. You turned your back on everyone that came to an MCW show with their face-painted like you, and you turned your back on the men and women that found inspiration in your exploits...
They have not forgiven you, and they have not forgotten. The people, the fans of the NWC have determined that it is now time that your wretched memory be wiped clean from the NWC slate. That the time has come for the "great" Dread Saxon to be taught some humility. It's time that you're taught that there is penalty for actions that are based in greed...
(Logan finally turns the steel green eyes towards the camera. They burn with an intensity that no other NWC star can match...an intensity that threatens not only to consume those who gaze into them, but the man himself.)
Logan: ...and "The Warrior" Logan Gunz...he'll be your teacher for these lessons. At Basic Training, I'm going to reintroduce Logan Gunz to the NWC. Not as "The
Lone Enforcer" of old, the man that was hell-bent on the destruction of everything in his path. Logan Gunz will be introduced once again as "The
Warrior", "The Last Warrior". The man that is hell-bent on not only destroying everything in his path, but obliterating the legacy of evil that they attempt to leave behind.
The people have asked for a savior in these dark days and times, a man that they can look to make a stand. A man that cannot be corrupted by the evils that accompany this sport, and a man that does not know the meaning of the word,
"surrender".
They've found that man, and his name is Logan Gunz... (Gunz raises up and stands atop the rock. He raises both hands to the sky....) Logan: DOOM ON YOU!!! Dread Saxon, for "The Warrior" has come...with the mission to close down "The Franchise".
(His face deathly serious, his demeanor echoing that expression. Gunz jumps down from atop the rock and begins to walk off into the darkness that has fell over Georgia.)
(Can he recapture the NWC stage? Can he finally fulfill his destiny and goal of becoming NWC Champion? One thing is for sure...either he will accomplish those goals, or he will die trying...)
(END)
