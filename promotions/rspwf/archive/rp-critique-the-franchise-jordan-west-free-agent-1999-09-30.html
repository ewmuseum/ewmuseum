[RP Critique] "The Franchise" Jordan West (FREE AGENT)

30 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am a free agent, and I am looking for the following in an e-fed: -InsideTheWeb Message Board to RP -Original wrestlers ONLY -Preferrably established for a month. -Talented roleplayers -Don't overdo the hardcore gimmick. Any fed with Extreme or Hardcore in the name will be discarded. It can have hardcore matches and some hardcore wrestlers, but I don't want feds based on hardcore.
-One card per week ONLY, except PPV weeks. -Dedicated Prez and/or Staff -Full, in-depth results. No funny garbage, no highlights only, no effortless results. -Based on quality of roleplays, not _so much as_ quantity -ABSOLUTELY NOT based on simulators, dice, or whatever. -Decent web design, but don't overdo it. Fast load times. -No popups whatso-fuckin-ever. I'm so sick of those. email: [detail withheld] AOL IM: [detail withheld] ---------- Now to the roleplay. A little background info here: Jordan West is a pompous cock known for his abrasive remarks and brutal attacks. He is somewhat of a pretty boy. He is heel, but the crowd loves him anyway. He has short dark brown hair slicked back, electric blue eyes, the build of say... Shawn
Michaels, and his attire is nearly identical to what the Hardy Boyz wear. Here's the roleplay. The descriptions aren't as detailed as usual, it's short just because of the setting of this particular RP. Fedheads, check it out.
Fellow RPers, critique please. Oh and, be gentle :). --------- [The scene fades into a dimly lighted, empty wrestling arena, or to be exact, the very arena the PPV will be held. A spotlight is on a figure in the squared circle. The figure is sitting on a steel chair in the center of the ring, with his head hanging down as strands of hair falling over his face, and beads of sweat trickle down his forehead. As the camera closes in, the man reveals himself to be "The Franchise" Jordan West. He has his wrestling tights on, suited up and ready to go, regardless of whether or not he has a match at the
PPV. The camera rotates slowly around West.]
Jordan West: You know, I don't resent criticism... I don't resent it, at all. Even when, for the sake of emphasis, it parts for the time with reality... d [Jordan stands up and screams, with his voice echoing throughout the arena with every word.]
Jordan West: BUT WHEN A MAN GOES ALL OUT AND TALKS SOME UNREAL FUCKIN' BULLSHIT, YOU HAVE TO TAKE ACTION. [West runs his hands through his hair to push the loose strands away from his face and puts on his sunglasses. He calms down somewhat, but is still pissed as hell. He then walks over to the ropes and rests his arms on it.]
Jordan West: And, Sgt. X, YOU are unfortunately that man, and YOU are going to get your ass OBLITERATED by me WHETHER YOU LIKE IT OR NOT. Be it Massacre, be it Fright Night, hell, even in your god damn sleep I'll be kickin' your military ass from here to the yellow brick road, and the fact that you can't handle even the first kick means nothing because Franchise holds NO MERCY for
ANYONE, even when it means West gets screwed too. Face it, live with it, and die a happy fuckin' man, because I can assure you, Sarge, come hell or high water West WILL get what West wants.
[There is a brief pause of silence as West regroups the feelings of anger. He slowly paces around the ring while talking.]
Jordan West: Don't get me wrong... I hate making guarantees, I hate having to live by my word every single damn time I confront someone... but you know what?
If you ever... EVER get in my way, I'll make damn sure of it that I will be all over you like FLIES ON DOGSHIT. Trust me, you don't know just how much punishment I can dish out. So lift those weights and get that training,
'cause, huh, you're goin' to need it when you're up against the finest wrestler in the industry today.
[West pauses in signalling a change of subjects. He brushes the sweat off of his forehead with his forearm and continues to pace around the ring.]
Jordan West: Now, let's talk about some high class idiots, like Justin Time. Frankly, all he is to West is Just-a-Moron, and I couldn't give a rat's ass whether he was the champ or not, either way, he is what he is, and I feel no need to go further into detail. But let's talk about this guy's attitude and personality. Just like all the so-called IWL stars, he's another one of those egocentric bastards that claim to be "Elite" and frankly, it's makin' me sick.
This guy's got a little more than just a few screws loose on the head. He proclaims the outrageous idea of being the Icon, when in fact he's got jackshit to back it up. He goes around and acts like he's the picture-perfect embodiment of wrestling prowess. And then, he has the audacity to consider me an ALLY. Let me tell you something, Time. I trust no one, and no one better trust me because I will jump up and bite you in the ass like all the other rabid dogs in the IWL, so I suggest you cut the crap about roots, and that
4-Life, 'back me up' bloodbrother bullshit 'cause it's trite, it's overused, and frankly, it's making me SICK. What's my point? Just watch yourself,
Justin. You've got your head so far up your ass you couldn't even give your mother the time of day.
[He makes another brief pause to change the subject. He paces around behind the steel chair and rests his hands on the backrest.]
Jordan West: Last, and for the most part, LEAST... Enter the Bomb Squad. Now, I look at all the wrestlers that run away from these schoolgirls and ya know what I do? I LAUGH. Yeah, I find it fuckin' hysterical that Chris and his prom date have made a name for themselves... Hmm, what's the word? It's on the tip of my tongue... Oh yeah, MORONS. If these girls aren't a bunch of full-blown, grade-A MORONS, I don't know who is. Let me tell you something,
Chris. Just because YOU feel that you have the responsibility in 'guiding the
IWL to the top', sure as damn hell doesn't mean that what you say goes, and it's the 'be all, end all' decision of the world, let alone this organization.
Now, listen... do you think anyone here gives a good god damn what you think? Do you think that anyone here would risk LIFE AND LIMB just to see a sorry ass, insignificant little prick like you 'rise to the top'? I for one DON'T, and I really wouldn't give two shits if you two morons dropped dead in front of me.
Now, I see that the Squad is slightly of a higher status than the rest of the
IWL wrestlers, I'll give you that, but, you know what? I, for one, am not scared the least bit by you. Chris Therin... Dan Slate... The Bomb fuckin'
Squad... Notorious tag team renowned, the true heroes of the Elite! HAIL! [West stops to hear the echo of his voice, that was abrasively used to mock the duo. He has a caustic smirk on his face, and spits in disgrace to the name of the IWL and the Bomb Squad.]
Jordan West: BIG F-CKIN' DEAL. [Jordan's voice echoes once more, and he slowly exits the ring, walks up the ramp, and disappears into the curtains, with an echo following his every step.
The scene slowly and eerily fades to black.] -- AFKAK3:16--The Artist Formerly Known as KuRuPt 3:16 The Innovator of Fire Pro VIOLENCE "Who's your daddy?!" "Why can't people just FLUSH?!" -- Triple H on Stone Cold TV Remove "nospam" from email.
