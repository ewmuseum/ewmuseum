I focused more on describing my character than the actual dialogue as he dosn't really have much to talk about, just that he wants in a fed, I didn't want to have him blab on any longer about that. This is my new character. Please tell me what you think. Particularly, I'm looking for a fed that my character would fit in as a mid-carder. I don't want a fed that'll have me as a main eventer, but I don't want to constantly job either. Most of my RP's will be longer than this due to the fact that I have something to talk about usually. But, this is my sample. If you want Tru Thug to join your fed, please E-mail me at
