[Another AWA Roleplay]Tony Lattanzio

3 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in] [The scene is once again the den of Tony Lattanzio. He is wearing the same clothes, but this time more buttons are unbuttoned down his shirt.
His head is in his hands, and looks very frustrated. The huge grandfather clock reads 2:24, very late at night. Once again, the small light source can bearly penetrate through the powerful illumination of the darkness. Tony lifts up his head, but doesn't even acknowledge the camera. He gets up from out of his chair, and walks over to the massive bookcase. He searches for awhile, then finally takes his choice from out of the stack of literature.
It is an old book. The camera picks up the title, "Holy Bible." Tony sits back down, and begins to read the Holy Bible. After a couple minutes of deep-reading, he glances up towards the camera.]
Tony Lattanzio: Ressurection. The Savior, Messiah, Yahweh. Jesus Christ came into this world 2,000 years ago, on the 25th of December. Christmas we call it. 30 years passed of miracles, preaching and professing his faith. 30 years his life ended. But wait. Was it just his "bodily" life? Yes, after the third day rose from the dead, and for 40 days he was back on earth, professing his faith and showing his love for his Father. Then after those 40 days, he ascended into heaven, and is seated at the right hand of the Father. I love reading the Bible, it releases all the worries and tensions of the daily life. I may be a brutal, inconsiderate bastard, but I do believe in the
Trinity.
[pause] Tony Lattanzio: I am having my own "ressurection" to put it bluntly. After being down in the depths and slums, I have risen to the glourious place, a winning streak. Soon, I will be seated at the right hand of the wrestling gods, with the AWA World Heavyweight Title wrapped around my waist. The devine spirit will declare me the ruler of all that wrestle. My path is laid down, whether it be a red carpet or a yellow brick road. It is stated in the holy book of Apex that one man will ascend above all others and settle the score with the side of evil, the side of darkness, the side of the
Badassess. Yes, that's right. Not only am I going to go after BlackJack, but the Rough Riders and Big Bully Brandon have tricked "Eve" into biting the fabled apple. The AWA is rotten with evil spirits, and Tony Lattanzio's mission is to rid them all, and take his throne at
