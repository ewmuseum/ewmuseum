[Critique][ ' Pure Extacy ' Jason Jarrett ]

21 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just A Little Background Info, My Character Is Part Of A Stable Called Elite N' Revival, One Of My Opponents All Hannan Was In The Original Elite, Muffy The
Clown Is His Partner, Mine Is Stephen Stratford. Also My Whole Gimmick Is To Be
A 'P.I.M.P' Politically Incorrect Mother Phucka! Well Critique It Nicely, If
You Don't Like It...Then YOU SUCK!
-J a Y [A thick hazy fog fills a neighboorhood street, nothing can be seen as the camera speeds foward, fog rushes around it moving faster and faster as the camera burns down the street. nothing can be seen for the complete one-hundred and eighty degree viewing area of the camera. Fog, everywhere, like a plague overtaking a neighborhood...the camera slows down...there is light...it is a faint glimpse of hope in an otherwise worthless and useless attempt to avoid this 'plague.' The light gets brighter and brighter...shimmering off of a car on the right, a stop sign to the left...and ahead...a brick wall...the camera slams into the wall and shatters into a thousand pieces...another camera placed above the brick wall aimes downward and shows the shard of camera pieces laying on the ground. The camera pans up and shows the fog and then turns around and leaning up against a tree is none other than xWs Phenomenon Jason Jarrett. He has a bit of a smirk on his face as he begins to speak.]
Jason Jarrett: In case you missed the complete point of that whole series of events...it's my portrayal of All Hannan's career...complete nothing for several years...and then a faint glimpse of hope...and then [ raising his voice in anticipation ] BAM! It comes to an abrupt, and violent halt. Similar to that which will occur this weekend. See All, or Tim as your friends like to call you...you are right now in the light...you have just come to the high point of an utterly useless and pathetic wrestling career, you have reached your pinnacle...and just like the images you just saw...YOU WILL SLAM INTO THAT
BRICKWALL TIM! Your career will come to an abrupt and violent halt this Sunday at Sickness, when you slam into that brickwall...that brickwalls name...Jason
FUCKING Jarrett! Yes Tim, the one man you hate the most in the xWs...the one man that you despise more than the thought of Playgirl being taken off the stands. Well Tim, I am more than honored to be the one that ruins your career...I am more than honored to crush your pathetic attempt at reviving a worthless career.
See but Timmy, the difference between you and me, the difference between my sucessful career, and your poor generic excuse for a career, is that I am focused...something you lack. You seemed more interested in the fact that Joe
Montouri made the right decision to dump your ignorant punk ass and side with me...as did Chris Cage...and then you want to go on and cry because of Bonz
Williams, well hey, I'll do you a favor, I retired his bastard ass just a few months ago...if Joe gives me the opportunity I will happily do it again! But
Timmy, it's okay, I would want to skirt the issue if I was fighting somebody like me and Stratford. I would be scared as well, especially with somebody like
Muffy The Clown as my partner. You obviously weren't thinking when you picked him for the All Hannan Team, much like when you called me and Stratford Elite wannabe's...what the fuck are you talking about ignorant fucker? Just because
YOU weren't invited into the reformation of YOUR STABLE, doesn't mean you have to go and cry Timmy...I mean it'll be okay, you have guys like FOB and Muffy and Jason Perfect watching your back...I mean fuck, all we got over here is The
World Champion, The Riot/Hardcore Champion, The President of the xWs, the greatest female wrestler in the xWs, and then of course THE MOST TALKED ABOUT
WRESTLER OF THE MILLENIUM...ME! Shiiit Timmy, I almost feel bad because the deck is stacked, hell you could even add Bonz Williams tothat line up, it matters not to me, just another victim of Jason Jarrett...as will All Hannan and Muffy The Clown will be this weekend!
[ Jason starts walking closer and closer to the camera at the edge, overlooking the wreckage below resembling All Hannan's career. ]
Jason Jarrett: Well Timmy, ya know, you have been away for a while, and maybe when you left I was a low to mid-carder, but what I was when you left has nothing to do with what I am now are two entirely different things...you will find out first hand, just like Bonz did. And as for Sinnery turning on me...WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT...I never once was aligned with Tom
Sinnery, and as for the East Coast title...piss on that title, I never once even fought for that title, so your stupid ass better get your ignorant facts straight. And not main eventing? SHIT ARE YOU STUPID? I have main evented more times than you can imagine...so how about you and your fourth grade intelligence level take your punk bitch ass back to whatever third world country you came from and try to learn something!
Now Timmy, I listened to everything you had to say, several times even, and you had the audacity to say that I had no talent, to say that I don't deserve to be where I am...ya know what Timmy? FUCK YOU! I served your little boyfriend
Bonz...I served your little boyfriend Blade...and you...well you'll just add the to FORMER ELITE TRI-FECTA THAT JASON JARRETT is going to complete. Timmy you want to go on and cry about all the other little bastards you want to get your hand on...Garrett Caldwell, Ned Mace, and even Tom Sinnery, as if you hadn't learned enough already...Sinnery has your number Timmy...you can't beat
Tom Sinnery...but I, I on the other hand, PROMISE to do what All Hannan, Bonz
Williams, Blade, and even Johnny eXtreme couldn't do...I WILL RUIN TOM SINNERY!
I will get my hands on him in due time, and I will do what All Hannan never could! And Timmy, you know you don't have to worry about me coming half-assed,
I have been doin this shit for too long to fuck around with a pussy like you...I'll come in, get the job done and leave just like that, it's that
FUCKING SIMPLE Timmy...you're ass is mine...and if Muffy thinks he can fuck with me, then he is more than welcome to try but he will get his ass beaten just like you Tim.
[ Jarrett gets to the edge and looks down at the wreckage, he lets out a slight laugh before returning his attention to the working camera... ]
Jason Jarrett: It's almost sad Tim, that you think you have a shot at winning with somebody like Muffy The Clown as your partner. Somebody who has...done absolutely nothing in my recelection. I could give a fuck less about this little Barnum And Bailey REJECT...he is just gonna get in the way of me kicking your ass Tim! Just prolonging the inevitable, avoiding the truth, kinda like the constant 'racist' comments I am getting from you Timmy...I am not a racist..faggots are not a race...jews are not a race...mexicans aren't even people in my book, so therefore they CAN'T be a race. And by mexican I include lame ass bastard 'Ricans' just like that worthless rican fucker Imagine...in my opinion Ricans, Cubans, and anybody who looks like a dirty wetback, is, well just somebody who is ashamed of being mexican and is just trying to avoid the fact that he is just in-store for a life of building houses and mowing lawns!
Apparently Imagine broke the mold...but he is still not considered a person in my book, so therefore when I make fun of him and his wetback ass family, then I am not being racist...do you understand Tim? Or do I need to break it down for you even more? Maybe you, Muffy, Perfect and FOB can get together and think about it for a while, I'm sure combined between all of you is somewhat of a brain!
But then again Timmy, I can't say much about yours, look at who you've surrounded yourself with. You go from leading one of the most talented stable, with guys like Cage, Montouri, and even Stallion, and Blade, I wouldn't say the same talent-wise about you and Bonz, but you were surrounded with talent, and then you go and surround yourself with these stupid fucks...what the hell is wrong with you Hannan? ARE YOU THAT FUCKING STUPID? Well obviously if you think are going to win this match...remember Timmy, this is your career! [ the camera pans down ] And this week you will hit that brick wall...and then Timmy, as if that wasn't enough...I'm gonna PISS ALL OVER YOU! [ the sound of a zipper coming down is heard and then a shot from behind Jarrett shows a stream of water come from in between his legs and drain down onto the ground below as the camera fades out. ]
