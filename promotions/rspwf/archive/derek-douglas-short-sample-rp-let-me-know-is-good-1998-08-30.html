[The scene opens in a room with black and white posters hangin all over the walls. A light is shining directly on a figure sitting in a steel folding chair. The man looks up and the man is Derek Derek Douglas... Douglas slowely stands to his feet and begins to walk into the shadows of the one light. He stops looks around and begins to speak]
Derek Douglas: The match I have wanted for along time will come very soon. A man that does nothing except for hurt others will finally get what he has coming, but thats another story. Lets get to the close future of CROW. Suicido, you and that other dude will not be able to handle whats comeing for you. Heat
Wave 98 is coming up very very fast... The no rulez anything goes match that will make the number one contender for the world title. Dare, Goat, who ever the hell has the belt at Spooky Things will be in for the hell of their life...
Once I get passed this speedbump knows at Suicido it's on to the world title. The title that everyone and their mothers want to own. Suicido, it's you and me in the ring to decide this entire thing...
[Douglas looks over tot he posters on the wall and begins to talk] Derek Douglas: Look at the walls Suicido... Look at the light and the dark posters... Look at the coloration of the ways of insanity and reality... You
Suicideo are the white reality that so many men in the world have to deal with.
You are a man that lives in reality and dosen't know what else to do with his life. Now look at me Suicido, look at the insanity that runs throuhg my life. Look at the things I have done in my life time. I have been in three way barbwire cage matches. Electrice fence matches, and a shit load of first blood matches. Look at the scars that run all over my body and that will prove the insanity that I live... This insantiy is the darness that runs through my veins... The blood that runs through my veins is red, but not a normal red. It is a cold crimson red... Suicido, your gonna see what kinda of red it is that runs through your veins because your gonna BLEED!!!
[Douglas turns around and looks deep intot he camera] Now, Steve Houston.... My former stable mate... You felt the need to carve ESM into my chest. Well, if you think that will prove where my heart lies, well your_DEAD_wrong. You want a piece of me and show everyone that I'm EASTSIDE until I die... Well you did what you thought would brand me for life, but no...
[Douglas rips off his shirt to reveal the marks of **ESM** carved into his chest] Derek Douglas: Look Douglas at this and you'll feel all warm inside, but you fail to think very clearly... You think this will be the thing that proves I'm
ESM? No, you think this will make me think, and it did. I thought of some way to prove it's over and it will never get me back to the way you want it to be.
Look on Steve cause this you will love... [Douglas pulls out a buck knife and begins to carve a big_ X_through the letters ESM. The blood runs down his chest like wildfire and Derek just continues to carve. He looks over tot eh wall were some of the blood squirted onto the posters. He looks at them and wipes his finguer through it. He slowley pulls his hand toward his mouth and licks the blood off his fingers]
Derek Douglas: Houston, look at this and you'll know that it's over and done with. I have nothing to prove to you or anyone else that thinks I'm East Side till' I die.... Like I always say theres a big difference between thinkin and knowin, so Steve unless you want some of this... Stay the hell out of my face... I'm sic of you and everyone else interfereing in matches and from here on out it's back tot he way...
Shawn Jamison, your well on your way to hell, and hell is waiting for you... It's time for The DEvil to be unleashed.... Just remember when you feel the blood running out of everyo opening of your body you'll know....... You'll know you just got hit witht he FLATLINER... The Devils Child is here and he is coming for BLOOD!!!
