Well, this is a pretty-much, godawful roleplay. I think so atleast.. so, if anybody has an ideas, suggestions or flames, please post them. This a roleplay
I did, for EwF (
[detail withheld] ). I had just currently, taken out all of the "big" stars, the league had.. =========== The True Franchise Johnny Rebel Mess with the best, and come crashing down like the rest.. (The scene opens, down inside the very small EwF arena. It's complety empty, as the ring crew are re-aranging and starting to breakdown the ring and lights.
The chairs are empty, as not a sound could be heard by the ring crew. The camera switches back outside, to a dark, cold and rainy night. The camera man is breathing quite hard as frost is coming out of his mouth, and the little bit is caught by the camera. They begin to walk around, as they come across a dark alley. A couple of loud-pitched moans are heard, as Johnny Rebel is seen sitting on top of a dumpster. The camera crew come walking down the alley, in a very quick pace. They pass cats, who scatter around trying to find food. Rats run out from the trashcan, as Rebel is sitting there hanging one leg off the dumpster. Rebel waves to them with his hand to walk over closer, as they do.
Rebel begins to speak) "The Franchise" Johnny Rebel: Extreme Wrestling Federation.. the time has come. See, nobody was expecting the un-expected, and thats what got everyone. I'm not the greatest, wrestler of time. Oh no, I'm just the Franchise of pro-wrestling.
Is that right Jeff Williams? I'm just the worst wrestler, there has ever been. But yet, I'm the Franchise of pro-goddamn-wrestling? Obvisouly, I'm the Franchise of this league. It's just as simple as that.. if you look around for some talent in this fed, good-luck. The only REAL talent, that this league will ever see, is sitting right infront of you. Sitting on this dumpster, and looking into your eyes. I came into this profession, looking to get some money, have some fun. But I did more than that. I came into professional wrestling, as a little pathetic jobber. Like most of you in EwF are, compared to Johnny
Rebel. I came in, and it took me months to actually win something. So, nobody here in the EWF knows anything about Johnny Rebel. You can only hope and dream, that Johnny Rebel is nothing, to what everyone says. See, people tell me all the time. They think that I'm the greatest, wrestler ever to come around this damn profession. They're about seventy-five percent right. See the other twenty-five percent, they're not so right. They've got a problem with Johnny
Rebel. Those are the people, that have been hit in the head one to many times.
Those are the people, that just don't understand anything about Johnny Rebel. Most of them, just aren't thinking strait. Which is perfectly fine. But what some people don't realize is that when those people have the nerve to talk about Johnny Rebel, they can't say anything worth a left-nut. Some people, just sit there and talk about, what they want in the pro-fessional wrestling world.
I don't talk about what I want, I talk about what I'm going to do. That's the diffrence between, me and most pro wrestlers.
(Rebel pops down off the dumpster, as a bunch of animals scatter away in fear. Rebel begins walking down the alleyway) "The Franchise" Johnny Rebel: Now onto buisness. Marcus Wilson, the wannabe
Franchise. It's only fitting, that you would want to be me. I mean, look at me then look at you. There isn't a comparsion anywhere. I had to steal my minutes of airtime? I don't think so. I had EwF President Williams, on my nuts, just to get me to come out and do anything. See, you have everything completly turned upside down. I used racial slurs, in a good-way. Not to disturb anyone, so therefore how could I lower myself that way? Tell me that.. See, once again.
This is where we differ, I wouldn't make a move towards you.. PERIOD. I don't know how you like to live life, but thats another story. But finally you come across a point, that I can live with. I wan't to win this match as well, my return match to the EwF. And the same goes for you, if you do anything stupid, it'll just be two seconds before you wind up with your head up your ass. There is something, that I have that you don't. You have compassion. I could care less about anybody in this promotion. I used a chair, and I knocked both of them out. Do you have a problem with that? Because if you do, I'll be glad to introduce you to that little thing they call a steal chair. Now that you've scared everyone, with your puny and pathetic little biceps. That's nothing..
you can flex and do everything infront of a mirror, it doesn't matter to me. I need two things, a balcony and a table. I come here to perform, not to flex around and wrestle with little headlocks and armdrag takedowns. No no no, I'm a performer see. Screw wrestling.. it's not about suplexs and sleepers anymore.
This is the EXTREME Wrestling Federation, and I'm going to show everyone the true definition of Extreme. Which is obviously something, a couple of you have never heard of before. And EwF President Chris Wlliams, got a little taste of what it's like to go extreme. He created a monster, and wanted someone to tame it. So, I'm here to make it bigger. Something, that you would never do Marcus.
I've headlined more cards then you've even been in. I've wrestled infront of more people then you could even dream of. Been places, you only see in your dreams. Marcus, just stay out of my way and I'll stay out of yours.. but mess with the best, and you'll be crashing down like the rest..
(Rebel continues walking, as he is past the arena now. Walking down a main road, as the lights along the street are lit and glowing down on the streets.
The town looks deserted as their isn't a soul in sight. All the cars have been removed from the parking lots, and all the employees from their jobs have gone home. The moon covers the town like a blanket, as a little dog is seen sleeping next to a trash can. Rebel begins walking and stops at a bench in the park. The camera catches a slide and a swingset as Rebel stretches out across the bench)
"The Franchise" Johnny Rebel: As I look around this place, I remember what it was like to have fun. Bouncing around, from swing to swing. That was fun. Now, what has happend? Everybody has taken the fun out of wrestling. People like you, Jeff Williams. I bet you, until Johnny Rebel kicked your ass, nobody had even heard of you. But now, you've got people buzzing around. The sports page in your local newspaper.. "Pro Wrestler gets his ass beat by Franchise." And I can almost garuntee, that you've NEVER had a main event match. Now, I told you
Johnny Rebel made you into something. Your gonna get your main event match, hell, we might as well call this the Franchise match, because that's all it's gonna be. I can almost garuntee this too, that your not going to get a bit of action in. Well, since we all hate each other. Why not just make this a
Four-Way-Dance? It's just as simple as that. Now, William's since you are such the "tough" guy. You should put up a good fight. I really like your gameplan, it's just a little complex. Now, let me get this strait. Your going to drop me on my head, and I'm not going to get up? Man, that is just great. Oh but wait,
Here's a thought num-nutz. What happens, when I get out of your little "drop"
and your the one the ends up getting dropped on your head? But I won't stop there, see I know HOW to wrestle. I don't just walk in the ring and drop someone on their head. Man, your great. I think I'm your biggest fan dumbass. I don't want any of you, because I don't roll down that alley. I don't want to take all of you either, because once again, I'm not that kinda guy. I hit your ass with the chair, because I wanted to. I don't wanna get in a fight with you, oh no. Your just to scary for me. You might actually beat me, and I would be the laughing stock of the Extreme Wrestling Federation. Does anybody out there, smell the sarcasim? I think I feel it. Don't let it go to your head, asshole.
YOUR A NOTHING, and thats all that needs to be said. Um dude.. put your shirt back on. I don't care to see your puny chest.. go ahead and "pretend" like you have something, but when it all comes down. I've got two advantages on you..
one, you just suck and I don't.. and two, well you just suck really bad. Do you get the drift? I think I've got it through your thick ass head, that you suck.
Wait.. wait.. wait.. please, hope to god, tell me that you did NOT just say something about whiping someone's ass. Jesus christ, how old are we Jeff? A whole twenty-five years old? But if you plan on whiping my ass, then we've got a serious problem. Because, gay-bashing is a hobby for me. Ya know, you like to whipe people's ass and then you take off your shirt for guys? I think there is a pattern devolping. You just stay the hell away from me in that ring, and if you touch me in ANY form of gayness, I will beat the living snot out of you.
Okay, while we are talking about people growing up. See, EwF President, Chris
Williams doesn't need to grow up. It's all about you, GROW-UP.. learn something from Johnny Rebel.. thats all you've got to do, shut your mouth and listhen up, because when it all comes crashing down.. and everybody see's what the EwF is really about.. be afraid.. be VERY afraid!
(Rebel stands up, off the bench and begins walking away from the scene.. as the moon is begining to go down, and the sun has begun setting very bright. The cars are beginning to show up on the roads again, and the animals have dissapeared back down in the alley. As the camera crew leave the park area, and the camera fades out to the EwF logo)
