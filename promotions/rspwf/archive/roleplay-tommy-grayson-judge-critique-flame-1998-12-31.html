[Roleplay] Tommy Grayson; Judge, Critique, Flame

31 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This right here is an RP for JPW. So, guys, gimme your best shot and tell me what ya think. In other words, FLAME AWAY! :)
===================================================== [SCENE: Cameras open in a trashy hotel room somewhere in New York City. Clothes are strewn all over the place and shredded porno mags are on the floor. In the center of the room, against the wall, there is a cheap-looking bed with a relatively small TV set in front of it. On the bed, we see "Loose Cannon"
Tommy Grayson sitting there, leaning against the wall. He has an emotionless face on his look.]
[Grayson is wearing a black Guns N' Roses "Use Your Illusion Tour" t-shirt,
Levis blue jeans, and a pair of black sneakers with white trim. He has a small, silver cross around his neck. His Yankees baseball cap is sitting there on his lap all crumpled and creased. Grayson looks quite rugged with his black, crewcut hair and his stubble, which may get trimmed once in a while.]
[He flips the TV set on to a JPW TV show. It appears to be a recent edition of
Friday Night Burnout. Grayson grins as he watches the show. He leans the back of his head on the wall and turns around to look at the camera with a sadistic look on his cold, brown eyes.]
[He turns his attention back to the loud, noisy TV set and turns it off. Then, a woman in orgasmic pleasure is heard coming from the wall he's leaning on. He bangs his fist right against the wall, causing the ceiling to vibrate slightly.
The noise stops. The camera gets a frontal shot of him and he begins to speak.] TOMMY GRAYSON - Julevic Professional Wrestling. Certainly one of the most elite federations in North-freakin'-America. A lot of these guys...they weren't in a lot of trouble before. [Chuckles] But now that I'm here, things are gonna begin heating up, and I DO mean heating up. I can gurantee you that the level of violence will increase, there will be more rampage and yes, you can expect some riots. You could say it's an early Millenium or another L.A. Riots for you guys, so to speak.
First and foremost, I'd like to say that I'm starting to recognize some faces in the JPW. I see "The Diamond" Shane Diamond is on the roster. [Evil laughter]
I bit the [BLEEP]'s damn ear half off! I still can't forget that, Diamond. It still sticks in my mind like yesterday...hell, I'm sure somebody already found that piece of ear...and lemme give a piece of advice to that kid: IT AIN'T
WORTH A DAMN NICKEL!! You see, if MY ear had been bitten off, imagine how much that would've been worth. Heck, it's from the most hated guy on Earth...so, I could imagine. Anyway, I'm damn glad you could, um, pull yourself together for this one. You claim it doesn't hurts, but I know better, man. I know better.
[Suddenly, somebody else's sexual pleasure begins interrupting Grayson. Grayson gets up from his bed and it appears he's searching around for something. He finally lays his eyes on the TV set and gets an evil look on his face. He pulls the cord right out of the back of the TV, picks it up...and tosses it against the wall! Sparks fly everywhere as the TV impacts with the wall...the debris from the TV falls to the bed and the floor, and there is a large hole from where the TV hit.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - SHUT THAT BITCH UP!! [Calms down] Well, I guess that did it. I'll admit it. I'd do anything for some silence once in a while...no wonder I get pissed a lot.
[Grayson begins laughing uncontrollably.] TOMMY GRAYSON - Whoa...did I just show the JPW that I'm a guy that shouldn't be angered? Well, that's just a dandy! I did it and I didn't even freakin' know it! Well, I guess I didn't have to a "it-sounds-like 'Smells Lke Teen Spirit'
but-it's-really-'Rape Me'" kind of thing, huh? Anyway...I think it's save to say that JPW is gonna live...
...LIKE... ...A... ..._SUICIDE_!! [Grayson walks out of the room. As he closes the door, the scene fades to black.] James (or Jim, whatever floats your boat) -Handler of "Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson "Live...LIKE A _SUICIDE_!!" -Voted as ICW's "Biggest Lunatic" [How true..] -Voted as ICW's "Most Underrated" [Was. Was.] -The innovator of "The People's Tongue"
