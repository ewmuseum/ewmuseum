[Critique] Michael Darque

18 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Michael Darque sits comfortably on a soft, burgundy-leather chair in a well-stocked library. He's dressed in black leather pants and a black poet-shirt, with his dark hair neatly trimmed forming a widow's peak...
his dark black goatee well-manicured... not quite Goth, but still giving off a definite dark atmosphere. He looks up at the camera, and closes the book he has apparently been reading. A warm, pleasant grin plays across his lips as he welcomes the camera into his library.]
MD: "So, Pierce Bleed... despite all your posturing and all your ranting, you ended up on the losing end of our contest. Not that you didn't try. I have to give you credit, you put in a valiant effort. Unfortunately for you, it just wasn't enough. No matter how many times you chanted "Dork"...
as if I have never heard _that_ before... no matter what you tried... you failed. Such is life, and there's little that one can do but to accept that fact and move on. Of course, for myself, moving on means moving into the
Quarter-Finals of of the Lion's Grand Prix. How ironic, in it's way, that to advance in the Lion's Grand Prix now, I'll have to defeat the Golden
Lion.
I'm not yet ready to comment on Fujiwa-san, though... so I'll leave that for another time."
[Darque stands up and slowly walks across the room to a large globe, mounted in an oak stand. The globe opens with a slight push from Darque to reveal a small bar. Taking up a small glass, Darque takes one of the decanters from the bar and pours himself a drink... taking a little sip.]
MD: "I have another task ahead of me, you see. Myself and Greg Gabbard are scheduled to face Eiji Yatsu... and Shoji Suzuki. Jet Jaguar II is the name
I believe I remember you under, Suzuki. Yes, I watched your matches in SJPW with some interest. You have talent, Suzuki-san. You have pride and honor and you have the fans solidly behind you. How nice for you. It must be a great sense of joy and accomplishment to know that the fans chant your name when you wrestle... that they look up to you... that they respect you.
I've watched you in action Suzuki, and I've seen the rapport you hold with the crowd. It's very impressive, I'll admit. More impressive however..."
[Darque pauses to take another sip of his drink... smiling as he savors the taste.] MD: "... is your sister, Misae." [Another pauses follows, along with a wicked grin. Reaching into his shirt, he produces a photo portrait of Shoji's sister, Misae Suzuki.]
MD: "What a sweet young thing she is. All pure and innocent, cute and warm. So tell me Shoji... just how well do you know your sister, hmm? What if I were to tell you that week in, week out... she is chained to a wall in one of the "alternative" clubs... where she begs to feel the stroke of the flogger and the sting of the cane? That she crawls upon all fours to my feet, begging to be treated like a slave? Would you believe me? Of course you wouldn't."
[Darque sets the now-empty glass aside and closes the globe again, giving it a little spin as he does. He then brushes past the camera, forcing it to turn to follow him as he walks out of the library, down a narrow hallway.
The paintings hanging on the walls are all tasteful, and Darque stops to adjust one of the paintings, making sure it hangs straight.]
MD: "You know your sister, though, don't you Shoji? You know that she'd never do anything so degrading or humiliating. Or would she? You can simply write off my statements as a psychological attack, or the words of a sick and twisted individual... but can you really just ignore them so completely? No.
In the back of your mind you'll be wondering. It's the human condition, Shoji... to wonder about things like this once they've been brought up. What can you do about it anyway? How can you uncover the truth of things? Can you just ask Misae?" [Darque just chuckles as he continues down the hall. He stops at a door, which is locked with a large clasp and padlock. On an endtable is a large keyring, with several long keys on it. Darque picks up the ring of keys and selects one of the brass keys to unlock the padlock.]
MD: "Of course you can. You can ask your dear sister if once or twice a week she wears a leather dog collar and crawls on all fours at the end of a leash, lapping at her Master's heel. I'm sure she'll be happy to divulge her most private information about her life. And it's not as if Misae knows anyone who has fallen into that dark side of life, now is there? Oh wait...
there was someone... Susan something, wasn't there? A tag team partner of hers at some time. I remember seeing her stripped naked in the middle of the ring, humbled and obedient... but I'm sure Misae would never do anything like that, now would she Shoji?"
[Darque leans back against the now unlocked door, setting the padlock on the corner table and idly jingling the ring of keys. Again, the wicked grin plays over his features.]
MD: "In any case, I didn't intend of bringing your sister into this
Shoji,
so please.. forgive me. I certainly meant no disrespect. Certainly I didn't intend to insinuate that precious little Misae carries my riding crop around between her teeth until I decide to use it on her. All I really wanted to say is that I'm looking forward to facing off against you, Suzuki-san...
along with Eiji Yatsu. I'm sure that you'll both be more than worthy opponents for Mr. Gabbard and myself." [Darque turns his back to the camera, opening the door, revealing a dark stairwell. The only illumination are small, bare lightbulbs set intermittantly into the cobblestone walls that lead down. Darque seems about to descend the stairs, but he turns again, giving the camera his attention once more.]
MD: "Now, I'll have to admit.. I don't know much about Eiji Yatsu. I don't know much about Greg Gabbard. Still, that's really neither here nor there at this point. The match has been signed, and so the four of us will square off and the better team will win. I happen to think, rather naturally I'm sure, that it will be Mr. Gabbard and I that will be the victors. Call it ego...
call it overconfidence... call it whatever you wish. It really doesn't matter, because it will be proven correct in the end. Now ... if you'll excuse me... I have a ... visitor... who needs to experience some pleasure..
and some pain." [Darque turns again, going down the stairs and out of the camera's view. After several long moments the sound of leather smacking flesh can just barely be heard coming up the stairwell. The soft moans and gasps of a woman filter upwards as well and the chuckling of Michael Darque.]
