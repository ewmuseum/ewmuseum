[Critique of Roleplay]

11 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please critique. Constructive criticism is welcome. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Morgan De Sade sits upon a bench in a locker room. His sweat-drenched body glistens under the florescent lights. He wipes off his face with a towel provided him by his ever-faithful valet, Tiang-Li. Her body-hugging, black leather outfit shows off every curve of her sexy feline form. The way it fits over her well-endowed form screams of passion and sex untold of. Morgan wipes his the back of neck and looks up and spits on the UWA sign hanging in the locker room]
MDS: The United States is a nation of laws and rules, much like the UWA is an organization of laws and rules... badly written and randomly enforced. For some reason people think I am not sane... that really isn't it at all... it's just that sanity calms, but madness is more interesting.
[De Sade takes a long drink from a water bottle and wipes his mouth with the back of his hand. Tiang-Li moves behind him and beings rubbing his shoulders with her long, delicate fingers. He reaches over and snatches a piece of paper from a nearby bench]
MDS: Jordan, Crash, Cannibus, Judge, Trash, Lucifer.... the list goes on and on... Nothing but a bunch of predator-wannabes who wish nothing more than to have a taste of the gold. But I have found an effective way to deal with predators... that is to taste terrible. So terrible that they quake at my coming... seems I face another predator-wannabe tonight in the form of Evan
Drake. This witless wonder is a true showcase of how not to wrestle. He has the poorest execution of the most basic wrestling maneuvers of anyone I have seen in the UWA. I think poor old Thomas Trash wouldn't have much difficulty in thrashing Drake.
[De Sade shoos Tiang-Li away and stands up. He grabs a championship wrestling belt and seven gold Olympic medals from the locker behind him. He smiles and places the medals about his neck and the the belt about his waist]
MDS: Look at these wonderful medals I claimed at the 1936 Olympics in Berlin. Oh my, this wonderful championship belt is all mine... I am simply the best this great organization has to offer... *BLECH!*
[De Sade beings tearing the medals from his neck scattering them about and then rips the belt away from his body and throws it in the garbage]
MDS: People place to much faith in trinkets such as these. I could care less of your accomplishments Drake. Bring your belts, medals, car, and old lady to the ring so I can deposit them, along with you in the dumpster. It is people like you and Scott Jordan that make the best champions of a pathetic organization like the UWA. You are made of paper, easily cut when President
Taylor tires of your face and easily folded and placed in his pocket anytime he tells you what to do. I have seen Taylor in his office with his cronies saying, "Everybody repeat after me, We are all individuals." He is going to be the death of the UWA. I may be at the bottom of the list in the UWA right now but remember this... A wise man can see more from the bottom of a well than a fool can from a mountain top.
[Looking over his shoulder he spies a janitor cleaning up the floor. The janitor grabs a wadded up piece of paper and tosses it from 20 feet away into the trash can and beings a mock dance of celebration. He looks over and notices De Sade watching him then suddenly slips on some water and falls into his mop-bucket of slop]
MDS: Success always occurs in private, and failure in full view. You are like the janitor Drake.... you will look good in private flexing in front of your mirror, but once you enter the ring, I will humiliate you beyond your wildest nightmares. Save yourself some embarrassment why don't you? Go to your freezer and grab some Ben and Jerry's ice cream.... perhaps your two favorites, Salty
Old Sailor or Latrine Licorice Supreme . Drown yourself in senseless calories and give in to the fact that you have zero chance of matching me punch for punch, or move for move...
[Tiang-Li wraps her arms about Morgan's neck and kisses him sensually on the cheek. De Sade smiles then swats her on the buttock]
TL: Master, will you tie me up tonight and hurt me? Please? MDS: Of course my dear, but that will come after tonights match.... Drake, I learned a lesson early in life, that is never let your sense of morals get in the way of doing what's right. I will pummel you until you beg me for mercy, then pummel you some more for even thinking I have a sense of knowing when to stop. You will learn tonight the answer to the question you've been asking yourself since you found out you were facing Morgan De Sade... Do you think you've got what it takes?
[De Sade drops his towel from his neck and walks off. The janitor in the background is still trying to get free of the bucket as the scene fades to black]
