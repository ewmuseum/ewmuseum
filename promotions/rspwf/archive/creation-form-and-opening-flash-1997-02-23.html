> > * Wrestler's Name: "Fang" Ross Starke > > * Height: 6'1 > * Weight: 256lbs > * Hometown: Southampton, England > * Heel or Face (no Neutral, please): Face > * Manager/Valet (optional): George Morris > * Description of manager (please give physical description, also does > he/she interfere?): George is a young man in his mid-20s with brown hair. His hair is brushed back away from his eyes although if he if down in the dumps he won't brush it, instead letting it fall in front of his eyes. He is a brilliant speaker, captivating people with his deep blue eyes which when he is talking a lot as is the case he almost hypnotizes the audience. George thinks of himself as a future Jim Cornette he concentrates almost solely on tag team wrestling in his stable. He wears his prized Adidas tracksuit to ringside which has badges sewn on all around the back from all the federations, citys and managerial schools he has been to. > > * Please describe your wrestler's gimmick/attitude/personality as much > as you can: "Fang" is a real tough biker with a real bad attitude.Heis really nasty and has a mean streak a mile wide, but only in the ring. Out of it, he is a nice family man who loves the fans and adores their cheers. People who get on his wrong side usually end up with their faces down on the ground as a result of his short temper and mean right hook. He is determined, going at his opponents with the tenaciousness of a terrier and a dogged determination. "Fang" dispises cheating, especially if his opponent cheats in a match, but isn't above a good aisleway brawl with a chair or other nice 'eavy weapon. > > * Please give us a little background on your wrestler (perhaps a made-up > history-- we just want to get a good idea of who your character is):"Fang" is a biker who, in his younger days was a member of the gang known as 'Overkill' he quit the gang after some illegal smuggling by the leader Greg Turbane and was chased by them for a few years. One day in a special 'toughest guy on the block' contest held in Detroit he met up with his pard' "Highland Terrier" Cameron McFadden. They both couldn't put each other away in the finals and a scout from a wrestling organization offered them contracts as pros. They accepted and are now the Dogs of War. > > * Entrance Music (please use an actual song, I don't want to see "The > Giant's WCW Music"): "I'm the Wild one" by Suzi Quatro > > * Describe your wrestler's entrance to the ring (does he have fireworks, > special lights, anything he does before he gets to the ring... if you > are creating a tag team, skip this category and fill out Tag Team > Entrance on the Tag Team sheet instead): > > * Describe your wrestler's physical appearance (please be as descriptive > as possible!): "Fang" is a broad wrestler with muscular shoulders andincredibly muscular biceps. He has a very hairy chest which is constantly being displayed. His arms are well developed and his enormous fists pack a punch of dynamite! > > * Describe your wrestler's ring attire (again, please be descriptive!):"Fang" wears his black trunks all the time, even at home when he is doing his favourite things, drinking cheap beer and believing everything on television. The result is a pretty banged up opponent or if he misses his queue a pretty banged up Cameron. > > > -----------------------------------------------------------------------OPENING FLASH (The camera cuts to a room where three men are standing, one is wearing a pair of black trunks with the symbol of a Bulldog with drool dripping from its snarling jaws. Another is wearing a tartan jacket and black jeans. These two stand either side of a smaller man who is wearing a red and blue adidas jacket. The three men stare at the camera and the smaller one in the middle starts to speak.) Small Man: Ahh, welcome everyone to the introduction of a great tag team. (The smaller man waves his hands presenting his two proteges.) Small Man: I suppose you are wondering who we are... well I am called George Morris not that it will make any difference and these two are... (He pauses to build suspense.) George: ... They are the Dogs of War. (The two men shove George out of the way and speak to the camera.) Man #1: Aye, George I am the Highland Terrier Cameron McFadden an I be looking for a little competition round here. Man #2: An I'm Ross Starke but the people who have been on the wrong side of dis.. (Holds up his hairy fist and points at it.) Ross: ... kinda tend to call me sir. George: Fang and Cameron have worked hard to get here and are ready to face some people who deem themselves worthy of facing the dogs. But first let me tell you that everything you see here is not all I manage I am a tag team manager extraordinairre and am looking to guide these two brutes to the gold and beyond. Fang: Yeah dats right we gonna ave to bang some eadz round here before you blokes take us serious like. Cameron: An that includes anyone of yoose who thinks ye is man enough to take on a Dog. Fang: Whateva ee said goes fer da lot of ya and we is gonna be da first to get our hairy mits on da title and dats dat. George: Goodbye everyone and take a long look at these two, the dogged determination, the tenacious terrier-like ability and the bulldog brawling is what makes the Dogs the BEST! (The camera fades as George and the Dogs leave the area.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------
