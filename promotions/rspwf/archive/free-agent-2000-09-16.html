JW: So what the hell was that all about? Sean: What was what all about? JW: You almost cracking me in the skull with that hockey stick of yours. I mean come on man, you a little bit edgy at the moment?
Sean; Well yeah I am actually. I know that back stabbing whackjobs like Bubba come out for revenge so I got to protect myself. That's why I was ready to lay your ass out but thank god I hesitated.
JW: Eh it's ok. I guess you gotta do what you gotta do. I mean they do have a right to be very angry with you.
Sean: I guess you can say that. JW: So why did you go out there and save Richens from them? Sean: I did it because I'm sick of seeing people held down. I'm sick and tired of seeing these big wig jerks going around thinking that they can push whoever they want to around. Well I applaud Claw for sticking up for all of the little guys. You see if people don't stand up and fight then they will be pushed around as long as the big boys want to. As I see it Claw can't do the job alone. Nobody could. Plus seeing that guy get beat up by a doofus like Brown just pissed me off.
JW: So in other words you think you're the savior of the NAWC...wait sorry the NAWA now. Sean: No way in hell. I'm just here sticking up for myself to show that I'm not to be held back. I guess you haven't been paying attention to anything that I've been doing for the past couple of weeks.
JW: Oh you mean the whole sumo thing? Sean: Well, sort of. I'm just trying to prove that I can run with the big boys and that I'm good enough to break out of the Lightweight stigma.
JW: I see. So pissing off one of the most powerful forces in the wrestling world is your "cry for attention."
Sean: I guess you could put it that way. Hell if I'm not going to take any initiative then how the hell am I going to be noticed?
JW: Well I think you did a damn good job of being noticed by attacking the head of the Pentagon. damn man I really do feel sorry for you.
Sean: No need to. I did it willingly. I know exactly what I'm getting myself in to. I'm prepared for it but goddamn it if this is what it takes to prove I've got the stuff to make it then I'll take my chances with the
Pentagon. JW: Well I'm glad that you're very serious about this but there were probably safer routes to take in order to get noticed.
Sean: I've been there and I've tried that. It was time for some drastic measures. JW: I guess you could call what you did "drastic" I'd more or less call is insane. Sean: Oh well, like I said I'm willing to take my chances. Plus Claw's nuts going out there alone. One must always bring back up of you're going to face uneven odds. That's why I'll be carrying old Bitchboy here.
JW: Bitchboy? What an interesting name. Sean: Well yeah. it's called that because every time I hit a boy with it they whine like a bitch.
JW: You are one twisted individual. Sean: And I'm damn proud of it. Otherwise life would be no fun being a boring normal person like yourself.
JW: I can se your point. Sometimes it is a little bit on the boring side. Sean: I'm glad we agree. But I need to go now. Visiting hours at the Mental Rehab center are almost over and I need to go see my mother. JW: Well thank you very much for your time and be safe! Sean: thanks. Ok let's go Bitchboy! *As the scene fades he grabs his hockey stick and leaves his dressing area*
