"Rodeo" Nevada Harris Roleplay [Opinions wanted]

13 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in] [SCENE: The home of Nevada Harris. The wind is blowing hard on this sunny day in the outskirts of Montgomery, Alabama.
Camera scrolls over and shows Nevada's house. Camera moves in on the house as the door by the porch opens. Nevada Harris, in a brown cowboy hat, burgandy flannel shirt, blue-jeans, and black boots, comes walking out while lighting a cigarette. He brings his head up and his thick black mustache is very noticeable.
He looks around as dust blows in the air. He laughs as he looks on. The camera shows him from an angle tilted up and toward him from a few feet in front.]
NH: [With his cigarette hanging from his mouth] "Ah, yes . . . Looks lahk it's gon' stoooorrrrm tonight . . ." [A smile grows upon his face. A faint rumble is heard as the sky gets darker.] NH: "I am lahk a storm, yes. See, when it storms here, it storms hard. When ah strike, ah strike hard. Storms heah are relentless. Ah'm relentless.
Brody Thunda . . . Ya say that if Eddie an' ah come afta ya, we'd be sent home with uh toetag. Tha's all well an' good, 'Lone Wolf.' Howeva, ah'ma hafta disagree with ya on that."
[Puffs his cigarette and looks down at the camera. He raises his head back up, anticipating the storm.]
NH: "Ya talk 'bout how we should aaaaalll shut up now. Well, ah'ma keep talkin' ya mick bastard. Y' see, ya stupid sumbitch, ah ain't neva lahked you and prob'ly neva gonna. Ah see that ya got yerself quite a list of accomplishments. Ah see that ya got yerself quite uh reputation. Lemme tell ya sumthin' big boy, ah ain't scared uh ya and ah ain't neva gon' be scared uh ya.
Brody . . . hombre, ya ain't neva gon' wan' no piece uh this righ' heah. See, ah ain't no great rassla, but ah'ma a great fighta. If you wan' summuhme, come an' get summuhme."
[Nevada walks over to an open window of his and reaches down. He comes back up with a shotgun.]
NH: "See, Brody, ah'm willin' tuh do whateva ah have tuh in orda tuh get what ah wan'." [Pumps the shotgun.] NH: "So ah guess ah'ma hunt me some wolves." [Nevada starts to laugh as he walks back into his house. Camera moves back.] [Fade.] -Ian Walsh CWP President Handler of: "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh [IWE, GLCW, NAAW] "Rodeo" Nevada Harris [SCW, MLW, ACW, ISSW] Uncle Istvan [MCW]
