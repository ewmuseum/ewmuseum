E-wrestler looking for e-fed.

22 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the first time I'm posting to the RSPWF and I am looking for an fed for my new wrestler. I've handled other wrestlers in other federations and then went on a two-month break. Now I'm back with another wrestler and eager to try a new fed.
The fed should: be booked, have roleplays sent out on roleplay packets, have a webpage where I can check results, news and events schedules as well as a roster page, have original wrestlers only.
Interested fedheads should contact me at [detail withheld] . The wrestler details are in the attachment as well as below. These details are not etched in stone and I am more than willing to work with interested fedheads in fine-tuning the wrestler. Note, I have other commitments besides e-wrestling so I am looking for a fed that isn't too taxing on my schedule.
And now the details: Wrestler's Name: Colonel James Jackson Wrestler's Height: 6'2" Wrestler's Weight: 251 pounds Wrestler's Hometown: Atlanta, Georgia Wrestler's Alignment: Face Wrestling Style: High-impact brawler Theme Music: 'Living In America' by James Brown Wrestler's Appearance: African-American man with a well-toned body; black, closely-cropped hair and a clean-shaven face Ring Attire: Military fatigues ie. green singlet, camoflage pants and army boots Finisher: sleeper hold Signature Moves: elevated half crab three shortarm clothesline consecutively two-handed choke slam 10+ Favourite Moves: atomic drop backbreaker spinning boot scrape bulldog double axe handle dropkick headbutt leg scissors stomp ax kick Russian leg sweep piledriver spinebuster football tackle abdominal stretch STF wheelbarrow suplex Sample Roleplay: [Scene: An empty wrestling arena.] [We begin with a shot of a group of men setting up the ring. The camera pans across the arena, from the front-row seats to the ones further back. A man is seated in one of the back-row seats.]
[A close-up reveals the man to be none other than Col. James Jackson. He is in his casual-wear; plain white T-shirt and blue jeans. He seems to be watching the ring crew in rapt attention. He begins to speak without turning to the camera.]
James Jackson: A few hours from now, these seats will be filled with people, people who paid a pretty hefty sum to be entertained. The fans, they want a good wrestling match. They want a good fight, but that's not all, they also want to hear from their favourite wrestlers.
[He pauses, then turns slowly to the camera as he continues talking.] JJ: THEY don't want hear or see a grown-up man who behaves like a ten-year-old and his little hussy insult and taunt them, insult the good name of their state and country or make fun of the way they look or live. I'm sure you know who I am talking about. It's not bad enough that these two have to make a fool of themselves week in, week out, they also have to make fun of the fans who pay to watch the show, the fans who put the money in their pockets. Sure, your paycheck comes from the company but how do you think the President comes out with all that cash?
[Pauses again to catch his breath before continuing.] JJ: So take it this way, if tonight, the two of you start your antics again, I will personally come down there and shut your mouths for you.
Why? Because someone's got to do the dirty work. [He gets up and begins walking towards the exit but stops halfway and shouts over his shoulder.]
JJ: And I tell you, I hate getting down and dirty, so think of the frame of mind I'll be in then.
[The colonel continues walking towards the exit as the scene begins to fade...]
