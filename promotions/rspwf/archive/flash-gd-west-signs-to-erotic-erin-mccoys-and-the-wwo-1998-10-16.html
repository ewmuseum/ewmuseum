[FLASH] GD West signs to "Erotic" Erin McCoys and the WWO!

16 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please, send comments to the group or to me privately! :-) Thanks! --------------------------------------- [SCENE: A press conference at the WWO Headquarters. Several WWO Executive
Committee members stand at a podium, flanked by a silent Erin McCoys. Many reporters from the wrestling industry abound, as one Official raises his hands as if to silence the crowd]
OFFICIAL: Ladies and gentlemen of the press...on behalf of myself, the Executive Committee, and Ms. McCoys, I welcome you to this press gathering. We have called you here today.... REPORTER #1: Is this about the Troy Majors/CHandler Stone situation?? OFFICIAL: No, sorry to disappoint, but this is some rather good news. Earlier this week, the contracts were signed, and the World Wrestling Organization is proud to officially announce our latest "catch." He was a champion in his hometown league, and came to us after one of our..."humble competitors"
decided to waste his talents. But he's now here, and we hope... [Suddenly a man, standing 6'2", dressed in a open, black zip-up hooded sweatshirt, blue denim jeans, and a black T-Shirt reading "T S M F !" in large, white block letters bursts onto the scene from a side entrance. He wears a pair of Ray-Bans, and his brown hair is cut into a spikey flattop. He also sports a lighter-brown goatee about his face. He storms up to the podium, carrying a bottle of raspberry Snapple in one hand. He basically nudges the Official aside, and slams the bottle down in front of Erin
McCoys...] WEST: Here you go, Sunshine, you look like you could use a bit of color other than black and blue in ya...
[Reporters and officials start murmuring, McCoys' face is unchanged in look] WEST: Now, before we go through any more hifalutin ass-kissin' muckymuck from my new bosses here, allow me to introduce myself, since I personally guarantee none of you will ever forget it. The name...is GD West.
REPORTER #1: Mr...um, West, wha... WEST: Hey, I haven't said my piece yet, so pack yours back in yer hole there! Now, the WWO was good enough to see the raw talent and sheer greatness of the
Circle City Rottweiler, GD West, so they just had to sign me up when I was released from a prior contract... [Notices a female reporter sticking a finger up, trying to get a question in] Hey hey, rag writer, wait yer turn!
Now before I get the *obvious questions*...let me tell you, what the Rottweiler, GD West, is here in the WWO for. Two things: number 1, to bust the heads of any worthless pig-fraggin' jackass that gets in my way, number
2, the World Wrestling Organization World Heavyweight Title, around my waist, before the end of 1999. That's about all you need to know, for right now, and that's what it is!...
[West backs away from the podium amidst the noises of the press corps. A few of the Officials stand in his way, with hands up, trying to convince him to make a few statements, and answer a few questions. West turns back]
WEST: I see everyone in the WWO wants a piece of me already. Alright, you there, the one who tried to get me earlier, go ahead.
REPORTER #1: Mr. West, I must say this is a pretty...bombastic entrance into the WWO! Already you've insulted the other wrestlers, including the world champ, Troy Majors! What are your feelings on Majors, seeing as you've already said you have designs on his belt?
WEST: Let me tell you a few things: One, I will insult, degrade, and knock
STRAIGHT TO HELL any punk that happens to be in my way on my quest for the
Big Prize here. This is wrestlin', that's how we do things here, last I goddamn checked. Two, I didn't say JACKSHIT about Troy Majors until now --
Troy's a fine guy, I'm sure, one of the greatest in our sport today. He's been through a lot of Front Office Shinola, which I can DEFINITELY relate to.
But y'see, when it comes time, for GD West to step into that ring, as the One and Only Challenger to that belt, it doesn't matter who's champion, it COULD
VERY WELL BE TROY MAJORS AT THAT POINT, whoever the Hell it is, he's gonna be
Westerminated! Next!
REPORTER #2 [The one from before, the woman]: You mentioned having similar experiences to those of Troy Majors? Care to expand?
WEST: Let's jsut say that broken promises, gross handshakin', and other Front
Office Horseshit kept me from advancin' to my rightful place in my...previous organization of employment, so they got the hint I wasn't gonna take it, and they released me, and here I am. Now...
REPORTER #2: You seem to want the World Title pretty badly, what if you're put in contention for any other titles?
WEST: Hey, a belt's a belt, I ain't gonna turn it down, but what it all comes down to is that World Title is gonna be in my hands before the end of 1999 or there's gonna be all kinds of Hell to pay! Next, you there, in the funny hat!
REPORTER #3: [A middle-aged man, somewhat overweight] Mr. West, you're coming in right before the Gold Rush PPV, that's certainly gonna delay your ring debut here for at least a couple of weeks? Any comments?
WEST: See, that's where you're wrong son, because I know damn well about Gold
Rush, and GD West is gonna be there, and he will be participating in it....
REPORTER #2: [cutting in] Howso, you're not signed for any matches? WEST: I didn't say I'd be competing, I just said my presence will be felt. Now.. REPORTER #1: Say, your shirt there? "TSMF"? What's that stand for? WEST: Happens to be a personal credo of mine. The T and M stand for "Tough" and "Mother," you figure the rest ou... VOICE: HEY! [All eyes turn to the back of the room. Noah DeMarco is standing there, wearing a loud, lime green leisure suit. Several groans are heard from the crowd]
WEST: Who's this nobody?! [Several laughs at the unintentional (?) pun] NOAH: For your information, wiseguy, I'm Noah DeMarco. You know...ASSISTANT
TO WWO PRESIDENT CHANDLER STONE?! Now, I've been here since the start of this charade, and I don't know what the OBSOLOTE AND LAME DUCK EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE were THINKING when they signed you, but the FACT is, Mr. Stone is in the midst of RAISING THE STANDARD of our competitors here in the WWO. This leaves you on the breadlines, bubba! The LAST thing we need is another person trying to be The Next Troy Majors, so where you think YOU get off...
WEST: Alright weaselboy, let me tell you a few things! First off, Chandler
Stone didn't hire me, therefore as far as I'm concerned he can't fire me!
Second, I could give two shits in a bucket what standards yer trying to raise, because you're looking at the goddamned Standard Bearer, right here, right now, and that's what it is! I'm here to rip new ones into people here, and nothing less! If Mr. Stone has a problem with it, you can tell him he's in the wrong goddamned business, and that's ONLY after he removes his lips from my ass! That's it, this interview is OVER....
REPORTER #2: Wait, Mr. West, any last comments? WEST: Yeah -- beware of Rottweiler, or you're next to be Westerminated. And that's WHAT IT IS, and there ain't NOTHIN' like it!!
[West turns away, and walks from the podium, leaving the room abuzz, and leaving DeMarco fuming....]
