[Flash] Torch

Not greatest...only spent 20 -30 mins on it...i might get flames i might not....but who cares it's only a game
[The screen opens up to a beautiful ocean view. It is totally stunning. You can see porpoise fins. Suddenly the ocean gets a little farther away as glass appears infront of the view and suddenly a man with a glass in hand is looking out to the ocean. They're are some mighty big waves out there. The man drinks out of the glass, apparently Champagne. He smiles as he steps back and you can see a view of the room. It's a hotel room. Ocean View of course. Torch goes over and sits down on the bed. He is wearing A plane white t-shirt and a pair of shorts. They look to be Union bays. It's impossible to tell the difference if they aren't. He stretches out as he reaches over to grab the remote off of the table and finds out it's stuck. he curses as he pulls as hard as he can and rips it off the table. He begins to flip through the channels. He sees
Some stuff about McGwire and Sosa....And you can hear him mutter "I hope Sosa
Wins." Then a clinton thing, then a thing about Transportation in New York
City going bad.....He cuts off the T.v. He looks at the camera, smiles and begins to speak to his ever-adoring public.]
^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v °(†)Jason "Torch" Gore(†)°: Hasn't been that long Beck since I spoke about you. But I just don't think I got all off of my chest that I wanted to in the last interview. I mean, there's a never-ending list of flaws that you carry within yourself. It just makes it so easy to criticize you in every way thinkable. Sure, call it being a smart-a** or whatever ya want, but when ya know it's the truth, you just know. Beck..Ahh..Yes my good friend.
TIm "Nugget" Beck. No matter what I have said before, I thought you would be a formidable opponent. But I was wrong. You appear to be nothing as I though you were. You have proved that truly you are nothing but a nugget and it's time you were flushed. You came out here and said some very harsh words to me that I did not appreciate at all. I mean you act like your the god of the ring. You with your little exclamations of I'm the best, I'm gonna beat you.
Blah Blah Blah. Everyone knows it's crap so your not fooling anybody but yourself. Embarrass Myself? I'm not the one that comes out here and says he's the best everyday then gets put to rest by this so-called "embarrassment." You said the EWA is putting a loser infront of you. Then why are you even talking about me. Why are you sweating it all? If your so good then you should be able to keep your mouth shut and go and there and annihilate me. But is that what your doing? NO!! Instead you decide to come out here and laugh and ridicule the Great Torch, Well If I'm a piece of sh*t I'd hate to know what you are. And you said every man that has come to you in competition has fell to your knees. And I know why you made sure to add that singles mark in here because in tag team competition. you fell to your knees for two different people. Once against me when I pinned you 1...2..3 and when you went to the locker room, got on your knees for TJ Knight, And Well the rest isn't allowed to be discussed on television. But Hell...I guess Knight is The Clinton and
Beck is the monica. Beware everyone look for the milk mustache. If you see it run. Because we all know what it is. We all know why he enjoys wrestling so much. Hell, you'd think he'd like to lose more though since he loves being
Rubbed Down by other guys. Jesus, what a sick fairy. You said they wanna make you look real good and me look bad? That's pretty hard because I don't know anyone that could make you look good considering everyone thinks your scum.
They consider you absolutely nothing. You don't get any respect around here because you don't deserve any. You talk about the past...and hell so do I.
But from now on it's no more talk about the past, it's all about the future and present. Because I will drop you will the all mighty Incinerator and I truly wanna see if your gonna have enough strength to get up off your sorry lazy a** and be able to keep on fighting. Not one man in my whole career has survived the Incinerator and neither will you. And if you tell me how your so great, and you hold the world title, and you've beat this person, that person...JESUS
I'M GONNA SNAP! You are only 2-1 in the eWa. I'm 6-0-0. You have beaten two people. you are not a freaking god man. If you can defeat me then you can brag but until then the fat lady has not yet sang and she will not be leaving the building until your on the mat bawling like a baby. It's just my kind of style. I never do something without doing it right. This Saturday Superstars marks the date when I step up to take my rightful throne as #1 contender and then you'll have no choice but to give me the world Title shot. I know you'll dread it so much but it's up to dan and the rest of the crew of prez's because my road to glory has begun and it won't end till I'm holding the World Title.
I already got the tag belts, but hell that aint good enough for me. I deserve better, I want better, and very soon I will be getting better for the simple fact that I will prove that you are not _Better_ than me and you could never
_Beat_ me on the best day of your career and on the worst day of mine. I've been Through every imaginable horror that comes in the ring. I've had arms, legs, and even my neck broken. But on the other hand I've broken Arms, Legs,
Necks, Back, Anything you can think of..I've hurt somebody by doing it. And who is there to say, beck, that this thing Won't happen to _You_! Who is there to say that you'll even be _Walking_ Out of the arena with the ability to move your body parts. I don't only plan on beating you. I plan on destroying you and every thing you've ever stood for or even thought of standing for. This is my chance for fame and fortune in the eWa and there's no way in hell that I'm gonna even think about grasping at that opportunity. You did the same when you had your chance to become World Champion. Now it's my turn. Because sooner or later your gonna have to defend that title. And why the hell not it be against me. I've earned it. I have the best Win Loss Record in eWa and I am currently a Co-Holder of the Tag titles. I've earned where I'm at so watch who's tree you bark up. If someone wants something bad enough, anything can happen and you can guarantee your bottom dollar that something will happen tonight and i see myself on top of this legendary scuffle we will have in less than 12 hours!
Because this fight will be remembered for a long time to come. I want it to be. I will work for it to be. Because whether you give two sh*ts less about me I care a whole lot about you because you hold that world title around your waist. Something that should be around my waist and i will work to all my extents to make it possible. Because this is gonna be the moment of truth.
Can you do what you say you can and take me out. only time will tell. [Pauses for a moment to ponder about something.] °(†)Jason "Torch" Gore(†)°: Beck, I am in this match 110% and I am Guaranteeing to you right now that if I'm going down, then your coming down with me. Because it's time for someone to finally put you in your place and the Booker and workers of eWa have decided for it to be me. Because they wanna see good action, because the fans wanna see two people beat the sh*t out of each other, because the wrestlers wanna know who to respect and to who not!!!!
And they will respect me. you can bet your bottom dollar on it because I have done more than earn it. I have worked for it and I will Show it at the Match tonight. Ok...so get on your sorry little knees ya dumb a** woman because I'm gonna plow you over and leave you crying on the ground. And they say that if there's a big enough tom cat then the dog runs and when the 2 year old has a killer right hook then even Tyson will fall. And This man right here is gonna break open some champagne out because...it's celebrating time.
"And why, Beck?..... Not Because Your Good.... _BECAUSE Your The God D*mn Worst_!" And Don't forget (O)When You Play With Fire(O) (O)You..... (O) (O)Get..... (O) (O) Burned..... (O) (The Screen fades out as Torch looks at the camera man, points to the door, and away he goes.)
