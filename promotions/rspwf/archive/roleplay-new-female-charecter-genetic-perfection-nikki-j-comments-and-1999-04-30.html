Roleplay new Female Charecter "Genetic Perfection" Nikki J - Comments and suggestions much obliged :)

30 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first RP with this charecter, wasn't much to talk about as it was basically an introductory Roleplay, short 'n to the point, let me know what ya think. . .
(The scene opens up inside of a large gymnasium. It moves around as we see people using the various machines. The camera moves till we get to a set of steps. It then goes down the steps and turns down a hallway as we here the
"Clank" of metal on metal. The camera turns into an entranceway as we see the free weights area. The camera moves and goes into the room then turns to the right as we see a group of extremely muscle bound men standing around a bench.
All we see are a pair of very muscular legs planted on the floor. A second later we hear the clank of the bar being put up. The men part as the person on the bench sits up and we see it is. . a Woman? Yes, not easily determined but by certain features we see it is a woman. . .a very large woman. She sits up on the bench and wipes her brow with a towel. . .)
(She is dressed in a pair of tight black spandex shorts, and a matching tight black shirt, low cut at the chest showing the cleavage of a large chest. .one of the few features which lets you know her female status. Her dark brunette hair is tied in a ponytail. The camera moves in on her as her breathing has gone down and she begins to speak, her voice masculine yet somewhat feminine all at the same time. . ]
WOMAN - International Championship Wrestling, let me introduce myself. My name is Nikki J, and as you can see by this body. .
[Nikki flexes her biceps. .] NIKKI J - I am the absolute epitome of Genetic Perfection. . . [Nikki gets up off the bench, she walks over to the wall sized mirror behind racks and racks of weights, she picks up a dumbbell and begins to do curls with it as she looks at herself in the mirror. .]
"GENETIC PERFECTION" NIKKI J - And it's time a real woman came into the ICW and hold the Woman's Championship. I am the only one good enough to do it. You can take all those prissy women like Mai, Mistress Sonia all the way up to Miss
Jasmine but none of them are equal to me. Miss Jasmine has had a nice easy ride as the only woman's champion in the history of the ICW. .
[Nikki lifts the dumbbell up and holds it there flexing her muscle, she smiles. .] NIKKI J - But this isn't history class, and the future of the ICW is here. Miss
Jasmine, you may have beaten everyone who has gotten in your path. .you may be the Crowning Jewel of wrestling, but unlike me you will never be able to reach.
. [Nikki drops the barbell to the floor and turns to the camera, she then turns back and looks into the mirror and does a double bicep flex. .]
NIKKI J - Genetic Perfection. . . [Fades out. . .]
