[RP] Please Critique (Also GLOW and AWMC fed heads, Please read. This is another sample RP for you).

7 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens to a view of a city from the sky. A caption appears reading, "Chicago Illinois." The scene pans around, and zooms in on Navy
Pier. The scene then zooms back out. The scene pans around some more. It pans to the left, Southwards, and stops at the Art Institute. The scene zooms in on the Institute, viewing the many art students going to and coming from the building. The scene zooms out once more, and continues to pan southward. Next it stops at the Field Museum, and once more zooms in. It's revolving doors spinning with customers flowing in and out. The scene zooms only partially out, and pans upward. Not far South, sits the grand Soldier
Field. The scene moves in over the stadium. The stands are full of cheering, and jeering fans. The camera zooms in. The Chicago Bears are playing some other NFL team. The camera pans upward a bit, up to the
scoreboard. It reads, "BEARS - 3 CHARGERS - 21." The scene pans all the way out again. It pans back to the right now and moves forward as it pans. The Sears Tower moves into view. The camera continues to move forward, ever closer. We see a group of people on a level with windows stretching from the floor to the ceiling. The camera pans slightly to the right and moves forward again, keeping one side of the building in view. We pass numerous spectators looking out the windows. We finally stop right in front of the
Northwest side. A man with cropped black hair and a goatee stands just inside with a pair of binoculars raised to his eyes. We cut to an inside view of the building. We move closer to the man which we saw from the outside. He is well built, stands around 6'7" or 6'8" and must weigh 280 pounds. A navy blue fleece covers his massive chest, arms, and shoulders.
Below it, a pair of light blue Gap jeans. And to top it off, brown leather boots. As he continues to gaze through the binoculars out the window, we turn and walk a few steps to a high-powered telescopic viewing machine. A black man, wearing a suit and tie steps up to the machine. After fumbling in his pockets, the man pulls out a few coins and slips them into the machine's coin slot. We cut to the view that he sees. We quickly look straight down at the busy street below; covered in city buses, pickup trucks, and the occasional semi. The camera then pans up to find a large building. A sign above its huge revolving doors reads, "LOEWS CINEPLEX."
The camera zooms further to find a flashing screen in the window of this movie theater. At the moment it is illegible. The scene cuts to a ground camera, viewing this exact same screen. The screen goes blank. Then, the words "Coming Soon ... to a Ring Near You" appear. These words fizzle and fade out. Then what looks like a mug shot appears. It is a picture of the man with the goatee in Sears Tower. Then, on either side of it, letters appear in a descending pattern. First, "D" appears to the left of the picture. The D remains and "A" appears. Now, like before, the D and A remain. This continues while an R and K appear, each below the other.
Then, on the right, in the same type of process as before, the letters W O L
F appear in descending order. The right half of the mug shot then flips to a shot of the right side of a wolf. Then the left side cuts to reveal a full shot of a wolf with the words remaining. A howl is heard and the words
"He's coming. Are you ready for him?" appear just below the wolf. The scene then cuts back to the Sears Tower. The man with the goatee, which we will call Dark Wolf from now on, approaches us. He rubs his chin curiously then glances back at the windows. He gives a quick smile. Then, in a deep, but smooth voice, he speaks.]
Dark Wolf: I've seen enough sights for one day don't you think? Besides, this is my home. I know this city like the back of my hand. So let's get out of here. The heat in here is nearly unbearable.
[Dark Wolf turns from us and makes his way down a short hallway to a small room lined with elevators. He taps the button to go down. A moment passes.
Dark Wolf patiently waits.] DING! [An elevator on the far end of the room slides open. We move over and step onto the elevator.]
DING! [The elevator's doors slide shut in front of us. The elevator begins its descent to the first floor. This takes several minutes. Finally it comes to a stop and the doors once again slide open. We step out again. Dark
Wolf signals us to follow him. We step out of the room into a the open city. The sun glares down, shining brightly off every building. A white stretch Mercedes-Benz limousine is parked at the curb. As we peer through the window, we notice the chauffeur, dressed entirely in black, reading a newspaper. Dark Wolf taps lightly on the passenger window, alerting the chauffeur of his presence. The chauffeur hurriedly buries the newspaper under his seat and steps out of the limo. He walks around the front of the car and opens the back door. As he holds it open, a young woman with a huge smile runs up to us. Her eyes gleaming and a tape recorder in hand.]
Reporter: Dark Wolf?! Beth Angston, Chicago Tribune. Mind if I get a few words with you? Dark Wolf: Sure but I'm about to leave. So if you want, you're welcome to join me. Beth: No problem. I'll have one of my colleagues pick up my car later. [Dark Wolf signals for her to climb into the limo.] Dark Wolf: After you. Beth: Thanks. [She ducks her blonde head and steps into limousine. Again we peer in as she leans against the far door. Dark Wolf climbs in afterwards. The chauffeur is about to close the door when Dark Wolf stops him.]
Dark Wolf: Let the camera man in. [We move out of the bright light of an afternoon into the dark, leather interior of the limousine. The door is shut behind us. Within a minute, the limousine is in motion. We pan back to Dark Wolf and the reporter.]
Beth: I'm writing an article in tomorrow's paper about the heroes of Chicago. Oddly enough, you've made that list. You're among the greats like
Michael Jordan, Harry Carey, and a few others. Congratulations!
Dark Wolf: Thank you. I'm actually quite shocked as I've been the bad boy of wrestling of late. But I will accept this honor gratefully. So Miss
Angston.
Beth: Call me Beth. Dark Wolf: Very well, Beth. What did you want to ask me? Beth: First of all, why get into wrestling? I hear that several people have died through wrestling. Such wrestling names as Owen Hart, Rick Rude, Louis
Spicolli, and Brian Pillman. Are you aware of the chances that you won't make it through the next night? What motivates you to continue to participate in such a brutish sport.
Dark Wolf: I continue to be a wrestler for one simple fact. I love wrestling. It is my life. As for those names which you called out, they knew the risks involved. As do I. Many people run the risk of death in their jobs. However they continue to brave it out. Does a Police officer think about losing his life while investigating a homicide? More than likely. But does it prevent him or her from doing their job to perfection?
No. What about firemen? Who have to fight roaring fires year round. They must be knowing that their lives could end any time. Yet they continue to put their lives at risk to do their job. My job is that of a wrestler. My job is to entertain. I know there are risks involved. But if I cowered away from wrestling, then my life would be empty. I'm willing to take the risk and stay in the business.
And another thing. Wrestling is not a brutish sport. In fact, it is not a sport at all, but let's not get into that. What motivates me to stay in this business? The appreciation I get from the fans. That's what motivates me.
Beth: Okay. That's basically all I wanted to know. Thanks. Dark Wolf: Sure. Beth: You can let me off at the next bus stop. I'll catch a bus for the rest of the way home.
Dark Wolf: Alright. [Turns his attention to the chauffeur] Driver, stop at the next bus stop.
[The chauffeur nods in acknowledgment. The limo stops next to a bus stop full of impatient people. The reporter opens the door and steps out. We remain with Dark Wolf as he relaxes in his seat. The reporter shuts the door and we are again in motion. Dark Wolf reaches forward to a CD player built into the car. He picks up a CD case. It reads "Creed" on the upper left and "Human Clay" in the lower right. Dark Wolf opens the case and removes its disc. He places it in the CD player and presses Play. He puts the CD case back. A moderately paced acoustic guitar begins playing. Then mild drumming mixes with it.]
## When dreaming I'm guided through another world ## ## Time and time again ## ## At sunrise I fight to stay asleep ## ## 'Cause I don't want to leave the comfort of this place ## Dark Wolf: This is the song I'll be using as my entrance song. It's called
Higher and is by Creed. A band that stands for morals. This song especially reflects how I feel and my goals in this business. I want to show the world that wrestling isn't all about big egos, hardcore wrestling, and beautiful women. It's also about the matches themselves. It's about athletics showcasing their skills.
[By this time, the song has played through a bit. As Dark Wolf stops talking, we can hear the song again.]
## Come on, let's go there ## ## Let's ask can we stay? ## ## Can you take me higher? ## ## To the place where blind men see ## ## Can you take me higher? ## ## To the place with golden streets ## Dark Wolf: Scott Stapp, the lead singer for Creed, speaks about a paradise in dreams. I'm not here to talk to you about dreams. However, this song illustrates well how I feel. It is nearly impossible to have a paradise on
Earth. A place where "blind men see" so to speak. But the important thing is, we can only make things better. That's why I'm here. I was once like many other wrestlers. I was the bad guy. The fans hated me. I swore. I cheated. And now I have changed. And I am going to make it my endeavor, to reduce the amount of evil that resides in wrestling. If not completely, then by all means by a recognizable amount.
[Again, we have missed much of the tune as Dark Wolf talks.] ## Up high I feel like I'm alive for the very first time ## ## Up high I'm strong enough to take these dreams ## ## And make them mine ## [The song stops and another begins. Dark Wolf reaches over and stops the song.] Dark Wolf: Those are my dreams, my goals, my endeavors. And I am going to put them into effect.
[The limousine jerks to a halt. Dark Wolf remains in the car as the chauffeur steps out and opens the door for him. Dark Wolf steps out and we follow. Dark Wolf hands the chauffeur a handful of cash then turns back to us. In the background, the chauffeur gets back into the limo and drives off. To the left stands a towering skyscraper.]
Dark Wolf: My condo is on the seventeenth floor. Follow me up. I still have a few things to discuss.
[The scene fades out as we head for the revolving door entrance to this massive building. Through the large windows, we can see a large lobby with carpeted floor, chandeliers everywhere, and several kinds of floor plants.
We cut back in to find a brightly lit room with a light blue carpeting. Dark Wolf stands to the left of the view, looking around.] Dark Wolf: Sarah?! [A short pause.] Sarah?! You here?! [Turns his attention back to the camera.] Good she's not here. Sarah was my girlfriend and valet in another federation. I've been having bad feelings about her lately. I think she's cheating on me, with my best friend. So things are pretty twisted around here.
[Dark Wolf walks to a large cream colored couch and sits down. We move to an armchair and sit down, facing Dark Wolf. Behind him, a lamp. Further behind that is a wall with multi-colored flowers. The camera pans to the right, viewing white curtains around high windows. The camera pans back to the left. Next to the couch is a small table cluttered with magazines and newspapers. Further to the left, a closed door. We pan back to Dark Wolf who stirs in his seat. He leans over the small table next to the couch and pulls up a small handful of magazines. As he drops them on his lap, he opens one.]
Dark Wolf: I've been looking at all these wrestling magazines. Looking for a new company. I know that it is unethical to mention the names of competing federations. However, I believe we can make an exception for this introductory video. For the past five months, I've been a wrestler in the
World Wrestling Association with my close friend Crazy Horse. [A pause.]
It was there that the evil enveloped me. It has all gone smoothly there. But on a recent card, Sarah, who goes by the name Wild Animal, came out with
Crazy Horse for a singles match against a rookie. The rookie attacked Wild
Animal as she was trying to help Crazy Horse. C.H. then attacked this rookie, knocking Wild Animal, Sarah, down in the process. Since then, I have been having bad feelings about the two having an affair.
[Dark Wolf holds up a magazine for the camera. The magazine is called,
"AWMC Magazine." Dark Wolf puts it down and opens it up. As he glances at the sheets before flipping them, he continues to speak to us.]
Dark Wolf: These suspicions of mine began after I sent an application to the
AWMC. I even put Sarah in to be my valet there as well. If I get accepted into that company, I'm afraid she won't be able to come along. Sarah and I split up. I don't need any heart breaks from her now. I hate to say this but I need to look out for one person. That person being me.
[Dark Wolf looks back down at the magazine in his hand. He tosses it to the side and picks up another. It's title reads, "GLOWing Hot News." He opens it and begins flipping through its pages.]
Dark Wolf: GLOW is another company that I'm keeping an eye on. To you GLOW execs, I sent in my application to you. You should have gotten it by now.
And if not, check your mailbox. It should be there. [He tosses the magazine, along with the remaining magazines in his hands, to the side. Dark Wolf folds his hands in front of him.]
Dark Wolf: If you two companies really want to get that all important edge over the other, then acknowledge, and accept my application. You won't regret it.
[Keys are heard jingling. Dark Wolf's attention is turned to a door behind us. We pan around to that same spot. The door opens, revealing a young woman, probably in her late 20s. She is dressed in an expensive, light green colored dress.]
Dark Wolf [To the woman.]: Sarah. What are you doing here? This is my home. I told you we were splitting up until I find out the truth about you and Crazy Horse.
Sarah: I told you. There's nothing between me and Crazy Horse. We are just friends. Is that wrong? [She turns her attention to the camera.] Get out of here! This is none of your business.
Dark Wolf: I think it is you that should get out. [We zoom out through the window, revealing yet again, the huge building. We continue to zoom out. Finally, we can see much of the downtown area of
Chicago. The scene cuts to black, leaving only an image of the wolf we saw in the movie theater window. Below it, a caption reading, "Dark Wolf
Productions." We fade out completely.]
