YOKOZUKI ROBOTTO! RP, please...well, read, at least.

25 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This was my second flash for Zenchi Puroresu...which folded before the match occured. Robotto never had a match. I'm depressed. Oh, well. Essentially this is a comedy skit that was to be shown on the "Chigo Chigo Variety Show" which is a funky flash packet with a japanese flavor. Sort of a cross between a RL cartoon and that Bloopers and Practical jokes show. The manager's the only guy in the whole skit who's not in on the joke.
Be kind, I'm not used to the character yet...well, never will be since Zenchi closed. Feh, just read. :( -- [Softly, softly, volume escalating, "Mr Roboto" by Styx is heard playing against the black television screen.]
#Domo arigato, Mr. Roboto,# [Getting louder.] #Mata ah-oo hima de# [Louder still.] #Domo arigato, Mr. Roboto,# [No longer could the music be considered quiet.] #Himitsu wo shiri tai# [The song goes instrumental, changing in tone to sound like elevator music and fading to background noise. Now, voices, faint and garbled, are heard... A moment longer, and a scene appears upon the screen...
The fans at home are treated to an aerial view of an electronics store. It appears as a security camera, looking down on the store at an angle. The shoppers therein meander around, picking up small items here and there.
Software, hardware, recorders, Pokemon, y'know...stuff... In one corner of the establishment there appears a glint, moving from behind a large display of diodes and circuitry... It's nature is unclear for a moment, the object itself being too far from the camera and blurred by lack of focus as the camera seems to hold it's attention on the center of the store. This object on the periphery appears strange...
It's not _just_ a shopping cart... It's not a trick of the sun... A quick and unexpected zooming in, and it is shown full screen. It is a man, _pushing_ a shopping cart...but the man is quite unusual. Metallic with no face, no hair just antennae for ears and red lenses as eyes. This is, of course, the heroic robot...Yokozuki Robotto.]
Shopping lady: Whu...whu...what is that!? Voice (off-camera): What? You never seen a midget before? [The camera pans to focus on a small figure seated in the back of the cart...a small figure. It is Shounin Takumi, the "creator" of Yokozuki
Robotto. He wears his blue mechanic's jumpsuit, toolbelt, bald head and fu-man-chu-like mustache that dangles from his chin.
The shopping woman brushes past Robotto and Takumi, seemingly afraid...or at least somewhat purturbed.]
ST (Shounin Takumi): My, she was rude...wasn't she Robotto? YR (pumping out prerecorded dialogue from Ed McMahon): You are correct sir! Oh-ho-hohaha... [The pair continue to poke about, the camera focusing on them with a quite security-minded interest. Robotto and Takumi probably being the most suspicious-looking types in the whole building after all. They seem aimless for a moment, then the camera abruptly cuts to a few minutes later. A man dressed as a store manager approaches the pair as Takumi examines an expensive color Pokemon, having removed it from it's packaging.]
Manager: Excuse me...I'm sorry, but I'm going to have to ask you to leave. ST: LEAVE!? Why? I'm simply obtaining components for use in my experiments. Why, my greatest creation, Robotto, here requires a new secondary processor for his wrestling match against Acinonyx... I simply must have these parts.
M: Robotto? [Speaking in his own "voice" for the first time since this clip has begun, Robotto gives off a metal tone, flat and mechanical.] YR: Yes? M: No, what is Robotto? YR: I am Robotto. M: You're telling me that you created this...this... I don't believe it. YR: You should not, I did not create me, Shounin Takumi did. M: I'm sorry, but I'm not buying this. Tanuki... ST: Takumi. M: Whatever, I'm still going to have to ask you to leave. YR: But the heroic robot requires components. M: What? ST: Robotto is a hero. Did you not know? M: But I thought you said he was a wrestler!? ST: Yes, yes he is... Robotto, please demonstrate for the man... YR: Affirmitive. Little child?... [The camera pans down to show Robotto's legs, and there, surprisingly, is a little boy clinging to his leg. This is unexplained, and quite strange to see. The child doesn't respond, and so...]
YR: I said..."Little child?". [The boy finally looks up, grinning a silly little grin, as a mischevous boy should.] Little child: What? YR: What is your mother's name? LC: Why? YR: I am going to utilize my remote transmitter to broadcast an announcement of your whereabouts over the store's loudspeaker.
LC: What!? YR: I'm going to call her. LC: Oh...okay. Her name is Akane. ST: Pretty name. YR: Affirmitive. **frzkt** [A crackling sound is heard, and a few lights flash on Robotto's face. Suddenly, and very loudly, his mechanical, droning voice is heard over the loudspeaker of the store. As it is, the camera zooms out to show a wide angle of the store again.]
YR: Would miss "Akane" please relocate to aisle three, next to the color Pokemon display. Your child is clinging to my leg. [A pause.] YR: He seems to think that I am a jungle gym. [Takumi cracks up, laughing loudly.] YR: Thank you, that is all. [The camera pans and zooms to show a pretty young Japanese woman...the same shopping lady from before. She wears a nice pink dress, and has shoulder length black hair. A few moments of silence now. The camera pans about the store, showing the stunned store manager, who is totally befuddled over
Robotto's usage of the store's PA system. The child finally pipes up...] LC: I don't think you're a jungle gym. I think you're a superhero! YR: Affirmitive... ST: Robotto is a superhero, small one... LC: Small one? You're the midget! ST: YOUNG ONE! Whatever... He is a superhero, my greatest creation...Yokozuki Robotto. Watch on the next Zenchi broadcast. You will see. He shall triumph over the great evil of Acinonyx... He shall set an example for everyone to follow.
LC: He's shiny! ST: Yes...he is shiny... [At this point, the woman "Akane" appears next to this clump of people. The manager (still in shock), the "heroic robot" and the...midget mechanic...
She does a double take, looking down at her child, and up at the metal man that he clings to.]
Akane: Taka...Taka, what are you doing!? LC: I found a superhero! Can I keep him!? A: YOU MOST CERTAINLY CAN _NOT_!!! Now...come here...come to mommy... LC: Awww...but mom... A: DO IT!!! [Shocked and scared, little "Taka" rapidly detaches himself from Robotto's leg, and scampers to his mother's side. "Akane" wastes no time in shooing her child away from Robotto. The manager rubbernecks between the girl and the robot...utterly stupified.]
ST: There. I believe that we've proven our nature. Now, may I proceed to disassemble this Pokemon into it's separate components for integration into
Robotto's system? M: S...sh... Just...don't...don't scare anyone else. Please!? YR: Scare? This does not compute... M: Whatever...whatever... Just shop and get out... [The manager walks away, shaking his head and still mumbling to himself.] ST: See Robotto? He was quite reasonable once he saw your great abilities. A hero...garners respect in this world. YR: Affirmitive... [Robotto goes back to pushing the shopping cart as Shounin Takumi remains in the cart's kiddie seat, fiddling with the Pokemon. The camera pans back out, and music thunders back in...]
#Domo arigato, Mr. Roboto, domo...domo...DOMODOMO!# [A sudden blackness envelopes the screen, and all goes silent. End...] -- Every last damn thing I've typed in this post is Š and Ž [name withheld], by god! Whoo-hah!!!
