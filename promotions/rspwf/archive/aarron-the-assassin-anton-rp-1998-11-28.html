Aarron "The Assassin" Anton--RP

28 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Throughout the years assassins have killed politicans, would be kings and even children. Webster's dictionary defines an assasin in the following text: "A professional killer.")
*A look at Aarron Anton* {The Scene} (A dark, drab corner of a secluded neighborhood or maybe a deserted building somewhere in the central American region. Or better yet, maybe he is in your home and waiting for you.)
Aarron "The Assassin" Anton: Politicans, major world leaders and even common people. None are immune from the "Assassins" clutches and when the "Assassin"
comes a calling for you, no one gets away because your life belongs to me. (The camera picks up a clear view of Aarron Anton as he steps out of the shadows and into some light. He is a 6' 3" male and has coal, black hair. He is wearing a black turtle neck shirt, black jeans and a black trench coat.
Smiling, he continues.) Aarron "The Assassin" Anton: I was born in Italy. I reside wherever the winds take me. I am the "hired" gun of the wrestling world per se'. I have traveled the world over and have been in many wars within many federations and now, I come looking for new blood. You see, I come for men like King Apples, James
Pillow, Jack Vars and Mad Goat. I look for a federation where men are men and women are proud of it. I look for men who dare me to do what I say I can do.
I look to destroy lives, careers and to further my own. Legacy and destiny is my goal, it matters none whatsoever to me whom I may face. My goal is clear:
"To kill." (Anton walks away for a moment and then steps up with an evil glare in his dark eyes and responds.)
Aarron "The Assassin" Anton: If there is a federation around that feels they can harnass the power of "The Assassin," then I dare you to sign me into your company. For if you do, you are hiring a "Professional Killer" and you may just not know what it is you are getting in to.
(Anton drifts back into the darkness and the camera goes to black.) **Anyone interested, email me at [detail withheld]
