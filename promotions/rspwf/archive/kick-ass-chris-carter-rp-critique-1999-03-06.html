'Kick Ass' CHRIS CARTER RP - Critique

6 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tell me what you think of this Roleplay... -=-=-=-==-=-=-=-=- {The Scene opens inside a bar somewhere in Cleveland Ohio. The camera looks up at the camera which says it's 10:24PM. The camera pans around looking around and see's numeros people lounging around with a pint glass in their hand. Some are asleep. Then the camera notices 'Kick Ass' Chris Carter, the new ICW
Television champion sitting in the corner finishing off his pint. The camera goes over and sits next to him as he looks at the camera. He is wearing a leather jacket, white t-shirt and jeans. He has a cigarette in his hand}
'KICK ASS' - Well well well, it's none other than an ICW camera man. Wassup? Fancied a pint and brought your camera to make out you've come to record my comments? CAMERA MAN - Well I... 'KICK ASS' - You are completely PATHETIC!! You should never drink on your job or you'll get my foot right up your ass, did you hear that?
CAMERA MAN - Yes Mr.Carter... 'KICK ASS' - Good! Now, last Friday night I came, I saw and I won the Television title in that five man battle royal. It was so great!! That was the day that Chris Carter conquered all when he through both Makku Torokko and
Konrad Curze's ass over that top rope and eliminating them from the match. Then
Dragon, how did it feel to be pinned in the middle of the ring, 1,2,3 by a
'Kick Ass' legend like Chris Carter? All four of you stupid pathetic S.O.B's found out that Chris Carter does indeed Kick Ass and that I am soon to be the greatest ICW Television champion of ALL time!!
{Carter finishes the last bits of his pint. Then raises his arm up with the glass in it and shouts}
'KICK ASS' - GIVE US ANOTHER ONE!!! BARMAID - Don't you think you've had enough sir? 'KICK ASS' - {Smiles} You know what....I DON'T THINK I'VE HAD ENOUGH YOU UGLY SLAG!! {The barmaid gets embarrased and runs behind the bar. Then a guy comes up to
Chris and looks down at him. Carter looks up and stands up to him}
'KICK ASS' - What the FUCK do you think you are doing? GUY - I don't think you should've said that to that barmaid now, do you? 'KICK ASS' - {Laughs} Well, I guess you're right. Sorry man... {The guy turns his back} 'KICK ASS' - Oh by the way... {He spins the guy around, boots him in the gut and gives him the Kick Ass
Stunner as the rest of the customers look on in shock. Carter then walks out the bar as the other people stay far away from him. The camera man then catches up with him}
'KICK ASS' - Stupid assholes. They dunno what they're getting theirself into messing with Chris Carter and that was just a prime example of what to expect,
Dante Calavria!! That's right Dante, next Friday night from Detroit Michigan in front of a sold out General Motors Place you will be another on the Chris
Carter hitlist who will find out why they call me ''Kick Ass''. Tha Dragon,
Makku Torokko, Patrick Dempsey and Konrad Curze all found it out last Friday why I am the NEW ICW Television champion and I'm gonna continue to be the T.V champion by beating your sorry ass up and down that ring like the jabroni that you are. Dante, bring all the back-up you can get and be prepared to get one hell of a beating...
{Carter see's a bench and sits on it} 'KICK ASS' - Dante Calavria, you are the unfortunate one who has been chosen to be the first victim of Chris Carter under his Television title reign. They see this title is the hardest title to hold onto which is true for the likes of Tha
Dragon and Freddy Floyd but now I'm the champion, there wont be many names going into the history books as Television champions. I mean come on...The ICW has signed two matches for me to defend the title in. One is of course this
Friday's match at Fallout against the Devastation Inc. loser Dante Calavria and the other is for Spring Slamathon where I must defend my title against two of the guys I eliminated last week on Fallout, Makku Torokko and Tha Dragon. Does that suck or what? Don't the ICW get it? I've already beaten them so why do they deserve another shot?
CAMERA MAN - Who knows.. 'KICK ASS' - Exactly...You know it doesn't really bother me because I'll defend this title at every card, every PPV and at every Rockin Da World Tour card if I have to because I know that there is NOBODY and I mean NOBODY that has the ability to step into the ring with Chris Carter and kick his ass and more importantly take his title off him, so I have nothing to worry about..
CAMERA MAN - You think you can take everyone on the ICW roster? 'KICK ASS' - Listen little man, I don't think I can...I KNOW I CAN!!! Makku
Torokko and Tha Dragon are two of the most sorriest guys walking the ICW. Note that I said ''Walking'' because soon, very soon, Spring Slamathon in fact.
Those two guys wont be walking EVER AGAIN!! You would of thought those two guys had a taster of Chris Carter last week on Fallout and would've backed away from fighting me again but no, they sign the contract to meet me again. Maybe, they like getting their ass kicked..Who knows?
CAMERA MAN - Yeah you saying that, don't you think you'll get a little tired defending the title week in, week out on national t.v?
'KICK ASS' - OF COURSE NOT!! When I won this title I knew what comes with it and that's defending it each week and that's no problem. The only problem is for all those young kids and the people who are weak at heart because all they'll be seeing is a whole lot of ASS KICKING, mostly coming from me. Gold is my color and that's the way it's gonna stay....
CAMERA MAN - What do you think of the competition in the ICW? There could be quite a few people who want the T.V title. Some people like Chuck Williams,
Johnny Gunz, FBK and 'HotStuff' Darren Henry...
'KICK ASS' - {Laughs} Don't make me laugh. Chuck Williams? Johnny Gunz? FBK? Darren Henry? Who are you trying to kid? These guys don't even deserve a shot at the ''Jabroni'' title!!
CAMERA MAN - Jabroni title? 'KICK ASS' - Is there a parrot in here? Yeah stupid, the Jabroni title. Chuck
Williams is nothing more than ''Mr.Loser''. Johnny Gunz doesn't know the meaning of 'Locked 'n Loaded'. He's so stupid, not only does he allign himself with that freak Xenon but he also could be a contender for MY title, which is a stupid thing to be. Then there's FBK. Many people say this guy could be the next wave of competition here in the ICW which is BS!! You are looking at the next wave of competition here in the ICW and this Full Blood Killah will find out what an ass-kicking is all about, Chris Carter style. Then last and by all means least, there's Darren Henry. Darren, no offense man but...YOU SUCK!!
Plain and simple and if you ever stick your nose in my business, you'll pay the consequences like the rest of this list and the rest of the ICW roster will, sooner rather than later....
CAMERA MAN - Any last thoughts on Dante Calavria? 'KICK ASS' - What are you, an interviewer now? Yeah, I do have a few last thoughts for Dante Calavria. Dante, I hope you saw me kick all the other four pieces of trash's ass on Friday because I'm gonna do that to you, TEN TIMES
WORSE!! Dante, I don't care whether you are a part of the Devastation Inc. or even the Ink pen because once you step into the ring with Chris Carter you know that I will....
..KICK.... .....YOUR..... ........ASS.... Just like Tha Dragon and Makku Torokko will find out at Spring Slamathon. Now turn that damn camera off, I'm getting outta here...
{Carter gets up and walks away as the scene fades into darkness} =--==-=-=-=-=-=-=--==-=--==--= Wayne P. Handler of.... 'Kick Ass' Chris Carter - ICW Television Champion. Former WCWA LightHeavyweight and FWF Cruiserweight Champion
