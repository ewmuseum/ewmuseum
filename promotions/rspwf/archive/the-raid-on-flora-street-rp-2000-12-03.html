The Raid on Flora Street, RP

3 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
by "Drastic" O. Skiller, from The Four Aces stable, WIW - http://wIw.8k.com ....34th district.... ....Flora St., 6..... ....Nov 23, 2000..... .......1:30
AM....... Sleep, sleep the great city of controversy and rivalry, sleep deep and sleep well, dream the nameless dreams of brighter future... and deny the rotten present. For in your very heart, oh, great city of great America, worms of crime and corruption reside; your urban jungles are contaminated...
with mafia!!! With their tires rustling against the sidewalk, several police cars and two
FBI vans are pulling over, encircling a regular two-stories house, like any other house in the area. Numerous cops and federal agents, in bullet-proof vests and helmets, armed with laser-aiming automatic guns, are spilling out, getting into positions, keeping a complete silence, both vocal and radio - it's a calm neighborhood, no need to alarm all the residents with the knowledge that a massive raid, probably with many casualties, is about to take place.
Until a sound of screeching wheels tear the quietness apart. "What the f##k?!" one of the federal agents prepares his rifle, he and several other colleagues of his approach the newly arrived vehicle.
Its doors swing open and four individuals, their faces clad with black protective masks, step out. One of them raises his palm, warning the agents, who are ready to attack, and briefly flashes his I.D.
"A.C.E." is all that the chief agent had time to read. "Yes," the stranger replies with a harsh voice, "ACE - Absolutely Confidential Emissaries for you. We are the ones who tipped you guys about this house being Barnetto's family's headquarters. Remember, code name I'm supposed to tell you - "bestseller". We're guaranteed to receive a full
back-up." "Oh, yeah... I sure hope, though, that your info isn't phoney, pal, or you and your buddies will be the ones arrested. What's you source, anyway?"
"Let's just say I got a letter from the past. (Read Skiller's previous rp) Well, I think we should move in..." "Curb your enthusiasm, pal! I'm the senior officer here, and I'm running this operation. You and your posse do whatever you came here to do, just don't stand in the way if some bullets start flying. Your tip is appreciated - but nothing more... All right, people, LET'S GO!!!!"
Years of training are sure paying off. BOOM! - the doorway into the house is gone in a explosion of smoke and splinters, as the feds and the policemen, covering each other's backs, aiming the laser-targets at every shadow that moves, are now rushing into the nest of mafia. Soon, the first shots are heard from the inside, followed by gas grenades popping and glass being shattered, as the thugs aren't quite ready to give up.
Meanwhile, on the outside, the A.C.E. troopers, holding nothing but a club each, are taking less known backdoor, they climbs a secret stairwell - all according to the same letter, and in mere seconds - while the agents fight for their lives against the armed mobsters only two rooms away - they are in
Don Barnetto's private office.
"All right, guys, she said her money savings is somewhere in this fat mafioso's safe, let's find it and bail, we can party tomorrow night... You know what they say, he who steals from a thief, is a saint."
They spread in the office, thoroughly checking the furniture and the walls, until a click of an unguarded gun makes them all freeze in their places.
Enshrouded in his night robe, his white hair a complete mess, his biddy eyes are blood-shot, Don Barnetto in person steps into the center of the room, pointing a gun at the one who spoke earlier. "Who are you, f##kers, and how did you get here?!!! I know you're not with the assholes downstairs, who shoot my people there!!!"
Bang!! - and one of the A. C. Emissaries clocks Don Barnetto across his back with the heavy iron club. The Don goes down, and the four jump on him, disarming him completely.
"Nice job, F'n Show!" "Well, they don't call me Ace of Clubs for nothing!" They first one, holding the gun, grabs a hold of the mafia's leader and raises him to his feet.
"Who... who are you all?... I know you..." The one holding him removes his helmet, and Barnetto's eyes widen, as he recognizes his nightly adversary.
"You!... You!" "That's right," and the helmet is lowered back to its place. "The one whose life your thugs made very unpleasant. Well, it's the last time your people have laid your filthy hands on Cammy, and this is the time for you to say good-bye to all the cash you were draining from The Goddess for your services. No more bodyguarding the SmithCorp, no more being Tito Demo
24-hours-protection, no more illegal deals being pushed as a part of a pro-wrestling show. I win, you lose, Barnet.. Ow!"
Barnetto drives a knee in the speaker's stomach, tears away his gun and points it at him again. The moonlight comes through the window, lighting up
Don's twisted face, a face of a beast that refuses to die without the last fight.
"Nobody f##ks with the mafia..." and his finger begins to squeeze the lethal trigger. Everything happens too fast, and yet too slow. The other three troopers are paralyzed to their spots, incapable to interfere without risking the life of their partner. The fourth one looks into the barrel of the gun, and it appears to him like the dark tunnel that many people report seeing, after having a near-death-experience. And exactly at the same eternal moment, a red laser mark slides across the demented face of the familia's head.
And the entire world of "Drastic" O. Skiller becomes one roaring explosion, and at that moment he is reborn, after deciding he is going to die. A bullet shatters the glass of the room's window and enters, in an unbearably slow motion, into the skull of Don Barnetto. Bits of brain and a lot (too much!)
of blood splash against Skiller's helmet, as both him and Don drop to their knees, Don being on his way to the fiery pits of Hell, Skiller just now starting to live...
One of the Aces approach the broken window, looking to the outside. Right there, in the backyard's garden, the senior federal agent stands, his rifle still smoking. It's then that they notice that the shootings have completely stopped, as all the thugs have either turned themselves over to the hands of the law, or joined their boss in Purgatory.
"Don't you think I've checked before giving a go to this operation?!" the agent shouted from the ground. "There isn't such thing as A.C.E., at least not in the field of law-enforcement. So I suggest you boys get the heck out of here, before any more unnecessary questions will rise... and thanks."
"Yeah, I think our faces have filled this place enough," whispers the one next to the window. "Right, Acid?"
"I sure don't get such action back in London, I can tell you that much. Come on, help me drag Skiller out of here, I think he's in shock. I'll buy you all a brewski later."
They safely get their friend downstairs, but, as they approach their van,
Skiller comes back to himself, insisting they'll let him stand on his feet.
He wipes the Sicilian blood off his helmet's shed, then takes the headwear off. He gives the last look to the house on Flora street, its walls now painted blue-and-red of the police cars' sirens, and whispers the last words for this amazingly long night:
"Falling, falling the tower of Babylon... Ruined, the house of Asher descends into the swamp... Crumbles and rots away, the empire that Smith built..."
