[CRITIQUE] Greg "The Great" Daniels

5 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ok, this promo was made in a promo thread, so some of the jokes might not make sense since it's based on stuff already said. The Role play is for AW's Gathering of the Elite, Second night.
[Greg Daniels is shown in some Philly ghetto, hiding inside a garbage can. He is wearing his usual shirt and pants, and to blend in he has a
band-aid on his right cheek] Greg: So yeah, unlike the rest of these fairies, I don't need to be on
TV every diddly fucking day. I'm so over, that I might make one or two appearances, yet I'm one of the most over guys when it comes to the night of the show. Do you see the ovations I get? Did you see the heat
I got from the Japanese fans, or the kinds of eruptions I got from
Scandinavia, Florida, New York, and Las Vegas? Infact, I'm so much of a star, that the worthless titles that Cobryn and Bunglestein brag about, I never NEEDED.
[Greg notices two 'Gang-bangers walking down the street, and ducks into the garbage can] Gang-banger1: So yeah, what you think of the market rally? Gang-banger2: It is very promising. This war, as many people in the world might be against it, just might rejuvenate our economy
Gang-banger1: Short term, yes, but long term? Gang-banger2: Long term definitely. Weapon manufacturers will be increasing productivity, and that means jobs. The more jobs, the more money the middle class has, and the more money being spent........
Gang-banger1: .....Consumer confidence picks up and we have another boom. That's a brilliant philosophy!
Gang-banger2: Glad you see things my way...... [The two walk off and Greg pops his head back out of the trash can] Greg: Anyway, Jobert and Cobryn-san, you guys just love the TV time, huh? I mean, I know you guys need alot to get over, but why shove yourself down people's throat......especially for the 18-35 year old males? If you're not talking about how good you are, then you'll talking about some title you won in a local backyard fed. Don't you realize, that no one cares? Those boo's you here, its not the quote-unquote "Heat", but rather the type of boo's that Tim Couch gets at the 'friendly' compounds of.......what the hell is that piece of shit called anyway? Oh shit, not again.......
Greg ducks in the garbage can, but before he can, some black guy grabs the lid and throws it to the ground. Greg pops up with his fist cocked and.........
Greg: Sucka? Sucka: Cracka? Greg: Sucka, it's you bro! Sucka: Yo cracka, what yo youngbull ass been doing? Greg: Nothing much.........wait, weren't you one of my prior "gimmicks"? Sucka: Hell no cracka, I just be another black wrestler who hanged out wit yo Greg: Ok, so Pagan or whoever the hell he is was just talking out of his ass, huh? Sucka: Yeah, what be new cracka? Greg: Nothing I guess.........Why am I in the ghetto again? Oh wait, I came to get some St. Ides for my buddy Cyrus "Insert generic moniker here" Williams. Have some on you?
Sucka: No.......plus dat stuff be fo pussies Greg: Yeah, I know, but it'll make Cyrus look tough cause only tough guys drink whiskey and stuff. So on that note, I'm leaving the ghetto and getting my white ass back to the apartment. See ya generic black guy.....err, Sucka!
[Greg begins to walk down the alley, and the camera stays right infront of him] Greg: Rally, spam rocks, huh? I mean, nothing beats a nice can of spam while watching Cobryn hang out at a supermarket, or Jobert try to act educated just because his dad supported his lazy ass and made him rich even though my good for nothing dad didn't give me shit.........THANKS
ALOT YOU PIECE OF TRASH! [Greg gets to his black BMW Z-3, and unlocks it. He begins to open the door, then stops] Greg: Yeah, I'd say something else here, but I want to give the rest of the jobbers a chance to get over. Oh well, driving is fun as hell
[Greg enters the BMW, and starts it up. Greg drives off as the scene fades to African American]
