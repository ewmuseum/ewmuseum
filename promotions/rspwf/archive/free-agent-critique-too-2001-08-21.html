[FREE AGENT] Critique, too

21 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, it's been a while since I've seen anything critiqued or whatnot. Anyway,
I'm back and I'm a free agent (as if anybody gave a damn). =)
Here's my first flash in a few months. It's a new character, he goes by the name of Serenity. I'm looking for a fed to take a new wrestler to. It should be one RP a week, one PBP card a week, RP packets, active ... you know a conventional decent fed. =)
Thanks, Briggs (Chris Winters' old handler) ********************************************* [The camera opens up to Serenity sitting in the same position as last week. Sitting against a tall Oak tree, under the Utica moonlight. The moonlight casts a shadow from behind him, hiding his face. He wears a pair of baggy jeans and a wifebeater tank top, exposing his nicely defined arms. He begins to speak in a casual, uncaring tone.]
SERENITY: I got a letter in the mail today. [pauses] Contrary to what I anticipated, it was a fan letter. I opened it up expecting a congradulations on my success in Philidelphia, prior to leaving the area. What I got, instead, was very short and simple. To sum it up, the letter said,
"Serenity, I just wanted to write you and ask you how it felt to get your first taste of MVW competition in this week?"
Which was of course followed by a few paragraphs of illegible nonsense. My instinctive reaction was fear. Yes, fear. I was horrified by the prospect of the two idiots I defeated this week being 'the competition'. I came to Mohawk
Valley Wrestling because I was under the impression that the talent was reasonsable. I thought I'd give me a challenge, something to keep me busy.
[Serenity scratches his head] SERENITY: I think in two matches, I spent a total of ten minutes in the ring. Each day that goes by, I grow more and more tired of this place. And I grow more and more tired of not seeing anything of interest to me. When I walk through the MVW locker room, I smell vanity, self promotion and conceit. But where I came from, when I walked through the locker room I smelled blood, sweat and tears.
Things are certainly different in this area. Especially when you have guys walking around calling themselves "Hot Stuff" and
"New Future" and "The Essense". And some even have nifty names, like ... say
... "the Sensation of Innovation". I was told in the locker room that next week I was going to be sent to the ring to take on a newcomer, just like me.
Except, this guy has a few wins under his belt, and was about to hit the ring for one of his toughest challenges to date. This man was Jakob Taylor.
[Serenity stops for a moment] SERENITY: Jakob, I'm not going to lie. When I heard that, I walked over and caught your match with Kevin James. I was curious. Christ, a name like
"Sensation of Innovation" and all, I figured you might be somebody that would impress me. You know, one of those guys that knows seventeen counters to the eye gouge. But what I saw, was you getting pinned by Kevin James in the center of the ring in about five minutes. Faster than the average man takes a shit.
I find that neither sensational nor innovative. When you step in the ring with me on Friday, you'd better bring your _A_ game and be prepared to use it, because you're not stepping into the ring with Kevin James. That's the scary part. You're stepping into the ring with Serenity. A man that won't feel bad about taking you apart for a half an hour before I cover you. A man that won't hold back when I hit you with the Corpsemaker. A man that might pick you up and hit you with it again after it's all over. Anything I can enjoy the rush for a while. Anything.
People should be really careful around here. People should really watch their mouths. I hear a lot of men slinging insults at eachother and claiming to be the best. I want to warn you not to involve me in any of your petty games and nonsense.
[Serenity lights a cigarette] And speaking of petty games and nonsense ... I hear I've been entered into an eight man elimination match. Yeah, that thing where the winners wind up moving on to Monday where they meet the winner of this and that. All for an opportunity to face the winners of three or four other clusterf*cks. Regardless of what you call it, I'm sided with "Hawiian
Hotstuff" Johnny Adonis. The Oneida County Champion. I don't have any friends and I'm not used to partners, but Johnny, I'm willing to focus on the
'opposition' if you are. It's no fun fighting a two front war, if there's bad chemistry we can settle things between us at another juncture. No sense in getting the sh*t kicked out of us by four other guys.
[pauses, ponders] SERENITY: But then, of course, [grin] if you don't agree with that we can have it your way. Anyway, that's all I have on my mind right now when it comes to
MVW. No reason in wasting my time and yours.
[he tosses his cigarette on the ground, stands up, and pushes the camera crudely out of his face]
SERENITY: That's right, leave. [fade to black]
