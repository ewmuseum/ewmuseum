(the camera opens out of focus . . . for several seconds, the picture remains the same, blurry . . .
an eternity of seconds pass as the camera slowly comes into focus . . . the once blurry object is now as clear as day, illuminated by a dull light produced from the nearby candles seen in the shot. The object you ask?
as the camera zooms in closer, we see a foot . . . a picture of perfection . . . shapely and well cared for . . . the pedicured nails on the ends of the elegant and perfect toes all a deep shade of red . . . on the middle toe, a small silver band of a ring rests . . .
as the camera zooms out, we see that the foot is attached to a leg . . . the lower portion of the leg, a combination of feminine shapeliness and a musculature gained from long hours in a gymnasium. The smooth and supple skin gained from long hours of pampering . . . moving along the calf, the camera moves upward . . . from the calf to the bare thigh . . . all a picture of perfection . . . tan . . . silky smooth . . .
past the knee, we see the other leg folded under the first one and like it's
"twin", without fault. Again the camera continues on it's odyssey . . .
here was millions of years of evolutionary perfection . . . again the camera continued ever slowly upwards . . . the bare flesh of the leg showing itself high onto the hip where just a hint of white cotton panty has peaked out from under the beige silk ruffled shirt that quickly covers the area as the owner of this work of art shifts to find comfort . . .
the camera pulls back slightly, this time showing the shirt now covering the upper portions of the thighs of this heavenly creature . . . moving along the ruffles of the light colored shirt, the ruffles part at the last button gives way to the smooth skin of this gorgeous creature's stomach . . . tanned and toned, a small golden ring protruding from her belly button . . . moving up, the soft flesh gives way to the deep cleavage just barely covered by the shirt allowing for a PG-13 rating . . . her chest rising and falling with each breath
. . . from chest to neck and from there to the face . . . the lips . . . pouty and full . . . bearing the same deep red shade as the polish of her toenails .
. . from the nose to the almond shaped eyes . . . beautiful . . . her dark eyes
. . . barely slits peering into the viewer's soul . . . her face framed by long raven colored tresses falling down over her shoulder on one side . . .
everything perfect . . . if Helen of Troy, with her legendary beauty, was the most beautiful woman in man's history . . . then surely tonight, she had met her match in Jade . . .
as the camera pulls back even further, we see her reclining comfortably to one side in a chaise lounge . . . propped up on one elbow, the sultry woman relaxing with chin on fist and the long red colored nail of her pinky just barely in her mouth . . .
Jade whispers quietly as she motions with her taloned index finger . . .) <Jade> Come closer . . . (as the camera moves in closer, Jade carefully moves sits upright.) There are two kinds of men in this world . . . those that "can" and those that
"will" . . . and like everywhere, these men exist in the BASE . . . but tonight, my only concern is three men . . . three men who you will become very familiar with in the next few weeks.
The first of these men is a man known as "Black Irish" . . . hailing from
Ireland, Tom Morgan is arguably the toughest of our three . . . a man who specializes in the "Art" of Brawling . . . a man who has come to the BASE with a reputation of injuring his foes and a long successful record to back up his claims . . . this man has come to the United States and the BASE to continue doing what he is simply the best at and that is hurting people.
Our second player, comes to us from California . . . "Pure Pleasure" Paully
Dean . . . here is a man who is quite possibly the best technical wrestler in the entirety of the BASE Cruiserweight ranks . . . and though he punishes his opponents in an altogether different manner, Paully Dean has an impressive resumé of men he has beaten, not only here in the BASE but elsewhere as well .
. . he has all of the makings of a champion. The final man in the equation is an unknown . . . (behind Jade, another approaches . . . the usually dapperly dressed, though now shirtless gentleman moves towards the chaise lounge . . . in each hand, a long stemmed goblet filled with a dark red liquid . . . reaching over the seat, he hands one to Jade who takes it and lifts the glass to her lips . . . taking a small drink of the liquid . . . a single swallow and she is ready to speak once again.)
in 13, we have a man who has been taken in and under the tutelage of this man . . . (she reaches up and and places her hand on the muscular forearm of the shirtless gentleman, his chest bearing many scars from previous battles . . .
patting his arm, a sly smile crosses his lips as he adjusts his small round rimmed glasses which hide his eyes, his long black hair falling down over his shoulders.)
a man who was once considered to be pound-for-pound the best fighter in the world as both a submission fighter as well as one of the BsWo's most recognized wrestlers . . . a man who has won championships against the very best . . . a man who has brought with him one of the most devastating submission holds in the sport of professional wrestling, today . . . a true Master of the Oriental
Spike . . .
and now, 13 has become the pupil of the master . . . and in turn, the protégé takes up the legacy as it has been passed down to him . . .
On August 2nd . . . Metropol, California plays host to the BASE's biggest event in their history . . . a Pay Per View of enormous proportions . . . August and
Everything After!
On this evening, these three men . . . "Black Irish" Tom Morgan . . . "Pure
Pleasure" Paully Dean . . . and 13 will gather for a titanic Cruiserweight battle that had to happen . . . a Triple Threat Match that can only lead to the crowning of the first BASE Cruiserweight Champion . . .
From the opening bell to the end of the match . . . only one of these three men will stand victorious . . .
In Paully Dean and Tom Morgan, you have two of the most talented and toughest men in all of professional wrestling . . . two men possessed with the speed, power and wrestling knowhow to stand heads and shoulders above the competition
. . . Both men who will always be remembered as "Men Who Can". What they have in talent and wrestling ability gets lost in their desire to excel . . . to be the best they possibly can . . .
Tom Morgan lacks the desire to be at the top of his game, foregoing his time in the gym in order to pursue his other passion. Rather than putting in the long hours necessary, he prefers to show up at his matches and allow the "Winds of
Fate" to blow as they may . . . and often, "Black Irish" drunken and bludgeoned, finds himself on the losing end of his battles . . .
Paully Dean . . . perhaps one of the most gifted athletes to step inside of the squared circle, likewise finds himself following his other pursuits . . .
dedicated to the pursuit of the "good life", Mr. Dean with his fast cars . . . fast women . . . and fast lifestyle . . . finds himself look up in the ranks at men he considers inferior . . . as well as on the losing end of important matches . . .
Both of you . . . "Men Who Can" . . . And facing you on Sunday, a "Man Who Will" . . . 13, with immense talents of his own added to that the knowledge passed down from mentor to protégé . . .
this superb athlete in his own right has dedicated his life to become the best
Cruiserweight he possibly can . . . always training . . . always studying . . .
he will become the second man, following in the footsteps of Blackjack Kevin
Brody to have been trained by this man (again she reaches up and taps the forearm of the shirtless fellow.) in Japan and to become a multiple champion.
From teacher to pupil . . . from mentor to protégé . . . it has been handed down . . . on August 2nd . . . in the Ironspire Pavilion . . . only one man will stand victorious . . . and it WILL be 13 . . .
(a smile crosses the lips of Jade as the camera pulls back. With an accusing finger pointed directly at the camera, the shirtless man speaks.)
<the man> Paully Dean . . . Tom Morgan . . . August 2nd, August and Everything
After! . . . be there . . . if you dare!
(the camera zooms out as the shirtless man laughs before we fade to black.)
