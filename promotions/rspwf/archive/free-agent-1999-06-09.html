Critique this if you want to, also. =) This is my GSWP character, Eddie Rue. [The camera focuses. We see a dark, desolate place. Suddenly, a large sound is heard. The camera gets closer. Seen is an oil burner. And then some pipes.
Along the pipes, many mice and rats sprawl. Just underneath, the camera goes. Noticing a darkened figure. It's only outline, a shiny jacket covering it. As the figure shivers.] ??- "Evil-eye-of-death-and-despair watches you closely, Lawrence Williams. Each night, you pull your cartoon covers over you, and tuck yourself in at night. But just before you do that. Your bound to check under the bed, in search of monsters lurking down below. Waiting to pull you underneath. You dare not check the closet for monsters. Or the ones from under the bed will grab you by the ankles, and bring you under to the world-of-no-return."
[The man laughs benevolently. All of a sudden, a flashlight goes on. The man is holding a flashlight. His face is now bright. And it's clear he has a hat on. On further inspection, it's verified that the man is "Heartless" Eddie
Rue. A slight sign of a beard, as usual, is seen.]
ER- "Where is the boogie man tonight, Lawrence? What nightmares will he spurn your mind with? The head games he plays with you. And although these games are not as fun as the ones you play with your friends, you have no choice but to play with him. For the boogie man is in your head. You can't run from your own mind. It's the one thing that never escapes your presence.
"It's there. There, that I will have the same fun playing with you, Lawrence. The same fun he gets from those nightmares you experience at night, asleep. I will revel in as well."
[A roaring laughter is heard, As Eddie rolls his head back. The flashlight shining on his chin, mouth, and throat. He reasserts himself, looking dead-forward once more. He removes his grin, and puts on a new face.
Dead-serious, he talks some more.] ER- "Do not fear the monsters, Lawrence. For they are not as real as the pain I will bind your nerves with. Power sinking you of your life. The very essence that makes you. Lawrence, I am Eddie Rue. You know me. I am
*your* soul reaver. "I don't need to explain my ways. The paranormal cannot even define me. I'm yet another mystery for you to play with. Your mind to boggle with. And your soul to get truly exasperated with."
[Eddie moves the flashlight over the left, leaving his face once more in darkness. The camera follows the light. Seen are etchings, paintings of Rue's past opponents on a wall. He scrolls the light from top left to bottom right.
Mike Wilson. Bradley. Fang. Parker. Glamour. Fang once more. AND THEN,
Williams. Each person has a thick red 'x' painted over their face.
Each-except for Williams.] ER- "You wanted to fight me Williams. Now, you have to suffer the consequences. It begins from now until Summer Showdown. I will put your mind, body, and soul through the *same* torment I've been through since I'd stepped into this world. You will learn to feel my pain. And if you cooperate? I just might spare you your soul ... but rest assured, your heart will belong to me."
[Eddie brings the flashlight a little more towards him, and shines it on a rat. He puts his forefinger and thumb around the rats neck and picks it up. Then placing it on his opposite arm, letting it crawl up to his shoulder. As Rue brings the light back to his face once more. He guffaws, showing his gangly teeth.]
ER- "Now. After all this said, Williams. Do you really understand what you've gotten yourself into? I am a cold-blooded killer in a warm-blooded persons body. I resemble the everyday man. Can you tell the difference? You can't. You know why Lawrence? I don't think you train for matches the same as me. You might think hitting the gym is going to save you this Sunday ... "
[Eddie laughs sadistically, then gives a crooked smile.] ER- "But what you really need to do is hit the books. The only way to catch a killer is to get in the killers' head. But assured, Lawrence. I am so far into your head now, we share the same mind. So I spared you the time of getting into mine, when I entered yours. We're now alike, Lawrence. We're almost in all irony, friends. Because friends like to play together. And we shall play. Let your mind represent the toy. Except I forgot to tell you-
"I don't play nice."
[Eddie begins to consume his mind, his thinking, as he breathes heavily, not aware that his eyes are dilated. But as he stares, he pierces straight through the camera man, as if he did not notice he was there.]
ER- "I don't play nice and I like to play rough. And rough I will be on your mind. As I break it down into sectors and vectors. Analyze this?
I will do more than just that. And eventually I will break it down into nothing. Sweep your mind. Paralyzing it. And when I achieve this. Your body's motions will disappear, Lawrence. I will then be free to carve out your heart. And plaster on my trophy case ... "
[Rue flashes the light over against the wall with the paintings.] ER- "My trophy case-the wall of infamy." [Rue brings the flashlight back over to him for the last time, dropping a final line.] ER- "I will ... kill them all — and let god sort them out later. "The next person to enter god's graveyard is you, Williams." [Cynical laughter is heard, although we don't see Rue's jaw move. The camera fades, and then blackens.]
