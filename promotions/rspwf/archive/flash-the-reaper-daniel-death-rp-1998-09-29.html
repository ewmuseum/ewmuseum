[Flash] "The Reaper" Daniel Death [RP]

29 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a rather good rp by myself. I have done better, but read this one and falme me is you wish, if not please tell me what you think. The end gets a little bad.
[...Fades in...] [^v^v^]...A few times in each mans life, a chance comes along. A good chance. A chance to gain what they have worked for, and what they have wanted. So few times does a chance such as this come along. So what is a man to do? Take it, of course. But what if there is a very large task involved? Such as brining down an entire building with your bare hands. Or picking up a two ton rock.
These tasks seem impossible. But it is such a chance, that no man can let up. They will try. Try not knowing that if they had sat back, and thought about what they where going to do, they would realize, that within themselves, they have the power to do the task that seems so impossible. God has created man, with the capability to do anything. The task that stands before me, is much like these other men's tasks. It seems almost impossible. For the men I must face, to gain what I want so dearly, will be all against me. For what I have done, is created hate between me, and the men in witch stand before me. But with the added power, in witch I posses, perhaps this task, isn't so impossible at all...[^v^v^]
[...The camera comes on, and it is a nice, clear day. You see that you are in the woods. For trees and plants hang about. As the camera pans to the right a bit you see an old broken down house. It has turned yellow with rot, and the roof is caving in. Bricks lay about the outside of the house. Bricks that once was a chimney. Overgrown weeds seem to reach up and over take the house.
Small mice, and large rats run about, as snakes and other such animals come in and out of this house. The windows are shattered, and are covered in thick spider web. The door barely hanging off the hinges. Right above the door, there is what appears to be a large crows nest with little babies in it. As the camera gets closer, you see that, that is exactly what it is. Suddenly the camera man is startled and jumps back. As a large man walks out from inside the old house....]
[...The man is very tall. He has arms and legs like tree trunks. Red wood tree trunks. He is wearing all black, and a leather jacket. He has shoulder length black hair, and small goatee. He stops at the sight of the camera, and just stares at it. You realize that this giant man, is The Reaper, Daniel
Death. He slightly shakes his head, and with a deep breath, begins to speak....]
"The Reaper" Daniel Death: "Another chance has come my way. One I have had, numerous times before. One time I walked away victorious, the second time I did not. And in these two match, both where classic battles. Probably, my match with Jimmy Davidson was the best this sport has ever seen. My one with X-man.... Well, that was good too. Now the chance rolls along again. Yet this time it is not to much the same. The man known as Reno is now Deatherv. And the Armageddon I once knew, is a much different man, who seems to speak his mind much more. Now I have seemed to underestimate Deatherv a little to much. Good thing we are not to far down the road, to the point where I cannot correct myself. Deatherv, I love the effort you are starting to put forth. You all know I look forward to classic battles, this being the one thing that has not changed. Now you have done many great things in your career. But one of them, has _NOT_ been beating me. And I feel you are to young and inexperienced to have the ability to handle such a great task. Especially if by beating me you hold a title, in witch only legends can hold. One day you will be up to par. I see this from deep within you. But not now, it is much to soon. Now I have listened to you twice now, and it seems as though you are trying to follow in Knightmares foot steps. Not a bad choice. He indeed is one of the greatest ever. Not a man can ever take his place, but we give you credit for trying. And give you credit to have the guts, to take such a chance. For it is something not many men will try. The first time I heard you speak, you handed us a great metaphor. I am not sure how many people caught on to that, but I did. Let me explain something to you. I have a far greater power than the turtle, the cat, and the hawk. And/or with ever other animal you may have spoken about. My power goes far beyond the power of any animal, or any human for that matter.
You see Deatherv, unlike most men, I have knowledge in witch no man has ever even dreamed of. This you shall see when we go face to face for the NcWa heavy weight title."
[...Daniel Death pauses...] "Then you come out a second time, and once again you start to speak your mind. This time you finished your story, claiming you had been the kitten, and leaving me and Armageddon to be the hawk. The lost and forgotten hawk. But you must realize something Deatherv, the lost and forgotten are the ones who always return to haunt you, with a new look, and a new attitude, lowering your sense of knowing what is what, and who is who. What you have done, is fallen into a trap. Like your story states, the greater power, in this story was the
Hawk, was the one who got the worst of it, but what you did not state was the
Hawks great return. This is where my story begins. Your story, to those who do not see it, was not a story. Nothing but a mere image of the truth. In witch was my career. How I was lost, lost after being the greater power at one time. And now I have come back, all you expecting me to be the same person I was, but you are only to see that this is not true and the man you expect, is the last man you shall receive. And last on you. How you tell us this ends your long 10,000 mile road. But what you have not seen, but has been there from the start, Deatherv, this road of yours in nothing more than a.... dead end."
[...Death flips his hair back and takes a deep breath. He then steps off the stares onto the cold dark ground. He then returns his face to the camera...]
"Then of course there is Armageddon. What a powerful man indeed. Nearly as powerful as I. Such a mythical person, he is. Everyday of this mans life, something new, something unusual. But nothing ever happened between the two of us directly, although you have caused something to happen, in witch had taken me out of the NcWa in the first place. You for one had helped destroy what was, the greatest stable ever. Death Row. This in itself is a horrible sin you have committed, and one you shall dearly pay for. But not only this. You stand before me, trying to take what I continually work hard for, and that is
_MY_ world title. Now many time throughout your great speak, you mentioned little things that you know anger me. But let me ask you, is this what you want to do? Greatening my already unexplainable anger? The anger that has drove me to more titles, than you have had title matches? Perhaps you are not as smart as you may seem. For I have expected much, much more to come from you. But instead, you come out with a Fury attitude, and talk much on my departures. Well, if this is the way you see these things, than so be it. For
I had my reasons, and as I recall, I returned and beat Fury, yet one more time.
And I feel now, it is time to put you in your place, and I feel it is time to finally reclaim my world title. And you, much like Deatherv expect a different
Reaper. You expect the old Reaper. But I warn you, this is _NOT_ the man you will see step trough those ropes. What you will see step trough those ropes, is a death you cannot excape from, and a man you will fear for the rest of humanity. And what you will see leave, is perhaps the next greatest wrestler there shall ever be, holding one of the most coveted titles of all time. Heed my words. It will be the greatest help to you, in your already bumpy road, to what you wish only to have. But be ready, I will be waiting there, when you arrive!"
[...A light breeze pick up, with it carrying many colorful leaves. They swirl about, and end up covering up Daniel Death. As they slowly fall to the ground, you see that the Reaper is gone. With a low crack, the small house falls in, and breaks away. You are left with a small light cloud of dust...]
[...Fades out...] [^v^v^]...Chances come and go, and go and come. Again I have the chance of a lifetime. And I plan not to let it slip by. You have heard my words, and we have heard the words of others. We are left up to the wrestling match itself.
It comes down to, who wants it more. And that it left up to time, and fait. Either way, this _WILL_ be one for the ages...[^v^v^]
