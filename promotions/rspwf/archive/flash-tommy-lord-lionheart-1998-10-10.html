{Flash} Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart!

10 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tell me what you think of this roleplay which I done for the ICW... =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- {The scene opens in the Florida mall, where Tommy Lionheart is surrounded by security as he is signing autographs. There is a que waiting to get his autograph and photograph of about 100 people. The camera man pushes through the crowd and goes behind the table behind Lionheart, he looks at the camera}
{Tommy '''Lord'' Lionheart}
Hey, what you doing here, it's my day off... {Another fan about 7 years old comes along wearing a blue cap and a Tommy
Lionheart ''Worship the Lionheart'' ICW t-shirt. He gives Lionheart his autograph with a pen.}
{Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart} Hey, what's your name? {Boy} My names Steven.. {Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart} Hello Steven, do you watch the ICW much on t.v? {Steven} Yeah all the time, especially when your on it Mr.Lionheart!! {Tommy 'Lord'' Lionheart, laughing} That's good, here you go... {He does his signature on the autograph book. Then his parents with a camera look} {His Parents} Steven, smile.... {Lionheart and Steven shake hands as his dad takes a picture. Lionheart then gets up as a spokesperson says that Tommy Lionheart must go as the crowd looks saddened and shockfed. He waves goodbye and then goes out the fire exit, the camera man follows. Lionheart shakes the manager of the Florida mall's hand and then walks to the car park with the camera man as a few fans go up to him as he just walks past them into his red Ferrari. The camera man gets in the passenger's seat}
{Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart} Wanna go for a pizza? {Camera Man} Sure... {Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart} Turn that off, til we get there.... {The camera man turns off the camera............................. .............................The camera re-opens in the Pizza Hut restaurant as
Tommy Lionheart and the camera man are sitting opposite each other}
{Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart} So...Today I'm gonna talk about a guy who had a lot to say about me the other day on the LowDown going by the name of 'Extreme One' Tommy Grayson. Now, you started off saying that you have seen my reputation and you said that you have seen that I am pretty good...OF COURSE I'M GOOD!! I have had eight fights so far in the ICW, I have won 6, drawn 1 and lost 1, now that's good if you ask me, wouldn't you say Grayson? Tommy Grayson, there's another thing your right about and that being that I AM one of the best highflyers, and techinicians and brawlers and well, come to think of it, I am one of the best wrestlers there is, so Grayson, you better be pumping some iron in those wimpy looking arms of yours and you better be working on some new moves because I've seen you and well, I think you SUCK!! Tommy Grayson, I don't care how many indy-league titles you have won in your 'EXCELLENT' career because as far as I'm concerned,
I'm gonna be taking out the trash come this Friday in Boston and the only piece of trash in Boston is you...
{A waitress comes over} {Waitress} Are you ready to order? {Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart} Yes, I would like the Chesse and Tomato stuffed crust with pepperoni and I'd like a Coca Cola with that too, ok?
{Waitress} Sure...And you sir? {Camera Man} I'll have the same but I'll have a Sprite instead of a cola... {Waitress} So, it's two cheese and tomato stuffed crust pizza's with pepperoni. One cola and one Sprite?
{Camera Man} Yeah, that's right, thank you.... {The waiter goes off as Lionheart looks at her as she walks back, he then looks back at the camera}
{Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart} Anyway, where was I...Oh yeah, as I was saying....Tommy Grayson was talking a lot of crap recently about how I look pathetic and how I will be taken to the extreme and when I stopped laughing, I thought to myself...What an asshole!!
{Some people wearing suits look round in shock} Pardon my French...Tommy Grayson, you go back to my days as Lord Humongus in my short spell in the ASW and say that you could kick my ass all around the ring, but well....That was then, and this is now...I am no longer a corporal puppet, I don;t have a name that the president wanted me to have, I am Tommy Lionheart and I am better than ever..Tommy Grayson, I don't care about your victory over that jobber Kuba Rico at Shockfest because that will go down as your best victory in your career in the ICW, because not only will I eliminate you from the the Continental title tournament, but I will also eliminate you from the ICW. I don't care whether you're a brawler, hardcore, extreme or a highflyer, after the beating you will suffer this Friday on Fallout, you wont be returning....
{The waitress comes back over with the drinks in her hand. She puts them down and then takes out a piece of paper from her pocket and gives it to Lionhear then walks off smiling. Lionheart looks at it and grins}
{Tommy ''Lord'' Lionheart} Hey Grayson, what did I say huh? I told you I was not only the greatest wrestler but also the sexiest and that proves it, I have to fight them off me, you know how it is right? Oh sorry Grayson, I forgot you've never had a girlfriend before have you, no...Grayson, talk is cheap and all I have to say is come this Friday night from the Fleetcentre in Boston, you will, mark my words WILL be eliminated from the Continental title tournament once and for all as I begin my route towards the top of the tree here in the ICW and as I become the NEW ICW Continental champion. Tommy Grayson, I'm only gonna warn you one more time, if you know what's good for you, you better...
WORSHIP........... ....THE........ LIONHEART!!!! {The camera fades into darkness as Lionheart looks back up the letter the waitress gave to him as he grins}
