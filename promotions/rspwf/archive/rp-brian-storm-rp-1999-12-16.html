<RP> Brian Storm <RP>

16 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's another roleplay. Hopefully this has no more impact and as always advice or even your opinions are welcome.
RPA ===================================== [Fade in. As all slowly comes in from black the camera begins a pan around an empty locker room. Locker doors are either flung open completely or just ajar. It appears as though a few people left in a hurry. <Clink> The sound of a closing locker can be heard just off to the left of the camera. In a few moments the source of the sound is revealed as Brian Storm, who can be seen sitting upon a bench with his black and silver gym bag next to him. Brian is dressed in a pair of blue carpenter jeans, a long sleeve black shirt that has a silver dragon running about both sleeves and a pair of black boots. His hair is still wet as he slowly begins to stand. As he turns about to grab his bag he notices the camera.]
Brian Storm: "Well, well, well... I must admit I didn't think the members of the IWC staff would find me here. No matter though I'm assuming you want a few words from the 'Wild Child'."
[Brian places on foot upon the bench and twists his neck from side to side.] Brian Storm: "Where to begin? Hmm... 'Bad Medicine' Todd Phelps, my one time tag partner and the current Television Champion of the IWC. Ironic isn't it how the man you felt should be flipping burgers at the local
McD's carry your ass in that tag match against the unworthy challenge of
Sean Copeland and Derek Douglas. I bet you thought no one noticed Todd, but I did. I saw the strain in your eyes every time you attempted a move. I saw the lack of confidence in each and every step. It's obvious that you knew you weren't good enough to be in the ring with me. <Brian cracks a smile>
Don't get me wrong Todd ... I understand where that belief comes from. I've seen it before in other leagues before. I mean hell 'Absolute' Aaron Anderson feel to the same fate in the APWI when he faced me in the ring. You should have seen fear in his eyes Todd ... wait you probably have. I can sense that your eyes widen in disbelief when you saw that I was the number one contender to your title. A title that should be mine if it wasn't for your hussy Lita telling how much I turned her on. <A smug grin falls upon Brian's face>
In reality, I can't blame her at all. I mean compare me to you Todd. I'm faster than you. I'm stronger than you. And I've got my talent in my pinky than's in your whole body. Face it mister 'Bad Medicine' you just want to be me 'Wild Child' Brian Storm. Come this Sunday though you have the chance to look me in the eyes from across that ring and decide if you want to just walk to the back and kept that gold strap or lock horns with the 'Wild Child' and watch the pandemonium that's follows. Either way is fine with me Todd, just know that your title reign is only a three count away from being over."
[Brian looks at his watch quickly.] Brian Strom: "I have a few moments more. So now Tommy "Lord" Lionheart. You believe you are the best pound for pound wrestler n the game today
.. I don't think so. You see Tommy the fact of the matter is I _am_ the best pure wrestler in the game today. Do you know why that is Tommy?
It's because I don't focus on the hardcore bull[bleep]. Why you may not be a hardcore icon yourself ... You still do something I don't. You see
Tommy, I don't want to be the climax of sports entertainment. So I'm not going out there to make friends by throwing out a couple of catch phrases, just to hear the roar of the crowd. Do you know why? It's becasue I know I can get the same reaction with my talent alone. So next time Tommy, realize that when you tell everyone to make a little noise for their lord, that's going to be the biggest reaction you'll get all night."
[Brian takes his foot off of the bench and hoists his bag up onto his shoulder.] Brian Storm: "Before I go I have just one thought to you all with. I am a 'Wild Child' meaning I do what I want when I want. So IWC be prepared to learn my name and remember it."
[Fade out.]
