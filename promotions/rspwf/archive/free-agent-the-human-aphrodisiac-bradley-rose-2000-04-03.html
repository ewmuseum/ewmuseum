------------------------- 3 -> Character's Personality Wrestler's Gimmick [A brief description will do] : Bradley Rose personifies the word "arrogant." If you ask him, he'll tell you that he's God himself, and his looks prove it. He believes that he's the epitome of perfection, and that no man stands a chance inside the ring with a perfect specimen, such as himself. He believes that when nobody is around atleast. If someone were to actually begin to give him a solid beating, he'd drop to his knees and plead for mercy without a second thought about it. He's the classic heel character, and although his cocky character would give you the assumption that he's quite the stereotypical blond. But, underneath all of that cockiness is a cold and calculating machine that is always plotting some kind of plan to inflict pain on his opponent's.
History : [What has led your wrestler to where he is today?] (Life Bio) Bradley Rose was born November the 1st, 1978 in Atlanta, Georgia. Born into a family of three sisters and one brother, all older than he, Rose's parents were the typical middle-class people, who work nine to five jobs to support their kids. Bradley often felt that he was the outcast, because of his age, and none of his brothers or sisters would ever want to play with him. He was insanely jealous of his brother's athletic abilities, and his sister's academic skills, which didn't come naturally to Rose as they did his family. He vowed that one day, they would all wish they were him, and set out on a quest to prove that he was the one child who would become the pick of the litter, so to speak. So,
Rose trained long and hard at both athletics and academics, and for the most part, he was doing it fairly.
By the time he got to high school, he began to play sports and make the honor roll and such. Rose became popular quickly, and that's where the trouble started. Bradley began to think that the world revolved around himself, and his friends suddenly didn't want to hang out with him anymore. Rose thought that it was because they were jealous of his looks and talents. So, one day, he told one of his friends just that, and they broke it to him that he wasn't all that he cracked himself up to be, and
Rose went crazy, beating his friend within an inch of his life. He told his friend that he'd prove to him and everyone else that the world couldn't turn without Bradley Rose in it, and so he got into wrestling in school.
His coach saw talent immediatley, however, his attitude worried him, and the coach told Rose to check it at the door, and for the first time in his life, Rose listened to an order. But, the only reason he did that was because he didn't want to blow his chance of proving everyone wrong.
So, Rose became a successful high school wrestler and received numerous scholarship offers from high profile, prestigious colleges, but he turned them down, choosing rather to jump right into the wrestling buisness, where he's at now.
(Wrestling Career) Rose is relativley young when it comes to the wrestling buisness itself, only competing in small organizations, however, he decided that he had the talent to make himself successful in the "big time", and is now looking to move up into the more prestigious organizations around the world.
He started his wrestling career in the WET, where he dominated just about everyone, and was the hands down favorite to become the first WET
Heavyweight Champion, but before the tournament to crown a champion was completed, the fed shut its doors. From there, Rose moved on to the
EMWC, where he competed in the Lightheavyweight division, working with Tracy Hudson and Dylan Rapture, causing problems wherever he went. During EMWC's "slimming of the roster", being a relatively new superstar (And I handled Strictly Business already. :), Rose was cut, and is now parking business in the IICW.
