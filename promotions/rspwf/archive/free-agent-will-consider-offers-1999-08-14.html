[FREE AGENT] Will Consider offers......

14 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWF, Hello, My name is [name withheld]. The handler of the now retired
"Magnificent" Mike Douglas. I am in the process of creating a new character to handle. All I can tell you as of right now is that he will be a pretty big face. If you are interested in employing my new character, Give me an e-mail and I'll see what I can do. I will probably only put him in one fed, Possibly two. But no more than two. Below is a sample roleplay from the retired,
"Magnificent" Mike Douglas. It will just let you know what I can do. I will get back to you on the federation that I choose.
||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||| [Chris] [Handler of:] ["Magnificent" Mike Douglas [retired] [A character to be named later] |||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||||| ========================================================== [The scene opens up to a bar in the hometown of Mike Douglas,Tampa,Florida. A bar that is owned by none other than "Magnificent" Mike Douglas. Before Mike broke into wrestling this is where he worked as a bartender. The bar was called the "Tampa Bay Pub" when Mike worked their. Now it is called the "Douglas
Inn.". The camera opens up inside the bar. A packed house on this night. People are sitting around in tables, Enjoying their drinks. Televisions are all around this bar, These televisions are playing some of tonights MLB action.The camera pans around the bar some more and the walls are decorated with Mike Douglas collectibles. Pictures from him when he won his first title, Lifesize standouts of him and other Mike Douglas collectibles. In the middle of the bar, We seee a large Flouresent sign hanging from the ceiling, It say "Douglas Inn" on it.]
[Finally, We see the man himself Mike Douglas sitting on a bar stool at the bar. Mike is wearing a pair of Baige Cargo pants with a black sleevless shirt.
Mike has his long brown hair slicked back into a pont tail like he usually does. Mike has a tan colored skin and he is very muscular. Bigger than most men. After a few moments a woman bartender comes over to Mike to see what he would like anything. She is wearing a black leather mini skirt along with a white tanktop which says "Douglas Inn." on it. She has long blonde hair which are pulled into pig tails and there is no doubt she is a very sexy woman. After a few seconds they begin to speak......]
FEMALE BARTENDER: Hey, Whats goin on Mikey? DOUGLAS: Nothin much, Just stopping by to see how my little establishment is doing. FEMALE BARTENDER: Would you like a beer? DOUGLAS: On the house? FEMALE BARTENDER: Would you have it any other way? [Both of them laugh. After a few moments she comes back with Mike's beer and they continue.....]
FEMALE BARTENDER: So, Hows life? DOUGLAS: Doesn't get any better. Women, Money, Fame, What else do you want? FEMALE BARTENDER: How is wrestling going? DOUGLAS: I'm Mike Douglas, So of course it's going great. I just recently signed with an organization called HHWO. I've seen the roster and I have to tell you, Their isn't one man on it that I can't beat with ease. Let me just give you a few examples...
FEMALE BARTENDER: Okay. DOUGLAS: First we have "2 Smooth" Setzer Van Strife. Now personally I have never wrestled the guy, But I have heard the name a few times. How "Smooth" can this guy be? Everyone knows I am the smoothest guy in this buisness.
FEMALE BARTENDER: I have to agree. DOUGLAS: Who does he think he is? If he thinks he can compare to Mike Douglas, He is sadly mistaken. As you know I am one of a kind and I cannot be replaced. Let me give you another example..Hmm....How about "Confident" Lawrence Williams? Confident....Hmm.....He's got a catchy little nickname doesn't he. FEMALE BARTENDER: Yes, But it cannot compare to "Magnificent". DOUGLAS: I like the way you think. But lets be serious, If this guy has _One_ match with me his confidence will drop from sky high to lower than a pregnant ant's stomach. I have never faced him but I don't have to in order to know I can defeat him. I'm just that good. I can beat any man in the HHWO any time I want.
FEMALE: I know, I've seen you in action and I must say, I would be lying if I told you I wasn't impressed.
DOUGLAS: You know, I think you deserve a raise. [The woman smiles] FEMALE BARTENDER: Thanks. [She winks at Mike] DOUGLAS: You know, I came to this conclusion a _Long_ ago, But I want to remind you just in case you forgot.
FEMALE BARTENDER: What is it? DOUGLAS: I am without a doubt, The _Best_ looking wrestler in the history of this sport, The _Single_ greatest entertainer in the history of this sport and without a shadow of a doubt, The _Single_ greatest wrestler in the history of this sport.
FEMALE BARTENDER: Your right. DOUGLAS: I can't help being great, Thats the way I am. Everything just comes naturally to me, And I mean _EVERYTHING_.
FEMALE BARTENDER: OOOhhhh, Sounds interesting. Maybe sometime I'll find out. DOUGLAS: You never know. FEMALE BARTENDER: So Mike, Do you ever think you want to hop in back of this bar like the old days?
DOUGLAS: For a living? FEMALE BARTENDER: Yeah. DOUGLAS: And leave the wrestling world Mike Douglas free? I just can't do that to all my Mike Douglas admirers. I still like to hop in back of the old bar here every know and then for a few laughs but thats about it. The wrestling world needs me. Without me the wrestling world will crumble. It will get so bad they will have to give tickets. I _Made_ the sport what it is today, I built the foundation for this sport. Without me there is _NOTHING_.
FEMALE BARTENDER: I understand. Hey, I was talking to Jim before and he was looking for you.
DOUGLAS: Really? Where is he right now? FEMALE BARTENDER: He's over by the door standing guard, Like he always is. DOUGLAS: Alright, I'm gonna go see whats up, Talk to ya later Babe. [Mike gets up out of his bar stool and just as he is about to walk away, The female Bartender grabs him by the shirt and pulls him towards her. She pulls out a small piece of paper and sticks it in Mike's pocket...]
FEMALE BARTENDER: [She smiles] Call me. [She lets go of Mike and Mike walks over to the entrance of the bar. When he gets their he finds the Bouncer of the bar at the door. The bouncer, Who's name is Jim is wearing a pair of Black baggy jeans with a blue shirt that has
"Douglas Inn." printed on the corner of it in white letters. The man is very muscular, He is slightly smaller than Mike though. After a few minutes they speak.....]
DOUGLAS: Jim!!! JIM: Mike!!! [They both shake hands] JIM: Whats goin on? DOUGLAS: Nothin much, I heard you were looking for me. JIM: Yeah, Just wanted to see how things were. DOUGLAS: Hey, I'm Mike Douglas, You tell me how things are? JIM: [laughing] I Know, When your Mike Douglas things don't get any better, Right? [both laugh] DOUGLAS: You know what? Your right. JIM: Hey, I heard the news, HHWO huh? DOUGLAS: Yeah, They have a few so-called "Superstars" so I figures I go down and dominate the HHWO. Like always, You know, Show em who the _Real_ Superstar is.
JIM: Thats a good idea. Got your eye on anyone yet? DOUGLAS: Are you kidding me? JIM: What? DOUGLAS: I don't have my eye on them, All eyes are on me. I'm the man to beat, Everyone knows that. JIM: I know, What was I thinking? DOUGLAS: It's okay, We all make mistakes. Well, Everyone but me of course. JIM: Yeah, So when is your _Big_ debut? DOUGLAS: I don't know, This president Wayne Michaels, Obviously doesn't know where his money is at. The first HHWO card is July 14th and I'm not booked on it.
JIM: [Suprized] Are you kidding me? DOUGLAS: Thats what I said. Everyone knows your money is with Mike Douglas. Without me on the program, The show will rate no higher than a golf tournament. This guy doesn't how to handle buisness. So I guess I'll make my in ring debut next week. And as always I'll dominate my opponent in my first match. What else would you expect?
JIM: You have to be the greatest wrestler I've ever seen hands down. DOUGLAS: I think I've discovered I new talent. JIM: What? DOUGLAS: I actually read your mind. I knew you were going to say I was the best wrestler......Everyone says that.
[They both laugh] DOUGLAS: Alright Jim, I've got to head out. I have a lot of things to see and people to do.
JIM: Isn't it the other way around? DOUGLAS: Maybe for the common man. [They laugh] DOUGLAS: But I am going to leave you with these words before I go. JIM: YES!!! I love it when you say this!! DOUGLAS: You can think your Good, You can think your Great, But.......You will never be.....
MAGNIFICENT!!!! [Mike walks out of the entrance]
