FREE AGENT!!!!

31 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
E-mail me if you want me in your fed...i don't come here often. My character is the Blackknight. I also have another character named Necrologia that i just started....
Sample Roleplay (A man is seen walking on the streets. He wears raggy, torn up black jeans, and a moldy, stale looking cape/cloak/trenchcoat, which is colored black. His hair is all tangled and matted, and he looks as if he doesn't smell too clean, as whenever he walks by someone, he is greeted by that someone backing away with a plugged nose. Someone runs up behind him and rips off the mans coat, and the man runs after him. The thief takes off into a building, but the stranger is very close behind. As minutes pass, spraying is heard. As the stranger walks calmly out of the building, the camera peeks into thge building and sees the thief, beat up, and above him on the wall, spray painted, but dripping, colored red, the word NECROLOGIA.)
<-----A flash is shown, and the camera comes back on at the middle of town-----> (Another, different, but familiar looking man is walking the streets. He has on quality clothes, but walks with a limp and a cane. He comes up to a newspaper stand, and takes a newspaper to the cashier. As he takes his wallet out to pay the clerk, a man runs up and takes it. The familiar man runs after the thief, and catches up with him on the sidewalk, and takes his wallet back. He also uses a spray can, and takes the thief into an alley. He takes the spray can, and sprays IS THE in red on the thiefs jacket.)
<----- Once more, a flash of light, and we're back to the stranger-----> (Again we are looking at following the stranger, but now his cloak is gone, and we see the back of the mans shirt is ripped to shredds. Obviously this guy has not had a good life, but thats not the point. He suddenly bumps into a man, who we know as the familiar man. They shake hands, but then turn towards the camera. The familiar man speaks first.
Familiar Man: I see you have already met my friend. For weeks I have told you
THE CURSE IS AMONG US. Now is the time to reveal that curse. I am in disguise, because most of you know me only when I am in my black bodysuit with green trim, yes, I am the one known throughout the DHW as Blackknight. And my friend here will cause havoc throughout the DHW, along my side, as we are known as the team called Goth. Not Gothic, nor Goth Warriors, just Goth. And my friend, now teammate, has some words to say.
Stranger: I am known as Necrologia. I shall be a doomsday reckoning for many of the fighters here in the DHW. Along with Blackknight, the Keeper, and the soon to be World Champion Facing Death, and of course Amber Fine, we are the Dark
Ones, and we shall rule all!!!!!!!!!
BK: Night, this week is the week you get to witness true education as you the student, I the teacher. I shall teach you what pain is, and what the word of loss means. you think yourself scary? You think yourself evil? Well, look across the ring on Sunday and you will see that you were wrong, you were not evil, the true heart of evil lies within Blackknight.
Necro: And Night, it seems your friend Brimstone is injured for a while, so I guess just to make things fair, we'll injure you too, so you don't have to go through that tag team thing all by yourself. We'll take your place. I hope you rest easy!!!!
(As the team known as Goth walks off, the camera spots a word on the ground, and as the screen fades, the camera moves in on the word CURSE.)
So what do you think?
