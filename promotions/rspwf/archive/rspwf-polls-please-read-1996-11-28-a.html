The RSPWF Polls (Please read)

28 November 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Why does everyone think the RSPWF polls are strictly for the "Loops" or whatever you guys call them? It's not. I run Atlantic Coast E-Wrestling, ACEW, and I'm proud to be in a poll with some of the best organizations in the world. If a league could personally endorse another league - it would be one of the leagues in the top three: EWA, CWF, or PCW. These leagues have the best writers, best bookers, etc around. Unlike another league on here, which tells people it is booked when it is infact simulated, and accusses the ACEW Booking Commitee of copying wrestlers, we respect and are good friends with some of the people who run the top e-feds. I'm going to be honest with you guys. Lots of people don't like my league, ACEW (Atlantic Coast E-Wrestling). Right now - we're on a search for a few good faces ..so if you'd like to join, email [detail withheld] for a sample card. If you don't think our writing is the best in the world, then check out that CHAOS! card with The Great Ceasar versus Tsunami!! I wrote that match and it was done very nicely. We might not have Aegis, or Super Scott, or Extremist, or DTP - but we have some of the best e-wrestlers on the planet in ACEW. We've got everything from brawlers like Hell's Angels to high-flying Luchadors like White Lightning!! Plus, we're in the best alliance an e-wrestling league could be in - LAXA, with NICW, etc. Before you go and make fun of us, give ACEW try. So what if we have Sabin Figaro?? So what if we're not EXACTLY LIKE the EWA, we're not supposed to be. So what if we don't have a legion of people behind us, we have a legion of people who make fun of us. Just give ACEW a try - we're ACEW and we take e-wrestling to the EXTREME!! Check out our homepage at http://www.op.net/~tservo/acew.htm or e-mail [detail withheld] . We can send you a sample card ASAP. (BTW, ACEW is a booked e-fed which goes by roleplays)
