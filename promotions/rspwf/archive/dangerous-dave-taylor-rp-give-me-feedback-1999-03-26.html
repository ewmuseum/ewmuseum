"Dangerous" Dave Taylor RP: Give me feedback

26 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Very Few walk with me. I choose to do certain things. So many of you criticize my actions but yet you claim to be my friend. How is this so? If you are a true friend...well..
LIFE STORY Saturday, July 10th, 1985....the man you now know as D.D.T. was but 10 years old. Confined to being in his 20 room mansion because of his parents over-affection, Dave was extremely unhappy with life. The high-class ballroom parties, the private schools, the million dollar house just wasn't his idea of fun. His parents kept him from doing anything and everything....Dave was left with one thing to do all day long: Watch Wrestling. He watched it, studied it, he loved it. It was fun...it was his life, untill that one night. The night he'll never forget for the rest of his life..the night he calls "The
Unthinkable". His dad was drinking, and his mom had just gotten back from playing B.I.N.G.O with her upper-class friends. His dad walked into young 10 year old Dave's room to bring him a present for no aparent reason, a new top of the art technology computer. When he walked in, what he saw enfuriated him.
After telling the kid a million times, he was STILL watching wrestling. That sport which he had so many times told Dave was "brutal, and neanderthal-esque".
He threw the new computer right into the television set, breaking them both. He smacked his son in the mouth. The man now known as D.D.T. stood there, with an un-earthly look upon his face. He kicked his dad in the growin, and then hooked his head and DDTed him right there. His dad eventually got up with a mad look upon his face, and walked out. That night, D.D.T. did "the unthinkable": He set the million dollar house on fire, along with his parents. And he ran, and ran...he caught a cab and had him drive from Cambridge Mass to East CuttyHunk then into the bronx of New York. He was begginingto have second thoughts about his decision when he saw the crack heads, bums, and drug dealers walking ther streets. He always saw movies with The Bronx in it, he thought it was just a way to make it seem like a bad place. In real life, it was just as bad, if not worse then what it was on TV. It was dark, and he was looking for a place to sleep. He walked down a dark alley. Out of nowhere stepped out 5 thugs, all wearing red bandanas. They told him he was walking on "their turf". Then they noticed his shoes....top of the art, high class Jordans. The thugs pulled out knives. D.D.T. was frightened, but he held his ground. Then out of nowhere came a huge boy, about the age of himself, with a barbed wire-baseball bat.
Together, they fought off the thugs. The boy introduced himself...he is the man you now know as dR. DeaTH. From then on, they were inseperable. They got money any way they could.....wrestling was one of those ways. One day, when they were working for a New York federation known as HWA, they were approached by a man they knew well. His name was Mikey Levvy....the famous "Show Stoppa" of CWA fame. He told them he had a contract waiting for them to sign. He saw talent.
Together, along with Phantom and The Angel of Extreme, they formed the first ever Dangerous Alliance. They made rivals with EVERYONE they met. "Dangerous"
Dave Taylor then settled down in XBWL, a federation marketed out of Lousiveville, Kentucky,most members define the words cocky, conceited, asshole, and prick. Alot of new wrestlers to this new wrestling word traveled into federations, the Dangerous Alliance ruled them all.....EwA, XowA, NDW, and GWF, yet they still claimed that they were the greatest. TAOE quit for personal reasons, and D.D.T. traveled to Nepal to continue his wrestling studies and meet with a new wrestler named Avalanche. DeATH was in NDW and IWF and KAWF destroying men, winning World Titles and making The DaNGeROuS aLLiaNCe and
D.D.T. famous. DeATH pleaded with D.D.T. to join him so they could re-start DA after it's glory days in GWF. They went to IWF alongside John Blake, Tony
Morris, Tyrone Glenn, Shawn Christopher, Slick Style, BuLLeT, Malik Mustafa and
Tommy Shades. They called themselves Soulz On Ice. One problem, there was no dR. DeATH. This angered him and led to a series of arguements amongst him and
The DaNGeROuS ONE. Then D.D.T. got DeATH in because after 5 defenses Malik and
Tyrone saw exceptional talent in dR. DeATH. Sadly, IWF closed down.
But now, they are brought together once again, along with NO ONE to face their life long enemies. Only this time, the NAWF, WWA, XBWL, and THE REST of The
Wrestling Word have played right into "Dangerous" Dave Taylor's and ???????'s plans. While D.D.T. is a part of the sensation that will take over XBWL, The
WWFW, he still has his own schedule to follow. In Japan, D.D.T. mastered the extreme style of wrestling. ???????? taught himself the ways of being
SUiCiDaL...The XBWL.....is in for a little surprise. SCENE [The Camera opens up to an open field. There are a few scattered trees in the distance. A lake is seen to the left. Birds fly by. Out of no where walks
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor. He is walking down the hill. He has on a wife beater and a pair of tan khakis. They are kind of dirty and they have a small rip on the side. He kicks around some grass and then continues to walk]
["Dangerous" Dave Taylor]: Ya know something XBWL..lately, I've done a lot of thinking. A REAL LOT. These past two weeks or so, I've lost twice. What is going on I ask. I woke up this morning. I stared at myself in the mirror asking myself 'Damn Dave, what the hell is happening?'. First, a loss to DeAndre Coleman, woke me up because frankly that win made him The
Champion. Then the second loss came to Hawk Henshaw, and next Johnny Rebel.
This little losing streaks need to be put to an end. I have no match saturday so I'm thinking..I should make a challenge. Francis Ceaser Bacon Marino

DDT away, if I lose..I say so long to my moniker and no longer run with the acronym D.D.T. and I will change my finisher. I have so much more on the line then you do. So if you still do not accept, it shows you are a coward, a bitch, and someone who isn't worthy enough to be in XBWL. [Sighes] DeAndre
Coleman, it seems as if the fans do not like you as much as you'd hope huh? If
I'm correct, you were ranked around 4-5, and I was #2. I appreciate it but really, the fans opinion on me has no bearing on my actions. If you hate me I will continue to do what I am doing, if you love me I will continue doing what
I am doing. I do not change for anyone. Coleman, you need to look inside yourself. i told you that i would give you a choice. It's not an invitation to join me bit it is an invitation to change. You are a very tough foe but i believe that you are nowhere where i am at because of one thing you lack. You do not understand yet who you are or who we are as a people. You feel that respect is something you give to those who are above or equal to yourself but that is wrong. Respect should be shown to all men regardless of who they are.
When you use say I am unworthy of your title and such you show disrespect to all of XBWL. I believe you when you say you don't mean it in a bigoted manner but when you use it you demean the people that lived before you and that have held that title. This is my offer to you. Look at who you are bro, take a deep look. Ask yourself what it is that you want peeps to see you as.
Everyday you build your legacy and every scratch you place in that legacy will remain until you are able to change. I ask that you do as i ask and look deep inside yourself and make that change.....You have the belt i cherished for around 40 Days. But yet, you think so many men are below you and what gives you that right to say such a thing? You have no bearing on how they appear on

At Wrestle War, it is about more then that title, it is now a personal vendetta, you think you are above me for beating me twice, then so be it, but Wrestle War I will prove you are not above me and I will bring you back to earth and wake you up for the final time.
