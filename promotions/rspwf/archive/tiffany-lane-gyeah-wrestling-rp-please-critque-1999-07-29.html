Tiffany Lane Gyeah Wrestling rp. Please critque.

29 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I was bored out of my mind so I decided to post one of my Gyeah Wrestling roleplays for the wolves of RSPWF to rip to shreads:-) I'm a sensitive soul so please.....be gentle:-P
(The scene opens to a large, crowded, wrestling arena where millions of faithful Gyeah Wrestling fans have gathered to see their favorite stars.
In the center, of the brightly lit arena, sits a large, wrestling ring, where the ring announcer stands at attention. The announcer, a handsome older gentleman in a black tuxedo, waves out to the fans. He carries a microphone in his right hand and the crowd begins to silence as he goes to speak.)
Announcer: Are you guys ready for some... (Suddenly "Miss World" by Hole blares over the speakers, heralding the arrival of "First Lady" Tiffany Lane. The fans are on their feet cheering as Tiffany steps from behind the curtain, flanked by her manager and best friend, Nina Larue. Tiffany wears a floor-length, white robe with a matching sash tied at the waist. The radiant beauty's long, blonde hair falls to the small of her back in a cascade of curls. A small, diamond tiara rests on top of her golden mane. Tiffany's flawless face is twisted in an angry scowl as she stares angrily at the ring. The equally gorgeous Nina is clad in an ultra-short, pink, mini dress that gives a tantalizing view of her long, shapely legs. Nina's long, black hair is tied in a ponytail and her violet eyes blaze with anger. Both women walk towards the ring, ignoring the cheers and whistles from the fans.
The ring announcer looks at each woman nervously as this was not at all a part of the program. A few beads of sweat form on his forehead as the two women enter the ring.)
Ring announcer: What a pleasant surprise! Let's hear it for.... (He doesn't get a chance to finish his sentence as Nina catches him with a swift kick to the crotch followed by a DDT. The raven-haired model's actions get a decidedly mixed reaction from the crowd. She picks up the microphone and tosses it to the fuming Tiffany Lane. Tiffany shoots the announcer a look of utter disdain, as he painfully rolls from the ring, before turning her attention to the fans.)
Tiffany: How in the /hell/ can you start Gyeah Wrestling's chapter two without me!?! (A huge applause from the fans) I am the queen of Gyeah
Wrestling! Tiffany Lane is the woman that helped put the Gyeah women's division on the map! You don't believe me? Just look at all of the publicity that flooded GW after I signed my first contract. We're talking interviews on the Tonight Show, photo shoots with Vogue magazine, and coverage in every major wrestling publication. That idiot
Lusty Lindsey couldn't claim that. The most she could muster was a cheap, layout in some men's magazine that only proved that she's more plastic than a Tonka truck! (she smiles slyly) By the way, welcome back,
Lindsey. I hope to make your life a living hell just like the last time!
(She begins to pace around the ring, the evil scowl returning to her face. Nina lounges seductively against the ropes, playfully flirting with a few of the male fans, in the front row.)
Tiffany: And how does Gyeah thank its queen for blessing them with her glorious presence? I'll tell you how! They try to ignore her and push her aside! How dare they try to ignore the sexiest and most beautiful wrestler on their roster! Not to mention the fact that I wrestle circles around these cheap floozies in the women's division! I look at the first
Backtalks and who do I see? Sandra? Alexandria Zamfir? Philia? Angel!?!
(There are cheers, from the fans after every woman's name is mentioned. Tiffany rolls her eyes n disgust) Give me a friggin' break! I am the only woman that should have had that coverage! These women are all a bunch of pathetic losers who are trying to share in my magnificent spotlight! (her stunning green eyes narrow) I've seen women like you all in every federation that I have ever competed in. Women who could never measure up to me in any category. Women who think that they can become somebody by siphoning off my obvious grace, charms, beauty, and sex appeal. Well, "ladies", and I use that term very loosely, I ain't having it! Tiffany Lane is laying down the ground rules now!
(The blonde vixen poses in the center of the ring, the microphone in her right hand and her left hand placed firmly on her hip.)
Tiffany: There's three main rules so all of you skanks had better listen up! First, I /am/ the Gyeah Wrestling women's division! I am it's monarch, it's queen. When you "ladies" refer to me it will either be as
Queen Tiffany, First Lady, or Miss Lane. Second, I /will/ be the GW women's champion, no ifs, ands, or buts about it. So if any of you thought that you had a chance in hell of taking that title, I suggest that you give up now and just enjoy yur God given role as my inferior!
Last, but certainly not least, do not, and I repeat, do not screw with
Tiffany Lane. Because if you do, you will regret the day that you ever decided to step foot in Gyeah Wrestling. Heed these rules because they won't be repeated and I strongly urge that you follow them to a tee!
(The fans reactions continue to be mixed but the boos definitely outweigh the cheers. Tiffany looks out to the crowd with a superior smirk.) Now the queen is going to give you fans everything that you've ever wanted. Enjoy, fellas!
(With that, Tiffany rips off her robe to reveal a sexy, white, lace,
Victoria's Secret teddy that hugs her sleek, curvaceous body. The teddy has a plunging neckline that offers a glimpse of her ample cleavage. Her diamond tiara glistens in the spotlight and white, spike heels rest on her feet. The "First Lady" is showered in a barrage of catcalls and whistles from the adoring fans.)
Tiffany: The bi....(she smiles slyly).er, witch is back and all hell is about to break loose! Hold on tight because it's going to be a very bumpy ride!
(Tiffany tosses the microphone to the side as "Miss World" begins again. The two beauties high-five each other and leave to catcalls and whistles.) Handler of a bunch of chick wrestlers, including: "Alluring" Arielle Starr (ICW), Tiffany Lane (Gyeah), The Goddesses of
Beauty (JIWWA), Leanna Love and Tawny Blake (IFWF)
