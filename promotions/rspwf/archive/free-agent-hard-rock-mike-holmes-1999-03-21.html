[Free Agent] "Hard Rock" Mike Holmes

21 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm the handler of "Hard Rock" Mike Holmes (formerly Mike "The Boy" Holmes). I'm looking to get my guy some attention, as well as check out some of the e-feds out there, so I'm looking to sign some one match contracts. If you are interested in having Mike Holmes wrestle a match in your fed, drop me a line. Below is his application along with a sample roleplay. I only have three requests:
1) The e-fed must be booked. I don't feel like roleplaying my ass off and getting beat by some jobber. 2) The fed must be an original wrestler e-fed. 3) Roleplays must be sent out through email, because I don't feel like visiting a message board to get all the roleplays. That's pretty much it. Again, if you're interested in having "Hard Rock" Mike Holmes fight a match in your fed, just contact me. ~Joe P.S.- This is an application I used to sign up for High Octane Wrestling, as well as a sample roleplay for there. ----------------------------- Basic Handler Info ----------------------------- Your name: Joe E-mail address: [detail withheld] IRC Nick (if any): [detail withheld] ICQ Number (if any): [detail withheld] Can you write matches? I sure hope so, I'm in charge of the hardcore division. If so, how often? As often as there is a hardcore match. ---------------------------- Wrestler Info ---------------------------- Wrestler's Name: "Hard Rock" Mike Holmes Height: 5'11" Weight: 197 Hometown: Austin, Texas Theme music: '180 Degrees' by NOFX Ring attire (what he wears to and in the ring): Knee length blue jean shorts with a yellow stripe running down the side, and a "Hard Rock
Cafe- Orlando" t-shirt. Give a basic description of your wrestler's attitude or gimmick here including type of wrestler he/she is: Mike Holmes is a hardcore wrestler. He was previously in High Octane Wrestling under the name
Mike "The Boy" Holmes. He took a long hiatus from HOW to go overseas and compete in Japan, where he won a large number of Lightweight titles. In a match against the famous Akura Naguta, Holmes was powerbombed from the top rope through the ring, resulting in a fractured skull and a threatened career. His injury slowly healed, and he worked at the "Hard Rock Cafe" in Orlando, Florida during his off time. Now he is making his return to the ring in his original stomping grounds, High
Octane Wrestling. List ten moves your wrestler uses: 1) Hurricanrana 2) Fallaway Slam 3) Snap mare 4) Guillotine legdrop 5) Body scissors submission 6) Running inverted powerbomb 7) Bow and Arrow submission 8) Somersault neckbreaker (ie Buff's Blockbuster) 9) Crucifix powerbomb 10) Octopus Hold Name of wrestler's finisher: Super Nova Technical description of finisher: Shooting Star DDT (opponent is standing dazed a few feet from the turnbuckle. Holmes jumps off the turnbuckle with a shooting star press and catches his opponents head for a huge DDT)
Wrestler's entrance(exactly how you want it to be shown; be specific to save time for the writers):
(As '180 Degrees' by NOFX begins to blast through the arena, the camera turns to the entranceway. Pyro erupts from the floor and ceiling, meeting in the middle for a fantastic display of brilliant white light.
A loud explosion echoes through the arena, and the pyro immediately disappears. Mike Holmes appears at the entranceway, and makes his way down the aisle, slapping hands with tons of cheering fans. Mike Holmes slides into the ring, and slowly spins around, looking out at the thousands of fans. His wavy brown hair hangs down to his shoulders, resting against his "Hard Rock Cafe- Orlando" t-shirt. The camera moves slowly down his impressive body, first showing his black elbow pads, followed by his jean shorts, black knee pads, and finally his black
Globe sneakers. Pyro erupts from the turnbuckles just as '180 Degrees' comes to an end.) ----------------- Sample Roleplay ----------------- (The scene opens to a shot of a busy road, with cars hurdling by at impressive speeds. The camera slowly pans from the street to the sidewalk, and finally comes to stop on a white building in front of the camera. The camera moves slowly up, and the "NBK Gym- Austin" sign comes into view. The camera moves quietly toward the door, and as the cameraman's arm reaches out to knock, the door swings open, and a giant man stands face to face with the camera.)
Matt Holmes- Come on in. I guess you're here for the press conference. (Matt Holmes moves away from the door, allowing the camera inside. The gym is brightly lit, with three rings positioned throughout the room.
There is a large crowd surrounding one of the rings, and as the camera zooms in, we see a familiar face in the ring. The face is that of Mike
Holmes, formerly one half of the Badass Tag Team Champions, with his brother Matt. Mike is wearing his traditional blue jean shorts with a yellow stripe running down each leg. He also wears a "Hard Rock Cafe-
Orlando" t-shirt. His long brown hair is tied back in a ponytail, and he holds a microphone in his hand.)
"Hard Rock" Mike Holmes- Ladies and gentlemen, thanks for being here today. What I've called all of you here today is to make an announcement that has been long awaited by the wrestling world. As some of you may know, I left HOW to wrestle in Japan, and while there, I suffered a career threatening injury. In a high intensity matchup against Akura Naguta, a well-known wrestler from Japan, I was powerbombed from the top rope, through the ring, and suffered a fractured skull. The doctors told me I'd never wrestle again, and that
I was lucky to even be alive. But I fought it out, and have now signed a contract with High Octane Wrestling, where I made my professional wrestling debut. I'm happy to be here, and am also happy to hear that a
Hardcore Division has been formed. This division should suit my style quite well, because I am willing to do what it takes to win, whether it be take a shot with a chair or take a shot with a barb wire bat. So, now that I've made my announcement, I'm ready for questions.
(Reporters hands fly up into the air, and many begin blurting out their questions hoping to have them answered. Holmes points to one of them, a tall man wearing a dark brown suit.)
Reporter- John Lee, World of Wrestling Magazine. In your absence from professional wrestling, what have you been doing to keep yourself busy?
"Hard Rock" Mike Holmes- To tell you the truth, it's kind of a funny story. My brother Matt is currently wrestling in the GPWF, and independent circuit running in the Austin area. Indy federations don't pay much, so I had to go out and get a job to pay for my medical bills.
Now no one ever accused professional wrestlers of being the smartest people on earth, but when you put down on a job application that your former job was a professional wrestler, you have little hope of getting a decent job. So I ended up working at the Hard Rock Cafe in Orlando,
Florida, while living with my parents in Lake Buena Vista. I guess you could say I was happy with my job, because a lot of times someone would walk in, and recognize me. You wouldn't believe how great it felt to have people still asking for my autograph.
(Mike lowers the microphone, signalling for another question. Again reporters fight for his attention, and this time Mike points to a small woman standing in the front.)
Reporter- Lisa Osbourne, Ring Insider. Have you been keeping up with the happenings in High Octane Wrestling? If so, who has impressed you.
"Hard Rock" Mike Holmes- HOW is full of talented athletes, and everyone has impressed me, but none more than the Cruiserweight Champion,
H-Bomb. Not many men fight Sgt. Nat Turner and survive, let alone fight their way out of the Taps. I've got to give this guy credit for the way he got his shot as well. No whining, no pleading, just fighting. In fact, he fought his way through four other men to get his shot. Another guy that I've had my eye on is the Gentleman. He's come a long way in this sport. When he first entered High Octane Wrestling, he had quite a losing streak racked up, but he's now one of the most talented cruiserweights in our sport. But not only has he done well in the cruiserweight division, but he's a former High Octane Wrestling
Heavyweight Champion, something I'm afraid I can't say about myself. (Holmes lowers the mic, and immediately points out a small man with thick glasses and curly hair.)
Reporter- Leonard Benson, Austin Tribune. What led you to sign a contract with High Octane Wrestling again?
"Hard Rock" Mike Holmes- I've been in this business for a while, and made my debut right here in HOW. This is my home, and this is where I want to stay. Not only does HOW have some of the greatest competition in the sport, but I must admit I'm very happy with the money they offered me to sign with them. After seeing my brother not even get paid half the time, it's nice to see that there is money to be made in wrestling.
(He points to another man, who is wearing a High Octane Wresling shirt.) Reporter- Roman Times, High Octane Wrestling Newsletter. Do you have a statement about [name withheld]' resignation as HOW President?
"Hard Rock" Mike Holmes- Dave was a great guy. He was the one who allowed me to get my feet wet in the industry, and helped me through good times and bad. When me and my brother won the Badass Tag Team straps, Dave was there to congratulate us, and he even took time out of his busy schedule to come to our victory celebration. It's a shame to see him leave, but the show must go on. Dave, if you're watching, great work through the years. HOW will never be the same without you.
(Reporters again begin firing questions at Mike, but he motions for them to quiet down and allow him to speak.)
"Hard Rock" Mike Holmes- As all of you know, HOW is making its grand re-opening on April 1st, so I've got to get to work training. I'm sorry to cut this press conference so short, but I've got to get in shape for my return to the ring.
(Mike Holmes grabs the top rope, and carries himself over, down to the floor. He makes his way through the ocean of reporters, who are still trying to get comments from him. Mike makes his way to a large steel door, and opens it. He walks inside, and closes the door behind him.
The camera slowly fades to black.)
