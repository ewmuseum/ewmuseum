[OT Post - Flash Review] "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao

26 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello Fellow RSPWF'ers, This is [name withheld] and I am here with one of my most recent flashes I had done for the e-wrestling world to enjoy. I am looking for some constructive critiscm (sp?)
please, for I am a rookie, and a rookie must be able to correct what he flaws. =-) Anyways, please, critique and I would be more than happy.. Also, I am a somewhat free agent for _1_ more fed, so if you want to, you can send me your fends FAQ or
Handbook. Thank's you guys. G'Night G'Morning. Sincerley, [name withheld]. ___________________________________________________________________ [Gong ... Gong ... Gong ... ] [These repeated beats continue to be heard, as our scene begins to come into focus. Our scene comes into complete focal point, on the island of Tokushima,
Japan, around the time 6:45 P.M., in front of a large and primitive white Japanese temple. The cool sunset sky blemishes the serene atmosphere of the island, as the mysteriously repeated gong beats continue to sound simultaneously. ]
[Gong ... Gong ... Gong ... ] V/O : [In a very calm and almost eerie voice] The children and families scurry along the island so peacefully, so calmly, without one serious worry. Their only worry, being the fact that they do not miss the seven o' clock mass..
I used to be among these people. Before the "Spirit Warrior" had ever arisen. Before the "Spirit Warrior" was ever brought from the scrolls and inhabited into my soul. I am in peace within my inner self but I only wish my soul was free from worries. But that, that is not what destiny inscribed for he who is known as the "Spirit Warrior."
Watashi.. The "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao.. [This man who appeared from no where, no notified to be the "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao, is attired in a brown taoist cloak. The Hood pulled over his head and his arms & hands, tucked within the cloaks sleeves. Tao, also bare foot with the cloak hanging down to about ankle length.
Tao continues to watch the children and families hurry along, towards the large temple, for evening mass. All of a sudden, Tao himself begins to head that way as he continues to talk.]
JT : My appearence seemed sudden, yet expected. My appoligies for spontaneously appearing like that. But might I say, Konbanwa; to you all.
But now, the reason for me appearing here to you all of the Gunyro Pro . Why am I presenting you with my presence? Why am I here before you all? .. [Tao Nods] My solemn advisory, "Kobe Wrecking Machine" Takaharu Watinabe. The gravest of all competition & a man who is out for the most authentic rationalism ever portrayed. Yes, that is how I shall describe the warriors actions.. Authentic
Rationalism. [Jin Tao then quickly nods again to the camera following Tao as yet again, we hear the ear-piercing..
Gong ... Gong ... Gong ... The chime continues as our speaker, the "Spirit Warrior" Jin Tao enters the white buddhism like temple. Before entering the double doors, Tao bows in respects. As our camera zooms into the inside of the temple, we see cement walls, candles lit, a red carpet leading from the entrence door to the podium, and Japanese Hieroglyphs distempered onto the walls and carpets.
As our camera pans back to vision of Jin Tao, we seem him in front of a row of candles as he suddenly lights one of the unlight candles. As he does so, he lowers his head and begins to speak.]
JT : Now, my friend, my advisory, my opponent, Takaharu Watinabe. Yourself & myself are scheduled to engage in combat tomorrow evening, in the Battlefield.
Mr. Watinabe, our paths have never crossed and our eyes have never met. I have studied you closely Watinabe admiring the day when we should soon meet. And tommorow is the day, the day of reckoning..
My friend, I ponder on the thought on how our eyes will meet and how our "Chi" will meet. I am nothing but respectful for you, I admire you. A man who almost acomplished full preponderance. I "awe" in your appearence.. [Tao smirks.]
[Tao steps away from the row of candles as he takes a planted knee in front of the empty alter. With others in there seats watching and listening from their seats, he continues. ]
JT : My friend Takaharu Watinabe, your actions may not always be vital but you have the heart for supremacy. I can see it deep within you, all that is left is the will to let it out. Sunday night is the night Watinabe, the night were bones will clash and on my part, respectiful battle will take place.
All I ask from you Watinabe, is that you respect what I believe in. What I stand for, and how I operate. My actions shall always be righteous and my ways will always be good, and that will never change. But respect..
This is all I ask from you ... [Jin Tao gets up from his kneeling position as he continues to speak. ] JT : Till the day of reckoning comes before us Mr. Watinabe, you may try to contain me. But to contain, is like trying to kill me. Trying to kill me is like trying to penetrate an unpenetrable force.
Let it be known. For I shall be born. Learn to respect. Learn to forgive. 'Tis' the only way, I was taught to live. [With this, Tao concludes his speech. He pans around and makes his way to the row of people which sit and wait for their mass to begin.
