[Sample Roleplay/UEW/IIeW] From The UEW Television Champion himself :)

8 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in an empty UEW Gardens. The camera pans around to get a shot of all of the empty seats ... and then a shot of the ring, which is set up in the middle of the arena just like when shows take place. Suddenly, you can hear an echo of a few voices in the distance. "The Handler" Liam Rogan and
Freak Show members Kieran Erotikka and Philzy emerge from behind the entrance curtain. They look around at all of the empty seats as the slowly walk down the aisle towards the ring. Philzy and Kieran are dressed in, among other things, bright red T-shirt that have a graphic of Rogan holding the UEW
Television Title with the caption "I'm With The UEW Television Champion. I Bet
YOU'RE Jealous". Rogan himself is dressed in his usual street clothes attire, however. Liam Rogan caresses his UEW Television Title ... which is draped over his right shoulder ... as he walks with Kieran and Philzy.]
Liam Rogan: Guys! This place looks even more beautiful empty than it does filled with fans! I'm overcome with it's beauty!
[Philzy and Kieran look at eachother and sigh.] Kieran Erotikka: Yeah ... yeah it does. Philzy: Why are we here, Mr. Rogan? The place is _empty_. It's Tuesday night. Liam Rogan: To practice, Phil! Philzy: Wha? Practice wrestling? Liam Rogan: No! To practice being a champion! Philzy: [under his breath] Oh dear God. [Kieran nudges Philzy slightly...] Kieran Erotikka: [whispering to Philzy] Humor him! Philzy: [whispering sarcastically] Yes, Mother. I'll humor the insane man. Kieran Erotikka: Why do you need to practice being a champion, hon? You're already a champion ... in UEW and WPCW. There's no need.
[Rogan throws his arms in the air excitedly. Philzy looks on with a somewhat frightened look on his face while Kieran watches Rogan with a worried expression on her face. She lets out a sigh.]
Liam Rogan: Whoa no! There's definitely a need for me to practice, Kieran! I want to be a champion that the brass in UEW want to keep forever and ever! I want to be the perfect champion for that IIeW event that's coming up! Did you hear about it? Did you hear who was on the card? Did you?!
[Philzy lets out a deep breath and addresses Rogan with a sarcastic smile.] Philzy: Yep, we sure did. A little birdie told us--- Liam Rogan: I'll be facing Shimm... I mean Mann ... oh, whatever the hell his name is! I'll be facing him in a two out of three falls match! By some freak of nature, he could win my title away from me! That means I really got to practice, guys!
Philzy: Yes ... practice _wrestling_. Liam Rogan: NO NO!! Practice being a champion!! Look! I've already started! Watch this! [Rogan walks towards the ring...] Philzy: [whispering to Kieran] He'll get better you say. It's only the suprise of winning the Television Title you say. It's only a phase you say!
Here it is over a _two weeks_ now and he's still acting like the Mad Hatter! Kieran Erotikka: [whispering to Philzy] Just humor him, Phil. Would it
_KILL_ you to humor him for a little bit? I've never seen him this happy.
Maybe he likes being like this for a change. Philzy: [whispering to Kieran] He likes being like what? A raving lunatic? Oh yes ... he's happy so let him act like Andy Kaufman on cocaine! Why not just let him beat the hell out of me while you're at it? You have some warped logic!
[Still walking down the aisle, Rogan finally reaches the ring. He stands on the ring steps and calls over to Kieran and Philzy.]
Liam Rogan: Hey! Watch this guys! [Rogan walks up the steps and then climbs into the ring. He walks over to the center of the ring and looks down towards the mat. He reaches into his one jacket pocket and takes out a small remote. He presses a button on the remote.
The PA system of the UEW Gardens kicks on...] PA System: RRROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... RRROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... RRROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... [Rogan stands in the center of the ring. He grunts to himself rather uncharacteristically and lifts the UEW Television Title up in the air ...
grunting yet again. After a second or two, he lowers the belt and looks over at Kieran and Philzy smiling.]
Liam Rogan: So what do you think, guys? Like a _true_ champion who's loved by the brass of his company, eh?
PA System: R-R-ROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... R-R-ROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... R-R-ROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... [Kieran and Philzy look on in disbelief. Kieran is speechless. After listening to the PA system and stuttering to himself for a few seconds, Philzy finally speaks...]
PA System: R-R-ROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... R-R-ROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... R-R-ROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... Philzy: TURN IT OFF!! [Rogan is taken aback by Philzy's sudden outburst. He presses another button on the remote and a distant whir is heard in the Gardens. He puts the remote back in his jacket pocket.]
PA System: R-R-ROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... R-R-ROOOmmmm... [A frown forms on Rogan's face. He looks at Philzy...] Liam Rogan: Why did you make me do that, Phil? [Philzy looks at Rogan with a dumbfounded look on his face ... looks back at
Kieran and then looks at Rogan again. He stutters some more to himself before finally saying something...]
Philzy: What _in the world_ was that??? [Rogan throws the UEW Television Title back over his shoulder smiles proudly at
Philzy and Kieran.]
Liam Rogan: That was my very own piped in chant! I got it made at Goldberg &
Associates down in Georgia! It only took a few days! You like?
[Kieran and Philzy look at eachother with a confused look on their faces. They look back at Rogan.]
Kieran Erotikka: Sure... I guess. Philzy: I'm sorry, Kieran, but I gotta ask him this. There is only so much I can stand. Why in the world do you want a piped in chant? You never cared about cheers before!
[Rogan looks at Philzy ... staring solely into his eyes. There's a hint of anger burning in his eyes. He takes a few deep breaths and it goes away. A playful smile finds its way onto Rogan's face soon afterwards.]
Liam Rogan: I don't care about the cheers ... but the corporate big wigs of
Ultimate E-Wrestling do! They want their champions to sell T-shirts and merchandise. They want their champions to attract a fan base and bring asses into the seats here and wherever the UEW tours! With this, I don't need fans!
I'll have my own homegrown fan base! Fans will hear this and think that they have to love me in order to fit in! The classic peer pressure ploy! It's a brilliantly wonderful plan! It'll keep the UEW Television Title around my waist FOREVER!!
[Philzy walks over to the ring and looks up at Rogan with a concerned glance. Kieran follows him.] Philzy: Umm ... boss ... that's what we've been wanting to talk to you about. Liam Rogan: Save it until later, Phil. I got something else to show you ... something else that'll make the UEW brass want to keep this title in my possession for a loooooong time!
[Philzy looks back at Kieran and shrugs. Meanwhile, Rogan runs over to the other side of the ring, gets on his knees, and reaches down to the outside to retrieve a small duffle bag. He picks it up, stands up, and walks back over to where he was. He unzips the bag and pulls a very large book out. He holds the book so the back is facing Kieran and Philzy's advantage point.]
Philzy: A book? Kieran Erotikka: That's it? Liam Rogan: Not just any ol' book, guys! It's... [Rogan turns the book around so the front cover is facing Kieran and Philzy.] "1,001 Professional Wrestling Catchphrases" by UEW's own "Real Deal" Shawn
Harrison. Philzy: What??? Kieran Erotikka: [mumbling] Well... this takes the cake. Liam Rogan: There's "Oh, you didn't know? Your ass better call somebody!"... Professor Payne's "Class is dismissed" ... Erin McCoys' "Ouch! Stop hitting me!" ... hell, even that relic from your former... Ummm ... significant other,
"Quote the Bruiser nevermore", is in this book, Saman. ... umm... I mean
KIERAN. [Liam smiles at Kieran ... who groans back at Rogan.] Kieran Erotikka: Just get on with it _please_!!! [Rogan opens the book and skims through the contents.] Liam Rogan: Well ... see guys ... every long-running champion has had a catchphrase ... one that's stuck in the minds of wrestling fans everywhere ...
one that's sold millions and millions of T-shirts, bumper stickers, and posters! Maybe if I can find one with this great book's assistance, the big wigs upstairs will do everything they can to make sure that I keep this belt for a long, long time!
Philzy: Man... Mr. Rogan ... whatever.... I wish you'd just see that the suits at UEW have _nothing_ to do with whether you keep the title or not. It all comes down to YOU and if YOU successfully defend it! GOD! I wish you'd just see that!
Kieran Erotikka: PHIL! STOP! [Almost immediately, Kieran tries to stop Philzy from saying anything else but he waves her away angrily. Rogan himself seems to be shocked by Philzy's sudden snapping.]
Philzy: No I won't stop, Kieran! I've kept quiet since the end of last month and I can't take it anymore! I wish you'd realize all of this silliness and this insanity isn't helping you but you know what, Mr. Rogan? You _won't_!
You'll never see any of this because you're too damned delirious about winning that godforsaken title!
[Rogan pauses ... scratches his head ... and looks around the empty UEW Gardens. He lets out a deep sigh which turns into a laugh after a few seconds.] Liam Rogan: Heh heh! You know what, Phil? After that outburst of nonsense, I don't think I will show you my GRAND FINALE! I won't show you what will keep interference out of my IIeW match ... or any other title defense that I have for that matter! You've LOST that privilege by being a damn jackass and spewing out nonsense to the UEW Television Champion! Get out of my house...
[Rogan points to the surrounding UEW Gardens...] ... And go back to the house and _think_ about what you've done, Phil! NOW!! [Rogan looks over to Kieran with an apologetic expression.] I'm sorry Phil had to cost you a glimpse of what could be the biggest suprise
... or interference stopper ... in the history of professional wrestling,
Kieran, but he did. Please escort him out of _my house_. I'll see you back at the house in a little while, Kieran. We can discuss our big "Sunday Night
Heat" party.
[Rogan smiles at Kieran and motions for her to take Philzy away.] Kieran Erotikka: Uhh... [After a hint of resistance, Kieran leads Philzy back down the aisle and they disappear behind the entrance curtain. Rogan looks to see if they are actually gone. Once he sees that they are truly gone, he puts the book down on the mat and takes the remote back out of his jacket pocket. He presses a button and the PA system of the UEW Gardens kicks back on...]
PA System: RRROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... RRROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... RRROOOOOOOOOGGGGGAN.... Liam Rogan: He'll see... Glitz'll see ... they'll all be shocked. They'll all hear the same thing that everyone else will hear at that IIeW event and at UEW events from here to eternity!
[Rogan walks over and stands in the center of the ring. He raises the UEW
Television Title in the air and let's out a _deep_ yell.]
THE WINNER OF THIS MATCH AND _STILL_ UEW TELEVISION CHAMPION OF THE WOOOOOORLD... "THE HANDLER" LIAAAAAAM ROGAN!!! [Rogan proudly continues to hold the UEW Television Title in the air ... letting out a cheerful childish cry of "Yippee!" as we fade to blood red.]
