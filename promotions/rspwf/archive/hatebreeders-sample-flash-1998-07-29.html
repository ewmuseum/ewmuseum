Hatebreeders Sample Flash

29 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A wild surf guitar line is heard as "Pipeline" by Dick Dale (w/Stevie
Ray Vaughn) begins to play. The scene opens to a mall in Syracuse, NY, where hundreds of patrons are enjoying a mid-day meal of pizza, hamburgers,
chicken, or some other poorly cooked, fattening food. Little do they know that their oh-so-eventful lunch is about to be interrupted by the most vile, evil, annoying human beings on the face of the planet...)
(....c'mon, I'm building suspense here.) (...hmmmm...la de dah.) (...I'm not telling you yet.) (...you should know this.) (...didn't your mom tell you you'd go blind doing that?) (...'ey! Pay attention!) (...I'm not budging. You're gonna have to wait.) (...I guess I can tell you now.) (...nah.) (...okay, I will...the...) (...MIMES!!!! Yep, these Crow-lookin' mistakes of God have invaded the mall today, in the vain hope of some applause and adoration. The patrons of this normally calm place recoil in horror at the mere sight of these unholy freaks of nature and their Mime-like antics. Somewhere, a child screams as a white-faced mime finds himself trapped in a box.)
(But luckily, for those of you watching at home, this is not a flash about mimes. In a far corner of the foodcourt, we see a triumverate of terror, a trio of tackiness, a...screw this. It's the Hatebreeders, and they're munching on cold, greasy Sabarro's pizza, all pepperoni and mushrooms, as the mime's have eaten all the good stuff. Asylum is wearing a Donny Walberg (SP?) 'New Kid's on the Block' t-shirt and tight leather pants, his electric blue hair on half his head tied into a Cossack style ponytail. The Jackal (aka Richard Spencer) is in a PimpBot t-shirt and jeans, and Johnny C. is wearing a weird black bondage outfit, with the cast still on his leg. Two crutches are propped up behind him. He looks at the camera in disgust and begins to speak.)
Johnny C.: Well, well, well, well...well, well, well...Pride, oh, Pride, I have to congratulate you on your victory this past Saturday. Not only have you become the belt-less World Champion, which, by the way, shout-outs to Cold Fury for that awesome job, but you have become *THE* single most wanted man in the UCWF. Any friends you
*MIGHT* have had are now enemies. Isn't it grand? (Johnny C. smirks, obviously trying to rip off Kazrick's world famous one, but fails horribly. He then takes a bite of his pizza, grimacing as he swallows a mushroom, foul fungi as it is.)
JC: But don't worry, you aren't at the top of our shi...I mean hit-list just yet. Jackal has entered the TV Battle Royale, and both of them are gunning for the tag gold. Once we capture those, we will begin to expand, thus proving we are the 2nd best tag team in the UCWF. Only then, will we come for your world title, providing you are able to keep it.
(The camera pans over to the Jackal, who is wiping his mouth with the tablecloth, since the Mimes have stolen all the napkins for stupid magic tricks.)
Jackal: Furious Styles, Sands of Time...even the MoC, we are coming to take those tag team belts, and frankly, there isn't a thing to be done. The MoC are a great tag team, and despite the fact they are our idols, we will destroy them if need be. MoC, we respect you both...but even the greatest tag team of all time will meet their end. I look forward to meeting you two in the ring.
(Jackal takes a bite of pizza, sans mushrooms, which are all piled on his styrofoam plate. He chews for about ten seconds before swallowing and taking a long drink of sweet tea.)
Jackal: Prudence Prunehead Pride, you still claim ownership of the Meat Grinder, or the "Pridebreaker", as you call it. I accept your challenge...loser never uses the hold again.
(OOC: Just a note, I did create the move back when I was Pres. of the WIWF. The move was originally created for a "2nd Finisher" for the prototype to Zeromus Sabin, the superdriver. The first wrestler to use it, however, was Rogue, as he had chosen Nuke's proprietory move, the Running Inverted Piledriver, on his creation form, so
I substituted the superdriver. When the UCWF started back up, I gave
Pride's handler permission to use the hold, without thinking I'd already used it w/The Jackal. But I have seniority, as I was the first to send in my UCWF creation form. I will still run this angle though, just because it could wind up being quite interesting. Just wanted to clear this up.)
Jackal: I have confidance in my abilities. Do you, Pride? I would, however, like to add one more stipulation: if I win, I get a world title shot. If I lose, neither myself or Asylum will ever get a
WC shot while you hold that title. Theoretically, we could never get a title shot under these stipulations, provided you held the belt until you retired.
(The Jackal glares towards the camera, which then moves over to Asylum, who is already on his third slice of pepperoni and mushroom pizza. Pizza sauce is all over his face, and a mushroom is stuck to his forehead. As soon as he literally inhales this slice, he belches loudly and goes 'tee-hee' at the camera.)
Asylum: 'ey yo, UCWF, guess what goys and boils? I kicked Piety's arse this Saturday! Yahahahaha!!! See, just to goes to show, y'know, that one match don't mean dookey-crap! I coulda beat him before...I coulda been a contender! But noooooooooo, I got screwed by some stupid rulekeeper guy! Damnit, I'm mad as hell and I ain't gonna take it anymore! WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOA boy, hey, I gots another l'il challenge thingy for youse out deah! Anyone who wants a
Russian Chain match, sign the dotted line, Nancy-Baby! Even Steve Toxakin, even though his career consists of workin' at Mickey D's as the toilet cleaner!!! Yeah! FEEL THE BURN!!! I can just see him now, mopping up all the aftermath of some poor Joe's terlit visit after eatin' a coupla McRibs and onea them there lukewarm milkshakes!!!
MIIIIIIIIIIIIIIIILK! GOT!!!!!!! MILK!!!!!!!!!! YEAH!!!! IT DOES A [BEEP!]in' body good!!! Build strong bones!!! Yeah, it does, it does, don't thank me, just tryin' ta keep the peace from the coppers out there, they's a still lookin' for me after I answered a phone call from Nature with their radio dealie...gave me quite the nasty shock, just like Mr. Howell used ta give me back home at the Oklahoma
State Home for the Mentally Insane! I felt relieved tho'! SKITTLES!
TASTE THE [BEEP!]in' rainbow!!! BITE SIZED CANDY!!!!!! Hey, I saw Andy Little the other day, he was walkin' around his office with nothin' but a shamrock and that thingy I'm seein' right now, oh
God it's comin' right for me and holy crap it's eating my eyes! It's the ballthing from "Phantasm"! Oh, God, it's the Tall Man, arrrrgh, lemme 'lone, lemme 'lone, I don't wanna be eaten by a ball, oh crapola, this sucks, Hey neat, aliens, coooool, please don't give me an anal probe mister alien sir...hey, neat, they's a morphin' into giant dandylions...don't run away dandylions,
I wanna play with yooooooooooooooooooooooooooou!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (Suddenly, Asylum leaps over the table, sending the cameraman to the floor. We get a good look at his eyes: dilated. Seems those were
...ahem, "special" mushrooms. Johnny C. just looks down at his half eaten slice, then throws it to the floor, not wanting to wind up like his friend. The camera does a 180, and we see Asylum prancing around, singing "Sunshine of Your Love" by Eric Clapton, albeit very badly.)
JC: Damnit. He's lost it. Jackal: Again. (A mime comes up from behind, and taps Jackal on the shoulder. He turns slowly around, giving the poor beast a look from heck.)
Jackal: What do you want? (The mime mimes out the action of being trapped in a box. Jackal just sighs.) Jackal: WHAT??? WHAT THE HELL DO YOU WANT??? (The mime continues this charade.) Jackal: Do you want out? Is that what you want? Huh? Do you want out? (The mime nods sadly.) Jackal: Are you sure you want out? (Nods again.) Jackal: Alright, if you really want out... (The Jackal clobbers the mime with a right hook, then executes the MEAT GRINDER on Satan's foul minion. Johnny C. does a spittake all over the table from laughing.)
Jackal: Stupid mime. (The Jackal takes a sip of tea with a bitter look upon his face. The camera pans around again, and Asylum is swinging around on a ceiling fan, screaming GOT MILK! at the top of his lungs.)
Jackal: Sigh...wonder when he's gonna come down. JC: Dunno...considering how many of them he ate, he'll probably be tripping for the next few days.
Jackal: Joy...sad thing is, he's not much better than this normally. JC: True dat. Jackal: Wonder if he'll be able to wrestle. JC: Dunno. Sigh. Stupid mimes. (Jackal sighs and looks towards his partner.) Jackal: Yeah...oh, well. Y'know, I see the Knights of Kazrick have an open contract. Cool. Well, we'd like to be in this match if possible, just out of respect to the KoK. If not, that's cool.
(Turning back to Johnny C.) Jackal: At least he's showing those mime's what for. (We see Asylum executing Shock Therapy after Shock Therapy (Dragon sleeper into a Diamond Cutter) on mime after mime, screaming "BAD
DANDYLIONS!!!!". Jackal can be heard sighing off camera.) Jackal: Man, it's going to be an exciting week. (End transmission.) *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~* [name withheld] ----------------------------- [detail withheld] ----------------------------- *~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*
