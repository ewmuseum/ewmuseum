[HaRdcOrE] This is my first roleplay for the WWA...

14 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<html> <body> This is my first flash for the WWA...I just joined them...seeing as though the
SWL sucks so badly they have a heavyweight champion that has not roleplayed in
4 weeks...but anyways tell me what you think...
<center>------------------------------------------------------------------ -------------------------</center> [The scene opens up to a shot of the Jerry Springer show on a television set...the episode is entitled..."I Love My Sister"...Jerry is standing in the aisle...he has his blue cue cards in his right hand and his microphone in the left hand...he starts to talk]
Jerry Springer: Well ladies and gentlemen...today's show is called "I Love My
Sister"...today's guests are here to tell the world there true love for another member of the family...on stage right now is my first guest...he is Billy Bob
Joe...
[The camera cuts to a shot of a tall fat man with a full grown beard and grundgy looking brown hair...a typical redneck...he starts to talk]
Billy Bob Joe: H-h-h-ello Jerry I reckon that my whole trailor park watches your show every day...
Jerry Springer: Thanks...I guess...Anyways...you are here today to tell the world how you feel about your sister...right?
Billy Bob Joe: Yes Jerry...I love Samantha Susie...Ever since we were little... Jerry Springer: Well on that note...let's hear from our next guest...she is
Samantha Susie...
[A fat blonde haired woman walks out wearing a dress that looks like it came off a pig's ass...the crowd reacts with boos...the lady sits down and Jerry starts to talk]
Jerry Springer: Well Samantha Susie...it seems that you and your...[coughs]...Lover...have been seeing each other for four months now...What do your parents think about this?
Samantha Susie: Well Jerry they don't like the idea of us seeing each other...but that won't stop us...We love each other...It's just that we are related...I mean how many of yous have ver loved someone in your family?
[The crowd starts to boo] Jerry Springer: Well let's hear what momma has to say...here is Momma Mary... [A huge grotesque woman walks out wearing a mumu and has her long brown filthy hair tied back in a ponytail...she sits down as the crowd has mixed reaction...Jerry starts to talk again]
Jerry Springer: Well momma what do you have to say about this whole situation? Momma Mary: Well Jerry...this is the way that things are gonna go on from now on...either they break up...or they move out...because I am nmot gonna have my other 2 children grow up and be like this...I do not need this in my life...
Samantha Susie: Fine then we will move out anyways...besides we can hardly ever have sex with you and grandpa always around...
[The crowd explodes on that one...you can hear ahhh's and boooo's for miles around...then Momma Mary starts to talk]
Momma Mary: _WHAT!_You two have been fooling around in my house...under my roof? Samantha Susie: Not only that...but...on your bed...!!!! [The crowd reacts with so many boo's it is like Russia won the war...then Momma
Mary stands up and grabs her chair and throws it at Samantha Susie and they start to go at it...then Steve and the rest of the security crew start to break it up...they restore order and Jerry starts to talk]
Jerry Springer: Well...so much for Family Ties? [Crowd starts to burst out with the chant of Jerry, Jerry, Jerry] Jerry Springer: Well we will be right back... [The camera starts to fade back away from the television and onto a man that is sitting on a recliner..the room is dark...so all that is seen from this man is his outlined face and body...he starts to talk]
Man: With the years of death all but before us...you would think that we would be able to put aside all the rage and misery...then there was a shining light...a light that showed hope...and I was the man that blew the light out...I am....
[The camera shows that the man lights a candle and now his face is shown...his face has a long scar on his left cheek...he is wearing an old t-shirt and some baggy black jeans...he has his hair slicked back]
Man: I am HaRdcOrE... HaRdcOrE: Well well...it seems that I am finally here in the WWA...huh...it seems like a place that i shall grow and flourish...a place for all of my followers and all of my dignitaries to worship...a place where not only will i inflict the message of 4:20 but i shall inflict the pain that i have had to grow up with all my life...my mother dying of a crack overdose...my father leaving me...being beaten into the gangs...getting shot in the knee...then when all seemed to be going on the up...losing my girlfriend to a rape/homicide...All this pain and rage that I have inside of me will be let out...and it will be let out soon...Now...why am I watching Jerry
Springer??...Well it is a very enlighting talk show with all of the essentials to be a great show on television...NUDITY...HARDCORE
ACTIONS...VULGARITY...Personally I love the fuckin guy...but other than that I am just bored...so if you want some of me then come get some of me...
[The camera goes back to the television as the commercials get over...it shows
Momma Mary and Samantha Susie in another fight as the camer fades to black]
