[Free Agent] "Vagrant" Jeremy Blake

27 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi. My name is [name withheld] and I handle a new character "Vagrant" [name withheld] Blake. Before I go any farther, I must say that I was tutored by [name withheld]. Anyway, here's a character developement roleplay that I have written. If you want Blake in your league, e-mail me at
[detail withheld] . [name withheld] [The camera opens up inside an old abandoned warehouse in Brooklyn, New York. The only source of illumination is sunlight that pierces through the cracks in the boarded-up windows. The camera takes a quick pan around the warehouse, getting shots of archaic crates with cracks in them, rusted barrels and cobwebs from head to toe.
As the camera finishes its panning, it catches a darkened figure in the corner. The camera zooms in, and it reveals the figure to be a man who's just sitting down. The camera zooms in further, and we can see the man's detailed face: shoulder-length black hair, penetrating blue eyes - and a short, stumpy, and black beard.
The man begins to speak, with an eerily deep voice.] JB: Having no fixed course. Having a fleeting, wayward or inconstant quality. Wandering about from place to place, usually with no means of support. [Pause.] JB: Those are the definitions of the word "vagrant." But more importantly, those are the defintions of me.
"Wandering about from place to place, usually with no means of support." That is the definition of my life. Born a bastard child. My mother struggled every day of her life to support me - and she did a damned good job. But the sick organization known as social services didn't think so. Needless to say, they took me away from her.
So they began taking me from house to house, in search of a new family. But none of the families wanted me. I was "sick" and "sadistic." I wasn't [throws up the quotes] "normal." So they kicked me out of the orphanage.
So I went around the streets of Brooklyn, New York, just doing what I had to do to survive. Sure.. I broke into houses to keep warm. Yeah..
I stole from the grocery stores to get food. Thou shalt not steal, but I will to eat. That was the way of my life. I wandered from place to place, in search of food, shelter and clothing. I did it all by myself. I didn't have any support. There was no family. It was strictly me, myself and I.
Vagrant. [Jeremy sniffs.] "Having a fleeting, wayward or inconstant quality." This is the definition of my heart. After living the life that I did, who could blame me for being half-assed? Sometimes I feel like going out there and winning a match; sometimes I feel like going to Burger
King and just resting. Basically, I don't give a damn. This society didn't always keep up its end of the bargain, so why the hell should I? It's pointless. I'm not going to die for this sport when this sport wouldn't die for me. I'm not going to bend over backwards for this sport when this sport wouldn't bend over backwards for me.
Vagrant. [Jeremy sniffs again.] "Having no fixed course." The definition of my in-ring tactics. You don't know what I'm going to do. Sometimes I can sit back and do that fancy-smancy technical stuff;
sometimes I don't wait.. So I go out there and flat-out kick your ass. You can't study me; you don't know me. What you see on film is *not* what you see in the ring. Don't bother studying my film. A *real* wrestler is able to adapt to his environment. I been doin' it all of my life - shouldn't you?
Vagrant. Three definitions. One man. A man who has overcame every barrier that society has thrown in his way. And to him, you're only one more barrier. Professional wrestling, get ready to feel Vagrant's Vendetta.
