[Flash] Arson Embry

24 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fellas... check this out. I thought it rocked. It's not written by me. I came across it somehow, I'm not even sure. I was just checking the mail that I've sent and saw that I had sent this to Steve to read.
After rereading it I thought I'd give you guys an opportunity to take a look. It may not be Macoroni-material, but I think it's stellar. Here goes: [The scene materializes effortlessly] [A gray, overcast sky leads us in. A flash of lighting soon follows, and a light drizzle begins to fall through the mystique fog that has settled towards the earth. The fog makes it hard to see exactly what’s what, but a group of people is hoarded into a circle some 20-30 feet in front of the camera. They’re all decked out in their Sunday bests, and black seems to be the color of choice for whatever reason.]
[“Woke up this Morning” begins to play lightly in the background. It’s a bluesy song; one that starts out slow and gradually picks up steam.]
##We're gonna take you down... Deep down to the front lines (deep down)## [A man emerges in front of the crowd. A long, black robe shields his body from the impending weather. He raises his arm, a black leather book firmly placed in his hand. The preacher hushes the crowd, and begins to perform what we can only assume is a eulogy.]
##You woke up this morning Got yourself a gun, Mama always said you'd be The Chosen One.## [The camera pans 45 degrees to the left bringing a man off in the distance into view. The fog still inhibits our view quite a bit, but he appears to be a fairly tall man. A long trench coat (black as you might’ve guessed) covers his body from the neck down to the top of his shoes. He peers in towards the funeral arrangement with a fierce glare.]
[A hissing voice snaps over the scene. This is not the voice of the preacher, whose mouth continues to move, but whose words are inaudible. This voice has been prerecorded and then edited back in over the scene. Its amazing what these tricky fuckers can do now-a-days.]
Voice-Over(VO): The Roman Empire is perhaps the greatest civilization that has ever existed.
[The voice oozes confidence. The words come slow and hard.] ##She said: You’re one in a million You got to burn to shine, But you were born under a bad sign, With a blue moon in your eyes.## VO: The ideals it held in such high regard far preceded its time. [Flash. The scene fades away and a new one emerges almost immediately. The
Roman Coliseum appears, or at least a shot of what we imagine it looked like.]
VO: There were those who said such a society would never crumble. [Flash. Back to the funeral. The Preacher’s words are now audible.] Preacher: Earth to earth.. ##When you woke up this morning All that love had gone, Your Papa never told you About right and wrong.## VO: But even the most pristine become soiled when they can no longer control temptation. Pleasure is the devil’s playground. And the Romans when isolated were weak. They succumbed to happiness. And they fell..
[Flash. A shot of the Coliseum imploding. A modern method of destruction yes, but its amazing what those tricky fuckers can do now-a-days.]
VO: PWT. ##But you’re But you’re looking good, baby, I believe that you’re a feeling fine,(shame about it), Born under a bad sign With a blue moon in your eyes.## [Flash. The funeral.] Preacher: ..Ashes to ashes.. VO: Allow me to introduce myself. ##Sing it now...## [Flash. The mysterious figure from the funeral – the one seen leaning against the tree in the background walks deliberately through a pile of rubble. Bricks and pieces of shattered dreams are piled on top of each other. The man walks on, no emotion on his face. His eyes never waver.]
##Woke up this morning (Got a blue moon) Got a blue moon in your eyes So sad God damn God damned shame about it## VO: I am your Mt. Vesuvius. [His pace quickens and his strides lengthen.] VO: And just as the great city of Pompeii, you shall crumble beneath my wrath. ##Woke up this morning (Got a blue moon) Got a blue moon in your eyes So sad God damn God damned shame about it## [He stops, and slowly begins to turn his body towards the direction of the camera. As his profile disappears we see his face cleanly for the first time. His triangular jaw line is his only extricating feature. Save for maybe his hollow, black eyes, which are most likely the result of some new contacts. His hair is a sandy brown, the tips have been frosted a creamy yellow, and there’s enough gel in it to make a porky pine uncomfortable. His head rises very deliberately and he peers into the camera. Its as if he’s burning a hole through the lens and into your soul.]
VO: It’s not a question of if, but more a question of when. ##You woke up this morning The world turned upside down, (Lord above), Things ain’t been the same Since the Blues walked into town.## [Flash. Again the preacher.] Preacher: …And dust to dust… [Flash. The pile of rubble remains, but the man is gone.] ##But you’re But you’re one in a million You’ve got that shotgun shine.(Shame about it) With a blue moon in your eyes## VO: I’ve been labeled ‘the thinking man’s’ wrestler because just when you think I’m cornered, I’ll pull the wool over your eyes one more time. And when you think I can’t possibly have another ace up my sleeve, here comes the fifth one. It’s a natural progression. I win. You lose.
##When you woke up this morning everything was gone. By half past ten your head was going ding-dong. Ringing like a bell from your head down to your toes, like a voice trying to tell you there’s something you should know. Last night you were flying but today you’re so low
- ain’it times like these that make you wonder if you’ll ever know the meaning of things as they appear to the others; wives, husbands, mothers, fathers, sisters and brothers. Don’t you wish you didn’t function, done you wish you didn’t think beyond the next paycheck and the next little drink? Well you do so make up your mind to go on, when you woke up this morning everything you had was gone.##
[The rubble is still the primary focus in the scene. In fact, it’s the only focus.] VO: An army of only the most worthy warriors will be assembled, and in time we will dominate as no other has dominated before. We will be the epitome of what greatness should be.
##Woke up this morning, Woke up this morning, Woke up this morning## VO: As of now, these claims are nothing more than idol threats and empty promises, but time shall open the eyes of even the most foolish sinners. It begins April the 29th..
VO: BY.. ##You want to be, You want to be the Chosen One. Yeah, you know it. Just can’t help yourself, yeah.## VO: ANY MEANS.. ##Got yourself a gun. Got yourself a gun. Got yourself a gun.## VO: NECESSARY. [An ECW style close-up of the man known as Arson Embry flashes across the screen. As it does so, we fade to black almost instantaneously.]
