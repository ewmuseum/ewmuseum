Flashing in RSPWF

8–12 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[8 August 1997 — [name withheld]] I've seen quite a few comments (called flashing around here) from handlers in character. Some airing gripes, some looking for new feds, and I recently read one where a point was trying to be made (I think). I suppose it works if it gets attention, but I have the feeling that this sort of thing is discouraged by the feds heads. It seems to me that the prefered way to go is to directly apply to the fed you're interested in joining. I'm noticing that some feds are now requiring a "sample roleplay" be included in applications, and this method would be preferred over flashing in a public forum. I had thought about writing a flash here, but again, I didn't think that it would be a good idea. After all, you guys are busy writing these matches and taking care of other business to wade through this newsgroup looking for new players. ------------------------ [9 August 1997 — LOCO] [name withheld] wrote: There are some good roleplayers out there stuck in some pretty shitty feds. The only way they can get some reconition isn to flash on here and claim themselves as free agents. Fed's put out "roleplays" in here on a group format. So I think the individual has a right also.
