[Roleplay Critique] "BASTARD MONKEY!" Kyohei Nakamura

5 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Once upon a time* Kyohei Nakamura got a phone call. This was a pretty rare occasion, and for it, Kyohei did a few laps around his living room, making sure to lap the coffee table, so as to avoid the last misfortunate happening. By the time he was done, the person had hung up. Despotic, Kyohei dropped into his comfy sofa, and took a load off.
* "Once upon a time" being a week or two ago. It might not seem like that long to you, but to Kyohei, this was generations. Later on that night, the phone rang again. Two phone calls in one day! Kyohei, thinking about it for a few seconds, decided to answer the phone first this time around, and celebrate afterwards. Lifting Hello! Kitty's ears up, and placing them to his own, Kyohei began to converse.
"Hello! I Kyohei! You call! YOU VERY GOOD!" Silence on the other end. Then a sigh. Then a voice. "Yeah. Well, Kyohei, this is Tommy Lionheart." said the voice on the other end. "Like man from Oz?! HE VERY FUNNY! If only had nerve. HA HA! Very funny! You have nerve?" "Ummm... OK then. I'm just calling because I know you're part of WILD..." "WILD, MAN! Surfer! Almost fail class! BUT PASS! NOT STUPID! VERY SMART!" "Jesus, do you ever stop?" "Not happily." As you can see, this conversation was going nowhere, and pretty damn quickly. So Tommy Lionheart decided to take a different approach.
"Do you like Tommy Lionheart? Yes or no?" Yes. The annoying prick approach. "Me not know. Me supposed to?" was Kyohei's reply. "Why... why... OF COURSE! I'm your HERO! I'm EVERYONE'S hero! I am Tommy
FREAKIN' Lionheart!" stammered Tommy, taken aback by Kyohei's lack of worship.
This made Kyohei think*. After a few minutes of silence, Tommy Lionheart broke in. * Kyohei thinking is much scarier than you'd figure. For instance, he knows if God actually exists. But if he were to tell you the honest truth, your brain would melt at the pure destructive logic contained within the explanation.
Yes, literally melt. So it's best left unsaid. "Well? Do you like Tommy Lionheart? Yes or no?" asked Tommy once more, doing so in a way that made the answer perfectly clear. At least in Tommy's head.
"If hero, yes! Hero good! Villian bad! FIGHT VILLIAN! Laser ray gun! Zap zap! Straight to yo skull, gee dawggy." replied Kyohei, answering Tommy's question... kind of.
------------------------------------ Now, let's pause for a moment. Most people know Tommy Lionheart as an annoying yet good natured cruiserweight superstar, who's naivety is a redeeming factor usually. But this was not the Tommy Lionheart who was calling Kyohei
Nakamura on this day. For you see, Tommy had become sick of pandering to the
"lowest common denominator".* And he was about ready to show the world what he could do when pissed off.
* The "lowest common denominator" being the fans in this instance. Normally "1" is the lowest common denominator. But if Tommy was pandering to the number one, not many people would give a flying frog about him.*
* Except the number one. The conversation Tommy and Kyohei had that day seemed pretty straightforward. Kyohei would make sure the "hero" would get his welcome.
Tommy would come in and be his hero, baby. He'd take away his pain. He would be his hero, baby. He... would take... his breath away.
Little did Kyohei know, Tommy didn't have good things planned for fair
Kyohei.* He was trying to use him for his own means. Whether or not that be to blame for losses, or to help him win, it didn't matter. All of Tommy's plans were nefarious.** And now he had him right where he wanted him. In his corner, in a tag team match. Kyohei was to do the work, with Tommy picking up the victory. He explained to Kyohei that it had to happen that way, otherwise how could Tommy prove his hero-hood to the pitiful fans?
* As fair as a young crazy Asian with a monkey flipping you off on his pants could be. ** Except the plan to open a free checking account. He has nothing but good intentions there. Will Kyohei Nakamura figure out that Tommy Lionheart is not good for his health?* Will Tommy Lionheart get his way and use Kyohei Nakamura to further his career?** Will you buy Halloween candy for 75% off at your local
Walgreens? All these questions, and so much more, will be answered when Fusion
1.4 hits the air! Well, except for that Halloween candy question. That's up to you.
* Probably not. ** The oxymoron contained within is your's to figure out.
