Wrestlers Seeking Feds: Reaper Of Lost Souls... Wrestler Name: Eric (Vespillo) Draven Nickname(s): The Reaper Of Lost Souls, The Heart of Darkness Age: 32 Hometown: Green Meadows Mental Hospital Weight: 298 Pounds Height: 6' 11" Face/Heel: Both and Neither (Neutral) Wrestler Description: Tall, menacing, and dark stature. Remains mostly silent around others, and usually only speaks directly (face to face) to those individuals that he either trusts, or hates. He's dark minded, and thrives on pain, both being dished out by him, and too him. Loves to push his body and well being to it's limit. He's somewhat on the Psychotic side, and isn't really fit for society's norm. Understandable, considering that he's spent most of his life locked up in an Asylum. He's Mysterious in the fact that you never know what his next move will be. Things with Draven are never as they appear. Another thing about him, personality wise, is that he 'just doesn't give a damn'. He doesn't care who he hurts, or how badly. He's extremely self-centered. If something doesn't benefit him, then he won't do it. He's the typical "What's In It For Me" type of person, just on a withdrawn, darker level. Appearance wise, Draven pretty much looks the same every time you see him. He's always wearing the same style of clothing (Leather...usually sheepskin) and the same colors (black, sometimes with red). His hair, (also black...or rather a dark brownish color) stretches slightly beyond his shoulder blades and is rarely combed. Occasionally, he'll sport a goatee. His Physique is monstrous (your typical psycho-criminal) and his arms are completely engulfed with tattoos of a dark theme (Grim Reapers, Skulls, Corpses, Dragons, Flames etc.). Fighting Style: His High Pain Tolerance (thanks to all those drugs from the Asylum ;) ) would force me to say a mix between a Phenom (ala Vintage 'Taker) and a Powerhouse Finisher Name: The Impaler; Fall From Grace; PsychoTron; Blackout Finisher Description: Impaler: Double Underhook Piledriver (to knees)...think of a Pedigree/Tombstone Piledriver Combination Fall From Grace: His Old Vintage Finisher. An Inverted DDT off the top rope, High Impact. PsychoTron: Submission hold. Combo of Crippler Cross Face, and Cobra Clutch. Blackout: Front Face, Full Nelson Slam (Full Nelson, Inverted to where opponent faces you...your hands interlocked underneath their chin. Lifts oppo. up and drives the back of their head into the mat.) Favorite Moves (including spots): 1) Single Arm Chokeslam 2) Flying Clothesline (Usually with opponent on the outside. Will Swandive over ropes, and hit clothesline) 3) High Impact Spinebuster 4) Fallaway Slam 5) Big Boot 6) Fist O' Fury (Ring Corner...pummels oppo. down with wild punches, then proceeds to stomp...usually done to pop* the crowd) 7) Inverted Death Valley Driver 8) Implant DDT 9) Falling Powerbomb 10) He likes to find various things around the ring and break them over his opponent's head. Examples of such would be: Shovels, Lead Pipes, anything glass, anything hard and metallic, and God forbid he ever finds anything sharp and pointed ;) Contract Length: 2 months Bio/History: I'm not giving away too much here, as I like to reveal parts of Draven's past slowly through the use of Character Dev. Promos. I will say this much though: He's been through numerous Federations, Asylums, and Prison Systems. Including the Pioneer Wrestling Association (through the AOWF..that's Alliance Of Wrestling Federations), the Underground Wrestling Association, the Organized World Wrestling Association, Shadey Grove Mental Institution, just about all the prisons in the state of California, and a few more that I'd rather not recall. Bio-wise...Simply put, he was adopted by a small town Priest at the age of thirteen after his parents were massacred due to ties with the Japanese style Mafia. Three years later, after a lot of the girls around town had came up missing, the feds bust down the boiler room door of the church to find Draven madly swinging a led pipe unto the skull of his adopted father...surrounded by the bodies of the missing girls. Some, including the only girl to have survived the ordeal, said that Draven was overall innocent, that the Priest (Eric's adopted father, in case you missed it earlier) was the killer, whom had forced Draven to watch the massacres out of his deranged vision that his 'son' was the Antichrist, and it was God's will that he witness the 'sacrifices'. Draven simply snapped one day, broke his chains, and opened a can on the priest. Others, like the rest of the town, who say the Priest as a 'loving, gentle, man' and Draven as the silent, 'gives-me-the-creeps', 'sumtin-just-ain't-right-bout-that-boy' type individual....said that Draven did it, and kill the Priest after having been discovered. All this in court lead to Draven being submitted to Mental Hospitals until he was 'Deemed Fit For Society'...basically a life sentence for him.
