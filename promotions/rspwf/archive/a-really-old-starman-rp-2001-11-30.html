A Really OLD Starman RP...

30 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Figured I'd post it, and see what kind of reaction I got. This is when I first created the character, when he actually dressed in the mask and suit 24/7, as if it were a normal thing, and was at least supposed to be a semi-heel. You'll also notice that the "narrator" was far more vocal. :) Feel free to give feedback, and if you've seen the newer stuff, feel free to even compare, contrast, and tell me which you like better.
Thanks :) Ray [[Fade in: Chicago, Illinois. Yes, finally STARMAN has come to a place where maybe even
HE fits in. The Jenny Jones show. A place for the refuse and refugees of society. Who can blame him for coming here. For whatever reason Eli Flair on
Rosie O'Donnell has driven STARMAN on a quest for national television exposure. Today he will be appearing on a show entitled "I dress like my dog, he dresses like me, we both need a makeover as you can see." I don't know why they make such a desperate effort to make those damn titles rhyme, but they do. Anyway, for your viewing pleasure, or displeasure, or probably more accurately for your daily dose of mind numbing stupidity, and view of the frightening decline of American values, and intellect, we present
STARMAN on Jenny Jones.]] Jenny: Hi everyone, welcome to the show, we're here today to talk about people who dress like their dog, and vice versa. We've got a ton of interesting guests. But really, we're here for ratings, and to see just how low people will sink to get on television.
Crowd: YAY! Jenny: Let's get to our first guest. He hails from Columbus Ohio, and has a cat named Snowball. He says that for whatever reason he and his cat constantly look the same. Let's bring out Chuck and his cat Snowball!
[[Some awfully crappy music plays and Chuck and Snowball appear from behind a curtain wearing matching United States Flag Sweaters. Hold me, please, watching this I'm scared. To know that there are people like this out there...oh dear.]]
Jenny: So tell us Chuck, how long have you and your cat been dressing alike? Chuck: Well Jenny, I've had Snowball here for 3 years, he's my best friend in the whole world. So I figured about a year ago, we should start showing how much we care about one another. So I started buying my clothes and his to match each other's.
Jenny: You are aware Chuck that cats have fur and therefore don't need clothes? Chuck: Oh of course I am Jenny. But like I said, it's a unity thing. Unfortunately lately Snowball and I have been having a bit of an identity crisis. He runs for his food bowl when the doorbell rings, and when I hear myself clink his food dish, I try to answer the door. We're so alike. I just think that it's time for a change, so I came here to you Jenny.
Jenny: Hold on Chuck, we've got a comment from the crowd. RandomAudienceMember1: This comment is for the guy on stage with the cat. Jenny: Chuck? RAM1: Yeah, Chuck. I've got to tell you Chuck, you just aren't fly, so why even try, it's time for you and your cat to say goodbye!
Crowd: HAHAHAHAHA WOOOOOOOO YAY! YAY! Jenny: Now that wasn't very nice. Chuck: I hear it all the time Jenny, he's just jealous. Jenny: OK, let's bring out our next guest, she's a hairdresser from Atlanta,
Georgia, she says that she and her iguana are so much alike that she finds herself eating more lettuce than ever, and that she needs a heat lamp in order to sleep. Let's bring out Loretta and her Iguana Iggy!
[[Some chincy crappy music plays AGAIN, and Loretta and Iggy come through the curtain. And I thought STARMAN was bad, these people are even worse.
That's as scary as that scene in Poltergeist, with the girl and the TV. Man, that freaks me out.]]
Jenny: Welcome to the show Loretta, so you find yourself desperate to move to a tropical climate because you've become so much like Iggy, is that right?
Loretta: Yes Jenny, I love Iggy so much, he's my life. We do everything together. I wanted to marry him, I had a little tuxedo all laid out on him, but when I went to the Justice of the Peace he wouldn't do it. Iggy and I were heartbroken. I don't think I can go on. We're planning on moving to the rainforest and settling down. Unless you can help me Jenny.
Jenny: Well, we're going to give you and Iggy makeovers later in the show. But right now we've got our next guest to introduce. He's a professional wrestler from parts unknown. He says that he and his dog, STARDOG, are the greatest pure entertainers alive. He says that he has no desire to get any stupid makeover, he's just here for the publicity, let's bring out STARMAN!
[[Some chincy crappy music plays for the third time and joy of joys it's
STARMAN. I don't know whether we've dug ourselves deeper, or climbed out of the hole of insanity to some degree. I guess we'll know more soon. STARMAN goes up to the other guests giving them "pounds" and basically hyping them up as if the group on stage, while clearly insane to those of us who live in the real world, are in actuality better than us. WRONG. But let's listen in.]]
Jenny: Welcome STARMAN. I know that's not your real name, would you care to tell us what your real name is?
Starman: Look, it's STARMAN, that's my name. Really. And this guy here, this is STARDOG. He does tricks, would you like to see?
Crowd: YAYYYY!!! Jenny: I think that they'd like to see STARMAN. [[STARMAN pulls out a baby bottle full of milk with a rope attached to it. He puts the bottle in the mouth of STARDOG. And oh my dear LORD! He's spinning the dog around, it's attached to the bottle. Some poor fan of the
Jenny Jones show is going to get a canine related concussion. Serves them right for going to watch these dregs of humanity. Wait, he's done spinning, and the milk is gone. DAMN THAT DOG IS TALENTED!]]
Crowd: YAYYYY!!! WOW!!! Jenny: That was fantastic. Starman: Thank you. STARDOG worked very hard to develop that trick. Jenny: We've got a question from the crowd. RAM2: STARMAN, you ain't nothin. I seen you before. And boy, let me tell you sumptin. Eli Flair is gonna beat the hell out of you. Cause that dog is more talented than you'll evah be!
Starman: Quiet thine tongue wench! Listen, I am far more talented than Eli
Flair could ever be. And you're lucky that this isn't Jerry Springer, or I'd mosey my &ss into that crowd and beat on you like a a midget pimp to his 500 pound ho on that show. Because I am one bad &ssed mother...
Jenny: Shut yo mouth. We've got another audience question. RAM3: Listen, you don't look like you've got it going on at all. As a matter of fact it looks like whatever you may have had go itself going on out of town a long time ago!
Starman: HEY! HEY! HEY! I didn't come on this program to be berated by a bunch of illiterate Jenny Jones loving Chicagoites who can't even form coherent sentences, and while they are different people say much the same thing every Jenny Jones show!
Jenny: Calm down STARMAN. These people are just expressing their opinion. You have your opinion about them and they have theirs about you. It's a free country and that is why America is so great.
Crowd: YAAAAAAAAYYYYYYY!!! Jenny: Ok, we've got time for one more question before our guests go to get their makeovers.
RAM4: Jenny, this guy is crazy. Look at that purple getup. You look like
Barney the Dinosaur on Phen Phen!

Phen Phen. Fat &ss.
Jenny: Ok now, settle down. We're going to go to commercial and we'll be back with our guest makeovers.

Here's a before picture.
Crowd: EWWWWWWW! Jenny: And now here's the NEW LORETTA!!! [[I'm not lying when I say I hate that music. I really do. Well, congratulate me, I found something worse than the music.

Loretta: I like it, but I don't understand the parka. Jenny: Well Loretta, here at the Jenny Jones show, we go 110% all the time. Jenny: So STARMAN, how do you like it? Starman: Jenny, I can honestly say that I've never been more embarrassed in my entire life. I look ridiculous.
