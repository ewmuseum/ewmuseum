[GCW] Ken Stanley Flash [Member of The Suicidal High Flyers]

8 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>>>>>>>>>>> [Ken Stanley is walking back and forth in some sort of wrestling memorabilia shop. The store seems to be closed, but maybe the owner gave him some time to do a segment in his shop. Right behind Stanley is a picture of Ken Stanley and Tommy Kain hoisting up the tag team titles. Next to that picture is a large framed picture of Ethan De
Sade walking down the aisle with his belt. To the left of the SHF picture is one of Derek "Sky" Walker. Destiny is holding his at the time and soon to be again TV Title. You also see many hard to find pictures like, The Brothers Drake, Armageddon Inc., The Shark, The
Original Triumverate, and much much more. This is a very awesome store that everybody should check out.]
KS: From the beginning ... [pauses] from the beginning Tommy and myself had a goal. A goal to not just be champions once ... but to be champions forever. You see that picture right there of Ethan De Sade, that right there is a champion forever. Jungle Head Hunter over to my right ... a champion forever. Jordan Thorn, Jeff Easthope, Brandon
Simpson ... even if he is afraid of me. All champions forever. We don't have that chance anymore. We only have the opportunity through
Derek Walker to be moral victory champions. And let me tell you this.
I would be, and will be proud to walk down that aisle with Derek as tag champions, but it won't be the same. You know, people question whether Tommy is really hurt as bad as he says he is. Well, allow me to answer those allegations. Yes, we did move out of the way. I ...
I happened to move quicker than Tommy did that night. You see, when
Tommy jumped out of the way ... as he was jumping, the front of the car rammed right into Tommy's back. Tommy fell right then and there, and that's why the doctor's do not want him walking on his own at all.
The car also ran over his ankle, as it was pulling away. We don't know how long Tommy will be in that wheelchair; all we know is his career is over. Now, some may think he doesn't need a wheelchair. If you were in as much not only physical, but mental pain as he is right now, you'd rather be able to do as little work as possible to move around, trust me.
[Cuts to a clip from Season's Beatings as Tommy Kain sets Ethan Zane
Street up on a table and executes a top rope somersault senton with a twist, and then goes back to Stanley.]
KS: So what's next for The Suicidal High Flyers? Unfortunately .. nothing. You see, with Tommy being done I'm not going to go out and find myself, an exact replica of his wrestling skills. It was
Tommy that gave me strength to do moves that none of you would probably ever do. He helped me learn that we had to do whatever he had to do in order to win the match we were involved in.
[Clip of Ken Stanley's splash through two tables while Kossuth and
Street are laid on them.]
KS: You might be wondering what I'm feeling about my loss to Joey
Hutchings at Hale To The King. Well, I wasn't trained to be a singles wrestlers -- it's something new to me now, so it might take some time.
I know that I had Hutchings beat, I just made one mistake. I can say that not only did I make myself look good in that match, but
Hutchings --- I made you look good as well. Take your win Hutchings, it wasn't about winning for me, it was about proving to myself I could run a singles career. I know now that I can do it, I just need to learn from my rookie mistakes and realize I don't have a partner to tag in.
[Clip of Stanley tagging in Kain is shown.] KS: After the match you all saw I went and maybe a promise to Tommy. See I know what Tommy really wants. He doesn't really want me to leave the sport I love. No, he wants what I want. He knows I didn't become a professional wrestler to feud with two jobbers, and get crap about it from everybody in the back. He knows this.
[A clip from VK's match with Ted Bruce at H2TK is shown.] KS: That all brings us to present day. Viktor Karasapolis ... The
Sable Dragon. At Superfights, we've got a match, like you didn't know. I'm sure you're training your heart off, but allow me to fill you in. No matter how much you train, your ass is getting beaten on
Tuesday Night. You see, my singles career has just begun, and unfortunately for you ---- you've been put in a situation that you can't get yourself out of. So come Tuesday Night after my hand is raised, you might as well leave the GCW, because I'm on a mission not only for Tommy --- but for my father and myself, and you are nothing but a has been.
[The camera fades out as Ken walks out the door and the storeowner flashes a poster with the store's address on it quickly, and then locks the doors.]
