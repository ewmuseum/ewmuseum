[HaRdcOrE] Free Agent

13 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am the handler of HaRdcOrE...and I am looking for a fed that has been opened for a while with good roleplayers and an extreme side to them...I only accept federations with web pages...so if your fed meets these qualifications please
E-Mail me..here is my stats... Wrestler Name: HaRdcOrE Height: 6'10" Weight: 310 Hometown: Boston, Massachusetts Allignment: Neutral Style of Wrestling: Extreme Brawler Entrance Music: "Bring the Pain" by METHOD MAN Finisher Name: The Brain Freeze Finisher Description: A Spike DDT from the top rope Past Feds and Belts: SWL Heavyweight and Brawler Champion...XWA US Champion...IWA Heavyweight, IC, EXTREME, Tag Champion...NSWO Heavyweight, IC,
Tag Champion Sample Roleplay: ----------This is from the SWL---------- [The scene opens up to a shot of the Jenny jones show...the crowd is applauding as the show has just opened...Jenny Jones is standing in the aisle with a pair of red pants and a white shirt...she starts to talk]
Jenny Jones: Welcome everyone...today's show is called..."I was a High School
Zero...but Now I am a Grown Up Hero"...let's see some clips of our guests when they were in high school...
[The T.V. shows clips of 3 boys that look like geeks in the chess club...one guy has black glasses with white tape...the other has a huge acne problem and the third looks like a kid that has no money in the family...he is dirty...then there is 3 girls...one is as flat as back...the other one is black and has some of the nappiest hair ever seen...the last girl is FUGLY and wore glasses...then the camera cuts back to Jenny Jones]
Jenny Jones: Well on that note I think that we should bring out our next guest...this is what she looked like back in High School...
[Camera cuts to a shot of the girl when she was in High School...The crowd is booing...then the camera cuts back to Jenny]
Jenny Jones: Well look at her now... [The camera moves to a shot at the stairway and a 25 year old, tall blonde, big breasted, beautiful woman walks down...the crowd is in shock...the camera cuts back to Jenny as the woman sits down]
Jenny Jones: Now your name is......Amy? Amy: Yes Jenny Jones: Now when you were in high school there was a bunch of guys that would pick on you...tell us some of the stuff they did to you?
Amy: OK...When I was in high school I used to get called a carpenter' dream...um...nappy is the world...they would pull my hair and light my shoes... Jenny Jones: Didn't you have a crush on one of the boys that picked on you? Amy: Yes...and he didn't like me then but now he would be kissing my feet... Jenny Jones: Okay...Well we gotta go to a commercial break...but when we get back we will have a treat for the EXTREME side of you...
[The camera cuts to a commercial...approximated running time = 2:26...then the camera cuts back to Jenny Jones...she starts to talk]
Jenny Jones: Welcome back...our next guest was picked on back when he was in high school because he was poor and grubby...here is a picture...
[The camera cuts to a shot of the boy as the crowd is booing...then the camera cuts back to Jenny Jones]
Jenny Jones: Well let's meet the man that would make fun of this boy the most...he is Joey...
[The camera cuts to the stairway where a fat 30 year old man walks down and sits next to the beautiful woman as the crowd boos...then the camera cuts back to Jenny]
Jenny Jones: Well Joey...what did you used to do to this man? Joey: Well me and my friends were seniors and he was a freshman...so we would have senior girls get him real horny and then have him run outside with no pants on...I know it seems mean but it was jokes...
Jenny Jones: Well he is not so poor anymore...he is now wrestling professionally in the Supreme Wrestling League...He is now called "HaRdcOrE"...
[The camera cuts to aisle where HaRdcOrE was supposed to come out...but he does not...then the crowd starts to ooooh and ahhhh...the camera cuts to a shot of the stairway where HaRdcOrE is standing with a metal chair...he has a microphone]
HaRdcOrE: Hey Joey...how do I look now?...Am I poor?...Who is the man?...I will show you...
[Then HaRdcOrE jumps off the stairway and hits Joey in the head with a chair...joey is knocked out on the floor...Jenny Jones starts to talk]
Jenny Jones: Well HaRdcOrE with actions like that you will not be on the aire for very long...but I wanted to ask you...what did they do to you in High
School?
HaRdcOrE: Well Jenny...I do not want to talk about that...I want to talk about my match with the winner of the battle royal...as far as I can see it i will be facing "The Cobra" Rob Bansin...and I would like to comment on a couple of things that he has been talking about...
Jenny Jones: Well HaRdcOrE we have to go to a break so we will be right.... HaRdcOrE: NO...we will go to a break when I say that we go to a break...now onto the Cobra...how can you possibly come out over and over every day and talk trash about me?...You are in the Pain Killers...you must suck...I mean you guys need to learn how to abbreviate...PK is Pain Killers not PnK...stupid...then we have you Cobra...you call me an old fat piece of shit...well if I am old then you are my father...even though i have a better record than you...even though I am more known in wrestling then you...even though I could kick your ass in any kind of match...Look at you...you fat piece of shit...the only reason you call yourself Cobra is because that is the only thing that has as bad of breath as you do...so Cobra...when we lock up...IF THE CARD IS EVER RELEASED...I will kick your ass...BITCH...
[Security guards start to take HaRdcOrE away as he swings his chair...he hits one in the face and misses the other 2...then he grabs the woman and hold her hostage...he walks off the stage and the camera fades to black]
