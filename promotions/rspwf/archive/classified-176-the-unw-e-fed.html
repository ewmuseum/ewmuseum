Feds Seeking Wrestlers: The UNW E-Fed No, this isn't an ad for the latest chicken at KFC. No, you won't find no Buy One Get One Free offers here, either. Not even a stupid e-fed announcment saying "WE'RE #1! THIS FED R0XORZ! WE KICK YO ASS!", or any crap like that. So, if you're looking for that, go away, now... ...OK, so you're interested in a fed, huh? Well, the UNW E-Fed might be the fed that you're looking for. The fed has been TEMPORARILY CLOSED, and is looking for the right men to stay dedicated to the fed. A member of REVOLUTION STUDIOS, the UNW has been called one of the biggest up-and-comers in that interfed. We don't promise you that we're the perfect fed that you've joined, and we won't give you your money back, because the fed is free, after all. So, take a look at some more info, and, please, turn off that Christina Aguilera music you're blasting, tough guy, because it's making me insane!!! OK, so you want the facts. The straight dope on the UNW. Well, sonny boy, the UNW is run by NickZ, an owner that wants his fed to excel, which is not happening until you type in the address "http://www.unw-wrestling.tk" in your "Address" field! So, man, you ready for a (insert word here) e-fedding experience? Go to the UNW Boards and post, and when the site comes up again, you've got an application. Thank you for your time. The UNW is waiting for you to help us out. So, what will happen? Stop reading and check the fed out, even if it only takes thirty seconds. We promise you that we aren't like most feds on the net, once we start up again, it's gonna be a whole other story, MAN! (Oh yeah, contact NickZ on AIM at [detail withheld] to talk with him personally about the fed. Don't worry, he won't bite.)
