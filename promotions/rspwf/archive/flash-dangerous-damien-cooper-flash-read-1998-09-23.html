[Flash] "Dangerous" Damien Cooper [Flash] READ!!

23 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
before you read this is my newest creation and last. My new character. I have room for one 2-3 more feds. It's not perfect but this is my first flash i sent to UIWF. So read and enjoy and if ya liek reply or email me. Its short but sweet!
[The scene opens in a darkened abandoned brokedown home. The camera fades into the darkened room where you see thin shingles hanging from above. And you can see the burnt wood of the interrior room floor, as you walk over the floors you can hear a squeak after every footstep. You then hear mumbles and whispers of a the man they may be looking for. Scenes then close into another room where they're trying to find there way to the man from the where abouts of his voice.
The camera comes up to a old brown shangled door opened widely back to the wall. The scene is about to travel in through the door as you hear the voice once more...the camera turns back around and the door opens as you see a man sitting with his knees lifted up and forearms imbraced around his knee cap. At that moment he starts speak...]
["DANGEROUS" DAMIEN COOPER"]: "The Glory Days" of an young child running around in his or her own prisom striving to fight for the one goal that they inbrace in their little minds of theirs. As you think back upon when you were a young one. There was only one thing on your mind. And that thing was to keep yourself happy...to enjoy yourself. But now...well it's alot different. You thought you'd have it easy when you grow up, but as you look back into those boyhood dreams, you had only that one goal in life. You looked up to people your whole life. As a young child you had heroes such as FireFighters and Policemen. That was the one dream that you wished you could be. That one goal that each and every one of us has had is what drove us to be what we wanted. Back then we did anything and everything in our power to accomplish the matter. And now, it's gone. All the boyhood dreams and fantasies are now what we are in, _SOCIETY_!
That is the one thing that kills us all inside. Right now at this moment...I consider myself no longer living...as my boyhood dreams was shattered by all society. Which brings me to UIWF, to recapture some of my long awaited dreams.
I see this as my chance, to rejunvenate what was lost ago, and to bring revenge upon the society that has destroyed my every and last hope in life. You dont know how it feels to have a dream, a future in head of you shattered and snatched away from you right infront og your very eyes. Now I've strived and tried to rebuilt myself up, but it's not getting me anywhere, and I've kind of picked up a nickname! Now they call me "DANGEROUS", why? I dont even know myself, so i just stuck with it. I dont know if it's for better or worse, but I know for all mankind, that this man right here...DAMIEN COOPER, shall have is revenge one day and when that day comes...well let the Armageddon take place as
I shall rule supreme for all _ETERNITY_, my wrath and force shall rock this federation in my distinct ways. I'll tell you what drives me to be what I am today...but some may think it's stupid, but does it look like I care what you people think? if so..then you better think twice my friend because I care of NO
ONE!! The only and important thinf that drives me is this big black heart of mine pumping with fear and hatred that boils deep inside. And now I have a victim...an innocent adversary may be destroy to set an new society which I
_SHALL_ control. My world...this is my world. And I'll be damned if what is mine be taken away yet once again. One small step shall set it all up, starting with my first victim to take the fall,Nick Demola! There's not a moment that passes through my mind of what can happen in the ring at any time and point.
But thats the things you have to contend with when you're on a mission, a quest for revenge! This one night my friend shall settle it all as you come to pass with your maker, and fall once to the feet of the prince of way with demented souls. And you are one of the many that will be caught up in my web of sucess throughout this episode crisis. The never ending game of life never ends quite where you want it to does it? You plan for one thing and iit backfires into the next. Soon Demola, you'll feel my enigma upon life, agony and PAIN!! There's oonly one thing staring you in the face, its called fear, and no one escapes, and just to say that darkness which pumps through my heart unites my hunger of pleasure of torturing another human being, another human soul!
[Damien gets to his feet and looks around as he walks over to to one of the shingles thats hanging over him. He rips it down and looks up in the big hole thats in the ceiling. He reaches his hand into the hole to see whats inside as he reaches higher, he begins to speak once more]
["DANGEROUS" DAMIEN COOPER]: We're at a point where we want to push ourselves to see how far we can go, well let me tell yuo my friend, next week on Thursday night Terror I'll show you how far I can go...how deep my passion, how deep the firey flames burn. And most of all...it'll be one night you'll _NEVER_ forget, and especially for one Nick Demola! Your demise shall be met, and you will suffer the consequences of steping into the squared circle with me, "DANGEROUS"
DAMIEN COOPER!!! Live in Fear...as it is you only hope. [The scene then fades out to complete darkness.] --Handler Of: Mystikal [IWF,CNWL] [CAREER: 73-11] Anthony Anderson [ASCW,LAW] [CAREER: 4-4] Damien Cooper [ICW, UIWF] [CAREER: 0-0]
