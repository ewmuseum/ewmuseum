[Flash]Matt Haden[UCWP]

4 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tell me what ya think... [The scene comes into focus on a monsterous 1986 navy blue Ford Bronco, the camera spans the side as a voice begins to speak.]
Man - "The UCWP. I thought about joinin' but never fully went through with it. 'Till now. Now that I've gone and joined up, I got _big_ plans fer a special few. Who they are, or who I am, will be answered soon enough. I guess ya'll haveta' wait. All in due time my freinds. But firs', let me tell ya'll a thing or two. I come from Waco, Texas. I'm a man o' little games...I'm out fer one thing and one thing only. No, it's not gold, nor is it money. I'm strictly out here fer the pure pleasure of bustin' some heads together. And beleive you me, the pleasure will be all mine. Ever since I was a littl' ol' boy, I dreamed o' wrestling. And now, I've made it...and now, I'll prove to everyone I ain't a lil' ol' nobody. Prepare folks. I'm a man with a mission, I got goals and I ain't lettin'
nobody stoppin' me. You watch out for lil' ol' me now." [The camera slides down the length of the Ford Bronco,coming to a pair of legs. The shot continues up the legs,the torso and settles on the face of the strange man, no one has ever seen him before. The camera focuses in on his face as he tips his cowboy hat and stares forward with a cigar clenched in his teeth.]
Man - "Fer a couple o' men, they think I'm the wors' thang ta happen. No matter. I couldn' care less about em'. Just like I couldn' care less abou' any other of ya'll. I'm a loner, like it o' leave it, that's what I is. I got one lil' ol' message for all ya'll out there. Stay the hell away from me. Plain an' simple, folks." [The man pulls the waning cigar out of his mouth and tosses it aside. He continues to keep a cold glare as the camera fades.]
-------------- J. B. "Matt Haden" [UCWP]
