As a public service to all those that pass by RSPWF... here is a little marketing and promotion :) as seen on XWA Soundbyte December 21st 1997 ==============Diesel Power==============   [Diesel Power - Ice Man JC and Big Daddy Rob - are in a locker room just back from a workout, still in workout clothes] JC: [looking at the camera] Ok.. so the killers seemed to get out of a christmas beating.. thats quite allrighty though.. They are just gonna ring in the new year in a hospital bed!  AJ, Crippler.. when we get done with you.. you are gonna fill our your name.. Dead Men! BDR: Ya know thier honeys called them dead men in the bed.. JC: Shhhhh..  AJ.. you seem to think the fans are for you or something.. the next thing you'll try to tell us is that OJ is still looking for the killer.. BDR: He hasn't found him yet? JC: heh.. not yet big man.. and he never will.. just like the Killers.. they will never beat the DP Machine! BDR: Crippler.. I will do you a favor.. I will tell Art.. BDR and JC: THE SHOWSTOPPER!! BDR: to have mercy on your hairy @ss this christmas.. JC: He might.. he's been in a good mood latley... BDR: And leave some of your scrawny little body for DP to kick around.. JC: Man.. this is just too sweet.. BDR: Where have I heard that before? JC: Art's birthday party.. rememeber how he whined about the cake being too sweet? BDR: Oh yeah.. JC: Anyways, Killers.. you are looking at THE XWA Tag Champs!  You are looking at the the man who makes more high risk moves than Marrion Barry, and the tallest man in the XWA!  7 feet of Big Daddy Rob! BDR: We are just unstopable, [gets the silly look on himself] cuz gold WILL look purty on us! JC: Quote the Prodigy... WOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!! BDR: Now get outta here so we can change.. JC: [smiles] Sorry ladies.. the show needs to keep a pg rating.. not X! [camera fades as JC takes off a shirt and covers the camera lens]   Diesel Power is THE top tag team in the XWA, and are assoicated with THE top man in the XWA "The Showstopper" Art Middleton.. together they form The Prodigy.. check up on this stable of the future at the Showstopper web site: http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/Field/9204
