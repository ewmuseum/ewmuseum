[Critique] Another flash for you people to devour...

17 December 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yea, this is one of my 12 Days RPs, and it's infact for the alst round agaisnt Jason Storm and Devin Demola. Critique it... Say what you like... All critism to me is constructive in some fashion.
=== [Fade In.] [There he is—Ryan Delaney, sitting in front of the 12 Days promotional banner, the Day 12 edition. Delaney's, Demola's, and even Storm's face crowd the black and red banner. Today Delaney is wearing an Ill
Nino shirt, along with his usual style of jeans—black and baggy. Most of his makeup is applied, except for his powder. Stitches suck sometimes.]
[His hands are resting in his lap, and he's leaning back in the chair, no expression on his face. He just seems to be staring off camera--something is troubling his mind.]
RD: Everyone laughed at me trying to work my away into this tournament. Everyone back home in the Carolinas thought I was crazy for coming the whole way out here for some tournament in Vegas. None of them wanted to make the trip. They thought traveling coast to coast just wasn't worth it. They had a million and one excuses on why it was a waste of time. But I didn't buy it. I didn't listen. I signed up, and used my frequent flyer miles. I paid the toll of sleepless nights. I had no problem dishing out the cash for the flights. I wanted to win this tournament. I wanted to prove myself in all parts of the Empire. I wanted to wrestle the best.
No, I wanted to beat the best. [That stone cold look on his face doesn't change, but now he is looking at the camera.] I wanted my skills challenged. I wanted my talent pushed. I wanted to have my back against the wall, and have to fight for every inch to get back in the match. I never got it. I never got my BLEEPing competition until day eleven when I fought you, Jason
Storm. [He pokes at the stitches lining his forehead without even wincing in pain. A sick, devil-like smile comes to his face, his faceless expression disappearing.]
Good match, Storm. What else can really be said? We went at it as hard as we both could. We made each other hurt. We made each other bleed. We made each other unable to answer the ten count. It was an hard fought match, a great match. But this three way?
It'll be even better. [He nods, knowing it's the plain and simple truth.] Now I know you, Storm, like the back of my hand. Now I have actual in-ring experience with you, and know what to expect. I know what parts of the body to attack. I know how to trap you, how to pin you...
How to beat you. [He begins rubbing his hands together, almost getting excited at the idea.] Crack your little jokes. Make yourself believe you're invincible, Storm. Keep laughing. Keep having a good
‘ole time. It's good for a man to enjoy his last few days. It's great to see someone so close to choking... to losing... to failing having such a good time, thinking he's untouchable and that nothing will go wrong.
But, please, don't misunderstand me, Storm. Don't get the idea that I'm sitting around here, licking my wounds, and telling the world that I'm the better wrestler. I proved that I can't on Day eleven. But you proved something as well.
That you can't beat me. [The camera moves in a little bit, cutting Delaney's legs out of the picture, leaving only his upper body, which is slightly rocking back and forth.]
Ah, what a situation we've been put in, Storm. We have to relay on that runt Demola to decide our fate.
We have to depend on a simple brawler with a legendary last name to decide who wins this match.
Since we all know he's can't beat you, let alone me. [Uh oh, that's not something Devin's going to enjoy hearing.] Yea, Devin, I have my eye on you. I knew from Day one that in order to win this tournament you'd play some pivotal role in the process, and look… I was right.
You're here, in the final round, stuck facing the two best wrestlers in all of RAW. And the bottom line is,
Devin, there's nothing about you I really like. Well, except for your theme song. [Notice the Ill Nino cloth Delaney's wearing.] Don't get me wrong, Devin, you're good. You're damn good. But brawling can only take you so far. Knowing a few basic moves here and there can only lead to success in the lower ranks, among the scum of the wrestling world. You're in the big time now, Devin.
You're facing the RAW Heavyweight AND the RAW Cruiserweight champ all in one match. And I'm wondering if you can actually keep up. [He does a hand motion that screams, "Hey, it's a concern of mine! Don't hate me for it!"] Storm surprised me last week, though. He was able to keep up when I thought he wasn't going to be able to.
Maybe you will too. Maybe you'll show off some of that Demola talent, and make this match an actual three-way dance, instead of just the rematch I see in my head.
[He bits his lip, and shrugs. Guess it's possible.] I guess stranger things have happened in the past. [Fade Out.] === Yea... - Delaney * hehe... pretty bad, eh?
