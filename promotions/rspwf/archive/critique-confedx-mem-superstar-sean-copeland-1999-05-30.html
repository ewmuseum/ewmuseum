Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first RP in about a month or so because i've been running and fed and stuff. This is an into RP to the DSWA.
[The camera comes on inside a luxurious jewlery store. The camera strolls around the store as you see tons of 14 karot gold watches, chainz, bracelets, necklaces, ect. The camera heads towards the entrance way and you see a kinda tall, muscular man, around 6'5 and 270 lbs. He walks in with a couple of bags in his hands. He yells out "SUPERSTAR is Here!" and soon you know its "God's
Gift To Wrestling" "SuperStar" Sean Copeland walking into the store. He's wearing long light brown khaki pants, a black button up shirt, with a white shirt underneath, his loop ear ring on his left ear, some nice brown casual shoes, a hollow gold necklace, and some Oakley's sunglasses. He walks into the store with his a smirk on his face and motions to the camera to come towards him. He snaps his fingers and everything in site, becomes frozen like a statue.]
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: I am Sean Copeland, the most highly recruited man by
Kenneth Morlock, the president of the DSWA or should I say, Dirty South
Wrestling Association. This man has given me the opportunity to shine and I will shine, like no man has shined before. I've already been put in two matches in his damn federation. Now let me give you a brief history backgroud on myself. I am "SuperStar" Sean Copeland, I am God's Gift To Wrestling. I was born on June 17, 1977 in Orlando, Florida, where I live today. I attended elementary school at Scornfold Elementary. I was on the verge of dropping out due to my tremendous misbehavior but I stayed in school, luckily for me, and soon graduate and went on to Middleton Middle School, become a well know scholar and athlete. I attended Hoppes High and became a popular athlete in football and basketball, getting scholarships to go to college wherever I wanted to go. I maintained a great education and went to UCLA, where I met a man by the name of Jeff Montgomery or as you guys know him as maybe All
American or USA Montgomery. He taught me the arts of karate, and wrestling. Throughout my college years, I played basketball while learning wrestling. After college I went on to construction working and learned the fine arts of
Hardcore Wrestling, working with 2x4, sledgehammers, ect. I learn the technical side of wrestling and met Jeff again as he introduced me to the dRw and IWA where I went a total of 4-0 before it shut down. I then spent a little time resting and have just signed with DSWA and TCW where I can currently participating in.
[Sean Copeland snaps his fingers again and the frozen people begin to move again as if the world was normal. A store employee go over to Sean Copeland.]
Employee: Hi, how may i help you! "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Get that damn smirk off your face. Stop trying to hide your dissatisfaction towards this job. Get the hell out of my way.
Employee: But sir, I wasn't acting at all, I was to just asking if you needed any assistance towards your purchasing of our products.
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Do you know who your looking at? Employee: No! "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: I am your worst enemy and if you don't get the hell out of my way, I'm gonna have to kick your stinking ass. I'm looking! I don't need any assistance right now.
[Sean Copeland walks over to the glass cases displaying the jewlery as he looks around. Another employee comes over to him.]
Employee #2: Do you need any help sir? "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: um.....no! Does it look like i need help? Employee #2: Yes! "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: [Looking at the employee] Dammit, give me some piece. I'm just looking. I don't need any of your assistance!
[The employee walks away.] "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: All these people don't know how to give someone some privacy around here.
[Another employee comes walking towards Sean Copeland. He has on a suit and a name label that reads "Store Manager".]
Store Manager: Hi, sir, would you like any help? I see you were being a little rude to some of my employees earlier.
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Nooooo! For the third time, nooooo i don't need any help. And me being rude. I'm not rude, those damn employees of your weren't leaving me alone, trying to decide what i want.
Store Manager: But these items seem to be out of your price range. Only wealthy people come to this store.
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Look at me, I'm "SuperStar" Sean Copeland, God's
Gift To Wrestling, I'm alot more wealthy then your stinking ass.
Store Manager: Sean Copeland, the same Sean Copeland that wrestles for the DSWA. "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Yeeeeees! Store Manager: I am truly sorry, Mr. Copeland. I've heard about you all over the world. "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Oooook, now get the hell out of my face, you dumb bastard before I leave the store with no goods in my hand.
Store Manager: I'm sorry! Buy why are you here, you should be getting ready for your first match as you are fighting in a 5 way match to crown the Number 1 contender for the Southern Title, plus your are in the main event battle royal to crown the first Southern Title Champion.
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Shut the hell up! I know what I'm doing. Those four pussies don't stand a chance against God's Gift To Wrestling.
Store Manager: Well Eric Van Steele, Dan Debacle, Venom, and Dylan Rapture don't seem like pussies to me. I've heard of them alot. Rapture has been crusing around the indies for a while now.
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: So, he's not Superstar. I am the true champion of the DSWA. Eric Van Steele claims to be the Icon but he can't be, I was label the Future Icon, therefore I am the Icon. I am the only man worthy of holding that name. Venom, I am da showstoppa. All my millions and millions of my fans come out and want me to fight. Rapture, I am the high-priced ticket. Everyone pays those buck to see me wrestling in my nightly 5 star match. Dan Debacle isn't even concentrating on his own match but trying to go for the main event stars such as Alex "The Hammer" Cote. Dan D, can go shove it up his ass for all i care.
Store Manager: Shush! There are children in here! "SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Don't shush me!! I"m the main eventer, unlike a Eric
Van Steele whose a high midcarder or a Rapture those a high midcare or Venom, a regular midcarder.
["SuperStar" Sean Copeland walks over a counter and points at a gold rolex. He points at that one.]
"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Yeah, i like that one! What was i just saying now??!!! Oh yeah, well Van Steele, Venom, Dan D, Rapture, Presence of
Persecution is coming closer and closer and soon you will feel the supremeness of the man they call Sean Copeland. hehe

"SuperStar" Sean Copeland: Southern Title Battle Royal, everyone in the fed,
I'm ready, i just hope you all survive my hardcore f'n pain. And you will feel the wrath of........
