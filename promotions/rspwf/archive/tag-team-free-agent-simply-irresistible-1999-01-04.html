{Tag Team Free Agent} Simply Irresistible

4 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm [name withheld] and I handle a tag team that is now on the market and looking for a good fed or two to get in. I am looking for a completely booked fed with a closed flash system. I would also like a federation that has a good tag team division. So if you would like to have me in your fed, just drop me a line at
[detail withheld] . Here is a sample roleplay: *************************************************** [Fade In.] [The scene fades in and you see people who are walking around on a sidewalk, obviously in the downtown of a city. People just walk by the camera, either not seeming to notice it or they just don't care. There is a bench close to the camera man, that has two men. One of the men seems to be built and his muscles and size make him stick out. He has a 20 oz. bottle of Coca Cola in his hand. The other, who is sitting next to him, doesn't seem as muscular but is by no means small. The bigger one of the two has on jeans and a white tanktop which shows off his muscles, making him stand out even more. The second one of the two has on black wind pants with a plain white t-shirt. The camera turns to survey the streets, seeing nothing but an endless sea of cars hurrying to get there passengers to work, home, or where ever they may be headed. The camera then turns back to the two men and begins to move toward them. Once the camera man is near them, they recognize his presence and look up at the camera. Once you get a good look at the duo's faces they are recognized as the newest edition to the tag team roster of the IWC, "Simply
Irresistible". The larger one is known as "Pretty Boy" Paul Stevens, and the smaller one, "Stunning" Simon Spencer. Simon speaks first.]
"Stunning" Simon Spencer: Well I know news travels fast, but I didn't expect camera men to be after us this soon.
[He chuckles and points to the camera man, which also brings a little smirk to Paul's face.] "Stunning" Simon Spencer: But, I guess people were going to find out sooner or later. Yes, it is true, Simply Irresistible has come to the IWC. Now, I know you people are saying, "Nobody knows who Simply Irresistible is." Well, I have one thing to say... give us time. All in due time, everybody will know the names of "Stunning" Simon Spencer and "Pretty Boy" Paul Stevens, who will be better known as the IWC Tag Team Champions.
[He stops and glances at Paul, almost urgeing him to speak. Paul turns away and stares at the camera.]
"Pretty Boy" Paul Stevens: Ever since we were kids it has been me and Simon. We are cousins and lived together, we laughed together, and we fought together. We are not going to expect anything different in the IWC. This is the biggest promotion we have been in yet, and we are going to lay down to no one. We have been around and have watched the tag teams in this federation, and I don't know if there is a tag team that is ready for us. I know for sure there isn't a tag team that wants it as bad as us. We have given blood and sweat in all the little promotions, and now it is our time to break out in a big federation, and that is exactly what is going to happen in this federation.
Love Hurts, Hah. Not that you don't have talent, but compared to us....there is no comparison. Now in another federation, you might have a chance but not with Simply Irresistible here.
[Paul stops and takes a long swig out his bottle of Coca Cola. While he is doing this Simon takes center stage and starts to talk again.]
"Stunning" Simon Spencer: Now Explosion, you guys are pretty explosive. But can you match power and intelligence with us...I don't know. But I can tell you one thing, if you could some how pull off a win against us, you wouldn't wrestle for a while because you would have given everthing you possibly could and then some. And then there is Teen Angst. I have to give it to you guys..
you are definitely on top of your game, and would really give us a run for our money. [A strange look comes across Paul's face and he promptly stops Simon from saying any more.]
"Pretty Boy" Paul Stevens: Teen Angst, its real simple, you guys better be ready. Because when you least expect it, all of a sudden your tag team belts are going to be around the waist of
