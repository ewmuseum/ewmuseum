Roleplay for Dave Bean, PLEASE CRITIQUE

25 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: 1:34PM, Cleveland, Ohio. We are inside a large, and heated gym. The room which we stand, contains over fifty various work out machines, nearly half being occupied. Several fans are blowing at angles, providing cool air into the atmosphere. Several hanging lamps, swing from the ceiling, lighting up the room. Men and women, walk around the room, switching machines, their reflections coming off mirrors which sheild the entire wall, each way around. The red carpet, shows several stains from sweat, sports drinks, and several other things. Soon, the scene centers on a free weight bench press, where a man lays on the bench, lifting what seems to be quite the load of weight, as a lovely, and beautiful woman stands aside to her.]
[As we get closer, the man is easily recognized, as a fellow
BFTW worker, "The Corruptor" Dave Bean. He's wearing a pair of black Adidas wind pants, a pair of Nike tennis shoes, and an Adidas t-shirt. His dirty blonde hair, is glistening with sweat, as it swings to the side of his horizontally placed head.
As he continues to lift, the lovely woman who stands next to him, is his lovely wife, Malinda, who wears a short pair of blue spandex shorts, and a short spandex top which matches the shorts. Her brown hair, is tied up in a small pony-tail, as it hangs behind her lovely face. Bean now lifts one more press, and then places the weights back on the rest, and he sits up, brushing his forehead, full of sweat.]
DAVE BEAN - Wheeww.....this crap get's tougher every year. MALINDA - Here you go hon... [Malinda hands her husband, a Lime flavored All Sport] DAVE BEAN - Thanks babe... MALINDA - Scoot off.... [As Bean drinks his All Sport, he also leaves the bench, while
Malinda takes off a couple of weights, and then slips her back onto the leather covered bench. As she begins to lift like mad,
Bean puts down the bottle and begins to speak.]
DAVE BEAN - Hey there, welcome to the best damn gym, in the country. This is the late Danny Brooks gym, a man who helped raised me through some of the toughest times in my life, and now, whenever I need to get away, think, or just do something, he's still here for me. In fact, this is the exact place, where I decided to become the "Corruptor", the new
"Garbage" man, if you will. And it seems, that things are going well for me so far, just a couple nights ago, I cleaned the Great Southern Rapist's clock big time, and if I were him, I'd be on the next plane out of the BFTW, because even though I feel a lot better, about wasting your ass, I still am not finished. So if you do decide to stay, here's an open challenge to you, any time, any place, any gimmick...You want it, you got it....Just be sure to think about putting your life, into the _CORRUPTOR'S_ hands next time.......
[Malinda takes a break from lifting, but then returns to pressing, what must be 200lbs. Bean looks at her, scan- ing her body, then returns to speaking.]
DAVE BEAN - Hmm...so what's up for the Extreme one nowadays? Oh...yes, Ralph Bronx. Well, I already know that you were pissed, when you found out that your going up against Dave Bean in the first round of the Hardcore
Title tournament, so I wonder how happy you were when you found out, that you get a warm up match! Oh Bronx, to tell you the truth, I dont know much about you kid, in fact, I've never heard of you, until the day that I found out it was me and you, one on one in the first round. But hey, let me help you out with something, here's a little back- ground information on Dave Bean. I'm the most _EXTREME_ man, the most _HARDCORE_ man, and the most _PAIN-
FUL_ man you'll ever, EVER, meet in your professional wrestling career. Does that help you out a little? More?
Well, I'm the Corruptor, of the BFTW, and the wrestling industry....what does the Corruptor do? He cleans up the sport, of cocky, and sick little momma's boys, that are ruining the sport, of wrestling. Since you don't seem to fall under the catagory of "sick", I'm labeling you as a cocky lil' Momma's boy, so ...
_PREPARE_ _TO BE_ _CORRUPTED!_ [Bean has a very determined, yet scary look in his eyes, as he then looks, and picks up his drink bottle, which sits on the red carpeted floor. He takes a couple swigs, and then replaces the bottle back on the floor.]
DAVE BEAN - Another thing, if your going to quit, I'm beggin you dont even show up, because I dont have time to waste, with people like that, alright? Hell, I'd have more respect for you, if you dropped the match, instead of quitting inside the ring......clear?
MALINDA - Hey...help me out..... [Bean runs behind the bench, placing the two hundred pounds of weight back on the rests. Malinda then sits up, and get's off the bench, reaching for her drink bottle, and she then turns to Dave, and begins to speak.]
MALINDA - So what are you talking about? DAVE BEAN - Ralphie Bronx, you know, Saturday? MALINDA - Pfff.....pushover..... DAVE BEAN - Yea, but I aint taking nothing for granted. MALINDA - Babe, that guy isn't nothing but another punk ass wannabe, you'll hit the bastard like there aint no next week!
DAVE BEAN - Damn I can't wait.... MALINDA - I can! I'm really not looking forward to go to New York,
_AGAIN_!!! DAVE BEAN - Dont worry, we'll be in and out.... MALINDA - Hmm.....what do you think Bronx is thinking about right now? His strategy, or his will....
DAVE BEAN - Wow, your in quite the mean mood today.... MALINDA - I'm just mad, because this place doesn't give you the matches you deserve..like contender, or title matches....How are you suppost to get respect from everyone, if you beat "jabronies"
like Ralph Bronx? Or _The Great Southern Rapist_, there's a real winner.... DAVE BEAN - I understand....but just give it some time....C'mon, let'd get out of here. [Bean places his arm around the side of Malinda, as they head to- wards the exit door, with the screen fading to black. Bean seems determined to take on Ralph Bronx, so is Bronx up for it? Be sure to watch Saturday Night SlobberKnocker, this Saturday Night!]
