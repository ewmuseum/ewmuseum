Which Way To Hostile Takeover?(High Flyer Rp's)

28 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a three part series for a fed called the IWO which I did earlier in the month to win the television title. Give me negative, positive, whatever type feedback you wish, but I'd like some.
============================== Which Way To Hostile Takeover? Part 1 ============================== (The scene opens up in a hotel area. It looks as if the classic hawaiian getaway hotel, as walls are replaced by grass, and hula girls surround the area. In the lobby in the distance is about four kids playing with a beachball, just trying to keep it up. In the foreground, there are three servants, two desk attendants out of the three, and the last one being a bellboy. The bellboy grabs some luggage and escorts a family of three to their room. Then, an elderly couple comes up and asks the one desk attendant something, you can't hear them. The other desk attendant is just staring off into space. Then, in walks High Flyer, wearing a hawaiian tie dye type t-shirt, frayed tan shorts, and flip flops. He pulls his silver Oakley off of his eyes and rests them on his forehead, as he just admires the scenery. Then, in walks a BellBoy, carring a load of stuff. Two suitcases, a duffle bag, and a cosmetic kit? A purse? What is this! Oh wait, Kate just walks in, that explains teh cosmetic kit and the purse, as she is dressed in a light blue two piece swimsuit. Her hair dangles down as she whiffs it back behind her head. She goes up to
Flyer, as the bellboy struggles with the suitcases. He picks up two, and then drops one of them going for the purse. He tries it again, picks up the purse, and then the duffelbag. He drops it on the two suitcases, and then picks them up. Kate then walks over.)
Kate:You forgot this. (Kate puts the cosmetic kit on top, as the weight forces the bellboy to drop all of the suitcases and everything. He starts to scold himself with some words that can not be televised, and then goes at it again.
The camera pans over to the service desk, where High Flyer is looking at the attendant. He stares at him(The attendant), and then Flyer waves his hand in front of his face. He sees the bell, and then rings it. The attendant just stands there.)
Flyer:What, are you one of those british guards or something? (Flyer rings the bell again, and then goes for it one more time, but the attendant quickly reaches over and grabs Flyer's wrist, preventing the action to take place.)
Desk Attendant(DA)(He is monotoned):I am knowlagable of your arrival here, but I don't really care.
Flyer:Oooooooo kaaaaaay. DA:So, what the hell do you want, it is my lunch break. Flyer:It is? DA:No. Flyer:You are a piece of cake, soo many confine the world, yet there's not enough to go around. I'd like to find out my room. I have reservations...
DA:Under what? Flyer:Well, I was about to say, before I was rudely interupted. Under the name of High Flyer.(Flyer smirks.)
DA:Is that something I should care for? Flyer:Well, I am an IWO superstar! DA:And your point would be... let's check it out on the old computer, huh? (The desk attendant goes over and opens up a file, and does some clicking and some typing. Then, he goes over and talks to Flyer again.)
Flyer:Well?? DA:You're reservations have seemed to be cancelled, but a man by the name of President Jamie.
Flyer:JAMIE?!?! What type of power trip is he on now? What right does he have to cancel my vacation! I deserve a vacation! It's summer, I have been working for over six months with the IWO, can't I take a breather, can't I party?
DA:That is what it says on the computer. (The other desk attendant comes over.) DA2:Does there seem to be any problems? Flyer:Yeah, for some reason, my reservations are canceled! DA2:Well, you must be that High Flyer person, am I correct? Flyer:Yes, I am. Why? DA2:Well, I recieved an e-mail from that same Jamie, two actually, and he told me to give it to you when you get here, and give you these.
(The desk attendant gives him two letters, as well as a plane ticket... or at least a copy of a plane ticket, what good is that? It is to go back to the continental united states.)
DA2:I am sorry for the inconvience. Flyer:Let's see what Jamie wants from me now. He always want's something. (Flyer walks off with the letters. He goes over to Kate, and begins to open the one.) Kate:What's going on? (Flyer ignores her as he opens up the one letter. It shows... Dear Mr. Flyer, It has come to my attention that you have requested a vacation from Vice
President John, but I do not approve. I wish to have you back in active compeition ASAP. I was the one to cancel your vacation hotel room, as there was no other way I could reach you. Pager, e-mail, nothing. Mr.
Flyer, it is dier that you make your match on Saturday, or else you shall never get another shot at the television championship. Enclosed should be a fax, of airplane ticket copies. Make sure you visit my one friend down there and get your tickets, and make Saturday's Hostile
Takeover. The other letter you have recieved is the card for the show.
Thank you. IWO President Jamie Kotsay (Flyer crumbles up the paper and tosses it behind his head, just as another tourist walks by with a fish jar. And guess what, SWISH! Right into the water. The women tourist get really made, takes the paper out, and hits Flyer in the back of the neck with it.)
Flyer:(Sarcasm)Thanks...(end) stupid tourist! Kate:What's going on? Flyer:Well, our room reservations have been canceled. Kate:CANCELLED! BY WHO! Flyer:Jamie, he says I have to get to Hostile Takeover right away! Kate:But that in... what, like... an hour? Flyer:No, no, he moved it to Saturday. The card was in delay of coming out. Probably some meeting hold up or something.
Kate:So, who do you have to face? Flyer:Well, I have to face... (Flyer picks up the other letter and opens it. He looks in and sees just the t.v. title match and his name.)
Flyer:WHAT! I have to face Shack for the t.v. title? Kate:What are they trying to do to you? Flyer:And if I don't make it, I won't get a rematch either, wait, hold up. (Flyer looks at the rest of it and sees Fugite as the champion and his opponent.) Flyer:Wait, I don't get it. I'm facing Fugite for the television title, but yet, he's not the champ?
Kate:Well, he doesn't have the belt you mean. Flyer:Yeah, that's what I mean. No belt, no champ monacure, NO TITLE
DEFENSE! Justin Shack has the t.v. title on his shoulder, how must I face Fugite for it?
Kate:I don't know, the IWO board works in Mysterious ways (the boards wrestler is ?¿?, the Mysterious One.)
Flyer:I'll take it though, I mean, any title match, even if it is screwed up, is good for the Cult. We will have the television title, the sooner the better.
Kate:What does this mean, we don't get to have our fun time? (Kate puts her head on his shoulder.) Flyer:I'm sorry, I guess not. We're going to have to have another vacation some time or another. I'm sorry, it's beyond my control.
Kate:So what, we have to leave right away? Flyer:Well, I've got to met Jamie's friend and get the tickets. We've got to go now if I want to get to Hostile Takeover, and I do want to figure out what I did wrong before. Come on Kate.
(Flyer turns around and begins to walk away, as he bumps into the fan, causing all of his luggage to fall out of his hands. I guess they ran out of bellboys?)
Flyer:Oh, I'm sorry(snicker), you need help? Fan:Yeah, I do... wait, you're High Flyer aren't you? You know you got a rematch with Fugite.
Flyer:Yeah, the former Television champion. Fan:Former? When did he lose it? Flyer:Didn't you see that masked man beat him and take his belt. The masked man then gave it to Shack.
Fan:Ohh... sure!(sarcasm) Flyer:Fine then, help yourself! (Flyer pushes the fan down as he walks off. Kate helps the fan up, but then attends to Flyer. The Fan gets the dirt off of his pants.)
Fan:What an idiot. (The camera pans back over to Flyer who's walking pretty fast.) Flyer:Dipsh*t. (Then, the bellboy calls him down.) Bellboy:I got your stuff into your room. Flyer:I don't have a room anymore, bring it to the car. Bellboy:UUUUUUUGGGGGGHHHHH!!!! (The bellboy storms off in disgust. Flyer gets into the car, as does
Kate. The scene fades as they wait in the car.)
============================== Which Way To Hostile Takeover? Part 2 ============================== (The scene opens up early saturday morning, as the camera is positioned inside a taxi. There is a driver in the front, wearing the traditional uniform. You can see his face in the rear view mirror, black, asian male, mid to late thirties. If you look at his photo id in the front, which you can barely see in the camera, his name is Joel Keeler, and he looks to be white, gray hair, beard, and not asian. All the characteristics that this driver does not have. Joel also has a height of five foot five, as the driver is at least six foot, three hundred pounds. Driving down the busy street, you are passing such stores as
7-11, Wawa, Mary and Mark's Italian sausages, and the Compact Disc
Center for some. Then, a voice is heard, coming from the righ side of the camera. It seems to be Flyer.)
Flyer:How much longer till we get to Jamie's friend? You better know where we're going! Taxi Driver(TD):Yeah, yeah, yeah, how many times do I have to tell you? (For some odd reason, even though this is not New York, but is hawaii, he still seems to have a New York Accent... Go figure)
TD:We'll be there in five. Flyer:You said that ten minutes ago, and five minutes before that! I'm getting a little restless.
(Another voice is heard, coming from the left side. It sounds like
Kate.) Kate:Come on, we are all restless. I mean, I just wanted to have a little fun in the sun, but NOOOOO! Jamie had to SCREW us over again!
Flyer:He screwed you over. I don't mind going back to work as long as this BUS DRIVER over here KNOWS where the HELL he is going.
TD:I'm a taxi driver, and I know where I'm going. Flyer:You better. I hope you stoped that meter of yours! TD:Nope, sorry. Flyer:Sorry, that's the best you can do? (The car pulls up to a huge house. Large front lawn, sprinklers all over the place. Large white gate in front of the yard, and just a mansion all together. You can catch the glimmer from the water that encapes off of the in-ground swimming pool. Flyer gets out of the cab, and shuts the door, just in awe of the amazing site. Then, Kate comes out with her makeup kit and her purse, as her jaw drops as well. Flyer goes around to the back and pulls out the luggage, as they just stare. The taxi driver gets inpatient, and rolls down the window.)
TD:HEY! YOU! Yeah, you! Who else am I talkin' to? Flyer:Oh, oh, yeah, sorry.(Pulls out wallet) How much is the fare? TD:Uhhh,(checks the machine) That'll be 45.68. Flyer:How come these fares never come to even numbers? And why the hell is it so high. I mean, the hotel is right down the street! I saw this place when I came to the hotel!
TD:Uhhhh.... you're guy is over there, the pet store. (The taxi driver drivers away as Flyer shouts "encouragement" to the driver.) Flyer:The pet store? So, Jamie's friend isn't rich. (Then, a man walks out from the Pet store. He's dressed in a brown and black vest as well as a white shirt underneath, and slack on. He is sweating like crazy.)
Flyer:I hope you guys have air conditioning in there, because if you don't, it's going to be hell, especially for you!
Guy:Well, I see that you are High Flyer. And this is the lovely Kate
Young. (He takes her hand very swavely and kisses her on her hand. Kate giggles.) Flyer:Who the hell do you think you are? Guy:Well, I'm the owner of this pet store. I'm Jamie's friend from high school. Come in, come in. Let's talk.
Flyer:Ok, but you can make it quick, right? I'm in kind of a rush to get back to the conintental us.
Pet Store Owner/Jamie's Friend(PSO):Yeah, I understand. This is under short notice, and you have to get to Hostile Takeover so you don't lose your shot at the gold.
Flyer:Ok, I'm going to be nice today, just because I want to get ahead in the world.(Smirk)
PSO:Ok, right this way. (The pet store owner walks Flyer into the room, as Kate stays outside and guards the stuff. Flyer walks in, and sees snakes, gerbils, hamsters, birds, and even a pet cat.)
Flyer:Well, let's see which animal you want to have to fight in the animal league. Too bad that little penquin of the Raging One died, maybe
I can buy him a replacement... ummm, let's see here, how about a snake?
No, that's to dangerous for a man with near to no mental capacity as the
Raging One. How about a bird? No, birds talk back. You don't want The
Raging One to continually talk all the time. How about a gerbil, they can't be wrong for the Raging One! Except for the crawl space...
PSO:Excuse me, I thought you wanted in and out. (The owner hands him a manilla folder. Flyer opens it, sees a plane ticket for this afternoon at one pm, which will get Flyer there by about three in hawaiian time, meaning about six. This doesn't give him much time to prepare.)
Flyer:This is good... no time, no time. I'm sorry, glad to have met you. (Flyer walks out of the store in a hurry. He walks back onto the busy street, and starts to hail a cab. One comes down, and pulls over. The driver gets out, and it's the exact same Asian person as before.)
TD:Oh no, not you again! Flyer:Uhh, yeah, it is. Can you take us to the airport? TD:Get in, get in, and hurry it up! (Flyer packes the bags in the back compartment as Kate goes in on the left side. Flyer goes in then on the right, and then the car slowly begins to drift away. The scene fades to "The Cult Productions, © 1999", on a black screen.)
============================== Which Way To Hostile Takeover? Part 3 ============================== (The scene opens to the hustle and bustle of people in an airport. There are long lines for luggage checkin, as well, as sourvenior stories, crying mothers sending children to their grandparents, most likely. All in all, it seems to be a busy place. Ahead of the checkin line is High
Flyer. Flyer is wearing a Cult t-shirt, cut off at the shoulders, and black jeans, the same thing they were wearing this morning. Kate is over talking to a newspaper salesman, as she has on a tanktop and denim shorts, exactly what she was wearing this morning as well! Wow, they didn't change!(I didn't say what they were wearing in the last rp, whoops) Flyer puts the duffle bag on the scale as it is weighed. The scale says 32 pounds. The camera pans up to the attendant who is a very beautiful women. Let's just say, if she was on a piece of paper, she would be BURSTING off of it! Her face is very beautiful, maybe like a god, but yet, she is still working in an airport.)
Attendant:Is that all? Flyer:No, I've got two suitcases here. Attendant:I'm sorry, there is a limit of two bags per person, and one carryon. I see you have that, cosmetic kit...?
Flyer:Oh, oh, sorry, Kate, come over here. (Kate pays the man for the newspaper, and then walks over to Flyer. She hands the attendant the ticket, showing for proof.)
Attendant:Oh, I'm sorry, I didn't know. Well, here, put the suitcases up. (Flyer puts one up, and the scale reads 19 pounds even.) Attendant:You are not caring any food products out from Hawaii. No achohol, nothing? Flyer:Damnit. Yeah, I am. (Flyer goes over to one of his bags and pulls out a fine Hawaii delicacy, their beer. It's a special beer I'm guessing.)
Flyer:Sorry Al. (Flyer hands the beer over to the women, who sets it aside. Flyer then puts the other suitcase up, and then weights it, reading 25 pounds.)
Attendant:Is that all? flyer:Yeah it is. Attendant:Ok, your gate is 29. It's down that hallway, past the pizza hut, and then follow the signs once you get to the twenties hall way.
Flyer:Ok, thanx. (Flyer walks away as Kate is already down the hall.) Flyer:KATE, hold up. (Flyer catches up as they walk together.) Flyer:Well, only a few more hours. What, we've got about seven or eight hours till we get to Hostile Takeover? Our plane leaves at 1.
Kate:Yeah, but your forgot about the time zones. Really, it's more like five or six. Flyer:Yeah, don't get technical with me. We've got a little 2 hour, three hour plane ride, and then we're there. So really, that means I have two hours to get prepared for the match. Is that fair?
Kate:Not really, but I haven't heard a peep from Fugite yet. Flyer:How could you! We haven't even been anywhere close to a television in a few days! Since we left for Hawaii on, what was it, Tuesday, wednesday? I'm so lost! We haven't been able to watch him or the IWO for a few days now. He could be just going off on me for some odd reason, and I wouldn't be able to fight back with snappy little comebacks! I'm still wondering how I'm facing Fugite for Shack's television title.
Kate:Well, Fugite is still called champion, even though Shack got the belt from the masked man.
Flyer:Yeah, Yeah, don't get technical with me. (They find the gate, and follow through. They walk to the waiting area, take a seat, and just stare away, as if they aren't sure of what to do.)
Flyer:I just want to slap the taste out of Fugite's mouth. People at waiting area:SHHHHHH! Flyer:Man, I didn't know this was a library. Intercom:All passangers boarding flight 606 to (Distortion) please board now. (Flyer and Kate get up, with their carry on luggage, and go into the line.
