One Last Roleplay

15 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry. I forgot The Titan's roleplay. Here it is. (Fade In) (The camera pans into your typical wrestling gym. There weights and punching bags around and a wrestling ring in the center. There are two men going at in in the ring while there are a few crowded around. There is some loose equipment lying around on the floors. The camera pans over and into a tall man standing at 6'11". He's at a punching bag throwing lefts and right. He's wearing a grey undershirt and black wrestling pants. His back is soaked with his sweat. He stops and grabs a towel that's sitting on the bench next to him. He grabs it and wipes his forehead off. He throws the towel across his shoulders and walks over to the ring and watches the match.
He stands watching and absorbing while he tightens the tape on his wrists.) (One of the men in the ring hits a flying bulldog and makes the pin. The ref counts to 3 and he wins. The two get up and shake hands. Their both of a medium build and in typical gym ettire. The ref is also a wrestler. He goes to the ropes and looks out at the others.)
Ref: OK, now I want an opponent. (The tall man speaks up.) Man: Allow me. Ref: Come on in. We need a ref. (Another guy jumps up into the ring.) Man: Ready at anytime. Ref: OK. By the way I'm Jimmy. You? Man: The Titan. Jimmy: Alright then. Let's go. (The two men lock up. The Titan has a 7 inch advantage and uses it to whip him into the ropes. Titan lifts his boot but it's ducked. Jimmy comes off the other side and hits Titan with a cross body block. Titan goes down but gets right back up. They lock up again but Titan rams his knee into Jimmy's gut. Titan then lefts him high and drops him in a Gurilla Press. Titan bounces off the ropes and drops his leg down on his throut. Titan picks him up and whips him into the ropes. Jimmy comes off and hits a dropkick which shakes up Titan but doesn't knock him over. Jimmy quickly gets up but is met by a kick to the gut. Titan hooks his arms, lifts him up for a powerbomb, and drops down with it. The ref counts three. Titan gets up and helps Jimmy up. The leave the ring. Jimmy sits down while other people enter the ring.)
Jimmy: Damn that hurt. What was that? Titan: The Titan Bomb. Jimmy: Bomb's the right word. So you going in the professional leagues or something? Titan: Well I'm set to debut in the North American Wrestling Council against Sean DuBose on Fight Night Friday. I think he's a hockey player that took one too many hits and thinks he can wrestle.
Jimmy: (laughs) Hockey player! Ha! Well I'm sure you'll kick some ass. Titan: Yah, me too. Well I'll see ya around. Oh and try and watch your neck. Jimmy: Will do. (The Titan begins to walk away while talking.) Titan: See, now that's what I like. Someone one who isn't a sore looser. I guess I should formally introduce myself to the NAWC. I'm The Titan... your future World Heavyweight Champion. Yah that's right. Screw Tom Knight, I'm the one that's gonna conquer this federation. I start with Sean
DuBose on Friday. This is my debut match where everyone will get a taste of
The Titan's skill and power. Right now I'm just warming up for my match.
But as the weeks go by you'll see me take you places where you never thought possible. I'll introduce new matches into this federation that no one has ever seen before. It'll all come in due time. But I must leave you now.
My time is limited. I must go back to my training. Next time you see me though I'll be in the ring. One on one. Man to man with Sean DuBose. I think we both know what's gonna happen. Until then.
(The Titan walks off to the weight section as the camera fades out.)
