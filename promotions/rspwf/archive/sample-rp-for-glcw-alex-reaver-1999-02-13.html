[SAMPLE] RP for GLCW (Alex Reaver)

13 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey Guys, I just finished a rather long-winded RP, probably was too long, but oh well, it's for a Heavyweight title shot! Read it -- no, NOT a free agent, but go ahead and deconstruct it. I'd like to get some feedback for once! Oh, and
[name withheld], if you're reading this, stop now... you'll get a chance to see this on Latenight later in the week. If you don't stop, well, you're just a big cheater! :)
-Zach --- [Fade in: At the bottom of the screen, a caption reads: TAPED AFTER ALL-STARS OF WRESTLING, FEBRUARY 8TH. The shot is inside the dressing room of Alex Reaver, which is a modest locker room in Cobo Hall. It's rather genic... a bathroom stall with a shower stall is in the back right hand corner, a bench, a rack of dumb bells, and a full legnth mirror make up the furniture. A small TV monitor is suspended from the ceiling. Other than that, a gym bag lays on the bench and a jump rope is scattered on the floor. From the back of the room, off screen in the bathroom area, Alex Reaver emerges. He has changed out of his gear into the tearaway pants and hooded sweatshirt he wore into the arena.
Wrestling boots are replaced by a pair of Adidas sneakers. Alex's hair is soaking wet from a fast shower and slicked straight back, but a few strands go against that and fall near his eyes, which are covered by a pair of silver rimmed Oakley's. Reaver notices the camera rolling and gives a curious look, sitting down on the bench and messing with the zipper on his gym bag.]
AR: Wilczak sent you to interview me already? Man, I just finished taking Tommy James to the limit all but a few hours ago and I've already got a camera stuck in my face. Alright, fine, I do have some words I want to get out to the people. As far as Tommy James goes... well, I dared him to show me what he had, and tonight, he did so in spades. James, you didn't win, but you impressed me. First you came out and got on your soapbox, giving a pretty candid speech about all that's wrong about today's wrestling society. And that's a noble thing to do, standing up for your convictions. Trouble is, armbars and hammerlocks don't always get the job done. The thought was good, though, even if you did spit on the same people who cheer me. In the ring, you backed yourself up 99%... a win would've spoken louder, but it was damn close.
I've gotta give you props for that, James. I dared you to see if you had what it takes to handle the big dogs, and you can most definently run with the pack.
[smiles] Pretty scary thought, huh? Right now, I'm the alpha wolf of the GLCW. I've been on one helluva tear as of late, and I got to wonder what's next. I don't suppose you'd have any information on that, now would you, camera guy?
[There's a slight pause. Finally, a gruff voice speaks.] CAMERA GUY (o.s.): That's why I was asked to get your comments, Reaver. With Sampson and Masters fired, there's a title vacancy. Wilczak and the rest are holding a press confrence shortly, but they wanted to alert you before hand to give you enough time to train.
AR: Train for what? CAMERA GUY (o.s.): Your Great Lakes title shot, sir. Two weeks from this evening, GLCW Centre in Ann Arbor, you and Dave Diamond.
[Pause. Alex stares into the camera, letting it sink it. His confused look slowly changes to an unbelievably happy look. Reaver's eyes light up, and he pumps his fist in the air.]
AR: So this is where it all leads up, huh? After months of trying to get my head straight after the disaster that was the end of my time in Newton, the battle with the pain killers, all the weeks searching for an indentify and a place to feel at home. Finally, I've gotten that, and now I get my reward... a shot at the Heavyweight title. I can live without it, but this means a great deal to me. When Masters held it, I discarded my #1 contendership because I had already beaten him. Why climb a mountain when you've already been to the peak? Now, it's a whole new mountain... and a chance to give the GLCW the stability it so desperately needs. Lestat fled with the belt, Masters got fired with it. Now I'm being given the shot to give the GLCW a champion it can respect. I might not have been here in the beginning... hell, I wasn't even around until October... but since the day I have signed, I have dedicated myself to being the best I could and making GLCW feel as right as possible.
Now I know for sure... this is where I belong, inside those ropes, fighting for a shot at glory.
It sends chills down my spine... the chance to be called 'champ.' At another place in another time, I had that privledge. It was perhaps the greatest moment in my life, a life that hasn't been full of too many sunshine daydreams.
But I had my time in the sun. The shadows grew dimmer since then, but these past few weeks, the light's slowly been creeping back. In two weeks, I can reclaim my long-lost glory. There'll be no question in anybody's mind at my credibility, nor any question on whether I hit my peak too soon and burnt out.
Well, the last time I had a belt across my waist, I was young and foolish. Today, I'm still still young and folish, but I've matured. I've grown. I have evolved, if you will.
Now, perhaps it's time to come full circle to that time when everything clicked and fell into place. For so long, I've questioned my place in the GLCW, in wrestling in general. Reaching that apex of championship glory will answer all those doubts. You see, nobody wants to be a loser... we all have our aspirations of raising a belt high and heading thousands of people react; it's an opportunity to be the most influential man in the promotion. I'd be lying if I told you I wouldn't _love_ to wear that Great Lakes belt.
[Alex pauses, trying to catch his breath as his rattlings have gotten him excited. He breaths deeply, running a hand over his mouth.]
AR: Winning this particular match isn't die or die, ya know. I mean, yeah, I just went off on how much the title means, but there will be other nights, other shots, other opportunities. Losing to Diamond isn't the end of the world. I'll survive; I always do. I can climb back to the top of contention again... it ain't easy, but what is? I did it once, I'll do it again if needbe. But, if I can get it done on the first try, well that's great. I'm gunning for it, you can count on that. Now, I don't really have a grudge with
Dave Diamond. In fact, I know next to nothing about the man. But I know enough. I know that he's all that stands between me and the title. I know that he's the one that wants to deny me what I deserve. I know that he's always relied on his little clique, Hollywood After Dark. Apparently after tonight, the group's got problems, but I'm sure he'll have some right-hand man or lackey with him by February 22nd. Dave, I know you fancy yourself a big-city lights kind of guy. You're at least ten years my senior; you've got more expirience.
You know every way to cheat and get away with it. You'd like to believe you're some kind of hotshot, and I'd like to believe that you're so friggin' elite...
'Fraid I can't, not yet. I don't give a s[BLEEP] what you did in the ring elsewhere or against other guys in the GLCW. It don't mean jack to me until you step in the ring and kick my ass all over the place. 'Til then, don't expect me to pay any heed to your warnings or rants these next two weeks. It's all talk, I want to see action. You're probably questioning me right now...
here's some punk kid, not even legally able to drink, who's been in the federation for four months and thinks he owns the place. Well, maybe it is stupid for me to dare you like I dared Tommy James... fact is, though, I'm usually pretty good about this kind of stuff. Yes, there are some major differences between us. No, I don't have the kind of expirience you do, nor money, nor friends, nor connections.
What _do_ I have? Survival instincts. Rough edges that make me that more dangerous. Heart. I don't need people watching my back, unlike you. I don't need some imagined Hollywood fast life to make myself feel more important, unlike you. I don't need the glitz and glamor you strive on. I'm a simple man, Dave... I'm just a wrestler. I don't really have anything else to do with my life. You? When you retire, you'll buy some forgein island or do porn films, something like that. When I retire? They'll lower my body into my grave. Get the point?
[Reaver pauses once more, rubbing his forehead where skin met concrete earlier in the evening. He reaches down and slings the gym bag over his shoulder, eyeing the camera.]
AR: There ain't much else to say, not yet. You can rest assured I'll run my trap plenty more before it's said and done, but it's been a long day and I want to go. So I'll leave you with this final statement, Dave...
Saddle up, bud, you're not in Tinsletown anymore. Come February 22nd, you're gonna visit a place south of Heaven, west of Hell... the GLCW Center in a ring across from yours truly. Lucky you... I'm gonna be your own personal tour guide.
Enjoy the trip. [With a slightly disturbing laugh, Reaver walks away from the camera and exits the room. The camera flickers for a moment, rolling in the empty locker room for a split second. Then, fade to black.]
