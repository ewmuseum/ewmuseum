[UEW] RP for William O'Leary {Critique Please}

30 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please Critique: [Scene takes place as William O’Leary, the “Servant” of UEW, is seen standing in front of a large building in Leesville, Louisiana. The building is made of cement blocks that are painted gray to look a little more fashionable and stylish. The building sits on a small grass lot between two intersections.
The sky is gray and the air is filled with high humidity and drizzling rain. Every so often, a light wind/breeze flows through the air. The tree’s limbs and branches sway with the direction of the wind. The lot sits down on a hill. Both intersections are filled with cars and other motor vehicles.
William O’Leary is dressed in his usual, sloppy style. He is wearing a pair of old, dirt covered shoes. The dirt is smeared where the water has flowed down the shoes. He is wearing black wind pants. The knees are faded from black to white where they have either been washed a lot or have been slid across the ground. With his faded wind pants, he is wearing a white
Budweiser shirt. On the front, it says Budweiser across the breastbone. Over the shirt is a yellow rain coat. The coat has dirt on it also. The coat is open so you can see the front of his shirt.
As the camera begins to focus mainly on William O’Leary, he turns and starts walking towards the building. As he walks, a small sign comes into view. It says “Vernon Parish Police Station.” As he continues to walk, he slowly begins to shift to his right. The doors of the police station come into view, as well as a wooden bench beside the doors. O’Leary walks to the bench and sits down. The bench is under a shelter so that the rain is blocked.]
William O’Leary: Hmm….. This place is __VERY__ familiar. I’ve been here so many times…. I don’t even think I can count the times on two hands.
[William O’Leary broadens a smile across his dirty face.] William O’Leary: But… I haven’t been here in quite a while now. Believe it or not, I haven’t drunk in two hours! To many people, that isn’t long at all, but for me, that is a __VERY__ long time. I usually drink a twelve pack to a case per day. I know it’s a lot, but at least I don’t smoke pot or weed.
[William O’Leary smiles once again.] William O’Leary: So, since I haven’t been to this joint in a while, I thought
I’d come by. I mean, this was like a second home to me until about six months ago. Six months ago is when I got into wrestling. I started training and doing other stuff. Of course, I didn’t have enough money to go to a gym or anything, so I would just walk around Leesvile and Fort Polk, lift rocks, and do other __FREE__ stuff.
[Willaim O’Leary bends over and picks up a large rock. He puts it in his right arm and starts doing repetitions.]
William O’Leary: Of course, it isn’t as good or better than going to the gym, but it gets the job done… and it’s free! I mean, you can’t say it doesn’t work. Look at me.
[William O’Leary flexes his right arm. His bicep resembles a rock.] William O’Leary: That is what it has done to me. So, just think of what it could do to you.
[Suddenly, the glass doors to the right of the bench open. Two large men walk out. Both are dressed in navy blue police uniforms. The men walk right by
William O’Leary and pay no attention whatsoever to him.]
William O’Leary: Whoa! Those two guys look like Prince Ju Ju’s bodyguards from a few weeks ago! I’m sure they aren’t, but they sure looked like them.
Well, as I was say…. [William O’Leary puts his balled fist under his chin. He lifts his hand.] William O’Leary: Prince Ju Ju is in Fight to the Finish with me! What better time to talk about his rich ass. Prince Ju Ju, I am still surprised at what you did a few weeks ago. I mean, how many people come up to you and offer to serve you for free? I seriously doubt that there have been many if any. You were the first person to have a chance to be served by me who is known as the servant of UEW. Yes, I am a sorry piece of trash, but that doesn’t mean I can’t serve. That is what I have been raised to do.
But… since you did not accept it, that means that I can come into Fight to the
Finish with no master. In other words, I can just come in and whip everyone's ass. Yes, it will be my first decent match, but that doesn’t mean anything to me and shouldn’t mean anything to you. Those first two matches are just warm- ups to the __REAL__ debut of William O’Leary.
[Suddenly, a huge sheet of rain flies in sideways. William O’Leary ducks his head.] William O’Leary: That reminds me of another thing I can relate to this Fight to the Finish match. It also comes from my jail days. Anyway, I was once in a jail cell. I had been brought in earlier that day for D.U.I(Driving Under the Influence). It was back when I still lived with Major Alstadt. I was boring his car and drove up to a bar in Leesville. It is all so clear. It was Las Margaritas. Well, I had a few too many drinks… compared to their standard. So, they pulled me over and took me in. When I got there, they threw me in this cell with 3 __HUGE__ guys. I mean, they all had to weigh at least 400 pounds. Anyway, I made sure they didn’t mess with me.
That is just like what’s gonna happen at Fight to the Finish. I’ll sit back and relax, but as soon as someone comes near me, I’m gonna teach ‘em a little lesson. Believe me, you don’t want to be the learner in that situation.
I’ve been thinking of how and what I should use to get people out of the ring, and I think I have come up with the perfect solution…
S E R V A N T S S U P L E X!!!! Yes, that is exactly what I’ll do. First I’ll knock someone out. Then I’ll lift them up and nail a Servants Suplex. I won’t drop them in the ring tough. I’ll drop them on the outside concrete. They might not be dropped on their arm like an __ORIGINAL__ Servants Suplex, but they’ll be out alright.
You know, I really don’t have anything against anyone in the UEW except for
Prince Ju Ju. Of course, that is now. When I find a master, I will have something against __EVERY SINGLE__ person in UEW. That means I will be allies with __NO ONE__ that my master isn’t allies with. So, if I have a master by
Fight to the Finish, it will __REALLY__ be on. If someone helps me, good for me… but don’t expect me to help you. I’ll just whip your ass along with many others.
So, I’m going to end this interview with something very true… UEW, Punishment will be…. S E R V E D -----------== Posted via Deja News, The Discussion Network ==---------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Discuss, or Start Your Own
