HISTORY: The Neutralizers Started in 1997, in UIWF. The original members were Scorpion, Law, Black Bear, Wolf, and The Neutralizer. As time went on, the 2nd Generation of Neutralizers started, in the UWF. In that time the Neutralizers were a very top notch stable. Soon the 3rd Generation of Neutralzers were born in IWA. Finaly the 4th Generation was born in AWA, this year. Scorpion has been the leader of the Neutralizers for 2 years. WHO ARE THE NEUTRALIZERS: The Neutralizers are a top notch Stable that specializes in high rping. Generation, by Generation we got better. NOW we are the best. WHAT ARE WE LOOKING FOR: -RP Board prefered. -Open for a while. NO NEW FEDS. -2 cards a week. -Large roster prefered.
