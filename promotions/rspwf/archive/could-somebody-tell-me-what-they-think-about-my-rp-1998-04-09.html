Could somebody tell me what they think about my RP?

9 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
:::The scene opens up in a gym where there are a bunch of guys training. In the background, you see men who are lifting vast quantities of weight. Out of the locker room comes a fairly built man with short reddish brown hair. He isn't wearing a shirt, but is wearing a pair blue Nike mesh shorts, and a pair of sneakers. He sits down and is about to start lifting when he notices the
camera::: The Machine: For those at home who don't know who I am, I am the newest force in the MWA, The Machine. In the following interview, I am going to talk about my history...not in wrestling...but in life, my present state in MWA, and then my future in the MWA. First, my past.
:::The Machine looks deep into the camera and rubs his left bicep::: The Machine: Ok, I was born Trenton, New Jersey on March 15, 1973. I grew up in a small town called Florence and then went on to go to Providence College.
While playing ice hockey there for the Friars, I was scouted by several professional teams. But, they would always say that I wasn't tall enough. I'm
5' 11", but that isn't tall enough for the NHL...then I got a deal from a wrestling federation. At the age of 18...I went to wrestle and learned from the greats. Yada yada yada, I showed up on Sunday in the MWA and I make my debut this weekend.
:::The Machine gets up and walks over to the gym's ice box. He pulls out a
Gatorade and drinks it down. He looks refreshed, recycles the bottle, and then sits down on the bench again:::
The Machine: Right now, I am in the Missouri Wrestling Alliance. I went to
Sunday's show and watched the talent first hand, but I have been following the
MWA since the beginning. I know the wrestlers and I know their tricks. But now I would like to address this match this weekend with Jake Steed. I have never heard of the man and from his record he appears to be knew to the MWA too. Now, this match should be a victory for me, but he'll be psyched up for it because it could start his career. I know what happens when your over confident and showboat and that's why I focus in on the match. Steed, prepare to face one of the best wrestlers in the NWC.
Now, what is my future in the MWA? Who's side am I on? What is my alliance? Well, nobody knows what the future holds. Currently, I am by myself in this league. I don't think that will last for long, but who knows? As soon as I can go to the future, I will tell you everything that is going to happen in my future. Until then, don't hold your breath.
:::The Machine rubs the top of his short hair with is hand and continues speaking::: The Machine: Actually, I have one more subject to discuss and that is the
Missouri State Heavyweight Title. Mr. Sinister, I just want to get one thing straight, I don't like you. I have seen what you've been pulling the past few weeks and I think it sticks. You might think that you're so bad and that you're so tough because you can jump people from behind...but you're not. As soon as I get a few wins and make a name for myself, I am going to take that title away from you and beat you down because you deserve it.
:::screen fades to black::: The Machine- a person or organization tha resembles a machine (as in being methodical, tireless, or unemotional)
Could you please tell me what you think about my RP? This includes you too
[name withheld].
Punks like you are a dime a dozen. "Mean" Mark Green [NWC](BAWF) The Machine [NWC](MWA)
