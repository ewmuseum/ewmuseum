David "Despair" Zane opening RP

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[[The scene fades in at a art museum with a man staring at the screamer. He continuously stares at it with a grin. He seems to be over 6'6" and weights over 200 pounds. His hair is short black hair with a strip of blond hair. He turns to the camera and with a hand motion tells it to come foward. He turns his attention back to the painting and continues to stare]]
[Man] Lovely isn't it. [[Turns to the camera]] The painting I mean. How he... [[goes back to the painting]] screams out. As if he is in agony. That he lost all hope of life. That his dreams, hopes, and fantasies are now....[[makes a puff of air]] Gone. That he is in utter and complete... Despair.
[[Goes to the bench and sits down. He slaps his forehead and shakes his head]] [Man] Where are my manners? I'm sorry I don't believe we are introduced, I am
David Zane. But my colleagues call me DDZ. David "Despair" Zane. I am here to introduce to this federation, hell, mayhem, chaos, destruction, and most of all.... despair. Ah yes, despair. The only thing that sees all people as equals. Man, woman, or child, old or young. Rich or poor. It doesn't matter to it's eyes. It sees all in need of it's presence. It's gift of bringing reality into these little people's lives. I am here to show you simplitons that hope is nothing more than a mere fantasy. An illiusion which you people are too afraid to wake up from and see the nightmare you have created. When I was giving my graduation speech in my class I have warned them not their chance of getting their dream job is not existiant for this I was almost killed. I am a messenger of truth which you people are to afraid to hear and thus must reject the messenger. But this time I will not be rejected. I will make you see the truth now.
[[He stands up and walks to "The Screamer" again]] [DDZ] See this? This man who painted this was able to see despair. He was able to paint it for all to see and has become a popular piece of art. Because it holds truth. That [[in a disguised voice]] hopes and dreams [[back to his normal voice]] is nothing more than a dream. People and their dreams [[scoffs]]
it disguists me. How we are to be supposed to be the most intelligent form of life on this planet and we can't even see the cliff we are walking to. But thank the gods that I am here to prevent everyone from drowning to death in the sea of false hopes. That I shall help them see the reality of the world. That
Despair is the only friend we HAVE to rely on. Because it will never betray us.
Despair will never abandon us. It will never LIE to us and cheat us out of life as many times as "hopes" and "dreams" have on so many occasions. But I am prepared to face people who refuse to learn the truth. And with making these preparations it will be only a matter of time before I open each one's eyes.
Beware everyone..... watch your step... for you will fall into the pit....of..... Despair.
