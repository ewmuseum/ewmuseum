Stephen Parker RolePlay

10 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings to all of those who chose to read this. Please give me some advice on my roleplays. I think, least in my opinion, that I am average or above.
I just want to take it to the next level where I can make the Top 200 List. By the way, this isn't my most recent post, to read that, head on over to http://bsw.virtualave.net/ .and here is the roleplay: Subject: Go Kill A Hamster Name: "Sensational" Stephen Parker Federation: BlueGrass State Wrestling (A NWC Region) Body:          [Fade up.]         "What's in a name? That which we call a rose, by any other name would smell as sweet."         [Eerie pause.]         "That which we call a lose, by any other name would taste as sour."         [Deep Breath.]         "Congratulations Jerome, my friend, you defeated me. Fair and square, though, an insect by the name of Stone screwed
      up what I had planned, you still won Jerome. I hope your victory sits as well with you as this loss has sat with me. Losing       is agony, I pray to never feel it again. And Jerome, you never know when a strike of vengeance will hit you but
Jerome,       you will know who it's from. Jerome, I will never lose to you again, and that blasted National Wrestling Council
Title will       be mine someday. I guess that our meeting wasn't my time, not quite yet."         [And now we fade up. The dark words of "Sensational" Stephen Parker still ring in our ears? He has gone to       Shakespeare for material now? Not something the irrational Parker would have done. Any ways, we start to fade up and
      it isn't exactly bright where we are at. The dark night is filling the screen with an eerie purple and red mixture and
      Stephen seems to be walking the streets alone, at night, in Kentucky. A man in sadness and engulfed by sorrow, maybe.]
        "And thank you Bryan Taylor. Thank you very much you ungrateful bastard. BlueGrass State Wrestling was failing
      miserably Bryan and you needed me to step up. I did, I did what you said. I tagged with Victor Valentine because you
      promised me the National Wrestling Council Tag Title shots, by hook or crook. I then went after the main shot, the singles       World Title shot and by crook, I got it. The big bastard didn't have a shot in hell against anyone unless he was coming off
      a month rest and he didn't have the time to prepare for Jenkins.         Who did you need to face Jenkins, Bryan? You told me to face him. You gave me the shot. A shot I happily took. But       then, you springed on me the fact that the Prime Time Players were involved in a Family Fatality match at Friday
Fury. A
      match I never wanted to be in, but Bryan, because you asked, I tried my damnedest to win it. I fell short to a monster of
      a man named Atticus De Lane but on my way to the final minutes of the match I eliminated Mr. Sinister. A man
I have a
      few cross words to speak to in a moment.         But that wasn't good enough, was it Bryan. Because I didn't do exactly as you wished, because my partners f[bleep]ed       up, I ended up in the doghouse, right? Of course, you fired the slacking Mimic and the born loser, Vic, and proceeded to       suspend me. All that I understand, Bryan, you suspended me because your attempt at past glory failed. Oh well, life goes
      on. But then I received a notice, I was facing Mr. Sinister, Andrew Pierce, Ahmed Reed for 100,000 dollars.
Sadly, the       loser is forced, by Mr. Schukar, to leave the National Wrestling Council, his Wrestling Council. Funny, I thought I was
      suspended Bryan. Sucks when the biggest dog goes over your head, doesn't it."         [We can now see exactly what Stephen is doing. Stephen, dressed in a long, brown, new trench coat is walking forward.
      The walk is slow and the sky is dark. The Kentucky night seems to devour everything behind Parker as he walks towards       the camera. The coat is wrapped around his body and his hair is combed nicely forward. The diamond stud in his right ear
      flickers underneath the street lights of the lonely Kentucky street. A nonchalant smirk covers Parker's face.]
        "That all brings me to 'No Mercy' this week. I get the task of facing Andrew Pierce, Ahmed Reed and Mr. Sinister.
All       men that at one time were apart of an Upper Echelon. Oh yes, I have done my homework already. Mr. Ahmed Reed       was apart of the Original Upper Echelon, not the phony second version. As we all saw with the Prime Time Players
      2000, the second copy is never quite up to the standards of the original genius. Though, that is being mighty nice to the
      original Prime Time Players.         Any ways, Ahmed, lately, you have been on a tear. Interfering in any match you wish. You even had a run in with
      Hannibal Stone. Good for you Ahmed. Least you have something that will soften the blow when you don't receive that       cash from the Executive Council. Ahmed, you are the dark horse in this match. The underdog, but I am the same.
You       see Ahmed, I shall stray away from insulting your mistakes and defections because in that ring, you may be my only help.
      As we both know, Ahmed, Pierce and Mr. Sinister are in the same faction and that only spells trouble for us.         Don't take this the wrong way though, Ahmed. I don't trust you. I wouldn't hand over my first born into your insane arms       and I wouldn't risk my body to save you from a monumental beat down. If Pierce and Mr. Sinister decide they want to
      waste their time and energy on you, I will stand back and watch and that is the god d[bleep]ed truth. But Ahmed, I       expect that if they decide to go after us both at once, that you will run across the ring and purposely hit me.
For, Ahmed,       you don't want me to do the same. Trust me, you don't want me to take out this wrath and frustration on you. For hell is a
      worse price to pay than the angry that flows through my pissed veins."         [Stephen continues to move forward, giving a sigh.]         "Mr. Sinister, do you remember Friday Fury. Do you remember what happen in that ring? Of course I expect a       pantywaist like you to have some excuse for why I eliminated you and Michael Bold. Was it Bold's momentum that
      carried you over the top rope right in front of your friend Tony. Or was it pure luck? But never mind, I expect you now,
      after hearing my comments, to come out and commend me. Isn't that what Jenkins tried to do? Live and learn Sinister,
      live and learn.         I must say Sinister, I respect you. Your a National Wrestling Council Champion, a former Champion, but a Champion at
      that. I failed at my attempt to get the gold. I couldn't gain that ever important victory over your stalemate
Jerome Jenkins.
      Just more reason for me to take that cash and run. Now that Bryan Taylor isn't pumping money into me, I might have to
      start earning my stuff. Ah well, [sarcastic] never did that before, right?         From Michigan to Missouri to Texas, you have been everywhere, right Sinister. Or is my order screwed up there.
It       seems, least of the first two counts, that when a region of the National Wrestling Council became strong, you ran away.
      Go ahead and defend yourself but you, in your cold, black heart, know the truth about the matter. I won't burrow any
      deeper into the mystery of Sinister, but Mr. Sinister, remember, though this ain't Texas. You don't have to worry about a
      'B[bleep] in Britton or a Piss ant in Raymond Pierce but in Kentucky, Stephen Parker won't back down."         [Parker sighs again. This scene isn't much to look at but hearing Stephen speak again is enough to keep watching, right.]
        "And lastly, we have everyone's favorite little brother, Andrew Pierce. "The Hurricane"'s nickname is almost as snappy
      as my "Sensational" moniker. I was hoping to have a long list of snappy insults and quick comebacks but Andrew, your a
      man that I would look up too. I would if you weren't such a sniveling bastard. And no, that isn't the literal sense of the
      word, I am using it as an insult. Andrew, I honestly don't have much to say to you. I was hoping for a five minute speech       on your short comings but just as Mr. Sinister, I am not as experienced as you. I haven't dealt with you before. Though, I       promise, after 'No Mercy', your going to know exactly why wrestling me brings about the phrase...
        "Ain't Life a Bitch".         Sleep tight Andrew. You too Ahmed. Mr. Sinister, go kill a hamster.         [Fade to black.] END [name withheld] -Handler of "Terrific" Tomas Bailey -Handler of "Sensational" Stephen Parker -Wants to be on the RSPWF Top 200 List
