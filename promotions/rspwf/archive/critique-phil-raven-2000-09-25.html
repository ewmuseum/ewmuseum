(Critique) Phil Raven

25 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens with pure blackness, nothing in sight. Slowly... EVER so slowly, the lighting rises, bringing into view a man sitting on a locker room bench. He's a solid individual, although nothing on his body is too over developed muscular-wise. He seems to be busy preparing himself for a match, already in his tights. They're navy blue, and down the leg is the word
"BRUTAL" embroidered in white, jagged lettering. His face remains down, his gaze on his boots. He begins to speak in a low, yet sincere tone.]
"They say that you just can't keep some people away from what they love ..." [The man continues to concentrate on tying his wrestling boot, almost as if he doesn't know the camera is there.]
"After I retired over a year ago, I thought for sure that I'd never come back again to this crazy sport."
[He finishes the knot and glances up at the camera, cracking a small smile. The man is none other than "The Could've Been, But Wasn't" legend himself,
"Brutal" Brian Floyd. ]
"All the bumps I took, all the tables I broke ..." [Brian stands up and begins to put his street clothes into his duffel bag.] "Only an idiot would want to come back to something that almost ended his life." [He finishes packing his duffel bag and puts it in his locker, shutting the door with a loud clang. He turns to the camera and shrugs matter of factly.]
"And I guess that's what I am, an idiot! The ring has been calling to me for months upon months. I tried to ignore its enticing beckonings but they all sounded too good to resist."
[Brian cracks his knuckles and gives his trademark half cocky, half crazy smile.] "I mean, I'm at HOME here! The sweat, the blood, the agony, the gold ..." [Brian suddenly becomes animated with his hands, gesturing to himself.] "This is me... "BRUTAL" Brian Floyd! I was born to wrestle, so it's what I do. I can't change that!' [He suddenly stops. His tone lowers and he gets up close and personal with the camera, only inches from its lense.]
"And like they ALSO say, if you're born to do something... you often times DIE doing it..."
[The trademark smile flashes again, and the lighting begins to fade out as you see Brian's face grow fainter and fainter.]
"With that said... I'm back, boys. I'm back." [Still shot on Brian's face as the camera fades out to complete blackness.]
