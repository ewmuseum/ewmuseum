Neko roleplay: every bad roleplay element thrown into one RP! Yeah! :D

25 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The fancy Lier jet touches down upon the tarmac at Skyharbor Airport in
Phoenix Arizona. It taxis toward the terminal as Neko sits inside, dressed in an expensive fine-tailored outfit, typing upon her state of the art lap-top computer (because she is inhumanly rich..or something). Her eye shifts back and forth, as she wonders just what the hell she is doing there.
The stewardess approaches and says; "We've arrived Ms.Daisuke. You can unbuckle your seatbelt now." Neko stands akwardly, not used to the fancy-ass clothes and hands the laptop to the stewardess.
"Uh..okay." Neko says slowly, still looking around. "Here, you can have this. I hate these computer thingies."
The stewardess smiles as Neko walks by. Then, as Neko's back is turned, the stewardess smashes her over the head repeatedly with the laptop computer. "Aow, Quiddit!! Dammit, that hurt!" Neko cries and yanks the machine away. "Geez..some people's kids..."
Neko gets off the plan, rubbing her head and walking toward the terminal. Suddenly four ninjas leap from behind a luggage cart and surround Neko. "PREPARE TO DEFEND YOURSELF NEKO!" says the first one. "Ahh! The so-called
_DRAGON_!" says the second. "NOW YOU WILL *DIE*!" says the third. (the fourth remains silent because I can't come up with any dialogue for him)
Neko looks back and forth at each ninja with an expression that can only be described as "what the f*ck?!"
"Umm..okay. But why are talking like that? You know...all wierd like that?" The first ninja does a power-ranger style kata and shouts; "WE ARE BADLY
DUBBED! WE ARE SUPPOSED TO TALK LIKE THIS!" The second ninja does a similar routine and yells "YES! NOW, YOU WILL _*DIEEEEEEEE*_!!!!!!!!!!!!!"
Neko ducks the flying exclamation points and bashes the closest ninja over the head with what's left of the lap-top and then uses him as a shield against the other three ninjas. Quickly she dispatches them because, as you know, she's one bad mutha...
Neko continues on her way, leaving the ninjas behind and wondering, "What is going on? This is like some bad jobber's roleplay. The bookers must hate me. I guess that's what I get for being the People's Champ. I wonder what Steve Austin and Hulk Hogan would do in a spot like this?"
Suddenly a mob of Janet Munez's fans rush up to Neko brandishing pitchforks and other assorted utincils! "Neko! You fuckin suck you goddamn sack of fuckin shit! Die bitch!" Neko blinks and throws a 'censored' sign at the shouting fan. The fan tries to shout, but it comes out as "Hey, what the F*ck?! D*mmit...you can't do this to us you sh*t."
Neko grins. "Much better. You know..um...Janet Munez retired from the EFW without even wrestling me, and..um...I was the LEWA world champ several times, and I can sing. Plus I have this nifty eyepatch!" (gameshow host smile)
The rabid fans suddenly ooh and ahh. "Wow Neko, you're cool! We want to be your fans now! Can we, please???"
Neko smiles. "Oh allright. It's always nice to win new fans." Neko continues on her way. As she leaves the airport, she is suddenly innundated by people, men and women alike (and they all look like models, of course), all screaming "We Love you Neko! We want to make love to you.
Ohh..you're so sexy! We wanna show you a good time!" ect ect... Neko shrugs and looks at the camera. "Hey, what can I say? The fans love me." She winks an a star appears, making a "ping!" sound.
