Jaysin Jarrend looking for fed to join.

8 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[In a posh hotel in Las Vegas, Nevada a press conference is under way. A crowd of International wrestling reporters are gathered as well as an I.W.C.
reporting crew for this special event. Everyone is milling around in anticipation, when a lady appears and approaches the podium.]
[The lady is known as Renee Ramirez and she in known world wide as the valet of "The Narcissist" Jaysin Jarrend. She is dressed in a full length blue sequined dress, her brown hair is tied up in a bun and she is smiling with the news she is about to present. Now...Let's here this interview.]
Renee Remirez: It is with extreme pleasure that I announce the signing of myself and "The Narcissist" have signed a multi-million dollar contract with the IWC. We look for great things to happen for us in this organization. And now...Without further adieu, I introduce Jaysin Jarrend.
[A side door opens and in walks the man known as "The Narcissist." He is wearing a simple three piece suit with black leather shoes. With his gold rolex shining from the lights above, he runs his rights hand through his long blonde locks as he approaches the podium. At 6' tall, he is a very well rounded man and one can see why he truly is in love with himself. He begins to take his place and to speak.]
J.J. It doesn't take a rocket scientist to see that the I.W.C. has made the best decission with signing myself and Renee. I mean when you look at this magnificent specimen and see that I am truly the very best looking man walking
God's green earth, there truly can be no question as to why the I.W.C. wants me. As I look down the distinguished list of contenders and pretenders, I can truly say that it shall not be long before I am wearing the gold here.
R. R. What we have here is the best athelete in the world today. I mean he has superstar written all over him. We are looking forward indeed to showcasing Mr. Jarrend's talents and to show the world that the future in wrestling is with "The Narcissist."
J.J. At this time, I will take a few questions from the press. [Reporter 1 waives his hand and Jarrends signals for his question.] Rep. 1 What makes you think that you can walk into the I.W.C. and automatically think that you will wear championship gold?
J.J. Well, it is like this my dear sir. Not just because I have the best looking body in the world. I come from a wrestling background. I was raised on it and I have lived and breathed this sport since age 5. I have great scientific skills as do I have a great aerial repotoire. So, it is with these skills that I make this claim. Besides, I've seen the I.W.C. Roster. Their
I.W.C. Women's Champion is a better athelete than their top 5 wrestlers, so what does this tell you. Next question.
Rep. 2 Renee, what is the relationship between you and Mr. Jarrend? [Renee looks to the "Narcissist" and reples.] R.R. That is of no concern to you. Next! Rep. 1 Mr. Jaysin, whom do you wish to face first? [Jarrend smiles and replies.] J.J. Justin Blade would be a great first match. However, it really doesn't matter who it is I face first. My intentions are perfectly clear. I will meet and I shall defeat all whom may accept the challenge. So you see, it really doesn't matter whom I face. I am the best and I will say this..."I will be the future I.W.C. Champion and that is all I have to say." Now if you will excuse me, Ms. Remirez and I have a plane to catch.
[With that, "The Narcissist" and Renee Ramirez exit the podium and out the side door leaving the multitude of reporters stunned.]
