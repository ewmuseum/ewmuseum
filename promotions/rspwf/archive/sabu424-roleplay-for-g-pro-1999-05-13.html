now i am an official g-pro wrestler (see sig for details) this is my first roleplay. i want to feud for the title please.
sabu424 is walking down the street hanging cool with his friends the rock, mideon and K-dogg.
k-dogg - yo yo yo let me speak on this sabu424 man sabu424 - speak K-dogg k-dogg - now we are part of the g-pro federation we have got to go for the title. the rock - shut your mouth and know your role k-dogg k-dogg looks pissed at the rock mideon makes a star shape with his hands the rock - you are going to the G-pro to lay the smackdown on the wrasslers there.
sabu424 - yes they all suck shit through an old sock. k-dogg - yo yo yo the g-pro lacks any good wrestlers now fletch has let you in there is one class act.
sabu424 - what do you say mideon my tag team partner. mideon - Zokugun Sangai, i challenge you both to a mideons rules match. no countout no disqualification and to win you have to put your opponent through a table.
they all laugh like devils. the rock - Kashan Akuma and Frazer Fury you need to feel the smackdown lain on you and lain badly.
k-dogg - arriba la raza. sabu424 - all of you wrestlers in g-pro need to watch your backs as sabu424 and the sabu424 green streets posse, my main man k-dogg, the rock and mideon are now a part of hte g-pro and we came here for the gold and the gold alone.
k-dogg - hey let's look at the roster, what do you think of Toshiaki "Pyro" Takada sabu424 - sucks shit through an old sock they all laugh like demons mideon - what about Seijin Banpaia. sabu424 - a jobber like shawn parker they all laugh like dragons the rock - are you gonna lay the smack down on Jisatsu sabu424 - he sucks dick and should not be here but pimping his ass for money for crack.
they all laugh like furies k-dogg - and what about "Blood Angel"Simon Ezra, sabu424 - who k-dogg - here he is ezra is walking down the street. sabu424 gives chase as the rock, mideon and k-dogg chase after him.
