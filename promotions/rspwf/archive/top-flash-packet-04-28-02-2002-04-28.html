[Rick Taylor & Kiyoshi Sakai] 3 - HERO Ishikawa 4 - Mike Johnson 5 - Keiji Kobe 6 - BRAT Dragon Mitsu 7 - Takayuki Morita 8 - Yoshikazu TENZAN ============= DAVID BISHOP ============= [The shot opens up to a clip of TATAMI 1 "Poison Fists of the Pacific Rim" and the match between David Bishop and Freestyle/Shooto expert Nahoa Tanimura. Tanimura shoots in for a single leg takedown on Bishop, who sprawls and delivers a massive elbow to the back of Tanimura and then a knee to the face. You can here the fans buzz with excitement. Bishop delivers a second knee]
Keisuke Oyanami: Tanimura is staggered. Huge left hook by Bishop connects, and now a right. Tanimura is down! Tanimura is down for the second time in five minutes. Hyosuke Eda steps in-between the fighters and calls for the bell.
Eiji Sekozawa: We knew Bishop was a wrestler, but it seems he's a boxer as well. He levels Tanimura with a good overhand right, and I think his nose might be broken.
Keisuke Oyanami: We'll have to ask Tanimura when he wakes up. [The shot cuts to the closing moments of the TATAMI II press conference. An argument between Wayne "I'M COOL" Coolidge, and Harry Gotch, trainer of
David Bishop. Gotch's words are inaudible over the bustle of reporter's leaping from their chairs, as well as Bishop's bellowing. Oyanami, plus former Japanese Olympic silver medalist in Judo (and TATAMI color man) Eiji
Sekozawa do their best to quell the melee, each man attempting to hold back
Bishop and Coolidge,
respectively. Gotch has to also help Sekozawa in order to subdue the enormous Bishop The media are having a field day, climbing over each other to get the best angle for a photo, when Coolidge tosses his entire smoothie in the vicinity of Bishop and Gotch, blanketing nearly everyone in a 4 sq. meter circumference with pureed fruit. Complete chaos breaks loose as Bishop bowls over both Sekozawa and Oyanami to lunge at Coolidge.
But the fleet-footed middleweight boxer is able to dodge and reel off two lightning-quick jabs into the eye of the behemoth shooter before getting wrapped up from behind by his seconds and strangely enough, his scheduled opponent for tomorrow night, Minoru Yamakawa. Then it literally takes Gotch, Sekozawa, Bob Richardson from TKO productions, two men w/ navy blue 'guest services' windbreakers, and one of the annex's wait staff to finally ground Bishop]
[The shot cuts to the next day at TATAMI II "Everything is Accepted" and the open moments of the David Bishop/Tatsuki Kwan match. Tatsuki Kwan goes for a leg sweep but the monstrous Bishop hardly budges, instead he retaliates with a hard straight left, staggering Kwan.
[fans pop at the landed strike] Keisuke Oyanami: Now Bishop rushes the off-balance Kwan, throwing a wild series of haymakers, connecting on two or three. This knocks down Kwan, who was covering up, and referee Hyosuke Eda quickly separates the two men, ordering Bishop to a neutral corner.
[fans are popping and buzzing big-time for this opening assault] [The shot cuts to later in the match, Bishop is in control with a side mount and has Kwan distracted by the choke/arm lock, Bishop moves his leg over into a full frontal mount and from there it is bombs away as Bishop really lets the leather fly, trying to pound Kwan into submission or get a stoppage]
Eiji Sekozawa: David Bishop grinds Tatsuki Kwan with a painful forearm to the neck and goes back on the striking offensive with a brutal flourish of fists to the side of the head. This is followed by a hard series of knees, as he goes North-South to the head and sides of Tatsuki Kwan!
[sensing the kill, the crowd rise out of their seats and go ballistic] Keisuke Oyanami: Kwan is not responding and Hyosuke Eda wedges himself in between the two men, waving resolutely for the bell..
TICKER- David Bishop d. Tatsuki Kwan (referee stoppage/ 7:20 / R1) SEGUE TO- press conference area set up in the west corridor leading to the locker room. David Bishop does not take a seat, just looms over top the microphone, and declares himself "the best fighter in Japan AND the world."
Also added, "What the -BEEP- do you think? That BITCH out there is DEAD!" CUT BACK TO- broadcast area Keisuke Oyanami: Who is going to stop DAVID BISHOP?! Eiji Sekozawa: Hai, hai.. (The shot cuts to the inside of a gym, and the massive frame of David Bishop. Beads of sweat trickle down his bald head as he does squats with huge barbell balanced across his shoulders. Bishop's ears are well cauliflowered, silent testimony to the numerous trips to the mat he has made. His nose is bent, and shadowed by his heavy brow, giving almost giving Bishop a Neanderthal like appearance. The monstrous Gaijin's oak like legs pump up and down, hardly taking notice of the added weight of the barbell. He presses the weight up and locks his arms at the summit of a squat, before placing the weight on the ground. Bishop walks away from the camera and shadow boxes the air. It seems the man who so fearlessly fought in NHB fights, is camera shy. Bishop was never one to walk away from a confrontation, he faces the camera again, still hooking the air. He shoots in and screams at the camera. Any fear is gone now, as he is filled with his famous rage. As Bishop begins to speak, a translation rolls across the bottom of the screen.)
Bishop: Who is going to stop David Bishop?!? I remember the night Oyanami asked that fateful question- "Who is going to stop David Bishop?" You see at that time many had tried, and all of them had faild. Tanimura tried, and he failed. Kwan tried, and he failed. Coolidge tried, and he failed.
Sekozawa tried, and he failed. The police and the politicians, they tried too... They tried to lock me up, tried to keep me out of the county. They tried to take away my license, tried to keep me from fighting. But
Oyanami's question still goes unanswered, because I'm here in TOP now. It's now up to Sakai and Taylor; it's up to Morita and Suzuki; it's up to
Koyama and Asakino to TRY and stop me. But before you do try. Ask yourselves something, fellas'. If shooters, Olympians, and even governments couldn't stop me, what chance do you have against me?
(A wicked grin flashes across Bishop's face bearing his less than perfect smile, yellowed and missing a front tooth. The camera zooms into the gap until all you can see is blackness and the shot slips away.)
====== B.O.A. ====== [Interior of a small room. Medium shot of TOP announcer, Hadi Moditian, in a black business suit, seated in a swivel chair.]
Moditian: Good evening, TOP fans. Hadi Moditian here. As we approach the return of TOP, I am going to speak with a returning name, and a new face here in TOP. When TOP went on hiatus last year, we were able to work with a top name in puroresu, possibly the top gaijin in Japan at the moment, one of the biggest stars in the history of RJPW, Rick Taylor.
[Camera switches shots to Taylor, sitting in a swivel chair as well, dressed in black training pants with white stripes down the legs, and a black and gray BOA logo hooded sweatshirt. He nods in acknowledgement to
Moditian.]
Moditian: Also, we have, tonight, a new face to TOP, but one of the most impressive young lions in the country right now, and the current tag team partner and protege of Rick Taylor, Kiyoshi Sakai.
[Camera switches shots to Sakai, sitting in a swivel chair as well, dressed similarly to Taylor. He also nods in acknowledgement to
Moditian.] Moditian: Gentlemen, thank you for sharing your time with us today. First of all, Rick, what was your reaction when TOP was abruptly put on hiatus last year? Rick: Well, I was surprised. I had been wrestling Gustav Jaeger for a shot at the TOP Heavyweight title, so that was a bit of a disappointment, but I understand now that Mr. Fujiwara had some personal proiblems to deal with and that he had to give up everything when he had to do so. I tried to get back into the US scene, but it just didn't click. I've been wrestling exclusively in Japan for the last 3 years now, after doing so about half time for about ten years before that, and after those 3 years,
I just cannot return to the US to wrestle. It's a joke. I belong here in Japan. And it's good to see TOP back in business, and I am happy to be back and working for Mr. Fujiwara.
Moditian: You mentioned that, right before TOP went on hiatus last year, you were in the middle of a Best-of-Three series with Gustav Jaeger to determine the #1 contender to the TOP Heavyweight title. Do you anticipate being considered for a title shot?
Rick: [laughs] No, I don't want to start hotshotting myself here before we even begin. I am primarily interested in establishming myself,
Kiyoshi, and BOA, here in TOP. I would love to challenge for the title, but I do not believe that I would be an ideal first challenger.
Moditian: Ah, you beat me to my next question. What IS this BOA? Is it just your tag team name for the two of you?
Sakai: BOA is not just the name for our team, but a mentality as well. It's all in the name, Badass Offensive Attack. Badass because in this game you have to be tough. If not, you won't last long. Offensive, because you cannot just hang around and wait for the fight to come to you, you have to bring the fight to the table yourself. And Attack because you act first, always, so that you do not have to react, which takes away your control. And Rick and I are certainly in control.
Rick: Right now, BOA is just about me and Sakai doing what we do best, go to the ring, either by ourselves or as a team, face the best the world of puroresu has to offer, and wrestle the best matches Japan has ever seen.
We're not about taking over, it's not about egos. If it's about challenging the status quo, then that's only when the status quo is about lousy wrestling, and I know we don't have to worry about that here. But hopefully, we can raise the bar even just a little bit, even if it's not much, but you should always strive to overachieve, and we're about being the best at what we do, and doing it better than anyone else.
Moditian: Do you think a bid for the TOP tag team titles may be in the near future? Sakai: It'd be great to be presented with the opportunity, but we will cross that bridge when the time comes.
Moditian: Kiyoshi, why align with Rick? What made you come to that decision? Sakai: Because Rick understands this sport. He understands it better than most. Some might criticise me for not aligning myself with a native star, rather than Rick, but I think anyone with any intellignence will see that that mentality is not right at all. Rick may not have been born in Japan, but as he will tell you, his heart is here. His dedication is for puroresu, not what the US scene promotes. Plus, he is one of the biggest stars in Royal Japan history. To succeed against the odds as he has, I do not see how my career can suffer by aligning myself with Rick.
Moditian: Rick, the signing of David Bishop is an interesting development. Some people feel that his arrival is a threat to you. How do you respond to his arrival in TOP?
Rick: Well, I don't really know Bishop. Nor do I really follow MMA fighting, where he made his presence known, as I understand it. I say it's great for us to have as much top talent as possible. And if that means there's more competition for me, then I'm all for it. I'm not interested in any cakewalk here in TOP, just as I've never been about a cakewalk at any point in my career. I hope David Bishop can live up to his hype, because whatever means more talent in this company, the more competitive we will be as a whole, and the better company we will become.
I'm all for it. Moditian: Do you have anything to say about your upcoming match on the return show against Kentaru Yamazaki & Shataru Mayashi?
Sakai: It will be a good match. I think that the match will give Rick and I a chance to show the TOP fans just what BOA is really all about.
Moditian: Thank you for your time, gentlemen. [Both men nod in response, as we fade to black.] ============== HERO ISHIKAWA ============== *crotch chop* [~! [....] [Errm....fade.] ================== BRAT DRAGON MITSU ================== [The camera opens with an expansive shot of...well...grass. A small grassy to be a little bit more descriptive. From the not too far distance we can hear a loud sound getting closer. It's easily identifiable as the sound of a lawn mower. After another moment a John Deere riding mower appears at the top of the hill. The driver is a young man of Japanese descent. Fans of TOP's first run will recognize him as former "Media Darling," BRAT Dragon Mitsu! Mitsu, driving the mower, does not look his most glamorous. He's still VERY muscular, wearing just an old pair of jeans without a shirt. The sweat is pouring off of him, and he appears exhausted. Right in front of the camera, BRAT stops the mower, hops off and wipes the sweat away from his forehead with a rag from his pocket. He takes a moment to look at the lawn he just finished cutting, and begins to speak in a deep, raspy voice.]
BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "I'm going back. Back to the city I was born and reborn in. Tokyo, Japan. The home base of TOP, the Tokyo Organization of Puroresu.
It's been about year since I've been there. A year since I wrestled actually. Thinking back to that time is like taking a trip back to the Emerald City. The good times. I spent months trying to establish myself. Everyone ignored me.
The media, the office, the fans. Then out of no where a few key matches go my way. I pin Jet Jaguar II and Eiji Yatsu within one month. Suddenly I'm the golden boy...the Media Darling. Suddenly there's talk of ME working a program with Shoji Suzuki. Suddenly there's offers for ME to play the starring role in a feature film based on the popular ULTRA Cop-X comic book. Suddenly I'm a star."
[BRAT stops speaking, staring down at the freshly cut grass for a moment. His voice gets quieter, almost reflective.]
BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Then just as suddenly, it all went away..." [He lifts his head and looks directly at the camera now. There is an intensity in his eyes that perhaps fans of TOP had never seen.]
BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "It was all taken away by you, Shishi "Liar" Fujiwara! You see Liar, I respected you. I owed you everything for what I was becoming. You gave me my shot when no one else on either side of the globe was willing to.
You gave me my break in this sport...and then you broke me. When you checked into rehab to deal with your dirty little secret, you left every other person in this company high and dry. We didn't even know where you were for weeks, or if you were coming back. Real considerate, boss. When TOP closed down I had to take work with some Tokyo indy groups. I couldn't even afford to keep myself here. With my tail tucked between my legs I hopped on a flight back to
Atlanta. Back to my father and his house. I had to look him in the eye and ask him for money. I had to admit to him that I failed. As my father handed over the money I had asked for I watched a smile spread over his face as the last of my pride dripped away and left me empty inside. Do you know what I've done for the year that TOP wasn't running Shishi?"
[BRAT points to the lawn mower and the landscape around him.] BRAT: "I have cut lawns. I gave up my dream and started doing work normally reserved for fifteen-year-old boys! While you were in some plush resort you call a rehab, I was sweating and burning in the hot sun doing mindless chores!
Why lawn care? Because the grass will always need cutting. It's reliable. The same cannot be said for you Liar Fujiwara. Now I stand on the final lawn that I will EVER cut. I'm going back to Tokyo. Once I played the forgotten man. I will not do so again. The spotlight and the fame I nearly had will be mine. I'm almost twenty-six years old and if I don't break out soon I never will. I will not let lawns become my life forever. No one will stop me this time. No one at all."
[BRAT tosses the rag he'd been holding towards the camera, covering the picture and effectively ending the promo.]
================ TAKAYUKI MORITA ================ [Open to a young Japanese man standing against a nondescript off-white wall, wearing a plain yellow t-shirt and blue jeans. The man is in his early 20s, with a dark complexion and a rather handsome face. His jet black hair, neither very long nor extremely short, is spiked up and standing on end. He smiles a bit nervously at the camera.]
Young man: Hello, TOP! [He gives a short bow.] Man: I'm sure most of you don't know me, so allow me to introduce myself. My name is Takayuki Morita. I am a student of Ketsueki Ikeda, who you may remember from Gunryo Pro-Wrestling a few years back. I made my professional debut just last year with Oita Pro-Wrestling, but after they went out of business... I'm not sure if it's the economy or what, but I haven't had much luck finding work.
Not a very good way to start off a career, eh? [Morita half grimaces, half smiles wryly.] TM: So, I'd just like to take this time to thank Mr. Fujiwara for this opportunity. And to apologize in advance... I hope I'm not too hard to watch!
[He laughs nervously, scratching at the back of his head in embarrassment.] TM: In all seriousness, I am very happy to be here. I know that we're only small right now and that there seems to be some kind of a smear campaign going on against Mr. Fujiwara, but, for what it's worth, I will stand behind Fujiwara and TOP no matter what. I think under his guidance, with wrestlers like Koyama,
Taylor, and Suzuki, we'll show the wrestling world our spirit and grow in no time!
[A look of determination on his face, Morita accentuates the statement by a pumping a fist. However, he seems to remember himself, and his boldness melts away into a sheepish smile.]
TM: And, well... hopefully, this year will go better for me than last year did, nee? [Morita laughs, giving the camera a quick wink before the scene fades out.] ================= YOSHIKAZU TENZAN ================= [The camera shot opens to a blanket of darkness. The encompassing shadows and obscurity are soon broken by a small little kindling flame that glows inside a large steel barrel. Not giving off enough light to illuminate the entire screen, however, only the immediate surroundings of the barrel can be seen.
Slowly, across the top of the barrel reaches a pair of heavily tattooed arms. Placing the arms across the edge of the barrels top, a face slowly makes it's way into the light. Along with the mysterious face, a head of shoulder length black dreadlocks swing out across the light. The camera begins to pull closer toward the man to get a more defined look at him. Black lipstick paints the kids lips and a black liquid eyeliner outlines his right eye. A nasty scar bisects his right eyebrow, as well as in between his eyebrows. The young
Japanese boy stares deeply into the flame, his eyes trying desperately not to connect with the camera, and the light bouncing off his face making it a dramatic picture. He slowly begins to hiss at the camera in a confused and garbled Japanese accent.]
"Join us, they said. Please, bring yourself back to Japan. Showcase your talents with us."
[The boy lets the weak light reflect off of his composed face without any expressions at all. Dropping his chin to the brim of the barrel, the boy lets his head rest atop his own forearms. After letting out a sigh, he continues.]
"Yoshikazu Tenzan, please, come work with us. We will feed you competition like no other. You will be the certain leader of this promotion."
[The eyes of the now identified Yoshikazu TENZAN begin to swell with irritation.] YT: "I don't want to lead this place, idiot. I'm here to make a fool out of it. To walk across your competition with spiked shoes and enjoy as they scream in pain. To bathe my hands in the blood of my victims. To cloak myself with their bruised flesh."
[A small grin slowly shines across the boys face.] YT: "I have no intentions of being your hero, your leader, or someone you look up to. I'm here to embarrass those types of men. The cuddling, smiling, cheerful weak type. I'm here to make sure those individuals never reveals their face again."
[The boy quickly gestures in disgust.] YT: "The wrestling men of these days are at a weakened state. They work not for them self, but for the people that watch from the side. They work for their endorsement, their money, their fans. It's making me sick to even think about it. How could a person be so insecured?"
[Again, Yoshi sneers toward the camera.] YT: "My work is done not for you bastards, but for someone much more important. Someone who cares not of these material things or heartfelt emotions. Someone who would much rather rip your lungs from your chest than to wish you a happy day or a good morning. A distinct, cold, ungodly man..."
[Brushing his dreadlocks away from his face, the boy continues.] YT: "Who is this demon, your asking? Look no further than right before your very own eyes."
[The kindling flame begins to spark a bit, shooting ashes toward the face of the boy. Still concentrating, the unbalanced Yoshikazu TENZAN continues.]
YT: "The rebirth slowly strides closer and closer. TOP, a new beginning, a new era... New talent and a new attitude. You can all count your lucky stars and pray to your god's that I won't show up next week, but remember this..."
[Raising his resting head from his arms, Yoshi slowly slides his arms down the barrel, grabbing the brim with both his hands on the way down.]
YT: "You can run..." [The boys pink tongue slowly flops out of his mouth and strokes his bottom lip.] YT: "...but you can't hide. One way or another, you _all_ will die." [And with that catchy little phrase, Yoshi shoots a startling look over at the camera and pushes the barrel completely over. As the only source of illumination now falls quickly toward the ground, the face of the young boy disappears into the blanket of shadows that envelope the screen. The camera slowly fades out to the picture of the fallen fire in a barrel.]
