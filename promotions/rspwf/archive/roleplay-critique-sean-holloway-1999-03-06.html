(Roleplay) Critique [Sean Holloway]

6 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a roleplay I did for Stone's WWO ... tell me what ya think. Keep in mind, this is only the third time I've roleplayed with this gimmick.
-- [ Sadistic laughing can be heard as we slowly fade into woods ... THOSE woods. Lightning strikes across the sky ... another bolt explodes into a tree, sending a shower of sparks to the ground, twenty feet below. A huge thunder clap booms thoughout the woods ... the wind bends the trees almost in half. The camera quickly sloshes through the red mud ... he comes to the same door he has visited so many times ... he quietly pushes it open and enters the house. More laughing can be heard as the camera moves around in the darkened house. Floorboards creak as the camera turns the corner and heads into a room. A small fire crackles in the corner ... the glow reflects off the walls, and the smoke curls around at the top of the ceiling. Outside the storm rages on ... more lightning strikes the trees
... in the distance cracking can be heard as a tree falls over. Suddenly, an unseen man in the corner raises his head ... looks straight ahead ... and speaks. ]
"The Phoenix" Sean Holloway : Steve, Steve, Steve ... apparently I have mis-judged you greatly. What once appeared as a great man in my eyes, is no longer so. What once appeared as a man who could back up his words, is no longer so. What once appeared as a man who could rise above making jokes, is no longer so. Steve Dumars, I apparently ... was greatly wrong in my assumptions that you ... were a true man.
However, I now see you for what you really are. A man who can only hide behind the words ... a man who can back up nothing he says. A man ... who has made a grave mistake.
Steve Dumars, I said from the outset that whomever has to make jokes to please the fans ... to please the corporate suits ... to try and make himself look bad ... was a man who could not ... who *will* not ... make it past me.
Steve Dumars, you ... are now that man. [Pause] Steve, I had once made the statement that you were the biggest threat to me in this matchup. Dumars, I now once must retract what I said earlier ... I was wrong ... and for that ... you must pay.
However, Steve, give yourself a pat on the back ... for it is not often that one can say that they had the wool pulled over *my* eyes. I usually see right through frauds ... and see them for what they truly are ... corporate shills whom will say whatever they want as long as it makes them feel good.
Steve, I now realize that you aren't a great wrestler ... no, no ... you proved me wrong by those remarks you made just a short time ago. You have now lowered yourself to the ranks of someone like ... Tyrone "Purple" Hayes.
[Sean speaks with bitterness as he says this name.] On the subject of Tyrone Hayes. Hayes, you must think that because you have experience ... that you are older ... more wise ... and more of a comedian
... that you can say whatever you want with no reprecussions. Well, Tyrone, suprise, suprise. You can't.
[Pause] Tyrone, for now you are safe hiding behind "Uncle" Todd ... but soon ... soon enough ... you will have to face the facts ... and Hayes, Dumars ... I am as real as it gets. [Lightning strikes, adding effect to his words.] Others in the WWO will soon make the mistake of judging me without even finding out anything about me ... So far Tyrone and Steve are the only ones to make the mistakes ... others *will* join the list ... when and where no one knows. But I guarantee I will take care of each and everyone of them that does ... and *that* is a promise, WWO.
[Sean turns and faces the camera.] Steve, you then try to insult the intelligence of not only Belial ... but the Bible as well. The book that expresses God is not one to toy around with Steve ... Dumars, I am sure that at this very moment Belial is planning your demise. Steve, he isn't the only one you have to worry about ... for when you climb into that ring ... I will no longer be a memory ... a voice
... I will be flesh ... flesh, Steve ... muscle ... a human being ... a human being ... that will make sure you perish ... for as the good book says
... I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me ...
[Sean stands and gets in the camera.] Forgive him, Lord, for he is sin. [Sean moves away from the camera, exits the room, and the camera watches the flames get higher and higher ... until we fade to black.]
-- Have a good one, ~ "Adamant" Joseph N., owner of Global
Championship Wrestling Alliance. So what if it's an AOL federation? I just stole Daniel's signature!
~ Handler of "The Phoenix" Sean Holloway. Watch out for him in '99! [WWO] ~ Handler of "Superstar" Sean Holloway. [same person, different moniker] Watch out for him in '99!
