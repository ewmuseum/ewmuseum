[Critique] Flash

17 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Any and all feedback is welcome. [name withheld] ======================================================= [The visibility gradully increases upon a dimly lit room. Standing on a chipped and aged wooden stool is none other then James Morgan. Attired in torn, grungy, faded blue jeans with black boots and a sleeveless T-shirt reading "N.E.W.'s Bane" across the chest. Morgan cocks his head to the right, resting both arms on his thighes, slouching down. His three metallic eyebrow piercings in his left eye glare in the overhead light as his eyes hang low, obviously heavy form fatigue.]
JAMES MORGAN: Intricate surroundings are not needed. Within this simplicity,
I obtain complex settings. It is hard to see but can I imagine the weak minded to see what I do? No, because miracles like that do not happen every day.
Whatever higher power or deity you all bow down and pray to could not perform such a task. So how could you all expect Morgan to?
[For the first time, possibly ever, Morgan emits a soft chuckle.] JM: A man once said that other men, himself included I'm sure, fear that which they do not understand. Never a truer statement spoken, says I.
There are many examples within this World that wear their ignorance and naivity on their sleeve. Some show mercy upon those because they simply do not know.
Others just don't care. I fall in between. Although how you live and whether you do infact live is an issue in which apathy stains me, head to toe, I do see it fit to educate the uneducated. Guide the directionless and teach those in need of teachings.
[Morgan slowly cocks his head to the left, slouching down further, resting both feet on the floor. His tired state shows through.]
JM: One such example, running rampid like the spoiled brat he grew up as and has spawned into adulthood as, is the Devious One himself. Yes, dare
I mention a "Headliner?" Me, the pathetic rookie. Am I bending another cardinal rule? Remind me to mourn my errors after, will you? Devon, what you wish to obtain and achieve is beyond me. To be more precise, what you wish to become, I couldn't care. But I do care about your current existance and how you see it right to interject yourself into my affairs. Is this the extent you'll go to gain the respect of the mindless fans or your peers? Will you oppose Morgan to be put in a different light amongst the rest? Do you foolishly take bumps just to be recognized?
[Another chuckle, this one a bit more noticeable and louder.] JM: Pathetic. No one will remember you when you're gone, Devon. Right now, you are the talk of the N.E.W. Entrapped in affairs and issues amongst many men, losing yourself in the soap opera. How disillusioned have you grown to become, Devon? How ironic. The one thing you used to get away from the every day life is becoming the one thing that is overcoming your life.
How fitting. Once you burn out, never to spur from your own expended ashes, you'll scratch and crawl for the last crumbs of your success. From one
Indepent Promotion to another, you'll use your name to bring you whatever bit of success the Mistress that is Fate might toss your way, out of pity.
[Morgan leans back, tilting his head up. Both cold, grey eyes stare into the lens.] JM: That is what you'll become, Devon. A man of pity. No one will worry about you. They will feel sorrow for your pathetic being. And they will cite your existance as a pitiful attempt at being famous.. and trying to be noticed. Your own quest is your own Waterloo. The life of a fool. I could warn you ahead of time .. but no, I see it more fit to watch you fall the greater distance. Because it is quite elementary when you break it down, Case.
The higher the fool builds himself up .. the longer he will fall. And in the end, you'll realize how hard it will be to climb back up. You wil soon Fall
Into a Deep Depression, Devious One .. but quickly realize one thing ..
[Morgan stands up and begins to step towards the Camera, whispering something.] JM: You're not important.. You're not important.... [Morgans body covers the entire lens as his hands grasp both sides. His voice, still picked up.]
You're not important ....... You're not important........... [Fade to black.]
