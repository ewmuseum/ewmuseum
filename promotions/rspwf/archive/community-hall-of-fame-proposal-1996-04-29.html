Proposal: RSPWF Hall of Fame and Memorial

29 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I think it is about time RSPWF had a Hall of Fame to celebrate and remember the great players, fedheads, characters and general contributions of RSPWF, so that in ten years time, there will be an anchor to what we ahve know. I also want to do this to give [name withheld] somewhere to be remembered. If you don`t know, Noel was this great player who died a while back (And he died a champion as well). So in this HoFaM, I`d like several sections. Greatest Players - Players who were entertaining, made good characters and basically was a good player. Greatest Characters - Characters who really deserve to go down in history. The Hulk Hogans and Ric Flairs of RSPWF, no matter the fed. Greatest Fedheads - Fedheads who were basically great, and should be immortalized, whether it is for their consistency, their imagination or whatever Great Contributors - People who have done things to help the overall nature of RSPWF, namely people like [name withheld] who regularly put out the FAQ or perhaps do interfed things like organising major interfed tourneys or somethings. In Memory - For those member sof this group who have passed to the great e-wrestling fed in the sky. [name withheld] is of course, on this. Perhaos also greatest feds, but that may go against the RSPWF constitution. How do you get on the HoFaM? Well, you can`t nominate yourself. You have to be nominated by someone else, and then seconded, and also have at least another five people supporting you with reasons why. I`d like these proposals on the group, so people can see them and either contest or support them. This is to make sure everything is open. Then we can see from peoples judgements whether they should be accepted in. So, if you`re proposing, please entitle it "(HofaM Proposal) Whatever" There will be a three week window to open debate etc. and make decisions. I will be forming a consultancy body to make a final decision. If you think you should be a part of the HoFaM exec, e-mail me with reasons why. Thanx. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
