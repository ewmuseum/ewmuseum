BBWF sample RP (Please tell me what you think)

12 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{Lightning flashes and up on the hill in the distance is a castle. The castle is from the late 300's. At the gate two huge gargoyles stand their ground, waiting for an intruders to pass through their gate. Lightning flashes again and a light appears in the highest tower in the castle. Within the tower a figure is sitting at a small medieval table. The figure is wearing a long black robe, that covers his entire body and face.}
????: Hello my uninvited guests, welcome to me and my friends new dwelling. I have been here in the BBWF for roughly two months. Who am I you may ask. I'm your worst nightmare REBORN!! Now everyone in the BBWF beware
I'm coming after everyone!!!!! {Lightning flashes again, you can see a glimpse of the figure's face. It is scared with what appear to be claw marks.}
????: I've changed myself into the most dreaded thing in the world. I can destroy anyone from the inside out. What am I?
HATRED!!!! {Hatred slides his hood down. His head is covered with the same type of scars that are on his face.}
Hatred: Now who was I? I know it is hard to tell who I was! I WAS the wrestler known as Thrasher! Now I'm here in the BBWF to remake a name for myself, that is with the help of my friend Greed!! {A huge man appears out of the shadows. He is a monstrous human. His arm's are the size of tree trunks.}
Hatred: Let me introduce you to my dear friend Greed! {All Greed does is stand there with his arms crossed with a death look imposed on the camera. He nods his head in approval.}
Hatred: My dearest friend Greed was formally now as the Stranger. It was about time that he got a new name that truly represented him well. He is greedy for the BBWF World
Heavyweight Title! Soon he will be wearing the belt around his monstrous waist. {The door behind Hatred opens and in steps Apocalypse.} Apocalypse: Now BBWF "Superstars" the Insane Asylum will still be the most powerful stable here in the BBWF! We will have ALL the belts in our possession.
{Terrax appears beside Apocalypse. In his hands he has a piece of paper.} Terrax: We now own this castle. This deed is the proof. This place isn't the only thing we will own. We will own everyone belt in the BBWF!
{Apocalypse just stares at Terrax and shakes his head in disgust.} Apocalypse: Come on Terrax let's go!! {Apocalypse and Terrax leave the tower.} Hatred: As you can see the Insane Asylum is more powerful than ever. We will own every BBWF title. Who will be the first on my list of casualties? I know, Stargazer and Greed wants you Pyscotron in the ring as soon as possible.
{Greed nods and smiles.} Hatred: Now I believe you have over stayed your welcome. Please leave before Greed has to show you out the hard way! {The camera fades to black as Hatred starts to laugh.} ????
