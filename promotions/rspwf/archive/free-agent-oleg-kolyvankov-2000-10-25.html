Free Agent {Oleg Kolyvankov}

25 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
After a break from e-wrestling I am planning to return and am looking for federations who will 'adopt' the following wrestlers. I have listed as much infomation as I could and included a sample roleplay. So please take a look and if you think these guys could fit in with your federation please e-mail me at
[detail withheld] Free Agent Number 1 Name : Oleg "Red Square" Kolyvankov From : Vladikavkaz, Russia Height : 6' 2" Weight : 265 lbs Theme Music : The Russian National Anthem Entrance : Oleg always makes his way to the ring waving the old Russian flag (red with the hammer and sickel) as a reminder of how strong a country Russia used to be. He is usually abused by the crowd and responds by threatening them whether they are elderly ladies or young children. Upon reaching the ring Oleg slides under the bottom rope and waves the flag in the centre of the ring.
Wrestling Style : Power/Brawler Finisher : The Kremlin Description : Oleg whips his opponent into the ropes and as the return he hits a powerful clothesline, sending them sprawling to the mat. Oleg scoops the opponent up and lifts them into the air before hitting a piledriver and covering for the pin.
Favoured Moves : German Suplex, Neckbreaker, Gut Wrench Powerbomb,
Underhook Suplex and Russian Leg Sweep (well, it had to be didn't it!)
Appearance : Oleg is a powerfully built man. He has dark hair which is greying despite his relatively tender years and the hair is cut very short. He has slinted eyes which have a look of fury in them and he rarely smiles. In the ring he wears an all in one vest/shorts suit which is red. He also wears a pair of black wrestling boots.
History : Oleg is a former Olympic weightlifter and made his debut in
Atlanta '96. However after a promising start Oleg failed a drugs test and was sent home in disgrace, his career in tatters. Ever since he has vowed to take revenge on the American people who robbed of his career
(He ignores the fact that he was doped up to his eyeballs!).
Sample Roleplay : Scene opens with a shot of a typical New York city deli. Inside a middle aged lady serves a young professional man dressed in a suit. The deli is quite small yet very respectable. The camera moves inside and the door swings open. Both the man and lady look up and in the doorway is Oleg Kolyvankov. Oleg is dressed in a tatty suit, grey with holes on the elbows and missing buttons. He looks disgruntled and depressed as he moves over to the counter. The lady continues to serve the suited gentleman. Oleg reaches into his pocket and plays with some change before slamming it onto the counter. The lady jumps up startled dropping a bagel onto the floor. the camera focuses on the change, about 75 cents in total. Oleg leans over the counter and speaks...
"A sandvidge, now." Oleg's strong Russian accent is slurred, he has clearly been having a drink as the lady moves back, disgusted by his breath. The man next to
Oleg replies...
"I think you may a couple of dollars short pal. Can I lend you some to get yourself a bite to eat?"
Oleg looks up off the counter into the mans eyes... "I don't vont you charity, who do you vink you are?" The man looks surprised... "I-I-was-" Oleg cuts him off mid sentance and staggers to his feet. Oleg approaches the man, who backs off into the corner...
"You Yanks is all the same. Giving your money to people - But ven, ven you ask for it back and more of it! We dont vant your charity. Nobody does. How dare you judge me. Yes I am Russian but I am not poor."
The man looks fearful yet puzzlled... "Okay, don't take it. I will be on my way. Please-please dont hurt me." Oleg grabs the man by the throat and talks once more, causing the man to hold his breath...
"Okay Yank, go get outta here. Go back to your vife and children. eat your vanksgiving dinner. I do not care. The cold war is not over.
Russia will rise again!" The man leaps away from Oleg and darts out of the door jumping into the nearest available cab. Oleg staggers back over to the bar and starts to laugh totally out of context. The laugh is similar to that of a crazed killer in a cheap film. Oleg puts his hand on the counter and it becomes clear that the man left his wallet in the deli. Oleg picks up the wallet and puts a twenty on the counter...
"Now may I have a sandvich?" The scene ends as the lady shakes her head and begins to prepare a snack for Oleg. So if you think Oleg could fit into you're federation please e-mail me at [detail withheld] However I am looking for a federation which has the following features... - Roleplay Board (not e-mail) - Has been running for at least three months - Has a roster of at least twelve wrestlers - Does not have a roster full of 6' 6" plus wrestlers - Professional look (Poser not needed) - Number of cards does not matter - Would like to hear from Indy or Regional feds Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
