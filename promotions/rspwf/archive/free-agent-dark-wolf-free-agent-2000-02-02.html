[FREE AGENT] Dark Wolf [FREE AGENT]

2 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
That's right folks. Dark Wolf is making room for one more fed. Here is some info about him.
Name - Dark Wolf Age - 29 Height - 6'8" Weight - 278 lbs Eyes - Hazel blue Physical Description - Dark Wolf has short black hair and a goatee. His eyes are narrow. His opponents believe he is sizing them up. He has a tattoo of a wolf on his left shoulder. He has hazel blue eyes.
Wrestling Gear - Dark Wolf goes to the ring wearing a black leather vest zipped up a little. On the vest are the letters "DF" standing for Deadly
Force, Dark Wolf's tag team with Crazy Horse. The letters are in a silver coloring. Dark Wolf wears black pants and boots. He wears black fingerless gloves on both hands plus black elbow pads. As he gets into the ring, Wild
Animal removes the vest from his shoulders.
Hometown - Chicago, Illinois Theme Song - The Shortest Straw by Metallica Ring Entrance - The Shortest Straw begins to play. Fireworks and rockets shoot off the stage and the aisleway. Dark Wolf steps out with Wild Animal on his arm. The two make their way down the aisleway. As they pass lights on the floor, rockets and sparkles shoot off. As they climb the ringsteps, Dark
Wolf lowers the ropes for Wild Animal as she slowly steps through. Dark Wolf climbs to the second turnbuckle from the outside. He turns toward the crowd and raises his hands in the air. He then springs himself over the top turnbuckle to the ring. Fireworks shoot off from the four corners of the ring and form a loud barrage overhead.
Stable - Stable - The Stealth Assasins Only the elite are selected for this group. Dark Wolf is also a member of a stable known as Killer Force in the WWA. Affiliation - In some feds he's a babyface but in others he's a tweener that leans close to being a heel.
Valet - Wild Animal Valet's Description - Wild Animal has long blonde hair and beautiful blue eyes. She stands at about 5'5" and weighs 117 pounds. She is always seen smiling.
Valet's Ring Attire - Valet's Ring Attire - Wild Animal comes to the ring in a black leather sports bra and blue denim shorts. White Reeboks cover her feet.
Does the Valet Interfere? - Yes How? - If ref is knocked out, she gets into the read and hits the opposition with a spinning heel kick with her high heals on. Also, if opposition is thrown to ringside, She pushes him into the guardrail or ringsteps. She never does these if the ref is looking. If opposition is going up to the top turnbuckle, she pushes him off. If opposition is going for a pin, she'll pull the ref out. She also acts like Debra McMichaels to distract the opposition by using her bodily features to get their attention away from her man.
Moves list - belly to belly suplex, head and arm suplex, gut wrench powerbomb, german suplex, DDT, reverse DDT, belly to back suplex, superplex, frankensteiner, throw over top rope onto floor, double underhook suplex, sommersault legdrop, corkscrew legdrop, armdrag takeover, drop toe hold, missile dropkick, shoulder breaker, side slam, gorilla press slam, atomic headbutt (Brett Hart's falling headbutt to the lower abdomen), death valley driver, fallaway slam, running lariat, powerslam, spinning heel kick, spinwheel kick, roundhouse kick, mule kick, springboard DDT, springboard moonsault
Signature Move - Guillotine Legdrop off the top turnbuckle Finisher - The Fangs of Fear Description - A camel clutch submission move executed like the Steiner
Recliner Also please note that I would only use Dark Wolf as a face. I've been using him as a heel and am getting a little tired of it. Below are two of my best roleplays sent to the WWA.
ROLEPLAY 1 ------------------- [The scene opens and we're at a beach. But the weather is not as it should be. Dark clouds cover the sky like a blanket and rain pours down. The camera moves down to find Dark Wolf sitting on some rocks. His feet dangling over the foamy water. He is wearing a pair of jeans, a black leather jacket and a pair of boots. His hair, dripping wet, is slicked back. His hands cover his face. Lightning cracks nearby. But Dark Wolf doesn't flinch. The camera looks back to the sky. The tape speeds up to show the clouds rolling bye. After a moment, the clouds have gone out of sight. The camera slows to a normal pace. It appears to be night time as there is no light. The water is still wild but is constantly calming down.
Little by little. We look back at Dark Wolf. He removes his hands from his face and places them on his knees. He looks us and then quickly turns away.
At this time, the sun peaks over the horizon and begins to rise. The words,
"A new day ..." appears at the bottom of the screen. Dark Wolf takes the soaked leather jacket off his back and flings it into the sea. We watch as it is thrown back and forth. Dark Wolf turns to us again.]
Dark Wolf - It is a new day for the Dark Wolf. Many people believe that I have no talent, no wrestling ability, and no ability to take the Hardcore shots. It is true. I have been a sniveling puss that couldn't stand getting beaten on. Then, last night, at Massacre, I faced The Machine. And to my misfortune, he beat me. Oh well. I'm not gonna cry over spilled milk. I'm going to take it like a man and move on. We all have losses in life. But we have to handle them. I've realized that now. And I look on the bright side of things. That finisher The Machine uses, the Suplex DDT, is a combination of my two favorite moves. So I've honored to have been beaten by such a devastating move.
[Dark Wolf looks off to the horizon and continues to talk. We struggle to hear him.] Dark Wolf - Now people are going to complain that I've lost my focus. That
I'm taking things too easy. And that I'm not suited to be in Killer Force anymore. Well when I joined Killer Force, I made a commitment. I will not go against that commitment. And just because I'm handling life a little better now, doesn't mean I won't do well in the ring. When I step through those ropes. And raise my hands before the crowd. I'm a totally different person. I'm a Deadly Force.
[Dark Wolf looks back at the camera. You can tell he is thinking hard. A minute passes before he speaks again. His face is expressionless.]
Dark Wolf - I've made some enemies quickly here in the WWA. There's Judas, the man who wants to be the only dark soul here. He tried to introduce me to his gods but failed. There's Fontana Stuart. The man wants to beat me for speaking bad of his manager Taylor. There's Scott Dakota. The man wants to kick my ass because I whined when he made me ride the escalator.
There's Innocent Sleen. The man's aware that I want a shot at the Hardcore title so he's after me too. Which reminds me of a better reason why Dakota wants to beat me. He wants a shot at the Hardcore title too so he wants to put me out of the picture. And then there's my good buddies the Metal
Maniacs. I ruined their career, helped kick them out of Killer Force, and then shocked the world when myself and Crazy Horse beat them at
Crash-N-Burn. [Dark Wolf kicks off his boots and dives into the water. He swims for a short time and then climbs out.]
Dark Wolf - Judas, you failed. I hope your gods are unpleased. [Dark Wolf snickers to himself and then speaks under his breath.] Dark Wolf - If they even exist. [He then clears his throat and speaks in a normal tone of voice.] Dark Wolf - Now on to Fontana Stuart. You're right. I'm an impatient bastard. I don't have a problem with you. Yet. Let's keep it that way.
Next there's Stuart's rival Scott Dakota. I may not have been ready for the
Hardcore division before but I am now. Like I said earlier, today is a new day for me. A new beginning. The beginning of a fiercer, stronger, meaner, and tougher Dark Wolf. And the end of the puss that whines all the time.
I'm done whining. If the Booking Committee decides to put me in two, three, or even four matches on a card, fine! That equals more time in the spotlight for me. Bring 'em on. Dakota, we will hook it up on the next
House Show. And the whole world may not see it. But a couple thousand will witness the new fighting machine that is ... Dark Wolf. And they will witness the Fangs of Fear being locked on. And big ol' Scott Dakota tapping out. And then there's Innocent Sleen and the Metal Maniacs. Three people that I fight in a huge tag team match. Well first of all, Sleen, you felt what I am capable of. And my sights are still aimed for that Hardcore title. Watch your back. Cause I'm coming after you. And then, my buddies the Maniacs. Trash and Doom. No, I didn't say Thrash's name wrong you morons. That was on purpose. So Trash and Doom will be in this tag team battle also. Never mind the fact that they have to get beat AGAIN by myself and Crazy Horse at Riot House.
[By this time, the sun has risen into full view. Dark Wolf continues.] Dark Wolf - You all want to get at me. But I have my own list of people to take out. There's the Metal Maniacs of course. A team who I am determined to ruin permanently. Then, there's Scott Dakota. A small thorn in my side as I strive for the Hardcore title. Dakota will be defeated. And I will move on. There's Gravis. The old man thinks he's so much better than myself and the NEW members of Killer Force. Gravis, let me tell you something. Tradition has always been thrown out the window. You, the
Maniacs, and whoever else ran the 'Force have been removed. You are old.
The younger, faster, smarter, and more powerful generation of fighters that is now Killer Force, are the future of the WWA. You all, are just a waste in space and valuable show time. It's time to move on. Why don't you retire and move to that house made of Twinkies you've always been dreaming about. Next on my personal list is Innocent Sleen. The man who claims to be "innocent" somehow latched onto the Hardcore title. Now what's wrong with that picture? Huh?! Somewhere, someday, Innocent Sleen, it'll be you, and me. For the Hardcore title. And you know what happens after that
Sleen? I'll leave that ring. But I won't leave the loser. Oh no. I'll be leaving that ring with the Hardcore title draped over my shoulder. And the fans chanting my name. And you'll be laying face down on the canvas. You will have just experienced what it's like ... when the Fangs of Fear are upon you.
[Dark Wolf stops momentarily. He takes a deep breath and continues.] Dark Wolf - But before that ever happens, there's one thing that is more important. So count your lucky stars Sleen. Cause you'll have a little time before I strip you of that title. You see, myself and Crazy Horse joined the WWA to pursue a career as the Tag Team Champions. And we have not forgotten that. We continue to strive for that goal. And we will reach it. Now I don't care who has the titles at that time. Be it Thugs Xtreme, be it the Metal Maniacs, it doesn't matter. Because when we get that shot.
There'll be nothing to stop us. [Dark Wolf stands up and walks out of the view of the camera. We follow and watch as he steps onto a Suzuki motorcycle and starts it up. He speeds off.
The camera fades but returns a minute later. Dark Wolf has parked his bike and is walking towards us. He walks right past and goes through a door. It is obvious that we're in a WWA arena due to all the logos and pictures on the wall. Dark Wolf turns a corner and walks down a hall. He gets to a dark room where he turns and begins to head down another hall. At that time, a man wearing a balaclava and clown suit jumps out from behind some crates. He has a chair in his hand. He runs up behind an unsuspecting Dark
Wolf and cracks the chair over his head. Dark Wolf falls forward on his face. The clown continues to deliver several chair shots to the head of the unconscious Dark Wolf. Security rushes the area and we cut out.]
ROLEPLAY 2 ------------------- [The camera rolls and we're on a large private jet. Dark Wolf and Wild
Animal sit next to a window. Dark Wolf is closest to the window. Crazy
Horse sits one row behind them. Dark Wolf is wearing a Killer Force t-shirt and jeans. Crazy Horse is wearing a blue and green flannel shirt with khakis. Wild Animal is wearing a white blouse and blue jeans. Her long blonde hair is tied back into a ponytail. In front of Dark Wolf, on the pull down table, sits a WWA Tag Team title belt. His right hand is resting against it. His eyes are shut as if sleeping. His head is tilted back and slightly to the side. Wild Animal reaches toward him with her right hand and gently touches his arm. His eyes snap open and he leans forward a little. Taking notice of Wild Animal's hand on his arm. He then leans back and puts his arm around her.]
Dark Wolf: At last. They are ours. Deadly Force has come through on our promise. And we add two more belts to the collection which is amounting for
Killer Force. Our good friend Tsunami has the Intercontinental title. My lovely Wild Animal has won the Women's title. And Deadly Force recently won a 9 team Battle Royal for these straps. Thugs Xtreme, Goth, Gravis and
Steven, Sleen and Mad Dog, Sloan and Stuart, Ban X and Zero, Dakota and
Rancid, and a few others battled us for these belts. We were the fourth last team to hit the ring. Sleen thought he had me beat but I hung on. And
I got rid of you Sleen. You unworthy excuse for a former champion. Stuart and Dakota thought they had us but they forgot the little fact that we had to be thrown OVER THE TOP! Not through the ropes! The Thugs came out acting like tough shit. But we quickly disposed of them. Then Gravis tries to throw me out. Well Gravis, just like at Riot House, you failed! Crazy
Horse sacraficed himself to help me out. And I thank my best friend for that. And as myself, Crazy Horse, Gravis, and Steven went over the top, I knew that this was our chance! I knew that this match wouldn't end without a winner! And I knew just how to get Deadly Force on the map! And God damnit! I did it! I held onto the ropes!! To give Deadly Force the WWA
Tag Team titles!
[The scene pans over to Crazy Horse who has perked up his attention, listening to Dark Wolf. Crazy Horse looks at the camera and begins to speak.]
Crazy Horse: Big things are gonna go down at Cold War. But I'll talk about that a little later. First, let's talk about the upcoming Overload. This
Monday, I debut as a singles competitor. As I face off against Sean
Shanoski. A newcomer to the WWA. Just like Dark Wolf and myself were only five months ago. And it appears that Shanoski is trying to imitate us.
He's trying to take this federation by storm. Well Sean boy. Deadly Force did it back in August. And we don't like when people try to imitate our successes. So Sean, you may have a big match against fellow Force members
Lashar and Replica. And indeed you will have your hands full there. But don't overlook your match with me on Monday. Because I have proven myself time and again that I, like Dark Wolf, am a superior fighter. So Playgirl, throw all the sickly wrestling skills you may have acquired at me. The end result will be the same either way. A tombstone and the War Cry will mean your doom. ... Have you ever heard the War Cry Sean? Trust me, it can be a terrifying sound. ... And when you hear that cry, you will know, ... that a submission is the only escape for you. So Playgirl, when I put the War
Cry on you, you will tap out. Steven may have been lucky enough to get out of it at one time. But that's a rarity. A rarity that I intend to make so rare, that I am making it my mission, to never let it happen again. Sean
Shanoski ... YOU'RE MINE!! I will make you easy pickings for my buddies,
Replica and Lashar. So, you're tagging with Azmyth right. Well what good is that going to do you against them? Tell me.
[The camera returns to Dark Wolf and Wild Animal as Crazy Horse leans back in his seat. Dark Wolf's arm is still around Wild Animal. He pulls her gently towards him a little.]
Dark Wolf: Crazy Horse is right you know? Sean Shanoski will not stand a chance against him, or against Replica and Lashar, the two newest additions to the Force. They will have their first taste ... of tag team victory.
Just as we did in our first PPV. Now, on to other matters. I have been given the great pleasure of facing the WWA's former President at Overload.
That's right. This Monday, I'm fighting you Brock. ... And I'm going to make an example of you. I'm going to prove to the world, that you're not worthy of a shot at Tsunami's Intercontinental title. Much less a shot at one half of the current WWA Tag champions. You may have rejoined with the
Thugs. But that means nothing to me. You see, I single handedly disposed of them last Overload. I took that extra step toward becoming a Tag
Champion. Brock, the only "Total Chaos" that's going to happen this Monday, will be the total chaos, that I bring upon you. Still see me as a rookie do you? Well I think by now I've proven myself of being one of the toughest rookies in this federation! So Chris Brock. Or should I say ... former
President Chris Brock, the Fangs of Fear can clearly be seen in your future.
You see Brock, the Guillotine will fall ... and the Fangs of Fear will be upon you. You think you've got a chance at beating a superior fighter like myself? WELL IT AIN'T HAPPENIN'!!! And if you don't like it ...
Deadly Force: TOUGH!!! Dark Wolf: And let's move right along shall we. Right along to the matches scheduled for Cold War. I'm unwillingly teamed up with Gravis. Crazy Horse is teamed up with Mister Innocent Sleen. And that other team will be facing off in this 3-way Twisted Tag Team Championship match. Gravis, you know I'm going to stab you in the back. You're not my partner.
Crazy Horse: And the same goes for Sleen. Dark Wolf: That's right. Gravis, Sleen, Steven, and Howling Mad Dog, you might as well quit while you're ahead. Because it is inevitable that we,
Deadly Force will get that win ... And retain our titles.
Crazy Horse: This is a big match for us. And it will be a big match for the
Force's fans. Because in this match, you all will see, two of the finest competitors go at it. That's it. There's no doubt in my mind, that myself and Dark Wolf will lock it up a couple times in that match. Not to prove who's better. That is not an issue between us. We are of equal ability.
But because we want to prove to the world, that we are THE BEST!! Dark Wolf: Well said Crazy Horse. Even though it's so twisted, Deadly Force will once again prevail. Just like we have always prevailed. Later in the show Tsunami will face my opponent of this week, Chris Brock. And we all know, that this will be a cake walk for Tsunami. Brock, former President
Brock I should say, doesn't have a hope in Hell.
[Again the camera returns to Crazy Horse.] Crazy Horse: The main event of that great event, is the Blood Bowl. Now,
I'm sure you all know what that is. So I won't go into much detail about it. What matters is this. One way or another. One of us is coming out of there with a title shot. Now, Dark Wolf says that if he wins it, he'll want a shot at the Hardcore champ.
Dark Wolf: That's right. Crazy Horse: Personally, I'd take a shot at the Television title. So, if
Thane still has it then, or if Rancid has managed to grab it, if, and when I win that Blood Bowl, I'm taking my shot at the Television title.
Dark Wolf: And as I have said before, there is no question in my mind, that one of us members of Killer Force, will get that win. It may not be myself.
It may not be Crazy Horse. But, one of us, be it Tsunami, Replica, or
Lashar may get that win. Or, it could be myself or Crazy Horse. But one of us WILL get that win.
[The camera focuses in on Wild Animal now.] Wild Animal: So Candy Hart is pissed that I won the Women's title. Well
Hart, that's life. Live with it. I improvised. I was the better fighter.
I was the more intelligent fighter. This is my title. And whether you like it or not Nurse, this title is staying around my waist. And as Dark Wolf and Crazy Horse said before, if you don't like it ...
All three: TOUGH!!! [The scene fades as the three begin laughing.] One final thing. Before sending me your applications, I have a few requirements about feds before I join them. please review these before sending me your info.
The fed must be established for a minimum of 6 months. The fed must have a roster of at least 25. The fed must have a website with a nice design. The fed must have a talented card writer. That's basically it. I will scout both e-mail based, and message board based feds. However, I am not very knowledgeable of HTML so if I join a message board fed, HTML can not be required when writing roleplays as this is discouraging to me.
