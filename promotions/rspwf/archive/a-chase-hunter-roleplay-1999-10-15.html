A Chase Hunter roleplay...

15 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To all you funky people on RSPWF, I've very VERY rarely posted. But for some odd reason, I felt like posting one of my recent roleplays that I did with one of my only wrestlers, Chase
Hunter.
Most of you are giving large question marks to the name, I'm sure. Maybe that's why I'm doing it, to get the name out there a bit more and more well known. Maybe I'd like a new name to be talked about, other than the SVS', the Pillow's and what not.
Or maybe it's just that it's 3:00 AM, I'm about to go to bed and my brain's just not functioning properly.
Anyway, feel free free to pick apart this as you see fit :-) I could certainly pick out a few myself.
I'm not really asking for a critique, that's why I didn't put it in the topic. But, like I said. Do with it as you please :-)
Later, [name withheld] (Handler of Chase Hunter in UWF and Kid Canuck in NLWP. Also the current vice president of NLWP)
___________________________ DR: Well, before we continue any further, we’re going to show you some footage from earlier today. We spoke with Chase Hunter, the Great Northern
Champion, and to hear his comments about his match coming up later tonight.
Have a look... [The scene opens, as any scene normally would, with a fade in. We are treated to an inside shot of a gorgeous hotel suite in Atlanta, Georgia. The cameraman walks through the doorway that comes from the hallway, and into the room.
Chase Hunter is stretched out on the bed, wearing nothing but a bathrobe and moose slippers. The bed seems to have a vibrator motor in it, as it’s shaking profusely. Surrounding Chase, on the bed, are oodles upon oodles of candyy wrappers...health bars, mind you...all opened up and quite empty.
Also, there seems to be a lot of commotion on the television...a lot of...moaning? Also in the background, the Great Northern Championship, is nicely sitting on the small table by the window.]
Cameraman: Ahem! CH: Dwaa!!! [Chase, in utter shock of the intrusion, rolls right off the bed. We hear a harsh thunk as he crashes onto the floor. His hand reaches from the floor and flicks the off switch on the vibrator. The bed quickly stops shaking, of course. He plops his head up on the bed, and rests his chin on the covers.]
CH: <sigh> Don’t DO that!! Geez. Sneak up on a guy in the middle of his...er...well, okay, there’s not technical term for it.
Cameraman: Relaxing? CH: Relaxing! Yeah, that’s a good one! [Chase quickly rolls back onto the bed and props his head up with his hand. The moaning on the television isn’t getting any quieter.] CH: So, whatcha here for this time, Mikey? Camerman: Interview, Mister Hunter. CH: Oh right right right. Something about that big challenge ‘o mine, huh? Okay okay, lemme think. Where should I start? Oh yes! I know... [Chase grabs the phone by the bed and dials 7....] CH: Yeah, hi, room service? Bring me up some spam slices, will ya? Great. Oh! And a basket of apples, too. Yup. Uh huh. You betcha. Okay, buh bye. [Hangs up the phone.] So speaking of spam, let’s get right into the first contender. Lukewarm Spamson. Now Lukey, you worry too much, you know that?
You worry about being the expendable and not the extrodinary. But don’t worry! There are plenty of other guys you can beat up to stop them from pinning me! Heck, you’d be doing me a favor. So buck up, Spamson, you’ll get plenty of snowballin’ moments. Just...not this week...
[Chase looks at the TV for a second. The cameraman pans over to show that the television is showing...the National Twister Competitions, and not the porn you sick minded people were thinking. Chase flicks the TV off with the remote. He stands up on the bed and starts bouncing on it, just for the sheer fun of it.]
CH: So who’s next? Hmmm. How about that wacky crazy guy, Sherrick? Now
Sherry, you and me have a bit of history together. But there shouldn’t be any bad blood. After all! You got the chance and the dream of everyone out there and be the great *snicker* and admired *chuckle* superhero that all the kids loved!!!
[Chase goes into one final bounce, and drops back first onto the bed. A lot of the bar wrappers fly everywhere in the air. It begins to snow candy wrappers as Chase continues.]
CH: But I digress about Sherrick. We’ll just leave it at the fact that I’ve faced you before and I know your game. [Chase rolls around on his stomach and props his chin on both hands] And SPEAKING of guys I’ve faced!!! Talk about your deja vu, huh, Terror? Well listen pooky, I beat ya before, and that won’t stop me from doing it a second time, sawright? Besides, me and
Louie Garvello are pretty good buddies after him helping me out and all. In fact, he’s told me he’s gonna lend me a hand in that gauntlet match. I’m sure when push comes to shove, I’ll have to worry about him, too, but that’s besides the point.
[The Vesatile One rolls off the bed again, and walks over to the glass sliding door. He swishes it open and steps out onto the balcony, grabbing his Great Northern Championship along the way.]
CH: And last but certainly not least, that AreJeans AreMade of Steele... [The cameraman whispers over to Chase. “Psst. That’s Arjen Steele, Mr.Hunter.”] CH: Our Jenn?... Cameraman: Arjen, damnit! Arjen! CH: Maybe you should go lie down, you’re just not making any sense. [Chase shakes his head from all the confusion and tries to keep this interview going.] So! This Steele guy [He pauses like no man has paused before!]
Yeesh, how many Steele’s are there in this business? But anyway, Steele, you go on and on about how you want this belt...and then you go and say it’s not an important one? Well listen, boopsey, this belt [slaps the GN belt that’s strapped over his shoulder], this is made in Canada material here, baby. You insult it again and you can say hello to a v-drop, courtesy of yours truly.
[Chase looks over the balcony and we get a breath taking, eighth floor view of the great city of Atlanta.]
CH: But you know, OurJenn, you did make a pretty cool quote that I think I’ ll sort of switch around. Have any five of you guys ever read Shakesphere’s
Julius Ceaser? [Chase cups his hand over his ear] Aw, sorry to hear that,
Terrorino, but I hear the hooked on phonics program works for people.
ANYWAY, back to what AreJean said about havoc and all that...well... [Chase climbs up onto the metal railing and does a balancing act. He stetches his arms out, faces the city of Atlanta and shouts as loud as he can...]
CH: Cry havoc and let slip the dogs of war!!! That this foul deed shall smell above the earth!! With carrion men, groaning for burial!!!
[The Vesatile One hops back down with ease...just as there’s a knock at the door.] CH: I think you get the idea there, guys. ‘Scuse me. [Chase walks over to the door and opens it up. Why, it’s a bellhop, delievering the spam and his basket of apples. He takes the clipboard and starts to sign on the dotted line, he pauses and looks up at the astute bellhop.]
CH: Is Beeby spelled with a “Y” or an ay ee? Bellhop: A “Y”, Mr.Hunter, sir. CH: Oh pish posh, call me Chase, will ya? Oh, and here’s a handsome tip for ya [scribbles more on the clipboard], just add it to the tab. After all...[Chase looks to the camera.] The Beebs owes me one.
[He winks to the camera as it begins to fade out.]
