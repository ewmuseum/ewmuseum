[CRITIQUE] Anyone wanna tackle something big?

8 December 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
K, for the last five months I've handled "Homicide" Dutch Blackheart in the
HWO (Hell Wrestling Organization). In that time I have don more RPs than I can count, written matches, helped with the Web site and generally done what
I could to help the fed along. I finally decided I needed some time off but not before doing one final grand work. What follows is a resume of sorts...it is an all encompassing RP of sorts taht represents my ability to
RP my character, write matches, tie segments together (i.e. like oyu would an event), as well as my primitive HTML skills and limited Flash design.
What follows the explanation here is a brief RP I did as a lead in to my much larger segment and I'll include the lead in here for the sake of ease.
What i have here is rather large, and if anyone is actually interested in critiquing all or part of it I'd be most appreciative. It's a LOT to digest, so any feedback on any particular segment is good. Contribute as much or as little as you would like to regarding the RPs, layout, or anythign else you feel compelled to comment on. Please note that matches I wrote have links that are flanked with *s should you want to see how I do matches as well. Originally I had simply thought of doing a bio page, but decided everyone has one of those. I wanted to do somethign a bit more original that reflected my skills on a variety of levels and wanted to design something that could demonstrate to prospective leagues what I had to bring to the table. Thanks in advance for your comments, look forward to reading them.
------------------------- *Scene opens as a brief-case toating Neil Berkley, flanked by Yvonne Peterson and her ever presnt clip booard make their way briskly down the halls of the HWO Headquarters. Berkley nervously looks at his watch as he open's a door marked "Board Room", ushers Yvonne in, and follows her. They take their seats at the head of a table, the camera pans around, facing them and shooting over the shoulders of the shadoy, faceless men that make up the
BoD. One of them begins to speak.
BoD: Berkley, thank you for coming on such short notice. The BoD has been going over this month's financial statements and we're a bit concerned to say the least.
BoD2: Since you released Dutch Blackheart and Virus, our profits have taken a swift decline. Ticket sales are down, phone calls to the hotline number are down, concesions are down, in fact...the only thing that's UP is the sale of Virus and Blackheart merchandise.
BoD3: We understand why you did what you did, and we did agree to it, but we're starting to have second thoughts about you, your choices, and your capacity to pull the HWO out of this financial rut.
Berkley: Gentlemen, I am well aware of the current financial situation of the hWO, I too receive the reports. The BoD appointed me as a spokesmen and everything I have done has been withour approval. I get the nagging suspicion you guys are trying to blame ME for the current financial trend.
When we agreed to taking the HWO over, we did so KNOWING that sudden changes in format and style typically affect the short-term revenue, but provide long-term financial boons. The HWO is still in an adjustment period and you have to bare with me here.
BoD4: And in the mean time, what do you have planned. Chazz and Purr are still making a mockery of your leadership and the entire BoD is suffering from this blow.
Berkley: That is being taken care of as we speak. In the mean time, I have an idea that will make the BoD a MINT in profits.
BoD5: And what might that be? Berkley: Well, since we DO have the exclusive rights to the Blackheart character and merchandise, I have decided to exploit this fact. Blackheart took a lot from this fed in terms of paid out law-suits, workman's comp, and other expenditures directly related to his behavior in and out of the ring.
BUT, he is gone and our losses are now cut back to a minimum. That doesn't mean we can't still profit from Blackheart.
BoD2: Go on... *Berkley opens his brief case as Yvonne rolls out a TV/VCR combo on a rolling stand. Berkley takes the remote, turns on the machines, and places a video cassette from his briefcase inside the machine.
Berkley: Gentlemen...sit back and watch as I SHOW you the newest line of HWO products, the video department has been hard at work and I think the fans are REALLY going to approve of our newest division, HWO Home Video. Let me showyou the first product that I am now submitting for your approval...
