[Flash] "The Handler" Liam Rogan

21 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in. Dateline: Sunday night, June 11th. The scene opens in one of the many hallways backstage at the London Arena. The evening's installment of
"Sunday Night Heat" is still raging on outside. We see "The Handler" Liam Rogan sitting down on a stool in the corner of the hallway. Dr. Steven Galetta, one of the assiduous medical officials on UEW's "damage control" team is standing in front of Rogan, examining Rogan's left arm closely. Dr. Galetta finishes his examination and smiles at Rogan...]
SG: "It's just a little bruised, Liam. No big deal, really. Just lay off using it for a day or so and things should be fine. It should be as good as new by the time your next match rolls around next week."
[Rogan looks up at Dr. Galetta, rubbing his arm and grinning...] LR: "Thanks for checking it out, Doc. I just wanted to make sure that it wasn't broken or anything. Tracker did a real number on it tonight. I expected your diagnosis to be much, much worse."
[Galetta shakes his head, still smiling...] SG: "Nah. It's fine. Your body takes a licking and keeps on ticking, Liam. It really absorbs a lot of pain." [Rogan smirks at Galetta...] LR: "I'll take that as a compliment." [Galetta pats Rogan on the shoulder...] SG: "Well, I better get going. There's a Hardcore match coming up. I gotta get my gauze and stitching at the ready. Those matches always have at least one casualty. If you need anything, just give me a call."
[Rogan shrugs Dr. Galetta off, laughing...] LR: "Bah! I don't need any of those damn painkillers! I should be fine. Thanks anyway, Doc." SG: "Night, Liam." [Rogan turns to face the camera as Dr. Galetta walks off-camera. He looks down and massages his left arm for a few seconds before looking back up at the camera, nodding...]
LR: "I'm on my way. I'm on the road to reclaiming _my_ title... the UEW Television Championship. Tonight, I survived a brutal encounter with Tracker. I defeated the so-called 'Innovator of Extreme' by hitting him with something he really didn't know how to combat - good, ol' fashioned _WRESTLING_."
[He nods again, smirking...] "Sure, he _attempted_ to outwrestle me. The poor bastard actually tried to show some in-ring skill tonight. He gets an 'E' for effort from me for doing that... but he really couldn't pull it off. He's too accustomed to hardcore fighting and brawling all over the arena. When it came to pure grappling, he floundered. He was no match for me. He succumbed in a little over eight minutes."
[Suddenly, the smile leaves Liam's face...] "Next week, in France, my next obstacle awaits me. At 'In Fast Forward' next Friday night, I'm taking on a filthy, disgusting _germ_. I'm facing that limp-wristed _sicko_, Jessie Muldoon." [He shakes his head...] "He doesn't have [name withheld] or Dysfunction by his side anymore... but that doesn't take away from the fact that he is the most revolting piece of [BLEEP]
that has ever played a part in this sport. I fear for my life now that I've been signed to take on that... that... fat-assed load of _gay trash_."
[Liam shakes his head again, wincing at the camera...] "I'm not afraid of Muldoon the _wrestler_. His cookie-cutter arsenal is laughable and the way he lumbers around the ring like a drunk Sasquatch does very little to intimidate me. I'm just fearful of Jessie Muldoon, the
_fruitcake_. I'm afraid of catching something terminal from that freak of nature. Muldoon has been bumping nasties with men for God knows how long now.
He's probably been infected with more sexually transmitted diseases than a whore in Bangkok."
[He shadowboxes a quick punch at the camera...] "All it'd take is one stiff punch to bust Muldoon's lip or something... and then it could all go to hell in a hand-basket for me. I'd have a pretty good chance of getting contaminated with whatever the hell viruses he has festering through his veins."
[Rogan leans back against the wall, leering sadistically at the camera. He shakes his head...]
"But I'm not going to let all of that distract me. Sure, I'll be careful... but I won't let that caution get in the way of something that I've been yearning to do for well over a year now - beat the holy hell out of professional wrestling's sickest joke."
[Rogan points at the camera. He grits his teeth angrily as he speaks...] "Muldoon, I've hated your guts ever since you, [name withheld], and Matt Booth pranced around UEW like some kind of homosexual incarnation of the Three
Musketeers. Ever since I saw you acting like a an effeminate goofball with those two, I've wanted to snap your neck."
[Rogan half-smiles as he looks into the distance...] "In a little over a week's time, I finally get my chance to do just that. It's gonna be a proud moment for me. I'm gonna release over a year's worth of hatred and disgust onto Muldoon. It's not gonna be a pretty sight. In the end, I will be victorious... as well as one step closer to regaining the
Television Title... and Muldoon and his repulsive actions will be _gone_ from this sport _forever_."
[Rogan turns and looks directly into the camera. He addresses the camera in a cold, vengeful tone...]
"Muldoon, you illiterate son of a bitch. I hope you're listening. I hope you're ready to suffer for your deplorable ways, you sick bastard. On Friday night, June 23rd, I'm gonna _destroy_ you. The beating that I'm going to give you on that fateful night will make what those guys did to Matthew Shepherd look like a high school Glee Club rehearsal."
[He shakes his head...] "All the K-Y Jelly in the world won't be able to save you from the excruciating evening of destruction that awaits you. It's time to meet your maker, Muldoon.
It's time to suffer for _all_ of your sins." [A vindictive smile materializes on Rogan's face...] "It's time for you to see just how a _real man_ takes care of business. Prepare to be _erased_, little girl." [Fade to black...] -[name withheld] The King of All Assholes "You're an asshole." - [name withheld] "It's Rob's world, and we're just living in it." - [name withheld] "I'm friends with Rob, so I don't get into feds." - [name withheld]
