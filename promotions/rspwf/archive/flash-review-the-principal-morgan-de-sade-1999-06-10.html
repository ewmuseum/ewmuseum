[FLASH REVIEW] "The Principal" Morgan De Sade

10 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the first character I have ever used besides Valtharius the Mad since starting e-wrestling back in 198. Just wrote a role play for your enjoyment and review. I am used to writing in sub-human language that Valtharius uses so fluently. <g> Now i have to "use" the education I recived in college and actually write something more than "SQUIRREL NOT NICE!!! ME SMASH SQUIRREL!!!"
I hope you enjoy it.. this roleplay is a bit longwinded for my tastes but then again, I've had all those words pent up in me since '87. <g> Let me know what you think....
Thanx! Patrick (a.k.a. Gateway) Handler of Valtharius the Mad and manager Karachel, The Voice of Reason (MLW) And now handler of "The Principal" Morgan De Sade (IIWA) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Slow fade from black to a hallway with young men in their early twenties in school uniform filing down the hall with sullen and zombie-like looks upon their faces. Standing at the end of the hallway with a riding crop in hand is the form of Principal Morgan De Sade. De Sade looks over his students carefully with a disdainful glare and grabs a nearby pupil by the ear for disciplinary action]
MDS: Do you realize Mr. Burton that shuffling down the hall like that is against school policy? Do you need *another* reminder of what happens to those that do not obey the rules? [pauses and smiles with evil intent] My rules, Mr.
Burton? [The student looks to Principal De Sade and shakes his head with definite conviction that he understands. De Sade lets him go and continues to look around. As the last pupil walks into a classroom the door closes and the bell rings]
MDS: The school bell is much like the bell they use to signal the start and end of a boxing or wrestling match, don't you agree? To me, wrestling and boxing are like a ballet, except there's no music, no choreography, and the dancers hit each other. Silly of me to say, but I hate dancing but on the other hand I do enjoy hitting people!
[De Sade pulls from a school bag, that was laying carelessly upon the floor, a book. He pages through it and gives careful thought to his words]
MDS: I see little Anthony Thompson left his bag in the hallway again.... this does not bode well for him. He thinks he is as sly as a cat as does DT
Richman... Well Mr. Richman, you like little Anthony Thompson are in for a big surprise once the school bell rings and I begin educating the pathetic trash of the IIWA. Do you really know what a cat is Mr. Richman? Have you studied your science book?
[Pulling out a science book from the book bag] MDS: A cat, simply put, is a soft, little puffball provided by nature to be kicked when things go wrong in the domestic circle. Or in this case, the squared circle. Mr. Richman, you will stay after school!
[De Sade drops the book and walks down the hallway and peers into an open locker] MDS: I see someone left their locker open. Do you realize the effect this could have on my insurance rates if someone was injured because a student carelessly left their locker door open? I think that the denizen of this locker shall be punished accordingly! And what is this???
[De Sade reaches into the locker and pulls out a deck of cards] MDS: My goodness! Gambling is not permitted on school grounds! [He calls over a security guard of the school who is dressed in the death black uniform of the German SS]
MDS: Karl, find out whose locker this is and bring them to my office after school. And here, take these cards for evidence.
K: Yes sir! [De Sade scratches his chin] MDS: You know, I'm reminded of a story.... you've probably heard it. The king and queen of this country were playing golf with five clubs. Their son Jack remarked how strange it was that they only had two hearts between them. And just then his sister Little Deucy and her dog Tre started singing "Four
Diamonds Are a Girl's Best Friend," whereupon the whole family beat her to death and buried her with two spades. This reminds me of when I read that "The
Devil" Jake Adams' father gambled and lost his life to a nasty drug habit.
Tell me Jake, did you bury your dad with a spade or the nice shiny shovel you used in your schools sandbox?
[De Sade walks to a water fountain and takes a drink and wipes his mouth] MDS: Being a principal is hard work. As a principal most of the time I don't have much fun. The rest of the time I don't have any fun at all.
[De Sade chuckles an evil laugh and steps to a doorway and looks in then sniffs] MDS: Ahhhh... the joys of breaking rules and smoking on school grounds! I do so love the opportunity of educating others on the evils of smoking! These miscreants shall be taught a lesson! Once done they shall bare the markings of having been disciplined! I'll will not treat their wounds, nor will I heal their wounds, and I will not bind their wounds. What will I do? I will inflict their wounds! Everything around here will be just fine if there's a little less smoking and a lot more
learning. We need obedience. We need discipline, not this chaos. [De Sade walks into the bathroom and everything goes silent for a moment. Suddenly a scream for help emanates from the restroom as a body comes flying out followed by three more. De Sade walks out calmly as security guard Karl comes running down the hallway to see what is the matter]
MDS: It is students like you that are constantly out of uniform that give this school a bad name! And it is students like you who don't belong in uniform.
You are like wild dogs running around loose on the playground... dogs that are about to get run over by a large truck named De Sade!
[De Sade leans down and slaps one of his students upside the head] MDS: You, like most of the wrestlers of the IIWA, are born mediocre, whereas wrestlers like Crow, McZax, Brace and Manson have to work their asses off to achieve the lowest levels of mediocrity. Some of the poor morons in the IIWA have mediocrity thrust upon them. Wrestlers like Orr, Thomas, and Yoder come to mind. Poor helpless slobs. Their minds wasted on the life of a common man.
Beer, pretzels, and confined to wearing ill-fitting underwear while watching reruns of Gilligan's Island!
[De Sade motions Karl to pick up the students and take them to the boiler room for punishment. Karl picks them up as he draws out his nightstick]
MDS: Since I am on the subject of wrestlers of the IIWA I may as well speak on the one man I truly admire.... "Hollywood" Dave Diamond. I have nothing but admiration for him. You do know what admiration is don't you? Admiration is nothing more than our polite recognition of another's resemblance to ourselves! But Mr. Diamonds downfall is his over inflated ego. Egotism is the anesthetic that dulls the pain of stupidity. My friend, Mr. Diamond, you upset one of my mentors in the MLW by the name of Karachel. How stupid could you actually be? But then again, you are a lot like Anthony Mislan and Justin van
Drake.... Stupid. Everyone is entitled to be stupid, but some abuse the privilege.
[De Sade pauses then walks away while slapping his riding crop against his thigh. He turns and speaks]
MDS: As far as what you have to say about me, I could care less, so long as it isn't true. [He walks a little further towards the Principals office and notices the television is on and an IIWA commercial comes on]
MDS: Watching IIWA wrestling on television rots the mind. The wrestling is supposed to be entertainment. All I ever see are a bunch of steriod clowns running around making absolutely no sense whatsoever. Do the morons that watch wrestling know what entertainment is? I think not! Entertainment is any kind of amusement whose inroads stop short of death by injection. What *isn't* entertainment? Ever notice that fifteen minutes into a Jerry Lewis telethon you start rooting for the disease? Jerry Lewis is definitely *not* entertainment but he could wrestle circles around the likes McZax and that country singer cult murderer wannabe Billy Ray Manson.
[De Sade laughs heartily and walks through the open office doors and sits behind his mahogany desk. He places his crop down and picks up his briefcase and begins placing papers within]
MDS: In a little while we will be having a surprise fire drill for the student body. This generally makes them happy as they can leave their studies behind for a few minutes. Happiness is nothing more than good health and a bad memory. I will be inflicting nothing but sorrow and pain upon the "student body" of the IIWA. Nothing but misery for those that wish to remain uneducated. Don't think some of you can pull together and form a stable or a happy little family to stand against the Board of Education. [Pulling out a
2x4 and slamming it on top of his desk] It's too bad when whole families have to be torn apart by something as simple as wild dogs.
[Karl knocks on the door] MDS: Come in Karl. What do you have to report? K: All four of the students that were caught smoking are in the boiler room awaiting your punishments.
MDS: Thank you Karl. You are most helpful! [De Sade smiles as Karl walks off to patrol the school grounds] MDS: Men like Karl are hard to come by now days. Karl is a second generation
American. His grandfather, bless his heart, served proudly in the German SS and had a strong sense of discipline. Needless to say he taught it to Karl's father and Karl's father to him. I couldn't ask for a better security guard!
Come, follow me.... [De Sade picks up his top hat and places it on his head and grabs his riding crop and walks down the hallway to a huge iron door. Above the door reads a sign that says "Boiler Room - Abandon all hope all ye who enter here". Several students can be seen in the background with a look of fear on their faces as
De Sade opens the door. The door swings inward with the sound of a gauntlet being shivered down the face of a chalkboard. De Sade steps inside and closes the door with a deep slam of resounding finality. He walks down the stairs and through several small corridors until he comes to four young men tied by their hands to hot steam pipes above. The four men are drenched in sweat as the humid air in the boiler room allows for very little comfort]
MDS: As you can see, these four are going to pay the price for not obeying the rules. This too reminds me of when we used to laugh at Grandpa when he'd head off and go fishing. But we wouldn't be laughing that evening when he'd come back with some whore he picked up in town. But we would be laughing again when the Sheriff would come out to the house and pick his wife up when Grandpa was through with her. You see Grandpa did not obey the rules of normal society and ended up serving 15 years for solicitation of a prostitute. The Sheriff did 10 for being a pimp. These four students have a chance to repent and put their crimes behind them by answering a few questions....
[Principal De Sade steps over to one of the men. The student struggles a bit then realizes that it is all in vain]
MDS: John Hanson, you have been found smoking cigarettes on school property. Do you wish to avert punishment by answering a few questions? [John nods his head in the affirmative] MDS: Is your Principal, moi, a better grappler than say.... "Keen" Kurt Orr? [John nods his head in the affirmative again] MDS: Very good! How about that worthless bumbler Jacob Yoder? [Once more John nods his head in the affirmative] MDS: Where does rain come from? J: What? MDS: John, I asked, where does rain come from? [John thinks foe a minute then answers with apprehension] J: Uhhhh... God is crying??? MDS: Cute! Very cute I must say... though he is crying because of something you did. You have not passed! If one of your compatriots can finish this next
De Sade quote you may all go free.
[John looks expectantly at his friends] MDS; Listen closely.... finish this De Sade quotation , "As I bit into the nectarine, it had a crisp juiciness about it that was very pleasurable, until
I realized it wasn't a nectarine at all, but a..." Now finish it...
[Toby, John, Mark, and Harold mumble among themselves for a few minutes then
Toby looks to De Sade with hope in his eyes]
T: Uhmmm... uhhhh.... an orange? [De Sade throws his head back and laughs with glee] MDS: No you insipid fool! You are as stupid as "Lone Wolf" Anthony Mislan! The answer is... "As I bit into the nectarine, it had a crisp juiciness about it that was very pleasurable, until I realized it wasn't a nectarine at all, but a... HUMAN HEAD!!!"
[De Sade laughs with the pent up evil of eons unleashed] MDS: Karl, prepare them for tonight! K: Yes Mr. De Sade. It will be done. [De Sade walks off as the four men scream in frightened insanity. He walks up the stairs to the hallway proper of the school]
MDS: So, in conclusion you pathetic apes, I will cause harm upon you all. Destruction unparalleled since Nero fiddled as the seven hills of Rome burnt to the ground! But in the mean time, I need to calm down a bit. Sometimes when I feel like killing someone, I do a little trick to calm myself down.
I'll go over to the persons house and ring the doorbell. When the person comes to the door, I'm gone, but you know what I've left on the porch? A jack-o-lantern with a knife stuck in the side of it's head with a note that says "You." After that I usually feel a lot better, and no harm done. Not until I meet them on a dark street.....
[De Sade walks into his office and turns and speaks] MDS: Those that know, do. Those that understand, teach... you however, members of the IIWA, have come to the right place to gain your education. I know. I will teach. You will learn. Class dismissed!
[De Sade smiles and closes the door as the school bell rings as students file out of their classrooms in eerie silence and the scene fades to black]
