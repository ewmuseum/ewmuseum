Free Agent Tag Team

25 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
No BS, here's a sample roleplay. Only original, booked, closed flash feds need apply. Send all inquries to the above email address.. Thanks... ["Are you excited?"] [Those are the words on the lips of every single fan of GRW. For the last few nights the entire GRW has been buzzing about what's been called the biggest upset in the history of GRW, Apocalypse Now goes down to Zero Gravity. The two time Georgia State Tag Team champions fall to a team that's wrestling together for only the second time, and for the first time under the ZG moniker. Their breathtaking aerial display has set the more modest, and grounded, GRW on it's ear. Everyone's wondering exactly how high the Dynamic Duo from Florida can go, and how high can they take the small Georgia promotion with them.]
[Of course, ZG isn't allowing their new found success to go to their heads, either.] [SOMMERS]: Winning those GRW tag team titles is going to be sweet! Dude, do you know how good I, how good WE look in gold?
[Enter Shawn Sommers, former action movie star of "Man Of Action" fame. He stands, the raspberry slushy that Misty promised him in hand, next to a table at the local Dairy Queen. His smooth, clean shaven face is glowing...his deep blue eyes glimmer like stars in the sunlight. A little dressed down today, a pair of black jogging pants and a t-shirt with the ZG logo on the front and the phrase, "Are You Excited?" on the back.]
[His enthusiasm is easily recognized, it's the same sort of youthful exuberance that the fans took to immediately. It's not everyday in this sport that two men with the talent of Shawn Sommers and Jonny Zoom exhibit such an untouched innocence. To these two, it's not about winning the belts, it's about having a good time...something that the fans of this sport just aren't used to.]
[ZOOM]: You know it, daddy-o!! Those straps are going to look Zero Grabulous around our lady-killing waists!!!
[Seated at the table is his partner, Jonny Zoom. The boy genius turned major beach bum. He takes another slurp of his slurpy and grins at Sommers with a pair of red lips that not even a good lipstick can produce, there's nothing for lip reddening like a raspberry slushy!! Zoom shoots Sommers a rather confident look over the mirror lensed Ray-Bans that usually cover his dark brown eyes. As usual, his tan is impeccable, and even in 35 degree weather he wears a tank top to make sure that everyone else knows that it is as perfect as he does. He also wears a pair of jogging pants, monogrammed with the letters "ZG" on the hip.]
[SOMMERS]: So, who gets to be the next in line for the two sexiest budding superstars on the planet?
[ZOOM]: Not sure dude, all I know is that they're called "Domination". [SOMMERS]: Domination? What are they, S & M perverts? They don't wear leather pants with the backs cut out of them, do they?
[Both Sommers and Zoom cringe at the thought.] [ZOOM]: Don't think so dude, I think they're supposed to be, like, really tough and mean and nasty. They're probably just two Joe Schmoes though, they'll probably ask for our autograph...so bring a pen with you.
[SOMMERS]: No doubt, better bring four of those promotional photos...I'm sure that they're girlfriends will want one too.
[ZOOM]: You really think so? [SOMMERS]: Well, I mean, just look at us! [Zoom looks over "Superstar", and then glances down at his own "package"..] [ZOOM]: Good thinking, daddy-o! Y'know I get so used to looking this good that
I almost forget not everyone does.
[SOMMERS]: We need to get rid of some of these photos anyways, since we're going to have to replace them with shots of us with those belts anyways. I'm thinking "action shot" of us doing the Gravity Bomb with the belts on, what d'ya think?
[Out of nowhere...] [MISTY]: I think you need to concentrate a little more on winning the belts, and a little less on what you're going to do once you get a hold of them.
[The fearless leader of these two, Miss Misty, appears on your screen. She's a buxom young 19-year old with a rather girlish smile, and a great mind for this sport. Her auburn hair is done in a french braid and holds tightly to the back of her neck as she looks over her charges with a pair of deep brown eyes. Zero
Gravity is her creation, and she intends on making sure that the guys stay focused on the agenda. She's not devious, nor malicious, just determined...determined to continue the success of her family in this business.
Her oldest brother was very upset when she decided to take up managing the
"Dynamic Duo" from Florida, he was hoping she'd help oversee the career of the youngest boy in her family. Misty chose to go her own way, though. Finally breaking out from under her elder brother's wing and emerging with something she could call her own.]
[Her scandalous body curves are covered by a sweater and a pair of blue jeans, but it's already plain to see why she has become one of the more "popular" GRW personalities with the men. She sits down across from Zoom, with Sommers leaning down on the table between them...]
[SOMMERS]: Don't worry babe, we've got it covered! We're only "The Most Exciting Tag Team In The Universe"! You just stand out there, look pretty, and me and the Zoomster will go in and take care of Domination!
[Misty casts a skeptical eye upon Sommers.] [MISTY]: You really think that it's going to be that easy? [Sommers grins without hesitating] [SOMMERS]: Not a doubt in my mind... [MISTY]: It's time for you two to wake up if you want these tag belts. Because, if you go into the match Halloween night with that kind of attitude, Domination may not only hurt you...they may finish you for good.
[Zoom and Sommers have a look of utter shock on their faces.] [ZOOM & SOMMERS]: They'd hurt us? [MISTY]: In a second. [ZOOM]: But, we're so...so...so...LOVABLE! And, dangit, we've got really awesome tans! [SOMMERS]: Yeah babe!! They wouldn't hurt us, we're Zero Gravity! [MISTY]: And a combined 200 pounds lighter than they are. Volcano almost outweighs you two by himself.
[ZOOM]: *gulp* V-V-Volcano? He sounds mean! [MISTY]: He is, and he and Wick are going to do - [SOMMERS]: What kind of a name is Wick? [MISTY]: Dunno, maybe his mother didn't like him or something...but, that's not important. What is important is that we make sure to get a good gameplan before we head to the ring. ZG is fast enough, and explosive enough to take down
Domination...but you two are going to have to be in the game. One false move from either of you could be the end of the night for us...or the end of the road for Zero Gravity.
[Both Sommers and Zoom drop their heads...] [MISTY]: C'mon guys, why the long faces? [Zoom and Sommers look at each other...] [ZOOM]: Why are you always so down on us? We're just looking to have a little fun. [SOMMERS]: Yeah, you act like you don't think we've got a chance against these
Domination freaks.
[MISTY]: Guys, listen to me. You've got a great chance at taking down Domination, I think you're the better team. But, you two are my two best friends on Earth...and I don't want to see you get hurt either.
[SOMMERS]: That's right!!! No disfiguring this sexy bod of mine! [MISTY]: Yeah, the 14 year old girls would be sooooo upset! [Misty grins] [SOMMERS]: HEY! [Sommers shoots a serious stare in Misty's direction] [ZOOM]: Dude, don't sweat it. After this Sunday, after Domination has been sent into the stratosphere by Zero Gravity, and after we've strapped those Georgia
State Tag Team Title belts on...I'm sure you'll be able to get a chick more your age.
[Sommers turns his glare to Zoom] [SOMMERS]: I'll have you know that the "Superstar" is well known for entertaining the ladies!
[Misty and Jonny roll their eyes and stand from the table. They walk right past
Shawn Sommers who continues to plead his case with them. Shawn whirls in frustration and pursues them out the door...]
[SOMMERS]: Guys, I can get chicks my age...really, I can!!! I promise!! [Misty and Jonny continue ignoring Shawn as they walk out the door of the Dairy
Queen and into the brisk Georgia afternoon. Shawn can continually be heard talking as the "Tantalizing Threesome" walk out to Misty's awaiting jeep. The scene fades into blackness and these words appear...]
Zero Gravity vs. Domination "ZERO Chance" [Fade to black]
