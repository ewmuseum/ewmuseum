Feds Seeking Wrestlers: Pacific Northwest Wrestling out of the WfWA Seeks Wrestlers I'm President [name withheld], and I've spent all of my ewrestling career as a handler in either the indies or with the NeWA, specifically the PNW. Jeff Fox, my partner, talked me into bringing the PNW over to the WWA, and with a little luck we'll be a success. We just need people to join. Anyone and everyone is invited to join. There's going to be no preference given to anyone. If you think you don't have a chance to be in a big angle because the fed has world champions in it, you're wrong. If you want a fresh start I'd advise you to take a look. If you have any questions you can email me or contact me on AIM. The PNW is a fed with a great deal of tradition, and we want to add to it. The main point is having fun, and you will definitely have fun if you join the PNW.
