PLEASE GIVE A CRITIQUE OF THIS ROLE-PLAY!!!! (... or die!)

21 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey folks, Just wandering what you all think about the RP below. I've been worrying that some Americans might not "get" the humour in my role-plays so if you could please give it a quick once over and reply telling me what you all think constructively then that would be great.
Thanks! --- (The screen cuts to the rather disturbing sight of Alexander Strakoi, that fresh faced picture of loveliness himself, standing up against the soiled door of a mottled toilet cubical, somewhere in the arena...)
VOICE: AAAHHHHHHHMMMMMMMMMPPPHHHH!!! OOOOOOOHHHHH!!! Alexander: (bored) You okay in there Pierre? Pierre: JESUS CHRIST!!! THE AGONY!!! Alexander: Ah, I see. Pierre: OH SWEET JESUS, MAKE IT STOP!!! (There is a slight "plopping" sound from behind the cubical door as
Pierre lets out another yell of agonising pain.)
Pierre: (whimpering) .... oh God.... that one was as big as my arm....*sniff*.... Alexander: Never mind Pierre, I'm sure if you your tense abdominal muscles you.... Pierre: AAAHHHHGG!! ..(Another "plopping" sound and then a slight pause)... but surely I NEED that!
Alexander: What? Pierre: Alexander, I think I've just crapped a lung. (Alexander, having possession of a compassionate soul, promptly bursts into a fit of hysterics.)
Alexander: Oh don't be silly Pierre! Pierre: But I'm not, I really do think I needed that last one in order to sustain life. Alexander: Well it's not my fault you decided to drink four bottles of
"Easy-Squeeze Laxative" the night before our first Pay-Per-View event.
Pierre: Oh come on Alexander, there was no booze left and I just HAD to drink something. (sobbing) I was starting to see those jumping blue onions again!
(Again Alexander collapses into a fit of hysterics.) Alexander: How many times do I have to say this Pierre, those onions are just a figment of your alcohol soaked mind, they don't really exist.
Pierre: But what about those green flying tomatoes that you were telling me about earlier...
Alexander: (suddenly serious) No, the tomatoes are different... now just shut up. Pierre: But... Alexander: Just shut up Pierre!! Don't ever mention those tomatoes in my company again okay.... (mumbling to himself) I was collage at the time... everyone experiments...
(The stomach churning sound of an old decrepit toilet flush can be heard as the door to the cubical is flung open to reveal the mottled figure of Pierre de la Bergerac fixing the zipper up on his urine stained tweed trousers. Pierre notices the camera in front of him and a look of shear-unbridled horror can be seen on his unshaven face.)
Pierre: Hang on Alexander, are going to tell me that you've allowed this camera crew to film you while I was inside having a shi...
Alexander: (grinning cheekily) Yup. Pierre: What?! Even when I started to cry? Alexander: (devilishly) Oui. Pierre: Even when I began to think I was the new Dhali Llama? Alexander: I certainly did Pierre! Pierre: YOU BASTARD!! Alexander: (fawning innocence) What? Pierre: Alexander, I've just had the entire world witness me having probably the most death-deifying bowel movements I have ever experienced in my entire life and you say "what?"!!
Alexander: Yeah. Pierre: (fuming) Why did you let them film?? Alexander: I thought it would be quite funny actually. (sniffs the air) And it was. (Alexander absentmindedly begins to whistle.) Pierre: (changing subject) Well..... aren't you supposed to be doing last minuet warm-ups for your match against Bob Bobson?
Alexander: Don't worry Pierre, I've already spoken to my doctor and he says that I can have a full medical as soon as I win the match.
Apparently he suspects that Bob might well be carrying the pox. Pierre: But have you gone over your fight strategy? Alexander: Of course! What kind of snivelling little son-of-bricklayer do you take me for? Believe me Pierre; after I'm done beating Bob into a living turd the so-called "wrestlers" in the
NEO will soon be dribbling down the left leg in fear.
Pierre: Well you certainly seem confident of your chances. Alexander: (grinning, slyly) Oh I am Pierre! I am! (With that Alexander turns and leaves the restroom, leaving Pierre to sniff his right index finger with an almost unhealthy fascination!
Anyway, the screen then fades back into the wrestling event.)
