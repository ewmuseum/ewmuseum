Imperia Wrestling Corporation

Feds Seeking Wrestlers · July 2003

Feds Seeking Wrestlers: Imperia Wrestling Corporation We are a GREAT e-fed looking for good talent that is relieable and ready to take that step into the spotlight. Our roster is deep in talent and very diverse. Cards are always on time. We work a strat system for matches, and give the wrestler more creative control than the next federation. We still reserve the right to deny certain things, but, we are open minded people. Now, I bet you wanna know some information about the federation. First, we have weekly cards on sunday, a two roleplay limt and PPV's every three months with a three rp limit. The Imperia Wrestling Franchise is unique in that it takes into account every facet of real wrestling -- wrestlers' physical and mental attributes, wrestlers' alignments, gimmick matches, wrestling techniques, and overall entertainment -- as well as players' participation with regard to Role-Plays (RPs) and Strategies (Strats). In addition, the simulation of wrestling is based on NHB and MMA competition (UFC/PRIDE) rather than professional wrestling, making IWF totally unique in every way! All wrestlers and tag teams created must be fictional. Please be original and creative when you are designing your character, but remember to be realistic. Also, be sure not to leave any blank fields on the character creation form. When you create your character, you must rate the attributes Strength, Agility, Endurance, and Intelligence. The higher you place your Endurance and Intelligence, the better you you will be expected to RP. These scores may be adjusted without notice based on your performance. Please note that there are size restrictions to Strength and Agility as follows: * When choosing a Finisher, make sure your choice is realistic and compatible with the fighting styles of the wrestler. * When composing a wrestler's history, be thorough and creative. A wrestler with a rich background is much more interesting and entertaining. Also, a wrestler's background helps you enrich RPs while also helping writers write your wrestler's matches. The base concept behind IWF RPs is the revolutionary "Closed Circuit Television" concept, which was created due to the fact that the "Realism" concept that serves as the base for IWF itself needed to be balanced in order to allow handlers more creative freedom. The "Closed Circuit Television" concept compliments the "Realism" concept by protecting characters from legal consequences. Due to the "Realism" concept, tapes of RPs in which characters participate in illegal activities would normally lead to characters being arrested for their crimes. With the "Closed Circuit Television" concept, only IWF employees are allowed to view RPs, and only in the arena where the next event is to take place, thus protecting characters from having their own RPs used against them. This means, basically, that you need not worry about whether certain activities are illegal or not. You may RP as often as you like for character and angle development, but remember that only TWO RPs count toward your match, THREE RPs for a Pay-Per-View match. RPs are judged by depth and overall quality. Your match must be mentioned in the RP and talked about, but "Trash-Talking" and "The Match Interview" need not be the center of the RP or even the basis of the RP. In fact, "Plot-Driven" RPs are most favorable and most likely to bring about a win. The content of the RP is the key; spelling, grammar, format, and other such things DO NOT MATTER. Every wrestler will win matches and every wrestler will lose matches; avoiding losing is IMPOSSIBLE. Be careful who you challenge. If you always challenge the toughest opponents, you will find your wrestling losing more often. Carefully consider players' RPing abilities and wrestlers' attributes when making challenges. You will usually be booked for matches without having to make a challenge, in which case you will always be given an equal opponent. A wrestler advances in the rankings by wrestling in matches. The outcome of each match determines how many ranking points a wrestler receives. A clean loss is worth 1 point, a loss by disqualification (DQ) or countout (CO) is worth 2 points, a loss due to a screwjob is worth 3 points, a draw is worth 5 points, a time limit draw is worth 6 points, a win due to a screwjob is worth 8 points, a win by DQ or CO is worth 9 points, and a clean win is worth 10 points. * The the Imperia Wrestling Franchise Champion is always ranked #1, regardless of points. Slightly outRPing a Champion will only result in a title change if it makes sense angle-wise. Otherwise, the Champion must be greatly outRPed for the title change to occur. RPs are uncensored. the Imperia Wrestling Franchise is a firm supporter of the First Amendment Right to freedom of speech. This does NOT mean that "anything goes" on the RP Board or on the OOC Board. Note that discussion of abortion, religion, or politics on the OOC Board is FORBIDDEN. For all other things, use common sense. Complaining is frowned upon, but constructive criticism and asking for explanations to decisions is allowed as long as you do it professionally and without an attitude. Complaining about losing matches, however, is FORBIDDEN. There will be NO exceptions to this rule. IWF is fun overall, and a good place to meet your e-fed needs. if I managed to keep your eyes this long, then look at the page and join!! Any questions, e-mail me.
