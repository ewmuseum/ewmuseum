[Debut RP] "The Lethal Injection" Darren Scott(Las)

29 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: The camera is placed in front of what looks to be an island. The camera is sort of bobbling up and down, so you can tell it's on a boat. The camera slowly turns and a man is seen sitting there, in the driver's seat. He is wearing a pair of Rusty board shorts, and a silver cross, with a pair of silver
Oakleys. His hair is spiked up and looks to be _JUST DYED_ blonde, and he is looking off into the distance. When he turns to the camera, he takes a swig of a bottle of water he is holding then begins to speak.]
Man: It's been a long time comin', but I finally arrived. You have all been hearing the rumors, the talk amongst the crowd. Well, here I am. For those of you that don't know, get a clue. . . I am "_THE_ Lethal Injection" Darren
Scott. Straight out of the training house of none other than Killer Kowalski, and ready to rough some shit up.
[He tightens the bottled water's lid, and throws it to the front of the speed boat. He then regains his speech.]
DS: I think I am finally ready to break into the wrestling world, and see what
I can do with all that I have learned. I am not going to sit here, and bore you all with a long, life story. . . but I am going to shed some light on myself. I grew up with wrestling great Scotty Drake, he's like a brother to me. I saw what career he choose, and decided to look into it a little. Well, it turns out
I have found a passion, a career I love. You know, I once was told, "If you find a career you love, you never have to work one day in your life.", which is true. I have chosen a career I love, and I haven't found myself working one time; and I guarantee none of you wrestlers can work me one time. Not saying I am a wrestling god or anything, but I am pretty damn good! Nothing further to say... except, if and when you wrestle me, you will get hit with the
__LETHAL__ __INJECTION__ because I am... __TOO__ __LETHAL__ __FOR__ __MY__ __OWN__ __GOOD!!__ [The camera fades off of Scott and spins slowly. It refocuses on a setting sun as the screen fades out, and the words "Lethal Injection" appear on your screen in yellow writing.]
-Las "Big names don't make a fed; a fed makes the big names." -Myself, 1/24/99
