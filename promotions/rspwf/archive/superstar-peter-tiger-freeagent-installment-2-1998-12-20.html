"Superstar" Peter Tiger (FreeAgent) Installment # 2

20 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
" S U P E R S T A R " Peter Tiger "Superstar" Peter Tiger's Free Agency Installment Numer 2 [ As a white limosine pulls up along side of the Pepsi arena, in Albany New
York, the camera shows the seventeen thousand plus on there feet becasue they know what it is about to happen. The greatest wrestler ever to set foot inside of the ring is about to make his way down the isle. The camera cuts back to the man who is walking down the hallway toward the entrance ramp with ten security guards behind him. The roar of the crowd can be heard as "Superstars
Dream" comes over the loudspeaker and the man stops right behind the curtain ]
Announcer: Ladies and Gentlemen, about to make his way down the isle. He weighs in at two hundred and sixtyfive founds, he hails from right here in
Albany New York, ladies and Gentlemen, I give to you "Superstar" Peter Tiger!
[ Tiger brusts through the curtain and opens his arms wide to take the enormous chorus of cheers, and admiration. He walks down to the ring and rips the microphone out of the hands of the announcer, who is taken aback by this manuver and leaves the ring. He then motions for everyone to be quiet, and puts a stern look on his face ]
Tiger: Alright listen up, right now I want all of you rickshaw ridin, pig wrestling, mud rolling, hehaw rejects to shut your mouths, clean out your ears, and pay close attention to what i have to say.
[ The crowd sits in astonishment, and a few boos are let out ] Tiger: The simple fact of the matter is, I am too good for Albany, New York. In fact I am too good for this whole country. That is why i am now making my new home home in Blackpool, England. You see over in England people have class, people have money, and people have the common sence to realize that
"Superstar" Peter Tiger is the biggest wrestler ever to hit the ring, and that there is no one out there who can even put a candle up my wrestling abilities.
[ The crowd lets out a huge chorus of boos, and they stand and begin to throw things inside the ring ]
Tiger: Yeah, go on ahead and throw all of the garbage you want, becasue thats all you people are, is garbage, and that is why i am leaving this hoprrible country. As a wrestler I need work, and since I cannot find any federation here in the grand Old Uniuted States of Garbage that has enough talent to match up hold for hold with me. So at least i know that over in the United Kingdom, where the men work hard at there craft, and where the general population does not consist of beer guzzling, big gutted, slobs who will never amount to anthing, and should be classified as a living, breathing, waste of space.
[ The boos from the crowd are defening, Tiger begins to flip them off, and more trash is thrown into the ring, very disgruntled Tiger begins to speak. ]
Tiger: Thats it, I've had enough of you scumbags. The next time you see me, it will be on telivision, thousands of miles away from all of you American dirtbags. Just know thats it people like you who make me sick to my stomach knowing that I too am an American. I'm out.
[ Tiger steps up onto the second rope and spreads his arms out to take in the boos from the fans. The look on his face is one of endulgence. He loves hearing the boos, knowing that the Americans hate him as much as he hates them.
Peter leaves the ringside area, and heads back into the dressing room where his security guards lead him through the hall way, and to the back of the arena, where his Limosine is waiting. He gets in and it pulls away as the screen fades to black ]
" S U P E R S T A R " Peter Tiger
