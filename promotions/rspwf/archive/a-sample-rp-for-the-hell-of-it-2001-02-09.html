A Sample Rp for the hell of it....

9 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It might be cool if you didn't flame. I would prefer mature, intelligent discussion about it please and thank you
:-) ************************ [The scene opens up in an empty, dark bar. The chairs have been placed upside down on table, each placed neatly and in order. The hardwood like floor has the dull shine of just being cleaned. A small dance floor with a corner DJ booth occupy on end of the pub. A long, black bar complete with taps, glasses and all the typical accessories stretches along a wall. Black and white pictures and sports memorabilia, most of it relating to boxing or wrestling, is scattered here and there decorating the room quite nicely. All said and done it has a nice, somber, ambient feel to it. Somewhere you would go and relax with friends, sharing beers and stories.]
[There is but on person in this establishment and he sits on a stool about 3/4 of the length down it. A half empty glass of beer along with a half eaten sandwich sit forgotten in front of the large stack of papers he is looking through. This person? Rosco Riggins of course.]
[Riggins is a well built man with thick wide shoulders and well proportioned limbs. A handsome but strong featured face sits upon a muscular neck and is framed by a nearly bald scalp. Only a small shading of dark hair is visible, much like a 5 o'clock shadow. Ice blue eyes peer out from thing rimmed, stylish glasses, their intensity
even visible in the scant light afforded to his reading. Riggins is dressed in a pair of jeans and dark T-shirt, once again proving that not every wrestler wears their own merchandise all the time despite how
some may dress. You would think they could afford good clothes considering many of them have huge mansions and expensive vehicles, but
I digress.] [The large stack of documents that he reads are unknown as far as content goes but he seems highly engrossed in them. So engrossed he does not even look up as the door opens. Instead he merely waves them off.]
RIGGINS: "We are closed, go away." [A familiar whisper of a voice wafts over, catching his attention.] VOICE: "Good old Rosco. Up at this hour doing work.....and alone." [The voice is stern and gravely yet full of wisdom. Riggins recognizes this voice. His eyes widen. His jaw drops. He forgets everything he is doing and drops his pen, turning his head ever so slowly towards the doorway.....
.............To see no one.] RIGGINS: "Damn I need to get some sleep." [Riggins mutters to himself under his breath, something indiscernible, and walks over to the slightly opened doorway. A slight breeze opens it even more, revealing the sounds and scents of a sleeping city. A siren can be heard off in the distance, quickly fading away. He looks around, feeling refreshed in the crisp night air. He deeply inhales, closing his eyes and trying to drain out the army of thoughts currently invading his mind. A stretch of the arms is interrupted by the whirring and crunching caused by a streetcleaner going by. ]
[The loud noise of the heavy engine drowns out the regular sounds. It is so loud that Riggins doesn't even hear the meows from below, only realizing there is someone there when he looks down, wondering what is
rubbing his leg. A ragged, dirty, scrawny cat rubs it mal-nutritioned hide against him, letting out a shrill cry of hunger and loneliness.] RIGGINS(lifting his leg away): "Shoo cat. Go. Get out of here. Go bug some homeless person."
[Despite his best efforts the feline seems to not want to go away, instead it scurries past him and through the open door into the bar.
Riggins lets out a big sigh while shaking his head and turns to go after it. Walking in he sees the poor animal sitting right in the middle of the floor, quiet as a mouse, staring right at him. ]
RIGGINS: "Well you certainly are persistent. I suppose it wouldn't hurt for me to give you a little bowl of milk now would it?"
[As if answering , the cat gives a quiet meow, following Riggins behind the bar. A few moments later the cat is happily lapping at a bowl of milk, a small plate of what appears to be fish right beside it. Riggins has went back to his papers, seemingly making progress through the pile. The homeless cat interrupts him once again though, hopping onto the ;long bar and walking right in front of Riggins, over papers and all.]
RIGGINS: "Geez! Don't you have any manners?" [Riggins collects everything as the cat knocks the papers about. He keeps on watching it with an interested gaze as it walks down the bar some more only to stop on another small gathering of papers. Riggins looks on curiously as the cat simply stops and stares at him.]
[He stares back.] [It keeps staring.] [He keeps staring.] [It stares and stares.] [Then he quits realizing having a staring contest with a cat is useless, much like wrestlers doing movies. He shakes it off and walks over wondering what the cat is up to. ] RIGGINS: "Now what are you doing over here cat? Causing trouble are we? Well think you should just come back over here."
[Riggins cradles the cat up in his left arms and is about to walk away when he sees just what it was the cat was sitting on: a newspaper and the latest company newsflash from the WWA.]
RIGGINS: "Well you certainly have strange taste for a homeless cat." [Once again he is answered with a shrill meow.] RIGGINS: "Well let's see what you were so interested in." [Riggins picks up the newspapers and right away sees the big headline.] WORLD WRESTLING ASSOCIATION IN LEGAL TROUBLE [The former PTA member and former NATV champion reads intently over the article, his gaze not faltering once. The cat remains motionless in his arms almost as if reading it himself....or herself. I don't think
Riggins has really lifted up it's tail so who knows what it is. Rosco continues reading on, lifting an eyebrow or even scoffing at what he is reading. He goes on for several more seconds. Finally done he rolls the paper up in a bundle, puts the cat down on the bar and walks around
to the back area of it. The paper gets unceremoniously dumped in the garbage and he walks over, pulling out a bottle of water from one of the many fridges, opening with a swift twist of the wrist.]
[He leans against the sink area, taking a deep drink only to notice the cat standing there once again staring right at him. ]
RIGGINS: "What?" [The cat keeps looking on, crooking it's head at a weird angle almost as if questioning him. Riggins tries to ignore him but he just cannot
seem to, the animal never taking it's glare away. This of course starts to make him as uncomfortable as can be, forcing him to finally speak up.] RIGGINS: "WHAT? What do you want? " [Getting even more odd the cat looks down at the garbage can, meowing once more. Riggins looks down as well and then back at the cat who is once again staring at him unblinkingly.]
RIGGINS: "The newspaper? What about it? Wondering what I thought about the article? " [The feline keeps staring.] RIGGINS: "Well if you really wanna know I will tell you. All it talked about was Spazzz getting killed on TV. They were in an uproar with all kinds of groups wanting to shut the WWA down and have Capitol charged with murder."
[Still it stares, not moving even the most minute bit.] RIGGINS: "And I bet you want to know what my opinion was? Well how about I tell you and then you quit staring."
[Nothing happens, it is just a cat after all. All it does is stare.] RIGGINS: "What am I supposed to think. Spazzz wasn't a friend or anything. I mean we didn't really talk, we never traveled together. He just happened to be in the same company as me. As a matter of fact we never were in the ring together except for that one tag match
against Capitol and whoever else it was. All I remember is stretching poor Cap out. After even before that not a thing was said. But yeah, he was killed and it was a damn shame to see that happen to anyone." [The weird cat keeps looking on, sitting up straight, almost like it is interested in what he is saying.]
RIGGINS: "I don't really know what to think. Everyone knows what a piece of sh*t Capitol is so it isn't a surprise he would go to far.
That far though? Only one man who I thought had it in him to do something like that and he is in Alcatraz. Makes you wonder though
when they draw the line. It is not the first time he got out of hand. Look at when he broke Tremor Christ's neck. Look at any segment of his on War of the Words or on Monday Live. He is out of control but for some frigging reason the schmoes in the WWA offices never did
anything about it and they aren't now. Who am I to talk though. They let crap like Vindy and Detson to run around, impregnating other people's wives and stealing children. They are the bosses, obviously they know better."
[Riggins takes a long drink as his new found friend looks on.] RIGGINS: "But really Cat, I don't worry about that kind of stuff. I need to only worry about one guy and that is Rosco Riggins. I will stay away from all that crap and worry about what I got on my plate.
It's about time the WWA starting respecting who I am and that is exactly what I am going to be out to prove. That I am indeed a viable contender around here and it is about time I got my chances. I never
worked my ass this last year for nothing. Name anyone else who has beaten three former world champions and ran another out of the WWA. Hell I beat Dark Ages by myself and they beat Stone and Haley, two schmoes who seem to always be put ahead of me. Hopefully my little demonstration on Live showed them I mean business. Those words that came out of my mouth just weren't for ratings."
[Riggins gets up from his leaning position, walking down the space between the bar and the back wall. He walks over depositing his empty bottle in a cardboard box laying on the floor. He turns around, to go back only to see that strange cat standing right there beside him on the bar, atop the WWA newsflash. It looks up at him and his incredulous look of disbelief, pawing at the papers below him. Riggins can't help but a chuckle, a slight and I mean _slight_ smile coming over his normally very serious visage.]
RIGGINS: "Well you certainly are persistent. Let me take a look at that then, there must be something important on it. You wouldn't be making this fuss for nothing."
[Riggins picks up the papers, quickly scanning over and turning the pages. His eyes widen as he gets to the second page.]
RIGGINS: "Maybe they are noticing finally. Ranked second behind the two the IN champ. Both big belt holders ahead of me and that is it. Not bad at all. " [Riggins shakes his head approvingly, reading on. He puts the paper down picking up the cat once more.]
RIGGINS: "Bedrock in two weeks, kitty then my chance at the entire
WWA. I think these idiots are going to need to take more notice of me, don't you think? But we will only worry about Bedrock. Let's go Cat,
I am damn tired."
[Riggins hits a switch on the wall then.......black.] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/
