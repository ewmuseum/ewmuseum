Just above the mountains we see a bright orange sun also
spray-painted on the wall.] [Suddenly, As our view is still empty of actual people and items some bells begin to ring faintly. A voice is heard just over the bells..]
Voice: " Japan has fought for many things...We have fought for our heritage, our respect and what we thought was our freedom."
[The bells begin to pick up in tempo and volume...] Voice: "This time Japan is fighting to be heard...This time Japan is fighting to be seen, and most importantly this time Japan is _really_ Fighting for respect."
[Into the picture comes an older man...Prolly in his early fifty's, He has some gray hairs mixed in with his long black hair. He has a goatee made of almost all gray hair, and a smile on his face so big, You would of thought he just got threw doing something great.]
Man: "I am not the leader of this war, I am just a voice..I am just a consultant to the man sitting on the throne..I am Father Atashi, Proud Father of Horo Atashi...A man born into this world to lead, To bring respect to our country and most importantly love our country no matter what, Even when it makes mistakes. Something in which we all do."
[The old man steps a little closer to the camera, and focuses in on the lens. The bells stop ringing, and he continues with his words, but this time there a little more stern, as that huge smile no longer exist on his face.]
Father Atashi: I have been to America, and seen what the "Land of opportunity" has for people from other countries. We were pushed away there, and now we return home. Hopefully we will not be pushed away here in our own home. If we are though we are willing to struggle and survive. This is our home, and we will not be pushed away any longer. This is where we belong, and this is where we will make it no matter the situation."
[Father Atashi holds his arms out and looks away from the camera to the side at something. At this time into the picture comes a young man of very good qualities on the outer half. Long black hair, excellent body and dark eyes.
Father Atashi puts his arm on the man's shoulder, and looks back into the camera. The young man also looks into the camera with a very straight face.]
Father Atashi: "This is my son, this is the leader of the war for respect, and hopefully the man all of you in Japan will follow with at least your hearts if nothing else...He may be small in size, but I promise you all he is big in heart. My son has been around the world, and seen it all. He has trained in dungeons and in your typical gymnasiums...I promise you Kuramochi and Leon De
Negro you have yourself an excellent partner for this match coming up in TOP."
"You may or may not believe that when I say it, but soon you will find out that you indeed do have yourself a great partner, while men such as Tenzan, Brat and
HERO have themselves a great opponent."
[Father Atashi glances at his son, and with a smile on his face like we saw at the start continues.]
Father Atashi: "I would let Horo tell you how he feel's on camera about being in TOP, But that is not something he wishes to do quite yet. Horo promised me he wants to let himself sell himself, and not let his words sell himself.
Japan, This man loves you and is willing to give you all he has got every time he has the oppurtunity. Whether it be in six man tag matches, Tag team matches or in single competition My son will give you his all."
[Quickly you see Horo's hand pop up to his heart, and he holds his hand over his heart nodding with the words his father is saying.]
Father Atashi: "He will wrestle with his heart, He will fight with his mind and
He will make sure the Fire is unleashed."
[Both father and son smile towards the camera....] Father Atashi: "TOP, Prepare for Horo Atashi if you can, but be sure to believe me when I say. You have never seen a man with such class as my son. Leon De
Negro, and Kuramochi once again I say thank your lucky stars he is wrestling with you, Instead of against you...As for the other gentleman. You might want to ask yourself what exactly you did wrong to be at this moment in present time..."
" HERO, Tenzan and Brat are definitely the bigger names in this match. They are definitely the favorite to win, but with the speed and power of the kick's my son has. That chould change in a moment of seconds. Horo returns to Japan this
Sunday, and beleive me we want the homecoming to end as all other people want there homecoming to end...._IN VICTORY_.."
[The two men embrace in a moment of love, Respect and compassion, as the bells once again begin to play the camera slowly fades out.]
============ DAVID BISHOP ============ (The shot opens into the locker room of the massive David Bishop. He is being filmed from an up shot, making the already gargantuan shooter look more intimidating as he towers over the camera. Glare from his bald head obscures the shooter, leaving only his odd outline of a man with his head fussed to his shoulders. The light shifts revealing his cold eyes gazing down into the camera.)
Bishop: Shiro Mayashi- they tell me you've been trained by that Fookada guy. Well if it's true, I pity you. Because having seen that tomato can in action, it's clear he never learned when to quit, so I don't hold out much hope for you knowing when to quit. Fookada should of quit five- ten years ago, if he wanted to ensure any quality of life. But you need to quit today, because unlike these sandal wearing sissys, I won't give you chance to once that bell rings.
There won't be any kid gloves used to handle you like Koyama used on your coach. I am a fighter, a warrior, the last of the gladiators, and when I play, I play for keeps. You step in that ring, you had better be willing to deal with what
I send your way. And what I'm sending is pain. Suffering, and enough leather to cover an entire chapter of the Hell's Angles. You got a problem with all this, you speak up now, cause this is your last chance to do something about it. You got second thoughts about having to drink all you meals through a straw, you speak up now, because when that bell rings I'm in control. You may think Fookada prepared you for me, but he couldn't. He's never fought any one with my ground game skills. He's never fought any one with my striking skills.
More importantly, neither have you, because if you had fought some one as good as me. You wouldn't be in wrestling today.
See, I'm a little like you're coach, Fookada, I don't know when to quit either. I don't stop when I hear the bones crack I push harder! When your squirming gets more frantic, I don't let go, I squeeze harder! When the blood starts to flow, I don't pull back, I hit HARDER! (Bishop inhales deeply, trying to savor the aroma of battle in his mind's eye.) When that scent hits, that potent brew of blood, and sweat, and fear, that is when I am the most deadly. Because at that moment, you stop being a threat to me, and you start being an example to every one else. You quit being my opponent, and you start being an example to Taylor as to what a REAL AMERICAN
WRESTLER is capable of. When you are no longer a threat to me, you become practice for when I systematically destroy Koyama for the belt. You become a warning to the likes of Morita and Kojima.
At that point, Mayashi, you cease to be an active participant in the match. You will no longer matter, to me or anyone else. You will have gone from being an opponent, to being a statistic. You will be just another notch on my belt, another line in the win column, another zero to my pay check. If you wait till the this point in the match to give in, it will already be too late. The damage will be done. You arm might be broken, your nose might be busted, I don't really care. You might care about two months of apple sauce, and if you do, now is the time to speak up.
(Bishop cups his hand to his ear as if to listen.) What's that? You've nothing to say? Well don't say I didn't warn you. (The room fills up with steam till you can no longer see Bishop.) ====== B.O.A. ====== [Interior of a small room. Medium shot of TOP announcer, Hadi Moditian, in a black business suit, seated in a swivel chair.]
Moditian: Good evening, TOP fans. Hadi Moditian, once again. Tonight, part 2 of my discussion with the members of the Badass Offensive Assault, or better known as simply BOA, Rick Taylor and Kiyoshi Sakai. Gentlemen, good to speak with you once again.
[Camera switches shots to Taylor and Sakai, both sitting in swivel chairs as well, both dressed in black training pants with white stripes down the legs, and black and gray BOA logo hooded sweatshirts. Each man nods in acknowledgement to Moditian.]
Moditian: Sakai, you are one of our lightest heavyweights in TOP at this time. In fact, I believe the only heavyweight we have smaller than you is Takayuki
Morita, one of your opponents at TOP's first card back in Korakuen Hall, this weekend. Do you feel that you will have a hard time being taken seriously as a heavyweight, when you are only 3 pounds above the limit for being a junior weight?
Sakai: Well, some people might have a problem with it, but I know that I am not one to be taken lightly, and I know that I am more than capable of holding my own against the largest of opponents. I am a heavyweight, and I will show that I am deserving of the respect that that distinction commands, as TOP re-establishes ourselves. Soon enough, I feel that I will prove to even the most hardened of critics that I am a heavyweight to be taken seriously.
Moditian: Any possible future plans of dropping a few pounds in order to make a run at the Jr. title?
Sakai: I don't see that as very likely. Right now, my mindset is on doing everything I can to make BOA as legitimate a force as possible, and becoming the Tag Team Champions with Rick as soon as a tournament can be organized. I do not see dropping weight and trying to get a hold of the Jr. title as a likely path for me to take at this time, or in the future. But I never say never.
Moditian: You mentioned the yet-to-be-decided TOP Tag Team Championships, both of you have been talking about them quite frequently as of late. Mr. Fujiwara has made no announcements about a tournament being organized any time soon, but both of you speak of it often. Why the insistance?
Taylor: We know that Mr. Fujiwara has a lot of other things on his mind, primarily with repairing his own public image, as well as whatever friction there is between him and Koyama at the moment. Right now, making TOP a reliable name in this sport is his #1 concern. But we also feel that we are the #1 team in TOP. And when the time comes for Mr. Fujiwara to organize a tournament for the Tag Titles, Sakai and I fully belive that we are going to wear those titles.
Moditian: Rick, your path to this moment has been unique. When you were 19, you were discovered by a Royal Japan talent scout and you then began training in the Royal Japan Dojo. In fact, you have almost been brought up entirely in the Japanese system. Recently, the Weekly Gong called you an "adopted son" of the wrestling fans of Japan, that you are "as close to a native star without being born in Japan." What do you think about these statements?
Taylor: I think it's incredible that the Gong spoke so highly of me. I don't think there is a single honor that I could be presented with than to be viewed on the same level, and with as much love as the biggest native stars here in
Japan. Over my career, especially the last three years I've been wrestling exclusively in Japan, the Japanese fans have become my favorite audience. No other corwd has made me feel so appreciated and loved as the fans here in Japan. And I'd be lying if I said I wasn't just a bit jealous of the biggest native stars here in Japan, because for a long time, I just lived with the assumption that I'd never be able to get that kind of reaction, even though the fans liked me. But recently, the fans have shown me appeciation and love that I never thought possible. When the fans here react for me like that, it's unlike anything I ever felt wrestling anywhere else in the world. Not even wrestling as a big name in my hometown did I ever feel as loved, appreciated, and accepted as I have here in Japan. If the fans here truly have accepted me as a
"adopted son," and view me as being on the level of the biggest native stars here, then I can't think of a greater honor that I could possibly receive.
Moditian: What thoughts do you have concerning your match against Champion
Koyama and Morita this weekend?
Taylor: This match will give us a chance to truly show off what BOA is all about, and what we are capable of. The fans didn't see the whole picture at the Fan Expo, because we ended up facing less than stellar opponents. But I fully look forward to the challenge we'll face this weekend. I don't think any fan at Korakuen Hall will leave on the twelfth thinking that the main event was less than characteristic of what BOA is all about.
Sakai: The twelfth is only the beginning for BOA in TOP. Every fan there will see what we are all about. TOP will prove how good we really are, and BOA will be leading the charge.
Moditian: Thank you for your time, gentlemen. [Both men nod in response, as we fade to black.] ========== KEIJI KOBE ========== (The camera opens up onto the orange and black mask of the Iron Chef, Keiji
Kobe, standing in front of the logo of TOP for what looks like a promotion for their first official show. He seems truly excited to be in front of the camera, but his enthusiasm truly comes from thinking about his up coming match.
An event he's work toward the past several weeks.) Kobe: Kobe vs Suzuki, the Iron Chef vs Jet Jaguar, it's been a match the fans have clamored for years and every match maker in Japan has been scrambling to make for almost as long. We were nearly their last year, and thanks to the work of Fujiwara, and certain some fans of mine in high places, we're about to give you the fans what you asked for. The first step is this weekend, when I team with Mike Johnson, a man who's been called the Dynamite Kid reborn to take on Suzuki and his friend, Asakino. Imagine the possibilities for a second,
Suzuki and myself in the same ring. It's a fans dream come true. No, not just a fan's dream, it's a dream for me too, and for Suzuki.
Suzuki wants to face me because he knows what I'm capable of. He knows that I able to push him farther than he's ever gone. He knows I can challenge him like no man ever has. He knows I can beat him, so he'll have to work harder than ever. Then there is Johnson. Special since this gaijin doesn't feel the need to motion to his testicles every five seconds. He doesn't try to scare people. He wrestles, and he doesn't do it for money. He wrestles for respect.
I look at him, and I see limitless potential, he's got a good heart, and I know
I can work with him. And then there is that drunken lout, Asakino. A man so out of touch with reality he compares ME, Keiji Kobe, with TENZAN. A man so full of liquid courage he calls his team unbeatable, and yet he must hide behind the protective skirts of reporters and appointed officials. I may be the Iron Chef, but it seems Asakino has perfected the recipe for disaster.
I don't know what bottle you crawled out of to spew your lies from, nor do I care. All I wonder is how many reporters were in that bottle with you that could take your drunken ramblings as actual news. Not that any of this coverage you get bothers me. Who am I kidding? Yes, it does bother me,
Asakino. I'm bothered that the press portrays you as star in Mexico when your name couldn't attract enough fans to fill a phone both. I'm bothered that
Fujiwara has to waste valuable money looking to make sure you don't make a bigger fool out of yourself. Not that any of his efforts will matter after our match.
You see, Fujiwara can send men in black to protect you from your own short comings out of the ring, but he can't protect you in the ring. This weekend, when you face me, I will make plain what so many have tried to hide. That in the face of real competition, you just don't measure up. I will exploit your every weakness, expose your every flaw, until you lay helpless pinned to the mat. Why do I do this? For one, because you're way to cocky. But I'm not beating you simply because you're pompous jerk, Asakino. I'm going to beat you because in your own words you are standing between me and the TOP title. A belt I would crawl on my belly through broken glass to get at. Beating you we be just frosting, the fact that beating you will put me that much closer to my goal is the real cake.
Last week, I made a challenge to Suzuki, he accepted. So building on that success I would like to make another challenge, this time to the fans. Not just the fans of TOP, but the fans of JPA, of GAMMA, of Neo Japan, of Burning
Japan, of SHVF, and even TATAMI. I challenge you to buy a ticket to TOP's show and see the first match of what is sure to be classic. I challenge you to come witness the Junior revolution that myself and Johnson-san will usher in. I challenge you come to the table and taste the true flavor of the Iron Chef, because if you do. You will walk away satisfied.

[ More applause and fade out.] ================== BRAT DRAGON MITSU ================== [SCENE: The TOP Headquarters in Tokyo, Japan. The press room to be exact. The room is nearly full of reporters patiently awaiting tonight's guest. After what seems like forever, an unknown TOP official walks to the podium on the stage in front of the room. The chatter amongst the crowd stops as the official clears his throat.]
TOP Official: "Ladies and gentleman, join me in please welcoming, TOP JR Superstar...BRAT Dragon Mitsu!" [A polite applause from the assembled reporters. After a moment, Mitsu has still not come out. The official looks confused as he whispers frantically towards the off-stage area. Something is going on just behind the curtain. It is another moment before the official looks to the curtain with an apologetic look, and steps back to the podium.]
TOP Official: "Ladies and gentleman, I am deeply sorry for my mistake in presenting Mr. Mitsu. If you all will allow me another chance, that'd be golden. Now please join me in welcoming...'THE MEDIA DARLING' BRAT Dragon
Mitsu!"
[The crowd again applauds, this time a little bit confused. Mitsu steps out from behind the curtain wearing black dress pants and a sleeveless silk shirt meant to accentuate his large arms. His black hair is currently pulled up into a tight pony tail and his goatee is freshly trimmed. Mitsu looks out over the reporters with an amused look of contempt. He steps up to the podium and begins to speak.]
BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Well, well, well. A SELLOUT crowd here in the TOP press room, huh? All for the 'Media Darling' BRAT Dragon Mitsu! Seems like we won't be having the same troubles as we had the first time around, will we folks? No.
This time you know who the future of Japanese Junior weight wrestling is! I'm here to take a few questions...so begin."
[A young, female reporter near the front stands up.] Reporter #1: "You are one of the few stars returning to TOP. Could you please briefly describe the difference between this time and the last time?"
BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Sure sweet-baby. During the last run of TOP we had guys like Hayato Ikeda, Takao Kimura and a few other upstarts. They were good, but they weren't on MY level at all. That is why none of them returned for another go. Hey, at least they were smart."
[A reporter sitting in shadows near the back speaks without standing up.] Reporter #2: "BRAT, you recently teamed with HERO Ishikawa and Yoshikazu TENZAN. There are rumors that the three of you could make a regular alliance. Why would you do that? History has you pegged as a loner. Could you comment on those two and that situation, please?"
BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Well Mr. Reporter-Who-Couldn't-Be-Bothered-To-Show-Up-Early-Enough-To-Get-A-Decent-Seat,
I'd be happy to. HERO is a bit goofy, I'll admit it. He's a strange little man who is obsessed with calling attention to his own crotch. And TENZAN? That little boy wears better make-up than most of the women in the Tokyo area. I will go on record right now and admit that neither is the photogenic, made for
TV, Media Darling that I am. So why team with these two? You saw TENZAN and
HERO in the ring at the Fan Expo. You saw what they could do. If the three of us were to ban together, there isn't anything we couldn't accomplish. Shoji
Suzuki would fall, along with his pet dog Kazuya Asakino. More importantly though, ALL the attention would be on us! Whether we are a regular thing or not remains to be seen, but if we were to get together, believe me...you'd all feel it. Next question."
[A chunky reporter stands up on the left side of the room.] BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Yes, Fat-Wrestling-Writer. Ask your question." Reporter #3: "You mentioned Shoji Suzuki, the Juniorweight Champion, earlier. We all remember you targeting him last time around. Why do you hate Shoji so much?" BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Hate Shoji? You don't understand. I don't hate Shoji at all. No. I love Shoji. For years I watched that guy in all of his classic matches. He was my hero. Hell, when I was younger I used to imagine that one day Shoji would pass that Jet Jaguar mask down to me."
Reporter #3: "Why do you target him?" BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Simple. To beat Shoji Suzuki means you are the absolute best. During TOP's first run Shoji never noticed me. He proclaimed Hayato Ikeda the 'future'. He brought in his prize-student Jet Jaguar II. He constantly talked about how great Eiji Yatsu was. He spent all his time thinking about hacks like Demon Boy Ishrinku and Kyojin Yakuma. But when I called him out, what happened? Nothing. He ignored me. He didn't even feel I merited a reponse.
Towards the end I proved that I was not to be ignored. I pinned Yatsu and JJ and I would've taken that JR title as well. It was, and is, only a matter of time. Next question..."
[An oddly thin man stands up. BRAT points to him.] BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Yes, Diseased-Looking-Fellow." Reporter #4: "Keiji Kobe, The Iron Chef, had some strong words for you recently. Any comment?"
BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "I will say this about Kobe, at least he has an interesting persona. His gimmick has drawn money just about everywhere he goes. Kind of like me, Kobe is a bit of a Media Darling. However, unlike me Kobe can't back that up in the ring. He is a mask and a gimmick and that is all."
Reporter #4: "What about the nickname he gave you? Gaijin Gard---" BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "DO NOT SAY IT!!!" [Mitsu's attitude has entirely changed. His eyes narrow on the offending reporter and he pounds the podium for good measure.]
BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Say that nickname and I promise you I will wade through the sea of useless journalists and strangle you where you stand. Kobe wants to call me...that name? First of all, I'm no gaijin! I was born in Tokyo, raised in
America. I am the best of both worlds. Second of all, Kobe you dared to dishonor me by calling me that name? Well you fired the first shot. When we do get into the ring together, and I promise we will soon, I'm taking your mask off of you. Without your gimmick you are nothing. Let's see you support your fat wife and two bastard children after I take away your one selling point.
Then you'll find out what is to be low!" [Mitsu points out towards the crowd of reporters.] BRAT Dragon Mitsu: "Now because one of you felt the need to ask such a disrespectful question, I'm leaving. This conference is over."
[Without a second thought Mitsu marches off stage.] =============== TAKAYUKI MORITA =============== [Open to Hadi Moditian, wearing a grey suit and with microphone in hand, standing in front of a white wall.]

[Morita steps onto camera, wearing a white TOP t-shirt and black ADIDAS athletic pants. He smiles and bows slightly towards the camera.]
TM: Konnichi wa! HM: Morita-san, thanks for being here. TM: (laughs) My pleasure. HM: First off, let me congratulate you on a successful debut against Yone
Dajiro earlier this evening.
[Morita bows his head slightly.] TM: Thank you. It was a tough fight. We both gave it our all, and I'm proud to have won. HM: Next week promises to be even tougher for you, as you team up with Takeshi

HM: A lot of people are already drawing comparisons between you and Sakai. TM: Well, we're close to the same size, but to be honest, I don't really see much similarity in our styles. We're both smaller young guys that are looking for our chance to move up the ladder. A win here for either of us would be a very big step up.
HM: Undoubtedly. Any chance that after this match we could see Koyama and yourself continue teaming?
TM: Hmm... it's hard to say. That's not my decision alone. It's certainly not my place to speak for him. He's the champion after all! We'll have to see what happens, I guess. Koyama's... pretty volatile. (laughs)

[At this, Morita shrugs and laughs good-naturedly.] TM: I intended no slight against Kobe-san, and I'm sorry he perceived it that way. However, I'd be interested in taking him up on his offer of a match. I expect I could learn a lot from wrestling him. But that, of course, would be up to Mr. Fujiwara.
HM: Of course. Well, Morita-san, that about wraps things up. Once again, thanks for your time and good luck.
