A RP FROM CRAZY CRITIQUE PLEASE...

6 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am Crazy from the CCW probably don't know me probably will forget my name by the time you finish reading this. But what the hell here is a very recent rp of mine.
(We open up in the conference room in the Tokyo Hilton. There are dozens of reporters and photographers. In the front of the room is a podium with a good 15-20 microphones. Behind the conference table on the wall is a banner for CCW's Life In Death's Hands. About a good 2-3 feet back from the main podium there is a smaller podium with just one microphone. From the back coming in quietly. Wearing a black pin strip suit, and wearing black gucci shoes is the President of the CCW Chris Macauley. He is clean shaven and his hair is combed back tightly. A couple cameras flash and flicker as they try to get a photo of the president. The President walks up to the podium and speaks with a deliberate tone.)
CM:Ladies and gentlemen tommorrow night we will be holding Life In Deaths
Hands just a few miles from here in the Tokyo Coliseum. And one of those matches scheduled is Crazy versus Sibon. We could'nt get a hold of Sibon so we only half one half of that match Crazy. He will come out here answer a few questions and take a few publicity photos. Ladies and gentlemen currently one half of the tag team champions, and one of the fore fathers of the Untouchables...Crazy!
(Crazy comes walking from the back with no music is playing. He is wearing a white undershirt with a black western shirt over top of that no buttoned, he is also wearing some black wranglers and black snake skin boots. He is clean shaven. His jet black hair is pulled back tightly. He has black sunglasses on but he takes them off and puts them on his undershirt collar when he gets near the podium. His tag belt is in his right hand. Both
Chris and Crazy shake hands and exchange a few words before Chris lets him pass and go to the podium. Once he gets up there he carefully folds the straps of the belt and places it on the table itself so people can get a good camera shot of it. Once the first foray of camera flashes has stopped
Crazy speaks as Chris sits to the left of Crazy.)
Crazy:Who's got a question? (The whole room explodes with uninteligble questions until Crazy picks one out of the crowd. It is a wiry Japanese man with a semi dorky plaid suit and polka dot bow tie.)
REP.:Crazy you and Sibon have fought in the past and on both occasions you lost. Now your going for your third time. Everyone knows about your slightly abnormal training methods. But in the past that seemed not to get you anywhere with Sibon. Have you done anything different with your training or studying this time around?
Crazy:Thats a very good question. I do have slightly abnormal training methods. This time around I had to question my training and look at any mistakes I am making. And maybe not everyone knows this. I am sure the
"sheets" on the net do but I video tape my training sessions and weightlifting sessions and so on and so forth. From what I saw in my tapes
I noticed certain things I would do when I was sparring. I ain't going to divulge that information but I have patched up any holes I did have in my training. So yeah I have trained differently for my match with Sibon. But to be specific I wont say what I have changed. Next question?
(A older, heavyset american man walks up to the podium. He is wearing khaki pants and shirt, with a brown pif hat and sandy blonde sandals.)
REP.:In recent weeks you and Jaguar have had some problems. Ultimately leading up to Jaguar retiring his post in the Untouchables. What effect, if any, do you think that will play a mental part in your match?
Crazy:Thats a good question and thank you for asking it. It has had an effect over me these last couple of days. I may not have shown it on national tv but believe me it hurt. I understand what I did to Jaguar a couple of weeks ago was in some peoples eyes not right. But as most people close to me can attest I don't do anything without a reason. I did'nt join the Untouchables without a reason. I did'nt decide to tag with Jaguar without a reason. So in my heart it was right. And as most of you in here you have seen me talk about Jaguar in high regard. I respect him as a pro and as a friend. My answer to this question is absolutely no. What I did I know in my heart was right. Wether anyone realizes it or not at this point in time. Next question.
(A buff, tall Chinese man walks up to the podium. He is wearing a black suit with a red undershirt, with black gucci shoes.)
REP.:What are your views on the Grinnin and Triple H match scheduled? Crazy:I think the way Triple H got that World belt and how he has portrayed it as earning it is sickening. The world title is supposed to be around the waist of the best. And Triple H is not the best, hasen't been in a long time. I have never fought the man, even though we tried to have a match a couple of weeks ago. But I know he is not what he used to be. A couple of months ago he was the type of wrestler I wanted to aspire to be. I wanted to have the shear strength and power he had when he walked into that ring.
Whenever someone would walk into the ring with him they would get beat down like they were nothing. But now that he has joined the LOX's army he is nothing but a whipping boy for the LOX who if it serves him right will kick
Triple H to the curb and not give a second thought. But right now triple h does not see that alls he sees is world domination. My opinion on this match is that Grinnin is going to give Triple H the beating of his life.
And I will be more than happy to watch as Grinnin is awarded the world title. Next. (A midget german man walks up the podium. He is wearing a pair of blue jean overalls and a white shirt underneath. Once he gets up to the podium he realizes he needs a boost so after a few moments of someone finding something to stand on, which happens to be a metal briefcase, he finally talks.)
REP.:Sorry for taking so long. Crazy:(amused) Not a problem. REP.:Every week in and out you come out to the crowds risking life and limb. I am wondering do you think you will be getting a world title shot soon or do you not want one right now?
Crazy:Right now, no. But let me give you my reasons. I still have so many things I need to do before I would allow myself to get a title shot. Things such as finishing my business with the LOX, taking care of things with
Iceberg and getting things straight with Jaguar. And leading the "Alliance"
of the CCW against the LOX. A title shot is not far fetched for this year but in my opinion it is going to take a miracle for it to happen. I just dont want to get a title shot, win it, then people say to me or on tv that I never earned it. I want all doubt removed from everyones mind that I have earned it. Ok last question.
(A skinny nerd type american walks up to the mic. He is wearing blue jeans and a blue shirt with air jordans on.)
REP.:What is your objective for tommorrow night? What do you want to prove to the world? Crazy:That I am a tought sob. That I can trade blows with anyone and still walk away. I want the respect from people who just think that I am a gimmick stealer. Or someone who does'nt belong in a wrestling ring. I want to let everyone know when they see me in that ring that I play for keeps.
And most importantly I want the LOX and his cronies to realize that those two men in that ring are the ones that they have to deal with including
Solid Snake and the rest. Thank you for your time and hope to see you tommorrow night at the show. Peace!!!
(Crazy walks away from the podium and he and Chris go over to the left side of the room to do some publicity shots. We fade out on Chris and Crazy standing side by side shaking hands. We go to a commercial for a new video in the popular superstars series, How To Beat Sibon as shown by the masked wrestler.)
