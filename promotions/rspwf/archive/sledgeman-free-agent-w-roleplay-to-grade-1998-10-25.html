[Sledgeman] Free Agent w/Roleplay to Grade

25 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm not posting this flash so I can get my wrestler on the RSPWF 200 (You can still vote for him if you want ;-), but because I want it graded and to announce him being a free agent.
[The camera fades into Sledgeman and his 7-year-old son, Nick, sitting in their living room watching TV. Sledge is a large, heavily built man, who is well over 6 feet tall. He's wearing a '33' Michigan State football jersey and jeans with a bright green stripe down each side. There's a look of pure horror on his face. Nick is totally dwarfed by his father, and is wearing the same thing he is. El Hijo del Sledgeman (The son of the Sledgeman for you none Spanish speakers:-) isn't as interested in what he's watching as his father is, and is just looking around the room. As a shot from the television is heard, Sledge drops to his knees, covers his face, and let's out a loud wale.]
Sledge: This can't be happening! Minnesota just beat the Spartans, 19-18. [He turns to his son.] Nick, this has to be the sign of the end of the universe.
Go pack, because we're going to be leaving his world real soon. [Nick what his father said way to seriously and leaves the room to go pack to
'leave the world.' Sledge in the meanwhile, sits back in the couch and turns off the TV. He turns towards the camera, acts startled, and then gives a big grin.]
Sledge: I guess you must be here for my interview. Cameraman: Yeah. Sledge: This isn't the best time, but I should get this over with. [Sledge puts a look of semi-seriousness on his face.] You must be wondering who this guy is. My name is Sledgeman. Why do people call my that? Probably because of this. [Sledge picks up the 20-pound sledgehammer that was laying the coffee table in front of him.] Makes sense, right? This hammer is often used to describe me. Strength, pure power, destructive, dumb as a rock. I'm not sure about the latter two, but strength and power is what I'm all about. I've worked hard since I was in junior high to get to this size. You probably wouldn't believe it, but at one time I was a weak 150 pounds, now I'm over 250. What does that say about me? I say it means I'm determined, and most people I've met will agree with that.
Right now I'm under contract with a small company called the Elite Shoot
Wrestling Promotion. We've gone through some hard times, losing our funding and all, but the little company in Chicago still lives. The ESWP may not be the best known, our wrestlers may not be the best known, but I can tell you one thing, we're no slackers when it comes to the skill department. Don Hall,
Chris Michaels, Firestarter, myself. We're probably the best Chicago's got, but after October 31 my contract will be out. Yes, I could stay, and challenge again for the World title, but it's my choice to move on. On
November 1, I will occasionally become a free agent. So, corporate executives, why would you hire me? What have I done? I may not be the most successful wrestler on the planet, but I shown I that I can handle myself in the ring. 17-8-1, career record. I held the IEWA TV title longer than anyone else there, and that was when the TV title was the only men's single title. In the ESWP I've held the Television title twice, tying the record set by ESWP Hall of Famer 'the Spartan' Troy Walcott, and the current holder of this title.
[Sledge points to the title belt above his head. The camera focuses in on the belt. It reads 'ESWP Brass Knuckles Champion.' The camera focuses back on
Sledge.]
What does that tell you? I'm tough? That's for sure. I can take it to any man, no matter the size. At ESWP Halloween House Party '98, I'll be defending this title for my last time in a Pentagon of Pain match. If I lose, I be disappointed, but I'll give the new champion my blessing. If I win, I'll hold onto the BK-strap forever and the division will end. Nice send off, don't you say?
In closing, I'm a guy people want to see. I'm a guy who will give anyone, no matter who, a run for their money. I'm a guy that respects anyone who's willing the step in the square circle. I'm a guy that'll make you feel the power of the sledgehammer.
[The camera fades out.] So tell me what you think, and let me know if I'm someone you'd like in your fed.
