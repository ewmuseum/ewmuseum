[KC Slick] "A Christmas to Remember"

4 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
KC Slick is my guestbook fed wrestler. I'm not looking for a fed, I just want you to read this, and give it your thoughts. I realize Christmas has passed, so please don't point that out. Now, on with the rp:
-=A Christmas to Remember=- ["Deck the Halls" plays over the scene, which opens up outside a quaint brick 2 story house with smoke coming out of the chimney, on Christmas eve.
Its lightly snowing and Christmas is in the air. The camera gets a shot of some carolers on the side walks.]
Carolers: #SILENT NIGHT# #HOLY NIGHT# [the camera switches to some kids throwing snow at each other. We get a feeling of peace as they laugh. Finally, it switches back to that quaint little house, and the music fades out. The camera does one of those nifty little fade in things, and we find ourself inside the house. There is a toasty fire in the fire place, and a wonderful Christmas tree. Two kids, a boy and a girl, are laying on the floor gawking at the few presents under the tree. A man, obviously the father, is seated in a Lay-Z-Boy Recliner, smoking his pipe. Finally, the mother, she is seated on the couch sewing and occasionally petting the fluffy grey cat laying next to her. It's a beautiful scene that illustrates the meaning of Christmas, togetherness.]
Dad- "Well Kids, its time for bed.....its about time for you know who to show up." [the kids eyes grow wide and the turn and look at each other, then run up stairs.] Mom- "What great kids we have" Dad- "Yeah....I love you" Mom- "I love you too dear" [Peace. Love. Thats what Christmas is about. Suddenly, the front door of the house burst open and "Hot Shot" KC Slick emerges dressed in a Santa suit, and carrying a bag.]
Dad- "Who the hell are you?!" KC- "I'm Santa Clause, who else?" Mom- "Get out of my house" KC- "How can you kick Santa Clause out on Christmas?!" [Dad grabs one of his golf clubs and begins wacking KC with it] KC- "Ouch! Alright, I'm not Santa Clause!" Dad(relaxing)- "Well then who are you?" KC- "I'm not anyone really, this scene was just too happy for me. I couldn't take it, someone had to do something, so who better to ruin your Christmas than the man everyone envies most.....KC Slick! Besides, why shouldn't I?
Every year I've written Santa, and EVERY year I've asked for an attractive woman, but EVERY year he gives me a tie! I DON'T WANT A TIE!"
Dad- "Ruin Christmas, how dare you!" [Dad begins wacking KC with the club again, until finally he takes it away bends it in half]
Dad- "Golly, that was my nine iron" KC- "I'll show you nine iron" [KC wraps the club around Dad's neck. Then whips him into the Christmas tree, which falls over on top of him. Mother then charges KC and slaps him a few times]
KC- "Hey WOMAN! Get your hands off me! Go wash some clothes or something!" Mom- "How dare you!" [KC picks her up and dumps her in his bag, tieing her inside] Mom- "mmmm....mmm.mmmm..mmm" KC- "Shaddup, ya crazy broad!" [From down stairs come the two little kids hoping to find Santa Clause. The get a look at KC and a smile crosses both there faces]
Kids- "SANTA!" [KC notices the two kids and pulls his fake beard up back over his chin] KC- "Uh....HO HO HO...Merry Christmas kiddies!" Kids- "WHAT DID YOU GET US!!" [KC gets a grinch-like grin across his face, you know, on of those grins that would make you not wanna touch him with a 33 and a half foot poll.]
KC- "Well....." [he reaches inside his coat and pulls out the two most crappy gifts kids could recieve.....]
KC- "For you little girl.....a pink little dolly with its head ripped off and no clothes, and for you little boy...the Hanson CD"
[the kids look sadly at their gifts then begin to bawl] Kids- "WAAAAAAAAHHHH!!" KC- "Aww kids...no need to thank me!" [KC begins to walk out the door when he hears. Its the sound of sleigh bells, they're ringling and jing-jing-jingling. He looks at the chimney and see's some ashes fall, following by a hugh ass covered by red pants. The owner of the ass, you guessed it, Old Saint Nick, falls out of the chimney and is shocked to find the state in which the place is in]
Santa- "KC.....how could you? Why on Earth would you want to ruin these nice people's Christmas?"
KC- "Well....I....I..." Santa- "I know what you need....a gift, that'll get you in the spirit!" KC- "OH WOW! A gift....for me?!" Santa- "Yes sir" [Santa reaches in his back and pulls out.....] Santa- "Here you go KC!" KC- "A TIE!!!" [In his hand, KC holds a red tie. That's it, a red tie.] KC- "A DAMN TIE!" Santa- "Merry Christmas KC Slick!" [KC's eyes turn a shade of red, then suddenly, he does something every person who has recieved a bad gift on Christmas has wanted to do. He grabs
Santa around the waist]
Santa- "MERRY CHRISTM...." (SPLAT) [Santa is out cold due to a devistating belly to belly suplex. KC then climbs up the chimney, hops on the roof, and STEALS Santa's steigh. As the sleigh fly's off, "Deck the Halls" begins to play again, and KC chants]
KC- "MERRY CHRISTMAS TOO ALL, AND TOO ALL A GOOD NIGHT!" [camera fades out]
