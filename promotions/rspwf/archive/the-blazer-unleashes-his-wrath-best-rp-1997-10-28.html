"The Blazer" Unleashes his Wrath...<-BEST RP!

28 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Blazer Barry Blades (First off, the Cain in this RP Is not that one in the WWF But I wrestler that I`ve known along time.) (Before any sort of scene opens "Rock and Roll Part 2" By Gary Glitter begins playing. ((The song they play at basketball games with the "HEEEY" Stuff.)) The Scene Opens up to a man laying on a table with blood all over his face and chest. He looks very much like Billy Kidman before he turned to Raven`s group. The Table is outside of the ring and there`s thousands of screaming fans sorrounding the ring in a Very Big Arena. The ring has a blue apron and a light blue canvas that has NJPW Written in gold in the center of the ring and on all four aprons. You see two men inside of the ring. One is the one and only Barry Blades and the other looks to be Mr. Ganosuke, a NJPW Star. Barry Blades has a a Black leather vest and a pair of black baggy jeans on. Mr. Ganosuke has a pair of short red tights with white stripes down the side. Mr. Ganosuke has a blue metal chair in his hands and is holding it up like he`s about to hit him. He swings but Blazer catches it and then kicks Ganosuke in the gut and swipes him in the head with the chair. Ganosuke flies to the ground face first, knocked out, and "The Blazer" Looks around holding the chair above his head while all the fans are screaming very loudly. He then points to the Billy Kidman look-a-like on the table and shakes his head. He the runs to the top rope, leaps off and in slow-motion dives onto the man crashing through the table, sending splinters and peices of wood everywhere. Then The Words "BARRY BLADES" Fades onto the screen. You hear a man scream in pain and the camera fades and quickly reappears. You now see Barry Blades standing in the center of The "BLaZeR aReNa" . For those of you who don`t know, the Blazer arena is an old warehouse in the suburbs of Seattle. Barry Blades has started to fix it up in hopes that one day he`ll be able to get his own thing going on. Barry Blades is wearing a black leather vest that shows his muscluar chest and abdomen. And he also has a pair of baggy jeans and blue and black Nikes. The Leather vest says "THe BLaZeR" In Green letters. "The Blazer" is looking up at one of the huge Graffitti panitings on the wall infront of him. It says "THE BLAZER" In HUGE Red, Green, and Black letters. The Blazer stands there with his hands on his hips looking at the Humongus painting. Barry looks around at the once warehouse and picks up a rock off the ground. He starts throwing it up and down in short little throws in his right hand. He starts walking toward the camera and now passes by it. "The Blazer" Barry Blades heads into a hallway. The camera man follows and you know see that on the Walls of this hallway there are very many more different paintings. There`s only one that Blazer is looking at and it`s the first one on the left side. The Camera moves in and you see that it`s Ben Salas, Barry Blades first and only father figure and mentor, standing there holding Blades hand in victory. Barry Walks on to the next picture. It shows Cactus Jack and Barry Blades laying in a pit of wood and splinters. Barry Looks at it and smiles. "The Blazer" walks on, looking at the next picture which is of A Pancrase Association ring. He kind of Looks at it with a regular look. Nothing too special but then he stops and looks at it but once again, moves on. The next picture is of An IEUW Logo. Barry Looks at and has a big smile. He once again moves to the next picture. It has Legal Eagle, The Stomper and Mr. Submission standing behind The IEUW TELEVISION Title. Barry Looks at it with a big look of rememberence. He reaches out and touches the title, stays there for a sec and moves on to the next. It shows CAIN. The Blazers face slowly turn to a straight face. All of the sudden he Punches the concrete wall with A Lighting quick Punch. He then just turns around and walks off. You can see a trickle of blood running Down his knuckles as it slowly drops to the ground. The camera zooms tot he right and the picture of CAIN now has a small crack in the forehead of the picture. The camera man runs out of the hallway to catch up with Barry Blades as they head back out to the center of the warehouse once again. Now Blazer throws the rock out in to the blackness of the shadows. "The Blazer`s" look is furious as he paces a few times and then looks at the camera. He walks to the left and picks up a base ball bat. He looks at the camera one more time and begins to speak.....) "The Blazer" Barry Blades: You know somethin, I remember times when wrestlers got what they deserved. Wether it be matches, belts or a good ole ass whoopin`. But Pro Wrestling as of late has turned into nothing but Butt kissing and brown nosers. You Know something else? I remember back in the glory days of The IEUW. I Remember when All the litte superstars, TTP, The Ultimate Ass Kisser, and Damian, A Man I have Respect for but Also a man I think could`ve worked a little harder for those titles... But then there`s CAIN. A Man that some say deserved his titles, that some say deserved his matches but I say NO. I say CAIN, You didn`t deserve a damn. If Anything I deserved a title shot. You see, While all was fine and dandy on Mount Olyimpus, The true workers and true fighters were down in the valley working theri asses off and giving it all they got... And I was one of those men, Cain. None of you people, Including Cain, Understand what I went through. And you won`t ever will. I had to change my gimmick three times before Anthony Feeds though I was "CHAMPIONSHIP MATERIAL". And even after I changed it, each time I came back and worked my ass off... Did you? NO. CAIN, Look at all of your accomplishments. Do you really deserve all of them? Just think long and hard. Yeah, sure, you deserved a few. But do you think you worked as hard as other people tried to? NO. Cain, you go to the presidents of the Federations, tell em to bend over and you kiss their asses. AND YOU KNOW IT. You`re just like the rest... Tsk Tsk Tsk As many other people think of when they hear the word IEUW, They think of TTP, CAIN, Altimus, and others. The so-called legends of the sport. The only legend in those three is Altimus. The other two are fakes, losers, and wanna bees.... So CAIN, When this Thursday rolls around, While you`re in your dressing room, five minutes before the match, when you got butterflies rolling around in your stomach, Just think to yourself, "Do I deserve this?" And just be prepared to get somethin you truly deserve. Get ready for Your first clean loss, because it`s going to happen sometime and sometime is this THURSDAY. Just four days away. Heh Heh Heh Cain, The end of a legacy has now begun... Even though it`s only one match, thats all I need.... (The Camera Slowly fades as Barry Blades chucks the bat far off into the distance. He slowly walks off . The Camera focuses onto the biggest of "THE BLAZER" Paintings.Scene Fades as you see the Large Blood Stained Canvas again. In slow motion the camera zooms out and you see a white man fall flat on his back. His face covered in blood and his black stringy hair bobbling everywhere. Then The Words " THE BLAZER BARRY BLADES" Fades onto the screen. You hear a man scream in pain and the camera fades... Once again "Rock And Roll Part 2" By Gary Glitter begins playing and A Low Deep Voice begins talking. As you hear the man talk scenes of Barry Blades fighting different people begin showing up.) Deep Voice: As a kid his mother was the only one whoever paid any attention to him, he had six brothers and two sisters and his dad was never around. A Neglected child, he decided to run away and join some gangs. One day when he was about 17 he was in one of his many gangfights that he was invloved in and luckily, wrestler Ben Salas saw Blades beating on some other kid. After the fight Ben built up enough courage to go ask Blades to sit down and talk with him. Ben Salas trained Barry for about a year and then moved him to Japan when Blades was 18. Blades fought many rough matches especially his most unforgettable match when he faced Cactus Jack in a Double cage bomb match. Even though Blades lost the match he showed alot of promise. As Cactus and Blades fought On the top of the 28 feet high cage Blades Gave Cactus a Frankensteiner but Cactus Jack held onto Barrys legs and both men went flying through a giant stack of tables but even worse, Blade`s Elbow and right shoulder was cut severely.... After gaining some recognition he was hired by the Pancrase Association and was on his way to some big titles there. In The Pancrase he greatly built up his Submission and Karate Skills. Ufortunatly only two weeks away from his title match he and Salas were caught in a car accident. Blades, once again injured his right arm and Salas knees were crushed. Blades fought on and despite the pain he went into the match. His opponent seriously damaged Blades left knee with a devasting drop kick. Salas had to throw in the towel.... Blades, Now 22 went back to the states and went into rehab. While he was in rehab he got back into the gangs. One day when he got back into his apartment he found Salas dead from a gang shooting..... Blades decided to go to A Federation called IEUW the biggest and best federation on AOL. Blades gained a rep for being the best all-round wrestler. He gained a nick name as "The Blazer" as he Blazed through the competition. But Blades was getting held back, when ever he was about to get a title shot something "happened" and the officials didn`t want him to do so. They said he wasn`t "fan material" but Blades just couldn`t leave. He asked them if he could come up with an all new gimmick. They said yes and Jex was born. Jex became the best high-flyer of the federation and was considered the most Extreme man on earth, in each of his matches he promised a table would be invloved and thats exactly what happened. In one match vs The Stomper Blades Piledrived his opponent through five table but Jex (Barry Blades) sprained his left foot when he accidently got it caught in the fall. Once again, he fought through the pain and was awarded a TV Title shot against Mr. Submission. Mr. Submission was known as ,well, the Master Of Submission and Barry knowing his New High-flying gimmick would not work so he came out one night and told the entire world his story. He then went into the match with more fan support then anyone could ever imagine. He beat Submission in a very close match and he brought new found popularity to the IEUW TV Title.... "The Blazer" had made a valiant return after his first three title defenses he forfeited his title saying that there was too much to do and the belt was only holding him down... He Left IEUW after these comments and left for Japan. Since then he has seriously increased his Submission and Pitfighting skills and is considered one of the most dangerous men in the world because of his AllRound skill. He knows every style of fighting and has mastered each one. He bases his fighting technique on whatever his opponents is. Wether it be Submission, brawler, high-flyer, anything, he`ll match ya. (The Scene Fades One more time...) "You don`t stand a chance when you decide to dance" "I AM THE BLAZER, CAIN IS SHIT" You copy andy of this stuff, I kick yer ass.
