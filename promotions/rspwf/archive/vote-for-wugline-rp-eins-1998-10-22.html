[Vote for Wugline!] RP eins

22 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Heya.. Blackie and I decided that we may as well do some plugging for votes in the
Awards thingamies and top 200 tags, even though we might've left it late to start being lamers. Anyway, here are two RP's from the team of "Bullywug"
Glenn Hudson and Rob "Blackline" James, from their earlyish days in the
Longhorn Wrestling Council. The first begins after winning the Twin Pistols championship after a pretty nasty Bunkhouse match thing, putting Wugline in the hospital,.
Anyway, enjoy Gareth PS- they're long.. sorry about the spam (-; -- [We open to a trolley filled with crumpled-up old sheets, pillow-cases, and various other stuff. We hear a slight metallic rumbling sound as the camera rises to reveal a chute of some kind, mounted into a wall. Suddenly, a
Championship belt drops out of the chute and lands safely into the laundry, followed by another identical belt. We barely have a moment to recognise them as the LWC Twin Pistol belts before a louder rumbling sound can be heard.
"Bullywug" Glenn Hudson then drops through the chute, head-first and onto the soft landing. He is wearing a hospital gown, and a cut about his right eye is stitched-up. Scant seconds later, the blurred form of "Blackline" Rob James comes shooting out as well, landing in a heap on top of Wug, an overstuffed duffel bag landing on top of Blackie, further squashing both of them...]
Wug: Arrgh! Watch it! Blackie: Hey, sorry... but this WAS your plan... [Blackie pushes the duffel bag off of him and stands up. He's wearing a neckbrace, has a bandage on his forehead up near the hairline, and a hospital gown. He pulls a pair of tattered-looking jeans out of the bag and slips them on under the gown while Wug gets up, eyeing him strangely.]
Wug: Do we really have time for this? What do you think you're doing? Blackie: Getting dressed. Hey, don't look at me like that, it's not MY fault you couldn't find your clothes...
[Blackline fastens the pants, and then picks up one of the Twin Pistol belts and fastens it around his waist, over the jeans and the gown. One could almost say that the Bunkhouse Battle Royale for the titles, or more accurately, the assorted head traumas therein, may have scrambled Blackie's brain just a bit... or maybe QUITE a bit.]
Wug: C'mon, we're not out of the woods yet, Champ. [We cut to a different scene - an alleyway running alongside a building, a fire-escape and door in front of us. The door quickly opens and Hudson's head pokes around the corner. He looks one way... then the other...]
Wug: We're clear. Blackie: The car should be over that way... I think... unless it's the other way. Wug: Feel free to make up your mind whenever it's convenient. Blackie: [looking completely unsure] Um... ok, it's that way. [points] Yup, definitely that way...
Wug: Now.. are you sure? Blackie: Yeah... uh... sorta. Well, kinda, but not... no, I'm not sure. But it seems like a good guess...
[The duo bolt out of the doorway and begin to sprint down the alleyway, away from us. We only get a small dose of gratuitous nudity today, as the camera changes scenes within a second.]
Wug: [off-camera] C'mon! Go! Go! Go! [We are now looking at a carpark, mostly full, but with a few people driving around, walking between their cars and what we now can see is a hospital. In the distance, two men wearing white hospital gowns suddenly appear from around a corner of the hospital. Sure enough, it is Blackie and Wug. They start to hurriedly make their way towards us, heads bowed-down as they scurry towards us, as if they are trying to avoid gunfire and explosions in some kind of a warzone. Unfortunately, they're in broad-daylight and everyone stops and stares. They dash across a road, Hudson almost getting hit by a car that only just manages to brake in time. The young Australian quickly apologises, but doesn't stop. Blackie, apparently oblivious to Hudson's near death experience, flashes a smile at the driver, and actually offers to sign an autograph, while Wug simply stares in astonishment for a moment, until...]
Wug: Go! Go! Go! Go! Go! G- ow! Ow! Ow! [A look of confusion appears on James' face, changing to pain as the signals slowly reach his currently mis-wired brain.]
Blackie: Owowowow... hot, hot.... [We now notice that it is a very warm day, and that the concrete is most like to be very hot. From the way they are both hopping along on barefoot, it is almost a certainty.]
Wug: Shi.. Gawd, geez! Aaah.. Blackie: This isn't in m- ow, ow, my contract! [They hop and run a short distance, past a stunned elderly lady. Blackline stops and starts to offer to sign an autograph, pointing at his title belt, but Wug grabs him and gets him running again. They continue bolting across the parking lot, Blackline staring around at the sky the entire time, and then they stop at a very distinct-looking car. It's a black 1969 Mustang, tubbed in the back, blown in the front, and looking altogether like a very fast hot-rod. The so-called "vanity" plate says "Blkline" - it's kind of obvious who's car this is... Wug dives in through a window, Blackie opening the driver's side door and climbing in. He fishes around in his duffel bag, which he's thrown into the passenger seat, for the keys. He finds them just as Hudson starts talking.]
Wug: I know it was drastic.. [Blackie turns the key, and the rest of Hudson's sentence is drowned out by the Mustang's big block V8 as it turns over and roars to life- loud, loud life. Real loud... After a few seconds, it quiets down to a noisy rumble as it's idling, and Blackie slams it in reverse, adds a millimeter or so of protective rubber coating to two narrow strips of asphalt in the park lot, slams it into first, and boom, they're moving... boy, are they moving. The engine's still loud, but... tolerable. Well, almost tolerable, anyways. When they continue speaking, it's in raised voices, necessary to be heard over the motor- and Blackie hasn't even touched the stereo yet...]
Blackie: What'd you say back there, Champ? Something after "drastic?" Wug: .. but we have a title defence to prepare for.. Blackie: Oh, ok, that answers that, then- no, wait, no, it doesn't answer that. Where are we supposed to be going?
Wug: The Rodeogrounds... But first, I need to find some clothes. Any second-hand shops around here? Maybe we can pick up a bargain.
[The realisation of something unpleasant hits him.] Wug: Damnit! My wallet is back in there! [Hudson points vaguely over his shoulder.] Wug: You have any cash or tradable goods in this car? I'll pay you back - honest! Blackie: I've always got money... um... somewhere. We might have to stop at an ATM or... or... an ATM or... [James shakes his head a little and blinks]
or a bank. But yeah, I've got cash. Wug: Anyway... well, we actually have two defences... Didn't anyone tell you? Blackie: I... I dunno. All I remember is somebody telling saying about breakfast... but I don't even remember eating any bacon, and even if I had, how could they smell it over the phone?
[Wug looks at Blackie like he's lost it for a moment, then shrugs and carries on.] Wug: First we have to get our good selves to Legacy, which is basically where we need to go in the very near future.
Blackie: Who's at Legacy? Wug: Ambitious... and Nutricious... [Wug grins.] Wug: In case you can't remember, we kinda "got" them en-route to winning these.. [He taps the Championship belt, lying in his lap.] Blackie: Hey, that's the guy with the... um... that thing... he hit me with it. I had to wash grease out of my hair!
[James looks annoyed, in a confused sort of way, for a second- but it passes, prolly a little too quickly.]
Blackie: So... um... you said two, right? [Wug nods.] Who's the second team? Wug: Pleasure... and Pain... [Wug grin.] Wug: In case you can't remember, we kinda "got" them en-route to winning these.. [He taps the Championship belt, lying in his lap. Deja vu is cool.] Blackie: Whoa... I have the strangest feeling I've done this before.... [James shakes his head again, blinking quickly a couple of times, and re-focusing.] Blackie: Ambitious and Nutritious and Pleasure and Pain and... um... I feel dizzy.... Wug: I don't really have much to say, to be honest. Except that Osakgu bashed me with a plank, and that this gown is kinda comfortable. Ah feel-ah free ahgain!
Blackie: Planked you? He PLANKED you?!? That basta-hey, a Long John Silver's... plank... chicken planks... you hungry, Wug? Wug: Hell, yeah, I'm hungry. I'm sure we can spare a few minutes after all.. Blackie: Cool... besides... we've already got belts, but nutrition is always important... it'll be my pleasure... um... I don't feel so well...
[Wug takes another tentative peek at James.] Wug: Hey... stay with me, pard'... I know you might be in a little pain right now, but we're Champs... and as Champs we have to have a good time at the expense of everyone else. Ambition is very important too.
[The Mustang whips HARD into the parking lot, narrowly avoids the very "zen" experience of becoming one with the drive-thru window, and slides to a stop, parking in approximately 2 3/4 spaces. The door opens, and Blackie stumbles out, wobbling a little. He actually looks thoughtful for a moment, and...]
Blackie: Um, Wug? We didn't get you any clothes yet... Wug: Damn... oh well, we'll worry about it after food. Walk right behind me. [The camera goes slow motion as Hudson in his hospital gown and James in his gown, jeans, and title belt walk into the Long John Silver's, heads swaying from side to side, "Misirlou" playing... Watusi to black...]
