[Free Agent] Jeff "Madfox" Matthews addresses Simon Lebec

17 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera opens with Magnus Colby sitting at his computer reading the ramblings of "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec, in response to the blatherings of "Madfox"
Jeff Matthews. Colby is wearing a black UEW T-shirt. It has a 1000 dollar bill on the front that says... "Money isn't everything it is the _ONLY_ thing!")
Simon, Simon, Simon. I have heard so much about you. And they really do say you are one hell of a wrestler in the ring. But, worth listening to you are not. cuntrag? Good word. I believe the proper term is douche. Not that I see the signifigance of calling a man of Matthews' caliber a femine hygiene product. But, if that is the intention then please do use the correct word.
Oh and by the way... Mr Big Bad Lebec. Is this newsgroup big enough for both me and my ego? Or do I need to start packing my bags for fear of retribution?
Excuse me I have to go clean my underwear. I believe I have soiled them worrying about the immortal Simon Lebec.
(fade to black) [name withheld] handler of Magnus Colby Former UEW American champion Former IWC World champion Former IWF/WOW Tag champion Former IWC North Eastern champion Former? DWO Eastern champion "I never held a title I couldn't job"
