Handler Information ------------------- Name:Kenneth Email Address: [detail withheld] ICQ Number:[detail withheld] IRC Nickname: Character Information --------------------- Name:Xavior Packson Real Name(if different): Hometown: Seattle Washington Height:6'6 Weight:290 Character History and Biography ------------------------------- he was born and raised in a small town in Iowa. He lived on his families corn and wheat farm till he was 17 years old. All his life on the farm he had to do nothing but manual labor to help his family make ends meet. When he was 16 years old his whole family exluding his father all died in a car accident. The accident tramitized him and his father very severly because they both witnessed the accident right in front of there home. Xavior continued to work on the farm because it was in his blood. When he turned 17 he decided that it was time for him to move out and try his hand at something else. He moved to Seattle to try and make a better living but just didnt seem to fit in with what was going on in the new fast high tech changing world. He decided that with his strength and speed he could try out becoming a wrestler aft5er seeing advertisements for a local indy fed. He was a success right from the start. He worked and worked in the fed until he was finally scouted by a big time organization in Canada. He worked the Canadian market wrestling for a few years and then moved back to the states in Washington where he is now continually wrestling.
Your Character's Appearance --------------------------- He is a rather tall white man that has a great upper body and great muscled up arms he also has a great six pack as well. He has a huge burn mark scar across his left arm by his shoulder. The burn mark is covered up by a huge tattoo of a barb-wires X in red and black. He has short brown hair and baby blue eyes.
Out of the ring he is usually wearing plain old jeans and t-shirts. From his early years in life he has always gotten anything he has ever wanted. His father was an investment banker and his mother was a Vice President of
Operations of a major TV station in Seattle. When he was about 13 years old in an all white school, he got into many fights being the only black child. He began to carry a switchblade at the time. When he was 15 years old he once again got into a fight, but this time he finally used the switchblade. He was arrested after cutting 3 of the boys across the face and he was tossed in juvenile hall until he was 18. When this happened his father decided that he didnt want that kind of thing happening within his family and totally disowned his son, Xavior. Xavior did not know about this until his 18th birthday when he was released from the Juvenille hall. Xavior totally went balistic and ever since has had a vendetta against anyone and everyone.
Your Character's Appearance --------------------------- He is a very tall man. He also has a great upper body with great arms. He also has a great six pack as well. He has a red and black barb-wired X on his left shoulder as well. And across his back from shoulder blade to should blade the word "Factor" is written in barb-wire letters. He has a bald head and dark brown eyes. He usually wears white out contacts over his eyes.
