The massive beast creeps out from the darkness and his pressence literally shakes the camera. The man stands around 6'10" and just knowing he is there the camera man is nervous. Aries: I try to live my life as it but the peasants of the world insist on egging me on. I hold my fist back but the ropes attached are only so strong.
Your kind, there ego's are so large and their souls are not peaceful yet one man insists that he is me, this man goes by the name of Scorpio.
(Aries pulls out a sheet of paper reading) > Scorpio is the god of war not Aries Aries: That's funny, the last time I read a book I was called that. Yeap, I'm pretty sure of it. But, if it makes you feel better I'll check again.
(Aries grabs a book from behind which reads Greek and Roman Gods. Aries flips the page to pg. 024.)
Aries: Hold on a sec, he's right! I'm not the God of War, I'M THE GOD OF
WAR!!! You see, it's a well known fact that I am a walking piece of our lives. I come to you in peace, all of you, but you've all brougth this upon yourselves. Years ago I was a simple man living a life but now I have vengeance in my blood and terror in my eyes. So to Scorpio, and whoever else wants to see what I'm made off, why don't you just come and find out.
