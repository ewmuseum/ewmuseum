จจจจจจจจจ`'''ฐบฒณฒคv^v^v^Scene^v^v^vคณฒบฐ'''ดจจจจจจจจ It is dark on the quiet dirt roads of Kansas. It is still in the wee hours of the mourning, and light has not yet dawned on the small little country town.
The cock will soon be crowing, and everyone is still resting in the comfort of their beds. Tucked in with the covers to their chin, lie the small children of this small town. A cold breeze slowly flows over the streets, and into everyone's houses, through the cracks of space left open in the windows. A shives the sent through the heavy dreamers of the night, and with that one breeze of air, their happy dreams filter into dark nightmares. From a distance a dark figure is seen making his way up the dirt road. Behind him, dust particles arise and blow in circular motions. As he draws near the rustling of his boots on the ground can be heard. As he moves in, it is easy to see that the man is none other than "The Preacher of the Ring." He steadily keeps walking, until he approaches nice home. Outside the house there are chickens and a dog. The dog is asleep in his dog house. About half of the chickings are asleep and the other half seem to be awake. There is a large barn house, from which the mooing of cows and neighing of horses, can be heard. There is also a very long strip of crops. "The Preacher of the Ring" slowly makes his way over to the house. He initially walks to the door, but then turns back and heads around the back of the house. he approaches a window and stands next to it. He speaks....
จจจจจจจจจ`'''ฐบฒณฒคv^v^v^"The Preacher of the Ring" Jacknife^v^v^vคณฒบฐ'''ดจจจจจจจจ Behind this wall. Two children sleep. They dream of fairy dust and houses made of candy, but sometimes that is the opposite of what they dream. Sometimes they have, what people call, bad dreams. I call them reality. All dreams have symbolism. Most of the time a dream is made from recolections of what went on in the past day. If you you had read about a person who had fallen off a cliff and died in school, you might dream that you were flieng across the sky, and then suddenly fell and rolled down a cliff. Far- fetched? I think not. 99 percent of the time, you do not even know that you are dreaming. You wake up and have no recolection of your dream, at all. So, when you say that dreams arn't realy, think twice, because dreams come from what happened that day.

Quest for a Champion. Is this also the dream of my opponents? Most likely, but
I will turn their dreams of bieng a champion, into their worst nightmare. The only difference is that, in this nightmare, when you are about to die you won't wake up. Zane Christian, Shadow of Style, I don't know if you enjoy pain, but when it comes Thursday the 28th, you better get used to it, because you know how i feel about the Triple threat title, especially you Zane Christian. You know that for this belt I will do anything, even if it means death, and if it will help the outcome to go my way, you may as well say, death to us part, because death is not a major priority.
จจจจจจจจจ`'''ฐบฒณฒคv^v^v^Scene^v^v^vคณฒบฐ'''ดจจจจจจจจ "The Preacher of the Ring" then approaches the vast corn fields and walks up and down the strips of crops. As he walks by he picks at the leaves. He pulls one ear of corn off the stem, and starts to peal at this covering. he drops them to the floor. When he takes all of the covering off he drops the ear of corn to the ground. He just keeps doing this over and over as he speaks...
จจจจจจจจจ`'''ฐบฒณฒคv^v^v^"The Preacher of the Ring" Jacknife^v^v^vคณฒบฐ'''ดจจจจจจจจ Zane Christian, you know how i can be in Triple Threat matches. You got to experience it first hand, when we faced in that historic match. Do you remember how it wind up ending? It ended in a disqualification. "The CornerStone" Chris
Champion had to come out and do a little run in. He came out and knocked my ass with a Cannon Blast. The week later it was me, you, and Chris Champion. This was a match that would have delighted even Siscal and Ebert. Three of the best went into a battle for the gold. That night I remember the main event was something like Tommy Stylz versus BMF. I remember sitting back in my locker and thinking,"We could have been in the main event." Tommy Stylz is a Great, and
BMF... well he's BMF, but I remember thinking that our match had main event calibur. Anyways, that match ended with me losing my Triple Threat Title to
Chris Champion. In that match, Chris Champion and Zane Christian also decided to double team me. Now I feel that was the cowards way out, but that can no longer be changed. Ya know, Zane, when you came into UwF me and alot of other thought you were gonna be unstopable, but then something happened. I hit the low blow. I stopped you in your tracks. You know what i mean by that, so nuthing firther is needed to be spoken. After that match my UwF career went down the tubes. I teamed with The Pro, and well everyuthing from there is just disgusting. I didn't make a comeback until I left the Pro. I came back and was within days of winning the U.S. title, but then UwF closed down. Now it's back, and I am not going to make the mistake of any way teaming with Pro.

He walks over to the hay and picks some up. he then walks over to the cow and drops the hay in front of it. The cow then stops mooing and eats the hay.
