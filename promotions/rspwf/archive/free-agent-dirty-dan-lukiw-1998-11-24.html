[FREE AGENT] "Dirty" Dan Lukiw

24 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a roleplay for my new cruiserweight character. This is when I was president of the fed, so it's done a little differently, but the whole attitude of being a complete and total prick will still stick. Here you go.
------ [Scene opens up to a shot of the stage of a local college gymnasium. The camera then pans around to reveal a crowd of reporters who are all gathered around with their cameras, notepads & tape recorders to ask questions and get comments from the NIWF President himself, "Dirty" Dan
Lukiw. All of the sudden, we hear a voice over which says: "Ladies and gentlemen, the president of the NIWF, "Dirty" Dan Lukiw." Lukiw comes out and is dressed in a pair of red & white zubaz and has a t-shirt on that says "Runs with Simmons!" on the back and on the fron of the t-shirt is a picture of 'Asshole' Andy Simmons and the NIWF World Title underneath his picture. Lukiw also has his arm in a sling.]
"Dirty" Dan Lukiw: Ladies and gentlemen of the press. I thank you for taking the time to join me here today. Unfortunately, I'm running on a tight schedule so I will only be able to take a few questions of yours and
I must be on my way. So, let me start off with the ESPN reporter in the front over here. What is your question.
ESPN Reporter: Mr. Lukiw, as some of us may or may not of seen last week, Todd Lucchesi won the NIWF Purist's Dream Title after he defeated Arthur Essex. However, as I hear about the tradition the NIWF has, no wrestler can hold two titles at once unless stated otherwise. My question to you in this: Which title does Todd Lucchesi hold and which title is going to be given to someone else?
"Dirty" Dan Lukiw: I'm glad you brought this issue up. First and foremost, Todd Lucchesi is no longer the NIWF TV Champ. I really didn't feel it was fair in the first place the way this TV Title has been going around, so what I am going to do is give the title back to the original owner who never lost it in the first place and whom I stripped of the title after I fired him. I am talking about none other than Tony Bettis. Bettis has already made a positive impression with me here in the NIWF as he came back and basically saved my life in the ring. So, the TV Title is now officially in the hands of Tony Bettis, if he chooses to accept it. And I just want to say sorry to Amber for not letting her hold onto the TV Title but she is going to have her chance at the Women's Title which seems to have gotten many members as of late. As for Todd, the title I expect him to defend with honor is going to be that NIWF Purist's Dream Title.
However, it's going to be hard for Todd to do so considering all the people who will be after that title. I'm sure Arhur Essex would want that title back and I know Reggie Smythe would want that title as well. I can see Ian Lightfoot making some sort of run for that title and now recently,
I have signed two men to make their return to the NIWF ring. These two men are none other than Patty Babes Carter & Zack Sharp. With the addition of these two on the NIWF roster, I'm sure it only makes the competition stronger. Next question please.
E! Reporter: As you know Mr. Lukiw, we are always trying to get the low down on what is going on. Now, rumor has it that you made numerous offers around the Net to try and get various athletes to come into the NIWF.
Obviously, two have responded back to you, but can you give us any insight as to who you want to come into the NIWF?
"Dirty" Dan Lukiw: Well, let me tell you whom I made offers to so they can join the NIWF. There are plenty of guys out there that were really good and that I would of loved to see in here, but I took the cream of the crop and offered them all contracts. Now, I haven't gotten responses from each and every individual, but there are some who gave me their answers already. Let me run it down for you real quick and throws out some names.
Former NIWF star, "Real Deal" Shawn Harrison. Former NIWF star, Patty Babes Carter. Former NIWF star, Zack Sharp. We have "Iron Man" Mike Madland, whom has competed here before. I sent and offer sheet to one of the best out there in Tommy Rifles, in hopes that he would make it here. I noticed a man by the name of T.N.T who I sent an offer sheet to. Old NIWF superstars such as Raekwon and Mr. Excitement Dropkick were once again offered a contract here in the NIWF. I made an offer to a N.E.W star in hopes that he would come in, the man's name is Brass Knuckles. I also made an offer to INWI star, King Apples. I found former BWF star, Dennis
Windham and made him an offer. I made offers to both Dallas "Perfection" Reed and "Suicidal" Nate McManus. I found "Homeless" Joe on the stree and told him that if he wanted to make more money so he can support himself and look like a real classy bum, that he should come to me because I pay well. I tried to bring "The Extreme War Machine" Corey Irons back into the fed as well as I sent him another offer sheet. However, the one person I really want to see in this fed and has been the talk of all night long has been "Erotic" Erin McCoys. It's not her looks that I found appealing, it was her talent. I did receive one answer from her that said something to the extent of "I'm only 5'3" and 104lbs" but never received an all out yes or no. She has been one of the best I have seen and if you want her in here, give me a buzz and I'll tell you how you can get a hold of her. Next question please.
Playboy Reporter: Mr. Lukiw, I noticed that you have finally got some talent for the women's division. Could you please tell us whom has signed with the NIWF to compete? Also, any of the ladied interested in doing a
Playboy shoot? "Dirty" Dan Lukiw: Well, I'm not the one whom you should be asking about the women's division, but I'll give you the answers you are looking for.
As I said before, I have an offer to "Erotic" Erin McCoys out there and I think she could use a little "convincing" from some of the NIWF guys here.
As for who has signed, let me run down the list and tell you. First off, Amber is a definite shoe in for the women's division. There have been others and this division looks to be shaping up to be a classic. I have people such as Heather MacGregor in as well as Elyse Lightfoot and a newcomer by the name of Li'L Cheetah. 'Asshole' Andy Simmons also spoke to
Doug Hardy about getting in another newcomer to the Women's Division as well. I'm not sure who exactly it is, but with Andy, you never know what kind of stunt he will pull. Will he dress up as a woman or what? Oh yes, I also forgot to mention that Gwen has joined as well and is looking forward to competing. She is so excited, that she has even volunteered herself to be the first NIWF Woman to do some sort of photo shoot in Playboy magazine. So, if you are interested in obtaining her for a few days, I strongly reccomend that you get a hold of Doug Hardy and he will make the arrangements for you. Next question please.
Toronto Sun Reporter: Mr. Lukiw, did you not find it unfair that Axel Schmidt flat out had to, ahem, "do the job" to Poe when Axel was the clear winner in the match to begin with. Can you explain that one to us?
"Dirty" Dan Lukiw: I most certainly can. The reason why Axel Schmidt lost was because for one night, I gave the booking duties to my Vice President for half of the matches and that match just happened to be one of them.
Axel was none to happy when he found out he was going to lsoe the Diamond Title. However, as Richard Balsley is my Vice President, he is going to be taking the blame for a lot of things and I don't care if I piss him off or not because he's lucky to even have the job. Now then, I blame it on my
VP. As far as Axel is concerned, I apologize on behalf of my VP to him because he should of been the clear winner, but the fact is this, now Axel is not considered the Diamond Champion and Poe is techncally the champ.
Well, I'm going to do one of two things. I am giving Axel Schmidt the choice to make. Axel can either take the loss, and gracefully receive the title back as I strip Poe of the title which he does not deserver or he can..... get this. Or, he can let the title go and make it be declared vacant and enter himself in the 5 Man Lunatic Fringe Match which I am going to have for that title so I can finally declare a true winner. The one problem is this, I won't announce whom his opponent's will be just yet. It's Axel's choice, he can take it or leave it. Now then, that
Diamond Title has the words "screwjob" and "conspiracy" written all over it. That title has caused me nothing but trouble, and maybe finally I can restore it back to it's original state. You'll see what I mean in a few weeks. Next question please.
FOX Sports Reporter: Is it true that the NIWF is going to soon be declared bankrupt and if so, how to you tend to fix this situation without having to shut down the NIWF?
"Dirty" Dan Lukiw: Son of a bitch, where did you hear all of this? Ok, ok.. I gotta let the truth be known. Ever since the BWF vs. NIWF PPV, I was counting on all the profits and all the buy rates from that PPV to help keep the NIWF alive however, due to the law suit from [name withheld]'s attorney, I was not able to receive any profits because all of it went to
[name withheld] himself. It's rough to keep things going, especially when people in this fed even owe me some money. I'm talking about people like
Marcus Porteaux, who owes me $100! Syrus O. Beck & Diamond Doug Hunter who owe me $100. "Cold Blooded" Chris Hunter, Reckless Madison Spike, "the
Chick Magnut" Asault, Patty Babes Carter, Mr. Johnson, "Livid" Chris Vivid, Spons, "The Strangler" Shawn Osborne, Rodney "The Ram" Bennett, Survival and "The Gambler" Devon Dice all owe me money and have not paid up. It's been rough to try and keep paying all of these athletes considering the fact that the NIWF is nearing a state of bankruptcy.
However, I refuse to shut it down under any circumstances. Right now, I have a deal in the making. This deal is so HUGE that it could keep the
NIWF running for the next 4 1/2 years again. I can't reveal what type of deal it is or the source of the deal just yet, but I can gurantee you that you will find out next weekend at the next press conference. Now, as I said, I'm short on time so thank you again for joining me and have a nice day.
[Lukiw leaves as the reporters continue to try and hound him for some more questions as Lukiw complete ignores any attempt they make in trying to ask another question.]
