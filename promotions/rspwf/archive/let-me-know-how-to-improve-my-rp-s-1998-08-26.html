Let me know how to improve my RP's.....

26 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, I went against the best in the federation I am in, and I lost because I knew he was way better than me. But that just wore off the day before and cranked out some quality RP's. Still lost, but I will face him again later on down the line. Yes, I know to some the whole magic thing is different to some people, but that is the whole face of
Thanatos and how he has lived this past year. And now.......the RP that
I would like constructive criticism on.......
(Camera fades in on a forest in the middle of the night. The camera shakes a little and it catches someone wearing black holding the arm of the cameraman. He is being led somewhere and they walk past a bunch of trees, and then there is a lot of activity with people in black robes all around. The person lets go of the cameraman and the person walks in front of the camera to remove something from the cameraman...it is a blindfold. Thanatos is seen surrounded by a bunch of the Druids and he sees the cameraman walking closer to him. He waves off the Druids and walks closer.
Thanatos is wearing black leather armor, and he has a sword that has an onyx stone in the hilt.)
Glad to see that you could make it here Mr. Cameraman. Yes, I have seen the footage of Alstein ranting like the little psycho he is, and now is the time to address him. Alstein, you have to be the most ignorant, stupid, and psychotic people to ever step into the squared circle. I have not been ducking you since you been going after me.....and you had other people that you challenged even though you focused on me. So would that mean you were ducking me? You lost your belt in that Gauntlet Match because you focused on me and allowed
Doomer to pin you because you thought they couldn't do anything. You have been ducking me as you said I have been, but it is truly you that ducked me.
How can I hide behind presidential power when I'm am not the president? Whatever you are taking, I am sure stoners all around the globe would just love to get their hands on. You say you are cheered and I am booed? I think not
Alstein....that is proved false when you go and look at Death on the
Horizon and see that you were booed, while my men and me were cheered beyond belief. Go rent it again and see what I'm talking about. I have been cheered in every federation from start to finish beginning with the very first one that I joined. As I recall, when you were being booed, you had this big scowl on your face and you didn't enjoy it one bit.
You have to remember that I never ran the PWF Alstein. If I did, then how exactly were there two people standing there for press conferences when the Prez came out and I came out as the
Commissioner? I did my job as I had to being the iron fist of the federation not abusing my power. When you break the rules, you must pay for what you did. Is it my fault that I have a few friends that are celebrities? They happen to like me in and out of the ring, so I hang out with them and sometimes get some interviews with them. Since you like belts being held by true champions, you should like me because I defend my belt at every opportunity against whomever they put me against. Knox didn't want me to put my belt up against War Dog since he holds the World Title, but that is his business.
You won't be able to do what you say Alstein. Mainly because an entire stable was just hired by President Knox, and I know them from the old ICWA. I would say that you belong there, but you aren't good enough to be a member of their stable. Get your little white jacket on with the long arms on them.....you will need it where you are going. So let it be written, so let it be done. None shall stand in my way!!!
(Thanatos puts his sword on his shoulder and walks away from the cameraman. Picture fades to black on his shining sword.)
Thanatos 2 time Co-Holder nWa IC Tag Belts, 2 time Co-Holder SWF Tag Belts, nWa
Canada Champion, SWF Extreme Champion, ACWF Intercontinental Champion
Master of the Cage, Lord of Eternal Damnation, Tormentor of Double A
