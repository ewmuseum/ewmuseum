USCW Flash Central 5/4/98 Pt. 3

5 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Da Lench Mob Atomic Punk Storm w/Venus Scott Dakota -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- <you see noumenon and eightball> eightball: looks like the horseman have not had enough insult and beatings to satisfy there ignorant, horse smelling like asses. ive just got one question for you horsemen. what you gonna do if your goat is about to have a baby, will you rush out of the arena to be there for it. lord knows if the goats baby was one of yours and not your cousins, your mom might get jealous.
mopreme: as you can see, the power of my people is leading me and dlm to greatness. my people are now on the rise and will no longer fear the man or his wrath. for the mans wrath is no more. what you have now is the power switching hands and the man is desperately trying to hang on to his power. it just scares him to think that me or seagram or eightball or any other of my people will be rising to power. but the man needs to brace himself for my people are coming.
seagram: you know i now have more money then anyone in my intire family has made in a year. now isnt that something? but it looks like the horseman think they can take on the new tag team champs. they sure ass hell will have another thing coming as if they havnt figured out there first win against us was a staged victory for them. it was all just part of the mastermind plane by eightball to get us the tag team titles. as you can see, by us losing that match it gave us something to argue about and that is why i said it was all mopreme fault we lost. and you all know the rest of the story. i know there is no way that a bunch of copenhagen chewing, mamas panties wearing, back woods rednecks will ever have a chance to win in a match with any member of dlm involved.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (Camera fades in on Atomic Punk throwing darts at a local bar..) Well Battle Bowl is over and I am not 50g's richer, thats ok because I learned alot and now I will be better for it. Now as for this Storm fellow, I am looking forward to our meeting. I am not to familar with you and you are sure not too familar with me, but after this week You will not want to know anymore because I am gonna beat you down until a snake could piss on your head...HAHA....Now get that camera out of my face....
(camera pans to the dart board where a picture of Storm is taped to it and AP darts hit Storms face......fade to black.....)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- Storm: Atomik Punk........what the hell is an Atomik Punk? I mean is it a diease? Is it contagious? Why the hell am I facing a loser? See AP, I am a two time UWF/SWA World Champion, a Cruiserweight title holder as well as a former SWA/UWF UNITED States champion. And your credentials are?
Venus: All I have to say is one word...JOBBER!! Storm: Ah well, that pretty much explains it. Look Punk, I don't know who you are and frankly I don't give a rats ass. All I care about is the money I'll receive when I string your ass up to dry.
Venus: Ya, I think its time that I bought someone. Storm: So Punk, I really do have better things to do then comment on our match, you know like watching paint dry or watching the grass grow. I'll see you Wednesday.....Punk!
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (The camera fades in and Scott Dakota is sitting on a chair in what looks to be the living room of his house. He is watching the TV, on which is his last match where he defeated "The Machine" Ron Matthews. He turns his head towards the camera and begins to speak.)
DAKOTA: Matthews, I have given alot of thought to this. I know how badly you want a rematch against me, I know how much you want to hurt me and prove that my win over you was nothing more than "a fluke". Well, after many hours of deliberation, I have finally come up with a way to settle this. Now, you blame your loss against me on the referee, even though we all know you are the one to blame. This time around there will no way you can place the blame on anybody, because it is going to be an I QUIT match. Yes, that is right, an I Quit Match. The rules are simple, there are no rules. The only way to emerge victorious from this style of match is to make your opponent quit. I am not a submission wrestler, but I know that after I beat you to within an inch of your life, that you will finally just give it up and quit and realize that I am the better man.
(The screen on the TV shows Dakota nail the Silencer on Matthews and then cover him for the 3 count. Dakota clicks the TV off and the room is now totally dark, suddenly a match is lit, and Dakota lights a candle with it. Then he continues speaking.)
DAKOTA: This will be the true test on who the best man really is Matthews, this is the one for all the bragging rights. I just would like to inform you that Scott Dakota does not quit, there is absolutley no way in hell that any man can make me give up. Especially a man like you, someone who thinks they are a wrestling god, well, I am going to prove you wrong. I have not submitted once yet in my career and I sure as hell do not plan on submitting to you. Matthews, you better be totally on top of your game for this one, because I intend on hitting you with everything I have. And if you act all cocky and take me lightly like you did the last time, the outcome will be strikingly similar, you will be
SILENCED once again. (Dakota blows out the candle and the screen goes black.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------
