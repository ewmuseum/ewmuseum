[Free Agent] Jeff "Madfox" Matthews addresses Simon Lebec

17 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This ought to be interesting ;D.... [Camera fades in through black, showing Lawrence Williams in his computer room standing next to some white guy with glasses. Williams is wearing a black shirt entitled "SHUT UP DAMNIT!" The white guy has a white dress shirt on with a pen protecter on his left breast pocket.]
Lawrence: Aight then... you wanna play uh lil' bit uh that game, eh? Guy: Alright then. You want to play a little bit of that game, eh? [Lawrence smacks the white guy.] Lawrence: Shut up, boy. I ain't tell ya' ta' speak. Anyway... Colby, you jus' like ev'ryone else . . . ignorant. Ya' didn't unda'stand muh words yet you responded ta' what I said? Well, in the event that ya' might not be unda'standin' what I'm sayin', I brought in my translata'. So... let's get started, shall we?
Translator: So, let's get started, shall we? Lawrence: Ya' might not be in UEW no mo', but when ya' wearin' black shirts entitled "UEW," somebody's gonna assume you in it, jackass.
Translator: You might not be in UEW anymore, but when you're wearing black shirts entitled "UEW," somebody's going to assume that you're in it, jackass.
Lawrence: An' I'll associate you wit' whoever I wanna...an' ya' wanna know why? Translator: And I'll associate you with whoever I want to, and you want to know why? Lawrence: 'Cause I'm the best in this got damned business... In other words: 'Cause I *can.* Translator: Because I'm the best in this Goddamned business. In other words: Because I *can.* Lawrence: So now ya' wanna call me primitive, eh? What the hell does primitive mean? [Lawrence smacks the translator.] Translator: Mr. Williams, it means that you're original. Lawrence: Ya' damn straight I'm original... rat-faced bastard. Translator: You're damn straight I'm original, rat-faced bastard. Lawrence: Ya' might think I'm uh stain on society, but if I *eva'*, *eva'* get muh hands on ya', you'll be uh stain on the sidewalk... outlined in chalk, bitch!
Translator: You might think that I'm a stain on society, but if I ever, ever get my hands on you, you'll be a stain on the sidewalk, outlined in chalk, bitch.
Lawrence: So, until next time, you can kiss muh ass... an' ya' wanna know why? 'Cause thas the truf, because the truf hurts. Translator: So, until next time, you can kiss my ass. And you want to know why? Because that's the--
[Lawrence smacks the translator.] Lawrence: Shut the hell up, boy. Ya' don't do it right. [Fade.]  -Daniel "He that is void of wisdom despiseth his neighbour: but a man of understanding holdeth his peace." - Proverbs 11:12
