[FREE AGENT] Enigma

4 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey...   I am interested in joining a fed...   Here are some stats....   NAME: Enigma AGE: ?? WEIGHT: 245lbs HEIGHT: 6.5"   Here is my roleplay....Not my best, but I have no one to pick on, no one to mention, and no angle to talk about, apart from my enigmatic self of course....
  (The scene opens in a dark room. The only illumination is coming from a fading log fire in the centre of the room. A dark figure can be made out, sitting on a chair reading a book. The figure flicks the last few pages of the book over slowly and then throws the book to the floor. He starts to talk with his back still to the camera.)
  Enigma: A new day, a new month, a new year, a new life. A new federation for me to wreak havoc, a new federation for me to control, a new federation for me to destroy.
  Enigma: I have been around the circuit a couple of times under a different name, so I know quite a few of you, but you don't have a clue who I am. I am back under this name to start again in the professional wrestling world, and to gain revenge.
  Enigma: Many people in the wrestling world double crossed me and in time I will extract my revenge, slowly and painfully for all those involved.
  (Enigma gives a short sadistic laugh, and turns to face the camera)   Enigma: You see, fans and wrestlers alike: I don't give a f*** about myself or anyone else and this makes me dangerous. You people can spout about how you beat this person, and that person, but really, I don't give a f*** because you havn't beaten me, and you will not beat me. Not unless I let you........
  (He pauses to light a cigarette, and takes a long pull on it.)   Enigma: So prez, who will be my first opponent? Who cares? Whoever it is will fall before Enigma, like so many have done in the past.
  (Suddenly the fire flickers and burns out leaving the room in total darkness apart from the tiny dot of light emerging from Enigma's cigarette.)
  Enigma: Who knew.....my favourite color is red.....like the bloodshed....what a line...   (Enigma laughs and gets up. He picks up the book he dropped on the floor and sits down again.)
  Enigma: The Crow. A graphic novel, a book, a comic. Whatever. A very good book. Evil, sadistic, bloody. Just like me....
  Enigma: Before I depart I have a message for the champions of this federation: Be afraid...be very afraid....because Enigma is coming.
  (Enigma takes his cigarette out of his mouth and slowly puts it on the pages of the book until they catch light. He then throws it into the fire place, which quickly ignites illuminating the room in an orange/yellow light. The camera man focuses on the fire and then swings back to the chair.
Enigma is not there. The camera swings up to catch a glimpse of Enigma's evil smiling face as he leaves the room)
