[FLASH] "The Master" Ken Stone - please critique!

13 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, Haven't done anything serious with my character 'The Master' Ken Stone in a while. This is a flash I did with him just minutes ago. Could someone
_please_ critique this. I hope to make him a more common character soon. BTW, this flash was for an 'indy' fed that I am to further develop my character without the pressures of a real fed.
Flamers, Feel free to do your thing. :) =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [Pre-recorded comments from the immediate conclusion of Rampage.] [Fade into the locker room of the one and only 'Master' Ken Stone. He still has on his wrestling attire - consisting of red 'brief' style tights, knee high black and red boots, as well as red knee and elbow pads. Stone is taking begining to unravel the white medical tape from his hands and wrists. His short black hair is still sweaty, and he has sweat running down his face. He has a calm look on his face as he works on pulling off the tape on his wrists.
When he notices the camera, he turns to it, and begins speaking.] "THE MASTER" KEN STONE - Ahh, another day in the life of a pro-wrestler. Pretty interesting, eh? I'd say so. Not only were we turned on by that low-life Adam
Blast, but we were also attacked by half the damn roster. The Lost Soul, Blast,
3T. But hey, things happen, it's nothing to cry about. Blast was nothing but that fifth wheel anyways - we never needed him - and we never will. It is now time for the "true" Misfits to show through in the forms of Kenneth Karby and
Ken Stone.
[By now, Stone has finished un-taping one of his wrists. As he walks over toward the wastebasket and discards of the dirty, sweaty tape, he continues.]
"THE MASTER" KEN STONE - Y'see. It has been me and Kenneth all along. From the
NCWO, to the EWGP, to the OWA, and now to the GCW, it has always been
Kennethand myself. Yeah, we had the "Extreme Alliance" once, but I never really felt as though I was wanted or appreciated by anyone but Kenneth. I felt like a true "misfit" when I was with the rest of the boys. Don't get me wrong, I had some good times with them, but it was _nothing_ compared to what me and Kenneth
've done. God those were fun times.
[Stone has now made his way over to the mini refridgerator. He opens it and grabs out a bottle of water. He spins off the top, throws it across the room into the wastebasket, and takes a sip.]
"THE MASTER" KEN STONE - Ahhh. That's better. Anyways, Kenneth and I have been through it all. We've been abandoned, and we've been discarded, but we always stick together. It's more than just a friendship - it's a bond. A bond... in which not many men have. Kenneth and I will be friends 'till the end, and there's nothing, and I mean _nothing_ that anyone can or will do to change it.
[Stone pauses and takes a swig of his water.] [He has now made his way over to the white leather couch placed in the middle of the room. He has peeled off his boots and knee pads, and removed his elbow pads, all of which are setting on the floor beside the sofa. He is sitting in it, the water on the table next to him, and he begins unpealing the tape from his other hand. As he does so, he continues.]
"THE MASTER" KEN STONE - Now, I've got a lot to discuss. First on the list is that two-timing weasel, Adam Blast. Blast, I never really knew you.. and honestly.. never really liked you. But Kenneth had a good thing going with you, and I went along for the ride. The "new" Misfits were to reign supreme here in the GCW. But no longer. It is now a stronger force that will rule - and that's the Misfits.
[Stone pauses, picks up his water, and takes a drink. He sets it down, continues with his taped wrist, and continues speaking.]
"THE MASTER" KEN STONE - Accept no cheep imitations, it's for real. The true
Misfits are here, and here to stay. And now is the time when all must be afraid. The Misfits have been "let out of the cage" if you will.. and believe you me, the result.. will not be a pretty thing.
Y'see. Not many people have seen the real Misfits in action. We had to deal with that "fifth wheel" Adam Blast. Well, now that that chump is gone, the times 'll be changin'. It'll be myself and Kenneth Karby who will reign supreme once again.. and we're gonna do it...
... Misfits' style. [Stone pauses once again and takes a swig of his drink before continuing.] "THE MASTER" KEN STONE - Now, on to other things. Such as the 3T. Young, Thunderdome - you two punks need to be taught a lesson. Young will get his at the pay-per-view.. and.. if he has the balls.. so will Thunderdome.
Max, you were once a mentor to me. You were the founding father of the Extreme
Alliance and one hell'va guy. But not anymore. I am no longer that same rookie.
I will no longer be that clay in your hands and I will no longer be molded to fit your needs. I am my own man, and don't need your advice anymore. So Max, consider our friendship _over!
[Stone now has somewhat of a more serious expression on his face. He continues.] "THE MASTER" KEN STONE - Max. I've got one question to ask ya': Do you still got it? Well, let's see. I challenge you to a no disqualification _steel cage_ match at the upcoming pay-per-view. Max, do ya got the balls? If ya' think ya'
do, than let's get it on. And if you decline, consider yourself cowardly and washed-up. Either way, you _will_ be embarrased. It's your call Maxie.
Next on my agenda is our newly named president, the 'esteemed' Jeremy Wright. Wright, you couldn't hack it back in the SFW, so what thinks you think you can hack it now? Yeah, yeah, I know. You're not fighting, you're just being a
"suit". Well Wright, you sure had some "colorful" opinions about me on Rampage.
"The Imposter", eh? Well, let's just see how how well you talk when your foot gets shoved down your [censored] throat! Oh Wright, what a bright day it will be.. what a bright day it will be.
[Stone now has the tape unraveled. He sets it on the table and picks up his water. 'The Master' takes a drink, than sets the water back down. He stands up and heads over toward a door.]
"THE MASTER" KEN STONE - Wright, believe-you-me, you'll get yours soon enough. [Stone opens the door. Inside is a bathroom. He steps in.] "THE MASTER" KEN STONE - A revolution is upon us. And it's gonna be hell when the Misfits run this joint. Take it to the bank, 'cause you can bet your life it'll happen.
GCW, you've yet to see what kind of hell we can raise. But believe me, when it happens, you _will_ be afraid, and you _will_ fear the us. Kenneth Karby and
Ken Stone are two names you will _never_ forget. Let's get ready. Get ready for what?
Ready to do it... ...Misfits' style. [Stone slams the door shut. Water can be heard running in the background as the shot fades to black.]
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Thanx in advance, -Rick- Handler of: ["The Phoenix" Evan Manchester] - {ICW, PWU} ["The Master" Ken Stone] - {GCW - ["Indy" fed]} --"If words were wisdom, I'd be talking even more." --"If you don't expect too much from me, you might not be let down."
