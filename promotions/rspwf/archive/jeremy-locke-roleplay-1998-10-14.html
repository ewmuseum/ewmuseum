Jeremy Locke roleplay

14 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I am a newcomer to this game. My character name is Jeremy Locke and I am currently in the UUW, run by a classmate of mine at the
University of New Hampshire. He told me about this e-wrestling thing a little while ago, so I've decided to give it a try. My guy is a poetic, spooky, silent, introspective man with long hair, a muscular build, and a trenchcoat he wears everywhere.
There was an injustice done to him when he was wrong... actually, a couple of injustices. His father worked for the government until he "knew too much" and was murdered, but it was covered up as a suicide. His mother was taken to a mental hospital after this tragedy, leaving Jeremy alone at age 10.
He then moved into the desolate streets of America. He goes from city to city, with just his trenchcoat keeping him company. To eat he steals from rich restaurants. He feeds his homeless friends this way too. He is not another "homeless" character, he is a loner and a vagabond in the classic sense of the word. He is a nomad, and he chooses to be.
He is well educated and versed in poems and music of all kinds. He got his education by studying books in the great libraries of universities.
He has come to wrestling to take down the hierarchy and fight in an organized way, rather than the fighting he usually does in the streets.
He loves causing pain, the same pain he feels in his heart every day of his life. So read the roleplay, tell me what you think. If you like it, give me feedback. If you don't, give me feedback. I'm not looking for new feds,
I just want feedback.
N.E.L. [The man walks the streets alone. The man walks in solitude and confinement. Confinement that befalls anyone who is truly free, the confinement of the mind. The confinement of what other people use to opress you. Here, in New York City, in the night lights that glow with an effervescent intrusion, the man walks the streets alone and mumbles to himself...]
JL: "As if I was supposed to know. As if I was supposed to care. You continue to pain the heart that is defeated, And now you want me to scream for you, To make you feel a taste of what I'm feeling, For your own delight..." [The man lets out a blood curdling scream, echoing through his chiseled chest, his air-filled lungs, his angry heart, his hollow soul...]
JL: "And so it is done, and you can continue. Like you did to my father, Out on the street with nowhere to go but down. Like you did to my mother, Out of her mind with nowhere to go but the room, With padded walls And embracing jackets that signify failure To embrace the world as you would see fit to." [The long blond hair of the man flows through the winds that swirl below the all-encompassing buildings above him. His black coat, long and flowing down to his ankles, seems to trail behind the now faster paced man as he dodges numerous city obstacles... mailboxes, lampposts...]
JL: "And you believe that you are right. I am the forbidden son I am the omega I am your pain, so feel me. I am the stranger that cries in the night, I am the orphan, left lost and afraid in the darkness By you You may be right, But I will prevail over your tyranny. You cannot do to me what you have done to the others." [The man escapes into a dark alley with just one phrase coming out of his mouth.] JL: "When you see the shadows of the night you will understand." [The voice fades, as does the man.] -- Jeremy Locke "Embracing the sound of silence"
