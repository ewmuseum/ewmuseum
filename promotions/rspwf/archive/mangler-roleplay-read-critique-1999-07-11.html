{Mangler} Roleplay.... READ!!!! (Critique)

11 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{The camera shot opens up to a forest... a Great Big forest. Only something seemd wierd with the picture... is it the ground? No. They have leaves and stick stroon about. Is it the Trees? No... they seem fine. Is it the sky?
Yes... yes, it's the sky. It has 2 suns. On top of that, the sky is purple. How strange is that.} {The camera starts to shake and then it spins around. Big strange birds are flying through the sky, ans sitting on branches. As if the camera man is turning it towards himself. The person who's holding is... Mangler.. or as he likes to call himself.. Jackyl. He has a big grin on his face as if very
VERY happy about something. His messed up shoulder length looks as if he's been working out. We can see he has black marks on his eyes. He appears to be breathing heavily...}
Jackyl: So, the truth has finelly come on... The Mystery Man has a name, and his name is Jackyl. Some of you may remember my greatness, but most of you probally won't. I am not... I repeat, am not, Mangler. Call me Jackyl. I used Mangler to show all you retarded mortals that anyone can be a Champion.
That anybody can get Respect. {He pauses as if to catch his breath. He looks a little fatigued. Then he continues...} Mangler: I am a God... and thats been painfully obviouse since the first
AEWO. Since I won your stupid heavywieght title after beating Phear666 over a year ago. Well... some of you are probally still scepticle about my alliance with Lord Phear. Well, it's simple, we are both of Devine heratige.
It's only natral that we for a sort of unity... that's what makes us...The
Omnipotent Ones. Because thats what we are, Omnipotent.
{Big Smile is on his face} Jackyl: It is my understanding that I might involved in a match this week for the World Title again... Now first let me say that I've one it befire, and I'll win it again. Not because of my incredible skill or strength, but because of my mind... You puny mortals only use 8 percent of your brain capacity, I will use 50... Hey, if i used 100.. that would be cheap for your retarded minds. So, 50 percent will do.
When someone steps into the ring with my, they have an instant feeling of emptiness and horror. Why, you say? Well, cause like Phear said before, I am the God of Pain and Sorrow. But don't get me wrong, life being me isn't hard at all.... Just those followers of mine... and thats why they follow me, because when they follow me... They know there's hope. People always need to follow someone who's better then them, because it gives them hope..
So, with that I say... Good Bye till next time.. I won't miss you at all!!! {He starts to laugh uncontrolablly as he turns the camera back around.}
{The Camera then appears to be thrown into the air... when it lands. There is nothin but static. Only Static... as a bright blue and white Jackyl logo comes onto the screen, and the shot fades out.}
[Fade Out]
