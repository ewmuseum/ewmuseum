[Old Ass 800 Year Old Roleplay]Myths and Millions

7 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
By popular demand!!!! Without further ado!! And be merciless in your critiques..... [Fade in to black screen....] A Thin Redd Line Productions A Myths and Millions Presentation Allen Redd & Alex Garrison in... REAL RAYS!! [The scene fades in on an aquarium. The camera opens inside, with a large stage and a large fish tank behind it with all sorts of sea creatures, including
MANTA RAYS!! That seems to be the main topic of discussion, because there is a lady on the stage dressed in a brown uniform: khaki shorts, a vest, light brown shirt and some necktie thingy that's brown. There is a table on the stage, and it has plastic manta rays and manta ray skeletons on it. The lady is speaking in front of a large group of small kids seated on the ground Indian style.
Their parents are behind them, seated in a small cafeteria-like room, ideal for watching children since it's open to the area and there's coffee and smoking is allowed (always a plus for parents). The lady stops in the middle of the stage and clasps her hands together with a big smile.]
Lady: Now children, we have a treat for you today. There are two professional wrestlers from Maple Leaf Wrestling here today, and they have asked me if they can talk to you about manta rays. [Some mothers come to take away their kids upon the mention of "professional wrestlers," much to the children's dismay] So
I've given them permission. So children, let's give a nice round of applause for Myths and Millions!
[She goofily claps in a circle, and some of the kids mimic it laughing. Myths and Millions then walk on-stage from off camera, as she walks off the opposite end. Allen Redd is sporting a blue and tan Hawaiian print shirt with a white wife beater underneath, khaki shorts, blue Saucony's, a pair of sunglasses and a nice gold watch to compliment the gold frame of the glasses. Alex Garrison is not far behind him, wearing the usual weathered trench coat, black jeans, and a black T-shirt with white writing that says "Size Does Matter" and an arrow pointing towards his "belt" that is neatly tucked in. Other mothers come and take their children away. Alex then takes off the jacket and lays it on a clear part of the table. As he turns around, you can see "Myths and Millions" written on the back of the shirt. Allen Redd takes his glasses off and hangs them on his undershirt as Alex joins him in the middle of the stage.]
Allen: Hello kids. How are you today? Kids: [nowhere near in unison unlike wrestling fans] Fine! Great! Nifty! Allen: Well, that's good. Now, today we're gonna talk about Rays. Alex: [with that European accent] But not just any Rays, Ultra-Violent Rays. Lady: [from offstage] Ahem, that's manta rays! Allen: Whatever lady. Anyway, let's talk abou-- Some Kid: --My mom said wrestling is fake and it's smut or something and it brainwashes people and gives them bad ideas and kids shouldn't watch it cause it's past their bed time and cau--
Alex: --That's real cute chap, but it isn't any of that. We'll show yah how fake it is in ah minute too.
Allen: Yeah, just sit tight and listen to our Ultra-Violent Rays presentation. Lady: Uhh, that's mant-- M&M: WE KNOW!!! [the kids laugh] Another Kid: [he is wearing a Fantastic Four shirt] I think you mean Ultra-Violet Rays. Alex: Nah, it's Ultra-Violent. Same Kid: No. The Fantastic Four got shot by Ultra Violet Rays and got their super powers. Allen: [to Alex.] And that's how The Thing got ugly too [laughs]. Sorry to tell ya kid, but these guys are a lot less than super.
Fantastic Four Mark: Oh [he looks down, then back at them confusedly.] You sure? Alex: Positive kid. Nowhea' close. [pauses to think] On second thought, "Devil" Ray Davinci is like Tha' Thing in ah way. He's big an' ugly. Reeeeeeaaaaal ugly. Allen: Yeah, and we're gonna rearrange their ugly faces. Those mutha{BLEEP}as have no chance of beating us.
Kids and Parents: GASP!! [more parents take away their kids.] [They look at the parents taking away their kids, then look at the camera which is still there and smile. They turn to the kids and continue.]
Alex: Kids, it's reallay a shame they think they can beat us. Well, I'm not sure wha' they think. I'm jus' naturally assuming they think they can beat us.
But it really doesn't matta', 'cause wea' gonna beat em anyway. Allen: [he turns towards Alex] Yeah. We've got waaaayyy too much money, and waaaayyyy too many women to lose to them. Not to mention the talent. [pauses, then continues.] Hey, wasn't you in a grip of fights back in tha day?
Alex: [turns towards Allen] Yah. An' I'll tell yah I fought ah lot tougher guys than yah see in these MLW tag teams, especially tha' Rays. [thinks] Oh yeah, and tha' Loonay Bin.
Allen: [turns completely to Alex.]The Looney Bin is a joke. Number One Contenders, and they don't even know it. Neither do they deserve it. But lets not look past our match against the Gays, I mean Rays. [yet more kids and parents leave, but he doesn't notice.] These two are the paragon of imperfection, and we are the absolutely incomparable tag team. The only thing is, we gotta work our way up in MLW. But it won't take long. With our talent, it won't take long at all.
Alex: [turns to Allen completely and looks with confusion written on his face.] Paragon of imperfection? Isn't that ah bit contradictory, or an oxymoron? Allen: [turned towards Alex, using hand motions.] Two answers to that. With the
Rays, it isn't contradictory. They are perfect in their imperfection. So it's more like a paradox. I'm glad you picked it up though, your English was coming along pretty bad.
Alex: [laughs] I know REAL English from England, mate. Ha ha. I don't need no stinkin' language to kick somebody's ahss though, I'll tell yah that. An' tha'
Ultra-Violents are gonna find that out. Allen: There's no way they're gonna beat us. It's a double-debut match, and we need to win our first match. We've focused in on our target, and there's nothing left to do but strike.
Alex: [he cuts from talking to Allen and looks around at the remaining kids, who are bored from most of the talking that didn't have to do with manta rays.]
Uh, hey kids! Yah still wanna see how real wessling is? [Numerous nods.] Allen: Okay, let's show em what wea' gonna do to tha' Rays, dawg. [They walk over to the table with the props. Allen picks up a plastic ray and snaps it in half, and Alex takes a skeleton ray and smashes it to powder. The children look with wide-eyed horror, then cheer for some odd reason.]
Allen: Now you know. So that's what we're gonna do to the Rays on Monday Night, understand?
Kids: [now in unison like wrestling fans] Noooooo! Alex: Break it down for em, patna. Allen: Okay, repeat after me. We're gonna kick their {BLEEP}ES!! KICK THEIR
{BLEEP}ES!! Alex: KICK THEIR AHSES!! KICK THEIR AHSES!! Kids: KICK THEIR {BLEEP}ES!! KICK THEIR {BLEEP}ES!! [The parents go ape[bleep] and they try to take their kids away. But they kick and squirm away, as Myths and Millions have lit a fire and started a mini-riot.
The kids storm the stage shaking little fists in the air.] Alex: WHO'S AHSES?!?! KIDS: WE DONT KNOW!! KICK THEIR {BLEEP}ES!! Allen: Ray Davinci and Ray McWEENY, that's who!! Kids: RAY MCWEENY!! RAY MCWEENY! [Six security guards come, and grab Myths and Millions and pick them up by the feet and upper body and start to take them away. The mothers start to subdue their children.]
Alex: [being carried away] Ultra-Violent Rays, we got yah numba'! Monday Night, it's sink or swim!!
Allen: [also being carried away] And you guys are gonna be the ones sinkin'!! [M&M are carried off camera by the security guards, and the camera fades out to faint shouts of "RAY MCWEENY"]
