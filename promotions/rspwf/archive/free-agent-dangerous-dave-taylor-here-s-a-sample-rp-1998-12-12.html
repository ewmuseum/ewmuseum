(Free Agent)"Dangerous" Dave Taylor-Here's a sample RP

12 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Act 1. -[Scene]- The Camera opens on the Mass Turn Pike. "Dangerous" Dave Taylor is in the drivers seat, driving his traditional red Hummer. He has some music playing..hardly can you make it out. He pulls up to the toll booth and reaches into his pocket for a quarter. He slowly stops his car at the booth. A young adoloescent male is sitting there playing with his gum. He is staring deeply into a tiny TV he has on a little shelf.
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor: Here ya go....{the kid just sits there as if he's deaf}HEY KID!{The kid snaps out of it and slowly looks at D.D.T.}
Kid:...uh.oh..sorry about that....25 Cents Sir. "Dangerous" Dave Taylor: Yeah don't be sorry just don't do it again. 25? Here.......{flips the kid a coin}... Kid:...Cheapskate..... "Dangerous" Dave Taylor:..WHAT!? I paid the damn poll you idiot. Damn..your almost as dumb as FrAsCo.
Kid: FrAsCo....The Wrestler in XBWL?? "Dangerous" Dave Taylor: No FrAsCo in "Days of our Lifes!" Kid:..Oh I don't know him "Dangerous" Dave Taylor:...Stupid kid...YEAH FRASCO FROM XBWL!! Damn kids these days.....stop smoking all that damn pot and drinking all that damn liquor.
Kid:..No Sir, I don't do that stuff. Speaking of LIQUOR...what's that bottle near your lap.
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor: Listen punk, don't get cocky with me!! I'm also
24...soon to be 25...a little bit older then your sorry (CENSORED)!!
Kid:...Oh..I'm 16!! I can do whatever I want, whenever I want, HOWEVER I
WANT!! My dad's Paul Celluci, Governor of this great state!! Too bad I gotta work in this hell hole....
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor:..I was born in Boston, raised in New York, and then I moved back to Boston....when I was first here I don't remember to much...I remember The Kennedy's...yeah good ol' Drunken Ted!! Hahaha....yeah there's an
UNITED STATES I-CON! When I left...I left on my own, New York is a tough plce to live as a kid. But I made it...and when I moved back to Boston 4 years ago....we had one fool named Bill Weld Governor.....Once again, all the Pro
Sports teams sucked, and many people were out of jobs. So listen kid, be thankful you got a job...and as far as you doing WHAT YOU WANT, WHEN YOU WANT, and HOW YOU WANT TO is the biggest amount of BS I've ever heard.....there is only one man who can do that and your being taught a lesson on life by him right now!!
Kid:...Oh Yeah? Well I can do this....{The Kid SPITS on D.D.T.'s FACE!} and
ANOTHER thing....I hope BEAST KICKS YOUR (CENSORED)!!
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor: YOU LITTLE (CENSORED)!!! You'll pay for that......hahah and SO WILL BEAST!! {D.D.T. chucks the beer bottle at the kid and misses purposely...glass shatters all over the wall and it fires back and
stabs the kid numerous times in the head!! The kid screams in pain and then drops to the ground and the door opens up and he rolls out. D.D.T. pulls away in his Hummer as another car pulls up and just barely misses hitting the poor son of a (CENSORED).....The Camera slowly fades out}
Act 2. -[Scene]- The Camera opens up out side of The Boston Fleetcenter. Thousands of fans are waiting in the cold Boston weather for the gates to open to get in side to watch an XBWL Arena Show. Suddenly..the ever so famous red hummer pulls up as the fans go absolutely ape (CENSORED) at the site of the car. "Dangerous" Dave
Taylor parks in the parking lot assigned for wrestlers and fans hold up there signs....and start to cheer loudly as D.D.T. walks by...one fan catches his attention. A few signs being held up read "MOD SUCKS!", "Monster
Mac+Andrews=WIN FOR MAC", "D.D.T.=NEW XBWL HARDCORE CHAMPION", and "PPP WAS
SCREWED". "Dangerous" Dave Taylor stops and talks with some fans as an XBWL
Camera Crew comes out followed by Johnny Diamond.
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor: Hey Johnny....haven't spoken to you in a while.... Johnny Diamond:...Yeah Well Dave hopefully this isn't permanet. That fat slob
Doc was too lazy to show up tonight. I'll be doing interviews AND commentary now! Anyways...now that I'm here I'd like to ask you a few questions.....the fans love you...a title shot is within your grasp...and a world title shot...something many think you DO NOT deserve is possible for you at the Pay
Per View....but right now.....the main problem...ALLIES.
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor: Well Johnny...the love of the fans to me is like gas to a car engine...it keeps me going. It looks like A hardcore title shot is in my future but I need to pass FrAsCo this week....exact some revenge for last week....yeah well I probably don't. But I'm going to make the best of it. I'm going to try my hardest.....the hardest i've EVER worked for something to win!
I have a great team behind me, many think I won't even make it to the Battle
Royal....they think I WON'T SURVIVE...well I will...and my motivation is the critcism....the skepticism...that's what got me so damn active in the first place.....so Eric Bohringer and Sean Kanyon II....you two fools go right ahead and make your "predictions", it'll be MY pleasure proving you wrong!!!!!!!
Jack Diamond: Woa...well...Dave....best of luck in doing so. I hope you go far....you have a bright career ahead of you!
-[Scene]- Johnny Diamond slowly walks away out of sight. D.D.T. gives some fans high fives. And he walks into the arena.
Act 3. -[Scene]- The Camera opens up inside of the Fleetcenter. The fans are rather quiet after a squash match between Johnny Rebel and Sweet Shawn. Suddenly..."DANGEROUS" by
Busta Rhymes plays over the XBWL Speakers. The fans all rise to their feet and chant "DDT!". "Dangerous" Dave Taylor walks out from behind the curtain wearing a black "Dangerous" Dave Taylor-Shirt. The front reads..."You will be".....and the back "DaNGeROuSLy DeSTroYEd!!!!!!!!!". His slick, short black hair gleams in the light. He walks up the ring steps and climbs the rope and jumps into the ring. He grabs a mic as the fans slowly quiet down.
"Dangerous" Dave Taylor: XBW F(CENSORED)ING L!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! {Fans go insane cheering and start up a "XBWL" chant that soon dies}...Wednesday
Night.....an event of sorts will take place. FrAsCo....the outcome of the match will be MUCH different this time. My body is in great shape...but to be pure.....I must be in great shape mentally. And now I am. But according to
XBWL's IDOL..I'm just TOOOOOO COOL! Yeah I'm that along with many other things, talented, clever, funny, brave, strong, and risky. Yeah, I take risk....life is full of them. In 48 hours...FrAsCo will be but one more little satistic in D.D.T.'s book. FrAsCo..it seems as if you are still riding high after last weeks win over me. You must learn...not to dwell on the past...but if you must....BE MY (CENSORED) GUEST!! Over time....things change....and people change. I've changed since last week...I have a new look at things.
And FrAsCo....my new vision.....pictures me kicking your (CENSORED) from pillar to post wednesday night at the XBWL Arena!! After I beat you.....I'll go into the Pay Per View....and I'll have to fight many times. Possibly 3 times. Once against BEAST...once as a member of Team 2.....and once as a member of The
Battle Royal. I WILL MAKE IT TO THE BATTLE ROYAL, don't get me wrong. I will try SO DAMN HARD TO WIN THAT TITLE. But...if infact I do win The Hardcore
Title...will I work a little less in The Tournament? Hell NO! I'll work even harder! So Wednesday.......Sunday....FrAsCo......BEAST......Team's
1,3,4,5,6,7,8.....and any lucky man who makes it to the battle royal.........let me sum up your fate for you in 5 words!! YOU WILL
BE...........DaNGeROusLY DeStRoyED!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! -[Scene]- "DANGEROUS" by Busta Rhymes blast as the fans all stand on their feet loudly screaming and cheering on D.D.T. "Dangerous" Dave Taylor stands on the top turnbuckle with his arms in the air as the fans erupt into a "DDT" chant!! He throws his shirt into the crowd as fans dive after it like maniacs!!!!!! He leaves the ring way as the music slowly dies out.........
