{... You see "The Immortal Maruader" Chris Xavior pull up to the McDonald's of
Corpus Christi which is only about 45 minutes away from Woodsboro. He exits his pick up truck and enters the McDonald's. ...}
{... He goes up and orders a goddamn Big Mac. He looks up at the picture of the Big Mac on the menu deal above the manager. The burger arrives and he opens it up. The manager is the man who served him. ...}
|Immortal Maruader|: You know what pisses me off? Manager: What, sir? |Immortal Maruader|: That this burger is all flat and shit. {... Points at the menu. ...} |Immortal Maruader|: I want my burger to be big a great like that one up there, but you come and give me this old piece of shit that could have been sitting on the warming rack for weeks. Get me a _FRESH_, nice, juicy burger. I don't what this shit.
{... Chunks the burger on the floor. ...} |Immortal Maruader|: Oh, I will just let the help clean up that crap burger. Manager: I am sorry, Sir, but I will have to ask you to leave. |Immortal Maruader|: Have you heard the saying that the costumer is always right? Manager: OK, Sir. You have made your point. I will get on your order. {... The manager turns his back and orders in _SPANISH_ for the Mexican to make another burger. ...}
|Immortal Maruader|: Damned Sand Niggers. Takin' over our country. Pretty soon, we ain't gonna be in Texans we will me Texicans.
Manager: Here you go, sir. {... The manager comes over with a big mac that looks exactly like in the picture. Chris Xavior pays for it, and goes into the corner to eat. ...}
|Immortal Maruader|: Finally a good burger from McDonald's. This must be a first. Now, Madison I said I was gonna whip your ass and I did. I beat you like your Daddy did. Well, it seems to me that no one on God's Green Earth can beat me. Look I ran over Steel and Madison. 2-0. This is only the start of my streak. Think Goldberg was something? Wait till you see me get going.
Now, Madison I didn't talk trash I like I said. I did win. Now I see that is me vs. Red Hot vs. Chris Moss. {... Takes a bite out of his burger. ...} |Immortal Maruader|: Well first thing is first. Chris Moss... I do respect you and what you bring to the ring. I know your good. And I also know that I can beat you. Moss, your not a road block your just a speed bump.
{... Sips some of his vanilla milk shake. ...} |Immortal Maruader|: Red Hot... man I cannot respect a man who runs around with a whore on his arm. Look at your "valet." She will be standin' on some street corner sellin' her ugly ass for 10 cents a kiss, 5 cents a hug, and I wonder what I could get for a dollar? You are some messed up piece of trash to be liven with a whore. I mean where the hell is the respect for yourself? If I was ever with a whore I would hang myself by a guitar string from a deer stand.
I'll be damned if I will been seen with a whore. {... Takes the last bite out of his burger. ...} |Immortal Maruader|: Lets go take a little question to the people in this crap food house. {... He gets up and dumps his tray into the trash can. He walks over to this booth were 4 very pretty girls are sitting and eating ...}
|Immortal Maruader|: Howdy! Can I ask yall a question or 2? Girl #1: Sure. |Immortal Maruader|: OK. Now you all watch IEWA? Girl #2: I never miss it. |Immortal Maruader|: Well as you may know I have a match with Red Hot and Chris
Moss. But the question is do you really think Red Hot is red hot? I mean he is an ugly son of a bitch.
Girl #3: Yuck! He is so ugly. If I ever saw him naked I would die. Girl #4: Really! He is a total loser. I would never go on a date with him in fear of being seen.
|Immortal Maruader|: Well, what do you girls think about his whore, I mean valet? Girl #1: She is a whore. Look how she dresses. She has no self respect. Girl #2: I would never do what she does. Girl #3: She dresses like a slut and must be looser than a Monica after the fleet has been in.
|Immortal Maruader|: Well , folks there you have it. Red Hot is a ugly loser, and his valet is a whore. There is no question in this world about that.
{... Walks out the door of the McDonald's, and he stops and talks. ...} |Immortal Maruader|: Everything that I have said has come true. Everything... I said I would beat Steel and Madison. Now I am saying that one of you will be pinned. It don't really matter who is pinned cause I can and I will fight you and win. I don't give a damn if I fight both of you for the title. There is no way that a couple of pieces of trash are gonna beat a fine SOB from South
Texas. Even though I do respect Moss, I am not scared of you or your skills.
Hell, you meet me in a bar with a gang to back you up and a baseball bat in your hands, I would still fight you. You think I am just gonna run off like some little pussy? Well, you got another thing comin'. I will beat your ass all the way to hell and back. Moss you act like your a "preacher's" boy. A little God damn goodie two-shoes. What annoys me is you. You tryin' to be so perfect just annoys the hell out of me. But then again, your gonna deny all this. You do cuss, but hell the pope has cussed a few times in his life.
Moss... Red Hot... You're all gonna get whipped on Tuesday. {... Fades to black. ...} please E-Mail what you think....
