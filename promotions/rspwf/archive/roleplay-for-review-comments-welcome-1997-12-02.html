Roleplay for Review, comments welcome.

2 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first roleplay for my new fed the IWC, and in fact in all of ewrestling. I would like some feedback from the more experienced rp'ers as to whats good, whats bad, and any advice that might be useful. Thanks a lot. My character is "Superstar" Simon Brock, a mat wrestler from Los Angeles, about 225lbs and 6'0 tall. "Superstar" Ricky Reeves (mentioned) comes to the ring in sparkling ring atire and sunglasses. ***BEGIN RP*** (The camera is in a typical Los Angeles wrestling gym. Its a shabby sort of place with non pained walls decorated by various posters and advert for upcoming events, many of which are several months old. There is a ring with wrestlers sparring towards the centre of the gym, 3 or 4 wrestlers on weights with their coaches supervision can be seen in the background. There are a couple of others watching videos of matches.) (As the camera is away from the ring, a deafening cry fills the building. The camera cuts to the ring just in time to see a wrestler frantically tapping out from a chicken wing cross face. The man is helped to his feet by those at ringside and everyone but the victor exists the ring. The camera focuses on that victor, "Superstar" Simon Brock, who is now standing in the squared circle.) SSB: You know people, there used to be a time where you could to a place like this and test yourself. You could find guys who were tough as nails and just loved this sport. (Pauses to look around the gym) Not anymore... You see, this sport has contracted a virus. That virus is money. The fans of today would rather see some punk come to the ring dressed as a clown or see some guy defend a title once a year using about 5 moves just because you remember him from the 70s, that’s what the so called fans of today apparently want to see, so that is what they're playing for. (Pauses to wipe his face) Well, you know that really pisses me off if you'll excuse my language. You see, I’m a natural. I don’t come to the ring in a sparkly suit and sunglasses, you get what you see, I’m a wrestler and a damn good one, no, a great one. (gestures to his opponent who is laying down with ice on his shoulder) I’m not here to get famous or to get your cheers, I’m here to wrestle, and if that makes me famous, so be it. If you cheer me then I guess your the type of fan this sport needs. If on the other hand you want to see clowns, go to the circus. If you wanna see guys in sequinned trousers and perms who claim to be "Superstars" then I guess Ricky Reeves is your man. (takes a brief time out to clench his fists and relieve some frustration) You see Reeves, I EARNED the nickname "Superstar" in the centre of the ring, what the hell have you ever done? I guess your some kind of actor or musician, I have to guess because I aint heard a damn word from you since I entered the IWC. You get in the ring with me and I’m gonna show you what it takes to earn the name of "Superstar" with regards to wrestling. You see when I lock in my chicken wing cross face you wont be able to act or sing your way out of it, you my friend will tap out. (pauses and focuses on the camera for about 3 seconds) See you in the ring ICW. (SSB exits the ring and wipes his face with a white towel. The camera once again focuses on the former opponent of SSB you is still in pain from the chicken wing cross face. The camera fades to back.) ***END RP*** Regards Simon - remove the spamblock if replying by email
