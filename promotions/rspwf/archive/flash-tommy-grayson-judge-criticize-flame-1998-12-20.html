[FLASH] Tommy Grayson - Judge, Criticize, Flame.

20 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's an RP I did for EFW. Tell me what you think of it. ========================================== [SCENE: Cameras open inside a church somewhere in Queens, New York. The period of day is around midnight and the church has a creepy, gothic feel to it. The camera slowly pans from the cross hung behind the podium to several confession booths. The camera goes into one confession booth, and we find that there is nobody in there...until the window inside opens and we see that Tommy Grayson is inside the part of the booth where the priest is usually is. Grayson's black hair is in a crewcut and he has a stubble on his face as usual. He looks up from the floor and glares at the camera with his cold, brown eyes.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: [chuckling] You know, when I was a teenager, I used to think that I could do any damn thing I wanted to...and then I'd confess to it at the end of the week to the father. But I guess I was kind of wrong when I was caught in the act, huh? In fact, it was like being damned when I wasn't allowed to watch TV or do any of that crap for a week. Hell, for one week, it felt like a crucifixion. Speaking of crucifixions, I saw Despair's little segment. I must say, that is some scary crap, Despair. Scary enough to scare The Pope. Don't even think of intimidating me, Despair, because it ain't gonna work. But I gotta say...you've got uglier scars than I do...but you know what? You don't have tattoos, man. A real tough guy's gotta have some tattoos.
[Grayson steps out of the booth, and the camera view switches to a shot of him standing there, next to the booth. He is dressed in a black motorcycle jacket, a black Motley Crue "Dr. Feelgood" t-shirt, Tommy Hilfiger jeans, and a pair of white Adidas sneakers. Grayson pulls off his jacket and tosses it on the wooden floorboards, revealing his muscular arms. He raises his left forearm towards the camera, showing off a scary-looking tattoo depicting a skull with a knife going through the top of it, and the tip coming out the bottom. On the skull, it reads in gothic-style letters "When sin lies bolder, I'll pluck out thine eye."**]
TOMMY GRAYSON: You see that, Despair? Got it back in '93, and I'm still freakin' proud of it. I'll tell you what it represents, Despair...it represents me kicking your ass around that ring on Extreme Monday Night. [chuckling] You think I'm whining about that loss last week? I don't think so, Despair. How would it feel if you got beaten through the floor by four guys while the referee is busy getting some tongue? How would you feel if the ref made the count after you got hit by twenty chairshots from four guys? How the HELL would you feel? I wouldn't know because you're such a damn freak, Despair...but I'll tell you how I feel. I'd feel pissed off, screwed over, and I'd have that intense desire to severely kick some ass. This week, Tommy Grayson gets his revenge on CoD. I'm not gonna let a group of gothic idiots dominate me. You could talk about getting crucified, you could talk about the end of the world, and all that prophetic BS. I'll tell you what, Muhammed was a prophet. A more honorable prophet than Krypt, Pariah, Despair, Kestral, or even AsH will be. A lot of people might say I'm outta my mind, but I know a bunch of idiots when I see them. CoD, like I've said time in and time out, are a bunch of idiots. Now, let's talk about the PAIR of idiots in CoD, Krypt and Pariah. I'm surprised by these guys. Hell, I can't believe that they even got close to winning the tag team titles at one time. These two spend so much time bickering and bitching to each other! Krypt is whining to Pariah, and Pariah's acting as Krypt's mommy and telling him, "you just shot yo' mouth, o' I'll slap yo' in tha' face!"
God...it's like watching Stephen King's version of Abbott and Costello featuring Gene Simmons and Eddie Vedder! I knew KISS and Pearl Jam wouldn't make a good team. Metallica and Guns N' Roses didn't! Axl totally bitched out on Montreal and caused a damn riot when he refused to perform after Hetfield over there got his ass burned. Krypt and Pariah...the two hypocrites. They call me a whiner...but they're whining, too.
Now, back to Despair...he said something that hurt my feelings. Severely, as a matter of fact. You said I was a "once-great, washed up athlete who whines about losing, and thinks back to the glory days when he still had the ability to win." That's where you're wrong, Despair. I'm not washed up. I'm still up and running. I'm not once-great...I'm once-sane. What glory days are you talking about, Despair? The Bruce Springsteen glory days? I still have the ability to win, jack. And I just might think you'll find that out on Extreme
Monday Night. You wanna see glory days when somebody still had the ability to win? Take a look at "The Boss." Good 'ol Bruce Springsteen. He had the ability to make number one hits back then...but look at him now. He's nothing but an acoustic piece of crap. Oh, glory days, how they pass us by, glory days. But you know what, Despair? Yesterdays mean nothing to me. They're nothing but old pictures that I'll always see. In other words, I think towards the future,
Despair...and I'm thinking towards destroy you and becoming the number one contender to the TV title.
You know, Despair...you reall showed me something in that little "skit" of yours. You showed me that a loin cloth is only so big. Don't be pulling that
Ted Nugent crap on me, Despair...you know that you can't match up to Nugent when it comes to Christian rock and loin cloths. As a matter of fact,
Despair...you're kind of like Marilyn Manson meets Ted Nugent. As far as I'm concerned, you're the freakiest piece of trash I've seen in my whole damn career.
[Grayson bends over and picks up his jacket. He puts it back on and begins walking down the isle towards the entrance. The camera follows him along the way.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: You see, Despair...I'm not really a religious guy. So, if you think crucifying yourself is really gonna help you beat me, so be it. I'll just slap you in the face while you're shivering from shock like Jesus did.
[Grayson gets to the entrance and turns his back to the camera. He then turns back for one last glance.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: By the way, Despair... Live if you _DARE_...die because you _MUST_... [Grayson walks out of the church. FADE] ========================================= **That's from "Bleed The Freak" by Alice In Chains, in case you might not recognize it. :) ------------------------- [name withheld] -Handler of "Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson [EFW, EWA, ICW, CWC] -The "Axl Rose" of e-wrestling -"Live if you _DARE_...die because you _MUST_..."
