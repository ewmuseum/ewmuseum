Here is my flash. Feedback is MANDATORY!!! =)

24 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Y'all, Since I just did the good old plug for my character, I will post a flash too. It's my most recent one from UEW. I'd like feedback if I could.
Good and bad. -- "Lethal Who?" ***The scene opens inside what looks to be a computer store. There are many products all around the store, and there are a few people walking around doing some shopping. The cameraman decides to take a look around the store. He begins to walk around, passing by games, modems, mouses, and many other computer gizmos. After a minute of walking around he can see a man sitting at a computer desk. The man is wearing a red and black jester's outfit, he is the Trixxster. The cameraman picks up the pace and runs up beside the Trixxster. He taps him on the shoulder, and the jester turns around.***
Trixxster: How did you people find me here? <He quickly turns around and shuts off the computer moniter. Just before it goes to black we can catch a glimpse of what looked like a drawing.>
Cameraman: President Steele has sent me to get an interview from you.
You've been awfully quiet lately, and people are getting upset! Trixxster: Yes, I understand. All the little Trixxsters out there in TV
Land want to see me on the tube. And I have dissapointed them. <Proudly sits up.> I apologize to all the little Trixxsters out there.
<Almost immediately Trixxster begins to crack up laughing.> Trixxster: Hehehe, now that that is done with. Let's get down to business shall we? Let's start with my last match on Anarchy against the
Christmas character Jack Frost. Although it wasn't the greatest performance I have put on, it was great none-the-less. And as always, the jester came out with the win! So that continues my undefeated streak here in the UEW! Hehehe, Chris Tyler, LOOK OUT! A new streak is ready to be set!
<Trixxster removes his jester's cap and places it on the computer desk behind him.> Trixxster: Now with that match with Mr.Frost behind me, I can fully concentrate on my next match. The big one! Me, and a partner of my choice, will be taking on the Lethal Leaders for the UEW TV Tag Titles!
Hehehe, everytime I think of this match, I get little goosebumps all over my body. Ever since I got this title shot back at Rumble In The
Bronx, I have been pondering some choices as to who should be my partner. But before I discuss who will be on my side of the ring, let's discuss who will be on the opposite side! The Lethal Leaders!!!
<Trixxster sits up straight in his chair and gives a funny looking glare at the camera.> Trixxster: Now, I have a question for everybody. Where the heck are those Lethal Leaders? It's something I'd really like to know! I bet
Cancer and Heatstroke are in their house right now, grabbing each other for saftey, watching this interview. You know why? It's simple. Because they fear the jester! They were praying that I wouldn't get that title shot back at RITB because they know they can't beat me! The poor lads are scared of me by myself without a parnter! Just think how scared they'll be on Halloween night when they see me with someone on my side!
<Trixxster spins around and reaches under the desk. He pulls out a plastic bag and inside it, is a mask. Actually, he holds TWO masks! And the faces on them look awfully familiar.>
Trixxster: Behold! The brand new Cancer and Heatstroke Halloween masks! Hehehe, once you put these masks on, you'll be a loser just like the actual Lethal Leaders! Take me for example. Right now, I am a funny, hip, cool, swinging jester! But.....
<Trixxster puts the Heatstroke mask on his face.> Trixxster: As soon as the mask goes on, I become a boring, sluggish, loser type of a jester.
<He then rips the Heatstroke mask off of his face and puts them both back in the bag.> Trixxster: Hehehe, Lethal, I really do hope you two come out of that little log your hiding under. Because if not, I might just have to take the titles by forfeit. And that wouldn't be too much fun. Hehe, so come to Saturday Night Fright, and bring those shiny titles too. I have many surprises in store for you, and my partner will be sure to shock you both!
<Trixxster begins to giggle a bit, but suddenly stops.> Trixxster: And speaking of my partner, well, I feel as though this is the time to tell you all who he is. Yes, this is the time! Here goes nothing. My partner is a man who will stand at my side throughout the entire match! He is a man who is capable of being a great champion with moi! He is...........
THE CAMERAMAN!!!!!!! <Trixxster begins to break out in laughter and the cameraman just shakes his head.> Trixxster: Hehehe, okay. So maybe it ISN'T the cameraman. Did you actually think I'd reveal my secret to you all? Ha! Not a chance boys and girls. But I will say that I have narrowed it down to a total of four individuals. And I will give you all a clue as to who each one is.
One man, is someone who I have stepped into the ring with before. Another is someone who is always looking for some gold. The third is someone I was flying high with. And the last is someone who is a legend here in the UEW. <Trixxster picks up his cap and places it on his head.> Trixxster: Hehehe, there you have it. Lethal, you two can ponder that for a while. But until then, remeber that you will be losing those straps come Halloween night! And on October 31st, the most common thing to do is trick or treat. And all you have to do is look at the name, and relize that October 31st is my night to shine! Hehehe, and if you two want to disagree, well........ THE JOKE'S ON YOU!!!!!!!!!!!
***Trixxster bursts out in laughter as he grabs his plastic bag and gets out of the camera's view. The cameraman then checks to make sure that
Trixxster is gone. He then sits down in the chair and flips on the computer moniter switch. We see a picture that says "FOOLED YA!".***
