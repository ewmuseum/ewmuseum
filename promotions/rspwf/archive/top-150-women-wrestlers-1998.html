Much of 1997 was spent in a long heated feud with Keiko Mita whom she had trained with years before in Japan and suffered a broken knee. Mary claimed Keiko did it on purpose also injuring several others out of jealousy. Keiko claimed it was an accident but did admit to injuring others. Mary & Keiko had been trying to form a tagteam but the knee injury ended that. Mary & Keiko eventually had their feud come to a climax when Mary stipulated in a match that if she lost she would admit that her broken knee was an accident, if Keiko lost she would publicly admit she did it on purpose. Keiko lost in a brutal match that left both injured. She lived up to her end of the bargain making a public statement of intentionally trying to injure Mary. Their feud cooled after that & both went on to other rivalries. Both publicly admitted respect for each other but nevere rekindled their once friendship. Mary had heated matches with her good friend Medusa Rage which she patterned herself after at times having Medusa act as a mentor. She also befriended Sierra and looked after her when Medusa couldn't. Mary still holds the Rages in high esteem despite distancing herself from them. Mary continued on trying to raise herself up the ladder at GDWA wrestling the likes of Patra Marchand in a short rivalry that saw her beat Patra twice. Mary was first to take on Patra who had great difficulty getting a debut match. Mary credits herself as being largely responsible for Patra leaving the GDWA as an active wrestler. Mary often offered to give novices a chance by giving them a debut match. She later in 1998 publicly announced her disdain for novices but recanted and continued to actively support novices. Early 1998, saw bitter disappointment when Mary was brutally beaten by Organized Crime and members of the Syndicate. Mary had been courted as a tag team partner by Andrea Chandler and got in the middle of a fray between Organized Crime and The Syndicate after The Syndicate started to fall apart. During a long convalescence of a month Mary turned her back on her friends and allies after no one came to her aid during the beating. She even threatened to fire her own stable of The High Flying Dolls, The Gladiatrixes and Ana Conda. They made up. Things were never the same with Medusa and Mary. Medusa & Sierra Browne were both on the same card and neither aided her claiming they didn't know what was going on. Mary had often acted as a bodyguard for the Rages. Mary's ratings dropped due to her inactivity and what she thought was a conspiracy by the GDWA to keep her from title matches jobbing her out to lesser wrestlers. She blamed Medusa for holding her back and showed her disdain by attacking her with a chair during a Darkside Show. Mary & Medusa never really started a feud, just a cold stalemate. Mary cooled down and apologized to Medusa after Medusa suffered an emotional breakdown & left the GDWA. Mary continued to hold Medusa in high esteem as well as Sierra and the Rages. Mary's next big feud came mostly with a war of words as she again gave a newbie a chance. That was Candi Bratton who like Patra Marchand couldn't get a match. Mary obliged. Mary saw something she liked about Candi, abit of herself despite Candi's vulgarity. The war of words culminated in two matches, the second being one of the wildest brawls the GDWA ever saw. The two nearly killed each other in what could only be called a spectacle. Fighting throughout the arena they used everything at their disposal, their bodies, chairs, ringposts, meat forks, popcorn machines, soda dispensers, toilet, doors. Mary won but barely. Both were taken to the hospital. Both had a new found respect for each other that continues today. Next enter Heidi Leick, another brawler Mary admired despite her annoying mannerisms and demeanor. After another long war of words, they eventually met with Mary defeating Heidi in another wild match. No where near as wild as Candi's but that would be nearly impossible without one going to the morgue. Mary had wanted an alliance with Candi & Heidi seeing how all were brawlers and extreme style wrestlers. Heidi however was a prima donna and wanted her own posse. Mary and her never got together at least yet as Heidi continued her war of words with Mary and went on to other feuds with the likes of Candi Bratton for example. Mary still holds hope the three can form some kind of alliance. We'll see in 1999. Maybe the setting for the beginning of a millenium apocalyse?! Mary finished out the year of 1998 with a goal finally reached. The GDWA World Heavyweight Title held by Black Lotus. The only title match she ever had. Mary had tried to get a match with Lotus since she joined the GDWA and after a long time finally got it as the result of an open contract. The fact that Lotus also held the title made it sweeter. Lotus had won the title from Mary's friend and idol, Medusa Rage and Mary intended to win it back in honor of Medusa. Mary lost the title in a controversial ending when she was pinned with Lotus holding her tights. Mary is far from through with Lotus and without revealing any plans at this time Mary plans on making life hell for Lotus in 1999. Mary who announced late 1997 that 1998 would be her year and not disappointing with all her ups and downs leaves 1998 still top contender, dubious fan favorite, quite an acomplishment for a heel and loving it. Mary announces again that 1999 will again be a great year for her. Look for her heating up the rings with her rough style and who knows what other skullduggery Mary has planned. We'll see! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 09 "The British Bad Girl" Lisa Drake ( EFW,NWCI ) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ (5'6", 130 lbs., London, Engalnd) (NWCI, EFW) - The British Bad Girl is new to the American wrestling scene, but has established her presence quickly...She is the younger sister of Jacob and Edward Drake, collectively known as the Privateers, and serves as their manager...But her wrestling is also top notch...Lisa combines a savvy technical game plan with some high risk aerial moves, and although arrogant, she remains methodical in her wrestling approach...Rose to championship status quickly in the NWCI, starting her American career their in May 1998, and winning the North American title two months later...The league closed down in July...She and her brothers ventured to the EFW, where she has risen to top contender status for the EFW women's title...Won a battle royal in September, outlasting many of EFW's top stars...She and her brothers have shown up in CSWA, and she is rumored to be wanting a women's division established there...The 24 year old British Bad Girl has some background wrestling in England, though she remains secretive about it...Has not lost a singles match since her debut in the United States. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 08 Sexy Spice ( EFW ) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Sexy Spice entered the EFW womens division in 1996 , She was the # 1 contender for the EFW Womans Title when the EFW and FWF merged thier womens Divisons, to form the EFWF. She came up short in that tournament,... but two months later, she was named # one contender again.. and this time.. she was on the winning end.. Securing her first Title Reign as the EFWF Women's Champion.. handing Heidi Leick her first Ever defeat. Sexy Spice joined The EFW Stable " SEX-Treme ", and has been thier Bodyguard, Valet, Manager, and Personal Friend and Escort to Sex-Treme's Stupendous Steve throughout his EFW Career.. They Accompany each other to the ring, and everywhere else it seems. Spice lost the EFW Woman's Title after a year, in a controversial loss to Casey Collins, Spice wen't on a mission to reclaim it.. At EFW's Season's Beatings, in a Triple Threat Match where she'll have to face EFW Newcomers, Neko Daisuke, and Lisa Drake. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 07 Miss Perfect ( WFWL ) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Standing just 5 feet 3.5 inches tall and weighing less than 120 pounds, Miss Perfect, Sandy to her friends, is the youngest of 5 sisters and early on she established her dominance over her sisters at home. She was practically "perfect" in everything. She was cheerleader captain, the Junior and Senior Prom Queen and President of her class. She graduated as Valedictorian and in a lighter vein was a charter member of The Future Homewreckers of America. Sandy was the first girl to wrestle on her High School team and earned the nickname, "Miss Perfect" by being 11-0 her senior year. Her eldest sister Diane (38) was her valet and slave. In the ring, Sandy loved to dominate bigger and older women, probably because of her relations with her sisters. She humiliated opponents by stripping off their suits, tying them hand and foot, then spanking them (calls it the "Perfect End".) She usually keeps the suits as souvenirs and has a wall full of souvenirs that she took from opponents in catfights during High School and over summer vacations. If an opponent is into sex stuff, she'll turn the tables and do to them what they had planned for her, but outside the ring she's not into "lesbo shit" only using it after fights on rare occasions as a humiliation tactic to an opponent who deserves humiliation. She's sexually quite straight in her (limited) experiences. Her boyfriend, Mark, was a collegiate wrestler and also her coach as well as her lover until he was murdered by Slymenstra Hymen. Sandy's still learning the professional wrestling business, but she's a quick study. Her Aunt wrestles and suggested she enroll in a training school, but Miss Perfect doesn't have time for school, she's on her way to the top and isn't going to accept any delays or detours. In late 1998 Sandy surprised everyone by winning the WFWL World Championship in a controversial fight that involved outside interference against both she and her opponent. In the end, Sandy was awarded the title, but after a couple of defenses, she lost it again in an equally controversial manner. Since then, she’s moved in with Sexy Sherry Ann, fired her sister and given up any thoughts of being world’s champion again. Now she fights solely for the fun of conquest and competition without regard to winning or losing. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 06 Tanya- ( WFWL, SWEL, IFWF )- ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Tanya AKA The Cuban Godess aka The Barefoot Bandit has only been in the game for about a year. However the things she has accomplished throughout this year are truly remarkable. Tanya has an obsession for feet and makes her opponents suck her toes after she beats them..Sometimes even if doesnt beat them. The list of people who have sucked on those toes ranges from Sherry Ann to Lil Cheetah,Tanya began her career in the IFWF where she bounced back and forth between stables. Tanya fought and beat Tina " The Pitbull" Collins and Shena and was semi succesful there in her early run. But as time went on Tanya matured and soon she got her shot. Though no one expected her to win the title Tanya became the IFWF world champion. The youngest heavyweight champion of all time by defeating Heather " the claw". Tanya lost the title soon after in part of a conspiracy plot dealing one the games worst wet spot Gayle Ashton. Ashton her self was scared to face Tanya and ran off before the rematch could ever take place. Tanya left the IFWF and went on to the WFWL. While in the WFWL Tanya quickly began her new stable. Tanya leads a stable called " THE FAMILY" Tanya started out slow in her recruiting but she started out fast in the WFWL> Tanya quickly was in the finals of the world title tournament and quickly became the WFWL world Tv champ. The Family Began to grwo and towday is up to 18 members. Tanya lost the tv title to Miss Perfect and went on a hiatus. When she got back she recruited the likes of Ecstasy, Chicita, Slymie Hymen and Kelly " The Tiger" Kanyon to be in the FAMILY! Tanya defeated 3 girls in one night by the names of Tammy Sanchez, Nyki., and XENA THE WARRIOR PRINCESS> Tanya dominated Xena and she got to have Xena as her slave for the week. But she wishes Xena had washed before coming to serve her. Tanya got engaged in a bitter feud with the federations other top stable the BODYSHOP So far Tanya and THE FAMILY have gotten the upperhand on THE Bodyshop but anything can happen. Tanya finally got another chance at the WFWL title and she won that title from Rosalina Roma on December the 13th of 1998. Tanya belives Rosalina was the toughest opponent that she ver fought and hopes Rosalina will join THE FAMILY but that seems unlikely. Tanya will go on to win many more titles. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 05 Sierra Browne ( GDWA, IEWA ) ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ Height: 5'10"1/2 Weight: 140 lbs She's won every lower title you can win in the GDWA, multiple times on most of them. "The Show" that says it all. Sierra Browne first came to wrestling under the tutelage of Medusa Rage in the OLW division of the PWA. She was a fine talent in combination with her sister, Indigo Browne. They moved on together to the GDWA where the team was extremely popular, but Sierra's ambitions as a fan-favourite singles wrestler were thwarted. She didn't succeed, but she was cheered. That irritated this proud wrestler, who kept expanding her game until she became the most versatile athlete in the GDWA. When she won she was booed. It didn't take long before she was one of the most hated wrestlers around as the 'Golden Girl.' Sierra Browne alienated everybody by becoming the first wrestler in Grand Dragon to be a two-time bi-titled champion and then blossom to be a tri-titled champion. She had the longest title reign of any wrestler in Grand Dragon at 15 months. Nobody could beat her for a belt one-on-one. She is that promotion's only Cruiserweight champion on record. And no one can forget her stunning run as Ms. Jack Johnson, a run which sank the GDWA into controversy and shame, alienating the federation with the ESWP after Sierra tried to humiliate that federation's proud champion, Princess Kallista after goading her into looking bad. Sierra Browne then became "The Show" as Cruiserweight champion she abandoned the Ms. Jack Johnson gimmick and went deep into her athleticism and ring savvy, proving to the world that gimmicks aside in the most competitive division in the GDWA she had what it took to be the best and was unbeatable when it counted. No one. Not Andrea Chandler, not Lady Tiger, not Micki Duran or Keiko Mita could put her down when the belt was on the line. She wrestles for the spotlight. She believes she is a star that is bigger than wrestling. She is the Show. She is TV. She is a human high-spot reel, but she puts on phenomenal matches which leave the fans always drooling for more and more. Sierra wants their jeers. She wants their heat. And she will stop at nothing to get it. She always bounces back from injury and always produces new styles, varies her attack and her matches. She is a true performer in the ring. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 04 Slymenstra Hymen (WFWL, IFWF, SFeWL, WeWL, FFWF ). ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ She hasn't won a title so far but she is never concerned in winning a title. Her entrance to the world was in the exciting but short lived LEWA. Her first ring appearance set a standard for deviousness and cruelty for other "heels" to follow.
