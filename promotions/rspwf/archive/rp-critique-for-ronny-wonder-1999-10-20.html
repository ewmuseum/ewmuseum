RP Critique for Ronny Wonder

20 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've never put an RP on the RSPWF, but I figure now is as good a time as any. Critique it, flame it, give constructive tips, or just don't read it at all. This was used to counter a dream RP my opponent had done. (We fade into some old hairy guy sleeping. He seems to be in a nice, relaxed dream state. You can tell because the eyes under his eyelids are moving like crazy. The camera backs up a bit to see Ronny Wonder standing over him with a notepad and pen. Standing next to Ronny is none other than Spunky Wunky and
Kim, the plastic blowup doll. She's dressed in a tube top and a very short, very tight skirt. They are both black and almost paper thin. And because Arun
J is the only person who's probably watching, and he's most likely already
"finished off" just thinking about Kim, we shall move on. But before we do, lets remember that she's just a blowup doll. The man is snoring but the three people move away from the man and into a better shot. And this is where we begin.)
Spunky: Ron, what the hell are we doing here? Why are we watching this guy sleep, this sucks.
Ronny: Spunky, you are my personal asskisser, do not question my actions, just praise me for letting you hang around me. You know there are millions of people who would love to spend all day kissing my ass, and you are that man right now. So unless you want to lose your job, I suggest you do less questioning and more kissing.
Spunk: Good idea Ronny, you are so good. I'm sure whatever reason you're here watching this guy sleep, its one that will no doubt bring the world to its knees.
Ronny: That's better. Now, we're here for a very important reason. For the past month, I've had 4 different opponents put me in a dream sequence in one of their segments. Now, I have pondered this action and decided that I would like to study how this is done. I mean, if 4 guys have done it too me, then it must be a very unoriginal, very overdone process. I just want to see how its done.
Spunky: Oh ok, I knew there had to be a reason for all this madness. I wondered why we would ever go to a dream factory.
Ronny: Right, now, I've been watching this guy sleep and I have yet to see how anyone could possible record this thing. There is only one possibility left for me to try.....go to sleep myself.
Spunky:...........My God Ronny, you are really willing to take that risk? You'd let yourself actually fall asleep. Ronny Wonder, you are truly brave. I can't imagine someone who would actually put themselves on the line like that. You are really a man of honor and a man of god. Amazing.
Ronny: Hey, that's the spirit. Good job Spunky. But I think the only way to find out how these guys get their dreams into their segments, is to actually dream myself.
(Ok, so we're fading from one picture into the next. This time its Ronny who is sleeping. He's got a whole bunch of those things attached to his head. And there is a whole bunch of movement on the medical screen behind him. Spunky and Kim are standing above Ronny.)
Spunky: Ok guys, we've attached this special thing to Ronny's head which is going to put his dream straight onto the screen. Anything you see from here on out, is Ronny Wonder's dream. Don't blame me for anything that might offend you, remember, this is Ronny, he's weird enough in real life.
(The picture of Spunky and Kim dissolves and a new picture...uh...solves? up onto the screen. Its a field filled with pretty pink roses and green leprechauns. There are giant bears dancing in unison. And a cross-dressing midget is Riverdancing, with Purple Monkey's behind him drinking Orange
Shasta soda. Right in the middle of the field is Ronny Wonder. He's got his orange mask on, as well as a Superman costume on. Instead of the big S on the chest, its a big W. He's about 2 inches taller and total muscle. And there is an enormous bulge in his pants.....hey, give the guy a break, its his dream isn't it?)
Ronny: Hey, I'm here, dreaming. This is so cool. I feel so much taller and more muscular. And of course, much....bigger in some areas. Like my brain.
What did you think I was talking about?....oh that, nah, its always like that, you just never noticed. So, we're here, lets see if I can do anything cool, alrighty?
(Ronny looks around for a few seconds trying to decide what he wants to do first in his dream. He spots a beautiful woman standing next to the Teal
Polka dotted trees. He runs over to her.)
Ronny: My God, you're Christy Taylor, remember me, I'm Ronny Wonder. Christy: Oh yeah, Ronny. Why are you wearing a mask, you were always so great looking back in high school.
Ronny: Yes I know, but why did you say you'd only make out with me in my dreams?...And why did you say it many, many times?
Christy: Because you were always so weird. But Ronny, I always wanted you so bad. So so bad.
Ronny: Well, we are in my dreams now. Perhaps this would be a good time to make due on that promise.
(Christy looks around for a second. She sees the Bear Riverdancing, she sees the Teal Polka dotted trees, she sees pink roses and the Purple Monkeys drinking Orange Shasta soda.)
Christy: You still appear to be very weird though Ronny. But ok, I guess it is just a dream.
(The two lock lips and just start going wild. Their hands are moving all over each other. I'm pretty sure this is about to get R rated....yep, just got R rated. So we'll fade to the trees for a few seconds....er, minutes. We hear the birds chirping, then some other sound that we won't go into, but they turn up the bird chirping sound to drown the other sound out. At least 15 minutes later, the camera moves back to where Ronny was standing. He still has the mask on, but the Superman costume is on backwards. The girl is gone.)
Ronny: Oh man, only in my dreams would that have happened. Jeez, I love this dream thing. What else can I do here?.....yeah, I guess I could do it again, we are in my dreams, but I don't want to bore you all. I need to find something else that could only happen in dreams.
(Behind Ronny stands a huge man. And we're talking freaking enormous. He's about 7'4, 450lbs. And he looks mean. Ronny spins around and takes a step toward the man.)
Ronny: Hey, its big Bubba Wiltrout. You were the guy who used to beat the crap out of me and take my lunch money every day in grade school. I always told you I'd beat the crap out of you.
Bubba: That's right, the only time you could ever beat the hell out of me, would be in your dreams......oh shit.
(Ronny kicks Bubba in the shin, then delivers an elbow to the back of the head. Then drops Bubba's face down onto his knee, breaking his nose. Ronny picks the huge man up and throws him down with a thunderous boom. Ronny stands on the huge man with one foot and holds his hands in the air.)
Ronny: I knew I could do it. Of course, it could only happen in my dreams. But I did it, I beat him. I won.......of course, it isn't quite as satisfying because it is just in my dreams.
(A giant pelican with a huge beak swoops down and lands next to Ronny. It drops a piece of paper down onto the ground and sits there. Ronny picks the paper up and begins to open it, then looks down at the pelican, who is still staring back at him. Ronny shakes his head and pulls a greenish, red fish out of his pocket. He throws it to the pelican who takes it and waddles away....suddenly the pelican has become a duck. Don't ask me folks, this is
Ronny's dream.)
Ronny: HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA!!! This piece of paper says I am getting a World
Title shot. That is funny, this must be a dream......hey, wait a second. I must be waking up. Damn, this was fun though. Ah well, as long as my dream doesn't fade away in black.
(The dream sequence starts to fade away, you can tell because of the wavy lines along the screen. And it fades to........white. Then we fade back to
Ronny, Spunky, and Kim. Ronny lifts his head and looks toward the camera.)
Ronny: I think this pretty much shoots J's dream thing to shit. Arun, I really hope you enjoy your dreams, because that is the only place you will ever beat me for a World Title. Welcome to reality, and in real life Ronny
Wonder wins the World Title.
(The camera fades away from the trio of tyrants. It fades to a light shade of yellow, because yellow is a happy color.)
