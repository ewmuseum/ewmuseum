Crituque My RP, "Preacher of the Ring"

22 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~{The First Ever UwF Shadow Champion}~~ ~~{The Former UwF Tripple Threat Champion}~~ ~~{The 1999 UwF Face of the Year}~~ ~~{The January 2000 wrestler of the month}~~ ~~{The January 2000 co/ match of the month winner}~~ ~~{The Preacher of Evil}~~ ~~{Evil in the form of man}~~ ~~{The Preacher of the Ring}~~ ~~(Jacknife}~~ Scene (The UwF fans in Nevada are pumped, on this cold winter's night. In the arena the fans are asthetic with excitment. Everyone is on thier feet to see the results of the current match between Gordita king and a local jobber. Finally, the well over waited match ends with Gordita's finisher, a 450 splash, as
Gordita gets the win. Then the lights go out and all is dark. There is one small strobe light over the entrance curtian. It is a bright firey red. A
Churchy hymn plays over the PA system. A small amount of fire works streak across the arena in flares of red and white. When the Fireworks settle from the curtian emerges, "The Preacher of the Ring." He stops at the peak of the stage, puts his hands on his shoulders and looks around. Jacknife then proceeds. On his way up the ramp is Gordita King. Gordita bumps into Jacknife and Jacknife stares at him as if he has just been spit at in the face. He chokes Gordita to his feet, and stares into his eyes with a grimsome stare. Gordita is grasping for air. He is tapping his neck telling Jacknife he has had enough. Jacknife carries Gordita towards the stage. He stops at the peak of the stage, were there is a 20 foot drop. Jacknife pick him up by the neck and hangs him in the air, over the edge of the stage. Jacknife looks into his eyes and speaks...
Jacknife: How Dare you test the Integrity of the Preacher! <The crowd cheers as they do for any wrestlers catch phrase> Did you look where the Fuck you were going! I will preach to you the proper f'n way to respect wrestlers as great as me! You can learn my preaching the easy way. < He points Gordita's head towards the drop> or the easy way! 1,When you see me you will treat me with proper respect. 2, You will not only get out of my way, but you will clear whomever is in my way! and 3, You will apoligize for your wrong doings!
Gordita King: My apoligies to you. It will not happen again. (Jacknife then lets go of Gordita king, and Gordita runs away in fear. Jacknife then makes his way towards the ring. He climbs through the middle ropes and recieves a microphone from the announcer. He then begins to talk...
The Preacher: Fuck everyone, and most importantly, Fuck all Authority! I hate all three of those suns of bitches! Let me start with the biggest idiot,
Kinkade. You overgrown moron. You were once called the younger version of [detail withheld], but now they call you by "The Worst Wrestler of the month." While i'm bieng named "the Wrestler of the Month." Kinkdae, you beat me for the Shadow title, which is realy a SHADOW of it's former self. Kinkade if I had your ass in the rin, i would beat it around the ring, like a stuffed doll. And
Kinkade, if you walk out with the win on Monday, my friend The Pro, wants a piece of your ass, and after he hands you your ass, i'll package it for you and stamp it as, rump roast.
(Crowd pop) Now on to Jesse Madrid. Or may i say The Rock. Now Jesse, don't even bother wasting your breath to tell me that I am like the Undertaker, cuz I heard the bull already. I do not think i ever saw the Taker curse out people like i do.
He prefers to walk around like a biker these days, so forget that thought. But Madrid, you ran into the ring after my match and stuck you head too far up Champion's ass, and now it's mine. Me and you will have our time, o so soon. Now on to Champion, now you said that i would have done the same thing, that you and zane did? Well I don't know if you noticed but, I DO not!, and i mean
Do not! like Zane Christian. I would never team with him. I have some dignity! Champion, me and you will have another match down the line. and just so you know. This Is not the END. You will be seeing me in the ring really soon!
Chris, you are nothing but a pathetic, washed up, old WannaBe! And when i get my rematch with you, You will finally understand that it's time for you to hang up your boots.
<Gives of a Smirk> TRU, you know, Who the HELL do you think you are! You act like your the god damn dali lama! Now i don't know what is up with you and BMF, but alls I know is BMF would kick your ass any day of the week, and on Sunday me and the Pro will kick yours! You and BMF need to settle thier differences and pay attention to your opponents, becuase me and the Pro have more talent than you three or two, whatever you are, will ever have in your lifetime. TRU, you need to go back to the jobber feds. Then you can be something! Now, as for you doing tha e-mail thing. That was low, but I gaurentee you that you will se lower. On
Sunday, i will pound you so far into the ground you will start growing roots! Now, you say that i don't scare you, well boys i'm not trying to scare you. When i preach my word. I Preach for meaning, not for scaring! And My word is not like your word. My Word has meaning. Now a word like yours where it means I agree, but my word that has true meaning, becuase it is preached from the the true meaning of wrestling. Then you said that i couldn't get the jobe done with
Zane and Champion so it wont get done with you idiots. Well thats wrong for three reasons! First, they have more talent than you. Second, When i said they would pay, i didn't mean short term. Their time will come. And Third, You SUCK!
Then you compare me to the Taker, well I dont know if you noticed but you are Wannabe's. You act like your black. You are just a buncha white trailer park trash! get LiveS!!!!!!!!! As for you saying you will kill me and the Pro so we can have a reunion with satan. How long did it take you to think up that one? a whole 5 minuites? And anyways, I don't preach good. and I don't preach Evil! I preach My WORD! And None others! TRU, your fate is on Sunday....
