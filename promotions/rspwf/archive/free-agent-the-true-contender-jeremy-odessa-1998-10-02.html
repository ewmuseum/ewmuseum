(Free Agent) "The True Contender" Jeremy Odessa

2 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm looking for a good booked E-Fed that uses RP to determine match outcomes and where quality counts most. Here is a sample RP and the addresses to my homepage for more info.
http://www.geocities.com/colosseum/arena/7859/odessahome.html ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ <i>(The scene opens to a dark stretch of highway about two hundred and fifty miles from Boston. On the side of the road there is a bar called
"The Ring Rust" with about fifty bikes parked infront of it. A roar is heard in the background as Odessa's bike pulls up in the parking lot. He steps off and walks into the bar. Inside, Odessa walks up to the counter and sits down...)</i>
Bartender: Want something? Odessa: No, I'm waiting for someone. <i>(A very large biker walks up from behind wearing gothic style leather and a OSWA Official Freelance T-Shirt. He sits down next to Odessa...)</i>
Gothic Biker: Hey! Hey you! You're that Jeremy Odessa guy from wrestling. Yea, I recognize you! You are going to fight Freelance and DL
Extreme this Wednesday?!?!
Odessa: Yea, are you a fan? Gothic Biker: Yep, a <b>Freelance</b> fan! Odessa: That's nice, Freelance is a nice guy. Gothic Biker: Ya know, I've got three hundred down that Freelance is going to win! I saw you wrestle last week and you were pretty good. A little too good! I'm not about to loose that kind of money, especially when the other guy is sitting across from me:-)
<i>(The biker stand up and shoves Odessa off the stool. He leans back to throw a punch but Odessa kicks his knee out from under him. Jeremy grabs him by the hair and slams his knee into the biker's face. Several more bikers step up to confront him. The scene goes to commercial as loud thumps are heard in the background...)</i>
<b>COMMERCIAL BREAK</b> <i>(The scene comes back as Odessa walks out the door of the Bar and stands next to his bike. The OSWA van pulls up next to him and a cameraman gets out...)</i>
Odessa: You're late! Cameraguy: Sorry but you've got to slow down! The van doesn't go over eighty! Odessa: I say you could tag along to get intervues but you've got to keep up! Anyway, Boston is getting closer and so is Wednesday. I have a feeling that after Wednesday the words "Triple Threat Match" are going to have a whole new meaning in Boston! I've stopped at every gym and indy card in the last seven hundred miles and I'm ready for this match! This TV Title that's sitting in my bag isn't going anywhere! I am the true TV Champion and soon i'll be two more wrestlers down and two less to go! Maybe you guys can get this belt when I win the World Title but until then, it's staying with me!
<i>(The Gothic Biker stumbles out the door and passes out...)</i> Odessa: Oh, and Flank...I hear what you've been saying about me and you'll get your turn in the ring with me! I have a long memory and I won't forget about you! Freelance, DL Extreme, don't get me wrong! I respect you guys a lot and I don't have a problem with you other than the fact that you're contending my title! Whatever I do to you in the ring, it's nothing personal, it's just business! My destiny is unfolding another turn tomorrow, prepare to make history!
By the way, the police will be here in a few minutes so you might want to leave before they get here:-)
<i>(Odessa gets back on his Harley and rides off down the highway. Sirens are heard in the background as the picture fades to black...)</i>
