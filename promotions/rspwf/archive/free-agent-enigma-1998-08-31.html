FREE AGENT-ENIGMA

31 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the biggest free agent on the market ... my new character ENIGMA!!! he is kind of a cross between Raven and Edge, but I am ORIGINAL, I won't sit him in the crowd all the while, and make him to the exact same interviews as Raven. His gimmick is a lot different anyway!
Anyway, heres a sample roleplay, with the HTML and everything... ENJOY!!!! <center><h1><font face= "Tudor">Enigma</center></h1></font><p> <font face= "arial"><i>The scene is a very busy New York City. It is raining, and the roads are <i>extremely</i> busy with cars, cabs and buses. The camera starts to pan in to the top of a block of flats, where you see a dark figure. As it draws closer, you see a man, with long, greasy, black hair, wearing jeans and a rain coat, half undone, with no
T-Shirt. He is dripping wet, and he begins to speak.</i><p>
Enigma: I have come. I have come to the mortary of destined souls. I have come to the graveyard of men with borrowed time. Their time is borrowed off my lord. The lord of all doing. The lord of all seeing. The lord of being. He is not <i>your</i> lord. No, he is far stronger than the one you follow. Your lord is no more than makebelief. Mine is real.<br>
I've been to the depths of hell and back... I've seen my lord... I know of his realness. I know of his greatness. I know of his power. I am here to do his work. I am here to take the staples of today's modern society, the degenerates that are our humanity, and send them to hell for their deeds. They have no purpose here, but to destroy what was once a promising world. I am here to see to it that all is well again.<p>
<i> He looks below the flats, to New York City with disgust and spits.</i><p> Enigma: The people of New York City: the leaders of this degenerative world. They are the originators. They serve no purpose for us. Don't you see? Don't you see how they are hurting us.... How they are hurting the world around us. I will stop them. Follow me. Follow me and my lord.<br>
<center><ul><li>My eyes look on with sadness <li>My tears begin to run <li>My heart starts to wrench <li>My hope shatters on a blood-drenched pavement.<br> And that is the beginning of the story of this Enigma... <p></font></center> <font face="Tudor">Enigma....<br></font><font face= arial"> Believe it Follow it
