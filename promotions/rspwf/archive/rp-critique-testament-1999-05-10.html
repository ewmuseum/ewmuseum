[RP CRITIQUE] Testament

10 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A new character I created. I would be greatly obliged that this would be critiqued, although it is a short one. Also, I would also be greatly obliged that if anyone wishes to Flame me that they grow the testicles to do it to my by actually E-mailing me and not posting on with this RP. This isn't a discussion... It's a request for a Critique.
[Dusk. The city grows deeper into the abyss of darkness as the sun retreats into the horizon. The camera moves over to the local airport, as we watch a man walk out of the terminal. He holds a satchel over his shoulder, wearing a long brown trenchcoat and a fedora, leaving most of his face from view. As he looks around, he hails a taxi cab down. A Yellow Cab stops and the man gets in. As the cab drives down the highway, the man in the fedora looks out the window at the gaping skylines.]
Fedora Man: Home... At last. Cabbie: So, where ya comin' from, pal? Fedora Man: Pal? You believe me to be your friend? You believe me to be anyone's friend? People like you shunned me... People like you scorned me...
People like you condemned me for being what I am. Cabbie: Hey, relax, pal. Sheesh! Some people today... Fedora Man: Some people indeed... Even as I return to my home, I am still treated in the same regard as I was when I was a child. I look different, and they turn me away.
Cabbie: Oh, you're one of the hard-luck cases, ain't ya? Hey, I know how it is. Fedora Man: Do you? Cabbie: Yeah. Kids were draggin' on me because my folks were Italian Immigrants, and I had this accent I only recently got out of. Fedora Man: So the people turned you away because you couldn't speak correctly? Truly, that is a sad story. But you ridded yourself of that accent, and now people accept you.
Cabbie: Yeah, how'd you know. Fedora Man: SHUT UP! You know NOTHING of how it goes in this world. It is classic Xenophobia. People fear what they don't know, don't understand, or just don't like. Look at you. You complain about an ACCENT? Try complaining about something that actually holds substance... something that people fail to forget. An accent is one thing... but THIS!
[The Fedora Man removes his fedora and lowers his overcoat some to reveal a HORRIBLY disfigured visage. His entire face burned from a fire, and traces of his skull seen through the charred skin. His eyes are slightly covered from the disfigurement. His entire face giving him a freakish and hate-filled look.]
Fedora Man: THIS... is unforgettable! [The Cabbie screams in terror as he looks at the horrible visage of his passenger. His tires screech as he slams his breaks to a halt. He only looks in the mirror, afraid to say anything.]
Fedora Man: Don't tell me I frighten you? That I SICKEN YOU. You don't even know me, and already your impression of me has been made crystal clear to me.
To you, I am a freak. TO EVERYONE, I am a freak. I am... because I do not look like you. Your pitiful accent means NOTHING, because that can change! I CAN'T
CHANGE! I can't because I choose not to, but you pathetic people cannot see the light in my decision. All you see is my face and you stare... you point... YOU
LAUGH! Cabbie: H... H-hey! I didn't m-mean anything.... Jus.... Ju... Just... Fedora Man: Just go? You want me to leave? YOU WANT ME TO LEAVE? I suppose I was correct. You are fightened. And what right minded simpleton wouldn't be.
Humans FEAR what they don't want to accept, so they shun it... they scorn it... THEY LAUGH AT IT! Well, soon I shall have the final laugh. Because they will never see it from one they have already dismissed... HE will never see it from one he has already dismissed. He knows me by one name... but never shall he see me as this..
Cabbie: W... Who are you? Fedora Man: I am the Walking History of Hatred... I am the Living Tribunal of Terror... I AM THE REAPER OF THE WHIRLWIND... [The Fedora Man breaks through the glass and grabs the Cabbie by the scruff of his collar. He brings him closer, and he leans into his ear, with an evil grin on his face.]
Fedora Man: [whispering] Testament... [The man, Testament, quickly exits the cab. The cabbie quickly drives off, leaving Testament in the road. He takes his satchel and looks at the horizon.]
Testament: All of those who believe they are safe, comforted by their normal lives, shall be shattered by the approaching devastation... All who see me shall tremble before by feet and bear witness to the terrors I bring... All who hear my name best be prepare to reap the Whirlwind... For Testament has arrived... and no one is safe.
[Testament begins walking down the long road. As he passes a sign that reads "New York - 102", he realizes he has a long journey ahead of him before the final conflict begins.]
