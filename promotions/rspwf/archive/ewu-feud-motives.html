FEUD MOTIVES by [name withheld] What starts feuds between people in real life? And what compels people to continue combat? The simplest way to understand is to look at the motive, and in a feud, at least one person should have one motive for starting the war - and many more should develop along the way for both parties. At the beginning though, there is usually just one person, with one motive to start things off. The other person is initially just reacting to that. Overtime, the motives behind the feud may well change. For the heel or for generally heelish motives, just look at some of the deadly sins, and other types of 'negative emotion'. Jealousy, anger, intolerance, vendetta, destructive nature and many others. For faces, the motive is often more honourable, including things such as honor of self or other, ending an evil, helping someone, being friendly competitive, being betrayed and many more. They're other interchangeable, but the point is, when starting a feud, or wanting to start a feud, pick a person and think what motive might go with it, or pick a motive, and find some way of fitting a person to it. And if you're looking for angles or storylines or other inspirations for feuds, don't feel bad about looking at the feuds of yesteryear in the real feds. As with characters, copying an idea or a concept is great and groovy as a starting point, as long as you let it evolve into it's own thing.
