[RP CRITIQUE] Osama Bin Laden: Unveiled...Part 1: Introducing The Free World

27 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(EIGHT MONTHS AGO) [In southeastern Afghanistan, 15 miles west of Kandahar, you can see the Punjab peoples rebuilding their meager houses after American troops, Northern armies, and Impoverished persons raided the rural towns surrounding the former Taliban stronghold. The camera spreads over the desert, boundless and bare, and over the heads of these people who curse as they plaster their walls onto makeshift housing...
Soon, the camera is moving faster...more people, more houses...some already built ones now.
Faster: A blur of left-to-right visual graphics until... Everything stops...on a damp, dark cave... A familiar looking tall Arabic man stalks out...looking both ways before he crosses the sand, and then high tails it...running to a get away camel as we Fade...]
(March 19th, 2003) President George W. Bush: "My fellow citizens, at this hour American and coalition forces are in the early stages of military operations to disarm Iraq, to free its people and to defend the world from grave danger."
[A tall pile of tattered rags sits next to a store window, and looking up, watches the President make his speech post first strike.]
Preisdent George W. Bush: "On my orders, coalition forces have begun striking selected targets of military importance to undermine Saddam
Hussein (news - web sites)'s ability to wage war. These are opening stages of what will be a broad and concerted campaign."
[The camera comes in from the side...and then, that's when we see something of the man behind the rags...
A tear on dark skin. As Bush continues, a man comes up from behind him...at this juncture, the teary-eyed hobo wipes away his ladrimas and approaches the white capitalist walking by him...]
Hobo: Hey lo sir, I am nut-ting but a poor man driven froom my homland. I was wonder-reenk eef you half a dime to spare...
Capitalist: Oh please...you filthy beast! Hobo: Sir, it ees only fair! (Grabs the man's luxurious suit) I am hungree, and half not eaten in!-
Capitalist (Sticking a finger in the man's face): LOOK YOU! I don't usually stop and lecture you...people...but this time, you have gone
TOO FAR! [The Capitalist tosses the man's arm off of his suit, and pushes the
Hobo against the wall.]
Capitalist: You people...you just want to take hard earned money from hard working decent folk like me! And for what!? So you can drink yourself to death? So that you can not use it to pay for all that
CHILD SUPPORT I'm sure a man of your stature and...color show.
[The man in the rags throws up his hands...his face mostly covered...except for his jaw, which looks open in fright.]
Capitalist: It's people like you who wish to destroy the VERY RIGHTS of DECENT Americans...your whole kind are nothing but BANES on society...and worse? On MY bank account! Because I have to shell out extra money for taxe-
[The Capitalist stops as the jaw turns from an open gap to a smile.] Capitalist: Oh! Oh you laugh now! But just you wait...just you wait til justice is done! Yes, when the Kingdom of God faces you when you-
<SMAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAACK!>
<Thud> [A HUMONGUS white man from behind holds a steel chair...which just a moment ago cracked against the skull of the large, wealthy white man.
The Brute looks to hit him again, but the man in rags puts out his hand...the Brute stops. The man in rags then bends over the wealthy man...and says...]
Hobo: YOU JUST GOT KNOCKED THE _ALLAH_ OUT OF YOU, MAN! [The man in rags then snaps his fingers as a signal to the huge brute to despose of the Capitalist...he does so by putting the chair under the man...and lifting the chair and the man, takes the unconscious capitalist into the alley...soon, the Brute is back with a hefty wallet being jammed into his side pocket, and the bloody steel chair.
The Brute looks proudly to his ringleader.] Brute: Poor bastard, never saw it comin' his way... Hobo: No...stoo-peed americans never do... [The Brute Smiles at the Hobo. The Hobo Smiles back.] Brute: With all of this money...do you think? Hobo: Yes...It will start soon. Brute: Wait...It? What is It, exactly? [The Hobo smiles...and then...] <FLOURISH> [Removes the rags over his face to show none other than World Rebuked
International Terrorist...OSAMA BIN LADEN! The man looks into the camera...eyes dormant and pondering...until finally he slithers from his mouth one word...]
OSAMA BIN LADEN: JIIIIIHHHHHHHAAAAAAAAAAAAAAD~! [Fade Out...for Part 1. See Part 2 when I have time.] -[name withheld] <]:*| What is OSAMA doing now, RSPWF demands? Stay tuned and find out on the next installment of...OSAMA BIN LADEN: UNVEILED!
