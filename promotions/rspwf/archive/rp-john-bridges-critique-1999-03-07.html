Heh. I am going to amuse you. As usual, I [name withheld](my real name!) have created another character. Most of you have grown to love..cuddle...and snuggle my mexican creation known as the tortilla chip lovin' guy...Jose' "1 POP, Can't
Stop" Tortilla. A little different from Jose'. "Simply X-Sexsive" John Bridges tries to slightly humour with seriousness. While his style is totally new to me, I think I did a good job. [name withheld] already fed my ego plenty...so I dont need a great boost(I'm lying!! Feed my ego!!!). Afterall...when you constantly RP as bad as me...LOL...
[Scene: A tall, medium-skin toned man, with a nice early summer tan, and blonde short hair, sits quietly, awaiting to be served at his dining table. He nervously begins tapping...rapping on the table. He takes a small sip of mineral water, to help soothe his nerves. He insists still, on looking down onto his hand, checking the time every couple of seconds.]
[The man has on a casual sweater, over a thin white shirt. Right down to his Slates, and Doc Martens, you can tell this man is an arrogant, rich, stuck-up hide. He leans back into his chair, folding one arm across his body, and placing his elbow atop it. He then starts to rub his chin. Thinking. As he does so, he notices a camera. Sweeping his attention to the camera, he lets of a disturbing frown.]
JB - Ah, all and around, far and away, the best of the best...excellence of Sexellence...Mr. Appealing himself. That is me. And...this is me...waiting for some damn service. [starts to turn his frown upside down] It's kind of funny.
These street rats around me get service faster than I. How can this be? It's something called being black-balled. And I don't appreciate it one bit.
[Walks upto a table right next to him. He picks up a bottle of the champagne on it. The couple sitting at this table, are awefully distrubed at this man's rudeness.]
JB - Imagine that. The champagne's not Korbel. Hey, pal. Mind telling me if you paid for this with a coupon? Really. Merlot? That's petty 4 dollar wine. Now.
You got this fine...perhaps beautiful danty princess across the table with you. And you think she does not deserve the best? Hang your head down in shame, and praise me now...for what I am about to do.
[The man takes out a small black book from his pant pocket. He pulls out a credit card. A Visa Platinum Club card. He snaps his fingers, and calls the waiter over to the table. Looking at the waiter's nametag, he begins to speak.]
JB - Geoffrey, please, rid this couple of that wine. Give them your best bottle in the house. I'll be paying for it. Here's the card. Charging to Mr. John Alex
Bridges. Geoffrey - Very well sir. Shall that be all. [JB claps his hands twice, and shewing away the butler, communicating with his hands.] JB - No. Your presence is no longer needed, nor desired. Now please, fetch this young amazon and her custard companion that champagne.
[Brief Pause, as he turns back to the camera.] JB - Now. I know why you are here [Referring to the cameraman] You wanted me to cut you a skit for International Championship Wrestling, no? I'm naturally assuming that you already went to the likes of James Drake, Dylan Rapture, exsetera, exsetera, exsetera..And you came to me last, and not first? That's cool. You know why? Because come this Fallout, in the mini-battle royal, last is a good thing. You want to be last man standing. If you're not the last-man, you might as well be the first man. Let's face it, no one remembers who won 2nd place in the 100 meter run at the Olympics in 1992. For just this reason, I refuse to settle for anything but the...top of the mountain.
[Rolls his fist] Success is a journy, not a destination. My competitors are just like me. New in International Championship Wrestling. Some of us are trying to buy plane tickets to this battle royal, hence thinking there success lies in a destination. Some of us...like me, are climbing the mountain, and exploring the yukon, in search of that glory.
[Brings up the name of an opponent] Dylan Rapture [Chuckles] Man. How many times are you gonna brush us with this bull sh.. about how you are still undefeated? Your rant reminds me of a babies whine. You seek attention, crave the spotlight, want the mic, and so forth. [scratches head] But I am wondering.
With someone of such little grammatical output such as yourself, how could you be proud of your literary, verbal, _AND_ motor skills? I fear for god the day the ring attendant hands you a strap, and crowns you champion of anything. For
I will truely know, what happens when pigs fly, and hell freezes over. *shudders* Yes. I know I know there are some people that will always lean toward you, and want to help you..perhaps drag you behind them, as they themselves reach glory. It should be most satisfying for you...not having to do any work as usual, slacking off, waiting to turn up in the spotlight...only to have someone eventually point out to you that your standing in the shadow...of a man _IN_ the spotlight. Not to say I like you, or even think your good at wrestling, but I've seen you wrestle under other alias's bro, and let me tell yah, your penchant for tagging along has become a little too obvious to alot of people.
[Walks over to another table. This time grabbing a fortune cookie. he crumbles it in his hand, onto the table. Taking the remains, a fortune strip in his hand.]
Perhaps superstition won't help, but it's always fun to hear the dumb things that come out of it. "You will meet destiny in the face." Heh. What a fortune.
I didn't know destiny had a face, but if I am going to meet it, I think I should dress properly...
[brief pause, as he bites his lower lip, thinking of his next words.] Ah. The man simply by the name of James Drake. James. I know it's sort of hard for you to understand. But I hear your bragging about beating me at a house show? C'mon man. Give me some credit. You,...beat me? That's...
[Begins to bend over in laughter...After several 5 second intervals of enjoyment at that notion, he begins to talk again.]
That's pretty funny. Just because you may have pulled something out of your *ss the last match, and managed to stun me with some quick moves, doesn't mean much to me. As far as I am concerned. I won that match, infact, I think I beat your all about that ring, left you so stupid, it makes me hinder if I hit you to hard. But take heed, small man, if I didn't finish the job the first time up at plate, I will complete the job the second turn at bat. Call it...a whole new ballgame?
Well, The big 10-hit, John Bridges, plans to go the distance, make for the grand slam, round his way to home, and leave the pitcher to ice his sore.
[cracks his knuckles] Yes sir. The Fourth Dimension has arrived. It makes all the women box clever, throb with glee, and want to
_BOUNCE_ _ON_ _ME_ Simply because I... ....OWN THEM,.... ....TAME THEM, and.... ....TRAIN THEM! [Takes out a small silver cigar case, as he opens it, a fine Cuban Cigar willed with the pleasures of life's goodness comes out. He slowly lights the cigar up.
Puffing on it's top-dollar flavor.]
