["Shoot" Interview] Done by me for DSWA.

29 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Give me some intelligent feedback on this one, please. :) I apologize for whatever word wrap does to this.... ==== This is totally IN-Character ==== ["Living Dead Girl" by Rob Zombie begins to play and the arena light's slowly fade. Around the entrance way a few lights begin to strobe, creating an eerie effect. Out from the back walks Eric
Van Steele, he is wearing his usual pair of black tights, that have gold trim on them. On the back of the tights it reads "Legend!". He aslo is wearing a large black elbow brace on his right arm, a brace on his knee, and a pair of gold boots, with black tassles hanging down. He has on a black t-shirt that says "Got Legend?" on it. He suddenly throws out his arms and firworks explode on either side of him, he begins to make his way down to ringside, EVS has a small limp. With him as always is Rachel, she is wearing a short black dress and a sweater top, and her curly red hair is pulled up and back. They jaw a little with a few people in the crowd, and finally get into the ring. Where they both wait.]
[EVS pulls the middle rope up and allows for Rachel to slide underneath, which the fans all seem to like. EVS then slides in as well. EVS doesn't do anything cute in the ring, instead he walks over to the other side and demands a mic. He taps the mic...]
EVS - "Cut the Music.....! [for Rick Rude]" [The music stops on command.] EVS - "Well, here we are Rach, another fed, another paycheck, yet another booker trying to f*ck over Eric Van Steele! You damn right, I am talking about Kenneth Morlock, and yes I refuse to call him by his cute little nickname, that he gave himself. Him and his spineless, hopless, and talentless Vice President Matt Allen have left the "Man of Steele" out of the Universal Title
Tournament. When are these assholes going to learn that you should put your eight BEST wrestlers in the tournament, and not just feed one guy the belt! You know who you are...and you know that without me in the tournament, they might as well just hand you the belt. But however, it's not a matter if you are trying to be 'put over' by the bookers, the final verdict always comes from the fans! You wanna push me down, you wanna try to bury me Morlock? That's fine with me, but you should know that Eric Van Steele will rip apart any of these young stallions and with send them off to the glue factory, way before their time! Morlock, I have been up and down, I have seen the top of the mountian and I have been buried underneath the bottom of the mountain, but for the past fourteen years, I have done nothing but put on the best show f*cking possible for these fans and no matter what, the cream always has a way rising to the top and bitting you in the ass. So do what you will.... I don't give a sh*t."
[The fans give him a nice pop after that part of the rant. There is a small chant of "EVS", but when EVS raises his hand, the fans quiet down.]
EVS - "You fans are in for a treat, the DSWA has thrown out it's 'best' possible card for you. Of course, for you stupid marks, it's going to be the greatest night ever. The match I am looking forward to, is of course the Freddy Floyd/Kuba Rico match.... I have seen these two before, and both men know how to work a match. I know I am not supposed to say that, but as I said before, I don't give a sh*t. As for my match, it appears that the DSWA booking staff doen't think to highly of my skills and are sticking me in with four greenhorns, to go after the third place belt. Don't believe the hype people, it's nothing more then a T.V belt. But, if I get that belt around my waist, I will make sure that I wear that belt with honor and I will take on ALL f*cking comers, new or old, if you're a 'name' or not. [EVS begins to spin around and point at people in the audience.] Why? Because you fans deserve a f*cking champion worth cheering for, you fans deserve a champion who doesn't piss and moan about defending his belt, and you fans deserve a champion that is going to put on a show that is worth a sh*t."
[Again, the fans begin a small chant of "EVS". Eric, passes the mic off to Rachel.... the chants quickly change to that of "Show your Tits". She gives a smile to the fans, and teases them by lifting up her sweater a tiny bit, but then waves them off...]
RACH - "I liked the "EVS" chants better [sticks tounge out]. As always, I have the back of EVS...
not that he needs any protection, well in the ring I mean. It's about time, that I have gotten a chance to speak about something that pisses me off, and should piss you off, if you are a smart fan. EVS, has, is, and always will be the 'Lone Wolf' of professional wrestling world. He speaks his mind, whenever and where ever he choses. But now, there is this big movement... they are called 'stables', this is the stuff that really pisses me off. A group of nobodies who team up and cause havok, well do that if you must, but EVS and myself will over come any odds. PERIOD! [A pop from the crowd.] Now, I have one last issue, backstage this April Bedilla, sitting here [points at her], was hanging all over President Kenneth Morlock. I don't know where this sister comes from, but just because you act like a tramp, don't expect to get paid like a tramp!"
[Rachel flicks off April, then passes the mic back to EVS.] EVS - "Can you fans feel it? It's in the air, and it is coming from both me and Rachel...[takes a deep breath.]...Yeap, that's the smell of victory. And tonight Eric Van "the Man of" Steele, will do what he does best and that is what I always do.... STEELE......... THE............. SHOW!!"
[EVS drops the mic, and once again "Living Dead Girl" by Rob Zombie begins to play and they both make their way out of the ring, security has to hold back some of the fans which are trying to shake the hand of Eric Van Steele. He mouths the words "I SEE YOU ALL LATER" towards the ring announcers and then some of the fans.]
