Hot Free Agent.

2 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You want me? Check me out, in this roleplay. If you like what you see, drop me a line. ::The scene opens to the GodFather's estate in Detroit, Michigan. The camera is only big enough to see half of the house. A water fountain is out front, as the water trickles into the fountain. It would be pitch black, but the lone illumination comes from the side lights of the fountain. Every few seconds a lightning bugs light up around the fountain. The camera then goes up to the porch. There are several chairs outside. Everything is perfectly clean and kept well. The camera then goes to the long driveway. A black porsche 911, a silver
98' Eclipse and a forest green Lexus jeep are sitting there. The camera then goes back to the porch. A light comes on, and the GodFather swings open the door. He has a glass of red wine in his hand, as he steps outside. Inside, you can see a spanish girl wearing a red night gown. The moon shines full in the sky, and the clouds are non-exsistant. The stars shine bright, as the GodFather looks up to the sky. The scene seems very peaceful, with the exception of the presence of the GodFather. He wears a nice white dress shirt, along with khaki pants. He closes the door behind him, and sits on a chair outside. He takes a sip of his wine, and puts it on a glass table next to the chair. He inhales deeply, and seconds later exhales. He shuts his eyes for a few seconds, opens and begins to speak::
"Sexy" Steve Sire, does this man every shut up? Just for one minute, be quiet. Listen to the sounds of real life. This man is living in a dream world. He actually thinks that he can defeat me in a match that I am famous for. Sire, you want to sit there and tell me I am not extreme? Back a few months ago, when
I came into the Motor City I defeated the "Extreme Icon" Cardiac. Not once, but twice. Supposedly, he was the most hardcore man in M-C-W. Personally, he was no challenge at all. I breezed by him, and then came upon some others who weren't on my level. I still haven't found anyone on my level for that matter, but that's another topic. Now, I find myself in a fued with you Sire. Now, I spoke with "Infamous" Taylor and he has instructed me to control my anger. Now, I got one hell of a temper if you didn't know, but his words of wisdom have calmed me down. What you said in your interview didn't bother me a bit. I think it's hillarious that you think I am not an extreme wrestler. What is coming to you, is bad things man. I would like to inform you about a few things though.
::The GodFather takes a sip of the red wine, and places it back down onto the glass table.::
Now, you talk about the past wrestlers in the 60's and 70's. Whoever said they weren't extreme? Extreme wrestling is something that's been around for several years. You say there's no such thing as "Extreme Wrestling" well be prepared to witness it first hand. I am the man of the 90's, and you damn right I will take that mentality into the next century.
Onto another topic about "Extreme Wrestling" and I don't use that term loosely. You contradicted yourself. You went off for a few minutes about how "Extreme
Wrestling" isn't real wrestling, and then you made a big deal about Abdulah The
Butcher and Sheik. The more you talk, the more people become educated about you. Your a simple homosexual idiot. I noticed how you didn't say anything about me commenting you about your sexuality. Lisa is a fucking cover up, and you know it. Your a fag in pink.
::The GodFather begins to laugh a bit. He stops laughing, and a different look comes upon his face. A look of anger. His eyes turn ice cold, and the lights go off on the porch. The only illumination is coming from the side lights of the fountain. He sits there dead silent for a few moments. Clouds begin to cover the stars, and the moon shines even brighter. The GodFather then talks again, almost screaming::
YOU WANNA TALK BOUT EXTREME PARTNA???? YOU DON'T KNOW ME LIKE DAT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! ::He lowers his voice a bit:: I've set peeps on fire. Yes, the man was blazed. Then a week ago in Vegas, the
Firm did the same thing to some wanna-be hardcore wrestler. So that's two people that crossed the Firm, and they got what is coming to them. If I get you in that figure four, it will break ya thorax. I will rearange your rib cage like a twelve gage. That's all the threats you will hear from me. I'm not going to sit here and send idle threats to you, that's pointless. You should know what's coming to you. What I am going to tell you about, is the life of the
GodFather. You want to compare accomplishments? We can do this fo' sure. I've always said "Let's not dawn on the past" but Sire it's come down to this.
::The GodFather finishes off the wine, and puts the glass down onto the glass table. The door screeches open, he spanish beauty stands in the doorway with her legs crossed over each other in her red night gown. The slip goes up her thigh, as she has zero flaws in her body. She speaks in a soft voice::
Spanish Girl: Hon, when are you coming to bed? GodFather: In a few hon, go back inside. ::The girl goes back inside as the GodFather proceeds:: I started off in APWA. I worked for several months to climb to the top, then one night I found myself in a title match. I was a rookie at the time. My stomach was in knots, I thought to myself before the match that I didn't care about my opponent. As long as I got my hands on that title. That night was a tough match for me, and you damn right I won that title. I threw Brandon "The
Hurricane" Honeyman off the top of that ladder several times that night. I held that title in the air with pride. That's my first professional wrestling accomplishment, and far from my last. I then ventured into new worlds. United
States Wrestling League. I fought in that league for an entire year. You know how many matches I lost there? Three, you can count that on one finger. I defeated a man known as the Stoner for the Global Championship Belt. From there, I held that belt for a solid 4 months. I found myself in a position where I could not wrestle for the corrupt president Atomic anymore. He was selfish, and didn't give me the respect I deserved. He threw his top competitors at me with no success. Finally, I couldn't take it anymore and I lit that belt on fire and took off. That's when I came to NWC. I was touring
Texas when I saw an add for Lone Star Wrestling. I went to a try-out and was immediatly accepted. From there, I worked my way up the rankings. Success struck again, I defeated Kiwi Justice, and became the LSW Heavyweight Champion.
In my first defense, I lost the title to "Modern Day Hero" Brad Henry. I resigned from LSW a few weeks later with more personal problems with the region head. From there I settled down in Hardkore World, a league that says it all by it's name. That was the first time, I felt the ultimate pain. Ghandi the
Butcher..Abdulah's grandson set me on fire. I did get vengeance. I set him and his partner Deez Nutz Brown on fire a week later at Palm Springs Punnishment.
That's where I got my obsession with fire. I know what it feels like to be set on fire, and it's not too pleasant. I like to inflict that pain onto my opponents. From Hardkore World, I found myself in my homestate now. Born in New
York and I now reside in Detroit. I've worked my ass off here in MCW, and a little peasant by the name of Sire isn't going to fuck up my chances. I work hard, and win matches on my own, he relies on PoA. I can do it myself, I don't need help of others. I created the Firm, I am the leader. I call the shots.
Coboslam, is only a few days away. Sire, your my feast on this night. We are the Firm, the only family that always stays on top. Come get some.
::The GodFather turns around, and walks into his home. The camera, shows the
GodFather winning APWA World Championship Belt, then the USWL Global
Championship, from there it goes to the LSW Heavyweight Championship, then to the MCW Motor City Championship. Each one, the GodFather is dripping with sweat and holding the title up high. The camera fades out::
