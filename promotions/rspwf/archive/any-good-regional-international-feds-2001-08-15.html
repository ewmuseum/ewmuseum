Any good regional/international feds?

15 August 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm looking for a good regional (ie Pacific Coast, Northeast, Florida) or international (Mexico, United Kingdom) fed to send my Japanese wrestler to. Who ever, please let me know if you know of some good ones. Fed heads, below is an app I put together for a fed that seemed to die.
Name: Eiji Yatsu Height: 5’10 Weight: 210 lbs. Hometown: Fugiwara, Japan Alignment: Face Theme Music: “Intergalactic” by the Beastie Boys Gimmick Description: Yatsu is a young Japanese man who left is native country to prove himself as a man and as a wrestler.
Wrestler's Style: A strange fusion of judo techniques, Japanese-style puroresu, and Mexican lucha libre.
Wrestler's Appearance: Eiji has a relatively compact build, like that of an NFL defensive back. His eyes are a reddish color and his black hair is done up in dreadlocks that come down to the bottom of his face. Yatsu’s bangs are braided so they don’t obstruct his view. Red rubber bands hold the dreadlocks together. In ring Yatsu wears full-length wrestling tights with a layered fire design. The rest of his attire includes black wrestling boots and kickpads that come up over his knees, and his judo black belt.
Wrestler's Background: Yatsu was somewhat of a child judo prodigy, winning several national titles in his youth, but his post-18 years have been less of a success. After being told he couldn't make it in the shootfighting leagues, Yatsu was trained in lucha libre and made a tour North America, tagging with a Mexican wrestler named El Lobo
Blanco using the name NEO. Their biggest stop was in the MCWF, an innovative promotion in the New England area of the United States.
After this, Eiji heard of a possible Jr. Heavyweight division in the new Super Hyper Visual Fighting promotion, whose dojo he soon entered.
Before he could make is debut, however, it shut down. After making quick stops in Dream Factory K, G-Pro, and several fly-by-night companies, he found a home in the Japan Puroresu Association. The match wasn't made in heaven, as Eiji joined with Ryo Inoue to help form Total Attack Puroresu. With the JPA dying, Yatsu choose to abandon ship and enter Goukai Alpha Mixed Martial Arts, but it was short lived, as the company was bought out by the Tokyo Organization of Puroresu. Yatsu was named one of the rising stars of puroresu and was offered a shot at the TOP Jr. Heavyweight Title. However, Yatsu didn't believe he was ready for it and decided to set out on a challenge to prove his ability to the fans and to himself.
List of Signature Holds: 1. jujigatame - cross arm-breaker 2. hizajujigatem - crucifix kneebar 3. wakigatame – Fugiwara armbar 4. keylock 5. stiff, swift kicks 6. Wanizame Bomb ’98 – Death Valley Bomb swung over into a Northern Lights Bomb 7. Muscular Clutch – a standing, hands-free Indian deathlock while striking a muscular pose 8. La Majistral 9. dropkick 10. Sky Striker – leg lariat impacting with the knee to the head Finisher Set-Up: A pair a knee strikes to the temple Finisher: Yatsu Special – Eiji delivers a frankensteiner and then locks on a Triangle Choke submission.
Sample Roleplay (required): [The camera fades into Eiji Yatsu standing in front of a white wall. The shot is from the waist up, only showing him in his white GAMMA t-shirt and red headband. He has a look of cool confidence on his face. He starts with his arms crossed in front of his body, but as he begins to speak, he unfolds them.]
Yatsu: Again I come to TOP, this time a little more humble… just a little. At the last GAMMA rally, Suzuki, you were able to knock me out. I won’t claim it was a fluke to try to keep my pride; you beat me fairly and convincingly. You showed why you are without a doubt the best junior heavyweight in Japan. Keiji Kobe and Tasogare no
Prince have seemed to drop off the face of the earth, that leaves only us in the real contention. Ikeda, he’s become a joke.
Uninspired, it’s amazing he made it as far as he did in the TWMC. Johnson is just another gaijin pretender that can’t live up the high standards you’ve set, and I’m coming so very close to match. No disrespect to your prodigy, Jet Jaguar II, but he’s no near the level you were at that age. Nagoyaka, he seems to be a very solid competitor, and I look forward to fighting against him. Kimura is another solid combatant, and I wouldn’t be surprised to one day see him holding the TOP Jr. Heavyweight title.
But that is tomorrow; Suzuki, you and I are today. We are without a doubt the best there is to offer. The fans are screaming for another contest between us. I plead to Fujiwara to allow me to face Suzuki for the TOP Jr. Heavyweight title. We each have a win over the other, and it must be determined who the better is. What better way is there to do is?
[He pauses for a second.] I’ve already made mention of the combatants that are involved in the contest at TOP’s Fan Appreciation Day. Ikeda, I’m telling you now, I’m going to be after you. You were lucky you had Suzuki on your side at the last GAMMA show otherwise you’d been destroyed by Kubo and I. GAMMA has seemed like an afterthought to you. I want to give a reality check. GAMMA, like Kubo and I, are building the future. You aren’t part of that, so you are part of the past we must erase, to be replaced by a stronger, better future.
Suzuki owns today, but I hold the deed on tomorrow. [The camera fades out.]
