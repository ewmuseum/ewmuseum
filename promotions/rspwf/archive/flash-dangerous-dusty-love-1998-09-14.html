[FLASH] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE

14 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene fades in and we see a very colorful and busy street. People are walking on the sidewalks, talking and laughing. Very few cars drive down the road, but the parking lots of the nightclubs are jam packed. This is Beale
Street of "Down Town" Memphis, TN. On one side of the street we see flashy lights on a huge sign above a building. A picture of Elvis Presley is on the sign and in flashy letters, the words, "Elvis Presley's Club" is written. Next to that building, we see Rum Ball. Yet, another dance club for the partiers out there. Then the camera focuses across the street where we see women walking down the sidewalk with their boyfriends or husbands. Music is heard very faintly coming from each of the clubs. The camera scans over the people and we see a dance club by the name of Alfred's. The pink flamingo on the walls of the building is seen as the camera moves on a focuses on the entrance.
A few seconds pass and we see a very handsome man walk out of the club with a woman on each arm. The lady on the left is a very attractive blonde. She is wearing a black mini-skirt with black heels. She has a nice body and apparently is having a very good time by the look on her face. On the right of the man, we see a dark-brown headed lady. Again, a beautiful woman wearing a white mini-skirt. She has a nice body as well. And the man is wearing black dress pants, a tight white shirt, and over that a black tight vest. Very nicely dressed. He has a Rolex watch on his left hand and a gold necklace with an attachment on it. The attachment is the initials, "DL" filled in with diamonds. He has his long, dirty-blonde, hair pulled back in a pony tail, a whisker-beard, and a pair of circular lense sunglasses covering his eyes. The camera then zooms in on the man, and he is recognized as "Dangerous" Dusty
Love. He looks at the two women and says something to them and they all begin to laugh. Dusty Love see's the camera and begins to speak.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Well, well, well... Looks like ya caught me, again, in the middle of another one of my hard workouts.
[Dusty and the girls, again, begin to laugh. They calm down and Dusty begins to speak again.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: If you ladies would excuse me for a few moments... I have a little bit of business to take care of.
[Dusty Love, then, kisses both of the beautiful women on the cheek and he begins to walk with the camera man. He begins to speak.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: I'm actually glad that you got a hold of me, because
I have quite a bit to talk about right now. Starting with what went down on
Friday Night Fallout just yesterday. All I have to say about Fallout is this -
It's behind me now... And I'm movin' on to better things. Shane Diamond,
Westside Walavan, "Pretty Boy" Andrew, and D.B. Bugg just better be happy that they came outta that match _ALIVE_, more or less, the winners. The Supreme
Dream Team pulled that win out of nothin' but pure _LUCK!_ And I assure you that you'll _NEVER_ see me on the losing end of a match again. And you won't see any member of the Perfection Connection lose a match again either. When you're this good, you don't lose... And I'm about to start provin' that point all over again. Starting with Friday Night Fallout. The 30 man battle royal... That's where I'll start my rise to the top. The winner will get a shot at the International Champion, Tony Paseno... My best friend.
[Dusty Love stops walking and looks into the camera with a smirk on his face. He continues on...] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: I take a look at the list of wrestlers that'll be participating in this thing... And some of 'em I know... And some of 'em I don't know. But to tell you the truth... I don't really _CARE_ to know 'em.
But the fact of the matter is this - When the smoke clears and it's all said and done... Everyone of 'em _WILL_ know me! I'm the man that's gonna be standing tall at the end... I'm the man that's gonna dominate the _ENTIRE_ time... And most of all, I'm the man that's gonna personally send each and every man in that ring, over the top and to the floor... Leaving me the winner... Like _ALWAYS!_ For a normal man, winnin' this battle royal would be a challenge... But to me, it's just another match. I mean, damn... Just look at me! I'm young... I'm fast... I'm the _BEST_... And I can go all night long! And if you don't believe that, then just ask those women over there.
[Dusty Love, then, smiles a smile of conceit. He looks in the direction of the women and waves. They wave back and he looks back into the camera and continues on...]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Now, when I heard this thing was gonna go down... I took a look at the list of competitors that'll be in it. And I noticed a few new names in there. And when I think about those names, the first one that pops in mind is a man that calls himself "Mr. Main Event"... Matty dee. What a nickname, eh? If ya ask me, I find it funny. Considering, I've _NEVER_ seen him headline any event. Matty, I don't know why you're even wasting your time in the ICW. You know as well as I do that you can't hang with _HALF_ the men here. You don't even belong in the ring with the rest of 'em... And you sure as hell don't belong in the ring with me. Remember the UHW days? I know you do because those were the _WORST_ days of your life. I had the World
Championship... Ring a bell? The same World Championship that you couldn't get from me if I _GAVE_ it to ya! Matty... You couldn't beat me back then, so you wouldn't stand a chance now. Times change... People change. You might've gotten a little bit better since then... But Matty, I've gotten a _LOT_ better since then! So, if you happen to step in my way during this battle royal...
You better make sure that you have a good dentist to put those teeth back in,
'cause I'ma knock 'em out.
[Dusty Love rubs his chin and smiles again. He begins to speak again.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Another name that sticks in my mind is Shawn Walsh. Walsh, you show up on Fallout and you send challenges in every direction... Especially in the direction of "Big" Tony Paseno. You want some of him? Well, he's a very good friend of mine... And if you want him, then you gotta go through _ME!_ And personally... I don't think you can. You're not big enough... You're not bad enough... And you certainly ain't _GOOD_ enough.
I'm not even gonna refer to you as "Da Greatest Ever", because that's something that you _AIN'T!_ I'll tell ya that right off the bat. And after Friday night, you'll know who the greatest is... And you're lookin' at him. When you threaten a member of the Perfection Connection... You threaten _ALL_ of us.
And when you do that... You _DON'T_ get away with it. And you'll find that out first hand on Friday, when I steam roll right over ya. The next guy I wanna talk a little bit about is a guy that I've seen before... The Immortal
One. I only have one out-look on him - He's a _NOTHING!_ That's right...
Immortal One... You're a complete nothing. That's the way it is. You couldn't beat me on your best day and on my worst. Someone needs to strap this guy up and put him in a padded cell. That's what I think. But when I see you in the battle royal, I might just knock some sense into ya. You just better hope you stay outta "The Love Man's" way... Or this foot right here [Dusty points to his right foot] will kick you right in that big head of yours. And that's a promise, pal.
[Dusty Love begins to walk slowly again as he continues speaking.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: Ah... Another man that I must send a quick message out to is none other than Tommy "Lord" Lionheart. I just wanna let you know that I'll _DEFINANTLY_ be seein' you in this thing. When you beat me last week... That was the biggest _FLUKE_ I've _EVER_ seen! And I'll make sure to show you what I'm really about. If you ask me... You never really beat me.
You didn't pin me, and you sure as hell didn't make me give up. Looks to me like you _PAID_ that guy to attack you with the chair. But either way...
You'll get yours on Friday night. I'm gonna deliver your ass, personally, to the floor of the arena... Count on it! And I also see the ICW North American
Champion in this thing. Ya know... I've been waiting and waiting to finally get in the ring with you, Jack Vars. I've seen you beat people up... I've seen you run your mouth... But I've never actually gotten in the ring with you. The closest I came, was back in the LeWC. And I must say, you're _VERY_ lucky that the federation closed before I could get my hands on you. But this
Friday night, I'll see ya there. And I'm lookin' forward to tradin' punches with ya. But I'll tell ya this... I'll like it a whole helluva lot more than you will. Seein' how you'll be on the _RECEIVING_ end of the punches!
[Dusty Love turns around and looks to the women, who seem to be getting restless. He smiles, revealing his perfect set of pearly whites. They smile back and wave. Dusty Love raises up one finger, as if he's telling them to wait a minute. He, then, begins to speak again.]
"DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: I'm about to have to jet, man. But before I go... I just _HAVE_ to address a man that came out on national television last night and insulted me... Insulted my friends... And mostly, he challenged me. You know who I'm talkin' about... Arthur Essex. Essex... You come out here and you say that I avoided you?
[Dusty lets out a grin and laughs a little bit before continuing on.] "DANGEROUS" DUSTY LOVE: I avoided you, eh? Is that what you think? Since you don't understand why I didn't give you your shot... I guess I'm gonna have to tell you. Listen up and listen up good... The reason that you never got a shot at _MY_ World Heavyweight Championship, is because you weren't good enough... You didn't deserve it... And most of all, you wouldn't have stood a
_CHANCE_ against me! And it's the same to this very day. If you were to get in the ring with me... You wouldn't stand a chance. You know it... I know it... And the fans know. You bad mouth me on television and expect me not to respond? You must not know me very well. You don't talk to me that way...
You talk about me that way... And most of all, you don't challenge me! But you did... And look what ya got. You got me in the ring at ShockFest. Ya know what they say - Watch what you ask for, ya just might get it. And Mr.
Essex... You got something that you _DIDN'T_ want... And that's me in the ring. But the best part about it is, I get my hands on you before October 4th.
I get you on Friday night as well. But when Fallout is done with... There'll be a question to if you can even compete on the fourth. Know why? You know...
But just in case you didn't, I'll let ya know. Because, Arthur Essex, "The
Love Man" is coming to Fallout to...
"...LOVE... ...YOU... ...TO... ...DEATH!!!" [Dusty Love grins and walks in the direction of the two women. He greets them both with a hug and a kiss. They walk down the sidewalk as the camera fades...
To... Black.]
