24 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In.] [There he is--Ryan Delaney, standing outside his family cabin, which lies in the middle of nowhere in case that isn't apparent to the masses. Trees surround Delaney as he stands there, shirtless and holding a nice looking compound bow. He pulls up an arrow, aims, and fires. As his fingers releases their grip, "It Makes them Disappear" by Pantera rises out of the background, softly at first, and then at full volume.]
##_ The time had finally come to kill the vein. ##_ And I'm dropping down the dirt on your grave ##_ It might hurt so bad, but I can't feel sad ##_ Rid of a fool like you [As Delaney loads another arrow, the camera pans away from the deranged soul, and towards his target. The target? A old, soiled mattress leaned hot dog style on a oak tree. But it is what's on the mattress that is quite strange. It's three pictures--Steele, Roadkill, and Diamond.
##_ The wrath has just begun ##_ The time has finally come ##_ And it hurts so bad, and it drives me mad ##_ Life with a blinded view (of you) [Steele's smiling face is full of arrows. Same goes with Roadkill's, even though this monster doesn't really smile, and his face is never seen. Another arrow pierces the mattress, landing right in Diamond's left cheek. Crunching the fallen leaves, Delaney walks over towards the pictures, smiling ear to ear.
The camera fades from this very odd, alcohol induced scene to a more gruesome one.] ##_ - Gone - [The new scene takes the audience back to 'Acts of Malice. There is Corso, grinning at Sheilah heading to Steele's corner, claiming Steele's own chain as his own. Corso moves quickly, wrapping Steele with his own chain, and tossing him like a feather over the ropes, hanging him just like Steele did to him. That silly boy Roadkill Rick attempts a storming of the ring, but Delaney is holding the big Texan at bay with his bottle of Vodka and prized Zippo. Fire shoots every time Roadkill gets close, as Delaney smiles as if he's in paradise.]
##_ So the coward has up and run to be saved ##_ But with each step taken down, you sink in your grave ##_ It might hurt so bad, but I can't feel sad [The camera zooms in on Steele's face, as it turns from flesh to a plum. The words, "RIP - Mike Steele and Co." fade onto the screen.]
##_ Rid of a fool like you [Fade Out.]
