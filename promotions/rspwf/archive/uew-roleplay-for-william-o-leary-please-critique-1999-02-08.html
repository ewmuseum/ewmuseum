[UEW] Roleplay for William O'Leary {Please Critique}

8 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Subject: Now that Fight to the Finish is over... what's next For William
O'Leary? [Scene takes place as William O'Leary is seen sitting at a bar. The bar is a long table with barstools sitting on the outside. The table's top is made of a metallic blue tile. The grout, which holds the tiles together, has been turned from its original color of white to a dark and grungy brown color.
Inside the bar, the back wall is lined with three refrigerators along with a large ice chest. All four of which are full of beers and possibly a few soft drinks such as a Coca Cola or Dr. Pepper. The refrigerator on the left is a light brown color. It looks old and is making a soft "hmmm." The refrigerator in the center is bright white and looks new - possibly bought this week. The refrigerator on the right looks almost exactly like the first
- light brown and old. As the camera continues to move along, we reach the ice chest. The ice chest is old and dark blue with a white top. Also behind the bar are three men who are serving drinks.
One of the men is dressed in a pair of blue jean shorts and a black tee shirt. He has long blonde hair down to his shoulders and no facial hair. The second of the men is wearing blue jeans, a tee shirt, and a Denver Broncos hat. The third of the men is wearing a pair of khaki pants and a Tommy shirt. He has a shaved head and a black goatee.
William O'Leary is dressed in his usual. He is wearing a pair of black Nike tennis shoes. The shoes have a white Nike check on the outside of them;
however, the white check is more of a brown. The bottom of the shoes are covered with hardened mud. William O'Leary is also wearing a pair of faded blue jean shorts. The shorts look like they were originally indigo, but are now almost white. His shirt, a white tee shirt, has dirt spots widely spread across his shirt and a mustard stain on the right.
As the camera settles on William O'Leary, music can be heard. A man can be heard saying the last few words of a song. The camera settles on William
O'Leary and he begins to speak. He sounds slightly depressed.]
William O'Leary: Well, I must say that ***Saturday Night Special** * was a great card. Lets have a run-
Down of the card. We saw "Machine" Chris K defeat "The Handler of some unknown Substance" Liam Rogan and "The Dick Master" Nicolas Christian for the Television Title, The Explosion defeated Perfectly Perfect
Duo for the UEW Ultimate tag Titles, Retain his American Title, Alex Extreme win
Fight to the Finish, and "Ladykiller" Chris Tyler defeat "Top Dog" Rick Styles for the most prestigous title, the UEW Ultimate Title. [A man on the stage behind him says a few words.] Man: Lenny Kravitz is alive! I'll be back in about ten or fifteen minutes with my hit "Fly Away!"
William O'Leary: Damnit! I'm trying to talk here! Well, it looks like I have about fifteen minutes before I
Get interrupted __AGAIN__! Well, as I was saying, ***Saturday Night Special*** was a Huge success. Believe it or not, even for me. Yes, I know I lost Fight to the Finish, but I Did damn good for a rookie and a first timer
Last night had to be one of the best of my life.
I mean, I don't even remember who threw Me out of Fight to the Finish last night! One, because the guy that threw me out threw me On my head, and two, because I got __TOTALLY__ wasted. I've been saving money Since Fight to the Finish was announced so I could get drunk. As for the guy who threw Me out, good job! To my knowledge, by what fans told me, I came in around number Twenty-four and was thrown out around number twenty. I mean, hell! I was in longer Than Prince Ju Ju, Johnny "I wish I was Sexy" Bod, and Shimmer Glitz! As for the rest of the winners at ***Saturday
Night Special***, congratulations. [One of the three men serving drinks walks up to William O'Leary.] Man: Hello Mr. O'Leary. It looks like all those drinks last night didn't give you a __MEGA__ hangover like
I expected. [The man smiles.] Man: So, can I get you anyhting? Beer? William O'Leary: No thanks. My blood is still tingling from all the beers I had last night.
Man: OK. Just call me if you change your mind. William O'Leary: OK. Thanks Well, as I said earlier, I was in Fight to the
Finish longer than Prince Ju Ju, Bodily Fluid
Man, and Shimmer Glitz. That brings me to my next question… who can be my master? Who can I serve? Well, I Was shooting for Shimmer Glitz, but now, I am kinda doubtful about that one. I mean, I Am the suckiest piece of [BLEEP!] in the world, and I was in Fight to the Finish longer Than Shimmer Glitz. That right there almost definitely cancels him out. That is the Most important quality masters must have, dominance. I mean, how many masters do you See that have servants that are better than them? In my twenty-four years on the face of
This earth, I have seen none.
Well, since it is doubtful for Shimmer Glitz to be my servant now, it looks like I have to Find a new master to serve. I am really getting tired of looking for people to serve, but I Have to do it. It's my job to serve!
So, I have to narrow my choices down to the top two Or three… __ONCE AGAIN__! Right now, in first would probably have to be "Black Knight" Alex
Martinez. I would take him because you can always use someone that Knows how to polish a belt don't you Martinez? Well, believe it or not, I am the person To do it! In the number two spot, I would have to go with CandyMan simply because he Hates Judife just like me. He may not be a "serving" type of person, but he would work For me. In the number three spot, I would have to go with… well… there isn't a number Three person. At least not now.
[Suddenly, a man walks on stage and talks.] Man: Lenny Kravitz is back! Here it is! The one… the only…. Fly Away!!! [The crowd cheers like crazy.] William O'Leary: Well, looks like I better be going. It's getting kind of late, and this jackass is going to be
Singing. He needs to "fly away!" [William O'Leary smiles. He stands up and pulls up his blue jean shorts. He walks over past the bar to the door, pushes it open, and exits. As he exits, he smiles and waves.]
