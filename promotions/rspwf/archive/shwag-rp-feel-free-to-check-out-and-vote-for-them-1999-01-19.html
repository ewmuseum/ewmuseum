[Smooth and Buff are inside a limo. Buff has a black eye, and Smooth has a bandage on his face. The two men look pissed off, and begin to drink some of the beer that they have in the limo. Labatt Blue for Smooth and Buff is drinking some After Shock Liquer. Smooth looks into the camera with a stern face.]
Smooth Meloy- West Coast Connection back from the ghetto? [Pauses.] Didn't we chase your asses out of the IWC awhole ago? [Nods "yes"] Then why do you want another ass kicking courtesy of Shwag? Are you two homeboys that stupid to but your ass on the line against the tag team champions of IWC?
[Buff begins to laugh.] Buff Adams- [Smiling.] West Coast Conenction.....Mac Daddy and 8~Ball! What a lousy excuse for talent! We proved to you gangsters that Shwag is far more supiouer than your gehtto style fighting. If you guys want to throw down for round two then Smooth and I are more than willing to kick your asses. [Pauses with a smile.] To bad that you guys aren't the number one contenders though huh? [Laughs.] I guess who two nimrods have to talk a number of "Shwag's Shit
List". It looks like we have a date with two gothic freaks...The Nightmare Men?
[Both men begin to laugh.] Smooth Meloy- Have the gimmicks got that bad as of late here in the IWC? For crying out loud we don't want some blood sucking vampires in the same ring as us! No way, I think I have to call up Hardcore Carey and tell him to talk to
Jimmy because I don't want to get HIV ir AIDS from these blood sucking bandits!
Hell No! [Smooth picks up the telephone and begins to dail a number.] Buff Adams- It bad enough we are going to get stuck into the ring with you freaks, but as soon as we take your asses to school then we have to take those two fake street bums to school too! [Rolls his eyes.] You can't teach those kinds anything at all! I mean they can't even speak our language. I'm not racist at all, but I have no respect for those nimrods that can't even talk proper. I might have smoked way to much pot in my 21 years of living, but I can still write and talk this beautiful language here in America. I try to watch your interviews that you conduct and I can't tell a god saken word that came out of your mouth.
[Looks over to Smooth who is talking on the phone with someone.] Buff Adams- I don't know who is harder to understand. TTP or you to morons. That's it! At the PPV I think TTP should take you to two thugs on in a handicap match. That's it...[Looks over to Smooth.] If you are talking to Jimmy tell him
I have a wonderful idea. [Smooth shakes his head "no".] Bummer! Well he is my idea. This could be an awesome gimmick match here in the IWC. Let's make it at a PPV. TTP vs West Coast Connection in a handicap match. What are the rules?
[Laughs.] It's actually an easy match. The first person to make a complete sentence that makes sense wins the match!
[Smooth begins to laugh as he hangs up the phone.] Smooth Meloy- [Mocking TTP] Well, ye all seez I'z notz thez thoughest fart in thez IWC. I'z gotten to ole to wrassle thez youngsters here anymore. I best retire thez ole rotten stanky wrasslin bootz. I ain't young anymore.
[Buff begins to laugh.] Buff Adams- [Mocking 8Ball or Mac Daddy.] Yo, yo, yo! Peep tis nigga! I be runnin dis game like no tomorow foe! We be like tag team chumps. Word is born homey. Yo, if you ain't down wit my shit i'll blast yo ass wit my nine. Pop, pop...fool you are down. Where me chronic at boy?
[Smooth is laughing so hard he begins to cry.] Smooth Meloy- [Laughing.] Stop that shit! I'm going to die! Stop it. [Smooth can't stop laughing.] Buff Adams- Catch your breath bro cause we are going to hit this bong as soon as Carey comes over here. I mean not Hardcore Carey since he's a gentlemen!
That other Carey guy. Shit you know who I mean. Let's talk about those losers
The Nightmare Men one more time. [Pauses.] Steve "Don't Call Me Twisted Sister"
Albini and his gothic partner in crime David "Mom I'm Sorry I Wear Dresses" Yow. [Laughs.] Is that Chinese? [Smooth shrugs his shoulders.] Buff Adams- Great! We have China and Africa after us now! That's just great! Maybe we'll have to join up with The Militia after all and borrow the keys to that tank that they ride around in. Then we could blow up Afica and China and no one would ever cause us havok again here in the IWC!
[Buff takes hit from his pipe.] Smooth Meloy- Save that shit for when Carey comes fool! [Yells.] I mean it! [Looks back to the camera.] Nightmare Men we have something that every tag team here in the IWC is gunning for. We hold the tag team titles around our perfect bodies. I guess you two boners have worked your way to the number one contenders for our gold. That is something to be proud of seeing how weak the compatition is here in the IWC. I guess now that Jimmy has signed The West
Coast Connection again businiess is going to blast off huh? [Shakes his head
"no".] I don't think so Jimmy. It looks like Shwag will reprsent the IWC tag team for at least another year to come.
[Takes a hit when Buff passes it to him.] Buff Adams- [Coughing.] That's some killer shit Smooth! Damn! [Coughing again.] Oh shit that's some killer bud. Damn, you know where the hook ups are at man. Let's keep this real to the IWC though. I mean West Coast Connection, The Metal
Headz, Team Xtreme, and The Nightmare Men. It looks like Shwag has a busy schedule ahead of us in the next couple months. Don't look for us to drop these titles anytime soon.....
Put.... That....... In.......... Your....... Pipe........ And....... SMOKE IT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [The scene ends with Buff and Smooth passing the bowl back and forth to each other. Then the doorbell rings and Buff walks over to answer it. The scene fades to black as soon as we are about to find out who is at the door.]
