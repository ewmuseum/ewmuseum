[Flash] "Black Flagg" Vincent Lockheart <I'm bored :P>

24 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well it's been awhile since I've posted a flash. .and I was bored so I figured what the hell...
PJ: Wait...something's going on in the back. SL: Something...what? PJ: I don't know, let's find out. [Cut abruptly to the backstage area. A few ACWA wrestlers walk by as the camera moves about..]
SL: Again, what? PJ: Again, I DON'T KNOW! [We then hear the sound of a door slowly creaking. The camera swings around the source of the sound as we see a doorway slightly ajar. The camera moves in on it as we see a dark room with only a slightly glint of light inside of it. The camera moves in slowly as we see the back of a familiar figure standing at the other side of the room. The doorway opens more and the camera moves into the room as the figure moves causing the camera suddenly stop moving..]
SL: Creepy. [The camera now in more as we see what was quite obvious and that is that the man standing there is none other than Vincent Lockheart.
Lockheart glances over his shoulder, the dim light of candles on the table in front of him shining off of him. The look of some kind of..face paint on Lockheart is seen but we are unable get a good look at it. The camera pans down slightly as we see Lockheart's hands moving in specific motion. The camera moves in a bit closer as Lockheart turns his attention back what he is doing..]
SL: Ohhh boy. I'm not so hot on this. [Just then Vincent lifts his hand slightly then drives it down as we hear the sound of something being stabbed into the table. Lockheart once again lightly looks over his shoulder..]
"BLACK FLAGG" VINCENT LOCKHEART - Steve Starr, it almost seemed like you were different from all the others. .But in the end even you couldn't handle the carnage...
[Lockheart chuckles..the sound of a maniac in his voice..] VINCENT LOCKHEART - The referee couldn't handle it..I expected that, but you Starr. You decided be the hero, save a man whom is your enemy. .Well
Starr you were of no interest me, but you stepped onto the battlefield and now you're going wind up as nothing more than another casualty...
PJ: Starr. He wants Starr!? SL: Oh man! Oh man! Where's Shakes!? VINCENT LOCKHEART - And The Canadian Kid you walked out of the ring with your title, but let me ask you..
[Lockheart turns around, finally facing the camera, his black hair down over his eyes as usual, but matted down more than usual..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - Was it worth it? Is the title worth the suffering you endured? You hit me with everything you could...
[Lockheart pauses and takes a deep breath..] VINCENT LOCKHEART - Everything you had. But the fact of the matter is you may not fear the reaper..
[A devilish grin pierces the lips of Lockheart as he steps forward into what little light there is as we see dried up blood on his face and in his hair matting it down...]
PJ: Uhhh... SL: Okay, I'm not happy with this at all. VINCENT LOCKHEART - But you can not beat the reaper. Canadian Kid, you have your title.. [Lockheart raises his hand his head and slowly moves it down his hair and face..] VINCENT LOCKHEART - But I have your blood... [Suddenly, a great deal of mixed heat is heard from the arena. Lockheart once again turns away from the camera and picks something up from the table and goes back what he was doing moments ago..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - McMullen, for you I have but one question ask of you. You preach your ways the ACWA. The ways of tradition..But I have ask you McMullen, when it comes it do you have it in you..
[Lockheart slowly turns around the camera once more, a dagger gripped in his right hand. He then takes his left hand and grips the blade with it then slowly pulls the blade down, cutting the palm of his hand...]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - be a Martyr.. [Lockheart lifts his left hand up as he closes it tightly and blood drips out of it onto the floor..]
VINCENT LOCKHEART - ...for your beliefs.. [Lockheart stands there for a couple seconds before turning the table behind him and jamming the dagger into the wood. He then turns and walks off camera as the camera moves in on the table as we see various daggers sticking into the wood. The camera zooms in on the table more as we see each dagger has been stuck into the center of a mans name ranging from
The Canadian Kid Bulldog McMullen and Steve Starr. The camera continues it's zoom then fades black..]
