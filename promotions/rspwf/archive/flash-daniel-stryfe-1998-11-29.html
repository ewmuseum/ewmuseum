[FLASH] Daniel Stryfe

29 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is my most recent RP for the UCHW. An _AOL_ fed! So it is long. I only repeated myself once by accident. :) Other than that, all in all, a good RP.
Feel free to flame me. But be gentle. :( He-He. I expect FireofFury to come out and say something gay about it, I can't wait to see what. ::: chuckles :::
Anyway, here ya go. Do you know me? [xXx] ... Do you all really think you know who I am? Do you all actually think I am this dark character? Do you think my life really is the way I say it was? Was my father a drunk, who gave me such good advise all the time? Did my mother really have a stroke and die? What about everything else? Where is my brother? Well, it is all true. See, no one knows who I am, you may think you do, but you don't. Daniel Stryfe, is exactly who he says he is. My life was like that. I did not live in a ten story mansion, with everything I wanted. I lived in a shake, and had to work all night and go to school all day. And ended being nothing, until one day I was picked, and from there I had become a wrestler. A wrestler, with an attitude that fit my life. And that was "Certain Death" For all my life is, was and always will be, is Death. And now, to take out my anger from life, I wrestle. And every man who steps into the ring with me, knows that, and they will never want to face me again, whether it be a win, or a loss. So the truth is, no one, and I mean _NO ONE_ knows me, nor will they ever ... [xXx]
Place: [v*v] ... It is a cold, cloudy night, with flurries falling from the sky in
New York. The parking lots are packed and all the fans are already inside.
Inside the greatest and most famous arena of all time, "Madison Square Garden" ... [v*v] Scene: [...The scene in black. Nothing can be seen but pure black. In the back you hear noise, but it is to far away to make out what it is. But it gets, closer and closer. Until you realize what it is. It is the sound of hundreds of thousands of fans cheering at the top of there lungs.....]
[...As the camera comes on, an arena with more people than imaginable can be seen. Each one holding there own, custom made sign, and cheering at the top of there lungs. In the middle of these people, and there seats, ten feet beyond the guard rails there is a ring. With red, black and white ropes. A black apron on it, and write upon the apron letters in which spell out "UCHW"...]
[...Suddenly the lights go out and the fans that moments ago where cheering louder than ever heard before are dead quiet. They wait a few moments and nothing and happens. Someone that was sitting at the announcers table gets up, and goes into the ring. He then begins to talk...]
Announcer: "Ladies and Gentlemen, thank you all for showing up here on this great night in New York. ::: fans cheer ::: And right now, I would like to introduce to you, on his way with "The Dark princess" Carissa Fina, Bear, and Dangerous Don
E. Kaution, here is the man, the myth, "Certain Death" Daniel Styfe!!" [...The fans erupt in cheers, when suddenly white pyro's erupt from either corners of the platform. "Death by Darkness" slowly begins to play, eventually getting faster. "The Dark Princess" Carissa Fina is the first to walk out.
She is wearing a long, tight black dress. The next to walk out is Bear. He is wearing a A black shirt, with baggy pants. The walks out Dangerous Don E.
Kaution, wearing torn pants, and a ripped shirt, and his left hand.. A beer. They slowly make there way to the ring, with all the fans running to the guard rails in hopes to get a better look at the stable of "Death Row" The three step into the ring, and there is another loud eruption as red fireworks are set of from the corners of the turnbuckles. Then they stop and everything goes dark. The fans still cheering and the music playing. There are a few flashes of lightning, and then the music stops. As the smoke clears the ring, and the lights return you see "Certain Death" Daniel Stryfe standing in the ring, next to everyone else. When the fans see them they get ever louder, and continue cheering for what seems like hours. They all wait until the fans quiet down, and then the announcer begins to speak again...]
Announcer: "Thank you very much for joining us out here, Mr. Stryfe. It is a pleasure to get the first interview with you, and what an interview it will be, for there is so much to talk about. There is the situation with nLs, The world title battle royal, and I understand you have yet another member of Death Row. But I think I will start here with Dangerous Don E. Kaution. He is not on the UCHW roster, so what is he doing here?"
"Certain Death" Daniel Stryfe: "First off, thank you for the nice words, but don't kiss my ass to much. Now, as for my good friend here, DDK. He is just here for now as a friend, but soon he will be on that roster. For he is one of the original and founding members of Death Row. And without him there would be no Death Row, the same goes BeaR and myself. We are the three original members of Death Row, and we have been together for years, and years. And trust me there are many more members on the way."
Announcer: "Speaking of that, I hear you have a new member of Death Row. Who is he? Is he here? Can you please tell us who he is?"
Daniel Stryfe: "Calm down little man, the new member of Death Row is here today. As a matter of fact he is right up there behind those curtains."
Scene: [..Stryfe points up to the curtains, and everyone looks and quiets down. The lights go out and you hear Daniel Stryfe continue talking...]
Daniel Stryfe: "Ladies and Gentlemen, the newest member to be added to Death Row in over six months, here is the most hard-core man to ever walk this planet, 'The Hard-core
Legend' Madman Pondo!!"
Scene: [..."Halls of Illusions" by Insane Clown Posse plays over the house PA as fire surrounds the entranceway and down the aisle way all the way to the end of the ramp. The entire arena turns to blood red as "The Hard-core Legend" Madman
Pondo emerges from the smoke filled entranceway. He wears either a Black sleeveless shirt that expose his heavy scarrage on his left arm. He also wears a pair of black wrestling tights that have a strand of barbed wire wrapping around his right leg and on the left side, down the side, it reads "Pondo" in old English Letters. He has long, wet-down and scraggly brown hair and brown eyes. He has a medium build and sports a strand a barbed wire tattoo on his right arm and he also has a barbell through his eyebrow, not to mention his pierced tongue and his ball bearing necklace. As he makes his way to the ring the fans have mixed emotions. Some boo, some cheer. But Pondo pays them no regard. He gets on the ring apron, climbs the top rope and flips into the ring, landing on his back hard. He gets up with a smile on his face as the lights return and the music stops. The Announcer starts to talk again...]
Announcer: "Wow! That is a big surprise. I bet the members of nLs are kicking themselves now! But Pondo, why Death Row?"
Pondo: "Why not Death Row? Me and Stryfe have become good friends, and I think DDK is one of the funniest men alive. I wanted in a stable, and I figured Death
Row needed an extreme man, so here I am, and I am here to stay!"
Announcer: "Well, I guess that is good enough reason. It seems like the tides have turned now. Death Row is now one bigger than nLs. Stryfe, what exactly is your relationship with nLs, and why is there so much tension between the two groups? And why to such desperate measures to destroy them?"
Daniel Stryfe: "See, for years early on in my career, it was always nLs and Inner Circle that was the best stables. But eventually Inner Circle died out and it was left down to Death Row and nLs, that is after we got rid of GoD. Anyway, there then came a series of great, historical matches between the two groups. Ranging anywhere from an all around stable war, to One Night Only. Each time Death Row winning. Finally it came down to EWA, me being the owner of that, and good old
Angelo being the remaining member of nLs. Well, as the EWA closed down, so did nLs. They where no more. They where gone. Death Row had destroyed them, I had expected to never return. But low and behold, here we sit again today, face to face. Bring back the retired Death Row, to take on the cold beyond dead nLs. So I am going to once and for all shut down nLs, this time never to return. So I have gone out, gathered great talent, that is Death Row and added one more. Thus putting the final stake into nLs."
Announcer: ::: with a shocked look on his face ::: "Well... That was very surprising. Anyway, it seems like you are going to get your first shot at that this Tuesday, when you, the lone member of Death Row, steps into the ten man battle royal for the world title. Two men of which, are nLs members. Do you think will effect the outcome of this match?"
Daniel Stryfe: "Well, it might. But then again, there are seven other men in this match. So
I am not exactly sure how it will go. All though I am sure they will eventually double up on, trying to get the win. But I don't see it effecting the outcome of the match. The reason being I cannot let nLs hold any sort of victory over me. That is why, it is a _MUST_ that I win this match. For this will be a large stepping stone, in the final destruction of the No Limit
Soldiers." Announcer: "Well, earlier on the members of nLs had a few choice words for, and they also added a new member to there stable, the Ringmaster. Any thoughts on that?"
Daniel Stryfe: "Well, the Ringmaster was a sad attempt to get someone that might power up the stable a bit. To bad it did nothing more than add the 'JOB' into 'JOBBER'. As for what they said, it was the same thing over and over again. How Death Row is nothing and we mean nothing. We are just a bunch of BS. One thing they said that did catch my attention was they said, and I quote 'To bad, it could have ended in a tag title match'. Well, if you wish it to be, then that's the way it will be. See, me and my men have already talked about this, and we will do that. Me and BeaR will take you two on, when the tag team titles come out.
Now, it also seems that one of them has brains and the other does not. Kandyman seems to think we are good, but not great, as for the Penetrator, he thinks nothing of us. With mixed emotions such as that, the force of nLs must be pretty weak. Now, lastly, again on the Ringmaster. They say if he beats
Poe, he is in. Well, that's not going to be to hard, being that Poe left. Now as a former member of his stable, I must say, Ringmaster you had no chance, and
I will be sure to take you down myself, just for you thinking you ran him out of here!"
Announcer: "Now there are two more things before you go. One is the man called Ice. He also was out here earlier, do you have any comments or replies to this man?"
Daniel Stryfe: "I have got to say, this guy is extremely funny. He knows what he is doing right off the bat. First thing the guy does, since being in the UCHW, he crashes into nLs's car! I mean, come on, that was great. He aims pretty much everything he says at nLs. He is a smart kid. And a good wrestler. Only problem with him, is that he is my way to the gold, and that's not a good place to be."
Announcer: "Now, lastly there has been much tension between you and 'The Intoxicated
Mexican' Salvo. Now, he spoke on you a little bit earlier, and he just about rephrased everything you said, the responded to you. Do you have any comments on perhaps your most dangerous opponent?"
Daniel Stryfe: "Oh yes, Salvo... I gave that man one inch of respect and he took it and ran for a mile. Well, I think I might have to send him back from where he came from. All the way back to Mexico. Now, you doubt that I lived under the bridge, well, that's fine. Think what you will. Only I know how I was raised.
And it was not in any ten story high mansion... MENG! And I never said I was from the hood, and I don't believe that I will get jumped because of that. But just in case, I invite everyone who wants to try, to bring it on, for I fear nothing and no one. Then you tell me how I am such a small American. :::
chuckles ::: Take a look at me. I am Six foot seven inches tall and weigh much over two hundred and fifty pounds. I am a huge American, so I don't know who you are talking about. Must have been a little dizzy and seeing double, because you didn't see me. You go on to say that I don't know what I am messing with. And that's sad. For you won't even remember you said that tomorrow. And the truth is, I know exactly what I am messing with. A drunk
Mexican. The fact of the matter is, it is that you do not know what you are messing with. And this is where I get the very point, and those oh so important question, can you understand me? No. If only you could...
Understand me...." Scene: [...The announcer goes to say something but suddenly all the lights in the arena go out and "Death by Darkness" again starts to play. The fans erupt in cheers as the music plays. As the light return the ring is empty. The camera slowly fades to black and finally cuts to commercials...]
No you don't know me: [xXx] ... There is a message hidden in my words. Can you find it? If you do perhaps you will know me, maybe even understand me.... Then again... Maybe not
... [xXx]
