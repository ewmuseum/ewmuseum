[Critique/Free Agent] "The Bad Seed" Brian Bradley...

2 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey folks, here is a short Brian Bradley promo I did for the WWO(Erin's). You may critique, laugh at, flame, make me a free agent offer, read to your mom...whatever you want with this.
[SCENE: A pretty damn bare scene I must say. There is only a table under a sim light, and whatever is behind that remains unseen. Sitting at the table is none other than wrestling's original Bad Seed...Brian Bradley! Bradley has a thoughtful look on his face, as his long blonde hair hangs down into his eyes.
He has grown his goatee a bit longer than usual, and he seems darker in general. He is wearing an old Jack Daniels WWO T-Shirt, and a pair of jeans.
In front of Bradley sits a large bottle of vodka, a shot glass near that. The camera seems to lock onto the unmoving eyes of the self proclaimed demon...]
Brian Bradley: "After 'The Big Show', everyone is wondering what is up with
Brian Bradley. They wonder why he was so out of focus in his match with Dan
Lewis. They wonder how he allowed himself to be beat so quickly...and why he kept looking out at the crowd. Do you wonder these things too?! Well sit down...pour yourself a shot or two, and get ready. You just accepted an invitation into the mind of a demon...."
[Bradley follows his own advice, pouring himself a shot glass full of vodka, and downing it quickly.]
Brian Bradley: "Bad Seed...or good guy? You don't which I am. You don't even know what you want me to be. You've confused me fans, so I blame this all on you. When I nearly broke my neck, you cheered me. The next week you forgot.
I tried to dedicate all my matches to you fans, but you didn't care. I tried to be good, but damnit, you wouldn't let me! So know what? What is Brian
Bradley gonna do now? Will he reinvent himself yet again to satisfy the fans and backward bums on the booking commitee?! Will he put those fangs back on and suck the blood of the innocent?! Hmmm...not just now. Now I think it's time to go back to basics...it's time to have a little fun..."
[Bradley takes one more shot, and has a strange, sadistic smile on his face...] Brian Bradley: "It's time to go back to Bradleyville...have a good trip..." [fade in to the wild eyes of Brian Bradley to close.] =================================<> [name withheld] Handler Of... "Young & Restless" Dr.Jax Jasberby[PEZ], "Bad Seed" Brian Bradley[WWO].
