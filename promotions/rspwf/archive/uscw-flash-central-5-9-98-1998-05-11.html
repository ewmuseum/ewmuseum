USCW Flash Central 5/9/98

11 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scott Dakota Kid Sharpe Abyss The Delinquent Storm w/Venus -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (The camera fades in and a Navy Blue Saturn pulls up to the back parking lot of the arena where USCW's Weekend event is being held. The driver side door opens and Scott Dakota steps out and then reaches in and pulls out a gym bag with his wrestling equipment in it. He starts to walk towards the arena door and manages to make his way through the swarms of fans looking for an autograph. He enters the arena and proceeds to walk down a corridor until he reaches a door that says "SCOTT DAKOTA" on it.
He opens the door, then flicks on one of the light switches so the room is only half lit. He puts his bag down onto the floor and then pulls a chair over and sits down. Dakota puts his head into his hands for a few seconds and then sits up and begins to speak.)
DAKOTA: It's time to put up or shut up. Matthews, we have both made the claim that we will never quit. Well, tonight is the time to find out who was just saying it, and who really meant it. I have never been more prepared in my life for a match, this is the absolute biggest task in my wrestling career. Last time we fought in that ring it was an intense battle. You had the upper hand, I was down, but instead of taking advantage of me and trying to get the win, you simple taunted me and flaunted to the crowd. You made lazy pin after lazy pin, and each time I kicked out. Then when the time was just right, I took advantage and hit you with the Silencer. Instead of showboating and trying to impress everyone, I simply made the pin and got a well deserved victory. You go and blame your loss on the referee, who you say made a "slow" count.
Matthews, when are you going to become a man and admit that I defeated you with out any help at all? Are you just too self-absorbed to admit that for one night I was the better man? I belive you are, and that is why tonight, I plan on beating the life out of your cocky arrogant ass.
(Dakota chuckles and then reaches into his gym bag and pulls out a pack of Kamel Red cigarettes and a green lighter. He opens the pack, takes out a cigarette and puts it into his mouth, then lights it with the lighter and takes a few drags before continuing.)
DAKOTA: I mean every single word I say, I do not like you Matthews, not one single bit. You think you are some sort of "god" some kind of indestructible machine, well, it's about time someone shows you that you are just like everyone else, a simple human being. You are comprised of bones, skin, and blood, just like the rest of the human race. But you seem to think that you are superior to everyone else, it is time that somebody put you in your place.
(Dakota takes a few drags off his cigarette, then reaches over and clicks on the radio. The song "How's It Gonna Be" by Third Eye Blind is on, Dakota then clicks over and hits the play button on the tape player, the song "Mouth For War" by Pantera starts to play. A smile comes across
Dakota's face as he continues to speak.) DAKOTA: Pain, that is what this is all about. Two mere mortal men, who have a strong dislike for one another, tonight we both have the same gameplan. To go out to that ring and try to inflict as much pain as humanly possible onto the other, in hopes that they can mutter the words
"I Quit". The man who is able to get his opponent to say those words will be declared the victor, and will get all the bragging rights.
Matthews, I sure hope that you have every move planned out, I hope that tonight you give me the best damned fight of your life, because I want to show the whole wide world that I can defeat you when you are on top of your game. This is going to be an epic battle that will no doubt go into the history books as the most grueling and intense match I have ever had to go through. I think that, wait, I know that I am up to the task.
(Dakota takes a few more drags off of his cigarette, then reaches over and ashes it into the ash tray before continuing.)
DAKOTA: I think that I have pretty much said all that needs to be said. The time is now Matthews, in just a few short minutes we will find out who is the real man, and who is the boy. In closing I would just like to wish you good luck, lets make this one hell of a match, oh yeah, and be ready for anything, because if you are not, there is no doubt in my mind that you will be SILENCED!
(Dakota takes another long drag off the cigarette as the camera fades to black.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- {Kid Sharpe is at the USCW arena. He takes his time making his way to the locker room, favoring a knee. He reaches his locker and sits on the stool. The
Kid turns to the camera....
Kid Sharpe: You see, I'm a real champion, as I'm sure that Shawn Evans is. When you're the champ, you have to do anything to keep the belt just another day.
Wrestling injured, wrestling sick, wrestling when you don't want to. It's what proves that you are a champion. Stephens, how can you write me out of a match?
You aren't the champion, and you basically have no weight around here. You say you beat me in a fair match so you write me out? I beat you too pal. You had that picture, well with the stuff today I could make a picture of Abyss actually with Amber Miller. You know what happened and you can't accept defeat.
It's up to Shawn who is in the match or not. And speaking of Shawn, I love the way you guys think. This eight man match would be perfect for things to be settled once and for all. I'm in indefinately. Now we just need word from
Razor, we'll see if TSE is still alive and well. I have a feeling it is. There is a rumor that I threw the match to you Daniel. That's a lie. You beat me, you were the better man. But if you weren't so stupid you would be the champion now. Atleast I lost the belt to someone I respect. You gave the belt to Shawn, when it could be yours. Did you doubt yourself that much? I mean, did you think that the only way for me to lose that belt was through cheating, that you couldn't beat me by yourself? Of course you did. And then you turn around and beat me. Obviously intelligance isn't your strong point pal. Stephens, you are a thorn in my side, a nuisance. And I've learned that the only way to end something like this is to humiliate you beyond belief. To give you a string of loses so long it's unbelieve able. It starts at the triangle match between
Shawn, you, and ME.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <Abyss is seen sitting in a hotel suite. The camera rotates inside the room showing a 64" TV, a fire place with a bearskin rug in front of it, a wet bar, spa, walkin closet, and finally the kingsize waterbed which Abyss is sitting on. The TV title is sitting beside him.>
Abyss: I love this life. I love this belt. I love Amber Miller. And I love
Amish beatings! Yoder, usually I wouldn't be in a fancy hotel like this, but the power that this title has is unbelievable. People practically bow down to me when they see me coming. I can only imagine how it feels to be Viper about now. And you Yoder, can only imagine how it feels to be me. You'll never hold this belt Jacob, it's better then you. Face the facts Amish boy, you got nothin on me!
<There is a knock on the door> Abyss: Come in. <A bell hop enters with a small cardboard box> Bell Hop: Sir, I believe you ordered this. Abyss: About time it got here! I've had this runny nose for hours. <The bell hop gives the package to Abyss. Abyss tosses him a roll of twenties and the bell hop exits the room. Abyss rips open the box to show a Jacob Yoder
T-shirt. On the front is a picture of Jacob with the words "The Loose Bull"
above him. On the back is the words "The new generation of Amish".> Abyss: Hahaha! New generation my ass. Once I put an end to Jake all the little
Amish pricks will go back to their villages and churn butter again.
<Abyss holds the shirt to his face and excessively blows his nose on it> Abyss: Ewww! <Abyss shows the shirt to the camera. Snot is all over Jacob's face.> Abyss: Yoder, there's a picture of what you'll look like tomorrow! Just imagine that it's blood instead of mucas. Hahahaha!
<Abyss throws the shirt in the fireplace and the screen fades out> -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Delinquent is sitting in front of the camera in a comfortable chair) Delinquent: Evans...how many MORE times do I need to tell you that I am NOT joining DLM before you will understand??? Geez...and by calling me a juvenile
I guess that proves even FURTHER how stupid you are, Evans. But, I am willing to let it slide. I haven't had much to say lately, but that will ALL change after the card this weekend. I have to fight Vanilly...I think...well no matter who it is I want to get in there and outta there quick because I have bigger fish to fry. I will kick his ass, leave, and finish all my business...I have two surprises for all of you for the weekend. If any of you want to call me names and say I am inferior, why don't you try to back it up in the ring? Is it because you can't? That's EXACTLY what I thought. Well, I am gettin outta here. Later...
(Camera turns off) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- Storm: I really don't have time for this but I'll grace you morons out there with a few words. First off Chris Evans, so far son you haven't done a damn thing to make me think you can beat me. Ya you have some fancy finger toy but
I could get one to out of a gum ball machine. See Chrissy, I have traveled all over the world and beaten the "so called" best in the business. I have won major titles and have been the envy of every wrestler. Every federation I have competed in have tried like hell to get rid of me. But you know what Chrissy?
they all have failed... Venus: And miserably I might add. Storm: I really have nothing to prove in this match. Why I have already beaten you so everyone is expecting me to repeat my performance. Of course, once I pin your ass for the second time, it will be time to move to much bigger things. See, I can't waste my time with little mid-carders like yourself. The spot light must be on me and the SWA. Do you know how much better off this little bush league will be with me as World Champion? The possibilities are endless. So Chris, you can enjoy that little ring you have because it would just turn my finger green any way.
Venus: Oh and Evans, when you get frustrated in the future, get a women, get a room, get a motel but leave Storm out of the equation.
Storm: Like I said early, I'm making this short because you people especially
Chris, make me sick. Come on Venus, we are OUT OF HERE!!
Venus: SWA 4 LIFE!!!
