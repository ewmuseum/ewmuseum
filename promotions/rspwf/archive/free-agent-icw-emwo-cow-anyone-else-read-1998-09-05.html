[Free Agent] ICW, EMWO, CoW, anyone else read!

5 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yet another fed I was in closed down, seems to me that I don't have a good taste in picking feds so once agaIn I'm back to join another fed. I want a fed that promises at least another year being open! I am really interested in joining ICW, EMWO, and CoW so if you think I have what it takes please get back to me but I am open to all invitations. I currently have 3 characters:
Jonathan "Buff" Uss (Cruiserweight/Tag Team) "The Stuff" Jeff Walters (Tag Team) Enigma (My newest creation) I can handle all 3 in one fed but Enigma has nothing to do with Buff and
The Stuff. I have included a sample roleplay, it's just an intro for a fed I joined so it's nothing much and it's not real "heel" but here it is:
(Shows Jonathan "Buff" Uss and "The Stuff" Jeff Walters in a studio being filmed. Jonathan Uss is sitting to the left and Jeff Walters is sitting to the right, both in drector type chairs. There are lights all around. A man with camera walks by and almost hits Jonathan Uss in the head.)
[Jonathan Uss] Hey watch it you dumb shit! [Jeff Walters] Hey Buff! [Jonathan Uss] Yea? [Jeff Walters] Where the hell are we? [Jonathan Uss] I'm not sure. (Looks over to a lady who is untangling some wires) [Jonathan Uss] Hey you! [Lady] Who me? [Jonathan Uss] Yes....YOU! [Lady] Huh? [Jonathan Uss] Where the hell are we? [Lady] Sir, you should know that this is The Barely Legal Wrestling
Federation. [Jonathan Uss] Barely Legal? [Jeff Walters] I don't think we should be here, cause Buff and The Stuff aren't legal in 49 states!
[Lady] Well then should I show you the door? [Jonathan Uss] I think we can find our way out. [Jeff Walters] Wait Buff! Let's stay for a bit and just so we can add some more gold to our collection! I mean, I have held 19 titles and you have held countless cruiserweight titles along with us being the greatest tag team in the world, we could show those young punks that compete in the BLWF how to actaully wrestle a match!
[Jonathan Uss] Or even get a name! I mean did you see some of those guys on the roster sheet they gave us?
[Jeff Walters] To tell you the truth I was too busy to even notice. [Jonathan Uss] Let's see, theres a 300 pound "monster" named Mr. Big. Yea right buddy give me a break I may be only 5'10 195 pounds but I could knock his fat ass down and roll him around the ring like I was a
F'N umpa lumpa!
[Jeff Walters] Ah, Charlie and the Chocolate Factory! [Jonathan Uss] There might be a problem getting him in Buff's Stuff though, you know those fat f**ks have chuby little arms. I don't know if
I could get him in an abdomadable stretch into a powerbomb?
[Jeff Walters] I would have to agree with you there! But who else is on the roster? [Jonathan Uss] Duuuuhhhhhhhh, how about žžFastball? [Jeff Walters] Curveball? [Jonathan Uss] No fastball! [Jeff Walters] Who gives a shit! The fact of the matter is no one in
BLWF has the balls to take on any one of us, or even both of us in a tag match. By the end of our stint here in BLWF we will have held all the titles. And Buff, I think there only one thing left to say!
[Jonathan Uss] FREAK'N MONKEY! ****************************************************** So if anyone is interested e-mail me or respond
