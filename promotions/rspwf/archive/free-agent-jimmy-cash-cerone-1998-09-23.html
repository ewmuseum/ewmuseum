{FREE AGENT} Jimmy"Cash" Cerone

23 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am the handler of the free agent Jimmy"Cash Cerone. He is a wrestler that has the image like a mobster, or gangster. He talks and acts like one. Well contact me if you have a quality fed that I can join and have a chance to win a title. Here is a sample draft for all you fed presidents:
[ The scene opens in a rudown looking street in Chicago. People are walking by busily, not making eye contact with the camera. Cars are driving by at a rather fast pace. Then a strongly built man walks up and is nicely dressed.
He stares right into the camera. It is Jimmy"Cash" Cerone. He has on a black
$2,000 Armoni three piece suit. On his head he has a black fedora. He has black shoes that are so shiny you can see your reflection off the top of the shoes. A guy that is decently dressed walks up to him and they start talking.
They are whispering so the camera can't pick up what they are saying. Jimmy smiles and pulls out a big roll of cash and pulls off a 100 dollar bill and gives it to the guy. "Way to go" you hear Jimmy say. He then focuses his attention back on the camera.]
Jimmy"Cash" Cerone: Hey youse listen to me. You see all this? This is where I grew up and came from. You see all this poverty? Well, this was my life when
I was a kid. But look at me now.[ He points at his clothes] I grew up on the streets where you had to fight for your life and pride. I grew up all around the Mafia and organized crime. I lived the life that the gangsters lived, I stole like the gangsters stole, and if I had to I killed like the gangster's killed.
[ He points to a restaurant/club on the corner of the street.] Jimmy"Cash" Cerone: You see that place? Thats where I got my first break in life. I got a job working for a guy. I ran errands and took care of some
"heavy work". He was a loanshark, and if somebody was late on payments, he had me take care of it. I got in many a fight with people over the money they owed him. And if I couldn't convince them on my own, me and a blackjack would get the job done. I was kicked out of school in the fifth grade for beating up a teacher, so I took to the streets at a young age. I never got a hand out or a free ride in life, I had to earn everything I ever got on my own hardwork. I started running "rackets" of my own a little while, and made all the money I have now. Me and my crew specialize in strongarm and extortion. If you think you can run a club on this street without paying me for protection? I dare you to try. I'm not afraid to bash skulls with anybody and everybody in the whole damn world.
[He starts walking toward a nearby building. The camera follows his every step. Another guy walks up to him.
Jimmy"Cash" Cerone: How's that thing going, you talked to me about yesterday? Has jerk off given you your money yet? Unknown Man: No, he still says he doesn't owe any money. He's lying through his teeth. Jimmy"Cash" Cerone: Well then show this jerk off who's boss. Do what you gotta do, just get the money, capische?
[Jimmy shakes hands with the guy and then walks into the building. He leads into some sort of a front office. There is a long line to get into some sort of a main room. The people there immediatley stop helping the other people and usher Jimmmy into the room. The place has the scent of sweat. There are people boxing in a nearby ring, and people hitting a punching bag. You can hear trainers yelling at young fighters, pushing them to be the best they can possibly be.]
Jimmy"Cash" Cerone: This is where I started to put my fighting skills to work. I started out as a boxer. I worked like hell day and night, and then became a small time prize fighter. I fought in some rough fights that all the young toughs and old time hoods in the neighborhood would come and place there bets on. I was incredible, but then all of a sudden I just got sick of it and decided to try something more challenging. I am now entering the world of wrestling.
I started in this sport a few years back. I didn't have any experienced instructor to help me or teach me, I had to teach myself. For years I was in this gym trying to teach myself how to be the greatest wrestler of all time.
And you might wonder, how can you teach yourself how to wrestle when there was nobody to wrestle? Well, I did it when I first started. Then I found some other hoods who had the same idea I did. I started wrestling them, pounding on them until they refused to wrestle with me any longer. So I went back to my old methods of training by myself. I was biding my time just waiting for somebody to come along and say they could out wrestle me. I studied and watched everything about the sport. I waited, waited, and waited and tried to decide when the right time would be do bust onto the wrestling scene, and I have decided that this is the time. Right here and NOW! So all the wrestlers out there better watch themselves, because from here on out the wrestling world will be dominated by Jimmy"Cash" Cerone. Be it now or one hundred years from now when I'm in jail, it's gonna be the way I say it is.
[ With that Jimmy turns and starts to walk away. The screen fades to black as he throws a hard right cross on a punching bag.]
Dave
