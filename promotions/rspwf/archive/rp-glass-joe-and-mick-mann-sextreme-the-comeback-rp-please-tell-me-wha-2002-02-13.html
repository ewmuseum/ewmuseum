[rp] Glass Joe and Mick Mann (SEXtreme) The comeback RP (please tell me what you think)

13 February 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please keep in mind that it has been almost 4 years since we've done this, but
I welcome all opinions. We plan on using this as a sample RP for when we apply to feds. So now...the RP. (You kids do still call them "RP"'s right?)
("Over Now" by Alice In Chains plays as you see photo still of people getting arrested, homeless people, and crying children. Then, the logo for the television show,"The Behind the Mat True Not-so-Hollywood Story" appears. Then comes a voiceover as a camera shows an empty arena with a ring in the middle.)
VO: Over the years, many wrestlers have made their marks in e-wrestling. Tonight's subjects are not those wrestlers. They didn't even leave as much as a skid mark on their jock straps. Tonight, we bring you the story of two overly hyped wrestlers...Glass Joe and Mick Mann...better known as SEXtreme.
(A slow motion video of Glass Joe and Mick Mann giving a interview in front of a EFW banner is shown)
VO: Their story begins in a independent wrestling federation based out of New
York. At first, their paths came no where close to each other.
(Glass Joe is shown sitting in a steel chair at ringside of the empty arena. He's wearing black Adidas windpants, cheap black flip flops, and a black shirt with "SEXtreme" written in red on the front)
GJ: I was this happy go lucky jobber with the fed. They where paying me a whole eighty dollars a night!
(Mick "The Prick" Mann is sitting here wearing an old black System of a Down
"hand" hooded sweatshirt, long black jean shorts that go to his shins, black
Skechers boots, and a Dodgers cap worn sideways on his head. You realize he's wearing the hat like that because he's a weirdo and not because he's trying to be "street")
Mick: I was this masked luchador type at that time called "The Amazing" Mick
Mann. I'd slap the hands, kiss the babies and sign the autographs for the little mongoloids at the retard hospital. But where did that get me?
NOWHERE!!!! I was the exact person I have come to hate today. I was a ham-n-egger. MMMMM HAM N' EGGS! LETS GO TO DENNY'S, FRIEND!!!
(Joe looks at Mick urgently as if to say "quit it") VO: Then, in 1997, they joined a mighty force with each other. (A video is shown of Glass Joe, Jon Morse, "The Amazing" Mick Mann, and Zero
Tolerance in a EFW ring announcing the beginning of their stable "The
Innovators of Violence) GJ: That was pretty fun. The EFW decided to give me a personality. They put me together with an AWESOME group of guys and said that I should take to drinking beers in the ring. I had to start with the non-alcoholic stuff at first because those regular beers where buzzing me wayyy to hard on me after....like one.
Mick: I was going nowhere fast...like a (censor)-hungry dirty (censor) in a crowded (censor)house, ha ha ha ha ha ha ha..yeah. So Joe here comes up to me out of the blue and asks me if I wanted to form a stable with him, and told me that "you were the only Cruiserweight I had on my mind" while hiding a long list of Cruiser names in his hand with about 6 or 7 names scratched out.
VO: Then, the beginning of their "renegade" status started several months later. (A picture of "Sexy Ass" Mick Mann, Glass Joe and Jon Morse is shown) GJ: We thought it would be a good idea one night if we just said,"Screw the fans, let's have some fun and do some obscene things that make absolutely no sense." I think it was my idea for the most part...I had a whole three beers that night, so it could've been the alcohol talking. (GJ shrugs)
Mick: Yeah we finally realized what we had to do to get ahead in this business. I dropped my mask on purpose and lost a Mask match and unleashed this adonis of a face on the world. I MEAN COME ON, LOOK AT THIS....LOOK AT IT!!
(Mick gets up and shoves the right side of his face into the camera lens. He doubles over backwards clutching his jaw and screams in pain. He sits back down.)
VO: Not very long after their new style, the IOV made a drastic, dare I say dramatic, change.
GJ: That Jon Morse guy was a total barfoid. We'd be driving from show to show and he'd keep taking the last soda from the ice chest without asking. One day, we went to ride a rollercoaster on Coney Island and Jon threw up onto Mick's fake leather pants. Mick paid twenty bucks for those. I thought Mick was gonna cry
Mick: Yeah those puppies cost me an Andy Jackson!! That's like two night's work!!!! I so didn't share the rest of my box of Junior Mints with him after that.
So anyway we realized that we needed a change. So one night live on local access television we punked his ass out and had a man by the name of Stupendous
Steve join us. Steve blended very well with our newfound direction and wasn't a boring stone-faced fuddyduddy like Morse was.
VO: With this group of outlaws running loose on the EFW, the future was looking bright for SEXtreme.
(Glass Joe puts some orange Oakley straight jackets on) GJ: With us, a group of outlaws, running loose on the EFW, the future was looking bright for us.
(Mick looks around like something redundant happened) GJ: I got a raise to a hundred and forty buck raise. I about crapped my pants, but I couldn't afford to ruin them. It was great, we got to be all over the TV and where making hundreds of thousands of dollars in merchandise for this poor company. From there, we started going nationwide with our federation. The fans where loving us. Mick lost his virginity to a SEXtreme groupie in Butte,
Montana.
Mick: Right-o my friend, and it's no coincidence I left my virginity in a place called Butte. Her name was Yoko.....and when I pulled it out Yoko said "O NO!!"
HAHAHAHHA, ya get it?? VO: Then tragedy struck this group of friends. The "Sexy Ass" Mick Mann was put in the hospital.
(A picture of Mick strung up to a bunch of hospital machines is shown) GJ: Mick was hurt? Mick: Yeah remember, the explanation the fed gave was "internal injuries"...but that was just a cover for what was really long.....I contracted.......
(big echoey voice) THE CLAP ("clap" echoes a few times) Mick: The STD for some reason caused a very traumatic identity crisis...thinking I was both "Amazing" AND "Sexy"...not sure what I wanted to be. I later settled on staying "Sexy". As "Sexy Ass" I took the Cruiser division by storm, winning the belt, wreaking havoc, and being an all around bad boy. However you will call me "The Prick" from now on. "Sexy Ass" is retired....I'll get to that painful, painful story on another day (bites fist and looks away)
VO: During this time Glass Joe rose to the top of the federation and later became the EFW World Heavyweight Champion.
GJ: Dude, that was so cool when I won the big heavy belt. For some reason, they cut my pay back to eighty dollars a match. It didn't matter though, I had the whole locker rooms respect. And respect from my peers means more than getting off welfare. Did I mention I was the last EFW World champ?
(Joe pulls out the EFW World Heavyweight Championship from underneath his chair) Doesn't the globe of the world look cool? VO: Then the EFW shut down. (Joe drops his belt and begins to sob into this hands) VO: Finally, Mick and Joe realized that wrestling just wasn't paying the bills. So they went on to jobs in which they knew would.
GJ: Not many people know this, but I have a degree in education. So I went and applied at this elementary school to be a teacher. Next thing I knew, I was teaching a wide eyed group of whipper snappers who where in the third grade.
(A video is shown of Glass Joe dressed in khaki slacks, brown shoes, and a navy sweat vest with a black collared shirt underneath screaming at kids with a stick in his hand. Every once in a while, Joe stops the yelling, closes his eyes, then opens them with a crazy look and starts swingin the stick about.)
GJ: That job was the best. I got to mold the minds of our nations youth. And if they didn't mold... ::Joe pounds his fist into this palm:: And the single moms, man where they so HOT! There was this was one lil number... ::Joe fades
out:: VO: As for the man whose nickname was "Sexy Ass"... Mick: While Joe was doing his state sponsored child abuse, I took a different.....seedier route in life.
(Images of a dark, dirty street flash across the screen. Then random shots of a large pink neon sign that reads "Captain Poontangaroo's")
Mick: (quivering voice) You'd think a place called "Captain Poontangaroo's" would be a female strip joint......but it's not.....it's a male strip joint, owned by the Captain. I was rookie of the year, forced to show off my manliness to fat, middle-aged, trailer park housewives...and their fat....out of shape...sweathog thighs..... OH GOD
(Mick dives under the ring apron and you hear heaving noises. He comes out wiping his mouth and holding his stomach)
Mick: What a man will do for a fifty cent piece. (Joe, feeling bad for his friend, tosses Mick a cold beer, Mick doesn't see this and it bounces off his skull. You hear bird chips)
VO: Unfortunately, things didn't work out for them in their new career fields. GJ: ::With a look of anger on his face:: SOME LIL PUSSY WENT HOME AND CRIED
TO HIS DADDY THAT MY "TOUGH LOVE" APPROACH TO ADDITION WAS NOT SOMETHING HE
ENJOYED! SO BIG BAD DADDY COMES UP TO THE SCHOOL AND TALKS TO THE PRINCIPAL!!!
(Mick comes by and sits next to Joe and puts his arm around Joe) GJ: So I was called in for a conference. I smelled a total screwjob and I wasn't willing to put these folks over. They tried to hit me with this (does finger quotations) "bad guy image" which I wasn't gonna sell. So finally, the dad goes,"If you're not going to confess up to your wrong doings maybe I'll have to do something about it?" And being that I wasn't going to confess and I felt he was going to do something about it, I powerbombed him onto the principals desk. Needless to say, I was fired. And escorted off the school property by security guards. They wouldn't even let me go back to the classroom to get my stick.
Mick: I was finally getting adjusted to my new job, adjusting to the swinger lifestyle. You know, getting shag carpeting in my apartment, rubber women, latex gloves, the whole deal....when I discovered Captain Poontangaroo was cheating me out of my paycheck. I didn't take kindly to this so in a flustered, spit-flying, red faced rage I poked him in the eye, my infamous "Mick Prick".
Then I proceeded to grab his neck, turn, jump up and execute my old finisher
"The Lewd Awakening". I had done this all out of instinct......RASSLERS
INSTINCT. (makes a fist like G.I. Joe) VO: So once again, the boys from SEXtreme found themselves on hard times. Now, instead of wrestling being the cause of their problems, it became their solution. GJ: We got invited to come wrestle in this Boston Bruiser benefit card. Mick and myself hadn't wrestled in almost two years by then. So this is how it goes down, the match starts, I'm in the ring and I successfully pull off a arm drag take down. It was so crisp that I just got too excited. I went over to high five Mick who was equally excited and I started pumping my fist over and over.
I didn't notice our opponent coming up behind me and rolling me up for a pin. At least we got back in the ring. Mick: That reminds me, during that Bruiser Memorial trip, I watched a LOT of TV in the hotel room Joe and I shared. I mean what else is there to do in
Boococky, Idaho anyway? Well apparently they don't get any quality shows like Insomniac with Dave
Attell in Boococky, Idaho. It was just Oxi Clean and Epil-Stop And Spray infomercials.
I think I must've been hypnotized after awhile because when Joe came back to the room I was screaming "LOOK AT THIS, LOOK AT HOW OXI STOP N SPRAY GETS OUT
THESE GRASS STAINS AND REMOVES MY HAIR. LOOK AT THIS" while rubbing powdered donuts into my scalp and all over my shirt.
Man that was a fun trip!! (Joe just stares at his...."special"...friend) VO: So now, it appears as SEXtreme is back and ready now more than ever to conquer the wrestling world.
GJ: I'm not in it for money....I'm really in it for the health care plan. What, did you think I was gonna say I'm back for the long road trips, crappy hotels and the other stupid wrestlers? Pshaw, I'm in for the dental baby!
(Joe pumps his fist) Mick: I'm in it for the easy hotel maids. I've given a new meaning to "bed time mint". Boooya.
(Joe and Mick wave goodbye like at the end of the Beverly Hillbillies, as the credits to "The Behind the Mat True Not-so-Hollywood Story" roll as "Over Now"
by Alice In Chains plays again)
