[Critique] Am I getting better? Well... AM I????

3 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In.] [There he is--Ryan Delaney, sitting on a stoop in some city, somewhere in the U.S. A Marlboro Light (Not everyone smokes Reds, damnit!) is in between his fingers; the nicotine in his lungs. He's wearing a cloth that reads "The Only Good Clown is a Dead
Clown." Urm, wonder if that's a hidden message... His pants are the same as always--black and baggy. No hoodie to be found, not even his prized Coal Chamber beanie. Delaney's hair is blowing around, thanks to the wonderful wind. He looks around, his eyes never focusing on the camera. He raises the cigarette to his lips, and makes the cherry really red. The smoke begins funneling out, as... yea, you guessed it--he speaks.]
RD: Everyone thinks the same thing about me. Everyone, from Ewiak to Akiva to Steele, thinks of me as something that somehow ended up here, a thing that doesn't matter on any level. No one looks at my track record in the ring. No one looks at the wrestling talent that has been passed on to me.
No one looks my way, period. [He holds the Marlboro Light out in front of him, watching it burn slowly. A smile comes to his face.]
I gave Saul a run for his money, made him work for that first round victory many months ago. I made
Cutlass wrestle his best to pin me. I made "Fast" Eddie bust his balls to beat me. And while those two are fighting in is what to be one classic match, I'm stuck as the runt. I'm stuck as the underdog in some
Cruiserweight battle royale, fighting for a title I helped bring into existence. But no one cares about that. [The smile fades, as he brings the smoke back to his lips, giving into the nicotine craving once more.]
I'm just some crazy goth. I'm just some sadistic freak. I'm just a nothing, a waste a space, a non-entity. And though I swear ten times over I'd get that monkey off my back, it never has left. People doubting my ability, my talent... It never stops happening. Everyone gives me the once over, thinking the same thing:
I'm a poor imitation, at best. [He laughs, flings the cigarette off screen, probably at someone knowing Delaney.] How I love to be able to prove them all wrong. How I would love just to be able to shut them up, and make them realize the true talent. I thought coming to the
Carolinas, and establishing myself there as a dominating force would do it. That didn't happen. I thought finding a blood brother, and destroying lost soul after lost soul would rid myself of such demeaning reviews. That hasn't worked. So what am I left to do?
Win the coveted RAW Cruiserweight title. [He stands up, putting his hands in his jean pockets. Looking around and finding nothing, he finally turns his glance at the camera.] I wish it was that simple because even being the Cruiserweight champ of this Empire someone would find something wrong with me, and call me a disgrace.
Someone would have a problem with me, and the issue will live on, never really dying out in the first place. But still... Holding a title high in the air, after surviving a battle royale? That has to mean something.
[That evil grin...] It'll be a step in the right direction, at least. [Fade Out.]
