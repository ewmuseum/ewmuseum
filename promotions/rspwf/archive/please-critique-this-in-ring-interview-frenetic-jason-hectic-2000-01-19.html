Please critique this in-ring interview. ("Frenetic" Jason Hectic)

19 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I think I'm a good roleplayer, but I'm up for constructive criticism. What am I doing well and what should I work on?
-------------------------------------------------------- ("The Scavenger" by Lemon Drop Kid begins to play. The crowd in Providence starts cheering loudly.)
Manning: You know what THAT music means! Gordon: Oh MAAAAN. I was hoping he'd forget to come back. Manning: Here comes Jason Hectic! And he wants the microphone right away! *Hectic: Ladies and Gentlemen... My FAITHFUL public... (The crowd cheers.) Manning: No matter what twists and turns this man's volatile career takes, he always maintains the power to make these fans adore him!
*Hectic: I stand before you... A man... who has failed. A man... who has been wronged. And a man... who has been cheated. (Loud boos emanate from the crowd.) Gordon: You should have stopped at FAILED, Hectic! You're washed up at the age of 24, haha!!
*Hectic: I looked up at the lights, I stared in disbelief at the incompetent referee, it hit me that I had just lost to that worthless idiot who is now our champion... And I realized something. In the immortal words of the late great, Owen Hart... "I TRIED TO BE A NICE GUY!"
Manning: No one plays the game with more tenacity and respect than Jason
Hectic. Tried? As a good friend of his backstage, I'll tell you, Jason
Hectic IS a nice guy.
*Hectic: Well it seems to me... Nice guys come in SECOND to last... Just barely ahead of all you fans that consistently buy tickets, only to have this BULLSHIT force-fed to you on a weekly basis!
Gordon: Can he say that? Manning: Well, it is live television! *Hectic: You fans that pay these jackasses' salaries... You fans that pave the way for them to drive Porsches, to hang out at disgustingly pretentious gala dinners, and to live in comfort ludicrous for even a KING, much less these greasy out-of-shape and out-of-touch BUREAUCRATS.
Gordon: Hey! Those guys pay YOUR salary, jackass! *Hectic: And just as your favorites, just as your heroes are coming down the home stretch, looking to take the prize, some PISSANT referee catches their feet, allowing undeserving, underworked, and overpaid HACKS like Xanthus to stride across the finish line in first place... Well, pardon me if I got a little bit tired of the losers' circle.
Manning: As well he should! *Hectic: Frankly, the nice guy gig SUCKS, and if that's how this company wants to play, well then by God I'll tie my laces a little tighter, I'll run a little faster, and I'll SPIT directly in the eye of ANY convention that tries to get in my damn way.
*Hectic: Now I can hear the stir going through this crowd. Does this mean that I'm turning our backs on you? The answer: HELL...NO!! (The crowd erupts!) Manning: Atta boy, Jason!! *Hectic: Make no mistake about it. Jason Hectic has ALWAYS been about entertaining. The only difference THIS time is, when the Total Entertainment
Value gets SCREWED, someone is getting SLAUGHTERED.
Gordon: Just who does he think he is? Manning: He doesn't think... He KNOWS who he is. *Hectic: So from this moment forth... Any wrestler trying to climb that ladder by yanking me off of it... Any REF incompetent enough to let some ass like Wolverine or Chris Hoff get a shortcut win over me... Any BACK OFFICE
WEAKLING found guilty of handing out favors to ANYONE over me... Will be left in a PUDDLE OF BLOOD and a PILE OF BONES...
*Hectic: In a company with no justice, we offer vigilante justice. Jason Hectic... is on the warpath... Now Bad Luck 13... Tonight we will walk that aisle. You are a good man. You're a damn good wrestler too. May the best man win, buddy... And between the two of us, we WILL tear the roof off right here in Providence! (The crowd noise increases the last iota possible.) This has been Jason Hectic... GOING OFF!!
Manning: Quite an emphatic statement made by Jason Hectic, as the break from the company certainly doesn't seem to have cooled his competitive fire in the least!
Gordon: I'm just looking forward to him and Bad Luck 13 beating each other to a pulp!
