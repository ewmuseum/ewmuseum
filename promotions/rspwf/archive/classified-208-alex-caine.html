Alex Caine........

Wrestlers Seeking Feds · July 2003

Wrestlers Seeking Feds: Alex Caine........ NAME: Alex Caine D.O.B: September 8th 1981 Theme Music: What Doesn’t Die - Anthrax Where Your From: Leamington Spa, UK Style: Martial Arts, Risk Taker Favorite Moves: Snap Suplex, Explosion (VanDaminator), Spinning Heel Kick, Half Crab (2nd Finisher) (The Injection), Corkscrew Moonsault , Huricanranna, Belly To Back Suplex, Reverse DDT, 450 Splash, Rolling Thunder. Weapon Of Choice: Nunchukas (Caine carries these to the ring) Alignment: Heel Finisher: The Shot Description Of Finisher: Implant DDT Look: Face: Brown eyes, a serious 'down to buiness' look. Clean Shaven. A Sharp red triangle that starts just above his right eye and reaches straight down and comes to a point just about where the top of his mouth would be, the width at the top is just larger than the width of his eye will be. Body: A well toned, fit looking Cruiser style body. (e.g. x-pac, kidman) Hair: Style and color: shoulder length and black. Think X-Pac style. Race: Caucasian Height: 5' 10" Weight 226lbs Weapon of Choice: Nunchuckas Attire: Shirtless, black baggy trousers with flames creeping up the right hand side to thigh level. Black boots. History: Caine is a trained and extremely skilled Martial Artist, so skilled that many believed he would become the face of Martial Arts one day, he was a cocky as hell but everybody still loved him because to the Martial Arts world Caine was known as a fun loving young man but it was all just a front, kept hidden away from the world was a sickly violent side of Caine. This 'violent' side was all revealed during a tournament, Caine had beaten a guy called Sandy Peters and received a jibe about his corrupt family. Upon receiving this jibe Caine snapped and stomped the guy into ground, not stopping with some well deserved punishment Caine took things too far by stamping and jumping on the spinal chord of Sandy Peters leaving him crippled. Caine’s relentless assault led to a worldwide lifetime ban from competing Martial Arts tournaments, the top brass in the Martial Arts world couldn't and wouldn't allow such a lack of discipline after a match. So with a love for fighting Caine left the UK and headed to the United States in the hope of becoming a professional wrestler but with his name everywhere for what he had done no trainers would go near him let alone train him to be apart of something they loved, however one man would help Caine, a former champion Andreas Thomas took Caine under his wing and helped Caine develop some minor technical abilities and risk taking tendencies, finally the time came for Andreas and Alex to say goodbye and Alex has set about carving himself an ever lasting place in the wrestling business……. Entrance: What Doesn't Die by Anthrax blasts over the arena and the lights flicker in time with Charlie Benante's bass drum, and as John Bush's vocals kick in a trenchcoat wearing Alex Caine steps out onto the ramp and strides slowly into a mass of orange smoke that’s covering the entrance way, the lights go white and roam the crowd who are on Caine’s back immediately hurling boos and abuse. Alex is carrying a set of nunchuckas that he shows off with randomly on his way down the ramp while he scowls at the crowd from left to right, Alex rolls into the ring and stands still in the middle of it before pushing his arms outward with his fists clenched and his face looking to the ground. The lights come on properly as he throws his nunchuckas into a corner of the ring and slips his shades into his coat pocket before he removes his coat throwing it to the outside. Sample RP: -The scene opens up and we’re immediately opposed by a photograph that looks to be a picture of Caine in his early days. He’s wearing a pair of red shorts and a plain white t-shirt, he is located in a garden by the looks of things holding a red ball which has white polka dots scattered over it. However the most noticeable feature of this picture isn’t the young Alex’s jet black hair which contrasts which the pictures mass of overwhelming bright colours, it’s the smile he has, he’s so happy, Alex’s smile is quite literally like an elastic band at full stretch- [The camera shifts over to right] -The next picture a class picture of Alex and his classmates, Alex is in the second row from the front and is wearing and Alice In Chains t-shirt which features the front cover of Dirt. Again in this picture he looks pretty happy, the two guys either side of him have big red crosses on their faces as does the tall blond haired guy in the back row who stands 3rd in from the right and the black kid in the front row, the guy furthest over to the left hand side- -The scene fades out and we find ourselves back in 1992- -We’re in a classroom lead by Ms. Hunington, a 43 year old with short grey hair and a big brown mole on her left cheek, the kids would call her ‘moley’ for laughs. The room we’re in seems to be a Mathematics room, the walls are decorated by various ‘helpful’ posters on using times tables and different equations like Trigonometry and Pythagoras’ Theory. The room has the pupils sitting at double desks and Alex is seated at the back of the room next to Jason, the tall blond haired kid from the picture.- Jason: [whispering and leaning towards Alex] Psst, Alex. -Alex looks to Jason- Jason: Help me solve this equation man. -Alex smiles to himself- No chance. -A disappointed looking Jason reaches into his right hand pocket, he pulls a packet of Fizzy Jerks and sits them on the desk- Jason: Please Alex. Come on. -Alex tries to restrain himself from laughing as not to gain Ms. Hunington’s attention- Jason: What’s so funny? Dammit Alex, you know I’m crap at Maths. Cry me a river. -Jason shakes his head- Jason: That’s��that’s��that’s……well noted you tight fisted little dork. -This time Alex bursts out laughing- Hunington: CAINE!!! ARCHER!!! -She walks towards Alex and Jason’s desk- Hunington: Would you care to share the joke with the rest of the class? -Hunington looks directly at Jason- Jason: Eh, no Ms. He emphasises the ‘Ms.’ -She now turns to Alex- Hunington: Alex? No Miss. Hunington: It’s Ms. To you young man. Fair enough Ms. -Hunington looks straight into Alex’s eyes before drawing back so she can look at both while talking- Hunington: Any more outbursts like this and I’ll see that you both pay a visit to the headmaster AND leave this classroom with extra exercises at the end of the day, are we clear? -Both Alex and Jason agree that their clear with Ms. Hunington.- -The pair go silent for a minute or two, with Alex getting on with his Maths and Jason sitting looking lost- Jason: (He speaks while gritting his teeth together) Alex, theres a packet of Fizzy Jerks in it for ya? They’re already half eaten. (Jason thinks for a second) Jason: No, I mean, Errrrrrrrr, I’ll buy you a fresh packet at lunch time. -Alex nods his head- Ok, you got yourself a deal. -Alex leans over and starts explaining to Jason how to do the work- Hunington: CAINE!!! ARCHER!!! I WARNED YOU BOTH!!! I was only helping him Ms. Hunington: A Likely story indeed young man. Jason: No Ms. He was honestly. Hunington: If your stuck then you ask me. Jason: Yeah I know, but… Hunington: But nothing. Jason: At least let me expla…….. Hunington: Enough of this, principals office now. BOTH OF YOU! -Alex and Jason get up and head for the door and mumble to eachother about how they hate ‘moley’- Male voice: Back the fuck up against the wall and I won’t blow you away. -A tall longhaired man in desperate need of a shave steps into the classroom holding an AK47- -Screams from all the children echo around the school, Alex and Jason throw themselves down behind a plant. -The gunman stands in front of the class and looks out at the class- Gunman: I’m gonna be straight with ya’ll, your all gonna die. (he smiles at one of the children sitting at the front row desks and lifts the gun up to take aim at her) -Ms. Hunington makes a break for the fire alarm in the classroom- -THE ALARM GOES OFF AND PEOPLE START FLOODING THE CORRIDORS AS THE CLASSES BEGIN TO EMPTY- -The gunman turns his attention to Ms. Hunington and fills her with bullets, she staggers back into a wall and slides down it lifelessly leaving a stream of blood behind her- -The children start crying and running about the classroom, frantically trying to get out of the locked door but the gunman simply opens fire and unleashes a fountain of bullets into the kids. Like dominoes they drop to the floor clutching various wounds and spilling blood everywhere, 90percent of them lie dead, the other 10percent are mainly coughing and spluttering their lives away into oblivion- -Alex and Jason though are still tucked away behind the plant and they have been since the whole ordeal started- Gunman: You two, behind the plant, show yourselves. -Alex and Jason stand up and look at the carnage, tears fall trickle down each of their faces- Gunman: If its any consolation your about to join ‘em. Now turn around. -Alex and Jason both turn around- -Sirens can be heard as police cars arrive outside the building Gunman: (The gunman sways his aim as he says the rhyme) eenie meenie minie mo, catch a tigger by the toe, if it squeals let it go, eenie meenie mini MO!!! -He fires the gun and fills Jason with lead, his body shakes back forth as his blood hits the wall in front of him and also splatters over Alex as well. Finally after what seems like an eternity Jason’s body falls to the ground- Gunman: One down, one to go. -Alex is still standing, his knees shaking, his t-shirt and face soaked with Jason’s blood. Tears slowly sliding down his face- Gunman: Bet ya never thought it would end like this, huh? -SMASH!!!!! One of the classroom windows breaks open and the Gunman falls down having taken a bullet to the head, the swat team fly into the room from all angles, one of them goes over to check on a distraught Alex, the man covers Alex’s eyes as he escorts him from the classroom, but Alex’s already seen the carnage, he knows what’s happened- -The scene re-opens at the class picture- The red crosses my compadres represent the lives of my friends that were taken by the powers that be, well the faces of the ones I cared about anyway are the ones crossed out. That day has helped mould me into what I am today. That day stole my innocence, 13 years old and I watched a classroom full of kids in front of me, not to mention standing beside my best friend as he was destroyed by that cowardly bastard. Why he did what he did I’ll never know and probably never understand but from that day on I’ve stood alone, the survivors, four of us there were, where all shifted to another school to be educated but I didn’t fit in, I didn’t want to fit in. I had other goals, other dreams aside from school and also I’d acquired a sever rage which often needed a release, violence was that release, violence is that release. I’m always angry for many reasons, not just that tragic day but for numerous things. So for any of you who think I’m not ready for this hardcore rumble, for those of you who think I’m gonna go running. You’re wrong. Back in 92 I was the last man standing in that classroom, I intend to repeat that feat and be the last man standing on Thursday night. African American Hero, it was nice to hear from you at last, its nice to know that I have your respect, possibly after the match on Thursday night you’ll have mine also, maybe you’ll show something that you didn’t against the Pyro, I ain’t entirely sure but I’m looking forward to facing you anyway, it seems as though your definitely looking to bring ‘it’ to the ring on Thursday night and you better make you sure that you do if you want the PPV spot Johnny Olympus, where can I start? Firstly I don’t have a clue who you are or who your man Hunter Tazi is. I’ve never seen you before in my life and yet you think you know me? It’s possible that you might know me, but from where? So many questions, so little answers. This Thursday I will put your curiosity as rest, I will be at the ring unmasked, so you will see my face and then you’ll know and so I will I whether or not we’ve been acquainted before. As far as your advice goes, thanks but no amount of talent, muscle, numbers, friendship or trust is going to save them from me if any of them get in my way and that includes President John Goodhue. I’m quite happy to let The Firm fairy around the WIA for as long as they wish, but the second any of them stand in the way of my quest to the top then the shit will hit the fan and I will take out anyone and any number of them. It pleases me to hear that you Mr. Olympus would also be willing to talk Hunter into opposing The Firm but like you say, Thursday night is about getting the job done no matter what. And 8 Ball, well well well. That mouth of yours talks helluva big, but all its done is earn you enemies in this match up, there’s no chance you’ll be left standing at the end of the match, Goodhue including you in this match was a big mistake, you’re a dead man. The change to the match favour you the most I think, maybe that's why goodhue did it but I’m gonna have you. Yep, I am, unless of course Tazi or African American Hero get their first and you’d better hope they do because I’m the last fucker in that ring who want a square go with. Miami Missile, 8 Ball, El Vampo, Morbius, Chimera, African American Hero and Hunter Tazi. Come Thursday it you guys who’ll be in the firing line. I will walk away from this match with a spot in the title match at the PPV, whose coming with me? -The scene fades out-
