Please tell me this ISN'T happening!

18 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From the recent OWE card. [BEEP! BEEP! HONK!] FL: Oh man, what now!? LM: Someone got ahold of the PA microphone I think... [HONK HONK! KA-LOOOOOO-HAH!] VO: AHOY KINGERGARDNERS!!!! TIEM TO GET ON TEH BUS FAGORTS!!! # EVERYBODY DANCE NOW ! ! ! [C & C Music Factory takes over the arena as two men strut their stuff down the aisle weilding microphones. One is wearing a leather yellow-black checkered jacket and yellow sun glasses.
The other is wearing a tshirt featuring Miss Frizzle and the Magic School Bus.] FL: I think they're wearing name tags... Mad School Bus... and Mad Taxi...? [Some kid from behind the commentating table steals the headset of Lance Marshall.] SPECAL GEUST COMENTATER: U MEEN MAD TUXI AND ROOL BUSS!!!!1 LOLOLOL CLOWNBOAT!!!!!!1 [Lance takes the headset back.] LM: What the hell is going on?! MAD TAXI: ROLFOL!!!1 BUT U CANT RIED TEH BUS WILLY BECUZ U RIDE
TEH SHORT BUS AND TEHIS IS TEH LONG BUS LEIK TEH HOTDOGS AT KENNYS
NOT LEIK WHITE CASTEL HAHAHAHAROLOLOL EAT A GOBESTOPER!!!!!!!!
MAD SKOOL BUS: HEY WILI I BRUHT U SOEM SLIDERS FORM WHITE CASTEL
OH YUM YUM!!!!!1 ROFLOL SEING AS HOW U WERK THERE AND ALL MABE U
CUOLD TELL US HOW TO MAEK THME TASTE GOOD CUZ TEHT TAST LEIK CRAP!!!!
[Crowd chants R-O-L-R-O-L-O-L-O-L-O-L-R-L-O-L-O-R. Both Willy and Sabbath have seized fighting.]
Frankie: How did they all keep in sync like that? MAD TAXI: TODAY TEH MAGICK SKOOL BUS BROUT U A GIFT!!!1 [ Skool Bus holds up a jar containing a mosquito! ] MAD SKOOL BUS: WITH TEH WEST NILE VIRIS!!1 [ The crowd gasps! ] LM: The West Nile Virus has already claimed dozens of lives throughout the midwest! Brought to this country through Africa, the virus spreads through mosquitos that bite their victims!
Most commonly effected animals include birds and horses, but sometimes humans.. FL: SHUT UP! IM GETTING OUT OF HERE! [Frankie throws down the headset and darts over the guardrail. Nobody else in the audiance seem to care that their health may be at terminal risk. Mad Skool bus runs into the ring, both
Sabbath and Weldon flee.] MAD SKOOL BUS: WHY U RAN FAGORT ROLFOL IST JUST A BUG HAHAHA WILI IS SCARD OF A LADYBUG ROLFOLOL [Crowd chants FAGORT - FAGORT - FAGORT. Skool Bus opens the jar, the mosquito flies out and bites him in the neck.]
LM: That can't be good. MAD TAXI: UHOH FRENDS IT SEMS TAHT SKOOL BUS GOT INFECKTDE WIT TEH AFRICKEN NEIL VIRIS BUT DOTN GET YUR UNDERWEER AL WORKDE UP YET BECUZ TAXI HAS TEH ANTIDOT!!!!1 [Mad Taxi weilds a syringe filled with a thin red sugary substance. He proceeds to enter the ring, and jab the needle into the spot where the bite occured. As soon as he injects the liquid, Skool Bus collapses to the canvas and begins twitching invoulontarily.]
MAD TAXI: OOPS ROFLOL TAHT WAS TEH KOOL AID SHOT ROLFOL KOOLAID MAN IS BIG!!!!!1 [Crowd chants R-O-F-L-O-L-O-L-O-L-O-L. Willy Welden gets an idea and jumps into the ring.] Lance Marshall: Welden has something up his sleeve! Kick to the gut, DDT! DDT to Mad Taxi! [Taxi falls to the canvas head first and takes the hit. Welden grabs him by the hair and begins to place him on the top turnbuckle. ]
LM: Sabbath from behind! [Sabbath grabs Welden from behind and spins him around into a vicious powerbomb! Welden is out cold and Sabbath goes for the pin!]
ONE! TWO! THREE! LM: Sabbath sneaks in from behind and takes the victory! [Frankie Lobeck returns with a hotdog.] FL: What happened? [Welden is furious! He yells at the ref but there is nothing to dispute, it was a clean pin. Somebody drives down the aisle in a go-kart at the same time that Welden focuses his attention back on Taxi.]
FL: Those things are fun. MAD GOKART: IM HEAR TO SAEV U JUMP ON BORED WELL DITHC THESE FAGORTS ROLFOLOL FATE X FOR TEH AOL 3 WILL HAX0R U!!!!!1 [Gokart pulls up, both Taxi and Skool Bus hop on and escape. Sabbath has already left and Welden is still in the ring, angered by the whole situtation. What the hell, right?
ROFLOL.]
