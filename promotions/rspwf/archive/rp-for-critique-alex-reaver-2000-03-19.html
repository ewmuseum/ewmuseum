[RP] For critique - Alex Reaver

19 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys. I just finished this, and I'm very happy with it. For those of you that know me, you know that I try to link everything together with my oldest active character. His history is rich, and while a lot of it was unplanned, I'd like to think I've woven everything that's happened in his fictional life and 3 year career to a logical character development.
Anyways, this is for the next edition of GLCW Latenight. It's a little after the recent PPV, when Reaver beat Sean McKinnon (hi Dave!) after his girlfriend
Chloe, hit Sean with Alex's barbed wire Singapore cane. This after the past few weeks when Chloe had been fed up w/ Alex's recent bitter heel turn and appeared to have been going to Sean for protection/help from the sometimes mentally unstable Reaver. He promptly dumped her, post-match, revealing it to be the first of his revisions of his history. ([1]Alex used to have a hero complex and [2] his previous GF turned on him, by nearly crippling Sean for trying to play the hero and cutting loose his "conscience" and ending things on his terms, he was rewriting his "wrongs")
So, that's the backstory, but really, there's a LOT more, dating back to his fictional upbringing and then his 3 year career dating back to his GFWA days.
Wow, long winded intro. Anyways, here's an even longer winded RP - but worth it, if you're patient. Critiques welcomed. Later, -Zach --- [Fade in: The camera opens to the spacious terminals of an unidentified European airport. The place is fairly busy for a Saturday night. Many grown men in suits bustle by, as well as the occasional family or single woman. The camera is not focused specifically on them, so only the buzz of their conversations are picked up, no real words in any language. The camera searches for a while, trying to locate a terminal, before finally coming to rest on one of the few areas that is barely full. One man stands out among the handful of people in the plastic seats, because he is the only one not sitting in a seat, instead sitting on the floor, legs spread, knees up, with his back against the glass plate window panels that show off the runway view. A large, overstuffed black backpack sits near by the tired looking form of one Alex Reaver. He is attired in a pair of black Converse All-star tennis shoes, baggy light blue carpenter jeans, and a white and blue Quicksilver logo tee shirt. A white Adidas visor rests on his head, backwards, with the red strands of hair parted in the center, hanging over the edge of the visor just slightly. Most of the old black dyed strands are not visible. In his lap, a silver Sony discman lays, with a black wire leading up towards wrap around ear phones. In one hand, Alex holds a red boarding pass and ticket, flicking it with his other hand.]
[Noticing the GLCW camera crew is in position, Alex looks up, rolls his eyes, and removes the earphones, allowing them to hang around his neck. Stroking at his chin with his free hand, Alex tilts his head, squinting up towards the camera. He looks disinterested.]
AR: I suppose you're after some trivial sound bite? [He nods, answering the rhetorical question silently to himself.] AR: For the first time in the longest while, I have a sense of freedom. In the past, I have always had these limitations to contend with, these boundaries
I could never go past. Something was always holding me back. Be it fear, guilt, confusion - whatever. I could never fully be myself. For the longest time, I tried to be who I thought the masses wanted to me. I finally stopped playing that game upon my return. But even then, I was not completely free.
I had a sympathetic, doe eyed angel acting as my ball and chain. Oh, sure, she meant well and all... [His voice trails off for a moment before he just shakes his head, almost smiling.] AR: But I just needed to be free. To be able to say f[BLEEP] the limitations, f[BLEEP] the boundaries, f[BLEEP] the perceptions of what I can and cannot do... to give the finger to the world... and I did.
Finally, I am happy. I am free to waste away, to make my nightmares the world's problems, to use my bitterness and resentment to the cards I have been dealt against all that cross my path. It's beautiful, really. A sick sort of beautiful, but that doesn't diminish it. It's like the music made of bones being broken and grown men crying like babies... it's like the art created by the bloody messes on the canvas that resemble ink blots... it's not conventional, no, but it's real.
It's more real than real. [Alex stops, sighing. He pauses, seemingly shifting gears.] AR: I was fired from the UWF in late December. I f[BLEEP]ed up. Guaranteed money, big plans, the whole deal. Some people said it was because I couldn't handle the pressure. They thought I just had some sort of nervous breakdown and collapsed from the stress. [shrugs] Maybe that's true. The last therapist I went to gave me that bullshit, too. But you know what I think it was? My breakdown was a plea from my soul... a desperate cry to stop living in a sugar coated, plastic wrap fantasy land.
I heard the message loud and clear. Maybe to others, it looked like the latest personal trauma in my life. Another crash and burn, typical Alex Reaver. But it wasn't. It wasn't. It was the part of me I thought had died.
But really, it was the part that never got the chance to breathe. [Alex stops. He pauses, trying to think of his words. He appears to finally be interested in talking.]
AR: People think I'm delusional, I guess. It's f[BLEEP]ing wrong, because for the first time, I'm thinking crystal clear. Some say I've finally gone over the edge... lost my grip on things... I mean, here I am, bloodthirsty, wielding a Singapore cane at every match, masterminding a giant mind f[BLEEP] on both
Sean McKinnon and Chloe... but if they stopped to think for just one goddamn second, they'd realize that everything makes sense - everything.
So, when I had my quote unquote breakdown, I finally began to live. You see, I made a New Year's Resolution this year. Normally, I don't - they're just empty promises, something I despise.
But this - this would not be an empty promise. I made a promise to myself that night - a silent vow. I would not be a victim. [He pauses, gritting his teeth.] AR: Never, ever again. [For a moment, Reaver's calm, subdued attitude shifts back into the intense, shaky, aggressive one he had been in since his GLCW return. His voice also gets louder.]
AR: Never again would I let myself be the fall guy! Never again would I be the scapegoat! Never again would I let myself care what anybody - anybody - thought about me! No - no - that was the past. The past is dead.
But I'm not. I'm alive. More alive than I've ever been. Because you see, my eyes have finally been opened. Only one thing brings me joy - only one thing brings me happiness - only one thing can make me smile...
The beauty of pain. [Alex stops, laughing softly to himself. He relaxes, closing his eyes.] AR: When I left last September, I said I didn't have anything left to prove. But I was wrong. I had a lot to prove. But not to the people - not to the fans, the critics, other wrestlers, or Andy Wilczak. [opens eyes] To myself.
That was what the realization was all about - I was given a second chance. I could erase my mistakes from before, if I only cut loose the last strings that tied me down.
And thus the example was made of Sean McKinnon. I made the mistake of trying to be a hero. So did he. I showed him the error of our ways.
And Chloe, well, I explained it before. I made the mistake of letting my first love ruin me when she ended up a gold digging back stabber. This time, I would be the one to end things - on my terms. It also left me without a conscience - free at last.
So, you see, I'm already fixing the past. I'm not done. Not by a long shot. So many things... so many mistakes... so much pain to share with the world. [Pause. Reaver stops, biting his lip. Then, a smile. He turns up towards the camera, as if he's realized the punch line to a joke.]
AR: I'm like some sort of goddamn vigilante... in a twisted sense, of course. I'm on a mission to resurrect my burnt out soul. Instead of bringing justice,
I'm a harbinger of violence. I'm like a musician, and screams of the world when they see my pain are like my symphony... like an artist, and the blood of the wounded is my paint... and I aspire to show the masses the nightmares that
was my life. Was - past tense. Alex Reaver, the victim, died. The martyr of the damned has ceased to exist. Now, all that remains is the real Alex Reaver... the desires to hurt, the angst at the world, the unstability of a sociopath... just pure, raw emotion.. and the best part is... there's not a goddamn thing holding me back anymore.
[He sighs, crossing his arms across his chest.] AR: F[BLEEP]ing beautiful, isn't it? [Alex rocks a little in his seated position, then places the earphones back on, closes his eyes, and slips back into his subliminal state of mind. The camera lingers for a second, but then slowly backs off. Fade to black.]
