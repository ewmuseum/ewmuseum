[FLASH] Sea-bass-chun

6 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Instead of talking about some random, meaningless poll or "Hall of Fame" or whatever (which I will *not* vote upon, by the way), I am actually going to post a roleplay. Am I asking for a critiquement? No. Am I asking for advice, help, or a flame? No.
I am merely posting something that is actually supposed to be on the newsgroup. To my knowledge, flashes, cards, and genuine e-wrestling discussions were intended to be on the board. However, those seem to be quite rare, as of late.
Anyhow, here it is. Enjoy it... hate it... whatever. At least it's better than, "BRODY THUNDER'S BALLS", right? Well... maybe not. :) -- [The red, white and blue... the glory of this nation is seen blowing in the wind as the camera fades in. It's just another replica of Betsy
Ross' original, seen atop a flagpole as the cloth ripples back and forth in the constant breeze.]
[The camera then cuts to a still, black and white photograph of yet another American flag. As the picture ascends, this one is seen on a flagpole as well, stuck into the ground... of the moon. An astronaut is seen in the picture, posing for the photo while holding the flagpole in his right hand. The camera draws back still, revealing a gold plaque underneath. A hand graces the bronzed faceplate, as a man is seen next to the famous snapsot -- current WWO superstar, Mike
Sebastian. Dressed in a pair of khaki shorts and a white Polo shirt, the former Television champion flashes his trademark arrogant grin at the camera and turns his attention towards the picture.]
MS: On July 20, 1969, the world changed. With this man, [pointing to the photograph] setting foot on the moon's surface, families all across America had their eyes glued to the television like never before. It brought tears of astonishment and pride all throughout the nation... as the unthinkable had happened. Man had walked on the moon.
And twenty years later, the unthinkable happened again. [Mike smirks, as he glances at the picture and back at the camera.] MS: No, I'm not talking about some NASA space shuttle launch, even though that launch in the summer of '69 is still talked about today. Why? Because it was the first time it had been done.
Since that time, there have been a slew of people to accomplish that feat. It's no big thing, nowadays. Now it just seems like a run down to the market for the space program. "Run down to the moon and pick up some moon sediment", NASA might say. Heh, but
I'm not interested in that. It's not why I came out here. Because see, everybody remembers who the first was. Neil Armstrong -- first man to walk on the moon. It's stuck in their heads like how to ride a bicycle. You just don't forget something like that. But the rest? The second, the thirds, and so on and so forth? They're just, well... forgotten.
As will you be, Tyrone Hayes. [Crossing his arms across his chest, Sebastian continues.] MS: Everybody in the world is going to remember that Mike Sebastian was the first man to defeat Roundhouse. Anybody after that is just following the trend _I_ set, including you. Yeah, people may remember it now, cause it just happened... but a few years down the road? Eh-eh. No way, Jose.
Remembering me being the first to take down Roundhouse is just one of the feats they'll remember about Mike Sebastian. Beating the holy hell out of you this Tuesday night will be just another one,
Hayes. And hey, while I'm at it, you can throw around all the 'lil homosexual, food condiment references you want at me, big man. The fact is, if you want to get cute and comical with everybody, that's none of my damn business, nor is it any of my concern. I know, come Showcase... my ass ain't gonna be the one handed to me when the night's over.
[Sebastian nods in approval, cracking his knuckles in the process.] MS: And if you want to say I haven't faced any "worthy" competition and that I don't know what the hell I want, that's fine, too.
All I'll say is that I beat the tar out of Roundhouse, the man _you_ couldn't beat, without losing to him yourself! How's _that_ for competition... [quotation marks with his fingers] "Purple One"?
Which, if you think about it, isn't the color I would be wearing if I was walking around talking about homosexuals and kinky mayonnaise fetishes.
[The patented smirk appears.] MS: But, like I said, that's none of my business. The only business I'm concerned with is the one surrounding this title tournament. You and Todd "The Rod"... another thing I wouldn't say any higher than a whisper... can have all the fun and games you'd like,
Hayes. The fact is, you say I'm not "at your level" yet? Please. The only thing you've done that I haven't is lose to the big ogre. Believe me, Ty... [Sebastian looks off camera quickly.] MS: HEY! Get those damn Beanie Babies outta here! [The smirk returns as Sebastian turns his attention back towards the camera.] MS: I'm not gonna let you forget that one. And as soon as the Showcase has come and gone, I'm not gonna let you soon forget that you're ohfer against moi...
So, if you've got any qualms about what my intentions are, or "what I want" now, Hayes, I suggest you take some of this and buy yourself a fu[BLEEP]ing clue, pal.
[Sebastian flaunts a wad of hundreds, which he has taken out of his shorts pocket.] MS: Nevertheless, this Tuesday night, Hayes... [Sebastian slaps a hand across the Neil Armstrong picture.] MS: I'm taking one small step for man... [Mike, with an obvious half-smile, half-smirk.] MS: ... and one giant leap for my career.
