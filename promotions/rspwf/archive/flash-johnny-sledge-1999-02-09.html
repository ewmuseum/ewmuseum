[Flash] Johnny Sledge

9 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up with some crappy band music. You see that it is Max
Weinberg and the Max Weinberg Seven playing. The camera pans across the studio of "Late Night," and when it comes to the main desk, you see
Conan
O'Brien sitting behind the desk and his side kick, Andy Richter. The two are sitting there, trying to hold of laughter, and when the music ends
The
two begin to talk.]
O'Brien: Wow Andy, that had to be by far the funniest bit we have ever done. I've never laughed so hard in my life.
Richer: I know Conan, the people that were lucky enough to have seen that will be able to sleep a little better tonight.
O'Brien: And those that missed it will never have the chance to see that skit ever again. Richter: Uh... well there's always a rerun. O'Brien: Yeah, and maybe if we have another one of those Prime Time
Specials, but other than that there is no way you will ever be able to see that skit again.
Richter: I guess you're right on that one Conan. O'Brien: I know, that's why they pay me the big bucks. Richter: They pay you? O'Brien: Yeah the best artificial meat and dairy products money can buy. Richter: That's a sweet deal. O'Brien: You can say that again. Richter: That's a sweet deal. [Conan gives a dumb look to the camera and begins to laugh.] O'Brien: Ok, our next guest... [the audience begins to cheer before he even comes out] seems to have a lot of his fans here.
[The audience cheers loudly again.] O'Brien: He currently wrestles in the hottest federation on the planet, the Extreme Wrestling Association. He is the currently the EWA North
American Champion, the Number one seed in the tournament to crown an new
EWA Global Hardcore Champion, and is even the number one seed to crown and
EWWF Undisputed Champion of the World... here is... The One... The
Only... The Original... Johnny Sledge. [The band begins to play an instrumental version of Faith no More's
"Epic." The seemingly pro-Sledge audience is now screaming wildly as he comes out from the back of the stage. Sledge is wearing a pair of black
Dockers, black shoes, and a black sport coat with a red EWA mock turtle neck shirt underneath. Sledge is carrying the EWA North American Title with him. He is walking with an obvious limp, and appears to still be favoring his left arm. Sledge no longer has a bandage on his head, but you can see what's left of the scar that he got from the ladder match.
When Sledge gets to where Conan O'Brien and Andy Richter are, he is first greeted by Richter. The two shake hands, and then he shakes hands with
O'Brien. Conan taps Sledge on the left shoulder and Sledge winces in pain. Conan sees this and you can make out that he is saying I'm sorry.
Sledge just nods it off, and all three men sit down for the interview.] O'Brien: Johnny it's good to have you here on the show. Sledge: Thanks Conan, it's an honor to be here. O'Brien: That's a first... somebody actually being glad they are here. Sledge: hehehehe... Really I watch your show all the time. It's the only thing that's on that late.
Richter: Yeah we try our best. Sledge: That's good to hear. O'Brien: Well Johnny you seem to be quite popular with the audience here tonight... [the audience cheers] have you gotten used to all this attention that you have been getting lately?
Sledge: It's really weird Conan, when I started out it was a miracle if
I
had one person ask me for an autograph a week, now I can bearly go out without being swarmed with people around me. At first I was a bit overwhelmed, but now I'm actually starting to enjoy it. It's fun being in the spotlight... especially in the wrestling industry, where you're popularity can disappear overnight.
O'Brien: But being as popular as you now are must have some set backs, right? Sledge: Yeah, I guess. I don't have as much privacy as I used to have. My wife and I never seem to have time for just the two of us anymore, which does suck... oh can I say suck?
Richter: Why not, this shows on so late the seven words that your not supposed to say is only down to four at this time.
Sledge: hehehehe... Anyway, it's gets tough sometimes, but I remember that this is what I have been working for my whole career, and now I finally have it.
O'Brien: You sure seem to be at the top of your game. Those few accolades I mentioned in your intro are just the most recent of a pretty illustrious career for you right?
Sledge: I'm not one to brag but... yes I have accomplished a lot in my career. Through hard work and a "Never Say Die" attitude I have been able to outlast all of the hardships and obstacles that I have been faced with to get to the point where I am at now. Especially within the last two to three months though is where I have reached the pinnacle of what I have been trying to achieve.
O'Brien: I said that you are the number one seed in two tournaments right now. Can you explain to the people that don't wrestling what these two tournaments are for?
Sledge: Certainly! Well the one for the EWA is for it's vacant Global
Hardcore Title. This belt is the equivilant of some other federation's
World Title. There is a field of sixteen of the top stars of the EWA, and at the next PPV, which is called Death From Above, the new champion will be crowned. I hope that the person who wins that title will be me.
O'Brien: Now the belt that you have now is the... [Sledge holds up the EWA North American Belt so the camera gets a good shot of it.] Sledge: It's the EWA North American Title. I won this at the January
EWA PPV, Yuletide Beatings. I beat a wrestler by the name of the
Irish Rebel to take this belt. Since then I have successfully defended it numerous times all of the United States and Canada. The most recently at the last EWA PPV, Arctic Fury. If you can still see it now, that's where
I got this scar on my forehead. O'Brien: Yeah I saw that match, you and that other guy... Tommy Grayson... virtually killed one another. How many tables did you end up putting him through?
Sledge: Two, but trust me, they hurt me as much, if not more than, him. But that's not the point... We fought hard and the better man that night won, luckily that man was me.
O'Brien: What a great segway to our clip we have for you. This is the end of your match with Tommy Grayson where you retained the EWA North
American Title.
[Sledge slowly starts to climb but you can see that he is exhausted. Grayson somehow pulls himself to his feet. He sets up the second table and then starts climbing up the opposite side of the ladder from Sledge.
Both men are nearing the top. Sledge slams Grayson's head into the top of the ladder. What the hell is Sledge doing? He climbs around. SLEDGE
LIFTS UP GRAYSON FOR THE WRECKING BALL. They both go through the table.
Both men are covered on blood. The referee looks on wondering if he should stop the match.
Sledge somehow starts to roll over to his side. He pulls himself up with the ladder. Slowly rung by rung Sledge is going to the top. He is almost there. Honey is screaming for Grayson to get up. Grayson somehow wills himself to his feet but Sledge is at the top. Grayson begins to shake the ladder. Sledge has a finger on the belt. Grayson is rocking the ladder. Sledge almost has it. Grayson dips his shoulder and the ladder starts to tip but Sledge uses his last remaining ounce of strength. He grabs the belt. The ladder falls but the belt comes away.
HE GOT IT!!! Sledge falls to the ground but holding the North American title. Grayson collapses from exhaustion as the referee goes over and raises the semi-conscious arm of Johnny sledge.]
Richter: Damn, you must've been dead after that match? Sledge: You couldn't believe how bad I felt after that match. My entire body felt as if it was hit by a truck.
O'Brien: Now what I don't understand is that you litterly fought for you life for that title but you are going after another one, why are you doing that?
Sledge: That's easy... even though I am greatful to be the North American Champion, my dream since the day I started in the EWA ten months ago was to become the top man there. I am almost there now, and by winning that title I will finally reach my dream.
O'Brien: I hear stories sometimes wrestlers can have very grueling work schedules, is that the case for you?
Sledge: Most definitely! Being a champion you're expected to defend your title, but I defend it more than any other champion before me, because I promised not to be a closet champion. I have even accepted challenges from guys who have barely been in the EWA, just to prove that
I
will never back down from a challenge.
To say, though, that it doesn't get to me is a vast understatement. I would love to just be able to sleep late some mornings, but I know I can't take the time off just yet. Hopefully down the road, I will be able to take some time off, but I'm cool with it right now.
O'Brien: And looking at that clip of your match, as well as most of the matches I've seen of yours, don't you get any injuries at all? I mean when I...
Sledge: If you don't mind I would really appreciate it if you didn't talk about injuries at all.
O'Brien: If you say so... So, Johnny, you didn't mention the other tournament that you are currently in, that one is for the EWWF. Does that have anything to do with the EWA?
Sledge: Yes it does. The EWA is actually part of a huge alliance of
Federations from all over the United States, and some International
Federations. Every year the EWWF Alliance holds a tournament to crown it's Undisputed Champion of the World. The EWA actually had the 1998 winner in it's roster, Cyris. He was the man who held the EWA Global
Hardcore Title last.
Richter: So he was just a little good. Sledge: You could say that. Yeah so this year is the first time the winner of the tournament will remain the Champion and defend it. There is a field of only eight for this one, and I had to win a battle royal to become the number one seed for this tournament.
O'Brien: You seem to be very proud of where you are right now in your career... Sledge: Very much so. O'Brien: Is there anything that you are hoping to do in the future? Sledge: Well, winning the these two tournament's I'm in are my number one priority, but I am always looking ahead. I own a gym/wrestling school, and I've actually had one of my students come out of the school and is now beginning to have a successful wrestling career.
O'Brein: And who might that be? Sledge: He had promised me to keep that a secret, he doesn't want to get anything just because he was trained by me. He said he wants to do it on his own.
I also hear a lot of times when wrestlers retire they just go nuts, and end up coming right back. I see this with a lot of the old timers that were around when I was just starting out...
O'Brien: Do you think that they are taking away from the younger talent of the wrestling world?
Sledge: Not in the least. These guys were the men who enstilled upon the newer generation the desire to enter the sport. The only thing I hate seeing is when they stay in it too long, and their name gets dragged through the mud before they go.
Richter: That's a very commendable thing to say Johnny. So I take it you understand your roots.
Sledge: Yup, I know that if it wasn't for the generation before me, those stands would be empty, and I wouldn't be on this show. You never hear many thanks to the guys before us, but they deserver more credit than anybody else. They are the one's who brought us here.
O'Brien: Johnny, you mentioned a wrestling school, besides that do you plan on staying in wrestling after you retire?
Sledge: Hopefully, like I said before, it's hard for a wrestler to leave the sport. Maybe when I'm done, the EWA will give me a nice cushy job as a talent scout or something. I know a lot of the execs now, all former wrestlers themselves... some I have even faced in the ring.
O'Brein: That must be weird, you're trying to maim each other one minute... and the next he's signing your paycheck.
Sledge: Not really, that's the way it is in wrestling now. What a better person to run the show than a former wrestler.
O'Brein: Didn't you even feud with the current President of the EWA,
Wayne Rothschild, before he was injured and became an executive himself.
Sledge: Yes we did, and for a while at that. We only had one match against one another and he beat me. I joke with him now, saying if he ever gets out of the wheel chair, I am going to ask for a rematch.
Richter: That would probably be a match of the century, huh Johnny? Sledge: Probably Andy. O'Brein: Most wrestlers spend a lot of time away from their family, but that's not really the case for you, is it?
Sledge: Nope, my wife, Jessica, comes with me for most of my matches. Sometimes the stuff that happens to me bothers her, but she understands that this was what I have always wanted to do.
O'Brein: And isn't she here? Sledge: Yeah, you want her to come out and say hello? [The audience screams "yes."] O'Brein: Ok, so here is Jessica. [The band plays "Epic" again and Jessica comes out. She is wearing a lovely red sequin evening gown. She comes over and sits next to
Sledge.] O'Brein: It's nice to have you on the show. Jessica: Thank you. O'Brein: So why do you let Johnny kill himself like that? Jessica: Like he said, he's always wanted to do it and I am not about to stop him. O'Brein: How long have the two of you known each other? Jessica & Sledge: Since Highschool. Richter: Wow, did you plan that? Jessica: No, we just get that question a lot. O'Brein: Jessica, do you have anything to add to what your husband has had to say. Jessica: Nothing much, other than he is the hardest working man in the sport today, and deserves every accolade and honor that he has, and will, receive.
O'Brein: Oh, how sweet. Well, I'd love to continue to talk but I am being told by the director over there that we have to cut to commercial.
Johnny you are welcome on here any time, and next time have Jessica come out sooner so we can talk to her more.
Sledge: Not a problem Conan, and it's been a pleasure. O'Brein: Johnny Sledge ladies and gentlemen. [the audience applauds] We'll be right back with the comedic performance of Carrot Top. [The camera begins to pull back and Conan and Sledge say some things and shake hands. Fade to black.]
