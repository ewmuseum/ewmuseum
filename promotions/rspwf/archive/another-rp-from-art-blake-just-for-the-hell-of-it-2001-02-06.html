Another RP from Art Blake, just for the hell of it...

6 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade in to a rather messy locker room. Clothes and towels are strewn willy-nilly about the room, tables and chairs are tipped over, empty jugs of mango juice are piled up in one corner, the place is just a disaster. Art
Blake walks into the room from the shower area, a towel wrapped around his head, another around his waist. He turns a bench upright, and sits, elbows on his knees, face buried in his hands.]
Art: "What have I done?" [He looks up, momentarily, at the one chair in the room that looks to have been spared by the tornado that ripped through the room previously, then turns away, almost in tears.]
"How could I have let it get this bad? I'm usually better than this. It's just not right."
[He jumps to his feet and points accusingly at the chair, a look of anger and rage upon his face.]
"WHAT HAVE YOU DONE TO ME YOU FOUL TEMPTRESS?!" [He falls back onto the bench, and buries his face in his hands again, sobbing.] "It doesn't make any sense. I did everything you asked of me, and still." [He looks up at the chair again, then shakes his head in shame.] ".and still you sit there, mocking me with your silence. Not even the Gods can help me now."
[He stands and grabs a pair of blue jeans, a dark blue t-shirt, and a black baseball cap out of the locker nearest him, and walks back toward the shower area. He returns a few moments later wearing said clothing, returns to the bench, and sits near the locker. He pulls a pair of socks out of the locker and puts them on his feet before pulling a pair of dark blue Doc Martens out of the locker. As he puts on the boots he occasionally glances up at the chair spitefully. Once he has both boots on, he stands and takes a large olive drab green, military style canvas duffle bag, the big kind, from the locker and starts filling it with his personal items. The bag fills quickly, and before long he is closing the bag. He pulls his trench coat out of the locker, it having been returned by Lenny earlier in the day, and puts it on. From out of an inside pocket he gets his sunglasses, and places them on his face. After a final look over at the chair, he lifts the duffle bag and hefts it over his shoulder. He turns to the chair and smiles wryly.]
"It's time to go." [He reaches down and picks up the US Title off the chair, and tucks it securely under his arm, as he would a book, and walks toward the door of the locker room. He opens it and steps out into the hall. Several members of the ring crew step out of his way as he walks down the hall. His sister,
Thighs of Death, runs down the hall towards him, a frightening sight indeed.]
T.o.D.: "Art." [She trots closer.] T.o.D.: "Art.are you alright? You seemed kind of." [He brushes past her without so much as a word, or look in her direction. She stands for a moment, staring at him as he walks away, shocked by his actions, or lack there of. She hurries to catch up to him.]
T.o.D.: "What's wrong? Where are you going?" [He continues walking, ignoring her, and everyone else on his path. All eyes are on him as he pushes open a pair of metal fire doors, and walks out into the frozen night. A small crowd gathers around the open doors, watching as he disappears into the distance. They share confused looks as they look at each other for some explanation. When he is finally out of sight, the crowd begins to disperse, all except for Thighs of Death. A tear rolls down her cheek as she finally admits to herself that he's not coming back. She steps back from the doors, allowing them to close silently. Fade out.]
