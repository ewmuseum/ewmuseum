[FREE AGENT] Jason "The Bandit" Blake

3 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am looking for a good fed to join as my latest endeavour has just closed it's doors. So any information you may need is at.....
http://www.angelfire.com/mb/dowjones/blake.html Any interested feds can email me at [detail withheld] but I am looking for the following in a fed... 1) Email Flash Packets 2) At least 15 people on the roster 3) Has been open for at least a month 4) Not a hardcore federation (Having hardcore title is okay) Here is a sample roleplay... -------------------------------------------- (The scene is set in the Expo Square Pavilion in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Jason
Blake is sitting at a table signing autographs for a large group of fans standing in
line. He is wearing a pair of khakis and a silk shirt and has the NAWC
Television Title around his waist. From around the corner, Gregory
Hollinsworth appears and approaches the table. He taps Jason on the shoulder and is met with an intense stare.)
Jason Blake - Can I help you Greg? Greg - I was hoping you could spare a few minutes to say a few words about your upcoming match.
Jason Blake - I am kind of busy right now. Do you think you can come back when all of these people have got what they came here for. I've been signing for the past hour and a half and I would like to be sure they all go home happy.
Greg - Sure, not a problem. Jason Blake - Thanks. I think the fans here will appreciate it. (The scene fades into another scene, showing Jason Blake at the same table, but all the people have left. Greg goes up to him again.)
Greg - You sure have a lot of fans nowadays. It must be nice. Jason Blake - Well since the former Heavyweight Champion bailed out on all his fans, and Rick Stevens is nothing but a chump who needs the owner's help, these kids need someone to look up to. I find it an honour to be looked so highly upon. It's a shame that too many guys on this roster still underestimate me.
Greg - Well you have beaten many of the best wrestlers in this company. Jason Blake - Exactly. But it all means nothing right now. I don't care much about the past. I am looking towards Friday. I will probably be facing one of the best new talents here in Ben Tuckwell. I know he's far from being a rookie and he is very skilled. And most importantly, he earned what he's getting, just like I did at Nuclear Winter almost three months ago. That's right, I have been the NAWC Television Champion for almost three months. And everyone knows who was the unfortunate soul who lost this title to me. So I expect a gruelling battle, but as always I am confident of achieving victory.
Greg - So where is Amy today? Jason Blake - She's out doing her own thing. I'm not her owner. She's probably working out or something. She has her first Women's title defence on Friday and she is looking forward to it.
Greg - I see. So what is this relationship that exists between Dow Jones and yourself? A lot of people are wondering as he mentions your name quite a lot.
Jason Blake - Well you see, Dow and I attended college together, and we've been friends since then. He's the one who broke me into professional wrestling. We've always been best of friends since college, but he has a tendency of taking business seriously, and the friendships stay out of the ring. I totally respect that. He is probably the one man I fear most in the ring. He knows how to win. In feds I've slumped in, he's dominated.
He has been successful everywhere he's gone, and he doesn't intend on stopping here. But he knows our paths will probably cross eventually. I personally think he's a little jealous that I'm higher up on the ladder than him. He's always felt superior over me, but now it's time to prove myself to him and the entire world. So I have a little propostion for him.
Greg - And what may that be? Jason Blake - Well provided I win on Friday, which there is a good chance of happening, I will defend my title against Dow Jones. But only if he wins next week on Napalm. If he loses, then he gets absolutely nothing, and he'll have to prove himself worthy all over again.
Greg - But what if he has an easy opponent? Jason Blake - Well I won't let that happen. I have been doing some scouting of my own, and I know someone who can pose a threat to him. So
Dow will face an opponent of my choosing.
Greg - And who will that be? Jason Blake - Well I think that he needs someone that can be a lot like him. I had some contacts in another fed and I think that Copycat will be formidable competition for the Blue Chip Investment.
Greg - But what if Dow refuses this matchup? Jason Blake - Then he can kiss this title shot goodbye. It's that simple. But if you'll excuse me, I have a match to get ready for on Friday. I have a strict training schedule to keep up.
Greg - Well I won't keep you any longer. (Jason Blake leaves the scene and you can hear the arena door closing behind him.) Greg - Well there you have it. Will Dow Jones accept the match with Copycat? I'll be sure to get you all the news as soon as it happens. This is Gregory Hollinsworth, signing off.
(The screen fades to black.)
