WWO - Tanke Brute Roleplay for the Young Gunz Battle Royal.

24 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, my name is Nick, and I control an e-wrestler named "The Pitbull" Tanke
Brute. Here is a roleplay that I am using for WWO's "Young Gunz" Battle Royal.
I'd _greatly_ appreciate it if I could get responses to this. If you'd like more info on Tanke, I'd be more than happy to inform anyone. Thanks!
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [Scene opens to a snowy, fuzzy, black and white screen. In the background, sounds of bombs and missles exploding can be heard. Finally, the camera changes to a shot of a bomb dropping. As it hits the ground, a mushroom cloud appears, as "SuperBeast" by Rob Zombie begins to blare. The camera then switches shots from the mushroom cloud, to small animations of Tanke Brute
_BLASTING_ a man in the head. Then another shot of Tanke using his _DEADLY_
Rolling Armbar, and dislocating a mans shoulder and wrist. Then some more shots of Tanke in bouts show as "SuperBeast" begins to fade.]
[The scene opens back up in what appears to be a gymnasium. Not much light is coming through the old windows that hang high on the wall. Finally, the camera begins to move down, untill what appears to be a cage is in front of the camera. The camera moves in, untill a figure is seen standing inside the cage like structure. Then, the lights shoot on, and Tanke Brute is seen standing inside of an old octagon. His standing on a logo that reads, "UFC The ULTIMATE
Ultimate". He seems to be deep in thaught. He then turns his head and looks at the camera. He then begins speaking.]
[TANKE]: "I've had some great memories here..." [The shot then shows a still of Tanke on top of a man, getting ready to palm a man in the head.]
[TANKE]: "Unfortunetly, my career was cut short here..." [Another shot of Tanke, this time, being hit by a _THUNDEROUS_ right hand, directly to the temple.]
[TANKE]: "That's why I've ventured to the WWO. Here, I can continue my dominating ways. At Road Warriors, I took out Tee Tee easily. I threw a couple of shots, and DVD'ed his ass to the mat. No questions asked. But now,
I go into something bigger than a Road Warriors spot."
[Tanke begins to walk around, admiring the mat he stands on.] [TANKE]: "Now, I go into Unfinished Bussiness. Here, I enter the "Young Gunz" Battle Royal. I see some talent in there, but none that could match up to my skill in the ring. I see a lot of pretty boys entering this Battle Royal.
First, there's the schmuck known as Alex Star. I seen his little thing on
Prime Time. Alex, there is no way in _hell_ I am letting you win this Battle
Royal. This thing is _MINE_. It has _MY_ name written on it. Star, when me and you come face to face in that ring, prepare to get hurt. Prepare to feel pain..."
"... Now, there are more people that talk a good game, but can't back it up inside the squared circle. Alan Reed, Mad Goat, Daniel Howard, That Harrison schmuck. They all can go on TV and say to the fans that they will see them come out victorious at the Young Gunz Battle Royal. But, they are _so_ wrong.
They don't call it Young Gunz for nothing people, and I, I will be the only young gun in this thing, and I will win it."
[He then paces around again, cracks his neck, and looks back into the camera.] [TANKE]: "People, I am going to win this, and I'll do it anyway I can. Wether it be pin, submission, or throwing _every_ one of you over the top, I will win. WWO.. Remember one thing.. _THE_PITBULL_CALLS_THE_SHOTS_!"
[His glare continues into the camera, as it slowly fades to a snowy screen again.] [End Transmission.] =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- Well, that's it, I'd love to hear responses! =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [name withheld] AIM: [detail withheld] Handler Of: "The Pitbull" Tanke Brute Found In: [WWO and AWA] AWA United States Champion =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- -----------== Posted via Deja News, The Discussion Network ==---------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Discuss, or Start Your Own
