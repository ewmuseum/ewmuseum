Wrestler Information Wrestler's name: Cade Dakota Height : 6'7 Weight : 290 Age : 27 Hometown : Detroit, Michigan Anything else : -- Ring Entrance ["When World's Collide" by Powerman 5000 cues up. Blue and white pyros shoot off all over the entrance as Cade Dakota steps out from behind the curtain. He is wearing white wrestling boots, white knee-pads, blue latex wrestling shorts with thick white lines down the sides and a white wife-beater. His hair is short, spiked and white. He begins to jog down the aisle as the pyros die down and he slaps a bunch of fans' hands. As he gets to the ring he slides in and pops off which sends the ring pyros into a fury of blue and white pyros and the crowd is giving off a huge face reaction. The pyros die down along with "When
World's Collide".]
Alignment :Face Appearance :Fairly well built, 2 earrings, left eyebrow pierced, blue or white hair(Changes every week), blue eyes.
Personality :Believes in the art of wrestling, not the entertainment side. Loves having the backing of the fans and uses that as energy. Gimmick :Good ol' American face. Believes in the crowd giving off pure energy that he uses in the ring.
Wrestling Style : Technical Trademark Moves :Gord-Buster, STF, Reverse-neckbreaker and a whole arsenal of suplexes. Finisher : "Frost-Bite" description : Reverse Implant DDT(Gangrel's Finisher
Reversed)
