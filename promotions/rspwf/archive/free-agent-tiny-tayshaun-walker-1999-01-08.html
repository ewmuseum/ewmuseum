<Free Agent> "Tiny" Tayshaun Walker

8 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A brand new Dodge Viper pulls up to a riverbank and stops. Two men get out, they are Rich Armani and Tayshaun Walker. They look over the water.
Tayshaun is wearing a blue silk suit and a pair of designer shades, and
Rich Armani is wearing jeans and a white t-shirt. They set down on the river bank and begin throwing rocks into the river. )
Rich Armani: You know, it's unbelievable. I am having tons of sucees, and I am managing a true champion all at the expense of my brothers death. And your damn right I'd give it all up to have Ed back, but I know that's not happening and it's time to move on. It's time to move onto the next battle between Tayshaun has a match with Phoenix X, the greatest of the greats. We know what Phoenix X can do, and he can do a lot. Mr. Walker is prepared for that, he is prepared for anything and everything. You know in the AWA anything can and has happened. You have to be prepared for what can happen.
You know what I'm saying. It's like things change at the snap of your fingers. Nothing stays the sam in the AWA, nothing, except for the fact that no matter what you do, you can't hold the Elite Extremists down. Hell no, it ain't happening. See, I've went back to my roots, I have put my feet on the ground and I am focusing on Tayshaun and my future in the AWA. AWA,
I'm going at it 100 % so get ready for a revolution, extreme style.
Tayshaun Walker: Rich your right, nothing remains the same very long in this sport. Look at Kris Warren, or as you guys called him Clockwerx. Look at Ed, Triple B, I mean things are changin'. And the next thing to change is the AWA World Title, it's coming to me baby. It's gonna be mine, ya heard me? I ain't gonna fool around in da ring, hell no. I's smarter than that. I know not to mess around with Phoenix X, you gots to go straight for the kill. It's that simple. The plan is simple, but to execute the plan, I know it's gonna be hard.
Rich Armani: Hey Tay, Tay. Hold up. Phoneix X, I have to tell you that I am really sorry for what happened to Jade, I know how you feel, and after you and Tiny duke it out, let's go get those bastards. You know who I am talking about, those damn Shooters.
Tayshaun Walker: Phoenix X, think about what Rich said, but also think about what my ass is about to say. Look in my eyes boy. What do you see?
Fear? No. Worry? No. You see determination, thats what you see. You know I am ready for you, don't you? You can feel it, you can detect it. Well feel this, you are now looking in the eyes of the man that is taking yo strap from ya. I know you like wearing the gold around here, but I'm here to rain on your parade, and when it rains it pours sucka.
(Tayshaun stands up and dust off his backside. Rich is staring into the water. He rolls in and goes under deep. Tayshaun stands there for awhile.
About a minute has passed and Rich hasn't came back up. Tayshaun dives down and about ten seconds later comes up with Rich. Rich is motionless.)
Tayshaun Walker: WHAT THE HELL??!!! WHY'D YOU DO THAT??!! RICH??!! RICH??!! OH HELL NO!!!!! (Tayshaun puts Rich in the car and drives off very fast heading to a hospital.)
