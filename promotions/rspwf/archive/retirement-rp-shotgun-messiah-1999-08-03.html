[Retirement RP] Shotgun Messiah

3 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ For one last time, a voice is faintly heard over soft playing music. The music hits a high key, that rings throughout the mind, body, and soul. Soon, the voice speaks. ]
[V/O] "There was a village, built of crumbling clay and rotting wood, and it squatted ugly under the broiling sun like a sick and mangy animal, waiting to die. This village had a virus, shared by its people. It was the germ of squalor, of hopelessness, of a loss of faith. For the faithless, the hopeless, the misery-laden, there is time, ample time, to engage in one of the other pursuits of men. They begin to destroy themselves. Throughout the years, this village has done such, despite these harsh conditions. Yet, now, clouds begin to approach, and the village must do what it was supposed to do -- pass on."
[ Fade in. ] [ In a dark room, sits a man. Lying against a dark and shattered wall, the man sits with his dirty hands rested within his face. Burried are those hands, covered with blood. Crying can be heard from the man, as he does not pick his head up for one moment, rather sits and cries for eternity. ]
[ The blood continues to boil from the man's hands, and tears continue to be shead. His faint voice is soon heard, as his face in unknown, but he identity may and must live on. For he is Shotgun Messiah. ]
[SM] "I never proclaimed to be the best, nor did I say that I was a hero to my people. I was rather a Warrior, a man who walked out with his head up, chest out, and soul in. Now, my soul must pass on, for new
Warriors shall approach. This is the way of things; this is what must be." [ The blood vanishes, as the spirit of Shotgun Messiah is layed to rest. ]
