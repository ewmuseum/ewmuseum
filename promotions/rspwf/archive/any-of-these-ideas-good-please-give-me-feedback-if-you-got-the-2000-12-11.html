Any of these ideas good...Please give me feedback if you got the time

11 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please feel free to give me feedback, Email me or critque.....Just trying to see what kinda vibe my characters get.
Well...In my boring American History class my Third and Last class of the day...I decided I would Mess around...I started off by writing a note to some girl I would love to make scream my name..Then I talked for a bit, but then I decided....Hey why not do something e wrestling related....I came up with some characters....15 of them to be exact....of course some I already developed....but hey...I counted them to.....Now tell me what yall think of these guys
1 -"The Mercenary" Outlaw - This guy has been around for close to 7 freakin years....He has been my hardcore guy, before hardcore was the popular way to go in an angle. Back in the days when I had to beg the presidents to let me put somebody threw a table or break out the trademark barbwire bat. In 7 years
Outlaw has went threw a few gimmicks....I will admit that I did not stay consistant, but hey....Over 7 years can you blame me ?.....He has been a gangster, a cowboy, a soldier, a fam killer [ malik era ], a hardcore comedian, gothic even and for the last year and a half he has been the mercenary type...Do anything for money, fame and glory...Only wrestles in hardcore matches at this point in his life. His first glory came in the tag team division, but he no longer does that stuff..He won 69 on the rspwf awards this year which was a fitting number for the man married to lady love for six years now........ He currently isn't active, but he will be competing in TNL when
Kade starts it....He is also co president of the NMW..Playing the heel role as usual.
2 - Steve Greedy- Greedy is your typical rich boy....He has the blonde hair, blue eyes....I have used this guy off and on for two years. He was one of the top cruserweights in 1998......Of course making the top cw list isn't that hard to do...espically back then so I assure you im not bragging...Greedy is anti hardcore.....To the maximum.The one hardcore match he ever did was a chaotic cage match against Chaotic Chris Cage.....and he won the match, but got hurt and put out of the sport....Greedy is now making a comeback...He is managing
Kevin Klass in Hwa and NMW.....But will soon be preforming in MCW.....Greedy is a southern boy, so traveling to the island in which MCW will be at will be a new adventure for our heel friend.....I could use some methods in which to improve this guy, but for the most part....His gimmick, mindset and actions is set.
3 - Southern Pride - This tag team of cowboys is my newest creation in which I actually think has Poiental...."Southern Messiah" Joe Smith and "Hangman" Joe
Chapman are two cowboys in which stand for justice, the right way and hard work...but...they aren't above getting the job done brutally or punishing offenders very violently. There from Dubach, Texas and our just your typical redneck cowboys. there free agents right now and I have only done three flashes with these guys so far, cause I have been having problems staying in cowboy dialect for a whole role play lately and NMW my federation is draining me....and I can't rp drained as Southern Pride..Like I can with Outlaw and
Greedy..I have to be fresh and roaring to role play...My motivation and dedication with them is lacking, but I assure you my short spots of ideas is not.
4 - "The Bayou Assassin" James Monroe - Prolly my worst creation yet...SCW was this mans debut about a year and a half ago...Ya he won two hardcore titles and one Tv title and somehow found himself a good tag team partner in the fed by the name of Dan Starr....but.....Man my role players were less than pleasureable.....definitely a failure..... This character was a rugged nawlins boy from the Bayou's.....loved the animals.......and loved to fight like one.
He was as hardcore as SCW had ever seen.....but....his carrear only lasted six months there....I brought him back in one fed for god knows what reason, but he only lasted three months till I changed characters and went with Outlaw who had a very fine ICW carrear.....thanks to Monroe's very horrible ICW carrear
5 - EL Tigre' - This is my South American Cruserweight....In the ring he is breathe taking, out of the ring he is shy, timid and lacks mic skills...but his dad was a former wrestler.....who did well in SA but in the states had a 500 record..He speaks for his son usually....Tigre is a face, and loves his south american country and is loving his tour of the states...So far this character is a free agent but has worked a few house shows around the E scene......
6 - "Pure Poison" Josh Bailey - This is a new creation of mine...No flashes have been done, No applications have been filled out. Pure Poison though, Is gonna be a character I use alot in lower type feds.....to play with and maybe one day develop into my next Outlaw. I have plans of him being an educated gangster....A white one at that which should draw heat right there....He is gonna be heavy into drugs, Needles, Pills and others....but he is also gonna be educated. People are gonna have a hard time figuring out why a guy like this is the way he is. If anybody is interested in letting me test this character out in there fed drop me a line and we will see what We can work out.
7- "Firefly" Horo Kashi - I used this guy......in a fed bout two years ago. I preformed with him three times, but then the fed closed down...Now in NEO he will soon be returning to action. He is a face From Tokyo, Japan..Loves his supporters, his family, friends and country......He is a cruserweight who has very lil power but has a huge heart and can really manage a wrestlin ring. I guess mentally I modeled him after the Great Sasuke.....Catch some of his work in NEO very soon....Which looks to be a fed on the rising in the e scene of
2000.. 8 - "Religious" Jameston Pope - A guy I thought up today...Words from the pope could be a segment on every show......him doing some bibical babble....He is gonna be a face for a while, but will eventually turn heel as his faith in god changes a bit...He gets a lil more ruthless...Kinda like Gods Anger...as his patience runs out, he just kills people....Don't beleive me ? Read some scripture...His love can be quiet ruthless....Jameston will be a missionary from London, who has took Wrestling as his way of doing his calling...meeting such bad people as gangsters, drug dealers, rich men who have sex without being married and corrupted vultures in the front office....Prolly a heavyweight here guys.
9 - "Manifested" Timothy Taylor - He is gonna be a person who thinks he can manifest anything he wants...and was himself manifested by his horrible parents who needed a bright light...He is in his early twentys....Real Satanic and this guy isnt really for the politically correct federation....or the federation who doesn't like religious issues to be brought up....Real layed back fed is the best for this guy. His work will be realistic....but...His work will be sick....and you will wish it wasn't realistic....Another Cruserweight here looking for action
10 - "Sexually Active" Steven Riquiah [ For you Mike ].......Sexually Active is exactly that....He is the true def of a man who likes sex....well wait...thats us all, but anyways....He is active as they come...he gets alot of girls but theres rumors he pays them....who knows.....Steven is another cw in my lil stable here. I guess I mark out for cw's....cause so far only three of Ten haven't been cw's....He is a heel...well in the men's eyes and a face in the ladies eye...might not be a mixed reaction, but he will get somewhat of a pop everytime he enters the arena
11 - "Fisherman" Johnny Jackson - Double J is an old character of mine from
NOWA AND HOFW.....He is for comic releif no doubt and I want to bring him back...He is a cruserweight, but usually don't wrestle in the cw division...He likes to make the big guys look bad or good ......depending on the night..Loves to fish, loves to drink and you might of guessed his catch phrase is hook, line and sinker....I don't claim to be a comic genius...but I have done some good flashes with this guy...as somehow this guy has captured two world titles in two a lil better than average feds.....Don't ask me how....Maybe Fisherman does more than Even I know...Maybe he gives good head or something, but as far as this handler goes......He is a good midcard guy for any fed....and if the right angle comes up...Who knows
12 - "The Dark Warrior" Michael Draven......This guy is a ninja from Hiroshima,
Japan....He is silent, but deadily....very in charge of himself at all times and deadily in the ring...The fans boo him, but respect his actions.I guess he would be a neutral..I know...I hate neutrals to, but thats what he is....He is a heavyweight though...are yall proud of me I found a heavyweight.....tada......Anyways, most his flashes are a use of light, scenery, foreshadowing and voices.....but he does pop in some words.....takes a president who is real detailed and perceptive to like this guy..Hard to find the right fed for this guy
13 - "Big Cock" Chuck Rock - The name says it all....The kid has a big dick...Nuff said....and he loves to talk about it and show it off......as much as possible...I was gonna run some real angles with him for a while, then do a bit bout how he enlarges his dick before every match and it gets exposed...or something....to emberass him...His body is a cruserweight but his yack isnt.....Real heel here, this gimmick has been done before, but if done right it's always a good one....He was in AWA briefly a year ago.....but it closed down after only five matches with him.....So I am not sure how good I can do with him or how bad I can be with him. In this case, Bad might be better....
14 - Darkness Visible - The orginal darkness Visible was a book, then it was a tag team with me and Rob Stylz which stole the UXWA belts, but now its
"Darkness" Antwoin Rogers who is the blackest dude you will ever meet _LITERALLY_ and "Satanic" Joshua Moses...Named after two guys in the bible...oh did his parents mess up on this one.....holy potatoes.....he is crazy and a devil worshiping fool...while his partner isnt into the devil really but is into well.....vampire gothic stuff.....crazy tag team right here guys.....Both men are heavyweights.
15 - " Powerhouse" Tommy Taylor - I used Taylor in a few feds over my seven year carrear....and.....well.......he never got over. I guess the oh i got big muscles and im gonna show em off with pure power is hard to get over in feds...but with a few spark plug ideas who knows...I stay in character perfect with this guy and he is definitely a heavyweight....heel to.......Tommy has proven he can wrestle in the ring, but power is always his strong point...if you know what I mean. He's kinda dumb though......can be taken advantage of and heres one more.....a character of mine in which got over in aol feds back in the day, but no longer gets over in aol feds and prolly wouldnt in net feds
16 - "The orginal Gangsta" Prison.....This guy is announcing in NMW now and used to be a dwhs and kawf champion......and tag partner of Outlaws....He is a cruserweight who loves to scrap.....claims to be the orginal gangsta and he loves to drink.....talk bad and be your typical gangsta.....nothing fancy bout this one
Much Respect, Outlaw
