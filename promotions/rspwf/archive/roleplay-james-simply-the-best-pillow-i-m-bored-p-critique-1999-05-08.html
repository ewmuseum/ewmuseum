Ok, well I'm bored. . .and haven't done this in awhile. . .this is an in-ring for GLOW<so ya don't really need to critique the setting since it's his basic entrance,blah,blah> Anyay, here it goes. .
[The camera moves to the entranceway as SIMPLY THE BEST <Instrumental version> plays over the arena. Over the entrance is a pane on glass with the initials "STB" shone on it, the glass then shatters as the music screeches out like the needle being drug across a record and a familiar guitar riff begins to play over the PA as blue and gold smoke come out of the entrance and the crowd rises to there feet, the opening chorus begins as we realize the song is "Whatever" by Godsmack!!
"AND I WONDER. .DAY TO DAY. I DON'T LIKE YOU, ANYWAY. I DON'T NEED YOUR SH*T TODAY, YOU'RE PATHETIC. .IN YOUR OWN WAY!!" . . .The camera zooms in on the entrance way as James Pillow steps out from the smoke and the crowd goes BALISTIC!! James has on a pair of black jeans, and a matching black silk shirt. He also has on a pair of black ADIDAS sneaekrs,blak oakley sunglasses and. . the GLOW World Heavyweight title strapped around his waist. James stands in the aisleway looking around then begins to walk down the aisle way slapping hands of fans. .
"I FEEL FOR YOU. .BETTER FU**ING GO AWAY. I WILL BEHAVE." . . .James climbs up into the ring and begins to pose as the lights dim and gold fireworks shoot off all around him as the crowd goes nuts, the music pumps up even louder. .
"I'M DOING THE BEST I EVER DID. I'M DOING THE BEST THAT I CAN. NOW GO AWAY!!" . . .James then goes to the ropes and points down at into the crowd and smiles his usual smile. He thenclimbs up to the top turnbuckle and raises his hands in the air as the crowd grows even louder chanting "PILLOW,PILLOW,PILLOW!!"
"I DON'T NEED TO FANTASIZE. YOU ARE MY PETS ALL THE TIME. I DON'T MIND IF YOU
GO BLIND. YOU GET WHAT YOU GET UNTIL YOUR THROUGH WITH LIFE. ."
. . .James then asks for a mic which he gets very quickly. . .] "I FEEL FOR YOU. BETTER FUCKING GO AWAY. I WILL BEHAVE, YOU BETTER GO AWAY! I'M DOING THE BEST I EVER DID, I'M DOING THE BEST I EVER DID, NOW GO AWAY. . [James smiles and gives the cut sign. The lights come up and the music fades away. . ] JAMES "SIMPLY THE BEST" PILLOW - Well <Rubs belt> Jonathan Hammer, just as I expected we put on a match that will go down in the anals of the history of
GLOW. . .But when you get to the end of it, you will see one thing. .
[James unstraps the belt and holds it up. .] JAMES PILLOW - That's the fact that ole Es T Bee was the man to walk out with the gold strapped around his waist. . .I'm not going to say it was easy. .
[James grins and shakes his head. .] JAMES PILLOW - Because it wasn't. . .you gave me a run for it, but in the end. . [James holds belt in front of his face then moves it down to his side. .] JAMES PILLOW - I was the man. .I was the _CHAMPION!_ But Hammer. . [James grins arrogantly yey seriously all at the same time. . .] JAMES PILLOW - Anytime you want a shot. . just whistle and I'll come running. . .But like last time <smirks> I will walk out bearing the goods. . . [James places the belt over his shoulder, he then changes the mic to his other hand and goes on. .]
JAMES PILLOW - That goes for all of you in GLOW. . Anyone who wants a shot at it, bring it to me. .
[James grins crookedly. .] JAMES PILLOW -And I'll send each and everyone of you back the same way you came to me, as nothing more. . .
[James smiles slyly and looks down at his bare waist. . .] JAMES PILLOW - Than contendors. . . [James switches the belt to his other shoulder, he pauses for a moment then breathes in. .]
JAMES PILLOW - <Exhaling> I know everyone is going to be calling me out. .. so
I'm going to beat you to it, I'm calling eachand everyone in GLOW out to try and take this belt from me and I think I'll start. . .
[James pauses then smirks. .] JAMES PILLOW - Next week, it doesn't matter who. . .because in the end, first,second. .last, each and everyone of them will be sent packing, and each of them will se why they will always meely be second best when compared to. . .
[James places his free hand on the GLOW World title belt and grins his usual arrogant grin. .]
JAMES PILLOW - Simply _THE_ Best. . . [Whatever comes back on over the PA as James exits the ring and makes his way to the back. . . ]
