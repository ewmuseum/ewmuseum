Greedy isnt the type of character your fed needs...I am very..Hmmm whats the word ? Flexible...
I also handle a tag team by the name of Southern Pride A south American Cruserweight El Tigre' And Outlaw.....But It would take a GREAT angle for me to bring him back out to the world.....I am saving him for Kade's fed....So I can win another high stakes title right Kade =)...
Real Name:[name withheld] AIM/AOL Name: [detail withheld] E-mail Address where you would like me to send PPV Info: [detail withheld] IRC Nick: AND NOW IN RANDOM ORDER ... Wrestler Name including Alias, etc.: Steve Greedy...Sometimes called the rich one as well...but usually announcers say that, Not him personally.
Height: 6"0 Weight: 225 pounds Age/Birthday: January 13, 1977....thus he's 23 =) Hometown: Little Rock, Arkansas Alignment (Face/Heel/Tweener): Heel Brief Physical Description: Greedy is your typical blonde hair, Blue eyes pretty boy who thinks he is on top of the world and everybody else. He has wavey blonde hair, No scars...No tatoo's.....It's part of his self proclaimed perfect body. He wears a bunch of fine suits..Armoni, Rolex the whole Cliche attire which goes along with being rich...His body is prolly most built like
Saturn..Very well defined, but not bulging out of his mind....He works out, but he spends alot of time Bsing....so no Lex Luger here
Type of Wrestler (brawler, technical, etc.):Technical wrestling.....Yes...He is an excellent technical wrestler who hates hardcore and has refused hardcore matches before [ as angle ]......He can also do submission wrestling and even though he is a cruserweight...He hates to high fly.....Wrestling is his only real weapon...And cheating....Greedy matches would be boring to write if it wasn't for the certain heel spots Greedy does in most matches...You just WANT to see him lose everytime he steps into the ring....In your heart it makes you feel good to see his shoulders get pinned.
_Brief_ History: Greedy grew up as your typical rich boy...His dad gave him and his brothers everything, but unlike his brothers Greedy always took advantage of his dad...So when his dad died he was left with very little and his brothers got everything...Greedy did get enough money though to do some things with his life..He bought a decent home, a decent car, some suits and landed a job as a promoter for his dads business...the KAWF...A wrestling federation...Eventually
Greedy got suckered into the ring by a former kawf cruserweight champion and beat him...Thus he decided to try his carrear in wrestling....He did alright in his carrear winning some big matches, Only losing two......but he got hurt in a chaotic cage match in scwa against of all people chris cage..He won the match but didn't wrestle anymore.
After about a year off....and plenty of money cause he begged his brothers and they felt sorry for him.Greedy got married to a beautiful _BITCH_....She made Greedy get a job....and Greedy gladly went out and got one so he could get away from her...Now he is back in wrestling and looking to make an impact..His attitude is meaner than ever cause of his wife and he loves to work now....He just doesn't do it for the money now.
Entrance Music: How do you want it by Tupac Shakur Ring Attire: His actual attire is a white t shirt, with a gold necklace hanging down, Khacki pants and doc martin type boots.....but he wears a nice suit to the ring and takes it off before he gets going......
Entrance (Something Cut and Paste of Easily Modifiable): I do an in ring and entrance for every card I am ever on.....so depending on the match, the arena, the oppenent....but I promise I will get you one for any matches Greedy
Particpates in 15 Or So Basic Moves: Signature Moves: He has signature spots really....Not signature moves..I am talking like for each match in my strat he will have two or three things I beg of you do...But there semi different every match......He does love to execute the super plex though if you want to consider it a trademark type move..He usually floats over for at least a pin attempt
Set Up Maneuver If Applicable: None..part of the charm of the finisher...Catch you from nowhere =)
Finishing Maneuver (Name and Description): Bankrupt.....- Evenflow DDT Strengths (3): 1 - He is very intelligent..He can make you so mad you mess up, or slowly wear you down to make you want to quit
2 His Money.....He uses his money to manipulate people and get what he wants.... 3 One of the best wrestlers left in this sport of hardcore..... Weaknesses (3): 1 - He's not very strong...He can't execute alot of power moves and he Can't over power many people to counter...He has to slip away from them
2 - His Attitude - Sometimes his attitude can get him in trouble...Him thinking he is better than everybody can hurt him in the long run as he doesn't give the wrestlers there props.
3 - His injury - He hurt his back in the chaotic cage match...and he still worries that it could very well happen again.......that match was the only hardcore match he ever did all out and wham......he gets hurt
Like I mentioned in the application...This is Greedy's comeback...So what we got here is I don't have a greedy flash
If Greedy doesn't fit in...I got one more character I could work in the memorial...but....Hopefully he does
Here is a sample role play I did with my tag team Southern Pride....Just to give you an idea of how I work.
Much Respect, Outlaw [. We appear in an open field with nothing in the desolate area except for a steel post in the ground which goes up about twelve feet high. There is a rope hanging from the post. The camera focuses on the object, with the only occurrence being a small wind stirring up some dirt behind the post.]
[. Suddenly, You hear the sound of a vehicle, Probably a truck of some sort heading in the direction of the camera. We pan to the East just a bit and see a huge amount of dirt flying up. Screaming can be heard, but no words can be made out from the distance there located from us. The vehicle gets closer, as finally we see a Blue Ford truck prolly from the 70'S in our eye sight. One man is hanging out the truck looking backwards, and another man with a budweiser in hand is driving. We now hear the same hollering we heard earlier from a distance, but this time we understand what it's saying.]
"Help me !!!!! Please help me !!!!!! " [. The Truck does a 180 degrees turn and we now see the back end of the tuck where a man is tied to a rope on the back of the old truck. Blood is dripping from the man's face and he continues to cry for help. Two men then get out of the truck. The driver, Has a beer in hand still, some cowboy boots, Tight wrangler jeans with a noticeable skoal can in the back pocket and a nice collared shirt. The other man has a rope in hand, The same boots and pants as his friend, A black cowboy hat and a White T shirt showing off his better than average upper body.]
[. These two are both having a good ol' Time as smiles the size of that Twelve foot post in the ground our on there faces. The man with the rope unties the man from the truck and grabs him by his Hair to bring him to his feet. The two men then carry him to the Post in the ground and begin to put the rope around the man's neck. Once the rope is placed properly on the man, They slowly anchor him off the ground. Once his feet our off the ground completely, They tie the rope to the post and watch the man gasp for every air. Both men are silent at this time, No more smiles or laughter.]
[. The man's face goes from the normal skin tone to pale as we now realize his life has been taken from him. Once there is no more kick in the man, the Two men Shout out.]
" Yeeeeeee Hawwwwww " [. There enthusiasm over the deceased is amazing, Both men then go back to the truck and grab a fresh beer.]
"Ahhhhhh" [. The man with the rope then gulps his beer and with a devilish smirk on his face speaks. ]
"Howdy, How you gentleman of the New Millennium doing ?...Having a great day I suppose as you just put out a good ol' show called Ground Zero. The happenings on that there show was More than Pleasing to my eyes, and I reckon' It would be to the rest of the South as well. They know how to handle criminals just like we do. Know damn Yankee organization could do it quiet like that...."
[. The other man cough's to clear his throat. The man with the rope looks at him and then acts like a lightbulb has went off in his head.]
"Execuse me Joe....I forget to introduce myself. I am not used to not being known. Around there here parts I am considered a << A small blush >> Legend of some sorts. My name is Joe Chapman, and this here rope on my shoulder is how I got my nickname. You can call me the _HANGMAN_. I can do anythang with a rope, and I do mean anythang... The man over there in the nice shirt. That fellow is also a legend in this here parts, and not just for his rare talent to be able to piss on a snake, skin it and tell the witness's what kinda snake it is before the snake can even think about striking with those pretty ol fangs. He is known for being one tough Podnah...His name is Also Joe, Joe Smith it is...Some people around here in Texas call him the Southern Messiah."
[. Both men take one last sip of there beverages and toss the beer can's in the back of the old Ford Truck. The Hangman glances back at the post to admire there work, and while he is side tracked Joe Smith speaks with a very serious tone. Almost sends a chill up your spine.]
[ "Southern Messiah" JS ] : " Joe, I must say your the best I ever damn seen at

"Enough about around This nice quiet place though. It seem's we got to travel out of our town a lil ways, and go to Good ol San Tone, Texas..Not but a two day horse ride from here, but still not where were known. I suspect that will change in the next few days though."
[Looks up at the man hanging on the post.] "Ya...I suspect we will go just a tad bit higher than those Amigo's think. <<
