[Flash] Tyrone Parker... Let the flames begin!

4 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a flash I did against Ronnie D in the HHWO.. [SCENE] The scene opens up inside the locker-room of the HHWO's own, "Superstar" Tyrone Parker. He is sitting there cursing to himself after he lost his recent match to Creed. He has on on a "Mr Once a Night" tee-shirt and baggy jeans. He sees the camra. Exspressing his emotion, he kicks the man down.
Parker walks out of the room slowly. He walks down the corridor that leads the the exit of the building. He exit's the arena, and walks to the parking lot. It is dark out by this time, with only one car left, a 1998 White Blazer. Parker put's his hands in his pocket, getting keys out. He slowly talks into the camera.
'Superstar" Tyrone Parker- Well Creed, you beat me. But, you know what, if it weren't for that fool at ring-side, "Playboy" Ronnie D, I'd have beatin you into tommorow. Now Ronnie, me and you are fighting a No Holds Barred Match at
RockTober. So here's what a I want, Creed, you are going to ref this match, for the simple fact you hate me, I hate you, you hate Ronnie, and Ronnie hates you.
Now Ronnie you claim to be the biggest thing going in ewrestling at the moment, but like all good things, they come to a end.
Now sure, you may have The Body Shop, and to tell you the truth, the only member of Body Shop I respect is Simon LeBec. Now that man has talent, and another stipulation I want, is I want this in a cage, so no one intereferes. I have been in a ladder match, but never a cage, but in stipulated matches, I am
1-0. Now Ronnie, we are about the same size and weight, and I think I have more talent. Yeah, that's right.... _A LOT MORE_.
[Parker opens the door and get's in.] Why dontcha come on in for a little joy ride... [The camera man get's in, putting his camera in the back-seat. He look's around. Making sure this is no joke.]
Ya'see, I have been in the HHWO now for three weeks, and every day I say to myself, Tyrone, ya need some serious back-up. But no, it won't be Scott Wright or Cyrus Green. And no, it won't be King Fake. But the man I want as my back-up is non-other then, the one, and only, "Mr. Main Event" Chris Douglas. Ya wanna know why? Because he is a man of his word, and he know's how do get things done. So Creed, be ready.
Now back to Ronnie D. I realize you love reading your play-girl magazine, but that is not the issue. Yeah, sure, you suspect I am just tryin to make a name for myself by fighting you, well you are a sick person if you think that is how
I do business. I have beatin Nick Demola, a HHWO superstar, and coming up at the next HHWO card, RockTober '98, more people will have some hell ta pay, if ya know what I mean.
Now Ronnie, I knew you were the scum of the universe, but you cost me a match vs Creed, and that's the man I have wanted to pound for weeks! I swear, if one more guy get's on my case, the HHWO will be in trouble, because my energy is jacked up, and there ain't nuttin holdin me down.
[Parker turns on a dirt roade, he looks around, and see's know cops. He starts going 60, then 70, soon he is up to 110. He turns a cornor and slows down.]
Did ya like that rush? This is what I like to do before I go into a match, cool down and drive a hundrerd on the roades. Now, Ronnie, you have criticzed many people. in fact, you have criticzed Brody Thunder, and others, but the thing is, you backed it up. Which I do respect. But I excel to my best when I have to fight a tough preformer which you are............. but I am so damn better!
[Parker stops at a bar. And walks inside. The room is filled with smoke, and men playing pool and drinkin some beers. The people in the room look out of it, as they can't even pick up the check. Parker makes his way to a bar stool, and orders a coke with rum. And starts to speak again.]
[But first, Parker takes a sip of his drink. He makes a jesture like he is ready to speak again.]
Ah, refreshing. Almost as refreshing as it will be when I beat Ronnie D at
RockTober. Now the key to this macth-up is, No Holds Barred. But I want it No
Holds Barred in a steel cage. Heck, maybe Creed will ref, and maybe he won't, all I know is I have to get my point across ya don't mess with me!
[Parker pays the man with a five and tell's him to keep his change. He then finishes up his drink and walks back to the car.]
Now, D. You have been in Body Shop for a while, and you have Trey Porter, Zach
Malone, Simon LeBec and a whole lot more, but that won't stop me from kickin your teeth in. Now sure, I don't have a stable, or any friends, but I have proven myself enough to know that I can beat anyone in the HHWO. I proved that to Demola in what looked to be a stinken handi-cap match. I had Creed beat, till you distracted me, alowing Creed to roll me up for the One-Two-Three. And that really got me thinkin, he is makin my life a hell, so why not make his one too! And that's what I am here to do, make peoples life a hell.
[Parker cruises up the high-way to a Holliday-Inn. He locks his car, and goes inside. He waits for a elavator. As it opens, he goes in.]
Now, what is think I hear about Creed winnin the world title? I wonderd how that happend... I mean he beak King Fake and Mad Float in one night as well as me.... Hmm.... Well that don't matter. All I know is Creed is now a two time
HHWO World Champion, but once he gives me a re-match, nothing will save him.
***DING*** ***DING*** [That Ding means someone else is coming on the elevator, but as the door opens, no one is there. Parker presses the close button, and then the elevator starts back up.]
Stuiped kids, ya'know, I may only be 18, but on October 5th, this Monday I will be 19, one more year of maturity under my belt, and more dissadvantages for that punk Ronnie D..
***DING*** ***DING*** *** DING*** [Parker get's out of the elevator, and walks back to his hotel room.] Untill next time, this is HHWO's only damn superstar signin off! [Parker enters the room, as the camera fades out.] [name withheld] Handler Of- Tyrone Parker HHWO,SWP,RWF, 17-1-2 RWF National Champion! CNWL Cruiserweight Champion!
