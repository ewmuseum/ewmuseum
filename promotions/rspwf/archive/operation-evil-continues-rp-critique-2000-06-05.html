Operation ~EVIL! continues [RP critique]

5 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
You know the drill: Read this, love this, and get this character name ingrained in your brain so that you will be compelled by forces beyond your will to vote for him on the RSPWF midyears when they finally come around...
Oh, and this RP/promo is basically a parody/mockery of the styles and tendencies of two of Mike's principal rivals at the time, so some stuff that I find absolutely funny may leave you scratching your head and saying "WTF?" simply because you don't get the joke because you're not familiar with all the kooky characters in the UWC.
WARNING: This one is rather silly. But hopefully funny. And you can send me apps for your fed, but I'm not going to have time to join or even fill 'em out. So there. (See, I know if I tell you not to send them, you'll send them anyway, so what's the point?)
********************** [Did the pot just call the kettle black?] [Did Tom "I invented the irrelevant subject matter promo" Collins just criticize someone for talking about something that doesn't really have anything to do with wrestling?]
[Fade in to Mike Masters doing nothing noteworthy.] Masters: "Very good, Collins, very nice indeed." Joanette Napolitano: "He's an idiot." Greg Gaffin: "Look, buddy, I know you're bitter about your lot in life, but just because you had to pretend to be gay to get anyone to notice you doesn't give you the right to lash out like this."
[Oh, what, you didn't know Greg and Joanette were here? I'm sorry, I didn't mention that, because of course, it has nothing to do with wrestling, so I thought I should keep quiet about it.]
Joanette: "What was that?" [Greg Gaffin notes the ninja assassin hiding behind the tree ("What tree?" ''Shut up and quit asking questions!'') and crushes him with a rolling clothesling, K-Dawg style, that leaves him laying. The ninja threat has been neutralized.]
Gaffin: "O LA LE!" [Joanette also says "O LA LE" because she's always providing guest backing vocals on Bad Religion albums.]
Masters: "What does any of this have to do with wrestling? Nothing, really, except it has everything to do with a wrestler named Tom
Collins." Kid #45: "He's gay. I don't like him." Kid #43,816: "Dumbass! Don't stereotype people like that! Besides, he's just faking it to get attention."
Kid #7: "Hey, wait a minute." Kid #45: "What?" Kid #7: "Where did we come from?" Kid #43,816: "I don't know!" [The three kids instantly dematerialize, as they cannont justify their existence in the present environment, because they used Tom Collins logic to get here. Since they cannot justify their presence, they cease to be present. I don't think, therefore I am not. Poor kids, now they're consigned to float in limbo forever. Collins, you bastard.]
Masters: "Now I'm going to go beat up Puff Daddy and taunt Jennifer
Lopez!" [Masters goes absolutely nowhere, because beating up Puff Daddy and taunting Jennifer Lopez -- fun though it might be -- has nothing do with wrestling, and doesn't prove anything, so he really shouldn't bother, right Tom?]
Masters: "My apologies, Collins, for trying to inject a little culture and intellect into the daily occurences of this place. The truth is, though, I'm bored with you pretending to be gay, I'm bored with
Wallace's Devil's Advocate and gratuitious celebrity cameo schtick. I'm bored with Marcus Payne's ripoff of Wallace's Devil's Advocate schtick that he does with that Heaven person. I'm bored with Ray Hagan's everything because I like everyone else can't help but get bored with him when he cuts 3 promos a day, 4 on Sundays."
Wonder Woman {Narci's superhero lesbian lover} : "It's called oversaturation." [Bet you'd like to see what they do with that rope...] Masters: "I'm bored with Luni's "I call drugs by the euphamism of candy" schtick." [Joanette puts a sock puppet on her hand, and starts speaking through it.] Mr. Dave-o: "You realize, of course, that by directing your promo towards Collins and Wallace and others you are ensuring a loss against
Kain and Hagan, right?"
Masters: "No I'm not. Collins almost never aims a promo at his actual opponent, yet have you noticed he always wins."
Mr. Dave-o: "Yeah, but you don't have the psychological advantage of everyone flipping out because they think you're gay. You also don't have a superhero on wait for run-in duty when you're about to be beaten."
Masters: "Don't need them. I've got talent, and I don't need to rely on shock value like Collins does. And besides, I did make a Ray Hagan reference, so that base is covered."
Mr. Dave-o: "Oh. Well what about when he broke out that old footage from Hardknocks, about your artistic days?"
Masters: "You mean the stuff that pertains in no way whatsoever to the
UWC and/or to the fact that I'm a superior wrestler to him and Kain?"
Mr. Dave-o: "The footage that proves you weren't always rich, yeah." Masters: "The footage that proves how Hagan has depleted his supply of promo subject matter already because he cuts 453 per diem? The footage that shows how desperate he's getting?"
Mr. Dave-o: "It's not even your money, though, it's hers." [Having nothing better to do, Greg Gaffin quotes the footage...] Gaffin: "MM: Like we care about Philistines of YOUR calibre... we have the gold... and with Narcissa's new inheritance, we have the where with all to buy you. Ursa, and Galava Arena 3 times over... go back to your retirement home Luciano, time has passed you and the Tarnished One by..."
Gaffin: "Why is it this guy always has to pull the Luciano card? It's like he has to justify himself by saying "Yeah, I know Luscious Luciano, and I hung out with him, n' stuff."
Masters: "Very interesting, Greg." Mr. Dave-o: "But getting back to that quote..." Masters: "Her new inheritance from when her uncle finally died, IN
ADDITION TO THAT WHICH I ALREADY HAD, was what I meant. Hagan, there's a difference between not HAVING wealth and not FLAUNTING the wealth you have. So I didn't always flaunt it. Big deal. So I didn't always allude to my birthright. Big deal. It doesn't mean it wasn't there!
Like your genitalia, for example. You never show them on television
(thank God). That doesn't mean I should assume you don't have any, or that if you were to show them off now when you didn't do it before proves you used to not have them, does it?"
Kid #7: "Well, actually..." Wonder Woman: "Shut up, you don't exist." Kid #7: "Sorry." Masters: "You, Ray, pretended to be something you're not. So did I, because I thought it would be fun."
Joanette: "I should point out here that this has nothing to do with the
UWC, so who the hell cares?"
Masters: "Good point, I'll shut up about it now, then. Instead, I'll talk about how you are not a sanctioned UWC referee anymore, because you were assigned as a special guest referee to that match and THAT MATCH
ALONE, so there is no need to strip you of your powers, because your powers were only applicable in that match anyway, and that match is over. Sorry, Ray, you are not going to be able to register a legit 3 count in your own match by fast counting me while Kain has me in a pinning predicament."
Mr. Dave-o: "You don't know he's going to do that." Masters: "No, but it's an educated guess, based on how much Ray keeps alluding to the ''fact'' -- note my sarcasm -- that he was never offically defrocked by any UWC executive. It's not going to count. And if any of our would be executives attempt to act as though it does, I will litigate the lifeblood out of this company."
[Fade to something totally irrelevant to wrestling, which thus makes
Mike Masters a drama fag, even though he's A) heterosexual and B) not in the dramatic arts in any form. He's a wrestler, you idiot Collins.]
[Go to the ghetto.] "Due to the fact that Masters beat Wallace six ways from Sunday, put him in the hospital, and scrambled his brain and generally made a mess of his life, there is no longer anyone for me to mock/parody. I'm hurt. But still, if Masters can change Wallace's behavior that significantly, I don't mind that much, because it simply goes to show who is the superior one
(hint: It's not Wallace) in all of this. In fact, Masters hopes WW3 and Collins go to a draw, so that Mike can wrestle and defeat BOTH of them next week. You're welcome."
Masters: "And as for Tommy Kain..." [What? You didn't think that was the end of this promo, did you? HAHHAHAHA! We're using Collins logic today you idiot. Did you forget that little fact?] Masters: "You fancy yourself a tag team specialist. Okay, I won't dispute that. You've done a lot in tag team wrestling, with a lot of people. BUT... so have I. Vince Clark. Jedah. Burt Clifton. Two of those three I didn't like -- one of which I was planning on backstabbing the second he ceased to be useful to me -- and the third one, I'm sure, you and many others would contend I do not respect. Won tag team titles with all three of them. And, as for the King of X-mas tourney? Hell, I won a tag team match with Nas as my partner! Don't like him, don't respect him, and he didn't have the mental capacity to understand the battle plans I proposed to him. I still won. So I really don't think your tag team record means that much here."
Joanette: "Especially since it's an individual accomplishment anyway, and this is team competition."
Mr. Dave-o: "But then Masters' is irrelevant too, and since he brought it up and it was irrelevant he looks like a hypocrite in this argument, and thus he definitely loses the match this week."
Joanette: "Oh shut up." Gaffin: "Don't argue with yourself, Joan. It makes you look insane." Wonder Woman: "This whole segment is insane." Mr. Dave-o: "Oh, go lick carpet with Narci." Masters: "So yes, Kain, you and Hagan are the more experienced team, and while that's an advantage, it's not a guaruntee. Especially when your opposition is the two best pure wrestlers on the planet, united by a common goal."
[FADE to the intellectual letdown, because Wyatt Wallace stands alone, because nobody wants to tag up with him, because he sucks.]
Yogi Berra: "It ain't over 'till it says Intellectual Letdown." [Thanks, Yogi.] [Intellectual Letdown will soon commence...] Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
