[SAMPLE ROLEPLAY] "The Handler" Liam Rogan

23 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi all, I've been in e-wrestling for a few years now but this character is somewhat new... not even six months old. He's a tweener but I'm in the process of turning him full fledged face in one of the feds that I'm in. I handle him in
UEW, MWF, and IWF/WOW.
Well, you know the drill. Any criticism, praise, or wisecracks... yadda yadda yadda :) Thanks, Rob [The scene opens with a shot of a rather large, dark, and dreary park. As dreary as the park is, you can still see specks of natural beauty peeking out in such things as the trees and the endless streams of grass and flowers. As the wind whips wildly through the monstrous oak trees in the park, various litter and debris blow around through the confines of the park. It's also raining quite hard outside. The camera zooms in to get a shot of a man walking in through entrance of the park but his face is somewhat hidden by the barrage of rain falling. But, as the camera zooms in even closer, it is revealed that the man is none other than "The Handler" Liam Rogan. The wind runs through his bushy, unkempt hair as he looks up and begins to address the camera.]
Liam Rogan: I've been coming to this park ever since I was a young child. In the couple of decades that I have been coming to this park, one thing always remains the same... one very peculiar thing. It's always raining when I visit this place. I've been coming here for almost twenty-five years and not once have I seen this place without rain falling. Maybe it has to do something to do with me. Maybe it's God trying to tell me something. Hell, maybe I've just been picking bad times to go for my walks.
[He smiles slightly.] In retrospect, this park is alot like UEW. Look around. First, you have the beautiful flowers and trees in this park. They remind me of the talented and hard working athletes in the UEW... like Nick Leshenn, Jason Keening, Chris
Tyler, and so forth and so on. Then, amongst the beautiful grass and trees, you also have filthy leaves that have been pissed on by dogs... and garbage that has been discarded by litterbugs blowing around the place... much like UEW has garbage like Johnny Bod, Marcus Manson, and the New Alliance running around in its ranks. Then, you have the occasional storm.... which reminds me of the turmoil that sometimes finds its way into UEW.
Last and certainly not least, there are guys like me. There aren't many of us here. Just me, Nickedemus Payne, and Alex Extreme are the only ones that come to mind right now. We're the humble trash collectors... the guys who take it upon themselves to at least _ATTEMPT_ to rid the UEW of such trash as the Steve
Spector and the New Alliance.
We try our best but that doesn't mean we always succeed... particularly when it comes to the New Alliance. "Mr. Excitement" Alex Extreme has been trying to exterminate the New Alliance out of this organization for what seems like forever. And Nick Payne... while the man has driven many pieces of trash out of UEW, the New Alliance has always seemed to escape his grasp, too. I plan to break that mold. I plan to be the first man who will be able to safely say that he quieted the New Alliance. I _want_ to be the man who ends up being known for ridding the UEW of the biggest pieces of trash in the wrestling industry once and for all.
[Rogan spots a five pack box of action figures on the ground. The label on the front of the box says, "New Alliance Gift Pack" and there are five small action figures inside the box... a "Top Dog" Rick Styles doll, a Alex Martinez doll, a J'ster doll, a Lance Studd doll, and a Max Studd doll.]
Speaking of trash, look at that horrible piece of litter. Well, it doesn't really suprise me that no one would want that trash. I wouldn't even line a kitty litter box with those loads of bollocks.
[Rogan bends down and picks up the box. He walks over to the trash bin and tosses the box in. He wipes his hands on his jacket before turning back around to face the camera. He now has a satisfied look on his face.]
But, before I get my chance at the New Alliance, I have the pleasure of taking on another piece of human debris, "Armitage" Steve Spector. The man claims to be the holder of some fifty odd wrestling championships. Frankly, I don't care if Spector is the God appointed Champion of the Universe. It won't mean diddly once he steps through those ring ropes to face me. He will be looking across the ring at a man who has alot of hunger, alot of anger, and a [BLEEP]-load of frustration brewing in his system. He will be looking across the ring at a man who wants to use his carcass as an example... an example directed towards the
New Alliance... to show them just what Liam Rogan is capable of. There isn't a championship belt, plaque, or trophy that can save him from that unfortunate fate.
[A look of anger sweeps over Rogan's face...] You've already crossed me not once... but _twice_... and you act really damn proud about it too. On Wednesday night, not only did you beat the hell out of some sorry lookalike of your's truly as well as some Freak Show lookalikes just to make yourself look all mean and vicious. Hell, I wasn't even in the building. I wasn't even in the same damn town. Me and the Freak Show were sitting in the comforts of our own home watching "Anarchy". At any rate, you also went on to challenge me and my entire Freak Show to a seven on one handicap lumberjack match.
You know, people say that you've snapped but that ridiculous challenge shows me how utterly retarded you are. I'm sorry to say it but Joe Malloy must've been the brains of the operation when you two tagged up. Heh, if Malloy is smarter than you, you have problems.
Steve-o, this Sunday night, our match is going to be nothing more than a walk in the park for me.... pardon the pun. It will merely be a warm-up for the destruction of the New Alliance. So call your momma, notify your next of kin, and damnit, boy, put on a clean pair of underwear 'cause on October 25th, you will be...
ERASED. [The rain begins to fall harder and there is a crackle of lightning. The camera gets a shot of Liam Rogan picking up one final piece of trash. It's a worn and torn cardboard box with a white label on the left side. The label says, "Armitage Incorporated". He tosses it into a waste bin as we fade to blood red...]
