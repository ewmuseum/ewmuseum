(Free Agent)Levi Ash

22 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello RSPWF My name is Levi Ash, I have been in e-wrestling for 4 years, I have competed in alot of feds, won alot of titles, beaten alot of people. (And lost alot by not rping.
hehe) Anyways, I have tried a couple of times now to get back into main stream e-feds with feds like NLWP and DWF. But both closed down before I could debut, or rp for them
A sample rp is included at the bottom. It was written abut 3 to 5 months ago. I am looking for a fed that is main stream, been going for atleast 3 months. Has great rp'ers, rp packets, and is a e-mail fed. Orginal wrestlers only thanks ~Levi [The scene fades in to a black screen. Then simintainiously a voice begins talking and the same words the voice says pops up on the screen in bold white text.]
Voice Over: "A few weeks ago the horror began. A man entered Elite Pro
Wrestling and bought 50% of the company. That man being Ash Bombay the
Third. From there he he enlisted the help of Ice, Greger Anderssen and
NeO Tanaka. Forming what has been dubbed the greatest force in EPW history. The 'True Icons'. Ash Bombay III then plowed through typical
EPW talent and earned a spot in the main event for the EPW World title at the ppv 'Opposites Attack'. But before crunch tme, before the ppv, he must team with his True Icons stablemate Greger Anderssen against his foes at the ppv. That being Mason Storm and L-Triene."
[The voice then fades out as does the words on the screen. A still picture then shows President Icon announcing that INSANITY would be the special guest ref for the tag team match.]
V/O: "From there the unjustice began. President Icon showed up and started handing out match stipulations like he owned the place. Even though the rich Ash Bombay III is half owner. But the madness was just beginning to pan out what has been a long week."
[A vignette begins and shows the action that happened during the tag team match. It slows as it shows three masked men interferring on behalf of Mason Storm and L-Triene. Which in turn wound up costing Ash Bombay
III and Greger Anderssen the match.]
V/O: "The crime was then committed. Three unknown masked men stuck thier noses in buisness that did not pretain to them. But now the crime has spread even deeper to those close to the victim. Because now the members of True Icons have pointed thier fingers at Ash Bombay III and cursed his name. They have said he has brought shame to them. All of EPW has dispised Bombay since his entrance."
"But now the hunted because the hunter. Because one man is sick of it all and ready to silience the doubters...."
[The voice over then fades for good. The scene then fades into a dim lited room. there is but one window which shows the dusk of the outside enviroment. Around the room is but a plain room. White walls. No bed. No decorations to take up wall space or room on the floor. Just one small chair which sits the hated himself. The one and only multi-millionaire.
Ash Bombay III. Bombay sits wearing his black parachute pants with a white sash posing as a belt. He is clad with a 'True Icons' t-shirt.
Black boots. No head covering his short dyed blonde hair. His face freshly shaven as a symbol of new happinings about to come. He is a bit distrought however. He holds his trusty brass cane in one hand. And a simple classic pistol in the other hand. The kinds pistol that holds only six bullets. Ash Bombay 3 then begins to speak]
Ash Bombay III: "Money is the root of all power. Everyone has thier price. But with money comes the haters. The doubters. The jealious. I have been spit on, cursed at, had trash thrown on me. And further more.
Being dubbed nothing more then a spoiled brat throwing around my parents money. But I am much more then that. I am what everyone truly dispise. I am greatness. But being greatness everyone has there downfall. Maybe thats what this gun is for."
[Ash Bombay III then puts the gun to his head. He looks dead ahead at the camera with his eyes open. He pulls the trigger.
All that is heard is a *click*] Ash Bombay III: "Like defying all these odds of not just putting a bullet through my brain, I shall over come the odds of the doubters who do not believe in money. I will put to rest the jealious like these bullets do. I shall kill the image of the spoiled brat with his parents money."
[Bombay then puts the gun under his chin. He once again pulls the trigger. After a pause. All that is heard is another *click*] Ash Bombay III: Another click. This must be the sign of the clique that call themselves my friends. The friends who wear the 'True Icons'
t-shirt. The friends that point fingers at me when they fail. The ones only in it for the money. My Money. Those like Greger Anderssen and Ice.
Those who further boost there ego at my expense. Those who still wear the TI t-shirt. Those are are no longer associated with me. Because, of now, I QUIT TRUE ICONS!."
[Ash rips off the True Icons t-shirt and looks dead into the camera once again. He then puts the gun between his eyes. As Bombay looks down the pistol he pulls the trigger. As everyone holds thier breath, a *click* is heard again as once again no bullet exits the gun.]
Ash Bombay III: "OOOO miss again. Just like Triene, Mason Storm and
Greger Anderssen will do in thier vain attempts to rid me of the battle royal. So far, three clicks. Normally in a minor game such as baseball.
Three strikes and you cats would be out. But in a game of death. We play til one has truly fallen."
"My money has been my power, but my wrestling skills prove that I am the
"Million Dollar Icon". The one and only. The one who will walk out of
Opposites Attack with my EPW World Heavyweight championship."
[Ash then puts the gun in his mouth. He looks dead into the camera unafraid and pulls the trigger for a forth time. This time the suspence kills. But again a sharp *click* is heard and nothing happens.]
Ash Bombay III: "Incredible. I have over came all the odds. Still no death for me. If I can't waste me self. How shall my opposers waste me?
If I can't destroy the one true great athelete in EPW. How shall three others? Mason Storm, Triene, Greger Anderssen. You three laugh at me like I am some kind of joke book. You throw my pressence around as if it was some worthless penny. You mock my lifestyle of being rich. You call me weak. You call me spoiled. You all know nothing. You know nothing of what money can buy, do, or destroy. You are just jealious of the dinero
I have. You are green with envy like the money you make. You're not making enough, so you come after the one who is."
"You hate people in power. You hate authority. You hate me. But unlike you three I see through the smoke. I know I am the prey in this match. I know I am the one every one wants to get there hands on. But, a champion becomes the hunter. You three are my hunted. You three will be caught, thrown over the top rope, dumped on your head, and will never ever have the class to oppose what is greater then you. I am greater then you."
"I have far more class, skills, and talent then you three put together. You three are no more then simpletons who look at the book facts. You think, oh he has been quiet he must be scared. You don't think he is quiet, oh know he is thinking of a plan for my demise. But alas, when I show up Sunday and throw you three out of the ring with ease, you will then and only then know, money, talent, and the "Million Dollar Icon"
Ash Bombay III over comes all. " "Two pulls left. I either die in front of all you on camera, or squash the odds and continue to squash the pipe dreams of three who shall never get the opportunity again and get what rightfully is mine. And be crowned the EPW World Heavyweight Champion."
[Ash then puts the gun to the middle of his chest. Where his so called black heart lies. He pulls the trigger. A loud THUD is heard as Bombay slams his cane on the floor. But yet, a small death defying *click* is heard. Bombay then smiles, knowing he has over came all. Bombay then points the gun at the camera.]
Ash Bombay III: "Greger Anderssen, Triene, Mason Storm. My watch reads
14:59, so smile and pose for the camera, your 15 minutes of fame is up.
Now, BANG YOUR DEAD!" [Bombay pulls the trigger as a bullet sails out of the gun and hits the camera. The scene then goes to static before fading out.]
