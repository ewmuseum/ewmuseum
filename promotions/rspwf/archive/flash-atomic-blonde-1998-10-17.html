[Flash] Atomic Blonde

17 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Thought I'd post some of these that I did a year ago. Haven't done much new stuff since, but what the hell. Talk to me...
_____________________ <The shot opens up to the corridor of the arena, where the Atomic Blonde sits shirtless, in only his tights and a pair of black sunglasses. His head is tilted back, resting against the wall. The camera pans into full view to see his half laced boots and a faded blue dufflebag to his left. He lets out a sigh and sluggishly pulls the dufflebag onto his lap. He peers into the bag and begins to speak...>
"Tomorrow marks the end of one man's career and the humilation of 19 others. Billy Reel... I give to you the gift of life and death. Hopefully after I end the current one, you'll come back into this world as something with more brains than balls. Perhaps as a female..."
<Blonde laughs quietly under his breath> "Either way, It looks like you're coming back the same way you went out. As my personal whore. You're nothing more than a hair on my ass. And a few short hours from now, the hair on my ass becomes shame of NBW."
<Blonde pulls a pair of dirty, stained elbow pads from his dufflebag. He pulls them over his wrists and up to his elbows, tugging lazily.>
"With that out of the way, I suppose it's time to disappoint 19 other wrestlers. The same night that I put Billy Reel's ass on ice, I hang 19 other fools out to dry. I should probably take a lesson from every other participant in this event and prepare for this battle royal soley by ranting & raving about becoming the next U.S. Champion, eh? Well, Dupree... how's this for a kick in the ass. I don't want your vacant title! What the hell does it matter to win a title currently held by NO ONE?!? I'd feel more satisfied maiming the 18 other participates and handing that belt over to the remaining man, then dropping him on his head the following week to win that title from SOMEBODY. Dupree...you're going out of your way to make second rate wrestlers look like first class champions. You wanna bend over backwards for these guys? The Blonde's not a second rate wrestler and he doesn't compete for second rate titles. As far as
I'm concerened, you can bend over backwards and shove your battle royal where every other wrestler already has their nose crammed. Maybe the stained steel will be worth more on the way back out..."
<Blonde pulls a roll of athletic tape from the bag, and casually wraps it around his wrists, hands, and knuckles... >
"Christmas is apon us... and just as my wish list is adding up, my hit list is growing with every passing day. Reel will be the first casualty of the Blonde's seasonal bliss, followed then by 19 more of NBW's `elite`. As I walk into the battle royal with blood stained hands and the scent of Billy Reel's flesh on my breath, that vacant title is the least of my concerns. I don't need Gold to feel a sense of accomplishment. It hasn't done much to boost the status of men like Pagan, The Bullets, and Griffen in the eyes of anyone but themselves and the ever-so-anal broadcast team. That title belt does nothing more than shield an exterior of lies."
<Blonde pushes his sunglasses down to the tip of his nose, exposing a pair of badly bruised, bloodshot eyes. He reaches down to his feet, and gives his boots a tug. He finishes lacing them up and continutes...>
"I have nothing to hide... nothing to gain.. and nothing to lose. I'm running through NBW with enough wins to fill Dupree's waistline and the last thing I need is a 20 pound piece of gold weighing me down."
<Blonde braces his hands against the tiled wall behind him, and pushes himself to his feet. He slings the dufflebag over his shoulder and walks away as the camera fades out...>
