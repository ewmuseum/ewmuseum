[RSPWF 200 Feedback] From Selena

17 December 1997

Well, as the newest regular around here, I guess I'll add to the comments here. I'll throw in a plug for the IEWA and say congratualtions to #127 Medusa Rage, the first and only woman on the list. I don't know how Derek Edwards manages to kepp all of his wrestlers straight in his mind, but I'm impressed with the way he portrays Medusa and all of the other women. I know that Medusa and Jessica are going to eventually meet in the ring, and I look forward to that day, believe it or not. Anyway, congrats to Derek and Medusa. Congratualtions to all of the SJPW, NLWP, and IIWF wrestlers who made it to the list, especially Brody Thunder. Rod Knowles has him down pat, doesn't he? I enjoyed reading his exploits in the IIWF; looking forward to another great year reading all you guys. Perhaps Jessica will make one of these lists next year, although looking at this year's lists, I have some hard acts to follow. If Medusa can make it, though, why can't Jessica? It has been suggested by private e-mail, and now on another newsgroup message, that a separate awards list be created for the women wrestlers. Personally, I'm not sure how familiar the readers of these cards are with the women, outside of the GDWA and SJPW. The other e-feds who do have women (IEWA, ESWP, WWA, and others) don't post their cards here on a regular enough basis for the readers of this newsgroup to become familiar enough with them to vote for them. Judging by those that made this year's list, it's probably not necessary to do that, but if the fans don't know they exist, how can they vote for them? Just a thought from the newbie, though I probably drop the new status come next year. Oooohhh..... I'll probably get flamed for this one. [name withheld] handler of Jessica Starbird (IEWA)
