[FLASH] "Dr. Jazz" Johnny Trumpets!!!

1 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here is a flash of my newest character. He's in GWI and looking for more... Even perhaps on spot on the RSPWF 200 : ) [The scene fades in to a beautiful video picture of a New York city view. The camera begins to focus in on the New York Stock Exchange building. It slowly zooms toward the large structure. We can barely make out the shape of a large figure, watching the view from the observation area of the huge building. As the camera moves even closer, we can see the man stands quite tall. He is wearing a large brown trenchcoat and has slick black hair. It's "Dr. Jazz"
Johnny Trumpets. The scene fades out for a moment and fades back to reveal an inside view of the observation area. Johnny is leaning against the window pane with his forehead resting against his forearm.]
JT - Magnificent, isn't it? [Trumpets pauses for a moment, lost in his own thoughts. As he continues to speak, he does not keep his eyes off of the view.]
JT - Those were the words I used over a year ago. Oh, how things have changed. Personalities have changed. Stars and non-stars have come and gone.
Injuries, retirements, federation movements. All have been woven into a web of constant change in a business that remains the same as much as changes.
["Dr. Jazz" points to one area of the city.] JT - And down there, people walk by us on a secondly basis. Clueless to the real world around them. One of lies, of deciet. Proud Americans? Don't make me laugh. What is there to be proud of? That big business corporations waste the precious human life so that they may make marginal profit? Sweat factories laboring children not old enough to even go to school by our standards? Is that what we are to be proud of? When will civilization learn that the world is never as it seems? That even the bible itself is filled with unknown and undiscovered lies. Kings and monarchs took their place in the Bible by changing what they felt was not leaning towards their own patriarchy.
[JT turns his head slightly and looks at the camera out of the corner of his eye.] JT - The wrestling world is no different. We make up stories that people will believe. The people believe one to be injured... one to be evil... one to be pure. All it takes is either one moment, or a series of moments. Regardless, the wrestling cycle never stops. Until today. I am bringing forth a new way of the future. One where
no lies shall be heard. Before that happens, though... [Johnny finally turns completely around to face the camera.] JT - I must put an end to my past. All, including myself, have been a part of this chronic wrongdoing. I've told the lies, for my own self betterment and not, nonetheless, I've told them. The future's what lies ahead, and promises to stay away from the storytelling have been made. I can easily forget the past as new promises forge, let's just hope it's not until those are broken as well.
Brushing his hand through his hair, Johnny takes a deep breath and continues.] JT - Forget the past... Live in the future. [Trumpets swings takeews off his trench coat and swings it over his shoulder and begins to walk towards the elevator. He stops and looks back for a moment.
Again, he looks over old and forgotten memories of the past. Trumpets, not keeping his eyes off of the room still, presses the elevator button.]
JT - I am the new way to go... I am the way of the future. And Woe Woe Woe to the three inhibitors of the squared circle: Freeman, Method,and Blockbuster;
those who wish to stop me, and those... [Scourge steps into the elevator. The scene begins to fade out as the door closes and Johnny mutters his final words.]
JT - WHO MUST FACE THE MUSIC! [Black.] --- [name withheld] --Fang-- EWC HHWO US Champ E-Wrestling's _First_ Legend #86 on '96's RSPWF Top 200 #25, #22, and #10 Brawler [Replacement] #6 A-N Wrestler --"Layed Back" Lawrence Christian-- ACW, SWF and BFW SWF TV Champ
