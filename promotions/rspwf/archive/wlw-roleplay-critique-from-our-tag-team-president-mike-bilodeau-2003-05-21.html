WLW Roleplay Critique - From our tag team president, Mike Bilodeau

21 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey folks, normally I don't put WLW roleplays on here, just the cards...but I thought this was one was deserving of a look, very different rp...
[name withheld] WLW President ----------------------------------- Writer - Mike B. Subject - R.I.P. (Scene opens inside a dark room, somewhere in the satelite offices of Deadly Enterprises somewhere in Europe. The glow of a television screen illuminates three shadowy figures surrounding the screen. We see the figures of WLW Tag Team President Mike Bilodeau, Dark Vanity and Matt Booth surrounding the screen, Vanity and Booth on both sides of Bilodeau's wheelchair.)
Mike Bilodeau: Bow your heads. Matt Booth: Huh? Mike Bilodeau: I SAID BOW YOUR HEADS IN RESPECT FOR THE GODDESS OF
THE DARK! YOU WILL PAY YOUR RESPECTS AS SHE TAKES HER FINAL BOW!
Tonight, the world loses one of the dark lords. Tonight, the world loses a precious entity. Tonight, the world loses Buffy The Vampire
Slayer. BOW YOUR HEADS I SAID DAMMIT!
(Booth and Vanity quickly bow their heads.) Mike Bilodeau: She has slain a thousand vampires, all in the name of good. She has tasted love, and felt it ripped away by the darkness. She has died, saving her family, saving her friends, only to be ripped out of heaven to continue her fight for the light.
She's lost her mother, her loved ones, her friends, all in the name of good. She has paid the ultimate sacrifice to ensure we all have the freedom to walk alone at night down those dark and desolate streets and allies. She protects us from the things that go bump in the night. Thought tonight, the world will witness her departure from her duty, her spirit and her strength will live on in all of us. We hail you Buffy, queen of the darkness and say goodbye.
Matt Booth: Uh, Mike, what exactly are we doing here? Mike Bilodeau: I'll tell you what we're doing here! We're giving the proper respect to a show that changed a generation. A show we all connected with at one point in time, and wished we were part of. I mean, you remember when Buffy fell in love with Angel, and then found out he was a vampire with a soul. A VAMPIRE WITH A
SOUL! How creative was that? He was cursed by a gypsy to help the good, as a way to save his soul from all the wrong he caused as a vampire, a creature of the night. They fell in love. Their love was whole and pure. But the curse said that if Angel had one true moment of hapiness, his soul would be taken away, and he'd go back to his evil ways. So Buffy and Angel made love, and the next morning, Angel became Angellus, Buffy's worst enemy. Angel, threatening to open the gates of hell, wanted to kill Buffy, because it was his duty as a vampire. But Buffy killed Angel and stopped him from ending the world, sending him to hell instead. Angel was cast out of hell though, and he came back a saved man, thanks to a spell performed by Willow, Buffy's trusted friend. The love affair was reborn, though in the end, Angel knew their love could never be real. And he left. In a touching scene, Buffy, at the senior prom alone, was shocked when Angel walked in the door and shared with her one last dance, before he departed for Los Angeles.
Matt Booth: Yeah, I remember, you make us watch those DVD's every day. Mike Bilodeau: But you don't understand, Buffy, after returning from the dead herself, only wanted to feel, so she began a sexual relationship with vampire Spike, a vampire with a chip in his head, so he can't bite humans. A vampire who was once a mortal enemy, turned friend when Angellus stole his girl, Drusilla, now loved the slayer. Buffy finds herself strangely attracted to Spike and they screw like bunnies. Spike continues falling in love with Buffy, but
Buffy admits she was just using him for the sex. Spike leaves, and returns with a soul, a feat to impress Buffy, because he underwent the tests of hell to get it back. Buffy, however, has no time to be impressed with Spike's feat. The First has arrived, and he's threatening to open the gates of Hell once again, and eliminate the
Slayer and her potential replacements. Buffy must protect the replacements in training from the First, a demon that can take the form of any dead person. The only problem with the First, he can't take coporal form. Therefore he cannot touch anybody. But his agents can. His agents? Caleb, an evil preacher obsessed with killing "dirty" girls. The First gives Caleb his power to take out the Slayer and the potential Slayers, so that he could unleash an army of Super Vampires on the World, and take over the World. Buffy must stop the First, but her obsession leads her into a deadly trap that takes the eye of Xander, her best friend. The potentials realize she is careless and ask Buffy to leave. Spike finds her, in an abandoned home. There he confesses his love for her once again, and Buffy tells Spike to hold her and spend the night. Buffy leaves while Spike sleeps to fight Caleb to the death. During the fight,
Caleb gets the upper hand, and ANGEL RETURNS TO SAVE HER LIFE!
Buffy is happy, but must finish Caleb off. Buffy takes Caleb down with a knife to the gut. Buffy and Angel kiss, with Spike watching from the shadows. WHO WILL SHE END UP WITH! THE MAN SHE LOVES, BUT
CANNOT FIND HAPPINESS WITH? OR THE MAN SHE IS TO AFRAID TO ADMIT
SHE LOVES? She has to make that choice tonight.
Matt Booth: But Mike, why are the WLW TV Cameras here? What does this have to do with the WLW?
Mike Bilodeau: It has everything to do with the WLW Don't you see? Matt Booth: NO! Mike Bilodeau: Because the WLW could learn a lot from Buffy! What about Willow, the shy computer geek in high school, turned savvy witch lesbian in college? She falls in love for the first time with
Tara, but Tara is scared that Willow is using too much dark magic.
Willow promises to stop, but can't. The magic is too seductive, like a drug. It overpowers her. So Tara leaves, crushing Willow.
Willow vows to never do magic again. Tara eventually returns and the love affair continues. Until the day a stray bullet meant for
Buffy crashes through the house window and kills Tara. Willow, enraged, turns back into Dark Willow, a witch capabale of destroying the world as we know it. Willow extracts her revenge on Tara's killers, but the pain isn't gone. The only way to rid her of her pain is to destroy the world. She's about to do it too, but good old Xander stops her, confessing he loves Willow, and her friends love Willow. There's no reason to feel alone. There's no reason to hurt anymore. So Willow doesn't destroy the world, but needs witch rehab. Giles takes her to London, and she returns when the magic is under control. But the Slayer needs the witch, and her magic. So
Willow attempts to do spells, but every time, the magic becomes so powerful she starts reverting back to Dark Willow. HOW IS WILLOW
GOING TO HELP BUFFY AGAINST THE SUPER VAMPIRES TONIGHT? WILL SHE
FACE HER FEARS AND EMBRACE THE MAGIC THAT HAS MADE HER WHO SHE IS
TODAY? OR WILL SHE FALL COMPLETELY INTO DARKNESS FOREVERMORE? HOW
WILL SHE HELP BUFFY? Matt Booth: Mike, this is pointless. Mike Bilodeau: POINTLESS? What about Xander? He already lost an eye. What else is he willing to sacrifice in the name of good?
Xander has always loved Buffy. But he also loved Anya, a former vengeance demon. He left Anya at the alter, because he wasn't ready to be a husband. Tonight, will the ordinary guy be able to help in the fight for the world? And what about Dawn, Buffy's supernatural sister? She used to be the key of the Gods. Will that key be instrumental in defeating the dark evil? What about Anya? She usually runs at the first sign of trouble? How can she possibly help? Faith? The redeemed evil slayer. She's back to help her old enemy Buffy save the world. Is she really redeemed, or will she taste the sweet dark again and never return from the grasps of evil? There's so much going on in one show, how could they possibly just end it?
Matt Booth: Mike, you're getting yourself too stressed out. There's no need for it. Mike Bilodeau: It comes down to a choice. Who will stand? Who will fall? Who will Buffy choose? Will Spike ever get his kiss from Buffy? Is there a future with Buffy and Angel regardless of
Angel's curse? Will Faith continue her slayer duties? Will the potentials survive? Will Willow ever use her magic for good? Will
Dawn save her sister? Will Xander survive? There are so many questions, that I need answered right now! NOW DAMMIT!
Matt Booth: Mike, calm down! Mike Bilodeau: DON'T TELL ME TO CALM DOWN! BOW YOUR HEAD! BOW YOUR DAMN HEADS! (Scene fades to black.)
