[RSPWF] Rookie Of Year Candidate and Free Agent

23 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yep...this is Logan Gunz again. I have posted again hoping to garner some more offers. I am currently considering the ASWF and the CNWL, but would like to see who else is out there. I am looking for a reputable federation...open or closed flash. I am willing to bring either Logan, or his *evil* cruiserweight brother, Jonathon....or both into a good fed. I'm also looking for a little exposure for Logan for the RSPWF awards this year. Below is my latest RP from
SWF (NWC), Logan is preparing for a forest match with "Psycho" Sam Leever.
Though not updated in a month...his homepage is http://members.aol.com/LoganGunz/wrestler.html Sample RP (The lush green trees and leaves of the forest litter the skyline as the camera turns to the sky. The camera stares to the canopy of the forest, then begins to follow the trunk of the tree as it descends toward the forest floor. The bark of the tree is a dark brown and secured tightly to the tree. Down the camera pans, past a few lone branches....past a flock of birds with feathers that are like a rainbow. It continues down, past a vine that curls towards the top of the tree, threatening to strangle the life from it's very trunk. Down to the floor of the forest...it is considerably darker down here. The canopy of the forest has choked the ground of sunlight, but the vegetation is still quite lush. The sound of animals stalking behind the trees and drowning themselves in the camoflage that the forest provides.)
(Suddenly...out from behind the tree steps a figure. The camera is startled by the sudden appearance of the man and drops back a few steps. Since the distance is changed, the camera is now out of focus. After a few seconds of blur....the picture begins to sharpen. As it sharpens, a man is coming into view. He is wearing a pair of faded blue jeans, a pair of worn leather gloves, and a blue bandanna on his head. The camera notices that he is not wearing a shirt....his large pecs are exposed to the forest air, the panthers tattooed on his arms stand watch at his flanks. The stare on his face, the burning of his steel green eyes tell the world that this man means business. He stands, chest expanded, arms at his side.....desire burning from his soul.)
Logan: Sam Leever....the appocalypse of your career draws ever closer. (Logan stands completely still...his lips barely move as he speaks.) Logan: It has walked through the valley, drug through the desert, reached the top of the mountain....and now it peers down into the forest. It stalks around every tree....careens through the branches.....and stalks your every move. With the eyes of a cat, it watches you Leever...it has been watching you. It watched as you interfered in the Emerald City title match between myself and Shooting
Star....it watched closely. Close attention was payed as I Shotgun Pressed
Shooting Star and covered him....the match was all but over...the Emerald City title was in my grasp. Then....in one quick moment...it was stolen from me. In that same moment, Sam Leever, your entire future was taken from you.
(Logan turns ninety degrees to his right and starts to walk among the folieage of the forest.)
Logan: Perhaps the forest is the perfect arena for our battle. The forest...the end of the road. The home of the devil, according to the Puritans.
Deep....dark...merciless and unforgiving. Once you enter the forest...once you walk among the trees....once you begin to feel the despair, it will forever grip your soul.
(The bushes and ferns are nearly waist high on the six feet, four inch Logan
Gunz. He wades through them...forcefully pushing aside all those that intercede in his progress.)
Logan: Not only the forest to deal with Leever....but the best predator in it. The killer that makes the forest his personal playground. He moves silently amongst the trees...he creeps through the bushes...and lies in wait around each and every corner. Cold and unfeeling...his green eyes beading down upon you....waiting, he prepares himself for the moment that you will slip. That you stray just off the beaten path...that you venture only a few feet into the darkness. That, Sam Leever, is the time that he is most dangerous. That is the time when your life is no longer in your grasp. When you walk into a forest dominated by the panther...life and death are at his discretion...
(Logan dissapears behind another tree...the camera waits for him out the other side....but no luck. It steps back behind the tree...and finds only the forest floor...)
Logan: Nothing in the forest is as it seems. The moment you think you have the advantage....the moment you think you are ahead.
(The camera pans up, just in time to see Logan spring from a limb and land on the forest floor.)
Logan: Is the moment that you are truly at the back of the pack. The forest Sam
Leever...the perfect ending to your story. Like those before you...and those yet to come, you admire the beauty of the forest...but when you venture in, you'll find your worst nightmare....one man...me....Logan Gunz...."The Lone
Enforcer".
(As Logan finishes that statement...he grins. This grin is not one of chuckles nor of happiness, it's the grin that an alligator may posses before finishing a kill...the camer fades to black.)
