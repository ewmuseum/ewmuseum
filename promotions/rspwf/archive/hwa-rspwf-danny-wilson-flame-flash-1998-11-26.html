[HWA/RSPWF] Danny Wilson flame/flash !!!!!!!! :)

26 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up on a bench. On that bench sits a man. The man is wearing nothing but a jock strap, and is running around in circles. He takes out a bag with a bottle in it, he takes a sip, then passes out. He wakes up the next day, he puts on some pants, and a shirt, and starts walking, the man is non other than Ian Walsh, but wait, it can't be, he is dead. The camera pans again, and we can make out " The Wannabe Peoples KooK" Danny Wilson, he looks at the camera, and speaks.]
DW: Well howdy, look at me, I am a hick, so let's all go to a rodeo!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!
[Wilson starts walking, but stumbles over a baby. Wilson kicks the baby in the fed, knocking it out.]
DW: Darn it Kotceki! [Wilson picks uo the baby and does a Liger Bomb to it. A Man comes over and starts beating on Wilson with a night stick.]
DW: THE HUMANITY!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! MALICW GREEN!!! MALICE GREEN!!!!!!! [Wilson starts running though, he climbs into a tree, only to see Ronnie D, and
Mark Nutter, er Cutter making out. Wilson runs away in shock.]
DW: A Canuck, and a Hick, together?!?!?!!?!?? SAY IT AIN'T SO!!! [Wilson runs some more, but trips over a drunk Goat and lands on a lobster.] DW: NO, THE SPUNKY LOBSTER!!! NO!!!!!!!!! [Wilson turns, but runs into a man who has black on. The man is Dr Jam 666. Jam takes out his flame-thrower, but shoots it the wrong way, killing him.]
DW: LOOSSSSSSSEEEEEEEERRRRRRRRRRR! [Wilson finally stops when he sees a Homeless guy. Wilson drops a quarter and as the bum goes to pick it up, he steals his bottles, Wilson runs till he hits a car. As the camera fades, you can here Wilson yell "MWHAHAHA"
