RP Scene - The inside of a wrestling arena, it is the middle of a G-Pro card. The match has just finished and the announcer is in the ring.
<Announcer> Next it is my honour, my pleasure and my privelege to introduce to you the newest wrestler on our roster. A man who has taken G-Pro by storm and invited in by the fed head, [name withheld]
due to his skills and talents making him into a valued free agent. There is a reaction from the crowd <Announcer> Accompanied to the ring by his friends, Konnan (Evenflow by Pearl Jam starts to play and the crowd pop), The Rock and Midian the man who is the eyes of the ministry here isssssssssssssssssssss
The crowd are at fever pitch cheering madly Announcer - SABUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUUU424 Sabu424 appears with The Rock, Midian and K-Dogg. They laugh and congratulate each other on making it to G-Pro. They walk to the ring,
Midian is making the shape of a star with his hands, The Rock shoots an eyebrow at the camera and K-Dogg bawls "Arriva La Ratza".
(signs in the crowd "[detail withheld] is a quitter", "Janis was right",
"Hulkamania is runnin wild", "Nada is a nacho", "[name withheld] is a nobody", "

<Announcer> Gentlemen please if we can conduct the interview. Sabu424 goes over to him and is given a mic <Sabu424> Well we made it to G-Pro. The world of wrestling doubted Sabu424 mania but we made it and I have my friends to help me too, GIVE IT UP FOR K-DOGG, MIDIAN AND THE ROCKKKKKKKKKK The crowd are wild with happiness <Sabu424> AS you can see I am the Icon of the G-Pro and I will beat any challenge that is sent my way.
Midian makes a star sign with his hand <Sabu424> THIS MAN IS A GENIUS. MIDIAN IS UNDERRATED. WORSHIP HIS GENIUS. The crowd cheer <Sabu424> And people say Midian is not over The crowd boo <Sabu424> That tells me otherwise. Midian makes a star shape with his hand, K-Dogg yells "Arriva La Ratza" <Announcer> And what do you say to the people who complain about the way you say things. <The Rock> Hey Jabroni you need to shut your goddam mouth and listen to the Rock. The Rock says you need to judge a man on what the message is and not how the message is sent. If you smell what The Rock is cookin.
The crowd pop like crazy and a chant of "Sabu424 rocks" starts. <Announcer> I think we all agree (Midian makes the shape of a star with his hands, and the announcer points at him) Does he talk.
<K-Dogg> He is silent and violent. Every man in the ring laughs like a devil. <Sabu424> We are now here in G-Pro and WE are going to win.The title will be mine. Midianis my tag partner and we will WIN.
The crowd are popping like mad. Endorsements "GREAT JOB!
