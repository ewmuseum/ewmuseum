Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The U.S. Champ "The Franchise Sony" Record- 2-0-0 Last beat: Tim Beck Who has beat me?: No one [United States Title Tournament Finals!] Sony V. "TGDS" Tim Beck This match ended up in a no rules/No DQ match, both men were busted open and
Sony came out on top...these two don't like eachother very much
[The sun beems threw the window and is throwing its rays onto Sony and he puts on his shades, and he smiles as he puts his U.S Title on his sholder and then walks out of the door of the beach house.] [He is wearing Blue Nautica swimming trunks and on the bottom in Orange is reads NAUTICA. The wind blows on his pants and flurishes his hair and he takes off his sunglasses and puts them on his shirt. Then a couple kids are playing with a beach-ball and he hits it and then he begins to speak as he pats his gold.]
"The Franchise" Sony- Tim Beck, this gold that i hold, you think that was going to be yours? Tim Beck, i have held this WWA
U.S. Gold twice. You think that i dont deserve something like this? Well you don't especially when you lose to the great one....
"The Franchise" Sony- As i see it, i have a match_up with Trinity this guy is known for defeating people, and i give him his due respect. Going under another alias [Shotgun Messiah] he was one of the IWF's best. But this is a different time, different place and i don't have any more repsect for him. After changing his name and thinking he's cool. Well, this is not your fed this is my federation.
"The Franchise" Sony- Trinity, your going to hit Rock Bottom as i step into the ring and give the crowd a show. Where they came from, how much money they payed for their seats, no matter where they are sitting, they are going to get the best show of their lives because as I as a wrestler am intitled to give them the best show of my ability and i am going to fight you to the death, and then some.
As i get my hand raised, talk to me and maybe we can work something out. [Fade]
