[Supersonic] Sample roleplay, lemme know what you think

7 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a roleplay for a new fed, the SCWA. This is the first sample roleplay I will put up, of course this is not the best one of my choices to post. Any comments or criticism (constructive preferred) just let me know.
------------------- [The scene opens on an outdoor area, obviously no where near a city. A river slices the middle of the scene, with banks on the sides which lead to forests.
The trees show off a variety of colors, from red, to orange, to yellow, to brown.] [The camera pans to the banks, where a man sits. He sits on the side, facing the water. He is wearing a pair of jeans, a black t-shirt and a blue fleece over it, because it is cold outside. He is also wearing a pair of Nike sneakers. His hair is brushed flat, with nothing special about it. He is none other than Supersonic.]
Supersonic: Since I am fighting a guy by the name of Bryan Forrester, I figured
I'd take a look at what this guy represents. He's proabably some kind of bear-wrestling logger or something. But you know what? I really don't care.
Considering that fact that the SCWA is a new fed, everyone here is on an equal playing field. But that is only until Friday. After that, everyone will realize who the true best in this federation is. And that is none other than me.
What wrestlers here can claim that they are legends? Other than me, none. So what does that tell you? That I am the best thing to hit this fed, and I will keep that title until I win an even better one, the SCWA World Title! But until that day comes, I might as well make some rather bold statements that each and every one of you wrestlers and fans need to know. I do not take any crap. From anybody. You have a problem with me? Well, let's get it on in the ring. I assure you that you will realize how right I really was. Even if I don't change your opinion, you'll still be feeling pretty sore. And after I put each and every one of you back-first on the mat, you will all realize how right I was. Because when you hear the ref smacking the mat three times while you're on your back, you know what that means, don't you? It means that I have just racked up another win, and you're gonna hit the showers. _ONCE AGAIN_!
[Supersonic picks up a rock and tosses it into the river.] Supersonic: The rock sinks. Just like my opponents will. They will sink to the bottom of the rankings. Sink in their chances of winning. But I will just keep on floating. I will hardly need to tread water to become the World Champion, because I know for a _FACT_ that that title will be around my waste in no time.
I promise each and every one of you fans that I will be the first ever SCWA champion. And when I say that, I am speaking FACT. Not fiction, like most of these wrestlers like to say.
They like to inflate themselves to mass proportions. Make something out of nothing. I however, do not need to. Because my actions in the ring back up each and every word I say. But most people have to go around saying how great they are, but they sport around an 0-5 record. Give me an L-O-S-E-R. What does that spell? Figure it out, smart guy.
[Supersonic picks up a nearby stick and tosses it away.] Supersonic: Now Bryan, I haven't really mentioned you much so far. Frankly, I don't really know much about you. Actually, I know _NOTHING_. But will that stop me? No, not really. People like me are able to surmount things like that.
It doesn't give me any type of dilemma. People like you, on the other hand, sometimes worry me. You just stick to one wrestling style, never making any adjustments. Sure, at first it works pretty well. You're winning matches. It's all you know. But soon people catch on. Then what? You're screwed. Well, people like me are able to make those adjustments. And that's why I succed so much.
Sure, throwing a guy to the mat may be great, especially if you're so much bigger. But once other people learn that's your only move, they will make simple adjustments, and before you know it, you're on line at the unemployment office. So when it happens to you Bryan, don't come crying to me, because I know that the SCWA is your only change at making it big. Too bad for you all your plans are going to explode right in your face. And all because of me.
You know, sometimes I feel kinda bad about crushing some kid's dreams of making it big. But I can't help it. I'm just too damn good.
Signing a match against me is like signing your own death papers. And at the count of three by the ref, it's as if you're pronounced dead. It almost makes me feel bad.
[Supersonic smirks.] But not quite. Bryan, I bet that as a kid you dreamed of becoming a pro wrestler, of winning titles and beating up people, yadda yadda. I had the same dream. Only mine came true. Yours is like a ticking time bomb, ready to explose. And your fuse is running short. And on November 13th, it will go off, and you will be sent into the basement of the rankings.
All thanks to me. God I love my job. [Scene fades.]
