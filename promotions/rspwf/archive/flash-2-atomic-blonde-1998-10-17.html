[Flash 2] Atomic Blonde

17 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
These aren't really in any sort of order... _________________ <The camera looks on through a rusted chainlink fence, to a wide shot of a run down park filled with broken playground equipment and a basketball court left in ruins. Stray boards lie in the dirt, surrounded by broken bottles and littered trash. Several small children can be seen in the background passing the camera in an out of focus blur. The camera pans to the left to see the
Atomic Blonde, who is wearing a long brown overcoat, behind the fence with his fingers gripping the jagged links of the fence tightly>
"Nice view, eh? I'd bet if you asked NBW's finest, they'd say it doesn't look quite this tragic through the tinted window of a limo, though. Growing up, the kids spent every waking hour of every dying day here. This is where the kids came to shoot hoops, shoot up, and just shoot the shit... and this is where I came as a child to get pissed on and laughed at."
<Blonde drops his head and spits down onto the cold, hard dirt benieth him>
"While the rest you were choking on your silver spoons, I was licking mud and playing in puddles of urine. Ever play dodge ball with bricks? You dodge that
F(censored)ing brick, or you don't get a second chance to play! Ever been force fed a pile of dirt? I doubt it. That would just tarnish those silver spoons."
<Blonde squeezes the fence tighter and repeadedly brings his head smashing into it over and over, opening a large gash in his forehead. The fence rumbles and shakes, crashing harder and harder against his skull. As the blood rolls down the bridge of his nose, he rests his forehead against the fence, squinting his eyes... slightly trembling, and continues...>
"WELL, THEY MADE ME EAT DIRT, GODDAMMIT!!! They treated me like <censored> and wondered why I came back everyday. I came back because I F(censored)ing like the taste of dirt!! I stood in this playground for ten years feeling sorry for myself. Everyday, going home with a few new buises and a few more enemies...
but always sheading a few less tears. I'm gone from this life... I threw it all out the window when I walked through the front doors of NBW headquarters. Now
I've got a new playground. The rusted fence is replaced with three ring ropes... and the bloodstained concrete has taken the form of a bloodstained canvas. I've got a new playground, and this time *I'm* the bully!"
<Blonde slowly lets his hands slide down the links of the fence and drops to his knees. Again, he tightens his grip and begins running his face across the fence, opening the cut on his forehead wider and wider>
"Billy Reel... welcome to my playgound. Wednesday, you got a taste of how the
Atomic Blonde plays... and I have a feeling you won't wanna play with me ever again. You wanna come to that ring waving those hands in the air like a low class moron? Good... because I hope you feel like JUST THAT when I pull your lanky arms out of the sockets and beat the hell out of you with them. If you think you can step foot in MY playground, you'll be dragging your bruised ass and your limp arms back to the lockeroom before that bell rings. They ran me out of here once... now I'd just like to see you and the rest of these bastards try to run me out of this one. Try to rub my face in the dirt one more time..."
<Blonde reaches down, picks up a small chunk of busted concrete, and slowley tosses it from hand to hand>
"I just hope you can dodge the bricks." Mark Handler of: "Your Mother" Sorry... I don't do macros.
