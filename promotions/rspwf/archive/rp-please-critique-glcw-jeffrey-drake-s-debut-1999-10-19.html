[RP, PLEASE CRITIQUE] [GLCW] Jeffrey Drake's debut

19 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera fades into a ritzy, expensive...yet very beautiful section of
Beverly Hills, California....and we all know what this means...this is the living area of new GLCW superstar "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake. We see the many palm dress surrouding Rodeo Drive, and then, the mansions emerge...many famous celebrities make their homes here in town..and Drake is probably the most obscure..but, he lives here, among various other folks livin' "The Good
Life". As Drake's mansion appears in the distance....the stone fountains...marble walkway....freshly mowed lawn...luxurious cars in the driveway...and the metal gate blocking the entranceway...with "JD" emblazoned on them. The gates open, as Drake was expecting this interview to take place, and the GLCW Camera Crew drives eagerly into this paradise...as they pull into the driveway behind Drake's 1969 Chevy Camero....Drake is already standing in the doorway, waiting for our faithful cameraman to step onto the walkway, and haul his prosterior into the mansion. Drake has his eyes shaded by dark sunglasses, and his black Armani suit shows him with a touch of class and style...his neatly pulled back black hair shows that he is all business...yes my friends, this shows him as the true "Cornerstone" that he is. Drake motions for the cameraman to come inside, and as our cameraman ogles the double walkway leading upstairs...Drake instead pulls him inside to the bar area, where Drake sits down, pours himself some champgane, and begins his first interview for
Great Lakes Championship Wrestling.]
["TC" JD] Well...well...well...finally....my first match in the GLCW...my first chance at greatness. I'm already billed as a top cruiserweight...so, yes, they know what a true talent they have on their hands..and when it comes time for All- Stars of Wrestling at the GLCW Centre...Ann Arbor,
Michigan..I prove just how valuable of an asset I will prove to be down the line. I will....but that is for some other time....right now, three other men stand in the way.
...THE MASKED SUPERSTAR JOBBER ...CURMUDGEON ...AND...[evil grin] MONEY DRIVEN...MIKE SEBASTIAN... ...I'll get to Mikey later...because I have a little "history" with him. ...But, Superstar Jobber Nimrod...whatever you prefer to be called....now, you and me have no past...no major rivalry that got heated up over time...no trash talking..hell, before signing on with GLCW...I had no idea you existed...but,
tomorrow night.... ....I'm gonna make you hate me. ...By overshadowing you...and making you a loser in front of a GLCW hometown crowd...my, my my, you're in for a horrible night....friend.
...Curmudgeon....same for you with what I said to jobber boy before...you're gonna hate my guts after tomorrow night...and then you'll probably drag me into some meaningless feud that will just make you look completely pathetic because you want to prove you're somewhat better...yes, that is what will happen..[sighs] Oh well...if that's what you'll want come the Wednesday Morning
Hangover...fine...I'll take you up...but, we'll take the task at hand first.
...Now ...Ol' Mikey Sebastian... ...Buddy...Pal...Mi Amigo... ...MY WORST ENEMY!!... Mikey...me and you...one more time...forget the past...screw the present..I want you this time...and I want revenge...you took me for a fool last time, and
I was eay prey...but tomorrow night......man oh man...will you be in for a shock...
...CORNERSTONE KICK...1,2,3.... ...Midnight isn't striking tomorrow night.... ...STOP YOUR CLOCKS AT 11:59 PM!!!... ...Mike...WELCOME TO MY JUNGLE.... ...YOU'RE GONNA DIE.... ...Bitch. [Camera fades out.] Jeff H. --- Handler Of: "The Cornerstone" Jeffrey Drake (GLOW, LAW) #179 1998 RSPWF 200 The Most Underrated Person in eWrestling Aw dammit...gimme a good angle!!!
