RP for you to poop on...I mean critique! :P

12 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
COURTESY OF ROBBIE-TV [Scene fades in on what looks like the courtyard of a large Victorian estate. Girls in thong bikini's are briefly seen waving at the camera and giggling. We pan around the courtyard. There's a Mercedes stretch-limo parked outside the sprawling mansion. A pair of doberman's chained near the door make sure the visitor keeps his wits about him. The camera man is obviously keeping his distance and reluctant to enter the house, as the dogs snarl and bare their teeth. Then the door opens and a large figure emerges, clad in a black suit and cap. He looks grimly at the camera crew for a moment. The tag on his coat reads BUTLER. He then yells something incomprehensible in German to the doberman's and they immediately become docile and quiet. He then motions for us to enter]
[We fade in on the inside of the house. The walls are lined with famous pieces of art work and the floor is covered, from wall to wall, with expensive
Persian carpeting. We enter the living room.
The camera pans around the living room as the butler stands near the door with his arms folded. Then we hear a voice behind us]
"Hey, yo, this way...." [voice trails off] [The camera turns around. Standing there is ARMAGEDDONS favorite drunken stereotype and future World champ, Chavez. He is wearing a T-shirt that reads
I WONDER WHAT THE POOR PEOPLE ARE DOING, a pair of shredded and torn jeans, cowboy boots and a bandanna. He is a stark contrast to his equisite surroundings. Nonethelss, he throws himself on the leather couch as a couple of girls in thongs instinctively enter the room and sit on either side of the drunken Indian. They both smile vacantly]
"I know everybody wants to know what the richest man in ARMAGEDDON is up to. Hey,. you didn't know? Go see my savings. Three thousand grand baby! So consider this a little sneak preview of an R-TV exclusive.
[A graphic appears on the screen in an elaborate font; LIFESTYLES OF THE DRUNK
AND FAMOUS. The graphic fades off screen to Chavez grinning]
"I thought of it myself! Well, your rolemodel is about to get richer! The real fun starts at Catalyst of Champions when I defeat paleface scum Page to become the champ. And I'll get to that piss pot in a minute. I'd like to take this time to talk a little bit about me. Everybody wants to know all about the man behind the myth. The person behind the legend. Well, it all started when..."
[Chavez voice is reduced to a meaningless drone as he rambles on and on. The camera man is preoccupied with the mystical and sacred, and as of now unopened bottle of Sidewinder 69[tm] GOLD. It sits in a glass case with a huge handwritten sign over it that says TOUCH THIS BOTTLE AND YOU WILL DIE, LOVE,
CHAVEZ]
"And that's when I got - Hey, what are you looking at?" [Chavez realizes the object of attention was the mystical bottle. He shakes his head and sighs]
"I feel sorry for anybody who goes near that bottle. I don't know what I would do to anybody who would dare touch, or God forbid, drink my sacred
Sidewinder 69 [tm] GOLD. DON'T ANYWHERE GO NEAR IT!!!!"
[Chavez places himself between the camera man and the glass case bearing the bottle] "There's a curse on the bottle anyway. If anybody should grab that and not answer to me, they might have to deal with
Pawatatatawahamatatatamawahahachuchucha, the Navaho god of VERY bad luck. Now, where was I? Oh yes. Page. Page, Page, Page. You correctly stated that this is your second chance at a World Title in three months. However, what you failed to mention was that your last shot was against a three foot midget..."
[As Chavez speaks, an image appears on the 20" TV. We zoom in on the on-screen image and notice that it's a video clip of Shawn Page, or rather, a bad look-a- like, wrestling a midget in a green and red bodysuit while canned head is heard in the background]
"That's right. It was a midget. And even though Page fought valiantly against this...little man. In the end..."
[The midget bites "Page" on the knee causing him to yell "Mommy!"] "It was the midget wrestler who prevailed. The end!" [A slow motion clip is shown of the actual match. Shawn Page gets locked in
Troll's revenger and passes out, losing the match which is in the confines of a chain-link cage]
"But the stakes are so much higher. So much higher. And it appears that I and
Professional boy are now working for the same company. What a coincidence.
Welcome to R-TV, Page. Just remember..." [The video footage on the 20" TV fades to snow. Chavez is getting a shoulder rub from the two girls in thongs]
"We aren't just your employers!" [Chavez smiles insincerely into the camera as if cutting an ad for an insurance company] "We're your friends, too!"
