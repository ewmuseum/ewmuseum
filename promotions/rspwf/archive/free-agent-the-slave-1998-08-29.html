[Free Agent] The Slave

29 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am listening to federation offers, but only from top notch federations. [Fade in.] [The camera opens up on a beautiful setting. The scene is obviously in the countryside as we see hills and trees and animals. The sun is out and shines down on the hills. The camera then starts to move and it seems as though the camera man is beginning to walk forward. He seems to be joined by another camera man as we can hear low mumbling. ]
[After a bit, the pair arrive at the large gates in front of a large, impressive looking mansion. The gates are large, too high to climb and they are also locked. One of the camera man is seen moving nervously forward and then pressing the button which is on the gates. Instantly, a door is opened from the mansion and out steps an elegantly dressed butler. He comes over to the gates and asks the man what they want. The other camera man replys that they would like to speak to the Slave. The butler gives them a filthy look, but allows them to come through the gates.]
Butler: ''Follow me.'' [The camera men are allowed through the gates and we now get a good look at the garden of the house. There is a wide range of colourful plants in the garden, and it looks quite splendid. The camera pans upwards and we now see the amazing height of the house. The butler escorts the men into the mansion, right into a long, wide hallway.]
Butler: ''Edward, there are some people here to see you.'' [You get quite a surprise as out, from the front room steps a very shabbily dressed man, sporting a very plan expression. The man's face is very white and he doesn't look to be feeling very well. He is a tall man though, and of medium build. He has short, dirty blonde hair and the camera shows his brown eyes.]
CM 1: ''Excuse me, are you the Slave?'' Man: ''Yeth, I am.'' [Suddenly, we hear a loud voice from inside the front room.] Man 2: ''Edward! Who are you talking to out there. When did I give you my permission to speak to strangers!''
[The second camera pops his head into the front room, just then. He stops and gazes in awe at the huge room, elegantly furnished and decorated.]
CM 2: ''We are sorry to bother you sir, but we are from the CyberNetic Wrestling Federation, squared. We were hoping to get some comments from the
Slave.'' [The man sighs, then waves the camera men in. He motions for the man not holding the camera to take a seat. The Slave walks into the room and goes to take a seat.]
Man 2: ''Edward! I did not give you permission to sit on one of my confortable, leather seats! Go and stand in the corner where you belong!''
[The Slave walks over to the corner of the room with his head down. He says nothing but brings his head back up to watch the camera men.]
Man 2: ''Allow me to introduce myself, you can call me James. I am James
Connolly, one of the world's richest and most important people, as you can probably tell from the nice little home that I've thrown together over the years.''
[The reporter doesn't know what to say, so he just smiles slightly and nodds his head.] CM 2: ''Actually, we were hoping to get some comments with the Slave. We want to talk to him about the CNWF².''
James: ''Very well. However, you should know that I handle all such matters. Basically, he is what his name suggests. Nothing but the Slave. Let me tell you a bit about him, eh? When I first saw this man, he repulsed me. He still does to this day. He lived on the streets, got in trouble with the police, he was the regular punk on the streets.''
CM 2: ''If that's what you think of him, why did you get associated with him then?'' James: ''I was coming to that. I have a dream you see. My dream is to own the finest wrestling federation in the world. There was a problem though. The CNWF² was currently in that spot. I decided that I needed to get it out of the way.
So what I did was, I went out to find someone who I felt could take down the competition. I was told about a wrestling training camp which was taking place in Boston, Massachuttes. I went along to see the talent. There that day, there were many talented boys. I felt that with his build and the fact that he can take a good punch, the Slave was one of them. Although, there were others. .
.'' CM 2: ''Then why did you choose the Slave, or Edward, as you call him?'' James: ''The reason was simple. Edward was a poor boy. He has nothing. The rest of them were snobs, who's parents bought them a boat for their every birthday. I realized that they would not work with me, the arrogant little brats that they were. However, when I saw Edward, I saw a boy who was depending on me to give him something in life. I saw a boy who knew that he couldn't afford to lose, because he knew the consequences. I saw -the- boy who I decided was going to take down the CyberNetic Wrestling Federation, squared for me. It took him a bit to get in, but he managed it and now, the next part of his mission will slowly start to unfold.''
CM 2: ''Yes, it will. On Thursday afternoon, Slave, you will take on Thorne
Timberwolf, an exciting new-comer to the CNWF². Do you think that you can handle him?''
Slave: ''I. . .'' James: ''Don't you dare try to answer without my permission Edward! You know that is my job! Apologise to me, right now!''
Slave: ''I'm sorry.'' James: ''Better. Now, back to your idiotic question. Of course I think that
Edward here can handle Thorne Timberwolf. I am a buisness man but it seems as though you are taking me for a fool, yes? Didn't you hear what I've been saying. Do you think I would have taken on a boy who could not handle Thorne
Timberwolf?''
CM 2: ''I suppose not.'' James: ''You suppose not. Edward, tell these people if you are going to beat
Thorne Timberwolf.''
Slave: ''I am, sir.'' James: ''And why, Edward?'' Slave: ''Because you want me to, sir.'' James: ''Good answer. You see, moronic fans of the CNWF². The Slave knows that he must beat Thorne Timberwolf. Not because his reputation is on the line.
Because _my_ reputation is on the line. He knows what will happen is he damages that reputation, don't you Edward!''
Slave: ''Yes, sir.'' James: ''Right. I think you reporters have got everything you need to know. Be off with you.''
CM 2: ''There was just one more thing. . .'' James: ''I don't care. Good day.'' CM 2: ''Edward, do you think. . .'' James: ''How -DARE- you go against my command. DANIEL!'' [The butler comes back into the ring, closely followed by two mean looking bodyguards. The camera men quickly get up and leave the room, and are shoved out of the door by the butler. They start to walk back to the gates, seemingly angry. A few seconds later, the butler presses a button on the door, and
Doberman's are released from a pen at the side of the house. The camera men see them and drop the camera, then scramble towards the gates. The camera is pointed at them and they just manage to escape before the dogs can get them.
From back inside the mansion, we hear laughing. . . hysterical laughing.] [Fade to black.]
