The Mistress Sonia RP [Critique]

3 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Not opening RP so you may get a little confused [[The scene fades in at Tampa where Sizzler will be held. Mistress Sonia, for once, is wearing conservative clothing. She's wearing a cotton white tank top and some denim pants with tennis shoes. She's in the theme park Bush Gardens looking at the elephants... mostly at their tusks. Children crowd around her to get her autograph and with a cheerful smile she gives it to them. The parents are surprised on seeing a wrestler act like this and they believe it's about damn time this happened]]
[Mistress Sonia] [smiling gently] Isn't life grand... all life is precious right? Now, excuse me children... I've got to go now... And remember
Children.... Always brush your teeth. [[She heads for the exit... smiling]] [parent] Now there goes a wrestler that could be a role model. God bless her. [[Mistress Sonia goes out of the theme park.... smiling, she goes to her car.... smiling,.... she enters the hotel.... smiling.... she enters her room.... pissed off.]]
[Mistress Sonia] If I have to smile anymore I might as well kill myself. All those children [[smugged]] bright eye and cheery [[her normal voice]] It's enough to make ANYONE sick. And the only thing I saw intresting in those elephants are those tusks.... they would make great thrones. Maybe a good back scratcher. Oh god. Well, here I am in Tampa. The place where Sizzler will be held... I'm here, in the federation's expense of course, to start getting the sights of this cheery town. Now if you excuse me... [[Tugs on her clothes]]
I've got to get out of this awful outfit and into something more casual. I don't know what are in those stylists mind, thinking this was "in".
[[She goes into the bathroom and throws out the white tank top, then the denim pants, and then out goes the shoes right through the window. She comes out tying her hair into a ponytail and she's wearing a leather tank top, leather pants, leather boots, and her six foot long leather whip. She gives a big sigh of relief]]
[Mistress Sonia] Finally! Something comfortable... not much I can say for this so called "four star hotel suite"... it's not worth the $150 a day fee, in the federation's expense of course. And the room service is TERRIBLE, I ask for their best wine which costed 149 bucks, in the federation's expense of course, and they send me something 34 years old... I don't know why I don't have them go to get food from another restaurant, sure it will cost more but I'm sure the diamond wouldn't mind. After all I am a Mistress, and Mistresses deserve nothing better but better than the best. And frankly I can't find it here, so I settled for this dump. The federation should be happy that I settled for this.
I might as well be sleeping in a dumpster. Now lets get to Business. The silent sisters broke their silence. Isn't that just crazy. Well, anyway here they are thinking that because that Tara Marshall defeated Nikki J that they are the best women wrestlers in the ICW. Well, news flash girls, I defeated Nikki J as well, and not only that, I defeated every one of you. I kicked each of your asses, starting with Tara, than Arista, than Tiff. And then they figured something out... which is hard to believe but it's true, they actually figured something out by themselves. We have to give them a round of applause for that, but later. They figured out that they could only "hurt" me, like anyone could, by having all three gang up on me at the same time. Well, sorry kiddies, that didn't work. Why? Because I am a Mistress. But I guess, for someone with you three intelligence, put together mind you, I have to draw it in crayon. So lets do that... [[She gets out a crayon and draws a picture of three stick figures and she holds it up]] Now, here is something if you three put your minds together, even you can understand. These three are you. Small, insignificant, waiting to die by a gracious hand, such as myself. Now here is something that will be beyond your comprehension but I will show it anyway. [[She goes to a wall and pulls out a huge painting of herself. He standing on a rock with grace. She looks out with pride and dignity. Seeing a bright future.]] Now, I didn't paint this, if that's what you thinking. I can't paint. So I hired an artist,in the federation's expense of course. This cost 2,967 dollars and worth every penny. I gave him a 500 dollar tip, on the federation's expense of course. This is showing my power over all three of you combined. And I'm going to show it on one of you three. Tara, when you ask why this happened to you, sorry you've been chosen at random. Put your ass in line.... it belongs to me.
[[She walks to the phone and dails]] [Mistress Sonia] Hello, room service? Yes, can you send one of your bellhops to the most expensive restaurant and have them get the most expensive meal, wine, and desert. I'll pay you 200 dollars and the payment of the meal. That's right, on the federation's expense, of course.
[[Fades to black]]
