<RP> Precious Moments

24 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Like I promised, here is the second of many Precious Moments RPs to come. Enjoy The Binky Fairy and The Winky Fairy, people! --------------------------- *We open the scene where we left off, with The Binky Fairy and The Winky Fairy prancing around in pink too-toos through a field, The Binky Fairy in a pink leotard, while The Winky Fairy is in a light blue leotard. Both are holding hands, and skipping. We zoom in a bit more, so we can hear them*
Both, in unison: We're off to see the wizard, the merry old wizard of AWMC. Because, because, because, becausssssse... BECAUSE OF THE EVIL THAT HE BREEDS IN HIS FED! *Both stop and do a little jig, laugh, then join hands again, skipping once again* The Binky Fairy: So, Winky, what do you think should be our first order when we get to the AWMC?
The Winky Fairy: I think we should go and see that merry old wizard that runs the place. We'll have to convince him that he needs to eliminate all that evil he let into the world, and that we should be the ones to do it.
The Binky Fairy: You think that will work? The Winky Fairy: I don't know. But as long as we do it together, I'll be happy. *Both stop, look at each other longingly, move towards each other, their lips parted... and give each other eskimo kisses* Both, in unison: Hee hee hee hee hee hee hee! *The join hands again and resume skipping, as we fade out to black. When we fade back in, the two men also known as Precious Moments are still holding hands, still skipping, only this time, they are in downtown Detroit. Out of place doesn't even cover what they are right now. Everyone moves out of the way as they skip merrily through the streets of Detroit, some in fear, some in disgust, and some lending their comments on what they think Precious Moments are*
Black Man Moving Out of the Way of Binky and Winky: Watch where you're going, f<BLEEP>ts!
*Binky and Winky ignore all of this, as they arrive at a taller high rise. They look up, look at each other, and smile. They enter the building, arm in arm, and run quickly to the front desk*
The Binky Fairy: Excuse me, but I was wondering if you could direct us to the offices of the AWMC?
*The secretary at the front desk looks up. Her jaw drops, and she can't speak for several seconds. Finally, she starts laughing, uncontrollably, and points over to a door on the far right side of the building*
Both, in unison: Thanky very much, madam. *Both Binky and Winky turn to each other, giggle, and run over to the door, swinging it open, the sound of the secretaries laughter getting louder as they do so. They walk into the room, and approach another desk, this one with a male in a nice business suit behind it. He is typing quickly, and doesn't look up to see who has walked into his room. Instead he just speaks to the two, in a gruff voice*
Man Behind The Desk: I thought I told Margaret not to let anyone in. If you don't mind, I'm kind of...
*The man looks up, and stops mid-sentence. He stares for a couple of seconds, then bursts out laughing* The Binky Fairy: Look, Wink. We're already spreading joy to the populace! I can't wait to get into the AWMC!
The Winky Fairy: Nor can I, Bink. This is going to be so much fun! *The man tries to regain his composure, but it doesn't work very well. But inbetween his laughter, he is able to stammer a few words* Man Behind The Desk: Wait... HAHAHAHAHAH... you want to.... HAHAHAHAHAHAH... be WRESTLERS?! HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAH! OH MY GOD! YOU'RE HIRED! That has got to be the single funniest gimmick I've ever seen in my life!
*Binky and Winky look at each other, confused* The Winky Fairy: Gimmick? What is that? We're here to eliminate the evil that the AWMC houses in it's very walls!
The Binky Fairy: Evil is everywhere, right now. They could be listening in... RIGHT NOW! *Bink and Wink look around, paranoid. The man behind the desk bursts out laughing once again, as Binky looks under his desk, and Winky starts pounding on the walls*
Man Behind The Desk: I LOVE IT! You're hired! We'll let you know when you're going to debut. Until then... HAHAHAHAHAHAH!
*The man behind the desk can't help laughing harder, as Winky checks up Binky's too-too* Man Behing The Desk: GET OUT OF HERE BEFORE I HAVE A HEART ATTACK FROM LAUGHING SO HARD! HAHAHAHAHAH! *Binky and Winky both courtsey to the man behind the desk, join hands, and skip out of his office, causing the man behind his desk to hoot and holler even harder. As Bink and Wink make their way out of the office, the secretary behind the front desk is just starting to regain her senses. Unfortunately, she looks up, sees the two skipping merrily out of the building, and starts laughing twice as hard as before. With that, we fade to black*
