Hey everyone. I handle "Handsome" Joey Johnson, or in the CIWF, Joey
Johnson. I would like some people to check out a roleplay from both of them, and tell me, honestly, what they think, and who they think I roleplay better with. Thanks. I'm at
[detail withheld] ************************************************************************************ "Handsome" Joey Johnson ************************************************************************************ [Scene opens with an open field of grass. In the background, light piano music is playing. "Handsome" Joey Johnson walks into view. On the bottom, the phone number, 1-800-GO-KIDZ Appears. The music grows quieter, and he speaks]
"Handsome": Oh, hello everyone. I want you all to meet someone. [Yells to someone offstage] C'mon kid, get your ass out here!
[A small child runs in a young, uncordinated way.] "Handsome": Everyone, this, is little Jimmy. Jimmy has a very serious problem Jimmy: Hey, I know you. You're "Handsome" Joey Johnson. The most beautiful person in CIWF! "Handsome": Yes, that's right. Little Jimmy was left on a street corner when he was six years old...
Jimmy: Umm.... Mr. Handsome, I'm only five years old right now. ["Handsome" whispers in a voice that is a little too loud: "Shut up
Punk! What are you trying to do, make me look like an idiot?" He then continues]
"Handsome": So many children each year are left on street corners, just like little Jimmy. They are left without anyone in the world to care for them. Who knows where his parents are!
Jimmy: Acutally, my Mommy and Daddy are over there [points somewhere off camera] "Handsome": Ah, who needs you anymore? You're part of the commercial is done! [Shoves Jimmy off camera] Now, how about you lazy bowling-ball butts that watch CIWF action get off the couch and do something about it, and call 1-800-GO-KIDZ. They can get you enrolled in programs like the Big Brother system. Remember, kids aren't something that we can just neglect and shove into the gutter and expect them to stay out of the way. They are to young and incompatant to get a job, and mooching from parents will only get them so far. So what do ya say? Pick up them phone, and call 1-800-GO-KIDZ today, because, maybe you'll get a less annoying and bothersome kid than the ones you have now! Unless you get someone like Jimmy, which, I pray doesn't happen to you. [Off camera, a child screams, "BLEEP You,
Dicksome!!". "Handsome" replies with "BLEEP it, ya little BLEEP". Off camera, someone yells, "CUT!! That's a rap!". The lights go down, a loud bell rings, and "Handsome" loses his dopey smile. Then he starts to talk to the CIWF camera]
"Handsome": Hello there. I just wanna address a few things, then I'm outta here. First of all, "Ultimate She-Male", I don't have any beef with you. I'm glad that you didn't call any attention to that silly mishap at Stormy Weather. But a few things I wanna get outta the way.
"He is so insignificant that they can't even spell his name right! Joey, I'm not even gonna waste my valuable time!" I belive were your exact words. 1.) Can't spell My own name right. [Sarcastically] Ya know, I looked into that, and the reason my name was spelled wrong was because Slimmy [From off camera comes a confused, "What? Huh?"] was put in charge of spelling. [Unsarcastically] What on Earth do you mean? I belive I can spell my own name she-male. 2.) He is so insignificant. Belive me pal, I wouldn't be talking about insignificance. I make so many waves in CIWF, that when the ocean looks at the CIWF, IT gets Sea-Sick. OK, that wasn't too funny. Sorry.
But you get the point. 3.) I'm Not gonna waste my valuable time. [Slimmy walks aimlessly behind "Handsome", confused] Well, that's fine with me. Just get your ass outta my face, and quit acting all high and mighty. Your time is just as important as mine. Do us all a favor, and get your nose out of the air. Mac Mommy C doesn't smell that bad. 4.)
What's the deal with your stupid [Here, "Handsome" Joey Johnson does an impression of Chris Del Rio that makes him out to be a blundering nincompoop] "Oh, look at me! I'm C-Lucious". Well, In reverance to your "genius" to come up with such a witty term, I think I'll call myself "Z-God". Yeah, I spent a whole five seconds thinking that up, just like you did C-Lucious. Well, I've got another commercial shoot for 1-800-GO-KIDZ coming up, so I gotta go. I'll see you next. This is
Z-God, signing off. [Scene fades to black as "Handsome" Joey Johnson walks off] ********************************************************************************* Joey Johnson ********************************************************************************* NOTE: This one takes place during Joey Johnson's face turn, so it's not truely a Joey Johnson rp.
[Scene opens with "Handsome" Joey Johnson sitting by a lake by a quiet field. He seems to be deep in thought. After a few seconds, he gets up.
He walks up a steep hill, which the lake was at the foot of. Once he gets to the top, a small farmhouse can be seen. "Handsome" sighs.]
"Handsome": Home.... [He walks towards the house, and enters. On "Handsome"'s right is a dinner table, with Mr. and Mrs. Johnson sitting at it. Mr. Johnson is a
big, burly man, with flat, grey hair, and a long, flowing, grey beard. He seems to have many wrinkles and scars from a hard life as a farm hand. Mrs. Johnson looks equally old, with equally grey hair.]
Mrs. Johnson: Cletus, you're late for dinner! What exactly do you do down there! "Handsome": Oh, Mom. You know I don't like the name Cletus! It's embarassing. Mr. Johnson: Go wash up for dinner son. ["Handsome" turns left, and walks down a narrow corridor. He turns right, to reveal his old room as a child. The walls are covered with posters of swimsuit models, great wrestlers of yesteryear, such as
Bruno
Sammartino and "Playboy" Buddy Rose. On the left is a closet, on the right is a small, twin size bed. Straight ahead is a window, and in the
left hand corner is a mirror/drawer. "Handsome" walks up to the mirror. He sees a man who is becoming more and more like his father everyday, although you'd never get him to admit to looking like "Some rundown hick
who doesn't know a hammerlock from a leglock", which were the words he used to describe the man when they had a fierce fight, causing
"Handsome" to move out and live on his own. His good looks were fading, his hair was turning to a robust silver, which would go grey before falling out, just like his father. Taped to the mirror, are pictures that he knows all too well. "The Ultimate Male" Chris Del Rio was taped
to the top, Gabriel Whitecross was taped to the right, and The WestSide Riders were taped to the left. "Handsome" just kept on staring at his face, then Del Rio, then himself, then Del Rio, for what seemed to be hours, but only turned out to be ten minutes. Then his father came in to
check on him] Mr. Johnson: What's the matter, son? Your dinner's getting cold! "Handsome": I'll be there in a minute, Dad. [After Mr. Johnson left, "Handsome" was filled with rage. The sight of his father just about made him explode. He punched the mirror, shattering it, ripped down posters, threw down the mirror/drawer, took a
drawer out of it, threw out the clothes, and threw the drawer through the window with all his might. Then he fell on his bed, weeping in cupped hands. Mr. Johnson came in to see what was the matter, and saw the destroyed room. He sat down to comfort "Handsome" as the screen fades to black]
