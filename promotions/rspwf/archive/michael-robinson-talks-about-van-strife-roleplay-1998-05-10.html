Michael Robinson talks about Van Strife! (Roleplay)

10 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene directly opens directly to the man, who should be wearing the National
Championship title, the one and only Michael Robinson. He seems to be in a interviewing studio, where the background of where he's at, is nothing but a black background atmosphere...The only thing that you can actually see in the black background, is Michael Robinson. Everything behind Robinson, is just peach black. Michael Robinson seems a little upset about something..He is just starring at the camera with hate and with a deadly cold stare...He is wearing a black muscle t-shirt, his golden brown hair is hanging down his mid way of his shoulder. His blue hazel eyes, is starring at the camera with a cold look. He has his right hand held up to his chin..He is also wearing a gold chain around his neck. The camera isn't showing anything below is waist, so we can't tell what kinda pants or shoes he's wearing. But we can tell he is in a standing position...He still has his right hand, held up to his chin. He finally then releases it from his chin, and folds his arm..He then starts to speak, in a low but very deep voice...]
Michael Robinson: You know Jay Ralston, you're full of (beep)!...You are the biggest son of (beep), I've ever seen. You are also the biggest liarer I've ever seen. I am sick and tired of this! You've screwed me twice now, and I haven't even been here longer than a month!...You see Jay, when you rescued me from AEWA, I thanked you for that..When you signed me up in a 1.5 years contract with lots of money, I appreciated that. When you told me, that you were gonna treat me with the proper respect, and you were gonna put me in the standards that I deserved, I beleived you!...But now, you just made your self very untrust worthy to me, and Jay, you stupid son of a (beep)!...
[He starts to point to the camera with his right hand, and we can see that he is really upset...His tone of voice, has gotting louder too..]
Michael Robinson: You just chose the wrong man to lie at, and piss off at. And
Jay, you are gonna pay for that...How?...(laughs evily)...I have something set up for Saturday!..I am gonna make your life here in the IeWC, a living hell!...I am tired of getting screwed out of what I should be getting! I am tired, of seeing these washed up egg heads, like the current National champion we have now, getting all this attention he doesn't deserve..But most of all, I am getting tired of you constantly liaring to me Jay Ralston, you bastard!
[He has his arm folded again...We see a evil smile form up on his face...His voice has now toned back down, but he is still pissed, as he starts to speak..]
Michael Robinson: But Jay, be sure that I am gonna get my revenge at this
Satuday Night Showcase! Because like I always say, "Whoever (beep)s with
Michael Robinson, be prepaired to get embarrased"..And Jay, this Saturday, I promise you all hell and havoc is gonna break loose. And Jay, there is nothing you, Sykic, or your ass kissing staff members are gonna do about it! Because I promise you, that if any of your officials, referee, or even you get in my way on ths Saturday, I am gonna knock the living crap out of you...And Jay, you know me...And you know I ain't kidding around! You are probably biting your nails right now, trying to find a way to stop me from embarracing you infront of millions...But Jay, (laughs evily)..Do all you want..Cut me off when I come out, and cut to a commercial...That still isn't gonna stop me from causing havoc!
[Michael Robinson pauses, and puts his right hand on his chin, and his right arm is around his chest...He then starts to speak again..]
Michael Robinson: First, you sign me up in a match with Brock Carden, and you tell me that this will be a good way to start up...Then, at the last minute, you fix the match, and you told the bell ringer, to ring the bell when brock had me in a submission hold...Because you know, that I did not giveup..So, on my debut, you SCREWED me out of my first win..But then, you called me up, and told me that I shouldn't worry about it, that I still got a great chance to win the National championship title...I said fine!...Then, at the twenty-nine men
Royal Rumble, once again, you screwed me, and caused me to get thrown out by a loser called Setzer Van Strife!
[We see Michael Robinson's face turn red, in anger..We can see the tone of his voice get louder than before, as he starts to speak.]
Michael Robinson: I've had it!...And JAY, at Showcase, I will NOT be held responsible for what I do to you, and what I do that peice of (beep) Van Strife during that three men tag team match!
Speaking about the tag match, and Van Strife, I heard you shooting off your big fat mouth, about how I'm a rookie, and I'm a nobody?
[Starts to laugh in a evil laughter tone..But he straightens himself up, and starts to speak..His voice has wen tback to normal now...He folds his arms again..]
Michael Robinson: Setzer Van Strife, you lucky ass bastard!...Listen up here you cocky bastard...Just because you won that title, doesn't make you the man..Just because you ate six bowls of Lucky Charms before the Rumble, and barely won, doesn't mean I or ANYONE respect you! Because clearly VAN STRIFE, I hate your damn guts! You come out on the camera shooting our your mouth without knowing anything. Son, I've won more titles, faster than you can lace up your boots. I've won more matches, than your little brain can even count! I've defeated more wrestling superstars, than you have shoes...And for you to say that I'm a midcarder, it shows just how unprofessional and a big moron you really are!
I don't like you, I don't respect you, I don't want to like you, and neither do
I want to respect you! You are not worth a minute of my respect! You suck!...Your winning of the National title, was a FLUKE! and on Saturday
Showcase, when I team up with Jonothan Hammer and Guru, I'm gonna prove to you, that you are nothing but a small time playa', about a half an ounce!
And I'll tell you this Mr. Van Strife, this is not gonna be a little three man tag match. When I get inside that ring, I'm gonna handle my own damn business!
I have no beef with Steve Wllace or Christian Hardcastle..But if they decide to be morons, and start up something they can't finish, they are never gonna forget the name of Michael Robinson!..But as for you son, (points to camera.)
YOU is the person I want!...And when it comes for the Main Event, I'm gonna go after you like you stole something from me..Then, we will find out who got more guts, and who REALLY is the Main Eventer, and Showstopper!..And SETZER VAN
STRIFE, it's gonna be a man, who rightfully deserved that belt you undeservely have around your waist..It's gonna be a man, who is gonna cause havoc and rampage at Showcase...It's gonna be a man, that WILL get his re-match at
Saturday..And it's gonna be a man, who is gonna kick your ass from pillar to post on Saturday Showcase!..And that one man, is the one and only, MICHAEL
ROBINSON!...So Van Strife, (laughs evily) your days before you get demolished, is very near...I suggest you get ready son..Because this is one battle you will
NEVER forget!
[Right after that comment, we see the camera zoom in more to Michael Robinson's face,. He still has his arms folded, and he still has the cold stare he had from the beginning. As the scene cuts to a Jerry Springer, "Too Hot for TV"
video commercial...]
