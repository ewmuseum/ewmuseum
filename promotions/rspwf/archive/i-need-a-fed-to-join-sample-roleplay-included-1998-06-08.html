I NEED A FED TO JOIN- SAMPLE ROLEPLAY INCLUDED

8 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am the Prez of my own e-fed and now since I've got it running smoothly I need a GREAT e-fed to join. Now I want some competition and you must have some sort of guestbook to roleplay on your site. I don't like bulk roleplays through the mail. Also no HARDCORE feds. Here is a sample roleplay if you have a great fed please contact me and we can work something out.
( Shane is sitting in his den the daily paper laying beside him. Theirs a glass of champagne on the table and he is watching <b>Superstar's last interview. When he is finished he pauses the VCR and chuckles then looks at the camera and begins to speak)
Superstar buddy, pal. Your living in a dream world. Ok if you saying you beat me fair and square is going to make that much of a difference go ahead and tell the world about it, I know your proud of the victory over a man who didn't interview. Another thing you say your wife beat me up, well your right about that she beat me real fine back in that dressing room and she can attest to the world now by experience I'm truly am the <b>Sexiest man alive</b>.
( Shane walks over and gets a tape down from the shelf. He pops it in the VCR and begins to play it. Its an old tape of a Superstar match, Shane watches for awhile then stops it and continues to speak.)
I just don't see why you think so highly of yourself. You have no wrestling skills and your definitely not sexy. I guess thats part of the reason your out here bragging about your 22 major titles. Man I hate to inform you of this I don't have to come out here and brag about all my titles and all my wins over wrestlers. I have UNGODLY skill and when I step in the ring I'll show the world exactly why <b>I am the Sexiest and
Most Talented wrestler in the world</b>. I could brag that I've held World
Titles and I could brag about all the big name wrestlers I've defeated, but
I'm not going too, I don't have too. All my titles and all my wins don't mean nothing in the SCWA. I will start fresh in this fed and you
<b>Superstar</b> will be the first to realize just how GREAT I really am.
( Shane walks back over and sits down in his recliner. He picks up the paper and slowly begins to read. He then pauses and looks back at the camera.)
Now <b>Superstar</b> I already knew you were a bum and had no skill whatsoever, but now I realize your just plain out stupid. You keep telling Steve to sign the match this Saturday so you can fight Two men in one night., well Idiot he already said no so shut up about it. I have signed the contract and on the next available card we'll get it on. You may of been able to back your words up in the past against some of the lesser talent in the wrestling world today, but buddy your stepping in the ring with a major player and all your <b>SHINING STAR</B> will be is a light for <b><BLINK>"SEXY" SHANE SUMMERS</B></BLINK> path to the top.
( Shane looks back down and starts reading the paper again. He takes a sip of his drink as the scene slowly fades away.)
This was an interview I did in another fed. Rick aka Sexy Shane Summers
