[RP] Trent Shaffer...Please Critique...

18 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello fellow RSPWFers, This is one of the first rps I've done for my new character, Trent Shaffer. He's got a gimmick similar to Raven's, only he's out to prove his worth and gain respect. The fed the rp is for, is the Council of Pain, an RP board fed.
Anyway, on with the roleplay.... ++++++++++++++++++++++ (The scene fades in to the outside of a rusty, tan colored door. The cameraman pans around the corridor only to see more doors like it. The number on the door reads 102. The cameraman rattles the brass knocker lightly.)
(A few seconds later, the door opens. Out steps a well-built man with blonde, ratty, shoulder-length hair, a goatee, and brown eyes. The man isn't wearing anything except a pair of torn-up jeans. He has a dazed look on his face as he approaches the cameraman.)
Man - Whaddaya want? (drowsily) Cameraman - Uhh, I'm here for an interview with Trent Shaffer... Man - Whaddaya need him for? Cameraman - He schedueled an interview with CoP. Is he here? Man - You're lookin' at 'im. Come inside... (The cameraman and Trent Shaffer walk into the apartment. The cameraman pans around as he walks in. The space is kept reasonably clean, apparently. Crumbs of food are scattered across the floor and weight equipment lies on the other side of the room near a window. In the room's west wall lies a tan-colored couch. On the opposite side sits an entertainment center, complete with a
36-inch television, stereo, and racks upon racks of CDs. Trent takes a seat over by the weight equipment and motions the cameraman to sit down on the couch.)
Trent - Now...what is it that this interview was schedueled for? Cameraman - You have a match schedueled at Slash against Rey Bano. I'm just here to get your thoughts on that.
Trent - Rey Bano...The guy who talks to plungers and stuff, right? It's obvious that he has problems, but ya can't help, but respect him. I had heard about his reputation for being a real 'all out' kinda guy. Still it's pretty weird to know that you hafta face a guy who's like that..
Cameraman - Sounds like you honor his reputation. Trent - Why shouldn't I? The guy's got more experience than I do and he's probably a lot better suited to the task at hand. In the end, however, I'll come out on top...I've been fighting guys even worse than him since I was in high school and I barely broke a sweat then. I'm not saying that he'll be easy to beat, but as I said before, in the end, I'll come out as the winner....
Cameraman - I see. There have been rumors flotaing around that you've developed a new finisher. CoP's heard that it's more powerful than Street Pizza. Is this true?
(Trent chuckles at the cameraman a little.) Trent - Who knows? That rumor you heard could be true. You'll just have to wait and see. Cameraman - What are your thoughts about CoP in general? Trent - Interesting place... Cameraman - CoP? Trent - Yeah. It's an interesting place with a lot of different wrestlers with a lot of different styles. You've got guys like Bano who are so 'all-out' that they lost their sanity. Then you have guys like Hurricane and Brian Douglas who've had it pretty bad, but are here to prove themselves. then you have guys like me who are just starting out and are hoping to see just how well they measure up with everybody else. It'll be pretty cool to see how it all turns out.
Camerman - Mr. Shaffer, we're almost out of time, any final words for the CoP? Trent - Yeah, so long... (With that, the scene fades out and the cameraman backs off. As he's doing that, the screen fades to black.)
