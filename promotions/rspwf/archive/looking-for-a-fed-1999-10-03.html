Looking for a fed

3 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey "24 Karat" Kerry Powell is looking to make a wrestling comeback. He has been retired for over 2 months and wants to come back. So can someone post the sites to some decent feds? It would be greatly appreciated.
Here is an RP I did about 5 months ago. I dunno wnat you will think, but I thought it was better than mediocre.
===================================== [The scene opens up outside the ICW headquarters. Lexus, the ICW lead interviewer not to mention a beautiful women, and a cameraman are packing things into a van. They break into a small conversation about where they are going. Lexus takes a sip of her coffee and then begins to talk to the cameraman]
Lexus: So Roy, What do you know about this Kerry Powell guy? Roy (Cameraman): Well lets see, I know that he lives in Malibu, California and
I know that it is going to be a long plane ride to get there.. Here is a picture of him..
Lexus: Wow, I can easily see myself with a guy like that. Very, very fine. Well, we might as well get going. Don't wanna keep a guy like that waiting.. Roy: You do know he is only 19 right? Lexus: Well, that's close enough to me! Roy: Heh, Yea, Whatever you say.. [They both climb into the van and start their ride to the airport where they will catch a flight to Malibu. Waiting in Malibu will be Kerry Powell. While riding in the van Kerry Powell once again becomes the subject of a conversation]
Roy: [Reading an old wrestling magazine] Well here is a "story" on Kerry Powell, Wrestling Fan magazine says, and I quote: "Kerry Powell is a bright young wrestler, look for him to make a huge splash on the world on Intense
Championship Wrestling"..
Lexus: You know, I always found it alittle scary how Wrestling Fan knows more about us then we do..
Roy: Yea it is kind-of freaky.. Well lets see what else this book has to say.. "Kerry Powell, a 6'1, 231 pound rookie, is set to make a big impact on the ICW. He has experienced great fortune in a few indy feds but has yet to make it big. ICW might be his chance." Lexus: Sounds like he has some potential.. Well let's catch that plane.. [They both get out of the van and walk into the airport. They get into the waiting room and sit down. They break into another small conversation but not about Powell, about Powell's upcoming match.]
Lexus: So who does that hunk, Powell, have to fight? Roy: Umm.. I think Brian Storm and Chris Steele.. Hold on let me check.. [He pulls out a small peice of paper and look at it] Yea, Brian Strom and Chris
Steele.. Wait, Shouldn't you know that.. You are the interviewer..
Lexus: I know that! Ok, Let's just get on the plane.. Roy: [Rolls his eyes] Yea.. [They get out of their seats and walk over to the boarding area of the airport. Lexus makes Roy carry her bags and he puts them on the cirular ramp. They get close to the plane and them Lexus says something to Roy.]
Lexus: Oh, By the way.. ICW only gave me one First Class ticket.. You have to ride coach.. Roy: In would not have hurt to tell me this before, I could have gotten something to eat.. You know a peanut cannot hold a man over on a 12 hour plane ride..
Lexus: Umm, Ok.. I'll see you in Malibu.. [She walks into the First Class section on the plane as Roy mutters and walks back into coach]
(( 12 hours later )) [Both of them get off the plane and see each other in the lobby of Malibu
Airport and Lexus walks over to Roy and they begin to talk]
Lexus: So how was your plane trip? Roy: I dislike you alot.. Lets get to Powell's mansion.. Lexus: Ohh, He has a mansion.. Everthing I hear about this guy makes him more attractive.. Roy: Get in the Van! [They both climb into the van as it drives away] (( 8 minutes later )) [They get out of the van and Lexus just stands and stares at the house, well mansion would be a more fitting word]
Lexus: Wow!! Look at how big it is.. Well lets go knock on the door?? Roy: Well we gotta start the interview somehow.. [They begin walking up to the door. Roy rings the bell and an old man, very similar to Alfred of Batman, opens it and leads them in.]
Old Man: Mr. Powell will see you shortly.. Lexus: Thanks.. Old Man: You may wait in here.. Please feel free to eat the food, It was prepared for you..
Roy: THANK YOU!! [Because he only got one peanut on the plane ride he practically "jumps" on the table and starts to eat. The Old Man rolls his eyes as he walks out of the room]
Roy: Wow this is great!! I like Powell already!! Lexus: [Holding the pictue of him in her hand] So do I... (( 5 to 10 minutes later )) [The Old Man walks back into the room] Old Man: Follow me, Kerry will see you now.. Lexus: Great!! [The Old Man opens the door and lead them into a room where a young, good-looking blond man is sitting chating with an amazingly attractive brunette. The man a wearing a grey dress shirt and black dress pants while the women is dressed in a long black skirt and a dressy tank-top. The man stands up to greet Roy and Lexus]
Lexus: You must be Kerry! [She runs over and hugs him] The Girl: And I must be his girlfriend... Kerry: Erin, calm down it was just a friendly gesture.. No need to get annoyed.. Well anyway I guess you must be Lexus and You are...
Roy: Oh, I'm Roy.. Kerry: Alrighty.. Have a seat.. Where would you like to start... Lexus: Well First off lets start out with your upcoming debut match in ICW against Chris Steele and Brian Storm..
Kerry: Well considering I never heard of either of these two guys.. I don't think I am going to sweat it too much.. Trust me.. I know my limitations and that is that there isn't any for a wrestler of my caliber.. These two guy and hell, all of ICW, might not know who Kerry Powell is but after the 24th everyone is going to know who I am.. And everyone is going be chanting my name the next time they see me.. That I will bet anything on...
Lexus: Even you.. [With a flirtty smirk] Erin: No not himself!! Kerry: Heh heh, No I'm sorry but I am for one women and one women only and that's Erin.. Sorry..
Lexus: Oh.. I see.. Well back to the interview.. [With a fake smile] So how do you think you will fare in this match..
Kerry: I think I mentioned the fact I will be winning.. Did I not? Lexus: Oh maybe you did.. But I was probably to busy staring at you.. Erin: Ok.. Stop it! Kerry: Well would you mind if I just talked for a few seconds.. Lexus: [With dreamy eyes] I would love it... Erin: Ok.. I'm leaving.. I'll come back in a while Kerry... Kerry: Alrighty.. Roy: Umm.. Ahem.. You were saying... Kerry: Oh Yes.. Now before I say anything else.. I wanna thank.. No nevermind.. ICW is doing me no favor.. Actually I'm doing them a favor.. So ICW.. Or Brandon Black.. I think I will be expecting a writen or announced thank you soon.. And Chris Steele and Brian Storm can expect an apology for what I am going to be forced to do to them.. See I don't think Brandon tryed to sign me because he needed someone to get you over.. I think you two have the job of getting me over.. But I doubt I'll need your help at that..
Lexus: Huh.. Need help you say.. Hehe.. Kerry: Umm.. No.. But like I said.. I'm very happy with Erin.. So please just finish the interveiw and go back to Maryland... Not to sound rude..
Lexus: You can sound whatever way you want... Kerry: Forget it.. Roy, That's your name right.. Roy: Yes... Kerry: Would you like to interveiw me? Roy: [Under his breathe] My big break.. don't mess this up Roy [Normal Tone] Ok, sounds like a good idea.. So Kerry, You realize that this match is almost for the lightweight belt.. Well not actually but in a sence.. These are the 3 best lightweights around.. All in one ring.. What do you have to say about that?
Kerry: Well quite frankly.. That sucks.. They should just give the lightweight belt to me.. I know none of the so-called "wrestlers" around here have ever seen me.. But Mr. Black has.. And I have no clue why he wouldn't just give me the belt.. He has to know that I am the best wrestler since.......
Roy: Since? Kerry: Umm.. sorry.. I'm trying to think about that one.. I don't think there has ever been a young talent such as me.. And if there was.. Well I never heard of him...
Roy: Well you know you are just a young man.. There is most likely hundreds of great wrestlers you have never heard of..
Kerry: [With a smile, pointing to himself] But none quite like me... Roy: I suppose.. Lexus: None like you at all... Kerry: [Rolls his eyes] Yes whatever you say... Lexus: [Her eyes light up] What if I say something dirty! Kerry: Ok.. I think we are done here.. Lexus: Now let the real fun start.. Kerry: Roy, Think you can get her out of here.. Roy: Sure... Kerry: Just one more thing.. Chris Steele.. Brian Storm.. You may think I have no right to say the things I did about you because I have never seen you.. But it doesn't matter.. You could be the 2nd best wrestler in the world.. But I would still have it in the bag.. Cause you will both be in the ring with number
1... [Fade to Black] Josho Handles- "24 Karat" Kerry Powell [Looking for work, Email me for a sample] Justin "Extreme" Pain [A humor-fed wrestler, also looking] [name withheld] hates me... =(
