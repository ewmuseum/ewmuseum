[Roleplay Critique] "The Only Star" Eric Dane

13 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Black.] "I am Eric Dane." [So much for introductions.] "I am The Only Star." [The video has still yet to kick in.] "I am the World Champion." [This is going somewhere, I promise.] "Until you beat me, fuck you. You're nothing to me." [Before the inevitable fade in, we are met with a banner.] --On the posted rp, there is a banner here.-- [With the same quickness of it's being, the banner dissappears from sight.] [Up.] [This is The Hilton in Atlanta, only the best for the World Champion. We are in suite number three-oh-eight, in the den between the full-sized kitchen and dining room. There is a bar, and the World
Champion is there, sitting on a barstool, nursing a glass of Brandy.]
"I don't care if you're the four time NWA World Champion Michael
Lennox, undefeated UWF Champion titan, two time ACW Champion Simon
Kincade, or Uncrowned World Champion Ryan Corey. Until you beat me for my ten pounds of World Championshop Gold, you're nothing but another wannabe trying to ride my coattails to the top."
[Eric is dressed in an old CWF t-shirt, cargo pants, and Vans. His hair is uncharactaristially down, flowing over his shoulders. There is a sour demeanor about him.]
"Lennox thinks he can walk back into the House that Dane built and take back a title that he shouldn't have won in the first place. Titan thinks he can bully his way into getting a shot at a title that he neither deserves, nor has the skill to commandere in the ring. Kincade thinks that just because he hangs out with Nightmare and once wrestled in Florida that he has what it takes to pry this strap from my hands.
Ryan Corey thinks that just because he's been around since the beginning of time he's going to walk into Mexico and beat me."
[He takes a sip from the glass.] "Well let me tell you all something. When there is a World Title on the line, Eric Dane is more than capable of surviving. Ask anyone who has ever faced me in a World Title match, I am damn near impossible to beat. Byron Tanis beat me for the NWA Title because Lennox took my knee out. Lennox beat me for the WFWA Title because I blew my own knee out, landing the Falling Star Splash. Michael Lennox did pull one out of his ass over in the NWA though, and he pinned me fair for the title. That's it, out of every World title match I've ever had, I've only lost one fair, and unlike the lot of you, I've wrestled quite a few World Title matches. That takes the odds on it happening again down to slim and none."
[The Apex of Arrogance downs the rest of the alcohol, and reaches for a cigarette from the half-empty pack sitting on the bar across from him.]
"So anyway, next Teusday I got to finish the job I started on Simon
Kincade just the other day."
[The camera pulls in tight on him.] "Didn't I tell you I was going to put you in the hospital, Simon? Didn't I tell you not to fuck with me? But you didn't listen. You kept pushing and pushing, telling me that I didn't have the balls to do something nasty, well I suppose you found out differently, did you not? Now Teusday night, all I've got to do is put you through a few tables. That's fine, I did my time down in Florida, I can handle it.
You and I both know that I don't have a problem cracking one of those
Light Tubes over your head, or carving my initials in your head with the shards either for that matter."
[Eric lights the cigarette, and exhales a puff of bluish-gray smoke as the camera pans back. Perhaps the camera man is alergic to smoke?]
"I swear Simon, I'm not sure where you get off thinking that you've got a chance in hell in beating me. You know if I drop you on your head hard enough this week you may not even get up. You know that it's not going to take anything more then a thump to the forehead to open those cuts back up. Or maybe you'll breath in some more of that dust from the insides of the lights, then what? Is Kelly gonna bounce out to what's left of the ring to give you your inhaler? Face it Simon, with the beating you took Teusday, maybe you ought to just sit this one out, otherwise you're just gonna end up warming a Hospital bed for the rest of your life."
[The Champion sneers in the direction of the camera.] "Face it Simon, I will end you if I have to. If it takes me spilling every ounce of your blood, or jabbing every one of your appendages full of glass, I'll do it. If I have to drop you on your head in every angle I can think of, I will. You're livelihood means fairly little to me. Maybe I'll grind some of that glass up and rub it in your eyes, try seeing with a face full of blood and two eyes full of glass, I can pretty much assure you that it won't be too easy.
And then what? Is Kelly supposed to Kellycaranna me through three
Light Tube Tables? Is your buddy Joe gonna come out and all of a sudden be your friend again? It's not very fucking likely, Simon, so do yourself a favor, sit this one out, wait until the next time I come around to Georgia, and try your luck when I haven't just gotten through destroying you."
[The sneer turns into a smirk.] "And as far as Ryan Corey is concerned- " [Before that rant can start, a knock is heard.] "What the hell?" [Eric crushes out his cigarette, makes his way away from the bar and through the den to the door. He opens it, and almost jumps down the throat of whoever this is, when he see's and old friend and associate.]
"Damn Eric, you look stressed." [This is Ronald J. Hirsch.] Eric - "Ron? What the hell are you doing here?" [That's right, this is just about the only person in the wrestling business that Eric hasn't turned on at one point or another.]
Ron - "Well shit Eric, I do work for the ACW you know. I thought I might get a few words from the Champ!"
[Eric snorts, and lets the ACW's Lead Journalist into his hotel room.] Eric - "Yeah, yeah. I just wasn't expecting anyone. How the hell are you?" Ron - "Fine, as always. By the way, Congratulations on the big title win, sorry it's so late, but you're not the only one traveling the roads with a wrestling company you know."
Eric - "I know, I know, and thanks. It's been a long ride." Ron - "So anyway, what's the deal with you lately? You've been dropping people like flys! First Titan in the UWF, then Titan again in
ACW. Then Simon Kincade, and just last night Ryan Corey in UWF. Are you on the candy again? Or are you just trying to piss off the entire
Alliance?"
[A chuckle escaped the Champion's mouth.] Eric - "I've got my reasons, Ron. Titan needed to be taught a lesson, and he gor it two-fold. Simon asked me to hurt him, and so I did. And
Corey, I got tired of looking at him with a title around his waist.
When he loses to me in Mexico, he'll be left beltless and pitiful, much the same as Simon Kincade is at this very moment."
Ron - "Yeah, but don't you think that putting two people in a hospital in one night is going a bit far? The Alliance is gonna come down hard on you if you don't stop hurting the talent."
Eric - "The fact of the matter, Ron, is that I singlehandedly put the
Champions of arguably the two hottest regions on the planet, in the same hospital, on the same night. That's never been done before, and likely won't be again until I so desire it to happen. As far as Corey, he needed to be reminded that nothing happens in this Alliance without my consent, and it wasn't my will for him to walk into our match holding his own little belt. It's a simple case of the King manipulating the pawns, as Corey would put it."
Ron - "Yeah, but are you gonna be able to take the punishment from a
Deathmatch with Simon, and still work a wrestling match with Corey two weeks later? Not to mention you've still got Hallock on the eighteenth!"
Eric - "For starters, Hallock is a fuck. Is, was, and more than likely will continue to be. Second, there is no way in hell that Simon will be able to do anything but sit there and take everything I give him.
The man can't breathe, can't see, and can barely stand on his own power. Combine this with the fact that the match is three days away, and I'm perfectly healthy, and Kincade is just out of luck."
Eric - "Corey will be dealt with. Corey can't beat me in a fight, he can't beat me in a wrestling match. On top of that, he damn sure can't beat me in a World Title match."
Ron - "I suppose you know what you're doing. You always did have a method to your madness."
Eric - "And I still do. Anyway, enough business, let's have a drink." Ron - "Yeah, let's do that." [Cut.] [Elsewhere.] "So, do you think he can do it?" [That was Leonard Kimble, the awkward, yet wry accomplice of the World
Champion.] "Sure, he's got a plan." [That was Solita Caine, the Punk Rock Princess as you know her.] [Up.] Leonard - "He always has a plan, but will it work?" Solita - "Of course it will, he's unbeatable." [Cut.] [World Title.] [No, seriously the scene opens up to a closeup shot of the WFWA World
Title.] [This is ten pounds of diamond encrusted gold. A globe is engraved in the fold, with the insignia of the Alliance printed across the front.
On either side of the large plate are two smaller ones, each holding their own significance. All of this is fused to a black leather belt.
It's quite expensive.] [However.] [Most of it's value comes from the name currently residing on the nameplate at the bottom of the ten pound plate.]
[It reads simply:] [Eric Dane.] [Cut.] [End.]
