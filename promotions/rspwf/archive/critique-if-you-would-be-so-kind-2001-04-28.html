[Critique] If you would be so kind.

28 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I recently created a new guy and put him into [name withheld]'s KCW (One the best feds, IMO) I just wanted to get some feedback on what you guys think about it.. it's not long, so it's easy reading.
Oh, if you want you can email me if you've got a fed needing a face heavyweight.. I'm looking to expand somewhat.
Well, here goes. -------------------- [Camera fades into the Motel 6 across the way from Norfolk Scope, the scene of last night's KCW PPV. Coming out of the bathroom, is the man who made his debut just last night in KCW, James Marshall. He is decked out in a pair of blue jeans and a white t-shirt that simply reads "Noose Inn". Slowly he comes on over to the camera.. still proudly looking over his room.]
JM: I can say this about Kay Cee Dubba.. I've been rather impressed up to this point in time. Hell, they put me up in this very motel. It even had running water, cable TV, and a telephone line. Just in case I wanted to make some calls.
[Just then he digs something out of a bag at his feet.] Check this out! Free soap and shampoo. I have never recieved such a warm reception to a new federation. Of course, when you're in the Carribean you need to leave the lights on or won't be able to pick off the roaches when they crawl up on you. And as for Japan, well a guy six foot six doesn't fit into beds designed for five foot guys... you'd rather sleep on the floor.
[Looking over his clean room, with a large sized bed stuck in the middle.] This is the high life, I'm sure of it. [Marshall drops the shampoo and soap back into this bag. But soon his smile fades away from his lips.. he now has a more serious look on his face. It seems that he now has business to attend to]
JM: Now to business at hand. [Marshall's eyes simply gaze into the camera. He wipes his hand over his very short crew cut, and brings it down to his waist.]
JM: I heard someone talking some serious trash during the PPV and his name was Vic Parker. He went on to say that he's the best damn wrestler in all of Kay Cee Dubba. Well, I've asked and now have a match against the best damn wrestler in this federation. Mr. Unbelieveable, I sure hope you were serious when you went on to say that you're the best, the most awesome.. blah blah blah.
You see, all that talk is just fine and dandy to me. Hell, I'm sure if you got drunk enought off of some whiskey you could probably make a case that a horse told you there was gold in his ass. Either way you look at it Parker, you're elbow deep in shit..
[Marshall smirks.] I don't envy your position one bit. You're going to find yourself in a bad place, and I'm going to be the one that puts you in the hurt locker.
[The camera fades out as James Marshall, who seems to be done packing his luggage, leaves the motel room with his travel case wheeling out behind him.
On the back of his shirt, you can read "You look like a size 18." with a noose encircling the words.]
- Duff AIM: [detail withheld] "Behold the power of cheese."
