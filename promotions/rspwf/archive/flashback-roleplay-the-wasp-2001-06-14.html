Flashback Roleplay - The Wasp

14 June 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Critique if you like, although my writing skills have improved a little since I wrote this back in early '98. It details, however, how the start of the "Wasp vs. Sally Struthers" feud started, and where future references may come from. This was fun for me to look back on, because it was before The
Wasp was anybody, and before he had really gotten off the ground in terms of any recognizability (not that he has any now :P). Enjoy.
======================== SETTING THIS UP, I CUT OUT *LITERALLY* OVER HALF THE FLASH (I WAS LONG
WINDED THEN... BEGINNER :P). SALLY STRUTHERS IS GIVING THE "YOU CAN MAKE A
DIFFERENCE FOR THE PRICE OF A CUP OF COFFEE" SPEECH. THEN SHE IS ABOUT TO
INTRODUCE HER GUEST. ------------------------------ Sally: Today, we've got a very special guest to help us out with the greatest gift of all... Life. He may be viewed as a person of questionable character, but this man is a Humanitarian to be sure. Wasp, from the SFWL, has come to this saddened country today to help in the charitable cause of saving the children. Let's go see what he's up to, so that the people at home can see, that giving... is more than an opportunity... It's a pleasure.
(The camera fades to black, and as it comes on, we see Wasp standing among several children who are standing there with big smiles on their faces, jumping up and down at the 6'4 Wasp. Wasp has a pissy look on his face, and surprisingly, looks like he doesn't want to be there.)
(A voice comes out from behind the camera, as the camera man tries to be as quiet as possible, yet is a little pissed off.)
Voice from behind the camera: (Like he's talking through his teeth) I said, ACTION. (Wasp looks toward the camera with a blank expression on his face, then gives one of those silent 'OH!' looks. He smiles toward the annoying kids, and picks one up and makes his way to a milk crate then takes a seat.)
(Wasp gives a sadly portrayed sad expression, then looks at the kid and back at the camera.)
Wasp: Hello, most of you know me as the Wasp from the SFWL... Contrary to the Wasp you all see on Television, I don't spend all my time beating on people, smashing their heads through tables, and dishing out punishment...
There is a softer side to the Wasp... A more... Giving side. I have come to
Sudan to see what *I* can give, because these children deserve a better face than the....
(Wasp shakes his head, and squints his eyes toward the camera... An observant person would pick up on the fact that he's reading a cue card...
Wasp clears his throat.) Wasp: Ahem... These children deserve a better PLACE than this, because not a single one of them asked to be here.
(Wasp turns his head slightly to the side and shoots a nasty look toward the camera... A sigh can be heard coming from out of view.)
Wasp: Anyway, some of the more intelligent fans of the SFWL... Which of course, are VERY few and far between, know that the Wasp is a Humanitarian.
It goes much, much deeper than just giving out beatings... Giving free losses to the needy, and even giving free Psychology sessions in making others realize that they are far inferior to me. Oh no, the Wasp is a true giver... I have given these kids what they need most in the world... A role model. If even ONE of these children could grow up and be anything close to the level of superiority that the Wasp has achieved, it would make a huge difference in this community.
(Wasp sets the kid down and smiles. The kid, rather excited at the thought of being held by a person of fame, tries to jump back on Wasp's lap. Wasp kind of smiles, and sets the kid back down and just before he can speak, the kid jumps up on his lap and lays down across Wasp's knees.)
Wasp: (gives an annoyed sigh) As you can see, these children are very sweet... And I am very sure that if it were them who was sitting at home enjoying the luxuries of the 24-hour SFWL Cable Network, they would surely find it in their hearts to give next to nothing to those who are in more need of some of the basics in life.
(The kid tries to jump up, to sit rather than lay on Wasp's knees, and his head comes right up under Wasp's chin. Wasp's head flies back, and in a moment of rage, stands with the kid in his hands like a rag doll, and turns... Just before Wasp does the unthinkable, he calms himself and slowly sets the kid down, all the while smiling toward the camera with an embarrassed look.)
Wasp: Ahem... See? These children are no different than our own... They like to be played with, thrown around, but due to the poor state that their country is currently in, things of this nature is out of the question.
See... The people running this country are a bunch of idiotic buffoons who have nothing better to do than start fights with one another then sit in their offices while these poor children's parents go out to fight a battle that they know nothing of. Of course, America isn't much different, but...
(Sally Struthers runs in and steps in front of Wasp with a faint smile on her face, obviously forced. She glimpses back at Wasp with a glare, then turns back to the camera.)
Sally Struthers: We would REALLY like to thank Mr. Wasp for joining us today here in this dying country, and any contributions that can be made, can once again be phoned in by calling 1-800-Save-The-Children.
(Wasp is standing behind Sally Struthers, looking just a wee-bit pissed that she cut him off. When she turns around and glares at him again, Wasp's face almost switches to a look of rage... But he catches himself before he does anything. Just then, another young child, noticeably different in appearance than the rest, runs up and flashes Wasp the SIE Sign. Wasp's gaze follows the kid as he runs right past, and back out of view. Suddenly, the kid comes running in again, but from the same side as before like he's running circles around the camera. Sally Struthers tries to reach down and grab the kid, while trying to look as "sweet" about it as possible, but just misses. Wasp picks the kid up, and grins.)
Wasp: I'd like you all to meet my good friend, Starvin Marvin... He's a poverty stricken child just like the rest here, but he's got a lot more intelligence. You see, Starvin Marvin has been to the United States... And he was able to attend a school for roughly a year before the police came and picked him up, sending him back here basically to die. Marvin, what exactly did you do when you got to go to the United States?
Starvin Marvin: Hell, man, I ate Orville Redenbacher chicken, and got to mush my face into some'a that good ole' American Stir Fry! I ate McDonald's, buffets, sizzlin' salads, the whole 9! Hell, man, I even had some lasagna once before those damn people comin' out of the Theater to watch 'A Bug's
Life' came stormin' out and trampled over me!
(Wasp chuckles, and turns slightly to get a better view of the camera. Right about now, Mrs. Struthers is going absolutely berserk.)
Wasp: Well, you can see why we call him Starvin' Marvin. But that's not the gist of it all... Marvin, tell them what happened when you went to the SFWL event.
(Marvin spits a nasty snot filled lugie, then looks at the camera with some serious disdain.)
Marvin: I had a sign that says 'Hades the SFWL Mascot Sucks' and those bastards threw me out, man! They ripped up my ticket then when I got to the
Manager, the bastards told me I had to leave 'cause I didn't have a ticket!
Ain't that some... (Sally Struthers rushes in and grabs Starvin' Marvin out of Wasp's hands... She looks to the camera and gives a quick 'cut' sign by moving her hand along her throat. The scene fades.)
(But it only faded for the network channels that were covering the event for their annual Save The Children-athon... The SFWL cameras were right there to pick up on the action.)
(Starvin' Marvin is kicking the hell out of Sally Struthers, and Wasp is looking at her like 'What the hell are you doing?'... Wasp, beginning to get really pissed off, just stands there in disbelief as Sally Struthers starts to go off. She gives Marvin a solid slap to the side of his face, and begins yelling 'DON'T... YOU... EVER... TALK TO ME... LIKE *THAT*!!!' Wasp, in disbelief, grabs Sally by the back of her hair. As he finally manages to pull her back from Marvin, Sally tries to kick Marvin with all the extension of her legs that she can muster. Starvin' Marvin runs away, screaming.)
(Sally finally gives up, and is panting heavily... Wasp releases his hold on her hair and turns it into a claw hold on the back of her head. Sally realizes what is going on, as she calms down from her fit of rage, and slowly turns toward Wasp. Wasp turns her face straight into his, to reveal to her a sick... Twisted grin.)
Sally: (begging) Oh.. I... I didn't mean that. You know, I was (faint giggle) trying to help the kids out, and I don't know what happened, I'm really...
(Wasp slams her forehead into his, bringing her to tears as they stream down her face.) Wasp: Sorry? Is THAT what you were going to say? You're not SORRY yet... Not by a long shot.
(Wasp turns Sally back around, and she pisses her pants... She stands with her knees buckled together, while her feet are about shoulder width apart.
The Wasp puts one leg in front of hers, and steps to the side of her setting her up... Wasp leans over and looks down at the wet spots slowly making it's way down her leg, and shakes his head in disgust. Despite the look of fear on Sally Struther's face, Wasp suddenly springs forward toward the ground, tripping her up with his leg, and slamming her face to the ground with the firm claw hold he has on the back of her head, executing a near-perfect
Stinger... In fact, the form is impeccable, and when she lands, a sickening thud is heard. Wasp stands, and brushes himself off, then suddenly realizes what he's just done... Or more correctly, he realizes that the camera has taped everything that happened. However, the look of surprise doesn't last long when the Wasp sees that it's the SFWL camera crew that caught the action and not the network channels. Wasp grins.)
Wasp: Her mind was in the hunt... But her heart was not with her prey. (The Wasp throws his head back and begins to laugh hysterically. Starvin' Marvin runs up and jumps right into Wasp's arms. Wasp gives a thumbs up to the camera, then sets Marvin down.)
(The scene fades as Marvin gets his kicks on Sally Struthers... No, literally, he's kicking the hell out of her and Wasp stands watching with amusement, and almost a look of pride... The Wasp picks her up and whips her into the far wall, sending the backdrop down with her to reveal a 'Universal
Studios' sign.)
