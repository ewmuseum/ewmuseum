[Critique] Flash

30 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's been a long time since I've posted a flash on the board... but, eh. I thought it was a pretty nice one, and I'd like to see what you thought about it.
Feel free to critique, flame, ignore, whatever. -- [The camera now fades in to a nice, quaint living room, only lit by a lamp, which contains the United States champion, who is none other than our hero, Tommy Grayson. He looks quite mellow and relaxed, as he usually does (not considering the past few days, of course)... but from the looks of things, you can easily tell he's fucking pissed at the barrage of things being thrown at him lately.
"Mr. Softcore" is in his vintage Black Sabbath t-shirt, a pair of semi-baggy plaid pajama bottoms, and he's wearing the U.S. title belt itself around his waist as he slightly slouches in his chair. The shoulders-length dark brown hair is somewhat slicked back.]
TG: It's late at night. And I'm annoyed. No... _extremely_ annoyed. A- freaking-nnoyed! Okay, I think it's getting pretty obvious here, folks.
The chips I'm balancing on my shoulders... I'm getting pretty cheesed off at it. [An "oh well" shrug.] TG: Then again, those are the responsibilities of a [finger quotes] "good" United States champion, aren't they? I mean, represent the people in this great country--some of whom can't read a _simple_ _freaking_ ballot--and battle off the horrible forces of evil such as the sadistic Communist... or even the angry, nationalist Japanese dude armed with some kind of thrust to the throat, right?
But... [More sarcasm cuts the air like a knife as Grayson snaps his fingers.] TG: Oh, damn! Looks like I'll be battling off the total corniness and campiness of Adam Mitchell and his [finger quotes] "proh-moes"! Oh, goodie!
And not to mention the inevitable interference of Art Blake, who I'll get to later. Anyway, Adam, I know what you said. Adult population, boy bands, candle, half-an-hour urinating and defacating... the rest of all that smack.
Gotcha, buddy. I can tell you're a smart man, Adam. I _know_ you're a pretty damn good wrestler. Unfortunately for you, I'm smarter... and I'm
_more_ pretty damn good.
["Mr. Softcore" matter-of-factly pats his belt.] TG: And hence the gold around my waist. Sure, Wyatt Wallace was a push-over and maybe even less. I'll admit that. But the fact is, I got the shot because I was putting away you whores out there left, right, up, down, diagonal, and horizontal.
Heck, I put one of the last nails in the coffin of the career of one Tom
Collins. And Mitchell, you think that patronizing me in your... [mockingly] "oh-so- special" little way is gonna hurt me? Think again, you little putz! You're lookin' at the man who fought through the mean streets of _surburban_
Long Island to get to this point! [That Jim Morrison-esque smirk appears on his face.] TG: And no, you're not gonna take... *tap, tap* this belt away from moi. The Graysonheads in the Suburbs will _not_ be let down! Oh, but wait! There's more, isn't there? Ah, yes. Art Blake. My ex-boss. Art, I gotta say, judging from the past few days, it looks like you're gonna get the commentators saying, "he doesn't want the title. He wants to put Tommy Grayson in his place. Now, that's a dangerous man."
Hogwash, amigo. You don't have some kind of wicked psychotic ambition as you want everybody else to think. Art, you _are_ jealous. You're just jealous you don't have the same talent as I do when it comes to getting some starry-eyed, young, fresh-outta-college actress to be your valet...
which is pretty cool, might I add. Or is it the fact that you... well, you're just, to put it bluntly...
[With a smug look on his face, Grayson says the following:] TG: An idiot. [A brief pause of a dramatic nature.] TG: Well, I'm gonna get some Z's. Wake me up when these people get a lethal dose of reality.
[Fade to black.] -- Jimmy T. - Thinks his current work is better than his last year's work.  Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
