[Critique]§howtime §uperstars w/ Chris Hurte

29 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is an RP my brother wrote for a Tag Team we have, and Hurte is in it. We've done a few RP's, so I just wanted to know what you guys thought about them. I'm gonna reply and post another one too, one that i wrote. tell me what you think. constructive criticism only. [name withheld], dont reply unless you have something positive to say as you have flamed me every time i posted. You might not understand some of it, as it has to do with some of our opponents in a fed.
If you want me to explain, just ask. Here's the RP <center>Lethal Weapons II</center> Scene: A back alley in Chicago. It is night and the pothole filled alley is dark. A cat scampers across the alley. To the right is a faint silouhette of a black Lexus. A truck pulls up into the alley and stops next to the Lexus. Out hops three figures, and a man steps out of the Lexus as the camera pans in.
Chris Hurte: Play it cool guys, this dude is mean. (The camera pans in to show Chris Hurte and the §howtime §uperstars in full force. They all have on heavy jackets and jeans, and Angel still has on his mask. The man that got out of the car is african-american. He is bald with a goatee and has on a suit with a trench coat over it. He is also pretty big. He throws a cigarette to the ground and it filters away. You can't even see the actual faces of the people, just shadows.
Chris Hurte: Hey, Madd Dogg. Been a long time man. Madd Dogg: Yeah, it has. So, what can I do for you?? Chris Hurte: I could use some "short-range" weapons. Madd Dogg: Last time I got a request for some of those was years. I still have some in stock though. Follow me.....
(They walk around to the back of the car. Madd Dogg opens the trunk and a light inside comes on, letting us see the stony face of Madd Dogg for the first time.
He pushes around some rifles and guns, to reveal a board of closer ranged weapons.) Madd Dogg: Watcha need?? I got it all.... Chris Hurte: Like I told D, we got some Vampire problems that need to be handled, and a rabid rottweiler in heat.
Madd Dogg: Let's see here....I got a few wooden 2x4's, some garlic in the passenger compartment from this Italian meal I had today. And for the dog, some tranqueilizers. Of course, I'm the only dogg that is a threat....
Chris Hurte: I'll take the 2x4's then. And let me get the garlic as well as the tranqs. And we got some syndicate guys we gotta take care of.
Madd Dogg: I got some brass knucks here and a black jack. That's the best I can do. Chris Hurte: That's fine. Madd Dogg: Sure thing. Lets see, that'll be about 50. Cut ya a deal. Chris Hurte: Sure. Madd Dogg: Here ya go. (He hands Chris the knucks and the black jack, then he goes to the the back seat and gets the 2x4's and the tranqs. He goes to the front and gets the garlic and puts it all in the back of Chris' truck as Chris pays Mr. Dogg.)
Madd Dogg: Thanks. See ya around. Chris Hurte: No, thank you.*grin* (Madd Dogg gets in his Lexus and drives off down the alley. Chris and the §howtime §uperstars pile into the truck and head the opposite way. The camera inside catches the action.)
Angel Gonzalez: He was kinda mean. How many people has he killed in his lifetime?? Chris Hurte: I dunno. I lost count. Francisco Lopez: Well, enough about him and this tag title match, that is the least of our worries. What about that Rebel guy. Now there's a threat if I ever seen one.
*laughs all around truck* Angel Gonzalez: It doesn't really matter, so let's do scissors-rock-paper for it. Francisco Lopez: No, it's scissors-paper-rock. Chris Hurte: It's rock-paper-scissors you fools. Angel Gonzalez: Man, calm down. Francisco Lopez: Well......it don't matter. My nephew could whoop his @$$. Watch, that kid is gonna be a great... Angel Gonzalez: Anyway, he ain't under contract. So one of us is gonna have to beat him up.
Francisco Lopez: Yeah, and not try to fall asleep at the same time. Chris Hurte: Let's just play dammit. 1-2-3. ( They play, and Angel and Chris get rock while Francisco gets scissors.) Francisco Lopez: YES!! I'm out. Chris Hurte: 1-2-3. (Chris gets paper and Angel gets rock.) Chris Hurte: DAMMIT!! Guess I gotta step in the ring against some jabronie again. Angel Gonzalez: YES! I'm saved!! Hey Chris, look at it this way, it's a good practice dummy.
Chris Hurte: You're right there. But practice dummies don't cuss that much, or not at all. Francisco Lopez: He did have somewhat of a foul mouth. Good thing for the editing crew here at HCW or we'd have some crying little kids in the day care center, built for hoes like LILITH that have too many dam love-children!!
Angel Gonzalez: How'd she become brutality champ?? That's like sayin Cerberus is a three-headed monkey.
Chris Hurte: He is about as lethal as a three-headed monkey. Someone needs to tell little pooch there to calm down.
Francisco Lopez: He ain't nothin but a fat poodle. More dangerous than Rebel though, or any part of the Non-nefarious Swine Sexers.
Chris Hurte: Especially that racist bastard Bonecrusher. Angel Gonzalez: Funny how LoP didn't even care when he called us spics, but the second he calls Kion a gook the tag titles are up for grabs.
Francisco Lopez: If all we had to do was make him make racial slurs then the titles would've been ours a long time ago.
Chris Hurte: Just make sure you don't make racial slurs to people when you win the titles. I dont', haven't, and never will.
Francisco Lopez: Gotta set and example for the youth today man. I'm tellin ya. Or else they'll all end up racist pricks like Bonescrushed. Angel Gonzalez: I feel a Save the Children special comin on, and I can faintly hear "We Are the World" playin in the background. Let's stop with this.
Chris Hurte: We haven't talked about that Jake Thunder guy. Maybe because he is in Bonescrushed's shadow.
Angel Gonzalez: Nobody takes him seriously any. So who cares. He doesn't talk either. So I'm not even trippin off of him.
Chris Hurte: He's no headache. Hell, he ain't even a bruise 1 millimeter in diameter. Francisco Lopez: I can't even see things that small. Oh, what about Adam Manson. Angel Gonzalez: I get goosebumps just hearing his name. Chris Hurte: I'm sick of him and I'm gonna put him in the grave....FOR GOOD. Francisco Lopez: That's the spirit. Kill the nightwalker. KILL EM ALL!! Angel Gonzalez: YEAH!! Chris Hurte: Calm down. I'm ecstatic as well, but if you keep it inside, you'll have more energy left when it is time to face him. Kind of like anger....except....different.
Francisco Lopez: Yeah, if you have to much anger you could lose. You can't fear people either, and if by some chance you do, you can't let them see it because....
Angel Gonzalez: (in best Yoda voice)fear leads to anger, anger leads to hate, hate....leads to sufferiiiiiing.
Chris Hurte: Yeah. Sure. But I know one thing, I'm gonna give him a Darth Maul-ish death. And then, another vamp will bite the dust. Francisco Lopez: Duh, duh, duh, another one bites the dust. Angel Gonzalez: duh duh duh, duhdu duhdu duh duh duhduh. Chris Hurte: HOOWAH!! Francisco Lopez: That's what I'm talkin about. (Chris runs a red light in his singing frenzy and a cop pulls him over.) Francisco Lopez: PIGS GO HOME!! Chris Hurte: DAMMIT!! Just great. Cut the f*ckin camera!!
