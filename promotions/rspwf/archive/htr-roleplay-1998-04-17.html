HTR Roleplay

17 April 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I just thought I'd post a roleplay to see what people think. «—---——----—-•¤[ Begin Transmission ]¤•—---——----—-» <The camera fades in.> <The camera drop-focusses and shows a dark and empty room. No sounds can be heard. You can tell that the camera is moving eventhough you cannot see anything. The lights turn on and show an empty room with four pearl gray, concrete walls. There is a puddle in the far left corner of the room. Looking along the wall, you can see water trickling down the wall from the ceiling.
"Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh can be seen sitting on a folding wooden chair. He is wearing blue shorts, a blue "Chump" t-shirt, and he is wearing black and white, Reebok sneakers.>
<Upon his face, he has some sort of evil grin. His eyes show his usual spaced out look. With the angle of the camera and the light's reflection, a discolored part of his upper forehead can be seen. He is clean shaven and his short, dirty blonde hair is matted down.>
<The camera slowly zooms in on Ian Walsh. Ian Walsh starts to speak.> "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh: "Well, well, well... 'The Wrestling Revolution' Tommy Rifles. I see that you and I are scheduled to face off on
May third. <He shakes his head.> Do you actually think that you have a chance in winning this match? I bet you do. You have plenty of good reason for having that mindset. You have a victory against James Pillow. In I.W.C., you beat him for the International Title. Does that impress me? <A smile breaks out upon his face.> No... You see, Mr. Rifles, I am in this for the glory.
This match will be one of the matches that will headline this event. Well... What can I say? I am going to go into this match with the same type of mindset as my opponent. I will go in thinking that I will win."
<He pauses for a minute and crosses his arms. He continues.> "Everybody knows that anything can happen in a wrestling match. However, I don't need to think about it. I don't need to speculate. I know what will happen in this match. I know that I will emerge victorious. I am not saying that I will end any careers, but I am saying that I will win. Tommy Rifles...
You don't make me worry. You don't scare me. You don't have me nervous. <He chuckles> I am perfectly content. <In a mad tone of voice and pointing at the camera> I am very content!"
<He calms down.> "What is it that makes a man go completely off of his rocker and sign their death warrant in disguise? You, Tommy Rifles, have made a mistake. You see...
Not many people survive matches with me. However, I have the feeling that you will. So what I will do is beat the crap out of you, pin you or make you submit, and then I will celebrate my victory."
<He looks at the camera.> "Ya know... I hate you like I hate all people. It's not like I go down to where you work and slap the dick out of your mouth. It's not like I told you to bend over and take it like you daddy taught you. No... I am just saying that this will be a great match and that I will emerge victorious. You see, this is part of my destiny. This is one of my goals. I want the P.T.W.F.
World Title. That is my purpose here. Of course I want recognition. Sure... I want glory. But... The way to get that recognition and glory is by winning the P.T.W.F. World Title. <He lets out a small laugh.> Tommy Rifles... You won't stop me. Nobody will stop me. This is my time... My era... I am ready. Are you? <He looks into the camera with a serious look on his face.>
Are you ready? You better be, Tommy Rifles... You better be."
<The camera slowly zooms in on Ian.> "You see, Mr. Rifles... You need <pauses shortly> to be ready. If you aren't ready for me, you will lose."
<Ian stops and looks down at the floor. The camera slowly zooms out. Ian suddenly says a final statement.>
"Oh, and Tommy... <He looks at the camera.> Prepare to meet your demise..." <Camera fades out.> «—---——----—-•¤[ End Transmission ]¤•—---——----—-» This preceding was a roleplay done by... Ian Walsh Handler of: "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh (NEW, IWC, INWI, IeWC, IEWL, PTWF, GNW, UXW,
