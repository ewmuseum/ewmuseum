[Scotty Travis]- I want your opinion . . . Fed Heads- check this out.

4 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Scene: The long stone walkway alongside Horseshoe Falls in Niagara Falls,
Ontario. It is late at night, and the light of a purple spotlight casts a mystifyingly beautiful yet strangely eerie glow on Horseshoe Falls. The area is empty, temporarily closed for the evening, yet one man remains. This man is seemingly broad shouldered, and stands at an avaerage height. The camera fades in closer and he is recognized as ASW Lightweight Champion: Scotty "The Body"
Travis. He wears a tight "What's A PAKaholic?" T-Shirt, loose fitting blue jeans, and a pair of Nike sneakers. Travis glances at the falls, then leans against the guardrail. ]
"Ah, yes . . . Niagara Falls . . . truly one of the most awe-inspiring and popular tourist spots on this planet. It's an amazing thing;
two-and-a-half thousand feet of magnificence, nearly half a mile of splendor, with thousands of gallons of water catapulting down every minute. However, what truly makes this spot a thing of grandiose awe is its pure power, its incredible capability of annihilation harnessed by nature and man."
[ Travis chuckles half-heartedly. ] "It's an interesting comparison; a force of pure power harnessed by man not only applies to these falls, but to the Lord of the Lightweights: Scotty
"The Body" Travis. Some people cease to realize this though. Cole Steele. A
ROOKIE, yes I called him a ROOKIE. Cole Steele, do I seem scared to you?
Obviously I'm not. What is your record here in ASW? What is your record in your whole CAREER? I have probably won more matches in ASW ALONE, than you have in your *ahem* "illustrious" career. Who have you defeated? Mr.
Excellence? Timmy Lightning? I see little kids like you all the time. I always have to deal with them, I guess that's what happens when you are on top of the world. Little punk ass bitches like you enter the league and you think you can step straight into my world. Think you can wrestle the upper card?
Guess what-- YOU CAN'T!!! _I_ am the only lightweight who draws a crowd. I am the only lightweight who wrestles on the upper card besides the man I am going to carry throughout the match. I am the one and only "showstopper", if you will. There is no match for my skill. I have seen you Steele-- you aren't anywhere NEAR the competition I have faced but if you want to step into the ring with this, well then get a contract in front of me and dammit I'll sign it. Beating your ass will be a who sh*tload of fun."
[ Travis pauses and smiles SADISTICALLY . . . ] "Now, a man from my past has arised here in MY promotion. Scotty Lee, there isnt much I can say about you. I have beaten you twice, maybe three times. You are a man with some talent, alot of hype. You praise yourself like you are somehting to fear, something to worry about, when the truth is-- YOU
AREN'T!!! Lee keep touting yourself . . . after you beat Mr. Excellence then report back with me and I'll shoot your ass down like I have time and time again. Life is like driving around in a car, and once you meet up with Scotty
Travis-- you've met the end of the road-- a dead end. Here's a warning for you Lee-- don't make a wrong turn, because I'll be waiting for you." [ Travis pauses . . . then sighs. ] "Now Shane Diamond. You are probably wondering where I was Thursday night. Well you know what, I aheared to your wishes and I didn't show up, like you asked. Now I know all you ASW fans are wondering what I mean . . . Well, here it is . . . "
[ Travis reaches into his pocket and pulls out a folded piece of paper. He unfolds it and then stretches it making it nice. He clears his throat and begins . . . ]
Dear Scotty, Hey buddy! Well you know I have been thinking about this match we have at Fever and I was wondering if you could do me a favor: Don't show up. I know you are the champ and your reputation is on the line but I really need this favor. See I used to think I could beat you, and I did once, but I think now everyone knows the "suits" made you take a dive, now I know you have the advantage and truthfully, I FEAR YOU! So please grant me this one wish . . .
Thanks champ! Your #1 Fan, Shane Diamond [ Travis chuckles and folds the paper back up and puts it in his pocket. He scratches something on the top of his head then resumes . . . ]
"Now all the fans know. Sorry Shane but I had to let them know the truth, not lete everyone think I was scared. It actually was sort of convienient considering my ankle is in alot of pain. See I was going to go out there and beat you like my bitch but I feel for you Shane. You are past your prime. A has-been in the lightweight circuit. I guess you could think that I gave you a night off. Let your sore, aging back recover a little bit.
Unfortunately it is only a matter of time before the ASW suits throw you to the hungry wolf again, I'm waiting Diamond . . . "
[ Travis smiles that oh so wicked smile once again and the camera slowly fades to an annoying electric blue . . . ]
x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x-x Well, what do you think? This is my most recent ASW RP which just shut down. Travis is the FWF Cruiserweight Champion, and the last ASW Lightweight Champion. E-Mail [detail withheld] and give me your opinion. E-Mail me again. -Bobby
