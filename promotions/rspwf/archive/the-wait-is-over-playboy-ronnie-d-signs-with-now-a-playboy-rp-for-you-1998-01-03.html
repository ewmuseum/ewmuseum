THE WAIT IS OVER! "Playboy" Ronnie D signs with NOW! A "Playboy" RP for you!

3 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
e-Wrestling Press -- Yes, last night, on January the 2nd, right before the main event of NOW Survival of the Beast 2, where "Chaotic" Christopher
Childress defeated Christopher Stonebreaker for the NOW World Title,
"Playboy" Ronnie D walked out to a packed house to announce that he had signed with Network Online Wrestling. His role in NOW is unknown at this time, but Ronnie said it should be revealed on January 10th, when he makes his grappling debut against "Blazer" Barry Blades.
Luckily, one of our reporters was able to transcribe Ronnie's speech from last night. What follows is the text of the speech:
------ [The lights go out. Some pink heart-shaped spotlights pan through the crowd. The crowd seems perplexed as to who is coming out now. Who could it be? Well, he's one of the NOW's many surprises, so we can't tell you just yet! The lights pan to the entrance way. Just where the wrestlers enter, in front of the curtains leading backstage, dry ice creates a smoke effect that can be seen through the pink lights converging. A drum beat starts and it can be recognized as the opening to "I'm Too Sexy" by Right Said
Fred. As the music starts, the lights and cameras and the eyes of all in the arena focus on the curtains. Then, as the second verse starts, the curtain bursts open and out comes a throng of busty women. There are approximately seven women in the group. Their affection seems directed to someone in the middle. As the camera pans, in the middle of the crowd we can see one of NOW's newest aquisition... "PLAYBOY" RONNIE D!
The crowd bursts out in boos as red fireworks go off at the entrance as the female crowd makes room for "Playboy" as he poses for pictures. He has pink-tinted sunglasses on and has his hair let down. He is wearing his wrestling gear: black tights with silver-gold-and-black striped leopard spots with a vinyl cherry-red broken heart on the seat and black wrestling boots with gold trim. He also happens to be modelling the new Ronnie
D-Mentia t-shirt. He also, naturally, has a microphone with him. He walks down the aisle slowly with the spotlights following him and stops in front of a good-looking young lady. He invites her into the aisle and they dance for a few seconds. Before he's about to send her back into her seat, he makes as if he's about to give her a kiss, but at the last second, he shakes his head and walks away, leaving a disappointed girl in the aisle to go back to her seat. The crowd keeps booing as he and his female entourage hit the ring and the music dies. The lights slowly turn on and the booing is much more audible now. With the lights on, we can see that Ronnie has let the dye out of his hair and it is now dark brown as opposed to light brown. It's still long, though. He has his usual maple leaf tattoo on his right bicep (where else?) and apart from the hair, not much else has changed.]
Playboy: OWWWWW! HELLLLLO CHARLESTON AND HELLO NETWORK ONLINE WRESTLING! [Some of the crowd cheers because he mentioned Charleston and NOW and some of the crowd boos just because he's the "Playboy." You know, the icon of e-wrestling, the showstopper, yadda yadda yadda...]
Playboy: You have GOT to know by now that I've dumped the NeCW like a two-dollar whore and hauled ass all the way over to the NOW! I got sick of them, after I whooped 'em all, so I decided to bring my act here! Yes, you ugly, out of shape idiots are LIVE and IN THE HOUSE with the main event, the big kahuna, the icon, the GOD of e-wrestling! And ain't it sweet?
["Playboy" has rubbed more fans the wrong way now. About 75% of the crowd is booing him now. You know, most people don't like him calling himself the icon. Or the god for that matter.]
Playboy: And do you want to know WHY I am the showstopper, the main event, the icon and the god of e-wrestling? Well, think about it. I mean, look at these rugged good looks. The six-pack of abs, the hulking biceps and this picture-perfect face. Not to mention my little friend right around here!
["Playboy" vaguely motions towards his crotch. Not too blatant, but enough for everyone to get the picture. All the people that get the picture boo.
There's a fat guy in his 30's yelling stuff at him. No need to mention what. Ronnie leans over the ropes and yells the following at him: ]
Playboy: What's wrong? Upset that it's bigger than yours? [As "Playboy" chuckle, the fan lobs a half-full beer cup at Ronnie. Ronnie ducks it and it splatters in the ring.]
Playboy: Hey! Whoa! That's all I'm taking, idiot! I certainly didn't come here for this! I'm not continuing 'til this ignoramus is OUTTA HERE!
SECURITY! ["Playboy" folds his arms and waits as security drags the guy out. A fan quickly gets in his front row seat. The crowd boos.]
Playboy: That's better! You know, that guy wasn't as dumb as he looks, but then again, no one could be! Now, where was I?
["Playboy" strokes his chin thoughtfully.] Playboy: Yeah, that's right. Physically, I'm perfect, the total package. Then of course, you ALLLLLL know what kind of attention I get. I mean, the chicks dig my great looks, and no one can stand the way I get away with what I want, when I want, how I want. I just showed that I can do it. So of course, everyone wants a piece of me!
[The crowd boos some more.] Playboy: Then of course, we can't forget that I'm naturally hip. I don't need fast cars, designer labels, lots of money, lots of women or a big mansion. Of course, just because I have it doesn't mean I need it!
["Playboy" laughs at his joke while the crowd boos.] Playboy: And on top of it all, I can run circles around anyone in the ring and I can push anyone's buttons. I have all the talent in the world! I can fight anyone, anywhere, anyhow! I am the single most talented wrestler
EVER! BAR NONE! I can beat anyone and anything be they male, female, animal, vegetable or mineral!
[The crowd erupts in boos. I mean, this is really loud stuff now. Loud enough that "Playboy" has to pause momentarily while it all settles down.
He stands, waiting for the crowd to calm down as trash flies in his direction. He doesn't seem to mind it as much now, but the coins rapping off his forehead don't really improve his disposition. Finally, the crowd calms down, but small projectiles keep getting projected.]
Playboy: I am what EVERY wrestler that ever sets foot in the ring wants to be! I am the single most recognized face in the wrestling world; not that
I'm just a pretty face in the crowd, even if my face IS pretty! I am the object of every wrestler's hate, every man's envy and every woman's
DEEEEEE-SIRE! [A few girls squeal in delight while the majority of the crowd boos. Boy, they really do like booing!]
Playboy: Look at this BEAUTIFUL face! [Ronnie puts his hand on his chin and flashes a smile.] Playboy: The hair Alberto created his shampoos for! [Ronnie fluffs his hair and runs his hand through it.] Playboy: The AWESOME physique! Look at the pecs! LOOK at the quads! LOOK at the BEAUTIFUL BICEPS!
[Ronnie flexes and runs his hand down his biceps.] Playboy: Everyone, wrestler or not, wants to be me! And I back up what I say because can! Who else can say THAT? Any of you? Not likely. Any of the wrestlers here? Even LESS likely! And I'll prove it later.
[The crowd boos very loudly. So what if that's all they do? What else are they supposed to do?]
Playboy: Everyone wants to be me but you know what? They can't! No one has as much attitude, style or talent than I do! They all run around calling themselves icons, legends, the greatest thing since sliced bread, but there's only one man, woman or child that is the icon, the living legend,
THE GREATEST THING SINCE SLICED BREAD! And that's the Real McCoy! The Real
McCoy has all the looks, all the talent, all the attitude and ALL the women! And I'M the Real McCoy!
[The crowd boos a lot more.] Playboy: And you know what? I look everywhere in this place, and all I see is a bunch of ME wannabes!
[Ronnie points at a caucasian guy in the front row. The guy has a blue denim shirt on, not that it matters. I guess he's proving that he can back up what he says now...]
Playboy: You! You want to look handsome, right? [The guy nods his head as "Playboy" leans over the ropes.] Playboy: You want to be famous, right? [The guy nods his head again.] Playboy: And of course, you want lots of women, right? [The guy nods his head, rather reluctantly. The girl beside him gives him a dirty look. So THAT'S why he did it reluctantly.]
Playboy: Yup. Just what I thought! Another "Playboy" wannabe! And each and every one of you are "Playboy" Ronnie D wannabes! Why? Because I have all the simple things everyone wants. Looks, attitude, talent. And I'm going to use my looks, my attitude and my talent to get my way here in Network
Online Wrestling! Why? Because I can. And if you don't like it, you can lump it! I'm the single hottest thing going in wrestling today and there isn't a man, woman or machine in the world that can stop me from going right to the top, where I belong!
[What does the crowd do? Duh! The crowd boos some more.] Playboy: Network Online Wrestling, you just watch it, because you're about to get an extreme case of Ronnie D-Mentia, it's going around! Hit the music!
["Playboy" does a few patented Ronnie D hip thrusts to another round of boos as the lovely ladies converge around him. He pushes a few off and steps through the ropes. The ladies follow him as he makes his way up the aisle to the back, heckling a few obese fans.
As he reaches the top of the aisle, just in front of the curtain, the ladies stand just behind him. He gives the crowd one last hip thrust, to a huge round of boos, before walking backwards into the ladies and behind the curtains as the music dies, making the boos sound louder.]
