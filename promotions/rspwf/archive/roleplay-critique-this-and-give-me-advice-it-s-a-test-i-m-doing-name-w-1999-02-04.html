Hey Guys, I made a new character, so far I've only got good responce from it,
I got the idea while writing a UEW Flash and well, from there I made this guy up... I think it's quite a guy to use, if I get around to it :)
So check out his uncomplete application and sample roleplay, I did for a federation, which I didn't end up joining..
Name: [name withheld] Email: [detail withheld] & [detail withheld] Manager: None Wrestlers Name: "The Mask" Maxwell Monai. Height: 6'5" Weight: 250 pounds. [I used these weight and heigh, because it's in the middle and if he is to mimmick people.. he will need it that way.]
Age: 22 Hometown: Not known, as Maxlwell's origins are mysterious.. no one knows if his name is even his, his face never revealed.
Allignment: Varies, with each opponent. Mostly a Tweener, I would guess. Wrestler's Style: All around/ Varies with opponent. Entrance Theme: [I need someting here, like any songs named "Enigma?.. or like that give a mystery.. or ya know relating the character?]
Finisher: Varies with masked worn at time. But, for desperate times.. "Masked
Fury" Finisher Description: A Vertical Suplex, into a Powerbomb type move. Wrestler's Personality/Gimmick: Who is this man? Nobody even knows! He calls himself Maxwell "The Mask" Monai, but to many, he just could be a John Doe, a nobody.. mysterious many say. He approaches everything differently, as there is not one same person but instead.. 1000's of different people combined, mixed into others. His attitude, can be one of love, hate, or just nothing..
his mood constantly changes. He will never give up, he is a true fighter.. wanting to make it to the top, for a reason, unkown as to why he acts this way.
His gimmick is simple.. he wears masks to the ring, never revealing his face, always seeming different.. but smooth and calm. The fans are a figment of his imaginery he can't care less what they think of him, yet.. they all look on, wanting to see what's next for him to do.
He's a rare mimmick, he can do it on the outside, in interviews, anywhere.. but the main place he mimmicks them is in the ring, wearing around 3-4 different layers of thin masks in the ring, he begins to wrestle with a plain mask, symbolishing his normal self, only.. during the match, he will take the mask off to reveal the next.. It could be ANYONE! A wrestler, the president, a fan.. but with that mask, comes a new attitude, a new assortment of moves, a new person. This continues, until he feels the mind games are over.. this is "The Mask" Maxwell Monai.
Wrestler's Looks: Nobody knows, he wears differnet clothes, he copies other people. Will they ever known though? Who knows! His hair, is a rich golden color down to his shoulder length, that is known though as it is seen through the mask's which he wears.. his eyes are a green color, eerie yet intimidating. He clothes constantly changing, yet his a muscular man, don't get me wrong.. what can be seen of his arms, look very strong, so does his chest, developed greatly.. tanned also.
Past Accomplishments: None, nobody knows who he is! Sample Roleplay :) =-=-=- [The scene opens up.. flash, flash, flash.. constantly flickering into a dark and white matter!]
V/O - Some people, they stick to themselves. [The flash comes to a stop, no more flickering, just a black screen.. images fall down the screen, pictures of people, kids, husbands, wifes, dogs, cats..
all different things in the world.] V/O - There are thousands of things out there, why stick to just one thing? [All the pictures blend in together, revealing a ..Mask] V/O - Yet, why not be everything? Do everything? Be "The Mask"? [A hand reaches out, his arms of a tanned complex. The arm grabs the mask, lifts it up.. showing the true beauty of it. It's color filled with different beauty, black, red, white.. it's all there, each giving meaning to "The
Mask". It's patterns, giving even more class to it.]
V/O - But this isn't the ordinary mask, oh no. There's much more to be seen, much more.. [With this, the mask in the hand begins to change. All different outlines, colors, patterns, show casing over 1000 masks. It continually changes as the voice over is done..]
V/O - Sure, there are thousands of mask's but some, mean more then others. [The changing of the mask's stop. But then, with a slight second the masks change to faces of wrestlers in the federation.. Adrian Blades, Warlock,
Oliver Closeoff.. all the wrestlers "Masks" are shown. Each of them, looking real, yet only being a mask.]
V/O - These masks mean quite a lot to one man. US-x, better get ready, because.. "The Mask" is coming, and if you got a gimmick, get ready for the
_Mimmick_!
[A laugh is head, the mask goes back to what it originally was.. the words
"Mimmick" and "Gimmick" repeadly repeat itself, over and over again.. then suddenly, silence.. The hand holding the mask, raises it up in the air, to move off the camera screen. Only for his hand to come back down, with nothing in it..]
[The person turns around, showing himself. The camera at this point moves back, zooming out so the person can be seen in full view. He wears, black boots, his tights are mutil colored. One side of his leg being in the colour
Red, while the other Blue, giving him more personality. On the outter side of each leg, there is pictures of many masks which fill the trunks up. Also the words "The Mask" are on each leg of the trunks..]
[His top half on the other hand is much different.. his hands are visable, they're quite muscular and tanned. He wears a light green muscle top.. his chest looks very developed also in this picture. As for his face.. "The Mask"
covers it, yet what can be seen through the mask.. his eyes, filled with fury.. as they're a dark green and eerie type.]
[His hair, rich golden blonde.. flowing down to his shoulders. He just stands there, looking on, with what appears to be a smirs through the mask. He steps forward, in a strong stance.. looking at the camera.]
MAN - Us-X.. [His voice, alternative and complex.. as it seems strong, it also seems an awkawrd one of now..]
MAN - Welcome, the next era.. the millenium.. chapter.. page.. book.. story, in other words welcome me.. .."THE MASK" .. MAXWELL .. MONAI [He lifts his head up sharply, his hair brushes back as he does this before he looks straight back at the camera..]
"TM" MM - New faces, new races, new places. That's what Us-X is all about, I see people all over the place, I see what I like the best
_Gimmicks_! When there is a gimmick, there is always.. I repeat _Always_, a
_Mimmick_!! [A chuckle is heard..] "TM" MM - You ask who? Never heard the name? Oh, I hope you better not think, I'm like all the others, because this ain't no dream, movie.. this is the real life thing! People get scared over Freddie, Chucky, Candyman.. all fantasy, welcome to my world, were fantasy becomes fiction and fiction becomes..
..REALITY!!!! You don't need to lock your doors, cause I won't steal anything. You don't need to hide your childen, because I won't kidnap them. You don't need to hide your money, cause I won't take it. But what you need to hide, is yourself.. because once, I get you.. you're
_Mine_! All you can hope to do is, hide behind a "Mask"... Because if not, Maxwell "The Mask" Monai, is going to grab a hold of something, you don't want people to hold...
..YOURSELF!!!! [The man, steps forward once again..] "TM" MM - So get ready, for a bumpy ride. Us-X a place with many gimmicks.. ..A place for me to _Mimmick_! Look into my "Mask".. it will tell you a story, a different one each time! It might even tell you you're own! But in the end, it's all the same, because it's going to bring me one place, on top.
An Adrian Blades? A Warlock? A Oliver Closeoff? Welcome to the millenium.. Welcome to.. ... THE ... YEAR ... OF ... THE ... MASK!!!!! Is your mask, my next attire? [Flash... Flash... he's gone, fade to black.]
