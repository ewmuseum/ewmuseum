Please Check This Roleplay & Give Me FeedBack Please...

7 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, I am in the federation IWC, and I am getting a World Title shot at the
PPV called Back to School Bash 98 against EMT, the fued has esculated to where EMT attacked me during an in-ring, and pushed over my wife-valet, and carried her away with him, and now she has a case of Amnesia and he is feeding her with lies and brainwashing her against me....this Roleplay is for the Friday Card comming up, and I was wondering if I can get some positive and Negative Feed Back on it...Thanks Alot =)
[name withheld] [Lollipop by Aqua is played over the P.A System as all of a sudden the crowd gets to there feet, but this time the lights dont go out and there is no pyro lasers as CandyMan comes out walking directly towards the ring, with a look of concern on his face, he still gets a huge reception from the crowd, but there is no CandyFloss beside him because of the obvious.....CandyMan hops onto the ring apron, as he stands there for a second, before entering into the ring and grabbing a microphone, as he calls for the music to cut, as the crowd can still be heard cheering him on...]
CandyMan: "It's been awhile since Monday....and since then alot of things have happened, and what better place to talk about this, then on the first edition back of Ring Wars.....[CandyMan smirks, as the crowd cheer...] You all watched in on Monday didn't you all? [The fans cheer on, as to say they did..] Well that means you know all that has happened...and that means you all saw me in that ring against Drew Mahony for the third time....where once again, something happened and the match went to a no contest....[CandyMan just looks around...] Once again, there was no clear winner....which makes me wonder, when will it happen where the announcer at the end of the match will say the words...and the winner is....when! [CandyMan shakes his head...] Drew
Mahony...One day this match of ours is going to come to and end, and I am going to put my money that it will happen at the CoW PPV, where I will not only defeat you...but will capture the North Eastern Title in the process....sure I have said this many times before, but I can feel it, that this time its going to strike luck upon me....
You have to realise what the main cause of our match's going to no-contests...it is because someone keeps on getting involved one way or another, if its not a friend, its an enemy...but always there is someone to make sure, the match doesnt come to a close..that is why we must make sure, next time..._Noone_ gets in! [Slight cheer by the crowd on this..] I have got to say on Monday, it was our best match yet...we both once again went neck and neck, and anyone could have came out the victor..we both where filled with anger, and it brought out the best of us...but our anger, got worse with the inteference that took place! Next time there wil be no EMT and no Scottie
Extreme to take part in the match...only me and you, and when my hand is raised....Finally, I will taste the _Sweetness_ of victory against you
Mahony...no hard feelings!" [The crowd cheer on this, giving credit to both CandyMan and Drew Mahony, for putting on great matches together in the ring, as Candyman continues to talk...]
CandyMan: "Now, on Monday.....an old friend of mine called me out, he wanted to know one thing...he wanted to know if I needed his help, in getting things straightened out around here, his name....Steve Vicious! [Slight burst of cheers as CandyMan continues on...] I would like to talk to this man about what he said...he told me I was fighting a war with the odds against me, but
Vicious havn't I been doing that from the start.....You see this isnt a war where the odds are against me anymore, because from the beginning nobody knew who I was, and everyone thought I was a joke....now look at me, and even thought many try and stop me from achieving where I want to be...I keep on fighting, cause no matter the odds...I still _Win_! [Fans cheer lowdly, as
Candyman goes on...]....
You asked me about aquiring your services...but from the beginning, I aquired no services....I learnt of Magnus Colby, and with that he taught me to stand on my own to feet and fight like a _Man_! Me and you might go along way, but this war is one where I must stand on my own two feet....things are getting personal, and when things get that way with me, I take it to heart....and when I take it to heart, I get _Nasty_! [Fans cheer on this...] You said you wanted your answer by Friday, and I must still give you what I rightfully think so....What happens in here, I must take like a man....If I want to be the best, I have to get there on my own...and that means fighting by myself...I like being the Underdog, because it gives me more reason to impress and come out the victor....So to answer your question, Thank
You...but _No_ Thanks....My Own Two feet is all I need!" [There is a huge burst of cheering as the fans credit CandyMans manhood by saying that he does not need anyones help, as CandyMan stands there looking around at the great crowd on hand...]
CandyMan: "Now....I come to the man, who I will stand my ground to fight against no matter what occurs...his name, is short and clean, his tactics are dirty....E...M...T! [Lowd roar of boos as this name is being said...] I see tonight you have a very important match, as you will be facing the other champions in deciding who is the best champion in the NCWA....Colby,
Trixxter, Extreme and you....each want to be known as the best, but only one can come out on top and I hope that man which comes out on top is you...So when I defeat you at the PPV, I know I have beat the best...to _Become_ the best! [Great reaction from the crowd on this comment..] You think you can interfere in my matches, and you think you can do whatever you are because you are a rebel of somewhat to say.....
But when I think of your name....EMT, I do not think of you...but I think of the actual Emt's which will be carrying you away once I am finished with you....the lesson you will learn is valuable...the beating you will take will mabye even put you in a _Coma_! [CandyMan smirks on this comment, as the crowd cheer...] You see on Monday you where lucky....all the lies and all the tapes you had been giving into CandyFloss, got the better of her....ever since the first day you took her, you have been feeding her with lies....that is what you are all about, why? Because you cannot handle the Truth....
That Monday, when CandyFloss hit me, and saved you...it made me realise, that for now...until I get my hands on her and make her wake up and smell the coffee...nothing I saw is use, because even thought she realises for a second, that I am the true man of her life...you end up bringing those lies, and manipulating her brain.....At the PPV Time will eventually catch up with you, and when the School Bell rings, and the class is dismissed....you will be left with _Nothing_! [Huge Cheer on this as CandyMan, works the crowd well...] EMT, your World Title....my CandyFloss....and most of all everything that has had any value to you, will be _Gone_....and then, I will be standing atop of the IWC, looking down on you, with my foot raised..knowing that anytime I could lower it and...[CandyMan stops his foot violently on the ground, as it makes a huge sound...] _Squash_..."
[The crowd cheer lowdly once again as, CandyMan goes on...] CandyMan: " Its only a matter of time EMT....your stay at the top has been overstayed....and the man to De-throne you, will be me....and there is nothing you can do but sit down on your throne for now, and watch it happening...piece by piece! [CandyMan look on, with anger...] Sure you might be one up now, but in the end....your going to be....all the way _Down_!
[Great responce by the crowd on this..] You see EMT, I know how you work and
I know the only way you think you can win, is by playing Mind Games and with help from others.....but what is going to happen, when the games are no more....and the help is gone? Will EMT Still stand above the rest....or will you crumble, piece by piece at my hands![ Crowd Gives good reception to this...]...
That is all I have to say about things EMT....[CandyMan hits his head, with his hand...] Oh Yeah, before I forget, bring CandyFloss along on Monday...and you come along too, because I got someone that wants to do a checkup on
CandyFloss...and according to this paper..[CandyMan pulls out a piece of paper from his pocket, and shows the crowd...] You must do it....because matters are getting a little out of hand, and the Doctor has to make sure nothing is wrong with CandyFloss......[CandyMan being sarcastic..] But according to you, she is fine and pefect....and _Nothing_ is wrong with her, so what have you got to lose...[Candyman laughs to himself for a second...]
_EVERYTHING!_...[Crowd give a huge cheer...] So get ready EMT...cause your fun is comming to an end...why? [CandyMan looks directly into the camera...]
Because your Grades just came in and you _FAILED_!...." [The crowd go crazy, and cheer even lowder as CandyMan leaves the ring and shakes some of the fans hands at ringside, as he goes to the backstage...]
