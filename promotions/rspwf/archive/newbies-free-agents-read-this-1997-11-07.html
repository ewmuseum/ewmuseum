Newbies/Free Agents Read This!

7 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you the casual Role-Player? Tired of joining a Fed only to have it close before it even gets started? Looking for a quick fix of e-wrestling? Are you a newcomer to e-wrestling that just wants to try it out before jumping into a Fed? Then this is the event for you. This is not a new Fed opening up. This is a one-time independent wrestling event. Depending on the success of the initial event, more events may be held in the future. There are no titles to compete for (now). What I do offer: complete match descriptions written out and I guarantee that this event will be entertaining. What I ask for: Good role-playing. RPs don't have to be huge, just good. High Rollers Hotel and Casino Presents: Vegas Championship Wrestling: The High Rollers Hotel and Casino is in Las Vegas, NV. Unable to attract high price entertainers like the big name hotels in Vegas, the High Rollers has lured in wrestlers to compete in a night or spectacular wrestling. The event will be held in front of a sold-out crowd of 300 people in the High Rollers Hotel and Casino Event Center. The event will be taped and can be viewed on High Rollers Hotel and Casino closed-circuit pay-per-view (meaning it's only available to people staying at the hotel) throughout the month of November. Wrestlers: It may be called the High Rollers, but they definately aren't big spenders. This event was only able to attract mid-carders and old-timers. No big name main-eventers here. What this means - I'm looking for 8-10 wrestlers (need Faces!) and 2 tag teams. Preferrably guys that aren't active in feds elsewhere (but they won't be excluded if they are active elsewhere). Do you have an old-timer that just can't cut it with all the youngsters competing today? Well bring him on in. Good characters are needed. (no Undertaker clones or Crows though. And we have enough Steve Austin bad-assess already). There will be a handful of singles matches (3-5), a tag match (if I can get two teams) and a high-stakes battle royal main event featuring all the competitors of the night. Winner of the battle royal gets $500 in chips from the High Rollers Casino. Matches will be booked, so the better your Role-Play the better your chances of winning. If I get enough people signed up in time, the event will be held on Friday, Nov. 14. If you aren't interested in competing, but would still like to see the event, e-mail me and I'll send it to you. A note about me: I am the former prez of Action Packed Wrestling (APW) that was in existance from Jan - May 1996. The fed closed when I had to leave the net for awhile. I was always prompt in getting cards out on time and wrestlers from the old APW will agree, the quality of writing was better than most. We were also one of the first feds to use an imaginary money system in e-wrestling. If any old APWers are out there, I would love for you to bring back your wrestler(s) for this event.
