Sample Roleplay from Wuzho

24 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I thought I'd take this opportunity to introduce my e-wrestler to you all. It's a classic style RP, but it is the most recent one I did and it goes over his personality pretty well. ---------------------------- [Camera cuts to a radio station studio. Radio DJ Mike Makkay sits in a chair, smoking a cigarette facing Wuzho. Wuzho seems a little tense but tries to relax as much as possible. Makkay smiles to the Romanian as the "ON AIR" sign lights up and the previous commercial ends.] MAKKAY: All right, you are still listening to the Mike Makkay show on KTCL 93.3 FM and we are back! Now, for all you wrestling fans out there I have quite a treat for you. I am now joined by World Wrestling Organisation star "Wuzho", who is just one of the many "rasslin'" superstars which will be in town over the next few days for the big "Season's Beatings" event extravaganza to be held in our fair city. Wuzho, welcome to Denver. WUZHO: [a little uneasy] Thank you, Mike Makkay, it is a pleasure to be here. MAKKAY: First time here in Denver? WUZHO: It is my first time in Colorado, period. MAKKAY: Good, I hope you enjoy yourself. Now, I'm a big fan of pro wrestling myself and of the WWO, and I can tell you it's a thrill to have such a great bunch coming over here for your big event. WUZHO: I hope that we do not let the fans down. MAKKAY: Now I know this interview thing is making you pretty uneasy… [both men chuckle] but I can assure you you're doing great. WUZHO: [giggles a little] Well, this type of function has never been my forte. Being a solitary person, especially since coming to the United States, I have never been quite comfortable in situations where I had to bear myself, let alone sit in front of a microphone and talk about myself and be the centre of attention. But, it's important for me that I do warm up to these kinds of activities so that my fans know that they are important to me. MAKKAY: And what a fan base you have created for yourself in your short time in the WWO. WUZHO: I have been very fortunate to have been graced by the fans of the WWO almost immediately upon my arrival. They have been extremely supportive and give me much of the energy needed to succeed in a match. I hope to learn to appreciate these "public appearances" more as the weeks go on because I truly want the fans to know that this is my way of thanking them MAKKAY: How long have you been in WWO? WUZHO: About two months I believe. MAKKAY: Two months and you've already built up a pretty good name for yourself, defeating a few large names in the WWO, Michael Fulton is one of them, his bodyguard Black Caesar, Zac Schneider, but then again who can't beat him… [both men chuckle] more recently Mean Mason's huge bodyguard Department of Corrections and, WWO big boy Iron. This is not necessarily your usual cast of easy wins for a rookie here… WUZHO: There is no such thing as an easy win, and to tell you the truth, each of these men put up their best and I gave them my best. The fact that I was able to pull off these victories proves in which direction which I am going and that pleases me a great deal. MAKKAY: What about your win against Iron… a lot of people would call it "controversial" at best… WUZHO: I believe that I have said everything that had to be said about that battle. It was definitely not the ending which I was expecting, and I definitely know that it was not the ending Iron was expecting. Controversial, I would say no. These types of attacks and set-ups are unfortunately part of the business we are in. Our livelihoods are dependant on getting people angry at you, getting people to want to get revenge. And more you can get a person angry at you, the better it is for you and your promotion. And as much as people like Iron, the Alliance, and even myself would love for this kind of situation to never happen, it unfortunately does not work that way and one must adapt himself to that reality. I know I have. MAKKAY: So are you satisfied with you win over Iron? WUZHO: No, I am not. Principally because I had Iron in a victory situation which I was cheated out of. But I am satisfied with what ensued in the battle and Iron proved to be the technician and combatant he says he is. MAKKAY: So we should expect a rematch? WUZHO: Down the line, yes, but none too soon. I have other items to settle beforehand. MAKKAY: Probably with Randy Bryson, the guy who "screwed" Iron and gave you the DQ win at that fateful battle. Am I right? WUZHO: I cannot begin to explain to you, Mr. Makkay, how bad I want to run Randy Bryson into the ground. I have the impression that he believed that he would only have to deal with Iron's anger, but he unfortunately will have to subject himself to mine. MAKKAY: So will you be issuing a challenge? WUZHO: Soon. Very soon. He disrupted what was to be the most anticipated match leading up to Iron vs. Andros at Season's Beatings. We both wanted to see who was the better man and it remains inconclusive. And besides, nobody makes my business their business in such a fashion without expecting some vindication. Randy Bryson is definitely a marked man and I will run him down with all the rage which he succeeded in inspiring in me. MAKKAY: I'll be looking forward to that one. But closer to us now is your long-awaited rematch with "Bruiser" Bob Hacker which will take place this Monday night at Season's Beatings… this one has been a long time in the making. WUZHO: Yes it has. MAKKAY: Looking at your yet rather short career in the WWO, Bob Hacker has scored your only loss. How does that make you feel? WUZHO: Hacker confronted me in a precarious situation in my life which I would prefer not getting into. He took advantage of it, acquired the victory, and for that I cannot blame him, for I would have justly done the same. You have all heard the adage of a "fair fight", well we gypsies believe in the fair fight but also believe in the capacity of winning the fair fight. And if your opponent is not in the capacity to win the fair fight but fights it anyway, your win is just as credible. But for me and my honour, this is a blemish that must be wiped away for my own conscience. Hacker beat me back then, but the question is will he be able to defeat me now. I have been through much in the past weeks and I have gained a sense of confidence and have cultivated a heightened hate of the world which Hacker had not encountered back then. Our battle will come to an end at Season's Beatings with the most intense challenge Hacker will have had ever since coming into the WWO. MAKKAY: So you are basically saying that you will beat him. WUZHO: Incorrect, my friend. I am saying that he will be in a complete state of shock as he sees me coming at him with no inhibitions and no control. My rage will guide me in the righting of the wrong and there will not be a single thing he will be able to do about it. If he defeats me again, then it was meant to be so. But nothing can prepare him for what I have in store for him. With everything that has happened, especially now that I want to get my hands on Bryson, he is my last demon before I can do so freely. I need to exorcise him out of my system to concentrate on my future endeavour against Randy Bryson. Until then, the rage boils as to what Bryson did to me, it tightens my muscles and enflames my tendons, and Hacker will be the recipient of this rage. MAKKAY: So a word out to Bob Hacker and Randy Bryson, look out for the angry gypsy… you speak quite a lot about your anger, Wuzho. Is it that much of a part of you? WUZHO: The way I see it, Mr. Makkay, is that anger is a part of everyone. In the times where I rode the Romanian countryside with my fellow brothers and sisters before coming to North America, I was witness to terrible ordeals, to horrible events, to debase hideousness which I never though a human could lower himself to. I learned to live with anger; anger at those who kicked us down, anger to those who left me in the gutter, anger to those who murdered my ancestors in cold blood, anger to those who sat back and did nothing but pity us… this anger gave me purpose, gave me the willpower to want to achieve and become something much more bigger than what I was destined to be. Life has dealt me a hand of cards which I strive to rid myself of. Only because of my heritage, of my background, I am seen as a cancer which must be cleansed. That is why I constantly remind myself of my past, of my ancestral background, of what suffering my people have endured over the centuries, because they were only attempting to try and live a life in which they could be respected and feel that they were no longer spat at or hated. And in the name of all those who tried and died trying, and even in the name of those who did not but who perished ONLY because they were gypsies, I will rise above my lowly station and prove that I am worthy of the praise and the glory that anyone else can get. My drive is my anger, Mr. Makkay. Purely and simply. MAKKAY: Very enlightening. Tell me Wuzho, now that you have been a little while in the WWO and you have got yourself acquainted with most of the guys, who do you think are the top dogs in the WWO? Who do you think, as you guys like to say it, is "simply the best"? WUZHO: [thinks] I would have to say men like Rusty Bowman, Robert d'Artois, American Eagle, Al Simmons… and Iron. These are men I respect for their talent and their drive. Although I may disagree with their motives sometimes, their determination and skill cannot be denied. MAKKAY: Okay… who do you think is "simply the worst"? WUZHO: I'd rather not go there. MAKKAY: Understandable, thought I'd try my luck. The big card is Season's Beatings, this Monday Night the 15th at the Pepsi Centre… best match of the night? Aside from yours of course… [both men chuckle] WUZHO: I would have to say a close call between Eagle/d'Artois and Rusty Bowman/Sapphire. MAKKAY: Okay… Iron and Andros, World Championship on the line… who walks out of that one with the belt? WUZHO: Iron if Randy Bryson can keep his nose out of it. I'm expecting that match to be exhausting. MAKKAY: Wuzho, I wish you the best of luck against Bob Hacker, we hope to have the WWO back here in Denver soon and thank >you < very much for stopping by. WUZHO: Thank you for having me. MAKKAY: And now Wuzho's theme song "Serial Thrilla" by the Prodigy rocks the house so that you keep listening to KTCL 93.3… [a bright white flash and everything goes black]
