My Freinds First net Roleplay

6 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
My Freinds first net roleplay, actually his first roleplay ever. Tell me what you think. Flame it. Critique it. Do whatever, it doesn't matter. But give him a chance, this is his first roleplay. -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- By: [name withheld] - handler of: Rob knight -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=- [ Scene opens on an abandoned old football practice field, where Rob Knight is seen in the background taking shots on a lacrosse goal ........cameraman walks up to within a few feet of him and stops ]
CAMERAMAN: Uhhhh.....Mr. Knight...is it o.k. with you if we ask you a few questions on tour upcoming match with "Cunning" Chris James?
ROB KNIGHT: O.K.?....Hell, I can never seem to get rid of you people. Anyways..why the hell are you asking me if you can? If I say no, you persistent little "camera people" from ICW will keep buggin' the hell out of me until i say yes. So....YES!!, go ahead and ask your damn questions!
[ Rob Knight takes another shot while he waits for the cameraman to ask the first question ]
CAMERAMAN: Well...ok. The first thing on everybodys mind upat ICW is ....if the first loss of your ICW career is going to change your confidence on the
Friday night fight against the newcomer "Cunning" Chris James.....Do you think that it might have any significant impact on this fight?
ROB KNIGHT: Impact?....Is this the conversation that comes up at the ICW meetings? Oh god!....Well first of all...you can tell everybody who's
"concerned" that there is no way in hell that the first match of my ICW career...which I lost...will affect the outcome of Friday Night Fallout against
Mrs. James..and second........you can run back to prettyboy "Cunning" Chris
James and tell him that if he plans on winning his first match.....he better be ready to run with a big dog....or get his skimpy ass back on the porch.
CAMERAMAN: Well..Mr. Knight....I'm sure that Mr. James will be pleased to hear your plans for this weeks fight. I have one more question that I need to ask you before I go.
[ Rob Knight throws his arms in the air and sighs loudly ] ROB KNIGHT: What the hell is it now! CAMERAMAN: Ummmm..I'm sorry to take up your time like this, but some .....rather most of the people....and fans of that matter.....seem to think that your ICW career is ...well, how should I say it.....going down the drain.
ROB KNIGHT: HaHAHa!!..."Going down the drain" they say? (sarcastically) LISTEN
UP!!!....This statement is for all of you out there who seem to think that Rob
Knight and his career are going down. ........After I was kicked off my lacrosse team , what did I have left to look forward to in life?....go on, think about it a second. NOTHING!! ..Now that I am in the ICW..I have a reason to kick some ass.....well two reasons actually...for one, the league allows it...and two, I'm looking for a place to get rid of all this built up anger inside of me. And I have a pretty good feeling that it will be coming out at
"Cunning" Chris James this Friday night. ......now if you would excuse me....I'm trying to catch up on a little practice here. Have a nice day!
[ Rob Knight turns around and begins walking the other way...laughing quietly and shaking his head ]
CAMERAMAN: THANK YOU FOR YOUR TIME MR. KNIGHT! [ Scene fades to black ] - [name withheld] Handler of: [name withheld] [ICW] 0-0-0 (On the LAW Waiting list) Bought Jack Vars #28 spot for $2 and a Bean Burrito w/ Taco Bell Hot Sauce - Sucks royally at Slingo
