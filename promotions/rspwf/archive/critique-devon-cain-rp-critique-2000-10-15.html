[Critique]Devon Cain RP [Critique]

15 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The following flash was the frist flash that I d id with this character and it happened to also be his introductory flash for the ASCW. I would appreciate some feedback as I am hoping to continue to use this character in the AGW, and I would like to be able to improve as time progresses.
Rob Handles "Wild Child" Brian Storm [KCW, WAO] Devon Cain [AGW] ================================================ [Fade in. The camera comes into focus revealing a barren basement. Cobwebs line the walls and even the lone window which allows the minimal amount of light to penatrate into the room. Slowly the camera pans the room revealing a staircase. As it zooms in upon it a lone figure can be seen squatting underneath in the shadows. It appears as though the figure is motioning the camera.]
Voice: "Come closer ... closer ... remain there." [As the camera zooms in every so slightly the details of the figure remain hiding deep in the shadows of the stair well.]
Voice: "Welcome to my humble parlor, and allow me the honor of telling you a short story." <Pause> Voice: "In Mark Chapter 13 Verse 36 It was said 'The Lord said to my Lord, Sit at My right hand, Till I make Your enemies Your footstool."' And here as I sit in the shadows of the ASCW I see beings making deals with their own personal devils, Higor Rosa and Tommy Grayson.
Two individuals making promises that they may or may not be able to keep ... just to play a game of one upmanship against one another."
<Pause> Voice: "Two presidents claiming that the other is evil ... promising status and titles to their loyal followers ... to weaken the strength of the other. And while all this occuring there is a neutral party sitting idlely by. Why? Is he awaiting his chance to strike ...
to pick up the pieces ... perhaps, perhaps not." [The figure shifts his weight slightly, remaining squatted and in the shadows at all times.]
Voice: "Yet, new followers ... perhaps pawns is a better phrase, are being added to the mix constantly, I just happen to be one of them.
Yet, Higor Rosa and Tommy Grayson, I'm not like Keenan or Bracci. I can't be swayed by empty promises of glory and fame. For I now that my eternal glory is granted by my Lord and Savior, not from your meaningless promises."
<Pause> Voice: "For I am Devon Cain, a seeker of knowledge and a man on a quest. A quest to bring redemption to unworthy souls of the world ... and a quest to perhaps help others understand the knowledge I have gained. Rosa and Grayson perhaps you will aid me in my quest or perhaps you will hinder me ... but either way bare in mind that our paths shall cross .."
<Pause> Voice: "And when they do what a tangled web it shall be. For perhaps each of you has knowledge that I need ... and there is the possibilty that one of you or maybe both shall become an ally to me on my journey. Or you may become an enemy to me. When our paths cross we do not know what shall unfold, but it has already been foretold by Him. And so it shall occur for it is what He deems neccessary. Slowly the chapters are unfolding and soon all the pieces shall fall into place. The only question left is are all the players ready for it to be written?"
[The camera begins to zoom towards where the figure has been squatting. As it so the sound of shifting weight can be heard. And withou a word the figure is gone. Fade.]
