[Critique?] Eddie Rue 1/2

30 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
--[Some words from Eddie Rue]-- [The camera focuses. From a distance, we see Eddie Rue sitting underneath a cherry tree. From there; - he seems almost freindly. Dressed in all black;
boots, pants, undershirt, and overcoat. He sits there under that cherry tree, his head against the trunk. Breathing heavily, as if almost out of breathe. His pupils' in his eyeballs are dialating. The eyeballs themselves bulging out of their respectful sockets. A tainted grin on his face almost hints he is having fun in an otherwise destructful face.
The camera goes in closer. Rue is holding a knife in hand. He twirls the knife with his left hand. And then sticks the tip of his knife under one of the nails of his right hand fingers. Picking out the dirt beneath. The knife slips on
Eddies' hand, cutting the tip of his finger, as blood spurts outward. He looks confused. As if he'd never seen his blood before. Afterall, he'd been so used to seeing his foes own blood, it was rare to see his own. Eddie procautiously sticks the wounded finger against his tongue, locking off the blood.]
ER- "What strange effect sharp objects have on the skin. One wouldn't think such a small cut would spew so much blood. But a large cut; - ah. One really must wonder the bloodshed from a victims' wide-open wound. Does it threaten ones state of mind to see their own blood coming out? Fang, perhaps you can help answer these many questions..."
[Eddie faintly raises his hand. And puts that hand up to his ear.] ER- "You hear that? That is me." [Eddie laughs.] ER- "You see. I am a silent killer." [A stern look of lust and distaste crosses Eddies' sodden face.] ER- "I'm going to cleave the meat off your bones, Fang. I'll then drain your blood as it drops slowly out of your body. And then, and only then, will I have satisfaction in destroying you once and for all."
[Eddie, enraged with delight, turns about to the cherry tree and jabs his knife into the tree. He then slowly turns about. Rue is breathing hard. His eyes bloodshot with bad moon.]
ER- "If there was one thing I set out to do when coming to Garden State Wrestling Promotions. It was. . .to take the world title without any questions needed to be answered. My goal shall now slowly be formed beginning this
Sunday. I will see to it that I will not only beat Fang to one inch- no, not even one inch of his life. I will leave nothing left of him. The remains of his dead body shall be rat food by the next mournings' day. And his soul- Fang's soul shall belong to me. Forever waiting for me to release it back into the world. But I hold the talisman of time that decides if and when I let him free.
Ah. . ." [Eddie whistles galantly. As he turns his head from the camera. His eyes look back, just enough to see the tip of his pupil's staring at the camera.]
ER- "The abundence of fun it shall be to slay thy. If only you could find fun in the pain I will inflict on you Fang. Then we both could have a jolly time as I whip you from pillar to post, and drag your face along the ringmat.
And then we could laugh together as I'll pour the blood you cough from your mouth into a shot glass. And drink your blood."
[Eddie laughs sickeningly.] ER- "But you know what? This Sunday I am the birthday boy at a ripe age of
27 years old. I am well in my prime. And I shall not lose. I shall not drop down on my back. But rather..."
[Laughs for no reason other than his own demented purposes.] ER- "Rather I shall grab you by the neck. Take a look into your eyes. And let you take a look into mine. As the last thing I want you to see before I knock you out for good, Fang, is me holding the Rue de Die. And you knowing there is nothing you; - or your mongoloid fans can do about it."
[Eddie slowly sprawls to his feet. Pulls the knife out of the tree, and slaps it against his pants. Cleaning the tree sap off the knife.]
ER- "Fang...I warn you once as a safety measure to your own health and maybe... "PROCEED WITH _EXTREME_ CAUTION!" [Eddie pauses. Then grins, looking through the camera man as if he weren't.] ER- "And If you don't? I'll take your casket measurements. Because your next stop is the newspaper obituaries and the graveyard tombs."
