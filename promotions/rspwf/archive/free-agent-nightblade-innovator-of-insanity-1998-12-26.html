[FREE AGENT] -NightBlade- "Innovator of Insanity"

26 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, my name is [name withheld]. Handler of NightBlade. I am currently looking for an ewrestling promotion for my character. Here is some info on me. I've been a roleplaying in the "ewrestling business" since MARCH 1997. I have an ewrestling experience of about 1 Year and 10 Months now. I personally feel that I am a pretty good RPer. I try not to sound concided but I am confident in my work. I have held many titles to date yet through my entire ewrestling career thus far, I have stayed 90% loyal to the "one wrestler/one fed" theory.
You wouldn't see Rocky Maivia on WCW TV when he wrestles for WWF. You wouldn't see DDP on WWF TV when he wrestles for WCW. That was the reason of my decision. Anyhow, I am now a free agent and I am currently looking for a promotion. Don't worry, I am looking for more than one promotion now that the fed I was loyal to for so many years have closed down. If anyone is interested, please contact me. My contact info is stated below and below that
I have pasted in a sample RP which was the last one I have done. -[CONTACT INFO]- AOL or AIM: [detail withheld] ICQ: [detail withheld] Email: [detail withheld] Character's Homepage: http://members.aol.com/HELLRAYZAH/ ****SAMPLE ROLEPLAY**** [Scene opens to none other than the Dark Mass Dungeon. The dark green neon light and smoke like fog is in the view is it is obvious it means one thing....
NightBlade, The WOLF World Heavyweight Champion is in the presence. The camera continues into the other room where NightBlade is standing, with the
WOLF World Heavyweight Title on his left shoulder. He is wearing his ring attire and is pacing back and forth with his long black wet hair in his eyes, covering his face that seems to be filled with violence and rage.... boiling with pure insanity. He stops and looks towards the camera and begins to speak....]
NightBlade: Ahh yes, it's great to be back here in the Dark Mass Dungeon. Other than that, I have some things to speak about. First off, since EVERYONE seems to think that SHOOTING is the new thing to be "cool" around here, let me shoot a little. Derrick Watson, I'm aiming at you as my target. You call me up on the phone earlier today and ask me ways that you can improve your wrestling in the ring when you know damn well we have a match on Monday. I get these emails from you saying "NightBlade... Be my teacher. Help me out"
Derrick Watson, you just like your father. Felt the wrath of NightBlade before he had the chance to talk his way out of the match. But as a man, as a wrestler, and as a fighting World Champ, earlier on the phone today, I gave you those tools in how to face me and being confident, I KNOW you won't use them correctly and to your advantage as I am all too much for you. It amazes me how some guys complain that they don't get a push yet they don't show or prove themselves to DESERVE a better chance at something. Take Python for example...
he feels I'm getting a push. He want's better things handed to him yet he is put in a match against Rain and from the looks of it, Rain will DOMINATE him
and the entire match. Personally, from one wrestler to another or from an inspirational outlook... from a World Champ to a future World Champ, RAIN take it from me... you DESERVE a shot at something here in WOLF as you have been impressing me lately yet some higher ups seem to be holding you back. Now you see, RAIN is a man who earns what is given to him like myself. Not a man who whines and complains that he isn't getting a "push" when he hasn't done a damn thing to deserve one... the only thing he has is ONE WIN under his belt and several losses. HMMM.... .seems to be the same status as one Derrick Watson.
HAH!! Kid, you think your going to beat me? Like that one song from Judas Preist says, "You've got another thing coming"... and Derrick, it's not in the form of a being, it's not in the form of a christmas present, and it's certainly not in the form of something mystical..... it's in the form of
something that describes my well being, my ways of torment. The ways of violence, mayhem, and carnage that I inflict unto my opponents. Derrick Watson, this coming Monday Night on Showdown, YOU will be that man who will be consumed by the insanity....... by none other than the man who innovates it...
that man is myself. The WOLF World Heavyweight Champion.... NIGHTBLADE!!! [NightBlade paces the floor and with his right hand, he moves the hair out of his eyes. While holding the WOLF World Title on his left shoulder, he continues to speak some more....]
NightBlade: Watson, I didn't say that those "men" were pushovers. Look at you, you don't even deserve a damn World Title shot and your beating up on sparring partners. Sparring is what helps on endurance, speed, and agility...
what I saw was a Professional Wrestler who got his wrestling license by the skin of his teeth beating up on two high school amateur wrestlers. That's what it was and your boasting about it. Derrick, you couldn't win a match more or less beat me in a match.... Watson, you couldn't even lace up my boots if it was my worst day and your best. But then again, you probably don't even know how to lace up your boots but that's a different story. Point is, you don't have what it takes to defeat me. I give you credit for having the guts to step in the ring with a man with such a caliber of myself but if you think for once that you will defeat me, then you my friend are sadly mistaken. You say that training isn't part of my IMO?? Whatever that means to you, take it for what it's worth Watson, shows how much you pay attention to my interviews as I am
CONSTANTLY training and working out improving my game. Look at where I am today. I didn't get here by picking up peanuts in a zoo for ten cents an hour.
I got here with blood, sweat, and violence. Derrick Watson, you say that your going to come into the ring like a bat out of hell and defeat me for the World Title.... HAH!! You think you can do that??!!! Damn, you must have been eating too many magic mushrooms because your starting to halucinate more than a fiend at Woodstock. Moving on, you said that you have a surprise for me... Oh gee, am I supposed to be scared? Is some guy going to interfere? I bet if it is, that punk bitch can't even lace up my boots either more or less know how to put them on. Watson, I watched you CLOSELY last week during your match with
Blackangel. I saw as you came out of the locker room. You seemed to be walking funny but later on after Blackangel defeated you, I SAW what could have been the losing factor. Derrick Watson, you had your boots on the WRONG FOOT!!
Yes, you heard me right. Your left boot was on the right foot... your right boot was on the left... And you plan on defeating me for the WOLF World
Title...... [NB pauses for a second] BAAHAHAHAA!!!!!! [NightBlade laughs so hard he stumbles to the floor as the WOLF World Title falls off his shoulder onto the floor. NB is laughing so hard, tears are coming to his eyes. NightBlade gets ahold of himself and picks up the World
Title and puts it on his left shoulder again. He continues.....] NightBlade: Watson, you say that your going to force me to wrestle a scientific match up. Well uh.. if you say so. There has NEVER EVER been a man who can force me to do ANYTHING and especially as a champion. A WOLF World
Champion if you will. You don't have the balls to indulge in a match that I'm used to wrestling in Japan so if you don't want to wrestle me, say so now otherwise take the risk of serious traumatizing injuries. No man can tame what
I am, what I do, or how I wrestle therefore Watson, you can not "force" me to wrestle the way you want me to yet if I did, I bet I can clean your clock quicker than you can say Lisol Disinfectant. Fact of the matter is, your having a hard time understanding your fate. Well not to worry Watson because in a matter of days, you will succomb to The Eclipse and feel my wrath of vengeance.
Oh, and I'm not trying to take credit for the past AaA attacks on your family... I ALREADY was a part of it. A PART OF IT!!! All of AaA was responsible and therefore I am as well. You can take all your anger and all your frusrations out on me because you and I both know it'll be like kitten
trying to fight off a pride of angry and hungry lions. You say that you were upset seeing your grandfather Rick Watson hobble around on a bad ankle, well son I'll tell ya why he was hobbling around on a bad ankle. The dumbass put his boots on the wrong foot too!!! See, it runs in the family.. The Watson
Curse lives on.. it's in the bloodline. Now Watson, don't be surprised if you start hobbling around with a bad ankle either. The Watson Family Curse is just like the Armstrong Family Curse of today yet they wrestle in that sad southern promotion down in Atlanta [Using a southern accent] where the "Big Boys
Rassle"..... Derrick Watson, prepare for the innevitable. Prepare to indulge in a battle beyond your imagination. Enter the Dimension of Darkness where violence and insanity dwells. Prepare to enter the mind and the domain of...... NightBlade!!!
[NightBlade walks away out of camera view whispering the words from a distance. It is quite unmanagable to understand but it sounded like he said "Prepare to be consumed by the Insanity"...... Screen fades to black as the whispering is saying "Blackangel" continuously over and over again..... all that is left is fuzz. Transmission Cut]
