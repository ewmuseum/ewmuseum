6'4.... ....265 lbs.... ....PISSED OFF! [The scene: Inside Tha Dragon's locker room. Tha Dragon is seen standing against the far wall, with his face tward it, one hand on the wall above his head... the other on his hip. His heavy breathing pauses, as he turns and notices the camera. His long jet black hair dangles in front of his face, moving slightly from each breath that pushes it away from his mouth. He is wearing his black full length trunks which he wrestles with, and a "ICW:
Friday Fallout!" t-shirt. He still has on his big black wrestling boots, and an elbow pad on the right arm. He starts moving tward the camera, and stops at the couch, taking a seat. He slicks his long hair back behind his head, revealing the half steel mask on the right portion of his face... clearly the right side of his face is exposed, giving everyone a good look at what race/color/gender he is. He also has a beard-only goatee, which he strokes through lightly, before reaching in his pocket, and producing a rolled up legal size paper. After opening it, he rolls his eyes up at the camera, head still pointed downward tward the paper... grins lightly, then lifts his head. He again slicks his hair away from his face, and begins to speak.]
Tha Dragon: You know... there's a few things on Tha Dragon's mind as of late. Two HUGE things. One of those things being the fact that Thad Thorton and company are obsessed with calling me a "chink," "gook," "slant eye," etc. Lets get a couple things clear, guys... do you see my skin? Do you see the color?
Look at my eyes... what do you see? Are they slanted.. are they crooked? No.. I am as American as apple pie, people. I was born and raised, by myself I may add, in the Rocky Mountains. I don't know where my family is, nor do I know anything about them... but I know I am nothing that you racist people claim me to be. So, with that, let's talk about idiocy for a moment. Thad Thorton.
The richest man on the planet, huh? Well, you obviously didn't get there from your academic accomplishments, now did you? You have beat me three times? Are you on the crack again, buddy? We have wrestled THREE times.. and yes, you have won two of them. But both times you won because of some moron outside stickin' their nose in my business. All three times, I had you sinched in Tha
Dragon Sleeper, and all three times I had you beat! You have admitted it, you little shit. You know I am a better tech wrestler than you, you know I have more skills.. and you know I will rise a head of you come hell or high water!
I can think of a simply way to explain this... you're a baloon, and I am the pin. This fued is ready to pop, and fly into oblivion... and I hold the key, pal.
[Tha Dragon stands up, and walks over to a chair, where he takes a seat, opens a cooler, and takes out a Pepsi. After a long deep chug, he tosses the can against the wall, splattering Pepsi all over the room. He then stands.. kicks his chair, and pushes the cameraman back away from him. Slicking his hair back hard, he looks deep into the camera lense]
Tha Dragon: You know... I tried to play nice. I came to the ICW to continue my career, and you little pricks just had to follow me, didn't you? Irish
Devil, and Miss Toy; you're just as bad as Thad. All three of you need to find lives, and get outta mine. But ya see, [Tha Dragon pulls out the paper again] I have a little something here that none of you wanna see. I wasn't going to say anything, but now I am. This paper right here holds the key to eliminating one third of the "Toy Soldiers." This here is a contract, signed by myself, and Miss Toy... showing an agreement that she is my Valet. Got that
Thad? Got that Devil? She belongs to ME. Did you take that all in guys? Do
I need to talk slower? Anyway... ya see... I don't really want her. Hell, you can have her... but this will be real simple. You guys keep her backstage during any and all of my matches between you, or anyone else, and this will all be fine. If she shows up... and is seen on camera _IN ANY FORM_ during one of my matches, or is involed, or seen on camera and/or by a witness during any attack by one of you two, or anyone else... I take her ass to court, and you two punks become Valetless. From then until eternity, I will be known as the man who took the "Toy," out of "Toy Soldiers." [Tha Dragon chuckles lightly]
Oh, by the way guys... I've talked to her about this, and her response was "I never agreed to be your valet in ICW." Well, my sweet little hoe... we never added any guidlines to this here contract about where and when you will valet me, except that you _ARE_ my valet. That means all the time. Be smart Toy..
and keep your career away from me while I still allow it to start. [Tha Dragon chuckles as he stashes the paper back in his pocket.] Tha Dragon: Now, let's talk about the next ICW Pay-Per-View. I take on the
Irish Devil.. AGAIN... and I will beat him... AGAIN. Hey Pillow, I know you're listnin' Pres.. take the part that says "w/ Miss Toy" off my match.. cuz she won't be there. She'll be back in the locker room, watching the monitor as I stash Irish Devil into the garbage can he came from, like yesterday's stanky trash. And Devil... I have a little suprise for ya if you even dare have
Thorton help you out. Speaking of which... what the hell do you call yourself?
The Ultimate Destroyer? Ahh.. whatever.. you may as well call yourself: Irish "Piss Drunk Cock" Devil, because that's all you are. Ya know what? I know a guy with a pet snake you'd get along great with. [Chuckles] So, here's a question, Mr. Leprichaun... why don't you take the clover out of your ass, set the lucky charms down... and face me fair and square. How about that, buddy? Do you have the balls.. well, I know ya don't.. so why do I bother?
[Tha Dragon shakes his head in dissapointment] Let's talk about the 30-Partcipant Battle Royal. The winner gets a shot at the International Champion at the next Pay-Per-View. Same crap, different orginization. And Tha
Dragon, being the KING of Battle Royals should be a definate odd's on favorite to win! I'm going to walk in... and walk out. It's that simple. Throw me in at #1 Pillow! I don't care... any way ya slice it, it's still bread... right?
So, I leave you all with these words of advice and wisdom. Watch out everyone.... Tha Dragon is headed your way... he's taking names, and then erasing 'em.... here he comes.... and I will see ya all....
....NEXT TIME ....THE LIGHTS.... ....GO OUT!!! Tha Dragon: Prepare for the match of your life. Bring your friends, bring your family... and ya might wanna bring some water, sand, and the whole damn fire department cuz ya.....
MIGHT.... ....GET.... ....BURNT!!! ["The Usual Suspects" by Mic Geronimo cues up and is played lightly in the background. Tha Dragons long black hair falls over his face, as his face falls still and calm.. and the scene fades black.]
