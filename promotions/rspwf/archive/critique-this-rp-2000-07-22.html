I usually do very long rp's but that seems to be frowned upon here. So I decided to post the shortest one I had. Anyway right now I'm using Destiny as an old Hogan type of character who is purley good, and all about doing the right thing so if it sounds cheezy it's kinda supposed to.
"The Future" J.B. Destiny (The Scene opens up in a Wendy's located somewhere in Memphis. J.B. Destiny has just sat down and is about to start eating his burger and fries when a kid comes run up to him.)
Kid: Ummm...I've seen you on T.V. before, your one of my favortie wrestlers. Ummm...can I have your autograph??? (A smile comes across J.B. Destiny's face.) J.B. Destiny: Sure do you have a pen??? (The kid then hands J.B. Destiny a piece of paper and a pen.) J.B. Destiny: So what's your name??? Kid: Alex... J.B. Destiny:(as he signs it.) Well there you go Alex, From J.B. Destiny to
Alex my favorite fan.
(J.B. Destiny hands the paper and pen back to the kid. The kid looks at it and a huge smile comes across his face.)
Kid: Gee, thanks!!! (The kid runs back to his table to show his brother and father the autograph. J.B. Destiny then looks up at the camera.) J.B. Destiny: Ahh, kids you've gotta love em'. That's why I'm so glad that
I decided to become a role model, somebody they could look up to. Somebody who is the exact opposite of The Kleman Brothers. You see they might be talented in the ring, they might be former four time EVCW Tag-Team Champions, but what there not is smart about trying to get title shots. You see Klemans in my last stint here in The XBWL I was just like you hot headed, and short tempered. I felt just because I was one of the best wrestlers on the independent circuit that I deserved to have everything handed to me when I got to The XBWL. But I was wrong and I found that out right away...I found out that the level of competition was higher in The XBWL than anywhere else in the world. I even had a few North American Title shots against the then
champion Lincoln Stone and yeah he beat me pretty bad every time. Basically I learned two things, one is that in The XBWL the comitee will give you opputunities but it's up to you to capatalize on them, and two if you start letting your ego get the best of you and you come down to the ring and make wild demands like you guys did just a few days ago then your never going to get anything accomplished and your just going to keep getting more, and more frustrated. So my advice to you boys is to just be patient because that's how things are here in The XBWL. You have to be around for awhile to get anything accomplished and you boys havn't been here long enough yet to demand
World Title shots, right now I don't even feel that Steve Edwards and myself are in a spot right now were we could demand a Tag-Team Title shot. Even though we did win a number one contenders match and never got our Title shot.
But there's no use crying over spilt milk. (J.B. Destiny takes a bite out of his burger.) J.B. Destiny: Anyway I don't really wanna talk about The Kleman Brothers title status. I wanted to talk about our match tommorrow night. And I wanted to talk about some of the comments The Klemans made about The New
Icons that are absolutley false. I mean there comments didn't make sense at all...first they asked The XBWL bookers how they had the nerve to book them against The New Icons. Well let me explain something to you, you guys should look at this match as an oppurtunity. If you guys can knock off The New
Icons you will be right in line for a Tag-Team Title shot. We're a world class team and the booking comitee realizes that, maybe you should too. Then later on in your interview you talked about all the "great" teams that have graced The XBWL ring. You mentioned International Army 2 and High Intensity.
Which I don't understand, now don't get me wrong thoose are two good teams but if you call them "great" how can you call The New Icons an insult to step into the ring with??? We've beaten High Intensity, as a matter of fact I believe we made them look pretty pathetic, and we've beaten IA2 four times that's right four times. So let me ask you this if there so "great" and we're such an "Insult" then why do we basically own them??? I'm not blaming you for what you said though. You proably just didn't get a chance to see us in the ring yet, but you'll get your chance tommorrow and you'll find out what so many teams already have, that The New Icons are THE absolute pinnacle of Tag-Team wrestling, we represent what Tag-Team wrestling should be...
(J.B. Destiny pauses for a second.) J.B. Destiny: I hope I don't sound to cocky but it's kinda hard talking about Me and Steve's Tag-Team history with out sounding just a little arrogent because yes we HAVE beaten alot of great teams, yes we HAVE forced wrestlers into early retiremnet, and yes we HAVE held The XBWL Unified
Tag-Team Titles, but what we have done isn't importan anymore. It's all about what we will do and we will once again hold thoose belts, and we will once again bring back the competitivness of the tag-team division. I remember just last year there was about three legit teams in The XBWL and one was The Beast and Matt Austin who I personally don't even consider a team.
Anyway once J.B. Destiny and Steve Edward signed on others followed in our foot steps, it was teams that were looking for a competitive division, teams that were looking to compete for a pair of tag-titles that actually meant something. Then when we leave the division fizzled away again so The New
Icons returned and helped build a tag division that was stronger and more competitive than ever but in less than a month that too dwindled away. So we're back once again and we will help The XBWL's tag division rise to domanince once again. So Will and Adam you should appreciate what we're trying to do. This isn't just for us, it's for every tag-team who is looking for a stage to perform on that's worth there time and effort, and most of all it's for the fans.
(J.B. Destiny takes another bite of his burger.) J.B. Destiny: Well I think I've said enough about Adam and Will but before I get outta here I wanna just let all of the fans know that even though Steve says some things sometimes, and even though there not the greatest, or the truest things to say you guys don't have to worry about The New Icons breaking up again. Steve doesn't mean what he's saying he's just as we say in the business getting "over". Now I'm sorry I have to cut this interview so short but I have to hit the gym before tommorrow.
(J.B. Destiny takes his shake and the rest of his fries and heads to his car as the scene fades to black.)
