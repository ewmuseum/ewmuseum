[Tropical Storm:Interview]

28 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera opens on the sunny California coastline. As the scene begins to zoom in, four men become visible, standing just in front of the surf.
Two of them, both with the compact muscular builds of high-flyers, stand with ESW tag team championship belts over their shoulders. The third, an equally compact young black man stands next to a shorter gentleman dressed in sharp three-piece suit and a "Planet Hollywood" cap. Long-time e-wrestling followers will recognize the foursome as the "California Kid"
Key Atkinson, Harry "Head-Hunter" Harris, L.T. Hurricane, and Johnny Gere... Tropical Storm] Key: The Storm's been out of the game for quite some time, now, and e-wrestling world, it's time to batten down the hatches, 'cause we're ready to come back!
Harry: We've been itching to get back in the ring, training hard to make sure our comeback would be as great as our first run.
L.T.: Ladies and gentlemen, what you see in front of you is true excellence. My companions here form arguably one of the most dominant tag teams in
e-wrestling history, compiling easily the best win/loss percentage in the sport. As for myself, my four ESW lightheavyweight title reigns speak for themselves. Add into that Key's tenure as both ESW lightheavy champ while half of the championship tag team combination, and you'll see that we've got what it takes to bring home the gold.
[At this, the man in the suit, Gere, steps up to the camera...] Gere: That's the long and the short of it, folks. Alone, in pairs, or all three at once, it doesn't get any better than Tropical Storm. Youngsters, ask the veterans, and they'll back up what we say. Problem is, I've got three top-of-the-line wrestlers, and no federation. That is a problem
I intend to solve soon, and when I do...you might as well just turn over the gold to the Storm...
[The camera fades...] --------------------------------------------------------------------------- What this amounts to, guys, is that I'm ready to bring the Storm back somewhere, but with all the feds out there, I've got no idea where to go.
If some of you guys (Aidan, Mike, Shawn, Nat, Josh, JB, BJ...you know who you are ; are still out there, I'd appreciate your input on the different feds. Just a quick e-mail to [detail withheld] would be appreciated. Later, Tim
