Looking for a fed ... try this one, great cards.

30 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<A HREF=" http://www.geocities.com/Pipeline/Slope/6563/ ">Championship Wrestling Federation</A> Brett Logan vs Minister Massacre ***Barbed Wire Hell In A Cell Weapon Heavy Metal Title Match*** Vanessa: This is Minister Massacre's last match, and at his request, we will have no introductions.
Eric: Yes, we know both men too well. Brett Logan, the Heavy Metal champ,
Minister Massacre, the retiring legend .. Heavy Metal match in a cage.
Matt: This is it, Massacre's last match, so let's get to it..... [The lights go down in the arena, an there is an eerie quiet. Smoke is released from underneath the CWF ramp leading to the ring with a loud hiss. The speakers start to hiss and crackle as a "Tubular Bells" starts to play. The walls light from top to bottom with red, overhead lights, making the arena look like it is filling with blood.
Yellow lights start to flash and an orange spotlight falls on the top of the ramp. Massacre walks, confidently, to the top of the ramp. He looks out on the crowd and raises his arms and his head. The crowd starts to clap, and their claps turn to cheer.
Massacre remains standing at the top of the ramp .. the crowd stands and start to chant his name, repeatedly. He takes his first step toward the crowd and the spotlight turns to white and Massacre continues to walk toward the ring.
He reaches the ring and takes the stairs gently into the cage. He walks slowly to the center of the ring and stops, turning towards the ramp.
The crowd continues to chant the name of the man they have all despised at one time or another, whether burning, hanging or crucifying their favorites, he has done it all. He has held every title imaginable, and usually more than once, but tonight is his last CWF match, an all-violent cage match.
He stares around the ring cage and the floor, as there are weapons everywhere. There are the standards: garden tools, bottles, bats, wire hangers, and oddly, the statue of an owl.
The lights fall again, pyrotechnics explode, and "Chemicals Between Us", by
Bush, starts to play. Brett Logan runs down the ramp with a buggy full of other weapons, and Massacre quickly exits the cage to meet him.
Logan releases the cart and it slams into Massacre. Logan grabs Massacre's back and walks him up the steps to the opening of the cage and pushes him in. Then, he jumps off the steps and starts to throw the contents of his cart into the ring; 2 bedpans, a VCR, a taser, 2 chairs, a kendo stick, and a box wrapped in plastic.
Massacre pulls himself back to his feet as Logan finishes his emptying and swings the kendo stick at Logan.
The bell rings, the match has officially begun.] Eric: Logan takes a hard shot, now two, now three from Massacre, and now a kick in the stomach.
Matt: Massacre was looking pretty serious at the beginning of the match, last match, will it be his...
Vanessa: Massacre whips Logan into the corner and charges him ... Eric: Logan kicks a bedpan into his face, Massacre is staggered .. Matt: Logan jumps to the top rope .. no .. the top of the cage ... BULLDOG from the top of the cage.
Vanessa: Massacre is down .. Logan is searching for something .. he grabs a chair, he's running toward the downed Massacre .. pushing the chair into
Massacre's throat. Eric: Does that count as a pin attempt? Matt: It would, if his shoulder's weren't up. Vanessa: Massacre struggles against the chair. Logan stomps his stomach and throws the chair aside.
Eric: It looks as if the Minister is ripe for a quick farewell. Matt: Logan hooks Massacre's leg ... 1 -2 - kickout!!! Vanessa: Logan starts to look for something else .. he finds his kendo stick. Eric: Massacre is on his hands and knees. Matt: Logan hits him across the back, once, twice. Vanessa: Massacre keeps crawling toward the ropes. [The crowd starts to chant Massacre's name again] Eric: Logan kicks Massacre in the stomach. Matt: Massacre has grabbed a hold of the bottom rope. Vanessa: Logan climbs to the top rope ... topei. Eric: He just crashed onto Massacre's back. Matt: Logan picks up the box that was wrapped in plastic, he is trying to get it open. Vanessa: Massacre is getting up, he's pulling himself up the ropes. Eric: Logan gets the box open. Cigars are spilling out all over. Matt: He's putting it in his mouth, he has a lighter .. he's lighting the cigar. Vanessa: That looks like a cheap one. Eric: Massacre is up .. he's breathing hard, but he's running toward Logan. Matt: Logan steps out of Massacre's way and puts the cigar out on Massacre's ear. [Massacre yells out .. then stops. He turns toward Brett, his eyes open wide] Vanessa: I think Massacre just snapped. Eric: Logan sees it too, he starts to back away from Massacre .. then charges at him. Matt: Massacre grabs Logan, mid-spear .. he picks him up ... POWERBOMB. Vanessa: It seemed that early on, Logan just wanted to hurt Massacre, now it looks like Massacre may be taking that cue.
Eric: Massacre grabs the shovel from behind the ringpost ... he's slamming it against Logan's back.
**Massacre: Can .. yoooou... dig it? Matt: Logan is crawling now, with each smack of the shovel. [Logan rolls away from final shovel shot] Vanessa: Massacre missed with that one, but Logan is still hurt. Eric: Minister grabs a hanger, he brings it down across Brett's back repeatedly. **Massacre: Nooooo wiiire hangers!!!!!! Matt: Logan's back is bleeding. Vanessa: Massacre grabs him by the hair and leads him over to the door of the cage. **Massacre: Open the fucking cage. Eric: Massacre is going to get out of the cage. [Massacre tries to throw Logan out of the cage .. but Logan resists, and elbows him in the stomach]
Matt: It looks like neither man is trying to win. Vanessa: Logan breaks away from Massacre and grabs, he grabbed a VCR, He fell on it. Eric: Massacre is helping Logan up .. Logan clocks him with the VCR Matt: Massacre is bleeding from the top of his head. Vanessa: Logan whips him into the ropes .. he hotshots Massacre into the cage. Eric: Massacre is down between the ropes and cage .. Logan is looking for the right instrument to play a symphony on.
Matt: It looks like a baseball bat. **Logan: Hay batter batter, sawiiiing. [Logan hits Massacre in the stomach. Logan puts the bat between his legs and spits on his hands, then rubs them together.]
Vanessa: Massacre looks like tee, with his head the ball. Eric: Logan pulls back to swing .. HE MISSES. Matt: Massacre fell to the ring apron, Logan can't really hit him well from there. [Logan grabs Massacre's hair and drags him out from under the ropes] Vanessa: He's spreading Massacre's legs out .. placing a bedpan on his groin. **Logan: I always wanted to be a field goal kicker. [Logan kicks the bedpan over the top rope, and it obviously hurts Massacre.] Eric: And it's goood! Matt: It looks like Logan is primed for the win ... He's going for the top rope. He turns around.
Vanessa: Moonsault!!! Eric: Right onto the chair next to Massacre. Matt: Both men are down .. both are hurt. Vanessa: This is a non-refereed match, so there will be no countout. Eric: A ref could get hurt in there. Matt: The situation .. Massacre and Logan have both hit each other hard, now both are feeling the effects, recovering from them.
Vanessa: Massacre starts to sit up, he looks at Logan .. he's falling back down, he must be exhausted.
Eric: Logan starts to get up ... Massacre isn't moving ...Logan checks his arm. It is limp ... He is starting towards the door. Matt: Logan is going to win. Vanessa: Massacre is sitting up, he's got something in his hand. He's pointing it at Logan.
Eric: Logan just stopped .. what's happening. Matt: Logan just fell down. Vanessa: The taser Logan brought in the ring .. Massacre just used it on him. Eric: Masscare sits up and wags a disapproving finger toward Logan. [The crowd cheers and starts to chant "Massacre". Massacre stomps on the taser, destroying it in the process]
Matt: Good strategy, I wouldn't want to feel that after I used it either. [Massacre walks over to the stunned Logan and drags him to the center of the ring.] Vanessa: What is Massacre setting up? Eric: He's reaching down to the ground ... picking up a smashed cigar. Matt: Someone just threw him a lighter. [Massacre lights a crushed cigar, then starts to cough, and walks over to Logan and drops it on him, then rubs it into Logan's stomach]]
**Massacre: It's eeeeeeeasy to quit smoking. Vanessa: Massacre is picking Logan up ... bodyslam on a chair. [Massacre plays to the crowd, cupping his ear and summoning the crowd. The crowd responds with his name.]
Eric: Massacre bounds off the rope .. BIG LEGDROP. Matt: Logan grabs his throat and rolls over to his stomach. Vanessa: Massacre picks up a chair. **Massacre: Violence before violence sake. [Masscare swings the chair and connects with empty canvas.] Eric: Logan rolled out of the way .. he superkicks the chair .. Massacre falls down. Matt: Logan climbs to the top rope ... kneedrop! Vanessa: Massacre is stunned!!! Eric: Logan's putting his shoulder into Massacre's gut, he's lifting him up .. to the top rope. Matt: He's setting him up for the Final Judgement ..... Vanessa: From the top rope, Final Judgement. Eric: Massacre is down ... Logan is up .. up to the top rope again. **Logan: Oooooooooh Yeahhhhhhhh. [Logan dives off the top rope connecting to Massacre's chest with an elbow drop] Matt: Logan is grabbing the other VCR .. he throws it at Massacre's head. Vanessa: Logan is lifting Massacre up .. looks like the Redeemer. Eric: Massacre kicks him in the groin. DDT!!! Matt: Massacre is shaking his head .. he looks around. Vanessa: He's picking up the Owl statue .. what the hell is that from? [Massacre points at Logan] **Massacre: Logan .. when they ask "Whoooo whoooo is the best" you tell them to give a hoot and don't pollute. [Massacre puts the owl on the mat and lifts Logan up] Eric: Massacre sets him up ... Bloody Benediction!!! Matt: Logan is out on the mat .. the crowd is chanting Massacre's name. Vanessa: Massacre stops .. one weapon hasn't been used .. he's picking it up. Eric: A beer bottle? Matt: Logan is in trouble. [Massacre breaks the bottle against his own head and starts to bleed. Then he exits the cage. the bell rings.]
Loudmouth; The winner of the match ... Minister Massacre! Vanessa: I'm gonna miss the big guy.
