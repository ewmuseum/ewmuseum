Critique - Tommy Dreamer post

21 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there. The name's Rob, and I handle Tommy Dreamer in XCW. I was wondering if you could look over this introductory RP and tell me how it is.
Strong points, weak points, where I could improve, etc... I'd appreciate the help. Thanks.
[The camera opens on a bleak, dismal dressing room. The room is modestly furnished, with the very basics; a few metal chairs, a wooden table, and a small radio, from which Alice in Chains' "Man In The Box" blares into our ears. Sitting on a steel chair by one of the eggshell-whit walls is a dark-haired man with his head bowed down. His face is invisible, and he is dressed in loose black pants and a black T-shirt. He can be heard breathing heavily for a few seconds before he speaks.]
"The more things change, the more they stay the same." [The man slowly raises his head, to reveal a familiar face. Complete with goatee, slightly pale complexion, and fixed look of determination on his face, Tommy Dreamer reveals himself to us, his eyes focusing on the camera lens.]
Tommy Dreamer: Since the beginning, I have put my heart and soul in ECW. Yeah... the same ECW that was born in Philadelphia, that was little more than a gutterpunk wrestling fantasy. From the days of Tod Gordon to the
Paul Heyman era. I'd bleed myself dry once every three weeks in a little bingo hall out of Philadelphia, and get paid [BLEEP!] wage. And you know what?
I loved every minute of it. Then came ECW's rise to the public eye, and more and more beatings. Bleed myself dry more often... and get paid more than before. And I still loved every moment of it. Then came the downward spiral which closed ECW and left me without a job... without my title of "The Innovator of Violence".
[Dreamer bows his head again, as a sort of act of respect towards the company that he helped build over the course of the 90's. He then lifts his head up and stares yet again.]
TD: Then the WWF came a-callin'. I didn't have to bleed anymore, and I still got paid well. The WWF was not a bad place... but something was missing; the way I had made a trail of blood and broken bones over the years in bingo halls from Philadelphia to Fargo. The way I had gotten so much respect by the fans who KNEW what entertainment was; not some catchphrase-o-rama with guys like the Rock and Ric Flair, but men sacrificing their bodies for the entertainment of the fans. I love doing that, and no sum of money could change that. Sacrificing my body, risking life and limb, it's all I know. But now...
[The Innovator of Violence cracks a smile.] TD: The XCW is here. The XCW is a promotion that will let me be what I made my reputation on being. "The Innovator of Violence". The man who risked himself for the entertainment of the fans... and left behind a trail of blood, both his own, and those daring enough to cross him. The man who ate, drank, slept, dreamt, and BLED ECW. ECW is dead, and there's nothing I can do about it. I won't make my time in XCW about shoving ECW down everyone's throat. But I WILL spend my time in XCW doing what I did in ECW. Jake
Roberts, on Friday Night X-Treme, you and I are going to face off, but if you think that this match will be a typical "pounce on the rookie" deal like you were hoping for after your loss to Terry Funk... you got another thing coming. Jake, you have a history of being a low-down, dirty sleazeball.
One of the greatest psychologists in the game against one of the most violent men to ever lace up a pair of boots.
TD: You may be the intelligent, cunning snake as you say you are, but I'm a man who shows no fear, even when facing the most fearsome opponents. Jake, they say the best way to kill a snake is to cut off its head. Come Friday night, Tommy Dreamer will be the knife that slices your head off. Jake, I'm in XCW to stay, and that's not good news for you.
[Dreamer bows his head down once again, as a feminine voice interrupts...] "Tommy... which do you like best?" [The camera pans right, to see a beautiful young woman facing Tommy, holding two outfits by hangers.]
[The Queen of Extreme herself. Beulah McGillicutty.] [Pan left to a grinning Tommy Dreamer.] [Fade out.]
