[We open up backstage, at one of the AWMC's many house shows. We are looking at a door backstage, to be precise, a door that reads "The Threat" on it in gold lettering. The door opens up as if something magical was influencing it to do so, and we peer inside the dressing room of The Threat... or what once was The Threat's dressing room. Strewn about on the walls is black tape, stretching from one side of the room to the next, on all four sides. But that's not the worst part. The worst comes when we see what else is being used to "decorate" the room. A clear substance is spread all over the walls, making them shine, as dozens of empty K-Y Jelly bottles lay open on the ground. Also, hanging from the ceiling like ornaments from a tree, are sex toys galore, from dildos to vibrators to unblown-up sex dolls. And highlighting all of this, in the middle, is the king of the dildo... The Destroyer. Twenty four inches of pure steel beauty. And standing under The Destroyer is The Binky and The Winky
Fairy, which as we all know go under the name Precious Moments when they are together]
The Binky Fairy: Look! Look at all the pretty stuff we did! The Winky Fairy: We found all of this stuff in that bag over there... [Winky points towards a leather bag with "The Threat" printed on it in the same golden letters that were on the door]
TBF: And we decided that we needed to make this room look nicer. So we used what we found in the bag to decorate this place. Doesn't it look a lot more friendly, now?
TWF: I especially like the big centerpiece. It's like a shimmering stick of glory, hanging amoungst the other objects of beauty that the room contains.
TBF: That was an amazing description, Winky. Sometimes you shock me, you're so smart. TWF: What can I say? I learned from the best. TBF: Who did you learn from, Winky? TWF: Well... you, of course. TBF: Awwww... [Binky bends over and gives Winky an Eskimo kiss, then stands back up straight, a huge smile on his face]
TWF: Well, now that we've made The Threat's room all nice and stuff... you think they'll stop being evil?
TBF: I don't know... they're pretty bad. I think we need to make sure that they're good. I think we should set-up a nice, kind, happy meeting at Above
The Law. TWF: Good idea! That way, we can judge their goodness. TBF: Yes, and if their goodness isn't good enough, we'll just have to show them that evil doesn't pay off in the end.
[Winky looks around the room, nods, and smiles] TWF: We did a really good job here. TBF: Yeah. We'll have to do this more often! TWF: Who's next on our list of people to cheer up? TBF: How about that one president guy, Akira Musushi? TWF: Moosooshi? TBF: Yeah. Should we decorate his room? TWF: Why not? This was fun! And it's going to make The Threat VERY happy, I'm sure. Why shouldn't we spread the joy?
TBF: That's not all we can spread... [Ummm... this is rather disgusting to say, but Binky is now reaching into his tights. He's tugging and pulling at something. Please... God... don't let him do what I think he's going to do... I swear I'll go to church every Sunday...
say grace at meals... stop masturbating as frequently...] TBF: THERE IT IS! [Here it comes! I can't look. It's a... a... wand?] TBF: MY MAGIC FAIRY WAND! [Thank GOD it wasn't his... nevermind] TWF: Now we can spread our joy by binking random people! TBF: Let's go! [Binky and Winky now leave The Threat's dressing room, which is trashed beyond repair, and start walking down the hallway, looking to the left and to the right. As they come upon a janitor sweeping the halls, Binky rushes over to him and touches his forehead with the wand]
TBF: BINK! Janitor: What the heck was that about? TWF: Be happy! You've just been binked! Janitor: I will not be happy until you explain why you're touching me on the forehead with that... wand thing.
TBF: You see, when a fairy wants to spread his love and joy, he takes his wand and binks people!
TWF: You didn't know that? How do you spread YOUR joy? Janitor: Usually I spread my joy by spreading a girl's... but that's really none of your business, now is it?
TBF: Ummm... I guess not. But are you at least a little more joyous because I binked you? Janitor: You know... you did make me realize how much "joy" I spread. So I guess I am a little happier. Thanks, guys.
[As the janitor walks away, humming the theme from Shaft TWF: WOO HOO! WE MADE SOMEONE HAPPY! TBF: We definitely get Fairy Points for this one! TWF: Pretty soon we're going to be Fairy Scouts instead of Fairy Cubs. TBF: I CAN'T WAIT! [Binky and Winky hug each other, then begin to skip down the halls, looking for their next victim. Since I really don't feel like going through the trama of hearing another utterance from Binky's or Winky's mouthes, we're going to have to end this here for now. Until next time... which will hopefully be later rather than sooner]
