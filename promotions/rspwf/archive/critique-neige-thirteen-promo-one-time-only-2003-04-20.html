[CRITIQUE] Neige Thirteen promo - ONE TIME ONLY!

20 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yep, I usually don't submit anything on these boards; but this was my first, honest-to-God, actual SERIOUS promo. Haven't done one of those in years. So yeah, intelligent and constructive critisism is gladly welcome and do tell if you appreciate the change of pace. On a side-note, that promo took a SHIT-LOAD of time to write out so that it made sense.
Set-up: Neige Thirteen qualified for the Jersey Heavyweight title by defeating Nathan Smith. He takes on Nathan Holmes, Outlaw and Nick
Demola in a four way match next week. GSIW is a two flash per card fed, so the idea I had was to make a non-conventional promo for the first flash packet, and the second flash packet would be the more traditional promo. It has already been established that Neige is a mute ninja, but whose mastery in the arts of hiding objects inside his ninja outfit allows him to pull out pre-made fan signs.
________________________________________________________________________________ ________________________________________________________________________________ DAYDREAMING IN JERSEY OR THE PAUSE BEFORE THE BATTLE [The scene opens up on the unending bright blue sky over a residential area in Newark, New Jersey. Cloudless, with the sun nowhere in sight; just blue, more blue, blue for as far as the eye can see. Camera pans around, capturing nothing more then the clear skies until it turns over to the various apartment complexes of the city. There, we come across GSIW wrestling ninja superstar Neige Thirteen, resting on top of one of the cities rooftops.] ???: Neige… NEEEEE-EIGE??? [For those of you who do not know what the hell a Neige Thirteen is; well if your name is Nathan Smith, a Neige Thirteen is someone who just pinned you one-two-three. For everyone else, a Neige Thirteen is a ninja that looks like the bastard child between Shinobi and Frosty the Snowman. A ninja snowman if you will; you know, in recognition of his birthplace:
cold-as-f*ck Québec, Canada. Neige Thirteen is a masked ninja with a rubber carrot nose, three black buttons over his white ninja gi, a blue scarf of the Québec Nordiques and wooden branches coming off his armguards.] ???: Neige!!! Answer me, where are you? [So we start off with Neige Thirteen daydreaming on top of a roof in
New Jersey. The camera stares silently as Neige simply looks skywards, lost in thought, his head resting on a bag overflowing with mail, petting a dark-gray cat sleeping over his tummy. This serenity is broken once
Christine Champignon Colde, sister to Neige Thirteen, slams the door leading to the roof open. Upon seeing the heavyweight championship contender wasting his day away under such circumstances, she ordinarily would've given her brother a good verbal trashing. Instead, she takes two steps towards this great blue world and remains awe-struck by it's immense beauty.] CCC: Neige... There you are. I was looking for you... [Neige doesn't answer. This would seem par for course for our mute ninja, but Neige now says even LESS then he usually does, if such a thing were even possible. And it is not.] CCC: What, no girls with you today? You feeling sick or something? [Neige silently pets his cat, lying on his back and staring up at the clear blue sky. Try as she might, Christine Champignon Colde just cannot get the attention of her brother and her inability to do so frustrates her greatly. Her options exhausted, Christine decides to give up and steps up next to her brother, staring up at the sky along with him.] CCC: Heh, fair enough. I guess I should congratulate you on your match: the guys said that was the first time they ever saw anyone reverse a german suplex with a Majistral cradle. Don't know what the hell that means, but apparently it was quite impressive. [Neige doesn't answer, he just keeps stroking his cat and stare at the sky. Were I in a more humorous mood, I'd say Neige was stroking pussy, but let's not go there. Instead, Christine sits down for a little heart to heart with her brother.] CCC: You know, mom would really appreciate it if you could visit her once in a while. The whole family misses you terribly. Dad says you've been gone for 16 years, and I believe him... Couldn't you just go back home and say hi after all this is over? [Neige Thirteen turns his head to face her sister. Carefully, so as not to disturb his sleeping cat, Neige reaches into his ninja outfit and delicately pulls out a fan sign. "CAN'T TALK, REMEMBER?", it reads. That reaction from Neige is not what Christine expected.] CCC: You don't need to "SAY" hi, I simply meant that mom and dad would really appreciate it if you'd visit them once in a while, tell 'em all was alright! Geezes Neige, you can be SO literal! Why are you always like that? [Neige's cat perks up, woken up from it's deep slumber by Christine's interjection. Raising it's evil head upwards, the near-black cat gives off an evil eye in the direction of the offending woman as it prepares for action.] CCC: Godammit Neige, you haven't even told me what you were doing here in the first place! Wrestling a whole bunch of costumed idiots, and for what? To be the "King of Jersey"? Whoop-dee-freakin'-f[BLEEEP]! What's up with you, I don't remember you being so vain! Hell, one of the only memories I have of you is of us... up on the hill, in Cinq-Mars... staring up at the empty sky... Yeah, just like that... [And then there was silence. Meanwhile, nothing offends a cat like being rudely woken up from it's slumber and then ignored. So as brother and sister stare vacantly into the sky, the dark cat hisses and growls, it's head slowly growing to fifteen times it's own size until it threatens to swallow
Christine whole. Only by pure stroke of luck that the girl manages to notice the mutant cat.] CCC: That cat is new. It's really cute. [She said, oblivious to the danger as she scratched underneath the psycho cat's chin. Neige held up a sign as the cat purred contently, it's head returning to normal as if nothing out of the ordinary ever happened in the first place.] Neige's sign: "IT'S NAME IS WOOT. A STRAY I FOUND IN AMERICA" [Christine ignored her brother as she played around with the cat. Neige ignored his being ignored by continuing on with the silent conversation.] Neige's sign: "ON A SIDE NOTE, IT'S A REALLY BAD IDEA TO TEACH YOUR CAT THE WAYS OF NINJA" [Christine kept on playing with Woot, teasing it with a loose string. Cat and girl fool around until they stumble over the bag of mail that
Neige's head was resting over, causing a few of the letters to flutter about. Interested, Christine bends over to pick up one of these letters. Nonchalantly, she opens one up and reads it out loud with that perky voice of hers.] CCC: Hey, you get fan mail? That's so cool! Can I read them? Too late to stop me! A-hem: "Dear Neige, how do you type with boxing gloves on?" No, that's stupid, I'll read that other one instead: "Hey Neige, love your spazzed out high flying moves! You're like a mad ninja stick of dynamite! I hope you totally flip out and kill all those other forty year old retards at the King of Jersey tournament! You know I'll be there, so come up and see me after you win. Mandy." Damn bro, you a player! Can't understand what the girls see in you, but I can't understand what girls see in Jeff Hardy either, so it's not like I'll ever understand. Here's another one: "Yo
Neige, raised in Jersey but born in Mexico here, and I heard you wanted to be a huge movie star like Santo. Well let me tell ya, you don't have to be Hollywood, you're the real deal. While all the other phonies in wrestling just talk and act for the camera, you actually train hard so as to spaz out with the intensity and totally ninjafy these losers to death. Dress how you like, but never forget your roots. Signed: G." Hey Neige, looks like you got yourself something of a following, hey bro? [Christine chuckles as she watches over her brother's lying body. The cat has snuggled up against Neige's chest and the ninja doesn't have a care in the world. "How can so many people have such a misshaped idea of my brother", she wonders.]
CCC: Hey Neige, I'm letting you stay up on the roof for a little while longer, but only because I don't want you ending up like everyone else in that stinkin' wrestling federation of yours. These last few letters
I've been reading, they got me to think... Spending your whole life inside of gyms or inside the ring, training, fighting, doing everything to win... That ain't a life worth living, and there's no way anyone like that can be considered a winner in the end. You listening to me Neige? If you end up winning that belt, I want you to stay just the way you are. Don't end up like those other creeps whom wrestling is all they think about, guys like Nick Demola or the Outlaw who actually believe the monikers they've given themselves. You hear me Neige? You're a lot more human then all those creeps and jerks you're working with, so don't let anyone tell you otherwise, understand? [A few tense seconds pass with hardly a reaction from Neige. Christine never grew up with her brother and you can tell that she doesn't feel at ease with him, as if he was still a stranger to her. But then Neige raises an arm. And on top of that arm is a thumb, raised high in the air. Christine rejoices.] CCC: I knew I could count on you bro. I'll tell everyone at home to watch when the Supercard comes! Dad actually saw you when you did that wrestling PPV for WILD: he said it was the weakest wrestling match he ever saw in his life. Hey Neige, I'll call the GSIW crew for Wednesday, we'll film them a video that they'll never forget! Alright Neige? Alright? Yeah! Once the bell rings, we'll see who's lying on their backs! We'll blow everybody's socks off, NINJA STYLE! [Exit Christine Champignon Colde, stage right. Until she comes back again.] CCC: Oh yeah, and Neige? Once you're king and all, can you make me a princess or something- no wait, princess are daughters of kings, that won't work: how about a countess or a baroness or something? Make it a real cool title, okay? Bye!!! [Exit Triple C, stage right, for good. Camera stays with Neige Thirteen and his cat for awhile as they continue skygazing. Camera keeps filming for some time, until we catch the unthinkable: actual words from Neige Thirteen.] Neige: ... ... Baroness, shit! [And we fade to black.]
