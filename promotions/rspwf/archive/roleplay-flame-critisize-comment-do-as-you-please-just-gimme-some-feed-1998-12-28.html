[Roleplay] Flame, critisize, comment, do as you please! Just Gimme Some Feedback

28 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first RP for WWO. [The WWO cameras begin to roll. The scene is in a rather large office. Inside the office on the right wall of the office there is a large trophy case. Inside the trophy case are the following. There are six gold belts. "ICW World Title",
"AAWF World Title", "CWL European Title", "UWO U.S. Title", "RTW Tag Team
Title", "EWWF East Tag Team Title." There are two trophy's, one reads "UWO Most
Improved Wrestler" and "UWO Most Hated Wrestler". There are also other various smaller trophy's in the case. On the left wall of the office are two pictures, one is of Kenneth Morlock in the ring putting a figure four on an unknown wrestler. The other pictures is of Kenneth Morlock sitting posing in a brand new leisure suit. In the middle of the room there is a rather large dark oak wooden desk. On top of the desk, there are files and papers in a disarray.
There are staplers, pencils, pens, etc. There is a brand new computer sitting in the desk as well. There is a nice leather chair behind the desk. Also behind the desk is a huge view window. The middle of the window is showing the outside. It appears to be very high up, the rest of the window is covered up by some blinds. The room has a cold dark, look about it. All of the sudden the door of this office opens up and Kenneth Morlock walks in. Kenneth Morlock looks at the camera and chuckles to himself. Kenneth Morlock is wearing a pair of Tommy Hilfiger jeans, a red, and white Tommy Hilfiger sweatshirt, and a pair of Nike gym shoes. Kenneth Morlock walks around the desk to his leather chair and sits and props his feet on the desk and leans back in his seat.]
[Kenneth Morlock] Well, well, well WWO. The World Wrestling Organization. Well, it look's like you all have been graced by the presence of Kenneth Morlock. Yes, that is right. Kenneth Morlock, one of the greats has decided to bring his talents to
WWO. And why not. All you hear about these days is how great WWO is, and how all the real talent goes to WWO. Well, I thought to myself, why not me, I am real talent, I am a true gem in the wrestling ring. So why shouldn't I come here to WWO to showcase my talent. I am a natural blue chipper in the squared circle. But I have been holding myself down by staying in mediocre indy feds.
Well, WWO Kenneth Morlock is here and he is definitely ready to start the
Showcasing! [Kenneth Morlock takes his feet off of the desk. Kenneth Morlock leans onto his desk and cuffs his hands and puts his jaw in his hand, as he talks.]
[Kenneth Morlock] I look at the names here in WWO. And I have to say that this roster is truly blessed. Names like Eric Draven, Knick Man, Troy Majors, Mad Goat, James
Pillow, and Shawn Harrison. I tell you, I see nothing but pure talent here. But that doesn't mean I am going to back down because there is talent. That means that I am going to step my game up yet another notch. I mean, my skills are already up at the top, but now they will be at immeasurable heights. Who will be able to stop me? Well, that will be something that we will just have to wait and see on, because I truthfully think I am the best and will try my best to prove it every time I step in that ring. There may be all these great names here in WWO, but you are just getting a Kenneth Morlock now, so it was never really complete till now, and when I get the gold I deserve around my waist. It will be like sweet joy for myself and all my fans and followers. Some may say it is cocky to talk about myself in these great ways, but momma always taught me to tell the truth and to always eat fiber. And how can I be more truthful than to call myself awsome. I mean anything less would just be a total disgrace to my name. And that name is a name that all of you will remember for a time's and time's to come, Kenneth Morlock. Pretty Plus Power Equals ME!! Playtimes
Over Little Children, the Maker has arrived!
[The WWO camera begin to fade to black as Kenneth Morlock gets up from his desk and walks to the camera and then laughs and then walks out of the large office.]
Kenneth Morlock WWO~~ [0-0-0] EIWF~~ [0-0-0]
