Free Agent

22 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am sick and tired of joing federations only to have 1 of 2 things happen. After a few weeks that shut down of the president is to stupid to run things. If you run a good RP federation that RPs throw email and has been around for a long time and isn't going to shut down emial me at [detail withheld] with the subject Free Agent and let me know about it. I have included a sample RP. I would like to have Syn {Fake} and Rob Van Dam, but I have held titles as Dean
Malenko, Eddie Guerrero, The Rock, Kanyon, and Jacqueline. {Jacqueline is sitting down on a couch in the hotel room that she will be staying in on the nights before the card. She has just finished watching the
New Age Outlaws interview and can't help but laugh. She is dress only in a robe and with a name like "Sexual Chocolate" there is no telling what she has on under it. Jacqueline stands up and walks over to the refrigerator, she pulls out a bottle of water and takes a few sips trying to stop from laughing, it doesn't work. For some reason hearing that the New Age Outlaws have fantasies about her spanking them is just too much to handle.}
"Sexual Chocolate" Jacqueline: New Age Outlaws, you don't seam to understand, you can't beat "The High Flying Revolution," no one can. Think about it you come down to the ring and cuss everyone out, aren't we big boys now. I am going to tell everyone the truth right now, no one absolutely no one can beat "The
High Flying Revolution." It's a simple as that, we can't be beat. New Age Outlaws while you where out in the ring talking all your trash you did get one thing right, you are going to be all over the television, because after "The
High Flying Revolution" gets through with you it will be up to the doctors to see if they can put you back together. I mean hell it seams that the two of you are a little preoccupied with me already. It seams to me like you can't stop talking about me, that I don't understand since it's not me you have to worry about it's Syn and Rob Van Dam. Why do you think that those two have been getting calls from all across the world, because they are the greatest and people want them to wrestle in their federation? Let me explain something to you New Age Outlaws, Syn and Rob Van Dam are known world wide because they are the greatest and everyone wants to see them, you on the other hand are known only in America, and you only got that because of a "push." The fact is while
Syn and Rob Van Dam paved their way in wrestling you where begging the promoters to give you a chance. Oh and Mr. Ass.........
{Jacqueline lifts up the back of her robe showing off a pair of blue, G-string panties and the finest black ass the world has ever seen.}
"Sexual Chocolate" Jacqueline: .......you can only hope that someday you will have an ass as good looking as mine.
{The camera fades to black, but as "The High Flying Revolution" is known to do there is still more to come. When the scene starts up we see Syn and Rob Van
Dam standing nest to each other. They too have just finished watching the New Age Outlaws interview and like Jacqueline they can't help but laugh.} "The Sexiest Thing Alive" Syn: Let me get this straight New Age Outlaws you really think you can beat us? Seriously, you think you can win? It seams that no matter where we go there is always some little punks running around claiming to be the greatest. Well, make no mistake about it New Age Outlaws, we are the greatest and your not and the reason why is we can prove it. Myself, Van Dam,
Rey, and Juvi have proven to everyone in this sport that we are the greatest, you two on the other hand have proven nothing. But don't worry because when you step into the ring with "The High Flying Revolution" all those so called rookie mistakes as they call them will all come to light. The second you screw up and believe me you will screw up we will be there and that will be the end of the match. You two have a snowball's chance in hell of beating us, hell no one here can beat us. So instead of talking about shit and each others asses I sure hope that the two of you have hit the gym, notice I said gym, not each other if you see where I am going. Because if you don't work out and you come to the ring like to out of shape drunks all the ladies in the crowd will know who the real "Sexiest Thing Alive" is. I mean after all it was the ladies in my life that gave me that name. I would tell you all their names, but that would take all night and frankly I have better things to do with my life then sit her and name every women in your families.
{Rob Van Dam does the now infamous double thumb salute then starts to talk.} "Mr. Monday Night" Rob Van Dam: New Age Outlaws are the shit, yet "The High Flying Revolution" gets the interview on Sunday Night Supershow. It seams that the New Age Outlaws have got who the real deal and who the jobbers are mixed up. "The High Flying Revolution" are the real deal, after all we are the greatest, New Age Outlaws, nothing more than some jobbers that got lucky because a promoter needed to fill some space in a show. Not only is "The High
Flying Revolution" going to win the tag team titles, but we are going to humiliate the New Age Outlaws. Better yet we may just win the titles and then let Jacqueline show why her ass looks so much better than Mr. Ass and let her humiliate the New Age Outlaws. After all it isn't that hard to humiliate some stupid ass jobbers. "The High Flying Revolution" is the greatest are we are going to prove it by not only beating the New Age Outlaws, but by going on and winning every titles that FCW has to offer. New Age Outlaws don't even show up if you have a fear of losing, but then again when you go up against "The High
Flying Revolution" you always lose. And Mr. Ass thanks for the reminder. I'm the whole fucking show.
{The scene fades to black, but once again that annoying whistle is heard, which can only mean one thing, Bill Alfonso.}
Bill Alfonso: "The High Flying Revolution"...........is the greatest.............and they will prove it. Not only..........will they be the future.................FCW Tag Team Champions............but we will have every belt in FCW. Because Rob Van Dam is the..................whole fucking show.
{A commercial is shown on Rob Van Dam, Syn, Juvi, Rey and the formation of "The High Flying Revolution."}
