Dr. Mayhem flash

15 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[[The scene fades into a hospital and there stands a man with a nurse discussing some things. The man seems to be over 6 feet tall and at least over
250 pounds. He turns and sees he's on but a mask is covering his face. He's wearing a mask like Kane's as well as a normal doctor's mask that he would wear in an ER. He has a massive built body. He looks as curious as he could look with those two masks on. Then he shakes it off and nods to the cameraman]]
[Doctor] Welcome. [[He checks his chart]] You seem to have an appointment. Please, please come in. [[The Doctor and the Nurse walk into an office and the Doctor sits down as the nurse makes some coffie]]
[Doctor] My name is Dr. Mayhem. Yes, DR. Mayhem. I wouldn't suggest anyone questioning that. Well anyway. I guess you are here about me and the HWoAR correct?
[[The screen goes up and down for a while]] [Dr. Mayhem] As I thought. Well. As I said I am a doctor. I've been suspended from the practice of medicine for the patient mishap.
[Nurse] But Doctor. Didn't you injuried those people on purpose? [Dr.Mayhem] Yes. But that's was my chance to prove my theory! Come with me. [[He goes to another room and pulls down a screen and turns off the lights and puts on a slide]]
[Dr. Mayhem] My thoery which I proven was that the only cure for anything was great amounts of PAIN! As you see here is the make-up of the human body. I used my time in medical school studying up on pressure points, and bones weakest points. Anything that would damage the body greatly but could be repaired with time. When I was given a chance I seized it! And that's how I proven my theory!
I cured patients of colds, flus, and dangerously high fevers instantly. But those ignorant, blind, closed-minded fools only saw the negitives. So I gave them a broken bone or two. So I dislocated a shoulder. So what.
[Nurse] But Doctor. What about that man you made crippled? If you ask me a simple common cold is better than no more use of my legs.
[Dr. Mayhem] No one asked you. Besides that was the man with a very high fever. He would of died! Well, anyway. I was punished by having my licensed terminated.... forever. But even if I can't prove my theory in the ER. I can prove it in the ring. That's why I joined the HWoAR. There are a lot of unhealthy people which are in desperate need of my theory to help them. They sick in the body and they sick in the head. I shall heal them with my abilites.
I swore an oath to do all that is possible to heal that person and I choose to stick by that oath. And that is what I am going to do. I shall have the mentally ill think straight! I shall have the weak in body be strong! And I shall prove to the medical world that I am right with my theory. That I am the greatest Doctor to of ever graced the Emergency Room! That pain is the only cure for anything. And this lovely lady here is my assistant. The Nurse.
[[A thin lovely woman. A little tall for a woman but very thin and attactive. She seems to be wearing the nurse's hat and the short skirt. She slowly walks to the Doctor as he clasps his hand and puts it on his shoulder.]]
[Nurse] Yes Doctor? What can I do? [Dr. Mayhem] Just be your lovely self right now. This is my assistant who left her profession which she loved so dear to help me prove me right. She knows much about pain as I do and also as much of the human body. She's as dangerous as she is sexy. [[He walks behind the nurse and hugs her]] She can fight with the best of those poor suffering people. God help me save them. Well, she'll help me help them. Anything you would like to say Nurse?
[Nurse] Yes Doctor. [[She turns to the cameraman]] I will be honored to help those suffering people. I have absolute confidence in the doctor's abilities. I believe that he will heal your problems. I believe that he shall make your life a better one at the end.
[Dr. Mayhem] Thank you Nurse. Well that's all we have for today. I'll be expecting our next appointment to be real soon? Great! Nurse will you lead the man out please?
[[The nurse shows the Cameraman out the door and waves goodbye]] [The Nurse] I hope you enjoyed your visit with Doctor Mayhem! [fades to black]
