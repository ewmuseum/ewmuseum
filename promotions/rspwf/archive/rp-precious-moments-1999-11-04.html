<RP> Precious Moments

4 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(We open where we left off, with The Binky Fairy and The Winky Fairy, collectively known as Precious Moments, skipping arm-in-arm through the streets of Detroit. The ink on their contracts with the AWMC is still fresh, and now that the contracts are signed, they have new things to worry about. Their initial in-ring debut... their eventual first match... their first big feud...
and of course...) The Binky Fairy: Where the hell are we? (Binky and Winky stop, look at each other, and then look at the camera. Winky has a look of concentration on his face, as Binky just looks confused)
The Winky Fairy: Let's see. The last place I remember was us in the fields of
Fairyland, frollocking gayly towards the evil of professional wrestling. And now, here we are in this weird, loud, rude, strange, and evil place. Is this professional wrestling?
TBF: No, this is where professional wrestling takes place from. Professional wrestling isn't a place, it's a thing. I hear the humans refer to it as
"sports entertainment" a lot. TWF: So it's a sport? TBF: No, it's not a sport. It's something like a sport. TWF: I'm confused. TBF: Me too. (Binky and Winky look at each other, tears in their eyes. They both sniffle, then lower their heads. All around them, people are walking by, either avoiding them like the plague or laughing their heads off at the tu-tu wearing pair. Finally, Binky and Winky burst into tears, and grab onto each other, into a big hug)
TWF: I'm scared, Bink. TBF: So am I, Wink... so am I. (Just as this highly emotional moment is taking place, the camera zooms out a bit, to reveal that Binky and Winky are standing in front of an electronics store. TVs, VCRs, microwaves, and assorted other items are displayed in the window, the TVs tuned into The Weather Channel. Suddenly, all the TVs change from The Weather Channel to AWMC's Never Say Die, at the exact moment that The
Hatebreeders are talking about Bink and Wink. How convenient! Also, a horizontal bar appears on the bottom of the TVs, with VOLUME on the far left side of it and several vertical bars inbetween the VOLUME and the end of the horizontal bar. More vertical bars appear, as we can more audibly hear the words being said on the TV. Bink and Wink stop hugging after hearing their names, and turn to the store, interested)
Johnny C: A new age is dawning on the AWMC...an age of anarchy and chaos, an age of violence! The age of the Hatebreeders! The tag team scene here is a pitiful cesspool of mediocrity, and we're here to bring a little life to the equation. And we're going to start with those two fairyboys,
Precious Moments. Tell 'em J. [Jackal grabs the mic and looks around at the jeering crowd, and begins to speak in a voice that is suprisingly softspoken and intelligent.]
J: The time has arrived. Chaos. Fear. Pain. Hate. We bring all of these things to your door, AWMC. It's a virtual Pandora's Box. You see, we're not here to entertain anyone, or make you all like us. Frankly, we could care less. And it seems we're the very object of Precious Moments' quest.
We are the "evil" they speak of. You might have some bad guys here in the AWMC, like Dylan Dice, Sinister, Chris del Rio, and the like...but they are lacking something. Call it style, call it panache, call it what you wish. But they're missing something that the Hatebreeders have. Bink and
Wink, you two can go running around and talking about ridding the AWMC of evil all you want...but too-toos aren't much good against a crowbar or broken glass or a good swift kick to the groin. The challenge is there..."boys." We're waiting.
(With that, the televisions click off. Bink and Wink look at each other, both bearing looks of shock on their faces. They look at the camera, then back at each other, then back at the camera, then back to each other)
TBF: What was that? Who was that? And why was that? TWF: You forgot "where was that?" TBF: Oh yeah. Where was that? (Just as Binky asks that question, a stammering drunk passes by both Binky and
Winky, muttering to himself)
Stammering Drunk: The Hatebreeders are evil... they beat up two tag teams... they did it because they could... they did it at Joe Louis Arena... they did it at "Never Say Die", the AWMC's PPV... they challenged Precious Moments... they are evil.
(Suddenly, the stammering drunk stops, looks over at Bink and Wink, turns to the camera, smiles crookedly, then heaves all over the ground, a putrid green liquid vomit pouring out of his mouth like... a putrid green liquid vomit. So
I'm not good at comparing two alike things... SHUT UP ABOUT MY ENGLISH SKILLS!
DON'T MAKE ME... nevermind. You're not worth it. The stammering drunk guy stammers drunkly out of the scene, leaving Bink and Wink to consider what he said)
TBF: The Hatebreeders are evil... TWF: They beat up two tag teams... TBF: They did it because they COULD! TWF: They did it at the Joe Louis Arena! TBF: They did it at "Never Say Die"! TWF: They challenged Precious Moments! (Both stop, look at each other, then look back at the camera, their jaws dropped) Both: THEY CHALLENGED US! (They stop for a moment, confused. They think for several seconds, then turn to each other, their jaws dropping once again)
Both: THEY'RE EVIL!!! (Binky and Winky both get looks of anger on their faces, which is more funny than intimidating, as the sight of the two red-faced, angered, grown men in tu-tus and leotards has caused several passersby to chuckle to themselves)
TBF: We must do something about this, Wink. TWF: Yes, we must. TBF: Well then, it's settled. We will do something about this. Now the question is, what?
(As The Binky Fairy finishes speaking, the TVs come back on, this time with a far different scene. This one is in a restraunt, with The Hatebreeders sitting, waiting for a table. Binky and Winky look as if they are in deep thought, as Bink bites his bottom lip, while Wink scratches the side of his head. They don't even seem to notice that the TVs are back on)
Johnny C: Now, it took us a lot of planning, but we finally figured out how to make you guys *REAL* men.
[Johnny begins to dig around in his trenchcoat and pulls out a video. He proudly displays it to the camera. It reads:
"The Best of German Sh*t Videos: The Scat Years." An old lady at the counter in front of him turns around, then pukes at the cover, which is censored out. Johnny looks at the video, doubletakes, and chunks it across the room, nearly beaning a frat boy upside the head.]
JC (nervously): Now how did *THAT* get there? *ahem* [He begins to root around again, this time producing yet ANOTHER video tape. Go ahead, look, it's nothing bad. See, it's just "The
Best of Happy Days," starring Henry Winkler. If you didn't know that, you should be drug out into the street and beaten with sacks of doorknobs by [name withheld]' army of midgets.]
JC: Happy Days! The greatest television show of all time...well, except for "The A-Team." This video right here is all you need to be cool. Just follow Henry Winkler in the madcap hilarity that was His life as The Fonz. Let His words be your guide...become a disciple of the Fonz. It worked for us.
We'll bring you this tape...a copy of it, actually, because we don't trust you. Take it home...watch it. And then give your life over to Fonzie. Learn to grow in your daily walk with Him. Let
Him be your guide in all that you do. And *MAYBE*, if they aren't sold out, we'll include two of these wrist bracelets:
[Johnny shows his left wrist, which has a purple bracelet with white lettering on it, which reads "W.W.F.D."]
JC: "What Would Fonzie Do?" The bracelet will remind you to act as Fonzie would in a difficult situation. See, my friends over here have similar bracelets. And you notice what they did when faced with a nice ass? They looked at it, and commented on it, just as Fonzie would. Henry would be so proud of them.
All you have to do is watch the video and say this prayer...and this goes for the rest of you out there in TV land..."Dear Heavenly
Fonz, I am not cool. I admit this and recognize this. I come to You and Your infinite cool factor on my knees. Please become a part of my daily life and help me to live my life as You would.
In Henry Winkler's name I pray, EYYYYYYYYY!". (With that, the TVs turn off again) TBF: I've got it, Wink! We must watch Henry Winkler as the Fonz over and over again! TWF: Hey! His name is like mine! Where'd you get that idea from, Bink? TBF: I don't know... it just came to me. But I do know that we have to get the tape from Johnny C, the manager of The Hatebreeders.
TWF: What's a manager? TBF: Got me, but whomever it is, we have to find him. Now where could he be? (And the TVs come back on again, and again it's part of The Hatebreeders prior spot, only this time it's before the last piece of footage we saw took place, at the start of it, when the "W A F F L E H O U S E" sign was shown. PAUSE appears in the upper left hand corners of all the TVs, as Winky starts spinning around in circles, seemingly thinking. Finally he stops, facing the TVs. He spins back around, and looks at Bink)
TWF: We must go to the Wafflehouse. TBF: What? TWF: We must go to the Wafflehouse. (Winky starts walking, seemingly in a trance. Binky stares after him for a second, shrugs, and walks off screen after Wink. The TVs in the back turn off again, then turn back on, this time with light blue dust slowly falling, until it forms three words onscreen. They all read the same thing; To Be Continued.
With that, we fade out)
