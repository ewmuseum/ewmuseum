William Devonshire RP Critique? PLEASE!

19 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey-- If any of you all would be so kind, I was just posting this to get some opinions. This is a fairly new character and I was just wondering what people may think of it. This is a roleplay following a four-way dance for the IWF/WOW World Title...
--------------------------------- [...screen slowly reveals a dusty, humid locker room interior. Rows of old blue lockers, the paint gradually chipping away line the cold, cracking concrete floor.
Wooden benches that have remnants of dark red paint that once covered them scattered over the old boards and rusting bolts. Long neon lights line the caged off ceiling, several flickering due to faulty bulbs. The room is lit poorly, as the camera weaves through linen carts and benches, around lockers and through corridors. Finally, they come upon a monster of a man, hulked over on a bench.
Several men attend to him. The straps of his black and red tights are pulled down, his upper back and chest covered with dried blood, his face behind a mask of wet crimson. One paramedic pulls blood soaked strands of hair from his face and tie them behind his head with her own rubber-band. Another begins wiping away the area of the laceration and sterilizing it. The man's hands quiver at his sides, a trainer next to him looking on edge...]
[...the camera shifts to a cropped view, close on the man's face. His brilliant blue eyes are screaming, his brow furrowed, and saturated with sweat. One bead makes it's way down his high cheek bone and around his upper lip, gradually turning red through it's journey, then plummets from his unshaven chin to the concrete below.
An EMT conveniently shifts the wrestlers head so that his hard, defined face is directed towards the camera's view. The man is a battered and marred, "Dangerous"
William Devonshire. He is visibly exhausted and obviously hurt. A large gash runs from somewhere in his thick brown and red hair, down to his right eyebrow. Another smaller one on his left temple. As a paramedic begins taping white gauze to the smaller cut with care, Devonshire slowly opens his mouth, choking on his words...]
DEVONSHIRE: Shane Sanders...Mickey Charmer...JUSTIN ARCOLA. You three dragged me to hell and beat me senseless...I enjoyed it thoroughly.
~ sickening grin ~ DEVONSHIRE: Justin Arcola, you NARROWLY escaped with your title intact, I will NOT let this happen twice. I learn from experiences, I tear them down in my head, analyze them looking for the mistakes. Then, I keep them with me for when I am in a similar or concurrent situation. This situation will reoccur one week from now. I will have you again...I will destroy you again. The difference being Justin...I will make you QUIT. Seven days from now I will step into that ring with the taste of gold on my lips, and the smell of your blood in my nostrils.
[Devonshire shifts uneasily, a fire in his eyes that is slowly growing. His chest tightens and his hands tremor as he forces out his thoughts...]
DEVONSHIRE: You foolishly unshackle the rules of the ring from my wrists in our meetings Arcola. This is only your first of many mistakes. One equally ignorant is the fact that you felt my wrath and barely escaped it...and you keep returning for another serving.
[Devonshire winces in pain as the resident doctor pierces the first stitch through his open wounds on his forehead. He is flushed with yet another thought which causes him to clench the wooden bench with his right hand, nearly ripping it from the floor...]
DEVONSHIRE: The REASON you escaped a very HAPPY ENDING was because someone stuck their big, unwelcome nose in my business once again. TREVOR STORM, you incompetent bottom-feeder. You simply don't learn, do you? You could burn your arm into disfigurement and you would play with matches the next morning. You're an imbecile, and are barely worth my time. But you have still yet to learn the lesson I have attempted to impress upon you in the past. So...I will do so once more. Then, on my favorite holiday of
Halloween...I will finish you. You have swept all feelings of apathy and mercy that once occupied my heart for you Storm. For this, you will feel regret...not to mention amounts of pain that will remain unparalleled for the rest of your career. That is...assuming when I'm through with you you'll be physically able to CONTINUE your 'career'.
[Devonshire's eyes are ablaze with fury now. He struggles to contain himself...but cannot. He jolts suddenly to his feet, breathing heavily. His gigantic arms tremble, as he glares into the camera...]
DEVONSHIRE: TREVOR STORM! I _WILL_ HAVE YOU! I WILL TAKE YOU INTO THAT RING AND WHEN I AM THROUGH YOU WILL SIMPLY BE A REMNANT OF AN EASILY FORGOTTON WRESTLER!!! [Devonshire screeches, screaming his words while inflicting pain on himself. The suturing needle is hanging by the thread, dangling. As he yells, a trickle of blood falls over his face and down to the floor...]
DEVONSHIRE: JUSTIN ARCOLA! YOUR RULES WILL NOT SAVE YOU! AND TREVOR STORM WILL NOT SAVE YOU! I WILL DESTROY YOU! IT _IS_ THAT SIMPLE!!! [The trainers and paramedics converge on Devonshire and begin attempts at calming him. Eventually his personal trainer convinces him to take his seat once more. A EMT towels the new blood off his face and the doctor returns to his repairs. Devonshire is handed a solid iron rod for each hand to clamp onto. His knuckles instantly turn white, his fingers red. A large white towel is wrapped around his massive shoulders and back, resembling a cloak. He hulks back over, staring at the ground. The camera is escorted from the room by security, unwillingly. A shaky shot shows Devonshire shrinking away until he can be seen no more...then the shot fades to blackness...]
