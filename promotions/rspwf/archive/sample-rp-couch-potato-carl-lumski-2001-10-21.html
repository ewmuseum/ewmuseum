[Sample RP] "Couch Potato" Carl Lumski

21 October 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens with a shot of a television set. On the television, we see a scene from the 1989-93 NBC Saturday morning situation comedy series "Saved
By The Bell". We see Bayside High School's resident goofball Screech Powers
(played by world renowned TV icon Dustin Diamond) talking to Kevin, his faithful robot companion. A Carl Lumski voice-over is soon heard...]
CL: Screech Powers. When you hear that name, you immediately think all sorts of ill thoughts. The words "nerd", "pinhead", "doofus", and "fool"
spring to mind. You're reminded of all of his pratfalls... his eccentric clothes... his wiry hair... and his shrill voice. But, when you get down to the nitty-gritty, that's not what Screech was all about. His nerdiness often distracted everyone from seeing the REAL Screech. You see, much like the owls on "Twin Peaks", Samuel "Screech" Powers was not what he seemed to be.
[The camera zooms out and turns around and we see a familiar sight. We see the "Couch Potato" himself, lounging in his easy chair and watching the aforementioned TV, all while enjoying a can of Mountain Dew. Lumski is dressed in a white Homey D. Clown t-shirt, a pair of gray, stained sweatpants, and white socks. He takes one last swig of his beverage and tosses the can to the floor. Then, he shakes his head, addressing the camera...]
Screech wasn't a naive geek. He was a clever young man who yearned to be a leader. He wanted nothing more than to lead his Bayside buddies in their various misadventures... and he would've done just that, if it wasn't for that glory hog Zack Morris.
[Carl scowls angrily...] The Zack Attack... oh, how I loathed him! He held Screech back on so many different occasions. I think he did it because he was jealous. Screech was bright and he was a ladies' man, too! Hell, he even got a piece of Kelly
Kapowski before Zack! Of course, this angered Zack. That annoying little dickweed didn't like the attention that his friend was getting... so he kept on creating situations where Screech looked like a fool!
[He sighs, continuing...] Zack continually made Screech look like his brain-dead lackey when, in reality, he was much smarter and much more likable than he could ever be.
He did all of this in an attempt to make himself look better than Screech. Sadly, a lot of viewers fell for this... but I sure as hell didn't. I knew deep down that Zack was holding Screech back. The fact that I couldn't do anything about it saddened me.
[Carl points to himself...] Now, here I am... in Screech's place... with Eric Travers assuming the role of Zack. Travers believes that the world of wrestling revolves around him and that he has the ability to demean anyone he chooses. He thinks he's the bee's knees... when, in all honesty, he's more like the monkey's testicles.
What's even more pathetic is the fact that he thinks he has the right to put me down and make me feel like garbage. That egotistical dumbass thinks he has the right to call me all sorts of names and question my leadership skills. That... that... that JACKASS has no idea what the hell he's talking about!
[He rolls his eyes...] Take his latest diatribe, for instance. It had so many factual errors; it looked like a goddamn "Dateline" segment. It seems like Travers has no problem making slanderous comments as long as they stroke his own ego. He doesn't care how wrong they might be. His disparaging remarks about my work ethic prove this.
[Carl points to himself again, shaking his head...] Hey, I'm not going to lie to any of you out there. I'm a TV fan. More specifically, I'm a HUGE fan of "General Hospital". I've been watching the show since the age of six. I tune in every day to see what's going on with
Luke, Laura, Liz, Lucky, Gia, and the rest of Port Charles. But that doesn't mean that I'm not as prepared as Travers or anyone else participating in Wrestlebowl.
[He tilts his head sideways, arching an eyebrow...] You think I haven't watched videotapes of every single member of the MBC/UWF team? You think I haven't gone over all over their strengths and weaknesses? Travers, do you really think that I'm DUMB ENOUGH to sit on my ass and do nothing in preparation for the biggest match of my career?
[Carl holds one of his hands up, as if to say, "Stop!"] In the immortal words of Mr. Belding, "Hey hey hey hey hey! What is going on here?!" [He points to himself, nodding his head...] I've done my homework, Travers. I've scouted every one of our opponents. I'm MORE than ready... and I'm MORE than qualified to be the leader of our team at Wrestlebowl.
[Carl grins slightly, shaking his head...] You don't intimidate me, Travers... not in the least. Your bully routine may scare other guys... but it falls flat on me. When I look at you, I don't see someone that I should be afraid of. I see a cranky, rundown has-been whose ramblings make less sense than a rerun of "Star Trek:
Voyager". [He folds his arms across his chest and nods his head confidently...] I'm going to make you eat your words, Travers. I'm going to make you wish that you never doubted my in-ring aptitude. Rock... Maverick... Thranton...
the same goes for all of you, too! I'm sick and tired of you guys treating me like some kind of joke. At Wrestlebowl, the laughter stops. Get ready to be pleasantly surprised... and incredibly shocked. Consider yourselves
CANCELED.
