Simon Pretswell sample roleplay.. Any good feds out there?

29 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SPRESTWELL wrote in message < [detail withheld] >... > Scene > <Simon Pretswell is in his locker room. He is looking at a slip of paper. It >is the list of superstars who will compete in the Extreme Championship Title. >He quickly skims down the list and then spies his name. He smiles at this and >looks at the first name on the list. It is Diamond Dallas Page. Then he starts >to talk as the camera fades in...> Cut...cut...snip...snip... You need more pauses. The way you wrote this RP your character would be talking like Jim Cornette...on...and on...and on without taking a breath. Your character does nothing but talk? Maybe he should have read the paper...crumbled it up. Make your character do something action wise to break up the LONG run on rants. Here is a excellent example. Really you can't get any better than the Toastman. <We see a stove and a kitchen counter-top with all sorts of ingredients: bowls of pancake batter, measuring cups, bowls of fresh fruit, slices of bread, butter, cartons of milk, eggs. It looks like somebody is getting ready to whip up a wholesome, nutritious, breakfast. And who else is better for that job than Breakfast Bobby Toast !> It's time... It's Time... IT'S BREAKFAST TIME !!!! Hi all you, you,... youngsters and NPWA fans. It's Breakfast Bobby Toast and by, by golly, I'm just a jittery bundle of nerves. I've been drinking nothing but... but... coffee since The Traction Zone ! Gosh oh jeepers ! I'm... I'm... a nervous wreck ! That Senator walked out and cost me the world title. That no good Fair Warning jumped me from behind. And this Saturday I'll be tagging with Steve Sampson to go up against Warning and Brian Masters. GOLLY ! I don't know what's happened to Steve these last few months. I don't know where his head is at, but, but,... I'LL BE READY BY GOSH !!! <Toast picks up a pair of donuts, holds them up to his eyes, and looks through them> Oh Warning... Oh Fair Warning.... I can see you... Can you see me ? <Toast stuffs the donuts into his mouth and starts chewing> Oh ya !!! <Munch munch> It's going to be a fine time for Breakfast Time. And kids, at In Your Mall, stay the heck away from that food court. Gee whiz, they've got nothing but bad, bbbaddddd, things to eat. It's all bad junk food. It's not as healthy as these all-natural eggs !!! <Toast takes a pair of eggs and cracks them open into a frying pan> Did you see that ?!? Did you see that Fair Warning and Brian Masters ? That's what may happen to you. I don't know what greasy spoon breakfast horrors Steve Sampson will serve up, but I'm going to scramble ! I'm going to fry ! I'm going to sizzle ! And it's going to be over hard for you ! <Toast starts to whip up a bowl of pancake batter> I'm whipping, whipping, whipping. Do you like batter, Fair Warning ? I can batter too ! I'm battering right now ! Jeepers, take a close look ! Because you just might get a bit battered yourself at Saturday Night Slaughter ! <Toast pours the batter into a frying pan> See that ! You might end up as flat as this pancake, and just as light and fluffy after you get out of Bobby Toast's Breakfast Kitchen ! <Toast moves on to some oranges which he cuts in half. He starts grinding them into a juicer> Gotta.... make.... sure.... it's... it's.... FRESHLY SQUEEZED !!!! This might be you Fair Warning ! Look at at the pulp and the juice from this orange ! It's quite healthy, but at In Your Mall, you're going to wish for concentrate instead of freshly squeezed. BUT THE PANCAKES ARE READY, FANS !!!! <Bobby takes a spatula and lifts up the pancake and puts it on a plate. He pours some maple syrup on it> Mmmm.... fine Canadian maple syrup. BUT NONE FOR YOU FAIR WARNING !! You want some ? You want some of what Bobby Toast's got ? Then you better come get some, before it gets cold ! At In Your Mall get ready of the Wake-Up Call of your life, because Superstar Steve Sampson and I aren't leaving any leftovers ! I'm Breakfast Bobby Toast and it's Breakfast Time in the NPWA ! More coffee.... somebody get me some more... more... coffee... Must... stay.... alert....
