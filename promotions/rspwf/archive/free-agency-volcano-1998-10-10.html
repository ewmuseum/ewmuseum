Free Agency! (VOLCANO)

10 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm a free agent since I retired from wrestling in my own fed after the talent level grew. My record in all the E-feds I've competed in is 11-2, you say not a whole lot of experience? Your right I don't have a lot of experience, but I'll will be the first to tell you that I'm no mid-carder,
I've placed 4th after losing the consenlation in the HIWF's TOC this summer.
I lost to "Da BOMB" Tim Haught, and a guy by the name of Derek Tuttle, one of the greatest guys you'll ever meet. I'm looking for a booked fed, using roleplay boards, rather than a simmed fed or an E-mail fed. I'll now provide you with one of my shorter roleplays:
: <i><b>Barry Windsor's face appears on the big blue screen, as he appears at the top of the ramp. The fans are at first speechless, then yell out so loud that it sounds as though an airplane is taking off. The fans wave their arms as he struts down the ramp towards the ring. He has a mic in his back pocket. He is wearing blue trunks, and a red shirt that reads The
Elite on it. He slides under the bottom rope as he has for so many years.
He walks to the middle of the ring and pulls out the mic. He waits for about 1 minute before the fans let him talk. He raises the mic to his perspiring lips and begins to speak.</i></b>
: <b><u>Barry Windsor:</u></b>I think I'll fit in nicely here! I didn't know if you guys would be as good as my old fans. I'm here for a couple of reasons. Reason number one is that I couldn't stand retirement, I had to get back in the ring once again. Reason number two, I came to the WWPW because I heard about these Nation guys being so dominating and all, and I figured that I could show them what dominating was all about. That is why I have united with the greatest wrestlers in the world, to form the Elite. We are determined to come out hear and show you guys what is up.
: <b><i>The fans cheer with excitement, and the ring announcers listen intently as the vetern continues to speak.</b></i>
: <b><u>Barry Windsor:</u></b>I once watched a man fight here. A man by the name of Volcano, he had what he called fire. A fire that I thought no man except myself knew about. A fire that I have, and unlike Volcano, I have harnessed that fire, and can and will use it to my aid. Do you know what brings about that fire?
: <b><i>The fans shake their heads to indicate that they don't.</i></b>
: <b><u>Barry Windsor:</u></b> I'll tell you then, it is that sheer act of
Competition. It is amazing that the same thing that made america made me,
Barry Windsor. Competition, is the act of rivalry or competiting; a trial of skill or ablitity, a contest between teams and individuals. Competition not only against others but to myself, to succeed, to learn, to go to the outer most limits to be the best that I can be. Most people compete to see who is the fastest, or strongest, but I compete to see who has the most hearts, the most guts.
: <b><i>The fans rise to their feet an applaud the hero who has returned, out of retirement. They all rise and clap, and yell, and whoop their cries of joy.</b></i>
: <b><u>Barry Windsor:</b></u>So Nation members beware, Barry Windsor and the rest of the Elite are out to get you. I think that my first match in the WWPW should be against a good wrestler, although not as good as some of the other wrestlers in the Nation. So Hannibal I challenge you to a regular old match, except no stable interference. If you agree then sign the match and lets go at it on Monday. Together I have united with these guys to fight a common cause. The Nation is that cause, those who said yall were unstoppable will now be proven wrong. You should hear from other members of the Elite later in the night.
: <b><i>Barry Windsor throws his mic into the crowd, as the fans rush to get it he slowly walks to the edge of the ring and slides inder the bottom rope.
He jogs up the ramp, and slaps high fives as he goes. He jogs back into the
WWPW locker room, as the fans go crazy!</b></i>
This is when I was still Barry Windsor in the WWPW, I have since changed my name to Volcano!
regards, RYAN S.
