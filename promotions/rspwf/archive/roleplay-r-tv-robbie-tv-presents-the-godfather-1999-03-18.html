--ROLEPLAY-- [R-TV] Robbie-TV presents "The Godfather"

18 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up in a dimly lit office. There's a large desk with flowers, a bottle of Sidewinder69tm, a half full glass of the potent drink next to it, and a man in a black suit and red tie standing behind the desk. We zoom in on the man, who's hair is also slicked back. As he glances at the camera, we recognize him as Chavez, the insane, drunken
Indian. He gazes morbidly at the camera as the familiar violins and piano play the theme to "The Godfather." Don Chavez looks slightly surprised as somebody walks into the office and prostrates himself on the floor in front of him. We recognize him as Al Coholic, owner of
Meenan's Wine and Spirits and co-owner of Robbie-TV. Don Chavez maintains his cool, icy demeanor as Al grovels on his knees]
Al: Oh Godfather, I come to you on my knees asking for a favor... [The music fades into the background so we can hear the dialogue. Don Chavez looks non-plussed and gazes coolly at Al. . The Don then speaks in a gruff, hushed voice]
Don Chavez: You come to me...with a favor? As I recall, you refused me that last drink, when you claimed I had too much. And now, you come to me with a favor?
[Al Coholic starts to sob as he prostrates himself further] Al: Oh Don Chavez. All I ask is for justice...You must grant me this favor... [Chavez casually brushes a hand through this slicked back hair as he paces back and fourth in front of Al]
Don Chavez: Very well. I'll grant you this favor. But remember, one day, and that day may never come, I might ask *you* for a favor...
Al: Thank you, Godfather... [Don Chavez profers his hand to Al to kiss it as is the custom but then wonders where Al's mouth has been recently, and quickly withdraws his hand]
Al: As we both know, there's a mole in our midsts. We *know* him to be
Harry Sacz. This mole, he needs to be taught a lesson. And I come before you on my knees, and ask that justice prevails...
[Don Chavez, not changing his expression, pours himself a glass of Sidewinder69tm from the bottle on his desk, and paces back and fourth, unnerving Al, who is still prostrated on the floor]
Don Chavez: Very well. Consider it done. Now, remember that favor you were going to grant me?
Al: But...But Don Chavez: Hey, I said that day may never come. But oops too bad, it did. Now listen. I've got a lot of time on my hands until I get my hands on Shawn Page and *my* Heavyweight title. Until then, it would be nice to have some gold.
So me and my padre Robbie have decided to take those tag team titles from La Cossa Nostra. But we need the shot. You need to go to the ARMAGEDDON front office and get them to give me and Robbie a shot at those tag team titles. Before we arrange the hit, the paper work has to be taken care of.
Can you handle that, Al? You don't want to let me down, Al... [Al Coholic looks up at Don Chavez with a worried expression] Al: But, how, what do I say? Don Chavez: Make them an offer they can't refuse... Al: I'll do that, Godfather. I'll do that. [Al crawls out of Don Chavez' office on his hands and knees, grovelling the whole time. Don Chavez then diverts his attention to the waiting camera, once again, with a steely, penetrating gaze]
Don Chavez: La Cossa Nostra, that Russian shmuck, your little pencil neck geek manager, you better hope and pray Al get's lost on the way to the front office and we *don't* get the shot.
[Scene fades out from the dimly lit office to another room. This is somebody's bedroom and it's at some ungodly hour in the morning. As the camera pans around the room, we recognize that it's Harry Sac'z bedroom, because of all the Sanction posters everywhere. Sure enough, there's
Sacz, half buried underneath a pile of blankets. He apparently wakes up from an uneasy sleep, gasping for air. He looks over to his left and see's a familiar looking shape. His eyes widen with terror. He reluctantly pulls back the sheet...It's a dismembered horses head! Actually, it's just the top part of horseman's costume but nonethless, Sacz eyes widen with terror as he turns to the camera]
Sacz: AAHHHHHHH YOU BASTAAAAAARDS!!!!
