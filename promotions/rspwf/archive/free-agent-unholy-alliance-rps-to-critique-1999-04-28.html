(FREE AGENT)-Unholy Alliance(+ RPs to critique)

28 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there. with the recent closing of HOW,I find I have five free agents at the moment.They are The Annihilator,'The Archangel'Mark Gabriel,Fire,The
Zealot,and Demonspawn.They are collectively known as the Unholy Alliance.Zealot and Demonspawn are known as the Unholy Alliance.Below is a sample RP from two of these guys,Annihilator and Fire.Please Critique them please,and if anyone out there wants to make me an offer,please do.
STATS Annihilator:6'9",289 lbs. - longest holder of HOW GP Belt(gimmick match title) Fire:6'8",281 lbs. - Former #1 Contender for SFW TV Title Zealot:5'11",198 lbs. - Several CW Title shots in HOW Demonspawn:6'4",480 lbs. - Undefeated in singles competition 'The Archangel'Mark Gabriel:6'6",263 lbs. - No Experience to date Sample RP's Annihilator (The scene is a gloomy one.It is a path in a dark wood.Mist swirls around randomly in the chill breeze.The Annihilator is seen walking along the path towards the camera.He gives of a feeling of depression and lowness.He stops as he approaches the waiting camera.)
Annihilator:I speak with you tonight,with a heavy heart.I have come to a crossroads in my career.With the breakdown of the Metal Militia,and my friends leaving to pursue their respective careers,I find myself unable to concentrate.I was caught cold in a match which could have got me a shot at the World title.Everyone knows that the last time the
Annihilator got caught cold was when I was a rookie,when Das Traumer defeated me in my debut.I promised then I would never again be caught cold like that again.But it happened again.I failed.
Lobo,you may be thinking that after my showing in the World Title Tourney,you have just got to show up to win.I have heard you call yourself Hardcore on occasion.Let me tell you something.When you hold the High Octane Wrestling Grand Prix Title for as long as I did,you are
Hardcore.I have been hit with leather straps,thrown onto tables,hit with razorbats.But by God,I still got up.Even though I am in a depressed state of mind,I have enough to defeat you and send you back from where you crawled from.
(The Annihilator sighs and looks to the ground) I used to be a Badass Title Contender,but now I feel like I don't deserve a shot at what I made MY title,the Grand Prix belt.But now longer.I will become the Annihilator of old again,and I know of only one way to do it.May God have mercy on my soul....
(The Annihilator then solemnly continues walking up the path,past the camera.The camera turns with him,and as the screen fades to black,a church,surrounded by darkness and swirling mist,can be seen in the distance.)
Fire The camera turns on,but the scene is pitch black.A voice speaks up from the darkness, "Welcome".A small lick of flame appears in the floor a few feet in front of the camera.A man's black boots cna be seen form the new light level in the room.
Voice: Welcome all in the SFW.This is your soon-to-be World Champion here.I have come to tell you the truth about Super Fire Wrestling,and how I will rule it.
The flame increases in strength and a little more light is available.The man's red tights can be seen,along with a yellow and orange flame motif on the side of his tights.
Voice: I have seen the roster in Super Fire Wrestling.There are men I know well,friends,enemies.There are unknowns as well.Men I have never had the chance to put the Fireball on.But that time will come soon enough.I am here for one reason:GOLD.Anyone who gets in my way,whether they be friend or foe,will feel the burn of the Fireball......
The flame shoots up in the air revealing Fire as the man behind the voice.he stands confidently in the centre of a training ring,draped in red and orange.
...For I am Fire,and like the element,I am at my most dangerous when I am angry,or have been crossed.And soon the first two will understand what it means to meet the Fireball.Flame,nobody likes a rip-off.Especially such a corny one as yourself.As far as I am concerned.I am copyrighted,and all copies must be eliminated.So here is what I propose.You take your cheesy,no-good,copycat ass,and get in the ring with me in a No-DQ,No Countout match.All must learn that I am the original,real deal,and not some wannabe like yourself.
Now,on to Cerberus.If that is what you call yourself now,Berus.I never got my shot at you in High Octane Wrestling.But god knows I deserved one.So let me prove to the world that my confidence is not misplaced,Chris Berus,Cerberus,Hell's Watchdog,whatever you want your ass to be called.Get in that ring,and give me the chance to beat your ass down.I am gonna mess you up so bad,they will be calling you John
Doe.Prepare to feel the burn.... The flames gradually die down until they have completely gone out,returning the room to pitch darkness,as the camera fades out.
