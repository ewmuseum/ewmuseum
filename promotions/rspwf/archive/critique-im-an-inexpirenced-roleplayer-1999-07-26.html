(One by Metallica plays as the fans erupt with cheers. HW makes his way out from behind the curtain. He has a huge smile on his face as he makes his way down to isle. He rolls in to the ring and the mic is tossed to him.)
HW: Well It looks like MNM had it's upsides and downsides. Well I lost the rumble (The fans boo in disapointment) I know, I know. But At least
I Kicked the livin ###### out of the 2 biggest losers there is, there ever was, and there ever will be. Of Couse I'm Talkin about
Ninja and diesel. Now I wanna talk about this wednesday. I'm in on that
4-way IC match and well there's noone that can't beat me here. I'm training for this match as well just so I can whoop you pussies allover the ring. Now Pennypacker I saw what you did to TTB at the rumble, and let's just say I've known him for awhile and well he doesn't like getting screwed like that. And Natural, you think your gonna get away with that? After Wednesday, Your gonna get yours buddy.
So tell me fans who's gonna be your next Intercontinetal champion? (The fans scream back "YOU HW" The Homewrecker smiles looks at his black boots) That's what I thought.
(One by Metallica blast as HW makes his way out of the ring, Not noticing he's still has the mic in his hand. He walks up to a cute blond girl and says "I've got 2 words for ya..." And the crowd responds "SUCK IT" HW looks shocked as he notices he still has the mic in hand, the girl blushes as The Homewrecker drops the mic and leaves with the girl as the crowd cheers him on.)
