(CRITIQUE) Mickey Charmer

21 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey all, usually confident enough in my work so I don't need to do this. But
I'm embarking on changing Charmer's gimmick back to the purist gimmick he's used in most of his e-w career. And want to know if y'all think this sets up the switch well.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------- {Fade in backstage at the Mayhem Arena during the Nov 14th edition of Mayhem. After having lost his grudge tag match to start off the show, Mickey
Charmer stands tensely rooted to the floor watching the rest of the card with his brother Mervyn Davis on a monitor in one of the utility corridors.
A close look at the screen shows the closing moments, of the Maverick/Damian
Malcolm North American Title cage match. From the bright red tone of
Charmer's face, it's obvious the match is not going his way. As The Prophet and Robert D'Artois pull Damian Malcolm out of the cage, wrestling's true ladies man lets it all out.}
Charmer: AAAAAAAAHHHHH NO!! MURDER!!! MURDER I SAY!!! BLOODY MURDER!!! Merv: Oh come on, it can't be that bad. Charmer: STUFF IT, BRO. I KNOW A PLATITUDE WHEN I HEAR IT. IT CAN'T BE THAT BAD, MY FAT ASS!! Merv: Indoor voice, Bro. Indoor voice. {Charmer turns his back to his brother and begins pushing his hands down at the floor to try and control his anger. To limited success as he is still screaming.}
Charmer: Screw my indoor voice!! First that damn sound tech loses my Footloose soundtrack, forcing me to fall back on that overwrought Journey intro!! Then Cooper Concrete and Shane Sanders decided to get their sh*ts and giggles by robbing Bilal and me blinder than a couple mice in a children's song! And now THIS!! This perversion of justice!! GGGGAAAAAAAADD!!
Merv: I hear ya, Bro. Prophet and D'Artois did do a pretty sweet job of ripping off poor Maverick.
Charmer: That's not even the half of it, Bro. Maverick shouldn't even have
HAD that belt tonight. I had a crack at that no-talent shlub on Rage about a month ago. But I had my head so far up my ass I let him manuever me into a double countout. And now my chance at a rematch is gone with the wind.
Merv: No wonder you're pissed, Bro. Charmer: Damn straight I'm pissed,Merv. I think the facts I have a right to be are pretty much conclusive. Concrete, Sanders, Prophet, D'Artois,
Malcolm. I could stand here and throw out challenges to each and every one of you. And Lord knows I would love to get my pound of flesh from each and every of you. But Mitchell James Davis is no dummy.
Merv: Even if you did kinda resemble Charlie McCarthy as a kid. Charmer: Bro, did you learn NOTHING growing up about making me hurt you? {Merv holds his hands up directly across from his pecs and begins inching away.} Charmer: Point is, boys, they may call Concrete "The Man with the Plan". But next time you see this face, I'm going to have started fleshing out MY plan.
And when I have my plan in place, it will make Concrete look as intellectually inept as your favorite Hollywood eco-freak. Concrete.
Sanders. Prophet. D'Artois. Malcolm. The perversions of justice you commited tonight can not stand and will not stand. End of subject.
{Fade out as Charmer stamps away from the camera.} [name withheld] Handler of Mickey Charmer (IWF/WOW) The Dime Notes (Free Agents) and Bonnie Bender (JIWWA) Because life's too short to watch crap. - Adam West, promo for TV Land
