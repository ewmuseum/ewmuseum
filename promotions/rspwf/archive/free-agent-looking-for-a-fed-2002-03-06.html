Free Agent looking for a fed

6 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there. I'm the handler of Johnny Ziro, and I'm looking for a fed to join. Here's some info on Johnny and a sample rp. Height: 7'0" Weight: 320 lbs. From: Nashville, TN Race: Caucasion Hair: Black (Long) Attire: Black Jeans, White T-shirt (Sleeves Torn Off), Black Boots, Black
Finger Gloves Facial Hair: Black Gotee Body: Muscular Finisher: Powerbomb Affiliation: Face Theme Music: Hard Workin' Man by Brooks & Dunn For this sample roleplay, I'm pretending that Johnny Ziro has a match against
Stone Cold Steve Austin on an upcoming episode WWF Raw.
(The scene opens. Johnny Ziro is leaning against the front of his Mack truck, with his arms crossed. He's wearing Blue jeans, brown boots, a white T-shirt, a red and black flannel button-up shirt over it, sunglasses, and a black baseball cap that says "Mack" on the front in silver letters. The sleeves on the flannel shirt are rolled up to just above his elbows. Johnny is looking directly into the camera.)
Stone Cold Steve Austin, this Monday on Raw it's me and you. I know you think you're the toughest SOB in the world, but I'm pretty tough myself. Now I'm not gonna stand here and say how I'm gonna run you down. I know you're gonna give me a good fight, but you're not gonna run me down either. I'm anything but a pushover. Now you can run around and flip people off, cuss me out, and drink your little beers, but you're not gonna intimidate me.
(Johnny then stands up straight.) I don't care how many times you've held the championship, or how many matches you've been in. This Monday night, I'm gonna give you a fight the likes of which you've never seen. And after all's said and done, you're gonna be laying in the middle of that ring at my feet...a beaten man.
(Johnny starts to walk around the front, and to the driver's side of his truck. The camera follows. He then props his right foot on the bottom step. ) Stone Cold, you've done a lot in the WWF, and I've got a lot of respect for you. But when we step into that ring, respect goes out the window. When I climb into a wrestling ring, I climb in to win. For me, it's all business in that ring. I don't care if you're my best friend or my worst enemy.
(Johnny reaches up and opens the driver's side door, then climbs into the cab and shuts the door. Johnny looks down at the camera from his seat.)
This Monday Austin, me and you will go into that ring alive and well, but when you leave, you'll be alive, but you won't be well. I know you'll give me a good fight, but it won't be good enough. I'll see ya Monday, Steve.
(Johnny starts the truck, puts it into gear, and drives off. The scene fades to black.) Also, any if any feds are looking for a tag team, and allow handlers to have more than one wrestler, I also handle Matt Ziro, Johnny's little brother.
Together they make the tag team Thunder and Lightning. Here's Matt Ziro's info. Height: 6'0" Weight: 220 lbs. From: Nashville, TN Race: Caucasion Hair: Black (Long) Attire: Black Pants, White Tank Top, Black Boots, Black Finger Gloves Facial Hair: Black Beard Body: Average Finisher: Shooting Star Press Affiliation: Face Theme Music: Renegade by Tim McGraw
