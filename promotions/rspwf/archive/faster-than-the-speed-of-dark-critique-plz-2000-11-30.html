On to the roleplay, titled: "faster than the speed of dark..." ? " ..... Even on the clearest of days and in the brightest of lights -- darkness reigns supreme...."
[The already familiar raspy, sepulchral voice of the one called Cross echoes over darkness..]
[Slience.] [Fade Up.] [A deep orange-yellow-reddish beam of the evening sunlight does its best to penetrate the cracked, aged window of the now abandoned chapel that Cross calls home. The Beam hits the chest of an onlooking Cross. Hair disheveled and clothes unkempt, Cross looks on with deep solemnity & urgency..]
?cross?(flat): "Kash Money thinks this is a game..." ".. he thinks this match is a game." ".. he thinks the darkness I represent is a game." (icy) ".. he thinks that I'm playing." [An interrogative tone of voice escapes his lips.] ?cross? "maybe.. he thinks... too much." (dry) ".. or maybe he's not thinking at all." [A look of matter-of-factness appears on his face, as if what he's about to say is already obvious to the world, and is of general knowledge...]
?cross? "... but it's painfully self-evident that he's not thinking at all, else he'd be thinking about the last match of his all but significant career and perhaps his life."
[Cross' voice grows lugibrous ] ?cross? "The truth about darkness... you want the real truth?" "The REAL truth about darkness is it's worse that you'll ever know; darkness never goes away, it only..gets..bigger; darkness is all encompassing;
darkness is all around you, and there's no way to get away from it; The truth about darkness is it's a double edged sword..... The truth about darkness is it is mine to control -- and that my friend is no joke.."
[Silence.] ?cross? "Kash, your ignorance is obvious.." "What I embody is what scary movies are based on... I AM darkness.. I AM pure-walking evil. I AM the bringer of nightmares, keeper of shadows.
I............AM......... what . you . fear..!!!!!" "Whether you like it or to, you WILL be Crucified for your ignorance..." [.... after a long pause, the rhythmic hiss of nostrils flaring is heard.. his venomous words ensue..] "I will not be consternated nor intimidated by a fabulist who romanticizes and exaggerates his talents. A man that believe he knows what evil is because he wears face paint; A man that beleives he knows about the true dark carnEVIL; A man that actually believes that pure walking evil is to be taken lightly. Go ahead, take me lightly -- it only enrages me further, causing the imminent to occur earlier.."
"...I mean not to boast, I only speak the truth........ And the truth is you will bear your cross this upcoming sunday."
[Cross slowly turns around and looks into the camera, accenting his tattoo of a red upside-down cross under left eye..]
?cross? "Like a good friend of mine used to say..." "Sunday Blastoff; Extreme Title. Match; the concept is simple. Two men enter, but only one man walks out. The way I see it is that you, dear old
Mr. Money won't be the man that will be walking out. Instead, you'll be helped out of the ring with the help of the EMT's."
"Kash Money.. Are you afraid of the dark?" ".....You'd be insane to to..." [A crooked, demoniacal grin escapes his lips, as the the screen fades to black. Cross slowly resumes his place, looking out the window, at the slowly setting sun..]
