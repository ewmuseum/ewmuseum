(Official New York X-Press) with partner Kameron Gauge. They feuded with Style over Substance for some time for the UKCW tag titles. He also appeared in GWA as part of the Northern Invasion. Biography: Bivins originally set out to use wrestling. He startyed his career as a face, known as "True MC" Marc Bivins. He was using wrestling to restart his fallen rap career. He came to see that his calling was wrestling, and he got more of a darker edge, turning heel on the fans and becoming "Mastermind". He doesn't care who he steps on to get what he wants. Appearance: Marc is an african-american male. He is bald and he has no facial hair. He has two hoop earings in his left ear. He has a tatoo of a microphone on his right arm.
