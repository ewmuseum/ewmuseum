[] *Legacy* Shawn Johnson ^The Legend Continues^ Every ten thousand Millennia, a species intelligence jumps, many call it evolution, others call it de-evo- lution. The Homo-Sapiens had their turn to evolve this Millennium. There is now a new breed of the human race. The prototype has been made. Faster, smarter, quicker than all the others. Enter Shawn
Johnson. [Intro] Before there was Nate Walker....Before there was Scorpion, before there was Trevi Hackett, there was one man who dominated the force that has now turned into one of the most important forms of Sports Entertainment as we know it. There was a man who carried this sport on his back from the beginning of his career. The most enigmatic man to ever rise above the others in such a way, they made him a cult ICON...And on this say of July 29th, 1999......The Quest for Supremacy begins yet again.....
[Scene] Fans across the arena are silent....They are awaiting the return of the most controversial man to ever mark his grace to the 'squared circle'.
Suddenly, with a bang, the lights go out in the arena. Within a split second, the lights flicker back and forth.....EXPLOSION! White fireworks blast out of the ICONTron as Shawn Johnson's official entrance theme, "Portrait of a Legacy"
by X HBlock X, begins to kick into effect. Shawn slowly walks out from the back..He has his long blonde hair tied back in a pony tail, he adjusts his sun glasses as he walks to the ring. Shawn is sporting the new UWF shirt, sponsored by him. On the front is a picture of Shawn Johnson sitting down in a chair, looking at the ground. On the back, it reads 'The Legend Continues"...Shawn makes his way to the corner post of the ring as he grabs a mic...Before he can speak a word, his music cuts off and the sound of a woman "climaxing" can be heard. Then, from the back comes a tall, blond woman, resembling the late
Carolyn Basette-Kennedy. The men in the arena hoot and holler as Johnson stares at her from across the arena. She steps into the ring as Shawn now begins to speak.......
*Legacy* Shawn Johnson: [....the fans across the ring let out a pop that can only be equaled to that of the late Hulk Hogan....Johnson lowers the mic and climbs the turnbuckle and throws up the sign of the Canadian Coalition, the group that always refused to lose....The fans die down as Shawn lifts the mic to his lips again...] Damn......it feels good to be back...[...the fans pop huge once again...] And to all my loyal fans out there who stood by me in my absence......[..Johnson pauses for a few seconds as he speaks again...] Can go to hell!![..the fans across the arena grow silent in disbelief at what Johnson has just uttered....] That's right, all of you pieces of CRAP out there never stood by me. Spineless, gutless cowards who listen to every word that comes from their bosses mouths. I chose the way of the
Warrior, I showed to the whole world that Shawn Johnson doesn't give a DAMN about authority. As far as me fighting Brian Marshall goes, I think that I showed the whole friggin' world what I felt about him.

Neandrathals. It came across as if it was machines, all talking about something that was irrelevant, and I for one know that the ratings dropped as soon as you came on..

And if anything is going to get blown, it's me...Tonight.,....[Jaime gives a little smile to Shawn, as he winks back..] Something like this would never happen in Canada....You can bet your bottom dollar that Shawn Johnson ain't gonna put his ass on the line in this match, because after I pull a Roy
