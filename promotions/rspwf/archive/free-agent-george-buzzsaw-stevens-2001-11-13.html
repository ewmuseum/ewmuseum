[FREE AGENT] George "Buzzsaw" Stevens

Howdy ho. I've finally decided to dedicate some time to my favorite character of all, George "Buzzsaw" Stevens. I'm in EMWC with Keller and UWF with Nicky D, and know I can pick up a maximum... maximum of two more feds. One would be preferable.
So here's the app, with a sample roleplay on the bottom. Write me at
[detail withheld] if you're interested. Figured I'd go public with this, rather than just writing to a bunch of fedheads.
Groovy. Texas Panhandle [8] Heel or Face (no neutrals or tweeners): Heel (if I have to go with one, I say heel. But he can easily be a face. Easily.)
[9] Any trademark weapons/foreign objects? (optional): The spurs he wears on his cowboy boots, a bullrope on special occassions
[10] Manager/Valet with description (optional): ** Appearance Info ** [1] Describe your wrestler's physical appearance (i.e., race, hair color, scars, facial hair, physical built, etc.): Skin tone - Caucasian. Needs time in the sun, frankly. Head - Stevens has short, cropped light brown hair. His eyes are a soft, somewhat glazed, blue. He has a fu-manchu mustache and very defined facial features.
Torso - Bulky. Not much muscle to be seen, but he's not fat either. A couple scars on his arms and stomach. Legs - Extremely thick and strong. [2] Describe your wrestler's ring attire: Black leather vest, blue jeans, black cowboy boots with spurs, and a baseball cap (with various teams or messages on it, depending on the week. Usually throw in insider references for e-w marks to spooge over on those hats).
[3] Does your wrestler wear or carry anything that he leaves at ringside when wrestling? The spurs on his cowboy boots, his leather vest. Sometimes that damned bullrope. RA: ... GEORGE "BUZZSAW" STEVENS!!! [The man rolls his head and stretches his arms as he walks to the ring with a cool, almost lackadaisical swagger. He sighs a bit as he reaches ringside, his baseball cap reading "Marty Lunde's Bar-B-Que"
clearly visible.] # Cheap... thrills # # Don't you know I like # # Cheap... thrills # # Night after night # # Cheap... thrills # # Well if you ain't supposed ta do it I will # [He steps onto the ring apron and between the ropes, where he stands in his corner stretching, waiting for the action to start, looking uninterested and even a bit lost.]
** In-Depth Info ** [1] What makes this character unique? There are a hundred cowboy characters out there, but Buzzsaw breaks the mold. He's laid back, he could care less about the men he's fighting. That makes him dangerously stiff in the ring on one hand, and a whole lot of fun during his promos on the other.
[2] Wrestler's attitude/personality: Laid back. Could care less. Goes into the ring to do his job and brutalize people. When he's a heel, he almost comes off as cocky.
When he's a face, he's more endearing in his approach. [3] How many years of professional wrestling experience does your wrestler have? (pro/non pro?)
One year. A few shows in AVW, and a year of Japanese wrestling under his belt, where he got a well-deserved reputation as being stiff as all hell and putting on a damn good show, despite not being the best
"technical" wrestler out there.
[4] Strength and weaknesses. Please include a balance of strengths and weaknesses. Strengths: 1) Toughness 2) Courage 3) Uncaring attitude Weaknesses: 1) Not a great wrestler 2) Courage gets him in some tough situations 3) Years of wear and tear from bar fights [4] Summary of experience/history. Please be descriptive. This is important. George Stevens is a graduate of Bulldogg University, the school that
Nick Demola has trained wrestlers such as Anti-Social Sublife, The
Kings of Hardcore, and others. Immediately after leaving the school, at age 25, Stevens joined a puroresu fed in Japan. He stayed there for nine months, getting tired of sushi and returning to the states.
But not before earning a reputation as a stiff worker and a man the crowds clamor to see... even if he can be something of a jerk.
He moved on to AVW for a very brief stint, making his presence felt at the AVW mid-year awards and on a pay per view, before the apparent shutdown of the fed led to his departure.
He's since been hanging out in his tiny shack in Straight Shot Gulch, located in Texas. Nobody really knows what he's been up to... except for drinking. Everyone knows he's been drinking. That's a given.
** Wrestler Make ** [1] What is the name of your wrestler's finisher? Rocky Gulch Bomb [2] Description of the finisher: Northern Lights Bomb (aka Snow Plow) [3] What are the situations in which your wrestler could perform the move? Anywhere, anytime. Usually after a low blow, though. [4] How can it be countered? By escaping Stevens' grasp, by rolling through into an armdrag, and for really skilled technicians... by turning it into a small package.
That is not for the weak at heart, though. [5] What is your wrestler's overall style (examples: technician, brawler, high flyer, luchador, fighter, shooter, etc)? May use primary and secondary.
Primary - Brawler Secondary - Hardcore [6] And not to limit your style but what Pro Wrestler does your wrestler most resemble? If not one, what mix of wrestlers? (optional):
Ringwork wise, Stan Hansen for the most part, with a touch of Carlos
Colon in terms of getting into bloodbaths... and a bit of the hardcore aspects of Bam Bam Bigelow as well.
In terms of promo style, put Rob Van Dam's non-chalance with a bit of
Arn Anderson tough trash talk (though not a whole lot, he actually doesn't realize he's trash talking when he does it... it's just natural to him) and throw in a country twang and you've got his style.
[8] List 15 (or more) non-basic moves that your wrestler will use regularly (and ones which fit his style!):
1) Boston Crab 2) Piledriver 3) Stomping a mudhole in someone's ass in the corner 4) Superplex 5) Standard Backbreaker 6) Running Bulldog 7) Swinging Full Nelson (meaning that he lifts the opponent's feet onto the ropes and hangs on to the full nelson) 8) Low Blow 9) Stiff Openhanded slaps to the chest 10) Front Layout Suplex 11) Hammerlock Slam 12) Russian Leg Sweep 13) Belly to Back Suplex 14) Running Shoulderbreaker Signature Moves: 1) STIFF right cross - his signature punch. He usually shakes his fist after hitting it, as if he's trying to get feeling in his hand 2) Hard Stuff - Leaping Leghook DDT 3) Thunderbird Bomb - Release Crucifix Powerbomb 4) Down In Rocky Gulch - Lifting Full Nelson Faceslam Secondary Finisher: 1) Buzzsaw Lariat - Very fast, VERY hard lariat ** Summary ** [1] Please writer a summary for your wrestler. George "Buzzsaw" Stevens 12/25/1975. 6'8, 298, from the Straight Shot Gulch in the Texas
Panhandle. He's a bulk of a caucasian man, and is in desperate need of some sun... but he don't really care 'bout a tan. He don't really care 'bout much fer that matter. Jess kickin' ass 'n takin' names...
'cept he don't 'member th' names fer very long neither. A stiff brawler, a hardcore fighter, 'n th' baddest of th' bad, bad guys.
** Sample Roleplay ** (optional) Setup: Stevens makes his debut in AVW at the mid-year awards show... as only he can. [The camera spots a blue pickup truck with rust spots dotting the sides of it pulling up to the red carpet. Julio Garcia gives a look of confusion to the camera and begins to approach the truck with security right behind him.]
JG: We're going to have a talk with this next... superstar? [The driver's side door opens and out pops a large man in a pair of ratty jeans, a worn "Buzzsaw's Bar & Grill" t-shirt, and a baseball cap that reads "R.I.P. Sylvester Ritter" on the front of it. He tosses his keys to a kid in a red suit.]
GS: Ya park that thang fer me 'n keep it all nice, 'n there might be a dollar in it fer ya.
[The kid flips the big man off as he turns to the interviewer with a smile on his face.] GS: Ya gotta know howta treat the children, know what I'm sayin' Pedro? JG: The name's Julio. [Stevens puts his arm around the hispanic interviewer.] GS: Ah whatever! A great night we have tonight here Jew-lee-oh, ain't it? JG: Well it is. But who are you? [Security starts to move in around the big man.] GS: Oh now. Don' tell me ya don' know who ah am! [Julio just shrugs and Stevens removes his arm and throws his hands in the air.] GS: Keeeeeid, th' name's George Stevens. "Buzzsaw" if ya weeeeeill. JG: Oh. You're the new guy. GS: Sure am. JG: Well... uh... great. GS: Sure is. Yer an easy guy to agree with, Jew-lee-oh. Ah'm here ta take a look at th' Eh-Vee-Dubya 'n see how these folks like ta wine
'n dine.
[Stevens looks down at Julio's tuxedo.] GS: And damn! Ah can see they like ta wine 'n dine here pretty fancy! I feel a li'l bit like a slob, Jew-lee-oh. But at least they dressed you up. Look at you! Betcha don' see mucha those clothes down in th' bare-eee-oh!
[Stevens slaps Julio on the back, but Julio looks unamused.] GS: Damn Jew-lee-oh. Loosen up! Ya look like an Oklahoman who jess realized that th' reason his wife barked so much durin' sex is 'cause she's a doberman.
[Julio just raises an eyebrow to him.] GS: Okay. That wasn't very good. How 'bout this one? You'll 'preciate this one. How many officers does it take t' arrest a Mexican? JG: Oh God... Mister Stev- GS: Give up? Four. Now ask me why four. JG: Oh man... I deserve a raise. Why four? GS: One t' arrest him, th' other three to carry his oranges. [Stevens slaps Julio on the back and laughs hard.] GS (laughing): Wasn't that good? Come on Jew-lee-oh! Ah thought ya'd like that! JG (not amused): Mister Stevens... I think your table is waiting. GS: Oh yeah! Sure! They don' got me sittin' nexta anyone in there. 'N I ain't brought no one with me. Ya wanna join me fer some dinner, Jew-lee-oh? JG: I couldn't. I'm working. GS: Oh sure. Gotta make that cash ta git yer family over the river. Ah unnerstand. [Stevens gives Julio a friendly slap on the back.] GS: Been damn nice talkin' to ya, keeeeid. Damn nice. Ya have a good time out here, 'n if ya need some company, drop by th' table.
[Stevens spits the tobacco out of his mouth before he enters the doors, then wipes his mouth off with his hand, and smiles at a doorman and wipes his hand off on his jacket.]
