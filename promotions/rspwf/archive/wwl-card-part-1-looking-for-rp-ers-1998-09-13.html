WWL Card Part 1(Looking For RP'ers)

13 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Static fills the screen and then the letters, WWL, appear followed by the crowd in Hyannis, MA cheering. Signs that say "Dell Thomas Is THE ICON!!!," "I Love
Alexis," and "Gangsta Related Rules!!!' appear. The camera cuts to Dell Thomas and Chris Jackson.
DT: We have a great show for you tonight!!! We have our first round of the single elimination tournemant for the WWL Championship. Matches include M-Dog
vs. Stoner, Hunter vs. The Iconoclast... CJ: The Icon vs. Ryan Renchen, and our main event, the human "Ken Doll" himself, "The Goldenboy" Jimmy Johnson vs. The Follower. Let's get to the first match!!! Dope Show by Marilyn Manson blasts throughout the arena!!! Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, accompanied by his manager Booker B, weighing in at 308 pounds... M-Dog!!!!!
The crowd cheers a little as the gold wearing man from Lathem, New York makes his way to the ring.
Announcer: And his opponent, from San Antonio, Texas... Stoner!!! The crowd goes wild as Stoner stumbles down the aisle greeting the fans as he gets to the ring.
DT: This match should be a classic. CJ: Just think... One of these guys could be WWL champion in only 2 weeks!! The guys lock up and M-Dog Irish whips Stoner into the ropes and does a diving tackle. On top of him, he chokes Stoner for a bit, then starts hitting him.
M-Dog then picks Stoner up and gives him a pile driver. He looks at the crowd and screams as loud as he can.
DT: This guy is psychotic!!! CJ: I LOVE IT!!!!! M-Dog picks up Stoner and gives him a belly to belly suplex followed by a cover... 1... 2... no he kicks out!!! He then picks up Stoner and goes for a backwards ddt but Renchen elbows M-Dog to get out of it. Stoner follows it up quickly with a ddt. He musters up the strength to roll on top of M-Dog.
1...2.. no he kicks out. They both lay in the ring lifeless. DT: Both of these guys have just about been pushed to their limit. CJ: Ryan Renchen has made a magnificant comeback but all will be for nothing if he can't follow it up!!!
They both slowly get up at the same time. M-Dog runs and does another diving tackle to Stoner and screams to the crowd again as he walks around the ring quickly. He picks up Stoner and gives him the, signature, M-Dog Driver 1...
2... 3!!!! DT: M-Dog wins it and will face the winner of our main event in the next round of the World Wrestling League Title Tournemant!!!
CJ: Whether it's The Follower or The Goldenboy, it oughtta be a great match!!! We are already set for the next match!!! Announcer: Coming down the aisle, Hunter!!! No reaction at all from the crowd as Hunter slowly walks to the ring alone. He finally gets to the ring when all of a sudden Diesel Power by Prodigy blasts!!!
Announcer: And his opponent, weighing in at 250 pounds, The Iconoclast!!!! Crowd cheers as the masked wonder jogs to the ring full of energy. DT: We saw Hunter last week for a bit but this is both of these athletes in-ring debut!!! CJ: Yes it is!!! Hunter and The Iconoclast circle each other as Hunter goes after a leg and takes The Iconoclast down. Hunter then stomps on The Iconoclasts' package.
CJ: Oh no!!! His nuts!!! Hunter then goes to take the mask off but The Iconoclast gives him a crotch punch. With Hunter bent over he gives him a ddt.
CJ: I'd hate to be both of these guys now. Wait who's that??? The ICON with The Follower walks to ringside and sits in a chair. The Follower stands beside him as they watch the match. In the mean time, The Iconoclast picks up Hunter and gives him a Samoan Drop. He then goes to the top rope.
With Hunter still laying down he does a big splash or as he calls it "The
Ending." And covers him 1... 2... 3!!!
DT: A short match but still pain-filled for both parties. Hunter seemed to lose it when he forgot about the win and tried to unmask the Iconoclast, but in any event, it was a big win for The Iconoclast! The ICON who was there for an unknown reason is already in the ring. Ryan Renchen will be joining him soon.
CJ: Just another word about the match... I'd like to say that neither of them will be fathering children soon. We'll take a commercial, The WWL will be back with The ICON 's debut against Ryan Renchen!!!
