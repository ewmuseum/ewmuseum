<<Reckless Abandon is sitting in the locker room following ASWF Bloody Sunday. He is bloodied, bruised, and broken. He is wearing his traditional blue jean cut offs and a shirt that reads "Reckless Abandon" on the front and "No Hope,
No Abandon" on the back. The ASWF Cruiserweight Title is laid down on the bench. Terry Boulder is seated next to Reckless Abandon in his wheelchair.
Abandon's dirty-blonde hair has a reddish tint from all the blood. It is sweaty and sloppy as he brushes it back from his face.>>
TERRY BOULDER:It was a good match, champ. <<Reckless Abandon looks at Boulder with a look of disgust.>> RECKLESS ABANDON:Mr.Boulder...there are MANY things you do not understand about me. You don't understand what I have gone THROUGH to get to this point. You don't understand the SACRIFICES I have made. I've gone around the world wrestling for 7 years! I've done it for the fans! I've BROKEN my neck TWO times!!! I've broken arms, legs, noses, fingers...you NAME it!!! And why have I done it?
TERRY BOULDER:Son, it ju- RECKLESS ABANDON:NO!! I'll tell you WHY I have done it! There's no better
FEELING than when I jumped off the top turnbuckle before, and I SMASHED that table when I executed the RECKLESS FLYING!!!
TERRY BOULDER:What? RECKLESS ABANDON:Oh...you think the PAIN bothers me? It DOESN'T!! It just makes me stronger! You think the money attracts me? It DOESN'T!! Its good, but not good enough! Its not the FANS either because I don't give a <<bleep>> about them!
TERRY BOULDER:Then why, son? RECKLESS ABANDON:Because I've tested the limit...I've come to the edge, and then I took a step...I've tried to see how far I can go, and I am not stopping until I get stopped! THAT'S WHAT I LOVE, BOULDER!!! I've done everything there is to do on the Independent circuits, and the ASWF was the first big-time federation to give me a chance to prove myself, and what the <<bleep>> do I do,
Boulder? I WORK MY ASS OFF! I GO THROUGH TABLES! I BLEED! I GET CUT WITH
BARBED WIRE!!! I GET SMACKED WITH BRASS KNUCKLES!!! THEN WHAT HAPPENS? I end up in a DRAW!!!
TERRY BOULDER:Don't worry, son. You were giving up lots of weight. You will face Pondo again. Its coming up at Brawl For It All.
RECKLESS ABANDON:Oh...I know...I know. I'm definitely getting ready, but if I look angry, Pondo, its because I AM!!! Now...once again...I put my CAREER on the line to face you. You know the doctors say if I break my neck once more, I must leave wrestling? Of course, they said that the first two times too, and it still hasn't stopped me. Why, you ask? Because I go to the edge, and then I continue an extra step just to test my bounds! Ramsey put special stips on this one, Pondo! We both get fired if there is no winner! Well...then...I guarantee you...Reckless Abandon will be coming out on top in this one.
TERRY BOULDER:Listen, Madman Pondo. For 8 years, I have been involved in
"big-time" wrestling, and never ONCE have I seen a man as maniacal as this one!
Plain and simple story:He is the best wrestler EVER to NEVER appear in a big time fed. Why? No fed wants to worry about the insurance problems with him on their roster. No fed is looking for a wrestler who has been unproven in any big fed, that will definitely cost a lot of money due to all his injuries.
Until now...Reckless has done everything possible on the INDY circuits, but never done anything on a worldwide stage. I heard about this kid, and brought him to the ASWF. Paul Toth wouldn't give him a contract, but then Ramsey gave
him one because he has enough money, and just wants to put on a show. The ASWF is using him for a draw...he is using the ASWF to become the best wrestler around, and since you insist on getting in his way...you will once again be in store for one of the most brutal matches of your life.
RECKLESS ABANDON:Madman Pondo...Brawl For It All...this Sunday...you and I wrestle in a scientific wrestling match, and if one of us doesn't win, we both get fired. Prepare...prepare...because when you step in the ring with RECKLESS
ABANDON...when you step into the ring with ASWF Cruiserweight Champion...you'll have NO HOPE, and there will be NO ABANDON!!!
<<FADEOUT>>
