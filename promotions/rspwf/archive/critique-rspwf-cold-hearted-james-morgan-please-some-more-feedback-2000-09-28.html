[Critique RSPWF] "Cold Hearted" James Morgan - Please, some more feedback.

28 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I wrote this for LVPWA a few weeks ago. Appears that Card or Flash Packet won't ever be released, so I didn't want this RP to be a total waste. I hope I get a little more feedback this time around, rathern then a moron trying to get back at me, ;) and the second was good, thanks Kharma.
-MDR ==================================== [The visibility gradually increases upon a large image. Covering the entire screen is the interior of the Excalibur Hotel in Las Vegas Nevada. The home of the Las Vegas Pro-Wrestling Association. The house lights are all off with the exception of two solid white Scaffold Lights, high above the ring. They provide a dim, bleak light that cast an illumination upon the empty ring. Or is it empty? Upon further examination, with "Come As You Are" by Nirvana faintly playing upon the Hotel Speakers, a figure is seen within the Ring. All by his lonesome, the figure sits. With the view zooming in, the figure is more then just that - a figure. The person occupying a solid, blue steel chair is
"Cold Hearted" James Morgan. Attired in a torn, tattered Leather Jacket, semi-torn Blue Denim Jean Shorts, black Boots, three metallic eyebrow piercings, and a very a grim stare. Morgan is straddling the chair, his elbows hanging on the backing and his chin resting upon his hands. Call him a Rookie
Monster, a Rising Star, or a Freak. But you must call him .. James Morgan, The
Cold Hearted Voice of the LVPWA.]
"COLD HEARTED" JAMES MORGAN - .. It's quite disenchanting seeing the Road To The Gold events transpire. To be rather blunt, it is disgustingly discouraging. Can James Pillow forever provide us with a Home, lacking equality, and think we'll all put up with it? Perhaps in my speech, I stand alone. But what does that matter? I wouldn't want the trend to break, suddenly. Two days short of a month from the day James Pillow promised me my
Bane would appear and physically disable me from the LVPWA, I stand here almost a week from that Night and I am in one piece. Burn marks heal. Bruises heal.
But broken promises .. <CHUCKLE> .. They last a life time. Nothing can remove or heal the wounds they leave. A guarantee - or pact even, something that you should feel assured of. Yet, when the smoke and mirrors fade away and truth shines through as the eye-blinding light, guiding you to righteousness, what are you left to see? James Morgan, sitting within the upcoming Battle Ground -
Lacking faith, trust, and enjoyment. How foolish of me to think my time had come. To think that finally, James Pillow would unmercifully throw a Lamb at me that would hold up longer then it takes for Chris Courtade to utter a trendy catchphrase or buy onto an overused gimmick. How simply foolish it was of me.
[Morgan slowly leans back, clutching the backing of the chair with his right hand. A deep sigh emits from Morgan as his eyes drift upward, towards the
Camera Lens.]
"CH" JM - I asked you to come to the LVPWA because I knew what you were made of. You're not flesh and bones. You are hype and corporate jargon, tossed together into a finalized product. All that remains is a
Gimmick and a truly injured body. But what does that prove? To think it is over is only to prove your naivety. You'll drift aimlessly around the shores of Salvation, wishing that you will be set free. Before your time truly comes, you'll be warranted a Push .. A Home .. and a fat Check. What will you do?
Like the mindless pawn you are, accept it. And continue your little charades of sipping some Alcohol and washing it down with a Book of Matches, only to regurgitate a flame. It must have been strenuous to learn how to do that. Or perhaps, you'll dance infront of a Camera with pink contact lenses and dip your skin in a pale residue, to remain an Albino Freak, caught up in the heat of vengeance. To even delve into the chasm of pity and sadness which you remain as one of the proudest victims of it, is to draw out a Chapter of my Life I don't think I want to expend energy on explaining. Where are you now?
Shuffling amongst the pathetic, mediocre talent? Striving for lost credibility? Or are you in seclusion, hoping the moment of embarrassment will pass like a mid-afternoon storm? Wherever you may roam, James Morgan quickly follows. Our Storyline is far from over. Many Tales remain to be told. And your shade, hanging in the Den of Depression lacks a Soul. It's waiting Gomez
..
[Morgan abruptly pauses, for quite a long time.] .. I'M WAITING _GOMEZ!_ [As Morgan's eyes light up and his entire face now aims towards the Camera, he leans forward. Zooming in slightly, the larger pictures still remains of
Morgan and the Chair, with the ring ropes in the background.]
"CH" JM - I suppose the next Drone, thrown forward is just to pacify me? Do you think it will work, Pillow? A Title lacking value is supposed to silence me? Is this your 'devious' plan Pillow to distract Morgan?
Did you snicker and smile like a _BRILLIANT_ fool that you tricked James
Morgan? How pathetic. I did not forget. I will not forget. I will not silence myself. I will not be distracted. BUT, I WILL BECOME LVPWA LAS VEGAS
CHAMPION .. Something you will learn to regret, James. -- Giving me a Shot, ..
You'll regret it. I expected more from you. Alex Cote? Is he the unlucky man, cast into Depression - caught in a whirlwind of troubles he did not cause or add to? <CHUCKLES> And the saddest part? He will .. _VALIANTLY_ defend his Title of this Promotion. He will oppose Morgan, in honor of his President.
All for what? A pat on the back? A thumbs up? Or a sum of money, titled a bonus? Cote -- are you so ignorant to the truth, your eyes sting when they close?
[Morgan softly chuckles to himself.] "CH" JM - If they don't, stop oppressing the truth. The pity that hangs over you, like a relentless cloud, almost brings me to sorrow. It almost makes me want to shower down upon you with perpetual mercy because of your foolish ways. However -- what remains as key is I said _ALMOST_!
[Morgan cast a very, very rare grin.] "CH" JM - What you will find Monday Night is not the Entrance to success. Nor the passage to James Pillow's heart. However, you will find a harsh reality, awaiting your arrival. And when such things occur, Alex Cote,
Former LVPWA Las Vegas Champion - You Will Realize The Epitaph Was In Your Name
.. ALL ALONG .. But it'll be to late, because you'll already Fall Into A Deep
.. Deep
[Morgan leans forward and in a raspy, whispering tone ..] __DEPRESSION!__ [Fade to Black]
