[Thoughts Welcomed] "Arch Angel" Gabrielle Cross & Envy: All in The Family

Here is a flash me and my buddy Jon ( [detail withheld] ) wrote for the WWO. He's new to ewrestling and I was helping him out. If you enjoy sick S & M,
You'll enjoy this :)
========== [SCENE: Cameras show an underground sex club in some undisclosed spot in America. Men and women are chain upto walls, wearing masks and clad in black leather and vinyl. Some women are seen whipping other women and men with chains and whips. The cameras slip through this village of sin to quite and emptier room off the main dance floor. The room has a large leather bed, a table and 3 chairs. Sitting on one of the chairs, smoking a cigarette and nursing a bottle of beer is "The Arch Angel" Gabrielle Cross. Cross's thick blonde hair is tied back into a braid. He is wearing a pair of black blue jeans and a black silk button up shirt with several buttons unbuttoned at the top. Gabrielle also has a silver cross chain around his neck. Laying on the bed is ENVY and a man. He seems to be no older then 18. He's wearing a pair of jeans and a white RUSH tshirt. Envy is wearing a very girly, pink floral dress and a pair of black mary kate shoes. She's actually looking very sweet and innocent at this point.
The boy tries to kiss the neck of Envy but she pushes him away] ENVY: *giggling and blushing* I .. I just don't know.. I've never really been at a place like this before. To be honest.. I'm a virgin...
BOY: *smiles and rubs her leg* Don't worry baby.. I'll be very gentle... I'll take my time with you. I don't bite...
ENVY: *smiles* That's good [The boy begins to kiss Envy more passionately. She begins kissing down his neck and suddenly, out or no where, sinks her teeth in and bites down. The boy begins to scream but before he can act, Envy pushes him down and ties his hands to the wall. She reaches into her bag of tricks that was on the floor next to her and pulls out a roll of duct tape. She tapes up his mouth and straddles his waist. Envy smiles and licks some blood that's lingering on her fingers]
ENVY: You don't bite? It's a good thing I do... GABRIELLE CROSS: Will you quit playing with that boy Envy? ENVY: *Frowns* Such an old man my brother is. You forgot what fun is. I must teach you. GABRIELLE CROSS: Fun has it's place just not right now with me. [Envy smirks and gets up from the boy who still has a look of complete fear and shock on his face. Envy walks over to Gabrielle and positions herself on her knees in between his legs]
ENVY: Let me make you feel all better Brother.... [Gabrielle smiles down at Envy, picks her head up by underneath her chin and kisses her on the forehead. He takes the matches off the table and dangles them in front of the face of Envy. Much like a little child being offered candy,
Envy smiles brightly. Gabrielle puts the matches in Envy's hand. She scampers off back to the boy. The camera focuses in on Cross but the sound of matches being lit and then screams of pain are heard dully in the background]
GABRIELLE CROSS: It's been said that in the WWO, the streets are lined with gold. I saw the WWO as an opportunity to show the world that there's more to me the meets the eye. I have been lucky. A thousand blessings have been laid before me and they come in the shape of Brian Bradley and my sister. Brian
Bradley is my own personal savior. He is a man above all that I respect. He is a man who started it all. When you look at the line of villians you see before you, Brian Bradley's name still tops everyone's mind. I respect that. I want that. I want to feel that. I want to feel that power and control. Brian is a man who contiunously puts the WWO on the mat as far as shock value. That's something no one else has yet to do.
When I look into the mirror, I see a lost angel wondering the earth in serach of guidence. I have finally found that guidence. I have finally found something
I can latch onto. Gabrielle Cross won't be lost forever. Every Arch Angel finds it's cloud.. Mine is right here with my family. Black Roses, Eternal Damnation,
Village of the Sacred.. Call it whatever you want it still means I can finally be what I want to be. No longer must I play by the rules of other federations.
No longer must I be what they expected me to be. I can be myself and enjoy every single moment of it. No more shackles, no more restraints.. Just
Gabrielle Cross being the man he tried to hide for so long. In my past lives, I've played the cruiserweight. It was only a matter of time before the WWO saw my skill and asked me to breath life into their divison. The cruiserweight champion RAPTOR trying so hard to upload Japanese traditions and styles. Too bad that this is America and American wrestling is at an all time high. I got into a 4 way dance as my first match up ever in the WWO. I go in there against opponents I don't know and don't care about. Shawn Harrison,
Gregory Brians, Axon Blade.. None of your impress me. I look at you as nothing more then pawns leading me to bigger and better things not only for myself but also for my family. Brian Bradley has dealt with the worst, now it's time to deal with the best. I'm no Jarek Munroe. Envy is no Chanel. This is nothing you've ever seen before in you before you your life..
ENVY: Or you'll see again. [Envy walks over to Gabrielle and sits on his lab] ENVY: Darling.. What about Westside Wavalan? I don't think Daddy will appercaite him sticking his nose into our business again.
GABRIELLE CROSS: Then we shan't let him have a nose to stick in shall we? ENVY: *Giggles* He's mine... GABRIELLE CROSS: You have fun with him baby.. You deserve a toy. Don't let happen to him what happen to that rabbit I bought you.
ENVY: Don't worry Gabe... I'll play nice... GABRIELLE CROSS: You always do... [Envy licks the lips of her brother. Fade out] -[name withheld]- *Handler of Eric Draven & "The Walking Orgasm" Studly Scotty Davis (WWO) I also claim to be your worst nightmare when you act STUPID
