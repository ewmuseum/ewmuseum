Please Critique

23 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, I'm trying to better my rp's but I'm not sure how to go about it. So I need someone to tell me how I can make my rp's better and get better wins etc.
Scenario: X-bonez was last night screwed out of the hardcore title that he was defending against Ice by Kid Extreme who knocked both men out and counted them both down.
RP (The scene opens in an office, showing the back of a chair with the top of a man's head protruding from it,and it is shadowed. The office is fairly modern and well decked out with a lot of things. Then a knock is heard on the door.)
Man- Come in! (The door opens and in walks X-bonez, the man who along with Ice last night was screwed by Kid Extreme. The camera rotates slightly and sees that
X-bonez isn't wearing the Hardcore Title around his waist, in fact, the hardcore title is lying on the table in front of the man, who appears to be
President Pat Daechaelson.)
X-bonez- Well, you just go and rub it right into my face dont ya?!(points to title) Pat- Well X-bonez, I didnt mean to rub it in your face, but I didnt even expect you to come barging into my office. But I'll let you off becau-
X-bonez- YOU let ME off? Pat, you're lucky I'm up here and not down there looking to kick Kid Extreme's ass after that little stunt he pulled last night!
Pat- When you put it like that, it does sound a little bad. X-bonez- Well I aint up here to bitch about how I'm gonna kick Kid Extreme's ass when I catch a hold of him, I'm here to get that damn belt back!
Pat- Well X-bonez, I dont know... X-bonez- Well think about it. The champion always gets the benefit of the doubt, the only time they didnt was that screw with that crappy wannabe from conneticut, y'know the WWF. When that bloke and the bitch had to share the belt.
Pat- I know what you mean... X-bonez- So I'm getting my belt back? (X-bonez stands up from the chair he was sitting in and grabs the Hardcore
Title and goes for the door.)
Pat- Wait! (X-bonez throws the belt back onto the table and sits back down.) X-bonez- What? You saw what was going on last night, I had that match won! Pat- Actually you were lying on your back and Ice was about to make the cover. You might want to thank Mr. Extreme for his efforts.
X-bonez- The only way I'm gonna thank that bitch is right upside his head! Pat- Wow, wow, wow. Hold it right there, I am not going to give you that belt right here, I am going to make an announcement regarding the Hardcore
Title later in the week. I'm not going to tell anyone what it is, but it will regard you.
X-bonez- Pat, I'm telling you, give me the damn belt and call it a day, coz you wont like me when I'm mad. I retired Coady just a few weeks ago, I kicked his ass and the sorry sonofabitch never showed his face again in this place. Do you want that?
Pat- No that's the last thing I want, I would love to see you, X-bonez, the man that has held that title more than any other, as the champion, for the fifth time, but I cant release any information on what will be my final decision until I actually announce it, sorry X-bonez...
X-bonez- You want me to kick that SOB's ass again worse than I kicked it last night to get that damn belt back again? Just name it! I'll even kick your ass right now to get that damn thing back! Now I see there aint no point tryin to get it back, so I'm gonna go and find that jackass Kid
Extreme and show you what I'll do if you make the wrong decision about the belt. One piece of advice, keep a close eye on me between now and then.
Pat- X-bonez, I strongly advise you not to do it, that will only make things worse. X-bonez- I dont give a crap, I'm outta here, and like I said, keep an eye on me bitch! (X-bonez stands up and throws a few things from the desk and across the room before yelling a few obscenities at Pat Daechaelson and leaving.)
>>Scene fades<< (After a match between two big names in XWA, the Xtreme-tron shows a picture of a corridor, it is deserted except for the fans that are waiting outside their heroes' locker rooms, hoping for autographs. Then a lot of shouting and banging is heard for a few seconds, then X-bonez appears from around a corner. He is screaming Kid Extreme's name and tipping tables. The fans that are there try to rush over to him to get the autograph, but he pushes past them and carries on. He opens a locker room door, no luck there. He tries another. Same. But as X-bonez is coming out, who should be standing there, but the man he fought last night, Ice. Both men stare each other down for about 15 seconds before X-bonez speaks.)
X-bonez- I guess you're looking for that sorry SOB too huh? Ice- Yeah, I'm gonna make that asswipe wish he was never born when I catch a hold of him. X-bonez- If I get there first, there wont be nothin' left of him to wish. You keep looking for him, I'm goin' to ringside to find out whether he's deaf or stupid. My guess is both!
(X-bonez brushes past Ice, nudging him with his shoulder as he does so. Ice grins to himself.)
Ice- That guy never ceases to amaze me.*sigh* (The camera shows Ice walking away before it cuts to the view of the arena. Where Devil Wihout A Cause starts to play and X-bonez comes out. No pyros. X-bonez has a mic in his hand and he proceeds to make his way to the ring.) C1- Well I guess Ice and X-bonez must be really focussed on Kid Extreme, because they could've tore each other apart right there, but they chose not to.
C2- Yeah, these guys must really want to kick Kid's ass all over the state. C1- What is with X-bonez? No entrance pyro, he just walks out, oblivious to the world. No entrance moves like usual, he just walks out with a look on his face that could melt ice. No pun intended.
C2- The guy has finally snapped, the incident which took place last night was what finally pushed X-bonez over the edge. 13 years on the street make the guy as tough as leather, but inside, he's as weak as any regular man.
C1- Maybe so, but X-bonez is a man on a mission and I dont intend on getting in his way! (X-bonez climbs through the ropes and doesnt climb onto the ropes as he usually does, he just goes and sits on the floor in the ring.)
X-bonez- Kid, I have given you one too many chances back there to shows your face and take it like a man. I have had enough of being the hunter. If you got a problem with me, if you wanna cost me matches for the rest of my career. Bring your little problem out here and I'm sure we can sort it out.
After all, there's nothing a good asskicking cant solve. C1- I guess that's one way of sorting it out like men! C2- Yeah, but not one I would've thought of. C1- I know. (The fans laught and cheer at the same time, but they get no response from X-bonez.) Kid, we had never had a run in in our time in the XWA, we had never had a problem, we had never even spoke each others' names. But last night, you started the ball rolling. Because Kid, showing up at my match is meriting of a good ass-whupping, but interfering is another. But you went further than that. You decided to mask yourself and act as the referee so you could count both of us down. You must be real stupid to ahve done that, that's why I prepared a little footage so I know then that you realised the full extent of what you actually did! Roll it!
(Footage is shown of Kid Extreme interfering in X-bonez' match last night at
Jacked Up, when he put both men over each other and made the three count.)
C1- I dont know about you, but I think that was an incredibly stupid thing for Kid Extreme to do as he has put X-bonez in a foul, foul mood.
C2- I dont know, I think he may have the edge over X-bonez mental wise, because I think X-bonez has cracked, But hell, who doesnt have the mental advantage over X-bonez?
C1- If he hears you insult him, dont expect me to help you out. Now Kid, I am officially calling you out right now. Let's see if we can settle this like men. Let's see who is the real man out of us, me and you, we'll see who is the real man out of the two of us. Hell, if you wanted a
Hardcore Title shot, all you gotta do is just walk on up and ask, bitch. But now you done it, now you made me decide your fate. No Title shot, just a good old fashioned ass kicking! Kid, I am gonna go back there and look for you again, coz it dont look as if you want me to publicly humiliate you in front of these jackasses (crowd boo) So Kid, watch your back coz fear's comin to town and his name is X-bonez!!
(Devil Without A Cause blares out over the speakers and X-bonez throws the mic well into the crowd as the fans give him another mixed reaction and he just walks backstage wearing the same look as he did when he entered.)
