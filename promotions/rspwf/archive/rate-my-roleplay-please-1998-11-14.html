Rate my RolePlay Please

14 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please rate this RP by me, Shane Andrews. In this match, I was going to face
Marvel. ::The scene fades in, and you are standing outside of a store. The cameraman pans around, and you see bundles of people walking down the sidewalk, and some enter the store. The cameraman looks up to show you that this is a comic book store. The cameraman brings the camera back down very fast, making you sick.
The camera is out of focus for a heartbeat, then it comes back. The cameraman reaches out and shoves the door open. He walks inside, and immediately you see a crown of people standing around one glass booth. The cameraman walks over, and in the front of the crowd is none other than the "International Superstar"
Shane Andrews. He is wearing a black shirt that says "I'm Better Than a Perfect
10..." on the front in red, and on the back it says "I'm a 12, Goin on 13" in red also. He is wearing khaki cargo shorts, and wearing Adidas sandals with no socks. His hair is down and his beard is thick. He has some Arnette sunglasses on his head. He is holding a comic book with a picture of the Incredible Hulk on it. He turns around and sees the camera. He shuffles his way through the crowd to speak to the camera.::
Shane: Well, what do you guys want today? Cameraman: Have you heard who you are facing this Monday night? Shane: As a matter of fact, I have. I do get informed ya know. Cameraman: Well, what are your thoughts on Marvel? Shane: Well, this is just a coincidence that I am inside of a comic book store. Trust me, I will make no remarks about the Marvel comics similarity to the
"King of Cable", Marvel. Because when people try to make jokes out of that, well, it makes them sound like a punk who is trying to get over. But, frankly,
I think it's immature. Marvel, you know me, and I know you. So that means no cheap, one line jokes coming out of my mouth. I speak what I feel, but I give no cheap shots.
Now, I do want to talk about this Monday. Flat out, this will be a historical event. Two superb athletes going at it to get not one, but TWO titles. I think that we will give the JPW fans what they expect, and more. But one thing keeps making me think differently. It's no secret that you and Ralph Julevic are friends. You both have verbally said it, and I'll be d*mned if I am going to lose because of Ralph. I was screwed by him last Monday, and Ralph, I don't know what was going through your head, but I am maybe your most popular athlete right now. I am ratings, and if I lose my IC Title, there is a possibility that ratings will fall. Yes, I am shooting from the hip, but I am saying what I want to say. You had a plan all along when you decided to give Blade the shot at my gold. When you nailed me with that steel chair, it was then that I knew I was in trouble. Somehow, I came out alive, and retained my Intercontinental belt.
But one question flies through my head every second of every day, why me? What did I ever do to you? I've been a hard worker for you, and I never gave you lip. I don't pull Shawn Sylkk's or Triple X's. I work my God d*mn *ss off, and you screw me over? Well, I know exactly what I'll do about that. But that is later on.
I need to address Marvel about this Monday. Marvel, you've accomplished a lot lately in the JPW, and so have I. We're both in the top ten, and we both hold gold. But I see one distinct difference, and that difference is that I did it on my own. I didn't get help from Ralph Julevic. Because, honestly, I don't need it. I've never used a boss's power to get me things I don't deserve. Wait,
I'm not saying you don't deserve that TV Title, but you really didn't work your way upto top contendership. Now, why do you choose to take the easy way. I chose the hard way, and I'm loving it. I can look at myself in the mirror every morning. I can go into town, and people literally stop and clap for me. They know how hard I work. The respect me for that. But you don't get that. You were so good when you were on your own. Numerous titles, popular name. But now, well, you're known as the boss's right hand man. You're known as the corporate kiss *ss. But enough on the politics, I'm going to get into the actual ring talent.
Marvel, yes I admit, you are a d*mn good wrestler. I also admit that you are now a top contender for MY title. But, all that mean sh*t when you step into the ring with the Superstar. You can be a perfect ten, but 100% of the time, I am better. I will always be on a higher level than other stars in this sport.
The reason being, is that I have every aspect of the game. I can over power you, I can drain you with countless submissions, and I can fly from pillar to post if need be. I am very ring smart. I know exactly where I am at all times.
I've been in the sport for many a years, and I know the ring. Also, I am experienced to the point that nothing scares me. No position is a bad one.
Because when you have me locked into, say, a Boston Crab, I'm thinking of a way to reverse it. And by the time you realize that I reversed it, it's too late.
That's when I strike. Not only do I have those characteristics, but I have others. For one, I am fast. And I'm still under the age of 40. I've been around the world, so I have faced every type of wrestler ever breeded. You are just another typical. So, count down the days until Monday, and then realize who the
ICON really is in this sport. Marvel, the "Dream" awaits you.
::Shane puts his glasses over his eyes, and leaves the store as the camera fades::
