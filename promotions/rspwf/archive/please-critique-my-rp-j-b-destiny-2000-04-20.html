Please critique my rp. {J.B. Destiny}

20 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Destiny's Homepage Dangerous Alliance "Failure Is Not An Option" "The Future" J.B. Destiny (The Scene opens up in The XBWL Arena, Pyro-Technics go off as the fans start to go nuts. As the camera pans around the arena you can see various signs such as: RJ Frasco Is A True XBWL Legend, MEASE = Ratings, Destiny is Destined For
Destruction, In Dudley We Trust, Hey Mac Get Well Soon. Pyro-Technics go off again and the fans get even louder the camera quickly pans over to The XBWL
Announcing position. Sitting in the booth is The Wrestling Doctor.
Pyro-Technics go off one last time as a small XBWL...XBWL...XBWL chant quickly starts and finishes. The Wrestling Doctor then gets his cue to begin and does so.)
Wrestling Doctor: Well fans we're just a couple days away from the first ever
Sunday Night X-treme, and one of the matches booked for that card is a North
American Title Match. Of course there is an XBWL twist though it's a barbed wire match. And earlier this week I had the oppurtunity to talk with one of the participants in this match. And I think the clip is ready...yes I'm now getting word from the back that the clip is ready so why dont we take a look at my exclusive interview with the self proclaimed "Future" of proffesional wrestling J.B. Destiny
(The crowd turns its attention to The XBWL big screen. The Scene is in the back of The XBWL Arena somewhere. There is a white back drop with a dark blue XBWL trade mark "X" in the center of it. J.B. Destiny who has the build of Brian
Christopher and has short black hair with blonde highlights is wearing a pair of slightly baggy carpenter jeans and a long white sleeve T-shirt that reads in red print across the chest "Dangerous Alliance either your with us or you wanna be with us" is sitting on a chair across from The Wrestiling Doctor who is wearing a blue long sleeve button up shirt which reads XBWL in dark blue print in the upper left hand corner along with apair of Dockers stone whashed Kaki pants.)
Wrestling Doctor: Now first off what would you like to refer to you as??? Because I know you refer to yourself as many different things. J.B. Destiny: You can call me The Future XBWL North American Champion. Wrestling Doctor: (Looks like he wants to slap the over confident J.B. Destiny in the face.) Alright then J.B. Destiny "The Future" XBWL North American
Champion. You are going to be involved in the first ever Sunday Night X-treme, and you will infact be involved in the first match ever to take place on
X-treme. It will also be a special match for you because you will be getting your first oppurtunity to capture singles gold here in The XBWL what are your comments on that???
J.B. Destiny: Well the way I see it is, The XBWL isn't honoring J.B. Destiny by putting him on the first Sunday Night X-treme match ever there trying to bring in the ratings so The XBWL can be the number one league in this business once again. Hell I can see what the conversation was like right now, Micheal
Wilkins and "Mean" Mark Mease were sitting in Wilkins's office and asking each other what can we do to make people tune in??? Then they said to themselves of course we can have the most dominating superstar this sport has ever seen...J.B. Destiny wrestle not once but twice in the same night, which in my book was the first idea either one of thoose men have ever had. Now as far as me getting my first shot at singles gold here in The XBWL. I just wanna say it's about damn time, J.B. Destiny has went out in that ring and proven himself time and time again and as far as I am concerned I should have been booked in the main event for The World Title instead of Chang Takata when I made my return. But instead the genusis in The XBWL front office decided to have me open the show versus the unknown "Dangerous" Damien Kass. And of course as everyone suspected I went through Kass like a knife going through hot butter.
And now Kass comes out and has the audasity to claim that he almost beat "The
Destined One" well guess again Kass you did not even once come close to
beating J.B. Destiny. And you can say that my plans backfired all you want because in reality Kass I didn't need a game plan to step in the ring with you because I'm flat out more talented than you, don't feel bad though I'm talented than every other so called "Superstar" in The XBWL and you were just unlucky to have your debut match against the biggest name in the industry today. But Kass do your self a favor and stop intaginizing me because if you push J.B. Destiny you can bet he's gonna push back. Kass you say I only beat you because I was the better man Saturday night but the truth is that I was the better man that night, today, tommorow and forever. Kass you will never be as talented as J.B.
Destiny your the type of guy who can be mediokre at best in the minors but now that you've come to The XBWL you have to realize that your in the big leagues now and your going to struggle along side guys like Ken War and Mandante so my advice is go back to the gym and get the hell out of my federation because you can't survive here anyway.
Wrestling Doctor: You mentioned Mandante. He happens to be one of your opponents in the three way dance for the North American Title. What are your thoughts on this match and your opponents???
J.B. Destiny: Well like I said it's about time J.B. Destiny got a title shot, and I plan on walking out the new XBWL North American Champion it's as simple as that. And the way I see it is that Mandante and Lincoln Stone are less than formiddable opponents. Mandante is a big man but I have beaten much bigger men, hell I beat I giant of a man in thirteen seconds flat in my first match ever. And I like to think that the bigger they are the harder they fall, and
Mandante is no exception to this rule. Now as for Lincoln Stone he seems to be obssesed with talking about how he is not gonna walk in the ring and hand his title over to J.B. Destiny. Well Stone do you think that's what I want??? Do you think I've been coming out here and claiming that you were gonna give me the belt??? Sure you proably should save yourself the pain and embarassment by just handing the belt over to me, but what I've been saying I was gonna do all along is that I'm gonna step into that ring Sunday and take the belt from you.
I plan on pinning you one...two...three in the middle of the ring and proving to you, Mandante, and all thoose idiot fans that J.B. Destiny is by far the better man. Stone you say it never really registered with you that you were even in a match with J.B. Destiny, well belive me come this Sunday when your back is on the mat and your looking up towards the lights it will have registered by then, and if not it will surely have registered when you go back to your dressing room and realize you are no longer the XBWL North American
Champion. And Stone again what's with the whole army angle. Every other word out of your mouth is either some obssenity or an army reference. Listen
Lincoln you don't have to keep shoving the army gimmick down the fans throats my presence in the match will be enough to bring in the ratings. So why don't you do us all a favor and stop preaching about how the barbed wire is usually used as a defens in the army much like land mines, because this is The XBWL
Stone and this is wrestling not the armed services, and in wrestling barbed wire has always been a weapon not a defense mechinism. Stone your in for the fight of your life, and this may be the first acctual war you were ever involved in. Bottom line is J.B. Destiny is walking out of X-treme the new
XBWL North American Champion.
Wrestling Doctor: Alright thank you for your time. (XBWL Big screen fades to black and the camera is once again placed squarely on the wrestling doctor. He begins to speak.)
Wrestling Doctor: Well there you have it J.B. Destiny claiming he will walk out of X-treme the new North American Title holder...
(All of a sudden Living On A Prayer by Jon-Bon Jovi without the words begins to play over the arenas loud speaker system. The fans begin to boo. Douglas
Wellington Jr. steps out from behind the curtain. He has his light brown hair which is about shoulder length tied back into a pony-tail. He is wearing a black business suit. He is followed by the beautiful Amy who has brown hair which is about shoulder length which she keeps down. She is wearing a tight red belly shirt and a red and white plaid mini skirt. She is followed by J.B.
Destiny. When J.B. Destiny steps out the fans boo's become louder. J.B. Destiny has the build of Brian Christopher. He has short black hair which has blonde highlights. He is wearing a pair of red wrestling tights (The Pant
Kind) with a blue flame going up the left leg, and a pair of white wrestling boots which read D.A. in black print on each side. J.B. Destiny puts his hands on his hips and with a slight glimse of a smile on his face he scans around the audience. When the music hits its highpoint J.B. Destiny, Douglas
Wellington Jr., and Amy begin to walk down the aisle towards the ring. J.B. Destiny and Douglas Wellington Jr. ignore the fans as they walk down but Amy slaps the fans hands as she makes her way down the aisle. When they finally reach ringside Douglas Wellington Jr. and Amy make there way up the steps.
Douglas Wellington Jr. holds the ropes open for Amy and when she steps in he follows her. They wait in the center of the ring for J.B. Destiny. J.B.
Destiny climbs up onto the apron and wipes his boots off before he steps in the ring through the ropes. After he enters the ring he walks over to a ring post, when he gets there he climbs up to the middle turnbuckle and raises both hands over his head only to be greeted with more booing from the audience. J.B.
Destiny then climbs back down and joins Douglas Wellington Jr. and Amy in the middle of the ring. Douglas Wellington Jr. takes a microphone out of his inside jacket pocket. He hands it to J.B. Destiny. J.B. Destiny waits for the booing to die down a bit, when if finally does he raises the microphone to his mouth and begins to speak.)
J.B. Destiny: So you guys all saw my taped interview with The Wrestling Doctor, but I havn't talked about the fifteen man "I quit" match to much so I figured I would come out here and tell you about how J.B. Destiny is gonna domintate the fourteen other competitors in this match and walk out the number one contender. So like almost every other man in the match has done I am gonna run down the list of every person in this match, and tell you why J.B. Destiny is better than them. First is "Dangerous" Damien Kass. Kass I beat you once already and I am looking forward to doing it again, Kass you come out here and talk about how your going to fly by everyone in this match with ease, but you must have missed my name on the list because if you had seen it you would never have made such an idle promise to theese people out here. I personally don't see why you try to play up to theese people when you have not even learned how to stay in character yet. Basically Kass the way I see it is, that you don't belong in a match were you have the oppurtunity to become the number one contender for The XBWL World Title. Hell you don't even deserve a contract you need to be sent back to wrestling school were you might beable to muster up a little bit of unfound skill, but that is very doubtful. I acctually don't know what your doing in this match now that I think about it. There are plenty of other guys they could have thrown in there V-dawg, or Big Rasta would have been much better choices in my opinion. Kass I'm classifiying you with the likes of
Doomsday, Subird, Ken War, and Maximum. Guys that are gonna be in the same ring with me but deffinatley don't deserve to be in the same ring as me. I plan on desposing of you five men very quickly and with virtually no trouble at all. So the way I feel is if you guys arn't gonna be in the match for long
J.B. Destiny shouldn't waist my time on talking about you. So onto another man in this match...and I am talking about you Beast. You can come out and claim that J.B. Destiny is a homosexual all you want but it does not matter how much you want that to be true, because I am engaged to Amy and everyone knows that.
So you can stop dreaming because I don't lead the same life style as you, mind you I'm not out here to judge what you do with other men in the privacy of your own home because frankly it's none of my business. But what is my business is that your in the "I quit" match and that means your just like all the others I have already mentioned. Your nothing but a speed bump on J.B. Destiny's way to
The XBWL World Title. You may be a former Hardcore Champion many times over but that means nothing now you are a part of The XBWL's past but right now you are looking at The XBWL's "Future". Beast I have beaten you before, and I promise you J.B. Destiny will put a blemish on your record once again. And that is one of the reasons I find it amusing that you refer to me as "The Other
Wrestler" when in fact I am the only wrestler, that's right I am the only wrestler theese fans tune in to watch night in and night out. Why because I'm the guy who embarrasses so called legends like your self. And as much as they don't want to admit it they like seeing J.B. Destiny beat and embarrass men like you, who come out and claim to be this and that when in reality your nothing but a mediocre wrestler, with a medicore track record and a bad gimmick.
(Crowd boo's and in a show of support for there hero, The Beast.) J.B. Destiny: What??? It's true isn't it??? I thought so, no I don't think I am mistaking but it seems to me as if there are two Beasts in this match, no wait my mistake one of them is the man who wants to be Beast almost as much as
Brandon Thorton wants to be J.B. Destiny. Claw I will be the first guy to admit that I don't know anything about you, but I will also be the first guy to admit that I don't need to anything about you to realize your a no talent wrestler; again just like beast with a bad name, gimmick and persona which is also just like Beast and seeing as how I have beaten Beast before I feel as if
I will have no problem making you say I quit. O that reminds me alot of you are proably wondering what kind of moves J.B. Destiny is gonna use...since I am not known as bieng a submission style wrestler but I do have one submission hold which is proably the greatest hold of all time. My late-great Father taught me how to do it. And I am talking about The Pheonix Death Lock or to the common wrestling fan the move known as The Texas Cloverleaf. Anyways back to the opponents. Hawk Henshaw...another self proclaimed legend. Let me ask you a question what do you have to do to become a legend around here, cut an interview??? I don't understand how a man like Henshaw can be considered a legend, He is nothing but a dirty foriegner who lacks the talent to become a true superstar in our prestigious industry, but because a few fans cheer for him when he walks down to this ring he gets to call himself a legend. What a joke!!! Hawk Henshaw is no more of a legend than Kass is infact that's another guy you can throw in with the likes of Kass and Sunbird. Now Henshaw has nothing better to do with his time then to watch old WCW tapes and come out and wine about us supposedly stealing the name of The Dangerous Alliance. Well guess what Henshaw sure using the name of The Dangerous Alliance might not have been the most creative thing to do but it fit. Look at us we are Dangerous, infact I would say much more Dangerous than the original group. And Sunday I am gonna prove to you Henshaw that we deserve to be called Dangerous, becuase that is exactly what we are. The next guy is Kruger Roberts...a man who is obviously terrified of me because I come out here ever week and verbally abuse him and he gives me no respons. So either he is deaf or he is scared to talk about me, but can you blame him??? I mean he realizes if you say something about me I am gonna make you regret the fact that you opened your mouth.
Roberts you have already gotten a beating curtousy of J.B. Destiny and The
Dangerous Alliance and come this Sunday your gonna get another one but you will not be alone...as you would know if you have been listening to any of this.
But I have to admit the company is pretty lousy. Then of course there is the current XBWL North American Champion Lincoln Stone...now Stone I'm gonna have the pleasure of beating you twice in one night, first I am gonna take away your
North American Title, then I am gonna make you submit and ruin your chances of ever wearing The XBWL World Title. Stone I already told you what I had in store, I will be suprised if you even make it to this match, And if you do O well I'll just have to keep giving you the beating of your life. Speaking of giving people the beatings of there lives I'm ready to give Steve Edwards a beating one more time. Steve how can you have the audasity and gull to say you don't respect me??? God knows that you should damn well respect me. I have carried you to numerous Tag-Team Titles and I pinned you one...two...three in the middle of the ring. Hahahhahaha and you say you don't respect me as far as
I am concerned you should be worshiping the ground I walk on. Maybe I just have to beat you one more time for it to sink in. So this Sunday, Steve I am telling you right now expect no mercy. I love you like a brother but if you don't respect me I am going to have to teach you to respect me. What do you think about that???
(Crowd boo's, some fans have littered the ring with garbage.) J.B. Destiny: Now the man I am looking forward to hooking it up with the most is The J.B. Destiny wanna be Brandon Thorton, Thorton as I understand it our match is all set and the office is just working out all the details. And you've gotta believe that I can't wait to sink my teeth into you Thorton. But that match will be the main course. And this Sunday I will only get a little taste of you the apetizer if you will. Any way you put it Thorton the bottom line is I'm coming for you this Sunday. And when I am finished with you, you won't beable to spew lies anymore. You come out here and claim one that you have had the nickname of "The Future" longer than me well guess what, you haven't!!! Second you claim you were in The XBWL longer than me...well guess again, you see Thorton it may sound good or impressive when you tell the fans all theese things but sooner or later all the lieying is gonna catch up to you and you will be caught in a web of decite with no were to run and no where to hide. And after I make all thoose men tap, there is only one piece of the puzzle left and that is taking the belt from you "Sensational" Sean Dudley.
"Sensational" huh??? How Sensational can you be when you needed me to win The
XBWL Tag-Team Titles for you??? Dudley we both know I am better than you even though we have never stepped in the ring together we know what would happen if we did. And the end result would be like so many other matches. J.B. Destiny standing over his beaten opponent with his hand raised in victory. Sean I'm coming for you. You my friend are DESTINED FOR DESTRUCTION!!!
(J.B. Destiny hands the microphone back to Douglas Wellingon Jr. Douglas
Wellington Jr. takes it and puts it back in his jacket pocket as Living On A
Prayer by Jon-Bon Jovi without the lyrics begins to play over The Arena's loud speakers once again. Amy, Douglas Wellington Jr., and J.B. Destiny all exit the ring via the steps and make there way back to the dressing room. The boo's in the arena are as loud as they ever have been. J.B. Destiny and Douglas
Wellington Jr. ignore the fans as they walk up the aisle but Amy slaps there hands. Eventually they dissapear behind the curtain.)
Wrestling Doctor: Well it looks as if we've gotten a little more J.B. Destiny than we bargined for, well be right back.
(The Scene fades to a Chrysler commercial.) This Has Been A J.B. Destiny Interview And As Usual The Pleasures Been All
