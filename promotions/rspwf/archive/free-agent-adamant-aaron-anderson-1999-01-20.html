Presidents, Well, I'm back. That was a longer retirement than I had expected, and I must say: it felt pretty darn good. Anyway, I plan to be bringing
El Dub back, along with Valiant, but I have a new character -- "Adamant"
Aaron Anderson. Now this took a lot of people to help me, and I would like to thank all who helped me on this gimmick/character/wrestler.
Anyway, I have included a sample application and a character development role play (the catch phrase sucks... I'm going to be making a new one), so that can give presidents some stuff to look at, or something. I can't say that role-playing with Aaron is going to be roses and daisies, but I'm going to try as hard as I can, or something like that... So here are the application and the sample role play. Enjoy, and send all invitations, information, etc. to

Wrestler's Entrance Music & Artist: "Iron Man" by Sabbath Wrestler's Gimmick: His moniker should say it all. He's a force to be reckoned with, so to speak. He's a huge man, who will utterly destroy an opponent in the ring with his power/brawling interfusion. Although many people think that the unstoppable wrestlers have no mic skills,
Aaron will try and prove that incorrect.
Wrestler's Wrestling Style: Power; Brawling Wrestler's Ten Favorite Moves: (1) pump-handle slam (2) powerslam (3)tilt-a-whirl powerslam (4)fisherman DDT (5)firemany's carry brainbuster (6)piledriver (7)sidewalk slam (8)spinebuster slam (9)german released suplex (10) gut-wrench powerbomb Wrestler's Favorite Chain Moves: (1) whip to the turnbuckles, clothesline, (opponent rebounds forward to the middle of the ring), full-nelson suplex (2) opponent goes for a cross-body block but Andrews catches him, fall-away slam, Aaron rebounds himself from the ropes... ELBOW DROP!! Wrestler's Finisher: Diamond Drop Finisher Description: Top-Rope Fisherman DDT Finisher's set up: Brainbuster DDT Will your wrestler do anything to signal the finisher? He'll hold out both fists into the air and do a manly yell, inspiring the crowd to pop. Wrestler's Apparel: A plain black pair of wrestling tights that Scott Steiner used to wear. (I don't know what they're called. :\) He'll have a black stress pad on both forearms. Black calf-high wrestling boots.
Wrestler's Description: Aaron is a deep-tanned white male. He's very built, with a strong upper body. He has black shoulder-length hair, with no facial hair, and piercing blue eyes.
Wrestler's Story: Steve Anderson and Jody Anderson celebrated; their first son -- Aaron
Jacob Andrews -- was just born. Jody was tired, but excited, while Steve was ecstatic. Their marriage was going great, and their newborn child was a beautiful little boy.
Jody and Steve raised little Aaron in a middle-class neighborhood in
Prince William County, Virginia, a small county that lay on the outskirts of Washington, D.C. Aaron rarely encountered any physical problems with kids; he was friends with most of the kids around the block and in different neighborhoods.
Jody and Steve watched little Aaron Anderson grow from a small child attending Minniville Elementary School to a young man attending Gar-Field
Senior High School, a school that Anderson would never forget.
When Aaron began attending Gar-Field High School, he was 14-years-old, and he only stood in at 5 feet and 10 inches. For some reason, all of his friends turned their backs on him, laughing on him, and picking on him for being so thin and nerdy. Not only did this affect Aaron's self- esteem level, but it also affected his grades.
His parents began to become worried about Aaron's mental health. What had gone wrong? Well after voluminous trips to different psychologists, they couldn't find the answer to their problems. And then one day, their question was answered:
Aaron hit a growth spurt. During that summer, Aaron grew an amazing five inches -- not only vertically, but horizontally, too! That sophmore year, he went in at 6 feet and 5 inches, weighing in over 200 pounds! He immediately gained popularity, and his self-esteem -- and grades -- skyrocketed back to normal.
But this wasn't enough for him. He wanted to go out and do something with his size and height. So he joined the wrestling team, thinking that was the most logical place to use his strength. And week after joining the wrestling team, all of the local schools began noticing him. He was a powerhouse, defeating all of his opponents within one minute.
And Aaron didn't stop growing! He was 6 feet 8 inches by the time he got out of high school, and when he was out of school, he had a perfect record of 100-0 in wrestling! Everyone was amazed, and everyone wanted to know him. But Aaron knew that it couldn't stop right here. He wanted to take it to the next level.
So he did. He joined a small indepedent organization called the East Coast Wrestling Association. At first he jobbed his matches, opening up the show with a few losses. But he begged the owner to give him a few rant segments and a decent angle. The owner succumbed, and the rest, as they say, is history. Aaron dominated everyone in the league, drawing crowds from everywhere just to see him. He quickly became the Atlantic Champion -- the equivalent to a World
Champion -- and he was noticed. Noticed by (the story starts with my first real league.) Wrestler's Manager Name: None yet Manager's Description: None yet, damnit! Will your manager interfere? I TOLD YOU I DON'T HAVE A MANAGER! AARON ANDERSON'S CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT ROLE PLAY: [SCENE: The scene opens up in a small gym in an unknown location. As the camera begins to zoom in, one man is seen doing push-ups in the corner of the gym. The camera zooms in further, and simultaneously the man stands up, wiping his hands and face off with a white towel.
The man is wearing a black shirt with a purple diamond on the front, black sweatpants, and black and white Nikes. After taking a sip of his bottled water, the man begins to speak.]
AA - The name's Aaron Anderson. Some people call me "Adamant," which simply means that I'm unbreakable, or impenetrable. And you know what? I believe every bit of that.
Now some of you might go on and already label me a conceited egomaniac, but in the end, I'll prove that's wrong. I don't go out and call victory; I go out there and I earn it. I'm not going to go out there and *demand* respect; I'm going to go out there and *get* respect.
[Aaron takes another sip of water.] AA - The difference between those self-centered jackasses and me is that I don't go around disrespecting everyone and then start whining when everyone pounds me into the ground. When the self-centered lose a match, they try to find every possible excuse to make it seem as if their loss was excused; when I lose, I will admit that my opponent was the better man. But it's rare that I lose, so I don't get into that frame of mind.
[Aaron flashes us a smile, before taking another sip of water.] AA - Wondering about my past? Well, my past is simple: I was the best wrestler in the state of Virginia my Sophmore, Junior and
Senior year. I never lost a match, so I went to the state finals
*every* year, and I won it *every* year. I'm not a man of false claims; I'm a man of crudentials. I'm a paragon of preeminence, and it's not because I talk a good game; it's because
I wrestle a good game.
I can systematically take an opponent out of the match *without* submission maneuvers. To me, submission wrestling is boring. That is why I choose the power wrestling and brawling side of wrestling;
I choose the flow of the match. And when you're over three hundreds pounds, it's pretty easy to control the flow of the match.
You musn't forget it: it's not cockiness; it's proven fact. What
I have done in the past *might* not have any say in what I do in the future, but I'll tell you this: you had better not look over what I did before, because what I did before will walk all over you in the ring. Why?
[Aaron takes a sip of the water.] AA - Because I'm adamant. [Fade.]
