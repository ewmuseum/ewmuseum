I'm trying to start a new fed, and I need to know if the card I wrote is good enough to start a fed with me writing the cards, if I should hire someone else, or if I should just use a simulator. I'd like as many responses as possible.
[Camera pans around the arena. The PFW theme song blasts over the PA. Pyrotecnics shoot out around the arena. Long, loud cheers erupt from the fans. Signs flood the packed arena. The camera pans around the arena again, and stops on Johnny Farnsworth, PFW head commentator, who stands in the middle of the ring. Johnny wears a gray, three-piece suit. He shoes and undershirt are black. His brown hair is combed back, and he has a microphone with him. He motions to the crowd to quiet down.]
Johnny Farnsworth: Hello, and welcome to another edition of Monday Night
Wrestling! [Crowd pops big time.] Johnny Farnsworth: We've got a great show lined up for you tonight. Rob Van
Dam makes his PFW debut against fellow ECW jumper Justin Credible, tag team champs, The Warriors, have an announcedment to make for next month's pay-per-view, Left Out, and World Champion Mike Fairview will take on Don
Dra...
["When Worlds Collide" by POWERMAN 5000 suddenly plays over the PA. The crowd immediately pops. Out from the back, Mike Fairview makes his way into the arena. He wears his usual black tights, boots, and pads and his customary white vest. He carries the PFW Heavyweight Championship with him down to ringside. The PFW Belt is thrown into the ring as Mike Fairview rolls in himself. He grabs the belt and climbs to the second turnbuckle. As his hands raise, the pop gets louder. He hops off and turns to Johnny
Farnsworth.] Johnny Farnsworth: Nice of you to join me, Mr. Fa... [Mike Fairview grabs the microphone from Johnny Farnsworth. Johnny Farnsworth gets a worried look on his face as Mike Fairview stares at him. Fairview then smiles, and Farnsworth's look changes to that of a releive fellow. The crowd starts chanting "Fairview, Fairview", and Mike takes a second to rejoice in the chants of his followers. He waits for them to die down before talking.]
Mike Fairview: As you can tell, I've been on a bit of a roll here in PFW. I went through Dave Harley, The Masked Scorpion, and, just last week, Justin
Credible. [Crowd boos these competitors.] Now, tonight I have to face Don
Dranion. Dranion, I haven't heard much about you except you've won the
All-Japan Pro Wrestling World Title. That might not mean much to this crowd.
[Waves hand around the arena.] But I know what it takes to wrestle in Japan. You've got to have some pretty good skill to even compete with those guys. So, since it's quite obvious you have just an ounce of toughness in you, why don't we make our match tonight a _LADDER_ match? And, because I am the
World Champion, I expect an answer right now...
[Johnny Farnsworth is given the microphone from Mike Fairview.] Johnny Farnsworth: Let's see if... ["Don Dranion" by Green Day blasts over the PA. The crowd boos just because he is Mike Fairview's opponent. Don Dranion steps out from behind the curtain dressed in street clothes: khakis, dress shoes, and a AJPW shirt.
He's of obvious Italian descent, with slick black hair and eyes. He has a microphone in his right hand.]
Don Dranion: Mr. PFW Champion wants a ladder match? That's it? Just a little itsy, bitsy, teeny, weeny _ladder_ match? How about I up the ante? Let's make this bad boy memorable. Let's add a fifteen-foot steel cage to the mix.
Are you up to that, Mike? Mike Fairview [Grabbing the microphone from Farnsworth.]: Your damn right
I'm up to that. The first person to grab the belt and exit the cage is theh winner.[Points to Dranion.] I'll see you tonight.[Crowd pops.]
[Mike Fairview throws the microphone back to Johnny Farnsworth. He grabs it excitedly.] Johnny Farnsworth: There you have it folks! Fairview versus Dranion in a ladder-cage match! [Camera fades out. Clips our shown from the last pay-per-view, Mob Rule. The
Gambler pins Carl Joseph with his finisher, a moonsault, Lucked Out. New
Television Champion, Lightning, hits his opponent, Takao Omori, with a
Powerbomb through a table. Omori is then shown hitting a Super T-bone suplex. The last clip shows Lightning pinning Omori with a tornado DDT. The last set of clips shown are of the Mike Fairview/Captain Canuck match. It shows Captain Canuck dominating most of the early match with various holds and suplexes. In the end, however, Fairview's running powerbomb, the
Fairview Bomb, ends the match in Mike's favor. Camera fades out.] Voice: PFW Monday Night Wrestling is brought to you by: Square, makers of
Final Fantasy VIII, Skittles: Taste the rainbow, and by the new Volkswagon
Turbo: Drive the new element Turbonium. [Camera fades in.] Johnny Farnsworth: Welcome back. Let's get right to the first match. ["Walk" by Kligore blasts over the PA. Crowd pops for the arrival of recent signee Rob Van Dam. Van Dam pushes away the curtain. His ring attire includes colorful tights and top. He wears black boots, pads, and long gloves. As he walks down to the ring he points to himself and slaps hands with various fans. Absent is his usual ECW manager Bill Alfonso. Van Dam rolls into the ring and points to himself again. The crowd pops.]
["Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck" by Grinspoon blasts over the PA. The crowd boos Justin Credible. Credible walks down to the ring in denim shorts, a "Hardcore Icon" shirt, and his usual kendo stick. He argues with fans on his walk down to the ring. He climbs up the black ringsteps and walks through the ropes. He gets in the face of Rob Van Dam, and the two have a stand-off. Referee Paul Smith seperates the two into their respective corners.]
Ring Announcer: Introducing first, fighting out of Battle Creek, Michigan, he weighs in at two hundred thirty-seven pounds and stands at six feet tall...ROB VAN DAM!!!
[Crowd pops.] Ring Announcer: His opponent, fighting out of New York City, New York, he weighs in at two hundred twenty-five pounds and stands at six feet, two inches tall...JUSTIN CREDIBLE!!!
[Crowd boos and jeers.] Johnny Farnsworth: This looks to be like a classic. [Both men circle around the ring.] Van Dam and Credible lock together. Van Dam grabs the Hardcore
Icon in a headlock. [Van Dam squeezes down.] Credible pushes Van Dam into the ropes. Here comes Van Dam. Ducks the clothesline. Comes back off the ropes again, but Credible stops him with a back body drop. Credible stomps on Van Dam. Van Dam stumbles up. Credible bounces off the ropes and hits his opponent with a big clothesline.
[Justin Credible jumps on the ropes and taunts the crowd in his patented fashion. The crowd boos him. A fan can be heard yelling, "F(!* YOU!"
Credible jumps off the ropes and turns around only to be met by a now standing Rob Van Dam.]
Johnny Farnsworth: Van Dam knocks down Justin Credible with a big right hand. Van Dam with the advantage now. He lifts up Credible. Brings him back down with a quality snap mare. Immediately locks on a reverse chinlock.
[Referee checks for a chokehold. Credible grabs at Van Dam's hair. Referee throws Credible's hand off. Van Dam lets go and hits Credible with a swift kick to the back.]
Johnny Farnsworth: What a kick! Van Dam runs off the ropes, somersaults, and senton splash on Justin. That was a picture perfect senton splash from RVD.
[Crowd pops.] The crowd really appreciated that. Van Dam goes for the pin. [Referee counts.] Two count from referee Paul Smith. Both men up, Van Dam hits Credible with a beautiful spinning heel kick that sends him crashing to the canvas. Credible quickly back up, however. Credible big rights and lefts send Mr. PPV to the corner. [Credible whips him across.] Van Dam whipped acrossed. Credible charges in, but RVD slips out! Van Dam with rights and lefts of his own. He sets up Credible on the top turnbuckle.
[RVD climbs the top turnbuckle. He hits Justin Credible with a beautiful hurricanrana. Crowd pops.]
Johnny Farnsworth: Beautiful move from the self-proclaimed "Whole F'n Show." Van Dam climbing the turnbuckle again. Looks like he's going for that frog splash of his. Van Dam flies off, and...nails him with it! He's going for a pin. [Referee counts.] Credible kicks out at two. Van Dam quickly up.
Credible struggles. Van Dam hits a suplex. [A fan throws Rob Van Dam a chair.] Van Dam grabs the chair. He throws it at Credible...Credible ducks
RVD's kick. RVD ducks a chair shot. Credible slides under a clothesline, and hits RVD a top the head with that steel chair.
[Crowd "ohs" the authority of the chair shot. Van Dam twitches.] Johnny Farnsworth: Holy s#^(! What a chair shot from the "Hardcore Icon!" That one was heard throughout the building. Van Dam appears to be out cold. Credible back on the offensive. Credible finds his kendo stick. Lifts up Rob
Van Dam and whacks him across the head with it! He beats on Van Dam's limp body with it. [Credible throws the kendo stick back to the corner.] Credible pulls RVD's hair to get him on his feet. Credible goes for a clothesline, but Van Dam catches him in a waistlock. Quickly into a schoolboy! [Referee counts.] One count; Credible quickly out. Both men quickly up. Quick staredown. Collar-elbow-tieup. Credible into a go-behind. Ducks an elbow.
Ducks another. Throws RVD over his head in a German suplex! But RVD lands on his feet! RVD grabs the chair. Throws it over to Credible. Credible ducks again, dropping the chair at the same time. Van Dam turns around, and is hit with That's Incredible. THAT'S INCREDIBLE!!! [Referee counts.]
One...two...three! ["Snap Your Fingers, Snap Your Neck" by Grinspoon blast over the PA. Justin
Credible taunts the crowd.]
Ring Announcer: The winner of the match, in fifteen minutes and twenty-four seconds...JUSTIN CREDIBLE!!!
[Camera fades out to a picture of the tag team champions, The Warriors.] Johnny Farnsworth: Up next, tag team champs, The Warriors, have something to say about next month's pay-per-view.
[Camera fades out to a picture of Mike Fairview and Don Dranion.] Johnny Farnsworth: Plus, the world title match between Mike Fairview and Don
Dranion! Stay tuned!
Voice: This portion of Monday Night Wrestling is brought to you by: DayQuil: The itchy, sneezy, so you can stay up and work medicince, Man on the Moon: the life story of Andy Kaufman. Starring Jim Carey and Jerry Lawler, and finally, Frito-Lay: Can't eat just one.
Johnny Farnsworth: Welcome back to Monday Night Wrestling on ESPN. If your just joining us, you missed Justin Credible beat Rob Van Dam in a great match. But next...
["Warrior Power" by Monster Magnet blasts over the PA. Out walk tag team champions The Warriors. They wear their wrestling attire, black tights with fire flaming from the bottom. Black boots an elbow pads. Warrior One stands a few inches taller than Warrior Two and is better built. The two walk down to the ring with the tag straps and Warrior Two carries a microphone in hand.]
Johnny Farnsworth: I guess it's right now... Warrior Two: We've been here in the PFW since the beginning. We've held these [Points to his tag title.]longer than anyone else. Because of this we've become a tad bored here in the PFW. So here's the deal. Next week on

The last two standing in the battle royal will go on to take us at Left Out. So all of you chumps back there [Points to the back.] get ready for your invitation...if you're ready! Hahahahahahahahaha!
Johnny Farnsworth: After the break will be Mike Fairview and Don Dranion in a steel cage, with a ladder erected, and a belt hanging fifteen feet in the air!
[Camera fades out.] Voice: This portion of PFW Monday Night Wrestling is brought to you by: PFW: Sound Tracks Volume 1. Listen to all the music of your favorite PFW wrestlers, Skittles: Taste the rainbow, and by ESPN: The worldwide leaders in sports.
[Camera fades in. The arena crew is just finishing the cage setup. Camera pans over to Johnny Farnsworth.]
Johnny Farnsworth: It's the time you've all been waiting for. Fairview takes on Dranion in a cage, with a ladder erected in the middle of the ring. This match could cost one of these competitors their career. A steel cage is dangerous enough, but with the addition of a ladder?! Things could get ugly.
