HIP FLASH SHOW!!!

31 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HIP FLASH SHOW - 10/30 Hello everyone, I am Jack Kennet, the person in charge of hosting all the highlight shows, as well as these interview shows. We're only 1 week away from the inaugural card. There are reports that most of the tickets are sold, so we are expecting a large crowd. We will start with The Demolitionist, who is going to be facing Mason Kidd on the card. DEMOLITIONIST <Demolitionist is standing in a dark alley with a flickering neon light in the background.You can not see what he is wearing but you can hear his voice> "MASON KIDD!!!We meet at the first card HIP has ever put together, but that doesn't matter!!What matters is,are you prepared for what is going to be labeled as a massacre?? Are you prepared to have your flesh and guts ripped out of your now deceased body because of the beating I will inflict on you? And then, BANG!!!!DOOMSDAY DECAPITATOR!!!Your whole life will be shattered into little pieces as I shatter your lifeless body into the mat!!! Mason Kidd,if I were you I would reconsider your career because at the Inaugural Incident nothing will be left you accept your signature from the match signing!!!!" <The camera fades as Demolitionist walks deeper into the darkness> Now that we got those words from the Demolitionist, it is only fitting that we hear from his opponent, Mason Kidd. MASON KIDD <The flash opens with the evil smile of a Jack-o-lantern filling the screen, lit by the flickering light of a candle. As the scene widens, a human face, painted in the image of a skull can be seen in the background illuminated by the glow from the pumpkin. Only the face of the erry unknown figure can be seen. Suddenly, the voice of Mason Kidd echoes forth from the demon...> "Halloween...the day of the dead is coming...a day to embrace the dark...a day to walk in the shadows...a day to honor what mankind fears...trick or treat...yes, yes... trick or treat!" "Demolitionist, your day too is coming." (shaking his head with a smile now on his face) "You unfortunate fool!" (now grimacing and raising a fist) "Unending pain and misery is all that is in store for you!" (lowering his fist, but still grimacing in pain, his voice deepens) "You my friend will come to understand what fear really...what it feels like to fear the dark, what it feels like to have to constantly look over your shoulder, where sleeping is your only escape." (looking down now) "Mason Kidd has come to HIP. 'Every mother's nightmare' is now here." (Looking up again) "Demolitionist...welcome to my nightmare!!!" (the figure quickly strikes down with both fists, crushing the jack-o-lantern, extinguishing the light, causing the scene to go black) "Trick or treat... yes, yes...Trick or treat Demolitionist..." (The scene ends with a hideous laugh cutting through the darkness) Well, that will be an interesting match between the Demolitionist and The Mason Kidd. Both seem to be evenly matched, with the only major difference being "The Family" which Mason Kidd is a part of. It is hard to tell whether or notthey will interject themselves. Speaking of "The Family", here are some comments from the other two thirds; the team of Pain & Hate, along with their manager, Maxwell Gold. PAIN & HATE <The scene opens in Jim Thorpe, PA, ringside in the empty auditorium where thr HIP inaugural event will soon be e. Standing ringside are Maxwell Gold, flanked by the team of Marcus Larouche and Larry Watson> GOLD: Fans of HIP, in just over one week wrestling history will be made. Not that it is the inaugural HIP premier, but it is the premier of wrestling's greatest compulation of athletes..."The Family". an organization that will rule all of wrestling. No wrestler or title will be safe. Flanking me are two members of "The Family", The Cajun Crippler Marcus Larouche and Dirty Larry Watson who make up the team of Pain and Hate. Larouche: Wrestling history...too bad it can't take place in the great state of Louisiana though. You dumb Yankees better appreciate it for a new age in wrestling is dawning. The age of "The Family" and it's values has finally come. Many men will fall to us on November 6th. Careers may come to an end. Welcome HIP to the new age, welcome to our family. Watson: I have only two things to say today. First to all the tag teams in the elimination tournament, take notice boys because one of the best tag teams to come around the bend are standing right here. We're not looking to beat you guys up, we're lookin to kick your stickin teeth in. Chairs, chains, garbage cans, semi trucks, inside the ring, outside the ring, even in your momma's house, it's all fair game. Secondly, to all those frickin Yankees there in Pennsylvania, me and the Crippler are planning on stopping by and beating up on the Phillies and Eagles, we figure evryone else does so why shouldn't we!!! Gold: So on November 6th you stupid people, the very foundations of wrestling will be shaken when Maxwell Gold, "Every mother's nightmare" Mason Kidd, and the team of "Pain and Hate" take to the ring. And just remember this, friends and money come and go... Gold, Larouche, and Watson: But "The Family" is forever!!!! The team of Watson and Larouche are obviously not expected to be crowd favorites when we get to Glen Thorpe. There is also another team that will not be heavily supported, the team of Texan Tough. TEXAN TOUGH <The team of Texan Tough is sitting on a hay bale, their arms crossed. Both are wearing blue jeans, t-shirts, and black cowboy hats. Jed spits a large wad of chewing tobacco from his mouth and speaks.> There is going to be an 8 team tag match next week that we are taking part in. It is being held way up in Pennsylvania, and do not think for a second that we are going to go up there to lose. We are focused, and we are ready. Seven other teams are going to be involved, so luckily for a few there is a good chance we may not get a chance to manhandle their sorry asses. But whoever is the last remaining team from the 7 had better be wary, because we will win, no matter the way. It will be interesting to see which team will win it. Here is another team involved who say they will, the Timewarp Twins. THE TIMEWARP TWINS <Retro and 8-track are sitting in a 57 Chevy listening to some 70's music and singing along. Attached to the drivers side sun visor is a replica of a disco ball. Both men are wearing polyester shirts and pants. Retro turns off the music to the chagrin of his partner, and starts to speak> Hey there to all our great fans out there. I am Retro, my main man who is sitting with me is 8-track, and we are the Timewarp Twins. We are going to bein an 8 way tag match nexyt week, and we plan on being the victors. Both of us are fun, cool, and dancing fools, but we are going to turn our action up a notch, and do what we have to do. Sorry for the fact that we have to be serious, but we have to do it to win the match. And speaking of serious, the other teams will be seriously stunned when we nail them with the "blast from the past". But for all of the fans, we will give them a special treat: after the match, you will see us groove to the music after we have grooved our way to victory. Now that we have heard from some of the participants of the 8 team elimination match, it is time to hear from the man who will be wrestling the former boxer Kurt Keller on the show; the Silencer THE SILENCER <The camera pans in on The Silencer. He is sitting in a chair wearing a black suit, shoes, tie, sunglasses, white shirt, and a black hat in his hands. Buddy Guy plays in the background> My first match in HIP is going to be against some guy who couldn't hack it as a boxer. Well come this Thursday night he's going to find he can't hack it as a wrestler either. I've fought boxers, karate experts, wrestlers and street fighters and I've never backed down from anyone. What I'm really looking forward to in this match is the fact that you are about my size, although nowhere near my ability I'm sure. Once I get through this first piece of garbage I'll work on the rest. One thing is for sure... I will show all of HIP why I am the top contender for the Heavyweight Title... and you will be SILENCED! This man obviously is not to impressed with Keller's accolades in the boxing world. But Kurt Keller does not seem impressed with The Silencer. KURT KELLER <Kurt Keller is standing in front of a shelf. There are many pictures of him boxing, some boxing trunks with his name stitched in them, and a pair of boxing gloves. He lifts up the gloves and puts them over his large hands. He stares at them and smiles.> I have worked hard and far to get where I am today. I was a top notch boxer, but I wanted a challange, so I picked wrestling. But I said I wanted a challange, not the Silencer. Maybe he is a little bit of a challange. According to his reputation, he will stoop to any level to win. Hell, he would probably admit it. But I will be smart enough to avoid that. And I hope that he is smart enough to realize that he will be the loser of the contest, because I hope he does not have false hopes. When I finish him, he will need a doctor, a dentist, and a lot of prayer. Because while he may silence some people, I will KO him into early retirement. This Keller guy has a lot of additude, and can run his mouth. But he needs to have some skill to back that up, or he will probably be silenced. There is a man who nobody has been able to silence. That man is A.J. Awesome. A.J. AWESOME <The setting is a nightclub somewhere around Sacramento, CA. Obscured in a dim corner are a row of tables. At the far end is A.J. He lights a candle that is on the table, and pulls a small mirror out of his vest pocket. He looks into it using the candlelight to see himself. He smiles as he puts the mirror back into his pocket and blows out the candle.> I just love being in places like this. All the attention goes to me, because after all, I am always the Awesomist one in the house. That will not change when I head up to Glen Thorpe next week. Everybody else are nowhere as awesome as me. That goes double for that unfit, uncleansly, untalented bum "Madtown" Moe. Hell, he is even from Wisconsin, which makes him the dumbest. I mean face it: Their cheese stinks, their sports teams stink, and we will see if his wrestling stinks just as much as his armpits do. But no matter the answer, he will one way or another find himself in the "Awesomizer", and find himself with a loss. <A.J. looks to the entrance door, and sees a cluster of young blond ladies coming in. He sprays some Banaka into his mouth, and casually struts to them> Boy, is this guy confident or what? But we will have to see if he can parlay his confidence into victory. The following wrestler plans on parlaying his brawling skills into victory, the Alleyway Rumbler ALLEYWAY RUMBLER <The Alleyway Rumbler is standing against the brick wall of a downtown store. It is completely covered in graffitti, and some of the brick is chipped. He smiles as a homeless man approaches him for money. He then punches the guy square in the ribs, doubling the man over. He throws the man into a nearby alley, and follows him in. The shadows cast show him ramming the mans head into the wall repeatedly before he laughs and walks out of the alley.> That poor man was just the start of my wrath. Next week, the Top Rope Master will be added, and I will make my HIP terrorization from there. I do not care if what he can do when he gets to the top, because he will not have an opportunity to get there. The only place that he will be going to is to the canvas after I use him as a punching bag. And when I put him down, he will not get up until the paramedics are sent in. The rumbler was short, and to the point. He does not seem to think that he will have a problem with him, but the Top Rope Master thinks otherwise. TOP ROPE MASTER <The Top Rope Master is standing on a ladder, with gym mats surrounding it below. He leaps off, and performs a perfect shooting-star press. He then goes up again, and comes down with a legdrop. Finally, he rests, grabbing a towel & sitting on a bench.> Next week I finally get a shot at the big-time, and a shot to prove myself for all of the people out there who do not think I can do it. There are going to be tons of great fans out there, and I hope that they will be on my side when I face the Alleyway Rumbler. I will admit that he will be real hard to beat, especially if he lives up to his namesake. But I have been sparring extra hard, and when I get in the ring with him, I plan on going to the top and making sure that he, and nobody else, underestimates me. <He climbs the ladder once again, and comes off with a frogsplash. Just as he hits the mat, the segment ends.> That is all that we have heard from our wrestlers, but tune in for our Monday show. I hope that all of those who did not get aired are on that card. 1997, High Impact productions
