RSPWF's Hottest Free Agent: "The Showstopper" Simon Lebec

6 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["The Showstopper" Simon Lebec continues to scroll through the cradle of human idiocy that is "RSPWF". He stops, and begins reading a message before replying]  On 2 Oct 1998, MAGCOLBY wrote:  > > I'd plug a solidly worthwhile fed called UEW, but since Creed doesn't exist > in Lebec's world, it might be pointless.. :grins: > > Creed > > > Well, I can plug UEW too... but now that everyone knows how well Creed is > doing... afterall, he has bragged to half the damn world about how well he did > in his debut... I would guess that the common thought is... if he can succeed, > then _anyone_ can. > > > Bottom line is this, Lebec, if you are all that, come to UEW and prove it. > Afterall, if Creed can succeed, surely you can right? Or can you... come find > out why don't ya? > > > (Colby throws down the UEW gauntlet for the second time in as many days.) > > > UEW where the big boys quit playing around and get down to business. Lebec: As much as I'd love to join your little orgy that it UEW, where you flamers "get down to business" with each other, the fact of the matter is quite simple: I ain't got nothin' to prove to ya. You or that Creed wannabe. The Stuff That Dreams Are Made Of is above that. When I say that I'm a free agent, I'm lookin' for talent, not Jivy hacks and gay parades. Sorry dingleberry, but my search continues. [Camera fades]
