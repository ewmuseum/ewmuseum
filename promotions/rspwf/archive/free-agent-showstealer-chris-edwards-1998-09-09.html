[Free Agent] - ["Showstealer" Chris Edwards]

9 September 1998

Well, this is my first post here on the RSPWF. [name withheld] told me about this place, and since I'm looking for a federation to sign up in, and I saw about a dozen other free agent ads, including some annoying ones, I thought ah, what the heck. So I guess, here's my first roleplay. Anyone interested, e-mail me.
Also, any feedback would be nice. -Chris [SCENE: Chris Edwards Home.] [TIME: Midnight.] [The camera shot opens to inside the beautiful and magnificent home of the "Showstealer" Chris Edwards. We see nothing but a blue leather couch, a brown oak table with a coaster on it, and a shiny glass table near the television set, with the legs sitting on a newly installed blue carpet.]
[The camera then pulls up to find none other than Chris Edwards sitting on the blue leather couch wearing a Las Vegas Open 24 Hours a day T-Shirt, a pair of regular Blue Jeans, along with his short white socks. Glaring at the camera not moving at all, he speaks.]
"SS" CE: Wrestling ... The newest force has arrived. He's none other then the most exciting and RADIC-AL man on this planet!
The man who just steals the show, Chris Edwards. Sure, maybe I'm only a rookie. But I've got the pipes, [Shawn rolls down his sleeve and flexs showing his fairly big bicep. He then continues.] The equipment which I cannot show at this moment... [Chuckles.] And all-around talent worked into one human being.
[Chris places his feet up on the glass table, and continues.] "SS" CE: No one, shall ever come close to me when it comes to talent and ability! No one, shall ever come close to me when it comes to speed or agili-TY... But most importantly no one will be stealing the show ...
From _ME_. I am the show! I am the man that the people want to watch! I am everything you've ever imagined all worked into one and fellas ...
The "Showstealer" is coming ... To steal everything from YOU. [Chris chuckles as he picks up the television remote next to him. He presses the power button as you can slightly hear the noise. We then fade to black.]
