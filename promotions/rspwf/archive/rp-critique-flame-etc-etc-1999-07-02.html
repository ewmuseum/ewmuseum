OOC: Just bored and wanted to see how bad of a rper I really am. And just in case if any of you fed heads are wondering...no, I'm not looking for a fed.
Read and enjoy...hopefully. ;) ======================================== [Fade in...] [Scene: The camera fades into a shot of the exterior of the MIA Headquarters in beautiful Downtown Los Angeles. It then fades into a shot of the nameplate on an oak-polished door. Blurred beyond recognition, the camera zooms out and refocuses, the letters on the gold-plated hallmark clearing and becoming visible, reading "Juan Vasquez". The door is opened, where we hear this familiar voice sitting behind the screen of a computer...]
Voice: Goddamnit, connect damn you!!! [The camera swings around, where we see, much to our surprise(okay, maybe not...) El Cholo! Yes, the undisputed king of beers, the numero uno lord of the alcohol, el rey de cerveza, the Duke of Drunk, the Earl of Heineken, that guy that beat God's Tears last night, one half of the greatest tag team champions in SWf history, the man who's got a bunch of nicknames to describe himself in this description :) , El Cholo. Dressed in casual Friday(everyday is casual
Friday at the MIA!) shirt and baggy pants. He seems to be bored out of his mind waiting for the computer to connect on this slow as hell modem.]
JV: S*beep*, how long does it take to connect to the f*beep*in' super information highway!?
[Finally, the weird connection sounds die down and this familiar little jingle plays...] J: Welcome! [Cholo sits back into his chair, smiling.] JV: Finally! J: You've got mail! JV: Coo. [Cholo points the mouse over to the mailbox. He clicks on it and suddenly another voice picks up.]
J: Good bye! [Cholo stares at the screen in disbelief.] JV: What the hell was that!? [The screen reads.] "You have been logged off for inactivity. You have spent 1 minutes online. Comeback soon!" [The camera shifts back over to Cholo who looks on in disbelief.] JV: Mannn...how the f*beep* is anyone gonna Yahoo! when this crap keeps on happening!? [The door suddenly can be heard swinging open. In steps the other half of our should-be tag team champions, the under-utilized and less charismatic one himself, Chico!]
EL: Sup sup? [Chico has opted to go for the street thug look today, complete with unbuttoned baseball jersey over a "wife-beater" tanktop and below the knee black shorts.
He grabs a chair from the side of the door and takes a seat beside his tag team partner.] JV: Tryin' to get online, but it's mo' f*beep*ed up then the tag team division's booking. [Chico bumps Cholo out of the way to take a gander at the problem. He then stares at Cholo and points furiously at the screen.]
EL: There ya go! That's the f*beep*ing problem... JV: What? EL: You're on AOL 2.5!!! [Cholo shrugs his shoulders.] JV: What the hell does that mean? EL: I dunno, but I guess all those AOL 4.0 commercials mean that it's a bad thing. Try 4.0.
JV: Aight... [The camera fades out. White letters bleed into the black screen. It reads
"Five minutes later..."]
EL: What the hell they mean I only know two moves!? JV: No foo'....that's Fury they talking about. EL: Man, I knew the internet was f*beep*ed up. Everyone knows he only got ONE move. JV: Oh yeah. [Cholo starts typing(actually, he's pounding one finger on the keyboard and slowly writing out URLs.) on the keyboard.]
JV: Maybe they got somethin' on No Mercy. EL: Like someone actually would waste their time making webpages devoted to No
Mercy. [On the computer screen, the camera focuses on something in the upper left-hand corner of the Yahoo! search results.]
Search Result  Found 238687 web pages for No Mercy SWF [Cholo turns and looks at Chico with an annoyed expression on his face.] EL: Well, I could be wrong...heh. [Cholo clicks on one of the webpage links. A window pops up and starts loading.] JV: What does that say? "The Shrine of Simon Murdock"!? Oh...my...god. [The camera cuts to a shot of Simon Murdock flexing his biceps admist a backdrop wallpaper design with Murdock's face and little red hearts all around it.]
JV: I think I'm gonna be sick. EL: Close the window, close the window!!! [Cholo frantically clicks on the mouse as the webpage is immediately closed. The Killaz breathe a sigh of relief. Cholo once again begins to type clumsily.] JV: Let's go to a chat. Maybe they asses know somethin'. [The camera focuses on the computer screen, as a small window opens. On the side, various names enter and exit the chat room. The Killaz spend a few minutes reading the conversation.]
OnlineHost: *** You are in "SWF Chat Room 1" *** DeAdMortis: SHHH IS THE BEST!!!!!!!!!!!!!! cHolzGrL: Shh is hella lame. Viento3213: If anyone has pics of Kirsten naked, im me. Please review your terms of service at keyword: TOS xGTearzx: El Cholo sucks d****!!!!!!!!!!1 xGTearzx: El Cholo sucks d****!!!!!!!!!!!! [Cut to a shot of El Cholo with his mouth agap.] JV: What the f*beep*!?!?!? Ah man, we got a punter? EL: No, Pho deleted it when he was downloading the Pokemon prog. JV: Damn. [Cut back to the chat room.] cHolzGrL: Cholo does not suck!!! xGTearzx: God's Tears kicked his ass!!! Why else did he pin him like 50 times? JagedEge: It was on purpose stupid. Bachelor69: where the hell's Tom Pho!? xGTearzx: Who's Mr. Meteorite 666? MIAsux: your momma. cHolzGrL: lol JagedEge: hahahahha pAgAnCOP: I heard Mr. Meteorite #667 was Kip Douglass making his retunr!!! MIBShhh: mad hatter sucks now. cHolzGrL: Cholo owns you all!!!!
