Kyle Moon-Roleplay-(For Critique)

15 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(...A forest comes in to picture slowly and fades away just as slowly, it comes back into picture but from a diffrent point of view, now, a man standing in a large open area is seen, he is just taking a drag of a cigarette and drops it, it shows the cigarette drop in slow motion and a close up...as it falls to the ground and the dry leaves, you see a leaf, an old leaf, brown in color and crisp in touch, but as the eyes can see, you know what is bound to happen, the leaf catches ablaze and spreads just as quickly, it then shows the man again walking away...he has a unusually large grin on his face as he moves torward the camera...and then....alomost simoltanously as the man leaves the screen the forest explodes....you see a gasoline holder fly out and instantly you know it was planned, the camera spans around looking for the man who left...but he is no where in site...and when you hear a car start up...the camera starts running, moving in and out the maze of trees and sees the Ford Bronco speeding off, the camera zooms in on the liscence plate: DarKSide. And the camera is left in the dust....
........the camera comes in to focus at a small diner...the door opens and in walks in Kyle Moon, instantly everyone in the diner greets the man...Kyle
Moon...he says nothing but walks to the register as the man in charge asks him what he wants...he simply places a dollar on the counter as the man is puzzled...he then realizes that he wants coffee, he goes and gets it and brings him the small cup of steaming coffee. The man warns him of the hot content but he still remains silent, he walks over to the corner table and sits down with his back tunred from everyone, he then pulls out a small tablet and begins writing.....)
Kyle Moon: Tony Houston, the man that shall meet his doom, the man that shall meet me in the ring this Trauma, as I sit here and think of things to come, I wonder, what am I in this for? Not the money, for I have that already. Is it to fill the empty hole inside me that consumes me? Is it to run from the pain that
I have tied to forget my entire life? All the questions have been asked before, and yet I never get any answers, I only try to keep busy and not think about these things, and this upcoming Truama I shall be busy, I shall be placed in a cage with the none other Tony Houston, he calls him self Mr. Hard-Core, well, we shall just see how hardcore this man really is, does he actually think that
Kyle Moon can be taken down? I have proven myself to no one, nor do I feel like i have to, for who are these people, they have no rights to my personal life, but this man Tony Houston has insulted the Darkside, and for all fakes out there who think they actually know what the darkside really is, I hope we never meet in the ring for you would not want to know, for the horror of the darkside is more than any can comprehend, nor would they want too, for the darkside shall consume and then destroy, killing all living organisms who stand in it's path, just like Tony Houston, who's fate shall intervene in his personal dreams, this man shall meet Kyle Moon in a one on one match in a cage, with a weapon of his choice, but that means that I shall enter in this match with a weapon as well, and everyone should know as of what weapon Kyle Moon shall be bringing, a baseball bat, the only true tag team partner in my book, but in
Tony Houston's....that's a whole new story. But a story that needs to be told, in a book of some sort, so that I can become what I have always wanted to be, an author, for Tony Houston, I shgall write a whole new chapter in the book f
Tony Houston, and it shal be the final chapter of your life, this truam I plan on ending your life and getting rid of you, for you my freind are nothing but a pure annoyance, and I don't plan on being any one's baby sitter, so Tony
Houston shall be out of everyone's hair once and for all, "The Cold Calculator" shall come to an end, it's button's pushed one to many times, and I bet that calculator runs on solar energy my freind, and guess what, the only light that night shall be a full moon, Kyle Moon is on the rise...and that means a bad moon is on the rise as well. Trauma shall be your final resting spot, for the great Tony Houston can not stand the pressures of Kyle Moon, for the pressure's of Kyle Moon are too great for any man, no matter what the stature or what belt the hold, for they don't matter, nor do they belts...unless I hold it, and yes
Mark Underwood, or who else shall hold it when I get my shot, that title shall become in to the hands of the Darkside and shall forever stay there, never leaving the shadows of death only to go to the arena or house show or whatever the occasion to defend it...and then, I shall defend it succesfully.
(...Kyle Moon motions for a refill as a waitress strolls over and pours it for him...and walks back away, Kyle Moon returns to his tablet...)
Kyle Moon: The sun setting represents a new time for some, it represents a time in which they can forget the past and overcome what has happened allowing them time to think on what they have done, and move on with their lives, bur for the seldom, the choose to dwell on the past so they are able to lkearn from thier mistakes and never make thsoe same mistakes over again, and that is what I do,
I am the seldom that dwell on the past, not only will I dwell on the past, but
I shall dwell on your weaknesses Tony Houston, for you have many, for starters, your cockiness, it rules your words and actions in such a manner you can't control, it is almost like you can't stop it, can you? I don't think so, you see there is so many flaws in your system that are so easy to penetrate, and I shall! You can't stop your cockiness, and I wonder what can you stop. Can you stop me? Danny Edwards was the first to fall victim to the Bad Moon Rising, and you shall be next, a Bad Moon is Rising just for Tony Houston, and the bell tolls for thee, you shall be escorted out of the arean personally by a cpouple of ambulence workers and your very own body bag, the calculator shall calculate no more, your very moves shall be your last, you breaths now shall be the ending of your life, and if some how you come out of this match with your life, you will be in the hospital I garuantee, for if I win, everyone in DSS shall be allowed in the cage, armed with thier very own weapon of thier choice, and then shall continue the beating that I started for 5 straight minutes...you shall have no help, for the cage shall be locked, and there shall be no way you survive, Tony, you know this is your end, itthat was written in the celestial books of time, and you can not change that. Only I would be able to change , and I don't plan on it, the only thing planned is to put you in your place. And
I will complete my task. Tony Houston, your reign as Hardcore Champ was ended by me, and not to forget Perfect Pain, you see, when I first came in to this fed, I talked to Perfect, and he told me he was going up against the Hardcore
Champ for the belt, I laughed when he told me who you were, and I still laugh at the mention of your name, but you Tony, I know what you do as you react to the name of Kyle Moon, your blood begins to boil, your face turns red in anger and you can't stand it, for you know that the very man you shall be going up against on Trauma is the man to blame for you not having that precoius belt you called "yours" a short while ago. And I know that makes you as mad as can be, and I find that funny...no one can concentrate when they are so mad, just another one of your flaws I talked about, and you know it's true, and I shall zero in on that and buckle down and put the man that hates the mention of my name, and I laugh some more, the hatred is consuming your body, I know it, with every word I make you mad, you can't control it and you never will, it's somethinginside you that makes this happen, it makes you....tick. And as the grains of sand begin to fall in the hour glass of your career, you can do nothing to stop it, your ending is coming soon...and you know it as well.
Kyle Moon: Tony Houston...don't make it hardr than it has to be, you and I know as well that it's inevitable, your defeat is at hand and the demise of Tony
Houston is rising, don't run from the truth, it shall only come back to haunt you, that's the cowardly way out...to run, and I hope that isn't what you do...run. I have had many men to do that when I defeat them, they run off and hide, I just hope you aren't like that, for I shall be enraged, why? Because I hate it when some of the biggest trash talkers come out, think they own wrestling, get beat...and then, what do they do? They scatter like a baby, no
Tony Houston, you shall not be like that, for I shall leave you no legs to run away with, for the brutal things soon to come shall have your legs shipped to you in a box...if you live, or shall we ship you in a box to your mother? She would be thrilled to have a delivery only to find that her dear son is in that box, what a shame for me to ruin a poor man's soul when that soul deserves to live when he has so much more to accomplish, but Tony Houston, you asked for it when you bad mouthed the name DSS, and now you must reap the consequences of your actions, and that consequence is....death! The ultimate sacrifice...and I am willing to give your body as a sacrifice for the Darkside...you shall no darkness when you step in the ring with Kyle Moon. Night has fallen and a new day break has arrived, and Tony, your time is over. And when you and me reach that ring and I am waitning for you to come down that aisle, your music blaring or what not, you climb in that ring with all the confidence in the world, but when that bell rings and I look up into your eyes, that is when you shall know fear, if you are scared now then you haven't seen nothing yet, my eyes will look up, the cold stare of a heartless man looking deep with in your soul, that is when you will know, that is when you will realize that it would have been a good day to stay home, Tony, your death simply awaits you. No more playing with chair's, none of that, on Trauma, we shall settle this and end this once and for all, only the strongest shall survive this time, my baseball bat and I shall overcome you as an obsticle and prove that DSS overpowers SoS, and I think Tony already knows this, prepare for end of time....there's a Bad Moon
Rising!!!!
(...Kyle Moon stands up as all eyes are upon him, he leaves a dollar tip and leaves the diner as the bell rings on his way out, he jumps in his Ford Bronco and speeds off.....
......At Kyle Moon's house.....he is sitting down at his personal bar, you can tell he is quite tied and he is feeling the gash on his head from the hit from the cane....fades to black...)
