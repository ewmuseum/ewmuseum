Bored? Critique this... It's something to do, at least...

10 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In.] [There he is—Ryan Delaney. His knees are pulled up to his chest, his head resting in the valley created.
Delaney seems upset, depressed even. His eyes look red, as if he's been crying. His arms look brown, as if dried blood is clinging to them. His nose is white, and bleeding from both nostrils. He seems to be slightly rocking back and forth, like something has taken over him.]
RD: I feel the anger _growing_--the hate and pain building block by block. I feel it all taking _over_.
I feel it all ruining what was once known as Ryan Delaney. I feel as if I'm _dying_, as if I never was, and as if I'll forever be. [Delaney's shirt is unseen, and his pants look the same as always—black. His hair is pulled back behind his ears, keeping it out of his face for once. Though with the rocking, his hair is slowly moving forward, and slowly covering his unshaven face.]
I feel _nothing_, but yet there is this voice in the darkness, _nagging_ at me to get off my ass, and do something. There is just this _one_ little voice when all the lights go off, telling me to _change_ before it's too late, telling me to take care of the problems and _stop_ running from them.
I _shot_ that voice last night. [A sick and twisted smile comes to the surface. Delaney doesn't stop rocking, as more of his black hair slides in front of his eyes. The blood from his nose has stopped, and is beginning to dry on his face.]
_Nothing_ is what it used to be. _No one_ seems to be who I thought they were. They've all changed. Why?
Because of this job… Because of how it's _changed_ me… [Delaney lets go of the death grip on his hands, and holds on right in front of him. It shakes uncontrollably. Delaney frowns, and resumes his rocking position. His distant stare can only be sorta seen from behind his hair now.]
I grow _colder_ every day. I lose my _sanity_ with each passing hour. The shaking… It won't stop. The headaches… They won't go away. Pot _doesn't_ help anymore, coke _never_ did, and heroine _never_ will.
The Beast… It's eating me alive. It's devouring all that I hold dear, and leaving me the _shell_ of a human.
It's caused me to become everything I _never_ wished to be. [Delaney sinks his head down in his bloody lap, hiding from the world. His last words… Only a faint murmur, picked up luckily by the EWWF microphone.]
It's time to _kill_ off the Beast, even if it means killing _myself_ in the process. [Fade Out.]
