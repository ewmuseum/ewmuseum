[IvCW]Baddest moffo's on the planet...[still looking for another fed to join]

11 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene] [nearly 2am] [Bloodsport and Roughouse are enjoying a nightly round in the club circuits. This time in the clubs of LA] Bloodsport: Damn, the place sure is packed tonight... [Bloodsport hands the guard a $100 bill. The guard accepts it, then looks up and realizes who the two men are. The guard hastily returns the bill, lifts up the velvet rope and ushers both men inside.]
Roughouse: Damn...I didn't know people knew that much on the West Coast. I mean, we rarely pulled hits for the bosses down here. You know, with them having beef with the bosses from Nevada and all. But I guess we still rule this b****
Bloodsport: I told you my friend..we're the baddest moffo's on the planet. [Bloodsport and Roughouse take a seat inside the noisy club. Bloodsport stands up as one of his former contractor's approach him]
Fat Tony: Hey, Bloodsport... [Kisses him on both cheeks] Fat Tony: [In a heavy Italian accent] How you been eh? It's been such a longa' a time since you've been down here to the West Coast. You ain't here to pull a hit right? You ain't here to whack your old friend Fat Tony, right???
Bloodsport: Of course I'm not here to whack you. Why, you share the same name of the Mafia
Boss on The Simpsons, I wouldn't dare whack a man of that stature...
Fat Tony: [Heavy Italian accent] Smartass...Let's go to somewhere more quieter eh? I hear youse two are wrestling nowadays..is this correct what I hear?
Bloodsport: Yup.. [Bloodsport and Fat Tony head over to a secluded section of the club where armed body guards stand protecting a door. Bloodsport realizes the Roughouse is still at the table in the noisy part of the club...]
[Bloodsport goes to where they were sitting to find Roughouse with three lapdancers] Bloodsport: Roughouse, you idiot. We got's to go. I got a Mob boss wanting me to go in his room. I don't think that sounds all too wholesome to.
Roughouse: It sounds kosher to me. Bloodsport: Remember that time I hooked you up with that fine girl that kept giving you the shaft? After I convinced her what a great guy you were. not only did she give you 'shaft,' she gave you some 24/7. You remember how you said you owe'n me one for that?
Roughouse: I gotta owe it to you right now!? Bloodsport: Yes...or else I blow Mr.Happy off...And then I put that damn tampon you keep carrying around in it's place!!
Roughouse: No!!!! Not the tampon!!! Alright..sorry ladies.. [Roughouse brushes off the lades and fixes his shirt as Bloodsport and Roughouse head over to Fat Tony's office] [They walk through the door as Tony ushers them in] Roughouse: Wow s*beep* this is a huge fricken' office! Bloodsport: Damnit Tony, this ain't no office, it's a miniature palace! Fat Tony: [Heavy Italian accent] Of course kids. When you run operations like these. You gotta have something to flaunt. You see, I'm still in good graces with the Bosses back East. Not like alot of the other upstarts running around. I pay my tributes each and every single damn week. I don't like to, but there are alot of people out there who have the patience of a rattlesnake. Once you've been in this as long as I have, you learn to pay up and shut up and run your business.
[The phone begins to ring as Tony, Roughouse and Bloodpsport sit down in front of the TV. Fat Tony sits down in a lush and plush recliner as Bloodsport and
Roughouse take seats on leather retro chairs]
Roughouse: [Feeling chair] Damn this is comfortable... Fat Tony: [Heavy Italian accent] So, tell me kids, what's up with this wrestling stuff now? I know alot of people have started to invest in this. I know a lot of bosses down in Philly have really gotten serious witha' this a stuffa'.
[Turns on tv to the (IvCW) Wrestling Channel] You a see what I'm a saying? This stuff is so popular nowadays you can make yourself rich exploiting it...
Bloodsport: Well..we came into the wrestling business.. Roughouse: Hold up..no offense Mr.Fat Tony, but you sure this place ain't bugged? Fat Tony: No...but why would it be bugged? Nobody suspects an old Italian man who's on the FBI Records under 12 different aliases, and has been known to run a crime syndicate in over 5 different states...
[Bloodsport and Roughouse look at eachother] Roughouse: I got this one..We started wrestling a while back because working as a Waste
Management's person just doesn't pay enough. We worked a few indie feds and hit the big time on a few separate occasions. But we're so damn skilled that we had to find a new home. And this new place is the IvCW. so, now we got a new gig here in the IvCW.
Fat Tony: Well, that's pretty much a damn good explanation for nearly 10 years of waste management and then a wrestling career.
Bloodsport: He's good at summarizing..hey, Fat Tony..where'd your heavy Italian accent go!? Fat Tony: My what..oh..oh..yea...sorry son..I mean..Bloodsport... [Heavy Italian accent] Well...what about that match youse two kid's have? Roughouse: [under his breath..] Damn old bastard almost blew it.. Fat Tony: [Heavy Italian accent] Don't think I didn't hear that punk..I'll pretend you didn't say that..this time.. [Bloodsport elbows Roughouse in the ribs] Roughouse: OOHHH S*BEEP* THAT HURT!!! Bloodsport: Good.. [Roughouse takes the tampon out and threatens Bloodsport with it] Bloodsport: Put that damn thing away... Bloodsport: Let's just talk about that damn match and get this s*beep* over with. Fat Tony: [Heavy Italian accent] Good idea.. Bloodsport: So, now it seems that me and Roughouse are the only two moffo's in this fed with the cahonas to say something. I think we deserve some award or something.
Now it seems as even our opponents don't got the balls to say something. I mean, if we instill so much fear in you two, tell us. So that we'll know we have to injure you two a lot more than what we planned on. Natural Disasters?
You two are nothing but Natural Jacka****. We're the only people in this fed who seem to be giving a damn, while you two are off somewhere in some corner of your locker room hiding.
Roughouse: I ought hit you both with a tampon for that. It's really dissapointing to see that the whole fed, especially you two are just so stupid you can't even give out an interview. I mean, it just makes the Prize of this place and us look bad. Just because all of you jerks have no skills doesn't mean you can't come out and admit that you all suck compared to our greatness. Natural
Diasater's..show us you have at least something. Show you have enough pride and self-esteem so as to direct one comment towards us you cowardly bastards.
Bloodsport: Damn, and if they still don't give out an interview by the time our match rolls around, I'll hit them with a tampon..
Roughouse: That's my gimmick... Bloodsport: Who cares!? Roughouse: I do.. Bloodsport: Ok, ok, I'm sorry, there you happy? Roughouse: Yes. Bloodsport: Back to what I was saying. Natural Born Losers, you two have a snowball's chance in hell. At least be men and come out to admit that. Because by the time the match rolls around, you won't have a chance to tell us how good we are. You two won't have a chance to bow down before us and worship the ground we walk on during the match. So, be nice morons and hope you don't get on our bad side..
[Scene:] [With that said..Bloodsport and Roughouse start watching TV with Fat Tony as the screen fades out...]
