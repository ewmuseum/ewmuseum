[RP CRITIQUE] "Body and Brains" Bobby Baines

20 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi guys. I haven't posted a roleplay for critique in quite a long time. Not with any of my characters. I used to post them once or twice every couple of months or so with Fang, but I haven't really been doing much posting at all on the board recently. Anyway, here is an RP I did for KNI (Kozak Newcomers
Invitational) a couple weeks ago, with my rookie character... "Body and Brains" Bobby Baines. He's basically still a character in the making, so I thought I'd get some feedback and some possible ideas for reform. I'd greatly appreciate any comments, both critical and positive.
Enjoy! ----- [We open up to a shot of a swimming pool. The sun shines on the rippling water as children gleefully splash and swim in it, throwing balls around and screaming "Marco," and "Polo," at the tops of their lungs.
Suddenly, a man wearing yellow trunks and a blue-and-yellow striped muscle shirt, a towel around his neck and a bullhorn in hand walks beside the pool, disrupting the peace.
The man with slick black hair and the yellow trunks is none other than the icon, the marquee man, the originator and the innovator, the god of wrestling... "Body and Brains" Bobby Baines!
He starts barking over the bullhorn...] "BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : ALL RIGHT! _EVERYBODY_ OUT! NOW! You, you, and especially that little fat kid!
Hey, fatty! [The little round child that Bobby is berating tries to swim for the edge of the pool, but is having a hard time. In fact, all he's really doing is breathing and floating. He looks at Bobby as 'Quadruple B' continues to talk to him. He slowly makes progress towards the edge.]
"BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : Fatty, when your parents tied a pork chop around your neck so that the dog would play with you, I'll bet you ate it! And
I'll bet you ate the chop, too!
[Baines cackles as the child, visibly upset, crawls over the edge of the pool. He's about to run away, but Bobby talks him into staying.] "BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : Hey, kid... I got something for ya. [The child's interest is piqued. He looks at the KNI participant, Bobby
Baines, expecting a gift.]
"BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : Turn around and I'll give it to ya... [The child turns his back to Bobby as Baines dips his towel into the pool. Baines twists the sopping towel out, but keeps a bit of water in it. Just enough water to make it heavy.]
"BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : You sure you want it, kid? You want the surprise present?
Fat kid: YES! GIMME GIMME GIMME! "BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : [Under his breath] Too easy... [Bobby snaps the towel back, and sends it flying forth, hitting the fat kid squarely on his plump rump! The kid yelps loudly and runs away, crying as
Baines laughs.
As soon as all the kids are out of sight, Bobby peels off his muscle shirt and dives into the pool. He swims for a bit, getting his hair wet, and then he returns to the edge of the pool, which he leans against. He gives a big grin to the camera, a Hollywood grin, and then begins to speak.]
"BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : Yeah, folks, it's the Fonz, live and in
Technicolor. You know, Kieran Erotikka, I was thinking about what you said about me last week... You know, how I _think_ I am the smartest and best looking guy in the game. How
I THINK that I am the best athlete in the Kay Enn Eye tournament... I just got to thinkin', well -- first I laughed at your stupidity -- but then I really thought about it. Really trying to decipher if I was the best looking person in the invitational. I came to a conclusion.
Yes. [Baines smirks, then brushes his hand through his hair.] "BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : ... and I thought some more. Am I really the smartest person in the invitational, I thought to myself. [cackles] Damn straight! Then I asked myself one more question... Am I the best athlete in the invitational. After about, well...
... one solitary milli-second of hard thought, I again found a one word answer... YES! Being that I was in a good mood, and I wasn't really busy, I thought some more. I thought... well, what if I was like those other punks out there? What if I wasn't the best athlete on this planet?
[Baines spits in the water, then continues.] "BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : It still wouldn't matter. You see, I don't need to be a great athlete. I don't need to be smart. Nope, I don't even need to be as damn sexy as I am... all I need to be is what I am by nature -- a MAN!
'Cause there's no way in hell I am gonna get beat by a little girl. No, Kieran, you are going to have to kill Bobby Baines to pin his muscular shoulders to the mat...
... and even then you better pray like _hell_ for a fast count! [Baines shakes his head, and bursts into laughter. Composing himself, he continues in his usual sarcastic and arrogant tone.]
"BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : But, I digress, 'cause that's not what the script says. See, the script says that I shouldn't waste my breath talkin'
about Ms. Erotikka. The script says that I should look past her... and if you look closely enough, you'll see that my script says her and her no good bum-for-a-husband Liam Rogan aren't worth the air time! _My_ script says I should spend some time addressing my possible opponents for the second round.
Who are they? The King of Rock and Roll and one of the world's greatest classical composers. Two musicians past their primes... past their peaks. In fact... dead. Just like their two wrestler counterparts. Devil Elvis...
You better believe that I am gonna be steppin' all _over_ your blue suede shoes, and when I'm done, I'm gonna shove those shoes straight up your ass.
'Cause face the facts, Elvis... You ain't nothin' but a HOUND DOG! [At this point Baines hoists himself onto the ledge on the side of the pool, climbing out. Just the same, he contiues his rant.]
"BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : And then there is Mr. Bach. Perhaps the most significant German songwriter in the world's history. A genius. The creator of the Brandenburg Concerto's. Simon... you are such a poor representation. You don't have half the brain or skill that Johann had. Now, I heard you speaking a while back about how this tournament was unfair! That's absolutely correct,
Bach. This tournament is VERY unfair . . .
. . . for you! [Baines grins, as he wraps a towel around his body.] "BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : 'Cause for the first time in your life, you are actually gonna have to FACE THE MUSIC!
But again, the script pissed me off! It doesn't matter to me who I _might_ be facing in the second round if someone from the first beats someone else from the first. That's just statistics. You want to figure them out, talk to a scientist. But hell,
I'm sure I'll find out soon enough after I put the GIRLS' shoulders to the mat for the count of three. Bach... Elivs...
[nods] ... it doesn't matter who you are. You don't have to step into the ring to get beaten by the man with the BODY _and_ the BRAINS. Chances are . .
. [Chuckles.] "BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES : . . . he's already beaten you. [As the camera fades to darkness, Baines tosses the towel down, bends over and picks up his shirt. He wipes his facde with it. Black.]
