USCW Flash Central 5/17/98 Pt. 3

19 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Storm w/Venus & Chris Carter VooDoo Spectre The Demolitionist -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Scene opens up at SWA head quarters. Storm, Venus and the rest of the SWA members Chris Carter, Annihilator, Poison, Ethan Maguire, Ron Matthews, Nick
Maverick, Jinx, Spoon, and "????" are gathered around to discuss the possibility of expanding to other federations.
Storm: With the UWF under and soon to be USCW under our control........ (knock at the door and Storm's secretary enters) Secretary: Excuse but Storm, someone from the USCW is here to see you. Storm: For christs sakes!! Tell the ass to bugger off. Secretary: I told him that but he mumbled something about an upcoming match with some guy named Punk.
Storm: Send the ass in. Cameraman: Storm, glad to see ya bud. Venus: Cut the crap ass wipe and get on with it. We haven't got all day. (Cameraman looks around the room and sees all SWA members staring at him) Cameraman: I didn't know there were so many of you. Carter: Well asshole, I guess you were wrong. (Cameraman looks across the room and sees a very familiar face from the USCW) Cameraman: Oh MY GOD!! What are you doing here? Poison: Excuse me there dick head, you are here to interview Storm. It is none of your business why he's here.
Cameraman: Are you kidding? When the USCW finds out he's here, he is going to be a marked man.
(Chris Carter gets up from the table and approaches the Cameraman) Carter: Now look it, the dear Poison asked you...no, she told you to forget about it and to get on with the business at hand.
(Carter reaches in to his pocket and pulls out 5 crisp $100 bills) Carter: See this? This is probably more money then you make in a month. So, if you keep your mouth shut, this little gift here is all yours. You will forget about what and who you saw here. GET IT?!!!!
Cameraman: And if I refuse? (Carter grabs the Cameraman by the throat) Carter: You won't! Cameraman: $500 sounds mighty good about now. Poison: I knew you would see it our way. Storm: Now, what the hell do you want? Cameraman: Well sir, I just wanted to get your thoughts on your match with
Atomik Punk and your up coming tag match against Dylan and Syske.
Storm: First let me address the Punk. You know, I have faced many jobbers in my career. But none of them have gotten under my skin the way this Punk character has. Oh I know he gloats about how he won our last encounter but really now, what the hell is there to brag about a disqualification victory? I suppose in his little world, its the only shiny moment. But his world is about to collapse. See Punk, you have gotten yourself in such a bind that your going to snap under the stress. On one hand you have got to tend with me......and right now after the screw job I got from the UWF, I'm not a happy camper. And secondly, take a look around this room. In here, you have got the toughest sons of bitches this sport has ever produced. What am I getting at? Well
Punk, as the saying in the SWA goes, when you face one of us.....you face us all. And I certainly don't like your chances against this army.
Cameraman: Is that a threat towards Atomik Punk? Venus: Its amazing you can breath and walk at the same time. Storm: Take it as you will but either way Atomik Punk is in for one hell of an ass kicking. And as for the two dinks Matthews and I face, just what the hell do those pricks know about tag-team wrestling? There is no other cohesive team in wrestling history as successful as Team SWA. Syske, well, he's just another wannabe. As for Dylan, I have already beaten this punk once, and the second time around ain't going to be much different then the first.
Venus: There! Now turn that damn camera off. You've got what you came for so now I suggest you take your pasty white ass and head out that door before somebody in this room takes that little camera of yours and shoves it up your ass!!
Cameraman: You know, you could be a tad nicer. Venus: And I could also shove my fist down your throat. Whats your point? Carter: Don't forget little man, spill one word of what you saw in here and
I'll spill your blood.
Storm: Guess it sucks to be you. Annihilator, show this freak the door. Cameraman: Now, no need for that. I'll find my way out. Venus: And don't forget that the SWA just isn't an attitude, its.... Entire group: 4 LIFE!!!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ::flashy lights go around blinding everyone with water spraying on the fans:: VoodooSpectre: I will destroy you all..... fight me if you dare... Don't you realize I am to powerful?. This time...I won't curse souls....this time...I will curse your bodies. You must understand that....nobody can
defeat me... you all are nothing. VooDoo is to powerful for you. And to show my power ::whispers a chant:: I will...use...an announcer....as my toy....::announcer's shirt starts to turn hot red...and then suddenly disentegrates into nothingness:: Now...Rocket....do you see what I could do to you? That was just a taste of my power...I have ample more...You will give up....You will not win....I mustn't take any more time of...fighting puny ones...like you...I demand..getting tougher opponents...to practice my...Bad Mojo on....And I am...aiming to get...a title....soon...very soon...So title holders...You must beware to...MWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAAAA!! And by the way...I am looking for a fight with Abyss... Iplan to make my mark in this fed...Abyss....You may be a good wrestler...But are you Good ENOUGH TO
BEAT ME!!! I challenge you Abyss...I hope...you are...a worthy...opponent... :: lights flash once again blinding everyone and VooDoo Spectre is no where to be seen:: -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (The demolitionist is in his gym doing sit-ups and finally he begins to speak) Demolitionist: Mike Adams, The "enforcer", we will see who the better man is. It is true that you have been out of action for a while and maybe you have been working out but all that doesn't matter. (does a sit up) What matters is what you can do in the ring. (Does a sit up) Mike, I have nothing against you but you will be defeated. The new Demolitionist is here and there is no turning back. (does a sit up) I will see you on Midweek Madness. Be prepared for a fight. That is all I have to say. See you later, Adams.
(The Demolitionist does more sit ups as the scene fades to black) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------
