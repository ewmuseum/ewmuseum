Please Critique My RP

28 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first RP i wrote for a new fed I'm in, I'd like you guys to critique it... The Biggest Man Of The WWW Is HERE! The scene is set at a BSW house show, on a cool October night. The moon is full...and so are the frips....
Announcer: Ladies and gentlemen, as soon as we have the remains of the
Spanish announcing table cleaned up, the matches will conti....
[At that moment, he is interrupted by the deafening sound of bagpipes through the arena, the theme of some yet unknown wrestler...
A spotlight shines on the entrance to the ramp... The crowd waits to see, who...or what will come out... Their question is soon answered... As a three foot tall midget steps into light??? He is wearing a kilt, dancing a little jig, and playing a small set of bagpipes he's carrying...
The crowd voices their OBVIOUS disapproval to this new wrestler] Heckler: YOU SUCK!!!! Heckler 2: I could wrestle you, you Mini Me wanna be!!!! Heckler 3: My DICK is bigger than he is!!! [ As rotten vegetables and small animals are being tossed at the little man on the ramp, a booming voice with a thick Scottish accent breaks through the music ]
Fatty McChubbs: Lay off da comic relief...I'm the one ya shuld be throwin' food at!!! [ Out into the light step fatty McChubbs, a VERY fat Scottish man, fully decked out with a kilt, knee socks, and a bucket of chicken(something every Scottish man has) ]
Fatty: Thass right boys and girls, afta a short stay in WOW, Fatty
McChubbs is now a BSW man!! [pausing to wave a chicken leg at the audience] But ahve come out here, becuss ahve got a few THINGS to say... Fist of all...Jackoff Josh...ya won't be worth my time on Wednesday.... But...ahll wrestle ya anyway, ah need a nice WARMUP in das league... [pausing to take yet ANOTHER bite of chicken, and continues to talk while chewing]Amph fwe mack afds aee!
[ The midget pauses from his jig, and looks at Fatty with an inquisitive look... Fatty just kicks him in the ass, and points at the bagpipes. The midget continues his 'thing', but not before flipping off Fatty as he turns back to the crowd ]
Fatty: [Finishing up his mouth of chicken] Ya don't need to ya to critique me good English Blarney Bob, kep playin' dem damn bagpipes! As ah I was sayin'... Jackolope Josh, ya'll see who gets jackhammered when ma fat sexy ass jackhammes ya little head.... And ahs long as ahm out here, ah might as well tell
Sucko Sam right naw...ah plan on gettin' that Heavyweight
Championship, whether it's on ya ass or some otha dumb ass...becuss ahm obviously the heaviest man here...Wall kids, thass all fa now...tune in next week, same Scottish time, same Scottish channel!! Ahnd if it's not Scottish, it's crap!!!!
[At this point, Fatty's music come back on, and he makes one final chicken wave at the crowd, picks up his little midget frien and exits the ramp. All that the crowd can wonder is...who the hell was that freak?]
