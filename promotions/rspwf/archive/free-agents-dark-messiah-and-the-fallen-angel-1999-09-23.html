Free Agents: Dark Messiah and the Fallen Angel

23 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Before we get to the wrestlers and the sample RP here's what I'm looking for in a federation:
The fed must be BOOKED, simmed feds need not apply. Either a mailing list (preferred) or a posting board (preferably HTML compatible if you go with this) for flashes. This is how my first two feds were and this is where I do the best, writing two or three approximately one page flashes a week rather than the one five page flash other places have expected, it also helps me to have other peoples flashes to play off of.
Feds with created wrestlers only and a minimum of goofiness like wrestlers named "Al Coholic" or "Masked Superstar Jobber". Also Kayfabe should be important in the fed, if someone can use the word jobber in there flash and still win a match don't bother responding to me.
A fed that is not "hard core" or "X-team". A small hard core division within the fed is one thing, and my wrestlers may participate in it, however
I'm not looking for a fed that is DEVOTED to such things.
Well written matches. Please, send a sample card or at least a match, or a link to one if you have it on a web page.
I want at least on of my characters involved in one match a week minimum. I once role-played my a** off for three weeks in a row without a match.
Needless to say that was no fun. If I role play that week I deserve a match that week. Speaking of my characters both of them should be allowed to have a singles career as well as their tag team one.
I want a fed that won't suddenly fold. Finally I want a fed where a role play about the length of the one included here is considered long enough that I don't get jobbed. I have a life and can not write five page role-plays, nor do I have the time to read ten to twenty five page role-plays.
E-mail Address: [detail withheld] Wrestler's Name: Dark Messiah Height: 7' 0" Weight: 450 lbs. Style: Power House/Brawler Alignment: Heel Description: A towering monster of a man he is capable of intimidating even the bravest with size and presence alone. He has long stringy black hair that often hangs over, and sometimes conceals, his face. His skin is pale, as if he rarely goes outside while the sun is in the sky and his gaze is direct and piercing with the fires of either madness or religious fervor burning in his eyes.
Ring Attire: Black tights, bare chested to reveal his chiseled upper body. Gimmick: A cult leader type, similar to the old Kevin Sulivan <sp?> or to
Raven when he had the Flock . . .
Entrance Attire: A black robe. Entrance Music: Leper Messiah by Metalica Ten Moves: 1: Interment/Dark Baptism, one of his signature moves he performs
Interment by applying a face claw, lifting his opponent, pivoting while his opponent is being held aloft by his face, and driving his opponent into the mat/floor. Dark Baptism is a variation he uses against those he finds too large to lift, he applies the face claw as with Interment and sweeps his opponents legs while pushing them backwards as hard as he can.
2: Revenant Choke Slam, his second signature move is a counter to an attempted pin. He grabs the poor soul in a choke hold and powers his way out from underneath. Once standing he delivers a massive chokeslam.
3: Short Arm Clothesline, usually used as a prelude to . . . 4: A Cobra Clutch style sleeper, he never really attempts to put his opponent out with this, he just holds it long enough to take a little fight out of them and then still maintaining the hold moves on to either . . .
5: A Cobra Clutch Slam, or . . . 6: An Atomic Drop, in either case he immediately breaks the hold afterwards
(and likely kicks his opponent a few times while he's down).
7: Bear Hug, like the Cobra Clutch this really isn't an attempt to subdue his opponent but just to slow him down by depriving his body of Oxygen, he maintains the hold for a while then does . . .
8: A Belly to Belly Suplex, he occasionally retains his grip and repeats the
Suplex three times ala Chris Benoit.
9: Power Bomb 10: Tombstone Pile Driver Finishing Hold: Judgment Day, he lifts his opponent into a Crucifix position and holds him up while spinning in a circle as if performing an airplane spin, then Power Bombs his now dizzy opponent.
**************************************************************************** **** Tag Team Partner Wrestler's Name: Fallen Angel Height: 6' 0" Weight: 200 lbs. Style: Luche Libre Alignment: Heel Description: Small compared to most wrestlers he has a wiry strength that compensates for his smaller stature. His face is usually emotionless as is his voice, when he does show emotion watch out. His face is made up in the style of Darth Maul, unlike most wrestlers he uses temporary tattoos instead of face paint so it doesn't come off during his matches (at least it's
ASSUMED their temporary with someone as twisted as him you never know). His dark hair is cut short to prevent his opponents pulling his hair.
Ring Attire: Black tights with red flames swirling around the legs. Gimmick: Dark Messiah's follower, his first disciple in any fed they enter. During Dark Messiah's almost sermon like flashes he often jumps in with interjections such as "They will burn in the flames of our glory!" or "Our enemies shall fall!" making the statement sound like an exclamation of
"Hallelujah" at a tent revival. Like Dark Messiah he has his own singles career as well as the tag team but Dark Messiah always accompanies him to the ring. Even when Dark Messiah doesn't physically interfere in the match
(he usually will) the Fallen Angel obviously fights better under Dark
Messiah's watchful eye then he does when Dark Messiah is absent, it seems he is dependent on Dark Messiah in some way.
Entrance Attire: A black leather jacket with black angel's wings attached to the back. Entrance Music: Leper Messiah by Metalica Ten Moves: 1: Huricanrana 2: Tilt-a-whirl Body Slam 3: Plancha (all variations) 4: Moonsault 5: Head Scissors Take Over 6: Irish Whip into the ring post (preferably after he or Dark Messiah has removed the turnbuckle pad) followed up by either . . .
7: a Hand Spring Elbow or . . . 8: a Bronco Buster 9: Missile Drop Kick 10: Tilt-a-whirl Back Breaker Finishing Hold: Judgment, a Corkscrew Moonsault off the top rope, usually set up by a Huricanrana
**************************************************************************** **** Sample Role Play: (The scene opens outside an old building down town, it's covered with graffiti, windows are broken out, it generally looks as if it should be condemned. An interviewer new to the promotion stands in front of the door.)
Interviewer: "Hello wrestling fans, I'm Jim Taylor. Earlier today I received an anonymous phone call telling me to come here today for the story of the Millennium. I'm not sure what's going on but hopefully we can find out inside."
(As Jim turns and knocks on the door he can barely be heard muttering under his breath . . .)
Jim: If this is someone's idea of a joke . . . (The door swings inward creaking on rusty hinges, in the doorway stands a six-foot man with the build of a cruiser weight. His face is covered in red and black markings similar to the Darth Maul character in the Phantom Menace and he wears a ceremonial black robe. He looks at Jim and the camera crew coldly and speaks in an emotionless voice.)
Fallen Angel: "Enter Jim Taylor. The Master has been expecting you." (Jim hesitantly steps across the threshold and begins to question the mysterious figure.) Jim: "Who are you?" Fallen Angel: "The name given to me by the Master is the Fallen Angel. Now come with me, the Master does not wish to be kept waiting."
(The Fallen Angel starts towards a door in the other side of the room. Jim and his camera crew follow him to the door where the cruiser weight knocks lightly and calls out in the same dead, emotionless voice . . .)
Fallen Angel: "Master, the man you sent for has come." (From within the room a deep confident voice calls out.) Dark Messiah: "Enter my Disciple." (The Fallen Angel opens the door revealing a room lit by flickering candles. On the far side kneels a massive figure clad in a ceremonial black robe similar to that worn by the Fallen Angel. He kneels before a blasphemous alter, most of which is concealed by the censors with one of those big blurry dots so as not to offend anyone with this broadcast, on the wall above the alter hangs a goats head.)
Jim: (Softly, in a mixture of horror and disgust) "Oh my God . . . " Dark Messiah (rising from his knees): "Do not speak His name here. This is
My domain, the domain of the Dark Messiah. I have called you here because
My Disciple and I are entering this federation and We intend to dominate.
We have the strength, We have the skill, We have the passion for GLORY!" Fallen Angel (interrupting, his voice filled with emotion for the first time): "They will burn in the flames of our glory!"
Dark Messiah: "Yes my Disciple. They will burn." (Growing more excited he begins shouting like a hellfire and brimstone preacher at a tent revival)
"Burn like the infidels they are! For I am He, the man who will bring about the new era! The skies will be rent with fire and the rivers will run with blood!"
Fallen Angel (interrupting again): "Our enemies will perish in the flames!" Dark Messiah: "Yes my disciple!" (He begins to pace rapidly in his excitement) "For only those who are my chosen people will be saved from the flames! Kneel down before me in worship! Give your soul over to ME and be saved! BOW TO THE MESSIAH!"
(As the Fallen Angel drops to his knees before his master and Jim Taylor looks on in horror the scene fades to black.)
