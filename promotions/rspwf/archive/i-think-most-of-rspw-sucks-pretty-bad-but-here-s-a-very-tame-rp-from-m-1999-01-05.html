I think most of RSPW sucks pretty bad but...here's a VERY TAME RP from me

5 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OOC: You RSPW guys...this is NOT a typical RP for me. I'm usually pretty off the wall. X-treme's gimmick is he is a junkie...addicted to Liquid Acid.
Now...to the rp! (EWWF Camera crews are at the apartment of X-treme. He is see sitting in his recliner with his EWWF Extreme title across his lap. X-treme reaches to his left and picks up a small vile of blue liquid, squirting 4 drops onto his tongue...)
X-treme---(Looking very much like he is walking on cloud 9) "Ahhhh. I've finally paid my dues. 1 year of extreme torture and I've finally done something in the EWWF. My torture isn't done yet though. This Extreme title that I have in my lap is what the EWWF is all about! FUCK the world title! This is EXTREME World Wrestling Federation.....EXTREME!!! I'm, gonna bring this belt back to prominence...back to where it should be.....the #1 belt in the EWWF!!! The World Champion is a paper champion....he comes and goes as he pleases. The Ironman Title is a joke and The Internet Title is rising with my main junkie Luni carrying it. But seems to me....an old man wants "his"
Extreme Title back. Who is this man? Farley fuckin' Lane. I man who CONSTANTLY ducked me while I wrestled as Assassin. A man who, when I came back to EWWF as
X-treme....PEOPLE ACTUALLY THOUGHT I WAS HIM! DOES THIS ANSWER YOUR FUCKIN'
QUESTION PEOPLE! I NAILED FARLEY FUCKIN' LANE IN THE BACK OF THE HEAD LAST
NIGHT! Now...why did people compare us? I haven't the foggiest. Farley was outrageous...as am I. But did Farley ever take LSD on live TV?!? Did he lose a match because he thought the ropes were turning into snakes...READY TO BITE HIM
IN THE ASS?!?! NO! Did he ever kill a dog because he thought it was FBK? NO, I don't think anyway. DID HE EVER SEE PINK FUCKIN' ELEPHANTS WITH HUGE DANGLING
NUTSACKS FLYING AROUND IN THE EWWF ARENA! NO!!!! DOES HE FUCK FAT SPANISH WOMEN
WITH HAIRY UPPER LIPS? MAYBE, BUT MOST LIKELY NOT! DOES HE TALK TO A MOUSE WHO
THINKS HE IS MOHAMMED ALI? HELL NO! DO KITCHEN APPLIANCES THREATEN TO KILL HIM?
NO!! DOES HE GET CHASED THROUGH DOWNTOWN PROVIDENCE BY FUCKIN' LIONS WITH HUMAN TORSO'S? I THINK NOT. (X-treme rolls his eyes in a "Jesus Fucking Christ" kind of way and begins to speak in a calm voice...)
X-treme--- "All Farley Lane ever did was cum all over some important EWWF official or something, big deal! I did an interview buck nekked, jerked off in the middle of a sentence and cummed on my own chin!! NOW WHO'S THE MAN? Wow, I do some fucked up shit when I trip. Anyway....Farley, you think you can take a long hiatus from EWWF and just come back and demand a title shot? Well, Mr. FBK, my good buddy is the NEW EWWF commish-naa and ummm....I dont think he'll sign that one buddy ol' pal. How about earning it? I dont care if you were undefeated for three months as Extreme champion!! You were only undefeated because you always ducked me!! Anyway, earn your shot and I'll have my man FBK call your man...Dan
"I used to be Original Gangsta, now I'm just retired" Falk!!"
(The affects of the LSD are now kicking in and X-treme is getting very hyper...) X-treme--- "Well, seems that the EWWF Old Wrestler home is free again. They all came back tonight. Immortal Doomsday came back. Who cares? I mean...what the hell has he done. Chris Rollins came back...again. Gee, I wonder if he'll wrestle one match and leave again? Probably seeing as Bobby can't help him win anymore. Scorp's back....for the 37th time. I wonder if he's gonna want his federation back?
Lets hope not, cuz god knows this place will go to shit again. Marc "Ironman" Spencer/Flame came back. Uhh, he came back a couple months ago for 3 minutes. How about a little longer this time? I thought you were some kind of "Ironman?" 3 minutes, ehh? Oh...and now Phantom is back. God I can't wait to trip with this guy. He cant wrestle a lick but damn...I WANNA TRIP WITH THIS SICK
BASTARD!!!." (X-treme is walking all around his house now, very, very antsy...) X-treme--- "Well....FBK. What can I say? Our plan was great. A couple weeks ago, me and you battled for the World title. We destroyed each other...I hit the Definition
Of Extreme on you....knocked you out....pinned you...and the lights went out.
GREAT PLAN! We had this little stable planned awhile ago. See FBK didn't want to lose his belt and he didn't want to make me look bad, SOOO...we came up with the plan of the lights going out. FBK keeps his strap...and I look like I get screwed! HA! FOOLS! FBK...your a fuckin' mastermind. Morlock...I've always wanted to work with you. I remember back when I was Assassin I was gonna join the South Syndicate Soldiers but our negotiations hit a snag. It's time to work together bro. Damn, remember when the whole EWWF thought I brought the UWO into the East? People are always accusing me of shit. Being Farley Lane...bringing in the UWO....oh well. Now....Simon Solkoff. I've had my on and offs with you.
When I burst onto the EWWF scene as Assassin, I targeted you...the World Champion. You ignored me up until I handcuffed you nekked to the guardrail with your brother and invaded your offices! After that...you wanted me to join the
HellRaisors but again...negotiations hit a snag and you left EWWF for the 1st time. Well, its time again Simon. Time to make this stable as strong as the
HellRaisors once were. Don't go running off and retiring again. This is gonna get huge. Well folks...sorry to disappoint you buy not planning this interview at the time of my drug peak but ehhh....you the fans can't drop in on Planet
Motherfucker all the time! Next time I promise some outrageous, off the wall, psychedelic, see shit that aint there and hear shit that aint there interview."
(X-treme gets up and grabs his phone. Fade as bright yellow, pink, blue and green "slime" flows from the top of the screen to the bottom.)
