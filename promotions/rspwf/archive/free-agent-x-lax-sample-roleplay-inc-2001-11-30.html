[Free Agent] X-Lax: Sample Roleplay inc.

30 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey.. My name is [name withheld]..I'm pretty new to the game was looking for some feds to try out. I'm in two already [IGA and AWP, which are fun to be in] but, looking for one more to try out. Below is a sample roleplay to look at [a critique would be nice as well.]
Anyway if you're interested in the character drop me a line at [detail withheld] . Thanks, Mark I have no sig therefore I have no life. ======================== [Camera cuts into a hotel room. We see double beds filling up most of the room. Towards the back is a sink with a huge wall mirror above it. Sitting in front of the mirror on a wooden chair is a young kid. We can only see part of his face from the reflection of the mirror as his long black hair covers his eyes. The kid is talking on the phone in a soft tone voice.]
Kid: I know..I know I got to get away from him. He's all I know. He's practically family.
[We can hear mumbling on the other side of the phone. As the kid sighs he slicks his hair back and we can now make out more facial features. Most notable is a scare under his right eye.]
Kid: It's not right? I know it's not. The things he makes me do are barely leagal. What am I supposed to do?
[Suddenly we hear a loud..... !!!SSSWOOOOSH!! ...as a toilet in an adjacent bathroom flushes. From out of that bathroom steps an overweight balding man. The man is dressed in what can best be described as a used car salesmen suit. The man sees the kid on the phone and...
!!!!SSSMACK!!! ...slaps him hard on the face. The man slams the phone down and grabs the kid by the throat.]
Man: WHO WAS THAT? WHO THE HELL YOU TALKING TO. [The man tightens the grip on the kids throat.] Man: WHO WAS THAT? HUH? WHO? Kid: I can't breath..please.. [The Kid gasps for air.] ..It was only a friend. It was only a friend. Let go. Bubba please. Bubba: Only a friend? I'm the only friend you got this world kid. I'm the only family you got. Now listen to me X-Lax I got you to where you are. I took you out of your world of depression. You owe me..you owe Bubba Jack
Erdman. [Bubba pushes X-Lax into a chair. X-lax just puts his head down and sighs.] X-Lax: I know. I'll do anything for you. Anything. BJE: That's what I want to hear now listen up. You got to get our stuff packed. We're moving. We're moving today.
X-Lax: Again? Where we headed to this time? BJE: I got us a job. We're working for IGA now. Big time..Big money. You know how Bubba Jack loves that money.
X-Lax: Wrestling? I'm barely trained. [Bubba Jack Erdman grabs X-Lax by the hair and gets nose to nose with him.] BJE: you'll do what I say when I say. Now get your bags packed..we're leaving. [Bubba Jack pulls a cigar from his pocket and leaves the room. X-lax is left to pack the bags. Camera fades.]
