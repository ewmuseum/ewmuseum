{RP Critique} Looking for some feedback or advice.

30 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Below is a rp I just did. Was wondering what people would think about it as I am pretty much trying out a character that I haven't used a whole lot. Would appreciate any opinions you might have or cnstructive critisism. Thanks.
[SCENE] The lights in the XBWL Arena fade to a dim setting and three different spotlights all focus on the ramp where all the wrestlers make their entrance on their way to the ring. "Spoonman" by SOUNDGARDEN begins to play over the speakers and the crowd all stands so they can get a good look at the wrestler making his way out from the locker room area. The people in the crowd are all holding up various signs supporting their favorite wrestlers or advertising their websites. There are scattered cheers and a lot of people booing as Prince
Prodigy walks out accompanied by his manager Craven Morehead. Craven Morehead is wearing his normal black Nike jogging suit and sunglasses, he has his long black hair pulled back into a ponytail and his goatee is neatly trimmed. Prince
Prodigy is wearing his usual wrestling attire which consists of dark green wrestling trunks, and matching knee pad and elbow pads. Prince has a knee brace on his right leg and is also wearing a black t-shirt with the arms and sides cut out, his long blond hair is wet and stringy and is pulled back into a ponytail and he has the normal blond stubble on his face like he hasn't shaved in a couple of days. Both men stop at the top of the ramp and look out at the crowd who is mostly booing them and throwing things at them. Prince gives them a smug look and raises both his arms above his head pointing at the ceiling which entices the fans to boo him even more. Craven Morehead gives the fans a screw you gesture and then both men make their way to the ring. Craven jumps up into the ring through the ropes on the side as Prince Prodigy walks up the ringsteps and steps into the ring through the middle and top ropes. Craven walks over and picks up a microphone that has been left on the mat and begins to speak.
[CRAVEN MOREHEAD] Welcome fans to the activities of the XBWL. I am Craven Morehead the greatest manager to grace the XBWL with his presence in a very long time. I also happen to manage the greatest wrestler in the XBWL. A wrestler who really needs no introduction. A wrestler who is destined for greatness here in the XBWL. A wrestler who will soon be the talk of everyone in the back because they will realize that he is indeed the greatest athlete in this pathetic league. Ladies and gentleman I present to you the one and the only, PRINCE PRODIGY!!!
[SCENE] Craven Morehead steps to the side and hands the microphone to Prince Prodigy. The two men exchange some words that no one else but them can hear and then
Prince begins to speak.
[PRINCE PRODIGY] Since my return here to the XBWL I have noticed a few very big changes. First of all my first stay in this league was very brief but it was still long enough for me to know how good the competition was back then. However, since my return
I have noticed that the competition seems to be much weaker than I remember.
The wrestlers in the league now don't seem to have the dedication that the wrestlers had before, and that's a bad thing. I plan on changing that and changing that quickly. There are certain guys in this league that I do respect, but there are very, very few of them. Now even though I respect those guys that does not mean that I will be friendly to them. Let me start off with one man that I do have respect for, a man that has accomplished quite a bit here in the
XBWL and a man that has a great reputation. The man I am referring to is the man once known as TNT but now goes by the name of Nick Stevenson. Nick is a man known as a hardcore son of a bitch and he is a big time competitor. He is a lot like me in the fact that he gets off on a good feud. He fears no man and looks at a loss to a decent wrestler as a learning experience not a set back. I am the same way, I fear no one and if I lose to a quality competitor then that's fine. Nwo I know for a fact that I am probably biting off more than I can chew but I want my first major feud here in the XBWl to be against Nick Stevenson.
Why would I want to start off against someone that is supposedly a guy that I respect? Well that's a simple thing to explain. Nick is a legend and by stepping into the ring against a legend I will gain valuable knowledge about wrestling and maybe even learn a thing or two. However, not only will I learn things in the ring but just by being in the ring with such an athlete it will draw attention to myself because I am basically a rookie trying to take on an established superstar. Now I'm not saying that I will definately beat Nick
Stevenson but I don't think I will lose to him either. No people will view this in different ways. Some people will say that by me saying I don't think I'm going to lose that I'm saying I'm going to win. They have a good arguement but what I really mean by that statement is that I am going in thinking that I will win but that may not be the case. I can accept defeat to Nick Stevenson if that happens but I will not go into a feud with him thinking I am a beaten man. You have to be confident in this business or you will be run out of it before you even get to really get into it. Now Nick I know you might be saying to yourself, why would I want to feud with a rookie? Why would I want to feud with someone who has not accomplished anything in this league while I have accomplished so much? Well the answer to that is simple as well. If you watch me in the ring you will see that I am a capable and talented wrestler and that
I will indeed be able to give you a run for your money. Not only that Nick but you are a smart man and you know as well as I do that this league is not where it used to be and it needs a jumpstart to get it back to the level of greatness that it once enjoyed. A feud between the two of us would be great for not only the two of us as I think it will put us both into the spotlight as two of the
XBWL biggest talents but it will also help give the league something that it is lacking and that's a decent feud with two decent people going at it. Right now the XBWl is full of lackluster wrestlers who only put forth as little effort as possible. Most feuds are so lopsided that you know exactly who will win the match every time they wrestle each other. It's a sad state the XBWL is in right now and only you and I can give this league what it needs. Think about it Nick, you know what I am saying is the truth and all I am asking is for you to agree to a series of matches after this months pay-per-view. I know that if we both work together we can put together one of the most memorable feuds in XBWL history and that we could eventually headling the pay-per-view for the start of the new millineum. Nick I have a ton of respect for you, I just hope you don't ruin that by side stepping me just because I am a rookie. I'm sure you know talent when you see it so step up to the plate and accept this invitation so we can put the XBWL back on top! Oh and Nick, when you accept because i know you will, I'm coming at you with everything I have and I will expect nothing less from you!
[SCENE] Prince Prodigy hands the microphone back to his manager Craven Morehead. [CRAVEN MOREHEAD] Nick we will be awaiting your response. [SCENE] Craven drops the microphone to the mat as he and Prince Prodigy exit the ring and make their way back to the locker room area.
