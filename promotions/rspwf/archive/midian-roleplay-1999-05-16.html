Midian Roleplay

16 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Midian is lying asleep on the bunk in his prison cell, he awoke and snapped to attention leaping out of the bunk and onto the floor and assumed a crouched position. <Midian> Where am I ? a retorical question as there is no-one here. <Midian> WHERE AM I ? he hears a whistling, a loud tinny whistle, it is his jailor. footsteps approach his cell and he hears a key in the lock. The key turns and the door swings open to reveal Shane McMahon standing in the doorwa y laughing. <Midian> What am I doing here ? <McMahon> Your career is locked away Dennis. I have the key to your career and I have ended it. (he laughs like a maniac as Midian clasps his hands to his ears and sinks to his knees) The alarm clock buzzes and Midian awakes, he is at home in his New York Apartment, he smells the waft of cooking waffles and rubs his rather slender stomach. Midian has a hunger but not just for the breakfast, also for success. This is going to be a new dawn for Midian in the wwf. Endorsements "GREAT JOB!
