RP Critique, Alex Martinez

6 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
well, every now and again, the masochist rises in me sufficiently to actually post an rp out here, and open myself up to being blasted.
Generally, no one ever comments on it, though I usually get about twenty offers to join feds, which is always cool. But, I kinda like this flash.
It was done for LWC, against "Sex Dandy" Ed Wazombles. and so, here it is [A dark soundstage, that slowly lights up, to reveal a very simple set. A beige colored room, dimly lit by low quality lights. A chair in the center of the room. And in that red metal folding chair, sits Alex Martinez, the Last American Badass. Dressed in what is typical attire for Martinez: black T-shirt, torn blue jeans, and his beloved (if somewhat blood stained) leather jacket, Martinez sits forward, hands clasped in his lap. His hair is loose today, rather than in its usual ponytail, and with his head leaned forward, his face is obscured by the wavy black strands. The sound of exhalation can be heard, before Martinez lifts up his head, and brushes away enough of his hair that his scarred face can be seen from behind the ebon veil. In his usual whispery, intense voice, the giant speaks.]
When I was in junior high, there was a group of about a half dozen high school aged kids who’d come around. They called themselves the Ninth
Street
Spiders. A street gang, of sorts. A bunch of young bullies preparing themselves for a life of crime. They came around twice a week, and demanded money, and beat the hell out of anyone who refused to give them their money. And, additionally, they beat the hell out of anyone who was different. Fat kids, skinny kids, ugly kids, pimpled kids…
Tall kids… [Martinez draws in his breath, and leans forward slightly, his hair once more cascading over his face. The breath is exhaled, and the behemoth continues.]
I got my butt kicked by them kids damn near every time they came around. I was tough, even back then, but I can’t say I’ve ever been tough enough to take out six guys all at the same time. I remember, one time, in particular though. See, this morning, I just decided I wouldn’t wait for them to jump me. I decided I’d take them out, as soon as they set foot on campus.
So, as soon as I saw them, I rushed ‘em, determined to show ‘em that I wasn’t gonna take their crap.
So, I got the hell beat out of me early that day. [The massive man’s shoulders shrug, almost uncaring about the events he is detailing.] But thing was, that beating was worse then usual. Not ‘cuz I attacked
‘em. But because I didn’t never go down. I refused to get on the ground, and play dead for ‘em. Every they hit me, I told ‘em to do it again. Every time they told me to give ‘em their money, I called ‘em a different curse word. Each and every time they tried to put me out, I let ‘em know that
I
was here to stay.
And I remember, sitting in the school nurses’ office, looking down, as blood dribbled from my nose to the ground, and made a puddle before my feet, what that nurse asked me. She said “why?” That nurse wanted to know why in the hell I didn’t just lay down, and spare myself the beating I took.
Wanted to know why I wouldn’t roll over, and play dead for ‘em. And, finally, after wiggling a loose tooth around with my tongue, I looked up at her, wiped some of the blood off from my lip, and I gave her the only answer I really could give her....
There wasn’t nothing else I could do. [Martinez pauses for a long, long time. His hair is brushed away entirely from his face, allowing the viewers to get a full view of the man’s face.
It is the face of a warrior. Dominated by a long, angry red scar, that runs diagonally across his face. Minor scars dot the rest of his features, on his forehead, cheeks, and neck. His nose is crooked, a result of numerous breakings, and “on the fly” settings. His eyes are a soft brown color, the shade of milk chocolate, but hide behind them a burning, furious intensity.
This is a man who has been down the road numerous times, a survivor, who has survived a life time of battles that would have broken lesser men.
Finally, after many minutes of silence, Martinez speaks again.] See, even back then, I was a fighter. Even at that young age, I knew that win, lose or draw, I couldn’t never back down from a fight. Something in my heart wouldn’t allow it. I been fighting a long time. And now, every time
I walk down the street, and someone recognizes me, every time I go to the doctor and get stitched up, every time a promoter looks at my medical bill, and every time my wife looks at bloodstained towels, I get asked the same question. Why?
I guess they just don’t understand. Sure, there’ve been times in wrestling when I coulda backed down, but that woulda been like taking a back seat to the Spiders. And that just ain’t an option for Alex Martinez. It ain’t worth it, if I don’t fight for it.
Maybe it’ll get me nothing more than a broken body, and a wheelchair. But it don’t matter. I been fighting so long, I don’t know any other way. And even if I did, I wouldn’t take that route. When its all said and done,
I
don’t want no one to remember anything about me, except for one thing, and that is, I was a fighter. All the titles I’ve won, all the men I’ve beaten, all the cheers I’ve heard, it don’t matter nearly as much as me staying true to what I am, and that’s a guy who fights for everything. Sure, there’s an easy way to do things, but I ain’t never been one for easy ways.
[Another pause, as Martinez looks down once more, hair sliding into its position as a mask over his marred features.]
Ed Wazombles... Sex Dandy, I hope you’re listening. I know ya think this is gonna be easy for ya. I know ya think that ‘cuz I’m hurt, I’ll just roll over for ya. I know that ya think this is gonna be a cake walk for ya.
Just remember who you’re dealing with though. Just remember what kinda man you got facing ya. Remember, ‘cuz see, soon, someone’s gonna ask you why.
They’re gonna wanna know why you’re in such bad shape. They’re gonna know why you’re in the ER. And Ed, ya can look ‘em square in the eye and say...
well, its real simple. See, I met Alex Martinez, and then, I got myself... BURNED! [Fade to black.]
