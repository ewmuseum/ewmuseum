RP Critique time! "Hatchetman" Billy Gnoll

30 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yes, ladies and gents of RSPWF, you get treated to a little spoiler. The debut of my character "Hatchetman" Billy Gnoll (in WILD) is coming up, and you get to have the first glimpse at this new fish in the pond.
As usual, all comments/critiques are appreciated, even if you're an SMP'er. This is a new guy and a new gimmick, so feel free to get harsh with your crits. *** [We fade in to motion as we move through the illustrious backstage area of WILD. The camera is bumping and jerking in an almost Blair
Witch'ish fashion (without the motion sickness, thank God) which leads us to understand that the camera operator is in motion himself. That, and his resounding footsteps are a dead giveaway. He speaks in a rushed tone as he jogs down the hall.]
CM: Good afternoon everyone, this is Cameron Mann, and I just received word that one of WILD's newest acquisitions, "Hatchetman" Billy Gnoll has finally arrived, and we're on our way to greet him right this
very-- [Without warning, poor Cameron Mann is pounced upon by a stocky ball of matted fur with the head of a monstrous jackal. Mann cries out in shock, falling onto his back with an audible thud. The camera follows him, and we're treated to a shot of the backstage hallway ceiling.]
CM: WAAHHHH!!! GOOD GOD! [Wow, ceiling tiles.] BG: HEE! I scared you, huh? [The jackal-like head comes back into view. We can see that it is indeed a mask, although it's frighteningly lifelike. The muzzle of the creature is covered in flaking grayish skin, and even has whiskers.
The black nose glistens as if wet, like a real dog's nose. The mouth is closed and the eyes are black, and even at this close range, there is no discernible way to see who is under there.]
BG: Sorry about that. Here, lemme help you up. CM: No, that's okay, I don't-WAAGH! [Cameron is hoisted to his feet, righting the camera as he comes up. Now instead of ceiling tiles or strange jackal creatures, we see a shot of the hallway.] BG: Down here! [The camera pans down, and we see the strange hairy jackal creature hunched over and looking up at the lens. He looks to be at most five foot ten, and is dressed in a black leather tunic/loincloth sort of getup. Oh, and that's in addition to his shabby-looking mossy green-brown fur. Over his back is what appears to be some sort of acoustic guitar, and a small hand-axe is on his belt.]
CM: What on God's green earth are you supposed to be? BG: I'm a Gnoll. CM: A Gnoll? BG: Yep. Think Troll, only smaller in stature, and a lot meaner. But I'm not all that mean, really. In fact, I'm a Bard! CM: ... [Gnoll raises a paw, brushing off Mann's silence.] BG: I know, I know. Don't let the looks fool you. I happen to be quite good at what I do. Now this little ditty is from my Greatest Hits
Compilation... [What the Hell? Where did that musical accompaniment come from? And why does it sound like a karaoke version of Billy Joel's "You May Be
Right"?] BG: It's named after my finisher, and I call it "You May Be Unconscious"! [Gnoll whips the guitar-like object from his back, and we now see it is a medieval-styled lute. He uses his paws to mock-play it as the song begins. This strange little creature...a Gnoll, if his word is worth anything...actually begins to -sing-. And the scary thing is, he's doing a very credible impression of Billy Joel...]
#Monday night I'll throw a suplex #Send your crippled ass back to your duplex #Come Tuesday night you come back for some more #Are you really that insane #Cause I dropped you on your brain #I'll do it again you whining little whore [Gnoll's rocking out with his Lute out, and Cameron Mann is capturing it all. We even hear him mutter under his breath "That does it. I've officially seen -everything-".]
#I never wrestled for the Combat Zone #But in the rings of WILD I stand alone #You've never seen another wrassler just like me #The Gnoll is here to stay #So you'd better run away #If not you're gonna end up R.I.P. [We hit the chorus, and Gnoll does a little dancing and mugging for the camera...well, as much mugging as a masked man can do. Don't argue with me over the fact that masked men indeed -do- commit a great deal of muggings. You know damn well what I meant.]
#You may be right #I may be crazy #And it looks like it's an asskicking you're looking for #I'll put out your lights #And steal your babies #You may be wrong but then again #You may be unconscious [The song fades out (Thank God!) and Gnoll swings his Lute back over his shoulder. He extends his arms, throwing his head back.]
BG: THANK YOU, MILWAUKEE!! [Cameron Mann barely has enough composure to keep filming, so he neglects to correct our little furry friend on where we actually are.
Gnoll wipes his furry brow with a paw, panting a little.] BG: Oh wow, that sounded great! You're beautiful, baby...what's your name again? [Baby?] CM: ...Umm, uhh...Toxic Shock. BG: Great! Great! CM: Yeah! You'll usually see me in one of those Biohazard suits. I'm not hard to miss. BG: Fantastic! Well listen. I've got to go get ready for my debut match. After I'm finished that I'll do a set for you. You know how to play the kazoo? You can sit in if you like.
CM: Yep, yep, whatever. Look for me after your match. I'll be the one in the Biohazard suit. Don't forget!
[Gnoll nods vigorously and slinks away down the hall.] CM: They really need to give me a raise. [With that, we fade out...] *** Praise be to [name withheld], who's giving me a chance to do my thing with this...character.
Now let's help me do this thing -right-. Fire away.
