[Free Agent] Fabulous Freddy Floyd Sample RP ]

24 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ F A B U L O U S F R E D D Y F L O Y D ] [ As the camera pans the sold old crowd of the Pepsi Arena in Albany New York,
The Lights dim, then slowly fade to complete blackness. As the crowd falls to a hush a single spotlight shines brightly onto the entrance ramp. Out from the darkness of the backstage area emerges Fabulous Freddy Floyd. Floyd steps into the light, and as he raises his hands into the air, Rhapsidy In Blue begins to play over the loudspeaker. He walks into the ring taking the microphone ]
Floyd: Ladies and Gentlemen, the day that you have all waited for has arrived. The day that greatest wrestler ever to set foot into this very ring has come back, and once again graced the wrestling tights. I do this for one reason, and for one reason only. That reason is my need to be the best. The hunger that burns inside of me is one that needs to be fed. Being the best ever is what I always have been. Staying the best ever is what I need to do, for my own sanity. Gone are the days when I step into the arena and the you can see the fear on the faces of these so called superstars of the squared circle.
Becasue latley they have seen me walk in with my buisness atire, and breifcase. Well today, when I stepped into this arena with my gym bag in hand, and the look, yes the look, the look that when I stepped out of the shower this morning and looked into the mirrior was gazing at me. I knew this was it, I could wait no longer. The time is now.
[ Floyd walks over the ropes and looks out at the fans who sit quietly and watch on ] Floyd: You see that, that right there is my reason for coming back. I hate each and everyone of you pukes, and the fact that your not booing at me right now is enough to make me scream. I mean, if you were inb my position looking at a bunch hilbilly, hehaw rejects, you would want to puke too.
[ The crowd lets out a huge chorus of boos, screaming, and they start throwing things at the ring ]
Floyd: Yes, Yes, that is what i need. That right there, oh yes, scream it to me my people, becasue wheather you like it or not, I am your champion, I am the one who comes out and puts on a show like no other, I am the one who has brought the world of wrestling this far. I am the one who like no other has taken chances that have payed off. And now, with all of that behind me, I am here to start a new generation. A generation of the "The Crucial". As soon as the time is right, and the right people have been selected, the crucial will go out, and take over which ever federation we so choose. Do not belive me now, but when it all comes to play you will be the ones who shiver, you will be the ones that run, you will be the oens that hide from our fury, but you, and everyone else will be caught, and Fabulous Freddy Floyd will be right back ontop of the E-wrestling world where I belong.
[ Floyd goes up to the second turnbuckle where he opens his arms almost as to enbrace the boos of the fans. He gets down and leaves the ringside area ]
