[SeGmEnT] Scott Jamison... Critique if ya got time

9 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Segment I did for L.A.W. Tell me what ya like... Tell me what ya hate... Just tell me! (Maximize for correct spacing...) ------------------------------------------------------------------------- [Fade in...] [We see a jam packed Los Angeles Wrestling house show... In the ring, two of the mid card stars are going at it in a fierce battle... The fans are getting a little bit restless, and are tireing. After a piledriver, and a standing moonsault, the masked wrestler gets the win. Both men clear the ring... just then, all the lights in the place cut... Complete silence... Complete darkness... Fog fills the arena, and the smell of moisture is building. The fans stir, and begin to scream in anticipation of what is to come... A voice over is heard...]
V/O - *_"Franchise"_*... [On the Jumbo-Tron, a clip of Scott Jamison disposing of Donovan Phoenix via
"Light's Out" is shown...]
V/O - *_"Golden Boy"_* [A clip is shown of Scott Jamison nailing Juan Garza with a spinning heel kick, knocking him near unconsciousness...]
V/O - *_"Show_Stopper"_* [A clip is shown of the Los Angeles Wrestling association Skyweight belt. The fans begin to cheer as they are all anticipating the much awaited arrival of the LAW's one and only Show Stopper, Scott Jamison.]
V/O - *_"Can you stop the reign?"_* ["Beautiful People" by Marilyn Manson begins to blare throughout the P.A. system... Blue, Yellow, and Red beams pan throughout the arena... Fireworks
_ERUPT_ and the lights come back on as Scott Jamison walks out from behind the curtain. Jamison has on a pair of khaki pants and a white polo shirt. Along side Jamison as always is the very lovely Miss Tammi Winters. Miss Winters has on the identical outfit... Jamison walks slowly down the isle... greeting his fans with high fives, signing a few autographs, shaking a few hands. He makes his way to the ring apron, hops up, and holds open the ropes for Miss Winters.
Both enter the ring, and walk around, smiling for what seems to be an endless amount of flashes from camera's. The ring announcer tosses Jamison a microphone, and Jamison begins to speak in that *_Cocky_* tone of voice we have grown to know and love...]
SSSJ - Chalk up _another_ win for The Show Stopper... [pop] Juan Garza put up one hell of a fight, but it seemed I was just a lil' too much for the Jumpin' bean to handle! Oh... and it sure didn't hurt that I got a little help from my _BEST_BUD_ in the whole world, the Los Angeles Wrestling's Skyweight Champion, Mr.
Eddie "I hit the wrong guy" Deegan! . Yes Yes Yes... you see, I reviewed a tape of my match with Mr. Garza, and I am not at all pleased with the tone of voice... or the content of the voice for that matter of Eddie Deegan. Mr. Deegan is obviously horribly jealous of not only my superior wrestling ability, but also my _stunning_ good looks. [Huge Pop...] Y'see, Deeg', my main man...
When ya' play with *_"Fire"_*... Ya' get *_"BURNED"_*! and let's just say that I am on FIRE right now in the LAW. I am the number one contender for 'yer Skyweight belt, and
I am gaining the much needed fan support, and the momentum that I am going to need to push me to the farthest limits that are necessary... and I will capture that Skyweight title. Oh yes,
I will... don't doubt me. [Huge pop for Jamison... he strolls around the ring a little bit, playing up to the crowd like he does so well... the speaks again...]
SSSJ - Chris Madison is in the house... Mr. Chris Madison from the MVW has decided to grace us, the peasants of the wrestling world, with his superiority, grace, and all around
_GODLINESS_. Hey Chris. Welcome to the LAW. My name's Scott... [Jamison grabs his crotch and spits on the floor, as if he were mocking the credibility of Mr. Madison... huge pop from the crowd, as Jamison continues...]
SSSJ - Listen up, chief... I don't care what rock you crawled out from under, you are in _MY_HOUSE_ now. I don't care if the all-might God himself sent you here on a personal mission to take me out, I am putting up a fight. I am going to put up a fight like you've never seen in the MVW... cause
I got what they don't... and other than a _GANG_ of talent... that is heart. I am here because I _want_ to be here. I don't get the big ass contracts like Big Fig, or The Movie Guy... I am here because I love what I do, and I am going to prove a point to the world that Scott Jamison _CAN_ and _WILL_ be the next Skyweight Champion of the LAW...
[Jamison takes a breather and calms down... He continues softer, and much more undere control than before...]
SSSJ - So, Madison... bring 'yer MVW flavor... and hope that Eddie Deegan whacks me from behins with a steel chair.. 'cause, frankly, that's the only shot 'ya got! But beware of one thing,
Mr. Deegan... I _VERY_ close friend of mine is in the process of making a deal with Mr. Barnes and the LAW... Will anything come of this? Perhaps... Perhaps not... but just be aware that
I have someone, other than Tammi, watching my back... [Jamison drops the microphone, and holds the ropes for Miss Winters to climb out of the ring. Jamison hops over, onto the floor... and the two exit the arena as suddenly as they had arrived...]
[Fade out...] `~*_ The Original_*~` Kydd Handler of -Scott Jamison [NEW, LAW, JPW, EULW, CNWL] -#1 Duane Gill Mark -Leader and Co-Founder of the DGFC (Duane Gill Fan Club) -"The People's Worst Nightmare..."
