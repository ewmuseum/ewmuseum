USCW Flash Central 5/5/98 Pt. 2

6 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Jacob Yoder The Marauders Kid Sharpe "The Superstar" Joey Maxx "The Machine" Ron Matthews -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- **Jacob Yoder is standing in front of some lockers with USCW interviewer, Jack Chance.** Chance: Nice showing in the tourney Jacob. How does it feel to come so close to winning the title, only for it to be yanked out of your reach?
Jacob: It feels like crap, Jack. I would have taken Viper if it wasn't for
Sinister. Viper is a fake champion who doesn't deserve his title.
Chance: What of Storm claming that you did not pin him in the ring at your last fight? Jacob: Let us take a look at the tape and see what happend. **The screen flips back to the Jacob Yoder/Storm match:** Dave: Del Rio turns around and is trying to get Jacob to release the Claw. Storm comes behind Jacob and hits him in the back. Jacob releases the Claw and now puts Storm in the Claw. Sinister falls to the mat and rolls out. He is outside the ring trying to recover. Jacob is signaling to the crowd. The
Crowd responds and yes!!!!! Jacob Yoder has hit the Butter Churner on Storm.
Storm is trying to release the hold but Jacob has lost all time and is just refusing to release the hold. Del Rio is asking Storm if he wants to give up but Storm refuses.
Lance: Del Rio has to stop this match. Storm has too much pride to give up. Storm's face is turning a deep red. Mary is on the outside of the ring, pleading for Jacob to release the hold, but it is too late for that, Jacob isn't hearing anyone. I think Storm has passed out and Del Rio is counting the
pin. 1....2.....3. We have a winner! **The screen flips and shows Jacob and Chance standing in front of the lockers again.** Jacob: As ye see, I hit the Butter Churner on ol' Storm and he passed out. He was laying in the middle of the ring, both shoulders on the mat, with me applying the Claw. The ref clearly counted the pin. He is beaten. I destroyed him like the English bully he is. He hast nothing to offer me by fighting him again. Storm is a loser, just like his stable. he can take his bar whore, he calls Venus, and just run back to the UWF.
Chance: Those are strong words, Jacob! Jacob: Like I said Storm, Ye must get in line with thee other losers that wantest to fight me. Thou art a mid carder, and Thee will always be Jacob
Yoder's Jezabel in the ring.
Chance: That is all the time we have today, thanks for watching. -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- ( Scene is " The Marauder " Chris Evan's house. They are watching television, and are sweating after long training. They are watching what else? But Game #7 between Edmonton and Colorado. When Chris Evans sees the cameraman there, he turns off the TV. )
Chris : Edmonton 4, Colorado 0. HAHAHAHA. Chris : Back to business anyway. No one can train that long. That was my break. But believe me when I say that I am more ready for Viper than ever. And I still have a month to train. Viper, before you start talking about Yoder and Storm and all those other guys your defending your title against, think about " The Marauder " Chris Evans, the guy that actually EARNED a title shot at the next PPV. I'd advise you NOT to underestimate me, but if you don't listen ; well, that works to my advantage.
Chris : Storm, you want a match with the Marauder? Next Midweek Madness, or the next one, or whenver you want a match with me, come and face me. I know you beat me in your second match. But that was with the help of that prostitute you call a wife, and your SWA buddies. So next time, I'll be more aware. Chris Evans is sitting high at the top.
And when I'm sitting at the top, I'm feeling pretty damn good, so come on in. Step into the squared circle with
" The Excellence " and try to pin me 1, 2, 3. I'm waitin. Shawn : Hey, Chris. Don't forget this one. Ottawa 4 games to 2 over the New Jersey Devils. Gotta love those Canadian hockey franchises.
Too bad the Americans.... Shawn : Anyways, I see that the NEXT Midweek Madness after this one, there are open spots for challenges. Well, Kid Sharpe, Daniel Stephens,
I know you hate each other. And I'm not sure if I give Daniel Stephens a crack at the Lightweight title JUST so I can beat on him, or if I give the more deserving person the title, Kid Sharpe. So whichever one of you wants a shot, you know what to do.
( Lifts the belt up, which is lying on his couch. Shawn then lays it onto his coffee table. )
Come and get it. Shawn : I'm even considering a triple threat match. I'll see how it goes. Chris : I'm not done makin' my point yet. Maxwell Chamberlain, you don't need to make excuses about not winning the Battle Bowl. None of you do. You all know that the more deserving man won it that night.
You all know that the most hardworking man won it that night. You all know that the most determined man won it that night. But that's the past. It's time to look into the future. This Weekend Edition, me and Old Max get into the ring. Chamberlain, you're always talking about Americans and what they always do wrong. Well, this Sunday, I'm showing you the meaning of the word CANADIAN, and it's a lesson you'll never forget. So Max, this Saturday. Storm, next Wednesday AFTER this.
And it leads all the way to Viper at the next PPV. You'd all better be ready. Shawn : Don't forget, Sharpe or Stephens. Step through the ropes, and try to get this ( lifts the title ) from the owner.
( Screen fades with the two going back into the basement to resume training. FADE TO BLACK )
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- {Kid Sharpe is dressed in full wrestling gear standing infront of a backdrop with his logo on it. He is drinking from a bottle of water when the camera man tells him they're ready to film. He puts on the cap and tosses the bottle aside. The Kid turns to the camera and speaks..................
Kid Sharpe: Well, it looks like my only choice will be to become a three time champion. Something not achieved in the USCW before. Stephens, you will get you're payback soon. I gaurentee it. Not only do I beat you but you cost me a match. I'm not letting this turn into a petty fued. I have no need for it. All
I care about is proving that I am better than you. Shawn, that is your belt now. Sure, I would like a title shot tommorow morning, first thing. But it isn't going to happen. To much paperwork and everything in the front offices. I do want a re-match but it's up to the booking committee to set a date. Until then, don't worry about it. You're a champion, enjoy it.
The Kid: Razor, it's been awhile since you've walked down to the ring with your own music heard. It's about time. I would love to get back to the old ways with
"TSE". I can't say re-form because we never really broke up. The Sharpe Edge will dominate as it once did, and I can't wait to walk down to the ring beside you big bro.
Kid: It seems that I always will be defending something. If not a belt around here than the name of the USCW itself. I don't know alot about RFWF. Evidentlly they're fantasy wrestlers, maybe that's why none of them are here, with the real wrestlers! The USCW is the place to be, and declaring a war on it is signing a death wish. I don't know how this will take place but you can bet
I'll be on the front lines. Thank you.
{The screen fades to black.} -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- {Joey Maxx is standing in the USCW front office. In the background you can make out his entrance theme, "Save Yourself" by Stabbing Westward. He continually flirts with the secretary while waiting to be emitted. After a while she waves him on by. He walks around the halls and comes to the first of his many appointments. He gets his USCW ID Pass made, get's the physical, does the drug test, and meets the appropriate people. Finally he gets to the training room, where he truly wants to be.
Joey Maxx: It's hard to believe that "The Superstar" would be put through so much BS just to wrestle here. Joey Maxx needs no ID, everyone knows who he is.
He also is in tip top shape, unlike some of the people around here. Nor does he use artifical sweeteners. "The Superstar" is the real deal, guess they can't cope with that. Joey Maxx has only been around a short while, yet he has figured out the top players in this game. Of course, none can beat him. Now
Joey Maxx's first match is with some odd little man who calls himself Willy
Vanilly. There must be a reason for it, just like there is a reason Joey Maxx calls himself, "The Superstar". Now, "The Superstar" does not wish to belittle the Vanilly. But how can he not when he's sure that the match will not last a mere four seconds? "The Superstar" does not like to brag, he feels that everyone should already know his capabilities. But for those that don't. Joey
Maxx is a fearless tactful fighter. He choses his shots wisely, but executes high risk manuevers with ease. After "The Superstar" completely berates his opponent he preforms "The Double-X Plex", which to all of you not in the know is a slingshot backsuplex. Then "The Superstar" backs up, allowing the opponent to regain enough wit to stand to his feet. "The Superstar" then finishes off his dazed opponent with a "Superstar Kick" appropriately named after, "The
Superstar" Joey Maxx. It is all to easy for "The Superstar" who doesn't even break a sweat, but breaks a few hearts along the way. It's amazing how he does it really.
{"Save Yourself" starts to play again as the screen fades out.} -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- ("The Machine" Ron Matthews is working out in his home gym. His ankles are fastened to the top of a wall and he's doing ab crunches.)
Ron Matthews: So, Scott Dakota, you want me in an "I Quit Match"? Well, yeah,
I accept. Your victory over me was a damn fluke thanks to the shoddy officiating. An "I Quit Match" is a match more in my style. A match where you will scream and beg for mercy. Remember, Dakota, machines don't quit. They can be worn down from time to time but they never quit. At USCW Weekend
Editiomn, you will utter the words "I Quit" because I'm not walking away with a loss again. Referee's have cost me both my losses. One thought Viper was SWA and the other just couldn't count.
(He lifts himself up and unties his ankles. He crashes into the mat below. He gets off the mat and grabs a bottle of water from a nearby shelf.)
Ron Matthews: I don't think you realize what you've gotten yourself into,
Dakota. I don't quit. I've finished the Ironman Triathalon on a broke leg.
I've had my shoulder seperated during a match. I didn't give up either time. I went on to finish the job. I will not give up on Sunday because I'm just too damn stubborn to. You're going to have to kill me, Dakota, if you want the win.
