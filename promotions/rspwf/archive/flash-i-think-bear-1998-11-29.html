[FLASH] (i think) BeaR.

29 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
listen, this is my rp. I dont care if people criticize me. It isn't one of my better ones. If you take the time to read it, you might actually like it. but I do this for fun. I don't take this seriously. Well.. here it is.. oh, and dont even mention my excessive cussing, it is like an inside joke with some people in the fed.. here is the rp...
[BeaR is sitting in front of a camera man. He has a camera. BeaR begins to speak.] BeaR: Hello. Im BeaR. you all remember my many diferent personalities. Now, I think that being dark sucks. big time!! so, Im not dark anymore. now, I need to quickly introduce my manager, the Rock. Here is the Rock.
[BeaR puts a rock on the table. It has a little eyebrow taped onto it. Only an eyebrow. Nothing else. ]
BeaR: That's the Rock. He is my new manager. He is going to help me beat Derek
Wheeler. Now, I need to talk about DDK. I am the man. I beat Kaution. Nothing new there really. I continue to win. Im still the greatest. Blah Blah Blah.
FUCK YOU!! Im sick of all of the fan's shit. I have taken enough from the fucking fans. And I am taking no fucking more!! Now, I have a match for the next card. This makes me very happy. I want to fight. But I do not know who my opponent is yet. The prez told me that he would tell me today. I want to continue my winning streak to two wins in a row! And for god sakes, I want a title!!! Im the #2 person in the IC rankings thing... but I want the US title!!!! Now, I am bored. And Since I won't be coming out to the crowd for a while and the rock is now in the bathroom, I am all alone here. So, I am going to call the prez. And find out why it is taking so long for him to tell me who
I am fighting!!
[BeaR calls the NcWa Headquarters. The prez picks up the phone.] Jihad: Hello, this is my office, how may I direct your call? BeaR: Shut the fuck up bitch!! [BeaR hangs up the phone laughing his ass off.] BeaR: I guess he is in a bad mood. HeH, too bad. I really wanted to know to. [BeaR's cell phone rings. He answers the phone.] BeaR: Domino's pizza. How may I help you? Jihad: Shut up. I know that it is you BeaR. I am watching NcWa television right now. Your interview IS live ya know.
BeaR: Im sorry, but that deal has expired. However, you COULD get a large 1 topping pizza for $6.99. That deal is still in effect.
Jihad: BeaR.. You not funny. Now, I know you want to know who you are facing at the next card. That is why you called me in the first place. Now tell me who you want to fight.
BeaR: No silly, we don't serve Cheeser! Cheeser!'s. This is Dominos. Not Little
Ceasers! Jihad: HaHa. Very funny. BeaR: No, you cannot order another pizza as your topping on your first pizza! Jihad: What?!?! You remember what happened the last time you tried to be funny... Everyone got mad at you!!.....
BeaR: Ok. That'll be $7.63 . Please drive up to he first window.. [BeaR hangs up.] BeaR: Hmm.. Who do I want to face? Well, I think I will want to fight a high ranked Title holder guy first. But one that sucks, and that I will get an easy win over! Wait, I know. I want to be one of the mystery guys in the Jobber
Battle Royal!! Although it is not a title match...
[Phone rings again. BeaR picks up.] Jihad: NO! I already have two mystery guys!! Don't even try it. Now how the hell did you know that there was a battle royal?? You know that, but you do not even know that I have signed a match for you already! Don't you read the NcWa mail?!?!?
BeaR: Me no Habla Inglish. [BeaR hangs up the phone.] BeaR: HaHaHa!! Damn im funny! Well, I have noticed that the camera man has kept a straight face through all of this, so I guess im not funny. Hey camera man, who am I facing?
Camera Man: you are facing Derek Wheeler for the Intercontinental Title!! BeaR: Why didn't you tell me earlier you bitch?!?!?! [BeaR hits the camera man with a chair.] BeaR: Bitch.... anyways.. Wheeler... hmm... you dickhead. I have been waiting so fucking long to face you. Now, let's see what's on the line in this match..
hmm... you have the IC title here in the NcWa. I am number 1 contender. When I win, I should get the title . And then you would be what? Well.. That's not important, because it is you and not me. But I better get that title. You beat
Rampage in the Insane Title Tournament. but, well, fuck you. I am not Rampage.
I am BeaR. The best wrestler the NcWa has ever seen, right next to Bad Boy. Oh, and Manowar. but other then them, I am the greatest! Now, I believe that The rock is done in the bathroom, So we are leaving now. Derek Wheeler, Your ass is going down... oh yeah, compliments of 3D, I have a present for you.. FUCK YOU!
(That's the present.) bYe! there it is.. tell me what i should do to improve, although i didnt even half way try to make this rp have any quality. it was just made to entertain.
