[Thoughts Wanted] Character...

8 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Really, I haven't been active in e-wrestling for three years until I joined
EMWC about three months ago. I would especially like feedback from anyone who was in or read AMWC from November 1999 to April 2000, since that was the last previous fed I was in as my title character, 'Heartless' Eddie Rue. I want to restore him somewhat to the style I had back then, when I still thought really high of the work I did.
Read as much as you'd like and then give feedback. I dont necessarily want to hear whether you like the character or not, since that doesn't help me improve.
I'd rather you tear the work to pieces and make me look like I have no business wasting my time writing. Here are some recent things I've done with
Rue so far in EMWC:
------------- [Eddie Rue, in appearance and every other way, is caffeine. We all know that caffeine makes us more alert. Some people even experience insomnia or
"jitters" after having a lot of caffeine. However, these and other side effects of caffeine usually disappear when it is consumed regularly or in smaller amounts. The difference? Rue is irrepressible. He may be a mild annoyance for some. For others, he is disabling. Somehow, he never fails to gaurantee dissatisfaction. Even his unintentional plastic smile is displeasing.]
ER: You know, our whole life is based on going shopping, but right now, because of the bad economy, there's not a whole lot to sell. Or, things are just too costly to buy. However, the rubberband hasn't popped on one sector of the economy. It is increasingly common now to sell lies to people, especially because of how elastic lies are. You can stretch them as much as you want.
Eventually, it reaches a point where others think, "Who wouldn't want to be him?" I wonder... Can you blame others for your lies, even if they don't know you're lying? I see it all the time. It's called "the well-intentioned lie," as if all other lies were unintentional and bred by Human Ignorance... As if you lying is somehow more acceptable than a preist or police officer lying... As if you have "good reasons" to lie... As if lying reeeeally improved your bad situation.
[Rue tips his head down in a bowed position.] I don't expect you to feel guilty, [Rue raises his head and uses his God-like eyes to direct his message.] ...but don't deny your shame! Don't deny you are selling people lies. Don't deny the cost others pay for your facade. You are costing them alot, because they believe, they buy your faux celebrity image, and they think your the reeeealest person they know.
But, you know, you know... And, inwardly, you are thinking: [Rue turns his head to the side, as if to step into the third-person.] "I know, I know,... my lies contradict reeeeality and blind others from the truth." [Rue looks directly back into the camera with those eyes of his.] You are willing to do anything to catch upto your false world of chest heavy expectations. The sad thing is, there is a cure for your Human Greed, although
I doubt you would care enough to do it... because you Fear the repercussions.
You fear that if people see who you reeeeally are, if they see the face behind the mask,... that your rubberband will snap.
[A familiar whistling sound. Want more caffeine? Listen to the tea kettle calling. Fade to black...]
---------- [Open to Eddie Rue. Rue is folded out, relaxing with a precious chance to rewind, silencing the hum of the world's hurried existence. No need to budge, not even for a fire alarm. Here, sitting on a drum, leaning against a wire mesh fence, the basic principles of simplicity, harmony, and apt association.
A perfect setting for Eddie Rue.] ER: You know, Grayson, for having such a big head you're pretty goddamn ignorant. I mean, boom, you beat me, suddenly -- a birth of a legend!
Exciting, sure. Important, maybe. But life-transforming? Come on. Had I understood the importance of that day, had I seen how it was a rite of passage,
I would've grabbed some chicken and a couple of drinks to coronate your manhood. It was like you were experiencing a sexual awakening. Something had stirred inside of you and come to life. Nothing could've prepared me for that sight. I mean, you're parents must made a big deal potty-training you.
Everyone must've been confessing something, crying big tears, hugging estranged mailcarriers, parading your newfound emotional growth.
You know, it's amazing. This has to be one of the cruelest ironies. You knocked me out, and now you're floating on air. I mean, for god's sake, how about drifting back into conciousness? The dream sequence ends... TONIGHT.
-------- [NO PICTURE. Jump-cut to a static laden soundbite of Eddie Rue LAUGHING.] ER: You know, Eric Wayne... [PICTURE. A close-up of Rue's face, awkwardly dried in blood and yet smiling... it seems so misplaced... how can he be happy? Even his eyes are unearthly and out-of-tune.]
Some guys can bullshit right up to the limit. They tell the tallest tales to prove how reeeeally tough they are. It seems that's how you get status in the
EMWC. As with everything else in life, the truth is merged, fixed, and reconstructed into a lie. They've discovered, by passing off the BIGGEST B.S.
IMAGINEABLE, that lies are what looks cool to people. People suddenly value you and -- would you believe it! -- they reeeeally dig your looks now!
You've got people diggin' you now, Eric. All that B.S. is working for you -- you're leaving sacks of flaming dog[BLEEP] wherever you go! BUT... this past week you left it on the wrong person's doorstep...
And some day, some day -- you'll be the poor sap to stamp it out. [CUT.]
