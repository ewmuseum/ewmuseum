Here is my first RP I did for the ICW, flame away.....

26 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[A darken room is all the camera can pick up as it slowly fades in. The outline of a man sitting at a table is all the can be seen. With notice he flares up a match and his face can be seen in the glow of the burning match, the man has blonde hair and a somewhat serious look upon his face...he is almost staring at the way the match is burning down towards his two fingers, but soon uses the match to the light the somewhat large candle that is standing in front of him. His, now glowing red eyes look deep into the burning flame of the candle, and a sinister smile washes over his face, as he prepares to talk...]
"All my life I have been compared to greatness, be it the time I caught four touchdowns in high school, and they called me the next great wide reciver of our time. Or the other time when set the high jump record for track, and they said that I was Olympic bound. But you what gets to me the most?
...It's when they call me the next great Van Steele, the next to carry on the name, the tradition, of what it means to be a Van Steele!! But, see my brother Eric was nothing like me. Sure, he was a 6 time World Champion, a
3 time Tag Team Champion, but that only makes him one thing. The King of
Independant Feds! But see, I am not Eric Van Steele, I am James Van
Steele! [the very animated James Van Steele begins to wave his arms all about as he really tries to push across his point....He almost knocks over the candle a few times but manages to miss it, during his speech.]
"And what James Van Steele is, is something that man who calls himself the
Inferno could never handle! In Japan, where I made a name from myself, I have taken on some of the most dangerous men in the world. I took them all to the extreme and beyond, and with almost few execptions, when the smoke cleared, there I stood with my arm raised. So, what does this have to do with my opponent Inferno....I really don't have a clue, but as long as I get a chance to talk about myself it makes me happy!"
[He begins to rub his chin gotee, and smiles even more.....the camera fades to James Van Steele wrestling back in Japan. It is towards the end of one of his most famous matches.....James Van Steele adds the voice over...]
"I remember this one, ahhhh...July of 95' . I think that people that watched this classic match between me and Anito Masagobi, will remember this one as well. Masagobi was my tag partner before he was offered a bribe to turn his back on me during our tag team title match, being the greedy bastard he was, he took it, and sent me to the hospital. I of course got better and this match was a grudge match to end all grudge matches. I think the match time was somewhere in the neighborhood of 45 mins, we fought up and down the aisles, in and out of the crowd, and so on and so on."
[The tape shows James Van Steele, hit Anito Masagobi with a chair across the face, sending him out of the ring and onto a table that was placed out there. James Van Steele climbs to the top ropes....]
"This was when I knew I had him, one of my favorite moves that I have ever preformed and thankfully it was a piece of trash like Masagobi!! I told myself over and over, that this crowd had just seen one of the most horrific matches, and just a normal dive through the table wouldn't blow their socks off. So, Improvised....and pulled off something most people have never seen. Just watch"
[JVS, stands at top of the ropes looking down onto Anito Masagobi who is laying flat on the table and pretty much helpless. JVS does a corkscrew plancha onto the table, sending both he and Masagobi crashing through the table and onto the floor, JVS covers and makes the pin...]
"Ahhh, just another notch in the victory belt for good old JVS, and on the
29th JVS is going to continue his winning ways, and Inferno...you are going to be snuffed out!!
[JVS slams his hand down upon the buring candle and puts it out....He smiles and the camera fades out.
