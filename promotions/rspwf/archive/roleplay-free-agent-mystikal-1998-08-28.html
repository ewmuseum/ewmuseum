I'm a free agent looking for a top notch fed to compete in! If you're interested then email me or IM ME if online, thanx! Here's a sample flash from
CNWL. Read and Enjoy, hopefully to be in a top fed...thanx!!!
[Fades In] [*The scene opens with Mystikal walking into the Lobby of an Marriot Hotel in
Downtown Philadelphia. Right behind him comes in a bellboy with all his bags on a wheel in tall cart and a few strap on his shoulder. Mystikal walks up to the
Information desk and asks for his key to his room. The operator checks on the reservation list on the and pulls up his name and right by his name is his room number. She tells him his room number is 1427 and that he can catch the elevator or there to the right. He slowly pulls off his sunglasses and reaches in his pocket and unravels two $50 bills from his money clip and throws it down on the desk saying thank you. The operator passes him his key and smiles.
Mystikal then signals the bellboy which has his things and tells him to follow him. He runs catching an elevator that was about to leave as he makes room for the bellboy and the cart. The doors close heading in a upward movement. A chime is heard throughout the elevator letting the people know that they've reached the next floor...*]
[...he looks up and there he sees the number 14 appear electronically in a digital format. The elevator comes to a haulting stop and the opens as Mystikal and his luggage starts its way to his room. He looks at the card that was gaven to him looks around the halls to try to get an lead on where his read is.
The bellboy tells mystikal to tag along as he knows where the room is. They make an quick right from the elevator and the bellboy unlocks his door with the key given. He then rolls in the cart then unstacks things just as he leaves he begins to rub his index finger across with his thumb as a symbol for an tip. he pulls out an $2 bill and hand sit to him just as he is shoved out of the room.
Mystikal looks around his room and smiles, he jumps on the bed and folds across his hands behinf his head laying down...kicking off his shoes and then walks around the beautiful room. He takes a look out the window and catches an breeze of the fresh air and then goes back sitting on the edge of the bed as he pushes under his shoes he starts to speak..*]
Mystikal: Ahh the great fresh breeze of the outside air. The smell of fresh air is like rejuvenating a part of me that has been missing for years, remembering the past memories of greatness of when I've been up and when I've been down. I dont expect to go downhill in my career here in the CNWL. It's going to be all up for me, straight to the top without any stops, but one. One man that blocks my path to victory and yet a man that i couldn't give two [BEEP] about. So whats the point of wrestling him or brawling with him you ask? One thing, maybe more...to strike with revenge and to release the anger that has built up from the beginning. I am now exploring my chances here in the CyberNetic Wrestling
League. Exploring the chance of being on top, capturing titles, and winning match after match. Here in the CNWL, the game isn't about winning nor losing, it's all about survival and who really has what it takes to make it on there own, or got what it takes. I sit here on my small throne in the CNWL awaiting the one day that my oppurtunity comes knocking on the door. But before that, I have one small little barrier blocking in my distance. A man known as
"Devastating" Damon Knight. The man I uphold the most hatred, and I fear, fear that I might go over board and too far by taking his life in battle. Me and my nemesis shall meet up one day, wait, I almost forgot! That day approaches us as the world turns into the next day.The date is set: Sunday Night at BattleZone
`98, August 30th. To some just an ordinary day in life, but to me a spring into the next millenium, into the next generation. August 30th, something I'm very well looking forward to. You want to win the match Knight yet alone survive?
You dont know what it takes. And let me tell you...Interiority, honor, strength, heart, and courage. Something I am full of and something you'tre lacking of. It's hard to believe, that I've got caught into another one of your plots of attack! But you know, I had the feeling you'd so up on the tour
Thursday trying to cost me the match. I guess it was a little payback from the day I cost you the Television title. That probably has to be it. No matter where you are, I will find you catch you...then you will know and tell me how it felt to be dominated and getting the nick nack doggy smack down! Speaking of laying the smack down...I think me, myself and IronFist did a good job on you and all the rest of those pitiful chumps in the Revolution X. Last time I checked you was getting your clocks cleaned. You know for the past few weeks you've been all over me, cramping my style at which you try to adapt yourself to. Something that you will never do. Nick nack doggy smack will be layed upon ya as you would say. You see dumb [BEEP] like yourself have theese little ego implants or shall I say ego trips that get you thinking you're the [BEEP] but i have nes for you...and after our match has taken place you'll be off that little pedistal of yours so far up in the air. better yet, it'll be broken, right along with the spine that runs down your back with yellow streaking mark with it. I'm just thougher then you; more dangerous and deadly; and just SIMPLY the best at what i do and that is getting the job done. Something that you havent accomplished in matter of days, hell, weeks even. I mean look at you, you're not coordinated. I bet you got me scouted down as an 1 dimensional wrestler huh? Well thats where you're wrong...I have also a numerous variety of moves. I can perfect the front face DDT [Diamond Cutter] anywhere from that a
Powerbomb, chokeslam, or Camel Clutch and alot other not to be mentioned. I am prepared, the question is are you? I dont think so. Time is damn near running out, and if you did lose, at least you'd lose to with dignity, hmph..with that in mind, maybe not...there's no one else with the upmost talent in this federation then me. Now onto another subject...I'm in the backa nd all of a sudden I hear my name bounce off the lips of the now World Champion, Guru!
Guru, it's nice that ya notice me since I'm a midde man, mid carder...but I'd keep my eye on Anthony Gold, because it seems that you can't handle him, if you can't handle him, then how in the world would you compare to me? I give ya credit where credit is due but I dont have no beef with you, if you wanna make something of it then, go ahead, i like to brawl...thats what I do, I FIGHT and it seems you're struggling to hold on to your title. Because less then a week or so you wont be sporting that there gold strap no longer. Then there's Shawn
Parker, a man without a doubt one of the best superstar's the CNWL can produce, but one thing is one thing but trying to still my glory is something you won't do. Now we all saw, I mean the world...saw me come down and help you hang on to that title so dont go around and run your mouth and trying to steal the credit for it. because last that I saw, was you laying on the mat about to get your ass pinned til I jumped in and saved your ass!!! Now i feel that i should get my shot at a title, I'm not picking out an particular title but get my shot.
Anyway, as i was saying before...Damon i know you take great pride in this match and you have your pretty little heart on winning, but there's nothing blocking you but yourself. We're equal in in size, weight, talent but when it all comes down to it, it's gonna be about who wants this match more, me or you, and who has the determination to get it? Simply, well i can answer that one with my eyes closed. I got the stamina, the heart and determination, and believe you don't know jack [BEEP] about me. This match is easy for the pickings.
[He pauses...] [He scratches the top of his head...] Mystikal: Days pass, even nights...as I daydream off into the fantasy dream world, where you often drift, and have flashes of of me laying the smack down putting you to rest once and for all. In less then a week, BattleZone `98 will take place my tasks may be implemented. Different people perform and carry out different traits, skillz and talent...and it seems one for you is just plain, running your damn mouth. Thats one skill i also can match...but the question is can you match up with me in speed and strength...and agility. I dont think ya can, but it'll be put to the test also this Sunday Night. Testing your masculinity? You seem more feminine. I know you're all the woman that you say you areI am the GREAT one...so live it..learn it...LOVE IT! Because here forever I will stay and for once and for all I'm gonna finally get my hands on you one on one with no back fighting...and then knock a little sense into your ass! Don't let the paranoia build up on ya, because there isnt a day that goes by that i dont think bout BattleZone.

Mystikal: You see, the value of my name, my reputation, and my career are all on the line. And i dont think i wanna blow it all right here and right now at this point. Give me something to strive for, like the World title...I'll put it all on the line. Butb you are nothing, I dunno why i am wasting my valuable time competeing to you? Always been a wonder of mine, cause this is one [BEEP]
you dont wanna fuck with. I'll eat ya up and spit ya out like a cannibal. Mr
Devastating...i guess you want everyone in the world to kneel at your presence huh? Well i dont think so...well I aint one that'll bow to anyone, if anything, you'll be bowing to at my presence! Respect shall be shown, whether I earn it the easy way or the hard way. it must be hard for someone like you to keep up this here front or little act you put on. [BEEP] you aintr being real to yourself. It's unbelievable that the way you act here and around with yo friends...well i hope we can settle everything out this coming weekend. You will find out what Mystikal is truely about. Until then, let the day fall upon my grasps!
