-[Free Agent]- 'PsYcO Rage' Chris Page

5 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey whats up all. I just wanna say that I am now a free agent. Now some of you may of know me from my stint in the GPW as World Champion or I was the only wrestler in SCW to hold the Brass Knux Champion three times. Well right now I am only currently in the UEW and am looking for another fed.
Well Page is apart of the PsYcO CoNnEcTiOn(just like Dan Debacle). Page stands at 6'6" 255 pounds. Page is known for his intensity inside the ring and is not afraid to do some extreme moves using many weapons and causing havoc when he fights.
I have a lot to give to federations and love to roleplay for some great matches. I am not the best of roleplayers but one do I do give to a fed is I give my hardwork and determination. I love to work some great angles and have some great feuds I am just looking for a fed to do it in. Below is a sample roleplay of mine if interested drop me a line at
[detail withheld] . Send me a webpage link and a handbook for the fed! << The camera slowly opens up, unto a whole word of color. The scenario, is a simple one. A nice, almost "beautiful" living room. Of course, this is the living room, of Chris Page's home. There is an expensive, white "Lay Z Boy"
Reclining chair, and couche a few inches from the wall. Infront of these two items is a brown coffee table, and a Large screen Telivision set. On the light blue rug, there is a Stereo. You hear the song "Life After Death" From the
UnderGround, Detroit acid Rap group "Natas". The song blasts at high volumes, as the camera pans around the whole living room. The
T.V.is not in use right now, and sitting on the beautiful couche, is Chris Page. His hair, now died a lighter brown color, in some places. His attire includes his infamous, PsYcO
CoNnEcTiOn t-shirt, and baggy dark blue "Caffeine" brand pants. Once he realizes he is being filmed by the camera man, he shuts off the music, and sighs. There is a short silence, before Chris begins to speak.
[PAGE] Psychopathic Wrestling Alliance. In only about ninety six hours, you will witness a battle like no other. You have made a fatal mistake, by simply putting David Thorne, and 'The American Rage' Chris Page against each other. By putting two men, with such raw talent, and determination, on opposite sides, you have basically taken years of both of our careers. You have forced me, to square off against a great friend of mine, and for that, I am anything but grateful. But yet, thats life. At times, you are forced to do things you may not enjoy, RiddleBox, is one of those times. <<short pause>> I've got a question for you, Thorne. How are you a tougher challenge, than Debacle? You are no better than him, and you are better than me. You say I am not ready to walk that extra mile, for a win. Now, if you judge that, on what you've seen in the ring, and camera, you might be right. It may seem, like I am an "under acheiver", but what you see could be deceiving. What you think, is completely wrong. I am 100 percent ready, to take it up a notch, Thorne. To show the world, what I am truly made of. The time of Redemption, is coming, and soon enough, it will be here. Soon enough, I will rise to the occascion. I will show you what determination, hard work, and experience can get you. Don't get me wrong, I may lose this match, but I am NOT going to lose the WAR. Thats for sure.
<< Page looks into the camera and Page has those crazy eyes while staring into the camera.>>
[PAGE] You see, Thorne, I'm sure you remember the last stint of the NCW, about nine months ago. I sure do, you basically battling back from your sopposed injury to once again reclaim the World Championship but that never happened did it. I won many battleshere in PWA, just like I said I would, but just look how much a better of a wrestler I have become I am now fighting for the World
Heavyweight Belt. I beat the living crap out of your two goons Scott Styles and
Dane Matthews, and now I will become champion. I never did lose our first battle, Thorne. Yeah you pinned but you see every since then I have gotten the better of you and could of pinned your shoulders to the mat so many times but I wanna end your reign at RiddleBox. A Belt that is rightfully MINE, is now being put up for grabs. Do you honestly think that I will fail, in a mission, to win back a belt, that I NEVER Lost, in the first place? The whole concept of how that title isn't in my possession right now, is bullshit, but if all goes as planned, it will be a concept, very short-lived. In your first interview, you basically asked me if I thought you would let this chance slip away. And that if I did, I didn't know you as well, as you had thought. But you see Thorne, it has nothing to do with "slipping away", I am sure that you will put in everything that you've got, and I'm sure I will put in everything that I've got. Nothing is going to slip away, Thorne, its simply what was meant to be.
Who had the better luck, on that night. But skill also does play a big part in this match. Some say I am better than you, some say you are better than me, its now time to see which opinion was infact, the right one. You say you will do anything for a win, but sometimes your best is not enough. There are times, when you step up to an opponent, who can counter your best, and still come out victorious. I am not implying that I am better than you, or that I will definitely win this match, Im simply telling you this could be one of those occascions. This crap could go either way, my freind, and I am ready for either out come.
<< Page takes a breathe before continuing...>> [PAGE] Now excuse me, I've never claimed to be something that I'm not, Thorne. Stop trying to put words in my mouth, because that simply isn't going to work. You say you will watch, and laugh at my skills ? You think this is funny ?! Ha, the things I've gone thru, are anything but comickable. The things I've seen, the places I've been, the matches I've been apart, they are sickening. I've learned, from some of the most sadastic competitors, on this planet. I've been taught, by men, who are truly mercinaries. Men unlike me, and you, men who HAVE no hearts. You seem to find my experiences, laughable. It is nothing like that, like I just said. It is nothing to laugh at, especially when that experience, those 4 years, that could make me the winner of this match. Your ignorance towards my distant past, could be your downfall. I told you before, a match I lost one month ago, has no relivance to our match, or even to your "logic"
theory. I could come out now, and I would probably defeat Hunter Douglas, what would you have to say then ? Where would your logic be then? It would be what it is now, meaningless. You call me an underdog? You say the odds are stacked against me ? Allow yourself to think that, Thorne. I want you to come into this match, cocky and arrogant, like you are right now. I want you to think that there is no way you can lose this fight, and I want to teach you a lesson. I want to show you, just how fatal a mistake underestimating your opponent could be. The bullshit respect you get from the fans, and the critics, follow a trend you've been making, because they also, mean nothing. They are people in this sport, that are completely underestimated. People that never get the respect, that they deserve. I consider myself one of those people. You really do not know just how good I can be, Thorne. You don't know what I am truly capable of doing, until you've stepped into the ring with me. You've proved yourself too be a little bit less intelligent, then I had thought you once were. All of the shit that you've messed up with, all of the meaningless theorys, all of the ignorance, all of the underestimating, it will all add together, and it just may haunt you, when this match is said and done. You can doubt me all you'd like, you can say whatever you'd like, but I've heard it all before. I've seen this song and dance, many times, and most of the time, it does lead to failure.
Step into my word, Thorne. Step up, and get ready...Screw what you heard, this is what I've got to say !
<< With those words, Page grins. A Look of satisfaction, and determination occupies his face. He pauses for a moment, and then lounges back on the couche.
Ian seems relaxed, as he sticks up both middle fingers. A Gesture everyone is probably used to. The camera pans closer to Page, then backs out slowly, as it fades...to eternal blackness. >>
