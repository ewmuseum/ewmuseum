{critique please} ERIC CAGE FLASH

1 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Camera fades into a face shot of the undeniable one Eric Cage. He wears his usual ray ban glasses and the camera starts to zoom out. Behind Eric, a stage is set up that says Eric The Undeniable One Cage written all over it.
Eric sits in a leather chair facing a apparent audience wearing a white buisness suit that is unbuttoned)
Cage: Welcome everyone to the greatest show in sports entertainment today...MY SHOW!! with your host......ME! As you know this is a weekly show where the scumbag fans can ask the greatest wrestler ever, questions. So lets get right to the show.
(A man steps up to a microphone situated in the crowd) Man: Eric Cage, what were your thoughts on David Ogles at the PPV? Eric Cage: You know, im glad you asked. This piece of crap says he's the greatest wrestler of all time, yet he wont prove it? Any good champion never backs down from a fight and if he does, he should be champion! Plain and Simple! However i give the stipulations for our matchup and being the p*ssy he is....he rejects? David Ogles, if you are afraid of me, why didnt you say so. Little man, im gonna cut you a break and leave you alone from now on. We all dont want to see you cry in the ring if we ever locked up.
(A lady steps down to the microphone) Woman: Mr. Cage, what is your thoughts about your matchup at the PPV? (Cage laughs uncontrollably but after a while regains composure) Cage: All i have to say is pathetic. A bunch of men walk down, give me a beating like the pansies they are, i might add...3 on 1, what is that?
Anyways they come down and give me a beating and i still walk out victorious? All i have to say AGAIN is pathetic, on both Luke Force and
Genuines part. What a joke... what a joke.
(Another man walks down and stands at the mic) Man:What are your thoughts about your upcoming matchup against Matt Ogles? Cage: I have a question? Why do i get stuck with some guy where crappiness runs in the family? First David Ogles shoots his mouth off and now i have to beat up on his younger brother? Or whatever he is. They both suck so it doesnt matter. Whatever it takes to beat you however Matt, it will be done.
You are no better than your relative and thats sad. This is a hardcore matchup and The Undeniable One Eric Cage intends to show it this week. This is not your wussy kind of matchups your used to Matt, this is my territory and you will feel the mercy, because YOU CANT DENY THE GREAT ONE!
(The fans boo as Eric Cage stands up from his chair. Cage gives them the old finger and walks off the stage. the camera fades to black)
