[Retribution] Free Agent

RSPWFers, This is the newest character that I have created. His name is Retribution. His stats and other information is located at the bottom of this page. I hope you like this guy. I personally think that this will be my best creation yet. I will be needing one, and only one federation to join. The federation must have a web page and it must be e-Mail based. Here is the information. -Chris -- ret ri bu tion (ret're byu'shen), a deserved punishment; return for wrongdoing. n - Out of Ring Name- Jason Ericson - In Ring Name- Retribution - Height- 6'5" - Weight- 260lb. - Entrance Music- "You Came Up" by Big Pun f. Noreaga - Allignment- Heel - Wrestling Style Most Used- Technical, Brawling - Wrestling Style Never Used- Lucha Libre - 5 Favorite Moves- 1. Punisher's Sorrow - Standing Suplex 2. Never Came Up - Boston Crab 3. 6 Feet Under - DDT 4. Reverse DDT 5. Piledriver - Primary Finisher- Deserved Punishment - Description- A suplex into a neckbreaker. - Set-up Move- Spitting at the crowd. Can be put on at anytime. - Secondary Finisher- Wrongful Doing - Description- A brain-buster from the top. - Set-up Move- A kick to the stomach. - Out of Ring Looks- Dressed in grundgy looking hip hop style clothes, Retribution is not that different than his in-ring look. He looks like a grundge skater gone bad. He has tattoos all over his body. He usualy wears an old hat to the back. - In Ring Looks- In the ring Retribution is dressed as he is out of the ring. Black ripped jeans and no shirt. He has black boots on as well. - Physical Description- Retribution is a well built man. He has a muscular build of a Wrath or a Undertaker. He has a bald head. He has a blck goatee as well as piercing in the ears, tongue and eye brow. He has a distinctive tattoo on his chest, it says "Retribution 5:19." Let the invites BEGIN! I have not done a sample flash yet, but when I do it I will send it here first.
