Here's a roleplay. Do your job and critique it.

27 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I don't know why I'm doing this. I am now a lamb for the slaughter. You guys scare the hell out of me. RSPWF is full of strange sorts and different versions of human life.
But here's an introductory roleplay I did for my character, Awkward
Billy, in a new fed called ACCW.
...And don't expect me to ever, EVER post on RSPWF again... unless of course I want to. [Tonight is the night of the landmark first ACCW installment of an epic weekly showcase from Our hero, Awkward Billy, entitled "As the Billy
Turns"! Get an inside look at the man you'll come to love and/or hate over time. And now, it's time for the opening ceremonies. You will now be introduced to Your hero, The Awkward One...]
[From a pitch black screen, appear these lines...] --Detroit, Michigan-- --12:00 noon-- --The beloved mansion-- --The beloved Station Wagon-- --Enter... Mr. Billy [Scene flips to a beatutiful enormous three story brick mansion with a large three car garage and a healthy green lawn surrounding it all. In the garage's great big driveway, is an out of place looking beat up old
Station Wagon. And sitting on the hood of that station wagon is our subject, Awkward Billy... Mr. Billy... The Awkward One... or just plain
Billy.]
[Our hero is not a great big man. He is 6'3", 250 pounds, with an ugly rough mug and extremely tangled and dirty short brown hair. He is dressed in the same fashion that he has dressed for many years and the same way he will dress every second of every minute you lay your eyes upon him. Billy wears a dirty, stained, wrinkled, messy old white t-shirt, blue jeans torn in random places, and army boots.]
[The Awkward One doesn't currently have much expression on his face. He looks pretty blank. Billy gives off a big yawn, looks over towards the camera, and flashes a small half smile. He's obviously not ecstatic about giving off the first installment of "As the Billy Turns", but nevertheless, Our hero isn't one to disappoint all his
Awkward-A-Maniacs. With no stand out tone, Billy gives us our first earful of his deep, uneven, and sometimes squeaky voice...]
~Awkward Billy~ Hello... I bet you're sitting there asking yourself who this guy is, right? Or am I wrong? Actually, my guess is, you're sitting there...
and just waiting. You're simply waiting for me to get my useless rant out of the way, and my useless behind out of the way, so you can listen to and lay your eyes upon more worthy of people than that of myself. I knew it! Look, you listen up now... I know I'm not the most beautiful, intellectual, fabulous person out there right now! And in fact, I know there's probabaly people in ACCW... wrestlers and staff that are much more classy than I. They've probabaly been in the business many more years than me and have had a whole goll darn bloody heck of a lot more success than me. They've probabaly won all these countless world titles and have these extraordinarily big bank accounts...
Well, I guess I shouldn't be talking! Hehehe, I've won my fair share of titles and most definitely... most goll bloody darned definitely can compete in the bank account portion! Yep, as you can see behind me
[turns to look at his mansion] you can tell that I'm doing pretty good for myself already. I'm financially set and have provided security for my family, which consists of Me and... that's about it. So what do you think is my reasoning for joining this spiffy organization called
Atlantic Coast Championship Wrestling? [Billy for the first time in the segment shows lots of emotion as he gets a crazily excited look on his face, jumps off the hood of the station wagon, onto the driveway, and raises his hands in the air.]
~Awkward Billy~ [yelling in happiness] Yes! [Calms down, but is still happy and excited] Woo hoo! There it is! The very first question for the
Awkward Billy hotline, in ACCW! What an event... what a goll bloody darned great couple events I have created for you! We have the first ever ACCW "As The Billy Turns", and now my very first ever hotline question! Well in case you missed it [smirks] the question was... and I repeat...
"Why has The Awkward One joined ACCW?" You think you know the answer? You really think you understand why a piece of trash athlete such as myself would ever think he could compete against the likes of "2 Smooth" Setzer van Strife, and "Hot Property"
Flex Masterson? Well if you got the answer... call The Awkward Billy hotline at 1-800-AWKWARD, and tell us your crazy notions!
--Awkward Billy hotline costs $6.32 a minute, only those over the age of 42 need call, and NO DEAF RETARDED MIDGETS ALLOWED! Operators standing by 2.4 hours a day... call NOW!--
[Billy happily plugs his hotline, then starts prancing and frolicking with glee around his large cement driveway. I think we are now seeing what Our hero is like. Get used to it! Billy stops, looks into the station wagon, then back to the camera and gives us a little wink. With a big smile, he pulls open the back door and yanks out... A HUMAN
DUMMY!] [Our hero's only real friend has made her first of many regular appearances in "As the Billy Turns". She is wearing a smiley face, a short black dress, and a poofy blonde wig. Billy continues to dance and prance around with her for a couple seconds until he stops right in front of the camera, huffing and puffing for air.]
~Awkward Billy~ Everybody... this is Betsy! Betsy... this is the cameraman. But Betsy, do you see inside the lense of the this camera? [pause as Billy looks at Betsy] Yes, that's good. You know, there are millions and millions of people in there! They're all watching every move you make, Betsy!
[With that, Billy throws Betsy to the ground where the camera can't see her... or it.] [smirk] I guess Betsy doesn't take very well to the fact that so many people can see her. She's a little shy you know. [looks down] Betsy, come on! Get up... please? [looks back up] Oh well, Betsy just doesn't want to be on TV right now I suppose.
[Our hero steps over to the hood of the station wagon, and takes the same seat he had in the beginning of the segment. He sits, blankly looking into the sky and scratching his chin in wonderment...]
~Awkward Billy~ That can't be all... can it? [extreme excitement] I smell another hotline question! [excitement quickly dies down] No, just kidding.
But I just know I'm forgetting something. Let me think... what am I gonna be doing anytime soon? [snaps fingers] Got it! I believe I'll be wrestling pretty soon! The Awkward One's ACCW debut will be made on
March 6, 1999, at Daytona Beach, Florida. You know, that's pretty far away from my home here in Detroit, Michigan. I'm a gonna probabaly have to fire up the good ole station wagon here and drive myself on down tonight. Yep. So this match... this battle royal that I'm in is pretty goll bloody darn important, ain't that right? It decides all our seeds for Crowning Glory. Well all you little Awkward-A-Maniacs out there know, along with me, than Your hero will do really goll bloody darn good in that battle royal! In fact, he won't just do good... but he'll do out right great! Not just great, but Outstanding! This is a big opportunity and I won't let it pass me by.
[gets stern look on his face] But what if... if... if I happen to do... well, lets just say... not so very good on the 6th? Then I might never be able to pull myself back up to the top. [snaps back to cheerfullness] Oh the heckitty heck with it! I'll continue my reign as
Your Unofficial Awkward ACCW Champion no matter what takes place! But you know, realistically, I have a great chance in this here event. I mean, look at all these guys...
"Hurricane" Mike Winters? "2 Smooth" Setzer van Strife? "The Highlight" Dylan Ryder? "Hot Property" Flex Masterson? "Upstart" Tommy Shaw? "Darkstar" Blayde Wyndham? "Superstar" Ken Warren? Steve Bundy? Mr. Bungle? "The Millionare" Richard Miller? Devon Case? Jeff Keenan, "The Goldenboy"? "Gamesmaster" Rob Ackerman? "Iceman" Dillon Freeze? [frowns] Okay, so maybe I got some goll bloody darn good competition. [yelling] Maybe I'm just totally screwed up the You Know What! [calms down] But like I said, I'll still be #1 in your hearts! I'll still be your hero! [in happy tears] Even if I don't do spectacular, you'll still love me! I know... I just know that all the Awkward-A-Maniacs out there will still love their hero... ME! [returns to normal again] So
I'm just saying that I know I've got some pretty stiff and tough competition. But when it comes down to it, whether you like it or not,
The Awkward One is ranked right up there with all you big name hot shots. I will do goll bloody darned great in the battle royal and I'll even score myself a few eliminations! [laughs with joy] Maybe... just maybe... It could be You! Ha ha ha ha...
[Our hero continues to laugh with happiness as he jumps up onto the roof of the station wagon, proceeds to jump back off, does a few somersaults, then prances on out of the scene. The camera moves down to show Billy's good... "friend", Betsy. She is limp and twisted, lying on the ground, with her wig askew on her cotten head. Hmm... what might be going through Betsy's mind? Well if she's thinking anything, she's obviously thinking to herself;
"I know my man Billy will do just grrrreat in the Battle Royal on March
6th. He's the best thing to happen to ACCW since ACCW became ACCW!"
[On that note, we bid adieu... or say goodbye. Tune in next week for the continuing epic saga of "As the Billy Turns". Scene fades out to pretty pink polkadots... then to black.]
