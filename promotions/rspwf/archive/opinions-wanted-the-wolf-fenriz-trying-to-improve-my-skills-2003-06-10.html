[OPINIONS WANTED] "The Wolf" Fenriz (Trying to improve my skills)

10 June 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yea, this is the debut flash for the very first SEX card. To me, it is a basic representation of every Fenriz flash I do. They have a point, a meaning, and somehow you end up not really liking him. I'm just looking for ways to improve my character since he is really what
I want to start working with in the 'big time.' All of my other characters have been nohting more than mediocre, I say Fenriz has a chance of being more, so I want to see what I can do to get him there.
If this flash doesn't satisfy your thrist, I can post a few more if need be. Anyhow, onto the flash...
------ THE BEGINNING IS ALWAYS THE MOST DIFFICULT PART I plan to start off on the right foot, what about you? PART ONE OF ONE - "Waging a Timeless War" [Black. Nothing but black fills the screen. Not a single light, nor a noise. Just black. Cold, heartless, unwelcoming blackness.]
[That all changes with His voice, the voice of the Wolf.] The Wolf: Every man no matter how strong or powerful has certain weaknesses in his armor. Even I, despite being basically almighty, have my own set of weaknesses. They will be exploited in time, just like everyone else's. And soon, even I will hang my head low in shame. However, that time is not now, nor anytime in the next few weeks because there is a giant man standing in front of me with the greatest weakness of all--Catholism running through his mind, body, and spirit..
[A small light flickers, almost candle like, on the bottom the screen, casting its pale light upwards towards the midnight sky. The shadows of the Wolf cloaked in his infamous black flowing robe dance before us, but his face like usual lies hidden behind the impenetrable hood.]
["Faith. It is a great thing to have, but only when the faith has its roots in something... right and not filled with blatant lies."]
The Wolf: All through the years no greater bloodshed has been taken in the name of your God. All through the ages no more deaths have be attributed than to the name of God. Catholism is a faith based solely upon slavery, hypocrisy, and bloodshed. Faith and devotion to such a belief system not only creates mentally deranged followers that enjoy raping of the innocent and killing more than normal sheep,... They create a weak, pathetic, herd-like bond to immoral morals and unpractical ethics that have no place here in this great world.
[The Wolf is playing with something in his hands, a book perhaps.] ["Catholism--the true evil of the modern world."] The Wolf: But it is okay! Men like me are here to help you choose the correct path. We are here to end the brainwashing and teach you sheep to think for yourselves. It is time to stop following a book written by <Major importance screams in his tone!> man for man and start to worship yourselves! Practice indulgence, not abstinence! Practice self-worship, not self-denial! The first step to such a amazing breakthrough comes this week, if you all pay close attention to what I say, <Whatever was screaming died.> with the demise of the New Age
Pope.
[The light grows brighter, allowing the picture before us to be fully realized. The Wolf is sitting in a room of some sort. The walls are a off-white, and the floor is a gray concrete covered in filth. The
Wolf, cloaked in his robe, is busy starting down at a book in his hands--focused to the pages before him. There is even a table behind the Wolf. On it is an assortment of papers and pop cans, most of them seem to be grape Faygo, The Wolf's favorite. Only one thing is out of place--a orange lighter sitting right in the middle of the table.]
["Where is it... I know it is here somewhere..."] The Wolf: This so called great book tells us to eat flesh and dine on blood, like the Neanderthal I am supposed to be. It tells us to drink wine in abundance <A mockery tone.> just like niggers. <The mockery is over.> It gives us great bedtime stories, nothing more! It is the perfect way to brainwash small children into fearing my God instead of loving him, like it should be!. I love my God for what he has done, is doing now, and will do in the future. I love untrue and complete lie filled accounts in his name.. I love the idiots running around lying to the world about what my God is really about. The choose to write untrue lies disgracing my God's name! <The passion is so very apparent.> I love my God. I cherish his very presence. But you would find fault with my God, Pope, for his is Satan, the true master of this realm.
[The Wolf doesn't look up from his book.] ["I know it is here somewhere... I just have to find it..."] The Wolf: <Questioning tone in his voice.> Your "New Age" title amuses me for I wonder what this 'new age' will bring. Will it bring your beloved priests to justice for their crimes upon the precious children? Will it actually perform what it preaches inside the houses of true sin, instead of doing what they preach against?? I hope this
'new age' is a revolution, not a false promise. I really do, Pope.
Not for the sheep in the stands. Not for the little boys backstage. But for you. I do not enjoy being lied to, and all my life your precious church has done nothing but lie, rape, and steal from me. I say it is time for a bit of payback. Dare to change my mind?
[He looks up from his book, his face still hidden deep behind the hood.] ["I found it!"] The Wolf: This my command: Love each other.--John 15:17 <Snicker.> You
Catholics have really followed Jesus' word. I applaud you for showing so much love and compassion for each other. I applaud you all for being so open minded, and loving each man, women, or child for what they are, whether or not you agree with their decisions. <Mad laughter.> What I think I love the most about this book <The Wolf reaches back and snatches up the lighter off the table.> is the simple fact that it refers to its followers as sheep, and that is exactly what you people are. Well, Pope, I am the wolf hunting you down, and soon the Vatican <He flicks the lighter, and brings it towards the
Bible. Soon the pages are on fire.> will be in flames, just like your wrestling career.
["What I love more than anything is how this book refers to its followers as sheep... I would take offense at being called such a weak animal."]
[Mad laughter ensues as The Wolf drops the Bible, allowing it to burn away slowly. He walks away, obviously proud of himself as the black invades the screen.]
------ Pretty bad, eh? I hate the ending... - Delaney
