USCW Flash Central 5/10/98

13 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Marauders Jacob Yoder Atomic Punk Viper The Bishop Abyss Joe Mills Daniel Stephens -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- ( Scene is inside the Marauders locker room, just before the USCW weekend edition. Shawn and Chris look like they're thinking of a strategy to use. Both are wearing the Marauders T-Shirt, and the
Marauders baseball cap. And both of them look up when the camera comes into their locker room. )
Shawn : Daniel Stephens, I can't believe how STUPID you are. You want the match between me, you, and Mystery. Well, let me tell YOU something. Kid Sharpe gets the shot, no matter what you want. You aren't doing ME a favor by getting this shot. I am doing YOU a favor, cause you are the one getting the title shot. Why would I let YOU choose who I'll face?
Shawn : But nevertheless, if Mystery, who I've beaten before, thinks he was robbed, I'll give him a shot. I'm a fighting champion. So let's make this a four way dance. Daniel Stephens, Mystery, the Sharpeness and me, " Shotgun " Shawn Evans. It doesn't take a genius to figure out that it'll start out with me and Sharpe, against Stephens and Mystery.
But if that's how the match goes, so be it. I'm the champion right now ; I decide who gets a shot, not you Daniel Stephens. And Kid Sharpe is the most deserving one right now.
Shawn : On to the next topic. I'm glad Kid Sharpe wants to team. Daniel Stephens, you can decide who you want on your team, but I know who I can trust in the USCW. I'm still waiting for Razor Sharpe's answer, but I know I can trust him. He is probably one of the most righteous wrestlers today, who will help his family. Stephens, you can bring all your friends. But when we step into the cage, me,
The Enforcer, Kid Sharpe, and Razor Sharpe are more ready than ever. Chris : Hey, I got nothing to say, except that I WILL show Chamberlain that I am capable of beating him. I WILL show him why I am " The Excellence ". And I WILL beat him in front of millions of fans. And that's the bottom line.
( The locker room door opens, and Mike Adams, better known as " The Enforcer " walks in with his gym bag. He notices the cameraman, and starts speaking. ) Mike : Hey, how's it going, USCW fans? I don't have much to say, except that I am ready to take on Daniel Stephens and his bunch of cronies with Shawn, and the Two Sharpes ANYTIME he wants. 'Nuff said.
See you at Weekend Edition tonight. ( Mike pushes the cameraman out, as the Marauders once again start discussing their game plan. )
FADE TO BLACK -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- **Jacob Yoder is walking down the street, minding his own business. He is looking in the window at a dress he might buy his wife Mary. In the reflection of the mirror, you see three men approaching Jacob Yoder from behind. **
Bully #1: Hey, Amish guy! Didn't we tell you and your kind to beat it?!?!?! **Jacob just keeps looking at the dress in the shop, like he doesn't even here them. ** Bully #2:**To Bully #3** What? He is acting like he didn't hear us? Go Get his attention! **Bully #3 walks over and grabs Jacob Yoder by the shoulder. Jacob spins around and backhands Bully #3. Bully #3 falls back on to the ground. ** Bully #1:**Suprised** You're gonna pay for that, you butter churning freak! **Bully #1 runs at Jacob and takes a swing at him, Jacob easily ducks the punch, grabs the Bully and applies the Claw to his forehead. ** Jacob:**Screaming** I will show you The Butter Churner you English bully!!!! **Jacob picks the Bully up while still applying the Claw and slams him into the ground and still continues to apply the Claw. Bully#1 is clawing madly at his head, screaming like a little baby. Jacob's face is all red and the veins in his neck are bulging out. **
Bully #2: **To Bully #3** Lets get out of here. Bully #3: Heck ya! This Amish man is crazy! **The two bullies run away and leave there friend in the hands of Jacob. After a couple of min. Jacob releases the hold and Bully #1 just lays there. Jacob turns toward the camera. **
Jacob: See what demons I have Abyss? What I goest through every day of mine life? I am going to destroy thou tonight my little friend. And then this feud wilt come to the bloody end that it deserves. So, you are apart of WTF? Bring them down here too! I wilt kick their butts also. Me and Sinister have met before. And I dare to him to remember what happend the last time he tried to interfere in one of my matches. The bull is out, Abyss, and he is loose.
**Jacob Yoder turns around. Delivers a swift kick into the side of the bully and walks over him. Whistling a hymn as he walks down the road. ** -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (camera zooms in on Venice Beach where you see Atomic Punk pumping iron with the locals. He sees the camera and waves it over...)
Hey Storm it looks like you needed help. You couldn't do it yourself. No matter if I had to face me I would want all the help I could get. I am personally going to haunt you. Be prepared at all times because you never know when I will pop up. I want a rematch. I know I won, but you pissed me off. One on One this time you worthless piece of trash. So keep your SWA pansy's out of it. Now as for Big Mountain lets have that rematch. You and the Horseman alliance are the only respectable wrestlers here and I want to prove to the USCW that I can hold my own with the best. What do you say? Now if fyou will excuse me I have to get back to my public. SWA beware the Atomic Punk is comming for ya.....
(Atomic Punk goes back to working out...camera fades to black...) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Scene opens to Viper sitting backstage at the upcoming USCW card... Viper has his wrestling gear on with a "What the Fuck are ya gonna do now"
T-Shirt on over it... Next to Viper on the bench is the USCW World HeavyWeight Title and the UWF Unified Title... Viper is taping his hands up as he looks up at the camera grinning)
Viper: Yea, I know, I know, I don't actually HAVE a match tonite, but hell ya NEVER know when ya might haveta make an appearance... So it always pays to be ready... Speakin of being READY!
Viper: Shining Path, I am MORE than ready fer yer sorry ass to step in to the ring with me Wednesday... I WAS thinkin we'd have ourselves a good match, OBVIOUSLY that won't be happening anytime soon... So instead I'll look forward to ANOTHER ass kicking by ME... Hope yer pain threshold is raised enuff to take what I'm gonna be givin out boy...
Viper: Shining Path, I THOUGHT you'd be a good opponent and a good contender, OBVOUSLY I was wrong, and I HATE being wrong... SO I'm gonna haveta take that out on you also... basically boyo, Wednesday night will be your MOST painful night ever in the USCW, and I'll be walking away the
USCW World HeavyWeight Champion... As Always...
Viper: So then, Marauder, yer my next PPV opponent, I think I've said all
I NEED to say to you, at least all I need to say up til now.. But just remember Evans number 2... I AM the World Champion for a reason, I'm not the World CHamp by a fluke, I'm the world champ 'cause I AM the baddest motherfucker who was in that tournament... Evans, Marauder, whatever...
Next PPV, you'll be seein WHY IT IS, that I'm the World Champ... Ask Yoder, ask Homosexualicide, ask anyone you want... Stepping in the ring with ME is close to suicide, and what yer planning to do... WILLINGLY mind you, is commit suicide and step in to MY ring
Viper: So there ya have it, not a whole lot goin on today in my mind... I'd talk about Dare, since itz so much fun... But hell, he hasn't said a word on his own in a week or so... THAT just shows the type of LOSER he really is, so Dare, I'll be seein you REAL soon... Watch for me buddy, I'm comin for ya...
Viper: By the way Spicey, IF I remember right, yer little free wrestle-a-thon is tomorrow isn't it??? You KNOW you can't have a free event w/out The Snake showin up... I'll be sure to give all the kids and their mom's a GOOD show, hope ya have yer lawyers and yer EMT's close at hand... 'Cause you'll be needing them before I'm through tomorrow night!!!
'Nuff Said -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- From the Church of the Holy Alliance: Bishop: Sinister, I am surprised at you. Here you are faced with your sins and your coming crucifixion and you still refuse to repent. In fact you defy the Lord even more with your blasphemy. You will surely pay for your evil ways. The Holy Alliance is not to be mocked. You will confess your sins to me. You will do my penance and you will be crucified.
Sinister I never would have thought you were so stupid so as to mock not only myself but also the Lord. The Lord is very angry with you. I spoke with him last night and he guaranteed me a win over you. You have two choices Sinister, you can come to the ring tonight, repent of your sins and I shall beg the Lord to forgive you of your blasphemes against him.
Otherwise, I shall come to the ring and destroy you. If that is the course you choose you will be condemn to eternal damnation. Repent
Sinister or be Crusified! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- <Abyss is sitting on a coushioned bench in his locker room. He's dressed is his wrestling gear, a black tanktop and torn jeans. He's also wearing a piar of dark shades and a new gold eyebrow ring. In his left hand he holds the USCW
World Television Title and in his right he holds a rather large weight which he is using to do curls with>
Abyss: Yoder, you see this belt? It's mine. And it will be mine after tonight. The closest you will EVER come to this title is tonight when Del Rio holds it up for the world to see! You'll also notice a little change on the belt tonight
Yoder. A change that'll shock the whole fed, fans, and executives. This will be a first in USCW and I'm positive someone after me will decide to copy me and take credit for it.
Abyss: Tonight, everyone will see Abyss' outlook on his LONG-TERM reign as the
World Television Champion. Now to our match. Last time I overpowered, outwitted, and outshowed you. Tonight, I'm gonna do all that AND I'm going to shock you with a few cards up my sleave. Now, to your poor wife Marry. I pity you Marry, I'm sure you didn't want to end up with a loser like Jacob, but then again, who else on God's green earth would want a wrinkled ole' battle-ax like you! I'm part of the few talent that this fed has, Yoder, how can you compete with a man of my skill? <Abyss continues doing curls and the screen fades to black> -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------- (Sitting in a dark room is Joseph Mills. The only light that is shining is a
"god light" from
overhead.) Joseph Mills: Once again, children search for fame and fortune. Entering a profession in which they are truly not ready for. Pain, torture, and agony are all the byproducts of this existence. Over the past few day there has been silence from the camp of The
Commonwealth of Pain. I'll explain to you why. There is no longer any such thing as the Commonwealth of Pain. Maxwell Chamberlain came to the realization that he is a washed up old fool who can no longer "hang" with the younger current talent that makes the USCW. He has gone back to England to hide his god awful face from the light of society. Tommy Lowe. Tommy Lowe got fired when he quit making choices that where better for the team and started making choices that filled his Swiss bank account! So I sent him to be with his money and I sent one of his arms to the St. Zappa's Museum as a visible witness of what happens when I get screwed! Me and D.C. are still tight but this is my thing now! I have been on a journey. A journey to strengthen my soul, my body and my mind. I walked across the valley of darkness never fearing the evil
that lives apart of it brushing it aside, destroying it when I needed to, proving that I had
control over the course of my own events. And as I walked throughout that land of silence and truth, my truth was revealed to me. I was a man standing with the WOW North American Singles Title. I was myself on two different times standing with the WOW Worlds Championship. Then my life moved to the BWFE and I saw myself stand as the United States Champion. Then Tommy Lowe came along and told me to try out tag team wrestling! I did and did a damn good job holding the world and the intercontinental titles. Then my life became Total
Destruction and the singals career that I stood as master was also totally destroyed. As I walked in this land I also was a man that cared not for the well being of others around him. Taking all of the chances, the extreme chances that need to be taken. Table, chairs, barbed wire, bats, and branding irons. Any and everything he could get his hands on. And I saw my happiness.
The joy that I once held in this profession. Now Joseph Mills makes his walk back down the road that he owned when he start in this sport. Now Joe Mills starts collecting all of his toys of mass destruction and is ready to show everyone that extreme is in the heart and is not something that is forgotten or suppressed. I have been suppressed in the world of tag team wrestling way too damn long. The singals division must once again feel the fear that it felt when Joseph Mills stepped through the door for the first time. Prepare yourselves for the extreme realization of your worst nightmares. Joseph Mills has spoken.
