[Free agent] Bubba Logan

7 May 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, I thought I'd try out a new character. Whaddaya think? Wrestlers name: Bubba Logan Birthplace: Venice Beach, CA Height: 6'6" Weight: 275 lbs. Disposition/Attitude: Ego-centric jerk that the fans just love because he can go from being beaten down to suddenly surging with adrenalin and not feeling pain.
Ring Attire: Black vest that says "Bubba Rules," black gloves, black bandana on his head, black jeans and black boots.
Personal Appearance: He's a big guy, with a blond Fu Manchu mustache. He's got "the largest arms in the world," and they enable him to pick up ANYBODY. He is going bald, but that's why he wears the bandana.
Personal Background: Bubba used to work for that promotion up north, but he left because he was bored. He's a multi-time
World Heavyweight Champion everywhere he's been, and in fact, the only time he's ever lost were total screwjobs where everyone was out to get him. He's led stables, and he's been a loner.
Name something to do in this great sport, and he's done it. Right now, the fans love him and want him to kick everyone's ass. Single's Entrance: "Voodoo Child" by Jimi Hendrix plays, and the crowd goes wild, chanting "LOGAN! LOGAN!" Bubba walks out and cups his ear to hear the fans chant his name. He hits the ring, pointing at his opponent and motioning that he's going to "break his back."
Primary Wrestling Moves: 1. Punch 2. Kick 3. Chop 4. Bite 5. Body Slam 6. Elbow Drop 7. Belly to Back Suplex 8. Atomic Drop 9. Vertical Suplex 10. Back Rake Set up Move: Big Boot Finisher Name: Leg Drop Finisher Description: Bubba whips his opponent into the ropes and catches his opponent with a big boot to the face, then bounces off the ropes and drops a big leg drop. No one has EVER kicked out of it. EVER!
Sample rp: [It's a stale blue and white interview background, and Bubba Logan is pacing in front of it. In walks Joey Cole, the interview guy.] JC: Logan, you're absolutely out of control! What's going on here?! Logan: Well you know Joey, I'm just sick and tired of the politics, the backstage backstabbing and all the bullcrap that's been going on. You've got people running around calling themselves "big names" getting all sorts of preferential treatment, and brother, you know that gets under my skin.
Because when you go back and look at the history books, look at all the title histories, look at the big picture, you're only going to see one name: Bubba "Hollywood" Logan.
So for all you so-called "big names," get ready to be served notice, dudes. Because I'm cocked, rocked, loaded, and aiming straight for you, brother, and what are you going to do when
Bubba "Hollywood" Logan runs wild on you?! [Bubba rips off his vest and begins flexing, veins popping out everywhere. Fade to an over-the-hill star trying to maintain his prior glory.]
