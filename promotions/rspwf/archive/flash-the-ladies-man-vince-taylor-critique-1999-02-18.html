[Flash] "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor [Critique]

18 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a roleplay I did for the WCWA. Please tell me what you think, and things that I need to improve on.
************************************* [Scene: Los Angeles, California. Time: 4:30 P.M. The camera opens to the streets of Los Angeles. They are crowded, with people who are hurrying to get to where they need to be. The people are rushing past the big buildings that make up the metropolitan area. A street with cars rushing by is also in view.
Business men going home from work, and crazy cab drivers trying to make a buck. The camera keeps walking with the flow of people, continually passing different buildings. But then as you pass one of the huge skyscrapers, "The
Ladies Man" Vince Taylor emerges. He starts to walk the oppposite way of the camera.]
[Vince is wearing a white three piece Armani suit, that has black pin stripes running down it. He has on black wing tip shoes on his feet. On his head, concealing his shortly shaved black hair, is his usual white hat. A slight goatee is growing on his face, but nothing very noticeable. Oh his nose rests his trademark, gold framed Rayban sunglasses. On his wrist, his shining Rolex watch is visible. Also on his hand is a small diamond pinkey ring. The camera turns around and follows Vince, and at first he doesn't notice the camera. But when he does he begins to speak...]
"The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor: You know, "The Ladies Man" has truely come a long way. I came into the WCWA as a newcomer, somebody that hadn't really proven a thing to the wrestling world. I showed up and got a win over a great wrestler, Tony Paesano. I told the world that I was the best damn lightweight to walk this Earth, but not everybody listened, although I was turning heads.
Then I somehow managed to take a step backwards, and lose to Xenon. Then people looked at me as just another guy out there trying to make a living. But then I once again proved the world wrong when I stepped into the squared circle at No Love Lost. I was up against Kidd, with the Lightweight Title on the line... and more than likely if they were betting in Vegas, the odds were probably against me. And if you played the odds, you definitely lost. I walked into the ring and absolutely _DOMINATED_! And now look at me, the
Lighweight Champion of the WCWA.
And this Saturday, at Saturday Supreme, I will be defending my title for the first time. Of course it will only be the first of many to come. I am up against "Kick Ass" Chris Carter, who is a former Lighweight Champion, and I emphasize _FORMER_! He got his shot at taking over the Lightweight division, but he just couldn't handle it and he lost the belt. Well now the belt is with
"The Ladies Man", where it will stay for quite a long time now.
[Vince keeps walking, and eventually they walk into a park. The park has a few people there. A few high school kids are shooting basketball, while other people are just out taking a stroll. Vince walks over to an empty bench and leans up against in...]
"The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor: [Smiling.] I'm the WCWA Lightweight Champion, and it feels pretty good. In fact, it feels damn good. I definitely don't wanna lose this feeling, and I'm definitely _NOT_ gonna lose this feeling. I feel I've got an edge coming into this match Saturday. I'm coming off the biggest win of my career, while your coming off the biggest loss of yours.
I've got the title around my waist, and you don't. I'm am on cloud nine, and it will take Jesus Christ himself to knock me off.
And then its the little things that are going to make a difference. Its staying the extra half hour at the gym. Staying home instead of going out with the women so you can study tapes of your opponents. Laying off the alcohol, so you don't damage your ability to perform in the ring at all. These are all things that I've done in order to get ready for this match. And they are also things that I would be willing to bet you aren't doing. I've trained, studied tapes of you, and then trained some more... for one reason. And that reason is so I can stay on top, so I can retain my title. This more than likely will be the most intense match that I have ever wrestled in it, and if by some unforseen reason I would lose this match, it won't be because I wasn't prepared.
[Vince then moves his body off of the bench, and quits leaning up against it. He walks around and takes a sit on the bench. He adjust his sitting position and the continues...]
"The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor: I don't look for something like that to happen though. I'm just too prepared to have anything go wrong. Mentally and physically, I'm in the best shape of my life. But I also know Carter, that your in some great shape yourself, but as good as "The Ladies Man?" I highly doubt that. In fact, I'm sure your not. Your good, in fact you better than most people, but then againt, I'm not most people. I'm a cut above the rest...
a step above. That goes for everybody, including you Carter. Sure, if I wasn't here, you probably would be the Lighweight Champion right now... but thats just tough luck.
And then I have to address Kidd. Kidd, you better not show up at this match. Because even though I'm fighting with Carter, we both hate your guts. And I'm pretty sure Carter would help me beat your ass if you came out to interfere in any way, in anybody's favor. You got that? And if you don't, I'm sure a Money
Maker off the top rope would help you to understand.
So Carter, I'll go ahead and beat your ass come Saturday night. And you know why? Because...
I'M THE BEST.. DAMN LIGHTWEIGHT.. TO EVER.. WALK THIS EARTH! [Fade to black.] [name withheld] Handler of: "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor [WCWA 2-1] WCWA Lightweight Champion [NDW 0-0] "Excellent" Evan Andrews [WCWA 1-1] Simply Irresitible [WCWA 2-0] WCWA Tag Team Champions [ICW 1-2]
