(ESSAY) RSPWF as a network

1 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello again everyone. I know I missed last weeks essay but that was because I couldn't think of anything to write. As a special thing I am writing an essay on an idea of [name withheld]'s about RSPWF being like a wrestling television network. I personally am fully for the idea of RSPWF being a network. Since lots of federations post their shows on RSPWF already it would hopefully eliminate pointless posts (CFW and DPW SUCKS!!!!!!!!!! comes to mind as one.) RSPWF is pretty cluttered as it is. You get everyone giving in their shows, people writing essays and discussing ideas. It would help if RSPWF was a network. Now onto the extremely controversial part of this essay. I for one have posted 'Join my fed' posts to RSPWF telling about the fed, the rules and the application form. Other posts like one awhile ago which he posted about 15-16 times with the same message only a different title are totally stupid. If the RSPWF network idea becomes reality I think that moderators should be used to approve of 'Join my Fed' posts. That way all the feds that close after a week or have 10', 567lbs wrestlers (Rook and Knight you know who you are) that are identical twins and beat three wrestlers on their own etc., etc. If people don't like that idea then how about an RSPWFN newsgroup. I have no idea how you get one but how about a vote for the idea (Yeah bote for me!!!) RSPWFN would be the Fantasy Wrestling Network (FWN) and would be a great thing for getting your feds particular shows on the air. It would possibly eliminate certain posts (My FEdS dA beSt so YU mUsT JoIn.) Well there it is folks. I hope you liked but if you didn't tough luck. Now everyone can go off and get their little controversy essays writing so that everyone can flame me. NEXT TIME: Expanding on [name withheld]'s 'What If?' essay.
