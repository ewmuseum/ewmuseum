Jade 'The Blade' Interview.

14 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~~~{The scene opens up in Vero Beach, Florida at Jade 'The Blade's' palacial home. The camera is focused on Jade as he is sitting comfortably in his lavish easy chair. Jade is wearing a red silk dinner jacket and has no longer the long blonde hair that his trademark. Now and only now he has it cut short, much like that of a Malenko. Jade begins to speak...}~~~
```<Jade>```Alot of people have wondered where I have been and what I have been doing. Many couldn't care less and that is okay by me. I have done my best to try and prove that I have changed my attitude and if people can't accept that, well then I can't fault them.
~~~(Jade looks around and with a smile, continues...)~~~ ```<Jade>```Now, after a long hard process of singling out a federation to return to on a full time status, I have decided upon the EWC. It is a superb federation and is ran fairly well. Coming up on July 26, 1998, I will be in an
EWC battle royal to attempt to regain my Midwest title belt that I forfeited just weeks ago. Many great competitors in that one, but I predict that I will wear that gold strap once more.
~~~(Jade gets up and starts to stroll around his den and nears his championship collection, he points at it and then speaks.)~~~
```<Jade>```Many of different federations have I been a part of and I have had my ups and my downs. I have been a tag champion and of course many time singles champion and I must say...
"I could never have reached the point I am at now, if it hadn't been for Ian Walsh." This man gave me my first break into this sport and I owe what success I have had to him.
```<Jade>```In closing, I can only thank everyone out there who has been by my side. Such stars that are my friends are Jack Vars, Iron Man and many others that would take too long to name. To all the stars in the EWC, I say beware of
'The Blade' because I am back and as for the other stars throughout the different organizations, I can only say that I appreciate each and every one of you.
~~~(The camera man gets up to leave, but Jade stops him and replies...)~~~
```<Jade>```I am Jade and come the 26th of this month, I will once again be the
EWC Midwest Champion.
~~~(Jade then motions to the camera man to leave and the scene fades to black.)~~~ Jade 'The Blade' [detail withheld]
