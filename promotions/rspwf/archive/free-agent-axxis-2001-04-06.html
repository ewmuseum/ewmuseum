~{Free Agent}~ Axxis

6 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
We're a teag team interested in being signed with an e-mail based fed. Weekly cards & flash shows, monthly PPV's, and an all around fun experience requested.
Send any interested replies to [detail withheld] -------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------- ">(The scene opens to a large street in Anywhere U.S.A., the sky is a deep indigo indicating that it is late at night. The camera pans across the sky to reveal that there are no clouds in the sky, so the stars are shiny brightly, and the moon, not only full, but has a unique orange hue to it. The camera returns to its normal position and taking pictures of the street.
The only action on the street is that of late night revelers, and early morning delivery trucks. A bundle of newspapers are dropped on a corner, the trucks drives down the street, leaving bundles of papers and doorways of every convience store on the street.
The camera pans over to a building that looks much like an abandoned warehouse. The camera zooms into a window to see, that a large training facility has been built on the second floor. A ring stands in the center of the room, stained with blood, and sweat. Two figures stand just outside of the ring.
One man, who appears to be in his late twenties or early thirties, points to various points in the ring. The scars and lines on his face indicates that he has been a few battles in and outside of the ring. Ripped jeans, steel toe boots and a skeleton tee-shirt is what this man is wearing. In each of his hands, he holds a bottle of Wild Turkey. A wide smile appears on his face as he continues to explain something to the second man.
The second man, is visibly shorter than his friend, however his physique is better defined. This man is wearing a wrestling mask with a symbol across the front of the mask. Black tights and boots, with the same red symbol on the tights and the boots. Large welts and open wounds cover his back. He listens intently to what is being said.
The two of them look to the door, as the wind begins to rattle it. Both assume a fighting stance, but after they figure it out it's the wind, they do nothing.
They both look at each other, chuckle and continue to talk. Suddenly the phone rings, and the masked man answers.)
Taurus: Yes...This is he. Oh really...yeah he's right here....I can....okay...if you want to here he is. (hands the phone over to Carver)
Carver: You've got a minute, speak. No. We don't. Ever. I don't give a damn how much you're willing to shell out! No toy stores! (Carver slams the phone back down on the receiver.) What the hell goes on in their minds? Let's take
Axxis, they love to cripple people, and bring them to our toy store. What do they think, you can tell the little maggots about living on the streets and that'll sell more Pikachu dolls?
Taurus: Yeah, I don't know what to make of any of those idiots, the fans. No matter where I've been, my message of truth has fallen upon the ears of the deaf. What is it going to take them to realize them that we speak the truth, and they are just pawns in the game of their disposable heroes. Buy more shirts, buy our posters, that is the <b>only</b> thing that they are concerned about. Putting money in their pockets. We tell you to your faces that we can't stand you, while they smile to you, while in the backstage, they're bad mouthing you, your moms and your dogs. No one ever bad mouths my dog....
Carver: Your dog is dead. You told me about it. He died when you were twelve, you told me yourself. We're about to get back into the pain game, don't let your emotions get the better of you at this stage of the game.
Taurus: Emotions, things of the weak. Although I must admit I did love once, but like everything else it left me.
Her name was Raisin, a six year old Irish setter. She was the family dog. She used to follow me everywhere. If I would go off base to buy bread, right
behind me would be. Well, on that fateful day, which will be twenty years ago next week, the day they took my parents away, Raisin went as well. The dog pound, maybe, put right to sleep, most likely. I've learned that this government would in fact kick you when you are down.
Carver: Kicking...is something Axxis will be doing a hell of a lot of. Keep that in mind, Taurus. Soon we explode upon the scene in NAWF, all the years of hate and rage can be unleashed at long last. Apparently we're in some gigantic tag team match to decide the top team. It was bound to happen sooner or later, of course. Perhaps the "powers that be" in NAWF have more sense and taste than their contemporaries, and see the truth...That Axxis can knock down the very best they can throw at us, and without breaking much of a sweat.
Taurus: Hannibal, they have us opening the show, f'in curtain jerkers. Do they not know about the trails of bodies we've left here and in the Far East?
Body counts, maneuvers banned due to the just brutality of them, and we're opening with the show against four idiots and a five knuckle shuffler by the name of "Nukeman." The very thought of stepping in the ring with someone with that moniker, makes my very bowels ache in a most uncomfortable manner.
What NAWF is going to find out, is that these "newbies" will put their veterans in their place, the intensive care unit. We will set the standard of which that show will be judged. In fact all of the other matches will pale in comparison to the bloodletting and the blantant disregard for human life. So,
Nukeman, Too Fly J, Anthrax, Dennis Fontaine, and Mike Dynasty, I hope your stay in NAWF was a pleasant one, because Axxis is here to send you packing to the great beyond. Hell, I don't care if the Maze Brothers, or Slash even show up. It'll be more fun for us to have anyway.
Carver: Yes, but this has nothing to do with fun. This has everything to do with business. Having us open the show is an insult, but an inescapable one.
It's what is known as "paying your dues". Even though you and I have paid dues that would drive most men insane, we must start out at the bottom of the ladder here in NAWF. Nukeman...I won't even begin to mention what I think of your name. Anthrax...I guess you could see you are to be the first victim of
Axxis...as I shall make you the first to fall prey to the plague we shall bring forth, "spreading the disease", you might say. Too Fly J...Once you feel your bones pop as I hook you in the Silence of The Lambs, you'll be lucky if you're
"Able To Go To The Bathroom Without The Aid Of A Trained Monkey J". Fontaine,
Dynasty, don't worry...I haven't forget about your skinny asses. Axxis will be more than happy to assist Slash and the Maze Brothers in putting you on the shelf, permanantly.
Is this the start of a beautiful friendship between ourselves, the Maze Brothers, and Slash? I know Slash can deal out more than his share of carnage, and I'm hoping the Maze Brothers are wearing their very best ass-kicking shoes to the dance as well. If we can cripple the morons on the opposite side of the ring together at Destruction, then all three men will have taken a step in the right direction, as far as Axxis is concerned.
(Carver looks over at Taurus, nodding at the young combatants in the ring) Watching these weak-kneed idiots is depressing me. I'm going to kill this bottle and take a nap. I'll meet you outside in a half hour.
(Taurus nods, and shakes hands with Carver as he leaves. He miiediately turns his attention back to the ring, where he chastises one of the combatants for applying a Step Over Toe Hold that his grandmother could get out of as the scene fades to black.)
