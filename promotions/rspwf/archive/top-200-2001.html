Simon Solkoff [SEWF] 25. Roddy Taylor [SPW] Taylor the current SPW World Champion is locked in a heated fued with more than one person right now. Not much can be said about this person, he is just phenomenal. 24. Burt Clifton [SEWF] From passive-aggressive golden boy to all around bad man, Clifton has transformed himself into something to behold. 23. Adam Mitchell [SEWF] A truly dynamic heel who ruled the SEWF for most of 2001 with a rare display of anger and hilarity. 22. Quinn Brown [MBC, UWF] Brown came into his own this year, challenging Youth Gone Wild to a best of seven series of matches that thrilled the world. Though he officially lost the tally 4 to 3, Brown risked life and limb each time out and only succumbed to a cheap victory or two by Wild. He won the annual Road to the Gold in August of 2001, and is nipping on the heels of the most prestigious title in the game, the UWF Title. 21. Doc Holliday [UEW] The man that smashed every UEW record holding the UEW title from September to April. Also an impressive showing in MOTR IV and MOTR VI favorite to make the finals. He's not wrestling in a promotion since UEW closed but if he shows up, he's an instant threat to any title. 20. Steve Spector [EMWC] One of the most overlooked wrestlers in the game today, Spector has quietly held his own against bigger and tougher opponents in EMWC, recently ending the record setting World Title reign of The Gremlin to reign supreme as a World Heavyweight Champion for the first time since MLWO closed more than three years ago. Kudos to the champ, he deserves it. 19. Reaper [AWP, CSW] The Reaper is a relative new-comer to this "circuit". Coming into the spotlight at the beginning of this year, bursting onto the scenes and making the mid-years. Reaper hasn't shown many signs of slowing down. 18. "Legacy" Luke Sampson [UWF] Current Heavyweight Champion of the World in UWF, Luke began the year by competing in the EiT for the services of Erin McCoys, only to lose out to Dan Kauffman in the finals. No matter, as Sampson and McCoys proved to be in cahoots the whole time, sending Dan into semi-retirement. By the end of the year Sampson had grabbed the world title, gotten into a blood fued with Chris Courtade and generally ruled the UWF with an iron fist. 17. "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop [EMWC, NEO] Deserves all the credit in the world and definitely has become one of the better wrestlers in the industry in recent times. Came into NEO Wrestling like a storm, taking the Heavyweight Championship only a few months after debuting. 16. "Dirty" Doyle Woodall [AVW] This dirty Texan came in to the AVW, an unknown, but after just a few months they are calling him the "biggest, baddest" man around. From the outset his goal was to raise hell and chalk up some W's in the process. He takes no prisoners, well, other than the Appalachian Title. 15. "Hot Shot" Stevie Scott [KCW] Longest reigning KCW Volunteer [World Title] champ of all time will was the king of a big kingdom. The creator of Stevietainment was on top of the world in KCW holding the main title for a good six months before losing it to the Gremlin at Summer Heat. This guy is as good as the hype if not more. 14. "Blackheart" Casey James [EMWC] The Killing Box fued alone gives James the honor of being one of the best wrestlers of the year. The reigning King of the Death Match, Casey James lost a finger in his thrillingly sick victory over Caleb Temple in the Killing Box at Showtime. Gotta love the Blackheart. 13. Art Blake [SEWF] He was right up there with Adam Mitchell, battling with him for the better part of the year. Art is the quintessential "wrestler's wrestler." He's got good mat skills, he's good on the stick, and he's champion, as well. 12. Orbit [MBC] This unlikely hero scored the biggest upset in MBC history when he defeated longtime champion Tom Landis for the MBC World Title at Kingdom Come 2001 in the summer. Prior to that Orbit competed as half of the Trailer Park Assassins, one of the top tag teams in wrestling. Now with the MBC shutting down operations, Orbit looks to defend his title one last time and retire the strap for good. The quiet storm, the technical master...pro wrestling's answer to Rain Man. 11. Lance Dewlock [FWG] He, Lance Dewlock, is a former FWG TV Champ and is the current FWG World Triple Crown Champion. Has rassled and defeated the best that FWG has to offer, and he's heading into BUWE looking for more gold, in the form of the BUWE World Heavyweight strap. The "Man of Tea and Crumpets" is truly one of the best up and comers in the sport today. 10. "Cool Hand" Luke Kinsey [EMWC, RCW] Kinsey had an iron grip on the RCW Light Heavyweight Championship for six months before finally dropping it to Raya Oscura. In EMWC, he came out the winner of their Extreme J Tournament, winning the EMWC World Junior Heavyweight Title along the way....and much more will come of Kinsey in the coming year. 9. "Hot Stuff" Brian Von Braun [UWF, GLCW] Von Braun spent much of the year as a member of the Invaders, and shortly after took the North American Title for a span of months. In a memorable moment of 2001, BVB turned on then-partner Sylvester Landeau and threw a fireball into his face. The results caused Landeau to miss months of action, having only returned a short time ago. And don't think Von Braun won't gloat about that. 8. "Mr. Flawless" Jake Davids [AVW] Davids has held the AVW Bluegrass Title for about 8 months going into Minutes To Memories. He was been the inagural AVW Bluegrass champion, and has faced all comers, rejecting them at each point, before losing the strap to Brian "Thunder" Storm at MTM. 7. "Pimpalicious" Leevon Parks [AVW] Leevon has smacked his bitches around all year in AVW. From the start to finish he's been in the mix of everything, holding the AVW Appalachian Title for a good portion. Solid, and hard working.....he's always around somewhere. 6. "Outlaw" Travis LaGrange [RAW, AVW, MLW] The Outlaw's rise through the ranks of RAW was nothing short of meteoric, winning the World title from William 'Jugger' Bennett at Battlezone, which capped a wonderful first 6 months or so in the fed. Add participation in the last ever MOTR tournament, and there can be no other number one. This guy is the goddamn future of E-Wrestling. 5. Caleb Temple [EMWC] Hmm.......let's see. Blew up Jeff Matthews, knocked up Alex Martinez's wife, and cost Casey James a finger.....all in one year's time. Many still believe him to be the best wrestler and most disturbed in the world....not many can prove that wrong, as there are VERY few well written religous characters. He is the best. Bar none. His feud with James was one of the best of all time and though he lost...he re-established himself as one of the best. 4. Brian "Thunder" Storm [AVW] He has one of the best old school heel acts in the book, and now the AVW belt. This man is the man. Came into AVW in a "storm" (pardon the pun), and forced the retirement of Jim "The Crusher" Johnson. Rebuilt the Rudeboys with Loaded and Spikyjim. Took out Max Pain with 5 chair shots at Going the Distance, and is walking around with the Bluegrass belt around his waist. 3. The Gremlin [EMWC, KCW, GLCW] He ruled all this year. Unquestionably one of the best in the game, winning the every last title possible in the world today......well, alright, almost. He's grabbed EMWC World Title, KCW World Title, and the GLCW Triple Crown. I mean shit, you can't do much more then that...definitely not in a single year. The foul mouthed Gremlin is one of the fierciest competitors in this sport and his track record proves it so. 2. Devon Case [EMWC, RCW] The year Devon Case was born. He captured the RCW National title at the No Limits supercard. He also had one of the most thrilling feuds in EMWC with Jake Shaw that climaxed with a 90 minute time limit 2/3 falls contest at Showtime. Also had a short run in N.E.W and is currently tearing the rings up in RCW, as Case has become a true top name in RCW with that National title of which he's carrying around. Just edged out The Gremlin to take the number two spot. 1. "Cold Blooded" Chris Courtade [EMWC, RCW, UWF] Legend killer. Headlined NEW, headlines UWF, though both federations were slow at times, he was the elite of the elite for a long time putting down name after name after name. A UWF World Heavyweight title reign (held on to it for record length), he also saw a reign as N.E.W. World Champion early in the year, and also held the UWF and EMWC North American Titles along the way. It doesn't matter what your name is, chances are you have fallen to Courtade. The past people who have ran the 200's always talk about how much the first place rassler won by a landslide, well, if this is any indication, Courtade almost doubled Devon Case in points.....so talk about landslides.
