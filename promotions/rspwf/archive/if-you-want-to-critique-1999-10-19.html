If you want to critique...

19 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a post for my character "Future Man" Wooten. I'm basically ducking a match to further an angle that's coming up soon. The site's the Imperial
Wrestling Alliance ( http://www.angelfire.com/az/ecwarzone ) and I have to say right now that I don't think i'm the best player in the fed at all... just the one posting to RSCWF now.
-------------------------------------------------------- "A Phone Call..." On a frisbee golf course outside of Nashville, Tennessee, "Future Man" Wooten stands slightly away from a group of guys under a canopy of trees. The leaves intermittently falling are showing the entire spectrum of autumn colors.
Wooten's holding a cellular phone to his ear. "What do you mean I got a match? All i know is that somebody Fed-Exed me a dead bird with its leg tied to a coke bottle, with notes on both!"
The sound of coughing comes from the group behind him, and Futch turns to them while shaking his head.
"No, Greeves wasn't in the room when I was talkin'... "He did? Well g*ddamn! I guess that tournament is important to him! "...Well, okay you can tell him that I... hold on a sec." Wooten sets the phone down on the ground and pulls a frisbee out of his bag. He sets up on the tee-off pad, and delivers a straight-on throw that lands about twenty feet shy of the target. Then he picks the phone up again. It's a very leisurely game today.
"Okay, I'm back.Just tell him i'm a little bit stove up, okay? I can hardly..." He has to pause as one of the group hits a hole in one to jump up and down, muffling the phone and complimeting a killer shot.
"Sorry, I can hardly put any weight on this leg at all. "... Yeah, I'm sittin' in a chair at home, of course! "There ain't no way I'm gonna be able to fly to Moscow, no, too much goin' on with the home front this week.
"... Somebody else? Well, who thought it'd be cool to send me... whatever the hell it was I got. The dead bird.
"... Heh! And he didn't get into the empty slot? Well, let Greaves officially know that I'm going to, and make sure you quote me on this, 'bequeath my semifinal slot to my new buddy Harm,' okay?
"...Yeah. Harm Wiggins, that's right." At the mention of that name, a couple guys in the group perk up a little bit. They must be part of the cult. "... I don't care! Let the big fella get another shot at it! Just... just tell him, okay? "Well, yeah, I know how this looks, but Cooper'll get his. I gotta go ice this knee up a little more. See you after the show, all right?
"... Oh yeah, that's gonna rule. Whatever. Catch you later." He turns off the phone at that, and picks up his disc bag, because his group has finally set off to finish this hole, and he really wants to keep up with the game this time.
Futch
