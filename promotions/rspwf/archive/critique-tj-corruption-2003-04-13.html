[CRITIQUE]-[TJ Corruption]

13 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First of all, yes Handy, I know the name sucks.. Second of all, it's just a quickie I wrote up for EWC, just trying to get a little feedback.. It's nothing special but I thought it was a pretty decent flash for about 15 minutes of work.
========================== [ Fade In.. ] [ The scene fades in as you see the familiar face and build of "God's Gift" TJ
Corruption. The camera pans out further and you see Corruption atop a large pure green grassy hill. The sun is barely setting and Corruption leans up against a tree.. As if just noticing the camera Corruption turns his face towards it and motions for it to come closer. The camera zooms in on Corruption and your speakers pick up as he begins to speak.. ]
"GOD'S GIFT" TJ CORRUPTION: Ah, Ireland.. Pretty decent country to say the least. I make my glamerous EWC debut here come tomorrow evening.. Yet, this land is also pretty significant to this industry as a whole. Did you know that the Irish race of today is popularly known as the Milesian Race, because the genuine Irish, or Celtic, people were supposed to be descended from Milesius of
Spain, whose sons, say the legendary accounts, invaded and possessed themselves of Ireland a thousand years before Christ.
The races that occupied the land when the so-called Milesians came, chiefly the
Firbolg and the Tuatha De Danann, were certainly not exterminated by the conquering Milesians. Those two peoples formed the basis of the future population, which was dominated and guided, and had its characteristics moulded, by the far less numerous but more powerful Milesian aristocracy and soldiery.
[ Corruption reaches into his pocket, pulls out a Turkish Gold cigarette, lights it, and then places it back in his pocket. Corruption takes a few drags off of it and then exhales looking back into the camera. ]
"GOD'S GIFT" TJ CORRUPTION: If you don't find that significant to the industry of professional wrestling Mr. Hostile, then your even closer to the brink of down syndrome as I previously believed. It's completely unskilled, untalented, incompetent pieces of shit like you that make that brief history lesson significant.
When the Irish or Milesian's came to the land of Ireland, although much more powerful then the native tribes just simply couldn't fully exterminate them.
Therefore, they hung around, just a big incompetent group of people. People like you Hostile, your like the past native tribes of Ireland.
[ Corruption takes a few more drags of his cigerette and a smirk creeps onto his face.. ] "GOD'S GIFT" TJ CORRUPTION: The absolute significance? Trash like you seem to stick around even though your constantly getting beat on.. Try to stick around even though your completely inferior to the upper echelon of the game.
Hostile, I _AM_ that upper echelon. I am _THE_ echelon, I am _THE_ king of this industry, and kid, it's good to be king. It's good to know your a king, it's good to know that no one can hold a candle to you. Evidently, nothing is very good for you Hostile. You've got nothing to look back on and praise, you've done nothing in your career, you will _NOT_ do anything in your career, and it's even an over statement to say it's a career to begin with.
[ Corruption finishes off his Turkish Gold and then flicks it to the right of him.. Corruption puts his hands in his pocket and leans lower against the tree.
] "GOD'S GIFT" TJ CORRUPTION: I mean I'll tell you flat out right here that I feel sorry for you. I feel sorry that you think your some sort of golden god, I feel sorry that you feel you have an iota of skill, and I feel sorry that you truthfully believe your going to beat me in my wrestling ring, my _KINGDOM_ if you will.
I hope your prepared to take a devestating loss tomorrow evening. Honestly, I want you to take a look at your life after you get up from being pinned and ask yourself why you lead such a worthless life. Ask yourself why you continue to work as a child in an industry among men. Finally, ask yourself who the king of this industry is, and if you don't have the answer then simply remember who pinned you and walked out on April 14th laughing at your pathetic existance. I can guarantee no two-bit peasant like yourself will _NOT_ dethrone the king.
Take my words to the bank, they are as good as gold. _You've_ _Just_ _Been_ _Blessed._ Goodbye.
