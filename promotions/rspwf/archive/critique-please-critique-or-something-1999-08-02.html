Critique! Please Critique or something

2 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a new guy for me, and his first RP, coulda been a lot better, but gimme some feedback please!
[Fade In] [The scene comes into night, on the top of a somewhat secluded hill. There are trees all around the base of the tree, the moon shines down, lighting up the land below. The lights of a distant city can barely be seen, and there are few of them, everyone has gone to sleep, it is obviously very late at night. The camera pans, and there is a single tree on the top of the hill, with a man sitting under it. He is leaning up against the tree, with his legs stretched out in front of him, he is totally covered by the shadow of the tree with the exception of his large arm, with a tattoo across the bicep that reads
"Nyghtfire". The man sighs and begins to speak.] Damien Nyghtfire: So peaceful this world is at times like this. Right now, it seems as if there could never be any problems on this earth whatsoever, all is so calm....But looks can be deceiving. The earth, and the people that dwell on it so have so many obvious parallels. Both can be so calm, and seem so pleasant, but we all have an ugly side...We all have those times when nothing is going to be easy, and we lash out at the world. Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to the ugly side of wrestling...welcome to the VRW!! A new force in the world of wrestling, it is where I have chosen to begin my wrestling career at, for it seems as if it is the best place. Within less than a week, the world will be shocked by the actions of the VRW as it begins its climb to the top of all wrestling promotions, and fans, and wrestlers alike, will see a new force coming to power. No, I am no longer talking about this promotion, now I speak of a different force. Damien Nyghtfire's time has come, and I will seize this opportunity..Victim number one...Beefcarver....
Damien Nyghtfire: Beefcarver...I cannot say that I have heard of you before, thus I know not what to expect. But I guess that is fine, for the world knows nothing of Damien Nyghtfire, yet soon it too will have to deal with me. Beefcarver, you and I will lock up in the VRW's version of a strap match,
I do not know what the powers that be in the VRW have in store for us..but as for you, I know exactly what is in store for you. Beefcarver, Friday night, you take your own little personal tour of the hell that so many more will be going through before my time here is done. One day, in the not so distant future, Damien Nyghtfire will reign over the VRW, and you will always be able to say that you were the first, you were the one that took the pummeling before anyone else had to, it may be a bright spot in an over-powering storm that you must face Beefcarver. It will hit quickly, and end just as fast, but the fury this storm will carry, you could not possibly fathom in your mind right now
Beefcarver..prepare yourself, for your days are numbered!
[Nyghtfire slowly shifts his weight, and his exposed arm is no taken into the darkness of the shadows. He sits motionless now, and begins to speak again.]
Damien Nyghtfire: I know what you're thinking, you think I'm just another person who had a rough childhood, and went into a shell to live the rest of my life, right? Well, in that sense, you may very well be correct.
But I am far from your classic "dark" wrestler, as I soon will show each and everyone of you. I know as you sit out at home, and you listen to this right now, you think I am just blowing off a lot of steam, and I will fizzle myself out before I ever get to take off...[chuckles] Think what you will, yet soon...very soon, you all will wish you could go back in time and re-think your first impression of me. Beefcarver, I would advise that you take my warning very seriously, that you do not know what you are facing here. This will be a dangerous match..Not only because of the stipulation, not only because of the federation....but because of who it is that you are facing. Do you doubt me?
By all means, please do...I want you to Beefcarver, because when you do, your screams and pleads for mercy will sound that much sweeter to me!
[Nyghtfire wipes sweat from his goatee] Damien Nyghtfire: What a glorious day it will be, when finally I can step into the wrestling ring and show the world who I am, and what their future holds for them. I am a new-comer to wrestling, but I have been around the sport for quite some time, an acquaitance of the wrestling legend known as
The Reaper, the very man that trained me to step into this sport. The legacy that he left behind is one that cannot be matched, yet, now a new member of the darkness will begin a romp through the world of wrestling. None of you believe me, and that is very understandable, who would? But I will guarentee this, it will not take more than one or two matches for my point to be proven, and then you all shall know that I was not lying...when I speak, it is the truth!
Beefcarver....heed my warning, do not try to be a man and mock this, it is no laughing matter! Friday Night....you belong to me! Beefcarver...May Darkness
Imprison you! Beware and be warned, for nothing else matters, and nothing is safe again! Welcome.....to the DarkSide!!
[Nyghtfire leans back against the tree, and sits motionless looking out into the scenic landscape.]
[Fade Out]
