*Flash* Tell Me What I Need To Do To Make It Better. . .

23 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Subj: FLASH MAXIMIZE IT FOR BEST VIEW Date: 6/23/99 1:54:04 AM EST From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] *MAXIMIZE* [The Scene Opens up in Atlanta, Ga. You can see the sun begging to set. The sky is three different layers of colors. The first layer is a bright orange then it fades into a lighter ten. Then into a purplish Blue tent.]
[The Viewer Sees a sign that say's Golden Globe Gym. The building is made of complete crick. It appears to be kinda old. There are a few cars in the parking lot not to many.]
[You see a White Dodge Van Pull up. It has Black Bold italic letters written across the side. They Say N. E. W. The Driver Side Door opens up and a Man step's out. He appears to be Thirtyish. The man is wearing a hideous Black
Suit and has his thin hair parted over his Bald Spot. Trying to make it look as if he is not that old. The man Looks in the rear View mirror and
Straightens his Tie. Then the sliding door opens up and your normal Average
Camera man steps out. You can tell he is an Announcer by the mic in his hand.
You know the one who his the beard and has his hat turned around backwards. The "Druggie" type of Modern Day Hippie.] [Then You see the Golden Globe Gym Door open up and you see a Dark Complected man walk out. He appears to be about Six five or so. He is wearing a Black
T-shirt that Say's N.E.W Written across it in white letters. The sleeves are cut off. The man is also wearing a pair of White Elastic Tommy Hilfigure
Shorts.]
[The Man has his black hair slicked back into a tight pony tail that comes down to his neck but maybe a little further. He has light Gray eye's. You can defiantly notice the mans Muscular arms. They look very Sturdy and the Tattoo of barb wire around his left arm. On the other arm is a Tattoo of Fire and right below it Say's "Sure Fire".]
[The Announcer approaches The Dark Complected Man.] [N.E.W Announcer]: "Hi I am Jack Gatlin and I am from N.E.W and I was wondering if I could get a word with you Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin on your match for the Florida State Championship."
[Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin]: "Sure Why Not? I mean I am new here and all the air time I can get I will need. Like look at all the big names we have in this federation. We have "Iron Man" Eddie Jacobs, Joe Boxcar, and the, list goes on and on. . . .
"Well, you know I am countdown the day's until I get to step into the ring with the 'Unbeatable man" Jay Emes. . . . ."
"Jay when you step into the ring you think about what you are getting your self into. I mean Look at me. I Do not look like that much do I _Jay_. I bet you are thinking this is going to be a walk in the park. Jay do not underestimate your enemy. I just might Surpass you and the rest of these people in this
Small Royal Rumble."
"As I look through the many names stepping into this Rumble I see all the talent. This is going to be in the match. You know I have always dreamed of the day when I get to make a name for my self. I mean this will decide the
_men_ from the _Boys_."
[Mitch reaches down into his pocket and pulls out a pack of Marlboro Lights and a lighter. He opens the pack up and pulls out a cigerit. He lights it up and continues Speaking.]
[Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin]: "Well let me just go through the people who are going to be in this match up. . ."
"First We Have "So Damn Cool" Jay Emes. Which If I have said it once I have said it a thousand times? "Super Man" Jay Emes the so called unbeatable man.
I am Gonna bring some Criptinight (SP?) for you." "Then We have Vendetta. . . Well, I cannot say I have herd of him that much but he has to be atleast decent to get into the N.E.W So I am not going to count him out of this match."
"Up Next is "America's Last Chance" Johnny Washington. Johnny my friend America has no last Chance. It is already shot to hell. I mean have you ever watched the news lately my friend. Well, You Should so Johnny I am gonna give you one las tchance. One Last chance to step out of this match before you get your ass handed to you!"
"Hmmm let's see we have "Magnificent" Mike Douglas up next. I mean what can I say about him. I have never herd of this guy But just like I said about
Vendetta. You cannot count no oneout. . . ." "The rest of the guy's I have never herd of so I am not gonna say anything about them. All I am gonna say I am not trustinganyone and I am not gonna take
NO _Prisoners_."
"N.E.W You Are All Going To Hell and I am DRIVEING THE BUS!" [Scene Fades To Black] -=[James G.]=- [Handles] [Shane Austin] "Ranked 12th on E-Wrestling Heaven. . .
