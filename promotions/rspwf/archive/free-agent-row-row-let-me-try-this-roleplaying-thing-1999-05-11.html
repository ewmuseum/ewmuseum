(Free Agent: Row Row) Let me try this roleplaying thing...

11 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
::Screen fades in on at the entrance of what appears to be a bar. Men are making out and flirting with each other and it quickly becomes obvious that this is a gay bar. Suddenly, up walks a man, wearing black boots, black jeans, a blue t-shirt and a black baseball cap, turned backwards. The blue t-shirt appears to be the Superman's death logo, except with an R, mirrored on itself, replacing the S. He has long brown hair and blue eyes, and is no one else but
Row Row (pronounced Rowww Rowww). He looks around the outside and laughs. He quickly decides to check out what is going on inside. He walks through the heavy steel door and sees a smoke-filled room loaded down with many people he has seen on television. Music is blaring and, upon listening, can tell it is distinctly "Faith" by George Micheal. He walks to the bar, passing John Davis of KoRn and Pat Patterson. He sits down at the bar and watches some of the goings on. Brian Christopher is spanking Scott Taylor at a nearby table, with
Raven cheering them on. Paul Heyman walks up to Raven asking if he wants his job back. Raven just turns him down. Shawn Micheals is at the other end of the bar going on about how he is the showstoppa, the main event, and the icon. A couple of rednecks are at one end of the bar waiting for some flaming member of the group to go outside and get beaten to death. Drag queens are picking up on some of the manlier of the group. As he looks on, the bartender asks him what he would want for a drink. Row Row says a beer and the bartender leaves. The tender returns with a Schmitts Gay, and Row Row just laughs at the irony. He pops it open and smells it, and then just sets it down. Suddenly, three rednecks grab Row Row and pull him out into the street. One of them speak.::
Head Redneck- So, <Censored>, you want to get <Censored>??? Row Row-Go <Censored> Yourself, Hillbilly. ::Two of the rednecks grab his arms as the head redneck tries to punch Row Row in the ribs. Row Row kicks the Head Rednecks arm away, and then does a lucha libre-esque walk up his back. He then does a double hiptoss to free himself from the other rednecks. He then grabs a nearby trash can and bashes both of the lesser hillbillies with it. He then delivers an All-Japan style German
Suplex on one of them. He picks up the other lesser redneck and delivers a
Michinoku Driver 2B. He then picks grabs a nearby reed stick and beats the lesser rednecks into a bloody unconsciousness. Just then, the head redneck, who was holding back as Row Row layed out his friends, kicked our hero in the testicles. The Redneck pulled out a gun and points it at his head.::
Head Redneck- Prepare to die, <Censored.> ::Unbeknownst to the redneck, Row Row pulls something two objects from his pockets. He just smiles and cooly answers.::
Row Row- As I said earlier: Go <Censored> yourself, Hillbilly. ::Row Row strikes the two objects together in the head redneck's face, producing a large fireball that knocks the idiot on his back. Row Row then runs and delivers the Anti-Christ Elbow, the set up to his devastating finisher. He then sets the hillbilly on top of a dumpster. He jumps up top and sets him up for a powerbomb. He then lifts him up and hesitates. He then jumps in the air and pushes the Hillbilly up and quickly sends him down in a face-driver. Row
Row laughs as the head redneck lays, facedown and unconscious, on the concrete.
He just felt the Fall From Grace©. Row Row then turns to leave, but sees Paul
Heyman standing in his way. Paul speaks.::
Paul Heyman: I am impressed by that beating you delivered to those rednecks. Would you like to join my company? Row Row: Hell no, I am not gonna work for free. Heyman: Come on, I know how you can get a great push. Row Row: I get over by being hardcore in the wrestling ring, not by sucking the promoters <Censored> in his parent's house.
Heyman: I saw you in the bar, I thought... Row Row: Many people have. I am just here to prove a point. I can go wherever the hell I want to go, because I can. I do, and don't do, whatever the hell I want, because I can. For those who think I am gay, they can kiss my ass. They, obviously like you, just wish I was. They say that because their women scream my name at night when they make love to them. They also say that because I am the type of person who will go anywhere, regaurdless of who is there, be it rednecks, be it bikers, be it fellow wrestlers, be it even homosexuals. How can
I go anywhere without people thinking stuff like that? I care not, however, I am who I am, and I do what I want to do, and if anyone has a problem with that, well, you see what will happen...
::Row Row points to the carnage behind him.:: Heyman: So, you won't accept my offer? Row Row: How's this for an answer... ::Row Row sends Heyman ribcage first into the side of the dumpster. He then spins him around, kicks him in the stomach, and delivers a fisherman's buster.
He laughs as he walks out of the alley. He then turns and looks at the camera, saying these words.::
Row Row: So, it seems people think I am gay because I hang out in gay bars. Their logic is "When in Rome, one does as Romans do." I don't. I go places to see what is going on. I don't partake in such actions. Nor do I intend to in the future. And those who think I do, as I mentioned to Heyman, get their skulls cracked. Speaking of skull cracking, let me ask the question: who can take a Hardcore wrestler like Row Row? I want to see a wrestling organization that has a division that can stand up to my kind of brutality. Also, I want to send a warning to any fool who is willing to challenge me: you will have a
Fall... From... GRACE!!!
