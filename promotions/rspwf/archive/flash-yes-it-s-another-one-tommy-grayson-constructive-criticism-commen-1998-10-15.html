[FLASH (Yes, it's ANOTHER one)] Tommy Grayson - Constructive criticism, comments.

15 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay, maybe you guys don't wanna see another subject that reads [FLASH] Jabronie Mahoney - FLAME FLAME FLAME...but, I was kinda wondering what you
RSPWFites would think of this "I'm here, and I'm here to stay" RP for the
WWO...here it is. Maybe it's not "kick-ass," but it's definitely "he's gettin'
somewhere...WINK WINK" material. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [SCENE: Somewhere near the Metropolitan museum in New York City. The camera opens up on a black Jeep Cherokee parked next to the sidewalk, which is next to
Central Park. The man sitting in the driver's seat of the jeep appears to be the one of the newest additions to the WWO rosters, "The Extreme One" Tommy
Grayson. Grayson's short, black hair is combed back, and his goatee is neatly trimmed. He's wearing a blue parka over a black WWO Road Warriors t-shirt. The rest of his attire can't really be seen because of the camera angle from outside of the car. Grayson turns to the camera.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: <chuckles> I'm sure you ALL saw what happened on WWO Primetime...I dubbed WWO as "HELL." I wanted to make some kind of impact here, but you know, I couldn't think of anything because of the other VERY important things on my mind...then, I was flipping through the channels, and I came across "High Plains Drifter" on TBS...since Clint Eastwood is one of my idols,
I decided to do what he did...paint "HELL" on the town sign. <chuckles> I loved it. I just loved it. Now that I'm OFFICIALLY in the WWO, good 'ol TG needs to find an opponent to beat. Who should be the one to be taken to the EXTREME? I don't know, and I don't give a damn...I never will give a damn. Plain and simple. <pauses> You know, I see guys like Scotty Davis, Ryan Walters, Gregory
Brians, Eric Draven, and Logan Gunz....these are guys who are in desperate need for a prescription from Dr. Grayson, your friendly neighborhood
"pharmaceutical"...a prescription for an ass-kicking! [Grayson looks across the street for a split-second, and then he looks back at the camera after taking a drink of Naya water.]
TOMMY GRAYSON: Any one of you guys in the WWO roster can expect good 'ol TG to come and, as the song goes, rock you like a hurricane, because I stand down to absolutely NOBODY...and I know for a fact that everybody wants a piece of TEO, because when it comes down to pure aggrivation, when it comes down to just plain pissing people off and making some grudges, I am the MASTER.
I'm still wondering about who the WWO is gonna throw to the lion...and when picking out the victim, make a good decision and pick somebody who's not a soft-ass who gets his nails done and cares too much about his hair....because I tend to feel sorry for those kinda bastards, and frankly, I tend to just plain
BEAT them instead of BRUTALIZING them...but if you give me one of those "tough"
guys, then, I can guarantee you that I can give you one HELL of a show.... ....PLAIN ...AND ...SIMPLE!! [Grayson pauses for a few seconds.] TOMMY GRAYSON: You can bet that I'm gonna be snapping some fingers and snapping some necks, as the song goes. Anybody who will step in my path willl
DEFINITELY...undoubtedly...POSITIVELY...taken... ...TO ...THE ...EXTREME!! [Fade to black as Grayson drives away...] My Sig: 'Da man wit' 'da plan I call all the shots, and rock all the spots... and I will take you TO THE EXTREME... Jim - [handler of "The Extreme One" Tommy Grayson and "Wicked Weapon" or "Hot Shot" Jeremy Taylor]
