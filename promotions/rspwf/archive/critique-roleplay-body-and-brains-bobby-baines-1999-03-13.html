[CRITIQUE] Roleplay: "Body and Brains" Bobby Baines!

13 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens to the exterior of a large gray house, obviously late at night. Several windows are illuminated, while others remain in darkness. After a brief pause, the camera cuts to the interior of the house, where the unmistakable figure of "Body and Brains" Bobby Baines sits, surfing through numerous TV channels. Dressed in blue jeans and a black IWC "Tuesday Turmoil"
T-shirt, Baines is clearly displeased with the choice of programs. Raising his eyes skywards, he eventually switches the TV off, turns towards the camera and begins to speak.]
"BODY and BRAINS" BOBBY BAINES - So this is what it all comes down to, huh? Ten men confined to one ring, because they couldn't agree on an answer to a very easy question. Who's the best newcomer to hit the IWC scene? So now, to settle what they feel are irreconcilable differences we have the biggest match of them all. I mean... Can't we all just get along?
[Baines breaks into laughter at the thought of such a prospect.] BBBB - _Hell no_! I've waited weeks for this -- The oppurtunity of a lifetime. How often can any man enter a ring, against ten men he despises, under strict instructions to 'search and destroy'? Could heaven, itself, be a disappointment after this?
[Placing his hands behind his head, Baines leans back on the reclining chair, before he continues.]
BBBB - So why don't we just take a look at who lies in front of the man with the BODY _and_ the BRAINS, Bobby Baines. Firstly... and obviously I'm not going by order of importance, there's Bobby Ransom -- the man who couldn't even touch Bobby Baines with a ten foot poll, yet got a screwjob victory over
'im. Bobby Ransom... the one man in this entire universe who can claim to have a win over God's gift to the squared circle... but now it's a new setting.
Eight other men in there with us, Bob-o, and no Liam Rogan to hit me with a bell. Sure, Manson Combs will be there to help ya out too, but trust me... He won't last long. So take a look at what's left... What you have going for you.
Your pure wrestling skills? Ha! Stamina? Ha! The BODY?! Not a remote chance, and after Tuesday's showing, certainly not . . .
THE BRAINS! [Grins, the strikes a look of arrogance.] BBBB - So to put this in its simplest terms, it's the main event, the spotlight, the marquee man, the whole show, the franchise... BOBBY BAINES . . .
... versus ... . . . A NOBODY! [Nods.] BBBB - And Manson Combs... You helped cost me a win... And I use "helped" as loosely as can be, 'cause after your cheap threat, I got back up and exedcuted the BainesBuster on my opponent. Hell, you couldn't even take me out from behind, and after ten minutes of wrestling nonetheless. [sighs] But just 'cause you couldn't do your job, don't mean we're finished. No, we ain't finished, and we _won't_ be finished until the day the entire world acknowledges that on your best day, you weren't fit to lace the boots of "Body and Brains" Bobby Baines. Combs, tomorrow we step into the same ring together for the first time, and with nine other men as well. Tomorrow night, we battle to see who the best newcomer in the _Insane_ Wrestling Corporation truly is. So many things go down tomorrow night, Manson, but tomorrow night is the start of the darkest journey of your life... and take my word for it . . .
. . . There's NO WAY back . . . . . . and there's NO WAY out . . . . . . 'less I THROW you out . . [Baines chuckles as he contemplates his preceeding comments.] BBBB - But I guess that would end my late night sessions with your mother, huh? Well, ya win some ya lose some, an' trust me... She _isn't_ a big loss. 'Cause you, Combs, you were a thorn in my side. For one brief solitary second, you stuck your nose into my business, and unless your a member of the most elite group in all of sports entertainment, the Gentlemen's Club, [slower]
that's something you do not do. And what of everyone's favorite "Genetic Marvel"? Why, I can be speaking of none other than Michael Staziak -- the guy who, if I'm not mistaken, is [doing his best Mike Staziak impersonation] "taking over" around here. Well kid, you'll go easy on _me_, right? I mean, I think you're great. I don't think you're a giant piece of horseshit at all. You truly exemplify perfection...
Won't you _please_ go easy on "Body and Brains"? [With that, Baines erupts with laughter. After a few seconds, he composes himself, and with a wide grin, he proceeds.]
BBBB - But seriously folks... The three men I just named are my best opponents, and to be frank, they're average at _best_. So who has a chance? Who can beat Quadruple B? Only one name comes to my mind, and there's a slim chance at that . . .
. . . God. And even he'd have to pray for a fast count, and there's just one reason why. When ya got BODY and BRAINS. . . . . . It drives the women INSANE . . . [Million-dollar smile.] And the men, well, they just get MIGRAINES! [Fade to darkness.] : ) [name withheld] - All around good guy, and...
