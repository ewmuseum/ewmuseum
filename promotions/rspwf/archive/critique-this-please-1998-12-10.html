CRITIQUE THIS PLEASE!!

10 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Keep in mind that this is for a card as a shoot and will be on a html document so it would look alot better.... It's a set up for a ladder match with Tommy Hardbody for the MGW Heavyweight belt....
[ "For Those About to Rock" by AC/DC blast's over the arena loudspeakers and out come's Shane Webster wearing a pair of cacky slacks and a black polo shirt with a number of championship belt's from his former employer's draped over his shoulders.
Once he get's to the bottom of the ramp, a fat kid wearing a Kryenik t-shirt shoves a sign that read's "Shane Webster=Hardbody's bitch" in his face then flip's Shane off. Webster immeadiately tear's it up then spit's in the kid's popcorn before spining around and pointing to the entrance where Mona Lisa stroll's out pushing a wheelbarrow and wearing a see-through black body suit with a yellow thong.
Once they reach the ring, Shane help's Mona in then unloads a can of gas and some old Hardbody poster's from the wheelbarrow. He then bunch's them into a pile in center ring and grab's the mic...]
[Shane] CUT THE FUCKIN' MUSIC!!.....I'm gonna make this short and sweet! [Shane] I wanna know how many of you fat assed Georgia Redneck's out there are
Tommy Hardbody fan's?
Decent pop... [Shane] Not too shabby Hardbody! Guess your just bread country! Crowd boo's... [Shane] Alright, alright! Now how many of you podunk's paid to see "The Clutch"..? Mixed reaction. Half the crowd pop's, the other half start up chant's of
"Limpdick" and "The Corporate Rag". The later directed at Mona Lisa...
[Shane] Yah! Boo us if you want but if it wasnt for me and Mona, you'd all be sitting at home on your porch swing's drinking instant lemonade and huffing paint! We gave this fed life, not your hero Mr.Everything. He just came along for the ride! But come two week's at Merry F'N Christmas his ticket's up! And then it's MY time!
[ As he speak's, he whip's his belt's to the ground and begins to bury them in the Hardbody poster's. He then douses them with gas and pitch's the empty can into the audience, nailing a security guard in the back of the head...]
[Shane] You see this? These belt's are leftover's from some of my greatest accomplishments. You've got the UEF United States and Television belt's that I carried at the same time. You've got the Extreme High Octane
Heavyweight Championship, the AIW Intercontinental, and the IIXF
Submission's belt that I won when I'm not even a friggin' submissions wrestler! The crowd pop's pretty big for the now defunct IIXF... [Shane] You know what these belt's mean to me now? THEY MEAN SHIT!! [Shane] This isnt the IIXF. It sure as hell aint the AIW. It's the fucking MGW and as of right now Shane Webster is a one fed man, and the MGW is a one horse town!
[ With that, he strike's a match against his boot and tosses it into the gas soaked pile of gold and paper and soon he's standing in front of a 7 foot bonfire...]
[Shane] Come Merry F'N Christmas Tommy Hardbody isnt gonna be the town horse NO
MORE! I'm gonna beat his ass!...I'm gonna take his belt!...And I'm gonna take this fed into 99' in a blaze of fucking glory!!! You dumbshit's can run WITH me....Or you can run AGAINST me. But 1999 is the year of "The
Clutch" and it's time I set the standard of Excellence because the guy who's got the job now aint cuttin' it!! All he is is a piece of shit mark who wouldnt know a hankerchief from his fuckrag!!
[Shane] NOW HIT MY MUSIC!!! [ Once again, his music scream's through the arena and Shane start's to taunt the audience. After a few moment's someone launch's their chair at him. Shane duck's then tosses it into the fire. He then rip's his shirt off and tosses that in as well. Then, like a swarm of killer bee's, the crowd erupt's with riot and dozens of chairs take flight into the air feuling Webster's fire and destroying the ring.. Security then surround
Shane and Mona and guide them to the back through the mob's of hysterical fan's as they continue their rampage and the ring blazes away!...]
