(Critique) (MCW) Phil Raven

3 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Phil Raven calls for a meeting with the other MCW Wrestlers. All the wrestlers are seated in a room, around tables. Phil gets in the room, papers in hand, and he start to talk)
PR: So, hi to you all, guys. (Wrestlers are cheering Phil) PR: No problem, you can continue to sleep. (Half the guys are laying down on their table and start to snore) PR: So today I'd like, as planned, to take a few minutes of your time and mostly of mine, to talk about some of your actions. Listen up guys : Let's say that since they allow alcohol in the arena's cafeteria...(Deep breath, looks down at sheets he have in his hands).The situation is getting worse.which means it gets out of hand, for those who didn't know.
PR: Let's take last month as an exemple: When you hanged up Scottsfield..Ok,
I understand it was party time but.sorry ?
(Eric Taylor says something) PR: Oh, he didn't pay for the beer ? Yeah, right..Maybe so, but I think it was a little radical.By the way, after this meeting is over, you'll be nice and go free that bus driver you handcuffed to that tree over there..
err.it would be even nicer to give him back his clothes, there's a kind of woodpecker that is messing with his ass or something.
PR: There's also the problem of the No-Shows. Like you Cashera: You missed all of last week, your gonna try to make me believe your clock didn't ring in the morning..what ?
(Max Cashera says something) PR: Oh, you have a written reason ? I'd like to see that. (Cashera gets up and gives the paper to Phil. Phil starts to read) "Max wasn't at at work last week because he was summoned to the Supreme
Court to have his preliminary investigation. Depending of the coroner's report, it's possible that he will miss again sometimes down the road. (Phil makes a pause) Edward L. Tiozo, Lawyer."
(Phil looks at Max, raise the eyebrow and a big question mark appear above his head.) PR: Ok, Max, good reason.nevermind my sarcasm of earlier..(Phil is like he's thinking). PR: There's Copeland that's asleep on his desk.can somebody open a window to bring some fresh air in ? Good.(He turns around and face the board.)
(Breaking Glass sound !) PR: When did I tell anybody to pass Copeland thru the window ??? Anyway.that didn't even wake him up...But that's alright, I was about to talk about vandalism.
You know, when MCW started, all offices had just bought chairs, just bought tables... right now, the offices are looking like we held the hardcore championship tournament in them. I understand some of you can't resist to break tables and chairs when you see some..just don't look at them damnit
! ...Devine, when I am talking, I'd like that you take that headset off.DEVINE TAKE THE HEADPHONES OFF, ARE YOU DEAF ????
(Very, very, very loud music comes out of the speakers when Devine takes them off) PR: DEVINE, PUT YOUR HEADSET BACK ON !!! DEVINE, PUT THEM BACK ON !!!!!! DEVINE.. (The music stops and Phil continues.) PR: No wonder why that guy have both eardrums glued to one another.when I think that he missed a show to have his ears pierced..and that he came back with a ring in the nose.
(He looks at his sheet) PR: Mojo, you are the wrestlers union's president. So be it..but I think you should at least give a good exemple..I mean, the other day, we found an bloody arm in your locker room.That's a new meaning to the words "Parts
Unknown" I suppose ? What ?
(Mojo says something) PR: Oh, I see ! You were practicing armbars on it, yeah...whatever. (Takes a deep breath) PR: Anyway, I just wanted to told you guys to slack down on beer if possible.puke on the opponent is never a good finishing move..remember the first rule of wrestling: "Choke Them All, Kill Them All, Beat Them All" but for Pete's sake have some decency. And vomiting over an opponent is not decent behavior.
(Phil leaves. As the camera spans the room, guys are getting vodka bottles out from under the tables and they start to drink until they all are puking their hearts out all over the floors)
