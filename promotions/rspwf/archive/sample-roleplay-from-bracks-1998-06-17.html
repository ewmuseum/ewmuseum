Sample Roleplay from Bracks

17 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey Guys.... This is one of my first RP's for my new character..."Unforgiven" Mark
Curtis. It's a bit shrt, but I working on my length a bit more, so pardon my mess :) I would like all those interested to post or mail some feedback please. Thanks
<The camera focuses on a television set..the set..obviously very run down...fizzles it's way through the videotape of Daniel Garza. Garza walks with a cokcy stride as he meets his opponent..the opponent rushes at Garza and throws a punch. From there, the video practically turns into a Garza highlight film...with him doing all sorts of crazy maneuvers. In the middle of a moonsault body press, a cigerette flys across the room at the
TV....obviously, the assailant is not satisified with this weak attempt. He walks over to the television set...his face not being shown..and picks up the TV...and walks slowly to the balcony. With a final grunt, he opens the squeaky sliding-glass door..and steps out. He heaves the battery operated tele over his head....>
Man: I'll see you rot in HELL, GARZA!!!!! <The man picks up the TV and throws it down to the parking lot....it hits a run down, old clunky car with a satisfying smash....as the owner, standing twenty feet away, wearing an ill-fitting Hawaiian shirt and jeans puts his bags aside and yells, loudly and angrily up toward the man on the balcony>
Tourist: You dirty little PUNK! Why don't you pay for that car you just totaled?!??
<The man looks down in contempt and lights up another cigerette. He puts the Zippo on the balcony railing and talks back..calm, but yet a hint of anger in his voice>
Man on balcony: I'd pay for the car, but I need the fifty cents for a cola... <The hefty tourist..obviously hurt by the remark... shouts back this time with full-fledged anger>
Tourist: You know how much damn time it took to fix that thing back to the point where it's drivable!?!? Do you?!?!?
Man: Obviously not enough. Even though I've experienced pain...had my life turned to the point where it isn't worth living...to where I delight only in my own self-destructive nature.....I wouldn't be caught dead in a car like that...
Tourist: IT WAS MY CAR!! What gives some steroid freak the right to throw a TV off a balcony and destroy private property? I should have you arrested!!
Man: Like I even care! The only prison I worry about..is the one of my own personal hell....my rage...one that I will never escape from until I die...
<The Man puffs on his cigerette..pondering..seeming to question himself..he is only brought out of his self-examination by the tourist>
Tourist: Listen to me you FLAMING PSYCHO!!! I've had enough of your crap! I was a military man! Special forces! You ain't gonna pay for my car! I'll beat it out of ya! I may have put on a few pounds... Man: A few? Tourist: I could still kick you @$$!! Man: I don't make a habit out of beating up tired old coots. Get out of here.... <The Tourist fumes heavily at this..wipes his white mustache with the tip of his fingers and prepares for the strongest words of all>
Tourist: You........COWARD!!!! <The man who was making his way back into his room from the night air...turns around with speed.as if the words had just snuck up on him. The tourist is thrown off his rhythm by this but continues on with a hint of hesitation>
Tourist: You can throw TV's out the window and bust up other people's things...what else are you? You're yellow...a punk. A COWARD!!!
<The man reaches over and blindly turns on the lights. When, the light shines..flicking due to the crappiness of the bulb..it reveals "Unforgiven"
Mark Curtis...smoking a cigerette and with a crazy look in his steel-grey eyes. He wipes his short, jet-black hair..and smooths his black vest>
Curtis: Let's go... <The tourist looks up at who he's just insulted..and recoils in shock>
Curtis: You hear me? I've heard trash my whole life....I've made my mistakes..and I'm suffering for them. I got no home...I'm a nomad. I've lost everything. I once was an American hero..fighting for justice...what a joke. I'm worthless..less than human..my secrets gutting me from the inside. But you know what? I get enough crap from myself....AND I DON'T
NEED ANY FROM YOU!!!! <A dog barks in the distance...howling at the sudden noise....there is a bit of eerie silence..punctuated by traffic sounds...a horn blares and then the tourist finally speaks>
Tourist: You're...you're that "Made in America" Mark Curtis guy... <Curtis chuckles a bit and throws his cigerette butt to the ground..picking up another...and just staring at it>
Curtis: Don't you wish....that guy's gone..meet the new guy in town. My name's Unforgiven..and I'm about to go beserk...
Tourist: I heard you went off the deep end..disappeared for two years y'know? <Curtis nods> Tourist: My army buddies told stories about you..say you got some sort of dark secret..and that you'd given up wrestling. But then you started beating people up in bars...bad....psycho bad...
<Curtis smirks> Curtis: All true... <Curtis turns angry> Curtis: ALL TRUE!!!! <Curtis throws his pack of cigerettes back in his room..obviously in mental agony...remembering the darkness the plagues him>
Tourist:<backing up> Listen man...I'm sorry...I didn't mean to bother you...That's not even my car...<the man obviously lying looks at another car and claims it> THat's mine. Memory, you know? I'll pick it up later...uh...bye..
<The man walks frightened back into the hotel leaving his bags behind. Curtis watches from overhead..then returns to his room..slamming the door shut...> Man from nearby room: HEY SHUT UP!!!! Curtis: I'm not in the mood, pal! SO SHOVE IT!!!! <Curtis then turns to the camera> Curtis: Hear that Garzas? Hear that Killer Kimachi and anyone else who I'm gonna face. I'm NOT IN THE MOOD! You wanna fight..you got one. And if you expect a win..be ready and willing to pry it out of my cold dead hands. I'm running on rage...and I'm gunning for you all I may be "Unforgiven", but that also means I'm not forgotten...
<Curtis paces back in forth..the anger welling up inside of him> Curtis: You see that guy who I was talking to....he knew who I was. He knew "Unforgiven" Mark Curtis! My reputation precedes me..and you better hope that you receive the same message he did..and get the hell out of dodge.
<Curtis stops and reaches for his Zippo..realizing he left it on the balcony> Curtis: !@#$!!!!!!! Why when I need a smoke the most???!?? <Turns back to camera> Curtis: I'm gunnin' for you all..and I ain't going to stop. Garza...you brought this on yourself..you brought this on your family...they're all going down with you...
<Curtis imitates taking a shot at something in the sky with a rifle. He smiles and returns to his pose>
Curtis: Shot down..and it's too late...too late to save them..to late to save yourself. I'm aiming for you...and I'm not going to stop until you've all stepped into the ring with me and tapped out to the American
Nightmare...If there is one thing I am..it's persistant.. <Curtis walks to the balcony to retrieve his Zippo lighter, but says one last thing to tthe camera>
Curtis: But I'm also Unforgiven... <Curtis stares out into the night as the scene fades to black> -- Bracks
