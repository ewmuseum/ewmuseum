I paste just get get on Ronnie's nerves. :) ***************** Gameshow Flash ***************** [The camera opens to a gameshow set a lot like Jeopardy. 3 contestants wearing "wrestling" shirts span the right side of the screen. The names
"Ron", "Steve" and "Bob" are printed before each man. They have buzzers in there hands. A festive theme song begins to play. A voice comes over the speakers... a packed audience begins to listen...]
Voice: Ladies and Gentleman... children of all ages.... WEEEEELLLLLLLCCCCCCOOOOOOMMMMEEE TO ANOTHER EXCITING EPISODE OF....... LOCO THIS!!!! [The crowd erupts in cheers] Thisss isssss YOUR HOST.... THE ONE... THE ONLY... THE LEGGGGEND HIMSELF.... LOCO!!!!! [LOCO comes running out wearing black jeans and a painted on tuxedo shirt. His long tangled dark hair is tied in a pony tail at the top of his head. The 6'6" athletic wrestler/host is smiling and the crowd loves it]
LOCO: Welcome to LOCO THIS! a game show all about the exciting world of wrestling and it's hero's. Tonight's show features Night of Glory... the
Supercard that has captured the heart of the wrestling community. Let's meet our players.
[Camera pans to Ron] LOCO: Welcome to the show Ron. Tell me about yourself. Ron: I'm a big time wrestling fan. I'm a member of the -I Love Spreads
Fan Cl.... [LOCO cuts him off] LOCO: Okay that's enough of that.... forever. [LOCO exhales] LOCO: Steve give us the 411 on you man. Steve: LOCO I'm glad to be HERE!!! You are my hero. [Steve pauses... LOCO says nothing.] LOCO: Go on! [He smiles] Steve: I've followed your career from the start man! I remember when you wrestled Cree.... [LOCO cuts him off] LOCO: WAIT... WAIT ... WAIT... It's the night... "I dominated Creed"... get that right! NEXT! [Camera pans to Bob] LOCO: Bob... we don't have time for you... let's play our game. Bob: LOCO thanks! LOCO: Shut up and stop kissing my ass... we're moving to round 1. [Carnival music begins to play as LOCO makes his way behind the podium.] LOCO: Ok remember this episode is all about Night of Glory. Our first segment is called... "Who Am I"... I'll read you a description... LOCO style.... and whoever rings in first with the right answer get's 100 dollars. Ready... on your mark... LET's PLAY... WHO AM I...
[Fireworks explode on the set. Neon flickers on and off. The crowd cheers.] LOCO: Ok for 100 dollar... "I am a overratted jobber who stuffed the ballot boxes for RSPWF Half Year Awards '98... I am a rookie... My best friend is King Snake... Who Am I?"
[Bob buzzes in] Bob: RONNIE D!!! [LOCO looks to the judges as there seems to be some confusion] LOCO: I'm sorry... Ronnie is not a rookie... [Steve buzzes in] Steve: "Heartbreaker" Steve Wallace! DING DING DING!!! LOCO: YOU ARE CORRECT!!!! 100 smackers to Steve. [Steve 100] LOCO: Next clue... I run with Snakes... I am known as Minor League to some.... I made my name between two D-Cups! Who am I?
[Bob buzzes in] Bob: Ronnie D! [LOCO looks to the judges.... again] LOCO: The judges say we *HAVE* to take that answer. [LOCO looks disgusted] LOCO: We also would have taken Chris Myers. [STEVE 100, Bob 100] LOCO: But anyway. [LOCO shakes his head.] LOCO: Ok let's keep this moving... I drive a piece of shit Malibu... I bore with long interviews... My name might indicate I'm from
Georgia...Who Am I? [Bob buzzes in] Bob: Ronnie D! LOCO: WRONG hot shot! You know Bob not every answer is Ronnie D! Bob: I'm sorry. [Ron buzzes in] Ron: Nick "The Bulldog" Demola! Ding! Ding! Ding! LOCO: Ron you are correct! [STEVE 100, Bob 100, RON 100] LOCO: Moving on... I bored you for months on the RSPWF... People make fun of me in IRC... I made my name in the D-Cup... I've gotton myself over with great promotion... and little talent...Who Am I?
[Bob buzzes in] Bob: MARK CUTTER! [Everyone laughs as LOCO shakes his head] LOCO: You ain't the sharpest crayon in the box are ya? [Steve buzzes in] Steve: Ronnie D? LOCO: Yes Steve... it is Ronnie D... [Sirens go off. More Explostions go off.] LOCO: Steve congradulations... you have made it to the final round. The round in which you spin a wheel... it will land on one of 20 selected wrestlers in Night of Glory... I'll be blind folded and you give me no more than 5 clues on who he is... IT'S WORTH 10 THOUSAND
DOLLARS....Steve you ready? Steve: Sure thing LOCO! [LOCO and Steve go into the back of the stage where two chairs sit. LOCO sits down and a blond bombshell comes in and blindfolds him.]
LOCO: OHHHHH BABY! [The crowd cheers] LOCO: Now Steve.... go spin the wheel of death... and pick your mystery wrestler... I hope you know your wrestlers...
Steve: I know most of them. [Steve spins the wheel.... it spins around a few times... and lands on.....] LOCO: Ok Steve you ready? [Steve looks at the name on the wheel] Steve: Please let me re-spin! LOCO: Sorry, Steve those are the rules of the game. [Steve sratches his head... and begins to smile.] Steve: Ok LOCO I got it. LOCO: Ok Steve... give me 5 clues... and after those 5... if get it... you get 10,000 dollars. [A Jeopardy type theme begins to play in the background] Steve: Clue 1: I am known in the wrestling world as a "jobber" Clue 2: I walk in the shadows of a "legend" Clue 3: I have never ever pinned anyone of note. Clue 4: I made my name in the "Lynch Mob" Clue 5: I don't even belong at Night of Glory. [Steve pauses. The tention mounts] Steve: LOCO who am I? [LOCO smiles] LOCO: Steve my boy... relax... I knew it after the "jobber" clue. Steve
You Are... [LOCO pauses] SETZER VAN STRIFE!!! [Sirens go off... fireworks explode... ballons fall from the ceiling... a big neon sign flashes the words "WINNER $10,000"] Steve: YES! YES! OH MY GOD!!!! YES!! [LOCO rips of the blindfold. He looks to whe wheel where there is a picture of Setzer Van Strife eating a twinkie]
LOCO: STEVE.... YOU .... ARE.... THE ..... MAN!!! [LOCO pumps his fist in the air.] LOCO: Thank you for watching... this has been great... stayed tuned for next week's LOCO THIS!!! where THE TRUTH "ALWAYS" HURTS!!! It's been great but YOUR LOCO TIME... is over.... thanks and get out!
[The camera fades to black]
