[Critique Roleplay] "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor

16 August 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: Los Angeles, California. Time: 4:30 P.M. The scene opens to the view of a back porch, a type of cobana. There is a table with a set of chairs around it, and an umbrella over it to keep the sun from beating down. Sitting at the table is only one man. He is dressed in a pair of khaki shorts and a plain white tight fitting designer suit. He has a Rolex watch on his his right wrist, and a diamond stud earring in his left ear. He has sunglasses on his face that are sort of concealing his who he is. He takes off the the sunglassess when he sees the camera and smiles. You immediately see it is "The
Ladies Man" Vince Taylor. . .]
VINCE TAYLOR: You know I've come, I've gone, I've come back, and then I've gone again. That's the story of myself in the world of wrestling. I've climbed to the top, left, and the had to climb all the way back up to the top of mountain.
And both times I took control of every federation I was in. "From rags to riches" is the story of my wrestling career.
But during the short hiatus that I've been on from the ring as of late, I've noticed some trends. I've seen that a lot of guys are coming out and trying to flaunt their money, and show off their good looks. <Smirks.> In other words they are trying to be like their hero, "The Ladies Man." But let me set the record straight. There will never be anyone on God's green earth that will ever, and I mean ever, be like me. And so I guess it was people trying to be like me that dragged me back into the squared circle. And it seems that I've popped up in the wrestling promotion that most people would have never thought of.
[Vince pauses and reaches toward a small mixed drink that is in front of him. He sips it slowly and then continues. . .] Instead of laying by the pool, soaking in the rays, I'm gonna be giving it blood, sweat, and pride toward the goal of taking control yet another federation. The RIPWF... yeah you heard me right, Vince Taylor is going to be wrestling in Rhode Island. I know people are saying that Rhode Island isn't a big enough market for a hot commoditte like myself, but I think that I'll be the best thing to hit Rhode Island since the Revolutionary War. I'm gonna put this small little state on the map. Instead of kids knowing about it from school, when they hear my name they're going to think of state of Rhode Island and the great things that'll be done by me.
And it'll start on the Sunday the 22nd. It'll be a night that'll be remembered for years. One night, one World Title tournament, and one man walks away with the belt around his waist. Sixteen men start, but one remains standing. Is it gonna be tough? Hell yes it will, but it's nothing that I'm not used to doing.
Defying the odds is something that I take joy in. I've gonna through more than one man in a night before, and at In The Beginning I have plans of winning four matches and going down in the history books as the first RIPWF World
Champion.
[Vince stops again and smiles. He twirls a thin straw that is in his drink and starts to speak once again. . .]
I start out in this little tournament against a guy that's gonna be out to make a name for himself. "Hells Fury" Arthur Montgomery I'm sure will come into the ring full of energy and excitement looking to step into the limelight by knocking off a man of my stature. Well I got a little advice for 'ya Art...
you can check those aspirations at the door, 'cause it'll be a cold day in hell before it happens. I'm gonna go through you like a baby goes through diapers.
It's not that you're not a talented wrestler, I never questioned that, it's just that when gold is on the line "The Ladies Man" is at his best.
And when I move on to the next few rounds I really don't give a damn who is in the ring with me. It could be Tommy Lionheart, Tommy Lionheart, Sean Cox, or
Jesus Christ for all I care. No matter who it is, I'll make the guarantee that
I beat them from pillar to post and just keep moving through the rounds. So all the competition in the RIPWF better bring every force that they can.
Because whether you're bringing "Hells Fury," a "Lionheart," or anything else at all, it's never going to be able to contain. . .
. . ."The Ladies Man" [The scene fades to black as Vince picks up his drink and starts to take more sips from it. . .]
