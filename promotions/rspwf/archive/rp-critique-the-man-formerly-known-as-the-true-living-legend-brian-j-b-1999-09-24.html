RP Critique - "The Man Formerly Known As The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie

24 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a new format I'm using for my RPs. It works out quite nicely, actually. Here's the background. Blottie recieved a phone call in his last
RP, and immediately became very morose. He is taking on "The Devil's Advocate"
Damian Saturday, and because of this, Damian set it up to seem like Blottie helped him get away from the police by calling in a prank under Blottie's name.
---------------------- Sitting in a pile of filth, Brian J. Blottie stares at his TV, which is off. He lowers his head, shakes it back and forth, and you hear him weep gently then sniffle. He breathes in deeply, and stands up.
Walking over to the table that houses his phone and answering machine, he presses the red flashing button on the machine, and starts to listen, tears in his eyes.
"Brian, this is Sherry. Richard passed away this morning. I... I know that he meant a lot to you, so I called around until I got this number. The funeral is going to be Saturday morning. I'm so sorry Brian. Call me back as soon as you can. You know the number."
Blottie picks up the answering machine, and tosses it against the wall. He drops to the floor again, weeping heavily. He meant a lot to him... she didn't even know. Richard was the only friend he had when he was younger. The only person that didn't laugh at him, and make him feel inferior. Richard was his best friend. His only friend. And now he was gone.
Blottie forces himself off the floor, and looks over at the phone. He thought of calling Sherry. But how could he explain that he wouldn't be at his best friend's funeral? How could he explain that he couldn't deal with the loss. Not now. Maybe not ever.
His thoughts were cut off by a knocking at his front door. Blottie makes his way to the door, and opens it. He takes a step back, as three police officers stare back at him.
"Be careful, guys. For some reason, these damn wrestlers have a problem with authority. So keep your guard up at all times.", says the front officer, as he directs his attention to Blottie.
"Excuse me, sir, but what's the problem?", asks Blottie. "We're going to have to take you down to the station. Seems that you made a prank call that enabled a known felon to escape, and we think that you and him may be in together."
"What? What are you talking about? What known felon?" "You know him as "The Devil's Advocate" Damian." With that, Blottie's eyes light up with hatred, as he slams his fist into and through the dry wall of his apartment. The officers spring back, and draw their weapons, pointing them directly at Blottie.
"FREEZE. Don't move, or we'll shoot" "What proof do you have that I did this?" asks Blottie, the anger in his eyes increasing as he stares at his fist, which is still implanted firmly in the wall.
"We have a taped conversation from the 911 operator. We're going to take you to the station to do a voice comparison tests."
Blottie pulls his fist from the wall, and throws his hands out in front of him, causing the officers to jump back. Blottie looks at them bitterly.
"Let's get this over with. I've had a VERY bad day, and I don't need to be sitting around a bunch of po-pos all day."
"You from Detroit?" asks one of the officers, recognizing the Detroit street slang for police officers.
"Originally. I moved to Grosse Pointe Farms after my business became successful." "If you're business is so successful, why are you living in this hell hole?", asks the head officer, as he handcuffs Blottie, making sure that the cuffs are tight.
"My business went under when I went through some... personal problems." The officers nod, knowing how business is, and lead Blottie out of his apartment, to one of two awaiting police cars. They allow him to sit down himself, then both cars take off, in the direction of the local station.
------------------------------------- "Well, Mr. Blottie. We ran the voice comparison test through our computer, and you're correct, it wasn't you that made the call that night. In face, we ran a scan of Damian's voice through the computer, and it matched it perfectly.
Looks like "The Devil's Advocate" was trying to set you up." Blottie looks up at the officer who spoke to him, and shakes his head. "So I'm free to go?" "Yes", replies the officer. Blottie stands up, and starts to leave the holding cell, when he stops, and turns back around.
"How would you like to finally get your hands on Damian?", asks Blottie. The officer smiles, and walks over to Blottie, so that only the officer and
Blottie can hear the conversation. After several minutes of heated conversation, the officer shakes Blottie's hand, and Blottie begins his trek to the outside world again. As he walks, he seems to be deep in thought. Then, with each step he takes, confidence starts to return. He no longer seems morose. In face, he seems relieved, like a burden has lifted from his shoulders. He sees a pay phone, walks over to it, and picks up the receiver, inserting quarter after quarter into the coin slot. He dials a number, then waits while it rings on the other end.
"Yes... can I speak to Justin Russell?" To Be Continued... -------- Controller of... "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie "The Sadist" Brian J. Blottie "The German Gangsta" Jakob Jahovski AND THE LEADER OF THE RSPWF SECRET SOCIETY... [name withheld] P.S. Nada, Ish, Las, and Moze are all in!
