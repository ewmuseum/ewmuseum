RP, please comment...

28 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm using a new character by the name of "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk.  This is my third RP as him and I'm wonderin' whatcha guys think of it...
    ^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^ ^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^v^^ [The scene is the Redondo Beach peir where a big crowd is gathered.  "Whatcha Gone Do?" by Link is playing loudly. 
The crowd is yelling the lyrics of the song as it goes on...]   Crowd: ...HOW WOULD YOU LIKE IT IF I HIT YOU WITH A 69?... ...WOULD YOU LIKE ME ON TOP?... ...CAN I HIT THE HOTSPOT?... ...CAN I RUN UP IN THE MIDDLE?... ...WHA?  WHA?  WHA?... ...HOW WOULD YOU LIKE IT IF I HIT YOU WITH A 69?... ...CAN I HIT IT FROM THE BACK?... ...DO YOU LIKE IT LIKE THAT?... ...LIKE IT REAL FAST, REAL SLOW?... ...WHA?  WHA?  WHA?...   [An African-American man walks out of the crowd.  The crowd can still be heard...]   Crowd: ... NAAAAAA NA NA NA... ...NA NAAAAAA... ...NA NA NA NAAAAAA... ..NA NA... ...WOOOO OOOOOOH OOOOOOH....   [The man smiles.  He's wearing a black bandana tied around his head, 2Pac style.  He's also wearing a Los Angeles Kings hockey jersey with baggy black denim pants.  He has on black Adidas hightops, gold rings on each finger, a gold Rolex on his left wrist.  He takes the
Ray-Bansthat were resting on top of his blad head off and puts them on his eyes.  It becomes clear that the man is "Shoot the Shit" Shawn
Sylkk.  He sees the cameraman and begins talking to him...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: You know, as much as I'm enjoyin' this *rip-off* stable called Degeneration X's *lousy* attempt at satire and sarcasm, I must intervene for a second.
  [Sylkk pauses for a second, then resumes talking...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: As much as you guys *want* to take credit for endin' NBK's career...   [Sylkk smiles slyly and pauses.  He then continues talking...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: ...it *ain't* happenin'.   [Sylkk walks to a juicebar and orders some lemonade.  A few seconds later, the vendor comes back with a sixteen ounce paper cup filled with lemonade with a top and straw in it.  Sylkk goes into his pocket and hands him some money, then receives his change which he places in his pocket.  He takes the cup, then a sip, and tells the cameraman to follow him.  They take a walk along the shoreline.  Sylkk resumes talking...]
  "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: This whole week, NBK has been makin' noise about retirin'.  He announced his last CNWF² match would be this Monday night last Friday afternoon.  He's going to announce his retirement in the DEWA sooner than ya'll think.  In the ICW, if he even thinks about goin' out there Friday night, he's gettin' an ass kickin'.  In the EPW, he's goin' ta announce his retirement later today.  He told Frankie about his retirement plans before the card and he's been tellin' Madman and Davison about his retirement this whole week.  He plans to finish his career *solely* in the BKWL and I'll leave him alone there.  XPac...
  [Sylkk slaps his forehead...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: ...duh, oops, I mean Psycho, I *know* you're in the BKWL.  If you wanna shoot the shit about
NBK, do it there and not here where he can't defend himself against your bullshit.  So, DX,
if you wanna continue to make asses outta yourselves, go right on ahead...   [Sylkk raises his sunglasses and looks into the camera.  The smile disappears from his face and he resumes talking...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: ...but I'm goin' ta have to put the smack it up, flip it, and rub it down on you poser bitchass wannabes.
  [Sylkk smiles again and takes another sip of lemonade.  He flips the Ray-Bans back down and resumes...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: Although I *still* feel NBK is an overrated cocksuckin' asskisser, I ain't aboutta let a man who ain't even here to defend himself get trashed and watch you little bitchass posers take credit fo somethin' you ain't got the right to take credit fo! 
And if ya'll wanna gang up on me, that's fine.  It'll just show what kinda bitchass cowards you are.
  [Sylkk pauses and his smile becomes broader.  He resumes talking...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: Yanno, I *seriously* doubt that little skinny Toy's chair shot made NBK retire.  NBK looks at himself in the mirror on a daily basis.  That's enough punishment fo a lifetime.  So I really doubt NBK retired cause of the chair shot.
  [Sylkk pauses, laughs a little bit, and takes another sip of lemonade.  He resumes talking...]
  "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: I began shootin' the shit and I'll resume shootin' the shit now.  There's a guy here by the name of [name withheld].  About two weeks ago, he was tellin' everyone about how his friend died.  Look, I know a lotta guys in the locker room area have been talkin' and some of them don't have the balls to say this in public...
  [Sylkk pauses and his smile becomes as braod as it can get.  He resumes talking...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: ...but I do.  Most everyone I've talked to since you came to the DPW think you were majorly bullshittin' all of them when you said your friend died.  You said you're going to quit wrestlin' and shut down your fed.  What happens?  You're still in as many federations as ever and your fed is up and runnin'.  Funny how you said your friend died *only* in the DPW and AoLcW.  You thought people would take your word at face value.  I've been watchin' the DPW on
TV since its inception.  A few weeks ago, you were bitchin', complainin', and whinnin' since DX disgraced your friend's gravesite.  And what do you do?  You join then a few weeks later.
  [Sylkk raises his sunglasses.  His eyes are full of intensity...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: If you made the termination of a human life up, you're one sick bastard of a human bein'. 
If you disregard what the stable had done to your friend, you're one sick bastard of a human bein'.  You don't fuck around with the passin' of a human life, whether it be reality or fantasy.
  [Sylkk's voice deepens and his eyes become more intense as he continues talking...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: Lord, FORGIVE ME IF I'M WRONG, but shit don't fit.  It would be like Davison makin' friends with people that mocked his friend's misfortune a few weeks ago.  It's like someone becomin' friends with people that helped to conspire in a murder against a loved family member.  Fantasy or reality, IT JUST DON'T
HAPPEN!   [Sylkk relaxes, flips his Ray-Bans back down and continues on...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: You and your little friends can deny what I'm sayin' all you want.  But, you *have* to look yourself in the mirror everyday and cope with yourself.  DPW members, you
*have* to look what the facts I'm presentin' very closely and make your own decisions.   [Sylkk smiles again and continues...]   "Shoot the Shit" Shawn Sylkk: If there's one thing I always liked about NBK, it was his mistrust for people.  In a cold ass world like this, you can't trust anyone who doesn't keep faith in you through thick and thin and no matter how big of an ass you may act.
  ["Go Deep" by Janet Jackson begins playing.  It sounds very faint as they are a few yards away from the peir.  Sylkk turns towards the peir, turns back to the camera, and resumes talking...]
