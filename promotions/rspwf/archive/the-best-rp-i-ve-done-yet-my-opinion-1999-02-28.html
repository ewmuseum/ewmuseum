The best RP I've done yet (my opinion)

28 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In my personal opinion, this is the best RP I've done yet. I'm not sure if it's going to go through, because I "touched" NACW employees, but it's such a good RP, I'd be surprised if it didn't...
Please let me know what you think of it. I'd really like to know the positives and negatives. So just take a few seconds out of your time, and just write a few words about it. Thanks!
*Scene opens with the NACW production truck pulling up to "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie's curbside. The cameraman is talking to his production manager* Cameraman: Are you sure this is alright? He threatened us last time we came unannounced. Don't you think we should at least call?
Production Manager: Listen, who's the boss here? Who's the man who call's the shots? ME. The upper management said they want more pieces on this Blottie guy. They say he's drawing a lot of heat, and when there's heat, we have to keep it up. So I want your ass to walk up to that house and talk with this guy.
Cameraman: But what if he won't let us in? Production Manager: I don't care if you break into his house. If you don't come back with this story, you can consider yourself "relieved" of your position. Got it?
Cameraman: *Getting out of the truck* Yeah, yeah... *under his breath* Stupid son of a... *The cameraman walks up the long drive, towards Blottie's house. Huge trees, nicely trimmed bushes, and beautifully cut grass adorn the walkway up to the estate. As the cameraman comes to the doors, he hesitates a second before knocking. He waits a few minutes, then knocks again. The door opens, and "The
True Living Legend", wearing nothing but a pair of black pants and ratty shoes stares for a second. He doesn't look happy*
Blottie: What the HELL are you doing here? Did I or did I not tell you to CALL before you show up at my door?
Cameraman: We just need a few words, Mr. Blottie... Blottie: No, you call me "Mr. Legend". And you want a few words? How about these ones you stupid <BLEEP>. *Blottie pushes the cameraman* I want you and your camera off my property in ten seconds, or else I'm going to take you apart piece *pushes the cameraman again* by bloody piece *pushes the cameraman
AGAIN*. Then I'll call the police and have you carted off to the nearest jail, where you can explain to the fed heads why you came for an interview and ended up costing them $2000 worth of bail money.
Cameraman: I wanted to call you, it was the production manager. He's a <BLEEP>! He threatened my job if I didn't come get the interview... *Blottie is very agitated at this point. His brow is furrowed, and he looks like he's about to kill someone. The cameraman sees this look and takes several steps back*
Blottie: Take me to this <BLEEP>. I want to set this <BLEEP> straight. *The cameraman nervously leads the way, as Blottie walks VERY quickly behind him. The production truck comes back into view, and Blottie breezes past the cameraman, towards the truck. Blottie opens up the back door, disappears inside for all of three seconds, and then the production manager flies out the back door, followed by Blottie walking out, screaming*
Blottie: LISTEN UP! CAN I EVER HAVE A LITTLE PRIVACY? IS THAT TOO MUCH TO
ASK? FIVE, TEN MINUTES WORTH OF TIME TO RELAX? NO! I'M NOT ALLOWED TO RELAX!
I HAVE TO GIVE INTERVIEWS AND DO LITTLE SHOOTS FOR YOU <BLEEP> EVERY OTHER MINUTE! Production Manager: Man, who the hell do you think you are? I'm going to go to the VERY top with this. I'll sue you! I'LL SUE YOUR GOSHDARNED GRANDCHILDREN
WHEN THEIR BORN! Even better, I'll have you fired!
Blottie: You'll have ME fired?! The single hottest prospect to have ever entered pro-wrestling? "The TRUE Living Legend"?! No, buddy, I'll have your
ASS fired. And that's a <BLEEP> promise.
*Blottie turns to the cameraman, who has taped this whole episode* Blottie: You want a piece on me? Here it is. All of you in the Rookie Brawl... I'm a LEGEND. Whether you like it or not, I am going to grind each and every one of you into the mat like the amateurs that you are. No mercy will be levied. No man will stop me. Not some PETTY production manager. Not some PETTY low grade that wants to call himself a pro-wrestler. I am "The TRUE
Living Legend". And if you want to cross me... I'll cross your name off the roster... permanently.
*Blottie turns his attention back to the production manager* Blottie: Is that what you wanted? Is that all? Or do I have to take you down to a tattoo parlor, get a swastika tattoooed to your forehead, then drop you off in downtown Detroit and let you find your own way back? Or maybe you just want me to kick your ass myself? But I think you'd be better off in Detroit.
I REALLY highly doubt you could handle even three SECONDS of me on my WORST day. Do you get it?
Production Manager: *Shaking on the ground in fear* I... I get it... Mister.... Blottie... Blottie: *Very visibly upset* NO, THAT'S "LEGEND" TO YOU, LOW GRADE! Production Manager: Ok.... Mister... Legend... Blottie: Now get the <BLEEP> away from my house and don't come back without CALLING FIRST! *Blottie storms off, bumping into the cameraman on the way back up the drive. The camera follows Blottie, then turns back to the production manager. The cameraman snickers as he zooms in on his boss*
Production Manager: What the hell are you looking at? Help me UP, GOSHDARNED
IT! Is that camera on? IS THE <BLEEP> CAMERA ON!?!?
*The cameraman quickly has the camera FADE TO BLACK* -------------- "The True Living Legend" Brian J. Blottie Member of the NACW and the WWEF Soon to prove that the name isn't just a gimmick. "Mama say mama saw mamacusa" - Bloodhound Gang "Don't be that guy" - [name withheld]
