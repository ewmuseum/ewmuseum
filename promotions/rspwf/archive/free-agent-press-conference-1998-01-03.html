[FREE AGENT] Press Conference

3 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene opens in the lobby of the CNN Center. A podium is set up in front of a bunch of chairs, as news reporters from all over gather in the small room.
Cameramen from various sporting organizations, like CNN-SI, Sports Illustrated,
ESPN, and more are setting up their equipment. The reporters quiet down and settle in their seats, when they see two men approach the podium.]
Man #1: Good Evening. Many of you are wondering why we have called you all here on such short notice for. Well, we are going to explain it to you. Allow me to introduce myself. My name is "Cold Kick" Dave Martin, and this is my partner, "Iron Fist" James Paxton.
James Paxton: We are to here before you, the press, to officially announce that, because our contracts are up with our former employers, we are now in search of a new wrestling organization that will accept the baddest force in the history of e-wrestling!
Dave Martin: We are former world tag team champions, as well as each being champions in the singles bracket, too.
James Paxton: Now. We are not men of many words, so we want to keep this brief. We are calling ANY and ALL e-wrestling organizations to sign us to a contract. You WILL NOT be disapointed.
Dave Martin: There is one catch, however. This is a first-come-first-serve offer. Act fast or you can say goodbye to your chances of being the best federation on the 'net!
James Paxton: There. We have made our point, sorry to take up your valuable time, but we had to make that statement. Goodbye.
Reporter #1: Wait! Wait! We have questions! Reporter #2: Lots of them!!! Dave Martin: That's nice... go and tell Reader's Digest! James Paxton: We don't have time for you, talk to our agent... Reporter #1: But...but...but! [Camera fades as the two men walk off the stage, leaving the reporters dazed and confused.]
