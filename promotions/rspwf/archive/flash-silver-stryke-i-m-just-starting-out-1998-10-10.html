[Flash] Silver Stryke (I'm just starting out)

10 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Note: I'm just starting out in e-wrestling. Let me know what you think. ============================================================ [Promo] [The wind blowing down the streets of Pleasantville Mississippi. Is there such a town? There is here. The grass is green and the sky is blue there is no pollution unlike the city. There is no noise, there are no fights there are no killings and basically there is no stress. The formatting of such a town isn't made by the United States government, it's made by the people. There is a man, a former government military operations major working outside the lines of this town, waiting for the bust to go down. Little does he know that the bust will be made on him. He is ready for whatever may come and expects lots to go down.
When he gets shot in the leg and betrayed by the government he begins to train and train and gets in the best condition of his life. He is now a professional wrestler, not only wrestling, but scoping out each city for his former partner that betray him. He is.... Silver Stryke!
[Setting] [Crystal clear Portland Maine. Hiked out in the woods with the wind blowing around some leaves is a man named Silver Stryke, one of the best damned cruiserweights in the biz. He begins to scope out his surroundings and is aware of everyone and everything just like they train him to be in the military. He is dressed in a pair of blue jeans with a t-shirt, a sweatshirt over that and vest on, just sitting. He begins to think of all the times he's had in the military and all those that have been on his side and he laughs for he's not here in the DPF to make friends, but to win titles. He stands up and looks at the camera as he throws the cigarette out of his mouth and he begins to speak...
[Silver Stryke] The first chapter in my wrestling book will be in this fed, so it better be damn good or It's going to get bashed all the way to hell with me.
Now, I'm here in order to get reconized and in order to finally achieve the unaccomplished by myself and that would be to finally get some sort of title.
I've never been in a federation so you must think that I'm not good and that just earning a title is something that you spit upon because you've all earned so much correct? Well in the army, they teach guys...guys like me I'm only 6'1 and I'm only 235...but I'm a mean mother, that's for sure. The time has come in my life to do something and here I am, doing it or well getting ready to. I have a match this Monday, and that makes my goal almost that closer due to the winner of this Battle Royal will take on the winner of another one for the
Extreme Title. Well, Extreme seems to be a good point in my aresenal of weapons, because I've been trained by the best. Who you're wondering...well you'll find out soon enough. A good friend of mine and all around great guy he is. I think that he's taught me well and I'm not going to let him down. Now, let's talk about my match-up!
Now in the Battle Royal with me will be KoRn? Do I dare ask? I mean, I don't know you but It seems to me like you're going to be a walk in the park, or should I say cornfield? It's tough to think about, but I'm the hottest prospect right now and you're all probaly just jealous. I'm going to take title after title after title in this fed, starting with the Extreme and if you don't like it well then you know where to stick it! KoRn, you don't have a very intimidating name so I doubt that you'll even make me break a sweat and we wouldn't want that would we? The military is very strict and they taught me something...never judge a book by it's cover. I knew of this man Jake McBrady, looked like the biggest pussy you'd ever seen. Then one day in camp this really big guy said something about him and they had a little fight, and guess who won? Well Jake did, all 5'6, 133 pounds of him. Knocked the big guy right out.
So, I'm not going to say I'm going to beat you, because I don't know you. The next man I'm going to have to face in this Battle Royal is Midnite. Yeah, it's going to take that long for me to finish beating the living hell out of you. Haha, if the names have any indication of what's going on, I'm going to be in for an eeeeeasy night. Midnite, I've seen you in the gym and I've watched some tapes of you and all I can say is <yawn> I don't care what you think of me because that's not what is on trial here, what's on trial is you're skills and I'm going to have to find you guilty. Guilty of not having any that is!
Then we have Brad "King of Hardcore" Grisham. If you're so hardcore, we'll see in this Battle Royal for a shot at the Extreme Title. I love how people claim to be extreme when the most extreme thing they've ever done is drink milk
2 days past the expiration date or eat peanut butter out of the jar with a spoon and then put the spoon back in and take more! That's extreme to some of you lackies and the days are lingering until that big Monday Madness program when I'm going to take the losers down.
Until next time and there will be one because I don't stop unless I die, so you losers buckle down and get ready for the thrill of your lives!
[Camera Fades to Black]
