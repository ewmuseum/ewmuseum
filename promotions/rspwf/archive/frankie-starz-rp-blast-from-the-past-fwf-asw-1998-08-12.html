Hades is a common man with average talent who's nothing but a lowlife.. thats why no one wants to be near him.. plus the fact that he physically smells, and you can tell the fanzines to quote me onthat, I on the other hand am Sensational.. Natasha: ( To Death ) you Sure? I mean it's his room right? and are you Sure of who you saw in there? ( to the camera crew ) Just Keep Filming.. you'll understand when we get there..

Hooker: You guys aint no Vice squad are you? Am I gonna be's on HBO? Natasha: No we're not cops.. and yeah, you'll be on HBO..or Something like that.. ( Smiling ) We Just want you to tell us who you were with in there, and what you did.. Hooker: Well ok...Do I'se get paid? Natasha: pay the lady.. Hooker: ( takes the $10 dollar bill ) I was in there With this man and a big fat lady.. he called her Jasmine and She called him Hades.. he said his real name was Jon something....
