#4 Sample Mark Slade RPs for all of you out there asking for it

27 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
When you look into the abyss, the abyss also looks into you. -Friedrich Nietzsche [The scene opens in the waiting room of a hospital in San Antonio,
Texas. It is late in the evening as a nurse sits at the counter typing something on her computer keyboard. The outer doors open and a cold wind blows in. She looks up as a man walks in from the outside. The first thing she notices is his medium length silver colored hair. He appears to be in his early thirties, but there is a look on his face which makes him appear older than that. His icy blue eyes stare sweep around the room slowly. She notices that his jeans and black t-shirt are dirty and torn as if he has been wearing them for a long time. The man slowly walks towards her and stands at the desk]
Mark: Excuse me, could you tell me where room 2208 is in? Nurse: Uhm sure. Go down the hall there and it's the 2nd one from the end on the right. Mark: Thank you. [Mark walks off down the hallway as the nurse stares after him. She thinks about calling security for a moment and then shakes her head and goes back to her typing. Mark walks down the hall looking at the room numbers. Soon he comes to the room and stands there. He hesitates for a few seconds before taking a deep breath and pushing open the door. As he walks in the room he sees a man on a hospital bed. IV tubes stick in his body at several places and a respirator mask covers his mouth as he lies there motionless. He is very skinny and his body has several lesions and sores on it. He stares at Mark silently as
Mark walks in. Mark walks to the side of his bed and sits in the chair there. They stare at each other in silence for several minutes]
Mark: Well i'm here. I almost didn't make it, but now i'm here. [Terry stares at Mark mutely] Mark: You know I almost didn't come. As a matter of fact I still don't want to be here. I mean when I see you like this it makes me think about...never mind. Your probably wondering why I took so long? Well that night the doctors called me I sort of snapped. I started thinking about everything and I guess I had some kind of breakdown. I got drunk as hell, hopped in my car and just took off. I thought I was driving aimlessly, but after a lot of hours and a lot of bars I wound up at Jesse's. I don't know how I made it though. I was so damn drunk. I think I hit a tree or something. I'm lucky I didn't have another accident like the one before. Anyways I wound up at
Jesse's and stayed there for a couple of weeks. Just getting drunk and feeling sorry for myself. I guess Jesse called Keith and he ended up coming there. We had a talk and things really didn't go well, but he did get me going. After that I ended up out in the desert and...well...some strange things happened. I don't know how to explain it, because I really don't know what happened myself. All I know is that I realized the truth out there. I thought I was going to die out there and I almost did, but...
[Mark shakes his head unable to find the words] Mark: After that I just kept walking, found the highway and ended up hitching a ride. I found a bus station and came here. While I was on the bus I did some thinking. I'm going back to wrestling. You and
Keith had to quit, but i'll still be there to carry on for us. And i'll definately be looking up some of those ex-NeCW punks soon.
[Mark pauses for a few moments thinking] Mark: You know I thought before that I was just wandering around in my car. When I was at the bar I thought that I had somehow wanted to end up at Jesse's. Now I realize that I was headed down here. I wanted to come here and knew I had to, but I just wasn't ready to see you like this.
[Mark looks away for a few minutes trying to compose himself. Terry just looks at him silently]
Mark: I just couldn't stand to see you like this. This isn't anyway for you to end up. We're warriors. People like us are strong. We fight and we survive. If we get beat then we keep coming back until we win.
We're not like other people, we're survivors. We live on a level that most people can't understand, but this...this isn't how we are supposed to end up. They've got you hooked up to all these machines and tubes like some kind of science project. And when I look at you all
I see is how you used to be. It's like your a prisoner, but there's no way to stop this. There's nothing I can do and I couldn't handle that.
[Mark looks Terry in the eye and sees the tears that are forming there] Mark: Anyways when I was in the desert I saw the truth. I knew what happened and I knew what I had to do. When I see you laying there all weak and helpless I see myself. And I know that I would rather be dead than have to suffer like this. And inside I know that you would too.
[Mark stands up next to the bed. He looks down at Terry for a long time holding his hand. Mark bends over and removes the respirator mask from
Terry's face. Terry pulls Mark's hand down to make him bend over]
Terry: [whispering] Do it. [Mark looks down at Terry and closes his eyes while taking a deep breath. When he opens them again he knows what he must do. He reaches under Terry and takes a pillow. As he looks at Terry's face he sees understanding in his eyes and knows that there is no turning back. He places the pillow over Terry's face and presses down. For several moments Terry does nothing and then he weakly struggles against it. He is weak though and cannot do much except grab at the pillow. His body convulses a few times and then lies still. A loud alarm suddenly goes comes from one of the life suppor machines. Mark hold s the pillow in place for a few more moments and then lifts it up. He slowly puts the pillow back under Terry's head and puts the respirator mask back on his face. He turns and walks out of the room as a nurse comes rushing past him to the room. Mark keeps walking down the hall and out the door]
