[Critique] Little Blue Super Jew Roleplay

10 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
LBSJ: "Ahhh!!! It hurts my brain!!" (Television, the mother of all inventions. The greatest, most wholesome and pure creation ever to be spawned from human thought.
The Television, transmitter of information and pleasure, purveyor of all that an owner could ask for. Entertainment at its purest form, whether it be run through a cable or transmitted via sattilite, television is the perfect, be-all-and-end-all of household conveniences. On city streets, shops have sets lined up in their windows. In any electronics store, televisions will be displayed prominantly, reaching as much as fifty or fifty-four inches, showing us the greatest movies of all time, the shows we love, the games we've all waited for. Nothing, nothing in the entire world, is better than television. It satisfies all needs, at all times, and has done so since it's inception. So why, Little Blue Super Jew wonders as he watches the television screen hung up in what appears to be some sort of workshop in his house, why is it so grossly misrepresented? Why is this grand, nurturing device which has given us "The Dukes of
Hazzard," "Cheers" and "Baywatch" heralded in the IWA by a complete moron? As we see the first few seconds of the most recent Brian Quinn interview come to a close, the camera spins around to find the newest
Hebrew inductee of the IWA.)
(First, a quick glance about his workshop reveals a few odd things. First, there is a straight cutting saw against the wall with a large piece of plywood within it. On the plywood is a human outline, and even though the camera can't confirm it, it seems to conform to the body shape and height of Phat Boy Blue. Okay, no solid evidence there. The camera turns around a little more, and we see a turning stone that appears to have been used recently. Hung up above it is a series of T-Squares, each glinting with new-found razored edges. They are wrapped up tightly and bound with wire, with a sheet of paper hung to them which reads "Reserved for Blue Boy." Alright, that's a little more suspicious, but we can't confirm or deny anything. Another quick turn from the camera, and we see a large board with several worn-out hammers lying next to it. The large board has spray-painted upon it a perfect air-brushed representation of Phat Boy Blue's face. Or at least it was a perfect represenation of Phat Boy Blue's face until someone drove a series of thick nails through the eyeballs and into the teeth, with a few diagonally shoved up through the nostrils.
Fine, fine, let's not dwell on this too long. Let's just find LBSJ.) (The camera executes one more quick turn, and we see finally, Little
Blue Super Jew, busily cutting something in half, only to have dropped his saw and begin clutching his temples.)
LBSJ: "Must...reach...remote!!" (The television, still braying out Brian Quinn's voice, is all the way across the workshop, much too far away for LBSJ to reach. But the remote lies on the cutting table, just out of reach. Quinn begins to talk about Joe Sixpack, and LBSJ howls in consternation. He tries to shut his eyes, but just like with a bad car wreck, some things are just too awful to not watch. Finally, unable to take it anymore,
Little Blue Super Jew makes a wild leap across the table and grabs the remote, rolling to the side in a flurry of shop tools and broken boards. His hand shoots up, and in a flash, the power button robs his television of life. The voice of Brian Quinn is hushed. There is a moment of silence, and then LBSJ's hand comes up from beneath his table, clawing up through the wreckage and dragging the rest of him along with it. He pants, unable to mentally cope with the stupidity he just witnessed. After a long series of drawing in breaths and releasing, he brings his eyes back up, and spots the camera. The new cameraman, still as unaware of the fate of his predecessor as the IWA staff and the local authorities, takes a cautious step back, but the
Master of the Megillah makes no move to attack. He takes a slow look back at his television screen, then back to the camera, and then shudders before finally speaking.)
LBSJ: "Curse my Jewish eyes. That's it, I'm fed up with people wasting my time. First Phat Boy comes along and ruins my good mood by professing his extreme desire to be severely irritating until I drag him through a bed of hot coals by his exposed entrails, and now I find out that the IWA's Television Champion still beats his chest and swings from vines, and that I have to waste time that I could be doing something productive watching his interviews. I could be off clipping my toenails or watching paint dry, both would be more entertaining and useful than listening to Brian Quinn talk."
(Before Little Blue Super Jew can talk any more, the interview suddenly takes a startilingly new direction! The house begins to rumble, causing LBSJ to lose his balance and tumble back into a few loose boards. The workshop continues shaking, and LBSJ struggles to regain his balance, still shaken up by the idiocy of the Television
Champion.)
LBSJ: "What the @*#& is going on?!" (Then, the rumbling stops, and a voice blares out from somewhere outside.) Voice: "LITTLE BLUE SUPER JEW. WE KNOW YOU ARE IN THE HOUSE. COME OUT UNARMED AND READY TO SUFFER A SAVAGE BEATING." (LBSJ's expression is much the same as the one he had when he realized that Phat Boy Blue was actually trying to f*ck with him. He gathers himself and walks over to the window, which he has to go up a stepladder to see out of, revealing that his workshop is apparently in his basement. A gigantic tank is outside, and clambering all around it are boys decked out in blue, heavily armed and looking like they haven't shot anything in days. One of them, standing atop the tank, lifts a megaphone to his mouth and speaks again.)
Man: "WE KNOW YOU ARE IN THERE, LITTLE BLUE SUPER JEW. YOU CAN'T
FOOL US, ALL THE FANS PHAT BOY BLUE HAS IN THE WORLD. COME OUT WITH
YOUR HANDS UP." (LBSJ hops down from his window, donning a look of consternation.) LBSJ: "Dammit! There are nine of them out there! This could get tricky. I'll have to move with caution."
(The house rattles again, and one of the T-Squares we saw earlier tumbles and falls off of the wall. Little Blue Super Jew turns and regards it, his visage blank. The overhead light shakes a bit, casting a gleam off of the sharpened surface of the measuring instrument. There is a long silence.)
LBSJ: "Okay, new plan." (Our scene shifts, and we find ourselves outside, in the ranks of the nine men who have identified themselves as the collective fan base of
Phat Boy Blue. They watch the door and all other exits to LBSJ's house scrutinizingly, not letting a single thing escape their notice.
Two of them, watching the backyard, are the particular focus of the camera. Both wear tight blue jumpsuits, revealing their ungainly girth, and blue helmets, carrying semi-automatic weapons. It appears that since they are such a small group, and obviously ridiculed and reviled by everyone they run into, Phat Boy Blue fans have taken to arming themselves quite well.)
Fan #1: "He's gotta come out sometime. He can only have so many potato pancakes and Manichevitz wine in there to sustain him. We'll starve him out!"
Fan #2: "It's quiet...eerily quiet....he should have made a move by now. What do you suppose...?"
Fan #1: "Quiet!! I think I heard something..." (We see a flash of movement come down from the roof, and the camera topples over. A black boot hit the ground, stomping the blades of grass, and hear two starteld cries. Gunshots ring out, and then a scream. More gunfire, and sounds of confusion from the other side of the house. There is a quick, ugly ripping sound, and a scream cut off into a gurgle. Someone reaches for the camera, and it rises back to it's original position. Little Blue Super Jew, T-Square in hand, is appraising the surface of his new-found weapon.)
LBSJ: "Damn, it never gets this messy when I use a Menorah. Hey!! Keep the camera pointing up. If it dips down to see what's at my feet even once, you're a dead man."
(Phat Boy Blue's fans, alerted to trouble, begin pouring into the backyard of LBSJ's house. LBSJ takes off before they get over the fence, running in through the back door of his house. Three more of
Phat Boy Blue's fans appear, though one quickly throws up on the spot, and the other begins crying. The third only stops and stares at the ground the camera has been ordered to avoid, his mouth agape in terror.)
Fan #3: (Fresh from reliving his lunch) "GAAH!! How could he DO that to someone!?!" Fan #4: "This was a mistake! I knew we should have gone to that Star
Trek convention instead!! Why do we even like an idiot like Phat Boy
Blue?! I WANNA GO HOME!!"
(The fifth fan turns around quickly and rears back, bringing his hand down across Fan #4's face. It's meant to be a slap, I guess, but given their strength and physical condition it's more of a girly
chest-slap thing.) Fan #5: "Quiet!! We are the uneducated, inbred, unloved, overweight, balding morons of America, and as such Phat Boy Blue is our natural leader! Never question him in front of me ag....g.....oh...no....Oh
God...."
Fan #3: "Say, what happened to their guns?" LBSJ: "TIME TO DIE, MOTHER F^CKERS!!" (We hear the sound of glass breaking as the three fans turn around, and then the camera tumbles down and into the grass once again. Some bullets go whizzing past, and we hear them careen with the pool house further down. Screams come in through the audio again, and a few lines of static cut through the feed. Without warning, a human body topples down in front of the camera as the terrified cries are cut off, and a moment later it's dragged out of sight before we can see anything besides that it was dressed in a blue uniform. A shaky hand comes in front of the lens again, and slowly this time, it rises back up. LBSJ is standing, one semi in each hands, observing his handy work.)
LBSJ: "Now that sh*t is kosher." (Now Little Blue Super Jew takes the offensive. He runs out of camera shot for a moment, and it swivels around to see him running towards the backyard fence. With a vaulting leap, he clears the fence and emerges on the other side. Gunfire breaks out. The camera rushes to try and catch up to him. As it does, another man in a blue outfit leaps the other way, not quite clearing the fence and struggling to make it over, screaming for his life. Before the cameraman makes it to the front yard, the Phat Boy Blue fan is dragged back over and we hear the sound of a gun's butt ramming into a human face. Don't worry yourself trying to figure out what it sounds like, unless you smash cantaloupe for a living. The cameraman reaches the gate and undoes the lock, coming through to the other side. Three men in blue uniforms lie on the grass, unmoving, but before the camera can get a good shot of them, it moves up to see Little Blue Super Jew standing in front of the tank parked outside of his house.)
LBSJ: "So you're the leader of this group of fan-boys." Fan #9: (A pudgy, sweaty man with a red face and the look of someone who's played way too many video games.) "So, you defeated them all.
That makes me the last fan Phat Boy Blue has on Earth. But I shall not fail our master. Little Blue Super Jew, for your crimes against
Phat Boy, you will die here and now!!"
(Fan #9 leaps off of the tank, his foot extended, and time slows down. He comes at Little Blue Super Jew in absolute slow motion as the world turns around him, letting us see every nuance and facet of the scene unfolding. Dew drips from an early morning flower on LBSJ's porch. A single leaf rustles on a tree regaining it's leaves for spring. The tank, which the idiot should have used, sits useless as he leaps off of it.)
(Then we see LBSJ, looking at the guy as he comes at him through the air in super-slow speed, regarding him as a complete schmuck.)
LBSJ: "Welcome to real time, sucker." (LBSJ's hands come up and unleash a spray of gunfire. Fan #9, still moving at Matrix Speed, moves to dodge them, but unfortunately the bullets, moving in real time, don't give him the chance. He drops like a bag of bricks. LBSJ walks over him, unnoticing as he steps on his lifeless face, and regards the tank.)
LBSJ: "Now this baby...this I can use." (The cameraman, badly shaken by the ordeal, cuts out the feed, and we fade to blue....) ............ (We come back in to the scene moments later, and Little Blue Super Jew is standing with eight of Phat Boy Blue's "fans." All are laughing, and all are unharmed.)
LBSJ: "You see Brian Quinn, that's good Television. I don't know what makes you think you can hold that belt with any kind of credibility when every time you open your mouth people decide to go see what the Home Shopping Network is up to. Yeah, I know it was a little unbelievable and all - "
Fan #4: "Come on, Phat Boy Blue having FANS? That's waaay out in left field." LBSJ: "- well, it was exciting nonetheless. And thanks to rubber bullets and trick photography, no one got hurt."
(There is a slight commotion from the side.) Fan #3: "Wait LBSJ! Lenny's not moving! He's bleeding from his ears!! I think you really jammed that gun into his face!!"
(In the space of an eyeblink, Fan #3 is chewing on the barrel of a semi-automatic ready to launch some latex down his windpipe.)
LBSJ: "ARE YOU QUESTIONING MY ACTING ABILITY!?" Fan #3: "Mmph! Mmm Nmn nmmm!*" *No! Of course not! LBSJ: (Removing the gun.) "Good. That's what I thought. Now if you'll excuse me, I've got to find more ways to torment those two idiots Blue and Quinn."
(Little Blue Super Jew hops off of the giant "tank" prop and goes back into his house.) Fan #3: "Can we at least use your phone to call an ambulance?!" LBSJ: (Slamming the door behind him.) "What do you think?" (The nine...well, eight, actors are left outside, looking at one another blankly. Finally, they all break up and walk off whistling.
The cameraman walks off, and the scene fades to blue....)
