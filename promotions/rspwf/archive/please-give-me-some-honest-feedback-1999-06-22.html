PLEASE give me some honest feedback....

22 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello to all out there! I have been around e-wrestling for about 1 and a half years now, and I was wondering if someone here...preferably multiple people..could read my roleplay, and give me some honest feedback of what you thought of it! Thank you a lot guys! This is a roleplay for my entrance into the World Wrestling Alliance (WWA)! So, tell me what you think! I have been told by a few people that I am very good, so let me know what ya think!
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Fade In...] [A man in a black hood is standing before a fire. The man seems to be warming his hands up with the heat from the flames. He pulls the ottoman from the reclining chair, and sits in front of the fire. He sits there thinking about something.....as of now he is making no movements what so ever. All of a  sudden, we are taken inside the mind of this man.
We are taken to his past.........] [We arrive in this man's mind. We will call the man, he....and we will call his opponent, "Justin". In this particular memory, he is making his debut in the EPW. He seems to be doing quite well in his match. The men move to the ringside area, and He smashes "Justin" over the skull with his metal bat. "Justin" falls to the ground holding his forehead. He goes under the ring, and pulls out a table. He sets it up, and lays "Justin"
on it. He climbs up the ring apron, and continues on up to the top turnbuckle.  He then proceeds to do a Shooting Star Press from the top turnbuckle onto
"Justin". He crashes onto "Justin's" ribs, and directly through the table.
"Justin" looks to be in serious trouble as He rolls  over onto "Justin" and goes for the pin. He gets the 1, 2, 3....and you can hear the ring announcer say......]
The winner of this bout...."The King Of Hardcore" Shawn Cage!! [You have finally figured out this man's name...his name is, "The King Of Hardcore" Shawn Cage. We are suddenly flying through memories of
Shawn Cage's past matches, which lead up to where he is today. The memories continue until we get to the present. We are taken back to the living room of Cage's house, where he is still sitting on his ottoman, before the fire.
Cage stands up, and begins to admire the many pictures above the fireplace. The pictures are of Cage winning various titles, and just winning in general. Cage isn't a man to brag if he wins, unlike other wrestlers, who always brag about winning. Cage is unlike many other superstars....Cage can go at it hardcore....Cage can go at it technically.....Cage can go at it brawling style.....Cage can do everything. Cage sits back down on the ottoman, and begins to speak...]
[Shawn Cage] - It seems like I have found yet another resting place...a place where I can truly find out if I am the best or not. I am going to surpass all other superstars in this league..Now, not
to discredit all other men in here...god sakes I know they are going to give me troubles. In fact, that
is what I am here for. I have been in league after league, and what do I find? No talents....wannabes
if you will....I am hoping to find somebody....or everbody in here to give me some good competition. My style is very simple...I do everything, whether it be technical, extreme, high flying...all I know is
that I can do anything...and everything that is needed to win.... [Shawn Cage just sits on his ottoman, and the camera begins to zoom out...] [Fade Out....] [Fade In...] [The camera pans the outside area..of what seems to be a bar. This particular bar is called the One Night Stand. The camera is going into the bar now, and the camera is panning the whole bar...looking for something.
The camera instantly finds "The King Of Hardcore" Shawn Cage sitting at the bar, drinking a glass mug of beer. Cage slams the mug down,
signaling that he is done with it. He calls for another one, and the bartender gives him one. The bartender begins to speak...]
[Bartender] - Hey buddy, haven't you had enough today? I mean, you have had like 4 mugs already. I don't feel like having to clean up the bar too much after you puke all over it...
[Cage] - (Without looking up) ....Don't worry 'tender. I won't puke. I am able to control myself. I don't go past my 5 mug limit because that is when I start to get freaked out..and then you will have to clean the bar...
[Bartender] - Okay, just be careful drivin' home man.... [Cage] - I will, don't worry... [Shawn Cage looks up at the bartender for the first time, and the bartender says something...]
[Bartender] - Wait a minute...I thought I knew who you were! You are that guy...You call yourself the "King Of Hardcore" Shawn Cage! You are awesome man! [Cage] - Yeah, that is me...thanks a lot..I have been wrestling since I was 15 years old, and it is good to see that someone appreciates my talent... [Bartender] - I mean, you just whip those guys into oblivion! I saw you in the HWF, and you were great! You won the World Title like 2...no, 3 times...didn't you? [Cage] - That is correct... [Bartender] - Wait...you also had won the US Title 2 times in there..am I right again? [Cage] - Correct yet again...you are on a roll... [Bartender] - Well, I know that you left the HWF, what happened after that? I haven't heard of ya since the HWF... [Cage] - Well, I went onto the WWS, and won a few titles there. Then, I became a free agent again. That is when I stumbled upon the WWA. Who knows how good I will do in here... [Bartender] - I just wanna wish you luck with whatever you do.. [Cage] - Thanks, you too... [Shawn Cage slams the finished beer down, and he turns around in his chair. Cage walks up to the bartender, gives him his money for the beers...along with a little extra for the compliments. Cage then walks up to the door, opens it, and he shuts it as he is leaving. Cage
walks to his '97 Mustang, pulls out the keys from his pocket, and unlocks his door. Cage opens his door, and enters the car. Cage turns the key, and the car instantly starts. the camera zooms out, and we see Cage driving the car out of the bar's parking lot. Cage drives away, northbound, towards his house....]
[Fade Out....]
