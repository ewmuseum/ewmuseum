Simon Pretswell sample roleplay.. Any good feds out there?

28 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Scene <Simon Pretswell is in his locker room. He is looking at a slip of paper. It is the list of superstars who will compete in the Extreme Championship Title. He quickly skims down the list and then spies his name. He smiles at this and looks at the first name on the list. It is Diamond Dallas Page. Then he starts to talk as the camera fades in...> ''Sizzling'' Simon Pretswell: You know, IWF. Every young boy has his dream. Many want to become good at sports. Very few succeed. I was one of those people who succeeded. But you know, Not until I was THIRTEEN years old did I want to become a proffesional wrestler. Not until I was THIRTEEN years old did I decide that I wanted to compete with the most, Hardcore, Fittest, Most Dangerous, greatest athletes in the world. When I was a kid, I knew that whatever I wanted to do, I would be able to do it. There is nothing that ''Sizzling'' Simon Pretswell cannot do. You can look at some of the superstars in proffesional wrestling today, And they make you sick. They make you wonder, why you decided to join this sport when you could have done anything that you wanted to. They make you sit up and wonder just why you haven't already kicked their CENSORED out of wrestling. It is people like that who give wrestling a bad name. It is people like that who get TV Ratings of 2.1. Myself? I have never had a lower rating than 3.8 and if you ask me that is impressive. But that is excactly what I am! I am impressive. I am a star. I am without a shadow of a doubt, The greatest wrestler in the world today! You can have all your little rankings with all wrestling's top officials trying to decide who is best but I know, And the world knows that there is only one trun legend, Only one true ICON, And that is me. The rest of the superstars in the IWF or in any federation for that matter can come out, week after week boring the pants of the fans with their long LECTURES, but the fact of the matter is, Nobody cares. Nobody cares what they have to say because they are not the best, And people only want to listen to the best. That is why they sit up and take notice when Simon Pretswell comes down to the ring. Now where was I? Oh, Yes. Now, I knew that whatever proffesion, I decided to take on, I would succeed. I knew that. Until the age of THIRTEEN, I had not even considered becoming a proffesional wrestler. Then one day, I was watching the USA Network and I saw some man by the name of Mankind on. He was beating up his opponent so badly, He got his ear ripped of in the fight. The crowd were all horrified. Me? I had a smile on my face. Not because of what happened to Mankind. No, because at last I had discovered what I wanted to do in life. I wanted to be a wrestler. It was when I saw that match, When I saw how much punishment you could dish out on your opponent, When I saw how EXTREME you could be, I thought to myself. 'Simom, That is your job''. So after about a month of training {I was so talented I only needed a month you see}, I made the breakthrough. The rest of my past is not important at this moment but there was a point to that story. You see I wanted to be a wrestler at the age of THIRTEEN. That is the number of titles for me to win {And I will win them!} here in the IWF. And I broke into wrestling because I liked the fact that it ws EXTREME. That is the first title that I get a shot at winning {And I will win it!} and it will happen this Wednesday Night. You see my opponent for Monday Night, Scorpian realized that he had not a chance of beating me. He realized that I would beat him up so bad, That I would rip HIS ear of if he fought me, so now he is running scared; He is trying to get out of his IWF contract. But I have been told by the IWF President that I will get the chance to fight him on Monday Night so don't you fans worry. It will just be a warm up match for me though. It will be a match when he will not be able to even get a punch in against me. I will totally dominate the match and use it as an opportunity to show all you fans what ''Sizzling'' Simon Pretswell is really made off! BTW, Before I forget, I have added two new members to the ''Elite Force''. I don't want to go into that right now but just remember. If you want in the Elite Force then you better tell me quick. I am the best superstar in the world today, and if you don't wnat to be with me, Then you must be rather stupid. But now onto something more important and that is the battle royal for the Extreme Championship Title. Now this title was formerly held by Mystic who I beleive was the best wrestler in the IWF. Then I came. You can think of any reason that you want why Mystic left the IWF but I can tell you the truth. He really left because he knew that I was going to take over his spotlight. He realized that I was going to be the man who was going to win all the THIRTEEN IWF Titles. He knew that if he stepped into the ring with me then I would embarras him so badly that he could never wrestle again. So he left. Call him a coward, Call him smart, Call him whatever you want. I still call him a coward. It seems like everyone is running from me these days. Scorpian, Mystic who will be next? Anyway onto the battle royal. Who do we have first on our list? Oh my goodness! Diamond Dallas Page! Can someone please come and explain to me what this guy is doing in the fight? I mean sure if it was an over eighties Battle Royal then he would fit in fine but not in with superstars such as myself. I mean, I will destroy him. He will be having a heart attack after I have finished with him. DDP, I don't think you should even bother bringing your 80 year old <Bleep> down to the ring on Wednesday. There are a number of reasons. One of them is, you are so slow that you would either hold up the Battle Royal for about a week or something or you would miss it completely!! You are a loser Diamond Dallas and there is no way that YOU could toss me over the top rope. So don't waste your time coming. You would be as well staying at home with that schoolgirl, Kimberly you little <Bleep>. But that is enough about you! Who is next? Stone Cold Steve Austin! I mean, another loser. Sure good in the past, Big in the WWF. About 10 years ago! This is real wrestling now Stone Cold not some kind of fantasy wrestling! There is no way that you could beat me. No chance at all. I tell you what I will do. If you throw me over the top rope on Wednesday Night then I will give you a free haircut!! Oh sorry, There is nothing there to cut!! You were bald the second you were born and you stayed that way. And that a'int the only place you are bald, and Shawn Michaels told me that. There a'int any chance at all of YOU eliminating Simon Pretswell from the Battle Royal and that's the way it's gotta be! Next on the list? Wildcat. Give me a break. Him and the rest of the UWO losers couldn't defeat me in a century. I mean the guy is about as Wild as Marc Mero!!! I am not going to bother talking about him because he is the lowest form of scum on this earth! Next? Dangerous Danny. A more appropriate name would be ''Dangerous to his Granny''. That is the only way he is dangerous. I heard that she had to get a 24-Hour security watch because he kept trying to beat her up. Sure he couldn't but that is not the point. Then we have Rage. He can let out all the rage that he wants and he still couldn't get Simon Pretswell of his feet. His <Bleep> will be the first that I toss over the top rope on Tuesday. Then we have got Asman Jaguar or some sad name like that. One word sums up him. L-O-S-E-R. He can't defeat me and he never will. Then guys like Damien, Scarface, and Darkman are as good at giving interviews as they are wrestlers. Get the picture? Lord Bodycount.. He is a man who was a star. Now he is just a washed up nobody. A Has-Been. There is no point him competing because he is just not going to win. Mog? Nope, Never a good wrestler! KO Kid? Good potential but not smart enough. Mystical? Don't get me started on him. Executioner? Same as Bodycount. Prince Diablous? Enough said. Pac? A no talented loser who is only here for the money. Shawn Michaels? We all know how I feel about him. So it should now be clear to you all that I am the only man who has a chance of winning this Battle Royal. Everyone else can think about winning it. They can have their dreams. I did....and it came true..... Only 13 to go.... >>
