Ian Walsh roleplay . . .

23 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I haven't done this in a long time, but just felt like getting some opinions on my roleplaying . . .
[Fade in.] [A man sits atop a large boulder in the horizon of a sunset. He sits with knees bent and looking away at the dimming light of the setting sun. He sits almost still except for slight movements in his feet. They make almost a tapping movement. His head bobs slightly, back and forth. Eventually, the movement slows and comes to a digressive halt.
The camera seems to revolve around him in order to get a shot of him from his front. The remaining light from the setting sun glares off of his pale, clean-shaven face. He generates a presence of "fear me" as his face shows no emoition and as he looks out into the clear of nothingness before him.
The camera sets focus on the man, the boots hewears on his feet, the blue shorts, the black and green, vertically striped, silk shirt that is open at the nape and blowing in the slight mountain breeze . . . the description places him as Walsh. His light skin, his now short, dirty blonde hair, his hypnotic blue eyes . . . He has his right leg bent at the knee, he folds his hands over it, places his head on it, and continues his gaze into the darkening horizon. As he does this, he speaks the following in a clear, calm, and soft voice:]
IW: "Destiny, eh? My life . . . the hopes and dreams that I had, I am finally going to achieve and surpass them."
[He turns his head into his knee, but moves his mouth into open space so that the words that he speaks are not muttered.]
"Many o' time, I have walked down the aisle, the road, if you will. I have seen my opposition awaiting my presence. I thought about my opposition and thought that it would all just be a piece of cake.
Well, boys . . . I guess I was wrong. As I may be undefeated in the
W.W.W.A., I cannot sit back and relax. I cannot let other surpass me in my race.
After my match at Clear the Lane, I felt my head throbbing and said that this is easy to figure out. I help a man, take payment from a man, and the man who lost out came for revenge. Do I blame him? [Chuckles]
No . . . As a matter of fact, Mr. Jensen, I applaud you on your effort." [Nods his head.] "However, what ole 'Boardwalk' forgot to consider was what would happen if he did not take ole Ian out of the game for good. Mr. Jensen, you have been pleading with officials, you have been stating in your interviews, you have taken action . . . But for what? Why? What is it that one such as yourself is looking for? Could you be searching for the answer? Are you searching for the master? If not, then what? then who? Have thou not straightened his own priorities? Have thou seen bitter and grown desire to extinguish the raging fire that is Walsh? If so, then extinguish me. If not, then extinguish yourself.
Since I usurped the natural tradition of wrestling, people have seen me in a light of a star, of a figure, of a legend. I see thee make bootless attempts at reciprocity at thy Walsh. I also see thee make fruitless attempts at damnation of thy Walsh."
[Chuckles] "Ah Jensen, petty fool . . . you are my foe, indeed. But in this war, survive you shall not. In this war, win you shall not. In this war, defeat . . . you shall receive."
[Jests a fraudulent sign of peace.] "Peaceful world we are not. We are a world at war, we are. What this will be is another battle, another fight, another contest to see just who continues on and to see just who lags behind. Yes, oh yes . . . we shall see only one winner, only one victor, only one survivor.
But the person who I oppose is yet to be named as the two possible contenders face each other to see who faces me. First, a man who I have already made mention of, Mr. Jensen. Second, we have a man who I have faced before and defeated, King Agony. The two men will battle, pillar to post, top to bottom, north to south, east to west . . . until one wins . . . and goes on, in a battered heap, to face the lord, the king, the god of torture . . . the master pf pain, the master of all . . . the
'Human Torture Rack' . . . Ian Walsh."
[Holds himself content.] "From what I have heard, facing thy Walsh is not a reward, but a chore and a punishment. Facing thy Walsh is an opportunity, oh yes, but when ole Walsh enters that ring, you tend to think twice about the opportunity . . . and think more about your life."
[Moves his head to look once more at the now darker horizon.] "Whether the opposition be Mr. Jensen or King Agony, my mindest is more focused, my arsenal of maneuvers is much larger. Therefore, for either man, glory will have it's price. And the payment . . . your career.
Prepare to meet . . . . . . your demise." [Ian jests another fraudulent sign of peace, kisses his fingers, points at the camera, and makes a downward pulling motion that leads to darkness of the picture.]
[Fade.] -Ian Walsh President of: [I.M.W.A.] http://members.aol.com/TheOESW/Main.html Handler of: "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh Currently in: [W.W.W.A., I.W.E.] Handler of: The Abortionist Currently in: [I.M.W.A. (Low carder in I.M.W.A.), L.W.A.]
