"Black Feather" Scotty Phillips Flash(USx)

15 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The opening notes of "One" by Metallica are strummed as the camera opens with a shot of "Black Feather" Scotty Phillips' new apartment, in
Sacramento. Now, the room is done, as the bed now has sheets, the TV is hooked up, and the trophies, certificates, and the poster of Cameron
Diaz are all over the wall. Scotty Phillips is lying on the bed on his side, facing the TV across the room. The remote is in his hand, and a
Mountain Dew is on the nightstand. It is getting dark outside, and the alarm clock next to Scotty's bed reads "5:22 PM". Scotty flips through the channels on the TV for a minute, then takes a long sip of the
Mountain Dew. He then sits up, gets comfortable in a corner of the bed against the wall, and faces the camera. The music stops.)
"Black Feather" Scotty Phillips: Chapter Two is done. Tommy Nyce, you thought you had the match going your way. But you made the fatal mistake that one man must make. You wasted time, and you missed the flying elbowdrop. I capitalized, shifted the momentum in my direction, hit the
Sidewalk Slam, and then, I finished you off with the Floating Feather.
So Chapter Two ended with the drop of a feather that had the impact of an anvil. Now, Chapter Three begins, as I move up to the bigger fish. A mysterious
Haitian by the name of Lodin "The Progeny" Inconnu. As they say for me, the announcers say you're on your way up in the rankings, Lodin. I don't know what a "Progeny" is, but with two matches and two wins underneath my wings and black feathers, I have a feeling I can win this one. I requested this match because I wanted some competition. Mike Lee and
Tommy Nyce were outmatched. You, on the other hand, have a few wins under your belt, as do I. We're both undefeated in the USx. But next
Tuesday night, we'll meet in the ring at Xtremes, and only one of us will walk out still undefeated. The other will remain in the ring counting the lights and contemplating the loss. Of course, I don't know that it won't be myself on the losing end of the match, but there's a good chance it will be you, Lodin. You've definitely got the experience edge, as I've only wrestled two matches, compared to I don't know how many for you. Who knows what I've got the edge in? We'll find out this
Tuesday night. Either I will beat you, or you will beat yourself. You can dominate all night, but there's always that fatal mistake you make that leads to a three-count for your opponent. Tommy Nyce made a big mistake, and you could be headed the same direction. Then...you've seen the drill, but you haven't been through it...it's the Sidewalk Slam, and the Floating Feather lands on you. And after that, the drill continues.
The Floating Feather has gotten me the win twice before. That's going to continue this Tuesday night, Lodin. Good night.
("One" by Metallica begins to play again, as Scotty turns away from the camera. He lies on his side once again, facing the TV and holding the remote. He takes another sip of his Mountain Dew, and changes the channel. He begins surfing through the channels, as the screen slowly fades to black.)
