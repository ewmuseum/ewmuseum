Critique: Steve Sovey

29 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<We open up in the Skyreach Center in Montreal, Canada as PWSFe Hate is about to begin! The ring crew is setting up the ring as they have already raised the steel cage which will be used later on tonight for the PWSF Pay-per-view No
Escape! John Jenkins and William Trotter make their way into their seats and prepare for the upcoming matches!>
Jenkins: It's going to be a long night Bill... Trotter: Yeah...Hate and No Escape...we just don't win do we? Jenkins: Like I said, a LONG, LONG night... <The crew finishes up some last touches as Hate is still a few hours away. Some wrestlers have made it out to the ring to sign some pre-match autographs...a typical way of life for these men and women. Among the wrestlers near the ring are PWSF Developmental wrestlers Taylor, Danny Moore, and Chris Cross and PWSF
Superstars Jared Hart, Kid Gorgeous, and an old vet in Pyro who is just visiting some friends.>
Trotter: Look at some of the superstars out here John! Jenkins: Yep, there is Jared...oh there is Chris Cross.... Trotter: And ex-PWSF superstar Pyro is there as well. He had quite a little rivalry with Steve Sovey if you remember!
Jenkins: How do you remember if you wer'nt here yet? Trotter: .....uh... ???: HELLOOO MONTREAL!!!!! Jenkins: What? <The crowd goes absolutly crazy as Steve Sovey is standing on the entrance ramp! He is dressed in black jeans and a "Syco" t-shirt! He smiles at the crowd as the women go crazy! Sovey then makes his way down the ramp taking time to greet the fans!>
Jankins: Hey it's Steve Sovey....10 bucks says he loses to Bane tonight! Trotter: Yes, our own PWSF superstar is here....I wonder what he has to say? ....you are on...10 it is..... <Steve walks up the ring steps and grabs a mic lying at the corner! The fans are still cheering as Sovey poses for the fans in the middle of the ring!>
Jenkins: Um...look at Pyro.... <Pyro is seen staring at Sovey....obviously still mad about those embarrasing losses back in the day!>
Sovey: HELLO CANADA!!!!!!!! <MASSIVE pop by the fans!> Sovey: What a night we have in store for all of YOU! Not only do we have NO
ESCAPE, which is sure to be the best damn show of the year, we also have Hate, where all of tomorow's superstars fight today! Matter of fact, PWSFe Hate is why I am out here right now!
Jenkins: What in the hell does he mean? Sovey: You see my shirt? It has a PWSFe Developmental wrestler on it! Yes folks....it is none other then the man who challenged the mysterious
"Franchise" to a match tonight on Hate! It's none other then Syco! <The crowd boos at the mention of Syco!> Trotter: But why is he bringing up Syco? Sovey: Now I am sure most of you are wondering why I am bringing up Syco! Someone who is obviously well below my talents. Well it's simple really. Think very hard folks....think VERY hard! Tonight Syco goes one on one with "the
Franchise"!
Jankins: Wait a minute.... Sovey: Now folks.....I do believe there is only 1 FRANCHISE! Trotter: Oh my gawd.... <The crowd is starting to cheer as they realize just what Sovey is talking about!> Sovey: Looks like you have finally guessed it! Syco, you made a HUGE mistake by not attempting to figure out who "the Franchise" actually was! You see
Syco....tonight on Heat....in order to prepare for my match against Bane, you will be going one on one with "the Franchise" of the PWSF STEVE SOVEY!
<The crowd goes absolutly crazy at the declaration made by Sovey!> Trotter: This is huge John! Jenkins: Syco versus Steve Sovey!!!!!! Sovey: What's wrong Syco? I have a weird little feeling I won't be hearing that much talk anymore!
Jenkins: I feel bad for Syco now....he has dug himself iknto his own grave now
Bill... Sovey: Syco...I'll give you whats due...you are one crazy son of a gun! You are the epitomy of evil....but that is ALL you are! All you are Syco is a saten worshipping, bible burning, clown spanking, MOTHER FU.....let's not go there!
Trotter: Uh oh looks like Sovey is mad! Jenkins: Clown spanking? Sovey: You heard me Syco....after tonight all, and I do mean ALL that is going to be left of you is this little 5 dollar souvenir I am wearing on my chest!
Actually..... <Sovey rips off the shirt to the delight of the fans in attendance and rips it into twos, heaving them into the crowd!>
Sovey:.....not even that is going to be left! <Then out of no where Pyro runs into the ring attacking Steve from behind!>
Jenkins: That ex-superstar is all over Steve!
<Pyro is laying into Steve until Taylor runs into the ring and attempts to wrestle Pyro off Steve! Taylor gets the upper hand until Pyro low blows him.
This gives Steve some time to recoup though and once Pyro turns back around he is met by a sick superkick! Pyro is out cold as Sovey locks on "the Betterman"!
Pyro is screaming in agony! Sovey breaks the hold and throws Pyro to the security guards telling them to get his ass outta here! Sovey then helps Taylor up!>
Jenkins: What a sick man that Pyro is! <Sovey picks up the mic and shakes Taylor's hand!> Sovey: Here is your next PWSF superstar folks! This kid has it all..... <Taylor then takes the mic from Steve.......> Taylor: Thank you....and good luck against Syco and Bane! <Sovey smiles and grabs the mic back......> Sovey: That's right...I forgot all about Baney! Bane, tonight it ends here! For the past few weeks we have been nothing but a thorn in each other's sides but tonight that all ends! It all ends in a steel cage! Just you and ME, NO
ESCAPE!!! <The fans cheer for Sovey as he throws in that little pun.....> Sovey: There will be NO interferance, no S.I.N, no Soul Assassins, just you and
ME! Bane, you have decided to step in the ring with the wrong person this time!
I will make you feel pain Bane! There are no rules, that means ALL goes! Everything Bane.....just think about that! Do you really want to get in the ring when everything goes with a very pissed of me? Do you really want to test you luck? Push your limits? Because that is what you will be doing Bane! You will be pushed to the limits, the very limits no one else has been able to push you to! I will push and push and push until you SNAP! Snap like a rubber band!
Jenkins: This feud is getting extremely heated Bill! Trotter: This might just be the bet match on the card! Sovey: I will not lose Bane! All I have heard over the past week's is I am losing it, I am getting to old for you rookies! You are all to good and that I just don't have what it takes! Prior to the last few weeks I had only lost a few matches, then all of a sudden it seems like I am on a never ending losing streak, first to SKiTZ, then Griffin, and now to Stomp, well I'm afraid just like your career, it ends here! I will NOT lose tonight, in fact I will single handidly dismantle S.I.N and from there the rest of the PWSF roster!
Trotter: I hav'nt seen Sovey like this ever John! Sovey: It all ends tonight Bane! Our careers are on the line! We'll just see who is the future of the PWSF!
<The scene fades out with "Alive" pouring through the speakers and Steve Sovey making his way backstage to prepare for his matches!>
-Eh, France is a bunch of queers who are good at nothing but sippin wine, rollin their eyes, and losin wars....We should give them back to their rightful owners....Germany!
