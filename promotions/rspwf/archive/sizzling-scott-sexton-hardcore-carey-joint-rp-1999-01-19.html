"Sizzling" Scott Sexton/Hardcore Carey Joint RP

19 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera slowly fades in, showing a quick glimpse of a very expensive restaurant from the outside. Its called "CHEF DAY LIO'S," and the aroma that is carried around that building is nothing short of magnificent. Suddenly, on the outside, a limousine is shown pulling in, bringing curiosity to all the people around. The driver quickly
gets out, and opens the door to the way back, and out walks none other than IWC superstars, Scott Sexton and Hardcore Carey, dressed like complete gentlemen.
Sexton has on a forest green sweater, and below that, a pair of dark tan khakis. Hardcore Carey is actually dressed nice as well in a blue button up shirt and a pair of black khakis. Both men quickly step into the restaurant, and the camera fades out again. It comes back on now as both men are sitting at their respective table. Scott smiles as he breathes in the smell of the restaurant and then begins to speak.]
["SIZZLING" SCOTT SEXTON] Isn't this great? This is what being a gentlemen is all about John, I mean, get used to it cause this is gonna be us everyday of the week! You know what we're all about, its _got_ to be stylin and profilin, rockin and rollin, strutting and strolling!
That is the motto of the Gentlemen's Club! Now my question to you is, John ... [hesitates] Can you dig it? HARDCORE CAREY - Wow. This is a the life. Fancy clothes, fancy restaurants, fancy food...and the best part is that it's all ours. Wow. It's a shame that
Tressa isn't with us because she would love this place. She loves the taste of for this place. She used to tell me that she wanted to come here, but I always made her settle for some McDonald 10 piece chicken nuggets instead, but now I'm kicking myself. [Pauses.] Did you hear that low life Eric Emerson couldn't think of any come backs so he reversed my come back and called me a waste of sperm. Be original there slick. I mean christ think of something yourself........
[SSS] [interrupting] No, no, no, no ... This is a day of celebration and for one day, I don't want to here a peep about any activity in the wrestling world.
What we're here for John is to feast about the superiority of our organization within an organization! We are here to say cheers and pay homage to all great stables of the past, but from this day forward, we will be _the_ stable!
We are the Gentlemen's Club, and John Carey, you're a valuable player. We're all valuable players and as true gentlemen we can take the federation ... [nods head] No, no, no ... Mr. McDaniel's federation and bring it to a whole new level! A level that could have never been reached before!
Thats all I want to say about that, now lets get a waitress over here. You know what you want, right John? I'm getting the Chicken Parmesan.
HARDCORE CAREY - [Laughing.] What the hell is this? I think we some Mexicans must have been sitting here before us because I have no idea what the hell this says. Pasta fagg-ioli? Shit this is pathetic. Can I have a beer? I need some real food, what in the hell is up with this stupid thing! I can't...what the fu....
[SSS] Now John, thats not the gentlemen way to act. Remember that .. [smiles] Why don't you try again? Rephrase what you just said, and I'll be happy to help. HARDCORE CAREY - I know. I'm so sorry. Let me see. This menu does not appeal to my satisfaction thus making my appetite weak. Let me see. [Carey closes his eyes and points to something random on the menu.] Ahhh, the.....Wait I can't pronounce that one. [Picks again.] There we go I'll take this one. Ummm, sure!
Call over one of those fine ladies to take my order please sir. [Sexton raises his hand, and inches one of his fingers back and forth, signaling for a waitress to come to their table. Finally, one does, a good looking one as a matter of fact and Hardcore Carey can't help but lick his lips at her. Sexton gives him a dirty look and even kicks him underneath the table, bringing Carey back to his senses.
The waitress now, begins to speak towards Sexton in a flirtatious manner.] WAITRESS - Hello ... [smiles] Welcome to Chef Day Lio's, and I hope you enjoy your evening here. Our specials today are ...
[SSS] [interrupting] You don't need to worry about that. We already know what we want, so if you'll excuse my rudeness, we are ready to take our order.
WAITRESS - Oh ok. Thats fine, anything for you cutie. HARDCORE CAREY - Thanks! Boy they just love me everywhere now! WAITRESS - I was talking about the other gentlemen ... [Sexton smiles, Carey just puts his head down] WAITRESS - So what would you like? [SSS] My stomach is yearning for your chicken parmesan actually. So, if you could get me that, and a bottle of you finest champagne, I'll be all smiles.
HARDCORE CAREY - Ummmm, I would actually like to have the same thing. WAITRESS - Alright, that food will be with you in a few moments. Thank you for coming once again and have a nice evening!
[SSS] No ... thank you. [smiles] Alright John, now is as good an opportunity as ever. You wanted to talk wrestling, well, we'll talk wrestling.
HARDCORE CAREY - Ahhh, yes. Let's me see who the hell I want to trash in this segment. [Pauses.] There is Eric Emerson, Shaun Chaos, but who stands out the most is that little punk Robby Blaze. [Smiles.] Let's get one thing straight here you loser. I smacked you over the head with that steel chair because you needed an attitude adjustment! I was sick and tired of you thinking that you were the greatest champion in this federation, and hell let's face it no one wanted to face you because they knew you were just a waste of mid-carded time.
[Picks his teeth with his tongue.] Well, son I'm no mid-carder...well if I am a mid-carder I should be the world champion of the mid-carded division. I am "The
King of Hardcore" punk and sooner or later you my friend will find out that
"hardcore" is nothing that you want to get involved with. Speaking of
"hardcore" there is that one loser walking around claiming he's so tough and he's so "hardcore" and he's this and he's....[Laughs.] Didn't you get punked the other week? Didn't that knock any sense into your head at all? Thus brings me to the rumble this Tuesday....are you ready for the match? Fiscus maybe you can see what "The Gentlemen's Club" is all about!
[SSS] Yes ... Alain Fiscus, the man who seems to loathe talking crap about
Triple S, and pardon the unoriginality, but I'll _always_ be Triple S. [grins]
Anyways, Alain Fiscus lets talk a little business here. You want to talk trash about
Scottie Sexton, well be my guest, but be warned that you _will_ pay the consequences!
You may claim to want a match with me, but do you think _I_ believe that, do you think the world believes that? [nods] No, not now, not ever.
Everybody and their mothers know that you're scared of Scott Sexton, that you spend your nights with your pillow over your head just wishing that I would just go away!
Well buddy, Triple S is here to stay! HARDCORE CAREY - Triple S baby! In the house. Man, Fiscus shouldn't even me in the same event as you. What the hell, why are you wasting your talent on him?
You won't even break a sweat! You'll punk him like you did the other night! [SSS] You see, I'm a very unhappy man, and until the day I walk out of the arena as an International Champ, I'm gonna remain bitter. From day one, til the end,
Scott Sexton is the supreme talent machine in the federation and in the world
... With the _true_ king of hardcore watching my back, and the man that is just, Simply the Best on my side, there ain't all that much that could go wrong for the Sizzling one!
Alain Fiscus, I'm walking in Monday night and I guarantee ... [hesitates, and then repeats very slowly in a serious tone] I guarantee that I will win that battle royal, through hell or high-water, I'm gonna pull it off just you watch! Fiscus, I'm far from all talk, I'm the man to beat in this federation, and I plan on proving that!
Eric Emerson, you want to call me an over-achiever? [grins] You want to question my accolades in this sport? How about I turn this around on you?
What have you done for the IWC, for wrestling lately? Absolute SQUAT! So if you wanna question _my_ credibility, my talents, then you do that, but who in their right mind should go ahead and take you seriously?
Emerson, yes ... I am a gentlemen, but even I can get a little rowdy from time to time. So with you, I leave you two words, BLOW ME!
HARDCORE CAREY - Triple S! That's not very gentlemen like of you sir, but I guess when you hang out with Hardcore Carey...he brings the worst out in everyone! [Laughs.] Just like when Robby Blaze or whatever other bimbo steps into the ring with Hardcore Carey...I will simple bring the worst out in them.
I will expose that they are no talented scrum that don't deserve to be in the same federation with the like of the most elite and electrifying organization in all of e-wrestling...."The Gentlemen's Club"!
[SSS] Thats right, its the Club for life my friends! There ain't _nobody_ that dare stop us, especially the Militia! [laughs] Our food is here ...
HARDCORE CAREY - Its about god damn time! [Sexton gives a dirty look to Carey and then acts normal again. The food is put in front of them by the waitress and Carey just gives a look of shock as he looks at what is put in front of him. Sexton does the opposite as he grins in pleasure and then begins to feast. Carey doesn't, and begins to speak.]
HARDCORE CAREY - Oh my God! What the hell? I thought we ordered chicken? What in the hell do you call this shit?
[SSS] What is that .. [looks around, and whispers] Shit? Why John, thats like the best food in this whole darn universe! Try it, you'll like it, trust me.
HARDCORE CAREY- Alright, just for you Sexton! [Carey takes a sample bite of his order, and then his face turns yellow. He spits the chunk out of his mouth and it flies across the room thus finding it's final resting place down the dress of some wealthy lady. The women is in shock with this chunk that just flew down her breasts! Carey begins to speak again, sounding pissed off.]
HARDCORE CAREY - [Pissed off.] This is terrible! Shit! What the hell! This is the shittest shit I've ever tasted! What do the cooks do shit and then heat it?
Yuck! I want to see the asshole that prepared my platter. Where is that asshole bitch at? [Carey stands up out of his seat with his napkin still covering his chest.] Bring it bitch? I want the management! Are they trying to poison me? Do they know who the are f*cking with? What the hell!
[SSS] John, calm down, act like a gentlemen, alright? HARDCORE CAREY - F*ck that shit! There is a point when being a gentlemen doesn't pay off and that is now! Robby Blaze must be the cook and he's trying to kill me! He's trying to kill me I tell you! That asshole! What the hell are all you shitheads looking at? [Carey yells to everyone in the restaurant.] Do you want me to beat all your asses? I'm an ass kicker damn it!
[Some rather big Italian figures walk up behind Carey, and the Hardcore one slowly turns around and notices them. The middle one begins to speak.]
BIG ITALIAN MAN - Sir, you're gonna have to leave. HARDCORE CAREY - Leave? [Gets into the mans face.] I'm not leaving this shitty store until I get some answers! Do you understand me you daygo piece of shit?
What are you doing to do...break my f*cking knee caps? Huh? I'll beat your meat ball eating ass from here the whole way back to Sicily if you tell me again that you are going to kick me out! Do you understand you piece of shit? That's it, stop looking at me! [The man puts his arm on Carey's shoulder.] That was the stupidest thing you could have ever down prick!
[Carey quickly knees the man in his stomach and clocks the other one in the face, finally the third man wraps his arms around Carey, holding him, but
Sexton gets up and propels himself off the table and kicks him in the face, showing impressive skill.
Carey and Sexton both taunt the men as they run out of the restaurant and jump in their limo. Carey and Sexton begin to laugh, as the limo drives away. The camera fades in to the back of the limo where they are sitting, Sexton begins to speak.]
[SSS] Boy, was that fun or what? HARDCORE CAREY - Did you see the way I knocked those two wide asses out. Man, I told you that I'm an asskicker! I told you that I was going to beat someone's ass if they messed with me! You...[Carey shakes Scott's hand.] That was an amazing move off the table! Shit! Maybe you should join me on the "hardcore"
take over of this federation. That was pure...that was AWESOME! [SSS] Hey, John, seriously though, that was _not_ gentlemen-like. [nods head] I'm all for fun, but you really gotta tone it down if you want to stay in the
Club. Lets just make sure McDaniel doesn't find out about this one. This is between us!
HARDCORE CAREY - I know. I know...I got carried away. We acted up, and if Jim finds out he will kill me! I knew I screwed up in there. I'm sorry Scott! I'm so sorry!
[A phone ringing noise is heard. Sexton reaches into his jacket which was left in the back seat and pulls out a cellular phone. He begins to speak.]
[SSS] Hello? [pauses] Hey, whats up? [notices the camera is still on] Hey, hold on a second there ... John, get this camera off!
HARDCORE CAREY - Asshole you heard the man, shut this piece of shit off. [Pauses.] Sir, I will ask you very polite and kindly to turn this machine off in which you operate to much perfection!
[Fades to black.]
