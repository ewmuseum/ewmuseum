USCW Flash Central 5/4/98 Pt. 1

5 May 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Marauders Da Lench Mob Storm w/Venus Sinister Jacob Yoder Tommy Lowe -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- ( Scene is just after May Day '98. A bunch of enthusiastic young fans are standing outside the arena. A USCW announcer is talking to them, playing a game of " what do you think when you hear this name. " The announcer mentions Viper. " Evil " , " Fighter " , " Champion " are many words that come from a variety of kids. Daniel Stephens.
" Loser ", " Whiner ", ( BLEEP ) Idiot " are words that come out. The Marauders. " Excellence " , " Champions " , " Rolemodels ". Suddenly, the three Marauders walk out the arena door close by. Cameramen are struggling to get pictures and talk to the Marauders. The crowd gives a
HUGE pop ( Well, for the number of them ). " The Marauder " Chris Evans raises his hand and shows the Battle Bowl Ring, which just makes the crowd ERUPT. " Shotgun " Shawn Evans doesn't show the crowd his belt. )
Cameraman : Chris, how do you feel about Viper, your opponent for the next PPV. Chris : Well, Viper deserves to be champ, and probably more for all he's done. But I'm gonna give him my best shot, and I'll fight on no matter what it takes ( POP ). This ring ( Crowd cheers ) is a HUGE step for the Marauders, cause it leads to bigger things like the championship belt. The Marauders stand for one thing. And that's striving to be the best, which is what we're doing. ( Cheer ) No settling for some TV
Title or some other title anymore. I'm aiming for the #1 spot. Cameraman2 : Shawn, how do you feel about Daniel Stephens cheating Kid Sharpe out of the title. Shawn : I think Daniel Stephens has probably had too many drugs or somethin'. He can just imagine whatever he wants, like Bullet interfering or being the new Lightweight champ, but imagining isn't gonna get the job done. Daniel Stephens is a perfect symbol of the losers we're gonna take it to in the USCW. ( POP )
Shawn : As for Kid Sharpe, he deserves another shot. I don't feel that the Lightweight belt has been earned on my behalf, so I would like to make it up to the Kid. Sharpe, you can give me your best shot for the
Lightweight Title anytime you want. Cameraman3 : ( yells out ) Are you in cahoots with Stephens? Shawn : You, and all the people know me better than that. I would never hook up with a LOSER like Stephens. I wouldn't have pinned The
Sharpe One if I knew that Stephens had hit him with an object. Daniel's done a good job of getting on my nerves, so I'm gonna stop it. I'm putting this strap ( raises the Lightweight belt up to a HUGE pop ) on the line against him ANYTIME, ANYWHERE. I wanna beat him so bad.... Cameraman4 : Mike, do you feel you had a poor showing at the Battle Bowl? Mike : No. I put in my full effort, and sometimes that just isn't enough. I finished in the Top 5, which is PRETTY GOOD, but pretty good won't cut it in the USCW. My friend Chris here has done a great job, so give him a round of applause ( CROWD EXPLODES ).
Cameraman5 : Chris, any challenges you have to make? Chris : Yeah. I realize now that I'm the #1 contender, I'm a mark. I still have business to finish with Maxwell Chamberlain and Total
Destruction ( BOOS ), but anybody wanna prove to me that they're good enough for this spot, then come and get me. ( CROWD CHEERS ) #1 isn't easy to get, so don't expect a cakewalk over the Marauder ( Cheer )
Cameraman6 : What are you gonna do with all that money? Chris : ( LOL ) We'll see. I'll find something. The ring is just a symbol of my hard work, my dedication, doing extras, and the money is just an extra to that. The Battle Bowl victory has a lot of sentimental value to me, but now I have to look ahead and start training to beat
Viper, cause I'm gonna have to push myself up to yet ANOTHER level. Cameraman7 : Any luck finding any new wrestlers to join? Chris : ( LOL ) I'm still waiting, so if you want in, notify me. But be warned ; you have to be able to stand for justice and tradition, and not move an inch from where you stand. There's a lot of, we'll just say anti-traditionalists in the USCW, and not moving any closer to their side is a hard task.
Chris : It's been a hard night. We all need some rest now. ( The Marauders push through the cameramen to a mob of fans, just waiting for autographs. The Marauders are signing autographs, as the scene fades to black )
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- <you see seagram with the two belts over his shoulder> seagram: <smirks> dont you just love it? ha ha ha ha. check it out, one man defeated that common wealth of pain. and also took the belts. ha ha ha. as you know i have had man problems with mopreme lately. and at this time i would like to call out my manager.
<eightball comes out> seagram: thats right, eightball is my manager. <he smirks> chek it out. eightball: hello, i told you dlm would never die. we just keep getting better and better now that we have the tag champs. we are just that much better you say tag champs? what? you say we have the belt? but seagram dose have a partner? i bet right now a lot of you are thinking oh, i bet his partner will be delinquent. but guess what, you are all wrong. let me call out his tag partner. the other half of the tag team of noumenon. thats right!<he smirks>
<mopreme comes walking out. seagram hands him a tag title belt and the two laugh>
eightball: dont you just love when a plan comes together. you would be surprised what some blood packets and some good acting would do, ha ha.
mopreme: now this is the life. i would like to give eightball his props for putting together such a great plan that paid off in a big way. sure me and seagram had to do alot of good acting, and we also had to sacrifice a little.
but if it takes a little sacrifice to win something as prestigious as the uscw tag team belts, noumenon is willing to do it. and for all of you who say dlm sucks. just look who brought caused the cwop to lose all the gold they have and become the inbred zeros that they are.
seagram: oh shit, lowe and company like we have said many time and we will say again, if you fuck with us, you fuck yourself.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- Storm: So, should I be impressed with Viper winning the USCW title? Not really. Actually, I was so bored during the match I fell asleep. The Snake boy versing the in-bred red neck...........not the most scientific match of the century. But in any case, you Viper do hold that title and your right, I will want a crack at it.....but when. When is the right time to challenge for it?
When ever it is though, I will win it. See Viper, your good friend and turn coat Sinister has already said that he will make sure I win that title. It s a nice offer on his part, and well.......how can I refuse? So, I guess it sucks to be you Viper. But I won't take that title from you just yet. No, before I claim the title,I have a little problem with an in-breeder and his sister to deal with.
Venus: I'm going to get my hands on that witch and.......... Storm: See Jacob, I have a little problem with you. The fact that you married your sister is your problem but my god, your children shouldn't have fours eyes.....each. My problem with you is that, well, you haven't beat me. You might have won, but you never pinned me or made me submit. But yet some how, you went on to face whats his face in the finals. I also have a problem with that thing that follows you two the ring.
Venus: No, I've got the problem with her. You want to get involved in our match freak, well you have better prepare yourself for an ass whipping. So here it is, Storm and I challenge you two red necks to a mixed tag team match.
The sight of you Mary makes me want to hurl so the sooner I send your ass packing, the better I'll feel.
Storm: And so the hit list stars. Chris Evans, the winner of the battle bowl, what a bloody joke. I pinned this jobber in my second match here!! I picked him up and drove his head through the mat. So Chrissy, I'm going to show you one more time why I am better then you. And then from there.....well I have to win that World title. But Viper, I'll save you for another day.
Venus: The USCW can kiss our ass because the SWA has arrived and we are ......4 LIFE!!! Storm: See you losers later!! -------------------------------------------------------------------------- -------------------------------------------------- (After the huge BWFE super card, fans everyone are buzzing about what went down and who the new champion of the world is. Fans everywhere are rushing to by WTF clothing and such, but one man seems lost in thought, leaned up against the building where the huge card was held. In the shadows Sinister can be scene looking down into the dark puddles of water as the rain turns up a notch. His head slowly rises and his face cracks into an almost insane smile.)
Sinister: Dare........... (The USCW superstar slowly moves out of the shadows and puts his hands to his mouth and screams the name.)
Sinister: Daniel FUCKING Dare!!!!!! (Everyone around him stops and stares, then as they notice who he is, many turn tail and run while others try and move on as quickly as possible without drawing attention to themselves. As the street clears,
Sinister begins walking slowly down the sidewalk, rain pelting his shoulders.) Sinister: Well now, it seems my good old friend Daniel Dare has returned! how I missed buddy squirrel! how he used to beg to join us.
how he used to follow us around and try to look interesting to the other wrestlers. How he just completely adored Viper and myself. It is just a damn good thing that buddy squirrel is back. I've missed you Dare......
(Scene of Sinister being hit over the head with a bat by Rick Wagner as Dare rolls over for the cover flashes across the screen.) Sinister: Pay-back time kid. One thing I have never truly been able to live with was your tainted little wins over me. I don't blame you for those, win any way you possibly can and do as much damage to your opponent as you are doing so. My problem is i never got a chance for revenge. For now i am sure Viper will want you all to himself, but I can wait, I have waited for so long that i can wait a bit more to finally get back at you. As far as the Sin man goes you have nothing to fear, it's the snake you need worry about, but I will always be there, lingering in the shadows, and you will know that when you see Viper I must be but a step behind, waiting for the right moment, for your weakest time. One day Danny boy, ther will be no Viper, no SCK, no WTF, no SWA, no Rick
Wagner, no Dunk in the way. it will be just you and me, and the goriest battle in the history of the world. For now I would deal with Viper, but eventually you will be forced to deal with me Dare old boy, and I will drive your Brit loving ass right back to hell.
(Sinister stops in the middle of the street. the rain is really pouring down now and he is completely drenched. he does not seem to care as he just stands there in the middle of the street staring up at the sky.
Eventually he finishes crossing and stops to turn to the camera.) Sinister: Well now, I can't go on without talking about tonights little encounter, now can I? The famed hWo, little boy Jobicide and his side kick Dicko thought they could handle WTF in the match we love the most.
Hell in the sell, and I put BOTH their jobbing asses in the hospital! When the physical therapy is done for Homicide and he can finally walk again after being dropped from the sky, he will look back on his wrestling days, his short career, in absolute horror! He will remember the free fall from the top of the cage and his body spasming as it struck the steel far below, ending is career once and for all. Did you jobbers truly thing you could handle us? Did you really think you could fight the stable that invented Chaotic wrestling? Now Syncho was the best part. I got to send a message to old Chrissy Carter and his bother
Storm. Somewhere in the boon docks of Louisiana, Fat boy and his family were sitting at the dinner table, chowing down on some possum, when they saw it live and in color on the tube, Syncho flying through the flaming table. They saw as he burned, and Carter shivered. Storm swallowed back a cry of fear, and the whores of the SWA weep as they all remember that i did the exact same fucking thing to Carter. I got this thing about burning people. now Carter always wanted to get pay back and do it to me, but as you saw tonight boy, I like the flames. So when your kid brother Storm comes a calling on the Sinmans door for a fight, be sure to remind him I am not a man, I am a GOD! I don't feel pain! I don't feel remorse nor regret for anything I do! I am not a wrestler, but a for of chaos that was let loose onto this world. For generations to come, the family that is the SWA will tell there kids stories to scare them at night, stories bout me and the stuff I did to there family. I am your devil Storm. I am the boogy man you feared so much growing up, and one day I will be the man that destroys you once and for all. Like tonight,
I am going to walk thought the USCW and, one by one, do this to ever other man AND woman in this federation. I want you all to look back on the steel cage match that took place tonight and ask yourselves if you really want to hang around much longer.
(Sinister turns and leaps up on to the roof of a parked car and crotches down low and punches thought the glass. He slides into the drivers seat and turns to look out the passenger side window.)
Sinister: Hey, cars cost money! Even the Sinman finds ways to dave a penny here and there!!
(Sinister laughs maniacally and turns on the engine. he peels out and drives off into the storming night.)

I do not believe in microphones, so this will have to do. Viper, I do not care about losing the match at the PPV.
**Jacob throws down the cardboard roll and leans over the podium. Do you feel good about your victory? You are no world's champion in my eyes. All you are is an English bully that needs to be taught a lesson. I am going to rip you limb to limb Viper.
