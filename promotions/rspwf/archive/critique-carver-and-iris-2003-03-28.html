[CRITIQUE] Carver & Iris

28 March 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[In.] [A parking lot, a poorly lit one at that.] [A beat up green station wagon pulls up. Out walk two familiar figures.] V: I'm fookin' starvin'. [Hannibal Carver.] V: I'm starving, too, Daddy! I am starving really, really, really, really bad! [an obnoxious giggle] I want flesh, Daddy.
[... and Iris Galiver. Carver runs around, opening the door for his
"little girl" and yanking her out of the seat by her hair... which turns her right the _fuck_ on.]
HC: Business later, baby. Time to eat at a classy joint, for once. [As they walk towards the door, we see the name of the "classy joint".] [...] [Denny's.] [Ugh.] [Anyway, the two walk in, ignoring the waitress at the front, and taking a booth by a window.]
HC: Hey, tons o' fun. [An overweight waiter walks over.] HC: Two coffees. W: Right away si- HC: Now, fatbody. [Iris suddenly begins to scream and cry. Tears run down her face as she sniffles.] IG: No, Daddy, no! I want juice! [Carver grabs the waiter by the back of the shirt, nearling choking him.] HC: Make one of those an OJ, knucklefook. W: Y-yes sir. [The fat waiter scurries away, as Carver looks at his love.] HC: Heh... did yeh see the look on the nun's face when I pulled out of yeh? IG: Mmmmm... it was so naughty, Daddy. HC: Yeh... still didn't compare to DeFay, though. When I hoisted him up in the air and then...
"WHAM!" [Carver slams both fists on the table, making Iris jump and giggle.] IG: That was so... tasty, Daddy. HC: That? Eh. What I got in mind for Nelson... it'll make that shit look like a firm fookin' handshake, in compariso-
[Carver's thought is interrupted by the waiter, who brings the drinks. Carver fixes him with a deathly glare as he sets down the coffee and orange juice.] W: So... are we ready to order? [Carver grumbles to himself, looking at the menu. He nods.] HC: Yeh... I'll have this. [Carver points to an item on the menu.] W: I'm sorry sir... I don't have my glasses. [Carver shoves the menu closer into the waiter's face.] HC: THIS! YEH BLIND PRICK! THIS!! W: I... I... [Carver mumbles under his breath, "Fookin' prick, makin' me say this shit...".] HC: FINE! I WANT A FOOKIN' MOONS OVER MY HAMMY! OK?! [The waiter begins to sweat, nervously jotting down Carver's order.] HC: HAPPY NOW?! [The waiter looks over at Iris.] W: And for yo- HC: I... dropped something. [And with that, Carver goes under the table. As Iris looks over her menu, we hear Carver rustle under the table...]
IG: Daddy, what... that's my pretty dress... [_Iris'_ side of the table.] IG: Ooooooooh! [The sound of a tongue flicking flesh, as well as sucking sounds, can be lightly heard.] IG: I'm g-gonna have... oh Daddy... [Iris throws her head back, moaning.] IG: I'm gonn- OH! YES! DADDY! OH GOD! OH DADDY! OH GOD! OH DADDY! OH GOD! OH DADDY! YES! YES!! YESSSSSSSSSSSSSS~! [Iris drops her head, panting. Carver gets back up off the floor, wiping his mouth.] HC: On second thought... just give us the check. [Smirks.] HC: I've already eaten tonight. [Out.] ------------------ Rich ~Adios.
