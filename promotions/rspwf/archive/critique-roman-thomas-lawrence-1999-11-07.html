[CRITIQUE!] "Roman" Thomas Lawrence

7 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the second roleplay for my new character, "Roman" Thomas
Lawrence, in NWP. I believe he's got a lot of potential, but that's just me. Do whatever you like with this -even flames! =) Just give me something to look at...
[Scene fades into a well lit hallway. The walls are very wide apart, considering it is a hallway, and the floor is carpeted with a very nice, off white color, carpet. The walls are covered with an off white colored paint. Along the top and bottom of the walls, they are coated and lined with a cadet blue colored paint giving the wall a nice, "professional" look.
Slowly, we see a bellboy walking down the hallway with a tray full of, obviously, food. Lids on plates, etc covers all the food. He is dressed in a normal outfit for a bellboy. He is wearing a cadet blue suit type thing along with nice black shoes and a bellboy's hat.
As he continues to push the cart down the hallway, the end of it comes into view. As he reaches the very last door on the right, he knocks on the door. To his surprise, the person in the room says, "Come in"
instead of opening the door. The bellboy opens the door and pushes the cart in to a large room - not quite a suite though.]
BB: Here you go Mr. Lawrence. Would you like to pay now or for us to put it on your check?
TL: Just go away little boy. I'll pay for it when I get around to it. BB: Yes sir… [The bellboy waits around for a tip, trying not to be too noticeable.] TL: What are you doing?! _YOU_ think _I_ am going to give you a tip? All you did was push that stupid little cart down the hallway from the elevator. And you don't even know who I am at that! Just haul your ass out of here.
BB: Sorry sir… [The bellboy turns around and hurries out of the room.] TL: Good Lord! Is it possible to get good service around here? I mean, ever since I came up here to Ontario, all I've seen is a bunch of idiot canucks who can't talk _OR_ recognize me as a st-
Let me rephrase that… they don't recognize me as _THE_ superstar I am. [Lawrence walks to the opposite side of the room and sits on the large white leather sofa. On his right sits a small table with a Miller
Lite sitting on it.]
TL: Monday, that ghetto freak Lawrence Williams was declared the winner in our match, not because he performed better than me, but because he used a chair _ILLEGALLY_ to defeat me. But, you know,
_EVERYBODY_ in NWP would have probably done the same, knowing that I am the next superstar to develop here in NWP. Williams knows that if he didn't use that chair, he would've been beat, and he just didn't want to do that.
But, I'll always know that he _COULDN'T_ beat me fairly. So, am I saying out with the old, and in with the new ass kickings. These new ass kickings will come this coming Monday, live in Copps Coliseum.
Once again, NWP puts their one and only superstar on the show to fascinate the fans. If they didn't, how many people would they draw?
Four or five maybe? But when you put me on the show, how many do you draw? You get a sellout, guaranteed.
[Lawrence reaches over and grabs his Miller Lite. He takes a drink and sits it back down.]
TL: Yes, this Monday, everyone will get to see me kick at least eight asses. And you know what? It may even be more than that. _IF_ someone cheats, like they always do, I may not win the first match, but I will win one of the two and move onto face two other men at
Northern Exposure. Whether it be Doc Holliday and James Pillow or Doc
Holliday and Devon Case, I _WILL_ come out on top and claim my stake - the NWP Greater North Title.
Lets talk a little about my opponents… first we have that crazy, hardcore XeRo. For all of you uneducated people out there, I followed TWL very closely during its existence. So, in other words, I got to see a lot of XeRo. XeRo is crazy, hardcore, and stupid to make it short. If you don't believe I'm telling the truth when I say he's crazy, just look at the name… XeRo. I believe the guy is trying to spell zero, but just doesn't seem to know how. Now why would a guy want to be named Zero? Obviously, because that's his IQ level! If the guy had any brains whatsoever, he would have learned some impressive technical moves, like myself. But instead, he decided he wanted to use a hardcore style of wrestling which my pet dog Louie can do.
[Lawrence turns on his television, and "Lifestyles of the Rich and
Famous" is just starting. Mumbling under his breath, he says,
"That'll be me one day." He turns the television off.] TL: Next, we have my opponent from last week, "Ghetto Freakin'" Lawrence Williams. If I didn't know better, I'd say this guy was just as stupid, if not stupider than Zero. For God's sake, can a man please talk right? I'm sure everyone took English in school. Well, on second thought, half the people on NWP's roster didn't attend or finish school, so it may be a little hard for them to know the correct way. Anyway, Lawrence, we aren't in the "hood" my friend. We are in
Canada, home of the canuck people. Another thing for you - it is illegal to use a chair in the ring, but you did it anyway. My friend, you won't be using a chair on me this week. If you bring a chair in the squared circle with you, you're gonna leave with an imprint of your face in it. I won't say exactly what I'll do with it… I'll just leave that for you to speculate upon…
Who's next? Ah yes, "The Ladies Man" Vince Taylor. This guy right here is just as pitiful as Lawrence Williams. I mean, this dude is so ugly, he tries to lift himself out of depression by calling himself
"The Ladies Man." But you know Vince, if someone hasn't already told you, your 'ladies man' attitude is not too terribly successful.
Actually, it's horrible. If I was you and I was depressed, and I went for an attitude that failed that terribly, I would fall into an even deeper depression. But, it isn't my fault if he comes to Copps
Coliseum even uglier after he slashes himself continuously with a knife. All anyone can have for this idiot is hope, and my friends, I don't have it for him.
Then we have James "Simply the Best" Pillow. To start off, I have to ask the obvious question… what are you the best at? Nothing I've seen you do, but all I've seen you do is wrestle. So, that right there eliminates one of the possibilities. And, to tell you the truth, all
I care about is your wrestling skills. If they call you the best because you play croquet like a god, well that's good for you, but I don't give a rat's fat ass. You know, people have heard of you, so they'll probably put your chances of winning higher than mine, but they are full of it. The _ONLY_ reason people have heard of you is because you were a little above average when you were a rookie, but now how old are you? Fifty, sixty maybe? I'm not the least bit worried about you…
Mving on, we have some guy I've never heard of… "Child of Fire" Kossuth. Tell me, what the hell kind of name is Kossuth? Is it ancient Guatemalan or something? And also, are you the son of Satan or something. I mean, I just don't get some people's names and nicknames. I mean, first we had XeRo who can't spell, and now we have
"Child of Satan" Kossuth. Let me tell you, you are one screwy little person. I'm not gonna waste my breath or time on you… lets move on.
El Puerco. The bean bag man. This guy is hilarious and has some skills, I'll admit, but he needs to put his body over mind, unlike most people. This guy is crazier than Zero! El Puerco, let me tell you - if you get a bean bad _ANYWHERE_ near me, I'll rip it open and shove it straight up your big Mexican nose. Puerco, you may be able to take B.U.M, but my good buddy, you won't take me like that. I mean, lets look at B.U.M for a moment. This team consists of
"Homeless" Joe Boxcar and a guy off the streets named Larry. I'm sure everyone has heard of William O'Leary, and he will fit in perfectly for this example. O'Leary, like Boxcar and Larry, was homeless and boy did he stink like a skunk's ass. Puerco, that right there tells you how bad they truly are.
Moving on, we have "Delicious" Dave Davidson. I watched this guy compete in TWL as well. I remember his last match - the one where that stupid guy named Wood beat him and two other guys to become the
TWL Television Champion. Dave, that's al that should be said about you. You had your chance, and you lost to Wood!? I mean, for all I know, that guy may have been handling his "wood" right before you two were wrestling. I'm not touching you my friend. Just _GO_ away. You aren't worthy of a chance at a title shot anyway.
Last, and definitely least, we have Devon Case. Can everyone one say
"Case sucks!"? I'm sure you can. And, since your throats will get tired, I'd go ahead and record it, because you are going to be playing that a whole lot this Monday. I mean, this guy will be lucky to make it to face me. Since I know the NWP "suits" have noticed how great I am, I'm sure they'll give me one of the final spots in the gauntlet.
Oh, and by the way, isn't Devon a little bit of a weak name. Did your parents think you were female? Were you that abnormal? Well, I don't want to embarrass you more than I already have, so I'll stop.
[Lawrence takes another sip of his Miller Lite, finishing off, and throws it in the garbage can.]
TL: Well, I've got some people waiting, namely my wife, so I'm gonna kick this camera man out
On that, the camera slowly begins to move towards the door until finally it gets pushed out the door and fades to black.]
