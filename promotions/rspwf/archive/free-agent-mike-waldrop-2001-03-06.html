Richard "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop Character Sheet Version 1.0 Character Name: "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop Height: 6'2" Weight: 230 lbs. Birthdate: June 14th, 1981 [19 years old] Hometown: Manhattan, New York Character Years of Experience: January 8th, 2000 Theme Music: "Just Go" by Staind Alignment: Heel Physical Appearence: Mike Waldrop stands at 6'2" and weighs in at 230 lbs. He has long locks of golden blonde hair that hang down along the tops of his shoulders. His eyes are a crystal blue in color. His face is very young and handsome looking. His body is well defined and toned, not chisled out of stone per say, but you can see the definition. Not a single scar, scratch or blemish on his 19 year old body. Purity in its purest form.
Attitude: Mike Waldrop, as the name suggests, is the self-proclaimed "Cream of the Crop." He's good, he knows it, and isn't afraid to show it. Often,
Mike Waldrop will belittle his opponents and anyone else around him. I guess you could say he has an inferiority complex of sorts. He talks a HUGE game, and can definitely back up every single word he says in the ring.
Character History: Mike Waldrop debuted in the wrestling scene in January of 2000 in Gordon
Adair's New Wave Wrestling Association. Mike Waldrop's very first match was againt Sabbath, in which he lost, but put on one hell of a performance. From that point on, people started taking notice. Waldrop went on to wrestle for the NWWA on and off throughout 2000, and had a successful stint there, but didn't achieve as high of a status as in other leagues.
Waldrop went on to wrestle in the DWF around March of 2000. This was where
Mike Waldrop got his chance. In a feud with Bob Muretic, Waldrop was able to capture the DWF Atlantic Storm title. This feud has been regarded as the feud that put the DWF on the map and one to watch. These were two superstars that no one had really heard of, and were stealing the show. This brutal feud ended when Waldrop lost the DWF Atlantic Storm title to Bob Muretic after MOTR III, and then the DWF mysteriously closed.
At the same time Waldrop was in the DWF, he was also in Shootfire Pro Wrestling. To this day, Waldrop is still in SPW and is the current heavyweight champion after nearly a year in the league. Waldrop started small, and slowly climbed the ladder after impressing just about everybody in the promotion. People claimed that Waldrop's youth would get in the way and he'd be too "green" to get anywhere in his first year. After a highly successful stint as a part of the supergroup, the Empire, Waldrop went on and challenged his own mentor, Havoc, for the Shootfire Pro Heavyweight
Title. In an ironic turn of events, Waldrop retired his mentor, the man who brought him into the league, and still carrys the gold to this day.
At MOTR IV, Mike Waldrop showed the world just exactly why he was called
"Cream of the Crop." In the first round qualifying match, he defeated James
Pillow, Tommy Lionheart and Alex Extreme in one hell of a bout. Following that, he moved on to face Elijah Flynt in yet another hellacious bout that
Waldrop barely made it by. In the finals, against Victor V. Hill and The
Wasp, Waldrop was eliminated due to his own high risk mistake, but despite the losing effort, Waldrop gained a tremendous amount of respect.
Wrestling Style: Primary: Highflying* Secondary: Technical* *Waldrop doesn't have one style of wrestling. He is a very versitle wrestler. If I had to pick one style, though, it'd be highflying, however Waldrop can adapt to anything. Technical wrestling and highflying are his trademark styles, but he'll adapt to his opponent. This is the reason why Waldrop is refered to has "The best pure athlete in the game." He can do it all.
Favorite Moves 1. Superkick 2. Tornado DDT 3. Shooting Star Press 4. Triple Jump Moonsault 5. Corkscrew Plancha 6. Swinging Neckbreaker 7. Backwards Underhook DDT 8. Springboard Roundhouse Kick 9. Brainbuster Suplex 10. Enzguri Strengths 1. Youth 2. Agile 3. Versitile Weaknesses 1. Youth 2. Arrogance 3. Strength Specialty Moves: The Vertebreaker: Waldrop will take his opponent and underhook the arms. He will spin around, and in what seems to be a reverse Pedigree, jump and drive his opponents face to the mat.
The COC: Waldrop will hold is opponent in a fireman's carry position and then spin him around, and then change the move into a tombstone like piledriver.
Finisher: Da Waldropper: Death valley driver [Can be applied through a table, off of the top rope, etc.]
