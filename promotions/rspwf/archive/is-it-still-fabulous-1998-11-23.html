States Champion of the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance, with the aspirations to become World Champion. World Heavweyight Title. Simply got to big for the Worlds Finest Wrestling

Championship, and had the belt stolen from him by Talon, thoug ha match never took place bbetween the two, Talon physically stole the belt during a tititle defence. This didn;t matter Floyd, as he went on to win the Unified All Pro Wrestling Coalition Universal Championship in only his second match there. The APWC closed, and Floyd felt down.
