As the camera begins to raise, The man's shorts are seen, followed by a t-shirt which reads " Shade.. Tormented and loving it." Finally, his face is shown, and by the familar seen of his black shades, we all realize who it is... - Shade: Mother always said, play with fire and you get burnt. Daddy always said, be careful with fire, it may backfire. Yet even though, my parents didn't care about me, these words I followed, I don't like the heat, it burns my soul, and makes an outcast feel welcome. I learned early, that the flames can cause harm, and precaution was needed.
- Shade drops to his knees, and turns over a piece of burnt wood looks at it, and drops it. - Shade: On the 15th of August, destiny met reality. I walked into a cage,
I
had been in before, and began to attack, my prey. I sacraficed my body, and as the blood trickled down my cheek, I realized, I was in my salvation, this was my heaven. Days past by, yet minutes drag on, and so it seemed that way, when the minutes had ticked by, had I known the next man, later on, would surpass my destiny, I would have not been in the situation I had been.
He decided to put my body, into an inferno of blaze and flames. As the advice of my parents, burnt in my soul, my flesh and bones burnt side by side with them. I made my way to the top of the cage, hoping for the spirit of those who had forsaken me, had mercy on me.. a _MERCY_ no one has ever had, a Mercy I never wanted, but needed on this day.
Shade: As the fire, burnt the flesh from my bones, and put my blood at a boil, the pain I knew oh so well, came back.. and at that moment.. I realized, the destiny I had been seeking, is not the destiny which will be
put forth. My body came crashing down, to a floor so cold, it froze the flames into my joints, into the pores in my body, the pain unbearable, yet a pain.. I began to cherish, for it was mine. As the pain surfaced into my body, and as I made my way into the ring... I realized, that this was a journey, I would head into, not knowing where I was heading, not knowing my location or destination, not knowing.. why.
Shade: I stepping back into the cage, and in mere moments, a ball of fire, was sent hurling into my eyes. Being blinded, I could not see, what others
could.. for the places were reversed. I was sent out the door, and became > an elimination. For that man listening, Devistation, mutilation, and anticipation can all be put into perspective, and when seen through the eyes of a blind man, neither can be pointed out, and all 3 seem to be the same.
Devistation, is something which happens all the time, and without the anticipation of it, the moment is decompitated, and Devistation, doesn't become devistating.
Shade: All though, the glamour of being the Heavyweight champion has now risen, and Stagger Lee is in the spot light, it seems I must continue to fight and show why I am here. Being put on fire, falling from the top of a cage, being blazed in the eyes with a ball of fire, was not enough, for some compassion, nothing I ever do, or will do can make the people care, since the don't really hate me, they hate themselves, what they have become, and are jelous, that the truth I know,is something they can not face, and come
to a realization, that a man of my statue, is right. So it seems, that ALOW #3, seems to have me stepping back through the ropes, yet again to confront a man, who I know nothing about. The truth is, I know about you, pretending I don't I realize, what I must do on the third. Realize that you fear me, you despise the fact, that I speak the truth, a truth you can not face.. because you do not believe, in the truth, you've don't even know what it is... Earl, Devistation can be fatal, but Reality can be worse...
and so the skies, darkened, and so this is the truth... Don't hide behind a shadow, for there is only one place, you must seek... home.
