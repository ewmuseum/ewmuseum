Pure silence is heard in all of the crowd. A few new wrestling fans who don't know whos entrance this is yell, "Whats going on?" They soon know as a small amount of grim music starts to fade into their ears. The music is "Diamond Sea" by Sonic
Youth. At the entrance a black figure takes shape and starts to slowly walk to the ring. The music stays silent as many puzzled faces look at this figure. Many children look away in fear as babies start to cry and even some adults look down. The figure is now seen clearer as he walks closer to the ring. The crowd see a long cape like coat drag behind him but his face is still hidden in the shadows. He rolls into the ring under the bottom rope and looks up. The dim light starts to fade back to a bright light. The crowd talks amongst themselves as the figure is still unidentified by his long black hair hanging in his pail face. He moves to the center of the ring and slowly shakes his wet ebony hair out of his eyes as his face is revealed. It is Jason "the prophet"
Forgotton. Some fans in the back rows cheer loud. They are the fans that follow Jason's career and have watched him wrestle in many federations in the past. Some fans are in awe becuase they have heard the legend of Jason
Forgotton but thought he was just a myth. They thought that surely this wasn't the Jason Forgotton who jumped from the rafters at a height of 67 feet. This couldn't be the one who was in the "Junkyard Judgement" match years ago and killed his opponent. This couldn't be Jason Forgotton. The prophet and essence of hardcore wrestling. Jason slowly removed his coat and threw it outside the ring. He had on his usual black tights and a sleavless Nirvana t-shirt. His muscles show on his arms. Many fans think that this man is a fake. One crazed fan yells, "Your a fake! The real Jason Forgotton has
scars!" Jason looks at the man disturbed. Jason then pulls up his shirt very carefully and shows everyone scar after scar on his back and sides. The crowd then knows that this is...Jason "the prophet" Forgotton. A mic appears in his hands out of nowhere and he begins to speak.
-=Speach=- Hello again my friends. Jason Forgotton has yet journeyed to another fed to spread decay and filth upon. For in the world today there is nothing but that left. I was born in this plagues world alone. My life has been nothing but a simple scar on the world. For I am on a nomadic journey going to fed to fed, destroying wrestlers careers and spreading the gospel of true f*cking hardcore wrestling. I am the prophet of hardcore wrestling cause simply, I am the best at what I do. I know everything and have experienced everything in this sport.
I've been on top and I've been at the very bottom making ten dollars a match. I've been to Mexico and have seen the styles of Lucha Libre hardcore wrestling. I've been to Japan where extreme wrestling is prime. And I have done old fashion American hardcore wrestling. Oh, all those places I have found none that suit my style of hardcore. I am above all wrestlers and I have come to show you the pure meaning of hardcore and why I am the essence and prophet of it. This fed is nothing but a mear stepping stone in my holy career. My first match is against a man, or shall I say boy, by the name of Sniper or something.
I've never heard of him so obviously he must not be important. He will feel
The Pendulum just like the others. He will be yet another victim in my path of destruction. Nothing will stop me for I am the one who will bring the world down to my level, I'm the one who will show the world true hardcore, and I shall be the one who kicks Sniper's ass this Sunday! He says his finisher is called the Silencer, well the only thing thats gonna be silent is his mouth after I snap The Pendulum on his weak ass. He is nothing to me. He is nothing but dirt along the road of carnage that I am on. He shall be defeated. He shall never see daylight again. I am quite insulted to be in such a mild match. This match is nothing but childsplay. This isn't hardcore, I was doing matches of this sort when I was in grade school. I was nothing but an unblossomed flower but now the posion lotus has opened and is fully bloomed.
Be ready to feel the pain of The Pendulum and slowly close your eyes as you plunge into an eternity of darkness and torment. The blood that flows through my body is not like most peoples. It is black blood, stained from years of punishment in treacherous matches. Fragments of glass and pump in my bloodstream, my tears are as black as my soul and you will soon find out who exactly Jason "the prophet" Forgotton is. Well, I must be going for now.
Farewell my friends and sleep tight. -=Closing=- The lights slowly fade out again as "Diamond Sea" by Sonic Youth chimes through the arena. Fans look in disbelief of what they have just seen. The dread the wrath of Jason Forgotton. He slowly rolls under the bottom rope and out of the ring. He jumps the railing and fades into the crowd.
