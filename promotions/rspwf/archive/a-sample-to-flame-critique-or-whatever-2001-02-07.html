Off my www site I got this, updated it alittle(Azrael Ravenell used to be Caleb Azallion) It's part character development, and part speech to the opponent ---------------- Leaves crackled and crunched beneath booted feet as the tall, shadowy figure walked through the woods. Plop, plip, plop, plip. The soft sound of dripping water could be heard as it fell like a crystal tear from the canopy of fire-hued leaves, the last remaining drops that were caught falling gracefully as the brisk November breeze caught the leaves.
The shadowed figure halted his methodical pace, a hand with black lacquered nails gracefully rose,wiping the drop of water that slid down his face, the trickle tickling his face. Crouching down on his haunches, duster flaring out behind him as he does so. Reaching into a coat pocket, Azrael Ravenell pulled out a pack of beaten up and worn out cigarettes, the brand unrecognizable. With careful puffs into it the cigarette lit and, after a minute of frustration as the wind blew out his lighter, a dully glowing red ember in the shadows of the wood.
A pool of water had collected in a furrow in the moist earth, caused, logically, from the recent rainstorm, of which the lingering remnants could be seen in the still boiling gray storm clouds that lingered in the air and could be felt in the chill and biting nor'easter. White luminescence was reflected in the muddy depths as stray moonlight stuck the pool where it filtered through the canopy of leaves. Black-tinged-red hair whipped behind his head in a haze as he stared into the muddy, swirling depths of the pool. Black eyes stared hard into the pool like it was a tool for divination. What could he be seeing that entranced him so? What form of vision appeared before him?
* *just a reflection * *just a glimpse * *just a little reminder * *of all the what abouts * *and all the might have been * *could have beens Images formed, swirling in the mists of once possible futures. Yes,
ONCE being the keyword, because these things before the dark, sooty eyes of Azrael Ravenell are never-can-be's, those dark, what-if's that haunt the world of the waking when they finally succumb to sleep. What if I would have had a different father? What if I wasn't scarred, he couldn't help but think as he looked down at his palms, permanently disfigured from repeated slashes of a knife? Tears pooled in the corners of his eyes as he thought of more, a flash, a beautiful, poisonous face.
Angelic in appearance, a vague picture, the most distinguishing feature her pale, platinum blonde hair. Another flash, and it was gone, and in the absence there was left a hole, a void that cut to the core. And around that core, Azrael Ravenell felt the old hardening of his soul, that old familiar cyst of the soul that encrusted and hardened him. Oh, he knew well about that. He knew more than he wished.
* *another day * *some other way * *but not another reason to continue * *and now you're one of us * *the wretched He stared down into the pool, eyes still entranced, mind and soul caught in the depths. His eyes unfocused.
Images formed in his mind, flashing and spiraling with dizzying speed and intensity. Imagine one of those old, cheesy, 'This is YOUR life' segments of an old TV show, and speed it up to the nth. Then you have a slight feel of what it was like.
FLASH. A stumble, a fall the world spinning topsy-turvey. There's the ceiling, there's the floor, ceiling again, floor again. Pain, that's all he felt, intense pain as the stairs, the oh so painful stairs, struck his head. Oh, mommy, it was an accident. Oh, mommy, daddy didn't mean it, don't be mad at daddy. That was how it always was, and that's how it was that time as well.
Then mother was gone on the fateful night, dead... dead at his own hand. A fading image, like a setting sun. And then there was night; cold and merciless night.
Then there were doctors, doctors doctors and more doctors. All diagnosing, checking, injecting. Talking to him, all of them, telling him what happened . . .
Telling him how Elizabeth Calisa Ravenell... his beautiful mother, the kind, gentle lady, always by his side... telling him how they found her, her beautiful face disfigured... Tears streamed down the now-grown man's face.
Then he was back home, doped up and medicated. And there was his father once again, blaming him, his face always a mask of rage as he took out his grief on his son.
Crimson blood, flowing down his hands as he held in tears of pain. That only made it worse. Blood coursed down his fingers from the deep, vicious gashes in his palms. Gashes inflicted by a man who wore a cold, hard mask, a mask that Azallion donned when in the ring: a mask of sadism and cruelty.
The mask of Donovan Azallion. The mask of his father. * *the hopes and prays * *the better days * *the far aways * *forget it His eyes remain locked, but his voice spoke. It had an odd sound, automatic and emotionless, like a robotic automaton. "I'm facing the newcomer. Or a newcomer, we have so many, don't we? But, Duckworth, I'm almost sad to face you, Ryan, you know that? Oh," a flash of emotion like heat lightning flashed across Azrael Ravenell's face. Whatever it was was indiscernible, and his eyes remained locked on the water pool.
"Not because I fear you. Win or lose, you cannot do anything to me that
I haven't done to myself," that did bring a small smirk, but his eyes remained locked. "But, for what I might do to you. And, no matter the moral compunction nor the regret I may feel afterward, when I'm in the ring with you, I won't think about what I might feel like afterwards. I won't CARE how I feel afterwards. You show promise, and, at least at this point, I might feel bad about it."
His eyes lifted, a dark and haunted cast to them hanging like a pall of death. He grinned, a hideous parody of life twisted darkly, only emphasizing the haunted darkness in his eyes. "Consider yourself forewarned, Duckworth, but, I don't know if it'll, in the end, help anything . . . ."

"Nothing ever turns out the way we want it, does it? Maybe it won't turn out for me right, Bartlett, but it doesn't matter . . . you think you're high and mighty with you gold strap wrapped around your waist.
You think it finally completes you. Well, whenever we fight, remember this, Bartlett. I'm going to tear it out of your hands, tear part of your soul away, tear a part of YOU away, when I take that title from your cold hands. Because, Bartlett, in that you're like me. If you get your hands on something you're persistent . . . but persistency will yield you nothing, NOTHING BARTLETT, YOU HEAR ME!!!?" His voice echoed across the woods, startling birds into flight. A squirrel, unusually brazen for one of its stature, chittered incessantly at Shadow
Fire, as if remonstrating him for the disruption.
A swiftly chucked lighter striking near the small animal caused it, which, upon hitting a stone he hadn't seen, exploded, and with one last chitter the squirrel scampered off. Shadow Fire got to his feet slowly, dusting off his duster with deliberate slowness. With a muttered curse, something having to do with a waste of a damn fine lighter, he looked down at the pool. But his earlier introspection was replaced with derision; interest with annoyance. "What am I doing here, damn it?
Pondering the past, like it has anything to do with today. Agh, stupid . . ." he grunted in annoyance, his eyes darkly glittering in the stray rays of the luminous moon. "But, Bartlett, this is your one and only warning. If you face me, if you come face - to - face with me," his black-gray eyes blazed in fury. "You WILL know what it feels like to be wretched, you WILL know what it feels like to be bereft of your pride and you WILL know, Bartlett," his voice lowered to a deadly hiss, "What it feels like, once again . . . to lose everything."
