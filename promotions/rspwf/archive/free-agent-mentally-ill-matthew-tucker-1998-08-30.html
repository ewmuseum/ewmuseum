I'm looking for a few feds to join. Comments are welcome. If there are any parts of this character that you don't like, it's probably the parts that Chris Douglas helped me with:)
And if anyone consider flaming me.. :open a bag with e-money in it I'll give you that for not doing that. And if you still wanna flame me.. :Takes a bigger bag with e-money in it I'll give that to The Promoter for making you look bad and me looking good. If he made Chris Douglas look good, he can make me look good:)
One more thing before i end the silly introduction and get to the RP: Please, do NOT vote Matthew Tucker as rookie of the year. Thanks:) -------------------------------------------------------------------- [The camera fades in a living room. There, on a couch, two men are sitting. The men are "Mentally Ill" Matthew Tucker and his mamager/lawyer, Dan Fast. The camera gets closer to them , and Tucker begin to speak]
Tucker: First of all, i'd like to say good morning, and all of you childrens, don't forget that breakfast is the most important meal of the day! And the best breakfast is no doubt ko..-
[Fast interrupt him]
Fast: You'll have to excuse my client, he just got a proposition for doing a commercial... So he's a little exacted. So tell them what you wanted to Matthew. Tucker: As i was saying, for only 4.99$ you can get.. Fast: MATTHEW! Wrestling! Remember? Tucker: Oh, yea. Bla bla bla, i gonna kick your a** , cuz bla bla said so. Now, about the deal, this special price is only for..
Fast: Matthew! I'll blow the deal if you won't start being serious! Tucker: ok, ok, so i'll talk about those people who for no reason hit me in the ring. Who do you want me to talk about?
Fast: Chris Douglas! Tucker: Oh. Him. Well, let me tell you all about him. We only met one time. When he was facing Mad Goat in a Rabid Goat Death match.
so, Douglas was about to win, and was fighting Goat near a shed full with rabid goats. WEll i saw mad goat need help so i..-
[Fast interrupt quickly] Fast: He means he saw those poor goats need help. Right Matthew? Tucker :No, i meant i saw..- Fast: Commercials contract! Tucker: Yes, yes, i saw those rabid goats need help, and i went to help them. Fast: Many of you don't know, but Tucker is a qualified vet! [The camera fades in to a vet's clinic. There, sitting on a chair , is
"Mentally Ill" Matthew Tucker. In front of Tucker there is a couch, and there is a dog sitting on it. Tucker start talking]
Tucker: So, you have a headache. When did the pain started? [The dog barks , and Tucker nods his head] Tucker: I see. Where exactly in the head does it hurts? [The dog barks again, Tucker nods his head again] Tucker: Ok. I think you are just a little stressed . Was there any special event in your life lately? Something you are expecting?
[the dog barks again] Tucker: Oh. I'll tell you what. I'll give you some Aspirin, you'll take three pills per day, and try to avoid stress. Take a few days off from work, i'll give you a note. And Ne..-
[Camera fades back to the living room] Fast: So my client, being an animals lover, came to help those goats, and Douglas start attacking him! Tucker: A very stupid move by Douglas, That cost him the match. So,
"mr..- [Camera shows a picture of Mad Goats shoves a Goat's horn in Douglas's
A**] Tucker: ..-a goat's horn was stucked into my ass in the main event " Chris Douglas, you thought you can just hit me and get away with it? Maybe with another wrestler, but not with me. I took it to court, with my lawyer, Dan Fast. Take a look on what happened there.
[The camera fades into a court house. At th judge's chair you see
Matthew Tucker sitting, wearing a white wig, and wearing a judge's robe. On one side of the court you see Dan Fast standing. On the other side, there is a midget who wears Chris Douglas's outfit, and look a lot like him]
"Judge" Tucker: So, Mr Fast, why did you sue Chris Douglas? Fast: Well, your honor, he attacked my client for no reason. "Judge" Tucker: When did he do it? Why? Fast: In the Rabid Goat Death match between himself and Mad Goat. Tucker just came there to help those poor goats, being a qualified vet, and Douglas just attacked him!
"Judge" Tucker: Why did you do that, Mr Douglas? "Chris Douglas" : I had no choice ! My career was going down, i knew i was going to lose the match..
[start crying] "Chris Douglas" : So i thought this way i'll get some attention.. And have an excuse for losing..
[collapse on the floor] "Judge" Tucker: Someone please help this.. Creature to his feet. [Some people come and pick "Douglas" up] "Judge" Tucker: Chris Douglas, i find you guilty of attacking an innocent man. Your sentence is : You will lose all matches you'll have from now on, and will be known as "Mr Loser" Chris Douglas. Is that clear?
"Chris Douglas" [still crying]: Ok your honor, but if you don't mind, i'l just retire. There is no point for me to stay in this sport when there is a great wrestler like Matthew
Tucker in there. I'll just retire and start cleaning houses for a living.
"Judge" Tucker: That's fine with me. Nest case! [The camera fades back to the living room , where Tucker and Fast are still sitting on a couch, smiling]
Tucker: So as you can see, once again, i solved another problem. Chris
Douglas will no longer have to worry about being beaten in the ring, and he looks quite happy in his new job!
[The camera turn to the other side of the room, where midget "Chris
