[FREE AGENT] - Brad "The Marksman" McNeil

26 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm lookin' for a federation that I can call home, basically. ;-) And please critique this flash. It's short because I didn't really have anything to say.
;-) But they DO get better. --- [The scene opens up in a dark and some-what scary looking park... The camera scans over the view and we see a bumb laying down on the park bench... He's wearing raggedy, dirty clothes and you can hear the slights sound of a snore...
Next, we see a slight image, of what appears to be a man... The camera focuses on the man, as he walks towards the camera... He's wearing a black, leather jacket... Jeans... Timberlain hiking boots... And an "ICW:
COUNTDOWN" T-shirt underneath the jacket... His hair is slicked back, with a few strands of hair out of place... And as the man gets closer, we recognize him as one of the newest members of the ICW... BRAD "THE MARKSMAN" McNEIL...
He begins to speak...] BRAD McNEIL: Minnesota... My hometown... In fact, the hometown of numerous great wrestlers. You see the list... You hear the names... And you speculate on who's the best... Or should I say... You speculate on who's the man that made Minnesota famous. In my humble opinion... I'm not the man that made
Minnesota famous... We all know who that is... But I _AM_ the best wrestler out of the great state. Some say that I've done nothin' to back those words up... But the ones who say that, won't be sayin' it long... Because here in the ICW... I plan on bringin' me some respect... I plan on bringin' me some gold... And most of all, I plan on bringin' tradition _BACK_ to wrestling.
[McNeil stops walking and looks around... Now, we see bumbs holding their hands by a barrel of fire... And even a few people sleeping with newspapers over them...]
BRAD McNEIL: The world of wrestling now-a-days is just like this park... [We hear a lady scream and see a man running with a purse in hand...] BRAD McNEIL: You've got theives, garbage, and bumbs... In both wrestling and in this park. But the thing aobut it is... Here in the ICW... I plan on takin' out the theives, garbage, and the bumbs... _ONE_ by _ONE_, til I reach the top. James Pillow... You line 'em up and just sit back and watch me knock
'em down. The scene is set... The ICW. The time is set... _NOW!_ And the mission is set... And this mission _ISN'T_ impossible. My mission is to climb the ladder of success... Capture some gold... And finally, bring back
_TRADITION!_ [McNeil pauses... Then, finishes up...] BRAD McNEIL: And just remeber, _BAWS_... I'm a _SURE SHOT!_ And I _NEVER_ miss! [The scene then fades out, as Brad McNeil turns and walks away.] Brad "The Marksman" McNeil [1-0-0] -ICW -CWC "I never said that I was the biggest, I never said I was the strongest... But
I've ALWAYS said I was the SMARTEST."
