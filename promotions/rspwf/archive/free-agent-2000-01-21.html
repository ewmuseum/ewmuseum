Free Agent.

21 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey there, "24 Karat" Kerry Powell is looking for another fed. Here is a sample flash from the UWC..
--------------- [The camera pans to a shot of Kerry Powell in the middle of the UWC ring, holding The UWC World Title in his hands in disbelief, as Ric Beauty looks on.
That scene slowly fades away to a shot of "24 Karat" Kerry Powell sitting in a chair, it could be called a throne but for all purposes, it's a chair. He stands up out of the large velvet chair, simultaneously running his fingers through his golden blond hair. He slowly strokes his goatee of the same color, beginning to speak.]
Kerry Powell: "UWC, It's been awhile my good friend, perhaps too long. I'm sure all of you remember my first run as UWC World Champion, which I may add, never ended. For all reasons I should still have that prestiged gold belt around my waist, still the crowned champion of UWC.. But no! Just because the company switches hands.. I GET SCREWED!"
[Powell takes a few seconds to regain his composure, and sits back down in his chair. He runs his fingers through his hair once again and picks up a mug of what appears to be coffee.]
Kerry Powell: "Well, maybe screwed is too harsh. I'll keep it at "ripped off." Well actually, that's rather harsh as well, but I'm sure you get the idea... As far as I am aware, there is going to be a tournament held, to crowd a new holder of _my_ title!"
[Powell clears his throut and takes a quick sip from his mug. He wipes some coffee off his upper lip, and again begins to talk.]
Kerry Powell: "What's that you say? Yes, It does get better. Blair goes ahead and signed half of my old home.. Yes, the mWa.. Well, at least this proves that their will be alittle more than me and Beauty this time around.. But don't worry good UWC fans, I, Kerry Powell, the only man with the right to hold the
UWC belt, will get it back.. Mark my words...
[Kerry drinks a last sip of the coffee from his mug as the image slowly fades to black.] ---------------- Sorry it's so short, It was all I needed.. If you have an offer mail me at
[detail withheld]... Thanks!
