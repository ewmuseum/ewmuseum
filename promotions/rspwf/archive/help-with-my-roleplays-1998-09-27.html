Help with my roleplays.

27 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Below is my most recent roleplay written for High Octane Wrestling. I was wondering if any of you guys could give me some suggestions on how to make my roleplays better.
(The screen opens to a mid shot of a large, rusty farm machine sitting in a field. The camera slowly begins to turn, and soon we are brought a view of the annual Ferepenny, TX Harvest Festival. The camera pans across the hundreds of people, some square dancing on the dance floor, some eating pumpkin pie in the dining area. We see a horseshoe pit, and in it playing horseshoes is none other than Chaos. He's without his mask, and is wearing blue Lee jeans and a white t-shirt. The camera continues to turn, and we see Road Warrior chatting with another man about this year's harvest. Behind and to the left of Road Warrior, we see Mike and Matt Holmes talking to their grandmother, Mary Holmes. The camera slowly moves closer to Road Warrior, and when it reaches him, waits for him to finish speaking to the man.)
Rick: You ready for the interview Shawn? Road Warrior: Yeah. Let's go get Chaos, and then we'll see if we can tear Mike and Matt away from their grandma.
(Road Warrior and the cameraman make their way over to Chaos, who is in the middle of a game of horseshoes.)
Road Warrior: Chaos, you wanna do this interview now? Chaos: Yeah, sure, I was losing anyway. (to the man he was playing with) Good game Bill. I guess I never will beat you.
(The man laughs as the wrestlers make their way towards the Holmes'. When they reach them, the old woman is holding onto Matt's arm and telling him about how she walks 3 miles a day.)
Road Warrior: Hello Mrs. Holmes. (The woman see's Road Warrior, and they embrace.) Mary Holmes: Shawn, I haven't seen you in a while. How've you been. Road Warrior: Fine. Do you think we might be able to borrow Mike and
Matt for a few minutes so we can do an interview?
Mary Holmes: (laughing) Be my guest. I was getting bored of them anyway! (All five of them begin to laugh, and the wrestlers make their way toward an old barn standing on top of a small hill.)
Road Warrior: We're going to do the interview in this barn up here. Hopefully we'll have some privacy there. Mike "The Boy" Holmes: Sounds good to me. (The wrestlers walk across the green grass to the red barn. When they reach it, the door is open, and there are a few men standing around drinking beers.)
Road Warrior: Hey guys. Do you think we could use this barn for a few minutes to do an interview?
Guy: Sure Shawn, no problem. (The men make their way slowly out of the barn, and it is apparent that they are already drunk.)
Road Warrior: Ok, let's do this. Matt "The Enforcer" Holmes: This coming Thursday, the committee has pitted me against Alexander Magnus. Magnus, you're new here, so when we get in the ring, I'll show you around. I'll show you the turnbuckle when I whip you into it, I'll show you the floor when I throw you outside. I'll show you the bottom of my boot when I kick you in the head, and I'll show you the mat when I hit you with the Lady Killer. I defended this belt successfully against Baron Blob, one of the few men in HOW that is bigger than I am. What makes you think you can do better than he could? Because the truth is, you can't. At least, you can't without the help of your puny Thug Squad. And even then, I doubt it.
So when we step in the ring next Thursday, you'd better be prepared for the beating of your life. And don't count on winning my belt, because the only thing you're gonna walk out with next week is a hell of a lot of bruises.
(The camera turns to Mike Holmes, who is for once not wearing his usual
NBK shirt and beat up shorts. He's wearing rather tight blue jeans, and a flannel shirt, resembling many of the other men at the harvest festival.)
Mike "The Boy" Holmes: Every day I get closer. One more step towards my hard earned World Title shot. Don't know what I'm talking about?
Well, as you know, the winner of Random Riots will get a World Title shot the next Tuesday. That's when I'll finally become the HOW World
Champion. But before I get my shot, I have to team with Ricky "The
Grinder" Grant. Grant, we've had our differences in the past, but I beg you to put them aside for one night, so we can beat Scotty Pinage and
Dollywood Dan Davidson into the mat and advance to the battle royal.
You are the only thing standing in the way of my World Title shot, and I hope you don't try to ruin it. Now, there is one other thing I have to address. Rudolfo DeRago, your time as Cruiserweight Champion is almost up. This Tuesday, you face me in a non-title match, and after I win that match, I get a Cruiserweight Title match. The cruiserweight title is the only reason I joined HOW. I seriously don't care that much about the World Title, except that it will bring me a lot of matches, which will allow me to challenge for a Cruiserweight Title shot. The World
Title is for the heavyweights, but that's not me. The Cruiserweight
Title is the title of the working man. As many people know, cruiserweights are a lot more agile, and have a lot more energy than heavyweights. Our matches, in my opinion, are more fierce, and demand more wrestling ability. The cruiserweight title shows that you work hard, and that's what I want. I've always worked hard at whatever I did, and that will continue long after my Cruiserweight Title reign. So
DeRago, prepare for the Super Nova!
(The screen turns to Chaos, who is adjusting his large gold belt buckle.) Chaos: What kinda hick wears one of these things all the time? Matt "The Enforcer" Holmes: Hey, that's my belt buckle! Chaos: Did I say hick? I meant cool guy. Anyway, Grant, you ruined my match yesterday against "Hell's Watchdog" (snicker), and I won't forget it. I dominated that match from the beginning until the end, but you had to bring an abrupt end to it before I could. But, that's not what
I'm worried about right now. Next Thursday, I get to step in the ring against that priss The Gentleman, and show him how a real man wrestles.
Gentleman, if I'm not mistaken, you haven't done very well in your short career here in HOW. But, by all means, try your hardest to beat me. I like a bit of a challenge. But, you'll learn what all the others did.
That is, why they call me Chaos. (The screen turns to Road Warrior, who is handling a small sickle. When he sees that it's his turn, he puts down the tool and faces the camera.)
Road Warrior: (turning to Mike Holmes) Mike, you are really confident that you are going to win the battle royal at Random Riots. But don't forget, you'll have to go through me when we get there, if we even get there. And just because you're my friend doesn't mean I won't throw you over the top rope like everyone else. I have my eyes set on regaining the World Title which should still be mine, and I pity anyone who gets in my way, including you. But before I even get to the Battle Royal, I have to team with Holt "Rotten" Roberts against the Gentleman and someone from High Society. Roberts, there's no doubt in my mind that a team of two great wrestlers such as ourselves can beat these two men, but we have to be a team. If you want the World Title, you'll team with me, no questions asked. If you don't want the World Title, let me know before hand so I can beat the shit out of you before our match so you can't attack me. It's your choice, but I urge you to keep your mind on winning the World Title.
(The screen closes as Mary Holmes makes her way into the barn and warns that wrestlers that the harvest games are about to start, and that they better hurry if they don't want to miss it.)
