[- Flash Critique: Hybrid -]

6 August 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, This is a flash from May 10th of this year, while I was handling in the NAIWF
(soon-to-be reopened by me, by the way). Anyway, since I have never posted a roleplay on RSPWF before -- and I am extremely bored right now -- I figured why the hell not. Let me know what ya think. Peace.
-------------------------------------------------------------- [- The scene opens to a view of the Tulsa Riverparks amphitheater on the West banks of the Arkansas River in Tulsa, Oklahoma. The camera pans across the rather large stage, out onto the numerous rows of concrete benches, reminiscent of the Roman Coliseum... As the camera pans upward, towards the top of the steep climb of seats, looking out over the vast park, is none other than
Hybrid. The camera zooms in on him, and we are able to get a better look at him. His short blonde hair now appears a little longer, a little fuller, and he is dressed in a gray "Tulsa Webster Baseball: 4A State Champions" T-shirt, light beige AE cargo shorts, and brown leather Dr. Martens sandals... Hybrid's gaze seems to extend to the very edge of the horizon, and does not seem to know anyone is watching him. Suddenly, he turns in the direction of the camera, and begins to descend the steep concrete steps. Only then do we notice that over his right shoulder he carries his newly attained NAIWF Intercontinental Title.
It appears to have just recently been polished, as it shines brightly in the sunlight.. As Hybrid reaches the bottom of the stairs, he reaches into his pocket and pulls out and puts on a pair of titanium-frame Oakley E-wire sunglasses. He then turns his head facing directly into the camera, and begins to speak in a very calm voice. -]
Hybrid: For over fifteen months I have been waiting patiently for my turn. Standing in line waiting for my opportunity to shine, while at the same time busting my ass day in and day out, hoping someone would finally take notice...
and at last, they did. [he smiles] John Voss, you and I are by no means on friendly terms. However, I respect you as a competitor. You have given me a hell of a work out to finally earn this championship gold, and for that I thank you. I wouldn't want it any other way... And I can guarantee everyone, that my second reign as Intercontinental Champion will not be for a mere fifteen minutes, as the first one was. Oh no, I intend on holding onto this baby for quite some time...
[- He pauses as he gently pats his left hand on the Intercontinental Title. Hybrid then retrieves a folded-up piece of paper from one of the many pockets in his cargo shorts. He unfolds the sheet of paper, and quickly skims over whatever the content of the paper may be. As he looks up from the paper a second later, he has a rather confident smirk on his face. He folds up the piece of paper, and clutches it in his hand as he speaks yet again. -]
Hybrid: I received this fax earlier today from the NAIWF offices. It seems that
Reon has had a few choice words to say about yours truly, and I guess the NAIWF felt what he had to say about their Intercontinental Champion was important enough to forward on to me, instead of waiting for me to check out what he said when I got back to New York... It more or less appears that Mr. Reon Wilson feels that he *handed* John Voss to me, and inturn handed me the NAIWF
Intercontinental Title. [his smirk turns to a broad grin] Y'know Reon, I gotta tell you. Those petty little comments you made about me had got to be some of the funniest things I have heard in quite some time. Y'see "Franchise," you will never "handle" me as you put it, because you are not worthy of an
Intercontinental Title shot. Plain and simple, it is *you* that is the worthless piece of [censored], not me.. I couldn't care less about what you -- or John Voss for that matter -- have to say about your IAWF resume'. It is very apparent to me that you posses a room temperature IQ, otherwise you would remember what I had to say about you the last time you tried to intimidate me with your IAWF reputation. Therefore, I will repeat myself: all the things you did while in the IAWF means absolutely jack to me, little man. You think you deserve a shot at my Intercontinental Title? The last thing you should do is brag about doing this, this, and this while you were in the IAWF. Show me what you have accomplished during your NAIWF career... and so far, you haven't done a damn thing. You think you are the franchise? I got three words for you: prove it, son. Until you can back up your claims, I suggest you start at the bottom of the NAIWF ladder, and talk to me in about six months. And, as they say Mr.
Franchise, put *that* in your pipe and smoke it. I'm out. [- Hybrid flashes his pearly whites for the camera, then turns and walks away. The scene slowly fades to black as the camera zooms in on the writing on the back of his shirt: "Warrior Baseball: Busting Ours, To Kick Yours!" -]
