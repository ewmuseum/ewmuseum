Role Play to Critique (H. Hotbody)

1 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a role play for one of my kooky, little characters. Feel free to flame, disembowel, destroy, etc. etc.
Dante' Handles a bunch of wrestlers in a bunch of feds. ========================================================== (The scene opens at Club Cheetah, a small gentlemen's club in downtown Los
Angeles. The club is empty on this sunny afternoon. As the camera snakes past the bar and tables, it finally lands on the stage, where a large pole is vertically situated. Suddenly, the clicking of heels can be heard as "The
Seductress" Holly Hotbody steps from behind the curtains. Holly wears a blue sweater, open to reveal a white, button-up shirt. The shirt is tied in the front, showing off her taut, toned midriff. The sleek beauty also wears a blue, pleated, micro-mini dress, offering a tantalizing glimpse of her matching, blue
G-string with each movement. Completing the "Catholic School-Girl-Gone-Bad"
look are white socks and brown, patent leather shoes. Her long, auburn hair is fashioned in pigtails, bangs resting above her hazel eyes. With a smirk upon her lovely visage, Holly stalks the pole before grabbing hold of it and spinning in a fluid, graceful motion. She sensually spins on the pole before slowly lowering her form onto the stage, landing a perfect split.)
Holly: (smiles) Now, /that's/ what I call dancing. I don't know what in the hell Marissa Monet called herself doing, a few weeks ago, but it sure as hell wasn't this. Watching that walking case of Roid Rage allegedly bump and grind on The Tease was enough to make every stripper from here to Tokyo want to turn in her G-string. What I do is considered an art, Marissa. What you did on the
Tease is considered a joke. I want you to remember that the next time you do another of your piss poor performances, pumpkin.
Now, I know what you're all thinking. You probably think that this is all sour grapes after my defeat at Marissa's hands. Well, nothing could be further from the truth. I realize that "Susie Steroids" beat me and I have no problem admitting it. Granted, she had The Syndicate sticking their noses in our match, distracting me, but that's another story. (smirks) Regardless, she still managed to overcome my attack and pin my shoulders to the mat. (in a sarcastic tone) Congrats, Marissa. You deserved it. Big whoop. (speaking in her normal tone again) But don't get too cocky or too sure of yourself over this lucky, little win. You may have managed to get the drop on me once, Monet, but you won't be able to do it a second time. And, believe me, there will be a second time. I promise. So, go on and enjoy your victory because, before you know it,
Holly Hotbody will be gunning for you again and, this time, you'll be the one left laying.
(Using the pole, Holly pulls herself up, standing to her feet. Wrapping her arms around the pole, "The Seductress" shoots the camera a dazzling smile.)
Holly: You know, it feels really good to be on The Tease again. I really missed you guys and I know that you all missed me too. (she blows the camera a kiss)
After all, without my sexsational presence to keep you all....stimulated (a sly grin), you all had to suffer through the rantings and ravings of bores like
Nikita Marx and Lida Yanigasawa. Well, have no fear because I'm back and ready to tell it like it is and how it's going to be.
As you all know, I'm soon going to be competing in one of the more exciting matches of my short, wrestling career. It seems that the GDWA has scheduled me to face Keiko Mita, Heidi Leick, and Bloody Mary in a four way, Ironwoman match, the winner earning a shot at the world champion. Well, I'm honored that the GDWA brass decided to add me into the mix. It shows that they believe in me and my abilities, something that couldn't make me happier. This is the type of match that could really launch my career and I don't plan on wasting the opportunity! Besides, after my poor showing in the Demonica Challenge, I have a lot to prove to the fans. It will be a difficult contest but I'm more than sure that I have what it takes to come out on top. (with a sly grin and wink) After all, I've always had a knack for coming out on top.
(With that, she grabs hold of the pole, swings, and lifts herself, wrapping her legs around it. In a quick motion, the busty brunette finds herself upside down, suspended above the stage, her legs and arms holding her aloft on the pole.)
Holly: Now, people have been asking me my thoughts on the competition in this match. My response is: what competition? True, Keiko Mita will probably give me fits. After all, she's one of the more talented wrestlers that the GDWA employs and I think she's one of the few women here that I actually respect. But Heidi
Leick and Bloody Mary!?! (She rolls her eyes) Give me a break! Mary's a washed-up lush, whose best days are way behind her, and Heidi is a one-note garbage wrestler with delusions of being "Hardcore". They're both way too one-dimensional to ever offer me too much of a challenge. After all, Mary's fake ass brawling has long since gone the way of the dinosaurs. Todays pro wrestlers are way more diverse than that and don't just have to rely on punching and kicking to win a match. I'll throw in a few topes here, a handspring elbow there and grandma won't know what hit her. It should be especially nice to beat Mary into submission after those snide comments she made about me when I was making my GDWA debut. Don't think I've forgotten you,
Mary, or your cheap insults. You thought that it was funny talking trash about
"the little stripper"? Well, let's see how funny it is when this "little stripper" sticks her foot so far up your ass that you'll be coughing up shoe strings. And, if Ana Conda sticks her pointy nose in the match, it won't be a problem for me to slap the taste out of her mouth, as well.
Then, there's Little Miss Heidi Leick, (rolls her eyes) "The Hardcore Icon". Whatever. At first, I couldn't decide who I wanted to beat on most, Mary or
Heidi. Since Heidi seems to be associated with those nosy skanks in The
Syndicate, she wins the contest. Hands down. I couldn't care less if you're a
Syndicate member or just one of their lackeys, Heidi. The fact that you even allow yourself to be seen in public with Chandler and Duran is enough for me to want to kick your ass. Plus, I owe you for the cane shot you delivered a few
Catfights ago. Don't remember? Well, let me refresh your memory. I was coming to ringside to offer Keiko Mita some support when you attacked me for no good reason. Well, it's payback time, Heidi, and I'm the bitch that's delivering.
You may be "hardcore" and you may know your way around a Singapore Cane but that won't mean anything when we come face to face in that ring. You see, this won't be a Hardcore match or any other such barbaric fare. This will be a wrestling match, sweetie, with rules and referees. In other words, you will be fighting in my domain and, sorry to say, chairs and stop signs won't be allowed. You may have the experience over me, Heidi, but I've got the wrestling ability, moves, and maneuvers that are going to leave you beaten and broken.
Since I haven't been able to get my hands on Duran, you will make a suitable replacement. You'll make a fine example for Chandler and the chicken. It's time to show those Syndicate floozies that I mean business! If I have to stomp Heidi
Leick into the ground to do it then that's just too bad. I won't shed any tears.
You see, ladies...and Mary, you're looking at the next GDWA Ironwoman and there isn't anything that you three can do to stop me from winning the contest. Some of you might be stronger than me, others might have more technique, and others might be a little more fearless but none of you have the heart, desire, and determination that I do. Winning this thing is the next step in my master plan of making Holly Hotbody a household name. We're talking T-shirts and posters here, girls, so you know I ain't playing!
Besides, isn't it about time that you all had a sexy, beautiful Ironwoman? For goodness sakes, aren't you all tired of seeing ugly cows like Micki Duran holding gold? Don't you finally want a champion that isn't an eyesore? Don't you finally want a woman representing this promotion that looks good enough to grace the cover of any fashion magazine? (smiles) Well, you've found her and she's going to be the next GDWA Ironwoman. Believe it. Plus, the fact that I get a shot at either Chandler or Monet only sweetens the deal and motivates me to go out there and really kick ass! Watch out, girls, because the heat is definitely on. Let's just hope that you don't get burned.
(She slowly makes her way from the pole, landing on her feet again. The camera closes in on her, catching a glimpse of a silver, fox pin attached to her sweater.)
Holly: (eyeing the pole) Often imitated but never duplicated. (smirks at the camera.) Right, Marissa?
(Fade to black.)
