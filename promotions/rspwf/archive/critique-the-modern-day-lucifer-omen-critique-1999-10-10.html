[Critique]-["The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN]-[Critique]

10 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This indeed is quite an average roleplay..But it is the first I have done in along while..Please give me your input..All comments are appreciated..
[Scene] We open up at the Trinity College in San Antonio, Texas. You see many blossoming flowers, great large beautiful green oak trees. And very well kept green grass. The camera then pans over to the right, and we see a soft red track, running in and out throughout the College, all the way around the football field, to the tennis courts ect. We see a very familiar face running down this track, taking the curve around the soccer field, and seems to be coming right at you. The man comes extremly close to you, and suddenly begins to slow down to an easy jog. The man walks very closely to the camera, and smirks, as he reaches down onto a bench, and grabs a white towel. He wipes the sweat off his face with the towel, and throws it over his shoulders, as he leans up against the towel box. He puts his right arm on top of it, and his left arm hanging down by his side...He laughs once, and then looks down, scratching his chin, he looks back up into the camera...
["The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN] ::smirk:: Scotty Lee Xtreme...Well kid, the time has finaly come? You have always said you were better than me..always hinted towards the fact that you would have the edge over me in any classic battle..But now Scotty....can you really back up the words that you have once preached? This is yet to be foreseen..Now you see Scotty, this shit means alot to me..I take this to the extreme..I don't sit back, and blow shit off..fuck nah..I don't go out full speed one night, and half-ass the other..That shit ain't like me..I am full-pressed towards making this shit happen, to my fullest..But you Scotty..I see you slacking off very often..I find myself looking towards you Scotty, and seeing much lack-luster skill..Scotty, I know you have the skill to succeed in almost anything you do..But you see
Scotty..you do not come through with all of your talk..you don't go at it to your fullest..You half-ass it, and look the other cheak. But Scotty...This will be your downfall....You do not have the mental drive, that I, myself have..I know this shit is real..You think this shit is.."Hey, I am fighting a "jobber"
who gives a shit..." Nah Scotty..it ain't like that AT ALL. You see Scotty..you need to realize that, anybody..any one man you decide to go up against, is just as good, just as athletic, just as phenomenal as the next..The only differance between all men, is that some have the mental drive that others..simply don't..Scotty..You used to be a man of great respect..But now in my eyes..I don't see exactly much..I see a frightened child, I see a man who is not exactly sure of all of his decisions..Scotty..You involve yourself in someone's business, and take your business to someone else, just as fast...Scotty, fuck everyone else kid..You can't think of anyone else..ANYONE.. Fuck your wife, fuck your kids..fuck it all....If you want to stand a chance in this world..in this promotion..and against me at the PPV..your going to need to think about what? Wrestling..Only wrestling..only me..and only the Universal title..wait nah..Fuck the universal title..This shit ain't about the universal title..This is about two men, battling it out..to prove a point. We both have talked shit..Both preached that one of us is better than the other..It's just that now..we got to actualy back what the fuck we preach. I know I can back anything
I say..But the fact is..you don't..your not sure..your not sure about ANYTHING.
You want to walk the edge like me Scotty, yet you back away slowly..hoping nobody sees your flaws..your frailtys..But you see Scotty..I see that shit..I know what's going down with you..And well..it don't look good for you right now
Scotty. You see SLX...this isn't going to be your normal beat down..This is going to be two men beating the shit out of each other. I know..for myself..That I won't stop till either I am dead...or you can no longer go on..But Scotty....How are you going to fight against a brick wall? Will you continue to throw yourself at me? Until...at one point you have beaten
YOURSELF, so badly, that you can no longer go on? For..I see your future through the eyes of myself..and your future sports yourself..begging for the mercy of thyself..But you see SLX..that mercy is something in which..I can not give...::smirk:: Mercy is not in my book..I have never showed mercy..and I am not about to do so..You can beg for my mercy..but mercy, you shall not recieve.
Even when your ass is laying on the ground, covered in your own blood..I will smirk..pick your ass back up again, and send it back to the mat...And when your down on the mat again..Once more shall I pick your ass up, and send it down, until you either quit..or are fuckin' dead..And kid, I am serious to hell about this shit....
[Scene] "The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN, chuckles as he walks over to a coke machine, and shuffles through his pockets, finding three quarters, and inserting each into the machine. He selects a button along the right side of the machine..And walks back towards the camera with a bottle of "Aquafina". He now stis down upon the bench, and puts a white towel over his shoulder, and the edge of his head, and looks up into the camera, while taking a drink of his
"Aquafina"...
["The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN] Shotgun Messiah eh? Well kid..I don't know how the fuck you earned your spot here..me and Scotty, fought long, hard battles to reach this position in the X8oD..but you..a walk in title shot? Please...Now
Shotgun, your skills-so to speak, are based on your reputation. You got tons of respect, and think if you sit there on your ass and lose..your respect level will not raise, nor shall it lower. But Messiah..this isn't about your reputation..or gaining respect..This is about raising to the top of the
X8oD..anyone of us can become the leader of the future generation here in the
X8oD..it's just...who fuckin' wants it more...I for one...would love to have the title..I haven't had a fuckin' title since last year, and now..Raising my standards, I have placed myself on the top of the game once more. Messiah, when we step into the ring..your reputation means nothing...your so called "skills"
mean nothing, and your fuckin' respect means nothing. It's who the fuck wants it more..who the fuck has the drive to take this matchup and this promotion to the top. I am going out there this next Sunday..and I am going to show the world that I am no joke..That I can run with whoever the fuck they put in front of me..Whether it's for the title or not..Whether it's against a "jobber" or not..I am dedicated 100%..nah..200% to make it to the top. It's judgement day yall...We got to show the world..That the X8oD wrestling promotion is no joke, and that us three in the main event is no joke. We got to put on a show like no other..We got to beat the living shit out of each other..This isn't fuckin' old school, left hook, right hook, suplex..Fuck nah...This is 450moonsault through fuckin' tables, ddt's through fuckin' chairs...this is the real fuckin' deal yall..We ain't goin' to mess around..We going to beat the living shit out of each other. I know for a fact, that I would never submit..I would never quit..Never giveup..That's just not my way of fighting..I never give in..NEVER.
The only time I would indeed stop..is when..well..my body can not go on anymore..my heart, and soul wish to continue..but my body is so badly bruised...so badly cut..so badly destroyed that I can not continue, yet I wish to..but the inevitable force of my body, holds me back from taking action...
[Scene] "The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN stretches out on the bench, and smirks as he tosses the "Aquafina" bottle over his right shoulder, and into the trash can. He stretches out across the bench, and then leans farther back on the bench..
["The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN] The Universal title eh? The most recognized title in sports entertainment. Well..it's going to come down to three men..fighting it out..giving their all..But only one man may succeed, and in fact do become the Universal champion. For some reason..I feel that this man, is none other than myself..You see..I want it that much more than you two..I want to feel the gold around my waist..that much more than you two indeed do.
You see..I pack more punch then a double cappuccino, and that's exactly what it will take to wake each of your asses up, after I am threw wit' ya. There is not one single soul across the entire globe who can duplicate the show I'm capable of putting on. Ive got it all, the personality, the ability, the heart, the charisma, the skill... and what my friend do you two have huh? Certainly not personality Scotty, cause we have already uncovered the fact that you have the mind of an adolescent child. Nor do you have the ability or else you would have held a major title little man.
[Scene] "The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN, smirks as he stands up, and places the towel, and other belongings into a black "X8oD Entertainment" bag. He then proceeds to walk towards the parking lot, talking as he continues to walk..
["The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN] We got a long hard road until this pay-per view...I for one, am going to work my ass off, until I am in top notch shape for the matchup..Until I KNOW, that if I go into that ring next Sunday, I can stick it up, with either one of you..whether it seems you guys have worked out as well..I have worked out _MORE_. I just want it that much..I just need it that much..I am not exactly sure about you two's dedication...you two's drives..But what I know, is a simple fact....Come next Sunday, you two will be on a devistation course with "The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN....Shotgun
Messiah..your old school bro..You ain't got the determination anymore..You no longer have the drive to succeed.. Scotty Lee Xtreme..You want to drink from the ecstasy cup...Yet you won't abide..This shall be your downfall..Prepare for the worst..
[Scene] We slowly fade to black as we see "The Modern Day Lucifer" OmeN reach his 99' Jeep Grand Cherokee, toss his gym bag in the backseat, and drive off.....
