Subject - In the Screening Room part 1 - A look at the Champs... [SCENE:The camera opens up to what appears to be a lecture hall. The camera mvoes around as we see a screen set up and a projector at the back of the room.
A moment later the doors open and peoples begin to swarm into the room and sit down in various seats. SOme talking, sitting on the desk instead of the chair.
The camera remains on them as we hear the sound of a door open and close. A few seconds later the entire class cringes as the sound of nails dragging across a chalkboard is heard .The camera quickly swings around to the front of the room as we see James Pillow standing there. . .]
[APPEARENCE:James has on a pair of black khaki slacks, a navy blue buttone up shirt and a black blazer. He also has on a pair of gucchi loafers, and a pair of glasses in his hand with round eye pieces. He grins and turns facing the class. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - You're regular classroom schedual will not be in effect today as I will bring you all a very special presentation. .it is a two parter, today is part one. . .And I will look at and review the champions of the UEW one by one. . .
[James places the glasses on his nose and picks up a clicker and a laser pointer. . .] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - Now someone get the lights. <Lights go out> Alright, let's get to it. .and take notes because tihs will all be on the test.
<chuckles> [James pushes the clicker once and a picture comes onto the screen, that of the
Ultimate Tag Champions, The Perfectly Perfect Duo. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - The Perfectly Perfect Duo. .probably the most well knwo, and the most succesful tag team in the history of this sport. With an old friend of mine Sensuous Samantha at there side, they have won gold where ever they have gone. . .what do they have to do with me here in the UEW? Think about it for a second <pauses> Ok, times up. The answer, Absolutely NOTHING! So let's just move on because tag teams do not intrest me. .
[James clicks the button and POwer and Honor appear but he quickly pushes it again and they disappear off and are replaced by Liam Rogan. . ]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - As I stated, Tag teams hold no intrest for me so I am not even going to bother with the second rate tag champions. . .now, we come to the new UEW Televison Champion, Liam Rogan. The virtual living freak of the wrestling world. Rogan captured the title at the last Pay-Per-View. . .he walks around with a group of people that make even Rogan look normal. As far as ring skills is concerned he has it, as he has shown. .he can win matches but can he beat this man?
[James clicks the button and a picture of himself appears on it.] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - Highly unlikely. . . as this man here possess pure athletic ability that can not be comapred to be any others. So what will happen if Rogan puts his Television title on the line against him?
[James clicks the button again and James picture disappears and is replaced by the words "NEW CHAMPION!"]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - That is correct, a new champion would be crowned. . .and the Freak Show wo't be able to stop it. Next on the list. . [James pushes the button again and the words disappear and a picture of Alex
Martinez appears. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - The American Champin, Alex Martinez. .yes, he as well won his title at the last UEW Pay-Per-View. Martinez has a pretty solid record thus far in the UEW. . .he proved that he has what it takes to wear the gold when he became the new American Champion. _BUT_ the question is, when the belt is palced on the line agaisnt a certain man. .against. .
[James clicks the button and once again his picture comes on the screen. .] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - _THIS_ man, will he walk out still the UEW American
Champion. .or will he walk out as a chump. That is the question, which would it be? Champ. .or Chump? Let's find out. .
[James presses the button again and the word "CHUMP!" appears in palce of the picture. .] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - That says it all right there. .Alex Martinez would walk out as the chump, and once again. .
[James clicks the button yet again and the words "NEW CHAMP!" come onto the screen. .] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - A new champion would be crowned. . .and that brings us near the end of today's lession and the last man on the list. .
[James presses the button and Rick Styles' picture comes onto the screen as he holds the Ultimate title. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - "Top Dog" Rick Styles. . .The UEW Ultimate Champion. The _TOP DOG_ <pauses> And yes, the pun was very much intended. <James picks up a glass from the desk and takes a sip.> How many times Ultimate Champion? I dun lost count. . .but it doesn't matter in the least bit becuase when the day comes the Mr. Styles steps in the ring with this man. .
[James clicks the button and. .yep, you guessed it, his picture comes onto the screen again. .]
"ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - The Top Dog will go home with his tail in between his legs, and no gold around his waist as yes. .you guessed it. .
[James clicks the button and the words "NEW CHAMP!" come onto the screen. .] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - A new champion will be crowned. . . [The bell rings. .] "ES T BEE" JAMES PILLOW - Well that concludes today's lesson. Tonight study one
Butch Morrison. .and the participants in the 30 Man fight to the finish for a shot at the UEW Ultimate title. .
[The class all gets up and begins to file out of the room as James sits on the desk with an apple and a knife in hand. He slices a piece off and puts it in his mouth. He then picks the clicker back up and presses the button, the camera moves onto the screen as it reads
SIMPLY. . . THE. . . BEST!!!!! The camera zooms in on it and slowly. . .] [Fades To Black. . .]
