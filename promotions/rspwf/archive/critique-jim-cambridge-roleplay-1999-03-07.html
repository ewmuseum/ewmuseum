(Critique) Jim Cambridge (Roleplay)

7 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
OK Guys, this is my VERY FIRST roleplay that I ever wrote, so not too harsh eh? My Subject: The day before a big match vs my arch rival, "Crazy" Lou Comacho. This roleplay is a little out of character because he is my arch rival and it has built up to pure utter hatred.
:::: The scene opens with a 50's style B-movie being shown on a movie theatre. The screen shows a giant rat blowing fire on buildings and in general tearing up a city. The camera switches and we see Jim "The Clan"
Cambridge sitting in the movie theatre. The lights are dim and he is eating popcorn. He takes his eyes off the screen and looks at the camera. Wiping his mouth he begins to whisper ::::
Jim: "Revenge of the Lab Rat".... A damn good flick. Lots of people have always put down these type of films, calling them "cheap" or "cheesy". Not me though, uhh uh. You could learn alot from these type films, they always have a good moral. Great morals actually, basic stuff that a lot of people in the industry seemed to have missed out on. "Crazy" Lou, you should take a break from getting fatter one day and pop in "Revenge of the Lab Rat". Learn some of the basics of wrestling.
::::::::Jim pauses to watch the film for a few seconds:::::::::: Jim: The first thing you can learn from these films is to not f$*k around with things you don't understand. All the classic monsters were the results of people f$*king around with things they didnt understand, nuclear bomb testing, a mission to space, or in the case of the lab rat, an experimental medical test. The same goes for me Lou, I wasn't always the monster you see right now, you MADE me Lou.. (Jim points at the camera) you MOLDED me together, peice by peice. You f$*ked around with something you didn't UNDERSTAND and as a result "Crazy" Lou, you created a monster, you created ME.
:::: Jim stops whispering and focuses on the movie and lets out a smile. Jim turns back to the Camera and begins again ::::
Jim: But the thing that gets me about these B-movies are the people in the city always running from the monster, running for their lives. You might think that there stupid Lou, but actually there smarter than you. You know why? Because they have their basic instinct for survival. They have the common sense to run from something they can't beat, something they can't defend themselves from. Compare me to that unstoppable force, "Crazy" Lou
Comacho, I AM that force. Learn to do that Lou, learn to run. Its not too late, Lou, not yet. Let your survival instincts kick in, do what you know is the right thing to do and run, RUN.......for your life.
::: Fade scenes with Jim giving a cruel menacing smile ::::
