[Critique] Hidden Essence [Flash]

3 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Subj: Hidden Essence *1* Date: 99-02-25 15:08:47 EST From: [detail withheld] To: [detail withheld] [ Scene starts in the corner of some market in Manhattan called "Bodega." Hidden Essence sits down in one of the stair ways talking with a latino next to him. ] HIDDEN ESSENCE: I am telling you things in ICW are very very.. strange! LATINO GUY: Yeah I know what you mean, man! I mean every Friday I watch and all I see is ICW give air time to the SUPREME DREAM TEAM! I mean those guys suck bad, man! Everytime I see them come out I go looking for my taco shells!
HIDDEN ESSENCE: [ Laughing ] You crack me up! I would say thay suck? Actually... Si! I do say that! LATINO GUY: Its true man those guys are a bunch of xolo's! HIDDEN ESSENCE: Whats a xolo? LATINO GUY: You don't know what a xolo is ,man? A xolo is just a big chihuaha, hairless and they look stupid!
HIDDEN ESSENCE: Ugh.. if you say so! LATINO GUY: But enough about that ,man! What about this mathc you have with THE HAMMER? HIDDEN ESSENCE: What is there to say, I got a match with THE HAMMER. I mean ofcourse I am going to win but why should I waste my breath on that moron?
LATINO GUY: Calmate brother! I was just saying! I was watching last Tuesday
Rockin Da World Tour.. and that moron came on and gave an hour long speech about the match! I really wasn't paying attention cause I was concentrating on
Pepe, Jose, Maria, Jesus, Roberto ,Mario y Albertico...
HIDDEN ESSENCE: Ugh, who are those people? LATINO GUY: My kids,man! What you think! Did you know that back in Mexico I use to seaman? But now I just put it all over my wife!!! [ Laughs ]
HIDDEN ESSENCE: [ Laughing ] Your a pretty funny spick! You don't meet funny spicks nowadays... usually just look funny you are just funny!
LATINO GUY: Is that an insult? HIDDEN ESSENCE: No thats just your green card! LATINO GUY: Whatever xolo! But look enough of that man! I like you skills in the ring you bring like some luchalibre into ICW.. you respresent la raza!
HIDDEN ESSENCE: La raza? I don't represent no spicks.. I just respresent an
"ass beating" to everyone who gets in why way! See that Alex Cote guy he is opening his mouth and talking the shit.. but its going to be time for him to back it up this Friday! Can he back up his big damn mouth? Maybe he has against less competitiors but he has never been in the ring with Hidden Essence and once Friday is over he will never want to face Hidden Essence again!
LATINO GUY: I don't ese! I mean Alex Cote is good in the ring to I mena he beaten some of the big ICW superstars! He been in D.o.V. and he looks like a pretty tough guy, so I don't know if you can ese!
HIDDEN ESSENCE: See what your little mexican intellect you can't see who's the better man? Yeah he has a better record than mine.. but every man I beaten has never wanted to face me again cause they know I was the greatest wrestler without a shadow of a doubt that they ever squared against!
LATINO GUY: Stop making fun of the raza.. yo that ain't cool! HIDDEN ESSENCE: Why don't mexicans like to barbeque? LATINO GUY: What man? Why? HIDDEN ESSENCE: The beans are always falling through the grill!!! LATINO GUY: That ain't funny man stop lowering la raza! HIDDEN ESSENCE: Shadap you and your raza! [ Suddenly some guy passing by with a boom box and he is jamming to "Hammertime" by M.C. Hammer as then Hidden Essence and the Latino Guy laugh the guy holding the boom box looks over and just shrugs and keeps walking. ]
HIDDEN ESSENCE: Yeah theres a guy that Alex should admired.. M.C. Hammer.. both of them will be right in the bottom once I am done with Cote this Friday! Believe me Cote.... he will feel hammered.. he will feel like he has never felt ever in his life cause he will feel what being in the ring with Hidden Essence is about.. and once he gets that loss... that loss he will know came to me and forever he will wnat to have vegeance over that loss.
LATINO GUY: How can you be calling it a win now you don't know if he will beat you on Fallout? I mean he has beaten Bugg before something you haven't done yet!
HIDDEN ESSENCE: Maybe you didn't tune into the Tuesday RDWT where it was a four way match between me, Grayson, Madison and Cote! Where I pinned Madison and I took the win on that night!
LATINO GUY: But your not wearing the ICW International Title.. so that win means nothing! HIDDEN ESSENCE: It means more than what you think... I am not going to get into that whole "Screwed up Boss Thing" but... either way I broke the winning streak of a certain individual by the name of, Chrisitan "The Spotlight"
Madison! Now I am going to break the same dreams of Cote... thinking he can be the International Champion all that Cote will be in my eyes is an international chump.. when it comes to HIDDEN ESSENCE all that COTE will be is second best cause I am the one friving the number one spot here in the ICW!
LATINO GUY: Ese I just wish you luck! HIDDEN ESSENCE: Luck? Nah.. I don't need luck that bastard I am facing is going to need all the luck he can get cause I am walking out with another "W"
in my record after Friday whether anyone in the ICW likes it or not I am leaving Friday Fallout with a win against Alex Cote... and even Cote himself knows he...
..... FEARS ..... ..... THE ..... ..... 1-2-3!!!!!!!! ..... HIDDEN ESSENCE: No one in the ICW can stop me.. much less some half twit punk by the name of Alex Cote!
LATINO GUY: Would you happen to know ese were I could buy some good black beans? HIDDEN ESSENCE: Do I look like a spick to you? No! [ Walks off as he then grabs the guys face and pushes off as he leaves laughing. ]
[ Screen fades to black. ] [name withheld] Handler of "Hidden Essence" [ICW] Handler of The Movie Guy [JPW]
