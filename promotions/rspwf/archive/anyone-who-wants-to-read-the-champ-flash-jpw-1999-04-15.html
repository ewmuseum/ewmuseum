[[[Anyone Who Wants To Read]]] The Champ: Flash: JPW

15 April 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Keep in mind, this is my first roleplay ever with this character, so if it sucks, bera with me, it'll get much, much better.
----------------------------------------------------------- [[Camera fades in.]] [[SCENE - A mall food court is shown. The mall is packed with tons of shoppers, as is the food court. Barely any seats in the giant court are open.
Restaurants such as McDonald's, Taco Bell, and a few one location stores fill the court. The camera starts closing in on the food court and one particular restaurant. It is called "Hercules Gyro." A man is shown sitting at a two person table eating a gyro. It is none other than JPW's The Champ. He is wearing a pair of baggy Ralph Lauren Carpenter shorts and a white polo sport t-shirt. He is enjoying his gyro as he notices the camera closing in on him.
He drops his food down to his plate and immediately wipes his face clean with a napkin. As the camera approaches him, he glances at the camera, pretending he never noticed it coming and gives his best "GQ" expression.]]
THE CHAMP - I see The Champ has a fan club. And by the looks of it, it's a fat guy holding a camera. What the hell do you want?
[[The Champ gets no response from the camera man.]] THE CHAMP - Well, since somebody can't seem to get up the courage to speak in the presence of The Champ, you better just turn your little ass around so The
Champ can read your jacket. [[The camera spins around momentarily and then faces The Champ again.]] THE CHAMP - JPW huh? So you came to get a few words from The Champ about R.A.W. did ya? Well, lucky for you, The Champ is willing to give you those words. Let The Champ just start off talking a little trash about the superstars of the JPW. First of all we have The Movie Guy. The Champ wants to know exactly what the hell that is supposed to mean. Oooh, The Champ watches a movie now and then, could he be called The Movie Guy? Hell no! Because The
Champ thinks that is the stupidest gimmick that he's ever heard of. Then there are superstars like James "Simply the Best" Pillow... The Champ has nothing but respect for him. How many guys have wrestled in every single federation there is and have been half way successful? That's why The Champ likes Pillow. Or, we have guys like Westside Walavan, who can kiss The Champ's ass. Walavan is uses whoever he can use to get to the top of the JPW because there is no chance in hell, his fat ass can make it there on his own! And then we've got guys like Jesus Christ. JESUS CHRIST!!! That is about half sac-religious. The
Champ believes that Jesus Christ is the biggest, ugliest piece of shit walking the JPW locker room today. And you never know when guys like Triple X will join the JPW for a week and pack their bags. Personally, The Champ sees nobody around here who can defeat The Champ.
[[The Champ pulls out a pair of Oakley's and places them over his eyes.]] THE CHAMP - The Champ is getting one hell of a glare from the skylights here and he's not liking it. Anyway, where was The Champ? Oh yeah... the JPW talent. Enough about that cuz The Champ is getting bored with it. Now let's focus on Monday Night R.A.W. The Champ makes his JPW debut, going up against
"The Golden Child" John Midas. Well, as you can see, Johnny, I've been doing a little research. Midas was the king in Greek mythology who had the "golden touch," hence Johnny calls himself "The Golden Child." Pretty creative there
Johnny but I'll tell ya what The Champ thinks. The Champ thinks you should go buy a little foam belt from a toy store, turn the thing to gold, and hand it to
The Champ, because that's what The Champ is after! Kicking the hell out of people like John Midas means nothing to The Champ. If he wanted to do that, he'd just take his ass out to some bar, pick out the fattest and drunkest of the attendants at the bar, beat his ass, and toss him out on the street. So obviously, The Champ has not a care in the world for what happens to John MIdas on the nineteenth. Now get outta The Champ's face before his food gets cold!
[[The camera starts to fade back as The Champ goes back to eating his gyro.]] [[Fade to black.]] [name withheld] Handler of The Champ & "The Incomparable" Dylan Rapture [Free Agent] JPW: 0-0-0 N/A Lifetime: 0-0-0 10-0-1 "I do all of this, and get drunk a couple times a week... wow, I should teach classes on how to budget free time." - [name withheld].
