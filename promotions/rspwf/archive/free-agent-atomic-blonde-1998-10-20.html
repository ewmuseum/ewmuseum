[Free Agent] Atomic Blonde

20 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Shown is footage of an interview taped immediatly following the Atomic Blonde's December 24th match with Billy Reel at `Christmas Eve of Destruction`...] <The camera zooms in tight around Blonde's head and shoulders, as he sits in the locker room with his head hung low, staring at the floor tiles. A wet arena towel is draped over his head, drenched with blood and sweat from his severly damaged skull. With his head aimed low, he slowly begins to speak...>
"Billy Reel... impressive. Very impressive..." <Blonde pauses> "... wasn't I?" <Blonde raises one hand and slowly pulls the blood stained towel from his head revealing the bruised and battered face of a beaten man. His eyes swollen shut... circled with purple and black rings. Hardened blood is plastered across his face. His lip and forehead busted open. He winces in pain and spits blood onto the floor before continuing...>
"Tonight, Billy Reel, I fed you a mouth full of canvass and an ass full of turnbuckle. But looking at the two of us, you'd never know that you were the one at home celebrating a victory while I was the one on my way to a hostipal bed to have rectal thermometers shoved up every orfice of my body. Sounds tastey. You left the arena a little worse for wear. Big f<censored>ing deal. I was a headshot away from leaving in a body bag. You're walking out of here of your own free will, but I couldn't care less. I got my paycheck, I got my airtime, and I got to see your blood flow... that's all the matters. You left that arena with a win in the record book & a raised sense of self esteem... but you also left with a steel toed boot permanently lodged up your ass. It kinda balances itself out. Pretty f<censored>ing cool, eh?"
<As Blonde grins, blood trickles down the sides of his mouth & drips from his chin. He brings the towel to his face, wipping the blood and sweat from his mouth and forehead...>
"You made me bleed. 80 percent of this is your blood, but nonetheless, you made me bleed. All my life, I've bleed for other people. But tonight, regardless out the outcome, I made you bleed just a little more. The entire organization wanted to see me cough up blood.. and this I have done. All my life I've bled for other people. Well, bastards... I'm not gonna bleed for you anymore."
<Blonde begins upwrapping the athletic tape from his wrists while continuing...> "Now, just as I kick on piece of trash to the curb, it's time to deal with another. Legacy... even before the battle royal began, you were blaming your predicted horrible performance on me. I told everyone my intentions coming in.
I have no desire to win a second rate title. I guess you didn't make it clear that your intentions were to get your ass handed to you on a silver platter.
For future reference, watch who you direct your comments to, because next time, you'll be silenced with more than a verbal attack. Reel, Legacy, NBW... just get off my balls."
<With that, he tosses the towel over the camera lens as the scene fades out...>
