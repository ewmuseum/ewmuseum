To All Card Writers, and Vice Prezes....

27 October 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Are you one of those people that have been scrummaging around looking for a fed with actual competition and GOOD Cards??? Well, Let me introduce you to what will become the next IIWF... Wait a sec, I`m not going to Say that.. more like an A Better IEUW (I`ll explain later) But Neither feds matter because will soon become something better then those... If you think you`re intrested, read on. There isn`t any Wrestler Application or anything like that because this isn`t one of those kind of things. I`m a e-wrestler that has been wrestling for about two years. I`ve been a World Tag-Champ on three occasions, a North American and Intercontinetal Champ on four occasions and a two time TV Champ. I Fell I`m a very expeirienced Wrestler. When I first decided I Felt experienced I decided to run a fed. BIG MISTAKE. Two reasons, I had no staff whatsoever and It was Right after new Years Eve which meant school`s back. I dealt out two great cards, one featuered a desk top Brawl between two wrestlers that had a huge fight that led them up to the Executive offices of the arena the card before. Then My next Attempt was EMWA, A Real wrestler fed. This was a great fed that lasted awhile. One problem No staff and too many members. I did Have a VP However that helped all he could but we still couldn`t pull it off with two people considering how good the fed was and how much talent was in there. After that I went to a federation Called IEUW. In my opinion IEUW WAS THE GREATEST... BAR NONE. IIWF would never compare to what IEUW Had to offer. For all of those internet people out there, I`d like to inform you that there is VERY many Feds that are Strictly based on AOL members only so you guys may not have heard of IEUW And the stuff it had. Besides, It never posted on RSPWF. So here`s my idea... I`m going to start something here. I want to bring the style, savvy and uniqueness of AOL Feds on to The Internet into Something that will form the standard for E-Wrestling Federations WORLD WIDE. Sure, it`s a hard thing to say but I truly think I can do it. But not by myself of course. So here`s my offer to all of you out there. All of you card writers who sit in the back and do everything your prez says, to you VPs there who watch the prez take all of the credit for something you`ve thought of, or just to bored Card writers or VPs out there, OR EVEN Prezes themselves... I`m offering you a chance of greatness a chance to be part of something that will truly change e-wrestling. If you think you want to do it while having fun at the same time, E-mail me... Thanks Alot for reading this and taking the time.
