1 Post, so I don't want ANY RECRUIT letters as I just don't have the time for any more federations. Peace and a Prayer ~Joel "The Most Called Upon
Match Writer in the history of e-wrestling" [name withheld] CROW Prez http://www.crowfed.faithweb.com/index.html IWE VP #13 ASCII Designer of 98 Handler of.. Lord of Darkness [UIWF]0-0-0 [IWE]4-3-0 [UCWP]3-1-0 The Torturer [MLW]4-1-0{IIWA/MLW Extreme Champ} Jessie Muldoon[UEW]3-4-0-1-0{-2pts) God's Justice [IWE]4-1-0{Former IWE Tag Team Champs} [UIWF]0-1-0 [MIW]0-0-0 [TWL]0-0-0{NEW} "The Bard" Johnathan Shakespeare [GLCW]{NEW} ============================ [Fades out as the soft harmonious notes of "Ode to Joy" are softly played in the distance. The cameras then begin to fade in just a little as "The Bard" Johnathan Shakespeare and Lady Jane McDuff are seen seated around an old piano. Johnathan Shakespeare is wearing a pair of silver tights, has short grey hair and is of around age 20. He has clear blue eyes and has no facial disfigurements about him. Johnathan Shakespeare then turns towards the camera and then begins to speak.]
JS: "O that which has behooved me now. The call of the times that doth lead me through the rickety roads and the thorny paths. O what of that
O kindred soul? Shall I dwell here within or shall it be the end? O that it not be. For I shall not despair. My conscience is like a thousand tongues and each one brings in a several tale. Likewise, so shall the tale be told as I greet them that the Bard must upstage."
[Johnathan Shakespeare then turns towards Lady Jane McDuff, who isn't of sound mind. Lady Jane McDuff can be seen wearing a pair of tight red trousers, she also has on a tight top, which is tied at the sides. Lady
Jane McDuff then just begins to look at Johnathan, then begins to speak.] LJM: "O Johnathan, it is a rather dangerous and wicked world out there, O how are we going to get past the terrors and turmoils of our previous walks of life. It shall come back, I know it shall. It shall keep on haunting us for the rst of our lives. What can we do to prevent it?"
[Lady Jane McDuff then just sighs as Johnathan Shakespeare just looks up with a rather prudent look towards him.]
JS: "There it be. A place, a city on the hill it dwelleth. Dwelleth like we shall dwell here. For the ancient shall be overcome by the past, Yea we might be living from the past, the future of this place is our future and thus it shall be done. The GLCW, like the waters which flow endlessly, we shall flow forth like the flowers which blossom from the trees far and wide. Yet, those who come before us,
O what of that? [Pauses to read a few points.] The supernatural, the evil, the misguided nature, there can be only. That one be Caliban. From the times, you also dwell like us within the past. Caliban, evil ruptures of your mind and heart. Will it lead you on your way or will you tremour at the conseqeunces? O Caliban, 'twill be the dawning when the sun doth arise in these parts. It shall be our dawning, for this is now the place where we dwell. The future shall be overcome by the past and it shall prevaileth like the prevailing winds and sands of time.."
["The Bard" Johnathan Shakespeare then just pours into a goblet, a minor amount of red fermented wine. He then brings the cup towards his lips and then with a sigh, just throws it to the ground.]
JS: "The vapour which poureth over like an overflowing river, yet it shall not open its ways into my ways. Yet then we come as we draw further towards them. It be there, Kurt Orr. O what of this? Naught
I know of about him. Yet 'twill the way to follow. Kurt Orr, don't forget the past for it can always come back to haunt you. 'twas the hour, 'twill our hour now. For soon the GLCW shall be shown the dramatics of ones inner mind and as what 'twill be 'twill be for men make dreams, but dreams maketh men
As the yellow leaf falls from the tree, so shall old age dwell no more. And that which doth accompany age, as honour, love, obedience, freinds, I shalt not look to have. I have naught but myself and that is the way. For my high intelligence, personal bravery and love for my wife is all I need to get the job done. And I gurantee that whoever tries to show themselves, will be by mine own bloodstained hands, be shown up for being nothing but folly. All shall be folly in the land of the court jester. Just as Feste was in Twelth Night, I shall as I make my humble entrance be felt, learn to know and understand the minor things of that which shall be done, for the Bard hath spoken.."
[Fades out as "The Bard" Johnathan Shakespeare then just curls up around the knees of Lady Jane McDuff.]
