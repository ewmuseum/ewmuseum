Nemesis Height: 6' 6" Weight: 265 lbs Hometown: Parts Unknown Alignment: Heel Theme Music: "Closer" by Nine Inch Nails ========= Personality: ========= Cold and calculating, Nemesis is one with the Dark Side. Nemesis often uses what he terms as his "superior intellect" to lure his opponents into situations that suit his advantage, from which he then attacks without mercy. Firm in his belief that his skills are second to none, Nemesis'
arrogance is endless. His mental stability is sometimes questionable, as his behavior can be quite bizarre at times, characterized by erratic mood swings. He often acts on impulse, with little regard for the consequences of his words or actions, as he is totally dedicated to putting on a grand performance, despite his proclaimed disregard for the fans. Win or lose, it doesn't matter so much as seeing his enemies carried off in stretchers.
This reputation, along with his penchant for anarchy and chaos, has earned
Nemesis his status as one of the most hated villains in the sport.
====== History: ====== Mystery abounds the man known as Nemesis. Rumors persist that he has been trained since birth in various fighting styles and has since traveled the globe perfecting these skills. The most visible portions outside of the US where Nemesis was seen were Japan and Europe. Eventually, these travels brought him to the States in the Spring of 1998, where his wrestling career officially begins. Joining along with wrestling mainstays such as
Executioner, CJ Swift and Hellbent, the Brotherhood was formed. After causing much chaos and devastating many an opponent across the country, Nemesis brought the Brotherhood to the Gyeah Wrestling (GW), to do the same, which they did, and quite well one might say! It didn't take long for Nemesis to make an impact in the GW. After entering the 32 man tournament for GW's top prize, Nemesis became the first GW Heavyweight
Champion, and if you ask Nemesis, it was fair and square, despite what anyone else may say. Along with the much hated Brotherhood, a reign of terror was instigated within the GW, one such that the sport of wrestling had never ever seen. Not hesitating the least, many fell victim to the brutal and savage attacks as the Brotherhood, after recruiting the
Nightmare, Aron Rave, assured themselves a position on top of the wrestling world. Some of the more memorable events involving Nemesis during his tenure at
Gyeah Wrestling was the trickery of his long time rival, Blue Moose, by one time friend and Brotherhood stalwart, CJ Swift at the Art of War. Another key moment transpired at the GW December PPV, Season's Beatings, as Nemesis defeated his most hated enemy, Damien King, in typical Brotherhood style, as
Kyle Blaque shocked the world by joining the Brotherhood and making life for
Damien King a living hell. Nemesis and former Brotherhood founder,
Executioner, also crossed paths at Ground Zero, both men proving to the world their insanity by participating in the first ever Carmageddon match, which saw Nemesis the victor, in perhaps the most extreme match ever witnessed by wrestling fans. Only months passed before Nemesis would reclaim what he long proclaimed was rightfully his, the GW World Heavyweight Title, as Nemesis would become the first repeat World Champion in GW history, defeating Psycho Soup with assistance from Jason Hawke, a man who would later join the ranks of the Brotherhood as well. And it was at Scar Wars that Nemesis, for the first time, unmasked before the entire world, and making an example out of Aphex, who would later be revealed to be his
half-brother. As it is now, returning to the sport that made him infamous, Nemesis is ready to step back into the spotlight of professional wrestling. The one constant in all this? One never knows when they will be the next in the long list of victims of Nemesis!
========== Appearance: ========== Outside of the ring, Nemesis can be seen dressed in a wardrobe reminiscent of a renegade rock star. Preferring black leather pants and designer shirts that sometimes can be quite flashy. He will often also be seen with a black leather jacket, as he tends to never leave home without it. Around his neck will be countless necklaces with arcane symbols, while his footwear, in and out of the ring, most times consist of Doc Marten boots that clearly show signs of use. Nemesis' in-ring attire is a sleeveless, black body suit, which has a red stripe running down the legs and shoulders with a red strap around the waist. His face, once covered by a mask that had become synonymous with terror, now is view to the open public.
Nemesis' physical attributes are handsome features as well as a hidden 'dark charisma', of sorts, one often sees Nemesis' trademark expression etched on his lips, that of a slight smirk, perhaps a sign of the man's endless arrogance and his chaotic nature. He has long black hair, which he sometimes ties back or in a topknot...sometimes braided at the tips depending on his mood. Other facial consists of long, angular sideburns as well as letting a petit goatee grow out from time to time. His build is rather muscular, most likely resulting from his near obsessive tendencies for perfection, leaving
Nemesis as not the kind of guy you want to encounter in a dark alley.
================= =================== Primary Wrestling Skill: Secondary Wrestling Skill: ================= =================== Mat Technician Martial Artist ============= Ring Psychology: ============= Although Nemesis claims to hate the fans, he takes great pride in impressing them with his wide array of wrestling wizardry. Often times after hitting a move he thinks is impressive, Nemesis will pause to let the fool fans know this, either by showing disrespect for his opponent or staring out into the crowd. He has a short temper with the officials, not agreeing with anything that doesn't go his way. Nemesis will also times resort to stall tactics, in an effort to distract his opponents into his advantage. This may either be by collecting his thoughts on the outside frequently, pretending to lockup only to break away at the last moment, or indulging into that test of strength trap that everyone always seems to fall for. Clearly, Nemesis is not a man that you would want to turn your back to. Almost will always let his opponents get the early advantage, so as to come back and squelch their fighting spirit with a counterattack. His martial arts background gives his punches added punishment as Nemesis clearly knows where to hit for maximum pain. A victorious Nemesis will often kneel down to one knee and crossing his forearms above his head towards the crowd, while in defeat, he will limp away exaggerating his injuries, or seek a revenge sneak attack while his opponent celebrates.
============= Wrestling Moves: ============= -------------------------- Finisher: The Chaos Effect -------------------------- Description: An elevated double underhook piledriver. Nemesis places the victim into a standing headscissors position, while hooking both of the victim's arms, as well. Then, Nemesis lifts the victim up into the air, to where the victim's head is at Nemesis' waist, where Nemesis then, in one fluid motion, abruptly drops down to a sitting position, driving the victim's head into the canvas and causing severe spinal trauma in the process.
----------------- Signature Moves: ----------------- The Spinal Tap - Reverse Shoulder Neckbreaker Gourdbuster Suplex - Front Suplex Guillotine Face Driver (from the ropes) - The victim is bent over near the corner of the ring, as Nemesis, takes to the ropes, placing one leg over the back of the victim's neck and jumps up, using his weight to drive the victim face first into the mat.
Standing Jumpkick to the Chest - Nemesis sends the victim into the ropes, where Nemesis then levels the victim with a precise kick to the chest.
Spinning Vertical Suplex - Standard suplex where Nemesis powers the victim into the air, holding him there momentarily before Nemesis adds his twist, so to speak, by spinning around before dropping the victim to the canvas.
-------------- Power Moves: -------------- Samoan Drop (from the second rope) Cradle Neckbreaker Face First Powerbomb Belly to Belly Overhead Suplex Belly to Back Brainbuster ------------------ Secondary Moves: ------------------ Japanese Leg Roll Pin Rolling Body Scissors Cradle Pin Thumb to Throat Chokehold Throat Strike European Uppercut Heart Punch Rake to the Eyes Jawbreaker Octopus Stretch Hold Painkiller Armbar Chinlock Shortarm Clothesline Palm Thrust to the Chest ----------------------------------- [A slight fadeout ends when the scene of a typical interview setting opens up. Two chairs are set up in the room, facing slightly towards each other while in the middle, a small table providing separation. However, our attention firmly lies in the two men occupying the chairs now, for GW's announcing stalwart, Marc Osborne sits in the chair to the left while renowned sociopath and former GW Heavyweight Champion, Nemesis, slouches in the chair to the right. Disregarding the obvious No Smoking signs in the background, Nemesis surrounds himself in a vapor of cancerous smoke from a cigarette while Marc Osborne kicks off the interview...]
Osborne: Hello GW fans, I'm excited by this opportunity here to present you one of the more unique personalities in GW, as well as one that seems to bask in controversy every chance he gets. I, of course, refer to none other than Nemesis, the former mastermind of the Brotherhood. First of all, thanks for sharing your time...
Nemesis: ....... [Nemesis refuses to even acknowledge Marc Osborne with a response, instead exhaling a stream of smoke through his nostrils and into the face of
Osborne, who hesitates for a moment...] Osborne: Ah...where to start? With Sunday's Battle Bowl PPV now in the books, we saw a return to form, so to speak, from yourself. While perhaps not as successful as you might have intended, you still managed quite an impressive run, lasting well over 40 minutes before being eliminated by
Psy -
[Osborne is suddenly interrupted by Nemesis, obviously agitated by a few of
Osborne's choice words...]
Nemesis: First of all, whether or not you considered my showing this past
Sunday a success or not, I really don't give a damn. I've never given much credence to what you or what anyone else has to think, so why should I start now? As for Battle Bowl, I fully expected to repeat my performance from the very first Battle Bowl, which we all know, I won.
Osborne: That is right, as you have achieved some of the more prestigious landmarks here in GW history. You were the very first crowned Heavyweight
Champion and the winner of the first Battle Bowl. However, with the elimination this past Sunday at Battle Bow at the hands of Psycho Soup, do you feel that you are past your prime? Afterall, we haven't seen you wrestle in more than a year...
Nemesis: *Laughs* You obviously didn't pay much attention to Battle Bowl, yes? I'm quite sure that Damien King himself will convince you otherwise.
Osborne: Now that you mention Damien King, it's obvious the hatred you two share for each other hasn't healed over time. But after the events at
Battle Bowl, which saw you and the Innovation Sensation, Jason Hawke, attempt to hang King, one must have to question on whether you went too far.
[Nemesis lifts his chin up a bit, motioning to a noticeable scar located on his throat...] Nemesis: You see this? I received this awhile back at a match you might even remember, Oz?
Osborne: The Scaffold Match at Self Destruct with Damien King versus yourself, back in 1999? How could I forget...many fans regard it as one of
GW's finest matches of all time.
Nemesis: *smirks* In regards to whether Hawke and I went too far in disposing of the walking deadman, Damien King, I'll just point you a reminder of what happened in that match and only comment in this regard...
what goes around, comes around. Osborne: You have no remorse at all for your actions? Nemesis: Look closely ... Do you see any remorse in my eyes, Osborne? [Marc Osborne shifts somewhat uncomfortably in his chair before resuming the interview, changing the subject...]
Osborne: Now, on to the subject of the Brotherhood. We all remember with vivid detail how much the Brotherhood's power was felt in Gyeah Wrestling.
However, with Aron Rave's wrestling career now apparently over and Kyle
Blaque and CJ Swift retired from the sport, that leaves only Jason Hawke and you to carry on the mantle of the once feared Brotherhood. This marks for the first time in your wrestling career that you will be going solo, as one might put it.
Nemesis: For many months, the Brotherhood was Gyeah Wrestling...period. But like you said, Hawke and I have pretty much decided to go our own ways. Forte wanted a Brotherhood reunion of sorts, simply to satiate his drive for ratings, but at this point, Forte and I aren't on the best of terms.
Osborne: But President Forte was once aligned... Nemesis: Like I said, we aren't on the best of terms anymore, I'll leave it at that. Osborne: Very well. Let's now talk about the rumors that have been heard ever since you reached superstardom here in Gyeah Wrestling. When you won the GW Heavyweight Championship at Chapter One, it was seen by many wrestling insiders as a shock, as you haven't been in the business all that long, really.
Nemesis: It was a shock at the time, but Jon Forte gambled on myself being able to carry this federation and make it the driving force that it is today. If it hadn't been for me, there would be no celebration of Jaamal
Jammer as the new champion. There wouldn't be Gyeah.com for idiots to spend countless hours wasting away upon. Overall, I'd say that Forte's gambit has paid off in huge dividends.
Osborne: But what of your past history, with much speculation has been given to? We've all heard various exploits of you wandering from territory to territory, bringing chaos and anarchy in your wake. Some have even labeled you the Antichrist of professional wrestling, citing your contempt for tradition as well as what many perceive as a difficult personality to work with...
Nemesis: Come on, Osborne, do you take me for that type of person? [Nemesis chuckles out loud while Osborne looks down for a moment, clearly choosing his words wisely in the presence of a man known for violent outbursts...]
Nemesis: What am I supposed to say? What do you truly expect me to say to all the so-called critics that think they know jack about Nemesis?
Osborne: Why, I - I don't kno- Nemesis: How's this ... [censored for your protection] them. If people want to critique something, how about let us make it my fist up close and personal? People can say what they want to say, they can believe what they want to believe. As a matter of fact, I enjoy the thought that you people don't know the truth from the fiction. In the end, it's the violence that creates my hell.
Osborne: The last topic of discussion...Psycho Soup? Nemesis: You mean the walking definition of trailer park trash? [ponders...] Nemesis: You know, Soup ... we've been through this since the beginning. For the most part, Soup, you kept your distance, I kept mine; it was nothing personal when I took the GW Title from you all those months back, just business. If anything, Soup, you should have learned one thing...to contend with the Nemesis is to lose. For your sake, I hope you understand the consequences of adding impersonation of the Nemesis as another one of your antics, for Soup, you are about to find out one thing:
Battle Bowl was only the beginning. [Fade back to the GW studios...]
