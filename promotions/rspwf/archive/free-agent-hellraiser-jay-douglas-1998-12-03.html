[Free Agent] "Hellraiser" Jay Douglas

3 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys. I'm looking for a place to introduce my newest character,
"Hellraiser" Jay Douglas. What I'm looking for is a small independent organization with original wrestlers only. The fed must have been open for at least six months, and must have a Cruiserweight/Lightweight
Division. It doesn't matter if your fed is message board or email based, but it must be a booked federation. Finally, no "Hardcore" or
"Extreme" federations. Ok, here's what you've all been waiting for.
The sample roleplay: (The setting is Wigwam park in Stratford, Connecticut. It is nearing evening, as the sun is setting, creating a multicolored horizon. The camera pans across the park, revealing a baseball field, pond, bleachers, a few benches, and quite a few trees. Finally, the camera comes to a stop on the basketball court, where a large number of kids are playing. There is only one kid not playing. Someone gets knocked down, and the playing stops. One of the kids approaches the one not playing.)
Kid: Come on Jay, why aren't you playing? Jay Douglas: Come on, you know basketball isn't my thing. Kid: Then what is your thing? All you ever want to do is wrestle. Jay Douglas: Exactly. Wrestling's my thing. It's what makes me happy. Kid: Ok, whatever. (to the other kids) Ok, let's play!!! (The kids continue playing basketball as Jay approaches the camera. He continues walking, and the camera follows him. Jay is around 6' or
6'1", and has a medium build. He's wearing blue jeans and a JNCO t-shirt, as well as a WWF Attitude hat. As he walks, he begins talking, apparently to himself.)
Jay Douglas: All they ever want to do is play basketball! I guess it just means they're scared. But, I can't blame them, why wouldn't they be scared? Anyone who wrestles me loses, and that's a fact. Not only am I fast, but I've got the moves to put anyone away. I just wish I'd get recognized.
(Jay continues walking, and after a moment begins to speak again.) Jay Douglas: Who am I kidding? What kind of wrestling federation would sign a 16 year old? I don't even have my license yet. But, then again,
I do have talent, and some people might notice that.
(Jay approaches the bleachers, and sits down. He sits for a moment, silently, head down, when something hits the bleachers right next to him. He looks next to him, and then puts his head back down. Again something hits the bleachers, and this time he looks up. We see another kid standing across the baseball field from him, holding a small rock.
The kid tosses the rock up in the air, catches it, and then throws it towards Jay. Jay stands up, and begins to yell at the kid.)
Jay Douglas: (yelling, angrily) What the fuck?! I'm about to come over there and kick your fuckin' ass!
Kid: (yelling) I heard you wanted someone to wrestle!! You think you can beat anyone! Well try and beat me!
(Jay begins running toward the kid, and the camera follows close behind. Jay reaches the kid, and plows over him with a shoulderblock, sending both of them to the ground. The kid gets on top of Jay, and punches him in the face, bloodying his nose. Jay hooks his legs under the kids arms, and takes him down. Jay jumps to his feet, as does the kid he's fighting. The kid throws a punch at Jay's face, but Jay ducks it, and hits him with a low blow, dropping the kid to the ground. Jay begins to walk away, smiling.)
Jay Douglas: And another one feels the wrath of the Hellraiser. (Jay begins to walk away, toward the sidewalk. He looks back, and the kid is still on the ground clutching his groin. Jay continues walking until he reaches the sidewalk. He looks down the sidewalk both ways, and decides to take a right. He walks for a couple hundred feet, while the camera stays behind. Jay turns a corner, and the camera slowly begins to fade to black.)
~Joe
