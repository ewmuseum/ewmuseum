The HeadHunter sample RP. Comments and fed offers are welcome..

20 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The long stone walkway alongside Horseshoe Falls in Niagara Falls, Ontario. It is late at night, and the light of a purple spotlight casts a mystifyingly beautiful yet strangely eerie glow on Horseshoe Falls. The area is empty, temporarily closed for the evening, yet one man remains. This man is seemingly broad shouldered, and stands at an avaerage height. The camera fades in closer and he is recognized as the IEUW Superchampion and SPWO United States Champion, the HeadHunter. He wears a tight "It's Great Being Great" T-Shirt, loose fitting blue jeans, and a pair of Nike sneakers. HeadHunter glances at the falls, then leans against the guardrail. ]
"Ah, yes . . . Niagara Falls . . . truly one of the most awe-inspiring and popular tourist spots on this planet. It's an amazing thing;
two-and-a-half thousand feet of magnificence, nearly half a mile of splendor, with thousands of gallons of water catapulting down every minute. However, what truly makes this spot a thing of grandiose awe is its pure power, its incredible capability of annihilation harnessed by nature and man."
[ The HeadHunter then looks up into the sky and stands for a moment, watching the birds fly about the sky. ]
''Then, we have the birds of the world. Beautiful creatures really. They fly high and fly low and nobody can keep them down. That reminds me of one thing. . . my career! ]
[ HeadHunter gets a more serious look on his face now as he says this. ] ''On the first stop on the WWO's ''Road to Independance'' I took on Al
''Groovin'' Toobin. I heard all about how he was so great and I heard how he was going to defeat me. In the end, he was proved wrong and the true wrestling great walked out with his arm raised. I used by technical skill, I used my high flying talents, I used my great versatility and I did what I told the world I would. . . improve my WWO record. So you see, I am like the birds. . . you can come after me, you can maybe wound me, but you _can't_ keep me down! ''
[ HeadHunter chuckles half-heartedly. ] "Al Toobin really was no problem for me. I never had to put in any effort to beat him. I simply choose to work hard, in order to make myself look very good. . . and make him look bad! As far as the High Rollers go, I'll deal with them somewhere down the line. They have hell to pay for attacking me but I guess they did get me the victory. I am not a man though to rave on about a simple victory. It is now time for the HeadHunter to move on, to his next match. That is the way I work, taking one match at a time. I am not present on stop number two although that show is looking quite impressive. Names such as
Pelius of Clamorous and Jason Agony impress me. They shall hold that show together. Stop number three intrests me though as I am wrestling on it.
Saturday, June 27th at the J.F.K Collisium in Manchester, New Hampshire is the place. The HeadHunter versus Da SuPerFaN is the match. Two big names athletes, both who have competed in the CyberNetic Wrestling Federation, squared., both who have competed in Intense Extreme Uncut Wrestling. ''
[ HeadHunter stops for a breath then continues speaking. ] ''Lets talk a little history, shall we Da SuPerFaN. Yes, there was a time when you were mighty, when you were a 'big deal.' You were the CNWF
Intercontinental Champion, something I never managed to do. I respected you for that. Then though, there was the TN-IEUW days. The days when you _promised_ that you would be the one to eliminate me from the fifty-man Battle Royal. When
I clotheslined you over the top-rope, it was a great feeling. I don't know what happened to you Da SuPerFaN. You just faded. Recently, in eXtremely HARDCORE wrestling, you were defeated by Mike Johnson. The losses have been mounting for you and this Saturday, I shall inflict another loss on you. Al Toobin was the first WWO superstar this time around to feel the effect of my great talent and this Saturday, I shall let you know what some ''Hunted'' really feels like. ''
[ HeadHunter is smiling as he continues to speak. ] ''It shall be a momentus day come June 27th. It shall be the day when a
CNWF legend is beaten by the man who all the CNWF stars thought very little of.
The man who was told he couldn't cut it has now found the time and patience to be able to leave his mark in the wrestling world. This coming Saturday, the world shall witness the rebirth of the _true_ ICON and Da SuPerFaN. . . you'll witness it first hand! ''
[ HeadHunter pauses and smiles . . . ] "I have been telling the world for some time now, in various federations, that the HeadHunter truly is the ICON. The NCWF saw just how I worked and I had them running so scared, the federation was shut down. The IEUW has saw my presence and it shut down. I dominated the NAW until it closed.
Federations like the FWF and the IIWA shall soon witness the HeadHunter's talents. Who knows how long they shall last. The SPWO has been tough to shut down because of the quality competition that has built up but now, I am working there and with the United States Title in my possession. . . the federation is starting to become scared. ''
[ HeadHunter pauses . . . then sighs. ] "Some of you people _still_ don't realize my stature as the ICON. Some of you just see me as a worthless piece of trash, unable to make the grade in the WWO. Well, I now have proof for you all. Let me just take a look at this letter I received from my agent earlier today. . . "
[ HeadHunter reaches into his pocket and pulls out a folded piece of paper. He unfolds it and then stretches it making it nice. He glances it over is quickly . . . ] ''Lets see what it says here shall we? The SPWO wants me to become the highest paid athlete.. so do the EULW. Oh look, I'm wanted by the NAW and the
UvWa are after my signature as well. I tell you something folks. . . it's great being great! ''
[ HeadHunter chuckles and folds the paper back up and puts it in his pocket. He scratches something on the top of his head then resumes . . . ] "The next order of buisness would have to be WWO's Fight for Independance '98 which happens on Saturday, July 4th from the CoreState Center in Philadelphia. My team was drawn out and I can't say that I am impressed.
Firstly, we have Flashover, a man who has certainly failed to impress me. There is Broken, someone who I have seen or heard very little from so I cannot really comment. Then, there is Skitzo, a protegy of Jason Agony. One can only live in hope that he will turn out just as sick. On paper, my team may not be great, I may hate each of their guts but I shall be talking to them and going over exactly what they have to do in order to get us the victory. . . leave everything to me! ''
[ HeadHunter pauses for a moment, thinking, and then continues. ] ''Our opponents may prove to be slightly difficuly. Tony Ramson of
Electric Youth is obviously not going to be a problem for me. Tag Team wrestlers don't impress me. I'll eliminate him at the snap of my fingers. The other three competitors may cause some problems though. The world knows of my respect for Jason Agony, the world's sickest wrestler. I see him as being one of the best wrestlers in the world today and I beleive that he has what it takes to make it to the very top. Shaggy 2 Dope though is a man who I am not too sure about. In his day, he was one of the greatest athletes but now he seems to be fading. One last try for stardom is what he is doing just now and I really doubt whether or not he shall be sucessful. Then we have got Fredrick.
B. Inkel. Last I heard of him, he was propping up the CNWF2's rankings. Then I heard he had given up the sport. Another attempted come-back shall be foiled by the only man who is capable of doing it. I shall then move on to the Battle
Royal and _earn_ my shot at the World Title like a true ICON should! I don't want handed a title shot, I'm close to a European Title chance but all that concerns me right now is my main goal, the WWO World Title and you better beleive something. . . I will get it! ''
[ HeadHunter smiles that wicked smile once again and the camera slowly fades to an annoying electric blue . . . ]
