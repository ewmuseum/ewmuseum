Roleplay feds

8 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I would like some info from some of you that run your own feds. I have been in a certain fed for about 8-9 months now, and am about to quit. Both of the co-presidents have told me many times that I am a good roleplayer, yet in all my time there, I have had only 8 matches (there are at least 5-6 cards a month, usually more) and only 2 wins. This is a roleplay based fed. Not to toot my own horn, but from the looks of the competition in that league (tag team was what I was in), I should have dominated. My team won the tag belts in their second match (thus second victory), then never won again. We kept the belts through DQ for 2 more matches. Then we began to job. There was only one instance of a lack of roleplay, which was Christmas break. Despite my efforts to please the higher ups, I still lose, and lose badly. I have asked them what I might do to correct this. I received replies from both guys, and each said something different. The guy who had held the league the longest said that I was ok and should hold on. The other said go singles. Yet I still lose... My questions to all of you: If you have a roleplay based fed, how do you decide who wins? Do you ever keep someone from winning because you don't particularly care for their gimmick? Do you let someone win because you have met them online (ie. chat room, bulletin board, etc.)? And do you think I am right in quitting a fed where I am obviously getting nowhere? Post here, or email me. I'm interested in what others have to think about this situation. Andy
