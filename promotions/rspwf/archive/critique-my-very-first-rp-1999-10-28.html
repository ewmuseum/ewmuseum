-[Critique]- My VERY FIRST RP!!

28 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello All! If you didn't get the title, this is the very first RP I've ever done. It was my sample RP I did for Armageddon. So, if you could, give me your thoughts on it and tell me if you think I'll get into Armageddon with it. Thank
You!
--------------------------------------------------------------------------- -X Scene X- Welcome to beautiful Chicago, Illinois where you are in what appears to be a conference room. The room has chairs set up in front of stage, where a podium is sitting. You can see security guards standing in front of double doors, apparently holding them closed. One gaurd lifts his radio up and listens to what he is being told. He nods to the other gaurd, and they open the double doors. A slew of reporters run in and take seats, all trying to get as close to the stage as they can. Finally, everything is settled down. A buzz of excitment falls over the audience as a Gentleman walks out.
-X Jason Frindley X- Hello, and welcome to Chicago. If you do not know, my name is Jason Frindley. I'm the manager, trainer, agent, or whatever you want to call me of the man you are all here to see. So, I ask you, when he comes out, please be courtious to him. He is only nineteen years old, and is very emotional. Give him some breathing room. Okay? Without any farther ado, I bring to you...
__ 'Enraged' Justin Hvizdzak __ -X Scene X- The reporters stand up and begin to clap as the nineteen year old rookie walks out from the off-stage area. Justin Hvizdzak is wearing tan cargo shorts, with a 'Polo Sports' dress shirt on. His bright blue eyes look out into the ocean of reporters and they seem to widen with amazement. This has to be a great feeling to him. He steps up to the podium slowly. He begins to talk, but stammers a few words from being nervous. His agent, Jason Frindley, tells him to calm down and just go with it.
-X 'Enraged' Justin Hvizdzak X- WOW! I'm amazed at the amount of people that showed up at this conference to see me. But, I'm happy. I guess I'm getting more exposure than any other rookie would. Now, let me get to the subject at hand. Yes, I'm only nineteen years old and I'm putting my feet in cold water getting into the wrestling world with no experience. But, this is a last chance for me. I've got anger built up inside of me that is fighting to get out of me. This is the last way I could think of doing it. I just have to get into the ring and fight my heart out.
Now, with that little introduction speech aside if you must, I'll begin to take questions. -X Reporter X- Mr. Hvizdzak, we are all wondering why you choose such an established fed like Armageddon to beging a young, but promising, career like yours.
-X 'Enrage' Justin Hvizdzak X- Really, that is a no brainer. It is mostly common sense. If a fed, as Armageddon, is already devolped, then why would you choose a fed that possibly has no talent and could fall through anytime, mostly when you least expect it.
Then again, on the other hand, Armageddon is so well devolped that it may have talent that is above my talent level by far. But, that could also be considered a good thing. I'll watch and learn from those people. And hopefully become as good as them. As for right now, I'm happy.
-X Scene X- Jason Frindley steps over to Justin and whispers something into his ear. -X 'Enraged' Justin Hvizdzak X- I'm sorry, but I've just been informed that I have a meeting pretty soon and
I have to go get ready. Although, I'll be back some other time to finish this up. Until then, watch out for me in Armageddon if all goes well with my contract.
-X Scene X- The crowd stands up and gives Justin Hvizdzak a standing ovation as he walks off the stage and into a limo to his meeting.
