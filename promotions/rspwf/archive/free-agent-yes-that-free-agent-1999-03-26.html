[Free Agent] yes... THAT FREE AGENT!

26 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Dear Fed Heads, The time has come for me to offically throw my hat back into the e-wrestling ring. My one month hiatus is over. I've rested, endured and over came. Now I'm ready to get back to work.
First off I'd like to thank everyone who mentioned me as in their Top 10 dream wrestlers poll. Makes a guy feel kinda appreciated if you know what I mean. :)
Now is the time for you fed heads to make a case to why you want the one and only LOCO [yes...THAT LOCO] in your feds. Everyone promising titles will be deleted. Everyone claiming to have the _best_ fed on the net will be laughed at and thus, deleted.
Well then what do I want? I want you to tell me how you book. How many shows you have. What your expectations of me are. What kind of role do you see for me in your fed. Plus any other info you deem appropriate.
I've wasted too many of my damn days in feds with no vision. Reply by email. Below lays a resume and sample roleplay. Enjoy. LOCO [yes... THAT LOCO] ****** Resume ****** -Title History CCW World Champion CCW World Tag Team Champions CCW World Champion X2 XCW World Champion EWF World Champion WOWA World Champion EMWC World Television Champion UIWF World Champion IGW World Champion -Other Awards 1996 Cyber Championship Wrestling's Wrestler of the Year 1997 7th Place Finish: RSPWF, Best Interviewee 1997 10th Place Finish: RSPWF, Best Wrestler 1998 10th Place Finish: Snapshot Awards, Best Heel 1998 14 ranked Wrestler in the World: RSPWF Top 200 -Super Cards 1997 Signed to appear at King of Champions 1998 Wrestled at International Invitational of E-Wrestling/D-Cup I
1998 Invited to Zielinski Cup
1998 Invited to D-Cup II 1998 Invited to face Curtis Hansen at "Weekend to Remember" 1998 Wrestled and defeated Setzer Van Strife at "Night of Glory" *************** Sample Roleplay *************** [The man of myth and legacy survey's the dark and musky warehouse. He moves into a position over a worn floor in an obscure area in upper New
York. He stands and takes the classic pose, LOCO; at once both terrifying and benovlent, the king of cosmic dance, both deystroyer and creater, bends his right leg, lowering his center of gravity, and then lifts and slightly crosses his left leg over the right. His arms are loosley spread towards the iron cross that stands before him. The master of mayhem, otherwise known as LOCO, sits in a trance like state before opening his eyes. He begins to speak in his all too familer low booming voice.]
LOCO: I've heard a lot of talk about the past. Some would have you believe that's just where I belong... locked away in the caverns of one's mind. Some would even tell you that I'm washed up... a relic of a long forgotten time. A memory... if you will.
[LOCO's eyes breifly close... then reopen] LOCO: I'm here to tell you nothing could be further from the all inspiring truth. I *might* not be as good as I was... but I'm sure as hell better than you... and that's a fact. You don't have to like it for it to be so. Now let's address some issues one by one, shall we?
[A small laugh is heard.] LOCO: Randy Acorn? Who? You don't even belong here boy. I don't know who you suckered to get in this match. I don't know what kinda strings you pulled of what kinda pictures you have of Evans and sheep... I just think you should up and quit. Much like your Daddy should have done the night he concieved you... PULL OUT. Go home... get the hell outta dodge.
No one cares about you anyway. Hell it don't take a rocket scientist to see you don't belong.
[A smirk creases Loc's face for an instance... and then turns in a manical smile.] LOCO: "Evil" Alex Roberts. You have no concept what _evil_ is all about. You made the mistake of saying you beat me. Hell you can't lace my boots jobrone. I know many nights when your in bed late... alone.... dressed in your Spiderman underoos... wrestling with your Teletubbie sheets and pillows... you probably had a dreamt you beat the legend. Probably under the impression you rolled me up for the 1...2....3. But much like a_wet_dream ...
I'll leave you wet and confused and laying limp on the floor. Don't even try to pretend your in my world. For that's a dangerous game that generally ends in the victim hanging from their neck... fighting for air.
Got my drift sunshine? [LOCO raises from his position. He walks the base of the warehouse where he comes across a old tattered poster of 4 men. The words "Mob" and
"Lynch" can be deciphered from behind the dust. LOCO raises his right hand and dusts away the faces of the 4 men... LOCO, SVS, PICO.... and finally... Rey Jeran. He stops for an moment and glances at the reminder of the stables triumphs. His eyes clued to the poster of "The Greatest
Stable of ALL-TIME". He pauses a moment more and proceeds to speak.]
LOCO: ..... and then there where 4. [Still looking at the poster.] LOCO: What a legacy we have. We were Gods... yes we were Gods. No one's accomplished what we have. No one ever could. We left more than a mark... we left a *dent*. We "were".... and we "will" again.
But first.... First I have to deal with this championship match... and most of all... my good friend... Rey Jeran. [LOCO spins around and looks into the camera.] LOCO: Brother Rey... we've had some wars... [Images of LOCO vs Jeran I... flash on the camera.] LOCO: We've walked the lines like soldiers.... like brothers... [Images of Jeran and LOCO embracing in ONE.] LOCO: But many would have us believe we don't have to do this. That one of us should step down from the match and let the other proceed.
Although I've been guilty of that myself... although I wanted to clear the way for you... I CAN'T... I can't deprive you of the opportunity of *winning* that belt... the belt I *gave* you once before. You should have the chance, just like everybody else, to win the title I_never_lost. The belt that should still be wrapped around my waste today. I can't deprive you of that nor will I. If you leave that cage as champion... you would have left it going through me. You would have EARNED IT. For as much as I love you.
For as much as your friendship means to me. The charity stops here. If ya beat me... YOUR THE CHAMP... I'll shake your hand and I'll raise that arm. Hell I'm probably carry ya on my shoulders straight to the bar and buy you a beer. I'll toast ya for the champion you are.
BUT FIRST... ya gotta beat me. I'm not laying down for you or anyone. I'm coming to fight. I'm coming to take back MY belt. The belt I never lost... and I'll go right through you to do it...
And that's the bare bones truth. It don't get anymore real than that. [LOCO turns around and inspects the poster again. Smiling... he begins to walk up the aisle. He comes across a showcase which includes 6 World
Championship belts... and one noose. He stares into the case. His reflection casted in the glass. He begins to speak.]
LOCO: You know we been through a lot together.... you and I. I'm asking for your help one last time.
[At this point it is confusing just who LOCO is speaking too.] LOCO: You've never let me down. You've always been by my side. I know your mad I didn't take you to EMWC. I know your feeling are probably hurt. But don't worry... I'm not the Independent Superstar... anymore.
I'm a legend.. and legends can pretty much do anything they like. So what do ya say? The TRUTH... for old time sake?
[LOCO eases open the glass door and reaches inside. He clutches the noose and pulls it out. Closing the door behind him. He open the head of the noose and flips it over his neck.]
LOCO: At last..... the *Truth*... and soon everyone will see... every one will feel... THE TRUTH HURTS... DON'T IT!?! [Camera fades to the trophy case and the 6-World Championship belts.]
