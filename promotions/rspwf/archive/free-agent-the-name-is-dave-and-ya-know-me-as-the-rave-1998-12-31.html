Free Agent-The name is Dave and ya know me as The Rave

31 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Let's see where should I begin. I started my e-fed history about 5 months ago...tough to believe I could achive such hieghts in so little time, especially when I took October off. Anyway I, being a jackass, wondered around the top 100 lists, and futile searches to find weak fed after weak fed, and I usually got bored very quickly. However earlier today after another fed I was in closed I was left again to search, when I came across The RSPWF. Now I came and see stuff about all these great "BIG" Feds, and let me tell ya I'm chompin at the bit. So right now I would like everyone to know that Delicious Dave
Davidson is open for business. Now not many of you have probably heard of me but I have held 6 different titles in my career, and I have been in 5 different feds. Delicious Dave is mostly a comic wrestler, I don't take anyone seriously, and I laugh at those who try to be "gangstas" or gothic. I like to stir things up and make a lot of people hate me, and while the name would suggest I'm very sexual in RP's, I'm not. I'm sorry I can't give a sample RP cuz I haven't written one in 4 days and the last one I did sucked wang because I had no decent competition. Basically I want one of the big doggs, one of the RSPWF worthy sites to give me a ring, will it happen...I hope so, but if it doesn't
I'll still be a pain in the ass tomorrow. So to all those top 200 punks look at next years #1.
"I'm Delicious Dave and I'm The Mother Fuckin Rave"-Who do ya think said it. Dave
