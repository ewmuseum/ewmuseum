[Critique] "Midas Touch" Johnny Glamour

2 June 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, here's a flash to critique. The reason I am posting it is b/c I decided to change my style a little bit and not talk so much about wrestling in the flash, but instead, just have fun with a little bit of spontaneity and let whatever happens, happen. Tell me if this works or if I should stick to the more traditional style. Also, if one of two people out there actually think
it's good, maybe I can get a few people to join the fed. Well, flame away : ) [The scene opens up to a caption that reads "Glamour For The Gold On Tour" in big bold letters on the bottom of the screen. As the caption fades out, it becomes apparent that the camera crew is in the inside of what looks to be a huge mall. The camera zooms around the busy mall as many people can be seen coming in and out of various stores and shops. Suddenly the camera stops and sets its attention on a very uncrowded booth in the middle of the mall. On top of the booth reads "Get Johnny Glamour's autograph for only $25.00!"
Inside the booth and sitting down in a chair looking like he has all the time in the world is GSWP's self absorbed superstar, "Midas Touch" Johnny Glamour.
Glamour who is dressed in his usual attire consisting of a snug fitting gold colored "Welcome To GlamourVille" shirt, his trademark skin tight black leather pants and black boots, takes a deep breath and sighs before noticing a rather chubby child walking near his booth. Glamour tries to get his attention as he stands up from his chair and begins to talk ... ]
Johnny Glamour: Hey fatboy, it's me, your hero, "Midas Touch" Johnny Glamour. Why don't you get that ice cream cone out of your mouth and fetch me 25 bucks so I can give you my autograph. I mean, hurry up and get one while you still can. This line is gonna get huge any minute now.
[The chubby boy looks towards Glamour and the non existing line of fans waiting for Glamour's autograph. The kid laughs and takes a lick of his ice cream cone before walking over towards Glamour and beginning to talk ... ]
Fat Kid: Hey, aren't you that guy in GSWP -- The guy who thinks everyone worships him. You're cool, man.
[Glamour seems pleased that the kid recognizees him. Glamour runs his fingers through his long bleach blonde hair and then nods his head in recognition as the boy continues ... ]
Yeah, you're really funny and stuff, Johnny. I mean, I don't know how you keep the serious look on your face when you talk about how you're gonna beat my hero, Fang, for the World Title at the GSWP PPV. You have to know your outmatched. All my friends say you are a has-been, but until you joined GSWP,
I had never even heard of you. But I gotta admit that you do a good job of playing your gimmick of a sissy-bitch.
[Glamour appears outraged and begins to climb up out of his booth before realizing better. He then takes a deep breath and continues..]
Johnny Glamour: Sissy-bitch? You think that's my gimmick? I bet you're just jealous because your mother has Johnny Glamour posters scattered all over her wall. Yeah, that's it. You're just saying that because you're mad that your mother spends all of her money on my hotline instead of feeding your mouth full of twinkies, fat boy! I'll have you know that I'm no "sissy-bitch" and "Midas
Touch" certainly isn't a gimmick. [pauses] So are you gonna buy my autograph or what?
[The chubby boy shakes his head and laughs before continuing...] Fat Kid: I wouldn't pay $1.00 for your autograph, let alone 25! Johnny Glamour: OK, how bout $.99? Fat Kid: No way! I'm saving up all of my money to buy the new second series limited edition super tough and rough Fang action figure! It's almost impossible to find. The only figure I ever see at the store is yours, 'cuz nobody ever buys it.
[Glamour's jaw drops again. He appears totally offended as he continues...] Johnny Glamour: Bah! You're just like the rest of them! Jealous, you're all jealous! Well, I have news for your tubby, come June the 13th, your hero Fang is in for the beating of his life as he steps up and takes a most unglamorous ride to "GlamourVille!" That gold belt is as good as mine! [Screaming] So you can take that ice cream cone of yours and shove it up your ass!
[Glamour's comments get the attention of other customers of the mall as they make their way over to the booth. Glamour continues to rant and rave ... ]
Why are you all giving me these dirty looks? Is this any way to treat your hero, your inspiration, your sole reason for existence, "Midas Touch" Johnny
Glamour? I think not! Now open up your wallets and gimme your money! I have autographs to rip off, err, I mean sell.
[The crowd begins shouting things like "You're a bum, Johnny," "Fang's gonna murder you at Summer Showdown" and "Go back to GlamourVille, you punk!"
Glamour looks absolutely furious as he continues ...] Screw you! Screw you all! You obviously don't appreciate talent when you see it. Well, I've got news for you and all of the GSWP fans out there watching at home... I'm not going away! Nah, this has only just begun! As sure as I am glamorous, you had better believe that it's a "Glamour World" out there and whether you fans like it or not, you had better deal with the fact that after
June 13th, I will be your World Heavyweight Champion and there isn't a single thing that Fang, or any of your morons can do about it! You can trust me on that!
[The rowdy fans begin to get hostile as they start to pelt Glamour with bottles of soda and other debris. Glamour swiftly hops up and over the booth and begins to run away as the scene slowly fades out as the mob of people follow after him...]
[name withheld] Handler of .... "Midas Touch" Johnny Glamour [GSWP] --"Glamour For The Gold In '99"-- Yeah, I know I was retired, but I came back, ok? : )
