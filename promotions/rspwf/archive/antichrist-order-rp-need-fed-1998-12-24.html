AntiChrist Order---RP---Need Fed

24 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
AntiChrist Order Pope Sixtus the 6th (6) Cardinal Syn 245 lbs 240 lbs 6'3" 6'1" Hometown: The Vatican Hometown: The Vatican Complete History: VWCF World Tag Team Champions (5x), single handedly retired
The Mindset Empire and Sir White Collar.
{Opening Scene} [Day 1] (In Rome, Italy at the Vatican stands two men. They are men of courage, men of prestige and men with purpose. One stands tall in a full, solid white robe, with a pointed white hat which is about 12" in heighth. He has deep, blue eyes and a serene smile and he is holding a book. Standing next to him is his personal companion and confidant. He is wearing a full length solid red robe and he has a more menacing look to him. Knowing full well this is their time, they begin to speak.)
[Pope Sixtus the 6th (6)]-In the Bible it says that their will be an end time, that an anti-christ will come to rule for but a short time and during that short time there will be carnage and misery for all. I believe truly in this, because it is now the time that has been foretold, with the only exception being we shall rule forever. This is my compatriot...Cardinal Syn.
(Pope Sixtus looks to the right and the Cardinal begins to speak, as Pope
Sixtus looks on approvingly.)
[Cardinal Syn]-Prophecy has been foretold, destiny is upon us all and when the time comes and we enter your federation all superstars will understand and become AntiChrist Superstars. For you all will join us and pray to our religion and listen to our words that we speak, or you shall all die for your follies. No team is safe from our carnage and no, yet no title is safe from the "AntiChrist Order."
(Cardinal Syn begins to chant something in Latin and seeming pleased, Pope
Sixtus begins to finish his message.)
[Pope Sixtus the 6th (6)]-Our future is now, your future is now and your destiny lies with us. For we are your lords and we are your gods, to resist us is futile and to challenge us is to perish.
(With these words spoken, the AntiChrist Order turns from the view of the camera and walks quietly into the Vatican.)
{Scene Ends}
