[FBK] Latest Flash for ICW...

8 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ The large FBK estate on the coast of Virginia Beach, Virginia. The sun is setting and nothing is heard except for a few birds chirping and the waves crashing in the backyard. The sky is a red-orange color and it paints a beautiful picture. The house's front yard is landscaped nicely, hedges trimmed to perfection in different shapes, grass looking nice and fresh. The camera man walks up the steps to the porch and on the porch is a few chairs and a table, nothing too fancy. The camera man knocks on the door. After a few moments, a short man in a penguin-like butler suit answers. He has on a pair of glasses that rest on the tip of his nose. He opens the door and invites the man in. He then leads him down into the basement...]
[ The basement is dimly lit, kind of hard to see. A television set is heard, commentary for a basketball game. The camera looks into the room and laying on a futon is Full Blood Killah. FBK has on a pair of Structure sweatpants along with a Devestation Inc. T-Shirt. FBK looks pretty tired and looks up at the camera. His eyes are half opened and there are "bags" under them. FBK stretches out while grunting a little bit. He then throws a blanket off of him and sits up...]
[ FBK cracks his neck a few times and then lets out a deep breath of air. He rubs his eyes to open the wider and looks into the camera. He picks up his bottle of Snapple Iced Tea and takes a sip. He then swallows and then looks into the camera...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: [Moaning] Ugh, you woke da Killah up...now is that a way to treat the most exhilarating, breath-taking wrestler in the industry today? Da
Killah doesn't think so. Well, you know when you get so scared you almost pee your pants? The other day that almost happened to da Killah when he heard the
ICW was going to close. Da Killah would've been out of a job for christ sakes!
No unemployment for FBK, no way in holy hell. But thank god there were some front office talks and ICW has decided to keep going, under new ownership. But the president as of now, James Pillow is having one last card while he still holds the reigns. The "Last Hoorah" da Killah believes is the name of it. Well
James, before da Killah addresses his match for that card...let me say thanks for the opportunity in one of the greatest wrestling federations to date.
[ FBK pauses for a few moments. He picks up a piece of paper and reads it over. He keeps nodding his head over and over. He then looks back up at the camera, licks his lips and continues...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: But back to the Last Hoorah card. When da Killah saw this on
ICW TV last night, his eyes practically bugged out of his head. This is
_BETTER_ than Pay Per View type quality. It includes the whole damn roster! The stakes just don't get any higher than some of these matches. Da Killah's good pal Devon Case has a big match against a great wrestler in "Brass Knux" Jason
Williams and Patrick Dempsey. Then another friend of da Killah's, Tommy
Grayson, has to put his North American Title on the line against Hidden Essence. What a match that'll be. And then yours truly has one of his biggest match up to date in his ICW career. FBK is taking on Tha Dragon, "Fabulous"
Freddy Floyd, James Van Steele, Jason Nyghtstorm in a King of the Hill match-up. Hell, FBK sees that you're chances really rely on the luck of the draw! Well, da Killah'll go through all of the other four guys to win the damn match. And the best thing about this match is...the winner gets an
International Title shot the next week on Fallout! Well boys, let me just say one thing...the shot is _MINE_! Da Killah don't know much about the competition just yet because he hasn't done his "homework" just yet. But let da Killah go down the list on what he knows about each guy so far...Tha Dragon, a former
Television Champion, he's got skillz and da Killah's aware of it. But does he have the ability to beat the best thing in ICW up to date? Hell no, _NO ONE_, none of these four men, anyone on the god damn ICW roster can beat da Killah because he's just that _GOOD_! Wait, let me rephrase that...he's the flat-out
_BEST_! And these four _JAY-BRONES_ are gonna find that out first hand in two weeks. But back to da Killah's shit list...Freddy Floyd, also a former
Television Champion and you beat Konrad Curze for the title...impressive to some, but not to da Killah because he beat the living _POO-POO_ right out of
Curze and right before FBK was about to take the win, that Killah-wanna-be,
Westside Walavan stuck his long nose where it wasn't needed. Walavan, da
Killah'll get to you in a second. But Floyd, da Killah gives credit where its due...and you, well you just don't deserve any credit! Floyd, da Killah also realizes you lost that TV Title you held to Tha Dragon, so in a sense...he's better than you. But don't take that hard because da Killah's better than every god damn wrestler in this match! Then there's James Van Steele, a guy put in the wrong match at the wrong time...sonny boy, da Killah's got a foot heading straight for your ass! Because when its all said and done, and if you have to square off against _THE_ man called FBK, you'll be leaving this King of the
Hill match-up with an imprint of da Killah's foot straight on your
_AS[BLEEP]LE_! Da Killah guesses its called facing the consequences of having to face him. Then last but certainly not least on da Killah's shit list, Jason
Nyghstorm. Lets take a walk down memory lane...Friday Night Fallout, the mini battle royal...who won that one? Umm, oh yeah..._FBK DID_! And who was the man he eliminated last? Jason Nyghtstorm! What a co-inky-dink! Well Nyghstorm, you've been in ICW for about the same amount of time as da Killah and you're making a name just as quick as yours truly, but the only thing is...my name is
_BIGGER_! And after da Killah kicks your ass from start to end and takes the honor of winning this King of the Hill match and getting the International
Title shot the next week on Fallout, da Killah will leave Jason Nyghstorm in the dust as he jumps to being even bigger and better, and you just look like a bigger scum on the face of the earth. That goes for all four of you down-right
_LOSERS_! Because when you look at the truth of it, not one of you four, hell, all four of you at the same time couldn't wear...
..._THE_... ..._JOCK_... ..._OF_... ..._FBK_!!! [ FBK snickers to himself and then stands up while stretching. He then walks up the stairs and walks into the kitchen of his large house. He sits at the table and his butler brings him over a bag of McDonald's food. FBK pulls out a Crispy
Chicken Sandwich, super-sized fries, and a super-sized coke. FBK goes crazy, eating like a wild animal. He then stops for a moment and looks up at the camera. He's out of breath from eating so fast. He takes a sip of soda and proceeds...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Too lazy to cook so da Killah gets the goods! Heh Heh...but, da Killah just remembered he promised to talk a little about Westsyyyyyde
Walavan. Walavan, da Killah hasn't forgot when you interfered in his match. And he's got a little surprise for you Walavan, so make sure you're listening at the Last Hoorah because there's plenty coming from FBK heading your way. So order the Kleenex in advance, because after you see and hear some of the content da Killah has planned, you'll be crying your little eyes out. Walavan, just remember, _YOU_ started this by interfering in _MY_ match. You weren't needed, therefore you brought this on yourself. And just like Bugg and the rest of that lame-ass stable called the Supreme Dream Team...da Killah, along with
Devestation Inc. is gonna knock all of you straight on your _HEINIES_! Walavan, da Killah's gonna kick your ass just like El Nino wiped out all those towns which spells..
..._TROUBLE_... ..._FOR_... ..._YOU_!!! [ FBK finishes up his meal and washes it all down with the rest of his Coca
Cola. He then stands up and leads the camera man toward the front door. He opens up the storm door and unlocks the screen door. FBK then concludes...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Well, da Killah's had enough mic work for one day. All da
Killah is thinking right now is...phew, the ICW's still alive thank god. And then when da Killah read the Last Hoorah card, he just got even happier. Its about fifty miles from da Killah's hometown and he's got the home crowd behind him. Da Killah'll take any advantage he can get. Dragon, Floyd, Van Steele, and
Nyghstorm...da Killah can guaran-damn well-tee he's gonna beat the living
_SNOT_ right out of each and everyone of you and win the match and the right to face the International Champion a week later. Da Killah's all jacked up, he's pumped, he's _READY_ to kick some booty. Da Killah feels pity for his opponents because words can't describe the pain you're gonna feel when its all said and done. Da Killah's going to his extreme roots for the hometown crowd and he's gonna give them the best damn show they've ever seen because hell, FBK is the show, he's the ticket people pay to see and on March twentieth at the Last
Hoorah, it ain't gonna be no different. As for Walavan, just wait and see what happens to you pal, heh heh. Last Hoorah...Pillow, da Killah's gonna make sure he's gonna win for a going away present for you since he's such a giving person. So that means my four opponents, well...da Killah's gonna make all four of your lives living hells...
_FROM_ _BELL_ _TO_ _BELL_!!! [ FBK then grabs the camera man by the shirt and tosses him out the front door. The door is heard slamming shut but the camera goes to TV snow so nothing is seen... ] -Ryan -Handler of: FBK [EWWF, DSWA, ICW] "Sexy" Shane Barrons [Retired] ICQ#- [detail withheld] AIM-[detail withheld] & FBK Rulz "I'm not very opinionated...just always _RIGHT_!" :)
