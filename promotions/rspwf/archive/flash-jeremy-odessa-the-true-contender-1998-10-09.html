(flash) Jeremy Odessa "The True Contender"

9 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to an empty arena somewhere in America. In the middle of the building there is a wrestling ring set up with a lone figure standing against the ropes. The building looks old, like it hasn't been used in several years. The floor is covered in dust with a clear trail of footprints leading to the ring from the entrance. There are banners with pictures of various independant wrestlers that haven't been seen in a while. Several names jump out at you as familiar, "Exodus", "TUK", "Dread
Saxon", and "Jeremy Odessa". The image pans in on the ring and you realize that the man in the ring is none other than Jeremy Odessa himself...)
Odessa: I know it's not much but this building and I have a lot of history. It's the old EWA arena. I know it's been almost three years since the EWA closed it's doors but I can still hear the cheers from the seats and feel the heat from the lights. This is where I started, I was just a rookie with marginal wrestling skills but this became my home. A few of the guys are still around but most retired long ago. This very ring is where I won the
EWA TV Title for the first time. When I felt the ref raise my hand in victory I almost went into shock! I had done it, I had proved that I had what it takes. I had been told many times that I wouldn't make it or that
I'd never feel the gold around my waist but I knew in my heart that I would one day be a champion. I knew that if I worked hard enough and wanted it bad enough that I could realize my dreams. I could be champion.
It's been a long road from this place. I've got a few more scars and a lot of memories but I've also got experience. That's something that you can't buy but you have to earn!
Now I'm in the EWO. It's not that much different, the names have changed but the game is still the same. In a few days I'll be competing agains the ten best the EWO has to offer in a battle royal. I made it no secret that I was going for the World Title and I'm getting my shot. That's all that I ask for, just a shot! I'll prove to everyone that I am The True Contender!
(Odessa reaches down and picks a flier up off the floor...) EWA Presents... All Hallows Eve on PPV!!! Main Event: Exodus vs. Jeremy Odessa (TV Title Match) Odessa: I remember this match, I debuted my finisher "The Forearm Sleeper". When I slapped it on him he didn't know what hit him! That's exactly what they're going to say after this battle royal. This time I get my shot and I will prove myself once and for all!
(The image pans out leaving Odessa alone in the ring again...)
