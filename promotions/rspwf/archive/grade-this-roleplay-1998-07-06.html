[The screen pops into a blurry white and teh lens starts to focus. Once again we come upon the familiar house, now known to be owned by EWCF wrestler Vince. The camera man walks down the side walk and up to the house. THe door being unlocked he pushes it open and enters. He doesn't bother taking the time to notice all the pictures in the front hall way this time as he walks straight into the living room, only it's empty.
The TV is turned off, the couches are devoid of anything except left over pop corn here and there, and the lights aren't on. We hear some noise from off somewhere in the backgound. The camera man focus on another hallway that leads to some bedrooms and a back door. Just as he starts heading to them the guy we saw eariler known as Steve opens the back door and walks through the house.]
Camera Man: Why your...your...your Sti-- Steve: Shut yer mouth. [Steve walks past him and goes into the kitchen. He comes back a minute later with two glasses of soda and a big bowl of chips. He walks past the camera man and heads towards the back door.]
Camera Man: But your...your...your Sti-- Steve: I said shut yer mouth. Get yer ass out here and come get an interview if you want it.
[He walks out the door and the camera follows him. As the camera man comes outside he's astonished at the site he sees. To the right is a huge swimming pool with Diving Board, Cover, a polo goal, any thing you can think of. To the left is a half court of basketball where we see
Vince shooting a free throw. Past that to the left is a beuttifull rose garden with tomato plants and a an apricot tree. The camera man steps off the deck and walks over to them]
Camera Man: Nice place you got here Sti-- Steve: Did I say you could talk? No. So shut your damn mouth. [Vince laughs alittle then shoots the ball. It looks perfect all the way to the basket and goes in the rim but rolls out]
Vince: Dammit! I hate it when that happens! It always rolls out on me! Steve: That's what you get for taking the risk. Your ass is back to the starting line it's my turn now.
Vince: Yah yah yah. [Vince tosses him the ball and goes over to two nice lawn chairs and a small table to get the drink Steve left for him. Steve walks over to the the three point line lined up with the basquet. He starts to dribble the ball. He takes a shot and it sinks perfectly. He moves over to the corner of the court on the three point line and shoots again. Another sink.]
Vince: [seting the glass down and watching Steve sink a third one] Shit man! We're playing a real game next time.
Steve: This is a real game your just pissed your losing. [the camera man walks forward making his presence known more. Vince looks over to him then looks back at the hoop.]
Vince: yah just a second...this is his last shot. Steve: Allright now...watch this...this one is going straight in... [He shoots the ball up...and it airs. Vince starts to laugh enthusiastaclly] Vince: ha ha! yah yah yah...straight in huh? Steve: Shut up. I'll take the risk there's now way I'll lose it miss in a row. Vince; Try and hit the rim this time will yah? Steve: Like you did better Mr. Miss the free throw line 6 times already. Vince: Just shoot the ball. [Steve grabs the ball again and dribbles alittle. He shoots....swish!] Steve: Game point, you lose. Vince: Damn! Steve: So where's my money? Vince: I ain't paying you no damn money. [Steve laughs alittle then takes off his shirt. He walks over to the swimming pool and dives in. The camera looks back over at Vince.]
Vince: Damn...6 freethrows? [Vince mumbles to himself alittle then picks up the ball. He fakes a shot at the free throw line]
Vince: I swear I'm never playing around the world again. [As Vince is about to take another shot the camera man coughs loudly. Vince turns around] Vince: Oh yeah, you. Hold on a sec. [He shoots and misses yet again. He curses under his breath then walks over to the lawn chairs. He takes a sip and lies down on one of them.]
Vince: Ok now, after rewatching the press conference I noticed something. All the titles are gonna be defended Saturday except two. One, the cruiserweight title but that's not important cause we don't have enough cruisers. Two, the Tag Team Championships. The belts were just handed to it's former owners. M.O.C. or..uh.. Sinister
Alliance. Now I've got no problem with them being handed the belts. They were great champions they were the ONLY champions. But what I do have a problem with is them not defending it. If your the champs you gotta defend your belts and be fighting champions. This is the first card in at least a month and I think it's about time the Tag Team Championships got put on the line. Now I know Eric, or Dave, or whatever his name is,
I know he already has a match for SNS. He's gonna be a part of the Hell in a Cell which should be one of the most gruesome matches we've ever had not to mention it will be 1 on 1 on 1 not just a two man scuffle.
But...but since you and your partner are the Tag Team Champions you still gotta defend them no matter what the way I see it. So...now..after going on and on I'll just come right out and say it. We, Steve and Me, are challenging this SNS. The Bad Mouth Boyz vs the Sinister Alliance.
However... [He nods his head and puts up a hand to wait. He grabs his glass and takes a big long drink. He chugs it all the way down and puts it back on the table.]
Vince: However, since this would be our first tag match here and we haven't proved ourselves I'll be willing to do something. Give us any tag team, give us any pair of guys, and we'll beat them to prove ourselves. If we beat them then we get a title shot, of course if you'll dispense with the whole damn thing then we'll get it on this Saturday for the belts no questions asked. Your choice fellas your the champs, take your pick. This Saturday, Us vs You for the belts, or next Saturday if we manage to beat the two men you pick. It's your decision I'll leave it up to you.
[Steve rises out of the water and walks over to Vince. He pulls a towl out from under the empty chair and starts to dry off]
Steve: Did you challenge them yet? Vince: Yah. Steve: You didn't give them that little "We beat somebody to face you.." option did you? Vince: Yah I did. So what? Steve: Aw man, come on. If we don't give them the option then they'll just face us this Saturday.
Vince: It's already said and done, besides don't you want to prove yourself? Steve: I've done that for 15 years. Vince: Not here you haven't, that was in... Steve: Urrgh fine I guess we can let them decide. Their asses better accept to fight us cause if they don't thier pussies.
Vince: They'll accept, man I know those two thier good guys. Steve: Quit kissing thier ass, you kiss everyone's ass. You and your "I respect you cause you..bla bla bla".
Vince: Better than your "I'm better than everyone else bla bla bla" Steve: Well---hey wait.   [looking at camera man] Your still here? Get the hell out of here already he gave you your damn interview!
[He starts walking towards the camera man as he backs up...
