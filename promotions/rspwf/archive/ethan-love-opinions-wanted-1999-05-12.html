[Ethan Love] Opinions Wanted

12 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my app for my new character. I hope everyone will look over it and tell me what you think of it.
Wrestler Name (Original Names Only!): "Hate Child" Ethan Love Weight (190-600): 237 Height (5'10"-7'4"): 6'0" Age (18-50): 25 Hometown: Philadelphia, PA Theme (Song and Artist... Please no wrestler themes ex. "Stone Cold's Theme!) "Dead Bodies Everywhere" by Korn Ring Attire (Please be specific and include what your wrestler might wear to the ring, but not in the ring): To the ring he wears a multi-colored tie-dyed shirt with the words "Shut the F-ck Up!" on the back. He also has his long red tights on. When he wrestles, he takes his shirt off.
Personal Background (Basically a biography of the life story. Tell us a little about his family so we can talk about it during his matches and understand his gimmick more clearly): Ethan Love had a horrible childhood. For most of his childhood, he was neglected. When he did get attention, it was only when he got beaten by his parents. He grew up in a world of drugs and alcohol.
He partied every night. Finally, the drugs got to him, and he needed help. Well, nobody was there for him, which drove him into insanity. That is where he is now. Experience, History, Titles held: Not titles, one fed - BCW (Bloody Championship Wrestling) - got kicked out for various reasons. Appearance (what is your wrestler's build, defined, fat, skinny, etc.): He has a very big build for a man his size. He has dirty blonde curly hair down to the bottom of his ears. He has the PERFECT teeth, and you see them when he does his devilish/insane smile.
Gimmick (Please be original and have some fun... A fed can only have so many Stone Colds.): He is an insane SOB. He hates everyone, loves no one, hates himself, and hates the world. He changes his personality so many times, you never know what he's going to do.
Alignment (Face,Heel, or Neutral): SUPER HEEL! Wrestling style Luchador, Brawler, Technician... You know the rest!): All-Around, main style - power, but does alot of insane high risk maneuvers. Also, he loves going hardcore. Im sure a Love/Rico feud would be good. :-) Setup for the Finisher (What does your wrestler do before closing it out?!): "The Time Bomb" - Running Lyger Bomb (Running powerbomb with pin)
Finishing Move & Description (What does your wrestler call his finisher, and what actually is it? Submission... Pinning Combo?): What Love does, is beat his opponent with THEIR move. If he can't, he'll use the Time Bomb. MAKE SURE
THIS FINISHER HAPPENS! Foreign Object (What's your wrestler's favorite illegal weapon, if any? Does he use it in an extreme manor?): chair List 10-15 of your wrestlers favorite holds: 1. power bomb 2. running plancha onto the outside 3. twisting plancha 4. superplex 5. Tornado DDT 6. springboard flip onto the outside 7. moonsault 8. belly to belly 9. german suplex 10. missle dropkick 11. superkick 12. flying cross body ENTRANCE: (This is _very_ important and something I must have. The rules for this are below...)
JACK BRYCE - Introducing now ... from the city of BROTHERLY LOVE, Philadelphia,
Pennsylvania ... weighing in at 237 pounds ... here is "HATE CHIIIIIIILD"
ETHANNNNNN LOVVVVVVVVE! ["Dead Bodies Everywhere" by Korn blasts over the PA system as Ethan Love pops out into view. The fans immediately let a chorus of boos. Ethan Love has on a tie-dyed shirt on that says "Shut the F-ck Up!" on the back. He is laughing insanely as he walks down the aisle. Fans boo in his face and flip him off. He stops laughing and gets in a fan's face. He then screams "I HATE YOU!" and continues to the ring. He slides under the bottom rope and slouches down in the corner.]
-- Doug
