[Critique] Michael " 5000 " Watts

16 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, I've never done this before, but I've seen other people doin it, so I would like it alot if you would critique it, if it sucks, tell me, i don't mind, but if it does, please tell me some ways to make it better...
-------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------- Michael Watts stands near a shanty shack, that is barely standing, a light flickers inside, the light of a t.v., Watts is slowly walking towards it talking about his upcoming match with Outlaw...
Well I see it comes time for me and Outlaw to get inside that squared circle one more time. Outlaw, a man whom I have had many a past encounters with. And if my memory serves me correctly, Outlaw you were my first opponent, and we were the first ever main event of the Wild Out Wrestling promotion, and that night Outlaw you came out on top...then we aligned each other together,
Untouchables 2000, remember that Outlaw? But YOU MADE THE MISTAKE...you turned your back on me and the rest of U2G, and that wasn't even the last thing, just last week Outlaw, you let me down last week Outlaw in the xWs, you remember that, well if you don't, I do, and trust me redemption will be mine...just wait and see Outlaw...just wait and see!
Michael Watts nears the front door and he pushes it open, the creak causes the rodents lining the wall to scatter into corners, as the light inside the room reveals a t.v. and a rotting old wood chair...
Outlaw, you are a mere mortal man, you have more weaknesses than I possess...you are like this chair...I don't know that it is weak, it just appears that way. That chair may possible be able to hold well of my weight, but would you risk it Outlaw? Would you put all your faith into this chair holding you up? No, you wouldn't, the same goes for this match...I look at you,
I see a weakened man, a man who hasn't won a match in a very long time, then you see me, a man whom hasn't lost in four matches, if it came down to it, who would you put your faith into win? I rest my case Outlaw...you are the weaker man in this case, you may of been the best in the business, but Outlaw my friend, the business has changed, this isn't the same business you once dominated, this is my business now, I run things, you my friend cannot even dare to call yourself the best in the business anymore, have you seen the recent defeats you have been suffering? Well I have some tapes here of your recent matches...
Michael Watts puts in a video tape and it shows Outlaws match with Johnny eXtreme for the xWs World Heavyweight Title. It shows Outlaw being knocked out for a ten count after Johnny eXtreme hits ' Thats eXXXtreme ' from the second tier...then it cuts to Outlaw being pinned by All Hannan last week...
Now Outlaw, I do not know what kind of man you are, but I can probably bet you are not a very wise man, most men losing two or more matches in a row would extremely weaken their self-respect and their self-esteem, but Outlaw, I cannot assume on your behalf, but Outlaw, I can believe you are atleast in a weakened state of your former self...but I cannot underestimate you...I won't come out here and say that you are a talentless wrestler, you do not win championships being talentless, but you cannot say the same about me, despite what you may choose to believe, you know and the rest of the WoW knows and have seen the abilities I possess. You have all seen me beat the best that this promotion has to offer, and the streak will continue this weekend Outlaw. You got by me the last time we fought...you were the better man, I was unsure of my abilities, I was unsure of what I could do, but this time Outlaw, times have changed. The last time, was when you were in your " best in the business " mode, when you
WERE the best that this business could offer, but Outlaw, like Ric Flair, like
Hulk Hogan you don't know when it is time to retire, when it is time to hang up the boots...you are slippin, slipping into an oblivion Outlaw, and this weekend, you will slip into MY OBLIVION!
Watts lets out a wicked laugh and then speaks again... Outlaw, I have a little something for you...just sit back, in whatever you are sitting and listen...
Whisper to God your prayers to keep away the nightmares Then dare to look inside in that place where you hide Pause. So wipe away the tears and tell me all your fears to compare them all to mine you'll watch me cross the line Pause. Show me the wrists that you slit and tell me about your pit then look deep into my eyes and see where real pain lies Pause. I know you can't compare with my evil stare So silence to all your lies I'm deaf to all your cries Pause. For you I can't feel because your pain's not real You may think you're dead but it's all inside your head Pause. Let time remove your tears and get yourself away from here because it's you that I seek to feed the small & weak It's now things turn bleak... Pause, Watts then grabs a bottle of water, revealing a small circle, that has no dust on it, quite possibly the ONLY spot in this run down shack that has not been exposed to dust...he takes takes a swig, then caps the bottle and sets it down on the dusty floor...
Outlaw, you do not know what you are getting into this ring with...you have never faced a force of this magnitude, of this destructive carelessness...Outlaw, you're disregard for others in the past have led you to this point...your previous mistakes have led you to the end of your career...it is all going to come to a culmination this weekend, fate has led you to me
Outlaw. It's gonna be real simple this weekend, the self-proclaimed best in the business, who is far from that, taking on the young prodigy, the man whom many believe to be the next greatest wrestler...but I have non such expectations for myself...never expect things, you always get let down, Outlaw, you are expecting to end your career as the Wild Out Wrestling World Champion, but I am here to stop you, I am here to crush that dream, and that is exactly what I will do...
Now Outlaw, since we have had a flashback on your career, lets take a look at mine... Watts slips another tape into the VCR and it runs down a history of his past few matches...beating PaRaDoX and Kevin Kool, beating Jason Gelsomino, Dark
Side, then after a few minutes of tape it cuts to static...the sound drowns out to Watts's voice...
The inevitable cannot be stopped...Outlaw, there are rules to everything, you see, and there are rules to life...and you do not know how to cheat these rules...but I do, and I will. Rules are made to be broken, but there is a certain way to go about breaking these rules...and Outlaw, my friend, my one time conpadre...you do not know anything about these ways of living...but I will teach you, you will become my pet Outlaw...be prepared, because time will stop for you Outlaw, your career will not be the only thing ending this weekend...your life will come to a complete stop, it's almost a shame that you are going to be ending on such a sad note. I have recently spoken with Johnny eXtreme, and he had his heart set on ending your career, and being the lucky one that gets to send you out with a loss, but I see that honor has been bestowed upon me, and trust me Outlaw, I will take full advantage of this opportunity, you can believe that Outlaw...and just remember this one thing before you show up Saturday...COME PREPARED...
OR DON'T COME AT ALL! Watts stops talking and exits the little shack, a few seconds later the camera shot is outside, and nothing can be seen except a few trees and an owl...off in the distance a full moon shines down...the scene fades out while zooming in on the moon...
