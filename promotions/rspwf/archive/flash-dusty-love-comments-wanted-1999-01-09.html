[FLASH] - Dusty Love - Comments Wanted

9 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens up... We see a very empty plain. It appears to be a desert. There's hard sand on the ground for what appears to be miles. No trees, no bushes, no grass. Just desert. The ground is decorated with cracks because of the dry land. The sky is blue and the clouds are puffy and white. Just like cotton. A beautiful day, is what it is. The sun is shining down upon the tan colored ground. We hear the wind blow slightly. It's so peaceful and calm.
We hear nothing. Not even a bird chirping. The camera begins to turn very slowly and steadily.]
[Now we see a bright, yellow sign in the distance. It's far away, but just close enough to notice that it's there. The camera then begins to zoom in, slowly but steadily. No rush. The object gets closer and closer by the seconds. Finally, we see it up-close and personal. It's a sign. In the middle of nowhere. It's a diamond shape, yellow, "DANGER" sign. The camera is zoomed in so close that you can't see beside or around the sign. It's right up against the screen. The camera then begins to fall back. Slowly, it moves away from the sign.]
[As the camera backs away, we see a man standing beside the sign. He wasn't there before. But now the camera stops. It now moves and focuses in on the man. As we look at him for a second, we recognize who it is. The face is unmistakeable. The sunglasses are one of a kind. The clothes are very nice and noticeable. And his long, sandy-blonde hair is flowing down to his shoulders. His chin, covered with small, stubbley whiskers. His mouth is shaped in that of a million dollar smile. This man is none other than...
. . . " D A N G E R O U S " D U S T Y L O V E . . .] [He stands his ground right beside the sign. He's wearing a white shirt that has the words "TOMMY HILFIGER" written across the chest. Over that shirt, he's wearing an unbuttoned button-up Hilfiger shirt. It's blue and white, in color.
He's wearing a pair of some-what baggy Hilfiger blue jeans. And at the bottom, he's wearing a pair of Timberland hiking boots. His sunglasses support the round lense frame, in the brand of Mossimo.]
[Dusty Love stands there, looking into the camera with a smile etched out on his handsome face. After a few seconds, he begins to speak...]
DL: Read th' sign. "DANGER"... Sounds like someone I know. [Smiles.] If ya' don't believe me, just stay tuned to th' Eye Inn Dubbya Eye programs, and you'll see exactly what I'm talkin' about. But lemme just tell ya' right off th' bat.
You're lookin' at 'im. [Dusty smiles and pauses. Then, he continues...] DL: On January 12, in Boston's Fleet Center, I kow a very unlucky man that'll find out what I'm talkin' 'bout, first hand. And that's you,
"American Hater" Seth Maxo. "American Hater?" Yeah, I know what
'cha mean. I feel th' same way sometimes. Especially when I'm wrestlin' in Boston. [Smiles.] Seth Maxo, whoever ya' are... I just wanna let 'cha know one thing. You're th' next victim in "The Love
Man's" way to success. And even more-so now. Why? [Smiles.]
'Cause I face ya' at ma' Hot Spot debut. And listen carefully and comprehend what I'm about ta' say...
I don't lose on _ANY_ debut. And especially to a guy like _YOU_. But fans, set those VCR's because "The Love Man" will be at Hot Spot, and ya' never know what to expect when I'm around.
[Love smiles and winks.] DL: Th' more I think about the Eye Inn Dubbya Eye, th' more excited I get. I'm in here with guys like King Apples, Tony Houston, Eddie Jacobs... Guys that I've heard all ma' life that I couldn't beat. But now that
I'm here, I finally have th' chance to show 'em wrong. 'Cause there's one thing about bein' in here with all these guys...
I _KNOW_ I can beat 'em. And th' fact of th' matter is just this... I
_WILL_ beat 'em, just as fast as I get th' opportunity.
Dusty Love is here... And he means binnis'. So, this Tuesday on Hot
Spot, Dusty Love debuts. And everyone'll be tuned in ta' see it. And believe this, I _WON'T_ disappoint.
[Dusty Love delivers a wicked smile.] DL: Seth Maxo, Eye Inn Dubbya Eye as a whole... "The Love Man" is comin' ta'... . . . L O V E . . . . . . Y O U . . . . . . T A ' . . . . . . D E A T H . . . [Dusty Love smiles to the camera again. Dusty Love turns around and begins to slowly walk away.]
