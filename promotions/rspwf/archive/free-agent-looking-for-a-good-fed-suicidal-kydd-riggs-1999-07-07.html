[ Free Agent ] Looking for a good fed. [ "Suicidal" Kydd Riggs ]

7 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys, I've been in E-Wrestling for about 3 years now, I am not the greatest, but I work hard and RP when needed. Here is a sample, and I am not looking for anything specific, just a good all around fed, with a good front office.

Bloodbath...]
[ A.J. ] "Fury by the ropes, grabs Riggs by the hair. Riggs, quick to pull Fury to the floor, and both men exchange blows. Lefts and rights by both men, and Riggs takes the advantage. Riggs whips Fury into the steel steps CRASH!!! Fury is in a heap on the ground. He may have dislocated his shoulder. Riggs throws the steel steps away, and picks up Fury. Riggs has Fury by the hair. Don't tell me! Ringpost shots by Riggs onto Fury? Yes, it is. There's one, and another, and another, and another, one more for Fury, and he falls to the ground. Fury has been split open."
[ Tom ] "You would be too if you just had your head rammed into the steel ringpost five times. There's no give in that ringpost A.J."
[ A.J. ] "Geez, Tom, thanks for pointing that out to me. Riggs is in control, as he throws Fury back into the ring. Riggs whips Fury into the ropes. Flying back heel kick by Riggs catches Fury right in the mouth. Riggs to the top, coming off, and landing with a diving headbutt. Cover by Riggs 1 . . . . . 2 . . . .
. and Fury with a kickout just in the knick of time. Riggs badmouthing the official for a slow count."
[ Tom ] "He's really laying into him. He needs to watch for Fury, because he's making his way to his feet."
[ A.J. ] "Fury is up. He turns Riggs around, and is leveling him with lefts and rights. Whip to the ropes, clothesline by Fury. Fury is on a roll now. Off the ropes goes Riggs, and he's caught with a big boot from Fury. Fury looks to be setting Riggs up for a piledriver. Riggs drops down, and low blows Fury.
What a cheap shot! Riggs up, has Fury for a Double arm Underhook powerbomb, and he nails it. Here's the cover 1 . . . . . 2 . . . . . 3 ! The rookie has upset the veteran here tonight in the first round of the World title tournament."
[ Tom ] "The Suicidal one showed that he is for real here tonight. He looks as if he's ready for the Champ right now. There's no stopping Kydd Riggs."
[ Promo scene ] [...The camera fades into a recorded picture of Kydd Riggs...] [ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "NcWa, I told you so. You all didn't believe me, but I proved myself, and I am one step closer to carrying that NcWa heavyweight gold!"
(¯`·.¸.·­» End ProMo «­·.¸.·´¯) [...Fades in...] [ Scene ] [...The parking lot of an NcWa arena in Tampa Bay, Florida. It's a warm, dark night, with a sprinkle of rain here and there. It is the night after
Bloodbath, and the wrestlers are leaving after a long house show. You see each wrestler get in there own car and drive away. All is now quiet, then you hear a screech of tires, as a white, 99' Camaro come speeding around the corner, and come to a stop about fifteen feet in front of the camera. As you look into the car, you see that it is the newcomer to the NcWa, Tim Beck. As the camera focuses in, you hear a door open in the back, and two people talking and laughing. As the camera pans around, you see Ace Curtis, and Kydd Riggs walking, each holding there bags. They walk over to the car, and Curtis gets in. Riggs signals for them to hold on a minute, then turns to the camera...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "NcWa, I told you all, didn't I? Fury, don't feel so bad, you underestimated the rookie, that's all. It could have happened to anyone. But don't worry,
I'll be more than happy to grant you a rematch someday. But now I have to look past this win, and now look at my next match, with none other than Tyrant. But first, let me respond to what that guy BeaR had to say. BeaR, don't look at it, as if I cost you the win, because it looks like everyone was out to get you last night. Not just me. But if it makes you feel any better, after I win the world title, you can have a shot, if it means that much to you. [ chuckles ]
Now I know you are going to try and get me back, but look at it this way BeaR, if you come down in my match with Tyrant and cause me to lose, you will probably end up in a match with him for the world title, _but_ you will never, ever win, because I will not let you. See, if you screw me over, I will be there, and hound you at every chance I get, and I will never let you get anywhere. On the other hand, if you let our match take place, and let it go fairly, I will give you a fair shot at the title, if I win, and I will promise it will be one on one, with no one to interfere. Think about that, ok?"
[ Scene ] [...Riggs walks over to the back door of the car and opens it, he proceeds to put his bag in there, but before getting in, he looks back up to the camera...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Oh yea, Tyrant. I almost forgot about you! [ sarcastic laugh ] Guys, this may take a little longer than I had planned, so you think you can hold on?"
[ Scene ] [...Beck shakes his head, and turns off the car. Curtis reaches over, and turns the key a bit, just enough to turn on the radio. He flips thru some stations, until he comes to one that is playing "YMCA" by the Village People.
They each laugh, then sit back in their seats...] [ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Now Tyrant, you have a lot of people to thank for you being in the position you are in right now. The only reason you are even in this match with me, is because you have friends, and BeaR has a lot of enemies. But see, things are going to be a little different for you this time around. I am not going to be so easy. Twenty people are not going to come down and jump me. See, when I wrestle you, I am going to have those two men in my corner. [ Points to Beck and Curtis ] So you can tell everyone in WoA, and that guy Matt Hardgrove to step off, and let you take the beating fairly. Because if anyone should come down in this match, there will be hell to pay. And if you think it's all good, just because you out number us, again you are dead wrong. See, just because you only see two men at my side, doesn't mean there are not more. What I am getting at here is very simple, if you think you are going to get an easy cheap win, you're wrong. The bottom line is, you're going to have to fight me one on one, and get the win that way. Like I said to Fury, if you are going to beat me, you're going to have to work hard for that win."
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Now Tyrant, I heard you spew out some words of wisdom before, but as I paid more attention, I realized you were just rambling on about this, that and the other thing making no sense what so ever. Now, just for the sake of my interview, I will try and clear something you had said, up. For starters, you say you have been pushed harder than I have, and you've had more competition.
[ chuckles ] You then say that Fury is one of the best in the NcWa, but also say that he was an easy win for me. [ smirks ] OK, I'm confused already. So, what you are saying is that Fury is one of the best here in the NcWa, and I got an easy win over him. Therefor, I am a top star? [ chuckles ] Why thank you,
I am much obliged. But maybe that's not what you meant, who knows, right? I don't think anyone in that arena understood a word of what you were saying.
Try finishing your sentences, before you start a new one. [ laughs ] You sounded like you had cerebral palsy. Now Tyrant, you also said that this is my return to an NcWa ring. What the hell are you talking about? I've never wrestled here in the NcWa before. And why is it you seem to think I am The
Reaper? Do you think I lost over one hundred pounds, and four inches in three months? [ chuckles ] Dude, seriously, what medicine are on? Maybe you should lay off it a bit, or stop hittin' that crack pipe. And you say I don't what have what it takes to become a world champion? How the f[[beep]]k would you know? You've only watched me wrestle from the back, once. Ya' know, it seems to me you have some mental problems that need to be dealt with."
[ Scene ] [...Riggs laughs, as do the men in the car. He then steps into the car and sits down. Beck once again turns the key, and you hear the car start up.
Riggs closes the door, then rolls down the window...] [ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "Oh and Tyrant, you say I have no friends or allies, because no one wants to back a pathetic, worthless wrestler? Then what the hell do you think these two men are? My ass buddies? I think not. But think what you want, I'd rather not argue with you. It would probably confuse you more than you already are.
Well, I've got better things to do, rather than sit around here and waste my breath on you. See ya' soon Tyrant."
[ Scene ] [...Beck hits the gas, and the car turns around and starts to speed away, before the brake light suddenly come on, and the car backs up. Riggs then stick his head out the window...]
[ "SuiCiDal" KyDd RiGgS ] "By the way, and before I forget. Matt Hardgrove. [ pauses ] Kiss my ass!" [ Scene ] [...Riggs pulls his head back in the window, and rolls up the window. Again,
Beck slams on the gas and the car speeds away, and around the corner...]
[...Fades out...]
