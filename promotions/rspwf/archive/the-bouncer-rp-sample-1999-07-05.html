The Bouncer RP sample

5 July 1999

I've also got another thing that I need help on, if you can possibly give me suggestions on a theme music for this guy it would be helpful
[[The cameraman walks into an alley way of a popular street club. Outside the club is a long line of excitied party-goers on who are talking about nothing else except on getting in. While they got closer some groan when they saw the door and the man next to it. A 6'7" man who seems 400 pounds and fat. He appears to be chinese with a goatee and mustache. He was wearing a tex and a silk shirt with a bowtie. He wore nice pants and shoes He keeps his arms folded and looks on the party goers dismissing them if they weren't on the list or were underage. Some people even tried to bribe him and he picked them up and threw them from the door to the end of the alleyway. Some of the crowd didn't even try to get in as if they knew who this person was. The cameraman walks closer to the man and he puts his hand into the cameraman's chest]]
[Man] Sorry, you're not on the list and no video camera are allowed into the
Star Club. [The cameraman showed the sign of the wrestling federation to the man and he seemed to got the idea]]
[Man] I'm sorry. It's just it's another day for the Bouncer. That's right, I'm
The Bouncer. The best in the business and most famous for these punks in line.
They know they can't muscle me into giving them access, and they KNOW bribes never work. But lately I have grown tired of this business. Sure the pay is good, and I get to kick someone's ass around once and again. But, I've set my sights to a bigger goal. It appears that some wrestlers here aren't in the list. That they somehow snuck in and this federation needs a bouncer to give them the boot. The boot out and so that the REAL wrestlers will some more elbow room. And I'm happy to do that job for them. I get to kick someone's butt around for a half hour or so and I get paid for it. The more the beating the more paycheck. Now that's sweet. Hold on.
[[A man in dreadlocks comes to the front and tries to get in but the bouncer places his hand on his chest and stops him in his tracks]]
[The Bouncer] Are you in the list? [Man] Hell no, punk. Now get out of my way before I have place you out of my way. [The Bouncer] You're new aren't you. [Man] And you're dead! [[The man punches him in the stomach and grabs it in pain. The Bouncer laughs and grabs the man's shirt]]
[The Bouncer] You're new. I'll go easy on you. [[The lifts the man up and throws him to the street where he runs from getting hit by cars and runs into a parked bus]]
[[Next a woman waits in the front and the bouncer checks the list and sees she's on it]] [The Bouncer] Welcome to the Star Club Miss. [[Suddenly a bunch of guys try to bum rush their way in and The Bouncer closelines them all down with one swift stroke]]
[The Bouncer] Sorry about that. Now where were we. Oh yes. The wrestlers. Well
I've got something to say to all of them directly. You're not in the list and you're in the fed? You're gonna get the boot
