My first roleplay....feedback desired

17 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The fans are disappointed to see the smallish man in the tuxedo walk out with the microphone. They begin to boo loudly, full aware of what's coming next. The man steps through the ropes, shoves the real ring announcer out of the way, and begins his speil.)
Andy Winger: "Ladies and gentlemen, and I use those terms VERY loosely....allow me to introduce someone who really needs no introduction.
He is a star of stage, screen, and television....he has more money than any of you rednecks will EVER see in your entire pathetic, useless lives....he is married to a woman so beautiful, that she makes the fat gloublous bulges of cellulite that you toothless inbreds call wives look inhuman....and, oh yes, lest we forget that he is the GREATEST professional wrestler in the industry today. From a four story mansion overlooking the beautiful Pacific
Ocean in pristine Santa Monica, California, weighing in at precisely 227.28 pounds, he is the man, the myth, the legend, and....oh yes, the FRANCHISE,
"The Predator" Jeff Monroe!!
(The fans are, by now, booing to the point where nothing else can be heard. The projectiles start flying as Jeff Monroe makes his way out, with "Ironman" Mike Crawford leading the way. Crawford attempts to use his body as a shield to protect his boss from the flying debris as he yells for the other security guards to "do their f-cking jobs" and remove the unruly fans from the arena. Monroe's stride is quick and determined as he skips through the ropes. Winger hands him the mic, and the "Franchise" begins by clearing his throat.)
Monroe: (sniffing in a deliberate fashion) "Do you smell something? I smell something...." Crawford: "What would that be?" Monroe: "Wait....it's coming to me now....(snaps his fingers) I've got it! It's the putrid odor of mediocrity emanating from the locker room!" Crawford: "Really? Hm. I thought it was Kevin Marshall's corpse." Monroe: (glaring angrily at his bodyguard) "Now, Mike....you know that's a very sensitive subject. What happened to Kevin was a senseless tragedy, a horrible crime obviously committed by a group of drunk teenagers, or perhaps an elderly woman who thought she was on her way to the local Safeway."
(Winger, in the background, begins to hum the chorus from "Little Old Lady
From Pasadena," then begins stifiling laughter. The camera pans around the arena, catching signs reading "Monroe's A Murderer!" and "Predator 3:16 says
'I just ran your ass over!'")
Monroe: What surprises, nay, dismays me is the fact that there are people out there, people in the locker room, people on the street, even people in the AWF's front office, that still believe I was the perpetrator! Now, while it's true that *sniff* Kevin and I.....didn't....see eye to eye...."
(Crawford puts his arm around Monroe, who is seemingly fighting back tears. The boos are at a high; no one's buying the charade....) Monroe: "....but there is no way that I, a God-fearing Christain, could possibly take the life of another man. Case closed, end of story, the funeral was yesterday. I had my secretary send flowers. Honest...."
(Boos) Monroe: "Now, onto other matters at hand....let's see....I wrestle Dan Karma this Saturday. Dan Karma....Dan Karma....who...."
(Crawford whispers something into Jeff's ear) Monroe: "Oh, yeah, Quadro's butt-buddy. I knew I had heard his name somewhere....did you say he was 398 pounds, Mike?"
(Jeff's stooge nods) Monroe: "Oh, goodie. So I get the satisfaction of knowing that I kicked the snot out of someone nearly twice my size. Wonderful. So, to sum it all up,
I, as my good friend O.J. Simpson said, am absolutley 100% not guilty, and...."
(At that moment, The Hornet, of all people, comes up from behind Monroe and
Crawford and hits Jeff in the back with a double-axehandle. Monroe breifly drops to one knee, then pops right back up, just in time to see Crawford pounce into action, smacking the masked jobber in the back of the neck with his ever-present billyclub. Crawford tosses Hornet in the corner, and Monroe walks up to him, rubbing his back.)
Monroe: "Edward, my good man....I'm....I'm shocked. What on Earth would possess you to attack me from behind? More pressingly, since when did you grow the balls to pull off a sneak attack?"
Hornet: "I....I....I was just gonna....gonna collect the bounty....I'm sorry.....I'm...." Monroe: "Tsk, tsk, tsk....I thought better of you than that....you know,
Eddie, I never had anything bad to say about you....you're a good man, and a damn fine jobber. (At this point, Crawford and Winger pick up Hornet and hold him, one on each arm. Monroe is headed outside the ring.) But, I never in a million years thought you'd be so stupid as to attack me from behind. I really don't want to have to do this....(Monroe now has a steel chair; he tosses it in the ring)....I really don't like doing this. Eddie, I'm sorry."
(Jeff waffles Hornet in the head with the chair. He falls to the mat in a heap.) Monroe: "I really (stomp), really (stomp), really (stomp) hate to do this
(stomp), but you just (stomp) have to (stomp) learn where not to (stomp)
stick your nose (stomp). Again, I'm sorry, Ed...." (Monroe slaps on the Predator Armbar. Hornet's already out cold, but the pain is undeniable; his arm hyper-extends to the point where his right elbow is due north of his left ear. Jeff has it cinched tight, and is violently yanking upwards. Monroe smiles as he hears joints popping, and the dim hum of muscles tearing. He knows he's done some major damage. After about 25 seconds, he lets go. Hornet's right arm falls to the mat, straight out, perpendicular to his body. It's dislocated, to say the least....)
Monroe: "But some people need to be taught lessons. It's sad, really. Take that piece of trash out of my ring. In fact, let's blow this whole joint....I have a headache from the smell."
(Monroe and company begin to exit the ring. Crawford gets in one last cheap shot on the invalid jobber, taking the top of the steel chair and driving it down straight into Hornet's right shoulder. If there wasn't serious muscle damage before....Crawford races in front of his boss, just in time to intercept a bottle of Naya that had Jeff's head right in it's scope. Mike's soaked....he doesn't care. He's well paid. The trio exit the arena.)
