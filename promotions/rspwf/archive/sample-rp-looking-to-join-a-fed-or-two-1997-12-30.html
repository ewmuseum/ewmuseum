sample RP. looking to join a fed or two!

30 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is a sample RP from one of my wrestlers. I am looking to join a quality fed. or two, maybe more. I have been role playing with this guy since 1992. I started with him in the Play-By-Mail fed. IWA, and have since moved on to the PBEM's. I have been away from the e-mail wrestling circuit for a while, and am looking to get back in to it!!! If you have a fed., and provide a quality fed., please contact me via e-mail at [detail withheld] , or [detail withheld] , and I will get back with you asap! ************************************************************************** ************* [The lights go low, and "LaGrange" by ZZTop plays over the loud speaker. Stunning Steve Blanchard walks to the ring, as the fans boo him all the way. SSB slowly climbs in the ring, and parades around for several moment. He then calls for the microphone to be brought to him.] That's right! It's Stunning Steve Blanchard again you jerks. I have a few things to address. First, I have seen a lot of wrestlers come out here to this ring, and talk about being the best, but open your eyes boys, you are looking at the best right now!! Now, my partners, Alex Anderson, and the rest of the CARTEL are ready for some serious action. Our plan is to win at all cost! Like i've always said, the end always justifies the means. The bottom line is quite simple. There are winners and there are losers. The CARTEL are winners and have been from the start. The CARTEL does what has to be done, and if your not part of the CARTEL, then you're the enemy!! [the fans boos become louder than ever. the chant of Blanchard sucks rings out from the audience.] Shut you rstinking mouths, you low life scum! No body cares what the hell you think about the Stunning One!!! It has been said before that I am an ass, but I never claimed to be a nice guy. I'll beat your face in to the mat, and grind your nose in to the pavement. Hell, i'll kick the living crap out of your daddy if he gets in my way! [the crowd again lets out a series of boos, and another rousing chant of Blanchard sucks, intermixed with the couple voices screaming SSB, SSB!] I told you to shut the hell up! There ain't nothing that is going to stand in my way to the top, and if you want to be the first to fall, then just step up to the plate! Remember that when you step in front of a moving train, you get run over, and the CARTEL is the fastest moving train you'll ever see! 'Til next time i'm still Stunning Steve Blanchard and your still scum! ["LaGrange" again plays over the sound system. SSB struts around the squared circle, and slowly exits the ring. Blanchard walks to the back even more cocky than when he arrived.] ************************************************************************** ****************** I am very serious about re-joining the world of e-mail based wrestling, and can't wait to hear from some great feds.! I have several other wrestlers that I Role Play (Heartthrob Dan Corvette, Rick Ringlord, The Almighty Cain, & The Evil Soultaker)! Hope to hear from you soon!!!! Brian
