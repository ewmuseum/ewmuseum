============ SMOkE ============ This guy is your basic undertakerish type of character I guess. He's fairly big (6'10" 349lbs)and I usually play him out as sort of an honerable warrior. I started out a website for him once upon a time (even with a stolen quoat from the movie 'Blade') and it's at
http://www.geocities.com/SouthBeach/Club/9062/index.htm if you wanted to check it out, anyways, In this RP SMOkE had kinda cracked up because he lost an inferno match, He's not normally as aggressive, but still kind of quick to get angry(I like this RP only cause I had SMOkE not even say anything)...
*********** (The scene opens in the ECW head office. The camera focuses on the doors of the large building as a dark shadow appears over it. * Smash * the glass in the doors shatters, with shards of glass flying inside the building with amazing amounts of force. SMOkE steps through the now broken door. His Dull black boots making a crunching sound on the broken glass, His huge muscles bulging against is tight black jacket, His fist clenched tightly, and his small dark sunglasses shroud his eyes from the view of the camera. On one side of his face, Burn marks, blistering and contorting skin. However the scorching from the flames seems minimal compared to the rage that seems to be screaming from his hard emotionless expression. The people working in the building have come to a stand still, as if Satan himself was walking towards them. They stare as SMOkE walks past them, some cringing, others trembling with fear. Another cameraman steps into view and walks in front of SMOkE, seemingly trying to get a better look at his face. SMOkE ignores the man at first, continuing his purposeful walk, However the cameraman seems a little to brazen for SMOkE’s liking and he is swiftly taken off his feet and into unconsciousness with one swing of SMOkE’s mighty hand. Shortly Mr. Gower can be seen walking into view, talking to some of the others who have swapped their pride for a suit and a large paycheck. They look up from their papers that seem insignificant in the eyes of a warrior such as SMOkE, and stand completely still, paralyzed by fear and intimidation. Mr. Gower steps forward as walks up to SMOkE, and even he seems unwilling to deal with the fury of the warrior…)
{Mr. Gower} “SMOkE, bad luck about your match with X-plosin.” (SMOkE moves closer to Mr. Gower. He removes his dark sunglasses from his face and the true extent of the burns on SMOkE’s face can be seen. SMOkE’s eyes blaze with the power of uncontrolled rage and the hatred for mere words that can not solve a situation that SMOkE has been placed in. He looks into
Mr. Gower's eyes, and even the man who is in control of the CWI seems a little more than disturbed by the expression on SMOkE’s face…)
{Mr. Gower} “SMOkE, I know you must not be happy with what has happened, But we must look forward. Lusty has challenged you to a match at Deep Cover, I need you to sign the papers…”
(One of the men in the suit steps forward, his arms fully extended holding a clipboard with the match papers on it. SMOkE’s emotionless yet Aggressive look doesn’t move from Mr. Gower's face as he takes the papers from the intimidated man in the suit, who quickly walks away from the menacing man who he just handed the papers to. SMOkE stands up straight; he towers over everyone in the building as he takes a small envelope knife from a near by desk and extends the index finger on his right hand. He put the Clipboard onto the desk and drives the knife into his finger, a small amount of blood rushing out. He drops the knife onto the floor and picks the clipboard up, and with blood as cold as a dead winter SMOkE scribbles something onto the paper, not once removing his aggressive look from Mr. Gower. He throws the clipboard at one of the men wearing the suits and turns and walks away. His walk is strong and confident, yet aggressive and menacing as he turns a corner and disappears from the camera’s view…)
{Man In Suit} “Umm, Mr. Gower sir…” (The man holds up the clipboard, and the camera begins to focus on it. Across the whole page scribbled in blood is the words ‘I Quit Match… SMOkE…’
Mr. Gower looks at the piece of paper with a worried expression…)
{Mr. Gower} “If that guy wants it an ‘I Quit’ Match, you book it. I’m a little worried about what he is capable of.”
{Man In Suit} “What do you mean by that Sir?” {Mr. Gower} “Look at the tapes from when he was in that cheap ass backyard fighting thing in Thailand, He was a fighting machine, No-one could stop him. I thought he might have lost a step since then, but now, I’m worried he might take it too far and take someone out.”
{Man In Suit} “Take someone out? Like seriously injure them?” {Mr. Gower} “Worse than that… Much worse I’m afraid to say…” (Mr. Gower begins to walk away as the man in the suit looks at the message written in blood. He looks towards the camera, His face looking like the apocalypse is coming and he hasn’t told his family he loves them…)
{Man In Suit} “God help us all…” (The man walks away slowly as the scene fades to black…) ********* Ok So there's one of my guy's, On with the next one.
