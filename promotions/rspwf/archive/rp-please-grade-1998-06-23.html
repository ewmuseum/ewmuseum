RP... Please Grade! :)

23 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: Cameras show the WWO Offices in downtown Atlanta, Gerogia. The cameras pan to inside the building and up to the 34 floor. We see a huge wooden door with a gold plate that says, "WWO BOARD ROOM 1. PRIVATE". A tall, slender, dark haired man steps in front of the door and into camera view. He is wearing a pair of pleated khakis, a white tshirt and a green and grey sweater vest. He is holding a microphone and nods towards the camera]
Steve Hardison: Hi and Welcome to the WWO Office buildings in Atlanta, Gerogia. We are LIVE and in color! I've been sent by the Pro Wrestling Machine to get some comments from WWO Owner, Erin McCoys for our new weekly show, The Grind.
With the recent "Takeover" of the WWO by former ASW wrestlers and the Chanel feud not giving the smallest sign of dying down, Erin McCoys has more to comment on then ever. Let's see if we can get some words with her. Let's go.
[Steve slowly pushes the door open and ushers the camera man to go inside. We see Erin McCoys sitting in a chair and going over some papers with VPs Dave
Regan and Matt Betchel behind her. They don't even notice Scott walk in. Erin is wearing a red slip dress with spagetti straps, black lace trim and some white roses embroidered into the collar. Her hair is pushed back with a red silk headband. Dave Regan is wearing a conservative dark blue suit with a grey tie. Matt Betchel is wearing a black suit with a matching black leather tie.
Erin contiunes to flip through the papers and talk to her VPs] Erin McCoys: Yeah, I like what I saw but where will he fit in with the company? He comes highly recommended and surived the Lion Den's training school. Dave Regan: Is he worth the money he's asking for. That's the question. Matt Betchel: With or without him, the WWO isn't going to suffer. Steve Hardison: Ahem. Erin McCoys: [without looking up] Just leave the coffee on the table Janice. We'll be ordering lunch in about 15 minutes. Steve Hardison: Miss McCoys, it's Steve Hardison from the Pro Wrestling Machine. I was wondering if I could get an interview with you for our new show,
The Grind. Dave Regan: Are we live? Steve Hardison: Yes sir. Erin McCoys: [smirks] Well I guess I don't have much of a choice then right? Gentlemen, why don't you go ahead and order and I'll be out to join you in a few. Get me the usual.
[Dave Regan and Matt Betchel leave. Erin shoves a rolling chair over towards
Steve and earges him to have a seat. Erin rests her hands underneath her chin and gives an innocent smirk]
Erin McCoys: So, what do you want to know? Ask away Mr. Hardison cause the ball is in your court...... I bet you practised this moment in your head a thousand times and now that your actually living the moment, your mind has gone blank. I can tell by the look on your face you have no clue what you gotten yourself into. Your a rookie so I'm gonna be nice. [Erin slips him a piece of paper]
Here's a list of topics that were going to be discussed at the next WWO Press conferance but you have just gotten first bids on them. Call it a gift from
God.
Steve Hardison: [taking a few seconds to glance over the statement] Miss McCoys, the WWO speaks only briefly about the so called "takeover" by former
ASW Wrestlers.
Erin McCoys: Your observant. Your gonna need that. There's a reason for that like there is a reason for everything I do. Right now, these former ASW workers aren't much of a concern to us. The only damage they really did was try to man handle a commentator and like anyone in this sport knows, that's the quickest and easiest way for a new guy to get some much needed heat for himself. Vincent
Estacio tried it with Amanda Wells but it backfired on him. Now we see guys like Jack Knife, Folklore and Kraven. If it was not for the WWO, they'd still be weeping for their beloved ASW. And as far as the whole masks go, I don't care. It could be Dennis Rodman under one of them for all I care just so they are entertaining. People are expecting me to be like other female Promotion owners and get all emotional and upset that people are talking bad about the
WWO. Like I've said from the beginning, I'm not like every other woman you'll ever come across.
It's the oldest trick in the book to come into a federation and start bad mouthing. It's called cheap heat and those boys are using it for all it's worth because they know real men like Troy Majors, Eric Draven, Quicksilver, Sledge,
Iron Man are in the WWO and enjoying their stay. I think once those boys compare the differences between the WWO and their other employers, they will understand, business wise, what's a better choice.
Steve Hardison: What were your reacts to Vincent Estacio's challenging you to a match? Erin McCoys: I laughed hysterically. It just shows the difference between the
WWO and the ASW. In the ASW, stuff like that would demand attention. Here, it just gets laughed at. This isn't some circus, this is a running, operating, business and company. Save those side show antics for somewhere else.
Steve Hardison: What about the rumor that lawyers from the ASW camp have contacted the WWO about stopping the discussion of ASW on WWO TV?
Erin McCoys: [looks towards the table and smiles] I am not permitted to talk about that in detail because of fear of legal action but I will say this. The
ASW was brought into the WWO by it's own wrestlers. They tried to take over and they expected us to become all scared. Not here honey. I don't want this turned into an all out war between quote, unquote "Team WWO" and "Team ASW" but if it happens, I'm going to do whatever I can to keep it entertaining. Only thing I'm worried about is keeping the entertainment value the fans of the WWO are accustomed to at the same great level it's always been. The last thing I want is a WWO\ASW War because it's a war the ASW has no chance in winning simply because, it no longer exists.
Steve Hardison: Will the former ASW Wrestlers can the same fair treatment everyone else gets?
Erin McCoys: Of course they will because they signed WWO contracts. WWO contracts by law force us to give the same treatment we would give anyone else.
I have a salty past when it comes to fair treatment in federations. I know what it's like to be screwed and honestly, some people do deserve to be screwed over but a part of me won't allow myself to do it.
Steve Hardison: Well what business inside the WWO itself? You seem to be getting ready for a new PPV and it's the WWO's First attempt at hardcore.
Erin McCoys: Well I personally never liked the whole hardcore style but all we can do is give it a try. If it works, great. If it doesn't, well we'll learn from our mistake.
Steve Hardison: What about Chanel? Erin McCoys: [very serious] Sigh.. the timeless question comes up again. Chanel is not going away. I"m not either. Something has got to give and I don't know what. When I find out, the world will too. As for right now, I have more important things to worry about.
Steve Hardison: More important things to worry about then Chanel? Erin, think logically. She is a huge force looming against you.
Erin McCoys: Mr. Hardison, I am expected to be and do many things at once. A wrestler is not one of them. My whole feuds have to wait for the good of the company. I'm not here to push myself but the talents of others. I was so prepared to take a backseat to everything in the WWO but Chanel brought be out and dragged me right into the center. What I'm doing right now is trying to trag myself out of the mess I was brought into. Now is that such a problem?
Steve Hardison: No but.... Erin McCoys: I thought so. I believe you got what you wanted. Thank you very much Stevie. Janice will show you the way out. As for me, it's now time for my lunch. Good day.
[Erin McCoys leaves the room to an astounded Steve Hardison] Steve Hardison: Uhh... Back to you Greg [Fade]
