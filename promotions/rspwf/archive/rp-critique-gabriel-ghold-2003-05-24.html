[RP Critique] Gabriel Ghold

24 May 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*** SARS... despite what some would try to deny, this was the topic of discussion on may people's minds across the globe. News agencies across the world were doing stories all about how organizations like the GWA were treading headstrong into Toronto... knowing full well of the outbreak. Truth be told, many within the GWA ranks were concerned, and gave wonder to as to why the mangement would continue such a move. President Freeman remained silent on the issue. Perry Lewis had booked the current Breakdown for
Toronto, and President Freeman and Vice President Ozstryker obviously had no objections to such a move. ***
*** The citizens of Toronto were obviously excited about the GWA. What they needed now more than ever was some basic old fashioned entertainment. Anything to take their minds off the current crisis at hand... if even for a brief moment. Starting Monday night, the GWA provided just that. The surrounding smaller cities and towns played host to house shows where up and coming talent could showcase their abilities, as well as give the locales a chance to get up close and personal to their fave GWA superstars. In the city of Toronto itself, those wrestlers who weren't afraid, gave public appearances, photo shoots, autograph sessions, and the like, to the delight of fans. All in all, this looked to be the "shot in the arm" that Toronto was looking for. ***
*** One such individual who wasn't afraid to venture forth was the GWA's own "Gentle Giant", Gabriel Ghold. Oh he knew the risks, but also knew the healing value one could have to just get out and mingle with people. Healing not in a physical way, but more psychological, mental. To know that someone cared... someone outside the crisis center (the city), and more than that...
someone famous. Gabriel has been on local televison promoting the GWA events for the week, as well as making a number of public appearances across the city... which is currently where we find him now, at a local mall. ***
*** Here we find a mammoth multi-story complex, with every manor of store one could imagine. The cameras are currently catching the massive food court, which hosts a wide variety of food choices, easily 20+ restaurants just on a rough estimate. The center hosts tables of varying sizes, and even with the outbreak on everyone's mind... many of the tables are full with people eating, drinking, and surprisingly having a good time. Many can be seen with surgical style masks around their necks, some of the more cautious types keeping them on even while they eat... only removing it long enough to take a quick bite. Gabriel though, is more than easy to spot. A very large crowd can be seen at one end of the court, in front of a massive waterfall type fountain that accents the decor rather well. He sits at a table with a large stack of photo's which he appears to be signing and handing out to people as they approach. A handshake, even quick conversation... Gabriel makes certain that this isn't just another signed photo, quick hello, and you're outta here. ***
*** Gabriel wears black leather pants, black boots, and a golden t-shirt, tight against the chest. Most similar to his actual wrestling attire, Gabriel appears to be most comfortable in these clothes, as he wears this sort often... espeically for public meeet and greets such as this one. As the camera moves in closer, Gabriel looks to be at ease with his surroundings. The threat of SARS has no showing on his expressions or body movements... and from the size of the crowd, one can assume that they know this, and greatly appriciate it. Gabriel looks up to notice the camera crew, and motions for them to move in closer still, a big smile on his face. Just then, a small child, not over 8-10 years of age steps up to the table,
Gabriel handing him an autographed photo and offering his hand to shake. The small child shakes his hand and looks like he would really like to say something but is reluctant to do so. ***
{Gabriel Ghold} So... what's your name? {Kid} Jason. {Gabriel Ghold} All right Jason, you look like you want to ask me something. Go ahead... I'm not going anywhere, and I'm sure these folks behind you don't mind waiting while you and I have a short chat.
{Jason} (hesitant) W-why are you here? I mean, with this disease thing all over the place... I just can't figure it out. Most people have decided to stay away, and those that do come... have as little contact with others as possible.
{Gabriel Ghold} That's a good question there Jason. (Gabriel gives a small grin) It's an easy one to answer though. I'm here because I want to help. No... I'm not a doctor or scientist, but if I can bring just one person happiness, just one... if I can get one person to forget about the problems their dealing with right now, even for a moment... then the risk is worth it. (pauses gauging the boys response) Do you agree?
{Jason} (thinking) I guess so. So... have you guys sold out the Centre yet? My brother and I wanted to come, but we don't have any money. That's why I'm here today. I heard you were going to be here, and hoped I would get a chance to at least get an autograph.
{Gabriel Ghold} I'll do you one better Jason. How about you and your brother attending
Breakdown this weekend... as my special guests?
{Jason} (his face lights up, total surprise... coupled with smiles and laughter) Y-you really mean it?! No foolin'?! {Gabriel Ghold} I really mean it Jason. I think there are still two tickets left... and probably on the front row too. (Gabriel grins)
{Jason} Gee thanks Mr. Ghold! (reaches over and gives a hug to Gabriel, who returns the affection) {Gabriel Ghold} No Jason... thank you. You just show up at the Center early and I'll give you and your bro a backstage tour... maybe introduce you to some of the other talent.
{Jason} (more total shock & surprise coupled with joy) You bet! This is best day of my life! (Jason gives another hug, then runs off telling the world about his experience and his free tickets and backstage tour)
{Gabriel Ghold} (looking at the camera now) That's what it's all about people. I know guys like Heath Starr, and even his companion HHH... they don't really get it.
They see fame, fortune... I've been down that road. I've had more money than the entire salries of every GWA employee combined. Was I truely happy? No. I worked for one of the most powerful business enterprises in the world today... definately one of the most famous. Was I happy? Not really. I had to literally lose all I had, all that was important to me, or what I thought was important to me to fully realize this.
I know that HHH and Benoit both have the current match on their minds... and to some extent so do I. But more than that, I have the people of Toronto, the fans... I have them on my mind. Winning is of course something I want very much to accomplish, but more than that... I want to give something to the fans here. I want to allow them a chance to escape. Escape from the reality of their lives for even the briefest of moments. I do that by giving it all I have in this match, and promising nothing more, nothing less.
All I can offer is to give my best, and hope that someone out there enjoys what they see. Some I think miss the mark. They get too focused on winning, on obtaining a goal... and they forget the whole other side of the picture.
Can one entertain the crowd and still not produce a win? I think so. Winning some say would be an encouragement to entertain. To entertain one must do well. Doing well, one stands a very good chance of winning. I think so, the majority have it backwards. They do not do well to entertain. They do well to further their own goals. And if by some chance someone out in the audience finds what they do to their liking, so be it. If not... they could simply care less.
What I can promise is that when Benoit, HHH, and myself meet, I will be there to first and foremost to entertain those on hand. I have no vendetta against either of my opponents, no ulterior motives... I simply wish to entertain the crowd and make them glad they attended for the evening. Aide in their escape from reality if you will. So does this mean I won't be trying for the win? Not in the least. It means that a win or a loss doesn't compare to the reality of the situation before me. SARS is real. It is something that these people are having to deal with on a daily basis. I can't accept that somthing as trivial as a golden wrestling belt would compare to them and their situation. Yes a win would move me even closer to gaining a shot at the gold, and I hope that I can win. Should I lose however... I will be reassured in knowing that I did my best, I gave all that I had... and the fans were hopefully pleased and happy about the match and my performance in it.
Now if you will excuse me, I do have more people to greet and get to know. I'm glad that the camera crew made the trip out here today. Until we speak again then... *** Gabriel gives a giant grin and a wave as the next person in line steps up to the table and accepts a handshake and autographed photo from him. The scene slowly fades to black... ***
