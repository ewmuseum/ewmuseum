[FLASH] Marcus Nyghtwulf

11 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is an RP I did for INWI. Open for Criticism, and stuff. PS:FireofFury, your thoughts aren't welcomed, because well your a Jobber :D ---- [] Camera Fades in [] [The Screen is Dark, you can't make anything out, but you know something is going on, for the sounds of breathing fills the room. The Darkness blocks site, but you get the sense that somebody is directly infront of you. The
Silence is Cut, with a Startling Laughter. The Laughter Fades away, as
Marcus Nyghtwulf Picks up]
MN: You Know, taking a look at what is what, and how things go. I would Venture to say, maybe things are on the Up and Up, if you will. I'm _EXTREMELY_ looking forward to a few things that are yet to come, here in my career. Exactly what do I mean? Oh, very soon, things will be
_FRIGHTENINGLY_ clear... [Marcus Chuckles] MN: ...And don't Say you didn't know, because all I have to say is, "I Told You So." I Told you it was Coming, but you didn't listen. I told you it was near, but you didn't listen. And when it is upon you, and you are being beaten into Oblivion, don't say I didn't warn you, Don't say you didn't know. Cause, when its all said an done, it will be _MY_ choice, if I let you live long enough, to say _ANYTHING...
[Marcus Pauses a Bit, and then picks back up] MN: ... _ANYTHING_AT_ALL_! But don't take my word for it. Don't let this come at you out of nowhere. Prepare. Be Afraid. Be _VERY_ afraid. [The Camera doesn't move, but a light turns on in the room. The Light casts a Shadow upon the Figure sitting in a chair, a few feet infront of the camera. The Light brightens up half of his face. Leaving the Other Half in darkness. The Picture gives an Erry feeling, as Marcus begins to speak]
MN: You Know, You can tell allot about a man, when you look into their eyes. You can see all their fears, you can feel all their pain. Eyes are a
Window to our Soul. A Window, no matter how hart you try, no matter how long you beg, cannot be closed. This leads to an interesting way to see a man, in his entirety, despite the words that Profusely leave his mouth. Its then, when you can see if they are scared. This is how I thrive, this is what I use. Your on the other end, may god have mercy.
[Marcus Smirks, as the lighten half of his face shows his facial expressions] MN: Rebound, where to start, where to start, oh, Where to start. So many choices, so many chances, but only a few _Choice_ spots, to sink my teeth into. But, as if the INWI knows what's coming, they choose a path for me.
well, an _OPTIONAL_ path, if you will. [Marcus leans fully into the Light, his full face is in view. He has an
"Evil" Grin on his face, as he begins to speak]
MN: More of the same, maybe its chances. Redemption, my stake to claim what I may have lost. Al the Same, yet one is Different. The Four Pay
Match, at the Pay-Per-View, Pitting 4 men Against each other. Consisting of
Myself, American AJ, Seth Maxo, and Attrition. And it seems as if the INWI will keep the _BULK_ of that group going. But this time, a Tag Team Match.
[Marcus Chuckles, and Crosses his hands out infront of him] MN: The "Path" the INWI has set down for me, leaves me to have somebody "watch" my back. This doesn't sit right with me. Tag Team isn't my Business. But, I guess life goes on. Maybe this will all be the same, another 4 way Match...
[Marcus Chuckles] MN: ...No No, Mark Johnson, don't run. The INWI teamed me with you, so in other words, no matter the results, you're going to get a _W_ in the
Win\loss Column. So, you can Watch my Back, if you wish. And, I give you my solemn word...
[Makes a motion, using his finger, to "Cross" his heart"] MN: ...I Cross My Heart, I wont do _ANYTHING_ to you. [Marcus Laughs, and leans back, into the shadows] MN: So Mark, The Focus, well, My Focus for now, will be off of you, and onto the Opponents they have set forth, on a silver platter, infront of us. American AJ, and Seth Maxo. I'm only Pretty sure that things between you two and Myself, will only get _DEEPER_ as times move on. But, I'm gonna miss the fun times, the fond memories, after I
_DRIVE_YOU_STRAIGHT_THROUGH_THE_RING_! And, I give you Two my Word, American AJ, Seth Maxo err, what ever the hell your name is, I Give you
_MY_ Word. You Will not be able to drive me way. You Will _NEVER_EVER_ forget my name. And, when the smoke clears, and the INWI moves away the
Rubble, the Broken Bones, the Bruises you have sustained, will be a reminder..
[Marcus Smiles, and brings his hands behind his head] MN: ...A Reminder of what I told you. because, I warned you. But you Didn't listen. Why you Didn't listen, is beyond me. But, its plain and simple. Your Life, in Exchange for my Happiness. Well thought out Trade, wouldn't you say?Ill Let you Sleep on it, for this, just may be your Last
Chance, for Survival. Take Care, take _EXTREME_ car.
[Marcus Laughs, as the Camera pans away. He begins to laugh a Bit more, and stands up. He looks into the Camera once more, as a stern look falls upon his face. He then winks. After the Wink, the Screen cuts to black, and there is complete Silence.]
