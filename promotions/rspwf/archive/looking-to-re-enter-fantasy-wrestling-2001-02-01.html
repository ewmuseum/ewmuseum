Looking to re-enter fantasy wrestling

1 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I used to be involved with fantasy wrestling up until last summer, when a lack of time and fed closures curbed my interest. But I have come up with a brand new character that I would like to try out, and would like to enter a fantasy wrestling promotion.
I am looking for a fed that has these particular qualities: *Has a website and a message board that RP's are done on (I have tried both message boards and e-mail RP's and find that the message boards are better.) *Only has imaginary wrestlers (Please no feds with any real wrestlers in it.) *Please no simmed federations Sorry about being picky, but I think feds that have these traits will be a lot more beneficial to me and my return.
------------------------------------------------------------------------ This is a roleplay that I created for my previous character, Crossbones. {Scene opens inside Veterans Memorial Stadium, late into the evening. A grounds crew of roughly two dozen men are in the upper decks of the large structure, cleaning the trash left by the people who attended the baseball game earlier in the day. The men are all uniformed in identical yellow shirts emblazoned with the words "EVENT STAFF" on the back, and wear matching black baseball caps. A variety of music, most of it current pop & country hits, play over the public address system, although a lot quieter than during game time.
Camera view changes to inside the home teams' dugout, where a man sits in the shadows of the scoreboard lights. He stands up and reveals himself to be
Crossbones, the CPW wrestler. He is dressed in a pair of blue jeans, which are covered with what appears to be a combination of mud stains and stains of blood.
He also wears a matching blue T-shirt and a pair of industrial strength work boots. His face is without face paint on this night, his deep, vicious scars are revealed to the world by the bright light hitting his forehead. Crossbones turns to the camera, and speaks.}
So, people thought I was going to get upset and angry when I was pinned by the "Man from the Midwest" at
Countdown to Mayhem last week. They thought I was going to become volatile and dangerous, and be a danger to others and myself. They believed that I would meet the edge, and proceed to go over it.
I wasn't then, but I certainly am now. What happened during my match against Kreig Blitz was unbelievable.
I face him one-on-one, and for the better part of the match had him on the ropes, on the mat, and on his back. I almost pinned him during several occasions, and while he had a halfway decent match considering his formidable opponent, he still was not the better man. But yet he managed to win.
I don't really consider what he did to be a true win however. The record books will state that my wrestler was counted out, the victim of the ring steps and the referees ten count. But although it counts as a loss,
I do not really agree with that. To me, a loss is a pin or submission, and the occasional situation when a wrestler is beaten beyond comprehension, and the referee decides to call for the bell. It is not when a wrestler is left outside the ring for a predetermined timeframe, prompting the referee to disqualify him.
Kreig, I'm sure that you are pleased with your victory, tainted though it is. You probably know in your heart that you won the only way you could, I think that you would be unable to put me down with a pinfall or force me to cry out in submission. I don't believe that you could beat me to the brink of unconsciousness, or maybe even beyond that point. But
I do know you could beat me by count out, which is what you did. Maybe you deserve more respect and credit than I have given you thus far, much like I deserve a rematch to prove my accusations to be true.
So if you have the guts to see whether it was a one match fluke or if you are actually as great as you have been billed, than I encourage you to accept my challenge.
While speaking on the subject of "one-match wonders"; let's talk about the aforementioned Garret. The man who reeled off a stunning pinfall against me, the man who ended my win streak and my television title ring.
After his tremendous loss at the hands of Drago, I guess that you could also say that he's the man who has earned credit to being the person with the shortest title span in the CPW. This just shows you how much of a fluke that his victory over me was. He said something about having unfinished business with me, so I guess that means that he wants yet another round with me in a CPW ring. I'd advise him to quit while he's ahead, but I know what Garret is like, and how he will not take a denial at all. But maybe he will prove me wrong, and just might take the high road and walk off content to have our singles record tied.
Many people are wondering now that I am no longer television champion what role I am going to play in the CPW roster. Honestly, I plan on doing what I did when I had the strap, and even before I won it. Quite simply, I'm going to go into the ring, beat down my opponent, execute the crosscheck, and then walk back out with a victory. There will be title opportunities in the future; don't think that because I lost the television title that I won't have a chance of winning the larger titles down the road. North American,
Heavyweight, maybe a run at the tag titles. But that is in the future; right now I'm just going for wins and the opportunity to dish out punishment to the rest of the CPW roster.
{Crossbones pushes open the bullpen door, and starts charging down the foul line, ala John Rocker's entrance, but then suddenly stops. He looks up at the scoreboard, and then starts walking right down the line.}
In baseball, there is always a winning team, and a losing team. They don't win based on if their opponent is counted out of the batters box, they win on what they do in the box, and how they handle their opponents when it is time to go to bat.
Unfortunately, Kreig Blitz found another way to win, a way which has gotten me beyond the boiling point in terms of my demeanor. So whomever I face next will get to feel the full wrath of my offensive onslaught, and my bitterness over the events that have unfolded.
{Crossbones gets down to homeplate, and is seen by a member of the events crew, who sees him and then begins running down from the seats, in chase of
Crossbones. Crossbones nonchalantly turns around, and walks back towards the dugout.} You people probably have questions about the blood that is on my jeans. Well, I used to always have a problem whenever I went to watering holes in my native
New Mexico. I always had these toughguys that wanted a piece of me, and got far more than they wanted.
They thought they were dominant, that their actions would be as forceful as their brags. They weren't, and the blood that they shed was proof of that. I have been a little soft as of late, something I plan on correcting. I tried to play nice and act in an appropriate manner for the people watching me on television, but that has obviously blown up in my face.
{The events crew guy catches up with Crossbones just as he was parallel with third base as he walked the foul line. He grabs Crossbones by the shirt and starts yelling at him.}
Guy: What the hell are you doing here. You have no right to be on this property, it's called criminal trespass you a**hole. Who the hell do you think you are?
{Crossbones just glares at him with a menacing expression.} Guy: You might be big, but you seem pretty damn stupid. I asked who you are, and I expect an answer from you.
{Crossbones grabs the guy by his collar with both hands, and hoists him overhead, prompting the other event crew members to race towards the fracas, abandoning their job.}
Crossbones: The name is Crossbones, CPW wrestler. Maybe I have been the recipient of bad luck over the past couple of weeks, but things are going to change.
People are going to know my name. {Crossbones walks over to the third basepad, and proceeds to execute a sit-down powerbomb to the diminutive man, rendering him into unconsciousness.
Crossbones walks back down the foul line to the dugout, while the other men help the powerbomb victim.}
Crossbones: I guess that puts a whole new spin on the popular baseball phrase "out at the plate".
{Crossbones snickers at his comment in a sadistic manner, and then walks into the bullpen, closing the door behind him.}
