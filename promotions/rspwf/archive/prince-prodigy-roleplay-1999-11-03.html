Prince Prodigy Roleplay

3 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ scene ] Prince Prodigy and Craven Morehead are seen sitting on a couch watching last night's tribute to Legacy. Prince is shaking his head and has a look of disgust on his face.
[ prince prodigy ] You know Craven this is sick, just plain sick. I thought I would be facing
Legacy for that World title after what good old Awesome Australian said but now it appears that I have to face Matt Austin for it. You know what makes me so sick about that?
[ craven morehead ] What's that Prince? [ prince prodigy ] The fact that out of that tribute to Legacy, Matt Austin was the only one who had the balls to stand up to that big retard and tell the truth. Legacy was a bad man all through his stay in the XBWL. He tried to destroy it and take it over with the WWFW all the time, and now these guys come out and lie and say how much respect they had for him. What a total bunch of horse sh#t! You know what else man? I had a lot of respect for "TNT" Nick Stevenson because he was really a great athlete, but now I look at him as a stepping stone because he's got to be weak. How can you forgive a man that tore your ACL or did things to your mother? Legacy almost ended his career for christ's sake! Nick has become a huge p#ssy! I can't believe this sh#t! If this is the type of competition in the XBWL now I'm not going to have a bit of trouble dominating this place!
Where did all the talent go? I appear to be the only one with any talent, any kind of a backbone, or any kind of an attitude besides Matt Austin. I hate Matt
Austin too but maybe I should try and feud with Matt Austin instead of Nick
Stevenson. what is this league coming to Craven? When Johnny Macaroni asked me to return I thought he was just trying to recruit another great wrestler but now I see he intended for me to save this dying piece of garbage league. When you have guys that are supposed to be tought like Nick Stevenson and Beast all acting like big wussies and sucking up to Legacy how can you expect the league to survive. They should have sent Legacy out of here on a stretcher! They should have added the insult to the injury! My god this is pathetic. I guess for me to save this league I'm going to have to beat Matt Austin and TAKE that world title away from him and the I'm going to have to go through the ranks of the XBWL and embarrass people like Nick Stevenson, The Beast, and especially
Legacy's b#tch The Element. Once I do that people will have to recognize me as the greatest athlete in the XBWL and then maybe some new guys with some hunger to prove themselves will come to the XBWL just to try and beat me, to test themselves against the best the pro-wrestling world has to offer. I mean when I look out at this league at the competitors and I see guys like Michael Shocker,
Fallen or SeVeRe PaiN being pushed as top notch superstars that's telling the world all at once that the XBWL is the worst place to wrestle in the world because we have to push the worst wrestlers in the world. This is absolutely terrible. All I know is if that Awesome Australian goes back on his word and does not give me the world title match then he and I are going to have MAJOR heat. I'll come to every card if I have to whether I'm scheduled to wrestle or not and I'll interfere in every match, I'll attack every unsuspecting wrestler
I can find and I'll even hurt the announcers so no one can call the action.
Heck maybe I'll do that anyway and go out there and call the match myself! The
XBWL needs all of Prince Prodigy it can get. If they give me the ball and let me run with it I'll take this league back to the top where it used to be and where it could be once again, the only question is if the front office is smart enough to let that happen. They have no stars right now, they can say Thornton,
Austin, Element, or Nick Stevenson are it's stars but the truth is none of those guys are doing anything right now to really help this league so it's time to give the ball to a new, young and hungry superstar and let him prove himself.
[ craven morehead ] Well Prince a lot of what you say is true but you know as well as I do that even if you do get the title shot they will do anything to keep you from winning the world title. Matt Austin will cheat and stoop to any level to keep the title but you can really expect no less than that because we would do exactly the same thing. The thing that we would have to watch for is when the office sends in other wrestlers like maybe Nick Stevenson, Beast, Brandon
Thornton or even The Element to interfere and keep you from winning the title.
They will send everyone they can at you and try to get you side tracked and get you into a feud against some nobody so that you can't challenge or hold the world title. You know as well as I do they will stoop to whatever means neccessary to hold you down just because you are a rookie.
[ prince prodigy ] That's true but I can always make arrangements with those guys once I find out if I do get the title shot. I have a match against Nick Stevenson coming up, I could talk to him and convince him that if he helps me win the title that I'll make our match a title shot so he gets a chance at holding the gold again. I could even go to The Element and promise him the same thing. If he helps me win
I'll grant him a title shot and give him the chance to follow in his mentor's footsteps by holding the world title. In fact that's not a bad idea, maybe I'll go to them with that right away. This league is in such bad shape that maybe if they did help me win and I give The Element a title shot then we could quickly establish two new young wrestlers as superstars and then start to rebuild this pathetic league. Jyst think about it, I beat Matt Austin and become the youngest and quickest rookie to win the world championship and then I defend the title in a triple threat match against The Element and Nick Stevenson. That would have to get the XBWL some ratings, I mean how could it not?
[ craven morehead ] Well Prince I think you are getting way ahead of yourself. First we know that
Awesome Australian will try and find a way to get out of giving you a world title match all together, and then if he has to give it to you he will find a way to make sure you don't win it. So all these other plans don't mean squat.
[ prince prodigy ] Yeah I know you are right but how does this league think it's going to pull itself up out of this hole unless they start to build up new talent and give this league a fresh look? You know they are going to try and stick me in some worthless feud with guys like Michael Shocker or SeVeRe PaiN, guys that aren't even capable enough to tie their own boot laces. I can wrestle circles around those guys and embarass them but what good will that do because when fans hear the names Michael Shocker or SeVeRe PaiN they consider it a lucha libre bathroom break. They will never get to see me wrestle becuase they won't care about the match. If I wrestle worthless opponents it's not going to matter how good I really am because the opponents are too shabby. Craven you are my manager, get in contact with the office and try to explain this stuff to them, sell them on the idea of pushing the younger talent like myself and The Element instead of worthless guys like Shocker and PaiN.
[ craven morehead ] Ok, ok. I'll get on that now. Let me go try and call them. [ scene ] Craven Morehead gets up and walks over to a phone while Prince turns his attention back to the television and begins to replay the Legacy tribute show and watch on in disgust.
