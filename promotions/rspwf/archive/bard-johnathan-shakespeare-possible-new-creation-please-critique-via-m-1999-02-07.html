["Bard" Johnathan Shakespeare] Possible New Creation, Please Critique VIA MAIL

7 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings RSPWF in the name of Jesus our Lord and Saviour, I am posting today, a sample of what could quite soon be my third Wrestling Character. Yet that depends on what people think of the work which I can do with him.
First of all let's give you a run-down of the character: Setting of Character: "The Bard" Johnathan Shakespeare was born in Stratford Upon Avon, London,
England on the morning of March 29th 1976. At the age of just 14 he realised just what a soliloquie was and he started practising them each and every day, until one day he was invited to the RSA. The Royal
Shakesperian Arts. The director of the company, a William J. Goldstone who at that time was a leading Shakesperian actor saw some of Johnahan
Shakespare's work and then invited him to take part in an audition to deterimine if he really could be the next William Shakespeare.
After a few hours time, Johnatahn Shakespeare was picked from out of the entrants. He then spoke a Scene from Henry V, which reads and I quote
"..Can this cock-pit hold the varsity fields of France? Or may we cram within this wooden O, the very casques that did affright the air at
Agincourt? O pardon: since a crooked figure may attest in little place a million, and let us, ciphers to this great account, on your imaginary forces work.."
The Director of the RSA was so shocked by the quality and level which
Johnathan Shakespeare could speak at, that he decided he would be the best man suited to stand in for the now dying James Shakespeare who just simply lost touch with the outside world. Johnathan then at the age of 18 experienced a major scare. During a rehersal one day for the leading Role in Macbeth at the Globe Theatre, for no reason whatsover. He passed out. It was then round about 2 days later when the Doctors came and told Johnathan Shakespeare that his dream of being a Bard was going to have to come to an end.
Johnathan Shakespeare was then so mad that he just left it all behind. He then got on a plane to Canada and then set up a new life for himself there. Johnathan then at the ripe age of 19 then located a lovely lady who he soon after married. Her name was lady Jane McDuff. Jane McDuff then experienced some difficaulties during a days work at the RSA of Canada. Johnaathan was then called to see Jane McDuff and he found out that McDufff had developed a rare disorder known as Shakespeare Syndrome.
Lady McDuff would belive she was Lady Macbeth. Johnathan was then called for comfort and he was able to reverse the ilness and now
Jane McDuff is by his side at all times. Jane though wanted Johnathan to do something worthwile and thus he decided that he was going to use his
Shakespearian style inside the confounds of the wrestling ring and thus this is what he is now about to do.
Thus 'tis be the setting of the Character "The Bard" Johnathan Shakespeare. Once thou understandeth the charcater, then please cast your eys over a sample of his work and please feel fell to critic it as I feel. Johnathan Shakespeare brings out my Shakespearian side. And thus this may lead to my third creation, yet that depnds on how well you feel he is at role-playing.
Anyway, I shall submit the role-play below to you and any comments you have, please feel free to air them. Send them to me as I only sometimes check the board at <
[detail withheld] > =A Sample of "The Bard" Johnathan Shakespear' work.= [Scene opens up inside a secret training location in Montreal, Canada. Johnathan Shakespeare is seen in the background dressed in a velvet tunic with a white frilled shirt and a pair of black trousers. Johnathan
Shakespeare then turns to Lady Mcduff who is seated in a old wicker chair. Johnathan then begins to speak out in a soliloquie.]
JS - "If it were done when 'tis done, then t'were well it were done quickly. Hakiro Hotha it shall be done quickly. The assanination could trammel up the consequence, and catch with his surcease sucess-that but this blow. Might be the be-all and the end-all here, but here, upon this bank and shoal of time, we'd jump the life to come. Hakiro Hotha, I shall end it here.."
[Johnathan Shakespeare then turns to Lady McDuff who then begins to speak.] LM - "Come, you spirits from the Darkside that tend on mortal thoughts, unxsex me here and fill me from the crown to the toe top-full of direst cruelty."
[Johnathan Shakespeare then turns to his mother Lady Mcduff and then continues his soliloquie.]
JS - "But in these situations Hakiro Hotha, we still have judgement here, that we but teach. Hakiro Hotha, I shall teach. Teach you the lesson of the Bard himself. Bloody instructions which, being taught, return to plague you. Hakiro Hotha, the Darkside shall not plague me for it shall go to plague the inventor. Thou art that inventor, the weakned vessel of light and thou art more evil then the blackest heart of stone and thus when we doth meet, I shall teach you the ways of the Bard and then maybe thou shalt impressth me.."
[Johnathan Shakespeare then turns to Lady McDuff for one last time, he then speaks out his final soliloquie.]
JS - "Hakiro Hotha hath born his faculties so meek, hath been so clear in his great place with the Darkside, that his virtues will plead like angels, trumpet-tongued against the deep damnation of his taking off. Oh Hakiro, you wait on natures mischeif, but it no more shall be. For Hakiro Hotha like the willows weeping thou shalt weepeth no more, for tis be it, tis be the ending and thus tis be it over, for the Bard hath
Spoken.." [As the last soliloquie is rendered, "The Bard" Johnathan Shakespeare then just sits down by the right hand side of Lady McDuff. It then fades to black.]
