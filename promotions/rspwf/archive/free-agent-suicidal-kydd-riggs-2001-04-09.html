[Free Agent] -["Suicidal" Kydd Riggs]-

9 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, that last topic "What has E-wrestling become?" made me realize, like you all said, I am in the wrong circle of E-feds. And no, I am a free agent. :)
Here is a Kydd Riggs RP. Not that great, but the only one I could find. ...Fades in... [ Scene ] [...The scene opens in the front yard of Kydd Riggs' home. There isn't much, other than an open rock driveway, paved near the front, where a basketball hoop is. The lines are marked off as well. Off to the right, in a grass yard, there is a large shed, which has glass doors as much of the house does. Inside you can see a quad, two snowmobiles, three mountain bikes and other such things. Off to the left of shed, under a pavilion are three jet-skiis, all chained together, and well taken care off.
Suddenly, a loud car system can be heard, and off at the end of the driveway, a gold 2001 Pontiac Grand Prix can be seen pulling in. The little bit of sun that makes it through the thick trees, shines off the cars great shine. As the car gets closer, you can hear that is it "The Way I'm Rollin'" by Limp Bizkit featuring Wu-Tang, Redman and DMX. The car screeches to a stop, kicking up some dust and rocks from the driveway, as it pulls over to the left of the basketball court, but to the right of the house. It pulls in next to a white
76 Corvette. The music shuts off, followed by the car. The door opens and out steps "Suicidal" Kydd Riggs. He is wearing a sleeveless "Fading Hope" T-Shirt, black jean shorts, and a pair of red oakleys, that match his gray and red streaked lugz. He looks at the camera, as he closes the car door, then looks down at the door, as he locks it and puts his keys in his pocket. He slowly walks toward the camera, then makes his way toward the deck, which leads to the front door. But he stops at the bottom of the steps, and reaches under the small deck and pulls out a basketball, which he starts bouncing as he heads toward the hoop. He dribbles the ball back forth between his legs, before he finally leans back, and tosses it up from beyond three point range. SWISH..
The basketball hits nothing but air and net. Riggs claps his hands sarcastically, as he runs for the ball, and begins to speak...]
[ Riggs ] "Damn I'm good..." [ Scene ] [...Riggs gets the ball, and continues to bounce it back and forth through his legs, then around his back and so on, as he walks slowly toward the camera. When he reaches it, he stops bouncing the ball, places it under his left arm, and continues talking...]
[ Riggs ] "Hard Rock and Goregoof should keep that in mind as Discord approaches..." [ Scene ] [...The front door suddenly opens as Jacen Drake steps out...] [ Drake ] "Hey, want to play one on one?" [ Riggs ] "Maybe later, I just got home, and I want to finish this interview.." [ Drake ] "Ight', cool.. Guess what?" [ Riggs ] "What?" [ Drake ] "Rhinos should not be killed for their tusks, but for their toenails..." [ Scene ] [...Riggs gets a stupid, confused look on his face, as Drake goes back inside. Shutting the door behind him. Riggs just stands there, thinking to himself for a moment, then continues talking...]
[ Riggs ] "You know, if you think about that, it almost makes sense.. The keyword there, being almost.. [chuckles] Anyway.. Goregoof, and
Rock Hard... The days are counting down, and neither one of you have really shown me any kind of smarts yet. Well, Rock Hard, you haven't shown me anything. Not even your face.. And goregoof, you've only shown me you're an idiot who doesn't understand anything. Which makes me think of something. You have such hate and anger toward me for some reason, yet you don't know me.
Which I find kinda funny, because for the most part, and almost always, people who smoke, don't have tempers. Which makes me think you're nothing more than a wussy gimmick man, who probably doesn't even inhale. [laughs] You are a true loser, seriously... True Story, True Story... [chuckles] But then what's your excuse for being so damn dumb? Huh.. That's kind of confusing.. And not really worth thinking about. And neither are you, but for a few more days I have no choice.. [smiles] So where to go from here? I've explained you're fake, you're dumb, and you suck.. So what does that leave me with?"
[ Scene ] [...Pyrex suddenly interrupts Riggs as he comes bursting out of the woods...] [ Pyrex ] "What about his small Johnson?" [ Riggs ] "Not really worth talking about.. I already explained to him it's only second best. I don't want him losing it, and trying to kill himself, or wasting his money on a triple sized penis enlarger. We'll just leave it at that.."
[ Pyrex ] "True.. Give me the ball..." [ Riggs ] "Okay?" [ Scene ] [...Riggs hands Pyrex the ball. Pyrex then begins to shoot around...] [ Riggs ] "Where the hell did you come from anyway?" [ Pyrex ] "Oh, you know.." [ Riggs ] "No I don't. That's why I asked." [ Pyrex ] "Oh." [ Riggs ] "Well?" [ Scene ] [...Pyrex ignored Riggs...] [ Riggs ] "I get a lot of that anymore.. Especially from the lame ass pukes in the EhWa. The only moron to respond to me recently was goregoof. And he is basically meaningless to me.."
[ Scene ] [...Drake opens back up the front door, and walks out dressed in an old New York Knick jersey. He walks over to Pyrex, and the two begin to play one on one...]
[ Riggs ] "What the hell, I thought I was playing?" [ Drake ] "Get a partner, and you can play." [ Riggs ] "This is my house, and you're telling me what I can and cannot do?" [ Pyrex ] "Basically." [ Scene ] [...Riggs stands silent for a minute...] [ Riggs ] "Okay, I'll get a partner.. KOOOOOO!" [ Scene ] [...Suddenly from around the corner, Kooter Brown comes running up, in a bear like position. On his hands and feet. He rolls as he reaches Riggs' feet, then stands up, and declares he is ready to play. Riggs shakes his head...]
[ Riggs ] "Well, let's go. Oh, goregoof, and Hard Rock.. I'll see you at Discord..." [ Scene ] [...Riggs checks the ball with Pyrex and their game begins. The camera watches them for a minute, then begins to fade. The ball is heard bouncing as it goes blank...]
...Fades out... Let me clear some things up, so you're not confused. The True Story, True
Story thing is an inside joke. (happened before Kurt Angles "It's ture, it's true" thing)
Drake is an idiot (in a funny way) and usually gives me pointless quotes to put in my RP's. That = a gimmick in case you don't understand that.
And Kydd Riggs, a lot like Chris Jericho likes to make up names for his opponents. And Kooter Brown... Well, that's to hard to explain.. Thank you.
