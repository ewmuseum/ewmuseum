My first RP!!! Please critique.

8 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello- Before I give you my roleplay I just want you all to know, that this is my
VERY first RP in my fed. This is also the first time posting on this newsgroup. I know there is lots of talent here but do you think I could make it to be a great wrestler? Well anyways, here it is.
_____________________________________ [The scene opens as DJ Sparkle is in his upstate New York skyline apartment. He is wearing a NO LIMIT baseball jersey and a pair of black jogging pants. He has headphones on and is doing a little mixing. Along the walls are pictures of his all time favorite rappers and MC's. He seems to be really into his work. There are tapes all over the turntables but otherwise the room looks really clean. The carpet is blue and there are numerous sets of tables and chair arranged across the room. Then about his desk is DJ
Sparkles most prized possession. His triple-platinum CD. It is decked out in a gold frame which is tightly secured to the wall with numerous brackets and wall mounts. DJ Sparkle takes a glance up and sees the camera. The takes a jump back and you can here the screech of the LP's. He takes his headphones off and puts them around his neck as he begins to speak]
DJ Sparkle: I see I have a match against you tonight Mr.. Irish Heartbreaker. And I also see that you have been saying a few things that didn't need to be said. Look, I don't know where your coming from calling me a 'fudgepacker' when you know absolutely nothing about me. Things like that get to me, I'm not going to get angry yet because I am a very sensible man. I understand that you want to win this match badly. But so do I. I am going to do everything to the best of my ability in the HWF. I have made my way up to many federations by using hard work, determination, and most of all...staying cool under pressure. That's something that I don't think you have IHB. You have a cocky attitude and that won't get you anywhere in wrestling. You may have money, women, THE WHOLE WORKS, but that's nothing compared to the excitement of a gold belt around your waist. I know were both looking for that feeling in the HWF but it all comes down to who is the best wrestler. Not the cockiest. Now, I think you should think about that before you mouth-off to a wrestler you don't even know.
[DJ Sparkle takes his headphones off of around his neck and sets them next to his turntables. He turns off the turntables which he didn't know were running. He casually walks into his kitchen and pours a little bit of wine into a crystal clear wine glass. Then he slowly walks to his stereo and puts on his CD, making sure the volume is down. Then he walks over to his desk. Before he sits down he takes a look at his triple-platinum album.
Then he sits down with a smile on his face. He slides some papers into the corner of his nicely organized desk, takes a sip of his wine, then slowly sets his glass just to the right of him as he begins to speak.]
DJ Sparkle: Oh, one more thing Irish Heart Breaker. Along with your cocky attitude you've been talking a LOT of so called 'smack.' You've disrespected me and all the wrestlers in the HWF when you called them all a bunch of 'Dino Bravo wannabes.' Now, I don't think that makes any sense so
I'm sure most wrestlers here didn't think much of it, but they still know that your here do disrespect. I don't 'dish' out that kind of stuff like you do. If your respectful to others, they will do the same back. That's what I try to do, and that makes me and the others in the HWF good wrestlers. And like I said earlier, that kind of attitude is just going to lead to lose after lose in the HWF. I'll let you off with that. I will come prepared tonight for our match. I hope you are to, I'm looking forward to a very exciting match.
[DJ Sparkle finishes his wine, gets out of the chair and walks to the kitchen to set the glass on the of the shinny black and white tiled counter.
He opens the window staring at New York's evening sky-line and a rush of cool air hits him. He keeps starring out of the window as the camera fades out to black]
