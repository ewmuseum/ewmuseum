"Steel" RP, please comment

13 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[the scene opens outside of Steel's home in Coldwater. Its a gloomy Michigan day, the sky is gray and the clouds overhead are an even darker shade of gray. A light drizzle has picked up, and the ground has a sheet of water blanketed over it. A small breeze picks up and debris flies by the camera. Suddenly you hear a gunshot and a gang of hoodlums are seen running out of the nearby alley way. They run across to the other side of the street, nearly falling, and then you hear sirens and the cop cars of blue and black come streaming by in pursuit.
The camera pans across the street to a closer shot of Steel's home. Its a humble little home, a nostalgiac shade of grayish blue in bad need of a paint job. The shingles are dripping the remnants of the rain season and along side the house, you see a small chain-linked fence with a sign that reads "BEWARE OF...ME!" Then a massive sized german shepard and an equally large rottweiller hop up on their hind legs barking to the skies at the sight of the camera crew. Then the camera wobbles up to the front door and is just about to knock as the door swings open on its own.]
[The camera cuts to a shot inside of Steel's home. After a moment of waiting, the camera and its crew crew decide to move through the home.
As the camera moves throughout the home, you see numerous title belts hanging from the walls and displayed in glass cases. Not just of federations, but you also catch a glimpse of a UFC title belt from UFC III.
In the same tour, you see almost and endless wall of plaques, documents of titles, photos of Steel's accomplishments and numerous hanging gloves, boots and even sweat towels. In a closer look, you notice that the photos are of with famous fighters from around the world. Photos with the ultimate shoot fighter, Dan Severn. The world's most famous family, the Gracey's, and even photos with such wrestling legends like
Lou Thesz, Ed "Strangler" Lewis and Bruno Sammartino. The camera moves the the entrance way of a room secluded from the rest of the main hall, it lies at the end of the secondary corridor near the very back of the home. Inside, the camera pans around and sees a room of weaponary. Blood-stained brass knuckesles, chains, trash can lids, broken glass bottles, lead pipes. On the opposite side of the blood- stained weapons you see a wall and glass case of sterling silver weapons. A chain is hangin around the rooms border lining the wall twice, its at least 200 feet of sterling silver chain. Finally the cameras reach Steel's rumpous room, where he is seated at the bar having himself a drink. The camera awaits him to notice them, while Steel serves himself a row of shots of 100 proof vodka, and just guzzles them down like water, without so much as a flinch. He finally takes heed of the camera crew, and greets them with a cold stare. Then he pours himself another shot, and downs it. Then he stands to his feet, a little wobbly, but remains standing. He's wearing baggy black overalls, with the straps worn down and a tank-top under shirt, well-fit.
You notice his numerous tattoos covering his body. You notice his right arm is covered in tattoos, the only ones you seem to make out cleanly are of a snake which begins at his upper shoulder and wraps down around to his wrist with the head on the back of his hand. On his uppershoulder, you also make out a thorn-styled tribal band and a steel skull his outside bicep. Down his left arm you see a tattooed skeleton arm. He takes a quick stretch and cracks his neck and knuckles, then signals to the camera.]
STEEL: Well, here I am kiddies. Thats right, for the first time in months,
I'm here on live TV. Without those head office bastards holdin me back...I'm free to say what I want! When I want! So here's the dish!
You know that Hammer is injured, and the world knows that through it all, we'll BOTH be at Prime Time in North America to claim what is rightfully ours! Those tag belts might just be belts, and personally , I wouldn't care much even to use it as a booster for our thrid coffee table leg. But anyway, Hammer is just in hiding for today. He's comin' along GREAT! So am I! I got my bandages off, I got my ribs
supported with a lil hip bone...everything is goin' just fine. I'm just sorry I can't say the same for the Wreckin' Crew...or am I?
[chuckles] You lil low-life rodents have crawled the dirty, dust-filled floors of this concrete jungle for your entire lives. Come this weekend, you'll get a new home! I hope you enjoy the ER, 'cause come monday...the first face you'll be seein' in the mornin' s is that Nurse Hemmingway...ya
know...the 350-pound nurse with a God complex and enjoys fondeling her unconscious patients? Yea, ya know the one. All the years of trainin', workin' our ever-lovin' asses off has finally paid off!
Hammer and I have come a long way from the streets where we started. You see Wreckin' Crew, even though you still thrive on the pain and embarassment of others, soon...we'll see if you will still thrive after
YOU'RE THE ONES bein' put through extreme pain and embarassment! [Steel serves himself yet another drink and throws it back. The bottle of vodka is now a lil under half full. Steel raises the bottle rather slowly, the alcohol is beginning to take affect on him. He looks at it in wonder, then turns his head slightly as if to see it better. He slams the bottle down then rises to his feet gingerly. He shakes off his drowziness, somewhat, then stretches a lil and focuses back on the camera...]
STEEL: Leviathan, I just remember your lil singing thinger...'er sumpin 'er other. Anyway, I just wanted to congratulate you on that Grammy, you deserved every bit of it. I don't agree with the Cult though.
You should really stick to your guns...quit wrestling, completely, you don't know how to fight. But, I will give credit where it's due. You sure got a set of lungs...use'em.You could be a fan....or um...er, maybe be a vender at a sporting event, HEY! Even wrestling! There ya go!
Just stay outta my way. With Hammer's contract comin' up soon,
I might either find another partner...or hop into the singles division!
Never know! But CWC, you have just sat back and watched us put our bodies and souls on the line for long enough. We've dominated and carried your tag team division for long enough, and now its time to pay the piper! Down with the accolades...down with the petty words of encouragement! We are comin' after your stars! One by one, each and every so-called "fighter" will go down at our power! Steel, thats me...and Hammer have already won the tag championships...we just need to worry about HOW we'll finish off the Wreckin' Crew.
Cannonball , I've been lookin forward to this match a long time. You really tried to take it to hammer last time in the Boiler Room, this time I get vengeance in the most deviant way! By pure, brutal force! Eclipse, you might have though it was funny the way you treated me last time too. But no more...
this time around, there's more than the belt on the line! The entire compilation of the Hardcore Icons' effort has been thrown and focused completely on this match! We might be similar in many ways, but in those ways, we are also different. Personally, I like to have a bottle in front of me...while you like to have a frontal labotamy! I like to have power and dominance...you guys like the to be over-powered by your leather-clad dominatrix! You see? The list goes on and on!
[Steel walks over to the other side of the bar and takes out a letter from underneath the bar shelves. The large yellow envelope says "CONFIDENTIAL:
FIRST CLASS" on it. Steel has already torn it open. He removes a letter from inside. Skims it for a few moments, then throws it on the floor and gives the camera a pompous smirk of confidence.]
STEEL: Inside this lil yellow envelope is a letter which says that the CWC has agreed to some certain terms I laid down a while back. Its been a bit long, and really I had completely forgotten about it, but it's finally here and I have been given almost the same power as a god! Wreckin'
Crew...I don't like bein' mean, but you guys have finally drawn in the most annoyin' thing that could've triggered a deadly attack from us...
you bragged! Yup...you guys fought us...gained absolutely NO EDGE, and STILL bragged, tryin to demean our accomplishments!
[He just glares at the camera sternly for a brief moment, then snaps his finger as he walks over to the window and stares out into the city. Then he turns back to the camera and leads them to the small house gym he has. Nothing special, but he has a weightset, heavy bag, treadmill and steel bars hammered into the wall. He seats himself on the weightset bench.]
STEEL: I got a lil sumpin' to show y'all. Before the Hardcore Icons graced the scene of the CWC, we were crushin skulls in separate parts of the world. We fought like men, no trashy gimmicks...no stupid
blind-sided attacks...LIKE MEN! Do you know what fighting like a man is all about? I didn't think either of ya did! We're gonna do a lil spring-type of cleanin' this weekend. We're goin into Prime Time, armed with nothin' but our fists and a seethin' head-full of anger and hatred! Here's that lil demonstration for the CWC...
[Steel then reaches into his pockets and reveals a flyer. He slowly unfolds it and then flashes it to the camera for a moment...]
STEEL: You see this Wreckin Crew? Its a flyer talkin' 'bout you guys? Can ya dig that? I was laughin' myself when I saw it. What does it say here?
Check this out... [Steel quickly clears his throat as he begins to read the flyer...] STEEL: [reading] Prime Time in North America...the world will be watching as a 4-way dance of death is put up...yada yada yada...AH! Here it is! The fire has been stoked over and over, now the raging inferno that is the heated feud between Hardcore Icons and the LA Wrecking Crew climaxes as the team face off in the match to end it all. The Tag Team championship will be on the line...who will show up> Who will walk off with the titles? Will it be the homicidal style of the hardcore ciruit that the Icons bring to the table?
Or will the upstart, unpredictable and relentless youth of the Wrecking Crew that takes the gold? This could be the dethroning and handing of the torch from the veteran Icons to the juvinated Wrecking Crew... [stops reading] That says it all...
[Steel tries to hold back a laugh, but doesn't. He bursts into laughter and starts slapping his knee in hysteria. He manages to calm himself for a brief moment before exploding into a maniacal cackle once again. After finally calming himself down, Steel looks into the camera and smirks, barely cracking a smile.]
STEEL: Thanks to all of you pitiful fools who have decided to be my sacrificial lambs, it means a lot. Whether I will admit it directly or not...this match will be a test of wills as well as of toughness and skill! It will
NOT be a
handing of the torch in any WAY, SHAPE or FORM! This is what I think of the entire CWC amd especially the Wreckin' Crew. Come here...
[Steel leads the camera down the hall and then he stops right in front of a closed door, still holding the flyer in hand. He turns to the camera and signals for them to wait.]
STEEL: Okay, I'm gonna show Cannonball, Eclipse, the CWC, its head office and booking committee what I think of'em. Hey Levi..., pay attention, 'cause ya know that if I done did it once...I sure as hell am gonna do it again! Just like we dominated the Tag Team battle royal and carried the tag division thus far in every fed we've taken part in, we've reached the time again. So get off yer asses, put down the chocolate quik and take a listen kiddies...
[Steel opens the door to reveal the bathroom. He shows the Prime Time in North America flyer to the camera then just drops it in the toilet. Then he pulls put his biogrpahy photos of the CWC wrestlers then drops them in the toilet along with the PTiNA flyer. He cracks a grin to the camera, then turns. You hear his zipper unzip then you hear him begin to urinate on the photos and flyer. You see him from behind, obviously having fun pissing on the papers. He begins whistling the "Andy Griffith Show" theme song as he concludes his "session". He zips up and motions for the camera to go closer...]
STEEL: See CWC and especially the LAWC, I don't give a DAMN who you say you are, 'cause we'll treat y'all like the fleas you are! So LA
Wrecking Crew, remember, I didn't really try to piss you off, but you just got PISSED ON! DEAL WIT IT! The rest of the CWC, I still don't like any of you, you money grubbin' fools. I got a contract, so legally, you can't do a damn thing 'bout this! Wreckin Crew, the
TRUE Hardcore Icons have come, and now this is our home. Either abide by the rules, or suffer the feelin' of HARDCORE HELL!
[Steel stares directly at the camera with a stern look. Then he flushes the toilet, the paper swirl around and disappear. The scene fades to black as the camera sees Steel laughing hysterically...]
