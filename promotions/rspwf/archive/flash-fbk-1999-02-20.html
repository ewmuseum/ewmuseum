[Flash] FBK...

20 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I'm looking to improve since I came back from my absence in e-wrestling so any constructive criticism is welcome...
[ SCENE: Post-Friday Night Fallout at the Pyramid in Memphis, Tennessee. Its only a few minutes after Fallout has just ended, most wrestlers just standing around doing post-card interviews. ICW's very own Tyler Rich is seen with and he waves the camera to come over to him. Rich has on a nice tan armani suit along with fine italian shoes and his hair is slicked back. He licks his lips and then takes a deep breath...]
TYLER RICH: Ladies and Gentlemen, you've all just witnessed another ICW Fallout that has to go down as one of the best to date. Some great things went down tonight and it keeps all wrestling fans across the nation on the edge of their seat waiting to see what ICW will do next week.
But now I'm gonna try to catch up with the man that won the "mini" battle royal earlier in the evening and came out with a challenge that shocked the world, ICW wrestler, Full Blood Killah. Just one moment...
[ Tyler Rich then walks down a hallway and comes to a door with the name "FBK" on it. He knocks on the door and a few moments later the door swings open. Standing a few feet behind the doorway is FBK. He's dripping wet and in a towel...most likely just got out of the shower. He speaks...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: What does your scrawny ass want? TYLER RICH: [Trembling in fear...] Hello sir...we haven't been formally introduced, I'm Tyler Rich, ICW interviewe--
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Da Killah don't give a damn what yo' name is! From here on out, you're camel crap in da Killah's book.
TYLER RICH: Very well sir, c-c-camel, well I can't say the other part, just not my nature. FULL BLOOD KILLAH: [Grabs Rich by the collar] Well its of da Killah's nature and you'll do what da Killah says, got it?
TYLER RICH: [Gulps] g-g-g-got it. FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Good to hear. Now that we're introduced, what can da Killah do for you? TYLER RICH: I'm here for a post-Fallout interivew mister Killah. Can you help me out and do so?
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: [Snickers] Of course...I win a battle royal and now I get some respect, thats the way it is I guess. I know the ladies watching at home would like nothing else than FBK sitting in a towel, sopping wet just out of the shower. It makes their hormones go wild, I know it. So what the hell...I'm up for it, I'll do the ladies that admire the most exhilarating, breath-taking wrestler in the sport today a favor. What do you wanna know Ty?
TYLER RICH: [Takes a deep breath] Phew, okay...well FBK, you've come of a win thats raised you in the ICW rankings so to speak. Was this a big win emotionally for you?
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Hades yes! Da Killah tossed out a lot of pieces of camel crap and almost got tossed out myself toward the end as well. But you know the rules, both feet gotta touch the ground; and I used that rule to my advantage and it helped me win the thing. Nyghtstorm put up a helluva fight, but he didn't finish the job and for that, it cost him the match. Nyghtstorm, as the old saying goes..."close but no cigar"...well, you're a prime example of it and as a good friend used to tell me...
..._SUCKS_... ..._FOR_... ..._YOU_!!! TYLER RICH: Very well then. After you won the battle royal, during the final hour of ICW Fallout, you came out and had yourself a little confrontation with
ICW's very own North American Champion, Tommy Grayson. Some words were exchanged and in the end, a challenge was made that you hope will happen next week on Fallout. What was the purpose of this challenge? You don't even know
D.B. Bugg!
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: I know I know. But this is the only way I get a shot at
Grayson and prove to him I'm the most exhilarating, breath-taking man in da eye see dubbya; that is if I get the green light from James Pillo--
[ Suddenly the door swings open and standing in the doorway is a man with a huge grin on his face. He has on a cheap suit with some tacky wing tips for shoes. He has a piece of paper in his hand and he holds it above his head, in the air...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: What the hell? You scared the poo-poo out of me for crying out loud! Everyone, this is my agent...Johnny Dyer. John-Boy, what do you want?
JOHNNY DYER: [At the top of his lungs] We did it! FBK is in the big time! Read the paper Killah...
[ Dyer hands FBK the piece of paper and FBK reads it. He then looks into the camera and flashes a sadistic smile, with an evil look in his ice cold eyes. He then proceeds...]
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Well well well...someone just got the respect he's been asking fo'! Ladies and Germs...next weeks Fallout Main Event is Tommy Grayson and Devon Case up against D.B. Bugg and...
..._ROUGH_... ..._AND_... ..._TOUGH_... ..._EFF_... ..._BEE_... ..._KAY_! Well those enormous pieces of camel crap in the ICW front office have smelled the dirty socks and came to their senses. FBK is where he _SHOULD_ be, in a main event. Next week when me and Bugg team up and beat the stupid right out of
Case and Grayson, the world is gonna recognize D.B. Bugg is great, and FBK is main event status. Bugg, I don't really know you and vice versa. But our common enemy is Tommy Grayson...I'm not asking to play the best friends game, but I'm asking you to be my partner, for one night just to show Grayson what a piece of garbage he really is. We'll teach you a lesson Tommy, one you'll never forget.
Grayson, you better forget about Pretty Boy Andrew for one night because next week on Fallout you better focus on me because next week, its time to...
..._MEET_... ..._YOUR_... ..._MAKER_!.... [ FBK snickers and hands the paper back to Dyer who then leaves after they shake hands. FBK looks back at Rich...]
TYLER RICH: What a main event match-up that promises to be, I'm looking forward to FBK making his ICW main event debut too! But don't get too tangled up in that match because you have a match at Rockin' Da World as well against a big player in other federations who's decided to try his luck in ICW...Jack
Daniels. Whats your comments on him? FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Jack Daneils...major player in other federations? Excuse me...[FBK then turns his head and bursts into laughter] Are you kidding me Ty?!
This guy is so stupid, its funny! I mean, he's obviously had one too many shots of Jack Daniels and ain't playing with a full deck. He's off the wall, and in the battle royal earlier tonight, I tossed his scrawny ass right over the top rope. He doesn't stand a chance against da Killah one on one. Hell, he doesn't even deserve to step in the same ring as da Killah. Jack, some people are made to be wrestlers and some people aren't. I am made to be a wrestler...you on the other hand are not. Use that information and run far, far away. Because Jack when we both walk the aisle on Tuesday and fight for a victory, I'm coming out with the upper hand, and well, you're _NOT_! Jack, sorry to tell you, but when you step in the ring with da Killah...
_YOU_... ..._GET_... ..._KILLED_! TYLER RICH: That could be the case for good ole' Jack Daniels this week at
Rockin' Da World, toon in to find out. Well FBK, its been a pleaure just as I suspected. Good luck next week against Jack Daniels and when you get your hands on your new nemesis Tommy Grayson...any closing comments?
FULL BLOOD KILLAH: Hmmm...yeah, shuttup Tyler! Jack, Tuesday you get a shot at the guy who eliminated you from the battle royal earlier tonight so I wish you the best of luck...because you're gonna need all the luck you can get because there ain't no way in hell you're gonna beat me. And a week from today, during the main event of Fallout, as the thousands [deep breath] upon _THOUSANDS_ of da Killah's fans chant his name...and the millions of Killah addicts have their eyes glued to tha tee vee as they watch FBK kick Tommy Grayson's scrawny ass up and down the ring from start to finish, they will realize how great FBK really is. Why? Because I'm...
..._THE MAN_.... ...._THE MYTH_.... ...._THE LEGNED_.... ...._DA KILLAH_!! [ Brief Pause ] FULL BLOOD KILLAH: And Grayson, Case, if you've got some sort of problem with that...come knocking at FBK's door...and I can guarandamntee he'll be ready to make your lives living hells...
_FROM_ _BELL_ _TO_ _BELL_!!! I'm looking forward to it...I can't say the same for you two... [ FBK then gets up and walks into the bathroom. He slams the door shut. The camera along with Tyler Rich walk outside and the camera then begins to FADE ]
