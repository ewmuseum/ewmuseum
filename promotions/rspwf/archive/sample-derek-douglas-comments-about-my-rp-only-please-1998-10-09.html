[Sample]Derek Douglas.. Comments about my RP only please

9 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The scene opens in a white and black painted room with only one little night light lighting up the room. in the shadows of the room can be seen a man sitting on the floor. Half of his face is showing as the other half is covered in shadows. The man is none other then the crazy Derek Douglas... Douglas moves his head around and slowley stands to his feet. He walks a few steps and disapears into the darness, but suddenly a light flickers on... The room is covered with pictures of Candy Man giving guys DDT's. Posters of Candy Man giving the crowd the thumbs up, and the life size doll of Candy Man is sitting in a rocking chair. Derek Douglas steps back into the picture and looks around like he is seeing things... Douglas is wearing a Candy Man t-shirt, Guess jeans, and a pair of combat boots... He looks around for a bit, and suddenly looks deep into the camera. He smiles and begins to speak]
Derek Douglas: Candy Man, a man for the people.. Cany Man, a sweet man that loves the fans more then he loves himself. A man that does what he does for one thing and that one thing is for his fans... Candy Man, I know you don't know me very well, but I would like to be your friend. If you ask Steve Houston about me he'll tell you I am a very good friend to have. Because I will do whatever is necessary to stay friends. If that means kissing your feet or washing your clothes I will, but please just be my friend...
[Douglas stops and smacks himself across his face. He looks into the camera again but this time with a frown across his face. He looks at the posters of
Candy Man on the wall and begins to speak]
Derek Douglas: Candy Man, I know your the world champion here in the IWC but do I care, No way. You Candy Man will be one of the victims on my way to the top. You all know who I am and you all know I'm the most dangerous man in the history of the wrestling world. So, Candy Man and or anyone else here in the
IWC... {Slight Pause} ...If you want to step into the ring with me it is your funeral....
[Douglas falls to the ground and begins to shake rapidly. Suddenly he stops and just layes there with his mouth wide open and his eyes rolling in the back of his head. Suddenly his eyes go normal and his mouth goes shut. He slowly steps back up to his feet and walks over to the tv monitor that is sitting on a table in the room. He turns the monitor on and watches with his eyes wide open]
Derek Douglas: Listen, this guy is good... {TV Monitor} [The scene opens in an arena. Derek Dougals is standing in the ring with no one even in the seats of the Arena. He is wearing a wife Beater, Guess jeans, and a pair of combat boots. In the arms of Douglas is the life size *DOLL* of Candy
Man. Douglas begins to move the _DOLLS_head around and begins to speak]
Candy Man[Derek Douglas]: Well fans, I think I should tell you all about this man standing next to me. This man Derek Douglas is one of the best wresters
I've ever seen and yes, we are friends. In fact, I have learned almost every wrestling move from this man. He is _THE_ most dangerous man in the world and I for one would never want to be in the same ring with him... Wrestling I mean because he would surely beat me senseless...
{Monitor Done} [Douglas turns off the monitor and begins to speak] Derek Douglas: See... Candy Man is my friend, and he said I was his teacher. Thank you Candy Man because you have just made me one of the happiest men in the world... Candy Man, I only wish you and I could hang out more often because you are such a nice guy...
[Douglas stops looks at the posters of Candy Man again and begins to swear under his breath. He looks into the camera again and begins to speak]
Derek Douglas: This sh*t on the wall must go, and it will as soon as I get to kick the sh*t out of Candy Man. Candy Man, do you know what a crimson mask is?
Well if you or anyone else has no idea of what one is just watch any of my past matches because I have given out so many of them it is now an expected thing.
Candy Man, I've seen you get beaten in a seven tables of death match and I've seen you win battle royals. Candy Man, one thing I've never seen is you and me in the ring together one on one. So, Candy Man if you have the guts you'll get into the ring with me because you know what will happen. You know you'll end up with a crimson mask...
[Douglas pulls a razor blade out of his pocket and cuts a gash in his forearm. He smears his hand through the blood on his arm and then splatters it across the poster of Candy Man. He grabs a towel and begins to wrap his arm up]
Derek Douglas: See Candy Man, this is your future, so be prepared because
I'm_NOT_EXTREME_ I'm Fuc*ing_CRAZY_!!! SO DEAL WITH IT... HaHaHaHaHaHa!!!
