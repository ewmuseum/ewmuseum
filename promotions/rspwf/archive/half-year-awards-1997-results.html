We have some words from him in a moment, but first of all, let me explain why [name withheld] was chosen. Many were considered,the closest runners being [name withheld] for his Anti-Janis action, and closer still, [name withheld] for his essays. [name withheld], fedhead of the IIWF, deserves this award though for many reasons. Now, I`ve got to say, I`ve been a critic of the IIWF, mostly during my time there, which was born out of frustration. I was used to leagues where you could do just about anything you wanted. In the IIWF, [name withheld] was not so lenient. He had a level of control that made for more coherent story lines, and furthermore, his required standards really brought the best out of a lot of players. It`s something I can now truly appreciate. In short, he has created a league which nearly everyone agrees is head and shoulders above the rest in terms of the quality of cards, maturity and approach. In fact, it`s a whole new approach that has never been done before and I doubt could be done again. [name withheld], most importantly though, has provided a model for fedheading that should not be ignored for it brings the best out of players, and is a welcome alternative to the styles of the Extreme/Hardcore leagues. "It really came as a surprise -- a pleasant one, of course -- to learn that I had been honoured with this award. I have never thought of the IIWF as doing a service to e-wrestling; I could certainly never claim to have such a lofty aim in mind when I started the league, along with a few like-minded souls, back in May last year. I simply wanted to create a league which conformed to the ideals I had in mind for e-wrestling. I had next to no experience in the "sport", despite having been a wrestling fan for some years. In February 1996, I joined my first e-fed. In April, it folded after only five short, delayed and badly-written cards, booked in such a way as to ignore the ideas of the players. I wanted to set up a league which would offer what handlers were looking for in a league, and within weeks, the IIWF was born. If one were to read the product that the IIWF put out in the first few weeks of its existence, one would find only the imprint of my creative hand. But as time has gone on, more and more people have given their input into the league in every aspect, from the most obvious ones of character and story development, to the writing duties of matches and report shows, to general ideas about the development of the league. Although I have retained overall control of the league's direction and output, it is now a very much more rich and diverse endeavour than it could ever have been had I alone worked on it over the past year. The IIWF only exists because of the players involved in it, and I am fortunate to have some of the finest players in all of e-wrestling involved in the league. It has been the players who have shaped the league into the entity that currently exists -- and I believe the fact that the IIWF consistently puts out product of a truly professional level on a weekly basis is due just as much to their contribution than to the overall control that I and my Vice-President exercise. I have taken a great deal of criticism, both personally and "professionally", about the direction in which we have taken the IIWF. It is probably true to say that it operates on criteria different to those of just about any other league you care to mention. Its golden rule remains "good writing = good push", but that now means so much more than it did when the league opened last year. Originally, "writing" simply meant sending in flashes once or twice a week. Now, it encompasses flashes, angle ideas, match strategies; everything in the IIWF is meticulously planned, if not always by the players, certainly by the bookers. I am proud of the degree of coherence the IIWF builds into its storylines, and hope that its action is interesting to even those who have no direct interest in the league. I also know that the IIWF is not to everybody's tastes. It's not an "extreme" fed, it's not a fed which is booked by the seat of its pants, it's not a fed where politics are a deciding factor. People have told me that reading an IIWF show is more like watching a show from the big two promotions on TV than anything else; perhaps that's true. I certainly aim for the same production values. I don't really know whether the IIWF has done a great service to e-wrestling. Of course, that is for others to judge. People now know the acronym, even if they aren't familiar with the league, and I am happy that the league's success has engendered such loyalty in its players. I would like to dedicate this award to everybody who has helped with the IIWF in whatever capacity over the past year, and in particular, to my former Vice-President, [name withheld]. In the days following the IIWF's first birthday, Steve left the post he had held since October in order to write a book, and the IIWF's success over the past year is in no small part due to his input; from devising the most devious angles conceivable to calming me down when problems, whether e-wrestling related or otherwise, reared their heads, Steve was a greater influence on the league than he would probably admit. The IIWF will move on, hopefully ever onward and upward, but I will always be grateful to Steve for all the support he has given both myself personally and the league. Thanks again! I hope that the IIWF continues to do service to the e-wrestling community, and I truly am honoured to have this award bestowed upon me." [name withheld], IIWF President. Well, that`s it folks. I wish I could have included comments people made about wrestlers, as well as the spoof awards and predictions, but time is running out for me. P.S. If one fed is thinking "Hey, there are supposed to be another set of results in there!" All I can say is that when I get 6 different people voting for exactly the same thing, with exactly the same text-layout, and with one person giving their reason for who they voted for as "because the fedhead told us to", I can smell a bit of vote-rigging and because of that, all six of those votes were discounted (they wouldn`t have won anything anyhow). P.P.S I`m never doing this again - too much hard work. P.P.P.S Thanks for the 174 people who voted.
