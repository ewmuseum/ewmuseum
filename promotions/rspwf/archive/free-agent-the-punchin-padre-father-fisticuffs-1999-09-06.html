*Free Agent* "The Punchin' Padre" Father Fisticuffs

6 September 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
First off, I am looking for a league that is based purely on roleplays. Also, I would prefer if it had only one card per week or maybe one every other week, as I have other stuff to do. I also prefer a league with only one or two championship belts. Please e-mail me if you are interested in the Pope's favorite wrestler, "The Punchin' Padre" Father Fisticuffs.
--------------------- Character Info: --------------------- Name: "The Punchin' Padre" Father Fisticuffs Location: Vatican City Height: 5'11" Weight: 255 lbs. Allignment: Face Description: In the ring, Father Fisticuffs wears a sleeveless, black, buttoned-down shirt, complete with the priestly white collar. He wears a pair of black shorts, black socks, and meticulously shined pair of black shoes.
Outside of the ring, Father Fisticuffs dresses in traditional clothing of the priesthood. Black pants, black shoes, and long-sleeved black shirt, with white collar.
History: Father Fisticuffs grew up on the mean streets of Vatican City. A poor orphan boy, Father Fisticuffs was often the target of street thugs and bullies. Needless to say, Father Fisticuffs was forced to learn to fight to survive. It was during a scuffle with a local street gang that Father
Fisticuffs met an individual who would have a profound effect on his life,
Sister Mary Suckerpunch.
On her way to Mass, Sister Mary Suckerpunch witnessed a young boy valiantly battle a group of young ruffians single handedly. Sister Mary
Suckerpunch suprised everyone when she came to the aid of the young orphan boy and, one by one, knocked out each member of the terrible street gang. The young boy was bewildered, yet thankful. Sister Mary Suckerpunch invited the young boy to stay at her convent, where he would be fed and cared for. It was here that the young boy learned where Sister Mary had learned to punch like that...
Sister Mary Suckerpunch, beside being a devout nun, was also a very skilled boxer. Sister Mary Suckerpunch was the Ms. Vatican City Gold Gloves
1945! With an undefeated record and the potential to bring female boxing to new heights in exceptance and popularity, and a chance at personal fame and fortune, Sister Mary Suckerpunch retired from the boxing world in order to further persue her religious calling.
Sister Mary Suckerpunch recognized the natural talent of the scrawny, young orphan as she witnessed him valiantly fight against the street thugs.
Sister Mary Suckerpunch took the young boy under her wing, encouraged him to enter the priesthood and trained him in the art of boxing. Within months, the once scrawny boy was transformed into a top contender on the amateur circuit.
At the peak of his career, the boy won the Vatican City Gold Gloves Title. And then at this peak, he retired...
Reporters were baffled, and the young man refused to answer questions, as he had moved to a convent with a group of monks and taken a vow of silence.
Father Fisticuffs slowly faded into obscurity, devoting himself full-time to his religious studies.
It was during his stay with the monks that Father Fisticuffs had a religious revelation. According to Fisticuffs, a voice told him it was his duty to travel to America. In America, he was to become a proffessional wrestler and use this priviledged position to clean up pro wrestling by promoting good sportsmanship and brotherhood and use his earnings to open an orphanage for street children like himself.
Theme Song and Artist: "Like a Prayer" by Madonna Entrance Write-up: (Please follow example) [Googly Moogly's "Fat Chicks Eat Fat" starts to play.] ["Like a Prayer" by Madonna plays over the loudspeakers.] ----------------------- Wrestling Style: Boxer/Brawler ----------------------- Finishing maneuver(s): The Hail Mary Description of maneuver: Basically, a flying fistdrop. Father Fisticuffs climbs to the top ropes, leaping off and nailing his opponent, standing or on the mat, with a right fist.
Set-up maneuver: Uppercut 3 Strengths: 1. Extremely brave. Never cowers in the face of a challenge. 2. Skilled boxer and brawler. 3. Fan favorite. Very charismatic. 3 Weaknesses: 1. NEVER cheats or fights unfairly. Strictly adheres to the rules, sportmanship, and good morality. 2. Extremely friendly, sometimes naively so, to opponents. 3. Expects others to adhere to the ideals of good sportsman-like conduct, fairplay, etc., even Faces who may bend the rules, boast, or brag. Needless to say, this attitude costs Father Fisticuffs some friendships with other wrestlers.
------------- Manager Info: (Optional) ------------- Name: Sister Mary Suckerpunch Height: 5'0" Weight: 115 lbs. Gender: Female Attire: Traditional nun-garb. Description: Sister Mary Suckerpunch is an elderly old woman with a terribly mean-looking face. Despite her tiny demeanor, she is one tough cookie. Her face always sports a scowl.
History: Sister Mary Suckerpunch has played an important part in Father Fisticuffs life. She rescued "The Punchin' Padre" from the streets of Vatican
City, helped enter him into the priesthood, and personally trained Father
Fisticuffs in the art of boxing.
Sister Mary Suckerpunch, herself, was an extremely talented female amateur boxer. Her extreme devotion to the nunhood prevented her from attaining international fame and fortune, as she retired from the ring at the peak of her amateur career.
As Father Fisticuffs manager, she is more willing to bend the rules than the Father. She frequently makes verbal threats against those who bend the rules, threaten Father Fisticuffs, or are generally bad people. Following defeats, Sister Suckerpunch has been known to publicly humiliate and punish those of questionable morals by using her ruler and slapping them on the wrist and spanking them on their buttocks.
Allignment: Face Object: (What does your manager bring to the ring?) Sister Suckerpunch brings a metal ruler to the ring. She uses the ruler to publicly humiliate and punish opponents.
Does he/she interfere? Sister Mary Suckerpunch only interferes when another wrestler or manager interferes. She has been known to come into the ring and throw a few punches that she was famous for in the old days.
