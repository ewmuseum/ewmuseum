Sengir seeking a quality fed

28 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hmm... since TMW bought the farm and died I'm looking for a new fed for one of my characters... I have included a sample flash and an application form.
Easy G ________________________________________________________________________ Wrestler's name: Sengir Hometown: Cologne, Germany Height: 6'1" Weight: 218 lbs Alignment: Neutral Theme Music: Vangelis: "Conquest of Paradise" Appearance: Sengir is a young man, maybe in his early twenties. He has long, jet-black hair, down to his shoulderblades. Sengir usually wears a black t-shirt, often with a gothic theme to it or the shirt of an alternative band, as well as faded and torn black jeans and trainers or cowboy boots. He wears a small golden crucifix in his left ear and had a red rose tattoo on his shoulderblade, usually covered by his ring attire. Sengir sometimes paints his face white with a red rose or a red heart on his forehead. Sengir is well-built but not musclebound, no body fat.
Profile: Sengir grew up as Tristen Monette, the happy-go-lucky youngest son of a wealthy German family. In his late teens, however, he was involved in an accident orchestrated by a jealous ex-girlfriend. He spend almost a year hospitalized, hovering on death's door, and when his condition finally stabilized and he made his recovery he realized that most friends had abandoned him for dead and to his father he was little more then constant expenses while in hospital. Tristen grew bitter.
After the long draining struggle with death he had nothing left. Tristen abandoned his name and adopted the ring name Sengir, a vampire from a fantasy saga. He became a wrestler and shootfighter in the small
European circuit in order to survive and regain his self respect. However, he was a cruel man who more often then not drowned in self-hatred and resorted to cruelty. Since he had no real wrestling background he made up his lack in technical skills with sheer intensity.
His success in Europe was not unnoticed by American talent searchers and he signed a contract with the ESWP in 1997. He soon joined the most hated stable in the ESWP, Death Enterprises, under the guidance of the
Firestarter. Under his leadership Sengir soon rose to the very top of the ESWP Cruiserweights, and on July 30th, 1997, the inevitable encounter between him and then-ESWP World Junior Heavyweight champion
"Enigma" Takezo Musashi took place. Just when Musashi had ascended the top turnbuckle for his Starsault Press Firestarter hit his ankle from behind and enabled Sengir to regain momentum, and a Superbomb later
Sengir was the new champion.
However, he was not happy. While watching the tape afterwards he realized that without Firestarter's interference he would have clearly lost the match, and his crusade to regain self-respect and dignity drowned in his self-hatred and the boos of the fans. Sengir made a decision, and he and some other members of Death Enterprises, among them his friend and valet Jezra Wagner, left the group and former the Dark
Sovereigns. For the first time in his life Sengir was cheered when the
Sovereigns stood up against Firestarter and his pack, and the crowd reaction paired with the careful guidance and soul caressing of Jezra resulted in Sengir gradually regaining some of his long-lost dignity and self-esteem, and after so many years Sengir once again was found happiness and a reason to live.
Over the next few months Sengir and the Sovereigns battled the likes of
Death Enterprises and the Harlequins, and Sengir always stood up for his believes against all odds. While Sengir is still a cynic and tends to get very cocky at times, in his heart he is a warm, fun loving individual and a wrestler who has never backed down from a challenge and will fight for what he believes. He is a cynical soldier at times, and a childish prankster at others.
Finishing maneuver: "The Setting Sun" (Tombstone/Belly-to-Belly Piledriver) Sengir's trademark move is the Setting Sun, a devastating belly to belly piledriver that has gained him many wins in the past and is executed with deadly efficiency. The drawback of this move is that it can only be used effectively against opponents he can scoop up, thus eliminating chances of him using it against big heavyweights.
Five signature moves: "The Embrace" (Inverted DDT) Sengir uses the Embrace out of nowhere, with incredible speed. It is often used as the countermove against various Suplexes and other holds involving Sengir's neck. It was his prime finisher when he was still only wrestling in Europe, and he now uses the Embrace primarily against opponents who are too heavy to scoop up into the Setting Sun.
Flying Clothesline The Flying Clothesline is often the set-up for the Setting Sun, and despite Sengir's small size, his intensity and the speed when executing this maneuver is enough to send all but the heaviest opponents to the mat.
Knife-Edge Chops to the throat While Sengir has no martial arts background, he can deliver nasty chops to his opponents' throat in rapid succession, sending them staggering back and gasping for air.
Savat Kick/Superkick Sengir rarely, if ever, resents from brawling and powermoves, but his agility allows him to connect with devastating Savat kicks he often uses against charging opponents, usually hitting them precisely in the jaw.
Small Package with pulled tights. Sengir very rarely cheats; in fact, his attitude and code of honor will never permit him to resent to foreign objects (unless the rules of the match permit this) or outside interference. He wants to beat his opponent on his own, in the middle of the ring. However, in certain situations, especially when he is indifferent about his opponent, he sometimes gets cocky ("What am I doing here against this twit? Let's get this over with") and will resort to a good ol' tight assisted small package or even simply walk away.
Priorities: I) Endurance Sengir, like the old saying goes, "can take a licking but keeps on ticking". His unbreakable will and pure intensity will always push the very last out of him, and he will rather have his body give out on him then to quit.
II) Brawling He has no martial arts or amateur wrestling background but his furious brawling flurry consisting of rapid Knife-Edge Chops, Forearm Smashes, and European Uppercuts has brought many stronger and bigger opponents down due to his sheer intensity. However, against opponents clearly outweighing and outpowering him, he may be at a considerable disadvantage.
III) Intelligence Often battling against the odds, Sengir has developed quite a keen vision against outside interferences and ring positioning.
Manager: "The Ice Queen" Jezra Wagner Jezra is one of the former members of Death Enterprises who broke away with Sengir to form the Dark Sovereigns. At 5'7" and 147 pounds, Jez is a stunning woman in maybe her mid- to endtwenties and has long, whitish-blond hair, ice grey eyes and very pale skin. She usually dresses in white, blue or silver and wears elaborate jewelry showing snowflakes or icicles. Jezra is a cocky bitching foxy snotnose, no mistake about it, but she loves the contact to the fans and showing her curves. A Siberian native, she speaks with a soft Russian accent. She will very rarely accompany Sengir to ringside, unless his opponent has a female valet himself who she needs to keep an eye on, and being a talented submission wrestler herself with multiple ESWP title reigns she does so quite well. Her real talents are behind the scenes where she uses her assets and charm to get Sengir various advantages, be it booking favors, air time, or simply a better hotel room.
______________________________________________________________________ (Fade in: a secratary is sitting behind a rather messy desk, papers, pens, and various magazines are lying all over the table. The secretary is a short-haired blonde busily painting her fingernails and looking generally unmotivated. Suddenly, it knocks from outside the door. The secretary sighs, lights a cigarette, then mutters a "Come in" and the door opens. In step Sengir and Jezra Wagner. Sengir is wearing black jeans and a plain black tee-shirt, Jezra is wearing a white leather vest, jeans shorts and cowboy boots, her long hair is hanging down freely)
Secretary: "Yeah, what is it?" Sengir: "Are we right here? We are looking for Twin Cities Wrestling
Front Office..." Secretary (taking a drag off her cigarette): "Yeah." Jezra: "Well, honey, my client had a complaint he wanted to report." Secretary (sounding completely uninterested): "Okay, let me get the form... what was the name?"
Sengir: "Sengir." Secretary (yawning): "Sengir... and your last name?" Sengir: "None. Just Sengir." Secretary (shrugs): "Okay then, just Sengir. What kind of complaint?" Sengir: "I am unhappy with my current situation in Twin Cities Wrestling and am convinced that booking a reigning World Champion in the undercard of a houseshow against opponents like Mr.Norman and Mr.Complete, who are not my caliber, is a waste of..."
Secretary: "I am sorry, I cannot help you with that. You would have to talk directly to the Twin Cities Wrestling Presid..."
Jezra: "That's what we're here for, snotnose." Secretary (frowning): "Unfortunately right now he's not available. Anything else I can do for you?" Sengir: "Well, I would be very grateful if you can pass my complaints and challenges on to the TCW booking committee and..."
Secretary (taking another drag off her cigarette): "Sorry, I cannot do that. None of my business. I guess you'll have to come again sometime next week."
Sengir: "But..." Secretary (looking at her watch): "I am sorry, but I have to ask you to leave. It's five o'clock now and I'm outta here."
Sengir: "Uhm..." Secretary: "Bye." (Sengir turns to Jezra, and they exchange a few whispers before Jezra, all of the sudden, launches herself over the table, with amazing, catlike agility, and lands crashing on top of the secretary, who cries out loud and drops her cigarette. The chair collapses and both Jezra and the secretary crash onto the floor. Jezra, fast and furious, quickly turns the secretary onto her stomach, grabs her foot and twists her ankle around in a Shamrock-style anklelock submission hold)
Secretary: "IIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAAAAAOOOWW!!" Jezra: "Now listen to me, you little slut. This man here is a world champion. You will forward every single one of his wishes to the bigwigs here and make sure they listen, understand?"
Secretary: "But... but... OWW!! Let me... that's not right... you can't do that... EEEEEEEEEEEEOW!! STOP! PLEASE! OW! OW! AAAAAAAAAAH!!
AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAHH!!" (Jezra once again twists the poor secretary's ankle around who shrikes and screams, and tears of pain appears in her eyes. Jezra releases the terrible anklelock for a second and asks the secretary again)
Jezra: "Let me ask you again, you cheeky little snotnose. Do you understand?" Secretary: "Yes! Yes! I understand! I will do whatever you want! Please... my foot..." Jezra: "I will make sure you will never walk again if you don't listen very, very carefully. First of all, you will tell those bigwigs never again to book my client, a two-time world champion, against farmboys with inferiority complexes like this Ultra Idol clown. Got that?"
Secretary: "Yes! Yes! It won't happen again, I will make sure... but please... my foot..." Jezra: "And then you will tell your most exalted President that my client demands a title shot at the TCW Cruiserweight championship. His ability, as everybody could see, paired with the fact that he is the reigning ESWP World Junior Heavyweight Champion, makes him contender number one, and no one is worthier to carry your little piece of copper around then him. Got that?"
Secretary: "But... but... I can't do that... Contender number one is... AAAAAAAAAAAAAH! OWW! IIIIIIIIIIIIIEEEEEEEEEOOOOOWWW! PLEASE! PLEASE!! STOP!! MY FOOT!! AAAAAAAAAAAAAA-OWW!" (The secretary is clearly in major pain as Jezra, still on her back, twists her ankle around in a very unnatural angle. As Jezra finally releases after a few seconds the secretary is shaking all over, she is sobbing and crying)
Secretary: "P-Please... no more... I got it, I got it, I will make sure you will get all you ask for... my foot..."
(The secretary is sobbing now uncontrollably. Jezra sneers, then gets up and releases the poor and now humbled secretary from her pain. Sengir walks over to her and picks up her cigarette, takes a deep drag, then winces and shivers why Jezra angrily walks out of the door)
Sengir: "You know... you should really not smoke. Cigarettes can really be bad for you." (The secretary is holding her badly hurt foot, still sobbing and crying. Sengir takes her head in both arms and gently turns her towards him) Sengir: "Hey, hey... now we're not gonna cry, are we? Big girls don't cry, do you know that?"
(The secretary looks at Sengir with big, frightened eyes full of tears) Sengir: "Now, don't you want to tell me your name, little one?" Secretary (reluctantly): "L-Laura..." Sengir (smiling): "Now that's a really nice name, Laura. You know, you were unbelievably lucky that Jez was in a good mood today. You know, if she's mad you just don't want to be next to her. You are lucky she wasn't mad. You don't want Jez to be mad at you, do you?"
(Laura shakes her head) Sengir: "Good! You are a very bright girl. So then you will surely do all the things she told you to, will you?"
(Laura swallows hard, then nods her head, tears still running down her face. Sengir takes another drag from the cigarette, then plants a kiss on Laura's cheek, smiles and leaves the room as well, as the scene fades to black)
