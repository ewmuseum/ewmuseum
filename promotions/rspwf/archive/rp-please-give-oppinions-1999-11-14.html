(The scene opens inside a Project-X flash back in a cold and damp cell inside the military base known as Area 51.There are two scientists looking at Project-X through a one way mirror on the wall.
In the room there are no beds,chairs or funiture of any kind.Project-X is sitting on the cold surface of the concrete floor with blood driping from his body.That is what you get for trying to escape.
The scne then cuts to the other side of the one way mirror where two scientists are looking on at the beaten and bloodied Project-X.The first scientist is a bald unatractive man and the second is a long brown haired average looking woman.)
Woman:Do you think we went too hard on the punishment this time? I mean he does not look too good.
Man:Nonsense,you should know by now that Project-X has a quick healing system like none other.He will be on his feet by tommorow.I heard you let the father go today.I thought they were going to dispose of him,he knows too much.
Woman:No problem,we have sworn him to secrecy.He did go of though rambling about something that they will reunite.
Man:I dont really cae what that evil little man said.All we needed was his cemen and we got that.I must say this for him though he produced a fine specimen.
Woman:It was a pity the mother died at birth. Man:That was a pity wasnt it,we did not get a chance to study her.But she will live on thgrough her son.He is the most interesting specimen I have had the plesure to study.He grew to his full size in a month and started speaking a month later.Is doctor Morbius ready to conduct the last experiment?
Woman:Yes,he says to bring Project-X down whenever your ready. Man:Excellent,Project-X will become the first in his line of government assassins.After this experiment he will be loyal and obey our every command.This will make the months of research worth while.Send them in.
(The woman scientist presses a red button on the counter in front of her and we can see through the mirror six muscle men walk in to the cell carrying knight sticks.Project-X stumbles to his feet holding his ribs.One of the muscle men goes to hit Project-X with the knight stick but Project-X moves out of the way and nails the man with a right hand.The other five muscle men then tackle Project-X to the ground and hold him down.Another scientist then enters the cell carrying a long pointed needle.Project-X struggles but securly held down by the muscle men.
The scientist then sticks the needle in to Project-Xs arm and sedates him.The muscle men then carry Project-X out of the cell and lower him on to a stretcher.They then strap Project-X securly in to the Stretcher as the scene focuses again on the two scientists.)
Man:Lets hope this new sedative works this time.His imune system bloked out the last one and he nearly escaped.
(The scene then cuts to Project-X being rolled down a corridor on the stretcher.As they roll him to lab of Dr.Morbius,little do they know that the sedative did not work and Project-X iss acting.
They eventually reach a door with the name tag Dr.Morbius on it.The scientist that unsuccessfuly tried to sedate Project-X,types in a code on the panel beside the door.The door then slides open like something out of star trek.
The muscle men then roll Project-X in to the lab and place him under the opperating light.The muscle men then leave and Project-X is left with
Dr.Morbius in his lab. Dr.Morbius can not be seen by Project-X as he is looking up at the roof.Then suddenly an old wrinkled face pops up above Project-X,it is Dr.Morbius.)
Dr.Morbius:Ok now,stay still and you wont feel a thing. (Dr.Morbius then grabs a scalpal and lowers it down to Project-Xs head.But before he can cut anything,Project-X snaps his restraints and grabs
Dr.Morbius by the neck.Dr.Morbius starts screaming for security as Project-X choke slams him through a research table.Dr.Morbius keeps trying to reach over to the security alarm on his desk but can not move as the choke slam took out his hip.
Project-X then trys to leave the lab but the door is locked tight.Project-X then takes a few steps back and bursts through the solid steel door with his shoulder.The alarms then go off as they have detected one of the doors being breached.
Project-X does not get far until he is blocked off by a dozen security guards.Project-X smiles at the security guards and then jumps about 20 feet in the air landing on top of about five of the security guards knocking them out.The other seven guards circle Project-X as they all eye each other carefuly.
Then suddenly one of the guards breaks the tension and leaps out at Project-X.Project-X then knocks him out in short order with a right hand.Three more guards run towards him trying a triple clothes line,Project-X then ducks the clothesline and hits all three of them with a clothesline of his own.
The other guards then run off in fright as a loud voice booms through the base. Voice:Security lock down in 10 seconds. (Project-Xs eyes widen as he runs down a corridor.) Voice:10,9,8,7..... (Project-X then reaches the end of the corridor.To see a way outside.Everybody is running around as they all wish to get to their desired place before the lock down.Project-X then starts running for the exit as the voice counts down and the door starts to lower.)
Voice:......6,5,4,3,2....... (Project-X reaches the door but the door is near the ground.Project-X then drops down to he ground and rolls under the door,outside in to the Nevada desert.)
Voice:.......1,lock down complete. (Project-X smells the air an his eyes open wide with excitement as he has never been outside before.He then starts talking to himself.)
P-X:Prepare your self you insignificant Humans.Because you kept me captive I will destroy every one of you.Prepare for the new age of evil!
(Project-X then starts making his way through the desert with out any water or food.He has a good head start as the doors of Area 51 will not open again until tommorow.The flash back then fades and we see Project-X sitting in front of the camera in his dressing room.The scene then fades as Project-X gives the camera an evil smile.)
FADE OUT
