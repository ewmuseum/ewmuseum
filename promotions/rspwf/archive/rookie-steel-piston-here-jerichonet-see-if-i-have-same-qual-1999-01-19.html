[ROOKIE] STeeL PiSToN.. Here JerichoNet.. See if I have same Qual..

19 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Alright.. This is Kyle.. Aka to all you E-wrestlers... Steel Piston.. When
I posted my last rp a few people wanted to see my next rp and to see if I stayed on the same great quality I was at before.. So here it is guys.. Tell me what you think and critic.. This is my first rp with an opponent.. This was done for D.S.W.A.
Enjoy :) [Fade in] [Down in the outskirts of Atlanta, Georgia, down beneath the ground, there lies an abandoned subway station... No trains, not even any rodents scurry the repugnance of the forsaken grounds.. The only sound that can be heard is thus', feet scraping the grounds of the subways underpass...]
[As the scraping continues, the camera begins to pan to the inside of the abandoned station.. As the view reaches the inside of the subway it begins to concentrate hard on any action that begins to stir up.. Finally, a bulky, muscular figure, begins to converge.. This figure, is running constantly without stopping, up and down the train track's of the station.. Thedark shadow of the man keeps pumping its arms up and down, continuously.. The few lanterns that swing from the roof of the subway station are barely producing enough light to bring anything into complete focus..]
[Finally, without any sudden warning, the scraping stops and the shadow of the man comes to a halt... Stillness sweeps the grounds.. For a matter of minutes no action is seen or heard until a large "Scream" is unleashed by the man in the shadows.. The scream unleashed, obviously from the pain of the mans training and conditioning.. After the echo's of the scream comes to a hush, the black flesh and skin of the Black man comes into focus.. The man, who is wearing a gray Nike sleeve cutoff T-shirt, a pair of Nike shorts, and a pair of
Nike cross trainers, begins to walk towards the camera in a calm yet collective manner..]
[As the man reaches the area where the camera stands he stands in a fixed and gazed look.. He stands there for a few seconds without any further movement.. Out of no where, the man begins to speak out in a state of tranquillity...]
[The man begins to speak...] [Steel Piston]:The pain and the undergo I go through to make myself fit and sculpt for battle.. Too make myself in-shape for anything I may encounter in the future that awaits me. [*Exhales a breath*] While I was active and a contender in the I. B. F. I was never overmatched nor' overpowered.. I was never out of shape or slothful. Now, with me coming off of a two year leave of absence from the ring, I must once again, take myself to the point I was once at.. The point of excellency.. The point to where, every night, I showcased my talents and my skills.. To where every night, I laid out the man who opposed and the man who "Once thought" that he would actually be a contender against myself.. The great, Steel Piston.. [*Smirks while exhaling another breath*]
[Steel Piston begins to walk around, circling the same spots over and over again as he begins to speak while doing so.. ]
[Steel Piston]:Now, as the tremor and the awaiting stops, I have finally been granted with an opponent.. My opponent, the man with the biggest ego and the littlest intellect I have ever seen in my professional career.. [*Snickers*]
The night of January 26th, 1999, a new era will begin.. A new saga will unfold.. And a new contender will be unleashed.. Myself, playing that role and well.. My opponent, Daddy Desire, playing the role of a punching bag for _me_.
[*Intense Look*] I have awaited this date for too long. _I_ have waited and waited to shine my greatness upon the D. S. W. A. and to show all of you, who watch and observe the D.S.W.A., I am gonna show you all that hard work..
[*Chuckles*] I am gonna show you all _Hard work does pay off_..! [*Calms down a bit*] [Steel Piston stops in his tracks and doesn't move for a few seconds.. The lanterns that are holding the lights begin to dim down to an even more dimingly light.. Now you can _Barely_ see the figure of Steel Piston..Yet,he continues to speak...]
[Steel Piston]: As I am surrounded by the little light that does appear, it still is enough to clear my vision.. Though, Daddy Desire, when a swift right cross comes your way, you will be seeing nothing but pitch black..
Your future will become unclear.. If you are lucky enough to still have a future.. [*Smirks*] Now, Mr.Desire.. Before I came here to condition myself, I observed your last interview and what I saw and heard was just to funny to witness.. After the first half of your interview I abrupted with laughter and just _Had_ to switch off the television.. [*Bursts into laughter*] Though...
[*Calms back down and gets more serious*] Though Mr. Desire.. What I did view,
I would like to speak about right now... [*Pauses for a second*] First off,
Daddy Desire.. Where did you get the idea that you had to face me _And_ this fool named Jake Kelly? Uhh uh.. You should be glad Desire, glad that you
"Don't" have to face the two of us, because you already have enough to carry on your shoulders with _Me_ alone as your opponent... You have now convinced me
Desire that you have _No_ brain cells at all, nor' do you care about our match, because if you did you would have read the Monday Night card more carefully..
[*Bows and Shakes head*] You must think that this is all fun and games Desire, when it's not.. Maybe, in other federations, you might of had some luck with you getting jobbers as your opponents.. Maybe, but that is not for me to judge.. What you _must_ worry about _now_ though is the fact that, next Monday, in the Astro Dome, with more than 10,000 people watching, you must brawl with the "Best.." You must brawl with _me_... Can you pull it off? Can you overcome those who are greater than you? Can you withstand all odds and stand toe to toe, face to face with me? I doubt it, few agree... Only time will tell...
[Steel Piston]: Now.. Mr.Desire, before I leave you to ponder on my words I would love to speak about one more subject. The fact of your ignorance and your childish comments.. Now, Desire, you said these following words towards me in your last interview. You said, "Then there is Steel Piston. What a name. I wonder if the wrestler is just as dumb as the damn name." [*Chuckles in disgust*] First off, Daddy Desire.. When did the name of the man make the man?
The men make the name.. Now if I were to say _anything_ about your name there would be "Too Much" to criticize about it.. That is why I have not and will not shed light on that subject..You are a lucky man though Desire that I decided not to.. [*Smirks*] Now, before I depart, I would like to say one more thing to
Mr. Desire.. [*Pauses for a second*] Mr.Desire, the lines have been drawn for battle.. The teams have been made..And _IT_ has begun.. Where do you stand?
Where will you lie..? Think about that for a little, Mr. Desire and until you come up with an answer.. Maybe these last words will float your boat..
[*Brushes hand across chin*] Damn.. It is good to be me.. [Closing Scene] A big smile and evil grin begins to appear on the happy face of Steel Piston. He begins to stretch his legs trying to get all the cramps out and drops his leg back to the ground. He begins to looks around the train sation for a second and notices this is where he once played with his child-hood friends... Yet another smirk comes to the face of Steel Piston.. He then, with that smirk and grin exits the train station in a jog... [Fade to Black] [name withheld] Handler of: Steel Piston Currently Competes in: D.S.W.A. DSWA Record: 0-0-0 "Get in my face and I _WILL_ put you in your place.." "If you mess with Steel, then you don't know the deal."
