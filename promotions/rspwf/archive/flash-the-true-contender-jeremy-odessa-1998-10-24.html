[flash] "The True Contender" Jeremy Odessa

24 October 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens to a jam packed OSWA house show. Metallica's "Enter Sandman" blasts over the PA system as the entrance lights up with pyro. Jeremy Odessa runs out to the ring carrying the World Title and slides under the ropes. He grabs a mic and begins talking...)
Odessa: Ladies and Gentlemen, the OSWA Women's World Champion....."The
Toughest Woman Alive" Holly!!!!!
(The music starts up again as Holly runs to the ring with her title wrapped around her waist and a box under her arm...)
Odessa: First, I want to congradulate Freelance on being the new Television
Champion, you earned it. Second, I want to address Patriot. You proved yourself worthy of a title shot and on October 28 you're going to get it!
(Jeremy Odessa unstraps his title and drapes it over his shoulder...) Odessa: A great wrestler, who's name I can't say on the air, once said something that has stuck with me. He said that it's not the title that makes a wrestler, it's a wrestler that makes a title. Only a true champion can carry a world championship with the honor and respect that it deserves.
That's why I brought this. (He reaches into the box that Holly is holding and pulls out the "EWA US
Title" and wraps it around his waist...)
Odessa: This is the EWA US Title. I defended it undefeated for over six months until an injury caused me to retire from wrestling for almost a year. On my last night in the EWA I hobbled out to the ring with a shattered ankle and and defended this title for the last time. I fought for my life and managed to win on one leg. After the match the president walked out to the ring and named me the lifetime EWA US Champion and gave me the belt.
(Odessa looks down at the shiny gold plate...) Odessa: In that ring as I looked at the title I invisioned it hanging on the wall of the Dungeon with a nice display but I knew that it deserved better. I looked out into the crowd and saw thousands of screaming fans that had made me a superstar and then I knew what I had to do. I took that title and threw it out into the crowd. It sailed through the air and was caught by some guy that had his son with him. The kid looked like he was about ten and this was probably his first live wrestling match. The guy wrapped the belt around his son's waist and I left that arena a true champion.
About three months ago I got this package in the mail, it had the belt and a letter from that man.
(Holly hands him the letter from the box and Odessa starts reading it...) Dear Mr. Odessa, I am the man that caught this title the night you left the EWA. I gave this to my son and he loved it. He would walk around the house wearing it and pretending to be a wrestler. He defended the title against all of his stuffed animals and he put up a big poster of you on his wall. What you did for my son was truely the act of a champion and I just wanted to thank you for giving my son so many happy memories. He charished this title more than anything else and it brought him a lot of happiness.
Unfortunately, last month my son lost his long battle with cancer. His last wish was that you get this title back and that I let you know what you did for him. Thank you very much, you are a true contender.....
(Holly wipes a tear from her cheak as Odessa hands her the title and she puts it back in the box...)
Odessa: Patriot, next week you get your shot. You have the opportunity to become World Champion but first you have to go through me. I know what it means to be a real champion, you're in for the fight of your life! This one's for you kid.
(They leave the ring and walk back though the entrance. A preview of the upcoming card flashes on the screen...)
