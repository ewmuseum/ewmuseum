[FREE AGENTS] Jedah & The Fabulous Fisticuffs

1 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The name's [name withheld]. Most of you know me, some don't. Well, I'm looking for my return to handling. I stopped for a while to attempt Fed Heading, but that didn't work out. So, I'll give you a little bit of lowdown on each of my characters, and if you feel like you want them in your fed, drop a line. Don't just send an application. I want to see what the fed has to offer. If possible, include the fed's talent roster.
SINGLES WRESTLERS Jedah - Also been known as Jedah the Damned and "Dark Savior" Jedah Talikite. 7', 310 lbs., a vicious heel powerhouse. He's a very dark, gothic character. Similar to what The Undertaker is right now, but Jedah started his career back in '97. He's a follower of Death(not Satan or any form of the demon), and harbors no emotions towards anyone. His history is very enigmatic, as is his entourage. Has a career record of 52 wins, 15 losses, and three draws. Has held
IWF/WoW North American Belt(2x), GFWA Eastern Heritage Belt, and DWI World Championship Belt(2x/Undefeated). "Ravishing" Roy Rainer - Character based on myself, although I never really wrestling him much. 6'4", 250 lbs., an arrogent face/heel technician. Cocky son of a bitch, with an extensive technical knowledge in wrestling. He's a mix of the Old School and New School, but doesn't recognize either one. He only looks out for one man in life, and that would be himself.
TAG TEAMS The Acolytes of the Damned - Belial and Azmodan, Jedah's Acolytes. Both are of equal size, 6'7" and 290 pounds. Both heel power/aerialist. Sort of a package with Jedah. You get Jedah, you get the Acolytes one way or another. They are very zealous in their master's quest, and will not allow anyone to interfere with their master's business.
The Fabulous Fisticuffs - "Il Duce" Antonio Scalisi(6'0", 250lbs.) and "Dragon's Rage" Xiao Li Kui(6'5", 350lbs.). Face. Scalisi is the aerialist, Xiao Li is the power expert. They haven't been around in over a year, but they are known by some people. They're managed by "Ravishing" Roy Rainer, the brains behind the Fab. Fisties. The only goal on their mind is to prove that there are none better than them, and that no one else is worthy enough to wear the belts besides them.
I can provide a complete record and history of Jedah ONLY! He was the only one I took the time for doing so. Drop me a line and tell me who you want and I'll let you guys know.
