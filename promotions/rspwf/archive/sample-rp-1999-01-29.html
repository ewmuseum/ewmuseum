Dave Subject: Shelter From the Storm. . . [A long, abandoned stretch of highway sets the scene. It is somewhere out in America, among one of its many arid, dusty desert roads. The sun is at its apex, and beats down mercilessly on the cracked concrete, splintery telephone poles, and sand that dominate the scene. Slowly, the camera focuses in, the zoom becoming tighter and tighter, until it stops on a sight familiar to most UEW fans, the sight of the UEW American Champion,
"Black Knight" Alex Martinez. Martinez sits atop the back of his car, a bright red Corvette convertible. The car is in remarkable condition, it is clear that the giant's stop here was a deliberate one. Martinez wears only a plain white t-shirt, that is soaked through with sweat, and a pair of dusty black pants. His long black hair is left loose, and though it is matted to his head with perspiration, occasionally, a hot breeze will lift from the sands strong enough to buffet his ebon locks. The long, angry red scar that runs diagonally across Martinez's face, from the right side of his forehead to just below his left cheek is interrupted only by his silver
and black mirrored sunglasses. Slowly, the seven foot enforcer of the New Alliance turns around, and sets his gaze on the camera. Despite the fact that his eyes are hidden behind the reflective mirrors of his sunglasses,
Martinez seems to be looking beyond the camera, seeing into some far off place that only he knows about. Faintly, in the background, Bob Dylan's
"Shelter From the Storm" can be heard playing, and occasionally, when
Martinez falls silent, a bit of the melancholy song can be picked up.
Before speaking, Martinez draws into his muscular frame a long, deep breath, allowing the opening to be heard.]
### 'Twas in another lifetime, one of toil and blood When blackness was a virtue and the road was full of mud I came in from the wilderness, a creature void of form. "Come in," she said, "I'll give you shelter from the storm."#### Martinez: Imagine, if you will, a place of utter darkness. A room perhaps. Inside that room, there is no light at all. You are left utterly in the darkness… You can see nothing, and no one is there, no one is left to keep
you safe. You have no hand to hold, no voice to reassure you. And yet, despite the eerie isolation, you can somehow… sense things. Despite the fact that the blackness has numbed your senses, and has invaded your heart, you still know things.
[The giant falls silent, and as he does so, the song playing in the background comes to the forefront again.]
##### I was burned out from exhaustion, buried in the hail, Poisoned in the bushes an' blown out on the trail, Hunted like a crocodile, ravaged in the corn. "Come in," she said, "I'll give you shelter from the storm."##### The first thing you know is this: that all around you, this place is rotten and corrupt. The filth on the walls leaves you with a palpable sensation of sliminess. You can almost feel the slow ooze of corruption and decay, as all around you, the walls to this dark room decay. You have the sense that once, this place was radically different, that where there is now nothing but rot, there was once a vibrancy and a joy of life.
Far from being a place of festering despair and unimaginable filth, this place was a virtual paradise. But, the time of that paradise has long since come and gone. No longer will you find any trace of that joy.
Not only are you trapped in this room, not only are you completely unable to find the doorway out of this place, you have been here an eternity.
You have dwelled within this cavern of darkness so long that memories of the light, thoughts of the paradise you once inhabited are nothing but
distant, and very fleeting memories. Daily, a memory of the light dies from your mind, and it shall never return. You find yourself counting the remaining memories, and begin a dire countdown, because you know one day, the last memory will succumb to oblivion, and you will drown in the quagmire.
[Martinez pauses, and lowers his head, drawing forth another breath. Once again, during this pause, Bob Dylan's voice comes through.] ######Suddenly I turned around and she was standin' there With silver bracelets on her wrists and flowers in her hair. She walked up to me so gracefully and took my crown of thorns. "Come in," she said, "I'll give you shelter from the storm."######### Now, into your world of darkness, you are startled to see a light. The light is red in color, and thought it is small, it burns with a radiance you would have thought impossible. Somehow, this tiny light vibrates and shines with such force, that, while it does not destroy the darkness, it makes it tolerable.
You find yourself attached to the light, it becomes your only companion, your only hope of salvation. Anything and everything that you are becomes wrapped up in this light. It brings you warmth, and mental shelter, and you know that this light is the most important thing in the world to you.
You are devoted to this light, and though you still dwell in the darkness, you no longer mind, because you have your red light.
But, your tiny slice of heaven doesn't last. Soon, the light becomes… erratic. You sense a loss, but, you know that you will be lost without the light, so you cling, you hold on desperately, praying against all hope that you can keep this one last bit of happiness with you, as you find yourself choking in the stale, dead air of this fetid room.
It is no use though, because the tiny red light, it leaves. As it turns out, the red light was not the shining beacon it had masqueraded as. It was nothing more than a temporary illusion, a tiny flash, with no substance behind it. The light has flown, gone off to find more of its kind, it will no longer dwell in the darkness with you. . .
[Martinez falls silent yet again, as he reaches up, fingers lacing through his sweat-soaked hair, and then diving beneath his sunglasses to wipe away the sweat, or is it tears, that sting at his brown eyes, threatening to blind him. As before, the music can be heard again.]
######In a little hilltop village, they gambled for my clothes I bargained for salvation an' they gave me a lethal dose. I offered up my innocence and got repaid with scorn.####### You are alone again. Once more, you are a victim of the taint. All the putrid matter that you thought the light had driven away, had only been covered by a thin, gilded surface. But the gilding was nowhere near strong enough to block the filthy, slowly but surely, you realize that the thin veneer of salvation has peeled away, once more revealing the festering corruption of the room that has come to be the only world you know. But the deceptive red light has left another legacy, one remnant of its presence. For in the center of the room, there is a yawning, bottomless chasm. You dwell now upon the precipice of this chasm, forever at its cusp, your life not more than a single step away from being plunged headlong into the abyss.
And from that yawning abyss, you hear a constant howling. They say that madmen hear voices, and that is how you know this is not mere madness, for you hear no voices, just an incessant howling. But, you find that your lack of madness is hardly comforting, the reality of this howl is far worse than any sort of madness you could endure. The sound of this eternal howl is difficult to describe, because it is something unlike any other experience you have ever encountered. It is mournful, shot through with the pain of a mortal wound, perhaps the sound comes from a man dwelling in the twilight area between death and life, futilely clinging to life, even as the last breath escapes his lips, and his blood runs freely until his body becomes little more than a desiccated corpse. And yet, along with its mournful quality, it is angry as well. It burns with a passionate, white hot rag, it shoots through your body, entering at the base of your spine, and resonating deep within your mind.
You are yourself filled with great agony, it feels as if a thousand needles are being laced through your body simultaneously. You might find solace in the eventuality of numbness, you reason that eventually, the pain will lessen, because you will have gone beyond your capacity to feel… but such an event never occurs. The wounds are healed and then reopened in an eyeblink. You lose none of your capacity to feel, and each infliction of the pain is as agonizing as the initial stabbing.
There is but on respite from this torment. Because now, you have learned that you can lure people into this dark room. While you yourself are trapped here, you are somehow able to bring people in with you. You fight them, because it is the key to your relief, however momentary it might be.
Because you see, when you are causing them pain, when their pain-filled whimpers, or horrified screams fill your ears, it drowns out the howl of the abyss, their pain is your liberation. And equally so, should they harm you, the pain deadens the howl's effects. Though you ache, you find that you welcome such damage to your body, because now, you will do anything to alleviate the howl. So, you seek out others, you try to harm them, and in turn, you welcome the harm they bring to your body. Pain becomes your ally in a way, because no human can hurt you enough to even come close to approximating the howl's agony. Try as they might, no one can hurt you, they can only offer you release.
[Martinez draws another deep breath, and his head angles downward, chin tucked against the top of his collar bone. As he does so, his sunglasses slide down his nose, which has become slick with perspiration. Viewers are afforded a rare glimpse into this man's tortured existence, via the sight of his hollow brown eyes. Demented, yes, but he is also a man who suffers, a man whose pain is far worse than anything he has ever endured in the wrestling ring.]
Bryan Sullivan, Devil's Advocate, for weeks you have claimed you would take my soul. You have tried to lay claim to it. Sullivan, understand that the ordeal I have just described for you is the very landscape of my soul.
The "red light" was, of course, Veronica, my former wife. The woman who managed me in the first part of my wrestling career, only to betray me by bringing another man into her bed. She was my last hope for peace, the last chance I had to find some joy in the face of such agonizing torture...
she is gone now, leaving only the Abyss, and its incessant howl. Understand that there is nothing you can do that will bring fear to me… you see, my personal experiences transcended fear long ago. Nothing you can ever dream of doing will come close to the terror I endure each and every second of every day in my life. I welcome the pain you think you can bring to me, because it will cease the howls, I rather enjoy the thought of a few moment's peace.
You say you want my soul Sullivan? I doubt that, because possessing it would leave you curled up in the fetal position, thumb in your mouth, as you whimpered and blubbered, calling to your mother for comfort.
But, take it if you can Sullivan, I might enjoy a bit of relief… [Martinez slumps down, into the front seat of his car, and slowly turns the ignition, and as he passes into the sunset, we here the last of Dylan's song]
###### Well, I'm livin' in a foreign country but I'm bound to cross the line Beauty walks a razor's edge, someday I'll make it mine. If I could only turn back the clock to when God and her were born. "Come in," she said, "I'll give you shelter from the storm."##### [Fade to black]
