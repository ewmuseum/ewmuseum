"Idolizer" Trace Michaels Roleplay

21 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Kent posted one of his and I think Curt posted one, so... WHAT ABOUT
ME?! :) This one's kind of long and features two other peoples wrestlers. Let me just explain ITM's gimmick first... He's the world's biggest DTP fan and that's about it. And Janis, if you think this isn't e-wrestling, don't bother making corrections like you're a fucking
English teacher. :) Now... Proceed.
------------------------------------------------------------------------- ("Idolizer" Trace Michaels sits in a chair in a small studio type place. He's wearing a loose black half shirt with a cross on the back, that has the word "DREAMLOVER" written on the cross. He has on a pair of faded blue jeans with holes in them. On his feet are Military style combat boots. He is wearing rounded rimmed glasses, one side has a flip-up lens with a cross on it. The earring hanging form his ear spells out
"DREAMLOVER". His blond hair is in a pony tail and he has a red bandanna on his head, Rambo style. He front of him is a table. On the table sits a phone, a magazine, and a coffee cup. The coffee cup is red and says
"DTP" on it in black letters. But oddly enough, there isn't any coffee in the cup, it's empty. He looks up at the cameras and begins speaking.)
"IDOLIZER" TRACE MICHAELS: Hey EWA fans and welcome to the first ever
ITM Show! This is a show to talk about things... Things like
"Dreamlover" Trey Porter... "Dreamlover" Trey Porter's titles... "Dreamlover" Trey Porter's accomplishments... "Dreamlover" Trey Porter's history... And other "Dreamlover" Trey Porter related things. We take calls, have guests, and other fun things like that.
Okay... We have our first caller. This is John from Maryland. John? Are you there? (John speaks over the speaker phone as "John - Maryland" flashes on the bottom of the screen.)
JOHN: Yeah, I'm here. MICHAELS: Okay John, what's your question? JOHN: I was wondering what you think of Sir Olympia and your match with him at Halloween Hell.
MICHAELS: Well... That's not a very "Dreamlover" Trey Porter oriented question, but I'll answer it anyway. I'm not very pleased with Sir
Olympia's comments on certain issues such as race and other things that he's said about Cherry Cheesecake and Holly Daye. Sir Olympia's not a very nice guy, I didn't say anything bad about him, then he starts bad mouthing me and people I care about. I'll teach him how to behave properly at Halloween Hell. Thanks for calling, John.
JOHN: Yeah, thanks for answering my question. (John hangs up.) MICHAELS: Lets go to our next caller. This is Stacy from Minnesota. Stacy? (Stacy starts talking over the speaker phone.) STACY: Yeah... I was wondering how serious you and Holly Daye are and if you'd ever be interested in someone else.
MICHAELS: Well... Holly and I are in love and at this time, I wouldn't dream of being with anyone else. Maybe Cherry... But that was in the past. I didn't really think Cherry returned my affection anyway... I knew she liked me, but I didn't think she liked me THAT much, like I know Holly does. but, Holly's the one for me. Why do you ask?
STACY: Just because I think you're soooooooooooo cute. MICHAELS: Oh yeah? Thanks, Stacy. (smiles) You really think that? STACY: Uh huh. MICHAELS: What about "Dreamlover" Trey Porter? He look pretty good too, doesn't he? STACY: Yeah, but I like you better. MICHAELS: Wow... Better than DTP?! I'm really flattered. Thanks for calling Stacy. STACY: No problem. (Stacy hangs up.) MICHAELS: Okay, our next caller is Mick from Minnesota. A lot of Minnesotan callers this morning... Mick? Are you there? MICK: Yeah, I'm here. MICHAELS: What's your question Mick? MICK: How did it feel when I stuffed your scrawny ass in that locker? (Mick hangs up.) MICHAELS: Uhhhhhhhhhhhhhhh... I think that was Mick Morton... But to answer your question Mick... It kind of hurt... And I was sad that you did that... That was just downright mean. I thought you were a lot nicer than that, but I guess not. Anyway... Lets get to the guests on our first and possibly ONLY show.
The first guest is a newcomer to the EWA, but is a great athlete. He has competed in the WEW and won the WEW International and World Titles. He claims to be the World Champion... And claims that he can beat
"Dreamlover" Trey Porter... So without further ado... From V-Town,
Washington... "Tha Heartbreak Killa..." STIXX!
<"Mo Murda" by Bone Thugz N Harmony plays as Stixx enters the studio with a big smile on his face... he's dressed in his normal, gangsta style attire, complete with Blazers Starter jacket and Stixx hat... he has the WEW World title belt slung over his right shoulder... he approaches ITM and extends his hand as if to shake it... but makes a thumbs up sign and points it to himself... he then stands silently, looking at ITM for a moment... and begins to laugh at seemingly nothing... no one the set knows quite what to make of Stixx as he continues to giggle as he sits down...>
MICHAELS: Hey Stixx, welcome to the show. <Stixx leaps to his feet angrily, and points at ITM...> STIXX: DO I HAVE TO KICK YOUR ASS TOO?! I'M TIRED OF THESE MESSED UP
QUESTIONS! I *NEVER* LOST TO THE ROYAL CHAMPION!!! I *NEVER* LOST TO
ICEMAN!!!!! DO YOU HEAR ME, IDOLIZER?!?! YOU SHOULD WORSHIP ME! *I* AM
THA ONLY WORLD CHAMPION HERE!!!!! <Stixx stops suddenly... and gets a bright smile on his face...> So, how're you doing today?
MICHAELS: Ummmmmmmmmmmmmmm... I'm okay... Lets get to the questions... You just signed with the EWA after you found yourself without a wrestling league, since the WEW closed. I've wrestled against the EWA wrestlers and they're tough... What makes you think you can beat them so easily?
STIXX: I defeated the greatest in the world in the WEW. I continue to do so. I beat big names like Deranged... NO ONE in THIS... league, if you even want to CALL it that... could compare to an athlete the calibur of Deranged, MUCH LESS tha UNDISPUTED World Champion... tha King of
V-Town, Stixx. And... since... what? The WEW... closed? <Stixx laughs>
You must be thinking of something else, friend... WEW is tha greatest promotion in tha world. NO OTHER league has tha talent that it does. WEW would be tha LAST in tha world to close... and IF it did... wouldn't
*I*, tha WEW World title holder, be tha FIRST to know?! I think so. But tha fact of tha matter is, I've watched from afar... I know who's here. And I've YET to see someone who can measure up to tha skillz of tha
Heartbreak Killa... are any of THESE guys HERE undefeated like *I* am?!
I don't think so. Guys like Hollywood, Dravin, Gaines, Super Scott... they'd be jobbers in WEW. They'd drop matches to Mo Mo the Chimpy Monkey
Boy in under two minutes... I came here to polish a couple moves, before my next big PPV WEW title defense... I figure squashing a guy like Alex
Adams would be a good warmup for a tough title defense against a guy like... Mark Donovan. <Stixx smiles sweetly>
MICHAELS: Ummmmmmmmmmmmmmmm... Yeah... Moving right along... You said that Sir Olympia is the best wrestler the EWA has to offer... I have a match coming up with him at Halloween Hell for a title that he made himself. He may be good... But you have to be kidding when you say he's the BEST in the EWA. There are A LOT of great wrestlers around here.
Alex Adams... Drac Dravin... Gunnar Gaines... And a lot more. Did you just say that Sir Olympia is better than them to get them mad, or do you really belive that?
STIXX: I'm glad that you agree that Sir Olympia is the best EWA has to offer. He's impressed me more than everyone else here, including that
PTD fool... but... I'll get to him... Sir Olympia, you're a wrestler of legend. Where I've been, wrestlers are afraid to say your name outloud... <Stixx gets an evil grin on his face> You sir, ARE tha man.
You make the rest of these so called wrestlers in EWA look like little girls. Which in fact, they are, from what I can tell... this place is supposed to be extreme? I've seen more extreme matches between John
Tenta and Bubba Rogers... <Stixx laughs> You don't even have an EWA
World champion. Is it any coincedence he dropped the belt and ran to PCW just as I got here?! <Stixx gives an evil smile> Of course it's not.
Dark Destroyer came to WEW, and feared me there... then he heard I was coming here with the REAL World's title. The title he knew he could
NEVER win from me, and he bolted... like a punk BITCH! Why don't you come tha fucc BACK, Destroyer?! You ain't no destroyer. You ain't dark.
You ain't SH1T!!! You KNEW I'd come right in here and scoop up that worthless piece of tin this two bit fed calls a World's title... <Stixx starts laughing hysterically>... a World title... HAHAHAHAAAHAHA!!! And you RAN... but hey... I guess I don't blame you... if I were you... I'd fear Stixx, too... just like TPD... <Stixx throws his head back and laughs manically...>
MICHAELS: You've been saying some stuff about "Dreamlover" Trey Porter... But why? You said before that the REAL reason he retired was because he knew you were coming. Trey Porter retired due to an injury he sustained in his EWA World Title match against The Dark Destroyer, everyone knows that. He's the greatest wrestler ever in the history of anything. What makes you think that YOU scared HIM to retire? I find that hard to believe.
<Stixx has been laughing throughout the entire statement... then stops...> STIXX: Normally, I'd tell you to get tha fucc off his nutsack... but since Trey Porter is a scared little ho, I don't think he's got a sack, or nutz. PORTER RETIRED OUT OF COWARDICE!!! HE FEARS STIXX, JUST LIKE
EVERYONE ELSE! Michaels... YOU should Idolize ME! AAAHHHAHAHAHAHAA!!!!!
MICHAELS: I don't know if you should be on this show talking about DTP like that... This show is PRO-DTP, you're obviously ANTI-DTP. But thanks for coming anyway... Wish you the best in your EWA career... Just don't bad mouth DTP anymore... See ya...
STIXX: BEFORE I LEAVE, I SHALL PROCURE THA STIXX-PLEX ON *YOU*, ITM!! <Stixx kicks over all the furniture and laughs as he leaves the set...>
MICHAELS: Ummmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm... Bye Stixx... I have one more guest...
But I better clean this place up before I bring him out. (Michaels sets all the funiture back up, the chair and the table. Then he sits back down in his chair. He looks down at the floor.)
MICHAELS: NO!!!!!!!! (The camera shows the DTP mug on the floor, shattered. It's in three pieces, broken by Stixx.)
MICHAELS: My DTP mug! Ahhhhhhhhhhhhhh! The show... Must... Go on. I'll bring my next guest out here now. We're not the best of friends, but
I'll try my best to forgive and forget so the show doesn't get out of hand. Here he is... GARY (gulp) GRAYSON!!!
(Gary Grayson comes out onto the set. He's wearing blue jeans with black leather chaps, black leather riding boots. His black hair slicked back, he's wearing black shades, and a black long sleeve t-shirt that says..."S & S" on the front, and "It's Coming" along the sleeves.
Michaels extends his hand to shake Grayson's hand as he sees him.) MICHAELS: Welcome to the show, Mr. Grayson. (Grayson slaps Michaels across the face) GARY GRAYSON: "Boy, don't you EVER <BLEEPIN> TOUCH ME AGAIN!!!!" (Michaels puts his hand where he was just hit on the face by Grayson.) MICHAELS: Ouch! What was that for?! GRAYSON: "Who's askin' the questions here, you or me?" MICHAELS: Well... It's my show... GRAYSON: "Yeah, but I'm <BLEEPIN> on it!!!" MICHAELS: So... What are you tryin' to say? GRAYSON: "Are you <BLEEPIN> deaf?" MICHAELS: Huh? GRAYSON: "Listen...Are you goin' to ask me a <BLEEPIN> question or what?" MICHAELS: Okay... But, can you please watch your language? There are kids watching at home...
GRAYSON: "<BLEEP> you..." MICHAELS: I take that's a "no," huh? Well... Lets get to the questions. You and Caleb Temple have gotten into quite a feud and you have a match at Halloween Hell. What do you think of him and your match coming up?
GRAYSON: "How do you feel about gettin' your <BLEEPIN> ASS KICKED by
"The American Nightmare"?"
MICHAELS: Ummmmmmmmmmmmmm... I thought I was supposed to ask the questions... GRAYSON: "You are son, I just wanted to hear the answer Caleb <BLEEPIN>
Temple is goin' to give after Halloween Hell..."
MICHAELS: Oh... Well... To answer your question... My back hurt a little bit and I had a few bruises. That good?
GRAYSON: "Don't lie now, son...tell 'em about how you wanted to <BLEEPIN> LEAVE the EWA...FOR GOOD...Tell 'em..." (grabs Michaels by the neck) MICHAELS (gulp) Okay... Well... I didn't think I was good enough to be in the EWA anymore, even though I only lost because Hollywood and Caleb
Temple interfered. But some people like Cherry Cheesecake and Holly Daye and a few others convinced me to stay.
GRAYSON: "Yeah...do you consider them your friends?" MICHAELS: Holly's my girlfriend. Cherry Cheesecake and some of the others are my friends. But I guess Super Scott doesn't like me anymore... I think DTP might be my friend though, I met him before on
The DTP Show.
GRAYSON: "Yeah, well this ain't Central Perk...this is the E <BLEEPIN> WA..." MICHAELS: Oh, you watch "Friends?" GRAYSON: "I only watch <BLEEPIN> REALITY...and guess what...I'm <BLEEPIN> ON RIGHT NOW!!!" MICHAELS: (gulp) What do you think of "Dreamlover" Trey Porter? GRAYSON: "Same thing I feel about you...NOTHING!!!" MICHAELS: Nothing? He's the greatest EWA superstar of all time. GRAYSON: "I was under the impression you were goin' to ask me some
"good" questions. So far, I haven't heard a <BLEEPIN> one of 'em..."
MICHAELS: Sorry... What do you want me to ask you? GRAYSON: "Boy...you're truly pitiful...now you're just givin' the show to me... Let me ask you somethin', then...why did you BACK OUT of the
4-Way Match?" MICHAELS: Well... I need to get ready for Sir Olympia. And I want to do stuff with Holly, if I was in that match, I'd have to train more and more and I wouldn't be able to spend any time with Holly and have any fun.
GRAYSON: "Is your nose growin', or is it just me? Face it, chump...You're a <BLEEPIN> <BLEEP>...aint' that right?"
MICHAELS: No... I just... (sniffle) Maybe you should leave. GRAYSON: "Yeah...I think I should...what a <BLEEPIN> waste of my time...Who do I see to get my <BLEEPIN> check?"
MICHAELS: Ask... (sniffle) The producer... GRAYSON: "Do you just have to be a <BLEEPIN> Loser to get a show like this, or just a <BLEEPIN> Cry Baby?"
MICHAELS: I'm not a... (sniffle) Crybaby... Or a loser... I think we're out of time. GRAYSON: "You're right...your time is <BLEEPIN> UP!!! And you know what that means..." MICHAELS: What does that mean? (BOOM... Grayson nails Michaels with a clothesline. He follows it up with a battery of forearms to the back of Michaels. Grayson finishes it up with a DDT.)
GRAYSON: "It means...Posin' Time...CHUMP!!!" (Grayson begins with a HUGE Double Bicep Flex, then continues a posedown routine.) GRAYSON: "Don't you ever....EVER...<BLEEPIN> ask an ASS KICKER into your "home"...it can only result in one <BLEEPIN> thing...YOU GETTIN'
YOUR ASS KICKED. Michaels...You're lookin' at YOUR WORST <BLEEPIN>
NIGHTMARE...<whispers>...Sweet Dreams...<Grayson slams the mic down and walks off the set, leaving Michaels dazed and confused...>
(Michaels staggers back up to his feet, in pain. He looks back up to the camera and speaks again.)
MICHAELS: That's all the time we have for... The ITM Show... Goodbye everyone... I'd like to thank all our guests, except for Gary Grayson and Stixx.
(Fade to Black)
