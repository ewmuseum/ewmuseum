[Critique] Victor Crow

4 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is my first actual flash with Victor Crow for a match. I've made one other which was before TNL started. TNL is the first fed I've entered with Crow. So, any response is welcome.
[Fade In: A golden color is seen closely. As the camera zooms out, to a wheat field. Standing in the center of this wheat field, a man, in pure black. His reaction to this camera: calm. Just then from the outskirts, is the noise of waves breaking. The camera man/woman then begins their journey to figure out this man. Minutes pass as the camera finally get close enough, to this man, in the black. For he stands in this wheat field, nearly motionless. For he lets his actions speak for himself. The camera man/woman gets a little closer, for now you understand this man to be Victor Cow, a new face to the wrestling scene. Then out of the corner of the camera lens, you see a man walk by.
Dressed is regular attire, this man known to the wrestling industry as Daniel
Emery. He then begins to speak, as Crow, who is further back, lights up a cigarette.]
D. Emery: Violent acts! Oh yes, Crow is just waiting for the right moment to snap on one, or hell maybe two. One amongst many will be James Morgan. A man, twisted in his own mind, very sketchy. His action: hatred, towards on man. That man is no other than Victor Crow.
[Victor Crow takes a few steps forward, for now he is standing a little bit behind Emery. Crow hits the cigarette, twice more and flips it to the ground.
He then drops his eyes, over to the left, where once again another wave break is heard. The camera then pans out to the far left, to look for any likes of water, but none is found. The camera moves back, as now the camera is focussed on both of the men. Emery continues.]
D. Emery: What has been done is done, that is the past. However, you always remember, everything that happens to you. For it was said pain hurts for only a little while, but last a memory. Yes, Morgan we all know this it true. [Pause]
In which two times have you inflicted pain towards one man and once to me! Don't you know what is in store for you, when we get our hands on you? V. Crow: [Interrupts] Morgan, each night that goes by, is a dream of another brutal shipment. The shipment, nothing mere than one man. His actions, much louder than his words. His motive? Only one knows, that one, being James
Morgan. Morgan, your a wealthy man. I'll give you that, but there's something missing. Your state of being? Your expressing? Your tone? Who knows, there's only one way to find out.
D. Emery: That's right! Now Terry Woods, on the other hand, has done nothing. Should he be punished come Sunday night, by this wrath, which Crow holds on too? Oh yeah! Woods is no different from the next man. Despite the fact or a different look, figure, name and all those other belongings. He is just another man. Like I, but never was I stupid enough to step in front of Victor Crow and plan to punish him, to a pulp.
[Victor Crow steps to the camera, as now he is in front of Emery. Crow, looks behind him once and then drops his head, once coming back to the camera. The camera, zooms in on Crow's head, where now you his shabby hair just hang down.
Crow picks his head back up, as his hair, now covering his eyes. Crow begins to speak.] V. Crow: You are my sun shine, my only sun shine, you make me happy, when I'm awake. [Pause] A tone to everyone's ears. However, how many men can actually take this to heart? Can you Terry Woods? Could Terry Woods, feel the pain, that
I still feel to this day? Yes, he could. However, his inter-self, wouldn't allow it. As most men wouldn't feel the need, to say they are desperate for ones company. [Pause] Woods, Sunday night, won't be for the weak hearted. It shall be pure aggression running off adrenaline. In which, Woods, you'll be left empty handed.
D. Emery: [Interrupts] Yeah! Sunday night, it pure trouble. V. Crow: But you don't know what that means. Never before, have you witnessed, what I, one man have done. Under going many bouts in under ground fighting establishment and death match promotions. Yes, Woods you can win, but to hurt a man, who has been hurt one too many times, at this stage in my life, is very hard. Woods, in this match, sure you could have the win, but you don't have a little bit of respect until you inflict pain on ones, memory, in which only one man has done.
[Crow, turns as he whistles a tune. The camera focusses on Crow, as he leaves the scenery, leaving Daniel Emery there alone.]
D. Emery: Woods you will crack come Sunday night. Even though you'll never see it coming, it will hit you and it will hit you hard. As you wont even know that it has begun.
[The camera fades to black.]
