DoD Sample RP

13 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi everyone, this is my most recent roleplay for the DOD. They are a mega-heel stable in the BBCW. Please give me any tips on how to make my rp's better.
========== BBCW =========== DoD About: Death Killas, Annihilators, Santiago, Haven Sanctuary =========================== (Scene opens up with the DOD; Death Eagle, Death Bear, Megadeath, and Mike
Dippi
sitting in a dungeon in their usual ring attire. Chained to the wall is a very large man. He is about 6 invhes taller than Megadeath.)
Death Eagle: He he he. (Speaking to the big man.) Your time is almost up, you have almost paid for your sins. Do you have anything to say? (He stares into the large mans eyes.)
???: Yes, I am gl... (Death Eagle smacks him across the face.) Death Eagle: SHUT UP!! (The large mans face burns with anger.) Death Bear: You see, this man is known as Goliath. He chose to go against us. He got destroyed, just like anyone else who tries to go against us. He tried to go against the Dungeon, he failed. Megadeath: He was a competitor in a now defunct organization. He fought Death
Eagle in a DOD match and won. The stipulations of a DOD match are this. The loser of the match gets locked in a dungeon for one year. Goliath's time is almost up. Goliath has spent 358 days in this dungeon, this is the 359th. That means, he has one week left, and then he is free.
"Deadly" Mike Dippi: That's right, soon he will be unleashed. All we have fed him for the past 350 something days, is bread, water, and any small animals he finds scurrying across the floor. I don't want to get in his way.
(The DOD start walking out of the Dungeon, and down a staircase into a large room.) Mike Dippi: But, that's not for another week. Right now, the problem at hand. Saturday Drive By. Why weren't we there? We should've been out there punishing you all. ARE YOU ALL AFRAID? Tanaka is afraid. He turned down my match. Who else is afraid. (starts laughing) I know you all are. Death Eagle: What's up with the DOD vs. Death Killas vs. The Annihalators and
Santiago? We've got four guys, Death killas are two, and the annihilators and santiago are three? Pres, the vacation's over, start doing your job and get this straitened out. I want to kick *** fairly.
Death Bear: Death Killas, just stay out of our way. We will destroy you, but I am warning you, don't come back for more after this match, unless you want to end up like
Goliath in the
dungeon.
Megadeath: Haven Sanctuary. We told you to either accept or decline our offer. You chose to decline. That's your problem not mine. Da Posse knows about that, either join us, or get punished. Haven you little punk. I challenge you to a match on next Wednesday, the 21st, seeing as though you are booked to get your ***- whuped every day before that.
Watch out, we wont stop until we destroy you. Death Eagle: Shadows of Death? Who are these DOD wannabees? Shadows of Death, prove to us that you can play with the Big Ballas, me and Death Eagle challenge you to a match for this Wednesday. Are you Big Ballas?
(Fade's to Goliath in the Dungeon.)
