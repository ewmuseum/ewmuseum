[Critique] Brandon Rockwell

19 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
"Tournaments . something that I had never really taken interest in, but this . this one seemed different. I will have a chance to prove to a lot of people that I am able to stick it out. I'm not just a 'flash in the pan'.
I can compete on the higher levels and still come out on top. I am determined and focused . what more could a wrestler do to prepare for a tournament like this. It will be a time for me to shine brighter than I've ever done before. I can do this . I might not win, but I'm going to give it one hell of a try. No one would be able to say that I didn't give my all .
and if they did, then they were lying through their teeth. When it comes to my career . hell, my life. I don't play around. I'm here for one reason .
the same as everyone else and that is to walk away from this tournament as the winner. I know that it is going to be rough, hell . I expect it. The harder the journey to the top means that the prize will be that much sweeter. Wrestlers from federations all over the World are going to be competing in this tournament. It is a chance to earn respect from people that I would otherwise never met and will probably never meet again. Well, this is it . my first match in this tournament and I'm to face off against a man known as 'Mr. Hollywood' Jake Menace. I know nothing about this man, but one thing is for sure . when it is all said and done, he will respect me. I'm about to take my first big step to earning respect and yes, I am a little nervous."
***The scene opens up inside of Brandon Rockwell's home in Providence, Rhode Island. His house is decorated rather plainly. The floored is stained hardwood, which plays host to a large grayish-purple throw rug that bares a white tiger in its center. The walls are painted an off-white shade. A large black leather couch is positioned in the corner of the living room. A
Chinese Fan Palm sits in a brass planter, which rests inside of a black wrought iron plant stand right next to the couch. A few feet in front of the black leather couch is a glass top coffee table. A cork coaster is sitting atop it, with a glass of what looked to be sweet iced tea atop it. On the wall adjacent from the couch is a large oak entertainment center. A 27"
Magnavox television set rests inside it. Along with a 4-head Magnavox VCR and a X-BOX game console, the controller sitting next to it. In the next room over, the kitchen, footsteps could be heard. The sound of a refrigerator opening and things being moved accordingly. Several minutes later, the noises cease and a figure comes into view. It is Brandon
Rockwell; he is dressed in a pair of gray Lugz, slightly faded Levis and a black t-shirt that bears a small silver triangle over the left breast. His short dark brown hair is slicked back to his scalp, which reveals scars from his previous wrestling bouts. His chin beard is neatly trimmed as usual.
While his grayish-blue eyes seem to have a sparkle in them as he walks into the camera's view. He walks over to entertainment center and turns on the television set, then he presses 'power' on the X-BOX and unwrapped the controller cord from the controller and walks over to the couch and sits down. He takes a sip of his drink as he awaits the X-BOX game to load.
Finally it does, as the words HALO fade into the screen. He glances into the direction of the camera and then back at the screen as he begins to speak.***
[Brandon Rockwell]: How do I start this off? Do I come out and being to badger my opponent whom I don't even know? Or I do just say what comes natural? (*He angled his head upward, making it appear that he was thinking about it*) I suppose that I will just say whatever pops into my head.
This tournament will be my first, but hopefully not my last. I was a little weary about tournaments, I figured that they were all rigged, but I've
overcome that superstition. Different wrestlers from different wrestling promotions have gathered here to see who is the 'best of the best'. I am familiar with three other participants that have entered this tournament, but the others are strangers. Strangers that I anticipate getting in the ring with. I can feel the butterflies floating throughout my stomach and the adrenaline jolting up my spine as I think about my first match.
***Brandon presses the buttons of the X-BOX controller as he battled the evil Covenant aliens in the last level. After several seconds, the clacking of the buttons quieted down as Brandon continued.***
[Brandon Rockwell]: 'Mr. Hollywood' Jake Menace . We don't know one another, but soon . very soon . we will be acquainted. I look forward to our first round match in this tournament. One of us will move on to round number two, while the other goes back to his federation a beaten man. I look to gain respect in this tournament. People doubt my abilities, yet they know that I possess the talent. No longer will I be the 'underdog' of the wrestling world. I will prove that I am one of the future stars of this great sport .
and I will begin with you, Jake Menace. Perhaps you are a great superstar in your league, but this is 'open ground'. Careers will be made and broken in this tournament. Respect will be earned and lost, I'm sure. I have nothing to lose, Menace. No one respects me as it is, but I hope to change all of that within this tournament. I might not be capable of winning this entire contest, but I can guarantee that I will not be put out in the 1st round.
I look at you as a test . a test that I will overcome to gain a spot closer to the prize that I crave the most . No, it isn't a trophy or crown . it's respect. Respect is everything in this business. You can have all of the money or hold all of the titles, but that doesn't mean half as much as having someone truly respect you. I used to think that holding gold was the greatest accomplishment in this business, but I learned quickly that it isn't.
Now, don't get me wrong. It would be nice to walk away from this tournament with a trinket proving that I won, but I will be just as happy if I can gain at least one person's respect. Ok, I've done too much with the respect part of my speech, but it's the truth. Speaking of truths . there is one thing that I would like for Jake Menace to know as well as all the other participants in this tournament. You might be able to beat me, it's been known to happen . but one thing is for sure. No one will EVER be able to break me! Try if you must, but you efforts will be exhausted, because it will never happen. Take my words to heart or with a grain of salt . it matters not to me. Jake . good luck in our match, for I'm sure that you will prove to be a worthy opponent. May the best man win.
***Brandon Rockwell turned all of his attention towards the video game as he pressed the controller button continuously. The scene finally faded to black as Brandon Rockwell's voice could be heard saying, "Yeah . take that you alien scum!"***
