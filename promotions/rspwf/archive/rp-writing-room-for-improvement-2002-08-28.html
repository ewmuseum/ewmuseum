RP Writing: Room for Improvement

28 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I like to consider myself a pretty good writer. I do feel though, that there is (like the header reads) room for me to improve. I enjoy the work I've put out in the past, (well...most of it anyways) but I want to bring the quality of my work up a notch.
I just don't know how. I'm not saying I'm perfect...hell no. It's just that I'm a poor judge when it comes to my own characters (and, well...to other people's characters too...well, let's just say I make a poor judge overall, mmmkay?).
One of the flaws I've had in the past with my writing was what I like to call the "Caliban Curse"...rambling on and on, writing massive RP's that no one wanted to read. Those people who recall my work in UWF during the days of the Dan Kauffman/Caliban, Annis/Caliban feuds know all too well what I mean. One of the reasons I retired Cali was due to the oversaturation I routinely inflicted on people.
Another flaw I have (as far as I know, anyways) is over-editing myself. I often scrap ideas which could actually work, before even giving them a proper chance to take a decent shape. Or, worse, I pick apart little details in my RP's and wind up removing the "heart" of the piece.
I tell you what. I'll post an RP here for those who are interested to peruse. Let me know what you think. If something jumps out at you, let me know.
FYI: This is an RP I wrote a while back and never used...it's for
Edmond Winston IV, the hardcore rich boy I handle in NEW and UWF. I'm toying with the notion of turning Winston face at some point, and was curious how well this RP would do on the road to turning him.
You remember what I said about self-editing? It took all the reserve I had just to post the RP without touching it up. *hangs head in shame*
*** [Fade into the office of Edmond Winston IV. He's forgone his usual
Armanis, and is dressed in a white tank top and cutoff jeans. He sits on his desk, wrapping his fists with tape. He tightens the tape, ripping it with his teeth and pressing it on. He flexes his fingers, and looks up at the camera.]
EW4: So what? It's casual day. [He shrugs as we fade out. We fade back in, and we're inside a large gym, obviously the one in Ed's building (considering the Winston-Salem logos everywhere...even on the towels, even overlapping the labels on the weights! Ed's at the heavy bag, throwing some sharp jabs into it.]
EW4: They say...I'm a pansy...a creampuff...can't wrestle...can't take abuse...I'm so damn sick of it.
[Edmond drives his taped fists harder and harder into the heavy bag...his eyes focused intensely on it. He's speaking between breaths, through gritted teeth.]
EW4: I've been fighting...for every...material possession...since I was a kid! [He throws another flurry of jabs, venting his obvious frustration. To finish, he balls up both fists and plows the bag with a double-ax-handle. Breathing heavily, he turns from the bag, mopping sweat from his brow.]
EW4: People think just because your daddy's the richest man on the
Eastern seaboard that you've got it made. Yeah, my father's a smart old bastard. He made his first fortune in tobacco, then made his second on Wall Street. But so what? I'm not my father.
[Ed walks from the heavy bag to a crunch-board. He adjusts the angle and gets on, hooking his feet in. He stretches backwards, and the camera follows with an upside-down shot of the tobacco scion.]
EW4: I'm just a man who grew up surrounded by money, and wasn't allowed to touch a dime of it unless I earned it myself. Like I said, my father's a smart man. He didn't want me growing up spoiled. You'd think I was, though. I worked hard, earned what I got...but kids thought I was another silver-spoon trust fund baby. I laugh now, because a week under my father's rule and those kids would have been screaming for their mothers faster than you can say "tough love".
[Placing his hands at the back of his head, Ed pulls himself up midway for a crunch. He holds it there, breathing regularly...speaking with his exhalations.]
EW4: I wasn't laughing back then though. Oh God no. You know...they say kids can be cruel? [Ed keeps holding the crunch.] EW4: Pick on a kid too long...they're the ones who get cruel. [He slowly lowers himself to the bench. He pauses...takes a few breaths, then goes back up, holding the crunch again.]
EW4: I learned to stick up for myself pretty early. Bloody noses...bruises and scrapes...not your usual fare for upper-class private school. One teacher called me a "mindless thug" the first time, though... when I broke another kid's collarbone.
[Ed releases the crunch, stretching back out on the bench.] EW4: I was about ten years old when that happened. Rory Jackson...his dad was the groundskeeper, so he didn't have to pay to go to the school. He was two years older than me. Took a real dislike to me when his mother died of lung cancer.
[Edmond shakes his head.] EW4: You can see where this is going, I'm sure. When Rory heard what
Father did for a living, it was as if someone had painted a bullseye on my face. It didn't help that when he started cursing at me, yelling about his mother, I asked him a simple question.  "What brand did your mother smoke?"
[Winston sighs, pursing his lips as he recalls the memory. He folds his hands over his chest, in a strange, almost funereal sort of way.]
EW4: See, that didn't matter to Rory. He was angry, and needed to take it out on someone, even if it was an innocent child like me. He dragged me out behind the snowbanks. He knew the teachers never checked back there to see what was going on. I believe you could say, he beat the daylights out of me. Even gave me one of those "facewash"
things when he was done. You know those...where they rub your face in the snow, and let the ice scrape you up?
I called home that night to let Father know what happened. He asked me one question. "What did you do to the person who did that to you?" I was stunned. No concern for my well being, no "Are you all right, son"...he just wanted to know that I gave as well as I got. And when I told him I hadn't even gotten a punch in, he hung up the phone. Hung up the phone, on his ten year old son!
Needless to say, as I went to bed angrier than I'd ever been in my life. All I could think about was how to pay  Rory back for what he did to me. Not just the pain, but to cause my father to treat me that way...oh God, I had never felt so outraged.
I paid him back all right. I walked into the cafeteria during breakfast and picked up a tray. I went over to the table where he was sitting by himself, and smashed him in the back of the head as hard as
I could. He went face-first into his quiche, and I brought the tray down again. Without realizing it,  I had turned it, so the edge came down into Rory's shoulder like an axe.
Now, this was no wimpy tupperware lunch tray, people. We had these really beautiful silver trays, inlaid with the school crest. They were sturdy, and heavy...so needless to say, it wasn't much of a fight.
Rory was down and screaming in those two hits. He wasn't the only one. I was screaming too...all that pent up rage had just been vented out all over a twelve year old boy. Through my tears I swung blindly at the people who tried to stick up for Rory...people who tried to defend this bastard who had attacked me for no other reason than the fact that my Father worked for a tobacco company.
[Edmond slowly rolls off the crunch board, sitting on the floor. He brings his knees up and wraps his arms around them. The usual jovial cockiness is gone from his face as he stares at the nearby wall. He chews his at his thumbnail, and returns his focus to the camera.]
EW4: Look. I am the same person you see showing off in front of the camera night in and night out. I show off because I'm proud of what I have and how I earned it. I show off because of I know I am damn good at what I do. You people can either accept me for who I really am, or you can blow the f[BLEEP!] away. I don't care.  Those people that don't love to hate me are the ones who hate to love me. Either way, I couldn't give a damn. I make my money by kicking ass and talking sh[BLEEP!]...
[Edmond's trademark grin slowly returns...] EW4: And I do make a LOT of money. *** Thanks again. Phil. *As a matter of fact, I -do- live in the basement. :-/
