[Roleplay Critique] Nick Hart

31 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Check it out.... -------------------------------------------------------------- (The scene: it's 4:21 PM outside the Olympus Gym in suburban Edmonton. In front of the cameraman is a pair of large glass doors, littered with happenings at the gym and bulletins. Icicles litter the upper lining of the doorway. The cameraman walks through the pair of glass doors into the actual gym. The gym itself is a huge, two-story facility with a weight room, an aerobics room, two locker rooms and the entire second floor as a treadmill/health machine room.
Our cameraman pans around the facility, taking in the overall hugeness of the building. Eventually, he walks over to the weight room which takes up nearly half of the first floor.)
(The cameraman pans the weight room, looking for a particular person. The weight room is filled to the brim with weight machines and sets. There are very few people in this room, three or four at the most. The cameraman finishes panning the room when he finds the man he's looking for. This man is wearing a pair of black workout shorts, a red mesh tanktop and a pair of white sneakers, most likely cross-trainers. The man has an instantly recognizable face, the face of "The Item" Nick Hart. He's currently working on an all-purpose weight machine and is bench pressing a great amount of weight from where the camera can see. The cameraman walks over to Hart and is at the appropriate distance when Hart slowly sets the weights down. Sweat is dropping down his face as he slumps against the machine's seat. He breathes heavily and looks at the cameraman through the corner of his eye.)
"TI" NH: I didn't figure you'd be ready for an interview so soon... (Hart hunches over weight machine and turns his head towards the cameraman. His green eyes peering into the camera lens.) "TI" NH: As you can see, I'm here in the Olympus Gym of Edmonton...Alberta,
Canada to work out and prepare for next week's Turbo. As far as I know, I'm up against Jacen "Crimson Fury" Canadi. Jacen, I'll get to you in a minute...
Folks, you saw me debut a good 2 weeks ago on Nightmare...Now, I know what you probably thought when you saw. You probably said something like, "God, this guy sucks!" Am I right? Well, after watching video footage of the match, that's what I said, too. I couldn't believe how poorly I did for both the fans and myself. I knew that I had to do something so that I could prevent that from ever happening again. My wrestling skills are something I couldn't change, only improve upon. Those would come later on. The only thing I thought I could improve upon was my attitude...
(The cameraman looks on as Hart's face changes from tired to serious and stern.) "TI" NH: Folks, I'm gonna tell you the truth here, I was never "The Item"....I sucked as "The Item" and "The Item" sucked when it was me...I'm not really like that, I never was like that...As "The Item", I was a poor wrestler and poor role model for you, the fans...That's all going to change....
NWWA fans, what you're going to be witnessing this Thursday on Turbo will be the death of "The Item" Nick Hart and the rebirth of the man behind "The
Item"...You won't be seeing "The Item" wrestle this Thursday...you'll be seeing
Nick Hart wrestle...I fight for the fans, I fight for what's right and I'll fight to get to where I want to be in the NWWA....Basically, it's time that there was man who was willing to do what he could for the people without caring about what he'd get out of it. Now, I'll admit that I want to go places here, but first things first, it's time there was a hero in the NWWA...I want to be that hero...
(The cameraman watches Nick get up from the seat of his weight machine. Nick is a fairly small man for a wrestler, but has a physique most men wish they had. He begins to speak once again.)
NH: Now, as I understand it, I am facing Jacen Canadi this Thursday...Jacen, I respect you and what you've done in the NWWA. I respect your record and your reputation. I also respect your wrestling ability and your overall demeanor...
However, that respect won't matter a bit in the ring. Jacen, for your sake, I hope you're ready for our match this Thursday. You're going to get worked like you've never been worked before, my friend. You're going to become my first big win in the NWWA and I plan on using you as the first tool for my ascension in the NWWA rankings....Whether it be by pinfall submission, or disqualification, Jacen, my friend, you will lose this Thursday, regardless of how much I respect you. The only thing I hope for you now is that you can accept defeat like an honorable man unlike other wrestlers in the NWWA....
(Nick turns his head over to the left with a grunt and crack from inside his neck. The interview is almost over and Nick seems to want to get out of it as soon as possible. The cameraman backs a step away from the specimen that stands before him.)
NH: Fans, I urge you to tune in to Thursday Turbo this week. If you do, you will see Nick Hart's, not "The Item" Nick Hart, but Nick Hart's first win in the NWWA...I'll see you all then....
(With that, Nick Hart walks past the cameraman back to the weight room's entrance. From there, the assumption is that he'll walk to the locker room to change. The cameraman stays in that same spot until the scene eventually fades out into the next interview.)
-Alex, the e-wrestler formerly known as: "The Wush" Currently handling: "Black Fire" Kuro Kasai (IWP - 0-0-0) Nick Hart (NWWA - 0-1-0) *KSGA Graduate*
