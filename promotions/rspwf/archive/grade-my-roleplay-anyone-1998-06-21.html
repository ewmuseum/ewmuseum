**Well Im new to e-wrestling and i want to get at it so anyone who is bored grade this shit for me** <i>(The sceen opens up in a graveyard, the camera scans the whole area then it finds Maxx who is at a gravestie in Formal Clothes and wearing sun glasses and
Doom who is also at the gravesite wearing formal clothes and sun glasses, Maxx takes out a rose and lays it on the gravesite and stands back, Maxx looks at the gravesite and then begins to speak</i>)
Maxx:10 Years, Damn has it been so long, I miss you J.C, when i was kid and you teaching me, i always wondered how life would be with out ya, things has changed i now wrestle in icw and i am the not only the solar champion but a stable champion too
<i>(Maxx takes out the 2 titles and shows them to the tombstone and starts to laugh but then he stops laughing cuase he realizes that the J.C cant talk back)</i>
Maxx:You know i hoped you dont mind me taking your mantle of the "Innovater", I know i cant come no where near you, you are the original, you will always the innovater, you are now you are in the furture
<i>(Maxx pauses then quickly says)</i> Maxx:Oh yeah i got a match tonight against the fake Crow and dennis Rodman has the guest referee, dont worry there aint no way in hell im losing my title to a loser like "Crow" and if dennis rodman thinks he is going to screw the innovater out of my title he has a another thing coming, cuase if he does my
"Enforcer" will take care of him
<i>(Maxx steps back from the gravestie and turns around and looks at the sky and looks at doom who takes a step forward)</i>
DOOM:You know i really didnt know you but from what Maxx is has been telling me you wear a good man, Maxx really needs ya he is going crasy, i mean between me and you i dont think he ever cared about one person more than you, he thinks about ya all the time, i also got a match tonight im teaming up with Cactus
Jack in the tag-team cage match, i never teamed with jack but i know we will be victorious cuase it is our time, there aint no question in my mind that we will win that match cause there aint no other teams that i think can beat us, wish us luck!
<i>(The camera then turns away from doom and focuses on a man coming towards the gravestie it then zoomz in and it is Shane Hell who is walking towards the gravestie, that catches Maxx's atention and he turns away from looking from the sky and says)</i>
Maxx:What are you doing here huh! Shane:Came to pay my respects, what you thought i didnt remenber <i>(Shane and Maxx start to laugh together and Shane walks over to the gravestie and puts o rose on it and steps back)</i>
Shane:He was a good man, worked all of his life good thing he went in his sleep huh! Maxx and Doom:Yeah! Maxx:Well that's and to SEAN CONWAY, anytime you want too feel Crimson Blood sign the match
<i>(The 3 of them start to walk away and the screen fades on all of there backs and Maxx puts up the 3 fingers, which was the symbol of the tripple Threat</i>)
