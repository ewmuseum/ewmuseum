The RSPWF Top 75 Wrestlers

19 October 1995
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
WRESTLING UNLIMITED, THE PRODUCTION COMPANY THAT BRINGS YOU WAR! WRESTLING FEDERATION NOW PROUDLY ATTEMPTS TO BRING YOU THE FIRST EVER RSPWF TOP 100 WRESTLERS. Okay, I suppose you've all been wanting to see this for a while. So have I. It's been on my mind for two or three months now, and to be fair, the result isn't exactly what I wanted. I didn't get replies from every federation, despite sufficient advertising. And then my computer got messed up so I MAY have lost some details, but I don't think so. I think one of the files I MAY have lost was EWC, but to be honest, I wasn't sure if I received it or not. And I didn't here from feds like SLAM! What I did do was look throught the newsgroup and find all the rankings I could from those who haven't replied. Again, I apologise for any mistakes that have been made. So when looking at this top 75 (I just don't have enough time or data for top 100 or 150), please remember there are bound to be quite a few discrepencies.
