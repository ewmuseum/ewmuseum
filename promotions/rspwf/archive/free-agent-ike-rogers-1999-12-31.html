I've been currently looking for a great, ELITE, E-mail RP Fed. But I haven't found one in a while. I've been RPing almost four years now and a sample RP is available upon request.
If anyone knows a good e-mail federation, that is mostly an AOL fed, please contact me. A website would be a plus.
E-Mail me at [detail withheld] or Instant Message at [detail withheld] Here's some info on my character Ike Rogers: Wrestler Name: Ike Rogers Nickname: "The Vindicator" Height: 6'4" Weight: 262 lbs. Hometown: Swanton, Ohio Favorite Quotes: "Your good kid, real good, but as long as I'm around ... you'll always be second best!" "I'm not cocky ... I'm just better than you." "How about I pony up some pennies, give em' to your cheap @ss and make you my b^tch in the ring."
Finishing Move: The Vindicator TKO Finishing Move Description: Top-Rope Outsiders Edge but only the opponent falls to the mat Ike keeps his balance on the top-rope and executes a senton splash for the pin.
Set-Up Move: Vindication Set-Up Move Description: A Stinger Splash. Career Highlights: PCW World Champion, PCW World Tag Team Champion (2), GCW
Intercontinental Champion, NEW King Of The Ring, NEW Universal Champion
Ike began his illustrious career training at the ever so famous Killer Kowalski
Wrestling School. He trained there for almost a year before getting signed by an independent federation. Ike showed great in-ring ability and signs of stardom on the mic. The GCW also notived this and hired him. The GCW gave Ike his first big break in which he obtained the GCW Intercontinental Title. His first ever title.
Since then Ike has competed in many federations reaching superstar caliber in each. He brings a cocky attitude matched with alot of talent and a great ability to work the mic. All of these aspects and more have helped Ike receive the PCW World Heavyweight Title, hold the PCW Tag Team Titles twice, become the
NEW King of the Ring, and also reach a huge heights by capturing the NEW
Universal Title a feet done by only two other people within the past two years. With some good catch phrases, many admirers, and god given talent, Ike has the possibility to become a huge name within the e-wrestling world.
