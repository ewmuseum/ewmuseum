The Drifter - Sample Rp

27 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey, this is [name withheld]. I've been on the RSPWF for a while and have yet to post an Rp. So, here it is.
=========================================================== [] [Scene] The scene opens up in a big room. There are no windows but it appears to be some sort of basement. The cameraman has just entered and to his left is a big steel cage enclosing a little area where there are hundred and maybe thousands of poker chips and cash registers and all sorts of things. There are two men inside there. One big guy in the back playing around with the chips and one small rat-like man with a ponytail near a cash register handling a long double-barreled sawed-off shotgun. In his other hand are many different kind of bills. He is at a window-like thing where people walk up and cash in their chips or collect them. The cameraman walks right by but the man yells out to him and tells him to come back here. The cameraman explains what he's doing and that he's looking for The Drifter. The little man's eyes widen and then he points over down the narrow hall. The cameraman continues and enters into a bigger room. Inside are a couple of small tables surrounding one big table. Around all the tables are many men...some playing poker and some watching, smoking cigars or trying to pick up the many tricks of the trade. But all the commotion is mainly surrounding the one main table. The cameraman tries to make his way through and briefly spots a tall, skinny Russian man with dark-tinted glasses. He is holding in his hands five playing cards. He has many chips down on the green felt-topped cover. The cameraman pushes his way through and finally sees the mans opponent...The
Drifter. He is wearing a 'Drifter: Simply Untouchable' shirt but that is all the camera can catch. He is slowly looking over his cards and has an expressionless face. He has many chips gathered. They now begin to place their bets. Drifter's opponent puts up about one-fourth of his chips into the middle of the table. The Drifter takes a deep breath and then puts in about half of his chips. The other man has a disgruntled look surfacing on his face. He then puts on about one-third that's left of his chips. The Drifter begins to crack a smile and puts in the rest of his chips. The other man shakes his head and pulls back. He passes and The Drifter takes all the chips in the middle of the table with a smile. The man puts down his cards revealing a Jack, Queen, & King of Diamonds. Along with a 9 of Hearts and a 2 of Spades. The Drifter then cracks a smile and puts down three five's, one of Hearts, one of Clubs, and one of Diamonds. He also has a 4 of Clubs and an 8 of Spades. The other man puts his head down in shame but then brings it back up with a determined ready to keep going. They get their cards dealt and The Drifter puts three down to change and the other man changes one. The Drifter stares at his cards and at his opponent's eyes as his opponent smiles and cracks his neck. They then put in their bets. The Drifter starts by putting in a good one-fourth of his cards.
The other man puts in easily half of his remaining chips. The Drifter doesn't let down and put in two-thirds of his chips. The other man starts getting a little man and puts in half of what's left. The Drifter then follows in by putting it all up and the other man quickly follows. The Drifter's opponent quickly shows his cards revealing a two 6's, one of Diamonds and one of Clubs;
and has three 10's, one of Diamonds, one of Clubs, and one of Hearts. The Drifter then smiles and slowly puts down his cards; first a 10 of Spades, followed by a Jack of the same kind, then a Queen of Spades, and a King of
Spades. The Drifter then hesitates as his opponent spots a trickle of sweat flow down the side of his face which is quickly brushed off. The Drifter then places his final card down, face down. He then slowly turning it over. It's an
Ace of Spades. A Royal Flush. The other man puts his head down into his arms and starts to mumble something. He then stands up quickly, knocking his chair over and walks away quickly. The Drifter then stands up with a smile as the dealer gathers up the chips. The Drifter then takes his chips, walks down the hall, and gives them to the rat-like man. The man says something about 50,000
Dollars. The Drifter gathers his money and puts it all into his jacket pocket. He then walks out the door with the cameraman and finally out into the street. They start to walk through the dark streets of San Francisco as The Drifter begins to speak...
[The Drifter] Now that was a good game. And Kamikaze-Shogun Kid...I hope you saw the expression on my opponent's face after I unloaded that Royal Flush because that's what you'll be looking like after wake up from The Bus From NoWhere. You'll know that you gave it your all and played the best hand in your life...but it still wasn't good enough for The Drifter. But it's
OK. You'll then see me go on to fight the World Champion and you'll realize that no matter what you did, you couldn't have stopped this man. There's been a lot of trash talking lately. Some from men who can back it up, like my old pal
"Tha Incidious" 6päc Valante [Laughs]; some from men who I have yet to see in action, like Death Grappler; and some from people that I know are just a waste of my time, like Chris Casa. There are others but they didn't say much. Now,
Kamikaze-Shogun Kid...notice how your name isn't on there? You haven't said anything since this fed has started. You put up a terrible showing against
Vinnie Owens, sure 6päc interfered but you know that you were getting your face smashed in. So, right now I view you as a very low class wrestler. A jobber even. Now, I want to clear this up for all the idiots out there...and there are a lot. I am Untouchable. Whether you saw the card or not...I am Untouchable forever. Always has been and always will be. And I didn't just join the
Untouchables. That was just showing that we're here. We always have been. The Untouchables are more than a stable, it's a family. We've known each other for a long time. And I'm an Untouchable because being an Untouchable is an honor and I believe in the Untouchable way. This is what I fight for. Many people thought that I joined for money or many of the wrong reasons but none of them know what they're talking about. They couldn't understand.
[The Drifter] Kamikaze-Shogun Kid, you haven't proven to me to be a good challenge. And I believe that I haven't proven anything to anybody as well. You see, the only people that have proven themselves to me so far are
"DaNGeROuS" Dave Taylor and Shawn "The Surge" Christopher. And that's because I fought them and they put up a great match. Maybe Surge had a little help on
Tuesday but he's proven to be a great challenge. And Kami, I doubt that you will prove yourself to me. But when you're laying on the ground, watching me get my hand raised, you'll know that you're looking at a future World Champion and that I've proven myself to you. And when you go home Tuesday Night, you'll have your head up...because you know that there would be no way past me and that you put up the best fight that you could. Kami, I've worked my way up to where I am...I proved myself to all my past opponents and I showed the prez that I deserve to be a World Class wrestler. And he believed me, so he put me here in the World Title Division. But what about you, Kami? Did you show the prez that you were deserving...did he ever see you in action, kicking major ass? No, it was all just luck of the draw. That's all it was. And this Tuesday,
I'll make you wish that you were in some other division. But, like I always say...talk is cheap. All that counts happens inside the ring. All that you can get out of talk are mind games and intimidation. But it's all a bunch of bullshit. I don't need to use mind games or intimidate my opponent to put him away. Everything I need is right in front of you. All the rest is worthless and a waste of time.
[The Drifter] Now let me clear this up. 6päc, you don't own the Stairway to Hell. Nobody owns it. It's a match. Nobody owns a Ladder Match or Falls Count Anywhere. It's a stipulation. I may have created it but I didn't copyright it or anything. Anybody can use it. I just prefer to use it in my hardcore matches. I know you're a little slow so I'm just clearing that up for you. Now, I was in the back, watching a normal CWA Saturday Morning show and I happened to catch Shawn Christopher's interview. And let me say, he just about spoke out of his ass for the whole fucking interview! What an idiot. Shawn, I wasn't doing horribly and you wouldn't be able to put me away at just any time.
Nobody was in control, everything was out of control. And let me clear this up...Shawn IT WAS A TAINTED VICTORY! You didn't win fairly. And Surge, you wanna know your opponent for Foolish Games? You're looking at him. I don't know if there's gonna be a card next Tuesday or if we're gonna have an extra week for Foolish Games, but either way...after Foolish Games, that title will have my name on it. Whether I fight Vinnie Owens or yourself...it really doesn't matter. Both of you have extreme talent and it'll be a great challenge but I'm a man that gets the job done. And don't worry, I'll do it the only way I can...The Untouchable way. Now if you'll excuse me I gotta run this money to charity.
[Scene] The Drifter the takes out his keys and presses a button. A beep is heard meaning that he has disarmed his alarm system. He then walks over to his 1999 white Mustang convertible and opens up the door. He gets in and drives away as the camera fades to an Untouchable promo...
