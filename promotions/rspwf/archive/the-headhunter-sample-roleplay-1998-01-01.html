The Headhunter sample roleplay...

1 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Headhunter HWF Scene There is a man sitting on a bench. He is in a park. He looks about 6'3 and is about two hundred and thirty five pounds. He looks to be about in his late twenties He is sitting looking rather unhappy. He is reading a newspaper. He looks at an article and puts on a sad face. Then he turns the page over and a smile appears on his face. The camera man zooms in and the title reads, ''More wild animals taken by hunters''. As he reads this, The man smiles and quickly skims the article and then notices the camera man zooming ever closer.
The Headhunter: Ah, Look. What have we hear. It looks like a camera man. No wonder you want to film me! I am famous. I am a wrestling star who is known all around the world. Where have you come from young man?
Scene The man now known as The HeadHunter leans forward and looks at the camera. He notices that it has the letters ''HWF'' printed on it. He gives a sign of recognition.
The Headhunter: Ah, Now I know who you are! You must be a reporter from that new wrestling federation that I just joined. The HWF! Am I right?
Scene The camera man nods his head ''yes''. He looks scared stiff. The Headhunter: Don't worry young man. Don't look so scared. I won't harm you. Well I might but only if you give me reason too. Now I suppose that you want a little bit of information on the Headhunter. I know that a lot of HWF fans know a lot about the CNWF so most of you will know who I am. I started going around federations when I was younger under the name of the Underfaker. I conquered and terrorised many federations then and I was well known in them.
But they were not big federations. Well they were, But not big enough! So I went to the top one in town {The CNWF}. There I wrestled as The Underfaker but could never concentrate fully on it. I was not a big hit there. So I re-thought things {After encouragement from Lex Victor} and changed my name to ''The
Headhunter''. With that name change, it presented a new style and personality for me. My real name will never be known BTW, Unless I choose to bring it to your knowledge. So I wrestled under the Headhunter and under that name I scored a big win in the CNWF {Just recently as well, Over ''Powercheck'' Pete
LaCroix}. I am now well on my way to becoming a big hit in the CNWF so you may wonder just WTF I am doing in the HWF as well! So I will tell you. I need more of a challenge. I need to be tested more thorughly. I need competiton. It would be too easy for The Headhunter to dominate the CNWF, If he was just concentrating on it, and it only. So I decided to try to conquer two top federations at the same time. I will do it, I promise you! Add to that, The
CNWF is on a two week hiatus and it is pretty obvious to see why I am here!
Scene He starts laughing uproariously. The camera man gives him a strange look behind his back. Headhunter sees it though and stops laughing at once.
The Headhunter: Listen son! Don't ever try to be smart with me! You hear that! Nobody ever tries to be smart with The Headhunter and comes out alive!
You should fear me boy and if you don't already, then you will soon. I told you, Don't give me any reason to harm me and you will be fine, but at the moment you are treading on very thin ice! I suppose the HWF fans will want to know a little bit more about me so I will fill them in just now. The Headhunter is basically what his name say. Don't worry, I won't chop of your heads or anything like that! {Although this camera man is just asking for it right now by trying to CENSORED me off!}. What I do though, is come after your heads. One by one the superstars {and officials so beware} of the HWF and CNWF will fall.
One by one, The Headhunter will track you down and finish you off once and for all. Scene He points to the article in the paper. The Headhunter: You see these wild animals in this newspaper. They were tracked down and killed. They were finished of. The same thing will happen to all the HWF and CNWF wrestlers. I will take them all one at a time and finish them off. I will destroy them all like the losers they are. And you know what?
I think I will start with The Backbreaker. I know him from the CNWF and I hate him there. Now I have met up with him in the HWF. So Kris Dizon decides to book me in a match with him for next week's Raw card. Backbreaker you are a loser in the CNWF and I would bet my last dollar that you are the same in the HWF! In fact I took it upon myself to do a little checking on you and it appears that you have not yet wrestled! It proves how much you CENSORED! I mean, When you step in the ring with me on Tuesday, Monday whenever the CENSORED we are supposed to be wrestling, Whenever the CENSORED Raw is, I will take your
CENSORED and give it the biggest and baddest beating that it has ever received in its life! In fact, It may be the only one because I heard that when you were growing up, You were a little momma's boy. You were a spoiled little piece of CENSORED that didn't desereve to be on this planet. You got whatever you want! Well let me tell you a little secret! I now get everything that I want!
And I want to score a win over you on Raw. We are both making our debuts then
Backbreaker and only one man can walk out with his record unscathed! That man will be me pal and there is nothing that you can do to stop it. You can beg
Kris to let you out of the match but it just won't happen because I am good and you are not! The HWF doesn't need you Backbreaker, The CNWF doesn't need you
Backbreaker, so just either go crying home to mommy or go to some loser leauge like the UCW or something like that! This is a real federation now bro' and I am a real superstar!
Scene The Headhunter looks positivly mad now! The Headhunter: You can train all you want, You can talk to your little buddies, but at the end of the day, none of it will count when you step into the ring with me! Stay in the UCW son, None of the HWF or CNWF wrestlers are dumb enough to compete there so you may have a chance. Man, You would even have to lose to some guys there. In fact, You couldn't even score a win in the HWF
WOMAN' DIVISION!! How you got into the mens divison in the first place I shall never know, But you belong in the ladies one! You couldn't beat anyone there and I am gonna tell that to everyone who will listen {It in't actually too bad-- Have you seen some of the women??}. But BackBreaker all that needs to be said about this issue right now is that I am going to win- and you are not! I will probably have some more to say about that though before Raw happens and I would set my VCR for that moment! If you are embarresed just now punk, Just wait until the second round!
Scene He seems to have calmed down a little now as he is laughing. The Headhunter: Now that brings me to my next topic. When I joined the HWF,
Everyone was talking about ''The Pack!''. Infact that is all that anyone talks about! Supposedly, ''The Pack'' is the hottest thing going in the HWF. Well that may have been true but the wrong tense was used {Corrected sentence}.
''The Pack'' was the hottest thing going in the HWF. Then The Headhunter came and stole the show. I am the new hottest attraction. But Malibu Spike and the rest of the Pack, I am going to make you an offer. I don't want to steal all your spotlight! {Well I do but I am going to give you a chance to re-claim some of it}. You have forty eight hours to invite The Headhunter to become the
Pack's newest member otherwise I will bring in a bunch of top quality athletes to the HWF who will invade it, conquer it and destroy the Pack! Now I don't have to resort to doing this but if it comes down to it, Then I will do it. I have many contacts all over the world. I have freinds in the UK, I have freinds in the CNWF, I have freinds in the SPWO! You name it, I have freinds there.
And if I must do so then I will bring them in to destroy the HWF. But Malibu you have forty eight hours to stop that from happening. Oh Sorry, My mistake!
You have excactly forty seven hours, Fifty-eight minutes and twenty-five seconds until the offer goes of the table and I bring in the ''new'' Pack who will destroy the ''current'' Pack and trust me, It won't be a pretty sight.
There will be blood all over the arena or wherever it is. Well lets not get into that. That is all I have to say right now... But I will be back...
