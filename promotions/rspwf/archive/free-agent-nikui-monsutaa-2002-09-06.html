I'm returning to ewrestling after an absence of too long. I've got a new character I want to try out who I think I can have fun with and work some awesome angles. I've got more time now to do this soet of thing and I want to get into a good couple of feds.
If you have a space on your roster for some good talent, make some space for some bad talent and email me! :)
I've included a character sheet I filled out for RAW Carolina to give you an idea of my (unoriginal) character.
[name withheld] ** Basic Info ** [1] Your name: [name withheld] [2] Your e-mail: [detail withheld] [3] Wrestler's Name: Nikui Monsutaa [4] Height: 6'8 [5] Weight: 368lbs [6] Date of Birth: Unknown [7] Hometown as announced when wrestling: Kyuusen (The nether regions) [8] Heel or Face: Heel [09] Manager/Valet with description (optional): Mr. Akkanka ** Appearance Info ** [1] Describe your wrestler's physical appearance (i.e., race, hair color, scars, facial hair, physical built, etc.): Monsutaa is an absolute monster of a man. He's a caucasian, 6'8 in height, 368lbs in weight, and most of that is muscle. Muscle all
over. Imagine Vader, but taller, more muscular and a little more lean. His face is scarred from burns, he has no hair - not even eye-brows, and his face looks like that of a burns victim. He does appear a true monster to behold.
[2] Describe your wrestler's ring attire: Monsutaa wears black wrestling trousers and boots and gloves. [3] Does your wrestler wear or carry anything that he leaves at ringside when wrestling? No. ** In-Depth Info ** [1] What makes this character unique? His facial burns, his manager, his power... [2] Wrestler's attitude/personality: Nikui Monsutaa is Japanese for Hateful Monster, and that sums him up. He is living hate. He doesn't like anyone except maybe his manager
Akkanka. He is a man focused on getting himself stronger, more devastating, on being a threat to every man and woman alive. He wrestles because it's the only thing he can do, it's the only thing anyone would employ him to do. The reason behind his hatred is unknown, but it is very real.
[3] How many years of professional wrestling experience does your wrestler have? (pro/non pro?)
Unknown, but he does seem experienced. [4] Strength and weaknesses. Please include a balance of at least three strengths and three weaknesses.
Strengths - Incredible, overwhelming power Cunning manager His hatred as a motive for hurting Weaknesses - Not very fast Prone to rage [4] Summary of experience/history. Please be descriptive. This is the most important segment of the application.
Nikui Monsutaa is the Japanese for Hateful Monster. Very little is known about him, and Mr. Akkanka likes to keep it that way.
Mr.Akkanka is the mouthpiece for Munsutaa. Monsutta is a 6'8, 368lb facially disfigured man of monumental muscle and hate. He doesn't really speak, and he isn't Japanse. He's an American repackaged by
Akkanka, a youngish Japanese-American (Akkanka means Evil Influence), smart, cocky, mouthy, cowardly - we're talking typical '80s WWF Heel
Wrestler/Manager combo here.
