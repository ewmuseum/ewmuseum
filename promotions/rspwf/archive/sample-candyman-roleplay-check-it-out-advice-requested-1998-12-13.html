~[Sample CandyMan Roleplay] Check it out.. advice requested :)

13 December 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey this flash is from a federation of mine called AIWA [Great Fed] [Cheap
Plug] :). Anyway, check it out.. see what ya think!
=== [The scene opens up on a card table. CandyMan is seated beside CandyFloss with some cards in their hands, whilst on the other side of the table there is a man and also another man who seems to be much older. Each of them with beards, like the classical poker player. As the man on the left of CandyMan shuffles the cards, the other one takes out his wallet and flaunts his cash.
This all leads CandyMan, to look on with a smirk.] CANDYMAN - Well, I guess I would best back up my words the way only I can. Through proof of things, Duff. You're probably sitting at home, looking in the mirror and thinking to yourself. . why am I calling you "Full of"
Bluff. Well, "Big Bad" Duff, take a look. [CandyMan smiles.] Deal 'em. [The man on the left begins to deal cards to each of the other two players along with himself. He ends up dealing so they have 5 cards each.]
Duff, this is the first mistake you always tend to make. As cards are not seen as they are dealt out, you *never* know what you are getting yourself into. You always speak before you look into things further, that is your first mistake in your line of failures.
Five cards lay before me. They're either a set up to my claim to fame, or a step down to failure. I can have a royal flush, or I can have a flock of dud's, that is why the key is to always look first before you start the talking, in case you can't back things up.
[CandyMan moves his cards closer towards him, still not turning them over.] You see Duff, when you attacked me with the New Breed, it was like predicting what lay before your cards. You took me out, costing me my World Title. You came out a week later, "Thinking" you ended my career like you got a royal flush, like I said, you called things to soon.
[CandyMan turns the cards over.] This is when the games, start. [The card are, a jack of hearts, a jack of spades, a king of clubs, a seven of spades and a two of hearts. He shuffles them around to make them look neater. As do his opponents, placing down money to continue on.]
The first strike. *Looking* at what you have, before you continue. That is what you forgot to do and that is where people like you pay. I came back when you least expected it, and dropped you like a bag of bricks. . .
....HARD!!! This is a part of how the game is played and you tried to take a short cut to make things end quicker but in reality, you came across a dead end and now you're stuck taking the long way, *Down*.
[CandyMan places money down, keeping the two jacks and replacing the rest of the cards. As the others decide what they want to keep, CandyMan checks to see the eye movements of the other two as they take their time.]
The second strike. *Predicting* the next bold movement. You're a member of the new breed and you have plenty of back up, though I have taken that stable down more times then Monica Lewinski took Bill Clinton down! [Grins.] I got a match against you on Thursday, are you going to fight like a man one on one?
Or will you need your friends, this is part of the predicting process. [Raises an eyebrow.] Looking before me, I see two men. They either got what it takes, or they don't in order to win this game. The man on my left, his eyes looking side to side cautious just like me, sweat drips from his head and he constantly looks at his cards, point noted.
The second man who is on my right, he looks at his cards and only at his cards. He doesn't worry about anything else, he simply plays thing cool. In my opinion a *Bluffer*, point noted.
I guess, just like things always in life, I will have to wait and see. [They each take the required number of cards, placing down more money with this. CandyMan brings the three cards and turns them around, showing the other two jacks in the set and also a ace of hearts. A four of a kind, lays before CandyMan's hand. The other two, proceed to do things their ways as well.]
MAN ON LEFT - Argh, I'm Out! [He drops his cards on the table, awaiting the other two to finish of the game. The man on the right, takes a look at his cards and raises the stakes to a high number in an attempt to make CandyMan drop out of the game.]
MAN ON RIGHT - I'll raise you 100. [The man stares right into the eyes of CandyMan, trying to intimidate but to no avail as CandyMan looks up, places twenty and drops his cards to the deck.
Showing his four of the kind, whilst the other man drops his cards to reveal nothing but empty numbers, worthless cards in other words his bluff. CandyMan sweeps the money up, during this time the other two get ready for the next game.]
Before we come to strike three, you must realize Duff. Their are two types of people, one that know when they do not stand a chance and don't try something which they can't end. When you attacked me, you should have realized this.
That man should have been you, but. . you fell right into the trap. Strike three. *Bluffing*, he tried and he failed. He had worthless chances and worthless odds of winning, yet he used his way to try and get the advantage. Duff this man is just like you, trying to intimidate, trying to trick, trying to defeat someone that has already defeated you, by bluffing.
"Big Bad" Duff, that is the game you're trying to play. A *Bluff*, tricking yourself into believing you can beat me, when you don't stand a chance. I've got the cards to remain a player, you don't have the guys admit you can't cope with the pressure. Duff, you're a big guy, but I've killed bigger giants than you in my career, remember that.
You know, that's three strikes and we both know what that mean's. . . ...YOUR OUT! [Chuckles.] I've proven what, I intended to. Come Thursday "Big Bad" Duff, I'm going to show you that you're. . .
..."SOFT AS" FLUFF!!!! ..."FULL OF" BLUFF!!!! AND I'M.. ..."GONNA GET" ROUGH! ...S W E E T D R E A M S!!!!!!!
