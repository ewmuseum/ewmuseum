-------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------(The camera fades in and Scott Dakota is in the gym preparing for his upcoming "I Quit" match against Ron Matthews. Dakota is lifing some weights, he pulls them up a few more times and then drops them to the ground and walks over to the bench and sits down, he pulls off his
Metallica t-shirt and pours some water onto his head which trickles down his face onto his chest. He runs his hand through his wet hair and then drys his hands on his shirt. Then he begins to speak.)
DAKOTA: Matthews, I am training harder than I have ever trained in my life for this match. I know that it is going to be the hardest, most grueling match-up that I have ever fought in my short yet successful wrestling career. Now, I fully understand that you have no intentions of quitting, and neither do I. As far as I know this match could go on until one of us cannot move, until one of us needs to be carried out of the arena on a stretcher, but that is alright with me. In case you did not know by now, I absolutley love pain, it is almost like a fetish for me. The feeling that runs through my body when I feel pain is literally undescribable. I love to get hurt, I love to bleed, I love to hear the sound of a bone snapping, it's just the way that I am. Now, I love the feeling of pain so much, that I also love to let other people experience the pain that I love. Matthews, I am going to inflict some serious pain on you, and I hope you inflict some pain on me as well. I say that I will never quit, you say that you will never quit, well we are going to find out who is lying this weekend.
(Dakota drinks the rest of the water out of the bottle and then brushes his long hair back again, then continues.)
DAKOTA: I am no quitter, never in my life have I ever submitted or given up, not for anyone or anything. As a young child I fell in love with professional wrestling, and I knew that it was my lifelong goal to be a wrestler, and to be a champion. I have literally gone through hell to get to where I am today. I have wrestled in the absolute worst feds, even worse than the RFWF. I have wrestled with broken bones and injuries against doctors orders, and I have still not tasted the bitter taste of defeat. If I was a quitter I think I would not have made it this far, to the big time of the USCW, I would have given it up a hell of a long time ago. But I didn't, and look where I am today. Matthews, if you think that there is a way to make me quit, I dare you to try your hardest to make me utter the words "I quit", it just won't happen. "Machine", I am going to break you down, and when I am done with you, you are going to be nothing more than a wreck of broken down parts that was once a fine tuned machine. You've messed with Scott Dakota, and for that you will be
SILENCED!! (Dakota gets up and walks away as the camera fades to black.) -------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------------------------------------
