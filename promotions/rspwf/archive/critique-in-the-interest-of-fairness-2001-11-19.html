[CRITIQUE] In the interest of fairness...

19 November 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Since I laid down a critique, I expect no less. This is a George
"Buzzsaw" Stevens roleplay I just randomly picked. Tell me what you think.
And yes, the accent is exaggerated on purpose. Scene: Buzzsaw waits for Khan to say something for him to respond to, but alas there has been nothing. So he sits, waits, and wonders exactly why Khan might not be talking at the moment.
[The camera fades into a bar. An old bar. Wooden barstools with sawdust on the floor, and George Stevens, clad in his jeans and leather vest with a baseball cap reading, "Three Biggest Lies" sits on a stool at the end of the bar, sippin' a brew and talkin' to th'
camera.] GEORGE "BUZZSAW" STEVENS (GBS): Well, ah been sittin' by th' ol' tee vee set at mah house, waitin' fer my upcomin' opponent, Khan, t' say somethin' so ah can respond t' it. Ah been sittin' so long, mah ass started hurtin'. So ah decided t' say "screw it", come on down t' th'
waterin' hole, 'n pop open a cold one. Ah know Khan ain' gonna say nuthin' t' me. 'N ah got some ideas as t' why. [Stevens takes a swig of his beer.] GBS: Ah figure Khan might'a got hit by a train er sumthin'. Maybe he got lost 'n cain't find his way back home. Maybe he forgot where RAW headquarters was, so he cain't send in any tapes he's done over the past couple'a days. Maybe he's jess that unlucky... er stupid.
Either way. But ah need t' give him credit here. Maybe he's not doin' too well. Maybe his pappy's sick wit' some rare form of venerial disease, 'n he's jess takin' care of him 'til he gits better. Givin' him that pers'nal touch. What a nice guy Khan is.
[Stevens chuckles and glares into the camera.] GBS: Er maybe he's scared. Yeah... must be it. Ah mean, he walked inta RAW as a Windy City champeen er sumthin' 'n thought he'd run through the competition. Then he saw ole Travis LaGrange, that nut
Spikyjim, 'n newbies wit' more talent than him like Simon Underwood 'n thought twice 'bout his braggin'...
'N then he saw his name on th' schedule... right next t' mine. 'N he knew he didn't belong here. He knew he was in over his head. He was like a puppy under a horse's ass right 'fore he shits. Ain' got no choice but ta close yer eyes, hold yer breath, 'n take it like a man.
But he ain' even doin' that. Nah... he's runnin' scared 'fore th' bell even rings. [Stevens picks up his beer again.] GBS: But ah'll tell ya what. Y'all come back here 'n tell me when he's gotten th' guts t' voice his opinions over the airwaves. Y'all tell me that he ain' no coward. That he's got a sack with a couple ah rocks in it, 'n he's ready t' fight like a man. If he says anythin', y'all come down here 'n git me. Ah'll be drinkin' 'n playin' pool 'n gittin' on wit' my life.
Cause there ain' no use sittin' at home... waitin' fer somethin' that's never gonna happen. [Fade to Stevens sighing and taking a swig of beer.]
