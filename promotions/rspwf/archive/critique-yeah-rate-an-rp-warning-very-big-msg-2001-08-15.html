Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just getting back into the saddle of an e-wrestler after some time in a fed not owned by me, although I never favoured my character one bit.... this is my first RP for BlackMarket[
http://blackmarket.steelramp.com ] so.... yeah, rate it. My character's history is below......     Rio Nexan was born on June 1 1975 in the sunny city of Havana, Cuba. Rio had a rough upbringing, as his parents emphasised on being strict. Furthermore, Rio had to take care of his two younger sisters. Rio's father died when he was only 6 years old, as the mechanic shop Rio's dad worked at caught fire and burnt to the ground. To make matters worse, Rio's mother had just lost her job as a secretary at a law-firm, due to increasing recession in the Carribean region. His mother sat Rio down one day and told him that he would have to be the sole breadwinner of the familty, as the only other job she could get was a job as a prostitute. Rio understood the severity of the situation and vowed that by the time he was 14 years of age, the family would have 1 million dollars in savings. For the next 8 years, Rio worked in a bunch of places, without letting his grades suffer. As his 14th birthday approached, Rio got anxious; he had still not liven up to his word. He then remembered about a underground fighting competition that had been going around, which had the prize money as 1 million dollars. Trouble was, even grown men couldn't win the competition. Rio was confident about his abilities though, and trained hard for the competition. The unbelievable happened as Rio Nexan emerged victorious in the competition, albeit almost at the risk of his life. Rio didn't care though;
he had proved to his friends who laughed off the idea of Rio being a street-fighter, and also accomplished his biggest dream. His family was once again riding the waves to cloud nine now, and Rio was happy. Throughout the course of the following year though, Rio Nexan excelled in school, particulary in gym class & English. While Rio tried to channel all his energy towards his weaker subjects, fighting had now become a part of his life, and most of his essays projected that feeling. Trouble was, Rio had no intention of becoming a fighter; he wanted to be involved in journalism. His teacher thought Rio was a class above that; his teacher believed Rio could become a script-writer for
Hollywood's box-office industry!! Rio started believing in that, and the dream culminated in him winning a nationwide essay competition; the prize being five thousand dollars!! With his mother and two sisters leading the peaceful life,
Rio made a decision that would impact his life. After 15 years in Havana, young
Rio moved to Hollywood to accomplish his aspiring dreams of becoming a script-writer. He made sure that his family was in good hands, before promising to wire whatever money he had at the end of each month.
  In Hollywood, jobs were in dearth but one small break saw him rise to stardom, as he conjured up scripts for blockbuster movies.
However, by now, Rio was in excellent physical shape. People around him noticed that and recommended he should get into pro-wrestling for the money. Rio was confident of his abilities, but wanted to join pro-wrestling simply because it was choreographed... scripted. Rio decided to throw caution into the wind and joined a wrestling academy. On his first day there, the trainer earmarked Rio as one who would potentially fail and quit the academy halfway through the course.
That fueled the fire in Rio, and Nexan proved the trainer absolutely wrong... by graduating halfway through the course!!
  When he turned 20 which was in 1995, he decided to fully enter the scripted world of pro-wrestling. He started out in a small indy circuit called the Philedelphian Wrestling League, which was to become his home for the next 4 years. During that time, Rio not only established himself as a solid competitor, but also has a great worker behind-the-scenes, as he contributed ideas for many angles. Jealousy was brewing between the other wrestlers, and they smelt something fishy when Rio, quite deservedly, got his push into the main-event. One wrestler in particular was current Heavyweight
Champ, Artrise. After a couple of months, Artrise was told by the brass that it was time for him to put Rio over. Artrise was none too happy about it and after laying down to Rio and thereby losing the Heavyweight Title, Artrise threatened to leave the company. The brass couldn't afford it as Artrise was their only major heel and were forced to come up with a way to make Artrise happy.
  On June 1 1999, exactly 4 years after Rio entered the circuit and after 5 months as Heavyweight Champ, Rio & Artrise fought in the rematch everybody wanted to see. It ended up in a moment of deja vu, as Rio was screwed the same way Bret Hart was screwed in November 1997. Rio was assured a
DQ victory before the match by the brass, and the promise was broken. Needless to say, Nexan was pissed and left the company. 2 weeks later, Nexan discovered that his sweetheart of 3 years, Melissa, had been having an affair, with none other than Artrise. This drove Rio over the edge, and during a tryout for another small indy fed in August 1999, Rio worked stiffly with the fed's lower-card wrestler. Rio was banned from wrestling for 18 months because of that incident. A couple of weeks later, the script he had been working on for a huge movie was burnt in a fire, and it was believed that Rio started the fire because of massive changes he had to make in the script. Rio's life was over.... or so he thought.
  On a whim, Rio decided he was not going to work with scripts ever again, and started work in a couple of shoot-fighting clubs. It proved to be a decision that wrecked the lives of many, as Rio took out all the frustations he had on everybody and anybody. 16 months into his ban, Rio decided to take a break from shoot-fighting, and returned home to Havana. His family, who had not heard from Rio since the latter was handed his ban, was very relieved. Rio used this opportunity to reconnect with his family and friends, and also to recharge his body. Finally, January 2001 came around... that meant that the ban was over, and Rio was free to return to the wrestling world.
However, Rio decided to stay in Havana for a while longer, and help his friend set up a mechanic business instead.
  Unknown to all around him, some of Rio's friends back in the States had been telling Nexan about some of the latest federations that had sprung up. Rio turned them all down though, as he wanted his next federation to be a fighting federation... a place that wasn't classified as a shoot-fighting club but wouldn't punish him if he had hurt anyone on purpose.
Apparently, Nexan had still not forgotten all the crap that was handed to him. His friends tried to convince Rio to join them in a wrestling federation, but
Nexan continued to decline them.
  In March 2001, Rio headed back to the USA, and upon touching down in Miami, heard about the Underground Fighting League, the latest subsidiary of the UFCorp. Rio was pretty excited about the propect of fighting in the UFL, as he thought that it was a non-booked fed. He was wrong though;
UFCorp owner Kamlesh 3:16 set the record straight and also warned Rio that if he wanted to be a part of the UFL, he would have to adhere to a set of rules, one of which was to NOT work stiffly. Rio was very disappointed, but had a feeling that a run in the UFL would cement himself as a legend. It would also give him the chance to be promoted to the Underground Fighting Federation..... and fight greats such as Bloodthorne, Jakks, Chirag Da-Man, Westly & Eddy "Too Cool";
all of whom were legends in their own right. Rio decided that he would try out for the UFL. During the tryout though, Nexan accidently worked stiffly with another UFL wannabe. After the tryout, Kamlesh 3:16 pulled him to one corner and told Rio that the latter had enermous potential and that he would be accepted into the UFL. However, Kamlesh 3:16 went on to say that since Nexan worked stiffly, he would have to start out as a "jobber". If he didn't like that or continued to work stiff, Kamlesh would get Nexan banned from wrestling for life.
As Nexan returned to his apartment that night, a million thoughts invaded his mind. After hours of deliberating, Nexan decided he would give up trying to vent his frustrations on something that he couldn't change and signed the UFL contract. But unknown to Rio, another chapter in his life was about to begin.....
  In June 2001, just as Rio was getting a mini-push in the UFL for his commendable work, Nexan logged onto the Internet and saw a new message in his inbox about a new fighting federation... Black Market. Rio's heart skipped a beat as at the end of the message, he saw a line that said that
Black Market would not hold anyone responsible if that someone injured or killed another fighter. Needless to say, Rio raced to the Black Market HQ, which was a warehouse at a wharf in New Jersy. Nexan was given a chance to prove the owners that he had what it took to be in BM, and although Nexan had a big match coming up in the UFL the next few days, he did enough to prove Lord Cheenu and
Cheese-Wanker that he belonged in BM. As Rio continues to prove himself as a wrestling fighter in the UFL, he anticipates the day BM opens.... for he can finally kill someone without it being in a shoot-fighting club...................

Nexan takes this opportunity to get up from his chair and grab a can of beer from near his gym bag. as he opens the can and sits back down on his chair, a message appears, asking if Nexan is ready to watch the "movie". the fighter from Cuba takes a sip of the beer and clicks "yes", before putting his feet on the table...]
