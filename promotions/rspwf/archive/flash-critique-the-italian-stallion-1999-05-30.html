[FLASH] Critique..."The Italian Stallion"

30 May 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I really don't know why I'm doin this...I haven't done this in a long time, but
I want to see what people think of my work nowadays. Nevermind the Pillow bashing, just some very cheap heat...HeHeHe. Sorry JP :-p (It was fun though)
Now this was a flash that I wrote for GLOW last week in my match against Chris
Hunter. Don't waste my time tryin to flame me by saying I suck...Just tell me what you like or dislike and leave it at that. Thanx
[The scene opens up with a shot of Central Park in New York City. There is a large group of people walking in front of the camera and as they disperse you see the one and only "Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno sitting on a park bench...
...Tony is wearing a pair of blue jeans with black boots and a white "Stallion" t-shirt on. He is supporting his usual pair of sunglasses with pictures of
Stallions on the lens'. His jet black hair is slicked back into a pony tail and his goatee is neatly trimmed into thin lines across his chin and his sideburns are trimmed in the same thin lines coming down just past his ears. As always,
"The Stallion" is looking his best...
...The scene then changes to a shot of Tony walking down the isleway, from last weeks Hot Spot, with Dead Skin Mask by Slayer playing over the loudspeakers.
The scene then quickly changes to Tony standing in the ring with a mic in his hand.] "The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno: Erie, Pennsylvania...You people finally get to hear the words of the biggest and baddest man that this sport has ever produced...
[The tape rewinds and plays again.] "The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno: The biggest and baddest man... [The scene freezes, but you hear the same phrase said once again.] "The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno: The biggest and baddest man... [The scene the quickly changes to side profile of "The Stallion" sitting at the park bench in Central Park.]
"The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno: G.L.O.W....Every last fan must be sitting at home with smiles on their faces and a happiness inside of them like never before after "The Stallion" made an appearance on Hot Spot this past week.
[Rolls his eyes as if he could care less about the fans.] Yet, on the other hand. Every wrestler currently employed by the G.L.O.W. must be sitting at home, at a hotel or where ever they are, with tears clouding their vision and their pulses racing. "The Stallion" showed his face and I have changed the lives of everyone involved with G.L.O.W. Last week I made an impact like none other. I came out and I told those guys who are running this show what they were gonna do and they did it. I said jump and they said "How High?"...
[Again the scene changes to another shot of last week's Hot Spot during Paseno's Hot Spot debut...] "The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno: Now don't go and get me wrong here. I'm not sitting out here and begging the suits in the back to give me a match next week...Hell I'm not even asking for a match...
I'm _Demanding_ It!! [The scene then switches back to Paseno sitting at the park bench. He sits there gathering his thoughts for a moment before he begins to talk.]
"The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno: It looks like the suits of G.L.O.W. know that when "The Stallion" speaks, that they had better listen closely. They've seen me around before and the know for a fact that I'm not here to play games.
I am here for one reason and one reason only...to put all the gold around my waist. It will come in due time. I'm gonna be making my run to the top starting this week and I won't stop until I become the G.L.O.W. Heavyweight Champion of the World. And by the looks of it, "Simply the Best" James Pillow will have to be forced into putting that belt on the line, if he's still got it by then, because he knows it and I know it...Pillow just don't want any part of me. He never has and he never will. Now, don't get me wrong, I don't have any problems with Pillow, I never have. But when I'm walking around backstage and Pillow walks the other way to avoid me, well it's gotta tell ya something. Actually it makes me think of a saying I've heard many times over...
_If You're Scared..._ _Say You're Scared..._ ...You don't have to walk around with fear in your eyes all the time Pillow because I'm not going to be able to take what I want from you while you''re out on the streets or in your locker room. But when the match is finally signed, then there won't be one man in here that would blame you for having that undisguisable fear in your eyes, or walking the other way when you see me coming. Enough on you Pillow.
[Tony rubs his head for a moments as if he's trying to remember something.] "The Italian Stallion" Tony Paseno: Oh...excuse me folks. I was just trying to remember the name of this guy I'm fighting this week on Hot Spot. Oh yeah, It's
"Cold Blooded" Chris Hunter. Have you seen this guy in the ring yet? If you have then you wouldn't be surprised that I couldn't remember his name. I honestly have no clue why they would stick me in the ring with someone so much lower than myself. Maybe they just wanna make sure that "The Stallion's" still got it in him. Hell, I got no problem with that. I don't mind warming up in the ring with someone that doesn't know what the word "Offense" means. I'll just slap him around like a little girl for a few minutes and get an easy one-two-three. Nothing to it. An easy win for Paseno this week on Hot Spot, but then again...They're all easy when you're this damn good.
[Tony stands up and walks away as the scene fades to black.]
