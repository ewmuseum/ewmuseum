Roleplay Critique

31 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In ...] [The picture on the screen opens up to the night sky. Stars ate lite bright on this beautiful night. The moon is shinning bright as it is a full moon. The night sky is anything but cloudy. At the viewing of the ngith sky, you seem to be very relaxed. Pleased, you set your head down and begin to think about things. As you get deep into the thought of the future, and what it holds, loud music is heard playing ...
... The camera pans down and noticed is nothing else by a brand new two thousand cherry red Lamborghini. this car is an absolute standout. Tinted windows, loud music, luxiorious gold rims. You can also notice a gold colored light which shines under the vechile. This, to you, tops everything else off, and you know that this car must be owned by someone young, exciting, and undoubtably wealthy ...
... As the camera pans around the car, a few other things are seen. A few things which would also catch the eye of the personalized vechile. As the camera is on it's third circle, the camera stops moving and goes inside of the vechile. This is another thing which is very special, and unique. A navigation system is seen, the stereo system is spelling out the name: DAMON KNIGHT. The black leather seats are extremely comfortable, and they seemingly form to your every move and adjustment. Suddenly, the camera changes angles all in a flash, showing the right half of the body of one Damon Knight ...
... Damon Knight, obvisiously lounging, is wearing designer clothes as he always does. This time Mister Knight is taking it easier on some of you. He feels that his usual attire is out of your league, and he has stepped down a little bit, wearing a black IceBerg Jean set, which has "ICEBERG" written all over it in glacier white letters. Damon Knight is keeping his corn rolls down with a black nylon skull cap, and he is bouncing his head to the recently released Black Rob CD, "Life Story". The son is one with Lil' Kim in it, and is quite nice. Dee Dee Kay, then pushes a button on the sterring wheel, turning the music down. He looks into the camera for a second then turns his head back to the road. You can feel the car accelerating, however, you are not too worried, because there are no other vechiles on the road. Suddenly, you all feel blessed as Damon Knight begins to talk ...]
DK : Ladies and gentlemen, once again, you are going to have the listening pleasure, the viewing pleasure, and well ... [Chuckles] All kinds of pleasures at my expense, Damon Knight. [Smiles] Your lord and savior.
[An semi-evil, over confident look comes onto the face of Damon Knight.] DK : Once again two fools on the Gee Pee Dubbya roster choke on something. [Smirk] They choked when put in tha spotlight. Kristopher Evans was thrown as
Damon Knight, and I pulled a Mark McGwire and set him out of the ball park.
[Knight shakes head in disbelief that he is even speaking of the men he has defeated.] DK : Then [Motioning to himself] tha ultimate male goes up against this sick and twisted individual in Zachary Shap. [Snickers] Zach Sharp
[Pause] a former Gee Pee Dubbya champion went down at tha hands of Dee Dee
Kay. [Knight catches the eye of you, and everyone else with the slightest motion. This time you are caught by the changing of the gears.] DK : Zach Sharp then lost his cool and had the audasity to put his hands on me. He had the audasity to put his filthy, discusting hands on Damon
Knight. Why? [ Shrugs] I don't know. Guess he couldn't help being tha main attractions' personal punching for tha night. Do I care? I regret to inform you folks, that I do care.
[Knight looks into the camera with a sinister smirk across his face.] DK : See, I was blessing the fans by acknowledging them after I made short work of you, Zach. You may not know it, but that was defiantly a mistake. You are nothing less then a tinker bell wrestler, and you were an even worse Brass Knux champion.
[Knights face is then seen with a frown on it, however, somehow the man is still as beautiful as ever. Must be great to be Damon Knight.]
DK : How tha hell do you have a moraless piece of tin, and do nothing for the title? [Knight looks stunned] Never in all my years have I seen something so repulsing. You are one of the reasons why I didn't want to put my house hold name on any Gee Pee Dubbya contracts.
Sharp, growing up I was taught to always make my first move, my best move. Welp, yours has come and gone. If I am not six feet deep, then I am put on a higher pedigree. You did all you could, still I set here in this beautiful automobile, and I contemplate about my next move, and what I should do with you. Well Sharp, i'ma address you first.
[Damon Knight rubs his chin in a thinking notion.] DK : Well, i'll carry it like this ... [Laughs] You better get some soap, attach it to a rope, and get that hole on your backside sewed shut!
[Stops laughing, and clears his throat] You do the math, tough guy. [As the song changes, Damon Knight takes a deep breathe and lets it all out. Knight continues to talk.] DK : Now moving on, I'll talk about my upcoming match. A match that could either make, or break good ole Dee Dee Kay ... Or that is what tha world is talking about. [Grin] Damon Knight verses Chris Page.
[Pause.] DK : For y'alls' knowledge, Damon Knight is a made man. And something else to stimulte your mind, [Smirk] Damon Knight isn't a damn toy, and I shall not be broken. Christopher Page you are gon' go down in tha Gee Pee
Dubbya history books of a loser. A never has been, and a never will be. [Knight chuckles before he continues to berate his upcoming opponent.] DK : Page, the buy rates for Global Pro-Wrestlin' house shows are up, and we all know why? [Suprised look comes across Knight's face] 'Cause tha devastation that is rockin' tha nation has joined this flea market federation.
I have straight up and totally devastated all opponents. Christopher Page after coming off of a tough loss, you are gon' take the role of Dennis Rodman and get the rebound. [Shaking head] That just isn't going to happen, not on my account.
[Damon Knight with a confident look comes onto the face of Knight.] DK : Page ever since joining Gee Pee Dubbya I have been watching you. I know that you are good, hell, some may even say 'great'. But Dee Dee Kay,
Gee Pee Dubbya's light at the end of the tunnel is devastatin'. I also don't give into tha hype.
I'ma break this one down real easy for ya Chrissy ole boy, i'll tell ya what'cha are gon' be. When you wake up the day after our match in some two bit, roach infested hotel, and the ladies are gone, the groupies are gone, the followers are no longer following you, and your neck is damn near broke you'll know that you have been _totally_ _devastated._
[Knight gets a pleased look onto his face, and he goes onto talk.] DK : Page, your bigger then me, and you are gon' go down at the hands of your lord and savior, the ultimate male, and the man that you wish you were, "Devastating" Damon Knight.
Now, I digress. Now I am to speak on such other losers who get on Television and make complete asses out of themselves. To keep it short, and for me to kick it sweet, i'ma let you know that Damon Knight is going to be making all of your dreams come to life. I'll be making them a reality. Damn tha elephant dung, set back, relax, and take a good look at your future. Ladies and gents, get used to seeing this face because everywhere you turn, it is gon' be starring right at'cha.
[Damon Knight accelerates and continues to talk.] DK : Yo, i'm ... i'm Lots of action ...Twice the satisfaction ... and If i'm not the main event ... I am DEFINITELY the MAIN ATTRACTION!! Page, you'll be hearing from me. Global Pro-Wrestlin' y'all can keep on hatin', and at tha same time be tha Damon Knight fan club commitee.
["Devastating" Damon Knight looks into the camera with that always over confident look on his face. He then clicks the automobile into the last and final gear before exceding the cars listed speed. With this being done, you know that Knight is going to be reaching his destanation in a faster pace then most. From this, the question flies around in your head, making you wonder if
Knight is going to be doing the same in Gee Pee Dubbya ...]
