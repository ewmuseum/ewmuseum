CycLoNe rp (please crytique)

13 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CycLoNe {"The Extreme Superstar" CycLoNe is sitting backstage in his lockerroom wearing his WBW United States Belt,WBW Tag Team Title(s), and his PWWD Brawler Bracket
Title Belt over his shoulder.} He is sitting down wearing a white cutsleeve shirt that says "CycLoNe the Extreme Superstar" in red and on the back it has a picture of him tossing Gary Brown off a cage while he set him on fire at the
PWWD National Wrestling Tournament.} He is sitting drinking a Mountain Dew and relaxing just arriving back into his lockerroom after beating "ICON" Robbie
Crasveck in a steel cage match in World's Best Wrestling.} As he lays back some more the camera zooms in}
CycLoNe- Well...well..well. So yet I see another oppurtunity for Cyclone here. Another place to prove tottal dominance and more. I just arrived in EWA and I couldn't wrestle due to a small injury. I am sure you will all learn who I am very very quickly. I've been from federation to alliance from tournaments to other federations and I have domanated most everyone of them. They don't call me the extreme superstar for nothing. I have earned everything, I've been set on fire, had my mouth washed out with soap, thrown off a cage, hit with a bat, and been powerbombed onto thumbtacts. I may be a smaller person but I always prove to be better in the end. Always. Failure is not a term in my book. I have won title after title, I have also had my hartships.
No I see some pretty stupid people here in EWA. Some being "Damien the Fallen" or whatever the hell. Now, just looking at this guy makes me wanna roll over and never quit laughing but I am playing the role of the serious man today. I mean this guy looks like a blast from the past. He is so stupid my kids won't even watch that fat trash. Sorry thing is I don't have kids. How bout this fruit they call "Desert Dog". This guy should be kept off television till he learns to vut an interview I don't fall asleep in. Boy, I don't even think that could happen to tell ya the truth. Well EWA is gonna undergo alot of changes now that I arrived.. first of all we gotta do something about the champions. We should put them all under my name. From watching this show I personally can't spot anybody actually good enough to take me on one on one. But that's okay. It just makes it easier for me to clean up the mess. To tell you the truth "Rick
Gerricho" is the only solid talent in this federation with exception of J.J.
Gold and Me. So like I said it'll be an easy clean up job. {"The Extreme Superstar" CycLoNe takes another drink of his Mountain Dew and the camera man is signaling to say something.}
CycLoNe- What is it camer man?? Camera Man- Well I couldn't help but over here you said you were one the most extreme people in this sport, well..what about that one fat pyscho named Jack
Cactus or is Love Dudes and ummm well ya know that fat guy who gets his ass beat all around. Kickm' Foley or something.
CycLoNe- See camera man you have alot to learn too... the difference between me and that fat deranged physco is that I am smart when I wrestle. You can look at anyone of these little pin dolls and notice how great CycLoNe really is, and there's nothing they or you can do about it camera man *camera man gulps*
Ahh yes lets get back to the point. I am intruducing myself to EWA, I am gonna make you aware of all my talents and more.... I am not afraid to trade on people. I have double crossed the best... if you don't take my word for it see it for yourself.
{ The camera man rolls CycLoNe's highlight footage.}Scene shows him and his
Triple Threat partner Mass Destruction. CycLoNe hits him with a chair and then
DDT's him on thumbtacts injuring him giving Mass Destruction a broken leg.}
Highlight 2 {Shows the PWWD National Tourney with a burning hell in a cell cage. Smoke is all around the cage. Shows CycLoNe and his friend KRaZ-e tossing him in a dumpster and setting him on fire. Then they show them rolling him off the cage.}
Highluight 3 {Scene starts at WBW Monday Mayhem with CycLoNe tying up all of the NwA members and washing out Scott Steele's mouth whith soap after beating him out cauntious}
CycLoNe- That's very little to me. I've won so many titles and whipped so many asses it's not even worth counting. After beat you guys so bad I'll even be nice enough to give a face job. If that's what you want of course. If they say the damage is too bad I'll fight you again and see what I can do. I am easily the most deadly wrestler in EWA. I've beaten plenty of people in this federation. Jim Hart. I've seen this guy get his ass beat so bad and so long it's not even funny anymore. I think Jim Hart would remember the man who kicked his ass twice and the same tourney. Although he did go on to be yours truly the greatest right here.
{The camera man signals to talk} Camera Man- Well sir, maybe you arn't really that great I mean you havn't even wrestled here in EWA and here you are yapping your jaws off. That footage means crap.
CycLoNe- Now listne here camera man, now matter where I go or whatever I do
I'll always be the best. These little indy feds are trash. I use them for garbage. Everyone here knows I am not only gonna kick there ass in but I am gonna make a mochery of them. To tell you the truth it will be real easy. I have one so many belts it makes me tired thinking about it camera man.
Camera Man- Well, I am sorry to bother you Mr. Extreme Superstar. CycLoNe- Damn right you should be, whenever you cut into my interview time think before you talk. Well on a bottom line note I am calling everybody out.
All I can say is I don't give a damn if I hurt you or not. So Prez sign me match next EWA show and I'll show this little trashy fed and everyone else how good I really am cause I am akways 3 steps ahead of the game and one more for my damn show, so be before you even think about challening me remember one thing.....
I AM THE WHOLE DAMN SHOW!! {Scene fades to black as CycLoNe takes another drink of Mountain Dew} Quality: It's okay I was itching for words Now get down on your knees cause I own you....
