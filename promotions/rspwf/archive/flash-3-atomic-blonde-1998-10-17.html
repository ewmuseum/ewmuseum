<The scene fades into a smokey pool hall, surrounded by think layers of cigar smoke. A mixture of loud music and screams echo throughout the hallowed walls, where severeal rugged men line the bar stools & card tables, reaking from a mixture of stale piss & sweat. The camera pans around, greeted by the Atomic
Blonde who sits atop a pool table wearing a t-shirt, blue jeans, dark sunglasses, and puffing fanatically at a cigar. His face is still badly bruised from his December 24th match with Billy Reel, with traces of faded blood in his hair and on his dirty face. With the pool stick in his hand, he extends it, tapping the side of the camera...>
"Anyone home? Are you there, Reel? Come out and play, B*tch. Let's go one more round. You've got a win under your belt and a foot up your ass. Now lets see if there's room for five knuckles. You wanna shatter someone's skull? Then go one more round with me, Reel. You wanna get nasty?"
<Blonde brings the pool strick across the back of his neck and violently snaps it in two, squinting his eyes and letting out a soft groan. He tosses the broken pieces to the floor and stands up on top of the pool table, looking down into the camera...>
"Well let's get f<censored>ing nasty, Billy Reel!!" <Blonde's words bring a halt to all other conversation in the room, as all attention is drawn to the pool table where he stands. His head sinks into his shoulders and he glares around the room at a sea of overweight, wide eyed, drunks...>
"Look at this, Reel.This is MY kingdom! Not a whorehouse full of teen age girls busting out of their bras at the sight of their hero, Billy Reel. This is the real f<censored>ing deal! Respect in and out of that ring. You can't take this away from me. Hell.. you can't even earn it yourself. You don't strike fear into my heart, and you don't send shivers down my spine. You leave a taste of disgust in my mouth. Step into that ring with me one more time, and I'll spit it back in your face."
<Blonde eyes a class coke bottle resting along the edge of the pool table and kicks it wildly, sending it across the room and crashing against the wall above the head of an old man. Blonde kneels down on top of the pool table and continues...>
"Wednesday, The Atomic Blonde returns to the arena he left in shame. Maniac
Mike... unless your name is Billy Reel, you've got no reason to be in that ring with me. Your name may not be Billy Reel, but since you seem to be so hellbent on playing his role, I'll be more than happy to treat you as his stunt double.You picked a bad time to plot your funeral.. and an even worse opponent to have carry your plan out. Consider your ass 24 hours away from being fried.
Rememeber... I stuck up foot up Reel's ass... and that leaves one more to use on you." <Blonde hops down from the pool table and makes his way to the bar, shoving and pushing his way through, as the shot fades to black...>
