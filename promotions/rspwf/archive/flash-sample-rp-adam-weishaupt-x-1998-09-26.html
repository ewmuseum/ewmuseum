26 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's a RP that I wrote for the Full Contact Wrestling Association (FCWA), which can be located at
http://www.fortunecity.com/rivendell/lunar/549/main.html : Tell me what you think, what improvements I could make, so on and so on... (fade in) [Scene consists of a typical high-volume day in a large city. Cars pass by, pedestrians hurry by, the usual. Except for one thing. A large throne sits in the middle of the sidewalk, with yellow "DO NOT CROSS"
tape surrounding the area nearby. Four large men make sure no one disturbs the border. Sitting on the throne is a muscular blond long-haired man, completely naked (except for the convenient blurring by the censors). The man looks slightly off-camera, straightens in the chair, and begins to speak.]
Adam: Greetings, peasants. My name is Adam Weishaupt the tenth. Yes, that's
"Adam Weishaupt X" for the many unwashed illiterates employed by the FCWA who aren't familiar with the Roman numeral system. God, I just looked through the ranks of this petty little federation. It appears they held a "Stone Cold
Steve Austin" imitation contest in a particularly dilapidated trailer park and picked the first twenty inbred morons who could string the words "ass", "hell", and
"DTA" into a sentence. But enough about you. Let's move on to something better.
Me. If any of you illiterate goons ever dared to check a newspaper (beyond the
"funnies",
anyway) you would see my name constantly repeated in different articles. Why is that, you may slobber out of your toothless maw? It's because I happen to be the richest man who ever lived, period. I own damn near everything worth owning and I would own this federation, too, if it weren't for the fact that I have a sense of fair sport.
Indeed, the only reason I am here is because when you're as rich as I am, the finical world ceases to be a challenge. You just own it, period. However, I have always been interested in the athletic realm...and have used my considerable wealth to hire only the best trainers, the best gyms, the best ...enhancements.
And as you can see [flexing muscles] I am six feet of pure talent just waiting to explode on a lesser being like YOU.
[Adam laughs for a few seconds.] One last question, you might ask. Why am I sitting naked in a throne on a busy sidewalk on the busiest street of the city?
Simple. Because I can. There aren't any police officers, any politicians, anybody PERIOD who won't listen to me. And if there happens to be an exception to this last rule...well...you'll see.
You'll see very soon. [Adam beings to laugh, a deep laugh which continues even after the screen fades to black.]
