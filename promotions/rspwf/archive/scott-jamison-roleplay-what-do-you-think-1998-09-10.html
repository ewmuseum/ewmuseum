[Scott Jamison Roleplay] What do you think¿?¿

10 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The screen fades in from black to show an overview of the Las Vegas Strip. "The City of Light's" is yet to let down it's many admirers. As the camera zooms in, the view changes again and again ... and finally fixes on a close up of the MGM Grand Hotel and Casino. Many fans of professional wrestling flock here annually to watch Pay Per Views from many different organizations, but maybe more importantly, they flock here to maybe catch a glimpse of one of
Professional Wrestling's hottest young stars, "The Show Stopper" Scott Jamison.
The camera cuts to outside the door of Jamison's 23rd story presidential suite, that he calls home 6 months out of the year. A Gentle knock at the door is answered Jamison's very lovely valet, Miss Tammi Winters. She invites the camera crew in, and then disappears. Sitting in a lazy boy chair right in front of the window, admiring the view is Jamison. He turns to the camera, and speaks in a rather low key manor...]
"The Show Stopper" Scott Jamison - Well, You all heard the reports. You all heard what the experts had to say about me. I am "Not Hot" as of right now.
[Jamison begins to mumble under his breath, yet knowingly loud enough for the camera to pick it up...] Damn Susan Douche thought I was pretty damn hot when she kept paging me last night! [Jamison continues in his normal voice...] But that is no matter to me. I did not expect to jump into the N.E.W. and become an automatic Main Eventer. I need to work my way up, and that is what I am doing right now.
[Jamison gets up from the chair, and leans against the wall...] Jamison - You know who I am like... I remind myself a lot of a professional basketball player that goes by the name of Kobe Bryant. He comes into the NBA, and is immediately labeled as "The Next Michael Jordan". Much like me, I came into the N.E.W., and immediately was labeled "The Next Ivan Ramius". Now me and Kobe can definitely hold our own, but it takes time to develop a champion.
It takes time to develop out skills, and adjust to our new surroundings. It isn't a matter of _"IF"_ we will be that great, because undoubtedly we will ...
it is a question of _when_ this will happen. [Miss Winters walks through the kitchen door, and walks over to Jamison. She whispers something in his ear, and then walks out again, not speaking a word to the camera...]
Jamison - Miss Winters informs me that I have yet another match to be critiqued this Friday. A man named Warren, Tim Warren. Again, another test that should prove relatively easy, until the nerves start kicking in. I mean,
Look at what happened last week! I tossed John Smith around the ring for a half an hour, I was toying with him ... and then he reversed my Moonsault.
That's not a loss in my eyes, simply a "Bad Break". Tim Warren will be much like John Smith ... only this time I will make quick work of him. No more lollygaggin' around. I mean, Hell, I don't even know very much about this
Warren Character, so I can't very well sit here and tell you all how bad he is
... but I can tell you that I won't back down, not for a second. I won't back down from Tim Warren, nor will I back down from any of the White Shirts in the front office of the N.E.W. I won't back down from any of the challenges that are put in front of me, and If I am unfortunate enough to catch another bad break, I won't back down from stepping in the ring again. I'll just keep on fighting, until I win, or I die. Yeah, I got attitude, but I know how to win too.
The New Wave... Of the N.E.W........ Has Arrived.
