Jakob flash ... Respond please!

13 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
** This is the first flash I've done with my new character, Jakob, and I'd like all the input you guys can give me on this flash. Thanks! ** [The scene opens at the entrance of a dark, elusive alley; location unknown. The time of day would appear to be night, considering that all is quiet and dreary. Now, going further into the depths of the alley you begin to see a foggish mist take form, slow at first but then building into cloud-like status quickly. In the background of the fog a shape appears. It is a huge man, but you can't make out exactly who it is. However, after you hear his low, harsh voice you immediately come to the conclusion that it is indeed
Jakob.]
There comes a time in a man's life when he must choose a path. There is the way he WANTS to go, and the way he NEEDS to go. Deciphering which is which is a confusing ordeal, and one must take great caution before he makes his final decision. For the slightest error in judgement can lead to a lifetime of regret and sorrow ...
[Jakob steps through the mist and now appears fully. In his right hand is the Reverend Sin cloak that he once wore.]
Donovan, why do you have to show your face again? I thought I had destroyed you months ago. I have tried time and time again to pretend that you never held a spot in my life ... that you never existed. But dreams of pain and agony torment me, and in the center of all the havoc is you.
[Jakob takes a deep breath, then continues.] To be perfectly honest with you, Donovan, the time I spent with you is nothing more than a blur. All I can see when I close my eyes and try to remember is a dark shade of red accompanied by hate, greed, and perversion.
My heart, too, feels like a dull blade has tried to cut through it, but failing in the process. I feel as if my whole existence then was nothing more than a waste of oxygen.
[Jakob kicks softly at the gravel on the ground of the alley.] I made a vow to myself to never let you in again. I've sworn up and down that all I need is myself. I spent a good deal of these past months rebuilding my soul and trying to expel any demonic thoughts.
[A smile slowly creeps up on Jakob's face.] But why do I have a gut feeling that I'm convincing myself that the inevitable will never happen? Why are people like Joey Jackson and the
Gospel Preachers annoying me so much that I feel the urge to tear them apart piece by piece? And most importantly, why do I carry this cloak around with me when I could just as well throw it in a nearby dumpster?
[Pause.] To be honest with you, I have no damn clue. It's as if a force stronger than anything else in this universe is forcing me to give second chances ...
to throw caution to the wind. It's as if I have no control over myself, but rather something supernatural is the guiding culprit. The main question that plague my mind is this ... will this force guide me the way I WANT to go, or the way I NEED to go?
[Long pause as Jakob stares deeply into the camera and gets a grim, evil look upon his face.]
Aside from that, there's a few people that I need to address ... First off, J.W. Brimstone, you say that YOU have to despose of ME. Well,
Mr. Darkside, I'm afraid you have it turned around. You see, I can understand why you feel threatened by my existence. You're new here, and I'm aware of that, but you have to respect your elders. I can tell you have alot of learning to do, and come Sunday Inferno when you "feel the onslaught" maybe you'll begin to comprehend a thing or two about me. And if you think that me leaving the Warriors of Light was a move to avoid the wrath of you, well ... you're sadly mistaken. There's nothing I'd like more than to completely annihilate you, Brimstone. After that stunt you pulled at Christmas Carnage, you have some SERIOUS hell to pay. Because as you will soon see, I get mad ... and I damn sure get even!
[Short pause.] As for Joey Jackson, "The Word", I have only a few comments. Number one being that I in absolutely no way have or will have any fear for you simply because you claim to be a man of God. I dare you to try to avenge your fallen soldier, Eric Wayne. He was a coward and he ran when things got heated. Is that how you're going to be, Mr. Jackson? Will you hide in your holy palace when the bad and the ugly show themselves? I suppose that's a question that will remain unanswered until the situation arrives, but I can guarantee you this, Mr. Jackson ... it WILL arrive.
[A sinister look appears on Jakob's face.] Secondly, Joey Jackson, you might as well get the idea of revenge out of your tainted brain. In no way, shape, or form am I worried about your presence right now. I have more important matters to tend to, and you will come at a later date. So I suggest you sit in your confession booth and ask
God to forgive you of all the nasty, sinful things you plan on doing to me, because you're not going to get at me whenever you want. You see, Mr.
Jackson, I'LL decide when I want to destroy you! For now, I suppose I'll just let you THINK about what you stated earlier ... and then see if you have any statements you'd like to withdrawl. I'll give you a second chance to back down, Joey.
[Jakob steps backward into the fog and is now seen how he was during the beginning of the segment, which would be barely visible.]
And if you don't, Mr. Jackson, I suppose you, too, will have to ... FEEL THE ONSLAUGHT!!!
