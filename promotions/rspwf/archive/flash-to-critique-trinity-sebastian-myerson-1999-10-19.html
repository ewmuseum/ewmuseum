[Flash to critique] "Trinity" Sebastian Myerson

19 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Brightness...so bright it makes you squint your eyes to the point where they're almost completely shut. The camera slowly pans back and down away from the blazing sun hanging high in the sky. The camera now picks up the small town of Athens, Georgia. The streets look practically deserted as the camera slowly pans around when suddenly the sound of church bells in the nearby distance are heard..]
"BONG, BONG, BONG!" [The camera follows the sound until we find ourselves outside of a church as the doors open and people begin to file out by the dozens. The townspeople for the most part seem to stick around the area, having conversations with each other, just as it looks as if everyone has exited the building the camera sees one last person step out from the church. He walks forward, a few people say hi to him. He then stops next to a few people and engages in conversation with them for a moment before he begins to walk down the stairs of the church leading down to the sidewalk. The camera follows him as we see he's dressed in a navy blue suit. He takes the jacket off slinging it over his shoulder as he loosens his necktie and unbuttons the top few buttons of his white dress shirt.
The camera moves in on him as he walks down the sidewalk..] "No one knows me...no one knows who I am." [He stops walking and turns facing the camera. The sun glistening on his dark blonde hair, his piercing baby blue eyes staring into the camera.]
"But that will all change, and everyone will know Sebastian Myerson and the legacy of the 'Trinity'"
[Myerson turns and continues walking down the sidewalk as the camera eagerly follows closely..]
'T'SM: "The days of the purists, of the true wrestler seem to be over." [Myerson stops short and pauses for a brief moment..] 'T'SM: "Or maybe just lying dormant, waiting for a new man to awaken it and bring a new legacy to this industry. Maybe it just needs a man who has what it takes.."
[Myerson makes a fist and puts it to his chest..] 'T'SM: "inside of him to battle it out day in and day out inside of the ring..and win or lose keep keep fighting and refuse to stay down.."
[Myerson once again begins to walk down the sidewalk..] 'T'SM: "In a world of cowards and men who would stab there own mother in the back to get to the top the true Warriors of the Ring have slowly died out.."
[Myerson merely shakes his head..] 'T'SM: "The men who's blood, sweat and tears soaked the mats night after night have turned into mere memories, that is..."
[Myerson stops and slowly turns around facing the camera, a look of utter determination etched on his face..]
'T'SM: "Until now. A new day is upon us, and in the end the Legacy of the
Trinity shall always.."
[Myerson stares intently into the camera..] 'T'SM: "Live on.." [Fades out....] [name withheld] Handles: "Trinity" [name withheld] Currently competing in - ?
