<RP> Vandal Gomez

8 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As GLOW's G-Cup goes on inside, we cut instead to the back, where we are witness to two of GLOW's security chasing after someone. One of them stops as they come upon a janitor cleaning up backstage]
Security #1: Did you see anyone run past here? Janitor: Um... yeah. Some weird looking dude. He looked sick or something. Really pale... Security #1: Which way did he go? Janitor: Out towards the staff parking complex, why? [The security doesn't even bother answering his question as they rush on in the direction of the parking complex. They pass door after door, and then finally arrive at a door marked "EXIT". As they plow through the door, a car pulls away quickly. One of the security attempts to give chase, but the other stops him]
Security #2 to #1: He's gone, man. Let's just go back inside and help the medics get Balial onto the stretcher.
[The security walks back to the doors, opens them, and walk in. Suddenly, the entire scene jerks to the left, as a gasp is heard from offscreen. When the scene is back to front and center, we see a dark, human outline residing in the shadows behind a production truck, no more than two feet in front of us. Then, a voice that sounds as if it came straight from the mouth of Vincent Price]
Voice: Don't touch the light on that camera. Don't scream. Just stand there. I will not think twice about ending your sad, lonely life if I hear even the slightest noise from you.
[The scene trembles a bit, but then levels out] Voice: Very good. You've got a smart head on your shoulders. [Suddenly, the outline shifts downwards, the head of it cocking to the side] Voice: Do you know who I am? [The scene moves to the left, then to the right... an obvious no. The outline shifts back up to a full vertical base]
Voice: Do you know what I did? [This time the scene moves up and down... a yes] Voice: And you're probably standing there, fearing me. Because of what I did to that man in there. That "holy" man. You have nothing to fear from me. As long as you do what you're told, you will be able to go home and live your life the same way you've lived it thusfar.
[An audible sigh of relief from off-camera] Voice: If only Balial had lived his life as simply as you. Instead, he had to take up this "holy crusade", drawing the ire of myself. And because of said crusade, he has been felled by my hand. Why did I do it? Why did I attack a man of "God"? Because I had to prove something to him. I had to open his eyes.
[The dark form begins to pace back and forth, fragments of light touching his pure white skin. The scene trembles a little, the constant movement apparently making the cameraman uneasy]
Voice: Balial, you believe so strongly. You sit there and believe in your false prophet. Your "god". Where was your God when my parents, both devout
Catholics, were "blessed" with my birth? Where was your God when my flesh seared a dark brown the first time I stepped into the sun? Where was your God when I breathed fire into your face, and tore open your flesh? Did his mighty hand stop me from torching your face? No. Will he stop me from destroying you, piece by piece, until you finally realize the err of your ways? No.
[The dark form stops pacing, and swivels around to face the camera] Voice: You keep believing in your false prophet. And I'll keep the torches lit for the next time I feel the need to take out MY vengenance on someone. Who's the true "god", Balial? Your false prophet, who does nothing to stop me? Or myself, who damns you to eternal suffering at my hands?
[The camera begins to tremble more, as the form's fist slowly extends out. As it makes it's way out of the shadows, the hand is revealed to be snow white.
The fist turns over, then opens. The inside of the hand is nothing but charred flesh, burns, and blisters. And in the middle of it all is a penny]
Voice: Penny for your thoughts. [The monotone of the voice seems to have gotten the best of the cameraman, who slowly but assuredly slumps down, the scene changing as well. And then a different voice, from behind the camera. A voice of utter fear]
Cameraman: Puh... please. Just let me go. [The hand turns back over, dropping the penny to the ground. The scene follows the penny as it plummets quickly to the ground, then strikes it, causing the metal on cement sound to reverb for several seconds. The scene does not move from the penny, as it just watches it, as it spins and sways to and fro before finally coming to rest on the cement. The scene moves back up, and the dark form is gone. And then the weeping begins. And the darkness settles. And you are left with nothing but questions with no answers. It is here that the scene ends. Until next time]
