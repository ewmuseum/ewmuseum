OK, Kent ... If you asked people to post a roleplay and try to determine who the best are ... then here's one of mine. It's a personal favorite. Yeah, it's long, but ehhh, whatareyougonnado? Here is where I make my case that Gunnar is pretty good! :-) Oh, and thanks, Trace, for citing Gunnar as number one. I appreciate it. Thanks also to the people who cited the Good Knights as the best tag team around. Mike, Eric, and anyone I forgot. :-) -Curt -------------------------------------------------------------------- [EWA] Mr. Gaines' Neighborhood released 17 September 1996 WARNING: PARENTAL ADVISORY: This is the type of thing that could upset small children or even scar them for life. I'm talking to YOU, Jimbo! ;-) Also, there is some foul language, as one would expect. U no like, I no care. Kapiche? --------------------------------------------------------------------------- [SCENE: We see a model of a neighborhood, and as the camera follows down the street, we hear this the jingling strains of this cute little song. It's the Mister Rogers' Neighborhood jingle.] [As it finishes the intro, and as the camera zooms in on a familiar loooking house, the camera switches to the inside of the house. We are looking at the inside of the front door. It opens, and in steps Gunnar "The Grizzly" Gaines, dressed in a shirt, tie, cardigan, slacks and shoes -- like Fred Rogers.] GUNNAR "GRIZZLY" GAINES: [singing] "It's a beautiful day in this neighborhood, A beautiful day for a neighbor. Would you be mine? Could you be mine?... [he takes off the cardigan, hangs it up in the closet] It's a neighborly day to beat Hollywood, He will no longer be a beauty. Would you be mine? Could you be mine?... [takes out sneakers, sits down, takes off dress shoes] I've always wanted to beat a wrestler just like you. I've always wanted to completely and totally destroy you. [puts on the sneakers] So, let's get this lovely match over with fast. Since you're a wussy I will kick your ass: Would you be mine? Could you be mine? Won't you be my victim? [gets up, walks over to closet] Won't you please, Won't you please? Please won't you be ... [tosses one dress shoe up, catches it] ... my victim? [puts the dress shoes away] Hi victim! And hi, neighbor! I'm Gunnar Gaines, filling in for Fred Rogers! [sits down on the couch by the front entryway] Mr. Rogers would be here, but it seems that he finally gave in to his desires and took a little trip to San Francisco with his friend, Mr. McFeely! Not that there's anything wrong with that. Can you say, GAY? [He grins] I KNEW you could! Super Scott has a friend named Stunning Steve who is gay. But we ALL know that Super Scott isn't gay, right boys and girls? However, we DO know he is a coward. Sometimes, I like to use more colorful terms to describe people like him and Hollywood. Can you say, LILY-LIVERED FART STAINS? [Another toothy, gleaming grin.] I KNEW you could! [Gunnar turns, and walks over to the trolley. He sits.] Have you ever sat down to make believe what would happen if SUPER SCOTT actually faced off against HOLLYWOOD in a match? Hmmmm? Let's pretend that they are going to do just that. What would that be like? Come here, trolley. [Toot, toot!] Well, trolley, it's now time to go somewhere that Super Scott and Hollywood visit quite frequently! It's the neighborhood of make-believe! [Toot, toot! Train music starts. The camera zooms in on the trolley as it enters the tunnel, and that circle closes on it, and then the circle opens up and we are looking at that castle, as the trolley comes out of another tunnel and rolls on by us.] [King Friday XIII comes out of the Castle. He's running, at least as close as a puppet can get to running. Queen Sara Saturday is chasing him holding a skillet.] KING FRIDAY XIII: Get away from me, bitch! All I said was I wanted to go watch wrestling at the Museum Go Round! [Queen Sara corners him and kongs him with the skillet.] QUEEN SARA: Take THAT, you worthless bum! Who wants to watch Holly Striped Tiger and Scottina Pussycat flail at each other and cower in the corner? Plus, you're just going there to diddle Lady Elaine Fairchilde! KING FRIDAY: Lady Elaine! You have GOT to be kidding me! She's ... well ... a bit "butch" for me, if you get my drift! Plus, that NOSE! It's so ... so ... phallic! I mean, it looks like a fucking PENIS! I mean, with a nose like THAT, and being the skank-infested dyke that she is, who needs a fucking DILDO? I bet it's been dipped MANY times into YOUR murky underworld, you skanky ho! Lady Elaine noses her way into you, sneezes, and WHAM! You're in heaven! QUEEN SARA: You jerk! [Kongs him with the skillet.] Now, get your ass down to the basement and get that paint. This dump hasn't been painted in the entire 30 years we've been on the air! You're PAINTING this castle, and then you're going to SERVICE me, or else I'm relying on BATTERY POWER for my needs from now on! KING FRIDAY: No WONDER I need to escape to see wrestling, you dumb bitch! [She kongs him with the skillet.] KING FRIDAY: OUCH! [Enter Mr. Handy Man. It's Ebeneezer "Geezer" Gaines, dressed in a mechanic's suit.] MR. HANDY MAN: What's the problem, you little whippersnappers? KING FRIDAY: SHE likes to sit on Lady Elaine's face, and she won't let me watch wrestling. QUEEN SARA: HE cheats on me with Lady Elaine, plus he won't paint the damn castle, plus he's a kinky idiot, ALWAYS insisting on wearing that crown to bed! What a loser! MR. HANDY MAN: Hahahahahahahahahahaha! Hahahahahahahahahahaha! Well, kids, it's simple. What if you BOTH watched wrestling, then took a "spin inside the Museum-Go-Round" with Lady Elaine afterwards ... together? KING and QUEEN: Yeah! THAT's a GREAT idea! KING FRIDAY: You've gotta wear those "come fuck me" go go boots to the card. Elaine will love it! She loves it when _I_ wear them, after all! QUEEN SARA: You think so? Well, I'll have to --- WHAT!?!? You've been wearing my clothes?! You BASTARD! [KING FRIDAY dodges another skillet whack, then runs past Queen Sara and runs into the castle. She chases him.] MR. HANDY MAN: Hahahahahahah! Oooooookay, I think I'll go visit the two wrestlers as they make their preparations for the big match later this week! [The camera now follows Mr. Handy Man as he walks over to the clock, where Holly Striped Tiger lives.] MR. HANDY MAN: Hello there, Holly, sonny boy! [Holly Striped Tiger comes out of the clock.] HOLLY STRIPED TIGER: Hi there Mr. Handy Man. I have a big match coming up later this week. Do you think I can win? MR. HANDY MAN: Gee, I don't know, sonny boy. It's against Scottina Pussycat, right? HOLLY STRIPED TIGER: Yeah. [looks down bashfully] I want to be ferocious, but I don't know if I can. MR. HANDY MAN: Trust me -- They don't call her Scottina PUSSYcat for nothing. HOLLY STRIPED TIGER: Really? MR. HANDY MAN: Yeah. HOLLY STRIPED TIGER: COOL! I'm just glad I don't have to wrestle Gunnar Gaines! Scottina Pussycat, maybe I can handle. I think I'll go talk to Cornhole S. Pecially to get some advice. MR. HANDY MAN: You do that, sonny boy! I'm gonna go see how Scottina is handling HER preparations! [Mr. Handy Man goes over to the tree, and is ready to knock on Scottina Pussycat's door, when out pops X the Owl from his tree.] X THE OWL: No! No! Don't do that! Scottina is awful jumpy this week! The EWA is coming to the neighborhood of make believe, and Super Scottina Pussycat has to wrestle Holly Striped Tiger. MR. HANDY MAN: Hahahahahahahaha! I heard! And how is Scottina doing? X THE OWL: Well, Scottina is pretty much scared shitless. She is afraid that Holly Striped Tiger might, well, you know ... BITE. MR. HANDY MAN: Hell, I think they BOTH bite! Hahahahahahahahahaha! X THE OWL: Shhhhh! Here she comes now! Be nice and try to encourage her! [Scottina's tree house door opens; out comes Scottina, cautiously.] MR. HANDY MAN: Scottina, bad news! You're not wrestling Holly Striped Tiger anymore. SCOTTINA PUSSYCAT: Meow meow GOOD news! Me meow meow pussy no WANT to wrestle! Me meow meow SCARED. MR. HANDY MAN: There's MORE! SCOTTINA PUSSYCAT: Meow? MR. HANDY MAN: INSTEAD you have to wrestle GUNNAR "THE GRIZZLY" GAINES! SCOTTINA PUSSYCAT: Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaiiiiiiiiieieeeeeeeeee! [Ducks back inside the tree house, and slams the door shut behind her.] MR. HANDY MAN: Hahahahahahahahahahahahaha! Hahahahahahahahahahahahaha! X THE OWL: That was incredibly cruel of you, Mr. Handy Man. I'm ashamed of you. It really depresses me to hear you talk like that. MR. HANDY MAN: Shove it up your tail feathers, bird brain! Hahahahahahahahaha! Hahahahahahahahaha! X THE OWL: I think that you should try to REACH OUT to Scottina and ENCOURAGE her. THEN, maybe SOMEDAY she CAN sign a match with Gunnar "The Grizzly" Gaines! MR. HANDY MAN: Ha! That little pussy will NEVER wrestle Gunnar! Not even in the land of make believe! She's too CHICKEN! X THE OWL: Just try to encourage her. Okay? MR. HANDY MAN: _Sure_. [Pats X the Owl on the head condescendingly.] [Mr. Handy Man knocks on Scottina's door ever so gently.] MR. HANDY MAN: [softly] Scottiiiiiiiina! Oh, Scottiiiiiiina! It's safe to come out! I was only teasing you! You don't REALLY have to fight Gunnar! [The door opens slowly just a crack.] SCOTTINA PUSSYCAT: [heard from within] Meow meow really? MR. HANDY MAN: Yes, Scottina. It is safe to come out, ya little whippersnapper! [The doors open wide and Scottina appears, shaking like a joy buzzer.] SCOTTINA PUSSYCAT: M-m-meow m-meow safe to come out now? Me not meow meow have to wrestle meow meow Gunnar or meow meow Holly Striped Tiger? MR. HANDY MAN: Actually, Scottina, sonny boy, you DO have to wrestle Holly! SCOTTINA: Aaiiiiiiiiiieeeee! I'm meow meow scared of qualified competition! Meow meow only like fighting jobbers! Meow meow not wanna fight Holly Striped Tiger meow. MR. HANDY MAN: You can do it. SCOTTINA PUSSYCAT: Meow meow really? MR. HANDY MAN: Yeah! When you get your neck broken you won't feel a thing! SCOTTINA PUSSYCAT: Aiiiiiieeeeee! [faints, and you see her sink out of sight] X THE OWL: Mr. Handy Man, you should be ashamed! Intentionally scaring a known coward like that! MR. HANDY MAN: Hahahahahahahahahahahahahahahaha! Hahahahahahahahahahahaha! [The trolley comes back out of a Tunnel, and we see Mr. Gaines standing next to the tunnel entrance, as we hear the train music.] Well, neighbor, can you understand the difference between REAL and MAKE-BELIEVE? You can? That's really something to be proud of! Right there, you're ALREADY smarter than Hollywood and Super Scott PUT TOGETHER! After all they MAKE BELIEVE that they can beat the Grizzly, and PRETEND that it's REALITY! See, if they only had to wrestle EACH OTHER, like they did in the neighborhood of make believe, they'd be LUCKY! But they AREN'T! See, Hollywood has to wrestle the BADDEST THANG RUNNING! And Scott will EVENTUALLY! And neither of them is going to like it! And ANOTHER difference! See, in the land of make believe, they each only suffered a little pain! But in REAL LIFE, they would suffer compound fractures, bruises, lacerations, and god knows what else! That's just what HAPPENS when you get in the ring with ME! [He gets up and walks over to the fish tank. It's empty.] Well, this is the part of the show where I'm supposed to feed the fish, but I made a few changes in the show. I took Fred's fish and fed them to the cat! So I guess, it's now time on this show to feed the cat! Here kitty! [A gray tabby walks up to Gunnar and jumps up onto the counter the tank sits on.] SCOTTINA: Meow! Meow! [pets the nice kitty] SCOTTINA: Meow! Meow! GUNNAR: Niiiiiice kitty. Niiiiiiice Scottina. SCOTTINA: Meow! Meow! [Scottina turns to look at the camea] SCOTTINA: Meow! Meow! GUNNAR: I named her after somene. Can you guess who? [another toothy grin] I KNEW you could! SCOTTINA: Meow! Meow! GUNNAR: You know, kids, Scottina here is something called NEUTERED. It means that the cat has no testicles. It tends to make the cat REALLY docile and cautious and cowardly. However, the cat STILL can, of course, make LOTS of harmless noise, as you can see. Can you say NEUTERED, boys and girls? [he smiles at the camera] I KNEW you could! [He reaches into a drawer, pulls out a packet of Tender Vittles, and dumps them into a saucer on the counter. The cat buries his head in the saucer, feverishly munchin'. Gunnar pets the cat.] Niiiiiiiice Scottina. Niiiiiiiice Scottina. Good cat. [He looks at the camera.] I named the cat after someone we all know named Super Scott, because, like the cat SUPER SCOTT makes a lot of worthless noise, and has no balls, either! Can you say EUNUCH? [Mr. Gaines grins.] I KNEW you could! I have another question for you. Can you say "Yes, I accept a match with Gunnar Gaines?" You CAN? WOW! You can ALREADY say something than Super Scott CAN'T, and HE's an alleged grown-up! Good for you, neighbor! [He walks over and sits on the couch by the front door.] Well, neighbor, the time for our visit is now over. We had a lot of important things to talk about! Super Scott's status as one of the gender-challenged ... Hollywood's hopeless inability to tell fantasy from reality ... and my impending victory over the latter! I'd beat Scottie, too, but he's too chicken to fight me! So I guess this song is JUST for Hollywood, as HE is the ONLY one who will even fight me. [Gunnar sings, as the music for "It's Such a Good Feeling" starts up.] It's such a bad feeling to punched in the eye. It's such a crappy feeling: You're broken inside. And when I wake up ready to say, "I think I'll break a snappy ... [snaps fingers twice] ... new bone or two." [music stops] OFFSTAGE VOICE: HEY! THAT didn't rhyme! GUNNAR: Who the hell CARES!? I break bones! That's what I do! That's what I will do to Hollywood if he's not careful! OR, to Super Scott! OR, to you! OFFSTAGE VOICE: Fine! Fine, Mr. Gaines! [music continues, he finishes the song] It's such a good feeling, a very good feeling. The feeling you know that ... I'll be back, when the day is new. And I'd like to, break you in two. I'll have things to tell Holly and Super Scott: "Hey, fuck you!" [a knock at the back door] Well, my new shipment of wood grain alcohol has arrived! I need to go get that, but first, a little reminder to a couple of my neighbors! Hollywood, Super Scott ... [the soft voice stops, and we hear the gruff voice we normally associate with Gunnar Gaines ... ] BEAT _ME_ -- IF YOU CAN! [he walks out the back door, and we see the Mister Gaines' Neighborhood logo superimposed as the picture switches to an overhead shot of a model of the neighborhood] [the picture goes to black as the tune ends]
