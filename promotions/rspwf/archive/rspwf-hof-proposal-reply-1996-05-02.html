In article < [detail withheld] >,  [name withheld] < [detail withheld] > wrote: >Jacob Kane wrote: > >> I think it is about time RSPWF had a Hall of Fame to celebrate and remember >> the great players, fedheads, characters and general contributions of RSPWF, >> so that in ten years time, there will be an anchor to what we have now. >> >> ... >> >> How do you get on the HoFaM? >> >> Well, you can`t nominate yourself. You have to be nominated by someone >> else, and then seconded, and also have at least another five people >> supporting you with reasons why. I`d like these proposals on the group, >> so people can see them and either contest or support them. This is to >> make sure everything is open. Then we can see from peoples judgements >> whether they should be accepted in. > >So basically it takes seven votes to nominate somebody? Aren't you >worried that this is biased towards current players/characters/whatever? >I mean, there are lots of people who have heard of "Bad Boy" Mark >Bagwell, but I doubt you will find seven people who remember the greats >of the EMWA, WeWF or UeWF. > >E-Wrestling is notoriously short-memoried, and I am concerned that the >proposed system would be biased towards newcomers. Wouldn't it be >a shame if the HoFaM consisted entirely of players and characters who >have only been around for six months or less, while many of the all-time >greats were simply forgotten? > >I think the voting system should be set up so that the all-time greats are >the ones who make it to the Hall. Baseball does this by requiring >nominees to have been retired for five years. Maybe the e-wrestling >HoFaM should have a similar rule? Although, the length of time should be >less than five years (since e-wrestling has only been around for five and >half), and it should be short enough that people are still on the net when >they are inducted, so they can enjoy it (or at least know about it). > >I propose that nominees be required to have been active in e-wrestling >for at least two years (not two years since they retired, but two years >since they began). This would mean that only established players >or characters whould make it, hot rookies would have to wait. Of course, >this rule would not apply to nominees for the "Memorial" section of the >HoFaM. > >> There will be a three week window to open debate etc. and make decisions. I >> will be forming a consultancy body to make a final decision. If you think >> you should be a part of the HoFaM exec, e-mail me with reasons why. > >I think that this committee should be composed of people who have been >around for several years and seen a lot of e-wrestling (and thus are >qualified to know who the all-time greats really are). I would suggest >[name withheld], [name withheld], the EWF guys (Josh and Nat), [name withheld], [name withheld]... and myself :-) > > > [name withheld] ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ I totally agree with everything you say. If you`re willing to do it, I`m more than willing to let you do it.
