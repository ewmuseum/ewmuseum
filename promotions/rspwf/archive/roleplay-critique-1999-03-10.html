roleplay critique

10 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[_ Outlaw _] [ The man you love to hate ,and Hate to love... ] _ Scene _ A Dark stormy Night In Monroe,Louisiana......The Rain is not dripping slowly,it is pouring out to the ground... Lighting is seen in the background looking as if it is gonna crash into the ground. Most people are in bed not just cause of the weather, but because of the time...
We see a graveyard with the Word's "Outlaw is here" on it...... We get closer and can even hear the song Outlaw Playing... A cap that say's "Real Mother
Fucker" Lies on the Grave. Then a Man wearing Baggy Millitary Pants, Black t - shirt which say's "Fuck off", combat Boot's and a Gold Necklace walks into the
Picture...He Kneel's down by the grave and Pick's up some of the soft dirt....The dirt slowly drift's out of his hands and then the Man raises his face.....The man is Outlaw and He has a slight grin on his face... He peaks back toward's the grave, and shakes his head as if he agree's with the
Tombstone.... Time : After midnight on a dark stormy Night Location : A local Monroe City Gravesite.. Reason's for being here : Unknown as of now. [_Outlaw_] See I am here, The stone does not lie, but why I am here ? That is a more approiate question......
[ Outlaw turn's his head from the Tombstone, to the opposite direction....In which only a Single tree is located ]
[_Outlaw_] So, Jason Nyghtstorm this is the Kind of Life you live eh ? Graves,dead people,bad weather and so much more. You are a man from the darkside.. Who Was raised in Hell and now haunt's the earth... Do you want to
Give my soul to the devil ? Or make my life a living hell ? Which one is it Big
Boy ? All you Dark People are alike.. What is your execuse for being dark
Nyghtstorm ? Devil molested your mom ? God sent a lighting Bolt after one of your Friends ? There is always some crazy story. Come on let me hear it, I mean
We Fight Saturday Night,So I would like any background I can Possibly get on you. Don't be afraid, come out and Play with The Outlaw.... I haven't Heard from you..... Does the Devil got your toungue ?
[ Outlaw reaches behind him and grab's an old type shovel and stand's to his feet...There is an evilish smirk still on his face ]
[_Outlaw_] So big Boy you want to make my life a living hell....To late my
Life already is... I mean everyday I get challenged and Life get's tougher never easier.Hell ain't nothing to me.I am used to it... What's left to do ?
[ Outlaw scratches his head, then Put's the shovel and into the ground, and start's to dig ]
[_Outlaw_] Maybe you can Bury me and put some sort of curse on me where I can never rest in piece. Here I will help you out and make your job that much easier .
[ Outlaw say's that very sarcaistly, After a big of digging just to make his point, Outlaw throw's the shovel over to the right of the grave and set's down with his back leaned toward's the grave. The song Outlaw is still being played faintly from somewhere in the area ]
[_Outlaw_] If you haven't already Noticed, I am making fun of your life style
Nyghtstorm... There is No body on earth who is from hell. I think you and your
Little Gimmick is An outrage to the soecity of life and to the wrestling world.. What kinda person are you really Nyghtstorm ? See, I am setting here in the dark and there is a storm going on..... But Am I from hell ? No, I might be a Person who many wish would go to hell, but I ain't there yet... Just open ya eye's Nyghtstorm....This is _Real_..... This aint some fake ass movie coming on late where the Dark mother fucker kill's alot of people and Finally a big breasted Whore end's up killing him. This is real and if you don't be careful A big mother fucker from Louisiana might end up being the one to kill you, Instead of some Chick'. I won't make it short and sweet, I can promise you that as God as my witness....
[ Pauses ] [_Outlaw_] Wait he probally mean's Nothing to you as is, Well Put it this way, your death is a guarantee and it won't be a pretty one...... By the way don't try and give me the Bull shit about you already being dead.....We both know that is far from the actual truth.
[ The Rain begin's to pour Harder than before and the Smile On Outlaw's face just get's Bigger and Bigger ]
[_Outlaw_] Some would say I am crazy' ,To those some I would have to say _There right_..... It is pouring down rain and I am standing out here just to prove a point or show my dertemination.... I don't know why A sane man would do this, cause Truthfuly All I have to do is show up Saturday Night and Fight you in the ring. None of this is nessecary, Yet I still do it. How Ironic.. I must want this World Title really bad, or is It Just I want to show the world I will
Honestly do "Whatever means are nessecary" to reach the top. Many say that, but very few mean it.
[ Outlaw bend's down and searches under the ground till he finds the Cd Player. He turn's the song off and lays the Cd player on top of the Grave along with the cap ] [_Outlaw_] It is all here waiting Nyghtstorm... My grave is half -dug, My name is on the tombstone, My music is even playing..... All you have to do is put me away and if you are trully from the darkside, I should not be much of a challenge. Time will tell if you are what you say that you are.... You have alot to prove, but just remember if you prove it, you will also get the Wwa
World Title shot.... How Interesting my man...
_Quote_ _The_ _Outlaw_ _No_ _More_ [ Fades ]
