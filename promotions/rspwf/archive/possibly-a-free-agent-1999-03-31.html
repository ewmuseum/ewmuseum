Well, I'm currently wrestling in only one fed right now, with my true pro career in a local indy league taking up my time, but I do have a few hours a week I could use to RP for another fed.
This is one of the gimmicks I TRULY wrestle as in this local indy fed. So I know how to play him really well. He is also the absolute funniest gimmick I have ever seen in all my life, in e-wrestling or pro, so on with some info
Name: Apu Nahedaposa Penapedalon Hometown: The Promised Land of Pakistan Height: 5'10" Weight: 201 Pounds Finishers: The Green Card (Reverse Russian Legsweep) and The Emigration Papers
(He picks them up into a dominator position, hooks his arm around their chin and drops them down into a DDT.)
Background: Came to the United States several years ago because he heard it was a very profitable market to open a corner store. He claims no one is as hardcore as he is, because it's impossible to be more hardcore than a Corner
Store worker who is contstanly being shot at.
Looks: Wears a silver mask, silver baggy pants and white boots. The silver pants have Apu down both legs in gold.
Any other info you want to know, send me an app or somethin and just ask me.
