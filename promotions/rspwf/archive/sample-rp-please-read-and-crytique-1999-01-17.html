This is the third time I have tried to post this, I don't think its working, but oh well. If its on there a lot of times, my bad, but I do need some comments. I thinks its a lil too long.
[Fade In] [The scene fades into the outside of a bar, on a beach. The sun is shining down on the people around, and then we see TJ Master sitting outside on a chair, there are many people around him, he is just lying there talking to people. We notice that he is wearing a pair of Mecca carpenter shorts, no shirt, and a blue bandanna tied around his head, and a pair of Gargoyles sunglasses]
TJ: Damn, it just don't get much better then this. Sittin' out here, surrounded by my fans, and comin' up on my second match, excuse me, second win in the LAW. My life is good, oh yes, I do rule the world. Just ask anyone around here, they'll tell you how it is. Now, I'm just takin' some time off,
I've been trainin' for this match, I've been bustin' my tail everyday, and now, now I'm going to take some time off, kick back and relax. Sometimes ya just need to kick back and unwind ya know what I'm sayin'? So that's why I'm down here in the Virgin Islands for, there just ain't no better place to come and unwind.
TJ: But now, its time to get down to a lil bit of business, I need to talk about good ol' Judas Lasher and my upcoming match against him. Judas.....wait, what kind of name is Judas anyway? OK, well, here in some days or somethin', hell whenever our match is, you're going to have to come into the ring and fight The Great One. How does it feel to have that opportunity Judas?
Hopefully you don't like it too much, cause its not going to go the way that you think it is going to go, or the way that you want it to go. See, there ain't but one thing I know to do in that ring, and that is win, cause that's all I've ever done Judas. And I'm not about to change my winning ways for someone like you, because I know I can beat you, and after our match, you're going to know that you can't beat me Judas. See, right now I know you're thinkin' that I'm all talk, well it just ain't that way Judas. I talk a big game, but I don't talk no stuff if I can't back it up, Judas you bout to get lit up my man. I'm gonna drag you around that ring and light you up like a
Christmas tree, then Bomb you, pin you for the one, two, three and walk away with my second LAW win of my career. Get ready for me Judas, cause everything
I say, its gonna happen to you whether you want it to or not, so you might as well get used to it. Judas, it may be a good match, because I really don't know what kind of athlete you are, so maybe you do have enough skills to make this match close, but if you don't, well then you're going to get humiliated just like that last mark I fought did, this ain't no game. I come to win, and
I stop at nothing to get that win, if that means stretching or breakin' the rules, oh well, I've done it before.
TJ: I did have a chance to catch your interview Judas, its about time you decided to say somethin'. Even though a lot of the stuff you said is a bunch of crap that you're just sayin' to look good, atleast you did talk, that's a step in the right direction atleast. You say you're one of the most miserable people I've ever wrestled? Well, that's not too good Judas, cause I'm going to make that a lot worse when you and I get into the ring. All that stuff you got built up inside you makin' you feel bad right now, is only going to get worse as our match progresses, and you're going to wish that you hadn't fought me, cause I'm going to kick your life even deeper into the gutter then it already is mark. But that's the price you have to pay when you fight someone like me, you should have known that before you signed that contract to fight me, now, ain't nothin' you can do but take the beatin' I'm gonna lay on you, and then lose the match Judas, that's it! Claimin' to be a thug? I don't claim to be nothin', I was born and raised out on the toughest streets in this world, and
I'm the way I am because of that, and I'm still out there in it. You say you hate people like me? Well, don't classify me with every other thug you know cause you don't know nothin' about me Judas, I can't be responsible for what ever black man does, I can only be responsible for what I do, and I'm tellin'
you right now, you're going to be fully responsible for what I do to you, because your big mouth is gettin' you in a lot of trouble right now mark. You need the money? Nice story, have fun tryin' to make it on the losers pay the
Judas, cause that's the only check ol' Barnes is gonna hand you after this match.
TJ: You may have grown up in a ghetto, but you don't know nothin' about where
I'm from, and don't even try to come at me like you do. You don't care where
I'm from? Then quit bringin' it up! You need money? Sounds to me like you need some bug spray up in your nasty ass house if you got bugs and stuff in your bathtub and all that. But ya know what? That's your problem!! Don't no one out here care about what you have in your home environment aight? You say you don't give a damn about where I'm from, I damn sure don't give a damn about your bug problems! The LAW has given you a slap in the face? Well, you're bound to get a lot more then that when you come to fight me, and in the end, when all is said and done, the only thing you're gonna leave that ring with is a loss on your record. Plain and simple!
TJ: [Looks up at the women standing around him] And as for the joke about my face Judas. Well, I want you to take a look around me, and then think real hard. Would they be around me if I looked like you? Nah, they wouldn't Judas, that's why I'm The Real Deal, and you're just another jabroni that's about to get beat by me. Ya know Judas, you kinda seem like you got some real mental issues. Last time I heard someone talkin' bout your face looks like a hamburger was back in grade school, so umm...buddy how bout you act your age now eh? You don't want me near you? Don't worry, cause after I'm done with you, you won't even know I was ever there. The Bronx Bomb will knock you so silly you won't know what happened. And yeah, I will get me a title. And you tell me to go to Toys R Us and be one? Well, I gots a question Judas, how did you know Toys R Us sold em? Hmm? Have a few yourself? I wouldn't doubt it, people like you have to get respect any way they can, even if it means pretending they're something good. Real scary threat Judas you just can't stop when you get going? Don't worry, you won't ever get to that point.
TJ: Judas, there ain't too much more to say to you. But there is just this one last thing I have to say.........
Judas when you step into the ring with me............ You find out why....... I'm_SIMPLY_PERFECT_!!!! [TJ smiles, and looks up at one of the women standing around him, she walks around and sits down in his lap, and well...you figure out the rest]
[Fade Out]
