(RP CRITIQUE) "The Mysterious One"

11 November 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(A camera is fading into a dark alley. All street lights are off, but then, a motor is heard as car rolls pass with it's bright lights. The car passes slowly as the cameraman starts to walk a little closer to the alley. Suddenly, the light on the camera is turned on and there is now light in the alley as the cameraman slowly walks into the alley. The cameraman now looks around the area, and he realizes that he's in the center of the alley. Buildings surround the cameraman and a sudden "POP!" is heard behind the cameraman. The cameraman quickly turns around, still holding onto the camera tightly. And in the face of the cameraman is the Mysterious One. The Mysterious One has a mean grimace on his face as the cameraman jumps. The light's brightness now gets toned down a little as the Mysterious One begins to speak in a mellow-toned voice)
The Mysterious One: I see that you've made it without any trouble. That is good for you. You know why you are here, don't you?
(The Mysterious One looks at the cameraman with no emotion. The cameraman hesitates, but finally gets his words out)
Cameraman: Well, not really, sir. Would you be kind enough to tell me? (The Mysterious One looks at the cameraman with a confused look on his face. His face now quickly returns to normal as he speaks in the same toned voice as before) The Mysterious One: What's up with this proper mess you're saying ... "sir" and "would you be kind enough to tell me?" The reason you're here is because I have to comment on Heaven.
(The cameraman begins to speak as the Mysterious One finishes his comments) Cameraman: Oh yes! That's right, I remember n... (The Mysterious One cuts the cameraman and he begins to speak in the same calm voice) The Mysterious One: Now Heaven, you come out here ... thinking that you're a big shot. Well, let me tell you something, Heaven ... the reason you have beaten me this time is because I wasn't concentrating fully on this match, but at Extinction Level '99, Heaven ... you will find out that I am one man you don't want to be in the same ring with.
(The Mysterious One suddenly stops and looks up at something in the air. He now walks over to a steel, black ladder. The Mysterious One slowly grabs the second ladder rung and pulls himself up by each of the rungs followed. He now reaches the top and jumps over a rail onto a porch that is attached to the back of a building. The Mysterious One now looks down at the cameraman. The audio on the camera is now toned up as the Mysterious One speaks in his calm voice)
The Mysterious One: Heaven, I am going to knock you down in the center of the ring. Then, I am going to climb up the steel cage, slowly ... and as I reach the top, I'm going to look down at you, next ... I'm going to jump high into the air ... and last, BOOM! I am going to hit you with the big splash. Now,
Heaven ... you may think that this is a joke, but let me tell you something
..... this isn't a joke. This is serious business!
(The Mysterious One now slowly looks at the view from the porch. He now looks up at something in the sky. The cameraman changes it's focus to the moon. The
Mysterious One is looking at the moon. The cameraman now changes it focus back to the Mysterious One. He is still looking at the moon as he speaks in the same mellow voice)
The Mysterious One: The time is near, Heaven. It will soon be time for your life to be ended by me. Heaven ... you had the chance to have me as your stablemate, your partner, your friend, but then you become stupid and turn your back on me. Well, at Extinction Level '99, you will feel the pain that you would have never gotten if I was still your partner. At Extinction Level '99,
Heaven ... you are going to be the victim of two of the most terrifying moves in sports entertainment today. Heaven, when you feel the pain and agony of the
Mysterious Bomb and the "Lights Out," you will hope that you stayed on my team.
(The Mysterious One slowly turns his head and looks at the camera. He now begins to speak in his usual voice)
The Mysterious One: You're going to be a bloody mess after I'm done with you because when I ram your face into the steel, you will then realize that you need to be sorry for all you have done. You see, the first and only sin that you are blamed for is turning your back on me. Why did you do something as stupid as that? I just don't understand. And at Extinction Level '99, Heaven
... my frustrations will all be put on you. Heaven, it's now time for you to wake up and smell your flesh burning into coil. It is time for you to realize what you have done ... and once you realize it, Heaven ... ask yourself ... did you make the right choice?
(The camera focus increases and now, you can see a good look of the Mysterious
One's face. His eyes are beginning to turn red, and he has an angry look on his face. He now begins to speak in a deep, angry voice)
The Mysterious One: I don't think so! Heaven, your final days are beginning to count down. What should you do, Heaven? Run away like a little coward or stay and get crushed anyway. Heaven, there is one last thing for me to say to you .... You've Been Warned!
(The camera's focus reduces to it's regular camera angle. The Mysterious One now turns his back and leans on the rail with his elbows. The light on the camera is now turned off as the camera fades to black)
