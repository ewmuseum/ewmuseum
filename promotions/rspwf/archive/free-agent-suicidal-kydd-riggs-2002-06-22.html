[Free Agent] -["Suicidal" Kydd Riggs]-

22 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is an old RP of mine.. I haven't RPed in some time.. And this RP isn't all to great. But I can't find a better one than this..
...Fades in... [ Scene ] [...The scene slowly fades into a bright blue, spring sky. But one puffy white cloud, rested on the sea colored sky. A short, yet brisk wind blows, stirring the tree tops and the leaves that have been left on the ground over the long cold winter. It soon dies down, once again letting all rest. A single leaf however is caught in the updraft, and the camera follows it as it slowly drifts back down to earth. As the camera levels off, and the leaf finally hits it's destination, you see the view of a large, wooden paneled house, nestled away behind a line of trees, at the end of a rocky driveway. A small wooden porch leads to the front door, which to either side has a set of windows from a room inside this house. On the roof there is a large panel of glass where a sunroof has been added. Meanwhile, in the driveway, there is a brand new, red 2003 Triple 500 Dodge Viper GT. Custom red and silver paint and wing. Parked next to it, is also a brand new 2003 Pontiac Grand Am. Off to the right, there is a small paved area, with a basketball hoop in place. White lanes have been pitifully painted on the court, along with a small amount of graffiti. The camera slowly approaches the porch, when suddenly the front door swings open, followed by the screen door to the porch. Out steps a man dressed in a white New York Giants away jersey, a pair of tan Tommy pants, red and black Lugz, and a black bandana on his head. He steps out onto the porch, letting the screen door swing shut behind him. He smiles and steps out a bit from the shadows cause by the sun hitting the other side of the house, you see it is new TsC star "Suicidal" Kydd Riggs. He leans down a bit, and takes a seat on the top step of the porch. For a short moment, all is silent, other than a few birds chirping and a small gust of wind here and there. But finally, with a short breath, Riggs finally begins to speak...]
[ Riggs ] "Didn't take you damn TsC camera men long do find me, eh? [chuckles] Must have been a little eager to hear from me, as I've been here a week and said nothing. Although I did partake in my first match where I slaughtered a couple of TsC wannabe's. And now that I finally have an important match, you figured you'd take it upon yourself to get a word from the so-far mysterious, yet well known Kydd Riggs. And I say, that's fine. I think it's about time to express myself. But I'm going to warn you and the rest of the TsC, you are in for something new. Something unknown. And something extremely obscene. Because now Kydd Riggs is in the house damn it. And I'm here to take the f*ck over! [laughs] No but seriously.. Well, yeah I am here to run things. But that's obvious of course. Because for all of you that know
Kydd Riggs, you know failure is not an option. And something I actually have yet to ever experience in my time. Seems like wherever I go, I am the top dog.. I was the man that mad and ended the NCW. I was the man plain and simple over in the EhWa. In one year I did more than most did in their careers. And if not for a fall out with their pathetic excuse for a president,
I'd still be running things. But I figured as long as the offer was on the table, I'd come here and show you all what a real man is. And give you a little lesson in wrestling. I mean, I'm Kydd Riggs man. An untouchable.
[smiles] I mean come on, I damn near have one hundred matches in my career, matched up with thirteen loses. Thirteen! Some of which should not even be counted, because they were tag team matches where a weak ass partner lost! Now that's something to talk about. Witch is why I was brought here. I am a ratings getter man! I mean, what would the world be without Kydd Riggs?
[shutters] I'd be afraid to know. [laughs] Now my second task here in the
TsC is a rather appealing one. I have a first round match for the Omega championship, and a spot in the hall of fame! I mean, I could start out my TsC career in the hall of fame! Imagine that. All I have to do is go through a few shcmo's. Starting with a man who claims he's 'the devil himself'.
Sebastian Creed is the name.. [laughs] The Devil Himself, eh? You got the numbers six, six, six on your forehead? You live in hell? Thrive in flames?
Take in the evil souls of the world? You're a killer? You have little horns on your head? Is your skin red? What the hell makes you think you're the devil himself? [chuckles] Let me guess, you're just another poser who thinks he's bad ass. You gave yourself that nickname, to make you feel cool. But the fact is, you're just a p*ssy wannabe. And you figured that name might scare a few people. Here's a news flash for ya' punk. I am the closest thing to a devil around here! Because when you step into that ring with me, it's like walking right into the fires of hell. Where I can torture your sorry ass into submission. [laughs] Unlike you boy, I didn't make up some cheesy nickname.
I ain't no god damn gimmick. I was given this name by the people who faced me. I am called suicidal because I'll do every damn thing I can to win! At all costs, with no regard for my own body, let alone my opponents! So don't walk out here, trying to act all big, bad and scary, Creed. Because honestly, you aren't even scaring the seven year old kids who watch the TsC. They look at you and laugh. And in the ring, you perform like those mid-eighties wrestling buddies, kids used to beat around for fun.."
[ Riggs ] "Fact is boy, you ain't meet no one like me, you ain't seen no one like me. And when the match is over, you're going to realize you'll never see someone like me again. You can think whatever you want. Say whatever you want. Think you're hard, but when match time comes around, you're nothing more than my b*tch. Plain and simple. [smiles] Now, where else can I really go? I mean honestly, I'll openly admit, I don't know you. All I know if your goofy name, and pathetic gimmick that you choose to follow. I've not seen you, I've not heard you.. And I don't really plan to. At least not for quite some time. You hang out and hide in the shadows, waiting for me to pop out. Waiting for me to make the first move. [laughs] What a mistake that is.
Thing is junior, you don't scare me with your silence. You just prove to me how much of a stinky, fish smelling p*ssy you really are. Words can't even explain that. But come the Reincarnation of Devastation tournament, I shall be able to prove that, when I smack you around in a barbed-wire fence match. Ten feet high, every which way you look. Surrounded by barbed-wire. _No_ way out.
[sadistic laugh] Now that's what I'm talking about. Truly my forte'. Blood, sweat and more blood. This is my game. This is where you see exactly what
Kydd Riggs is all about. You think those walls are really going to stay up though? Really going to hold strong? How many times do you think I can toss your ass into the fence, before it walls down? Before I wrap you in it, while
I beat you with some more? I guess only time will tell.."
[ Scene ] [...Riggs pulls his shoulders back and you hear a few cracks as he does so. He then reaches his arms up toward the sky and lets out a yawn as he stretches. He places his hands back on his lap, and continues to speak...]
[ Riggs ] "What some people fail to understand, is how important this first round match really is. For, as the saying goes, you've got to be in it, to win it. And if I was to fail that fast, what would I be? What could I possibly do? No, no.. I would much rather win, and I would love nothing more than to get my name into the TsC hall of fame, as well as become Omega champion. I mean, as a man who just recently came into the TsC, where better to start off, than.. On top? [chuckles] The only thing standing in my way to that goal, at this moment, is Creed. The TsC Colossal champion. Although you could have fooled me. Seems more like a paper champion. [laughs] A man who hides out and waits. Pretends to be bad once a month, wrestles every two weeks, and walks around with title? Where did that ruling come from? Creed, you're lucky that title isn't on the line right now. Or that'd be mine as well. But like you, you just want to hide it away and save it for another day.
Easy to hold onto titles when you never defend them, right? [laughs] How pathetic. But no worry, as soon as I pin you to the mat for a one, two, three.
I'll prove to the world not only how great I, not only will I soon be a hall of fame inductee and Omega Champion, that I should also be Colossal champion...
And I say, where would one go from there? The world title would be the next step. Sure, I am looking a bit into the future, but the ways things are going and seem to be panning out. There aren't to far-fetched really. Just gotta take it one step at a time. And as a man of few mistakes, I don't see all that much in front of me, that's going to cause to many problems, if even one at all. [smiles] Creed, you just hang out back in the shadows. Hide away. Soon enough you're going to have to crawl out of your hole and be a man. There will be no running, and I will expose you for the coward and waste of human life you truly are..."
[ Scene ] [...Riggs once again stretches toward the bright blue sky, before slowly lifting himself up from the sitting position. He takes in a long, deep breath, and lets out an equally long exhale, before he turns back up the steps, opens the screen door, as well as the front door. He takes a step in and lets the screen door swing shut behind him, before he pushed the front door closed as well. It closes with a click. And with that, you are left in the quiet out doors again. A small breeze and nothing more. The camera once again pans back to the clear sky, before the scene begins to slowly dim...]
...Fades out...
