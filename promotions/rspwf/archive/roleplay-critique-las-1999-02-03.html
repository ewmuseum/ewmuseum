(Roleplay) Critique [Las]

3 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene: A local park in Phoenix, Arizona. You see kids having fun all around you. The camera starts panning left, then in one quit turn, stops and the camera shows a man sitting on a bench. His arms are sprawled over the bench's back; he is wearing a black t-shirt that reads "I'm Too Damn Lethal" on the front in baby blue writing, a pair of Hilfiger blue jeans, and a pair of black
Lugz. In each ear, he has two small silver hoops; and around his neck hangs a silver cross. His hair is faded, and looks to be bleeched blonde. He then begins to speak.]
Man: Well, well, well... The IMWA has found "The Lethal Injection" Darren
Scott, and I bet you guys want some comments or something, right?
[The camera moves up and down as if saying "yes".] DS: Well ok... let me first start off by introducing myself. I am the one and only, "The Lethal Injection" Darren Scott. I've been everywhere from the
WCWA... right down the the INWI. But everywhere I've gone... it's been the same story. "Darren, I'm sorry but you're just not ready to be in the ring yet.
Maybe after a few months, or maybe years of training... you can come back and we'll see what happens." Well, I'll tell you all one thing... I did train. I worked my ass off, and I for damnsure ain't going back to the promotions that fired me. I for damn sure ain't going back to the promotions that didn't believe in me. But I will come to the IMWA, where Ian Walsh has treated me like a man, like a human being. He saw that I had talent inside of me, when I was wrestling in the IMWA Training Facility. When he contacted me about wrestling for this great federation, I jumped at the oppurtunity.
[He adjusts his t-shirt and begins to talk some more.] DS: Now, as for my first match this Saturday... in Topeka, Kansas, which by the way... y'all better be ready to rock the house because IMWA's first card is going to be pumpin'! Ok... now as I was sayin'... Dave Hamrick. I've known you to be a great wrestler. I've watched your matches and I know your strengths, and your weaknesses. You better believe this, I am not going to come into the
IMWA Arena, and walk out a loser. I plan to debut in one of my greatest matches. As for you, I hope you know where home is because that's where you're headed after I get through with you!
[He flashes a devilish grin, and then resumes talking.] DS: I know you're not a mat wrestler, and I know you're not a high flyer. But I am. I can also brawl, perform some martial arts, and anything else you can do.
But I can do it twice as good as you can. The only thing I have to do, is watch out for that submisson move of yours. Lucky for me, I know nine tenths of every submission hold, and how to get out of them. So, you can pretty much say you're screwed. Come this Saturday Dave... you'll find out why
__I'M__ __TOO__ __DAMN__ __LETHAL__
__AND__ __YOU__ __CAN__ __BANK__ __ON__ _THAT__!!! [He stands up and walks away. On the back of his shirt, you see "..and you can bank on that!" in baby blue writing. The screen fades out.]
-Las [Handles:] (___Scotty Drake - L.A.W | 2 - 1 - 0 | *L.A.W Pacific Coast Champion___) (___"The Lethal Injection" Darren Scott - IMWA & WCWA | 0 - 0 - 0 |___) "Big names don't make a fed; a fed makes the big names." -Myself, 1/24/99
