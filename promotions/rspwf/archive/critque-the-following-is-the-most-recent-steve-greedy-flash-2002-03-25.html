[Critque] The following is the most recent Steve Greedy Flash

25 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Fade In ] [ Standing at the door of someone's lovely home is IWF/WOW Interviewer Anthony
Edwards, Behind him is a small crew of IWF/WOW workers which consist of sound people and camera men. Edwards knocks on the door, and we await an answer.]
Voice : " << From behind them >> If your looking for Mr Greedy he is prolly at the bar around back."
[ Edwards and company turn around to see a beautiful Blonde in a bikini, whose smile makes you want to call her your own. She walks by the guys, and enters the house.]
Lady : " Are you guys coming " [ Smiling she keeps on going..The IWF/WOW Crew follows her threw the huge house, Until finally they are led back outside to a large outdoor bar with a roof over it, and a swimming pool with many beautiful girls laying beside it in chairs or in the pool relaxing on floats.]
Lady : " Here you guys go " [ She points to Steve Greedy who is setting at the end of the bar with a gorgeous red head in a short skirt. She then runs over towards the pool, and jumps in causing the girls to giggle and splash her with water.]
Anthony Edwards : " << Clears his throat >> Well, This way guys " [ Edwards takes one last look at the ladies in the pool, then focuses in on
Greedy. They walk towards him, and finally come in eye contract with Steve
Greedy. Greedy shakes his head, and whispers into the girls ear. She then walks by the IWF/WOW crew with a sad look on her face and exits our view.]
Anthony Edwards : " I hope were not disturbing you Steve, But you did say we could come out here."
[ Steve nods, and takes another sip of his mixed drink infront of him. Steve then positions himself on his bar stool where he is facing the camera. He motions for Anthony to sit down beside him, and Edwards does so. Edwards dressed up for the interview a little bit as he is in a fairly nice suit, but his Suit comes nowhere near the quality of the one Greedy is wearing, As we can tell now since there sitting by each other.]
Anthony Edwards : " Thanks for letting us come out here Steve, It is a privledge for us to be invited out here for an Interview to your actual home, considering this is the first time we have ever actually spoke."
[ Greedy smiles and puts his hand on Edwards shoulder gently. He looks away from the camera, and right in the direction of Edwards.]
Steve Greedy : " Don't consider it a privledge, I don't really want you here, and the only reason you are here is the IWF/WOW ordered me to do an "Offical"
Interview and I didn't feel like leaving Little Rock, Arkansas quite yet. I have to leave shortly to get back on the road, but I am trying to enjoy every moment possible while I have the chance."
[ Greedy takes his arm off of the shoulder of Anthony Edwards, and looks back to the camera with that trademark cocky smirk on his face.]
Anthony Edwards : " I see, Well if thats the case lets get right down to business. Since this is the first time we have ever spoke, Lets start with the basics. What do you think about your time so far in the IWF/WOW ? "
Steve Greedy : " I think I have been cheated, Used and took advantage of, but other than that just great Anthony << Steve rolls his eyes >> I am gonna be real honest with you Anthony, The IWF/WOW is starting to make me mad, but I do beleive I can fight threw this. I am much better than my 500 record. Much better.Whether people beleive me or not is beside the point. The point is I know I am better. The wrestlers here are good, but they are not great. Once I get a FAIR chance here in the IWF/WOW, I will show you all my true skills,
Until then. I am just here to make a little money, get with some cute blondes, and show glimpses of greatness."
Anthony Edwards : " Sounds like your trying to blame everybody but yourself for your average start here in the Federation."
Steve Greedy : " Don't insult me in my home please Anthony, I don't insult you at IWF/WOW shows, So don't insult me here. I can take blame, When there is blame for me to take, But in the IWF/WOW so far none of the blame should be casted on me. Leviathan cheated, Risley Cheated, Concrete cheated, and Wildcard
Cheated...My wins on the other hand, Beat Black fairly, Beat Chrome Fairly,
Beat Moe fairly, and beat Viper fairly..It seems to me when people want to go
One on One and wrestle a clean match up against yours truly..They lose, Which is how I think you all should judge me. By the matches in which I am not cheated in. Only makes sense in my opinion."
Anthony Edwards : " The idea you have does make sense, but the reality of it is. You have cheated for all your wins, and have not been cheated by any ot the guys who beat you in that ring."
Steve Greedy : " Once again you insult me, If your trying to test me, I wouldn't reccomend it. Here in Little Rock, I can get away with murder ya know
<< Winks >> As for you accusing me of cheating, I would never. I love this sport to much, and hold it to such a high pedistal to do such a thing. If you didn't see Risley cheat the other night on Mayhem, Then I must say your blind
Anthony."
Anthony Edwards : " What did he do that was cheating Steve ? Since I obviously blinked during yall's match up one to many times."
Steve Greedy : " Closed fist, feet on the ropes, Grabbing of hair, Pulling of tights, and the last one which really distrurbed me. Constant grabbing of the private areas. He is a young man, Maybe he isn't sure of his sexual preference yet, and it was kinda scary Anthony. It was sickening, and I let him pin me so
I could get out of the ring. His obvious cheating and bad behaviors made it not worth me putting my body on the line that night. I have a large amount of respect, Money and Fame already, I don't have to deal with rough situations such as that."
[ Edwards shakes his head in disbeleif of the lies Greedy is telling, but then continues, Now changing the subject.]
Anthony Edwards : " Point Taken...This week on Mayhem you fight Paul Kiljoy,
Any comments towards this man who seems to have alot of your quality's as well."
Steve Greedy : " What quality's do I share with this man ? " Anthony Edwards : " Your both blessed with money, cockiness, flash, wrestling talent, ladies and quickness."
Steve Greedy : " There is differences between us in each category you just mentioned though Anthony. I am rich, He is well off. I am cocky for a reason,
He is just stubbornly cocky. I am flashy cause it's how I truly am, He is flashy cause he thinks it helps his chances of making it in the IWF/WOW. I am the best technical wrestler to ever grace the ring, He is just a good one. I am excellent with the ladies, He has to pay them to be seen with him, Except for the occasional crack whores he can get by telling them he will bring them the world, when in reality he will bring them nothing, and quickness ? << Shrugs >>
He may be quick, but I am not quick at anything I do. Ask these ladies around here."
Anthony Edwards : " I meant in the ring Greedy, and you know that." Steve Greedy : " I meant in and out of the ring Anthony. Out of the ring I am a painter looking for a masterpiece, and in the ring I am the same. I move sharply and crisply not really quickly. I take my time, and create a work of art when I enter the squared circle Anthony. I create a work of _FUCKING_ Art you hear me. So don't compare me to Paul Kiljoy."
[ Greedy gets up from the stool with a little bit of an attitude, and walks around to the other side of the bar. Fixing himself a screwdriver.]
Steve Greedy : " Would any of you like a nice cold mixed drink ? " [ Anthony tries to answer, but Greedy cuts him off. ] Steve Greedy : " Good, you weren't getting one anyway..Insulting me in my own damn home." [ Greedy finishes making his drink, and takes a sip. He sighs as a sign of relief, Then walks back around to his bar stool. He sits down with the drink in hand.]
Anthony Edwards : " Sorry if we made you mad Steve, I am just trying to do my job." Steve Greedy : " I don't care what your trying to do, It is what your doing which bothers me."
[ Takes another sip of his drink.] Steve Greedy : " Matter a fact, I want all of you except the camera man to leave right now. You can wait outside for him."
[ Greedy points to the door with his free hand. ] Anthony Edwards : " I don't think I can do that Steve.." Steve Greedy : " Leave Mr Edwards..." Anthony Edwards : " But St." [ Greedy cuts him off in the middle of his sentance and sharply looks towards the door. Edwards and his crew then head out of view, Except for the Camera man. Once Edwards and company is out of sight. Greedy looks into the camera sharply, with the trademark smirk no longer being visible.]
Steve Greedy : " Listen to me real close " [ Greedy downs the rest of his mixed drink and puts the glass on the bar. He stands up from the stool, and locks in on the camera.]
Steve Greedy : " You can call me a liar, A hypocrite, or even a son of a bitch at most places, but you will not insult me in my own home by calling me these things, Nor anywhere in the state of Arkansas IWF/WOW. You could of came here and asked me questions about my first IWF/WOW PPV Wrestlefest 6, You could of asked me bout my Hopes, My dreams, My background, even questions about me and my wife's problems of late, But you came in here insulting me."
[ Greedy straightens his suit up, and leans back against the bar trying to catch his cool.]
Steve Greedy : " In arkansas I am GOD, And soon I am gonna be a GOD in the IWF/WOW.." [ Flashes the cocky trademark smirk ] Steve Greedy : " No offence there to the newbie Wes Copeland, but it is true. You and I are almost done playing games IWF/WOW. Things will start becoming more serious in the months to come. It might start with Paul Kiljoy this week on Mayhem, Or it might not....The only thing for sure is that Paul Kiljoy will go
BANKRUPT On Mayhem this week...You can take that to the bank, My bank.." [ Greedy walks away from the camera sharply, Hoping to leave an impression.] [ Fade out to the IWF/WOW Logo.]
