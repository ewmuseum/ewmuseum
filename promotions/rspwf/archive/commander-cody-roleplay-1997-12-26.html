Commander Cody Roleplay

26 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<The screen flashes on to reveal a familiar figure reading the Friday edition of the "Denver Post" newspaper. The figure flips to the sports page where he reads the following...> Molten Monday Night Location:San Antonio, Texas Arena:Freeman Coliseum Capacity:9,865 Time:8:00-10:00 pm EST Date:December 29, 1997 Matches:5 Punisher vs. Commander Cody <The figure is dressed in army fatigues and combat boots with a Colorado Avalanche hat on. He looks up and notice a camera man and begins to speak.> Commander Cody- Atten Hut!!! Listen up all you UCW scum sucking maggots, This is the Commander addressing you!! First off I have some advice for all of you so listen up because I'm only going to say this once!! In a Commander's world FAILURE IS NOT AN OPTION!!! Now this is good advice so take heed. When I come down to the ring I may high five the fans, dance and prance around but when the ringbell goes Ding, Ding I'm all bussiness!! Now I notice first off that we are doing a tour of the great state of Texas. Well Ye-Ha!!! I promise you that you will be in for the show of your life because every time the Commander steps into the ring he gives 110%. I know alot of other people say it but I mean it and if you live anywhere in the area of the Freeman Coliseum in San Antonio, Texas I personaly invite you to come down to the arena and if you are not completely satisfied then I personaly refund your money! That my friends is a promise that not many wrestlers will make!! President I will also make a promise to you. That promise is that I will sell out the Freeman Coliseum and again that is a promise I intened to keep!! Now about my opponent. Punisher, Punisher! <He smirks and lets out a little laugh> How original is that name? I mean there is at least one Punisher in every federation I have ever joined!! Now Punisher I know nothing about you because you where not in this federation when I was before, but I know one thing and that is you are only one Corporal Cutter away from a 1-2-3. Now Let me tell you a little something about myself... <The screen then fades out to a tale of the tape for the Commander.> Name:Commander Cody Height:6'3'' Age:21 Sex:male Race:white Hair Length:Standard military buzz cut Hair Color:black Ring Attire:Army Fatigues Entrance Music(A wav file if you want to send it to me):Rock and Roll Part 2 (The Hey Song) by Gary Glitter Hometown:Denver, Colorado Style of Wrestling:High Flyer/Extreme Style Wrestler would never use:Technical Face/Neutral/Heel:Face Fan Support:????(What do you want here) He is loved in Denver though!! Desired Ring Entrance:The lights go out and green spotlights circle the arena as fireworks shoot out of the ring posts. Background(Titles,ETC):Memember of HWA, Lpyle's fed, Your fed. Plus many Indies!! 20 Favorite Wrestling Moves:any highflying moves Setup to Finisher:Throws him into the corner. Finisher:Corporal Punishment Description of Finisher:a diamond cutter off the top rope through a table. Time of Match when Finisher may be used:Anytime Strong Body Points:? Weak Body Points:? Intellegence(Smart/Dumb):Smart Match Focus(Does he show off or just fight):Just fight Gimmick:Ex-Army Commander Will Wrestler Cheat:Yes <The screen now comes back to Commander who is doing one arm push ups.> Commander Cody- Punisher I promise that you have never felt any finishing hold like the Corporal Punishment!! In most states Corporal Punishment is band, in others people fight for it to be baned but no one can band my version and it is much more painful, trust me!!! Come Molten Monday Meltdown the capacity crowd will be yelling as you leave the ring bloddy and battered while I get my arm raised in the center of the ring marking my succesful return to UCW!! Monday, December 29th will go down as one of the greatest nights in the history of UCW and why you may ask? BECAUSE THAT"S AN ORDER!!!! (C)Commander Cody Production, in association with HFW Productions. December 25th, 1997. "Wishing you all happy holidays!"
