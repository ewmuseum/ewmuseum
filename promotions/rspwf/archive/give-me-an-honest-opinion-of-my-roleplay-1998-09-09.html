Give Me An Honest Opinion of My Roleplay

9 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
To all who read this, please give me your honest opinion to my latest roleplay. Some feds say it's great others say that it just plain sucks.
Can some of you clear up what I need to add or get rid of. Thanks a lot. ---------- (The camera fades in on an opening of a graveyard. It is late at night. Fog is rolling across the grounds of the cemetery. The night sky is filled with the haunting cries of a lonely owl. In the distance lightning can be seen, and thunder can be heard. It begins raining lightly as the cemetery gates creek open slowly and the cameraman goes in slowly. The camera pans around the cemetery showing thousands of unmarked gravestones. The cameraman then goes down a row that has a very high gravestone. The camera pans up to show a shadowy figure pirched on top of the gravestone. The camera zooms in to reveal this shadowy character as VoorTexx. He is looking around, as if watching over the cemetery. He is wearing his usual out-of-ring attire:
black boots, black pants, black shirt, black leather gloves, black leather trenchcoat, and a pair of rayban sunglasses. He looks down and notices the cameraman and the camera pointing at him. He looks back up and begins to speak silently.)
It appears that this Wednesday I have a match against a man that is confused between the sport, if you can call it that, of Golf and pro- wrestling. This confused person is the Golfer. Well after our match Wednesday Golfer you will no longer be confused because you will be destroyed and your body will lie beneath where I am pirched. You actually think you have a shot at the
WHW's world title? You have been totally mislead because there is truly only one that deserves the WHW world title and that one man is VoorTexx. I have worked my way from the bottom of federations and I have become the most hated wrestler in all federations. I have even been named the nost powerful force in wrestling. You think you can beat that Golfer, a man who is confused between two totally different sports? I have heard people talk and
I have heard you backstage at house shows saying that you are God's gift to wrestling, well, I am Satan's gift to wrestling. After I am through with you
Wednesday night, I will be sending you on a one way trip straight to hell where your soul will become my property.
(VoorTexx rises to his feet on top of the gravestone and does a forward flip off of it and lands on the ground. As his feet hit he ground a rumble of thunder is let forth. VoorTexx points to a lantern that is sitting next to the gravestone he was pirched on, and it all of a sudden lights up and illuminates the area. You can almost hear the cameraman gulp because he is frightened. VoorTexx, with hsi back to the camera, begins to speak.)
I can sense by now that you are wishing you did not have to face me Golfer, but it is to late. Your fate has been carved in stone...
(VoorTexx points the the gravestone and two bursts of flames shoot up out of the ground, knocking the cameraman back. The fire shows what's on the gravestone. The name "The Golfer" appears on it. The camera then goes back to VoorTexx who is now turned around with a shovel in his hand. He begins to dig as he speaks.) I want you to take a good, LONG look at these surrounding because this will be your final resting place after our match tomorrow night. I smell the fear coming from you Golfer and that makes me even more powerful to know that you fear the great one. Golfer there is no denying it;
I am your fate, I am your grim destiny. (VoorTexx plants the shovel into the mound of dirt and he then motions for the camera to come closer.)
Look into these eyes... (VoorTexx pulls his shades down far enough to reveil his eyes, which are pure black.) These eyes are the way unto a forsaken soul, my soul which was taken from me so long ago. I know look for other souls who do not belong on this planet. You are one of these people Golfer.
(VoorTexx pushes his shades back up and jumps back up onto the gravestone. He gains his balance and squats down as it begins to rain heavily now.) I have one simple question a confused man such as yourself can answer,
Golfer... What are you going to do when the black hole grabs a hold of you? (VoorTexx looks back up from the camera and looks around the cemetery as the rain beats down upon the camera lens. A bolt of lightning hits the freshly dug grave of the Golfer's. VoorTexx pays no attention to it as the camera fades quickly to a commercial.)
