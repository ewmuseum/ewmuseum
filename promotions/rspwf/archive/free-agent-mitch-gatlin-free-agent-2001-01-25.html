I am in two good feds right now as it is but I want one more to compete in. The fed has to have a lot of coemption. I would rather it be an older fed that has been around for a while. If you know of a good fed then I would appreciate it if you contacted me by E-mail at [detail withheld]
[detail withheld] or AIM me at [detail withheld]. Sample Flash. . . [-The scene opens up inside of an almost pitch black room. There is a TV that seems to be in the middle of the room. The light from the Television is slowlyrevealing the room. The only thing that can be seen so far is that there is a white leather chair in the middle of the room. The camera slowly begins to zoom in on the chair when the TV gets some what brighter than it was before and you can see a slight glimpse of a figure that is sitting in the chair.-]
[-You can see that this figure has a very strong and muscular complexion to his body. The camera zooms in a bit more and you can see that this man has on a
White cow boy hat that has one small brown strip around the base of the hat. This man is also wearing a Black shirt that has "The New Breed" written across the front in white bold letters and He has on some blue jeans and a pair of tan work boots.-]
[-As the Television gets brighter you can tell just who this man is. It is none other than the southern sensation. Mitch "Sure Fire" Gatlin. Mitch has an ash tray resting in the center of his lap. There is a cigar slowly burning away in the ash tray. Mitch reaches down and grabs the cigar in his right hand and takes a long draw and begins to speak.-]
M "SF" G: "Well, if it is not the GSWP. Where everyone and there mother has something to say. If I had aquarter for every time I heard someone say they are going to be the next GSWP world champion, I would be a very rich man."
"That is what makes me different from everyone else. It really does not matter to me if I will be the next champion. You see that is not what I came to the
GSWP to do. I did not come to just become the champion. Unlike most of the guys around here I came to prove a point. When the GSWP came knocking at my door the only reason I answered was because I had a good idea of who all would be showing there faces around here. People I have been eager to put on the shelf for some time now. I came here to further my career and end a few others. To end the career's of people who are just holding down this great sport. Guys who have done there time and has already hit the climax of there peak. Guys like Steve Houston, James Pillow, Scottie Extreme, and Jack Vars."
"Do not get me wrong they _had_ a major impact on this sport and they most likely at one point and time_was_ the best, but there is nothing more for them here in the wrestling business. It is all down hill from here for all of these legends of there time. Every one of these old timers has had there chance to be in the spotlight. They have had there chance to be the top dog."
"Now it is my turn." [-Mitch exhales the smoke and blows it into the camera.-] M "SF" G: "Let me start with Jack Vars. To consider him a great is just a joke. Plain and simple this guy has been around for years and has never made any sort of progress. He just needs to take what time he has left with his life because you know he is getting a little old and just retire. Go to some beach off of a resort island and do what you do best. Lay on your back!"
"You see Jack you are nothing. When people say your name they just laugh at you. They say when is that guy going to quit. He has been around for years and has not amounted to anything. He is just the same as he was when he started out ."
"Then we have Scottie Extreme. The man who never reallymade it out of the indy feds. He has always been thechampion of some small federation that no one has ever herd of. He is one of them guys that brag and brag about what he has done in the past and not what he isdoing now. You know why that is? It is because he to is just like VARs and I know that is not saying much but that is my point. They both have turned into big jokes."
"Now let me move down the line to "Simply The Best" James Pillow. Pillow, _was_ one of the best. He hasaccomplished just about everything that anyone possiblycould in there career. Pillow has somewhat of a hint that he is not getting any younger. I do recall himsaying in his last interview "For the last several months I've sat back and watched...I've watched top names leave the game, and unknowns take there places" Pillow you should have never deiced to lace up them boots of yours back up and step into the ring againbecause my friend I am the man who is not going to give you a choice if you get to just step out like you did before. This go around you are going to be carriedout and it will either be on a stretcher or a body bag and that my friend is your choice because some where down the line are paths will meet and you will see what I speak is the truth."
"Now the man that just pisses me off the most that sorrysack of shit "The Legend" Steve Houston. Steve you arenot getting any younger. The gray hairs are slowlyweaving there way into your pretty black hair. Your body is not what it used to be and you move slower than my grandma does. That is because you two are about thesame age. Steve my friend you are just a big joke. If there was any one man that I am dying to get into thering with that man would have to be you. Steve and lucky for you I get that chance. I get to show everyonehow
I am stronger, smarter, and faster than you. Justlike the rest of the guys I have spoke on you _was_ oneof the best. Now Steve you still think that you are one of the best but reality you fit right in with the rest of the old timers."
[-Mitch takes another long draw off of his cigar.-] M "SF" G: "The one thing that all these men have in common is that they all have been retired at one point and time. Then they all deiced that they cannot stay away from the sport. That is what makes them just likeme. They thrive on being the best and being in the spot light. The big difference between them and me is that I am in my prime. I am the future and they are just the past. You see Houston and Pillow. You do not remember when you were my age do you? Starting off eager and blood thirsty. You went through all of the older men and showed them they were washed up. Now think about this, "If you do not know your history then you are doomed to repeat it."
"Steve Houston you act as if you are a movie star or some shit. You are just a snob that thinks they arebetter than the rest of us and you have keep nose stuck up in the air. You act as if you are to good foreverything. Steve I know why you keep your nose stuck up in the air it is because you just do not want to smell how _shitty_ you really are. How you have became an old man and have nothing left to live for but wrestling. No one cares about you. No one loves you and no one would miss you if you were dead and gone. That is half of what made you come out of retirement. To tell the truth you were stuck in your bar drinking through the days and puking the night away. Boring who ever you could find about your past. How you was this and how you did that. Steve wake up it is the twenty-first century and wrestling has stepped up to the next level and I am just a representative."
[-Mitch exhales the smoke blowing little smoke rings as he talks.-] M "SF" G: "Enough about the past let me focus on the present. I have said enough about Steve Houston, so let me focus on one of my other opponents coming into this match up."
"Dave Pietka, this guy is a joke. He obviously does not have too much going for him inside of his head. He thinks he has to fight his own partner. What kind of loser is he. This man talks about how everyone thinks he is worthless.
"There is not much I can say about this guy but if you seen his interview then you naturally know he is a joke. When they let him into the GSWP it was just plain out wrong. They need to send him back down to the bush leagues because when this man steps into the ring with someone like me there is going to be some bloody results. I intend on making a fool out of this guy."
"Then we have Oz. This man is a nut. Plain and simpleall he talked about was eating cow patties and how theyare not a good treat. Well, Oz you are worse of a joke than Dave Pietka he don't eat cow shit. You see, you and Steve Houston have one thing in common. His life is shit and well you eat shit. Oz there is not much I can say about you but all I can say is that you are what you eat."
"Last but not least "Show Time" Shawn Sylkk. Shawn has been keeping to him self here lately. Shawn that is the best thing you could possible do. You do not want to embarrass your self like the rest of your team has done. Well,
Shawn since you have not said much Ithink I will cut it short on you because you already know the deal. Come time for resurrection Shawn the you know just as well as I do your show is over."
[-Mitch takes another hit off of his cigar.-] M "SF" G: "Now onto my team. We have the winning squad by the way it looks. We have Page, Viper, O'Niel and myself."
"Page is a very talented man and I believe he has what it takes to hold up his side of this match up. Page knows what will happen if does not hold up his side of the match and I can tell you it is not one thing he wants to get in to."
"Then we have to get stuck with a complete fool ShawnViper. He comes out and says he is going to beateveryone's ass. Well, Shawn you may have just bitten off more than you can chew on that one. First thing is we are a team. The second thing is if you want a piece of me then you do not have to ask me twice.
I am not a hard man to find and I will sure as hell not back down from you. Shawn if you come out here and try to show boat through this match I am going to be the one to kick the teeth down your throat not the other team. So Shawn you need to get your head on straight before I have to straighten it out for you."
"Now onto O'Niel. You better let your actions speaklouder than your words because you have not said a word. So O'Niel let me get one thing straight. You hold up your end of this thing and the rest of us will hold up are end. Just do not be the one to drop the ballbecause you will not get a chance to pick it up, if you know what I mean."
"So Everyone in GSWP, I look at it like this. I am backed against a wall in this match and I have nothing to lose and that my friend makes me a very dangerous man. Everyone wake up because the _future_ is here and You can Like
Me Love Me or Hate Me I do not give a damn but you will Respect me! That I promise."
