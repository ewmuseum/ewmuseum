[Critique] 'The Chinese Icon' Hushia Montaki

22 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Helly Guys! This is one of my first RP's. Take is easy on me, I'm a girl anyways! :) Give me fedback. -X Scene X- The scene opens up inside of the Tokyo Airport where the camera man is sitting in a chair as many people walk by and forth in front of him. Some people walk up to the camera man asking him what he is doing. He tells them, making people stick around to see Hushia Montaki. After a few moments, a huge cheer arises as
Hushia Montaki makes his way through the crowd of people and takes a seat beside the camera man. Hushia Montaki takes a look at all the people, grins, and begins to talk.
-X Hushia Montaki X- Finally, ThaDawg187 has opened his mouth about our match. It is about time
Dawg. But, the thing is, it might have been the WRONG time. What I mean is that is that you probably would have been much better of just staying in the shadows like you were. You weren't able to embrasses yourself by saying something stupid like you have now. But, then again...if you wouldn't have talked, then everybody would be saying that you are a big pussy and those kinds of things.
So, all in all...showing your face is a good thing. Atleast it gives me something to talk about. Anyways, onto what you had to say about me. Dawg, I have forgotten about Josh Ritter. I know he is nothing like you. Atleast I know for a Buda Given Fact that you have a touch of talent invested in your body.
Lord, that guy had nothing in him. I'm not sure even why he wrestles. On the other hand though, we take a look at you. I know you have some talent.
Otherwise, you wouldn't be the nWc Cruiserweight Champion. The question is though, are you going to still be the Cruiserweight Champion after our match this Saturday at Boiling Point? Who knows. But, I know what you are thinking.
You are thinking that you know that you are still going to be the nWc Cruiserweight Champion. Go ahead, think that. And I'll go ahead and think and
KNOW that I'm going to be the NEW nWc Cruisrweight Champion after Saturday. We both have our mind frames set, it is just who has better talent. And I'm not even going to go there because everybody knows that I'm the man with the most talent. Dawg, just because your the Cruiserweight Champion doesn't make you the best of the best. NO!! It makes you the best of the nWc's
Cruiserweights.....FOR THE TIME BEING!! Pretty soon, I might be able to say I'm the best of the nWc Cruiserweights. Only the nWc World Champion can say they are the best of the best. So, get your head on striaght Dawg and realize what you are and who you are.
Now, a big issue arises next. The fact that this is a United States and China match. You say that the United States is better and I say that China is better.
I know that everybody is on your side Dawg, so I know I'm going to be out-voted. But, if more Chinese people were here with me I'm sure they would say that China is better. See, it is a fact of your heritage. If you are
American, you think the United States is better. If you are Chinese, then you think that China is better. If you are Canadian, you think Canada is better. It just goes on and on that way. Yes, the United States has beat China in many things in the past Dawg, I agree when you said that. But, you forgot to mention the fact that China has the United States equally as much. And before you even bring up the fact that the United States beat China in the World Cup, who gives a care? We are wrestlers, we shouldn't be worrying about soccer. Anyways, it wasn't too long ago when China were the World Cup Champions, and who did they beat. You got it...The United States! Just because the Chinese don't look like everybody else in the World doesn't mean you have to rip on it. The Great Buda made us this way...Buda wanted us to stand out from the world. Why though?
Maybe because in the long run the Chinese kind are better. Look at the different between us. You guys have a messed up economy. You waste your money miserbley. Then look at China. We have a well balanced and stable economy. We save our money, that is why we have so much more new technology than the United
States. Enough of World talk though. I'm here to talk about you.
Dawg, you say that the nWc hasen't given me respect and that you MADE this match. First off Dawg, you didn't make this match....I DID! You just agreed with it and the nWc signed it. But why did the nWc sign it? If I didn't deserve this Cruiserweight Title shot, then they wouldn't have signed the match. You are just the person I have to beat. As for Rain or Rashawn Taylor...I can beat both of them. Really, I want Rain to win the Battle Royal because I want to face him. But, for that to happen I have to beat you. I cannot look ahead like that. I have to focus on this match then focus on beating whoever wins the
Battle Royal.
That brings up another interesting point. It seems like everybody in the Battle
Royal is talking like they are going to have to fight ThaDawg187 when, in reality, they have to fight me. Yeah, people may think I'm a Industrial
Loser...but I can show them all wrong. And I plan on doing it. I think I'm turning some heads in the National Wrestling Council. But, that isn't good enough for me. I want to turn some heads in the ENTIRE wrestling WORLD! And I think this is a good start. Winning the nWc Crusierweight Championship from who
I think is the best Champion so far. You see Dawg, I have all the respect in the World for you. It takes alot of work to win a championship, and whoever holds the titles...then they deserve respect. I know that you are going to say that you don't respect me....but I don't care. I don't need respect from you to win this match. Really, I don't need respect FOR YOU to lose this match. But I do, and that is all that matters.
Yes Dawg, I have won two matches out of two matches here in the nWc. But, those were not challenges. They were mearly bumbs in the road for me. But you, you are going to be a challenge. But, I have never lost to a challenge. Why?
Because I have faith. Then again, Faith doesn't make things happen...it simply makes them easier. Both of those men were Americans, and I'm pretty sure everybody I fight is going to be an American. You are no different Dawg. You say that you are an American that I cannot beat? 187, I have never ment a
American that I can't beat. And you don't hold an exception! You can show me how Americans fight Dawg and I'll show you how the Chinese fight. Deal? Deal.
As for why I had to drag you to Rome.....that was to make sure that we were on neutral ground. A place inbetween of America and China. It was only fair on my part.
Dawg, I agree that not only are we going to fight in the Colleseum but we are also going to fight all over the streets of Rome. And like I said, this is going to be a Hardcore match. As for you draging me to China...don't make me laugh.
-X Scene X- Hushia Montaki's flight number is called over the PA System. Montaki stands up and grabs his bags as Airport Security helps him get through the crowd. He makes his way onto the plane and takes a seat. He goes through all the procedures as the plane that off. They are in the air now as Hushia Montaki begins to speak.
-X Hushia Montaki X- Dawg187...Here I am. On my way to Rome for our match. And I sure know that you were on your way. It is just too bad that you got throwen in jail you beer guzzling, American, filth ball. Dawg, you talked about all this stuff and how you are going to beat me and stuff. You may say what you want to say. Words or just words. Only sticks and stone can hurt me. Pretty soon we are going to be in Rome fighting our hearts out until one of us gets two pins. Who is it going to be? Me or You. Only time will tell. As for now Dawg, I'm done. I'm sure we are going to hear from each other soon enough though. Bye-Bye.
-X Scene X- Hushia Montaki calls a attendent over and orders a Coke. Hushia Montaki looks at the camera and winks as it shuts off.
With A Smile :), [name withheld] One Of The Few Woman Handlers, Handles- [nWc] Hushia Montaki [2-0-0]
