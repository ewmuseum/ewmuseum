XENON Roleplay - Critique

6 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Now tell me what you think of the hardcore legend known as Xenon... =-=-=-=--==-=-=-=-=- {Scene opens at 1:19AM exactly. The weather is bitterly cold, the skies are dark blue and there is no one around. The camera pans around and then notices a guy sitting on a swing in the distance. The camera man walks forward as it fades into darkness.}
{The camera re-opens and notices that the guy on the swing is in fact, ICW wrestler and number one contender for the Hardcore championship, Xenon. He is wearing a long navy blue Adidas jacket, he has a black hat of wool with his greasy hair coming out of it. He also has torn up LeVi jeans and combo boots.
He is slowly swinging on the swing. He seems to be dazing on the floor. As the camera walks a bit closer he suddenly looks up with a surprised look on his face. He then drops his head back down and dazes into space. He then looks up again at the camera and speaks}
XENON - Do you guys work 24 hours a day? You seem to be out all day, don't you wanna be at home, in front of a warm fire with your family?
CAMERA MAN - Why yes, but Mr.. {Xenon interrupts} XENON - I thought so. It's alright for you. I haven't got a home. I haven't got a warm home. I haven't even got a damn family. The only place I call home is a doorway somewhere, or if I'm really lucky...A little bed and breakfast somewhere. Do you know I haven't had a good nights sleep in over TWO years now and I haven't had a proper bath or shower in almost FOUR months. But {Shrugs his shoulders} I'm not complaining, in fact...I LOVE MY LIFE!! That sucks huh?
CAMERA MAN - It's your life.. XENON - Exactly, it's my life. It's up to me whether I stay up all night, whether I don't wash my hair, whether I fight to survive or whatever. It's all up to ME!! And that is why this Friday night on ICW Fallout in front of a no doubt, sold out jam packed Pyramid in Memphis Tennessee, I am gonna walk into the arena with nothing to lose but walk out 15 pounds heavier, as I walk out with MY Hardcore championship belt. You know, my good buddy Johnny Gunz let me come 'round last night to listen to the ICW's LowDown and I heard some comments made by the current Hardcore champion of the ICW, Kuba Rico. Now, I say current because after this Friday night from the Pyramid I will go down as it's SECOND
EVER ICW Hardcore champion. You know, I was a little upset that the ICW just let Kuba Rico make this title up and that they let him hold the title before proving himself or even making a little tournament because that way I'd defenately become it's FIRST champion but oh well, atleast I'll say I held that title after this Friday night's event on Fallout. Looking forward to the match,
Mr. Camera man?
CAMERA MAN - Oh yeah, I really enjoy watching those hardcore title fights. But, how can you guys go through so much pain and still get up afterwards?
XENON - {Laughs} You know, I sometimes ask that question to myself but you know...THAT'S LIFE!! But you see, after I'm through with Kuba ''Spanish/Mexican
Loser'' Rico, he wont be able to get to his feet on his own, he'll need several officiall's help and I don't mean that as a threat, but as a FACT!! My good buddy, Johnny Gunz almost ended his career last week but thought not to and has let me polish Rico off. Kuba Rico, you call yourself the ICON of HARDCORE?
Well, you can say that all you want but the bottom line is...I'M THE KING OF
HARDCORE!!! Oh and don't forget, the next ICW Hardcore champion!! Oh Kuba Rico,
I'm glad I was of some assistance to you when I powerbombed you through that table and put your discs in your back, back together. But Mr.Rico, after this
Friday's Fallout, you wont be thanking me anymore, in fact...I DOUBT YOU'LL
EVEN BE ABLE TO SPEAK ANYMORE!!! Get ready Rico for your worst beating and several injuries because you're gonna be out for a very, very long time. I have a little something for you to Kuba Rico. I just wanna say ''Thank You'' for allowing me a shot at your Hardcore championship. I've been waiting a long time for a break like this and it's finally here, so thanks a lot. I owe ya one..
{Xenon laughs and then gets off the swing and walks along} XENON - You know, being alone in the cold nights is kinda depressing and upsetting but after Fallout, atleast I wont be lonely anymore and I defenately wont be depressed....
CAMERA MAN - Why's that? XENON - Don't be stupid all your life. After Fallout, I'll have a new friend to keep me company and that of course being....THE HARDCORE CHAMPIONSHIP!!!
{Grins} I could do a little stand up show with Mr.Hardcore belt. I could air my open weekly t.v show ''Xenon and Mr.Hardcore belt''. What d'ya think?
CAMERA MAN - You can't be serious? XENON - Why not? If Kuba Rico can invent the Hardcore championship, then surely
I can have my own weekly t.v show. Oh well, maybe I'll re-think that idea. Oh well, Kuba Rico, the time is ticking away. It's almost time for you to face me in what will surely be the most brutal, extreme and hardcore match in ICW history. Prepare for a beating, prepare to kiss your belt away because I have a
Tendency to Kill, starting with you. Until this Friday night, all I have to say is...
AU REVOIR!! {The scene fades into darkness}
