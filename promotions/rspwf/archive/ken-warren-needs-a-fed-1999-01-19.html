Ken Warren needs a fed.

19 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Introducing...Ken Warren Name-Ken Warren Nickname-Superstar weight-235 height-5'10" hometown-Nashville, Tenn. titles held-UEF Intercontinental Title 2x music-"High Enough" - Damn Yankees years pro- 1 year Email [detail withheld] [A crowded auditorium in Nashville, Tennesee has gathered at a local UEF championship wrestling event. The crowd is a raucus bunch with drinking beer and eating hot dogs, but they are here cheering one of their own. "High
Enough" by Damn Yankees begins to play throughout the auditorium as the crowd erupts in cheers.]
[Through the curtains walks "Superstar" Ken Warren. He is not very tall, standing at only 5'10" but he is very atheletic and has the heart of a true superstar. He has brown hair with green eyes and has a pretty impressive physique. He is wearing Levi blue jeans and a white t shirt with the wording
"SUPERSTAR" on the back in red, with black cowboy boots.]
[He walks down the ramp and slides easily into the ring and reached over to the announcers table and begins to speak...]
KW-Thank you ladies and gentlemen, if you would quiet down a bit I have something to say. {Crowd quiets} Now, for a year now, I have wrestled exclusively for the Nashville based UEF. Throughout this year I have been fortunate to win the Intercontinental Title on two occasions and have made many friends, as well as enemies. Now, after looking around and seeing so many federations with top stars throughout the world, I have decided to upgrade my talents and move into a larger based federation.
[Ken Warren looks into the crowd deep in thought, then reples warmly.] KW-So I say this to the would be superstars out there in the various organizations. "I am looking for the elite in competition and if I come into your federation, watch out because I am the man that can get the job done and I will never, ever, settle for second best. Thank you all.
[Ken Warren returns the mic to the announcers table and quietly exits the auditorium.] k k w w kk w w k k w w w k k . w w.
