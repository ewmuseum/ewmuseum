PLEASE COMMENT ON MY FLASH.....THANKS.

13 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Heavy Metal music plays over the P.A. as "Mean" Mike MuCuley, "Bully" Bob Brady and "Juvenile" Jonas make their way down to ringside. Each of them are wearing black jeans and black leather jackets that say "A Mother's
Worst
Nightmare" on the back. MuCuley is 6'3" with a medium, well defined build. He has short, spikey bleached blond hair. Bob Brady is 6'9" with a husky build like a biker. He has long brown hair and is wearing black shades and appears to be very serious. Jonas is 6'0" with the build of a lightweight wrestler. He has curly brown hair that comes down over his face and also has a tongue ring. As the three make their way to the ring, MuCuley is jawing away at the fans while Jonas is spraying the fans with confetti and laughing his head off. Brady just growls at the fans faking lunges at them.
MuCuley grabs a mic from ringside and climbs in the ring with Brady and
Jonas
following. There are a round of boos all over the arena.]
[MuCuley] Hey.....Hey.....(boos get louder) If you all know what's good for you, shut the hell up before I do it for you with my fist down your stinkin idiotic throats! (more boos) Jonas, (motions to him) give it to them.
[Juvenile Jonas goes into his pocket and pulls out a straw and starts shooting spitballs at the fans while laughing his head off. The fans become irate and start throwing drinks and food towards the ring.]
[MuCuley] All you morons out there don't know who you're dealing with here. We are three people who don't give a damn about nothing or no one. We have no respect for the law, we had no respect for our teachers in school, no respect for our parents, so if I were all of you, I wouldn't feel so safe out there in your seats because I oughta come out there and smack each and every one of you silly.
[As garbage is being thrown into the ring, Jonas is picking it up and throwing it back at the audience hysterically.]
[MuCuley] The trio of me, "Bully" Bob Brady and "Juvenile" Jonas are about to set a new standard in this industry, for we are the people to take this sport to new heights. No longer will the 'good guys' prevail, no longer will there be law and order, no longer will there be sportsmanship and class. Times have changed...and we are that change. It's all about "as long as I get mine, I don't give a damn."
[The fans are really irate now at the trio for their plans to take away the tradition of wrestling. Jonas grabs the mic from MuCuley.]
[Jonas] You know what I think Mike...I think these fans need to cool down and I think I know just the way to help them....maybe we should turn on the arena fire alarm sprinklers...better yet, I'm gonna give them a sprinkler of my own...
(laughs his head off) [Jonas drops the mic and climbs to the second turnbuckle. He starts to unbuckle his pants. Over the boos of the fans, you can read his lips:
"Drink my piss." He's going hysterically as if he's having the time of his life. Officials from the dressing room rush out in an attempt to stop the trio from continuing this spectacle but are met with bad results. All the officials receive the beat down of their lives from MuCuley and Brady as Jonas hops around the ring laughing and smacking the officials butts. When it's all said and done, all the officials are laid out all in the ring and around the ringside area. MuCuley grabs the mic.]
[MuCuley] What you saw there was an example of things to come. We have planted a seed, a bad seed, we are the Bad Seeds that will grow a vine of forbidden fruit and
I dare anyone to come and take a bite. [MuCuley drops the mic and the trio exit the ring to a deafening volume of boos. MuCuley spits on the fans, Brady growls at them and Jonas grabs at the womens breasts and laughs hysterically as they make their way back to the dressing room.]
