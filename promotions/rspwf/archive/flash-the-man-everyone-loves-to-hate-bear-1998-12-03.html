Ok, FUCK the constructive critism, just tell me what you think about my rp, without dissing me to much. If you have nothing better to do, then you could go over my EVERY word in the rp, like people do anyways. Just to make fun of me.
Anyways.. here it is.. Oh, and don't think im bad, if you dont like it, this is my first rp in the fed, with only seeing like 3 other rps from other fed people.
Scene: Lights go out.Two wrestlers are fighting each other as every thing that ever lived has almost been killed of sheer boredom. All of a sudden [like u didn't expect] Ruff Ryders Anthem plays as the fans get on there feet. BeaR comes out looking as constipated as usual, or whatever condition he has... He has a microphone and yells into it, "Helllllllloooo....... ladies!" Some non-BeaR fan, who is a lady throws a piece of cake at BeaR as BeaR gets hit in the head and falls down... The lady jumps the guard rail and beats the crap out of BeaR.. The guards come up and one hold the lady back.. BeaR gets up and shakes his head and makes fun of the lady.. He sticks his tongue out and acts like a flying squriel mating. He then acts like he is masturbating as the guards turn around.. He stops and starts whisseling.. BeaR yells, "Let me at her!".. the guards shrug there shoulders and let her go as BeaR steps back and looks frightened as this 200 pound lady jumps on him... BeaR looks over and gets some popcorn and puts it behind him as the lady sees it and gets off BeaR and starts to snack.. He gets up and takes one of the guards night sticks and starts to beat the lady senseless.. BeaR is bleeding and bruised as he walks down to the ring and watches as the wrestlers fight.. He gets in the ring and trys to act like the big boss man with the night stick and "twirls" it around..
He ends up droping it... Once again he picks it up as he looks at the two wrestlers fight.. he clubs them with the night stick and grabs a microphone and says "I got two words for ya" .. BeaR puts his butt next to the wrestlers face and farts twice really loud as he laughs and waits for the crowd to settle down. Then One of the wrestlers reachs up and grabs BeaR's face. And he pulls it off! it was a BeaR mask. Daniel Stryfe was under the mask! He runs to the back laughing his ass off. BeaR walks to the ring also laughing his ass off.
He is carrying The Rock. He grasbs a mic, stops laughing and begins to speak. BeaR: Hello, I am BeaR. The newest member of this fed. As you might have noticed, by my Death Row shirt, that I am a member of Death Row. But I am not a serious wrestler. My funny antics usually cause everyone to get mad at me. But fuck everyone outside of Death row. now, this is my manager. ::Holds a rock up in the air.:: His name is The Rock. Now, I have just arrived a few days ago, and I have noticed that there is a No Limit Soldiers stable. This makes me very happy. You see, Me and my friends took them out a while back. Well, at leas one of the little factions of the many. It was one of the bigger ones as well. nLs.
You don't know who Death Row is? Well, you will soon find out. I guarantee this! I am not going to come here and kiss your asses because you guys are nLs.
Hell no. nLs is nothing compared to Death row. Now, you guys are probably going to say something stupid in reply like "You guys suck, because I have never heard of you." Well, I have never heard of Kandyman. Or Penetrator. You guys really think that you are the greatest? ::A little smurf runs out to the ring with a piece of paper. BeaR grabs it, pushes the smurf out, and reads the note.:: Oh, this is pretty damn funny. Someone in the back, probably that little smurf, wrote me a transcript of The RingMaster's interview. Apparently he wants to join nLs. Well, that would be a great big step towards being the best stable in this fed's history. YEAH RIGHT!! Listen nLs. Here is some advice, take your sorry asses, and just leave. RingMaster, I don't care what jobber feds you have held the World Title in. You suck. If you guys truly think you are good, then lets put together a little match. How about it? Death Row vs. nLs. "Certain Death" Daniel Stryfe is on my side. He is the other member of Death Row. Hell, I am willing to go three on two against you chumps. Oh wait, I almost forgot. Daniel told me in back that "The Hardcore Legend" Madman
Pondo is now a member of Death Row! Well, Then I guess it could be three on three. Death Row is taking over. No one can do anything about it, except sit back and watch. Bwa hah ha ha. That's all I have to say, so start the fucking music!
Scene: BeaR drops the mic. Ruff Ryders Anthem begins to play over the speakers. Then BeaR goes up to the different turn buckles and throw up the Death Row sign. BeaR then does it in the middle of the ring. The crowd cheers. Everyone is taking pictures. The lights are flickering, pyros are going off, it is like a party. Then BeaR and the Rock leave the ring, and go to the back. Everyone then gets in there cars and drives away.
Tell me what you think!
