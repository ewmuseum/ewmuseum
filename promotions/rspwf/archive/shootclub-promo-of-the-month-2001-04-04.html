SHOOTCLUB Promo of the Month

4 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://shootclub.com The below promo received the most positive votes of the 135 promos cut in March. The promo was cut by former NSW star "The Power" Afwa Kilmanjaro. Afwa has since moved to the ESW to conquer the current ESW champion
"The Hornet". "The Power" Afwa Kilmanjaro [SCENE: The setting is that of a remote location. The picturesque view though depicts a red hot glow, like that of the sunset, rising from above the mountain tops. The air is brisk and comes from the southern part of the word. The red glow then begins to slowly depart and then we are taken as if on a journey through a set of trees. Sounds of trees rustling as the cameras pass them. The shot then shows as the cameras wade through the trees and what looks like an old wooden shack is then placed before the cameras view.]
[OLD SHACK: Upon entering the old shack, we can see it has no firm structure, as it rests on top of a rock. The shack is built from old timber. The mood inside the shack is serene and and is well lit up.
The cameras then pass over, picking up the sounds of the creeking wooden floor. They then begin to pan over to where one of thses hammocks, used for sleeping are. Some slight noise is heard coming from the the hammock.]
NOISE: ZZZZ....ZZZZ....ZZZZ...ZZZZ... [As the cameras get closer to the hammock, the noise of snoring begins to get louder, until they seem to be right on top of it, just then a startled figure is seen falling off the hammock and hitting the bare wooden floor.]
[APPEARANCE: The figure is of a non American heritage, and judging by the way he looks, one imagines he is from somewhere like Tonga or
Samoa. The figure has dark black hair, shaped in the form of an afro style. He has emerald green eyes and he wears what appears to be a neckalace of ivory around his neck, so maybe he is from The Ivory
Coast? As the cameras pass over the figure, he yawns, signalling that he has just awoken from his sleep. The cameras then come up close to the figure, we can then see that is dressed in a pair of black tights, with little straps made where the soles of his feet go, which are of course bare, He also has on a black T-Shirt with a palm tree in the middle and beneath it, in neon yellow graphology are the words "Fear what you can't hear.."
On the back of T-Shirt is the face of Afwa Kilmanjaro's father with the name "The Power" Afwa Kilmanjaro in deep purple graphology. The cameras then as the figure yawns once more cut in, as he seems to be speaking..]
"TP" AK: TOLAWA LAVA!![This means hello in Samoan.] "TP" AK: Welcome to my home, my shack, welcome to the shack of Awfa
Kilmanjaro, a name which every American shall learn to hear. Want some pineapple..
[Afwa then rummages around in his hammock and grabs hold of a pineapple.] "TP" AK: No.. It's mine..Afwa love pineapple. Pineapple Afwa only real friend. Pineapple make Afwa big and strong and fight off nasty
Americans. [Awfa then begins to stare at the pineapple..] "TP" AK: My uncle has taught me well, he told me that the juice of the pineapple would help me on my way and it now is to lead me away from my people, away from my island, away from Samoa and into this place,
America!
[Afwa Kilmanjaro spits onto he floor, at the sound of America.] "TP" AK: Afwa's father, The Great Tongan Tongue he told me that if
Afwa was to succeed then he would have to use three things in his kit of surviavl.
[Afwa for the lame American raises his first finger, signalling one.] "TP" AFK: POWER.. The power of a man can determine, just how easy he can dominate. Power is like a force. Back in Samoa, I learnt how to shake the trees and knock the pineapples to the floor. I was then able using only my bare hands to crush the juice right out of the pineapple and it is this skill of power that now I am in America, shall use in crushing those that try and stop the mighty Afwa Kilmanjaro, son of the Tongan Terror, the greatest Barbarian of them all.
[Afwa then raises his second finger, again signalling to the lame
American that he can count.]
"TP" AK: ENDURANCE. When I was a child, growing up amongst my people of Samoa, live every child, we used to play games. When Afwa cast his mind back, Afwa can think of one game, it's called Hunt The Pig. The concept of the game was to be the first to capture the wild pig and tear it apart whilst it was alive. To catch the pig required a lot of endurance, for one wrong move and the pig would have got away and the game would be over. Afwa is proud though, as he managed to beat his best friend Shaku and Awfa slayed the pig, tearing it apart with his bare hands.
[Afwa then picks up a pineapple which is on the table, he with his teeth rips it apart and then begins to suck out the juice.]
"TP" AK: Hunt The Pig is a game which Afwa loved to play. Awfa now happy that he can play it in America. Awfa is looking to hunt all the pigs here in America and just like Awfa did to the Pig, he shall do to all of you.
[Afwa then raises his third finger, the middle finger..] "TP" AK: BRUTALITY, a skill that Afwa has the most of. Awfa has seen those who claim to be brutal, yet have they ever see a WILD SAMOAN!
Awfa can withstand a lot of _PAIN_ and he loves to give out more. I remember Shaku and I, once he really got on my nerves, so by mistake,
I knocked him out, one blow with the deadliest move in the world and for ten whole seconds Shaku was out. This is something that all you
Americans shall have to get used to. Afwa Kilmanjaro needs no reason to cause a problem, just not being of the Samoan heritage, makes each and every one of your pathetic race the problem. Afwa Kilmanjaro shall prove to all you vile Americans that you may all think of yourselves as the way foreward, but there is nothing greater then the Isle of
Samoa!
[Afwa chomps away at Mr. Pineapple, tearing it apart using only his teeth.] "TP" AK: Through the training of the mind,, Afwa Kilmanjaro has managed to surface greater then any other superstar. Since Afwa cross over from Samoa, Afwa had the intent to become the NSW Heavyweight
Champion, Afwa now crush the vile American race and walk along the path of Powerful tendencies. Mr. Promo, you have a chance now to be like Afwa, you have a chance to be Afwa friend. You try and crush Afwa and Afwa crush you like Afwa does to Mr. Pineapple. Mr. Promo, you and
Afwa shall dominate the NSW Tag Team scene, making us the most destructive, not to mention collussul team ever to wreck havoc on the
SWA, so Mr. Promo, I'll be watching, seeing what you can do, for Afwa knows soon NSW Tag Title be his. Afwa want more and what Afwa want,
Afwa get.
[Afwa continues to chew as he speaks, the juice spilling out down his
Samoan face.] "TP" AK: Afwa Kilmanjaro has seen what excuses all of you are, he has seen that you all try and be like him, a wild, savage, who, thanks to
Richard Starr, Mr. Promo and not forgetting The Hornet and the so called best of the SWA, Zig Zag "T" Green, Afwa has risen above all of you. Now Afwa see that you all try and take his title, Afwa guard it, like it was Afwa friend. Afwa know that if he lets his guard down then
Afwa can be forced to subject himself upon vile Americans and that
Afwa not like..
Afwa now go to eat Pineapple.. Pineapple Afwa friend. All NSW and the rest of the world though shall remember like stones of the sea, each of you shall be tossed into the Lava Crush and then made to fear the words you can't hear.."
[Afwa Kilmanjaro then mutters some words in Samoan, he then spits the remains of the pineapple onto the floor, crushes it between his bare foot and then the scene fades to black.]
