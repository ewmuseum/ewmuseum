Aight.. Well, I'm just making my way back into eW and I did this flash. I've got postive remarks from the peopel that I showed it too.. So.. Please critque honestly and remember, I am not the scum bag named [name withheld].. Just some kid looking for help! :-)
=================================== [Fade in....] [As you wonder in from the darkness that consumed the screen, you begin to wonder what exactly is before you. You see a man that stands six foot six leaning down upon an old stone walk in what appears to be nothing more than a park.]
[Trees are down below the wall, reaching out against the night sky. The moon is shining bright and the out- skirts of an unknown city law below. The man before you seems to be dressed in a trench coat and holding something, but you do not know what.]
[The man, whoever he is, seems to crying or upset at something. The man then shakes his head, his hair moving with it.. And settles down, he then begins to speak in a voice you've never heard before.]
" As you watch me eat, as you begin to wonder why I have come now, at this crucial time in life to cause so much pain, to avenge my brother's death..
Begin to wonder what drives my mind, what makes me do what I do.. What makes me who I am. "
" My nails are painted, my lips covered in women's lipstick.. My face pale white due to make up. The world marks me as the freak but what about themselves? What about the fact that they can't handle the truth and I can?
They are the freak.. I am not. " " The world couldn't accept Shawn, the world wasn't *READY* yet. They couldn't stand his ego, his way of doing things... They wanted him dead and they got their wish. My brother never had the luxury of dying dramatically or loved. He didn't get the dignity of being killed in the ring..
Nah, that wasn't what the world wanted. They wanted to roast marshmallows and have a good time.. "
" There is a saying I live up too... What goes around, comes around. So world, you wanted to take my brother life, you wanted him dead? Well know, you have to live with his deranged brother.. Nathaniel Ryder! "
[Smiles.] " I just hope you realize what nightmare has been released upon your pathetic souls... I just hope you realize how much pain you caused, how much hurt you caused one suicidal mother[BLEEP]er.... And have I mentioned is all coming back in your face? "
[Lil' Ryder or Nathaniel then steps away from the stone wall, dropping a red rose he was holding and walks away. The camera zooms in on the fallen rose as the camera fades way....]
