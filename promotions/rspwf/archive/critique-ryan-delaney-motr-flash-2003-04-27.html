[CRITIQUE] Ryan Delaney - MotR Flash

27 April 2003
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade In.] [There he is--Ryan Delaney, sitting on a wooden chair that seems perfect for your average kitchen. A simple light on a chain hangs above him. The atmosphere is bone chilling. From the camera angle we have (It's showing nothing but Delaney's left side), Delaney is sitting there with not shirt on, wearing nothing but a pair of black jeans cut into shorts (The ends are frayed horribly). A knife is in his hand, and he seems to be staring at it with a lover's gleam. His hair is now completely black, but there is no makeup on his face.
Just the words, "REBIRTH" written on his arm... most likely by a black sharpie.] RD: So, "Showtime", you're the next level? You, Rick BLEEPing Marley, are, in fact, the next level?
[He snorts, as the camera rotates, allowing us to see Delaney's front. Something seems to be one his right shoulder.] I can understand why you would think such a thing. You've busted you balls for years, and accomplished a whole lot. Nothing I can name off the top of my head, but I'm sure somewhere there is something that would make your mother pride. You've done a lot for this sport, so
I've heard from the retirees, but I fail to have noticed anything for myself. But, I trust you Marley. I know there's something somewhere and it's my mistake that I don't know about it.
[A quick smile, but it fades quickly. Delaney's glare is still focused at the knife.] Never mind the trivial bullBLEEP... It doesn't really matter in the end. I just have one question, Marley. That's all... If you are so damn great, and almighty, why did the fans turn their back on you? If you were so unbelievably awesome that without a shadow of a doubt you can walk into this match and whip all of our asses... Why did they run?
Why? [He laughs, almost enjoying his words.] See, I fail to understand that part, Marley. I honestly do. I figure such a star, a man truly worthy of the title "Showtime", would be able to draw a huge audience. I figure a man with such amazing talent you supposedly have would be able to put people in the seats, and make them turn on the TV each week. With all the tears, and sweat, and...
<He smiles, and looks at the camera with nothing but love in his eyes.> blood... <He quickly turns his attention back to the camera> you've shed, shouldn't the fans love you to death, and want nothing more to see you complete your journey to the top?
[The camera rotates from Delaney's front to his right side, and now we see what is one his right shoulder. A cross, freshly carved, and still bleeding. Delaney whips his hands across it, covering his hand with blood. He drops the knife. It clutters as it hits the floor.
He just stares at his hand, shimmering with fresh blood.] Or, just maybe, Marley... It is all some giant fabrication created in your mind. Maybe these glorious battles filled with sweat and blood are nothing more than a lie created in your mind. Maybe all of your talent is upstairs, and no where else. It's possible. I've seen many men just like you, "Showtime", and all of them were really just lying to themselves the entire time.
But what do I know... [He begins licking his fingers.] I'm just some dumb drug addict goth not worthy of your time. [He laughs, as he licks the last drops of blood of his hands.] See you in the ring, Marley. I'll enjoy putting my theory to the test. [Fade Out.]
