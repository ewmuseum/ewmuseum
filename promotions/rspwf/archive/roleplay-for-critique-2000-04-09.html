Roleplay for critique

9 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fade in. The Late Show with David Letterman. Dave is at his desk wearing a grey suit & red tie. Paul has on a purple jacket and is with "The World's
Most Dangerous Band".
DL From the home office in Lincoln, Nebraska. The top 10 reasons to watch professional wrestling.
10. Colourful outfits & catchy music. 9. More entertaining than watching paint dry. Or baseball they're basically the same. 8. She has her soap operas, you have yours. 7. The moves you learned from watching Wreckage might come in handy the next time you get mugged. 6. You can appreciate it for what it is. Soft core gay porn. 5. Watching modern day gladiators in action. 4. Give a "Stunner" to your mother-in-law. 3. Two words: Cold Steel. 2. You can look down on the people who watch tractor pulls & fishing shows. 1. And the number one reason to watch professional wrestling. A**hole!!! Commercials. DL Let's welcome our first guests, soon to be making their debut in WCWF,
Bobby McCann, Greg Davies and the lovely Nightshade.
The audience cheers as Nightshade, "Ice Hammer" and "Ironman" come on stage. The guys are wearing their tights & black leather jackets. Nightshade is stunning in a black velvet dress split up to the hip.
PS Hey this isn't the right night for stupid pet tricks. Greg runs over and puts Paul in a playful headlock then noogies his bald head. GD Is that right Paulie??? (Laughing) Greg releases Schaeffer then goes to the interview area. DL Anyway. So what do you think of the WCWF so far? BM It looks like there should be some good competition. In fact our first match up will be against the US tag champs.
DL Isn't it rather early to be getting a title shot? GD Well Dave, this is a non-title match, unless they changed their minds, and it's not like we just wandered out of the woods.
PS No you did that seven years ago. NS You're a funny guy Paul. It really hurts to laugh in a full body cast. (Everyone laughs.) DL (still snickering) What do you think of Demetri Star and the man called
Dee? BM Well. there might not be a collective to talk about. So we'll split em up. GD Dee. To be completely honest we have a lot of respect for Dee. By the way, if you are watching man, congrats on winning the Hardcore title. Dee is a good wrestler and seems to be a good man. He's a big dangerous dude, but tag-team wrestling is based on trust, and with the so-called "Twisted Stars"
that trust just ain't there. A good tag-team can always beat two individuals. DL And Star. BM He's nothing but a punk! Demetri Star couldn't wipe the nose of a real wrestler. DL What about the rest of the WCWF? And President Jackson's actions of late? NS There are some good teams. The Atomic brothers, Hot T, The Young Bloods, KOA, Dubious Virtues, they're all teams to respect. As far as Jackson's "Dream Team" is concerned. You can count us out. We were there on Duke's side at the Lucan match so the WCWF knows how we feel. BM Speaking of the competition in WCWF. The way I figure it is that the US champs are automatically the number one contenders for the World Tag Championships. So when we beat the US champs on Wednesday Night Wreckage, title or non-title that'll make us the number one contenders.
GD What Bobby's saying is when we win we're challenging the Atomic Brothers for a World Tag-Team Championship match at NO WAY OUT!
DL So there you have it. Well before you go I was wondering if you could give us a little demonstration? We have a couple of "volunteers" to help out.
Two flunkies in padded clothing walk out. Cold Steel moves to an area of the stage where a ring has been set up.
NS I'm sure my boys can accommodate you. Commercials DL Bobby, so how does your finishing move work? BM Well Dave, I usually set it up with a bulldog. He grabs the fat flunky and throws him into the ropes. As the fat flunky comes back "Ice Hammer" leaps from the second rope giving fatty a spectacular bulldog.
NS Then Bobby will lock in what he calls the "Frost Bite" McCann grabs fatty's legs and puts him in a full Boston Crab. It does not take long for fatty to tap out... Ice Hammer then rolls him out of the ring with his foot, without even breaking a sweat.
BM Your turn "Ironman". Greg grabs the skinny guy's arm tosses him to the ropes. Bang! Powerslam, the echo bounces off of the back wall.
NS This is called "The Blizzard" Davies rolls Skinny onto his stomach and locks in his patented Cobra Lock sleeper. Skinny is out cold in about 3 seconds. Greg follows Bobby's example and rolls Skinny out on top of the still inert Fatty.
DL But don't you have a team finishing move? GD Yes it's called "The Big Chill", but there are no more "volunteers". NS We could always use Paul. (Laughs) DL I'm sure Paul will volunteer. PS NO WAY MAN!!! NO WAY!!!!! GD Nightshade, bring Paul over here. Nightshade goes to the bandstand and carries Schaeffer to the ring, kicking and screaming. GD Paul if you co-operate you won't get hurt. DL Yeah right (to Nightshade) Ironman hauls little Paul up into the torture rack as Ice Hammer climbs to the top rope. Bobby leaps from the top rope missing Schaeffer's head by about a half an inch. Greg then sets Paul back on his feet, none the worse for wear.
NS Normally Bobby's elbow would have come down on Paul's the throat or face. DL Well it looks like Dee and Star have their work cut out for them. Thank you for being guests and I'm sure Paul will be much happier without you around. (laughter)
Fade Out.
