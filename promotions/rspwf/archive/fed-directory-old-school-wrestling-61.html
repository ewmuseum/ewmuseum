Old-School Wrestling (OSW) Website: http://www.angelfire.com/retro/osw/ Owner: [name withheld] Description: A indy fed run out of Portland, Oregon by e-wrestling greats Michael Ice and Alex Perkins, Fire and Ice. Housed in a remodeled warehouse, no jumbotron or fireworks... in OSW, the focus is on the RING and what happens there, not what happens backstage. Keynote Members: The names most would recognize... "Fantasy" Brett Young, Magnus Colby, Dark Soul. But we work with a less known roster, being an indy fed, and don't focus on 'stars'. Federation Champion: T.B.A. Flash Show Descripition: Two flash shows... OSW Flashback is hosted by color commentator "Cool" Keith Robinson and is mainly an opinion show to get the character over, and OSW Interactive is hosted by ring announcer/interviewer Jeff Cunningham and previews the upcoming card. Regular Show Description: OSW Insession, held every other week, features from 5-7 matches, full play-by-play commentary. In-rings are limited, and there's no jumbotron to focus on backstage incidents... so everything that happens will take place in the ring. PPV Description: Yet to be officially announced in-character, we will hold supercards (though OSW hasn't signed a PPV contract yet, so they're not technically PPV's) 4 times a year.
