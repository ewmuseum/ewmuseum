[Flash] Tommy Grayson...VOTE FOR ME! :-)

14 February 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey guys...here's an RP I did for L.A.W...and with that, I quote Pat Benatar as I say... Hit me with your best shot. ;-) ================================================================== [SCENE: The gentlemen's club, DUMBARTON II in Queens, New York City. The time is
12:00 AM Midnight. The cameras open on the outside of the club which is under an elevated subway train. Cameras fade inside the club, and we see topless women dancing on the stage. Their breasts are blurred out, of course. The cameras pan around, and finally reaches the back of the club, where we see
"Loose Cannon" Tommy Grayson with two scantily clad beauties on each arm. He has a bottle of Bud sitting on the table in front of him. "Girls, Girls,
Girls" by Motley Crue is playing loudly in the background.]
[APPEARANCE: Tommy is wearing a black RANCID t-shirt, blue JNCO jeans, and black Adidas sneakers. His grungy, crewcut black hair is spiked with gel and he's wearing a pair of silver-rimmed sunglasses with mirror shades. Tommy also appears to have a vague goatee-moustache.]
[Tommy grins towards the camera, and then kisses the blonde girl to his left on the cheek. She gigles and strokes his chest. Tommy gives his trademark smirk towards the camera and begins to speak.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Well, I guess Branden Travers didn't exactly get the kind of punishment I wanted him to get. All because of that _TWO-BIT JABRONIE_,
[intensely] "Ain't Nuttin' Personal" Antawn Carter! [calms down] Well, Carter, I'll tell you one thing: I'm sorry I hit ya in that match, man! But that's the way the wheel turns, and the wheel has turned and run over you.
Then, ya had to come in and interfere in _MY_ match! Tommy Grayson's House of frickin' Pain! You locked on _MY OWN_ finisher on me...the Kata Hajime.
[Tommy takes his right arm from around the brunette and picks up his bottle of
Bud and takes a drink out of it.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Now, Antawn...I know you're gonna say some damn stupid thing like it "ain't nuttin' personal"...but ya know what? When I beat your ass sometime down the line, all I gotta say is...
..._NO_... ..._OFFENSE_... ..._INTENDED_!! [The girls sitting around Tommy giggle. The music in the background has changed to "Give It To Me, Baby" by Rick James.]
TOMMY GRAYSON - Don'tcha just love this song? Now, speaking of givin' it to somebody... [The girls giggle.] TOMMY GRAYSON - Heh...speaking of givin' it to somebody, I'm gonna be givin' it to "Brutal" Brian Floyd. I'm gonna be givin' him an _ASS-KICKIN'_!! Floyd,
I remember you from several months ago...ICW Friday Night Fallout. My debut match there. You frickin' sneak attacked me! But it doesn't matter anymore.
Why? 'Cause Los Angeles Wrestling has given me a chance to kick your stoned-ass from here to Bangladesh! BLONDE - You're gonna bang Ladesh? [Tommy slaps his forehead.] TOMMY GRAYSON - No, no...it's a country in Asia! Jeez! Well, back to the subject. Floyd, I might be partyin' my ass off right now with these two beauties, but when it comes down to business, you can bet that I'm gonna be throwing your ass around the L.A.W Arena...
..._FROM_... ..._BELL_... ..._TO_... ..._BELL_!! By the time I'm done with you, Floyd, you're gonna come out of the match lookin' like Farrah Fawcett...AFTER HER LAST MARRAIGE!!
Oh, and you can bet that our match will be taken "to the extreme," so to speak, Floyd. If you wanna do "extreme," I can frickin' do extreme! Besides, crack'n skulls with chairs and break'n bones with tables has _ALWAYS_ been my..._THANG_!! After I destroy you, Floyd, I'm gonna be...
..._ROCK'N_... ..._LIKE_... ..._A_... ..._FUNK-SHOW_... ..._BROTHA'_!! And Floyd, in conclusion, I only have several words for ya... ...LIVE IF YOU _DARE_!! ..._DIE_ BECAUSE YOU _MUST_!! [Tommy smirks to the camera once again and kisses both girls in his arms. "Sex
Me" by R. Kelly plays...FADE.]
