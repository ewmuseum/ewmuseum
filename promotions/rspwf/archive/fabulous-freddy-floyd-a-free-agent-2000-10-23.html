Yes thats right, I am looking to get back in the game. For those of you who don't know my charachter, Freddy Floyd is a two time ICW (Under James Pillow, then [name withheld]) Television Champion, and a two time Hardcore Champion. I have tons of experience and have held many titles. Here is a short below, contact me with links and info packs at this adress. Thanks a lot ~Mike
Sample RP: [Camera fades to the ringside area. Making her way to the ring is Claudia Battersby. Instead of her usual smiling ways, and waves to the fans, Claudia makes her way out with her down, and carries a microphone in one hand, and an index card in the other. She makes her way up the steps and into the ring, where she begins to adress the crowd]
CB: [Reading] Ahem, Ladies and Gentlemen, it is my ... distinct pleasure to introduce to you a man like no other ... A man who is not only the most intelligent man in the game today, but also the most pysically gifted. A man who I ... Wish was my father?! Uggghhhh ..... A man who when you say
"Fabulous," there is only one .. I give you ... "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd. V/O - Just When You Thought Things Had Setteled .... [ The Lights inside of the arena slowly fade to black, and a blue Spotlight shines down onto the entrance rampway ] V/O - Just When It Looked Like You Didn't Have To Worry .... [ The Curtain parts like the red sea, and two large men wearing black buisness suits enter onto the ramp, and seperate to the sides facing each other. Each holds a full size grand mirror ]
V/O - I Came Along and .... TORE .. THIS .. MOTHER .. DOWN !! [ Gershwin's "Rhapsody in Blue" blasts over the loudspeakers ] [ The Video Wall Comes to life, as Blue Flame explodes from the ring posts ] " F A B U L O U S " F R E D D Y F L O Y D [ Out from behind the curtian holding hands, first steps "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd, wearing a pair of blue bikini style wrestling tights, with the word "Fabulous: scripted along the back. Alongside
Floyd is the lovly and vivacious Alexandria Slikk. She wears a tight black buisness suit, and a revealing white blouse. ]
[ The two look at each other and then give a little peck on the lips, then they simultaniously turn toward the mirrors and begin to fix their respective hair do's, and give a final appearence check. ]
[ After a short time the two finally make there way down the aisle and to the ring, where Floyd holds the ropes up for the lady, and he himself jumps in over the top. ]
[ Floyd then runs over and jumps up on the second turnbuckle arms stretched wide, and takes in a thunderous chorus of boos from the fans. He steps down and walks over to Claudia, and forces her to put the microphone up to his mouth. ]
"Fabulous" FF: Ms. Bladderburst, Ladies and Gentlemen, trash and trashbags alike, the moment you have all been waiting for has finally arrived. The man who is the envy of every wrestler who has ever set foot into a wrestling ring, is standing before you tonight. You see morons, "Fabulous" Freddy Floyd is on the scene, and things are about to be changed forever. Now if you have some questions for me Ms. Bloatedballs I would suggest making it quick, I am a very very busy man.
CB: Its Battersby Mr. Floyd, and my first question has to deal with your alliance with Ms. Slikk.
"Fabulous" FF: First of all Ms. Bludderbins, don't get your panties all in bunch about your damn name, It just takes me a little while to pick up names,
I'm sure you understand. Now as far as my relationship with Ms. Slikk goes, it is strickly buisness. Isn't that right Alexandria?
Alexandria Slikk: Absolutly Freddy. Right now Ms. Battersby, you are looking at the most powerful duo ever to be formed. You see my dear Damien is in the hospital, and I feel that he would want things to go on as if nothing happened, so I'm here to let you all know that theres a new girl in charge, and your looking at her.
CB: Ms. Slikk I'm very sorry about what happened to Damie.. Alexandria Slikk: I'm sure, anyway, as I was saying the two most brilliant minds this sport has ever seen, are standing in this ring together. And what that means for everyone else in the All-Pro Wrestling Federayion is big trouble.
CB: Wait a minute, don't you have anything else to say about Damien Kni.. [Freddy rips the microphone out of Claudia's hand, and pushes her away] "Fabulous" FF: Thank you very much Ms. Bumperbuns, thats about all we need from you, the checks in the mail, now scram!
[A very shaken Claudia makes her way from the ringside area to the back] "Fabulous" FF: Now its time to get down to business. Tonight "The Fabulous Ones" are born, and frankly we have one goal, and one goal only, and thats to bring me straight to the top of the ladder, and put the World Heavyweight
Championship around my waist. It may sound like I am setting some heafty goals early on in my All-Pro Wrestling Federation stay, but what you people don't understand is that I have never been anything but a champion. You don't belive that, then ask Tommy Lionheart about it, see if he can turn his neck from side to side like any normal man, he can't! Becasue I almost broke it! Ask Shawn
"Scorpian" Viper about Freddy Floyd, ask him how long it took to get the feeling back in his right leg after wrestling Freddy Floyd. What I am saying here people is that when Freddy Floyd is in town, it is never good news for anyone else. And with the lovly, beautiful, and most importantly powerful,
Alexandria Slikk by my side, I am all the more dangerous. Alexandria Slikk: Starting tonight we plan to make a lasting impression, and all I can say is that I will not be held responsible for Freddy's actions.
What he does when we aren't together is his personal business. But I will send a warning out to the entire locker room. Be careful boys, this man is not one to be messed with. Lets go Freddy.
[ Gershwin's "Rhapsody in Blue" blasts over the loudspeakers ] [ The two make their way back to the lockeroom to a thunderous chorus of boos ]
