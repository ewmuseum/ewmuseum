Looking for a good fed, please contact me back if there is anything out there... ::The camera crews begin filming on the corner of Sunset Blvd and Highland ave. It is late, and the heavy Hollywood traffic slowly rumbles by. The cameras move over into the front courtyard of Hollywood High. Sitting underneath a tree are pro wrestling stars J.R. Lords and "Lunatic" Ace Custis. J.R. is holding a piece of paper in his lap, and is awkwardly rolling a cigarette. His concentration is peaked. Custis is rocking his head back and forth, and staring out into the busy street. J.R. finishes, and stands up. He is standing there in a tight black Mammajamma t-shirt. His short bleached hair looks uncombed, and the dark roots show on his scalp. It is dark out, but he wears a large black pair of Diesel sunglasses. His old jeans are tucked into a pair of black motorcycle boots. Custis is laying back on the grass, waving his arms and legs in a snow angel motion. He's dressed wearing an old grey lynch mob t-shirt that says LUNI in red letters, and a baggy pair of leather pants. He's wearing loud orange sneakers, and his hair is braided in a cornrow style. He has on a pair of wire rimmed purple lense spectacles. He sits up and turns to his compadre'...
LUNI: Man, I was checkin out the grass. Imagine if every blade of grass was a person man...that'd be alot of real small green people!
J.R.: Hey...these camera guys showed up. I better stay quiet, for fear of any assassination threats. But maybe you could explain to these people what exactly we're doing...
LUNI: WHAT WE DO EVERYONE NIGHT...WERE HERE TO TAKE OVER THE WORLD... J.R.: You idiot, that's my line...remember you're the stupid one? LUNI: I knew that...if you don't know who the fuck we are or your just to dumb to realize it let me explain something. One year ago, the greatest group was esembled. The Heavens Gate hit the scene and put the world on it's ear. The group had some of the best wrestlers out there. From stars like Higor Rosa,
Guru, DvD, Doomsday, War Enesemble to Harvester of Sorrow and the leader and the well liked Hellkid Johnny Lords. Many stayed together working around the world, others fell off or just crashed or some went on killing sprees or just got big egos named Mor-...let's not get into that. Anyway, let's go to the present now. You see this.
(Pointing to the paper J.R. is lighting and passes to him. He takes a hit, then another and passes it to J.R. He coughs a little, but seems to be okay.)
LUNI: As the world just burns up and slowly disolves. We two men continue to deliever as the greatest tandem ever to hit the wrestling scene. Just call us the Rock & Reefer Express. Which isn't to hard to understand. And our purpouse?
I look at all you and see that that you men are nothing but overrated with huge egos. J.R. and Ace are on there way to the top, steping over anyone and everyone. Soon, the end of your to good for everyone lifes are coming. But for now, let's get some CANDY!! WHO WANTS CANDY?!?!
J.R.: My candy starved little friend, calm your urges for just a few more minutes. I know plenty of candy dealers in Hollywood. I just want to give a call out to all wrestling federations. Once upon a time there were two cruiserweight wrestling superstars that overachieved in every fed they ever competed in. The Luni and the kid. Our careers have certainly been proud and successful. Now we are content with our singles success, and both wish to achieve championship status in the tag ranks. Is there a tag team out there that can match the determination and ingenuity of these two? I don't think so.
Not this year. Boys and girls, it's time for...Generation Death!! ( Luni does a cartwheel in the grass, then runs into the street. A red convertible corvette screaches to a halt just in front of him. Sitting in the car is a beautiful blonde and a hot redhead. Both girls are dressed scantilly in lacy dresses. Luni sticks his head in the car, then motions for
Lords,smiling. J.R. leaves the camera location, heads towards the street, then hops in the back of the car. Luni jumps in between the front seats and lays a louissianna liplock on the blonde. She revs the engine on the car, and
Generation Death dissappears into the Hollywood night...)
