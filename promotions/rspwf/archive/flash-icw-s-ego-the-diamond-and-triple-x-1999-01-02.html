[FLASH] ICW's EGO: The Diamond and Triple X.

2 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Scene- The scene is a hospital. Nurses and doctors are speeding around the halls keeping busy. Green plants are all over the place to keep the place
smelling nice, despite it's depressing atmosphere. One old man is wheeling himself around the halls in a wheel chair and a few families are heading towards the rooms of their friends and family members. The camera moves up the hall and turns right, heading down the next hall to Room 319. Inside you can hear a voice yelling.]
VOICE - GET ME A SPECIALIST FOR GOD'S SAKE!! [The camera moves into the room to see what's going on. One man is standing at the foot of the bed and another is laying on the bed holding his forhead. A glare coming through the windows blocks any facial details of the two men.]
VOICE - AND CLOSE THE CURTAINS ALREADY!!! I'M GONNA GO BLIND!!! [The man by the bed walks over and closes the curtains. As the glare comes off of the room he is revealed to be "The Diamond" Shane Diamond. He is wearing a pair of baggy dark green Dockers and a black knit sweater. He also has on black leather belt and a pair of black suede shoes. The Diamond also has on his silver cross chain and has his hair...well, spiky. Then the camera moves to the man lying in the bed. It is Triple X. He has his hands pressed tightly against his forhead. He is wearing a pair of baggy Adidas Tear-Away pants and a matching Adidas shirt. He is acting like he's dying. "The Diamond" tries to calm him down, but is unsuccessful.]
THE DIAMOND - Calm down, X-Man. The woman in the room beside you is having a damn child...try squeezing a thing the size of a watermelon through you know what.
TRIPLE X - [in a nagging tone] Well the woman next door and her stupid kid can just kiss my white ass. I'm the one who got half my head bit off by a midget!
THE DIAMOND - You just got stitches. The Diamond has had stitches. IT'S
NOTHING BIG. The blood...it's soaking through the bandages, you're acting like a crybaby. The Diamond knows best.
TRIPLE X - Yeah, you're not the one who's sitting here bleeding like a water fountain and has to face an ear-biting psychopath next week! Then I have to wrestle again an hour later!! I better get some overtime pay for this! Oh and then I've got to worry about John Beverly coming up at Winter Warfare, and then on top of all of that, I've got stupid little midgets pretending to be six year old boys to worry about! What did I do to get all of this?
THE DIAMOND - Listen, man, they're pushing you to the top here in the ICW. IT
IS YOUR TIME. So, stop acting like a wuss and get back up and go. The
Diamond...he's been in street fights, Torando matches, hour man matches and all that crap. BUT... he's still standing here. With scratches, sure. But, The
Diamond knows talent when he sees it and that is why you're with me, X-Man.
That's why you're part of EGO. You're gonna stop whining and get your ass outta bed and start training for that match with Grayson next week and John
Beverly after that. FOCUS MAN....FOCUS.
TRIPLE X - Okay, you want me to stop being such a pussy? FINE! But this better pay some dividends in the long run. Grab me a band-aid will ya? I'll put it on quick and then we can go get some grub. I'm starving.
THE DIAMOND - The Diamond will get you ten bandaids, man. [The Diamond smiles, pleasured his little chat worked and reaches for the band aids. He hands one to Triple X and looks back to the camera.]
THE DIAMOND - See this, X-Man, they saw you crying. THOSE FREAKS SAW IT. TRIPLE X - I don't care what those freaks saw. I'm the Continental Champion! I'm being worked like a slave, and hell, if I want to bitch once in awhile I will. But the bitching is over. It's time to get focused on Grayson.
[He looks at the band-aid. Triple X tosses it back to The Diamond.] TRIPLE X - Dude, I want Snoopy. THE DIAMOND - Scooby is better. [Diamond grins and hands Triple X another band-aid. He then stretches and runs a hand over his slight stubble.]
THE DIAMOND - Big night for EGO Friday was...all because of The Diamond's birthday. TRIPLE X - That and the fact that for the second time, I beat Westside Walavan! WOO!! GO ME!! That makes the series record, Wally a measly one, and me a whopping TWO!! You can't beat me Walavan, just give it up. I should be the International Champion right now. But no, I gotta do a little more work I guess to get to the top of the mountain. So now I gotta fight Hanibal Lector, a.k.a. Tommy Grayson. What's he call that tounge thing again Shane?
THE DIAMOND - Something about "The People's Tongue" but Sam done her job and bit that damn thing off. Wow, Sam stuck up for herself, hit Grayson with a chair...and well, let the good times roll. She's still with Grayson and The
Diamond isn't saving her. Let me tell you this, though, Grayson. You say you hate me...THE DIAMOND FEELS THE SAME. He thinks the feeling is mutual. You think you're gonna beat me? Good damn luck. You're gonna have to get through this man first.
TRIPLE X - First thing I plan on doing to "The Loose Cannon" is taking "The
People's Tongue" and cutting what's left of it off. Then I'm gonna wipe my ass with it Tommy! You're gonna kiss my ass, FRENCH STYLE!! Whether you like it or not this Friday night on Fallout!
THE DIAMOND - Whoa...whoa...whoa...you're getting sick there, Triple X. TRIPLE X - I don't think you've ever called me that before. But you're calling me sick? The man bit your ear off, shoved his gingivitis covered tongue down Sam's throat on numerous occasions and he's capturing kids and you're calling me sick. I'm just showing Tommy that two can play at this game!! He likes shoving his "People's Tongue" down people's throats? Well, this Friday, Triple X is going to take his "People's Right Boot" and shove it right up the "People's Ass" of Tommy Grayson! And then I'll take my foot out of his keister and make "The People's Tongue" lick it clean!
THE DIAMOND - Well, you do that and The Diamond will just kick his ass when it comes time. I've said this before and I'll say it again...GRAYSON WILL BE
KICKING DIAMONDGASMS. When it all comes down to it, I'll retain my precious gold.
TRIPLE X - Shane if you're planning on getting a piece of Grayson you'll have to pick up the remains after this Friday cuz I've got a little ass to kick.
Tommy Grayson always wants to whine... [doing a Grayson impression] "Sam stood me up!! I didn't have a date for the prom!! WAAAA!!" [normal voice] Just cry me a damn river Tommy. What's next? [back to Grayson voice] "Sam stole my bike when we were six so now I'm going to try to kill her!!! [normal voice]
Just because you're position as equipment manager of the chess team didn't get you a date for the prom doesn't mean you go around kidnapping and biting varios sense accessories off of people's faces. Now can we go get our eat on Shane?
I'm starving! THE DIAMOND - Hold up. The Diamond really thinks Grayson is a fruitcake...with plenty of nuts. Yet, he's still having blasts from the past. Let's take a ride back to the past...shall we? You're a good wrestler, Grayson. I take nothing away from you. You knocked The Diamond's ass out on Friday...yet, you still didn't win. Why is that? Because of the way you treat Sam. As X-Man said, no one cares about who broke up with you and who didn't. THE DIAMOND
WILL BREAK YOUR NECK. [The Diamond, now red faces starts looking around the room. Triple X takes him by the arm and pushes him out of the door.]
TRIPLE X - Now could we please go get some food? THE DIAMOND - After talking about how you're going to do all that to Grayson and knowing he's bit my ear off? FOOD AT A TIME LIKE THIS? The Diamond could go for some coffee.
TRIPLE X - Point is... can we go to the caffeteria? I haven't eaten since yesterday. THE DIAMOND - Yeah...yeah. You lead the way. TRIPLE X - Cameraman, you're coming with me. I've got a lot more to say. [Triple X leads the way down the hall followed by The Diamond and the cameraman. Triple X blows by some old guy and almost knocks him over.]
THE DIAMOND - Hungry? TRIPLE X - Very funny. [Triple X continues walking until he reaches the elevator and pushes the down button. After several seconds of waiting Triple X starts complaining.]
TRIPLE X - Can't this thing go any faster? THE DIAMOND - Kick it...maybe that'll help. [Triple X backs up and gets a running start and kicks the elevator doors as hard as he can. He falls over holding his foot.]
TRIPLE X - I THINK I BROKE MY FOOT!!! [The doors open and The Diamond walks in.] THE DIAMOND - It worked, told ya The Diamond knows best. [Triple X crawls in after him and The Diamond presses the "C" button.] TRIPLE X - The cafeteria has it's own floor? THE DIAMOND - The Diamond has his own floor as well. Why we gotta eat her? I want McDonald's.
TRIPLE X - I thought you just wanted some coffee because you're not hungry. THE DIAMOND - The elevator ride made The Diamond hungry. TRIPLE X - I don't want McDonald's. This is closer. Do you see a McDonald's nearby? THE DIAMOND - Right across the street. TRIPLE X - [looks out the window.] DAMN IT! You want McDonald's? THE DIAMOND - Come on, we can play in the balls! AND GOD DAMN IT...they better give The Diamond his toy today.
TRIPLE X - Okay, across the street we go. [Triple X walks towards the door with The Diamond close behind. Triple X storms out into the street and almost gets hit by a car.]
TRIPLE X - WATCH IT ASSHOLE!!! THE DIAMOND - The Diamond thinks someone needs a nap. TRIPLE X - Whatever. [Triple X starts walking again and he cuts off some old woman on his way to the door and opens it up for The Diamond who lets it close on the old woman's face.
They approach the counter.] THE DIAMOND - YES...The Diamond wants three Big Macs with EVERYTHING. Two orders of fries, a Happy Meal with Chicken Nuggets and a large chocolate Milk
Shake.
TRIPLE X - Shit! How you gonna eat all that? THE DIAMOND - The Diamond is a growing boy. TRIPLE X - [under his breath] The Diamond is in his mid-twenties. [Triple X approaches the counter.] TRIPLE X - Uhhh... number 8, Super Size it. REGISTER WORKER - That comes to $28.79. TRIPLE X - Jesus Shane! You're picking this one up. THE DIAMOND - It's all The Diamond. [The Diamond pulls out a wad of bills and takes a money clip off them. He starts flipping through it as Triple X looks at him.]
TRIPLE X - Do you even have a bank account? THE DIAMOND - Sure, The Diamond has ten bank accounts. TRIPLE X - Why do you carry all that money around? THE DIAMOND - Uhh... because I'm The Diamond? No idea, really. Just to make me look good I guess. Even though, I can't look much better.
[Triple X looks at him like he's an idiot.] THE DIAMOND - What? TRIPLE X - Well, you've got your money out... ever gonna pay the lady? THE DIAMOND - Nope... [puts money in pocket] You made fun of The Diamond. You pay yourself.
TRIPLE X - Fine. [Triple X digs into his pocket and pulls out a twenty and a ten.] TRIPLE X - Keep the change, [points to The Diamond] I stole it from him earlier anyway. THE DIAMOND - Wow, you let the bitch keep like twenty cents. The Diamond thinks Triple X is a little pissy pissy today!
[Diamond turns from Triple X and to the woman behind the counter.] THE DIAMOND - HEY WOMAN...I better get a toy this time and I damn well better have extra milk in MY SHAKE!
TRIPLE X - I'm the pissy one? Besides... you would cry too if it happened to you. [Both start singing the rest of the song.] TRIPLE X - Okay that's enough of that. THE DIAMOND - The Diamond just wants good service. TRIPLE X - It's McDonald's... don't get your hopes up. THE DIAMOND - Follow The Diamond. He knows where to sit. [The Diamond smiles as Triple X follows him. He walks to the outside onto the play land where a few kids play. Diamond turns around and shows off the place.]
THE DIAMOND - Like it? TRIPLE X - That sign says you can't enter if you're over four feet tall. You're taller than that on your knees. THE DIAMOND - Since when did The Diamond start obeying the rules? TRIPLE X - Good point. THE DIAMOND - Since EGO came to their damn place...I heard the reporters are gonna be here soon.
TRIPLE X - Then let's hope the food gets here sooner. But anyway, you've been in the ring with him... what should I look for against Tommy Grayson this
Friday?
THE DIAMOND - Expect him to bite you...try and kill you, practically. TRIPLE X - So he's hungry in the ring? Maybe I should save him one of your Big Macs. Think he'd like that? Sure tastes better than my ear... even though
I do wash with Scope!
THE DIAMOND - Just dodge him if he comes after you...you'll beat him. THE DIAMOND HAS CONFIDENCE. TRIPLE X - Yeah, I have confidence in me too... first I'll give him a left... and a right. And then another left just for good measure. And then I'll give him the X-Factor just cuz it's fun. Tommy Grayson, you can't bite me this Friday, some little bastard midget beat ya to the punch. Now all of your offense is gone. So get ready... cuz I don't care about your "push." Triple X is going to the damn top of the ICW!!! I want my International Title!! I earned it on Fallout!!!
[The Diamond glares at Triple X.] TRIPLE X - I mean The Diamond's International Title!!! BUT I STILL EARNED IT ON FALLOUT!! THE DIAMOND - BY GOD...X-MAN...that is THE DIAMOND'S TITLE. Whether you earned it or not, THE DIAMOND OWNS IT.
TRIPLE X - Yeah, yeah, whatever you say. But let's not worry about that now. I need you watching my back Friday night, incase anybody from DI. tries to get inolved. Or maybe, just maybe, the KING OF KHAKIS will make his presence felt.
THE DIAMOND - No prob, Bob. The Diamond will help out his fellow stablemate...I also need help from you. Jasmine wants a more exspensive ring.
What should I get her? We are engaged, ya know. TRIPLE X - This doesn't take a real genius... get her a more expensive ring! Duh. So when's the big day? THE DIAMOND - No day set yet. Jas said she wanted it to be special. TRIPLE X - Vegas? THE DIAMOND - Naaaaah...The Diamond and Jas are more classier than that. TRIPLE X - Reno? Wait that's a step down. THE DIAMOND - I was thinking Paris. TRIPLE X - They have "quickies" in Paris? THE DIAMOND - We don't want quickies. We want a biggie...like HEY! MY FOOD! [Suddenly, a man walks through the glass door, carrying the food on trays. He sits it down on The Diamond's and Triple X's table.]
THE DIAMOND - IT'S ABOUT DAMN TIME! TRIPLE X - They didn't forget your priceless toy did they? [Triple X grabs his nuggets and fries and drink.] TRIPLE X - Damn! I hate when my Sprite tastes like Dr. Pepper! Why does that always happen here?
THE DIAMOND - TOY TIME! [The Diamond looks at Triple X and cocks his head to the right before tearing into his "happy meal." He pulls out a toy...The Bug's Life lady bug. Diamond frowns and throws it to the side.]
THE DIAMOND - Done got that one. TRIPLE X - Give it to Jas. It's simplistic yet romantic... women love that shit. THE DIAMOND - Jas already has it, too. [A man walks down the street wearing a pair of khaki slacks and a Superman logo t-shirt.] TRIPLE X - Look!!! [points to the man] How funny is that? THE DIAMOND - [stunned] BEV! HEY BEV! COME BACK! TRIPLE X - I don't think it's really him Shane. Calm down. I think we call it a coincidence. THE DIAMOND - The Diamond thinks that Beverly is a smart ass who can't GET OVER the past. TRIPLE X - I think Bev is a DUMB ASS who can't get over the past. I mean,
"smart ass" Shane? He's not the brightest guy you know. He thinks he's going to actually beat me.
THE DIAMOND - That's true. He's gonna beat you about as good as Grayson is gonna beat me. It'll never happen...because THE DIAMOND won't let it.
TRIPLE X - We could hold on to these titles until the year 2000 if we damn well please. But I think we're going to be moving up in the world sooner than later. But there's not really a whole lot of room from improvement when you're as good as EGO. Tommy Grayson, number one contendor is all you'll ever be.
Maybe you can have the Continental Title if I forfeit it in the long run. But
I doubt that too... Sam will just kick your ass again. John Beverly, maybe you can be the International Champion again...
[Triple X looks at Shane.] TRIPLE X - AAAAAAHHHH HAHAHAHA!! Yeah right you might just be a champion ever again. THE DIAMOND - That's The Diamond's belt that fat ass Walavan is carrying around. He challenged me and we're going to fight in February and I am going to win MY TITLE BACK. THE DIAMOND'S TITLE. [drinks milkshake] Damn it...I still didn't get enough milk. Anywho. Walavan, you're barking up the wrong tree.
TRIPLE X - Maybe James wants your milkshake. Word has it in the SDT locker room that he's great at sucking.
THE DIAMOND - Oooooh...good one, X-Man. Good one. So, how long have we been on the air? TRIPLE X - About a year! This is getting to be long. Need to change the tape there cameraman? Cuz I still got a little more left in me.
THE DIAMOND - LET THE DIAMOND TALK. Now, Wally Monster, you upset me on Kennedy's Chamber. First off, you hit on the babe...even though The Diamond is engaged, Kennedy was his first crush. Kennedy lit up my life. [sniffs] AND YOU
TRIED TO MAKE HER LIKE YOU! BUT...fact of the matter is that The Diamond is going to kick your ass for it. Not only for the title, but because I didn't get to pin you in that ladder match. Sweet revenge is what it's gonna be.
Call it a ridicule. I'll call it my own venom. Wally, you don't realize who're you messing with, eh? [evil smirk] YOU...YOU ARE NOTHING COMPARED TO THE
DIAMOND. TRIPLE X - You can't even beat me! The Diamond jumpstarted my career and I kicked your Big Wally Ass! How you think you're going to beat The Diamond?
THE DIAMOND - He can't beat me...the president is on our side, X-Man. Pillow can't play politics anymore.
TRIPLE X - He couldn't beat you when Pillow was calling the shots, cuz not even Pillow can deny greatness... and Shane... you're greatness. And me... I'm pretty damn close to it if I do say so myself. But ya know who's not too great? The Motor City Maniacs. They're the weak link of Pillow's team for
Friday night. Wait, Pillow's the weak link... wait... Walavan's the weak link... THEY'RE ALL WEAK LINKS BECAUSE THEY ALL SUCK!!!
THE DIAMOND - THEY ARE WEAK AND WE WILL PROVE IT NEXT WEEK! Whoa, two words the same in one sentence...yet with different meanings. How much more we got,
X-Man? The Diamond wants to eat. TRIPLE X - Then eat... I'll wrap it up. [The Diamond smiles and looks down at his food. He begins digging in at Triple
X looks to the camera and speaks.]
TRIPLE X - This Friday, you're looking at a busy boy. Triple X has to take out the trash against Tommy Grayson, worry about John Beverly, and then on top of all of that... he has to step into the ring, and team up with The Diamond,
Jasmine, and GOD ALMIGHTY HIMSELF!! The BOSS!! Jax Jordan. Maybe we can get Kennedy to give us a pep-strip or something. That would get me all hyper. But
Grayson, you've got the misfortune of being first on my list, and incase you forgot, I'm the hottest, hardest working star in the ICW today damn it! Two matches in one night, and looking over my shoulder for Bev! I'm the man!! And he's THE DIAMOND!!! And this Friday night... EGO and Jordan are...
TRIPLE!!! XXX!!! TREME!!! TRIPLE X - Did I set that up well Shane? THE DIAMOND - You're learning, good man. You're learning. Now, what is life without no play? All work and no play can suck. THE DIAMOND IS READY TO
PLAY... [looks around] in the balls! Simply because...he's The Diamond.
TRIPLE X - You go have your fun, I've got some food to eat. [The Diamond sprints towards the ball pit and dives in. Triple X steals one of his Big Macs as he is away.] TRIPLE X - Don't tell him, kay? [Fade to black.]
