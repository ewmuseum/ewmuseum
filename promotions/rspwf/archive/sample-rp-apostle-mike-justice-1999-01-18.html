[Sample RP] "Apostle" Mike Justice

18 January 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yeah ... i'm bored. Gimme some feedback, I guess. Andj [ --- ] [Kelowna, British Columbia... Fade in on what appears to be the bedroom of a very well-furnished hotel room - the exact details of the room are hard to make out, due to the lack of light. The room's current resident has drawn the blinds, and while the sunlight does manage to sneak in around the edges of the window, it is not able to banish the serene darkness within.
The outlines of several objects can be made out, however with what weak light there is. A dresser of sorts runs along one wall ... a free standing entertainment center adjacent from it. Parallel to that is a king-sized bed, which currently supports a solitary figure ... Mike
Justice.]
MJ - "It's always good to step away from things for a moment, to stop and take a close look at the world around you. To focus once again on the path the Lord has set before you."
[He pauses for a moment, letting the silence creep back into the room, only to shatter it once again.]
MJ - "Alex Grey thinks he's hurting Salvation by signing the one symbol of morality in this wretched federation to a match against five lost souls. The same five ungrateful fools I attempted to save last week.
Why must you insist on making this more difficult than it has to be?" [A slight chuckle breaks free from it's prison, hollow and devoid of any humor, but a chuckle none-the-less.]
MJ - "Maybe the mindless prattle being regurgitated from the SCRA front offices has finally rubbed off on each of you.
Justin Love, Pedro Kozaro ... the true feelings in your hearts, the real desire to excell here, is being shadowed by some empty promise of grandeur made to you.
Al E. Gator ... a man who could very easily be saved by the
Lord, a man who has the potential, the spirit ... yet allows himself to be trapped, to be held back from reaching the next level.
Ivan Ramius ... [he shakes his head] A fool so blind, you'd think he was the third spawn of Chris Douglas.
And let us not forget, the Cowboy ... a man who's allowed himself to wallow in jealousy, self pity and hatred for one man, that he's lost sight what he stands for. He's forgotten his own purpose."
[Justice slowly pulls himself up to a sitting position, his hands resting on his knees.] MJ - "It saddens me ... watching as Alex Grey manipulates each of you as if you were pawns on a chessboard.
Don't you realize what's happening? Don't you see the big picture? You were set up - face the facts. Neither Grey nor Mota truly thought you would come out victorious in that Battle Royal.
Wasn't it obvious that the Butcher would win? Poor, poor Otto ... I'd expect your countrymen, those who chose you as Germany's 'Premium
Athlete' will have to reconsider their decision, considering you're some sort of maniac now."
[A second chuckle escapes, just as empty as its predecessor.] MJ - "But forgive me, I digress... The five of you walked into one trap this past Scraps, and each of you fell victim - hook, line, and sinker. I can't help but wonder ...
Would they try and sucker you twice in as many weeks? " [He pauses again, letting his words sink in to the minds of those watching and listening.]
MJ - "Perhaps I'm wrong ... Alex Grey isn't capable of doing such a devious thing. Or is he? [he lowers his head as if in thought, then looks back at the camera]
Aside from that, might I inquire as to how the meeting amongst the five of you has been going this past week? Surely you've been able to put aside all your differences in order to concoct some sort of battleplan. A plan to put me, the man so many of you seem to look up to, in his rightful place?
Those five-to-one odds are really concrete - aren't they? The collective force of five men ... their bodies, minds and souls working as one ... can defeat this one, lone man in any outing - can't it?"
[Justice swings his legs over the side of the bed, pulling himself up to his feet. He takes a few small steps away from the bed, stretching his arms out as he approaches the only source of light in the room, the window. His massive form is outlined in a pale glow as he continues speaking.]
MJ - "You may not see it, gentlemen, and I'm quite sure you don't. Take a long, hard look at the propaganda spewing from the mouths of Alex
Grey and his demon-lackey, Mota. Then review the history of the man who brought the light of righteousness into a land once blackened with hate. The man who dismantled the machine of the Devil himself - the toppler of the mighty Syndicate.
It's time for my five opponents, along with the entire base of the SCRA, to ask themselves one simple question ..."
[Grabbing the curtains at their center, Justice whips them open, allowing a blaze of sunlight to flood the room through the frost-covered window. He stands with his back to the camera, and slowly raises his arms. Clenching his fists, he puts one on either side of the window and leans forward, his head bowed.
We see him from the waist up - his long black hair spills freely over his shoulders, his muscular arms bulging from his sleeveless black t-shirt. Barely audible, Justice finishes his sentence, in a manner that would lead one to believe he's posting this question to himself as well.
] MJ - "What do you believe?" [Fade.] [name withheld] President of the GLCW Handler of: "Apostle" Mike Justice - [SCRA] Rick Kilgore - [LWC] The Kentucky Fried Outlaws - [Currently only competing in the IIeW & the
Reed Cup]
