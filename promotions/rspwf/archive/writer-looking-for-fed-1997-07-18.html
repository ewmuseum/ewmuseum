Writer looking for Fed

18 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi, I'm Ryan Z. I'm currently looking for a position to write for a Federation. I'm available to do 1 card per week, any day. I've been writing for 7 years. Below is an example of my work. John Bandle: Hello everybody and welcome back to the AAA Wrestling Federation. Let's get to ring side for our next match... Dan Morin: Let's... Announcer: This next match is scheduled for one fall with a fifteen minute time limit. Introducing first, from Detroit, Michigan and weighing in at 295 pounds, Bad Boy Brrrrrrrruuuuuuiiiiissssseeeeerrrrrrr!! ["Machinehead" by Bush starts roaring as Bad Boy walks down the entrayway towards the ring in his usual attire. His manager, Jim Thompson, flanks him. Big crowd pop] JB: Wouldja look at that. I say he's gained a couple of pounds since his last match four weeks ago. DM: Yeah, look at that, got a little flabby. I'm wondering if his opponent will take advantage of that bad left knee... ANN: And his opponent, from Bangkok, Thailand, weighing 275 pounds, Tiger Fury!!!!! [A traditional song of Thai Kickboxing music erupts, just as booing is ensued by the crowd] JB: Alright, both wrestlers are in the ring...They seem to be sizing up up eachother. They lock up but disengage. DM: Looks like Fury wants some room for some of his Thai kicks. They're circling eachother again... JB: Fury, comes in with an onslaught of kicks. He nails Bad Boy with a high sweeping Savate kick, very unorthodox for Muay Thai. He spins and nails bad Boy with a back kick to the groind. Bad Boy's in trouble.. JB: Nice kicks by Fury. He seems to be in top form. DM: I agree, and OOOOH! JB: Tiger Fury has Bad Boy Bruiser in a DDT position and is slamming knee thrusts into Bad Boy's chest. Ref starst to count....6...7...8...9.......Fury stops and irish whips Bad Boy into the ropes....misses with a clothesline....Bad Boy running back, Fury misses with a clothesline again. DM: Here comes Bad Boy. Springboard Moonsault off the rebound on the ropes, strange manuever. Flies through the air and misses! Bad Boy is down... JB: Here comes Fury, lifts up Bad Boy. Sets him up into Tiger Suplex position....but, OOOH! Bad Boy; rollover with the cover! 1....2.....KICKOUT! *Crowd Pop* DM: Nice move by Bad Boy. He knows that Fury is quicker and stronger than him, he'll have to use his technical abilities in this match. JB: Bad Boy now has the advantage. Sets him up...WOW! Dragon Suplex! That sends Fury into the mat. He gets Fury again...sets him up....Piledriver into the mat! *Crowd Pop* DM: Slight variation on the normal Piledriver, more like a hybrid Powerbomb/Piledriver. See the bridge, he uses his weight more than the slam. JB: Okay, they're both back up. Now Fury, with an elbow to the face! A right cross, a left cross, uppercut! Bad Boy is reeling now! Claw rake by Fury, he drew blood! DM: Look at those scratch marks. JB: Now Fury comes in and grabs Bad Boy, and puts him down with a Russian Legsweep. Here comes Tiger. He leaps atop Bad Boy and begins elbowing him in the face. Bad Boy's covered in blood. The crowd is booing madly. DM: Ooh god no...he's dragging Bad Boy to the toprope... JB: He throws Bad boy into the air from the top rope, intercepts him in the air and slams into him with a flying knee thrust. He twists around and slams him home in a double arm, belly to back Tiger Suplex! TIGER GENOCIDE! The count, 1...2.......3.... DM: It''s over, its all over. A totally one sided, dominated match by Tiger Fury, who is your winner. JB: G'nite folks!
