UCW Now Open

26 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi All, I would just like to announce the start of UCW, Underground Championship Wrestling, and invite anybody looking for a change in e-feds or a new one to come take a look and feel free to apply. What do we offer? Well we're keeping it basic, because when e-feds get too large or complex, they fall. 5 Titles are available as I write with one show a week to begin with, expanding to two with roster sizes. The first show is solely for the World Title and will occur very shortly. We would like to say that we're keeping e-fedding in reality but adding a controlled Hardcore environment much similar to that of the late ECW, whilst crossing this product with storylines. http://ucw-world.cjb.net http://ucwonline.hypermart.net Feel Free to take a look and thanks for your time,
