LWA - London based, indy fed - SOMETHING UNIQUE!

26 May 2000

/\ \ /\ \ __/\ \ /\ _ \ \ \ \ \ \ \/\ \ \ \ \ \ \_\ \ \ \ \ __ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ \ __ \ \ \ \_\ \ \ \ \_/ \_\ \ \ \ \/\ \ \ \____/ \ `\___x___/ \ \_\ \_\ \/___/ '\/__//__/ \/_/\/_/ =====London=====Wrestling======Alliance====== Welcome to the London Wrestling Alliance. The federation is based out of England, but specifically based out of London. The federation is a small time, _very_ indy based federation. Therefore, this won't be your norm, this won't be flashy, this won't be outlandish. You won't see crowds of thousands upon thousands. You won't see arena's that will boggle your mind, in fact, you might not even be lucky enough to see arena's period. What you will see, are some of the hungriest, and dedicated wrestlers in the entire world.
Whatever it takes, whatever blood must be spilt, whatever careers must be ended, that is what the LWA is about. Techinical wrestling at it's best!
============================================= President: [name withheld] Yes, that [name withheld], the one, and the only. Handler of Tommy "Lord" Lionheart, in UWF, UEW, and LVPWA. I have been in e-feds for 3 + years, and truly know how to make this work, THIS TIME. Things will be different, because, after all, this is a very, very different fed. At the same time, I won't playcate to "names", because this isn't a "name" fed, this is small town, small feel fed. I promise to do the best damn job I can to make this fed what I want it to be...FUN! That is the key guys, above all, this fed will be fun, and that is all that matters!
============================================= Character Presidents: James and Christopher (Chris) Smith Twin brothers, starting this federation on their own, out of their pocket. James will play the face role, while Chris will play the heel role. Both individuals have been scouting the world over about this industry. They both see the greener pastures that this can led to, yet realize the vast financial amount of capital this venture would take. With that in mind, they gathered the money, and began to create the idea in their head. They wanted to create a federation that favored techinicians of the sport, not the current modern day trend of high production, snooze's fest. James is all aboard for the idea, while Chris is leery of the lack of production value for this alliance.
============================================== Announcers: Brian Buck Buck is a loyal fan of the sport, and the industry. In contrast to the current trend of modern day announcers, he came to James Smith, and pitched to the him the idea of being a commentator. He loves the crowd favorites, their style, their heart, and their high moral standing. He realzies that other commentators get paid HIGH, TOP DOLLAR money for their services, but he doesn't care. He does this for the love, the love of the position, the sport, and more importantly, his friendship to James Smith.
Chris Smith (Co-Prez) Smith doesn't particularly like this position. After all, why would a Co-Prez accept such a lowly position as commentator? Well, quite simply, money is tight, and they have no other choice but to have Chris co- announce.
This angers Chris at will, and causes his trapped anger towards James. He calls em' like he sees em', never fearing from speaking his mind, and his true feelings. He hates the face, hates the man that playcates to the fans, and loses sight of the ultimate goal, victory. He plays a very, very heelish announcer role, and ultimately, those are his true feelings.
============================================== Referees: Mickey Dexter(head ref) Mickey is as straight forth as there is. Too the book, and too the tee, he follows the rules, and will bust your ass if you don't as well
Ron Fernandez Ron, ahh, what a story, Ron is currently being pursued by officials on the Carribbean, on some hefty charges. He is in hiding from his own country, but like the others, love this industry, and befriended James and Chris. He loves women, money, and the "high roller" image, leaves him to be pretty vulernable, wouldn't ya say?
Peter Dalley and Dave Downs Your run_of_the_mill officials. They work the undercard, and officiate in a very correct, yet sloppy fashion. Dexter is working with them, if they change, and how they change will be up to them in time!
============================================= Interview Team: Ian Hornsby The "dirt" in the LWA will no doubt come from him, and his snooping ways. He is the voice of the LWA when it comes to reaching your point across.
Born in London, the venerable Hornsby will remind you of other "interviewers" but will not be bullied, nor will he stop until the truth is known about a particular subject. James Smith(Co-Prez) Ahh yes, another loving job for James. He will assist in some backstage interviewing, but only when needed. He really and truly loves this job.
After all, he is a president, they don't hold mics, they get interns to do that, and alot of other catchy things with their mouths as well ( :) ).
None the less, he will do what it takes to gain support for his federation! ============================================== Ring Announcer: Anthony Brock Brock is a classic Brit. Very mild mannered, very formal, and very straight and arrow. He will only do the task at hand, nothing more, nothign less. He will attempt to rile the crowd, but hell, he will fail, because he is a square, and stiff Brit!

================================================ Titles: LWA National Championship The big daddy, the big dog, the one and only. If you battle yourw way through the competition, you get to own this lovely piece of gold, errr sorta gold. Much like the rest, these belts aren't the greastest things in the world either, but they stand for the work you will put forth in this federation. They stand for the blood, sweat, [you know the rest]. This is
_the_ title in the LWA, the one that all people will shoot for, but only one will be lucky enough to hold.
LWA London Championship Unlike the National Title, this title _must_ be defended each, and every week. This is our version of the "tv" title, and with that in mind, this title is essential to pull off the goal of turning this federation into a successful one. The champion must possess the physical, and mental abilities to fight each week, no matter what might hold he/she back from doing so.
This is the title that will be defended with the highest frequency, and thusly, must be defended with heart, and will.
LWA Cruiserweight Championship The little guys, the _not_ so large fighters that enter this federation, they too have something to put their hearts into. This division will possess men that are willing to take suicide-like bumps, because, in reality, fans love the cruiser more then any other title. The requirements are simple, under the weight of 225 pounds, and absolutely no conscience in, and outside the ring. You guys will entertain like no other...we will depend highly on all of you.
LWA National Tag Team Championsip The tags, seldom used, seldomly promoted correctly. Well, in the LWA, we love the tag teams, because, after all, our fans actually enjoy seeing the mental side of the sport. The quick tags, the team work, even the double team, _they_ crave it. So, naturally, we will need you to be some of the backbones that support this federation. In time, you will see the spotlight, the limelight, but that comes, like this everything else, along with federation success.
