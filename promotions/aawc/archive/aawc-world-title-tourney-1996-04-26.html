The AAWC World Title Tourney

26 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Friday April 26, 1996 Today I am pleased to announce the first round matches for the upcoming All American Wrestling Conference Heavyweight Title Tournament.
All 64 active singles wrestlers in the AAWF have been allowed to participate. It will be a single elimination tournament, with a champion to be crowned at BattleZone '96 - Title Turmoil. More information will follow...but lets wait no longer, as we anounce these terrific match-ups.
Round 1 "The Bad Guy" Razor Ramon v. "The Nature Boy" Buddy Landell Savio Vega v. "Stone Cold" Steve Austin Jerry "The King" Lawler v. Bret "Hitman" Hart Scott Norton v. Konnan TAZ v. Yokozuna "The British Bulldog" Davey Boy Smith v. Jake "The Snake" Roberts "The Loose Cannon" Brian Pillman v. The 1-2-3 Kid Justin "Hawk" Bradshaw v. Sabu The One Man Gang v. Hulk Hogan The Ultimate Warrior v. Kama "The Taskmaster" Kevin Sullivan v. "Big Daddy Cool" Diesel Vader v. The Booty Man "The Franchise" Shane Douglas v. Ahmed Johnson Rey Mysterio Jr. v. Eddy Gurrero Goldust v. The Sandman "The Nature Boy" Ric Flair v. "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan "The Shooter" Dean Malenko v. Psicosis Juventud Gurrera v. Jushin "Thunder" Liger The Disco Inferno v. "The Enforcer" Arn Anderson "The King of Harts" Owen Hart v. "The Crippler" Chris Benoit "Macho Man" Randy Savage v. Stevie Richards Diamond Dallas Page v. "Wildman" Marc Mero "Double J" Jeff Jarrett v. Barry Horowitz The Blue Meanie v. Buh Buh Ray Dudley Mankind v. Sting "The Total Package" Lex Luger v. El Puerto Ricano Duke "The Dumpster" Drossee v. Hunter Hearst Helmsley Alex Wright v. Bob "Spark Plugg" Holly "The Heartbreak Kid" Shawn Michaels v. The Undertaker Fatu v. Mikey Whipwreck Raven v. Tommy Dreamer Bam Bam Bigelow v. The Giant And there you have it. One of these sixty-four man will be the AAWC Heavyweight Champion come June 23, 1996. We here at the head offices of the AAWC would like to wish them all the best of luck in their quest.
The first round matches have already begun and will be posted through house show reports...The second, third, and fourth rounds however will be televised twice a week until we have out semi finalists. The Semi Finals will then be held on June 23, 1996 at the BattleZone PPV.
Anyone wishing to recieve these house show reports please send your requests to [detail withheld] . These matches promise to be entertaining, as well as exciting. So don't miss out on all the action. The first round postings will probably appear on the net some time today...and the others will shortly follow.
So stay tuned, because the future of professional wrestling is at hand... Here in the AAWC Thats All For Now [name withheld] (President of the AAWC)
