Interview: Togo gloats Card Statistics: Length: 2:15:57 Rating: *** 1/9 Title switches: 3 -=-=-=- TV-14 -=-=-=- -=Fireworks explode inside the Pepsi Arena in Albany, New York. The camera goes straight to the ring for the first match.=- And the opener was one for the ages. It featured great action. Many times, both men were inches away from going through a table, only to narrowly escape. The end came when Vito set Al Snow up on the table outside the ring. Then, he climbed to the top rope, and hit a flying Bodypress, crashing Al down through the wood. Vito LoGrasso wins the hardcore best of five series!
Everyone was wondering what effect Angle's attack on Shamrock would have. Basically, it made him mad. The two battled a great technical match, with
Shamrock countering a German Suplex attempt with the Ankle Lock submission in the middle of the ring. Angle had no choice but to Tap Out. Even still,
Shamrock kept the Ankle Lock on. Referees came out to break it up, but Shamrock held on for 3 minutes after the bell. The ref didn't reverse the decision, and
Angle's ankle is in serious jepoardy.
-=The camera went to the interview station for comments from Eddy Guerrero=- [Guerrero] I've only been in CNW for a week, and a friend has turned into a bitter enemy. Chris, I don't know whats gotten into you man, but I don't like it. I'm going to straighten it out right here, right now. This 2/3 falls match won't need to go the full distance, as the Latino Heat will put you out!
-=Then it switched over to Benoit.=- [Benoit] Eddy, you say it won't need to go the full distance. Well you're right. You step into the ring with the most technically sound wrestler in the history of the sport. And I will prove it tonight. Eddy, you think I'm not?
PROVE ME WRONG! -=Both men made there way to the ring=- The match was CLASSIC. It went the full 3 falls. The first fall came around the
15 minute mark. Guerrero hit the Frog Splash to get fall #1. Benoit then came back with the Crippler Crossface at the 21 and 24 Minute marks to win the 2/3 falls match with Guerrero. The sad story came after the match though. As it was reported by CNW Doctors that Benoit had tore his right triceps, and will miss some action in the upcoming weeks. Just how long is still uncertain.
-=Then it went back to the interview area for comments from Billy Gunn=- [Gunn] Austin, all my wounds are healed. They've been licked, cleaned, stitched and sewed, and now I'm coming back for more. The question is, are you ready for me? I will stop at nothing to have your blood on my fingers. Like you said, it will flow like mine. The only difference is, it will be yours staining the canvas, not mine. Whether it be by chair, your mirror, or some other foreign object I can get my hands on, you will be beaten Austin, mark my words.
-=Back to the ring=- Then the First Blood matchup came around. The only weapon visible at ringside was of course the ever present Stunning Mirror, but that was to change. Both men attempted Chair shots, monitor shots, and every other type of shot imagineable. The end came around the 12 minute mark. Gunn was holding a steel chair, and Austin his mirror. Both were baiting and ducking. Gunn went for a spinning chairshot which Austin avoided. When Gunn came back around, Austin hit a vicious mirror shot, shattering it, and breaking the skin, and Gunn's blood flowed. Austin then took up a shard, and ground it into Gunn's forehead, cutting him even deeper. The report came back later Gunn had severe lacerations on his forehead. No word yet on his condition though.
-=Mike Sanders came up on the screen from his locker room=- [Sanders] Tonight is my time for glory. I will win the CNW Western Hemisphere
Title, and bring it into the NBT. Not Storm, Teioh or Hayashi will be able to stop me. Why you ask? Because I am Above Average. I'm above the curve, above the Norm. You could call me deviant, because I don't go with the flow. I'm the salmon running upstream causing my pigmentation to change bright red. I'm the ninth inning game 7 grand slam causing your favorite team to win the World
Series, I am THAT GOOD! I'll prove it here tonight. Kaz, Lance, MEN'S You're all S.O.L., and you all know what that means.
-=Back to the ring for the Tag Team Battle Royal=- Before the tag Team Battle Royal, it was learned that Jaime-San would not be able to participate as he had the flu. So Kaz Hayashi would pull double time and take his place in the match. Everyone went after Edge and Christian from the get go, as they were the favorites. But to the rescue came CHRIS BENOIT and
LANCE STORM. Team Maple Leaf ran down with Chairs, leveling everyone, and tossing them over the top. They then announced Edge and Christian as members of
TML! All four men Shook hands and laughed at the fans! Edge and Christian are your first CNW Tag Team Champions.
-=The camera then caught up with Dick Togo=- [Togo] America. Land of the free. Free enterprise. Free drinks at happy hour. Free parking after 9 PM. Free demos. Free everything. Unless your foreign. So many wrestlers have gotten free rides to the top. Yet the rest of us work for it. Myself and Kaientai included. No one can hold us back now. And especially not you Misawa. Tonight in the ladder match, I will become the first CNW World
Champion. Watch for it, cause it will happen. Mark my words. But fans, don't look for the catchphrase, cause you won't hear it from me.
-=Then back to the ring for the Four Corners Match=- Kaz then came out for match two. That being the Four Corners match for the vacant CNW Western Hemisphere Title. His tiredness showed as he was the first man eliminated by Lance Storm. Storm rolled through one of Kaz's offensive moves to lock on the Maple Leaf. Kaz, was too tired out and tapped out. Next to go was Mike Sanders. Sanders went for the 3.0, but MEN'S Teioh somehow slipped out. Sanders then came off the ropes and ran right into the sitout chokeslam that Teioh calls the Miracle Ecstacy. The winner, in the end was MEN'S Teioh.
Storm came off the ropes as well, and in a replay of what happened earlier, he ran straight into the Miracle Ecstacy, allowing MEN'S Teioh to become the first
CNW Western Hemisphere Champion.
-=Then to the interview station for thoughts from Misawa=- [Misawa] I don't need to say much, as my points have been told over the past few weeks. I love being in the US, and I'll prove it tonight. Togo has no shot at defeating me in this match. The ladder will fall Togo, and it will fall straight over on top of you. I will be the CNW World Champion. And as far as you not having a catchphrase, why do you always have to re-iterate that you don't, and it always comes at the end of your rant? Choke on that, Togo.
-=Time for the Main Event=- And what a Main Event it was. The two men came up with spots never seen before in a one on one ladder Match. Togo hit the Senton Bomb from the top of the ladder, but Misawa was still able to get up. He caught Togo at the top, and
Went for the Emerald Erosion from the top of the ladder! Togo turned in midair and landed on top of Misawa however. Misawa's head was not so lucky as it caught a chair as it hit the ground. Togo, regained his strength, climbed the rungs, and retreived the CNW World Title belt, becoming the first CNW World
Champion!
After the match, Togo and Kaientai posed over Misawa, attacking him every time he tried to get up. The other four members of Kaientai stood at the corners of the ring, holding blue and white streamers. They then threw them into the ring, showering Togo with them as the PPV came to a close!
