CNW Monday Night Turbulence (09.18.00)

18 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Interview: Owner Karl Garrett welcomes everyone to CNW Simon & Swinger defeated Tiger Beat Express when Diamond pinned Christian with the Simonizer in 0:10:38.
Rating: ** 3/4 Interview: The world meets Mike Sanders [Western Hemisphere Title Tournament Quarterfinal]: MEN'S Teioh pinned Big Vito with the Miracle Ecstasy in 0:11:03. Rating: ** 3/4 Interview: The Olympic Gold Medalist: Kurt Angle Ken Shamrock pinned Mike Sanders after a Frankensteiner in 0:10:00. Rating: *** Interview: The Cat attacks Sanders Steve Austin pinned Billy Gunn with the Stun-Gun in 0:14:25. Rating: ** 3/4 Interview: Calgary, Alberta, Canada [World Title Tournament Quarterfinal]: Mitsuharu Misawa pinned Lance Storm with the Tiger Driver in 0:14:20. Rating: **** 1/4 Card Statistics: Length: 1:00:26 Rating: *** 1/8 TV Rating: 3.8 Higest Quarter Hour: Misawa vs Storm TV Revenue: $13,200 Attendance: 2250 Merchandise: 1350 (Biggest seller - CNW T-Shirt) Total: $15,175 (I Started with $5000) An ESPN2 Production... -=-=-=- TV-14 -=-=-=- -=CNW Turbulence scrolls onto the screen=- -=The scene opens inside the fairly filled Civic Center in Glens Falls, New York. Fans are still entering the arena, unsure of what to expect from the new wrestling organization. The camera pans down to the entrance way, where three large letters sit, C, N and W. Underneath the N is a door where the wrestlers enter the aisleway. Then it goes to the ring. The ring has a black canvas with gold apron. The ropes are gold, black, gold, with black ringposts and gold turnbuckles. On the black canvas, CNW is written in gold. It then goes out over the crowd again. The entire floor section is filled, with some of the upper level as well. Barely anyone has signs, as they don't know who has been hired by CNW. Only a few scattered CNW Rules signs are visible. All of a sudden, "#1
Stunna" by the Big Tymers starts up. The fans begin to cheer, although they know not what for. Then from the entrance, a man emerges. He is a black bald,
6'2 man. He has glasses on, and a black suit. The graphics at the bottom of the screen reads CNW: Karl Garrett. He reaches the ring and climbs up the ringsteps. He enters the ring between the top and second ropes. He is then announce by the ring announcer to the house crowd. He then takes the mic away=-
[Karl Garrett] "Welcome to the first edition of CNW Turbulence! First, let me introduce myself. I am Karl Garrett, the owner and operator of Championship
Network Wrestling. I once played high school and Division I football. Until a devestating knee injury ended my career early during my days in the Arena
Football League playing for the Orlando Predators. I thought I may want to own my own football team, but that dream seemed unreachable. So, I decided to go after my second love, pro-wrestling. Now, the same injury that ended my football career wouldn't let me wrestle. So I decided I'd own a company of my own instead, and after months of planning, recruiting and scheduling, here is
CNW! This is the first edition of Turbulence, which can be seen on the ESPN2
Network! Over the next couple of months, as we here try to win over you the fans, you will see many title tournaments. Starting here tonight with a couple of first round matches. So, without further adeiu, here is the official start of CNW, and Turbulence!!"
-="#1 Stunna" starts again, and Garrett leaves to a big ovation.=- The first match of the card was a tag team competition. The Tiger Beat Express
(Edge & Christian) took on the team of Simon Diamond & Johnny Swinger. After a back and forth battle, Simon got the pin over Christian with the Simonizer.
-=The camera cut to the back, introducing the crowd to another signee, Mike Sanders.=- ["Above Average" Mike Sanders] "Well well well. This is the big time? Ha! Whatever this is barely above average, and not anywhere near my level. It's mediocre at best. So then you'd ask why I even decided to take a job with a company starting out. Well, the answer is simple. Anything is better than the job at the sinking ship known as... well, let's just say down south. Now that
I'm here, Sanders' will have his time to shine, and it's gonna come soon. My name has been thrown into the hat for the Western Hemisphere Title Tournament, and I swear that I will become the first CNW Western Hemisphere Champion, or
I'm just not "Above Average."
-=We switch back to the ring for more action=- Match #2 was a first round matchup in the Western Hemisphere title tournament. Big Vito took on Kai En Tai DX member MEN'S Teioh. After a heated match with explosive spots from Teioh, and straight Staten Island brawling from Vito,
Teioh got the win with a Double Underhook Facebuster he calls "Miracle
Ecstacy", to move on to the semifinals. -=We then go to the back to meet Kurt Angle=- [Kurt Angle] Ya know? Right now, in Sydney, Australia, the worlds best are competing for something I already own. An Olympic gold medal. Back in 1996, I won the Wrestling gold medal in Atlanta. How many wrestlers on the CNW Roster can say they've done that? None! That's right. I could be back in Sydney, defending my medal RIGHT NOW! However, I chose to turn pro back in 1998, joining a northern company. How I'd wish to be in Australia right now, but alas, I'm not. I'm here, in the CNW. Now I look down the roster, and I see so many men with no direction in their life, no, how should I put this? Integrity.
That's right, no Integrity. Oh It's True. It's Very True. However, there are a few. Tonight, I have no match, but don't fear fans of your Olympic Hero, I will be on EVERY show here in CNW, whether it's by choice or voice. So have no fear, your Olympic Champion, is here!
-=More action=- Mike Sanders then took on Ken Shamrock in the next match. The crowd booed
Sanders' heavily after his comments earlier in the night. Saners' attempted his
Reverse Hangman's Neckbreaker, but it was countered by Shamrock into a backflip clothesline. Shamrock then jumped up onto Sanders' shoulders, taking him over with a Frankensteiner, getting the win.
-="I'm The Greatest, One, Two!=- After the match was over, "The Cat" Ernest Miller ran into the ring, and attacked Sanders! All the while telling Sanders he was the greatest. After the beating, he got on the mic telling someone in the Crowd to call his Momma!
The next match saw two seasoned veterans of wrestling. Steve Austin was introduced first. However, something was different about him. He wore golden tights instead of the black ones fans have grown accoustomed to, and his hair was grown out! He wasn't bald as usual, and his goatee was shaved. He was announced the crowd as "Stunning" Steve Austin. He had reverted back to his old persona! His opponent for the night was Billy Gunn, who was received warmly by the crowd. Gunn had control for most of the match, but then came the obligatory ref bump. Austin was hung over the second rope, and Gunn came over to him. As
Gunn leaned over, he was met in the head by the mirror, it shattered, and Gunn was lascerated on the head. He stumpled around, and right into Austin's
Stun-Gun. The three count was made as referee Dan Scroady reclaimed his senses. -="Calgary.... Alberta, Canada". Lance Storm made his way to ringside for his Main Event match=- [Lance Storm] "Hello, Glens Falls, New York. (The fans started to actually cheer for Storm) That's the ONLY time you'll ever hear that from me. Now that
I'm here in CNW, nothing has changed. I'm still Canadian, and America is still second fiddle, and always will be to the best country in the world. Canada. And
I'm from the heart of it, Calgary, Alberta. The greatest wrestling city in the world. The heritage is so rich and deep, it goes back generations. Back to Stu
Hart, and Stampede Wrestling. Now tonight, my opponent is not american, which makes me very happy. Tonight, I face a man from Japan, Mitsuharu Misawa, in a
World Title Tournament Quarterfinal. It's good to see an organization finally realizes that for a World Title to actually be a world title, the competitors have to be internationally renound, and not just from Miami, Florida. Now, although Japan has a rich heritage, it's not as good as that of Canada, and that's all there is to it. If it were any less, it wouldn't be...
Calgary... Alberta... Canada! -=Storm then waited for Misawa in the ring.=- The match was a Four Star classic. It saw both men getting busted open, and tremendous action. At one point Lance tried for his top rope spinning leg
Lariat, but it was caught by Misawa! Misawa changed his position, and dropped him with the Tiger Driver for the win, and the advancement into the Semifinals.
That's all for tonights show from Glens Falls, New York, see you next time fans.
