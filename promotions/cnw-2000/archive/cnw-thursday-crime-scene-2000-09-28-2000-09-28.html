CNW Thursday Crime Scene (09.28.00)

28 September 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Interview: Billy Gunn berates Stunning Steve Tiger Beat Express defeated Kaientai DX when Edge pinned Funaki with the Downward Spiral in 0:12:12. Rating: **** Interview: Dick Togo and Mitsuharu Misawa face off! Falls-Count-Anywhere Match: Al Snow pinned Big Vito with the Snow Plow in 0:22:08. Rating: *** 1/2 Interview: Sanders Speaks, what a surprise! Interview: Lance Storm gears up for Swinger. Lance Storm made Swinger submit to the Canadian Maple Leaf in 0:11:53. Rating: *** 1/2 Interview: Edge and Christian...? Mike Sanders pinned Mark Jindrak after a small package in 0:08:08. Rating: ** 1/2 Interview: Angle makes his way to the ring [World Title Tournament Quarterfinal]: Kurt Angle pinned Billy Gunn with the Olympic Slam in 0:13:10. Rating: *** 3/4 Card Statistics: Length: 1:07:31 Rating: *** 1/2 An ESPN Production... -=-=- TV-14 -=-=- --="Rock the Party" by P.O.D. comes in through your TV's speakers.=- #We came here to rock this jam# #Spread the love is the master plan# (It shows a shot of owner Karl Garrett in the center of the ring holding a mic) #Let the light shine bright like a star# #Cause everybody in the party knows who we are# (Lance Storm goes for the top rope spinning leg lariat, but Mitsuharu Misawa counters it with a Sitout Powerbomb)
#You gotta get down, dance around, floss ya style# #CNW guarantee make it worth your while# (Shot of Teioh hitting the Miracle Ecstacy on Big Vito) #Cross Checkin# (Shamrock hits a Frankensteiner on Sanders) #Homewreakin# (The Cat with a chair to the back of Sanders) #Keep Em Beggin For More# (Austin with the Stun-Gun on Billy Gunn) #We came to Rock the Party all night long# (Simon Diamond hits the Simonizer on Christian) #So everybody won't you sing this song# (CNW rolls across a black screen in silver letters) -=Limited fireworks explode at the entrance. The Big CNW letters are there again. In the upper right hand corner, you can read Live! From the Campus of
Temple University, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.=-
-="I'm an Ass Man!" plays and Billy Gunn approaches the ring. In his hand, he carries a mirror=- [Gunn] Hello, Philly. It's great to be back in a great town. And I'm not only doing that for a cheap pop, it's the truth. I love Philly cheesesteaks. On Monday, I extracted some revenge on "Stunning" Steve, by bashing him over the head with his own mirror, busting him open, which subsequently led to the Fame-Ass-Er.
Now, you may be wondering what this is in my hand. It's none other than Stone
Cold's precious mirror! Now, what could I possibly do with his mirror you ask?
The answer is simple. This. (Gunn starts admiring his ass with Steve's mirror) Isn't that a beautiful site, Steve? It's much better looking that what is usually seen in this mirror; Your face that I busted last week.
(Austin is seen in the back throwing a fit) Now, I know you're probably back there throwing a fit over your mirror, but don't worry Steve, I'll give it back after I'm done with it, I swear. Now, onto new buisness. Tonight, I have a quarterfinal world title match with Kurt Angle in the main Event. The winner goes on to face America hating Dick Togo in the semi-finals. Well, there'd be no one prouder than myself to represent the good ol' US of A in the match with Togo, and I plan on doing just that.
("I'm Too Sexy" starts up and out walks Stunning Steve!) [Austin] Gunn, you aren't going anywhere. The only place you'll be going is down. How
DARE you degrade the Stunning Mirror in such a manner. It is not forgiveable, so right now, I will take retribution from you in my own way!
(Austin rushes the mirror, and attacks Gunn. Gunn however cocks back and again nails Austin in the head with the Mirror, shattering it like before. While
Austin is bent over with blood dripping to the mat, Gunn jumps up and connects with the Fame-Ass-Er. Austin lies in the shattered glass and blood as we go to our first commercial)
Our opening match featured Kai En Tai take on the team campaigning to be handed the "stritles" the Tiger Beat Express. This match was a Classic Four star battle, both teams giving it their all. Funaki attempted a Russian Legsweep, but Edge Turned around, countering it with the Downward Spiral. Christian cut off Taka while the three count was scored.
(Kai En Tai's music plays and Dick Togo comes to the ring to a massive heel reaction. His words are translated into english)
[Togo] Billy Gunn, you have no idea what you are talking about. Have you even retrieved a real wrestling training in your silly little career? I think not
Gunn-San. You're just another foolish American with silly dreams of getting over by catchphrases alone, showing no real technical wrestling ability. Sure,
I will give some American wrestlers the credit the deserve, because they actually do have wrestling talent. Such as Benoit, Jericho, or Angle. Two of the three are wrestling where? In Japan, because they don't need catchphrases to save their careers. They do it by wrestling. Which is what I plan to do here in the CNW. I have advanced to the Semi-Finals of your World Title tournament and I plan on winning it come the day. And when that day comes I will defeat another American with the Senton Bomb
(Misawa's music plays and he comes out to a huge pop. Like Togo, his words are translated)
[Misawa] Togo, have you forgotten about me? You're not the only member of the Japanese culture to have advanced, and should you make it to the Finals, you won't make it any further.
[Togo] Misawa, you SELL OUT! Last time I came out here to speak, I made a HUGE mistake, calling you a pride Japanese could look up to. Boy, was I wrong.
You're no better than the rest, relying on American Style wrestling and the like to get yourself over. You should have stuck with Strong Style, to show them who really are the best wrestlers in the world
[Misawa] How does the saying go? When in Rome? [Togo] WE AREN'T IN ITALY! This is America! Don't adapt, that's what they want you to do. [Misawa] Strong Style is not the only type of wrestling in the world. It's easier to work with the American Style anyway
[Togo] What next? A Silly catchphrase? [Misawa] Well... [Togo] Well, here's another major factor in American Wrestling I can train you in. [Misawa] Whats that? [Togo] The sneak attack (With that, Taka, Teioh and Funaki attack Misawa from behind, resulting in a four on one attack. After Misawa has been layed out, Togo again grabs the mic)
[Togo] Don't wait for a catchphrase, cause it isn't coming. (He leaves along with the rest of Kai En Tai, to a thunderous heel reaction) Next we saw two fierce competitors in a hardcore falls count anywhere match. Neither man is a stranger to the style. The deranged Al Snow, and the South
Brooklyn brawler Big Vito took it to the streets. Al Snow eventually got the pin with the Snow Plow outside on the campas' front lawn!
(The Natural Born Thrillaz music plays as Jindrak, O'Haire and of course, Mike
Sanders come to the ring)
[Sanders] You know, tonight, I have a match. It's no normal match, oh no. It will, as usual, be above Average. It's a quarterfinal match in the CNW Western
Hemisphere Title tournament. And it's not against an average competitor, oh no. It's against none other than... Mark Jindrak. That's right ladies and gentlemen, my Above Average match will be with my partner, my confidant, Mark
Jindrak. Now, Mark, did you have anything to say about the upcoming match?
(He goes to put the mic in front of Jindrak's mouth, and as he leans in to say something, he pulls the mic away just as quickly)
[Sanders] That's what I thought. He's speechless folks! He's so dumbfounded he gets to step into the ring with Mike Sanders, that he lost his train of thought. But other than that, tonight, Mark, you don't face the Average wrestler, you face the Above Average wrestler, higher than the Kurve, so I'm sorry, but you're
S.O.L. and you all know what that means
(Sean O'Haire goes to reach for the mic, but Sanders again refuses. He and
Jindrak storm out of the ring with Sanders laughing)
(Lance Storm's music plays and he goes to the ring) [Storm] Attribution has been paid in full. Although I lost in the quarterfinals of the
World Title Tournament last week, I was rewarded with a Canadian Hemisphere
Title tournament berth monday, and I delivered like a true Canadian would do.
Now, we have Togo running his head off about Canada, and Angle claiming USA, but I am the purest athlete in CNW, if for no more than the fact that I am
Canadian.
Tonight, I have a match with Swinger, which is fine with me. He may be a stellar athlete, but I doubt that. Cause he's simply not from Calgary...
Alberta... Canada, and I am. So bring your ass out here Swinger (Swinger came to the ring to start their match.) Storm took it right to Swinger. Swinger, who has been conditioned to wrestle tag matches lately, was unaware and tried to tag out several times. While
Swinger was crawling to the corner, Storm grabbed his leg and locked on the
Maple Leaf to get the submission win.
("You think you know me". Edge and Christian's music starts and they come down to the ring through the crowd, and almost immediately, a "stritles" chant starts)
[Edge] Do you hear that Christian, they are chanting for it already [Christian] Right, we are so over [Edge] You are so right. It's unbelivable Christian! We're great [Christian] We are. [Edge] Yes, we are [Christian] Oh no, we were warned about this, we're babbling again, let's get to the point [Edge] Good idea Christian, good idea [Christian] Very good idea [Edge] No, no. Great Idea! [Christian] Get on with it! [Edge] Oh, right. Well, you know we've been campaigning for the CNW Stritles, and how much we deserve them, and tonight we proved it. We just wanted to come out here and say it would be such heniousity were we not given the stritles
[Christian] Exactly
[Edge] So, we just wanted to let the fans know we are still campaigning, and keep sending those letters to the CNW offices!
(Edge and Christian leave and everyone is left wondering what the hell just happened) Next Jindrak took on fellow NBT member Mike Sanders. Apparently, Jindrak didn't like the treatment he and O'Haire had gotten from Sanders lately, and starting beating on Sanders! After about 8 minutes of beating, O'Haire jumped on the apron saying thats enough to Jindrak. Jindrak was then rolled up by Sanders, getting the win and advancing in the Western Hemisphere Title tournament.
(Kurt Angle's Olympic theme plays and he appears, holding a microphone) [Angle] CNW completely lacks integrity. As does this town of Philly. Not only do you boo an olympic hero, but your simple Owls could never beat Pitt. Oh It's True,
It's True. We have too many superstars in CNW running around being degenerates and using weapons to one up their opponents. Vito, Al Snow, Billy Gunn and the like. Why is it you need to resort to that sort of violence to get the job done. I am an olympian, and I'll prove it right now in the ring, as I take on none other than Billy Gunn and I will move on in the World Title Tournament, Oh
It's True!
(Angle finally got into the ring and awaited Gunn) Kurt Angle is the third man in the semi-finals. Gunn had the upper hand throughout most of the match, but guess who shows up. Steve Austin came out, holding a steel chair. As Gunn went to step through the ropes, Austin cracked him with the chair. Gunn was busted open AGAIN, and stumbled back into the
Olympic Slam, allowing Angle to advance to the semi-finals. Austin shouted something at the fallen Gunn, and as the show closed, he threw the chair on the lifeless body of Billy Gunn while Kurt Angle was celebrating next to him.
