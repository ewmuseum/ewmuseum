Opening Comments The first, and some say the hardest, week has come and gone. I do think it went by fairly smooth. The Roleplaying was real good and now, a lot of direction has been set. The first cycle will be fairly long as the PPV comes up November 12th. Soon, the HWA will start traveling the Northeast. Also, the Web Site - www.x8od.com/hwa/ is going to come down very soon. But, it will come back just as soon as more focus will be put on that. In closing, if any of you can write, please let me know. HWA President Phil D. Payne II. News & Rumors - Rumor has it that Myssing Lynk was raised by wolves in the wilderness. - Following this weeks turn of events at Hardcore Tendencies, Mike Emery was noted to have been considerably angry with Phil Payne's decisions. However, on TV he played it off completely the opposite. - JJ Herman has inquired about adding a third Man to the Announce Team. He feels adding another person will give the Announcers Booth another much needed dynamic. - Terrence Steele was actually fined this week 2,000 dollars. The real reasons know one knows at Press Time but some are linking it to Mike Emery's comments. - Many wonder after the acquisition of Youthenasia, will Phil Payne continue to hire more Men? The Raid came and has gone and what an impact it had. Will there be more to the story or has the brunt of the Storm passed us? - The HWA will begin tourning Albany, Rochester, Buffalo, [Massachusetts:] Fall River, New Bedford, Roxbury, Worchester, and Taunton AFTER the November Pay-Per-View, Fall Fracas: Deadly Disputes. Week in Review Coming off of the first ever Hardcore Tendencies of the Millennium, things look very good for Phil D. Payne's HWA. Many people have begun to notice the New York based Promotion and with that, comes a rising interest in the product. With talent ranging from Jeff Keenan to Ethan Van Ryan to Warran Worthington, only good things can come from this. WARRAN WORTHINGTON beat JUSTIN A. HOLE via Elbow Drop RHETT GARLAND beat SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE after executing PURE HATRED MYSSING LYNK beat TIM PRICE via LAST RESORT PERCEPTUAL VIOLENCE defeated EUROPEAN EXCELLENCE TREY SLATER pinned JEFF KEENAN when TERRENCE STEELE covered JK with Slater. Saturday October 21, 2000 Coming from the HWA Arena, LIVE in New York City!! *OPENING MATCH-UP* Silhouette vs. "Priceless" Tim Price SILHOUETTE made his prescence felt this past Wednesday Night when he greeted his opponent in a rather abrupt manner. And it is because of that, he'll meet TIM PRICE in a specially stipulated I QUIT Match. *SECOND MATCH* "The Pinnacle" Brody Anderson vs. "The Sadistic Scholar" Sebastian Montague Coming off a tough loss, Sebastian Montague has a Pop Quiz in his Second HWA Match with one of the toughest men in the entire Promotion. Known for his excellent Tag-Team Wrestling, BRODY ANDERSON could definetly turn some heads with an impressive showing in his first ever Singles Match-up. *THIRD MATCH* "Doc" Rhett Garland vs. Warran Worthington It seems as if everyone RHETT GARLAND wrestles, there is a deep contrast in styles. This is partially the reason, he'll head into Saturday Night against WARRAN WORTHINGTON! Both men are coming off big time Wins and it'll be this week where one of them breaks. *FOURTH MATCH* "Simply Golden" Jeffrey Keenan vs. "Deadly" Taylor Douglas JEFF KEENAN is coming off a slightly discouraging Loss this past Wednesday. His time to sulk is short because this Saturday, he'll face one of the deadliest Wrestlers in the World, TAYLOR DOUGLAS! This could get extremely messy! *MAIN EVENT* Youthenasia vs. "Showtime" Trey Slater/Terrence Steele Payne's Camp, YOUTHENASIA will go into a Specially Stipulated NEW YORK DEATH MATCH with the duo of TREY SLATER and TERRENCE STEELE. We all know who the Fans will be behind but could we see Phil Payne's antics lead to the end of this Match-up!? Nothing is certain anymore. * Also, the growing popularity in the HWA could lead to some new signings! The second coming of Hardcore Tendencies could definetly be the moment where a few Wrestlers come to their decision. * Could we see more men come in aid to Phil D. Payne!? Last week, he promised us a surprise and he delivered! Will he make his presence felt Saturday Night or can everyones wishes come true and he stays away from the Cameras!!? * Tune in to LST to see Hardcore Tendencies, LIVE from the HWA Arena in New York City, Saturday October 21st, 2000!
