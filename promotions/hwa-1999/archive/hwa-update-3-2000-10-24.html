Opening Comments Well, The Card took a little longer then expected but it's done with and now, we're back on track. The Roster is slowly expanding but eventually, it'll be cut off. We have a pretty solid Roster and with most of you constantly aiming to improve, I think that there is one of the biggest positives. Continue to make strides and the HWA will be around.
HWA President Phil D. Payne II. News & Rumors - After Mike Emery's surprise on Sunday Night, Phil D. Payne is beyond upset. He feels betrayed and moreso, in fear of his Job because of the recent allegations of skipping payments for certain Wrestlers and playing favorites.
- Dan Stevens shocked many when he showed up Sunday Night in the HWA but that already is covered in by a dark cloud. It's being said he injured his right Ankle after a Springboard Dropkick. Stevens will not miss time but is still taking it easy.
- This week, it's been reported by Tim Nielsen that a former Commentator of a certain Promotion will surface in the HWA as Mike Emery has turned his spot over. - New talent continues to flow into the HWA. From Stephen Strattford to Brian Floyd, more and more Wrestlers are entering the Alliance to test its waters. - Can someone say sore losers? Very unsurprising, but Youthenasia was (obviously) very angry about their defeat on Hardcore Tendencies, at the hands of Terrence Steele and Dan Stevens. After the match, they were seen backstage trashing everything they could get their hands on, and hurting everyone in their way. Rumor has it, they were on their way to Emery's office before a few security guards stopped them. Imagine if they caught the poor guy. Oh, well, luckily they didn't get him.
