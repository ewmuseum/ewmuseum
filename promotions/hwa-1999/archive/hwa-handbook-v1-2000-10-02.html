Who runs this thing? Phil D. Payne. He has been the HWA Promoter since it began. Former Office Member of the Extremely Violent Championship Wrestling, Phil branched off to create his own Wrestling Federation. Thus, the HWA was born. In it's first tenure, the HWA showcased some of the youngest talent in the World out of New
York City. Now, Payne is back again to stab his claim back into the world of Wrestling. 2.) Why should I bother to join? A valid question. Now a days, eWrestling is losing a lot of people. Many say their frustrated with the fact they pour so much into a Fed and then it folds on them. Well, the HWA cannot guarentee being around for 5 years but it will be a Home for anyone who wants to give it a try. So instead of persuading you with nonsensical pleas and promises, all I can say is give it a try. Simple huh?

How can I contribute to the HWA so it can last? i. Application ========================================================= I. Introduction i. A History of the HWA ii. Roleplaying Roleplaying is essential in the HWA, we all know that. There are a few loose rules on the Roleplaying. The minimum for Roleplays during the Week is 1, but
2 is the maximum. On the HWA Report, you are allowed 1 Small Backstage Type Mic Time BUT pieces will only be added as they come in. So, more or less -- first come, first serve. On Suicidal Tendencies, In-Rings are allowed also.
SWEARING - This is evident in any Wrestling Promotion. But, just use some common sense. I don't want to censor anyone, but if i see "you fucking cocksucking, shit eating bitch! I'm gonna kick your fucking ass!" Then, we all know you'll probably be laughed at first off. Just watch what you say.
Anything you see on RAW, like "bitch, ass, damn, hell" etc;. But "fuck, shit, bastard" excetera excetera, should be placed with a [CENSORED]. There are those rare instances where a Wrestler might "shoot" and on a Live Show, some things aren't edited, just to add a realistic effect.
ATTACKS - Don't write up useless attacks on Announcers, Reporters, and Wrestlers. Unless it is OK'd by the Office, don't do it. We all know these types of rules, I feel stupid writing it but hey, some people still don't know.
FORMAT - No real "set" set-up. But there is a basic format most use. Still, do things your own way. Here is just a rough out-line of what is 'normal.'
[SCENE: Enter Scene here] WRESTLER: Talk here [SCENE: End it] That's pretty much it. LIMITS/MAXIMUMS - 2 Roleplays is ideal but 1 is perfectly fine. For Hardcore Tendencies, you have to write atleast 1 Roleplay and if you want, a Small Pre-Match bit. If you don't have a Match, it is still good to send out atleast 1 RP. It keeps your name out there so you're not overlooked. Just something to keep in mind.
iii. Strats Strats are not a requirement but they are very much appreciated by the Writers. It allows your Wrestler to be better represented so when it comes time for readers to depict how your Character is, strats can help a lot. Is he an agitating heel who begs off and cheap shots? Does he like to cut out all the
BS and go straight after his opponent? Strats help you out in that department. So, don't bother or hesitate to write one. iv. Angles Definetly the gas that will power HWA. I think it's vital to keep everyone involved. Interest is lost quickly when someone sits out a week or two with no direction. Never hesitate to propose an Angle to the Office. We'll always try our best to make sure it works out and to make sure collaboration amongst the
Handlers is met. v.. Booking HWA Booking is pretty easy to understand. Roleplays will be the deciding factor in Judging Matches. Also involved in the Judging, each having a slight amount of power in it - Are Angles [Certain wrestlers might have a lot of heat or an Angle where it is a must situation for a Win or a Loss.], Strats will not necessarily help in a regular Match but if it is very close, it will be added in. Strats also add a lot to a Match so please -- don't be discouraged to write them up. Also, the current situation will help decide Matches in certain situations. We look at what is best for the Wrestlers. The best Roleplaying will not always win. If you did out
Roleplay your opponent and lost, it is far from personal. The HWA wants to add in a sense of great unpredictability to add to the entire picture.
========================================================= IV. Profiles of the Alliance i. Owners [name withheld] is the Founder and Owner of the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance. However, in August of 1998, he turned the Promotion over to his Vice President Phil D. Payne when [name withheld] ventured off into a new Area of Pro-Wrestling. Alongside [name withheld], [name withheld] aided in running a stint of Promotions from
1998 to 2000. [name withheld] is back, however, this time he is doing little to nothing.
Phil D. Payne - From the historied Family of Wrestlers and Presidents. Such Paynes are Ed. E. Phil is no different as he ventured into the Wrestling World and quickly established himself as a Top Promoter in the Northeast. Payne will be doing it all in the HWA as he ventures into this alone. He's confident that he will handle what comes with the territory and anxiously awaits the start of the HWA.
ii. Commentators JJ Herman, HWA Icon! Herman is the Manager of the Infamous Mafia. Although sometimes embarassingly funny, where only he laughs at his jokes, JJ has a strong head on his shoulders when it comes to Wrestling. Very spuratic and emotional. When watching HWA TV, it is definetly a Show in itself watching JJ
Herman behind the mic. Alongside JJ Herman this time around, is none other then Mike Emery! The Owner of the HWA has decided to "strap on a headset" so to speak. Emery brings to the Announcers Table a very wise knowledge of Wrestling and Moves. He can take any situation, good, bad, or otherwise and turn it into something spectacular.
That "magic" just hangs overhead when Emery is present, enabling him to be one of the best showmen in Wrestling.
iii. Referees HEAD REFEREE - Fujinama Goto, a man with a vast knowledge of Wrestling. Goto has seen it all when it comes inside the Ring. He's called Matches such as
Flyguy Ragnarok vs. Deathwarrant and Gripper vs. Dr. Death. Goto is the man to call on when you need a Match called down the line in spectacular fashion.
Some people take for granted a Referee's presence in the Ring, but Goto reassures them they have a role and he does it better then anyone.
SECOND REFEREE - David Rodriques is the second Referee of the HWA. For the majority of his Career as a Referee, he has been in Japan's SJWP. Rodriques works for free in the HWA and holds a side job unbeknownst to anyone. He is just happy he can return to the States under the clause that he has a stable job.
THIRD REFEREE - Jimmy Lane is the final Referee of the HWA. He isn't exactley a Pro at it but he is far from a newbie. Lane has a hard time calling Matches because he is intimidated by the larger Competitors but also is subconcious of the fact he needs to make everything fair. So his timid nature constantly clashes with his good natured mind. Always a site to see.
iv. Reporters Tim Nielsen is the head of the HWA Media Department. He'll always be bringing in the news everyone wants to know. With a degree in Journalism, Nielsen reports nothing but the truth. He despises of speculation and especially
Reporters who make things up just to knab some attention. Tim embodies the "A1, Perfect Reporter." Chris Patrick is a new addition to the HWA. He has been around the Wrestling Scene for quite some time and off Camera feels he should be the Head. However, Patrick does his work and does it well. Patrick is the stereotypical Reporter. He's an Ex-Wrestler who ruptured disc in his back and can no longer Wrestle. Sometimes, in those rare occaisons a Wrestler says a little too much and gets Patrick to respond, physicall. In the end, it always ends up hurting Patrick, in more ways then one.
Richard Freedman is the Third Reporter. He is always on the scene. Most of the time when a Backstage Scuffle breaks out or a Wrestler request some quick time, Freedman is there. Dick, as some of the more brash Wrestlers like to call him, asks nothing but the tough questions. Sometimes he aggravates
Wrestlers with his implications which leads to some pretty interesting things. v. Ring Announcer The Voice of the HWA is but one man. His name is Keith Smith. Smith doesn't want anything to do with the Matches. "Let the Wrestlers Wrestle, let the
Announcer announce." The woman took a strong liking to Smith the last time around and many feel he'll try and use that as leverage this time around.
Smith is still one of the most respected men in the HWA Locker Room due to his willingness to take a "bump" or someone or just go with the flow, regardless of how much he really hates it.

Thunder & Lightning ========================================================= III. Titles i. World Heavyweight Title The Prized Possession. The one thing you take home and place on your mantel piece, .. UNTIL you have to defend it again! The World Title is the most prestigious Title in any Promotion. It simply says you are the current Best.
However, it has it's down sides. The fact everyone now gunns for you and your belt. Enemies and allies. The lines are blurred and only the true Greats of our Time can hold such an honorable Belt. Requirements exist within a Defense every 31 days and when the Champion so desires.
ii. World Tag-Team Titles The World Tag-Team championship was designed for the best two-man teams to show they're seniority over the others. The Tag championship is in a league of it's own, only available to the tag teams of the HWA. To be the Tag champions, a team needs to overcome all the odds from the other tag teams. This will be a highly desired for title, as some like to use it to boost them up a bit.
Teamwork will be the key here. Without team work, the ability to capture this title will be a difficult task. The World Tag-Titles will be defended at least once a month and whenever the Champion desires.
iii. North American Title Many consider this the "second step." But it is still no Title to slouch with. This Title states you are right now one of the best in the Game, bottom line. With this belt, you need to be World Title Material and Television Title material. Hence the "median" Belt which differentiates between the two. This
Belt will call on some very valiant defenses and more frequent then the World Title. To hold the North American Title, you must be consistent. One mistake and you can easily be dethroned.
iv. Television Title This Belt is summed up in one word. "Defining." To hold the TV Belt, you have to be on top of your Game constantly. Week-in, week-out, you have to be able to deal with some of the HWA's Best. Fending them off is the easy part.
Moving on to the next match is the hardest. Some call this a "Stepping stone," but that in no way says it's a Title not to be vied for. It has a great deal of respect carried with it. Only the true Warriors can hold the Television
Title. v. Cruiserweight Title Many Cruiserweights are pigeon-holed into being luchas. But, many Cruiserweights today are becomming more diverse. Around the World, and mainly North America, Lightweights do not have much success. Well, the HWA is planning on changing that. The Cruiserweight Title could easily catapult you to the top but no need to overlook it. The quickest, riskiest, most dangerous
Wrestlers will Plancha, Springboard, and Moonsault their way to the top of the Cruiserweight Division in hopes of becomming Champion. ========================================================== III. Scheduling i. Hardcore Tendencies Sunday Night Rage is a new addition to the WRX. It used to be called Saturday Night Rage. However, the Presidents realized with the regained interest in the Wampanoag Flea Market, a Saturday position would be very costly. So, Mike Emery convinced the owners of the Building to give the WRX it's own time on Week days and from 3 O'clock Sunday Afternoon on. Rage is the bigger of the two Cards that will definitely display the best in the WRX. Hard to say that,
"the best in the WRX." Competition will be extremely fierce. ii. Eye of The Storm The Flash Packet of the HWA. This will be the Ticket to getting your voice heard in the HWA. Every Wrestler booked is required to write up at least One
Flash for the Packet. Failure to do so will result in a Suspension if it persist. But Wrestlers who aren't booked should still make a solid effort at getting a Flash out. Eye of The Storm has no set Day to be released but will usually come before Hardcore Tendencies.
iii. HWA Report The Mid-week Report of the HWA. In this could be small Interviews, short clips, and a review of the Week. Any Flashes that were kept off Eye of The
Storm for whatever reason will also go here. The HWA Report will be hosted by JJ Herman, Jackson Payne, and Rob Watson. It'll definetly be a treat to anyone who watches.
iv. Tapings[House Shows] Every once in a while, to keep people on their toes, the HWA will have a taped House Show during the week. At HWA House Shows, just about anything can and will happen. Titles can be lost, feuds can be ended, and problems can arise and be solved in the same night. All eyes on the House Shows because they are definetly something to keep you all on the edge of your seat.
v. Pay-Per-Views Pay-Per-Views are the one time to go all out for most people. Well, in the HWA we go all out every night. But yes, the PPV Extravaganza's do call upon a higher notch. This is when the Stars of the HWA raise the proverbial Bar of
Pro-Wrestling only to look ahead a month and see how they can raise it again. Anything can happen at a HWA Pay-Per-View. Nothing is certain, no one is safe, 'cuz most of the time all Hell Breaks Loose! January -- Evolution '01 February -- Frozen Over March -- Pain, Pleasure, and Perdition! April -- Blood, Debts, and Careers: The Final Payments May -- A Bloody Massacre June -- Summer Showdown July -- Chained, Shackled, and Bound: No More Independence August -- Massacre in Massachusetts September -- Orientation October -- Spiritually Bound November -- Fall Fracas: Deadly Disputes December -- Fuck The Future: New Years Evil v. Columnist are also welcomed to apply. We could always use an extra Report or such thing.
But that is all really needed. I don't want to say anything and shoot myself in the foot by cutting some people off for the future. =)
