Opening Comments There was our fourth Card before our first ever Supercard. From top to bottom, I believe this Card was one of the most productive and also it was a 'table setter.' Each piece played instrumental in the Supercard and I believe because of that, Fall Fracas will go down in the History of the HWA as not only its first Supercard of the Millennium, but one of its greatest. HWA President Phil D. Payne II. News & Rumors - Heavy rumors continue to swirl about "The Bandit" Jason Blake signing with the HWA. Rumor has it Blake has kept a keen eye on the HWA and looks to venture into something new. - It's being speculated that the 26th, at Fall Fracas, Rhett Garland will wrestle his first ever Title Match but it'll also be his last match. Many within the HWA hope this is just another Internet Rumor. - Many wonder why the Main Event was cut short and HWA Officials are saying it was to lessen up on the work load, heading into what is a very important Supercard. - Terrence Steele is said to have a strained right knee and progressively worsening concussion. Regardless of these injuries, he'll work the Supercard. - Some say a big time Wrestler in semi-retirement is looking to rekindle the Wrestling flame by coming through the HWA. [*OPENING MATCH!*] In what could be an amazing Cruiserweight Four Corners Match, "CAPTIVATING" COREY HART will take on EL GUYO NEGRO in his HWA Debut along with JUSTIN A. HOLE and STEPHEN "SILVER" STRATFORD! Expect it all to be put on the line because all four of these men can benefit greatly from a big win. [*SECOND MATCH!*] A clash of the debuts pits "HEARTBREAKER" SIMON LAVERY against a man his total opposite. A no-nonsense, Veteran of War, in SCOTT "THUNDER" FARRELL. Both men will battle it out in a FIVE COUNT Match. [*THIRD MATCH!*] Falling a bit in the eyes of Phil D. Payne, WARRAN WORTHINGTON heads into Fall Fracas inside a KUMITE STYLE MATCH against HWA newcomer, H2O! Their saying this Match could become a sick encounter between two old foes. [*FOURTH MATCH!*] On Tendencies, we saw both teams go at it briefly with the newbies taking the upperhand. Sunday Night, the duo of "DEVISTATING" VINCENT DRAKE and "FIRST-RATE" KEVIN KLASS along with STEVE GREEDY will meet MANDATORY SELF FORNIFICATION in a FLAMING TABLE LADDER MATCH for, you didn't guess it, the HWA WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT TAG-TEAM TITLES! [*FIFTH MATCH!*] The long awaited rematch. Taking place in FIRESIDE HIGH SCHOOL, THE CLASSROOM DEATH MATCH! "DOC" RHETT GARLAND will brawl his way all over the New York Institute of Education, defending his HWA TV Title against the "SADISTIC SCHOLAR" SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE. It will be bloody as hell! [*MAIN EVENT!*] Right here, this is the big one. The Match everyone will come to see. In a previously stated DOUBLE HELL EXPLODING BARBED WIRE CELL WARFARE, 6 Men will enter but only 1 will leave. That man, out of TERRENCE STEELE, "BRUTAL BRIAN FLOYD, DAN "WINDY CITY" STEVENS, "THE EPITOME" DILLION McCAIN, "SWEET" JARRED BOMBAY, AND ETHAN VAN RYAN will do two things. One, become the HWA World Champion and two, bring his team PRESIDENCY OVER THE HWA! VIII.
