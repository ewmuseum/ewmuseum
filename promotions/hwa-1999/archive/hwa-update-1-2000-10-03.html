Opening Comments Well, I am extremely pleased to announce the resurrection of the Hardcore Wrestling Alliance! It is with great joy I do now say the HWA is ALIVE and WELL! However, the time to enjoy the return is short. Hardcore Tendencies quickly approaches and the Who, What, Where, When's will soon be answered. The HWA is a diverse grouping of talent and with that diversity come territories! How things will pan out, I'm not too sure! But I do know the HWA Arena in Queens, New York will be out of control. Lets give them a show they won't soon forget. HWA President Phil D. Payne II. News & Rumors - With new signings such as "Simply Golden" Jeffrey Keenan and "Spotlight" Trey Slater, the HWA Locker Room is already in slight dissarray! It seems Trey Slater has an overwhelmingly huge ego and many of HWA's finest are not taking kindly to it. - Hardcore Tendencies, the weekly HWA Broadcast, is said to be one of the premier Wrestling Shows on TV. Local New York Network, LST - Langston Smith Television, has provided the HWA with an outlet to the entire State of New York, Rhode Island, some Networks in Massachusetts, and all of New Hamspire and Vermont. New Jersey was denied HWA Televison when the Legislation Board passed a Law, banning "extreme wrestling" from the state of New Jersey. - Recent signings for the HWA are Terrence Steele and the Myssing Lynk. Both have been seen with Phil D. Payne, not at the same time though. It'd be amazing to see these two as a Team, whereas both men are towering figures and could easily bring forth a great deal of Power to the HWA Tag-Team Division. - Many rumors have begun to circulate that Ethan Van Ryan, one of the top HWA Signings, is on a bit of a "trip." He has contacted many of his friends to RAID the HWA! Yes, you read that correctly. RAID the HWA. Not many know about this but the ones who do are completely confused! How will the HWA Diehards react to his "invasion!?" - Many greats below the Boarder have now turned to the States for some exposure. Los Tomadores Del Riesgo ["El Psicosis Uno" Anibal Guerro, El Guyo Negro], are just two of the recent signings from Mexican All-Pro Wrestling. If these two do well, you can rest assured that more Mexican Wrestlers will travel North! - The Wrestling World has been functioning quite well lately and with the prosperity in store for the HWA, it is being discussed that plans for a Supercard could occur. The two Promotions in discussion? The Las Vegas Wrestling Association and the Premier Wrestling Organization. Imagine these three colliding?! Send All Rumors[Any kinds] to MDR CyD [detail withheld] . Be creative, realistic, or whatever you want. III. HWA Weekly Event Preview * EYE OF THE STORM * Los Tomadores Del Riesgo vs. European Excellence Warran Worthington vs. Justin A. Hole * HARDCORE TENDENCIES * The Resurrection of Hardcore! Tuesday October 10, 2000 Coming from the HWA Arena, LIVE in New York City!! *OPENING MATCH-UP* "The Sadistic Scholar" Sebastian Montague vs. "Doc" Rhett Garland INFO: England vs. Texas, to put it in a sense. The proper yet devious SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE will hand out a Final Exam to fellow newcomer, RHETT GARLAND. The "Doc" is said to be one of the toughest Brawlers from the Lonestar State. However, Sebastian is a prime physical specimen and a master of the Ring. To see the two styles clash will be something spectacular to kick off the returning HWA! *SECOND MATCH* Myssing Lynk vs. "Priceless" Tim Price INFO: Again, two clashing styles when TIM PRICE goes up against the bohemoth, MYSSING LYNK. To look at this from the outside, it is an extremely unfair Match where Tim Price stands little to no chance. Lynk is probably the largest man to ever step foot into the HWA when Price is one of the smallest. How will the Priceless One overcome the odds? He'll have to resort to some Hardcore Tendencies! *THIRD MATCH* Perceptual Violence vs. European Excellence INFO: Two dirty, dangerous, and deadly Brawlers will enter the HWA Arena with one goal: Destroy anyone who opposes them. The finesse duo of EUROPEAN EXCELLENCE will go against TAYLOR DOUGLAS and BRODY ANDERSON in what could be a show stealer. Can the traditional Two easily walk over PV? Or will Taylor Douglas and Brody Anderson show them the new way of Wrestling? *MAIN EVENT* Terrence Steele vs. "Simply Golden" Jeffrey Keenan vs. "Showtime" Trey Slater INFO: David and Goliath right here. Steele is a monstrous man who takes no BS. Keenan likes to prance his way through things but come Tuesday Night, that might not be an option. Add Trey Slater, who was a late entry, and you have a very lethal concoction. Keenan may be Simply Golden but after Hardcore Tendencies, will his Gold still glitter? * Also!, Phil D. Payne is said to have a State of Address for the HWA! What will he say!? How will he be welcomed back to New York?! Can we expect the rumored RAID of the HWA? * More talent continues to step through the Doors of the HWA. As word spreads, more and more realize HWA is where all they want lies. Come Hardcore Tendencies, could we see a few new faces to show up, unexpectedly? *PLUS, Tune in to HARDCORE TENDENCIES to see the newest Promotion in Wrestling take over the Airwaves!! VII. Lets kick of the HWA on a good note and lets take it easy and fall into the first week with some positives. Might be some rough few days ahead, weeding it all out but I can tell you all now, if you don't feel you can devote time to HWA, please do not hang on any longer. I don't want to cut anyone off but I don't want anyone to feel jipped come the time when things get heavy and you do not have time to handle it. Just don't do that to yourselves and if you EVER need time off, please don't hesitate to ask. Phil D. Payne HWA © 1998 - 2000
