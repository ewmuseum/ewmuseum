Pants.] RICHARD FREEDMAN: Well fans, just recently I tried to grab Corey Hart for some words on his upcoming Match-up with HWA newcomer Stephen Strattford but as you can see, no dice.
The Captivating One just told me .. "Strattford is a very well trained Wrestler and is a spec- tacular Cruiserweight Wrestler. It'll be hard for me to walk away victoriously but that won't stop me from trying."

God knows what these two could pull out at Tendencies but from the looks of it, I expect something great. Back to you, Markie Mark.
[The image dissolves into Mark Cleveland, holding up a thin stack of papers, apprantly bothered by that last comment.]
MC: Markie Mark huh? OK, DICK. Moving right along! The Second Contest of the evening is one of contrasted styles. One man, a rich - pompous - and arrogant Warran Worthington, riding a high along with his supposed "Business Associate" Sebastian
Montague. How long can this Double Teaming in Singles Matches go on for? Well, until one of our Presidents step in, I don't see a resolution any time soon. Especially with both being as pre-occupied as they are. But this week, with Worthington battling one of Wrestlings most monstrous men around, Myssing
Lynk - I doubt a Double Team will be effective! Lets go see why Lynk has something to think about this week.

Inside the ring, Sebastian has the Chair wound up to lay into Anderson but Referee Lane is trying to Wrestle the chair away from him. Worthington casually walks down the isle with a stainless steel Briefcase in his hands. Warran ducks a cup of beer and points in the general direction from which it came from.
Worthington stands behind Brody Anderson and without a second of notice, winds up and slams the Briefcase right into the back of Brody's head.
His body falls out of the ropes as Sebastian Montague dumps the chair outside. Worthington ducks down and crawls infront of the Guard Rail, off to the side. Montague lifts up Brody Anderson over his shoulders, spins around and drops him on his head with the Report Card! The image dissolves as Mark Cleveland, now standing up against one of the unmanned
Camera's.] MC: What lies ahead for the rest of the HWA, I'm not to sure. Warran and Sebastian are not as unlikely as a Duo as you might expect. Both grew up as spoiled brats, living the hog life because of their Parents wealth. It is because of that, both are where they are at now. Warran is a cer- tified Stock Brocker and one of the Worlds best Technical
Wrestlers while Sebastian is just a little Rich Boy, with a High IQ and a lack of compassion. The tide could turn real quick when Rhett Garland meets Sebastian at Fall Fra- cas, November 12th!
One thing I think that has gone unnoticed is the beating Worthington and Montague laid on an already weary Perceptual Violence. Now, I know Brody Anderson and Taylor Douglas and knowing them assures me there is bad blood between these for men. When you bring Rhett Garland into the picture and the HWA TV Title, god, my head hurts just thinking about the possibilities! And it is with that in mind, I turn it over to one half of the HWA's Most Hated Duo, Sebastian
Montague! [The Camera opens up to the Locker Room of Sebastian Montague which is fittingly remodeled to resemble a Class Room. fade in. The visual fades in showing the night sky. The stars spread out across the sky and the moonlight covering all like a blanket. In the midst of the darkness, emerges a light. The light is that of a lighter. The visual fades to the flame, watching as it is brought to the tip of a cigarette. Slowly, the tip of the cigarette flares and is followed by a cloud of dark gray smoke.
The camera zooms out slowly revealing the man smoking the cigarette. It is none other than the deadly Taylor Douglas. His eyes, half closed, trying to focus in the darkness which has turned everything into a uniform shade of gray. He removes the cigarette from his chapped lips and he exhales the smoke into the air. His eyes glance over, acknowledging the camera for the first time. A small smirk grows on his face as he spits to the ground. He crouches down hang-
ing his hands between his legs. All of this...seems so usual to him. Almost like a ritual he performs on a certain occasion. His lips part and his deep, weathered voice is heard]
[DTD]: Day by day I am improving. With each battle...with each day I grow stronger. I know...Taylor Douglas becoming more remarkable than he already is? Yes, even I can be something greater. My hard work has been seen though, there is no doubt about that. I stand here, an undefeated man in the HWA. Will I let this go to my head? You better believe it!
[Douglas lets out a small laugh and he raises the cigarette back to his mouth. After a brief drag, he holds in the smoke for a while. He lets the smoke run slowly from his nostrils and speaks again]
[DTD]: Warren Worthington...Sebastian Montigue. Two men who obviously have no idea what they have dug themselves into. What where these two bafoons thinking when they attacked my partner, Brody? I have no clue. I have no clue because I don't see why any man would purposely sign his own death warrant. Warren, Sebastian...I am not going to focus on one of you, because you both deserve to have my attention. And at this point in your careers in the HWA, that is something you will soon regret.
Sebastian, we are stepping into the ring soon, and it is then that you will get what's coming to you. As for you Worthington? Just sit back and relax, but do not get too comfortable. For if you do, it may put you in a_DEADLY_situation...
[Douglas stands and he dusts his pant legs off and then takes one last long drag from the cigarette. He lets out the smoke slowly from his mouth and flicks the cigarette into the night sky. His tongue creeps from his mouth and licks his lips
as he speaks again] [DTD]: "Doc" Rhett Garland. A man who I know little about. The reason being that he is a man who I don't need to know a lot about. My place in this match is not as the main attraction, no...that is the place of you Rhett.
And that half-assed wrestler Sebastian Montigue. I... [smirks] [DTD]: Am just a man on the outside looking in. Waiting for the right time to make my presence felt. Waiting for the two of you to finish what you call

Besides, if you think that I can't back up my word...then you are sorely mistaken. For now, focus on eachother. Try to forget the little bee buzzing in your ear. That busy, busy little bee...

We just heard from the always original Sebastian Montague and Taylor Douglas.., a man who means all business. But with that Commercial, it has brought me to the larger picture that is shaping the HWA. I'm sure you all know by know, I'm talking about the Presidential Struggle.
And I could not do justice to Phil D. Payne and Mike Emery by not ending this with them! What a crazy turn of events we've all witnessed the past three weeks. It all started on the HWA's first event of the Millennium, upon it's return to Wrestling!
The entire week prior to Hardcore Tendencies, the Internet buzzed with Rumors and supposed Reports of what was going to go down.
Right they were when many said their would be a raid of the HWA brought by Phil D. Payne. However, HWA Diehards ignored such ridiculous rumors, backing Payne with the utmost faith.
[The screen turns into a black image with a HWA Logo over it. Cleveland's voice comes over the screen as white text begins to appear, in sync with
Mark.] "When I first heard about these Rumors from all these pussy Internet Reporters, I had to laugh. These little kids playing around on their Daddie's Computer while he was on a Lunch break decided to make a dirt sheet like their Tim Nielsen or somethin'
and predict what the HWA is going to do. It's the exact shit Wrestlin' doesn't need. Especially an Indy." [Cleveland re-appears as the image fades out.] MC: It is obvious by this, the Internet World of Wrestling is still not universally accepted. Anyhow, I'm sure this one mans feelings are in line with many more. However, the Internet has a strong presence in all we do so I do see no solution in the near future.
But the HWA will continue to embrace the World Wide Web, where you can all go to www.x8od.com/hwa/ . We'll be back after these comments from Ethan Van Ryan. [EVR]: "Steele.. Stevens.. Both of you have made a pretty big mistake. Going against Payne was pretty bad, but now,.. Now you guys have made the biggest mistake you ever could have..

[EVR]: "HELLO HEADACHE!! GOODBYE STEELE!!! [EVR breaks into sadistic laughter, then smashs the chair into the camera, cause it to go to static. Fade to Commercial for
Terrence Steele.] He follows his own rules, if any at all. He has done what he wanted to who he wanted since he got here. He has displayed power of immense magnitude. He has shown the ability to take control of a situation. He has stood tall with men he shouldn't have been able to. He is the man who stands up to the talent, and then walks right by. There is no question the amount of talent in the HWA, but the questions are as follows.... Can the talent control the man who doesn't care how good they are? Can the talent over come his strength? Can the talent figure out his motives? And most importantly... Can the talent stop Terrence Steele? [Fade into Mark Cleveland, sitting at his Desk within the HWA Arena.] MC: Welcome back everyone! Continuing on with one of the biggest story surrounding the HWA, Phil D. Payne vs. Mike Emery! Two men who've known each other for years. They met in Ed Payne's, yes, Phil's brother, EVCW. There, both clashed for the right to take over Ed E. Payne's failing EVCW. Payne was handed the Company and drove it to the ground. However, Emery went off and created the HWA. A Promotion that was successful in the North East for almost eight months. Now, Mike has returned and for odd reasons, he gave Payne the Promotion!
[Cut to clip of Mike Emery and Phil D. Payne in a small Press Conference Room. Emery hands Payne a pen as he signs his name to the contract that ultimately gave him the HWA.]
It was here, fans, on October 1st, 2000 - Mike Emery and Phil D. Payne came to terms on the newly restored Hardcore Wrestling Alliance. However, Payne ignored the the small clause that stated if and when Mike Emery decided to return to his Presidential position of this Company, he could, unchallenged by anyone.
Payne included in that. [Cut to a clip from the first Hardcore Tendencies. Terrence Steele spikes Trey Slater with Trauma and tosses Jeff Keenan on top of him for the pin fall, as Youthenasia hits the ring eventually decimating Keenan and Slater.]
It all began right then and there. Steele was the clear cut winner but then he did the unthinkable. He gave up his hard earned victory and gave it to Jeff Keenan, who on that night had definetly put his best days behind him.
The Internet buzzed with rumors on why Terrence did so! When Youthenasia invaded the HWA, the pieces of the puzzle began to fall into place! V: I'm glad you made it, Terrence. [Steele turns around to see standing there, HWA President Phil D. Payne. Payne, in a black Suit, white Buttondown, and red Tie has a small bag in his left hand. As he struts up to Terrence Steele, he looks left-to-right in a cautious manner.]
