Opening Comments The Monkey is off my back. =) Card is out. Looks good but I'll let you guys be the ultimate decider of that. Anyhow, great turn out last week. Hope to see it continue. Some new faces have come in and some old ones have been cut off. So, lets keep things going strong guys. Our first big Supercard Event approaches. Also, the Deadlines have been a little tighter this week. You guys have roughly three days to strat/In-Ring and four to Roleplay. So, lets try to avoid the lack of time hamper our activity, if at all possible. HWA President Phil D. Payne II. News & Rumors - Immediately following Monday Night's events, it is being reported by the Revolution Report that some of New York's finest were needed to break apart the "Camps" of the HWA. Apparantly, it started when Dillion McCain spray painted EPTIOME on Dan Stevens' Car. It escalated from there. - Officials within the HWA are saying Justin A. Hole could get a Valet to bring a much needed spark to his entire appearence. - Tim Nielson, from the Indy Circuit, is trying to expose the HWA, by reporting Payne has backed pay checks on certain Wrestlers within the Company. Although not denied, Payne has plead the 5th concerning this topic. - Following his big time win, Dan Stevens' bought the entire HWA Locker Room, with the exception of some obvious names, a few rounds of beers and some say it's one of the main reasons Stevens is so well liked in the back. His "nice personality." - Terrence Steele suffered a minor concussion during the Main Event and took 37 stitches to keep his large forehead wound shut. We hope he has a speedy recovery. Week in Review STEPHEN STRATFORD pinning COREY HART via Silver Lining. MYSSING LYNK was defeated by WARRAN WORTHINGTON by outside help from SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE and The Huge Elbow From Above. In a 3 way, TAYLOR DOUGLAS and SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE were bested by RHETT GARLAND after Pure Hatred on Douglas for the Pin Fall. DAN STEVENS defeated DILLION McCAIN via It's All Jive. In the Main Event, TERRENCE STEELE was DISQUALIFIED due to OUTSIDE INTERFERENCE as ETHAN VAN RYAN was awarded the Victory. [Side Note: Payne re-instated the DQ Rule after it's banishment from the HWA two years ago.] V. HWA Weekly Event Preview * HARDCORE TENDENCIES * The Resurrection of Hardcore! Sunday November 12th, 2000 Coming from the HWA Arena, LIVE in New York City!! *OPENING MATCH-UP* Ask and you shall recieve is a title so fitting for this Contest. STEPHEN "SILVER" STRATFORD will take on MYSSING LYNK in a Match of serious size differences, among other things. Many feel Payne is sending Stephen to slaughter but he's aiming to prove them wrong. *SECOND MATCH* In a Debut Match for both Teams, WAISTED EXISTANCE will clash with MANDATORY SELF FORNIFICATION! Both teams are being highly praised from their respective Schools and the regional Action they've seen. It'll be a treat to see these four go at it. *THIRD MATCH* In what appears to be a squash, "SADISTIC SCHOLAR" SEBASTIAN MONTAGUE will meet SHANE "THE F'N MAN" MARTIN in a TABLE MATCH! It seems this Match has very little importance but from what I've been hearing, it could do a lot for both men. *FOURTH MATCH* For The Television Title, two enemies will clash in a bloody brawl. "DOC" RHETT GARLAND will once again get his hands on WARRAN WORTHINGTON! However, [name withheld] has fooled with the Match to assure a WORTHY Champion is crowned! They will meet in a Steel Cage and if someone does happen to Run-in, whoever they attack, will be awarded the Victory. *MAIN EVENT* Sending the Fans home wanting more, Emery and Payne, seperatly, have decided on a GAUNTLENT MATCH! Starting it off, "BRUTAL" BRIAN FLOYD and "THE EPITOME" DILLION McCAIN. From there, number three and four - "SWEET" JARRED BOMBAY and TERRENCE STEELE. Following them, at numbers five and six, ETHAN VAN RYAN and DAN "WINDY CITY" STEVENS. * Many rumors are circulating that some of the evenings Match will be changed come Sunday Night by Emery or Payne, in an attempt at getting a shot at one another. * The Internet is buzzing with the HWA and many men from outside Promotions are threatning to be in Attendence, to kill off any HWA Steam. Some feel the HWA's decline to some Supercard Events is a slap in the face and they'll get revenge anyway how! * LIVE, Sunday Night - November 12th, 2000 - From the HWA Arena, every Wrestler comes in touch with their HARDCORE TENDENCIES!
