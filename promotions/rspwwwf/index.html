A weekly parody broadcast that cast rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy's own regulars, under their own posting handles, as an all-star roster -- crowning a first World Heavyweight Champion across an eight-man tournament, staging a first pay-per-view, and booking a real, well-known Usenet nickname ("Net.Cop") alongside the group's own invented factions. Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

RSPWWWF opened its own first broadcast on 27 September 1998 declaring itself, in its own announcers' words, “the first-ever episode of RSPWWWF Uncut Uncooked Uncensored” — a weekly show it staged out of a different city each time, Charlotte, then Baltimore, then Cincinnati, building toward its own pay-per-view debut, In Your Pants: Fully Bloated.

Its own first storyline was a World Heavyweight Championship tournament. Kelly Vaters tapped out in the opening round to a rival's Sharpshooter; Conn MacKintosh won his own opener but was spike-piledriven on the concrete floor by a newly formed faction calling itself the New Age Net.Cops before losing his semi-final hurt; Alicia Snow beat John Henry DeJong in the other semi-final by countout, amid interference from a rival stable, and reached the final, where a challenger the surviving record does not name beat her with a bridging suplex to become the promotion's first champion.

That stable, the Troll World Order, broke apart on its own air the following week in a backstage brawl its own members turned on each other in; Gern Blanston, one of its own, was revealed as a plant for a rival faction, Pro-Moderation X, after winning a twenty-man battle royal for the next title shot. Mattie Carrington chokeslammed two different on-air authority figures across the same stretch of shows without breaking stride.

Stefan Damian, billed as the promotion's own first Internet Champion, took a leave of absence after a backstage skirmish; the wrestlers left behind organized under the name the JOB Squad and sang him a tribute number, in full, on the air, to bring him back.

The surviving record ends on 13 November 1998, with a request to join the promotion's own writing staff. Whether that request was answered, and how, or whether, the promotion carried on past its own first pay-per-view, isn't recorded.
