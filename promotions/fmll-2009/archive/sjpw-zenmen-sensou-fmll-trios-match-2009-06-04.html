SJPW Zenmen Sensou: FMLL Trios Match

4 June 2009
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ShawnHartXXX — 4 June 2009 The following was taken from Super Japan Pro Wrestling's supercard ZENMEN SENSOU in the summer of '04. It was my first attempt at getting a lucha league going and the fed's owner was kind enough to give us some time on the show. At any rate, it will give you at least somewhat of an idea of what some of the matches in FMLL might be like. Cheers.
[The camera cuts to the second broadcast position, where Castillo & Sandoval have replaced Martin & Coltrane from PWG...]
CASTILLO: Muchas gracias, Señor Otaku and bienvenidos a la Federación Mexicana de Lucha Libre! I’m Julian Castillo, sitting here alongside your color commentator for the evening, Rudolfo Sandoval, and Rudolfo...we are just about ready for what should be an excellent contest!
SANDOVAL: Julian, as you know, el capitán del bando Rudo is one of my personal favorites, the ever-suave EL SEGUNDO! With both the sea creature Serpiente Leviatán and the almighty PODEROSO at his side, I’m gonna have to say los ganadores, the winners of this match will inevitably be Los RRRRUDOS!
CASTILLO: Then again, their opponents here tonight at Zenmen Sensou could prove to be quite formidable as the world’s HAPPIEST luchador, EL ARCO IRIS will captain the colorful duo of Sol Brillante and Cielos Azules!
SANDOVAL: Natural disasters they are most certainly NOT! CASTILLO: Whether or not you approve of their styles, all three men are amazing athletes and competitors and it should be an exciting match!
SANDOVAL: Hold your tongue, we’re getting started here! ::PRIMERA CAIDA (First Fall):: CASTILLO: OK, now after some conferring in their respective corners El Arco Iris and Serpiente Leviatán have elected to start. Both are pacing around the ring, trying to get a feel for each other...What do you think of these two, Rudolfo?
SANDOVAL: El Arco Iris IS well versed in various styles, but I believe Serpiente Leviatán has his number.
CASTILLO: Well, after some hesitation, both lock up, and El Arco Iris quickly snaps Serpiente down with a quick arm drag and mugs for the crowd! Serpiente seems to have not expected that.
SANDOVAL: Serpiente knows, he's just surveying the situation, Castillo. CASTILLO: Serpiente is now eyeing El Arco Iris attentively...another lockup, El Arco Iris gets an amateur style headlock takeover with a cover, réferi counts UNO, Serpiente gets the shoulder up and is struggling to keep it up...Serpiente wiggles his legs up and headscissors Iris to take him off his shoulders, but Iris kips up out of it and both are standing once again! Now El Arco Iris is offering a handshake to Serpiente! Serpiente is hesitant...but accepts! But wait, Iris pulls him in!
SANDOVAL: BELLY TO BELLY SUPLEX! CASTILLO: No...no, it's just a HUG! El Arco Iris simply wanted a hug from his opponent. Serpiente sure isn't returning the feeling as he promptly shoves El Arco Iris off, kicks him in the gut, then takes him down with a foot sweep! Serpiente turns Iris over and grabs his arm...spinning arm lock from Serpiente! That has got to hurt Iris’ shoulder!
SANDOVAL: Leviatán knows better than to play into Iris' games. CASTILLO: Leviatán lays in a few stomps to that shoulder and no sooner locks in a Fujiwara armbar! El Arco Iris is squealing in pain! Here come Cielos Azules and Sol Brillante - they're trying to take Serpiente off of Iris...But here come Poderoso and El Segundo! Craziness ensues as this thing has become an all-out BRAWL!
SANDOVAL: All 6 luchadores are in the ring, Julian! CASTILLO: Not for long...Serpiente has just clotheslined Sol Brillante out of the ring and went flying out with him! Cielos Azules is tangling with El Segundo against the ropes.. and here comes PODEROSO, charging belly first and all three go flying over onto the floor!! El Arco Iris is the only one left in the ring... Oh no...What is he doing?
SANDOVAL: Looks like the Rainbow Man is gonna fly! CASTILLO: SPACE FLYING TIGER DROP ONTO THE WHOLE MASS OF FIVE! SANDOVAL: ¡Increíble! ..But El Arco Iris looks to have plunked himself somewhere in the cartwheel process.
CASTILLO: To be as high risk a wrestler as El Arco Iris or any other of FMLL's luchadores you have to have all your body at 100%... Oh no, El Segundo seems to have noticed Iris' situation...El Segundo has seized Iris by the arm RAMS him shoulder first into the ring post!
SANDOVAL: El Segundo may be Sr. 2nd place, but it's his ability to expose situations like this that makes him Numero UNO in my mind! Rudo, Rudo, Rudissiiimos are taking care of business! Sol Brillante and Cielos Azules are having one heck of a time on the outside...
CASTILLO: Poderoso scoops Cielos and slams him on the concrete, then throws him back in the ring.
SANDOVAL: ...Just as Rudolfo Sandoval takes care of business in the booth! CASTILLO: El Segundo has taken Iris back in as well... Leviatán lays in some quick kicks and punches to the side and chest of Sol Brillante, and whips him back in. The Rudo team is setting up their opponents...all three whip them to the ropes... but the Técnicos come back with a triple flying forearm!!
SANDOVAL: ¡Pobrecitos! CASTILLO: Poderoso has stumbled backwards and fallen over and onto Serpiente Leviatán! SANDOVAL: Even the sea creature cannot withstand the impact of Poderoso. CASTILLO: That leaves El Segundo...El Arco Iris is telling him to get up...Iris' partners seem to know what's coming... El Segundo is up and dazed...BUT NOT FOR LONG AS EL ARCO IRIS LEVELS HIM WITH A SUPERKICK RIGHT TO THE JAW!
SANDOVAL: Ah! El Arco Iris has stolen Segundo's smile with the HAPPY DANCE! Ayayay... CASTILLO: Iris covers! UNO, DOS, TRES! The first fall is over with the elimination of el capitán del bando Rudo!
::Técnicos: 1, Rudos: 0:: SANDOVAL: Exemplary teamwork from Los Técnicos, but my man Segundo will recover! CASTILLO: Well, he's barely managed to roll out of the ring and the Rudos are regrouping on the floor.
SANDOVAL: I'd like to take un momentito here before la segunda caida and thank Super Japan Pro Wrestling for allowing us to perform here, since Castillo forgot to.
CASTILLO: I get caught in the heat of the moment, Rudolfo, but yes, it's a great opportunity for our brothers in the orient to let us showcase our talent here tonight....And as our lovely model Juana shows, the second fall has started!
::SEGUNDA CAIDA (Second Fall):: SANDOVAL: Look for the Rudo team to exert their authority here... CASTILLO: Poderoso has stepped in for his team, and is telling Cielos Azules and Sol Brillante to take him on at the same time!
SANDOVAL: Poderoso is 200% machismo! CASTILLO: Both have stepped in...Poderoso is telling them to hit the ropes! Cielos and Sol hit a double shoulder block...but Poderoso doesn't budge!
SANDOVAL: It's like hitting a big, hairy brick wall, amigo. CASTILLO: They try for another one...but Poderoso takes them down with the flabbiest double-lariat I've ever seen!
SANDOVAL: Machismo! Just like you and I... CASTILLO: Cielos and Sol are shaken, but not stirred, as they confer a little bit on a strategy...and now they're running in opposite directions...in circles around Poderoso?
SANDOVAL: This is something I haven't seen before. CASTILLO: Cielos and Sol are making a blue and yellow twister around Poderoso, who just can't decide which one to try and catch! Round and round they go...Poderoso is dizzy! He's staggering!
SANDOVAL: Oh no! CASTILLO: Here comes Brillante with a POKE to the chest! SANDOVAL: Fingerpoke of DOOOM, but Poderoso STILL stands!! Brillante shot back by the sheer force of Poderoso's pectorals!
CASTILLO: Not for long, Sr. Sandoval, as Cielos Azules catches Sol off the rebound, grabs him by the arm and whips him right at Poderoso for the dropkick!
SANDOVAL: And here comes EL SEGUNDO!! CASTILLO: El Segundo lays in a hard right fist to Sol's jaw...and as he comes out of the corner drop toe holds him face-first to the mat! Segundo follows with a grounded headlock...Sol gets to his feet...backs against the ropes and throws Segundo off towards the opposite side...
SANDOVAL: Look at Serpiente Leviatán...distracting el réferi Robles. Veeerrrrrrry crafty. CASTILLO: ...and leapfrogs Segundo as he rebounds, but Segundo stops himself and throws his leg backwards, knocking Sol right in the cojones as he comes down! Blatant faul, but the ref wasn't looking! Serpiente gets away from the ref, and Segundo covers Sol! UNO...DOS...TRES! Sol Brillante has been eliminated in a most uncouth manner!
SANDOVAL: No faul, noooooo faul Castillo. Totally accidental. CASTILLO: Well, what do you call it, then? SANDOVAL: An errant flailing of limbs. I think he caught him in the thigh. CASTILLO: Yes, I'm sure El Segundo was showing us his tremendous interpretive dance moves. SANDOVAL: He has been spotted in many of the popular Japanese discos, Julian. CASTILLO: Either way, Sol is out and now El Arco Iris has come back in and charges El Segundo, who sees it and tries for that Fujiwara armbar on that messed up shoulder of Iris, but Iris is holding his ground and not letting himself get put in the hold!
SANDOVAL: It's interesting to see these two men in the ring. They have been rivals of sorts lately, and it is probably only a matter of time before they go mano a mano.
CASTILLO: Segundo is struggling to get El Arco Iris into the submission and has a tight grip on the arm...but El Arco Iris slides between Segundo’s legs and rolls him up in a cross-arm cradle with bridge! The ref counts UNO, DOS, but Segundo kicks out! El Arco Iris is simply unpredictable, Rudolfo!
SANDOVAL: I can say for certain that I never would have predicted a man to prance about all in rainbow in our FMLL ring. But in all seriousness, El Arco Iris is without question one of the most unique luchadores in any part of the world.
CASTILLO: Segundo is now fuming and wants to finish off Iris quick, he charges, but El Arco Iris catches him with an amateur-style double-leg takedown! El Arco Iris then hits the ropes, but Poderoso grabs his leg and trips him up! They drag him out of the ring, and Cielos Azules comes to take his place. Cielos comes churning full steam but Segundo catches him with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker!
SANDOVAL: ¡Máximo Impacto with the quebradora! CASTILLO: Segundo turns Cielos to his stomach and drops an elbow onto the small of Cielos' back! Another one! Cielos is not in too good a situation right now.
SANDOVAL: Poderoso is propping Iris up onto his own shoulders on the outside...Serpiente is on the apron...he TAKES OFF, but is tripped up by Sol Brillante, saving Iris from the flying lariat!!
CASTILLO: While they're taking care of Segundo's teammates on the outside, Segundo has locked in an STF on Cielos Azules! His back is getting wrenched like there is no tomorrow! Cielos grasps about to see if he could possibly, with some shred of hope, make it to the ropes, but he is in the dead center of the ring...he submits! Cielos submits to the STF and the Rudos win the second fall! Segundo’s teammates aren't done though, as they whip Iris and Sol into each
other! I think they hit their heads! All the Técnicos are lying broken and battered about the ring, Rudolfo!
::Técnicos: 1, Rudos: 1:: ::TERCERA CAIDA (Third Fall):: SANDOVAL: An excellent move employed by El Segundo, putting pressure on various points of Azules' body. The Rudos have imposed their will.
CASTILLO: El Segundo and his team are rolling the Técnicos into the ring...and placing them side by side... Poderoso looks like he's going to impose HIS WILL on them!
SANDOVAL: Like fish in a barrel. CASTILLO: PODERSO HITS A BIG SPLASH ON ALL THREE OF THE TECNICOS! Poderoso lies down for the pin! He could end the fall in mere seconds! UNO... DOS... NO! The combined strength of Iris, Cielos, and Sol let them kick out from under Poderoso's girth!
SANDOVAL: Pushed back with a force eerily reminiscent of the Care Bear's Stare! CASTILLO: The Técnicos have managed to get to their feet...Serpiente Leviatán, and Poderoso start brawling with Cielos and Sol and that spills to the outside...leaving los capitánes!
SANDOVAL: Here we go, Castillo... CASTILLO: Segundo charges with fists a flying on El Arco Iris...Irish whip to the corner, Segundo charges, but El Arco Iris gets a foot up!
SANDOVAL: Nice counter maneuver by El Arco Iris, but how long can he survive the onslaught of EL SEGUNDO?!
CASTILLO: Segundo gets his bearing and charges with a lariat, but El Arco Iris ducks and Segundo lands chest first with the turnbuckle! IRIS WITH A TIGER MASK-STYLE MOONSAULT KICK ON SEGUNDO'S BACK!
SANDOVAL: Segundo is sent flying! CASTILLO: Cielos and Sol on the outside have floored Serpiente with a double lariat, and he goes tumbling back and trips up Poderoso who stumbles headfirst into the ring post!
SANDOVAL: Ayayay... CASTILLO: Cielos whips Serpiente to Iris, who will look to get vengeance for that attack on his shoulder early on... Iris with a roundhouse kick...but Serpiente ducks and scores a thrust kick right to Iris' gut!
SANDOVAL: Let's see him smile that away... CASTILLO: Serpiente off the ropes, and Iris goes down after a leg lariat right across his throat!
SANDOVAL: Nice shot to the windpipe. I know from experience how painful such a blow can be, Julian.
CASTILLO: Serpiente hooks the arm with the injured shoulder...Chicken wing crossface! El Arco Iris is in horrible pain, but he is not submitting to this excruciating hold. He's fighting with all of his will to get to the ropes!
SANDOVAL: Leviatán really has it locked in, but I don't see Iris' giving up just yet...DESPITE the injuries.
CASTILLO: Iris is kicking his legs madly...and his foot gets to the bottom rope! The ref calls for the break!
SANDOVAL: Nice work by Robles catching it and making the call, but look at Serpiente! A-Ha...
CASTILLO: Wait, Leviatán no longer has to worry about Iris as Cielos Azules has made his way to the top rope and knocks Serpiente in the back of the head with a missile dropkick! Sol is in too...Serpiente is up, Cielos whips him to the ropes, then hip-tosses him onto Sol's shoulders!
SANDOVAL: Alert move from Cielos Azules, Serpiente is in a bad way noooow! CASTILLO: Sol drops Serpiente with a big powerbomb! Iris is on the top turnbuckle... Tornillo plancha hits Serpiente Leviatán square on the chest! Iris covers...UNO... DOS... TRES! Serpiente is ELIMINATED!
SANDOVAL: NO! Driven back to the sea by a smiley-faced crazy man! The Rudos are in big trouble!
CASTILLO: Poderoso steps in the ring and is calling the Técnicos' attention... so El Segundo can come from behind and hit El Arco Iris with a shining wizard right to his shoulder!
SANDOVAL: Poderoso shows there's more to him than just brute strength. CASTILLO: Poderoso then charges with a magnificent spear right to the mid-section of Cielos Azules, the same that was punished with that STF!
SANDOVAL: He's had a tough time in each fall, and now it's beginning to show. CASTILLO: Sol Brillante has no idea what's going on, he's trying to figure out the craziness, but too late as Segundo runs from behind and nails LA BOFETADA PLATEADA!
SANDOVAL: ¡Máximo Impacto! EL SEGUUUUUUNDO has landed! CASTILLO: Segundo covers, and neither Cielos or Iris can make the save! UNO... DOS... TRES! The game is tied and it's down to sudden death!
SANDOVAL: A sign of things to come, Castillo? Cielos and Iris seem to have lost a step since the start of the match.
CASTILLO: Segundo turns his attention back to Cielos Azules as Poderoso picks him up and grabs his arms so Segundo can lay in the fists to the ribs and gut... the ref is warning him not to continue this for too long by risk of disqualification...
SANDOVAL: Segundo is too smart, he won't allow his team to lose the fall in such a way. CASTILLO: But Iris is on the ring apron now, and Poderoso is telling Segundo to look behind him...
SANDOVAL: Look out! CASTILLO: IRIS WITH A SPRINGBOARD FLIPPING SENTON ONTO SEGUNDO! Cielos has escaped Poderoso's grip and takes the big man down with an Enziguri!
SANDOVAL: That quickly, the momentum shifts! CASTILLO: Poderoso is weebling and wobbling, but he isn't falling down! SANDOVAL: Too much MACHISMO!! Poderoso is like a great tree that can't be chopped! CASTILLO: Cielos wasn't to take him down, so he hits the ropes and goes for a tilt a whirl head scissors--NO! PODEROSO HAS HIM CAUGHT!
SANDOVAL: What is he DOING?! Aaaahh!! MARTINETE! What is he thinking?! This is DANGEROUS...and he’ll disqualify himself and cost his team the match!
CASTILLO: El Arco Iris says no and dropkicks his shin out from under him! Cielos falls on top of him!
SANDOVAL: Oooooooh that was close, Julian. Even I don't condone the use of the martinete. CASTILLO: El Arco Iris is telling Cielos Azules to go to the top rope as he manages to position Poderoso!
SANDOVAL: Cielos Azules is right at home up high...what is he going to do?! CASTILLO: FROG SPLASH ON PODEROSO! Now Iris is up top! SANDOVAL: Poderoso will withstand!! He must!! He is PODEROSO!! CASTILLO: I don't think it looks good for the big man...Iris with a 450 splash! All that color spinning into Poderoso's undoing!
SANDOVAL: Where is EL SEGUNDO?! The Técnicos are getting away with murder! CASTILLO: Uhhh...I think he's under Poderoso! Iris has covered Poderoso and Cielos Azules climbs on top to add extra weight!
UNO... ...DOS... ...TRES!! SANDOVAL: Oh no... CASTILLO: Poderoso has been pinned and the Técnicos win the fall and match! ::Técnicos: 2, Rudos: 1:: SANDOVAL: Oh my, my oh my... What just happened, Castillo? CASTILLO: Well, it looks like Poderoso's poder wasn't all that poderoso! SANDOVAL: I'm telling you, he's deceptively powerful! CASTILLO: He sure is doing a good job of fooling us as the Técnicos are victorious and celebrating in the ring!
SANDOVAL: Pobrecito...what a shame. El Segundo could not recover and save his teammates. CASTILLO: Look, the Técnicos are having a big group hug in the center of the ring! You can only see such joy and love in FMLL, folks!
SANDOVAL: Forget the joy and love, it's all about la acción...FMLL action! CASTILLO: Well, that's all from the Mexico side of things, let's turn it back over to Ken Otaku and Akira Ono and we'll hope to see you in the future.
SANDOVAL: On behalf of my colleague, Señor Castillo, and la Federación Mexicana de Lucha Libre, buenas noches!
ShawnHartXXX — 20 June 2009 I should probably note that this match was done in more of a lucharesu style, and that FMLL bouts may differ in some ways.
