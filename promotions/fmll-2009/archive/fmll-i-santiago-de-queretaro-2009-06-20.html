ShawnHartXXX — 16 July 2009 Intro/Lucha Triangular CUE UP: "Impacto" by Daddy Yankee. A veritable cornucopia of FMLL luchadores are shown in succession, mugging and playing for the cameras. From Checkmate to PODEROSO to Hijo del Esperanzo and so on, all of the grapplers are featured in rapid fashion. As the music hooks, the reel is brought to a crashing halt and the FMLL logo is stamped across the screen.
CUT TO: The studio, where PORCEL PORCEL is standing by. PORCEL: "Hola señoras y señores and BIENVENIDOS A LUCHA LIBRE FMLL!! This past week marked el evento primero - OUR FIRST EVENT - and, without question, it was IN-CRE-IBLE. We'll be bringing you the action later in the show, but first - the NEWS!"
CUT TO: The Noti-FMLL logo. PORCEL: "With the influx of heavyweight luchadores into la Federación, FMLL owner Arturo Sandoval has formed a committee to name a heavyweight champion."
Footage of Sandoval at a meet and greet is shown. PORCEL: "Under his direction, the championship committee will be evaluating los luchadores for a period of four events, after which the top contenders will compete for el campeonato. In his announcement, Sandoval also established Light-Heavyweight and Middleweight titles, stating that en la Federación, no preference or favoritism will be given to any particular weight-class; the top luchadores will be featured regardless of weight.
An image of the late, great Abismo Negro is shown. PORCEL: "The FMLL has also announced El Torneo Leyenda de Negro, a tournament honoring Andrés Alejandro Palomeque González, el gran Abismo Negro. The tourney will be held annually and feature luchadores from all over the globe. This year's tourney is expected to run in the coming months.
A picture of the FMLL ring appears next to Porcel. PORCEL: "That's the news, Yo soy Porcel PORCEL... coming up - Acción F-M-L-L en SANTIAGO DE QUERÉTARO! Here's Julian Castillo y RUDOLFO Sandoval on the call!"
CUT TO: The arena. Several hundred fans have packed in to a small arena and are stomping and blowing their air horns. In the ring, PANDA MASK, DIEGO EN FUEGO, and FANATIC are in separate corners.
CASTILLO: "Hola señoras y señores, I'm Julian Castillo and with me is Rudolfo Sandoval-" SANDOVAL: "'Ello 'ello!" CASTILLO: "¡Bienvenidos a la Lucha Especial, la Lucha Triangular a una sola caída and there's Panda Mask starting things off with a tope-tope-TOPE!"
SANDOVAL: "¡Hard headbutts from Panda Mask - Diego is doing la Watusi! CASTILLO: "Panda Mask - has Fanatic now, and..." SANDOVAL: "Tope-tope-TOPE!!" CASTILLO: "Fanatic is stumbling now.... and DOWN goes Panda Mask!" SANDOVAL: "All tope'd out apparently!" CASTILLO: "¡LAZO MAGNIFICO from Diego en Fuego! Fanatic did a full flip after absorbing the blow and has landed on his side! Diego with the cradle, el Réferi Robles is there-
UNO.......... DOS........... NO!!! CASTILLO: "Slow-count by Robles; Favoring los Rudos as always." SANDOVAL: "One might dispute that, amigo, but Diego en Fuego is back up in a hurry." CASTILLO: "And there's Panda Mask attempting un lazo, but missing. Panda Mask continues to the far-side ropes, bounces back with a full head of steam-
SMACK! SANDOVAL: "Big chop by Panda Mask!" SMACK! CASTILLO: "And Diego returns the favor!" SANDOVAL: "Pobrecitos... look at their chests!" CASTILLO: "Panda Mask looks like he's had enough and now he's backing into the ropes once again... coming back the other way now and - THERE'S a Huracanrana! ¡Diego esta en Fuego!
SANDOVAL: "LOOK OUT!" CASTILLO: "And there's Patadas VOLADORAS from the up-and-charging Fanatic! Both Panda Mask and Diego are down..... and Fanatic is climbing the turnbuckle!!
The fans ramp up their stomp-air horn combination as Fanatic reaches the top rope. SANDOVAL: "There he goes with el TORNILLO!!" CASTILLO: "¡IMPACTO ENORME from Fanatic! He looks a bit shaken up, but he still has the wherewithal to cover BOTH men-
UNO.... DOS.... And BOTH men kick out, sending Fanatic flying off of them despite a quicker count from Robles."
SANDOVAL: "Panda Mask kips up, but Diego is struggling to find his feet." CASTILLO: "Panda Mask - approaches Diego..." SANDOVAL: "¡Patada-patada-patada!" CASTILLO: "Hard kicks to the mid-section of Diego en Fuego... and now Panda Mask is turning his attention back to Fanatic. Panda Mask... propping his opponent up, and there's the Irish Whip! Fanatic hits the ropes - coming back now.... ¡Quebradora con Giiiiro! Panda Mask going for the cover...
SANDOVAL: "But there's Diego with an elbow drop-" CASTILLO: "Panda rolls out of harm's way, however, and Diego plants the elbow INSTEAD onto the chest of Fanatic! Panda Mask is up on his feet now and here he is with a Senton-"
SANDOVAL: "Both men roll out of the way and Fanatic has rolled his way right out of the ring!!"
CASTILLO: "Panda Mask hits canvas!! Diego quickly moving into the pinning position...BUT PANDA ROLLS HIM UP!
UNO....... DOS....... Diego en Fuego rolls him over -- Panda Mask's arm is under the rope, but Robles doesn't see it!
UNO..... DOS...... TRES!!!!" DING! DING! DING! SANDOVAL: "¡Diego es el ganador!" The fans pop for Diego en Fuego's victory. CASTILLO: "Panda can't believe it!! As Diego dances el Flamenco, Panda Mask is pleading with Robles!"
SANDOVAL: "Robles seems very apologetic, but Panda Mask is clearly not satisfied." CUT TO: Diego speaking with a second official on the outside. CASTILLO: "It looks like Diego was just made aware of what happened and he too looks displeased. As Panda rolls to the outside, Diego is apologizing and trying to console him."
SANDOVAL: "Nothing was settled here tonight. I think we need to see these two mano a mano."
CASTILLO: "Robles has a reputation for these kinds of situations.... hopefully your brother can do something for Panda Mask."
SANDOVAL: "Mi hermano will make it right, I'm sure.... but right now, Diego en Fuego is the winner."
CASTILLO: "We'll be right back." ShawnHartXXX — 16 July 2009 Mari Mari Mari/Lucha Semifinal CUT TO: Porcel in studio. PORCEL: "Alright fans, parejas coming up next... but following our first bout, in an attempt to console Diego en Fuego after he unknowingly participated in the botched finish with Robles y Panda Mask, Mariposa Guerrera caught up with the man backstage. Here's what went down..."
CUT TO: A dejected Diego, slumped over in a chair. CLOSE ON: His upper portion as he covers his face in shame. Suddenly a pair of hands with bright pink nails begins to work his shoulders. Diego begins to turn around, but is quickly rendered inutil by the hands.
DIEGO: "Mmmmmmmm...... ayyy...... ¡Mi Dios! The camera pans out just as Mari leans in and rests his (or her?) head on Diego's shoulder.
MARI: "You like that, Papi?! I can go aaaaaaall niiiiiight LONG!" Diego leaps from his chair, spins around, and nearly stumbles as he faces Mari. DIEGO: "What the (FCC)?!" Mari purses his lips. MARI: "¿Un beso, miiiiii amooooooooor? DIEGO: "¡AAAAAAAAAAAY!" In a flash, Diego was GONE. Mariposa Guerrera couldn't help but pout. CUT TO: Porcel, doing his best fake laugh in studio.
PORCEL: "¡Pobrecita! Thankfully, Mari was quick to find a new.... INTEREST in the main event! But right now, it's time for La Lucha Semifinal - Parejas EXPLOSIVAS con Hijo del Esperanzo, Aqua Caliente y Rey Amante con Arcángel. We pick up the action with los Rudo-Rudo-Rudisimos ahead 1-0 in the contest. Castillo y Sandoval, on the call as always."
CUT TO: Hijo del Esperanzo and Aqua Caliente regrouping on the outside - the air horn-stomping combination has returned with full force. A graphic is shown indicating the one fall to none lead of the Rudo team.
TECNICOS: 0 --- RUDOS: 1 CASTILLO: "Welcome back señoras y señores. Let us get you right to the replay of how la primera caída ended, as the team of Esperanzito y Aqua find themselves trailing."
CUT TO: A slow-motion replay of Amante pressing Aqua onto Arcángel's shoulders, then slamming him down to the mat by the shoulders as the Rudos connect on a double sit-down powerbomb. The impact is rewound and replayed several times in quick succession to show the angle of the head's impact against the canvas.
SANDOVAL: "Máximo impacto para la desnucadora devastadora by Los Rudos. Wow." CASTILLO: "The angle on the neck was such that one could argue it constitutes una falta, but el réferi counted the fall and here we are - Uno for los Rudos y NUNCA for los Técnicos.
SANDOVAL: "And Rey Amante is bringing la acción right to Aqua Caliente with las patadas rapidas!"
CASTILLO: "Amante with kick after kick to the stomach, and there's the whip... here comes Aqua off the ropes, and AMANTE with una quebradora! Esperanzito is back in the ring now to avenge his teammate, but Amante hits another quebradora! Aqua Caliente flips up to his feet and charges, but el Arcángel hops the ropes and there's the lazo from los Rudos!"
SANDOVAL: "Great teamwork from Amante y Arcángel." CASTILLO: "Amante locks in on Aqua Caliente with una CRUCETA now and Aqua is pounding the mat in pain. Arcángel joining in on the act now as he grasps Aqua by the arm and locks in una palanca! Los Rudos are stretching Aqua Caliente at both ends! El réferi is checking with Aqua - I dunno how long he can last!"
SANDOVAL: "They're really working him over." CASYILLO: "Look at Aqua's face. After an early burst of offense en la primera caída, Aqua Caliente now finds himself in big trouble."
SANDOVAL: "No good deed goes unpunished - just look at the expression on Aqua's face. They're REALLY stretching him."
QUICK CUT TO: Aqua Caliente covering his eyes with his free arm. CASTILLO: "El Hijo del Esperanzo meanwhile is climbing the ropes. What's he gonna do?! The fans are on their feet!"
CRASH! SANDOVAL: "¡GUILLOTINA!" CASTILLO: "Esperanzo with una guillotina destructiva on Arcángel and he breaks the hold on Aqua's arm! Amante is releasing Aqua's legs now, and as Esperanzo struggles to his feet..."
SANDOVAL: "¡PATADA-PATADA-PATADA!" CASTILLO: "Amante with the kicks and now he sends el Hijo del Esperanzo off the ropes! Esperanzo comes back with the Plaaaaaancha...... Amante ducks, and Esperanzito catches his partner, who had just found his feet! Los Técnicos are both grounded once again!"
SANDOVAL: "¡Pooooooooobrecitos!" CASTILLO: "Rey Amante y Arcángel joining hands now, and CONNECTING with los codos! They cover their opponents in tandem....
UNO.................... DOS..................... NO." SANDOVAL: "The Técnicos kick out in sequence! Both Rudos are up in a hurry and VERY displeased as they charge over..... but BOTH MEN MISS with los Sentones! El Hijo del Esperanzo and Aqua both roll out of harm's way!"
CASTILLO: "¡Ay caramba! And now Esperanzo y Aqua Caliente are joining arms, but MISSING with the elbow drop! Both Técnicos are grasping their heads in pain! Arcángel now... pulling Aqua up by los hombros and TOSSING him out of the ring! In the meantime, Rey Amante has Esperanzo up and whips him towards the far side ropes.."
SANDOVAL: "There goes Arcángel!" CASTILLO: "Esperanzo rebounds, is rushing back the other way and- CRASH!!! CASTILLO: "Arcángel bounces off the ropes off the opposite side and SLAMS into el Hijo del Esperanzo with una QUEBRADA! Amante drops for the cover... Arcángel provides some extra leverage as he presses into his teammate's back-
UNO........... DOS...... TRES!!!!" DING! DING! DING! SANDOVAL: "¡LO RUDO LO RUDO LO RUUUUUUUUDOOOO!" TECNICOS: 0 --- RUDOS: 2 CASTILLO: "Wha?! You don't usually support the Rudo teams!" SANDOVAL: "I know, but I LOVE Rey Amante's style! He is TRULY El Presidente en los Pantalones!"
Much to the fans' chagrin, Rey Amante and Arcángel celebrate while the Técnico team attempts to collect themselves.
CASTILLO: "Well, los Rudos were dominant for most of the match, and it will be interesting to see where these two luchadores go from here."
SANDOVAL: "Like them or not, nowhere to go but up for these gladiadores!" CASTILLO: "Maybe... right now, we've got a quick break, but THEN-" SANDOVAL: "La Lucha ESTELAR!" CASTILLO: "We'll be right back!" ShawnHartXXX — 16 July 2009 Lucha Estelar To be edited in
