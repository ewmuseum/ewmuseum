Archive: Fear Becomes Dual Champ JJ: Hello and welcome. Fear is expected to come out any moment now for the presentation of the IWF
Hardcore Championship belt. Max Rakus is expected to be crowned here tonight and I personally think its just sickening! Who the hell does Fear think he is anyway?
BOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMMMM! JJ: Here we go fans Fear and Max making their way to the ring. Just look at those two. This is absolutely pathetic! I can’t bear and watch this. This just isn't right. Shouldn't there be a tournament or something. Where's Mr. Grubermen? Fear just can’t do what he wants. This is too much.
Fear: Life is just wonderful these days! Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Fear: Before I make the presentation, I would like to say a couple of things. Crowd: A-HOLE! A-HOLE! A-HOLE! A-HOLE! Fear: SHUT THE HELL UP! Fan: You Suck! Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Fear: You know for quite sometime I've held the IWF Championship. I got to thinking. There really isn't any competition here in the IWF anymore. I mean the only one that had any balls at all to face me is in jail! The rest of the IWF Superstars are afraid of me, and quite frankly I think its great! I look back at all the matches, all the fame, all the celebrity guest appearances...
Fear: I've been thinking to myself that’s just not enough. Which brings me to the IWF Hardcore Championship. Tonight was suppose to be the night that Max would be crowned the new Hardcore Champion. Max: Wait a minute Fear, just what are you saying? Fear: Shut up Max and listen to what I have to say! I got to thinkin’ about this title and came to a conclusion. Who's the one that uses the steel chairs, tables, and anything else he can get his hands on?
Max: Well I guess that would be you Fear. Fear: I am the king of hardcore! So Max I hope you can forgive me for what I'm about to do. Business is business and life isn't fair all the time.
JJ: Oh what a shot by Fear to Max. This is unbelievable! Fear with a huge power bomb and Max is out like a light. What the hell is going on here? Fear a dual champion? This cant be! This just isn't right. Man, someday Fear is going to get his.
Fear: So without further ado I now introduce to you the NEW IWF HARDCORE CHAMPION, FEAR! Crowd: BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! Fear: I am the best there ever was, and the best this business has ever seen. No one and I mean no one will ever compare to me. I make the rules around here and I rule the IWF! Yeah, life is good. Until next time suckers. HAHAHAHAHA!
JJ: A dual champion my ass! Fear had this all planned. Max gets screwed out of the IWF Hardcore title and is disowned by Fear. Man I tell you Fear has been on a tear here at the IWF and I'm getting real sick and tired of it. Somebody has got to stop him. Please anybody!
