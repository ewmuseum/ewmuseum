IWF: Fear Loses It All

Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Archive: Fear Loses It All JJ: Welcome back fans, we are told that Max has a special announcement tonight concerning Fear. Remember just last week Fear backstabbed Max and crowned himself the new IWF Hardcore Champion!
Here's Max now making his way to the ring. Just what is running through his mind right now? I would be irate. Maybe Max wants to challenge Fear hear tonight!
Max: You know Fear last week I learned a valuable lesson. That lesson being, that nothing is given to you in this life. You just have to earn it and take it no matter what the cost is. Especially if it costs you a friendship. As I pondered on what transpired last week, I got to thinking.  It was you who pulled me out of that announce table. It was you who made me who I am today. Quite frankly who am I to bitch about not being the IWF Hardcore Champion.  After all, like you said you are the King of Hardcore and if anyone deserves that belt, its definitely you!
JJ: Just what the hell is Max getting at! Max: Fear will you please come out here right now so I can apologize to you face to face. JJ: Wow Max is being so passive about this whole thing I'm shocked! I would be pissed off if I were him. Well I wonder if Fear is going to come out.
BOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMMMMMM! JJ: Here he is! The IWF's first ever dual champion! Perhaps the best this business has ever seen. Man he's flying high right now. He's on top of the wrestling world! Max: Fear, I just want to say that I'm sorry for being so selfish. I should've known better than to accept the hardcore title. You are the only one that has earned that right because as you say you are the king of Hardcore.
Fear: If that is all that you want to do is apologize, then I accept. Listen Max I don't have much time. Being the undisputed world champion I'm busy these days. So we'll see ya around.
Max: Not so fast Fear! You see I want to get to that next level in my career. The best way to do that is to learn from the best. So let me tell you what I have done. I have set up a couple of matches for you. Not only for the IWF Hardcore Title, but for also the World
Heavyweight Championship!
JJ: Wow right here tonight! Fear: Wait a minute you can’t do that! I wrestle when I want to. You don't make the rules around here I do! Max: So without further delay here's your first opponent. This is for the IWF Hardcore Championship, here is your challenger The Grim Rapper! JJ: Here comes the Rapper and were under way. Fear and the brother of rap going at it. Oh what a close line from the Grim Rapper. Oh what a low blow from Fear! Fear now trying to go after
Max. What’s this, the Rapper has his ghetto blaster. He's going to hit Fear with it.
Watch out. CRUUUUUUUUUUUUUUNCHHHH! JJ: Oh no Fear is down! Here we go we have a new champion! Ref: 1..2..3 JJ: Fear has just lost the IWF Hardcore title to the Grim Rapper! I can’t believe it. This is unbelievable.
Max: Oh no I'm sorry Fear. Wait a minute don't go away. We have one more match for you my brother. This one for your precious IWF Heavyweight Championship. Your challenger...NEMESIS! JJ: My god this is unreal. Could it be that Fear loses it all tonight in one shot. Here comes Nemesis. Fear going up to him with a steel chair. Oh what a shot by Fear. Nemesis is not fazed by that. Another shot nothing. Look at Fear, he is beside himself. Oh here we go
Fear going for a ride, choke slam! What’s Nemesis doing he's setting up a table on the middle of the ring. He's got Fear and they’re going on the top rope! Oh my god
Nemesis is going to choke slam Fear through a table from the top rope. Here we go! CRUUUUUUUUUUUUUUNCHHHH! JJ: That's it Fear is out like a light. Nemesis going for the cover… Ref: 1..2..3 JJ: I cant believe what we have witnessed. Fear has lost it all in one night. Nemesis is the new IWF
Heavyweight Champion! What a night! All this orchestrated by Max! This is unbelievable!
Fear is a bloody mess and perhaps some broken bones! We have new champions! See you next week!
