Archive: Fear meets The ROCK FEAR goes one on one with The Great One! JJ: Welcome wrestling fans! Man do we have news for you. Fear has been very busy these days as the IWF
Champion! He's been asked to do an episode on Walker Texas Ranger.
JJ: So look for that in the near future. From what I understand Chuck Norris and Fear are great friends. Right now were going to take you to the world premiere of  "Beyond the
Mat".  Fear was there with you guessed it, the WWF Champion The ROCK!! A very anticipated documentary about the lives of  wrestlers and such. So let’s take you there!  FEAR and The ROCK!
Fear: Well here we are wrestling fans were at the world premiere for  "Beyond the Mat".  I’m with The Great One, The Rock and……. Rock: First of all Jabroni, what is your name? Fear: I'm F… Rock: IT DOESN'T MATTER WHAT YOUR NAME IS!!! Rock: What’s this little belt you have around your waist? Fear: Well it's the IWF Heavyweight Championship belt. A prestigious title I may add and I'm damn proud of it! Rock: Well The Rock says this, this belt you have around your waist looks like the toy surprise you get out of a Cracker Jack box!  You call yourself a champion?  Do you even know what it takes to become without a shadow of a doubt the single most electrifying man in sports entertainment today?
Fear: Wait a minute; I'm meanest, the badest, the most electrifying human specimen on this planet Earth!
Rock: The only specimen your going to get from the Great One is when I take that little toy you call a belt, shine it up real nice and pretty, TURN THAT SUMBITCH SIDEWAYS AND STICK IT
STRAIGHT UP YOUR CANDY ASS!!! Fear: Hold on Rock, your not going to embarrass me in front of my millions of fans! Rock: Just shut up and listen Jabroni. The People: ROCKY! ROCKY! ROCKY! ROCKY! ROCKY! Rock: I am the Peoples Champion! I am the Peoples Choice! Listen to the millions... The People: And Millions Rock: and millions of The Rocks fans. They know that the Rock gets all the Fame and all the glory. So REAR, The Rock has one more question for you. Fear: It's FEAR, Rock! What is your question? Rock: Do you like pancakes? Fear: Yes I do. Rock: Do you get that funny feeling inside, you know that...tingly feeling? Fear: Yeah, kind of... Rock: Well The Rock says, get your pancake ass out of here! Rock: IF YOU SMELLLLLLLALALALALALAOWWWWW!   What the ROCK... The People: IS COOKIN' Rock: IS...COOKIN'!!! JJ: Wow, Fear being embarrassed at the world premiere! Just when you thought Fear would steal the show, The Rock put him in his place.
JJ: Can’t wait to hear what Fear has to say about his experience with The Rock!  Do we have a possible match in the near future? We will wait and see. See you next time wrestling fans!
