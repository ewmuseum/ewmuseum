Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This Internet Wrestling Federation survives as a GeoCities site of roughly thirty episodes, narrated throughout by two recurring in-character announcers, JJ Tough and a rotating color commentator, over a copyright line reading “1999-2002”. Its own locker-room page lists an invented roster under Owner/President Ed Gruberman, headed by a champion calling himself Nemesis (“I'm your worst Nightmare”), a Women's Champion named CalcGirl, tag champions the Afrolites, and a Hardcore Champion called the Grim Rapper.

Its own longest-running story is the rise and fall of a wrestler named Fear. Fear's manager Max Rakkus, working with the fed's own owner Gruberman, orchestrated Rage's downfall in full: Rage's car and title belt were destroyed by rival wrestler Hugh, Rage was manoeuvred into a title match he had to win or be fired, and Max's own steel-chair interference cost him both the match and his job — “Rage is fired!” became Fear's crowning as champion. Fear went on to become the site's first-ever dual champion, holding both the Heavyweight and Hardcore titles at once, before Max turned on him in revenge, feeding him to the Grim Rapper and then to Nemesis in the same night and stripping him of both belts.

A later episode reveals the twist behind it all: a surveillance tape shows Rage celebrating with Max, the Grim Rapper and Nemesis, toasting how easily the plan against Fear had come together — Rage had been working with his own supposed rivals the entire time. By the site's own following season the site also crowned a new Women's Champion, CalcGirl, who beat the returning Silicon Sally after Rage himself, dressed as the match referee, counted the winning fall.

The Afrolites held the tag titles unchallenged for months and finally issued an open challenge from the ring; the site's own surviving episode ends on that challenge still hanging, the crowd roaring as an unnamed team answers the call, before the page cuts away to a commercial.

The site's own home page preserves what appears to be its final state: Fear back on commentary beside JJ Tough, shaven-headed, drinking and smoking on camera, credited only as having “lost both the IWF heavyweight and hardcore championship” and fallen into the life the home page shows. How the Afrolites' open challenge, or Fear's own promised comeback against Rage, played out beyond that isn't in the surviving record; the site's own last dated trace is an “Updated 2/18/02” stamp on that same home page.
