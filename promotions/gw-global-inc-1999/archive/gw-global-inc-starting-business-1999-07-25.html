Global Wrestling Inc. — Starting Business

25 July 1999
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Fade into the inside shot of a conference room, which is full of reporters.At the stage, a young man, in his twenties, is sitting behind a desk, with another man on his right. They are the GWI officials. Bobby Harvey, it's president, and Kevin Toman, the VP. Right in front of them, there are two microphones placed so they can easily answer the journalists' questions.]
BH: Ladies and Gentlemen, I declare this conference to be opened. [All reporters stretch out their hands, many of them holding pens and microphones ready as Bobby Harvey points at a young man in his twenties, who is wearing a nice dress with a tie.]
BH: You have a question, sir? Reporter: Thanks you, mister Harvey. My name is Charles Bensson, from Wrestling Daily. I'd like to ask what was the reason you called us all here for tonight.
BH: The answer is very simple, mister Bensson. I am here to announce the opening of Global Wrestling Inc., the new wrestling federation which will take the ratings by a storm...that's what we hope!
[Some laughs can be heard...] Reporter: Can I ask another question? BH: Well, as you are already talking with me, shoot! Reporter: I'd like to know what kind of shows you will host. BH: Let me see...hmmm...first of all, we will have a show each Sunday, with as many matches as we have time to show. All others will be untelevised or will be shown at another day, like Monday, when we will bring the
Update show, with interviews, statements, rankings and the overall happenings at Global Wrestling Inc. This will be the first possibility to see wrestlers who are under a Global
Wrestling Inc. contract talk. The other one will be Thursday, as we will host the "Show of 1000 words". There you'll be able to listen to statements made by the wrestlers.
Reporter: Thanks! It sounds interesting. BH: No problem. We are here for that. However, I'd like to explain why this press conference happenend today and not at another day. It's because Global Wrestling Inc. will held an inaugural card on August, 15th! That's right, ladies and gentlemen. In a little more than two weeks, Global Wrestling Inc. will present it's first card live from Melbourne, Australia! All wrestlers who are interested in paticipating at this card, at which all wrestlers putting up a good show will get a contract with the company can now send in the appplications, which will be later showed on this programm.
Hey Tom, it's your turn now! [Suddenly, a reporter who is standing far behind everybody else begins to speak.] Reporter: Mr. Toman, what makes this federation any different than the millions of others out there?
KT: What makes us different is that unlike other feds neither the president or myself seek to make any of our contacts in the wrestling world champions. We both are big wrestling fans and we want to see top notch competition. With the exception of our titles, we are probably the least political people you will ever meet. Simply put, the people who work the hardest and do the best will win.
Reporter: How would you characterize the wrestling your league will present? KT: The very best action in the wrestling world. Everyone in the front offices and all the talent in our promotion will give 150% to make sure every card is something spectacular. Forget feds which only have one or two decent matches a card. Forget feds that only give decent matches in PPVs. In GWI, every match and every card will feature nothing but the best action.
BH: Ladies and Gentlemen, we have time for just one more question. [All reporters raise their hands and try to be picked.] BH: [pointing at a lady in a white dress] Yes? Reporter: Thanks. I'd like to know from you what kind of wrestlers you are looking for? BH: Honestly, we will probably accept everybody. We are new and we want to start with a strong roster.
Singles as well as tag teams and trio teams are welcomed, no matter if they are brawlers, cruiserweights or heavyweights.
Reporter: Thanks. BH: Well, the time is up. See you on July, the 31st, as we will bring you an Update about GWI!

Application ======================================================================== ========================= 01. Introduction a) Global Wrestling Inc. is based in Melbourne, Australia. However, it has offices in London, Tokyo, New York and Mexico City, too. Global Wrestling Inc. tours all five countries (UK, USA, Australia, Japan, Mexico) regulary, holding Pay-Per-Views at the end of each tour, which is 5 to 8 weeks long. The owner, a man known as Bobby
Harvey, started the federation, as he thought that the wrestlers of our generation don't have enough possibilities to show their talent worldwide. Now, with Global Wrestling Inc. started, he is inviting all wrestlers - rookies and veterans, to participate, so they see how it is to work worldwide.
b) My name is [name withheld] and I once ran a federation called the AeWF (Anarchy e-Wrestling Federation), which was succefull for about a year, but closed down after a becoming one with the NaWF, which closed down some weeks after. I always enjoyed booking and writing e-wrestling matches, and I start this federation because I believe there is a place for an International federation after the closure of such feds as PJW, SJPW, CLLL, SCRA and others.
This idea was made by myself and my good buddy [name withheld], but after thinking it over again, we came to the decision that we won't open the fed together. However, I'd still like to re-open a federation, and that's why I'm writing to you.
The only thing I'd like to hear from you is if you are interested in helping making this new fed an established one (by joining and/or helping "backstage") and if you don't want to, what is the reason for your decision (no time? not a good fed? you would rather join an established fed? you hate me? you think the fed will close down?)
02. Stuff a) The president of Global Wrestling Inc. is Bobby Harvey, a man from England which followed wrestling since his childhood and started his own federation after winning a large amount of money. He contacted two of his best friends, Bill Daniels and Mike King, and asked them to become his regular commentators. They agreed and will commentate GW Inc. shows for you, along with five other commentators, who will be there during shows in their respective countires.
- The head-security is Todd "Killing Machine" Borton, an underground wrestler who hang up his boots some months ago, after a brutal feud, which was fought mainely in smaller arenas, with current MCW superstar Rune. Now, he returns to the arena, even if it's not to fight, but to keep the wrestlers in the ring and troublemakers outside of it.
- There are three different referees working at GW Inc. One of them, the head-referee, is Charles Duncan, probably one of the best referees in this business. He is very attentive on what happens in and outside of the ring and always tries to count with the same speed, to avoid false decisions.
- The second referee is Michael Brown, a man who normally shouldn't be a referee, as he doesn't know the rules very well, and he isn't always as impartial as a referee should be. He likes the heels, and sometimes even help them win their matches. However, it isn't possible for president Bobby Harvey to fire him, as Michael's father, James Brown, was one of the best referees in the World, thirty years ago, and he asked Michael to become a referee, too. As Harvey's father and James were good friends, Harvey promised to give him a job in his federation.
- The third referee is Brent Douglas, a young referee who is still new in his job, but tries to do his best. We'll see how it will work. b) Well, here's a quick overview of the stuff. Divisions There are five divisions in GW Inc. One of them is fought only in Mexico, and all four others are fought everywhere else and in Mexico, too. Here they are:
- Heavyweight division: +230 lbs. - King of the Air division: -230 lbs. - Tag Team division: no weight limit - Trios division: only in Mexico - Fight-a-Fight division: all wrestlers who think that they can survive a real brawl are invited to fight in this division. But be warned! There are many injuries after such matches and they are very risky!
a) Titles There will be 4 titles as of now. Here's a quick overview: Global Heavyweight Title - the main title at GWI. The man who holds this belt is the wrestler to beat! It has to be defended at least every four weeks.
GWI International Title - this title actually changes his name during every tour. For example, we call it the GWI Australian Title when touring Australia, etc.
Global Tag Team Titles - the top prize for every tag team under a GWI contract! All in all, it has the same sinification as the Global
Heavyweight title in singles wrestling. King of the Air Title - that's the title for the lightweights, or cruiserweights, or super-middleweights here at GWI. Call them like you want, they all fly!
