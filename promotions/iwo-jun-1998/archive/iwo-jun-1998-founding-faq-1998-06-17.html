NEW FED, IWO with FAQ

17 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, My name is [name withheld]. I have been in many feds, and I have always wanted to start my own. It has been something I have wanted to do for about a year. I am here to tell you about IWO, the International Wrestling Organization. It will be a roleplaying based fed. There will be cards held all over the world. This document is an FAQ about the fed. If you want to join or have questions, e-mail me at [detail withheld]. Thank you for your time and patience. President: [name withheld] Vice President: OPEN Web master: OPEN Sections: Section 1 (Application) Section 2 (Cards) Section 3 (Rules) Section 4 (Titles) SECTION 1 Here is where you tell me about your wrestler. Fill out the info and send it back to me. Name: Height: Weight: Manager: If so male or female: Does he/she interfere? 10-15 moves that your wrestler does: Setup move: Finisher: Finisher Description: Tag Team: Past feds: E-mail address: SECTION 2 CARDS There will be two weekly cards and one pay per view a month. The cars will be as follows...THE CARDS WILL BE OUT ON TIME UNLESS NOTIFIED Weekly cards Saturday Storm- bigger card, like Nitro Wednesday Warzone- smaller card, like Thunder SECTION 3- RULES I am not a big fan of rules, but I have some that HAVE to be address. Here they are. Rule 1 Swearing is permitted. Do not report someone for swearing or calling you names. This is a fantasy fed. No need to get hostile. Rule 2 Roleplays must be 10 lines long. The more you roleplay, the better chance you have of winning. If you do three 10 line rp's and the other guy does one 80 liner, he will win. Rule 3 If you want to do a interview at a card, or a run in, tell me please. Rule 4 Send roleplays to everyone in fed not just me, I will send out a list with the guys in the fed Rule 5 Have FUN!!! That is what it's all about. SECTION 4 TITLES Ranked in importantce 1. IWO International Title 2. IWO International Tag Team Titles 3. IWO European Title 4. IWO United States Title If you have any suggestions, e-mail me. Well, I hope you decide to join IWO. I desperately need a webmaster. I already have a web page setup that I will erase. Welll, thank you very much. [name withheld] IF YOU HAVE ANY QUESTIONS, E-MAIL ME AT [detail withheld]
