Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This CWA's own reopening handbook, posted in February 2000, describes a promotion already more than a year old that had closed and was now reopening its doors to new handlers, run day to day by a board and a pair of co-presidents.

The handbook laid out five championships — a World Title, a second-tier Supreme Title, and three regional United States belts defended weekly — alongside a stable policy capping membership at seven wrestlers, a twelve-rule code of conduct governing roleplay conduct and complaints, and a full singles and tag-team application form.

No further document survives beyond this one reopening notice, and nothing in the held record says whether the relaunch took hold.
