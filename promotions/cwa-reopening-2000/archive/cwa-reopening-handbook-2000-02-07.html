CWA Welcome. This is the cWa Handbook. The cWa has been open for over a year and then was close now to be reopened. These rules and regulations are in purpose of recruitment. If you wouldnt like to read on about the cWa please delete right now. But if you are looking for a good e-fed where you help with your own angles and can help with others this is the place. I have been a part of the cWa since day one and i am always looking to recruit new members. As of Now we are looking to make this fed go on further then it has been. If you wanna be a part of the cWa contact me or The Co-Prez's and we will talk to you and try our best to see you in the cWa. We do not turn anyone down and we look forward to new people joing the cWa. Thank you
š·­īŊ`·.Ũ. The cWa Rules.Ũ.·īŊ`­·š
Because our fed has become well known, it's time that we had to set down and write out rules for the cWa. Our main rule and like every where else is that there will be no tosing or threatining to tose another member of the cWa. If there is there will be action takin. I know we never had them before, but it's time that we get them out to everyone so that there hopefully will be no conflicts, so here they are....
1. You can only have 1 singles wrestlers, or one tag team 2. There is no limit to the length of a role-play, but the more you write, the more you tend to repeat yourself. Also Each Rp must be Forty-five Minutes apart. This is to prevent spamming and spammers from getting a cheap win.
3. No one is any better than the other. If you have a problem with a fellow role-player, challenge him to a match, IN CHARACTER, don't belittle the person. 4. For you to be considered a top contender for a title, you must do the following: a. RP for all matches given to you b. Try your hardest to win the match c. Be a part of the cWa, not just a roleplayer. Help out when you can. d. Don't be a whiner if you loose matches. Go on and try harder next time. 5. There will NOT be ANY junkmail whatsoever. If I see mail that is not an RP and is sent to the fed, and even BCC'd, which if it is, then most likely everyone got it, you will be immediately suspended. All titles or title shots will be taken away from you. If I see it again, you will be summarily let go.
NO WARNINGS!!!!!!! 6. If I see any role-plays that are in anyway out of character, for example, you talk as yourself at the bottom of your RP, you will be warned not to do it again. Rps are Roleplays. That is what is meant by it, and that's what it will be. You have something to say to someone, send them an email to them only or IM them. If I see it a second time, you will be suspended, a third time, you will be summarily let go.
7. If you feel the need to complain that you lost a match, and want to know why, you will email the board ONLY. They in turn will email the board and the member that complained. If I see the complaint as just a whining type deal, then you will be warned. You should be complaining to the effect of what can I do to make my role-plays better, not well this happened, that happened. You suck. I will not tolerate that. If I see it, you will be warned. I see it again, suspended, a third time, summarily let go.
8. If I find out people are being TOS'd, and I find out who did it. You will be FIRED immediately. No excuses. Nothing. I will not deal with babies that have a need to TOS people to prove themselves.
9. If you have something come up and cannot RP for a match for an upcoming card, you have 24 hours to let the board know. Otherwise you will be given a loss for not complying with this rule. Only exception to this rule. A Death in the family. And I hope no one is low enough to lie and say someone died when actually they didn't.
10. The cWa does not permitt Advertisments of other feds in the cWa. If we reciever word from five people in the cWa that they have recieved mail about a person advertising that person will be let go from the cWa. a person has a choice to join as many feds as they want or to run a fed, But we will not tolerate advertising of feds within the cWa.
11. Finally, just have fun. If you follow these simple rules, you will never hear from me, and there will not be any problems. But not obeying them WILL get my attention, and fast. I am also here to have fun, and that's what I want to do. These rules WILL be followed. No exceptions. If there is anything else that maybe you aren't clear on, please feel free to IM me or email me. I am here to help out the best I can for both the Role-player and the Fed itself.
12. You may not use another members character in your roleplays. If a person is on the roster then you can not use them without prior permission. If you do not have permissione you can lose a match for breaking this rule.
