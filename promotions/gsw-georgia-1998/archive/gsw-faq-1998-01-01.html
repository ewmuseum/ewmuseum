It's headquater's are in Atlanta, Georgia. Most of the cards will be held in Atlanta GA, but they can stretch across the state of Georgia and on up to Kentucky and Tennessee.
Most cards will be held at State Fairgrounds or other college gymnasiums, etc. GSW will be a booked and not simmed. 2. Who runs the GSW? Ficticious Operators - [name withheld]- The President and founder of GSW. He created GSW to make a living in the wrestling business. He has run many feds over his career and has ventured down to Georgia to take a stab at the business in the south.
Kyle Perry - This is the man that gets EVERYTHING glued in place. He makes sure that ticket sales are at their peak and handles all the advertising. He is also in charge of the schedule, where and when GSW will be appearing.
Jarred McDowell - Handles much of the firing business in GSW. He is the most hated in the office due to that he has to do all the dirty work. Most people fear this guy when they see him coming their way.
The R e a l People - WE NEED WRITERS! You only have to write _ONE_ match every week or so. And One or Two Matches every supercard/PPV. If interested please mail Stykes.
President: [name withheld] - [detail withheld] Vice President: 3. What titles are there in GSW? GSW Southern Championship. <-- The Main Title Main Title GSW Tri-State Championship. <-- 2nd Title GSW State Champions. <-- Tag Team Titles 4. There is no National or World title because we are a REGIONAL federation. That means we will not make European tours, we will not venture out of the three States (GA,TN, KY) area very frequently.
v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ II. Rules and Regulations 1. How are the Matches determined? Matches are decided by me, [name withheld] and Co Pres. I'm deciding roughly on this scale.
-45% Roleplaying -35% Heat/Angles (You do not have to be Heel to get heat) -15% Strats -5% Effort (If you put forth effort, you'll eventually get a push.) 2. What are the roleplay rules? You can do one to two roleplays a week. -or- You can do an in-ring + one RP. -or- You can do one studio interview and one roleplay. 3. How do I get matches? There are a couple of ways. - I sign you to a match - You challenge someone in a roleplay - You send in the 'formal' request 4. Are sneak attacks allowed? Yes, in moderation. I have the right to refuse a sneak attack, but normally won't do a sneak attack without your approval.
v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ III. Events and Shows. 1. What shows are there? Saturday Night (Main Show) - GSW Air Raid. - This will have 4-6 live matches on it. - Also have 2-3 Darks - This is where you in-rings take place Syndicated Studio Show - This show, comes out Tuesday-Friday depending on what TV Station you get it on :) - Has 1 live match - 2 Dark Matches - Review of Last Show - Preview of Next show - Supercard/PPV Watch 2. Are there PPV's? Yes, but not that many. We do have many more SUPERCARDS then PPV, when there is a PPV, it's in a larger stadium.
The PPV will, of course, be larger than regular shows and a bit more classy than Supercards.
3. Where are the shows held? -The Studio show is normally held in the head GSW Studio 9 (Atlanta GA), sometimes it's at the GSW Training Center, where you can get the inside scoop on the wrestlers up and comming. - The Saturday shows are held in areas around the Southern Three states for GSW (GA, KY, TN). They are held at bars, malls, parks, and other gymnasiums. This show is usually live, but has been known to be taped every now and then. v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^
IV. Titles 1. How will the titles be decided? They can be decided in a number of ways. There will be tournaments to decided the first holders of the Titles.
2. How often will the titles be defended? Southern Belt - No less than once a month no more than three times a month Tri-State Belt - No less than two times a month no more than three times a month. State Belts - No less than once a month no more than three times a month. 3. How can I get a title shot? - If the champion challenges you and I see fit. - If you're one of the top three active contenders for the belt. - If I see fit you deserve one. v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^
V. Match Info. 1. How often can my guy wrestle? There is no limit. Sometimes he'll wrestle twice on the same card. Sometimes twice a week. 2. Where are the matches held? In a regular match, they're held in an 18'x18' square ring. 3. Can I add stipulations to my matches? Yes, within reason. Remember we don't have very much money, so take it easy, or it's coming out of your paycheck.
4. What are the regular rules to an average match? Disqualification. (Some referees are stricter than others.) 30 minute time limit. 1 Pinfall v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^ v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^v^
