Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Global Wrestling League opened with a recruitment ad, “The All-New GWL wants You!”, 8 April 1997, signed by President Seymour Krebbs. It pitched itself against the crowded field of fantasy feds on four points: belts that wouldn't change hands every night, a small but growing roster, no copied gimmicks (“No Rex Jugars! All original!”), and roleplay weighted alongside its simulator.

Its inaugural card, Wednesday Night WAR, ran three days later: twenty matches building toward an elimination-style triangle match for its own World Title, called by a three-man announce team — Doug Harneson, Handsome Mike Walker and Greg Pettingil — whose running banter carried as much of the broadcast as the wrestling. The served script breaks off after the tournament's first round; who won the triangle match for the title isn't in the surviving record.

By 1 May its co-president, wrestling under the ring alter-ego Johnny Dice, was also its reigning Imperial Champion, and its roster had grown to include Roadkill, Killer Ken Wilson, Archangel and Commander Troyer.

Its first pay-per-view, Mother's Day Massacre, 12 May 1997, opened with President Seymour Krebbs apologizing on-air for a missed prior broadcast — “Someone stole the satellite truck” — then put every title up for grabs. Johnny Dice retained the Imperial Championship over Killer Ken Wilson; Roadkill's Outsiders took the World Tag Team Championship from the Angels of Death by countout; and a jungle-themed steel cage match between Commander Troyer and the Merc ended with the Merc victorious, only for an unidentified woman to run in afterward and pull him off of Troyer.

No document later than that card survives. Whether the World Title tournament that opened the promotion — left on record after only its first round — was ever finished isn't in the surviving record.
