ARES DH: The second of the feared pantheon<snickers>, sorry.....came out to face the Ultimate sans Beholder. And what a wrestling lesson Ares got. HMW: Don't count Blackjack out though. Personally, I just want him to win simply because he has a better name for his top-rope DDT. GP: It kinda makes sense...Hermes...the Herminator....I get it.... (the other two just look at him.....) What? DH: Next up is 8 ball versus Order..... HMW: I'm gonna get something. Anyone want a beer? (Silence for about a minute...) guess not......(leaves.) 8BALL VS. ORDER DH: 8Ball startsout in control of the match but Order comes back with a series of draining submission holds.....then the action goes outside ad the two men use everything they can against each other. The posts..the stairs, the ring......what an intense brawl.... GP: That just shows if you're gonna run with the big dogs, you gotta learn to piss in the tall grass..... DH: Where do you come up with these things? GP:8ball goes for the Corner-Pocket...but Order shakes off the hold! He's getting a second wind! DH: 8Ball with a belly-toback-suples......1-2-3! DH: Yes, you can have it. HMW: Okay. I'll collect my $1.75 later. GP: For those of you interested in the match, Law is incontrol for most of the situation. GP: This looks bad. Two Aliens AND Banzai Bradley? I think John Smith is in serious trouble. DH: The action tumbles to the outside. WINNER: SMITH---10:22 GP: Banzai is arguing with the ref. Smith leaves the area and the two aliens lock eyes....the one in the ring shrugs and takes off thru the crowd! Da Alien is pissed! GP: You'd walk up to Mrs. Lincoln and as "How was the show? Did I miss anything?" HMW: What the hell does that mean? You know Pettingil, you sure are stupid sometimes. DH: Instead he charges the ring and brings the rage to Captain Thomas before the bell.... ROADKILL VS. CAPT. THOMAS HMW: DiCicco is going backstage to talk with Hadeus...
