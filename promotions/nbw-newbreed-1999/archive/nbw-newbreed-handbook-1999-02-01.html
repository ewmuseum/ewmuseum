New Breed Wrestling Federation — Handbook and Application

1 February 1999

New Breed Wrestling Federation Handbook and Application Table of Contents PART I - Introduction PART II - Cards PART III - Roleplaying, Daily Flash Shows, and Deadlines PART IV - Titles PART V - Pay Per View and Supercards PART VI - Closing PART VII - Application ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [ Part I - Introduction ] Ladies and Gentlemen welcome to The New Breed of Profecional Wrestling. This is the place where you will find unbealivable inovations, yet still the same quality that has brought e - wrestling to the pinacle at which it now sits. I promice you that New Breed Wrestling will bring to you more than any other federation currently on the internet. Using a booked, closed flashing way of running things will bring the New Breed
Wrestling straight to the top of internet wrestling.
[ Part II - Cards ] - Sunday Night, Live and Hardcore This is the big show of the week for the New Breed Wrestling. There will eight - ten big matches, written out in play by play form, plus dark matches. This is the spot where the World Telivision Championship will be defended every week. Angle devolpments, in rings, and so much more will take place on this huge show, it is not one to miss.
- Thursday Night Power Hour The second card of the week for the New Breed Wrestling. This card will have eight - ten matches written out in summary form, and dark matches.
As well as a main event written out in play by play format. Just becasue its summary format doesn't mean big things won't happen such as sudden title changes, angle devolpments, and much more.
- NBW Newsletter This is the place where you will find the scheduales for upcoming cards, there will be news, rumors, and any other up and coming events. This is the place where you find a roster, win - loss record for your wrestler, and much much more. This will come out after each card.
[ Part III - Roleplaying ] The New Breed Wrestling uses a closed flash system. Which means that all roleplays are sent to me, and I send them out in a packet called "Listen
Up" This show will be send out every day, or every other day depending on the number of Roleplays to be sent. Scedualed Live Flash shows will also be put on the NBW's calender of events that can be seen in our newsletter.
Roleplaying is the most important part of this federation, if you are not willing to roleplay, or don't have time to roleplay then please save us both the trouble, and don't join. Becasue here in the New Breed
Wrestling, I am willing to let you take your wrestler as far as you want, and I will put just as much work in as you do, but you have to show the initiative, or your wrestler will be pulling a J-O-B.
Roleplays can be submitted for any given match up until the day before the event, strats and inrings are due two days before any card. This gives us a better cahcne of being able to incorporate everything you want into your match.
[ Part IV - Pay Per Views and Supercards ] "When The Earth Shakes, Its Only The Begining" The Opening event for the New Breed Wrestling. It will feature a tournament in which all of the winners of the first round will face off in a Battle
Royal to determine the New Breeds first World Heavyweight Champion. Then three days later on Sunday Night, Live and Hardcore we will determine all of the rest of the champions in the New Breed, those matches will be determined by battle royal performaces.
"Clash of the Titans" Will take place once a month in the New Breed Wrestling Federation. This supercard is like a pay per view, and will have every title on the line, plus a special type match such as a battle royal, or king of cable tournament.
[ Part V - Closing ] Well, I hope that you feel that the New Breed is for you, and if you have any questions about anything you have read here, please feel free to
e-mail me at [detail withheld] and I will get back to you as soon as possible. The application is below, and once again thanks for taking the time to look and see if the New Breed is for you.
