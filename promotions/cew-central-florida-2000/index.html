A Central Florida hardcore federation whose February 2000 handbook and opening-card vignettes introduced a five-title slate and a roster built for a weekly televised show. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A federation calling itself Cutting Edge Wrestling surfaced on Usenet in mid-February 2000, its own handbook dating the promotion's start to 12 February and locating most of its events around Central Florida. It billed itself as a booked, hardcore-leaning fantasy promotion mimicking the presentation of the era's mainstream cards, warning that a wrestler very rarely left one of its events without a trip to the hospital.

The handbook laid out a weekly show, “To the Edge,” claimed for a Monday-night slot on ESPN, a Friday roleplay show called “Talkin' Jive,” house-show reports, and a bimonthly Supercard. Three titles were named — a Florida Championship, a Tag Team Championship, and a Central Florida Championship — and the promotion asked its own wrestlers for entirely original characters, warning against real-wrestler stand-ins.

A 25 February “Talkin' Jive” broadcast introduced the promotion's own wrestlers ahead of its first card: a street-born loner calling himself Da Man, a veteran named Don Watson, a hunter turned wrestler once known as Venom, and a newly signed wrestler recounting a childhood spent inside a cathedral's walls. A tag team, Psychotic Youth, was introduced alongside its own manager in a separate home-video-styled vignette, and two further wrestlers were named as the first card's remaining opponents.

Through March the promotion moved its own address from one free host to another and revised its handbook twice, folding its separate singles and tag application forms into one detailed wrestler sheet and adding a Tuesday interview show, “Inside Edition.” A recruiting post that same month described the promotion as fast-growing and still signing talent; no document after 28 March 2000 survives in this record.

The acronym was shared widely across the hobby. A federation of the identical bare initials, hosted from an unrelated address, is held elsewhere in this museum from three years earlier, with no roster, staff, or address in common; two further, thinner claimants of the same three letters surfaced on the newsgroup that same spring.
