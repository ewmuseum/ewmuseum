First things first, CEW stands for Cutting Edge Wrestling. CEW's basis is to work an indies format. Small shows, small roster, no big effects, and little air time. To be honest, CEW is purely for fun. So bitching and complaining will be not be tolerated. CEW is based out of Florida, mainly because that's where I live and know the area very well. CEW will mainly tour the state of Florida, but on the occasion it'll leave the state touring Georgia and Louisiana. CEW has a hardcore element to it, with gimmick matches, etc; but it also has a traditional element to it as well. CEW is open to any kind of wrestler. Although, I would like a diverse group of wrestlers from Canada, United States, and International. The roster size will be about 20 along with about 6 tag teams. But, most important, CEW is to have FUN. 2. Who runs this thing? (Fantasy) CEW is ran by none other than Richard Leaker. Leaker is the President as well as the CEO. He opened CEW back in 2000, it ran very well before the company went bankrupt. Currently, Richard has garnered enough money to restart the promotion. He's a creative genius when it comes to story lines and angle ideas. Richard is a man in his early 30's. Before getting into the business he was in the stock market, where he made his fortunes. Leaker is very charismatic, with brown hair and hazel eyes. He stands about 5'10" and is stock for his size. Clad usually in an suit and tie, Richard is all business. 3. Who runs this thing? (IRL) 3.1. [name withheld] My names [name withheld]. I've been in e-wrestling for god knows how long, I've ran two successful promotions UWC and the old CEW. I handled "Your True Life Fantasy" [name withheld] and Takisuma Ogama in various federations. I'll be doing all the booking, most of the match writing, and all that other good stuff that comes along with the job. 3.1.1. How can I reach you? I have a couple of contacts in which you can reach me at. E-MAIL: [detail withheld] AIM: [detail withheld] IRC: [detail withheld] (Starchat.net) #cew 4. What are the titles here? Currently there are only two singles belts and one tag team belt in the CEW, they are... 4.1. CEW Florida Heavyweight Championship This is THE BELT. You hold it and your the king of CEW. It is defended at least once a month. Failure to do so means a instantly job on your next match. The champ however can choose to defend it more than once in the same month. 4.2. CEW-UWC Anarky Championship The Anarky championship was brought it by "War Machine" Damien Churchill from the defunct UWC. It doesn't have quite the significance as the Florida Championship, but does make you the number one contender. This belt has special stipulations that is...THE CHAMP GETS TO PICK THEM. He decides the match in which he defends the belt and it can sometimes get very nasty. Also defended at least once a month. 4.3. CEW Florida Tag Team Championships Self-explanatory... Must be defended monthly, like the singles belt. And champions can choose to defend it more than once, if they like to. 5. What are the cards in CEW? 5.1. CEW To The Edge This is the main card! Hosted by Louis Eckle and Dan Lovitz, this show is full written, all the big matches will be broadcasted here. This show is bi-weekly. The main idea for the card line-up is: 2: Dark matches (Summarized) 3: Matches involving superstars 1: Main Event 5.2. CEW Prelude To The Edge This is the "flash show", where there's space for everybody in the roster to speak. Hosted by Tim Mackoney, it can shows live interviews (by Tim himself), post-TTE action, backstage flashes, promos and such. 5.3. CEW Inside Edition This show reports the last happenings in CEW, such as TTE results, info about next cards, signings, departures, the rankings... And also House Shows results. This is the only weekly show at CEW. Flashes can also be broadcasted here. 5.4. Supercards As of current time, there aren't many Supercards planned. Once we get underway, supercards will take place on a bi- monthly basis. Trust me, we'll keep you updated. 5.5. Normal Schedule Okay, here's some info... As a bi-weekly fed, the schedule here permits more time for me to writing and for you to role play. A normal schedule would be like... 1st week's Mon. - CEW To the Edge 1st week's Thu. - CEW Inside Edition 2nd week's Mon. - CEW Prelude To the Edge 2nd week's Thu. - CEW Inside Edition 3rd week's Mon. - CEW To the Edge ... And that goes on. 6. Who are the staff members? 6.1. Front Office 6.1.1. Dave Completon The voices behind CEW...the announcers. 6.2. Announcers 6.2.1. Louis Eckle Louis is your traditional announcer, he calls it straight down the middle, taking no sides. He's full of knowledge of moves and holds from his many years in the industry. Louis is the play by play announcer of To the Edge. 6.2.2. Dan Lovitz Ex-Wrestler turned announcer, Dan Lovitz is arrogant and full of himself. He cheers the guys who resemble him (heels) and loves for some good old fashioned cheating. His son is also a staff member with CEW. Dan is the color commentator of To the Edge. 6.2.3. Tim Mackoney Tim Mackoney is CEW's Informer. He knows all the facts and has all the scoops on news and rumors. Tim Hosts CEW's Prelude to TTE, giving all the CEW fans the latest happenings. 6.3. Interviewers The guys you gotta (if you want) use in your role plays. 6.3.1. Tim Mackoney Aside from hosting Prelude To The Edge, Tim is also always backstage, getting all the dirt behind the scenes. He's an intelligent guy, but sometimes gets bullied around when interviewing the wrong wrestlers. 6.3.2. Vanessa Smith She's dumb as dirt, bot boy does she have a hot body usually clad it tight mini skirts and tops, the only reason she was hired was for some T&A for the CEW fans. Her blonde hair reflects her personality... "um...like...ya know..." 6.4. Referees They try and control the action...sorta... 6.4.1. Frank Petrowski Martin is a TRUE ref. He knows the job, he calls match right in the middle and allows no cheating. He isn't afraid of calling a DQ even because of a spit. You touch him, you lose! 6.4.2. William Lovitz Yep, it's Dan Lovitz's baby boy. Doesn't know a lick about refereeing, the only reason he was hired was because his dad has a little pull around CEW. Expect him to let almost anything go. 6.5. Ringside Crew 6.5.1. Juan "The Mouth" Havier "The Mouth" is right, Juan Havier has a voice like no other and he uses it to. Juan's the ring announcer for all of the CEW live shows. 6.5.2. James Piper A monster to say the least. A fan crosses the line and James will be all over him like stink on shit. He's CEW's head of security, and does a damn good job at it. 7. What do I have to do in CEW? 7.1. Roleplays/Flashes One of the most important thing in CEW is role playing. Plain and simple, if you don't role play, you don't win. There are some rules for role playing which are: - SWEARING: If Stone Cold can say it, so can you. But aside from damn, ass, etc; any other curse word should be covered with a [CENSORED] or something of that sort. - NO ATTACKS: No physical attacks on announcers/reporters/staff are allowed without the permission of the powers-that-be... a.k.a. me. If you do not ask for permission your flash won't run - KAYFABE: No mention of real wrestlers will be allowed... But other e-wrestlers and feds may be mentioned in your RPs IF they allow you too. Also, please keep the mention of "outside-CEW" things to a minimum. It's fine to know that you gained the XYZ World belt, but we shouldn't remember it at every RP! I wouldn't want any of you saying that "DSW sucks" or "Wooden's is a coward" in CEW cards. Go say that in THEIR cards. - FORMAT: CEW DOES HAVE A SPECIFIC FORMAT. It's similar to that of UKCW's. It goes like this. Your wrestlers name uses his initials. Example: Takisuma Ogama would be TO. TO would be followed by a : Then another space and writing. Action goes in [ these ]. Simple enough. Here's another example: [The camera pans around and finds Takisuma Ogama.] TO: I'll kill all comers. 7.2. Strategies Strats, if you don't know are your match strategies. They should consist of your game plan, several match spots (moves), the way you'd like the match to end, and what your wrestler would do if he wins/loses/dq's etc; 8. Do you need help? Can I help? ALWAYS! Right now, if I got some match writers, would be the perfect thing. But any kind of suggestion/help will be welcomed... CEW currently has a web site at http://www.efeduniverse.com/cew/ . Any contributions will be fine. 9. Okay, I gotta few questions... 9.1. What kind of wrestlers can enter CEW? Any and all sorts. Wrestlers should be kept realistic, but the more creative the better. 9.2. Can I enter a single and tag team? Or two singles and them combine them as a tag team? NOPE: CEW is restricted to one "personality" either one singles wrestler or one tag team. 9.3. Why does this fed has only a bi-weekly card? That sucks! I'd rather have bi-weekly cards that come out on time and have alot more work put into them, then have two cards a week never out on time and sloppily done. 9.4. Can I enter my BIG NAME, 10 time World champ in CEW? I prefer not. Remember that this is an indy fed... And a BIG name would probably make booking decisions more difficult. 10. Alright, I wanna join what do I do? 10.1 All you gotta do is fill out the application. It's on the front page of the website. After that, I'll send you a message stating whether your in or not, if so you'll start receiving CEW e-mails.
