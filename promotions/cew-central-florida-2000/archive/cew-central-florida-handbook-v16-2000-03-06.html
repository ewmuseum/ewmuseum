_____ ________ __ / ____| ____\ \ / / | | | |__ \ \ /\ / / | | | __| \ \/ \/ / | |____| |____ \ /\ / \_____|______| \/ \/ CEW Handbook v. 1.6 http://www.iwzcity.com/cew/ | [detail withheld] -->> INTRODUCTION Welcome to the CEW! CEW stands for Cutting Edge Wrestling. Cutting Edge Wrestling has been founded in Central Florida and most of the events will take place around that area. CEW is a booked, fantasy wrestling federation, that tries it's hardest to mimic real life wrestling promotions, only with a hardcore twist. Matches are wrestled in various hardcore situations and very rarely on a CEW event night will someone not take a trip to the hospital.
-->> CHARACTERS Your character is one of the most important things in CEW. Since CEW isn't a real promotion your wrestlers can't be real either. All characters must be completely original, no Stone Cold John Does, no Rocky Austins, the more original the better. However I must warn you, don't make your character one dimensional also, no wrestler will have extra ordinary abilities. Sure they can't act dark and demonic, but if you write a flash with them turning into a bat and flying away, you'll be canned real quick-like.
Your allowed one singles wrestler or one tag team. If you choose to have a tag team they can and will wrestle in singles competition from time to time.
-->> EVENTS CEW currently only has one show per week as well as various house show reports. Our weekly show, "To the Edge" airs Monday on ESPN 11 PM to 12 PM eastern time. TTE, usually has 3-4 matches on it as well as 3-4 dark match reports. Our House Show reports are just plain results from all over the place. CEW also features a roleplay show which is aired every Friday, entitled "Talkin' Jive". Once every two monthes we'll travel to various spots and put on a Supercard. Below is a look at our schedule.
Sunday - Nothing. Monday - "To the Edge" Tuesday - "Inside Edition" Wednesday - Nothing. Thursday - Roleplays for "Talkin' Jive" due Friday - "Talkin' Jive." Saturday - House Show report & "TTE" Deadline. -->> STAFF MEMBERS Here's a complete listing of staff members along with short descriptions. -->> CHAMPIONSHIPS Here's a listing of championships and short descriptions, the number signifies the importance.
CEW Florida Championship [1] The typical championship, you hold the belt you be the man! CEW Florida Championship is defended atleast twice a month but no more than four. CEW Tag Team Championship [2] Some may say Tag Team wrestling has died off, well not in CEW it hasn't. This is your typical tag team championship, however most title defenses have crazed stipulations.
CEW Central Florida Championship [3] The Central Florida championship symbols two things, one your the number one contender, two your one of the up and coming superstars in the promotion.
-->> ROLEPLAYING In CEW we use roleplaying for two things, one to draw heat from opponents and two to further angles. All I expect from you as handlers is one roleplay a week and a strat whenever you have a match. We try not to weigh you down with too many rules but here's a short listing.
1. Don't send in worthless roleplaying, if it doesn't further an angle or generate heat from an opponent then it doesn't need to run. 2. Do not use another handlers wrestler in your roleplay without his permission. If one is sent in I'll ask that handler if you had permission if you didn't the roleplay won't run and you'll recieve a punishment that I see fitting.
3. Try not to make roleplays too long, long drawn out roleplays tend to get boring and not many read them. 4. That's pretty much it, keep swear words to a minimum and any words other than ass and damn should be censored with a [beep] or [censorship] type deal. All roleplays should be sent to [detail withheld] . -->> STRATEGIES While most people tend to send strategies whenever they have a match, you may also send them in with ideas for an upcoming card and what not. Strategies should include how your wrestler will act, a offensive strategy including several moves, defensive strategy including several reversal moves, how the match will end, exactly how you want it, and angle ideas.
All strategies should be sent to [detail withheld] . -->> ANGLES & STORYLINES This is what CEW runs off of, angles and storylines are the single most important thing in CEW. Without them your character is nothing. You should constantly be thinking up new ideas and talking with other handlers working out angles.
