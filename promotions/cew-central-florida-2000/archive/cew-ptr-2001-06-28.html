NIGEL JACKSON ...................................................... [ D A R K N E S S ] [The sounds of a crowd cheering are audible as the scene fades from complete and total darkness to a smoke filled room where a large crowd has gathered in a circle. In the middle of the circle two men are engaged in a bloody and brutal fight.] [The smaller man is clean cut with a shaved head and no signs of facial hair. His black muscle shirt is spattered in blood belonging to both his opponent and himself. His black cargo pants sag as he bounces up and down on his bare feet, obviously trying to use his speed and quickness to his advantage.] [The larger man stands steady and still. His only movement comes when is a stray punch every now and then when the smaller man gets too close. The man's a good 6'5, 275 pounds, and soaked in sweat from his short curly blonde hair, to his dark tanned upperbody, all the way to his light blue trousers which have been semi-soaked as well. The only part of his attire which doesn't show the signs of a rigorous battle are his nicely polished black cowboy boots.] [Suddenly the smaller man lunges into the air, throwing himself into the waiting arms of his opponent. The big man catches him, bringing about a collective gasp from the crowd. One man watching the battle waves his money in the air as he shouts out, "Break him in half, AVH, Break him in half!!". "AVH" smiles at the pleading fan as he holds his opponent in the air.] [The crowd watches on, most screaming at the smaller man to make a move. Their cries go unheard. AVH drives the man down, bending him in half with a backbreaker. The crowd goes silent. AVH releases the smaller man as a woman screams, "Get up Nigel!".] [Nigel doesn't respond. AVH leans forward and sends Nigel into a semi-conscious state with a mighty blow. Blood trickles from Nigel's mouth and nose as he stares blankly at the floor.] [Most of the crowd shakes their head in disgust and head for the nearest exit. The man that pled with AVH to break Nigel in half waves his money in the air screaming, "I told you, I told you all! The mighty Ark Von Huron is the Underground King! Tell all your friends that Nigel the Pretender has been slayed!".] [As the man continues his rant, Nigel returns to consciousness. The Mighty Von Huron stomps around the room with his arms in the air, celebrating the victory in the face of Nigel's fans. Seeing this, Nigel becomes infuriated.] [The the World of Undergroud fighting there is one rule which all who compete must abide by: never attack a man when his head is turned. But in Nigel's book there is one "no-no" which cannot be tolerated: You never show up an opponent on his own turf.] [As AVH stomps around gloating and ignoring him, Nigel gets to his feet and and leaps onto the big man's back, locking on a kataha-jime and falling to the floor on top of AVH. The man with the money takes off toward the exit, yelling for the fight promoter.] [Nigel keeps the choke locked on until AVH goes limp. In a low growl, Nigel whispers in his ear.] NJ: "You fu[censored] with the wrong guy this time, hot shot..." [The money man and the promoter burst into the room with two huge men, presumably the "bouncers" of this underworld. The two mountainous men grab Nigel and pull him off, leaving AVH limp on the ground. The promoter points to Von Huron and the money man runs to check on him. The promoter addresses Nigel.] TP: "What happened, kid? Why did you do this?" [Nigel licks the blood from his bottom lip.] NJ: "He was too cocky .. he thought he beat me worse than he did." [Nigel looks down at AVH.] NJ: "But you were wrong .. weren't ya big man?" [The money man raises his eyes to meet The Promoters eyes.] MM: "We've gotta get him some help .. and quick." [The Promoter turns his attention back to Nigel.] TP: "Meet me in 'Charlies' tomorrow night at grub time. Let him go boys." [The Bouncers release Nigel and head over to AVH just as The Promoter motions for them to do.] [Nigel wipes the blood from his bottom lip and stares at his crimson soaked fingertips. Meanwhile The Bouncers and The Money Man leave the room carrying AVH. The Promoter follows them but stops at the exit and speaks to Nigel.] TP: "I don't why you did it, kid. I don't know if it was because you lost .. or whether it was justified. Either way .. when the law finds out what happened here tonight..." [The Promoter shakes his head in disgust and perhaps with a sense of sadness over Nigel's pending troubles.] TP: "Just lay low, kid.. and remember, 'Charlies' at grub time." [The Promoter climbs the steps leading up out of the room. Nigel walks over to a bench on the far side of the room and takes a seat. He grabs a black leather coat which lay on the bench and drops it in his lap. Resting his elbows on his knees and his head in his hands, Nigel sighs.] [Lowering his arms, he slips his right hand into one of the coats pockets. When he removes it he's holding a small white paper between his index finger and thumb. For moments he stares at the paper as if contemplating his next move. Getting to his feet, Nigel slips on the coat and grabs a pair of boots from under the bench. He stares at the slip of paper one last time before dropping it, letting it ride the breeze until it lands on the floor. Slowly he makes his way across the room, stopping to take in the scenery one last time. Flipping off the lights he makes his way up the stairs, leaving these final words echoing through the room.] NJ: "Cutting Edge Wrestling.. *hmpf* .. damn." EDWARD DANDRY ...................................................... [Four thirty-two. A slow pull back from the clock reveals an expansive room, generic in its color scheme (white) and sectioned by cubicles. Each is increasingly depressing in their own right. One of these stations belongs to Edward Dandry. He's young, but appears rough around the edges with unkempt hair and wrinkled clothes. He lights a cigarette, and as he does, we spin around him until we're in a ring.] VOICE Let's go, gentlemen. [Dandry flicks the smoke into the crowd and, bare-chested, begins circling an equally bare man. They are underneath several hanging lightbulbs and surrounded by others who hoot and holler. Dandry and his opponent come together in a flurry of blows--punches, kicks, scratches, etc. They're laying waste into each other without hesitance. Neither care; Dandry almost vertically drops the guy on his head. Sweat and blood, two men letting go of all their connections with the outside world...] TYLER DURDEN VOICE Forget about what you think you know about life. ['Fight Club' is on the television across from the sofa. Dandry snaps awake, his eyes wide and cold sweat dripping down from his forehead. It's late, two in the morning by the clock on the wall. The open bottle of beer is flat and warm and a cigarette has burned to ash in the tray. Dandry rubs his eyes, leans back, and tries to get more sleep.] VOICE One... [Dandry's being pinned by the man. The referee is counting. The other guys are flooding the ringside, screaming. From Dandry's point of view, the place is spinning, is hazy, is fogging and losing steam.] VOICE Two... [The muscles are weak, and the man of stout figure. Dandry strains. He tries to keep a grasp on this. He has to keep this going.] VOICE Three. [Dandry's eyes snap open again. It's a little after two in the morning. 'Fight Club' hasn't progressed much. He turns it off. His eyes close. Sleep has embraced him once more.] VOICE The report is due today. [The cubicle spins, the faces of executives above each side, going and coming faster, faster, faster. Dandry tries to work, tries to type. The pressure's too much. The monotony. He can't see his coworkers; he hasn't. Ever. His world is the cubicle, the faces above him gods. Faster and faster.] FACES Today, today, today. [Dandry's eyes snap again, once more, to the silence of his apartment. He's been asleep for five minutes, probably less. There's a stack of papers across the room, on his computer desk, forgotten about until now. Dandry breathes heavily. Panic. He searches. The clock ticks.] TYLER DURDEN VOICE How much can you know about yourself if you've never been in a fight? [The television's off, and the voice seems to come from everywhere. It echoes, echoes, echoes. Dandry grips his sofa and tries to regain composure. It ain't coming.] NARRATOR VOICE If you wake up in a different at a different time in a different place, could you wake up as a different person? [Fade.] ------------------------------------------------- [The glass of water on the nightstand has been refilled thrice. Clothes litter the floor of Dandry's bedroom. He's packing a suitcase on the bed, feverishly trying to decide what to take and what to leave behind. It's four a.m. Every few seconds he takes a look out the window. He's in a race with the approaching sun.] DANDRY (VO; filtered) Yeah, so can I get a flight for six a.m.? [In the bathroom, lights blinding his newly wakened eyes, Dandry begins picking items off the counter and tossing them into a duffle bag. Then he sweeps everything in one motion into the bag, zips it, and dashes.] DANDRY (VO; filtered) No. Before sunrise. [The kitchen. A cramped space with room for a sink, a stove, and a refrigerator. Nothing else. Dandry throws a heaping spoonful of instant coffee into his mouth, follows it with a glass of water, swishes, drinks. He perks up.] DANDRY (VO; filtered) No, I'm not wanted by the police. I'm just not a day person, all right? [A kitten trips up Dandry, who picks it up and moves it up onto the sofa. Then he pauses. The kitten. What to do with the kitten? He ponders... ponders... ponders... voila! Cut to a door. Not Dandry's door. This one has a flowery welcome sign bearing the name of the occupants: The Reillys. Dandry puts down a basket. In it, wearing a bonnet, is the kitten, and a sign before him reads: 'Love me.'] DANDRY (VO; filtered) Three hundred bucks? F[beep]... Oh, don't hang up, don't hang up. I'm sorry. [Five thirty-six a.m. on the digital alarm. Dandry rushes by, arms full of clothes. He stuffs these into a second suitcase, presses down on it, and zips. He checks the spread on the bed: two suitcases and three bags.] DANDRY (VO; filtered) Yeah, I'll take them. [The sky is beginning to turn colors. Dandry rushes to his car, pops the trunk, and dumps everything in. Then he jumps into the driver's seat, guns the engine, and roars off.
