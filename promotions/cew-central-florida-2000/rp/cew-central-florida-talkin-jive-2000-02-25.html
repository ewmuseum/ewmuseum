“Talkin' Jive”

25 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
================================================== CUTTING EDGE WRESTLING: "TALKIN' JIVE" http://cew.virtualave.net | [detail withheld] February 25, 2000 ================================================== 1. Da Man 2. Don Watson 3. Sean "Venom" Powers 4. Rob Payne <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> DA MAN <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [The scene opens to a panorama view of New York city. We see the classic New York skyline, then the camera pans downwards towards the streets. We come down through Times Square, past the Warner Bros. Store, and into the alleys.
As the camera gets close to the red light district we see more signs for Peepland, and others of the like. The camera closes in on a dark alley, and we see the shadow of man sitting against a brick wall.]
Interviewer: What are we doing here? Somebody told us to come here looking for a wrestler? Can you tell us where he is?
???: If you are looking for a wrestler you came to the wrong place. If you are looking for a role model you have the wrong number. And if you are looking for a friend, you'd better turn around and leave now.
But if you are looking for a savior, stay where you are. I was born a bred on these streets, I know what it's like to be on the bottom of the chain.
That's where everyone starts. I want you to know, that I am not a wrestler. I am not a role model. And I'm, definately not your friend, nor do I want to be. It's people like you who make men throw themselves through barbed wire, hurl chairs at one another and beat the living crap out of each other.
I have come to the CEW to be your savior, the representative of all that you people hate. I don't hit people with chairs, I don't throw people through tables, I am a fighter. I beat people. Come "To the Edge" you will see why
I am called Da Man. When I walk out of that arena with a belt around my waist, you will see that blood does not ned to be spilled to see a good match. The only reason it is shed, is because of you fans, you [beep]ing fans can't watch a decent match anymore. You don't have the attention span to do that. I will show you all the a match can be won by only using solid in-ring skills. You all will remember, Da Man!
[The interviewer shakes his head and turns to leave. As he walks away we hear him talking to the cameraman, "He's going to be killed", and I'll be right there to applaud when it happens.]
<><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> DON WATSON <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [ Fade In ] [ A burly fellow is seen standing inside the CEW locker room. ] Man: It looks like [name withheld], Owner of Cutting Edge Wrestling, has scheduled a
Don Watson/Da Man match for the first card. 'To the Edge' they call it. It seems to me like two different style of wrestlers, two different styles of men, will be clashing in this match. No .. I've never seen Da Man. No ... I've never even heard of Da Man. But the name itself shows the difference.
You see, Da Man, I've been in this business for a number of years. I've learned every trick in the book, from all the best. I've toed the line with some of the greats. Some I beat, some beat me. I've had my share of hard knocks in and out of the ring. If it so happens my destiny in the ring is to lose to Da Man, then so be it. But destiny is man-made. So the better man will walk out. The man without destiny on his side .. will be carried out.
[ Don Watson is a not an extremely muscular man, but his bulk makes him appear to be. He's wearing a plain, light blue, t-shirt and blue jeans. That's all that is apparent. ]
Now I'm not gonna stand here and tell you I'm going to be the one walking out at "To the Edge". But I'll be damned if I get carried out. It means too much to me. This business is just that ... business. But what goes on inside that ring is extremely personal, and it gets personal very quick. What I do in that ring determines what my family does outside of it. So I take the pressure and use it in my favor. I'm not in this business to impress the girls ... I've outgrown that stage of my life. My tights are not colorful .. my entrance isn't flashy.
But all that counts in professional wrestling is the 1-2-3. Thats it. So Da Man, you come to the ring with the glitz and glammer of the new age. You climb into the ring with your hair feathered, playing to the girls. Disrobe to the oo's and ahh's of all the women. Meanwhile I'll be waiting for the bell in the opposite corner. And when that ding starts the match, don't turn your back.
Cause even though I don't wear the Superman insignia on my chest .. it's gonna take a lot more than kryptonite to get the best of me. It's gonna take a man that knows how to take the punches, and come back with a few of his own. No small task.
Da Man ... prepare for what's coming. Get your mindset right. And get ready for the inevitable bloodshed.
[ Fade Out ] <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> SEAN "VENOM" POWERS <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> (Scene fades in on a cabin in the middle of the woods. It is dark and cold outside and the lens is starting to fog up a little bit. Finally the camera reaches the cabin and knocks on the door. The door opens but noone is standing there. The camera cautiously makes its way to the couch beside the fireplace.
Suddenly the door slams open and a man walks in with a huge Deer over his back. He slams it down to reveal the former Venom, Sean Powers. Powers is wearing a Forest green Flannel shirt and a pair of faded blue jeans. He walks over to the kitchen, with Deer in hand, and throws it on the table. He stops for a second to wash his hands as he begins to talk.)
Powers: Well it looks like its time to work now isn't it? For those of you who don't know me my name is Sean Powers. I am here to reach the top of my game and become the World Champion. Looking at the list of relative "Unknowns"
on the roster, it shouldn't be to hard. My first match is against some rookie named "The Precedent" Stanley Cash. Well Stan, you can think up all the nicknames you want, but it won't do a damn bit of good when I nail the Evenflow on your pathetic ass.
(Powers finishes washing his hands and turns the water off and walks over to the living room.)
Powers: Now Stan I don't know if you've figured it out yet, but I am not your ordinary wrestler. I have had matches in my career that you could never imagine. I even have my own Gimmick Match that I basically invented. I have had fights that actually got feds kicked off of television they were so violent. All I have left to say Stan is that on February 28, 2000 at "To The
Edge" you better call Optima, Blue Cross, or even Medicare to make sure your Medical Insurance is still in good standing for that long Hospital stay you will be enduring. Stan I'll see you on the 28th.
(Powers gets up and walks into the bathroom as the camera is turned off.) <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> ROB PAYNE <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [Darkness. The ever-dull, often mind-numbing darkness. A period of time in which television producers like to call "dead air". But also a period of time in which gets on many people's nerves. Nothing to listen to. Nothing to see.
It isn't even long enough to take a trip to the fridge or to take a leak. Five more seconds wasted in your pathetic life. While you sit there pick your nose, the audio kicks in and you hear something. It gets your attention and you listen closely.]
[Clack.] [Clack.] [Clack.] [Clack.] [Sounds like footsteps. The man couldn't be wearing sneakers, so it's got to be some sort of dress shoes. And I don't believe that sort of 'clacking' would happen if it were on concrete. Kinda gets you thinkin', don't it?]
[Fade up.] [The scene appears in an old cathedral. The walls are a yellowish white, aging every month. It has cracks in it and old paintings of Jesus, our lord and savior. The floor is made of old rustic colored stone. Some of the stones are cracked and some are missing completely. Years of dust has gathered on this floor. The pew, or benches to the less witted individuals, used to be a fine oak. Now they are faded and warped. Looks like termites have infested the once commonly used benches.]
[As you look up you see several displays of stained windows with all sorts of colors and pictures made out of them. A few of them are broken. The sun gleams through each of these windows, making the entire cathedral look almost like a toned down rainbow. Up at the front of the cathedral is higher elevated space in which sits several vases garnering dead flowers and plants. Hanging on the wall behind these dead flowers is a huge wood carving of Jesus Christ crusified on the cross.]
[Suddenly you see the man that was responcible for the footsteps. He wears a completely white Armoni suit, with black leather dress shoes shined so that you can see yourself in them. The man is an average-sized man, standing at over six feet tall. He has long, strait, brown hair that is pulled back into a pony tail. His short brown goatee is shaven perfectly. He also wears a pair of metallic looking Oakley sunglasses, shading his eyes from the colors provided by the windows. His hands are tucked deeply in his pockets as he strolls up through the middle aisle. As he walks his shoes leave footprints, as the dust is kicked up off the stone floor. He stops about ten feet in front of the camera. This man is Rob Payne, CEW's newest aquired wrestling talent. He begins speaking.]
"All of my life I have been taught not to steal. Taught not to lie. And taught not to disobey my superiors. When I was young I went to a catholic school my parents put me in. A place where you were not to steal. You were not to lie. And you were not to disobey. Basically a environment prone to sins."
[Rob stops and looks around at the old cathedral that he stands in. He thinks for a moment and returns back to looking into the camera lense.]
"I attended services twice a day in cathedrals just like this one. A place in where they held masses and sang church songs. I was taught to go to confession daily, and pray a night before I went to bed. My parents didn't like to allow me to see the real world. They didn't want to expose their innocent sweet young child to the violence and hatred that existed outside of this pure environtment. So they trapped me in this prison."
"A place where I didn't know what fear was. A place where I was unable to commit sins without holding a guilty conscience. They confined me in this solitude hoping that God would take care of me for the rest of my life. I was expected to be a preacher or bishop when I grew up by them. Not once would they have ever thought that I would be, what I am today."
[Payne pulls his hands out of his pockets and raises them to his sides at about the level of his head. His hands are opened.]
"They expected this place to purify my body, heart, and soul. But all it did was make me anxious. I wanted to see what was outside of the walls of cathlicity. Being held in here, not only phisically, but mentally, was absolute torture. Torture that a man like me couldn't handle. I had to escape. I had to see what was beyond these "walls"."
[Rob takes his Oakleys off and folds them up. He then sticks them in a pocket on the inside of his Armani sports jacket. He looks back up at the camera with his cold eyes. He continues.]
"I was never meant to be held in the arms of God. No, that aint me. I never liked the feeling of a guilty conscience, or holding the weight of the World's problems on my shoulders. But my parents had insisted on it."
[An evil grin spreads across Rob's face.] "Little did I know, that on March 7th, 1981 my parents would no longer by on the face of this earth. Flight 351 to Hollywood, California. I remember it as it were yesterday. When someone broke the news to me, I knew deep down inside
I was supposed to cry. I was supposed to be in deep sorrow of their passing. But I didn't have that feeling. Instead I felt pleasure. Something no normal human being would feel. For the first time in my life... I sinned."
[Rob's grin fades away and is replaced by a blank stare. His hands go back into his pockets.]
"Ever since that moment in my young childhood, I have never looked back. I do not want to experience the love of God. I do not want to pray and worship him.
When it gets down to it, I'm the only one that matters!" "Everything I have done in my lifetime, I've done on my own. I never had to rely on people, and I didn't expect that from anyone else. Sinning is my life, and I do it well."
[Rob takes a few steps to his right and leans up against a bench. He crosses his arms.] "Ya know, after being out of this buisness for a good four months, I was offered a lucrative contract by CEW Booker and Owner [name withheld].
Apparently he looked back at some of my past wrestling organizations and noticed me. Whatever."
"Fact of the matter is this... Cutting Edge Wrestling, you may not have heard of the name Rob Payne, but I assure you in time you won't be able to stop hearing it."
"All of my career I've been on the "other" side of the wrestling world. Well now morons, I'm invading your side! I've been looking around and have been noticing that guys like Brian King and Kerry Powell are the best you low-budget organizations have to offer. Well, lemme get something straight right here, right now. Not one of these wrestlers impress me at all."
"Each and every one of them are nothing but over-hyped, over-payed, wanna-be superstars. Now I could go on and on about how good I am, but I'm going to spare all of you the time. I know for a fact that all of these "superstars"
will see my face across the ring from them. And they will know that I mean buisness. End of story."
[Rob stops to ponder for a moment.] "I'm going to move onto my opponent for the first ever To The edge event. Apparently I'm facing some less than charasmatic wrestler known as "The Hero In Black" Widowmaker. [name withheld], who in the hell do you think I am?! Do you think I'm a person to f[bleep]k around with?! Hell no! I am the best form on talent you have in this organization, and it's people like Brian King that are stinking the place up."
"I suggest you pull your head out of your ass, put on your glasses, and see what's best for this company."
[Rob stands back up straight. He turns around and walks back down the aisle back towards the front of the cathedral. You can once again hear the
"clacking" his shoes made earlier. He spots and looks up at the cross with Jesus on it. He then slowly whips around and poses just like Jesus is.] "Sacrifice is the only escape..." [Fade out.] <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> PSYCHOTIC YOUTH <><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><><> [A pop-up image flies up on the screen. It reads "Psychotic" then another flies up reading "Youth". The screen goes to black then opens up to a locker room filled with alchohol and completely trashed. Sitting down reading a magazine is a very beautiful woman dressed in a low cut skirt. The camera is alittle shaky as if it was a home video being used. It then jumps over to a young man in his early twenty's. He smiles, while messing with his goatee. The young man is dressed in casual attire, he has black hair with blue eyes and a pair of sun glasses rest on top of his head.]
[JARMEL]: "Let me be the first to introduce you to the newest, baddest, and soon to be most hostile and psychotic team on the planet. I'm Vitor Jarmel and this is my partner Chris Vlass..."
[As the youngster mentions Vlass, the camera quickly spins around to a full blown shot of Chris Vlass's face. Vlass has bleach blonde hair with light green eyes. A nose ring covers his left nostril as he looks like the wild teenager type guy.]
[VLASS]: "That'd be me!" [The camera swings back around to a shot of Jarmel again. The camera is very shaky and often jerks back and forth.]
[JARMEL]: "The slut you see sitting down reading cosmopolitan would be Vanessa Right, and I'm telling you boy she sure gives good he--...." [He gets interuppted by an upset Vanessa. In the background giggling is heard from Vlass.] [RIGHT]: "If your gonna introduce me, please do it the RIGHT way! Names Vanessa Right, I manage these two walking hormones. Only god knows why I put up with there shit." [JARMEL]: "You put up with it because deep down inside you like it. Anyways, enough about her, take a look at me. Ladies look at this body..."
[Interupting.] [VLASS]: "Fellas too...you know he swings that way..." [JARMEL]: "Again I'll just ignore that comment. Like I was saying, Pyschotic Youth is on the verge of total tag wrestling domination. This Monday, we'll get our chance to prove it. Make no doubts about it."
[The camera falls to the floor and a shot of they're feet is shown.] [JARMEL]: "SHIT! You dropped it!" [Fuzz then comes over as the camera fades out.]
