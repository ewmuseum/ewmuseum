FWA Sunday Night Showdown (3/24)

28 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{scene opens on the familiar golden FWA logo on a black background. All of a sudden, the background lights up to show a jam-packed Pacific Coliseum in Vancouver, British Columbia. The logo turns red hot, and explodes, filling the screen with sparks. The camera pans down to show the two familiar figures standing at ringside, thus the start of this week's..} FWA *S*unday *N*ight *S*howdown Drake: Hello, fans, and welcome to this week's edition of Showdown! We've got some tremendous action lined up for you this week, including a match with limitless potential, as Federal champion Zeus takes on a white-hot 'Dynamite' Jack Watson in a title matchup! Fantasia: And who KNOWS who'll get involved in that one.. Drake: That's only the tip of the iceberg, fans, but let's not waste any more time.. to the ring! Announcer: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. First, already in the ring, from Iowa City, Iowa, at 252 pounds, the 'Great' Matt Britton! And his opponent.. from Chicago, Illinois, at 304 pounds, managed by Johnny Terror, he is the NEW FWA North American champion.. here is Apocalypse! {White Zombie's "I Am Hell" cranks as the massive Apocalypse, his new prize strapped around his waist, stalks to the ring, accompanied by the always flamboyant Terror. He reaches the ring, tosses the belt aside, and charges at Britton immediately.} Drake: And a quick start to this one, as the new champ nearly takes Britton's head off with a clothesline! He grabs the smaller man, and whips him into the ropes.. power slam! 1.. 2.. and Britton gets a shoulder up! Apocalypse backs him into the corner, and flings him across the ring! He follows.. and CRUSHES Britton into the corner with a running lariat! Britton slumps to the mat, and Apocalypse covers.. no! He grabs Britton by the throat, and lifts him up! He brings him to his feet! Fantasia: I think now Britton knows how it feels to be lynched! Drake: Apocalypse lifts Britton up by his throat.. he's choking all the air out of him! The ref screams at him to relinquish the illegal hold, and he starts counting.. and as he reaches four, Apocalypse PLANTS Britton on the mat with a mighty chokeslam! 1.. 2.. 3!! Apocalypse claims the convincing victory! Announcer: Here is your winner.. APOCALYPSE!!! Fantasia: I have all the respect in the world for Big Bad Bobb, but this guy is unstoppable! Drake: I'd have to agree.. fans, before we go any further, we want to get you caught up on a few matches that took place earlier this evening.. *** INDIAN NIGHTMARE -v- QUICKSILVER *** Fantasia: That had to be the hundredth two-count in this damned match! Drake: The Nightmare stumbles to his feet, and backs into the corner, leaping onto the middle turnbuckle.. and he nails Quicksilver with a flying axehandle! 1.. 2.. and Quicksilver escapes! He gets to his feet quickly, and all of a sudden, he charges at the Nightmare with a clothesline! He misses! He bounds off the ropes, and the Nightmare nails a back body drop.. NO! Quicksilver lands on his feet! He connects with a knee to the solar plexus of the Nightmare, and he runs to the ropes.. spinebuster by the Nightmare! 1.. 2.. and Quicksilver gets a shoulder up again! Fantasia: Back and forth, back and forth.. yawn.. Drake: The Nightmare picks up Quicksilver.. who pops him in the gut! DDT! 1.. 2.. 3!! Quicksilver takes it! *** MANTA RAY -v- THE BAKA *** Drake: And now the Baka drives Manta Ray's head into the canvas AGAIN! He pulls him up, and starts gnawing on his ear! This Baka is inhuman! Fantasia: Yeah.. kind of a nice change, isn't it? Drake: Hardly! Manta Ray dives out of the ring, and the Baka follows.. and Manta Ray catches him with a swift savate kick to the chest, knocking the wind out of the Baka! Manta tosses him back in the ring, and catapults himself over the top rope onto his foe! 1.. 2.. and the Baka gets out! Fantasia: This punk isn't even in the Baka's league! Drake: Felix, I can't think of ANYBODY who's in the Baka's league.. Jeffrey Dahmer, perhaps.. Manta Ray scales the ropes, and drops a big leg on the Baka! He's in a world of hurt! Fantasia: Yeah, right. Drake: Manta Ray goes up top again.. and the Baka gets to his feet! He grabs Manta Ray, and SLAMS him to the mat.. but Manta Ray holds on, and rolls the Baka into a pinning predicament! 1.. 2.. 3!! What an upset! Fantasia: Huh? What? HE WON?!?! Announcer: Here is your winner.. MANTA RAY!!! {camera shot returns to live action, as Cyrus Wilcox is heading to the ring.} Announcer: The following matchup is scheduled for one fall. First, from the other side, at 321 pounds, managed by 'Gigolo' Jared Butler, here is Cyrus Wilcox! {As Steppenwolf's "Born to be Wild" blares, Wilcox steps into the ring.} Announcer: And his opponent.. also from the other side, at 255 pounds, he is managed by Mr. Kang, here is the X-Ecutioner! {Suddenly, Black Flag's "I Am The Law" takes the air. As the fans look around, confused, the X-Ecutioner sprints from the locker room like a homing missile headed straight for Wilcox.} Drake: What is this?? The X-Ecutioner is pounding on Wilcox, who didn't see it coming.. and I'm confident he's not alone! He flings Wilcox to the ropes, and nails a BIG power slam! 1.. 2.. and Wilcox gets a foot on the ropes, with some assistance from Butler! The X-Ecutioner dives out of the ring, and Butler runs for his life! He dives under the ring, and the X-Ecutioner heads back into the ring.. and the waiting Wilcox gives him a size 18 in the mush for his troubles! Fantasia: What a dope this guy is.. and people actually LIKE him? Drake: Wilcox tosses X-Ecutioner into the ropes, and swings an elbow, but misses! X-Ecutioner bounds off the ropes, and nails a leaping shoulder block! He runs to the ropes, and delivers a big knee drop! The match is all X-Ecutioner! Fantasia: With all of Butler's resources? Don't be so sure. Drake: X-Ecutioner picks up Wilcox, and slaps on a cobra clutch! Wilcox is flailing his arms wildly, trying to escape! He finally catches the X-Ecutioner in the gut with a big elbow, breaking the hold.. and Wilcox quickly drives the X-Ecutioner's head into the turnbuckle! He picks him up.. jawbreaker! And Wilcox slaps on the Mandibular Nerve Hold! Fantasia: Say goodnight.. the party's over.. Drake: X-Ecutioner struggles, but can't reach the ropes.. finally he grabs Wilcox by the back of the head, and retaliates with a jawbreaker! Fight fire with fire, I suppose! He picks up Wilcox, and bulldogs him to the mat! 1.. 2.. and Wilcox kicks out, and gets out of the ring! Fantasia: X-Ecutioner doesn't seem to quick to follow him, Dick.. Drake: Indeed.. Wilcox finally returns to the ring, and the X-Ecutioner quickly charges, dropping his opponent with a running axehandle! 1.. 2.. and Wilcox evades the pin yet again! Fantasia: He's a survivor! Drake: The X-Ecutioner picks Wilcox up, and slams him to the mat! He goes up top! He leaps with a flying elbow.. and MISSES! Wilcox rolls to his feet, and quickly goes right back to his bread and butter, the Mandibular Nerve Hold! Fantasia: Try and get out THIS time, you masked boob! Drake: X-Ecutioner is reaching for Wilcox's head, but the former World champ deftly evades his wandering hands.. and the X man finally settles on Wilcox's left knee, driving a series of fists into it! Wilcox yelps in pain, and breaks the hold! He picks up the X-Ecutioner, and sets him up for a reverse neckbreaker.. and nails it! 1.. 2.. and the X-Ecutioner gets out! Fantasia: Finish him, Cyrus! Drake: Wilcox flings X-Ecutioner to the ropes, and goes for a back drop.. X-Ecutioner leaps over him, and Wilcox spins around.. and catches a brutal right hook! He hits the mat! Fantasia: That's a closed fist! DQ him, ya stupid zebra! Drake: Wilcox quickly gets back on his feet, and look at the expression on his face! He's feeling to see if his jaw is still in place! The X-Ecutioner grabs him, and nails a short-arm clothesline.. no! The clever Wilcox ducks it, and bounds to the ropes! And the X-Ecutioner catches him with a kick to the gut! Swinging neckbreaker! The X-Ecutioner picks up Wilcox, and places him on the top rope! He's going for the Nullifier! Fantasia: He can't possibly get Wilcox with that move! Drake: He climbs to the top rope, and sets it up.. he leaps, and Wilcox shoves him off! He hits the mat hard! Wilcox screams, and goes for a flying splash.. and the X-Ecutioner gets his knees up! Wilcox impales himself on them! He doubles over, holding his stomach in agony.. and X-Ecutioner sets him up for a piledriver! Fantasia: No way! Drake: He picks up Wilcox.. and drives him headfirst into the mat! And a cover! 1.. 2.. 3!! He did it! Announcer: Here is your winner.. the X-ECUTIONER!!! Drake: And listen to that crowd! The X-Ecutioner leaves the ring chuckling as Butler tries to revive Wilcox.. what a match! Fantasia: Well look who's here! Drake: 'Suicide' Ricky Watson and Mountain are making their way towards the ring, or more precisely, towards the X-Ecutioner! Watson sprints at him, and the X-Ecutioner dodges him, and takes him down! The two are throwing punches wildly on the ground, but here comes Mountain.. and who's this?? It's 'Superstar' Steve Adams! He jumps on Mountain's back just as he reaches the X-Ecutioner and Watson! Mountain reaches for him, but can't get a firm grip! Fantasia: OUCH! Looks like he didn't need one! Drake: Oh my! Mountain just fell straight backwards, crushing Adams under him! The big man picks up Adams, and grabs him by the throat.. we've seen this before.. choke slam! Adams is hurt! And Mountain doesn't look like he's done! Fantasia: Um.. see ya, Dick. Say hi to big man for me. Drake: Mountain is headed here.. get off of me, you thug! <feedback as Drake's headset hits the floor.> {As Drake and Fantasia dart aside, Mountain drags their table to the center of the ringside area, and places Adams on it. He rolls into the ring, and climbs up to the top rope. As the fans fall into a hush, he leaps, and drives his thumb into Adams' throat. Adams immediately begins convulsing and coughing up blood. Drake suddenly grabs his headset from the carnage.} Drake: PARENTS, GET YOUR KIDS AWAY FROM THE T.V.! Steve Adams has been brutally assaulted by Mountain, and he's obviously been seriously injured! The paramedics hit the ringside area, but Mountain won't let them get anywhere near Adams! My God, he's going to let him bleed to death! Now there are dozens of officials in the area, but Mountain won't budge! WAIT! Who's this?? A huge tower of a man has come to ringside, and he wades through the ringside masses, going right for Mountain! He grabs him, and incredibly, drags him aside, allowing the paramedics access to Adams! He's lost a lot of blood already, and I'd be amazed if there wasn't some serious permanent damage to his throat.. and this huge man is battling with Mountain right before my eyes! He's got at least six inches on Mountain, who is no midget himself! And they're brawling uncontrollably.. and now the Vancouver Police have arrived1 Thank god! They're breaking up the combatants with billy clubs and pepper spray.. this is unreal! They're cuffing Mountain and his huge foe, as well as X-Ecutioner and Watson! Fans, if I need to say it, we've got some serious work to do before this card can continue.. STAY TUNED!! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- end part 1 Questions/Comments about the FWA? The results are this: sadly, it seems that 'Superstar' Steve Adams' young career has come to an end. I'm no doctor.. Fantasia: Amen to that! Drake: ..but the damage that Adams suffered to the larynx and throat area will never fully heal, and from all indications, he shall not be returning to the FWA, or to the ring anywhere. In addition, Ricky Watson, the X-Ecutioner, Mountain and the man who we now know goes by the name David Goliath have all been arrested by the Vancouver Police. While in MOST cases the arrests were undeserved, they were beyond our control, and as a result the 'Suicide' Ricky Watson versus Akira Takazawa matchup has been postponed. Now then.. while we wait for a new table, take a look at this. ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ G-Funk: Man, dese suckas, da Powers of Pain, think dey deserve a shot at what we got, da FWA World Tag Team Title. What you think bout dat K-Dogg? K-Dogg: I think dat dey be dreamin'. Powers of Pain, exactly who have you beat? NOBODY!! We been bustin' our butts since day 1 of da FWA and we won da Tag Team Title TWICE. You walk in here, wrestle 1 match, lose by DQ to the SEC, and think you deserve a shot? Well boys, you got another thing comming. G-Funk: But, we decided to step in da ring witcha anyway, non-title, just to prove dat you two is just a couple of punks. Ya see, you seem awful cocky for some fools who have wrestled a grand total of 1 match, and lost. We here tonignt to remedy dat situation. K-Dogg: Dat's right. You gonna be sorry you ever challenged da Ghetto Boyz. We ain't pullin' no stops, we gonna whip yo punk a**es just like a couple a bi**hes. And at da end, you'll know why we is da 2-time FWA World Tag Team Champions. See ya in da ring, suckas. We outta here. ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- Announcer: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. First, from the streets of New York, at a combined weight of 601 pounds, here are Apollo and Goliath, the Powers of Pain! {The duo of bruisers head to the ring to a moderately positive response.} Announcer: And their opponents.. from South Central Los Angeles, at a combined weight of 520 pounds, managed by 'Bad, Bad' LeRoy Brown, they are the current FWA World Tag Team champions.. G-Funk and K-Dogg, the Ghetto Boyz! {Dr. Dre's "Keep Their Heads Ringin'" blasts as the champs head to the ring. They storm to the ring looking like men possessed as Brown, with an FWA World Tag strap over each shoulder, begins harrassing the ringside fans in his customary manner.} Drake: Well, the Powers of Pain pushed pretty hard for this match, but I'm not sure they want it now.. the Boyz look like they've been ready for this match for weeks.. and they start it off in a hurry! K-Dogg charges at Apollo, and hits a leaping kick to his face, knocking him through the ropes! The Boyz go to work on Goliath.. they fling him to the ropes, and drop him with a double clothesline! Fantasia: Still want a shot at the champs, punks? Drake: G-Funk and Goliath are going at it fast and furious, and G-Funk gets the better of it! He flings Goliath to the ropes, and makes the quick tag! He charges with a clothesline, but Goliath ducks it.. and catches a superkick in the face from K-Dogg! And it knocks him right into G-Funk's waiting arms! POWERBOMB! And K-Dogg comes off the top rope with a moonsault! The cover! 1.. 2.. 3!! Wow! Fantasia: The champs made quick work of the chumps. Anyone surprised? Drake: Well I thought they'd give them a little more of a workout than THAT.. Announcer: Here are your winners.. the GHETTO BOYZ!!! Drake: Well, it won't be so easy when the Quadruple-U's get their rematch.. Fantasia: Who? Oh, the Quadruple-pukes.. please! They want nothing to do with Brown's boys! Drake: We'll just see about that.. fans, we've just gotten word of a curious turn of events! Akira Takazawa, anxious to get his match with Ricky Watson, has sent his translator and personal assistant Dr. Lawrence Johnson with $2,500 to bail Watson out of jail! Can you believe that? Fantasia: What a sap! He's actually bailing Watson out 'cause he wants to face him? He's loonier than 'Suicide'! Drake: We'll keep you posted on that matter, fans.. but in addition, we have some interesting action coming up, as a new group has arrived on the scene in the FWA.. they have some words of wisdom before their debut next week, so let's take a look! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ [Scene begins with a hooded figure standing in a misty room. The hooded figure starts to talk in an erie, broken voice] THE LEPER: My name is The Leper. My mission is to seek revenge on a world which left me to die on a remote island. Since my escape, I searched for the perfect warriors to bring forth my revenge. I have found them, and I have brought them to the FWA to train them. Once my Army of Darkness has conqured the FWA, they will be ready to bring forth my revenge! [A huge figure appears from the mist. He stands next to The Leper grinding his teeth] THE LEPER: Perhaps you would like to meet your doom. First, meet Hellraiser. I brought him out of his Hell hole, and he serves only me to repay his debt. I have told him to do only one thing, destroy our enemies. HELLRAISER: I await first match. I rip off arms, legs, and eat rest for breakfast! You puny wastes of time, I destroy you all!!! Ha, ha, ha, ha! [Two more large figures appear from the mist. They are identical twins] LUCIFER: My brother and I are the sons of Satan. Together we fight as the Armageddon. We have come to the FWA to train for the coming of the end of the world. With the leadership of The Leper, we shall prove our worth to our father by offering him "human sacrifices". I pity the first people we meet in the ring. Tell them Damien... DAMIEN: Oh, yes. It is coming... The day is coming... The first people we meet in the ring will have the honor of being our first gifts to our father. Soon, it will come, and no one can stop it. THE LEPER: Plus, we have already allied ourselves with a wrestler already in this federation. His soul is evil to the core, and he has already proven himself worthy to join The Army of Darkness. Together, we are obligated to destroy everyone who stands in our way of the championship. We will do whatever it takes to wear those belts around our waists. So save yourselves pain and anguish, stay out of our way! [Then the four men walk deeper and deeper into the mist, until they are no longer visible] --------------------------------------------------------------------------- Announcer: The following matchup is scheduled for one fall, and is for the FWA Federal title! First, the challenger. From Livonia, New York, he weighs in at 216 pounds, here is 'Dynamite' Jack Watson! {The confident Watson strolls to the ring, and immediately begins stretching in preparation for the big matchup.} Announcer: And his opponent.. from Conway, Arizona, at 310 pounds, managed by Thor, he is the FWA Federal champion... Zeus! {The big God of Thunder storms to the ring, as Thor follows with the Federal belt. Zeus immediately tangles with Watson.} Drake: And what a matchup this promises to be.. Zeus backs Watson into the corner, and nails a series of chops! He flings him across the ring, and catches Watson with a quick lariat as he staggered out of the corner! Watson hits the mat.. and Zeus drops a BIG elbow! The cover! 1.. 2.. and Watson slips out! Fantasia: Sorry big man, but nobody can take Watson THAT easily.. Drake: Zeus picks his challenger up, and flings him to the ropes.. he swings an elbow, but Watson ducks! He leaps at Zeus with a flying cross body block, but Zeus catches him.. fallaway slam! 1.. 2.. and again Watson gets out! The champ picks him up, and scoops him onto his shoulder! He's going for a tombstone piledriver.. but Watson slips behind him! Cranium kick! The champ goes down hard! 1.. 2.. and Zeus powers out! Fantasia: You know, Watson's been seeking some FWA gold for quite a while.. Drake: Well, he has his work cut out for him.. he picks up Zeus, and catches him in a dragon sleeper! Zeus yells in pain, and tries in vain to escape.. and Watson drops him down with an inverted DDT! He covers! 1.. 2.. 3-NO!! Whew! Fantasia: A little slow on the count there, friend.. Drake: Watson turns Zeus over, and catches him in an Indian deathlock.. and now he bridges back to catch him in a chinlock! What a painful move! Zeus is trying to escape, but he can't! This could be it! Zeus finally manages to roll over, and breaks the hold.. and he drops down onto Watson, locking him in a painful clawhold! Watson makes it to the ropes, but Zeus won't break the hold! The ref counts, and finally he relinquishes his grip.. Fantasia: This match is downright NASTY, Dick! I love it! Drake: Clearly there is no love lost between these men.. now Zeus flings Watson across the ring, and charges.. and Watson jumps to the middle turnbuckle, and springs backwards onto Zeus! And Zeus takes Watson over with his own momentum! 1.. 2.. OH! Watson barely escapes! Fantasia: Hoo boy, a close call if ever I've seen one.. Drake: And I'll wager that you have.. Watson scrambles to his feet, and quickly CRACKS Zeus in the jaw with a thrust kick! The champ hits the mat! Watson climbs the ropes.. and Thor just threw his hammer at Watson! What is that?? Fortunately, he missed, and now Watson shoots a furious look at Thor, who backs off considerably.. OH MY! Zeus used the little break to scramble to his feet, and he charges into the corner, knocking Watson down to the cement floor! Fantasia: WHOA!! Drake: Watson is in a world of hurt! Zeus heads out of the ring, and tosses him back in! Quickly, he plants Watson on the mat with a DEVASTATING powerbomb! The quick cover! 1.. 2.. 3!! Zeus retains the title! Announcer: Your winner, and STILL Federal champion... ZEUS!!! Fantasia: Well, tough break, but that's not the last we'll see from Watson.. thank god! Drake: Well, fans, we'll have more great action for you next week, along with hopefully some more information on the Falcon situation.. Fantasia: Don't even mention that simp's name in my presence, Dick. Drake: Until then.. wait! What's this? Akira Takazawa is heading to the ring, ready for action! Fantasia: What's his damage? {suddenly, "Egg Man" by the Beastie Boys blares. Ricky Watson, still with handcuffs on his left hand, enters from the dressing room, Takazawa's translator Dr. Lawrence Johnson in tow.} Drake: What the.. Watson has Lawrence Johnson with him, and he's a bloody mess! Watson shoves him to the floor, and runs to the ring! He starts pounding on Takazawa, who wasn't expecting it at ALL! And now the bell rings! I thought they cancelled this match! Fantasia: You heard, Drake.. Takazawa went and bailed out Watson! What a BRILLIANT tactical move! Drake: Watson slams Takazawa into the mat face-first, and wraps those cuffs around his neck! He's choking the life out of him! Finally, Akira slips an arm around Watson's waist, and takes him up and over with a belly-to-back suplex! Takazawa gets to his feet, and drops a leg over Watson's throat! He covers! 1.. 2.. and Watson escapes! Fantasia: Now THIS is money well spent, eh Akira? Ha ha! Drake: Takazawa picks Watson up, and flings him to the ropes.. he jumps with a leg lariat, but Watson puts on the brakes.. and Takazawa kips back up to his feet! He charges at Watson, who back drops him over the top! Takazawa hits the cement floor hard! And Watson's going up top! Fantasia: Say goodnight, Akira! Drake: Watson FLIES.. and crashes down with an elbow to Takazawa's back! What a lunatic! He picks Takazawa up, and runs.. and powerslams him on the ring stairs! Takazawa's in a world of hurt! Watson hurls him back into the ring, and goes up top! He leaps, and nails a splash! 1.. 2.. 3-NO!! Takazawa powers out! Fantasia: Slow count! Drake: Watson pulls him up, and flings him across the ring! He charges with a lariat, and Takazawa catches him in the kisser with a dropkick! Watson crashes to the mat! Takazawa pulls him up, and nails him with a series of kicks to the chest! Now he flings Watson across the ring, and follows.. sunset flip by Watson! No! Takazawa rolls out of it! He swings a European uppercut at Watson, who ducks and knees him in his ailing back! Takazawa grabs his side, and Watson quickly catches him in a full nelson.. DRAGON SUPLEX!! 1.. 2.. 3!! What a move! Fantasia: Well, Takazawa wanted that match pretty bad.. I hope he got what he wanted.. Drake: Somehow I doubt it.. well, fans, it's been pretty bizarre, but I guess that's just par for the course here in the FWA.. we'll see you next week! Fantasia: Don't miss me too much, chumps! {scene fades to black} -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- end part 2 Questions/Comments about the FWA?
