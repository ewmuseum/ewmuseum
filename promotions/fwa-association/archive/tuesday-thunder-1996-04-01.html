[Announcer] The next match is scheduled for one fall, and has a 20 minute time limit. IeWS, stay out of our way. ***** ****** ***** ***** ***** ***** Apocalypse: You know Falcon, I used to have a lot of respect for you. You were the Federation's first North American champion, A title I myself would later hold after you gave it up. Then you went to defeat the great Cyrus Wilcox for the World Heavyweight Championship. You have the audacity to go to IeWS and renounce the FWA and criticize its wrestlers and its management. Well, Falcon, you know all that respect I had for you... IT'S GONE!!! You even had the nerve to claim yourself the greatest North American Champion of all time. You never lost the belt, true. BUT YOU DEFENDED THE DAMN THING A GRAND TOTAL OF ONE TIME!!!! AND YOU DIDN'T EVEN F**KING WIN THAT MATCH, YOU HAD A DRAW WITH THE MAN YOU SEE BEFORE YOU. THE MAN CALLED APOCALYPSE!!!!!!! I could have beaten you then if I had more time and you know it, that's why you went after Wilcox instead of granting me a rematch, ISN'T IT!!!! You are nothing but a spineless coward, seeking refuge in the IeWS. Well go ahead and hide, Falcon. If you don't like it here then get the f**k out. But as long as you are FWA Champion, you must stay and defend, and the Apocalypse will be there, looking right over your shoulder. You must pay for your actions, and belive me, YOU WILL!! The end is near for the reign of the Maltese Falcon, AND THE APOCALYPSE IS UPON US!!!! HA HA HA HA HA HA..... Now if only that'd work on Eagle, I'd be right where I wanted to be at this momoent in time. The point is is, Falcon is nothing more than a coward, he couldn't take the heat, so he got the hell outta my kitchen! He's lucky, and he'd better hope we don't run into him in the near future. Now onto another issue, Jared Butler, my friend, you've done a fair job of handling my boys while I was gone, but you <BLEEP> up! Your mismanagement of the great Cyrus Wilcox cost my camp the World title! I'm willing to forgive you, but only if you follow my directions very carefully. Like I said you did a fair job, I didn't expect you to do great, I mean hell I'm the greatest manager this sport has ever seen... oh sorry got off track there. What I'm telling you to do, is this: hand deliver the contracts of Cyrus Wilcox and Ricky Watson to my secretary within the next week, and all will be forgiven. I don't think you want to know what'll happen if you don't! ***** ****** ****** ***** ***** <Mr. Bill> -Well, well, well. What a situation we have here. First the FWA loses there best man when Mean Mountain Max had the unluckiest day of his life and lost a loser leaves fed match. Then they get disgraced on nation wide TV. So what happens. The comish turns to the only man who can fix the problem. Me! And then what happens. They allow the Man that Rules the World, Mean Mountain Max, back in the fed. Yes, you read right. Max is back! He will show the world that the FWA is not a bunch of wimps. (Looks at Ghetto Boyz and Apocalypse)Sure there are some. But my man Max is going to prove the FWA has the greatest around, even if he can easily beat them all. Mean Mountain Max- I AM GOING TO KICK EVERYONES @@% THAT GETS IN MY WAY!! Mr. Bill- But after talking to the commish a little more, he told me he needed more. Well, being the nice guy I am, I am bringing a new man in. He will scare everyone within 100 yards of him. Why you ask? His name says it all. He is EBOLA. No one in the FWA or any other fed has ever seen this man. No one will until the time is right, which will be soon. There are no words the explain this man. You all will have to wait and see. And last, but not least. This message goes out to MF. You made the biggest mistake by disgrace the FWA. The commish is very upset. He had put his trust in me and others of the FWA to prove you are very wrong in thinking what you do. You will pay for what you have done, I will be laughing the whole time. Trust me!!!!!!!! ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ [Guile] BWAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA! E.] Good day, and welcome again to the dojo. Today, I am greatly pleased to have with me the only other female managers in the FWA. Please welcome to the Dojo, Mississippi Queen! (As Mountain's "Mississippi Queen" blares over the sound system, the Mississippi Queen enters the Dojo alone. She is wearing a conservative navy business dress) [MQ] Thank you Miss Elyxabeta. It is an honor to be invited to appear here on the Dojo. We represent a dying breed, I'm afraid. Most wrestlers prefer to surround themselves with cheap floozies and airheads called valets instead of intelligent women like ourselves. [Mis. E.] And thereby lose one of the greatest assets a wrestler can posses. Yes, it is a common problem. It also seems that those who do not appreciate us single us out for verbal or physical assault. The both of us have earned the hatred of Felix Fantasia, for example. The self titled fabulous one has said quite a few unpleasant things about us. What is your reaction to this? [MQ] Well, I just consider the source. It takes a really big man to take a swing at a woman. Fortunately (looking at the Destroyer) we both have the necessary means to deal with such slime. Foremost on my mind, however, is 'Gigolo' Jared Butler. First off, we can see why he has a second job. Considering his looks, we know the gigolo business isn't paying his bills. It is no surprise that he chooses to align himself with the likes of Big Badd Bobby and Cyrus Wilcox. In fact, Mr. Gerber and I are in the process of challenging little Bobby for that North American Belt. We expect him to act like a coward and run away from any serious threat. Of course, he can't run forever, either Billy or the American Gladiator are bound to catch him. [Mis. E.] I remember how you "dealt" with "Pimp" Davis the last time your path's crossed. And frankly <evil grin> I found it brilliant. You turned his own profession, and his own lust, against him. It was not in my style, but it was truly masterful. But beyond that, to your title inspirations, I honestly think you will have great success. Mr. Gerber proved himself to me when he last defeated Speed, a man my own stable is troubled by. With Mr. Gerber's wrestling skills, the Gladiator to prevent interference, and your own cunning, talentless brawlers like Big Badd Bob should prove little challenge. And should Bob, or any other of his like, try to remove Mr. Gerber from wrestling after he has already won, you may counton assistance from my associate and myself <She nods towards Destroyer>. Your mention of Gladiator has brought up another point though. It seems his teammate, Ultimate Wrestling Machine, has been the victim of my competition in this slot, Mr. Guile. Your thoughts? [MQ] That development is most distressing. However, UWM's FWA past proves that he knows how to get out of tight jams. Guile is a snake, no doubt about it; but he is a *shrewd* snake. Hopefully he won't tear apart the Forces of Good like in the old FWA. The breakup of that team could hurt the tag division. The kids need more teams to look up to. Perhaps a Destroyer/Gerber pairing could provide such a team? By the way, congratulations are in order for Destroyer's first round victory in the Round Robin Tournament. Quite a formidable showing, I look forward to the time we meet at the ring. It will be comforting to face an honorable opponent. [Mis. E.] I thank you. A Destroyer/Gerber team up would be interesting. But I'm afraid we have already found a teammate for the Destroyer. You can expect to see a new tag team as soon as stableclash is over. Perhaps they will meet the Forces of Good soon. Only time will tell. Speaking of time, I have kept you from your business to long. Thank you greatly for taking the time to enter the Dojo. I hope to interview Gerber soon, as the North American champion. [MQ] Thank you and good luck. The FWA needs more honest managers in the federation.
