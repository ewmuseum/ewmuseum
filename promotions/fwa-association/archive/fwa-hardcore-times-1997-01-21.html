FWA-Our Newsletter

21 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This is the newsletter that we in the FWA use to help our league members enjoy the league a little more! Enjoy and maybe you will want to join the FWA! <><><><><><> FWA Hardcore Times 1/21 <><><><><><> In this Issue: 1.> Profile of new World Champion Steve Gunn 2.> Interview with Malice 3.> Top 10 4.> Clotheslines!!!! 5.> Word form the Editor <><><> PROFILE <><><> >Wrestlers Name:Steve Gunn >Height:6'2 >Weight:285 >Hometown:Cleveland >Years Pro:6 >Titles in FWA:FWA World Heavyweight Championship >Titles in other E-Feds:VSW Gold Champ >Toughest Opponent in FWA:Malice >Who you respect the most in FWA:Gangsta Crush >Other Comments:The FWA is the BEST fed I've ever been in...and I'm also part of the BEST stable The Too Hot Crew...Simply the BEST <><><>Interview<><><> FWA Hardcore Times Reporter Tony Capo recently sat down with the demented Malice for an interview..... >Tony Capo: How long have you been wrestling? >Malice: I've been running over the competition since about June >Tony Capo:Who would you say in FWA has been your toughest competition? >Malice: "Gangsta" Crush without a doubt, and I beat that punk within an inch of his life. >Tony Capo: Do you consider yourself more "hardcore" then Crush? And you say without a doubt, is there anyone that compares to Crush? >Malice: I am easily more hardcore than Crush, he can't compare to me. You see, when you aren't sane, you tend to do things normal men can't handle. I haven't seen anyone else who compares to Crush in this category. >Tony Capo: Is there any of the stables in FWA today you would consider associating with? >Malice: It's funny you ask that. I've currently betrayed a stable in another federation, and am having problems with another stable I'm in in that league. Malice is a loner, Malice is the man without loyalty. >Tony Capo: What would you do if a group like The Too Hot Crew, who currently own all the gold except the tag belts said come on aboard? Even if your styles clash. >Malice: I can work with other people, but they must respect the fact that I will change my style for no one. I do my own thing, and if they want my services, they must respect my wishes. >Tony Capo: I see, What are your feelings on the NwO? >Malice: I want to be as honest as possible. I hate seeing people who can't be original. But I see them as another stable that can easily crumble. I see them no different as anyone else. I see them as a group of people who can soon fall victim to me if I choose to go after them. >Tony Capo: What is stopping you from going after them? Do you honestly believe you as one man can take out the whole NwO? >Malice: No, I can't take them all down, but if I were to go after them, I'm sure I can take some of them out. The reason I do not go after them is because I have no problem with them at all. They've stayed away from me, so I shall not be a thorn their side. >Tony Capo: Well thank you very much Malice for your time, any final comments for the fans of FWA? >Malice: Thanks for wasting my time. You want a comment for the fans, I got one. I hate all you fans. Do you think I wrestle for you? I wrestle because it is a chance to cause human suffering. All you fans are just leeches, thinking we do it for you. I hate you all, and if any of you give me a problem I'll kill you all myself. >Tony Capo: Well with that oh so pleasant note we are concluding this interview, with in my opinion one of the toughest guys ever seen in FWA or anywhere. He has taken Extreme Gangsta Crush to the EXTREME and he may just be coming after you! <><><>Rankings<><><> This weeks rankings are based solely on my < the editors > picks. I hope you like it. We are gonna improve it, but I need feedback. I also need the STAFF to get back to me. We hope to make this the BEST just like the FWA the BEST!! Now I know what you thinking I am just kissing butt to get title shots, but thats all wrong. I am not one to discredit myself to get further. I tell it like I feel it. And the FWA is the best right now. As far as this newsletter, well its got tons of room for improvement. But we are working on it and will have it full go real soon.
