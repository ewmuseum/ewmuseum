He tries to kick out the Falcon's other foot, but the champ LEAPS and catches Takazawa in the back of the head with a brutal cranium kick.. and the newcomer just slumps to the mat! The Falcon picks him up.. and quickly locks him in a Dragon sleeper! Takazawa's neck has already taken so much tonight.. and he gives! The match is over! Announcer: Here is your winner.. the MALTESE FALCON!!! Drake: And look at this! The champ is helping Takazawa to his feet, and raises the youngster's hand! And the two are leaving together! Now, I think that's one of the few signs of sportsmanship I've ever viewed in my years with the FWA. Fantasia: And with any luck, it'll be the last. Drake: Well, fans, we know you're waiting for it, so let's not waste any more time.. for the Federal title matchup, we go to the ring! Announcer: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, and it is for the FWA Federal title! First, the challenger.. from Toledo, Ohio, at 280 pounds, managed by 'The Duke' David Jones, here is the 'Playa' Steve Speed! {Ice Cube's "Who's the Mack" takes the air as the popular Speed runs to the ring. He takes no time to greet his fans, and his face shows that he is totally focussed on the task at hand.} Announcer: And his opponent.. from Conway, Arizona, at 310 pounds, managed by Thor, he is the FWA Federal champion, this is Zeus! {Thor emerges from the locker room curtain, and raises his arms. He points back at the curtain with his massive hammer, and suddenly the new Federal champ Zeus appears. He holds the belt aloft, and waves it at the crowd, who boo him loudly.} Drake: What a massive task Steve Speed has ahead of him.. he's never beaten Zeus in one-on-one competition! More to the point, he's been beaten convincingly TWICE. Fantasia: Well, if it were the OLD Steve Speed, I might give him a chance. But with his new attitude? Yeah, MAYBE. Drake: Speed rushes at Zeus as soon as he enters the ring, and flings him to the ropes! He hits a quick powerslam, and covers! 1.. 2.. 3-NO!! Zeus kicks out at the very last second! Fantasia: What a fast count! I guess we know who the ref wants to win, eh? Drake: Please, Felix.. now Speed picks Zeus up, and shoves him into the corner.. he flings him across the ring, and follows with a lariat! Zeus stumbles out of the corner.. Speed runs to the ropes, and comes straight at Zeus.. and hits a running bulldog! 1.. 2.. and Zeus kicks out quickly! Speed pulls him up, and twists his arm.. and connects with a short-arm clothesline! He runs to the ropes, and quickly delivers a big leg drop! 1.. 2.. and Zeus forces his way out! Fantasia: C'mon, champ! You've beaten him bad two straight! Drake: As had the Quadruple-U's.. Speed flings Zeus to the ropes, and leaps, catching him in the jaw with a high knee! He grabs him again, and sets him up for suplex.. but Zeus blocks it! And he catches Speed with a brain buster! 1.. 2.. and Speed narrowly escapes! Zeus runs to the ropes, and drops an elbow! He picks Speed up, and runs him into the corner.. OH! He surges as he gets to the corner, taking Speed's head straight into the ringpost! Speed drops to the mat! His forehead is lacerated! Fantasia: Bleed him, Zeus! Like a sacrificial pig! Drake: Speed is writhing in pain, and Zeus pulls him to his feet.. and hits a swinging neckbreaker! He climbs the ropes! He gazes at the crowd, and leaps.. and lands a big splash on the challenger! 1.. 2.. and a half! He pulls Speed to his head, and flings him to the ropes, but Speed reverses! He charges with a clothesline, taking the champ over the top rope! He follows him out! Zeus gets to his feet, and Speed immediately charges at him with a chair, cracking it on his forehead! He quickly grabs the champ, and slams his head into the stairs.. RIGHT ON THE CORNER! Zeus makes it to his feet.. with blood dripping down his face! He sees the blood, and growls at Speed.. and tackles him! Fantasia: A bad move by the would-be champion.. Drake: Zeus takes Speed down, and nails a series of punches to his face! He picks him up, and tosses him back in the ring! Zeus climbs to the apron.. and Speed, who got to his feet as quick as a cat, dropkicks him in the face, knocking him to the floor! Speed goes right back out! He drags the timekeeper's table to the middle of the ringside area, and turns his attention to Zeus.. who pokes him in the eye! He grabs Speed, and heaves him into the crowd! Fantasia: Up close and personal with John Q. Public! Ha ha! Drake: Speed slowly makes it to his feet as Zeus returns to the ring.. and he climbs to the apron. But Zeus assails him before he can enter the ring! He swings a big fist, knocking Speed back, but he stays on the apron! Undaunted, Zeus sets him for a suplex.. he heaves, but Speed blocks it! And again! Speed lifts Zeus, and the two CRASH to the cement floor, as Zeus is suplexed onto the timekeeper's table! This is unreal! Fantasia: How can that not be a disqualification!? Really!! Drake: Speed shakes off the cobwebs, and grabs Zeus! He heaves him into the ring, and enters it himself.. and he pauses, holding his head! These men have both lost a lot of blood! He grabs Zeus, and sets him up for ANOTHER suplex.. he heaves, and nails the champ with the Sidewalk Slap! 1.. 2.. and almost three! So close! He shakes his head in frustration, and picks Zeus up.. he flings him to the ropes, and throws a lariat at the big man, who ducks.. and as Speed turns around, Zeus catches him with a clothesline of his own! Speed hits the mat hard! 1.. 2.. and Speed gets out! Fantasia: Dammit, ref, you gotta count the same for both guys! Get that through your tiny brain! Drake: Zeus picks up Speed, and flings him to the ropes.. he pressed the challenger in the air! And he just drops him out of the ring like so much garbage! He follows him out, and grabs another table! He puts it in the same place, and drags Speed up to the apron! He sets him up for a powerbomb! Fantasia: Heh heh.. say goodnight, Stevie! Drake: He lifts him up.. BUT SPEED SLIPS OVER THE BACK! He grabs Zeus, and leaps with a bulldog.. BUT ZEUS GRABS THE TOP ROPE! Speed crashes down on the table! Zeus falls into the ring, and scoots into the far corner! The ref starts counting! 1.. 2.. 3.. 4.. 5.. 6.. Speed makes it to his feet! 7.. 8.. 9.. and with some help from David Jones, Speed rolls into the ring! Fantasia: What a bad decision! Drake: Speed struggles to his feet, and Zeus charges him, dropping him with a shoulder block! The champ runs to the ropes, and misses with a running elbow! He bounds off the ropes again.. RIGHT INTO A POWER SLAM BY SPEED! 1.. 2.. 3-NO!! How did he escape! Fantasia: The champ's got this one in the bag, Drake. Thor will see to that. Drake: This is absolutely gruesome.. both men's faces are caked with blood, and the ring is a mess of crimson drops.. Speed gets to his feet, and shouts, raising his arms to the crowd.. and they respond! He runs to the ropes, and charges with a double axehandle, but Zeus ducks just in time! He bounds off again.. and Zeus hits a picture perfect tilt-a-whirl! The count! 1.. 2.. 3!! It's over! Zeus is still the champ! Fantasia: I told you so, Dick! I TOLD THE WORLD! MUAHAHAHAHAHA! Announcer: Here is your winner.. ZEUS!!! Drake: OK, fans, we've got to motor.. for the clinically insane 'Fabulous' Felix Fantasia, I'm Dickie Drake, saying goodnight! Fantasia: Sleep tight, simpletons. And when you see Zeus in your mindless nightmares, say hi for me! {camera fades to black} Questions/Comments about the FWA?
