FWA: the Maltese Falcon Jumps Ship

19 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
From: Commissioner Eagles's office.... [Eagle] I was watching the various SWA federations yesterday, and I have come upon this very disturbing segment on the show IeWS Spotlight...read this, and I will return with comments.... ---------- Forwarded message ---------- --<SNIP OTHER IeWS Matches>-- (The fans aren't quite sure how to react when "Flying High Again" by Ozzy Osbourne starts on the sound system. The lights go down and a single spotlight focuses on the entrance. A lean, well-muscled man with olive complexion and a black mask enters the arena, heading down the aisle with his black cape flowing behind him. The camera zooms in on the gold title belt around his waist... it is the FWA World Championship belt! The pops begin as some of the fans who also watch the independent leagues recognize him as the FWA World Champion, the Maltese Falcon! The camera zooms back in on the announcers, who are quite confused.) [MOB] Um, what's he doing here? [te] I don't know, but I'm about to find out! (The taN eeL grabs a house mic and heads into the ring just as the Falcon hits the ringside area. Many of the IeWS fans are a bit skeptical, however. Some guys in the tenth row holding r.s.p.e-w signs start chanting, "Shoot! Shoot! Shoot!") [te] Ladies and gentlemen, the FWA World Heavyweight Champion... THE MALTESE FALCON!!! (A general loud pop from the marks... they can tell that the guy's a face, at least. The Maltese Falcon just stands there, looking out over the crowd and nodding his head.) [te] Now, Maltese Falcon, could you perhaps tell us exactly what you're doing here on IeWS shores? You're the FWA World Heavyweight Champion and I know the IeWS has no talent-sharing contracts signed with the FWA. What gives? [Maltese Falcon] taN eeL, my friend, I am here for a reason. Listen to these fans, you can feel it. This is what professional wrestling is all about, the competition between men vying for superiority in this ring. >From my debut in WAR! and reign as Freedom Champion, I have relied upon my own skills in the ring. Through my time as the Heritage Champion in the UCWA, it was the Maltese Falcon giving his all for the sport. In the FWA, I was the greatest North American Champion in the history of the fed -- never losing the title, having to relinquish it in order to attain this! (The Maltese Falcon holds up the FWA World Title for the fans to see. He then holds it at eye level... and unceremoniously throws it to the ground!) [JP] My God! Did you see what he just did? That's not gonna go good with the FWA heads, I can say that much! [MOB] You're telling me... I can see the lawsuits coming in from the FWA now... [Maltese Falcon] BUT NO MORE!!! The FWA is nothing *I* want to represent. What good is it to be the best among no talent, unathletic brawlers who can do nothing but hit people with chairs? Is there any pride in being the Champion of a federation where wrestlers who have skills and are good workers are relegated to jobber status? Who could be happy where every match results in a run-in, fans are routinely attacked, and 'extreme' means the blade and not awesome workrate? The FWA is nothing but a second rate organization where the unemployable can find a paycheck just by hitting someone with a chair. If you have talent, you aren't welcome in the FWA. From here on out the FWA can find themselves another fool to wear this worthless piece of plastic and tin. (The Maltese Falcon picks up the FWA World Belt and throws it into the rabid, popping crowd.) IeWS PREPARE FOR THE COMING OF THE BLACK BIRD!!!! (Maltese Falcon exits the ring as the taN eeL just stands there, mouth agape.) ========== [MOB] As of yet, we haven't heard anything from the higher offices of the FWA, but I can tell that they're definitely not going to be happy with what the Maltese Falcon did, or that we allowed this to be broadcasted! [te] It's not like we were prepared to do it or he was scheduled to be here. What were we supposed to do... kick him out? [JP] That's what we should have done. But now, we're just gonna have to sit back and await the consequences! (cut to Commissioner Eagle's office. The man sits at his desk, and slowly takes his glasses off as he looks at the camera with a stern face) [Eagle] .....forgive me a moment, I haven't the words to describe the feeling I have right now, perhaps a merger of unhappiness and total anger at the IeWS. I HAD no ill feelings toward the IeWS. As a matter of fact, I was a watcher of the federation, as I watch all of the SWA federations. They are the so-called "elite" e-wrestling federations, and good things can be gotten from them. True, they were in a higher bracket than the FWA, mostly because of better markets than the independant federations. I am angry at the Maltese Falcon for his actions, he should have come to me, one of his biggest supporters in this federation, if he had any complaints. He should not have done what he did. But my problem isn't with the Falcon per se, but with the IeWS. WHY??? Why did you decide if would be nice to attempt to humiliate the FWA by showing this drivel by an angry, CHILDISH, IMMATURE wrestler who would do nothing but whine when he was attacked by other wrestlers? Getting hurt is a part of this e-sport, Falcon, but you obviously have shown your true colors. Go to the IeWS. The IeWS, in my view and the view of the fans of the FWA, hide behind the flag of the SWA, and the popularity of such federations as the EWF and AWI. They don't have the ability, nor the talent to defend themselves, that is why they must steal other wrestlers from other federations to do their work for them. Your coveted World Champion, IeWS, isn't even a member of your federation. It irritates me that you decide to pick on a smaller federation. Well, you've picked on the wrong one. The FWA, as compared to other feds, will fight back. Fight back hard. You hate blood and extreme actions, Falcon? You despise the attitudes of the wrestlers of the FWA? IeWS, accept this wrestler into your folds, WHILE he is STILL UNDER CONTRACT WITH THE FWA, and I will ensure you will pay for it. Falcon, you are the champion of the FWA, and YOU WILL BE THERE TO DEFEND IT ON THUNDER. You have no choice. There isn't a damn thing the IeWS can do about it. (fade out) -----------------------------------------------
