~1st Annual E-Wrestling Awards~

24 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
~1st~ Annual AOL E-Wrestling Awards Before we get started,here are some quick notes. These figures for the winners are accurate. I checked and doubled check and even triple checked the figures at hand. They are all legit. 54 ballots were completed! So,on with the show!!!!! (Screen opens to where FWA Broadcasters, Kip Thomas and "Dirty" Doug Flynn are sitting in front of a banner that says "AOL E-Wrestling Awards") Kip Thomas: Hello everyone! I am Kip Thomas and I am here with my broadcast partner, Doug Flynn and its our pleasure to bring to you,the AOL E-Wrestling Awards. Doug Flynn: Speak for yourself. I could be home watching SPICE right now! KT: I am sure you would be. Anyways, we will give you the top five vote getters,then one of us will open the envelope and tell you the winner. The winners should be very honored,for they have been voted on by their peers. It is very prestigous to win this award. DF: Are you going to talk all night? Lets give out some awards!!!! KT: Allright, the first award that we will be giving out tonight is the award for "Best E-Wrestler" And the nominees are............ Tommy Van Dam Gambler Adam Lash Jimmy Cougar Archangel KT: Those area all great wrestlers and each of them deserve this award. Doug,you may do the honors! DF: <opening the envelope> And the winner is................. Adam Lash of the USW!!!!!!!!!! KT: Congratulations to Adam Lash! You have been voted AOL's Top E-Wrestler by the fans!!! [When I say that,I don't mean,just AOL wrestlers,but out of all wrestlers] KT: Lets look at the rundown of the scoring! [1]-Adam Lash-26 pts Runner Up-Jimmy Cougar-18 pts [3]-Gambler [CWF]-15 pts [4]-Tommy Van Dam-13 pts [5]-Archangel [EWF]-13 pts KT: Anyone of those men could have taken that award! DF: Can you believe Lash won it? Its a disgrace of democracy! I mean come on, he has't done nothing here in the FWA! KT: Yeah that may be true, but elsewhere,the guy has wrestled well,capturing many titles. DF: None of those titles matter,FWA gold is the best that you can get! KT: I won't argue with you there. Lets move on to the next award which is,"Best Tag Team" DF: I can't wait to see who wins this,specially with the FWA Invitational going on. KT: So here we go! The nominees are................. Powerhouses Funk & Guru Sabu Manslaughter The Pack The Outsiders KT: <opening the envelope> And the winners are................. Manslaughter DF: WOW! Those guys got eliminated early on in the FWA Invitational, but hell, they are a awesome team and they do deserve to win! KT: They most certainly do. They may not have done well in the FWA Invitational, but they have been voted the TOP TEAM in E-Wrestling!!!! DF: Congratulations to the team of Manslaughter,again from the USW! KT: That USW must be one helluva league. Must have alot of talent there! DF: Either that or they stuffed the ballot box! KT: Ha Ha! I don't think so Doug,lets take a look at the rundown of the scores! [1]-Manslaughter-25 pts [Runner Up]-The Pack & The Outsiders-15 pts [4]-Powerhouses [EWF]-10 pts [5]-Funk & Guru Sabu-10 pts DF: Manslaughter really kicked tail in the voting! KT: Yeah, well I guess thats the way things go. Lets move on to our next award,"Best Face" and the nominees are.............. Joey Freedom Dan "Flash" Kauffman Assaf Levavy Chris Quigley Zahadum DF: Dan Kauffman is a legend! He is one,if not the best wrestler out there today! KT: I will give him that,he does have alot of talent. DF: He sure does! Now,for the winner <opening the envelope>.....and we have a three way tie between............ Dan "Flash" Kauffman Chris Quigley Zahadum KT: Well they all tie for the award of,Best E-Wrestling Face! DF: The Legend gets himself another plaque for his trophy room! Just another one to shine is what he is probally thinking. KT: I don't know about that,he most likely thinks its a honor,I hope,cause it is! DF: Like I said, he is most likely thinking, "Not another one!"
