FWA Surprise Event

1 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
{scene opens on 'Fabulous' Felix Fantasia lying on a beach, surrounded by several scantily-clad women. One of them is feeding him grapes, and he holds a sizeable drink in his hand. Next to him lies a silver briefcase, which is soundly locked. He looks up.} Fantasia: Hey, suckers! Yeah, all of you FWA superstar wanna-be's! Bet you're used to treament like this, huh? Yeah, right.. well, have I got a surprise for you! See this? <he indicates the briefcase> THAT just might be yours by the end of tonight.. because we're gonna be randomly picking some of you saps to have a shot at it! Just beat 19 other guys, and my friend here.. heh.. belongs to you. And I ain't gonna open it for you, but see if you can guess what's inside.. <he bursts into laughter> Sorry. Well.. take it away.. DICK!! {scene shifts to ringside in the Hubert H. Humphrey Metrodome in Minneapolis, Minnesota. The huge facility is packed to the rafters. Suddenly, Dickie Drake enters the shot.} Drake: Hello, fans, and WELCOME to an event that was as spontaneous as they get! An unknown supporter gave us a big check, and asked us to present it to a worthy athlete, so after tonight's main event.. SOMEONE WILL!! ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ T H I S I S A S P E C I A L F.W.A. P R E S E N T A T I O N ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ {camera pans to Dale Jones, clad in a dapper tuxedo, and a beautiful woman standing with him in an extravegant gown. Behind them is a rotating plastic ball filled with ping-pong balls.} Jones: Greetings, FWA fans! This is Dale Jones, investigative ring reporter, and with me tonight is new FWA journalist Clarice Alamain.. welcome, Clarice! Alamain: Thank you, Dale! And hello to you, FWA fans! I hope to be seeing a lot of you in the coming weeks, but tonight you'll be seeing all action! We've got a random drawing for tonight's battle royal, and let's waste no more time.. with this microphone, I can read the names right to our ring announcer. Are we ready, Dale? Jones: We certainly are, Clarice! <he stops the ball's spinning> Reach right in! Alamain: I've got it! And the first competitor in the battle royal is.. Announcer: ..Irish Stu! Next, 'The Great' Matt Britton! Of the Senate Ethics Committee, 'Sudden' Steve Kennedy! 'Pretty Boy' Jack Melon! Of the Gods of Thunder, Hercules! Of Generation X, Stone! The X-Ecutioner! Ultimate Wrestling Machine! Angel 'El Macho' Morales! 'King of Smash' Justin Dory! Rugrat! Of the Cyberpunkz, Neuromancer! Of the Coast to Coast Connection, 'Airborne' Eric Ollie! 'Suicide' Ricky Watson! Walrus! Of the Powers of Pain, Apollo! Also of the Senate Ethics Committee, 'Dangerous' Davey Dole! Battle Monkey! Rick Showboat! And of Wisdom, Manta Ray! {the bell sounds} Drake: And the bell rings about thirty seconds after the match started, thanks to Ricky Watson! He charged straight at X-Ecutioner, and is still grappling with him! Well, well, look who's here! Fantasia: <out of breath> Hey, they were paying those chicks, right? Drake: Um.. yeah, why do you ask? Fantasia: No reason.. hey! There goes Rick Showboat! Drake: The newcomer flies out of the ring, courtesy of the bigger Walrus! Fantasia: Wow, wasting no time, eh? Announcer: Rick Showboat has been ELIMINATED! Drake: Well, we've got a lot of representatives of tag teams here, but the Senate Ethics Committee has both members in! And they're in there doing a number on Matt Britton! Now Melon and Battle Monkey are teaming up on the Ultimate Wrestling Machine! They whip him to the ropes, and charge with a clothesline.. but he ducks it! And runs right into an Eric Ollie backdrop! Up and over.. but no, he slips back in under the bottom rope! Fantasia: Lucky sod.. heh.. I see Walrus is having fun! Drake: Perhaps.. after eliminating Showboat, he got attacked by Ricky Watson, who seems to have lost interest in the X-Ecutioner.. And now Hercules and Neuromancer are teaming up on Stone! And Manta Ray makes the save! Fantasia: Look at Butler's boys! Hey, they're making the Gigolo proud! Drake: The Senate Ethics Committee are working over the X-Ecutioner in the corner, and they're forcing him over the ropes.. HE'S GOING.. but in comes Battle Monkey! He wrenches Dole's arm, and he wails in pain! Kennedy swings at him, but the X-Ecutioner catches him fist! The Monkey hoists Dole into the air by his arm, and yanks him to the side! Dole falls right over the top! Fantasia: WHOA! Announcer: 'Dangerous' Davey Dole has been ELIMINATED! Drake: Now Kennedy backs off, and Battle Monkey drives an elbow into the unsuspecting X-Ecutioner's face! Ricky Watson screeches, and pounces on BOTH! Meanwhile, Justin Dory and Eric Ollie are tangling with Apollo and Matt Britton runs in, levelling Apollo as he was held up by the other two! He picks Apollo up, and nails a quick DDT! Now Hercules is still battling with Stone, and the smaller man is giving him some trouble! Fantasia: Hardly.. Drake: Stone chops him, and again! The big man is shaken! Stone runs to the ropes, and clotheslines Hercules! He hits the mat! He struggles to his feet, and Stone charges again.. PRESS SLAM BY HERCULES! But Stone shifts his weight, and Hercules staggers backwards.. he falls over the top! Both men hit the floor.. NO! Stone barely holds on to the top rope! He's trying to pull himself up.. and Irish Stu kicks his hand! Stone yelps in pain, and crashes to the cement! Fantasia: Ha ha!! Announcer: Hercules and Stone have been ELIMINATED! Drake: Irish Stu is standing admiring his handiwork, and he gets attacked from behind by the X-Ecutioner! The big man, who now wears a skull- adorned mask, drops Watson to the mat with a bulldog! Now X-Ecutioner gets jumped from behind by Manta Ray! He pops him in the chops again, and again! Meanwhile, Neuromancer pounces on the fallen Watson! He picks him up, and hits a quick neckbreaker! And Watson gets right back up! He roars at Neuromancer, who is just staring at him! And Ultimate Wrestling Machine nails him from behind, and his momentum carries him right into a Ricky Watson back drop! Fantasia: Brilliant! Announcer: Neuromancer has been ELIMINATED! Drake: What a sketchy move by the Machine.. and now he offers Watson his hand! Watson grabs it, and chomps down on his fingers! The Machine yells in pain, and Watson kicks him below the belt! Fantasia: Oooohh.. Drake: Watson grabs the stunned Machine by the scruff of the neck, and hurls him over the top! Announcer: Ultimate Wrestling Machine has been ELIMINATED! Drake: Now Angel Morales has Manta Ray in the corner, and the two are furiously exchanging punches! Morales grabs the Ray, and forces him into the corner! And Manta Ray does the same! Morales grabs him, and suddenly hits the mat, as Manta Ray suddenly gets wrecked from behind by a charging X-Ecutioner! Fantasia: What a lunatic! Drake: He picks Manta Ray up off the mat like a rag doll, and places him on the top rope! He's going for the Nullifier! He climbs up also.. and gets clipped from behind by Kevin Ollie! He falls to the mat! Ray gets onto the top turnbuckle.. and as X-Ecutioner rises, Manta Ray leaps! X-Ecutioner pulls Ollie into Manta Ray's path, and the man they call 'Airborne' hits the mat like a ton of bricks! The masked man picks him up, and charges with another clothesline, this one taking him over the top! Announcer: 'Airborne' Eric Ollie has been ELIMINATED! Drake: Now Angel Morales gets back onto Manta Ray, and they tangle in the corner yet again! They exchange blows fast and furious.. but here come Justin Dory and Steve Kennedy! They charge the corner.. and both Manta Ray and Morales duck quickly, heaving their foes up and over! Fantasia: Just like that! Wow! Announcer: 'King of Smash' Justin Dory and 'Sudden' Steve Kennedy have been ELIMINATED! Drake: What a move by Morales and the Manta Ray! And Now Apollo and 'The Great' Matt Britton are grappling, and they struggle right into the corner with Manta Ray and 'El Macho'! Morales shoves Apollo off of him, and slaps Britton across the face! Britton charges him, and nearly takes his head off! He picks him up, and drapes him across the top rope trying to force him out! Fantasia: And here comes Manta Ray from behind! Drake: No! Apollo grabs Manta Ray before he can get to Britton and Morales! He swings a short lariat, but Manta Ray ducks, and shoves him towards the ropes! Britton shoves Morales aside, and scoops up Apollo, dropping him on the top rope and heaving him over! Announcer: Apollo has been ELIMINATED! Drake: And we're halfway there! Only ten men remain! And the X-Ecutioner attacks all three men as they watch Apollo hit the floor! He drops Morales with a discus punch, and headbutts Manta Ray to the canvas! Britton swings at him, and misses! He scoops him up, and plants the 'Great' one on the top! He's going for the Nullifier AGAIN! He climbs up.. here comes Ricky Watson! He climbs to the second rope, and clocks the X-Ecutioner with a cranium kick.. and the blow knocked the X-Ecutioner into Britton, who plummets to the cement floor! Announcer: 'The Great' Matt Britton has been ELIMINATED! Fantasia: Well, I'm glad that's over with.. now let Watson at the masked goon! Drake: Watson is indeed all over the X-Ecutioner! He takes him down to the mat with a reverse superplex! Now he scoops the X-Ecutioner up, and flings him to the ropes.. right into Irish Stu! He drives a shoulder into Stu's back, and returns his attention to Watson.. but Stu grabs him from behind! He knocks the X-Ecutioner to the mat with a European uppercut! He picks him up, and X-Ecutioner drives an elbow into Stu's throat! He kicks him in the gut, and goes for a piledriver! He nails it! He drags Stu to his feet.. and gets tackled by the Battle Monkey from behind! Fantasia: Now rip his arm out of the socket, too! Drake: The Monkey forces him to the mat, and plants a knee in his kidneys! He wrenches his neck in a gruesome direction! The X-Ecutioner at last forces the smaller Monkey off of him, and rises.. and Irish Stu grabs him from behind! He holds him in a choke and shoves him against the ropes.. and a HUGE clothesline takes the X-Ecutioner over the top! Announcer: The X-Ecutioner has been ELIMINATED! Drake: Battle Monkey leaps to his feet, and nails Stu with a series of blows to the ribs! He flings him to the ropes, and Stu reverses.. and he crashes into Jack Melon, who was grappling with Rugrat! Melon gets up, and clocks the Monkey with a superkick.. no! The Monkey ducks! He grabs Melon's leg, and twists it, causing him to drop to the mat! He rushes at the fallen Melon, bashing him with a series of forearms to the face! Now he picks him up, and props him against the ropes. He goes out to the apron, and pulls down on Melon's neck, hanging him over the ropes! Now here comes Irish Stu again! He nails a swinging punch to Melon's jaw, and effortlessly dumps him over the top! Fantasia: Get the Monkey! Get the Monkey! Announcer: 'Pretty Boy' Jack Melon has been ELIMINATED! Drake: Stu grabs the Battle Monkey in a clawhold, and pulls him over the top into the ring with it! Now he pulls the Monkley to his feet, and THROWS him over the top! Stu is on a tear! Announcer: Battle Monkey has been ELIMINATED! Drake: And now Ricky Watson is throttling Walrus in the corner! He's kicking him in the face as he's slumped in the corner! He pulls him up, and delivers a double-underhook suplex! He gets up, and starts gnawing on Angel Morales' forehead! 'El Macho' is getting uglified by Watson and his bloodlust! Morales shoves him off, and pounces on him.. no! Watson drives a headbutt into Morales chest, slowing him.. and he drops him to the mat with a DDT! He picks him up, and does it again! Morales is hurting! Fantasia: Watson's doing a number on him! Drake: He picks Morales up, and he sets him for another DDT! What brutality! AND HE GETS NAILED! As Watson had Morales set up for a DDT, Walrus levelled him with a clothesline, taking him over the top! Announcer: 'Suicide' Ricky Watson has been eliminated! Drake: And then there were five! Only Manta Ray, Walrus, Rugrat, Irish Stu and Angel 'El Macho' Morales remain! Walrus and Irish Stu are going nose to nose, and neither man will back down! Irish Stu shoves the big man.. and Morales sucker-punches Walrus from behind, taking him down! Morales plants a series of punches on Walrus' forehead, as Stu approaches.. and Rugrat jumps on Stu's back! He catches him in a sleeper! Manta Ray stands behind Morales, and waits for him to finish with Walrus.. and he nails an impressive standing leg lariat as he rises, dropping 'El Macho' like a bad habit! Fantasia: Impressive move.. I brought that boy to the FWA, you know.. Drake: Yes, Felix, very impressive.. oh my! Morales leaps to his feet, and catches Manta Ray from behind with a similar move, knocking him against the ropes! He nails Morales with a mule kick, and hops to the second rope.. and leaps with a back bodypress.. but Morales catches him, and backs against the ropes.. and both men are on the verge of elimination! Uh oh.. Fantasia: Guess who!! Drake: Irish Stu flies against the two men, propelling both over the top! They're both gone! Announcer: Angel 'El Macho' Morales and Manta Ray have been eliminated! Drake: Which leaves three! Irish Stu looks at Walrus, and then at Rugrat, who glares back at Stu.. and Stu charges him! He drops him with a big lariat, and pulls Rugrat to his feet.. and Walrus charges, and hits Rugrat with the Walrus splash! They pull him up, and Irish Stu flings him to the ropes.. and Rugrat comes off with a flying body press! Stu catches him, but loses his balance.. and the big Walrus nails a dropkick on Rugrat, propelling Stu towards the ropes.. and he dumps Rugrat over the top! Fantasia: Only two left.. Announcer: Rugrat has been ELIMINATED! Drake: What a matchup.. Walrus against Irish Stu! Well, we certainly know who the fans are behind! Fantasia: They're cheering for Stu, right? Drake: Not exactly.. and Stu charges at Walrus! He nails a boot to the gut, and hits a Russian legsweep! And now a big leg drop! Stu pulls him up, and gives the signal for his move! Fantasia: He's going for the Potato Drop! Drake: This is his version of the power bomb! If he hits this it's over! He pulls Walrus up, and the big man pulls him down with a flying headscissors! What a move by Walrus! He climbs to the second rope, and unleashes a flying axehandle.. NO! Stu catches him with a punch in the gut as he misses! THE POTATO DROP! Quickly, he pulls Walrus to his feet! Fantasia: UP AND OVER! Drake: He holds him against the ropes.. clothesline! Walrus stays on his feet! Another clothesline! Walrus swoons backwards, but uses the ropes to steady himself! ANOTHER clothesline, and Walrus falls back.. he's GOING.. HE'S GONE!! IRISH STU WINS! Fantasia: What a supreme athlete! Announcer: Walrus has been ELIMINATED! Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner.. IRISH STU!!! Fantasia: If you'll excuse me, Dick, I have a presentation to make.. Drake: Indeed, up we go to the ring, where Felix Fantasia is with our winner! Fantasia: Stu, congrats, man! Take a look at this! {he opens the briefcase, which is filled with crisp, clean 1996 hundred dollar bills.. Stu's eyes light up.} Fantasia: That's fifty grand, sailor! How's that make you feel?! Stu: Pretty damned good, Felix.. so if you'll excuse me.. {Stu leaves the ring, and attacks Walrus. Security rapidly fills up the ringside area, and finally Stu leaves with his manager, General Mills, and a big briefcase filled with cash.} Drake: Well, fans, it's been a pretty bizarre evening, so for myself, Dale Jones, Clarice Alamain and of COURSE 'Fabulous' Felix Fantasia, I'm Dickie Drake saying so long and good night! ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- Questions/Comments about the FWA?
