And as the days went by,I looked up to you....because you are so big...you have to look up to see you... <Fans start to laugh> Adam Lash: And so wherever you go,you will always be EXTREME,cause thats what you are...because there are people here,who want you to win the World Title,I want you do win the World Title,so I guess I am sorta issueing a challenge for you...whoever wins later tonight...I want Gangsta Crush to fight you,tommorow Night on Mayhem...good luck....cause you are the real World Champion... <Crush refuses to take that world title. Crush takes the mic from Lash> "GANGSTA" CRUSH: Lash, I thank you for your treatment and all the nice things you said to me but I want no special charity. I will get that World Title shot myself and when I deserve I will get that World Title. Lash, I thank you for giving me your respect and for everybody else. Right now I have one more thing to say... THE FWA IS THE BEST FEDERATION AND I HOPE THAT I CAN MAKE IT EVEN STRONGER AND EVEN MORE EXTREME!!!!!!!!!!! <Lash and Crush raise each other's hands and all the fans cheer> KT: Well that is or was a weird turn of events. I mean, wow, Lash came out of nowhere with that. DF: Lash may or may not be able to be trusted like alot of people say, but he knows how to show respect. This should be great!!! KG Ryan: Ladies and Gentlemen, this is our MAIN EVENT!!!!!!! It is a 2 out of 3 falls match.
