Are you bored? Do you have time on your hands? Are you creative but have no where to channel it? Do you love wrestling? The KWF is THE most INNOVATIVE e-fed on the internet. Whether your a pro e-fedder or you've never been to an e-fed before, the KWF welcomes you. This e-federation is all about the creativity. We encourage great rp's and if you're a beginner the Management Team will help you wherever possible. NO other e-fed has such a close knit community. NO other e-fed has a management team that will professionally critique your RP if need be. Additionally, this e-fed is run by SWITCH a real-life UK trainee wrestler. If writing about wrestling isn't good enough to satify your desire, there will be an information page up soon about wrestling training in an area near you! Feel free to browse this site and post on the OOC boards accordingly. We are currently recruiting wrestlers.
