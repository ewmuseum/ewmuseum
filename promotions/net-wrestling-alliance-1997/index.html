Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Net Wrestling Alliance opened with its own recruitment post on 1 April 1997, pitched plainly against the direction the rest of the newsgroup was heading: “Are you tired of every new league being 'Hardcore?' Are you sick of winning matches with a chair instead of a figure four or a powerbomb?” It billed itself a booked league where roleplaying decided outcomes, open to real and invented wrestlers alike in stables of up to four, running a Wednesday card and a Saturday card its own ads compared to Nitro. Its own president made a point of running without a website at all: “I don't waste my time on a Web Page. I spend that valuable time making the league as exciting and as high quality as I can.”

By late April it described itself as “up and running for a little over a month,” with “a great core group of wrestlers” and feuds already underway, and announced its first pay-per-view for 1 May 1997. A community awards ballot elsewhere on the newsgroup independently credited that first supercard with two Best Supercard votes. A separate handler's own World Heavyweight Championship signature block ran across six of the fed's posts between May and August 1997, though no title match itself survives in the record.

Its own last surviving post, 11 May 1997, still describes the league as actively expanding, still recruiting through an application asking for a wrestler's strengths, weaknesses, and background. No closing notice survives to say when or how it stopped.
