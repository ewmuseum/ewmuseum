Samantha Hall: And his opponent, from Miami, Florida. At 7'2" and weighing in at 367 lbs, The A-BOMB!

Steve McClelland: Another classic battle between two veterans about to get underway. (Brutus Granowski calls for the bell as A-Bomb and Rizzo circle the ring. They lock up and A-Bomb sends Rizzo flying into the corner.

2... Rizzo kicks out.) Dennis O'Leary: Probably a little too early to expect to get a pinfall off a competitor like Rizzo, but thus far, this has been all A-Bomb.

Dennis O'Leary: Just when everyone had Rizzo counted out, here he comes... with a vengeance!

Samantha Hall: The winner of this bout, RIZZO! (Crowd boos: *******) (The screen goes back to the announcers' table.) Steve McClelland: What grit and determination shown here tonight by Rizzo. Dennis O'Leary: And with a win like that, Rizzo has to be considered one of the front runners to win himself a belt here in the CIWF.
