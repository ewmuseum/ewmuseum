With fireworks going off above the ring and the crowd popping bigtime, the camera cuts to the commentator's table.] BD: It's RESURRECTION time folks!! Welcome to the pay-per-view......welcome to AeWF's big night!! But we're not going to talk on and on about it......tonight is all about wrestling at its finest. Quite a match-up, and it looks like we’re ready to get it underway.
