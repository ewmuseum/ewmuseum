AEWF Info

Dec 10, 2003, 12:00:00 AM
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
1 INTRODUCTION The AEWF is an e-wrestling federation. Established by Reginald Humphries ([name withheld]). Roland has been e-wrestling since 1996 and ran the successful European E-Wrestling Federation from April 97 to January 98 when he had to shut it down because of lack of V.P's.
Roland re-opened the EEF under the AEWF name, but he soon had to leave, leaving [name withheld] in charge. Though Frank did a great job, a lack of support forced him to quit and give [name withheld] (me) the reins. I only lasted a month before the toll of running the fed single-handedly took its toll on me. I have decided to re-open the AEWF bigger, brighter and better, as I find myself with ample time during my working hours and also with better organisation it will hopefully last a lot longer than before. The AEWF is based on the same system that made the EEF a success. Created to provide a safe haven for good roleplayers all over the world who's primary interest isn't winning but to create good storylines, angles and find other people to roleplay against and have FUN with. The setting for the game is
Europe. The reason for this is to give handlers something new and different to what's out there at the moment, and to give players an extra challenge when roleplaying (e.g it's not easy for an American to be accepted as a fan favourite in certain places, such as France, etc.). Most wrestlers will have to be European, so bear that in mind when creating a character. We also want to create a good roleplaying atmosphere, that's the other reason we wanted to create a regional federation. We won't have any World Titles (we're a European
Federation), only European regional titles. We have also created a fictional history of the federation that the players can use in the game.
It has taken me this many years to reopen the AEWF because I wanted to wait until I was absolutely sure I had not only the motivation to undertake such a task but also the time to do it. I now have both and am looking forward to seeing this fed succeed.
1.1 MISSION STATEMENT OK this isn't the longest mission statement nor the best written one. What I really want to emphasise is that I will always try to remain as impartial and fair as possible. There is nothing to be gained by me not being so as handlers will soon leave if I start playing favourites. Handlers who work the hardest will be the ones rewarded.
Case closed. I firmly believe that the handlers make the federation. I am NOT your boss, the fed head and the fed itself merely serve as a forum for handlers to enjoy themselves, and lastly THAT is the main point, I will strive to make this game as fun to everyone as possible.
2: AEWF INFO - The AEWF is a BOOKED league, meaning that I alone decide who wins the matches by way of who deserves it most. I will always endeavour to be fair and as impartial as possible when choosing the winners. If
it's an AEWF Superstar/Tagteam Vs. AEWF Superstar/Tagteam match. The votes are based on three (3) things. * Participation. Being involved by either getting in an angle, sending out flashes or roleplays or by developing your character, etc The main way to participate is by creating angles. Creating angles is the most important thing in the AEWF. Angles are storylines you create with other handlers'
wrestlers or by yourself. If there's an angle going, perhaps we'll let you lose a match only to build up heat for your next match where you will win. Angles are what makes e-wrestling fun, SO MAKE ONE UP!!
*Flashing. Probably the most obvious way to show that you're participating is by flashing. Flashes are to be sent to The Touring
Main Event. Flashes should be sent by Thursday midnight, Beijing Time.
* The match strategy should be sent in before the match (which includes sneak attack strategy, bribing a ref, etc). This is an account of how your character will approach his match, or if it is a sneak attack, how he will interfere, etc, etc. Strats should be sent in before Thursday midnight, Beijing Time.
You get the picture. I (Waz) am the only one who decides the outcome of the matches. Simple, eh? 2.1 HANDLERS Handlers are allowed one single wrester or one tagteam unless certain circumstances exist. This rule exists to promote interaction among the players, which is highly encouraged and rewarded.
2.2 CREATING A WRESTLER As the AEWF is a European fed we're looking primarily for European wrestlers but some other wrestlers will be accepted to add some international flavor to the federation.
Name The AEWF is an original wrestlers only fed. No real wrestlers will be accepted. Ok if you name your wrestler Desmond "The Hitman" Harris he will be accepted. But if he starts acting and talking like Bret Hart he wont be accepted... never has, never will be.
Age You decide, however no wrestler younger than 18 will be allowed to wrestle (legal reasons), and if you create a wrestler older than 50 expect him to be a bit saggy and slow in his matches so take that in account when you're writing the strategies.
Height and weight. The height and weight limits for wrestlers are: Height - no taller than 7'3 , no shorter than 5'0 . Weight- No heavier than 450 pounds, no lighter than 150 pounds. The lower limitations exist because we try to make the fed "realistic" and as it wouldn't be realistic that a 120 pound wrestler would beat a 300+ wrestler they won't be accepted. The higher limitations exist because I personally think it's pretty boring and unfair to the other wrestlers if a handler should create a 8'0 700 pound wrestler. 7'0 foot wrestlers who are highfliers will also not be accepted. That's that: Wrestlers like this won't be accepted. It will also depend on what kind of wrestlers there are currently on the roster. if there are already 3-4 guys who are over 7'0 then I won't be allowing anymore. I would like a little variety.
Alignment. Decide if you're a good guy or a bad guy or neither. Heel Face or
Neutral it's up to you! Make sure that if you create a face he acts the part and vice versa for heels and 'tweeners
Entrance Music In the form of title and Artist or Composer. Try to be creative. I don't want themes like "Mr.Perfects" theme etc. Once again originality, originality!
Ring Entrance Write your own wrestlers' entrance, complete with Ring Announcer
Intro, plus describe what he's wearing, etc.
Appearance How he looks. I want a detailed description Describe him as you would to a blind man. Because your opponents can't see your wrestler unless we are able to describe him. Personality His background story. What makes him interesting? Why would you yourself like to watch him? Make sure that when you sign up there isn't already a wrestler similar to your character in the roster. I don't want 20 pretty boy wrestlers.
Strengths & Weaknesses What would you say are the strengths and weaknesses of your characters? Write them down here. Make sure that you are consistent with your character when noting these down. A 150LBS 5'10 character will have a hard time making me believe that power is one of his strengths. Also note that you must list the same number of weaknesses and strengths.
Type of wrestler What is your wrestler? A matwrestling expert or a top-notch brawler? We want to know. Common moves. What are his favorite moves? List them so we can get to know your wrestler better and by doing so, be able to write better matches with him. Finisher If it's an existing move just write it else we want a detailed explanation. Foreign object Does he use one? If he does what and how. Manager Does he have a manager? Manager description Self explanatory. 2.3 CONTRACTS Most feds don't have this feature but to make it as realistic as possible and to add another dimension to this game I have decided to include it. You can add options and clauses of your choosing, e.g you can add the clause that you MUST get a title match within the contract length (contract length differs from 1-6 months), that you want your very own locker room, etc, you think of one, but beware that the prez can also make his own clauses which can counter the above one with a clause that the handler must fulfill his contractual obligations satisfactorily, i.e roleplay consistently and well, show that he's participating, etc. As a side note you can never include the clause that will guarantee you a title within that contract length. That is not on.
2.4 JOBBERMATCHES This is something every "real" fed have. It's a way to let the superstars shine! So we have them too. Every once in a while, you will be required to wrestle a jobber. When you do, all you have to send me a written match. Don't worry the match doesn't have to be a classic or long, it is up to you. I will ask individual handlers to write one every now and then. You can use one of the AEWF jobbers or create a jobber yourself. Just make him
original, no real wrestler. See below on how to write a jobber match. 2.5 OUR CARDS Currently the AEWF has 1 card per week. The Touring Main Event (TME): The crown jewel in AEWF programming (the only jewel actually at the moment), coming from different arenas all over Europe, pitting the best superstars against each other. Exactly where will be listed on the schedule announcement every week so you'll know where your wrestler will be. The TME comes "live" at the moment however matches WILL remain short (anything from 5K to 10K, hey we are a relatively small fed). Also, keep in mind if a match does happen to finish quickly it doesn't necessarily reflect what I truly feel about your character. The reason for the shorter matches is so that I and the fed can concentrate on the most important aspect of e-wrestling which is that of building storylines and keeping the whole thing rolling smoothly and consistently, as well as providing the matches in
PBP format. Plus I find a wrestler being able to withstand 3 powerbombs, 4 superplexes, 6 piledrivers, plus the opponents finishing maneuver to be silly. The TME features 2-3 jobbermatches, 5-6
superstar vs superstar matches as well as the special Interview segment of "Rapping with the Rock" with Luke Anthony. Beware Luke takes no prisoners with his in-your-face style of interviewing. Keep in mind that depending on the location of the TME the fans will respond differently to your character. For example, in Mediterranean
Europe the flamboyant wrestlers will be preferred as opposed to
Eastern Europe where the tough-no nonsense brawlers are the crowd favourites. To get interviewed on the TME contact the interviewer and see if he's interested. 2.6 PPV'S The AEWF will have PPV's whenever the prez sees fit, which may not be often. There are only two confirmed yearly PPVs and they are "The
Grand European." and the Invitational Munich Cup, an 8 men one-night tournament. No details on their date, however, have been confirmed.
3.1 FLASHES This is one of the most important things if you want to be successful in the AEWF. Being descriptive, being true to your character, developing storylines, and writing flashes will get you far. As in real life swearing will be edited out of the flash as will "smart"
comments. With smart comments we mean using words like jobbers etc. that real wrestlers don't use. I hold the right to not air flashes as
I see fit. Handlers are required to roleplay for the Touring Main Event. Flashes must be short and to the point, just like backstage segments in Raw, etc. They can be promos, prematch interviews, vignettes, etc. Use your imagination.
The handlers send in the flashes to me with the subject heading [AEWF
TME] YOUR WRESTLER'S NAME.
*Important* No scenes of child abuse, sexual abuse, sexual assault, rape, or racism will be accepted. This is not a warning rule.. one time and you are
OUT.... We don't promote anything illegal.
4.0 RANKINGS AND TITLES The AEWF has 2 Titles as of now. The AEWF European Heavyweight Title This title is the most coveted and highest ranked title in our fed. To become the European Heavyweight Champion is the greatest achievement you can m
ke in the AEWF. I don't want to add anymore singles titles because I don't want to make them the be all end all of every single handler, plus it will add to the sense of achievement one gets if/when they win a title, but of course, that could change if I see that this system isn't working or if enough handlers want another one. Also, rankings will come out every week after each TME.
The AEWF Tag Team Title The title all the tag teams are competing for. 4.1 HOW THE TITLES ARE DEFENDED The AEWF European Heavyweight title, once decided, must be defended at least once a month and on every PPV or Supercard against an opponent ranked high enough
to challenge for it. The AEWF Tag team Titles, once decided, must be defended at least once a month and on every PPV or Supercard against a team ranked high enough to challenge for it.
*Contenders for the European Heavyweight title are those ranked 5 or higher. *Contenders for the Tag Team Title are those ranked 5 or higher. Champions can of course give wrestlers not ranked high enough a shot at the title(s) but that wont count as one of the mandatory title defenses. You are also allowed to compete in non-title matches but should you lose such a match the wrestler who beats you will be named the number one contender for your title and you must face him or the team in your next title defense.
If a wrestler is 1/2 of the AEWF Tag Team Champions, they may ALSO hold a singles title at the same time. (He would be busy though.)
If a champion(s) fails to defend their titles during the time limit of the mandatory defence, the title(s) will be vacated by the AEWF board of directors who will decide how the title will be decided.
The AEWF Champions themselves will have MOST of the say in who they defend their title against as long as they follow the guidelines.
4.2 RANKINGS Rankings are mostly decided on a subjective basis. Your win/loss record will still be shown next to your wrestler but his place in the rankings will be decided by me. Again, I will try to remain as impartial as possible when doing this.
The Tag team wrestlers are a special case in the rankings, however. Whenever a tag team wrestler competes in single action, the outcome is based in the single rankings. If he a tag team wrestler fares well in single competition he can be a contender in both divisions. Basically, what he does in each division is totally independent of the other.
5. JOBBERMATCHES Ok below is how to write a jobber match. Jobbermatches are used at the beinning for all newcomers to the fed just so that I know you are committed to being active here. Jobbermatches don't have to be very long or detailed, it is up to you as you will be writing them yourself. An example of a jobbermatch can be seen below:
RF: OK so let's get going with our next match-up... FH: Introducing first...hailing from Stockholm, Sweden...weighing in at 320lbs...THOMAS..."THE ELK"...MAGNUSSON!!!
["The Elk", clad in his usual brown tights and matching brown boots, walks past the curtains arms raised. There is a small crowd pop for the big man from Sweden]
RF: This is a big man with tree trunks for legs! ["The Elk" takes a few steps down the aisle] CRASH RF: OH MY GOD!!! FROM BEHIND "THE HEDONIST" HERNAN IGLESIAS HITS "THE ELK" WITH A CHAIR!!! [Crowd boos as Hernan Iglesias stands over his fallen opponent] CRASH CRASH CRASH RF: Three heavy blows right on the back of the head with that steel chair and I think "The Elk" is done for!
HF: And this match hasn't even begun yet! Great innovative wrestling by "The Hedonist" [Iglesias throws "The Elk" by the hair into the ring. The referee orders for the bell to indicate the start of the match
DING-DING-DING Iglesias stomps on a prone Magnusson a couple of times and then goes to the top rope] RF: He hits this one, the match is as good as over! [Crowd oohs] RF: FLYING LEG DROP!!! WHAT IMPACT!!!: HF: We're having elk a la casserole tonight! [ONE TWO THREE!!! DING-DING-DING] FH: The winner of the match…"THE HEDONIST"...HERNAN…IGLESIAS!!! [Crowd boo as the ref raises Iglesias' arm] That's it, not too lengthy considering there is no need to send in a strat during the week you have a jobbermatch on.
6. STAFF MEMBERS Broadcasting team Some of the most familiar voices and faces of the AEWF are the hardworking broadcasting staff. On this page you can get to know them a little bit better.
Reporters These brave people work around the clock to get comments from the wrestlers. They do in-depth interviews for all the shows, follow the wrestlers into their dressing room, etc.
Billy "the Kid" Kidman Big, bold, beautiful... and dangerous. Billy has been in the sport for years, rubbing elbows with all the men. And most of the men instantly fell for her hot looks.
But then again, who wouldn't. Billie has always associated with the dark side of wrestling, those who are heels, as she finds them more attractive. Look for her to take up managing as well.
Malissa Chance Malissa is a black, english woman in her late thirties. Has a wide background in broadcasting and sports journalism. Has joined the AEWF as a commentator/interviewer.
Malissa is a no-nonsense, straight-shooting woman who calls things as she sees them. She does, however, have a slight face bias, which comes out every now and then.
Tommy Song Born in Hong Kong in 1973 but came to Britain at an early age. Grew up loving wrestling so it was only natural he went to work for Giant
Sports after he graduated from Journalism school. Tommy has a genuine love for the sport but can sometimes be intimidated by the wrestlers.
The AEWF Touring Main Event commentators Rick Fuji Born in Los Angeles, California. Fuji has been a wrestling fan all his life. He especially loves the technical side of the game and can name virtually every hold that was ever executed. He is young and inexperienced and the EEF was his big break. But as he performed well he got his contract renewed when it was time to open the AEWF.
He is very excitable and will often behave more like a fan watching a match than an impartial announcer, but he is going to work on that.
"The Eurocrat" Hans Van Der Fluyt Formerly a jobber at the old EEF "The Eurocrat" somehow managed to scam his way into the TME's commentating team. Comes to the broadcasting table dressed in a grey suite. Has short brown hair and a mustache and a very arrogant smile. The fans hate him as he tries to portray the typical European Union Bureaucrat. He likes to argue with
Fuji telling him about new EU rules and regulations. He will mostly show a strong dislike for foreign wrestlers (especially Americans) but he is not above criticizing European wrestlers as well.
Fedheads Here are the leaders of the AEWF, here you can find out who is doing what. The owner Edward Beaumont The eccentric English millionnaire Edward Beaumont made his millions by running a successful and profitable clothing company which he inherited from his father. Edward Beaumont always had a love for wrestling and decided to use some of his fortunes to open the Royal
Wrestling Promotion in 1977. The Federation ran successfully for a number of years until Edward decided to retire from the wrestling game in 1990. Edward stayed happily retired until now. Seeing the decline of the AEWF, a federation built by his good friends Reginald and
Robert Humphries, Edward bought it and pledged himself to rebuild its past glory.
The Board of directors AEWF President Edward Beaumont See above Vice-President Piotr Galievich Meet Piotr Galievich. A fourty year old Ukrainian, balding, with something of a pot belly. His English is excellent, and was originally hired to truly expand the EEF into a European Federation. He is rather skillful at arranging matches, but he tends to lose his temper when questioned. His position with the AEWF will be the same. Acts in the best interests of the Fed, but has high standards which he expects to be upheld. Will hand out suspensions to anyone who crosses the line.
Other Staff members Ring announcer Frank Heineman Frank, a gentleman in his fifties is the official Ring Announcer for most AEWF events. He used to work for the old AEWF, has lots of experience and is a real wrestling fanatic.
The AEWF Medical team Dr. Paul Chrunak This Swiss doctor in his mid forties is head of the AEWF medical team. If you get hurt, he will be on the job. He is also responsible for the rigorous drug testing program the AEWF has to make sure no illegal substances are used by the AEWF superstars.
The AEWF Security team Security is very important in every wrestling event. They are the ones who make sure the fans don't attack the hated heels. They are also trying to prevent wrestlers from attacking each other.
Head of security Ivan Smolecki This former AEWF European Heavyweight champion is now in charge of the security team. Even though it was a while since he wrestled he's still in good shape.
Public Relations, Spokesperson Richard Tomlin A long time AEWF "closet" fan. Kept his love of wrestling a secret for fear of ridicule from peers. Had a strict upbringing and his parents wanted him to excel academically and he did. He gained a degree in
Communication and Media Relations and the first thing he did was apply for a job at Giant Towers. He is now employed full-time by the AEWF and handles all major announcements and press releases by the
Federation. Internet Correspondent, "Hunchback" Bob No one knows too much about "Hunchback" Bob only that his comments are sometimes very much un-PC and he has gotten on the wrong side of the
Women's Liberation Movement, The girl guides, The Socialist Workers'
Union, the IRA, the Italian Mob and the French Government. Read his comments and opinions every Tuesday after the TME.
Referees Here are the men making sure the matches follow the rules. It might be a good idea to study the referees closely as they are each a bit different.
Jonathan Small This American referee may be tiny, but he calls it right down the middle, he's fair, he goes by the rules. He is the referee who takes care of most of the Main Event matches.
Mark Henderson This young referee, tries to do his best at all times. But lets face it, he isn't very experienced and sometimes he misses a handful of tights. Perhaps he will learn as he gains more experience.
Tony Falcotta This Italian referee has been involved in several controversial matches. A lot of people have raised a lot of questions about his integrity but nobody has been able to prove that he takes bribes, at least not yet.
Harvey Smith Another young referee. Got his big break with the EEF when Jonathan
Smalls was out of action due to an injury. Goes by the rules but sometimes loses his temper and becomes frustrated resulting in DQ's of wrestlers he doesnt like.
Michael Addul Born in Libya Addul is one of the most courageous referees in the federation. He doesn't care how big or intimidating the wrestlers are. If he sees it he calls it. However his weak side is that due to his temper he can get caught up with arguing with wrestlers and managers, something that can cause him to miss things, especially in tag team matches.
7. CONTACTS You may contact the other wrestlers in the AEWF to get to know them, set up teams, stables, feuds, etc. Make sure that once you set something up, that both (or all) of the parties confirm it with me so
I know. There is a mailing list of the current AEWF Handlers that goes out every once in a while through the mailing list, so you know how to contact everyone. If you want to contact someone via the President just write who the message is for, then I'll see to it that it gets to the right person. If the message is in the form of a roleplay they will be treated as such.
Well I guess that's it! If you have any questions mail the Prez at
[detail withheld] and he will explain it to you! Well what are you waiting for? Go on and sign an application! The AEWF wants you!
