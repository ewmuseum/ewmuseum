Vicious Circle Tonight — 27 November 1997

27 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Mike King: Fans, we've got a real slobbernocker coming up. Floyd Malloy will face Sean Waltman in another one of their neverending battles. These guys have fought a few times and have wrestled to draws. It'll be interesting to see which one cracks the win column today. Jack Mama: And now, we're joined by the triple-crown winner and my little brother, Joe Mama! Joe Mama: 'sup peoples? I wanna say hello ta my friend Snake Eyes. D'ya have anything ta hold up yer pants now? Bwaaaaa haaaaa haaaaaa haaaaa! Mike King: Let me congradulate you, Mr. Mama, on being the first AEWF superstar to have held all the singles belt, except the King of the Air. Joe Mama: Yeah, well I'm thinkin' about tryin' ta take that belt, too. Which one o' them Snakes has it? Snake Eyes? Python? Cobra? Who cares, they all suck. Mike King: Sean Waltman is in the ring now, waiting for his opponent. He's glaring at you, Joe. I think he wants a microphone. (ring announcer gives the mike to Sean Waltman) PESW: Joe Mama, if you ever say anything about my wife again, I swear I'll kick your teeth in! Joe Mama: Hey, wussie, if yer feelin' froggy, jump! Mike King: Floyd Malloy has taken this opportunity to sneak into the ring behind Waltman and drop kick him! Waltman goes flying into the turnbuckle. Malloy grabs Waltman's arm and puts him into an arm bar. He's yanking on Waltman's arm. It seems that Inga Waltman has come to see her husband wrestle. I must say, she is an attractive young lady. Joe Mama: She makes attractive noises in bed, too! Bwaaa haaaa haaaa haaa! Mike King: Malloy whips Waltman into the ropes. Waltman clotheslines Malloy! Waltman rebounds and drops a knee to Malloy's head. Malloy is dizzy. Waltman picks him up by the hair and slams him down onto the mat! Waltman i a man posessed! Jack Mama: You would be too, if your manhood was insulted. Mike King. Waltman puts Malloy on the top rope...Superplex! Waltman goes for the pin 1...2...Malloy kicks out. Waltman goes down to pick malloy up again. Malloy punches Waltman in the head. Malloy picks himself up. Superkick to Waltman's head. Now, he puts Waltman on the top rope. What is he doing. He's heading outside the ring. Oh, no! Malloy executes a belly-to-back suplex on Waltman from the top rope to the arena floor. Neither man is moving. Joe Mama: C'mon Floyd! Get up! Get up now!!! Mike King: Inga Waltman jumps over the rails and helps Sean Waltman up. Wait! here comes that strange japanese woman again. She shoves Inga Waltman. Inga takes a swing at the woman. The woman is laughing. Inga is trying to get to the lady, but she is easily avoiding the advances. Sean Waltman has made his way back into the ring. He's holding his ribs. Waltman climbs onto the top rope and executes a springboard plancha on Floyd Malloy. That move took what was left of Sean Waltman's energy. he's bleeding from the mouth. Malloy pushes Waltman off of him and attempts to crawl into the ring. The ref is counting 5...6...Malloy gets back into the ring. The asian woman has left Inga Waltman alone, so Inga helps Sean Waltman back into the ring. Neither man can move! Jack Mama: I don't think any man has any energy left. All Floyd has to do is fall on Waltman, and he wins! Mike King: Malloy and Waltman have pulled themselves to their feet. They both rush at each other. Malloy ducks a Waltman kick and hits waltman in the Family Jewels. Malloy senses blood. He picks Waltman up and executes the Bastard Drop. Malloy rushes for the ropes, but Inga Waltman hits him in the back with a chair. Floyd is on the mat screaming in pain. His ribs are banged up, too. The referee issues a standing count 1...2...3...4..5..6..Waltman is trying to get to the ropes...7...8...Malloy is in pain....9...10! This bout is a double DQ. The Malloy-Waltman feud takes another bizarre turn. Joe Mama: This is bullshit! (Joe Mama leaves the broadcast table and enters the ring.) Mike King: Joe Mama is arguing with the referee. Obviously the official didn't see Inga Waltman attack Floyd Malloy. Joe Mama is disgusted and throws the ref out of the ring. Inga Waltman has the chair and comes into the ring. She's sneaking up behind Joe Mama. She hits him with the chair!!! Inga Waltman just knocked Joe Mama down with a steel chair. Inga goes over to help her husband up. Joe Mama is shaking off the effects of the steel chair. Jack Mama: Joe looks perturbed. He's unreasonable when he's perturbed. Mike King. Floyd is getting up ,too. Joe runs over and kicks Waltman in the ribs. He picks Waltman up and performs a backbreaker. Waltman doubles over in pain. Waitaminute...The snakes and the Evangelist are running twords the ring! Now Joe Mama is going to have to pay! Oh, no. Joe Mama just grabbed Inga Waltman around the neck. He picked her up. He has a mic in his hand. Joe Mama: One more step and Waltman's bitch gets Sucka Crushed. Mike King: This is dispicable. Joe Mama is using Inga Waltman as a shield. Floyd Malloy runs over to Joe Mama and grabs the mic out of his hand. Floyd Malloy: Joe! Stop it! This disagreement is between the Vicious Circle and the Snakes. Not Inga Waltman. Please, let go. Mike King: I've never seen Joe Mama so angry before. He looks over at Sean Waltman, who is trying to get up. Mama throws Inga Waltman into Sean Waltman. Joe Mama: OK, Snakes. Take yer fruity friend and his trashy broad and get outta here! Tha only reason I'm lettin' ya go is 'cause Floyd got all sentimental an' all. If Inga comes in this joint again, I'll let my bouncers treat her like tha slut she is! bwaaaaa haaaa haaaaa haaa! Mike King: Fans, I apologize for Joe Mama's actions. Now, we have a first on Vicious Circle Tonight. Joe Mama is putting his King of Extreme Crown up for grabs against a member of his own stable, Samael. Joe Mama is already in the ring with his microphone. It looks like Samael is over at the bar having a drink. Joe Mama: Well, it's time fer me ta do somethin' I hate doin'. Samael: I don't know, Joe. It depends on how hot and heavy the match is. Joe Mama: Fair enough. Mike King: The referee has just called for the bell, and the match is underway. Samael is standing in the middle of the ring, drinking his beer. Joe Mama still has his microphone. Samael is finally ready to start the match. Jack Mama: This should be a tough match. Both men are fierce competitors. Mike King: Joe Mama STILL has his mic. Joe Mama: OK, you ready, Samael! Ow! Ow! Leg cramp! Mike King. Joe Mama hits the mat, with his mic still in his hand. Samael covers him. The ref looks confused. Joe Mama is attempting to struggle...somewhat. Come on! We were expecting a real match. The Ref counts 1...2...3. Samael gets his King of Extreme crown back in a cheap excuse for a match. (Joe Mama gets up) Joe Mama: Samael, Put 'er there, pal! You won fair and square. Bwaaaa haaaa haaaa haaa Samael: Ha ha ha ha ha ha ha ha! Mike King: Here comes Karol and James. They don't look too pleased with the wrestlers. Maybe they'll make those two fight for real! (Karol and James climb into the ring) Karol: Joe Mama, what you just did was despicable. You have desecrated the King of Extreme title. That was supposed to be something that a wrestler wins after proving themselves in the ring. Joe Mama: Hey, beat it, Karol. I don't got time fer you and yer li'l diaper boy there. James: OK, Joe. I have a proposition for you. We'll let this decision stand, IF you defend the TV title tonight. And this time, you must fight. Joe Mama: Sure, but I get ta pick my opponent (James and Karol confer) Karol: That's acceptible. Joe Mama: Oh, Mon-STARR! (Mon-STARR comes out) Joe Mama: This is my opponent. Karol: That's fine. But this has to be a fight. A punch must be thrown. Or else, we'll strip you of ALL your titles! Joe Mama: Sure, no problem. (Karol and James return to their ringside seats) Mike King: The ref sounds the bell, and the match is under way. There must be at least a half ton of raw power in there, with Joe Mama and Mon-STARR. The two huddle up. Joe Mama chops Mon-STARR. Mon-STARR punches Joe Mama. Oh, no. Mama goes down. He's acting like he is knocked out Jack Mama: Well, Mon-STARR knew where to hit Joe. They've been friends for 5 years now, at least. Mike King: The ref counts1...2...3. Surprise. We have a new TV Champ. Mon-STARR. Karol looks mad enough to chew nails. He and James head back into the ring. James: What the hell was that? Joe Mama: Well, ya said a punch had ta be thrown. There was one thrown. I wuz even pinned by my buddy here. Karol: Why you...You just slapped all of the wrestlers in the ICWF in the face. Since titles mean nothing to the Vicious Circle, Mon-STARR will have to defend his title tonight against the Evangelist! Mike King: Whoa! That was a surprising development. Our next match looks as if it will be a Title match. Mon-STARR and the Evangelist are both making their debuts tonight. Jack Mama: This is unfair1 Both Joe and Mon-STARR fulfilled their contracts. That little twerp will be hearing from our lawyer! Mike King: Nevertheless, coming up next is Evangelist v. Mon-STARR for the TV title! -------------------==== Posted via Deja News ====----------------------- http://www.dejanews.com/ Search, Read, Post to Usenet Mike King: Welcome back, fans. Joe Mama has left the ring disgusted, while an angry Mon-STARR is arguing with the referee. Evangelist and his choir make their way to ring side. Mon-STARR turns around. He looks pissed off. Jack Mama: You would be too if you were being forced to defend your belt. A travesty of justice! Mike King. Evangelist quickly steps out of his robes and goes on the attack. He chops Mon-STARR. No effect. He kicks Mon-STARR. Mon-STARR looks at him, smiling. Mon-STARR Picks up Evangelist and tosses him into the turnbuckle. Jack Mama: If Evangelist has any hopes of winning this match, he needs to use what little speed he has. Mon-STARR comes into the turnbuckle for a big splash. Evangelist side steps it, and Mon-STARR misses. Evangelist is now on the offensive, pounding on Mon-STARR's midsection. Mon-STARR refuses to go down. Mon-STARR is trying to put the Evangelist through the mat! He reaches down again. This time Evangelist rolls him up into a small package. The ref counts 1...2...Mon-STARR Kicks out! Jack Mama: Mon-STARR's got this match right where he wants it. That was a desperation move by Evangelist. Mike King: I have to agree with Jack, there. The Evangelist hasn't been able to mount much offense against this behemoth. Evangelist is giving up a foot in height and almost 200 lbs in weight. Mon-STARR picks Evangelist up again...POWER BOMB! Evangelist looks spent. Mon-STARR is just toying with him Jack Mama: Come on. Pin Him! Mike King: Mon-STARR picks Evangelist up, whips him into the ropes...Big boot to the head! Mon-STARR is gloating. Evangelist is trying to get up, but Mon-STARR stomps on his back. Mon-STARR lifts Evangelist up by his neck...Choke Slam. Mon-STARR is showing off his entire repretoire of power moves on the helpless Evangelist. Jack Mama: Pin Him! Pin Him! Mike King: Now, Mon-STARR moves in to pin Evangelist. Mon-STARR Puts his foot in Evangelists chest. Woah! Evangelist just punched Mon-STARR below the belt. Mon-STARR doubles over. Evangelist picks himself up and manuevers himself behind Mon-STARR. Our new TV champ is the Evangelist, who, along with his choir, beats a hasty retreat. Wow! I thought Mon-STARR had it in the bag. What do you think, Jack. Jack Mama: Damn Karol Zielinski.
