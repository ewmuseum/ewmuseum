Hello, and welcome to this issue of the AeWF Newsletter. My name is [name withheld], and I am the editor of this newsletter. In this newsletter, I try to provide you with all the information you need to know about the AeWF. I hope you enjoy it. Without further adieu, let me get on with things. -------- **NEWS** Sunday night in the ECW Arena, "Pure Energy" Sean Waltman and Floyd Malloy defied all odds by winning the Tag Team Terror Tournament. With this victory, they are now they number one contenders to the AeWF Tag Team titles, currently held by Viper and Python. All night long, these two men were able to put their differences aside and win. At least, most people thought that they were putting their differences aside. However, Thursday at a house show in Dover, DE, they proved that they had already done that. Waltman left the Snakes and Malloy left the Vicious Circle, and they agreed to become full time partners, and vowed to win the AeWF World Tag Team titles from the Snakes. This bizarre turn of events just go to show how unpredictable the AeWF has become recently. In other news, Rune was introduced as the newest member of the Vicious Circle this past Saturday night at a house show in Baltimore. Joe Mama interfered in his tables and ladders match with Todd Borton, causing a DQ. Rune and Mama then beat Borton to a pulp, and Mama presented Rune with a Vicious Circle t-shirt. Rune replaces the now-departed Floyd Malloy. -------- **RUMORS** None ... you guys have to send some to me. Please? Pretty please?
