AeWF Newsletter — 4 January 1998

4 January 1998 (posted 10 January 1998)
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello, and welcome to the second issue of the AeWF Newsletter. My name is [name withheld], and I am the editor of this newsletter. In this newsletter, I try to provide you with all the information you need to know about the AeWF. I hope you enjoy it. Without further adieu, let me get on with things. --------- **NEWS** This past Friday night at a house show in Trenton, New Jersey, a new World champion was crowned. Joe Mama became a two-time AeWF World champion, pulling off a shocking victory over the Evangelist. The Evangelist was scheduled to defend his title against Alex Extreme, but Extreme failed to show. He sited an injury, suffered at the hands of John Smith, as his reasoning. Mama then demanded a rematch, which he got, and regained the title. The AeWF Anarchy title also changed hands this past week. On Thursday Night Whiplash, Moo Kame defeated Dreamwalker to win the belt. Kame's first defense is scheduled for January 6th in Atlantic City, where he and Dreamwalker will do battle in a rematch. Unfortunately for AeWF fans, the AeWF versus the World pay per view, previously scheduled for January 23rd, is being postponed until at least February. Hopefully, this will only give the event more time to become even better. ------------ **RUMORS** Succube, manager of the team known as Dark Side, was recently seen training in the ring. This could possibly be a precursor of things to come, as Succube may soon step through the ropes as a wrestler. This is not confirmed, however. A major superstar of the AeWF is planning on making quite an impact at the upcoming Anarchy in the UK II pay per view from Manchester, England. It seems that this wrestler hails from England, and feels that this would be the perfect place to make his presence known in a big way. For more informantion, stay tuned for AeWF's Anarchy in the UK II, coming soon. In a bizarre story, Whog Shrog was recently spotted screaming naked in a park, after what some are calling 'a strange celebration'. Sources say that he is scheduled to receive psychological counceling some time in the near future. Rumor also has it that Whog Shrog is working the independent circuit, tagging with an unknown partner. Recently, the American Patriot offered to form an alliance with Wardancer. Since then, Wardancer has been working out with a friend of the American Patriot's from GIW. There is no word as of yet, however, that Wardancer has accepted the American Patriot's proposal. ---------------- **CARD RESULTS** Zielenski Cup '98 first round matches - December 30, 1997 - "Playboy" Ronnie D beat the Evangelist - "Hardcore" Harley Robinson beat Rune - "Pure Energy" Sean Waltman beat Moo Kame - Vampyro beat Wardancer - Alex Extreme beat Samael - Jinsei Shakanuzi beat Mr. Excellence - John Smith beat Joe Mama - Rick Styles beat Professor Payne AeWF Thursday Night Whiplash - January 1, 1998 - AeWF Tag Team champions Viper & Python drew Dark Side - Whog Shrog beat Mon-STARR - The Dirty Pair beat AeWF World champion the Evangelist & AeWF Television champion Snake Eyes - Alex Extreme beat Samael - AeWF King of the Air "Pure Energy" Sean Waltman drew Wardancer - Moo Kame beat AeWF Anarchy champion Dreamwalker AeWF House Show from Trenton, NJ - January 2, 1998 - Vampyro beat Hibachi Hitman - The Specialists beat Lost & Forgotten - Rune beat Todd Borton in a falls count anywhere match - Cobra drew Wardancer - AeWF Television champion Snake Eyes beat the American Patriot - Joe Mama beat AeWF World champion the Evangelist ------------ **PREVIEWS** The Tag Team Terror Tournament (say that 5 times fast), which is scheduled to take place tonight at the ECW Arena in Philadelphia, has formed some unlikely partners. Pure Energy Sean Waltman & Floyd Malloy are teamed together, and they will battle Joe Mama & Snake Eyes. Who's to say that this won't turn into a Waltman/Snake Eyes versus Malloy/Mama brawl? Also scheduled to team, Moo Kame & Mon-STARR. These two have had a storied past, but they need to put it all aside if they want to win this tournament. Other interesting teams feature Alex Extreme & Wardancer, Samael & the Evangelist, Kuno & Balrog and Maggath & Ryoga. It seems as though the only logical paring in the tournament is that of the reigning tag team champions, Viper & Python. It should be an interesting tournament, and you won't want to miss it. Speaking of interesting tournaments, the second round of the Zielinski Cup is also scheduled to take place next Sunday night. Second round matches are: - "Playboy" Ronnie D v. "Hardcore" Harley Robinson - "Pure Energy" Sean Waltman v. Vampyro - Alex Extreme v. Jinsei Shakanuzi - John Smith v. Rick Styles ---------------- That's all for the this installment of the AeWF Newsletter. Send all comments, rumors, news, ideas, etc., to me at < [detail withheld] >.
