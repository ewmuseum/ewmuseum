AEWF Inside #01 — 20 June 1998

20 June 1998 (posted 21 June 1998)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The screen fades to black from commercials, and then changes to a picture of Shane Larson. He is holding the World title belt in his hands, and Robin is standing at right of him.] VOICE: "One man, probably the most unexpected one, won a hard fought 4 way dance for the World title, after almost winning a 20 men Scaffold battle royal and took the belt home. But he doesn't have the time to make a break, because hungry contenders want to see his belt on their shoulders. Even if he hasn't defended his belt yet, he will. At Sunday Anarchy. His opponent: the man who was probably the most screwed by Alex Extreme. The man who _BEAT_ Alex Extreme on Resurrection to win the title. The man who got stripped from his title only minutes after his long awaited man. The King. The king of Snakes. KING SNAKE!!! [A fast clip shows King Snake in some of his best matches, pinning some of the most well known wrestlers in the AEWF. The camera returns to the picture of Larson and Robin.] However, this match will be King Snake's last match in the AEWF. Win or not, he announced his retirement some weeks ago. What will happen? Will he prove in his last match that he's the man? Or will he be number one victim of the new king? Find out, this Sunday!" [With these words, the picture explodes in thousands of pieces and the following logo appears on the screen.] _ _____ _____ /_\ | __\ \ / / __| / _ \| _| \ \/\/ /| _| /_/ \_\___| \_/\_/ |_| ### # # # #### # ##### ###### # ## # # # # # # # # # # #### # # # ##### # # # # # # # # # # # ## # # # # # # ### # # #### # ##### ###### June, 19th 1998, with Mike King, Bill Daniels and Sly Stephens [The screen changes after a couple of seconds to the shot of an AEWF studio. There is a large desk, with King, Stephens and Daniels sitting behind it, and there is a huge AEWF logo behind them, on the wall. All three commentators are wearing tuxedos with the AEWF logo on the right.] BD: "Welcome ladies and gentlemen to our first AEWF Inside show! We are here in the AEWF studios in Amsterdam, and we'll stay here for the next 45 minutes, and we hope that you will too!" SS: "Who cares about the fans Bill? We work hard, we commentate the shows for them and they never write us any letters nor do they send us anything." MK: "As I see, you don't get any letters. But I get many! And in tonight's show, we'll present the fans some of them. On another note: There are many letters addresses to us asking for matches, but they have to go to the president, not to us." BD: "Here's what we've prepared for you on tonight's show!" [The screen changes to a list of things which will be on tonight's show.] 01. Discussion about the last Sunday Anarchy card 02. Preview of the next televised show 03. Discussion and clips of the untelevised match of "The Machine" Chris K. and Da Yellow Guy [The camera shows the commentators again.] BD: "Well, let's start with "numero uno" - the last Sunday Anarchy card." Sunday Anarchy 14th June 1998 Al Rich and Randy Blackstone debuted on AEWF TV. 01. Wardancer and "The Perfect Speciman" Brock Carden fought to a double pin. Jinsei Shakanuzi gave an interview 02. The Fairchild Foundation defeated the Dark Side to retain their tag team belts. 03. Mr. Excellent pinned "Wildcat" Jim Lewis Jr. with help of Rich and Blackstone to win the King of the Air title. After the match, Rick, Randy and the new champ gave an interview declaring war to Lewis Jr. and everybody else. Simon "ThunderStryke" McQuaid helped Lewis Jr. when he was attacked by the new stable. The TV station which was showing the Sunday Anarchy card had problems and the rest of the matches couldn't happen. MK: "It was really disappointing to see that we couldn't show the whole card, but all matches which did not happen last Sunday will happen this Sunday." BD: "Let's talk about maybe the most important topic: The "NECW" stable." MK: "Yeah. Rich, an ex-NECW commentator came out on the show and began to say how good NECW was and how bad AEWF is. But he wasn't alone: Randy Blackstone, I believe a newcomer in the wrestling business, appeared at Rich's side. They insulted Lewis Jr. and all other AEWF superstars. I believe that they have forgotten about one thing: the AEWF was in very good terms with the NECW, and if not it's closure, we would have be the same federation now. I wonder what ex-NECW president has to say about this. We'll try to get an interview with him for you at the next Inside show." SS: "Finally somebody who recognizes that the NECW was the best fed in the World!" BD: "So why are you working for the AEWF?" SS: "Because the NECW doesn't exist anymore!" BD: "Anyway. Later this night, Mr. Excellent joined them. They are three now. They are still outnumbered, but I fear that this could change." MK: "That's true. Saturday they were 0, now they are 3. If there would be more guys joining them, the AEWF would be in trouble." BD: "But we'll not go under so quickly! We still have some great superstars who will fight them! I am speaking about men like Shane Larson, Wardancer or Jinsei Shakanuzi!" SS: "Speaking of Jinsei and Wardancer. They had a little brawl on International Invasion `98 in the battle royal and Jinsei challenged Wardancer to a match at one of our upcoming cards." MK: "This would be their second or third match, and from what I've seen in the 2 out of 3 falls match at Resurrection, it would be nothing else then 100 % high flying action." BD: "But things changed since then. They were friends, now they are enemies. It wouldn't surprise me if Jinsei would use illegal moves during their match, what wasn't the case at our last PPV." MK: "Speaking of PPVs. Extreme Chaos II, the continuation of AEWF's first pay per view Extreme Chaos will happen on July, 31st. No much matches were signed, but with the new stable NECW, I wouldn't be surprised if the main event would be changed." BD: "Let's focus on last Sunday Anarchy card, since we have still about one month till the PPV." MK: "That's true. But the 1st match of our last card brings us again to the next PPV, as Wardancer fought Brock Carden in a match where the winner would be in team "Anarchy" on the PPV. Since King Snake will retire on our next Sunday show, I wouldn't be surprised of both would fight for the honor of the AeWF." SS: "You are fined!" MK: "I don't care. Nobody's listening to Extreme anymore." SS: "That's all a mind game from his part. He will soon again be THE MAN in the AEWF." BD: "That's quite possible. With all powerful friends like Jinsei Shakanuzi or Aries "God of War", he shouldn't have any problems to keep control over the fed." MK: "We can't forget that if team "Anarchy" will win the main event at Extreme Chaos II, there will be no more AEWF." BD: "That's what makes this PPV so interesting. The future of the fed is on the line." MK: "There was also a tag team title match between the new champions - The Fairchild Foundation and the challengers: the Dark Side. The double-F had no problems winning this match, but we can't forget that the Dark Side had ALWAYS been the underestimated team. They have talent, and they'll prove it soon. I know it." SS: "Yeah! They finally have to win the tag team titles!" BD: "In the main event there was a match between the King of the Air champion - "Wildcat" Jim Lewis Jr. and Mr. Excellent, the winner of the lightweight battle royal." MK: "This is where the two guys appeared again and helped Mr. Excellent to win the match. Let's watch this again!" <<Tape begins>> [Wildcat balances on the top turnbuckle, poised to jump on Mr. Excellent and finally win the match. Suddenly the fans start making noise about something off screen.] MK: "What's this?" [The camera pans to show "Legendary" Al Rich running to the ring. He climbs up on the apron and yells at the referee, who spots him and immediately starts telling him to get away from the ring.] BD: "It's Al Rich! Earlier tonight we saw him make his first AEWF appearance, alongside his new protégé Randy Blackstone." SS: "What's he doing? He's got no involvement in this match!" [Rich is on the apron on the side away from the aisle way. The referee is admonishing him severely, but has his back turned to the aisle and the ring interior. Wildcat has paused from his jump momentarily, watching what is happening with Rich. Suddenly, down the aisle like a shot comes Randy Blackstone. He jumps up on the apron and slams against the ropes, sending Wildcat plummeting to the mat.] MK: "NO! Randy Blackstone just knocked Wildcat off the top on purpose!! Turn around ref!" BD: "This doesn't look good for the young Australian champion!" [Blackstone is on the apron, and he grabs Wildcat, as Mr. Excellent gets to his feet. Blackstone sets up Wildcat into a powerbomb/piledriver position through the ropes, and then hoists Wildcat up into the air. Mr. Excellent comes over and grabs Wildcat's back.] MK: "NOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!" [They spike-powerbomb Wildcat into the mat, as the fans are yelling at the ref to turn around. Blackstone then drops to the floor and rolls under the ring, as Al Rich points out Mr. Excellent draped across Wildcat to the referee.] BD: "The ref has seen the cover......ONE.....TWO.....THREE!!!" <<Tape ends>> MK: "As we've seen, Excellent and Blackstone spike power bombed our ex-champ and I don't know anyone who would kick out of this." BD: "The show ended with McQuaid helping Lewis Jr. Let's look at it, to have a better view on the current situation, for those who haven't seen our card." <<Tape begins>> [Blackstone and Excellent get Wildcat up and sit him on the top turnbuckle. With Rich urging them on, Blackstone gets up and superplexes Wildcat to the mat, and Excellent bounces off the ropes and nails him with a vicious leg drop.] MK: "This is appalling!! Get security out here! Isn't someone going to stop this?" BD: "I agree! Wildcat could be seriously hurt!" [All of a sudden the fans erupt, as the camera shows Simon McQuaid sprinting towards the ring with a metal chair in his hands. He slides into the ring, scattering the rule breakers, who are out onto the floor very quickly, for fear of getting chaired.] MK: "It's Simon "ThunderStryke" McQuaid!! At least someone had the guts to save Wildcat from permanent injury!!" [Rich, Blackstone and Excellent are in the aisle, backing off towards the locker rooms. Every now and then they turn and point at McQuaid and Wildcat, saying words which cannot be heard. The aisle fans jeer them until they disappear through the entrance.] <<Tape ends>> BD: "The three quickly disappeared, what shows us their cowardly character." MK: "I am happy to see that the main event is the match it is. Let's have a look at this Sunday's card." Sunday Anarchy 06-21-98 01. "Aries of God" vs. Wardancer in an International Title match 02. King Snake vs. Shane Larson in an World Title match 03. "Intrepid" Ryan Howard/"The Machine" Chris K. vs. Z-Team 04. Randy Blackstone/Mr. Excellent vs. "Wildcat" Jim Lewis Jr./Simon "ThunderStryke" McQuaid MK: "I was talking about the tag team match between Blackstone, Excellent, McQuaid and Lewis Jr. Lewis will finally get the chance for revenge!" BD: "I am really looking forward to the card. The Aries/Wardancer match should be really exciting, as both are REALLY good wrestlers, and nor Aries wants to lose his title, nor Wardancer wants anything more then a title around his waist." SS: "The card looks good - finally." MK: "I wonder who will win the tag team match between the Z-Team and the Machine and Intrepid." BD: "Me too. Well, I believe that we'll have to wait to find out!" MK: "Now, let us discuss the untelevised match between Chris K. and Da Yellow Guy. A fan taped it on a hand held and send it to us, so we'll be able to show you the end of the match." [Both men are exchanging punches in the middle of the ring, as Brock Carden makes his way to the ring. Chris K. sees him and executes a DDT on Da Yellow Guy, and puts him into a hammer lock, to allow Carden to attack DYG with a chair. However, as Brock swings the chair, DYG counters and Brock hits K., allowing DYG to pin him.] BD: "Wow that was really unexpected!" MK: "That's true! I really didn't thinked that DYG will win this one! His record is at 2-0 now, and I don't see anybody who would stop him!" SS: "I see many guys, but well, that's just my opinion, and nobody cares about it." MK & BD: "Right!" SS: "Ah, <BLEEP> you!" MK: "Well fans. Since somebody has stole us all tapes where we had pre taped interviews with the wrestlers, we have to finish this show now. I hope you all enjoyed the first issue of AEWF Inside and that you'll watch our next issue on Saturday." BD: "Wait Mike. I just got an information from a camera man that we have an interview for you, from Caitlin Fairchild." [The scene opens in the "trophy room" of Caitlin Fairchild's lavish home. The camera pans around to catch shots of Caitlin Fairchild with various celebrities. There are photographs of her with the late Phil Hartman, Jay Leno, Oprah Winfrey, and finally, Dr. Drew and Adam of MTV's "Loveline". The camera then pans around the room to catch shots of the various trophies and titles Caitlin has obtained through the accomplishments of Lobo Fraggus and Jonathan Witt, aka The Fairchild Foundation. There are the UEW Television Tag Team Titles... the MVW Tag Team Titles... and finally, a shot of the AEWF World Tag Team Titles. The camera then zooms in to the red velvet couch in the center of the room. Dressed down in blue jeans and a "Long Live The Queen of UEW" Caitlin Fairchild t-shirt, she crosses her legs and begins to address the camera.] CAITLIN - Life has been rather nice to me, so far. It seems like if I've taken the wrestling world by storm. In the short span of their almost eight month career, Jonathan and Lobo have captured three different sets of tag team championships. They've beat some of the best teams in the world today. And, they've had fun kicking some behind. I, too, have had a wonderful time inflicting pain on those who cross my path. As a whole, we love beating the crap out of everyone who oppose me and my boys. Speaking of opposing my boys, I've heard rumors that the winners of the upcoming match between the team of Down and Jinsei Shakanuzi and the team of Ryan Howard and Chris K. will receive a shot at my boys' belts.... belts that they have held on two occasions, mind you... at an upcoming card. I'm gonna try to take this a step further. I _am_ known for taking it to the extreme. Why not have the winner of this tag team contest face my boys inside of a STEEL CAGE at the next AEWF pay-per-view extravaganza. I'm not talking about one of those wimpy "climb out of the cage and you're the winner" cage matches. I'm talking about a match... taking place in a CELL... where the winners of the match must make BOTH members of their opposing team submit. How does _that_ sound, boys and girls? Is there any team brave enough to step up to this kind of challenge? We soon shall see.... we soon shall see. As you all ponder about what I just said, remember this, children... whatever Caitlin wants, Caitlin gets. That, as they say, is the bottom line. [Fade to black...] MK: "Well, I believe that Caitlin is right when she says: "Whatever Caitlin wants, Caitlin gets", since he is now manager of two time AEWF World Tag Team Champions." BD: "Guys, a great challenge has been made and we will keep you informed if this match will happen. Anyway...for my commentator partners and myself, I say good bye and see ya tomorrow!"
