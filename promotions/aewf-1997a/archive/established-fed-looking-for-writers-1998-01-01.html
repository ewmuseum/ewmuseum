Established fed is looking for writers and wrestlers

1 January 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi everybody, my name is Karol Zielinski and I am the president of the AEWF. We are an established fed which has been existing since May this year. Currently we have two cards a week (a small and a big one) and three houseshows. We have more than 20 wrestlers, including a few tag teams, which are currently especially needed. We have the Anarchy title, the World title, a King of Extreme, a Television title and a King of the Air, as well as Tag Team Titles. If you are interested in the quality of our writing, just check out the first round of the Zielinski Cup which has been sent out yesterday. Of course the AEWF matches aren`t as long as these but they are also very well written :-) what you can check out, since we posted out Thursday Night Whiplash card tonight. What we need very much are writers which would be able to write one or two good matches a week. If you feel as if you`d be good enough for this job, just email us and we`ll give you a match to try. But of course we are still looking for wrestlers, ESPECIALLY tag teams. If you think that you can compete with the best, send us the application and a sample roleplay. You will be notified within hours whether we see you in our fed or not. Her`s an application form, just feel it out and send it to [detail withheld] : Your name: Wrestler`s name: Height: Weight: Age: Hometown: Wrestling style: Ring appearance: Gimmick (be descriptive): Face/Heel/Tweener: Ring Music: Titles Won in other feds: Finishing move: Divide 200 points in these categories: Work: Strength: Experience: Technical skill: Blading/Madness: Karol Zielinski AEWF President
