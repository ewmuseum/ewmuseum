Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Canadian Dominion Wrestling Federation announced itself on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 13 June 1995 as a coming e-wrestling promotion out of Dalhousie University, run by two student co-founders. It presented itself as a booked, tournament-driven fed: a four-title “Champion of the Ring” tournament, staged across two PPV-style broadcasts out of a fictional Toronto SkyDome, decided its early championship picture.

By 9 September 1995, its only surviving document — a recruitment post titled “Join the CDWF” — described the promotion as “already well underway with many of the greatest e-wrestling wrestlers there is,” and solicited new characters on a standard bio form: name, hometown, age, alignment, division, a manager, height capped at 7'6” and weight at 700 lbs, ring entrance and attire, tactics, favorite moves, strengths and weaknesses, a finishing move, and a point-buy split across strength, speed, agility, endurance and ring experience, and again across brawler, high flyer and technical styles.

The post was signed off by the fed's president under the nickname “Canadian Patriot,” who closed with a jab at the WWF's Dr. Isaac Yankeem gimmick and billed himself an “all around nice guy.” No further CDWF document survives in this collection, and how the “Champion of the Ring” tournament played out, or when the promotion closed, is not in the surviving record.
