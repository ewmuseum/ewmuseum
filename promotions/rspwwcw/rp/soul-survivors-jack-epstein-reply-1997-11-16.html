RSPWWCW Soul Survivors Part 2 (Jack Epstein's own reply), 16 November 1997

16 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>Pinzer: The winner of this Soul Survivors match, Raven, Mattie >Carrington, and Cactus Jack Epstein, RAVEN'S NEST!!! >Tony: Raven's Nest here with a 3-1 victory over The Four Horsemen. And >Epstein still with that Mandible Claw stuck in the mouth of the >Undertaker, who is spitting up rice or something. >Tenay: Undertaker foaming at the mouth, and every referee on hand is >trying to pry Epstein off of the Undertaker. I think clearly this >deranged Cactus Jack Epstein, has also got to be a force that the >Taskmistress will have to reckon with in the World Title Scene. Assuming >she gets by Hollyweb Hogan next Monday night. >Heenan: She's GOTTA beat HollyWeb Hogan! No way do I want to see that >creep be the RSPWWCW World Champion. LOL! I'm a contender to this piece of crap title, now? Wow. This RSPWCW shit is dumb as hell. Because of booking I have defeated 3 RSPW posters in a survivor series match. When does the excitement start? If I recall, I have the most total belts in RSPWWCW, and yet I've been pushed at midcard status... so to all of you "fake" wrestlers..... Go fuck yourself. Jack Epstein is back. "CACTUS" JACK EPSTEIN Rutgers fears Me
