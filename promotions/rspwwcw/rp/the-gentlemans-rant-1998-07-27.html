The Gentleman's Rant, 27 July 1998

27 July 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(slip into early 80's Bob Backlund mode) You know, I've only been in RSPWWCW a short time, but I've already seen the reasons why I was brought in. Our great commissioner, El Asso Wipo, called me out of retirement a few weeks ago and told me there was a problem here in his federation. He told me about the TWO, and he told me about the situations regarding improper handling of title changes. I doubted what he said, I doubted that it could be that severe, but I told him that as a favor to him, I would give him a hand. My first assignment here was to oversee the world's title match between [name withheld], the paragon of virtue, and Strength Rollins, then the Extreme Champion and #1 contender. All ran smoothly until the TWO decided to show their colors. A man calling himself Raven tried to use a stop sign to take out Mr. Benson. (replay of Benson superkicking the stop sign into Raven plays in background) Before I could stop things, Mr. Benson, respectable person that he is, took care of the problem himself. He pinned Mr. Rollins, and I counted him out 1, 2, 3. Problem solved, for the moment. My next assignment, just last night, was the cruiserweight title match between Bishop Chad Bryant and Peter Panaro, the self-styled "King of Flame". Again, as the match was coming toward its conclusion, a stop sign came into play. The infamous Taskmistress, Liz Michael, attempted to interfere. (Replay of Proulx grabbing the stop sign from Liz Michael in background) Fortunately, I stopped her and Mr. Bryant defeated Mr. Panaro. Again, it was a clean pin, and I counted the 1, 2, 3. I had had the opportunity to view Mr. Bryant, along with his associate, Ms. Vaters, and thought they were upstanding members of the federation. Therefore, I thought it would be the beginning of a turnaround here in RSPWWCW. I was soon to be proven wrong. The final match of the evening was scheduled to be a four-way match for the world's title between Mr. Benson, Ms. Michael, John Henry DeJong and Dr. Tom Zylkin Lord Arden. I was in the back watching that match, and I'm sure I don't need to tell you how that turned out. (Replay of Liz Michael being hit with sceptre and then covered with John DeJong in background) It was, in short, an abomination. Jeff Jones, you are a disgrace to the profession of refereeing. What you allowed to happen last night was inexcusable. I plan to appeal to El Asso Wipo and see that you are suspended and stripped of your referee's jersey. After that, I want to see you in the ring. It's been a little while since I donned the tights and entered the squared circle as a wrestler, but I would like nothing better than to show you what it's like to be in the ring as a wrestler. Then maybe you won't be so willing to allow such things to slide. As for you, Chad Bryant and Kelly Vaters, I used to respect you. I used to think you were the elite of RSPWWCW. Now I know you're just as bad as the TWO. Mr. Bryant, you won the title because I stopped Ms. Michael's interference. I know that I can beat you the same way. Once I've taken care of Jeff Jones, you're next on my list. To the TWO, I'm serving notice. I've stopped your interference twice now. I'll do it again and keep doing it until you drag your sorry..._butts_ out of RSPWWCW. If any of you want a piece of me, I'm right here. Raven, Liz Michael, Peter Panaro, Strength Rollins, and the rest of you low-lifes won't find it quite so easy to run roughshod over this federation now that the Gentleman has arrived. From now on, things here in RSPWWCW are going to be done... The Gentleman's Way. Thank you. "Gentleman" John Proulx
