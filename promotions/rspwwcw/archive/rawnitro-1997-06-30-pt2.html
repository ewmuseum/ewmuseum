RawNitro, 30 June 1997, Part 2

30 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********************************** RSPWWCW RawNitro 6/30: Heure deux ********************************** Monsoon: Welcome back to RSPWWCW RawNitro. In the first half of the show we had the Taskmistress be our guest commentator, but for the second half we have someone else joining us. (Perfect symphony. Maritimer walks out) Ventura: He's the man who claims to be perfect. He hasn't proved it in the last couple of weeks though. (Maritimer puts on headset) Monsoon: Welcome Maritimer. Maritimer: Greetings and salutations, it's good to be on RawNitro, but not good in be in Glen Falls, NY. Monsoon: Why's that? Maritimer: Look at the crowd! They're all cross-eyed and drooling! Monsoon: Now that's uncalled for. Making fun of the fans is not... Ventura: Look at the fans, Monsoon. Monsoon: Well, okay. Maybe they ARE cross-eyed, but still... Maritimer: Anyway, isn't there supposed to be some wrestling? ************************************************************ RSPWWCW Extreme Bandwidth match: Dollywood vs. Alex Demchuk ************************************************************ (Chris Benoit theme) Duggan Fan: HOOOOOOO! This match is for the RSPWWCW Extreme Bandwidth Championship, tough guy! Making his way to the ring, another one of those dirty foreigners, the so-called "Crippler", ALEX DEMCHUK! (crowd boos) Maritimer: Alex could whoop Duggan Fan's ass, so he'd best shut up. ("Sweet Home Alabama") Duggan Fan: And his opponent, being accompanied to the ring by John Henry and Robert Wallace Eddy, he's the leader of the VWO, and the Extreme Bandwidth Champion, DOLLYWOOD ZYLKIN! (crowd cheers) Ventura: I think that Liz would disagree with Dollywood being called the leader of the VWO. Maritimer: Who cares? Next week at In Your House you'll have The Idiots vs. The Whores. I'm still figuring out which title goes to which team though... Monsoon: Oh will you stop! (Bell rings. They lock up, and Demchuk immediately scoops and slams Zylkin. Dollywood gets to his feet, and is slammed again.) Monsoon: Demchuk with a definite power advantage. Maritimer: What do you expect, he's a Canadian! (Henry jumps on the apron, and Demchuk slugs him. Eddy jumps on the apron, and is hit by Demchuk too.) Ventura: Demchuk is focused tonight! (Dollywood attacks Demchuk from behind and knocks him out of the ring.) Ventura: And now he's focused on the floor! Monsoon: Demchuk is out in no man's land, with Henry and Eddy out there. C'mon ref, do your job! (Henry and Eddy approach Demchuk, but he grabs a chair and starts swinging.) Ventura: Oh, now why is he doing that? He should be disqualified! Monsoon: It's legal, because it's outside of the ring. Ventura: Oh, I see. So if Henry brought a GUN to the ring and SHOT Demchuk, that would be legal? Maritimer: You better believe it! Monsoon: ??? What? Maritimer: Get back to the match, Monkey Bo -er- Monsoon. Monsoon: Now Demchuk is in the ring, WITH the chair! Maritimer: Batter up! (bell rings as Demchuk hits Dollywood with the chair) Ventura: Get out of there, Dollywood! Monsoon: And that's what he's doing. Let's get the offical word. Duggan Fan: Ladies and Duggans, the winner of the match as a result of a disqualification, DOLLYWOOD ZYLKIN! ("Sweet Home Alabama") Maritimer: Man, now that match was quicker than a blowjob by Liz, if what Duggan Fan told me earlier was true... Monsoon: I don't believe this! Maritimer: I don't believe your jacket! Farmers have more style! (As Dollywood/Henry/Eddy go back to the lockerroom, the big screen shows Liz/Kare/LaRiche) Liz: Aw, poor baby. You can't handle even ONE man, and you think you can beat THREE women? Maybe you ARE as dumb as you look, little boy. Next week at In Your House, you'll find out once and for all why WE make the decisions around here. (All three women laugh as the screen fades out.) Monsoon: What was that? Maritimer: Hey, didn't they have a 1-900 number on the bottom of the screen? **************** commercial break **************** Monsoon: Welcome back, everyone. Ventura: Hey Maritimer, is it true you have a match next week against Death? Maritimer: Yeah, it is. But I've gotta tell ya, I've seen death before, and I kicked him in the balls. Next week will be worse. ***************************************** "Heartburn Kid" Patrick Edmond vs. FunkyM ***************************************** Duggan Fan: Guess what, tough guys. We have another match, and it's between another two stinking foreigners from Canada! Maritimer: To Duggan Fan, you're a foreigner if your home state doesn't start with "New". (Flash Funk theme) Duggan Fan: Coming down the aisle, from Ontario, I think. He says he's too funky, but we all know he's not a tough-guy, FUNKYM! (crowd boos) Maritimer: Bang, bang, baby it's a crash, crash, crash! Monsoon: Do you like this guy, Maritimer? Maritimer: Oh, I see BIG things in this guy's future! ("I'm a Lumberjack" - Monty Python) Duggan Fan: And his opponent, from Quebeek, or Kabec, or something. He's the HEARTBURN KID, PATRICK EDMOND! (crowd boos) Monsoon: Edmond making his RSPWWCW debut tonight. Maritimer: The 1-Stu-3 Kid did as well. Ventura: Yeah, and look what happened. (Bell rings. They lock up, and FunkyM backs to the ropes. Edmond throws him off the ropes, and misses a clothesline, and FunkyM comes back with a spinning kick.) Monsoon: Nice kick by FunkyM. A cover, but only a count of 1. (FunkyM runs off the ropes and misses a legdrop. Edmond picks up FunkyM and starts hitting him with forearms. Hits FunkyM with a headbutt, but staggers.) Monsoon: That'll give you a negative attitude. Maritimer: So does a kick to the groin. But I digress... (FunkyM dropkicks Edmond, and again when Edmond gets up. He runs off the ropes, but is caught by Edmond and given a spike slam.) Ventura: Edmond has some power! Maritimer: I just hope he uses it for good, and not evil. Monsoon: A cover by Edmond, and does he get him? No, he doesn't. (Edmond gives FunkyM one, two, three elbowdrops.) Maritimer: I don't think Edmond is sure what to do right now. Monsoon: Edmond with another cover... Ventura: Foot on the ropes! Monsoon: The ref sees it, and the count is broken. (Edmond picks up FunkyM, and slams him in the middle of the ring. Goes to the rop turnbuckle.) Maritimer: Now what's he doing? Monsoon: Edmond jumps and... MISSES a flying legdrop. Maritimer: Capitalize on it, FunkyM! Ventura: Now FunkyM is going to the top, and hits a shooting star press! Monsoon: This could be it! 1...2...and a kickout! Ventura: Wow! Edmond has impressed me by taking that! (FunkyM gets Edmond up, and throws him to the turnbuckle. Edmond reverses the throw, but FunkyM leaps straight to the top turnbuckle and hits with a moonsault.) Maritimer: Now THAT'S a vertical leap! Monsoon: FunkyM with the cover, and he gets him! (Flash Funk theme) Duggan Fan: Your winner of the match, that rotten Canadian FUNKYM! Maritimer: FunkyM is rotten? Duggan Fan needs to smell his armpits. Pee-yew! Monsoon: Anyway, coming up next is the all important CAGE MATCH! **************** commercial break **************** Monsoon: Welcome back, everyone. Ventura: Hey Maritimer, what are you doing? Maritimer: I'm going to announce this match the way it SHOULD be done. Watch an expert folks, and take notes. There'll be a quiz afterward. ********** CAGE MATCH ********** (Maritimer, standing on his chair with the mic) Maritimer: Ladies and gentlemen... no wait, I forgot, we're in Glenn Falls. *Ahem* Douche-bags and retards... (crowd seems somewhat miffed at this) Maritimer: This contest is set for one fall, inside a STEEL CAGE! It will be for the title that I SHOULD be wearing today, the TAG TEAM TITLE! Now that you know that, let's bring out the contestants. (Baruch hymn) Maritimer: On their way to the ring, accompanied by Epstein, Amdur, Tiszinkel, Fadner, and Chanoch. They are the UNJUSTIFIED RSPWWCW Tag Team Champions, and have YET to give us a rematch... en4cer, Blakenstein, PERFECTION PERSONIFIED! Monsoon: That was a little biased, don't you think? Ventura: Well, it wasn't THAT bad... ("Because the Night" - Natalie Merchant) Maritimer: And their opponents, making their way to the ring area. They are accompanied by ATM LaRiche, DoMinAA, Sable, Count Chocula -er- Sean Tobin, and Dana Dangerously. One of them STILL owes me a title shot as well, but with her title defence record, I'll get it at Christmas. They are The DICKmistress and PowrSUCK Kare, the Creatures of the Cunts! Ventura: Call it a hunch, but I think the Maritimer is a bit resentful towards the creatures. Maritimer: Now, a quick check over the rules. Since this is a tag match, you can win either by pinning one of your opponents, or having BOTH members of your team exiting the cage. There is a roof over the cage, and only one door, so this should be something! NOW... go ahead and kill each other! Monsoon: Kare/Liz and Perf. Pers. are in the cage, with everyone else outside. The ref is locking the door, and HERE WE GO! (Bell rings. General chaos ensues.) Monsoon: The four individuals are fighting IN the ring, and everyone ELSE is fighting OUT of the ring! Ventura: That's what the cage is for, Monsoon! Maritimer: So how'd I do? Monsoon: You're no Howard Finkel, that's for sure. (Inside the ring: all four brawl, with Liz/en4cer in one corner, and Kare/Blakenstein in another. The guys throw the girls to each other, but Kare leapfrogs Liz, and they clothesline the guys. Outside the ring: LaRiche hits Amdur with a chair, Epstein piledrives Sean Tobin, DoMinAA kicks Chanoch in the crotch, Sable rakes Fadner's eyes, and Tiszinkel suplexes Dana.) Monsoon: All hell is breaking loose! Maritimer: What is this, Altantic Grand Prix? Ventura: Who? Maritimer: Never mind. (Inside the ring: Liz DDT's en4cer, and helps Kare suplex Blakenstein. They throw him off the ropes, miss a double-dropkick, and Blakenstein double-clotheslines them. en4cer grabs Kare and starts punching her, as Blakenstein kicks Liz. Outside the ring: Amdur is choking DoMinAA, LaRiche bodyslams Fadner over the railing, Tiszinkel rams Tobin's head into the cage, Epstein DDT's Sable, and Dana kicks Chanoch in the groin.) Monsoon: You gotta feel sorry for Chanoch. Ventura: Hey, he knew what he was getting into. Maritimer: If this keeps up, he'll be needing a tube insertion! (Inside the ring: en4cer rams Kare into the cage. Kare loses whatever remains of her sanity and starts biting en4cer in various parts of the body) Maritimer: Cover your ears! Cover your ears! Ventura: Can anyone tell if she's foaming at the mouth? Monsoon: Put her to sleep, someone! Maritimer: I am NOT gonna take her to bed! Monsoon: I didn't mean THAT! (Inside the ring: After giving en4cer several bite marks, Kare starts chomping on Blakenstein. Liz goes to the top rope and splashes en4cer.) Monsoon: Liz is making a cover! en4cer kicks out at 2! Maritimer: Man, this is brutal. I mean c'mon, BITING instead WRESTLING? Ventura: Anything goes Maritimer. You know that. Maritimer: Yeah, but I was expecting something OTHER than the use of one's incisors! (Inside the ring: Blakenstein recovers enough to punch Kare in the groin. Liz suplexes en4cer, and is clotheslined from behind by Blakenstein. Kare goes to en4cer, and is given a reverse atomic drop.) Monsoon: Wait a minute, who's coming to the ring? Maritimer: Well I'll be... It's the VWO! Ventura: Dollywood, Henry, Eddy, Grant, and Myers! Maritimer: But everyone's too busy fighting to notice! Monsoon: They're pushing away the refs, and unlocking the door! Dollywood is entering the cage, and the others are guarding the doorway, facing the brawl outside. (Kare and en4cer are trading blows as Liz piledrives Blakenstein. Dollywood grabs en4cer, turns him around and swings at him.) Monsoon: He ducked! Ventura: And he hit Kare instead! Maritimer: Why, I do believe Liz noticed. (Liz clotheslines Dollywood.) Maritimer: Yep, she noticed. And the other VWO members have their backs to them! Ventura: Perfection Personifed are now double-teaming Kare! Monsoon: A double suplex, and now they're setting her up for a spike piledriver! Ventura: Liz is too busy fighting Dollywood! Monsoon: They hit the piledriver! en4cer covers, 1...2...3! (Bell rings. Maritimer takes mic) Maritimer: Here are your winners, and STILL RSPWWCW tag team champions, Pretty good Personified! And you guys can thank Dollywood for the win, too! Monsoon: Uh, oh. Now people are going through the cage, bringing some VWO members with them! Ventura: VWO, JWO, Creatures, they're all getting in the cage! Maritimer: And of course, you can't have a brawl without.. Monsoon: STONE COLD! Stone Cold Paul Smith is getting in the cage! Ventura: Now THIS is a brawl, Monsoon! Monsoon: Somebody stop the carnage! Maritimer: Don't worry, someone will. Monsoon: Huh? Wait a minute, NOW who's coming down the aisle? Ventura: It looks like Deadshot, and he's bring a rifle! Maritimer: Two rifles, actually. Monsoon: WHAT?? Maritimer: Hey Jesse, remember when I said that it's legal when someone is shot? Well HERE WE GO! Monsoon: Deadshot is locking the cage, and is now knocking out the refs! Ventura: The others are now stuck in the cage! Monsoon: PLEASE tell me he's not gonna shoot them! Maritimer: Oh relax. One's a BB rifle, and the other's a tranq rifle. No one will be permantly hurt. Ventura: And he's doing it! He's shooting people with the tranq gun! Maritimer: It's very quick. Lasts about two seconds, then nighty-night. Monsoon: They're dropping like flies! Kare, Smith, Tiszinkel, Amdur, Sable, Grant... Maritimer: IYH is gonna stand for "In Your Hospital"! Ventura: And this is your idea, Maritimer? Maritimer: Well Deadshot wanted to shoot them, but for real. I got him to tone it down. (Shawn Micheals comes down the aisle and stands behind Deadshot.) Monsoon: What? IT'S SHAWN! It's Shawn! Ventura: And he's tuning the band for the sweet chin music! Maritimer: I'm not worried. I was prepared for this. (Someone runs down the aisle and attacks Shawn.) Monsoon: What the? That's CURT HENNIG! Curt Hennig is attacking Shawn Micheals! Maritimer: Yeah! Take that, you pill-poppin', fudge-packin', cock-suckin', little queer! (Curt beats on Shawn and they go back to the curtains and to the locker-rooms) Monsoon: Deadshot's done! They're all unconscious! And he's shooting some of them with BB's, even though they're not awake! Maritimer: Well, it's now MY turn to do an interview. If you'll excuse me... (Deadshot unlocks the cage and they both enter ring among the unconscious. Maritimer holds mic up to Deadshot.) Deadshot: (holds up one of the rifles) See this? This.... is my BOOM-STICK! A hand-pump, double-barrelled tranq rifle. S-Mart's top of the line. You can find this sweet baby in the sporting goods department. That's right, shop Smart. Shop "S"-Mart. YOU GOT THAT!?!?! (Crowd recoils in horror while nodding their heads.) Monsoon: What the? Is that Liz getting up? Ventura: It is Liz! She's sneaking behind them! Deadshot: Now I swear, the next one of you primates... even TOUCHES me.... Monsoon: Liz is gonna attack! Deadshot: YYYYEEEAAAUUUGGGGHHH!!! (turns and shoots Liz four times with the BB rifle. Twirls the tranq gun and shoots her square in the chest. Liz staggers, then falls) Deadshot: Now, If you people would just shut the hell up for a minute the Maritimer and I will let you know why I just did what I did. Now, ever since the Maritimer and I teamed up in this slop bucket of a federation eight months ago we have tried to raise the standard of competition. We came and we gave you hillbillies one hundred and ten percent night in and night out. When we couldn't give you that high caliber of wrestling we vacated OUR championship titles. And they ARE our titles. If you call that travesy at Burrito a legit title win for Perfection Personified you ARE as stupid as your drooly-faces appear. How long did the ref let those two Jews tape us on the tables? He probably can't count to ten considering the low level of education here in the United States. However, that was just one of a long line of travesties perpetrated against the Maritimer and myself. Ever since we came back from our injuries we have been screwed out of matches that we should have won, we have been ganged up on by "wrestlers" who can out-wrestle us so they bring goons or have stupid stipulations added to matches so they can save their titles. Well, we have had enough. From now on we are going to start handing out the beatings and the punishment. Monsoon: I don't believe this! Ventura: Believe it, Monsoon! Deadshot: Tonight is a the dawning of a new era for the Perfect Canadians and for all of our true fans who appreciate high caliber talent when they see it. You see Canadians provide the most peace-keeping forces forces for the entire world. This means that we have a unique knowledge of various type of violence from all over the world. Now instead of stopping this violence, we are going to start implementing it. VWO, JWO, Creatures, it doesn't matter who gets in our way they are going down. To kick things off we are going right to the heart and soul of RSPWWCW to set an example. Monsoon: We're out of time folks! Join us next week for In Your House! (show ends)
