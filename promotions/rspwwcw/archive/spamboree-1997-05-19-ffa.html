Spamboree, 19 May 1997, Free-For-All

19 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********************************** RSPWWCW Spamboree 1997 Free-For-All ********************************** (Black and white footage, with voiceover) The more things change, the more they stay the same. At least that's how it is for The Taskmistress and Powrhug Kare, and with Deadshot and The Maritimer. When they last fought, The Taskmistress was the IG champion, Powrhug was the women's champ, and the Perfect Canadians were the tag team champions. Now that has all changed. The Maritimer is now the IG champion, and the Taskmistress is the women's champion, and the "Vamps as Champs" hold the tag team belts. One thing that hasn't changed, is that these four will square off yet again, and the fight... will begin... anew... (end of footage) (Free for All logo comes on the screen. Fades away to the ring and then goes to the commentators Tony Schiavone and Bobby Heenan.) Tony: Hello everyone, and welcome to the Spamboree Free-For-All! I'm Tony Shiavone along with Bobby "The Brain" Heenan. Tonight we have the match we did not see at Unkillfiled, The Vamps as Champs defending the belts against the Perfect Canadians. Brain: And even though he's already wrestling, The Maritimer wants to be a "fighting champion" or something, so he's defending the title tonight against the Blue Avenger! Tony: That match is not just for the IG title, but for the Extreme Bandwith title as well. Because of this, Commissioner DugganFan has ordered that the other three participants in the tag match must be in singles matches as well. And ALL of them will involve a title. Added to this, is a World title match between the VWO's man Dollywood, and the JWO's man, Epstein. Brain: And in case THAT'S not enough, there's also a 20-man battle royal, with the winner getting a shot at the world title tomorrow night at RawNitro! Tony: Let's go the ring and Howard Finkel, for our Free-For-All matchup between the Diamond Studds and the Heavenly Butts. ****************** Diamond Studds vs. Heavenly Butts ****************** (Smells like Teen Spirit: Nirvana) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this tag-team match is set for one fall. Entering the ring representing the VWO, Rich in KC, Diamondback Chris Myers, the DIAMOND STUDDS! (NOD theme plays. Heavenly Butts come out with Mista Speed and Gangsta Spammy) Finkel: Their opponents, making their way to the ring, escorted by Mista Speed and Gangsta Spammy, Dr. Love Oleg Issers, Gigalo Mark Davenport, the HEAVENLY BUTTS! Speed and Spammy: The Butts are in the house, alive, and in colour, Don't diss the team, or they'll diss your mother. Gigalo Mark, and Dr. Love Issers, Will beat you guys up, like some real old pissers. (Nation .... of Domination) Remember, by ANY MEANS NECESSARY! (bell rings. Issers and Myers lock up.) Tony: What the NOD seems to forget Brain, is that the Studds are part of the VWO. Brain: Who isn't a part of the VWO. They have more members then the NRA, or the IRA, or the IRS... Tony: We get the point. (Myers backs Issers to the ropes. Issers gives an eye rake. Clotheslines Myers, goes off the ropes and gives another clothesline. Puts him in a headlock, then tags Davenport.) Tony: The Heavenly Butts are quick to take the advantage. Brain: Yeah, speak it Brother! (Davenport kicks Myers in the midsection, then bodyslams him. Goes for an elbowdrop, but Myers rolls out of the way. Crawls over to tag Rich. Rich dropkicks Davenport, then does it again.) Tony: The Diamond Studds are coming back! Brain: Hey, don't ever mention studs coming back in the butts. Tony: BOBBY HEENAN!! Brain: Yes I am. (Rich bounces off the ropes, but gets tripped up by Spammy.) Tony: There's that outside interference the NOD are known for. Brain: I think they should be careful. With anyone else, that'd be fine, but they're messing the VWO here. (Davenport axehandles Rich, then tags Issers. They throw him off the ropes, and goes for a double clothesline. He ducks, and now gets tripped by Speed.) Tony: More interence by the NOD.. Brain: It's the VWO! Tony: It's [name withheld] and Lionwrath Zen Jericho! (Grant and Jericho come down and attack Speed and Spammy. While Issers and Mark watch, Rich and Myers get behind them. When they turn around, Rich gives Issers the DiamondCutter and Myers does the same to Davenport.) Tony: The Studds have DiamondCutted BOTH of the Heavenly Butts! Brain: Goodnight, Irene! (Rich covers Issers, and gets the pin.) (Sounds like Teen Spirit: Nirvana) Finkel: Here are your winners, the DIAMOND STUDDS! (Studds leave with Grant and Jericho, and they're all high-fiving each other and themselves.) Tony: An outstanding tag match to start things off. Now let's show you, the fans, some info on the 20-man battle royale. (Black and white footage, with voiceover) The RSPWWCW heavyweight title. With it comes the prestige and respect that being a champion brings. It is a goal that all men reach for. Tonight, 20 men will battle it out in a battle royale. One will go home tonight with a title shot. 19 will just go home. (end of footage) (Lights go towards a set with a heart-shaped bed and a "Heartbreak Hotel" sign. "I'm just a sexy boy" begins, and Shawn Micheals comes out.) Shawn: Hello chicks and dicks, and welcome to another night at the Heartbreak Hotel. My guests tonight claim to be perfect, but I think it's safe for us to assume otherwise. Tonight they will facing my fellow creatures Powrhug Kare and the Taskmistress for the RSPWWCW tag-team belts, they are Deadshot and The Maritimer, the "Perfect" Canadians. (Perfect Symphony begins, and Deadshot and The Maritimer come out to the set. Crowd erupts.) Shawn: Well, it looks like we can assume that you two will enjoy a home-field advantage tonight, especially you Maritimer, who I understand attend the university that owns this centre, the University of New Brunswick. (Crowd goes crazy.) Shawn: In fact, it will very interesting to see not only the tag-team match, but also singles matches that will involve all four wrestlers. Tell me Maritimer, do you think you can defend the IG title and still win the tag-titles? (Leans the microphone over to The Maritimer) Maritimer: Without a... Shawn: (brings back the microphone) Actually you shouldn't have to answer that as we already know that you two RAN from the two chicks that I do oh-so-enjoy. There's no reason for me to think that you two won't run away tonight before the match even starts. Do you agree, Deadshot? Deadshot: Listen you little... Shawn: (brings back the mic, again) Can't disagree, can you? In fact, I actually don't blame you. If you are actually dumb enough to enter the ring with them, I will assure you that it WILL be your last match. Any last comments, Maritimer? Maritimer: Will you... Shawn: Nothing to say, huh? Well, thank you for joining us tonight and I hope ... (Stops speaking as Deadshot grabs him by the throat and yells in the mic) Deadshot: Listen, you little shit! We came out here to say something, and by the hell you're gonna stand here and wait 'till we're done. Understand, you long-haired, penis-with-eared, knob-gobbling little copulator? Your turn, Maritimer. Maritimer: (takes the mic) Before Deadshot makes his big announcement, or before he strangles the gay-toy, I'd like to say something. Last week I won the IG title, actually no, that's not correct. I just formally became two things that everyone already knew I was. One, that I'm the IG champ, and two, I'm...PERFECT!! (crowds cheers.) Your turn, Deadshot. (Deadshot lets go of Shawn, who looks like he's ready to cry. Deadshot takes the mic) Deadshot: Y'know, I'm starting to get a little pissed with the VWO. With their constant interference, a guy can't win a match against them unless you bring an army. Well, I have someone with me, that'll help "attend" to my needs. When I was in the "Betty Rubble Clinic for Decarbonization", I met someone who I like to consider as... special. So starting tonight, I'll be escorted to my matches with my personal attendant, K-A Shoot! I know a lot of you want to see her, but you'll have to wait until my TV title match, where I'll be beating Mattie Carrington into a pulp. Anything to add, Maritimer? Maritimer: Why I think I will, thank you. Last week on Monday RawNitro, I accepted a series of matches with the entire creatures. Deadshot: Creatures? What are those? Maritimer: Y'know, Liz, Kare, ATM LaRiche, Sable, and a couple of no-names that I can't remember. I'm supposed to defend the IG title against them all in one night. Well, it'll happen tomorrow night at RawNitro, taking place in Halifax, Nova Scotia. Remember, you collection of walking used-douches, there's three things you can't outdo a Maritimer. You can't out-insult a Maritimer, (crowd cheers) you can't out-drink a Maritimer, (crowd goes nuts) and YOU CAN'T OUT-FIGHT A MARITIMER!! Let's go. (As they leave, Shawn picks up the mic) Shawn: Yo, Maritimer. You're forgetting one thing. (Maritimer and Deadshot stop, and turn back) When you wrestle all the creatures, you wrestle ALL the creatures, and that includes me! Maritimer: (goes to the mike) You won't be able to wrestle because of your injury. Shawn: Injury? What injury? Maritimer: This one! (Deadshot and Maritimer jump Shawn, and start pounding. They knock Shawn off his feet, and Maritimer grabs one of his feet about 3 feet up. Deadshot splashes on the leg. Shawn screams) Tony: Did you hear that? DID YOU HEAR THAT? Brain: Hear what? Who, me? (Creatures come out and attack Maritimer and Deadshot. The creatures knock them off the stage. Maritimer and Deadshot stagger to their feet and hurry to the dressing room. The Creatures attend to Shawn.) Tony: Folks, we gotta go. Join us in 5 minutes for the start of Spamboree 1997! -- The Maritimer - A member of the "New Brunswickans for a Live RAW campaign" (NBLRC) NBLRC website: http://NBLRC.home.ml.org Remember: I am what I say I am, and I say, I'm PERFECT!
