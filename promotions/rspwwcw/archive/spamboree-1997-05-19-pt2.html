Spamboree, 19 May 1997, Part 2

19 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
******************** Spamboree 1997 ******************** (When we were with them last...) Tony: The Taskmistress covering her.. and gets the pin. ("Because the Night": Natalie Merchant) Finkel: The winner of the match, and STILL RSPWWCW Women's Champion, the TASKMISTRESS, LIZ MICHEAL! Tony: The Taskmistess is your winner, and Brain, I thought that the New Demolition would have came in by now. Brain: Be careful what you wish for, 'cause HERE THEY COME! (New Demolition comes through the crowd, enter the ring, and attack Liz from behind. Double axhandles, bashes head against turnbuckle about 10 times) Tony: Somebody get some security down here! Brain: They're going for the decapitation.. they got her! Tony: Tigress is getting up, and look at this she's laughing. She giving the high five sign to them. Brain: Are they working for her? They're walking over to her.. Tony: And Hatchet gives her a high five! Brain: I knew it! I knew they were working for her! Tony: But now Crunch gives her a clothesline! Brain: I knew it! I knew they weren't working for her! Tony: Tigress, being stomped and now they giving her double axehandles. She's being set up to be decapitated! They hit it! Brain: Liz is almost back on her feet! Tony: Crunch is going towards her, and gives her a belly-to-belly. Hatchet picks up Tigress and DDT's her, as Crunch is climbing the top rope. Crunch leaps, and SPLASHES LIZ! Brain: That looks like Air Nova! Wait, HERE COMES THE VWO! (VWO charge the ring and New Demolition barely gets out in time. They go through the crowd again as the VWO dump Tigress out of the ring and tend to Liz.) Tony: What kind of a sick team would spread this chaos? Brain: Did you notice the splash? You don't think... Tony: Right now I don't know what to believe. Let's take you to Scheme Gene. (Goes to Gene backstage, who has a black eye, and has his head wrapped) Gene: Thank you gentlemen. I'm joined by the Blue Avenger, who is set to meet the Maritimer in a title-vs-title match. Your thoughts on tonight's match. Avenger: Genie baby, this is the big time. This is RSPWWCW, and this is Spamboree. And this is the last time you'll see the Blue Avenger with only one belt, because after tonight, I'll be walking around with TWO of them! Gene: You know, I talked to the creatures earlier tonight, and they seem bent on winning the IG title tomorrow night at RawNitro. It could be possible that they may not let you win the belt tonight. Avenger: LET me? What the hell are THEY sniffing? If they show they're skanky little faces during my match, well, let's just say I will not be responsible for my actions. Remember, it ain't over, 'till the Spice Girls sing. Gene: Back to ringside. ***************************** Title vs. Title match: The Maritimer vs. The Blue Avenger ***************************** (Wannabe: Spice Girls) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is a title-vs-title match! Coming down the aisle, from Great Britian, he is the RSPWWCW Extreme Bandwith Champion, the BLUE AVENGER! (Perfect symphony. Crowd goes apeshit) Finkel: And his opponent, hailing from Fredericton, New Brunswick. He is the the RSPWWCW Intergender Champion, the MARITIMER! (sign in crowd: "Now THAT'S perfect!") Tony: The Maritimer, doing battle against the Blue Avenger, in the Maritimer's back yard. (bell rings. Blue Avenger slips around and waistlocks Maritimer. Maritimer goes to the ropes, and breaks it. Lock up, Blue Avenger puts Maritimer in a headlock, Maritimer gives him a back suplex. Cover. Count of 1.) Tony: Tight match-up so far. Now the Maritimer throws the Avenger to the ropes and gives hima hiptoss. The Avenger lands on his feet, and gives Maritimer a jaw breaker. Cover, and a count of 2. Elbow-drop, and legdrop. Another cover, and another count of 2. Brain: Careful, Maritimer. Slack off, and you'll be beaten. (The Blue Avenger throws the Maritimer to the turnbuckle and charges. Maritimer boots him in the head, goes to the second rope, and gives a bulldog. Cover, and a count of 2.) Tony: Maritimer back on top. Has the Avenger set up for a suplex, but it's blocked. Now the Avenger lifts up the Maritimer, and SUPLEXES HIM OUT OF THE RING! Blue Avenger goes the top and MOONSAULTS the Maritimer on the floor. Brain: He's not the Extreme Champion for nothing. Tony: Blue Avenger throws him back in the ring, and is climbing the top rope. Brain: Wait a minute, are those creatures? Tony: They are! It's Natasha and Sasskia of the creatures running to the ring. The Blue Avenger is waiting for Maritimer to get to his feet. He's getting up, and the creatures jump in the ring and attack him! (Bell sounds. The girls knock the Maritimer off his feet, and the Avenger leaps and gives them a bodypress. Gets up, throws Natasha out of the ring, grabs Sasskia, and throws her on top of Natasha. The girls flee. Avenger turns around and is clotheslined over the top rope by Maritimer.) Finkel: The winner of the match, as a result of a disqualification, the MARITIMER! (Perfect symphony plays, and the crowd cheers. Avenger goes to the back, and the Maritimer stands in the ring with his belt. Leaves the ring.) Tony: Well, I guess the creatures wanted the Maritimer to have the belt for tomorrow night, and they succeeded. The Maritimer, at the curtain, turns and holds the belt up to the crowd. (Girl in white face paint carrying a bat sneaks up behind Maritimer) Tony: Wait a minute, that's SABLE, and she has a bat! Brain: She hit him! She hit him with the bat! (crowd boos) Tony: She's standing over the Maritimer, and picks up the belt. (crowd cheers) Brain: LOOK OUT! IT'S DEADSHOT! Tony: Deadshot clotheslines her from behind. K-A Shoot is there, and is helping the Maritimer up. Deadshot picks up the belt, and they're leaving rather quickly. Brain: They probably didn't want to wait for the rest of the creatures. Tony: Now, while we're getting set for the battle royale, let's check out the taped comments from some of the contendants. (Taped footage) Stone Cold Paul Smith: What the hell do you want? Listen up son, I don't know why I have to go through 19 pieces of trash just to get a title shot, then that's what I'll do. It don't matter if it's Dollywood or Epstein, because the fact of the matter is, your ass is mine, and that's the bottom line! en4cer and Blakenstein: en4cer: Ladies and gentlemen, you are viewing Perfection Personified, and tonight you'll find out why we are what we say we are. There's only ONE perfect tag-team, and you're lookin' at 'em. JWO (with Amdur, Tiszinkel, and Epstein) Epstein: Tonight I'm wrestling for the World title, but boys, just in case the VWO try and cheat like they always do, do us proud and win this battle royale. VWO (with Grant and Jericho) Grant: The rest of team are busy tonight, so it's up to us to prove why we're, as Liz would say, so SWEEEEEEEET! Jericho: Because once you're VWO, you're VWO, 4, life! Sabu424: RSPWWCW, tonight you'll be trilled to witness the debut of the one and only Sabu424. 'Nough said. FunkyM: FunkyM will win the battle royale, then FunkyM win title tomorrow night by kicking them in the balls, 'cause FunkyM is just too kewl. Big bang baby, it's a crash, crash, crash! (footage ends) ***************************** 20-man battle royale ***************************** (NOD theme is playing as Ravishing Rob E is walking down to the ring.) Tony: Rob E making his way to the ring that have 18 people already there. In case you didn't know, the participants are: Jeff Amdur, [name withheld], Blakenstein, Blackjack Gern Blanston, "The Canadian Crippler" Alex Demchuk, Robert Wallace Eddy, Ravishing Rob E, en4cer, FunkyM, [name withheld], John Henry, Lionwrath Zen Jericho, Conn MacKintosh, The Mick, Bogus Prophet Mike Palij, Sabu424, Evan Schlesinger, Steve Tiszinkel, Waru, (Stone Cold music plays. Crowd cheers. Paul Smith walks out) Tony: And this man, Stone Cold Paul Smith! Who do you think will win, Brain? Brain: You mean who do I think WON'T win. All 20 of these guys could win this. I can't make a choice. (Smith enters the ring with the other 19) (Bell rings. General chaos ensues) Tony: Let me tell you folks, commentating on a battle royale is one of the hardest things to do. Brain: Fortunately, you have me. Tony: We have Smith and Rob E going at it, as well as Sabu424 and FunkyM. Waru kicks John Henry in the groin, and goes for a clothesline. Henry ducks, and Waru is gone! Brain: There's the first casualty, and we're only a minute and a half into it! Tony: Amdur and Tiszinkel are brawling with Grant and Jericho, as there is no love loss there. Brain: Paul Smith is trying to throw out Evan! Tony: And he might succeed, but no, Evan pokes him in the eyes to get out of the situation. The Bogue Prophet, Mike Palij, is trading blows the Robert Wallace Eddy. Conn MacKintosh is coming over, and is helping Eddy! Mike Palij, struggling against them, but isn't very successful, and.. is.. gone! Mike Palij has been eliminated. Brain: Already we're almost at five minutes, and only two people are gone! Look for the numbers to increase as they get more tired, though. Tony: John Henry is picking up Sabu424. Throws him over, but Sabu slips underneath the bottom rope. [name withheld] runs off the ropes and gives the Mick a flying forearm! Brain: That's not a smart move. Running into the ropes can easily set up to be eliminated. Stay in the centre of the ring. Tony: The en4cer and Blakenstein are double teaming FunkyM. En4cer is holding FunkyM as Blakenstein gives a dropkick, to en4cer! En4cer falls over the top rope! FunkyM moved, and Blakenstein ends up eliminating his own partner! Brain: Now THAT'S perfect. Tony: Rob E walks up behind Paul Smith, and gives him a low blow! Smith falls to his knees, turns, and gives one back to Rob E! Brain: Eye for an eye, and all that. Tony: Zen Jericho is being double teamed by Amdur and Tiszinkel. They have him almost over.. Grant's coming to help Zen. He pulls off Amdur, but Tiszinkel pushes Jericho over the top rope! [name withheld] headbutts Amdur, then goes over and headbutts Tiszinkel. But here comes Alex Demchuk, and he clotheslines Grant over the top! Brain: Just like the that, both members of the VWO are gone! Tony: Robert Wallace Eddy goes over to Jeff Amdur and has him set for a piledriver. Brain: Too soon. Way too soon. Tony: You may be right Brain, and you are, as Amdur backbodydrops Eddy over the top rope! We're over the ten minute mark folks, and 14 people still remain. There's still the JWO, FunkyM, Stone Cold Paul Smith, Blackjack Gern Blanston, who's right now being doubleteamed by John Henry and Rob E. They pick up Blanston, and deposit him over the side. One more is gone. Brain: Look at FunkyM move! Tony: Indeed. FunkyM jumps straight to the top turnbuckle and moonsaults Evan Schlesinger! Conn MacKintosh attacks FunkyM and throws him over, no wait, he slipped underneath the ropes and MacKintosh. Conn turns around, and is dropkicked by FunkyM! Conn is over... and out! Brain: Paul Smith is in trouble! Tony: It looks like [name withheld] and Jeff Amdur are trying to throw out Paul Smith. They can't seem to do it. Evan is coming over, and helping the duo out against Smith. Can they do it, they do! Amazing! Stone Cold Paul Smith has been eliminated, and he's none to happy. Brain: He's coming back in! Tony: Yes he is, and he gives the stunner to Amdur! Now Evan! [name withheld]! He's not stopping! He goes to Rob E and gives him one! FunkyM! Blakenstein! The Mick! John Henry! Tiszinkel! Sabu424! He gets Demchuk, and now they're all laying on the mat! The refs are getting rid of Smith, and he's finally going... Brain: But I think he left his mark. Tony: People are getting up, as Blakenstein charges FunkyM. He gives FunkyM a clothesline, and they both go over. Wait! FunkyM managed to hang on, and is coming back over the rope, a.k.a. Ricky Steamboat style. But here comes Demchuk and Tiszinkel, and they have FunkyM set up for a double-bodyslam. FunkyM's hanging on, but now the Mick dropkicks FunkyM in the gut, and that sends him over, onto the floor. Brain: Nice performance, though. Tony: Indeed it was, we are now past the 15 minute mark, and over half are gone. 11 are eliminated, make that 12, as Rob E is deposited on the floor by John Henry. Jeff Amdur attacks John Henry, and is trying to throw him over. Brain: It's not working. Tony: Nope, and Amdur realizes it. He leaves Henry, but Henry recovers, grabs Amdur and BACK SUPLEXES him over the top! Brain: Ouch! Tony: Now it looks like his JWO partner, Steve Tiszinkel, will be gone. [name withheld] has him over, but Steve lands on the apron. He's trying to roll back in, but here comes Sabu424 and he gives him a baseball slide, eliminating him. Brain: And then there were six. Who's left, there's John Henry, Bob [name withheld], The Mick, Evan Schlesinger, Sabu424, and Alex Demchuk. Tony: The Mick has John Henry, and here comes Evan. Wait, he grabs the Mick, and is trying to throw HIM over. Henry recovers, helps Evan, and the Mick is gone! Demchuk and [name withheld] has Sabu424 in a precarious position. John Henry headbutts Evan to the mat. Henry walks over, and gives a shove as Sabu424 falls over and onto the floor. Brain: Now we just have four! How bad do you want it? Tony: Evan and Demchuk are fighting it out as John Henry and Bob [name withheld] are going at it. Evan gives Demchuk a reverse atomic drop. Henry piledrives [name withheld]. Henry taking [name withheld] and throwing him to the ropes. [name withheld] puts Henry in a crucifix! Henry backing towards the ropes, and trys to dump [name withheld]. They both end up going over, but [name withheld] grabs the ropes and hangs on! Brain: Now it's just Evan, [name withheld], and Demchuk. Tony: We have just passed the 20 minute mark. Evan and Demchuk are fighting, and [name withheld] is coming over. He grabs their heads, and knocks them together. He grabs Demchuk and tries to push him over. Evan comes, and helps [name withheld]. Demchuk is... gone. Now it's [name withheld] vs. Evan Schlesinger. Evan gives Bob a low blow, but [name withheld] grabs Evan, and backbodydrops him over the top! Brain: Evan's not gone! Evan's not gone! Tony: No he isn't. Evan landed on the apron, but [name withheld] doesn't realize it. He walks across the ring, holding his hands up. Evan gets him from behind, and throws him over, and [name withheld] hit the floor! It's over! It's over! ("Sympathy for the Devil": Rolling Stones) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the 20-man battle royale, EVAN SCHLESINGER! Tony: Evan now faces the world champion tomorrow night on RawNitro, whomever it may be. End of hour two.
