RSPWWCW SummerScam, Hour One, 3 August 1997

3 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
******************* RSPWWCW SummerScam ******************* (Black and white footage with voiceover.) The summer-time. The time to enjoy the outdoors. Or perhaps time to enjoy the indoors. Time to get in the ring. And time to fight all through the night. The summer. A Slam for most. A scam for others. (footage ends) (Fireworks go off and the crowd goes crazy.) Ross: Hello everyone, and welcome to Vancover, British Columbia, Canada! Welcome to the hottest PPV on RSPW! Welcome, to Summer Scam! (shot of announcers table) Ross: Hello, I'm Jim Ross, along with Jerry "The King" Lawler, and what a card do we have for you! The world title is on the line, the Intergender title is on the line, the TV title is on the line, the CRUISERweight title is on the line, and even the TAG titles are on the line tonight! King: Not to mention we have the four corners tag-match with the winner getting a title shot tomorrow night! Ross: Let's get you to the action! ************************************************************** Cruiserweight title match: Extreme Reverend vs. Patrick Emond ************************************************************** Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this opening bout is for the RSPWWCW Cruiserweight title! Currently in the ring, from parts unknown, he is the RSPWWCW Cruiserweight champion, the EXTREME REVEREND! Ross: Wait a minute, who's that coming to the ring? Lawler: Wha? That's Deadshot! And he's getting in the ring! Ross: Deadshot is attacking the Reverend! WHY? Lawler: Now Maritimer is coming in too! Ross: Deadshot lands a belly-to-belly, and Maritimer is entering the ring, but he's STOPPING Deadshot! Deadshot is stopping, and is now asking for a mic. Deadshot: First of all, I want to say hello to all of you Canucks, (crowd cheers) Deadshot: ... and all of you Yankees. (crowd boos) Deadshot: Now, as to why I did what I just did, it all comes down to prejudice. Not towards whites, not towards blacks, not even towards women. You know what I'm talking about, I'm talking about prejudice towards FAT PEOPLE! People like me! It's SO bad, they even make a CRUISERWEIGHT title, just so those skinny freaks can have something of their own! Ross: This guy's got a point. Lawler: WHAT? Deadshot: I propose one of two things. Either (a) get rid of the cruiserweight title, or (b) have the World Heavyweight title go to people that are at LEAST 250 pounds. Sorry Maritimer, but I had to get this off my chest. You may now continue on with your wrestling program. Ross: Well, Deadshot and Maritimer are now leaving... Lawler: Do we still have a match? Ross: I think so. The Reverend is getting up, and the bell is ringing. Emond attacking Reverend and clotheslines him, following with an elbowdrop. Lawler: C'mon. Give him a chance to recover! Ross: Emond rams Reverend's head into the turnbuckle. Now he's throwing Reverend into the other corner, but Reverend reverses. Reverend grabbing Emond and belly-to-belly suplexes him. Lawler: Yeah, now it's fair! Ross: The Reverend following up with a dropkick, then a clothesline of his own. A cover, and a count of 2. Lawler: C'mon Reverend. Emond is just a Canadian! Ross: Reverend throws Emond to the ropes and misses a clothesline. (Emond stops, waits for Reverend to turn, and hits him with a superkick.) Ross: Oh my! What a kick by Patrick Emond! He's making a cover, but only gets a count of two. (Emond goes to ram Reverend's head into the turnbuckle, but it's blocked and Reverend rams Emond's head to the turnbuckle.) Ross: Reverend now throwing Emond to the opposite corner, and is charging. Reverend leaps in the air and Emond moves! Reverend hits his head on the steel post and falls back to the mat! Lawler: What is he, unconscious? Ross: He must be. Emond covers him, and gets the pinfall! (bell rings. "I'm a lumberjack" - Monty Python. Crowd cheers) Finkel: The winner of this match, and NEW RSPWWCW Cruiserweight champion, The Heartburn Kid, PATRICK EMOND! Lawler: What a ripoff! Ross: I don't think Deadshot's attack before would've made a difference in that case, King. Now that we're ready for the next match, let's go to the Fink. ********************************************* "The Genius" Gern Blanston vs. The Dark Angel ********************************************* Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is set for one fall. Currently in the ring, from somewhere in the States, The Genius, GERN BLANSTON! (Before Finkel can continue, Dark Angel, wearing a mask, comes to ringside and starts badmouthing the fans, then the commentators, then gets back in the ring and yells insults at Gern. Then another Dark Angel comes out, again wearing a mask, and grabs the other one, and points to backstage while yelling at him. The first Angel looks at him, then leaves the ring area. Dark Angel grabs the mic.) D. Angel: Guys, I'm really sorry. I had my cousin with me backstage, I went to take a nap, and he took off with one of my outfits. By the time I realized what happened, it was too late. I... (Gern takes mic from him) Gern: Listen. I had a nice, mellow poem for this match. But because of the stupidity of your "cousin", I'm left with no choice but to use THIS one. (clears his voice as he opens scroll) May bloody piles torment you, may corns grow on your feet. May crabs as big as turtles, crawl up your ass and eat. And when you're old and feeble, and become a nervous wreck, I hope your head falls through your ass, and breaks your fucking neck! (crowd goes nuts) Ross: What in the world? Lawler: Hey, I think he deserves it, Ross! (Angel attacks Gern as bell sounds.) Ross: I think the Dark Angel would disagree with that, King. Now a gut-wrench suplex, followed with an elbow drop. A cover by Dark angel, and only a count of 1. Lawler: That's it. Get back at Gern. You know that nobody likes him. Ross: That's not true. Dark Angel with a back suplex. Dark Angel stops and poses for the crowd. The crowd with a less than positive response. Lawler: If you got it, flaunt it! (Gern gets up and as Angel turns, hits him with a reverse atomic drop.) Lawler: NO! Ross: Now Gern running off the ropes and hits with a flying forearm! Dark Angel gets out of the ring and goes back up the aisle. What is he doing, running away? Lawler: I think he's injured. Ross: Don't lie King. Oops forgot who I was talking to. The referee is counting as Gern stays in the ring. Dark Angel goes to the back as he is counted out. (Bell rings.) Finkel: Here is your winner as a result of a count-out, the Genius, GERN BLANSTON! Ross: Evidently the Dark Angel chose the greater part of valor for this match, King. Wait a minute, there seems to be a disturbance in the back. If we can get our cameras... (shows New Demolition leaving a locker room with officials. Camera goes in and shows that Deadshot is lying on the floor, holding his knee. Maritimer is getting up and tending to him.) Ross: What in the world? Now this is totally uncalled for! Lawler: I guess that four corners match is now a 3-and-a-half corners match, huh? Ross: Oh, you're a riot. ************************************************************** Television title match: Osmarus vs. "Diamondback" Chris Myers ************************************************************** ("Smells like Teen Spirit" - Nirvana) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is for the RSPWWCW Television Title. On his way to the ring, a member of the VWO, "Diamondback" CHRIS MYERS! ("Stormbringer" - Deep Purple) Finkel: And his opponent, coming down the aisle, he is the RSPWWCW Television Champion, OSMARUS! (Bell rings. Osmarus charges at Myers, and Myers moves out of the way. Myers clips Osmarus in the knee, knocking him off his feet.) Ross: Myers doing the smart thing and getting the big man off his feet. Myers, working on Osmarus's knee, and tries to put on a figure four. Lawler: No way. Too early. Ross: Osmarus kicks Myers off before it was put on. Both are getting to their feet, and Osmarus hits Myers with a series of forearms. Lawler: Where's the VWO? Ross: I have no idea. Osmarus throwing Myers off the ropes and goes for a big boot, but Myers slides under and clips his knee again. Lawler: Osmarus won't have a leg to stand on if this keeps up! Ross: Myers goes out of the ring, and grabs Osmarus's leg. He's dragging Osmarus to a corner and throws his knee on the steel post! Lawler: Watch it, don't get counted out! Ross: Myers does it a second time, and goes back in the ring. Osmarus getting to his feet as Myers gets in the ring. Lawler: Look out! Ross: Osmarus knees Myers, then picks him up for a sidewalk slam! He goes for the cover and Myers kicks out at two. Lawler: Osmarus is so powerful, ONE MOVE gets him a two-count. Ross: Osmarus picks up Myers, and Myers starts kicking the knee again. Myers runs off the ropes, but Osmarus catches him and spike-slams him! Lawler: He's finished! Ross: A cover, and a count of 2. Lawler: That's the kind of mand that Myers is, Ross. Ross: Osmarus gets Myers to his feet, and sets him up for a powerbomb. Lawler: If he hits this, it's over! Ross: Osmarus lifts him in the air, but his knee gives out, and they both fall to the mat! Lawler: The knee strategy worked! Ross: They're both slowly getting up, and Myers diamondcutts Osmarus out of nowhere! A cover, and it's over! (Bell rings. "Smells like Teen Spirit" - Nirvana) Finkel: The winner of the match, and NEW RSPWWCW Television Champion, "Diamondback" CHRIS MYERS! End of part one.
