RawNitro Is War from Pittsburgh, 29 September 1997, Part 2

29 September 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RAWNITRO IS WAR 9/29/97 Part 2 The Igloo, Pittsburgh Pennsylvania Announcers: Vince McMahon, Jim Ross, Jerry Lawler, Dusty Rhodes - ***************************** Deadshot vs. Alex Demchuk ***************************** - (Music: Jesus Christ Superstar-Murray Head) - Sunny: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. First the man who claims to be the saviour of RSPWWCW, here is Alex "The Salvation" Demchuk!!! McMahon: This match came about as a result of last week's main event match between Demchuk and the Taskmistress for the World Title. Ross: That's right, Vince. Demchuk blames Deadshot for his loss of the World title to the Taskmistress. Lawler: Well, Deadshot did splash him!!! McMahon: He was trying to interfere on Demchuk's behalf, as you can see in this replay, when Kim Wright hit him and caused him to lose his balance and Liz Michaels pulled Demchuk into Deadshot's way. - (Music: We're Not Gonna Take It-Twisted Sister) - Sunny: His opponent, hailing from Wolfville, Nova Scotia, Canada...One half of the RSPWWCW tag team champions...DEADSHOT!!!!! Ross: Good God Almighty! Deadshot is carrying a huge wooden cross down to the ring!!!!! Lawler: He's gonna crucify Demchuk!!!!!! McMahon: This is horrible!! We apologize to all of our viewers for this distasteful display. Ross: Deadshot has got the cross in a stand and he's carrying a bag as well. Oh my God!! He's pulling a hammer and nails out of the bag!!! This is disgusting! - McMahon: And Demchuk can't take any more. He leaps over the top and comes down on Deadshot with a plancha!! Lawler: I've never seen that move out of Demchuk! Ross: He's pulling out all the stops here tonight folks!! McMahon: Deadshot may have made a fatal mistake by bringing the cross to the ring. Ross: Demchuk is pulling up the protective padding at ringside. Now he is lifting Deadshot up and SLAMS him down on the concrete floor. Lawler: I think that cracked the foundation!! McMahon: Demchuk is back in the ring to break the count and now he's right back out after Deadshot. Ross: Demchuk hauls Deadshot to his feet and throws him into the steel steps! I think Deadshot has been busted wide open. McMahon: Yes, he has a cut over his left eye and it is bleeding profusely. The ref may have to stop this match. Lawler: I say the ref should just let these two idiots destroy each other! McMahon: Demchuk is back in the ring as Deadshot struggles to his feet. Wait a minute, Deadshot is reaching into the bag. He's got the hammer. He's hiding it just below the ring apron so that Demchuk and the ref can't see it. Ross: Demchuk is coming over to haul Deadshot back into the ring and Deadshot nails him with the hammer! Lawler: The stupid referee didn't see it!!! Ha! Ha! This is great!!! McMahon: Deadshot is in the ring now. He slaps on a camel clutch on Demchuk who is now also bleeding from the forehead where Deadshot connected with the hammer. Ross: This is a much more brutal match than anyone was expecting. It would seem that the Canadian Coalition is disintegrating before our eyes. Lawler: Speaking of which, have you noticed that neither the Maritimer or the Canadian Militant Alliance are around for this match. McMahon: Apparently, the executive committee headed by Lord Vader have ordered that they were to be locked in their respective locker rooms for this match. Ross: Anything to spread more chaos for Lord Vader's cause. McMahon: Deadshot has released the camel clutch and is now applying a Boston Crab. Ross: Demchuk is trying to power out of the move. He's using the ref to get leverage!! He breaks the hold and Deadshot flies head first into the turnbuckle. McMahon: Demchuk comes off the ropes and Deadshot stumbles backwards. Crucifix by Demchuk. Ref counts...one...two..Deadshot kicks out!!! - Ross: That was a hair's length from being over. McMahon: Deadshot and Demchuk are trading punches as the ref tries to break it up. Ross: Irish whip by Deadshot on Demchuk. Running lariat applied by Deadshot and Demchuk hits the match hard. McMahon: Demchuk is right back up on his feet and attempts a lariat of his own. Deadshot ducks and he hooks in a belly to back suplex. He fires Demchuk halfway across the ring and quickly starts to stomp the fallen Demchuk. Lawler: The ring is soaked in the blood of both men now. Ross: I think the referee should stop this. McMahon: Deadshot has Demchuk in position for a running powerslam. He starts his run and...HE SLIPPED! Deadshot slipped in a pool of blood on the ring and Demchuk falls on him. The ref makes the count...one...two...Deadshot kicks out again. Ross: Almost a costly accident for the Canadian native. McMahon: Deadshot has definitely hurt his knee and Demchuk is taking advantage. A series of kicks being delivered by Demchuk. Now Demchuk is slapping on a figure four. Deadshot is screaming in pain and trying to claw his way to the ropes. Ross: Deadshot reaches the ropes but Demchuk is slow to release the hold. The referee is making the count and Demchuk makes the break just before five. McMahon: Both men seem to be very aggressive tonight. Deadshot is hauling himself back to his feet using the ropes for support. Demchuk comes running towards him. Deadshot ducks and hauls the ropes down sending Demchuk over the top and he SLAMS into the wooden cross at ringside!!!! Ross: I think Demchuk is unconscious!! Deadshot is going out and he is dragging the unconscious body of Demchuk back into the ring. Lawler: I think we all know what's going to happen now!! AIR NOVA!! McMahon: Deadshot slams Demchuk in the corner and starts to climb the ropes. He's setting up for it and.....HE HITS THE AIR NOVA ON DEMCHUK!!! Ross: This one's over. The ref counts...one...two...THREE!!! Deadshot wins!! - (Music--We're Not Gonna Take It-Twisted Sister) - Sunny: The winner of the match...DEADSHOT!!! Lawler: What's Deadshot doing now!?! Ross: Oh my God!! He's bringing that cross into the ring!! Lawler: You don't think he's gonna do what I think he's gonna do, do you?!? McMahon: We need security down here right now! Ross: Deadshot is setting that cross up in the corner and pulling Demchuk out farther into the centre of the ring. Deadshot climbs the ropes and now he is standing next to the top of the cross. McMahon: Deadshot grabs the top of the cross and and jumps off the top!! He's splashing Demchuk using the cross!! Ross: Oh God!! Deadshot and the cross just came crashing down on Demchuk's knee!!! Did you hear that? I think Deadshot may have done serious damage to Demchuk's knee. This could effect the main event at Beware of Bitch on Sunday! Lawler: Well, at least Deadshot just nailed him with the cross instead of nailing him to it!!! McMahon: You are sick, King. Ross: Deadshot is leaving the ring and heading up the ramp while medical personnel are attending to Demchuk. I think Scheme Gene is attempting to get a word with Deadshot while the crews remove Demchuk and clean the ring for our next match. - Scheme Gene: Ladies and Gentlemen, I have seen some despicable things in my day but this one has to take the cake. Deadshot what do you have to say for yourself. (As the fans start throwing stuff at Deadshot.) Deadshot: Scheme Gene, I suggest you shut your mouth or maybe I'll find a use for these nails and hammer. As for Demchuk, if he's any sort of a saviour, he'll be fine in three days. HA! HA! HA! Gene: Why did you do this? After all you cost this man his title and now you may have put him on the shelf! He supposed to wrestle on Sunday against the Taskmistress! You may have cost him a chance to win back the world title!! Deadshot: Oh well isn't that too bad! Gene, this punk crossed me. I have been a tag team champion for so long that I don't think the singles wrestlers take me as a serious threat. They see a fat guy that they don't have to worry about. Well, if I had wrestled Demchuk last week, I'd be the champion right now, wouldn't I Gene. Demchuk, you were the first but you won't be the last. Little Kimmy Wright, I hope you were watching because, you know something, I didn't have any real problem with Demchuk, and look what I did to him. I hate you and your loserweight kind so just imagine what I'm gonna do to you!!! Scheme Gene: Back to you, McMahon. - McMahon: Unbelievable. How can they continue to allow this brutality, this barbarity to continue in RSPWWCW? When we return, the Taskmistress will defend her World Heavyweight Title against an old nemesis, whom you see there, the Man With No Soul, and Mr. No Soul, any comments on your upcoming match? No Soul: Are you still at this? Aww, for Christ's sake, Annie. Whenever I feel you're about to graduate to veteran status here, you pull off something stupid. And I even said to think before responding to my last message, too. Lawler: What the hell is he talking about? - ============== COMMERCIALS ============== - ************************************************************ RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Championship The Taskmistress Liz Michael vs. The Man With NoSoul (Vizh) ************************************************************ - (Dirty Laundry-Don Henley) - Sunny: The following contest is scheduled for one fall, with a 30 minute time limit, and it is for the RSPWWCW WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP. Introducing first the challenger, from Montreal Quebec Canada, weighing 190 pounds, here is THE MAN WITH NO SOUL!!! McMahon: In my mind a despicable individual. Imagine, saying another man's daughter is really yours. No Soul: Liz, you haven't evolved since you first landed in RSPW. Now, the last thing I remember from you is defending you against Evan last April, and reminding everyone that you'd won your battle against him when he submitted but tried to make it look like he'd won. My stand-in this summer tells me he had nothing to do with you. So apparently, defending you and staying out of your way for months is a recipe to be pissed on. Lawler: Don't bring that up. ATM LaRiche is is the building. - (Music-Bitch-Meredith Brooks) Sunny: And his opponent, weighing 219 pounds and hailing from Venice Beach, California, she is the RSPWWCW WORLD Heavyweight Champion, you go girl, THE TASKMISTRESS LIZ MICHAEL!!! McMahon: The Taskmistress, getting an ovation, unlike earlier when she was being heckled. Accompanied by Kelly Vaters, Sable, and Alexandra Theresa Marlena LaRiche, almost bursting out of her plastic. Lawler: Yeah, and her breasts are almost showing, too. Ross: The black and pink tights of the Taskmistress Liz Michael. Usually black and pink doesn't get cheered in the US. But I think there is all around disgust over the actions of No Soul. - McMahon: Both combatants stalking each other. Lawler: Don't say that. [name withheld] might show up. Ross: Liz and No Soul exchanging a pair of fists. And some more stalling. McMahon: No Soul seemingly apprehensive about hooking up with the RSPWWCW World Heavyweight champion, the BADDEST WOMAN IN WRESTLING. Ross: Well, the Taskmistress Liz Michael, probably has to be the greatest transgendered athlete in history. Lawler: Well, so what? It's not like she used to be a guy or anything. Ross: Now the Taskmistress body slams No Soul and Taskmistress misses an elbow drop. Assuming the Taskmistress remains the Heavyweight champion, she will defend it next against Alex Demchuk Sunday in a Steel Cage. That's IF demchuk is able to go, Deadshot may have seriously injured the knee of Alex "salvation" Demchuk. McMahon: No Soul off the ropes, and a back drop by the Taskmistress. Now The Taskmistress with axhandles chops across the back of No Soul. But No Soul fighting back with right hands. Ross: No Soul doing a little stalling now arguing with referee Stoink. Now, the Taskmsitress calling for a test of strength. We understand that on Sunday, the special guest referee for the cage match will be The Living Legend Larry Zbyszko. Crowd: VIZH SUCKS. VIZH SUCKS. VIZH SUCKS. VIZH SUCKS. McMahon: Well, the fans taking exception to the stalling tactics of the Man With No Soul. And I imagine he'll stall as much as possible with The Genius Gern Blanston on Sunday at the Fleet Center. Dusty: Well, Tathmistreth give a punch to da fais uh duh Man Wid No Soul. An' she clubberin' 'im. McMahon: Now Liz Michael arguing with Stoink while Sable is choking No Soul on the ropes. Now a boot to the face by the Taskmistress who argues with Stoink again, and now ATM LaRiche tries to OH MY GOODNESS! Lawler: She's trying to smother him with her, her, her..... Dusty: Yeah, she sayin' Missa No Soul, Scoop Dis! McMahon: Now the Taskmistress whipping No Soul to the opposite corner, but Stoink arguing with ATM LaRiche. Lawler: Oh no! John Henry tosses a Singapore Cane to the Taskmistress. And she's gonna....I can't watch, McMahon. Dusty: She hit him in hiz vatles dere. And ah mean yiou gotta be nuts ta thank dat dothnt huht. Ross: Taskmistress now with a crucifix into a sunset flip for a pinning predicament which Vizh, I mean, No soul gets out of by having his foot on the ropes. McMahon: Taskmistress takes No soul by the hair and dropkicks No Soul to the floor, where the Bitch World Order stomps on him visciously as Liz Michael tries to block the view of Stoink. - Dusty: Well, now yiou see wut ahm tawkin about, dat ddeez ah delevish wimmen dat we tawkin about here. Lawler: Careful, Dusty, one of those devilish women is your little baby girl! Ross: Michael dragging No Soul back in with an armbar. No Soul trying to punch his way out of this. No Soul now choking the Taskmistress. First offense we've seen out of the Man With No Soul. And VICIOUS rakes to the eyes by No Soul. A whip to the corner by No Soul and a charge, but a BOOT to the face by the Taskmistress. And another charge by No Soul, another boot to the face. Lawler: That looked real stupid, McMahon. Ross: Taskmistress a flying cross chop and now a big right hand that drops No Soul. This is not exactly a classic technical matchup. Liz Michael punching and stomping No Soul into the corner. Now Taskmistress on the outside, grabbing the legs of No Soul and RAMMING his lower midsection into the post. Lawler: Aw, nuts. McMahon: Now the Taskmistress, going for that figure four around the ringpost! She gets it in. The Taskmistress TRYING TO BREAK THE LEGS of No Soul, as Stoink is trying to break up the figure four, the Bitch World Order trying to prevent Stoink from seeing the figure four, and No Soul SCREAMING in agony over the figure four. Ross: Finally, Stoink getting in there and reprimanding the Taskmistress. Now counting down the Taskmistress who breaks just before the count of five. Lawler: Now, Liz arguing with this clown Stoink. McMahon: That's no way to talk about a referee. Lawler: Well, he IS a clown, McMahon. What am I supposed to call 'im? McMahon: And Liz Michael pushing Stoink. And Stoink shoving her right back. It wa snot that long ago that these two, the Taskmistress and Stoink, were stablemates in the Virtual World Order. Ross: Taskmistress in the ring now, and stands up No soul for an enzuiguiri kick to the back of the head. McMahon: And a cover, 1, 2, SHE GOT 'IM! Uh, no she didn't. Lawler: I think Liz Michael must be related to the Hart family or something. McMahon: And now the taskmistress going up top. The Taskmistress with a flying leap, but NO SOUL GETS UP, and PUNCHES her in the stomach! Lawler: Oh, oh. Liz might not even make it to the cage match. Ross: No Soul with a hurricanrana, and a cover, 2, NO! TWO AND A HALF! The title almost changed hands in consecutive weeks on Rawnitro. And what is this, somebody coming out of the stands in a trenchcoat? Lawler: THAT'S MARC MERO!!! McMahon: Wildman Marc Mero making his way down to ringside through the audience. Meanwhile, No Soul with an Irish whip reversed by Liz Michael. Taskmistress with a spinning heel kick, what a DEVASTATING maneuver. But now Marc Mero is out here. And Marc Mero is trying to talk to Sable, and that has caught the attention of the Taskmistress. Lawler: Sable doesn't wanna go with him, McMahon. Sable's real happy with Liz and John Henry. She'd rather be a BITCH, McMahon! McMahon: And No Soul with a TAKEDOWN, ONE, TWO, OH MY GOODNESS THAT was close. Ross: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! SABLE just gave a martial arts thrust kick to the stomach of her own husband, and now the Bitch World Order, Kelly Vaters, John Henry, ATM LaRiche, is beating down Marc Mero. Meanwhile, No Soul going for a hurricanrana, BUT LIZ CATCHES HIM, and TURNS IT INTO A SITTING POWERBOMB! McMahon: WHAT A DEVASTATING MANEUVER. Ross: Taskmistress with an ATOMIC DROP INTO THE SLAUSON CUTOFF! Lawler: OOOOOOWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!!!! McMahon: One, two, three. - DING DING DING DING - (Music-Bitch-Meredith Brooks) - Sunny: The winner of the match, and STILL RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Champion, the TASKMISTRESS LIZ MICHAEL! - McMahon: And I CAN'T BELIEVE THIS! The TASKMISTRESS walking away with Sable snuggling her, up the ramp, while the Bitch World Order continues to pound on the prone Marc Mero. Come on, security, get out here. Ross: I NEVER thought I'd see this. Sable going away, of her own volition, not even with another man, but ANOTHER WOMAN!
