Coming Soon. The RSPW OLYMPICS!!!!!!!, 23 December 1997

23 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Ladies and gentlemen, in the month of January, RSPWWCW will be sponsoring two events of renown. One is the RSPW Rawnitro Bowl in Honolulu, Hawaii. The other will be the REC.SPORT.PRO-WRESTLING GAMES OF THE FIRST OLYMPIAD! Or the RSPW Olympic Games from the great wrestling nation of Japan. Aside from the usual stuff of wrestling matches, I need your help in giving me ideas for every kind of wrestling match you have ever heard of under the sun, and a description thereof if it is not a commonly known thing. (Like a casket match, e.g.) RSPW Posters will compete for their COUNTRY in this, not in their established stables. Some of you who come from countries other than the US or Canada should identify to us what country you are from, so we know where to put you. :-) Everything is awarded as a Gold, Silver or Bronze medal. Dead serious about this. Among the categories I already know we'll have, are: Regal Rumble Singles Tournament for the Heavyweight Gold Medal Singles Tournament for the Cruiserweight Gold Medal Tag Team Tournament for the Tag Team Gold Medal I am also considering things like a Ladder match tournament, a Casket Match battle royal, and even a Plastic Surgery battle royal.....all participants must have had some kind of plastic surgery. :-) Give me your ideas. And if any of you want to write something, be my guest. ******* HOGAN FEARS TASKMISTRESS!!!!!!!!! ******* The Taskmistress -RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Champion - The Most Challenged Woman Wrestler on the Internet!
