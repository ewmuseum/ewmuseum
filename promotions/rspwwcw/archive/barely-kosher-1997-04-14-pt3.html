 RAWNITRO: BARELY KOSHER 4/14/97 Part 3 - The War In Heaven Announcers: Vince McMahon, Jim Ross, Jerry Lawler  ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++ Match 8. The War In Heaven ++++++++++++++++++++++++++++  Vince: WELLLCOME BACK EVERYBODY (Music-Theme from Patton) Vince: Here we go! The War In Heaven. JR: And this is gonna be an old fashioned Jerusalem Street Fight. There really are no rules except the fall only counts in the ring. Vince: Here comes Evan Schlesinger and HIS angels. They are apparently called the Truth Commission. They are marching out their members single file, in fatigues, with "Major" [name withheld] playing the lead. Standing at attention at ringside, and now Major [name withheld] gives them the signal to get in the ring and they do. JR: Well, I'm amazed for one that Evan Schlesinger's Truth Commission has this much discipline. I can't wait to see what the Virtual World Order does when they come in. McMahon: There is Schlesinger with his crown and with a black cauldron of some type. Lawler: I've heard of the pot calling the kettle black before, but this is ridiculous. JR: [name withheld] with his spiked helmet, Wawczak, Mattie Carrington, the Bogus Prophet Mike Palij, Robert Wallace Eddy with rope, [name withheld] with a surfboard and a camcorder, [name withheld] in bikini fatigues still on a leash but she's walking herself apparently. Where are they? JR: They are coming down through the stands, McMahon. There is the Taskmistress. She is wearing her tallis and carrying the Torah and an Uzi. McMahon: Well, I've never seen the Torah used as a foreign object before. Here comes Stone Cold Paul Smith carrying the jawbone of an ass, as he promised. Here comes [name withheld] with a bag and I can't imagine what kind of weapons he has in there. Lawler: And look. Powrhug Kare has her bag, too. McMahon: Rich from Kansas City the Diamond Stud with a bunch of jeweler's tools. And the NEWF recruit Diamondback Chris Myers is here also. JR: Mo-Man Sumler with his noonchucks and throwing stars, Dana Dangerously ladies and gentlemen, in her evening gown and megaphone. The Extreme Reverend with a crucifix. I have a feeling this is gonna get ugly. - - McMahon: [name withheld] is grabbing Liz by the hair and punching Liz. Lawler: He knocked her little beanie off. McMahon: That's a yarmulke, King. Lawler: Yamaka? I thought it was Honda. JR: [name withheld] going straight for the Taskmsitress with vicious forearms. Lawler: First time I ever heard Taskmistress and straight before in the same sentence. JR: [name withheld] kicking Liz in the gut, and a piledriver to the bricks. Lawler: That's kissing holy ground the hard way, McMahon. McMahon: Now Grant with [name withheld], double boot to the face from [name withheld] and Schlesinger. And Mattie Carrington with a flying splash off the apron on [name withheld]. That's gotta hurt. Lawler: Hey, I thought Ryan liked it on the bottom. JR: Now Stone Cold Paul Smith with Evan Schlesinger. Smith taking Schlesinger to the crowd, Smith grabbing a falafel from a fan, eating a piece of it, and smashing it into the face of Schlesinger. McMahon: Rich the Diamond Stud landing with right fists all around for the Truth Commission. He has a tool in those fists, though. Lawler: I think it's a Diamond Cutter, McMahon! JR: Now Stone Cold Paul Smith taking it to Evan Schlesinger. Smith now using that Jawbone Of An Ass on Evan Schlesinger. And the Taskmistress comes around and hits Schlesinger with the Torah. McMahon: Mattie with a flying tope off the apron wipes out Rich. Fooooorgetaboutit! JR: Wait a minute, we have a fan trying to get involved. One of the rabbis in the crowd is now trying to wrestle the Torah away from the Taskmistress, and THE TASKMISTRESS KNOCKS HIM OUT with the Torah. Lawler: That's called havin' the Law come down on ya real hard. JR: Fans you don't wanna get involved with these fine athletes, even if it is over the Word of God. Tremendous flying forearm by Lionwrath Zen Jericho knocks down Mattie Carrington. - McMahon: The Bogus Prophet throws [name withheld] to the ring steps. [name withheld] floors Stone Cold Paul Smith with a short-arm clothesline. Did YOU see THAT? Lawler: There's a big splash by Mattie Carrington on Smith. I like Mattie Carrington. She's a gorgeous woman! JR: The Taskmistress now putting that terribly painful move the Taskmission on the Bogus Prophet Mike Palij. McMahon: And the Bogus Prophet scremaing out in pain from the Taskmisssion, is that what they call it, JR? JR: It's called the Kata-ha-jime, or single wing lock. Lawler: Who can stop this path of rage, McMahon? JR: Oh Oh! Kare has Sweet Bitch Musicked a fan. Some fan grabbed Kare's breast area apparently and Kare just kicked him into oblivion. McMahon: I can't believe this. [name withheld] has a brick. [name withheld] has a brick from the Wailing Wall! How did he get that? [name withheld] is cleaning house and HE TAKES THE BRICK and SMASHES against the Taskmistress' leg. [name withheld] trying to break the leg of the Taskmistress. Lawler: Well, I know she's a fine actress, but when I said break a leg, I didn't mean it like that. McMahon: Now Schlesinger is pounding on Kare with his crown. Lawler: He's crowning Powrhug Kare. Get it, McMahon? Crowning! Ahahahahah! JR: Now this is unfortunate. Another rabbi is trying to take back the brick from the Wailing Wall, and [name withheld] is just beating the rabbi senseless with the brick from the Wailing Wall. Fans, don't do this at home. - McMahon: Now over in the corner there, these two ladies, Dana Dangerously and [name withheld] are sluugging it out. Dana has pulled off [name withheld]'s bikini top, and is trying to choke her with it. Lawler: The Chabad contingent is getting a free show here with [name withheld]'s breasts. I don't think the Orthodox Rabbinical Council is gonna approve of this, McMahon! JR: Mattie Carrington is pounding on Kare now. Now Mattie gives Kare to Robert Wallace Eddy who puts a front face lock on Kare. But Diamondback Chris Myers pushes Eddy to the ringpost. Eddy still holds the facelock and a sunset flip by Kare, NO , EDDY down on Kare's face. Lawler: Right where she wants 'im. McMahon: Now the Taskmistress has a barbell, and she has Evan Schlesinger down on the ground pounding him with that barbell. Lawler: She's just doing her lifts and thrusts McMahon. Gotta keep in shape, you know. McMahon: [name withheld] taking Rich from Kansas City and he is military pressing him against the Wailing Wall itself. Oh, oh, here comes another rabbi. And [name withheld] bodyslams the rabbi. Lawler: McMahon, this is no kind of place for rabbis! McMahon: I won't even dignify that one, King! Lawler: Well, what'd I say? JR: Look at this! the Bogus Prophet Mike Palij has scissors. And Palij is trying to stab Mo-Man Sumler with the scissors. Mo-Man with a martial arts stance, and Mo-Man Sumler, OH MY GOODNESS, he is unloading on the Bogus Prophet like Jackie Chan in a feature movie. Lawler: Isn't this where they play that Kung Fu Fighting song? McMahon: Oh, look, I can't believe this. [name withheld] stole one of the IDF Jeeps and ran over [name withheld]. He just ran over the Cruiserweight champion. And [name withheld] is driving around the ring area. Everybody's scattering, but he runs into a crowd of fans, and now it looks like some of the fans in the front row are injured. Lalwer: Great. I knew this would happen in a league where Paul E. Dangerously has part interest. - JR: Wawczak now with punches to the gut of the Taskmistress. Now Wawczak with broadarm shots to Liz Michael. Wawczak WHIPS Taskmsitress to the ringpost. Goes in and a size 11 puppy to the face by the Taskmistress, and she clotheslines him to the bricks. McMahon: Now Stone Cold Paul Smith cleaning house. Stone Cold Paul Smith slugging the Truth Commission with that Jawbone of an Ass. Now [name withheld] with a bullwhip. [name withheld] with a whip takes down Stone Cold Paul Smith. JR: Lionwrath Zen Jericho in fisticuffs, in a boxing match with [name withheld], and old fashioned slobberknocker. McMahon: Liz Michael with the Singapore cane against Schlesinger who has an audience chair. Taskmsitress and Schlesinger dueling with the cane and the chair. Lawler: Twist, thrust, parry, thwack. JR: Schlesinger slaps the Taskmsitress down with the chair. Schlesinger now slapping the Taskmistress (bitch-slaps). Now the Truth Commission have the Taskmsitress cornered. [name withheld] smacks the Taskmistress with the Singapore cane. Mattie Carrington with the Mattie Death Drop from the top turnbuckle onto Liz. Now the Truth Commission tying up Liz with rope. They are going to trying to draw and quarter the Taskmistress. Lawler: This is what they did to William Wallace in Braveheart, McMahon. McMahon: Truth Commission now pulling at the ropes in four different directions. The Taskmistress screaming in pain. JR: Stone Cold Paul Smith with a chain! Stone Cold Paul Smith beating on the backs of the Truth Commission. Smith and [name withheld] in a slugfest now, Smith drops the chain. Schlesinger picks up the chain and starts choking Stone Cold Paul Smith. - Lawler: Wait a minute, McMahon! Here comes Conn MacKintosh! McMahon: Conn MacKintosh coming out as the VWO is bringing in reinforcements. Lawler: But whose side is he on, McMahon? McMahon: Don't be silly, he's VWO for Life. MacKintosh coming to ringside. The Truth Commission clears out of the way. Schlesinger and the gang want no part of a fresh Conn MacKintosh. JR: Wait a minute! What's he doin'? He's picking up Stone Cold Paul Smith. Lawler: He's helping him to his feet. JR: OH MY GAWD! He CHOKESLAMMED SMITH! Lawler: NOOOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! JR: MacKintosh picks up the Taskmistress, he puts her head between his legs, and HE POWERBOMBS HER! MACKINTOSH POWERBOMBED THE TASKMISTRESS! Lawler: Ladies and Gentlemen, CONN MACKINTOSH HAS TURNED ON THE VIRTUAL WORLD ORDER! JR: Now he has [name withheld]. He is patting Ryan on the back. Oh, no, what is this. McMahon: He steps back and DROPKICKS GRANT. And now MacKintosh, goes to the top of the turnbuckle, OH NO! JR: Conn Mackintosh drops to the ground with the Disk Drive Crash headbutt to [name withheld], his own tag partner. Lawler: I can't believe this, McMahon! This is TERRIBLE! JR: Lionwrath Zen Jericho with a plancha onto several Truth Commission memebers. McMahon: Well, the VWO realize what's going on. They are slowly recovering, and they all now realize Conn Mackintosh has betrayed the Virtual World Order. JR: POWERBOMB! POWERBOMB! [name withheld] powerbombs Stone Cold Paul Smith on the concrete. This is brutal. Mo-man Sumler with some martial arts kicks to several Truth Commission members. - McMahon: And here comes Dollywood Zylkin and the Honky Tonk Man. Lawler: I hope they don't sell out the VWO, too. Maybe they're gonna sing for us again, McMahon! JR: Double superkick to Conn MacKintosh by Mo-Man and Kare. McMahon: Now wait a minute. I see somebody up on the top of the Dome of the Rock. Somebody is up there on that golden dome, wait a minute, I don't believe it! THAT'S GOD! Lawler: Look at this, McMahon. God has something with him, and I can't see what it is. McMahon: God, now, making his way down from the Dome of the Rock, and God just may decide to intervene in this matchup. Menawhile, the VWO and the Truth Commission are brawling. JR: Guess what? Powrhug Kare is beating on [name withheld] with his own camcorder. [name withheld] backing off under a barrage of blows from the Hugger! McMahon: And Powerhug Kare with the Powrhug! Kare with the Hug of Doom on [name withheld]. But Mattie with a chair smashes Kare and breaks the hold. JR: Now Taskmistress pummeling [name withheld] from behind. [name withheld] with a whip kick, and now a crescent kick to Taskmistress puts Liz down. Now, [name withheld] taking the chair, and he's sitting in the chair, and he is dragging the Taskmistress across his lap and he is spanking the Taskmistress with that riding crop. [name withheld] with the riding crop. JERICHO WITH THE PLANCHA off the ropes. Lawler: Zen Jericho with sort of a wishbone-leg clothesline puts [name withheld] on the concrete. McMahon: Now, Schlesinger in the ring with the Taskmistress. Schlesinger dragging the weakened Taskmistress by the hair. Chop to the chest by Schlesinger. And Schlesinger spits on the Taskmsitress the way he spit on that Intergender Championship. Lawler: HERE COMES GOD, McMahon! McMahon: God is making his way to the ring with, it looks like two stone tablets. Lawler: Those are the Ten Commandments, McMahon! JR: Schelsinger hooking up the Taskmistress for the pedigree, but a stomp to the feet by Liz! McMahon: Rake to the eyes of Schlesinger by Liz Michael, and now the Taskmistress, in the corner, GOD talking to the Taskmistress, God handing the stone tablets the Taskmistress Liz Michael. And Liz Michael takes the stone tablets and she SMASHES THEM OVER Evan's head! Lawler: They exploded, McMahon! McMahon: The Stone Tablets exploded into fire when they struck Evan. Count of 1, 2, 3! (DING DING DING DING) - Lawler: I guess Evan Schlesinger is on fire for God! Get it? On fire! Ahahahahaha! (Music: Helter Skelter-Beatles) McMahon: And the Virtual World Order have won THIS battle, and the VWO is congregating in the ring, and ladies and gentlemen, it appears that GOD has become a member of the Virtual World Order! ANYTHING can happen in RSPWWCW! Lawler: Well, how are the VWO ever gonna lose a match? They have GOD on their side. McMahon: As the Taskmistress does the Kliq sign with God. And...... (BOOM! Fireflash, lightning!) Lawler: Be careful, there, Liz! That God is a real electric guy! JR: Taskmistress sort of laughing about getting her finger a little singed there. Dollywood Tom Zylkin presenting God with his very own VWO t-shirt. And wait a minute, we're not done yet..... McMahon: Schlesinger and the Truth Commission are back, and they have fire. JR: Schlesinger went and got the Aish Flame, and he's.....I don't believe this! He's trying to set the ring on fire. McMahon: Evan Schlesinger has set the ring on fire with the VWO in the ring.
