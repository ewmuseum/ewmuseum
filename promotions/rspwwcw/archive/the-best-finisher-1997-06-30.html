THE best finisher, 30 June 1997

30 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here are the finisher for the wrestlers I handle...tell me what you think! Diamondback Chris Myers: Rattler - A sideways DDT A basic DDT, except Myers holds his opponent to the side instead of the front The Wild Cards Dead Man's Hand - Suplex into a Gutbreaker Deck suplexes the man, and holds him in the air, ala the British Bulldog, while Ace gets down on one knee. Deck then spins and brings the man down onto Ace's knee, stomach first. It used to be a suplex into a backbreaker, but got banned because I broke someone's back with it :) The Syndicate Hostile Takeover - Extended Razor's Edge Sean White gets on the top turnbuckle, and he grabs his opponent and sets him up for a top-turnbuckle Razor's Edge. His partner, Dave Breslin, then picks Sean up with his shoulders, so Sean's sitting on his shoulders with the opponent still set up. Then....CRASH!!! Cutting Edge Direct Hostility - Spin Kick-Powerbomb combo Blade sets the opponent up for a powerbomb, but holds him at the top, so his face is in the guys stomach. Laser then comes off the top turnbuckle with a spin kick, and Blade uses the momemtum to finish the power bomb. The Stormreavers Twilight's Hammer - Running Shoulder Block Maim lies down and benches press his opponent, and no, Maim's shoulders are off the ground when he does it. Rend then runs to the ropes, rebounds, comes off the other ropes, and hits the poor sould with a shoulder ram right into the side, and the guy goes flying!
