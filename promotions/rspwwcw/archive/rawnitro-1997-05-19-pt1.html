RawNitro, 19 May 1997, Part 1

19 May 1997 (posted 20 May)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****************** RSPWWCW RawNitro 5/19 ****************** (RawNitro intro, whatever that is. Fireworks go off, and the crowd goes crazy. Camera goes to Vince McMahon and Jesse Ventura.) (Perfect symphany. Crowd cheers) McMahon: Hello everyone, and welcome to RSPWWCW RawNitro! I'm Vince McMahon along with Jesse "The Body" Ventura, and tonight we'll have two first round King of the Ring matchups! Ventura: The King of the Ring, where he see who's the REAL man! McMahon: Also, we have the new RSPWWCW champion, Jack Epstein of the JWO, defending the title against Evan Schlesinger. Ventura: A week ago I would've picked Schlesinger to win. Now, I'm going with Epstein. McMahon: And starting now is the Maritimer defending the Intergender title against ALL of the creatures! Maritimer has a microphone. ************************** RSPWWCW Intergender Matches: Maritimer vs. Creatures ************************** Maritimer: Shut up, McMahon. I'm here to prove that a man IS a better wrestler than a woman. Actually, we already knew that, but six pea-brained spread-eagled whores seem to disagree with me! All right, you slippery holes. Bring one out. Anyone, it doesn't matter. (Because the Night: Natalie Merchant) (Natasha walks out) Maritimer: Coming down the aisle, one of the stupid bitches known as the creatures, NATASHA THE QUIFF! McMahon: Oh my goodness! Ventura: Shut up, McMahon. He has to wrestle six people. He can say whatever he wants. (Bell rings. They lock up. Poke in the eyes by Maritimer. Picks up Natasha and gives her a back-breaker. Does it again. And again.) McMahon: Maritimer giving it to Natasha early. Ventura: He's working on the back, so he can set up the perfectplex. Wait a minute, he's going for it! McMahon: He is! And he perfect-plexes her! The ref counts, and it's over. That was quick. (Perfect symphony. Maritimer rolls Natasha out of the ring and picks up the mic) Maritimer: Cut the music! I knew she was easy, but I didn't know she was easy in the ring! Bring out the next one. (Because the Night: Natalie Merchant) (Sasskia walks out) Maritimer: Making her way to the ring, one of the smelly holes we know as creatures, SASSKIA THE SKANK! Ventura: One down, five to go. (Bell rings. They lock up. Sasskia rakes the Maritimer's eyes. He headbutts her in the nose, drawing blood.) Ventura: I think she pissed him off. McMahon: Maritimer, going behind Sasskia, and a back suplex! Nicely executed. Maritimer, in control, putting Sasskia in a front-face lock and I thinks he's going for the perfect-plex! He does it! The ref is there, and another count of three. Ventura: The Maritimer's making it look easy! (Perfect symphony. Maritimer rolls Sasskia out of the ring near Natasha and picks up the mic) Maritimer: Cut the music! I mean c'mon people, I haven't even broken a sweat yet. Bring someone else down. (Because the Night: Natalie Merchant) (Sable walks out, with white face paint and a bat.) Maritimer: And my opponent, making her way down to the ring, yet another of these women that try to be intimidating. She has a batting average of .121, and does NOT look good in makeup! Ladies and gentlemen, SABLE THE MIME! Ventura: Four left, Maritimer. (Bell rings. Ref tries to take the bat away from Sable.) McMahon: C'mon Sable, you know that's not legal. Finally the ref gets the bat, but Maritimer grabs her and back suplexes her! Ventura: He's not wasting any time, and I don't blame him. McMahon: Maritimer, now standing over Sable, and SLAPS HER! There's no need for that! Maritimer picking up Sable, and perfect-plexes her! Ventura: Sable is just too fragile to wrestle. McMahon: Count of 1 .. 2 .. 3. It's over. Ventura: Maritimer's now half-way there, and he's looking great! McMahon: Wait a minute, Natasha and Sasskia are attacking the Maritimer! They're pounding on him, and pick him up and double body-slam him! Ventura: Sable's crawling towards her bat! McMahon: She has it! Natasha and Sasskia are holding Maritimer, and Sable walks towards him. Oh no, NO! She swings, and hits Natasha! Ventura: Maritimer managed to pull Natasha in front of him! Now he's throwing Sasskia at Sable, and double-clotheslines them! (creatures scurry outside the ring and run back to the dressing room. Maritimer takes the mic) Maritimer: Okay, I think it's time for a break. Continue on with the show, folks, and hit the music! (Perfect symphony. Maritimer walks away) McMahon: Now I guess we'll be having a First Round matchup of the Ring of the Ring! It's Waru against Blakenstein of the JWO. ******************************** KOTR First Round Match: Waru vs. Blakenstein ******************************** (Princes of the Universe: Queen) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, the following contest is a first round, King of the Ring match-up! Currently entering the ring, the man they call WARU! (Baruch Et Adonai hymn) Finkel: And his opponent, walking to the ring accompanied by en4cer, one half of Perfection Personified, BLAKENSTEIN! McMahon: Alright, Waru against Blakenstein, in a first-round, King of the Ring matchup. (Bell rings. They lock up. Waru moves Blakenstein to the corner, and starts hitting him with forearms. Rams his shoulder in Blakenstein's midsection.) McMahon: Waru, taking the early advantage, now throws Blakenstein to the corner. He charges and Waru misses! Blakenstein slamming Waru. Now an elbowdrop. Goes for a cover, and a kickout at 2. Ventura: Blakenstein can beat Waru, but I don't think he can become King of the Ring. McMahon: Blakenstein, now sets up Waru and gives him a neckbreaker! Another cover, and again Waru kicks out at 2. Ventura: He's going to the top rope! Bad move. McMahon: Leaps of the top... and misses a flying headbutt! Both men slow to get up. Waru runs off the ropes and gives a clothesline to Blakenstein. Waru off the ropes again, and another clothesline. Waru now picking up Blakenstein and power-bombs him! A cover, 1 .. 2 .. he got him oh no he didn't. Ventura: You STILL say that? McMahon: Say what? Ventura: Never mind. McMahon: Waru with a legdrop, now an elbowdrop. (en4cer jumps on the ring aprin) Ventura: en4cer is getting involved and Waru sees him. (Waru goes over, as Blakenstein gets up. Waru grabs en4cer and slugs him. Blakenstein grabs Waru and German-suplexes him.) McMahon: Oh! What a manuever by Blakenstein! 1 .. 2.. and he got him! (Baruch Et Adonai hymn. Crowd boos) Finkel: The winner of the match, BLAKENSTEIN! McMahon: Blakenstein beats Waru to advance to the next round of the King of the Ring. Ventura: They suckered Waru, and he should've known better. (Waru leaves, and goes to the back. en4cer gets the mic) en4cer: See what we're talking about? We're a well-oiled machine, we ARE perfection, and we WILL win those tag team belts! (en4cer and Blakenstein leave) McMahon: Well, that was to the point. (perfect symphony) Ventura: Looks like Maritimer's ready for another match! ************************************* RSPWWCW Intergender Matches: Maritimer vs. Creatures (continued) ************************************* (Maritimer comes out to cheers and goes to the ring. Picks up a mic) Maritimer: Alright. I had my Skittles and I'm ready for another match, with three people left. There were four, but last night Deadshot and I had other plans. Hey Shawn! (holds out bottle full of white pills) Here's your smile! In case you're still looking for it, go to Lawton's drugs. I'm sure they can help you out... Anyway, whichever bitch wants to show up now, go right ahead. (Goldust theme. ATM LaRiche walks out, and the crowd boos) Maritimer: My opponent, who is wearing more leather than a reupholstered cow, but enough about her mother. She is a member of some kind of group, that I can't remember the name of, she is ATM "FAT ASS" LARICHE! Ventura: Maritimer's feeling braver as this evening goes on. (bell rings. Lock up, and LaRiche stomps on Maritimer's foot, then a rake to the eyes.) McMahon: LaRiche starting with some underhand tactics. Ventura: Hey, whatever works, McMahon. (LaRiche gives him a bodyslam, followed by an elbowdrop. Cover, and a count of 2. LaRiche gives the finger to the crowd. Crowd disagrees with her opinion and boo.) Ventura: That's one way to become popular. (LaRiche grabs Maritimer and gives him a snap-suplex. Picks up the Maritimer, and throws him off the ropes. He ducks a clothesline, they come back and he tackles her in the midsection.) Ventura: What is this, football? McMahon: And now the Maritimer is on the top and starts hitting her in the head! Ventura: Yeah, he's upset. (LaRiche rolls him over, and does the same to him. He flips her off, and the both get to their feet. He clotheslines her over the top rope, but she grabs him and they both go over. They get up, and Maritimer punches one of LaRiche's breasts.) McMahon: I can't believe he did that! Ventura: Heh. He must be REALLY upset. (LaRiche doubles over, and Maritimer gives her a piledriver. He throws her in the ring, and follows. Picks her up, and a backbreaker. A cover, and a count of 2. Runs off the ropes for a legdrop, but she rolls out of the way. She gets up and lays the boots to him. Throws him off the rope and gives him a boot to the head.) McMahon: LaRiche, picking up Maritimer and POWERBOMBS him! Ventura: Maritimer might not have to worry about the next two matches, because he could lose this one! McMahon: LaRiche going for the cover, 1 .. 2.. and Maritimer's foot is on the rope. Ventura: If they were in the centre of the ring, LaRiche probably would've won by now! (LaRiche starts kicking Maritimer. Sets him up for another suplex. It's blocked. Maritimer grabs one of her legs, and perfect-plexs her.) Ventura: I don't believe it! A perfectplex out of nowhere! McMahon: and a count of 3! It's over! (perfect symphony. Sable, Natasha, and Sasskia charge the ring. Maritimer sees them, exits and runs through the crowd.) McMahon: The creatures charged the ring, but Maritimer manages to escape. Ventura: Yeah, but he still has two more matches tonight, and he took a beating this match. McMahon: Mind you, LaRiche's title was NOT on the line, even though Maritimer's is. More to come folks, stay with us! End of hour one.
