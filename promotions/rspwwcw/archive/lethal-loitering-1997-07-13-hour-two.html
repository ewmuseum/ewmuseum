RSPWWCW Bash at the Beach: Lethal Loitering, 13 July 1997, Hour Two

13 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
******************************************************* ******************************************************* RSPWWCW Bash at the Beach: Lethal Loitering (hour two) ******************************************************* ******************************************************* ***************************************** Tag match #5: Kare/Myers vs. Waru/Lord13 ***************************************** ("I'm just a sexy girl") Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our FIFTH tag team matchup. Currently dancing in the ring, and a member of the Creatures, POWRHUG KARE! ("Smells like Teen Spirit" - Nirvana) Finkel: And her teammate, entering the ring, a member of the VWO, "DIAMONDBACK" CHRIS MYERS! ("Princes of the Universe" - Queen) Finkel: And their opponents, with one of them coming to the ring. He is from somewhere in Maine, he is... WARU! (Blackjacks theme) Finkel: And his teammate, from parts unknown, with a weight unknown. He is LORD13! (bell rings. Waru and Kare lock up. Waru backs Kare into a corner, and starts pounding. Throws Kare to the opposite turnbuckle. Charges, but Kare gets a boot up.) Heenan: I'm surprised with Kare right now. Ross: Why, that's she's holding her own? Heenan: No, that she hasn't gone nuts yet. Ross: Kare, now gives Waru a bulldog. Reaches to Myers for a tag. Myers is hesitating, Lawler: Watch your hand. She might bite it. Ross: Myers tags Kare. He bodyslams Waru and follows it with a kneedrop. A cover, and a kickout. Heenan: Looks like Myers doesn't want another Eddy/LaRiche situation. Ross: Myers throws Waru to the ropes. Waru reverses, and clotheslines Myers. Waru pulls Myers to Lord13, and tags him in. Lord13 throws Myers off the ropes, and gives him a spike slam. A cover, and a count of 1. Lawler: Myers better not lose, or else Kare might do something unpleasant. Ross: Lord13 sets up Myers for a piledriver, and succeeds. A cover, and Myers kicks out. Lord13, now going to the top rope, and MISSES a legdrop. Heenan: Both of you! Tag in! Ross: Myers crawls to his corner, and Kare tags in. Kare stomps on Lord13, and dropkicked Waru when he cames in. Lawler: AH! She's BITING Lord13! Ross: Oh my! Kare is BITING Lord13! Waru attacks Kare to stop her, and Myers now attacks Waru. Kare helps Myers and they double bodyslam Waru, then Lord13. They throw Waru off the ropes and backbodydrop him over the top rope! Heenan: Lord13 is a sitting duck! Myers is motioning for the diamond cutter! Ross: And he hits it! Lord13 falls to the mat. Myers runs off the ropes, and planchas Waru on the outside! Lawler: Kare is waiting for Lord13 to get up! Ross: Lord13 IS slowly getting up, and Kare hits him with the sweet bitch music! A cover, and it's over. ("I'm a sexy girl") Finkel: The winners of this match, CHRIS MYERS, and POWRHUG KARE! Ross: Kare and Myers, showing some VERY unexpected teamwork tonight. Heenan: Well, it worked. Now they're both gonna wrestle in the battle royale. Lawler: Uh oh. Kare biting 15 others! She'll need her shots! Get it, SHOTS? Ross: Oh, you're a riot. ********************************************* Tag match #6: [name withheld]/Grant vs. CanSen/Emond ********************************************* ("More More More" - Andrea True) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our SIX tag match of the evening. Currently in the ring, from Santa Monica, California, he is [name withheld]! ("Smoke on the Water" - Deep Purple) Finkel: And his partner, walking down the aisle, is a member of the VWO. He is, [name withheld]! ("O Canada") Finkel: Their opponents, first making his way to the ring, from Edmonton, Alberta, and the self-proclaimed RSPW net-cop, here is CANSEN! ("I'm a lumberjack" - Monty Python) Finkel: And his teammate, on his way to the ring, from Montreal, Quebec, he is the HEARTBURN KID, PATRICK EMOND! (Bell rings. [name withheld] and Emond lock up. [name withheld] shoves Emond to the mat.) Ross: [name withheld] showing his strength. They lock up again, and [name withheld] throws Emond off the ropes, and Emond hits with a bodypress! A cover, and a count of 1. Emond runs off the ropes again but is hit with a kneelift. An elbowdrop by [name withheld], followed by a kneedrop. He tags in Grant. Grant hits Emond with an axehandle. Goes for a bodyslam, but Emond blocks it, and bodyslams Grant. Heenan: Make the tag! You can't beat both by yourself! Ross: Emond bodyslams him a second time, then tags in CanSen, who hits with a legdrop. A cover, and a quick kickout. CanSen suplexes Grant. Lawler: Here they come again! Heenan: The VWO are coming back! Ross: CanSen hits a backbreaker, then misses an elbowdrop. Dollywood, Myers, MacKintosh, Eddy, and Henry are all outside the ring. Grant gives a jawbreaker to CanSen, then tags in [name withheld]. [name withheld] piledrives CanSen, and a cover for a count of 2. Heenan: Here they go. Ross: Conn jumped on the apron, and is distracting the ref as Myers gives Grant a chair! Lawler: That's not all they're doing! Ross: Now Myers grabs Emond's leg and pulls him to the floor. [name withheld] holds CanSen to be hit with the chair! Grant swings! Lawler: AH! He missed! Heenan: CanSen moved! Ross: And [name withheld] gets hit! CanSen dropkicks Grant out of the ring, and covers [name withheld]. The ref turns, and makes the count. He got him! ("O Canada") Finkel: Here are your winners, CANSEN and PATRICK EMOND! Lawler: Well, the VWO screwed up again! Heenan: And Myers is the only VWO member so far, that's made it to the battle royale! Ross: They have to get their act in gear, if the VWO needs to be as successful as they claim to be. Now we have, I would say, a definite main-event status tag match. Let's take to the Fink. *************************************************** Tag match #7: Deadshot/Liz vs. Dollywood/Maritimer *************************************************** (theme from Army of Darkness) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our SEVENTH match in tonight's card. On his way to the ring, weighing in at 320 lbs, DEADSHOT! ("Because the Night" - Natalie Merchant) Finkel: And his teammate, being accompanied to the ring by the Creatures, she is the current RSPWWCW Intergender Champion, LIZ MICHAEL! ("Sweet Home Alabama") Finkel: And their opponents, one of them making his way to the ring accompanied by the VWO, he is the current RSPWWCW Extreme Champion, DOLLYWOOD ZYLKIN! (Perfect Symphony) Finkel: And his teammate, from New Brunswick, Canada. Weighing in at 170 lbs, he is THE MARITIMER! Ross: I don't want to even TRY to guess this one! (Bell rings. Zylkin and Liz start it off. They lock up, and go to the ropes. Zylkin chops Liz, and Liz punches Zylkin.) Ross: Now they fall to the mat and keep fighting. Lawler: Go Liz! Heenan: Go Liz! Ross: Liz gets up and clotheslines Dollywood. Dollywood gets up and gets clotheslined again. Zylkin tags in Maritimer. Liz turns to tag and Deadshot is standing on the floor! Heenan: What in the world? Lawler: I don't think Deadshot wants to wrestle the Maritimer! Ross: Liz turns and gets a reverse atomic drop by the Maritimer, followed by a dropkick. Maritimer puts Liz in a headlock and tags in Zylkin. Zylkin kicks Liz in the gut and gives her a side suplex. Runs off the ropes and MISSES a legdrop. Heenan: Tag Liz! Lawler: Tag Liz! Ross: Liz tags in Deadshot, who shoulder tackles Zylkin. Zylkin turns to tag and now Maritimer is standing on the floor. Heenan: They must have talked about this before the match so they wouldn't have to wrestle each other. Ross: Oh, I wouldn't doubt it. Dollywood turns and is given a belly-to-belly by Deadshot. A standing splash. Deadshot tags in Liz, and starts slapping Zylkin. Now they're both standing just looking at each other. Lawler: What are they gonna do, make up? Ross: No, they just each tagged their partners! Now THEY'RE going outside in their groups as Maritimer and Deadshot look at them, then at each other. Heenan: Maritimer vs. Deadshot. The match that never happened. Ross: They approach each other, then lock up. Deadshot throws Maritimer to the mat. Maritimer jumps up, runs the ropes, and is hit by a shoulder tackle by Deadshot. Lawler: Maritimer can't do a thing to Deadshot! Ross: Maritimer runs off the ropes again, and dropkicks Deadshot. Runs off the ropes again, and hits with a clothesline. Deadshot is staggering! Heenan: Keep on him, Maritimer! Ross: Maritimer runs off the ropes, but planchas Liz instead! Lawler: WHAT? Ross: The Creatures are attacking Maritimer, and the VWO tries to get the Maritimer out of there. Heenan: I don't think Dollywood wants to face Deadshot. Ross: The Creatures throw in Liz as the VWO and Dollywood fight them. The ref is out of the ring trying to break it up. Lawler: Why is he doing that? Heenan: Stay in the ring, and you won't get hurt. Ross: Maritimer starts to enter the ring as Deadshot grabs Liz in a bear hug! Lawler: That's treason! Ross: Maritimer runs off the ropes and hits with a Hart Attack! Heenan: Do something Liz! Ross: Deadshot is climbing the top rope, and the Maritimer piledrives Liz. Lawler: Creatures, do something! Ross: They can't! They're all busy! Maritimer moves, and Deadshot hits Air Nova! Heenan: He hit Air Nova! No-one has EVER gotten up from that! Ross: The Maritimer now picks up Liz, and perfect-plexes her! Dollywood gets the ref in the ring. Lawler: Here comes Kare! Ross: But Deadshot stops her as the ref makes the count. One... Heenan: Kick out Liz! Ross: ... two ... Lawler and Heenan: NOOOOOOOO!!!! Ross: ... three! (Perfect symphony) Finkel: The winners of this match, DOLLYWOOD ZYLKIN and THE MARITIMER! Lawler: I can't believe this! This is so unfair! This was planned as soon as their names were drawn! Ross: They must have figured that the VWO would fight the Creatures, and they used it to get to Liz. She STILL hasn't gotten up yet. Heenan: Somebody do something. There, now she's being helped out. *************************************************** Tag match #8: MacKintosh/Mick vs. FunkyM/Tiszinkel *************************************************** Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our FINAL tag team match. Currently in the ring, a member of the VWO, CONN MACKINTOSH! (theme from the Tick) Finkel: And his teammate, coming down the aisle, the MICK! (Flash Funk theme) Finkel: And their opponents, making his way down to the ring, he is "too kewl", FUNKYM! (Baruch hymn) Finkel: And his teammate, on his way to the ring, and is the current RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Champion. He is a member of the JWO, he is STEVE TISZINKEL! (Bell rings. Mick and FunkyM start off.) Ross: They lock up, and here comes the VWO *again*. Brain: They must feel cocky because of the last match. Ross: They grab Tiszinkel, and start pounding on him! Lawler: Here comes the JWO! Ross: And they're attacking the VWO as Epstein grabs MacKintosh. The VWO and the JWO are fighting outside the ring, as FunkyM hits the Mick with a spinning kick. A standing moonsault, and a count of 2. Heenan: The VWO and JWO aren't stopping for anything, and I don't think Conn and Tiszinkel are getting in the ring anytime soon! Ross: Mick pokes FunkyM in the eyes, and gives him a reverse atomic drop. Now a back-breaker. Mick lifts him again, and a side suplex. A cover, and a count of 2. Lawler: Instead of a tag match, all we really have is a singles match! Ross: Mick throws FunkyM to the turnbuckles. Charges, but FunkyM flips over him and the Mick hits the corner. The Mick turns, and FunkyM jumps up and gives him a hurracurrana. Heenan: That's hard to pronounce and harder to spell. Ross: A cover, and a count of 2. FunkyM throws Mick into the corner, but Mick reverses. FunkyM jumps straight to the top turnbuckle, and hits the Mick with a moonsault! Lawler: Conn better do something! Ross: He can't! A count, and FunkyM gets him! (Flash Funk theme) Finkel: Here are your winners, STEVE TISZINKEL and FUNKYM! (end of hour two) -- The Maritimer - A member of the "New Brunswickans for a Live RAW campaign" (NBLRC) NBLRC website: http://NBLRC.home.ml.org Remember: I am what I say I am, and I say, I'm PERFECT!
