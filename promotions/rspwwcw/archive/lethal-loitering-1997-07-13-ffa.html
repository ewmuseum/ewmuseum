RSPWWCW Bash at the Beach: Lethal Loitering, 13 July 1997, Drawing and Free-For-All

13 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Black and white footage, with voiceover.) 40 wrestlers. 5 titles. 8 tag matches. 16-man battle royal. And one ring. When they are combined, the results can be more than dangerous. They can be lethal. (End footage. Free-For-All symbol dances on the screen.) Ross: Hello everyone, and welcome to the RSPWWCW Lethal Loitering Free-For-All! I'm good ol' J.R., along with the King Jerry Lawler and Bobby "The Brain" Heenan. Folks, do we have a PPV in store for you! 40 wrestlers, 32 to be chosen to participate in 8 tag matches. Lawler: Not only that Ross, but the remaining eight will wrestle in a Survivor Series type, elimination match-up. Ross: Plus the winners of the tag matches will continue on to compete in a 16-man battle royale! Heenan: With the winner not just getting a title shot, but maybe even a tag-title shot. Ross: That's right, Bobby Heenan. The winner.. (Perfect symphany. The Maritimer walks out. Crowd boos.) Ross: Ladies and gentlemen, it seems that the Maritimer will be joining us at the broadcast table. Heenan: At last, someone that is almost as smart as me! (Maritimer sits down and puts on headset.) Ross: Thank you Maritimer, for joining us. Maritimer: For you Jimmy, no problem. Did you know that I'm the reason for the Lethal Loitering? Lawler: You? Slightly inflated opinion, even for you. Maritimer: You see, I went to the RSPWWCW championship committee and asked for this PPV. With all of the different gangs going on around here, I thought it would be interesting to see how people from different gangs would wrestle if they teamed up. Heenan: Yeah, someone from the VWO could team up with one of the Creatures.. Maritimer: Or it could be a JWO/VWO team-up. Or maybe none of them. That's the beauty. Now Ross, weren't you going over the rules? Ross: Yes I was. Now, the winner of the battle royale will either get a singles title shot, or a tag-title shot. It's their choice. If they want a tag title shot, they can choose their own partner. However, the singles title shot will vary with each wrestler. After the drawing, the 32 wrestlers in the tag matches will be divided into five categories: World, IG, TV, Extreme, and Cruiser. If that person wins the battle royale, he or she will get a shot at that title. Lawler: Isn't there an exception in there somewhere? Ross: Yes there is, King. If the World champion, in this case Steve Tiszinkel, wins it all, then he can go for the tag titles, if he chooses. He does NOT get a title match. Heenan: In a way that's good, but then again, you don't get a chance for another belt. Maritimer: Well he could go for the tag titles, but for singles titles, where do you go after the World title? Ross: The 40 participants are: Jeff Amdur, [name withheld], [name withheld], Blakenstein, Gern Blanston, CanSen, Crunch, Deadshot, Death, Alex Demchuk, DoMinAA, Robert Wallace Eddy, Heartburn Kid Patrick Emond, Jack Epstein, en4cer, Jeremy Fadner, FunkyM, [name withheld], Hatchet, John Henry, Jamal, Powrhug Kare, 1-Stu-3 Kid, ATM LaRiche, Lord13, Conn MacKintosh, Mike Malecki, The Maritimer, Miracle Max, Liz Micheal, The Mick, Diamondback Chris Myers, Osmarus, Jeremy Soria, Stone Cold Paul Smith, Stunning Steve Tiszinkel, Kelly Vaters, Waru, Kim Wright, and Dollywood Zylkin. Heenan: I'm sorry Ross, I missed that. Could you repeat it? ("I know you want me" plays. Sunny comes out in a string bikini, and the crowd goes apeshit.) Ross: Sunny is coming out to the ring to draw the names, and I never thought I'd see three grown men literally drooling. (sounds of static) Lawler: Wha? Oh, uh... Heenan: That is, uh... Maritimer: Man, she gives me a woody. Ross: Um, thank you for sharing that, Maritimer. Sunny now in the ring with the barrel full of names. Sunny: Helloooooo, Miami! (crowd goes nuts) Sunny: Right now we're going to start the draw to see who goes in the tag matches, and who gets teamed up with who. Heenan: "Who" isn't in it! Maritimer: I wish Sunny was in it... Lawler: You wouldn't be wrestling, though. Maritimer: Well, not in the ring... Ross: Okay, that's enough. All of you. Sunny: For those of you who don't know, the first 32 people to be drawn, are put in 8 tag matches against each other. The teams and opponents are made in order of being drawn. So without further ado... (Turns the barrel in a seductive manner. Don't ask how.) Ross: This is it folks! The drawing for the 1997 Lethal Loitering! (Sunny begins drawing names) Sunny: The first one... is Robert Wallace Eddy ... and Alexandra Theresa LaRiche! Lawler: Woah! Sunny: Their opponents, ... Jeff Amdur... and Blakenstein! Ross: Two JWO team members get to team up! Against two people who'll be fighting each other! Sunny: For the second tag team match... (draws names) The first one is the en4cer! Maritimer: JWO being taken early... Sunny: And his partner... the 1-Stu-3 Kid! Maritimer: Yeah! He's UNB for life, don't you know. Sunny: Their opponents... Stone Cold Paul Smith! Lawler: Woah! Heenan: He isn't gonna NEED a partner! Sunny: And his partner... Jack Epstein! Heenan: Oh my goodness... Ross: Epstein and Smith! I don't think they'll care if it's a tag match or not.. Sunny: Now for the NEXT tag team match... Lawler: I think they should make a Sunny PPV. Sunny: The first... is John Henry, and his teammate... 'The Genius' Gern Blanston! Heenan: Makes sense, since he likes to rhyme everything. Sunny: Their opponents... Crunch ... and Hatchet! Ross: New Demolition get drawn together! This is a definite advantage on their part. Sunny: For the fourth tag team match, the first one drawn is... Jeremy Fadner! Lawler: Hopefully his "partner" won't be too upset.. Sunny: And his teammate... Heenan: Oh, she didn't say partner. Sunny: ... is Mike Malecki! Maritimer: Um, I don't think Malecki's gonna like this. Sunny: And their opponents... Kelly Vaters ... and 'The Canadian Crippler', Alex Demchuk! Maritimer: ooo, two Canucks teaming up. This'll be fun. Sunny: Now for the fifth tag match... Lawler: We're half done. Heenan: Got a cigarette? Sunny: ... Powrhug Kare... and Diamondback Chris Myers! Ross: ANOTHER Creatures/VWO matchup! Sunny: And their opponents... Lord13... and Waru! Maritimer: Too close to call this one. Sunny: Our next match... Maritimer: That's it. Bend over.. Ross: Maritimer, please! Maritimer: What, you wanted to say that? Sunny: ... has [name withheld]... and [name withheld]! Sunny: Their opponents... CanSen... and Patrick Emond! Heenan: ... Maritimer: ... Lawler: ... Well, THAT was anti-climatic! Sunny: We're now down to our seventh match, and the first one is... Deadshot! Maritimer: Please team up with me, please team up with me... Sunny: And his partner... Liz Michael! Maritimer: Great. I don't think Deadshot will focus on helping his teammate. Sunny: And their opponents... Dollywood Zylkin... Lawler: Zylkin and Liz get to butt heads?!? Maritimer: Fine by me. Sunny: ... and The Maritimer! Maritimer: huh? Ross: You're going up against Deadshot! Maritimer: huh? Heenan: While teaming up with Dollywood! Maritimer: Well, shit a brick. Sunny: And now our FINAL tag match... has The Mick... teamed up with Conn MacKintosh! Heenan: You realize Tiszinkel hasn't been drawn yet? Ross: It may happen. Sunny: And their opponents... FunkyM... and Steve Tiszinkel! Maritimer: Talk about making it under the wire! Ross: The teams are now made up! While Sunny draws the names for the elimination match, we'll be going to the back with Scheme Gene! Gene: Thank you guys. The groupings for the titles are now completed for the 32 wrestlers. Steve Tiszinkel of course doesn't go under a title, since he is the World Champ. The five titles go as follows: World: Paul Smith, Jack Epstein, Powrhug Kare, Waru, Deadshot, Liz Michael, and Dollywood Zylkin. Intergender: ATM LaRiche, Jeff Amdur, John Henry, [name withheld], Ryan Grant, and The Maritimer. Cruiser: Robert Wallace Eddy, 1-Stu-3 Kid, Hatchet, Kelly Vaters, The Mick, and FunkyM. Extreme: Blakenstein, Gern Blanston, Crunch, Mike Malecki, Alex Demchuk, and Lord13. TV: en4cer, Jeremy Fadner, Chris Myers, CanSen, Patrick Emond, and Conn MacKintosh. Gene: That's it from here. Back to ringside. Ross: Thank you Scheme Gene. Well, the elimation match has been drawn, the teams are as follows: Miracle Max / [name withheld] / Death / DoMinAA vs Kim Wright / Jamal / Osmarus / Jeremy Soria Ross: This concludes the Free-For-All segment of the show. Any last thoughts, Maritimer? Maritimer: Yeah, after I'm done beating Liz like the bitch she is, I'll go through all 15 others in the battle royale. Then I get to choose either my IG title, or our tag titles. Either way, they're MINE. Ross: Um, okay. That's it for the free-for-all, we'll see you at the beach! (credits roll) -- The Maritimer - A member of the "New Brunswickans for a Live RAW campaign" (NBLRC) NBLRC website: http://NBLRC.home.ml.org Remember: I am what I say I am, and I say, I'm PERFECT!
