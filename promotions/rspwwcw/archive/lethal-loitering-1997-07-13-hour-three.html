RSPWWCW Bash at the Beach: Lethal Loitering, 13 July 1997, Hour Three

13 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********************************************************* ********************************************************* RSPWWCW Bash at the Beach: Lethal Loitering (hour three) ********************************************************* ********************************************************* ********************************* Elimination tag match: Miracle Max/Benson/Death/DominAA vs Wright/Jamal/Osmarus/Soria ********************************* Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, it is now time for the Elimination tag match! The winning team are the ones who can pin everyone on the opposing team. (Undertaker theme) Finkel: On their way down the ring, with a combined weight of 732 pounds, MIRACLE MAX, [name withheld], DEATH, AND DOMINAA. ("Poison" - Alice Cooper) Finkel: And their opponents, beginning their walk down to the ring, weighing in at a combined weight of 697 pounds. They are, KIM WRIGHT, JAMAL, OSMARUS, AND JEREMY SORIA! Ross: Wright is the RSPWWCW Women's champion and Osmarus is the TV champ! Heenan: I can't see Wright's team losing. (bell rings. Max and Jamal start off. Jamal throws Max off the ropes and hits a spinning kick. Ross: Jamal with the early advantage, and follows up with an elbowdrip. He tags in Soria, who hits Max with a forearm. Heenan: I'm taking a nap. Wake me when it's over. Ross: Max pokes Soria in the eyes, and hits a DDT! Max goes to his corner and tags in Benson. Benson bodyslams Soria, and covers for a count of 2. Lawler: Who cares. Get Wright in there. DoMinAA would make my day too. Ross: Benson is bending over Soria, and is grabbed at the crotch! Lawler: AH! Ross: Soria lifts Benson three feet in the air, and slams him! Lawler: Would that constitute as a choke slam? Ross: Soria tags in Osmarus, who gives Benson a power bomb. A cover, and a pin. Lawler: It's now four on three. Ross: Death jumps Osmarus, and hits him in the neck. Death runs off the ropes for a clothesline. Lawler: I never knew Death had it in him. Ross: Osmarus rakes Death's eyes, and tags in Jamal. Jamal dropkicks Death, and runs off the ropes. He's going for a bodypress but Death catches him! He turns Jamal, and tombstones him! Cover, count of 3. Lawler: One move? That's all Death needed? Brrrr... Ross: Wright comes in and starts hitting Death with kicks to the head. Lawler: Now THERE'S a woman. No offense to Liz. Ross: Death is staggering back to his corner and tags Max. Max comes in and is hit with a spinning heel kick! Lawler: Watch those legs! Ross: Wright hits Max in the gut with a thrust kick, and knocks him out with a cresent kick! A cover, and that's it. Lawler: Wright's team has the advantage again. Ross: Death comes in and clotheslines Wright. A bodyslam, now an elbow, and a cover for a count of 2. Lawler: Don't let him tombstone you Kim. Ross: Death chokes her on the ropes, and is given a crotch shot by Wright. Lawler: Who does she think she is, Soria? Ross: Both are staggering to their corners. Wright tags in Soria, and Death tags in DoMinAA. DoMinAA charges and hits Soria head with a flying head butt! Lawler: OW! Ross: Now DoMinAA takes off her high heeled boots and hits Soria's nose! Lawler: Look at the blood! I think it's broken! Ross: I think so too. DoMinAA with a DDT, and a cover. She gets the pin. Lawler: That girl is serious trouble. Ross: Osmarus comes in and grabs DoMinAA by the throat and chokeslams her! Lawler: AH! Get away, get away! Ross: She rolls to her corner and tags in Death. Death and Osmarus start pounding on each other, and it looks like Osmarus is getting the advantage. Lawler: He's over seven feet tall, for goodness sake! Ross: Osmarus gorilla presses Death, and slams him. And now a powerbomb! But he's not covering him... Lawler: Cover him! Don't take stupid chances! Ross: Osmarus tags in Wright, and holds Death in a bear hug. Wright climbs the turnbuckle and hits death with a spinning kick! Lawler: Yeah, that's worse. Ross: A cover, and a count of three. Lawler: Now it's DoMinAA against the two champs. Ross: DoMinAA grabs Wright and rakes her eyes. Now she's biting on Wright's ear! Lawler: Holy Tyson, Batman! Heenan: (yawn) Any calls while I was out? Ross: All you've been missing is a fast-faced elimination match. DoMinAA now knees Wright in the groin! Lawler: I wonder if that hurts as much as them as it does for us? Ross: Wright falls to the mat, and DoMinAA stomps on her knee. Now an elbowdrop. DoMinAA, choking Wright for four seconds, then releases, and now she's doing it again. Follows it with a kneedrop. A cover, and a kickout at 2. Now DoMinAA is going to the top rope. Heenan: She needs to if she wants to pin Wright. Ross: She leaps and MISSES a flying headbutt! Wright tags in Osmarus, who grabs DoMinAA, and powerbombs her! Lawler: That should be it! Ross: But he powerbombs her again! I don't believe this! Cover her already! And he powerbombs her again. Heenan: Now he covers her. Ross: Do I really need to say it's a three count? ("Poison" - Alice Cooper) Finkel: The winners of the match, KIM WRIGHT and OSMARUS! Ross: I think Osmarus took it a little too far tonight. Heenan: Hey, it got him the victory, didn't it? Ross: But he didn't NEED to powerbomb her three times! Anyway... ******************** 16-man battle royale ******************** Ross: Now, while the wrestlers are coming to the ring, let's go to Scheme Gene and see which wrestlers are eligible for which belts. Gene: Thank you Jim Ross. All 16 participants ARE eligible to go for the tag belts, if they choose. Steve Tiszinkel, if he wins, does not get a title shot, as he is the World champion. For the World title we have Paul Smith, Jack Epstein, Powrhug Kare, and Dollywood as the participant that would get a shot at this belt of one of them won the battle royale. The Intergender title has Jeff Amdur, and the Maritimer as the only two people for this belt. The Cruiserweight title has Hatchet, Kelly Vaters, and FunkyM as those who could get a chance for this title. The Extreme title has Blakenstein, Crunch and Alex Demchuk for possible contenders should one of them win tonight. And finally, the Television title has Chris Myers, CanSen, and Patrick Emond for that belt's contenders. Back to you guys at ringside. Ross: Thank you Scheme Gene. Right now the Baruch hymn is playing as the final four participants are entering the ring; Amdur, Blakenstein, Epstein, and Tiszinkel. All four are in it, and all four are in the JWO. Heenan: Considering that's a quarter of all the people in the ring, that's a pretty good advantage. (Bell rings. All Hell Breaks Loose) Ross: The action is underway! Kare is fighting Smith, Crunch and Hatchet are double teaming Demchuk, and FunkyM is fighting Myers. Lawler: There's Vaters, she might have Epstein gone... Ross: But the other JWO members save him. Heenan: There's Dollywood and Maritimer pounding on each other. Ross: Those two were tag partners earlier, but all that is behind them as Maritimer clotheslines Dollywood out of the ring! Casualty number one! Lawler: Now the New Demolition is double teaming Paul Smith! Ross: They hit him with the decapitation! Hatchet is still stomping away at Smith as Crunch battles Emond. Heenan: FunkyM is dropkicking everyone! Ross: He dropkicked Blakenstein, then Epstein, then Amdur, now Tiszinkel. He's taking on the JWO, but now they're pounding away at him! Lawler: Look at Vaters! Ross: Kelly Vaters going to the top rope, but she's knocked off by CanSen, and she falls to the floor! Heenan: Two down, 14 to go! Ross: Folks we want to remind you that tomorrow night RSPWWCW will be coming from the Wrestle Vessel for RawNitro! You'll see mixed tag matches between RSPWWCW and WWF superstars, like Powrhug Kare/Shawn Michaels vs. The Maritimer/Bret Hart. Join us tomorrow night, and it looks like Hatchet is gone as well! Heenan: Emond dropkicked him over the top rope, and now Crunch is getting his revenge. Ross: Folks we're past the five minute mark of this battle royale. Three wrestlers have been eliminated, and there are 13 left to go. Lawler: Blakenstein's gone! Ross: No wait, no he isn't. He held on. Demchuk threw him over, but Blakenstein held on to the ropes. He caught up to Demchuk now, and gives him a hurracurrana! Heenan: They're both going over! Ross: But Blakenstein held on to the ropes again. Demchuk however, is eliminated. Lawler: Amdur has FunkyM over! Ross: But FunkyM isn't out! Emond comes over and stops Amdur. Epstein and Tiszinkel are trying to get the big Crunch off his feet, but it's not working. Heenan: The Maritimer almost has CanSen gone! Ross: CanSen trying to hang on, and the Maritimer is kneeing him in the back. Oh, it worked! CanSen is now eliminated. Lawler: Paul Smith is back on his feet. Ross: Indeed he is. After being decapitated by the New Demolition, Paul Smith was out of it. He goes to Crunch, and gives him the Stunner! He grabs the next person he can find, which is Tizsinkel, and gives him the stunner too! Emond is trying to stop Smith, but he gets a boot to the groin for his trouble, and a toss to the outside, via over the top! Heenan: How far are we now? Ross: We're now just over ten minutes and six people have been eliminated. That leaves twelve people left, four of whom are the JWO. Lawler: FunkyM is leaping to the top rope! Ross: The last one to try this was Vaters and she got eliminated. FunkyM leaps, and hits Crunch! But Crunch hangs on! He grabs FunkyM and lifts him over his head! He carries him over.. and dumps FunkyM over the side! Lawler: Now how's THAT for a crash, crash, crash? Ross: Epstein and Tiszinkel, going to work on the Maritimer. Maritimer pokes Tiszinkel in the eyes, and dropkicks Epstein. Epstein goes out of the ring, but through the ropes, not over. So he's not eliminated. Epstein, on his way back in, as Tiszinkel throws Maritimer to the ropes. Maritimer reverses and Tiszinkel is GONE thanks to the Maritimer, and EPSTEIN? Heenan: Epstein grabbed the ropes to get up on the apron when Tiszinkel bounced off the ropes just at the same time! Ross: And now Myers and Smith attack Epstein. Meanwhile Amdur is almost gone, thanks to Crunch! Lawler: Blakenstein is running over to help Amdur! Ross: Blakenstein charges towards Crunch and.. Crunch moves! Blakenstein ends up eliminated Amdur! Heenan: That's TWO member of the JWO, being eliminated in part by the JWO! Ross: Now EPSTEIN is almost over the top, and Myers and Smith are leaning pretty heavily towards the outstide. Lawler: Here comes Blakenstein again... Ross: Blakenstein grabs Myers and Smith, but knocks them more off-balance and Epstein is eliminated! Heenan: Blakenstein is the biggest threat to the JWO, and he's a member! Ross: Crunch is trying to capitalize on Smith and charges, but Smith bends over for a bodydrop! Lawler: He's too heavy! Crunch is too heavy! Ross: Smith can't lift Crunch because Crunch weighs so much! Heenan: KARE! She's BITING CRUNCH IN THE BUTT! Ross: She is! And Crunch is wiggling, which is helping Paul Smith! He lifts, and Crunch is over! Lawler: I think Kare should be called Crunch now! Ross: Now's she's biting Paul Smith in the head! Myers comes over, and throws out Smith! Heenan: WOW! How many left? Ross: We are almost 20 minutes into this, and the only ones left are Blakenstein, Kare, Myers, and the Maritimer. When Myers threw Smith over, Kare almost went as well! But now he's trying to get her over! Heenan: Maritimer and Blakenstein are coming over, and they're helping Myers! Ross: She's gone! Powrhug Kare is gone! Lawler: I guess those guys didn't want to be bitten... Ross: Maritimer and Myers are duking it out. Maritimer hits Myers with a reverse atomic drop, then levels him with a forearm. Blakenstein gets behind Maritimer and back suplexes him over the top! Heenan: That leaves only two left! Ross: Myers tackles Blakenstein and they're both going over the top! Who will be eliminated? Is it Myers, is it Blake, is it.... It's Blakenstein! Myers held on the ropes! Myers wins! ("Smells like Teen Spirit" - Nirvana) Ross: Myers wins the battle royale and has a shot at either the tag titles or the TV title! Scheme Gene is entering the ring, let's find out. Gene: Congradulations Chris Myers on your victory. Unfortunately we're running short on time, so before we go, do you know which title you'll go for? Myers: (laughs) WHICH DO YOU THINK? That scrawny-assed, silly-named Osmarus robbed me of my Television title! It's only fitting that I do the same to him! Gene: So you're gunning for the TV title? Myers: DUH! That's MY title, Osmarus. Because remember, when you're VWO, you're VWO.... FOR ... LIFE!! Ross: We have to go folks, see you tomorrow night from the Wrestle Vessel! (end of show) -- The Maritimer - A member of the "New Brunswickans for a Live RAW campaign" (NBLRC) NBLRC website: http://NBLRC.home.ml.org Remember: I am what I say I am, and I say, I'm PERFECT!
