Spamboree, 19 May 1997, Part 1

19 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
*************** Spamboree 1997 *************** (Black and white footage, with voiceover) (Each name mentioned is shown with their belt) Mattie Carrington... ATM LaRiche... The Taskmistress... The Blue Avenger... The Maritimer... Dollywood Zylkin... The American Women... Each holds something very precious to them. Each stands to lose that same precious item. Some view defending a title can be hell on earth. For these people... ...they're living it... (footage ends) (fireworks go off. Crowd erupts. Tony Schiavone and Bobby Heenan commentating.) Tony: Hello everyone, and welcome to Spamboree! I'm Tony Schiavone with Bobby "The Brain" Heenan, and what a card do we have for you tonight! He have not one, not two, but SIX title matches tonight, with all seven titles on the line! Brain: Plus we also have a 20-man battle royal, with the winner getting a title match tomorrow night on RawNitro! Tony: Right now let's take you to Howard Finkel. Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this opening contest, is for the RSPWWCW Television championship! ****************** TV Title Match: Mattie Carrington vs. Deadshot (w/K-A Shoot) ****************** (We are the Champions: Queen) (Deadshot shows up, followed my K-A Shoot. Crowd cheers) Finkel: Coming to the ring, along with this attendant K-A Chute, from Wolfville, Nova Scotia: Deadshot! (sign in the crowd: Deadshot is a sure-shot for the title!) Tony: So that's his attendant, huh? Brain: What a beauty! At least she's not as big as Deadshot! (Theme from Dynasty plays) (Carrington walks to the ring, belt over shoulder) Finkel: And his opponent, coming to the ring as the RSPWWCW Television Champion: Mattie Carrington! Tony: Wow. She and Ms. Chute are about the same size. Brain: Is that a fat joke? Are you making fun of their size? Tony: No, of course not. Brain: I'm telling Deadshot. (Match begins. Deadshot and Carrington locks up. Deadshot backs her up to the turnbuckle. Begins laying forearms to her. Throws her to the turnbuckle. Charges, but Carrington moves out the way. She begins to kick Deadshot in the midsection.) Tony: You know, Brain, I'm wondering if the creatures will show up in this match. Deadshot DID attack Shawn Micheals along with The Maritimer during the free-for-all. Brain: If they did, I'd have been told. Tony: So are they? Brain: Are they what? Tony: Never mind. (Carrington chokes Deadshot over the ropes. Breaks at four. Goes for a bodyslam, but can't lift him up.) Tony: Carrington seems to have problems handling the weight of Deadshot. Brain: Is that another fat joke? Tony: What? Brain: That's it. I'm DEFINITELY telling Deadshot! (Deadshot reverses, and bodyslams Carrington. Splash from standing position. Throws her to the turnbuckle. Charges again, and this times hits her with an avalanche. Throws her to the ropes and gives her a powerslam.) Tony: Deadshot having his way with Carrington. Brain: I thought that was what K-A Shoot was for. Tony: Deadshot now climbing the turnbuckles! He could be going for Air Nova! Brain: Really? I thought he was going to attempt a flying dropkick. (As Deadshot gets to the top turnbuckle, Sasskia and Natasha charge the ring) Tony: Look! It's Sasskia and Natasha of the creatures! Brain: But K-A's taking care of them by herself! (K-A hits both of them, and starting beating on them. Kare and LaRiche charge the ring) Tony: And now it's Powrhug Kare and ATM LaRiche! Deadshot sees them, but jumps anyway! He hits it! Air Nova! Brain: Carrington is finished! (Kare jumps to the apron and distracts the ref as LaRiche enters the ring. Deadshot gets up, grabs her and throws her over the top rope on the other side.) Tony: And now The Maritimer's here! (crowd goes nuts. Maritimer grabs Kare and hauls her to the floor. They start fighting. LaRiche re-enters the ring and Deadshot re-throws her out.) Brain: The Taskmistress! She's here! She's here! Tony: And she's going after K-A Shoot! LaRiche is now helping Kare against the Maritimer! K-A Shoot turns around and LIZ HITS HER WITH A SUPER-KICK! Brain: Deadshot sees it! Tony: But Carrington's now getting up! She charges at Deadshot, and clotheslines both him and her over the top rope! Now Liz, Kare, and LaRiche are after Deadshot! Maritimer's coming around to help, and double-clotheslines LaRiche and Kare! Brain: Carrington's getting up again... Tony: And so's K-A Shoot. She's walking over to Carrington, and GIVES HER A BELLY-TO-BELLY! Liz and Deadshot duking it out. Maritimer tackles her and Deadshot rolls back in the ring. Brain: The ref's still counting! Shoot is pushing Carrington into the ring. (bell rings) What is it, a count-out? Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of this bout, as a result of a count-out, Deadshot! Tony: But he doesn't get the TV title. Brain: Look at him, he's pissed. Tony: And now the creatures, sans Liz, are entering the ring! They're pounding on Deadshot! Liz and Maritimer are battling it outside the ring! The officials are entering the ring, trying to break them up! Brain: Deadshot is really pissed. Tony: Deadshot is exiting the ring area, and is leaving along with K-A and the Maritimer. (Deadshot grabs a camera. Shouts into it) Deadshot: I am sick.. and tired.. of this BULLSHIT! Let's see how THEY like it! Tony: We apologize fans, for Deadshot's choice of words. Let's take you now, to backstage with Scheme Gene! Gene: (pause) Thank you Tony Schiavone and Bobby "The Brain" Heenan. Joining me now is the Commissioner of RSPWWCW, DugganFan. DugganFan: HOOOOOOO!!!! Gene: Now Commissioner, please explain to everyone watching why you had everyone in the tag-team title match wrestle in a singles title match, and why for our next match, you're have ATM LaRiche defend against a fellow creature in Powrhug Kare! DugganFan: You know tough-guy, when the Maritimer won the IG title last week, he came up to 'ol DugganFan and he said that no matter what, we was gonna defend that title tonight as well as wrestle for the tag titles! Now ol' DugganFan thought that it'd be only fair for everyone else in that match to wrestle an extra match as well. And to make sure that gave it their all, he put them each in a title match. Now as for Kare and LaRiche, let ol' DugganFan say right now that if it doesn't look like BOTH of you are trying in your match, then I'm VACATING the title, tough-guy! HOOOOOOOOO!!! Gene: Oh my goodness! Back to ringside! ****************************** Cruiserweight title match: ATM LaRiche vs. Powrhug Kare ****************************** Tony: Wow! Now that's something, Brain. Brain: It doesn't take someone of my intellect to realize that Kare and LaRiche have little choice but to give it their all. ("I'm just a sexy girl") Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is for the RSPWWCW Cruiserweight championship! Coming down the aisle, one half of the RSPWWCW tag-team champions, the challenger, POWRHUG KARE! (Goldust theme) Finkel: And her opponent, making her way to the ring area, the RSPWWCW Cruiserweight champion, ATM LARICHE! (Bell rings. They lock up. LaRiche hiptosses Kare to the mat. Follows up with a dropkick, then a clothesline.) Tony: LaRiche dominating early. Brain: There's a pun if I ever heard one. (LaRiche backs Kare into a corner, throws her, Kare reverses and gives LaRiche a short-arm clothesline. Goes for an elbowdrop and connects. Slaps on a chin-lock.) Tony: Well, so far I think they're putting effort into it. Brain: No doubt, but are they putting ALL of their effort into it? (LaRiche gets to her feet, and throws Kare to the ropes. Goes for a clothesline, Kare ducks, and connects with a flying forearm. Covers LaRiche for a count of 2.) Tony: Wow, what a seesaw matchup we have here tonight. Brain: Both of these ladies can win the match with only one or two moves, and they each know it. (Both get to their feet. Kare charges with a clothesline, and LaRiche backbodydrops her over the top rope.) Tony: OH! What a backbodydrop! Now LaRiche runs off the ropes, oh and a plancha through the ropes on top of Kare! Brain: As long as LaRiche doesn't make any mistakes, the match is hers. (LaRiche throws Kare back in the ring, and goes to the top turnbuckle. Kare gets up and dropkicks LaRiche, making her straddle the turnbuckle.) Brain: Ow. I wonder if it hurts as much to a woman as it does to a man. Tony: I haven't really thought about it and I don't WANT to think about it. Kare's climbing the corner, grabs LaRiche and SUPERPLEXES her to the mat! Both are slow getting up... (Two guys wearing black masks and pants charge the ring.) Tony: Wait a minute, who are THOSE guys? They're wearing some black leather outfit with studs on them... Brain: It looks like they're wearing Demolition gear. But they don't LOOK like Ax and Smash. One of these guys is quite bigger than the other. Tony: They double clothesline the girls. They're now pounding LaRiche with double axehandles. The larger guy gives her a back-breaker and he's holding it. The smaller guy climbs to the top rope and ELBOWDROPS HER! Brain: Hey, that's the Demolition Decapitation! Tony: Kare is staggering to her feet. One of them kicks her in the gut and they're hitting her with double axehandles as well. Brain: What is going on here?!? Tony: And now they're giving her the decapitation as well! Brain: HERE COMES LIZ! Tony: And the creatures are right behind her! (The two guys leave the ring and go through the crowd.) Tony: Those guys are running, but the damage has been done. It looks like they'll need some medics, and it's quite obvious that this is a no-contest. Brain: Who were those guys anyway? Tony: I have no idea.. (camera goes to broadcast booth) Okay, while they're taking care of business in the ring, let's talk about RawNitro tomorrow night. It was originally slated for Saint John, New Brunswick, but instead it's been moved to Halifax, Nova Scotia. On the card there'll be two King of the Ring first round match-ups (interupted by some guy coming out to the booth) Person: Who was responsible for moving RawNitro to Halifax? Tony: Excuse me? And you are..? Person: I'm Jeff Despres, the human Tealshark. Brain: What's a Tealshark? Despres: It doesn't matter. Who moved the card to Halifax? Tony: Um, we don't know... Despres: Fine. I'll find out from someone else.. (leaves) (Tony and Heenan look at each other, and shrug) Tony: Okay folks, as we said tomorrow night (stops. Listens to headset) Ladies and gentlemen, Scheme Gene is with the two men who made the attack earlier. Take it Scheme Gene. Gene: Alright thank you gentlemen. I'm in one of the washrooms with these two. First of all, who are you? Smaller guy: I'm Hatchet and he's Crunch. And we're the NEW Demolition. Crunch: Different team, but same philosophy, and that's us kicking everyone's teeth right down their stinkin' throats! Gene: Alright. Any chance of you two taking off your masks? Crunch: None whatsoever, runt. Gene: Fine. Why the attack? Hatchet: Why not? We're here, kicking some ass, and loving every minute of it! Gene: So will you run in during the next match? Crunch: Maybe, maybe not. Tell you what, you puny little geek. We promise NOT to run in during the next match. Okay? Gene: Okay. What a minute. Your builds.. so familiar.. of course! I know why you ran in during last match! You're the.. (The New Demolition then proceed to pound Scheme Gene. Hatchet picks up the fallen mic.) Hatchet: Back to you guys at ringside. HA! Tony: Did you see that? They attacked Scheme Gene! Brain: About time... Tony: What was that? Brain: Nothing. *********************************** Women's Title Match: The Taskmistress vs. Tigress *********************************** ("Eye of the Tiger") Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest for the RSPWWCW Women's title! Coming down the aisle, TIGRESS! ("Because the Night": Natalie Merchant) Finkel: And her opponent, making her way to the ring, she is one half of the RSPWWCW tag-team champions, and the RSPWWCW women's champion, the TASKMISTRESS, LIZ MICHAEL! Tony: Boy are we in for a match now! Brain: These ladies HATE each other! (Bell rings. Liz and Tigress lock up. Tigress monkeyflips Liz to the mat. Puts Liz in an armbar. Liz slides around and scissorlocks Tigress's head. Tigress puts her feet on the ropes and they break it up.) Tony: Very quick pace to this matchup so far.. Brain: They're trying to set the other up for a mistake. (They lock up again. Liz knees Tigress in the midsection, then DDT's her.) Tony: What a DDT by the Taskmistress. Tigress to starting to get up, but the Taskmistress has her.. Brain: Is she going for a piledriver? Tony: It looks like it, and she hits it! Brain: The Taskmistress is all over Tigress, not that she'd mind. Tony: Liz is now going to the opposite corner, is she going to try it? Tigress to her feet, here comes Liz, Tigress turns and SWEET BITCH MUSIC! Brain: Stick a fork in her, she's done! Tony: The Taskmistress covering her.. and gets the pin. ("Because the Night": Natalie Merchant) End of hour one.
