RawNitro Is War from Calgary, 4 August 1997

4 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Date: Fri, 08 Aug 1997 21:35:51 -0300 From: The Maritimer < [detail withheld] > Organization: Royal Canadian Kilted Yaksman To: [detail withheld] Subject: RawNitro Part one ******************** RSPWWCW RawNitro: Part one ******************** (Fireworks go off. Crowd cheers. Tony Schiavone and Bobby Heenan commentating.) Tony: Ladies and gentlemen, WELCOME to RSPWWCW RawNitro, coming to you LIVE from Calgary, Alberta, Canada. I'm Tony Schiavone, and I'm being joined by Bobby "The Brain" Heenan. Brain: Don't believe everything that you hear, folks. We're still live, and we aren't changing that in the least! Tony: Let's take you down to Finkel for our first match. ********************************************** RSPWWCW Women's title: Kim Wright vs. DoMinAA ********************************************** ("Because the Night" - Natalie Merchant. Mixed reaction) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is set for ONE fall, and is for the RSPWWCW Women's championship. Making her way down the aisle, she is a member of the Creatures of the Night, DOMINAA! Tony: DoMinAA not being accompanied by the other creatures of the night. Brain: And since she's from Canada, there's actually some cheers for her. ("Poison" - Alice Cooper. Crowd cheers) Finkel: And her opponent, hailing from Ireland. She is the RSPWWCW women's champion, KIM WRIGHT! Tony: DoMinAA getting a title shot tonight because of her pinning Wright last night on Summer Scam. (Bell rings. They lock up, and Wright throws DoMinAA to the mat.) Brain: Wright with a HUGE advantage over DoMinAA. No pun intended. Tony: DoMinAA now grabbing Wright's leg and trying to pull her down, but it's not working! Wright, now kicking her. DoMinAA, getting to her feet, and is back OFF her feet with a roundhouse kick! Brain: Are we going to need to time this match? Tony: DoMinAA, getting to her feet again, and here comes Wright with a superkick. OH NO! Wright hit the referee! DoMinAA ducked, and the referee took the kick! Brain: It's the creatures! Tony: Liz is here! LaRiche is here! Natasha as well! Brain: The professer and Mary Anne! Tony: What? Brain: Never mind. Tony: Anyway, they're entering the ring, but Wright is kciking them all! Natasha is out of the ring! Now LaRiche! Liz might hold her own, NO! She goes over the top rope via a superkick! Brain: Now THERE'S irony. Tony: DoMinAA has a chair! She went and got a chair! Wright is turning around, and GETS the chair RIGHT on the head! Brain: Good night, Irene! Tony: DoMinAA makes a cover as Liz arouses the offical. Brain: You know, you COULD have worded that differently. Tony: The ref is making the count. One.... two.... THREE! We have a new Women's champion! (Bell rings. "Because the night" - Natalie Merchant) Finkel: The winner of the match, and NEW RSPWWCW women's champion, DOMINAA! Brain: Whoda thunk it? Tony: Amazing, unbelievable, call it what you will, but it was an upset in the highest order. Now let's take you to Scheme Gene, who's in the back with Stone Cold Paul Smith. Gene: Thank you Tony. Paul Smith, you interfered in Waru's matchup last night, causing him to interfere in your title match. Any.. Smith: Shut up, you bald freak. Let me tell you something. Waru is an idiotic piece of trash that don't deserve to hold no belt, and that includes the Intergender title! Waru, if you're half the man I think you're not, then I challenge you for the IG title at Smog Wild. I'm so confident in knowing that I'll whip the hell out of ya, if I don't win, then I WILL kiss your ass! And that's the bottom line, 'cause Stone Cold says so! Gene: Uh, thank you very much, back to ringside! Tony: Alright, now we're scheduled for a TV title match, with Myers and the Mick, (crowd boos) Tony: What's going on? Brain: It's Dollywood Zylkin, he's heading to the ring. Tony: What? I guess you're right Brain. Zylkin is currently in the ring, and is getting a microphone.. Zylkin: SHUT UP! (crowd boos) What the BLOODY HELL is going on here? We have Michelle Steiner last night acting like she actually HAS power, after she was exposed for the fraud that she was! PLUS, she's supposed to be nothing more than a bloody cripple, after what happened to her! Brain: She got better. Zylkin: You know what? I am SICK and TIRED of my input being either ignored, or changed. I'm bloody well SICK of it! I have had it up to HERE with the bloody bullshit that RSPWWCW has become, and I'm prepared to do something about it! I want to wrestle Steiner at Smog Wild, and the LOSER has to LEAVE RSPWWCW forever! It's either HER, or ME! Steiner: Hey, you stupid Brit. (crowd cheers) Tony: She's on the big screen! Brain: I'm not commenting on THAT, either! Steiner: You want me? You got it! Just remember, don't the door hit you on the ass on the way out! (screen fades) Zylkin: Well that's bloody well GOOD! Because it's either MY way, or the HIGHway, you bloody little tramp! Tony: Um, okay. Zylkin, now leaving. Stay tuned for the second part of RawNitro, which will have the TV title, and the Tag titles up for grabs! ******************* RSPWWCW RawNitro: Part two ******************* **************************************************************** RSPWWCW Televsion Title: "Diamondback" Chris Myers vs. The Mick **************************************************************** (Theme from the Tick) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is scheduled for one fall, and is for the RSPWWCW Television Title! Currently in the ring, the MICK! Heenan: Nice intro. ("Smells like Teen Spirit" - Nirvana) Finkel: And his opponent, he is the RSPWWCW Television champion, "Diamondback" CHRIS MYERS! Tony: Myers is still pleased on re-winning the TV title from Osmarus last night. Brain: NO! You think? (Bell rings. Myers and Mick lock up. Myers with a hip-toss.) Tony: Now Myers with a dropkick, and a clothesline over the top rope! Mick is staggering out of the ring, and Myers runs off the ropes with a PLANCHA! Brain: Myers is STILL feeling the adrenalin rush. I just hope he doesn't get too foolish. Tony: Myers throwing the Mick back in the ring, and is applied a reverse chinlock. Tony: Folks, I want to remind you that this Saturday, August 9th RSPWWCW will be returning to PPV with SMOG WILD. Six, count 'em, SIX RSPWWCW titles will be on the line, plus we'll have Liz Michael against Powrhug Kare and ATM LaRiche vs. Robert Wallace Eddy. Call your local internet company today! (in the meantime, the Mick has gained control, and had spike-slamed Myers, then side-suplexed him.) Tony: The Mick, now going to the top rope... Brain: Myers is getting to his feet. Tony: The Mick jumps off and OHHH! Did you see that? Myers had turned and gave the Mick a diamond cutter as he was flying from the top rope! Brain: What a finishing move! Tony: The ref with the cover, one... two... three... and it's over! (Bell rings. "Smells like Teen Spirit" - Nirvana) Finkel: The winner of this match, and STILL RSPWWCW Television Champion, "Diamondback" Chris Myers! Tony: An outstanding win for Myers, and that leads us up to our main event. The tag team championship is on the line, with the newly crowned Perfection Personified defending the titles against the team who won the four-corner tag match last night, New Demolition. ******************************************************************* RSPWWCW Tag Title Match: Perfection Personified vs. New Demolition ******************************************************************* (Demolition Theme. Crowd boos) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, our following contest is our MAIN event, and is scheduled for one fall, or bandwith space remaining. Coming down the aisle are the challengers, from parts unknown. They are Crunch, Hatchet, the NEW DEMOLITION! (Baruch hymn. Crowd erupts) Finkel: And their opponents, on their way to the ring. They are the current RSPWWCW tag team champions. Blakenstein, en4cer, PERFECTION PERSONIFIED! Tony: Blakenstein being cheered on by his fellow Calgarians. (bell rings. en4cer and Hatchet face off) Tony: Hatchet elbowing en4cer in the jaw to start things off, as hold on. Wait a minute. Brain: What the hell? Coming down the aisle, is that... (en4cer gives Hatchet a jaw breaker) Tony: Crunch and Hatchet? But they're in the ring! (en4cer puts Hatchet in an armbar and tags Blakenstein) Brain: The Crunch in the aisle is wearing a black T-shirt like he sometimes does. Does this mean there's four members to New Demolition? (Blakenstein kicks Hatchet in the gut and gives him a back breaker) Tony: If that's the case, then who are they? (Hatchet pokes Blakenstein in the eyes and bodyslams him) Brain: I think that's pretty much the point. What I wanna know is, which two are the REAL members? (Hatchet tags Crunch, who elbowdrops Blakenstein) Tony: The ones in the ring, I suppose. (Crunch gives Blakenstein a belly-to-belly) Brain: Now the other two are coming down the aisle! (Crunch runs off the ropes and misses a splash) Tony: Blakenstein starting to go the corner, and he sees the other two members outside the ring! He's stunned. Crunch grabs him and puts him in a bear hug! He goes over to the Demos corner, as the two outside are going to en4cer's corner. Brain: The ref is looking for a tag, and en4cer, en4cer is getting attacked! Tony: Crunch tags Hatchet, and Hatchet runs off the ropes for a... Hart Attack? Brain: A HART attack? You don't mean... Tony: Hatchet picking up Blakenstein as en4cer get pummeled. Brain: Where's the JWO? Tony: Hatchet with a ... PERFECTPLEX? Brain: WHAT? Tony: The ref is shocked! But he's counting! Two and.. three! What in the world? Brain: Crunch is climbing the top ropes! Tony: Crunch leaps and SPLASHES Blakenstein! Brain: Like Air Nova! Tony: Oh no... (Crunch and Hatchet remove their masks to reveal Deadshot and Maritimer, respectively) Finkel: The winners of the match, and NEW RSPWWCW Tag team champions, the New Demolition? the Perfect Canadians? Tony: What's going on? Maritimer with the mic.. Maritimer: Greetings folks. Before we get any rumors started, I am NOT into leather! Now, in case you were wondering, yes. Deadshot are the original "New Demolition". Now the problem with that is the fact that the Demos and the Canucks were BOUND to bump heads. Therefore, we had to get "backup" members. Ladies and gentlemen, I introduce to you, K.A. Shoot as psuedo-Crunch and the 1-Stu-3 Kid as psuedo-Hatchet! Brain: Wow! I don't believe this! Maritimer: The New Demos were a back-up plan, JUST in case the Perfect Canadians lost the titles. That way, we could have TWICE as much chance as winning the tag titles. Brain: Makes sense. Tony: You're going along with this? Brain: I just said it makes sense! Maritimer: Then we had the four-corners match. Slight problem, since K.A. can't wrestle. So had Deadshot suffer an "injury" so he could wrestle as Crunch. New Demos win the match, and NOBODY suspects a thing. Maritimer: As of now, the New Demolition no longer exist. Instead we have the FOUR time RSPWWCW tag team champions, the PERFECT CANADIANS! Tony: How twisted a mind must you have to think of something like this? Maritimer: Now, as of the rest of the JWO, they were... occupied. You see, as seemly superstables come and go, a bunch of us decided to form a... coalition of sorts. A.. CANADIAN coalition. Not a stable, but an alliance. Right now it's the CMA that has the JWO busy in the back. They're members. We're members. It's an open offer for any Canadian wrestler. Join the coalition. We will wrestle each other from time to time, but it's better to see a Canadian champ fighting a Canadian challenger than to see an American champ at all. Tony: What the.. is that DoMinAA? Brain: It is! She's heading to the ring! DoMinAA: Maritimer, let me join you. I didn't need the creatures before tonight, I didn't need the creatures during the match, and I don't need the creatures EVER! I QUIT! From now on, call me... Memery! Maritimer: Memery, WELCOME to the Canadian Coalition! Tony: Here comes the creatures, but we're out of time! Folks, join us for Smog Wild! (end of show.) -- The Maritimer - A member of the "New Brunswickans for a Live RAW campaign" (NBLRC) NBLRC website: http://NBLRC.home.ml.org Remember: I am what I say I am, and I say, I'm PERFECT!
