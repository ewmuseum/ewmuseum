RSPWF Card Posting...Regulations?, 30 November 1997

30 November 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
> >Hello. I was wondering if anyone else thinks that there should be a standard >set of rules for people who post their federations' cards on this newsgroup. At first I might have said yes, but after thinking about it I would have to no. The two first questions that come up are who would decide the rules and how would you enforce them? >I am just getting sick and tired of the garbage that people are posting, Everyone's idea of garbage is different. What you are sick of might be entertaining to someone else. >claiming that they are the best federation, when basically, their >card quality sucks! Like I said it's all personal opinion. The reason that some people make these claims is because it's hard for them to be objective about things that they write. Also everyone has different taste. Take me for example. I started seeing some posts by Liz the Taskmistress called RSPWWCW RAWNITRO (or something like that) I thought it was crap, but some people like it. If you've seen them and the responses they draw then you will know what I mean. > If you would like to be involved in setting up a set of >rules, I have a couple of suggestions: As i'm sure everyone does. >1) All cards and PPV's posted MUST be from federations that >contain ORIGINAL WRESTLERS AND TAG TEAMS ONLY! I >was just looking at one card that had Sean Waltman (1-2-3 Kid, >Syxx of nWo), not to mention the tastelessness of the card and its >quality. Bottom line, if you have real wrestlers in your federation, >don't post the card to RSPWF! First you show some ignorance with this statement. I used to feel the same way about real wrestler feds until I actually listened to someone else explain the good points. With real wrestling feds the characters, feuds and angles are already there. This makes it easy for some fed heads and handlers. This is good for some newcomers to e-wrestling who aren't used to rp'ing and are trying to get used to the game. Second some people actually like to play their favorite wrestlers and don't want to make up their own. After all the newsgroup is called fantasy wrestling and e-wrestling with real wrestlers is still fantasy wrestling. >2) Don't post your cards or PPV's in 10 or 15 different parts. If it is >a good card, It should be divided into hours. Have a part for hour >1, and another for hour 2, and, if possible, a part for hour 3. This >way, you don't have to post as manny messages, and your >readers don't loose interest by the time they are done reading the >8 part. Some cards are so long that they won't fit on one or two messages and have to be stretched out over 8 parts. There is a limit to how much fits into a single post. I don't see how people could lose interest in a card simply because it is spread over 8 postings. >these are only a couple of suggestions that we can make >regulations for posting on RSPWF. Let's keep the garbage out, >and let the good stuff in! One man's garbage is another man's gold. I see what your saying and agee somewhat, but you have to keep an open mind and take the good with the bad. Some problems with the newsgroup will not change so you'll just have to ignore them (I still have a problem with this as most of you have seen). Take a look over at rec.sports.pro-wrestling. and see how crowded and cluttered it is over there with all the spammers and immature kids posting pure crap. Compared to there the RSPWF is heaven. >------------------------------------------- >"The Saint" [name withheld] >President/Owner of >New Internet Fantasy Wrestling! > http://ewrestling.com/nifw/ The Admiral The Excellence of Arrogance
