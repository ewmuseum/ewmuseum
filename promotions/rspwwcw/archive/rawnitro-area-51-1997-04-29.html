RAWNITRO IS ORTINWAR!!! For other Rawnitro Cards, click here Return to Wrestling Central For the Best of RSPW Matches, click here RAWNITRO IS ORTINWAR!!! 4/28/97 From Area 51 Announcers: Vince McMahon and Jim Ross-Ring Announcer: Howard Finkel Taylor Creed/Brian Wawczak (Nation of Moderation) vs. The Diamond Studds (Rich in KC/Diamondback Chris Myers Stone Cold Held Hostage The Heartbreak Babe Powrhug Kare vs. The Maritimer RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Championship Dollywood Tom Zylkin vs. Waru Benjamin Netanyahu vs. Yassir Arafat For the custody Rights to Stone Cold Paul Smith (Music-X-Files Theme) [The cameras show an empty room with only a ring and an announce crew ...as the fans quite frankly didn’t know where the hell to go...] - VM: Hello, everyone, I’m Vince McMahon along with Jim Ross and welcome to RAWNITRO IS ORTINWAR!!! It’s an eerie feeling, being in a smaller room than ECW’s bingo hall, but hey, we’re broadcasting live from Area 51. JR: Problem is, Vince, that they stripsearched all of us before they let us within 10 miles of the base.... VM: Well, who took you? JR: Don’t know...let’s get on with it, let’s just say that half of me didn’t get up when she stuck her thumb in my... VM: Let’s go to the ring and Howard Finkel!!! Taylor Creed/Brian Wawczak (Nation of Moderation) vs. The Diamond Studds (Rich in KC/Diamondback Chris Myers - (Mod Squad theme) HF: Vince and Jim, and fans at home, this contest is scheduled for one fall, and is a special tag team attraction...introducing first, representing the Nation of Moderation, the team of Taylor Creed and Brian Wawczak! = JR: Taylor Creed and Wawczak come out, and what’s this? Wawczak is grabbing the ring mic.... Wawczak: [META] Hey, that comment about half of you not getting up is non-wrestling related, and therefore, doesn’t belong on RSPW. (whines) See, if we were moderated, this problem wouldn’t happen.... - VM: As much as I appreciate your input, shove it. Let’s get back to the ring. - (Music-Smells Like Teen Spirit-Nirvana) HF: And their opponents....representing the Virtual World Order, the team of Rich In KC Schmiric, and Chris Myers, the Diamond Studds!!!!! - JR: Both of these guys are very talented, and part of the VWO! Can’t you just feel the electricity in the air? VM: JR, that’s probably from the electric walls in the room.... - JR: Whatever, as Schmiric and Myers get into the ring...Creed and Wawczak jump ‘em and this slobberknocker is underway!!! VM: Schmiric has the upper hand on Wawczak, and Creed and Myers are going at it...they’re going at it...Myers is thrown over the top by Creed and Rich is in a 2-on-1 situation here! Rich is trying his hardest to get out of this, but he’s having a hard time! JR: Oh, this is not good...Wawczak sets Schmiric on his shoulders and Creed goes up top...could he be using Moderation’s Death Drop? VM: Creed goes for a leap off the top and Schmiric spins it around.....frankensteiner! JR: Creed has hit head-first! Creed is in deep stuff now! VM: Schmiric goes after Creed and Creed is helpless....Wawczak is still hurt from the frankensteiner...he can’t help his partner, and Rich in KC is ready to rock ‘n’ roll! Rich walks over to Wawczak....... JR: Where the hell is Myers? VM: He is getting back in his corner.....he is recuperating..Rich doesn’t need any help right now.... JR: Rich has Wawczak set up in the corner, and he picks up Creed... they’re in opposite corners and he irish whips Creed right into Wawczak! Creed staggers out and he whips Creed back into the corner, he grabs Wawczak and he whips him into Creed! Wawczak staggers out now, and Rich CATCHES HIM IN THE DIAMOND CUTTER!!! HE COVERS...1..2..3!!!! - (Music-Smells Like Teen Spirit-Nirvana) HF: Here are your winners, the team of Rich In KC Schmiric, and Chris Myers, the Diamond Studds!!! - VM: What a match!!! Oh my God!!! Myers and Schmiric are victorious, and they have to be considered serious contenders to the World Tag Team Championship! JR: Well, Vince, if there’s any justice in this world, these two studs will be in a title match very soon! Return to Index Stone Cold Held Hostage VM: Ladies and gentlemen, you all are probably wondering what has happened to Stone Cold Paul Smith. Well, this video footage will hopefully explain much of the mystery. - (VIDEO) - HF: Introducing first, representing the Virtual World Order, originally from Victoria Texas, but now residing in Flint, Michigan, Stone Cold Paul Smith!!! - JR: That’s all nice, but where IS Smith??? - - McMahon voiceover: Well, Stone Cold Paul Smith had been detained in Jerusalem.....the PLO forces and the Israeli armies were both claiming him as a prisoner... - [Benjamin Netanyahu and Yasser Arafat are in the middle of an argument regarding the peace process] - BN: We’re keeping this part... YA: No way, it’s ours! SCPS: Listen up, son, I’m claiming this land in the name of whoopass, and that’s the bottom line because Stone Cold said so! BN: Who are you? SCPS: I’m your worst god damned nightmare. BN: What are you doing? SCPS: Watch this... (kicks Netanyahu in the stomach and slaps the Stone Cold Stunner on him...) SCPS: ...and I don’t give a rat’s ass if it IS Passover.... (proceeds to stuff Netanyahu’s mouth with leavened bread. Meanwhile, Arafat is laughing at Netanyahu...) SCPS: ...What the hell are you smiling for, son? You didn’t do a damn thing to help me out here.... (kicks Arafat in the stomach and puts the Stunner on him, and is immediately carted away by Israeli police...) - (end of Video) - VM: Well, that certainly is something, but even more revealing is this videotape in the PLO/Israel negotiations about Smith..... (snip) Israeli Negotiator: Smith belongs to us, because he attacked our man first. (snip) PLO Negotiator: Smith belongs to us, because he was arrested after attacking OUR man... (snip) JR: Eventually, from what I’ve heard, we will have a match between Yasser Arafat and Benjamin Netanyahu in our main event tonight, and the winner gets the rights to Stone Cold Paul Smith’s incarceration..... VM: Well, is he here? JR: Yes he is, I’m being told, and that's one of the reasons that RAWNITRO comes from Area 51 tonight..... VM: Well, let’s get back to the ring for our next match...... Return to Index The Heartbreak Babe Powrhug Kare vs. The Maritimer (the Perfect Symphony) HF: Ladies and Gentlemen, this contest is scheduled for one fall! Introducing first, from New Brunwick, Canada, The Maritimer!!!!! VM: He took a lengthy vacation, no word if he found his smile or not.... - (Music-I'm Just A Sexy Girl) HF: His opponent, from the Virtual World Order, "The Heartbreak Babe" Powrhug Kare!!!!! VM: OH MY GOD! Can she wear that on TV? JR: Who knows.....but I’m sure that she’ll turn Maritimer on with that...hell, that outfit would make Jeremy Soria straight! - VM: Ha! It’d probably make Bischoff straight too....but that’s another story...anyways, Maritimer is trying to get an early advantage by attacking right off the bat...he’s bludgeoning Kare with rights and lefts...oh man... JR: Kare is being leveled by the Maritimer....he is just a lumbering man...really a big powerful guy.... VM: Kare is being backed into the corner, Maritimer with a Flair-esque chop to the Powrhug....she is totally unaffected by it..... JR: Kare spins Maritimer around and begins kicking him in the chest... she’s working him over, she leaps up and delivers a monkey flip! Maritimer is down hard! Kare jumps to the top rope... VM: She leaps off with a bodyblock on Maritimer and catches it.... JR: She better watch out or part of her is going to fall out there... VM: She covers...1..2..3no!!! He kicked out! He kicked out!! Kare runs the ropes...rebounds and scores with a jumping stomp!!! She legdrops Maritimer...covers..1..2..KICKOUT!!!!! JR: Maritimer is getting back up to his feet, Kare picks him up and puts him in a rear chinlock...Kare is putting the squeeze on...Maritimer is back to a vertical base and he elbows Kare in the stomach...she lets go and he hits the ropes...she goes for the Sweet Bitch Music and MISSES!!! VM: Maritimer kicks her in the gut and catches her in the PERFECTPLEX!!! The ref counts...1..2..3!!!!! - (the Perfect Symphony) HF: The winner of this match, one-half of the Perfect Canadians, MARITIMER!!!!! JR: This match was a toughie all around and Maritimer deserves this victory. Return to Index RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Championship Dollywood Tom Zylkin vs. Waru VM: We have a commotion in the back...it’s between DugganFan and Benjamin Netanyahu.......it’s nothing big at all, but it must pertain to the match later on this evening... (Music-Princes of the Universe-Queen) JR: Well, let’s go to the semi-main event!!! HF: This contest is for one fall! Introducing first, from who knows where, WARU!!!!!!!! VM: This guy is one of the toughest RSPWWCW champions in the history of this league! He’s going to take it to this guy coming up.... - (Music-Sweet Home Alabama-Lynyrd Skynyrd) HF: His opponent is from the VWO, ladies and gentlemen, "Dollywood" Tom Zylkin!!! JR: No Honky Tonk Man? VM: Apparently not, he’s even shaved his sideburns!!! - JR: WARU ATTACKS ZYLKIN!!! Zylkin is down in the ring and who knows how he can recover from that stunning dropkick... VM: Waru sets him throat first on the second ropes...Waru bounces and nails him with a guillotine splash! Waru picks him up and hits him with an inverted suplex!!! JR: Aw, Waru is a house on fire! Waru climbs up top and moonsaults onto Zylkin! He counts..1..2..Waru picks him up! Waru is just going nuts here! Kicks left and right and man oh man........he takes Zylkin down with an enzuigiri!!! Zylkin is hurt! Who knows if something has been jarred loose upstairs? VM: Well, Schlesinger or someone else attacked the Taskmistress, and that’s why she is not here tonight on RAWNITRO Is ORTINWAR... JR: What? Another thing about Lizzy? VM: Yeah...she took quite a blow from Evan... JR: He blew her? I didnt know he even liked her!! VM: Take it however you will.....Waru picks up Zylkin, and he’s going for a tombstone piledriver!!! He can’t hold Zylkin up and falls down.... I think he’ll try it again....he lifts Zylkin up and again lets him fall ...third time’s a charm...he picks up again and FALLS DOWN...Zylkin has a leg sleeper on Waru!!! JR: That’s a choke out-figure four sleeperhold by Tom Zylkin! Waru is down and he hit REALLY hard.....Waru is fading...his arm is dropped once...twice...THREE! THIS MATCH IS OVER!!!! - (Music-Sweet Home Alabama-Lynyrd Skynyrd) HF: Here is your winner, and STILL RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Champion, Dollywood Tom Zylkin!!! Return to Index Benjamin Netanyahu vs. Yassir Arafat For the custody Rights to Stone Cold Paul Smith VM: Wow...well, it’s the match for Stone Cold’s rights, let’s get to the ring! Let’s get it over with! - HF: This contest is scheduled for one fall, introducing first, representing the Jew World Order, from Israel, Benjamin Netanyahu!!!!! VM: He’s with the Jew World Order? JR: Apparently so, here are Jeff Amdur and Didi Chanoch accompanying him to the ring, and they’re all wearing their yarmulkes..... - HF: His opponent, from Palestine, accompanied by the Iron Sheik, and representing the PLO, Yasser Arafat!!! VM: Arafat with the Sheik? JR: Stranger things have happened! VM: Arafat and Netanyahu are in the ring...I think they forget what this match is about...and Paul Smith is being led out by joint Israeli and Palestinean forces..... JR: Smith is jawing with both Netanyahu and Arafat, and Netanyahu is especially offended, he made him break his religion when he stuffed that bread in his mouth! VM: I Never understood that..... JR: You don’t need to understand it..... VM: Well, Arafat jumps out at Netanyahu and hits him with a lariat! Netanyahu is OUT OF IT.. JR: Of course he is, he’s not a wrestler.... VM: Iron Sheik hands Arafat a chair..... JR: ARABIAN FACEBUSTER BY ARAFAT!!! Netanyahu is hurt BAD! VM: Netanyahu is going out if Arafat goes for what I think he will... JR: He’s setting him up for the camel clutch!!!! - VM: He retches...he falls back....something is happening OH MY GOD... what is that? JR: ALL HELL HAS BROKEN LOOSE...WHAT CAN ONLY BE TERMED AS AN ALIEN LIFE FORM HAS JUST EMERGED FROM ARAFAT’S STOMACH!!! VM: DEAR GOD! ONE JUST CAME OUT OF NETANYAHU’S STOMACH TOO!!! JR: Who’s that coming out.... (both): IT’S BILL CLINTON!!! VM: He’s wearing a VWO T-Shirt and he is doing the "Bischoff Smile"..... JR: Clinton limps into the ring and tells the soldiers to let Smith go...they do and run down to tend to their leaders...... - VM: Smith gets a mic.... SCPS: Who’s back, son? I’ll tell you what, I was in that damned hellhole in the middle of the desert and both the matzoballs and the ragheads tried to say they owned me...well, ain’t nobody owning me but me, and if anyone tries to prove otherwise, they can kiss my ass! The bottom line is, Bill Clinton is in the VWO, and if any of you little third world countries try to step to my ass-kicking friend, remember...Who’s got the nukes??? - (With that, he throws down the mic and walks out of the ring with Clinton) - VM: We have gurneys out here to help Netanyahu and Arafat, but we’ll get back with their conditions next time..Folks, we’re out of time, see you next week on RAWNITRO IS WAR from San Antonio Texas!!!!!
