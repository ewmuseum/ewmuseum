Star Wars: In Your Hutt, 11 May 1997, Part 2

11 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
STAR WARS: IN YOUR HUTT 5/11/97 From Cloud City Announcers: Princess Leia, Luke Skywalker, Han Solo, Darth Vader ********5. Stone Cold Paul Smith vs. Terry Funk******** (Stone Cold's Theme) Han: What is that SMELL? Luke: That's Stone Cold Paul Smith riding in on a tonton. Han: Aw, MAN those tontons stink. Lando: Introducing the opponents for the next match, from Flint Michigan, riding in on his tonton, here is Stone Cold Paul Smith! Smith: Gimme the mic, son. Now before the match starts, I think it would be wise for Terry Funk to take his old ass back to the Double Cross Retirement Home. Cause old man, you are too stupid to realize I'm gonna give ya the beatin' of your life. And that's the bottom line, son. - (Music-Desperado-The Eagles) Lando: And his opponent, from the Double Cross Ranch in West Texas, here is TERRY FUNK! - Luke: And Terry Funk runs right into the ring, and we're underway! Funk tosses Smith around and Smith comes back with a shoulderblock, clothesline by Smith, and a vertical suplex shortly thereafter. Funk clotheslined over the top rope by Stone Cold Paul Smith. Han: This guy 'ill probably drop dead wrestling. But he's fun to watch. Luke: Smith follows him to the outside, but high back body drop by Funk to the titanium!. Funk now drops the elbow to the back of Smith. And Funk throws Smith into the front row of seats, which were occupied by Jabba the Hutt. But Jabba is apparently somewhere else right now. Han: Oh, oh! I'm outta here! Leia: Sit down, halfwit! Luke: Brawling in those seats by Smith and Funk, now back over the railing, and a piledriver by Smith on the floor. As we get a shot of that tonton. - (Camera shot straight into the face of the tonton.) Tonton: Grooooooooo-oouuuuuuuuu-ughhh-prrrtttt. Han: Doesn't Smith WASH that thing? Pee-yuuuuuuu!!! Luke:Back in the ring, now, Funk going to the top, Split-legged moonsault by Funk, count of 1, kickout. Stone Cold down on the mat in the corner and Funk a splash off the second turnbuckle. 1,2, no. Leia: Funk pretty aerial for an old guy. Obviously the Force is strong with him. Luke: Now Smith hits Funk with a crossbody block. Stone Cold a crucifix rollup, and Funk drops down on him, smashing him beneath his shoulders. Funk up top with another aerial splash, here'a a cover, but only gets the two count. Funk drops Smith with a short clothesline, then goes up for another corner splash. Smith catches him mid-air, powerslam by Smith. Leia: Now Smith is arguing with Slick Patrick, about what? Luke: He's claiming Funk pulled his hair? Han: Come on, that guy's balder than an 800-year old Jedi. Yoda: Resemble that remark I do! Luke: Clothine by Smith. Another one. Yoda, what are you doing up here? Yoda: Protection you want, Solo, from Jabba. Help you I can, hmm, yes. Luke: Stone Cold choking Funk with a chain. Referee trying to see if he has a universal object. Don't laugh Han, Yoda levitated my spaceship once. Yoda: Levitate the Hutt I will, hmm, yes. Luke: You shoulda seen it, Han. He was so proud he said he felt four feet tall. - Leia: Funk reverses a whip, sends Smith into the corner, charges, gets caught. Luke: Smith carries Funk to the center of the ring and slams him, and now using that chain again. Where is he hiding that thing? Leia: Probably in the front of his trunks. I mean, who's gonna check? Han: Well, you'd check, Leia. Luke: Smith laying in a few shots. Han: Especially on a gorgeous guy like me. Luke: Scoop up by Smith, hoisting Funk across one shoulder. Slam by Smith who now starts to jab at Funk's eye. Leia: In your dreams, Solo. Luke: Now a deep cut has burst over the eye of Terry Funk. Smith a belly to back suplex on Funk, Funk lands on the back of his head. Now, Stone Cold Stunner on Funk, 1,2,3. - (Stone Cold's Theme) Lando: The winner of this match, Stone Cold Paul Smith. Luke: Slick Patrick arguing with Smith and says he wants to search Smith for that chain. And Stone Cold lets him. BUT HE PUTS THE CHAIN IN HIS MOUTH. Smith is hiding the chain in his mouth! Unbelievable! Vader: Anything worth fighting for, is worth fighting dirty for. Luke: Now Smith putting the Sharpshooter on Terry Funk! Smith trying to break teh legs of the man from the Double Cross Ranch. Several officials try to get Smith to break the hold, but all they get is Smith extending his middle finger. C3P0: Master Smith! Master Smith! You have prevailed in the contest already, Master Smith! May I enquire as to why you are doing this? Luke: The hold is finally broken. SCPS: Shut up, you old bucket a bolts. (to Lando) Gimme a mic, son. Gimme a mic or I'll whoop your ass. Now I got somethin' to say to YOU, Lord Darth Vader. Vader: Hisssssssssss!!! Hissssssssssssssssssssssss! SCPS: You call yourself the baddest man in the galaxy. You aint the baddest man in the galaxy, cause I am the toughest SOB in the whole stinkin' universe. ****6. Four corners Match for the RSPWWCW Intergender Title**** Evan Schlesinger vs. Taskmistress vs. The Maritimer vs. John Henry (Music-Freedom-Rage Against The Machine) - Vader: Stone Cold Paul Smith will live to regret a challenge to ME! I guarantee that. Lando: This match is a Four Corners match, the winner of which will win OR retain, the Intergender Championship. Introducing in the ring, from Raleigh North Carolina, he is the FORMER Intergender Champion, JOHN HENRY! Han: Now this is a guy I want to see win this. But he needs to get tougher. More violent. More extreme. - (The Perfect Symphony) Lando: And coming down the ramp now, from Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada, the former two time Tag Team Champion, accompanied to the ring by Deadshot, and Doctor Kimona Wanalaya, here is THE MARITIMER! Luke: This is the guy *I* think will become the next Intergender Champion. He should have had it from the Taskmistress two months ago. And I think he WILL have it tonight. Maritimer: I am what I say I am, and I'm PERFECT. Han: Well, I think Doctor Wanalaya is perfect. I'm in love again! Leia: Listen, you stuckup scruffy looking scoundrel. Bite me. Han: I might take you up on that, Leia. - (Because The Night-Natalie Merchant) Lando: And coming down the aisle, accompanied by THE CREATURES OF THE NIGHT, from Venice Beach California, the two time holder of the Intergender Championship, former WORLD Heavyweight Champion, and current RSPWWCW Women's Champion, and co-holder of the Tag Team championship, here is THE TASKMISTRESS LIZ MICHAEL! Leia: No, THIS is the woman, who Maritimer RAN from like a scared puppy, Luke. This is the woman that took every that Evan Schlesinger could dish out, and THIS WOMAN, will win the intergender title for the THIRD time! Luke: Never happen. Leia: Will too. Luke: Will not. - (Sympathy For The Devil-Rolling Stones) - Vader: You're both wrong. Because THIS man will retain the RSPW Intergender Championship. And shall rule rec.sport.pro-wrestling, FOREVER! AHAHAHAHAAAAAH!!!!! Ssssssssssssssssss!!!!! Lando: And their opponent, accompanied to the ring by Truth Commission members [name withheld] and Mattie Carrington here is the RSPWWCW INTERGENDER CHAMPION, from Los Angeles California, here is The King, Evan Schlesinger! Vader: He will tear your Taskmistress, Leia, to pieces. Hawwwwwwwwwwwww!!!! Sssssssssssssssssssssssssssss!!!!! - Lando: And it is my pleasure at this time to introduce the special guest referee tandem, in the ring the referee, CHEWBACCA! Chewy: Arrghh! Aiiiiiig! Rrrrrrrrrrrddddddddd!!!!!! Lando: And in charge of keeping order outside the ring, is R2D2! R2D2: Beedebepp! Hwwwwwwwww! Hwwwwwwwww! (mocking Alphonzo) - Luke: Liz and Evan have been chosen at random to start the match. Taskmistress with a chest chop, and now Schlesinger is armdragged from the ring and she's tossing him around on the floor. Leia: He loves it. Vader: And enlighten us now on your association with the Taskmistress. Luke: Back in the ring now, Liz tags Maritimer, and Liz dropkicks Evan Schlesinger as a parting shot. Leia: We're friends. Vader: Is that what you call it, Your Highness? Luke: Now a crotch shot by Maritimer. Don't stop, Maritimer, he has two of 'em...hit him again! Leia: I heard he hasn't got any. Luke: Maritimer with a head scissors throws Evan out of the ring. And now a somersault to the outside. The Force is with this guy, I am telling you. And now a springboard splash by Maritimer on the floor. Vader: His opponents have banded together. They see the invincibility of Schlesinger! Luke: Whip of Evan to the ringpost. Maritimer kicks Evan into the barricade. Han: Chewy trying to restore order in this madness. Vader: Nobody is going to cheer for some hairy ape. Han: Yes, we are! If you can't grow something similar, that's your problem! Luke: Back in the ring a Frankentimer, 1, 2. Maritimer now a plancha on Evan Schlesinger, and another on John Henry who came in to break up a pin. Taskmistress comes in, dropkicked by Maritimer. Maritimer with a twisting plancha onto Schlesinger, and Taskmistress follows with a somersault tope onto Evan. Maritimer hit with a tope from Henry. And Maritimer lands in the second row at ringside hitting Jabba the Hutt. - Han: I feel more comfortable when Jabba is in plain sight. Now if we could do something about Vader. Yoda: Worry not, plans I have for Vader. Vader: Ssssssssssssssssssssssss!!!!!!!!! AAAAwwwww!!! Luke: Taskmistress a slide backs Evan and a twisting moonsault from John Henry. Vader: They are doubleteaming him. They are afraid. Luke: Liz attacks Evan. Chewbacca: Aiiggggg!! Ruuuuuuggggh. Awwwwoogggow! Luke: Chewy declares them the legal wrestlers. They trade punches. Clothesline by the Taskmistress, and another. Taskmistress throwing Schlesinger to the corner, and SHE BITES HIM! Vader: SHE CAN'T DO THAT! Luke: Chewy arguing with the Taskmistress. Chewbacca: Arrrrrgh! Eiyiiiiiiiaa! Urrgghawabbbawwwww! Liz: Aieeeeeeeggg!!!!!! Owwwwwulgabulgg! Errrf-rawlllllll-ieeeeee! Han: She speaks fluent Wookie! Yoda: Told you I did to underestimate her not. - Luke: John Henry now in, Taskmistress buttbumps him. But reverse atomic drop by Henry, and John Henry takes control. High knee by Henry sends Liz to the outside, and Schlesinger starts stomping on her. But ATM LaRiche comes out and throws flour in Evan's face. Han: That was Dagobarian flour. That stuff is nasty! Luke: Taskmistress back in with a series of punches to Henry. Taskmistress with a BULLDOG! Han: That one's a bitch. Leia: What. The bulldog? Han: Figure of speech, your worship. Luke: Evan runs in and Liz lifts Evan up by the neck. Evan Schlesinger high in the air! Chokeslam to the mat! Vader: Arrghhh! Luke: Liz pounding away on Henry, Taskmistress choking Henry now. Maritimer tags in by slapping Liz. Now, Maritimer with a couple decent dropkicks on Henry. Evan with a Stun Gun on Maritimer. Evan with a punch to Liz on the ring apron knocking her Down. Evan charging Liz on the outside with the laser wand, Taskmistress out of nowhere gets one of her own! Chewy is completely losing control. Leia: How do y'all DO that? Luke: LIZ THE WAND OUT OF EVAN'S HAND AND EVAN IS SINGED BY HIS OWN WAND! I gotta remember that one! - Han: Henry a vertical suplex, reversal and a CHOKESLAM by Maritimer. Evan runs in. Liz runs in. Liz suplexes Evan. All four wrestlers in the ring. Reverse neckbreaker by Maritimer on John Henry. Evan clothelines Liz over the top rope. Liz lands on her feet and grabs Evan's leg, attempting to bite his leg! THIS IS NUTS! Evan gets away form Liz, but A PERFECTPLEX BY MARITIMER, 1, 2, 3, AND MARITIMER IS THE NEW CHAMPION! MARITIMER IS THE NEW CHAMPION! - (The Perfect Symphony) Lando: HERE IS YOUR WINNER, AND NEEEEEEEEEWWWWWWWWWWWWWWW RSPWWCW INTERGENDER CHAMPION, THE MARITIMER! - Luke: Incredible! I was hoping he would pull it off, and he does. Your excuses, Vader? Vader: It is the fault of the Taskmistress. Yoda: Beg to differ, I do. Fed Evan on the Dark Side. His hatred of the Taskmistress diverted his concentration. Bright young man, Maritimer. Opportunity he saw, and acted. Vader: You are beginning to annoy me, Yoda. You know how I get when I am annoyed. Han: Yeah. He once killed a man for snoring too loud! Yoda: Think you I taught you all of the Force, Vader? Take you I still can. Vader: I am NOT afraid of YOU, little man. Yoda: Ooohh! You WILL be! You WILL be! (Evil look on Yoda's face.) *******7. RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Title******* ********Dollywood Tom Zylkin vs. Deadshot******* Lando: At this time ladies and gentlemen, for our final match, let me introduce to you the special guest referee for this match, C3PO! C3PO: Thank you, thank you very much ladies and gentlemen, so are SO so kind! - (The Perfect Symphony) Lando: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the final contest of the evening, and it is for the RSPWWCW WORLD Heavyweight Championship. Introducing the challenger, weighing 350 pounds, from Halifax Nova Scotia Canada, and accompanied to the ring by The Maritimer, and Dr. Kimona Wanalaya, here is DEADSHOT! Luke: Deadshot coming back to the ring, hoping to cash in on the second part of a double win and become, like Maritimer, THE PERFECT Champion. I am thrilled for Maritimer, and he looks like he is floating ten feet off the ground. Han: The kid has fulfilled his destiny, no doubt. And now Deadshot seeks to double up...they were Tag Team Champions twice. Leia: Before they faked an injury. Luke: Leia! - (Music-Sweet Home Alabama-Lynyrd Skynyrd- sung karioke by Dollywood and Honky Tonk Man) Lando: And his opponent, from Dollywood Tennessee, the RSPWWCW HEAVYWEIGHT Champion, accompanied to the ring by the Honky Tonk Man, here is Dollywood Tom Zylkin. Han: Are all these earth people this bad at singing. Leia: No, actually my research tells me that the Honky Tonk Man is the worst singer in the history of earth up until this date. Han: Well, thank the Force for that. - Luke: Big Deadshot with a dropkick. Deadshot pummeling Zylkin. Deadshot a Samoan Drop, and now a spike piledriver. Han: And I think Zylkin was hurt VERY badly by that piledriver. Luke: Deadshot beats on Zylkin some more, and now a sleeper. C3P0 looks for signs of life from Dollywood Tom Zylkin. Arms up and down on Zylkin. C3PO: Excuse me, sir, Master Tom. Are you awake, Master Tom! Luke: Zylkin gets the arm up. Leia: The droid can actually referee wrestling. Wonders never cease. Luke: Deadshot slaps on that sleeper again. But Zylkin drives Deadshot into the corner. Vader: You will excuse me, gentlemen. Luke: Deadshot a double-axehandle from the second-rope CAUGHT by Zylkin. Zylkin beating on Deadshot now. Han: Wait a minute. Why's Vader leaving? Luke: Short-arm clothesline by Dollywood, Maritimer runs in, and gets a DDT for his trouble, but Deadshot hits Zylkin. Han: Threepie must have his sensors closed not to see this. Luke: Deadshot a splash Zylkin, cover but Dollywood Zylkin kicks out. Han: I don't believe Vader left. I got a bad feeling about this. Luke: Deadshot runs to the ropes but gets tripped by the Honky Tonk Man. Oh, no! Han: Slowly but surely the Creatures have creeped into the ring area. Luke: Deashot drops the elbow, leans into a cover 1, 2. Deadshot rollup, 1, 2. Han: Sable comes in in back of Maritimer, but Maritimer catches her. But Taskmistress is there! Luke: Taskmistress puts the Diamond Cutter on Maritimer. And now Taskmistress stalking Dr. Wanalaya. Han: Can't blame her for that. Luke: Deadshot throws Dollywood into the turnbuckle, Dollywood jumps back over a charging Deadshot, goes for a rollup, Deadshot slips SITS right square on Dollywood's face. The Taskmistress jumps in. Taskmistress reprimanded by C3PO, and Taskmistress throws Threepie out of the ring. YOU BITCH! Han: The Creatures gang up on Deadshot and they are trying to bite him. This is terrible! Where are the officals? Leia: Honky Tonk Man is looking at the spectacle in amazement. (crowd starts a "Honky's got a Boner" chant.) Leia: And there are two new creatures. They are Sasskia and Natasha. Luke: My God, Leia. You KNOW the're names. Leia: YES! Hee hee hee! Lando: Storm troopers. Get the hell out of here! - (Imperial Storm Troopers have shown up. The broadcast crew scurries away. Referees come to the ring and explain the invasion, and everybody picks it up and runs away. Chewbacca runs in to get the prone C3P0. Camera shot of the crowd going to the runways piling on to escape craft. Lazerton takes the Millenium Falcon and gets all the RSPWWCW wrestlers onto it. Closing shot goes to a protective panel inside the Falcon, slightly ajar, through which you can just see a figure in a black mask clad in all black. As the Empire theme starts to slowly play.)
