Thursday Raw Thursday Nitro Thursday Live, 27 February 1997

27 February 1997

 RSPWWCW Thursday Raw Thursday Nitro Thursday Live 2/27 Coming to you LIVE from the Corel Centre in Ottawa, Canada. Announcers: Jim Ross, Bobby Heenan, Mike Tenay (Wanamaker and Amoja Sumler are already in the ring as the program begins.Mo-Man's theme, "One" by Metallica is playing. The bell has yet to ring. Wanamaker is dressed in yellow and red tights with a denim jacket. "Overlord" Amoja Sumler is dressed in Syxx style garb with a VWO leather jacket.) JR: Hello and welcome everybody to the Corel Centre in Ottowa for Thursday Raw Thursday Nitro Thursday Live. Our opening match is set to be Amoja Sumler of the VWO vs. Wanamaker the former Cruiserweight champion. BH: Instead of Live Thursday Raw Thursday Thursday Nitro Thursday Thursday Thursday, how about we just call it Live Thursday Rawnitro. JR: Because Thursday Raw Thursday Nitro Thursday Live is much more catchy. MT: Keep in mind that Jack Epstein will defend the Intergender belt against the Taskmistress on this program. --- DING!!!! ---- JR: There's the bell! Collar-and-elbow tie up now, Sumler forcing the larger man into the corner. Referee Mike Asparagus calls for a break. Wanamaker anticipates a clean one.... and takes a shot in the gut! BH: Oh please! Wanamaker just got duped. MT: Sumler whips him into the ropes and........POWERSLAMS Wanamaker!! Sumler has got to be only 170 lbs! That was incredible! JR: And Wanamaker's a good 220! BH: Two cruiserweights! JR: Savate kick to the throat of Wanamaker. Wanamaker stumbles into the corner. The Mo-Man runs in and starts nailing him with right hands. Asparagus again calls for the break. Again Sumler tries to dupe Wanamaker but NOBODY HOME! Wanamaker now with a set of his own right hands. MT: Great action here on Thursday Raw Thursday Nitro Thursday Live! BH: Not you too, Tenay!? JR: Sumler rolls to the outside and tries to collect himself.....but here comes Wanamaker!! The fomer Intergender Champion with a TREMENDOUS somersault senton plancha on Sumler. He rolls him in, tries for the pin..... BH: No he did not get him!!! MT: Wanamaker is definitely one of the top athletes in RSPWWCW. JR: Keeping in mind he did lose twice to a guy called Disco Trillionaire. ---- (Wanamaker gives Sumler a Stun Gun and gets a two count. The walkway to the ring begins to light up and a shadowy figure appears in the tunnel.) ---- BH: That was pretty close that time......what the hell?? JR: Speak of the Devil.... MT: It's the "Disco Trillionaire" Coot Thompson!! He's coming to the ring!! BH: Doesn't he have anywhere better to be? Like therapy? JR: Well he is the Cruiserweight champ. BH: He's also very annoying. MT: I dunno. I kinda like him. He has good taste in music. (Other announcers stare blankly at Tenay without saying anything.) MT: What?? BH: Sumler just accidentally bumped the ref!! JR: Accidentally?! Who are you kidding, Brain? MT: Whom. JR: What? MT: Whom are you kidding. JR: I'm not kidding anyone, Mike. Are you sure you're alright? BH: Coot's got some knucks!! He swings and misses Wanamaker!! MT: Wanamaker rolls up COOT!! Asparagus counts the 3!! BH: He just pinned the Cruiserweight champion!! JR: I have never seen this happen before in all my years as a play-by-play man. BH: So that was a different Jim Ross working for WCW in the late eighties, huh? ----- (Music: Eye of the Tiger) Announcer: The winner of this match by pinfall at 13:21 is Waaaanaamakeerrr!! ------ JR: Scheme Gene has got an interview with the World Champ Lex Loogie now. ------ (Music: Luger music) Scheme Gene: May I introduce to the people of Ottawa, the World Heavyweight Champion, the Subtotal Package, Lex Loooooogiiieee!! (Loud boos for Lex Loogie, who carries Liz Michael like a backpack on the way to the ring. ) Lex: Hello, Gene. I got a few things to clear up here. Gene: Which are?? Lex: First off, I want to thank the beautiful Liz (she cracks a giggle), Dollywood Tom Zylkin, Lionwrath Zen Jericho, and Stone Cold Paul Smith....aw hell....the entire VWO for getting me this belt. SUCKERS!!!!!!!!! Liz: What?? Lex: You know Liz, I'm gonna tell ya somethin'. You are a real babe, you know. And you are real hot! And I'll even say this: YOU WERE DAMN GOOD at all the kinky little sex games we had together. But as the VWO says, business is business. And the bottom line is babes, I used YOU (pointing at Liz) to get this.(pointing at the belt) And NOW, well ya know if you wanna play on the side I'm still game. But Liz, I have NOT joined the VWO. I will NEVER join the VWO. Because the VWO is a bunch of lowlife scumbags. I USED ya, baby! ----- JR: Wait a second, someone in the balcony is calling attention to himself..... BH: That's Dollywood Tom Zylkin!! Has he lost weight or what? He is in the best shape I've ever seen him in! ------ Dollywood: NOW JUST A SECOND!!! You don't know what you just did, pal!! The VWO... Lex: Oh yes I did, Tom. I got screwed out of the belt many times by the VWO, and so I decided that the only way to get the belt was to have the VWO on my side. Would you say it worked, Tom? Tom: GET HIM!! Get him, get him, get him!!! Go, go, go, go, go!!!! ---- (Liz Michael superkicks Lex Loogie from behind) JR: Oh my gosh! Sweet Bitch Music to the back of Loogie's head!! And here comes the VWO!! (Stone Cold Paul Smith, Amoja Sumler, and Lionwrath Zen Jericho storm the stage area. They start to beat up on Loogie. Liz grabs the belt and starts posing.) ---- JR: This is sick!!!!!! MT: There's someone with an NWO T-shirt jumping the barrier from the crowd heading towards the fray!! BH: That's *just* what we need. JR: That's former champ Jeff Katz!!! As if they weren't beating him bad enough... MT: The Taskmistress is embracing Katz!! They're gonna..... BH: WAIT A MOMENT!! JR: Katz just hit Liz Michael with the World Heavyweight Championship Belt!! She is OUT cold!! BH: Look at Loogie! He's fighting off Stone Cold and Amoja Sumler at the same time! JR: Dean Boardwalk has just entered the fray!! There's security everywhere and there's a discolored dent in the World Title Belt! We gotta go to a break now!! -- COMMERCIAL --- (Music: Ultimate Dragon music) Announcer: This next contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first, from fjRSPW, wieghing 234 pounds and accompanied to the ring by Sonny Onoo: El Ultimo Braguuuu!! --- JR: Mike Tenay, what have you heard about this El Ultimo Bragu? MT: How the hell would *I* know anything about this guy? JR: Well......I guess you're......you know....into that sort of stuff. MT: He calls himself El Ultimo Bragu for goodness sake, and I doubt that's really Sonny Onoo. (Ross and Heenan draw back in horror and whisper to each other.) --- (Music: "Wannabe"- Spice Girls) Announcer: And his opponent, from the United Kingdom- he weighs 227 pounds and is also known as Gilgamesh. He is the Bluuuuuee Avennngeeerrrrr!!!!! --- BH: Hey! He's got new tights! (referring to his blue tights with a big semi-colon on his butt.) (Blue Avenger starts out quick with a headscissor takedown into a cradle. Bragu kicks out at two. Bragu jumps to his feet and rushes Blue Avenger, who hiptosses him and applies an armbar. Bragu reaches the ropes. They lock up and Avenger drops Bragu with an armdrag, he tries to land a quick elbow drop but Bragu rolls out of the way, lands a legdrop, and applies a shortarm scissors.) JR: Tremendous action here on Thursday Raw Thursday Nitro Thursday Live. BH: Thursday Night Thursday Bowling Thursday TV Thursday Internet Thursday.... MT: Anything else you'd like to add, Brain? BH: Yeah, but then I'd get into Fridays. JR: Blue Avenger now breaks the hold with a Dragon Leg Whip. MT: Remember that this is the man who beat Dollywood Tom Zylkin a few weeks back. BH: It was just a lucky pin. Besides, he cheated. Ask Dollywood. JR: Avenger with a scoopslam and he goes out to the apron...OH what a maneuver!!! MT: A tremendous springboard somersault senton plancha backsplash by Blue Avenger!! BH: He just did a good move there, too! JR: Only a 2-count. Whips him in and goes for the lariat but Bragu ducks under. Bragu catches him with a spinning heel kick. Step-over Toehold applied by El Ultimo Bragu now. BH: He really doesn't have him good there. JR: Bragu puts the Avenger in a sitting position and..... --- (SMACK!) --- MT: He nails him in the back of the head with that judo kick. JR: El Ultimo Bragu nails the Avenger with a jump roundkick. The Blue Avenger falls to the outside. Bragu bounces off the farside ropes and.... MT: SUICIDE DIVE by Ultimo Bragu!! The Blue Avenger hit against the steel! JR: Now look at Sony Onoo laying in those cheap kicks on the Avenger. BH: What kicks? I don't see anything. JR: Bragu places the Avenger on the top turnbuckle. He's now climbing up on his shoulders. MT: He's gonna huricanrana him from the top! This is going to be DEVASTATING!! BH: Say *what*? JR: The Blue Avenger counters with a POWERBOMB!!! He's going up top!! MT: He nails him with a missile dropkick!! JR: He's bouncing off the ropes and jumping over Ultimo Bragu....and he buries him with a TRIPLE JUMP MOONSAULT!! BH: The 1 2 3, and the Blue Avenger strikes again! MT: He calls that move the *Midnight Vengeance Moonsault*. ---- (Music: "Wannabe" - Spice Girls) Announcer: Here is your winner: The Bluuuuuuuue Aveeengerrrrrr! ---- JR: And now we have an interview with former champ Waru, and he is none too happy. BH: His plants musta died or somethin'. (Music: "Gimme the Prize"(Kurgen's theme from Highlander)- Queen Waru comes out wearing black and red tights with a huge gold ceremonial headpiece with horns) Gene: I would now like to introduce to you a mysterious character who has just lost the RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Title: Waarruuu!! Waru: Gene, kindly shut up. Joe Rspwwcwfan did not pay a buck fifty to hear you squeal. There here because I'm here. May I remind you that I never lost the Intergender belt, and I never lost the World Title. I did not submit to the Spice Rack, because I don't feel pain. I can't wait 'til UNKILLFILED when I reunite with what was stolen from me. Gene: Tell me, Waru..... Waru: Shut up, Gene. Class is over. You are dismissed. You can go home now feeling good about yourself. Gene: Er, thank you Waru. More Thursday Raw Thursday Nitro Thursday Live after this. (Only said really fast like Gene usually does it.) --- COMMERCIAL- ---- (Music: NWO Music with a Country Twang to it and karaoke vocals.) Announcer: This contest is set for one fall. Introducing first, formerly of Sheen, England and Arden, Delaware, he now resides in Dollywood, Tennessee.He is the master of the *Best Little Death Drop in Texas*. He is accompanied to the ring by his mentor, the Honky Tonk Man. He weighs 276 pounds. He is the lead singer with the Golden Vocals for the Virtual World Order and the biggest ICON in country music today. He is: Dollywood Tom Zyyyylkin!!! ---- JR: He is fresh back from his suspension, and his loss to the Blue Avenger. BH: I'm telling ya, the Avenger cheated. Ask anyone in the VWO. ---- (Music: Canadian National Anthem with explosions in the background and voices yelling "run for cover, eh!".) Announcer: His opponent: from the Good Ol' US of A (loud boos from Ottawa crowd), he weighs 256 pounds, Nuuuuke Caaaanadaaaa!!! (Loudest boos you've ever heard in your life and lots of trash throwing for Nuke Canada who comes to the ring in Amazing French Canadian gear, only there's a circle around all the maple leaves and flur-de-lises with lines through them. He even has one of their flags with a circle and line through the symbols. A bunch of lowlifes from Buffalo with ringside seats start a "Here we go, Nuke" chant. They get in a huge fistfight with everyone around them. As Nuke Canada hits the ring, he tries to start a "USA" chant and that makes the boos even louder. Dollywood Tom Zylkin jumps him from behind and makes him eat turnbuckle for the legal 5 seconds.) ---- JR: Dollywood Zylkin looking as aggressive as ever! BH: And he is in such great shape, too!! MT: Zylkin with a tremendous lariat! He sends him sprawling to the outside! (Fans on that side of the arena start throwing all their trash at Nuke Canada. Nuke gets extremely angry at the crowd and diverts his attention from the ring. Dollywood nails him from behind with a baseball slide which sends him into the guardrail. A fan in the front row nails him with his coke. Dollywood then sends him headfirst into the steel steps and rolls him into the ring.) MT: I wonder if he even realizes he's in Canada. BH: Wait a second. If he's in Canada, then where are we? JR: Ottawa is in Michigan, right? --- (Dollywood puts Nuke Canada on the top and superplexes him off. Instead of pinning him, he opts to flex and pose for the Ottowa crowd.) ---- JR: Isn't he gonna pin him? BH: Why bother? ---- (Dollywood Tom Zylkin gives Nuke Canada a backbreaker and covers for a count of 2. He nails him with a headbutt and throws him out of the ring.) JR: Uh-oh. Watch Honky-Tonk! He's got Nuke Canada's flag! (WWHAAAAAAACCCKKKK!!) (Nuke Canada stumbles around in a daze and then falls face first. While he lies on the ground the crowd literally rains trash on him. Dollywood picks up, brushes him off, and throws him into the ring.) ----- JR: Zylkin with a tremendous swinging neckbreaker! BH: Looked kinda like the Shake, Rattle, & Roll. MT: A cover.....and he pulls his head up. He's calling for the BLDDT!!! BH: Uh-oh! Looks like it's time for the Hindenberg to fall from the sky. JR: He's setting up for the Best Little Death Drop in Texas! He's got him up in the crucifix......and he plants him!! BH: A victory for Dollywood Tom Zylkin with the BLDDT!! ----- (Music: NWO Theme with the Country Twang and karaoke.) Announcer: Here is your winner: Dollywoood Tom Zyyylkiiin!! (Nuke Canada stumbles back to the locker room while being pelted with trash.) ------ (COMMERCIAL) ------- (Music: "American Woman" by Guess Who) Announcer: This following contest will be a steel cage match for the Intergender Championship Belt. Introducing first, the challenger, from Anaheim, Calafornia (boos from the Ottawa crowd), she is an Amazonian Domina who forces all men and women subordinate to her to get down on their knees and beg for mercy. She is feared by all enemies of the VWO, the Taskmistress: Liz Miiiiiichaaaaaael!! (Large, mostly feminine pop for the Taskmistress) BH: She looks ready to tear Epstein apart in this match for that belt. JR: At least she didn't bring her sex toys to the ring this time. (Music: Rutgers fight song) Announcer: Introducing second, the RSPWWCW Intergender Champion, from Camden, New Jersey, he weighs 298 pounds, "Cactus" Jack Epsteeeeiiin!! (Epstein comes out in Mankind get-up sans the face guard (it's not like he needs it. The match starts out as a wild brawl with the Taskmistress and Epstein battling back and forth.) BH: This is probably the closest Epstein is ever gonna come to a real beauty like the Taskmistress Liz Michael. MT: Right, Bobby. BH: She's almost as attractive as Sherri, don't you think? JR: Sure, Brain. ---- (Jack whips Liz's head against the cage. She falls on her back and Epstein hits a legdrop on her. He then scoops her up, rams her backside into the cage, and gives her a shoulder breaker.) ---- JR: A tremendous move by Epstein who is really trying to hurt Liz Michael. (Jack powerslams Liz. He climbs up to the top rope and lands a leg drop. He goes for a pin and gets a two. He hits Liz with a belly-to-belly suplex and calls out to the crowd: "OH YEAH, BABY!!! TIME FOR SOME EXTREME JACK!!") JR: Liz just doesn't seem herself after that bump she took earlier tonight from Jeff Katz. BH: Epstein is almost having his way with her. --- (Epstein gives her a neckbreaker. He's literally jumping for joy all over the ring as Liz Michael struggles to get up. He nails her with successive clotheslines and celebrates some more. He DDTs Liz.) ---- JR: She just isn't all there. MT: I don't know if she should be wrestling in this match. JR: Jack Epstein takes Liz Michael's head to the steel! Now a piledriver. BH: He is literally beating her up. No man has ever been able to do that! JR: I don't think any man has ever taken it to Liz quite like this. BH: Somebody from the VWO has to stop this match. ---- (Jack Epstein begins taking to posing after he executes a devastating move. He lands three straight piledrivers on her and dances all around the ring.) ---- BH: I've never seen him so happy! JR: He's gonna go for the *Abdominal Stretch Plex*!! He's calling for it! He scoops Liz off the mat and sets up for it!! He's got her up!! Wait.... Liz is wriggling to escape...SHE'S GOT HIM IN POSITION!!! BH: Oh BABY!!! JR: BANG!! Slauson Cutoff!! Epstein is down! BH: He's never going to get up!! MT: She stumbles out of the cage and a victory for Liz Michael!!! JR: She's the new Intergender Champion!!!!
