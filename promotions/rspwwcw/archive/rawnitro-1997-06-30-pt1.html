RawNitro, 30 June 1997, Part 1

30 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Broadcast in at least six countries, in only one language, and read by over half a hundred posters. Rec.Sport.Pro.Wrestling-WCW, the newsgroup leader, in sports entertainment! **************************** RSPWWCW RawNitro 6/30 **************************** (Gorilla Monsoon and Jesse Ventura commentating) Gorilla: Hello everyone and welcome to RSPWWCW RawNitro! We're coming to you from Glenn Falls, NY, and do we have a show for you! We have THREE titles on the line tonight! Ventura: And one of them will be in a steel cage, Monsoon! Gorilla: Indeed it is, but we're keeping everyone in the dark on that one as we are expecting our guest commentator for the first half of RawNitro. ("Because the Night" - Natalie Merchant. Liz walks out, wearing her average dominatrix outfit) Gorilla: We're being joined by the Taskmistress, co-leader of the VWO, and the leader of the Creatures of the Night, Liz Michael. (Liz sits behind desk at ringside, and puts on a headset) Liz: Hey, what is this shit? A title on the line in a steel cage and you're not gonna tell us who it is. Who the hell do you think you are Monsoon? Gorilla: Excuse me? Liz: Well, they call you a gorilla, but you look like a jackass to me. Gorilla: Now that sounds familiar. Next time, write your own material! You know full well who's wrestling tonight. All the wrestlers know, it's just the fans we're keeping in the dark. Liz: Well excuse me all to hell. Now, Jesse here.....Jesse should be the next governor of Minnesota. You need to go up there and kick all those legislators' asses. Don't pussyfoot around, Jess. Ventura: Oh, you know me better than that, Liz. Liz: You better believe it BAY-BEEE! (To Gorilla) You lookin' at me? Gorilla? You lookin' at me? I don't see nobody behind me, so you must be lookin' at me. Quit lookin' at me. Quit looking at me. I know you want me, but you can't have me. Look at me...the beauty of the Taskmistress cannot be denied! ALL MEN desire to grovel at my pretty feet. Gorilla: Oh will you stop! Ladies and gentlemen our announcer for tonight is none other than RSPWWCW Commissioner Duggan Fan! Duggan Fan: HOOOOOOOO!!!!!! (crowd: HOOOOOOO!!!!) Duggan Fan: It's time for our first match, tough-guys! ******************************* Mike Malecki vs. 1-Stu-3 Kid ******************************* ("Banned in the USA") Duggan Fan: On his way to the ring, one of those stinky rotten foreigners from Canada, and a member of the Aryan Nation, MIKE MALECKI! (crowd boos) Ventura: So, Liz. What happened to Powrhug Kare last week? Liz: Kare just went nuts. You know, Duggan Fan was never too clear about Estado de Monterey rules, and screwing around with her Girlhood Dream. Now she's snapped, and you are going to see a more mean, more vicious, more ruthless, and more plain evil woman. You created a monster, guys. (1-2-3 Kid theme) Duggan Fan: And his opponent, another rotten foreigner from Canada, The 1-STU-3 KID! (crowd boos. Starts chanting "USA") Gorilla: Don't the fans know that neither of these people are from the USA? Ventura: That's pretty much the point, Monsoon. Liz: Well, this 1-Stu-3 kid sounds like he should be part of the VWO...but you're about to see why he ain't. He won't even beat a goddamned national socialist, how the hell is he gonna be in the VWO? (Bell rings. Kid immediately runs off the ropes with a failed shoulder-block. Gets up, and is bodyslammed by Malecki. Elbowdrop, cover, and a count of 1. Malecki puts the Kid in a headlock.) Liz: See what I mean? The man can't even stay on his feet. Monsoon: It's still early. Ventura: Yeah, like THAT will make a difference... (Kid throws Malecki off the ropes, leapfrogs over Malecki, and gives Malecki a reverse monkey-flip. Both get up, and Malecki hiptosses the Kid, but misses another elbow-drop.) Monsoon: The Kid now starting to come back. Ventura: It seems the only way the Kid is successful is when he's reversing Malecki's moves. (Kid with a snap-mare, followed by a legdrop. Cover, count of 2. Both get up, and the Kid puts a waist lock on Malecki. Malecki reverses it, lifts the Kid for an atomic drop, but the Kid flips over, and mule-kicks Malecki when he turns around.) Liz: Snort......heeeeee haaaaaawwwwwww. Gorilla: Will you stop? (The Kid throws Malecki off the ropes, misses a clothesline, and Malecki bounces back and hits the Kid with a flying forearm. Cover, count of 2.) Ventura: Malecki better get his act in gear if he wants to beat this kid. Liz: Oh please. Anyone who names himself "The Kid" CAN'T be taken seriously. (The Kid throws Malecki to the ropes, but Malecki reverses it, and gives the Kid a back-body drop, but the Kid lands on his feet. When Malecki stands straight, the Kid jumps on his shoulders and gives him a victory roll.) Monsoon: Victory roll, this could be it. Ventura: But Malecki's rolling with it! Monsoon: The ref is counting.. and Malecki wins! ("Banned in the USA". crowd boos) Duggan Fan: The winner of the match, even though he didn't tape his fists, is the stupid foreigner, MIKE MALECKI! Liz: What did I tell you. If there is one thing the VWO does not need is wimpy men. we already have Robert Wallace Eddy filling our affirmative action wimp quota. Monsoon: Right now folks, we have Scheme Gene in the ring, and he's about to do an interview with Stone Cold, Paul Smith. Liz: What? That loser? *************************************** Interview with RSPWWCW champ Paul Smith *************************************** Gene: Thank you Gorilla. Ladies and gentlemen, let me bring out the RSPWWCW heavyweight champion, Stone Cold, PAUL SMITH! (Stone Cold theme. Crowd cheers) Monsoon: Standing ovation for Paul Smith! (Smith enters the ring) Gene: First of all, let me congradulate you on last week's victory against Powrhug Kare. Smith: You don't need to thank me for nothin'. I did what I said I'd do, and that's whoop the ass of anyone in my way, and that included Powrhug Kare. Some say she's crazy, some say she lost it, but she never had it to begin with! (crowd cheers) Liz: Am I the guest commentator? Ventura: Yeah, why? Liz: Just checking. Gene: Alright, next thing I want to ask you is if you'll think wrestling Steve Tiszinkel next week at In Your House will have any problems, considering the JWO. Smith: Are you kidding me? Last week, and the week before that, I whooped the asses off the Creatures, AND the VWO! Do you really think, that next week will be any more difficult for me, just because they're Jewish? I have more problems with a bar of Ivory soap in the shower than with those ya-hoos! I'm the greatest champion of all time, and people better start believin' it! Liz: That's it. I've had it. Monsoon: What're you...? Liz is entering the ring, with her own mic. Stone Cold: Whaddya you want, ya worm eatn' piece a trash. I done whooped your Klingon ass once, bitch. Whaddya want now, rough sex? Liz: Look I am sick of hearing how you're such a damn great World Champion. You didn't even WIN the first match legit. You needed Estado de Monterey rules and a crosseyed referee to help you out. And you didn't even beat Kare the second time. You just walked out of a goddamn cage. You know when *I* was the World Heavyweight Champion, and I was the LONGEST World Heavyweight Champion in RSPWWCW history, I won this belt by pinning somebody in the ring in seven seconds. I defended it against everybody who showed up against it. I defended it against Lex Loogie. I defended it against Marc Mero. And when I lost it they damn well had to take both me and my opponent out on a stretcher......people may associate me with THIS belt because I won it three times (holding up the Intergender Championship.) But people really remember me for THAT belt. And it really pisses me off seeing you with it, given that you've pinned nobody, and made nobody submit. Stone Cold: Well, if what you're trying to imply is that you wanna get your ass kicked again, after I whoop Steve Tiszinkel's ass, I'll be more than happy to whoop your ass again. And that's the bottom line! (Dollywood Zylkin from up in the rafters, accompanied by Eddy and Henry) Zylkin: Hey, LIZZZZZ-IE! LIZZZZZZZZZ-IE! Hey, Liz! Up here, sis! Liz: Whaddya you want? We're gonna kick your ass next Sunday in Wilmington. Zylkin: Well, first of all, LIZ-ZIE! Neither one a you is the best Heavyweight Champion of all time. Everybody knows that the distinction belongs to the D-wood, brother! And everybody knows that we Three Dudes With Attitudes can take care or any Bitches anytime we want. But I wanted to make it even a little more fun than just a tag match.........I want a street fight. Liz: You want it? (big cheers from crowd) Liz: You got it. Except I don't think I wanna wait until Sunday night. I'm gonna come up there and kick your ass now. (As Liz leaves the ring and starts working her way through the crowd, and some other Creatures, Kare, ATM, DoMinAA.....start making THEIR way up into the crowd up toward the rafters of the Glen Falls Civic Auditorium, as everybody tries to grope them.) ***************** commercial break ***************** Monsoon: Welcome back, folks. Liz, what were you doing? Liz: Whatever I want. Ventura: I noticed that you commented on the IG title. Any other comments concerning that and your win over the Maritimer? Liz: Well, Maritimer forgot that Shawn Michaels could have been in the building. Now McMahon made up that story about a fight with Bret Hart. Uh, uh....this was planned ALL ALONG, for Shawn to join me in Mississippi, and give Maritimer a little payback. ********************************* 2Live Jews vs. The New Demolition ********************************* (The Baruch hymn) Liz: Come on, Monsoon. Stand up. Stand up. It's the Jewish hymn. Don't you have any respect, Monsoon? what are you, an antisemite? Monsoon: What? Are you crazy? Ventura: No, she's Liz! Duggan Fan: Ladies and Tough-guys, our next match is a tag team contest. Coming to the ring, with a combined weight that's too high for me to count, Steve Tiszinkel, Jeff Amdur, 2LIVE JEWS! (crowd cheers) Ventura: 13 is too high for Duggan Fan to count. Monsoon: Tiszinkel to face Paul Smith for the RSPWWCW heavyweight belt next week at In Your House. Liz: You already know my opinion on that. Monsoon: Unfortunately, yes we do. (Demolition theme) Ventura: Now here's a team that *I* like, listen to these new lyrics, Monsoon. ("Here come the Crunch, and here comes the Hatch-et. They'll grab your ug-ly face, and both'll bitch-slap it.") Duggan Fan: And their opponents, from Parts Unknown, which I think is near Jersey, on their way to the ring, Crunch, Hatchet, the NEW DEMOLTION! (crowd boos) Liz: These guys cost Kare and me the tag titles. And they're gonna get their asses kicked by the Creatures. Bank on it. Ventura: Well, I'm not sure about that one Liz. (Crunch and Hatchet remove their spiked leather outfit, and under their masks are... more masks! Only this time there is no spikes, only flat studs.) Ventura: They're wearing masks like Smash and Crush did. Liz: Hey, they can wear six layers of masks. Sooner or later we are going to catch them and strip them. Of their masks, that is. Nobody wants to see the rest of them.....Come to think of it, they're probably so ugly we'll put their masks back on after we kind out who they are. (New Demolition immediately attack 2Live Jews, and start beating them with axehandles. Bell rings.) Monsoon: They're not even waiting for the match to start! Ventura: That's ONE reason why I like them. (They throw 2Live Jews off the ropes, miss the clotheslines, and the Jews hit with clotheslines of their own. They double hip-toss Hatchet, then double dropkick Crunch. The Demos leave the ring.) Monsoon: 2Live Jews now cleaning house! Ventura: Ah, it won't last. Liz: Well, they are turning the ring into "Israeli Occupied Territory" if you will. Speaking of "if you will", where the hell is Dusty Rhodes? I brought my Dustybonics Dictionary. Ventura: I think he was last seen at a KFC, and he couldn't get his ass out of the chair. (Things calm down, and Hatchet/Amdur start off. They lock up, and Hatchet pokes Amdur in the eyes. Throws him into the turnbuckle, charges, but misses. Amdur grabs Hatchet, but he pokes Amdur in the eyes again and tags in Crunch.) Liz: An eye for an eye. A tooth for a tooth. Laid down by that great wrestler.....Moses. Monsoon: Moses was not a wrestler! Liz: Got proof that he wasn't? Monsoon: Well, no. Liz: Well then SHUT UP! (Amdur runs off the ropes with a failed shoulder-tackle. Quickly tags in Tiszinkel, who tries the same, with the same results. Crunch grabs him, and gives him a belly/belly suplex. Crunch gets up, and misses a standing splash.) Liz: SPLAT! Ventura: A slight inconvenience only. (Crunch tags Hatchet, who intercepts Steve before he makes a tag. Starts stomping, pounding, punching, and so on.) Liz: Well, this is some sound technical wrestling by Demolition. Technically they're wrestlers. Ventura: Hey, if it works, use it. (Hatchet drags Steve back to their corner, tags Crunch and beat him with double axe-handles. Tags Hatchet back in, and more axe-handles. Amdur tries to intervene, but the ref stops him, and the Demos use, yep, more axehandles.) Liz: Come on, Amdur....take a tip from the Bible. Cheat. Gorilla: That's not in the Bible! Liz: It's in mine. The Michael Millken Authorized Version........I remember meeting Jeff Amdur. He told me he was a cunning linguist. I told him to lick me. (This time New Demolition throws in some kicks for flavour. Double suplex. Crunch throws Steve off the ropes, and gives him a powerslam. Tags in Hatchet, and they hit the decapitation. Hatchet makes the cover for the pin as Crunch fights Amdur.) (New Demoltion theme) Ventura: See guys? New Demoltion is, in my opinion, the most powerful tag team in RSPWWCW. Liz: Big deal. Let's see how they do against a REAL team. Monsoon: But they're not done with 2Live Jews! (Hatchet throws Steve out of the ring and attacks Amdur. They beat him up for a few minutes, then hit him with the decapitation. Jack Epstein charges the ring, and the Demos attack him, and give him the decapitation.) Ventura: I LOVE this! Beat him up, guys! Liz: Well, this doesn't bode well. These guys need that wuss Epstein's help. And Tiszinkel's gonna wrestle for the World championship next Sunday? He's gonna get his ass kicked! Monsoon: Here comes en4cer and Blakenstein, and they've got chairs! (Perfection Personified charge the ring, and the Demos clear the ring and go to the back.) Monsoon: Looking at this tag match, I'll have to ask you Liz, any plans for new tag teams in the VWO? Liz: There are The Extreme Rockers with D-wood and John Henry, and there are Bitches With Attitude, that ATM and I have formed, and that is going to be the most killer tag team. It's like Mickey and Mallory, Thelma and Louise, Natural Born Killers, a love story mixed in with violence of the worst kind. Guys are gonna love it. If they live, that is. Because you know what, ATM and I both get turned on by the same thing.....beating the living crap out of a guy. It's erotic. It's better than sex, man! ******************************************* RSPWWCW TV title: Chris Myers vs. the Mick ******************************************* Duggan Fan: Alright, now we have our first title match for the evening, and it's for the RSPWWCW Television Championship! (The theme from the Tick animated series) Duggan Fan: Making his way down to the ring, a man who really is a tough guy, THE MICK! (crowd doesn't seem to care) Liz: Music from "The Tick"? Hey Monsoon, you DO know that The Mick is short for Mickey Mantle, don't you? Monsoon: Does he have a theme song? Liz: Well, no. Monsoon: Well then SHUT UP! Ventura: Hey, watch it Monsoon. ("Smells like Teen Spirit") Duggan Fan: And his opponent, coming down the aisle, he is a member of the VWO, and is currently the RSPWWCW Television Champion, CHRIS 'DIAMONDBACK' MYERS! (crowd cheers) Monsoon: Liz, what are your opinions on the other VWO members such as Eddy, Henry, and Dollywood? Liz: Well, D-wood's a great wrestler....hey, I take nothing from him. But occasionally he just feels he has to get a little froggy with the Taskmistress. I really don't like Robert Wallace Eddy. the guy is the lowest of the low...the scum of the earth....and he isn't a good wrestler either. Which, ya know, if it was a strategic advantage, I could see it. But Dollywood just did that because he knew it would make me mad. John Henry wouldn't make us mad. But Dollywood picked Eddy specifically because he knew it would piss me off. (Myers and Mick lock up, and Myers gives a hip-toss, but misses an elbowdrop.) Liz: Swing and a miss by Myers. (Both get up, and Mick gives Myers a hip-toss, and HE misses an elbowdrop.) Liz: Swing and a miss by Mick. (Lock up again, and the Mick with a go-behind into a waistlock.) Liz: Watch out, Chris.. I heard the Mick's a switch hitter. Monsoon: Well, you can't argue that this match is even so far. Ventura: Man. It's highly perceptive comments like those that make me miss commentating. (Myers elbows him in the jaw, then clotheslines him. Hits a kneedrop, and covers for a count of 2. Myers throws Mick off the ropes and hits with a flying shoulder-tackle.) Liz: Chris Myers doing one of them Pete Rose moves. Ventura: What, he's betting on the VWO matches? Liz: For his sake, he better not. (Throws Mick into the turnbuckle, charges, but Mick gets a boot up. Mick hops up to the second turnbuckle, and bulldogs Myers. Mick with an elbowdrop, and a cover for a count of 2. Both get up and Mick runs off the ropes. Myers ducks a clothesline, and catches Mick when he comes back. Hoists him up, and stun-guns him.) Monsoon: Oh, that could be it! A cover, and a kickout at two! Ventura: I'm impressed with the Mick, lasting THIS long. (Myers indicates the cutter-sign and picks up Mick.) Ventura: He's going for it! (crowd cheers) (Mick kicks Myers in the gut, then DDT's him.) Monsoon: Oh, I don't believe it! THAT will give you a negative attitude. Ventura: Mick is slow to cover him! Monsoon: A cover, and he kicks out! (Mick goes to the top turnbuckle and Myers hits him when he gets there.) Ventura: Mick took WAY too long. Monsoon: Is Myers going for a superplex? NO! It's a diamond cutter from the top! Liz: BANG!!!!! Monsoon: He covers Mick, and gets the pinfall. ("Smells like Teen Spirit") Duggan Fan: Your winner, and STILL RSPWWCW Television Champion, CHRIS MYERS! Liz: Now THAT'S something you'll be seeing for a while. A VWO member constantly defeating their less-than-deserving opponents. Monsoon: Well Liz, I wish I could say we enjoyed having you here, but I can't. Ventura: Speak for yourself, Monsoon. Liz: I wish I could say I enjoyed being near you, but I can't. Monsoon: Any last thoughts on next week's match at In Your House? Liz: Well, these three rats, are gonna see what the three baddest BitchesWith Attitude on the planet can do. We are more vicious. We are more dominant. And we are frankly, dirtier players. You don't think we have something in store for those jabronies? We will get to them WHERE it hurts. We don't believe that any three wrestlers, male, female, or whatever, can possibly take us. Monsoon: Thank you very much. We'll be right back folks, with the Extreme Belt on the line, Maritimer joining us for commentary, and the Cage Match! Stay tuned! End of Hour One.
