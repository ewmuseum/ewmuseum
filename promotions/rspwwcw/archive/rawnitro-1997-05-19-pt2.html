RawNitro, 19 May 1997, Part 2

19 May 1997 (posted 20 May)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
****************** RSPWWCW RawNitro 5/19 ****************** (RawNitro intro. Fireworks go off.) ******************************** KOTR First Round Match: FunkyM vs. Mattie Carrington (non-title match) ******************************** (Theme from Dynasty.) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this next contest is a first-round, King of the Ring match-up. Coming down the aisle, she is the RSPWWCW Television Champion, MATTIE CARRINGTON! (Flash Funk theme) Finkel: And her opponent, currently on his way to the ring, he considers himself to be "too kewl": FUNKYM! McMahon: Alright, FunkyM against Mattie Carrington. It should be noted that the TV title is not at stake, as this is a King of the Ring match. (Bell rings. Mattie goes for FunkyM but he moves out of the way. She turns and he dropkicks her. She doesn't fall, so he dropkicks her again. She still doesn't fall.) Ventura: Some people are built like a brick shit-house. She's as TOUGH as a brick shit-house. (FunkyM runs off the ropes, but Mattie catches him and gives a belly to belly. Leans off the ropes and misses an elbowdrop. They get up and FunkyM pokes her in the eyes, and dropkicks her, sending her over the ropes.) McMahon: FunkyM now, running off the ropes and does a plancha over the top rope! Ventura: He has to keep using those arial moves, McMahon. Overwise, he's as good as gone. (FunkyM gets back in the ring. Mattie gets in at the count of 7. FunkyM runs off the ropes and bodypresses her, but she catches him and powerslams him. Cover, count of 2.) McMahon: Mattie Carrington, throws FunkyM to the turnbuckle. She charges towards him, and he moves out the way. Ventura: She's almost off her feet! McMahon: FunkyM climbs the top rope, he jumps hits her with a moonsault! A cover, and a count of 3! He gets it! (Flash Funk theme) Finkel: The winner of the match, FUNKYM! McMahon: FunkyM advances in the tournament, but who's that? Who's that coming to the ring? Ventura: It's New Demolition! McMahon: It is! They're attacking FunkyM, stomping and kicking! Crunch has him, and gives him a back-breaker! Ventura: They're setting him up for the decapitation! McMahon: Looks like it, and it is, they hit it! Now they're attacking Mattie Carrington, Crunch manages to get her set up, and Hatchet lands with that elbow! (New Demolition leave) McMahon: Why did they do that? Neither of them are in a tag-team, there's no reason. Ventura: That's exactly the reason, McMahon. Noone was expecting it. Although Hatchet did look a little out of breath. McMahon: Well, both of them are getting up, FunkyM is leaving, and Mattie is almost to her feet. Looks like they'll be fine. Remember folks, we still have the World title up for grabs tonight as well as the Maritimer defending against the creatures. NOW who's that coming down the aisle? Ventura: That looks like Jeff Despres. He's coming over here. Despres: Mr. McMahon, Mr. Ventura, I'm here to complain about the way the residents of Saint John and the province of New Brunswick have been treated with the moving of RawNitro. We mad as heck, and we aren't gonna take it anymore, or something like that. Therefore, I have started the NBLRC: New Brunswickans for a Live Rawnitro Campaign. Our goal is to get RSPWWCW to hold a live RawNitro IN New Brunswick. We will not stop until that is met. Thank you for your time. (Despres leaves) Ventura: What the hell was that? McMahon: I really don't know. (perfect symphany) McMahon: But this I do know, it's time for the Maritimer to face the creatures. ************************************* RSPWWCW Intergender Matches: Maritimer vs. Creatures (continued) ************************************* (Maritimer comes out to the ring and is handed a mic) Maritimer: Whew. Wrestling a bunch of mindless bimbos is harder than it looks. Oh well, time to bring out our next contestant. ("I'm just a sexy girl") Maritimer: Coming down the aisle, one of the girls me and Deadshot beat the shit out of, so we could get our belts back. She thinks she's sexy, but we know she's not, one of the members of the VWO, as well as those asinine women, POWRHUG "PLEASE USE K-Y" KARE! McMahon: You'd think he'd be tired of insulting them. Ventura: You'd think he'd be tired, period. (bell rings. Kare and Maritimer just start slugging it out. Maritimer knocks her down, but Kare gives him a leg sweep, knocking him down as well. She jumps on him, and starts choking. Breaks at four. Chokes again, and breaks at four.) McMahon: C'mon ref, disqualify her! Ventura: She's got until the count of five, McMahon! (Kare gets to her feet, and stomps him. As he gets up, she kicks him in the gut, setting him up for a DDT.) McMahon: DDT! Ventura: That could do it! McMahon: The Maritimer kicks out at two. Kare helps him up, just to give him a neck-breaker! Another cover, and a kickout at 2 and a half. Powrhug Kare now, letting the Maritimer get to his feet. Ventura: What is she setting him up for? McMahon: She grabs him and gives a back suplex! A cover, and a foot on the ropes saves the Maritimer. Ventura: That's two times tonight that has saved him. (Kare drags Maritimer to the centre of the ring, and lets him get to his feet again. Another back suplex, but this time Maritimer flips over on this feet. Kare turns around, and gets a reverse atomic drop, followed with a belly-to-belly suplex.) McMahon: What a recovery by the Maritimer, a cover, and a kickout at only one and a half. Ventura: Kare is still too fresh. McMahon: Maritimer now with a backbreaker. Now what's he doing, he's standing over her, jumps up and SITS ON HER FACE! Ventura: He must've heard that she kisses ass and wanted in on it. McMahon: The ref is counting, and Kare kicks out at two. Maritimer picking up Kare, throws her to the ropes and a clothesline, nope she ducked, off the ropes and a spinning heel kick by Kare! (Kare gestures to the crowd that it's over. Maritimer gets on his hands and knees.) Ventura: This could be it! (Kare goes to picks him up, and he puts her in a small package.) McMahon: He gets her in a small package, and he pins her! (perfect symphony. Maritimer rolls out of the ring before Kare can react.) McMahon: Now there's Maritimer against Liz, and the World title match between Epstein and Schlesinger. Waitaminute. There seems to be some kind of commotion in the back. Let's see if the cameras can take you there. (big screen shows two large groups fighting in the back. Maritimer starts to walk back. Kare stays in the ring) Ventura: Hey, that's the VWO and the JWO! And there's Epstein! McMahon: And Evan's in there too! How in the world did this get started? Ventura: Who knows, McMahon. All I know is that the officials aren't able to do a damn thing about it. McMahon: The Maritimer sees what's going on, and he's confused. We don't know what this means folks, but I don't think we'll be able to bring you the Epstein/Evan match. Ventura: That only means.. (Because the night: Natalie Merchant) McMahon: You don't think... (Liz shows up at the entrance. Maritimer's jaw drops. Starts shaking his head and yelling "NO! NOT NOW! I'M NOT READY!") McMahon: Maritimer wants no part of the Taskmistress, and Kare is standing in the ring! Ventura: You'd think that this was planned or something. Maybe that's it! McMahon: Are you saying that the Taskmistress planned this? Ventura: Her, and the VWO! They attack Epstein, start a brawl, and the match can't start! That way there's no break for the Maritimer, just in case he beats Kare! Brilliant! (Liz walks down the aisle and Maritimer enters the ring. Turns and looks at Liz. Kare gets behind Maritimer, and he gives her the stunner. He rolls her out the ring and asks for a mic) Maritimer: What the HELL is this? The World title match is supposed to be now, and now this shit happens! Fine. Ladies and gentlemen, my FINAL opponent. From the sewer depths of California, someone who should stayed in bed with Commissioner DugganFan, a member of the VWO, the LEADER of the smelly cunts, LIZ "MY TITS ARE FAKE" MICHAELS! McMahon: Oh my goodness! Ventura: Liz isn't waiting for the bell to ring! (They immediately start brawling. Liz wins the fight, and Maritimer falls to the mat. Maritimer gets back up, and Liz hits him with an elbow. Maritimer staggers into the corner and Liz throws him to the opposite corner. Maritimer slumps in the corner as Liz comes over and starts stomping.) McMahon: The Taskmistress is NOT give the Maritimer a chance to breathe. Ventura: Well, when the others have, they got pinned! McMahon: Liz now lifting up the Maritimer for a standing suplex. Ventura: Now THAT'S impressive, McMahon! McMahon: Maritimer falls, but not towards the ring! He, he.. (Maritimer lands straddling over the turnbuckles. All men in the crowd faint, including McMahon.) Ventura: McMahon. McMahon! Fine, didn't need you anyway, wimp. Liz climbs the turnbuckles. She might give a powerplex, no she's going to the top, and gives Maritimer a superplex! Maritimer is not moving! But instead of pinning him, Liz is walking to a corner. Uh oh. She's tuning up the band for that Sweet Bitch Music. Maritimer still not getting up. She should've pinned him. No wait, he's starting to move. Maritimer's getting on his feet, and Liz is getting ready! (Suddenly New Demolition enter the ring.) Ventura: What the hell? Where did THEY come from? (They attack the Maritimer with double axehandles as Liz just stands there with an incredious look on her face. Bell rings.) Ventura: What's the decision? (New Demolition see Liz, and Hatchet fights her as Crunch stomps on the Maritimer. Kare comes in, and fights Crunch.) Finkel: The winner of this bout, as a decision of disqualification, the MARITIMER! Ventura: The Maritimer does it! But I don't think he knows. Is he awake? What the? It's Deadshot! He's got a shotgun! (Deadshot charges the ring, and starts swinging. All four exit the ring, and New Demolition go through the crowd.) Ventura: New Demolition now running through the arena. It must be cold, because Crunch is wearing his black T-shirt again. Now Liz and Kare are looking at Deadshot. (Liz and Kare get on the apron, on opposite sides of the ring. Deadshot stands over Maritimer, holding shotgun.) Ventura: Now the creatures are charging the ring! (Natasha, Sasskia, and Sable charge the ring. As they enter the ring, Deadshot turns to attack. Liz and Kare enter the ring and jump him from behind. All five creatures beat up on Deadshot, and kick away the shotgun, and Sable hits him with the bat. Ventura: Deadshot is in deep trouble now! (ATM LaRiche shows up, with a 12-pack of Pepsi.) Ventura: LaRiche is here! That's Pepsi she's carrying. Oh no. I think that's taking it too far. Deadshot had a problem, and he faced it and moved on. This shouldn't happen. (LaRiche walks down to the ring as the creatures still beat up on Deadshot. Sable hits him with the bat again. Deadshot is lying down on his back, and Natasha/Sasskia hold down one arm, and Kare holds down the other. Liz sits on Deadshot's chest, and LaRiche hands her the Pepsi.) Ventura: C'mon Liz, don't do it. Deadshot has already fought his inner demons. (LaRiche holds down Deadshot's arm with Kare. Liz opens a can and starts pouring it in his mouth.) Ventura: There's no call for this! None whatsoever. The Maritimer is starting to move. He's getting up. Kare sees this and goes to him. (Kare starts slapping Maritimer as Liz pours the Pepsi. Deadshot starts thrashing around, but the creatures hold him.) Ventura: Kare is now FONDLING the Maritimer! Ow! Now she kicked right where she was fondling! (Liz finishes with the can. The creatures get up to leave. Maritimer manages to stay on his feet. Liz sees him, and hits him with the Sweet Bitch Music. Maritimer falls to the mat unconscious. The creatures leave.) Ventura: That's all the time we have. Show up next week for RAWNITRO! Get the hell up McMahon... (The show ends with the camera on the ring, showing Maritimer laid out and Deadshot coughing in a puddle of Pepsi.)
