IN YOUR HUTT: THE EMPIRE STRIKES BACK (Star Wars Theme) ---------------------------------------------------------------------- When we last joined you, Lord Darth Vader, seeing the imminent demise of the Empire, sought out allies in 20th century Earth, a planet far far away, in a place in the distant, distant future. John Henry, along with Hector Guerrero, two wrestlers of the time, had become proficient in time travel. Thusly, this small alliance called RSPWWCW, was able to gain power through the theft of technology from the future, while using their mediocre pugilisitic skills as a diversion. Having travelled to our world once before, these odd warriors were now on a return trip. It is quite an unusual alliance for Lord Vader, who values order, while RSPW values chaos. Perhaps Lord Vader hopes to use this confusion to confound us. Clearly this is a time of great expectation and also of fear for those who cherish freedom. May the Force be with us.
