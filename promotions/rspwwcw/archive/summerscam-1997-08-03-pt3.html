RSPWWCW SummerScam, Hour Three, 3 August 1997

3 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
******************* RSPWWCW SummerScam: Hour THREE ******************* *************************************** [name withheld] vs. "Iron Man" Mike Malecki *************************************** ("More More More" - Andrea True) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is set for one fall. Currently in the ring, from Santa Monica, California. He is [name withheld]! ("Banned in the USA". Crowds cheers.) Finkel: And his opponent, making his way down to ringside, from Ontario Canada. MIKE MALECKI! Ross: Mike Malecki, going back to his "Iron Man" days. Lawler: He made of something, but it's not iron! (Bell rings. Malecki and [name withheld] lock up. Malecki with a takedown.) Ross: [name withheld] countering with a wrap-around, going into a waist lock. Malecki reaches for the ropes, but [name withheld] gives him a back suplex! Lawler: Oh yeah, Malecki's a REAL Iron man! Ross: [name withheld] with a cover, but only a count of one. [name withheld] with an elbowdrop, now a legdrop, and another cover. This time it's a count of two. Lawler: What good is watching a Malecki match if he doesn't DO anything? Ross: [name withheld] throwing Malecki off the ropes, bends his head over, and gets kicked by Malecki! Malecki with a clothesline, now another, AND another! Malecki kicking [name withheld] several times in the stomach. Lawler: [name withheld] grabbed his leg! Ross: And Malecki kicking him in the back of the head with the other! A cover, and a count of two. Malecki picking up [name withheld] for a suplex, but [name withheld] blocks it. Lawler: C'mon, [name withheld]! Ross: [name withheld] reverses and suplexes Malecki! [name withheld], going to the top rope! And he hits a flying head-butt! It's over! Lawler: Who's that coming down to the ring? (crowd cheers) Ross: What the? It's Kelly Vaters! What's she doing here? Vaters jumping on the apron before the ref can make the count, and now the ref arguing with her... Lawler: CanSen's coming through the crowd! (crowd cheers) Ross: And he has a chair! [name withheld] looking at Vaters, and CanSen's entering the ring with a chair! Lawler: LOOK OUT! Ross: Oh my! CanSen has hit [name withheld], and has left the ring. Vaters is getting off the apron, as he sees [name withheld] on the mat unconscious! Malecki crawling over and draping an arm over [name withheld]! Lawler: This isn't fair! Ross: The ref makes the count, and Malecki wins! (Bell rings. Crowd shows appreciation for Malecki with applause) Finkel: The winner of this match, "Ironman" MIKE MALECKI! Ross: That was unusual. Why was CanSen and Vaters helping out Malecki? They have no vendetta with [name withheld]. Folks in a minute you're gonna get the rematch that Blakenstein and en4cer have demanded for a month. ********************************************************* Tag title match: The Insiders vs. Perfection Personified ********************************************************* (Baruch hymn) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this tag team match, is for the RPSWWCW tag team championship! Coming down the aisle, they are the former champions, and members of the JWO. En4cer, Blakenstein, PERFECTION PERSONIFIED! (ECW Barely Legal theme) Finkel: And their opponents, wrestling only their second match. They are the RSPWWCW Tag Team Champions, "The Lariat" Dave Scherer and John D. Williams, the INSIDERS! Ross: The Fink hit the nail on the head. This only the Insider's second tag match, since they haven't defended the titles for a month. Lawler: Hey, they don't HAVE to for a month! (Bell rings. JDW and Blakenstein start off.) Ross: Williams throws Blakenstein off the ropes and hits a flying leg lariat! Lawler: I thought it was a spinning heel kick. Ross: I'm the commentator, I get to call it what I want. Williams picking up Blakenstein and giving him a scoop slam. Williams running off the ropes, and hits with a Flying shoulder tackle. A cover, and a count of two. Lawler: See? The Insiders don't NEED to wrestle more than once a month. Ross: Williams with a swinging neckbreaker. Now he tags in Scherer who comes in with a dropkick. Lawler: Can Perfection Personified raise their level of competitiveness to a level where they are at LEAST competitive? Ross: Scherer goes for the slam on Blakenstein, but Blakenstein goes behind and tags en4cer! En4cer coming in and hits with a low blow! Lawler: Well, I guess someone is gonna join the boy's choir after this match. Ross: En4cer with a headbutt to the kidneys, and now a belly-to-belly! A cover, and a count of two. Lawler: Okay, maybe they ARE competitive... Ross: En4cer picking up Scherer, and puts him up for a vertical suplex! Lawler: He's THAT strong? Ross: Williams has had enough! He charges at en4cer, but gets backbodydropped over the top! Now Blakenstein attacks Williams out the ring, and is sticking his between the railings! Lawler: WHAT? Ross: I can only call what I see, and Williams head is stuck between the railings! Blakenstein is going back to his corner, and is tagged in by en4cer. Blakenstein climbing to the top as en4cer lifts up Scherer for a piledriver, and they hit a SPIKE piledriver! A cover, a count, and it's over! (Bell rings. Baruch hymn.) Finkel: The winner of the match, and NEW RSPWWCW tag team champions, PERFECTION PERSONIFIED! Ross: PP getting the win, and I think they deserved it. Lawler: Oh shut up Ross. *********************************************** World title match: Alex Demchuk vs. Paul Smith *********************************************** (Stone Cold theme. Crowd boos) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our final match and is for the RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Championship! On his way to the ring, Stone Cold, PAUL SMITH! Ross: The Vancouver crowd not liking Stone Cold. Lawler: I don't know why, he's a fine, upstanding individual. (Crippler theme. Crowd goes apeshit) Finkel: And his opponent, coming down the aisle. He is the RSPWWCW WORLD Heavyweight Champion, "The Canadian Crippler" ALEX DEMCHUK! Ross: The Canadian fans love the man from Regina, Saskatchewan! (Bell rings. Smith and Demchuk lock up.) Ross: Smith headbutting Demchuk and throwing him into the corner. Smith, starting to stomp away at Demchuk. Demchuk grabbing his leg, and pulling him off his feet. Now Demchuk is on top of Smith and is pounding away at him! Lawler: No! Get away Paul! Ross: Both men get to their feet and Smith with a clothesline! Now Smith throwing Demchuk off the ropes and hits him with a spikeslam! A cover, and a count of 2. Lawler: We are looking at the NEW world champion, Ross! Ross: I'm glad YOU think so, King. Smith throwing Demchuk off the ropes, no, a reversal, and a POWERSLAM! A cover, and a count of two. Lawler: I can't believe that Demchuk did that. Whoop his ass, Smith! Ross: Demchuk, setting Smith up for a piledriver, but Smith back-bodydrops him! Demchuk getting up and charges Smith with a clothesline. Smith ducks, and follows Demchuk to the ropes and clotheslines him over! Lawler: They're BOTH going over! Wait, that's Waru! Ross: Where? It is! Waru is coming down the aisle! Waru, who didn't get to wrestle tonight because of Stone Cold, is coming down to ringside! Waru attacking Smith outside the ring! Lawler: Disqualify him, ref! Ross: For what, fighting outside the ring? Demchuk is getting back in the ring, but Waru now grabs Smith and throws him into John D. Williams, whose head is STILL stuck in the barracades! Lawler: Man, I forgot all about him! Ross: You weren't the only one, evidently. The ref is still counting, and now he's signalling for the bell! (The Crippler theme. Crowd cheers wildly.) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, the winner of the match, as a result of a countout, and STILL RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Champion, "The Canadian Crippler" ALEX DEMCHUK! Ross: Demchuk wins the match, but Waru and Smith are STILL going at it! Folks, we're out of time, join us tomorrow night for RSPWWCW RawNitro!
