Weaselmania, 23 March 1997, Part 1

23 March 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Weaselmania from Bob Carpenter Center Announcers: McMahon, Jerry Lawler, Bobby Heenan Vince: HEEEEELLLLLLLLLOOOOOOOOOOO, everay-boday from WEEEEEAAAAAAASSSSELLLLLLmania at the Bob Carpenter Center in Newark, Delaware!! I'm Vince McMahon with Jerry Lawler, waiting for the weasel himself, Bobby Heenan, to come to the booth. <<--->> <Wrestlemania theme song> <<--->> Vince: And HERE HE COMES!! Remember, tonight from WEEEEEEEEEAAAAAAASSSSELLLLLLmania, we're gonna see Dollywood Tom Zylkin battle Waru for the World Title, Kare challenge Paul Smith for the TV Title, Intergender Champ Liz will defend against Slugger123 Phil Stern, Alexandra Theresa Marlena LaRiche defends the Cruiserweight Title against the Blue Avenger, Coot Thompson meets Alex Demchuk in a Newark Street Fight, Amoja Sumler meets Dean Boardwalk, and much, much more!! Including a tag team match between the Power Macs Conn MacKintosh and [name withheld] and a team yet to be announced by Interim Commissioner as of this Monday, Duggan Fan 1. <<--->> <Loud chants of "WEASEL! WEASEL!"> <<--->> Vince: Just listen to this capacity crowd of around 40,000 greet the Brain!! King: Just shut up, McMahon. This is Delaware, there's no intelligent life anywhere. Vince: I'm sure the DuPont family would not agree. King: Oh yeah, the DuPont family! Just a bunch of inbreeding schizo wrestler wannabes. Brain: What are we talking about? The USWA? King: Hey! I resemble that remark!! Vince: We've got a Cruiserweight title match now. The Blue Avenger will challenge Alexandra Theresa Marlena LaRiche for the belt. King: That LaRiche is more than a mouthfull. Brain: She's more than a handfull, too. <<--->> <Music: "Wannabe" -- Spice Girls> <<--->> Vince: Wait a second, who are those ladies with the Blue Avenger? King: It's the Spice Girls, McMahon! The most truly dynamic force in music today. Brain: They're some handfulls, too. Especially that Easy V. King: The only Easy V I can think of is sitting next to me, and it ain't Pinzer. <<--->> Michael Pinzer: Our opening contest will be for the Cruiserweight Championship and is set for one fall. Introducing first, accompanied to the ring by the SPICE GIRLS, from the United Kingdom: The BLUUUUUUUEEE Aveeeeeengerrrrr!!!!!! <Music: Goldust theme> Pinzer: And his opponent, the champion: from Hollywood, California......she weighs 132 pounds, Alexandra.......Theresa....Marlena......LaRiiiiccchhe!! <<--->> Vince: Well, it must be WEASELMANIA if the Spice Girls are here. King: It must be Weaselmania if Heenan is here. Brain: Otherwise you'd just have the Spammies and some modern dance study. Vince: The two combatants shake hands in the center of the ring....NO..LaRiche with a cheap shot to the midsection. Brain: She's off to a good start. King: She doesn't stand a chance with the Spice Girls here. Vince: Enziguiri by LaRiche to the back of the Avenger's head. Brain: That's the exact same move that won Epstein the championship. Vince: Avenger goes to the outside and LaRiche has got her whip and is trying to hit him with it. Get in there, ref! King: What's the matter, Blue Avenger? Afraid of a girl? Vince: LaRiche arguing with referee Mike Asparagus....and the Blue Avenger BLINDSIDES her with a forearm shot!! He rolls her into the ring and goes up to the top and NAILS her with a missile dropkick!! Brain: It's like a riot at a shotgun chapel!! King: The Spice Girls are like the choir, right? Brain: Oh wait, I forgot about them. OK.....It's like a riot at a Catholic high school!!!! Vince: Let's forget about the Spice Girls for now and concentrate on this tremendous action. After all, it is WEASELMANIA, folks. King: Who cares? The Spice Girls are here. I'm gonna go meet 'em. Vince: LaRiche with a TREMENDOUS lariat. And now she nails him with a massive powerbomb. Well, the Blue Avenger gave it a good effort but this will be all. 1, 2, She got 'im...NO SHE DIDN'T!! Brain: He got that shoulder up. Vince: Jerry Lawler, come back here!! You're missing this match!! Brain: Who is this Blue Avenger punk anyway? Vince: Surely you remember when he beat Dollywood Tom Zylkin? Brain: I'm telling ya, he cheated!! Ask Dollywood. Vince: Oh yeah, he would know, right? Brain: Well he was there, wasn't he? Vince: Tombstone piledriver by LaRiche!! King: Hey, McMahon!! Victoria wanted my autograph!! Vince: A COVER!!!! No she didn't get him!! Alexandra Theresa Marlena LaRiche attempting a powerbomb....REVERSED into a FRANKENSTEINER by the Blue Avenger! Blue Avenger going up top! Brain: Something's about to fall onto Alexandra from the Great Blue Yonder!!! Vince: FLYING CROSSBODY!!! 1! 2! 3!!!!! He got her!!! Brain: There he goes again!! Cheating as always! King: He ain't nothing without the Spice Girls!! Vince: Spice Girls or not, he's the new Cruiserweight champ!! <<--->> <Music: "Wannabe" -- Spice Girls> Pinzer: Here is your winner and NEEEWW Cruiserweight Champion.....the Blue Avengerrrrrr!!! <<--->> Vince: Wait a second!! The Spice Girls are making their way over here!!! King: Probably gonna thank me. Spice Girl Victoria: We want to thank you personally, Hulkster, for giving us your autograph. We've seen all your movies. King: Aww shucks! It was nothing. Vince: WAIT A SECOND, JERRY LAWLER!! You told these lovely young ladies that you were Hulk Hogan? That's a disgrace, Jerry Lawler, and you know it!! Brain: Going bald on us, King? King: Hey, let's just concentrate on the great action here at......whatever it's called. Vince: You mean Weaselmania? Brain: I'm beginning to dislike the tone of your voice when you say that. Vince: Our next match will be between 4 Mexican mini stars. We'll be joined in the booth by Mike Tenay. <<--->> <Music: Rough and Ready theme> Pinzer: Introducing team #1, from Vizh's mailbox, at a combined weight of 334 pounds....the team of MiniVizh and Gernatita Blanston. <MiniVizh and Gernatita Blanston are wearing mini-Blackjack outfits, complete with fake black mustaches, cowboy hats, and wigs> <<--->> <Music: "We're not the Mounties" -- old Quebecer's theme> Pinzer: And their opponents...at a combined weight of 254 pounds, the team of Netcopcito and Evaniiiiiiiiitoooooo!!! <Evanito looks like a Mini Kevin Sullivan while Netcopcito looks like a Mini Mountie> <<--->> Vince: Some tremendous vertically challenged competitors from South of the border. Welcome to the booth, Mike Tenay. Mike: This looks like a great match-up. Vince: Evanito and Gernatita Blanston start off. Luchastyle armdrag by Evanito. Mike: And a devastating crescent kick by Evanito. Evanito dropkicks Gernatita Blanston into the corner. Netcopcito comes out and dropkicks Blanston. MiniVizh comes out from his corner! He tries to hit Evanito with a lariat but he ducks and MiniVizh ends up nailing his partner! Netcopcito nails MiniVizh with a spin wheel kick! A cover by Netcopcito on MiniVizh and a 2 count. Vince: But they aren't the legal men! Brain: Since when did this matter? King: Well they're the legal men now! Mike: Netcopcito whips MiniVizh into the ropes and gives him a monkeyflip. Now Evanito and Gernatita Blanston hit the ring.....and Netcopcito MONKEYFLIPS EVANITO ONTO THE OPPOSING TEAM!!! Vince: TREMENDOUS MANEUVER!! Brain: Do these guys do windows?? Mike: A tremendous somersault senton plancha by Evanito out of Netcopcito's monkeyflip. The three of them are on the outside.....AND HERE COMES NETCOPCITO!!! Suicide dive tope through the ropes onto Gernatita Blanston!! Vince: We should have THESE guys on Shotgun!! Brain: They MUST be on Prozac! Mike: Evanito with an ASAI MOONSAULT onto MiniVizh!! Netcopcito FRANKENSTEINERS Gernatita Blanston into the ring!! Vince: Only a two-count!! Mike: He tries to give him another Frankensteiner but Gernatita Blanston counters with a powerbomb. Vince: He tags in MiniVizh! MiniVizh attempts a lariat but Netcopcito ducks!! MiniVizh keeps on going and knocks Evanito off the apron to the floor!! King: It's like pinball, McMahon! Vince: Gernatita Blanston comes in and BULLDOGS Netcopcito from behind!! MiniVizh covers!! He got 'im!!!! <<--->> <Music: Rough and Ready> Pinzer: Here are your winners: the team of MiniVizh and Gernatita Blaaaaanstoonnnn!! <<--->> <Music: "Shake Shake Shake, Shake Shake Shake, Shake Yo' Bootay!!"> Pinzer: This following match is scheduled to be a "Newark Street Fight" with falls counting anywhere in the Bob Carpenter Center. Introducing first, from Dance Beat, USA......the Dancer and Financer for the Virtual World Order.....he weighs 226 pounds.....the "Disco Trillionaire"....Joseph "Coot" Thooompsssonnnnn!!!> <Coot comes in with a lime-green jumpsuit and blazer with dollar sign on back. He's wearing a transparent framed pair of "Foakleys."> King: Disco is here, McMahon!! And he's gonna make a comeback. Vince: This is one strange character.....how did he make his money again? Brain: C'mon, Vince!! He was the most popular icon on the La$ Vega$ Circuit!! The humanoids love him!! Vince: It's funny how the VWO has all these "icons." <<--->> <Music: Benoit's music> Pinzer: And his opponent, from Toronto, Canada....he weighs 237 pounds: The "Canadian CRIPPLER" Aleeeex Demchuuuk!! Vince: Quick start for this one!!! Coot nails Demchuk with his Golden Record!! Brain: Actually, it's a platinum record. Vince: Looks more like plastic from here......anyway.....Coot stomping on Demchuk in the corner. Making his first appearance as a ref in RSPWWCW is Oliver Posthlewaite, who is wisely letting them brawl without getting involved. King: He's not supposed to get involved McMahon!! This is a Street Fight!! Brain: You know, the Bob Carpenter Center is a pretty big place, how are we gonna be sure they stay in the arena area? What if they go to the basketball court? Vince: And what's gonna happen IF THEY GO OUTSIDE?? Everyone is just gonna have to bear with us. After all, it is WEASELMANIA!! Brain: And what happens if they go into a Delaware University school hall?? What happens then?? Vince: Coot with a swinging neckbreaker!! He's ALL OVER HIM!! A European uppercut rocks Demchuk!! King: Knocked the taste out of his braces!! Vince: Demchuk fighting back! He floors Coot with a TREMENDOUS right hand!! Coot rolls to the outside and Alex Demchuk follows. Brain: LOOK OUT, JOSEPH THOMPSON!! Vince: He NAILS him with the ringsteps!!! Now he's putting the steps on Thompson's head......ARABIAN FACE-BUSTER!! King: I think you mean Canadian Face-Buster. After all, Alex Demchuk originated the move. I know I've never seen anyone else do it. Vince: A cover on the outside!!! HE GOT 'IM....NO HE DIDN'T!! Brain: They're fighting up through the crowd now! King: And they're hitting eachother with everything!! Just think about it, McMahon. No rules!! THIS IS GREAT!!! Brain: Why don't you add an "Oh my God!" in there? King: Wait....I don't get it... Vince: They've reached the top....AND THEY'RE LEAVING THE ARENA AREA!! Brain: They're headed towards the tennis courts!! Vince: Jim Powers and Barry Horowitz are clearing a path for the two grapplers. AND DEMCHUK SENDS THOMPSON RIGHT THROUGH THE FRONT OF A COKE MACHINE!! <<--->> <Coot has blood all over his face and torso.> <<--->> King: Look at the Disco Trillionaire!! He's sweating!! Vince: They're heading through the tennis crowd!! Apparently, the University of Delaware has a women's collegiate match today. Brain: Coot's fallen and he's rolling down the bleachers....and takes about a 5 foot drop onto the playing surface!! And they're playing a point, too!! Vince: Alex Demchuk with a running plancha out of the crowd!! Posthewaite gets tangled up with a tennis player!! They're all in a heap!! Thompson grabs the racket and smashes it into a dozen pieces on Demchuk's head!! King: Now Demchuk's sweating!! Vince: A cover by Coot!! The Disco Trillionaire wins!! Brain: Looks like they're all in trouble now!! Vince: University Police have arrested our camera man, Joseph Thompson, Oliver Posthlewaite, and Alex Demchuk. And the tennis player is crying. King: Hey! It's the Campus Cops!! <<--->> Pinzer: Ladies and Gentlemen, due to the pinfall in an hostile location, this match has been ruled a no-contest. <<--->> Brain: Thompson won that!! You can't take that away from him! <<--->> <Music: "WE ARE THE NATION....OF DOMINATION" followed by Heavenly Bodies music.> Pinzer: This following contest will be for the RSPWF Extreme Bandwidth Belt with Extreme Style Rules. Introducing first, from studvile,usa: he weighs 264 pounds, he is 1/2 of the tag team the "Heavenly Butts" of the Nation of Domination with partner "Dr. Love" Oleg Issers, the "gigalo" Mark A. Davvvvvvvenport! <<--->> <Music: Theme from "Dark Shadows"> Pinzer: And his opponent: from Baltimore, Maryland, this person weighs 284 pounds.....the EXTREME Bandwidth CHAMPION.......Raveeeenn!!! <<--->> Vince: The NOD is not at ringside, but I have no doubt that they'll be here sooner or later. Raven had better watch out. And they start out swinging. Remember, this is the same title Scaia was defending in RSPWF, but before he went back to RSPWF he lost the belt to Jeff Katz, who gave it back after being forced to when RSPWF started. When RSPWF folded, Paul E. Dangerously bought up its stock and declared Katz to be the rightful champion. Then Katz lost to Raven at UNKILLFILED when Rick Scaia interfered against Katz. Scaia lost it to Katz when Kare distracted the referee and Disco Coot broke up his Figure Eight on Katz. Although since then, the NWO and the VWO have had a falling out. Since, Dangerously obtained the belts however, he made the Bandwidth Belt the Extreme Bandwidth Belt to be defended with Extreme-style rules. Davenport was a long-time Jobber Belt champ in RSPWF. Right now, he seems to have found his place in the Nation of Domination. King: Davenport is as dangerous as they come on his own, so he may not need them. Vince: I doubt that, King. Raven starts out with the advantage with a lariat!! He, or is it *she*, plants him with a piledriver!! Brain: Wait! Now where's he...er...or she going?? Vince: He...I mean *Raven* is going to the outside!! Raven grabs a chair!! Brain: Let the games begin!! Vince: Raven lays the chair on the mat and PILEDRIVES THE GIGOLO ONTO IT!! And here comes the Nation!! Raven tries to get a cover on Davenport, but they are all over him...er....uh...or her I mean. Brain: They're gonna win the match for Davenport!! Vince: Wait a second!! Raven is fighting back all by himself...er you know what I mean. Down goes Issers, down goes Rastaman, down goes Rob E., DOWN GO SISTER SUNNY AND MICKEY MOUSE!!! HE HIT MICKEY MOUSE!! I DON'T BELIEVE IT!! RAVEN HAS CLEARED THE RING ALL BY....um.......uh...er.... Brain: All by Raven's self.......and by the way, it's Mickey MUHAMMED, not Micky Mouse!! How dare yo call mah brotha bah his slave name!!!! Dis is oppression bah da Ma-an! Mickey Muhammed haz bin used bah da Ma-an tuh suit his purposes, an' now yo is nah ricognizen dat Mickey Muhammed iss now a free man. Word. Vince: Bobby Heenan, I'm worried about you....LOOK OUT RAVEN!! Davenport with a Dragon Suplex and a 3- count from behind!! Davenport, a longtime Jobber Belt Champion, is the new RSPWF Extreme Bandwidth Champion!! King: The Gigolo used some sound strategy there. Vince: Well, the Nation took advantage of those lenient rules. Brain: Lenient rules is better than no rules. Posthlewaite will tell you that. <<--->> <Music: Heavenly Bodies theme> Pinzer: The winner of this match, and NEEEEEEEW Extreme Bandwidth Champion: the "gigalo" Mark A. Davvvvvvvenpoooorrt!! <<--->> Continued in Part 2 *World Title - Waru v. Zylkin *TV Title - P. Smith v. Kare *IG Title - Liz v. Slugger123 *The Power Macs and DugganFan1 *Amoja Sumler v.
