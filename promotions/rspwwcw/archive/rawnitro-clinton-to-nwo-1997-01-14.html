RawNitro, 14 January 1997 ("CLINTON TO NWO")

14 January 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSPWWCW Rawnitro pt.2 Announcers- Tom Brokaw, Jim Ross, Bobby Heenan, Patrick Buchanan <Final Match- Bill Clinton vs. Newt Gingrich> ---------------------------------- <Music-Hail to the Chief> <Clinton enters the ring area, accompanied by Secret Service agents, wearing gym shorts and an Arkansas Razorbacks sweatshirt. During his ring entrance, Newt Gingrich rushes the ring and they start brawling.> ----------------------------------- JR: Now the President and the Speaker brawling. Clinton and Gingrich beating the living daylights out of each other. Prez with a whip into the ropes, Clinton follows up with a shoulder block, and MISSES it. Gingrich now with an armbar on the left arm of Bill Clinton. Buchanan: Bill Clinton is a disgrace to the Presidency, a disgrace to himself and his family, and a disgrace to wrestling. Brokaw: Well, I'm not clear that Bill Clinton claimed to be the greatest wrestler in the world. He does however hold a distinct height, weight and conditioning advantage over Gingrich. Brain: Yeah, and he can chase babes around his desk at a longer pace! JR: Clinton with a hair pull of Gingrich breaks the hold. Rake of the face by the President. Forearm to the back of the head by Clinton. And another. Clinton goes up to the second turnbuckle. Elbow drop by Bill Clinton dazes the Speaker. Bill Clinton now with the slop drop now on Gingrich, goes for a cover, 1,2, not enough. Clinton going for a chokeslam of Gingrich. Blocked by the legs of Gingrich and Gingrich SUPLEXES the President! Brain: This is desparation time for Gingrich. He was about to get beat. But he pulled a rabbit out of his hat. ----------------------------- JR: Both wrestlers dazed, now Clinton runs the ropes, misses a shoulderblock, Gingrich comes back. Double clothesline and both men go down. The President and the Speaker are both lying in the middle of the ring. The crowd going wild rooting for their respective men as Hebner counts them both. Brokaw: We have a disturbance in the rotunda, there are men running to the ring in black shirts. JR:Wait a minute! It's the NWO! It's the New World Order! Buchanan: Clinton's NWO! Brain: Nash! Hall! Hogan! all the rest of 'em. What are they doing here? JR:The New World Order have come into the ring they are stomping on Newt Gingrich. Hall and Nash are stomping on Newt Gingrich. Bill Clinton is now standing up and pointing, asking what's goin' on? They are holding up Newt Gingrich. Now what? Brain: I gotta bad feelin' about this. JR: Bill Clinton is walking up the NWO. Brokaw: Now secret service agents have surrounded the ring. Brain: OH! NO! HE HIT GINGRICH! CLINTON'S KICKING GINGRICH WHILE HALL AND NASH ARE HOLDING HIM! Naw!!!!!!! I can't believe what I'm seeing. JR: Bill Clinton hooks up Gingrich for the Quarter Pounder, and WHAMMMMM!!!!! Brokaw: I, I, I am stunned, gentlemen. Now I HAVE seen everything. JR: Ladies and gentlemen, the President has betrayed the United States of America. Bill Clinton has beocme a member of New World Order! Buchanan: You people thought I was lying, or I was delusional. I TOLD you all that Bill Clinton is NWO. I've tell telling you that all along. Brain: This is disgusting. I think I'm gonna be sick. That's it. I've had it. I'm leaving. JR: You're leaving the broadcast position? Brain: No, I'm leaving the country! ----------------------------- <Clinton grabs a handheld mic.> Clinton: I just want to thank all you good people. Hey, remember I just love y'all! Now I wanna invite y'all to the NWO Inauguration next Monday. Where I will be installed as the first President of the United States of the New World Order. <boos from audience> Brokaw: We should advise our affiliates that we may be running a little long, to report on this breaking story, that President Clinton has joined the New World Order. <Clinton hugging Hall, Nash and Hogan.> JR: This is disgusting. Here come members of Congress storming the ring. Members of Congress are trying to storm the ring as the NWO beats them away. now what's this Scott Hall has grabbed Strom Thurmond. Scott Hall has picked up Strom Thurmond. He's gonna give Strom Thurmond the Razor's Edge! He's 93 years old, how can he DO that? And here's Trent Lott. Kevin Nash has Trent Lott in the center of the ring, and he JACKNIFES Trent Lott. Now Fred Thompson gets into the ring! And SEVEN GUYS jump on Thompson, and are beating down Fred Thompson. Brokaw: Bob Dole has come into the building. Brain: Don't do it, Bob. Don't come back. You'll get killed. JR: Scott Norton has Bob Dole by his good arm. Norton with an elbow to the back of the head of Bob Dole puts Bob Dole down on the rotunda floor. And he's giving him the CODE RED! Scott Norton with a Code Red on Bob Dole's good arm! Brokaw: Kevin Nash just threw Congressman Kasich into the fourth row. Paul Wellstone now being beaten by the New World Order. Complete carnage in the Capitol. Congressmen lying all over the place. Masohiro Choto choking Patrick Moynihan. Big Bubba beating up Senator Domenici. ----------------- Clinton: Now I want to advise the Congress and the Supreme Court. I am giving you thirty days to change your allegiances from United States of America to the New World Order. Hey, it's up to you. You're either with us, or against us. Brain: Hey, Patrick. What the phone number for the closest militia group? Buchanan: Hey, at this rate, Bobby Heenan, I just may form an alliance with the Nation of Domination and the Carolina Panthers. I think those semi-automatics woulda come in very handy now. I got used pitchforks if ya need 'em! Brokaw: The First Lady, Hillary Rodham Clinton, is coming out with what looks to be a flag cake, except that the American flag is in black and white icing. Clinton: Honey, I want to thank you, and tell the American people how much I appreciate the support that you've given me in my career throughout all these years. And to show you my appreciation, the boys and I have a little surprise for you. Brokaw: President Clinton whispering in the ears of Hall and Nash. Clinton: Okay, boys. Get her! JR: I can't believe this! The NWO is beating up the First Lady! the NWO is beating up the First Lady. Now they're holding her. And Bill Clinton has the cake. He has the cake. And he SMASHES the cake into the face of Hillary. And now Scott Hall is picking up Hillary! He's gonna give Hillary the Razor's Edge. And Hillary is slammed into the canvas. And Chelsea is up in the audience, beside herself! Chelsea is in tears! Now, Bill is picking up Hillary and he's gonna give her the Quarter Pounder. WHHHHHAAAAAAAAAMMMMM!!!!!!!!! Clinton: Hey, now I don't hafta listen to that yap a hers no more! And guys, I wanna introduce to you, a lady that y'all know very well from few months back. Brain: Wait a minute. Who's that woman coming into the rotunda area? Brokaw: This is Sherry Rowlands. Sherry Rowlands, the woman that was involved in the Dick Morris scandal. Sherry Rowlands is now coming into the ring. Clinton: Now, America, I want to introduce you to my new manager, and my new main squeeze, "Sensational" Sherry Rowlands! JR: Bill Clinton, with his new girlfriend, Sensational Sherry Rowlands along with the New World Order are celebrating in the ring, as the 105th Congress is throwing paper and congressional bills into the ring. Hillary is being attended to by medical personnel and is being taken away on a stretcher. We've got to leave you, but I guarantee you you wanna listen to your late local news tonight. As Bill Clinton has sold out America! Brain: That's it. I'm leaving the country. I'm gonna go throw up now. Then I gotta pack.
