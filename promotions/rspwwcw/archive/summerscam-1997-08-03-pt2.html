RSPWWCW SummerScam, Hour Two, 3 August 1997

3 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
******************* RSPWWCW SummerScam ******************* Ross: Now we have the four corners match, and concerning the incident earlier involving New Demolition and the Perfect Canadians, Scheme Gene is in the back with Michelle Steiner. Gene: Thank you Jim. Ms. Steiner, you know what happened, you know that the Perfect Canadians and the New Demolition were in a locker room brawl, and that Deadshot now has an injured knee. The question is, what are you doing about it? Steiner: Well, Maritimer came up to me after the incident and asked if he could still be in the tag match. I agreed, but only with the stipulation that the match be made into an elimination match. That means BOTH team members must be eliminated for that team to be gone. Since Deadshot isn't going to wrestle, that means that Maritimer is the only one that needs to be pinned for the Perfect Canadians to be gone. Gene: Thank you very much, Ms. Steiner, now back to ringside! Ross: Well this is interesting, and may I say, not in the Perfect Canadians advantage. Lawler: Well, he agreed to it, so there you go. ************************ Four corners tag match: Bitches with Attitude New Demolition Power Macs Perfect Canadians ************************ ("Because the Night" - Natalie Merchant. Crowd boos) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is a four corners ELIMINATION tag match. The first team, coming down the aisle, are members of the Creatures of the Night. Liz Michael, ATM LaRiche, the BITCHES with ATTITUDE. (Demolition theme. Crowd REALLY boos) Finkel: The second team, are from parts unkown. They Crunch and Hatchet, the NEW DEMOLITION! Ross: Ever notice that sometimes Crunch is wearing a black T-shirt, and sometimes he's not? Right now he's not, but does he get cold or something? Lawler: You're REALLY stretching for a Hotline story, aren't you Ross? ("Smoke on the Water" - Deep Purple. Crowd boos) Finkel: The third team, are members of the VWO. They are [name withheld], Conn MacKintosh, the POWER MACS! (Perfect symphony. Crowd cheers. Maritimer walks out alone) Finkel: And the final team, represented solely by the Maritimer, the PERFECT CANADIANS! Ross: Remember folks, a wrestler can tag ANYONE else, and BOTH team members must be eliminated. (Bell rings. Maritimer and Liz start off.) Lawler: Oh gee, of all the people to start off, the two that hate each other? Ross: Liz with a headlock on Maritimer. Maritimer throwing Liz off the ropes and gives a kneelift. Maritimer goes to slam Liz, but she blocks it, and delivers one of her own! Lawler: Maritimer is gonna be the first one gone? Ross: It's too bad that he was randomly chosen to lead things off, otherwise he might've had a chance. Liz now tagging in LaRiche and they double suplex Maritimer. LaRiche "wooshing" over Maritimer, who pulls her to the mat and starts pounding on her! Lawler: AH! Tag out, tag out! Ross: Maritimer tagging in Crunch and he DOUBLE clotheslines them! Maritimer rolling out of the ring as Crunch with a belly-to-belly. A cover, and LaRiche kicks out at two. Lawler: Maybe it IS a good thing that it's an elimination match. Ross: Crunch picking up LaRiche and hits a side suplex. He tags Hatchet, and Hatchet climbs the top turnbuckle as Crunch hits a back breaker. Lawler: Could this be... Ross: DECAPITATION! Crunch stops Liz as the ref counts, and LaRiche is the first casualty. Hatchet signalling to the crowd as Grant comes from behind and BULLDOGS him! Grant with a neck-breaker, and doesn't cover! Lawler: Do it! You'll be the only team with two members! Ross: Grant tags in Conn, and it looks like, it is! FATAL CRASH DISK ERROR! Grant gives Crunch a flying forearm as the ref makes the count, and now the Power Macs are the team with the advantage. Lawler: Look out Conn! It's Maritimer! Ross: Maritimer hits with a flying forearm! They both get up, and Conn charges, but misses a clothesline. He turns and Maritimer hits with a spinning heel kick! Lawler: Grant is still fighting with Crunch! Ross: Conn doesn't know where he is and Maritimer puts on the PERFECT-PLEX! Liz is just watching as the ref counts to three and that brings everyone down to one. Lawler: Now Liz is coming in! Ross: And she and Maritimer are trading blows! A dropkick by Maritimer, and Liz rolls to the outside. Lawler: Grant and Crunch are still fighting out of the ring too! Ross: And now Maritimer with a PLANCHA onto Liz! They're getting up, and Liz rams Maritimer into the barricade. She's trying for a piledriver, but Maritimer is blocking it, and now a back bodydrop! They're trading punches, but the referee is still counting! Lawler: NO! The ref is calling for the bell! Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, Liz Michael and the Maritimer are BOTH eliminated, via a COUNTOUT! Ross: Crunch throwing Grant into the ring. Maritimer and Liz are STILL fighting, as more referees come in to try and break it up. Crunch coming in, and Grant hits him with an elbow to the head. Grant going to the top rope, and leaps off, and Crunch catches him! Lawler: AH! Where's the VWO? Ross: You got me, King. Crunch now with a POWERSLAM, and doesn't cover! What is going on? Crunch now climbing the top turnbuckle. Lawler: That guy's gotta be over 300 pounds! Ross: And a FLYING SPLASH! A cover, and he got him! (New Demolition theme. Crowd boos.) Finkel: The winning tag team of this bout, NEW DEMOLITION! Ross: New Demolition will now receive a tag title shot tomorrow night on RawNitro, against either the Insiders or Perfection Personified. ********************************************* Intergender title match: Waru vs. Jeff Amdur ********************************************* (Baruch hymn) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is for the RSPWWCW Intergender title! Currently in the ring, a member of the JWO, JEFF AMDUR! Ross: Amdur, coming straight from France to with us tonight in Vancouver. Lawler: I bet he wants a refund too. ("Princes of the Universe" - Queen) Finkel: And his opponent, on his way to the ring area, from somewhere in Maine, he is the RSPWWCW Intergender champion, the man they call WARU! Ross: Waitaminute, STONE COLD is coming out! He's attacking Waru! Stone Cold Paul Smith is beating up on Waru! He throws Waru into the barricade, and now he hits him with the Stunner! Michelle Steiner is now out, and Waru isn't moving. Lawler: Is his jaw broken? I hope so. Ross: Smith being escorted to the back, and Steiner is looking at Waru, and is shaking her head at the ref. I don't think is match is going to happen! (crowd boos VERY much) Lawler: Man, maybe Amdur SHOULD get a refund. end of part two. Coming up: World title match Tag title match -- The Maritimer - A member of the "New Brunswickans for a Live RAW campaign" (NBLRC) NBLRC website: http://NBLRC.home.ml.org Remember: I am what I say I am, and I say, I'm PERFECT! Also: Question: Why do Americans like RSPW? Answer: Because it's the only thing they can spell.
