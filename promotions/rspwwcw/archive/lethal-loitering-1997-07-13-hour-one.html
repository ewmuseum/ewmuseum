RSPWWCW Bash at the Beach: Lethal Loitering, 13 July 1997, Hour One

13 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(black and white footage with voiceover) The beach. A place to relax, forget your troubles, and enjoy life. Not tonight. Not for 40 of RSPWWCW's superstars. They'll be wrestling on a night, where they could wrestle against their best friend. Or, team up with their worst enemy. Eventually, 16 of them will go at it in a over-the-top battle royale. The beach. Fun for most.... .... lethal for others. (end footage) ******************************************************* ******************************************************* RSPWWCW Bash at the Beach: Lethal Loitering (hour one) ******************************************************* ******************************************************* Ross: Hello everyone and welcome, to RSPWWCW Bash at the Beach, here in Miami, Florida! It's the Lethal Loitering! 32 wrestlers, 16 teams, 8 tag matches. A battle royal at the end to determine the winner. (shot of announcer's table) Ross: Hello, this is good ol'JR, along with Jerry "The King" Lawler and Bobby "The Brain" Heenan, and folks, what a PPV do we have for you. Heenan: The teams have already been drawn, and almost all of them are not good pairings. Lawler: Who cares? We're going to see just how LETHAL it is at the Beach! ************************************************* Tag match #1: Eddy/LaRiche vs. Amdur/Blakenstein ************************************************* ("Aye's the bye that builds the boat") Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our FIRST tag team contest. Coming to the ring, from Memorial University, Newfoundland. He is a member of the VWO, ROBERT WALLACE EDDY! (Goldust theme) Finkel: And his teammate, on her way to the ring, and is a member of the Creatures, ATM LARICHE! (Baruch hymn) Finkel: And their opponents, they are both members of the JWO. They are JEFF AMDUR and BLAKENSTEIN! Ross: Two members of the JWO against the VWO/Creatures. A month ago I wouldn't have been able to guess a winner, but now, I'll go with the JWO. (Bell rings. LaRiche and Amdur lock up. Amdur hiptosses LaRiche to the mat. LaRiche gets up, and is dropkicked.) Heenan: LaRiche not having much luck against Amdur. Ross: LaRiche goes for a tag, but Eddy refuses to tag her! Lawler: WHAT? Come on, Eddy! Ross: Here comes Amdur, and he back-suplexes her. A cover, and a count of 2. Heenan: Why aren't the Creatures and VWO coming out? Ross: Probably because it might cause a fight that will cause both of their members to be disqualified. Blakenstein tags in, and they double-dropkick LaRiche. Blakenstein goes for a suplex, but LaRiche blocks, and reverses. She reaches for a tag, but Eddy again refuses. Lawler: Now this isn't fair! Heenan: She just slapped his arm! Ross: That counts as a tag, so Eddy has to come in. Locks up with Blakenstein, and Eddy is backed into the JWO corner. Blakenstein tags in Amdur, and they now they're stomping and punching Eddy. Lawler: See? That's what not tagging in will get you. Ross: Amdur gives Eddy a back-breaker, then an elbow-drop. Cover, count of 2. Heenan: The JWO are functionning as a team, which is more than I can say for Eddy and LaRiche. Ross: Amdur is now going to the top rope, and MISSES a splash! Lawler: Tag in! Tag in! Ross: That's what they're both trying to do. Amdur tags in Blakenstein. LaRiche reaches for the tag, here comes Eddy. Heenan: OH! She slugged him! LaRiche hit Eddy, instead of tagging! Lawler: WHAT? Ross: Here comes Blakenstein. He german-suplexes Eddy. There's a cover... Lawler: Do something LaRiche! Heenan: But she's just standing there! Ross: ... and Eddy is pinned! Amdur and Blakenstein go on to the battle royale. (Baruch hymn) Finkel: Here are your winners, JEFF AMDUR and BLAKENSTEIN! Lawler: What was THAT all about? Ross: Perhaps a sign of things to come. It's all a part of the Lethal Loitering... Heenan: And there's ANOTHER Creatures/VWO team-up later tonight! Ross: That true! Powrhug Kare will team up with Chris Myers, but that's later, and now let's go to our next match. ******************************************* Tag match #2: en4cer/Kid vs. Smith/Epstein ******************************************* (Baruch hymn) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this is our SECOND tag team contest. Coming down the aisle, he is a member of the JWO, the EN4CER! (1-2-3 Kid theme) Finkel: And his teammate, joining him in the ring. From Fredericton, New Brunswick, he is the 1-STU-3 KID! (Stone Cold theme) Finkel: And their opponents, the first one on his way to the ring, he is STONE COLD, PAUL SMITH! (Baruch hymn) Finkel: And his teammate, he is the leader of the JWO, JACK EPSTEIN! Ross: You KNOW this will be trouble with Epstein and Smith as TEAMMATES! Heenan: PLUS, en4cer and Epstein are both JWO! (bell rings. Smith and Epstein are arguing when the Kid double dropkicks them out of the ring.) Ross: That's EXACTLY what will happen when you're not paying attention to the match. (Smith and Epstein get up) Lawler: AH! Look at this! Ross: Smith just gave Epstein the stunner! Why in the world did he do that? Heenan: Now it's basically a handicap match! (Smith gets in the ring, and trades blows with the Kid. en4cer goes to see if Epstein is okay.) Ross: Now en4cer doing the less-than smart move of checking to see if his opponent is okay! Lawler: C'mon, en4cer! So what if he's JWO? (The Kid backs Smith into the ropes and jumps up to give a hurracurana, but Smith holds on the ropes and powerbombs him.) Ross: OH! What a powerbomb! He's going for a cover... Heenan: He's using the ropes! Ross: But en4cer sees it and knocks his legs off them, breaking the count. Smith picks up the Kid and STUNS him! Lawler: en4cer is on the apron! Ross: And Smith sees him too! He grabs en4cer and STUNS him on the top rope! (en4cer bounces off the ropes and crashes into the barricade.) Heenan: en4cer isn't moving! He's knocked out! Ross: And the Kid is actually getting to his feet! Now Stone Cold with ANOTHER stunner! Lawler: That's it for sure! Ross: Smith with a cover, and gets the pin. (Stone Cold theme) Finkel: Here are your winners, JACK EPSTEIN and STONE COLD PAUL SMITH! Ross: Austin is leaving the ring, and he starts pounding on Epstein! Lawler: This guy does NOT make a good teammate. Heenan: They won, didn't they? Ross: Epstein and Smith are fighting back to the locker rooms, as the Kid and en4cer are slowly getting up. Lawler: Man, what a match. That WAS a match, wasn't it? Ross: I think it was. It sure didn't look like a TAG match, though. ******************************************** Tag match #3: Gern/Henry vs. New Demolition ******************************************** Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this is the THIRD tag team contest. Currently in the ring, the Genius, GERN BLANSTON! (Gern reads poem) Gern: There once was a man named Gern, Who in this match, will be rather stern. He's teamed up with this John, Who's built like Mo Vaughn, And give a lesson that the Demos will learn. Heenan: Ah... Now THAT'S poetry! ("Freedom" - Rage Against the Machine) Finkel: And his teammate, coming down the aisle. He is a member of the VWO, JOHN HENRY! (Demolition theme) Finkel: And their opponents, making their way to the ring area, they have a combined weight of 495 lbs, the NEW DEMOLITION! Ross: New Demolition with a DEFINITE advantage tonight, as they are already a tag team. (bell rings, and all four brawl. Crunch bodyslams Henry as Gern clotheslines Hatchet. Crunch attacks Gern and hits him with axehandles. Henry gets up and throws Hatchet out of the ring. Hatchet grabs Henry's foot and pulls him out, and they brawl outside the ring.) Lawler: This isn't much more of a match either! Heenan: At least the teammates aren't attacking each other! Ross: In the ring now, as Crunch misses an elbowdrop. Gern gets up, runs off the ropes and dropkicks Crunch. A cover, and a count of 1. Gern runs off the ropes again and give Crunch a flying forearm. Another cover, and this time a count of 2. Lawler: And Hatchet and Henry are still fighting outside the ring. Heenan: But here comes the VWO! Ross: Dollywood, Myers, Grant, and Eddy are coming down to the ring, and they're joining Henry as they all beat up on Hatchet! (Gern turns and looks out of the ring as Crunch gets up.) Heenan: Don't look outside the ring! Worry about inside the ring! (Gern turns back and is Crunch lifts him over his head.) Ross: Crunch is gorilla pressing Gern, and he's going to the side of the ring. Lawler: Look out, guys! Ross: Crunch just threw Gern onto Dollywood, Eddy, and Myers! Henry is getting back into the ring. Heenan: Hatchet is still standing? Ross: Yep, and he clotheslines Grant over the guardrail. Crunch grabs Henry in the ring and belly-to-belly suplexes him. Lawler: C'mon guys, get up! Ross: They're starting to, as Hatchet is climbing the turnbuckle. The ref was lost TOTAL control of this match, as Hatchet SPLASHES Henry! Heenan: That's it! Ross: A cover, but Henry kicks out at 2. Gern is now on the ring apron, and is punched off by Crunch! Lawler: And the VWO can't interfere IN the ring, unless they want to get disqualified. Ross: Hatchet stands Henry up, and gives him a crotch shot! Heenan: Stick him in the boy's choir. Ross: Crunch gives Henry a back-breaker as Hatchet climbs to the top. Decapitation! VWO throws Gern in, as Hatchet makes the cover. Lawler: Do something! Ross: Crunch gives a belly-to-belly Gern, and Henry is pinned! (Demolition theme) Finkel: Here are your winners, the NEW DEMOLITION! Ross: New Demolition getting out of there in a hurry as they now get to go in the battle royale. Lawler: The VWO, they're beating up on Gern! Ross: Oh, come on! The refs are coming down, but the damage has been done. Folks, don't forget that after the eight tag matches, we will have an elimination tag match involving the eight people who were left over during the drawing. ************************************************ Tag match #4: Demchuk/Vaters vs. Fadner/Malecki ************************************************ (Crippler theme) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this next match is our FOURTH tag team contest. On his way to the ring, from Regina, Saskatchewan, "The CANADIAN CRIPPLER", ALEX DEMCHUK! (NYPD Blue theme) Finkel: And his teammate, on her way to the ring area, KELLY VATERS! ("Relax" - Frankie Goes to Hollywood) Finkel: And their opponents, one of them half of "Boyz in the Band", he is JEREMY FADNER! ("Banned in the USA") Finkel: And his teammate, from Ontario, Canada. He is the leader of the Aryan Nation, MIKE MALECKI! (bell rings. Fadner and Vaters lock up. Fadner throws Vaters off the ropes, misses a clothesline, and is dropkicked.) Ross: Fadner now gets up and rakes Vaters eyes! Lawler: Hey, there's no call for that! Ross: Now Vater rakes Fadners eyes! Heenan: Now THAT'S an eye for an eye! (Fadner staggers to his corner and tags Malecki as Vaters tag Demchuk. As Malecki gets in the ring, Fadner pats him on the butt.) Lawler: WHAT? Did you see that Ross? Ross: Yes, I did. But I'm not going to comment on it. Heenan: Malecki is upset at Fadner for it. (Malecki turns to yell, but Demchuk attacks him. Demchuk throws Malecki into the turnbuckles. Charges, but misses as Malecki moves.) Ross: Malecki bodyslams Demchuk, and gives him a legdrop. A cover, and a count of 2. Malecki gets up and goes for an elbowdrop, but misses. Heenan: Demchuk is hurrying to Vaters pretty quickly. Ross: Demchuk tags in Vaters, who dropkicks Malecki, and now hiptosses him. Vaters with a headbutt, and Malecki falls to the mat. Vaters with an elbowdrop, and a cover. Malecki kicks out at 2. Heenan: You can tell she's part of the CMA. Ross: Vaters now stomping on Malecki. She's getting him to his feet and gives him a DDT! Lawler: Cover him, cover him! Ross: She is, but only gets a 2 count. Vaters gets up and sets Malecki for a piledriver. She can't get it! And Malecki... back-bodydrops her! Heenan: Malecki NEEDS to make a tag! Ross: And that's what he did. (As Fadner enters the ring, he pats Malecki on the butt again.) Lawler: He did it again! Ross: Malecki is upset! He grabs Fadner, and gives him a stun gun! (Malecki gives him the finger, and leaves the ring area.) Lawler: What is Malecki doing, take classes at the Stone Cold College? Ross: Vaters gets Fadner up, and DDT's him! Vater, now tagging in Demchuk. Heenan: He's going up! Ross: Yes, and he... HITS a flying head-butt! A cover, a count, and that's it. (Crippler theme) Finkel: The winner of the match, KELLY VATERS, and "THE CANADIAN CRIPPLER" ALEX DEMCHUK! Lawler: Fadner crossed the line with Malecki, and it cost them both! (end of hour one) -- The Maritimer - A member of the "New Brunswickans for a Live RAW campaign" (NBLRC) NBLRC website: http://NBLRC.home.ml.org Remember: I am what I say I am, and I say, I'm PERFECT!
