RSPWWCW RAWNITRO IS WAR 6/9/97 Part 2 From Grand Casino in Gulfport Mississippi Announcers: Marv Albert, Dusty Rhodes, OJ Simpson ***************************** Hiroshi Ono vs. Kim Wright ***************************** - (Yokozuna's Theme) - Marv: Welcome back to RAWNITRO IS WAR. And we are pleased to herald the debut of two international stars. One is Hiroshi Ono, fresh out of fjrspw. HiroshiOno to mic: Things be much worse tomorrow. Give up now. Dusty: An dis here shoz da wuhld dat da wuhld in rec.spoht.pro-wrasslin, WUHLD chammpeenship rasslin, haz da wuhld's finest talent. - (Music-Poison-Alice Cooper) Marv: And this is professional kickboxer Kim Wright, making her way into rspw, she is from Ireland, and was discovered by Duggan Fan personally, I understand. OJ: Well, I have to say I am surprised. When I heard Duggan Fan discovered her, I expected her to be some crosseyed girl or a guy in a wig. But this Kim Wright is very attractive. Dusty: Well, we see if she iz a skild mahshall ahtist if yiou weeel, o jes a clubberah an a ragtagga. - Marv: Match begins, lariat by Wright. Half-DDT by Wright. Kim Wright now with an Orange Crush suplex, a cover, and a 2 count. Dusty: Well, Kim Wright getn owf ta a good staht. Marv: Kim Wright now all over Hiroshi Ono with kick and chops. Kim Wright, professional kickboxer making her debut in the wrestling arena tonight. Elbows to the back of the head by Wright. OJ: Well, she seems to have a number of wrestling skills to go along with her boxing repetoire. Marv: Somersault plancha by Kim Wright off the turnbuckle hits the mark. Wright with another lariat, whip kick through the legs of Hiroshi Ono. Ono not fazed wrestles his way behind Wright, and with a released German Suplex takes control. OJ: The best thing for Hiroshi to do is get behind this woman. That's when she's most vulnerable. Because you don't want those legs to come flying at you. Marv: Hiroshi, an Asai moonsault, a cover and a count of 2. Hiroshi, now with a tiger suplex, he does it agin. Double tiger suplex by Hiroshi Ono. Tiger driver by Ono, 1, 2, but Kim Wright gets the shoulder up. OJ: This is the troubling thing about HiroshiOno. You wrestle him, and an hour later you wanna wrestle him again. Marv: Ono, with a whip, and a backdrop driver, devastating maneuver, and a kickout by Wright. Now a Michinoku Driver. Dusty: Well, OJ iz borain Bobba Heenan's lines, but dis here Hiroshi Ono iz holdin himsef up well. Marv: Excalibur and a cover 1, 2. Whip to the ropes by Ono, TIGER DRIVER REVERSAL by Kim Wright! Now Ono back with a powerbomb attempt turned into a Frankensteiner by Kim Wright! OJ: Excellent reversal by Wright. Thios girl has it. Marv: Cross-body Northern Lights Bomb by Wright! UNBELIEVABLE! Dusty: Well, ahm am suhprized by dis display here by Kim Wright. Everybahday bin lookin for da special woman who kin taik ou da Tassmistress, an ah thnak we dun found heh. Marv: Diving neckbreaker drop off the top by Kim Wright. Enziguiri kick by Wright. Spinwheel somersault kick by Wright and Ono goes down. Jumping high kick send Hiroshi Ono reeling. OJ: This has been some excellent wrestling. Not like on Nitro. I'd rather hit my wife in the head with a shovel than watch Nitro. Marv: T-Bone suplex by Kim Wright. MERCY! Dusty: Well, Hiroshi Ono fell rat on hiz neck, an 2, 3. Das awl she wrote, an Kim Wright gon step up to da paywindah here on da Muthaship! - (Music-end song to Poison-Alice Cooper) - Marv: Kim Wright your winner, but Hiroshi Ono gave it all he had tonight, and redeemed himself in defeat. When we return, the Intergender championship is on the line. - ======================== COMMERCIAL ======================== - ********************************** RSPWWCW Intergender Championship Maritimer vs. Taskmistress ********************************** - (Music-Because The Night-Natalie Merchant) OJ: Well, I tell ya. I just can't imagine. Stone Cold Paul Smith just standing there next Sunday while Powrhug Kare does her little striptease. It send shivers down my spine. Marv: Well, that's how the song goes. As Taskmistress Liz Michael, in a Joanie Lee-like body suit, the three swords to the broken heart on the chest. Kare, the World champion, ATM Lariche the Cruiserweight champion, Chris Myers the Television champion, Dollywood Tom Zylkin. Liz Michael is the Women's champion, and if she wins back the Intergender championship, which she has held twice before, she will vacate that title. But all of that is premature. - (Perfect Symphony) Dusty: Here ah da Pehfect Canadians, well, only wun uv em iz rasslin tonight, but dey iz Maitiemah wit K A SHOOT an Deadshot. An I wundah if [name withheld] an [name withheld] iz gon intafeer in dis matchup. Cauz if Tassmistress wins, [name withheld] gets da nex title shot. Marv: That was a threat which was made earlier tonight by the Truth Commission, [name withheld] and [name withheld]. I wonder if Evan Schlesinger is still being held hostage in the Dungeon of the Taskmistress. - Marv: Maritimer an armdrag takedown. Maritimer with some forearms to the face. OJ: What's he trying to do, make her prettier. Marv: Maritimer dropkick send Michael to the outside. OJ: Well, with that blonding hair and dark skin, she reminds me of Faye Resnick. Bigger of course. Marv: Now a plancha by Maritimer, and a whip to the rail by the Maritimer. Both these wrestlers probably two of the best wrestlers in the RSPWWCW in terms of longevity during the match. These two, Tom Zylkin, Jack Epstein, Evan Schlesinger. Back in the ring, Maritimer with the suplex on Michael. OJ: It kinda pisses me off to see her with ATM LaRiche. Marv: Why? Juice. Why? Maritimer a sidewalk slam of the Taskmistress. Maritimer dominating Michael, excuse the expression. And a legdrop by Maritimer. What about ATM and the Taskmistress, OJ? OJ: I dunno, it just, just, reminds me of something I saw...I don't wanna talk about it. Marv: Maritimer a whip to the ropes, flying back elbow. Maritimer up to the top turnbuckle missile.....NO, POWERSLAM BY MICHAEL! COVER 1, 2, NO! Dusty: Well, yiou jes didn expect da Tassmistress to be in a match an show no awfense, if yiou weel. Maitiemah, plaid da strang a lil too lawng! - Marv: Vertical Suplex by Michael. Michael, in control, watch out! OJ: JACKNIFE! Marv: Michael for the cover, 1, 2, MARITIMER kick in the lower midsection by Maritimer to get out of the pin. Dusty: Well, when da Tassmistress do dat dey cawl it da Ballbustah. But Tassmistress ain got none a dat dere. So ahm not shore wutchu cawl dat. OJ: A clit slit? Marv: Cover by Maritimer for 1. Maritmer with a dropkcik to Michael as she was getting up. Tilt-A-Whirl Suplex and Liz Michael goes outside to be comforted by La Femme ATM. OJ: Alexandra making luvy duvy to the Taskmsitress, and here comes Maritimer. Marv: Maritimer with a chair to ATM Lariche. And now to the Takmistress. And referee Duggan XPT reading the riot act to the Maritimer. OJ: Well, I don't know what to say about Duggan XPT. If it's not a 2 by 4 or a taped fist it isn't legal. Marv: Well, Maritimer throws Michael back in the ring. Perfectplex attempt, HE'S GOT HER! But PORWHUG KARE kicks Maritimer in the leg. And Duggan XPT didn't see it. OJ: With those crosseyes the man can't see his own shadow. Marv: Belly to belly by Maritimer BLOCKED and MICHAEL with the suplex! Dusty: Das sumin else! - Marv: And takedown by Michael and the TASKMISSION! The TASKMISSION! She could be the new Intergender Champion. Duggan XPT being distracted by Deadshot. HELD BACK by Duggan XPT. And K A SHOOT dropkicks Liz Michael from behind, and OH NO! SHE GOT UP! LIZ MICHAEL IS NOW STALKING K A SHOOT! OJ: Taskmistress better turn around. Marv: And here is Maritimer. Liz turns around, and now the combats are throwing punch for punch in the ring. Michael with a quick chokeslam, and follows up with the leg drop to the face. K A SHOOT to the apron and the Taskmistress decks her. Now Deadshot to the apron grabs Michael. Duggan XPT is arguing with Deadshot, and SOMEBODY'S COMING INTO THE RING! Dusty: Sumbaddy dun jumpt in behind Maritimer. Marv: SUPERKICK KNOCKS DOWN MARITIMER! THAT'S SHAWN MICHAELS! THAT'S SHAWN MICHAELS! Dusty: Now, dis could be a problem bicuz Duggan XPT ain seen it. Marv: Liz Michael turns around, picks up Maritimer. PERFECTPLEX! 1, 2, 3, AND THE TASKMISTRESS has WON the Intergender Champion, using Maritimer's own move against him, having received the assistance of Shawn Michaels. - (Because The Night-Natalie Merchant) OJ: YOU know I am surprised Duggan XPT can see two feet in front of him with those crosseyes. Dusty: Well, Shawn Michaels wuz schediuld ta fais da Maitimah next, an Maitiemah an Deadshot don beet him up at Spamboree. So ah gess dis here iz payback. Shawn with others to camera: TOO SWEET! Marv: And now the VWO all make the Kliq sign with Shawn Michaels and we are out of time, folks we are out of time. And the Perfect Cnadians are beating the crap out of Duggan XPT, and now Duggan Fan and the Nation of Duggan is coming out, and the canadians are brawling with the Nation Of Duggan. We have run out of time.
