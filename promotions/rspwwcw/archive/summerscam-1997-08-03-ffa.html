RSPWWCW Summer Scam, Free-For-All, 3 August 1997

3 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
********************************** RSPWWCW Summer Scam: Free-For-All ********************************** (Free-For-All logo dances on the screen) Ross: Hello everyone, and welcome to the summer's hottest PPV on RSPW, RSPWWCW Summer Scam! This is good ol' JR, along with Jerry "The King" Lawler, and folks we hope you pick up Summer Scam from your local internet browser, because it looks like a doozy! Lawler: The world, intergender, television, AND tag titles are ALL on the line tonight! Ross: Not to mention the four-corners match to determine the #1 tag team contenders, who will get a title shot tomorrow night on RawNitro! ("Because the Night" - Natalie Merchant) But right now we have a women's tag match to get to! ********************************************** Kelly Vaters/Kim Wright vs. DoMinAA/Dana Hanna ********************************************** Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this tag team match is set for ONE fall, coming down to the ring, with a combined weight of, well, "none of your business", they are two members of the Creatures of the Night. They are DoMinAA and "Dangerous" Dana Hanna! ("Poision" - Alice Cooper) Finkel: And their opponents, making their way to the ring area, they are Kelly Vaters, and the RSPWWCW women's champion, KIM WRIGHT! Ross: Crowd giving a positive reception to Kelly Vaters, a fellow Canadian. Lawler: I can't believe we had to come all the way out to Vancover, British Columbia, of all places! (bell rings. Vaters starts off against DoMinAA. Vaters hiptosses DoMinAA. Then a clothesline, and an elbowdrop.) Ross: Vaters with a quick cover, but only gets a count of one. Vaters with a headlock, but DoMinAA with a back suplex! Lawler: Where are the other creatures when you need them, Ross? Ross: Both women go and tag their partners. Kim Wright kicks Hanna in the gut, and now follows it with a roundhouse kick to the head! Lawler: AH! Ross: A cover, and only a count of two. Lawler: Yay! The other creatures are here! Ross: Uh-oh. Liz, LaRiche, and Natasha are coming to the ring, as the action is going on inside the ring. Wright tags in Vaters, and they double-bodyslam Hanna. A cover, and DoMinAA breaks it up. Lawler: With the creatures here, you KNOW who's gonna win. Ross: Not with their opponents, King. Vaters running off the ropes, but is tripped by LaRiche! Lawler: Yeah! Ross: The ref is yelling at LaRiche as DoMinAA runs in and clips Vater's knee! And Liz grabs Wright and pulls her to the floor! The ref has lost all control of this match! The referee gets DoMinAA back to her corner, and Hanna back-suplexes Vaters. Lawler: NOW do you doubt me? Ross: More than ever. There's a cover, and Vaters kicks out at two. Hanna is kicking Vaters' knee as Liz and Wright are STILL fighting outside. Lawler: Someone help Liz out! Ross: Now LaRiche comes over and they double-suplex Wright! The referee comes out of the ring and tries to get them away from Wright. Lawler: Look! Hanna's getting ready! Ross: Hanna is going to the top rope! She leaps... and MISSES a splash! Wright climbs on the apron as the ref enters the ring. Lawler: No, NO! Stop them, creatures! Ross: Hanna tags in DoMinAA, and Vaters tag in Wright. Both come in the ring and Wright hits DoMinAA with a series of kicks to the legs and midsection. Lawler: Oh no! Ross: Now a THRUST kick, and DoMinAA falls to the mat, holding her stomach. Wright picks up DoMinAA, Lawler: Look at this! Ross: Wright's in a small package! The ref is counting, and the creatures get the pin! (bell rings. Creatures enter the ring.) Finkel: The winner of the match, DoMinAA and Dangerous Dana Hanna! (crowd boos) Ross: Well, that's it for the free-for-all segment of the show.. Lawler: WHAT? That's it? Ross: Hey, we started fifteen minutes late, King. Join us in a few minutes for RSPWWCW Summer Scam! (end credits) -- The Maritimer - A member of the "New Brunswickans for a Live RAW campaign" (NBLRC) NBLRC website: http://NBLRC.home.ml.org Remember: I am what I say I am, and I say, I'm PERFECT!
