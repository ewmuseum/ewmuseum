Spamboree, 19 May 1997, Part 3

19 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
**************** Spamboree 1997 **************** Tony: Now folks, we have the RSPWWCW World title match between Dollywood Zlykin vs. Jack Epstein, or more likely, the VWO vs. the JWO. ************************* RSPWWCW World Title Match: Dollywood Zylkin vs. Jack Epstein ************************* (Baruch Et Adonai hymn) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this next matchup is for the RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Championship! Coming down the aisle, accompanied by the Jewish World Order, JACK EPSTEIN! Tony: Epstein is with Jeff Amdur, Steve Tiszinkel, Len Tirulnikov, and David Chanoch. ("Sweet Home Alabama": Lynyrd Skynyrd) Finkel: And his opponent, being accompanied to the ring by the Virtual World Order, he is the RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Champion, DOLLYWOOD ZYLKIN! Tony: Dollywood was with him [name withheld], RichinKC, Powrhug Kare, The Taskmistress Liz Micheal, Lionwrath Zen Jericho, and Diamondback Chris Myers. (the gangs exit the ring and surround it, lumberjack style. Bell rings) Tony: It kindof looks like we have a bit of a lumberjack match. Brain: Evidently you were reading what was in the brackets. (Dollywood and Epstein lock up. Dollywood throws Epstein to the mat, and poses. Epstein gets up, and they lock up again. Suddenly...) Tony: What's this? The JWO's attacking the VWO outside the ring! Brain: Why are they doing that? There's more VWO than JWO. It's six on four! Tony: Someone's coming down to ringside. It's two people! It's Perfection Personified, en4cer and Blakenstein! They're attacking the VWO! It's now six-on-six! And Epstein and Dollywood are still going at it in the ring! The ref is looking out the ring, trying to make sense of this. Brain: Someone else is coming now! It's Sabu424! Tony: Jeff Amdur puts [name withheld] on the apron, and he gets on it as well! The ref is trying to get them off, as Epstein gives Dollywood a low blow! Brain: A chair! Sabu424 has a chair! Tony: Epstein holds Dollywood against the ropes, and Sabu424 hits him in the head with the chair! He hits him again! Dollywood falls to the mat! Brain: The VWO can't do anything, because they're being held busy by the rest of the JWO! Tony: Amdur and Grant fall off the apron, and the ref sees Epstein covering Dollywood! He's counting 1 ... 2 ... 3! (Bell rings. The Baruch Et Adonai hymn plays) Finkel: Here is your winner, and NEW RSPWWCW World Heavyweight Champion, JACK EPSTEIN! (Epstein rolls Dollywood out of the ring, and is handed the mic. The JWO enter the ring. The VWO stand around the ring, and start to attend to Dollywood.) Epstein: Let me just say right off the bat: Never outwrestle anything you can out-smart! Using our superior intellect, the JWO now rule RSPWWCW! (crowd looks at each other, shrug. Begin to boo) Epstein: Let me introduce to you the newest members of the Jewish World Order! Representing our tag-team ranks, they are Blakenstein and en4cer, Perfection Personified! en4cer: JWO 4 life! We are the ONLY perfect tag team here, and we'll prove it by becoming the tag-team champions! Blakenstein: Liz, Kare, you've ducked us since we showed up. Now you're gonna regret it, and that's a promise! (VWO begin to leave the area) Epstein: Ain't they great? Also, I want to introduce to you the man, who is making his RSPWWCW debut tonight, and has proven to be just one of the many deciding factors in this match, Sabu424! Sabu424: The first four spaces is my name, and the next three is my IQ. That's all you need to know. JWO 4 LIFE!! Epstein: Y'know, I wouldn't mind recruiting those New Demoltion fellows, except for two things. One, we already have a tag team, and two, we all know that they're the Perfect Canadians. I mean c'mon, they have the exact same build! Brilliant strategy guys, that idea was about as sharp as Paul Smith's bald head. Try taking US on, and you'll be in a fight you can't HELP but lose. Same goes for Liz, Evan, Smith, the creatures, the VWO, and anyone else that try to stop us. The JWO is here to stay, and may heaven help you all, because noone else will. Let's go, guys. (Epstein and the JWO leave) Brain: Wow. I think we're in for a gang war. Tony: Fans, let's take you backstage with Scheme Gene. Gene: Thank you gentlemen, I'm with RSPWWCW Commissioner DugganFan, DugganFan: HOOOOOO!!! Gene: and DugganFan, what have you got for us? DugganFan: Well Gene, let me just say that it's now offical, that tomorrow night on RawNitro we will have Jack Epstein defending the title against Evan Schlesinger. What the.. (Liz and Kare enter) Liz: Alright you cross-eyed piece of shit. Enough is enough. Bad enough we get our asses kicked by the alter-egos of the Perfect Canadians. But now the VWO no longer have the World belt. Gene: And your point is..? Liz: Shut up, you bald, fat fuck-head. What Kare and I want is something that we should've had back at UNKILLFILED. And that's a no-countout, no-disqualification match against the Perfect Canadians. Now do you have the balls to sanction this, or am I gonna have to rip Gene's off and shove them down your throat? DugganFan: Good point! All tonight has been is just a series of brawls and interference. I'm smart enough to realize that it's going to happen during the tag match as well, so Taskmistess, Powrhug Kare, you got it! No DQ! No countout! There must a winner! HOOOOOOOO!!! Gene: Woah! Back to ringside! Tony: I don't believe this Brain! Brain: You better believe it. Liz and Kare are getting what they wanted since the Perfect Canadians backed out two months ago. A no-DQ, no countout, every man/woman for themselves brawl for the tag-titles! ******************************** RSPWWCW Tag-Team Title Match: American Women vs. Perfect Canadians ******************************** (Perfect symphony. Crowd almost pee themselves, they're cheering THAT hard) Finkel: Ladies and gentlemen, this match is tonight's Main Event, and it's for the RSPWWCW tag-team Championship! On their way to the ring, from Fredericton, New Brunswick, and Wolfville, Nova Scotia, the Maritimer, Deadshot, the PERFECT CANADIANS! (Perfect Canadians enter the ring. Maritimer asks for the mic. Gets one.) Maritimer: Hellloooooo Fredericton! (Crowd eats it up like Skittles) Maritimer: Looks like we're gonna have a war soon, so let me just say, that the next time you'll be hearing from us, we'll have the titles. So Liz, Kare, when we get the belts, what are you gonna use to hold your pants up? Tell 'em Deadshot. Deadshot: What's gonna hold their pants up? Their fat asses, for one thing. You two say we're afraid. That we didn't want to face you under those no-DQ or countout stipulations. Well, they're in effect tonight, and you know what? We're not going anywhere! K-A Shoot isn't with us, because this is gonna get too violent for her. So as a wise man once said, "Come on down!" (American Women: Creedance Clearwater Revival. Crowd boos for some strange reason) Finkel: And they're opponents, charging to the ring, they're the (stops talking, as Liz and Kare enter the ring and starts fighting with Deadshot and Maritimer. Bell rings) Tony: So how long to you think this match will go before someone interferes? Brain: About five or six. Tony: Five or six minutes. Brain: Nope. Five or six seconds. See? (en4cer and Blakenstein charge the ring) Tony: That's Perfection Personified! And they're beating up on Liz and Kare! But here comes help! It's LaRiche, Natasha, and Sasskia of the creatures! They're attacking Perfection Personified! Brain: Now here comes the JWO! Tony: It's Amdur, Tiszinkel, Sabu424, and Tirulnikov! They're battling the creatures, and Sabu424 is fighting the Maritimer as Deadshot is fighting with Liz! Brain: Who's on whose side? Tony: I have no idea. Here comes Jack Epstein! He's walking down the aisle. Who's that behind him? It's Dollywood! He's attacking Epstein, and the VWO are now involved! It's Grant, RichinKC, Jericho, and Myers! They're all rushing the ring! Brain: We have another battle royale going on! But only four of them are legal! Tony: They're starting to clear out, two at a time, in some cases! Brain: Are you able to call this? Tony: I'm trying as best as I can, Brain. Everyone's out except for Liz and Sabu424. Liz hits Sabu with the Sweet Bitch Music, and Sabu is knocked onto the floor. Maritimer re-enters the ring, Liz starts pounding on him, and gives him a gut-rench suplex! Deadshot is coming in, and Kare is climbing the top rope on the opposite corner. She leaps at Deadshot, and he catches her! Powerslam! Liz elbowdrops Deadshot on the back of the head. Maritimer grabs Liz and gives her a neck-breaker! Brain: Everyone else is fighting outside the ring, and in the aisle. Tony: Fortunately, the ones are supposed to be in the ring, are. Now what's this, Deadshot and Maritimer, giving double axehandles to Liz! Now they're doing the same to Kare! Brain: They're the New Demoliton! Look at them! Wait, LOOK! I think that's SABLE! Tony: It is! Sable, with her white face paint, has come through the crowd, carrying a bat! Deadshot gives Liz a backbreaker, and holds it as Maritimer climbs the turnbuckle! Brain: Decapitation! He got it! And Sable's swinging the bat at anyone and everyone! Tony: Sable's hit Epstein, en4cer, and Amdur, and she's entering the ring! Deadshot attacks her, and Kare attacks him! Maritimer picks up Liz, and gives her a PERFECTPLEX! Brain: The girls have to do something! Tony: Sable gets away from Deadshot as the refs starts the count! 1 ... 2 .. and Sable hits Maritimer in the gut with the bat, breaking the pin! Brain: That's HOW many perfectplexes on Liz? Three in total? Tony: Sable is now walking towards Deadshot with the bat, and... what the HELL?? Is that who I think it is? Brain: That's the New Demolition! They're coming through the crowd as well! But Deadshot and Maritimer are in the ring! They look the same, although Crunch is now wearing a black T-shirt, but still.. Tony: AND HE'S CARRYING A SHOTGUN! CRUNCH HAS A SHOTGUN! He and Hatchet enter the ring, and attack Sable and Deadshot! Deadshot grabs Crunch's shotgun, and yanks it away! Crunch is fleeing the ring! So is Hatchet! Sable turns, and gets knocked in the head with the shotgun! The bat goes across the ring! Sable scurries away! Brain: It's Smith! STONE COLD PAUL SMITH! Tony: What? Where? It is! Smith is walking down the aisle! He's approaching Natasha, and gives her the stunner! He goes to Grant, and gives HIM the stunner! He's going to Epstein and Dollywood, who are obviously fighting, and gives them each a low blow, and a DOUBLE STUNNER! Brain: He must be bored! Why else would he be here? Tony: Back in the ring, Deadshot is helping the Maritimer, and Liz is picking up the bat! She's charging Deadshot, and know they're fighting with a bat and shotgun! Brain: That looks like Mortal Kombat! What were they, trained with staffs or something? Tony: Maritimer goes to Kare, and she gives him a belly-to-belly! A cover! 1 .. 2 .. and a kickout! Brain: What, he isn't dead yet? Tony: Perfection Personified enter the ring, and pound on Kare! Outside the ring, Paul Smith is still giving everyone stunners! He goes to Hatchet and gives HIM one! Crunch helps him to his feet and they going back to the locker rooms! Brain: For a couple of tough guys, they really aren't all that tough. Tony: Maritimer is back on his feet and runs at Liz, who's still going at it with Deadshot. She sees him, but he hits her with a tackle, and they roll out of the ring, into the stomping feet of Sabu424 and Tirulnikov! Brain: Perfection Personified is setting up Kare for a spike piledriver! Tony: And they hit it! They leave the ring, and Deadshot is climbing the turnbuckle! LaRiche jumps on the apron, and Deadshot throws the shotgun at her, hitting her in the head! Brain: That's what happens when you give a guy named DEADSHOT a gun. Tony: He's going for Air Nova, and hits it! The ref counts as Dollywood tries to enter the ring but is held back by en4cer, 1 ... Sable is in the ring .. 2, and 3! She didn't get there in time! The Perfect Canadians win! (crowd jumps up and down, screaming) Finkel: The winners of the match, and NEW RSPWWCW tag team champions, the PERFECT CANADIANS!! (perfect symphony) Tony: Maritimer is climbing back in the ring with the belts. He gives one to Deadshot, and they're celebrating, and giving the "3" sign! Brain: They're now three-time champions! Tony: Damn, what a show. Folks, we gotta go. Remember, tomorrow night we have two first round matches of the King of the Ring, a title match between Evan and Epstein, and the Maritimer defends against the creatures! Good night, everybody!
