World Internet Wrestling as it ran on fwrestling.com in 2002: Chaos on Tuesdays and Showdown on Fridays, scripted in full around its president's New Breed faction, with pay-per-views such as Wrestlefest II. Its own title histories reach back to the fed's founding in May 1999. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

World Internet Wrestling survives as one page of its own site: a title-history index, captured 21 August 2002, naming six championships — a World title, an Intercontinental title, a United States title, a Television title, a Hardcore title, and a Tag Team title — each one a link the page promises will lead to a history, alongside a note that new title-belt pictures were still to come.

Not one of those six links survives with any content behind it. No wrestler, reign, card, or result is preserved anywhere in the record; the index names its titles and nothing else.

Who held any of these six championships, how the promotion staged its shows, and how or when it closed are all outside the surviving record.

Affiliations

Primetime Central (PTC)

Crossover booking with WIW:NG (1999)
Its 29 July 2002 column says the fed linked up with PTC; on 2 August 2002 Chris Storm calls himself its PTC representative and challenges two PTC wrestlers.

XWO

Member of WIW:NG (1999)
Its rules page says that in June 2001 the fed expanded to create a big e-wrestling world with XWO; Wrestlefest II stages XWO titles.

Dominant Wrestling On-Line (DWO, 2001)

Crossover booking with WIW:NG (1999) (2002)
The DWO's own news of autumn 2002 has Chris Storm close out the "bad blood that people think is between the DWO and the WIW:NG" after a tag match against Kristi Kiss and Terry Woods: "both companies gave it a great shot."
