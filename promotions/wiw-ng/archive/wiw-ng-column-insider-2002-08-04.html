WIW:NG Column: The Insider's Rant, 4 August 2002

4 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Insider's Rant 8/4/02 by The Insider Well, hello there. I see that this little column section hasn't really gotten off the ground too much, with the exception of the one column done weekly by J Woods. And, well, lets be honest.. J Woods? Heh, no one gives a damn about J Woods and his views on WiW! They want the 'good' stuff! They want 'The Insider', baby! Yeah! Er.. ok. Anyway, I suppose a little explanation is necessary. In my weekly 'Insider's Rant', I will be bringing you a Top 10 of the week - which will basically be the Top 10 events of the week, a compilation of both shows 'Chaos' and 'Showdown'. I will also be bringing you an interview from a different WIW wrestler with every column.. that's right. We're getting the 'inside scoop' from the wrestler's themselves! But it doesn't end there. Along with the interview of the week.. I will list my 'Wrestler of the Week'! But without any further a due, I suppose I ought to get this edition started. Eh? So, lets get started with number ten. You know, the least important one on this little list. Heh... err, anyway - Number Ten: [.Bryan Statis vs. The Constable vs. Trauma for the WiW Television Championship.] Ok. First off, I have to give some props to Bryan Statis. Not a lot of guys can win a title in only their second week in WIW. Nice job. Other than that, I don't really have a reason for this being at the Number Ten spot - just needed to fill it with something. I know, I know.. I'm evil. Hehe.. But seriously, I see big things coming from Bryan Statis in the future. He may be the biggest jackass I have ever met, as apparent from the interview further down.. but this is probably only the beginning of what is to be an outstanding WIW career. Moving on, we have Number Nine!: [.Chris Storm vs. Johnny Sikah.] Ok, I have no intention of making this a 'Top 10 Matches of the Week' list, but hey.. its been a slow week. Sue me. Johnny 'Plague' Sikah and Chris Storm built this up very nicely, and the semi-feud between the two has really been fun to watch. Johnny Sikah, much like Bryan Statis is the future of WIW - in my opinion. He lost this match, but he showed everyone in WIW something special. Very good match. Next up, Number Eight: [.Trauma Revealed as Summer Anison's Kidnapper!.] After being kidnapped earlier in the week, Johnny Sikah's manager 'Summer Anison' was shown tied to a chair with tape over her mouth in the opening segment to Chaos. Her kidnapper was revealed to be Trauma, as he was shown holding a match in the dark room! A new side of the man was shown to us as he ended the segment by laughing like a sick freak. I don't know what we can expect from this, but two things are definite. Its going to be entertaining, and sick at the same time. Numero Seven: [.Jason Chae's 'Burger' Segment with e.T.o from Chaos.] Jason Chae, being the nice guy that he is, not only GIVES e.T.o a burger.. but he gives him a briefcase FULL of coupons for all sorts of free burger deals all around the country! And they say Jason Chae isn't a nice guy.. who else would, from the bottom of his own heart, give one of their worst enemies a 'FREE' burger, and a case full of coupons for 'FREE' burgers all around the country?! Jason Chae, you are my icon! Erm.. well, ok. So what if he was just trying to 'buy off' e.T.o into joining the New Breed? It was still.. heart warming. Alas, e.T.o declined, and we'll have to wait for Last Man Standing to see Jason Chae beat e.T.o, and force him to join! Yeah! Number Six: [.Reeco Suave's Wrestling Debut in WIW from Chaos.] After signing with WIW just last week as a manager for WIW World Champion, Shane Slade, Reeco Suave was mysteriously forced to compete in a tag team matchup with his managee! So Reeco Suave and Shane Slade vs. Erik Erratic and The End was booked as the main event for Chaos. Alas, after a dirty low blow from Erik the match ended with a LegENDer from The End to Shane Slade for the pinfall. Number Five: [.Shorty the Pimp vs. Travis Smallz.] From Showdown, this matchup had a lot going for it. Two WIW guys climbing the ladder. This match also saw the debut of a new WIW wrestler, Christian Neils! He ran in with a German flag, and took out both Smallz and Shorty the Pimp! Number Four: [.The Psychological Mind Games Played By Slade and The Unknown on Showdown.] Now, I'm not going to ruin my Number One pick.. but I have to say. Shane Slade and The Unknown played their cards right. They suckered you all into thinking they hated each other! Haha.. not me of course. I saw it coming a mile away. Sure did. Number Three: [.Sam Sykes Debuts as a Wrestler!.] In a nice transition from Number Six, right after the tag match on Chaos, Sam Sykes ran down and brutally attacked Shane Slade! After laying the World Champion out, Sykes took off his shirt and pants!! My god! Wait.. don't let your imagination run wild here. Sam Sykes returned to challenge Slade to a match on Showdown! The was definitely one of the biggest surprises of the week. Props to Sam Sykes for having the guts to challenge 'The Legend', much less attack him from behind. Sure, he got his ass kicked on Showdown.. but at least he got the first cheap shot in! Number Two: [.e.T.o vs. Erik Erratic.] In what proved to be Erik Erratic's second straight singles loss, e.T.o came out on top! Shameful, considering the fact that Erik Erratic is a number one contender to the WIW World Title. Add that to e.T.o attacking Jason Chae earlier on in the night, and without a doubt.. if it hadn't been for the main event, this would definitely have been e.T.o's night! Number One: [.The Unknown Fools You All!.] Haha! He had you going! For weeks on end, he had you going!.. but not me. I was in on it all along. Yep. Anyway, after Shane Slade defeated Sam Sykes in Sykes' wrestling debut, we saw The Unknown walk out in an XWO t-shirt! The XWO, of course being Sam Sykes' old wrestling promotion! With a bat in hand, he cornered Shane Slade and Jason Chae after the match.. but instead of hitting them, he turned and clocked Sam Sykes as he was getting up! After the brutal beatdown, The Unknown took off his XWO t-shirt to reveal a 'New Breed' one right underneath! Jason Chae then made an even more shocking announcement.. Sam Sykes will meet The Unknown at the Last Man Standing PPV! And as we saw, The Unknown has joined the New Breed! The ending moments of Showdown saw The Unknown holding his hands up with Shane Slade and Jason Chae! Haha.. what a week its been. With all that's gone on this week, you can pretty much be sure that this upcoming one will be even more hectic. Last Man Standing is getting close, only about two weeks away.. and anything can happen! So tune in next week for another rousing Top 10 Events of the Week! Now, recently, I sat down with WIW newcomer and Television Champion, Bryan Statis. We spoke about a few things, including his goals in WIW, and why he is here. It was quite interesting to say the least, and I hope you all check it out. So without further adue.. here it is: The Insider: 'Hello.' Bryan Statis: 'That's a rather simplistic greeting, have you nothing else to offer?' The Insider: 'Err.. how are you today?' Bryan Statis: 'I'm alive.' The Insider: 'Really? Well.. thats good to hear.' Bryan Statis: 'As if you'd hear my current status if I were dead.' The Insider: 'Right, well.. I suppose I wouldn't. But anyway, I suppose we should get going with this.' Bryan Statis: 'I suppose.' The Insider: 'To start off, can you tell us a little about yourself? Your past, where you came from, that sort of thing?' Bryan Statis: 'I'm sure everyone would be enthralled to consume such information. As you know, I live in New York. I've always lived there.' The Insider: 'Who *doesn't* live there these days?' Bryan Statis: 'Exciting, isn't it?' The Insider: 'Err.. sorry, continue.' Bryan Statis: 'Continue? I was finished.' The Insider: 'Well.. that was quite simplistic.' Bryan Statis: 'Quite simplistic indeed. Much like your greeting. I only give what I receive.' The Insider: 'Very funny guy, you are. Anyway, what brings you to WIW?' Bryan Statis: 'After an absence from the ring, everyone has a desire to make a return. FAW treated me like garbage, and I chose to leave on bad terms.' The Insider: 'Ah, now we're getting down to the juicy stuff. Can you elaborate on your FAW tenure, and their treating of you like garbage?' Bryan Statis: 'My longtime friend Hardcore Hoefel and I were paired together in a tag team match... to decide the championship. They knew I had no desire to participate in such a match. Hoefel, being heavily intoxicated, ended up costing us the victory.' The Insider: 'So I take it Hoefel and yourself are no longer on the best of terms?' Bryan Statis: 'I knew you'd make that false assumption.' The Insider: 'Well, I try..' Bryan Statis: 'You're obviously blind to the fact that I am a Legend. Hardcore Hoefel, leader of the Legends, is the man responsible for my career!' The Insider: 'I see. Well, moving on then... This past week on Chaos, you won the WIW Television Championship in only your second week in WIW. What was that like for you?' Bryan Statis: 'Having a leather strap in my possession isn't significant to me. Thus far, I've had bottom of the barrel competition. When i defend the title, maybe some credibility will be bestowed upon it. But until then, it's meaningless.' The Insider: 'Well, the Television Championship is looked upon as the 'bottom of the barrel' title in WIW. But its a step up nonetheless. Wouldn't you say?' Bryan Statis: 'In other words, you're classifying me as a "bottom of the barrel" competitor? I really appreciate your kind words.' The Insider: 'Agh.. no, no.. I meant.. you see.. you misunderstood.' Bryan Statis: 'I didn't misunderstand. You chose your words poorly. It's amazing that you've actually got a steady job.' The Insider: 'I know... I mean.. wait.' Bryan Statis: 'I have been waiting. Waiting for a strike of intelligence to hit someone besides myself.' The Insider: 'You're making me very uncomfortable.' The Insider: 'Judging from your words, I take it you don't rank your TV Title win too highly on your list of career achievements. Am I correct in this assumption?' Bryan Statis: 'Why is it that everyone assumes possession of a belt is an accomplishment? There have been fluke champions.. Trauma for example.' The Insider: 'Well.. winning a title gives you a certain status in WIW...' Bryan Statis: 'Holding the title for three days. Did that elevate Trauma to any special pedestal?' The Insider: 'Well, I'm not one to say... Trauma is one of our rising stars.' Bryan Statis: 'Trauma fell victim to me.' The Insider: 'That is true.' Bryan Statis: 'Of course it is.' The Insider: 'As long as we're talking about the talent in WIW.. looking at the overall picture, do you see anyone you would like to have a program with?' Bryan Statis: 'What, am I supposed to limit myself to one target?' The Insider: 'No, no.. what I'm asking is, do you see any one specific talent in WIW as a standout? Someone 'worthy' of competing with you?' Bryan Statis: 'If it were a standout, it'd be a target. Everyone is a target eventually.' The Insider: 'I see... you seem to be quite set on your goals.' Bryan Statis: 'Of course I am. Without goals, there is no purpose. I am certainly not in it for the money.' The Insider: 'That's becoming apparent with your every word.' The Insider: 'And that seems as good of a note to end on as any. I would like to thank you for your time, Bryan Statis. This has definitely been.. interesting.. to say the least.' Bryan Statis: 'I'm glad someone's interest was sustained. This was merely a waste of time.' Mr. Statis decided to get up and walk out at this point. Seems like a nice guy, don't you think? I mean.. he not only twisted my words around, but he was just a plain jackass!.. I.. mean.. tune in next week for an interview with another up and coming WIW superstar, Johnny Sikah.. fresh off of his spectacular match against Chris Storm on Chaos last week! To finish off this edition of 'The Insider's Rant'.. I would like to start the regular 'Wrestler of the Week' section of my column. Every week, I will name *my* personal Wrestler of the Week. I could care less if you disagree with me.. its my opinion that matters, not yours. That's right, sit down. Wrestler of the Week: Chris Storm - the guy has found time to not only compete in Prime Time Central's Golden Turnbuckle Tournament.. but has has successfully wrestled in WIW at the same time! Good luck to our Intercontinental Champion in his bid to become the Golden Turnbuckle Champion in PTC. And even better luck in his Intercontinental Title defenses! Thats all for this week folks. But tune in next week for a new Top 10. A new interview, with Johnny Sikah, and a new Wrestler of the Week! This is The Insider.. signing out.
