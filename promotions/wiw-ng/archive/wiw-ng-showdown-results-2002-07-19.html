WIW:NG Showdown Results, 19 July 2002

19 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live from the Gund Arena in Cleveland... The scene is in the parking lot of the the Gund Arena. Since it is summertime, it is still very much light outside, and it does look odd for a man dressed in all black sweater, pants, boots and wearing a black mask walking down the parking lot of the Gund Arena. Not only is it a parking lot, it is the special reserved lot for the wrestlers and the WIW crew to park at. So with this man in all black wandering around in a suspicious manner, a Gund Arena security officer approaches the man. Security Officer: Hey you! Take off that silly mask, let me see who you are! This wrestler just takes a piece of paper out of his pants and he hands it over to the security officer who starts reading it out loud. Security Officer: I am simply known as The Unknown. I will reveal the fact that I am an active wrestler of WIW. I was the one that put Zero out of commission on Chaos. That is all you need to know... The security officer looks up at The Unknown and he gets smacked right in the face with a punch! The Unknown then smashes the security officer's head into the a nearby car, a 1998 Ford Mustang GT. Then The Unknown then smashes the security officer's head into the rear windshield breaking right through it and the alarm of the car starts to go off. The security is unconscious and bleeding and The Unknown gathers himself and he runs over to a BMW Z8... he pulls out a set of keys and he opens the door and gets in the car and he speeds off. The opening instrumental sounds of "Master of Puppets" by Metallica plays as the opening video to Showdown plays. Various WIW wrestlers are shown hitting their finishing moves. The camera then cuts to the explosive blue pyro that goes off in the ring and now shown are the Friday Night Showdown commentators, Dan Selito and Chris Clybor! Clybor: Who is that Unknown guy? What does he want with WIW? Who's car did he take? Dan: I wish I had those answers Chris, but I don't. Clybor: What good are you then? Dan: (notices the camera is on them) Oh... um... hello our television audience... thank you for joining us on the first showing of Showdown! Clybor: What was the point of this name change? We changed "beat" to "show". We could've at least changed the whole word, instead of just the prefix. Dan: Hey I don't make the decisions here and you don't either. Just be happy your getting a steady paycheck. Clybor: Phh... man I could make a lot of money anywhere. I'm one of the most talented color commentators in the world! I can do just about any sport. Dan: I don't care to hear you babble about how "good" of a commentator you are, frankly I just want to get on with the wrestling, after all that is why we are here. Clybor: I'm here for the- Dan: Shut your hole! Just as Dan tells Chris to shut it, "Harder, Better, Faster, Stronger" by Daft Punk plays on the arena loudspeakers and the Gund Arena starts to boo as newcomer LSD comes out to the ring. Clybor: This guy is sick Dan! He has promos about killing people! Dan: Isn't that your type of liking? Clybor: I didn't say I hated him or anything, he is sick and I do like it! Dan: Oh boy... Then as LSD climbs up the stairs and gets in between the ropes, "Alive" by P.O.D. blasts throughout and Trauma comes down with his manager, Ric Waters both wearing their expensive Versace clothing. Trauma gets greeted with a mixed reaction, he doesn't seem to care and he mouths off to a group of fans that are booing him and he continues the walk into the ring. Dan: This guy quits and he just decides to come back? Clybor: He probably had to whore himself in the street during his unemployment so he came back. Dan: You actually like thinking this way don't you? Clybor: My mind is pure as a virgin child. Dan: Man you have problems. The two wrestlers circle each other and the ring bell sounds off. LSD vs. Trauma The match started with both men exchanging blows. LSD dodges one of Trauma’s punches and capitalizes with a series of punches of his own. LSD puts on a headlock but Trauma reverses with an Irish Whip. Trauma tries for a clothesline but LSD ducks. LSD bounced on the other side of the rope and sends a High Flying Lariat on Trauma. Trauma hits the canvas and the fans started cheering. LSD dominates the match with Chops. LSD forces Trauma at the corner. LSD tries a Shoulder Thrust on the turnbuckle but Trauma gets out of the way. LSD hits the steel post with an impact. Dan Selito: Trauma almost got hit by that thunderous Shoulder Thrust! Chris Clybor: Luckily, Trauma is smart enough to move away. Dan Selito: LSD got to be hurt… Chris Clybor: Well DUH! Trauma drags LSD out of the corner and tries for a pin…1…2…shoulder up. Trauma picks up LSD and Body Slammed him. Trauma grabs LSD’s head and puts on a Headlock. The referee checks LSD…1…2… LSD puts his hand up in the fans’ delight. Elbows to the chest of Trauma. Another. Another. LSD bounced to the ropes but he was hit by Trauma’s HUGE Clothesline. Chris Clybor: That was a BIG ASS Clothesline. LSD must be knocked out. Dan Selito: That might be one of the big turn of events of the match. Trauma goes up to the top rope. Waits for LSD to get up. He hits a Missile Dropkick. Trauma goes for the pin…1…2… kick out! Trauma picks up LSD and Irish Whipped him. Trauma tries to put on another Headlock but LSD reversed it to a Backdrop. LSD grabs Trauma’s legs and applies a Boston Crab submission. Trauma is struggling… He finally reached the ropes. Trauma is exhausted at the canvas. LSD gets ready for his finisher move, Acid Haze. Trauma struggles to get up. LSD tries to grab Trauma, but Trauma gets out of the ring. Chris Clybor: Smart move by Trauma. Dan Selito: Trauma’s a coward. Chris Clybor: At least, he’s smarter than you. Dan Selito: Whatever man. LSD goes out of the ring and followed Trauma. Trauma runs around the outside of the ring and finally goes back in. LSD goes inside too but Trauma hits a spear on LSD. Trauma goes for the pin…1…2…1/2…kick out! Trauma is getting upset. He picks up LSD and Powerbombs him…1…2…1/2… kick out again! Trauma picks up LSD again and tries another Powerbomb, but LSD reverses with a Hurricanrana. Trauma is dazed. LSD Irish Whips Trauma and catches him with a Side Walk Slam. LSD goes for the pin…1…2… shoulder up. LSD picks up Trauma and throws him to the corner. LSD goes up the second rope for Ten Punches. The crowd counted with him…1…2…3…4…5…6…7… but then, Trauma grabs LSD and send him down with a Spinebuster. Dan Selito: This match could go either way! Chris Clybor: Trauma would win. Dan Selito: How’d you know? Chris Clybor: Ms. Cleo told me. Dan Selito: Whatever man. Chris Clybor: You like saying that don't you? Both men are down… The ref starts to count…1…2…3…4… both men struggle to get back up, LSD gets back up first and he goes for the Acid Smash... but Trauma counters with a knee to the midsection and then he hits the Traumatized! LSD is covered by Trauma... 1... 2... 3! Winner: Trauma by pinfall Dan: A pretty damn good way to start this show. Clybor: I'd say... The camera then cuts to Jason Chae in his office with Shane Slade. Chae is pacing around the room and Slade is sitting down on a leather couch, eating a sandwich. You can hear the crowd boo in the background as this image is also being shown on the WIWtron. Chae looks angry and his anger is confirmed as he kicks over his chair. Chae: Who is that Unknown guy! He stole my Z8! You know how hard it is to get one of those? You have to be someone rich and important like me! You can't be some nobody that lives in Cleveland! Shane started crawling to the ropes… fighting… fighting… Shane Andrews finally reached it! The Constable won’t let go. The ref warns him and he finally lets go. The Constable sets Shane for his finisher, The Adjudicator. But Shane reverses and grabs Constable by the neck! Shane is getting ready for his finishing move, The Show Stopper!
