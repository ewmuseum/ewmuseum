Michael J, WIW:NG Hall of Fame

Michael J MICHAEL J Other than Jason Chae himself, if you ask anyone that was part of WIW in its first two eras, who was a big part of WIW... they'd say Michael J. His full name was Michael Johnson, when he first joined WIW he wasn't active for a couple weeks, but then... history unfolded. He was without a doubt one of the most successful wrestlers in WIW history. He was the man that the fans loved to hate. He didn't wrestle fair and always had his friend Thorne around to help him out... not to mention his big bodyguard, Teran. As a wrestler he even feuded with Jason Chae himself. After he retired as a wrestler, a surprise annoucement was made... he was going to be the vice president of WIW! He held that position for over an year. Even as a member of the front office, he had many feuds, with Jason Chae and Cruel. Michael J has moved on from the wrestling business, but he will always be a big part of WIW history. Michael J's Accomplishments: WIW World Champion 8/01/99 to 8/05/99 WIW World Champion 8/15/99 to 8/16/99 WIW World Champion 7/02/00 to 7/10/00 WIW Tag Team Champion 7/29/99 to 8/01/99
