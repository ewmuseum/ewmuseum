Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Live from the Reunion Arena in Dales, Texas! Earlier this week, at the Raleigh, NC MSMP [Maximum Security Men’s Prison]. We're in a jail cell, with bunk beds, it is the middle of the night, after lights out. A groggy prisoner rustles awake, with another prisoner beside him; much like a homoerotic scene from Oz. From behind, all you can see as the prisoner wakes up is a polka dot dress and a ‘mary had a little lamb’ wig, with pig tails on each side. In the darkness, the prisoner seems out of sorts. The prisoner turns to see the camera, and as he does, you can see that the prisoner is in FULL drag queen make-up, complete with the rosy cheeks, and the lip-gloss. The camera zooms in, as the lights in the cell turn on, to see that the prisoner is; in fact; ERIK ERRATIC!!! The WIW crowd gasps in amazement. A shaded prison guard walks over to the cell & smirks. Erik: Uuuuugh, where am I? What is that taste in my mouth? Why is my asshole sore? Uuuugh, why do I have this killer headache? Guard: ERIKA Erratic, I see. As Norm McDonald always said, the thing he hates about prison the most is the... well, you can pretty much guess, can’t ya? Making yourself right at home here, aren’t you? As the camera pans back, it sees that the prison guard is the End! The WIW crowd gasps in amazement. Erik, still groggy: What? What happened? End: Well, ERIKA, ya messed with the End. You see, you could have done ANYTHING to get revenge on me, and I’d be cool with it, but you couldn’t think up ANYTHING original. I shocked you and then I pissed on you a week ago, you shocked me and then pissed on me Friday. Pretty unoriginal. And watching UNORIGINALITY is even worse than watching one of your boring ass matches. So I decided to show you what ONE-UPS-manship is all about. You see, I don’t need to piss on you again - though, I did - I decided to do something original, something that would be hard pressed to beat. Sure, I power-bombed you through a table, sure, I sent you to the hospital, sure, I had every hair on your body shaved off, sure, I hit you with 10,000 volts of electricity, sure I embarrassed you in front of millions, sure, it was all childish and low class, but when EYE did it, it was at least ORIGINAL. No more Mary, I’m spent. Keep it down, okay? End: Mary? I like that better than Erika. You see, Mary, I have connections. LOTS of connections. And when you went out to dinner on Friday night, in celebration of your ‘revenge,’ on me Slade, I had Pierre - the waiter at the restaurant you went to - SPIKE your drink. You passed out almost immediately, right into your New England Clam Chowder. From there, I took your unconscious body & hooked you up with John - my friend the dirty cop who works at the Raleigh Maximum Security Men’s Prison - and got you a free pass into the BIG HOUSE. You’d be amazed what $5,000 and a hacker named ‘Squiggles’ will get you these days. Erik falls to the floor writhing in pain. End: Fool you once, shame on me, fool you TWICE, shame on you, Mary. Besides, I wasn’t finished explaining my revenge ala any James Bond villain. Anyway, so I bought you a criminal record, and bought you a one way ticket to prison, where you have been ‘sleeping’ off your sedation for about a day and a half. The prisoners, I see, have taken a liking to you, Mary. I have A LOT of incriminating Polaroid's; of you and Bubba consummating your relationship; that I need to get scanned and put up on then internet. So, I’ll be off, toodles. Erik: I’m going to KILL YOU!!! I’m going to kill you. I AM GOING TO KILL YOU! End: Yeah. Sure. Whatever. I think that, RIGHT NOW, you are an out of control prisoner, and I’m sorry, but you need to be calmed down, Prison Bitch style. End: Well, YOU, my friend, are out of control, and are in for the beating of a life time. See ya on Chaos. Try to copy THIS, Mary...
