WIW:NG Column: Pablo's Pages, 9 August 2002

9 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pablo's Pages Welcome fans of World Internet Wrestling to a second edition of Pablo’s Pages from Chicago Illinois, and the pen of Pablo Nezazi. The fact I’m back for another column means you all liked last week’s, thanks for your support. For those of you who do not know who I am, I am the Manical Mafia Man, ex-WIW Wrestler and wrestling coach to the Boston Brawler LSD. In this column I shall be charting some of the hits and misses of WIW bosses. Plus I will give my “Match of the Week,” “Wrestlers of the Week”, “Surprise of the Week” and the newly thought of feature “Asshole of The Week.” Before that I is gonna bore you with my thoughts on the weeks controversy. Nice. As last week I’ll welcome the few new wrestlers hired by the Front Office. Again I’m not gonna mention any names-you know who you are! As many of you will no doubt be aware, I don’t like the New Breed angle-I still don’t but its not as boring is it? The injection of the new, original idea into the old feud isn’t new but it works! Maybe it only worked because of the ruse-but I don’t think so. The Unknown is a good addition to the New Breed’s cause, it evens the odds and makes Last Man Standing more unpredictable-maybe we’ll be closer to finding out who this guy is! My money’s on Jole Tempest maybe Ripcord, and the outsider is Commissioner Lox-He has to support one side doesn’t he? Lets all applaud Jason Chae!-Why ? Surely we all hate the New Breed? Well its not actually for his role in the New Breed, but simply as a good President. Last Man Standing is an absolute HUGE card. Have you read it yet? EIGHT matches outside of Last Man Standing itself-that’s a total of FIFTEEN matches! Most of those pivotal to the future of WIW! I would also like to express my joy at the return of the Underrated Gauntlet Match! Tune in to Last man Standing-A week from Sunday, ONLY on Pay per View! Chris Storm. My Wrestler of the Week for last week has had a strange time. His feud with Johnny Sikah has moved on brilliantly to result in an Old School Cage match on LMS. Look for these two wrestlers in coming events, they’re extremely talented and should replace an ageing Shane Slade as a Main Eventer in years to come. Who was that loser on Chaos? No not Eddie Gigante, but the Color Commentator Joey Bear! What's wrong with Chris Clybor? Clybor was excellent, his love of food and the New Breed made the Commentary vibrant and interesting. With Joey Bear, Chaos was a shambles-Hell I had to plug my ears through most of it! Lets hope Showdown returns with Chris Clybor and…well whoever else does Play-by-Play. SURPRISE OF THE WEEK Return of Benshaw. Why the late mention? I like to “surprise” too you know! I think the introduction of one of the best Wrestlers ever in WIW as Head Referee will finally rid the company of poor refereeing and manager’s stopping refs counting out their wrestler!-No? I don’t think so either. MATCH OF THE WEEK Shane Slade vs. e.T.o on Chaos. It was immensely entertaining-especially because I was able to drown out the sound of Joey Bear on my TV set! Really it was good, two experienced wrestlers, feuding, with one in their hometown the other with a manager in his corner, it was good. ASSHOLE OF THE WEEK This week I have two assholes for you all. The extra one is for the fact that this feature wasn’t available last week so week 1 “Asshole of The Week” goes to…Deke Aurora. I wont waste any more time on this jerk. Read last weeks column to see why. This week’s Asshole has already been mentioned, and so for his Bland, monotonous, ear-aching commentary Joey Bear is Asshole of The Week! WRESTLERS OF THE WEEK Number 5- Johnny Sikah Number 4- J Woods Number 3- e.T.o Number 2- Chris Storm NUMBER ONE- The End That’s all for this week. Enjoy? No? Well tough shit Pablo Nezazi will be back next week. Till then ARRIVEDERCI!
