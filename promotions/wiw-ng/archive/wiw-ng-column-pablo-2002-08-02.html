WIW:NG Column: Pablo's Pages, 2 August 2002

2 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Pablo's Pages Hello WIW fans, this is Pablo Nezazi in the first edition of a hopefully weekly column “Pablo’s Pages”-catchy huh? For those of you who do not know who I am, I am the Manical Mafia Man, ex-WIW Wrestler and wrestling coach to the Boston Brawler LSD. In this column I shall be charting some of the hits and misses of WIW bosses. Plus I will give my “Match of the Week,” “Wrestlers of the Week” and the “Surprise of the Week.” If I can think of any other features I’ll be sure to include them. Apart from the aforementioned features, I will just jot some inane rambling. First off, welcome to the new talent, I wont mention anyone because no doubt I’ll forget someone and they’ll be pissed with me. Some of these rookies have impressed me a great deal; others are as flat as a pancake. I’ll mention Deke Aurora, no longer with us but it is no surprise to us normal viewers, his character was as dull as watching paint dry-sure he was good technically, but lacking power, style, charisma and awareness he was easily defeated three times. Come on just how many people “just happen to” walk into the Golden Steed? WIW is better for the loss of him. Now this new talent is good, but more people are being hired than WIW is letting go, and it is really clogging up the active roster, especially the singles roster. If we had a few more Tag-Teams and had a really competitive Tag Division WIW would be sorted. I hope you all can see just how boring the WIW is getting. Most of WIW’s time is spent on the progress of the “New Breed.” This hurts low-card wrestlers they get disenchanted with the company and leave for somewhere they are accepted as talent. Don’t get me wrong, e.T.o, The End, Erratic and Slade are Main Event material, but don’t drag up old dead angles just to keep them in the spotlight. If better guys come through they should be given a chance. The New Breed saga is a poor excuse for a “New vs. Old” feud. Reason? Not enough wrestlers, the so-called New Breed has one key wrestler Shane Slade, and one back-up guy Reeco Suave. If they were as great as they are supposed to be they would recruit more wrestlers-make it a feud right from the first match, or don’t bother at all! Another thing, if massive feuds like this was going on, the other guys would mention it, but no they just wrestle and keep their noses clean. WIW is also forgetting the fact that the last time a New Breed was established, it shut down for a couple of months. A trick missed by WIW came on Tuesday’s Chaos. If e.T.o HAD sold out to Jason Chae, the feud would have been even and more interesting, it would have been easy to explain, The End and Erratic got the most exposure, e.T.o the background guy is resented and decides to turn heel-simple! I realize the feud has come too far to end it now, but end it at Last Man Standing-please! For the sake of the federation this angle must die! One of the many other opportunities and Main Event angles missed is The Unknown’s destruction of matches- He has taken a back seat since the New Breed feud matches for Last Man Standing were announced. This is a very good angle, a surprise angle, an original angle but it’s replaced for an old WIW favourite. To be honest I think one of the biggest disappointments was Shadow vs. Storm. Two new guys, one livid at the other halting his ritual, the other livid for having to stop a ritual. It could have been a classic, two excellent, young, wrestlers going at it, could have brought a casket match or something at last man Standing but it isn’t to be. Right well enough rambling, time to give my opinion on… SURPRISE OF THE WEEK Hey its gotta be Sam Sykes return to wrestling at the end of Chaos. What I can’t understand is why he didn’t join the New Breed? I don’t know and quite frankly I couldn’t give a flying fuck. MATCH OF THE WEEK Chirs Strom vs. Jonny Sikah. Chaos had a few good matches but this was the cream, two young wrestlers battled to get noticed. Several tide turns and explosive action made it a memorable match. WRESTLERS OF THE WEEK Each week I will bring you the top five competitors in my opinion, don’t bawl if you aren’t in it, just do better next week asshole! No.5-Jonny Sikah No.4-Brian Statis No.3-Erik Erratic No.2-The End NUMBER 1- Chris Storm Well that’s your lot this week, its long but there were a lot of issues I felt strongly about. Until Next Week ARRIVEDERCI!
