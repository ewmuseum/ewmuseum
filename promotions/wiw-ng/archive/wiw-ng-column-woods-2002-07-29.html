WIW:NG Column: Words from Woods, 29 July 2002

29 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Words from Woods It’s another week in the brilliant life of J Woods. So it’s also time for another column to be published from yours truly, J Woods. This week I’m going to address certain things but this weeks ain’t gonna be as good as last week because quite frankly I don’t think any of you guys deserved to be written about this week. So the WIW has linked up with the PTC ? In my eyes it’s a great move from the WIW head office. If you check it out you can see all of the other federations and how they’re getting on. Or you can see how far behind they are from the WIW and it’s wrestlers! I’m not going to be doing any personal appearances this week due to work and personal reasons, mainly personal. On Tuesday evening I’m jetting back to my hometown of Liverpool. I’m going to watch a football match, soccer as you guys say. I’m also going to see some friends and family, all which will be right after Chaos. It’s a good job that I’m only taking on Travis Smallz! I’ll be back for Showdown though; these eyes are never too far away from the game! I think Chaos this week was quite entertaining. The comeback of Jole Tempest. Where did he go, I could have guessed he’d align himself with the New Breed, what a sheep, a great follower, I wish he’d go it alone again, but what can you do. Jole didn’t end the week with the New Breed, which again was a shame but hey, the less piranhas in the tank, the less bite marks for me! The race for the WIW Championship heated up at Chaos too, which culminated on Showdown. With Shane Slade taking the WIW Heavyweight Championship, what does that mean for the New Breed, it can only be good for them. Reeco Suave has arrived too, I don’t know the guy but if he’s aligned himself with Jason Chae, then he’s probably as twisted as Chae is! All of this weeks shenanigans within the top tier of talent has been phenomenal. These guys are obsessed with the WIW Championship; they’ll do anything to hurt each other, especially Shane Slade! The Last Man Standing show is shaping up to be something great. As you all know I’m involved in the tournament and this week Crazy Cooper was thrown out of the tournament after he was fired from the WIW. So Golden Hammer has been placed into the tournament in Cooper’s place. Will Slade have the title then? He’ll put on a great show if he’s fighting for it. The Constable is going nuts at the arenas that we’ve gone through. I can’t wait until our Last Man Standing match because I am going to kick his ass! The refereeing in his match wasn’t great but there was no apparent reason for him to go nuts like he did. He thinks that he’s some sort of revolutionary! What in the hell is going on with Jon E. Mazing? This week Shadow, the sick freak, was about to go ahead with the sick ritual he had planned. As he was about to start it Chris Storm done us all a favour and shut the gothic fool up. What does that mean for Mazing? We’ll find out soon enough. Now to the rumours, last week I predicted that End would join the New Breed, it didn’t last the night but he still joined. I also said that Heavy Metal could be looking at reuniting, who’s fighting this coming Tuesday, the Stone Brothers, thank you. Here are some more rumours! Reeco Suave hasn’t had a match yet and when I was walking by the New Breed’s locker room at Showdown I could have sworn I heard him talking about taking someone out. Who is it, stick around to see. The Tag Team department seems to have simmered down terribly since me and Shorty The Pimp took the titles once again. What will Jason Chae do about the poor turn out of Tag Teams? Can anyone say “Tournament”? Shadow had his ritual stopped dead by Chris Storm as already said. What will the weirdo have in store for the new Intercontinental Champion? Shane Slade seems to be getting involved in an awful lot of matches recently. One of the backstage guys that nobody ever sees isn’t happy about it at all. Could he be making a comeback to put a stop to the World Champions antics? Trivia question, I don’t want guys coming up to me with the answer but try to answer it anyway. Here it is: Back in the day, Mystikal and a wrestler known as Chaos forged a Tag Team, what where they called? That is this week’s column from me. It’s a little rushed this week and quality isn’t as great. Do I owe anybody anything? NO. The words here are my own, feel free to quote them if you want. Part of one of the greatest teams on earth and one half of your Two-Time Tag Team Champions, J Woods.
