WIW:NG Rules

captured 29 November 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I. Introduction WIW was started in May of 1999 after Jason Chae had went through many e-feds that had shut down after around two months of operation or less. Jason Chae was tired of not being able to be in a successful e-federation so he decided to make his own. WIW has closed on and off, but it ran for a long stretch from November 1999 to August 2000... now it has been running strong since November 2000. WIW closed again in March 2001. Now it has reopened nine months later, in December 2001. In June 2001 it expanded to create a big e-wrestling world with XWO. Read some of the archives if you are new, it will help you get a better feel for WIW. This is a guide to how WIW is operated and the rules of WIW. II. The Staff These are the people that bring you quality e-wrestling! NOTE: You may not attack any of these employees in a roleplay with my permission. Or if you use any of these characters in a manner I do not approve of (check with me if you have any doubt), there will be consequences. A. President Jason Chae The founder and president of World Internet Wrestling and the author of this handbook. Jason Chae is an Asian American in his late twenties. Jason Chae has a nice demeanor to people, until they try and cross him. B. Vice President/Director of Operations Lox a.k.a. [name withheld] Lox continues the tradition of having former WIW wrestlers come in and step in join the front office. There is no doubt that Lox will do a fine job. C. Commentators Chris Clybor, Dan Selito, Eddie Gigante Eddie Gigante and Chris Clybor make up the announcing team for Chaos, and Dan Selito and Chris Clybor make up the announcing team for Showdown. All three commentators are present to make a three man booth at Pay Per View events. Eddie Gigante is a longtime WIW announcer does Chaos play by play and all the Pay Per View events. He knows a lot about wrestling. He is well educated and knows what he is talking about. Eddie Gigante is a big man, six feet tall and two hundred and fifty pounds. He comes from a family with a Italian Mafia background. He decided that organized crime wasn't for him and went into broadcasting. Jason Chae was looking for a new announcer one day so he decided to hire his long time friend Eddie. Eddie is in his mid-thirties. Dan Selito was a rookie announcer when was first hired by WIW, but he has learned quickly and become a great play by play man for Showdown. Dan is very young, he is only twenty three years old. Chris Clybor thinks he knows everything, but he doesn't. He also thinks he is very buff and athletic, when in reality he is a 350 pound whale. Clybor is always eating at ringside whether it be a bag of chips to a full course meal. Clybor is the color commentator at all events. Clybor has said he is twenty eight years old, but he tells so many lies that no one knows what to believe. D. Interviewers Steve Sanders, Peter Castle, Kim Simpson Peter Castle is looks like the typical nerd with large glasses and he dresses in a nice collared shirt with dress pants. Peter Castle is pretty small, he is five feet and six inches tall and weighs about one hundred and forty pounds. Steve Sanders is the opposite. He looks very athletic and is six feet and four inches tall and weights about two hundred pounds. Sanders actually was a wrestler in the independent circuits, but he had to quit because of back injuries. Sanders then decided to go into broadcasting. Kim Simpson is the female interviewer, she was hired because of her sex appeal and figure. She is about five feet and three inches and weights about a hundred pounds and has long blond hair. E. Ring Announcer Pedro Sanchez He is the ring announcer of the WIW. As you can tell from his name, he is Mexican American. He's about fifty years old and a retired wrestler that wrestled in a hardcore Mexican wrestling federation. He is pretty small at five feet and eight inches tall and one hundred and fifty pounds. F. Security Dan Lawrence Just by looking at this individual, you can tell why he was hired as the head of security. He is a massive six feet ten inches tall and weights about four hundred and fifty pounds. He has the look of The Big Show. Fans know not to get out of control when they see this man patrolling near the ring. G. Refs Joseph Howard Bob Carr Travis Ware Kenny Kerns Bastion Dulsey The referees are a fair group and they do their job well. Jason Chae has instructed these referees to only use a count out or disqualification in extreme cases. Chae feels that the crowd would rather see a match get resolved then end with a count out or disqualification. III. Roleplaying Roleplaying for every match is REQUIRED. If you don't, there's a good chance you will get FIRED. There is no limit to how many roleplays you can write. The winner of the match will always be the wrestler that wrote better roleplays than his opponents. Of course, if the opponents agree to a preset ending for storyline purposes, roleplaying has no affect on the outcome. The quality of the roleplay is more important than the quantity. One excellent roleplay will always beat out two average roleplays. Here are some things you CAN NOT do in a roleplay: Use racial slurs. This will result in immediate termination. Controlling other wrestlers in a roleplay. Depending on how severe the use of another wrestler was, this can result in either a warning, suspension, or being fired from WIW. Using someone else's roleplay and trying to use it as your own original work. You can not take another wrestlers roleplay, alter minor details and say it is your own roleplay. This will result in immediate termination. If you copy a roleplay off a wrestler from a different federation and I find out about it, you will also be fired immediately. Personal property of others cannot be used in your roleplays. Say the John Doe is in the fed, a part of John's gimmick is that he carries around a "baseball bat". You can't use John's "baseball bat" and say you stole it in your roleplay or for any reason in you roleplays. Iif you use these following words in a roleplay, the roleplay will not be counted: RP (if used as an abbreviation for the word roleplay) Roleplay Handler (definition of the controller of a wrestler) E-Fed E-Wrestling This would be breaking character to an extreme degree, it is not allowed and I will simply ignore your roleplay as if it were never posted. IV. Rules If you win a match by disqualification, you will not get a win in the official records... the loser will not get a loss in the official records. If you win a tag team match with someone that is not your official partner, you will not get a tag team win. If you lose a tag team match with someone that is not your official partner, you will not get a tag team loss. Titles will not change hands by disqualification unless stated beforehand. I'll also use this section to talk about interference and run-ins in matches. FIRST OF ALL... ALL INTERFERENCE REQUESTS ARE TO BE SENT THE DAY BEFORE THE CARD DATE. Monday night requests should be sent by Sunday and Thursday night requests by Wednesday. But any interference you have WILL NOT determine the winner. As said above, ROLEPLAYS determine winners (unless all parties agree to otherwise). Your interferences, run-in plans that you suggest and use WILL HAVE THE POSSIBLY of not being used, backfiring, etc... Do not expect ALL of your requests to be fulfilled, that is not fair. V. Events WIW has two weekly events. Tuesday Night Chaos and Friday Night Showdown. Both shows are basically the same. Neither is primary or secondary to each other. Eventually, rookies will move on to Chaos and when lower card wrestlers get better they will also move on to Chaos. Friday Night Showdown will not occur on Pay Per View weeks. I believe there should be an extra time period to roleplay for Pay Per Views, since they are very important events. Also Chaos will not happen after a Pay Per View. Twenty four hours is a ridiculous amount of roleplaying time, so the next event after a Pay Per View is Showdown. THE DEADLINE FOR ROLEPLAYS WILL DIFFER FROM CARD TO CARD. I'll use whatever time that best fits into my working/school hours so I can get the results done in good time and have them posted. VI. Titles A. WIW World Title This the main title that everyone wants to get. Having this title will make you the top man or woman in WIW and you will get the top exposure and a lot of recognition. The World Title MUST be defended each Pay Per View and another additional defense must be made each month. The WIW World Title is not something a wrestler can look to just win within three weeks of being in the federation. That happens in only very rare cases. The level it takes to reach to World Title status is different with each wrestler. Some accomplish it in a matter of a couple months while others take almost a year to reach this level. B. WIW Intercontinental Title The Intercontinental title is the second most respected title. If the champion goes over well, the next step would be fighting in main event matches and a chance to win the World Title. The Intercontinental Title MUST be defended at each Pay Per View and another additional defense must be made each month. C. WIW United States Title The United States Title is also a respect title in WIW. A lot of talented wrestlers have held this belt and went onto better things for their careers. If you hold this title you are exposed a little less than the Intercontinental champion, but you are recognized as a top wrestler in WIW. The United States Title MUST be defended at each Pay Per View and another additional defense must be made each month. D. WIW Television Title The Television Title is usually the title wrestlers go after when they first come to WIW. This title is usually the target of rookies to get their name established. A lot of veterans have also held the title however, to make their presence felt. The Television Title holder can look forward to other titles if they have a successful run. The Television Title MUST be defended at each Pay Per View and another additional defense must be made each month. E. WIW Hardcore Title The hardcore title is sought after people willing to go through tables, bash in cages, and fight on ladders for some gold. This title is truly for the most extreme wrestlers in WIW. The Hardcore Title MUST be defended at each Pay Per View and another additional defense must be made each month. F. WIW Tag Team Titles The WIW Tag Team title is for the two wrestlers in WIW who work the together as the best team. The Tag Team Titles MUST be defended at each Pay Per View and another additional defense must be made each month. VII. Applying To apply to WIW either post a roleplay in the free agent board or fill out the application on this site.
