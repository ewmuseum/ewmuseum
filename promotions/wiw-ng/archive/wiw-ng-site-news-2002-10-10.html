WIW:NG Site News, October 2002

10 October 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Name: Da D'MoN Height: 6'10'' Weight: 305 Age: 25 Accolades: WIW Hardcore Champion Theme Music: "Tempus Vernum" by Enya This 6'10'' Monster stormed his way onto the WIW seen by winning the hardcore title. Recently he earned himself his first main event match at Showdown. Stay tuned for big things coming from D'MoN! [10.10.02] - 'Results Up...Kind Of.' Results are up... in a way... you'll see what I mean when you look at them. Explanation is on the OOC Board. [10.07.02] - 'Updates Galore.' Many updates were done to the site tonight, including the roster (yes, I know bios still need to go up, that will happen...)... the Retribution card for this Sunday has been finalized. Everyone that has been active in the past few weeks should be booked or in Da D'Mon's case, involved... if not contact me ASAP so we can get that fixed. There are two columns up for everyone's reading pleasure. One by J Woods and another by [name withheld]. I know the archives and hall of fame are still waiting for some updates... haven't forgotten about those either. [09.27.02] - 'WIW Chaos, The Video Game.' Today Nintendo released the first 5,000 copies of WIW:Chaos nationwide. On the cover are (left to right) Erik Erratic, Chris Storm and Golden Hammer. Since 8 o clock this morning half of the released games have been sold, making Chaos the number one selling GameCube game at the moment, be aware that Nintendo has more goodies like this in store! THIS JUST IN! Nintendo plans to release the second of the 5 game WIW video game deal for the GameCube on October 5th. The game has apparently been in the making for over 6 months but no information was released to the press. This product looks to be amazing, a shot of the cover can be seen below.
