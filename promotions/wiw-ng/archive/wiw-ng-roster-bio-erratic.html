Erik Erratic Handler Information [name withheld] [detail withheld] Wrestler Information Real Name: Erik Erratic Height: 6-0 Weight: 222 lbs Age: 19 Hometown: San Diego, California Alliance: Face Theme Music: "Till I Collapse” by Eminem Wrestling Style: Aerial Attacks, Technical. Finisher Name: Erratic Triumph Finisher Description: Reverse Falling DDT position to Reverse Pumphandle Drop Manager: N/A History: Erik Erratic grew up with a rich family. He always gets what he wants when he was just a kid. Toys, Cars, Clothes, you name it. Chances are, he got it. He had a beautiful girlfriend when he was 13 and they love each other very much. Some might say that it was puppy love but in Erik's eyes, it was true love. But then reality struck and he's family went out of business. Because of this, they have to leave their native country and migrate to U.S.A. Since then, Erik life changed. He was away from his girlfriend and that hurt him the most. In California, U.S.A., Erik and his family lived in a violent ghetto. There was shooting every night and fistfights every morning. Erik knew that he couldn't just go outside and play like he usually does. His parents won't let him go outside. He knew his life was actually changed. But one day, against his parents' orders, he went to a basketball park nearby. There were gangsters everywhere BUT there was no sign of any violence. Erik started playing basketball and he met high school drop-outs. They started playing everyday and Erik realized that the place wasn't all that bad. Erik started hanging out with the "homies" and he started to create a bond with them. All of them were like brothers. But unlike his friends, Erik has dreams. He doesn't want to stay in the streets and see kids sell drugs so they can live. Erik wanted a new life. He wanted to go back to the life he used to have. The RICH LIFE. He wants to be a professional wrestler... And to cut the long story short, after years of hard work, he was finally hired by a large company known as WIW. He worked his ass every single day and he lived up to his own promises of living a rich life again.
