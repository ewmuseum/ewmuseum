WIW:NG Chaos Results, 23 July 2002

23 July 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The WIW:NG Chaos for July 22 opens at the front of the Joe Louis Arena in Detroit, Michigan. The camera is looking out towards Detroit, from the front doors of the arena... it pans around, and sees a tall, skinny figure standing 10 feet from the camera. It is Frank Van Cruyff... the jobless man who was seen attempting to wrestle and perfect the art of holding a camera the night before... this time he is looking outside to the parking lot, as if waiting for somebody. He is wearing his usual sunglasses and suit. Soon after, his eyes widen, as somebody is approaching. He straightens his tie, and stands up straight, and the camera turns it's attention to the outside, where e.T.o, who is scheduled to face the Unknown later on tonight, is striding towards the entrance carrying a bag. The moment he enters the building, Frank begins to speak to him... Frank: Eexcuusse mee, but I foss funterink eef you could geef me a chob? I um unemployed at se moment, und you neet sum help fiff se bag... I fill carry eet for you... He tries to grab the bag, but e.T.o looks at him angrily, and Frank retreats defensively. An pleased with himself looking e.T.o strolls off, and asks a man in a WIW:NG shirt for directions to his locker room... the man almost spills his coffee at being adressed by somebody so great, but manages to compose himself and point to one of the long corridoors leading towards the heart of the Joe Louis Arena. e.T.o walks in that direction, and out of sight... the camera pays attention to Frank, who is now in the exact same pose as he was before, as if nothing has gone on... but his expression changes again, as Da D'MoN closes in on the arena. He, once again, straightens his tie. D'MoN is carrying nothing, but is wearing his usual black cloak with a green broach... he does not seem to have prepared for the night's match against Larry Stone. He walks in the doors without glancing at Frank, but Frank tugs the back of his cloak, forcing D'MoN to turn around... D'MoN: Yes? What do you want from your future Hardcore Champion? Frank: Fell, I fos thinkink sat you neet a manatcher... I need a chob, you neet a manatcher... eet makes sense! Just tell me fere your locker room ees locatet, und I fill be your assistent. D'MoN: Yes... you could help me with my match tonight... I'm facing a guy called Starry Lone... yes... you could definately help me... Frank: Yes... yes... yes. How much feel you be payink me? D'MoN: Let's see how well you do the job first... follow me. D'Mon walks off, in the direction that e.T.o walked just a couple minutes before, Frank Van Cruyff tagging along behind. As now it seems as if the WIW Chaos intro video is about to play... wait the scene is now inside the Joe Louis Arena, in what appears to be the suite of the New Breed. Jason Chae and Shane Slade appear and you can hear the loud boos coming from the Joe Louis Arena crowd in the backstage. Jason Chae and Slade are both wearing New Breed t-shirts and Slade is in his wrestling shorts while Chae has on a pair of khaki shorts. Slade is just standing around while Jason Chae is talking on a cell phone. Chae: Yeah... Uh huh... Okay, so this will all go into motion tonight? Great... I'll see you then... Jason Chae shuts off his phone and he puts it back in his pocket. Chae: Good news Shane, its a done deal. Slade: So there will be a new member of the New Breed today? Chae: That's right. This is going to be a fun night. As soon as Chae finishes his words, Chaos cuts to its introduction video showing various WIW wrestlers' names being shown on the screen in the glow green color as videos of wrestling action plays in the background along with the instrumental to "Bullet With Butterfly Wings" by the Smashing Pumpkins playing in the background. Then the camera pans across the crowd in the Joe Louis Arena where the city of Detroit is making a lot of noise. Then we go to the ringside view of the announcer's table where the number one Italian play by play man in wrestling... Eddie Gigante is joined with the most obese color commentator in wrestling... Chris Clybor! Eddie: Welcome to another showing of Chaos! It seems like the New Breed will have another member join them tonight. Clybor: It's the New Breed, people are flocking to it, because of it's immense superiority. Eddie: And it seems like Da D'Mon has a new manager! Clybor: Yeah, that Dutch guy gives me the creeps. Eddie: Why? Clybor: I don't know, he's just really creepy. Eddie: Okay... how about we just get this show on the road? Tonight we are starting with a debut match of two wrestlers that have very promising talent. Johnny "Plague" Sikah vs. "Mr. Infamous" Deke Aurora ""Leper Messiah" by Metallica blasts over the speakers in the Joe Louis Arena and Johnny Sikah comes down to the ring with his manager Summer Anison. Johnny is dressed in all black except for a cutoff red shirt that has skulls across the chest along with tribal signs. He is also wearing a silver necklace with a skull charm on it. Summer is wearing a black bikini top that matches the black leather pants she is wearing. The two are greeted with more boos than cheers in their first WIW appearance. Eddie: This Johnny "Plague" Sikah is not getting a warm welcome from the Detroit crowd. Clybor: Look at him, he doesn't have the commercial appeal of The End or e.T.o. Then "My Way" by Sid Vicious fills up the arena and out comes "Mr. Infamous" Deke Aurora, who is wearing long baggy white pants tied with a black sash. He also has on a long white leather jacket with a sunburst Japanese flag on the back. Eddie: This is a Canadian born wrestler that has wrestled and toured in Japan! Clybor: Eh... big deal. Eddie: Both wrestlers get in and Deke is taking off his jacket and there goes the bell. The match starts off with Johnny and Deke locking up. Johnny pushes Deke to the canvas and Johnny taunts Deke. Deke suddenly attacked his opponent with a spear and followed it with punches to the face. Deke picks up Johnny and Irish Whips him. Deke catches Johnny on a Headlock but Johnny immediately counters with a Chin Breaker. The move made Deke turn his back, so Johnny ran behind Deke’s back with a Face Crusher. Johnny climbs the tope rope and waited for Deke to stand up. Johnny tries a High Flying Crossbody but Deke reverses with a Huge Powerslam! Eddie: This two rookies are showing good moves. Clybor: Both men are sacrificing their bodies just to make a name for themselves. Eddie: Well, that’s how big NG is. Everyone wants to get in, but only few gets the opportunity. Both men lay on the mat. The ref starts to count…1…2…3…4… Deke Aurora is the first man up. Johnny Sikah is up as well. They exchange punches. Deke dodges one of Johnny’s punches and takes down Johnny with a Falling Neckbreaker. This time, Deke is the one climbing the top rope. But Johnny runs to the rope interrupting Deke’s move. Johnny goes to the top rope as well and executes a SUPER SUPLEX. Both men are down momentarily but Johnny was able to go over Deke for the cover. The ref started to count…1…2…2 ½… KICK OUT!!! Eddie: What a way to start off Chaos. Clybor: This two are determine to win. Johnny stands up and gives the sign for his finisher, Black Plague. Deke struggles to stand up. Johnny goes for the finisher but Deke managed to reversed it with an unusual pin…1…2…2 ½… Kick out. Johnny was daze and Deke goes for a School Boy pin…1…2…shoulder up. Deke goes for a Crucifix Pin…1…2…kick out again… Johnny is getting pissed with all the pin and sends Deke down with a Thunderous Clothesline. That gives Johnny some time to breathe. He leans to the ropes as he waits for Deke to stand up yet again. Deke sees Johnny planning something behind his back so he runs right away at the resting Johnny. But Johnny ducks making Deke fly away from the ring all the way to the floor. Johnny runs on the other side of the ropes and jumps down on Deke with a high flying Rope Flip. Both men are obviously hurt. Eddie: Oh my god! This two won’t stop at nothing just to win. Clybor: I won’t be surprise if Johnny dies here tonight with all that high risk moves… Eddie: Haha… It's all for the glory. Clybor: I agree with that one. Johnny gets up throws Deke inside the ring. Johnny goes inside as well but Deke catches him with a Belly to Belly Suplex. An unknown man starts coming down to the ring. Eddie: Hey, that's a new wrestler that just signed with WIW! Travis Smallz! Travis Smallz has a steel chair in his hands and he slides it in the ring as the referee is not aware of his presence. Then Travis jumps on the apron and referee Bastion Dulsey notices him and he starts arguing with him. Deke goes over to Travis and he starts puching away at Travis, trying to knock him off the apron. But Travis holds onto the ropes and this allows for Johnny to get back up and grab the chair that Travis slid into the ring. Eddie: Johnny Sikah has that chair and he slams it into the head of Deke Aurora! Deke falls down to the mat and Johnny tosses the chair out of the ring and he pins Deke... 1... 2... 3! Winner: Johnny "Plague" Sikah by pinfall Johnny gets back up and he gets a microphone from a ring crew member and he speaks. Johnny: Deke... I told you that I was hardcore. And I just proved it. I don't care about how many countries you've wrestled in, but this is America and it is different! Your little Japanese marital art moves don't work here, neither does your technical Canadian styles. America is all about being hardcore and I just proved it to you! Johnny tosses the microphone to the mat and he leaves with Travis Smallz and Summer Anison and the crowd boos the trio and they start a chant for Deke Aurora. Eddie: That was a nice match. But I’m looking forward on seeing Erik’s match. Clybor:: Hell no! Slade’s match will be the best. Eddie: Your a Slade mark, so I don't care. Clybor: Well, at least, Slade isn't a filler champ! Eddie: Can’t we stop arguing? We’re on a televised show, you know? Clybor: Well, commercial is next so we can still continue the argument… -Commercial Break- Eat butter... it tastes good! Eddie: We’re back! Clybor: But we’re still not finished. I still think Slade is better than Erik. Eddie: Why don’t you ask The End who’s better of the two? Clybor: Hell no. He is biased. He'll say both of them suck. Eddie: That we can agree on. So now, can we go back to the show? Clybor: I don’t know what you’re talking about? I’m working the whole time. Just let me introduce the next match, all right? Okay…our next match is between Da D’Mon and Larry Stone for the Hardcore Title Belt… Eddie: This is going to be REAL HARDCORE! Clybor: It’s always hardcore when Larry and his managers are in the house… Tits and ass galore baby! Larry Stone vs. Da D'Mon Hardcore Title Match "Detroit Rock City" blares over the sound system and Larry Stone comes out to a good pop from the crowd with his two title belts being held by each of his managers, Lisa XXX and Stacee. The ladies go off to the side and Larry gets in the ring as "Tempus Vernum" by Enya plays over the loud speakers and the fans cheer as Da D'Mon steps out of the curtains and he is with his new manager who is in full suit and tie wear, Frank Van Cruyff. Da D'Mon seems to have a weapon in his hands... a computer keyboard! It's a keyboard off a Hewlett Packard computer! Frank stares at the lady managers of Larry Stone and they wave to him and he just yells back in Dutch! The ladies back off as Da D'Mon gets in the ring. Clybor: I'm telling you, that Dutch guy is freaking creepy! Eddie: Okay... okay... I get your point. The match started with Larry Stone punching away at Da D’Mon, but Da D'Mon doesn't drop the keyboard. Da D’Mon returns the favor with punches of his own and he whacks Larry hard with the keyboard. D’Mon forces Larry to the ropes. D’Mon took a step back and then Clothesline Larry out of the ring. Lisa and Stacee helps Larry to get up. Inside the ring, D’Mon taunts Larry to get back into the ring. Larry most certainly plans on going back on the ring. But now, he’s bringing a steel chair, a fire extinguisher, a trashcan, and a kendo stick with him. Eddie: Now, business is about to pick up! Clybor: I would rather watch Lisa and Stacee walk around the ring than watch this brutal match. Eddie: Okay Clybor, save that imagination at home. Larry throws all the weapons inside the ring. Then Larry finally goes back inside the ring. But D’Mon drops the keyboard and picks up the kendo stick and hits Larry in his shoulder… Again then again then again. D’Mon goes for the pin…1…2…kick out. D’Mon picks up the trash can as he waits for Larry to get up. Larry grabs the steel chair as he gets up. D’Mon was about to hit Larry in the head but Larry hits the trashcan making the trashcan hit D’Mon’s face. Eddie: Damn, look at what Larry did to that trashcan! It’s all mess up now. Clybor: Damn, look at what Larry did to D’Mon’s face. That’s the better statement. Larry goes for the pin...1…2… 2 ½… kick out. Larry can’t believe it. Larry goes outside again. He takes out a table underneath the ring and throws it on the apron. But D’mon runs and Baseball Slides the table right to Larry face. Larry goes down to the floor. D’mon then followed it with a Vaulting Press down on Larry. Eddie: Da D’mon is on a roll. He might take the title tonight. Clybor: Just wait… Larry still got energy left. And you know Larry, he can go all night long! D’mon picks up the table and throws it inside the ring. D’mon picks up Larry as well and pushes him inside the ring. D’mon rolls inside the ring and sets the table…D’mon then grabs Larry and Snap Suplexed him. D’mon goes outside the ring again and grabs a another table and a ladder!!! D’mon sets the ladder and the new table inside the ring as well. Eddie: Just when I thought Deke and Johhny put on a good match, the fun is just starting. Clybor: This is gonna be good. D’mon grabs the exhausted Larry on the head but Larry Low Blowed D’mon. Larry then throws D’mon to the table. Larry goes up the ladder and signals for the MTV! But D’mon gets out of the table and goes up the ladder too. Both men exchange punches on top of the ladder. D’mon finally gets the advantage. Now D’mon is setting Larry Stone for a SUPERPLEX!!! Larry is fighting…But D’mon used all his power to bring Larry up the air and all the way down to the table on the mat. BOOOMM!!! Both men broke the table… Eddie: Oh my god! Are we watching pay-per-view or something? This is incredible! Clybor: Incredible??? That’s an understatement! This is CRAZY!!! Both men are hurt as hell. The ref started to count…1…2…3…4…5…6…7…8… D’mon gets up. So did Larry. Both men started punching each other. D’mon gets the advantage again and executes an unexpected D’mon Driver! D’mon goes for the pin…1…2…2 1/2 but Larry grabbed the fire extinguisher and sprayed it all over D’mon’s face breaking the count… D’mon is running around the ring trying to comfort his eyes. Larry catches Da D’mon with D’mon’s on finisher, D’mon Driver. Larry puts D’mon on the other table… Larry goes up the ladder again…Larry gives the sign for The MTV again… He leaps up and goes down to Da D’mon, but Da D'Mon moves out of the way at the last second! Larry Stone crashes into the table by himself! Da D'Mon then just covers him for the pin while Frank Van Cruyff is yelling in Dutch. 1... 2... 3! Winner: Da D'Mon by pinfall Eddie: That one stole the show… Clybor: No doubt man… No doubt. -Commercial Break- WIW is brought to you by Honda! Several of the WIW superstars are sitting in a room talking. There seems to be something of a debate going on. Bryan Statis: I am telling you guys, The End is joining the New Breed! The Constable: What the hell would you know? You’ve been in the WIW like what – 3 minutes? The End would never beaccepted in the WIW! My money is on Shadow. He is a man who could really fit in to the New Breed equation! Dru Jacobs: Listen, I am about to face Shane Slade in a hellacious match so if anyone should know just what type of a man would fit with Slade in the New Breed is, it is me. I guarantee you that it will be someone with a big name – someone who could make an impact. Bryan Statis: So who do you think it is? Dru Jacobs: It could be anyone from e.T.o to The Constable. Maybe even Erik Erratic… Trauma peers out from the shadows… Trauma: Or maybe it is someone we haven’t seen in a while… Jacobs, Statis and The Constable all look at one another the camera cuts back to the ringside announcer's table position. Eddie: The fact that Slade and Chae have announced that the New Breed will have a new member today has caused a lot of talk. It seems like it is on everyone's mind. Clybor: I wouldn't be surprised if it is someone huge... coming back! Like Joe Average. Eddie: Joe Average is retired for good. Clybor: Maybe it is Chris Nazareth. The two announcers look at each other and they bust out laughing. Eddie: That was a good one... right now the tag team titles are going to be on the line. The Renegades vs. Crazy Cooper & Gladiator Tag Team Title Match "Beautiful Day" blares over the arena sound system. The Joe Louis Arena burst into cheers as the Renegades come through the curtain. J Woods and Shorty the Pimp walk out holding up the tag team titles. They enter the ring and “One Step Closer" by Linkin Park starts to rock the arena. The fans cheer as Crazy Cooper steps out of the curtains and he comes down to the ring. Gladiator starts to come out but the Renegades have already started beating up on Crazy Cooper, who slid into the ring before his partner came out. Eddie: Not a smart move by Crazy Cooper. He should have waited for Gladiator. Clybor: What do you want from him? He’s not a tag team wrestler. This is him and Gladiator’s first tag team match together that I know of. Gladiator slides into the ring and instantly throws Shorty the Pimp out of the ring. He turns to J Wood but J Wood backs off and gets into his corner. The ref makes Gladiator get into his corner. As Shorty is getting back into the ring, J Wood tags himself in. Eddie: Well it looks like J Wood and Crazy Cooper will start things up. J Wood and Crazy circle around, looking for a chance to attack. Short reaches over the ropes and tries to hit Crazy. Crazy turns to hit him and J Wood attacks him from behind. J Wood rushes him into the corner and drives his shoulder into Crazy’s back. The ref gets J Wood off of him and Short starts choking him on the rope. J tags in Shorty and they double team Crazy in the corner. Shorty hits a back breaker on Crazy as J drops an elbow drop to his chest. Shorty pins. 1.. 2.. Gladiator is able to make the save. Clybor: That one was almost over. Eddie: Renegades are working excellently together tonight. He grabs Shorty and lifts him up in a choke hold. J enters the ring and nails a low blow on Gladiator. Shorty nails him with a face buster. J and Shorty tag back out and double team Gladiator. They whip him to the ropes but Gladiator comes back with a Double clothesline. The ref makes him get back in the corner. Crazy tags out. The ref gets Shorty out. Gladiator Head butts J before sending him down to the mat with a Knee to the Chest. Gladiator bounces off the ropes and tries for a massive elbow drop but J Woods rolls out of the way. J Wood bounces off the ropes and hits a Dropkick to Gladiator’s face. He tags in Shorty. They double team on Gladiator, keeping him on the mat. Clybor: Haha, look at Gladiator. Guy can’t get off the mat. Eddie: I must agree. Size doesn’t mean anything if you can’t get off the mat. Clybor: Renegades rule. No wonder they are tag team champions. Eventually Gladiator is able to get to his feet again and he fights off both members of the Renegades. He tries to whip Shorty into the corner but Shorty reverses it. J Woods grabs Shorty and whips him into the corner. Shorty tries to Clothesline Gladiator but Gladiator nails a Big Boot as Shorty is coming into the corner. J Wood goes to help but Crazy comes in and nails a Falling Neck breaker on him. Gladiator picks Shorty up on his shoulder. Crazy sees it and climbs top rope. Right before Crazy can deliver a massive Clothesline to Shorty, J Woods chop blocks Gladiator, causing Shorty to fall on his chest. J Wood runs and jumps onto top rope with Crazy. He grabs him and jumps off top rope, hitting a Buzzkiller on him as they land. Shorty covers Gladiator. 1.. 2.. 3! Eddie: The Renegades have successfully defended their titles! Clybor: Ha I told you they would win. I’m always right. Eddie: Well they did win and it was thanks to some amazing team work. The scene changes into pitch black... there is nothing, but then a candle lights up and not much can be seen... Eddie: What is this? Clybor: I have no clue... But the shutting of a door sound is heard and it seems like something heavy was just dropped on the ground. The camera adjusts to get a better view... it's the body of Jon E. Mazing!!! Eddie: Holy shit! -Commercial Break- Eat some Lay's potato chips today... bet you can't have just one! “Master Of Puppets” plays and the entire arena lets out a deafening chorus of jeers as Shane Slade and the president of the WIW, Jason Chae, make their way down the ramp. The two men are wearing New Breed shirts and Slade has a third shirt in his hand, presumably to give to the latest member of the New Breed. The heat that Chae and Slade are getting is unbelievable. Chae: Now, seeing as this announcement will rock the very foundation of the WIW I will not beat around the bush, I will simply cut to the chase. The latest member of the New Breed is none other than a returning superstar…it is none other than…e.T.o! You think that I have sat on the injured list for the past six weeks so I can come back to the WIW and join up with an egomaniac and his little bitch?
