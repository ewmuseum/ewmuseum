What's the holdup people??? Hasn't anyone got the guts??? You know it's nice that all of these fantasy groups are going good but it's time for a new kid to jump on the block here. How many other games are run by an actual independant pro-wrestler??? Check out my website if you'd like to see... www.geocities.com/colosseum/2655 All you have to do to join is come up with an original idea for a wrestler and or tag team and email it to me. I dont want a whole list of moves and all of that jumbo. You advance as you get more active in the game. So...With that in mind my email box should get floded... Jump in the Fun.. PJ
