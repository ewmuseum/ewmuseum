The IWF looking for roleplayers

7 June 1998
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The International Wrestling Federation (IWF) will be starting up near the end of June. It will be using real wrestlers, with a maximum of 4 per member. Their will be 2 cards a week, Tuesday TNT(secondary show), and Saturday night main event(the main show). Roleplays are sent to me, compiled, and sent out with the shows, on a show called "It's all talk". Roleplays are only required twice a week. There will be one PPV each month. The belts are as follows: 1. IWF Worlds title 2. IWF Intercontinental title 3. IWF Tag team titles 4. IWF Cruiserweight title If you'd like to join, or, want more info, email me at [detail withheld]
