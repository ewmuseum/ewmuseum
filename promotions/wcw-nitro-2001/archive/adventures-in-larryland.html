Adventures in Larryland: Greed preview

undated

Well it’s Pay-Per-View week in WCW this week fans and although the card isn’t stacked and the coward Bagwell hasn’t put his belt on the line it should still be a good show.
Big U.S title match in the Cage, take it from a man who faced Bruno Sammartino inside one, these steel cage matches are brutal. It hard to cheat in a cage match, as I found out and that’s why I expect Hardy to walk away with the U.S title.
Paul Wight been beaten down by The Elite since his return to WCW and at Greed he gets his revenge against HHH, Hunter is undoubtedly WCW’s MVP but I wouldn’t want to have to get into the ring with a seven foot tall five hundred pound Giant who wanted to rip my head off.
Jericho looks set to be the one who finally puts Mike Sanders in his place. These two guys might have big mouths, but make no mistake about it their both highly talented and no matter the outcome it should be a good match.
We’re gonna see the rubber match between Dustin Rhodes and Christian Cage and I think the young, cocky Cage is going to come out the winner. Cage has ruffled a lot of feathers since arriving in WCW and he reminds me of a young Larry Zybysko, a realist who’s not afraid to say what he thinks.
Since their debut two weeks ago The Dudley Boys have taken WCW by storm just like they have everywhere else they’ve been. These two are undoubtedly one of the best tag teams around and Michaels and Cade could be in for a nasty shock if the Dudley’s take it to the extreme.
Joe Hennig gets a shot at the T.V title as he takes on Mr. Slam, anyone who reads Adventures in Larryland will know that I’m a big fan of Mr. Hennig but he will have to put a five star performance to wrestle the title away from the undefeated Mr. Slam.
Finally we got Page and Bagwell taking on The Mamalukes, Page has been impressive in recent weeks going on an unexpected winning streak and he might just be able to carry Bagwell to victory in this match.
