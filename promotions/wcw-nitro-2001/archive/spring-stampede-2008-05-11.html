WCW (Monday Night Nitro, 2001–08) — Card

Spring Stampede: results

11 May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Spring Stampede "The Texas Longhorn" John Layfield -vs- Alex Shelley The bell is rang as we quickly get the match underway! Immediately with the sound of the bell Layfield quickly goes after Shelley while whipping him across the opposite end of the ring. John charges in to the corner with a huge clothesline attempt. But Alex Shelley ducks just in time sprinting away from the corner and coming back just in time to send Layfield down to the mat with a instant bulldog! Shelley quickly makes the cover. One…Two…Thr--! KICK OUT! Layfield barley kicks out of another lose. Both men get back to their feet, Shelley getting up just a second ahead of John. Shelley takes a few good swing at Layfield just before both men nail one another again and again. Backing up for a moment Shelley and Layfield circle one another just before tying up. Some how Shelley barley gets the upper hand hooking Layfield in a version of the side headlock. John Layfield bounces off the ropes shoving Shelley in front of him to the other set of ropes. Shelley bounces off coming back towards Layfield as he connects with a huge dropkick! Alex Shelley immediately runs up to the top turnbuckle before coming down and connecting on Layfield with a very effective moonsault! Shelley goes for cover. One…Two…Three! Shelley’s music begins to blare as he is claimed the winner.
Gerard Gillen -vs- Billy Kidman The match being one of the quickest of the night gets underway as fast as it ended! Once the bell is rang both men tie up with Kidman getting the upper hand nailing Gillen with a perfect belly to belly suplex. Kidman then makes his way up to the top turnbuckle as he nails a huge Shooting star press just before quickly making the cover! One…Two…Three! Kidman’s done it!
Matt Hardy -vs- CM Punk Both men eager to get the match underway make there way out to the ring in a hury. With in just a second after the bell during a few jabs back and forth Hardy catches Punk of guard with a stunning twist of fait followed by the three count and victory!
Rey Mysterio -vs- Michael Shane Once mysterio and Michael shane get the match started, Mysterio begins working on the knee caps of Shane after multiple martial art chops again and again! With a kick to the gut mysterio follows in with a huge swinging neck breaker! Michael Shane’s barley moving what so ever. Rey grabs a hold of the ropes while pulling himself to the top turnbuckle. Once Shane gets to his feet Rey puts an end to it all with a big Missile drop kick! Michael Shane flops back a couple feet just before Rey grabs both legs rolling him up for the quick Three Count!
Bill Goldberg -vs- Randy Taylor Goldberg and Taylor quickly get things going while tying up. Goldberg gets the upperhand whipping Taylor across the ring. Once Taylor comes back towards Goldberg he’s thrown up in the air as Bill Goldberg connects with a huge one man flapjack! Randy Taylor slowly gets up but as he does Goldberg goes for a belly to belly suplex. Just as he does, Taylor nails Goldberg with a couple elbow shots to the side of the head. Taylor then wraps his arm around Goldberg bringing him down face first in a DDT formation! With Goldberg down Randy goes to the top turnbuckle while taunting towards the fans the entire way up. Just as Randy gets all the way up and turns around he’s met by Goldberg! Bill grabs Taylor quickly tossing him back down towards the mat face first. Bill then sits in the corner waiting for Randy to get up. Once Randy Taylor gets up and turns towards Goldberg, he’s nailed with a huge SPEAR! Goldberg immediately makes the cover for the easy victory!
No Disqualification Lance Cade -vs- Shane Douglas As Douglas rolls in to the ring Cade goes on the attack. Lance crashes down with a double axe handle. Lance gets to his feet and starts stomping away. Douglas positions himself in the corner during the stomps. Cade hammers down with right hands in a flurry. He looks out to the crowd and catches his breath. Cade pulls Douglas up and whips him across the ring, but it gets reversed. Douglas rushes in with a big clothesline. Cade is stunned. Douglas snatches a big text book suplex. Douglas grabs a steel chair that's littered in the ring along with many items. He hammers the end of the chair down on Cade's knee. Douglas slams the chair down flat over the knee again. He stomps the leg and turns his attention to Cade's head. The steel comes down fast as it blasts him in the face. Douglas tosses the chair and makes a cover for two. He pulls Cade up and positions him for a piledriver on the chair, but Cade flps out of it. Lance stumbles about, grabbing two trash can lids. Douglas gets to his feet and gets battered by the two lids. Shane drops to his knees from the blow. Cade rushes back to the ropes and comes off with a boot to Douglas's head. Lance goes for the cover, but gets two. Lance pulls Shane up and runs to the ropes for a big lariat, the Franchise ducks and Cade keeps going to the ropes. Douglas turns for a big counter but gets nailed by the lariat the second time around. The referee makes the cover and counts to three!
Meng -vs- the Giant The match begins in a flurry as the Giant climbs inside. Meng strikes with devastating blows to the back and head. The Giant fights back with right hands and chops. He goes to headbutt Meng, but no effect. Wight nods and trades words with the Tongan Monster. He tries the headbutt again, but this time it seems to hurt himself. Meng strikes with a big side kick to the jaw, taking the Giant down. Wight is quick to recover and rolls over. Meng hammers down with forearms to the back. The Giant gets to his feet and Meng tries to whip him to the ropes. Wight reverses it and connects with a big boot on Meng. Meng gets up but is taken down again with a clothesline. He pulls Meng up and slams him down to the mat, following it up with a big jumping elbow drop for a two count. The Giant is suprised and locks on cobra clutch. He pulls Meng up and drops him across his knee, the clutch still in place. The Giant stands back up again with the clutch on, but Meng uses his 300 plus pounds to shove back to the corner. He elbow back to the Giant's head and then pummels him with rights and lefts. Meng nails a few headbutts for good measure and then whips Wight across the ring. Meng rushes in but is grabbed by the throat! The Giant walks out and goes for the chokeslam, but before he can hoist Meng up, a hand shoots out. Meng applies the Tongan Death Grip! The two struggle but after a few seconds the Giant falls to a knee, his grip loosening. Meng screams out and hammers down with a headbutt with the girp still applied. The referee is in position. Wight fights it for a minute, but Meng continues. With the deathgrip applied center ring the Giant slowly goes down to the mat. He struggles to break the hold, but Meng has it locked in like a pitbull. With the Giant's shoulders down the referee counts him out! one, two, three! Zybsko climbs up on the apron and yells at Meng to hold it on. Security hit the ring, and Meng lets go to fight them off. The Giant's face is red. As Meng fights off security for several minutes like a wrecking ball, the Giant gets to his feet, regaining his wits. Meng turns after clearning the ring only to be clotheslined over the top rope! Zybsko has both hardcore and television titles in hand as he pulls Meng along, up the ramp. Meng shouts out in Tongan and points at the Giant as we fade to the back.
Norman Smiley -vs- Brock Lesnar The match starts off quickly with Lesnar nailing a STO on Smiley. Lesnar pulls Norman up and hits a huge belly to belly release suplex. Norman's body shudders on impact. Lesnar pulls Norman up and hits a few big knee lifts before whipping him harshly across the ring. Norman flips out of the corner to the mat. Lesnar waits and measures him as he gets to his feet groggily. Brock rushes in and picks Norman up in a big football tackle before slamming him in the corner. Brock whips Norman across again, smiley nearly out on his feet. Brock rushes in but Norman gets a big boot up! Norman goes for a back suplex, but can't get Brock up. Lesnar turns it into a fireman's carry and connects with the F5 for the pinfall.
Diamond Dallas Page -vs- Christian Cage Cage jumps DDP as soon as he gets in the ring. He hammers down with forearm shots. Cage chokes DDP on the ropes before the ref asks for a break. Cage whips Page in to the ropes and catches him with a big clothesline. Cage pats himself on the chest and taunts the crowd. He drops a knee across DDP's neck and makes a cover for two. Cage locks in a sleeper hold on the mat. The ref keeps asking Page if he gives, but DDP hangs in there. After a few moments Page starts to fight up to his feet. He wrecks out a few elbows to Cage's gut and rushes to the ropes, but Christian gets a knee up. Page flips over to the mat. Christian starts to climb up the top turnbuckle and measures Page for a frogsplash. Cage nails it but only gets a near fall count. He motions that this is over ande pulls Page up for the Unprettier. Cage slowly twists it around, but before he can connect Page breaks free, spins him around and DIAMOND CUTTER! Page throws an arm over Christian, 1...2...3!
Sting -vs- Triple H The bell rings and both men stare down across the ring. They slowly cirlce one another and lock up. Outside of the ring the Exterminator stands with baseball bat in hand. Sting and HHH lock up, both men struggle for position. Sting slaps on a headlock and the Game pushes him back to the ropes. He shoots Sting off face, but he comes back with a big shoulder tackle, taking the Game down to a surpise. Hunter looks up wide eyed as Sting flexes with a yell. They lock up again, this time Hunter grabbing a headlock. Sting shoves him to the ropes and shoots him off. As the Game comes running off though with lightning speed, Sting military presses him over head and slams him down to the mat! The crowd roars with cheers as the Game arches his back in pain. Hunter rolls out of the ring, but turns to meet the Exterminator, falling back on his ass. The Game slides back in to the ring only for Sting to whip him back to the ropes. The Franchise connects with a big back body drop. Hunter staggers up to his feet and is met with a big atomic drop. Sting hits the ropes and bulldogs Hunter down for a quick two count. The Game fights up to his feet and backs up to the corner. Sting whips him across the ring and rushes in to end it all with a Stinger Splash. Hunter sees this and moves at the last moment, but Sting catches himself. The Game, with back turned, thinks he's outsmarted the Stinger. He turns to be met with right hands of punches and back fists. Sting knocks him back to the other corner and hip tosses him out. Sting rushes in for a clothesline, but the Game ducks and connects with a neckbreaker. Hunter is quick to get up and pulls Sting to his feet. The Game fires out a big right hand. Sting stumbles back and Hunter kicks him in the gut. He grabs Sting by the hair and hammers in with right hands to the forehead. Triple H pulls him out with a big suplex. Hunter makes a cover for one. He locks a sleeper on the Stinger, but the Franchise fights to his feet after a few moments. He repositions himself and shoves Hunter off to the ropes. Sting ducks his head down but Hunter catches him with a H drop to a knee. Sting bounces up and is met with a clothesline. Hunter pulls him up and whips him to the ropes, catching the sleeper again. After a few moments Sting whips it around into a back suplex. Both men are down. The referee issues a count and both men start to get to their feet around 8. Hunter kicks Sting in the gut and goes for the Pedigree, but Sting yanks his legs out and goes for the deathlock. Hunter wriggles and struggles to the ropes before it can be locked in place. The referee calls for a break. Sting backs up, letting the Game get to his feet, but rushes in with a big cltohesline over the top rope. Sting follows him out and hammers away with right hands on the arena floor. The fans are going wild. Hunter kicks Sting in the gut and slams him in to the steel post. He pulls Sting around in to a DDT on the arena floor. Hunter rolls back in to the ring. The referee continues his count, but Sting pulls himself back in after nine. Hunter pulls Sting up and hooks him in for the Pedigree, he connects and makes the cover, but Sting kicks out! Hunter can't believe it, he pulls Sting back to his feet for a second one, but Sting rolls him up in a small package before he can lock it in place. Hunter kicks out at 2. As the two brawl in the ring the Elite hit the scene. The Exterminator fights off Cade and Roode with the baseball bat. Shawn Michaels slides in under the ropes through the crowd. The scene happens like so many times before. With the referee distracted outside of the ring, Michaels goes to cltohesline the Stinger, but misses. Sting grabs him by the back of the hair and drops him in a deathdrop! The Game is on top of the Stinger now, he hammers down with blows and pulls him in for the Pedigree, connecting! Hunter takes a breather, and looks around. He gets to his feet and yanks the referee inside the ring. They go towards the Stinger, but as Hunter reaches down, Sting rolls him into a small package! the referee counts...one , two, three! The Game sits up aftwards with a shocked look on his face.
Roode and Cade have subdued the Exterminator outside of the ring. Michaels rolls back inside and along with Hunter batter Sting on the mat. The Game Pedigrees Sting a third time on the mat as the rest of the Elite join him in the ring. The four of them stand triumphant in beating down Sting and Roode boots Sting out of the ring. They all raise hands with Hunter as the crowd boos. Roode stomps on the Exterminators head as he tries to climb inside, knocking him back down. Michaels and Cade have the Game's arms still raised. Michaels lets go and steps towards the ropes. Cade yanks Hunter around and nails a big right hand, as Michaels follows it up with a super kick to the stunned Game! The crowd is silent. Cade rolls out and grabs a steel chair as Michaels slaps Hunter around on the mat while yelling at him. Roode turns and is shocked by whats going on, yet he doesnt know what to do. Cade places the Game's arm inside of the chair. Shawn and Lance stomp away on the chair as he batters the Game's arm. Hunter kicks around. Cade holds him in place as Shawn goes up to the middle rope and comes down with a stomp on the chair. Roode climbs out of the ring and walks up the ramp alone as the ring is littered with cups and things. Michaels and Cade continue to batter Hunter until Sting and the Exterminator make a recoup. They climb back in the ring and spin the two around and nail them with right hands. Both get knocked out of the ring as security and medics hit the scene.
-United States Title- Robert Roode -vs- Dustin Rhodes The two lock up quickly, jocking for position. Roode backs Rhodes up to the ropes. The referee calls for the break and Roode gives it with a big shove. Rhodes responds with a slap to the face. Roode returns the favor, but Dustin hits a second slap that nearly knocks Roode out. Roode stumbles back and Dustin grabs a headlock. Roode shoves him to the ropes, but Dustin hangs on. Rhodes wrenches it in place for awhile until Roode hits a back suplex. Roode gets to his feet and stomps Dustin before dropping a big forearm shot to the head. Roode chokes Dustin against the ropes before the ref calls for a break. He pulls him up and chops him hard. Dustin fights back with big right hands. The two start to brawl and drop down to the mat. Both men roll out of the ring. They brawl, rolling back and forth on the arena floor. Roode gets to his feet while on top and starts kicking at Dustin. Rhodes gets to his feet and both men start to struggle again as the referee counts both out.
-World Heavyweight Title- Shawn Michaels -vs- Sonny Siaki -vs- Mark Jindrak The match quickly gets underway as we start off the match everyone has been waiting for all night! With the sound of the bell Sonny Siaki and Shawn Michaels charge at one another with Sonny getting the Champion getting the upper hand. Sonny spins Michaels around in a modified arm bar. He then sends Michaels in to the corner with a few vicious chops again and again! Jindrak just stands against the corner post laughing as Michaels and Siaki tear one another up. After a couple more blows to the chest Siaki takes Michaels and attempts to whip him across the ring. Shawn reverses is sending Siaki chest first in to the turnbuckles. He bounces back in pain as he basically falls in Michaels arms and down in to a back body drop. Michaels goes for the cover! One…Two! Jindrak finally gets in on the action while stomping away on the back of Shawn Michaels. Michaels and Siaki slowly both get to their feet while having to fight off a vicious Mark Jindrak whose nailing any and every blow human possible. Once both men are to their feet Jindrak backs up bouncing off the ropes and charging towards both Michaels and Siaki in a double clothesline attempt. But its blocked as both men duck, Jindrak bounces off the opposite ropes coming back and being dominated with a huge Double Flap jack! Jindrak gets back up to his feet as soon as possible. Shawn and Sonny grab Mark whipping him across the ring, Jindrak comes back and this time nailing a huge flying Double clothesline! Jindrak immediately covers Michaels but barly reaches a one count. All three get to their feet again. Shawn nails Sonny with a Hard right followed by one to Jindrak, Another to Sonny, and then even another to Jindrak sending both men falling to the mat. Not keeping them down long though. Jindrak and sonny quickly get back up to their feet. Both Jindrak and Siaki charge after Michaels nailing multiple blows one after another! They continue on and on until the Referee is forced to break it up as Michaels is down in the corner. Jindrak and Sonny are both forced back for a moment but once the Ref gets out of the way Sonny sprints towards Michaels nailing with a well done baseball slide to the midsection! Just as Sonny gets up Jindrak nails a great standing drop kick sending Siaki face first in to the top turnbuckle! Pretty dazed Sonny spins around right in to a spinning neck breaker. Michaels now is beginning to make his way back up to his feet while pulling himself with the ropes. Siaki then gets up about the same thing but just as Sonny turns around he’s nailed with a quick Super Kick out of no where thanks to Shawn Michaels! Michaels barly able to hold himself up is then caugt off guard being clotheslined against the ropes by jindrak. Mark pulls Michaels off of the turnbuckle in time to nail a knee to the gut followed by a spinning DDT! Now with Sonny Siaki still out cold and Michaels barely moving around Jindrak rushes to the top turnbuckle waiting for either man to get back up to their feet. Michaels gets up first and as Shawn turns around he’s nailed with a huge Flying Clothesline! Jindrak lands it perfectly while holding on as the referee starts the count. One…Two…Three!! The Ref hesitates as the last second just as Michaels gets his foot on the bottom rope. Also, Sonny Siaki grabs a hold of Jindrak pulling him off of Michaels at the last minute but is obviously too late! The Referee confused on what to do leaves the ring grabing the World title just before speaking with a few more WCW Officials for a moment. He then comes back in the ring as all three of Jindrak, Michaels, and Sonny wait out to hear the decision. The referee immediately claims Mark Jindrak the winner and the NEW World Heavyweight Champion! Just as he’s given the World title Jindrak grabs his World title dropping down to his knees hugging it against he chest as the PPV fades away!
