Jim Ross: Superstar of the Week

undated
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings from Oklahoma, and from our black hat wearing friend of World Championship Wrestling! I write this as I prepare packing for the next Monday Night Nitro. I curiously wonder if on my flight I can be allowed some classic Barbeque.. Judging from my other countless ones, probably not.
Monday Night Nitro this week had a hard act to follow, falling behind in fallout from the very memorable night at Greed; where one of the greater teams the wrestling world's seen like them or not reclaimed the World Tag Titles, defended the United States title, and defeated Paul Wight. Nonetheless, Nitro tried it's best last week, and I for one say I was heavily satisfied with the end result. Our World Heavyweight Champion was forced to put his belt in jeopardy against five opponents! I'm sure Buff was about as happy as Triple H was seeing Rey Mysterio return. (a topic later on)
After Mark Jindrak came to the ring claiming Buff was a non-defending champion, and forced the match, I assume Buff shut the 'Commish' or whatever Jindrak is now up. However seeing him beat five guys makes me think that Mark will soon have the deck stacked even higher for Bagwell, giving him a Bowling Shoe Ugly match-up. Some of our WCW fans think even Bagwell's a transitional champion, and all that, and if they're right we may see a new World Champion very soon, I know it will take more than luck to get out of this problem! But after seeing Bagwell take out five guys, I think whoever steps up to the plate will give us a great match-up.
Something interesting was said however by Bagwell last week: "You were going to name yourself.." Personally I was expecting the same thing, and if it happened the nights could be even more interesting coming up to Spring Stampede. As we all know, Mark Jindrak considers himself a wrestler first, and an "authority" figure second. So seeing him beat Buff Bagwell would definately change the entire programming of Monday Night Nitro.
Well anyway, on with the proceedings you're here for.. So drumroll please.. This week's Superstar of the Week is none other than our longest reigning champion we've had as of late in WCW: Robert Roode. Rob is arguably one of the most popular, most talented individual we have seen lately in World Championship Wrestling, and it feels like to me he can have more Gold on his waist at any time. Robert is such a talented individual in the ring, and on the microphone, that as a fan, he makes you want to hate him, and his in-ring style is different, and unique to Rob. Also to mention his very prestigious United States Championship he holds. I have no doubt he will continue to be a force in WCW, as will the group he's in known as the Elite. So congratulations Rob, you deserved this week's title as Superstar of the Week, no doubt will you have heated competition coming for the title of SOTW next week.
Well, speaking of the Elite, last week they went head to head with the new claimed "Resistance" on Nitro. It was a nice match, which I was shocked to say the least to see Joe Henning, a very talented individual in his own right, pin the Shawn Michaels trained, Lance Cade. However after the match, Roode done the unthinkable and knocked Rhodes out! It was a great match for Nitro. So in the end I guess it really does "Pay to be Roode."
Also, Christian Cage a man I think has a great future in World Championship Wrestling, defeated the debuting Superstar of the Week, Chris Jericho. The two put on a classic match-up, and I was highly excited at the perfect blend of big moves, and chain-wrestling. However the end of the night, Christian had his hand held high, as he beat Jericho with leverage. Even seeing the shame, the record books still shows Christian Cage defeating Chris Jericho, and I feel that is absolutely a good notch on Christian's wall as he climbs the ladder to a title belt. There's no doubt in my mind Christian soon will have WCW gold, and I wouldn't doubt it if it happens sooner than later.
Finally for the record this week: I'm very excited to see two new fresh, young Champions in WCW in: the young relative of the late Curt Henning, Joe Henning, and a good friend of mine, Val Venis. Congratulations to both of you guys! Also before I forget; on behalf of World Championship Wrestling, and the fans, I would like to wish Michael Sanders the best in his endeavers apart from WCW. Whatever you want to do Mike, we wish you the best.
Continue to count your multitude of blessings, and you have my thanks for checking out the Superstar of the Week! I also like to thank your support on Nitro, and hopefully we can get a bigger show heading soon.
Boomer Sooner, Jim Ross
