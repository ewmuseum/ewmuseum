Hudson Ramblings: the top five superstars

c. May 2008
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello sports fans! This week is the very first week you the fans get to see more than just the interview side of yours truly, Scott Hudson! There has been a lot going on around WCW lately. For this week starting off I’m going to break down the top five superstars in my opinion currently in the ranks as of late.
So let’s get down to business with number five! A pretty obvious choice would have to be a man that’s recently returned to action and with in two weeks now is walking around with not one but two championship titles. Number five is none other than Meng. Even if Mengs one of the most ‘unique’ men to ever walk around these locker rooms he has proven himself in just two weeks. On Nitro just a week ago he defeated Christian Cage to become the Hardcore Champion. Then just this past Sunday night thanks to Wight putting his title on the line late in the night, he wound up walking away with the Television championship as well! So with out any question if he keeps this up its only a matter of time before he’s possibly fighting for the World championship as well. I guess time will have to tell though, huh?
Then sliding in fourth we’ve got a member of The Elite, Robert Roode. While his status in The Elite is a bit questionable his status as the United States champion isn’t. This past Sunday he defended his title against Dustin Rhodes which both men put on one heck of a match. Rob was able to leave the champion and defiantly is one man to watch out for from here on out.
Sting the man who no one can seem to actually get rid of, he comes and goes as he pleases. Now recently he’s proven himself once again which brings in as the number three spot. Spring Stampede he defeated Triple H which just may had ended some problems between both men. Especially after the attack Shawn Michaels caused directly after the match. Whether you like him or not Sting is always going to be around.
Which that now brings us to the man chose for the second spot. That man is none other than Shawn Michaels! Michaels has tried to push himself back up to the world title for a long time now. Shawn might not be making the best decisions lately according to some people after turning on long time partner Triple H but anymore it seems like all he cares about it the World title. He got that shot this past Sunday though when he faced off against not only Mark Jindrak but the champion Sonny Saiki as well. There was a point through out the match where it seemed as if Michaels had it won but neither Sonny or Michaels were able to over come the younger Mark Jindrak who obviously had everything going for him at least for that night. Michaels isn’t going to be giving up anytime soon and that may not be so good for my superstar picked for number one.
Now, there’s really no one else around that could deny the fact that this next man has finally done just about everything there is to do in WCW. Coming in at number one is the New World Champion, Mark Jindrak! Jindrak has been known as of late as the Executive Assistant but now he’s the World Champion. Which the world title is the one thing that has seemed to hold him back for far too long now. He can honestly say that he’s done it now. Some may not agree to it but like it or not he is the man currently representing World Championship Wrestling as the World Champion!
Well, folks thanks for listening to some ‘Hudson Ramblings’! Stay tuned because next week I come to you from Greenville, South Carolina as we take a look at a few up and coming superstars along with some very important current events!
