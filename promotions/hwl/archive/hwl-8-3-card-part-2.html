HWL 8/3 Card, Part 2

3 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HWL Page 2 Tom: Titles on the line tonight as 2/3 of the 6 man tag team title holders get their chance at 2 titles tonight, Coffee Cake takes on the House of Style. Match #5 For the HWL World Tag Team Titles House of Style {HWL World Tag Team Champions} vs. Coffee Cake 2/3 of the HWL 6 Man tag team Champions It was Greasecake and Armani starting off, Armani with a couple of leg sweeps and Greasecake went for his cakewalk right away with Armani sliding out of the ring to avoid it. Armani takes time to talk things over with Stud Lee then back into the ring he comes. Greasecake takes him down with an arm bar then a head scissors as Armani slips out of it and tags in Valentino. Romeo nails Greasecake with a clothesline, then it's a doubleteam effort in the corner as Stud Lee disrupts the ref. Greasecake is getting choked. Stud Lee throws Armani his cane and he nails Greasecake with it just in time for the ref to turn and see the whole thing as he calls for the bell. The winners by disqualification are Coffee. However, the House of Style hold on to their belts. Tom: Our next title match is for the TV Tag Titles as the Siberian Express wanted Tos and Coach in the ring and they got them but for their titles. Johnny: Anything can happen with Tos and Coach in a match and it probably will here too. Match #6 For the HWL TV Tag Team Titles The Siberian Express {HWL TV Tag Team Champions} w/Alexandr G. vs. Tos & Coach w/Ms. Wheat Of course you know who was busy writing people up before the match started and he even wrote Woody up at one point for not selling him Tos ready made Boogers. Coach started with Ivan Drago. It was Miss Wheat causing a public nuisance with her bickering and yelling at Bones Chirechella. Tos had a bottle of vodka or at least a bottle with something in it and sprayed the fans with it. Then laughed in their faces. Alexander looked like he wanted some of it. Drago tossed Coach out of the ring as Bolotov dropped from the top and slammed a chair into Coach on the outside prompting Ted O'Shea to come over and he tossed Bolotov into the timekeepers table smashing it to pieces. It turned into a Donnybrook as all four wrestlers were on the floor battling and Miss Wheat came up behind Drago with the bottle of Vodka and smashed it over his head knocking him to the floor and cutting him. Blood and vodka were flowing from the head of Drago as Alexander G. tossed Miss Wheat into the ring and went after her. Just then Cynthia Turner came from the back and nailed Alexander G. with her Women's title belt and dragged him out of the arena by the hair. This left only Andrei Bolotov left against Coach and Tos. Coach set up Bolotov on the top rope and superplexed him from the top rope and Tos came up from behind him with the Weegie. Bones Chirechella was trying to get both Miss Wheat and Coach out of the ring when Tos squirted ink in the eye of Bolotov and Tos covered him for the 1-2-3. Winners of the match and New HWL TV Tag Team Champions, Coach & Tos. Time of the Cheat...I mean match 5:23. Tom: Unbelievable Johnny that Tos & Coach got away with so much here that.....wait a minute it's Ted O'Shea and.... Tos: Well, well everyone. Guess what? I'm back. It's Tos on TV once again as he is 1/2 of the New TV Tag Team Champions, and guess what show I'll be starring in this week? Miss Wheat can you guess? Miss Wheat: I'll say Coach. Tos: Right, how'd you know. {drops the microphone} Johnny: Tom will get his headset back on in a minute or two as we have new TV Tag Champions and next up is or World Title rematch from last weeks Pay Per View. Tom: I'm back now, and I'd rather just be written up. It's T Rex Ron and Jack the Ripper. lets go to Ziggy. Ziggy: Ladies and Gentlemen, this is ........... Ziggy is interrupted as the Pain Squad come running down to ringside. Agony: Hang on Twiggy. Anguish and I have been waiting all night and we can't wait any longer. Hey Dog, No Life. Get your butts down here. Lets go right now. **Whole Life and Log run to the ring but Coach and Tos who were still on their way out attack them with their belts. Whole Life and Tos are going at it as Coach and Log are as well. Wait a minute here comes the Pain Squad. It's a riot in the middle of the aisle. 6 wrestlers going at it. Here comes Stapleton and Allan, here comes the Missile. Rebecca's there as well, the Delmarva Destroyer, here comes Goldylocks and Tie Albert. This match isn't going to happen tonight. It is in the middle of the Garden and it's a war out there. The PWF is heading into it. *** Woody: I'm kind of in the middle of things here and it seems it's Log and Whole Life for the most part who got the worst of it, mostly at the hands of Coach and Tos who have left the Garden and they are back in the dressing room area. One good thing is that both Jack the Ripper and T Rex Ron decided to stay where they were and not come out to help as they were next and wanted to save everything for the title match. Tom: Security and HWL officials are clearing the aisle and the ring area as we go back to Ziggy. Ziggy: This is our main event of the evening and it is for the HWL World Title. Coming down the aisle is the Challenger. A former HWL World Champion, a former HWL North American Champion and 2 time and current HWL Jr. Champion, part of Broken Bones Inc., Jack the Ripper. His opponent, is former HWL Jr. Champion, former HWL TV Champion and for the 2nd time the HWL World Champion, T Rex Ron. The Main Event For the HWL World Title T Rex Ron {HWL World Champion} vs. Jack the Ripper {HWL Jr. Champion} Alot of brawling right off the bat as they start off punching back and forth. The battle hits the outside quickly and it's Jack who takes the TV Camera and smashes it on the back of Ron who grabs the cord and trips up Jack then picks it up and wraps it around his neck. Jack pushes Ron away and grabs a soft drink from an old lady and tossed it in Ron's face. PepsiMaster would have been proud. The lady in turn throws here popcorn in Jack's face. Ron slams Jack over the Mexican announcers table and if you speak Spanish I suggest a translator because the Spanish broadcast is over. Jack finally gets up and both men get back into the ring. Some high risk moves by Ron doesn't pay off so he finally goes for the Heart Punch, here it comes, blocked by the Ripper, Jack executes the Ripper. Cover 1-2-3. Winner and new World Heavyweight Champion, Jack the Ripper. Tom: thanks for tuning in, we have new HWL TV Tag Champs as well as a new World Champion for Johnny Lightning, I'm Tom Trojan, goodnight everyone.
