(DEBATE) Role of Fedheads

28 February 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Please allow me to explain what I think the role of a "fed head" should be. I am the person running the HWL, which is a fourteen month old e-fed. I think everyone needs to be treated as an adult. We explain the rules to everyone up front in the FAQ. It's not my job, nor is it my responsibility, to babysit, to censor or to make all the decisions. I'm way too busy, and I have to assume that everyone else in my league has a life, too. It's my job to see that cards are getting out and that matches are being scheduled. It's my job to be the lead, but not the only, recruiter. It's my job to help those doing cards and roleplays by helping make them better. It's my job to remind people if they're stepping over the line. It is NOT my job to censor the mail. We trust everyone, and we insist that they send their own postings at their own leisure. We run a simulated league, but the roleplayers get better matches, which helps them in the title hunt. It's my job to make sure that all the "players" -- the real people behind the wrestlers -- have a chance to compete for belts if they want. This isn't rocket science; neither is it something that's all consuming. That's why the HWL is so much fun. We all take it with the proper grain of salt. A good fed-head does that. "The Emperor" HWL
