HWL 8/3 Card, Part 1

3 August 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
HWL Page 1 *****************************screen opens to fireworks***************************************** Tom Trojan: Welcome everyone to Madison Square Garden in New York as the HWL is live. I'm Tom Trojan along with Johnny Lightning. Tonight we have three title defenses as Jack the Ripper takes on T Rex Ron in a rematch of last weeks title match in which Ron regained the HWL World Title. Johnny: Yes Tom but we have news that the original 6 man tag match between El Hombre, Coffee Cake against the Prime Beneficiaries and Mark the Missile Vanderveyen has been canceled because the Emperor has sent some of his execs in and tonight at the Garden it will be the Prime Beneficiaries against the Pain Squad one on one. Now next week on the TV card it will be the same match but in a steel cage but tonight regular rules apply or don't apply. Tom: Wow, what happens with the other guys? Johnny: Mark will be in action tonight and also it will be Coffee Cake up against the House of Style for the World Tag Titles. Tom: With that lets get to the action and our opening bout. Match #1 Oogie Boogie Man vs. PepsiMaster HWL debut for the Oogie Boogie Man as Tough Guy came down to the ring with his tag partner and get this they are called, 2 tough to boogie. PepsiMaster came down with the Ninja Warrior and together they passed out snacks and Pepsi's to the fans. Once it got started it was PepsiMaster with a great series of arm drags and back drops. However, Oogie Boogie Man got some help from Tough Guy and it shifted in Boogie's favor as he took PepsiMaster to the top rope but Ninja Warrior came over and swung his Nunchucks but they missed and hit PepsiMaster instead and Oogie Boogie Man dropped onto PepsiMaster all 374 pounds of him with an Avalanche and they had to peel PepsiMaster from the mat as the ref covered and counted 1-2-3. Winner in 9:12 in his debut Oogie Boogie Man. Tom: Oogie Boogie Man wins in his debut and I understand Woody is standing by with the Boogie Man. Woody: I'm here with the two new men in the HWL, Tough Guy and his partner the Oogie Boogie Man. Oogie Boogie Man: I be Oogie Boogie Man and I will win all match's in wrestling federation. I be best with my partner, Tough Guy. Tough Guy: I'm Rough, I'm Tough, and I'm about the best partner in the HWL, I'm the Tough Guy. Woody: These guys don't say much Tom, at least that makes sense but neither does Tos, back to you. Johnny: Don't say much they are mourons. Tom: Large Lori is leading the way for Bodacious Brenda, lets go to ringside. Match #2 Dolly vs. Bodacious Brenda w\Large Lori As Lori came down to ringside the first row of fans hung on to their kids and food but as the match started it was all right to let go. Dolly showed her ring skills early with Brenda as Brenda ended up on the outside near Lori so Dolly went out after her and a big mistake it was as Lori sat right on Dolly causing of course rib injuries as she bounced up and down. Brenda tossed Dolly back in the ring and covered her easily for the win at 1:25 into the match. Johnny: There's a girl for you Tom, Lori she'd love you to death. Tom: Or squash me to death. Before we went on the air we'd like to give you the updates on the match's that were held prior to airtime. It was Goldylocks with an impressive win over Nuclear Plant as Goldylocks went to the DDT and covered Plant in about 5 minutes. Also in action before our live telecast were Stunning Stephanie and Christina Marie. It turned out once again Large Lori who sat on Christina Marie causing injured ribs and an easy win for Stephanie. Got you all caught up and lets go to the ring and our next bout. Match #3 Mark the Missile Vanderveyen w\CJ Sinclair vs. Hogman Hogman back from a long absence and it showed as Mark took the Hog to mudville with a sleeper hold and it was nighty night Hogman after 4:12. Match #4 Tiger vs. The Bomb The Bomb came exploding into the ring long enough for Tiger to catch him by surprise with his 2 partners Smokey and Rambo who were busy slamming The Bomb into the ring steps. It was Tiger then with the Hollywood & Vine to close it out and give him the win. Go to Page 2
