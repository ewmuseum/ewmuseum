Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Absolute Pro Wrestling announced itself to rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy on 5 November 2001 as, in its own words, “a brand new e-fed” on “a massive recruiting drive,” its organizer describing a search across the internet for talent to carry out what the post called the federation’s main objective: “Quality!”

It promised, alongside its shows and pay-per-views, a dedicated staff and a Real Audio program.

No roster, card, result or closure notice survives beyond that single recruitment post.
