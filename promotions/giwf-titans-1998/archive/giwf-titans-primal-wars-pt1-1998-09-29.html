Primal Wars, part 1

29 September 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The screen fades into the Richmond Coliseum in Richmond Virginia. Over 20,000 thousand people have packed into the arena to watch what could be the greatest show the GIWF has ever put on. Tons of big names are packed into the show, all in key matches. Every single title will be on the line tonight, and they show officially gets going with the lights going down in the arena. Hundreds of fans begin to hold up their lighters, and the huge television screen near the top of the entrance way begins to play a tape. A huge T-Rex is shown on the screen, devouring a animal that looks like a cow. The dinosaur suddenly morphs into Scorpion, the GIWF World Champion. The words "PRIMAL" appear on the screen, dripping blood. The screen suddenly "breaks"as we see the lineman on a football field. The quarterback says "Go!" and the massive men starting pushing and shoving and throwing each other down. The scene morphs into Revelations, and the words "WARS" appear in bloody letters. Fireworks begin to ignite all over the arena, flames ignite from the ring post in colors, one side green, another yellow, one blue and the last being red. The fireworks end, and the lights come back on as the fans are screaming at the tope of their lungs. The signs are back! "Big Mac may have 68, but I've got 70!", "CAWF Blows!", "My mom is in the hospital, but I'm here!", "Hey dad, I'm not in my room!", "Revolution 1 PoE 0", and "It's about damn time we had a LIVE card!" As the instrumental version of "Space Lord" by Monster Magnet is playing in the background, the camera heads over to the broadcast position, where Brad McGovern and the Enforcer are dressed to style and profile..) McGovern: WELCOME EVERYONE TO PRIMAL WARS! Enforcer: We're ready to bring to you almost 3 hours of the best wrestling found anywhere in the world! Not only will every single title holder by fighting tonight, but we've got some other matches guaranteed to light the fire underneath your ass. McGovern: We're going to be seeing the Jim "Crusher" Johnson's home federation, the CAWF have all four of it's champions here tonight. The 3-time World Champion Scorpion will be taking on former World and National Champion Revelations. Former two-time World Champion and current National Champion Dr. D. Capitator will be taking on former Costal Champion Flawless. Enforcer: Flawless dropped the Costal Title last week just to get a shot at the National Title last night. McGovern: Maybe so, the gifted newcomer from the NMIWF and the CAWF, Equalizer, will have his first test tonight against the "People's Champ", Grifter. Enforcer: That's a cage match! As you can see, the event staff is just finishing putting the steel around the ring. McGovern: The MWO is here tonight, as Fear Itself will be taking on the tag-team champion, the Trio. Enforcer: I still don't understand that name, a tag-team can only consist of two guys, not three. McGovern: The cage looks ready to go, so let's give it to HardCastle to start the show. (Camera goes over to the ring, where HardCastle is standing. He's got the mic, and is ready to go..) HardCastle: VIRGINIA, are you ready? (Crowd asks, "For What?") HardCastle: Oh, if you don't know then why are you here? It's time for (Crowd begins to yell out the letters as HardCastle is spelling them..) P-R-I-M-A-L W-A-R-S! (Crowd Pops..) HardCastle: You all know what's coming up next.....LEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEET'SSSSSS DOOOOOO THHHHHHISSSSSSSS!! (Crowd Pops as HardCastle goes right into the introductions..) HardCastle: The first match tonight on the PPV will be a cage match! (Highway to Hell by AC/DC begins to play..) Coming to the ring first, accompanied down by BlackStar, he is known as the People's Champion. The man with the move of destruction, he is the crafty veteran here in the GIWF. He is the GRIFTER!! (Met with a mixture of boos and cheers from the crowd..) McGovern: Grifter brings out his tag-team partner BlackStar with him, I don't think he will have a large effect because this is a cage match. Enforcer: No kidding, cage matches are suppose to keep men out. HardCastle: And his opponent, he's been a champion in the original NMIWF, currently looking to become the CAWF Television champion in a few weeks, he is the master of the Equality Bomb, and the Equality kick. This will be his first match in the GIWF, and he's told the world that he is the master of the cage, he is EQUALIZER!! Enforcer: So this is the punk the Crusher brought with him? McGovern: Actually, I think Equalizer and the Crusher are not united. They both decided to come to the GIWF because of the quest for gold. Enforcer: You can say what you want McGovern, but I used to wrestle schmoes like that. I know what goes through their heads. McGovern: Well, the cage door has been shut and locked and Primal Wars is officially underway. Grifter charges at Equalizer and baseball slides underneath him. He gets up and connects with a right hand. Enforcer: It's going to take more than that, Equalizer is a big man. McGovern: Equalizer begins to pound back, and we have a slugfest happening here folks. Neither man backing down, Grifter gets Equalizer to the ropes and throws to the other side. Coming off and high knee by Grifter takes Equalizer down. Enforcer: Nice move, keep it up son! McGovern: Grifter picks up Equalizer and body slams him. He runs the ropes and drops a leg across the throat. He goes up behind Equalizer and puts him in a reverse chin lock. Enforcer: I don't think this is going to work, Equalizer is a big boy. McGovern: After about 10 seconds, Equalizer is trying to get up......he does and he grabs Grifter who is still on his back, and flips him over his head. Equalizer grabs Grifter and picks him up. Enforcer: OH MY! McGovern: Equalizer has Grifter over his head and is walking around the ring with him. LOOK OUT! Enforcer: He threw him face first into the cage! That hurt. McGovern: Equalizer goes to the other end and begins to climb, he gets to about the halfway point and the Grifter begins to get up. He runs over to Equalizer and pulls him down. Enforcer: DAMN! McGovern: Equalizer's legs landed right between the ropes and he's in some pain. Grifter throws him off the ropes then climbs the second turnbuckle and lands a double-axehandle and Equalizer is getting back up. He goes over to him and ... Enforcer: ....desperation move by Equalizer by sticking the thumb in Grifter's eyes. Wussy move if you ask me, but it gives Equalizer the advantage. Grifter is having trouble seeing, and Equalizer swings around the Grifter and nails him with a back-body drop. McGovern: Equalizer picks up Grifter again and puts him on his back. SAMOAN DROP! Grifter is laid out in the ring, and Equalizer goes up to the second turnbuckles, splash! Enforcer: Equalizer going to try and escape! McGovern: Equalizer goes over to the cage and begins to climb, he's halfway there as Grifter begins to move. He's 3/4 of the way to the top, and Grifter begins to climb after him! Enforcer: Oh hell yeah! McGovern: Equalizer is kicking Grifter, but Grifter grabs his leg and pulls him down! Enforcer: Go Grifter! McGovern: Equalizer is stunned, he gets up and jumps but misses Grifter's leg. He climbs after Grifter and their both at the top. Enforcer: It's another brawl, this time the winner will win not only with the battle, but the war! McGovern: Grifter with a right....left...right, no it's blocked! Equalizer throws some major hay makers, left....right...left, Grifter is wabbling.. Enforcer: Look out below! McGovern: Equalizer just clothslined Grifter right over the top of the cage, and the Grifter fell 15 feet to the ground and to the victory! (Bell tolls..) HardCastle: Your winner at 18:42, GRIFTER! McGovern: What a bad break for Equalizer, he's mad and is climbing down the cage to get to Grifter. Enforcer: What a second, out comes that CAWF punk, Jim Johnson. McGovern: HE'S BEATING ON THE KNOCKED OUT GRIFTER! Enforcer: Johnson starts to stomp on Grifter, while Equalizer takes care of Blackstar. The CAWF stars are taking out Grifter and Blackstar. McGovern: Here comes John Beverly! Beverly splashes Johnson into the cage, and Equalizer decides to take the high road. Blackstar gets over to Grifter to see if he's ok, and Beverly is helping him out too. Beverly took a major fall two weeks ago on Last Bout. A little higher than Grifter's fall. Enforcer: He knows what it feels like. Equalizer went chicken, so did Crusher. They had to attack from behind those morons. (c)GIWF 1998 http://giwf.tsx.org/
