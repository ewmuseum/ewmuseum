Da Monkey: Ladies and gents, we're BACK! Whatta a match put on for the tag team championship! That was indeed amazing! There were bodies everywhere, blood, chairs, bats...just how I like it! Poppy: It was action packed..but I must say I'd rather watch a fair, WRESTLING match. Not a bloody brawl. Da Monkey: Cmon! Have some fun! [Poppy smacks Da Monkey in the back of the head] Da Monkey: HEY! Poppy: We've just been told that President Foster has given Generation X-Force their belts in the back. Good deal, now we don't have to break up another brawl! Da Monkey: Think AGAIN! We've got a 20 man battle roya for not one but TWO titles, and you think there isn't going to be a brawl! HA! [in ring] HardCastle: LADIES AND GENTS, THIS MATCH IS FOR THE GIWF NATIONAL AND WOOOORLD TITLES!! [crowd pops] HardCastle: This is a twenty man battle royal. The last man eliminated will be crowned GIWF National Champion, and the last man remaining in the ring will be named GIWF WORLD CHAMPION! [crowd goes nuts] HardCaste: Lets introduce tonights participants! ["Victory" by Puff Daddy plays and the participants start walking out.] HardCastle: Edgar "2 Real" Malone...Talon...Johnny X....Jason "Hitman" Budden*crowd pop*...Flawless...Maximus....Dr. D. Capitator*crowd pops* ...John Beverly*crowd pops*...Tony Flint*boos*...Rick Groove...Shawn Body....Big Pappa Puff....Cobra D...Titan....Savior....FragMaster.....Charles Eden.....Harold Eden.....Sigma...."the Hotshot" Matt James! And thats all, ...LETS DO DIS!!! [bell tolls] Da Monkey: AND WE'VE GOT AN ALL OUT BRAWL ALL READY! Maximus and Flawless are already slugging it out with Johnny X and Talon...whoa! Out goes Malone! HardCastle: Edgar "2 Real" Malone eliminated by Titan @ 1:01! Poppy: You heard it! Jason Budden is fighting with the Edens... and he's winning! He's giving them each a punch at a time! Budden just got NAILED by a double clothesline! Budden is now getting a mudhole stopped in him! Da Monkey: FragMaster is getting a new ASSHOLE stomped in him! the MWO is all over him! Whoa! Maximus just flung Matt James over the top and into the CROWD! HE TOOK OUT THE FIRST TWO ROWS!!!!!! HardCastle: Get some medics! Matt James eliminated by Tony Flint @ 4: 32! Poppy: We're down to 18 beating each other within a thread of their lives with one ultimate goal in mind...strapping the GIWF World title belt around their waste at the end of this hellacious night. Just out of curiousity...who's your pick to take this? Da Monkey: I'm routin' for my boy JOHNNY X! He's worn the belt once, he can do it again! What about you old man? Poppy: I think Jason Budden is going to take this. He's got what it takes. He has also worn GIWF gold in the past, but I think his time has come to strap that World title around his waist. Da Monkey: YEAH RIGHT! THAH CLOWN! Poppy: Clown!? Take a look at yourself you putz! Whoa! There goes the FragMaster at the hands of the MWO! The were on him like stink to shit since the start and now he's out of it! HardCastle: FragMaster eliminated by Savior and Cobra D @ 9: 41! Da Monkey: Look at this!!! The Edens layed out Sigma and their duking it out in a heated game of PAPER, ROCK, SISCORS to decide who throws him out!! Poppy: Who won? Cmon, tell me dammit! Da Monkey: Its a tie!!!! The both tossed him! HardCastle: Sigma eliminated by the Edens @ 11:57! Poppy: The Edens are focusing their efforts on Big Pappa something or another. He's taking a beating. Da Monkey: Ya know, I really like those guys style. Maybe they need a manager? Poppy: I think they'd rahter be alone that with a mexican monkey! Da Monkey: At least I have to pull up may pants, not my wrinkles! [Poppy nails Da Monkey in the back of the head] Da Monkey: HEY! Whoa! Dr. D. Capitator just executed that Face Lift reverse choke slam on Rick Groove. BAM! He just cought Body with it too! There they go! Both of em! HardCastle: Rick Groove and Shawn Body eliminated @ 14: 31! Poppy: Dr. D. made short work of those two! Da Monkey: The MWO is ganging up on Tony Flint now! Poppy: They're beatin' him like a 2 dollar whore! Wow! Wait now.... Flint's fighting back! Owww! One word for ya Flint...futile! Da Monkey: You said it. John Beverly is looking great! He keeps executing these high risk moves on Maximus. Hasn't knocked him over yet, but he sure looks good trying! HardCatle: Big Pappa Puff eliminated by Jason Budden @ 17:29! Da Monkey: What!?! Did you see that! I totally missed it! Poppy: Maybe if you'd quit picking your hair butt and watched the match you'd get these things! Da Monkey: Well you didn't get it! So step off! Poppy: I was testing you and obviously you failed you sideshow freak! Da Monkey: Cobra D just got the boot from Johnny X. And look! HE'S GOING TO DELIVER THE X-TINCTION...he's up...... Poppy: HE JUST EXECUTED THE X-TINCTION OUTSIDE THE RING.. HE THREW COBRA D OUT OF THE RING! OUCH! Call the chiropracter folks, thats gonna leave some purple and brown marks. Da Monkey: Uhh, yeah. HardCastle: Cobra D eliminated by Johnny X @ 22: 45! Poppy: HOLY MARY MOTHER OF GOD!!!!! MAXIMUS HAS BOTH TITAN AND SAVIOR IN A DOUBLE CHOKE SLAM SETUP!!! OUT THEY GO!!!!!! THEY LANDED ON THE GUARD FUCKING RAIL!!! HOLY SHIT!!! Da Monkey: Thats what I like to hear Poppy! Vulgarity, I love it! Poppy: Shut up! Maxiumus is doing it again!! My God! Da Monkey: Are you a religous man??? Poppy: MAXIUMUS JUST DOUBLE CHOKE SLAMMED SHAWN BODY AND RICK GROOVE! Their not out, but they sure as hell are wishing they were! Da Monkey: Talon is beating on Budden! This was a CLASSIC match back in the day! Talon finally made his way over to Budden. Budden is fighting back...inverted atomic drop by Budden...clip to the knees...SHARPSHOOTER! TALONS TAPPING THE MAT LIKE A DRUM!!! Poppy: That move is unbelievably painful! Da Monkey: Naww, really? Did ya lose sleep over that one ya fossil?! [Poppy smacks Monkey's head into the announcers table.] Da Monkey: Hey! How many times are you gonna do that! [Poppy smacks him again] Poppy: Makes an old fossil like me feel young again. Budden still has that Sharpshooter on Talon while Groove is slugging it out with Beverly! Clothesline by Beverly and OUT HE GOES! HardCastle: Rick Groove eliminated by John Beverly @ 37:12! Da Monkey: TALON SPIKE! SHAWN BODY FOLLOWS! HardCastle: Shawn Body eliminated by Talon @ 38: 24 Poppy: we're down to 10 boys left, 8 will leave dissapointed, and two will leave with gold! Da Monkey: BUDDEN WITH THE GORILLA PRESS...TALON IS OUTTA HERE!!! HardCastle: Talon eliminated by Jason Budden @ 41:01! Poppy: Dr. D. Capitator and Maximus, to big boys are slugging it out, and neither are backing down! This is a classic right here. The irrisistable force v.s. the immovable object! Da Monkey: Do you know how many times I've heard that! I've had to swallow that crap more times that I've heard my own name! Poppy: Whoa doggys! Maximus just got chokeslamed clear outta the ring! HardCastle: Maximus eliminated by Dr. D. Capitator @ 48:03 Da Monkey: Flawless and Johnny X are slugging it out! Poppy: X is choking Flawless against the ropes more in an attempt to hurt him than eliminate him, but it just may work! NO! NO! NO! THE EDENS JUST GOT A RUNNING START AND DUMPED THEM BOTH OUT! I CAN'T BELIEVE IT!!! HardCastle: Johnny X and Flawless eliminated by the Edens @ 50:31! Da Monkey: There's only four left! The Edens, Dr. D., and Budden!!! Poppy: Harold is locked up with Dr.D! Da Monkey: Charles is slugging it out with Budden! Clips the legs... SHARPSHOOTER!!! BUDDEN HAS CHARLES IN THE SHARPSHOOTER! Poppy: Harold is trying to bodyslam Decapitator! It backfired! the Doctor is on his feet...setup......FACE LIFT!!! Its all elementary now! Out goes Harold! HardCastle: Harold Eden eliminated by Dr. D. Capitator @ 53:07! Da Monkey: Budden and Decapitator are teaming up on Charles. He doesn't know what to do! He's backing up...still...DOUBLE BOOT! He's gone! Charles Eden is outta here!!! HardCastle: Charles Eden eliminated by Jason Budden and Dr. D. Capitator @ 54: 21! Da Monkey: These two men will leave the building with gold, but what kind! Poppy: Budden has taken much more of a beating than the Doctor during this match and OUCH! He still is! Big boot to the fact and Budden is down. Gorilla press by Budden....REVERSAL! CRUCIFEX! Da Monkey: Budden rolls out of the gorilla press and is vicously stomping Dr. D.! He drops an elbow...another! Budden is climbing the buckles....ELBOW KNEE COMBO OFF THE MIDDLE ROPE! Poppy: SHARPSHOOTER!!!! DR. D. CAPITATOR IS THE NEXT VICTEM OF THE SHARPSHOOTER!!! YOU CAN'T WIN IT THAT WAY! Da Monkey: That right! How long does he plan on keeping him in that! He can't just make him give up, its a battle roayal! Poppy: He's not letting go yet! Its been about a minute, and the Hitman is still wrenching up upon the long legs of the Doctor! WHOA! Da Monkey: Decapitator just flung Budden off! He powered out! He's trying to climb to his feet. Budden nailed the buckle, and he's not getting up! Dr. D. Capitator is up...he's dragging Budden to his feet....... Poppy: FACE LIFT!!!! ITS OVER! Da Monkey: Dr. D. picks him up, over his shoulder....he's walking to the ropes... he dumps Budden out, FOLKS...DR. D. CAPITATOR IS YOUR WORLD CHAMPION!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! HardCastle: Ladies and Gentlemen, Jason "Hitman" Budden is the GIWF National Heavyweight Champion, last being eliminated by Dr. D. Capitator at 62:34, making Dr. D. [Budden gets up and climbs back in the ring.] Lundquist: Mr. Budden, I'm proud to present you with the GIWF National title belt, Ladies and Gentlemen, your GIWF NATIONAL HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION.....JASON "HITMAN" BUDDEN!!!
