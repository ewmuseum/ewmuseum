Last Bout, 15 November 1998

15 November 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
----------------------------------------------------------- Show was LIVE from: Fleet Center in Boston, Massachusetts Attendance: 15,429 Mechandise Sales: $76,000. Among the favorites with fans were fragmaster masks, For D Ounce robot poster, Kingdom of Deity "Your Time is Now" T-shirts as well as the official GIWF program guide with Tony Penn on the cover. Best Signs: "What is a frag and why is he a master of it?" "Better eat your chunky soup!" "I am the extreme one" "Who is taking out da trash?" and "Lundquist, I want your job!" Biggest Pops: 1. For D' Ounce's "impromptu" appearance. 2. Revelations coming in to make the save. 3. Grifter's chase for revenge. ----------------------------------------------------------- ^^^^Dave "Too Extreme" Bean vs. Fragmaster^^^^ Time of Match: 6:35 Basic Summary: The first match of the night proved not to be dissapointing to the GIWF fans. The fans awwhed at the aerial attack being performed in this high paced match, the best being Bean hitting a top rope flying dropkick on Fragmaster on the outside while Fragmaster was sitting in a chair. Bean kept in control throughout the match, minus a few seconds where Fragmaster looked as if he was going make a comeback. Turning Point of the Match: Fragmaster finally caught Bean just before completing a high flying move and superplexed him from the top. However, Fragmaster wasted time gloating to the crowd, allowing Bean to pay possum. While going up top for an elbow drop, Fragmaster missed terribly, noticably getting up in pain seconds after. Ending: While Fragmaster struggled to get up, Bean got to the top, completing a hurricanrana for a three count. Afterwards: Bean gets his hand raised and leaves the ring. In an odd instance, Fragmaster actually grabbed the mic and looked as if he was gonna speak for the first time in months. However, he looks at the crowd, places the mic down and leaves. ^^^^Max Pain vs. Dargon^^^^ Time of Match: 10:36 Basic Summary: In a match that may in the future be a main event, Dargon and Pain gave the fans all they could give. While Crusher looked on from ringside, Pain finally proved to the world the phenom that he is. With a running powerslam and a piledriver, Pain left Dargon screaming in pain. Dargon however, capitalized on the lack of speed by Pain by trying to chop the big man down, but without much success. Turning Point of the Match: Dargon success finally knocked the big man down, however when he climbs to the top rope, he tries for a cross body but was caught by Pain, who then gave Dargon a series of three backbreakers. Ending: Pain picks Dargon up, motions to the fans, and applies the broken bones, his DDT finisher. Afterwards: Pain grabs the mic and says this man is only the beginning of his destruction in the GIWF. Pain and Crusher leave, and as the camera pans down on the crowd, we see Scorpion sitting in the balcony watching the action. ^^^^Kings of Infinite Pleasure vs. the Darklocs^^^^ Time of Match: 4:14 Basic Summary: KIP come down as confident as ever in the match, and they proved it with every second of the bout. The Darklocs looked out of it the whole match. It got worse when Crusher and Pain took a good eyeview looks from ringside, which the Darklocs could not stop thinking about. Throughout the match Penn proved why he is the national champion and Jones proved why he is one of the most talented up and comers in this business. Turning Point of the Match: Saint Des of the Darklocs finally got a break with a double clothesline on Jones. Both go to make the tag, and English pounds on Penn. However, on the reversal of an irish whip, he is tripped by the Crusher. Ending: While English lays on the mat, Penn quickly applies the Penn Killer, as English quickly taps out. Afterwards: Crusher and Pain come in and clear house, completely annihilating the Draklocs. Crusher takes out the trash on Saint Des. They celebrate with Penn, however when he goes to celebrate with Jones, Pain and Crusher seem a bit unnerved by it and leave. Jones and Penn each raise their hands, knowing full well a tag title shot is soon in their future. ^^^^Tiger Tanaka vs. Grifter^^^^ Time of Match: 18:57 Basic Summary: Tanaka dominated the match from the outset, attacking the Grifter as he reaches the ring. Grifter seemed to be favoring his ankle alot, maybe an injury from a previous match. Tanaka uses many submission maneuvers to keep Grifter down, and even a nice leg drop from the top rope. Grifter puts up a good fight and finally gets advantage with a rake of the eyes and a double armed DDT. Turning Point of the Match: The Grifter, growing confident by the minute, signals for the Great Plains Drifter from the top. However Tanaka's manager, Mr. Otaru, grabs Grifter's leg, allowing Tanaka to hit him from behind with a devastating punch to the back of his neck. Ending: Tanaka quickly gets Grifter in the Okasan cradle, winning the match. Afterwards: Grifter, regaining his composure, charges at Tanaka from behind as his hand was raised by the ref, successfully knocking Tanaka down. The Grifter proceeds then to chase Mr. Otaru from post to post, and back to the entrance way. Tanaka gets up and rushes to the back, hoping the Grifter didn't reach his manager. *GIWF Tag Team Championship* ^^^^Divine Intervention vs. Fear Itself^^^^ Time of Match: 21:55 Basic Summary: The Main Event title match proves to be the most exciting of them all, bringing the Fleet Center crowd to it's feet. Apollo Alpha showed his lightning fast skills against Titan, with a huge off the top rope dropkick. Back and forth throughout the match, with Divine Intervention using fast tags to keep Titan in their corner. Also During the Match: In one of the most hillarious acts in GIWF history, a woman comes out form the crowd to attack The Chosen One. She unveils herself, and now the world knows it is For D Ounce. Ounce hits the Chosen One with a purse he carried, undoubtably loaded with something. Security comes out to break up the fight, handcuffing both and leading them out of the arena. Divine Intervention were notibly distracted as Fear Itself took the advantage, pummeling Apollo Alpha. Turning Point of the Match: Titan misses an elbow drop, allowing Apollo to recupperate and tag in Seal, who cleans house. Cobra D attacks Seal from behind, but Alpha knocks him out of the ring, brawling on the outside. Titan throws Seal into the rope and ducks down for a sunset flip but is caught by Seal, who executes the Omega bomb. Ending: After Apollo Alpha knocks Cobra D into the steps, his does his lightning maneuver, a moonsault from the top, for a three count. Afterwards: Pain and Crusher come in and beats on Divine Intervention, knocking Seal out and executing the Takin' Out the Trash on Alpha. The crowd cheers as Revelations runs down with a chair, clearing house and almost nailing Pain with it. Crusher and Pain look on as they exchange word with Revelations. Scorpion comes in from behind, and as Revelations turns around, nails a Superkick to his jaw, knocking the chair into Revelations' face. Scorpion lays the World Title on Revelation's waist, and quickly jumps out as Omega Seal hurries to the aid of Revelations. Crusher and Pain laugh as the cameras fade to black.
