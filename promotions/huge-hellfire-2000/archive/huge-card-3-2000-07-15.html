HUGE Card #3

15 July 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The kitchen of the Hellfire Bar and Grill is a utter mess, food is all over the place, the stoves are dirty, the refrigerator needs a cleaning, rats crawl over the floors. Amazingly a good burger tends to still come out of the god forbidden place. Today a burger isn't coming out there though. No, it's gonna be Rex Fear's World Famous Chili and he's here to teach you the recipe.]
RF: Hi there folks, I know you can't really see that well, the smoke and grime really mess up the already shoddy cameras Barney can provide with us.
But I'm here to show you Chef Leroy Brown doesn't have a thing on me in the kitchen. Oh sure, he may pin my ass, but he can't make my chili! So here's how you do it.
½ pound of sirloin beef half a can of garbanzo beans/chick peas half a can of black beans (All GOYA is possible) half a can of kidney beans A quarter of a bottle or Francisco Renaldi or Krasdale Meat Flavored Tomato
Sauce 2 tablespoons of chili powder ½ a cup of Argo cornstarch Cook the meat in a pan, breaking it in to tiny pieces till it is well done. Pour tomato sauce and let boil. Once boiled pour garbanzo, black and kidney beans (DO NOT DRAIN BEANS) in to meat and sauce mixture. Allow to cook for a bit. Add and stir in chili sauce. Allow to cook for a bit again. Add corn starch when food seems ready. Mix. Place in bowls and serve with white rice. Serves 3-5 people.
RF: And there you have, a regular cooking lesson from Lord Fear. I can't promise you I'll kick anyone's ass, but I can make a damn good chili!
[Blackness. Static. Cut messily to a visual disclaimer printed on the screen in black on white.
"Disclaimer: The following is a highly volitile, offensive program that has not been edited for content. If you let your kids watch this, they'll probably knock you out with a steel chair, cuss a blue streak, and burn the house down with you locked inside.
You've been warned..." Then, more static and buzzing lead to silence and darkness. After a few seconds, the opening strains to "Loser" by Beck are heard.]

#with the plastic eyeballs, spray paint the vegetables# #dog food stalls with the beefcake pantyhose# [Cut to a second still. Well-formed but plain in the face, a "Goth Chick" holds a microphone whose cord hangs to the floor. She looks bored, as if waiting for something to happen.]
#kill the headlights and put it in neutral# #stock car flamin' with a loser and the cruise control# [Utterly hysterical, an angry negro youth in Black Panther-esque gear points and screams at two white guys who sit at a folding table. Leaning back in their seats, they look none too pleased at being berated.]
#Soooooooyyyy# #Soy un perdedor# #I'm a loser baby so why don't you kill me?# #(Drive-by body pierce)# [The drumbeat continues as everything fades once again to black. Then, without fade, a logo appears in steel lettering on a bright red background.]

Fuzzy, grainy, filmed on Sony handicams, the no-budget atmosphere of a Wayne's World-inspired wrestling league permeates the air.
A wrestling ring has been shoehorned into the middle of the former dance floor. Scaffolding and ladders are everywhere, signs that construction has been going on. The steel guardrail is entirely too close to the ring and leads back to what looks like a door into the place's basement.
Oh yeah, now THIS is truly the small-time... Instead of cutting neatly over to the announcer's table, the cameraman walks around the ring, catching a glimpse of the bigscreen TV that allows the bar patrons to see what the camera sees, and finally settling on a cheap folding table. Behind the table are a simple-looking fellow in a black T-Shirt and what looks like a Kid Rock impersonator. Lovely. When the pair speak, their words have an open-air quality that suggests that there is no direct audio feed into the cameras. Talk about low-grade quality TV wrestling.]
BJ (Barney Johnson): Hello folks, and welcome to Hellfire's Ultimate Grappling Excitement! Contrary to popular belief, we're still around a month after our inception!
DJ HV (DJ Har-V): Hell, I never even thought you'd get the first show on the air, man. Didn't even think you could get two losers into the ring, really.
BJ: Well thanks for the vote of confidence, Harvey, but I think I'm doing fine. DJ HV: Yeh, sure, fine. For now. Let's just see this place in a year. THEN I'll be convinced you can hang in the wrestlin' biz as an owner.
[Static, cut to later on. The crowd's grown considerably to something in the neighborhood of 200. Probably the largest crowd that HUGE has drawn in the last month. Not that it's saying much. It _has_ only been a month, and 200 isn't exactly big, y'know?]
DJ HV: Yo yo, listen up people! Let me drop some knowledge on all yo' heads! Spin da tunes! ["Bad Bad Leroy Brown" by Jim Croce blasts suddenly over the PA system as a mild cheer rings out among the fans. A big, mean looking black man then comes storming out of the Hellfire's kitchen, white hat on his head and apron around his waist.]
DJ HV: The kitchen is closed 'cuz the chef's come to fight! This man's a massive 240 pounds and he hails from right here in Portsmouth. Give it up for "Chef" LEROY BROWN!!!
[The cheers increase as Portsmouth is mentioned. Apparently there were some people who didn't know where Leroy was from. Most likely, they're new to the Hellfire.]
DJ HV: And the guy he's probably gonna beat up is comin' out too. Spin dem tunes. [Screaming is heard coming from the wrestlers entrance area and suddenly "Creep" by Radiohead starts up. Rex Fright leaps out of the entrance area like a cat, his mid-lentgh black/red hair covering his purple make-uped face, he wears a ripped up "ZRTLM - School For The Criminally Insane" T-shirt, black jean shorts and black sneakers. DJ HV: He's comin' from "The Streets of The World" and weighs 183. Give it up for REX FRIGHT!
[Rex climbs the steps to a top turnbuckle and jumps down in to the ring and stands still as he waits for the bell.]
*DING* *DING* *DING* BJ: Two tough individuals about to go at it! Both men, Rex and Leroy, definitely coming from a school of hard knocks kind of background.

DJ HV: Yeah, whatever man. [Though seeming to be ready at first, Rex's concentration quickly goes to the outside, where Lady Phear is handing out what seems to be free bowls of chili to the crowd. The free food seems to sit well with the patrons, some of who didn't even expect to find wrestling when they returned to their old watering hole.
So Rex talks to Phear, and the two have a happy little conversation, presumably about the chili. Then, Leroy interupts their exchange.]
BJ: Brown! Brown has Fright! Bodyslam! Clothesline sends him down again! Fright begging off, slides out of the ring!
[The crowd, which had been cheering, boos Fright lightly for half a second. Rex, however, ignores it totally, and pulls Leroy from the ring by his ankles.]
BJ: And the brawl goes outside! Brown angry at Fright, who took it upon himself to feed this crowd tonight. Fright--OH NO!
[After stomping the larger man for a few seconds, Fright catches Lady Phear's attention as she comes out of the kitchen with yet another tray of chili. This tray of steaming bowls, however, is not intended for consumption as Fright dumps it all down over the back of Brown who was trying to get to his feet.]
LB: AAAAAAAAAHHHH!!! RF: EAT IT! EAT IT!!! [Ramming Brown's face down into the brick floor, Fright does indeed try to make Leroy eat his chili.]
RF: TASTES GREAT, DON'T IT!? ADMIT THAT MY CHILI TASTES GREAT, DAMN YOU!!! [Static. Cut to later in the match. Leroy's back in control and both men are back in the ring.]
BJ: Brown has been scalded, stomped, bludgeoned and beaten, but he's still going toe to toe with Fright! This is amazing!
[Missing a lariat, the overly basic brawler bounces off the ropes not once, not twice, but three times. On the third rebound, Brown is backdropped...]
*CRASH!!!* [Over the top rope.] BJ: NO! GOOD GOD! Brown just fell to the floor! His legs hit the guardrail, his neck's been folded against the floor! Help him! Somebody help him!
DJ HV: Oh please. As if he's really hurt. BJ: What are you talking about!? DJ HV: Wrestling's fake. Everybody knows it. BJ: WHAT THE FUCK ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT!? This is real! Wrestling's real! LEROY'S NECK MAY BE BROKEN!
DJ HV: It's not fake? BJ: NO!!! DJ HV: Oh shit! Somebody help that guy! [Fright's eyes widen as he looks down on his opponent, but his concern fades as he shrugs and exits the ring, taking a seat with his woman and partaking of his own chili.]
BJ: Security finally coming out to lay Leroy flat. Harvey, give Rex the match. There's no way Leroy can continue after that. Hell, he could be dying.
DJ HV: Uh, yo, people, your winner by grevious frickin' injury's Rex Fright. Give our chef your prayers, or something.
[Silent, the crowd stares in wonder at the still form of Leroy as Mason Dugan and the other security personnel immobilize Leroy's neck, waiting for paramedics to arrive.
Static. Cut to show an ambulance leaving the outside of the Hellfire, Leroy presumably inside. Static. Then, a name in black on a white screen. "'Vicious' Vladimr Vorshenko" A burst of static clears the screen and a grainy, low-quality video shot appears showing the pale face of "Lady Phear" as she diligently burrows into her ear for an elusive piece of wax. Suddenly, her expression freezes as she notices the camera is on her. After a classic "deer-in-the-headlights" moment, she whips her finger out of her ear and screws her face up in rage.]
Lady Phear: FUCK! Tell me the next time you put the goddamn camera on, shithead!! [Snarling, she spins away from the camera and stomps over toward a stocky man with very short white hair who is reclining in a chair.
The man's eyes are closed and his mouth hangs open but he is otherwise recognizable as "Vicious" Vladimr Vorshenko.]
Lady Phear: Hey! Vorshe-whatever!! Let's do this interview, OK?? [Vorshenko doesn't answer, his chest rising and falling slowly. Frustrated, Lady Phear leans over and pokes him in the ribs.] Lady Phear: HEY!! I'm talkin' to you, Vlad! We've got an inter... [Halfway through her sentence, the gothic waitress/interviewer stops suddenly and sniffs for a moment as she looks down at the torpid
Russian.]
Lady Phear: JEEZUS CHRIST! HE'S PISSED HIMSELF!! WHERE THE FUCK DO THEY FIND THESE LOSERS?!?!? [In a huff, Lady Phear stomps out of view as the camera zooms in for an unsteady closeup on Vorshenko's face. Several days worth of dark stubble encase his chin and cheeks as he begins to snore loudly.
After a moment, the scene vanishes into another field of static. Then, another name. "'Sheriff' Bob Bobley" Static, cut. We pick up at the Hellfire's bar. Brown wood, stained and chipped, in well-used but serviceable condition. Joe Holliday is seated here, drinking, as a bartender is behind the bar, wiping off some glasses, when he hears a loud voice from the doorway.]
Voice: OOOOOOOH, IT'S INSPECTION TIME! [Joe winces... you would too if this guy barged in on you. It's Sheriff
Bob Bobley. Bob, in full uniform, minus his helmet, wanders up to the bar, looking around and writing notes on a pad. The lean officer with the greying brown hair marches right up to the bar, as if he owned the place.
The bartender averts his eyes, hoping Bob will go away. It doesn't work.] Sheriff Bob: So! Barney ain't around, is he?! Joe: No... Bartender: It's his day off, he's out fishing. Sheriff Bob: DON'T TALK BACK TO ME, BOY! Now... where's that drunk
Vorschenko?! Bartender: He usually doesn't come in until 4... Sheriff Bob: I SAID DON'T BACKTALK ME, DO AGAIN AN' I'LL RUN YA IN! Now... it's time fer the bar ta have it's bi-annual inspection! Bartender: But you're a highway patrolman! The local TAFD is supposed to... *WHAM* [Bob slams his old wooden stick down on the bar, creating a large crack.] Sheriff Bob: DOOOOOOOH! I warned ya! [Bob doesn't pay any mind... he pulls the liquor license down from above the bar.] Joe: NOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!! Bartender: When Barney finds out about this... he'll have your badge,
Bobley! Sheriff Bob: In a pig's eye! Hmmmm... [checks his watch] three-fifty-five! OOPS, gotta go! There's... uh... some black people out drivin' today, I gotta go ticket 'em! Gya-gya-gyah! [And then, a man in a Tobacco, Alcohol, and Firearms Department uniform walks in.] Man: *ahem* Time for the bi-annual inspection. License please. Bartender: ...I hate him. [Static, and back to "live" action. The Hellfire's crowd has grown even more since the ambulance came for poor Leroy. As usual, the worst possible situation attracts the most people.]
BJ: Folks, I have a little update on Leroy Brown's condition. He was primarily stunned, and has a strained neck, but he suffered no major injuries. As of right now, he's quitting wrestling, and has quit as the Hellfire's chef without notice...
DJ HV: Damn man, you'll be lucky if he don't sue your ass into the ground! He got hurt on your property, y'know.
BJ: Yes, well, heh. See, before his match, like with all of our wrestlers, Leroy had to sign a waver stating that we weren't responsible for what happens during the match.
DJ HV: Whoa. You slick, man, you slick. BJ: Actually, I have to give credit for that to Jack Britain. He's the one handling the talent and all that.
DJ HV: You just...what do you do exactly? BJ: I...own HUGE. DJ HV: Oh yeah, I see now. BJ: Just announce the next match. DJ HV: Oh yeah, right. Spin da tunes. [The opening of "Bad Boys" by Inner Circle begins to play over the PA. This song has become synonymous with law enforcement over the years, and a LOUD siren begins to blare... obviously not a recording. DJ Har-V struggles to make himself heard over it.]
DJ HV: From The Ohio Highway Patrol... weighing two-hundred eighteen pounds... "Sheriff" BOB BOBLEY!!!
[A highway patrol motorcycle begins to roll to the ring, and the crowd shouts horrible loud things. For Sheriff Bob Bobley is riding in, with his trademark black-and-white motorcycle helmet and dark shades. The lanky patrolman rolls up to the ring, and parks his vehicle. A long leg swings over the bike, clad in navy pants with a gold stripe up each side. Bobley removes his helmet, revealing his short, curly, greying brown hair. He then removes his dark blue uniform top, revealing a grey "PROPERTY OF OHIO HIGHWAY PATROL - YOUR HEROES AND SAVIOURS" tank top shirt. Bob wrings his black-gloved hands, and chuckles loudly with a mean grin on his face, before taking his ticket book and sliding into the ring.]
BJ: And there he is. The only man who's been able to get under the skin of HUGE's fans. The Patrolman who tickets people for no reason, and tries to get popular bars shut down.
DJ HV: Hey, how'd you deal with that anyway? BJ: Well, we're above code and...I just had a discussion with the inspector. It all worked out--
DJ HV: You bribed him. BJ: No, I-- DJ HV: Go on, admit it! YOU BRIBED THE INSPECTOR! BJ: Just announce Vorshenko. DJ HV: Right, right. Spin it! [The grinding technobeat of "Hatesong" by Cubanate fills the smoky interior of the Hellfire Bar & Grill as a scarred, white-haired figure appears at the top of the aisle. Ropy muscles stretch the fabric of a black T-shirt but the unmistakable bulge of a little pot belly can also be seen. Vladimr Vorshenko seems oblivious to the rowdy crowd as he stumbles down the aisle and rolls into the ring underneath the bottom rope.
Strangely, this time, he doesn't look drunk as he holds his head, blocking out the sound of his own music. His staggering seems more from the pain in his head than any inebriation.]
DJ HV: This man, you better believe's been imported straight from Kazan Russia like a fine kinda Vodka. He may stink of liquor, but he's still 248 pounds worth of muscle. Nevermind the back injury, folks, this man can still go. No, seriously.
Oh, and his name? "Vicious" VLADIMR VORSHENKO!!! [Vlad gets a decent cheer, and winces when he hears it, but it's nothing compared to the enormous booing Bob just got. Man, the way people hate that guy, you'd think they, down to the last man, had personal dealings with the Patrolman. Wait...maybe they do.
Rather abruptly, Bob grabs the mic, cord and all from a ring attendant, and rolls back into the ring to confront Vlad.]
Sheriff Bob: HEY YOU! RUSSKIE! This here's th' LAW talkin' atcha, boy! Gya-gya-gyah! Now, you been Drunk An' Disorderly fer weeks now! But I'm a kind, carin', compashanatin' man, so I'll let ya off with a stern warnin'. An' since yer probably thirsty on account of th' licensin' problems they got aroun' here... I'll even git ya a drink! Gya-gya-gyah!
[And Bob gives Vlad a bottle of vodka. Isn't that nice? Eager for a drink that will take his headache away, Vlad pops it open without even bothering to look at the label, and then SPRAYS the "liquor" out into the crowd.]
BJ: Good lord, what was in that drink!? Vlad looks like he's been poisoned! I--THE SLAMMER! THE SLAMMER!
[Vorshenko goes up, but his thrashing about staggers Bob, who in turn staggers towards the ropes. Vlad grabs the top rope and nearly falls to the outside, half landing on the apron.]
BJ: Thank god, Vorshenko's back couldn't take that kind of impact. [Still spitting and foaming, Vorshenko can't capitalize on his counter, and is grabbed again by Bob, who LIFTS the much larger man up, and staggers backwards (lotta staggering this match) in an exaggerated vertical suplex that very nearly has Vlad hitting the opposite ringropes.
Shouting and writhing in pain on the mat, Vorshenko reminds us all forcefully that he has a major back injury that he's not quite healed from. The crowd shouts down Bobley as he gives that evil "Gya-gya-gyah!" laugh of his yet again and covers.]
BJ: Cover! 1! 2! 3! Wait, there's no referee! Bobley jumped the gun, he didn't wait for a referee to come out!
[Ambling up the aisle comes a young man who is just now pulling a "HUGE Referee" yellow T-shirt on over his "HUGE Security" T-shirt. Sliding into the ring, he scrambles to count the cover.]
R (Referee): 1!!! *SLAP!* 2!!! *SLAP!* 3!!! *SLAP!* BJ: WAIT! NO! Vlad has the ropes! He was dropped too near the apron and he has a death grip on the bottom rope!
=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE= "Vicious" Vladimr Vorshenko -vs- "Sheriff" Bob Bobley =HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE=HUGE= [FACE POP as Bob dances around, thinking he's won the thing. So loud and squirmy is Vlad as he holds his head against the noise around him that the referee never thinks to inform poor Bob that he's not won, rather, he makes sure that Vorshenko can continue.]
DJ HV: Hey, who is that redheaded stepchild you got working the ref spot tonight? I don't recognize him.
BJ: That's Lowell...not sure of his last name. He's a Shawnee State student. Good kid, belongs to my old Frat, Alpha Delta Phi.
DJ HV: A fratboy, eh? BJ: Bob demanding to have his hand raised, but Lowell's nothing but confused. [Grabbing the kid by his collar, Bobley threatens him strongly. Lowell seems to explain, gesticulating wildly. The whole exchange draws an especially loud jeer from ringside, where some college kids are actually threatening to jump the guardrail.
Coincidentally, they're wearing Alpha Delta Phi shirts...] BJ: Bobley finally letting go. Probably a good idea considering that Lowell has about 20 fratboy buddies in the audience right now. Vorshenko...finally getting up.
[Potbelly billowing out and sucking in as he breathes shudderingly, fighting the pain that wracks his every cell, Vladimr Vorshenko pulls himself up using the ropes. Without the liquor to dull his pain, and with a hangover that could kill an elephant, Vlad looks like he could die in the ring without any help from Bobley.]
BJ: I thought the cold shower might wake him up some, but it looks like Vlad's still suffering from both his addiction and his injuries. Bobley!
[Bobley swings with a roundhouse right but it's blocked by Vorshenko who rams his stiffened fingers into the sheriff's throat. As Bobley gasps, fighting for air, Vorshenko grabs him in a front waistlock and sends him flying with a belly-to-belly suplex... only for the Russian shootfighter to drop to his knees, clutching his back in pain.]
BJ: REVERSAL! Bobley took it that time, but Vlad's still hurt! Now Lowell's applying a 10 count to both men!
[Bobley has a look on his face that shows extreme disbelief. He works his way up from the mat slowly as the screen cuts to static, then to later in the match.
Shrugging off a weak lariat by Bobley, Vorshenko headbutts him and then lifts him up in a bearhug. But as Bobley yells in pain, Vorshenko immediately drops him and clutches his forehead as if nursing the mother of all headaches. Bobley takes advantage by lifting the heavier man onto his shoulders and turning in place in an airplane spin before falling backward with a Samoan Drop. Vorshenko groans and again clutches his lower back.]
R: 1!!! BJ: KICKOUT! Vlad kicked out, but it looks like it just put him in more pain to do so. [Popping back up, Bobley stomps away at Vorshenko's chest and abdomen. The bigger man doesn't even seem to notice the blows, so pained is he in his head and back. Bobley should notice just exactly what Vlad is clutching, but he doesn't, instead opting to pull Vlad up and continue the brawling.]
BJ: Vlad not even noticing Bobley! The "Sheriff" is simply going wild! He came into this match with a great deal of confidence, and thought he had it won in the opening seconds, but it didn't work out that way. RIGHT HAND! LEFT!
[The big haymakers from Bobley connect and stagger the big Russian, but Vlad's eyes go wide and actually seem to tear from the blow...he's not going out, he's waking up.]
BJ: AGAIN! Vlad-- VVV: AaaaaaAAAAAAAAGH!!! [And in true shootfighter fashion, the big man snaps. Grabbing the smaller man, his pain seeming very far away indeed, Vorshenko HURLS Bob into the turnbuckle. When he bounces back out, Vlad hits a running forearm that turns Bob inside out and over. He pops back up again, but wobbles on his feet.]
BJ: BOB IN TROUBLE! It looks like Vlad is about to KILL him dead! Good god! CROSSFACE CHICKENWING! CROSSFACE!
[Face turning as red from oxygen depletion as Vlad's is from anger, Bob thrashes about, trying to escape the painful hold. He shoves back with his feet, careening towards the turnbuckle.]
BJ: Vlad to the turnbuckle, and that broke the hold! [Indeed, Vorshenko, his rage largely spent, staggers out, leaning on the ropes for the support his spine won't give him.]
BJ: It looks like Vorshenko's about blown himself out! That short spurt of energy's left him exhausted!
[Puffing visibly, Vorshenko gasps for breath with an exhausted look on his face. Behind him, Bobley crouches down and tries to sneak up on the Russian but Vorshenko spots him and sends him flying across the ring with an armdrag takedown. Retaining a grip of Bobley's arm, Vorshenko falls back and wraps his legs around the limb, locking up Bobley in a Fujiwara armbar. As Bobley strains to escape, large beads of sweat can be seen on Vorshenko's face as he pants heavily.]
BJ: ARMBAR! ARMBAR! HE'S GONNA TAP! [Closeup of Bobley's face as he screams. Suddenly, the referee falls on both men!] DJ HV: WHOA! The badge-wearer just pulled the ref down! Ain't that against the rules!? BJ: Bobley in pain, I don't think he knows what he's doing! Vlad...lets go of the hold. That could be a mistake!
[Static. Cut to later in the matchup.] DJ HV: Man, Vorshenko is going for... something... I get all these crazy holds mixed up. BJ: So does he, when he's intoxicated, or in this case, hungover... Bob locked on a side headlock, because Vorshenko was mixed up! But Vladimir uses his great instinct to suplex Bobley out of it!
DJ HV: Hey! Vladimir just fell down, Barney, he didn't suplex nothin'! Bob just tripped and fell under him! Stop lyin' to make your money-pit look good!
[The sloppy belly-to-back suplex hurts both men, of course. Vlad, puffing fit to suffocate, rolls slowly towards the ring apron. Oddly, Bob pops up relatively quickly, perhaps realizing his advantage, he shouts...]
Sheriff Bob: NOW I'M GONNA SHOW YA REAL WRASSLIN'! BJ: Bob Bobley... locks on a figure four? Well, he's trying, but he doesn't know how... DJ HV: Vorshenko kicked Bob in the scrotum! I don't think he even knew where he was kicking, but he got the traffic cop down in the ol' glove compartment!
[Lowell gets between both men, applying the 10 count and rubbernecking when suddenly... A foot flies up between his legs...] BJ: BOBLEY! BOBLEY KICKED THE REFEREE IN THE GROIN! DJ HV: SHIT! THE FRATBOYS ARE JUMPING THE RAILING! [Indeed, about a dozen members of the Alpha Delta Phi fraternity jump over the barricade and are cut off by HUGE's security force. Mason Dugan takes a few and the other off-duty policemen and sundry tough guys wrestle down individuals as best they can. Meantime, in the ring...]
BJ: Oh no... Bobley's up! He's not hurt! HANDCUFFS!? [After a short contest of strength, Bobley manages to latch one cuff onto Vlad's wrist. The struggle continues, and Bob can't get the other...]
BJ: No! Bob's pulling out the nightstick!!! *WHACK!* *WHACK!* *WHACK!* BJ: GOOD LORD! HE NAILED VLAD IN THE SPINE! Cuffs the other hand! PINS! [More specifically, Bobley rolls Vlad onto his back, hiding the cuffs, and slaps away at the back of Lowell's head to get his attention. The novice referee, only partly coherent, looks up to see the EEEEVIL Patrolman with one boot planted on the big Russian's chest, and counts...]
