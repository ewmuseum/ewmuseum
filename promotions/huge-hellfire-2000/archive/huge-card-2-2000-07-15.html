Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The words "Earlier Today" appear in cheesy low budget lettering as we see a fuzzy image of a black Honda pulling up outside of the Hellfire. The car slowly comes to a halt. X looks down at the gray sidewalk, a look of disgust on his face.]

[X angrily walks into the Hellfire as the screen statics out. Cut messily to a visual disclaimer printed on the screen in black on white.
"Disclaimer: The following is a highly volitile, offensive program that has not been edited for content. If you let your kids watch this, they'll probably knock you out with a steel chair, cuss a blue streak, and burn the house down with you locked inside.
You've been warned..." Then, more static and buzzing lead to silence and darkness. After a few seconds, the opening strains to "Loser" by Beck are heard.]
