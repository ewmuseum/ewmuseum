"The Cavalier" Tom Knight --------------------------- ("Ride of the Valkyries" begins to play over a montage of highlights of
Tom Knight's NAWC career. The sequence of clips ends with Tom Knight's victory over Killer Buddha to retain the NAWC World Title. As the music ends, the scene changes. We see Tom Knight and Guinevere standing outside an opera house, dressed in their finest. Tom is wearing a tuxedo, while Guinevere is decked out in a stunning evening gown and a fur wrap. A limousine pulls up in front of them. The driver steps out and opens the door. Before Tom can help Guinevere in, Greg Hollinsworth appears on the scene, out of breath from running. Tom turns to him with a trace of annoyance on his face.)
GH: Sorry to catch you here, champ, but I missed you before you left, and I got orders to get a quick interview with you. How was the show?
Cav: It was excellent, thank you. However, we do have to return to the hotel. If you've got a portable camera with you, you can come along and do the interview inside. I'd rather not stand outside the theatre, if you don't mind.
GH: No problem, thanks! (The scene changes to the interior of the limousine. Tom and Guinevere are seated right behind the driver, while Greg is holding the camera and is out of the picture. The World Title belt is over Tom's shoulder.)
GH: So President Capulco is claiming that you are ducking Rio, and that's why you didn't immediately grant him a rematch after you defeated
Rio D. Giniro for the World Title.
Cav: One thing our esteemed president is noted for is his tendency to run at the mouth. I didn't give Rio an immediate rematch because he didn't ask for one. Nor did Mr. Capulco immediately step forward and sign the match. Everyone in the NAWC knows that I do not duck opponents. I didn't duck from Rick Stevens, Killer Buddha, Thane Sloan, or any of the other men who've tried to defeat me. Rio held the World
Title, and that does indeed entitle him to a rematch. But considering his position within the company, perhaps Mr. Capulco should start placing the blame on the face he sees in the mirror every day.
GH: So you're saying you're ready to face Rio again? Cav: Certainly. I'm ready to face any man at any time. Mr. Capulco says I was merely lucky. In point of fact, I overcame every obstacle he tried to place before me. I was forced to wrestle another match only a few minutes before the World Title match, against an opponent who had held the World Title himself only a few short weeks before. The fact is that Dr. Hate,
Thane Sloan, and even Rio D. Giniro were nothing more than caretakers for this belt until it came into the hands of the one man who can best represent this federation.
GH: That man being yourself. Cav: Certainly, since I hold the distinction of being the first man to successfully defend his belt. I also find it amusing that the president would start off by claiming I was running from his man Rio, then in the next minute assert that he is the man who calls the shots and guaranteeing that Rio would leave Nuclear Winter as the champion. Make up your mind, El Presidente. If you call the shots, then you book the matches. If you don't sign Rio to face me in a one-on-one matchup, then it's you who is
avoiding the confrontation. You saw it that night; Rio is not capable of beating me. When I step into that ring, I am focused on doing whatever I must to succeed. I will take any pain, absorb any punishment. I will never quit, I will never give up. I will overcome any challenges put before me, and I will never back down from an opponent. Rio knows that, and he recognizes that there is indeed a spirit in the NAWC that he cannot overcome.
GH: But now you've got a triple threat match, defending the belt against two men, DErek Davies and Rio D. Giniro. What are your thoughts on that?
Cav: I'm very much opposed to the Triple Threat concept; a champion can lose a belt without being a factor in the outcome. But the reasons for it are clear; Capulco knows very well that Rio is not capable of beating me. However, he feels Rio has an excellent chance of beating Derek
Davies, which would make Rio the champion without having to pin me or make me submit. He may even be right. But I've fought Davies before, and he is not going to just lie down at Rio's command. He's a tough fighter, there's no question, and Rio's going to find it difficult to sneak in the back door that way.
GH: How do you respond to the comments made by Derek Davies, regarding your challenge to Rick Stevens?
Cav: Davies, you claim that I beat you before because of Thane Sloan's interference. Sloan was, in fact, trying to take me out of that match on your behalf. He failed, and you paid the price. Don't cry over spilled lager. You earned the title shot at the house card this past week. You might have escaped the Sword in the Stone had the match been allowed to continue without interference from Capulco's Secret Service.
You might even have come back and defeated me. We'll never know. But even if Capulco hadn't interfered in our match, I would have granted you the title shot, either at Nuclear Winter or immediately thereafter had I faced someone else.
But words are cheap, and I doubt you'll believe mine. But let's get one thing extremely crystal clear, Derek. You say I'm running around the NAWC, 'playing goody-goody.' Understand this: this is the real Tom Knight; I don't pretend to be something I'm not, I don't play the role of a hero and then go back to the hotel and turn into somebody else. I am this way
because it suits me, it suits who I am. Only once have I done something against my own nature, and that was when I first arrived in the NAWC and interfered in a match I had no business getting involved with. Since then I have been true to myself and to my nature. I didn't ask the fans to cheer for me; I don't ask them to chant my name every night. I simply give them what they truly want: a champion they can believe in, a champion who will stand up for what's right, not for what's convenient or easy. I'll face you, Rio D. Giniro, Killer Buddha, Rick Stevens...it doesn't matter to me! I offered the challenge to Rick Stevens because he had proven to me in our previous match that he was a worthy opponent. I accepted the match with Killer Buddha because I have never turned away from a challenge. I fought you at the House Show for the same reason, and now I'll face both you and Rio in a Triple Threat match, defending my title against two dangerous men. But the outcome will be no different than it was the past two weeks; I will emerge from the struggle beaten, but unbroken, and I will retain the title I have fought so hard to earn.
GH: Alright, I'd like to ask Guinevere how she's feeling about Nuclear
Winter. Not only do you have to watch Tom defend his World Title against two opponents, but you will be involved in the battle royal to determine the Women's champion. What are your thoughts on that?
Guin: As for Tom's match, I have every confidence in his ability to come out of there the way he'll walk in, and that is as the NAWC World
Champion. He's beaten both of his opponents before, and he'll prove to everyone why he is the man to lead this Federation in the year 2000. GH: How about your own match? Guin: Well, there aren't many experienced women wrestlers in the NAWC, and I'm not one of them. But I've trained with Tom for three years now, and I've learned a lot about the sport from him. There are some dangerous women in the NAWC, women like Lady Divine and Hellfire. They both have a few issues with me, and both of them would like to beat me.
But I believe I'm ready to face them, as well as the other competitors. Lady Divine, you hit me with a tire iron a while ago; I haven't forgotten that. And you're going to find out that payback is truly sweet. Hellfire, your very existence offends me, and I'll be glad to knock some humility into your sneering personality.
GH: How do you feel about the Women's match, Tom? Cav: I have every confidence in Guinevere. I have to admit it won't be easy to watch her going out to do battle, but I've promised her I'll be there at ringside with her, cheering her on as loudly as the fans in the arena will.
GH: There's a newcomer to the NAWC that mentioned your name, Jason
Blake. Any comments on his presence?
Cav: Jason and I have met before in Europe, where he was a World Champion. His presence in the NAWC will be felt early and quickly, of that there is no doubt. Don't be fooled by his size, either; he's a terrific wrestler, and one of the few men I would trust at my back. I hope he earns a World Title shot, because a match between us would be one for the ages. I know,
because we've met before. For now, I wish him the best of luck and all success here in the NAWC.
GH: Well, it looks like we're close to the hotel, so I'll wrap this up quickly. Anything else you'd like to say tonight?
Cav: Certainly. Davies, you say that the World Title is what you crave, what you desire. You aren't saying anything that hasn't been said by thousands of other would-be champions. Wanting, craving aren't enough.
You have to earn it. You've earned a chance, I'll grant you that. But that's a far cry from earning the title itself. I expect nothing less than desire from a man who is wrestling me with the title on the line.
If you don't want the title, why compete? You can try to psych yourself up all you want, but in the end it's what happens inside the ring that counts. And you know as well as everyone else does that inside the ring, I am the dominant force in the NAWC. I have slain the dragons that have been placed before me, I have attained the dream of a championship! I have proven to everyone that I am the TRUE World
Champion, and there is not a single man in the NAWC that can defeat me! As for Mr. Capulco, you dream of recapturing your lost glory by stacking the deck against the Cavalier yet again. Your purpose with
Davies is clear; you hope to use him as the instrument of your victory.
As you failed in your attempts to deny me this belt before, so will you fail again. For as the smoke clears from the war we will wage, you will watch as both Derek Davies and Rio D. Giniro feel the unfettered might of Excalibur, and you will bear witness to their fall from grace! And when
the referee sets their defeat in stone, one-two-three, you will understand that NOTHING can kill the Light that stands for Justice in the NAWC, and the scales shall fall from your eyes as you feel the exultation of the masses, eagerly embracing the power that will surround them, and you too shall join the vast legions of those who have SEEN THE
