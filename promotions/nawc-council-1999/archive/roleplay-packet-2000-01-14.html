Roleplay Packet, Napalm Revised & PPV Lineup Announced

14 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I know this should have been out yesterday. But things happen and I got caught up in something else. So here it is. Tonight we hear from
Richard "Madman" Miller, "The Cavalier" Tom Knight, Killer Buddha, and
Chris Phear.
Richard "Madman" Miller -------------------------- [Scene opens in a blue room. The lighting is pale and sickly, and there are two chairs set up in the center of the room. In one chair sits NAWC reporter Sharon Roberts, and the other chair is empty. We suddenly hear some easy listening music come up quickly, and the words "Debut Profile" come up on the screen. Sharon smiles, and she catches a hand from offstage point at her.]
Sharon Roberts : Hello, and welcome to another edition of NAWC Debut Profile, the show where we take a look at the up-and-coming stars in the North American Wrestling Council. Tonight, we welcome the rookie Richard "The Madman" Miller to the stage.
[A horrific scream begins to creep onto the speakers, then it is drowned out by a diabolical laugh as, from offstage, Richard Miller makes his way to the second chair. Richard is wearing a "U of B" muscle shirt and a pair of loose fitting blue jeans. His hair, although short, looks messy and unkept... As does his beard. The Madman looks uncomfortable in this setting as he slowly takes a seat in the chair opposite Sharon.]
Sharon Roberts: Welcome Richard. Richard Miller: Thanks Sharon. Sharon Roberts: So tell me a little bit about yourself Richard. I understand that you're a recent graduate of the University of Boston?
Richard Miller: True. [Sharon smiles.] Sharon Roberts: It's a half an hour show, would you mind expanding on your anwsers a little?
Richard Miller: I'm not the type of guy to sit here and tell you my entire life story Sharon... If you want to know something about me then ask some questions... If you don't, then I'll be on my way...
Sharon Roberts: Let's look into your childhood... You weren't raised in a cave or anything were you?
Richard Miller: What? Sharon Roberts: Well, you spoke before the age of 14 didn't you? Richard Miller: Of course... What kind of idiot would live in a cave or not speak until they were 14?!
Sharon Roberts: What about your wrestling name? Madman... Where did that come from? Richard Miller: Well, when I started in College athletics I wasn't as big as I am now... But my adversaries would always note that, although
I wasn't that big, I was very mean. Actually, I was more than mean...
I was mad. So that's where the name was started. Plus, my friends and classmates also new I had an odd demenor so the name stuck right away.
When I decided to become a professional wrestler it was a natural transition. Sharon Roberts: What was your major at the University of Boston? What was your grade point average?
Richard Miller: My grade point average was high enough to keep me competing in college athletics... That's all that really matters.
Sharon Roberts: Have you ever wrestled professionally before? Richard Miller: No, I'm a real rookie. Sharon Roberts: Well, what made you decide to come to the North American Wrestling Council to start your professional wrestling career? Richard Miller: I looked around and this is what I found... No specific reason Sharon. Sharon Roberts: Well that's good to hear. In your first interview you had some strong words for the NAWC World Champion... What made you think that you'd get a response from the World Champion when you're just a rookie?
Richard Miller: When I looked over the roster and watched the first interview show he was the guy who caught my eye. I don't know why I decided to talk about him, but he should feel priveledged that I spent my valuable time on such an unworthy opponent.
Sharon Roberts: Do you expect that you will find "worthy" opponents in the NAWC... If not, why are you here?
Richard Miller: I expect I will Sharon, maybe not right away, but I have faith that I will. I just don't know when. I'd like to say that every opponent will be a challenge, but I just don't see that happening...
Sharon Roberts: It's clear that you have plenty of confidence in your own ability. Now could you tell me about your goals in the NAWC?
Richard Miller: My goals are simple. I just want to beat people up. Sharon Roberts: I think you may be biting off more than you can chew with the NAWC Richard, but good luck. Well, we're just about out of time. Do you have any last comments?
Richard Miller: No. Sharon Roberts: Fair enough. And folks, that's all the time we have tonight on Debut Profile. For Richard Miller, I'm Sharon Roberts saying good night. Good night!
[Scene ends.] "The Cavalier" Tom Knight ---------------------------- (Sharon Roberts is backstage as the Cavalier and Guinevere return from the match. Cavalier is breathing hard, but still has the competitive fire in his eyes. Guinevere's eyes harden as Sharon approaches.)
Sharon: What a great match, Tom! You certainly meant what you said. Sloan didn't stand in your way tonight. Now you've got a shot at what you've termed 'the Holy Grail,' the NAWC World Title.
Cav: Sloan, you talk on and on about 'educating' the men you wrestle. Well, you're not the only man in the NAWC with higher education, Sloan. Don't let the reflection off your diplomas blind you in the future. And
I suggest you learn some new words; that one is getting boring quickly.
As you discovered, I didn't come here tonight for lessons; I came tonight to win the World Title. You let your ego blind you into believing that tonight was your night of destiny. The fact is this: Al Capulco did not set this match up to give you an opportunity to win the World Title. He put you in my way to make it easier for Rio D. Giniro to defeat me tonight.
Sharon: You had a couple of opportunities to put Sloan away earlier, but you didn't pursue them. Why not?
Cav: Two reasons. The first is that Sloan needed to discover some humility. He didn't see me as a real opponent, he only saw me as someone he had to step over to get a chance at the World title. He thought that because he defeated Dr. Hate after I had lost to him, that he would easily defeat me and face Rio. That was his mistake, and he paid for it. The second reason was that I am sending a message to Rio and the president. The Cavalier will NOT be taken lightly by anyone, no matter who he is. Thane Sloan had no comprehension of what he was up against.
Sharon: Strong words for a lot of people. How do you feel about going up against the World champion in the main event now?
Cav: I will do exactly what I planned to do in that match. The NAWC president may think he's played out a winning hand by putting Sloan in my way. Unlike Sloan, though, I didn't take my unexpected opponent too lightly. I went into the ring with my goal clearly in focus, and that focus is still there. Now I will face a man who admittedly is a bigger and more dangerous opponent. It doesn't matter. Rio is fresh, while
I've just fought a tough match against a former World champion. It doesn't matter. Rio has the NAWC president literally in his corner. It doesn't matter. Line up your soldiers, Capulco, and place a dragon to guard the belt; none of it will matter, because they will all fall before me tonight!
Sharon: Sounds like you're more than ready for this. Cav: There is no doubt, no fear. Tonight I seek the glory, the treasure, the prize. My quest is coming to its conclusion, and I will emerge triumphant. Thane Sloan, Rick Stevens, Derek Davies...all have tried to stop me. They have failed. Rio D. Giniro, your time has come. The Cavalier will not be denied. And when the end approaches, and you find yourself struck down by the mightiest of weapons,
Excalibur, you will hear the referee's pronouncement of your doom, one-two-three, and you will understand that nothing could stand between the Cavalier and the World title tonight! And as my arm is raised in glorious victory, I shall bend my knee, and present the greatest of treasures to my true lady, the lovely Guinevere, and we shall stand together as champions of the world! LET THERE BE LIGHT!!
(Tom and Guinevere head for the locker room as Sharon watches them go. She turns back to the camera.) Sharon: Well, he certainly looks ready to me. We could be looking at a pay-per-view-quality main event tonight. Let's get back to the ring.
Killer Buddha -------------- INTERIOR - KILLER BUDDHA'S HOME - SUNDAY MORNING - 1/9/00 Killer Buddha, his daughter Hope Brando, Jay Bennetto and his wife
Marie, are in the Brando's dining area. Killer Buddha is reading a wrestling magazine, Jay is chewing on the end of an unlit cigar, and
Hope is playing with the hair of her Lady Guinivere action figure. Marie
Bennetto is busy running back and forth, bringing in food from the kitchen. On this Sunday
morning the Bennetto-Brando clan is being treated to one of Marie Bennetto's famous breakfasts. We can see ham, eggs, bacon, toast, biscuits, orange juice, and much more on the dining room table. Marie
Bennetto finally sits down, but noone eats.
MARIE BENNETTO: O.K. Hope, You can say "Grace". HOPE (with a sly smile): "Grace"! (She immediately grabs a biscuit as the grownups laugh.) JAY BENNETTO (puts his cigar down): Well "K", How's your neck and back holdin' up? KILLER BUDDHA: They're fine Uncle Jay. The Docs say I'm going to be O.K. JAY BENNETTO: How do ya' feel about losin' the belt? KILLER BUDDHA: Being the NAWC Intercontinental Champion for that brief time was great. I don't have the belt anymore, but that's O.K. I still feel like a Champion. I still have my honour, self-respect, and love for wrestling. Chris Phear saw his opportunity and grabbed it. I hope he wears that title with pride and integrity. Someday he will have to meet me and I will be Champion again.
JAY BENNETTO: Well "K", I'm glad to hear that you're feelin' so confident cause you're set to meet Tom 'The Cavalier" Knight this Friday in Missisipi. "K", you're gonna' be wrestling for the NAWC Heavyweight
Championship title. You're gonna have the chance to make your dreams come true. We need to study some of the videos we got on this guy so we can come up with a good strategy.
(Before Buddha can respond, Hope reacts.) HOPE BRANDO: Daddy- You're not going to hurt the Cavalier are you? You can't do that. He is always nice to me when we go backstage, and
Guinivere always lets me brush her hair and use her perfume. The Cavalier never says mean things about you. You can't fight him! KILLER BUDDHA: Hope, The Cavalier and I both believe that we're the best at what we do. If we wrestle against each other the world will finally know which one of us is truly a great Champion.
HOPE (upset): It's not right, Daddy. If you fight him he won't like me anymore. Hope gets up from the table and runs out of the dining area, clutching her Lady Guinivere doll. Marie Bennetto gets up to follow.
MARIE BENNETTO: She's 8 years old "K", She doesn't understand. I'll go talk to her. (Marie leaves.) JAY BENNETTO: Don't worry "K", Hope'll be fine. KILLER BUDDHA: I know Uncle Jay, but I understand what she's trying to say. Tom Knight is not Max Cayne or Derek Davies. Cayne, Davies, Rio, and Stevens are a bunch of snot sucking maggots that deserve to get clobbered. Hope sees
that those guys are mean and that they're liars. But me fighting The
Cavalier is confusing for her because if Cavalier is the White Knight then Daddy must be the Evil Dragon.
(Killer Buddha puts his fork down.) KILLER BUDDHA: The Cavalier is one of the most honourable and bravest wrestlers in the NAWC. When he first showed up I thought he was a phony, a liar, trying to trick us all. I was wrong. The Cavalier has proved himself to everyone. Tom Knight is one of the best mat technicians around. He has speed, agility, and raw power. He also has weaknessess;
and I'm definitely going to take his Achilles heels' into account when we make our action plan. I'm going to wrestle the Heavyweight Champion,
Uncle Jay. Win or lose it's a dream come true and I'll be remembering
Chris when I stand in that ring.
(Jay Bennetto gives his nephew a worried look.) JAY: It's not that Fruity-Patooty The Cavalier that has me worried. It's the fact that both you guys have enemies that would love to get you two in the ring so they could clobber you. "K", You're gonna' hafta be lookin' over your shoulder during this whole match. Those walking turds,
Al Capulco, Rio, Rick Stevens, Davies, and Cayne are out to get you both. You need to be careful.
(Killer Buddha nods his head in agreement.) KILLER BUDDHA: You're right Uncle Jay; That urine stain with a brain Al
Capulco, and his girlfreind Rio D. Giniro are pathological liars and cheats. Those two idiots can't be trusted at all. Jeez, Alka-Seltzer is so stupid that if he spoke his mind he'd be speechless for a year. Now
Rick Stevens seem to be going through some kind of psychotic episode.
What else could explain his teaming up with Alka-Seltzer and that piece of chicken plop
Rio "The Degenerate" Giniro. As for Derek Davies, Max Cayne, and those other Kangaroo Kissers. Those guys are so stupid that they spend twenty minutes a day staring at an orange juice carton because it says
"Concentrate" on it. I'll be careful, don't you worry about anything. (Jay laughs.) KILLER BUDDHA: I'm a warrior. Tom Knight is also a warrior. I don't hate this guy. I don't consider him my archenemy. I don't feel that he's a threat to my family in any way; but because we are Warriors, we must clash in the squared circle, our chosen field of honour. On Friday in
Missisipi, I will give the world a great match. I will seek to become a
World Champion by using all my skill and all my power. I will defend my honour, my integrity, and my love for this sport. Finally, I will fight to prove that I am great Champion. The Cavalier has "Excalibur", and he has "The Sword In The Stone"; but he doesn't have Fate in his pocket.
Neither of us can determine our destiny. The Cavalier will not defeat me this Friday. The Cavalier is going
to learn that Killer Buddha is the Once and Future King of Wrestling and that Violence Is The Only Answer.
(Killer Buddha starts to eat his breakfast.) JAY BENNETTO (smiling): Eat "K", and then we'll watch those tapes and set up some kinda' game plan.
(Fade To Black.) Chris Phear ------------ [Backstage, Chris Phear's locker room. The door opens and he can be seen sitting on a bench inside. Still in his wrestling gear. Chris's black hair is drenched with sweat. Much of it drapes over his eyes.
The Intercontinental title sitting in his lap. He gazes down at it. A small grin upon his face. He looks up.]
Chris: Didn't I tell you? That things would change? Though I was not aware of my match at the beginning of the night. But you will hear no complaints here. Killer Buddha, Franchise, whether you have realized it or not, that match was every man for himself. We may have been a team you and I. But what you must have failed to see, is that whoever got the pin, got the belt. Hence, I got the pin. And the IC belt is mine.
This was no accident either. They had a much larger weight advantage over the three of us. But what did they show for it? Absolutely nothing.
[Chris looks back down at the belt.] Chris: And didn't I tell you? I said I'd get revenge on Derek Davies. And I did. Derek Davies, you may have won the Television title from
Rick Stevens. But you overlooked me. And that was a big mistake. You tried to prevent me, from getting what rightfully was mine. But you failed. No doubt we will meet man to man some day Davies. Be it for the Television title. Be it for the IC title. Be it for no title.
That is irrelevant. When it is only you and I in that ring, your friends will not be able to help you. They will fail just as they have failed tonight. The Phear will kick in. And that will mean your demise. It is inevitable Davies.
[Chris lays the Intercontinental belt over his right shoulder. Its shiny gold plating reflecting a nearby light.]
Chris: My next match is against Long Tran. The NAWC is as new to you as it is to me Long. Yet I have been fortunate enough to get myself a title. We shall meet next Saturday Long. But contrary to what you may think now that I have won this title, the match will not be for the title. No Long. You are not a worthy opponent for me. Your
Austrailian background doesn't qualify you for this title. Instead, I'm challenging you Thane Sloan. Your abnoxious and arrogant attitude will soon cost you. So I'm not asking. I'm demanding that you fight me for the IC title at Nuclear Winter. So Sloan, be there, or miss out. And if I win that match at Nuclear Winter, then I'll give you Killer Buddha, a shot at what I have taken from you. Since you are the current former
Intercontinental champ, you will be a formidable opponent.
Chris: And let me conclude with you Thomas Knight. Mr. Cavalier. Your obvious lack of respect for me, the man that once helped you, from the goodness in my heart. I see you have recently won the World championship. On a recent NAWC televised show. Tom Knight, don't let the gold go to your head. Look at me. The current NAWC
Intercontinental champion. And the gold is not going to my head. It shall remain where it belongs. Either over my shoulder or around my waist. So Knight, what I'm getting at is this. The Friday before
Nuclear Winter. I want to face you. Because the only way to gain respect from you, is by beating you senseless myself. But let it be a non-title match. I have enough gold to last me for quite some time.
[Chris stands up from his bench, throwing his still soaked hair back. He leaves the room.] ANNOUNCEMENTS First of all there are four new additions to the NAWC roster. Welcome back Garret Goebbels, who signed up around the time Rick Stevens signed up. So Garret is returning to the NAWC. There are also three others;
Joe Mama, Floyd "Octopus" Malloy, and "Rocket Star" Perry Harrils. Next there are some changes to the upcoming card.
