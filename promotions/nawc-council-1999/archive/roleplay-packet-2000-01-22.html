Roleplay Packet

I have a two and a half hour class tomorrow morning. Tonight we hear from Killer Buddha, "The Cavalier" Tom Knight, Long S. Tran, Rio D.
Giniro, Rick Stevens, Claw Richens, and Killer Buddha again. A few may have been sent to me before the release of Part 3 of Napalm. Killer Buddha -------------- EXTERIOR - BUDDHA'S DRIVEWAY - WEDNESDAY - 1/19/00 - DAY Killer Buddha's black Van is parked in the driveway of the Brando home. Jay Bennetto, Buddha's business partner and Uncle is in the front seat of the Van trying to install a new CD player. Killer Buddha is standing nearby watching his Uncle at work. Both men are wearing grey sweatsuits and white sneakers. Jay has an unlit cigar in his mouth. Buddha still sports his black
tail, a much darker goatee, and his mirrored shades. Jay tests the CD player. The Van's speakers begin to blast. Springsteen's "Born In The
USA" fills the air.
JAY BENNETO: Alot of stuff happened at Napalm "K", and now you gotta get ready for your matches at Nuclear Winter. Are you ready for em'?
KILLER BUDDHA: Well I'll be training and meditating non-stop for the
Hardcore Match and the Tag Team Match. I'm ready for anything. I'm going to Bash and Smash all the pathetic punks and slimebags that have crossed my path. It's no more Mr. Nice Guy and my enemies are in trouble.
JAY BENNETTO: That maggot Chrissy Phear sure has a lot to Ying-Yang about lately. I can't wait for you to give that punk some Instant Karma.
KILLER BUDDHA: Chris Fart is a piece of trash. He won the Intercontinental belt, but fails to realize he never pinned me for the win. I was happy for him at first, he saw his chance and hit that ugly
Gorilla, Max Cayne, with a mighty blow: but now this little runt is bragging about how he screwed Killer Buddha out of the belt. This snot sucking fool needs to be "Enlightened".
(Jay turns the CD player off and continues to listen to his nephew.) KILLER BUDDHA: Chris "The Fart" Phear is an arrogant, deluded, no talent piece of sewer snot that will meet me in the ring sooner or later. As far as I'm concerned he is no Champion. I'm not impressed by his wrestling skill or his pathetic attempts to be a tough guy. Chris Fart is a slime spewing cockroach, and Killer Buddha is going to step on him real soon. When I finally get in the ring with that goop eating vermin,
I'm going to beat him within an inch of his miserable life and force him to apologize to me for not keeping his filthy face hole shut.
JAY BENNETTO: You're right "K", Chris Phear is a little punk, who let that belt get to his head. But you still gotta be careful. A young punk like that will cheat, lie, and steal to keep feelin' like a big man.
KILLER BUDDHA: Yeah, the runt doesn't realize that the belt doesn't make you a champ. It's something that only a few have in their heart and soul that truly makes you a Champion. Phear is not only arrogant, he's ugly and stupid too. Chris Fart is so ugly his pillow cries at night. This slimeball is so stupid, he put a quarter in a parking meter and waited for a gumball to come out.
(Jay cracks up and shakes his head; amused by Buddha's acid-tinged wit.) KILLER BUDDHA: I'm hoping that the NAWC bosses will put me in the ring with this punk-brat before he loses his belt. Killer Buddha is the only man in the NAWC that can defend that title with the fighting spirit it deserves. Chris Phear has no respect for me. So, from this moment on,
Chris Fart is my enemy. I will Bash and Smash his little pumpkinhead every chance I get.
JAY BENNETTO: That punk better watch out, it sounds like his days are numbered. (Jay turns the CD player back on.) (Fade To Black.) "The Cavalier" Tom Knight --------------------------- (Greg Hollinsworth is standing backstage after the Cavalier's win over
Killer Buddha. As the champion approaches, Greg steps forward.)
GH: Cavalier, congratulations on a huge win! You became the first NAWC
World Champion to retain his title. How do you feel right now?
Cav: Killer Buddha was a very strong opponent, and he gave me a tremendous battle. But it isn't size that counts in the ring, it's heart. Standing there in the ring tonight, with the fans cheering me on, and Guinevere at ringside supporting me, I knew that tonight was a night of destiny. Tonight I can call myself a true champion, for I have upheld a champion's most important duty: to defend that championship.
Killer Buddha, you have nothing to be ashamed of. You fought hard, and you fought well. But your dream of the World Title could not overcome my destiny.
GH: So what's next for the World Champion? Cav: That has yet to be determined. Guinevere will be meeting with
President Capulco and Mr. Palmer tomorrow. Perhaps I will be chosen to represent the NAWC in the Owen Hart Classic, an honor I would give up this title for without hesitation. Nuclear Winter is approaching, and certainly I expect to defend my title.
GH: Is there anyone in particular you'd be willing to grant a title shot? Cav: It doesn't matter who I meet in the ring; I'll defend this belt against anyone who the front office deems worthy. I know there are several men who deserve a chance to face me, including Rick Stevens and
Rio D. Giniro. Killer Buddha has also earned another chance. Thane
Sloan would like a rematch, I'm sure, but he is already committed to an
Intercontinental title
match. In fact, all the names I mentioned are already signed for matches at Nuclear Winter. We'll have to see what happens.
GH: But if you could pick one man to defend the belt against, who would it be? Cav: Rick Stevens. GH: Why him? Cav: We had a tremendous match recently, which could easily have gone either way. As it turned out, because of time, there was no winner.
Stevens earned my respect that night, and I would grant him a title shot if he asked for one. Perhaps our personal differences were settled, or at least partly resolved, but in the ring, nothing was decided. And so
I am offering this
challenge to Rick Stevens: you and I at Nuclear Winter for the World
Title, no time limit, and there must be a winner. Lady Divine and
Guinevere may be at ringside, but Rio D. Giniro and Al Capulco remain at the back. No tricks, no gimmicks, just you and I to determine which of us truly deserves this title.
GH: That's quite a challenge. But Stevens is already booked for the Tag
Team Title battle royal. That would give you quite the advantage if he lasted a long time during that match.
Cav: Then the choice is his. He can refuse the challenge and compete in the battle royal; he can accept the challenge and eliminate himself and
Rio D. Giniro from consideration for the tag team belts; or he can test himself to the limits of his endurance, fighting in both the battle royal and the World Title match. It's been done before; I have fought two matches in one night, both times for the World Title. Others have done so and won. It remains to be seen if Stevens will accept the challenge.
GH: Sounds like you've given him something serious to think about. Cav: Good. A man who thinks improves himself. GH: I'd like to take a moment to ask Guinevere a few questions, if you don't mind. Cav: By all means. GH: Guinevere, at Nuclear Winter you're competing in the Women's Heavyweight title battle royal against four other women. What are your thoughts on that? Guin: It should be an interesting challenge. I don't have the experience that some of these women might have in the ring, but I have trained hard and I feel I'm ready to compete. But I will make it clear that I'm not entering in this match to tiltillate the audience; I am going in to make a bid to become the first Women's champion in the NAWC.
GH: Well, with the World Champion standing next to you, you'd make quite the royal couple with those two belts side by side.
Guin: Very true, Greg. Lady Divine and I have some differences to work out, although I hope that the respect that developed between Tom and
Rick Stevens will trickle down to her and I. As for Hellfire, she is a dangerous woman with enough ring experience to make her a serious threat to win. I have no clue as to the abilities of Daring Denise and Foxxy
Angel, but I'm confident that I'll be able to deal with the situation.
GH: Now here's something that's on a lot of minds right now; what's going on with this 'triangle' between yourself, Tom Knight, and Sharon
Roberts?
Guin: There is no 'triangle' here, Greg. Sharon has fixated on Tom since we arrived in the NAWC, ignoring the fact of our very close relationship that has brought us to this level of accomplishment. Tom is a champion, but we are a team, and that is something Sharon Roberts simply can't break.
GH: Your thoughts on that, champ? Cav: Guinevere speaks truth; Sharon's flirtations have been amusing, but
I don't take them seriously. I have no intentions of breaking faith with this woman who has stood by me for this long.
GH: That's pretty straightforward, and a bit surprising considering today's wrestling climate.
Cav: Perhaps, but that is who we are. GH: Alright, folks, you've heard it here first: The World Champion, Tom
Knight, has tossed down the gauntlet to Rick Stevens, giving him the opportunity to go after the World Title at Nuclear Winter! That puts the ball in Stevens' court, and what a choice he has in front of him!
Also, Guinevere gets ready to challenge for the Women's title at Nuclear
Winter, a match that is sure to attract a lot of attention! Thanks for your time,
Tom, and Guinevere, and best of luck to you both.
(The camera fades to black and cuts to an ad for Nuclear Winter.) Long S. Tran -------------- He sits alone in the locker room. Although it is late, the door remains slightly ajar, and the only source of light is the one penetrating through the slightly ajar door. As the door slowly opens, it pans around the dark room. In the corner, the young Long Tran sits in disappointment. Not disappointment that he lost a match, but rather the fact that he could have done better.
Long S. Tran:- (Back to camera) What do you want? (Pause.) (Long turns around to stare at the camera. He is still in his wrestling gear, and his sweaty hair hangs in front of his face.)
Long:- Well...? Cameraman:- Uh, Long, Mr. Tran sir... I was kinda hoping, that...*gulp* you could...say...
Long:- Say a few words? Cameraman:- Uh, yes Mr. Long sir, a few words perhaps...? Long:- First of all, im only 19. And second of all, its not Mr Long, it's Mr Tran. But call me Long.
Cameraman:- Yes sir, uh, Long. Long:- I suppose you all wanna know how I feel about my match with
Chris Phear? What can I say? He won, I lost. No excuses.
Cameraman:- But it was a good match nonetheless. Long:- (Pause) Yeah, it was. Cameraman:- So now what's you plan for now? Long:- Uh... Can we do this interview another time? I'm a bit tired. Cameraman:- Uh, sure. The cameraman leaves the room as Long closes the door. After a few seconds, the door opens again, and Long is dressed in casual gear with a bag with hsi wrestling gear over his shoulder. Just as he closes the door behind him to leave the arena, he mumbles to himself...
Long:- *Why DIDN'T Phear put the title on the line?* Scene fades as Long Tran walks off camera Rio D. Giniro ------------- (Open outside the office of current NAWC President Al Capulco. From the opposite side of the door we can hear Capulco screaming at the top of his lungs.)
Al: WHAT IN THE NAME OF THE GREAT GOD ABOVE IS GOING ON HERE????? ONE
MINUTE ALL IS WELL... THE NEXT MINUTE THE WORLD IS TURNED UPSIDE
DOWN... THIS CRAP MUST END NOW...... (The camera crew open the door to see the office is a wreck. Papers are strewn out all over the floor. The chairs are flipped over. We see Lady
Divine cringing in a corner. As the door opens completly we now see the
Ex-World HeavyWeight Champion yet still all around great Rio D. Giniro standing with a downed look upon his face. Ex-TV Champion Rick Stevens is beside him with his head down. Suddenly Al Capulco comes storming across the room face redder than a ripened cherry.)
Al: You two have let me down... You are supposed to be te greatest this disfunctional fed has to offer... Yet you both go ahead and lose your titles in one night... Leaving us with NOTHING... What do you morons have to say for yourselves???? (Pause)... Forget it. Dont say a word...
There is nothing to say... I am the one who is here to do the talking. You two are supposed to perform... At least that is what I thought you were here for... Of course I could be wrong.... Maybe I just need to get myself some new talent... Some one who can get the job done... Not do the "Job"....
(Both Rio and Rick look up with irritated expressions on their faces at the last comment. Al walks over and stops in front of them staring directly at them.)
Al: Oh what did I upset you??? Well let me tell you two something. You are not even half as upset as I am right about now. So dont give me those looks....
(Al then tur andfinally notices the camera has been on him the entire time. His face get even more frustrated at this.)
Al: Oh and what the H@LL are you people here for eh???? To see the us at a once in a lifetime low point??? To show the peons out there what happens when "STRATEGIES" dont come through?? To try and show that we arent as strong as we claim to be???? Well think again...
(Al begins to snicker. He takes a few dep breathers and regains his composure. A slight grin comes across his face.)
Al: Let me tell you something since you are here. Yeah things may not have gone as planned... Yeah both Tom Knight and Derek Davies my have pulled of the most spectacular miracles of the new millenium... And that is just what all this garbage was.. Miracles of god... Just so you people know... These things are just minor setbacks.. For it is going to be just a matter of time before things are back to the way they should be... With the World Title back around the waiste of its rigtful owner..
(Slaps Rio on the chest).. That is this man right here.... And the TV
Title is right back where it belongs... (Looks over to Rick)... Home with Rick Stevens...
(Al walks over and takes a seat at his desk. He reaches into the drawer and pulls himself out a cigar and lights it up.)
Al: I will make sure that the travesties that have taken place are corrected... Everyone knows that these men should still be the champions today... But for some unknown reason MR PALMER felt otherwise... He has something against us.. Maybe it is just that I personally know how to control this fed better than he does... (Places his hand on his chin)...
Hmmmm... That might explain just why neither of these two have been granted a ReMatch for THEIR TITLES... Even though they deserve them....
Because PALMER knows that if the rematches were granted. The title woud be right back where they belong.. IN OUR HANDS.. Once again giving us the power... Which of course PALMER doesnt want....
(Al once again begins to chuckle as he takes a quick puff from his cigar.) Al: The thing is PALMER you forget that being the Pres I am still able to throw a few wrenches into the grind... And with that said let it be known that these men will have THEIR TITLES back one way or the other.... (Cracks a n evil grin)... I Have Spoken.... So it is Written..
So Shall it be Done...... (Al picks up the phone as the scene fades.) Rick Stevens ------------- {Camera Fade} [As the camera slowly fades in you notice a street deep in the heart of
Detroit, cars pass by as the sounds of the city can be heard all around. The camera pans down at the sidewalk as you hear footsteps getting closer.. and closer... and closer... as the sound stops the camera slowly pans up to Rick Stevens, who is dressed in black boots, old jeans and a NAWC t-shirt. Rick removes his sunglasses and prepares to speak]
Rick: So Dereck, slick, ya got my tv strap.. [Rick smirks] Rick: That's fine, ya see I have bigger fish ta' fry. Ya see slick, you thought you had the cards stacked against me. And truth be told on that night you _were_ the better chess player. But let it be known, it will not happen again. We are on the verge of a new era, a era that will redifine the NAWC for years to come. And Dereck, you and your goof troop have first row ringside seats for what I'm sure is a event you won't want to miss.
[Rick opens the door and starts to walk in, he turns to face the camera] Rick: Ya see I've been here since the beginning, and I'll be damned if
I'll let some punk such as yourself call the shot. So Dereck don't blink because you never know just what might happen.
[Rick enters the building as the camera fades] Claw Richens -------------- [Scene opens in a gym. Hard bodies are everywhere you look, training on every type of weight equipment you could possibly imagine. The tune in the background, some pop song, plays oblivious to the work around it. The camera zooms around the gym here and there until it focuses in on Claw Richens, sitting on a weight bench doing single arm curls. He is wearing a white T-Shirt reading "Soul Survivor
98 - Only One Shall Survive" and black gym shorts. He is seen counting to himself until he is distracted by the form of a lady that walks by.
She is wearing a sports bra and tight shorts. He smiles to himself until he feels a slap against the back of his head.]
Voice : Enjoying the view a little too much, Claw? [Claw turns around to see another female form, this one more familiar. She is wearing a green sports bra and loose shorts reading "Live Alaska" on the side. He smiles upon seeing her and puts the weight down for a second.] Claw : Hey Nessa, what's up? Vanessa Green : Not too much. Some guy over there was making faces at me... kinda like the one you were just making at her, so I decided to come over here.
[Claw blushes a little bit.] Claw : Well, ya know. I was just.. um... Vanessa : Yeah, I know what you were doing. [She chuckles.] Vanessa : How much ya got on there? Claw : I started with about 55, I'm at 80 right now. Vanessa : Wow, nice! Who's on the line up this week? Claw : Well, the NWWA is still in turmoil, so I just have a match in one fed - the NAWC. Damien Douglas is his name I believe.
Vanessa : Seen or heard anything about him? Claw : Nada. Seems this guy just sort of popped out of nowhere. Vanessa : Are you ready for him? Claw : About as ready as I'll ever be. Just glad this isn't a title match, cause I would hate to defend against a guy I've never seen before. I've already got enough problems on my hand with Moore at the
PPV. Vanessa : I was watching Moore fight, he seems really good. Claw : I was lucky to come out of there with the belt last time. Especially since I'm not even a lightweight. Vanessa : Yeah, but you saw the scale too. It said 239. That's lightweight. Claw : That scale was wrong. I weigh 256, I have ever since I took time off last year. I can't possibly weigh 239. But I guess the way I figure it - if they're gonna give me the lightweight belt, then I'm gonna bulk up and be the biggest lightweight in the history of the division!
Vanessa : Oh, I heard something you might be interested in. Claw : Oh yeah, what's that? Vanessa : Long S. Tran has his sights on your gold. Claw : What's new. [Claw sighs.] He's probably mad cause he knows I'm not a lightweight. Well hell, if he wants to get into the same ring with me, ONE MORE TIME, and let me prove to him once and for all that I can beat him 1, 2, 3, then that's fine with me.
Vanessa : Don't tell me you don't accecpt Tran as a real competitor. You know what he can do with that cartwheel kick of his, don't you? Claw : I'll tell you this, I've got more important things on my mind right now: SCHEDULED matches. Tran needs to get in touch with Palmer if he wants a match. I've got Donovan more and Damien Douglas to worry about. And after I've beaten them, 1, 2, 3, the way I always do it...
that's when I'll worry about Long S. Tran. [Vanessa smiles as Claw takes off the weights and puts on new ones - 90 pounds.] Vanessa : That's a lot for one arm. So how does it feel to be undefeated again? Claw : Pretty damn good. And I'm going to stay that way for just as long as I did last time. I must have had the longest winning streak in the history of the NWWA back then. And I can do it again. I always do.
Vanessa : You never cease to amaze me, Claw. [She gives him a boyish smile.] Vanessa : I'm gonna take off. You wanna hang out tonight? Claw : Yeah, sounds good to me. Give me a call later. [Claw watches as she walks off. The second she's out of sight, his eyes return to the lady he was watching before Nessa walked up. He smiles, and begins lifting. Scene closes.]
Killer Buddha -------------- INTERIOR - KILLER BUDDHA'S DRESSING ROOM - FOLLOWING NAPALM Killer Buddha has a towel around his neck and his hands are in a gigantic bucket of ice water. Buddha is exhausted after his bout with
The Cavalier tonight. He is also very frustrated because he lost a chance at becoming the NAWC Heavyweight Champion. The small statue we have come to know as "Little Buddha" is on a bureau in the background.
KILLER BUDDHA: Cavalier, you are still the NAWC Champion. I hit you with everything I had and you still managed to use Excalibur against me.
Don't get too comfortable though, sooner or later you will meet Killer
Buddha again and the outcome will be different. I am going to be the
NAWC Heavyweight Champion someday.
(Killer Buddha takes his hands out of the ice water and smiles.) KILLER BUDDHA: Mr. Knight, you are now the mountain I have to climb, the dark road I must travel upon, you are the world I must conquer.
Remember, Killer Buddha is the Once and Future King of the NAWC,and one day I will claim my crown and prove to you that Killer Buddha is a true
Champion.
(Killer Buddha picks up his golden statue.) KILLER BUDDHA: I swear by "Little Buddha" that I will become an invincible force, an ultimate weapon, the embodiment of fire and fury.
All men will fear me, all Champions will regret that they challenged me. I am Killer Buddha and Violence Is The Only Answer!!! (Fade To Black.)
