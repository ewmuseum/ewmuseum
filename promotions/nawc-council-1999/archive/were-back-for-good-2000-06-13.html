We're Back for Good

13 June 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Greetings to all. The NAWC (North American Wrestling Council), now located at http://nawc2.virtualave.net , is returning to the net. On this time, we're back for good! With our first card, Return to Greatness, scheduled for the 23rd of this month, it's time to begin the search for top quality handlers to add to our roster. Let me tell you a little about the NAWC.
We hold two weekly cards called Tuesday Night Massacre and Saturday Night
Live Wire. Massacre is a summary show whereas Live Wire will be a fully written play-by-play show.
We are an e-mail fed and the majority of our handlers strongly believe that "e-mail is better." We have four divisions total: heavyweight, lightweight, women's, and tag team. With that in mind, we are looking for people who fit in all of these divisions, but more-so in the tag team and women's divisions.
We've got such well knowns among us as: Rick Stevens, "Top Gun" Ben Tuckwell, "Bloodhound" Gunther McKnight, Jonathan Hammer, and Jason "The
Bandit" Blake. We are also working on a farm fed system with the MAWL ( http://mawl.virtualave.net ) So we'll review all applications to the NAWC and refer them to the MAWL where handlers will have to prove their worth and dedication before being accepted into the NAWC. We also encourage you to join the MAWL as it is a great e-mail fed as well.
For more information, please contact me at this address ( [detail withheld] ) or the NAWC Committee at [detail withheld] I hope to see you in the MAWL and NAWC very soon. And believe me when I say, you won't regret joining.
[name withheld] - NAWC Owner http://nawc2.virtualave.net or http://nawc.cjb.net
