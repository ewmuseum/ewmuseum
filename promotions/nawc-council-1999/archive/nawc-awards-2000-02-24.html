No RP Packet Today Plus the NAWC Awards

24 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
There's no packet today. I didn't receive any roleplays since Monday before that packet. But, that's obviously because we're still in the middle of
FNF. But, to make up for that, I've compiled the results of the NAWC Awards and you'll get them 2 days earlier. Right now in fact. So here they are.
THE NAWC AWARDS Best Face - "The Cavalier" Tom Knight (Unanimous vote) Best Tweener - Killer Buddha Best Heel - Al Capulco Favorite Wrestler - "The Cavalier" Tom Knight Most Hated Wrestler - The Green Mountain Trooper Best Roleplayer - "The Cavalier" Tom Knight Gives the Best Interview - "The Cavalier" Tom Knight Best Technician - "The Cavalier" Tom Knight Best Hardcore Wrestler - Rick Stevens Best High Flyer - Jason "The Bandit" Blake Best Submission Wrestler - Garret Goebbels Best Brawler - Killer Buddha Gotten the Most Heat - Al Capulco Best Setup Move - Nirvana Smash Best Finisher - Excalibur Most Interesting Feud - Speedy Irons Vs. The Green Mountain Trooper Best Commentator - "Smokey" Joe Reynolds Best Interviewer - Sharon Roberts Best Non-Title Match - Nobody agreed at all. Therefore, I have declared the winner to be, Rick Stevens Vs. Garret Goebbels (Falls Count Anywhere match -
First NAWC Fight Night Friday)
Best Title Match - "The Cavalier" Tom Knight Vs. Killer Buddha Most Promising Up-and-Comer - Jason "The Bandit" Blake Best Lightweight Champion - TIE: Claw Richens & Donovan Moore (My tiebreaker vote didn't effect this at all)
Best Television Champion - Jason "The Bandit" Blake Best Intercontinental Champion - Killer Buddha Best World Heavyweight Champion - "The Cavalier" Tom Knight Best Overall Champion - "The Cavalier" Tom Knight [name withheld] http://nawc.cjb.net
