North American Wrestling Council F.A.Q.

19 January 2000

ABOUT THE NAWC a) History: The NAWC, short for North American Wrestling Council, opened on October 20, 1999. Three friends decided to put the fed together and make something special. From the start, it could be assumed that one man was putting in the full effort while the others were just in the background. That one man was [name withheld]. On his own, he built our website, hired the members, and booked the cards. Since then, he hired a booker to help him out as well.
b) Real Staff: [name withheld] - Owner - Palmer doesn't get involved in any of the shows. He's the guy that signs the checks and hires or fires wrestlers. You don't want to be in this guy's bad books. He can make your career miserable.
Derek Schilling - Vice President - Schilling is a quiet man. He has the ability to add or change matches at will.
Booker Extreme - Head Booker - The Head Booker has the ability to add or remove matches from any card. He can also change stipulations, etc for matches if he wishes.
c) Fictional Staff: Al Capulco - President - The President is the man in charge of shows. The manager of Rio D. Giniro, don't cross him or he'll set Rio loose on you.
Brent Jacobson - Ring Announcer - Brent is strictly business. He is a very cowardly announcer as he'll run off if a wrestler even looks at him.
Jenny Winston - Commentator - Jenny is a beautiful woman of around 30 years. She is a true feminist and is also a fan of the good guys.
"Smokey" Joe Reynolds - Commentator - Smokey is a true face commentator. He always roots for the good guys. But will call a match down the middle all the same. He is very opinionated and heel wrestlers often threaten him.But Smokey just keeps going. He's not afraid to speak his mind.
Gerry McKinley - Commentator - Gerry is a solid heel commentator. He always roots for the heels. He has a big mouth and often gets in trouble for what he says. He is very cocky. He is the Jerry Lawlor of the NAWC. He is well known for his color commentary at PPVs.
Doc Draven - Head Referee - Doc is a longtime veteran of wrestling. He knows the rules inside out. He gives people a hard time for cheating and interference. However, he'll let matches go on. He will count a pin at an average pace. And if knocked out, he will not disqualify anyone when he recovers. While counting the pin, he knows the exact spot to see all cheating attempts.
Kevin Bryant - Backup Referee - Bryant is mostly used for the Hardcore matches. He is a young referee that loves the newer styles of wrestling. Occasionally, when properly motivated, he'll side with a certain person or group and make them win at all costs.
Orlando Palla - Reporter - Orlando likes to interview the heel wrestlers more than anyone. He is very perverted and will often try to sneak into the women's locker rooms to get a peek at them.Valets beware.
Sharon Roberts - Reporter - Sharon is another feminist like Jenny Winston. She gets along better with the faces and fears the heels. She is often known to flirt with the faces. They like her and are usually willing to give an interview. Sharon is a very attractive woman and is extremely popular with the men. Valets often get jealous and fear that she will take their men away. This can often cause friction between her and the women of the NAWC.
Gregory Hollinsworth - Reporter - Hollinsworth is a wrestling buff. He wrestled professionally in the late 70s. A fan of traditional style wrestling, Gregory favors interviews with face wrestlers who are against the hardcore matches. If he is assigned an interview with a heel, he is often known to run off in terror.
2. CARDS a) About: Shows are sent out in segments. Each show can last a number of days. Usually, two to three segments are sent out a day. Weekly Shows are usually over by Monday. Pay-Per-Views can range any length longer. We hold shows all over the country, and outside the U.S. Even though we are based in the U.S., and our name implies North America, it does not mean that we don't have cards outside North America. We just hold them less frequently outside the States.
b) Weekly Shows: We hold one card weekly. It is called Fight Night Friday and is obviously held on Fridays. It may continue on until Sunday or Monday though. We also have a House Show on the week before a Pay-Per-View. This is due to two things. First, we don't want cards to overlap each other. And second, we want to give our wrestlers some time to concentrate on preparing for the Pay-Per-View.
c) Pay-Per-Views: Originally, I had planned to have Pay-Per-Views held once a month. But, while writing our first PPV, Injustice For All, I have realized that that does not give us enough time to develop feuds and angles. Therefore, PPVs will be held at irregular intervals. I will decide their names as the time comes.
3. ROLEPLAYING a) DOs: Roleplay at least once a week. Write more than ten lines. Send your roleplays to me (email address given when you join). b) DON'Ts: Swear more than what is said in the WWF. Use sexual content in your roleplays. Mention "real wrestling federations" such as the WWF, WCW, or ECW in your roleplays Mension other e-federations in your roleplays. Mention past titles you've won in other federations. We don't care here. You start out new and you have to work your way up.
Use e-wrestling or insider terms in your roleplays such as, "jobber, e-fed, roleplay, angle, feud," etc.
Don't use common catch phrases found in the WWF, WCW, or ECW. Such catchphrases include The Rock's "Do you smell what the Rock is cookin?" The Rock's "Know your role, and shut your mouth, " "jaboni" and the like.
c) Tips: Be creative. An uncreative roleplay would be something like, "Jobber's music plays. Jobber comes down to the ring with a mic. Jober says, "Hey, one of you wrestlers in the back! Come out here and i'll kick your ass!!!" Jober leaves the ring and goes to the back." These are the typical style of 12 year old handlers. Don't fall to that level. Also, set the scene in your roleplay. Tell us where your wrestler is. What he is doing. What's going on around him. Things such as these will make your roleplay more realistic and believable.
4. ANGLES / FEUDS We value angles and feuds highly in the NAWC. If you have a suggestion for a feud or angle, make sure to email me and the other handler in question about it. We'll go over it and if it sounds good, I'll incorporate it into the cards. Be original with your feuds and angles. Don't rip off other feds ideas. And, if you're in a feud or angle with somebody, and they win. But you roleplayed better, don't complain about it. Your loss furthered the angle and you'll usually get payback somewhere in the future against that same person.
5. MISCALLANEOUS If you would like more information regarding our promising new federation, please visit our website at http://nawc.cjb.net All the information you'll need will be there.
