[The promo for Fight Night Friday begins. Metallica's Fight Fire With
Fire plays in the background. The promo starts off with clips of an old match between Hulk Hogan and Andre the Giant. Then it shows clips from
Mike Tyson's second fight against Evander Holyfield. It shows a close-up of Tyson biting Holyfield's ear. Next, it shows some clips from the ECW. Finally, flames shoot towards the screen and the words
"NAWC Fight Night Friday appear behind it. The music stops and the scene cuts to the packed arena. The camera pans around the lively audience. Signs can be seen everywhere. Suddenly, an explosion can be heard. The camera cuts to a shot of the entranceway. It's a large steel skeletal frame holding the black curtain down. The curtain has the letters "FNF" on it. Still viewing the entrance. The entrance area leads out to a large stage. A walkway connects from it to the ring apron. Guardrails line the sides of the walkway to prevent fans from interfering in a wrestler's entrance. The camera moves around the other sides of the ring. Commentators' Table. The camera cuts to a close-up of our commentators. A man sits in the left seat and with a woman to the right of him. The man is wearing a dark blue suit, white shirt, and tie. The woman is wearing a red dress. She has long blonde hair. Another explosion can be heard. The camera cuts to a shot of the entrance.
