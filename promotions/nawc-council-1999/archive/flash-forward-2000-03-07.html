NAWC (1999) — Card Archive

Flash Forward

7 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
It's a small packet tonight due to we're still in FNF. That's understandable though. Tonight we have "Rocket Star" Perry Harrils and
"Bloodhound" Gunther McKnight & "Suicide" Lacey Fernandez.
"Rocket Star" Perry Harrils ------------------------------------- [ The scene opens in front of the locker room moments after the match between Perry Harrils and Richard Miller. The door is closed, but lots of cracking sounds and voices from Careen and Perry can be heard from the inside of the locker room. Gregory Hollinsworth appears with his usual work attire and a mic on his hands. He knocks the door waiting for an answer.
Careen answers the door. She seems surprised and tired ] Gregory Hollinsworth: Careen ? My name is Gregory Hollinsworth, I'm a reporter from the NAWC. Can we talk with Perry Harrils for a moment ?
Careen: I don't think this is a good time for an interview Gregory. Perry isn't in good emotional conditions to talk right now...
[ Sounds of locker door being hit could be heard ] GH: I see. It seems that he was really affected by his lost tonight against
Richard Miller. Well, thanks anyway Ms. Lizmarc, we can try to some other time.
Careen: I can do the interview in his name if you want. I know exactly how
Perry is feeling right now. It won't be the same thing without Perry, but I will try to do my best.
[ Gregory looks at Careen. He seems to be thinking. He signs for the camera to go back to the locker room ]
GH: Well, I think we can make something out of it. So Careen, Perry Harrils has just suffered an unfair lost tonight, and he is obviously unhappy with the results of the match. Are you two going to do something about that ?
Careen: Yes, we are seriously thinking about that. We all saw that Richard
Miller was cheating, and even so, the referee didn't saw it, so he gave the undeserved win to Miller. We are really angry about that and we will talk with the autorities of the NACW about that, trying to reverse the decision of the match, or at least, a fair rematch. I just hope we can be listened at least this time.
GH: Cheating seems to happen all the time Careen, I'm not so sure if the autorities of the NACW will listen to you two...
Careen: We are aware of that. But even so, we will try. One thing is for sure: Richard Miller won't get away with that cheap win so fast. With or without a fair decision, he will get what he deserves.
GH: These are very serious coments Careen, Richard might be seeing this interview. Careen: I just hope he does. The man is crazy. We can expect anything from crazy guys like him. It was expected that he would do some cheating anyway, but I never tought it would cost Perry the match.
GH: And what about Mojo Rising ? He seems to have assaulted after the match. Careen: He is another freak. No wonder it is in Miller's side. Thank god
Perry was there. [ The loud sounds from the inside of the locker room had stopped ] GH: It seems that Perry has calmed down a bit now. Careen: Yeah. I hope he did. Perry can really get mad after getting screwed up like this. I will see how he is.
[ Careen enters the locker room and closes the door in front of Gregory
Hollinsworth. A few moments later the door opens again and from out of it comes Perry with Careen Lizmarc. He is a lot calm now ]
GH: Hello Perry, I'm Gregory Hollinsworth, reporter of the NAWC. I was just talking with Careen.
Perry: Yeah, she told me so. GH: We were talking about Richard Miller and his cheap win. It was a really shocking result for all of us, afterall, he cheated, and everybody saw it except the referee.
Perry: That's right. Richard Miller was really cheap tonight. He just noticed this wasn't going to be his night so he used that foreign object on me. It is guys like him who really piss me off. And now he probably thinks that he is going to get away with it. That is, if he is mentally well enough to think about anything straight. Richard Miller may be big, he isn't two. I would say that I'm half of his size and even so, I can take him down. I've promised myself that I would beat him, and I will, no matter how long it takes. Sooner or later, he will fall. I promise.
GH: Are you going to do anything about that ? Perry: We will try to talk with someone who got autority in the NAWC to review the match and make a decision about it. I don't care who. The commisioner, the vice president, hell, even Mr. Palmer himself ! Whoever that have a small piece of dignity will take a look at the match and see how
Richard Miller cheated. It's all on the tapes, watch it !
Perry: Let's get out of here Careen, all this mess is making me sick. [ Perry and Careen leaves the arena ] GH: Perry just gave us impressive comments about his recent lost. The question is: will anybody in the NACW listen to Perry ? We will know in the next days.
[The screen fades out] "Bloodhound" Gunther McKnight & "Suicide" Lacey Fernandez ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------- <The scene opens to a small corridor. Doctors and Nurses can be seen rushing in and out of rooms, quickly slamming the doors shut to the rooms and latching them tightly. The camera suddenly turns onto another hallway, this one is different, but still very hectic. People, wearing white hospital robes, roam the hallways, they look frantically at the camera as they run to corners and hide... Some of them run into their rooms and slam the door shut.>
<The camera man suddenly turns to another room, as it sneaks in on four people, there is a woman, and three men. Two of the men in the room wear the same types of robes that were worn by the people in the hall, the third
man sits with a long black trench coat on. They are sitting watching a tape of some kind, it is ending, as the only female in the room pops in another tape. The rooms sits still for a second, before the video begins to play.>
*Video* Smokey: We're back folks. Up next we have a Women's Division matchup between Hellfire and "Electric" Amy Sparks.
*Video* Woman: Hold on, let's get to the good part.... <The woman presses fast forward the NAWC Fight Night Friday Tape. One of the men in the robes looks back. He taps the man in the trench coat on the shoulder. The man turns around slowly revealing a small scar on his face.
He let's a smirk out from his grim face. He looks at the man, and then turns around pointing to the television, where the woman pushes the play button.> Jenny: Yeah but she's trying to bear it! She pulls Hellfire up!! Tornado
DDT!!!! She's going to the top turnbuckle now!! She jumps off!! Shock
Therapy!!!!
Smokey: She got it!!!! A corkscrew moonsault into a legdrop!!!! That was amazing!!! She is hooking Hellfire's leg for the pin!!
1 ............ 2 ................ 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Smokey: AMY SPARKS WINS!!! SHE'S THE NUMBER ONE CONTENDER FOR THE WOMEN'S TITLE!!! <The woman snickers to herself as she turns to the three men. She looks at one of the men in the robes, this one has black spiked hair with the tips bleached, and begins to speak.>
Woman: So what ya think Stephan? I mean really and truly... This "Electric" Sparks chic hasn't shown me a damn thing... She beat some other bitch from the federation, and now she's the number one contender, but the truth of the matter is I could've beat either one of em hands down right...
Stephan: Yeah, Lacey... I mean, really and truly you could probably handle each and everyone of those guys. Too bad for Gunther we haven't seen any of those other guys. Except for that friggin mystery man who kidnapped that
Lady Bovine or something like that.
<The camera man suddenly steps in realizing he's in the right place.> Camera: Um, you guys are Bloodhound and Suicide.... or Lacey and Gunther which ever you'd prefer.
<The woman turns suddenly at the camera, smiling as she looks at the camera.> Suicide: Yes, it's us. We did tell you the right room number didn't we? <The camera man's reaction is delayed as he is staring at the beautiful woman's chest.> Camera: ....Oh, um, uh... yes. You did, you did. I just wasn't sure if I was in the right place.
Bloodhound: Yeah your in the right place, anyways the reason we wanted you to come here is to meet some dear friends of ours, a living testament to what becoming one of the Fallen can do for you. These men are Rondell and
Stephan, but they are known to you as Uzziel, he's the one with the long blong hair, and Azazel, he's the one with the spikes.
Uzziel: Hi. Azazel: Hello. Camera: Um, hello. That's all nice and well Bloodhound but the point of this interview was for me to talk to you, and well... Suicide.
<Bloodhound rises from his seat with a very harsh look in his eyes.> Azazel: Hey, BH... It's alright, don't worry about it. Me and Uz are used to that kind of bullshit in here.
Bloodhound: Alright, you sure. Well, we'll see ya later... <Bloodhound hugs the man, and then the other, as Suicide does the same. They signal to the camera man as they begin to walk out of the room. Bloodhound walks silently, as Suicide hugs and shakes with a few of the crazies as she is walking right behind him. The camera man turns back for one last shot of Uzziel and Azazel as he follows the couple. They slowly walk out of an exit into a parking lot. The camera is engulfed with a bright white light, as the glaring sun bounces off of the roof of a car in the parking lot. The camera fades to black.>
<Only a couple seconds later, the scene fades back into the insides of green
Jaguar. Bloodhound sits in the driver's seat, Suicide sits in the back she is leaning forward playing with the radio. The camera man sits in the passengers seat. Bloodhound pushes a button above him as the sunroof goes back, then he puts on a pair of Sunglasses as he cranks the car. Suicide gets what she wants as she slips a Metallica CD into the CD player. She sits back as she puts on a pair of sunglasses, and begins to dig into a bag in the floor board. Bloodhound looks at the camera man as he puts it into reverse and they pull out of the parking lot. Bloodhound waits patiently and then moves into the road at the stop sign. Bloodhound pulls a cigarette from his pack in the glove compartment lights it, and offers one to the camera man. The camera man shakes it off, then Bloodhound puts it back as he begins to speak.>
Bloodhound: So, this NAWC stuff, how long has it been around... not that you would know or anything just thought I'd ask a rhetorical question.
Well, as I see the NAWC right now I'm gonna walk in... take the title, and walk right back out. Because the NAWC is not on my level, and personally I don't think they ever will be. In years past I have been known to all as the "Fallen Angel" and so it shall remain, but as the "Fallen Angel" of the
NAWC I find it to be my soul purpose to lead all of the rest of those fallen ones who have not found what they are looking for. In the souls of many men here I find that it is very possible that they are lost. The possibility that they aren't where they want to be, where they could be right now in their careers. So, as the leader of those who are fallen, or lost, in their ways here on earth let the spawn of hell rejuvinate your soul and give you a purpose to rise into the sky above us. The sky that I have been eternally forbade from.
<Bloodhound puffs on the cigarette, flicking his cherry off out the window.>
Bloodhound: It is my soul purpose to allow the ones around me to rise, and in time I to shall rise. When the time comes for me to stop helping the fools surrounding, I will know it and it is at that time that I will rise to become the top of the food chain. It is then that I will take what is mine.
The things that are ours, the gold, the money, the fame, and everything else that goes with the best of the best. For now I stand in my original position, the Master of the Hounds, the Fallen Angel, and the King Nothing.
I am lost in yet another world of turmoil, and today I say my first piece to this eternal enigma of this world that you call earth. The enigma's pieces must come together eventually, and on that day, life as we know it will be absolutely nothing.
<Bloodhound pulls at the cigarette again, as he blows the smoke towards the camera. Bloodhound then looks to Suicide who closes a mirror as she blots her lipstick in with a tissue.>
Suicide: This time it will be different, that time it was gonna be different. Next time we'll be better, next time he'll be the best. I've heard it all before, and unfortunately for you fools... you will hear it for a hell of a long time. The last couple of times we've tried and failed... natural for two people who cannot do even the simplest of simplicity. We can't make the best out of something that is already the best. You see myself and Gunther have wandered through many places, and in many different ways. Always being lost in our ways and never finding the right way to straighten things out. But it wasn't always just the two of us.
<Suicide wipes sweat from her brow, as she straightens her bra strap.>
Suicide: It used to be four of us, and of course the dogs. Me and Hound got that big mansion, because of his crazy idea of becoming like 'Cactus, moving to Truth or Consequences, which I must say is the biggest piece of bullshit town I've ever lived in. Bloodhound and myself have lived their for about 4 years now, he wasted all his money, and we just can't get anyone to buy it... that might be because it's in the middle of nowhere. But who
knows? Anyway... those two you just saw... Azazel, Uzziel. They used to hang around with us. Maine and South Carolina their home states, but if you knew em' like we did you wouldn't have a doubt in your mind that they were brothers. They used to be one of the best, the Perfectly Crazy place did to them what it should have done to alot of us. It made them lose their minds, and fortunately the majority of us who had been brainwashed for so long finally broke the barrier and got out. But it drove those to insane.
<Suicide has a tear well up in her eye.> Suicide: It just drove them past what they could take, they went and screwed up their lives one night... alchohol and weed just didn't mix well that night... They killed two people, in a car wreck... when they killed those two people they just couldn't stand it anymore, they plead insane, and they won... they are insane, just not the same people as we used to know.
Stephan, Rondell... get better, we know what your going through. <Bloodhound pulls the camera back to look at him as Suicide throws her face into her hands.>
Bloodhound: Ok, the point is, that every walk of life that we have ever been in, we failed in. Bloodhound, Gunther... whatever you please, I've never been as successful as I should be and this... this place, you call the NAWC, should be known as BRC... Bloodhound's Redemption Center. Tonight is the beggining of what will be a contractual marriage to die for. As the
Master of the Hounds, King Nothing, and The Fallen Angel in one is here now before you... I swear upon my last day that I will have the championship
belt of the NAWC. A dead man can do anything he wants to. <Bloodhound laughs as the camera fades to black.> There was another one but I felt it would be better for that one to come out after the close of FNF. It was by Dao Shogun.
Good work everyone. [name withheld]
