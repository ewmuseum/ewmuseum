Keep in mind that a few of these might have been sent to me before I put out the part that they are refering to. Tonight we hear from Speedy Irons,
Brimstone and Hellfire, Killer Buddha, Killer Buddha again, Killer Buddha a third time, and Dynamite Derrick.
Speedy Irons ------------------ _ _ _\PEEDY IRON_\ ============== *Speedy Irons arrives at the Arrowhead Pond in Anaheim. Fans are cheering him on as he's about to enter the building. He's in blue jeans, wearing a white jersey of the Mighty Ducks of Anaheim. He also has his infamous cyan-green bandana with in silver Speedster. His black sports-bag is slung over his right shoulder.*
Speedy: "Anaheim! The Speedster is in..." *Fans yell "TOOOOOOOOOOOOOOWN!!!"* Speedy: "That's right! The Arrowhead Pond in Anaheim, home of the Mighty
Ducks!" *Fans cheer loudly!* Speedy: "Tonight another STAR will suffer the IRON DROPPER!!! 'Rocket Star' Perry Harrils, I respect you, dude! You're right, you're in the same style as I'm in... but I've got WAY MORE CLASS!!!"
*The Speedster pauses.* Speedy: "Perry, when I'm done with you, you're nothing more than a silly
FIRECRACKER!!! Because the Speedster can rock with the big guys and the small fast ones! Why? Because I'm faster and smarter than anyone in the
NAWC! Perry tonight you'll face all corners an kiss 'em! When I think you got enough the fastest rising youngster in the world of wrestling today is gonna lay you out with the IRON DROPPER! You think you can keep up with me,
Harrils? Prove it in that squared circle! Tonight it will be proven ONCE
MORE that I'm hot and the rest in the NAWC is DEFINITELY NOT!!! Good luck,
Perry!"
*The Speedster enters the Arrowhead Pond, screen fades out.* Brimstone & Hellfire ---------------------------- [As the scene fades into few, all that can be seen is fog covering the ground. Darkness is all around. However, the sky is white with clouds. The fog clears as the camera moves upwards and one can see snow falling all around. The camera zooms in on a building which is where the man behind the camera is appearing to be headed. It is very dirty and evil looking on the outside. The screams of many dead can be imagined coming out from inside of the demonic structure. As the camera gets closer to the door, it swings open with great force. Now the imagined screams become louder and real. A red hue is coming from underneath all of the doors. The man moves down the hall and with every step he takes , the floor yells with the same sound of fear and anguish as the people who have lived in this Insane Asylum. He starts to go up steps that lead to the room of death, as many people who know of this place call it. At the top of the stairs, he looks down a long hall way to this room. Suddenly the lights that were swinging with light and hope above his head all turn off and the only light he has to go by is the red light coming from under those doors. He walks down the hall to the room that he was instructed to go to by his employers. As soon as he opens the door, the camera lens fogs up because of the heat that pours onto it from the room. He wipes it off as he looks for the two which roam and live in these halls as they have all their lives. As he turns around the picture turns dark and he pans upward to see the faces of the employers, known to him as Master and
Mastress. They are more known as Brimstone and Hellfire. They motion for him to stand against the wall, all of which are covered with white blood stained pads, facing the window which is covered with bars. Hellfire takes Brimstone by his arm and goes and stands next to the window and stares out.]
{Hellfire} "Brimstone, do you remember the days in winter when they used to let us out and sit in the snow for hours on end?"
{Brm} "Yes. Why?" {HF} "They used it as a punishment as they called it. All it did was add to our overwhelming power. The bite of the wind against our bodies, the blood coming from our bodies which froze to the ground. I remember all this very vividly. But we lived through it because we are stronger than any normal human being ever could or even should be. We are freaks and mistakes of nature. We must be because we got told that many, many times by our pieces of shit that we had to call mom and dad because if we didn't they threated to send us away. At least it was that way for me. Finally I decided my life was too good for them and I tried to end it. They found me laying in front of my house face down in the snow laughing because of my pleasure of my great insanity. When they turned me over, they found out what my insanity, known as them and the rest of the world, had caused me to do. The crimson was all over the snow and my neck and wrists had been cut open with my own fingernails, as they later found out. That was when they decided that I was not going to get any better by sitting in my room alone for the rest of my life as they had made me do from the first day the light touched this body which carries the one of the darkest souls on the face of the earth. They sent me here. Windy City Mental Institute. The people here were no better.
They made me suffer more than I ever had before. I liked the way I was before and no one, AND I MEAN NO ONE, was going to change me. I met
Brimstone soon after and we found our true innerselves." [Hellfire turns around and faces the camera. Brimstone looks at Hellfire and smiles.] {BS}"Hellfire, you turned out much better than anyone could have imagined. They under estimated you. I did that a few times, but now I know better. You are the type of woman that only a certain kind of person can want to have by their side for all eternity. And I am that lucky man."
[Brimstone walks over to Hellfire and gives her a deep passionate kiss. After he pulls his lips away from hers, she turns to the camera.] {HF}"Onto the present and what the past has to do with it. Guinevere, you have my title. The womens title means more to me than my own well being. And as you can see, I don't die easily. This Friday I start on my journey through the ranks of the women of the NAWC in my quest for what is mine."
[Hellfire looks like she has an awakening by the look on her face.] {HF}"Actually, Guinevere, I want two things. The belt and YOUR ASS! Now onto the woman who calls herself, the "Foxxy Angel", Careen Lizmark. This is the perfect fight between "good and evil". Careen, you use the name of the holy protector of souls. I am the taker of souls from pitiful pieces of flesh like you. I will take you to the ring Friday and take you to a place that many have tried to be but could never get close. You will join me in my own personal hell. This place is better known as my mind. And when my mind unleashes the evils it contains on you, the angel will die of pure fear of the eminent death that will occur. Not to the body, but to your mind. I will destroy you and then stand over the body of the fallen angel which has just given up her soul to me in battle. Brimstone wil not interfere because as he and many others know, I do not need any help to destroy anyone at any time.This match will be the first win in the many that lay before me on my way to the belt. You will all fall to the Inferno. This is a promise that I intend to keep. Now if you don't mind, I want to get back to watching the snowfall along with the bodies of many people which only stand in my way."
[Brimstone and Hellfire stand together and in unison scream their cry of battle.] "NAWC WE WILL SEND YOU STRAIGHT TO HELL!" [As they say this, fire takes over the picture and the NAWC fades out to a commercial for the March to May-hem.]
iller Buddha ---------------- INTERIOR - JAY BENNETTO'S HOME - NIGHT Jay Bennetto is in his pajamas; the house is dark, except for the light emitting from a television in the living room that has just ejected a video
Jay has finished watching. Bennetto looks at the time as seen on his cable box. Jay seems worried, anxious about something. The doorbell rings. Jay
Bennetto opens the door. It is Killer Buddha.
JAY BENNETTO: Thanks for coming "K". It's important. Let's try to be quiet. Marie and Hope are sleeping. (Killer Buddha enters the house. Jay asks Buddha to sit on the sofa.) KILLER BUDDHA: What's going on Uncle Jay? What's wrong? Jay Bennetto bends down and pops the video he was watching back into the
VCR. The play mode immediately initiates itself.
JAY BENNETTO: Watch the video "K". We might have some problems. (Before Killer Buddha can reply or ask a question, the video begins.) The video opens with a closeup of a nameplate that sits on a large oak desk in an office that is the last word in impressive elegance. The nameplate reads, Aniello Roma.
ANIELLO ROMA (voice-over): Greetings Gentlemen. It's been too long. I've missed the brains and the brawn of Jay Bennetto and Ken Brando.
The camera moves slowly as the voice continues to speak. We see a pair of hands, folded. These are Roma's jeweled hands. The camera finally comes to rest on Aniello Roma himself. Roma is about 50 years old. He bears a striking resmblance to the actor Anthony Quinn. It is the face of a man who is inteligent as well as ruthless. The face of an immigrant boy, who received his early American schooling in a reformatory, then moved into racketeering and corruption and the wealth they brought. Roma lost the sight in his left eye which is now a ghastly milky white. Rumour has it that the man who caused Roma's blindness was found a week after his brawl with Roma, dismembered and castrated. Roma of course, was in Florida during the time of the murder. Despite the past, Roma today, appears the gentleman, the father of a son and daughter who "belong" in their upper-class worlds.
ANIELLO ROMA: "K", I'm interested in investing my time and money in some specific business ventures. I'm, hoping that you will assist me in bringing these plans to fruition. My plans involve your new career as one of my brawlers, or your retirement from wrestling altogether. Please come to my new home in Long Island. It's located in Old Westbury. I recently purchased several new homes with built-in gyms. One is here in New York. I have another in Chicago, and another in California. I hope to see you at my Long
Island home tomorrow night at 8 o'clock sharp. The address is in the envelope the video came in. Refusing this invitation would be very disturbing to me. So please "K", come see me and come alone.
(The video fades to black) JAY BENNETTO: What do you think, "K"? KILLER BUDDHA: That maggot Roma crawls out of his hole in California, and sends us this message? I got a bad feeling about this.
JAY BENNETTO: Are you gonna go see him? Killer Buddha: Yes, and I'm going alone. JAY BENNETTO: Sumptin's up "K". I think a bad time's coming, a real bad time. (Fade To Black) Killer Buddha ------------------- INTERIOR - BRETT CROWE'S LIVING ROOM - NIGHT The living room is an ornate imitation of what you might find in a proper home of wealth. Everything, including the penthouse view of Los Angeles, screams money and pretentiousness, from the two plated telephones to the array of impressionistic paintings and the sets of books, all selected for their uniformity of size.
Tawny Catrell fits the room and view. Blonde and blue-eyed. She is as hot and sexy as a page one tabloid photo.
Through an open door, we can see a portion of a card room and a few of tonight's players. One of them, not identified here is State Senator, Simon
Locke of New York.
(A phone rings) TAWNY (picks up the phone): Sure, Mister Roma. (Tawny grins) TAWNY: I always like to tell Brett you're calling. I like watching him jump. (Tawny turns toward the door; she calls) TAWNY: Brett! The card player who turns is Brett "The Brainsmasher" Crowe. He is the former Champion of the Underground Fighting League, and is now Aniello
Roma's personal Enforcer. Brett is a rugged man in his early thirties, secure in a bevy of strong beliefs; the power of his physical strength, his money, and his fearlessness. Brett Crowe loves having power and the enjoyment usually is in his face for all to see.
TAWNY ( gives mock salaam): It's him. Brett is up and on the way instantly... Tawny's grin compels him to deliberately slow up and unhurriedly take the phone.
BRETT CROWE (gruff; to Tawny): Shut the door and see if anyone needs a drink. TAWNY (as she leaves): I don't know about your Big Shot Buddies, but I'm so bored, I need one.
BRETT CROWE (as door closes; into phone): Yes, Mr. Roma? (Crowe frowns) CROWE: I didn't know it was the private phone ringing or I'd have answered it myself. (Brett Crowe listens longer) BRETT CROWE: So you sent the video? (Crowe listens again) BRETT CROWE: Our friends from New York, Chicago, Florida, and L.A. are here, and they promise that the cops, the courts, the unions, and anything you need is in your pocket.
(Fade To Black) Killer Buddha ------------------- EXTERIOR - ANIELLO ROMA'S HOME - LONG ISLAND - NIGHT The house, very important and impressive looking, is set back of the tree-lined street. It advertises wealth and refinement. Killer Buddha, exiting from his black Van, takes time to eye the house and the surroundings. Several expensive cars of different make are parked in the driveway. Rock and Roll dance music is coming from the house.
Killer Buddha locks the Van and starts walking towards the house. A Bodyguard, a heavy-set brawler type, emerges from the shadows to confront him. THE BODYGUARD (pleasantly): Who did you want to see? KILLER BUDDHA: Aniello Roma is expecting me. THE BODYGUARD (with smile): Sorry I didn't recognize you, Mr. Buddha. Go right ahead. Killer Buddha begins to walk up the stairs to the Roma's front door. Buddha stands outside the door. The brass knocker resembles a gargoyle... Buddha rings the bell and rhythmic chimes sound from inside. Now, a very proper
Butler opens the door.
BUTLER: Good Evening, Sir. (The Butler blocks the doorway.) KILLER BUDDHA: I'm Mr. Brando, Is Mr. Roma in? BUTLER: I'll see Sir. (The Butler tries to close the door but,) KILLER BUDDHA (steps in): I don't like the draft out here. CUT TO: INTERIOR - FOYER - ROMA HOUSEHOLD - NIGHT It is still impressive, important and in good taste. A series of openings lead to the livingroom, library, etc., and a carpeted staicase runs up to rooms above. Dance music is coming from a room to the left.
BUTLER (stiff): Please wait! The Butler exits to the right. Well-dressed teenage boys and girls, in the sixteen to eighteen age bracket, the girls in their best party garb and the boys in their best "Cool Look" clothing, wander through the foyer. There is dancing, talking, and giggling... Some of the kids sip cokes, etc. The kids recognize Killer Buddha but pay him only cursory attention.
Aniello Roma appears suddenly and silently. He is expensively dressed and in excellent taste.
ROMA (smiling; politely): This way "K". I promised my daughter her father wouldn't be seen or heard tonight.
CUT TO: INTERIOR LIBRARY - ROMA'S HOUSE - NIGHT (Roma opens the library door for Killer Buddha.) ROMA (easily, pleasantly): Well "K", I know you're anxious to find out why
I've called upon you.
KILLER BUDDHA: I'm worried about you crawling into my life again. ROMA (easy nod): No need to worry. This could be a great thing for us. The library is large and comfortable. It's books have been read. There are deep chairs and a large desk close to an active fireplace and two heavily-draped french windows that catch the eye. A series of photographic portraits reveal Mr. and Mrs. Roma with their oldest son Vincent and their eighteen year old daughter, Theresa. Above the fireplace, is an oil painting of a very old woman. She wears a shawl and a worried look. Killer Buddha eyes the painting.
ROMA (points proudly): My Mother-- a great old lady. (Roma smiles, pauses for a beat.) ROMA: They broke the mold when they made her. (Roma's voice drops, almost imperceptibly.) ROMA: She died last May. (Roma sits down and looks at the painting.) ROMA: She lived here with me. She had her own suite of rooms-- own bath -- everything. (Roma shakes his head.) ROMA: Never got over being surprised about my success. (Roma pauses again; finally realizing.) ROMA: But you didn't come here to listen to me talk about my Mom. "K", I have some plans for you. I'm thinking about acquiring a couple of more wrestling promotions and utilizing some of the bigger stars for my
Undergrounmd Fighting Events. I'll give you twice what the NAWC is giving you now, plus 30% of all the profits from Mechandising the Killer Buddha name if you come back to the UFL for a minimum of two years. I'm thinking you could persuade some of the other NAWC stars to come on board and we'd make millions together. I already have most of the Arena owners and bookers in my pocket; plus the police, unions, courts, everyone is going to protect my interests. I even talked to my Friend Al Capulco, and he and The Threat are hot for this idea. Of course I had to promise AL that he would eventually be the President and Owner of the NAWC. I don't see a problem with that since I am going to pay your Mr. Palmer a visit and make him an offer he can't refuse.
KILLER BUDDHA (flatly): I will never work for you Roma. You're a parasite, a walking piece of bad luck.
ROMA (slowly): I'm offering you almost 4 million dollars a year to fight in a couple of Underground Brawls for me, while you still do this "Sports
Entertainment" thing. I'd own the UFL and the NAWC, the whole shebang. "K", you'd be the number one man in no time. You'd get paid handsomely for your services and be involved in a real man's game. You're going to turn that down?
KILLER BUDDHA (nodding): Listen Roma, Do you think I've forgotten how you treated me when I brawled for you. You tried to get me to take "Dives for money", you hurt guys who wouldn't play your game. You had my manager
Vincent Gecko, cheat me out of millions of dollars. (Killer Buddha takes a beat.) KILLER BUDDHA: Forget it, You are bad news. I want you to stay away from me. ROMA (with frown): I'm trying to help you and you make accusations against me. This is my home and I don't like dirt tracked into it.
KILLER BUDDHA (ice-cold): I've violated your immaculate home- is that it? ROMA: You don't see what I'm trying to do for you? You're an idiot! KILLER BUDDHA (seething): I'm stupid because I won't do business with a lying dog like you.
ROMA (snapping): Shut up and get out! KILLER BUDDHA (persisting; biting): Sure, we don't talk about things like that in your house! It's too elegant! Too respectable! Cute kids, party, paintings of Momma on the wall! No place for an honest man!
(Killer Buddha is upset, he points a finger at Aniello Roma) KILLER BUDDHA: This is the place for a thug who built this house out of thirty years of corruption and innocent blood.
(Killer Buddha pauses for a moment.) KILLER BUDDHA: You couldn't plant enough flowers around here to kill the smell! ROMA (pressing a desk buzzer, angrily): I warned you to get out! You're finished. You're a has-been. I'm putting a bounty on your head. I'm going to give $50.000 to the man that gets you to join me or cripples you for life.
Either way I win. I'm also putting $25,000 contracts on some of your NAWC playmates. They either join up or end up hurt.
KILLER BUDDHA (disregarding the threat): I have a family, I have a home at the NAWC and now you want to put your slimy fingers all over it.
ROMA (presses buzzer; calling impatiently): Vincent, Hurry Up! KILLER BUDDHA (continuing): Maybe you thought I lived under some rock like you! You have no problem sending Uncle Jay that video, making me offers that are really threats, telling me lies--
Vinnie Roma enters the room. This is Aniello Roma's 28 year old son. "Vicious" Vinnie Roma is one of the Underground fighting worlds rising stars. Thanks to Daddy Roma's money and influence.
VINNIE: What's up Dad? ROMA (points to Killer Buddha): Get rid of this loser! VINNIE (takes Buddha's arm): Let's go Fat man. KILLER BUDDHA (holds back; ominously): Get off me. Punk! VINNIE (pulls at Buddha): It comes off when we get to your car. Killer Buddha pulls loose with a single movement. Unruffled, Vinnie grabs again, pulls forward with a powerful jerk to take a hammerlock on the arm.
With an abrupt yank, Buddha straightens his arm, pulls away from the lock, and slams Vinnie against the desk. The portrait study of the Roma family falls to the floor with a crash.
VINNIE (moves in): All right Asshole! Vinnie feints for Buddha's stomach with his left, crosses with a furious right, but Killer Buddha picks off the punch with his own left and gives
Vinnie a Buddha bash to the jaw! Vinnie goes to his knees, shaking his head in an attempt to clear it; but the dizzying haze persists as Vinnie sinks lower and lower.
ROMA (shouting): Get up Vincent! Get Up! (His Father's voice prods the young man. Vinnie tries to get his legs under him for leverage. Dull eyes stare up at Killer Buddha.
KILLER BUDDHA (softly ominous): Better not get up Vinnie, You're not ready for Instant Karma yet.
(Vinnie wets his lips. His dull eyes continue to stare.) KILLER BUDDHA (turns to Aniello Roma): Want him to tag you in Roma? Eyes glaring, Roma sinks back into his chair and makes no reply. Killer
Buddha turns and walks out.
