NAWC (1999) — Card Archive

Roleplay Packet

26 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight we hear from Richard "Madman" Miller, Jason "The Bandit" Blake,
Garret Goebbels, The Threat, The Titan, and Long S. Tran.
Richard "Madman" Miller ---------------------------------- (The scene opens outside the familiar mansion known as the Waterfield Asylum for the Mentally Ill. The camera cuts out and opens inside a room within...
The room is dark and nothing can be seen.) *Thump* *Thump* *Thump* Madman: Nooo... Not again. NOOO!! *Thump* *Thump* *Thump* Madman: First, Cyrus Waterfield says that he will send me to this blasted place, and he does. I wanted to come into the NAWC with a bang, not a dull thump. But no, Cyrus says blah, blah, I'm going to send you to the asylum... And off I go... Locked up forever and ever because the bastard put me here and then ran away... And he took the key!
*Thump* *Thump* *Thump* Madman: Then, I get my chance to make my bang... I get my chance to show the NAWC what a Madman can do... And some chump named Kasady doesn't even show up for the match!! Sure, I suppose you could say that I scared him too much for him to show up... I scared him so much that he left the league to get away... But a win via forfeit wasn't what I was looking for either.
*Thump* *Thump* *Thump* Madman: Then, finally... I get a chance to prove myself... A Madman and a man named Brimstone against one man. One monster of a man in Rio D.
Giniro... So, I figured, here is my first real chance to make that bang I was looking for... Here is my first chance to become known and feared throughout the NAWC...
*Thump* *Thump* *Thump* Madman: NOOO!! It couldn't have happened that way! I couldn't have been tossed around like a rag doll by a man who can't even speak for himself!!!
I couldn't have been tagged in, full of piss and vinegar again, only to get pummelled and pinned by a man named after a trashy city... NOOO!! This must be a nightmare... This couldn't be reality!!!
*Thump* *Thump* *Thump* Madman: I hope it all ends... The Cyrus Waterfield Asylum... The running scared Kasady... The big man, named after a trashy city, who doesn't speak, but still kicks my ass... I just hope it all ends!! It will end... When I wake up it will all be gone. How can I awaken? How can I make the lights come on?!
(Pause.) Madman: A pinch!! (Pause.) Madman: OWWWWWWWWWWWW!!!! *Thump* *Thump* *Thump* Madman: No, no, NOOOO!! *Thump* *Thump* *Thump* Madman: What's happening?! (Suddenly the door into the room opens and a figure blocks the doorway. Light shines in around him casting a large shadow.) Figure: Time for your medication... Madman: I think I could use it. (Scene fades.) Jason "The Bandit" Blake ----------------------------------- (The scene is set back in the locker room immediately after the TV title match. Jason Blake is seen punching lockers and Amy is trying to calm him down.)
Amy - Relax Jason. It was probably Threat just trying to get even with Tom. Jason Blake - I had him right where I wanted. All I had to do was pin him and that title shot would have been mine. What the hell happened?
Amy - Well the fact of the matter is you won't be getting a World title shot just yet and there is nothing you can do about it.
Jason Blake - Wanna make a bet? Rick Stevens will pay for this. He will pay dearly. He had no business whatsoever being there.
Amy - Well he was, and he beat Tom up pretty good. I hope he's all right. Jason Blake - I wouldn't doubt if Knight set that attack up just to weasel out of having to give me a title shot. He knows from our last match together that I am capable of beating him. He knows it as much as I do. He weaseled out of our last match too with it getting thrown out. So I am zero for two against him, but he has never come close to beating me.
(A knock at the door is heard and a muffled voice behind it.) Voice - It's me Greg. May I get some words from you? Amy - I'm letting him in. I don't care what you think. Jason Blake - Fine, let him in. (Amy opens the door and Gragory Hollinsworth walks in. He is met by a fiery stare from Jason and steps back a little bit.)
Greg - Um, so Jason, what are your thoughts on your match this evening? Jason Blake - What do you think? Greg - Probably not favourable. Jason Blake - I see you actually paid attention to the match. I'm proud of you. I'm disgusted with the result. It's a disgrace to have someone ruin a
match as good as Tom and I are capable of putting on. But it happened, and Tom takes a few bumps and bruises and doesn't have to worry about defending his title against me for a while. Now isn't that just convenient for him?
Greg - You're not assuming that it was a setup, are you? Jason Blake - Well it's obvious that Threat has extended an open invitation to Mr. Knight to join them. He knows that I am more than capable of beating him. So it only makes sense that he would join Threat to protect the only thing that has any value to him, his title. But he'll have to face me sooner or later, and I'll get my shot at him, and I will prove beyond a shadow of a doubt that I am better than him.
Greg - So what does this do to your plans for chasing the vacated Tag Team titles? Jason Blake - I really don't have any interest in those titles any further, well in a way I do, just not with Knight. If he's behind all of this, then he will get what he deserves when his time comes, but until then, I have another bone to pick.
Greg - And whom would that be with? Jason Blake - The other clown who cost me my victory tonight. Rick Stevens, you're taking the wrong path my friend. You've chosen the path that crossed mine, and that is a big mistake. Do you remember this belt Rick?
(Jason Blake holds up the NAWC TV title to the camera and the light is glistening off of it.)
Jason Blake - This is the belt that you have been the owner of twice, the most recent time when it was handed to you at Nuclear Winter. How long did you hold this the second time Rick? I believe it was less than an hour before I beat you and took it home with me. So in anger you decide to cost me my chance at the NAWC World Title. Jealousy will get you nowhere
Stevens. It's only going to get you another beating. So if you want revenge so badly, why don't we play your game? I'll challenge you for that
Hardcore title of yours, and I will take that from you as well.
Amy - Jason, that's enough. You're not getting into hardcore matches just for this guy. It's not worth it.
Jason Blake - Oh it's worth it. I can inflict punishment on him like he's never believed. You know how I get when I develop that mean streak.
Amy - Yes I do know, and I don't want you getting carried away again. Jason Blake - He has to learn not to mess with me. I've worked hard to gain immediate respect here in the NAWC but still there are those who take me far too lightly, and Rick Stevens is one of them. So now he has been hand picked to be the first person in NAWC to see the crazy side of Jason Blake.
So if you got guts Stevens, accept my challenge for your title, and I'll show you what hardcore is all about.
Greg - So Amy, What do you think of all this? Amy - I really don't want to talk about it and I surely don't want tensions start between Jason and Tom, but it looks now that I have no control of the situation. I just hope he doesn't do anything stupid.
Greg - So what about your future here in NAWC? I heard you've joined the
Women's ranks. Amy - Yes I am, and I hope to make the same immediate impact that Jason did in his debut. It should be a lot of fun and I've been training really hard this past week. It's a tremendous opportunity for me and I intend on capitalizing on it quickly.
Greg - I know you'll do just fine. So Jason, any last comments? Jason Blake - Actually yes I do. Tom, you've made a big mistake and I will correct it for you. You say I will see the light? Well it looks like that same light has blinded you. You seem to have forgotten who your true friends are. So you'd better pray to the almighty that I don't get you in the ring anytime soon, because you will get Blindsided like never before!
Greg - Well thanks Jason, I'll talk to you later. Jason Blake - Sure, please leave now. (Gregory Hollinsworth leaves the locker room, and as soon as the door closes, the screen fades to black.)
Garret Goebbels ----------------------- (scene fades in to Garret pacing in the locker room he then turns toward the camera) Garret: You know people I just realized something. I am to be perfectly blunt not succeeding doing things the way I am now. By that I mean the sucking up to you the fans and combating the Threat. Now don't get me wrong
I won't turn into the jerk that you all hated before but I can't take the fans cheers or booes to hart. From now on it's just business. I am doing this to live up to the fact that I believe that I should be living up to the potential that just got me picked as best submission wrestler.
(turns and starts pacing) Garret: As for the Threat well it's obvious that no one cares. I mean I make a plea for unity and what happens: every single person I ask ignores me
I guess that tells me how much the locker room thinks of me. Rick Stevens make no mistake I am still going after your hide but the rest of Threat can take a flying leap or control the NAWC I really don't care. As for you Rick
Stevens; you and I fought one heck of match back when the NAWC was starting but now you have turned into a mere shadow of yourself. The weakness is apparent to all Rick. Just remember when something in the wild shows weakness it gets killed.
(Garret stops pacing as the camera goes to a close up of his face) I noticed your weakness and since I have been ignored, brother I have been near wild. (camera fades out) The Threat --------------- [Voice Over: Some call me the NAWC legend, how did I come to deserve this title? time would tell]
{The camera slowly fades in to a grainy picture, the scene is the wood's in the dead of the night. As the camera focuses you see Rick Stevens standing in the middle of the woods. He stands with his leg's together and his arms at his side, he looks down at the ground as he prepares to speak}
Rick: Claw, you claim you can take my spot as the legend of NAWC, you claim you're the answer to the future of this company, you see I hold your future in my hands, and the Threat, the most dominating force this place has ever seen holds the NAWC's fate.
{Rick looks up to the camera' his face covered mostly by his hair, and he looks like he hasn't shaven in quite some time}
Rick: Claw, I am the answer to all of the question's before they are even asked. So, say what must be said, and do what must be done. But in the end there will be only one true legend, and you're looking at him.
{Just then from out of the shadow's steps Al Capulco, Rio D Giniro, Chris
Phear, and the first lady of the NAWC Lady Divine. Capulco steps foward dressed to a T in a pair of grey pressed suit pants and a maroon silk long sleeve. His hair styled in its common slick back way with his gold framed shades protecting his eyes from the moonlight that casts down through the trees. He takes a brief moment to look about the vast forest and listen to the deafning silence. Rio stands alone in the background wearing worn out faded jeans and grey t-shirt. He looks off into the distance wihtout interest. He seems to have a look of displeasure mixed with intense concentration. After a brief moment of looking about Al decides to speak.}
Al: You know, it is now that I know what it feels like to be empty. I know what it is like to have a vast area of silence inside of you... To be lost in the middle of no where listening for an echo to break your endless thoughts... Yet our silence is not that of nothing. But that of an anger so intense that you dont know just how to express. (Al looks over to Rio whos attention is still off in his own thoughts. Al dissapiontedly shakes his head and continues.)
Al: What has happened recently in the NAWC with myself and Rio has brought about this anger. To be stripped of everything that you have worked so hard to achieve. That you are rightfully deserving of. To just have it yanked our from underneath you like a cheap rug...
(Al brushes about the leaves that blanket the forest floor. A crisp breeze rustles through the treetops.)
Al: Palmer, Knight dont think for one solitary moment that what you two have done wil go unpunished.. I warn you not to get to comfortable with your current situation. Because we all know that things are not how there are meant to be. Though you may by some misconstrude determination see it otherwise. You are only fooling yourselves. For now things may seem still and calm now. But soon enough a harsh wind is going to blow through the forest of the NAWC and many trees are going to fall. Including the two mighty oaks that at this point towers over all... (Looks to the camera)... You know to what I am referring. When all is said and done only five will stand tall and strong.. Those are the ones that are planted before you now...
(Al once again looks over to Rio catching his attention. Rios face gets a very disturbed look. He turns and with a mighty blow he crushes a dent in the side of a nearby pine. He then turns and walks off into the forest. Al shakes his head in thoughts of what is to come.)
Rick: Time is a funny thing, the more you wait, the more it swallow's you. The day's of waiting are gone like the summer warmth. Time is of an essence now. For only time and fate will bring us to the pinnacle that we rightfully deserve. Anyone who wishes to oppose us, will fall like all the rest have..
{Camera fade} The Titan ------------- (The camera fades into a town carnival. You're able to see the bottem part of the Zipper slightly off center and to your left. To the right you can see The Orbiter. Both are in motion. Slightly in front of the zipper is a ticket booth. There are a few mothers and small children there. In the bottem right hand corner you can see some sort of carnie booth. You can't see what game it is, only the backs of a few people. In this entire scene many people are going back and forth. The dirt on the ground is being tossed around from all the movement. You now see the orbiter slow down.
The camera pans toward to. As The Zipper leaves view you notice the game is a dart game that you have to hit "The Cat in the Hat". The camera keeps going past the game. Behind it is The Spider with is loading people. The camera turns so that The Orbiter is the only thing you can see. It has completly stoped. People are starting to get off. Around from the back comes The Titan. He's wearing a long sleave shirt with a forest green pattern on it. It comes down an inch past his butt. Under it he's wearing a plain black t-shirt. He's also wearing a brand new looking pair of blue jeans. He's walking with woman arm in arm. She's about a foot and a half smaller than him. She has long blond hair. She's wearing a red tank top and tight blue jeans. They're talking a laughing carelessly. They exit the
Orbiter's gates when The Titan notices the camera. He gets an angry look on his face. The woman looks at him curiously and then sees the camera. You can read her lips saying, "Why's that there?". He reaches into his back pocket and pulls out his wallet. He pulls out some money and gives it to her. He puts his wallet back, says something to her, and gives her a little kiss. She walks off to the right. Titan walks up to the camera and looks past the camera at the camera man. He looks mad.)
Titan: Hey, didn't I tell you idiots that I didn't want to be desturbed on my off time? Camera Man: (studdering) Ahh, ahh. Just doing my job sir. Titan: Well I'm gonna have some words with Palmer. You try to have a good time with your girlfriend and this is what you get! Guess the new found fame after my one win over Sean Dubose is gonna get the media on me like crazy. Ha! But that's only the beginning. Oh course that was a lucky win for me. I mean, I'm not perfect. In this case it was Dubose that made the mistake though. I'd like to make a demonstration.
(The Titan walks over to the dart game seen before. He walks up to the carnie. The carnie's a man of about 30 years. He has short greyish black hair that is uncombed and greasy. He also has a scruffy beard and moustache. He has a black t-shirt on with many holes in it. He has a white apron that has grease stains on it. His arms are covered in tatoos. The booth is filled with different style striped hats.)
Carnie: What can I get you there dude? Titan: Prices? Carnie: Well since you look lucky I'll give you a deal. The normal price is 3 darts for $2 and 9 for $5. But I'll give you 9 for only $4.
(A man in the background yells out, "You gave me that deal too! Conartist!!!") Titan: (unenthusiastic) Great... I'll just take three. (The Carnie hands him 3 darts and Reeves gives him $2.) Titan: Now watch. I'm sure in the NAWC there are these guys going around saying, "I'm perfect and no one will beat me." I'll guarentee that they're lying. No one's perfect and I can even admit that myself.
(The Titan throws a dart. It just misses.) Titan: See. I missed (He throws another and it hits the hat.) Titan: But that doesn't mean I'm not good. (He throws the last one and it barly misses the cat's head.) Carnie: Sorry dude. Two hits is a winner. Titan: Yah, whatever. (The Titan starts walking around. You can see many different rides, games, and people in the background.)
Titan: Now on to business. I'm really looking forward to my next opponent. There's only one problem, I don't know who it is. And it really it doesn't matter. See, I'm only a straight road ahead for the NAWC Heavyweight Title.
Sure, that road has some pot holes in it but doesn't every road? And don't you get were you're going 100 times to one? It's all a matter of time. For all of my loyal fans, enjoy the ride. Take in the sceanry. It's gonna be a blast! But now I must go searching for Kim. That's my girlfriend. The one that you scared away. But until next time.
(The Titan walks off the screen.) (Fade out) Long S. Tran ------------------ Long S. Tran sits in the lonely locker room. This was the second time he had been given this opportunity, and much like the first time, he had once again failed to capture the NAWC Lightweight Title.
Tran is still in his wrestling gear, and his hair has been let down. He is deep in thought.
He left his last company on bad terms, and since joining the NAWC, he had been given chance after chance, yet he is still unknown in the NAWC. His workrate has clearly dropped, and the fans are really starting to lose interest in him. There is only one person on the NAWC roster that could possibly re-light the fire that once feuled him. Tran looks around the dark locker room. There is an old NAWC poster up. It is of former NAWC
Lightweight Champion Claw Richens holding the belt he recently lost. Long:- Claw... We both started off the same, and both rose up the ranks together in my previous home. We got cheered, we got booed. But no matter what, we made them "Make Noise!"
(Pause. Camera pans out to show a side shot of Tran staring, as if he was talking to Claw himself through his poster)
Long:- But since coming here, something changed. I guess I lost my spirit, but you somehow still had it in you. You managed to somehow win the Title that I hoped to hold, and ultimately lost it before I had a chance to face you for it.
(Tran's fists clench harder as he continues...) Long:- You made a name for yourself here, while I got pushed further and further back. Then, as if overnight, you had become a NAWC household name.
(Pause) Long:- Seeing you now "clawing" your way to the top reminds me of why I asked you to follow me into the NAWC. You see, in "that other fed" we stepped into the ring twice if I recall correctly.
(Pause. A smile seems to cross Tran's face as he remembers) Long:- I remember us fighting it out... Giving 101%. Title for Title, and so much at stake. You held Gold, I held Gold. We were two of the biggest fan favorites in the fed! So much at stake over one match. Neither would let the other pin them for three simple seconds. It seemed like we would fight forever, but some fool interrupted and we never got a clean finish.
We never did find out who would have won those two belts. And the second time in the Triple Threat Match, where we had a visitor. Once again, no clear winner between us. We've avoided each other for too long Claw... (Tran takes a deep breath as he slowly looks away from the poster, and into the camera) Long:- We both came here, and you somehow rose while I stayed at the bottom. Now I don't have any personal problems with you Claw. Heck, I brought you into this damn fed! But as you've risen the ranks and I've stayed at the bottom. That ain't right...
(Tran is clearly becoming more and more angered. Although he tries to keep it in, he can't deny human nature. Jealously runs through his body as he watches as Claw makes a name while he is clearly becoming just another name.)
Long:- ...That ain't right... Considering you've never beaten me before. (Long's head hangs down from a mixture of fatigue, and the fact his ego will not let the camera see him cry. His hair covers his face as he softly repeats to himself)
Long:- ...It ain't right... It ain't right... (Fade to black) Good work to all on this one. It was a decent sized one. [name withheld]
