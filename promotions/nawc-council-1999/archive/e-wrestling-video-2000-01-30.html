VIDEO FRAGMENT ========================================================================== CUT TO: Killer Buddha practicing his wrestling moves at the "Little Buddha" Wrestling School. We hear Killer Buddha speaking. KILLER BUDDHA (voice-over): Uncle Jay brought a house in Atlantic Beach and opened up "Little Buddha's". He named the school after the family pet, a bulldog named "Buddha". I went to school and spent alot of time at the wrestling camp. I would watch the guys train and I would dream of one day becoming a wrestler too. At about this time I was getting letters from my brother Chris on a regular basis.
CUT TO: INTERIOR - ST. MARY'S SCHOOL - OFFICE - DAY We see Mr. Lori, who has been a teacher at this school for over thirty years. Mr. Lori: Ken Brando, who you now insist on calling Killer Buddha, was an excellent student. He did have an occasional problem with some of the other students. The other children didn't know what to make of Master
Brando's quiet, intense nature.
CUT TO: Jay Bennetto at the "Little Buddha" Wrestling School watching several students in the ring. Jay responds to the camera.
JAY BENNETTO: "K" started to really train at da' school when he wuz 16 years old. He graduated from high school with honors and received some attention from a coupla' football Scouts. "K" went to Hunter College too. He spent a year there really gettin' into Mythology stuff and
Religion. But his heart was already into da' wrestling thing. "K" had one really close pal, Shane "The Flame" Furie. This guy and "K" started to work and hang out together.
CUT TO: Some old video footage of Killer Buddha and another young grappler working as a tag team during a student exhibition.
SHANE FURIE (voice-over): Yeah, that's me. Buddha and I were a great team. CUT TO: Shane Furie in a wheelchair. We are at his home in Los Angeles. SHANE FURIE: I was a brash, over-confident, hellraiser. Buddha was always more intense, he'd think things through before making his moves.
We both moved to L.A. in 1986. "K" was 19 years old, I was twenty. We began to fight in some Underground Fighting Competitions. There were no referees, no rounds, no rules. It was Last Man Standing gets the cash.
We eventually got signed by the Big Man on the fighting scene, a worm named Aniello Roma. CUT TO: We are with Aniello Roma. His office is luxuriuos. This is a man who knows what wealth and power are all about.
ANIELLO ROMA: Yes, Killer Buddha fought for my organization. I set up bouts for him all over the United States. In Los Angeles, Detroit,
Chicago, Texas, and New York. But I have nothing to do with that loser now, he is a nobody, a has-been.
CUT TO: Killer Buddha is at the "Little Buddha" Wrestling School. He is inside the school's ring, leaning on the ropes.
KILLER BUDDHA: In 1987, Shane Furie became the Undergroung Fighting
Leagues first Champion. I kept brawling all over the United States, gaining some recognition and some exposure. Shane lost the title in 1988 to a real monster named Brett "The Brainsmasher" Crowe. Shane was paralyzed from the waist down because of injuries he sustained during that bout.
CUT TO: Jay Bennetto is in the parking lot of an arena. He's getting ready to attend an NAWC event.
JAY BENNETTO: When I heard about what happened to Shane, I called "K" and begged him to come back home. "K" says "No Way!", he wants to beat this "Brainsmasher" guy first. So "K" signs with this shady but well known manager named Vincent Gecko. "K" goes on to become a UFL legend and finally beats Brett "The Brainsmasher" Crowe for da' title in 1989.
"K' wuz 22 years old. It was during this time that "K" took on da' "Killer Buddha" name for da' first time. ================================================================== END VIDEO FRAGMENT As I said before, you need to visit the NAWC website in order to purchase this incredible documentary about an incredible competitor.
