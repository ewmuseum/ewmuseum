NAWC House Show (22 January)

22 January 2000

— Part 1 of 2 —

NAWC HOUSE SHOW From the E Center in Salt Lake City, Utah Saturday, January 22, 2000 No titles are on the line. "Rocket Star" Perry Harrils Vs. Dynamite Derrick As these two locked up, Harrils took the early lead, driving Derrick to the corner and hitting a series of elbows. He then whipped Derrick out of the corner and held on, whipping him right back in. Harrils then whipped Derrick off the ropes and kicked him in the gut. Hitting a
Tornado DDT after that. He went for the cover but barely got two. He continued his attack, hitting a hurricanrana and then locking Dynamite
Derrick into an STF. Derrick held in there. Ricket Star eventually released it and began stomping Derrick. Harrils pulled him up and hit a back suplex for another two count. Dynamite Derrick fought back with a series of low blows. However, they only angered Rocket Star. Perry
Harrils fought back and hit his Ending Sequence, a sequence of discus punches and then a devastating thrust kick that rocked Derrick. From there, Perry Harrils went on to hit a Star Dust Press, a move he calls the Total Blackout. The 1 2 3 was evident.
Winner: "Rocket Star" Perry Harrils The Green Mountain Trooper Vs. Floyd "Octopus" Malloy The GMT totally dominated his opponent, leveling him with a series of punches and then a big powerslam. The Trooper then hit the Speeding
Ticket for the easy win over the newcomer.
Winner: The Green Mountain Trooper Garret Goebbels Vs. "Mr. Bitter" Jeff Walker These two were very well matched. Walker took the lead after executing a vertical suplex which he held for a while. He then hit a full nelson slam which pleased the crowd. But, their cheers and chants for Garret
Goebbels. Mr. Bitter continued to pound his opponent, hitting a wishbone leg splitter and then pulling Goebbels up for a hangman's neckbreaker. He went for the cover and nearly got three. Garret got a shoulder up at the last second. Jeff Walker pulled him up and continued his assault. Backing Goebbels to the corner and landing a combination of punches and kicks, even hitting a European uppercut that rocked
Goebbels. The crowd continued to chant for him though. Walker went for the Road to Apathy, a very short-arm clothesline, but Garret ducked it and hit a belly to back suplex. He then hit a side Russian legsweep and rolled Walker onto his stomach to lock in a Boston crab. Mr. Bitter fought his way to the ropes, forcing Garret to release the hold. The two then exchanged shots in the middle of the ring. Walker went for his
Road to Apathy again but Goebbels ducked it, pulling Walker onto his shoulders. This lead to a shoulder breaker. Garret then went to work on the arm of Walker, locking on a stepover armbar. After a minute or two, he released it. He pulled Walker up and hit his Minnesota Masher, a brainbuster suplex. He then locked on the Minnesota Armbreaker for the win.
Winner: Garret Goebbels Damien Douglas Vs. Claw Richens Damien came to the ring first, complaining that his opponent didn't deserve the Lightweight title as he wasn't a Lightweight. Claw came out and the two went at it. Claw took the early lead, hitting an armdrag takeover and then pulled Damien up for a standing dropkick. Douglas fought back after hitting a double arm DDT. He went to the ropes and hit a bulldog on Claw, covering for a two. Douglas then pulled him up and hit a head scissor takedown. Taunting Claw to hit one of his own.
Claw instead went for a hurricanrana but Douglas countered it with a powerbomb. He held it for a two count. Claw fought back after hitting a scoop slam out of nowhere. He then dropped a leg and went to the middle turnbuckle. As Douglas got up, Claw jumped off, hitting a double axhandle chop. He pulle Damien Douglas up for a fisherman's suplex.
From there, he hit a piledriver and a whipped Douglas off the ropes. As he came back, Claw ducked his head. Damien flipped over, taking Claw down with a sunset flip, but only getting a two count. Richens bounced back up and whipped Douglas into the corner. Claw went in with a lariat and hoisted Douglas to the second turnbuckle. Claw climbed to the first and hit a big snap suplex from there, called the Claw's Edge. The win was only three seconds away.
Winner: Claw Richens Speedy Irons Vs. Chris Phear These two were very well matched. Speedy only had a pound in weight advantage. However, he gave up 4 inches height. But it didn't matter much as he took command of this match. He hit a snap suplex and pulled
Phear up for a Saturn Driver. Speedy went for the cover but only got a two count. Phear fought back, hitting two hurricanranas in succession.
He then went to the top turnbuckle and hit a sommersault legdrop. He went for the cover but only got two. He pulled Speedy up and went for a swinging neckbreaker but Irons countered amazingly with a jawbreaker.
He then hit a 360 degree backer and pulled Phear up for a cradle DDT followed by FootLoose, a back kick to the gut. Phear doubled over and fell out of the ring. He pulled himself up by the apron as Speedy used the ropes to crash down on him with a springboard moonsault. But Phear got out at the very last minute. And Speedy Irons hit the floor. Phear turned and walked to the back. Speedy pulled himself into the ring.
Phear was counted out. But, as he was walking to the back, he was heard saying, "This match doesn't matter."
Winner: Speedy Irons by countout Thane Sloan Vs. Max "The Beast" Cayn Thane came to the ring first. And, as Cayn's music began to play, he appeared on the Supertron. This is what he said.
Max: Hey there Thane. Thought you'd get a shot at the future Vice
President of this company eh? Well think again. Because in case you have overlooked it, I've got an important match at Nuclear Winter. And
I'm not going to even break a sweat on you tonight. I'm not afraid
Sloan. I'm just smart. And being the future VP that I am, it's time to call my own shots. So, go back to the locker room. Because this match isn't happening.
Thane looked on with disgust in his eyes. Referee Doc Draven started counting Cayn out. Max came to the entranceway and flipped off both
Thane and Draven. He was counted out.
Winner: Thane Sloan by countout To Be Continued ...

— Part 2 of 2 —

NAWC HOUSE SHOW Continued ... Rick Stevens Vs. Killer Buddha These two went at it full force. Killer Buddha used his size advantage to get the upper hand on Stevens. But it didn't last long. Rick fought a determined fight. Hitting a series of big suplex including a belly to belly, belly to back, and German suplex. He then pulled Buddha up for a backbreaker. However it didn't seem to phase him as much as the suplexes did. He came back in a flurry, knocking Stevens silly. Killer
Buddha threw him to the floor and followed him outside. Out there, he continued his offense, whipping Stevens into the guardrail and then into the apron of the ring. He then drove a couple big elbows into Rick
Stevens on the apron. Stevens fought back after hitting a headbutt. He brought Buddha back into the ring where he hit a brainbuster and a sidewalk slam. Again Killer Buddha fought back. Again throwing Stevens to the floor. But this time, as Killer Buddha was stepping out, Stevens grabbed a chair from under Brent Jacobson and pasted Buddha. Draven immediately called for the bell, disqualifying Rick Stevens.
Winner: Killer Buddha by DQ Killer Buddha remained in the ring as Stevens unhappily left for the back. He told Draven to call Stevens back and restart the match, saying that the two would face off again at Nuclear Winter in a Hardcore match. Draven complied and Rick Stevens came back to the ring.
Killer Buddha Vs. Rick Stevens Rick Stevens came back into the ring with the chair again but Killer
Buddha unloaded a flurry of boots on him. Stomping him down. Stevens dropped the chair and Killer Buddha picked it up. He threw it into the hands of Rick Stevens and then headbutted it into his face. Killer
Buddha went for the cover but only got a two. He pulled Stevens up and whipped him off the ropes. He then went for Instant Karma but Stevens held the rope. He then walked up to Buddha and booted him. A DDT followed. He went for the cover and also got a two. He then pulled
Killer Buddha up and hooked him for a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Stevens whipped him into the corner and ran in after him, hitting the Last
Call. Stevens then got him in DVD position and hit the Painkiller for the win.
Winner: Rick Stevens Next Orlando Palla came out and announced that he was going to be interviewing Brimstone and Hellfire. Here's what was said.
Orlando: Brimstone, you're scheduled to tag up with Richard "Madman" Miller to face Rio D. Giniro later tonight in a handicap match. What do you think of that?
Brimstone: Palmer you above all people should know that it doesn't matter who I'm teamed with or put against. I go out there and kick anyones ass that I can get a hold of. That means that Rio, you will have your hands full with me tonight. And Madman, if I have my mind set right about you, you and I can take his ass out and show Pres Paculpo and
Palmer that we deserve top billing all the time. Madman just be careful to not cross a Hell Servant. The results are not nice. Orlando knows full well the results. Don't you?
Orlando: Yeah, but on to the future. Now, I noticed that you're not on the upcoming NAWC PPV Nuclear Winter. You should have had a tag team partner in Kasady in that Tag Team Battle Royal for the Tag titles.
What are your plans for Nuclear Winter? Brimstone: Kasady, you son a bitch. You ruined my chances at NAWC gold. If I ever see you again, your ass will be mine. But nevertheless, I will be a force to be dealt with at Nuclear Winter. As a matter of fact, all of the competitors in the pay per view better watch their asses because the Hell's Servants will be on the hunt for blood.
Orlando: Okay, now Hellfire, you're taking part in that Battle Royal at
Nuclear Winter for the Women's title and the chance for your man to get a shot at the Television title. Are you ready for this? What kind of experience do you have in wrestling? And who do you think you'd like to fight, or throw over, first?
Hellfire: All of the women in this match are just little valets only here for the enjoyment of the fans. I'm here for a couple reasons. The fans can look but can not touch. They can dream of having me in their hands to pleasure them. I am one man's woman,I am here to watch my man
Brimstone kick everyone's ass that he can. Another reason I am here is to kick some ass myself. All of the superstars here and the fans saw how much punishment I can take. I was nearly killed in that car. I came back from that stronger than ever. I am ready to kick all of those sluts asses. They do not know what I am capable of. If they really want to know, they should ask Sharon Roberts about it. Now I think that we have a match to get ready for soo I think you should get out of our locker room before I am forced to hurt you the way that you know I can.
Orlando: Well thanks to both of you. And good luck at Nuclear Winter. Hellfire, Brimstone, and Orlando Palla all went to the back as Brent
Jacobson called out the next match.
"Down Under" Derek Davies Vs. "The Cavalier" Tom Knight Davies and Knight stood face to face in the ring before the bell rang. Davies signaled for them to hold on for a minute. He then set a stipulation for the match. The stipulation was that if he won, he'd get a title shot at Knight for Nuclear Winter. Knight agreed and the bell rang. Davies took the lead with a slight weight advantage. It didn't take Knight long to get into his game though. He fought long and hard, wearing down his opponent. He hit a Russian legsweep followed by a hurricanrana out of the corner. He went for the cover but barely got two. Derek Davies fought back valiantly, hitting some big scoop slams and a delayed vertical suplex. He then whipped Knight off the ropes and landed a dropkick flesh in the chest. He went for the cover and got about two and a half. The two exchanged shots again. Both men getting the advantage. Eventually, Knight began working over the back of his opponent, hitting backbreakers and abdominal stretches. Then, as he locked Davies into the Sword in the Stone, Al Capulco, Rio D. Giniro,
Rick Stevens, and Lady Divine came to ringside. Rio and Rick crashed the ring. Attacking both men in the process, Rick pounding Davies and
Rio pounding Knight. Doc Draven called for the bell and it was announced that this match was a draw. Rio hit a sidewalk slam on Knight as Stevens hit his Painkiller on Davies. Then the two exited the ring and stood next to their manager and NAWC President, Al Capulco. He had a microphone in hand. Here's what he had to say.
Al: Listen up... I told you people in my interview that the recent travesty that took place would be handled.. And that is just what is going to happen... (Al pauses as the crowd boos)... Right right whatever... Heres the deal... Davies you want a World Title match at the
PPV.. Well thats fine by me.. But remember I said that things would be corrected soon.. So here is what is going to happen... You will get that shot you so desperately seek.. But I am here to add something to it...
(Smiles)... And that is the Big Man.. My Man.. (Crowd boos wildly at this then begins the chant of AssHole AssHole. Al smiles with ammusement and continues.)
Al: Seeing as though Knight here was to much of a scared rabbit to grant a rematch.... And Big Bad PALMER is afraid of me and the TRUE CHAMP being on top.... As President of this here fed. So said by the Big Boss himself... (Smiles devilishly).. I will grant it myself ... (Crowd start chanting AssHole AssHole again)... I know you all love it.. So thats that.. Come the PPV it will be a triple threat match for the World
Title... And as God is my witness... And as I have promised... Both belts will be back in the hands of their rightful owners... One way or the other...(Al looks around at the crowd smiling as they continue to chant)... I Have Spoken... So it is Written... So Shall it be Done.....
[All four went back to the locker rooms after this, leaving Knight and
Davies, still out in the ring. Paramedics came out to check on them.
The ring was cleared after a few minutes. Then, we went on to the Main
Event. Rio D. Giniro Vs. Richard "Madman" Miller & Brimstone in a Handicap match Miller and Brimstone came out seperately. Then Rio came out with President Capulco. Capulco had a mic in hand again and demanded that only one wrestler face Rio at any one time. Referee Doc Draven complied and announced it. Miller and Brimstone were unhappy but went along with it. Miller started out first with Rio. The big man totally destroyed his opponent. Throwing him around like a rag doll. Capulco looked extremely pleased at ringside. Finally Rio threw Miller into his own corner. Brimstone tagged in and he and Rio squared off. Rio had a large size advantage still over this big man. Rio pulled Brimstone up into a choke lift and threw him across the ring. Hellfire jumped onto the ring apron and began jawing with Rio, destracting him. Al Capulco came around to stop her. He tried to pull her off but she hit him with a back elbow, smashing his expensive glasses. Hellfire dropped off the apron and picked up a chair. She went to hit Capulco but Rio had jumped to the floor and stopped her. He grabbed the chair and motioned to smash it over her head. However, Brimstone came from behind with a low blow. Brimstone fell to one knee, dropping the chair in the process.
Hellfire picked it up and pasted Rio with it. Capulco was about to tell
Draven to end the match but Rio told Draven not to. Rio shoved Hellfire away into the guardrail. Brimstone unleashed a flurry of shots on him, rolling him back into the ring. Brimstone came back in and taged in
Miller. Miller came in and went for an armdrag takeover. However Rio blocked and hit a gorilla press slam on him. Rio pulled him up and whipped him off the ropes, hitting the sidewalk slam as he came back.
Brimstone came in but Rio hit a big clothesline to take him over the top rope to the floor. Rio sat up Miller and bounced off the ropes. He hit the SunSet for the win. Capulco jumped into the ring to congratulate him.
Winner: Rio D. Giniro Rio and Al went to the back, leaving the bodies laid out in the ring and on the concrete below.
We hope you enjoyed this house show. Nuclear Winter is only a week away. Get your tickets now! It's going to be great.
