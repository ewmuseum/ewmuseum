Garret Goebbels ----------------- (camera fades in on Garret; wrapped up walking in a snowy, wintery
Minnesota forest trail with a reporter trailing behind)
Garret: (turns and notices the reporter) Man! I can't even turn around without bumping into one of you guys since I came back.
Reporter: Well...um...you did make quite a stir when you returned to the
NAWC. My first question would be, how are you going to take on Perry
Harrils in your upcoming match to determine the TV title shot?
Garret: Well Mr. Harrills, you call yourself The Rocket Star. Just remember Perry that rockets have been know to crash. If you try any high flying on me your going to be in a world of hurt man.
(Garret is continuing walking as the reporter strains to keep up while asking questions) Reporter: What about "Down Under" Derrick Davies? Garret: I believe that I can beat him. I have seen his stuff and let me tell you I am not impressed. Like most big guys he relies on his strength and does not possess my techincal skill.

(Garret continues walking as the reporter slips on the snowy trail just as the camera fades out)
Donovan Moore ----------------- (Rain pours down on the streets of Tallahassee. A puddle of water is slowing growing with the rain. A brown boot stomps on the puddle and water splashes everywhere. The camera pulls up to reveal Denise walking. She has on a long, leather trench coat and carrying a black umbrella. She approaches the same run down building that Donovan has visited numerous

Alaskan Brawler like you need to feed that ego you are growing. God help me if I ever start to call myself "Superstar" Donovan Moore or something like that, I better just walk away and never look back.
Simply call me Donovan Moore, after Nuclear Winter you can call me the
NAWC Lightweight Champion as well.
(Moore lies back down on the bench and begins to bench press again. Denise spots him as the camera fades out.)
