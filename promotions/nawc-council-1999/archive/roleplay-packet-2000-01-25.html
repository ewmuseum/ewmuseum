Roleplay Packet & Announcements

25 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight we hear from "Rocket Star" Perry Harrils, Killer Buddha, Speedy
Irons, Rick Stevens, Big Wreck, Thane Sloan, and Claw Richens.
"Rocket Star" Perry Harrils --------------------------- [ The scene opens in the streets of Salt Lake City, Utah. There is people walking everywhere, going to their homes or to somewhere else. A limo shows up slowly in road. The window of the limo is open and a man seems to be enjoying the tour over Salt Lake City with a woman sitting next to him making a fone call on the celular fone. The man says something to the driver. Suddenly, the limo stops. The door of the car opens and out of it comes Perry Harrils ]
Perry: I'm going to take a walk from here. I meet you too in front of the E Center. Careen: No way, I'm going with you. [ Careen opens the limo's door and steps out of it ] Perry: Farewell then. Hey driver, I meet you in there then. [ The limo runs down the road while Careen and Peter walks in the direction of the E Center. The camera stands right in front of then while they walk and talk. Suddenly, a boy bumps on Perry. The boy falls on the floor ]
Perry: Hey kid, are you alright ? Kid: Yeah, yeah, I'm fine... Hey, aren't you "Rocket Star" Perry Harrils ? Perry: That's right. Here, let me help you get up. [ Perry offers his hand to the kid, helping him getting up ] Kid: Thanks, and don't call me kid, my name is Jonathan. Perry: Farewell then Jonathan. So, you are a NAWC fan aren't you ? Perry: Yes, I could say so. You are going to fight that loser Dynamite
Derrick. Perry: Why are you calling him a loser ? Jonathan: Because he is. He doesn't have a decent match in the NAWC, and
I don't think many fans like him either. I hope you can be better than him.
Perry: You will see... Here Jonathan, have this. [ Perry signs an autograph and gives it to Jonathan ] Jonathan: Thanks mister. [ The kid runs down the street. The camera focus on Perry again. He is now looking at the camera, while he and Careen keeps walking ]
Perry: Dynamite Derrick, a loser ? Hummmm, that's something to think about. I don't know much about this guy myself. What I know is that he is standing on my way. I want to cause a good impression on my first match you know... I have to agree that Derrick doesn't seen that great of a wrestler, nor the event seens like the way I've expected, afterall, it's just a house show. But time had the patience to teach me, and I've understood that big stars came from small events, against small creatures. The creature I must take down now is Dynamite Derrick. I don't fear him nor his wrestling abillities, especially if he is a loser like that kid said.
[ Careen grabs a piece of paper out of her pocket with the card for the house show and a picture of every wrestler in the card. She looks at the
]
Careen: Derrick doesn't look very smart either. Perry: Yes, maybe you are right Careen. Maybe I'm just wasting my time talking with a no talented wrestler, which, god knows why, is on this federation. But again... Maybe not. I'm ready to take many surprises, that's why I'm not going to underestimate my opponent. But I want to end this one quick, winning or losing, this will be the end of the line for
Derrick, no matter what the results say, I will give him some good lessons of my wrestling style, the Rocket style. And of course, get the attention of the crowd. This is the begining of everything.
[ Perry and Careen stops in front of the E Center. The limo already awaits then in front of it ]
Perry: So here we are. This will be the place were I'm going to have my first match. And this should be the place where I shall get my first win and my first fan recognicion, this is just the begining for the future new sensation of wrestling. Forever and ever, shall it be.
[ Perry and Careen enters the E Center. The camera fades out ] Killer Buddha -------------- INTERIOR - MARRIOT MARQUIS PARKING LOT - SALT LAKE CITY - NIGHT Killer Buddha and Jay Bennetto get out of a rented black Chevy Van. Jay is carrying a black gym bag. The duo walk towards the Marriot's underground entrance.
JAY BENNETTO: Hey "K", are you sure that Stevens and his tramp are staying here tonight? KILLER BUDDHA: Word on the street is that Runty Rick and his pet in a dress are here tonight.
CUT TO: INTERIOR - MARRIOT MARQUIS HOTEL - NIGHT The duo of Killer Buddha and Jay Bennetto enter the lobby. They make a beeline for the front desk.
FRONT DESK GUY: How can I help you, Sir? KILLER BUDDHA: Yeah, We're here from the NAWC. What room is Rick Stevens in? CUT TO: INTERIOR - MARRIOT MARQUIS - STEVEN'S HOTEL ROOM - NIGHT We see a large fist knocking on the door. The door opens and reveals an extremely muscular guy wearing a Rick Stevens T-Shirt in the doorway.
His name is Chisel. CHISEL: How can I help you folks? Killer Buddha makes no verbal reply, he just Buddha Bashes Chisel with a close fisted blow to the face. Chisel falls backward as Killer Buddha and Jay Bennetto move into the very luxurious hotel room.Another incredibly muscular guy, Pounder, jumps up from the sofa. He goes to grab Jay Bennetto.
KILLER BUDDHA: Hey Beastmaster, you touch that man and you're going to be having breakfast with St. Peter tomorrow morning, I guarantee it.
POUNDER: Whadda' Ya Want? KILLER BUDDHA: Where's Stevens and his pet chimp, Lady Divine? Pounder looks at Chisel. Chisel answers. CHISEL: They're not here, but you can leave a message if you want to. (Killer Buddha smiles.) KILLER BUDDHA: Oh, I'm going to want to. Uncle Jay, I think we should introduce these gentleman to "Little Buddha".
Jay places the gym bag he's been carrying on the sofa. He opens it and takes out "Little Buddha", handing it to his nephew. Jay Bennetto closes the door to the room. As soon as the door closes, the sounds of crashes, bashes, and bodies hitting walls and furniture can be heard. We listen for what seems to be an eternity. Suddenly the sounds from the room come to an abrupt halt. Killer Buddha and Jay Bennetto come out of the Hotel
Room. They seem to be relatively unscathed.
KILLER BUDDHA: Those guys must be Runty Rick's sisters. Buddha places a bloody "Little Buddha" back into the gym bag that his
Uncle Jay is carrying across his left shoulder. Buddha and Jay walk down the hallway.
KILLER BUDDHA: Hey Uncle Jay, where did you learn some of those moves? JAY BENNETTO: I was a Chicago Cop for twenty years, you learn everything on the streets of Chicago.
Killer Buddha laughs. The duo are waiting for the elevator. Buddha looks into the camera. KILLER BUDDHA: Roach Stevens, you sniveling piece of Gorilla Crap. You're lucky you weren't in tonight, I would have pulverized you. Killer
Buddha is set to bash you on Saturday at the E Center, and then I have to see your ugly face two more times during Nuclear Winter. Ever since you got married to that Urine Stain with a Brain, Alka-Seltzer and his pet monkey Rio "The Degenerate" Giniro, you've been acting strange. No.
I'm wrong. You've always been stupid and Lady Divine has always been ugly. Runty Rick, you're so stupid you think a hot meal is stolen food.
Lady Divine is so ugly, that when she walks into the kitchen, the rats jump on the table and start screaming. This Saturday I'm going to give you the ultimate Buddha Beatdown. At Nuclear Winter, I'm going to introduce you and that moron the Green Pooper Scooper to "Little Buddha"
during our Hardcore Title Match. Believe this, Killer Buddha will become the Hardcore Champ at Nuclear Winter. Later on that same night, The Killer Elite are going to make you and that slimeball Rio call your Momma's, then the Franchise and I will go on to become the NAWC Tag Team Champions. So Runty Rick, you and all the other
NAWC Superchumps better live in fear. Killer Buddha is coming for you and Hell is coming with me!!!
(The elevator arrives.) (Fade To Black.) Speedy Irons -------------- _ _ _\PEEDY IRON_\ ============== *Sharon Roberts stands in the back with Speedy Irons. She's dressed in a pink top and blue skirt, she also has blue high-heel shoes. The
Speedster's in his regular cyan-green outfit.*
Sharon: "So, Speedy, the first NAWC Napalm was quite the show for you! You beat three guys that night!" Speedy: "Well, Trooper was asking for it!!! As for Jeff Walker, we put up a good fight, but I was the better guy!"
Sharon: "Then something strange happened; you interfered in the tag-match between Douglas/Sloan and Stevens/ D. Giniro. Why?"
Speedy: "Well, isn't it obvious? Dinosaur Sloan was loosing the match, so this 'NOBODY' had to help him out!"
Sharon: "Well, you sure are making enemies here in the NAWC: Green
Mountain Trooper, Thane Sloan and now Rick Stevens!"
Speedy: "Well, like I said before; Dinosaur needed an assist! I mean did you see that match? Damien Douglas was doing fine in there, but then Sloan screwed it all up! Besides, Douglas is gonna be my partner at the PPV NAWC Nuclear Winter! Now the guy knows he can count on me.
I promise that we'll hold the NAWC Tag-Gold!" Sharon: "On to the House Show. You face the NAWC IC Champ Chris Phear!" Speedy: "Well, honey, I have no fear! Chris this is a non-title match, but I'll beat you nevertheless! Just to prove that the fastest rising youngster in the world of wrestling today can kick your sorry ass around!!! Phear, you better fear the FootLoose, 'cause it's followed up by the Iron Dropper! After that it's gonna be 1...2...3! ... another victory for me!!! Phear,
that's a promise, 'cause I'm hot and you are DEFINITELY NOT!!!!!" Sharon: "Well, Speedster, you're definitely on a roll here in the NAWC! Good luck!" *Scene cuts to a commercial for the NAWC PPV Extravaganza: Nuclear Winter.* Speedy: "So, the NAWC has a new show: NAWC Napalm! And the fastest rising youngster in the world of wrestling today is in it!!! I'm facing
'Mr. Bitter' Jeff Walker. Well, bitterness gets you nowhere, Walker!!!
So, Jeff, on the first Napalm show you're going down! I'm gonna beat all the bitterness out of you! That's a promise, 'cause I'm hot and you are DEFINITELY NOT!!!!! Next there's NAWC's PPV Extravaganza: Nuclear Winter! Quite a night for me;
first I team up with Long S. Tran against Raul Deganeir. Well that's a stupid name, Raul, so kiss your NAWC carreer goodbye! Next I'm teamed with Damien Douglas in a tag-team battle royal for the NAWC tag-straps!
Well I would've preferred Mr. Tran as my partner, but I won't ruin my first chance at NAWC GOLD!!! So, Damien, we're gonna hang in there
'till the end! That's it for now! Walker, prepare to go down!"
*Screen fades to black.* Rick Stevens ------------- [Greg Hollinsworth is seen making his way towards Rick Stevens locker room, he knocks on the door and waits for a answer]
Voice: Yeah, who is it? GH: Greg Hollinsworth, I was hoping to get a word with Rick, if you don't mind. Voice: Come on in [Greg open's the door and enters. The first thing you see is Lady
Divine wrapped in a towel blow drying her hair. Rick is against the far corner, lacing his boots]
Greg: Uhmmm I'm sorry Lady Divine, I can come back. Lady Divine: Nonsense Greg, you know you like what you see, have your little interview with Rick, and enjoy the view.
[Lady Divine flashes a quick smile, then continues to dry her hair] Greg: Rick, Tom Knight has offered you a shot at the heavyweight title at Nuclear Winter. What do you have to say?
[Rick looks up, brushes his hair out of his eyes and stares right at the camera] Rick: Tom Knight, you speak of respect and honor, two words that mean next to nothing to me. Ya see since day one of entering into the NAWC,
I've been held back. Forced to face goof's such as Killer Buddha. Now
Al Capulco has shown me the light, and has made my path to greatness clear. You want to issue me a challenge for your title? Fine I accept your terms. I _PROMISE_ Rio and Al will NOT be at ringside.
GH: Speaking of Rio and Al, you along with Lady Divine, Rio and Al have seemed to form a stable of sort, yet we know very little about it.
What's it's name? what's the gameplan? Rick: :Chuckling: You will know what we choose to let you know, fate works in mysterious way's and has brought two of the best the NAWC has to offer together, and in time all will bow to us.
GH: You promise'd that Rio and Al won't be at ringside, what's your plan? Rick: Again you will see when I want you to see. First off I have that hardcore title match, then Rio and I will destroy the other teams and go for the tag straps. Finally I will bring the heavyweight title back to
Al, Rio and Lady Divine.
GH: Since Rio was the champ, do you think that can cause problems if you win it? Rick: Not a chance, we are like brothers. If he win's it thats fantastic, and I know he feels the same for me. You see that is what puts us above the others. We are in this as a team.
GH: Lady Divine, if I could I'd like to get a few words about your match for the women's title
Lady Divine: Sure what's up, besides you? [Lady Divine smirks] GH: Well, uh... I.. [Lady Divine walks over and removes the mic from Greg's hand] Lady Divine: Well it see's he's at a loss for words so let me handle this. It seems to me at the pay per view our group will clean up..
First off Rick will bring back the Hardcore title, then Rick and Rio will win the tag straps. Then Rick will yet again win and bring back the heavyweight title. But in between all of these, I have a match for the ladies title. Ya see it's all about being a lady, something I know all to well about. I am the only one going into that match with wrestling experience. And I've
proven it time and time again I am not just about my looks, I have more experience then any of the other ladies in that ring. So come the 30th,
I will dominate and bring home the gold. Now I believe that's enough for now.
[Lady Divine hands the mic back to Greg who stands up and walks away, as the camera fades] Big Wreck ----------- The sun is setting over the mountains in Nevada. The sky is colored with a fiery red hue. In the distance, a dark bank of clouds foreshadows the impending storm.
A Harley is seen driving through the desert. It turns off the main road, and heads up one of the small mountains. The bike reaches the top, and is shut off by the driver. He sets his bike up, takes off his helmet, and walks to the edge of the cliff.
Staring at the sunset, and the world before him, he thinks about his past, his present, and his future.
"What have I really done with this life?? Living for years just to please an unpleaseable father.... Finally striking out on my own to live my life-long fantasy... Was it my dream that was actually accomplished???
In many ways, it was. I became a professional wrestler. But, it didn't work out the way I had hoped or wanted. I spent the last 2 years jumping from indy promotion to indy promotion, and when I finally get my shot at the big times, I blow it.
My dream has to be fulfilled. I need to find a place to call home. And this time, I will stick with it, live through the good and the bad time, because I know, I have the power to be the best. The rest of the world will find out...."
Deep in thought, he never even realized the wind had picked up and the clouds have become darker. A lone piece of paper floats by on the breeze and lands at his foot. The sheet seems to be an ad for the NAWC, promising to be the next biggest wrestling promotion.
Quickly, he jumps on his Harley, and heads for the address on the ad. Thane Sloan ------------- (Thane stands in the back with an NAWC camera crew. He is wearing black slacks and a gray Educator polo shirt.)
Thane: Damien Douglas, I brought you hear to watch my back. Now I am not so sure what the hell I was smoking when I came up with the idea. That is a problem I can correct.
Chris Phear my attention is now squarely on you. For the life of me, I don't know how you managed to manipulate the NAWC suits to give you a crack at the IC Title. Quite frankly, The Educator does not give a rats, but then you have the testicular fortitude to challenge me to a match. After I accept you continue to step out of line by proclaiming to the world that since you hold the gold that I have no stroke.
The Educator is going to squash that rumor right here and right now. You see little man, it is you who wants a piece of me. For if it was up to me, I would not face such inferior talent. That's right Chris your not in The Educator's league. Hell your not even in the ball park and you know it. Seeing you struggle with Speedy Irons is all the proof The
Educator needs. You see, I know Speedy. As I recall, it took me less than ten minutes to Educator the young man and he beat you tonight.
(Thane laughs to himself.) So consider this Chris, you challenged me, so you had no choice but to accept my terms or I would have refused to get in the ring with you.
Then you would have went to the owner and asked him to force me into the match. However, your pleas would have fallen on deaf ears after your performance tonight. Now that you know where you stand in this, don't ever defame The Educator's reputation again.
Finally Mr. Phear your Education will begin and will be completed at
Nuclear Winter. Then and only then you can get on the phone and your mommy and tell her that you to have been educated by The Educator.
While, I am on the topic of people defaming The Educator's reputation, I feel it necessary to reply to the remarks made by our beloved leader and
champion Mr. Tom Knight. Who said something to the effect that my routine is getting old. This comes from a man who runs around claiming he is a knight in shining armor, and has Guinivere by his side. The
Educator wants to know where in the world is Merlin. You talk about something that is old. Well Mr. Knight, dark days lay ahead for you, and in the end there will be no light after our fight. However, you will be seeing stars, after you too are educated by The Educator.
(Thane turns and walks away.) Claw Richens -------------- [Scene opens backstage at the E Center in Salt Lake City Utah. The crowd is still going crazy as Speedy Irons and Chris
Phear are beginning their match in the ring. There are people running everywhere backstage, operating the lights and Big
Screen, and running the sound system. We see Claw Richens exiting through the curtain at the top of the ramp with the
NAWC Lightweight Title Belt over his shoulder. The second he steps through he gets a smile across his face and looks around.]
"3 down." [He begins walking down the rampway into the main backstage area. There are moniters everwhere watching the action in the ring. Claw notices
Irons hit an awesome looking snap suplex as he walks by the moniters.
He hasn't had much time to look at other matches in the NAWC yet, but he has done some studying of his next opponent, Donovan Moore. He stops as he hears his name being called from behind.]
"Claw! Claw Richens!" [Claw spins around to see the shapley figure of Sharon Roberts chasing after him.] Claw Richens : Oh, hey Sharon. What can I do for you? [Sharon gives Claw a sly smile.] Sharon Roberts : Oh, I was just wondering if you had a minute to give me a few quick words....?
Claw Richens : Yeah, sure why not. Where? Sharon Roberts : Well, right here if you don't mind. [to the side.] Andy! Andy, grab your camera and meet me back here! [Andy, a young man off to the side nods, and disappears off to the side to grab his camera gear.]
Sharon Roberts : It'll just be a minute while Andy grabs his camera stuff. So tell me, how do you like it here so far?
Claw Richens : Well, I'm 3 and 0 so far, not to mention the lightweight champion, and I haven't felt much better in a long time. It's great to be back in an active league.
Sharon Roberts : Yeah, things are going great in the NAWC! Claw Richens : So it seems. I came at just the right time. Andy : [Walks up behind Sharon.] Hey. Sharon Roberts : Oh, hi Andy. Claw, this is our resident cameraman,
Andy Schuerman. He covers jobs like... well, like this one.
Claw Richens : Hey Andy. Sharon Roberts : Everyone ready? Andy : Yep. Claw Richens : Yep. Sharon Roberts : Great!... on me in .....5......4......3.......2......1.. [The red light hits the top of the camera, and Sharon puts on her game face. She brings a microphone up to her face.]
Sharon Roberts : This is Sharon Roberts here in Salt Lake City, and with me is the current NAWC Lightweight Champion, Claw Richens. Hello Claw!
Claw Richens : Hello Sharon. Sharon Roberts : Now Claw, a lot of people are going around saying that you don't deserve the Lightweight Championship because of your size.
What are your comments on that? Claw Richens : The fact of the matter is this, Sharon. The scale read 239. I can't argue with that. My scale doesn't read 239, but you know what - my scale isn't worth anything in the NAWC. It comes down to this
: I am the Lightweight Champion. And I will be the most dominating
Lightweight Champion in the history of this title, because I am the
BIGGEST Lightweight Champion in the history of this title.
Sharon Roberts : Well, we saw you dominate Damien Douglas tonight, and come out with a clean pinfall victory, after the devestating Claw's Edge. What did you think of your performance tonight?
Claw Richens : Throughout my career I've been called many things, and you'll probably hear a lot of them reiterated here in this league - but the one I'm going to say right now is that I've been called the Victory
Machine. What does that mean? That simply means this : When I give it my all, I'm damn hard to beat. There isn't a man in the locker room back here that I don't think I could give a run for his money, and I'm not afraid to admit it. Lightweight champion is only the first step for me here.
Sharon Roberts : Do you have any comments for your opponent at Nuclear
Winter, Donovan Moore?
Claw Richens : I just hope Moore doesn't think that he has this match wrapped up by any means. I am going to result to all tactics possible to win that match. I am the champion, and he would do well not to forget that. I beat him once before, and it could very easily happen again. Many have fallen in the Claw's Edge, and Moore will be just one more in my belt notch. Don't get me wrong Sharon, I believe Donovan
Moore is a worthy competitor to my title, but that's just it. He's not a worthy Lightweight Champion. And I, Claw Richens, the Most DOMINATING
Lightweight Champion in the history of the title, will come out victorious every time. It only took me two matches in this federation to get gold, and I'll be damned if I'll lose that gold any time soon.
Mr. Palmer, you got your money's worth when you invested in this Alaskan
Brawler. I garuntee you that Donovan Moore will be just the fourth of many to fall before me.
Sharon Roberts : And that's that. Folks, in just 6 days, Claw Richens and Donovan Moore will meet in the squared circle to vie for the NAWC
Lightweight Championship. I, for one, can't wait. For the NAWC, this is Sharon Roberts.
[Scene ends.] ANNOUNCEMENTS Great work to all on the roleplays. Also I wanted to welcome two newcomers to the NAWC. They are Chris King and Enfirno. Welcome to the NAWC you two.
That's all for now. Hope you all enjoyed the House Show. I'll send a packet out again on Wednesday.
