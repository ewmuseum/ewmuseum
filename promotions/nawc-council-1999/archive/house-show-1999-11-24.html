NAWC House Show

Sorry this is out so late. My computer froze last night and I lost a lot of information that I hadn't saved yet. So here's the entire House Show.
NAWC House Show Live from the Sports Medical Center in Birmingham, Alabama Wednesday, November 24, 1999 Hello everybody, and welcome to NAWC House Show. I'm Orlando Palla and
I'll be summarizing the latest NAWC House Shows for you.
As the night began, the fans that filled the Sports Medical Center were really pumped for the show.
Alex "Alligator" McKay Vs. Blackjack As Alex McKay came to the ring, he took the mic and began to bad mouth
American fighters. He told Birmingham fans that Americans didn't have any talent. Blackjack's music played. He ran to the ring and began and onslaught on McKay. Blackjack dominated for much of the early part of the match. He hit several big suplexes on McKay. The crowd was really behind him as he represented America. As Blackjack pulled him up for another move, McKay raked his eyes. Alligator then got control after hitting a great DDT. He then followed that up with an Ankle Lock.
Blackjack fought out of it and once again regained control. He hit a running powerbomb, then a choke slam, and finally hit the Clothesline
From Hell for his first win in the NAWC.
Winner: Blackjack Omar "Arachnis" Shakiem Vs. Raul De'Ganeir This one was very even. Neither of these men could get the upper hand for quite a while. Finally, Arachnis got the advantage after sending
Raul off the ropes and spearing him. Arachnis continued his attack by using several power and technical moves; including a bulldog, Russian leg sweep, and Evenflow DDT. He then pulled Raul up for a jackhammer and went for the pin. However, he only got a two count. He then went to work on Raul's knees and eventually locked him into a scorpion death lock. Raul fought desperately to escape. At one point, referee Doc
Draven thought Raul had fallen unconscious. He ordered Arachnis off
Raul and was about to call for the bell when De'Ganeir got up and mounted an incredible comeback utilizing several high risk moves. Many a time he went to the ropes and connected with a springboard move. He even nailed Arachnis with a beautiful springboard clothesline. The crowd was really into this great match. In the beginning of the match, they were cheering on Arachnis but as soon as De'Ganeir turned the tables and used his high flying abilities, they quickly sided with him.
After a long match, De'Ganeir finally got the win with the Black Magik. Winner: Raul De'Ganeir Brimstone Vs. "The Cavalier" Tom Knight Next we gave Birmingham fans a special treat in letting them see first hand the Second Round of the World Title Tournament. As this one started off, Brimstone took control with his brute strength. The
Cavalier quickly reversed the situation as he nailed Brimstone with several dropkicks and moonsaults. He then hit Brimstone with a devastating shoulderbreaker. The Cavalier went to the top turnbuckle and prepared for Excalibur. Brimstone got to his feet and The Cavalier leapt off. But, in one of the most memorable moments in wrestling history, Brimstone countered it with an arm drag takeover. He then went to work on The Cavalier. Hitting some deadly moves including a Pedigree that got him a two count. He then pulled The Cavalier up for a
Michinoku Driver for another two count. The frustration was clear as he pounded his fist on the canvas. Brimstone brought The Cavalier to his feet and prepared for the Call of the Demons. However, The Cavalier pulled out a surprise hurricanrana. He then went up top and hit
Excalibur. Three seconds later, The Cavalier got the pinfall. Winner: "The Cavalier" Tom Knight "Down Under" Derek Davies Vs. "The Extremist" Tedd Olczak We continued with tournament action. Down Under just dismantled The
Extremist. Davies hit several of his big moves, including a powerbomb, followed by a piledriver. Davies then pulled Olczak up. Davies hooked him and brought him up for a vertical suplex. He then hit a series of suplexes including a superplex off the top turnbuckle. He then hit a swinging neckbreaker followed by the infamous Whipcracker for the 1 ...
2 ... 3. Winner: "Down Under" Derek Davies Assassin Vs. The Franchise Vs. Agony in 3-Way Bed of Nails Elimination
Match This one was out and out a pure war. All three men went at it right away. The Franchise and Agony double teamed Assassin. They executed a very nice Russian legsweep reverse DDT combo. Moments later, Assassin was powerbombed by Agony onto the bed of nails. Paramedics rushed to ringside where they carefully removed him and took him to the back where they hoisted him into an ambulance. Meanwhile, Agony and The Franchise were exchange blows. The Franchise scooped Agony up and slamed him back to the mat. He then followed with an elbow drop. He missed as Agony rolled out of the way. Agony got the advantage and began to mount an offense. The fans were well behind The Franchise and encouraged him to fight back. Agony hit him with some spectacular moves like a running powerbomb and a brainbuster. But, as Agony went for the Art of War, The
Franchise blocked it and hoisted Agony up into a piledriver position.
He walked over to the bed of nails and piledrove Agony's head right into the bed. He too was taken to the hospitals. The Franchise was declared the winner.
Winner: The Franchise Randall Reign Vs. Cyric As soon as this match was underway, Cyric was using some cheap shots like an eye gouge and a face rake. After that, however, he was all business. To the fans' delight, he pulled out a series of fantastic high risk moves. He hit a beautiful Asai moonsault followed by a flying legdrop which he missed. Randall Reign got on the offense and nailed
Cyric with a series of stiff punches. Next he connected with a series of big clotheslines. He then slammed home a series of nice suplexes, a belly to back and a vertical suplex. Randall then went to the top and nailed a perfect missile dropkick. He went for the cover but only got a two count. He then pulled Cyric up and went for his patented Reign
Storm but Cyric countered with a low blow. Cyric then took him over with a released dragon suplex. Cyric rolled him over and applied a camel clutch submission. Reign fought hard for the break and after several minutes, he reached the ropes and got the break. But the damage was done. Cyric locked him in the Medieval Vice and got the win as
Reign quickly tapped out from the pain.
Winner: Cyric Dr. Hate Vs. Killer Buddha This one folks, was a war. Both of these men were very equally matched. Though Killer Buddha was much taller, and heavier than Dr.
Hate, Dr. Hate had the wrestling expertise to balance that disadvantage. Buddha started off using his strength to get the advantage. He delivered several nice suplexes; a vertical suplex, a belly to belly suplex, and then a german suplex with a bridge. However he only got the two count. He pulled Dr. Hate up and continued his assault. But, as Killer Buddha went for Instant Karma, Dr. Hate blocked it and countered with a back heel kick. He then nailed Buddha with several other kicks including a crescent kick and a roundhouse kick.
Buddha was quickly floored and shocked at this comeback. He then pulled big Killer Buddha up for a German suplex with a bridge. He got only a two count. Dr. Hate then whipped Buddha to the ropes and as Killer
Buddha returned, Dr. Hate executed the Assisted Suicide for the win.
Winner: Dr. Hate Rick Stevens Vs. British Wonder This match was yet another war. But only one side was firing the guns. Rick Stevens manhandled British Wonder. Hitting him with a jumping powerbomb for a two count. Stevens then pulled him up and executed a perfect sidewalk slam followed by a Tombstone piledriver. He pulled
British Wonder up for the Painkiller and nailed it. Referee Doc Draven made the count and Rick Stevens got the pin.
Winner: Rick Stevens MAIN EVENT Thane Sloan Vs. Rio D. Giniro - Non-Title match These two men went back and forth. Neither gaining the advantage. Eventually, Rio got Thane in a bear hug followed by a full nelson suplex. He then dropped a leg He pulled Sloan up and whipped him to the ropes. Sloan came off and Rio went for a drop toe hold but Thane jumped over it. Sloan came off the other side and connected with a shoulder block to take Rio down. The battle spilled to the outside as
Thane took charge. Throwing Rio to the steps and guardrail. Thane
Sloan was merciless. Rio regained the advantage and began hitting a series of sidewalk slams. He went for the cover but amazingly, Thane got a shoulder up. He then sat Thane up and nailed his SunSet. And to the crowd's amazement, he did not get the pinfall. Thane Sloan had kicked out of the SunSet. Something nobody else had done. Rio showed little concern and continued his offense. He pulled Thane up and lifted him into a gutwrench suplex. Thane fought back and made another comeback. The two fought back and forth for another while. But, as Rio went for his second SunSet, Rick Stevens jumped out of the crowd and attacked him. Rio felt the Painkiller. Referee Doc Draven let the match go on. Thane Sloan pulled him up and hit The Educator for the win.
Winner: Thane Sloan
