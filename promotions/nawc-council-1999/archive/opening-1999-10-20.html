NAWC (1999) — Newsdesk

Official Opening, 20 October 1999

20 October 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I've been working on the NAWC web site for quite a while now. Getting everything to work as I want it to. And now it's done. Today,
Wednesday, October 20, 1999, marks the day that the North American
Wrestling Council's doors have opened. Stop by the arena, located at
http://nawc.cjb.net We'll give you a free tour around. If you like what you see, just stop by the front office. Fill out an application form. And if the Executive Committee approves of your application, you'll join what will be the future of professional wrestling. We don't claim to be the best because we're not. But we need YOUR help to help us reach that level. Below is what we are about and what we are looking for in a roster.
The NAWC has one weekly card called Fight Night Friday. There may also be a smaller House Show on Wednesdays but these cards don't always happen.
Each handler is allowed two (2) wrestlers. We are looking for handlers that can roleplay at least ten good lines of roleplay. We don't use message boards. Instead, we use email. No real wrestlers or ripoffs are allowed (such as The Rock or Stone
Cold.) When applying, submit a sample roleplay so the Executive Committee can decide better if they should sign you. If you do not submit a sample roleplay, you will not be signed.
For more information, check out the Rulebook. You will find a link to it at http://nawc.cjb.net Please take a look and sign up today. Adrian Palmer - Owner North American Wrestling Council http://nawc.cjb.net
