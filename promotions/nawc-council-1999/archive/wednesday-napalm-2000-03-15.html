Wednesday Napalm (15 March)

NAWC WEDNESDAY NAPALM Live from Freedom Hall in Johnson City, Tennessee Wednesday, March 15, 2000 **********Dr. Love & Nurse Candy Hart Vs. Brimstone & Hellfire*********** (Mixed Tag Match) Dr. Love and nurse Candy came out very strong dominating their two opponents. Finally Dr. Love and Miss Candy hit a beautiful Superplex/Top
Rope Splash combo for the win in 4:25
***************"The Technician" Ian Walsh & The Green Mountain Trooper Vs. The Titan & "Rocket Star" Perry Harrils***************** This match started off the Ian Walsh and Titan in one corner trading blows and GMT and Harris in the other corner trading blows. The referee finally got control of the match and it turned out to be Walsh vs. Harris until
Harris made the hot tag to Titan, titan came in like a ball of fire, eventually defeating Walsh by a rollup pinfall in 8:56
*************Gravis Vs. Mad Dog************* Mad Dog had an impressive showing tonight with a total Domination of Gravis from the start, eventually Gravis used a Chair on Mad Dog out of desperate measures and was DQ'ed
*********"The Superstar" Jordan Howitt Vs. Tsunami*********** This match Never got underway and Rick Stevens and the other members of the threat got involved using Chairs and a lead pipe to take out these two men until Dynamite Derrick and The Bandit Jason Blake ran in and scared off the
Threat. At that point Dynamite picked up a mic and had this to say:
Derrick:"Blake it's kidna odd that you being the TV Champion would be out here right now because the surprise I have for everyone involves what you have <pointing to the TV Title.> You see Friday night Brimstone and I fought in a cage and Rick Stevens and his butt buddies <pop from crowd.> decided that they would get involved. However, if we watch the video again on the big screen you'll see that my feet hit the ground first."
<Big screen cuts to the video where Derrick's shown being kicked out of the ring and his feet hitting the floor first.>
Derrick:"So therefore, I have this statement from the NAWC office to read. "After reviewing the video of the Dynamite Derrick Brimstone #1 contender
Match we have decided that Derricks' feet did hit the ground first, so therefore the committee has decided to give the TV Title Shot at the next
NAWC PPV to Dynamite Derrick in a match style of Blake's choice. The NAWC
Committee." So there ya go Blake be prepared and shine my belt up for me, and as for Brimstone too damn bad."
<Derrick throws down the mic and leaves as Blake stands in disbelief.> ***************"Bloodhound" Gunther McKnight Vs. "The Cavalier" Tom Knight**************** Non-Title Match This was a wrestling clinic put on by both men in this Non Title Main Event. The two men went a classic 15 minutes, ending in a double pinfall, with both men being unhappy, the match was restarted by the referee and at the 29 minute mark Bloodhound pulled out an incredible German Suplex to Pin Knight in a non title match. The wrestling clinic sent all viewers and fans away happy.
