[Cain's music blares around the arena and the crowd gives a mixed reaction as he comes out. He looks at them with a confused look. He then makes his way to the ring as fireworks shoot off around him.]
Smokey: This newcomer has been given a huge chance here to prove himself! Jenny: You're telling me! A match against the NAWC's former two time World champion!! I sure hope Cobra's bite is worse than his bark tonight!
Because he has a tough fight ahead of him. Smokey: And don't forget his other opponent, The Green Mountain Trooper. There's another big man. Brent: And his opponent, from Bradford, Vermont, and weighing 333 pounds, THE GREEN MOUNTAIN TROOPER!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! [Trooper's music plays and he comes out in his usual Trooper's attire.] Smokey: Here comes the Trooper. We still haven't heard anything about his friend Hans. Word is he's not even in the arena yet.
Jenny: Well he better get here awfully quick! Because I'm sure Rio will have extra help in this match!
Smokey: No doubt about it! Jenny: The Trooper is getting in the ring now and Cain's going at him right away! Not waiting for Rio or the bell!! Cain's landing shots all over the
Trooper!! Brent: And their opponent, from Flint, Michigan, accompanied to the ring by The Threat, he weighs in at 325 pounds, RIO D. GINIRO!!!!!!!!!!!!! Smokey: Hmm. Rio gives up 8 pounds in weight to the Trooper! That's an interesting tidbit of information!
(The lights go out and "Copa Cabana" begins playing. The crowd errupts in a flurry of booing. The spotlight shines down upon the entranceway. After about a few moments Al Capulco steps from behind the curtain followed by
Chris Phear and Rick Stevens with Lady Divine. Rick and Chris each take a stand beside Al holding up their titles. Al holds up a sign that reads
"We've been robbed" on one side and "Here comes your HERO" on the other. Al looks back toward the entrance and out walks the wonder known as Rio. He bares a very angered look upon his face breathing heavy. Al then pulls a mic looking down to the ring.)
Al: What happened here tonight was a travesty... Palmer I know you can hear me. If you are wondering why I havent left well you forgot one thing... I still have a managers license. Which gives me the right to be here... As for you Tom Knight. Your time will come and you will have to suffer the conciquences... But for now we are just going to have to take it out on these poor saps.. I hope you two are happy with what is about to happen,,,,
(Al tosses down the mic and he and the rest of the "Threat" escort Rio slowely to the ring as the crowd chants AAAASSSSS-HOLE.)
Smokey: Ha! These fans here in Anaheim don't like The Threat at all! Rio's getting into the ring and he looks really mad tonight!
Jenny: Meanwhile, the Trooper and Chris Cain are still going at it!! Rio grabs Cain!! And throws him over the top rope to the floor!! And The
Threat is picking away at him!! Rick and Phear are getting in their shots as they pleased!! And look at Al Capulco getting in the cheap shots too!
Crowd: AAAAAAASSSSSHOOOLE!!!!!!! AAAAAAAASSSSSSSSHOOOOOOOOOLE!!!!!!!!! Smokey: These fans sure do know how to express their opinions!! In the ring, Rio and Trooper are going at it toe to toe!! Both men are huge so neither's getting an advantage! They lock up now!! And we've got a test of strength!! Wow!!! It looks like The Green Mountain Trooper is the stronger man!!! He's got the leverage on Rio!! But Rio with a big knee to the gut!!
Jenny: Ohhhhh. Smokey: Another!! Rio backs the Trooper to the ropes! Whips him across!! Trooper reverses!! Rio off the other side!! And Trooper with a clothesline!! Jenny: But Rio didn't even budge hardly!! Back at ringside, Chris Cain is laid out on the floor!! Rick Stevens and Chris Phear are setting up a table they got from under the ring!! How many tables do they have under that ring?!!
Smokey: I don't know Jenny but we have another stand still between the Trooper and Rio!! Trooper throws a big right hand! Rio with a left!! Trooper with another right!! Rio with a shot to the stomach!! And Trooper headbutts him!! Rio with a rake of the eyes!! And he backs the Trooper to the ropes! Whip across!! Trooper comes off!! Big running lariat by Rio!!!
and he got the Trooper down on that one!! Jenny: Rio's climbing up to the top turnbuckle now!! What in God's name is he gonna do?!! Smokey: Only God knows Jenny! Only God knows. But Stevens has put Cain on the table at ringside now!! Stevens and Phear are nailing shots in on him now!! It looks like Rio's gonna do some kind of aerial maneuver!! That would be a sight!!
Jenny: The Trooper is getting back up now!! And he sees Rio on the top turnbuckle!! Trooper runs!! And clotheslines him!
Smokey: RIO DOES A SOMMERSAULT DOWN!!! AND SENTON BACK SPLASHES CAIN!!!! I THINK HE DIDN'T INTEND TO DO THAT!!! BUT THE TROOPER SHOVED HIM OFF!!! Jenny: That was amazing!! And Chris Cain just got squashed by 325 pounds of muscle!! Smokey: Rio's back up now!! And he doesn't seem to be hurt in any way!! Maybe a little shaken up but that's all! Rio folds the chair up and puts it on the ground! Capulco straightens it up!! And Rio's pulling Cain up from that shattered table!! Rio has him in a side slam position!! AND NAILS IT
ON THE CHAIR!!! Jenny: The Green Mountain Trooper is coming out now!! And all of The Threat is going after him!! Stevens, Phear, Rio, even Lady Divine and Capulco getting in cheap kicks!! This is disgusting!!!
Smokey: But it's perfectly legal!!! [An unknown song begins playing and the fans are dumb founded.] Smokey: What's this?!!! Who's this?!!! Wait a minute!! It's Hans!!!! He is here!!!! Hans the Icelandic Enforcer!!!!
Jenny: Phear sees him and is going after him!! Hans catches him!! SPINEBUSTER ON THE STAGE!!!! Smokey: And Hans is proceeding to the ring!!! Meanwhile, Rick and Rio are rolling the Trooper back into the ring!! Stevens is staying on the floor!!
Rio is back in!! Stevens hands him another chair!! AND RIO PASTES THE TROOPER WITH IT!!!!!! Jenny: Rio's preparing him for the SunSet!! At ringside, Rick Stevens and Hans are battling it out!!! Hans with a big headbutt!! Stevens is reeling!! Hans boots him!!! And powerbombs him to the concrete!!!
Smokey: But in the ring, Rio just nailed the SunSet!!! And he's going for the cover!! 1 ........... Jenny: Hans is getting in the ring!!! 2 ................ Smokey: And boots Rio off of the Trooper!! Hans is nailing shots on him like a mad man!!! Jenny: But Rio's fighting back!! And the Trooper's starting to get up a bit!! Rick Stevens is getting onto the apron!! Rio sends Hans to the ropes!! And Hans bumps Rick off!!! Rio didn't even see him there!!
Smokey: Here comes Rio now!! BUT HANS CATCHES HIM!!!! AND CHOKE SLAMS HIM!!!!! The Green Mountain Trooper is getting back up!! Hans signals him to go for the pin!!
1 ............. Jenny: HEY!!!! HANS IS HOLDING RIO'S FEET DOWN SO HE CAN'T KICK OUT!!!! 2 .................... Smokey: Hans with a cheap shot to the midsection of a downed Rio!!!! But Bryant isn't seeing any of this!!! 3!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! Jenny: The Trooper gets the upset win!!!! All thanks to his buddy Hans the Icelandic Enforcer!!!! Smokey: An enforcer he was!!! Brent: Here is your winner, THE GREEN MOUNTAIN TROOPER!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (An older man wearing a white suit, cowboy hat and boots enters the ring. Hans picks up the battered Green Mountain Trooper.) Smokey: Who's this?! Jenny: He looks 90! Old Man - Well! It's great to be back in the ring! After being retired for so many years, I just had to come back and help my boys! Smokey: Obviously he knows the Trooper and Hans. Old Man - I'm Daddy Haggard! And these two men are here to kick some tail in the NAWC! The Trooper is here to protect the law! My Law! And Hans is here to enforce it! We don't take kindly to the Threat thinking they're some sort of big deal around here! We're moving in and you're not going to be able to do anything about it! Begin to Despair cause the Square is here!
The South shall rise again!!!!!! Jenny: We're out of time this week everyone! Don't miss next week's FNF!! It might be just as good as this one! Smokey: Or better! We'll see you there. [The scene fades out with The Green Mountain Trooper, Hans the Icelandic Enforcer, and Daddy Haggard heading for the back. Chris Phear has gotten up and upon seeing the two monsters together, steps out of their way.]
