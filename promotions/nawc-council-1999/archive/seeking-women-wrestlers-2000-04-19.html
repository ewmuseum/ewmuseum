Seeking Women Wrestlers

19 April 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The North American Wrestling Council is in need of female wrestlers. We have a Women's Division that we're trying to bring to the forefront of our federation. Let me tell you about the NAWC.
We have been open nearly six months. There's no chance we'll close down any time soon. We have a roster of around 40 capable handlers. Currently there are only 8 women in the Women's Division.
We have 7 titles including a Women's Heavyweight Title. We hold 2 cards a week. The first, Wednesday Napalm, is a summary show of matches between newcomers and lower level wrestlers. Fight Night Friday is a fully written, play-by-play show hosted by "Smokey" Joe Reynolds and Jenny
Winston. Both of which are faces.
We hold a PPV on the last Sunday of every two months. Due to a brief vacation a short while ago, our March PPV was postponed 'til last Friday.
It is called The March to Mayhem and we are in the midst of it right now. We are an e-mail based fed using a closed flash system (flash packets). Our packets, known as Flash Forward, come out three times a week: Mondays,
Wednesdays, and Fridays. The week before PPVs though, we'll have an extra packet on Saturdays. The average amount of flashes per packet is eight.
This should show you that our handlers give this fed an aweful lot of effort. For more information, I have attached our company FAQ. I encourage you to read through it. Also, please take a look at our website at
http://nawc.cjb.net If you have any comments or questions, please don't hesitate to contact me via e-mail ( [detail withheld] ), ICQ # [detail withheld], or AOL IM screen name [detail withheld]. I hope to hear from you soon.
