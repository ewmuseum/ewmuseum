Roleplay Packet

19 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Some of these may have been sent to me before sending out part 1 of
Napalm. I apologize for that. Tonight we hear from Speedy Irons,
Speedy again, "The Cavalier" Tom Knight, and Garret Goebbels.
Speedy Irons -------------- _ _ _\PEEDY IRON_\ ============== *Speedy Irons sits in his lockerroom. He's in his regular cyan-green outfit.* Speedy: "Well, Trooper, I guess this Speedster was just too fast for you!!!" *Speedy pauses.* Speedy: "So, the NAWC has a new show: NAWC Napalm! And the fastest rising youngster in the world of wrestling today is in it!!! I'm facing
'Mr. Bitter' Jeff Walker. Well, bitterness gets you nowhere, Walker!!!
So, Jeff, on the first Napalm show you're going down! I'm gonna beat all the bitterness out of you! That's a promise, 'cause I'm hot and you are DEFINITELY NOT!!!!! Next there's NAWC's PPV Extravaganza: Nuclear Winter! Quite a night for me;
first I team up with Long S. Tran against Raul Deganeir. Well that's a stupid name, Raul, so kiss your NAWC carreer goodbye! Next I'm teamed with Damien Douglas in a tag-team battle royal for the NAWC tag-straps!
Well I would've preferred Mr. Tran as my partner, but I won't ruin my first chance at NAWC GOLD!!! So, Damien, we're gonna hang in there
'till the end! That's it for now! Walker, prepare to go down!"
*Screen fades to black.* Speedy Irons ------------- *We're still at NAWC Saturday Napalm. Suddenly 'High Speed Brake-Out' by Hibria plays and Speedy Irons comes out again. The fans cheer loudly!* Smokey: "Here's our saviour once again!" Jenny: "Oh yeah! And he does look kinda cute too!" *Smokey gives Jenny a 'sigh!'. The Speedster jumps in the ring and grabs a mic.* Speedy: "Yo, Jackson - Mississippi, what's up? The Speedster is in..." *The fans yell TOOOOOOOWWWWN!!!* Speedy: "That's right! Walker you sure gave me some surprises tonight, but in the end it was the fastest rising youngster in the world of wrestling today!!! Now on to the Green Mountain Trooper. The big scary man of law and order! Well, Mean Green Fighting Machine, I saw you take out big NAWC Superstars tonight and it just made me sick!!!
Then you wanted to fight me again! Trooper, when will you understand that you'll NEVER..."
*Fans yell 'EEEEEEEEEEVER!' Speedy Irons laughs.* Speedy: "... Thanks, guys! ... Beat the Speedster!!! Trooper, the days of the slow MASTODONTS are over! These days it's all about HIGH SPEED
IMPACT!!! Trooper, I did what nobody else did: I laid you out TWICE!!!
Now, on to The Educator! Seems he had to say some stuff 'bout me! Sloan, you proclaim I was a nobody in the WWA and I'll be a nobody in the NAWC! Okay, let's see... WWA: numerous victories and four losses!
NAWC: 2 wins - 0 losses! Now, dinosaur, these aren't the stats of a nobody! Especially if you look at what I did to Trooper; he laid out five big NAWC Stars, but he couldn't take out this 'nobody'! Instead I laid him out!!! Let this be a lesson to anyone in the back! Don't mess with the fastest rising youngster in the world of wrestling today,
'cause I'll get you! That's a promise, 'cause I'm hot and you are
DEFINITELY NOT!!!!!" *'High Speed Brake-Out' by Hibria plays again and the Speedster walks back to the backstage area, slapping hands with fans on his way out.*
"The Cavalier" Tom Knight
--------------------------- (Sharon Roberts is standing backstage underneath the stands.) Sharon: Well, folks, it's just about that time. The Cavalier will be making his bid to become the first NAWC champion to successfully defend his belt. But he's going to be up against the biggest man in the NAWC,
Killer Buddha. Buddha lost his IC belt last week to Chris Phear in that wild six-man match last week on Fight Night Friday, and he's hungry to get another taste of
gold. But if my opinion counts, I've got to go with my man, Tom Knight to win tonight. (Guinevere's voice is heard as she approaches.) Guin: Excuse me? Did you refer to the Cavalier as 'your man?' Sharon: And if I did? Guin: I think it's time to set the record straight with you. I am
Guinevere, Tom's manager, girlfriend, best friend, and partner. You are a reporter who is sticking her nose where it doesn't belong a little too often. I suggest you start playing the field a little before I have you barred from interviewing Tom anymore.
Sharon: You can't threaten me, Guinevere. I'm not only a reporter, I'm the NAWC's best reporter. If Tom wants good air time, he's got to come to me. If you don't like it, leave.
Guin: If I leave, I take the World Champion with me. I wonder how Mr. Palmer would feel about that? All I have to do is say that we're leaving because of you, and that it's either we leave, or you do. How secure are you in your job?
Sharon: Look, honey, if you can't hang on to a man, that's your own problem. If he's not interested in me, then why doesn't he say so?
(Guinevere glares at Sharon, but before she can reply, Tom Knight walks up, wearing the World Title around his waist.)
Cav: Guinevere, we've got to get ready. Killer Buddha's waiting. Guin: Certainly, Tom. Sharon: Don't you have anything to say, champ? Cav: Briefly, but yes. Killer Buddha, you speak of dreams come true. I have partly achieved my dream in becoming the World Champion, and I know the road is a long and difficult one. But there is more to my dream than simply winning the World Title; I intend to be the first man in the
NAWC to successfully defend that World Title, and that means I will defeat you
tonight. You claim you do not fear Excalibur, nor the Sword in the
Stone. It takes a brave man to say those words, particularly when that man has already fallen to the power of Excalibur once before. You are certainly the most worthy challenge I've faced in the NAWC, but you are not invincible, and I will prove that when I stand over your fallen body tonight.
You claim that violence is the only answer? Look closely at me,
Killer Buddha. Violence is a part of my life. Not the mindless chaos that so many bring to the ring, where 'violence' is merely another word for 'random viciousness.' The violence in me is controlled, focused to the point of a sword, and that sword is what has brought me this far in my wrestling career. You will bear witness tonight to the effectiveness of that violence, as you take the inexorable journey to defeat at the hands of
the true World Champion of the NAWC. Your size matters not. Your strength matters not. Your karma matters not. It will not be enough to dethrone me as the champion. I fought two matches to win this belt last week, against two former champions. You will fare no better than they did.
But there is one point on which you are correct. This will indeed be a battle to remember. The NAWC will never forget this meeting, for it will present a turning point in the history of this federation that will stand forever. Tonight, for the first time, a man will walk into the arena as the World Champion and leave the arena with the belt still in his possession. A significant moment, and you will be a part of it. Winning a title is not enough to be named a true champion. Tonight I will take the final step to becoming the NAWC World
Champion in truth, when I lay you low and destroy the dream you hold so dear. And when you find yourself brought to earth by the power of
Excalibur once again, and you hear the referee cry out his triumphant cadence, one-two-three, you will know that the dream of Camelot is not dead, and that a true champion reigns in the NAWC. And you will join the ranks of those who have once again SEEN THE LIGHT!
(Cavalier and Guinevere walk off toward the ring entrance.) Sharon: That was brief? Good thing he didn't have a lot to say. Well, it's time for the men to step up to the plate and for the boys to slink back to the locker room. I know where my money is tonight. Back to you, Jenny.
Garret Goebbels ----------------- (camera fades in on Garret Goebbels in a gym doing push ups) (he then notices the camera and stops working out for the moment) Garret: Hello, well it was great to know that you all cared out there. It seems I have a shot at a title right off the bat at Nuclear Winter. Of course that depends on me winning my match against Perry Harris. I must admit that I know nothing about Mr. Harris yet but I will. When i do I should warn you Mr. Harris that I will win.
(Garret then gets up and starts to walk away from the area where he was doing push ups and towards one of those electronic rowing machines)
Garret: Which brings me to my next opponent if I win one Derek "Down under" Davies. I do remember you Davies as you were coming in about the time I left. I would just like you to remember who headlined the first ever NAWC card. That would be me when I fought Rick Stevens. Just remember Derek as well as you Mr. Harris that I will do any thing within the rules as well as my ability to become victorious in our battles.
Just remember that wrestling was once described by the great Billy
Robinson as physical chess and I have both the physical skill as well as the mental acuity to win these physical chess matches. Do either of you have what it takes?
(Garret then turns around to use the rowing machine as the camera fades out) Good work to all of you. I will have Part 2 of Napalm out as soon as possible. [name withheld] http://nawc.cjb.net
