At ringside, Lady Divine grabed a chair from under Brent Jacobson and held it up to Goebbels who went to take it but had it snatched away by Donovan
Moore. Moore opened the chair and took a seat. Brent came over to him and asked to have it back. Moore apologized and let Brent take it back. Back in the ring, Garret Goebbels was squaring off with Simon Watts. Goebbels whipped him off the ropes and then ducked his head as Watts came off.
"Slick" Simon tried for a leap frog over Goebbels but was caught and Goebbels nailed a spinebuster. He held it for a two count before Truckstop broke the pin. Rick Stevens hit the ring and we had a full out war.
Stevens and Truckstop exchanged shots while Garret worked over Simon's arm. Goebbels actually locked on the Minnesota Armbreaker but Truckstop Jones broke it with a big boot to the face. The Titan came down to ringside with a bat that was dripping in some kind of liquid. Jenny speculated that it could be karosine. He pulled out a lighter and set the bat aflame. He then hit the ring and nailed Rick Stevens in the stomach with the burning bat.
Doc Draven didn't see it as he was observing Garret Goebbels pound on "Slick" Simon Watts. The Titan then exited the ring and left for the back. Thane Sloan and Speedy Irons came out of the crowd and began throttling a downed Stevens with chokes and stiff punches. They pulled him out and
Speedy landed a boot to the burned skin. Thane placed a chair on the concrete as Speedy executed his Iron Dropper with Stevens head hitting the chair. They then slid him back in the ring. Doc Draven eventually cleared the ring of both Garret Goebbels and Simon Watts. Truckstop Jones pummeled
Stevens with shots to the ribs. He then tagged in Watts. Truckstop Jones put Stevens up in a powerbomb as Watts caught Goebbels with an elbow. He then jumped to the top turnbuckle and as Jones hit the powerbomb, he jumped off for the Playa Hata. Damage Inc. got the win.
Winners: Damage Inc.
