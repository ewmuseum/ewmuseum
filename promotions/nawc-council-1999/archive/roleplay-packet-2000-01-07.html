NAWC (1999) — Card Archive

Roleplay Packet

7 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
This morning we hear from, Rio D. Giniro, Donovan Moore, Killer Buddha,
Kasady, and Max "The Beast" Cayn.
Rio D. Giniro -------------- (Open inside of the office of the NAWC esteemed president Mr. Al Capulco. Al is seated in his leather chair behind his desk. Rio and Rick are both seated in leather chairs in front of the desk. Lady Divine is stretched out on the leather sofa. Al has the NAWC World Title on a stand on his desk.)
Al: I thought I would take a few moments to address a few of you who have been shooting off at the mouth... Seeing as though I am the Pres here it is my job to keep all you yahoos in line.... So lets get started shall we???
(Al looks over a sheet of paper he has lying upon his desk.) Al: First off there is our current NAWC InterContinental Champion Killer
Budha.. Listen up little man. If you are going on these little tangents to try and get some attention or better yet get a World Title shot. GIVE
IT UP.... There is no way on this great planet we call earth. That some half witted filth spewing no talent wish he was but never will be...
(Takes a breath)... Is ever going to get his hands on MY precious World
Title. So you can just get that thought out of your head. No matter how many clever little nicknames you come up with... (Smiles)... Which I am certain takes up most of your time thinking up... You know what would be an even greater challenge for you Budha??? Giving an interview that has some credibility and not just pointless ramblings of garbage nonsense and third grade name calling... Get through that and then maybe we will talk...
(Once agin looks over his sheet he holds in front of him.) Al: Aaaaah yes.. Next we go to you Derek Davies... What is this ramble about some hell in the cell title for title wrong turn at albequerque match???? Do you even listen to yourself when you talk??? What nonsense. You have less of a claim to MY Title than that Budha character... In the immortal words of the great Bon Jovi... You are living on a prayer... How can you even think about a future match with the great World Champion Rio. When you arent even going to make it through Rick Stevens this Fri....
(Looks over the sheet one last time then crumples it up and tosses it out.) Al: Finally to you Thane Sloan.... Geez boy. Could you cry anymore?? And what are you talking about that I have what is rightfully yours??? I have been telling everyone from our first day in this fed that the World
Title belongs to us. That it was just a matter of time before it was in our rightful hands... And look... (Points to the belt)... I was right..
Here it is.. Just like I said it would be.... As for your idol threat. Let me just say this... (Sticks out his arm and begins shaking his hand)... Oooooooooo I am so scared... Do you realize just who it is you are talking to punk???? I am the NAWC President.. I manage the NAWC
World Champion and greatest athlete this sport will ever lay its lazy eyes upon... Not to mention the backing of the current reigning NAWC TV
Champion... So your threats mean nothing to me little man... So wy dont ou grab yourself a glass of warm milk.. Tke a nice long nap.. And think before you talk mmmkay....
(Al stands from his desk. Rick, Rio and Divine then stand. Al removes the NAWC World Title from the stand on his desk. He drapes it over his shoulder.)
Al: Now that I have gotten that out of the way it is time for me to do somehting more interesting with my time....
(Al heads for the door followed by Rio Rick and Divine. They exit the office and the camera fades on the closing door.)
Donovan Moore ----------------- (It's a sunny day in Tallahassee, Florida. A few clouds dot the sky and there's a slight breeze. The camera pans down a street full of medium-sized homes, probably full of families spending time together.
It stops on a white, two-story house with dark green trim. The lawn is green and neatly trimmed. The front room curtains are open and reveal a sparsely furnished living room. A sheet covers the couch and the table is bare. Inside, Donovan Moore is on the kitchen phone sitting next to the table. He reaches in the refrigerator and pulls out a Diet Coke.
He's wearing Nike shorts and a white shirt that says "Florida State
Football." Denise walks in and reaches into the cupboard to pull out a bottle of something. She has her hair in a ponytail and red lipstick on; red workout shorts and a gray t-shirt comprise her outfit. She looks over at Donovan who is tapping a pencil against his can of coke in a rhythm.)
Denise: Are you calling the hospital again? Donovan Moore: Yeah, they put me on hold again. He was supposed to be released today. Denise: Are you sure you want to make a name for yourself this way? (Moore snaps his head up and looks straight at her. His hazel eyes feel ice cold.) DM: This isn't about making a name for myself, this about doing what I set out to do and that's clean up this business. I'm not going to sit back and watch one of my fellow competitors get nearly crippled by two behemoths that have no business near the ring in the first place. If he got hurt in the match by another wrestler, I wouldn't have a problem with it, but I'm taking a stand now. No more of this cheating, it stops here and now and it starts with Brimstone.
(Denise looks uncomfortable as she stares down at the table. She looks up at Donovan who again is focused on the phone.)
Denise: I just don't want you to get hurt. You have such a future ahead of you. (Moore signals for her to stop as he listens intensely on the phone.) DM: Yes, I'd like to know if Vampire de Muerte has been released yet? (There's a pause.) He has? That's good to hear. Thank you very much. (Moore hangs up the phone and takes a sip of the Diet Coke.) DM: Looks like Vampire is going to be ok, but I don't think we will be seeing him for quite sometime though.
Denise: I didn't expect him to recover right away. By the way, have you heard from the NAWC front office?
DM: I got a message the other day saying that I'll face Brimstone this
Friday in San Antonio. That's just fine with me. These NAWC guys aren't very personable, but they sure know how to handle these things.
They've given me what I want and that's Brimstone. Denise: Just be careful and have a plan. (She leans over and kisses him on the cheek and leaves the room. Moore sits alone and looks out the window to the backyard.)
DM: "Have a plan," she says. Everyone has a plan, but the great ones are able to adjust that plan. I came into the NAWC and I said I wouldn't stand for the cheating and senseless violence that has been going. What happens in my first match, cheating and senseless violence? I'm adjusting my plan to focus on you Brimstone. You obviously have some kind of grudge against Vampire and decided that the
Lightweight Title match was the place to take it out. Whatever happened to settling your problems face-to-face, are you that much of a coward?
You are 275 pounds and you have to attack from behind? Explain that to me. How fitting that the same ring in San Antonio will be the place where you have to answer for your injustice. If I wasn't involved in the most grueling match of my career last Friday, I would have showed you right then and there, what I think of your actions. But it's better that I get deal with you one-on-one when I'm fresh, focused and ready to teach you a lesson.
(Moore stands up and leans on the windowsill. He shades his eyes from the glare of the setting sun.)
DM: You've made a pretty bad impression on me Brimstone and those are hard to change. I think behind that rough exterior lies nothing more than a scared child. The best I could compare you to is a schoolyard bully. I'm sure everyone has encountered a bully or two. You go out to the playground and everyone gets fearful of the biggest kid as he robs another of some simple milk money. It will continue until someone has enough courage to stand up, then the kids get together and give the bully a bit of his own medicine until he runs home crying to his mother. You can think of me as the kid with the courage to stand up.
Since no one else in the NAWC seems to gives a damn one way or the other, I'll make it my business. If I don't, no one will and the circle of violence will continue. Consider me the square that won't fit in that circle.
(He turns and looks straight at the camera.) DM: Don't forget Brimstone, that I'm not out to make the NAWC, I'm out to make it better. (Moore walks away and the camera stays on the window overlooking the backyard as it slowly fades out.)
Killer Buddha -------------- INTERIOR - ALAMODOME, SAN ANTONIO, TEXAS - B4 FIGHT NIGHT FRIDAY The scene opens in an arena filled with enthusiastic NAWC fans. Springsteen's "Adam Raised A Cain" rocks out of the arena's PA system. The fans are on their feet, waving signs and playing up to the cameras. Some of the fans are holding signs that say "The Killer Elite Rules!" and "Instant Karma 4-Ever!". Killer Buddha steps through the entrance way, clutching " Little Buddha" in his left hand. Buddha is wearing his mirrored shades and stills sports his black tail and goatee. He's wearing black pants and shiny black cowboy style boots. The word "Karma"
is embroidered in lavender letters on the back of Buddha's pants. Buddha's oversized T-Shirt has the phrase "Vow Of Violence" emblazoned on the front. Killer Buddha is also wearing black gloves and black armpads.
Buddha begins to make his entrance and walks towards the ring. The
Videotron is displaying images of Killer Buddha clashing with other NAWC
Superstars; The lights go out and the laser show begins. Lavender, yellow, and neon blue lights surround Killer Buddha as he continues his entrance. The fans greet Kiler Buddha with cheers, chants, and excited ravings. Buddha talks to a few of the fans at ringside and enters the ring. Once in the squared circle, Buddha calls for a microphone, and begins to speak as the capacity crowd quiets down.
KILLER BUDDHA: Fight Friday Night is almost here. Killer Buddha is set to meet the NAWC's slimiest ball of rat goop, Max "The Yeast Infection"
Cayne. Now Cayne, you're nothing but an inbred paleolithic piece of
Dinosaur Dung. You are a coward and smell like Sugar, Honey, Iced, Tea, if you know what I mean. Killer Buddha is ready to enlighten you with
Instant Karma, and I hope your girlfriends "Diarrhea" Davies, Ex-lax
McKay, and "Bend Me Over" Reeves join the party. I'll be glad to beat the Hell out of them too.
(Killer Buddha pauses for a beat.) KILLER BUDDHA: Max Cayne, you are a foul smelling, Grizzly Bear molesting, pile of Dog snot. The best part of you ran down yor old man's leg. You're so ugly the last time I saw anything like you, I flushed it down the toilet. Cayne, Killer Buddha is on the Buddha Path, and you are going to be Bashed and Smashed tonight, I guarantee it.
(Killer Buddha studies the fan-filled arena.) KILLER BUDDHA: As for your putrid little pals. They're are bunch of no-talent pieces of trash. Cayne, you and those walking turds you hang out with are Losers. Davies is a beer soaked worm, and even Lady Divine is twice the man he is. Hey Cayne, I suggest you and those punks pull a no-show tonight. Killer Buddha is really psyched about bashing your ugly faces in. I'm hoping the NAWC Bosses decide to put the Intercontinental belt on the line. I want you to see the prize that you will never have.
(Killer Buddha takes off his sunglasses.) KILLER BUDDHA: Max Cayne, Derek Davies, and all you other flea bitten slimebags. Killer Buddha is the NAWC's Intercontinental Champion. My holding this title was written in the stars.
(The fans start to raise the roof.) KILLER BUDDHA: I am Killer Buddha! (Killer Buddha puts hid shades back on.) KILLER BUDDHA: Instant Karma is Coming To Get You! (Killer Buddha raises "Little Buddha" over his head.) KILLER BUDDHA: Violence Is The Only Answer!!! Killer Buddha tosses the microphone to a ring attendant, waves to the crowd, and walks out of the ring.
(Fade To Black.) Kasady -------- [The scene opens up to a darkened photo set...] [On the set, stand two large flashbulb lights, turned off reluctantly, and in the middle of the set is a camera on a stand. Also on the set, are two chairs, one empty, the other sitting a man in jeans, and a tee shirt, his black hair covering most of his face, and a shiny object over his shoulder....the backdrop, colored black, doesn't help the lighting factor..]
[As the scene zooms in on the man, the shiny object is seen to be a title belt, and is in fact the NAWC Lightweight Championship title....it's the one and only, dark, quiet and yet mysterious,
KASADY...] [The newly crowned champ begins to speak, as he slumps more into the chair...] KASADY: There really isn't much to be said right now....Friday Night
Fight is nothing but a travesty to my career....hell....I'm fighting some guy named CLAW....CLAW for god sakes. Jesus...a NAWC champion, royalty for god sakes shouldn't be treated this way. The fans and marks of NAWC shouldn't be treated like this....I've got the ability to put on matches, beyond the marks of man have seen, but I can't put these things on when I fight some low life, wannabe, that goes by the name of
CLAW.... [He pauses....sarcasticly sighing....sniffles, and begins again...] KASADY: To tell you the truth, this damn title means -BLEEP- to me.....it's doing nothing but straining my shoulder. Bottom line is this....CLAW....meat Kasady. That's right, your going into the ring with a professional now, no more independant road trips for your sorry ass.
How you got this oppurtunity, blows my mind, but I mine as well show your the ropes...meet the flame on Friday, cause the darkness is coming to get you...I'm out....
[Kasady gets up and leaves the set....scene cuts to dark.] Max "The Beast" Cayn ----------------------- [The camera rolls and we're in San Antonio, Texas. Just outside The
AlamoDome. We are looking at the arena from outside the loading bay. A semi with the NAWC logo in blue lettering drives towards us before slowing to a halt. It backs up and manoevers around us, backing to the loading bay doors. It is late morning as the sun is barely peaking over the many skyscrapers of the city. The semi's engines are shut off then its doors swing open. A group of workers climb out and go to work immediately. They begin opening the trailor of the truck and removing its contents with care. We watch as they hastily remove the NAWC ring from its resting place in the truck.]
BEEP!!!!!! BEEP!!!!!!! [We turn our attention as the camera swings around to find a red Dodge
Ram pulling into a spot near the semi. Its engine stops and the driver's door swings open. We hurry to the side of the truck to catch a glimpse of its driver. The seven foot giant known as Max Cayn stands before us. He is wearing dirty blue jeans, a brown leather jacket that is left open revealing a black sweatshirt beneath, and brown work boots. A Chicago Bears baseball cap rests atop his head and dark blue sunglasses cover his eyes. The workers, who have returned from inside the arena, notice Max's cap and start talking aloud about the Bears terrible losing streak.]
Worker 1: Hey Max! How'd the Bears do last Sunday against Tampa Bay?! [The workers shout over at Max as he locks up his truck. Max turns toward them.] Max: They lost! Worker 1: Hahahaha!!! You know, they always lose!! [The group laughs some more as they carry more equipment from the semi to the rear of the arena. Max smiles at the camera. He reaches into the back of his Ram and pulls up a black duffle bag which he slings over his left shoulder. He then turns and heads into the building. The scene fades out.]
[The scene returns and we're watching workers assemble the NAWC ring in the center of the AlamoDome. Ocassionally they glance over at us and begin to laugh amongst themselves. We pan around the arena to find that the seats are all empty. Except for one. Max Cayn sits in the front row of the floor seats at ringside. His legs are crossed. He still wears the same clothes we saw him in earlier. He pulls a pack of cigarettes and his jacket pocket. He removes a cigarette from its pack, replaces the pack in his pocket, sticks the cigarette in his mouth, and lights it. He then puts the lighter away and leans back in his chair, puffing at the cigarette.]
Max: Well, Derek and the rest are coming in to San Antonio later tonight. They went back to Austrailia for a bit to spend a little time with their families. They're supposed to get here around three this afternoon. In the meantime, I'm here on my own. But what does that matter? Palmer has me scheduled in a non title match against Killer
Buddha later tonight. You all saw me destroy Killer Buddha last week when I Cayn Bombed him down to the floor. But I, like the rest of the world, realized that son of a b**ch has a hard head. But you can count on it that I won't forget that Buddha. If there's one thing the Marines taught me, it's that you scout out your enemy. You learn his strengths, and his weaknesses.
[Max takes a drag from his cigarette before continuing.] Max: It appears as though one of Killer Buddha's strengths is that he has a hard head. Well Buddha. After I'm through with you tonight.
That hard head of yours won't be so damn hard! You may have gotten up from the Cayn Bomb last time. But tonight, after I've pounded the living Hell out of you ... and I've hit the Cayn Bomb one more time, your head won't be as hard as you may like to believe. Then when the referee counts the one two three, you'll thank your lucky stars that you didn't lose that belt you undeservedly latched onto. Derek should be the rightful IC champ. Only the hottest star coming out of Austrailia would hold that title with pride. You Mr. Killer Buddha, see it as merely an extra accessory.
[Max finishes his cigarette then flicks the butt to the ground. A large security guard, after seeing this, approaches Max.]
Security guard: Excuse me Mr. Cayn. But we don't allow smoking here in the Dome. You'll have to smoke outside.
Max: I just finished. And no I'm not going outside. It's too damn hot out there man! Security guard: I'm sorry sir. But those are our policies. Max: Wait a minute. Aren't you the guy that told those security people last Friday to keep me away from the ring during Derek Davies match with
Killer Buddha?!
Security guard: Yes sir. That was me. But, could you please take your cigarettes outside?
Max: Sure. [Max stands up from his seat. He grabs the duffle bag sitting on the seat next to him and steps out into the aisleway. As he begins to walk to the back, he stops and turns back to the security guard.]
Max: You know what? You caused my friend to lose his title match. Security guard: Sorry sir. But I was only following orders. Max: Orders from who? Security guard: From Mr. Schilling sir. Max: Well I've got a message for ... "Mr. Schilling." [Max drives a big knee into the gut of the security guard. He then drops his duffle bag and hits his running Kamikaze bomb, which he calls the Cayn Bomb, driving the security guard down into the floor.]
Max: You tell Schilling that he better watch his back!! [A group of security guards surround Max. He tries to fight out of them but their numbers are too great. They take him outside.]
Another security guard: This is why we don't like wrestlers in the arena too long before the show.
[The security guards leave Max and slam the door behind them. Max takes another cigarette from his pocket, along with the lighter. He lights the cigarette.]
Max: Schilling will pay for aiding Killer Buddha in that win. How could he have aided him you ask? By keeping me away from the ring where I can help out. Buddha!! You're gonna pay too!! Mark my words!!
[Max walks back to his truck, opens the door, and climbs in. We get watch him through the passenger side window. While taking a drag at his cigarette, Max fumbles with the radio controls. He flips through the stations. Each time grunting at the country or hip hop music that is playing. Finally he turns off the radio. He finishes smoking the cigarette and flicks it out the window.]
Max: Christ. I need to quit smoking. Killer Buddha! That match tonight WILL be for the Intercontinental title!! How you ask?! Because
I'm gonna beat the living Hell out of Schilling until he grants me that!! That's how!
[Max turns his attention to the radio again. Changing it to the AM waveband. He impatiently switches between the stations before turning it off again.]
Max: God I hate Texas. I swear it! They have terrible tastes in music! I mean, what's wrong with some good old rock music?! Ah to Hell with it! These Texans would never listen to anything worthwhile! But let's not stray from the subject at hand. And that is my match with
Killer Buddha. And since I've already discussed that with you all, let's move on to something of more importance. Alex has a match against
Chris Phear and The Franchise. Franchise is Buddha's buddy. So
Franchise, I'm personally watching for you tonight! Don't cross my path! Cause I'll make DAMN SURE, that you don't have a chance in Hell of winning that match!! As for you Chris Phear! You crossed the Down
Under Dogz once before! You won't cross them again! Because I'm making it my personal mission, of putting the ... "Phear" ... into you.
[Max puts his hands on the steering wheel and leans back slightly.] Max: Then, later in the show, Derek has a big TV title match against
Rick Stevens. Now Stevens and I haven't crossed paths yet. So Stevens, don't try anything funny. Because if you do, I'll be on you like flies on a pile of shit!! If you catch my meaning.
[Max puts the key in and starts up the truck.] Max: I'm going for lunch. After that, it's on to the airport where I pick up Derek, Alex, and Ben.
[Max pulls away from us and backs out of the loading area. We watch as his truck drives out of view. We then cut to back in the arena where the workers have completed setting up the ring. The scene fades.]
