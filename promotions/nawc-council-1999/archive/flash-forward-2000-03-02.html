Flash Forward

2 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yet another good packet tonight. Good work. Tonight we hear from Killer
Buddha, Long S. Tran, Speedy Irons, Rio D. Giniro, Richard "Madman" Miller,
The Green Mountain Trooper, and Claw Richens.
Killer Buddha ------------------- INTERIOR - KILLER BUDDHA'S HOME - SUNDAY NIGHT Killer Buddha and Jay Bennetto are sitting on the sofa in Buddha's living room. Killer Buddha has just tucked Little Hope into bed. Jay is chewing on the end of an unlit cigar. Jay takes the cigar out of his mouth.
JAY BENNETTO: Palmer and I had a long talk about Roma, and Palmer's not gonna take any crap from that lying piece of trash. Adrian hired a new security team to help keep the peace. He's promised not to sell you out to
Roma's rackets.
(Jay pauses for a moment.) JAY BENNETTO: The only thing I'm kinda' worried about is that Roma mentioned that Ugly Al and the Threat have joined his team. We might have some major problems to deal with here.
KILLER BUDDHA: Mr. Palmer is going to do his best to protect the NAWC. I have no doubt about that. But protecting Killer Buddha and his family is going to be my problem. The Franchise is missing in action which means The
Killer Elite is history. At Nuclear Winter The Threat helped Chris Phear keep me away from my Intercontionental title. I have a feeling that Roma had a hand in both of those events. That Maggot Roma, wants to destroy me, he wants to see me crippled. I have two choices; I'm either supposed to join his stable and be crippled in a bout, or I can stand alone and be seriously bashed by Murder Incorporated.
(Buddha takes a beat.) KILLER BUDDHA: Roma will hurt the NAWC because he knows I love this promotion. Roma wants to take anything I care about away from me. He'll use
Murder Inc., The Threat, and any enemies I have to ruin my life. He'll hurt my family, my friends, and even all the fellow wrestlers I respect in order to get my attention. Killer Buddha is going to have to crush Roma before he can gain any more momentum.
(Jay Bennetto stands up and begins to pace nervously.) JAY BENNETTO: "K", We gotta ask for help. Maybe some of the other wrestlers could help us put a bruisin' on Roma and his little pantywaists.
KILLER BUDDHA: Killer Buddha has to admit that I do need some help. Murder
Inc. by themselves is a force to be reckoned with, but when you add The
Threat and twenty security thugs into the mix, Killer Buddha is facing a stacked deck. On top of all this Roma crap, I also have a match against that primadonna Jason " The Walking Band-aid" Blake on Friday Night.
(Killer Buddha pauses for a moment.) KILLER BUDDHA: Blake is a spoiled, whining, no-talent punk. He's standing tall these days with a few impressive wins, but Killer Buddha is going to beat the crap out of him on Friday night. "Cry Baby" Blake had the nerve to accuse Tom Knight of being a traitor. I couldn't believe it - This little slime ball had the stones to imply that The Cavalier is running with the
Threat. Now Tom Knight and Killer Buddha have bashed heads, but I admire and have respect for the Cavalier. I know he woulld never betray the fans or the
NAWC. Now as for this little worm Jason "The Bleeder" Blake, I have a bad feeling about his loyalties. I think he's the one running with those Capulco
Slimeballs. It won't matter though, because on Friday night, Jason "The Cry
Baby" Blake will meet the Power and Glory of Killer Buddha. I'm going to stomp, bash, smash, pulverize, and genuinely assault that snot sucking punk and I WILL WIN the NAWC Television title.
(Jay Bennetto sits down again.) JAY BENNETTO: That's what I want to hear "K". I want to be sure that you're going to be O.K.
KILLER BUDDHA: Uncle Jay don't worry. Killer Buddha is going to take care of the Threat, of Murder Inc. and that incestuous little mutant Roma. On Fiday
Night, I'll deliver the Nirvana Smash and then enlighten that punk Blake with Instant Karma. Things are going to be fine Uncle Jay. Don't you worry.
(Fade To Black.) Long S. Tran ------------------ The arena is empty. Yet, the roar of thousands upon thousands of fans thunders in his temples. A wide shot of the interior of Key Arena is shown. The ring has not yet been set up, and a lone figure is seen sitting by himself in the never-ending rows of empty seats.
Silence fills the arena. The camera slowly pans closer to the lone figure. It is NAWC youngster Long S. Tran. He is wearing jeans and a black T-shirt. His long hair is covered by a beanie. He stares into the space where the
NAWC ring will be on Friday. Almost as if there was a match going on. Not a word is said, nor a muscle is moved as he stares blankly, almost as if he is hypnotized by the space where he will step into the ring with respected rival Claw Richens.
To a lot of wrestlers, this Friday is just another night. Some wrestlers will win, some will lose. But they will all go home and live to fight another week. But not for Tran.
The camera slowly pans from Tran's glassy stare to the center of the arena where the ring will soon be setup. From the deafening silence, the imaginary loud roar of the audience is once again heard through Long S. Tran's ears.
Tran slowly stands up and takes his time to walk down to the front row seats. He reaches what will be the security rail and leans against it, still staring in silence at where the ring will be set up.
A flashback hits him. Almost a year ago, in "that other fed" that a young 18 year old rookie made his debut in this very arena. A smile seems to cross
Tran's mouth as he remembers what it felt like to step into the ring and actually CARE about the fans, and about giving a good show. During this silence, Tran's voice can be heard via voiceover...
Tran:- Almost a year ago, I stepped into the squared circle for the first time. It was in this arena where I first heard the thunderous roar of a live audience screaming my name. Back then, I cared about what the fans thought of me. All I wanted to do was put on a good show.
(Pause. Tran hangs his head down in shame.) Tran:- (V/O) But a certain teenager who had it all developed an ego, and that changed everything.
(Another pause. Tran looks up again at the empty space where he and Claw will step into the ring)
Tran:- (V/O) Is Claw right? I brought Claw into this fed because I knew he could handle it. But could I? I am 19 years old, and already I am seen as a
has-been. (Tran is still mesmerized at the empty space. His fists look to be clenching together. He then turns to the camera)
Tran:- Claw... We've been in this fed for a few months now, and I gotta honestly say that I respect you. We've had quite a history together. We've fought each other, and we've fought side by side. But since coming here, we've drifted. You seemed to have lost what respect you had for me, and I don't blame you one bit. I can't remember the last time I stepped into the ring and actually gave an effort. Have I lost it? Maybe I have... But I am still young, and it's not too late to get it back.
(Pause. Tran once again focuses his attention on the empty space where the ring will be set up)
Tran:- I made my debut in this arena. It was the fans here at Seattle that first saw me wrestle. Stepping into this arena has brought back something I thought I lost forever... My will to entertain and put on the best match I can.
(Pause. Tran slowly turns to the camera. His face is emotionless as he removes his beanie to let his long hair drop over his shoulders)
Tran:- And now that I thought about it, we never DID find out who would be the first to pin the other's shoulder on the mat for three simple seconds.
Who WAS the better man in "that other fed?" We'll never know who WAS the better man, but here and now... We're finally gonna have the opportunity to see who will be the first to be pinned for three seconds.
(Pause) Tran:- Three simple seconds. Count it... One, Two, Three! It's not a long time, and I know that if I make ANY mistake, it can lead to me being on my back for three seconds. But those three seconds will prove once and for all who is the Future of this sport. You say that "you're game" to find out which one of us will walk out with our head up? Well, in my eyes, it doesn't matter who walks out with their arms raised. Win, lose or draw we're BOTH gonna walk out of Key Arena with our heads up. The only difference is one of us will take home the winner paycheck, while the other won't. But we'll live to fight another day, and although this isn't our first encounter, I know it won't be our last either!
(Camera starts to slowly zoom out as Tran continues to talk) Tran:- You and me, one on one. Let's finish what we've been avoiding for the past few months. When we step into this ring and stare into each other's eyes, we'll know the time for talk is over, and it's time to put our words into action. We'll lace up our boots, step into the ring, and as the audience "Makes Noise" we'll finally find out who will be the victor. See you in the ring.
(As the camera continues to zoom out, Tran slowly turns aroud to once again face the empty space where he and Claw will lock up once again. This time, there WILL be a winner...)

None of them however including Rio even notice the camera presence.

He lowers his sun mirror and sits up turning his attention to the camera.)

(Al turns to the camera).. You want someting???

To assure that there will be no distractions. This way we can increase our already outstanding odds for victory

(He places the file back down beside him. The camera pans down noticing the top of a sheet is slightly revealed. It reads "Binding Contract". Suddenly

Al: Dont you worry none about that. You will find out all about it come Fri just like the rest of the sheep in the NAWC...
(Al looks about yet again at the entertainment at hand with a pleasant smile. A burst of feminine giggles can be heard as the camera cuts over to show Rio giving the women a pleasing flex. It then cuts back to Al who lets out a chuckle.)

Rick had some personal businees to attend to and Phear wanted to prepare for his upcoming match and just didnt have the time to join us. So do go getting the wrong idea that something is amis. I know how you people like to make mountains out of molehills. Everything is fine...

She walks off bringing us back to the disapointing view of scrawny Al sipping a Pina' Coloda. He finishes his sip giving out a sigh of refreshment. Fri. Yeah you may be sitting atop the NAWC as of now. Clinging your grubby little paws to a title, that everyone knows all to well should be soaking up rays with us right now. But hey we cant cry over spilled milk. All we can do now is clean up the mess and throw out the trash. Unfortunately that is just going to have to waite... (Smiles with a gleem in his eye)... I guess.
We will however have opportunity to once again get you in the ring. Though it may... (Grins again)... not be for the title. We will be able to show the entire NAWC that you truely can't beat THE MAN.
As for your partner Sean Dubose. He is of little concern to me. I will allow
Stevens to deal with him. Either way he will receive a beating just as bad as yours... Whether it be at the hands of the greatest beside me here. Or

Al: Knight, it is you that we want. It is you we are going to get. No more, no less. We are going to prove to all who view it that you just dont have it in you to stop us from doing whatever it is we want...

Al: To you Adrian. Or should I call you Mr. Palmer? You thought that aking away my title of president was a funny thing. Oh yeah you showed he world that you have the power. That I just cant do whatever I want. That now I am nothing but a mere manager again... (Pause)... Thats ok because I am more than happy to be the greatest living manager of the most overpowering force in the NAWC... (Smirks)... Unfortunately for you Mr. Palmer hehehe. I honestly couldnt care any less.

We have cleared our heads and we are once again completely focused on our rightful place atop the NAWC... No more distractions... EXCUSES... Because we know we can... (Pauses)... We have done it before and we will do it again... View him...

Claw Richens ------------------- *WHOOSH* [The wind blows bitterly as the scene opens in Anchorage, Alaska. The clouds cover the sun and provide a blanket of chill across the land. Claw zips up his NAWC jacket and continues to walk on the concrete path. His shoulder length black hair blows in the wind, and he takes a look around his location.]
Claw Richens : Has it been that long? [The tombstones surround Claw like rats eating away at a fallen loaf of bread. Claw looks to the sky for reassurance, but finds himself confronted with nothing but the grey clouds. Giving a deep sigh, he continues to walk on. After a few minutes, he diverges from the path, and begins to walk on the grass. He continues to walk for a minue or two, and then he comes to a stop.]
Claw Richens : You should be wearing a jacket. [The camera pans down, and we can see the figure of young Vanessa Green, who kneels before a gravestone. She jumps about 3 feet at the sound of Claws voice, but then turns around to kick him in the knee.]
Vanessa Green : Damn it, Claw! Haven't I told you a million times to stop doing that shit?
Claw Richens : [laughing] Sorry! It was just too easy. Vanessa Green : [looking to the ground.] I didn't expect you to be here. Claw Richens : 7 months ago. Damn. It doesn't feel like that long. I can still see her... vibrantly.
Vanessa Green : I know what you mean. What a week that was. Man. God, I miss her Claw! Claw Richens : You're not the only one. [Vanessa approaches Claw and puts her arms around the big man. He responds with a big hug, and she backs off.]

Friday without a win? I'd never forgive myself, Nes.

Claw Richens : Could we, uh, not do this here? It's kind of creeping me out. Vanessa Green : Fine, lets walk then. [The two begin to walk back to the concrete path.] Claw Richens : Okay, this is all I'm saying. Jimmy and I have a rich past, and he even invited me to join the NAWC after he joined. But what I don't get is why I'm being picked to fight him. It's like, a ghost coming back to haunt me.
