Fight Night Friday (10 December)

10 December 1999 (posted in eight parts through 18 December 1999)
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NORTH AMERICAN WRESTLING COUNCIL FIGHT NIGHT FRIDAY LIVE FROM THE BARTON COLOSSEUM IN LITTLE ROCK, ARKANSAS DEC. 10, 1999 As FNF came on the air, we cut straight to the backstage. Brimstone was wandering aimlessly looking for Hellfire. We then returned to ringside where Smokey and Jenny remarked about the situation between Brimstone and Vampiro de Muerte. They then showed clips from Injustice For All and discussed the outcomes of the matches on that card.
CAREER MATCH: Assassin Vs. Blackjack Vs. "The Extremist" Tedd Olczak As the bell rang, all three of these men went at it full force. Both
Olczak and Assassin worked over Blackjack. They nailed a succession of big power moves. The action spilled out to the floor. A piece of guardrail from the walkway came lose. Olczak used it as he nailed The
Extreme Drop on Blackjack. Then, he and Assassin returned to the ring.
Blackjack was out cold on the floor. The action went back and forth. Assassin hit a series of suplexes and then went for the Crucifix. But, as he held "The Extremist" up, Olczak hit his Extreme Drop out of nowhere and got the three count.
Winner: "The Extremist" Tedd Olczak (Assassin and Blackjack are fired.) Cyrus Waterfield Vs. "Mr. Bitter" Jeff Walker These two certainly put on a crowd pleaser. Both men showing their big moves. Cyrus hit a spinebuster. Then a DDT. And then he nailed an incredible running powerbomb to the crowd's delight. Cyrus continued his assault on "Mr. Bitter." Cyrus went for a gut wrench suplex but
Walker blocked it and countered with a knee to the face. Jeff Walker then mounted a comeback. He utilized several effective technical moves to slow the mad Cyrus down. He hit a neckbreaker followed by an abdominal stretch which he had on for quite some time. Eventually, he released the hold and delivered a series of hanging vertical suplexes.
Waterfield fought back with a combination of punches but it didn't slow
Mr. Bitter down. Walker countered with a backbreaker. He then poised himself and waited as Cyrus climbed to his feet. As he stood, Walker connected with his Road to Apathy, a devastating short-arm clothesline.
Next, Walker turned a cobra clutch into a float over suplex, The Bitter
End as he calls it. And yes, it was the Bitter End for Cyrus
Waterfield. Walker got the three count for the pin. Winner: "Mr. Bitter" Jeff Walker We then cut to the parking lot to find Hellfire knocked out in a car. Vampiro de Muerte stands next to the car. He places a brick on the accelerator of the car and jumps out of the way as the car speeds off.
It crashes into a white limo. We then hear Vampiro de Muerte laughing. The camera turns back to where he was standing but he is gone. Yet we still hear the laughing. Brimstone and EMTs rush the scene. They carefully get Hellfire out of the car and hoist her into an ambulance.
The camera cuts back to ringside as the ambulance sped away. Chris Phear Vs. Alex "Alligator" McKay A heated battle quickly ensued between these two. Phear's combination of high risk manoevers combined with McKay's suplexing expertise made for a very even match. Phear surprised McKay on several occasions with a springboard move or a roundhouse kick. But McKay overpowered him with several suplex variations. He nailed a classic dragon suplex followed by a tiger suplex. The momentum went both ways again. Each man giving it their all. Trying to show up their opponent. Eventually, it was
McKay that took the upper hand. He continued to over power his opponent with a snap suplex followed by a DDT. He then signaled to the crowd and positioned Phear on the top turnbuckle. McKay followed him up. McKay hit a German suplex off the top. But, in midair, Chris pulled off the
Phear Factor out of nowhere. Both men hit the mat hard. Referee Doc
Draven started the mandatory ten count. It got to nine before McKay got to his feet. Momentum once again changed hands as Phear took the advantage again. The action spilled to the outside. Phear really took it to him. Eventually jumping over a guardrail and hitting the Phear
Factor in the process. McKay was left hanging over the guardrail while
Phear had inadvertently landed in a pile of chairs. Both men were out.
And were counted out. Winner: Draw via double countout [The scene returns to the arena and the screaming fans.] Smokey: Welcome back everybody. Up next we have a newcomer in Big Wreck facing big British Wonder. Let's go up to Brent.
Brent: The following contest, [Slow romantic music begins to play.] Jenny: What's this? Smokey: Don't know. This isn't on the agenda. Unless Big Wreck or

Divine. If you're a man, then let's sign the contract and get this done.

{Huge Pop!} Rick: Tom, ya want a match? You got it.. Hell ya want a title shot? You got it! But I do promise you one thing, and one thing only.. And that's that I will take you to a new level of pain, a level of pain that up until now didn't exist.

So you want your title shot, you got it, only lets give the fans what they want.. And that's a 60 minute ironman match. No weapons, No tricks.. Just a old fashioned style match.

Big Wreck Vs. British Wonder From the opening bell, British Wonder went all out on Big Wreck. He hit a series of scoopslams followed by a powerslam. Next he went airborne hitting a couple moonsaults and then a cross body block. British Wonder continued to dismantle his opponent. He nailed a thrust kick that took
Big Wreck to the mat hard. But British Wonder was relentless. He pulled him up and hit The Extremist, knocking Big Wreck to the floor.
British Wonder followed him out and landed a couple stiff chops across the chest. British Wonder brought him back into the ring and hit another thrust kick. He then positioned Big Wreck for the Beefeater.
But, right before he hit it, Big Wreck ducked out of the way. Big Wreck rolled British Wonder up for a two count. Both men got to their feet and exchanged blows. Big Wreck took the advantage now. He hit several of his power moves. He hit a vertical suplex and pulled British Wonder for another. Big Wreck continued his attack by hitting a shoulder breaker, a piledriver, and then a DDT. He then hooked his opponent and hit the Sledge Hammer, three powerbombs in succession. Then Big Wreck hit the Wrecking Ball for the upset pin.
Winner: Big Wreck [The scene fades back into the packed Barton Colosseum.] Smokey: Welcome back folks. Up next, Omar "Arachnis" Shakiem will be facing Raul De'Ganeir. But before we go to that, I'm told that Dr. Hate has an interview planned. He's coming out now. So let's go to that.
