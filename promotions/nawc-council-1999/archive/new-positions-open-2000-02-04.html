New Positions Open

4 February 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Well, working on the NAWC as a one man staff is getting too demanding of me. Therefore, I am in search of some staff additions to the fed. Here's what I want and the duties that it would entail.
Card Writers - 3 or 4 wanted A card writer would be required to write about two matches per show. There will also be a standard that I would like card writers to uphold when writing matches. More details of this will be given as soon as I get some applications for this position.
Site Reporter - 1 or 2 wanted A site reporter would summarize the latest goings on in the NAWC, for example, match summaries, plus the latest news circulating the fed. These news stories would be posted on the main page. A title would have to be submitted with each story.
Graphics Designer - 1 I'm not great at making graphics myself. The graphics designer would be in charge of making graphics for the website. These would include, a title graphic, an Fight Night Friday graphic, and graphics for the 6 PPVs the NAWC holds each year. Designers would be acredited for their work of course.
That's it. If you want one or more of these positions, don't hesitate to contact me at this address (
[detail withheld] ) When applying for the positions, you'll need to attach a sample of previous work you've done so I can decide if I'll hire you.
