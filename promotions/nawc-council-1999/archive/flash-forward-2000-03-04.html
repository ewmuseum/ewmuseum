Jason "The Bandit" Blake & "Electric" Amy Sparks ---------------------------------------------------------------------- The scene is set in a small Seattle cafe. Jason Blake and Amy Sparks are sitting at a table across from each other, holding hands. From out of the picture, Gregory Hollinsworth walks up to the table, and Jason motions him to sit down. Greg pulls a mic out of his pocket.)
Greg - Thanks for giving me the time to do this Jason. Jason Blake - The pleasure is all mine. I'm glad you could make it. Amy and I have a few things that I guess we need to get off our chests.
Greg - Good. So how do you feel now that your match with Killer Buddha is only a few days away?
Jason Blake - Well I am quite well prepared for this one. I have taken all the precautionary steps to be sure that Threat isn't a factor in this match.
Greg - And what are these precautionary steps? Jason Blake - I guess you could say I have taken out an insurance policy that will protect me from any interference.
Greg - Are there some stipulations that we don't know about yet? Jason Blake - It could be, but it may not be. I don't want to let my surprise out too soon. I'll just tease them a little bit more. I want to see if they make the fatal mistake of coming down to ringside during my match.
Greg - Does this apply to only Threat? Jason Blake - No, this applies to anyone who wants to spoil my match. I am going to keep this title by pinning Killer Butter in the middle of the ring.
(Jason and Amy begin to chuckle at the joke.) He's going to get Blindsided like he's never experienced.
Greg - What about the comments that Tom Knight and Killer Buddha have made in regards to your involvement with Threat?
Jason Blake - Well first of all, Knight is just trying to cover his tracks and make himself out to be the good guy in this and make me look like the one at fault. And as for Killer Buddha, he is just trying to kiss Knight's ass so he can get a title shot. He doesn't seem to understand that he wouldn't be getting a shot at my title unless I wanted him to. He should be worshipping the ground that I walk on, not biting the hand that feeds him.
I am easily the hottest new commodity in the NAWC, and Buddha isn't going to beat me. He actually thinks that his so called advantage of 217 pounds is going to make a difference. I mean the guy is almost twice my weight, he'll be lucky to get a hand on me inside that ring. But a word of warning, he should concentrate less on kissing Tom Knight's Championship ass, and more on trying to beat me. If he was as good at wrestling as he was at making insults, he'd be unbeatable. Unfortunately tossing insults is all he can do.
Greg - So what about the rumours that you're joining Threat? They seem to have increased since your match with Tom Knight.
Jason Blake - Let me put it this way for you. If I join the Threat, it's because I know that they are worth having me. I won't align myself with a bunch of losers, and honestly, they really haven't proven what they can do to warrant me joining them yet. But I won't rule out that possibility. If
Knight decides to be any more of a whiner, I may enjoy crippling him.
Greg - but I thought you were thinking that he was joining them? Jason Blake - Well I realized that he doesn't have the balls to make a move like that. He is one of those loners who has to be adored by people. He is the type of man who would help old ladies across the street and kiss babies, just so people will like him. He's attention starved and he's got no balls. But he'll get what's coming to him. I've learned that it's always good to keep my options open, and that's just what I'm doing. I just have to wait for my opening.
Greg - Well I don't know about your train of thought, but I feel you may be making a mistake if you seriously consider joining Threat.
Jason Blake - I didn't ask for your opinions, so just carry on with this damn interview. Greg - Sorry. So Amy, you have to face Hellfire on Friday. How are you looking at this debut of yours?
Amy Sparks - Well she is tough, I can say that much, but I am going to do what Jason here did, and prove that I in fact am a top talent, and I will earn my shot at the Women's title.
Greg - What do you think of her taking on Vampire de Mate at March Mayhem? Amy Sparks - I just hope he doesn't cripple her. I've never seen him wrestle, and she does outweigh him, so it could be interesting. But I'd rather focus more on my matches than others. I'm sure she's worried about that match and it'll affect her performance in the ring against me on
Friday. Greg - And what is your take on the whole Blake/Knight situation? Amy Sparks - Well I personally don't want to get involved in it. I think
Threat is just trying to divide and conquer, but Jason doesn't want to believe it. I just hope he makes the right decision, that's all. Tom is a good guy and I don't think he's do that to Jason. But then again I may be wrong. I guess only time will tell. I just hope everything works out for the best.
Greg - How do you think Guinivere is handling this new rivalry? Amy - I'm sure she feels the same way as me. Tom and Jason were the best of friends before, but it seems the gold has gotten to their heads finally.
But I can't read minds so I really don't know exactly how she feels. Greg - Well I'd like to thank both of you for your time, and I wish you both the best of luck on Friday.
Jason Blake - Won't be any luck needed for me. Killer Buddha will see what it's like to get Blindsided!!
(Gregory Hollinsworth gets up from his chair and leaves the cafe. Jason and
Amy go back to holding hands as the screen fades to black.)

Vanessa : Don't you think that Des would want you to win this weekend? Haven't you thought for just minute that maybe you owe this to her! Claw : .... Vanessa : Claw, it's not your fault she killed herself. She only wanted you to be happy. And she only wanted you to be successful.

Tran:- No!!! (He covers his ears) NO!!! THIS ISN'T HAPPENING! He is breathing heavily, and his heartbeat is heard thumping in the still of the night.
The "Loser" chant is still playing in his mind. Tran:- *Sigh!* Just a dream... (Tran slowly removes the blankets to get up when he feels something below the waist. He quickly removes the covers to reveal a damp patch at his crotch.)
Tran:- What the F... Claw:- ...Far out man!! What a LOSER!!!!! Check this out man!!! Tran wet his bed!!!!!!!! (From out of nowhere, laughter is heard filling the room. Various NWWA stars and members of Tran's family and friends all enter the room from seemingly out of nowhere and crowd around him. They all speak at once and over each other...)

SON... Hey, you have to wrestle a bed wetter this Friday... Hahahaha... "
The voices mock him, and the only thing he can do is just sit there and watch as his friends laugh and point at his little "accident."
-=-=-=-=- *AAAAAAAAAAAARGH!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!* Tran quickly sits up in a cold sweat (Yes, it's sweat not urine) once again as his voice echoes through the dark, quiet corridors for the second time.
He quickly removes his covers and quickly runs his hands on the lower part of his bed... Tran:- *PHEW!* Dry. Tran permits himself a smile as he once again falls back into bed. His eyes are bloodshot and tired, yet he cannot sleep. His most important match in the NAWC so far, and he can't sleep.
He lays motionless in the quietness. He is obviously thinking about his match tonight, and whether or not all the training and practice he put into this match will be wasted over a little simple miscalculation.
A few seconds pass, and the fatigue gets the better of him as his eyes slowly close and he drifts off in a deep sleep. The deafening silence is replaced by the calm breathing of a young wrestler.
Then, just like in the past week, the loud roar of an audience is heard through Tran's ears again while he sleeps. He is obviously having another dream about his much anticipated match with Claw. Will his previous dream come true, or will he defeat the odds? Will a stupid mistake cost him the match? He sees the worst in his dreams, and whether or not they come true now lies on his ability to concentrate in the match. Camera slowly zooms out of Tran sleeping in his room as the scene fades to black
