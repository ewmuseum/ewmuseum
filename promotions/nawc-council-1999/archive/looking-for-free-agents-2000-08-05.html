The North American Wrestling Council was first opened in October of 1999, and is back after a brief hiatus. I have just taken over the duties of running the NAWC, and right now we are looking for some new talent to grace our roster. The NAWC is a flash packet Email fed and we currently have one card per week, on Saturdays. We also have a women's division for anyone who wants to handle a female wrestler, or even have their valet compete once in a while. We are seeking Women, Tag Teams, and faces, but all applications will be seriously considered. The website has just been remodelled, with only a few links left to set up. So take a look at
