NAWC (1999) — Card Archive

Roleplay Packet & Quick Announcement

16 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight we hear from "The Cavalier" Tom Knight, Dynamite Derrick, The
Green Mountain Trooper, Killer Buddha, and Thane Sloan.
"The Cavalier" Tom Knight --------------------------- ("Ride of the Valkyries" plays over clips of the Cavalier's matches in the NAWC, up to his match with Thane Sloan at Fight Night Friday. The music fades out as the last clip is shown, the end of the World Title match with Rio D. Giniro.)
Jenny: The Cavalier hits Excalibur!!!! And he makes the cover!!! 1 ......... 2 ............... 3!!!!!!!!!!!! Smokey: TOM KNIGHT'S THE NEW WORLD CHAMP!!!! HE WON THE TITLE AGAINST RIO D. GINIRO!!!! AND WHAT A MATCH IT WAS!!! Brent: Ladies and gentlemen, here is your winner, AND NEEEW NAWC WORLD HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION!!!!!!!! ... "THE CAVALIER" ... TOM KNIGHT!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! (As Cavalier's music plays, he drops to his knees, holding the World
Title high over his head. Guinevere jumps into the ring, clapping her hands in delight. The two embrace, and Tom hands her the belt. As the crowd goes ballistic, he picks her up on his shoulder and salutes the crowd. Guinevere waves to them, holding up the World Title with tears in her eyes. As the
music continues, the two of them walk around the ring, holding the title for all to see and acknowledging the cheers of the fans. Finally Tom puts Guinevere down, and she goes behind him and straps the World Title around his waist. The two of them leave the ring to massive cheers, returning to the back.)
(In the back, Greg Hollinsworth is waiting with a cameraman. He offers a hand to Tom, who shakes it firmly.)
GH: Congratulations, champ! A great match, and a great win. Did you have any doubts you could win the belt tonight?
Cav: No, not for an instant. I felt all along that tonight was my night of destiny. I have achieved the Holy Grail of the NAWC, and I'm feeling euphoric right now!
GH: Two hard-fought matches in one night. You're not the first one to do it, but it must feel good right now.
Cav: It feels wonderful, yes. But with Guinevere at my side, and with the multitude of fans supporting me, there was never any doubt that I would win. Capulco's manipulations failed, as I said they would.
Giniro fell to Excalibur, as I predicted. Even Lady Divine got involved, which I must admit disappoints me. After the match between
Rick Stevens and I last week,
I had hoped that they would leave this match to Rio and myself. But it didn't make a difference, and the gold is mine.
GH: I've noticed that since Injustice for All, the World Title has not been successfully defended; Dr.Hate lost to Thane Sloan, who lost to Rio
D. Giniro, who lost tonight to yourself. And this Saturday you've got your first defense against Killer Buddha. Do you think you can break this 'curse' and leave the arena with the belt?
Cav: Yes. The three men who preceded me as champion were missing some key ingredients to being a true champion. Dr. Hate lacked the true commitment necessary to holding a belt such as this one. Thane Sloan thought far too much of his education, and neglected to take his opponent seriously. Rio D. Giniro relied on outside interference, both during and even long before his match, thanks to the machinations of Al
Capulco.
GH: And yourself? Cav: This is my dream, my quest. I defeated the odds tonight to win this belt, after being denied championship gold twice before by the slimmest of margins. I have the best manager in wrestling standing at my side, I have the support of the fans, and I have the power of
Excalibur to win the day. Killer Buddha is a dangerous opponent, but he has already fallen before me. I have slain the giants, I have vanquished the dragons. This Saturday, I will enter the arena as World
Champion, and I shall leave the same way. The 'curse' of the World
Title ends tonight.
(Sharon Roberts walks up, drawing a stare from Guinevere.) Sharon: Well, congratulations to the new World Champion! I knew you could do it! Cav: Thank you, Miss Roberts. Sharon: Oh, please. After all we've been through? Call me Sharon. Guin: We're in the middle of an interview here, in case you didn't notice. Sharon: Actually, I did. I just dropped by because I thought you'd be interested in hearing what's on tap for Nuclear Winter.
Cav: I know I'll be defending the World Title; have they chosen my opponent yet? Sharon: No, you're not mentioned on the card. But Guinevere is. Guin: What? Cav: What do you mean? Sharon: I mean that the Queen here is fighting in a four-woman battle royal for the Women's Championship.
(Cavalier and Guinevere look at each other in surprise, then turn back to Sharon.) Guin: Who else? Sharon: Lady Divine, of course. Hellfire and Daring Denise are the other two. Guin: I see. Sharon: And the winner of the match gets the Women's Title, as well as a #1 Contendership slot for the TV Title for her man. Guin: Really. And why would Tom need that when he already holds the
World Title? Sharon: He might not be by then, and you're sounding pretty confident already. Guin: I've known for a while that I might be called upon to step into the ring. Tom and I have been training together for three years now.
Sharon: You have? You've never wrestled before, have you? Guin: No, but I've been preparing for it. Lady Divine and I have the same issues to deal with that Rick Stevens and Tom had. So now we'll get the opportunity to clear the air somewhat.
Sharon: How do you feel about this, champ? Cav: I would like to think that she has me to fight the battles for her, but there are some battles that she must fight on her own. I have every faith in Guinevere's abilities, and I will be there at ringside to encourage her.
Sharon: There might be some out-of-ring stuff going on that night; you and Rick Stevens obviously haven't worked everything out yet.
Cav: Perhaps. But right now I have other things on my mind. First,
Guinevere and I are going to celebrate this victory. Then we're going back to the gym tomorrow morning. Killer Buddha won't be sitting around eating dinner with his family all week, and I have to be ready for him.
Sharon: Think you can beat him? Cav: Yes. I've done so before, and I will pin him once again. This title is mine, and will remain around my waist for a long time.
Sharon: Well, good luck to you. Kick him one for me, alright? (She steps forward toward Tom, but Guinevere steps between them, glaring at Sharon. Sharon hesitates, then shrugs and walks off.)
GH: Well, how do you plan to handle a man the size of Killer Buddha? Cav: The same way I wrestle against every other big man I've fought. Most of the opponents I fight are bigger than I, but at the end of the night I'm the one who is still standing. Killer Buddha and I met in my first match here in the NAWC, and he was the first to feel the power of
Excalibur. The higher powers of the NAWC have seen fit to give him a second opportunity to face me, this time with a much higher stake. It will not make any difference to the outcome, however. Killer Buddha, you have tasted the gold of the Intercontinental championship, and I know how you hunger for the belt strapped around my waist. It's a gnawing feeling inside you, almost painful. It's all you dream about at night. I know, for I've been there myself.
But there is a danger in that hunger, for any man who achieves that goal must take care not to relax his guard and forget that hunger.
I will not consider my goal accomplished until I have successfully defended this belt, and this Saturday I shall become the first NAWC
World Champion to retain this belt!
GH: I've got a different topic to ask you about. I was wondering how you felt about the Owen Hart Classic invitational.
Cav: It would be an honor to compete. As the NAWC World Champion, it is my duty to represent this federation in such an important tournament.
If I am asked to participate, I will uphold the honor of the NAWC against any opponent I am presented with.
GH: Well, that should be an exciting event for everyone, and I hope we get to see the Cavalier fighting that night.
Cav: As do I. But first I have to think about defending this title here in the NAWC. GH: Any last comments? Cav: President Capulco, you thought you could make Rio's title defense easier tonight by putting Thane Sloan in my way. You failed. You thought that Rick Stevens and Lady Divine would keep me from winning this belt. You were wrong. The Holy Grail is in my possession, and I will not lose it as quickly as others have. Let the wrestlers of the
NAWC line up to face me, and all will fall! Killer Buddha, you were the first to be struck down by Excalibur. You will be the first to fall before me as a champion, but you will not be the last. I fear no challenge, no obstacle, and no man! I am Guinevere's Champion, and so shall I remain! And when we meet this Saturday, you will feel the fury of a champion, the speed and strength that have brought me to the pinnacle of achievement in wrestling. And when you find yourself crashing to the canvas, felled by the power of Excalibur, and you hear the referee's pronouncement of your doom, one-two-three, you will realize that you are only the first to fail in your quest to dethrone me as World Champion, and you will take your rightful place among those who have SEEN THE LIGHT!!!
(Tom and Guinevere walk off like a royal couple back to the locker room as the camera fades to black.)
Dynamite Derrick ------------------ <NAWC camera start rolling in a hotel room where Dynamite Derrick is preparing to leave for Saturdays show. He's sitting no the edge of his bed.>
Derrick:"Green Mountain trooper? Sounds like some stupid GI Joe rip-off. Then I look at the lineup and I see he has a hard-core match at nuclear winter? Well son, you better hope you make it out of our match alive first. I'm an experienced wrestler. At one point even tagging with my father Rick Watson."
<Derrick then stands up and paces.> Derrick:"Which brings me to my next comment on the "Tag Team Battle
Royal Match." I see that I'm being teamed with a man that I know nothing about by the name of Omar Shakeim? All I know is you watch my back and
I'll watch yours. Like I said I'm a great Tag Team wrestler. Because as we all know "it takes a great singles wrestler, to make a great tag team wrestler." So everyone be prepared and watch your back because I'm coming in with a bang. Starting Saturday night and going on through
Nuclear Winter."
<Derrick then grabs his luggage and leaves.> The Green Mountain Trooper ------------------------------ (The imposing figure of the Green Mountain Trooper is standing in an office. A senatorial badge is hanging on the wall to the left of a large desk. Another imposing man sits behind the desk with a large smile. The Trooper is angrily talking to the man.)
Green Mountain Trooper - Senator, I implore you. The death penalty for jaywalking is the only way to stop that crime! Jaywalking is the number one hazard in Bradford!
Senator - Now, now little brother. I think you're being too harsh on them. GMT - Barnacle, you were always the more laid back of us. Mama always took it easy on you.
Senator Barnacles Jones - Well, I was being groomed for this position. And you were being groomed for the harsh realities of life on the streets of Bradford. And speaking of reality, how is your new career in
the NAWC? God, I miss getting in the ring with you and taking on opponents. GMT - Big brother, there was this one punk, named Speedy Irons who got a cheap win but I plan on getting back at him. But this week I got
Dynamite Derrick. BJ - Sounds like a loser. I hope you know how to take out this guy. He almost reminds me of... of...
GMT - Corporal Punishment? BJ - No, no... GMT - Stormin' Normand? BJ - Didn't I beat him? GMT - How about, Goliath Eternal? BJ - Yup, that's it! Dynamite Derrick reminds me of Eternal! Both - Bwahahahahahaha!!!! GMT - Yeah, Eternal ran out of the EFW with his tail between his legs and I'm going to do the same to Derrick! Dynamite! You might think you're hot stuff but wait until we meet in the ring. You might have thought what I did to Speedy was something but that's nothing compared to the beating I'm going to give to you! Prepare for a Speeding Ticket!
(Both men give a thumbs up as the scene fades.) Killer Buddha -------------- EXTERIOR - COLISEUM PARKING LOT - SATURDAY AFTERNOON (B4 NAPALM) The NAWC is sponsoring an all day autograph signing session. Thousands of NAWC fans have come to the parking lot of the Mississippi Coliseum to meet and greet their favourite NAWC Superstars. Killer Buddha is here taking a picture with two young fans. The two boys are wearing "Killer
Buddha Rules!" T-Shirts and are smiling happily. Killer Buddha is wearing a black sweatsuit with black sneakers. He is also wearing a black baseball cap and his mirrored shades. The Father of the two boys finishes his roll of film.
BOY WITH CAN OF SODA: Wow, thanks for everything Buddha! 2nd BOY WITH EYEGLASSES: Yeah, Hope you win tonight. THE FATHER: Thanks Buddha, You don't know how happy you made my boys. KILLER BUDDHA: Oh Yes I do. I remember what it was like to be a fan too. (Killer Buddha points to the boys who are putting each other in wrestling holds.) KILLER BUDDHA: The kids are the real fans, they're the reason I love this sport so much. (The Father and his two sons walk away.) Killer Buddha notices the NAWC camera crew and signals for them to come closer. KILLER BUDDHA: Saturday Night Napalm is almost here. Tonight, Killer
Buddha faces Tom "The Cavalier" Knight for the greatest title on the face of this planet. I promised myself long ago that I would hold a
Heavyweight title someday. It looks like the Buddha Path wants "Someday"
to be tonight. (Killer Buddha takes his black baseball cap off.) KILLER BUDDHA: Cavalier, I will bring everything I know about the art of combat to the ring tonight. Killer Buddha has prepared himself physically, emotionally, psychologically and spiritually for this contest. I believe that tonight's match will be one of the greatest battles in the history of sports entertainment. The NAWC Heavyweight title is the ultimate representation of what Life and Wrestling are all about. If I defeat you Cavalier, and become the NAWC World Heavyweight
Champion, it would be a dream come true. It would mean that Fate has finally smiled on a man who has learned much about triumph and tragedy in his lifetime. Cavalier, I do not fear "Excalibur". I do not fear "The
Sword In The Stone". I welcome this battle and the fury it may unleash.
On this Holy Night, Killer Buddha will enter into Camelot, I will take a seat at the Round Table, I will reveal to all in attendance the wonder of the true "Holy Grail", the NAWC Heavyweight Title, and then all will come to know that Killer Buddha is the Once And Future King Of
Wrestling. (Killer Buddha pauses for a beat.) KILLER BUDDHA: I am Killer Buddha! (Killer Buddha puts his baseball hat back on.) KILLER BUDDHA: Instant Karma is coming to get you! (Killer Buddha takes off his shades, we can see the intensity.) KILLER BUDDHA: Violence Is The Only Answer!!! (Fade To Black.) Thane Sloan ------------- (Thane sits in his office reviewing the Friday Night Fights tape. He is wearing a black NAWC Polo shirt and a pair of jeans.)
Thane: As much as The Educator hate to say this. Congradulations are in order for Mr. Tom Knight. Mr. Knight there will be another time, another day, another place, where our paths will cross again. When they do, you demise will be quick and painfail, so enjoy your fifteen minutes of fame.
Mr. Capulco, it seems the walls to your little world are falling in all around you. First Rick Stevens and then Rio D. Genitic loose their titles. But yet you still see fit to try to tormenate The Educator.
Who and the hell is Damien Douglas and why is he my partner. Is it because, I choose his debut to pay back Chris Phear. Damien, I don't who you are, but I know what your about. So let me give you some free advice. Bring your lame self to the ring,.. stand in the corner...
watch and learn as The Educator slices and dices through the Imbread
Wonder of the World and his father/brother/uncle/lover whatever Stevens relationship is with Rio. Then after The Educator has educated Rio for a third time, I will walk over and tag you in, so you can cover the imbread and get the one, two, three. Do you understand what I am saying
Damien?
Next on the agenda seems to be one Chris Phear. Well, well, Mr. Phear,
I see you are trying to get ahead the easy way. Lets review shall we, not scheduled to be on the card, mainly due to lack of talent, but somehow you inserted in a match for the IC Title. Then after you win the title, you immediately demand The Educator face you at Nuclear
Winter for the IC Title. Smells like you have spent entirely too much time in Mr. Calpulco's office under the desk.
Well Chris (or should we call you Christina), your wish has come true. But before our match takes place, you have to agree to The Educator's terms. (Thane stops and takes a drink of water.)
Lets face it Chris since the arrival of The Educator, the NAWC has not been the same. Several NAWC so called superstars have issues with me besides yourself, Damien Douglas, Tom Knight, Derek Davies, Dr. Hate,
The Imbread, Rick Stevens, Budda and old Speedy Irons. So since this is a title match and I want no outside interferece. Lets make this a Hell in the Cell match. Pinfalls mean nothing, the only way to win is walk
outside of the cage. No swerve's, no politics, just your opportunity for revenge. If you don't agree to this Mr. Phear, your education will begin and end before you reach Nuclear Winter.
Finally, The Educator can be silent no longer on this subject. Speedy
Irons, I don't know who invited you to the NAWC. You were a nobody in the WWA and you will be a nobody here in the NAWC. So says The
Educator. (Scene fades to black.) ANNOUNCEMENTS Great job on this packet. Kasady isn't really gone from the NAWC. Just the character. His handler feels that he isn't doing too well with Kasady and will be debuting a new character soon.
