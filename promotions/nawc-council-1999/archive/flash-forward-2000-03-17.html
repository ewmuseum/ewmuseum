NAWC (1999) — Card Archive

Flash Forward

17 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tonight we have: "Electric" Amy Sparks, Jaye "The Big Dog" Doyle, Mad Dog,
Long S. Tran, and Speedy Irons.
"Electric" Amy Sparks ------------------------------- (The scene fades in at a Gold's Gym in Nashville. The camera pans the gym until it spots Amy Sparks working out on a universal gym in the far corner, wearing a pair of black spandex shorts and a black sports bra. Gregory
Hollinsworth steps into the camera's view and walks up to Amy.)
Greg - Hey Amy, how are you doing today? Amy Sparks - I am doing quite well. I suppose you'd like to do an interview? Greg - Yeah I would. I don't think anyone has had an exclusive interview with the number one contender for the NAWC Women's Title.
Amy Sparks - Well it's nice to be recognized I guess. But I'm sure it's
Jason you really want to talk to.
Greg - Well it would be nice, but I don't see him. And besides, it's not often where you can get some attention without him around.
Amy Sparks - Well he knows I like to have my time every now and then. I just felt that I should work on getting ready for Friday. It's going to be a tough match.
Greg - By the way, where is Jason? I thought you would have been in Louisville by now. Amy Sparks - He's back at the hotel. He said he just wanted to get a closer view of some things on Napalm before Friday.
Greg - I see. So have you heard the comments from the champion, Guinivere? Amy Sparks - Yes I did. I thought she was better than that. It's beginning to become apparent to me that she's been listening to Tom a litle too much.
She used to be so modest. Now she's developing that Cavalier ego. She thinks she's all high and mighty because she won the title in an over the top Battle Royal. Well what she doesn't understand is that anyone could've won that. it doesn't mean you're better. It was luck that won that match.
I basically gave it away with a rookie mistake. She never had to deal with me in that ring. Even if I would've won that match, or Hellfire, or Lady
Divine, it still would've been luck. Sure I haven't won the title yet, but
I've still done more in this company than her. The only thing it appears she's been taught is how to be over-confident of herself.
Greg - How do you feel you stack up with Guinivere in the ring? Amy Sparks - Well to be honest, I am much more mobile than her, and I will use that to my full adavntage. And she may think I'm looking too far ahead, but it's quite the contrary. I am totally focused on Friday, and not one moment after. And while she's busy talking about her underwear, I'm in the gym getting ready for this. But the one main advantage I have, which will be a major factor in the match, is the fact that I have actually had a wrestling match in my career. All she did was win an every woman for herself catfight. She has no real experience in the ring and I guarantee that it will show. She will feel Shock Therapy like never before, and I will walk out of Louisville as the NAWC's Women's title. Then Jason and I will be the NAWC's Championship couple.
Greg - So what are your thoughts on Ian Wilson and Blaze joining up with you and Jason? Amy Sparks - Honestly, I am very excited. Ian is a great talent, and him and Jason are true friends, and won't stab him in the back, unlike a former friend that I won't name at this time. Jason and Ian are both unselfish and aren't people who will back down from anyone, or bail out of a match just to keep a belt. Blaze is really great too. I feel sorry if anyone steps in her way. I've actually seen her kick the crap out of guys before. I actually think she could win the women's title with no problems if she wanted to. But she thinks that when she steps in the ring, she want's to attempt the lightweight division. So I wish her all the best in that endeavour.
Greg - Lastly, the rumours that you and Jason have joined up with someone in order to take out Tom Knight are piling up recently. Some say Threat, and some say Capulco and Giniro. Is there any truth to these rumours?
Amy Sparks - I really don't know right now. Jason may have a hidden agenda that I don't know about, but whatever he decides, I will be behind him one hundred percent, but it's not something I worry about. Right now my attention is directed at winning on Friday and I can assure you that Jason will be there to ensure that she doesn't bail like Tom did to Jason.
Greg - Well thanks for your time Amy, and best of luck to you. Hopefully next time I interview you, you'll be the champion.
Amy Sparks - The pleasure is all mine. (Amy returns to her weights as the screen fades to black.) Jaye "The Big Dog" Doyle ------------------------------------ (Sitting down on his couch in his living room, reading the latest NAWC interviews posted in his local paper,
Jaye Doyle scouts for his first opponent. Dressed in a long, neon green bath robe and matching slippers, he frowns as he skins through the words of his fellow NAWCers. Finally, exasperated, Jaye tosses the paper aside, picks up the television remote, and flicks it on.)
Jaye Doyle: This is going to be a tough pill for me to swallow here. Being a rookie in this new fed and having to take a back seat to guys who wouldn't carry my jockstrap is going to be a completely new experience for me.
Nevertheless, this is what I asked for and so I've got to start somewhere. (Doyle finally settles on the Jerry Springer show. The topic today, "My
Brother's My Husband and My Son's
My Nephew.") Jaye Doyle: I do recognize a few names, Jason Blake, Sean DuBose, and Tom
Cavalier, but mostly just
unknowns to me in the world of wrestling. So how should I go about getting my first match? I could resort to some lame insults to try and get somebody's goat but that's just not my style. I could tempt you to test your skill by ranting and raving about how good I am, but I don't think that would be any better. So, here's what I came up with. I've signed an open contract for anybody within the NAWC who's got "newcomer" next to their name so if you're interested, give the president a call.
(Doyle realizes exactly what show he has on and gets disgusted with himself.) Jaye Doyle: What the hell is this crap? (Doyle quickly changes the channel and puts it on TSN which is currently showing some kind of extreme
skydiving.) Jaye Doyle: Anything is better than that stupid Springer show. Anyway, where was I? Oh yes, I will make exceptions to this as there are a few guys
I'd like to get my hands on. Mad Dog, I'd like to be known as the only
Dogman around these parts so I wouldn't mind getting you in the squared circle. Titan, I've known a Titan for years now and I'm sure he'd appreciate it if I beat you around a bit. Ian Walsh, I'm the real deal when it comes to technicians so I'd also consider locking horns with you as well.
(Doyle rolls his eyes at himself.) Jaye Doyle: Ah, who am I kidding. Just one of you guys sign the papers... anybody. I'm getting board sitting on my ass here. (The scene fades out as Doyle focuses on the NHL playoff report that just started up on TSN.)
Mad Dog ------------- (Scene opens in a dark basement where the sound of water dripping can be heard. A light is on and only illuminates the place a little bit. The sound of footsteps echoes through the background. A shadowy figure emerges into view and finally steps into the light. This man is Mad Dog and he has a grave, maniacal look on his face.)
Mad Dog: I made an embarrasment out of my first victim, barely getting touched throughout the course of a pretty easy victory. He was afraid of falling victim to The Mauler and instead chose the chicken (bleep) way out and chose to lose by DQ rather than pinfall. He was lucky to take a loss in that manner.
(Mad Dog takes a few steps forward close to the camera where a 2X4 with a nail in it can be seen in his hand. His hand is covered with blood and the sight is nearly sickening to see.)
Mad Dog: You see, DQ is the only way out for many. Or of course EMT can be another way out. Some are actually smart enough, or should I say DESPERATE enough, to choose such an easy, painless way out. But what they don't know is in the shadows I will be lurking, waiting, watching, thinking, ready for the kill. I like to watch my enemies bleed and noone will escape those chances. My first victory in a line of many more to come. Once I retain greatness in the wrestling world once more, there will be one more to join me in my path
of destruction. (He begins to step even closer to the camera and grabs the 2X4 with both hands in front of him.)
Mad Dog: My next victim I await to find out. And when he steps in the ring with me, SURVIVAL WILL NOT BE AN OPTION!
(After screaming thise words Mad Dog raises the 2X4 above his head and swings it into the camera causing everything to fuzz out.)
Long S. Tran ------------------ *aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaahhhhhhhhh* Long S Tran stretches his arms and legs as he wearily gets out of bed. As usual, Tran is not a beautiful person to look at in the morning. His long hair is all over the place, and his eyes are bloodshot due to lack of sleep.
*AAAAHhhhhhhhhhHHhHh* He lets out another loud, uncontrollable yawn as he slowly flings the cover off himself. Casually, he scratches his hair (messing it up even MORE) and gets out of bed.
He sits at bedside for a while in silence as he tries to wake himself up. He glances at the clock which is hanging on the wall. 11:21am Long:- Oh shit!!! I'm late!!!! (Tran jumps off the bed and quickly grabs the first pair of pants and shirt he finds. He runs to the shower and the scene fades as the door slams shut)
-=-=-=- Scene reopens in a park of some sort. Greg Hollinsworth is seen sitting alone on the bench. He looks frustrated and glances at his watch every now and then.
Just as he's about to get up and walk away, a voice is heard off camera... Tran:- GREG!!!!! (Hollinsworth turns around to see Long Tran running up to him. His hair is still wet, and his shoelace is untied. Tran catches up to Greg as the two shake hands)
Tran:- Sorry I'm late. I slept in. Greg:- So... Shall we begin? (Both men start a casual stroll through the park as the camera follows them.) Greg:- So Long... Now that your fight with Claw is over, you can go forward in the NAWC. What are you going to do now?
Tran:- My fight with Claw over...? As far as I'm concerned, Claw and I are jut beginning! Greg:- But... At the last FNF you and Claw fought to the limit! It was an excellent display of skill on both parts! A draw to settle the war.
Tran:- You obviously haven't been keeping track of Claw and I have you? We both have fought on numerous occasions, yet there has never been a clear victor! This was our chance to prove once and for all who was the better man in the ring.
Greg:- But... Now that you've fought each other, there IS no clear winner. You've been feuding...* Tran:- (Interrupts) No... We've NEVER feuded. Ever since we first started wrestling, we've avoided each other. From the early days of our careers, to the present. When we DID step into the ring, it was never a feud. Sure...
We had a little rivalry going. But whenever we stepped in the ring, it was never personal. Greg:- So why did you challenge Claw? And why was this match so important? Tran:- Well... We've fought, and as I said earlier, we never found out who was better. I've fought opponents that I've destroyed easily, and I've fought opponents that just as easily destroyed me. But I have never fought someone to whom I am so evenly matched.
Greg:- Well... Now that Claw has joined The Threat, are you still going to continue your rivalry?
Tran:- Claw and I have unfinished business, but this week I am facing someone else. Greg:- Sean DuBose. He is a lightweight like yourself, and this will prove to be an interesting match.
Tran:- Well... I care not for Mr. DuBose. Greg:- What about the comments he made about you? Tran:- What comments? Greg:- About prefering to have matches with Lightweights like yourself. He also mentioned that his goal was to eventually capture the Lightweight
Title. Wasn't your aim to capture the Lightweight Title when you first signed with the NAWC?
Tran:- A lot of things changed. When I first came here, I wanted to be the first to hold the NAWC Lightweight Title. I had been given chance after chance to bring that belt home. Each time I was given a chance, I would fail. I contacted Claw to join the NAWC, hoping his presence will help
"bring me back." What happened was Claw eventually got better... And I somehow "lost my touch." When Claw won the Lightweight Title... I suppose I got filled with jealousy. He had won the Title that he KNEW I wanted to win.
From there, things started to go downhill for me, until... Greg:- He answered your challenge? Tran:- Correct. When he answered my challenge, I felt something inside me I never felt before. It was like some sort of drug, and I found myself WANTING to get back into shape, the way I used to be. I kept living in the past. I am 19 years old, and already I was seen as a has-been. I kept refering to what I accomplished in THE PAST.
Greg:- Um... We got a bit sidetracked there. Back to DuBose... You and he have a lot in common. You both are lightweights, and he certainly has respect for you. He isn't taking this match lightly. Also, he is making it clear he would like to hold the Lightweight Title.
Tran:- Well... If he wants that Lightweight Title, I guess the first step is to step into the squared circle against a Lightweight. I'm as light as they come in the NAWC! The NAWC has a lot of good Lightweights, and a lot of them would like a shot at the Gold. Who knows... If Sean and I put on a good match, Donovan might even give the winner a shot at his Lightweight Belt!
Providing Speedy doesn't win it on Friday... (Just as he finished the sentence, they reach a black car which is parked nearby. Greg looks at Tran)
Greg:- Well Long... Before I go, is there anything you would like to say to Sean before your match this Friday?
(Tran thinks for a bit, then looks at the camera) Tran:- Sean... You seem like a great competitor, but just because I am a mere 19 years old doesn't mean you can take me lightly. I have wrestled the toughest men in the sport in my career, and although I lost to a lot of them... I also defeated a lot of them. You have held gold before, I also have held gold. We may not have held any gold in the NAWC, but on Friday
Night, I know we're gonna put on a great match. Who knows... Maybe one day we'll meet again with the Lightweight Title on the line...
Greg:- Thanks, and good luck tomorrow night Long. Tran:- Thank you. (Hollinsworth gets in his car and drives off. Camera then pans to a nearby public phone. Tran slowly walks over to the phone and reaches in his pocket for some change. He lifts up the receiver and puts a few coins in the slot.)
Tran:- (Thinking to himself) "Should I call her?" (He hesitates, but finally manages to dial the number after a bit of stalling.) *RING!* Tran:- (Under his breath) Come on Jeanie... Please pick up... *RING!* He starts to sweat and get nervous. Last time he called her, he couldn't talk to her because his ego would not let him.
*RING!* He starts to fidget as he impatiently waits for her to answer. Part of him wants her to answer so he can ask her for advice. The other part of him is looking for an excuse not to talk to the girl that he left behind...
*RING!* Jeanie:- Hello...? (And just like last time, he can not bring himself to ask her.) *CLICK!* (Fade to black) Speedy Irons ------------------ _ _ _\PEEDY IRON_\ ============== *NAWC's Gregory Hollinsworth stands next to Speedy Irons. The lightweight sensation is dressed in his regular cyan-green pants with in silver down the legs 'Speedster' and a black shirt with a picture of him posing and in cyan-green around the picture 'Speedy Irons' and 'Fastest rising youngster in the world of wrestling today' and silver boots.*
GH: "Speedy, recently your carreer is going a downward spiral." Speedy: "Whoa! Whoa! Hold it right there, Greg! Yes, I DID lose from
Harrils. And even Trooper got me down this time! However, in Trooper's case it took THREE guys to take out the fastest rising youngster in the world of wrestling today!!! Like I said, this one was the LAST match between Trooper and me, for now. Trooper beat me, but I gave him a hell of a time. I struck him TWICE with the Iron Dropper and I flattened Daddy
Haggard with the HOTTEST finisher in the NAWC!"
GH: "Somehow, the NAWC suits like you... they offer you the chance to go after the NAWC Lightweight title."
Speedy: "What are you insinuating? That I ain't good enough to face Donovan
Moore??? You should know that I should be NAWC Lightweight Champion allready!!! Afterall I kicked Chris Phear around on more then one occassion! I am the HOTTEST wrestler that graced a squared circle in the last two decades!!! I got the speed, I got the technique and, more important, I got the LOOKS!!! So, I certainly DESERVE a shot at the NAWC
Lightweight belt!!!"
GH (mumbling): "Well, your win-loss records tells a different story." Speedy (furious): "WHAT was that????" GH: "Nevermind! Something else now... Moore holds two belts: the NAWC
Lightweight belt and the NAWC IC-belt!!!"
Speedy: "Greg, I sure hope Moore has balls and puts both titles on the line!!!" GH: "Moore has badmouthed your friend Thane Sloan before he won his IC-title." Speedy: "First things first: Sloan AIN'T my friend, I just respect the guy! Next, Moore is gonna get the wrestlin' lesson of a lifetime!!! Fight Night
Friday in Louisville, Kentucky... the fans will see a FAST FURY Break-Out and a KENTUCKY FRIED CHICKEN!!! The first one refers to me the latter to
Donovan Moore! Moore, be prepared to suffer the IRON DROPPER!"
GH: "Moore has the beautiful Denise." Speedy: "Oh, yes, the beauty that guides the beast! Well, everybody knows the ladies can't keep their eyes of me! Denise is no different! When all is said and done I walk away with some NAWC gold and the beautiful Denise will FLASH, after I took out her piece of TRASH!!! And that, my dear Greg, is a promise! 'Cause I'm HOT and Donovan Moore is DEFINITELY NOT!!!"
GH: "Good luck, Speedy Irons." Speedy: "Thanks, by Friday Moore is old news!" *Camera fades to black.* Good packet tonight. Keep up the good work everybody. [name withheld]
