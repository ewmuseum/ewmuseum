A self-attested roughly two-year-old fed caught mid-handover from an outgoing president to two incoming co-presidents, its own November PPV ad naming a live Powerhouse and Cruiserweight title scene and a three-way title bout for a 'WWW/IWA' championship. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A note posted to the newsgroup on 30 October 1996, from a self-described member with two wrestlers on its own roster, announced that the IWA's president had stepped down — staying on with the promotion, but no longer running it — and that two new co-presidents had taken over. By its own account the promotion was already “about (or maybe more than) 2 years” old, and the handover note doubled as a recruiting call: with many roleplayers gone, it needed more.

Two weeks later, on 13 November 1996, a short notice pointed newcomers at a sign-up address for “the NEW IWA.” Ten days after that, a pay-per-view advertisement for a card called November Bash named the promotion's own live scene in full: a ladder match for the Powerhouse belt between Prince Vejita and Ricky “The Mangler” Deathwish, with the loser to leave the promotion; a Cruiserweight title defense pitting champion “Aquamarine” Kelly Hart against Vertabreaker, with Hart also booked the same night against Dark Angel; a triangle match for the “WWW/IWA” championship between titleholder The Titan, Crash and Frostbite; and a television tag title defense by the Equalizers against the Soldiers of Fortune.

Nothing in the surviving record says whether November Bash went ahead as advertised, or how or when the IWA closed; the pay-per-view advertisement is the last surviving document under this promotion's name.
