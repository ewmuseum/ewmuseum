American Wrestling Alliance — News

AWA newsdesk, 21 September – 6 October 2007

21 September – 6 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is American Wrestling Alliance’s own front-page newsdesk, nine in-fiction posts running from the launch of its temporary site to the morning after its first card. The byline throughout is the promotion’s own reporter persona.

House Show!

Posted By: Drew Spade | 10.06.2007

As we told you guys earlier this week, American Wrestling Alliance would host their first show from Lawrenceville Illinois National Guard Armory. At 10:00am until 11:00am the stars of the American Wrestling Alliance put on a spectacular show with a few suprises. If you’d like to read a transcript of the show, just follow the link below.

Road Agent Signed!

Posted By: Drew Spade | 10.02.2007

It has just come across my desk that the Owners Committee of American Wrestling Alliance worked out a deal with a former Hardcore Wrestling Organization star to act as a Road Agent backstage.

The job description of Nixx is to help the younger workers in American Wrestling Alliance to become more "well rounded" wrestlers inside the ring as well as working on interview skills. We wish the best of luck to Nixx in his new role, and see a bright future ahead of him here in American Wrestling Alliance.

Delays.

Posted By: Drew Spade | 10.02.2007

We here at American Wrestling Alliance would like to apologize for the delay in updates to this website. This past weekend has seen some major negoations taking place, and we’ve all been very busy trying to get ready for the launch of the American Wrestling Alliance.

First on the agenda is the new performers who will be appearing with the American Wrestling Alliance. News was broke recently of an unknown worker contacting the American Wrestling Alliance offices about possibly working dates. We can confirm he will be working with the American Wrestling Alliance and that his name is none other then Ninja K.

Also signed to work for the American Wrestling Alliance on a pay per apperance deal are the following wrestlers; ToK,

Johnny Legend,

Brock Metzer,

El Phantasmo,

Astro Creep, and

Juan Valdez. All of these performers will be working on the first scheduled American Wrestling Alliance show.

Speaking of our first show, American Wrestling Alliance is proud to announce that there is a show planned for October 6th, 2007 in Lawrenceville Illinois at the National Guard Armory. Tickets for the event are only $5.00 and can be purchased by emailing myself or you can also find tickets in the town of Lawrenceville. Bell time for the event is 10:00am.

Two Names Signed!

Posted By: Drew Spade | 09.25.2007

That’s right, American Wrestling Alliance has officially come to agreements with two men who will make an immediate impact on the roster once the first set of shows are booked. The first wrestler to come to a verbal agreement with American Wrestling Alliance goes by the name of Rush, though his real name is Mark Gionet.

The second signing comes out of left field literally. The man contacted American Wrestling Alliance two days ago and said that he wanted to speak directly to one of the co-owners. That owner was Carl Lucas who spent time wrestling under the name Lusus for no Brand Wrestling, Jolt Wrestling, and Hardcore Wrestling Organization. Though we can not release the name of this performer, we will say that he is a former Legacy Of Champions Legacy Champion.

Negoations Resume.

Posted By: Drew Spade | 09.23.2007

According to the committe of the American Wrestling Alliance talks have once again started up between TBS Superstation and this promotion. News that I’m getting behind the scenes is there’s talks of AWA getting a weekly prime time show on Sunday nights. If all goes as planned the deal could be announced as early as next week.

In other important news it appears that a canadian company by the name of "The Fight Network" has already come to terms on an agreement to air AWA shows. The international audience for AWA could be a stepping stone to future internation tours!

Top Sites!

Posted By: Drew Spade | 09.22.2007

As part of a promotional deal with a popular wrestling website titled "EWZine," the American Wrestling Alliance has joined their "top sites" listing feature that could very well make American Wrestling Alliance one of the top listed sites on the internet. You’ll notice a button for their page under the affilates section towards the bottom of the left side.

Television?

Posted By: Drew Spade | 09.21.2007

As of noon today contract negoations to bring American Wrestling Alliance to all major Cable Television providers has hit a stalling point. Officials of both Comcast and American Wrestling Alliance are trying to hammer out the numbers aspect of the deal, but there’s currently said to be a couple of points both sides aren’t happy with. We will keep our ears and eyes on the situation in order to keep you informed, first.

New Performers!

Posted By: Drew Spade | 09.21.2007

Earlier today, word was sent that American Wrestling Alliance has secured the talent of two very well known performers throughout Pro Wrestling. The official list of names has been with-held as of right now but we can officially say that by the end of the week the names of the two now associated with AWA will be released.

Temporary Site Launched!

Posted By: Drew Spade | 09.21.2007

The American Wrestling alliance owners committee has annonced that due to contract negoations going well with an Internet Media Development company who currently will be un-named, there is now a temporary site online for the AWA. Obivous to this fact, is that you are reading this message right here on the brand new AWA website.

The owners of the AWA also wanted me to make sure and mention that there are links on this very website that will allow for fans to see exactly who has been signed to take part in the AWA. Fans can also chime in on discussions about the AWA by following the "forums" link, towards the top of the page.

Another interesting thing to note is that there’s a link which gives information on how to send in footage that you might wish to have judged, that could potentially get you into AWA. This link is for prospects of the AWA only.
