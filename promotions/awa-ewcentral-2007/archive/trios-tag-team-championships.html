American Wrestling Alliance — Championship

Trios Tag Team Championships

October 2007

What this is American Wrestling Alliance’s own page for the championship, written before the belt was first awarded.

Not trying to be like anyone else in the sport of Professional Wrestling, American Wrestling Alliance has introduced their version of tag team titles. These three belts collectively are known as the Trios Tag Team Championships.

The heritage of the three man team stems back to Pro Wrestling in Mexico, and has many times been showcased here in the United States. American Wrestling Alliance plans to take that one step further and have Trios Tag Team matches featured on nearly every event.

The rules for a Trios Tag Team match are a little bit different then the modern American Tag Team match. For example, if one of your partners is to exit the ring for any reason, a new wrestler from your team will simply take that mans place without a tag being made. Another example of how these matches will deviate from the regular two man Tag Team matches is breaking up a pinfall is legal, and afterwards the team who broke up the pinfall is then given 8 seconds to perform a special "team" oriented move.

These are just a few examples of what will set the Trios Tag Team matches apart and make the Trios Tag Team Championships something very special and a reason to pay attention to American Wrestling Alliance.
