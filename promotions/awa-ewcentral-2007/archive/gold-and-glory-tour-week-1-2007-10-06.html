American Wrestling Alliance — Card and results

The Gold and Glory Tour, Week 1

6 October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is American Wrestling Alliance’s own transcript of its first card, staged at the National Guard Armory in Lawrenceville, Illinois: an introduction, three matches, and a mid-show announcement of the promotion’s first supercard. Served from the full-show page, which carries every chapter of the transcript.

A ruckus crowd of 276 people just about half way filled the Illinois National Guard Armory for what was set to be an exciting mid-morning of American Wrestling Alliance action. The fans had been piling into the Armory since nearly 8:00am and they were more then ready to get the show started.

From the back of the building, through a sheer black curtain hanging over a door way, came five men all dressed in casual attire. The first man was instantly recognizable as the former Lusus, who was one of the Co-Owner’s of American Wrestling Alliance, now going by his real name; Carl Lucas. Directly behind him was the much younger Dejuan Rapture. Following those two were Johnathon Biggs, Christopher Anston, and finally Nicholas Bluner.

All five men made their way into the ring as the attendant at ringside stood up and went to the side of the ring handing Carl Lucas a microphone. Though the sound in the building wasn’t the best, they made due and Carl began to speak.

Carl Lucas: "We would personally like to thank each and every one of you for coming out this morning to enjoy what should be an exciting hour of American Wrestling Alliance action."

Carl paused as the fans started to cheer, for the eventual wrestling they would witness.

Carl Lucas: "C’mon, can’t you make anymore noise then that? Let’s hear you guys be as loud as you can, and stay that way for the remainder of the show. I want to see everyone out here cheer for their favorites, and boo the guys they don’t like."

The fans cheered even louder this time at the command of Carl Lucas.

Carl Lucas: "I’m sure that each and every one of these four men standing next to and behind me would love to get a chance to speak for you guys, but unfortunately for them we also feel that we’ve already taken up enough of your time. I’m sure you came here to see wrestling, and not talking, so we are going to get down to business."

Carl Lucas handed the microphone back to the ringside attendant, and all five men exited the ring the same way they entered. They walked along the narrow entrance way, before disappearing through the curtain and into the back.

Opening Singles Match

Tok vs. Johnny Legend

"I Stand Alone" was playing over the speaker system in the National Guard Armory as ToK walked out from behind the curtain and into an anticipating crowd of fans. One of the little children near the guard rail stuck out his hand, to which ToK ignored and continued on his way to the ring. ToK was of medium size standing about six foot six and looking somewhere around two hundred and fifty pounds. ToK slid into the ring under the bottom rope and stretched out his arms and legs waiting for his opponent.

"Man Without Fear" by Rob Zombie began to play over the speakers as the fans stood on their feet and cheered for the opponent that ToK would have. From behind the curtain bursted a very athletic looking man named Johnny Legend. Quite a bit shorter then his opponent, looking to be under six feet tall and also a bit smaller in stature, the man slapped hands and got the fans riled up his entire way to the ring. Legend jumped up onto the ring apron before leaping over the top rope and landing in the ring.

Both ToK and Legend came face to face in the center of the ring as the referee called for the bell, and the match was set to begin. ToK and Legend locked up in the center of the ring with a collar and elbow tie-up, as ToK backed Legend into the corner. The referee tried his best to get in between both men and force a clean break but ToK refused the orders after a five count, and punched Legend in the face. ToK then backed off and Legend sprinted forwards with a cart wheel kick that sent ToK tumbling to the canvas. Legend went for a quick cover but only managed to get a one count.

Legend pulled ToK up to his feet and locked on a side head lock, then wrenched down as hard as he could. ToK using his many years as an advantage quickly reversed the hold into a hammer lock. Legend showed his prowess in chain wrestling though, and reversed the hammer lock into an arm wrench. Legend dropped an elbow down across the arm of ToK which took his opponent down to one knee. Legend used his free hand to lift ToK back up to his feet and then leapt into the air wrapping his legs around the head of ToK and hitting a picture perfect hurricanrana. ToK’s body slammed against the mat after the impact of the flip and then he rolled to the outside. Legend on the inside of the ring bounced off the ropes and then sprinted towards the set ToK was near. Legend leapt over the top of the ropes flipping in air and coming down on ToK. The fans in the Armory stood on their feet cheering the fast paced and high flying action that was taking place in the ring.

As the referee got to a count of six Legend lifted ToK to his feet and then rolled him into the ring under the bottom rope. Legend slid in as well, then covered his opponent for a very close two count. Legend climbed up the corner and onto the top turnbuckle, then leapt off backwards with a moonsault that was sure to put ToK away. Just before Legend landed, ToK rolled out of the way and Legend came down hard on the canvas face first. ToK moved over on top of Legend trying to take advantage of a mistake but only managed to get a two count from the referee. ToK staggered up to his feet and pulled Legend up with him, ToK bounced off the ropes and charged towards Legend with an "Axe Bomber" clothesline and leg sweep combo that he used as his signature move. Legend took the impact of the clothesline and did a complete standing back flip, landing on his feet and then quickly turning around directly behind a stunned ToK. Legend grasped the arms of ToK and twisted them above the mans head in a full nelson before lifting him up and dropping backwards with a full nelson suplex. Legend bridged on the suplex as the referee counted the pin fall, and Legend was awarded the victory of the match.

As Legend stood in the ring celebrating his victory, a man simply known as Greatness came running out of the back and slid into the ring behind Legend. The fans tried to warn Legend to no avail, as Greatness clubbed Legend in the back of the head. Legend went tumbling down to the canvas, as Greatness laid in boots. Greatness lifted Legend up to his feet and then hit a vicious looking power bomb before a host of security guards and extra referee’s hit the ring to detain Greatness.

The referee’s and security guards ushered Greatness to the back as Legend was helped up by a lone Referee and then given a round of applause by the fans for his victory over ToK. Legend walked towards the back, limping and slapping hands before disappearing through the curtain.

Trios Tag Team Match

El Phantasmo, Astro Creep, and Juan Valdez vs. Half Sliced and Rush

A new referee was standing in the center of the ring as stereotypical spanish music began playing through the speakers and a the triple team tandem of El Phantasmo, Astro Creep, and Juan Valdez walked out through the curtains. El Phantasmo was a masked wrestler from Tijuana Mexico. His partner Astro Creep was from an undisclosed location in Mexico and Juan Valdez was from Columbia. All three men had made quite a name for themselves in various Mexican Promotions and were here to display their triple tag ability for the American audience. As all three men entered the ring and waiting on their opponents, "Achy Breaky Hart" by Billy Ray Cyrus began to play and two very strange looking men in 12 Loafs and Butter Brown walked out from the back. Neither man looked like they had showered in weeks as the fans boo’d the duo. Then, Rush, their trios team partner walked out from the curtains and looked towards the crowd with a very hesitant look on his face. Near the ringside, 12 Loafs tripped going up the steps and fell on his face as Butter Brown tried to help him up. Rush ignored the two men and circled the ringside area looking on at his opponents.

All the men were in the ring as Rush and Juan Valdez stood in the ring and the referee called for the bell. Rush and Valdez stood face to face in the center of the ring as Rush challenged Valdez to try and knock him over. Valdez bounced off the ropes and came forward with a clothesline that did nothing. Valdez then challenged Rush and the results was the same. Rush challenged Valdez to try again, Valdez bounced off the ropes and came rushing forward, Rush leapt up and over Valdez though confusing him. As soon as Rush landed and Valdez turned around to see what had happened, Rush hit a hip toss that took down his opponent. Valdez was quick to his feet and charged forward but was caught with an arm drag. Valdez climbed to his feet once more and stumbled forward this time to be hit with a dropkick. Valdez rolled to the outside of the ring as El Phantasmo climbed through the ropes to test Rush.

El Phantasmo charged towards Rush and was quickly taken down with a drop toe hold. Rush hopped up to his feet and took a quick step towards the head of El Phantasmo and dropped an elbow across the back of El Phantasmo. Rush lifted El Phantasmo back to his feet and hit a back suplex that shook the ring. Rush pulled El Phantasmo up once again and whipped him into the ropes. As El Phantasmo came back towards Rush, he was lifted into the air with a back body drop that sent him up and over the top rope tumbling to the arena floor. Astro Creep came running into the ring to replace his fallen opponent.

Astro Creep ran towards Rush and hit him from behind with a clothesline that sent him up and over the top rope to the outside of the floor. This time both 12 Loafs and Butter Brown climbed into the ring and walked up behind Astro Creep hitting him in the back of the head with forearms. On the outside of the ring Juan Valdez, El Phantasmo and Rush were now standing on their feet, as Half Sliced lifted Astro Creep for a power slam and threw him over the top rope onto all three men standing ringside.

The fans cheered the high impact action as both members of Half Sliced acted as if the entire set of hand claps and screams were simply for them. Both men played paper rock scissors in the center of the ring in order to decide who would be the legal man. Butter Brown was left in the ring as 12 Loafs stood on the apron and a dazed Astro Creep got to his feet and slid into the ring. Astro stumbled into Butter Brown who lifted him into the air with a suplex and then sent him crashing down to the canvas. Butter Brown tagged out to his partner 12 Loafs who climbed up to the top rope and came leaping off with a leg drop on Astro Creep. 12 Loafs went for a cover but only managed a two as Juan Valdez and El Phantasmo came charging in the ring to break up the attempt. Juan Valdez whipped Butter Brown into the ropes and kicked him in the stomach as he came back doubling him over. Juan Valdez bounced off the ropes behind him as El Phantasmo and Astro Creep did the same on the side ropes. All three men charged forward with a triple drop kick on 12 Loafs. Astro Creep and Juan Valdez climbed back out to the apron as El Phantasmo went for a pin fall

Butter Brown and Rush climbed into the ring to break up the pin fall, to which they managed to do. Butter Brown and 12 Loafs lifted El Phantasmo to his feet and hit a double suplex as Rush came flying off the top rope with a frog splash. Astro Creep and Juan Valdez came charging into the ring, as 12 Loafs and Butter Brown caught the on coming assault and the referee made the three count off of Rush’s frog splash.

Rush picked up El Phantasmo and threw him out of the ring over the top rope with a hip toss and his partners helped him up. Rush climbed the corner celebrating his victory as Butter Brown and 12 Loafs played a thumb wrestling war in the center of the ring. Rush looked at the two confused and then exited the ring, shortly followed by his Half Sliced partners for the day.

A Special Announcement!

Carl Lucas one of the owners of the American Wrestling Alliance walked out from behind the curtain and stood near the entrance. He held a microphone in his hand as he began to speak.

Carl Lucas: "Are you guys having a good time so far?"

The fans stood on their feet and cheered for the action they had seen so far.

Carl Lucas: "Excellent. Now we had announced that we’d have a special announcement at today’s show and before we give you guys the main event I think it’s a perfect time to let you all in on what you can expect from American Wrestling Alliance in the coming months. Sunday November 25th, 2007 American Wrestling Alliance is set to debut on the InDemand network with the first of our bi-monthly "Super Cards" titled The Conclusion 2007. During this special event you will witness a one on one encounter of Ninja K, a former globally known wrestler."

Those in attendance who had known who Ninja K was cheered until their voices went hoarse. The others simply cheered along. Then in a surprise moment, behind Carl Lucas walked out the masked warrior vigalinte, Ninja K.

The fans continued to cheer even more, as Ninja K raised both of his hands then clasped them together in a praying position before dropping his head down and posing for the crowd. The fans continued to cheer as a small smoke filled the front of the entrance and as it cleared Ninja K had vanished just as quickly as he had appeared. Carl Lucas walked back through the curtains as the fans were left to anticipate and speculate just exactly who Ninja K would be wrestling!

Singles Match Main Event

- American Wrestling Alliance Heavyweight Championship Tournament Qualifier -

Brock Metzer vs. Matt Fury

"Bodies" by Drowning Pool began to play over the speakers as the announcer made the announcement that the following match would qualify the winner for the Heavyweight Championship Tournament. Out from the back came a fan favorite by the name of Matt Fury. As Fury climbed into the ring his theme music was replaced with "This Moment" by Disturbed and Brock Metzer came out from behind the curtain sprinting to the ring. Metzer slid into the ring under the bottom rope and ran straight towards Fury with rapid fire fists to his face and the referee quickly called for the bell.

Metzer ran off the far ropes and came charging back forward before jumping in the air and landing on the knees of Fury, monkey flipping him up and over. Fury did a full flip and landing on his feet as Metzer rolled up to his feet. Both Metzer and Fury charged forward as Fury threw a clothesline that Metzer ducked. Metzer and Fury bounced off the ropes simultaneously and both came back towards the center of the ring, Fury ducked down as Metzer leapt over the top with a sunset flip that Fury simply rolled through. Fury leapt into the air and drop kicked a sitting Metzer in the face.

The fans began cheering the fast paced action, as Fury went for a cover and managed to only get a two count. Fury lifted Metzer to his feet and whipped him into the ropes, as Metzer came sprinting back Fury lifted him with a very quick snap power slam and then went for a second cover, this time getting a two count. Fury pulled Metzer to his feet and threw him into the turnbuckles. Fury charged forward with his shoulder lowered, Metzer leapt up over Fury and rolled him up for a one count.

Both men were back on their feet staring down each other as Metzer lifted his hand daring Fury to take part in a test of strength. Fury obliged and locked hands with Metzer before both men locked their other hands together. Metzer and Fury slammed together chest to chest as both tried using their strength to control the test of strength. Fury showed impressive strength by grinding back on the hands of Metzer and forcing him down to the canvas backwards in what was a pin attempt. The referee counted two before one of Metzer’s shoulders shot up off the canvas. Metzer fought his way back to his feet then leapt into the air wrapping his feet around the head of Fury and rolling him to the ground with a side roll.

Fury rolled through the move and stood on his feet as Metzer had already hit the ring ropes and came charging back forward then slid between the legs of Fury. Metzer grabbed the back of Fury’s head and pulled in down under his arms, before grasping his tights and lifting him into the air for a reverse suplex. Metzer covered the prone body of Fury for a very close two count. Metzer pulled Fury up from the canvas and stood him in the center of the ring, Metzer ran and jumped onto the second rope slingshotting himself back towards Fury and knocking him over with a leg lariat. Metzer went for a another pin fall attempt and again only managed a two count.

Metzer lifted Fury to his feet and tucked his head under his arm, then grasped onto the tights of Fury and lifted him straight into the air for what is called the "Independent Clash," Metzer’s version of a Brainbuster Suplex. Fury kicked his feet, before kicking his knee’s down into the head of Metzer and being released of the hold. Metzer was doubled over in the center of the ring as Fury landed on his feet. Fury took advantage of the situation and pushed Metzer down between his legs then wrapped his arms around the stomach of Metzer and lifted him onto his shoulders driving him down into the canvas for a power bomb. Fury held onto it and lifted Metzer again, then sent him crashing down onto the canvas for a second power bomb. Fury once again held onto Metzer and lifted him a third time, Fury let Metzer sit on his shoulders as he repositioned his hands, crossing Metzer’s arms in front of his chest and dropping down in a sitting crucifix power bomb called the "Fury Bomb" to which the referee counted three.

Fury released Metzer and stood up before throwing his hands into the air celebrating his win as the announcer let everyone know that Fury had won the match. Fury leaned over and pulled Metzer to his feet as both men shook hands in the center of the ring. Metzer exited the ring quickly after and Fury continued to celebrate as the first ever American Wrestling Alliance show came to an end.
