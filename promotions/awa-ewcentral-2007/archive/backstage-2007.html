American Wrestling Alliance — Rules

Backstage

October 2007
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
What this is American Wrestling Alliance’s own orientation page for its writers, describing how the promotion worked behind the curtain: a chat room, an application thread the membership voted on, a locker room for each character’s own biography, and a Match Writing Aid per wrestler.

Courage is grace under pressure.

Welcome to the American Wrestling Alliance Backstage Section. This is a personal section for the handlers of the American Wrestling Alliance. If you are back here and not involved in the AWA, congratulations you have somehow managed to either breach the security of our system, or you’ve found out someone’s password. Either way, if you don’t belong here, leave!

Alternatively, if you are here because you are actually a member of the American Wrestling Alliance, there’s a lot of useful things that are in this section for you to concentrate on. First and formost is the link at the top titled "Contacts," this is a section that lists all the email and instant messaging contacts for various members of the American Wrestling Alliance. Use this section to contact other members of the efed, for various needs.

You will also notice a link called "AIM Chat," This link will automatically take you to an AOL Instant Messenger chat room (must have AOL IM). The purpose of these chat rooms is for fed wide discussions about upcoming plans and just general conversation.

The "Applications" link is one of the most important places you can visit as it will have a list of guys who have applied for the American Wrestling Alliance. You are welcome to read over these and then give your input on what you think about each person’s application, as well as give a yes or no, on if they should be accepted.

One of the most important links is the link titled "Locker Room." This is where you fill out all the personal information about your character that will be displayed on your roster page. This section is one of the most important, because we want the "visitor" portion of the site to look sleak and finished. Having mission biography pages will make everything look unfinished.

The final link is the "Match Writing Aids." These are very detailed descriptions of your character and important information about your character. Please submit one of these if you have not already. There will be an example MWA for anyone who does not know what should be included.
