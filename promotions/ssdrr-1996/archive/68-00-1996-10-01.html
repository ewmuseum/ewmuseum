SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 68:00

1 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the ring: Barry Horowitz, Ric Flair JIM ROSS: FLAIR HITS HOROWITZ WITH A RIGHT CROSS, AND THAT STUNS HIM! JESSE VENTURA: THIS COULD BE IT! JR: ANOTHER CROSS, AND HOROWITZ STUMBLING BACK TO THE ROPES! FLAIR TAKES
A STEP BACK, RUNS IN, HOROWITZ *COUNTERS* WITH A BACK BODY DROP ATTEMPT
BUT FLAIR SHIFTS HIS CENTRE OF GRAVITY AND GOES TO THE SIDE!
JV: WE ALMOST HAD IT, ROSS! JR: BARRY HOROWITZ DIDN'T HAVE THE STRENGTH TO POWER FLAIR OVER, AND NOW
THE NATURE BOY RETALIATES WITH A PUNCH IN THE LOWER RIB AREA!
JV: BOTH MEN'S EYES ARE GLAZED OVER, ROSS, THEY MIGHT NOT HAVE THE STRENGTH TO CONTINUE! JR: INDEED, AS Ric Flair just drops on his knees to the canvas, and grabs
Horowitz from behind in a blatant choke!
JV: He's got his hands wrapped around Horowitz's throat, now changing to the traditional headlock-style choke ...
JR: "Traditional headlock-style choke." That's a Jesse-ism if there ever was one. JV: Flair now putting the other arm behind the neck, and that should help impede the flow of blood to the brain ...
JR: Yes, it should, which would indicate to me that perhaps Flair's better off than he looks!
JV: Hey, we've seen Flair physically beat hundreds of times over, but nobody's ever been able to short-circuit that brilliant, conniving mind of his!
JR: Barry Horowitz now tapping the canvas with his foot, and that starts the crowd up, starting that famous Barry chant again!
JV: Slowly this crowd getting behind Horowitz! JR: Barry trying to make it to one KNEE AS THIS CROWD GETTING BEHIND HIM,
BARRY GETS TO ONE KNEE, NOW BOTH KNEES AS FLAIR TURNS IT INTO A SLEEPER,
BUT HOROWITZ CONTINUES TO GAIN MOMENTUM, GETTING HIS FEET UNDER HIM, FLAIR
JUMPS ONTO BARRY'S BACK BUT HE GETS TO HIS FEET ... JV: BUT HE'S JUST STANDING THERE, HE'D BETTER DO SOMETHING! JR: BARRY BACKING UP, AND HE DRIVES FLAIR BACK-FIRST INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! JV: AND THAT RELEASES THE HOLD! JR: AND BARRY COLLAPSES TO THE MAT, exhausted by that effort but he's bought himself a little recouperative time now, looks like!
JV: Flair still on his feet, but he's leaning back on those turnbuckles, he's going to recover better here because his airways are straighter!
JR: Jesse, just think about all the names that have been in that ring tonight ... we started with the Heartbreak Kid, Shawn Michaels, and Eddie
Guerrero ...
JV: Steve Williams, Big Van Vader ... JR: The Undertaker, Dean Douglas ... JV: The Hart brothers, Terry Gordy ... JR: And now it all boils down to these two men. JV: Barry Horowitz, the biggest underdog of them all, and the dirtiest player ever to play the game, the Nature Boy, Ric Flair ... well, at least we could have expected one of them to be here at the close!
JR: The last sentence remains to be written here tonight, Horowitz now trying to get to his feet again but he keeps stumbling forward, meanwhile
Flair out of the CORNER AND HE DROPS the elbow on the head of Horowitz!
JV: And Flair immediately struggles to get to his feet! JR: Barry Horowitz prone on the mat, Flair picks him UP BY THE HEAD AND RAKES BARRY'S EYES OVER HIS BOOTLACES! JV: IF NOW ISN'T THE TIME TO GO FOR THE EASY PAIN, ROSS, WHEN IS?!? JR: FLAIR grabs the left arm of Horowitz and twists it, small arm bar here, Horowitz still trying to get to his feet!
JV: He needs to get to a higher base right now, since he's on his knees now Flair is able to jerk that arm back a lot further, if he gets to his feet the Nature Boy won't be able to cause as much damage!
JR: Horowitz gets his right foot under him, Flair KICKS Barry in the short ribs, but Horowitz still gets his other foot under him! ANOTHER kick, but
Horowitz not fazed! AGAIN, BUT HOROWITZ SAW IT COMING AND GRABS THE LEG!
JV: I DON'T EXPECT TO SEE AN INZIGIRI KICK HERE, ROSS! JR: NOR DO I, AS HOROWITZ SWEEPS THE OTHER LEG, PUTTING FLAIR ON HIS BACK
IN THE CENTRE OF THE RING, AND *DROPS* THE ELBOW ON THE INSIDE OF THE
THIGH! JV: AND THAT WAS CLOSE TO BEING A LOW BLOW, ROSS! JR: IF NOT NOW, WHEN, JESSE? YOU SAID IT YOURSELF! JV: YES I DID, I JUST SAID THAT WAS A LOW BLOW BY HOROWITZ, I DIDN'T SAY IT WAS WRONG! JR: HOROWITZ NOW with a single-leg grapevine, going back to work on the left leg of the Nature Boy, but FLAIR reaches up and thumbs Horowitz in the eye!
JV: And Flair wasting no time retaking the offensive! JR: Flair grabbing THE LEG ... JV: HERE WE GO! JR: SPINS THE LEG AROUND, IF HOROWITZ IS GONNA GET THE OTHER FOOT UP HE BETTER DO IT NOW! JV: I DON'T THINK HE'S GOT THE STRENGTH LEFT, ROSS! JR: FLAIR'S GOT THE OTHER LEG, AND DROPS DOWN INTO THE FIGURE- ... no, he doesn't! JV: Flair couldn't swing his other leg around to lock it in, and just gives up on it! JR: I think both men have been totally drained now, we may not have a winner tonight if this keeps up!
JV: No, Flair rolls over, trying to get back to his feet! JR: And so does Horowitz! JV: I feel it, Ross ... I think this is it! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJORK RULES! - Go Tigers! (I know ...)
