SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 44:00

19 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Undertaker, Owen Hart, Roddy Piper, Dean Douglas,
Madusa, Diesel, British Bulldog
Entering: Bret Hart (As we last left you, as Bret Hart stepped through the curtain out to the ring, the crowd popped so loudly Ross and Ventura became uncomprehendible.
It takes a good ten seconds for the pop to die down enough so the announcers can be heard.)
JIM ROSS: ... NOT A SINGLE PERSON STANDING! WHAT AN OVATION! JESSE VENTURA: WHAT?!? JR: WHAT A TRIBUTE TO ONE OF THE GREATEST, IF NOT *THE* GREATEST, SCIENTIFIC WRESTLER THIS SPORT HAS EVER SEEN! JV: NO, I DON'T HAVE ANY BEANS! JR: BRET HART MAKING HIS WAY TO THE RING AS DIESEL CINCHING IN THAT FIGURE-FOUR ON THE UNDERTAKER EVEN TIGHTER! JV: HE'S BEEN WORKING ON THAT LEG FOR TWO AND A HALF MINUTES NOW! JR: NICE TO HAVE YOU BACK, JESSE! JV: WHAT DO YOU MEAN, BACK? JR: NEVER MIND ... the Hitman slides into the ring, who's he going to work on, he ... goes over to Diesel and STARTS STOMPING AWAY, AND THE CROWD
PICKS UP THE INTENSITY LEVEL ONCE AGAIN! JV: MAN ALIVE! YOU'DA THOUGHT THEY'DA LAST THEIR VOICES BY NOW! JR: YOU HAVE TO GIVE THE HITMAN CREDIT, THOUGH, JESSE, HE DESERVES EVERY CLAP OF APPLAUSE HE GETS! JV: I may not like his attitude, Ross, but that's the only thing NOT to like about Bret Hart!
JR: Owen Hart still caught in that back waistlock by Roddy Piper, BUT HE
GETS out with a back elbow, grabs Piper BY THE HEAD BUT PIPER HOOKS THE
BOTTOM ROPE with his left leg and Owen'll have a hard time getting him out if Piper can hold on!
JV: The Hitman stomping a mudhole in Big Daddy Cool! JR: He's going straight to the rib area, but now turns to the Undertaker,
DROPPING DOWN TO HIS KNEES AND A FLURRY OF LEFT HANDS! THE SOUTHPAW IS
TAKING THE GRIM REAPER TO TOWN! JV: THE BRITISH BULLDOG IS GETTING CHOKED! JR: Dean Douglas just rolled Davey Boy Smith up in a schoolboy, and he's choking him with his free hand, BUT THE HITMAN JUST GAVE HIM A PUNCH THAT
SENT HIM FLYING INTO THE CORNER! JV: HE SAILED EIGHT FEET IN THE AIR! JR: MY GOSH! AND NOW BRET GOING AFTER HIS OWN BROTHER-IN-LAW, THE BRITISH BULLDOG! JV: THIS IS INCREDIBLE! JR: MEANWHILE, Owen Hart just got a thumb in the eye from Roddy Piper, that backs the King of Harts off and Piper runs out and BULLDOGS Owen!
JV: Nobody does the running bulldog better than the Hot Rod, Ross! JR: Bret Hart has layed nearly everybdoy out, now he picks up Madusa who still hadn't gotten to her FEET AND THROWS HER TO THE OUTSIDE!
HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: MADUSA HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: THE WOMEN AREN'T DOING TOO WELL TONIGHT, JIM ROSS! JR: INDEED NOT, as now Roddy Piper going over to Bret, complaining that he wanted to throw Madusa out BUT BRET GRABS HIM BY THE HAIR AND THROWS HIM
OUT THE OTHER SIDE OF THE RING! FINK: "ROWDY" RODDY PIPER HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! JR: TWO ELIMINATIONS FOR THE HITMAN IN LESS THAN TEN SECONDS, AND THE ONLY MAN LEFT STANDING IN THE RING IS ... JV: OH BOY! JR: OWEN TURNS AROUND, AND FACE-TO-FACE THE BROTHERS MEET! JV: CAN YOU FEEL IT? JR: YOU COULD CUT IT WITH A KNIFE! ... OWEN TURNING HIS HEAD, AND *MISSES*
THE ROUNDHOUSE RIGHT, BRET WITH A RIGHT JAB, ANOTHER, ANOTHER, ANOTHER,
ANOTHER, GOING FOR THE ROUNDHOUSE ... HE NAILS IT! JV: HE'S THE ONLY ONE STANDING! JR: THE HITMAN IS CLEANING HOUSE! WASTING NO TIME, DROPS DOWN INTO A SIDE HEADLOCK ON HIS YOUNGER BROTHER JV: AND EVERYONE ELSE JUST BEGINNING TO STIR! JR: Big Daddy Cool, Diesel, pulling himself up on those ropes, he might have taken the worst of Bret's fury!
JV: I wouldn't say that, Ross, look at the two wrestlers we've got coming down the aisle beside us!
JR: True enough, and Diesel took the first blows so he's got the longest time to recover, Dean Douglas rolling over, hooks a half-nelson on the
British Bulldog but there's no power in it!
JV: But the Bulldog's not fighting it! JR: Things picked up so fast when the Hitman made it down to ringside, now
Diesel the first back to his feet, reaches down to grab the UNDERTAKER BUT
THE UNDERTAKER GETS HIS HAND AROUND BIG DADDY COOL'S THROAT!
JV: NOT AGAIN! JR: I DON'T THINK ANYBODY CAN KEEP THIS MAN DOWN FOR LONG! THE UNDERTAKER COMES TO HIS FEET, PICKS DIESEL UP AND TURNS HIM AROUND ... JV: OH GOD! JR: ... TOMBSTONE, AND DIESEL IS FIRMLY PLANTED! JV: JEEZ ... WILL IT EVER END? (5!) JR: WELL, (4!) MAYBE THE NEXT (3!) PERSON DOWN (2!) CAN DO IT! (1!) ::BUZZ:: JR: AND THAT PERSON IS ... JV: IT'S ... THIS IS STING! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJORK RULES! - Support MST3k on Sci-Fi! - Go Tigers! (I know ...)
