SSDRR: 54:00 - #30 ENTERS!

29 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Diesel, British Bulldog, Bret Hart, Sting, 1-2-3
Kid, Terry Gordy, Scott Norton
Entering: Barry Horowitz JIM ROSS: HERE COMES THE ONE, THE ONLY ... JESSE VENTURA: THANK GOD! JR: MISTER UPSET HIMSELF, BARRY HOROWITZ! JV: MISTER LUCK! JR: THIS CROWD WELCOMING THE MAN WHO JUST LAST YEAR BODYSLAMMED ANDRE THE
GIANT, AND SURPRISED EVERYONE WITH HIS TOP-TEN FINISH! Alright, back in the ring now Diesel with an over-the-knee backbreaker on the British
Bulldog!
JV: Meanwhile Sting elbowing out of Scott Norton's headlock, comes off the far side and NAILS a nice flying forearm!
JR: And Bret Hart still with that Fujiwara arm bar on Terry Gordy, but
Jesse, we have to ask ourselves now, who is going to be number thirty?
JV: I think it's gonna be one of the great extreme men of the sport -
Sabu, Ray Misterio Junior, Chris Benoit, Dean Malenko ...
JR: I don't think so, Jess, I think it'll be one of the more legendary figures in the sport, Randy Savage, or maybe the Ultimate Warrior ...
JV: ROSS, SINCE WHEN HAS WWF SUCCESS BEEN THE SOLE MEASURING STICK IN DETERMINING GREATNESS?!? ( this space added to further Eric Bischoff's shame ) JR: Well, we shall see as Barry Horowitz enters the ring, quickly going over to the prone 1-2-3 Kid and cinching in a nice half-nelson!
JV: Now Sting, he's signaling for the Scorpion Death Lock on Scott "Flash" Norton! JR: I don't think he can get those big, muscular legs around, AND NORTON
PUSHES THE STINGER OFF BUT STING RIGHT BACK UP AND LEVELS NORTON WITH A
RIGHT HOOK! JV: NICE PUNCH THERE! And from a man who's not known for getting down and dirty like this!
JR: Sting now going over to Big Daddy Cool, AND A KICK TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD JARS THE BIG MAN! JV: HE'S GOTTA NEGATE THAT HEIGHT ADVANTAGE! JR: AND WITH A KICK TO THE BACK OF THE RIGHT KNEE, HE DOES JUST THAT, JESSE! JV: I TOLD YOU THIS GUY WAS SMARTER THAN I THOUGHT ... wait a minute, that doesn't sound right ...
JR: Anyway, Barry Horowitz now turning the half-nelson into a Japanese arm bar, falls forward, then back into a Fujiwara arm bar of his own!
JV: That arm bar is quite popular tonight, who knows, maybe Pittman will be number thirty! JR: Well, the Hitman lets his go, Gordy grabbing that left shoulder and the Hitman STOMPS THE STOMACH OF THE 1-2-3 KID, THEN GOES OVER AND DROPS
THE ELBOW ON THE BRITISH BULLDOG! JV: BRET'S CUTTING A PATH OF DESTRUCTION! JR: Scott Norton, though, to his feet and he jumps on the Bulldog now, with a blatant choke!
JV: Now the Hitman back up, Sting with a double-arm cross-face on Diesel BUT BRET KICKS STING'S HANDS LOOSE! JR: AND THAT GOT DIESEL AS WELL, LOOKS LIKE SOME OF STING'S FINGERS GOT BIG DADDY COOL'S EYES! JV: BRET GRABBING THE STINGER BY THE HEAD, SETTING UP SOMETHING HERE, LOOKS LIKE A SPIKED PILEDRIVER ... AND ... HE NAILS IT! JR: MY GOD, JESSE, BRET'S TAKING IT TO EVERYBODY IN THERE! JV: IT WOULDN'T SURPRISE ME IF THE HITMAN WINS THIS THING! JR: Back on the other side of the ring, though, the 1-2-3 Kid trying to mount some kind of comeback, he's been laid out for awhile but now he's getting up, Horowitz loosing the Fujiwara into a standard arm bar!
JV: Mistake by Horriblewitz there! JR: The Kid trying to get that momentum and he LANDS the elbow to the gut of Horowitz! AND another! Horowitz turns it into a SLEEPER BUT THE KID
BACKS UP AND SANDWICHES HOROWITZ IN THE CORNER! JV: Now the Kid turning around, laying in those kicks to the short ribs
... JR: ... up to the second ROPE, AND A KICK TO THE FACE! JV: Terry Gordy's getting up now as well! JR: The Kid whips Horowitz out of the ROPES, HOROWITZ WITH A HANDSPRING, AND A JUMPWHEEL KICK TO TERRY GORDY! JV: THAT GOT HIM GOOD! JR: GORDY STUMBLING BACK ... AND OVER THE TOP TO THE OUTSIDE! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: TERRY GORDY HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: I DON'T BELIEVE IT, ROSS! JR: BARRY HOROWITZ ELIMINATING TERRY GORDY, AND WE'RE DOWN TO SEVEN IN THE RING! JV: LESS THAN TWENTY SECONDS TO GO! JR: WE'LL GO BACK UP TO EIGHT THEN, AS HOROWITZ CLEANS THE KID'S CLOCK
WITH AN UPPERCUT, AND THIS CROWD STARTING THE "BARRY" CHANT ONCE AGAIN!
JV: OH GOD, HOROWITZ COULD WIN THIS THING! JR: WELL, YOU DON'T KNOW WHO NUMBER THIRTY IS! JV: KNOWING MY LUCK, ROSS, IT CAN'T BE GOOD! (10!) JR: HERE WE GO! (9! 8!) JV: WHO'S IT (7!) GOING TO BE? (6! 5!) JR: LET'S FIND (4!) OUT! (3! 2! 1!) :: BUZZ :: JR: IT'S ... IT'S ... IT'S ... NO! OH GOD, NO! JV: HA HA HA! WHOOOOOOOOOO! IT'S THA NAY-CHA' BOY, RIC FLAIR!
