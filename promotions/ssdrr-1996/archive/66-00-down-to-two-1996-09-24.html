SSDRR: 66:00 - DOWN TO TWO!

24 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the ring: Bret Hart, Barry Horowitz, Ric Flair JIM ROSS: Alright, Barry Horowitz still with his arms around the head of the Nature Boy, trying to squeeze the fight out of him ...
JESSE VENTURA: Meanwhile, Bret Hart's coming to in the corner! JR: Bret trying to shake those cobwebs out of his head, blinking his eyes repeatedly, but I think he's not going to come out of that corner until he's ready!
JV: He'd better not, Horowitz really able to take charge over this past minute or so, he's sucking wind but I think he can leave Flair alone for a while!
JR: Bret turns around to face the ring, Barry hasn't seen him yet but it should only be a matter of time here!
JV: Hey, Horowitz may not realize it until it's too late! Never discount the stupidity of Barry Horowitz, Ross!
JR: ... Well, that's one way of putting it ... anyway, Horowitz sees the
Hitman's ready and releases the headlock on Flair, goes over to meet Bret in the CORNER BUT BRET GETS THE LEG UP INTO THE ABDOMEN!
JV: HE KICKED HIM GOOD, ROSS! JR: Bret, barely able to stand on his feet, still with the courage to follow after Horowitz, hooks him up, RUSSIAN LEG SWEEP!
JV: AND ALL THREE MEN ARE DOWN AGAIN! JR: BUT BRET NOW, MUSTERING THAT LEGENDARY RESILIENCY, ABLE TO ONCE MORE
GET TO HIS FEET, PICKS UP THE LEGS OF HOROWITZ, THAT CAN ONLY MEAN ONE
THING! JV: HE'S GONNA CUT THE BOTTOM OUT OF HOROWITZ'S HOPES! JR: NO, HE DROPS THE ELBOW on the leg of Horowitz, but quickly back up, grabs the leg a-GAIN AND THIS TIME HE'S GOING FOR IT!
JV: HE'S REAL CLOSE TO THE ROPES, BUT THAT DOESN'T MATTER TONIGHT! JR: AND HE CINCHES IN HIS PATENTED SHARPSHOOTER! JV: AND HE'S LEANING WAY BACK, TOO, THIS COULD BE IT! JR: THIS CROWD ONCE AGAIN TO ITS FEET AS THE HITMAN, ONCE AGAIN ABLE TO
KICK INTO THAT RESERVE TANK, NOW SEEMS TO HAVE THE MATTER AT HAND!
JV: Well, nobody seems to hold onto it for that long, Ross! JR: And as we look, literally from behind the shoulder of the Hitman here, great camera work by our crew, Ric Flair rolling onto his back in the center of the ring, he's getting the chance to get his wits back about him!
JV: Flair sits up, he's got his arms over his head, trying to suck in as much oxygen as he can!
JR: Now you have to wonder, at this point when will Bret release the
Sharpshooter he has right now on Barry Horowitz?
JV: If I were the Hitman, Ross, I would wait until the last possible moment! Of course, when I put my legendary backbreaker on, I could still kick somebody else with my feet!
JR: That you could, and often did, and usually it was your own manager,
Classy Freddie Blassie!
JV: Hey, are you implying something, Ross? Do you know how much money he screwed me out of?
JR: No more than his fair share, I believe ... JV: Just ... don't go there, Ross! JR: Flair now able to get to one knee, now slowly raising off that knee, getting back to his feet, as Horowitz grabbing frantically at the ropes!
JV: Hey, kid, it ain't gonna do you any good! JR: Well, maybe by grabbing those ropes he can get the leverage he needs to push Bret off! JV: Yeah, but as he's grabbing those ropes you notice how he's increasing the arc on his back, and that's just plain stupid!
JR: Maybe not, if he can get out, maybe the small amount of additional pain will be worth it!
JV: I don't know, Ross, nobody cinches in that hold better than the Hart brothers? JR: I think that's exactly what Horowitz is trying to do here ... HE'S
SCREAMING NOW, TRYING TO SHAKE THE HITMAN OFF ... AND THE HITMAN GETS
PUSHED OFF, STRAIGHT INTO FLAIR who grabs the back of HIS TRUNKS AND
THROWS HIM OUT! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: BRET "HITMAN" HART HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: HERE WE GO! JR: THE HITMAN FANS ABOUT RINGSIDE ARE DEJECTED, AND RIGHTFULLY SO, BUT WE
ARE DOWN TO THE FINAL TWO, BARRY HOROWITZ AND THE NATURE BOY, RIC FLAIR!
JV: ANYBODY WHO DOESN'T BELIEVE IN THE LUCK OF THE DRAW JUST GOT PROVEN WRONG, THE LAST TWO TO ENTER ARE THE LAST TWO IN THE RING! JR: THERE IS NOT A SINGLE PERSON SITTING DOWN IN THE UNIVERSITY OF MICHIGAN STADIUM, AS RIC FLAIR NOW TURNS TO HOROWITZ, WHO IS WRITHING IN PAIN IN THE CORNER! JV: I don't even think he knows that the Hitman's been thrown out! JR: Oh, I think he knows, Jesse, that crowd reaction should have told him something just happened, as now Flair methodically walking over as Barry turns onto his back ...
JV: My money's on the Nature Boy, Ross, and don't try telling me you think
Horriblewitz can actually win this thing!
JR: I think he can, Jesse, but if I were a betting man, my money would be on Flair! Flair now in the corner, bending down to pick up Horowitz by his hair, sets him up in the corner and ::SMACK!:: WHAT A KNIFE-EDGE CHOP!
::SMACK!:: BUT HOROWITZ RETALIATES! JV: THEY'RE TRADING BLOWS! JR: THIS HAS BROKEN OUT INTO A DONNYBROOKE, AND NEITHER MAN ABLE TO GET
MUCH BEHIND HIS PUNCHES, BUT THEY'RE GIVING IT THEIR ALL! WHAT INTESTINAL
FORTITUDE ON THE PART OF BOTH MEN! JV: SOMETHING'S GOT TO GIVE, ROSS! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJORK RULES! - Go Tigers! (I know ...)
