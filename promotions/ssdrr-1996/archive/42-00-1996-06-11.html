SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 42:00

11 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Undertaker, Bob Backlund, Owen Hart, Roddy Piper, Dean Douglas, Madusa, Diesel Entering: British Bulldog JIM ROSS: HERE COMES TROUBLE! JESSE VENTURA: YOU SAID IT! JR: The British Bulldog walking down to the ringside area, he's not in any hurry to get in there now!
JV: Why should he be? Let everyone tire themselves out for awhile, then jump in when someone's not expecting it and clobber 'em! It worked for
Big Daddy Cool! JR: Speaking of Diesel, he's been whipping the Undertaker's left leg into the ringpost for quite some time now! Diesel finally being told by the referees to re-enter the ring, Diesel backs off, NO! HE'S GOT EUGENE
HEPNER BY THE HEAD, DON'T TELL ME! JV: HERE WE GO! JR: DIESEL .. JACKKNIFES HIM! COME ON, JESSE, THERE WAS NO CALL FOR THAT! JV: HEY, YOU MESS WITH BIG DADDY COOL YOU PAY THE PRICE! JR: And NOW Diesel slides back in, and he just pointed at another of the referees and told him he was next! Who is gonna stop this man?
JV: Doesn't look like it's gonna be the Undertaker anytime soon! JR: The Undertaker clutching his leg, he's in as much trouble as I've ever seen him but in the meantime Roddy Piper has cinched in a camel clutch on
Madusa! JV: And he's got it cinched in perfectly, too! He's been working on her back for well over two minutes now, and that'll take its toll 'cause when your back is all bent like that for so long it's hard to suck that oxygen into your lungs!
JR: The British Bulldog now sliding into the ring, who's he gonna go after first ... he looks around ... goes over to Bob Backlund and starts stomping his stomach!
JV: Backlund still recovering from the powerbomb of Diesel, he's not gonna put up much of a defense!
JR: Dean Douglas, on the other side of the ring, starts rubbing the face of Owen Hart into the mat but here comes the Bulldog over, grabs Douglas by the head, pulls him up and INTO A FIREMAN'S CARRY AND AN AIRPLANE SPIN!
JV: OWEN'S GOTTA BE GRATEFUL FOR THAT! JR: THE BRITISH BULLDOG HAS GOT DOUGLAS SPINNING AT AN INCREDIBLE VELOCITY, FINALLY HE SETS HIM DOWN and both fall to the mat! JV: That's why I never did that move, Ross, as much as it dizzies your opponent it usually gets you even harder!
JR: Owen Hart getting a short break here, twrirling those arms around trying to get the circulation back as ... hold on, I just got a note up here from one of the assistants ... oh geez, Jesse, have I got some good news for you!
JV: I'll believe that when I hear it ... JR: You know how you've been whining about how you can't participate in these tournaments? JV: Yeah? JR: Well, [name withheld] has just delivered this press release, you are eligible next year!
JV: WHAT?!? (sound of paper rustling) JR: Next year's production will take place over multiple nights, and you do have broadcasting obligations on some nights, but any night you are not broadcasting if you want to don the trunks you can!
JV: FINALLY! NOW I'll finally have an opportunity to teach these punks a few lessons about wrestling!
JR: Back to the ring now, Diesel STILL continuing to work over the left leg of the Undertaker, has it draped over the bottom rope and REPEATEDLY slamming his full weight on the knee!
JV: Wonder if the Undertaker's knee will be healed by next year? JR: ... Jess, don't start that now. Wait 'til next year, for now you've got to come at this as a broadcaster and not a wrestler!
JV: Roddy Piper just left Madusa lying on the ground, and she's not moving! He really got her good!
JR: Now he's headed over to Owen Hart, as Bob Backlund struggling to get a leg under him as is the British Bulldog, Piper now with a back waistlock on the King of Harts, Owen trying a standing switch here but Piper has it sunk on too tight!
JV: The Bulldog's still dizzy! JR: Indeed he is, but he's on his feet, goes over to Backlund, MISSES the left hook as Backlund ducks, Backlund GRABS THE BULLDOG BY THE HEAD, RUNS
TO THE ROPES BUT BACKLUND IS THE ONE WHO GOES OVER! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: BOB BACKLUND HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: BRITISH BULLDOG PUT ON THE BRAKES THERE! JR: AND BACKLUND ALMOST WENT INTO THE SEATS! Bulldog now, shaking those cobwebs loose trying to get his equilibrium back, goes over to the still-prone Dean Douglas and cinches in a Fujiwara arm bar!
JV: If his equilibrium isn't back yet, this'll give him the opportunity! JR: Diesel now, he's trying a figure-four, he's got the Undertaker's legs hooked ... JV: Yeah, but he didn't do the spinning toe-hold first so this move isn't going to be very effective, I mean, no offense to Big Daddy Cool but what butt-munch puts on a figure-four leglock without a spinning toe-hold?
JR: Indeed ... Madusa has still not moved! JV: I don't think she's going to, Ross, Piper got her good! JR: Crowd kind of quiet now, we're getting close to another entrant though! (5!) JV: Who's (4!) gonna be (3!) next? (2!) (1!) :: BUZZ :: (As number 24 comes through the curtain, the crowd pops so loudly Ross and Ventura become incoherent; the next entrant is Bret Hart.)
