SSDRR: 36:00 REPOST

17 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I SWEAR, I sent 36:00 out Wednesday, but it hasn't shown up on AOL so I'm assuming it never got out, so I've got to repost it which means (guess what?) we'll have another week delay in the next round.
Currently in the ring: Goldust, Undertaker, Bob Backlund, Owen Hart,
Toshiake Kawada, Roddy Piper
Entering: Dean Douglas JIM ROSS: The Dean of wrestling making his way down to the ring ... JESSE VENTURA: As if it matters, it's over now. JR: Jesse, why do you say that? We've still got ten more entrants to go! JV: Ross, no matter how hard I hope it's not going to happen that miserable Undertaker just keeps pulling it out time and time again, he's just gonna win again tonight so why in the hell should we bother? God, I haven't been this nauseous since I went to Pastamania ...
JR: Well, Jesse, would you just TRY to get through the rest of this? Come on, think about who we HAVEN'T seen yet and figure that ten of them are going to get in here, fresh as daisies and eventually the law of averages says the Undertaker is going to get thrown out!
JV: I don't know, Ross, it just feels like the law of averages is working against ME now that I'm retired ...
JR: Let's just get back to the match, as Dean Douglas comes in and IMMEDIATELY rips Owen Hart off of Bob Backlund and cinches in a side headlock, Owen pushes him to the ropes, OWEN MISSES THE KILLER KICK
AS DOUGLAS HOLDS ON TO THE ROPES! JV: Douglas the thinking man's wrestler, but that won't work against the
Undertaker ... JR: Jesse ... look, just cut it out, okay! JV: Look at Toshiake Kawada, he hasn't got up since Hart had the sharpshooter on him ... JR: Indeed, but here comes the Undertaker over ... JV: See? JR: ... he PICKS HIM UP, AND THIS CROWD STARTS UP AGAIN AS ... JV: OH GOD ... JR: ... TOMBSTONE! JV: OH ... hold on, what's this? JR: Producer's turned the camera into the locker room as Big Van Vader is demolishing everything in sight! He's ripped all the lockers out and he's tearing the locker doors like sheets of paper!
JV: That'll cost him as ... what's Paul Orndorff doing? PAUL ORNDORFF: COME ON NOW, VADER, JUST CALM DOWN ... BIG VAN VADER: WHAT YOU SAY, BOY?!? JR: Orndorff in his street clothes, looks like he didn't make it into the thirty as VADER ALMOST DECAPITATES ORNDORFF WITH A
CLOTHESLINE! JV: YEAH! COME ON, EVEN IF YOU DIDN'T WIN TONIGHT YOU CAN STILL KICK SOME BUTT! JR: BIG VAN VADER, NO! NO! JV: BAM! JR: HE POWERBOMBED HIM ONTO THE TILE FLOOR, HE COULD HAVE KILLED HIM! JV: DO IT AGAIN! JR: NO, and thankfully we return to the ring here as Roddy Piper now just mauling Goldust with right fists!
JV: Oh man ... that cheered me up! JR: Well, then maybe we can return to the action here as Dean Douglas now
JUMPING on the Undertaker, getting a side headlock in and repeatedly punching the Undertaker on the forehead!
JV: And the Undertaker's down to one knee! JR: Dean Douglas is cutting the Undertaker down here, to both knees, and the Undertaker goes down!
JV: YEAH! KEEP GOING, DEAN, PUNCH A NEW MOUTH IN HIM! JR: Owen Hart getting up, BELLY-TO-BELLY ON TOSHIAKE KAWADA! JV: Douglas now, switching to dropping the knee on the head of the Undertaker, he really took the Undertaker to school there! JR: The Undertaker's endurance may be faltering a bit here! Now if he can just capitalize on the opportunity, but he just leaves him on the mat!
JV: Not a smart decision by the Dean here, as he goes back to Toshiake
Kawada, rips Owen Hart off of him and Owen runs straight into
GOLDUST WHO GOOSES HIM! JR: But Dean Douglas picking Kawada UP AND THROWING HIM OVER THE TOP ALL THE WAY TO THE OUTSIDE! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: TOSHIAKE KAWADA HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: HE SAW THE OPPORTUNITY TO CAPITALIZE ON KAWADA, BUT OWEN HART NOW TRYING TO THROW GOLDUST OVER, HE DOES IT! FINK: GOLDUST HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: INCREDIBLE! TWO QUICK ELIMINATIONS, AND WE'RE DOWN TO FIVE MEN IN THE RING! WE HAVEN'T BEEN THIS LOW SINCE VADER ENTERED! JR: THE RING STARTING TO THIN OUT HERE, as now Owen and the Dean meet again in a collar-and-elbow hookup in the center of the ring, Dean with a go-behind but it's reversed by Owen, reversed again by the Dean who picks the King of Harts up into a takedown into a hammerlock, Owen slips out and gets on top of Douglas with an arm bar!
JV: Incredible scientific sequence there, especially for a Royal Rumble! JR: Both men in their environment just there, Owen temporarily with the advantage as RODDY PIPER PULLS BOB BACKLUND'S
SHORTS DOWN! JV: NOW HOLD ON A SECOND! JR: THIS CROWD IS ON ITS FEET AS BOB BACKLUND HOPPING AROUND TRYING TO GET HIS BALANCE, BUT PIPER FLOORS HIM WITH AN UPPERCUT! JV: THAT IS UNFAIR, ROSS, EVEN FOR A ROYAL RUMBLE THAT IS JUST TOTALLY AND COMPLETELY UNFAIR! JR: WELL, NO NEED TO ASK THE BOXERS OR BRIEFS QUESTION, IT'S QUITE OBVIOUSLY NEITHER! JV: OH, NOW THAT'S IT, ROSS, YOU JUST LOST THE RIGHT TO QUESTION ANY OF MY JOKES FOR THE REST OF THE NIGHT WITH THAT ONE! JR: Sorry, sorry ... yeah, that was bad. (giggles) JV: That was tasteless, Ross! JR: Dean Douglas now getting to a standing position, Owen Hart's arm bar not having much effect now (5!) as Owen (4!) whips the Dean
(3!) to the ropes (2!) comes off and (1!) ducks an ... ::BUZZ:: JR: ... attempted clothesline, the Dean WITH A HI-CROSS BODY, WHO'S THE NEXT MAN? JV: IT'S ... not a man, but she's got a face like one, it's Madusa! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJÖRK RULES! - Support MST3k on Sci-Fi! - Go Red Wings!
