Before I start the ballot, I wanted to say a word about last round: the Undertaker eliminated Vader fair and square. The fact of the matter is, every other wrestler with the exception of Goldust had a higher statistical chance of getting the elimination, but by luck of the draw the Undertaker happened to do it. Now, it's no secret I'm a big UT fan, but recently I have begun to like Vader a lot as well. Trust me, you can e-mail the Jammer because he knows the entrance order as well, the Undertaker is NOT going to win this thing, not with the high- powered names coming up in the next few minutes.
