SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 56:00

5 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Diesel, British Bulldog, Bret Hart, Sting, 1-2-3
Kid, Scott Norton, Barry Horowitz
Entering: Ric Flair JIM ROSS: RIC FLAIR IS THE LAST PARTICIPANT! JESSE VENTURA: IT'S OVER, ROSS! JUST RING THE BELL NOW, SAVE EVERYONE A GOOD ASS-KICKIN' BY THE NATURE BOY! JR: Flair taking his time walking down the aisle, my god, Jesse, this is just ... unbelievable.
JV: Neither of us even thought about him, and now, here he comes, and he's got his lucky gold robe on, too!
JR: Well ... back to the ring, you can see Diesel still in the corner, collecting his wits, meanwhile Bret Hart with a camel clutch on the
Stinger, and the Hitman looking straight into the eyes of the Nature Boy! JV: He's letting Ric know that he sees him, and he's showing the Nature
Boy what he's got in store for him!
JR: Well, if anyone can give Ric Flair a good challenge it has to be the
Hitman, perhaps one of the few men in the history of our sport who can
out-strategize the Nature Boy! JV: Yeah, but Bret's been in there over ten minutes now, Ric's coming in fresh, if I were the Nature Boy I'd be looking to single out Bret early, try to get him over that top rope as early as possible!
JR: Hold on here, the British Bulldog setting Scott Norton up for that running powerslam ... AND HE NAILS IT!
JV: AND THE RING'S STILL HOLDING! JR: Not like last year, that's for sure, Barry HOROWITZ IN THE MEANTIME with a nice butterfly suplex on the 1-2-3 Kid!
JV: The Kid's performance this year has been bordering on the abyssmal, especially in consideration of his top-ten finish last year!
JR: Okay, Ric Flair comes in, SPITS IN THE FACE OF BRET HART BUT WALKS
RIGHT PAST HIM, OVER to Big Daddy Cool, Diesel ...
JV: And Bret still hasn't released Sting! JR: The Hitman staying focused while Ric FLAIR clips the leg of Diesel again, AND STARTS KICKING AWAY AT THE KNEE WITHOUT MERCY!
JV: I DON'T EVEN HAVE TO SAY IT! JR: WE ALL KNOW WHAT HE'S SETTING UP FOR, as the British Bulldog now slapping the face of Scott "Flash" Norton, punishing him for his earlier abuse!
JV: Flair picking Diesel up! JR: Setting up for that kneebreaker he does so well, picks him UP AND THROWS HIM OVER HIS BACK TO THE OUTSIDE! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: DIESEL HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: DIESEL WAS A THREAT TO THE NATURE BOY, AND HE RECOGNIZED IT! JR: FLAIR TAKING ONE OF THE BIG MEN OUT EARLY, BUT BARRY HOROWITZ JUST THREW THE 1-2-3 KID OVER! FINK: THE 1-2-3 KID HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: SIX LEFT! JR: THE KID TRYING TO TOSS BARRY OVER, BUT BARRY AT THE LAST SECOND, HERE ON THE INSTANT REPLAY, HIPLOCKS THE KID OVER INSTEAD! JV: AND RIC FLAIR DROPS THE LEG ON THE BRITISH BULLDOG! JR: OUCH! THE BULLDOG focusing too hard on Norton didn't see that leg drop coming, and Flair got him good!
JV: You see here, Ross, this is EXACTLY what made the Nature Boy one of the greatest ring tacticians in our sport today ...
JR: AS HE BLATANTLY GOUGES THE BULLDOG IN THE EYE! JV: He went straight to the big men, knowing what kind of a threat they can conceivably pose, and took one of them out early, now grabbing the
Bulldog BY THE HEAD AND THROWING HIM OUT AS WELL!
FINK: THE BRITISH BULLDOG HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: AND THAT TAKES IT DOWN TO FIVE! NOW BARRY HOROWITZ GOING OVER and *talking* to Bret Hart ... ? JV: And Hart releases the Stinger, Horriblewitz cinching a front-face lock on Sting now ...
JR: SO BRET CAN GO TO WORK ON THE NATURE BOY! RIC FLAIR WITH A DOUBLE-BICEP HAS HIS BACK TURNED ON THE EXCELLENCE OF EXECUTION, BRET WAITING ... BRET HART: WHOOOOOOOO! JR: ... FLAIR TURNS AROUND AND STUMBLES SO FAR BACK HE ALMOST ELIMINATES
HIMSELF! FLAIR OFF THE ROPES, MISSES THE RIGHT HOOK AS BRET *NAILS* THE
UPPERCUT, AND FLAIR HITS THE CANVAS LIKE A TON OF BRICKS! JV: I CAN'T BELIEVE FLAIR COULD LET THIS HAPPEN! JR: THE HITMAN PICKING FLAIR UP, GOING FOR A SIDEWALK SLAM HERE ... AND HE HITS IT! JV: BUT FLAIR QUICKLY UP AND DROPS THE KNEE ON THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF THE HITMAN! JR: FLAIR WAS PLAYING POSSUM! BRET GRABBING HIS HEAD NOW, THE Nature Boy whipping Bret into a corner now, hard, Flair over and (**SMACK!**)
THERE'S THAT KNIFE-EDGE CHOP, BUT BRET IS NOT FAZED! JV: AND BRET WITH A BLATANT THUMB TO THE EYE NOW! JR: TURNABOUT IS FAIR PLAY, AS BRET WHIPS THE NATURE BOY OUT, FLAIR WITH A
FLAIR-FLIP TO THE APRON BUT QUICKLY he goes back through the ropes into the ring, AND FALLS FLAT ON HIS FACE!
JV: NO! JR: BRET HART HAS CLEANED THE NATURE BOY'S CLOCK! JV: BUT HE'S NOT OUT YET, ROSS! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJORK RULES! - Go Tigers! (I know ...)
