Undertaker, Bastion Booger, Bob Backlund
Entering: Owen Hart JIM ROSS: Tell me, Jesse, why do you l,ike this guy so much? JESSE VENTURA: Are you kidding, Ross? This young man is the only decent
Hart son around! JR: Son? JV: Helen has good taste in men, so that redeems her, but the rest of those pug-ugly wimps Stu and Helen ...
JR: May I remind you, dear Jesse, that they are in attendance here tonight, and Bret probably is too.
JV: I'll worry about that when he comes up here ... take him twenty minutes to climb up the stairs with his walker anyway ...
JR: ... Jesse, I can't believe you sometimes! Anyway, Owen now in the ring, going straight to work on Bastion Booger, trying to get HIM over the top rope ...
JV: He should have disposable gloves for that! JR: Not gonna argue with you on that one as Big Van Vader absolutely PUMMELING the Undertaker, laying him flat out in the middle of the ring next to Bob Backlund on his way up ...
JV: And the Heartbreak Kid working on that abdominal stretch on Goldust as ... what is this crowd chanting?
JR: It's hard to make it out, something like "forty stalls" or something ... JV: FOR THE BALLS! THEY'RE TELLING SHAWN MICHAELS TO GO FOR GOLDUST'S BALLS! JR: WHAT? MY GOD, YOU'RE RIGHT! JV: THIS CROWD IS SICK AND TASTELESS, DO THEY HAVE ANY IDEA ... JR: WELL, WHY NOT? WHY NOT INDEED AS SHAWN, OH YES! JV: OTHER SIDE OF THE RING, ROSS! JR: WHAT, NOT ... NO, NOT A VADERSAULT! JV: HA HA, IT'S LIGHTS OUT FOR THE UNDERTAKER! JR: NO, I THINK BOB BACKLUND'S GONNA KNOCK BIG VAN VADER OFF ... NO! JV: HE _NAILED_ IT! JR: OVER THE HEAD OF BOB BACKLUND AND RIGHT ON TARGET, AND THAT HAS TO HAVE SUCKED THE WIND RIGHT OUT OF THE UNDERTAKER! JV: And Goldust with a hip toss gets out of the abdominal stretch, but Shawn's hand took its toll on the private parts of Goldust ... JR: Owen's close to having Bastion Booger over now, he's got him draped over that top rope but Booger is out of it!
JV: We need a cleaning crew out there, Ross. JR: You're right as ... OH THIS IS GREAT! BIG VAN VADER JUST POINTED OVER AT GOLDUST! JV: OH NO! STAY FOCUSED, YOU TWO! JR: THOSE TWO ARE GONNA HAVE A GO AS BOB BACKLUND NOW HOOKING IT up with
Shawn Michaels, AND A HARD LEFT BY GOLDUST HAS NO EFFECT! ANOTHER,
NOTHING DOING! JV: GOLDUST, YOU DON'T KNOW WHAT YOU'RE UGGH! JR: AN OVERHEAD HAMMER AND GOLDUST IS AS GOOD AS A GREASE SPOT! JV: OWEN'S GETTING BOOGER OVER! JR: BOOGER'S OFF BOTH FEET NOW AS OWEN, TRYING TO LEAN ON THE BASTION-MAN
BUT NOT FALL OVER HIMSELF AS HE SLIDES IN UNDER BOOGER'S RUMP, TRYING TO
PUSH HIM ... YES! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: BASTION BOOGER HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: IT TOOK A LOT OF DOING, BUT OWEN GOT BASTION BOOGER OVER AS HAKSUHI
COMES OVER BUT OWEN WITH A BACK BODY DROP ON HAKUSHI AND HE IS OUT!
JV: WOW! FINK: HAKUSHI HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: THAT HAD TO BE LESS THAN TEN SECONDS ROSS! JR: IT WAS MORE AROUND SEVEN, as our statistician getting me the end time of Hakushi ... twenty-nine minutes and twenty-seven seconds, oh so close to the thirty minute mark!
JV: And Shawn's over that mark now! JR: That's the one thing about Battleroyals and other events like that, they bring out the best in EVERYONE as we are back down to six in the ring!
JV: We've got Shawn Michaels and Bob Backlund tyring to mix it up scientific-style, Big Van Vader pummeling Goldust in the centre of the ring, the Undertaker STILL flat on his back and now what's Owen going to do ...
JR: He's going right after Vader! JV: When you're hot you're hot! Not a smart move, but if now isn't a good time, when is? JR: The Undertaker is still down as Owen Hart getting behind Vader and delivering a succesion of fists to the side of the head, but it seems to be annoying Vader more than anything else!
JV: Owen going for the sleeper, we can see this one coming ...
