SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 48:00

8 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Owen Hart, Dean Douglas, Diesel, British Bulldog,
Bret Hart, Sting Entering: 1-2-3 Kid JIM ROSS: DOWN COMES THE 1-2-3 KID, AND HE LOOKS CONTENT WITH HIMSELF! JESSE VENTURA: HE GOT SLOT 26, HE'S GOT GOOD REASON! JR: I WOULD CONCUR as the action still slow in the ring, three submission holds being utilized but the British Bulldog can see the Kid coming down the aisle, Dean Douglas looks passed out in that side headlock!
JV: He'd better go straight after the Kid, you give him time to operate and he'll knock everybody down!
JR: And indeed as the 1-2-3 Kid about to make his way to the ring the
Bulldog lets the Dean GO AND SAILS THROUGH THE ROPES AND HITS THE KID WITH
A HI-CROSS BODY! JV: I TOLD YOU, ROSS! JR: THE BRITISH BULLDOG, FOR ALL HIS BRAWN STILL HAS A GOOD DEAL OF BRAINS, AND HE TOOK THE KID OUT RIGHT AWAY, BUT BULLDOG MAY HAVE HURT HIMSELF WITH THAT ONE! JV: That's the kind of risk you have to take, Ross, as Bret Hart releasing his brother from that Indian Death Lock, going over to Sting and HE STARTS
PUMMELING AWAY! JR: BRET HART TAKING IT TO THE STINGER HERE, BUT OWEN HART IS TO HIS FEET
ALREADY AS STING RELEASES THE SCORPION DEATH LOCK ON BIG DADDY COOL TO
CONCENTRATE ON THE HITMAN! JV: Owen comes over and goes back to work on his older brother! JR: I had a feeling those two would keep at it as long as one of them was in the ring, as we have a donnybrooke erupting, STING WITH TWO EYE RAKES
ON THE BROTHERS HART, TRIES A DOUBLE NOGGIN-KNOCKER BUT THE HARTS BLOCK,
AND COUNTER WITH DUAL ELBOWS TO THE GUT OF THE STINGER! JV: THEY'RE WORKING TOGETHER, I CAN'T BELIEVE IT! JR: Bret and Owen bouncing off the ropes, and ... DOUBLE-CLOTHESLINE, AND STING GOES DOWN! WOULD YOU BELIEVE IT? JV: And now they separate, Owen going to Sting and Bret sitting down on
Big Daddy Cool with that chinlock!
JR: Well, we said anything could happen, and for some odd reason, one night a year it usually DOES!
JV: And next year ... it's gonna happen over multiple nights, what's it gonna be? JR: I have yet to hear confirmation, let's go back to the outside, the
British Bulldog finally able to stand after that amazing dive through the ropes, and the Kid has yet to move!
JV: The Kid wasn't looking out, maybe got a little too cocky before he even stepped foot in the ring!
JR: The Bulldog picking the Kid up by the hair now, throwing him back to the inside, Bulldog slides back in but he's adjusting his neck!
JV: That's probably where the brunt of the damage took place! JR: Well, here's the somewhat-less-than-instant replay, and, yes, that's it, the Bulldog hit his head on the side of the guard rail as he came down, back to the ring, the Bulldog stands the kid up in the corner and starts with those chops to the chest!
JV: Sting's back up after tying the King of Harts up in the ropes, he's trying to get at the Hitman but Dean Douglas has a hold of the Stinger's ankle, not letting him get over!
JR: Stinger shaking the ankle, trying to get loose here but nothing doing, as Douglas TRIPS Sting, and the Bulldog really laying in to the Kid, BUT
THE KID THROWS THE BULLDOG INTO THE CORNER, AND STARTS KICKING AWAY AT HIS
CHEST, UP TO THE SECOND ROPE, SPINKICK TO THE HEAD! JV: THE KID WAS PLAYING POSSUM! JR: THE BULLDOG GROGGY NOW, AND THE KID WITH A SAMOAN BULLDOG OUT OF THE CORNER! JV: And now Dean Douglas splitting the legs of the Stinger, he's going for a Scorpion Death Lock here!
JR: He's trying but Sting has the sole of the foot of the Dean so he can't plant it down, and STING MONKEY-FLIPS DEAN DOUGLAS OVER AND HE HITS THE
ROPES, UNTANGLING OWEN HART IN THE PROCESS, OWEN QUICKLY WITH A RUNNING
LARIAT AND THE STINGER DOES A THREE-SIXTY IN MID-AIR! JV: DOUGLAS CAME DOWN HARD ON HIS HEAD, THOUGH! JR: As the 1-2-3 Kid now with an STF on the British Bulldog, let's go to the replay, Sting, one of the toughest masters of this hold, knew the counter, stopping Douglas from stepping through, then flipping him over,
Douglas hits the ropes upside-down, then comes down on his head!
JV: Douglas not having much luck over the past few minutes! JR: Owen Hart now with a DELIBERATE gouge of the eyes of Sting, meanwhile
Big Daddy Cool, Diesel, standing up with Bret Hart on his shoulders, backing up ...
JV: HE'S GONNA TRY TO THROW HIM OVER! JR: DIESEL WITH HIS BACK TO THE ROPES, ALL HE HAS TO DO IS FALL BACK AND
THE HITMAN IS HISTORY BUT BRET QUICKLY SPINS IT AROUND INTO A SPINNING
HEADSCISSOR TAKEDOWN, AND STARTS PUMMELING AWAY AT DIESEL'S HEAD!
JV: THAT WAS CLOSE, ROSS! We are definitely seeing a lot more hostility here tonight that we did last year!
JR: Indeed, as our camera now getting a look at the scoreboard here in Ann
Arbor, that 27 means that contestant 27 is coming down in about fifeteen seconds, as the clock shows, and the 10 means that we have ten more eliminations to go tonight!
JV: And we're coming down to the final four entrants! JR: Owen Hart with a side headlock on Sting now but the Stinger to his feet, PICKING OWEN UP, TRIES TO THROW HIM OVER THE TOP BUT OWEN (5!) LANDS
WITH HIS FEET (4!) ON THE ROPES AND (3!) EXECUTES A NICE (2!) SPINNING
BULLDOG! (1!) MAN!
:: BUZZ :: JV: JUST WHEN YOU THINK SOMEONE'S OVER ... JR: AND WHO'S NEXT TONIGHT? JV: IT'S TERRY GORDY, AND DON'T INTERRUPT ME, ROSS! [name withheld]
