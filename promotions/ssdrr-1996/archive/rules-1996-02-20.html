SSDRR: Rules

20 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I. NOMINATION The wrestlers who go into the Dream Royal Rumble are voted upon by the readers of rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy (rspwf). Each person is given
100 points with which to vote, and they cannot give less than 5 nor more than 20 points to a single wrestler, and they must use all 100 points.
After tallying these votes, the top thirty wrestlers, in terms of points, are in the Dream Royal Rumble. Should there be a tie for the thirtieth or earlier slots (e.g. wrestlers #27, 28, 29, 30 and 31 have the same votes), a random draw will determine which wrestlers are in and which are not.
Should the nomination round conclude with less than thirty wrestlers voted upon, wrestlers will be given the remaining slots in the order in which they finished last year's Dream Battleroyal (e.g. if 29 slots are filled and the Undertaker (who won the Dream Battleroyal) is not among those, he will get the thirtieth slot.) For purposes of tallying, any wrestler who is seeded in such a manner will be considered to have gotten one "point".
In addition, the following wrestlers will not be allowed into the Dream Royal Rumble: Hulk Hogan, Howard Finkel and Jesse Ventura. Hulk Hogan has been barred from my future Dream competitions because he unfairly tipped the vote in the Dream Battleroyal (besides which, I already killed him once, let's let the mouse rest in peace ;) ), and Howard Finkel and Jesse
Ventura have other functions at the Dream Royal Rumble (respectively, announcing and keeping Jim Ross in check.)
II. SEEDING Seeding in the Dream Royal Rumble is done by random draw (like the actual Royal Rumble), but is weighted by the number of points a wrestler gets in the nomination round. Try to imagine a large fish bowl, with a piece of paper for every point garnered in the nomination round (e.g. If Shawn
Michaels gets 300 points in the nomination round, he would have 300 slips of paper in the bowl). Out of this bowl, a piece of paper is drawn. The wrestler whose name is on that piece of paper will be seeded #30 in the draw (i.e. be the last one to go into the ring.) The draw after that will determine who gets seed #29, then #28, etc., until the entire order of entry is completed. Hence, by doing it this way, wrestlers who get a lot of points in the nomination round will be much more likely to draw a late seed. (p.s. just to clarify, I don't cut down a forest and make my bathtub unusable for several hours to do the seeding; I do an RND function on my old TRS-80.)
III. VOTING The first round of voting will be at 0 minutes and 0 seconds into the Dream Royal Rumble (when the bell rings.) Wrestlers enter the ring with all the points they garnered in the nomination round, multiplied by ten, and the points they get after that are added to their previous total (i.e.
a wrestler never loses points or gets "zeroed-out" after a round.) The voters of rspwf are each given 500 points to vote with, and they can give up to 100 points to any one wrestler (there is no minimum.) However, they are not required to use all 500 points (they couldn't unless there were at least five wrestlers in the ring). Unused points do NOT spill over into future rounds (e.g. you can't use just 400 points in one round and then use 600 in the next.) Voting is open for a round from the time I post the ballot to noon Eastern time the following Sunday. Late votes cannot be accepted. If someone votes with two ballots in the same round, only the first ballot will be counted.
IV. POSTING OF ROUNDS Voting for a round ends on noon on a Sunday. I will TRY to post the results (as commentary, with Jim Ross doing play-by-play, Jesse Ventura doing colour and Howard Finkel as announcer.) later that Sunday, but I may have to postpone posting until the next day if my schedule gets out of wack (not an uncommon occurance with me.) ANYTHING relating to the Dream
Royal Rumble that I am running can be identified easily because the subject will start with [SSDRR] ([name withheld] Dream Royal Rumble.) All postings will be to rspwf, and will not be mirrored to other newsgroups.
Posting of round results can be identified by the subject "[SSDRR] 00:00," where 00:00 is the time when the round starts. All ballots can be identified by the subject "[SSDRR] 02:00 ballot", where 02:00 is the time when that round (the next) will start. Just to clarify, the wrestler who will enter the next round will be identifiable both in the end of the round result and the following ballot. (Rounds will end with the buzzer going off and either Ross or Ventura announcing who just came out of the curtain, like, "IT'S BIG VAN VADER!")
V. ELIMINATION To survive a round a wrestler must have a certain number of points, directly related to how long they have been in. The formula for this is:
x=m+200+100*(r-1) In this formula, x is how many points the wrestler needs to survive the round, m is how many points the wrestler needed to survive the last round, and r is what "number" round this is for the wrestler. Just to calculate this out for the first few rounds:
Round 1: x=0+200+100*(1-1)=200 points Round 2: x=200+200+100*(2-1)=500 points Round 3: x=500+200+100*(3-1)=900 points Round 4: x=900+200+100*(4-1)=1400 points Round 5: x=1400+200+100*(5-1)=2000 points and so on ... If a wrestler fails to meet the point limit required to survive that round, s/he will be eliminated during that round. To determine when s/he is eliminated in terms of chronology, that will be proportional to the wrestler's "final" point total in relation to the point limit required for the previous round and the point total required for the current round.
(e.g. If Sting finishes his third round with 700 points, he will be eliminated exactly one minute into that round, for a total of five minutes in the Dream Royal Rumble, since 700 is exactly halfway between 500 and
900.) To determine who eliminates that wrestler, I will calculate for all the remaining wrestlers how far they are, percentagewise, over the point limit needed to survive that round. (e.g. let's say Shawn Michaels ends his first round with 1000 points. Since he needed 200 to survive the round, he is 400% over the point limit.) Those percentages will then be used in a weighted random drawing, much like the seeding earlier, to determine who eliminates a wrestler. Hence, wrestlers will be much more likely to do eliminations when they enter "on fire."
VI. MISCELLANY To help people who felt "left-out" of voting in the Dream Battleroyal over their winter and spring breaks, anytime I am supposed to be "on vacation"
from school, even if I have access to my computer I will not post additional rounds of the Dream Royal Rumble. When that happens, I will adjust the length of the voting period accordingly, so that it lasts all during my vacation. My Executive Assistant (and whatever other titles I want to bestow upon him for his hard work) for this Dream Tournament is the Jammer, who will be keeping a running file of all the rounds, which will be all pasted into one long document at the end, as well as posting this document on a regular basis to rspwf, and on request, and will be keeping a copy of the order of entry (which he won't give out to anybody.)
Please thank him accordingly. Our e-mail addresses are at the bottom of this document. VII. POSTING OF THIS DOCUMENT This document will be posted to rspwf every three to four weeks, and is also available via e-mail request to the Jammer (e-mail address below.)
