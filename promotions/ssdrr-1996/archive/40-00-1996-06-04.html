Currently in the Ring: Undertaker, Bob Backlund, Owen Hart, Roddy Piper,
Dean Douglas, Madusa
Entering: Diesel JIM ROSS: HERE COMES BIG DADDY COOL, AND HE ALREADY LOOKS LIKE HE'S PLEASED WITH HIMSELF! JESSE VENTURA: HEY, HE MADE IT IN, AND A PRETTY LATE SEED AT THAT! JR: But take a look, Jesse! He's walking down the aisle slower than the
Undertaker! JV: You gonna go put a flame under his butt, Ross? I'd be MORE than willing to hold your headset for you!
JR: No thank you ... Dean Douglas STILL taking it to the Undertaker in the corner, working over the left leg, BUT THE UNDERTAKER WITH A CHOKE! AND
... CHOKESLAMS HIM ONTO the top turnbuckle, but Bob Backlund quickly over to take the Undertaker's leg out, he falls to the mat and Douglas quickly rolls to the inside!
JV: But his back is hurting! JR: Diesel now getting into a conversation with one of the fans about ring- ... WHAT IS ERIC BISCHOFF DOING HERE? I THOUGHT BAM BAM BIGELOW
KILLED HIM! JV: I GUESS NOT! I'M IN SHOCK! JR: Bischoff with a neck brace on, but he seems to be okay, as Diesel shakes his hand and starts back down the aisle ...
JV: ... you really never do know what's going to happen here! JR: Madusa working over Owen Hheadlock but Owen pushes her off into the ropes, comes back off AND RODDY PIPER OUT OF NOWHERE WITH A LARIAT AND
MADUSA DOES A THREE-SIXTY IN MID-AIR! JV: OUCH! JR: Bob Backlund now with a reverse chinlock on the Undertaker as Dean
Douglas still clutching his back!
JV: Those turnbuckles don't give much, and the Undertaker rammed the small of his back against the exposed steel connector, that has to hurt, Ross!
JR: Roddy Piper meanwhile slapping Madusa around, much to the delight of the crowd as Owen Hart now looks like he's going to go over to the Dean, and Diesel's barely halfway down the aisle!
JV: Hey, when you're that big and tough, you do what you want! JR: We need officials to get him into the ring pretty soon, there's no written time limit on entrances but this is ridiculous!
JV: Owen Hart in the meantime has Dean Douglas by the leg, dragging him out into the center of the ring, HERE WE GO, SCORPION DEATH LOCK TIME!
JR: Sharpshooter, Jesse, he calls it a sharpshooter and so should you! JV: Whatever, he's got the legs crossed but Douglas is blocking, Owen can't turn him over! JR: The self-professed King of Harts not able to turn the Dean over because the Dean's extended his base by getting his arms out, these two have provided some great action tonight, Jesse!
JV: You don't find two more gifted scientific wrestlers than these two! JR: We're almost a minute into the round and Diesel is just now getting to the ring! JV: Well, here he ... no, he doesn't want to come in that side! JR: Oh, this is RIDICULOUS, Jesse! JV: Give him time, this is an important decision here, which fray he wants to enter! JR: My god ... Douglas just pushed Owen off before he could turn him, sending him to the ropes, Dean with a drop-down but Owen hops over, back off, he BLOCKS the hiplock attempt, tries one HIMSELF but Dean blocks, AND
A SHORT CLOTHESLINE BY THE DEAN! JV: Well, Diesel's in the ring, no he isn't! JR: He entered then went back through the ropes to the outside, this is an appalling display the big man, not the kind of non-action we wanted tonight!
JV: Roddy Piper now with an STF on Madusa! JR: And Mr. Bob Backlund now with a front face-lock on the Undertaker, who rises to his feet, NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPLEX!
JV: NICE! JR: AND NOW DIESEL SLIDES IN, QUICKLY GRABS BOB BACKLUND BY THE HEAD ... JV: HE'S NOT WASTING TIME NOW! JR: JACKKNIFE! JV: POWERBOMB! JR: WHO CARES, BOB BACKLUND IS DOWN, AND QUICKLY BIG DADDY COOL TO THE UNDERTAKER, HIGH KNEE SENDS THE UNDERTAKER INTO THE CORNER! JV: Backlund is writhing in the ring! JR: That kind of move done with that kind of power sends shockwaves all through your system, Jesse!
JV: I know that, don't you think I know that, Ross? JR: Diesel now walks over, CLIPS that left knee and the Undertaker falls back! JV: And Roddy Piper just switched that STF around, take a look, Ross! JR: My god, he's got her chin under his armpit, yanking her head back, he could rip it off!
JV: It'd improve her looks! JR: Jesse! Diesel now sliding back out of the ring, grabs the Undertaker by his left leg and WHIPS his leg into the ringpost!
JV: Diesel knows what to do, he's had it done to him before, one big man to another! JR: Dean Douglas now with a full-nelson on Owen Hart, who is on his stomach, and the Dean driving his head between the shoulders of the Rocket
King, as my good friend, Gorilla Monsoon, likes to call him ...
JV: ::sigh:: Ross, please don't bring that man up! JR: Why? JV: Look, Ross, you know I don't think that much of you as a commentator
... JR: ... or a person ... JV: ... but if there is one thing I have gone on record as saying, it is that you make the BEST barbeque baby-back ribs on the planet!
JR: Well, thankou, Jesse! JV: But I CAN'T go to your barbeques because you invite that BABOON over and we have to keep listening to him tell stories about back when he tag-teamed with Chief Jay Strongbow and all that!
JR: Jesse, nobody's saying you have to listen to the man! JV: The way he talks, you don't have a choice! Want me to go on-line right now and prove it? (5!)
JR: That won't (4!) be necessary as (3!) we are ready for (2!) another entrant! (1!) ::BUZZ:: JR: Let's get back to the action, as here comes UH-OH! JV: THE BRITISH BULLDOG! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJ RK RULES! - Support MST3k on Sci-Fi! - I bloody hate hockey ...
