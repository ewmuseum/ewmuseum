SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 50:00

16 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Owen Hart, Dean Douglas, Diesel, British Bulldog,
Bret Hart, Sting, 1-2-3 Kid
Entering: Terry Gordy JIM ROSS: TERRY GORDY IS THE NEXT MAN DOWN, BUT QUICKLY BACK TO THE RING
AS THE 1-2-3 KID IS OVER THE TOP ROPE AND TRYING TO FIGHT OFF ELIMINATION
AT THE HANDS OF THE BRITISH BULLDOG! JESSE VENTURA: THE BULLDOG LITERALLY POWERED OUT OF THAT STF, THREW THE
KID OVER THE TOP BUT NOW THE KID IS TRYING TO HANG ON TO THAT TOP ROPE!
JR: THE BULLDOG PRYING THE KID'S HANDS OFF AND ... HE FALLS, BUT TO ONE
LEG, and now the Kid grabbing the Bulldog's left leg from outside of the ring, tripping him up, and he THROWS the leg violently on the side of the ring apron, that had to hurt!
JV: You bet it did, Ross, Bulldog holding onto that leg as back inside
Owen Hart cinching a front face lock on the Stinger ...
JR: BUT THE BULLDOG KICKS THE KID WITH HIS OTHER LEG, THE KID FLIES INTO
THE SAFETY RAIL BUT STILL MANAGES TO STAY ON ONE FOOT! THAT IS
DETERMINATION, JESSE! JV: HEY, I ALWAYS SAID THIS KID IMPRESSED ME, ROSS! JR: Now the Kid quickly sliding back into the ring to stave off the chances of an accidental slip, goes in and drags the Bulldog away from the ropes, and ... goes for the cover.
JV: Well, there's that inexperience factor kicking in ... JR: Terry Gordy rolls into the ring, quickly over to Dean Douglas and cinches in a Japanese arm bar, meanwhile the Bulldog raking the Kid's face, the Kid off the Bulldog AND THE BULLDOG CLIPS THE RIGHT LEG!
JV: HE JUST TOOK THE KID'S AERIAL ASSAULT OUT, THAT IS THE SIGN OF A TRUE
RING VETERAN, ROSS, HE JUST TOOK OUT THE KID'S STRONGEST SUIT!
JR: Big Daddy Cool, Diesel, in the meantime, now starting to emerge on the better half of a slugfest with Bret Hart!
JV: Bret started the slugfest, and I have to say that was not a wise decision on the Hitman's part!
JR: Well, normally Bret can mix it up in any style, but in a slugfest with
Diesel I think the Hitman's just got no chance!
JV: British Bulldog with a step-over toe hold on the Kid's right leg,
Bulldog again showing his ruthless efficiency!
JR: Back to the Hitman and Diesel, Diesel with the better part of this slugfest but both comPETITORS MAKING IT TO THEIR FEET TO THE CHEERS OF THE
CROWD, DIESEL ... MISSES THE ROUNDHOUSE RIGHT, HITMAN TRIES AN ATOMIC DROP
BUT DIESEL FLIPS ALL THE WAY OVER, GRABS THE HITMAN BY THE HEAD, AND ...
HITMAN ESCAPES, SLIDES UNDER THE BOTTOM ROPE AND TRIPS BIG D DOWN! JV: THAT WAS TOO CLOSE, ROSS! JR: Bret escaping at literally the last second, dragging Diesel over to the post, splitting the legs, OH YEAH, GO FOR IT!
JV: NO! JR: TIME FOR BIG DADDY COOL TO APPLY FOR MEMBERSHIP AT THE VIENNA BOYS CHOIR! JV: HE'S GONNA SLINGSHOT HIM ... PARTS FIRST, THERE'S NO NEED FOR THIS! JR: AND ... OOOOOOOOOH, THAT'S GOTTA HURT! JV: THAT'S DISGUSTING, ROSS! JR: BACK to the inside of the ring, Stinger with a suplex of sorts on the
King of Harts, that's one way to escape a front face lock as Terry Gordy picks up the Dean, whips him into the ropes, comes off, KICK TO THE
STOMACH BY GORDY, Terry criss-crosses AND A KNEELIFT! JV: Dean's gonna be out before the next entrant, Ross, I can feel it. JR: So can I, as Terry Gordy has yet to stop pummeling on the Dean, picks him up, AND A NICE VERTICAL SUPLEX!
JV: Gordy's going ballistic on the Dean! JR: I'll say, as GORDY PICKS UP THE DEAN AGAIN, WHAT ARE WE GONNA SEE HERE ... DDT! JV: The Dean is out of it ... meanwhile, Sting now, has the King of Harts on his stomach, cinching in a double hammerlock!
JR: That'll be an effective move if Sting can keep Owen down with it, I think Owen may be a bit too strong as Diesel is in utter and complete agony in the corner of the ring!
JV: And for good reason, Ross! JR: Meanwhile Bret Hart climbing back through the ropes into the ring, but
Gordy whips the DEAN RIGHT INTO HIM AND BRET HART WITH A MILITARY PRESS!
JV: I TOLD YOU, ROSS! THE HITMAN'S GONNA DUMP DEAN DOUGLAS TO THE OUTSIDE! JR: AND ... THAT'S WHAT HE DOES! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: DEAN DOUGLAS HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: AND TERRY GORDY NOT PLEASED WITH THAT FACT, RIPS STRAIGHT INTO THE
HITMAN, BUT BRET WAS READY FOR HIM, AND A COUPLE OF GUT SHOTS HAVE GORDY
REELING, AND THE HITMAN ... WITH A BULLDOG! JV: And the Bulldog with a half-crab! JR: ... what? JV: The British Bulldog has turned the spinning toe-hold he had on the 1-2-3 Kid into a half-crab! JR: ... oh, I thought you meant ... JV: You're losing it, Ross! JR: I lost it a long time ago, my god, Jesse, we're about ready to see number 28 go down the aisle!
JV: And after him, two more men left! JR: It sure seems a lot longer than an hour! Let's let the crowd count us into this next entrant ... (5! 4! 3! 2! 1!)
