SSDRR: 64:00

16 September 1996

Currently in the ring: Bret Hart, Barry Horowitz, Ric Flair JR: BRET HART HAS TURNED HIS BACK ON BARRY HOROWITZ, ATTACKING HIM AS HE WAS WORKING OVER THE NATURE BOY! JV: HEY, IT'S EVERY MAN FOR HIMSELF, ROSS! JR: AND THE CROWD DOESN'T SEEM TO MIND, AS NOW BRET TRYING TO SNAP MARE HOROWITZ OVER THE TOP! JV: And look at this, Ross, Bret got his arms between the top and middle ropes, then grabbed onto Horowitz's head! This way Barry can't reverse this on Bret!
JR: Well, Bret's got that ring savvy, if he can get Horowitz over now,
Flair could be slim pickings!
JV: He just waited, Ross, I bet you anything the Hitman went along with the double-team until he knew Flair was out of it, then he surprised
Horowitz!
JR: Bret trying to get the luckiest man in wrestling over the top rope, but Horowitz hooked the bottom rope with the back of his right heel, that's gonna stymie the Hitman's efforts!
JV: Flair rolling around now, he's trying to get some momentum going again! JR: The Nature Boy is trying to get back to his feet, but he's been taking the brunt of the damage over the past few minutes, I don't know if he's got it!
JV: Well, Hart and Horowitz seem to be in a stalemate right now, neither man really paying attention to what's going on behind them, but Flair not even to his feet yet AS THIS CROWD IS TRYING TO GET BEHIND THE HITMAN,
WHO'S STARTING TO SHOW SOME SIGNS OF PROGRESS AGAINST HOROWITZ!
JR: BARRY HOROWITZ HAVING TROUBLE STAYING ON HIS FEET NOW, THE HITMAN
SUMMONING THE STRENGTH TO LIFT HOROWITZ OFF HIS FEET, STILL NOT GETTING
MUCH BUT HE IS SHOWING SOME PROGRESS! JV: FLAIR'S BACK TO HIS FEET! JR: RIC FLAIR NOW STUMBLING OVER, HOROWITZ THRASHING HIS FEET VIOLENTLY TO
STAY IN BUT BRET IS SHIFTING HIS CENTRE OF GRAVITY, HERE COMES FLAIR AND
HE GRABS HOROWITZ'S ANKLES AND THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP, HOROWITZ TO THE
APRON BUT BRET KICKS HIM OFF! JV: AND FLAIR CLOTHESLINES THE HITMAN DOWN! JR: WAIT, WAIT, HOROWITZ IS NOT OUT! HOROWITZ IS NOT OUT! JV: WHAT?!? JR: HOROWITZ PERCHED PRECARIOUSLY ON ONE FOOT AND DOUBLED OVER, HE'S HIDDEN HIMSELF FROM FLAIR'S EYESHOT! JV: OH, COME ON, NATURE BOY, LOOK OVER! LOOK TO YOUR RIGHT! JR: FLAIR HASN'T NOTICED, AS HE PICKS THE HITMAN UP AND DRIVES HIM FACE-FIRST TO THE TURNBUCKLE! JV: AND HOROWITZ ROLLS BACK IN, YOU WANNA TALK ABOUT DUMB LUCK, ROSS! JR: OH ... oh my, that was the closest we've seen yet to an elimination that wasn't, Horowitz content to sit in the corner as the Nature Boy works over the Excellence of Execution with punches to the kidney area!
JV: And that'll take the starch out of anybody real quick! JR: Flair alternating between punches to the lower back and turnbuckle shots, as Horowitz able to climb to his feet, lifting up on those ropes for support, but he's not gonna get back into action until he absolutely has to!
JV: I'm surprised Horowitz had that type of stamina! I mean, last year he bodyslams Andre the Giant, now this ... FLAMINGO impression on the outside, and he's hanging in there with two of the cagiest veterans this sport has ever seen!
JR: Flair continuing to pummel the Hitman, but Flair looks like he's running out of gas! JV: He still thinks he's only got the Hitman to contend with, he doesn't realize he's got Barry behind him, he doesn't know he can't afford to rest right now!
JR: Horowitz coming out of the CORNER AND RUNS HIS SHOULDER RIGHT INTO THE BACK OF FLAIR'S RIGHT KNEE! JV: HE DROVE IT STRAIGHT INTO THE TURNBUCKLE! JR: FLAIR IS IN AGONY! I THINK HE MAY HAVE HIT HIS KNEECAP IN THE SOFT SPOT! JV: THE NATURE BOY IS SCREAMING! JR: HOROWITZ COMING OVER, WHAT ... JV: NO! JR: ... HE CINCHES IN A FIGURE-FOUR ON THE NATURE BOY! JV: THIS IS SACRELIGE, ROSS! JR: AND NOW THE CROWD BEHIND HOROWITZ ONCE AGAIN! JV: HE DOESN'T HAVE IT CINCHED IN GOOD, THOUGH, ROSS! JR: No, he's not yanking down on that right leg near the ankle, but he's still got Flair in INTENSE physical pain! And Bret slumped over in that corner, I think he may be out!
JV: Just when you think Horowitz is out, all of a sudden he is in control in there! JR: Flair now, trying to turn over and reverse this but Horowitz turns it loose! JV: But the damage is done, Ross! Flair has been crippled! JR: Horowitz struggling to his feet, it looks like he's still a bit woozy, it has been intense in there to say the least!
JV: He's on wobbly legs in there! JR: But he STILL manages to drop the knee to the forehead of the Nature
Boy! JV: The Hitman is out! He's still slumped over, he's barely able to move his neck around, and he still hasn't opened his eyes!
JR: Flair worked his head over good with those repeated turnbuckle shots, as now Horowitz, trying to get a sleeper hold in here but he hasn't got his left arm down far enough!
JV: No, but he's still got his good arm under the throat, that'll be good enough at this point!
JR: All three men are operating on instinct here, this is something! JV: This is how far a man will go in order to win a title, Ross, that should say something about the devotion these men have to this sport!
