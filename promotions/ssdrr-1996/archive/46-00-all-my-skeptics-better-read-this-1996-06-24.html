SSDRR: 46:00 (all my skeptics better read this ...)

24 June 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Undertaker, Owen Hart, Dean Douglas, Diesel, British Bulldog, Bret Hart Entering: Sting JIM ROSS: Now the man known as Sting getting the crowd up, as the action has slowed a bit in the ring ...
JESSE VENTURA: Bret Hart and Dean Douglas both applying some of the good, basic scientific maneuvers to wear their opponents down, may not be exciting to watch but it works!
JR: The King of Harts, Owen Hart, trying to find some measure of escape from this side headlock being applied by his older brother but he'd better get to a standing base first ... no, he still gets out by popping his head out and turning it into a hammerlock ...
JV: And the Undertaker having trouble getting to his feet! JR: He nailed Diesel good with that tombstone, but Big Daddy Cool inflicted so much pain on that left leg already that the man from Death
Valley having trouble getting to his feet, having to hold on to the ropes here for support!
JV: British Bulldog starting to stir, Dean Douglas's half-nelson looks a bit loose but the Bulldog's face isn't showing it!
JR: The Dean may have the palm of his hand right on one of the many nerves back there, and may be focusing his weight back there, instead of tightening the hold, as Owen Hart DRIVES the knee into the forearm of his brother Bret!
JV: I don't know, Ross, both men very accomplished scientifically, but with the history of these two you'd expect a ... oh, geez, what do you call it, a slabknock or ...
JR: Slobber-knocker. JV: Where in the HELL do you get these terms from? JR: Ask my mom, she taught me my basic vocabulary ... JV: Yeah: GIVE ME MORE FOOD. JR: Cut it out, Jesse ... Sting rolling into the ring, quickly goes over to the Undertaker, GRABS HIM BY THE HEAD AND THROWS HIM OUT!
JV: WHAT?!? HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: THE UNDERTAKER HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! JV: OH MY GOD! JR: STING ROLLS IN, AND THE FIRST THING HE DOES IS HE THROWS THE DEFENDING CHAMPION OUTSIDE OF THE RING! JV: MY GOD, ROSS, DO YOU KNOW WHAT THIS MEANS? JR: YES, IT MEANS WE'RE GOING TO HAVE A NEW CHAMPION THIS YEAR! JV: NO, IT MEANS I'M FINALLY GOING TO BE ABLE TO SLEEP AT NIGHT! JR: JESSE! JV: SHUT UP AND LISTEN, ROSS! I USED TO THINK THIS GUY WAS COOL, TOO, BUT ALL THIS GOTHIC (censored) HAS GOTTEN WAY TOO OLD, AND I'M SICK AND TIRED OF IT! AFTER LAST YEAR, I COULDN'T STAND LOOKING AT ANY OF THE OTHER WRESTLERS KNOWING THEY'D LOST TO THIS MAN, BUT NOW *FINALLY* I CAN BE PROUD TO BE A PROFESSIONAL WRESTLER ONCE AGAIN! JR: Jesse, you are so out of touch ... the Undertaker now starting to make his way back to th locker ROOM BUT WE HAVE A FAN WHO JUST JUMPED OUT OF
THE SEATS AND BEGINS PUMMELING ON THE UNDERTAKER, what's that 900-number on his shirt ... JV: THAT'S NO FAN, ROSS! JR: MY GOD, YOU'RE RIGHT! THAT'S THE HEARTBREAK KID, [name withheld]! WHAT IS HE DOING HERE? JV: WHY ARE YOU ASKING ME, ROSS? I DIDN'T THINK THIS GUY WAS FOR REAL! JR: I GUESS HE IS! HE IS JUST TAKING IT TO THE UNDERTAKER WITH RIGHTS AND LEFTS, WE NEED TO GET SECURITY DOWN HERE! JV: THIS MAN'S TRAIL OF CARNAGE KNOWS NO BOUNDS! YOU COULD PUT HIM IN ANY ARENA AGAINST ANY OPPONENT AND HE'D STILL KICK ASS! JR: THIS IS UNREAL! [name withheld] AND THE UNDERTAKER DUKING IT OUT, NOW LOOKING
LIKE THEY'RE GOING TO GO BACK THROUGH THE CURTAIN TO THE LOCKER ROOMS ...
(whew) ... MAN! JV: Only tonight, Ross, only flippin' tonight ... JR: Well, let's get back to the action here, we're sorry for that delay, folks, but it looks like we're back in business inside of the ring, while the mess with [name withheld] was going on the British Bulldog got the better of
Dean Douglas, and reversed that half-nelson into a headlock, Douglas on his keyster and the Bulldog leaning all his weight in!
JV: That'll slow the flow of blood circulation to the brain, Ross, and when you aren't getting fresh oxygen up there you start getting dizzy and worn out!
JR: And it looks like Bret Hart has once again got the upper hand on Owen, with a kneeling Indian Death Lock!
JV: And Sting now, gets the Scorpion Death Lock on Diesel! JR: And no reaction from the crowd! They're still in shock over what just happened! And you aren't going to keep calling Sting "This is" Sting all through the night?
JV: Believe me, Jim Ross, I have a newfound respect for this man! JR: And you look in the ring, we have probably the three best-known American masters of the hold that some call the Scorpion Death Lock and some call the Sharpshooter!
JV: The Brothers Hart and Sting, three of the better scientific wrestlers in the sport today ...
JR: But here, you can tell, Sting isn't leaning back with the Scorpion Death Lock, that could be a big mistake! You don't lean back, the hold is maybe, at best, a tenth of its normal effectiveness!
JV: That's true, Ross, but maybe he's trying to focus the hold on the legs instead of the back! In that position, he's probably inflicting a little more damage on the left knee!
JR: I hadn't thought about that, you could be right ... this crowd is still silent, here comes the countdown to the next wrestler, let's see if that gets them back up ... (5.) We need (4.) someone to (3.) electrify
(2!) this crowd, (1!) now they're getting ... ::BUZZ:: JR ... into it, but we need someone electric down that aisle right now, AND HERE COMES THE MAN TO DO IT! JV: THE 1-2-3 KID! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJORK RULES! - Support MST3k on Sci-Fi! - Go Tigers! (I know ...)
