SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 32:00

30 April 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the ring: Shawn Michaels, Big Van Vader, Goldust, Undertaker,
Bob Backlund, Owen Hart
Entering: Toshiake Kawada JIM ROSS: TOSHIAKE KAWADA NOW, GRABBING THE HEAD OF MITSUHARA MISAWA AND
DRAGGING IT TO RINGSIDE EVEN THOUGH HE WAS ELIMINATED MINUTES AGO!
JESSE VENTURA: YOU CAN'T STOP A GOOD FEUD, ROSS! JR: Indeed not, as Big Van Vader looks on ahead of him now that he's sent everybody to the mat!
JV: And Mitsuhara Misawa gets DRILLED into the steel ring post, and gets thrown back in by Toshiake!
JR: He's not supposed to be in there, Jesse! JV: I don't think he has a choice, Ross! JR: It would appear that way to me too, as now Toshiake quickly up the steel ring steps, climbing the top turnbuckle ... SKY TOP PRESS! Nicely done, and Vader takes a step back to look on, and Toshiake once again grabs Mitsuhara by the HEAD AND THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP TO THE OUTSIDE!
JV: THAT'S TWICE HE'S BEEN ELIMINATED TONIGHT, ROSS, THAT CAN'T BE GOOD! JR: Now Toshiake taunting his arch-nemesis from the inside of the RING AND VADER WITH AN AVALANCHE IN THE CORNER! JV: HE'S NOT WASTING TIME! JR: HE'S GONNA THROW TOSHIAKE KAWADA OVER BEFORE HE EVEN GOT A CHANCE TO START ON AN ACTIVE WRESTLER! JV: AND ... NO, SHAWN MICHAELS COMES FROM BEHIND TO SAVE THE DAY! JR: Activity resuming inside the ring after Big Van Vader had floored everybody, Bob Backlund now over to Goldust with a front face lock ...
JV: Michaels and Toshiake now double-teaming Big Van Vader in the corner
... JR: Giving him a taste of his own medicine with those fists, they're ... JV: IF YOU SAY CLUBBERIN', ROSS, I'LL KILL YOU RIGHT HERE RIGHT NOW, I SWEAR TO GOD I WILL! JR: ... I wasn't going to say that, Jesse ... JV: You'd better not! JR: Owen Hart gets to his FEET AND FALLS STRAIGHT BACK DOWN! JV: He came on a house of fire, Ross, he might have burnt out early! JR: Backlund now, still with that front face lock on the bizarre Goldust but Goldust RAMS him into the corner, Backlund stumbles out AND GOLDUST
WITH A DROP TOE-HOLD! JV: GREAT sequence there, Ross, you've got to admit it! JR: Yes, a nice couple of moves but UGGH! HE'S FEELING BACKLUND UP! JV: HE'S THE MASTER OF THE MIND GAME, ROSS! JR: HE'S A SICKO IS WHAT HE IS! Owen Hart now back to his feet, pulls
Shawn Michaels off Vader, whip to the far side, Michaels DUCKS the lariat and starts pounding away at the head of Owen Hart!
JV: And Vader now remounting an offense against Kawada with those forearm shots! JR: Shawn now, whips Owen Hart to the corner, Owen stumbles out AND THE
HEARTBREAK KID WITH A FOREARM SHOT THAT SENDS OWEN CRASHING TO THE CANVAS!
JV: OWEN'S IN TROUBLE, ROSS! JR: Shawn picks Owen back up, HE'S STRIKING UP THE BAND! JV: NO! JR: SHAWN ... OWEN DUCKS, *KILLER KICK!* Shawn stumbling forward ... OVER THE TOP ... SHAWN'S OUT! SHAWN'S OUT! JV: YES! FINALLY! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: THE HEARTBREAK KID, SHAWN MICHAELS, HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: THIS CROWD MOANING IN DISAPPOINTMENT, AS SHAWN MICHAELS ... THIRTY-THREE MINUTES, SEVENTEEN SECONDS IN HAS FINALLY BEEN SENT OVER THE TOP ROPE! JV: IT HAD TO HAPPEN SOONER OR LATER, ROSS! JR: Shawn now, getting to his feet and this CROWD GIVES HIM A STANDING OVATION, HE REALLY PULLED OUT ALL THE STOPS TONIGHT, JESSE! JV: YEAH, BUT IT WASN'T ENOUGH, NOW, WAS IT? JR: Not quite, but Shawn's a fighter, there's no question about it, he'll be back next year I can assure you!
JV: Back to the ring ... OH GOD! JR: THE UNDERTAKER SITS UP AGAIN! JV: OVER AND OVER AGAIN, WHEN IS SOMEONE GONNA BURY THIS MAGGOT SIX FEET UNDER? JR: The Undertaker going over to the corner where Toshiake Kawada and Big
Van Vader are, he THROWS Kawada off as Owen Hart still celebrating in the ring, and the Undertaker STARTS TAKING IT TO BIG VAN VADER WITH THOSE
KARATE CHOPS TO THE THROAT! JV: BACKLUND'S TURNING IT AROUND ON GOLDUST, TOO, AND CINCHES IN AN STF! JR: THE UNDERTAKER JUST PUMMELING VADER DOWN IN THE CORNER, BUT VADER WITH A LOW BLOW AND THE UNDERTAKER BACKS AWAY! JV: Owen Hart now with a back waistlock on Toshiake Kawada, picks him up and DRIVES him on the back of his head, nice German suplex!
JR: Vader now setting the Undertaker up in the corner, EUROPEAN UPPERCUT SENDS THE UNDERTAKER THREE FEET IN THE AIR! JV: SET HIM RIGHT ON THAT TOP TURNBUCKLE, ALL VADER HAS TO DO IS KNOCK HIM OVER ... JR: BUT THE UNDERTAKER FIGHTS BACK WITH MORE FISTICUFFS as Goldust in
INCREDIBLE pain with that STF on him, he turns onto his back and OH MY GOD
THAT IS DISGUSTING! STOP IT! STOP IT RIGHT NOW! JV: BIG VAN VADER'S CLIMBING THE TURNBUCKLE! JR: WHAT IS HE DOING, HE'S GOT A WAISTLOCK ON THE UNDERTAKER ... OH MY GOD! A NORTHERN LIGHTS SUPERPLEX! JV: UNBELIEVABLE! JR: INCREDIBLE display from the man from the Rocky Mountains and SOMEBODY stop Goldust, please! This is sick!
JV: Why do you always have to (5!) pick on him, (4!) Ross? I (3!) mean, at (2!) least he doesn't (1!) wear a skirt ...
