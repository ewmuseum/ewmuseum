SSDRR: 38:00

28 May 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
NOTE: I left home at 8:30 pm to type this, so I hadn't caught enough of
Nitro to know what the next entrant (after the Dean) will be in WCW, so I have to use his WWF gimmick for his participation in the DRR. Now that
I've really narrowed down who it's gonna be, might as well get to the action ...
Currently in the Ring: Undertaker, Bob Backlund, Owen Hart, Roddy Piper,
Dean Douglas Entering: Madusa JIM ROSS: Dean Douglas with an armbar now on the King of Harts, driving his knee into Owen's face as Madusa running down the aisleway ...
JESSE VENTURA: Uggh ... seen better faces on iodine bottles ... JR: Now, come on, Jesse, Madusa is a very lovely and vicious woman! JV: Oh, there's no doubt she's a VERY capable wrestler, Ross, but she looks positively butt-ugly!
JR: No she doesn't, and that's no way to treat a lady! JV: LADY?!? JR: Well ... a FEMALE, okay? JV: I've called this woman many things, but a LADY would be just about the last! JR: Well, she's in there now, and RIGHT AWAY CLAWS THE EYES OF DEAN DOUGLAS, AND REACHES DOWN AND CLAWS OWEN'S! JV: SHE DIDN'T WASTE ANY TIME! JR: AND SHE GOES OVER TO HOT ROD AND CLAWS HIS, AND BACKLUND'S, AND THE
UNDERTAKER'S! NOW SHE TURNS AROUND, GRABS BOB BACKLUND BY THE HAIR, TRIES
TO THROW HIM OVER BUT BACKLUND WITH THE SENSE TO HOLD ON TO THE TOP ROPE!
JV: She was looking at the monitor in the back, saw she didn't have a maze of humanity to get through and got a quick blow in on everybody, letting them know who's boss! Even I'm impressed, Ross!
JR: God, Jesse, I mean, you take a look at the ring now, as Dean Douglas with a European-style forearm to the chin of the Undertaker, we've already lost half our field! And the only one of the first fiveteen seeds left is the Undertaker!
JV: This level of action, Ross, can wear you out REAL fast ... I mean, Mr. Endurance himself, Shawn Michaels, lasted a LONG time but even he had to go sooner or later!
JR: You know, usually it's the later seeds that win this thing, and it doesn't look like much is gonna change ... MADUSA GOT BACKLUND OVER BUT
ONLY to the apron, he rolls back in an a variation on a drop-toe hold gets
Madusa over and Backlund wasting no time, step-over toe hold, HOLDING ONTO
THE TOP ROPE TO GET LEVERAGE but it's legal, I know, Jesse.
JV: Owen Hart now with a chinlock on Roddy Piper, should've tried a camel clutch on him, Piper's weakest in his back!
JR: Madusa looking for some way out of the predicament Mr. Looney Toons has got her in ... JV: Now was THAT a nice thing to say? JR: Well, the man deserves his, he is just not right, Jesse! JV: But you'd still vote for him! JR: OF COURSE NOT ... well, actually given the alternatives ... JV: There, see? JR: Look, Jesse, the man is insane, as he's got Madusa literally screaming inside the ring, MADUSA WITH A FOOT TO THE KEYSTER, BACKLUND ALMOST GOES
OVER BUT CATCHES HIMSELF, teeters back in and IMMEDIATELY with a fistful of hair and punches to the jaw, BUT MADUSA'S PUNCHING BACK!
JV: LOOK OUT! JR: THESE TWO ARE GOING FAST AND FURIOUS AS MADUSA WORKS HER WAY BACK TO
HER FEET, BACKLUND ... MISSES AN UPPERCUT, AND MADUSA PIMP-SLAPS HIM DOWN
AND HIS HEAD BOUNCES OFF THE MAT LIKE A BASKETBALL! JV: WOW! JR: Madusa raising her hands in victory, it's not over yet, but man, did she slap him silly!
JV: I told you, Ross, you do not want to get this woman mad at you, she'll humiliate you in the wink of an eye!
JR: Meanwhile Roddy Piper ELBOWS his way out of the chinlock applied on him by Owen Hart, Piper bounces off the rope, GOING FOR A SUNSET FLIP ...
JV: WHAT GOOD WILL IT DO HIM, ROSS? JR: I don't know, but he GETS HIM OVER, OH MY GOSH! JV: MAN ALIVE! JR: AS SOON AS PIPER GOT OWEN HART OVER MADUSA RAN UP AND DARN NEAR TOOK HIS HEAD OFF WITH A CLOTHESLINE! JV: SHE HAS BEEN A ONE-WOMAN WRECKING CREW IN THERE! JR: Dean Douglas in the meantime has been wearing the Undertaker down in the corner, has his leg draped over the middle rope and has been kicking away at it, good strategy on the Dean's part here, Jesse.
JV: Why do you think he got that name, Ross? JR: Oh, he gave it to himself, Jesse! JV: Well, it's still pretty hard-earned, he's got the right idea here trying to take away the legs of the big man ...
JR: Owen Hart, meanwhile, just grabbed Madusa's left leg, working on a half-crab here as we are now close to the forty-minute mark!
JV: Only nine more to go, and rest assured we haven't seen the best of 'em yet! JR: Madusa now climbing the ring ropes with her hands but Owen's maintaining the half-crab ...
JV: She's gotta get her foot under her, otherwise she'll wrench her back! JR: I don't know what she's got planned here as she WOW! (5!) IN THE
(4!) BLINK OF AN EYE (3!) A HEADLOCK (2!) TAKEOVER! (1!)
::BUZZ:: JV: INCREDIBLE! JR: MADUSA SETTING THE PACE NOW as who's next, he's sure taking his sweet time here ... JV: All the fans looking down the aisle, it's ... OH YES! JR: OH NO! JV: BIG DADDY COOL,IESEL! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJÖRK RULES! - Support MST3k on Sci-Fi! - Go Red Wings!
