SSDRR: 28:00

26 March 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the ring: Shawn Michaels, Hakushi, Big Van Vader, Goldust,
Undertaker, Bastion Booger.
Entering (the broadcast booth): Bob Backlund JIM ROSS: STOP HIM JESSE! JESSE VENTURA: WHOA, WHOA, MR. BACKLUND ... COME ON, BEATING UP JIM ISN'T
GONNA WIN YOU THE DREAM ROYAL RUMBLE AND IT CERTAINLY WON'T INCREASE YOUR
'Q' RATING! ROSS ISN'T SOMEONE YOU HAVE TO CONCERN YOURSELF WITH, NOT
NOW, ANYWAY .... there.
JR: Whoa ... I owe you one, Jesse! JV: Yes, you do, Ross, and don't you forget it! JR: Shawn Michaels now LEVELING a prone Goldust with right hands as Bob ... JV: ROSS! JR: MISTER Backlund makes his way down to ringside as the Undertaker going to work now on Hakushi in a similar way!
JV: Look out, Vader's setting up a Vader bomb for Bastion Booger! JR: NO! WE DON'T NEED ANOTHER COLLAPSING RING! JV: Don't worry, Ross, someone with some intelligence designed the ring this year! JR: Well, that's good as now Vader climbing to that second rope ... PUMPING UP ... AND ... DOWN, YUCK! JV: WELL, THERE'S THAT TWINKIE SANDWICH AGAIN! JR: THAT IS DISGUSTING, JESSE! JV: I AGREE, ROSS ... you should never wrestle until thirty minutes after you've eaten ... JR: JESSE! JV: HEY ROSS, IT'S TRUE! KEEP IN MIND, _I'VE_ DONNED A PAIR OF WRESTLING TIGHTS IN MY LIFE! JR: Okay ... actually, once I donned Dusty Rhodes' ringwear back in the mid eighties ... JV: I DON'T NEED TO HEAR THIS, ROSS! JR: Sorry, sorry ... Backlund IMMEDIATELY went over to Big Van Vader and slapped that ckicken-wing cross-face on him!
JV: Remember what happened last year, Ross, Vader KNOWS a scientific counter to this! JR: I remember it well, but I think Backlund's been practicing, then again, Vader over the twenty-minute mark here may not be able to take on a fresh Backlund!
JV: No, but he's got his defences as he SWINGS BACKLUND OVER THE TOP! JR: BUT BACKLUND LANDED ON THE APRON! He climbs to the top rope AND PUMMELS VADER WITHA DOUBLE AXEHAQNDLE, BUT LOOK, I THINK VADER LIKED THAT! JV: BACKLUND'S NOT STOPPING! JR: HE KEEPS PUMMELING VADER WITH FOREARM SHOTS TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD BUT THEY'RE JUST PUMPING VADER UP! JV: LOOK OUT! VADER: WHAT TIME IS IT! (crowd boos with a scattering of cheers) JR: It is VADER TIME AS A CLUBBING FIST TO THE TOP OF EACH SHOULDER SENDS BACKLUND DOWN LIKE A TON OF BRICKS! JV: That's gotta hurt, Ross ... JR: Meanwhile the UNDERTAKER with a chokeslam on Hakushi and the White
Angel is out cold! JV: And Shawn ... HERE WE GO! JR: BIG VAN VADER TURNED AROUDN AND SAW THE UNDERTAKER THERE AND WE'VE GOT ANOTHER STAREDOWN HERE! JV: Shawn Michaels with an abdominal stretch on Goldust, but o one is looking at that! JR: WHO'S GONNA STRIKE THE FIRST BLOW HERE, IT IS VADER WITH A RIGHT FIST
THAT STAGGERS THE UNDERTAKER! AND ANOTHER! THREE BLOWS AND THE
UNDERTAKER WILL NOT GIVE UP! (5! 4!) JV: HE'S NOT (3!) GOING DOWN SO (2!), NOW (1!) HE'S DOWN! ::BUZZ:: JR: THE FOURTH BLOW DID IT, AS IT'S ... ONE OF YOUR FAVOURITES, JESSE! JV: HA HA HA! THE KING OF HARTS, OWEN HART! -- [name withheld]
