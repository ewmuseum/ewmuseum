Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hard to believe, 42 minutes of action so far, and we still haven't hit all the A-1 seeds yet ... If you have never voted for the DRR, please do so! Voting's been down the last few weeks, so we really need to get those ballots in! Summary of the last two minutes: DIESEL (22) entered, 40:00 BRITISH BULLDOG (23) entered, 42:00 This round's ballot: 12. Undertaker 16. Bob Backlund 17. Owen Hart 19. Roddy Piper 20. Dean Douglas 21. Madusa 22. Diesel 23. British Bulldog You have 500 points to vote with, but you do not have to use all 500. You can give a singular wrestler up to 100 points apiece. E-mail your ballots to me by eight AM EDT Mon 10 Jun 1996. For a more detailed list of rules and regulations, send an e-mail request to the Jammer at [detail withheld] . Before I get to the leaders standings, I wanted to reiterate the rules on the persona a wrestler takes when they enter: I take the most current persona they have had at the time of their entry, and that is the persona they have throughout the tournament. Two cases in point: Madusa - at the time of preliminary voting, obviously these points went to Alundra Blayze, but since her move back to WC he entered, he had yet to enter the WWF, so I stuck with the Big Van part of his name, even after he entered the WWF as just plain Vader (or The Man They Call Vader, I don't want to get into that ...)
