SSDRR: 60:00

2 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Bret Hart, Sting, Barry Horowitz, Ric Flair JIM ROSS: ... BARRY HOROWITZ BACKDROPPING STING OVER THE TOP ROPE, BUT THE STINGER HOLDS ON TO THE TURNBUCKLE PADDING! JESSE VENTURA: YOU WANNA TALK ABOUT LUCK, ROSS! JR: STINGER TRYING TO PULL HIMSELF BACK IN BUT HE'S PROBABLY EXPENDING WAY
TOO MUCH STRENGTH IN HIS HANDS TO FOCUS ON PULLING HIMSELF UP!
JV: FORTUNATELY HOROWITZ HASN'T TURNED AROUND YET, NOW HE DOES BUT Sting drops to a sitting position on the ring apron and rolls back in under the bottom rope!
JR: Sting grabs Horowitz by the ankle and trips him onto his back, Stinger
UP BUT MISSES the elbow drop, Horowitz rolls off, up, into the ropes,
MISSES an elbow himself AND STING DIDN'T MISS THAT ONE!
JV: HE GOT UP and back down too quickly for Horriblewitz! JR: C'mon, Jesse, give that a rest, as Ric Flair still tightening his figure-four on the Hitman, Sting now grinding his forearm into the face of
Horowitz!
JV: A very simple and effective move here, Ross, you can see how Sting has that forearm right across the cheekbone, that can cause excrutiating pain, and you can also see that Sting is using the rest of his upper body in an attempt to suffocate Horowitz, stop the flow of oxygen!
JR: Stinger a master of his trade, that much is certain, proving his scientific prowess here as he rises back to a vertical base, taking
Horowitz with him, AND JARS HIM with a massive European-style uppercut! JV: Horowitz is reeling! JR: We are over the one-hour mark now, as ... JV: Hey Ross, I just realized something! JR: And what is that, Jesse? JV: Ric Flair is the only man to be in the final four of both tournaments! JR: ... that's an excellent point, as Sting WHIPS Horowitz back into the corner, maybe going for another Stinger Splash here, runs up AND HOROWITZ
WITH ANOTHER BACKDROP, AND THE STINGER COULDN'T GET THE TURNBUCKLE THAT
TIME! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: STING HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JV: I DON'T BELIEVE IT! JR: AND THEN THERE WERE THREE! HOROWITZ, FLAIR AND THE HITMAN! JV: FLAIR'S NOT WASTING TIME! JR: FLAIR LETS GO OF THE FIGURE-FOUR, HOROWITZ STILL CELEBRATING HIS
VICTORY OVER STING AND FLAIR WITH A KNEEPAT RIGHT TO THE BASE OF THE SPINE
AND HOROWITZ HITS THE CANVAS HARD! JV: Well, thank you, Sting, at least you got that dead guy out of here, better luck next year!
JR: Bret Hart is in agony on the other side of the ring, as the Nature Boy went straight to work on Horowitz, now with a cross-body ride here, pulling the head and ankle back!
JV: Flair knew Horowitz was the big threat, so the moment Horowitz took
Sting out he released the Hitman, knowing that Bret would be down for some time to come, and immediately sought to wear down the fresh man!
JR: But you look back to last year, Jesse, when we got down to three it was Shawn Michaels in the dominant role, Vader getting knocked around and the Undertaker spent on the mat, and it ended up with Michaels getting tossed over the top first, followed by Vader as the Undertaker was victorious!
JV: Yeah, but the Hitman's recouperative powers, though outstanding, are not quite as phenomenal ... of course, Bret's resiliency may make up for that, he's bordering on twenty minutes now and this is usually when he's at his best in a singles match!
JR: But this isn't a singles match, as Flair switching gears here with a headlock on Barry, and Flair had that figure-four on for nearly a full minute, not long enough to do any permanent damage, but when you've got the likes of a Ric Flair applying that figure-four maybe a minute is all you need!
JV: Nobody does it better than the Nature Boy! JR: He's mastered the hold, there's no getting around that, as Barry
Horowitz trying to get to his feet BUT FLAIR WILL HAVE NONE OF THAT AS HE
CLIPS THE KNEE OF HOROWITZ! JV: HE SNUCK THAT FOOT IN AND SPIKED THAT KNEECAP! JR: That was devastating, Horowitz now limping around, trying to ease the pain, Flair very methodically stalking his opponent, Horowitz turns around, MISSING a wild punch BUT TURNS AROUND AND NAILS THE BACK OF
FLAIR'S KNEE! JV: OUCH! JR: FLAIR STARTED TO STRUT, BUT HOROWITZ DIDN'T MISS A BEAT AND TURNED
RIGHT AROUND AND LUNGED INTO THE BACK OF FLAIR'S RIGHT KNEE WITH HIS FIST!
JV: AND ALL THREE MEN ARE DOWN, GRABBING THEIR KNEES! JR: Insert Tonya Harding reference here. JV: ... LAME, ROSS! JR: Sorry, never quite got the knack of that ... all three men looking equally beat here, Bret Hart the only one trying to get to his feet here, holding onto the ropes for support but Flair worked his knee over too good!
JV: Nobody else trying to get up, Bret may do good here to just hold on until someone else gets up, don't work that knee too hard ...
JR: That may be the best thing, Jesse, but you and I have followed the
Hitman's career a VERY long time, we both know that Bret just wouldn't do that, he's got just one gear and that is straight at you!
JV: And if he doesn't learn some patience know that gear may get stripped into "straight to the hosptial!"
