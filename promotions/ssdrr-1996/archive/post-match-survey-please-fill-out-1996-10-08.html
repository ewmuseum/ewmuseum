SSDRR: Post-match survey: PLEASE FILL OUT!

8 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Okay folks, here is what you've all been waiting for: THE POST-MATCH SURVEY!!! Just kidding. Anyway, do me a favour, fill these out and e-mail them back to me by the 21st, so I can get an idea of how I did this time. 1. On a scale from 1 to 10, 10 being best, how would you rate the Dream Royal Rumble? 2. On the same scale, how would you rate the action? 3. On the same scale, how would you rate the commentary? 4. What was the best moment of the Dream Royal Rumble? (Define best yourself - most shocking, most funny, etc.) 5. The worst moment? (Again, own definition.) 6. How would you compare the Dream Royal Rumble to the Dream Battleroyal? a) The Dream Royal Rumble was much better b) The Dream Royal Rumble was slightly better c) Both rate about the same / can't compare the two d) The Dream Royal Rumble was slightly worse e) The Dream Royal Rumble was much worse 7. Out of the 36 rounds of the Dream Royal Rumble, approximately how many did you vote in? What were your reasons for not voting in the other rounds? 8. Do you plan on voting in the Dream Battlebowl? 9. What ONE wrestler did you miss seeing in the Dream Royal Rumble? 10. General suggestions/comments: Thank you for your time. The entirety of the Dream Royal Rumble (all the commentary pasted together into one big lump sum) should be out shortly - it's not in my hands, it's in the Jammer's hands, so pester him if you're going to pester anybody.
