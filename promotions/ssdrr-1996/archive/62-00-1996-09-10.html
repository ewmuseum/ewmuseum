Currently in the ring: Bret Hart, Barry Horowitz, Ric Flair JIM ROSS: Alright, all three men still down, Ric Flair looking now like he may be the first to get up ...
JESSE VENTURA: He took the least powerful blow, it would make sense! JR: Indeed, as Horowitz looks to be the most out of it, Bret Hart somewhere in the middle, it'll be interesting to see who Flair goes AFTER
AND HE ANSWERED THAT QUESTION REAL QUICK! JV: A DIVING LUNGE AND THE HITMAN'S LEFT ANKLE, THE NEXT TO GO! JR: With all the punishment Bret's left leg has taken it'll be a miracle if he can stand! JV: And if you can't stand, you can't eliminate, or at least without some major effort! JR: The Hitman is on his back as Flair still struggling to get to his knees, finally sits up on his heels ...
RIC FLAIR: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO! JR: AND LOOKS LIKE HE'S READY TO GO ON! JV: HEY, HE'S THE ORIGINAL SIXTY-MINUTE MAN, ROSS, THIS MAY HAVE JUST BEEN HIS WARM-UP! JR: RIC FLAIR BACK TO HIS FEET, BOUNCES OFF THE ROPES WITH A NOTICEABLE
LIMP, AND AN ELBOW DROP TO THE HEAD OF THE EXCELLENCE OF EXECUTION!
JV: And Flair immediately back to the mat, cinching in a half-nelson! JR: Barry Horowitz still struggling to get to his feet, I don't know as though this is the right strategy for Flair, though, against the Hitman!
JV: Well, as great as Bret Hart is on the mat, Jim Ross, the Nature Boy's no slouch, either!
JR: Flair's no slouch, but then again, he's no son of Stu Hart, either! JV: Of course not, he can count to twenty-one without taking down his pants ... JR: (pause) ... I don't get it, Jesse. JV: I didn't think you would ... anyway, even though Bret may be the better technical wrestler, all that buildup of pain in his left leg may cause a short-circuit of strength throughout his body, plus the fact that he's been in so much longer and may start getting those calcium deposits in the joints, let's face it, Bret may not be able to suck it up!
JR: Barry Horowitz now to his feet with a very pronounced limp, Flair changing the half-nelson into a chicken-wing as Horowitz gets behind Flair and DROPS THE ELBOW to the small of the back of Flair!
JV: Flair recovers quickly, though, and cinches in a chinlock on the still tired Horowitz! JR: And Bret Hart is not moving ... with as much as Flair has control now,
Jesse, you would think that he'd call on all his reserves now, try to get this over quickly!
JV: I don't think so, Ross, if there is one part of Ric Flair's ring strategy that has won him more titles than any other, it is the fact that he can feel up his opponent at any time during the match, and know when to hold back and when to go in for the kill!
JR: So right now, you think Flair knows that Bret and Barry have yet to pull out their last punches?
JV: Something like that. JR: Bret now struggling to get to his feet, and so is Horowitz, Horowitz able to turn into the chinlock, up to one knee and finally to his feet as
Bret gets to his knees, Barry pushes the Nature Boy into the ropes, drop-down AND FLAIR CAUGHT IT FROM BRET HART!
JV: OH, HE'S REALLY LUCKY THIS ISN'T A REGULAR MATCH, OR THAT WOULD HAVE BEEN AN INSTANT DQ! JR: RIC FLAIR HURDLED OVER HOROWITZ, AND ON THE WAY DOWN CAUGHT ONE IN THE GROIN FROM THE HITMAN! JV: THAT WAS UNCALLED FOR, NO MATTER HOW LEGAL, ROSS! JR: FLAIR TRYING TO STYLE AND PROFILE, BUT HIS KNEES KEEP BUCKLING! BRET
HELPS BARRY HOROWITZ TO HIS FEET, THEY RUN UP, DOUBLE CLOTHESLINE AND
FLAIR GOES THROUGH THE ROPES, THROUGH THE ROPES! JV: SO CLOSE, ROSS! JR: WE ALMOST HAD THE FINAL TWO SET, BUT AT THE LAST SECOND FLAIR WAS ABLE
TO LOWER HIS CENTRE OF GRAVITY AND WENT THROUGH THE ROPES! MAN ALIVE!
JV: And Hart and Horowitz stepping back now, waiting for Flair to get back into the ring but why aren't they going after each other?
JR: I think there is some sort of partnership here, they know that Flair's got more momentum, being the last entrant, but I don't think Flair's coming back into the ring anytime soon!
JV: No, you can see Flair is coughing, one of the signs of extreme trauma to the groin, he may not recover!
JR: And it looks like Bret and Barry are gonna go at it here, but in an extremely gentlemanly manner, no handshake but they're circling each other, collar-and-elbow tie-up, the Hitman gets the better of it, pushing
Horowitz into the ropes ... clean break!?!
JV: The IDIOTS! If you're not gonna do the cheap shots NOW, then WHEN? Keep in mind, you've got the dirtiest player in the game about to get back into the ring! JR: They circle again, collar-and-elbow, this time Barry gets the better of it, into the corner ... and Flair grabs the Hitman's ankle and trips him, dragging him to the outside of the ring, leaning him against the apron **SMACK!** AND ANOTHER KNIFE-EDGE CHOP!
JV: NOBODY DOES THEM BETTER! JR: BUT BRET DOESN'T FLINCH! RIC FLAIR BACKING OFF, BEGGING FOR MERCY AS
THE HITMAN METHODICALLY STALKING THE NATURE BOY OUTSIDE THE RING, THEY
ROUND THE CORNER AND FLAIR'S GOING FULL-SPEED! JV: HE CAN'T GET ELIMINATED FROM OUT THERE, ROSS, THIS MAY BE GOOD STRATEGY ON RIC'S PART! JR: THE REFEREES CREATING A ROADBLOCK, FORCING FLAIR TO GET BACK INTO THE
RING AND BARRY IS THERE TO MEET HIM WITH REPEATED STOMPS TO THE BACK OF
THE NECK! JV: Bret and Barry are just working too cohesively as a unit, Ross ... JR: The Hitman rolls back into the ring, he's got something planned as he bounces himself off the far side, comes off AND DELIVERS A
DOUBLE-AXEHANDLE BLOW TO THE BACK OF HOROWITZ!?! JV: WHAT THE HELL?!? [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJORK RULES! - Go Tigers! (I know ...)
