SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 52:00

23 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Owen Hart, Diesel, British Bulldog, Bret Hart,
Sting, 1-2-3 Kid, Terry Gordy
Entering: Scott Norton JIM ROSS: QUICKLY BACK TO THE RING AS BRET HART TRYING TO THROW TERRY
GORDY OVER THE TOP ROPE, HE'S GOT HALF HIS BODY OVER BUT GORDY BRACING
HIMSELF ON THE SIDE OF THE RING! JESSE VENTURA: HE'S REAL CLOSE TO BEING OUT NOW, ROSS, BUT GORDY KNOWS HOW
TO USE HIS WEIGHT EFFECTIVELY AND STOP UP THAT ATTEMPTED EXIT!
JR: WE'LL KEEP AN EYE OUT FOR THAT as Scott Norton coming down the aisleway, British Bulldog still with that half-crab on the 1-2-3 Kid, turns the Kid back over onto his back, AND THE KID CATCHES HIM IN THE HEAD
WITH HIS OTHER FOOT! JV: THE KID SPRANG UP AND REALLY GOT HIS WEIGHT BEHIND THAT KICK! JR: BRET ALMOST HAS GORDY OVER, and over on the other side of the ring
Sting still working over Owen Hart with a double hammerlock, but the King of Harts able to get to his feet, JUMPS UP AND BODY-SCISSORS THE STINGER
BUT STING DOES NOT LET GO! JV: I DON'T THINK STING CAN HOLD ON, ROSS! JR: AND HE CAN'T and Owen escapes, crawls with his hands to the side of the ring, climbs the ropes with his HANDS, MAYBE HE'S GONNA TRY TO
ELIMINATE THE STINGER! JV: NOT QUITE, Owen instead throws all his weight back and Sting lands hard on his head! JR: Scott Norton not entering the ring, goes over to the side towards
Diesel who is still recouperating in the corner, Norton with a barrage of fists to the head of Big Daddy Cool!
JV: Referees not going to let that continue, though, as they direct Norton into the ring ...
JR: Where he can still punch Diesel just as effectively! JV: Both the British Bulldog and the 1-2-3 Kid getting up at the same time, and ... JR: THE BULLDOG CLIPS THE LEFT KNEE OF THE KID, AND BOTH MEN DOWN AGAIN! JV: THAT WASN'T MUCH OF A CLIP, BUT THE KID IS IN AGONY! JR: AND TERRY GORDY ESCAPES ELIMINATION ON THE OTHER SIDE BY GOUGING THE HITMAN IN THE EYES! JV: THAT'S ONE WAY! JR: Owen Hart, meanwhile, finally back to his feet, comes off the ropes and DROPS the leg on the Stinger!
JV: And Terry Gordy now with a front face lock on Bret Hart! JR: Gordy working over the Excellence of Execution, grinding the head,
Bret looking for a measure of escape here AND HIS BROTHER GIVES IT TO HIM
BY CLOTHESLINING GORDY DOWN! JV: INCREDIBLE clothesline by the King of Harts ... JR: And now Owen over to Gordy, punches him in the head a couple of times and then holds his arms back ...
JV: ... he's telling Bret to go after Terry! JR: Strange spirit of cooperation between the brothers, Bret comes off the far SIDE BUT GORDY DUCKS AND BRET CLOTHESLINES HIS YOUNGER BROTHER OVER!
HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: THE KING OF HARTS, OWEN HART, HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: BRET ACCIDENTALLY CLOTHESLINES OWEN OVER ... JV: I DOUBT THAT WAS AN ACCIDENT, ROSS! JR: The King of Harts trying to get back into the ring but the referees physically restraining him, and Bret looks happy with the situation!
JV: I told you! JR: The Hitman has four of the last five eliminations, Jesse, that is a feat! JV: I will grant you that, as Bret goes back to Gordy, meanwhile Scott
Norton has tired of Diesel and has moved on to the Stinger, working him over with an over-the-knee backbreaker!
JR: Flash has incredible strength in those arms, a former world arm-wrestling champion who has made the transition to professional wrestling very successfully!
JV: And he's got that mean streak about him, Jim Ross, and when he cuts himself loose his rage is matched by few in this sport!
JR: Bret grapevining the legs of Terry Gordy, a modified Indian Death Lock here, as it were, using his free leg to work on the right knee!
JV: Meanwhile the 1-2-3 Kid trying to stand in the middle of the ring but his leg has had it!
JR: The Kid prone to injury, that has been proven, and the British Bulldog finally able to get to his feet, he's walking over to the corner where
Diesel is still flat on his back! The Bulldog pulling Big Daddy Cool up by the hair, scoops him UP ...
JV: IT'S TIME FOR THE RUNNING POWERSLAM! JR: THE BULLDOG TO THE CORNER ... AND ... DIESEL SLIDES BACK AND PUSHES
THE BULLDOG INTO THE OPPOSITE CORNER, HE BOUNCES BACK AND DIESEL WITH A
BELLY-TO-BACK SUPLEX! JV: And Diesel executed that one flawlessly, almost dumping the British
Bulldog straight on the back of his head!
JR: Scott Norton now with a headlock on the Stinger, and on the other side of the RING TERRY GORDY MAY BE ABOUT TO THROW THE HITMAN OVER but instead perches him on that top turnbuckle!
JV: PUSH HIM OVER, TERRY, WHAT ARE YOU WAITING FOR? JR: The Hitman close to elimination here as Gordy CLIMBS THE ROPES, HE COULD BE GOING FOR A SUPERPLEX HERE! JV: NO, NO, NO! DON'T GO UP THERE, SOMEBODY COULD PUSH BOTH OF YOU OVER! JR: TERRY GORDY ... HAS THE FIRST ATTEMPT BLOCKED BY THE HITMAN! AND ... BLOCKED AGAIN! WHAT'S GOING TO HAPPEN HERE, GORDY ... GETS THE HITMAN UP BUT THE HITMAN FLIPS OVER AND TURNS IT INTO A POWERBOMB! JV: WHAT A MOVE! JR: WE HAVE TWO MEN LEFT TO ENTER, (5!) AS THE HITMAN (4!) WITH A FUJIWARA
(3!) ARM BAR ON (2!) TERRY GORDY (1!) IN THE CENTER ...
:: BUZZ :: JR: ... OF THE RING, AND THE NEXT PARTICIPANT IS ... ALL RIGHT, HERE WE GO! JV: OH GOD, IT'S HORRIBLEWITZ! [name withheld] - [detail withheld] BJORK RULES! - Go Tigers! (I know ...)
