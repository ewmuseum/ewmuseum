SSDRR: 18:00

12 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Sorry but the mail server finally got fixed yesterday afternoon ... now that I'm back on the air, let's celebrate by having an elimination this round!
Currently in the ring: Shawn Michaels, Eddie Guerrero, Hakushi, Big Van Vader, Steve Williams, Manami Toyota, [name withheld] Entering: (insert sound of your choice) Goldust JIM ROSS: Okay, somebody wanna tell me how in the hell Goldust got in this thing? JESSE VENTURA: The fans voted him in, Ross, you gotta love it! But why isn't he getting his special lighting treatment or the sprinkling of gold glitter?
JR: Uh, I believe [name withheld] wouldn't allow it. JV: What right does that little snot-nosed punk have to say that? JR: He's running the thing, as BIG VAN VADER WITH A CLOTHESLINE TO THE BACK OF THE HEAD OF SHAWN MICHAELS, WHO GOES OVER THE TOP BUT LANDS ON THE APRON and rolls back in ... close one there! JV: Shawn was posing after throwing Luna Vachon out, and it almost cost him there! JR: Meanwhile Hakushi not relenting on [name withheld], now working on a version of the head vice ...
JV: ... and [name withheld] may lose some more teeth before the night is over! JR: And Steve Williams is still down! He got it good earlier, as Eddie Guerrero literally having to crawl over to him to work him over! JV: Until Vader throws him off back into the center of the ring, and a CLUBBING forearm blow to the back of the head of Manami Toyota sends her back down, and Vader drapes over the body of Doctor Death HERE WE GO!
JR: NO! NOT A VADER BOMB! JV: HE'S GONNA VADER BOMB BOTH OF THEM AT ONCE! JR: NO HE'LL CAVE THE MAT IN! JV: NOT THIS YEAR, ROSS, SOMEONE WITH AN ACTUAL PURPOSE IN LIFE DESIGNED THE RING THIS TIME! JR: Vader starting to pump that rope AND ... HE GOT STEVE WILLIAMS BUT SHAWN MICHAELS DRAGGED MANAMI TOYOTA OUT OF THE WAY JUST IN THE NICK OF TIME! JV: TELL ME THERE ISN'T SOMETHING GOING ON THERE, ROSS! JR: I don't know but in the meantime Goldust has entered the ring and is rubbing the face of Eddie Guerrero into the mat repeatedly and viciously!
JV: Well I never liked that mustache anyway! JR: Jesse, will you be serious? In the meantime [name withheld] trying to get out of Hakushi's head vice with an ELBOW to the solar plexus AND
ANOTHER! He GOES FOR A KICK but Hakushi grabbed the leg, single leg takedown INTO AN STF! JV: HE SNAPPED THAT THING ON SSO FAST ANOTHER OF [name withheld]'S TEETH FELL OUT! JR: UGGH! Hope that doesn't continue, as Goldust now setting up Guerrero who was fighting back in vain himself eariler for a pancake, Guerrero reverses but Goldust slips out through the back door LOOK OUT!
JV: CURTAIN CALL TIME! JR: As Shawn Michaels gets another sleeper on Big Van Vader Goldust DELIVERS with the curtain call! JV: Take a bow, the night is over ... JR: ... this masquerade is ... well, you know. JV: Yeah. JR: Hakushi letting [name withheld] up from the STF, throws him into the corner and RAMS a shoulder into the gut! JV: But here comes Goldust over! JR: He bends Hakushi over backwards and a KNEELIFT to the back of the head and Hakushi falls to the mat like a ton of bricks, must have hit him right in the external occipital protuberance!
JV: ARRRRRGGGGGHHHHHHHHHH! JR: Ooops, sorry about that, as Goldust grabs [name withheld] by the head, runs to the other SIDE AND (5!) ELIMINATES HIM! (4!)
HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: [name withheld] E. (3!) [name withheld] HAS BEEN (2!) ELIMINATED (1!)
::BUZZ:: JR: [name withheld] IS OUT and ... the lights just went out, looks like we're still having some technical difficulties ...
JV: THAT'S NOT A TECHNICAL DIFFICULTY, JIM ROSS! JR: YOU'RE RIGHT! HERE COMES LAST YEAR'S CHAMPION! HERE COMES THE UNDERTAKER! JV: (softly) Here we go again ... [name withheld] -- [name withheld]
