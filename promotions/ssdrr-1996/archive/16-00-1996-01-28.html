SSDRR: 16:00

28 January 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Wow, got a round out on time ... this is cause for celebration! Currently in the ring: Shawn Michaels, Eddie Guerrero, Hakushi, Big Van Vader, Steve Williams, Aldo Montoya, Luna Vachon, Manami Toyota Entering: [name withheld] JIM ROSS: My god, you're right, that is [name withheld]! JESSE VENTURA: Why do you sound so surprised, Ross? Remember what Barry Horriblewitz did last year, [name withheld] stands a good chance here tonight! JR: I don't know about THAT, BUT SHAWN MICHAELS HAS ONE OF VADER'S LEGS OVER THE TOP ROPE but an eyegouge makes the Heartbreak Kid back off and Big Van Vader climbs back in the ring! JV: And Hakushi now letting go of the STF he had on Doctor Death following it up with a nice drop elbow!
JR: [name withheld] now in the ring, and he ... kneels in the corner? JV: Hey, he knows he can't match up against the people in the ring right now, why get yourself into any unnecessary trouble?
JR: Anyway, the catfight continuing between Luna Vachon and Manami Toyota and Shawn backs into it AND GETS TOSSED OVER THE TOP ROPE, BUT HE HOLDS
ON TO THE TOP ROPE AND PULLS HIMSELF BACK IN! JV: THAT WAS CLOSE, ROSS! JR: The crowd cheering the Boy Toy on as Eddie Guerrero now setting the very prone Aldo Montoya up here, picking him up by the mask ...
JV: You mean his upper jockstrap, Ross! JR: That's not nice, Jesse, as Montoya out on his feet and Eddie Guerrero stepping outside and GOING TO THE TOP ROPE ...
JV: Not a smart move here, Jim, not smart at all! JR: I would agree, as Eddie Guerrero COMES OFF, TRIED A DOUBLE AXEHANDLE BUT MONTOYA STILL SMART ENOUGH TO LAND THAT PUNCH TO THE GUT! JV: Close one, as Big Van VADER LIFTING STEVE WILLIAMS UP OVER HIS HEAD! JR: WHAT STRENGTH! WHAT A SHOW OF POWER BY THE MAN FROM THE ROCKY MOUNTAINS AND HE DROPS WILLIAMS ON HIS KNEE! JV: Tremendous backbreaker, as now Alpo going to the top rope ... JR: Jesse, it's ALDO, would you please stop ... JV: NO, IT'S ALPO BECAUSE HE'S DOGMEAT, HE'S HISTORY! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: ALDO MONTOYA HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: HAKUSHI COMING OVER AND DROP-KICKING MONTOYA OFF THE TOP ROPE, and he landed in the Fink's lap!
JV: Not exactly the best place to be, as [name withheld] TAKES ADVANTAGE OF THE DISTRACTION AND GIVES HAKUSHI A KNEEPAT TO THE OUTSIDE! JR: HE WENT THROUGH THE ROPES, though, Hakushi not eliminated as Big Van Vader PRESSING Steve Williams over his head again and WOW! JV: HE THREW HIM RIGHT INTO MANAMI TOYOTA AND BOTH OF THEM ARE FLATTENED INTO THE CORNER! JR: No ... AND VADER AVALANCHES THE TWO OF THEM! JV: INCREDIBLE! JR: Now Hakushi trying to climb back in the ring, [name withheld] trying to grab something but Hakushi grabs the ankle, trips [name withheld] up and drags him under the bottom rope to the outside!
JV: Referees coming over to break that up, both men have to get back into the inside! JR: Shawn Michaels in the meantime cinching in a headlock on Eddie Guerrero ... JV: The two men who started it, and two men who are very capable of finishing it, Ross! JR: I would tend to agree, as Hakushi now with a SERIES of devastating knife-edge chops to the chest of [name withheld], stagers him up against the ringpost ...
JV: Shawn Michaels picking Guerrero up, two-step bulldog! JR: And now Hakushi stepping back, runs up with a handspring, MY GOODNESS! JV: UNBELIEVABLE! JR: HAKUSHI WITH A JUMPING BACKWHEEL KICK, AND HE JUST SANDWICHED [name withheld]'S FACE! JV: Three, four, five teeth! And Hakushi not wasting any time, throws [name withheld] back into the ring! JR: Meanwhile Luna Vachon looks like she may have gotten away for now, but BIG VAN VADER WITH A BEARHUG ON LUNA!
JV: Hakushi back onto the apron, SLINGSHOTS HIMSELF BACK INTO THE RING AND DELIVERS A DEVASTATING FLIPPING LEG DROP ON [name withheld]! JR: Now Vader setting Luna down, SLAPS HER RIGHT INTO SHAWN MICHAELS AND WHOA NELLY! JV: THERE'S A LIPLOCK FOR YA! JR: SHAWN MICHAELS JUST PLANTED ONE ON THE LIPS OF LUNA VACHON as Vader goes back to work on Steve Williams, and LOOK OUT! (5!)
JV: SHAWN'S GETTING (4!) THAT FOOT (3!) READY! (2!) JR: SUPERKICK, (1!), AND LUNA'S OUT! ::BUZZ:: FINK: LUNA VACHON HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: THE CROWD ON THEIR FEET FOR SHAWN MICHAELS, AS DOWN COMES oh no ... JV: HAHA! IT'S YOUR FAVORITE WRESTLER, ROSS, IT'S GOLDUST! [name withheld] -- [name withheld]
