SSDRR: 58:00

20 August 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the Ring: Bret Hart, Sting, Scott Norton, Barry Horowitz, Ric Flair JIM ROSS: BRET HART NOW OVER TO THE NATURE BOY, GRABBING HIM BY THE HEAD
AND SETTING UP HERE FOR A BUTTERFLY SUPLEX, BUT FLAIR SITS OUT AND WITH A
SINGLE-LEG PICKUP TRIPS THE HITMAN! JESSE VENTURA: AND IMMEDIATELY HE YANKS THE LEG BACK, STRAINING THOSE LIGAMENTS IN THE HAMSTRING! JR: And it's the right leg, too, so Flair back to his usual gameplan! JV: Hey, Ross, look where it's got him so far! JR: Scott Norton, in the meantime, collecting his wits, yanks Barry Horowitz off of the Stinger and DROPS the knee right in the short ribs! JV: Oh, he got Horowitz good! JR: He did indeed, as Sting now getting to his knees, his head's been worked over for well over a minute, cutting off the flow of oxygen to the brain, so he'll be content with a short respite right now!
JV: Scott Norton setting up what looks to be like a surfboard here! JR: He IS setting Horowitz up for a surfboard, AND HE'S GOT IT ON! I didn't know that was in Norton's vocabulary!
JV: The only thing that isn't in Flash's vocabulary, Ross, is the word quit! JR: He is tenacious, but let's go back to the other side of the ring, as
Ric Flair continuing to work on the left leg of the Hitman, single-leg grapevine and he's pushing the palm of his hand right into the kneecap!
JV: Bret Hart one of the most gifted technical wrestlers ever, Jim Ross, but Flair's no slouch himself, he knows how to get down on that mat!
JR: Hart looking for a counter out of this, yanking the hair of the Nature
Boy, and Flair releases, BUT IMMEDIATELY UP WITH AN ELBOW TO THE HEAD!
JV: FLAIR GOT UP QUICK THERE, YOU WANNA KNOW HOW MUCH OF AN ADVANTAGE THAT THIRTIETH DRAW IS, THERE'S A GOOD EXAMPLE! JR: On the other side of the ring, Sting mounting the top rope, cardinal mistake by the veteran here but maybe not as nobody to CHALLENGE HIM AND
THE STINGER WITH A SPLASH FROM THE TOP RIGHT ON HOROWITZ, AND NORTON FELT
THAT TOO! JV: HE JUST DROVE NORTON'S LEGS STRAIGHT UP INTO HIS CHEST, AND THAT'LL
TEAR UP THE WHOLE MIDDLE PART OF YOUR BODY, AND HORRIBLEWITZ FEELING THE
EFFECT OF THAT AS WELL! JR: Barry Horowitz literally got caught between a rock and a hard place there, clutching his solar plexus, and I have to believe the way Norton's knees were planted right in the small of his back had to take its toll as well!
JV: The Stinger not wasting any time, straight into an STF, work over those short ribs so well!
JR: Scott Norton looks to be in excrutiating pain here, AND RIC FLAIR OUT
OF NOWHERE WITH A DOUBLE-AXEHANDLE BLOW TO THE LEFT KNEE OF NORTON!
JV: FLAIR QUICKLY BACK UP, KNEE TO THE GUT! JR: HE IS DISMANTLING SCOTT NORTON BIT BY BIT, now getting under him, getting Norton back to a vertical base, may go for a belly-to-back suplex
BUT HE USES SHAWN MICHAELS' OLD SLINGSHOT SUPLEX TO SEND NORTON HEAD-FIRST
OUTSIDE THE RING! HOWARD "THE FINK" FINKEL: SCOTT "FLASH" NORTON HAS BEEN ELIMINATED! JR: NORTON LANDED RIGHT ON THE BACK OF HIS NECK THERE, THAT MAY HAVE DONE SOME SERIOUS PERMANENT DAMAGE! JV: AND WE ARE DOWN TO FOUR! JR: FLAIR AND HART, HOROWITZ AND STING, ONE OF THEM IS GONNA GRAB THAT BIG BRASS RING TONIGHT! JV: AND ANY OF THEM COULD DO IT, YOU'VE GOT THE SAVVY OF THE NATURE BOY,
THE TECHNICAL EXPERTISE OF THE HITMAN, THE SHEER DETERMINATION OF THE
STINGER AND THE BLIND LUCK OF HOROWITZ! JR: INDEED, ALL FOUR MEN CAPABLE OF WINNING THIS THING, THREE OF THEM
FINISHING IN THE TOP TEN LAST YEAR as Flair now going back to the Hitman who's trying to get to his feet LUNGES forward with a punch to the gut of
Flair but he didn't have much behind it!
JV: Flair strutting his stuff, off those ropes AND A NICE boot straight to the head! JR: The Excellence of Execution still trying to get to his feet, Flair sneaking behind him, LIFTS HIM UP, GOING FOR THAT CLASSIC KNEEBREAKER AND
HE NAILED IT! JV: AND THAT'S THE SET-UP FOR THE MOST FAMOUS SUBMISSION HOLD IN THE HISTORY OF PROFESSIONAL WRESTLING, HERE WE GO! JR: RIC FLAIR ... CINCHES IN THE FIGURE-FOUR LEGLOCK, AND IMMEDIATELY GRABS ONTO THOSE ROPES! JV: AND THERE'S NOTHING ANYBODY CAN DO ABOUT IT, LEAST OF ALL BRET HART! JR: QUICKLY TO THE OTHER SIDE OF THE RING, Sting lets go of the STF he had on Barry Horowitz, ELBOW to the kidney area, Sting not letting up for one minute quickly up to his feet, grabs Horowitz by his ankle and drags him into the center of the ring, turns him OVER AND HE'S GOING FOR IT! HE'S
GOING FOR THE SCORPION DEATHLOCK BUT HOROWITZ HAS HIS HANDS UP, GRABBING
THE STINGER'S FOOT SO HE CAN'T STEP THROUGH! JV: AND BRET HART WITH A SINGLE-LEG PICKUP TRIPS THE STINGER, NOW HOLD ON! JR: WE MAY BE SEEING SOME KIND OF TRUCE HERE, HART AND HOROWITZ HAVING WORDS EARLIER, THEY MAY BE TEAMING UP HERE! JV: HOROWITZ OVER TO FLAIR AND THE HITMAN, HE'S TRYING TO RELEASE THE
FIGURE-FOUR BUT NOTHING DOING AS THE STINGER DIVING WITH A FOREARM SHOT TO
THE BACK OF HOROWITZ'S NECK! JR: Sting perhaps saving Flair, I ... never thought I'd hear myself saying that, now Sting back to his feet, arm bar on Barry Horowitz into an Irish
Whip to the corner, Sting HOWLS, PERHAPS SETTING UP FOR THE STINGER SPLASH
HERE, RUNS ACROSS THE RING BUT HOROWITZ BACKDROPS HIM ... (And wouldn't you know, that's all we had time for this week ... hey, come on, I have to have SOME suspense in this thing, now, don't I? Tune in next week, folks, same bat-time, same bat-newsgroup ...)
