SSDRR Tournament Archive

SSDRR: 70:00 - THE END!

8 October 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Currently in the ring: Barry Horowitz, Ric Flair JIM ROSS: Both men struggling to get to their feet near the ropes, it looks like Flair will get up first ... no, Horowitz stands up AND CLOTHESLINES FLAIR OVER THE TOP! HOROWITZ ... JESSE VENTURA: ONE FOOT! JR: YOU'RE RIGHT, ONLY ONE FOOT! MY GOD, WHERE DOES HE GET THE STRENGTH FROM? JV: BUT CAN HE PULL HIMSELF BACK IN? JR: HE ROLLS BACK IN, AS HOROWITZ HAS COLLAPSED IN THE RING, I THINK HE THINKS HE'S WON IT! JV: HE'S ABOUT TO GET A RUDE AWAKENING! JR: FLAIR STUMBLES OVER, GRABS HOROWITZ BY THE HAIR, TURNS HIM AROUND ... AND THROWS HIM OVER THE TOP ... JV: YEEEEEEESSSSSSSS! JR: ... BUT ONLY TO ONE FOOT? JV: WHAT? OH, NO! JR: FLAIR'S STRUTTING IN THE RING, BUT BARRY HOROWITZ PRECARIOUSLY PERCHED ON ONE FOOT ON THE OUTSIDE, HAS YET TO BE ELIMINATED! RIC FLAIR: WHOOOOOOOOOOOOO! JV: NO, RIC, TURN AROUND! JR: HOROWITZ CLIMBS BACK INTO THE RING AS FLAIR WITH THE DOUBLE-BICEP, BARRY HOROWITZ CHARGING WITH A HEAD OF STEAM ... JV: FLAIR SEES HIM! JR: FLAIR TURNS AROUND JUST IN TIME AS HOROWITZ CLOTHESLINES HIM OVER THE TOP, BUT FLAIR PULLS HOROWITZ OVER AS WELL ... JV: ... JR: ... AND BOTH LAND ON ONE FOOT! JV: OH MY GOD! JR: EVERYONE IS STANDING! HOROWITZ AND FLAIR TRADE PUNCHES WITH EACH OTHER, TRYING TO GET THE OTHER DOWN ... JV: UNBELIEVABLE! JR: THIS IS IT! EVERY LAST OUNCE OF ENERGY HAS BEEN CALLED UP BY THESE TWO MEN, HOPING TO KNOCK THE OTHER OVER! JV: I THINK FLAIR'S DOING BETTER, ROSS! HE'S GOT MORE BEHIND HIS PUNCHES! JR: HOROWITZ HAS STOPPED PUNCHING, AS FLAIR WITH REPEATED JABS TO THE HEAD, BARRY'S GLAZED OVER BUT KEEPING HIS BALANCE! OH NO, FLAIR MEASURING UP THE CHEST OF HOROWITZ ... ::SMACK!!:: AND A KNIFE-EDGE CHOP, HOROWITZ LEANS BACK ... JV: IT'S OVER! JR: BARRY HOROWITZ TRYING DESPARATELY TO KEEP HIS BALANCE, LUNGES WITH A ROUNDHOUSE BUT MISSES, FLAIR PICKS HOROWITZ UP FOR A BELLY-TO-BACK ... JV: JUST DROP HIM, FLAIR, THAT'S ALL ... JR: HOROWITZ SLOWLY FALLING BACK, GETS HIS BALANCE AND FLIPS OVER TO ONE FOOT! JV: WHAT? JR: HOROWITZ HOPS UP, GRABS FLAIR'S HEAD AND RAMS IT INTO THE STEEL RING POST! AND FLAIR'S EYES ROLL BACK IN HIS HEAD!!! JV: NO! JR: ... JV: NO!!! JR: ... JV: NNNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO ... :: DING DING DING DING DING DING DING DING DING :: JR: HOROWITZ WINS!!! HOROWITZ WINS!!! HOROWITZ WINS!!! JV: OH GOD, NO! NO!!! JR: LET'S GO TO HOWARD FINKEL FOR THE OFFICIAL ANNOUNCEMENT! FINKEL: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE W ... THE WIN ... THE WINNER ... OF ... (exhales) LADIES AND GENTLEMEN, THE WINNER ... OF THE DREAM ROYAL RUMBLE ... BARRY ... HOROWITZ!!! (Barry's theme music starts up) JR: BARRY HOROWITZ HAS ACCOMPLISHED THE IMPOSSIBLE DREAM! JV: HE MUST BE THE LUCKIEST MAN IN THE HISTORY OF THE EARTH, ROSS! FLAIR HAD IT WON, HE DID! JR: HOROWITZ HAS COLLAPSED OUTSIDE THE RING, REFEREE EARL HEPNER CAN'T RAISE HIS HAND! WHAT A VICTORY! WHAT A MATCH! JV: WHAT LUCK! OH, JUST WAIT UNTIL NEXT YEAR, ROSS! I'M GUARANTEEING MY OWN VICTORY RIGHT NOW! JR: AND WHILE WE HAVE THE OPPORTUNITY, AS BARRY HOROWITZ SLOWLY GETS TO HIS FEET, I WANT TO REMIND YOU FOLKS THAT JANUARY 1, 1998, MARKS THE KICK-OFF OF THE DREAM BATTLEBOWL! EIGHTY OF THE TOP SUPERSTARS OF WRESTLING'S PAST, PRESENT AND FUTURE, FACE OFF TO DETERMINE ONCE AGAIN WHO THE BEST WRESTLER IN HISTORY IS! JV: WAIT A SECOND, DREAM BATTLEBOWL? JR: THAT'S RIGHT, JESSE! JV: THIS ISN'T AN EVERY-MAN-FOR-HIMSELF FORMAT? WHAT HAPPENS IF I GET PAIRED WITH ... DINK? JR: WELL, ANYTHING CAN HAPPEN, JESSE, YOU'RE JUST GONNA HAVE TO HOPE FOR THE BEST, AS [name withheld] HAS COME TO RINGSIDE WITH THE DREAM ROYAL RUMBLE TROPHY ... JV: OR ... OR HOWARD FINKEL? HE'S ELIGIBLE, MY GOD ... JR: BARRY HOROWITZ HAS MADE IT BACK INTO THE RING, AS [name withheld] HANDS HIM THE TROPHY, AND BOTH MEN EMBRACE EACH OTHER, AND JESSE, YOU HAVE TO ADMIT, HOROWITZ EARNED EVERY OUNCE OF THIS VICTORY TONIGHT! JV: WAIT A SECOND, DIDN'T GENE OKERLUND TAG WITH HULK HOGAN ONCE IN THE EIGHTIES? OH GOD ... JR: JESSE, WOULD YOU CONCENTRATE?!? BARRY HAS THE MICROPHONE NOW, LET'S LISTEN IN ... BARRY HOROWITZ: (panting as his theme music fades out) JR: APPARENTLY BARRY NEEDS A BIT LONGER TO CATCH HIS BREATH ... JV: HE'S GONNA HAVE TO CATCH MORE THAN THAT IF I MEET UP WITH HIM IN THE NEXT TOURNAMENT, ROSS! JR: I THINK HE'S READY NOW, LET'S LISTEN ... HOROWITZ: ... THANK YOU! HI MOM! (his theme music starts up again to a renewed pop from the crowd) JV: "HI MOM"?!? JR: WELL, THAT PRETTY MUCH SAYS IT ALL! FOLKS, THAT'S GONNA DO IT FROM ANN ARBOR, AS ... WAIT A SECOND, [name withheld], WHAT ARE YOU DOING IN THE BROADCAST ... [name withheld]: SHUT UP! YOU GUYS THINK YOU'RE THE PREMIER FORCE IN INTERNET WRESTLING, YOU'RE WRONG! LET ME TELL YOU SOMETHING, *I* AM THE ONLY REASON INTERNET WRESTLING IS ALIVE IN THE FIRST PLACE, I *MADE* INTERNET WRESTLING, AND I AM GOING TO *BREAK* THIS TWO-BIT TOURNAMENT! JR: HEY JOE, YOU'RE A LITTLE LATE ... [name withheld]: SHUT UP!!! [name withheld] WILL NEVER BOOK ANOTHER TOURNAMENT AS LONG AS I LIVE, AND AS A MATTER OF FACT, I'M GONNA GO MAKE SURE OF THAT RIGHT NOW! JR: MR. [name withheld] ... [name withheld] HAS JUST LEFT THE ANNOUNCE POSITION, AND HE'S RUNNING TO THE RING, FOLKS, OUR SATTELITE TIME IS UP, FOR JESSE "THE BODY" VENTURA, THIS IS JIM ROSS SAYING SO LONG! (And as [name withheld] rolls into the ring, fade to black.) * * * THE END * * * [name withheld] - [detail withheld] Please respond via e-mail if you want an answer BJORK RULES! - Go Red Wings!
