CWWW — Founding

"CWWW" — founding/recruitment post

6 September 1995
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Welcome to the CWWW, the newest e-wrestling federation. The CWWW, which stands for Championship World Wide Wrestling, has three singles belts, a tag team belt, and even a women's division with a singles belt! There are different matches such as normal, texas death, submission, and two out of three falls! It's getting it's own page where you will be able to see the results of past cards, the superstar list, the current and past title holders, up-do-date info on the league such as different fueds, and schedules of the upcoming cards. Every friday on Friday Fire, there are scheduled matches, and sometimes title matches. Every month there is a card where there can be up to
16 fights and all the titles are defended. Every 2-3 months there is a PPV event, where virtually everyone fights and all the titles are defended. As soon as the CWWW gets enough wrestlers, there will be tournaments for all the different titles, one at a time.
