Comments from the Shadow master!
Well well well, what a bad weekend. The officals in the CWWW must be on some really good drugs after some of the calls this weekend.
Shadow connection lost thier belts, but proved this past wednesday that simpkins and smith were no match with out a drugged up official.
Simpkins when did we ever whine. Did we ever send any statements after
Friday, no. We knew Jamie would see how pathetic his offical was that match. You're the only one whining cause you went and lost the belts!
You couldn't hold them without a jobber offical! Now get lost! There are more worthy opponents out there now!
Loki you learned who not to mess with after your stunt on Friday. You pathetic waste of space. I hope your gods are punnishing you for such a lousy effort friday. Maybe they'll open your mind to what a real wrestler is supposed to be like!
As for Sid Starr, if that's how you have to keep a belt I feel really bad for you. At least my men can win matches without cheating, which is more than I can say about you. As for the Shadow Connection losing their belts...
we all saw Wednesday how the match would have went if the offical Friday wasn't on drugs. There was no cheating in the match Wednesday. Considering
Vorg fought two matches that night, and still faired well against you that night shows alot, because you needed that weapon to win. He had you and you knew it and you were scared! What this all comes down to is you'll see how hard it is one day when you actually have to do a title defense without chaeting. You know you'll lose and we'll be right there to laught in your face!!!
This Sunday Cryptical will enter the 8-man tourny. He will be out to prove himself and I have no doubt he'll do good. This Friday the shadow connection will be out to warm up for a defense on Sunday. They're hard at work training for Sunday, and Friday will be another step in that process.

LOKI

The camera is outside, on a snowcovered cliff plunging into the ocean far below. Loki is standing with his back to the camera, looking out across the water. His cloak and hair are being whipped around by the wind, but other than that he does not move.
[Loki] Aaah, the smell of the ocean. The feel of the forces of nature, ready to rip into you and destroy you if you are not careful....much like a battle Royal. However, Loki has defeated nature many a time, and I intend to stand triumphant at the end of the battle royal as well! Now, you may all feel you have me figured out by this time, but look again. Are you so certain you are right? Am I what I seem to be, or do your eyes decieve you? Have you maybe been fooled by one of the tricks played by the god of trickery?
The camera suddenly spins around 180 degrees quickly, and comes to rest on....a grinning Loki. Dressed exactly as when he stoop with his back to us at the edge of the cliff.

Mr. Terayaki speaks on behalf of the Great Oni:

Ninja Blade will not be left alone as he will be looking for title in tag action or single action."

TULLY ROEBUCK
(scene opens on a west Texas ranch, where a man in dusty chaps and a flannel shirt is busy breaking a wild bronco. When the horse finally submits, he dismounts and walks over to the camera.)
TULLY ROEBUCK: This heah battle royal's got me purty excited. Not as excited as that there filly, but close. You see, if I win this battle royal, and believe me, I will, I'll get one last shot at Simpkins.
'Cause winning this brawl gets me a shot at the North American Champ, the number one contender. And beating him, and takin' his belt, makes me the number one contender. And that gets me another shot at Simpkins.
Takin' his belt is the best way I know how to get him. Gideon's gone home for a while to take care of some business, so I'll be on my own here for a while. That's no problem. So watch out Simpkins, I'm comin' for ya!!
(Roebuck turns his back to the camera, and motions his hands to bring in the next horse.)

Joey: Ok Decimator, you call it in the air.

lets get down to more business. We are starting a stable, so far it will consist of myself, Dead Zone and Decimator any one who wants in just ask.
