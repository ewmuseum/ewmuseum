A confederation of roughly ten member federations that ran an annual tournament to crown fantasy wrestling's own 'undisputed' champion. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The EWWF Alliance called itself, in its own recruiting post of 12 August 1998, “the most famous Alliance of E-Wrestling, The EWWF, the one you see on 1wrestling.com,” and pitched member federations on rec.sport.pro-wrestling on a single shared purpose: joining together “to Crown Fantasy Wrestling's Undisputed World Champion.”

That same post refers back to “the 1997 EWWF Alliance Tournament” as an already-completed prior edition, placing the confederation's own founding no later than 1997, run as an annual tournament across roughly ten member federations rather than a roster of its own. A second tournament round is announced in the same post, run under a bracket naming its member federations, one of them styled EWWF East.

No closing notice survives. The confederation's last surviving attestation is this same 12 August 1998 announcement of its second tournament round; nothing records whether that round was ever completed or who, if anyone, held the undisputed title it was built to crown.
