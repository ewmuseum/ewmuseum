WIC FINALS!!!!

21 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Voiceover: The Deranged Animaniac. This rookie to wrestling first stepped into the ring in the first round of the World Invitaional Championships, and has since taken the wrestling world by storm, defeating everybody in his path en route to the finals of the tournament. Having never fought professionally before, the Animaniac stands two decisions away from becoming the undisputed heavyweight champion. The American Patriot. Known the world over for his techincal skills and his greatness in the ring. This veteran has won 11 world titles in various federations. After beating the Deranged Animaniac in a non-tournament contest, the Patriot felt that he had nothing left to prove, and wanted to give his shot to his friend Tonnage. But, the Patriot was told that he must wrestle. Now, after a controversial loss that cost both him and Tonnage the AWA World Tag Titles, Patriot is now on a mission, and the Animaniac is in his path. Tonight, who will be crowned Undisputed Heavyweight Champion of the World? Derek: Good evening ladies and gentlemen! Welcome to the finals of the World Invitational Championships! Welcome to Madison Square Garden! I'm Derek McMahon, and I'm joined by Robbie "The Genius" Keenan. Robbie: Man, tonight's gonna suck! A couple of low-life losers are going to fight for the world title. At least Patriot had some sense in him to try and get Tonnage in, but the idiots on the board killed that idea. D: So, who do you pick for the title? R: Well, my money is on Patriot, but I really wouldn't be surprised if Animaniac came out and did the unthinkable. D: Well, the title match is going to be a long one, so let's get to the ring for the first fall of the title! A: This contest is for the Undisputed Heavyweight Championship of the World! Here is the stipulation of this part of the match: If Patriot wins, the match will become a best-of-3 falls match. Regardless of who wins, the match will continue at the end of the evening. Now, introducing first, the man that made it through the tournament without a loss: THE DERANGED ANIMANIAC! (Crowd cheers. "Animaniacs" plays over the PA.) A: And his opponent: The man that came out of the one-loss bracket to earn the right to fight for the title: THE AMERICAN PATRIOT! (Crowd erupts into cheers. "Stars and Stripes Forever" plays on the PA.) DING! D: And, in an unusual start, the Animaniac bounces off the ropes and heads for Patriot. R: Of course, Animaniac is unusual. D: I'll have to agree with you there. Animaniac off the ropes and nails Patriot with a clothesline! Animaniac now with a leg drop. Animaniac going for a elbow drop but Patriot rolls out of the way. Patriot picks up Animaniac and slams him to the mat. Here's a cover! 1...2.. kickout. R: Animaniac looking good here in the early going, but how long can he go against the vet? D: Animaniac gets up and locks up with Patriot. Patriot pushes Animaniac to the ropes, but gets pushed away. Patriot now punching Animaniac. Animaniac kicks Patriot. Animaniac picks up Patriot and connects with a huge knee lift. Here's a cover! 1.. easy kickout. R: That was pathetic. It will defintely take a lot more to beat Patriot. D: Patriot gets up and locks up with Animaniac. Animaniac slams Patriot to the mat. Animaniac now climbs to the top rope and waits for Patriot to get up. Patriot goes for a punch, but gets shoved back to the mat. Animaniac comes off the top and connects with a double axe handle! R: Animaniac showing why he should be champ here. But there's still a long way to go. D: Animaniac now with a stomp to the head. Patriot gets up and punches Animaniac. Patriot picks up Animaniac. Patriot now off the ropes. Animaniac lowers his head, but gets a boot in the face. R: BIG rookie mistake there. D: Yes it was. Patriot picks up Animaniac and connects with the Patriot Missile! Here's a cover! 1...2... kickout at the last second! R: Wait! How did that happen? D: Well, remember, Animaniac did get out of both of Patriot's finishers the last time they fought. Animaniac gets up and locks up with Patriot. Patriot with a powerful knee lift on Animaniac. Patriot now slaps on the Revolution! R: THAT'S IT! D: But Animanic won't submit! R: WOW! Animaniac sure is tough, but how long can he go with this guy? D: Only time will tell. Patriot now with an elbow drop. And now a stomp to the head. Patriot goes for a leg drop, but Animaniac rolls out of the way. Both men are up, and Patriot kicks Animaniac. Patriot now drops the leg on Animaniac's face. Patriot tries to get to the top rope, but Animaniac stops him. R: So far, Animaniac has been able to go with Patriot. But, Animaniac never fought a long match in the tournament, so he really went un-tested. D: That's true. This will be a true test for him. Animaniac punches Patriot. Patriot gets up and kicks Animaniac. Patriot trips up Animaniac. Animaniac gets back up and locks up with Patriot. Patriot takes Animaniac and slams him to the mat. Here's a cover! 1...2...3!!! DING! DING! R: So, now we have 3 best-of-3 falls contests tonight. We're going to be here until the end of the week! D: Maybe, if we're lucky. Let's get the official word! A: The winner of this fall, at a time of 9:32 - THE AMERICAN PATRIOT!!! At the end of the evening, both men will come back out and fight in a best-of-3 falls, winner takes all, contest to determine the world champion! (Due to me just wanting to get this out, the other matches will not happen, basically because they were never accepted.) A: This contest is scheduled for the best of three falls, and it will crown the Undisputed Heavyweight Champion of the World! Coming down the aisle: THE DERANGED ANIMANIAC! (Crowd cheers. "Animaniacs" plays on the PA.) A: And his opponent: THE AMERICAN PATRIOT! (Crowd erupts. "Stars and Stripes Forever" plays on the PA.) DING! D: Animaniac starts out by kicking Patriot. R: He looks a little peeved after losing earlier. D: Well, he wants to win the title. What a party they would have at Warner Brothers if he won. Patriot locks up with Animaniac and nails him with a wicked knee. Here's a cover! 1...2.. kickout at 2 1/2. R: I don't think Animanic will get pinned on something like that. Especially this early. D: Animaniac rolls out of the ring, takes a breather,and slides back in. Both men lock up and Patriot shoves Animaniac to the corner. Animaniac pushes Patriot out of the corner. Both men lock up and Animaniac sticks a thumb in Patriot's eye! The ref warns Animaniac. R: Hey... now there's something I can relate to. Maybe Animaniac isn't that bad after all. D: Animaniac now with a huge clothesline on Patriot. Patriot gets up and locks up with Animaniac. Patriot pushes Animaniac to the ropes. Patriot whips Animaniac to the ropes. Animaniac comes off and hits Patriot with a drop kick. Here's a cover! 1.. easy kickout. R: It'll take more than that. D: Animaniac climbs to the top rope, jumps off, and splashes on Patriot. R: Patriot in trouble here. Can Animaniac capitalize here? D: Patriot gets up, only to be punched by Animaniac. Patriot gets back up and kicks Animaniac. Animaniac punches Patriot. Both men lock up and Patriot takes Animaniac down with a fireman's carry. Animaniac gets up and bounces off the ropes. Animaniac comes off and connects with a clothesline. Here's a cover! 1...2.. kickout. R: Well, he tryed to capitalize, but he allowed Patriot to gain some momentum. Might cost him later. D: Patriot gets up, but Animaniac kicks him. Patriot punches Animaniac. Both men lock up, and Patriot rolls up Animaniac into a small package. Here's a cover! 1...2.. kickout at 2 1/2. R: Patriot almost with the first fall there. D: Patriot looking to play the aggressor now. Patriot kicks Animaniac. Animaniac gets up and locks up with Patriot. Patriot smacks Animaniac with a wicked knee. Animaniac trips Patriot. Both men lock up. Patriot goes for a knee lift, but misses and lands on his head. Patriot trips Animaniac. Animaniac gets up and punches Patriot. Both men lock up and Animaniac connects with a suplex. R: I didn't know Animaniac could do that. Maybe he can beat Patriot. D: Animaniac goes for an elbow drop, but Patriot rolls out of the way. Animaniac gets up and kicks Patriot. Patriot rolls out of the ring, takes a breather, and slides back in. Animaniac punches Patriot. Patriot rolls back out of the ring, takes another breather, and slides back in. R: I don't think I've ever seen Patriot beaten up like this. D: Me either. Patriot punches Animaniac. Both men lock up and Animaniac slams Patriot to the mat. R: Why doesn't he cover him? D: I don't know. Animaniac now with a leg drop. Patriot gets up and locks up with Animaniac. Animaniac takes Patriot down with a fireman's carry. Patriot gets up, but gets tripped up by Animaniac. R: Animaniac could have won this fall five times by now. D: Well, I don't know what he's doing. Animaniac with an elbow drop. Animaniac goes for another elbow drop, but Patriot rolls out of the way. Patriot now with a handful of hair, bringing Animaniac to his feet. Animaniac kicks Patriot. Animaniac now with a leg drop. Patriot gets up, but Animaniac punches Patriot. R: COVER HIM! WHAT ARE YOU WAITING FOR???? D: Please, Keenan. Control yourself. Patriot trips up Animaniac. Patriot goes for a leg drop, but Animaniac rolls out of the way. Patriot gets up. Animaniac goes for a punch, but misses completely and lands on his face. R: HA HA HA! Told you! D: Please, Robbie. That was rude. Patriot now with a stomp to the head. Animaniac gets up, but gets tripped by Patriot. Patriot chokes Animaniac. The ref warns Patriot. R: Wow. Patriot breaking the rules. I gotta get my eyes checked. D: Patriot climbs to the top rope. Animaniac gets up, and Patriot climbs down. Animaniac comes up from behind and smashes Patriot's face into the turnbuckle. And again! And a third tim... NO! Patriot blocks it with his foot. Animainac whips Patriot into the corner, but it's reversed. Patriot picks up Animaniac and clotheslines him. Here's a cover! 1.. kickout. R: Patriot needs to come up with more than that right now. D: Animaniac trips Patriot. Patriot gets up, put gets punched by Animaniac. Patriot trips Animaniac. Patriot now with an elbow drop. Animaniac gets up and trips Patriot. Patriot rolls out of the ring and takes Animaniac with him. R: HERE WE GO! LET'S GET IT ON! D: Patriot climbs back in the ring. R: LOSER! D: And Animaniac follows suit. R: C'mon you two. DO SOMETHING! D: Patriot kicks Animaniac. Both men lock up and Animaniac shoves Patriot to the corner. Patriot reverses the move. Patriot whips Animaniac across the ring, and Animaniac hits hard into the turnbuckle. Animaniac gets up and kicks Patriot. Patriot returns the favor. Animaniac bounces off the ropes and connects with a drop kick. Here's a cover! 1...2.. kickout. R: I'm just surprised that Animaniac has gone THIS long. D: Patriot gets up and gets kicked by Animaniac. Animaniac with a stomp to the head. Animaniac picks up Patriot and goes for a clothesline, but Patriot barely misses it. Patriot punches Animaniac. Both men lock up and Animaniac pushes Patriot to the ropes. Patriot nails Animaniac between the eyes. Animaniac gets up and kicks Patriot. Animaniac punches Patriot. Animaniac picks up Patriot but can't do anything. R: How much longer will this match go? I think I missed Thursday! D: Patriot punches Animaniac. Animaniac gets up and bounces off the ropes towards Patriot. Patriot puts his head down, and Animaniac kicks him in the face. R: COVER HIM! NOW! D: No cover yet. Animaniac whips Patriot to the ropes. Patriot comes off and gets clotheslined! But still no cover! Animaniac climbs to the top rope. Patriot gets up and heads to the corner. Patriot punches Animaniac, knocking him to a seated position. R: Animaniac will be singing soprano for a while. D: Patriot punches Animaniac. Patriot takes Animaniac and connects with a huge superplex! Here's a cover! .. kickout! R: How could he get out of that? D: I don't know. Animaniac gets up but gets tripped by Patriot. Patriot goes for a leg drop, but Animaniac rolls out of the way. Animaniac picks up Patriot and slams Patriot. Here's a cover! 1...2...3!!! DING! R: FINALLY! Someone won a fall here! D: But how much more can these two take. Let's get the offical word! A: The winner of the first fall: THE DERANGED ANIMANIAC! D: Well, these two get a minute rest. R: Yeah, like that's going to be enough after the punishment they dished out to each other. D: We'll see. I'm surprised that the match has gone this long, seeing that Animaniac never really had a long match in the tourney. R: Me too. I don't think he can go much longer. D: We'll see. DING! D: And we start the second fall. Animaniac punches Patriot. Patriot kicks Animaniac. Animaniac trips Patriot. Patriot rolls out of the ring, takes a breather, and slides back in, but Animaniac stomps on his head. Animaniac chokes Patriot. The ref warns Animaniac. R: Animaniac looking to end it now. D: Animainac goes for a leg drop, but Patriot rolls out of the way. Both men lock up and Animaniac nails Patriot with a suplex. Patriot trips Animaniac. Patriot climbs to the top rope, jumps off, and splashes on Animaniac. Here's a cover! 1...2...3!!! DING! R: OH NO! NOT ANOTHER FALL! D: OH YES! One more fall to decide the world champ! Let's get the official word! A: The winner of the second fall, tying up the contest at one fall a piece, THE AMERICAN PATRIOT! R: Well, I don't think Animaniac has anything left in him. D: I don't know. He is defintely showing a lot of resilience. I think Patriot has been got off-guard, and is just now getting on track. It may be too late for the Patriot. DING! D: And here we go, the final fall of the 1997 WIC! Patriot bounces off the ropes and connects with a drop kick. Here's a cover! 1...2.. kickout at 2 1/2. R: That was close. Almost a three there. D: Very close. Animainac trips Patriot. Patriot gets up and locks up with Animaniac. Patriot pushes Animaniac to the ropes. Patriot now whipping Animaniac to the other side. Animaniac bounces off and gets elevated by a huge back drop from Patriot. Here's a cover! 1...2... kickout at the last second! R: Too close for me! I almost had a heart attack there. D: You and me both. Both of these men deserve to be champ. Animaniac trips Patriot. Animaniac stomps on Patriot's head. Patriot gets up and punches Animaniac. Animaniac punches Patriot. Patriot trips Animaniac. Animaniac gets up and kicks Patriot. Patriot punches Animaniac. Patriot chokes Animaniac. The ref warns Patriot. R: Both wrestlers have been warned twice in this contest, but I don't think the ref will disqualify either of them at this point. D: Neither do I. They've come too far to win or lose on a technicality. Patriot picks up Animaniac and slams him to the mat. Here's a cover! 1...2.. kickout! R: I think I have a new-found respect for Animaniac. He's proved that he can go a long time here. Especially after having short matches in the earlier rounds. D: Patriot goes for a stomp, but Animaniac rolls out of the way and gets up. Both men lock up and Patriot shoves Animaniac to the corner. Patriot pounds away on Animaniac. Patriot hip tosses Animaniac out of the corner. Here's a cover! 1...2.. kickout. R: WOW! What intestinal fortitude on the part of Animaniac. Most guys would have thrown in the towel by now. D: Well, Animaniac wants that title. Animaniac gets up and kicks Patriot. Patriot kicks Animaniac. Animaniac gets up and kicks Patriot. Animaniac goes for a leg drop, but Patriot rolls out of the way. Animaniac trips Patriot. Animaniac climbs to the top rope.
