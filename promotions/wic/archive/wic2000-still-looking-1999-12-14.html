WIC2000 — Still Looking for Participants

14 December 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hey! We're still out there looking for guys that are brave enough to step into the ring and take on the best of e-wrestling. If you're interested, go to
http://members.tripod.com/~SoulPlayer/index.html and sign up! Now, I know that a lot of people give this tourney a bad rap. And, that in the 4 other years that this tourney has run, only 2 times was it completed.
But, as in any thing else, you have to have the people to participate. If the people in the event don't participate in the tourney, why would you keep the thing going that year? You can ask anyone that has been in the tourney the times that it finished. No bad thing comes out of their mouths. It's from the people that unfortunately were there when we had to cancel. AND I
DON'T WANT TO DO THAT AGAIN! So, I place this personal challenge to ANYONE that has EVER been in the WIC. All you big mouths. If you think you're such a big shot ... if you think you've got what it takes ... come back up your words. Join WIC2000! Come have a good time! I promise those that join will have a great time!
