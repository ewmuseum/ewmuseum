Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Generation Wrestling described itself as running since 1 April 1998, and opened its own arena in Jersey City, New Jersey eleven days later with the first Sunday Night Showtime — a studio-style weekly broadcast with its own two-man announce team, Danny Huckins and Tim Robinson, bickering through the call, and ring announcer Robert Lux.

That first broadcast's opening match, billed by the announcers as the fed's own owner wrestling under the nickname “Sexy Boy,” ended in a submission loss to “Malibu” Mike Madison; a debuting wrestler, Setzer Van Strife, then ran in with a steel chair, calling it “Sexy Boy's” last match in the promotion and driving him through the ring announcers' table.

“The Irishman” Shamus O'Leary beat The Arsonist by submission with the Celtic Clover in the next match, cutting a promo first aimed at a rival he called Joey Dee. K-Solo won the match after by disqualification once his opponent, Zeb Williams, bloodied him with a steel chair rather than let the match end clean; a fourth match, a tag bout pitting Iron Maniac and Nutcase against the Hellions, opened with a pre-bell brawl before the surviving broadcast breaks off.

The promotion kept running a regular Sunday card into the fall. Its own last surviving document is a former member's November 1998 post recalling the promotion in the past tense and asking what had become of its wrestlers; no closure notice of its own survives.
