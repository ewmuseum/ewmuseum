Hometown: New Orleans, LA Music: Jimi Hendrix's "Voodoo Chile" Manager: Xavier Corleone (6 4, 275, former wrestler, about 38 years old. Three piece suits and stylish) Type: Blends power and science into devestating mold Finisher setup: Smokestack Lightning (Like a snap suplex, except a snap brain buster) Finisher: Blues Breaker (Swinging full nelson, resulting in either a submission or, by slamming the opponent down back-of-the-head first after spinning, a pinfall) Bio: AN overwhelming combination of power and science, Justice "The Conqueror" Joliet (real name Charles Justice Joliet) is one of wrestling brightest new stars. Born and bred in the bayous of Louisiana, not much is known about Joliet's life before he was discovered in a bar a few blocks from Bourbon Street in New Orleans by a vacationing Xavier Corleone. It is known that he has a college-or-higher education background, is into blues music (having even played in a few bands) and has a sports background from high school. Joliet comes to the ring with black pants-type tights, with the words FOR ALL on the back of them. He wears a long Old-West style riding coat to the ring, and dons blue-lensed John-Lennon type sunglasses. Extremely charismatic, is adored by the fans for being striaght forward and to the oint at all times, never pandering or talking down. Can be a little crazy in interviews at times, interviewing with a bit of a Roddy-Piper style, but perhaps a bit more focused. He is contrasted by Xavier Corleone, who has a quite but intense demanor. A former wrestler, Corleone _ a master of thousands of wrestling holds _ gave up his career to guide others. He has chosen Joliet as his latest protegee, and has shared with him a wealth of scientific and strategic knowledge. Rarely is an opponent's weaknesses or tendencies not noticed by Corleone -- a strength that has carried over into Joliet. Corleone has bleneded Joliet's natural power with intensive scientific training, confident that his man has the combination of charisma, intensity, inteligence, and talent to lead wrestling into the 21st century.
