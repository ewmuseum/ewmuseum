[For those who don't know these guys, let me lay it down.] [Sitting all the way to the left (your left) is a man who's eyebrows do all the talking for him. Estimating this character is around twenty-eight or so, one would guess that he's a very opionated and liberal announcer. Wrong. Sam
Braxton is his name and he is the epitome of conservativism. "Tradition is best, and heroes ALWAYS prevail," is his motto.]
[To the far right of the table (your right, of course) is the exact opposite of
Braxton. "Merciless" Marcus Mitchells, former pro-wrestler, now wise-cracker is this man. Wearing nothing more than a T-shirt with a pair of faded, torn jeans, he runs his hand through what little hair he has left on his scarred head, lets out a smile brightened souly by the caps that hide the gaps, and prepares to speak out against just about EVERYTHING.]
[But everything requires a moderator. A system of checks and balances. Seperation of powers. Centered between these opposing forces is none-other than "The Sexiest Voice in Wrestling" Chris Wilson. Though not as uptight as
Braxton, and by far not as lax as Mitchells, Wilson dresses as he pleases, yet maintains a fairly decent composure about himself. Today he is wearing a purple velvet jacket with a blue interior. Don't worry. He's not gay.]
CW: Fans, hello and welcome to the World-Wide Wrestling Association's first official "Clear-the-Lane: HANGOVER"!
SB: Yes. Out with the old, in with the new. MM: (laughing) What do you know of change? You've had the same hair- cut since senior-year.
CW: Well, with all things aside, this premier event will be going into all aspects about the World-Wide Wrestling Association and it's won- derful roster and amazing staff.
SB: Let's start with "Clear the Lane"! CW: Ah, yes. "Clear the Lane" which happened merely a night ago showed us all kinds of suprises!
SB: Especially in that Royal Rumble! MM: I can't believe both Jensen AND Agony got the victory! What a pile of horse-crap! SB: That's right! And both must face each other on the upcoming "Clear the Lane". CW: Yes. And that brings us to our first match-up scheduled for "Clear the Lane" THIS Sunday, February 28th, 1999.

Two cut-outs of King Agony and Timothy Jensen stand side by side with a large "VS" seperating them.
"Boardwalk" has on the standard Hawaiian shirt and tan khakis. Agony is just as sadistic looking as usual, with his half-mask and mis-matched wardrobe.]
CW: Last night, at the FIRST WWWA "Clear the Lane" Royal Rumble, both Agony and Jensen fought to a stand still. However, just as the two were trying to throw each other over the ropes, the lights went out!
SB: I remember! Truly, frightening. MM: Yeah, folks, I had to hold Sam's hand and tell 'em it was alright to keep watching. SB: Hrmph. CW: As the houselights came back on, we found ourselves joined by one half the owners of the World-Wide Wrestling Association, Lance
Younger! SB: And Jensen and Agony were knocked clear out of the ring! CW: Yes. Let's go to the clip... [CLIP: February 21st, 1999 "Clear the Lane" - Royal Rumble.] SB: HE'S PICKING HIM UP! HE'S GONNA THROW AGONY OVER! CW: AGONY'S RESISTING WITH ALL HIM MIGHT! MM: COME ON JENSEN! AT LEAST YER KINDA BETTER THAN JENSEN! CW: Jensen is breaking away... NOW AGONY'S TRYING! SB: THEY'RE BOTH ABOUT TO GO OVER! CW: WHO'LL DO IT?! WHO'LL... [SUNUVA DINGY-DING-DONG!] [THE LIGHTS WENT OUT!] [DON'T YOU HATE IT WHEN SHIT LIKE THIS HAPPENS?!?] CW: SOMEONE DO SOMETHING? [CLICK!] CW: LANCE YOUNGER?! [Standing beside the commentation booth with a mic in hand is none other than one-half of the WWWA's owners, Lance "I hate you!" Younger.]
LY: Too bad isn't it... SB: What's he doing here? MM: I gotta better question for you? WHERE IS JENSEN AND AGONY?!? [Neither man can be seen in the ring. Apparently, they have disappeared.] LY: ...both of you lose. All that work. All that hard, HARD work... down the drain. [Can we say... "BOOOOOO!"?] LY: King Agony and "Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen... both eliminated. But who eliminated who first?!
[Just in front of the entrance way, King Agony and "Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen stand up. They shake away the cobwebs from their head and notice that they have both been knocked out of the ring.]
[END CLIP.] [Back to a wide-shot of all three men sitting behind the large table.] CW: And that's how it all went down last night. SB: Was it Lance Younger that attacked them? Or someone else? MM: Maybe the doofuses just fell out of the ring. CW: That could very well be, Marc. SB: It's still impressive that these two men will be facing each other all over again this upcoming Sunday.
CW: That it is. And let's not forget what's up for grabs here. Whoever wins this match-up goes on to face "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh later on in the telecast.
MM: In a steel cage, too! Oh oh oh. [That's "Merciless' hardcore laugh". You only here it when he's overly excited over someone else's pain.]
CW: That's right, Marc. Escapes only by A: climbing over or B: going through the door on the far side of the ring.
SB: So it's "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh vs. either "Boardwalk" Timothy
Jensen or King Agony - two guys which detest Walsh?
CW: Apparently so. MM: (smacking Chris on the back of the head) You forgot something! CW: I'd really appreciate it if you didn't that, Marc. MM: Yadda yadda yadda - ANYWAYS! How can you forget about the stipulations that have been added to the match!
CW: Ah yes! Roll the clip! We'll get word from the co-Presidents themselves! [CLIP: February 21st, 1999 "Clear the Lane" - Post-Royal Rumble.] [Lance and Dave Younger, the WWWA co-Presidents (and family feudees, mind you) are both in the arena. Dave stands at the top with mic in hand, as Lance stands just to the right of Marc Merciless down at ring-side.]
LY: But I have ANOTHER stipulation... DY: (turning back) Yes? LY: _If_ my quote, unquote "boyfriend", "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh wins the cage match and escapes and moves on to the pay-per-view...
[Dave raises his eyebrow.] LY: You... Dave... will be forced... to... RESIGN! [Lots of boos go through the crowd. Dave merely shakes his head.] DY: Resign? Perhaps you think I fear that. Especially since I didn't want the fed to begin with...
LY: Do you accept? DY: Yes... LY: (laughing) YES! Then let it - DY: (holding up a finger) WAIT A SEC, will you?! If Jensen or Agony are to win this event...
LY: What? I gotta resign? DY: Nope. LY: THEN WHAT? DY: Agony or Jensen will get exactly FIVE minutes in the ring with you directly afterwards!
[THE BIGGEST MOTHERFAWKING POP EVER!] LY: Wuh... wuh.. what? DY: DO YOU ACCEPT?!? LY: I... I... DY: DON'T BE A CHICKEN, LANCE! LY: (pissed off once more) FINE! WHATEVER! IAN WALSH WILL WIN AND I'LL ONLY HAVE TO SEE YOU AT THANKSGIVING! DY: (smirking) Thanks, Lance. I knew you'd see it my way. [END CLIP.] [Goes back to the studio.] CW: So you heard it here first, folks. CW: Well, we just got a look in on the current roster/rankings of the World-Wide Wrestling Association. SB: Back to the match-ups! CW: Right! Well, with an excellent opener which will lead up into that amazing main event, the WWWA is giving the fans four more powerful matches on the same night!

CW: de Sade is one, yes. MM: Woo hoo! CW: de Sade shocked the WWWA fans, as well as "Brutal" Brian Floyd, when he came into the Royal Rumble dressed as the second of what we would learn would be THREE, men dressed as Ghost-Faced Killer.
SB: King Agony, Ethan de Sade and the original? CW: Yes. MM: Agony's always ripping the ideas from the masters. CW: Well, let's take a look at de Sade's shocking appearance last night on "Clear the Lane".
[CLIP: February 21st, 1999 "Clear the Lane" - Royal Rumble.] ## HUSH! HUSH! I heard you calling my name again, now! ## CW: What the? [As the three men in the ring try to stand, the next entrant into the WWWA
Royal Rumble moves his way down to the ring. From head to toe, he's dressed in black rags all except for one thing: his white, screaming face.]
CW: THAT'S... THAT'S GHOST-FACED KILLER?!? [Yes. It's a _second_ Ghost-Faced Killer.] SB: But there already is a Ghost-Faced Killer in the ring! [The second Ghost-Faced Killer slides into the ring and points at the now- standing "Brutal" Brian Floyd. Immediately, he takes hold of the top of his mask and removes it. Dark, rueful look. Cropped dark mass of hair spiked up in an unruly, "razored" look. Groomed, sharpened sideburns curve down to his jawbone. Squared jaw, granite features. Cold, detached eyes. Eyes filled with a sort of killer's amusement. Murder's eyes. Has a perpetual half sneer/smirk carved onto his lips.]
CW: OH MY GOD! IT'S... IT'S... [!!!] CW: ETHAN DE SADE! ETHAN DE SADE IS IN THE WORLD-WIDE WRESTLING ASSO- CIATION! THIS IS UNPRECEDENTED! [Floyd is in shock. This is the man who challenged him on "Say-It-Ain't-So" and this is the man who is about to kick his ass!] [!!!] CW: DE SADE JUST CLOTHESLINED FLOYD OVER THE TOP-ROPE AND FELL WITH HIM! BOTH ARE ELIMINATED! AMAZING! AMAZING! [END CLIP.] CW: What a powerful debut for one Ethan de Sade.
