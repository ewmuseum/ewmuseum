Well, this week's card should be much better. Another thing about last week, I had to write a match for one of the feds I handle in - the sucker was 43k without introductions. So as you can imagine, I was burnt out on writing ideas. This week though, should rule.

Thanks for writing guys. 2) "CLEAR THE LANE" RECAP ************ DARK MATCHES ************ (Will not count on your record, Dammit! They also count for shit angle wise. Basically, it's like a supercool idea put to use.) 8-MAN TAG MATCH OF DOOM! TEAM DAVE: "Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen "Brutal" Brian Floyd "Sensational" Steven Green King Agony -V- TEAM LANCE: "The Genius" Alfred Scarsone Big Daddy Dave "The Enigma" Joe Hutchings "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh Ended with King Agony and Walsh battling back to the locker rooms, Floyd chasing Lance Younger around the ring with a steel chair, Big Daddy Dave throwing salt at damn near everyone, Green and Hutchings pounding each others faces in in the audience, and Jensen acting like he's the top-dawg. "The
Genius" was able to nail Big Daddy Dave with the figure-four headlock, which he calls the Braincell Killer, and get the submission victory. Yee-uh. The fans marked out like some muthafuckas, too.
************ PART ONE: - Clips from 01-24-99 "Clear the Lane". King Agony knocking Lance Younger silly with the end of a steel chair. - Introduction. - Lance Younger comes out and blabs about how everything sucks for him and how there will be no main event. He claims that _he_ didn't book it, so there is no point in having it. Dave Younger then comes out and tells his bro' that
_he_ scheduled it. Naturally, Lance throws a shit-fit. - Promo for the World-Wide Wrestling Association's first pay-per-view, appropriately titled: "GENESIS." - Todd Phillips introduces the "Sensational" Steven Green. Green disses his combatant in the tournament, "The Enigma" Joe Hutchings, and then starts singing. Weird guy.
PART TWO: - BIG DADDY DAVE VS. "THE HIGHLAND WOLF" TIM MACGREGOR Big Daddy Dave fell easily to the "Wolf" feeling the sting of his top-rope jackhammer, the Thunderclap.
- "Crazy" Chris Hurt entered the arena and made it clear that he's not only crazy, but he has a rather dirty mouth. - Back in the locker rooms, Brant Johnson and Alex Thomas of For Unlawful Carnal Knowledge get their asses kicked by The Teen Age Killers, Hook and Ghost. - DAVID ST. CLAIR VS. "THE REBEL" TONY PRICE St. Clair showed that he's just not some loser as he defeated his opponent, scoring his first victory in the WWWA. He used his Daftendirekt DDT to nail
Price to the matt, and got the one, two, three for duh win. - Lance is on the phone over the jumbotron. And no, it's not phone sex! He's talking with booker, Thomas Plagam about some Royal Rumble. What's it for? Who knows and who cares? Those things rule. PART THREE: - Dave Younger introduces the first person he's hired for the WWWA. "Brutal" Brian Floyd. Apparently, Dave is trying to make some kind of hardcore feel to the fed. - GHETTO THUNDERSTORM VS. THE TEEN AGE KILLERS Well... it wasn't really a match at all. As GT came down to the ring, Ghost and Hook came out dressed as fans (not very good disguises, mind you!) The two then proceeded to attack Devon and Deon until two security guards broke them up! However, these weren't security guards at all! It was Brant Johnson and
Alex Thomas ready to get some pay-back on TAK. However, Ghetto Thunderstorm quickly didn't like that notion at all and all three teams got into a big brawl. The match was declared a no contest.
- Ken Warren talked some smack about "The Genius". - "THE GENIUS" ALFED SCARSONE VS. "ADAMIUS PRIME" ADAM MOWERY "Transformers, more than meets the eyes!" This guy Mowery was weird, odd and just plain ludicrous. However, he was unable to defeat "The Genius" who made the man submit with the Braincell Killer.
- The Genius then talked some smack about Ken Warren. PART FOUR: - "Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen and Dave Younger /w some hot chick came down to the ring to do commentation. - MAIN EVENT "CRAZY" CHRIS HURT VS. "SUPERSTAR" KEN WARREN VS. TREY LAWRENZE Well, Warren immediately got in the ring and started beating on Lawrenze. Hurt then came down in a straigh jacket (bloomin' weirdo). Warren threw him up and over the top-rope, spilling him to the outside - in turn, Chris Hurt was able to free himself somehow.
The match progressed to the outside, and Trey Lawrenze started zapping everyone. As he started showboating it, Hurt and Warren kicked the crap out him, zapped _him_ and did a two-man pin to get the victory.
With Lawrenze dead, these two started fighting it out big time, turning it into a one-on-one match-up. Warren was even able to get a ladder out and set it up.
They fought all over the place, knocking each other silly with chairs on some occasions! Finally, they got back to the ladder, and Hurt was about to do his Straight Jacket move (vertical suplex into a brainbuster) when Warren fought his way lose, causing the ladder to tip over! Both men fell into the ring.
Dwight Mallory, the ref of the match, made the three count, but since both men were down and have a hand over the other one, they ended up pinning each other.
They BOTH won. - Lance came out then, and bitched about how "this just can't happen." Mallory, the ref, said he was old and tired and then said something around the lines of, "THIS MATCH WILL BE A DOUBLE-VICTORY! OH HELL YEAH! BECAUSE DWIGHT
MALLORY SAID SO!"... well... not exactly. Dave finally got Lance to agree on a
3-way match the upcoming week between "Boardwalk" Timothy Jensen, "Crazy" Chris
Hurt", and "Superstar" Ken Warren. Woo!
- "Boardwalk" then came into the ring and threw a Monopoly card at both of them. It's okay though, they both dodged out of the way in time; but when they read it, it said, "PROCEED DIRECTLY TO BOARDWALK." Oh... scary!
3) THE UNIFIED WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT Well, if you haven't heard about it yet - WHERE'VE YOU BEEN!?! The Unified
World Title, the number one goal for all World-Wide Wrestling Associates is being held up at the top of a ladder - but not just any ladder! Instead of metal prongs or steps, you must go through fifteen mean, viscious,
I-spit-on-puppies-cuz-it's-fun wrestlers that want that thing just as much as you! Can you do it? Find out in the upcoming weeks of "Clear the Lane" from the WWWA!
In the end, there can only be one. Find out who at WWWA: Genesis when the two men who make it to the top, battle it out to see who's hot and who's going to get their ass kicked.
