Table of Contents: 1) Words from "The Boss" 2) "Clear The Lane" Recap 3) The Unified World Title Tournament 4) Rosters and Rankings 5) Upcoming Shows 6) Contact Information 1) WORDS FROM THE BOSS Dear WWWAers: Hrmph. Well, I didn't get as much out of the card participants as I did on the debut card, but hell - it was still a helluva card, eh? =)
Naturally, I have made a promise to myself. I will give out matches like so: A) if I see a roleplay by you on "Say-It-Ain't-So" the day before the informer is released, viola! You get a match the following week. And B) if you're new,
I'll see how much you want it by giving you a debut match and testing you out.
Now, I do understand that this whole mess with "Lightning Flashes" may have caused some greif, and that's fine. But for some of you, if I don't see a flash from you for this upcoming "Say-It-Ain't-So" you may either A) give me a flash or B) pack your suitcase. =)
I'm not a mean prez by any means. In fact, I'm purdy darn cool, yo yo yo. But, I do want to a run a fed at the optimum of my ability and that means that
I'll need your help as well.
Thanks for staying with us and I hope to make the trip as comfortable as possible! =) - "The Boss" [name withheld] PS: [name withheld] kicks ass. Thanks to her for writing BDD/Enigma. 2) "CLEAR THE LANE" RECAP ************ DARK MATCHES ************ "Ice Man" Craig Hudak -P- The Man Who Claims He's Bob Dole (fed jobber) "Adamius Prime" Adam Mowery -S- Decepto the Con Man (fed jobber) "The Highland Wolf" Tim MacGregor -P- "Lounge Lizard" Larry Lipkin (fed jobber) "The Enigma" Joe Hutchings -P- Jimmy the Pizza Guy (fed jobber) "Lightning Feet" Deon Langston (of Ghetto Thunderstorm) -P- BMG (fed jobber) ************ PART ONE: - Introduction with those fabulous Younger Brothers! It is now very apparent that there is a family feud going on over the organization you know and love. - Opening credits. Do do do... down! Get down! - Once again The Youngers making that all important announcement that "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh and King Agony would be fighting in the main event later that evening. Okay, duh. We know that. He also mentions that at the first ppv, "Genesis" two men will go to war over the title.
- For Unlawful Carnal Knowledge makes their ways out and talks smack about that new deadly tag team rumored to be a part of the WWWA. They act very gay and have women rant over them. I wish I could do that with the same results.
- Just before For Unlawful Carnal Knowledge got to fight The Teen Age Killers, Ghost and Hook of TAK had some rather threatening comments to dish out. Afterwards Ghost used his devastating judo chop to finish the match early... - TEEN AGE KILLERS -V- FOR UNLAWFUL CARNAL KNOWLEDGE ...but good thing for Alex Thomas of Carnal Knowledge who got the move Ghost calls the "STAB!", it was a lame, lame, LAME finisher. Thomas and partner,
Brant Johnson, easily nailed a quick victory with their powerbomb-neckbreaker combo called Extracurricular Activity.
- For the first of many times in the evening, "Human Torture Rack" Ian Walsh talks about demises. The fans respond with boos. Good for the fans. - Doh! As Walsh started to go backstage, he was jumped by King Agony. The two brawled it out until they were seperated by security and refs and stuff. Then, Lance Younger came out and bitched at everyone. Good for Lance. PART TWO: - Ever wonder who makes up what matches are going to happen? Well, we met those guys. Beavis, Butthead and Eric Cartmen (aka The Bookers) all were in a frenzy over some decision they had to make. Finally, they made their annoying selves dissappear on a search for Dave Younger.
- BIG DADDY DAVE -V- "THE ENIGMA" JOE HUTCHINGS Well, Big Daddy Dave is nothing without salt. The Big guy felt the wrath of
"The Enigma" and in the end, Big Daddy Dave fell to Hutchings' Shooting Star
Press that he likes to call "the Extinction Level Event." Woo-ha!
- "Superstar" Ken Warren appears and says some junk about "The Genius" Alfred Scarsone for no apparent reason. Later he dissapears. I smell a feud. - Then, King Agony came out to his cool theme song (hmmm... the lyrics look a bit different). Anyway, he basically said what Walsh said but vice-versa. PART THREE: - "LIGHTNING FEET" DEON LANGSTON -V- CHRIS WILLIAMS This would have been a match in Langston's favor if there had not been a rather rude interruption from Ghost and Hook of TAK. The two "killers" attacked both men, but in the end, a save from newcomer Devon Henderson came in very handy for Deon Langston. Oh, and more bitching from Lance Younger.
- More Ian Walsh. More demises. - At the end of the Walsh interview, those three nerdy bookers came onto the scene asking if he'd seen Dave Younger. Walsh hadn't seen him, so the three continued down the hallway asking other people like "The Enigma" Joe Hutchings and Judy Dallas. Finally, a very scary man in the shape of BMG who was currently trying to open his locker without a comination, pointed the three in the ring direction. Very odd fellow, that BMG.
PART FOUR: - "BOARDWALK" TIMOTHY JENSEN -V- "THE GREATEST EVER" SHAWN WALSH A "great" debut for Shawn Walsh, but not "the greatest ever". In the end,
Walsh found himself having to pay the toll of being on "Boardwalk" when Timothy
Jensen nailed him with his Tiger Driver/Doctor Bomb thingie he calls "The
Dreambuster." Does that guy kick booty or what?
- MAIN EVENT: "HUMAN TORTURE RACK" IAN WALSH -V- KING AGONY Well, this match started out pretty darn harsh for King Agony. Agony fell victim to an onslaught of attacks from Ian Walsh. Pretty much, the guy got his ass kicked for the first ten minutes. Finally, though, Agony got the upperhand again and started beating on Walsh. The two just took it out on each other until there was nothing else to take out. But in the end, Walsh was able to apply The Guillotine to make King Agony pass out...
PART FIVE: - But was The Guillotine enough? The bell rang before Agony's hand could be dropped a third time. Whew! It was a time-limit draw. - Walsh didn't know this and started bragging... however, Kevin Del Corazon and the ref, Jim Dawson told him off. - Lance Younger came out and said that he _WILL_ have a winner. So he threw out the time-limit all together. - MAINT EVENT PART 2: "HUMAN TORTURE RACK" IAN WALSH -V- KING AGONY Sigh. The two fell out of the ring and started fighting in the audience. They both were counted out.
- Lance, still wanting his winner told them that falls would count anywhere... back to fighting again... - MAIN EVENT PART 3: "HUMAN TORTURE RACK" IAN WALSH -V- KING AGONY The two fought hard and long. Both went up the aisle back and forth with a ton of nearfalls. Then, they fought in the unused section of the Continental
Airlines Arena. After that they battled it out backstage and had some kick-butt spots. But that's where Agony got the upperhand. Soon after Agony threw Walsh onto his shoulders and carried him back to the arena. But before
Agony could get to the ring, Walsh was able to slip out of the Fireman's Carry and get ready to do his switchblade kick. Agony prevented it though with a well-placed chairshot the head. Younger immediately called for the bell though, DQing Agony.
- Lance Younger got in the face of Agony and told him that falls-count-anywhere and no-time-limit were the only rules. There was never a no DQ rule. Naturally, Agony got pissed and played Tee-ball with Lance's head.
He then left. - The End. 3) THE UNIFIED WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT Well, if you haven't heard about it yet - WHERE'VE YOU BEEN!?! The Unified
World Title, the number one goal for all World-Wide Wrestling Associates is being held up at the top of a ladder - but not just any ladder! Instead of metal prongs or steps, you must go through fifteen mean, viscious,
I-spit-on-puppies-cuz-it's-fun wrestlers that want that thing just as much as you! Can you do it? Find out in the upcoming weeks of "Clear the Lane" from the WWWA!
In the end, there can only be one. Find out who at WWWA: Genesis when the two men who make it to the top, battle it out to see who's hot and who's going to get their ass kicked.
