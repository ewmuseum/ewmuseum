WWWA'S Illustrious Leader -- The Man You Call "Boss" Well, if you didn't know by now, my name is [name withheld]. I've been up and down the world of e-wrestling and I can tell you this: it can be the best thing in the world to do, and it can be the worst. We've all had federations that have been just great, and equally we've had federations that just plain sucked.
I hope for The World-Wide Wrestling Association to be the best it can possibly be. With what I feel is a tight booking format, the WWWA should run rather smoothly. All in all, I see it like this: if you work with me and help out by participating, you will not be dissatisfied.
Just have fun, guys. The World-Wide Wrestling Association's here for just that and nothing else.
- [name withheld] 2. How Things Work - Strats and Roleplays My job is to give you the shows. Your job is to roleplay and strat for these shows. WWWA is what is considered a closed-style roleplaying fed, meaning that you send the roleplays directly to the president, and he publishes them all together once or twice a week. I do this on two cards: "Flash Flood" and
"Lightning Flashes" - this gives everyone oppurtunity enough to get at least one flash in a week. How well and sometimes how often you roleplay is half of the determining factor of how I judge match decisions. People who roleplay creatively and when they're supposed to will always triumph over those who's roleplays are boring and stale and sent in once every two weeks (the minimum requirement).
Roleplays aren't the only thing that decides on who wins or loses - strategies do too. A strategy is a lot like the basic ring plan your wrestler carries to the ring with them. It conists of a few things: entrance (in case your wrestler does anything special), basic strategy (how your wrestler works in the ring from beginning to end), spots (certain occurences in the match that stand out from the rest) and possible finishers (how you end the match if it goes in your favor or not). Strategies, though optional, will be a big part in winning a match. An active roleplayer who doesn't strat will probably not triumph over another active roleplay who does strat.
All roleplays must be labeled like so: [WWWA] Roleplay for *wrestler's name* All strategies must be labeled like so: [WWWA] Strategy for *wrestler's name* - *card name and date* 3. Rules and Regulations The World-Wide Wrestling Association only has three big rules: A) You must be an active participant. In no manner will those who take up space on the roster only to not roleplay be accepted. It's a waste of my time as well as others. The minimum requirement for roleplays is two weeks. You don't roleplay for two weeks, you're fired. Bottom line.
B) All booking decisions are final. When a match decision is made, and it doesn't suit you, you may e-mail me about any concerns you may have. But I will not listen to bitching, complaining, whining or just plain arguing over it. Me, being The President, means what I say goes.
C) Get along with the other handlers. I've seen feds get literrally torn apart by handlers who just couldn't get along with on another. Whether it be in a chat room for all of the handlers to meet in or just by mail, you should always get along with the other players. WWWA is here for fun, not fighting.
All of these rules will be enforced with suspensions and firings. I hate to sound strict in this aspect, but if these are not abided by everyone, the fed will not have the quality it should have.
3. Titles and Shows Currently there are three shows a week. Here they are with a breif description: "Clear The Lane" This is the main show - the "RAW" or "Nitro" of the WWWA. As of now, it will appear on Sundays giving me the weekend to peice it together. On it, five to six fully written out matches will appear along with a collection of in-rings and other sorts of "regular wrestling happenings".
"Flash Flood" This show will appear before "Clear The Lane" to host roleplays and flashes that don't really fit well on the main card. These will often be peiced together in no particular format - aka just pasting the roleplays.
"Lighting Flashes" On Wednesdays (for now) there will be a show which will host both roleplays and about 2-3 "squash"-style matches. This card will be sort of like "Shotgun Saturday" or "WCW: World Wide", not really being that important (jobber matches!).
As of now, there are only three titles that are in the WWWA. These being: WWWA Unified World Title: This is the big one! Every WWWA wrestler's goal is to become the Unified World champion!
WWWA American Title: Not as big as the world, but hell, it's a title! And since there's only two singles titles this one will be as sought after as the
Unified World.
WWWA Tag Team Titles: For those that utilize their skills with a partner, here is your key to fame!
5. WWWA's Tentative Schedule -- Subject to Change Right now, the schedule appears in this format: Monday - Nothing planned for Mondays. Watch RAW. :) Tuesday - Flashes due for "Lighting Flashes" Wednesday - "Lighting Flashes" Thursday - Strats due for "Clear The Lane" Friday - Flashes due for "Flash Flood" Saturday - "Flash Flood" Sunday - "Clear The Lane"/Informer This is, as it says, subject to change as the fed grows and progresses. 6. The Cast! - Non-Player Characters Here is the line-up of commentators, interviewers and other members of the World-Wide Wrestling Association's staff. "MERCILESS" MARC MITCHELLS -------------------------- He's part of the commentation staff and they don't call him "Merciless" just because it sounds good. Marc Mitchells is one of those few commentators who'll tell you what he feels when he feels it. Beware his wrath!
CHRIS WILSON ------------ It's not too often a play-by-play commentator gets called "cute" but this young
"encylopedia of moves" has quite often been quoted being "WWWA's Main
Attraction". Besides... nobody says "Full Arm Drag and Twist" quite like this guy! SAM BRAXTON ----------- Truth, justice and the American Way -- that's what Sam Braxton is looking for. This aged commentator is only on the side of those who'd rather help an old lady across the street than fight a match. Let's just say him and Mitchells don't see eye to eye.
KEVIN DEL CORAZON ----------------- His name means "of the heart" and that's exactly where his ring announcing comes from! He's WWWA's local buffer and not only can he give you stats like nobody else in sports entertainment -- he can do it in Spanish too!
"PEPPY" TODD PHILLIPS --------------------- If something's going on, this guy's there waiting with a mic. Phillips is the
World-Wide Wrestling Association's main interviewer, working his hardest to get the interviews from the wrestlers that make up this wonderful federation.
JUDY DALLAS ----------- Call her hot... call her a bitch... all in all, she gets her job done! She's
Judy Dallas and she's the interviewer that will tell you straight up whether you're good or bad and what she thinks about it. But with legs like those -- who can argue?
