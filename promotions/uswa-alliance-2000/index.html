Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This USWA called itself the United States Wrestling Alliance and ran its recruitment entirely from a Tripod-hosted site. Its earliest surviving post, of 2 February 2000, promised fair treatment over hype — “Im not going to blow smoke up your ass by saying that this is the greatest fed ever” — and pointed applicants to the site's own grading rules for roleplays. Further posts through the month tracked its progress: a tenth wrestler signed and a first card set for 25 February, then a further call, once that milestone passed, specifically for tag teams to fill out a promised title tournament.

Its recruitment drew at least one hostile reply from another newsgroup reader, who accused “this guy” of “bastardizing the once great indy fed known as USWA for some e-fed” — a real, independent wrestling promotion of the same initials whose name this USWA's own critics felt it had borrowed.

No card, roster, or tournament result survives to say whether the promised first show or the tag title tournament ever happened. How long this USWA ran, and how or when it closed, are both outside the surviving record.
