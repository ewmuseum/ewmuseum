IRCCW Monday Night Cooked!!, 26 May 1997

26 May 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(Scene opens up on a chain-link fence somewhere surrounding the Three Rivers Stadium. It is late Sunday night.) ("The Franchise" Shane Douglas walks into the scene laughing, accompanied by a beautiful young (old enough) woman with long wavy brown hair and blue eyes. She's wearing jeans with a Shane Douglas t-shirt under a black leather jacket. Shane is wearing a pair of black jeans with black boots and a "Gold's Gym" t-shirt. His blonde hair is tied back.) SD: Hahaha. . PITTSBURGH! Are you READY for the RETURN. . of the "Franchise" Shane Douglas?! How quaint. . in a city which I grew up in. A city which grew up around me. Hahaha. . MY hometown. What an HONOR it is to be making my return in this great city. Now, let's cut to the chase. . .I'm not here to prance around and sing songs and girate my hips. I'm here because I have some UNFINISHED business with one; Rick Rude. Hahaha. . that's right. You think I don't remember the sh*t you pulled on me back in Philly? No, punk, that's FRESH in my mind. You may have gotten the girl, and you may have gotten the last laugh. . .but this isn't Philly, pal. This is IRCCW. Besides. . .my woman, TAMARA, *pulls her closer by the arm* is more of a woman than Francine could have ever been. She's even a better lay, too. . hahaha. So, Rude, there's the challenge. Do you have the balls to accept it? You've got to take a stand, Rick Rude. Step up to the plate. Let it all hang out. I don't care WHO I have to go through, either, if it's Tommy Dreamer, The Sandman, DDP, Steve Austin, The Giant. . and I don't give a rat's ass about that pretty boy, Remy, or his little female friend, Etheria, either. Maybe if there's still something left after I kick your carcass around the ring, you can be in the most prestigious stable in wrestling history. . *holds up three fingers* I don't think I have to inform you what that is. The bottom line is this. . when you step into the ring with me, you'll all find out what Vince McMahon, Eric Bischoff, Paul Heyman. . and even Rick Rude know. . not to mention, Tamara. Hahaha. . you'll find out just what the Franchise is all about. This interview is over! (Shane and Tamara walk off. Fade to black.) (The camara pans and shows Three Rivers Stadium dressed up in IRCCW Banners as a ring is set up around 2nd base as all "field" seats are taken as are most of the normal seats in the stadium. The lights are on as it is just dark in Pittsburgh Pa. The camara zooms in to Chris Hebert in a suit and RogueX in a Pirates baseball jersey and jean shorts.) RX: WELCOME EVERYONE TO COOKED IS DEKOOC!!!! Tonight as you saw in the begginning of our show the NEW Franchse Shane -- [the crowd starts booing heavily as the commentators and the camera look over at the aisleway, where Mr. Rob Spare is making his way toward the ring] RX: Well, I guess we're going to hear from him right now. He's demanding the microphone from ring announcer Matt MacDonald, who was ready to introduce our opening match... I guess we're going to go up to Mr. Spare. CH: Why?!? [Mr. Spare stands in the middle of the ring, dressed in a very conservative workout suit, a disgusted look on his face, as the stadium fills with jeers directed at him] RS: SHUT UP! I've got something to say, and I want all of you to shut your goddamned mouths. Let me tell you all a story. It's the story of a little wrestling promotion called IRCCW. It was back in February of 1996 that they hired ME as their very first color commentator after Sabol hired himself. I was the originator. I did the best damn job I could do, carrying Sabol and later YOU [points at Rogue] and then YOU [points at Hebert] and how was I repaid? Sabol told me I was "overshadowing" the play-by-play announcers. I "knew too much" and I was making the so-called "hosts" look like complete and total MORONS. The solution? "Start acting like an idiot," Sabol tells me. "Look like a dork, YOU be the moron from now on," he tells me. So... I did. I got my pants ripped off back at IRCCWMania I; I played the fool to Hebert when he came in; I filed a frivolous lawsuit so that everyone would call me "Mister"; I got carted out because of a little bump at IRCCW Trick or Treat; I got my ass caned on live television in front of millions of people around the world; in short, I made myself look like a JACKASS for the good of the company. But still Sabol said I was "stealing the spotlight" away from the play-by-play. The solution? I was moved out of the broadcast booth and into managing Ravishing Rick Rude and the Man They Call Vader, two of the top wrestlers ever to step foot in a ring. I was told we'd be given a HUGE push. "Main events, championship matches, headline feuds, all this and more for Rude and Vader if you agree to get out of the commentator's seat and manage them," Sabol says. So... I did. I gave up broadcasting and went into managing. STILL, I had to keep up the "dork" gimmick. I wore outfits that would give Mr. Blackwell nightmares. I got thrown around by men three times bigger than me. I got beaten up by women. But NO push for Rick or Vader. We were stuck in a meaningless angle with Team Extreme. Hell yes I wanted revenge for the caning, but enough is enough. The fact of the matter is that we were promised title bouts and main-event matches, and did we get them? Vader got ONE North American Title shot. ONE. Technically we got what we were promised, but I know and Sabol knows that he is a LIAR. [camera catches RogueX sitting at ringside shaking his head] RS: Don't even bother, Rogue. You know that everything I'm saying is the truth. You're in on it too, up there in the ivory towers in Hazleton, doing everything in your power to keep me down because I'm a better goddamn announcer than you could ever HOPE to be. Not only is the front office against me, but so are the in-ring officials. A perfect example would be one week ago at IRCCWMania II. Run that footage. Run that piece of tape right now! [Spare points at the video wall] *** IRCCWMANIA II -- May 19 RCL: Taz picking up Rude...He's got him with a Pumphandle..PUMPHANDLE SLAM? PLEX? He covers Rude!! Was it a suplex!?!?! Ross counts, 1.........2........3!!!!! HE GOT HIM!?!?!?!?!?!? CH: BULLSH<BLEEP>T...THAT'S A PUMPHANDLE SLAM! *DING* *DING* *DING* *** RS: Now, if even a guy who legit hates me, Hebert, agrees with me, what does that tell you brain-damaged idiots? Since when is a pumphandle slam considered a suplex? It's a conspiracy. A conspiracy designed to piss me off so much that I'll quit IRCCW, never to be seen again. Well Sabol, Rogue, Geraghty, and every other member of the front office staff... I ain't goin' anywhere. Having said that, I feel it is unfair to Rick and Vader that the officials' biases against me are having a negative effect on their matches. Obviously saying that won't change a thing, since the suits don't give a damn about anything but the bottom line, so I'll have to go a step further. Effective immediately I am stepping down as a manager. No longer will I be the jackass that every Tom Dick & Harry can push around and put through a table. But that doesn't mean you'll never see my face on IRCCW television again. With the assistance of my hardworking attorney, and no I don't mean Clarence Mason, I am currently in the process of being reinstated in my rightful position as color commentator. I don't care if it's here on COOKED IS DEKOOC or on Friday Night FURY, but my lawyer has told me that within seven days I will once again be co-hosting one of the two shows. That, you stupid sons of bitches... is a promise. [Spare drops the mic and leaves the ring to a loud chorus of boos; the ring has some trash in it that was thrown at Spare] CH: Oh, he's done already? RX: Indeed he is. In my experience, all of Mr. Spare's claims are totally false and without merit. As for his alleged return to broadcasting, I hadn't heard anything about that before. CH: I guess we'll just have to wait and see. RX: As I was saying before Shane Douglas and his valet Tamara come abck to IRCCW in his home town, he's looking to make a HUGE impact starting tonight.. CH: Also a street fight between Tommy Dreamer and Mankind should be interesting and in our main event the newly crowned Continental Tag Team Champions The ELiminators will defend their titles agains The Gangstas should be really interesting! RX: Also we hope everyone had a nice Memorial Day Holiday and today's Monday Night Cooked is Dekooc kicks off our tour of baseball stadiums across the country other stadiums will include Wrigly Field and Shea Stadium plus many more.. CH: Enough are we gonna go to the street fight now? RX: We are scheduled for that now, but I'm being told that they've already started! [cuts to the parking lot outside Three Rivers Stadium, where Dreamer and Mankind are brawling] RX: Dreamer slams Mankind head-first to the hood of a car! He scoops and slams him on the pavement! Pulls him up into a front-facelock for the DDT but Mankind stands and back-bodydrops Dreamer THROUGH A CAR WINDOW!!!!! CH: There is shattered glass everywhere! RX: Mankind yanks Dreamer out of the car and brings him around to the front of the vehicle... VERTICAL SUPLEX ON THE GROUND!!! Mankind gets into the driver's seat... what's that maniac doing? CH: It looks like he's hotwiring that car! You know, it's been said that Mankind went to reform school on a scholarship. RX: He gets the car started just as Dreamer stands up. He drives at Dreamer, who jumps up on the hood and breaks through the windshield to get at Mankind!!! Dreamer is slugging Mankind in the front seat and the car is driving all over the place! CH: Pretty flimsy windshield. Anyway, don't these guys know you should never brawl and drive?? <CRUNCH!!!!!> RX: The car just plowed into a couple others, hurting both men. Mankind is able to open the door, and both men collapse out the door. Mankind gets up and pulls Dreamer up, leading him back towards the stadium entrance. He tosses Dreamer through the doors back inside! CH: Are they coming to the ring? How is this match going to be decided?? RX: Mankind is taking Dreamer to the dressing rooms. He stops at one door and knocks. OH NO! That's the Elite Mob dressing room! Remy answers the door and Mankind throws Dreamer in there. CH: I can see Owen Hart, Chris Benoit, Dean Malenko, Psycho Sid, and of course, there's the ringleader, Raven. RX: Dreamer has just been "thrown to the lions", as it were, and look! Remy is handing over a fistful of greenbacks to Mankind, who shuts the door, pushes the camera, and takes off! CH: Nobody ever said Mankind didn't have a price. He's like a bounty hunter! [the cameraman rushes up to the door and tries to open it but finds that it's locked; we can hear muffled screams, slams, and other noises coming from inside] RX: This isn't good! Tommy Dreamer is trapped in there with the Elite Mob! Let's get some officials over here, please! CH: This will be interesting to see if Dreamer is alive next week...Oh well no big loss. RX: CHRIS! Folks we will be right back after this short break.. ****************************************************************************** ** Remy: Wanna look cool in the summer? <About 10 kids nod their head.> Remy: Well then buy my goddamn shirt..Look <Holds up the shirt> now go home and tell your parents to give me their money.. DID YOU SEE MISTERIO'S HEAD BOUNCE???? CH: Douglas could have beat him right there, but he is going to show off a bit more tonight! RX: Shane Douglas now pulling Misterio to his feet and holding him in the air with a delayed verticle suplex...CORKSCREW VERTICLE SUPLEX!!! CH: Now Douglas slapping Misterio who is out on the canvas and Douglas looking VERY impressive here tonight, and so is Tamara! He pins him rather easily! *DING* *DING* *DING* JF: Ladies and Gentlemen your winner in the time of 5:02 "THE FRANCHISE" SHANE DOUGLAS!!!! (Tamara enters the ring and hands Shane the mic.) SD: Listen, will you people just shut your goddamn mouths, already? *crowd boos* You'd think people from this city would have the intelligence to cheer a person of such high magnitude like myself. Hey, Rude. . .I know you're back there cowering in the bathroom stalls like I know you do every single time you see the Franchise wrestle. . . and I know you're impressed with what I do. You're probably equally impressed with WHO I do, also. Hahaha. *kisses Tamara* Now, I just took out this little Mexican mosquito punk like nothing. That's just a fraction of a fraction of a FRACTION. . of what I plan on doing to your sorry ass. He's missing the BALLS he needs in order to come out and face the Franchise. Ravishing NOTHING. There's no reason in the world why you can turn down my challenge now. HIT THE MUSIC! *gives triple threat sign* (Tamara and Douglas leave the ring.)
