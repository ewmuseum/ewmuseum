The Whoo! Wrestling Federation

10 June 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Fantasy fans, be sure to check out The Whoo! Wrestling Federation at Whoo! The Wrestling Site http://www.cybrsports.com/whwf The W!WF is unlike any other fantasy fed around. No worrying about sending in interviews, roleplaying, etc., you just check out the lineup for the next show (called Free-Per-Views), vote for who you think should win, and we take it from there. Free-Per-Views are held every two weeks. If you missed the last Free-Per-View, you could have voted on the winners of the following matches: Shawn Michaels & Steve Austin vs. Brian Pillman & Jim Neidhart Ken Shamrock vs. The Undertaker Sabu vs. Mankind Ahmed Johnson vs. Sycho Sid and more! The current Free-Per-View, in which voting runs until Sunday, June 22nd, features: Shawn Michaels & Steve Austin vs. Owen Hart & British Bulldog Sycho Sid, Ahmed Johnson, & Marc Mero (no injuries in the W!WF) vs. Faarooq, Crush, & Savio Vega (we put this card together before this week's RAW...oh well) LOD Animal vs. Kevin Nash Mankind vs. Hunter Hearst Helmsley Jerry Lawler vs. a mystery wrestler from ECW (you get to choose the wrestler...Tommy Dreamer, Sandman, or Taz) Ken Shamrock vs. Brian Pillman Sabu & Rob Van Dam vs. Doug Furnas & Phillip LaFon Whoo! Wrestling Federation matches are written by an actual pro wrestling booker, based on who *you* vote for to win each match. Using primarily wrestlers in the World Wrestling Federation, but other wrestlers have a way of showing up as well (Kevin Nash, Scott Hall, Disco Inferno, Sabu, etc.) So check out The Whoo! Wrestling Federation today at http://www.cybrsports.com/whwf Sincerely, [name withheld] Whoo! The Wrestling Site http://www.cybrsports.com/prowrest.html
