[WHOO!] Coming soon: The Whoo! Wrestling Federation

3 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Due to the incredible succes of the You Be The Booker area on Whoo! The Wrestling Site ( http://www.cybrsports.com/prowrest.html ), we have decided to take it one step further, and will be rolling out The Whoo! Wrestling Federation on Monday, April 14th. You Be The Booker presents fans with a certain situation. It puts them in Vince McMahon's/Eric Bischoff's shoes for a day. So many of us say, "If I ran the WWF, I'd do this and this and this." You Be The Booker lets you do that and that and that. And on Monday, April 14th, when it becomes The Whoo! Wrestling Federation, it marks a revolutionary concept. The Whoo! Wrestling Federation is a combination of You Be The Booker and your typical fantasy league, with several important differences. First off, the winners and losers of matches are decided by popular vote. No random roll of the dice, no wrestlers getting big money matches because they married the promoter's daughter, YOU decide who wins and who loses. Second, we will be using the current WWF roster in this fantasy situation. For example, we would post next week's card, such as: Bret Hart vs. Steve Austin Goldust vs. Mankind Flash Funk vs. Salvatore Sincere You vote for who you think should win each match. At the end of the week, we tally up the results, and give them to our booker, who is an actual pro wrestling booker. He will then create storylines and post them, along with the lineup for next week's card plus the monthly "Free-Per-View" lineups so you can continue voting. The Whoo! Wrestling Federation will be unique in that there are no politics involved. And by using WWF wrestlers, it will let everyone know who the consensus best wrestlers are, who's underrated and who's overrated. If the fans think T.L. Hopper should beat Vader, then doggone it, T.L. Hopper *will* beat Vader in The Whoo! Wrestling Federation. We have put up a FAQ for anyone wanting more information about The Whoo! Wrestling Federation. Just visit Whoo! at: http://www.cybrsports.com/prowrest.html and follow the banner link. And don't forget to be a part of history on Monday, April 14th, when The Whoo! Wrestling Federation debuts. Sincerely, [name withheld] Whoo! The Wrestling Site http://www.cybrsports.com/prowrest.html
