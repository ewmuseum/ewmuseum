[WHOO!] The Whoo! Wrestling Federation

14 April 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Whoo! The Wrestling Site, located at http://www.cybrsports.com/prowrest.html is proud to announce the birth of the Whoo! Wrestling Federation. Whoo! is the leader in original pro wrestling content online. In addition to housing the *official* web sites for over half-a-dozen wrestling promotions, we also feature many interactive games, including He's Shooting!, which is a Java-based shooting gallery so you can take a shot at your least favorite wrestlers. In that tradition comes a unique concept in interactive wrestling entertianment, The Whoo! Wrestling Federation. Why is this different from other fantasy wrestling feds? Simple. YOU get to pick who wins and who loses. And the wrestlers we use...well, they're probably familiar to you. Take a look at this week's "Monday Night CybeRaw" lineup: The Undertaker & Sycho Sid vs. Vader & Mankind Jesse James vs. Billy Gunn Ahmed Johnson, Crush, and more You can vote for the winners of each week's "CybeRaw", plus our monthly "Free-Per-Views". The results will be given to our booker (who is an *actual* pro wrestling booker), and you can check out the results every Monday. For more information, or to start voting, check out The Whoo!
