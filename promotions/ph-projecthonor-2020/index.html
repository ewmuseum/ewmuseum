Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Project: Honor ran on a Forumotion board in 2020 as a roleplay federation whose shows were called Proving Ground. Each card named an arena in the fiction, the Amway Center in Orlando on 20 November, the Greensboro Coliseum Complex on 4 December, and set a deadline and a limit of one roleplay per player with no word cap. Players answered with promos and short stories under titles of their own, filed locker-room sheets giving a ring name, a pic base, a theme song and an entrance, and were ranked in an Elite 5 and given Medal of Honor awards after each night. The owner, Chairman Rock Johnson, ordered the main events, and Dickie Watson stood at the head of the Elite 5 as Grand Champion with a record of 7 and 1.

A post of 22 November 2020, by a member who said they were not staff, says the community was moving to a ProBoards forum by the end of the month. The latest post here, on 17 December, is a run of new locker-room sheets.
