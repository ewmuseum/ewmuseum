WRESTLER’S IN-RING INFO CHARACTER DISPOSITION: Neutral Face TENDENCY TO CHEAT: Rarely MOTIVATION TO COMPETE: Passion; you live and breathe wrestling, winning or losing doesn't matter to you GIMMICK: Inside the ring, she tries to keep a quick pace to benefit her style. She doesn't really do a lot of power/strength type moves, so the faster the match goes, the better it works to her advantage. She doesn't always taunt the crowd, but will do it occasionally to get the crowd behind her.
Outside the ring, she’s a fiery, friendly kind of weirdo who sticks up for the underdogs and makes friends with just about anyone. She likes to have fun, and is all about spreading positivity and love.
FAVORITE MATCH TYPE: She's not particular, but she does favour anything that allows her to utilize her high-flying skills

Wicked Little Machine [Sharpshooter w/Reverse Facelock] BIOGRAPHY The adopted younger sister of internationally renowned wrestling star Phoenix Winterborn, Kasey is no slouch in the ring herself. After being taken in by the Winterborn family after her birth parents were killed in a horrific car crash when she was 6, she grew up loving wrestling as much as her older brother. Though they weren't related by blood, the Winterborn siblings nevertheless bonded straight away and both ended up pursuing careers in the squared circle. While Phoenix has rocketed to international fame, Kasey's career has been a little quieter, which she prefers anyhow. That being said, the promotions that she does find herself in, she gives 100% to at all times. She achieved some minor success across the United States and Canada, before spending some time out in Japan where she was trained by former fiance Nolan Rowe. Upon returning to North America, she had some moderate success in a few different companies, but nothing ever seemed to stick for her for one reason or another. With her signing to Project Honor, she's hoping to finally achieve the success that has eluded her so far in her career.
