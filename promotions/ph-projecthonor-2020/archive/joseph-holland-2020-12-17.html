DATE OF BIRTH:7/8/1997 HOMETOWN: Indianapolis, IN RESIDING FROM: Normandy, France PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION PIC BASE: Sami Zayn ENTRANCE DESCRIPTION THEME SONG: "Cult of Personality" by Living Colour ENTRANCE DESCRIPTION (OPTIONAL): Living Colour's "Cult of Personality" fades in, as fans start to direct their attention towards the stage. As the line "Look in my eyes, what do you see?" plays, Joseph reluctantly begins to walk through the curtain. Some fans dare to boo him, some still decide to cheer. But, to Joseph, the fans don't matter at all anymore. They're just people sitting in chairs trying to blind themselves from the cold, harsh realities of the world; something he is all too familiar with. Joseph slowly walks down the ramp, paying no mind to the crowds forming on either side of him. As he reaches the steps, he begins to climb up. As he does this, the boos seem to get louder and angrier; perhaps it was just because he was closer to them now? Or maybe it was because he knew deep down, that this is just how things work. He grips the ring post and takes a sharp, but deep breath before entering the ring. He stands in the center, and for the first time, he takes a good look around him. He frowns seeing all the disapproval. Not because they don't like him, but instead because he's disappointed in their lack of reason. He shakes his head as his brows furrow, before stepping towards any nearby corner.

'Da Vinci's Code' Koji Clutch BIOGRAPHY Joseph has been a professional wrestler for around five or six years. Despite that, he has had limited to no success whatsoever; and he couldn't figure out why for a long time. Maybe it was just the corporate structure? Maybe it was just not his time? What Joseph hadn't ever considered, was if it was himself holding back his success. He begun to realize that, maybe the reason he's not broke the glass ceiling is because people don't buy into the underdog shtick. His decision was innocent enough; start showing people who he really is under the facade of a happy-go-lucky underdog. Unbeknownst to him, the fans were quick to sour on him. It made sense at the time; they wanted the Joseph Holland with bright smiles and big aspirations. Sooner or later, Joseph began to notice that people had been trying to find every way possible to hate him. It became apparent that the fans were trying to pin their own frustrations and guilt onto him because they didn't have the courage to look inside themselves. Naturally, he didn't accept any of it; he thought it was disgusting, and voiced that opinion out to them. This, however, would only make things worse. After a while, Joseph began to tune out all the noise around him. Let them have their way, he thought. If they don't listen to him, he'll just have to show them.
