Pyro — 18 November 2020 BASIC INFO RING NAME: Pyro NICKNAME(S): The Nightmare HEIGHT: 6'1" WEIGHT: 205lbs. DATE OF BIRTH: 07/02/1989 HOMETOWN: Maidstone, Kent RESIDING FROM: Sanford, Florida PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION PIC BASE: Vinny Marseglia ENTRANCE DESCRIPTION THEME SONG: "Firestarter" (Metal Cover) by Leo Moracchioli - ENTRANCE DESCRIPTION (OPTIONAL): "Firestarter" (Metal Cover) by Leo Moracchioli hits the arena as the lights go completely out, some fans brave enough to boo and some scared to even react. The stage and ramp are lit up by fire the only source of light there is as “The Nightmare” Pyro walks out on the ramp. Pyro wears a horror themed mask as he stands at the top of the ramp. His eyes are so full of evil as he stands there wearing his jacket with different horror villains on it open to show his tattooed chest and his abs on show. His trousers the same design as his jacket tucked into black boots with blood splatters over. His head moves to the side in a creepy way as he holds his arms out wide. Most of the crowd in silence through fear but some being brave enough to boo. Pyro walks down to the ring slowly and methodically, ignoring everyone and everything around him. Once at the ring, the flames die out and the next thing you see is the lights come back on as Pyro stands in the middle of the ring. Once again his head creepily moves to the side as his hands remove his mask. Once his face is revealed, it shows a twisted and sickening smirk. Once again only some of the crowd were brave enough to boo Pyro. As Pyro’s music faded, he made his way to one of the corners of the ring and placed his mask down carefully. He then, kneels down in the corner of the ring with the same twisted and sickening smirk on his face.

'The Omen' Kimura BIOGRAPHY Pyro isn’t his real name, his real name is Sameal Eville born in Maidstone, Kent in England. All that he knows about his past is that a fire changed his life at the age of 8 years old. Rumours were that Pyro was the one who started the fire while his parents were sleeping. Not only that but apparently he was standing there watching the house burn to the ground with a twisted and sick smile on his face as his parents died in the fire. Pyro doesn’t always show it, but the therapists he has seen, have all had nightmares from what they heard from someone at just a young age.
Sameal was shipped off at 9 years old to the US where he was moved from Orphanage to Orphanage. It was believed that the other kids would torment him, which one day at 11 years old lead Sameal to set fire to the Orphanage, this time luckily no one was harmed but once again he stood there watching the Orphanage burn with the same twisted and sick smile. He ended up spending 6 years of his life in what could only be described as hell until one day, whether through luck or through a helping hand, he escaped from the asylum so to speak he was in.
It was at the age of 17 years old that he did whatever he could to make money, whether it was beg, borrow or steal. It wasn’t long before he was taken off the streets by a guy by the name of Damien Jones, a wrestling trainer in Las Vegas. Damien Jones was one of the best Technical and Hardcore wrestlers of his time. It was under him that Pyro learned and got the love for wrestling, whether it be hardcore style wrestling or submissions and technical wrestling. Pyro spent 5 years competing in just Hardcore Matches which is why he partly got tattoos to cover the scars. As much as he enjoyed it, he wanted to do more, he loved MMA training alongside his wrestling training and wanted to go do MMA for a while. At the age of 22 years old, while he was a MMA Fighter, he met Magdalena Scott, which after 2 years of being together, they got married now making her Magdalena Eville as of March 26th, 2013. Not long after they got back from their honeymoon, Magdalena revealed she was pregnant.
It was May 24th, 2014 that Magdalena Eville gave birth to a little girl. Sameal and Magdalena decided on naming her Annabelle Carrie Eville. Pyro knew he had to do whatever it took to provide for his wife and child. At the age of 26 years old, Pyro signed up to a local company in Vegas where he spent his time until he was 30 years old. At 30 years old, Pyro, his wife and daughter moved to Sanford, Florida. Pyro ended up competing on the independent circuit there, always making money above the means he needed to live.
Now Annabelle is 6 years old, and Pyro has been married 7 years old, he has decided to sign up with Project Honor. Pyro from the day his daughter was born, made the promise to her and his wife, that no matter what, he’d make sure they’d have the life they deserve. Pyro is all about his wife and daughter and would gladly set fire to anyone who hurt them, and stand there with a twisted and smirk on his face too.
