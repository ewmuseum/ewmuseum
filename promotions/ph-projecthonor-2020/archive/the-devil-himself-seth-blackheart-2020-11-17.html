GergIsHere — 17 November 2020 BASIC INFO RING NAME: Seth Blackheart NICKNAME(S): The Devil, Himself HEIGHT: 6’2 WEIGHT: 243 lbs. DATE OF BIRTH: 11/13/1988 HOMETOWN: Columbus, Ohio RESIDING FROM: Columbus, Ohio PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION Seth Blackheart has long, jet black hair that goes down to the small of his back and a pair of icy blue eyes. He has a build that is both lean and muscular, and is covered in many, many tattoos. Blackheart's standard gear is made up of boxer-like trunks, adorned with black and white skulls and various other images, almost like a painting was on his gear. The same goes for his elbow pads and kneepads. He wears solid black kickpads on his boots.
PIC BASE: Asdrall ENTRANCE DESCRIPTION THEME SONG: “Remains" by Aviators ENTRANCE DESCRIPTION (OPTIONAL): Static suddenly fills the titantron screen, and an ominous siren wails. Cutting through the static is the image of a blackened heart beating, a human eye opening in the center of it. The lights are cut, with the exception of a dim spotlight shining down at the top of the ramp, the slow hums of “Remains" by Aviators beginning to play. The figure of a man enters the light, his long black hair hanging in front of his face as he leans forward. Blackheart slowly rises up, his arms outstretched. As he rises the red glow expands now washing over the arena in a flashing red. Blackheart looks down to the ring and smiles as he strides down. Blackheart stands on the apron, his back turned to his opponent, and outstretches his arms again, soaking in the reaction of the crowd, before looking back to his opponent and entering the ring.
CHAMPION ENTRANCE DESCRIPTION (OPTIONAL): The same as the normal entrance, but Blackheart holds the belt in his hand, raising it up as he raises his hands.

The Glory gives you the status. The Gold gives you the power. The Punishment makes an example out of someone. The Platform gives you the ability to influence the industry.
GIMMICK: The Devil is often described as a dealmaker, often leading people down a dark path. Blackheart acts in a similar manner, offering a handshake to those willing to follow his orders in exchange for opportunities at whatever it is they desire. Blackheart manipulates people in order to realize his vision of a “better future”, something he will stop at nothing to achieve. Blackheart’s brutality in the ring can reach uncomfortable levels if pushed far enough.

BIOGRAPHY Seth Blackheart wishes to change the status quo, giving those who have struggled without any gain a “purpose”. He often describes the wrestling industry as cruel and unforgiving, and views the heads of companies as the true monsters in this business. He blames them for ruining the lives of wrestlers who they deemed unworthy of opportunities. But why?
Blackheart’s real name is Seth Miller and he is actually a 3rd generation wrestler. His grandfather wrestled in a small local promotion for most of his career, but due to injury, was forced to retire early. It was his father that had impacted Seth’s life, however. His father, known in the local promotion, BCW (Best Championship Wrestling), as “The Amazing” Charlie Miller, was quickly becoming a hot act in wrestling. It was during his farewell match in BCW, however, that things would take a turn for the worst. After taking an avalanche piledriver off the top rope, Charlie Miller had apparently broken his neck in several different places. En route to the nearest hospital, Miller would succumb to his injuries, and pass away in the ambulance. This was a match that Seth had watched live, in-person. It is due to this event that Seth had become the disturbed individual that he is now, and why he is hellbent on achieving his goals by any means necessary…
ACCOMPLISHMENTS Seth Blackheart has gained a reputation for being not only a greatly accomplished wrestler, but also earning a multitude of degrees in psychology, graduating top of his class at Stanford University. He also has a multitude of amateur wrestling championships under his belt. However, Seth had left amateur wrestling and a potential career as a psychiatrist to pursue a career in professional wrestling.
