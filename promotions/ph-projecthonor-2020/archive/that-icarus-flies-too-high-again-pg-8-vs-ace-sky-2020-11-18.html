*Daniel Horror walks into the abandon dark room with his switchblade* Horror : Last week, I overestimated the man and I was beaten, I admit to that. Now, this week at Proving Ground, I'm going to face that... Guru Ace Sky again.
Horror : I want to tell you... a little story. There were Daedalus the great inventor and his only son, Icarus. They were imprisoned by the king of Crete at the highest tower in the island.
Then, Daedalus, he was a smart man , he created two pairs of wings and escaped from the tower with his son.
However, Icarus flied too high and into the sun and his wings melted and the wings were disintegrated and Icarus fell down and died.
Horror : Just like you, Ace Sky but the different is you are too stubborn to understand how dangerous I am, what a stupid bastard.
Ace Sky, you make a fatal mistake, and I'm the one to be the consequence and... I GONNA MAKE YOU SUFFER. YOU HAVE BEEN WARNED. *Daniel Horror stabs his switchblade into Ace Sky's picture as the video is faded to black*
