[name withheld] — 5 December 2020 RING NAME: The Dragon Lady NICKNAMES: The Dragon Lady HEIGHT: 5 ft 2 in WEIGHT: 145 lbs. DATE OF BIRTH: 05/01/1992 HOMETOWN: San Francisco RESIDING FROM: Baltimore PHYSICAL DESCRIPTION: Short. Shorter than most. But she looks toned, and for her height and weight, she definitely has some curves.
PIC BASE: Elodie Yung as Elektra (Netflix) ENTRANCE DESCRIPTION All the lights in the arena would dim for a moment as the music began to ring... (you can run plays)... after a few seconds pass red and white lights flash around the ring and the ramp... fog would already cover the ground making the ring look as if it were floating. (The announcer would be saying at this point, "weighing in at 145 pounds, at 5 feet and 2 inches, The Dragon Lady!").
As The Dragon lady walked onto the ramp, up to her knees covered in fog... Then she would pause, her hands would go to her hips and she would look up as if looking up to the sky in a most heroic way. The crowd would go wild. "Dragon!Dragon!Dragon!" Would fill her ears. And then she looked forward... as if to stare down the ring... and any opponent who might be there. Her hands still on her hips. Half her face shielded by a white mask. Only her eyes can be seen. Then she began to walk forward slowly. Hands now at her sides a bit stretched out as if she were open for a hug... each step was slow... methodical... she had a purpose... she knew what she needed to do.
She walked up the steel steps to the ropes of the ring. Fog all around. The crowd clapping and screaming... for they knew The Dragon Lady had shown to show no mercy to the ones who truly deserved it.... she would let herself into the ring arms still slightly outstretched as she climbed over the second rope and into the ring... She looked once more towards the sky.... and then.... at you.... she then went to a corner of the ring... still very slowly.... as if she had been pacing the whole time just one way... then she waited.
THEME SONG: "You Can Run" by Adam Jones. WRESTLER’S IN-RING INFO CHARACTER DISPOSITION: Neutral Face TENDENCY TO CHEAT: Never. The Dragon Lady is honorable. She would not want to taint a victory with cheating.
MOTIVATION TO COMPETE: Financial Gain: Her mentor is very wealthy though neither flaunt this fact. To The Dragon Lady, making money is important, but not enough to make her upset if she lost out on cash. Unlike with her last boss C$J who she had previous conflicts with who did not pay her her championship bonus when she deserved it. This is the reason she left Carnage Wrestling. Not because she needed the cash, but because of principal. She wanted and still wants C$J to give her some sort of credit.
Passion: She is passionate about proving she can compete with the best. She gives it her all every match, and is known for becoming stronger after each match she competes in whether win or lose.
Glory: At this point glory means little unless it can bring her peace of mind which she has yet to find.
Gold: gold meant very little to her at the start of her career. After leaving Carnage, she feels especially dishonorable for leaving right after winning that gold she had made a goal for herself. She wants gold, very much, but gold means little if she cannot have her ultimate goal with it. Peace of mind.
Punishment: The Dragon Lady does not wish to inflict the most amount of pain she can on someone at any given point. However, she has taught herself to test her competitors to their limits. She prides herself on being able to push her competitors as far as she can. When she wins, she feels most positive when she feels she has fought someone when they are at their most strongest.
Platform: Besides her own selfish reasons to gain peace of mind, she hope's she can be an inspiration to any who need to feel braver.
FAVORITE MATCH TYPE: Singles matches as she doesn't need to focus on more than one person at a time. It has been a most difficult challenge for her in matches where she fought against more than one competitor at a time.
LEAST FAVORITE MATCH TYPE: She would feel pretty okay with any type of match even during matches where people can cheat. She would hope she can outwit her competitors so any cheating another competitor might do, would be invalid.
WEAPON OF CHOICE: Her fists WRESTLER’S MOVE-SET 1. Dragon whip 2. Rolling Senton 3. Yakuza kick 4. Hurricanrann 5. Tornado DDT 6. Diving Elbow drop 7. Triangle Choke 8. Arm Bar 9. Dragon Sleeper 10. Open Palm Strike SIGNATURE MOVES (5 MAX): 1. Pheonix Splash 2. Tilt-A-Whirl Headscissors Takedown 3. Dragon Screw Leg Whip 4. Roundhouse Kick 5. Scissor Kick (Because of Dragon Lady's shorter frame, she prefers doing a lot of leg moves and prides herself on trying to be quick on her feet as well)
FINISHING MOVES (3 MAX): 1. “The Dragon’s Nest” The Camel Clutch 2. “Breathing Fire” Contra Code AKA Sliced Bread #2 BIOGRAPHY The Dragon Lady's name is currently unknown to any wrestlers unless she chooses to reveal the information. She has a preference for keeping her identity private. One day after a long day's work when she was around 22 years of age, someone spotted her as she left her crummy job as a teacher's aid. It wasn't something she was very happy doing. More a job her parents wanted her to do. And after mom passed, and dad took off in a drunken stupor who has been missing for awhile now, the young lady had decided she just wasn't happy anymore.
A strange woman who walked with grace hidden in shadows followed the young woman after work that night. She followed her into a gym. She took a seat and sat in silence as she watched the young girl begin to throw punches and kicks at a punching bag… The young girl had stayed punching, grunting, and kicking until not a soul was left in the gym but her and the woman who walked with such grace and beauty.
Eventually, the woman walked up to the punching bag and held it firmly in place. She began saying encouraging words to the younger lady, words about moving her weight with each punch and kick. Words about letting that anger and frustration come out. Words on how to control what energy left her.
The young woman admired this older lady who became like a mother to her. And one day surprised her with a contract. "Mameha… I would be honored if you were to help me with this. Be my manager. Help me to make something of myself I am proud of." This would be the start of The Dragon Lady's legacy in the CW.
After about 2 months with Carnage, The Dragon Lady decided her path would be forged someplace else. She asked Mameha to take a life changing train ride into the future. Into Project Honor.
Strengths: Very agile because she is a smaller lady giving her the ability to be quick and light on her feet. Very flexible and her specialties is knowledge of contorting not only her joints, but her opponents joints as well. Knowledge of weaknesses in the human body.
Weaknesses: her small stature can be a weakness. She would be more easy to toss around because she is smaller than many others. People who target her morals and try to tear her down with words. Words can be just as dangerous as a final move to her.
ACCOMPLISHMENTS: She has won the CHAOS title. But gave the title up not long after she got it.
