RCW — Closure

Goodbye RCW

6 May 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ 1 of 17 — 6 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... Hopefully not forever. Kudos to Fletch on one damn fine e-wrestling establishment. Some of the best fun I've ever had in e-wrestling was in RCW...and I was glad to be part of the fed in the early goings. It was definately interesting to see the original guys go and the "big names" come into the fed...it really felt like an honest to goodness wrestling federation at times. -- -=-=- Mark "My mother never saw the irony in calling me a son-of-a-bitch." - Jack Nicholson (On Hell) "I had so much fun they kicked me out the muthafucka." - Richard Pryor -=-=- [ 2 of 17 — 6 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... I want to thank Fletch for allowing me to take part in probably the best e-fed I've ever been in. I'm not just saying that either because I've been a lot of them over the years. RCW was a place where that allowed a re-birth of sorts for me. From day one, I thought there was something special happening there. It was a place where hard work and dedication were rewarded. But most of all, it was about having fun. Fletch never forgot that. He knew that the day he opened shop and even when he closed it. On a different tact, Fletch was probably the best person to ever write my character in terms of matches. Sometimes it was like he knew him better than I did. I'm gonna miss that just like I'll miss RCW as a whole. [name withheld] is right, RCW grew from small time to big time. I'm just glad I got to take the ride all the way to the last stop. Thanks again Fletch. RCW will always remind me of the best e-fed there ever was. Jeff a former RCW champ :( [ 3 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... Gotta agree with everyone so far. Fletch gave me a chance to really showcase Riggins and I thank him for that. RCW was a damn fine fed, showcasing arguably the best talent around and arguably the best shows around. I will miss it for sure. Now...not to sound like a scavenger, but if any of RCW's talent are looking for homes, especially those not in other big feds, I may be able to help out, especially as far as tags and cruisers go. The guys just need to contact me. [detail withheld] or www.neo-wrestling.com Besides...we all know it'll be back. =) Walls [ 4 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... I really don't know what the hell to say, except RCW was one of my favorite feds to be in. Gave me a chance to meet some cool people and handle amongst some of the best writers I've seen in awhile. Well, good luck to Fletch and his endeavors. Vic Morrison is homeless, [name withheld] [ 5 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... On behalf of match writers who don't get their matches in on time, I just like to say...I'm sorry for killing RCW. :( Otherwise, if Fletch happens to come back and tell us explicitly it's not my fault for killing RCW, I will bypass all the guilt I feel at the moment and thank Fletch(Stalking victim #21452) for giving me and yeah...that Doran guy our big break in e-w and actually letting our cruddy little characters into his fed, over-pushing them, and then giving us the power to feel like bigshots. RCW was a great fed. Hell...greatest fed I've been in. It was all great... [Cue the cheesy goodbye music.] Alex Extreme's first title run. Chris Hopper/Gorilla beatdowns. The El Thugs. Making fun of Dan Kauffman's dead wife. The Crazed Animals parody. Our constant shooting for no good reason. Rosco Riggins/Juan Vasquez(Greatest 8k match _EVER_ and if any of you got a problem with that, take it up with...Wallie.) The PT-...uhhh...nevermind. Goku Van Keel. Fletch constantly DDTing Taliban supporters. And ummm...the aborted Lesbian angle(Running out of moments, aren't you, Terry?). Oh yeah...War Games. Ummm...that's it. [End cheesy music.] And Fletch, if you ever want it...my match is done. :/ But anyways, it's been a wild ride, people. See you in the other 34365 feds I happen to be in at the moment. Terry -RCW r0x0rz. -Jue betcha'...Jue betcha'. [ 6 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... Not that I was ever in RCW, but kudos to Fletch for running a damn fine fed, and putting out some of the best cards I've ever read. Hope it's back soon, James [ 7 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... >On behalf of match writers who don't get their matches in on time, I >just like to say...I'm sorry for killing RCW. :( [name withheld] weren't the only one so don't totally blame yourself as the only culprit. The cool thing was the RCW began with a bunch of nobodies and never will bees. The talent roster was nothing anybody would write home about. Fletch took a guy like Extreme and Vanguard reinvented them while giving birth to homegrown talent like Raya, Jordan, Kinsey, Vasquez, the Leading Men, the American Idols, and Vlad. In doing so, he was able to mesh guys like Eddie Jacobs and Team Canada. Later on he added Courtade, Case, and Kauffman. People ask if its possible to take a small fed and turn it into one of the best and well known. Fletch proved that the answer is yes. It can be done. And when Fletch comes back, if he comes back, it will happen again. I just hope I get a chance to be there. Jeff *RCW faithful from day one. [ 8 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... I suppose I should reply, seeing as how I was the, uh, Vice President or something. This probably sounds very dumb, but when I read that RCW closed I was a numb for about twenty minutes. They say it's just a game, but alot of people put alot of work into making RCW as great as it was. Thank you everyone for working your ass off, no matter if you were an RCW original (Frank, Jeff and the like) or were just getting your feet wet. I also owe a debt of gratitude to Fletch, who let two unknown losers like me and [name withheld] into his fledgling fed, then overpushed us and even gave me a bit of responsibility. Like a lot of people, I'll always consider myself an RCW lifer. Long live the mighty, er no, wait... long live the great RCW. Andy -a prick...and stuff -former RCW VP -executive type guy for hire NINTENDO! NINTENDO! [ 9 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... Well... That sucks. RCW was a cool fed. Fletch and I had a falling out, but I'm not gonna pretend that RCW didn't make a major contribution to the game. It was a top notch fed and the handlers there did a shitload of good work. I can't take that away from Fletch or anybody else. It's a shame to see a fed go down. Every time somebody starts to rival EMWC, they end up dead. I think we need a little more competition since a lot of the talent has been watered down and spread out so thin... but I digress. Best wishes to all involved in future endeavors. And 'cause cheap plugs rule, if you need something to do for the rest of the summer, A1 PRO is lurking around. Goodbye, RCW. If only there were more e-feds based in the Gateway to the West, the world would be a better place. A STL boy, -Zach [ 10 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW... This might not be the best time.....but in the wake of RCW closing down, I would just like to let everyone know that the exceptional talent which made up RCW is more than welcome to AWE. Here's to RCW, and giving its handlers a home to carry on its great tradition. [ 11 of 17 — 7 May 2002 ] Goodbye RCW...
