PWA -- Play By Mail Wrestling

17 February 1996
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Phantasy Wrestling Alliance is a play-by-mail wrestling league that has been in business now for five years and has hundreds of managers. The PWA presents comments and sneak attacks written by the contestants in a TV format with good guy and bad guy announcers. Six titles are available in each promotion (10 men's promotions, 2 women's, 2 men's and women's), including two tag teams and a six-man (or woman). Alliance-Wide Singles, tag and six-man for men and women are also crowned. Three pay-per-view titles for men and two for women are also contested for each pay-per-view event. A roster and brief description of all wrestlers is given in the newsletters each month (newsletters are free, costs average about $1 a match). Singles, tag team and six-man standings are also given in each newsletter. In addition, a Matchwatch column sums up all the feuds going on in the promotion as well as a Revue section that goes into depth on that month's action inside the ring. Other features include a "radio call-in show" and a rumor mill section. Remember -- you get what you pay for. And with the PWA, you get a little more!
