DWI "Dead Pool" 8/14...FIRST CHAMP CROWNED!

14 August 2004
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
http://www.nowyouredead.com "RUSS COULD HAVE BEATEN KURT" "WE WANT TRIPLE H" "GET WORSE SOON, TENTA" "WORLD CHAMPIONS SHOULD BE OVER 300 POUNDS" "HEROIN OVERDOSES = RATINGS" "HURRY UN DIE BRADSHAW - HITLER" Gordon Solie: "The Dead Pool weekly broadcast is back on the air again and we welcome you to another action packed card of wrestling. Tonight Monsoon, we crowd a new DWI Champion. There's only one belt in this company and that makes it all the more prestigious." Gorilla Monsoon: "One company, one champion, one leader. If you grab a camera crew and ask the annoying twelve year olds outside the arena, they'll all tell you, Big John Studd is the favorite. They like big men, Gordon, everyone does." Solie: "Brian Pillman doesn't. He's launched a full scale attack on Big John Studd, going so far as to call him "stinky"." Gorilla: "I don't know if those were his EXACT words, but this match tonight will feature either Pillman cleaning Big John Studd's body odor, or Studd cleaning Brian Pillman's mouth out with soap." Solie: "The filthiest parts of each man will be lathered tonight! Let's go to the ring!" Norm: "Ladies and gentlemen, the following is a match to decide a co-number one contender for the DWI Championship!" (An angelic harmony thunders into the arena and Russ Haas slowly walks down the ramp, basking in the spotlights and jeering with his arms spread wide.) ("Badstreet U.S.A." booms overtop of Haas' entrance theme, signaling the arrival of "An Original Freebird" Terry Gordy. He jogs down the ramp, stopping mid way to admire the crowd.) Norm: "To my right, from Houston, Texas and weighing in at 235 pounds... he claims to be the ONLY talented Haas brother... this is RUSS HAAS!!" Norm: "And to my left, originally from Soddy Daisy, Tennessee but tonight he is coming to you from BADSTREET, ATLANTA, GEORGIA... weighing 268 pounds... THE FREEBIRD... TERRY GORDY!!" Russ Haas Vs. Terry Gordy Monsoon: "Is it redundant to note here that Terry Gordy briefly was known as The Executioner?" Solie: "You are neither a gorilla nor a monsoon. Discuss." Monsoon: "Yeah, well, he's been, um, executioned." Solie: "Haven't we all? Stop beating a dead horse." Monsoon: "Call yourself what you will." Solie: "Russ Haas -- a true thoroughbred." Monsoon: "Obviously of good stock...look what his brother is doing on the flipside. Tag champ on multiple occasions...." Solie: "NCAA accolades to boot." Monsoon: "Two extremes here. A manchild in Terry Gordy, who began his pro career at thirteen and still towered over many. He's a veteran in every sense of the word." Solie: "Except how John Kerry uses it." Monsoon: "Bush too then." Solie: "And then the opposite....a star on the horizon that flamed out way too early. A technician, a brain. Should be a good one. Here's the bell and Gordy immediately locks up with Haas and tosses him into a corner. Russ rubs his shoulder and regroups." Monsoon: "Fear a man with red hair." Solie: "Yeah, you either get tough or die. Might as well name him Sue off the bat." Monsoon: "So I guess Gordy really is free as a bird now...." Solie: "He's got to get past Haas first.....Russ is up and dives at Gordy's feet....double leg takedown surprisingly works. Terry Gordy not expecting amateur moves." Monsoon: "Gordy got his formal training inside the ring. He's a brawler anyhow." Solie: "Haas quick with an ankle lock....but Gordy is too strong...he kicks Haas away." Monsoon: "But Russ is right back on him with a reverse chinlock....trying to wear down a man that most likely is not that well conditioned in the first place." Monsoon: "Unless you consider the ability to do Atlanta all night some kind of stamina!" Solie: "They didn't trademark Badstreet USA for nothing. It's just two different styles of wrestling. Old vs. new." Monsoon: "Gordy attempting to get to his feet...he's halfway there...flails his legs....and sits right down on his ass! Haas releases and grabs his jaw. Gordy pulls him up by the hair and tosses Russ Haas to the outside." Solie: "Gordy collecting his breath and deciding his next move. These two men have never fought before in any promotion, any venue, any dimension. He's wily though. Gordy now to the outside as the ref is forced to change his count." Monsoon: "The former Executioner in control with a definite size and weight advantage. Scoop slam onto the ring steps. That'll promote good posture. A couple boots for good measure now too." Solie: "The ref is warning both men to get back in the ring. We don't want our first DWI championship later on tonight to be won by default." Monsoon: "Gordy collects what's left of Haas and rolls him under the bottom rope....Gordy taking his time to get back in the ring....he's hit his old stride again apparently. Feeling it." Solie: "Haas takes off to the other side of the ring.....flying burrito sends Gordy flying! He hits his head on the guardrail outside!" Monsoon: "Oh no, not another seizure! GRAND MAL! GRAND MAL!" Solie: "The difference in stamina starting to show. Haas, though bruised and battered, still has gas in his tank. Gordy has been knocked silly and is now losing blood. Haas with a Russian leg sweep on the outside! BARE CEMENT!" Monsoon: "Did Bill Watts lay out the DWI Arena?" Solie: "Let's hope he lives forever." Monsoon: "Haas doing all he can to lift Gordy back into the ring....Gordy's instincts have taken over, but I don't think they are doing any good. I mean, he knows he needs to be in the ring to win but right now that won't do him any good." Solie: "Terry Gordy trying to sit up...." Monsoon: "Haas better immobilize him again before he gets up...." Solie: "What's this, Haas goose-stepping around the ring?" Monsoon: "HAAS OF PAIN! HAAS OF PAIN! HAAS OF PAIN!" Solie: "It's a seated full nelson clutch! Haas has him right where he wants him! The blood squirts out of Gordy's skull!" Monsoon: "GORDY REFUSES TO GIVE UP!" Solie: "But he's leaning! His eyes glaze over!" Monsoon: "He can't tap out! This is a full nelson!" Solie: "He's out! The ref checking him verbally!" Monsoon: "NOOOOOOOOOOO!!!!!" Solie: "TERRY GORDY IS OUT COLD! THE REF STOPS THE MATCH! RUSS HAAS HAS WON! HE'LL FIGHT FOR THE DWI TITLE TONIGHT!" Winner Via Submission Russ Haas Monsoon: "It was a Full Nelson though!" Solie: "Monsoon, how many wrestlers submitted to a Full Nelson in your era? This isn't a rare occurrence for you. Besides, it was both seated and arched, no man can survive that." Monsoon: "At least it wasn't a clothesline! You'll never see northerners be defeated by a clothesline!" Solie: "Hmm." Norm: "Our next contest will decide who faces Russ Haas tonight for the DWI Championship! It is also the first ever SOAP ON A ROPE... ON A POLE MATCH!" (A cannon blast hits the arena followed with some stalking music, leading Brian Pillman down the ramp. Pillman, now "high" on victory from last week, taunts the fans with even more arrogance. It only makes some of them cheer harder though.) (Big John Studd nearly rips the stage curtain down as he storms through it, targeting Pillman the whole way.) Norm: "To my right, he hails from Cincinnati, Ohio and weighing in tonight at 235 pounds... they call him THE LOOSE CANNON... BRIAN PILLMAN!!" Norm: "And introducing to my left, from Burke, Virginia... we weighs an impressive 346 pounds... this... is... BIG JOHN STUDD!!" Soap on a Rope on a Pole Match Brian Pillman Vs. Big John Studd Monsoon: "Studd's tights have black stars tonight, that's not a good sign for Pillman." Solie: "You've wrestled this man, probably, I don't really know, what's he like inside the ring?" Monsoon: "He's big, strong and sloppy, just how we like them." Solie: "You'd look up to anyone who could out perform you, Gorilla." Monsoon: "I would, and do. What's your point?" Solie: "That you look up to a lot of people." Monsoon: "I wrestled Sergeant Slaughter in a boot camp match!" Solie: "That was Pat Patterson." Monsoon: "That's no excuse." Solie: "Wasn't there a match going on?" Monsoon: "Yes! Big John Studd has had Brian Pillman in a shoulder claw for the past two minutes, he's really wearing him down!" Solie: "Studd tells this rowdy crowd to be quiet, and that's just the opening Pillman needed... forearm to the southern states!" Monsoon: "Studd, obviously a man of girth, is taken aback more than your average man. Pillman has a huge opportunity to go for the soap!" Solie: "He's unnerved thanks to that claw, but Pillman is still climbing! And he's cut off by Studd! Studd is going to toss him off the top rope!" Monsoon: "No, he grabs Pillman and pulls him down... right into a Bearhug! The pain must be jolting through Pillman's body like lightning bolts." Solie: "Studd squeezing with everything he has." Monsoon: "It's only a matter of time before Studd pops him like a grape. Pillman is caught in the deadliest submission move ever invented, there is no escape from a man Studd's size." Solie: "There's always one way out of it, the foot stomp, and Pillman is a master of said maneuver!" Monsoon: "Well, at least he doesn't have the unfair advantage that Kerry Von Erich has." Solie: "Pillman clutching his ribs as he escapes but still bounces off the ropes and manages a flying clothesline!" Monsoon: "Studd is still standing!" Solie: "Pillman with another one!" Monsoon: "Still erect!" Solie: "Pillman climbs to the top rope... DIVING CLOTHESLINE... and Studd caught him in mid-air! Oh no... POWERSLAM!!" Monsoon: "And Studd has a clear path to the soap." Solie: "Studd, even with his size has to climb the ropes, something he's probably never done." Monsoon: "Big men can woo the crowd without the aid of top rope maneuvers. You're former boss Bill Watts knew this when he banned it." Solie: "Bill Watts was declared legally insane in 1998, but that's beside the point. Big John Studd has made it to the bottom rope, and he's a little wobbly." Monsoon: "Pillman has had ample time to recover and he makes a b-line for Studd, knocking him off the bottom rope!" Solie: "Studd lands with a Thud!" Monsoon: "Cleaver. Bobby Heenan you're not. God I wish he was dead." Solie: "Gorilla!" Monsoon: "You know what I mean." Solie: "Studd has a major problem, he's incapable of climbing for the soap. Pillman to the top rope, easily I might add... and he can have the soap right here! No, he jumps off... CROSSBODY BLOCK ONTO STUDD! He takes the big man down!" Monsoon: "Not for long... Studd stands and he is hot... Pillman from behind... he clips the knee! Pillman isn't fighting like his daddy taught him!" Solie: "Maybe he is?" Monsoon: "Studd to one leg... and Pillman takes that one out too! Studd is incapacitated, and Pillman is ascending the ropes! HE'S GOT THE SOAP!" Solie: "And Studd immediately defends his armpits!" Monsoon: "Pillman trying, he knows he has to wash out his pits that Studd so proudly boasted in order to win." Solie: "He needs an opening!" Monsoon: "STOMP TO THE TESTICLES! THAT'S RIGHT, I SAID TESTICLES!" Solie: "And there it is!" Monsoon: "Pillman... PILLMAN... P I L L M A N.... LATHERS!!! IT'S OVER!!!" Winner Via Lathering Brian Pillman Monsoon: "This was a parody." Solie: "Oh what, a real Soap on a Rope on a Pole match?" Monsoon: "Of wrestling! We never had anything this crazy where I came from." Solie: "You should have stuck around a few more years." Monsoon: "I know what you mean, and it pales in comparison to the Viagra on a Pole match that was targeted to your demographic." Solie: "I don't know which demographic that match was aimed at, but it sure as hell wasn't mine." Monsoon: "RUSS HAAS! Haas hits the ring and almost knocks Pillman out of his boots! He demands the bell to be rung! What the hell, we've already heard introductions for both of them." DWI Championship Brian Pillman Vs. Dino Bravo Solie: "Guess he has the power to command the timekeeper." Monsoon: "Russ stomping the daylights out of Pillman, and Studd is making it to his feet as we speak." Solie: "Joey Marella making sure he exits the ring, BUT STUDD PUSHES HIM ASIDE! Studd back over to Pillman, he's not done!" Monsoon: "Studd picks Pillman up... REVERSE BEARHUG!!" Solie: "What else?" Monsoon: "Forward Bearhug?" Solie: "That's the Giant's job." Monsoon: "Haas is keeping the referee down as Studd goes to work on Pillman, and I think he's out cold! Is this some kind of set up, or can Studd not take a loss?" Solie: "If I unleashed the arsenal that Studd did tonight and didn't win, I'd be mad." Monsoon: "Excellent commentary, Solie. You're not half bad after all." Solie: "I was being sarcastic." Monsoon: "How so?" Solie: "Studd finally releases a rag doll like Pillman and exits, just as Haas finally let's the referee up. Haas truly is the smartest Haas brother." Monsoon: "There were TWO brothers Monsoon, that isn't an accomplishment." Solie: "As long as Haas knows he's better than his brother, he's fine." Monsoon: "If Charlie keeps hanging around that jezebel Jackie Gayda, he's likely to contract some deadly sexual virus. You know what that means." Solie: "THE HAAS BROTHERS EXPLODE!" Monsoon: "Now you're getting that New England marketing." Solie: "Haas easily handles Pillman... RELEASE BELLY TO BACK SUPLEX!" Monsoon: "German." Solie: "What?" Monsoon: "It's a German Suplex." Solie: "Not in 1990 it wasn't." Monsoon: "Haas gloating in front of this crowd, motioning that the belt will soon be around his waist." Solie: "You get all that from a smirk?" Monsoon: "I've gotten more from a eye rub." Solie: "Russ cracks his knuckles and rolls Pillman over, the Haas of Pain is imminent." Monsoon: "So is the rope grab." Solie: "Pillman, even while gasping for air... or whatever it is we breathe, if we even breathe, still grabs the bottom rope." Monsoon: "Haas overreacting and taking too much time... just drag him to the middle of the ring!" Solie: "Russ doing just that, but Pillman counters with an Inside Cradle!!" 1! 2! Monsoon: "Haas escapes, with a look of horror on his face! You'd think Jake Robert's snake had just been thrown on him!" Solie: "Or Terry Funk tried to suffocate him with a plastic bag!" Monsoon: "Or Earthquake had sat on him!" Solie: "Or Larry Zybisko had just crushed his hand in a car door!" Monsoon: "I can go all night, I'm just choosing to stop." Solie: "Fair enough." Monsoon: "Pillman whipped into the ropes, Haas expects an easy return but Pillman musters up the energy to leap frog him, rebound and hit a Cross Body Block. They sure did like Cross Body's back then." 1! 2! Solie: "He can't keep Haas down!" Monsoon: "Haas is supposed to be winning, not almost being pinned... what's the problem here?" Solie: "Pillman is kicking it into second gear like we've seen him do so many times before." Monsoon: "Against who, the Z-Man? Erik Watts? Please." Solie: "Pillman trying for a Suplex, but Haas blocks, and he's going for one of his own it looks like... he does, and drops Pillman forward across the top rope! Not the most technical move, but it gets the job done." Monsoon: "Pillman slumps to the apron and Haas wants to bring him back in with another Suplex... Pillman counters by ramming his shoulder into Haas' gut though! Haas is prone... SUNSET FLIP TO THE INSIDE!!" 1! 2! Solie: "Haas barely escapes!" Monsoon: "Pillman greets him with a high dropkick just as he stands, landing him prone near the corner." Solie: "Pillman rolling with it... to the top rope... TAKE OFF... DQ!! Oops, I mean DIVING LEGDROP! Haas is going into convulsions!" Monsoon: "Haas shakes himself right out of the ring and Pillman is forced to follow. There's a stern 10-count, so they'd better watch themselves." Solie: "Pillman points to the guard rail, probably not a good sign if your name is Haas." Monsoon: "Russ elbows Pillman in the abdomen as he's picked up, and there's a European Uppercut for good measure... Haas turns the tide and drops Pillman across the railing sternum first!" Solie: "Haas rolls back inside and Pillman is left to beat the count, which doesn't seem likely at this point..." 6! Monsoon: "Crowd members beginning to rally behind Pillman, some are haunting Russ with chants of "We Want Charlie"... morbid peons." 7! Solie: "Pillman crawling, Haas is taunting him the whole way." 8! Monsoon: "To the apron with Pillman, he still has to make it inside." 9! Solie: "Russ rushes over, but Pillman sneaks in and rakes the eyes! Haas stumbles backwards, Pillman springboards in..." Monsoon: "Springboard Cross Body. How shocking." Solie: "HE'S GOT HAAS HOOKED TIGHT!!" 1! 2!! 3!!! Winner Via Pinfall Brian Pillman (New DWI Champion) Solie: "PILLMAN IS VICTORIOUS!" Monsoon: "I don't believe this, how could Haas lose? He was obviously weakened by Terry Gordy." Solie: "Pillman was the one weakened, he survived multiple assaults from Big John Studd, including six million Bearhug's, and still came back to win the big one." Monsoon: "Kids, you're hearing it here first, next week, an open Battle Royale to decide who faces Pillman for the title next!" Solie: "Battle Royales are ALWAYS big hits!" Monsoon: "Don't I know it." Solie: "Good night, folks!"
