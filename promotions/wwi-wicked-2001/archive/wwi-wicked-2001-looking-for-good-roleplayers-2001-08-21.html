Each day I will send out a packet of the day's RPs. Each show you RP for has a 3 Roleplay limit, which means I will not accept anymore RPs from any member after their third.
Each roleplay is scored between 5-10. So, it is possible to win for doing only
2 RPs if you score a perfect 10 on each. In case of a tie, I will rely on strategies and the holds they use.
For each match you have, I'd like you to submit a strategy mail to me. The strat should include any moves you want to see or things you want to be done
(eg, Have Cyrus work on Jobber A's legs throughout the match to set him up for a Figure 4).
Also, it helps to have unique moves, because in addition to RPs, I will rely on the moves you list. If you put down "Bodyslam, punch, hiptoss", generally it will not match up to someone who has some new and creative moves.
