HCW-XCW...Time To Pay The Price

27-28 July 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<<In an abandoned New York warehouse former HCW and now XCW wrestler LOCO is pirched on top a stack of broken pallets. He's wearing black jeans and a black t-shirt which reads "HCW's MOST WANTED" in red letters. He looks down upon the camera men with great vengence and fury.....LOCO begins to speak in a cold somber tone>> "Funny how the passing of time heals so few wounds. Once the pride and the 'choosen one' in HCW is now the disgraced and humilated person you see before you. Look into my eyes and see the depths of my despair. For LOCO has been branded a broken man. A hero to his peers no more. Banished from HCW for crimes not my own. For standing up for what's right only to succumb to ignorance and betrayel. For this will be my legacy. It is written so now it should be so" <<LOCO climbs down from the pallets, to the floor. Broken glass scatters the floor like promises long since forgotten. A look of sadness flashes in LOCO's eye. A look of deep sincere and utter despair>> "I have not come here to beg for forgivness. For LOCO is just a victim of circumstance. I have come to clear my name once and for all. I have come to set 'the record straight'. I will be vindicated. I will be praised once more. Look into my eyes and know for what I tell you is the truth." <<LOCO clears his throat>> "I am no coward. When I found out there was a 6 man tag match to be feautured on an HCW card. I stepped foward and declared that my name be used. That LOCO as champion should represent XCW to the best of his abilities. However power struggles and complaining by some HCW officals made it impossible for LOCO to appear on the 6 man tag match. Simply they refused to have LOCO appear cause they were afraid of me. They were afraid of what it would mean to have the legend appear in thier own production as a center piece. They know as do you who the real deal is. They did not want to be over shawdowed by LOCO. They choose the low ground. To ride off into oblivion and simplicity and revel in their self proclamation that LOCO is a banished man" <<LOCO takes sunglasses out of his pocket and puts them one. A tear is seen running down his cheek. His voice is slow and gargled. He looks up towards the roof and stops.... if only for a second to take it all in.....once again the man.....the legend....speaks>> "So be it. So you have missed a chance at having the one and only true phenom wrestle on your card. Let's not forget it was LOCO who made Mega-Fight the most successful HCW PPV of ALL TIME. HCW you have challnged me many times and have always lost. From wrestlers to HCW "Shows" , LOCO has been an impact player in HCW. Just not the way I or you had invisioned it" <<The XCW Champion looks to be more and more energized. His spirit is picking up>> "XCW as your champion I wish I could be apart of the 6 man tag. Now you know why this is impossible. However with the talent you all have I am sure it will be the highlight of the HCW card. Give em hell......ladies and gentlmen....this interview is over" <<camera fades to black....only 6 words remain....STILL LOCO WITH OR WITHOUT YOU....>> ---overlay of a funky istrumental with a nice smooth beat----- <<Camera is focused on LOCO looking at a TV monitor of his first title defence in CCW>> LOCO: My second title defence in CCW I lost to a mid carder. <<video of LOCO getting pinned by Mission: Impossible>> LOCO: They said I'd never be a champion again. <<video of LOCO looking at Mission: Impossible holding the CCW belt>> LOCO: But they didn't know who I was. <<video of LOCO lifting weights , doing pushups , wrestling on a mat>> LOCO: And they didn't know how much I wanted to be champion again. <<Video of LOCO in HCW....Announcer "LOCO has Survival, picks him up <SLAM> covers him...1 2 3 !!!! LOCO WINS !!!!">> LOCO: So tell me courage doesn't mean a thing. <<Video of LOCO in EWF....Announcer "LOCO hooks Psycho's arm....places leg over the back of Psycho's head....jumps up.....SMASH...face foward right into the mat....HELLRAISER !!!!!!!>> LOCO: They sure never counted on the HELLRAISER. <<video of LOCO in XCW...Announcer "Out of nowhere LOCO with the HELLRAISER....that's how he does it....from any position....from any angle.....you think you have him beat.....then comes the...HELLRAISER>> LOCO: Tell me I'll never be the MAN!!! <<video of LOCO in XCW, Ring Announcer "Ladies an Gentleman , children of all ages....coming down the ilse standing 6 foot 2 inches tall , weighing in at 256 pounds....hailing from the Lone Star state.....here is Yoooooouuuuurrrrrrrrrrrrrrr XTREMEEEEEE CYYYYBERRRR WWREEESSSSTTTLING CH AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAA PION The One ......the Only.....the
