**A VOTE FOR LOCO IS A VOTE FOR LOCO** Well here I am....before you.....down on my knees [don't get excited... although it may come to that] asking for your support on the upcoming voting for RSPWF. I know I'm probably going to get flamed for writng out my resume for the past year. So be it. Atleast then I may get "most hated." Well here goes..... LOCO's Year in Review XCW--Won the XCW IC Title by defeating Jared Irons. Won the XCW World Title by defeating Eric Draven. HCW--Created the New Breed with RSPWF top 200 wrestlers, Vile Vince Viper and Mike "The Mechanic" Malone along with Immorality. Pinned Survival and won a big tag match in Survival's own home town [note...Survival was LOCO's tag team partner....long but good story]...got in major fued with El Perfecto --- To Excess Rick Williams -- which resulted in LOCO getting burned and thrashed and put through a burning table by El Perfecto and Johnnie Adonis. EWF--Won EWF World Title by defeating 18 other men in a tournament. ACEW-- Had a strong 6-1 run in ACEW before it closed down. WOWA--Undefeated in WOWA. Formed Lynch Mob with Vile Vince Viper [top 200], Immorality, Assault, Suicide, TLM, Lordzero, Iceblade, Confessor Rey Jeran and Hell's Angels. Won WOWA's Lethal Lottery '97 for a chance at the title. Defeated Rey Jeran as a masked "Vile Vince Viper" and won the WOWA World Title. Going into WOWA-HCW merge as top draw, the merged feds being named *ONE*. TWCW--Had a strong showing in TWCW towards the years end. **Thank You if you already have made it this far without flaming me...ha ha. Well before I leave you I wanted to post 2 roleplays from the past year from me. Not to say "Damn I'm great" but to more say please read and hopefully you will enjoy them. In advance...
