XCW Is Stronger Then Ever

23 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
XCW "we're the people your mom warned you about" XCW is a brand new fed with that old style flavor. We have some writers and an excellent homepage (follow link at bottom). We are commited to bringing the most "real" wrestling out there. Everyone claims they are the best. I won't lie to you and tell you we are. What I will say is come join XCW and make it the best. Be apart of the foundation and the history. We will do our best to be everything it is your looking for in a federation. I want to give you what it is you want. Come join XCW. We have applications on the homepage for easy fill out and access. We are a booked original wrestlers only league. Please come join.......we need you. Also looking for any additional card or match writers. Never can have to many. We offer an oppurtunity to showcase and mold your cards anyway you like. You decide 90% of the winners and 100% of how XCW will be viewed. Thank you for reading this. Come to XCW were you are needed and wanted. Brandon http://www.geocities.com/Area51/Vault/4210
