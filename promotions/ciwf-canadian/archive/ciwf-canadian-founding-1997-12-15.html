OH NO!!! Not Another E-Fed!, 15 December 1997

15 December 1997
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Yep, that's right! Another neophyte goes where angels fear to tread. Convoluted greetings from the Canadian Internet Wrestling Federation (CIWF)! We won't be ready to start up on a full-time basis for a while yet but we're hoping that by recruiting now, we can attract high quality and original roleplayers. The CIWF is a brand new e-wrestling league using only original characters and a simulator to decide match outcomes. (*NOTE*: Don't panic yet, the simulator just helps promote objectivity and pushes will have an influence on match results.) The CIWF would like to invite you to submit an application to have your wrestling character compete as a part of this latest e-fed. Please visit the CIWF's Web site at http://www.storm.ca/~shamus/ciwf/ for more details on the federation and how it will operate. There, your application can be made online. The current limit is for two characters per player although these can compete simultaneously as both singles wrestlers and as a tag team. This does not include managers or valets but check the rules page for more details. The CIWF will be kicking things off on February 22nd, 1998 with its "Winter Warfare Pay-Per-View" event and will be holding weekly cards every Sunday evening. We realize that this is a lengthy lead time but with the pending Christmas holidays and an unavoidable period offline in late January, this seemed to be the best time to begin operations. At the same time, we may organize special exhibition matches before February to allow new wrestlers to debut. Matches for the "Winter Warfare PPV" will be scheduled as follows: 1) The first twelve applicants accepted into the league will be entered into a Battle Royal Match to determine the CIWF World Heavyweight Champion. 2) The next twelve applicants accepted into the league will then be entered into another Battle Royal Match, this one to determine the CIWF Canadian National Champion. 3) The next four applicants accepted into the league have the choice of facing either a jobber in a singles match or a jobber tag team in a tag team match. 4) Any remaining applicants which are accepted will be scheduled against each other in the order in which their submissions are received. As a bribe, the CIWF will offer a free single-screen (800x600 pixels) Web page as a profile for any characters that are accepted. We will even assist you with artwork although no photographs of "real" wrestlers, please. This is provided, of course, that the character doesn't already have a Web page if they are being used in other federations. We hope you take this opportunity to check out the latest in e-wrestling! [name withheld] Executive Board The Canadian Internet Wrestling Federation http://www.storm.ca/~shamus/ciwf/
