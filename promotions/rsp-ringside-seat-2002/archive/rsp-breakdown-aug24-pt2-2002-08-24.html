[RSP] RSP Breadown [2/4 ]

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera cuts to the back where we see a white limo pull up to the loading dock. The limo parks next to a familiar looking S.U.V. As the doors of the limo open we see the masked henchmen X, Y,Z step out with ball bats in hand. Following behind them is RSPs president Sean Christopher. As Sean steps out of the limo he cackles seeing the S.U.V] SC: Men..do your worst. DO YOUR WORST~! [Sean enters the arena as the three masked men begin to smash the S.U.Vs with their baseball bats. We hear an evil laugh coming from inside the arena.] ["Gravel Pit" by Wu-Tang pounds throughout the arena as the crowd unleashes a THUNDEROUS POP!] Collen Blackwell: And here comes the manager of the 'black beast', Sinister! I know that I've been waiting to hear what Sinister has to say, especially because of the attack laid upon him by those three masked men when he came out here last week to confront Christopher! Vince Richardson: Ha! The idiot got what he deserved! NO ONE calls Sean Christopher...well, another way to call a cat a kitten...they're five little letters that I'm missin' here. Collen Blackwell: There you go with the Naughty by Nature lyrics again Vince. Vince Richardson: [Looks at Collen with a look of utter shock] I can't believe you actually KNOW about Naughty by Nature! Good lord! [Mr. Greene makes his way down the aisle wearing a dark brown Armani suit, navy blue dress shirt and leather dark brown dress shoes. He slaps high-fives with the hands that are extended towards him as he approaches the ring] Vince Richardson: You know, that guy just gets under my skin each time I see him! There's something about him that just bothers me! Collen Blackwell: The fact that he manages Sinister and has enjoyed quite a bit of success in doing so? Vince Richardson: Shut up! No, that's not it! It's because...dammit, he just rubs me the wrong way! [When Mr. Greene is roughly halfway towards the ring the arena lights go out. Suddenly, the aisles are illuminated with red lights as Sinister appears in the aisle as the beat of the music picks up. Sinister is grinning evilly as he glances slowly around the crowd wearing wrestling pants, the right leg in black and the left leg in red. Written in white on his right leg is "Black Beast" and written on his left leg is "Chi-Town". Sinister's black wrestling boots have "Black Beast" written on both sides of them in white. There is one large scar that runs vertically through the center of his right eye, extending from the top of his brow down to his cheekbone] [DEAFENING POP!] Collen Blackwell: What a presence this guy is! It doesn't matter how many times I see Sinister, be in the ring or just broadcasting thoughts and feelings from various locations, he always makes people stare in wonderment. Vince Richardson: Yeah, they stare because they wonder how this guy can have such a peanut for a brain and also how he can claim that he doesn't use steroids to be that damn big! There's no way that Sinister can naturally be 330 lbs. of solid muscle. You know he's using something CB! Collen Blackwell: Sinister has been accused of that in the past VR and he has cleared every single test and has refuted every allegation about him using illegal substances with evidence! Sinister works very hard and is genetically blessed, plain and simple. Vince Richardson: Yeah, yeah, whatever you say, I know the truth of it! [A tattoo reading "Black Beast" is seen on his right arm, "Black" in a semi-circular shape on his upper right arm/shoulder and "Beast" written underneath in a semi-circular shape. Between the two words is a black panther. On his left arm, "ChiTown" is tattooed onto his other upper arm/shoulder. He is carrying a black baseball bat that has the words "Soul Pole" etched onto it with white lettering. Sinister slowly makes his way down the aisle, knocking fists with the fans hands and fists that are extended. Once Sinister reaches the ring, he knocks fists with Mr. Greene, pulls himself up using the top rope, then enters the ring by stepping over the top rope] Collen Blackwell: Sinister, as always, looks ready to explode and is ready for one hell of a match against Christopher who who better train very hard for the no rules match he has against Sinister on August 18. You have to believe that Sin is favored in that match. Vince Richardson: Hell no! Sinister is the one expected to lose because Sean Christopher is just so much better than Sin and that's just the way it is! Collen Blackwell: I don't know Vince, Sin has proven time and time again that he can overcome very large odds to achieve success and victory. I've personally witnessed him defeat opponents that, by all means, he should have lost to because of the damage inflicted upon him during those battles. Vince Richardson: Oh stop suckin' up to Sinister! You know as well as I that he's greatly overrated and is lucky to even be in the same ring with someone the caliber of Christopher! Collen Blackwell: Oh? So Sinister's ranking number sixty-nine out of the top one-hundred wrestlers in the business, votes made BY wrestlers, managers and other personnel mind you, is bogus? VR: Dammit, shut up!! [He points the "Soul Pole" towards Ricard and yells "DECIMATED!! SINISTER STYLE!!" Sinister then thrusts his right fist skyward, the sign for the Chi-Town Massacre. As he does this, fireworks explode from the ring posts and the fans give a huge pop. Sinister then hands the "Soul Pole" to Mr. Greene and motions for the house microphone. The ring announcer hands the microphone to him and Sinister smiles quickly while giving a slight nod. He then slowly walks to the center of the ring and looks around slowly as a hush falls over the crowd] Sin: "Well, well, well. What's up RSP!? [HUGE POP!] Good, I'm glad everybody's feelin' aaaaaaaaaaight. [Laughter fills the arena] Before I get down to business, there's a little somethin' somethin' I need to get off of my chest. [He suddenly smiles widely and begins to scan the crowd slowly] I need to expound upon this whole uh...p-word thing." [A ROAR is heard from all of the men and screams from some women] Collen Blackwell: Haha, it seems that Sinister has touched upon a subject that everyone here loves! Vince Richardson: You're damn right we love it! Hell, I don't understand how anyone CAN'T love...well, you know!" Sin: "That's right, the jiggable pie! [Another round of laughter] It seems that one Mr. Sean Christopher [BOOS rain down from the crowd]...yeah, I feel the same way, trust me, the sorry ass, but anyway, it seems that he took offense to me calling him a...a..." CROWD: Sean's a PU$$Y...[clapclapclap clap...clapclap]...Sean's a PU$$Y...[clapclapclap clap...clapclap] [Sinister lowers the microphone and has a rather loud guffaw while enjoying the crowds reaction] Vince Richardson: See what he's done!? Dammit, now we've got to hear them associate Sean and...and that word! That just ruins it for me! Collen Blackwell: Haha, well, how do you think Sean feels right now? Sin: [Stops laughing and raises the microphone] "Now folks, if Sean had ANY grain of intelligence, do you know what he would have said in reaction to me calling him that?" CROWD: What!? Sin: "Oh no, don't you start that too! [Another round of laughter] If Sean had ANY kind of brains, he would have said, 'Hey Sin, you're right, I am a PU$$Y! Do you want to know why?'" CROWD: Why!? Sin: [Points to himself] "If Sean were ANY kind of man, like I was he would have said, just as I always say when someone calls ME a pu$$y, 'Sin, I am what I eat!'" [TREMENDOUS POP from the men and THUNDEROUS embarrassed pop from some of the women while other women SCREAM with pleasure] Vince Richardson: You know Collen, I now have new-found respect for this man! Sinister knows the deal! WELL SAID Sinister, haha! Collen Blackwell: I think the capacity crowd agrees with you and Sinister Vince! Sinister's havin' too much fun in there! Look at that smile, haha! [Sinister indeed is smiling like a kid in the candy store as he continues to pace the ring while nodding slowly and winking at some of the attractive ladies around ringside. Sinister stops in the center of the ring and his entire demeanor changes in the blink of an eye. The crowd, seeing this, immediately quiets in anticipation] Sin: "Now, onto business. Sean, it seems that you KNOW, deep down and in the back of your mind, that you simply-can-NOT defeat me on your own you pathetic scrub! [HUGE FACE POP!] So what do you do? You come out here, PRETEND to be a bad ass and then when I come out to call your bluff, you have X,Y,Z...A,B,C...1,2,3...DO-RE-MI...I can't see, pee on me, hung like flea, I need three...[UPROARIOUS LAUGHTER] jump me from behind with lead pipes, no less, and do one hell of a Pearl Harbor job on me! [HUGE HEEL POP!] Vince Richardson: Wasn't that great!? The big man was cut down to size after that lovely little event! Collen Blackwell: That was a pure shame and a sickening display! Sinister is fortunate that he didn't suffer career-threatening injuries at the hands of those three goons, whoever the hell they are! Sin: "That's all good though because one, I now know just how much of a sorry sap son of a bitch you are Christopher [LOUD MIXED POP!] but two, as all of you saw me being loaded into the back of the ambulance, who came out to check on me? The Big Chill. [HUGE MIXED POP!] Collen Blackwell: The Big Chill had us all fooled Vince! I thought the man was retiring from the sport entirely because of that fireball to the face and Tommy Rowlands was sure gloating! However, I bet you he's not gloating anymore, is he!? Vince Richardson: I don't want to hear that crap! It's bad enough Sinister is back on his feet but now he AND Chill are friends again!? God help us all! Sin: "Las Vegas Strip Slobbernocker huh? [CRAZY HARDCORE POP!] All right Sean, if that's the style of match you want to endure and watch your very career slip out of your grasp then so be it! It seems to me that you're another sucka' who has to learn the hard way that you do NOT f[BEEP] with me! [WILD FACE POP!] ON August 18, it's going to be my damn pleasure to teach you that lesson, one drop of blood at a time! Until then Sean, I recommend that you speak to friends and family that you've not seen for some time because when I finish with ya', your power of speech may not exist anymore! Prepare to be..." CROWD & Sinister: "...DECIMATED!! SINISTER STYLE!!" [CRAZY POP as Sinister thrusts his right fist skyward while looking around the crowd slowly. He then tosses the microphone back to the ring announcer and, with a nod, turns, steps over the top rope and makes his way towards the back as Mr. Greene follows in tow] Collen Blackwell: Ladies and gentlemen, I would NOT want to be in Sean Christopher's shoes right now! He's going to have to face a very angry and focused Sinister and that, in itself, can be a very potentially dangerous situation! Vince Richardson: Whatever Collen! Sinister is all talk and Sean is going to take that big oaf to school while beating him from pillar to post! Collen Blackwell: We'll be right back. The tag team titles are on the line...NEXT~! ========================================== V/O: She was on top of the world! [A shot of two nicely shaped legs of a woman are seen..] V/O: Then it all fall apart! [A pictuare of a nice mansion is seen..that fades to a shot of a old house falling apart.] V/O: Can she reclaim glory? Can she make it to the top once again? [A shot of the back of a woman is shown.] V/O: SHE'S BACK....... [The woman turns around..but before we can tell who it is the screen goes to black.] V/O: [in a whisper] deeessssirrrreeee ========================================================== [Camera cuts to the back where we see The Greatest Team Alive beating The Dime Notes down with hard chair shots.] Maddox: Get his ass to the ring~! [CRUNCH] [CRUNCH] [CRUNCH] Dax: How pretty do you boys feel now? Maddox: Take our titles? Screw with us? Dax: Not going to happen!! Maddox: Not going to happen!! [Dax slams Darby Sharpe into a cement wall. Maddox picks up Justin Simmons and suplexes him onto a table.] Dax: You're finished..You're finished!! [Dax and Maddox drag The Dime Notes toward the ring. As the camera cuts to the ring we see Joe "Twink" Jones and Tyler Christian standing in the ring.] Collen Blackwell: Well we were scheduled to have a tag title match. Vince Richardson: The tag team champs are being taken apart by The Greatest Team Alive. [Maddox and Dax roll The Dime Notes into the ring and ask for the ref to ring the bell... DING..DING..DING] Collen Blackwell: The ref going to allow this? Vince Richardson: Why not? Everything took place before the bell. Hell, half of it took place during the commerical break. [Tyler and Joe have some words...Tyler picks up Simmons and piledrives him. Tyler covers Justin as Joe looks on shaking his head... ONE TWO THREE DING...DING...DING] Collen Blackwell: You got to be kidding me! Vince Richardson: The ref is talking to Anderson! AM: Ladies and Gentlemen..the winner of the match as the result of a pinfall...AND NEEEWWW RSP TAG TEAM CCCCHAMPPPIONS..."GODS GIFT" TYLER CHRISTIAN AND JOE "TWINK" JONES!!! Collen Blackwell: I don't agree with this. Vince Richardson: From the looks of it neither does Jones! [Jones is handed his tag team belt but he throws it down. Tyler picks it up and holds both belts in the air. Jones grabs the titles and throws them to the mat. Tyler and Jones begin to push and shove each other. ] Collen Blackwell: We have new tag team champions and they don't like each other!! Vince Richardson: Joe should be happy, somehow that idiot is a double champion! [As Tyler and Joe begin to argue with each other Dax and Maddox throw The Dime Notes to the outside of the ring. Dax and Maddox begin to work on Tyler and Joe as officals rush to the ring to seperate the four men.] Collen Blackwell: Our second hour is about to begin..stay right where you are!!
