[RSP] RSP Breakdown [4/4]

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ - ]RSP-----------------------------------RSP[-] Main Event Gauntlet Match [-]RSP-----------------------------------RSP[-] Collen Blackwell: Well we're back and it's main event time! Vince Richardson: 6 of RSPs top names could collide in a few minutes. Collen Blackwell: It's been a high energy night already it's only going to get hotter. Anderson? AM: Ladies and Gentlemen the following contest is scheduled for one fall. It is a special gauntlet match to be contested under Rumble Rules!... [HUGE CROWD POP] AM: Introducing first....hailing from The Bronx, New York City New York...weighing in at 265 lbs....he is the current RSP Television Champion...here is T.....B......C.... The Bigggggggggggggggg Chillllllllllllllllllllllllllll........... ("Calm like a Bomb" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. The Big Chill walks out from the backstage entrance and stands still. TBC is wearing a black tank top with "Reign of Terror" written on the back, black wrestling trunks, black boots, a "RoT" doo-rag and black Oakley sunglasses. TBC has his head shaved with about a day's worth of growth on it and a goatee. He has a tattoo of an icicle on his right bicep, and on his left pectoral he has the words "Badd Intentions" written over a skull and crossbones can be made out.) I be walkin' god like a dog My narrative fearless My word war returns to burn Like Baldwin home from Paris Like Steel from a furnace I was born landless This is tha native son Born of Zapata's guns (Suprisingly , the crowd gives him some support. TBC looks surprised and smiles slightly. He has the RSP Television Title strapped to his waist. TBC raises one arm as pyrotechnics explode around him. He begins to walk to the ring. As he heads down the aisle, TBC picks up the pace until he is at a jog.) Stroll through tha shanties And tha cities remains Same bodies buried hungry But with different last names These vultures rob everything Leave nothing but chains Pick a point on tha globe Yes tha pictures tha same There's a bank There's a church a myth and a hearse A mall and a loan a child dead at birth There's a widow pig parrot A rebel to tame A whitehooded judge A syringe and a vein (As he approaches the ring, he slides under the bottom rope and jumps to his feet. The crowd pops. TBC extends his arms outwards and spins around a few times. He walks over to a corner and climbs up to the second turnbuckle. TBC raises the TV title with his right arm and the crowd pops again.) And tha riot be tha rhyme of tha unheard what ya say, what ya say, what ya say, what? What ya say, what ya say, what ya say, what? What ya say, what ya say, what ya say, what? What ya say, what ya say, what ya say, what? [TBC hops off the turnbuckle and begins to take off his warm up gear.] Calm like a bomb Ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite Calm like a bomb Ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite, ignite [TBC's entrance music dies down. TBC, with championship belt on his shoulder waits in the middle of the ring. "Gravel Pit" by Wu-Tang pounds throughout the arena] [CRAZY ANTICIPATION POP!] VR: "Oh this is GREAT! Right off the bat we have Chill and Sinister! Even though these two shook hands when Sinister was gettin' carted out of here in an ambulance, which I loved by the way, there's still some uncertainty between the two. Sinister even admitted that in the spot we saw while he was sitting in his Lincoln Navigator. I hate to admit it, but Sin-man does have that SUV lookin' sharp!" CB:"While that may be true, one can only imagine what is going through the minds of these three men! Mr. Greene is, no doubt, concerned about Sinister and his health. Is Sinister one-hundred percent after that attack by three men using lead pipes!? Is Sinister going to battle the Big Chill and try to defeat him? Is the Big Chill one-hundred percent after the fireball to the face? He claims he is but can we be so sure?" [The curtains part and Mr. Greene makes his way down the aisle wearing a dark green Armani suit, white dress shirt and leather black dress shoes. He slaps high-fives with the hands that are extended towards him as he approaches the ring. When Mr. Greene is roughly halfway towards the ring the arena lights go out.] [LOUD POP!] CB: "That can only mean that Sinister is here!" VR: [Sarcastically] "Yay, I'm so excited!" [Suddenly, the aisles are illuminated with red lights as Sinister appears in the aisle as the beat of the music picks up] [WICKED LOUD FACE POP!] CB: "And here is the man that many feel may very well be the next RSP Heavyweight champion!" VR: "That's bull Collen! Saul Akiva is the man, and if ANYONE is going to beat him, it definitely will NOT be Sinister! Big Chill is FAR better than Sinister!" [Sinister is grinning evilly as he glances slowly around the crowd wearing wrestling pants, the right leg in black and the left leg in red. Written in white on his right leg is "Black Beast" and written on his left leg is "Chi-Town". Sinister's black wrestling boots have "Black Beast" written on both sides of them in white. There is one large scar that runs vertically through the center of his right eye, extending from the top of his brow down to his cheekbone. A tattoo reading "Black Beast" is seen on his right arm, "Black" in a semi-circular shape on his upper right arm/shoulder and "Beast" written underneath in a semi-circular shape. Between the two words is a black panther. On his left arm, "ChiTown" is tattooed onto his other upper arm/shoulder. He is carrying a black baseball bat that has the words "Soul Pole" etched onto it with white lettering. The Big Chill is pacing back and forth in the ring.] CB: "It doesn't matter how many times I see Sinister, he is just as physically imposing as he was the very first time he stepped into a ring!" VR: "Yeah, and he also just gets uglier by the moment!" [Sinister slowly makes his way down the aisle, knocking fists with the fans hands and fists that are extended. Once Sinister reaches the ring, he knocks fists with Mr. Greene, pulls himself up using the top rope, then enters the ring by stepping over the top rope. He points the "Soul Pole" towards The Big Chill and yells "DECIMATED!! SINISTER STYLE!!" TBC just stares right at Sinister.] VR: "Uh oh, I think we just had our questions answered! That was an obvious threat to Big Chill and Big Chill hasn't taken his eyes off of Sinister since he stepped through the curtains!" CB: "It appears that Big Chill and Sinister are very much on opposite sides of battle here ladies and gentlemen. Neither man has made a move towards the other but Sinister's actions and the way that Big Chill is looking at him indicate more than meets the eye!" [Sinister then thrusts his right fist skyward, the sign for the Chi-Town Massacre. As he does this, fireworks explode from the ring posts and the fans give a huge pop. Sinister then hands the "Soul Pole" to Mr. Greene, turns and glares at The Big Chill while awaiting the bell.] CB: "Sinister and The Big Chill have now engaged in one hell of a staring contest! Look at the intensity these two men have! Despite the fact that Sinister towers over him, The Big Chill is not backing down one step as they slowly stride towards one another in the center of the ring!" VR: "Like I said, Sinister is an overrated fool and T to the B to the C is going to school his big goofy ass!" [The 6'10", thickly-built Sinister and the 6'2", athletically-built Big Chill step toe-to-toe, saying nothing but then again, their eyes tell the entire story. The referee, not sure if he should try to separate them or not, simply shrugs and signals for the bell to ring. The ref attempts to retrieve the TV belt from TBC, but with a single glance, the ref backs off.] [DING! DING! DING!] [POP!] CB: "There's the official bell ladies and gentlemen and here we go!" VR: "Well come on dammit!" [Despite the bell ringing, Sinister and Big Chill continue to stand toe to toe, neither man flinching or moving a muscle. Mr. Greene begins to pace the ring, holding the "Soul Pole" in his right hand and simply looking at the two men with a worried expression upon his face. After a few silent moments in the ring, Sinister breaks the staring contest by taking one step backward and beginning to mouth some inaudible words. Big Chill likewise takes a step back and while responding verbally, he begins to point to Sinister as well as the pacing Mr. Greene. TBC's face turns a shade of red as he is visibly getting excited. He points at Sinister and then back at himself.] CB: This situation is at the boiling point! Anything can happen! [TBC lifts the belt up to Sinister's face. Sinister slaps down at it. Sinister is yelling back, shaking his head up and down, eyes bulging. TBC slaps his own chest with a few forearms. TBC drops the belt and the ref scrambles to get it out of the ring. Sinister cracks his neck. Sinister spreads his arms as does Big Chill. Both men nod. In the blink of an eye, a FLURRY of lefts and rights begin to fly upon both men!] [HUGE POP!] CB: "It has exploded ladies and gentlemen and they are trading rights and lefts!" VR: "It's about time dammit! Come on Chill, pummel that sorry ass into the mat!" [Surprisingly, Big Chill begins to back Sinister into a near corner with quicker shots. Chill leans against Sinister and doubles him over with a right kneelift to the midsection. He then whips Sinister into the far opposite corner and follows in with an AVALANCHE! Sinister is stunned in the corner as Big Chill wastes no time in unleashing a VICIOUS knife-edgechop!] [WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!] [Sinister's face begins to tighten in pain...and anger] [Again Big Chill unloads a CHOPPING knife-edge shot! TBC lays the bad mouth on Sinister.] [WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!] [Sinister clenches his jaw...and SUDDENLY GRABS CHILL AND SPINS HIM INTO THE CORNER!!] [FACE POP!] [Sinister unleashes a series of right knee-lifts to the midsection that causes Big Chill to struggle for air. Sinister stands Chill straight up and BLASTS into his chest with a knife-edge chop of his own! TBC's face contorts with pain.] [WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!] [Sinister measures Big Chill and ROCKS him with a well-placed right back-elbow! Chill's eyes go wide in pain as Sinister measures him and DENTS his chest with another knife-edge chop!] [WHOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO!] [Sinister then locks his arms around Big Chill's back and TOSSES him across the ring!] [THUD!] [Big Chill arches his back in pain] CB: "These two have gone at it at a fast pace and Chill had the upper-hand at first but Sin has quickly turned the tides!" VR: "Dammit! Sinister makes me SICK!" CB: "You say that quite a bit these days Vince!" VR: "Because it's true, damn him!" CB: TBC back to his feet. Is he...is he smiling? VR: Chill is nodding his head. Sinister breaks into a smile as well. This is strange, to say the least. They circle and lock up again. [TBC and Sinister lock up and TBC grabs a side headlock. He goes behind Sinister and grabs hold of a hammerlock. TBC turns it into an arm bar. TBC drops to his knees and hits a fireman's carry, but holds onto the arm. TBC quickly up and drops a leg onto the arm and shoulder. TBC drives a knee into the arm and regains hold of the arm bar, leaning a knee against Sinister's head. TBC slaps the back of Sinister's head and releases the hold. Sinister gets to his feet. TBC points at him, talking trash.] VR: Chill's holding a wrestling clinic out there! CB: I don't know if I would slap Sinister in the back of the head! You're only liable to get him mad and angry Sinister is a dangerous Sinister! [Sinister motions to TBC to lock up. Sinister and TBC look up and Sinister executes a go behind. Sinister hits a waist lock takedown. Sinister floats over TBC and locks in a front face chancery on TBC. Sinister lets TBC go and gives him a slap to the back of the neck. TBC quickly gets to his feet.] VR: Disgraceful! Sinister is showing no respect for the Television Champion! CB: Simply looks like Sinister giving TBC a little of his own medicine! We've seen both men go toe to toe, now they are showing off the finer points of the sport! [TBC and Sinister lock up and TBC grabs a side headlock. He goes behind Sinister and grabs hold of a hammerlock. TBC turns it into an arm bar. He drives a shoulder into the back of Sinister's shoulder again and again. TBC dips his weight into the back of the shoulder and drives Sinister to the mat. With Sinister on his stomach, TBC pulls back on the arm and arches back. Sinister is in pain, but as quickly as TBC applied the hold, he releases it. Sinister hops to his feet. Both men go forehead to forehead and once begin to yell at each other.] AM: Drawing number three...VVVVVAAAANCCCE RRRANDHHHALLL [A weary Vance Randhal approaches the ring as Sinister and The Big Chill continue jawing at each other. Randhal climbs into the ring. Sinister and TBC seem oblivious of the latest entry to the match. Randhal attacks TBC.] CB: I guess Randhal figured that it would be smarter not to mess with the bigger Sinister. Randhal with rights and lefts to the head of The Big Chill. [Sinister levels Randhal with a vicious clothesline.] VR: OH MY GOD! Did you see Randhal's head snap back? CB: TBC back his feet slowly as Sinister grabs Randhal by the hair. [Sinister yells something at TBC. He plants Randhal with a Soviet suplex. TBC grabs Randhal by the hair and yells something at Sinister. TBC whips Randhal into the ropes. He catches Randhal on the rebound and hits a tilt-a-whirl suplex. He gets up and jabs a finger into Sinister's chest.] VR: What are they doing? Sinister saved Chill from Randhal's attack but they're not really working together. [Sinister slaps TBC's hand away. He picks up Randhal and whips him into the ropes. Sinister hits a spinebuster which rattles the ring.] CB: If I didn't know any better, I'd think TBC and Sinister are playing a game of "Anything you can do, I can do better!" Now, they're back chest to chest arguing! I don't get it! VR: It's called the spirit of competition Collen, nothing you would know of! Sin and TBC are two very proud wrestlers who want to prove that they are the best in the business! All we're seeing here is a battle of desire to prove that point! I think it's great! [We see Sinister mouth the words, "All right, let me see you do this." Sinister then lifts a dazed Randhal and proceeds to wrap a large right hand around his throat! Randhal's eyes go wide as the crowd pops with great anticipation!] [POP!] [Sinister then HOISTS Randhal high into the air and DRIVES him to the mat with a furious choke slam!] [BIG POP!] [TBC looks at the downed Randhal, chuckles slightly then pushes Sinister out of the way. He then slowly lifts Randhal whips him into the far ropes, and as Randhal rebounds, TBC plants him with a very nicely executed CRADLE DDT!] [MIXED POP!] [Once again, after Chill stands, he and Sinister are chest to chest, mouthing and pointing to the downed Randhal] CB: I don't think I've ever seen anything like this before ladies and gentlemen! Sinister and Big Chill are punishing Randhal just to try to outdo one another! VR: Yeah, isn't it great!? AM: ENTERING AT NUMBER 4....RSP WESTERN STATES HERITAGE CHAMPION....SSSSAUL AKKKIVA!! [Saul slowly walks to the ring taking his time. Inside the ring Chill and Sinister pick up Randhal and are about to throw him over the top rope. Akiva slides into the ring from behind and clotheslines Chill and Sinister sending Randhal to the floor.] AM: VANCE RANDHAL HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!! CB: Randhal is out. It was a tough showing for him. VR: I'm not sure that was a wise move by Akiva. Sinister and Chill now have a new victim. [As Chill and Sinister look at each other then Akiva. Akiva slowly goes back to the outside of the ring. Chill and Sinister turn their attention to each other and begin to trade blows as the crowd pops.] CB: Chill and Sinister going at it. They're not letting up tonight. They want someone to pound on..even if that someone is each other! VR: Akiva on the outside watching. He's the champ I guess he can do that. CB: WAITE A MIUTE..WAITE A MINUTE... VR: It's Fabian Cruz from the crowd!! [Cruz charges from the crowd from behind Akiva with a nightstick in hand! As Akiva turns around as Cruz stands up and smashes the Nightstick over Akiva's skull dropping the Western States Heritage champion like a dead weight! Blood starts to flow down Akiva's face and drip onto the floor as Cruz motions for the mic and stands over Akiva] Cruz: Say hello to my ugly stick... [Cruz pokes Akiva with the nightstick and grins down sadisticly] Cruz: See ya at the P_P_V big boy. [The ref motions for the bell as Cruz ducks out of the ring heading back up the aisle as Akiva rolls on the mat in agony!] AM: FABIAN CRUZ HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!! CB: Was Cruz even officaly in? VR: I guess so. CB: Well Cruz with the sneak attack. Akiva and Cruz continue to build hatred between each other. [Chill and Sinister see Akiva down and continue to fight with each other. Sinister whips Chill into the ropes and hits him with a hard clothesline then picks him up with and slam him. Sinister goes off the ropes and tries for an elbow drop but Chill moves out of the way. ] CB: Chill and Sinister it's hard to figuare these two out. VR: Good friends..better enemies..those two are the best example of that. [Chill begins to kick and stomp at Sinisters shoulder and elbow. Chill then applies a crossface to Sinister. As Chill puts on the pressure from the back X, Y, and Z come charging to the ring.] CB: X, Y, and Z.. VR: Sean Christopher out from the back directing traffic. [X, Y, and Z attack Chill and Sinister taking them both down with their baseball bats. Sean Christopher is at the top of the entrance ramp.] CB: X, Y, and Z once again taking out Sinister at Christophers orders. [X and Z whip Sinister into the ropes. As Sinister bounces off they try to clothesline him with the baseball bat Sinister just runs through the bat snapping it.] CB: SINISTER JUST SNAPPED THAT BAT!! VR: Akiva!! Akiva!! [Saul Akiva is getting to his feet. Y sends Chill into the ropes Chill runs into Akiva who holds the rope down sending Chill over the top.] AM: THE BIG CHILL HAS BEEN ELIMINATED!!. [Chill drags Y out of the ring and the two begin to fight toward the back. Akiva and Sinister set X and Z up] CB: Double powerbomb!! Sinister and Akiva with a double powerbomb on X and Z!! VB: Look out Sean!! [Sean Christopher turns around and is met with a kick to the stomach followed by a DDT from Vance Randhal.] AM: ENTERING AT NUMBER SIX....EEERIC EEEMERSON!! [As Eric opens the curtains he comes face to face with Vance Randhal.] CB: Randhal and Emerson!! Randhal and Emerson!! [Derek Douglas hits Randhal from behind with a piece of wood. Douglas and Emerson begin to double team Randhal.] VR: Emerson and Douglas draggin Randhal to the back. CB: Look in the ring!! [ROOF BLOWING POP] VR: AKIVA AND...AKIVA AND, AND SINISTER~! [Akiva and Sinister are face-to-face in the ring. Saul nods his head at Sinister then leaps over the top rope.] CB: Akiva eliminates himself!! VR: Why did he do that? [Saul points at Sinister and says, "Another day!..Your time is coming..Another day"] AM: LADIES AND GENTLEMEN SAUL AKIVA HAS ELIMINATED HIMSELF..YOUR WINNER...SSSSINNNISSSTTTTER~! CB: Sinister wins!! VR: Man Akiva. I'm not to sure what his motives were there. CB: Akiva is getting more manipultive, Akiva is getting more dangerous, Akiva is getting more arrogant then ever before. He was a monster now he's a deadly monster! VR: Can Fabian Cruz slay the monster? We'll find out only on pay-per-view!! CB: We'll see you in Sin City~! [Credits roll as the camera fades.] [C] 2002, RSP Entertainment Inc.
