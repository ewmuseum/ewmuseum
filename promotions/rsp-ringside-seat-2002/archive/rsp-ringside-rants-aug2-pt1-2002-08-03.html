Ringside_______________ Seat________________________Promotions PRESENTS Ringside Rants 8/02/02 [ ]Saul Akiva [RSP Western States Heritage Champion] [ ]Sinister [ ]The Big Chill [RSP Television Champion] [ ]"Top Dog" Damian Legacy [ ]Joe "Twink" Jones [RSP Cruiserweight Champion] [ ]"The American Rage" Chris Page [Debut] [ ]"Gods Gift" Tyler Christian [ ]Totaly Rad Connection[Debut] [Note: The following is a rebroadcast of an earlier interview taken from RAW T.V] [Fade in.] [We come face to face with a dilapidated and weathered wrestling ring. No more than 12ft by 12ft. Its canvas is covered in a layer of dust that befits the surroundings, a tired old boxing gymnasium. A punch bag rests on its side with its once proud red leather cover split and faded to almost white. It's dark, unused and has been laid to rest many years ago.] [Saul Akiva stands in the middle of the ring. The footprints leading to him carved through the dust. A few hints of colour try and burn their way through. His broad shoulders and powerful build make him seem gargantuan in comparison, this is a far cry from where he finds himself in a few days.] "This was where I escaped." [We swoop in to his face. Against the cold, grey background it is a breath of freshness. Piercing green eyes, dark tanned skin, even the flecks of silver in his hair sparkle. For the first time in a long time he looks healthy.] "Every Tuesday, after being able to stay up late the night before to watch my heroes battle it out. Sweat, bleed and curse their way through the greatest matches of their lives. Hardin, Thunder, Annis, James, Claw, Ezra, Styles, McCray, Creed, Spikowski, LaGrange. [He lists each name slowly, allowing time to picture their face.] Names that still make me shiver to this day. Matches that still make my mouth dry up just thinking about them." [His eyes revel as he recalls, it wasn't a happy childhood by any means but it's funny how you block things out. He stands in street gear, a brown leather carryall by his feet, crisp new blue jeans and a RAW logo T-shirt that hugs his stocky figure.] "I'd always put on my RAW T-Shirt, the only one I ever had first thing on a Tuesday. I grew up with it; it followed every curve of my body by the time it finally gave up the ghost. 'Started off black with that fiery red familiar RAW emblazoned across my chest but in the end it was grey with a crackled orange but it didn't matter. I never bought another though, not in all those years. I felt like one of my heroes. I could'a only been what/ 9, 10, maybe 11 when I first saw it. It made me feel special made me feel like one of 'em. Didn't matter to me that a million other kids had it, didn't matter, not one bit. Because when I pulled it over my head I was in a whole other place." [He takes a deep breath and coils his head back.] "Every Tuesday." [Running a hand through his short cropped hair he looks back towards us.] "And I still do it, I'm still the same kid I was all those years ago. I'm a fan above everything else but now, I get the chance to live out those dreams for real. I ran here day in day out. It's been abandoned for years now, but back then, when I stepped into this ring it came alive. I could hear the crowd cheering as I ran from one side to another. My heart pumping as I relived and re-enacted those memorable moments, and always, always at the end, after the most gruelling and exhausting match I could imagine I would have my arm raised. And then, I'd hear it... The chanting of my name as I was proclaimed the new World Champion, my heart swelled each and every single time. It didn't matter to me that there was nobody to see it, share it. It didn't even matter that it was all in my head because for those few seconds every day I felt what it was like to be a hero, a champion. That is my drive." [He runs his tongue across his dry lips, almost tasting what it was like all those years ago. He rubs palm and thumb together getting a feel for the air, feeling the atmosphere he used to create.] "That's how I came to be. I was so determined to make that feeling real. No matter what barriers and obstacles were put in my way, not matter what problems I faced I was going to make it. It didn't matter what it would take because I had those memories in my mind. The memories of running the ropes, the times when I beat my heroes, dropped them with a suplex, shook them with a DDT, locked them up in a Figure Four...It didn't matter what it would take." [He slides a hand up his clean shaven jaw line and rubs it against his temple, his eyes drop down to the canvas below as he moves his feet around full circle.] "It took a lot and I had more barriers than I thought I could break through at times but I kept going and I made it. My dreams have already come true, all but one that is. The feeling I had at the very end, when I heard you all chanting my name, all of you on your feet saluting a man who fought the best fight of his life to become the champion. In a few short days when I step out in front of the entire world my dreams will truly be fulfilled. Only this time it's for real. Creighton, Cardera, Vasquez, you were never in those dreams. We're all a generation apart from those heroes. We have the chance to give kids something new to dream about." [Saul kneels down and runs his palm across the canvas and as he parts the dust a bright, bright blue comes shining through. A smile breaks across his face as he rubs his palms together.] "I'll always have those memories, whatever the result but I want to add to them, I want to prove that I am... Quite Simply The RAW World Champion." [We fade out as Saul picks up his bag and pulls it up to his side. He stays standing in the ring as we circle back out.] ======================================================================= * The scene fades in to a still shot of a black Lincoln Navigator with white interior parked in a non-disclosed location. The sky is an overcast gray and a cool, steady breeze blows the trees surrounding the area. The RSP camera focuses upon the Navigator, specifically the license plate, and we see One Sin. This indicates to the viewing audience that the man known to the RSP as Sinister is the owner of this vehicle. Resting upon a very shiny pair of twenty-two inch rims and high-profile tires, the SUV appears to be showroom new. The back windows have a limo tint and we can faintly see a figure in the SUV. The camera makes its way around to the driver side and as it does so, we see the unmistakable bald head of Sinister. We can only see a beige-colored and ribbed sweater upon his person and he appears to be speaking to himself, gesturing occassionally and then falling silent. As the camera nears, he suddenly turns to face the camera and with a look of expectation, he holds up his right index finger, says something then turns the ignition forward to roll down the power windows. The window rolls down quickly and he chuckles * "Well, well, well, it's about time you showed up. [He shakes his head a couple of times quickly and sighs mockingly] I don't know what it is about you RSP camera folks. I mean, hell, do you all have a tracer planted on all of us or something? It seems that no matter where I go, one of you always manages to find me. [He laughs loudly for a few moments] Then again it probably isn't too hard to locate a six-foot, ten inch, three-hundred and thirty pound brotha' either. [He shrugs quickly while turning his palms up just as quickly] Oh well. [He gestures to the driver side with his head] Hop in." [The cameraman obliges and makes his way around the front of the Navigator and towards the passenger side door. Sin reaches over and opens the passenger door and the camera man steps up slightly and has a seat. A quick panning of the camera reveals a very electronically-laden console, complete with LCD screen, DVD/CD player, enough buttons to keep any child happy and electronic gauges that bounce all over the place though there is no audible music. Once the cameraman settles and closes the door, we hear a familiar voice emanate from speakers in the SUV] Greene: "Good afternoon." [The RSP cameraman laughs slightly] Cameraman: "Good afternoon Mr. Greene." Sin: [Sinister looks over at the cameraman] "See, I'm not crazy. I know it looked odd, maybe like I was speaking to myself, but I've not lost that much of my mind...yet anyway. [All three chuckle] Mr. Greene and I were just discussing the six-man gauntlet match that I'll be involved in tomorrow. Personally I find it amusing that I'll be in the ring against Saul Akiva, the [makes a very dramatic quotation marks gesture] 'man of the RSP', my personal favorite, EE, Eric Emerson, Fabian Cruz, a man on a mission, Vance Randhal, a man making a name for himself, and then, of course, there's Big Chill." [Sinister falls silent for a moment and begins to scratch his chin in silent thought. While the big man stares at nothing, the silence is broken by Mr. Greene's voice] Greene: "Sin, I realize that there are still a lot of questions unanswered in terms of you and Big Chill, but do remember that, despite all of the uncertainty and recent lack of communication, he was the only one to make sure that you were all right when you took a ride in that ambulance. He was the only one who was man enough to look you in the eyes and shake your hand. He was the bigger man and he stepped up Sinister, you have to keep that in mind. Rather than continue taking verbal salvo shots, he aproached you like a man." [Sinister nods slowly a few times while exhaling slowly. He sucks his teeth slightly then quickly licks his lips] Sin: "You're right pops. He was the only one who gave a damn when I was hauled into the back of the ambulance after X,Y,Z jumped me. By the way, is there any more information about those three stooges?" Greene: "Whoever they are Sin, they have done a very good job of keeping various people quiet. However, let me worry about that bit of business, you have to keep your mind focused on tomorrow's gauntlet match. You know as well as I that you will be watched under a microscope of sorts in this match to determine whether or not you are able to earn a shot at Saul Akiva, or whomever the RSP champion is when you earn that shot." Sin: "Agreed. I definitely have to leave an impression upon Akiva, Emerson, Cruz and Randahl because they obviously don't either respect me or they don't believe that I'm capable of handling business. That's fine with me because I'm always out to prove people wrong, but one thing does concern me pops." Greene: "And what concerns you?" Sin: [Takes a very deep breath] "I'm concerned that Chill and I will have to diffuse our recent burying of the axe and that will lead to even more problems between us. To be honest, I'm still a little bothered by the fact that he bad-mouthed your son, the Professional...well, Asante, but to a degree I understand why he said what he did. I just don't want to, well, you understand my point. However, this isn't the first time we've potentially had to face one another in battle, but still, it's just...odd." Greene: "I understand Sin. If that situation arises then deal with it the best you know how. In the meantime, keep in mind what the other competitors are trying to accomplish and what some of them have said about you. I'm sure that will provide enough inspiration for you." Sin: "You're damn right it will and believe me pops, when I step into that ring, each and every one of them, as well as the rest of the RSP, will know that Big Sin Daddy is a man to be feared and reckoned with! I'll see you in a while pops, I have to make a stop before I come home and we prepare for the battle." Greene: "Okay Sin, I'll see you when you call me from your house." Sin: [Looking over at the cameraman] "Hold onto your balls man!" * Just as the cameraman starts to open his mouth, Sinister turns up the audio system. The camera wavers as the focus begins to vibrate rhythmically to the beat of the music. Sinister smiles widely and starts up the SUV, quickly shifting to reverse as the picture fades upon the LED displays on the console * ======================================================================= (Scene opens in the parking lot out the arena where RSP Breakdown is taking place. A gaggle of reporters are standing around the wrestler's entrance. Walking toward the entrance, rolling travel bag behind him, is the RSP Television Champion, The Big Chill. I'm running late as it is. Reporter #1: Can you give us an update on your health? TBC: Healthy as healthy can be. Luckily, Rowlands is more of a psycho than a pitcher. TBC: The crowd is fickle, proclaims the emperor. I was definitely surprised and, to be 100% honest, it didn't feel so bad to hear a cheer here and there. Not after he left me hanging in the breeze in Japan. Not after I had to become the Rogue RoT'er because no one had the set to stand by my side. No, too many unresolved issues. If I got a shot at laying him out in the match, he knows I'll take it. Reporter #1: What of the rumors of you and Sinister teaming up against these masked men that attacked him? TBC: That's the first I've heard of it. I got my own problems. I don't need his. He feels like he needs help, he can call that pansy brother of his. Reporter #3: What of Tommy Rowlands? TBC: He jumped on the wrong man's back. He thought he was going to make a name for himself by taking out the best wrestler on television. He thought wrong. Come on, guys, I gotta get in there. Reporter #1: One last question.... TBC: Make it quick. Reporter #1: You worried that Rowlands is playing possum like you did? TBC: Am I worried? No, but that's only because I expect the worst from everyone. He knows where he can find me. I don't exactly cower and hide. I kinda like making a spectacle of myself. Doesn't exactly endear me to people, either. I'll tell you something, though. I'm glad he threw that fireball at me. Now, I understand that chump a little better. I know how low he'll sink. You gotta know your opponents capabilities and tendencies. He comes after me next time, I'll be prepared. Now, you gotta excuse me... (TBC pushes his way past the reporters and ducks through a door labeled "Wrestlers" as the scene fades to black.) ======================================================================= [Its another blustery overcast day in Seattle Washington] [Like thats anything new. We focus on a small run of the mill brown house as the camera approaches it. The camera stops at the sidewalk as a man walks out the front door in a pair of black slacks and a grey Cashmere sweater. He smirks at the camera but walks past it without a word, as it in turns follows him out into the street, and stops behind him as he flips through his mail] [Less than six feet tall, he's pretty thick at around two hundred and twenty five pounds, with dark short hair and an arrogant and outwardly hostile demeanor. Pan in further, close enough to read the mans name on his mail, and suddenly everything is clear] -Damien Legacy- [Is the name on the envelope as Damien stops flipping, opening the envelope with a RSP insignia. He turns and pushes the camera as he begins flipping through the contents.] [He flips to the booking sheet for Breakdown, and the look of surprise and disgust increases ten fold as he crumples the sheet and throws it to the ground] "Pretty soon I'm going to start sounding like a broken record. Pretty soon people are goin to start to tire of hearing me talk about how much more I deserve in RSP. Pretty soon, if I don't start getting what I deserve [pause] I'M GOING TO START CRIPPLING SOME MOTHER[BEEP]ING WRESTLERS! THIS IS BULLSHIT AND _EVERYONE_, FROM THE TOP OF THIS GOD DAMN COMPANY ALL THE WAY DOWN TO THE BIG CHILL, KNOWS IT! [Damien advances on the camera with a crazed look in his. The camera retreats out of fear, but has soon backed into a wall, and Damien gets uncomfortably close to the lense] "And the crippling, begins with the debuting "American Rage" Chris Page. Chris, if I really have to feel sorry for anyone about this whole situation, its you. Its not your fault I am robbed of victories almost weekly because of some attention starved has been feels like sticking his nose where it doesn't belong. Fabian Cruz cried about getting screwed in his title match against Akiva, the world _watched_ as I was robbed of an inevitable victory against TBC, by Rowlands and a [beeping] fireball. And what did I get out of it? A match against a tag team wrestler? While Cruz gets Akiva one on one at the PPV. Or could my retribution be that I get to wrestle Chris Page in his debut match?" [Legacy finally backs off a little, recomposing himself as he brushes off his sweater] "Page is going to get hurt, and the bloods gonna be on your hands Sean. Page, you're going to get quite the welcome to Ringside Seat Promotions, Christopher has seen to that. Its not gonna be the kind of welcome you like though American Rage. It is going to be brutal, it is going to be ugly, and _if_ you are able to continue wrestling after breakdown, always remember it is simply because I _let_ you. It is simply because I cannot hold you responsible for the injustices I am constantly a victim of. Sean Christopher and his band of office monkeys is responsible, so make sure and send them the medical bills" [The anger returns to Legacy's expression] "Hold me down RSP. Until you realize that Damien Legacy is not to be trifled with. I thought maybe you'd get the message when I attacked the Dime Notes. Instead you put one of them in the ring with me [spits] Stupid, very stupid. Well I can tell ya one man is going to wish you had listened. Maybe after what I do to Chris Page, my message will be a little more clear. Maybe after Chris Page is crippled you'll start to realize just what you have done. [He turns and begins walking back towards his home. A black Audi A4 sits idly in the driveway. Damien stops next to it, putting his fist through the passenger window without a flicker of emotion in his expression. He pulls the fist out and towards his face. Its now red with his own blood] "Come Breakdown, Sean, Chris Page, RSP" [He sneers as he licks his fist] "This isn't gonna be my blood. [He flashes a smile a serial killer would envy as the camera fades] ====================================================================== [Scene opens up with Joe Jones, The newest RSP Cruiserweight champion sitting on a bed. He is wearing only boxers and has a package of Ritz crackers laying next to him. He has been eating some here and there. With what clearly appears to be a hand held camera Joe sits on the bed and holds and caresses the Cruiserweight title. The bed is unmade, doesn't even have sheets on it. A old flat and what looks to be a very uncomfortable pillow lays in the one corner of the bed. The walls are white not that matters any. Flipping his hair behind his shoulders........] J "T" J: "Look at this." [Holding up the Cruiserweight title.] "Do you see this?" Joe asks. [The camera nods up and down.] "In a very short time I have captured my very first professional title of my short and undefeated career so far. I am the best of the best. That's what this title means. That's what it represents. I be representing the lightweights. I have a lot of pressure that has just be thrusted on to my shoulders. I stand for all that is good and honest about the guys who don't measure up to the heavyweights. Unfortunately as much of an honor this is, it's sad and I regret in saying this but it's a burden above all things. Not that I don't enjoy my bitter sweet victory over my opponents but let's face the facts shall we? What's going on in the lightweight or Cruiserweight division of the RSP? Besides me cruising to my way to the top of the cruiserweights nothing really. There is no one testing me, taking me to the limit of my abilities and driving me to the edge of sanity. Don't you people realize that I am the best of the best all ready?" [Joe smiles and laughs. He looks down at the title and then looks back up the camera.] "You crack me up. Look at you shrugging like that. It's too much. Now I know why I hired you." [The camera man says something, but we can't make out what it is.] "This is true. I'm glad you brought that up. Now not only did I beat what's his face but I also beat The God's Gift. If he is God's gift he isn't very gifted. It's a oxymoron or something like that. How ironic is it that Joe Jones, The Twink beat God's gift? What does that make me now? God? I mean I would think that the only one who could beat God's Gift is God right? I gave Tyler his gift therefore I know how to beat the gift of god. Now if I was god and gave a gift I would have picked a better gift to give. Ever since the debacle in Japan, .. I have strayed from Pro-Wrestling. And why I came back, I am not sure. There remains something about it that keeps drawing Chris Page back. Despite the politics, despite the garbage, despite the ego's.. something keeps calling me. It might be foolish to think I can rid Pro-Wrestling of all of those ills but .. it is a dream.
