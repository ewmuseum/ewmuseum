[RSP] Total Darkness [2/2]

3 September 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<> Main Event "American Beauty" Ashlyn Summers/Trinity McPhearson -VS- "Wild Animal" Sarah Mayson/"The Show" Sierra Browne <>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<> Mayson and Browne took early control wearing down the rookie McPhearson. Browne hit a nice spin heel kick on Trinity followed by a neckbreaker. Mayson was tagged in and used several elbow drops and a second rope fist drop to get a two count. Mayson hit a nice Death Valley Driver then a swinging neckbreaker to get another two count. Mayson and Browne used several double team moves to work on Trinitys neck and upper back. As Mayson tried for a flying elbow Trinity moved and hit Browne. This left Trinity able to make the tag to Summers. Summers went to work on Mayson hitting a german suplex followed by a northern lights suplex. Summers sent Mayson over the top rope with a hard clothesline. Browne took control hitting Summers from behind with a solid kick. Browne then suplexed Summers and got a two count. Browne whipped Summers into the ropes but Summers revered it and Browne crashed into Mayson who was climbing back on the apron. Summers lifted Browne up and tagged in Trinity who climbed to the top rope and hit a blockbuster. Trinity covered Browne and got the three count. Browne and Mayson wouldn't let that go so they attacked Trinity and Summers after the match. They left them laid out after several hard chair shots. Time: 9:09 Winner: Ashlyn Summers and Trinity McPhearson [Camera cuts back to Tonya Sanders..Rumor Mills has left the set.] TS: Well that about does it for us this week on Total Darkness. Breakdown is next up coming to you in just over a weeks time. For now I'm Tonya Sanders. We'll leave you with comments from Tommy Rowlands. [AS the scene fades in, we find ourselves looking at a close up of Tommy Rowlands. The familiar face looks someone different today for his long curly hair is tied back, and his face clean shaved. He holds a phone to his ear, and speaks.] TR: I tell you, I hate this place. I hate it so much. [As we examine the scenery around him, we realise this isn't the locker room. This isn't the home we have seen him in before. No. This is somewhere different, but none the less we recognise it.] TR: Its god-awful. [Over the phone we hear a mumbling, although we simply can't make out what it is being said.] TR: I know I can cope with it. Hell, I've been here before. To be honest, I doubt there is anyone in our business that hasn't. [Slowly the camera pans backwards, and we realise where we are. Tommy is still dressed relatively casual, but we know he is in prison, simply from the Perspex glass and the metal frame, the phone being the only means of connecting us to the other person.] TR: But, Oh, how it was worth it. Our plan went almost to down to a tee last night. It was brilliant. All the uncomfort of being in that box paid off. Digging that knife into the skull of The Big Chill, and feeling the blade scratching on his bone...Oh now that is what I was talking about. The feeling of pure ecstasy. I was a man possessed. I now understand why you commit such acts on a weekly basis. Its simply...A rush. I can say no more. [The look in Rowlands eyes become somewhat distant. This is a changed man from the man we saw last October when he first arose in AWP. This is no longer the man looking for respect as he once was. It is not the man who led Donny Cole to the ring in IWA. No, this man has been tainted. Corrupted if you will. He clearly is thriving off of the agony he pushed on TBC.] TR: The sight of blood streaming down his face. The sight of the blade simply standing on end in his head. Oh how I wish now that I had pulled that blade from his head, and gone after Legacy as well, let alone what I could have done to Cruz. I hope the two of them keep in mind that I do not forget the past easily. And I WILL not forget them. [Again the mumbling is heard.] TR: Ok, for now I will not look to Cruz. He can try and enjoy being champ, although I doubt he will... [Tommy lets out a laugh, which can also be heard down the phone. Who is the person he talks to? Still we don't know. The camera again pans out so we can see the other persons left shoulder. It is for lack of a better word huge. The man, like Tommy is well built, the black T-shirt clinging to the skin as his muscles dominate it...] TR: You know what though? This is just the beginning. As you know, all I'm in here for is violating my wrestling license. No violent acts to be committed in the state of Washington. Well, they can quite simply kiss my ass. There is a hell of a lot more coming. I mean, with the pathetic bail they put on me, I'll be out as soon as, well you pay it. [Again the mumbling is heard.] TR: Don't worry, I'll get you the cash as soon as I'm out. But really, that's beside the point. I swear, as soon as I get back into the ring, TBC, he's mine. I hope he enjoyed what I did at the PPV, because I swear there is a lot more coming. [Again the mumbling is heard.] TR: Ah yes, Sinister. I almost forgot. TBC teamed up with the big boy to try and protect himself. Like that will work. We can handle Sinister at the same time as TBC, hell, knowing you; you'll enjoy it! But as for TBC. Well, he's in for it. I have him in my sights. Maybe he did manage to get up, and battle back at the PPV along side Sinister. But next time, well, I will do the job properly. HE won't be getting up, and hell, Sinister won't be able to do s**t about it.] [Yet again, and somewhat frustratingly, we hear the mumbling.] TR: I know. Oh how I look forward to seeing the destruction we bestow on this federation. I tell you, if people thought Sean Christopher was causing a commotion, wait till they see what we do. Sinister, Cruz, Emmerson, TBC, Lecgacy, watch out. When we run loose, there will be no stopping us. And I'm sure Sean Christopher will be all to happy to give us the nod on...Well anything we want. But for now. Lets go and take care of business. [With this, the man sitting down opposite Tommy puts down the phone. He gets up, and we finally get a look at the mans face. To our amazement, it is none other than former RSP Western States champion, SAUL AKIVA!] [Fade to black.] [Static] [Quick cut to outside the holding area where Saul Akiva is standing against a cold grey painted wall. He wears a generic black T-shirt hidden, for the most part under a sweeping black leather coat that comes halfway down his tattered blue jeans. His dark blue eyes burn up through his lowered head, we catch them beneath his furled brow. It's pretty obvious that breathing hurts as he flinches his mighty shoulders with each sigh before cocking up his face. It's bruised, swelled and is exactly what you'd expect from a man who just went through a war.] "So, Cruz...ya'won." [A rye smile as he rolls his powerful palm across his left wrist. His eyes drop from the camera as he pushes his shoulders away from the wall and straightens up.] "Like I could give two sacks a'crap about that." [He casts a dark shadow across the wall.] "See, all this time that lump of plastic has been around my waist I've been trying to figure out jus' what the point of me having it really is. What's the point of being the champion if I don't get ta' go huntin'. I got a big bullseye on my face with that belt, jus' like you got one now...not my kinda game. It didn't give me a kick to walk out in front of all the moronic fans last night and it never has because I knew I was runnin' on borrowed time. Some bitch like you was only a second away from knocking me starry with a steel chair...well, f[BEEP!] that. Why the hell should I even bother playing at being the champion any more huh Cruz? Everyone knows it, I'm the guy in the history books and that's all the matters. I lost...I got thrown off a cage. You try getting up from that. Maybe I'll show you how it feels some time. " [He turns his angled jaw to the side and takes a painful gulp of air before running his tongue across his cracked lips, tasting the nicotine with glee.] "I'm getting numb to the pain now, after going through that window all those months ago I jus' figure I pump myself with all the painkillers, like they say, best way to avoid a hangover is to stay drunk huh? So now, I'm banged up but it doesn't hurt much more than usual, I got a couple a' cracked ribs thanks to ya' but it don't hurt more than usual an' I got rid of that strap... Now I don't got to play by the rules any more...I get to shoot the arrows instead of gettin' shot." [A smile breaks across his face and through his 5'O clock shadow.] "Me an' Tommy, we don't got on all that well in the past but we pretty much figure the same on this, wha's the damn point in all this crap if we don't get to dish out some damage ourselves. Cut loose once in a while. Take a look at it all...who set this federation on it's feet? Saul Akiva and Tommy Rowlands battling through a fu[BEEP!]in' street an' me getting tossed through a sheet glass window. That's what people were talkin' about. They were talkin' about me and Tommy bleedin' all over the fu[BEEP!]in' place. That's what they're talkin' about coming out of Dangerous games as well, they be talkin' about how Tommy stuck TBC like a pig an' how I landed on my damn ass from the top of a fu[BEEP!]in' cell." [A pause, a knowing one at that. Pretend all you want but you like to see some poor bastard get cut open and bleed, you like watching some punk get his face cracked open and you know you love a good stiff chairshot.] "So, that's the game now. You wanna roll the dice one more time Cruz, I'll bring a whole new meaning to the words Dangerous Games...but first, I think I want to break open Sinister's face and watch him bleed out on my new boots. Not because I can. Not because I want to. Simply because... I Am THE BEST." [Fade to the split screen of Tommy Rowlands and Saul Akiva.] Tonya Sanders: [Voice] OH MY GOD..THE MONSTER GOT TO HIS HEAD!!! [C] 2002, RSP Entertainment Inc.
