[Saul Akiva, in his own mind the man setting AVW aside from all the rest. He's maimed, brutalised and cheated his way to the shot at the RSP big prize and their isn't a damn thing you can do about it now.] [He waits backstage in front of the RSP communal locker-room, wrists heavily wrapped in white tape. The 'RSP' brand black t-shirt is drenched, clutching at his developed muscles. His old black workman jeans fade from one extreme of black to the other. Slowly he wipes a hand across his deep tan, squarely set rugged face.] SAUL AKIVA: "So, here it is. The biggest event of my career so far. I'm the future, I'm what RSP is gonna be all about by the end of tonight, not some washed up amateur like you. Well boys, you took one hell of a beating over the last few weeks. Oh...oh, wait a damns second..WHO THE HELL ARE YOU BOTH ANYWAY? Greedy, how in the hell you wormed your way into this match is beyond me. Hooks was a joke and now we get another one. That's the worst joke I've ever heard Steve. While I've busted my ASS week in week out for this against the three top guys in the company you get on your knee's and open wide for this opportunity. That may work for you Steve and I assure you, after tonight you will be MY bitch. And Fabian, I suppose you at least had to beat up some more no-name losers to get here. That's not going to do you any good because I am a different league to them, I'm a different league to you. I'm the best you've ever seen Fabian and tonight, you get to feel first hand why i am the future of wrestling. So how about you stop chasing something you will never be able to catch, ME and that GOLD. Get out of my way Greedy, stay away from me Cruz and stop taking up my AKIVA time Rowlands or I might just have to do something you'll regret.." [That got a slight rise out of the future RSP Champion, a bold statement? Another deep nasal grimace, a few flecks of water drip down from his short greying hair and settle on the bulb of his nose. A quick swipe of the hand before...] SAUL AKIVA: "Not because I can, Not because I want to, Simply because, I... Am... Better."
