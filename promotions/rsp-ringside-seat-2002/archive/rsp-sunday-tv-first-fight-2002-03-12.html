[RSP] RSP Sunday TV: The First Fight

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera opens to show the ring full of RSP stars. They jockey for position in the ring as two refs try to keep piece. The camera pans to Anderson Michaels who is on the outside of the ring on the steps.] AM: The following contest is a special EIGHT MAN BATTLE ROYAL [Crowd pop] AM: The following is RINGSIDE SEAT PROMOTIONS... FFFFIRRRSST FFFIGHT [The crowd pop gets louder.] AM: introducing the participants. _____TOMMMYYY ROWWWWLLLANNNDSSS___ _____FFFFAAAABBBIAAANNN CCCRRRRUZZZ__ _____MMMMAAAARKKK HHHHOOOOKKKSSS__ _____DDDDDAAAAMMMMIIIIIENNN LLLLEEEEGGGGAAACY__ _____SSSSAAAAULLLL AAAAKIIIIVA__ _____BBBBIIIGGG CCCCHHHILLLL__ _____MMMMMOOOTOOOR CCCIIITY MMMMADMAN__ _____JJJJOHHHNYYY SSSSPPPAAADDESS__ [As Anderson announces the last name the bell rings.. DING..DING..DING And the ref quickly exit the ring as a mad dash is made toward all corners of the ring.] CB: It's on!! The First Fight for RSP. DT: This is history the first match for RSP and it's a mad house already. CB: This is RSPs way of saying hello. [Johnny Spades squares off with Damien Legacy and connects with a solid shot to the forhead.] DT: Oh man..that's Spades way of saying hello to Legacy. CB: I'm sure Legacy appreciates that. Just a quick note folxs, as you may know this match was scheduled to be a nine man battle royal. DT: There's only eight in there. CB: Jimmy Taylor is out with an injury. It seems he tore a muscle in his leg getting ready for this match. DT: Yeah right..he chickend out!! CB: Not at all. We wish Taylor a quick recovery and I'm sure we will see the superstar sometime down the line. DT: Yadda..Yadda Taylor's loss I'm sure. What about what's going on in the ring. [Motor City Madman and Big Chill are trading heavy lefts and rights in the corner. Chill gouges at Madmans eyes then connects with a clothesline. Camera cuts to the outside of the ring and shows Fabian Cruz talking things over with Kendra Stone.] CB: What is Spades doing? He should be in the ring. DT: He'll get in there when he pleases...No rush. [Mark Hooks and Tommy Rowlands have squared off and Rowlands takes Hooks down with a hard suplex. When Rowlands gets up he's met with a german suplex from Saul Akiva. Akiva is hit from behind by Johnny Spades.] CB: It's wild in there. These men know what's at stake. DT: The winner gets a free ride to the championship match. The losers will have to fight their way through a round robin tournament. [Spades turns around and is caught by Motor City Madman. Madman picks up Spades and slams him to the mat with a double handed chokeslam. Legacy sees Cruz on the outside and dives through the second rope with a clothesline. The two fight on the outside. Legacy sends Cruz into the ringpost. Legacy charges but Cruz moves. Legacy crashes chest first into the post.] DT: That's gonna leave a mark. CB: Broadcast journalist rule number one. NO CLICHES. DT: As if you're a Broadcast journalist. [Inside the ring Mark Hooks hits Big Chill with several elbows to the jaw which rocks Chill toward the ropes. Hooks grabs one of Chills legs and tries to get him over.] CB: Chill is going to be the first eliminated! DT: Hooks has Chill up but can he get him over? [Saul Akiva goes over and tries to help Hooks. Rowlands attacks Akiva from behind. Chill is taken over the top but he holds on and slides under the ring.] DT: Rowlands and Akiva have heated things up even before the offical start of RSP. CB: It's a matter of respect. One man wants to earn respect. The other man thinks he's owed respect. DT: Akiva is the better of the two! [Motor City Madman sees Hooks and Chill fighting near the ropes. Madman charges but both Hooks and Chill drop down holding the ropes. Madman goes over the top!!] AM: THE FIRST MAN ELIMINATED...MOTOR CITY MADMAN!! CB: That's unfortunate. DT: Tough luck kid. [Madman slaps the mat in disappointment. He sees Legacy and Cruz fighting on the outside of the ring then charges at them. Madman takes both Cruz and Legacy dwn with a clothesline. He then picks Cruz up and presses him into the ring. The crowd erupts as Madman raises his hands.] CB: It's a moral victory for Madman. The crowd loves him. DT: Those two things will get him alot!! [Cruz stumbles to his feet when he turns around he sees Johnny Spades. Spades takes Cruz down with a Spear. The two men begin to roll around the mat trading stiff shots.] CB: It's broken down here.. DT: Here's another issue that has started even before tonight. Cruz and Spades, I wonder how that photo shoot went? CB: I'm sure it wasn't pretty. [Cruz and Spades get to their feet and tangle themselves close to the ropes. With their backs turned Saul Akiva comes up from behind and dumps the two over the top.] AM: JOHNNY SPADES AND FABIAN CRUZ HAVE BEEN ELIMINATED. [Without stopping Cruz and Spades continue their fight to the back as the crowd erupt with a hardcore brawl pop... RSP_RSP_RSP RSP_RSP_RSP RSP_RSP_RSP] CB: Well Spades and Cruz are out. DT: You know what Collen? CB: What? DT: We have our first bracket set! Spades, Cruz, Motor City Madman, and a person to be named later. CB: Yes, it looks that way. With Jimmy Taylor out his replacement will join the red bracket. DT: It's not over yet! [Tommy Rowlands grabs Damien Legacy and drops him to the mat with a t-bone suplex. Rowlands follows up with a double legdrop across the throat and chest winding Legacy. Tommy pulls the opponent up to his feet before sending him to the ropes. As the opponent comes back, Tommy hits a spear, sending them to the mat. Rowlands perches himself ontop of Legacy and lands several hard blows. Mark Hooks grabs Rowlands by the back of the air and flings him over the top rope. Rowlands holds on though and slides back into the ring.] DT: Hooks has eliminated Rowlands! CB: No! Rowlands is still in this. DT: What? LOOK OUT HOOKS~! [Hooks turns around and his caught with a forearm to the jaw. On the other side of the ring Big Chill and Saul Akiva are trading blows. Chill and Akiva are trading fists back and forth. Akiva takes the advantage and keeps hitting Chill with right hands. Akiva whips Chill into the ropes, Chill reverses it. Chill catches Akiva with a overhead belly-to-belly suplex.] DT: Nice move by Big Chill. CB: Akiva crashes into the mat. [Chill picks up Akiva. Chill irish-whips Akiva into the corner, face first. As Akiva is rebounding from the corner, TBC bounds into the near ropes and connects with a clothesline. Without breaking stride, Chill runs across the ring, bounds into the far ropes and comes back and drops a running elbowdrop across Akivas throat. Chill stands up and celebrates as he does Damien Legacy comes up from behind and tosses Chill over the top as the crowd pops.] AM: THE BIG CHILL HAS BEEN ELIMINATED. [Legacy taunts Chill who is sitting on the arena floor in shock. Chill sees Legacy and quickly gets up. Chill gets on the aprong and swings at Legacy. Legacy steps back right into the arms of Tommy Rowlands who it's a belly-to-back suplex. Legacy gets up dazed and is hit with a clothesline from Hooks and Akiva. Legacy goes over the top rope.] AM: DAMIEN LEGACY HAS BEEN ELIMINATED. [Legacy and Chill have a stare off on the floor as refs seperate them. Inside the ring Rowlands is left to fend off Saul Akiva and Mark Hooks.] DT: We're down to three. CB: Rowlands may be in trouble. It looks as if Hooks and Akiva have paired off. DT: Don't trust anybody! [Hooks and Akiva slowly move in on Rowlands. Hooks stops as Akiva lounges toward Rowlands. Akiva and Rowlands trade blows near the ropes. Hooks just looks on encouraging both men.] DT: Hooks taking the smart mans route. CB: Hooks just watching as Akiva and Rowlands tear into each other. [Akiva tries for a piledriver but Rowlands reverses it into a backbody that sends Akiva over the top rope.] AM: SAUL AKIVA HAS BEEN ELIMINATED. DT: TWO!! TWO!! TWO!! WE'RE DOWN TO TWO!! CB: Hooks and Rowlands. [Hooks is right on top off Rowlands. Hooks takes Rowlands down with a clip to the back of the knee. Hooks and begins to work over Rowlands knee by dropping elbows on it. He sets Rowlands leg on the second rope then puts all his weight on it using the ropes.] DT: This is what Hooks is good at. He'll take Rowlands apart body part by body part. CB: He's a former tag team champion. He's been to the top..he knows how to get there. [Hooks picks Rowlands up and whips him into the ropes. Rowlands comes off and Hooks catches him with a tilt-a-whirl slam. Rowlands holds his back in pain. Hooks drops several elbows right into the small of Rowlands back.] DT: Hooks is a master. CB: Hooks can easily deposit Rowlands now. DT: But he wont... [Mark Hooks climbs to the second rope and comes off with a fist drop. Rowlands moves out of the way and Hooks hits hard on the mat. Hooks gets up and his hit with a clothesline. Rowlands connecte with a hard knee lift then goes off the ropes and hits another clothesline.] CB: Rowlands has changed the momentum of the match. DT: The idiot wont give up! Damn him!! [Rowlands picks up Hooks and hits a brainbuster. Rowlands picks Hooks up again and sets for a Death Valley Driver.] DT: This isn't right. How can Rowlands win? [As Rowlands tries to pick up a deadweight Hooks. Saul Akiva runs back to the ring. Akiva gets on the apron and Rowlands goes after him. Akiva snaps Rowlands neck over the rope. Hooks gets up and hits a high knee to the back of Rowlands who goes over the top rope. Crowd: BULLSHIT...BULLSHIT..BULLSHIT..] AM: TOMMY ROWLANDS HAS BEEN ELIMINATED...YOUR WINNER...MMMMAAARKK HOOOKKS~! CB: Hooks wins..Hooks gets into the title match. DT: WHOO~! WHAT A MATCH!! CB: So the blue bracket is Damien Legacy, Big Chill, Tommy Rowlands, and Saul Akiva. DT: The red bracket is Motor City Madman, Fabian Cruz, Johnny Spades, and..and.. CB: And we'll find out next week!! DT: Mark Hooks is the winner of RSPs FIRST FIGHT and goes onto the championship match in a couple of weeks. CB: For my broadcast partner..I'm Collen Blackwell..we'll see you March seventeen at the Eagles Hall in Olympia Washington!! [The Camera shows Mark Hooks celebrating in the ring as the camera fades to black.] [C] 2002, RSP Inc.
