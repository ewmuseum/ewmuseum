CLOSING THOUGHTS --------------------------------------------------- ---===INTRODUCTION===--- Storyline: Ringside Seat Promotions came to life on February 13, 2002 as the brainchild of Theodore Stevenson, Esq. He had felt that a wrestling promotion would be a great money-maker, and being the shrewd businessman that he is, he got down to work. With a large bankroll, a love of the sport, he met up with a man by the name of Sean Christopher, who is an ex-wrestler. Stevenson agreed to give Christopher a 50/50 partnership in exchange for his knowledge . An agreement was reached, and RSP was born. The question lingered where would the promotion be based? Many locations were considered, although most of them (California, Florida, New York, etc.) were already cornered by other promotions. The answer finaly came...WASHINGTON! WIthout thinking, Stevenson applied to run his promotion out of Seattle. With the location decided and a new arena built Stevenson soon after had personal issues and sold his 50% to Sean Christopher. Christopher is now left with the daunting task of landing talent, producing a weekly t.v show, and any tending to any other business matter....it's a enough to make a man snap!! ---===OUT OF CHARACTER===--- In a nutshell, this fed is run by somebody who had been in and around the e-wrestling circuit for several years now. With this latest project, I'm looking more or less to uncover some unknown/underrated talent, and try to make it a fun environment where a team effort is necessary to succeed, both as handlers, and as us front office types. The more we co-operate, the better this will be for everyone involved. I'm looking forward to working with some new faces, and possibly some old e-w acquaintances. WHO AM I? [name withheld]---I've been in e-wrestling since 96. Been involved in every aspect of the game from handling to running a promotion. Most noteable characters are K.C Hagan in KCW and Sean Christopher from ICW and IWF/WOW. I most recently ran AWP which at the time was regarded as one of top feds around. --------------------------------------------------- ---===RULES===--- Yep, every fed's gotta have them and no, in this case rules were NOT meant to be broken. Any breaking of these rules will result in a long trip down the hall to the principals office [well really just an email from me asking "whatupwitdat!"] 1.) THE HAPPY HAPPY JOY JOY RULE - First and foremost, have fun! that's what RSP is for. If you don't expect or even want to have fun this isn't the place for you. 2.) THE DUMBASS RULE - simple don't be one!! There's no use for it in e-w or in life. 3.) THE BIGOT RULE - No racist, sexist or religous bigotry. This is a _ZERO TOLERANCE_ rule. There is no room for this crap in e-wrestling and there is absolutely no reason for it to be used. If I see this in a RP or on-card you better PRAY that I'm in a good mood because there is a good chance I will just can you right away. Sorry if that seems a bit extreme but it's an important thing to note. 4.) THE *@#^% RULE - Try and keep the obscenities to a minimum. I have no problem with your character swearing when the time is right but please don't over do it! 5.) THE KAYFABE RULE - I am actually very lax about kayfabe. Try to refrain from calling your opponents jobbers and such, but referring to some of the "kayfabe-breaking" words shouldn't be a problem. But here is one thing that you should really try to remember: EMWC, GIW, GLCW, and all the rest of the e-feds on the planet...they exist in the world of RSP, so if you feel you need to mention them, be my guest. WWF/WCW/ECW, TNA, XWF...they do NOT exist in the world of RSP, so please don't mention them. 6.) THE WHINY LITTLE GIRL RULE - Don't be a sore loser. Don't e-mail me bitching and moaning about losing a match. If you lose and you want to know why, e-mail me or strike up a chat with me and we will talk it out. I have no problem explaining why I had your characters lose but I will have a problem if you start yelling at me about being stupid and not knowing how to run a fed. This is another way to find yourself fired from this federation quickly. 7.) THE ORIGINALITY RULE - Unlike these rules [which you may have seen in another feds handbook :)] try to be orignal with your characters and roleplays. [note: thanks to the OTWA for some of the above.] --------------------------------------------------- ---===TITLES===--- Right now RSP has four [3] titles. These titles are awarded to the members of RSP who produce the most consistent quality work. The titles are the Western States Herritage Championship, The Television Championship, the Tag Team Championship, and the Cruiserweight Championship. Western States Herritage Championship- The main title for RSP. Right now RSP is based in and around the Seattle, Washington area. However as time goes by plans are to expand to all areas of the West Coast. Television Championship - Public Access is all RSP can afford right now however a pay-per-view is in the working stages. --------------------------------------------------- ---===HOW TO SUCCEED===--- Roleplays, Strats, and Angles all lead to success in RSP. ---ROLEPLAYING--- No roleplay..no wins. It's that simple. Send all roleplays to: [detail withheld] We don't ask for much around here. All that is required is one roleplay a week with a maximum of two allowed. Roleplays do not have to be long they just have to be quality. ---STRATS--- Strats are also sent to: [detail withheld] . Strats are another factor in determining who wins a close match. Strats for the most part are simple to do and should follow the format below. - Entrance (if it is different from thee one on your app) - General Strategy - Spots - A suggested finish. - What will your team do if they win/loose? If you have a question about strats fill free to ask. ---ANGLES/HEAT--- The more work you do the more heat your character gets..the more heat your character gets the better the angles get..the better the angles get the better your chances of having fun in RSP will get. --------------------------------------------------- ---===WEEKLY SCHEDULE===--- RSP Breakdown-Weekly Show from the RSP Arena in Washington. This show will feature four to five live matches. This is where all the important stuff happens. Ringside Rants--This is the flash show for RSP. You'll hear your favorite star rant and rave about the events taking place in RSP. RSP UPDATE -No need to explain this :) RSP Total Darkness-This is the basic review show. It sets up the Breakdown for that week and reviews what happend on the last Breakdown. There is also a chance for some dark matches to take place. Total Darkness will usual be on Wednesdays typical schedule will be Monday--free day Tuesday--free day Wednesday--Total Darkness Thursday---Due Dates for strats, roleplays, and oncards for Breakdown Friday---Ringside Rants/Breakdown air, Update sent Saturday---free day Sunday---free day --------------------------------------------------- ---===RSP STAFF===--- Vince Richardson: The veteran color man for RSP. He's the man that trash talks the "good guys" and has nothing put praise for the "bad guys". Richardson has become popular with the fans but still remains a headache for his broadcast partner. Collen Blackwell: At the ripe old age of 45, he's a true veteran of the sport. He's worked in many high profile wrestling companies such as AWMC and AWP. He's an old-school style commentator who was asked by Sean Christopher to apply his talents to RSP, and to add a sense of sanity on Television. He almost quit upon learning who his counterpart would be, but the temptation of a fairly sizable contract overrode the punishment of working with a brat. This promises to be the toughest test in Collen's distinguished career, but he's taking it head-on. (Note: All referees were brought aboard from the now defunct AWP) REFEREES: TOMMY "I'M NOT _THAT_ TOM JONES" JONES Always in tip top shape, and giving a 100%, this is the usual Main Event referee and RSP head official. BRYAN INFINITY Bryan always calls it straight down the middle...and is there to give the fans what they want...even if that means bending the rules a little. "OLD TIMER" STEVE MASSARO Steve is RSP's oldest ref, and perhaps the oldest on the RSP staff. Nearing 50 years old Steve has been around the ring long enough to know how to handle himself. not the fastest, or the most in shape, the most able to see, or even able to hear Steve does his best and doesn't back down from anybody. RSP Timekeeper: JAY HOWARTH Another young student of the game some say he has been weird ever since losing the Ring Announcer job to Anderson Michaels. Anderson and Jay seem to have harbored a training school rivalry which could explode as they sit together at the same table at ringside. Always trying to elevate his status and garner attention, Jay gets into the spotlight wherever he can...evening taking the mic from Anderson on occasion. RSP Ring Announcer: ANDERSON MICHAELS Anderson in his early 20's and is in good physical condition. Anderson is a young student of the game as he hopes to one day turn the mic in for a pair of boots. Anderson isn't afraid to take the lumps and bumps that may occur as he sits ringside. On a side note, he and Tonya Sanders have a bouncing baby boy. RSP Backstage Commentator: TONYA SANDERS The T&A of the RSP announce team. Tonya will handle all the backstage announce work as she uses her "talents" to dig under the sheets to get all the scoops.
