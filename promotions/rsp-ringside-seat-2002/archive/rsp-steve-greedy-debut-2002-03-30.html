Feedback Please - Steve Greedy - RSP debut

30 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[ Blank Screen ] Man's Voice : " Ouch. " Females Voice : " What is a matter. You don't like it ? I can do it a different way." Man's Voice : " It's not that, Just be more gentle will ya gorgeous..I am fragile there." [ There is silence for a moment, Until Suddenly...] Man's Voice : " DAYYYYUMMMMMM " [ We fade into a view of Steve Greedy sitting in a chair getting his haircut with a huge smile on his pretty little face. He has a towel over his nice suit, and even two seperate rags over his expensive shoes trying to cover them from the lose hair. A beautiful Blonde has some clippers standing behind him, and is looking at Greedy as he looks at himself in the mirror checking out his haircut. Which looks pretty similiar to the one he already had, For those out there who know Greedy from previous arrangements.] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " You did a good job girl. Makes me just want to kiss ya." [ The girl leans in towards Greedy expecting a kiss, Greedy glances at the girl, then glances away quickly, as if he didn't want to kiss her.The look on his face says something to the extent of "disgusting".] "The Rich One" SG : " But I better get going..I am running late already << Greedy gets up out of the chair tossing the Towel's, and rags to the ground. >> You did good though sweetie, Lil to much off the top, but overall not to shabby." [ Greedy hands the lady a One Hundred dollar bill, and smiles to her walking off. Once he walks out of the shop, He leans up against the door holding his head up high.] "The Rich One" SG : " I can't believe that nasty breathe bitch wanted me to give her a kiss...I mean ME...Steve Greedy..You would think she could of at least popped in some gum or something for me.. She did have a nice lil figure on her." [ Greedy then lowers his head, and notices the camera..He flashes his trademark smirk, then speaks.] "The Rich One" SG : " I didn't see ya there, How long ya been there ? << Pauses >> Oh nevermind, Doesn't matter...So let's see, What federation you from..SCW Maybe ? If so I aint talking bout my title defense this weekend, Maybe IWF/WOW ? Yes I attacked Seth Draken for a reason...Wait, Maybe your IGA ? I refuse to get on a plane and go to Australia again damnit.." [ Greedy leans in towards the camera, and notices the RSP logo on the side of it.He smiles, almost blushing and scratches his head.] "The Rich One" SG : " Oh...RSP Is it ? The new federation I signed up with. Well lemme see hear." [ Greedy digs around in his pocket searching for something. The first item he pulls out is a condom still in the package. He drops to that ground still blushing, The next item is tickets to Wrestlebowl IV, And then the next item is a piece of paper which is crinkled up. He puts the tickets back in his pocket, and trys to un wrinkle the pice of paper. Once he does so,He looks up smiling..] "The Rich One" SG : " Says here yall want me to fight Mark Hooks and Steve Jenson this Sunday in Tacoma for a shot at being in some Western States Heritage title match." [ Steve nods in agreement.] "The Rich One" SG : " I think I can arrange that. I don't got nothing else to do Sunday, Not like I am gonna go to Church or anything << Chuckles >> Plus it don't get much easier than fighting those two." [ Steve leans in towards the camera and whispers " Iv'e seen there tapes" Greedy leans back to his normal position and shivers.] "The Rich One" SG : " It's pretty sad...I watched the greatest moments of Steve Jenson once, and it showed him kicking out of an inside cradle..I don't know, I am sure somewhere somebody was on there couch going "Damn, That Steve Jenson is awseome" , But I personally wasn't very impressed." [ Greedy begins to walk down the concrete pavement, and the camera follows.] "The Rich One" SG : << Still walking >> " Last time I saw Steve was in AWP, And this Mark Hooks guy I have just heard about and seen glimpses of...Nuttin to serious, I don't think I have anything to worry about though, unless these two have got a little bigger, smarter and faster in the past couple months. Which I highly doubt." [ Greedy stops walking and turns around towards the camera.] "The Rich One" SG : " They haven't got bigger have they << Says in a serious tone >> Oh don't answer that It doesn't really matter.." [ Greedy gulps, and keeps on walking down the side walk area. We can see from this view, His suit is indeed Armoni..] "The Rich One" SG : " I have been a busy man as of late, Leaving my mark on many federations, and this one will be know different. Not just will I defeat these two men and take the spot at shooting for the title whenever that title match may be, but I will do something even more fun, and make these two men look like fools in there Sunday Night when that bell rings..I want you two guys to remember something very important, even if you block out every other comment I ever make in the RSP...." [ Greedy reaches a very nice Red Corvet ZR1, and stops at the door. He then turns to the camera.] "The Rich One" SG : " Remember, Money isn't everything, It's the only thing.." [ Greedy holds one finger up, and smiles toward the camera.] "The Rich One" SG : " That is the one thing you guys need to remember, and Gentleman..Nobody has more money than the man your looking at, I do mean _NOBODY_. So in that ring, even when your feeling good about yourself cause you landed a bodyslam or a suplex of some sort. Remember that saying in the back of your head, That way you will be prepared to go _BANKRUPT_ When the time is right, and the situation comes about when I am ready to win my debut match up in RSP. Which I do promise, I will win.." [ Greedy winks to the camera, and lowers his one finger down slowly to his side. ] "The Rich One" SG : " I don't mean to be rude, but that is the sad and true reality of the situation guys...Just keep in mind it's just business, Nothing personal. I am just a guy from Little Rock, Arkansas doing what I do best, Not a guy looking to torture with my above average skills week after week..If your lucky, This will be the first and last time you ever see me..Maybe cause the guys with the power will realize you guys don't need to be in the ring with me cause of the obvious talent diference, Maybe cause I will buy you guys out of ya contract telling you that you can still wrestle, as long as you avoid me, or maybe cause I will make you look so bad, You will never be able to bring yourself back into a wrestling ring. We will just have to wait and see which one happens." [ Greedy opens the door to the Corvet, and gets in..He starts the engine and rolls down the window waving to the camera as he peels off.] [Fade Out]
