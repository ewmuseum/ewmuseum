[RSP] RSP Breakdown [2/2]

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[As the camera opens back to the RSP arena "Legacy" by Papa Roach begins to play over the PA system. Just then the lights in the arena die down,and a white spotlight shines down upon the entrance.] ##Dead president, fuck the president. Mansons strive for power, while I work to save my residents. He owns you even though he never told you. Mark of the beast on ya hand now he owns you. Thrown down to the bottom level. Ruled by the fist and the fist is the devil. I medamorphisize and make myself level.## [Just then the crowd errputs into a major heel pop as the two most hated men in the RSP emerge from the back stage area. Those men being Derek Douglas, and "The Elite Sensation" Eric Emerson. Derek is wearing a pair of torn up jean shorts, a black sleeveless t-shirt in which a red "Godsmack" symbol is printed on the front. He is also wearing a pair of black combat boots, and his jet black hair is hanging down to his shoulders in a wet manner. Eric is wearing a pair of black latex shorts in which a white star is printed upon his rear. He is also wearing a pair of black boots, and white tape is wrapped around his wrists. And he has his bleach blonde hair combed forward with the front slightly spiked up.] ##WE ARE THE FUTURE, WE ARE RIGHT NOW!## [As the two men stand side by side looking into the crowd the arena gives off another thunderous heel pop. Eric smirks in a cocky fashion as Derek begins to yell at a few of the younger fans.] ##Right now... Right now... I'm taking a stab at the sick, sick, sick. I know what evil is and evil could suck my dick. Like Sam Hain and god ain't no joke. Ya better watch ya neck cause ya might get choked.## [As Eric and Derek make there way to the ring they don't do much besides play it cool and ignore the "ignorant" fans. AS they reach the ring area Eric walks up the ring steps and makes his way through the ropes as Derek rolls under the bottom rope and slides over to one of the corners resting against the bottom turnbuckle.] ##I'm dodgin arrows from the dark side everyday... And I'm a main target. Cause of what I say I'm dodgin arrows from the dark side everyday.## [Eric walks over to the side of the ring and motions for a microphone... After a few moments of waiting a man wearing a black suit walks over and tosses Eric a mic.] ##YOU SINK, WE SWIM... WE RISE, YOU FALL!## [He smirks, and lifts the mic as Derek continues to sit in the corner. Just then "Legacy" by Papa Roach fades, and the arena lights up.] EE: RSP... Feast your eyes upon the two most powerful men to set foot on RSP soil! [Eric continues to smile as the crowd gives off a huge heel pop.] EE: And just think... It was only a few weeks ago that we made our impact felt. Not by making a minor little terd lose a match. Oh no... We went for the gusto! We went after the man that makes the RSP tick... We went after and discarded none other then the "Suit" of the RSP, Sean Christopher. [Eric looks around to the crowd. He then begins to pace around the ring in a cocky fashion.] EE: But whats next? Saul Akiva... Tommy Rowlands... Or maybe Fabian Cruz? Who could it be... How about the over grown lump of cow sh[beep]t, Big Chill? [The crowd gives off a mixed crowd at the mentioning of Big Chill.] EE: Who knows... And who really cares? The fact of the matter is this... Whoever we want to take out we'll take out. Ya see... I'm the "Elite Sensation"... I am the greatest athlete to ever set foot in a wrestling ring. And tonight in my _SECOND_ match here in the RSP I'm going to prove it... [As Eric finishes his sentence Derek slowly pulls himself u off the apron. He runs his hands through his hair and reaches out for the mic. Eric hands the mic to him as the crowd begins to chant the word, "Freak".] DD: Say what you will... [Derek begins to pace around the ring as Eric walks over to the ring corner and takes a seat on the top turnbuckle.] DD: Sean Christopher... As I stand here in this ring thinking of last week I think. I think of how our war is over in a sense. Over because after last week you'll never wanna set foot in a wrestling ring with either me or him... [Derek points to Eric who gets a confident smirk across his face.] DD: But in another way it's only beginning. Tonight in this very ring you've gone out and hired a "hit man" so ta speak. A man by the name of Sinister... A former AWP American champion. A former AWP tag champion... [Derek pauses.] DD: Christopher... no matter what you do. No matter what you say... The two of us... We'll never go away. Because we're like an infection that has gotten out of control. And the infection... It'll not go away until it has eaten each and everything in it's path! [Derek cracks his neck, and stops walking. He looks directly into the camera with a serious expression.] DD: Christopher... The plan is in motion, and tonight step number two. Sinister... Prepare yourself... Prepare for a war! [Derek lowers the mic away from his mouth and drops it to the canvass with one last thud. He looks to Eric, as he jumps down off the turnbuckle. The two men leave the ring walking side by side. Eric with a confident strut, and Derek with a serious no none sense walk. The camera cuts back to Tonya Sanders] TS: Tonya Sanders here I'm with the president and acting CEO of RSP Sean Christopher. Sean can you explain to me what we saw earlier involving Greedy, Cruz, and Akiva? [The camera pans a little to show Sean Christopher. Sean is standing in front of a locker room that has Steve Greedy's name on it. Sean is dressed in black slacks and a black and white shirt on. He has a gold belt over his shoulder.] SC: What do you want to know? I mean Akiva ran his mouth and dared anybody to shut him up did he not? TS: ... SC: Yeah. So Greedy and Cruz took him up on the offer. It's not my fault Akiva got dealt the punk card. TS: Right but what were you doing out there. We saw you nod to somebody. Was it Cruz? Akiva? Greedy? Why are you in Greedy's locker? SC: I'm glad you're doing your job Tonya. Nice questions. TS: Thanks but can I get answers? [Sean smirks then gives Tonya a stern glance.] SC: Well it goes like this. I'm the boss you're the employee if you EVER give me a tone like that I'll be the boss and you'll be unemployed. Understood? TS: I'm sorry sir but.. SC: This is all you need to know right now. Greedy and Cruz have decided to flip a coin in their match tonight. Cruz won he's the number one contender. For being a good sport about this I have decided to award Greedy with this title. [Christopher pats the title on his shoulder.] SC: As of today your RSP Cruiserweight Champion is Steve Greedy. TS: What? SC: You heard me. TS: Right. One last question what's with the referee shirt? SC: Lets just say I will be keeping an eye on the main event. If you'll excuse me I have a party to get ready for. [Sean turns and enters the locker room. The camera gets a quick glance of the inside of the locker room. We see Fabian Cruz and Steve Greedy who are drinking champaigne and Sean Christopher who joins them. Camera fades back to ringside.] BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBB BBBBBBBBBBBB B R E A K D O W N Special Challenge Match Eric Emerson -vs- Sinister BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBB BBBBBBBBBBBB [Camera cuts back to ringside we see Eric Emerson still in the entrance way he's jawing with some fans. Derek Douglas is right behind him.] CB: Well our main event is up next. Vince it's an interesting situation going on between Greedy, Cruz, and Christopher. VR: I think we have the formation of an alliance but I don't know. Sean is usualy a pretty straight shooter. Was he joined the darkside? CB: The darkside? VR: Use the force Collen you to can see the balance of things. CB: Uh? VR: My young one you have much to learn. CB: Seen any good movies lately? [As Emerson heads toward the back Sinister steps out and both men are eye-to-eye.] CB: Forget about that I think we got trouble. VR: Sinister and Emerson face-to-face. This is a match I think alot have been looking forward to. CB: Both men former champions. Both men have earned a reputation in this industry. VR: What's going to happen? [Emerson takes a swing at Sinister. Sinister blocks and lands a shot of his own. DING..DING..DING] CB: The bell rings but I don't see a ref. VR: Well here he comes!! [Sean Christopher walks from the back.] CB: What? Sean is the ref? VR: Looks that way. CB: This doesn't look good for Emerson. [Sinister lands several hard rights and lefts into Emerson's jaw. He then rams Emerson into the guardrail. Sinister picks Emerson up and suplexes him to the cement floor.] CB: Sinister is a monster and he's showing this Seattle crowd why he got the reputation! VR: Christopher is letting Sinister take Emerson apart. CB: Well we all know the history between Emerson, Douglas, and Christopher. VR: Well it was at our last event Night of Champions that Emerson and Douglas took Christopher apart. [Sinister throws Emerson over the guardrail then grabs a chair from a fan and hits Emerson in the back with it. Sinister throws Emerson back over the guardrail and rolls him into the ring. As Sinister tries to get back into the ring Derek Douglas gets the timekeepers bell and hits Sinister with it. Emerson shakes some air back into his head then as Sinister staggers between the ropes Emerson pulls up on the second rope crotching Sinister.] VR: The great equalizer. CB: Ouch!! [Emerson doesn't let up as he lands some heavy shots into Sinister. Emerson back Sinister into a corner and starts to use him as a punching bag.] CB: Emerson is like a fly around a horse stable. VR: He's ontop of shit? CB: You can say that. He doesn't let up. [Emerson throws Sinister to the mat then hits a knee drop to the back of the head. Emerson climbs to the second rope and drops an elbow. Emerson follows that up with a leg drop.] VR: Nice series there by Emerson. CB: Move after move. He needs to stay on Sinister like this. [Emerson applies a headlock then slams a knee into Sinister's back. Emerson puts a sleeper on and Sinister begins to struggle to get free.] VR: The more Sinister fights the more this move works. CB: This is actualy smart of Emerson. Not only does this move use his body weight but it also uses Sinister's body weight. [Sinister drops to a sitting position with Emerson still holding onto Emerson. With Sinister almost out the ref raises Sinisters hand...... ONCE TWICE THREE.. Sinister's hand doesn't fall the third time. CROWD: SIN IS TER SIN IS TER SIN IS TER ] CB: Sinister trying to fight this. VR: The crowd is getting behind Sinister here. [Sinister gets to his feet he lands several shots to Emerson's stomach. Emerson lets go of the hold and bounces off the rope. As he comes off Emerson tries for a clothesline but Sinister ducks. Sinister bounces off the opposite ropes and hits a shoulder tackle. Emerson get to his feet and Sinister presses him. Sinister drops Emerson face first on the turnbuckle. As Emerson gets out of the corner Sinister hits a clothesline.] CB: Sinister with renewed life!! VR: Emerson needs to regain control. [Sinister whips Emerson into the ropes and powerslams him. Emerson staggers to his feet and Sinister grabs him by the throat. Sinister chokeslams Emerson and goes for a cover... ] CB: Why isn't Sean counting? VR: Sean wants Sinister to punish Emerson some more. CB: Sean is asking for the Chi Town Massacre. CB: No...No.. [Sinister sets Emerson for the Chi Town Massacre. As he does Sean Christopher slides in behind Sinister and hits a low blow. Sean then hits Sinister with an inverted DDT. Douglas slides a chair into Emerson who climbs to the second rope and leaps off brining the chair crashing into Sinister's face. Emerson covers Sinister... ONE TWO THREE] CB: Not like this!! VR: HA..HA..HA CB: What? Why you laughing. VR: Light bulb turn on yet Collen? CB: What? [DING..DING..DING] VR: Ha..Ha.Ha..wake up Collen. After you wake up you can wake Sinister up as well. CB: Damn it! VR: I would say April Fools but it's not April. [Emerson, Douglas, and Christopher begin to put the boots to Sinister.] AM: Your Winner.."ELITE SENSATION" ERIC EMERSON!!! CB: Folxs we're out of time but I'm sure we'll get some answers to this mess next week. VR: Answers? Don't you get it Collen. You been had. All these people have been had. Sean Christopher was the one holding the strings. CB: Damn you Vince..Damn you Emerson..Damn you all. VR: Ha..Ha..Ha. [C] 2002, RSP Entertainment.
