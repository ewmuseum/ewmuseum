[RSP] The Pre-Show

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
>======> >=>>=> >======> >=> >=> >=> > > >=> >=> >=> > > >> >==> >=> >======> >=> >=> >=> >=> >=> >=> >=> >=> >=> >=> >=>>=> >=> THE PRE SHOW [The camera pans into Tonya Sanders who is standing in front of a black curtain. Tonya is dressed in a tight black dress she has her hair pulled back in a tail. Tonya smiles as she begins.] TS: Welcome everybody to THE PRE-SHOW, I'm Tonya Sanders and for the next thirty minutes I'll be bringing you some highlights of some great RSP action. We will see right here the final matches of the round robin tournament as Motor City Madman takes on Paul "Tacks" Barker and Fabian Cruz takes on Johnny Spades. It's all right here as we get ready for RSP SUNDAY TV~! [The camera fades as the video highlights of the first match appear.] R_S_P____________________________ Paul "Tacks" Barker _V -vs- _V Motor City Madman _V _________________________________V Motor City Madman took early control of the match using his size and power to control Barker. Madman used a powerslam to get a early two count. The match turned after Barker went to the outside of the ring and used his brawling skills to wear down Madman. The two fought on the arena floor for a nine count before returning to the ring. Back in the ring Barker kept the pressure using a neckbreaker to get a near fall. Barker keeping Madman on the mat worked over Madmans neck and upper back with knee and elbow drops. Barker went to the second rope and landed a elbow to the neck and rolled Madman over for a two and a half count. Things turned back to Madman's favor after Barkers attempted DDT was turned into a sidewalk slam. Madman used the second wind and didn't let up finaly ending the match with a double handed chokeslam. Winner: Motor City Madman Time: 6:04 [Cut backstage... Tommy Rowlands is walking in the hallway as Damien Legacy appears behind him, clipping his leg and sending him to the floor. Rowlands attempts to get to his feet but is floored by a boot to the head from the Top Dog. Legacy begins stomping the knee] DL: You like that bitch! Just a little taste of whats gonna happen later [Rowlands swears at Damien, but Legacy just rips a nearby fire extinguisher off the wall] "CCCCCLLLLLAAAAANNNNNGGGGG!!!!!" [And drills him in the side of the head with it] "CCCCCLLLLLAAAAANNNNNGGGGG!!!!!" "CCCCCLLLLLAAAAANNNNNGGGGG!!!!!" [Legacy begins punishing the clipped leg of Rowlands, slamming the extinbuisher repeatedly into the knee. With a smirk he finally drops it on Rowlands midsection as he lies motionless. He brushes of his hands, and turns, disappearing down the hallway. Camera cuts back to Tonya Sanders.] TS: Damien Legacy with an attack on Tommy Rowlands!! Those two will met up on RSP Sunday TV. It's going to be a hectic night as the top half of the bracket is all tied up. On the other hand the man we will see in just a minute Fabian Cruz may have things clinched if he takes out Johnny Spades. R_S_P____________________________ Fabian Cruz /w Kendra Stone _V -vs- _V Johnny Spades _V _________________________________V A hard fought match going back and forth as both men showed their hate for each other. Spades took Cruz from post to post with several high impact moves. Cruz tried to take a breather and regroup with his manager Kendra. That didn't work as Spades wouldn't let up. Spades went to the floor himself and used a chair hitting Cruz with stiff shots to the back. Kendra got involved to help her man turn the tide of the match. Cruz once on offense wouldn't let up using every illeagal tactic he could think of as Kendra kept the ref busy. The end of the match saw Motor City Madman come out and try to hit Cruz with a baseball bat. That backfired causing Madman to hit Spades instead. This allowed Cruz time to hit his finisher and get the pin. Winner: Fabian Cruz Time: 7:03 [Camera cuts to the back as we see a white limo pull up. As it comes to a stop the driver jumps out and goes to the back to help the passanger out. The crowd gives a decent pop as we see Sean Christopher step out. Camera cuts back to Tonya Sanders.] TS: Well the boss is in!! Sean Christopher has arrived and he probably has one thing on his mind. Like who attacked him last week!! [A graphic appears on the screen showing the RSP Western States Herritage Title. A pictuare of Fabian Cruz slams onto the screen.] TS: Right, with his win tonight Fabian Cruz advances to next weeks three way dance for the Western States Herritage title. He joins Mark Hooks if Hooks can last the upcoming three way dance. R_S_P____________________________ Mark Hooks _V -vs- _V Steve Jensen _V -vs- _V Steve Greedy _V _________________________________V The stipulation of this match was if Hooks was pinned or made to submit he would lose his spot in the Western States Herritage title three way dance. Greedy and Jensen were out to make the most of their opportunity. Taking the early minutes double teaming Hooks. Hooks didn't have a chance to breath as he fought off one man he had to face off with the other. As Jensen and Hooks began an exchange of moves Greedy would hit the floor and have an exchange of words with the women at ringside. Hooks took over on Jensen with a Death Valley Driver followed by an inverted DDT. Hooks went for a pinfall but Greedy made the save at the last minute. Greedy and Hooks traded punches allowing Jensen time to get his composure. With Greedy and Hooks locking up Jensen took the chance to roll Hooks up from behind for a two count. Greedy again made the save then hit Jensen with a German suplex from behind and got a three count. Jensen the first eliminated! Greedy and Hooks didn't waste a moment as both went back and forth trading moves. Greedy seeming in trouble pulled the ref in front of a Mark Hooks superkick. With the ref down Hooks hit a low blow on Greedy then went to the outside. Hooks brought a table into the ring and was going to powerbomb Greedy on it but before doing so Matt Allen hit the ring. Allen hit several clotheslines on Hooks then powerbombed him on the table as the crowd exploded. Greedy used this opportunity to wake up the ref and as Allen left the ring Greedy made the pin. Winner: Steve Greedy Time: 10:13 [As the footage shows Greedy celebrating in the ring the camera cuts back to Tonya Sanders.] TS: Unbeliveable! Matt Allen comes in and costs Mark Hooks his title shot. The newcomer Steve Greedy making the best of his first RSP match earns a spot in the Western States Herritage Title three way dance. [We once again see the logo for the three way dance and Steve Greedys pictuare slams onto it. The caption under reads Greedy-vs-Cruz-vs-??] TS: Who will be joining Greedy and Cruz? We'll find out next..RSP SUNDAY TV IS NEXT~! [Camera Fades as credits roll.]
