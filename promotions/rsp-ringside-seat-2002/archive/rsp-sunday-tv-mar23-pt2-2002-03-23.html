[Camera pans back into the arena. We see Anderson Michaels standing in the ring ready to announce the next match.] AM: The following contest is scheduled for one fall. Introducing first Coming down the aisle, hailing from Denver, CO weighing in at 248lbs and being accompined to the ring by Kendra Stone...here is Fabian Cruz! #What did you expect? Fools often sometimes forget Who really knows what's the truth Often dignified How funny changing the tide Feels like you already knew # ["Praise" by Sevendust begins to play over the PA System as the arena rises to their feet booing the duo! Kendra steps out from behind the curtains 1st with her arms raised in the air, the boos being overshadowed by catcalls and whistles as she walks down the aisle slowly. A few feet behind her follows Fabian Cruz who ignores the crowd's jeers] #(Praise) like the devil (Connect) you would never (Sick Man) running circles Feels like you already knew (Praise) like the devil (Connect) you would never (Sick Man) running circles Feels like you already knew# [Stone rounds the ringside apron into their corner as Cruz climbs the steps and stops on the apron looking out over the booing crowd and shakes his head before ducking into the ring. Cruz moves over to his corner and squats down by the ropes leaning down and listening to Kendra for a moment.] #Save you I would never Deny you Even though your hate for me is strong# [Fabian rises back up to his feet and leans back against the corner glaring across the ring and then out to the crowd.] CB: Fabian Cruz one of the upstarts in this business. He has a bright future no doubt. DT: He may be messing with the wrong person. Johnny Spades is not a person to pick a fight with. AM: His opponent from Detroit...MOTOR CITY MADMAN~! [The speakers blast to life with the sound of Saliva's "Click Click Boom". Just as the pyro goes off in big bursts to each side of the entrance stage, the Madman makes his way out carrying a large framed mirror. The Madman brandishes a microphone.] MCM: I just thought I'd bring this with me so that when I beat the living hell out of you, Fabe, you can cry to someone who truly cares about you! [Madman advances to the ring and sets the mirror down at the ringside before jumping in to face Cruz.] CB: The Madman brings a mirror out here. I'm sure Kendra will enjoy that. DT: Madman is truly an idiot. [DING..DING..DING] CB: We have a bell and Cruz quickly jumps to the outside of the ring. DT: Cruz taking his time. Madman is the bigger of the two, I don't blame Cruz for picking his shots. [Cruz slowly slides back into the ring and offers a handshake. Madman accepts but Cruz is quick to slap Madman in the face.] DT: Nice shot! CB: Good way to wake the Madman up. [Madman charges in on Fabian who back peddles to a corner. The ref tries to pull Madman out of the corner. When he does Fabian lands a hard shot to the face followed by a knee to stomach.] DT: Cruz is the brains in this match. CB: He seems to be one step ahead. [Cruz bounces off the ropes ans tries for a clothesline. Madmans catches him with a nice slam. Cruz gets up and is taken over the top rope with a clothesline. Kendra is quick to her mans side.] CB: Kendra quickly to Fabians side. DT: I think their making diner plans! CB: Not likely. It's a strick business relationship between those two. DT: Riight. [Madman goes to the outside after Fabian. Fabian pushes Kendra in front of him. Madman gives out a big scream as the fans respond.] DT: Neanderthal! CB: Cruz is using Kendra as a shield. DT: If that beast lays a hand on her!! [Madman moves in on Kendra and yanks her out of the way. Fabian slides back into the ring. Kendra jumps on the Madmans back a scratches at his eyes.] DT: That's my lady! CB: Fabian had the ref distracted. [Madman rolls into the ring holding his eyes. Cruz comes off the ropes and leaps into the air coming down into Madman's back with the point of his elbow! Cruz continues his assult by driving his fist down into the back of MCM's head. Cruz sits MCM up and kicks him stiff in the spine!] DT: That can't be to comfortable. CB: As big as Madman is, even he will be hurting in the morning. [Cruz drags Madman to the center of the ring. Cruz goes over to a corner and leaps onto the middle rope. Cruz leaping off with a fist drop right between the eyes! Cruz quickly covers MCM hooking his leg for the pin! ONE~! TWO~! THR...] CB: Two and a half as Madman somehow powers his way out of that. [Fabian slaps the mat then questions the ref about the count. Cruz applies a headlock on the Madman. Cruz puts all his pressure on the hold using the ropes for leverage. Madman begins to move to his feet as the crowd begins to cheer.] DT: These people need to shut up. Thier messing up Cruzs concentration. CB: Madman to his feet!! [Madman powers out and slips behind Cruz taking him up and down with a belly-to-back.] DT: How did he do that? [Crowd: MCM-MCM-MCM] CB: This crowd is behind the Madman. DT: This crowd is on drugs! [Madman off the ropes and hits a clothesline. He bounces off the other ropes and hits another clothesline. Cruz stumbles to his feet and Madman picks him up with a double arm chokeslame. Madman goes for the cover.] ONE~! TWO~! THR~!... DT: Foot on the ropes!! Foot on the ropes!! [The ref stops the count as Fabian put his foot on the ropes at the last minute.] CB: I think you can give Kendra an assist on that one! [Madman picks Fabian up and presses him. He then turns Fabian upside down and drops him on his head.] CB: TOMBSTONE!! ONE TWO THR.. DT: No, somehow Fabian slides his shoulder up. CB: Madman climbs to the second rope! [Madman jumps off the second rope and hits a forearm shot. Fabian slides to the outside of the ring.] CB: Kendra on the apron yelling at the ref! [Madman goes over toward Kendra. Kendra tries to slap the Madman but Madman blocks. He grabs Kendra and pulls her top off~! CROWD: RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~!] DT: WHOOO!! CB: Oh my!! [Kendra tries to cover up as he heads to the back. On the other side of the ring Fabian grabs the mirror and rolls it into the ring. Fabian sets the mirror into the corner. Madman turns and charges toward Cruz. Cruz moves out of the way and Madman goes face first into the mirror.] CB: OH MY GAWD~! DT: Fabian with a spear!! [Fabian spears Madman as he stumbles out of the corner. Fabian covers the busted open Madman.. ONE~! TWO~! THREE~!] CB: Fabian with the win!! DT: YES!! YES!! YES!! CB: Madman is bloody! He went hard into that mirror! DT: Idiot! [As Cruz rolls out of the ring. The ref checks on the Madman.] AM: The winner of the match..FFFFABBBIIIANNN CCCRRRUZZ [Camera cuts back to the announcers at ringside.] CB: What a match that was. Could of gone either way. DT: But didn't..The man won!! CB: Right now.. ["I'm Gonna Be Your God" by Slayer blasts out over the PA system. This gets an instant reaction from the crowd, because they know who is about to come out and speak to them.] # I'm all jacked up, I want you here # # In my room, I want you dear # CB: Well here comes the winner of the battle royal. DT: RSPs first ever Western States Herritage Champion. [Out from the back walks out the the brown mop haired Mark Hooks. He wears just his wrestling tights.. well, boots and pads too, but nevertheless he wears his black wrestling tights as he crashes through the entrance portal. He can do nothing but smile as the crowd gives him a very negative reaction.] # I'm Gonna be your God # # I'm Gonna be your God # # I'm Gonna be your f~!king God! # [Hooks slowly walks to the ring, and climbs in making sure that everyone can see that he sort of flexes as he makes his way towards the farside to get himself a microphone from the ring announcer.. after arrogantly snatching the mic, he makes his way over to the middle of the ring and waits for the boos and the music to die down.] MH: And the man that stands before you is going to be the first ever Western States Heritage Champion. DT: Told You! [Boos follow.] MH: Why? Because for the next two weeks all of these so-called wrestlers are going to be out here figh- well, they'll be trying to fight each other.. but either way, while they are all out here trying to beat each other for the single chance just to stand in the same ring as the better half of the American Renegades... [Breather, because he's about to turn blue in the face.] MH: ..I shall be taking my vacation. Which is going to be fully paid by you moronic fans that have come out here toinght, by the way. [The crowd doesn't like that.] MH: Now, come April the Seventh... each and every single one of you will all pay witness to the crowning of moi, Mark Hooks. Now, you need to all remember to bring plenty of tissue with you to the arena, because I promise when the beautiful hunk of gold is hoisted upon this shoulder.. [Hooks Arm Flex~!] MH: ...there won't be a dry eye in the house! But back to the burning question at hand. How is Mark Hooks going to be spending his vacation? Well, the first thing I plan to do is to _not_ watch a single minute of Are Ess Pee TV. Why? Because I already know that not a single person on this roster can last up against a perfect genetic wonder like myself. [Another round of boos.] MH: I will also not be doing what all you people do on your time off, and that's sit on your collective lazy asses and eat cheese puffs. No, you don't get a body like this by sitting around doing nothing. You _also_ don't get a body like this by walking your fat ass over to Subway for a veggie on wheat! [Hooks nods to the crowd.] MH: And the final thing that I will be doing on my vacation, is getting one of those pina coladas with the umbrella in it. Yeah, and while I sip that tasty drink down, not worrying about who I will be facing, three dozen half naked chicks shall be fanning me, rubbing my feet and shoulders. Yes, I'm going to Hawaii and you all can kiss my ass! [Hooks tosses the mic to the ground and gives one last FLEX~! to the crowd as he makes his way out of the ring.] DT: A well deserved vaction for Hooks. He worked hard to get the battle royal win and now he gets to rest! CB: Not so fast. I have just been told that Mark Hooks will be in action..RIGHT HERE, NEXT WEEK!! DT: What? CB: Seems our executive committee has come up with suitable competition for Mr. Hooks. DT: Who? What? Why? CB: Next week Mark Hooks will be taking on "The Expert" Matt Allen!! DT: Who? What? Why? CB: Oh, stop that. Matt Allen alot like Mark Hooks a former AWP tag team champion! What a match that will be. DT: No, Hooks is going to Hawaii! CB: Not anymore! Anderson Michaels is ready to announce the next match! AM: The following contest is the main event of the evening introducing first...THE BBBBIIIIG CHHHILLL ["Calm like a Bomb" by Rage Against the Machine begins to play. The Big Chill walks out from the backstage entrance and stands still. TBC is wearing a black tank top with the words "REIGN OF TERROR" embroidered on the back, black wrestling trunks, black boots, a "RoT" doo-rag and black Oakley sunglasses. TBC has his head shaved with about a day's worth of growth on it and a goatee. He has a tattoo of an icicle on his right bicep, and on his left pectoral he has the words "Badd Intentions" written over a skull and crossbones can be made out. TBC, his face showing disgust, shakes his head. TBC raises one arm as pyrotechnics explode around him. He begins to walk to the ring. TBC gets to the ringside area and jaws a little with some of the fans ringside. TBC climbs into the ring and looks at the other wrestlers in the ring, examining the competition. He walks over to the announcer. TBC extends his arm to the announcer. The announcer doesn't move. TBC snatches the mic from his hand and points at the announcer, yelling. His entrance music dies down.] TBC: Boy, if there's one thing you're gonna learn is that when I ask for something, it ain't exactly a request. You do it! Hell, I tell you I want your pants, you better be dropping trou in a split second or I'll slap the taste out of your mouth! [TBC turns to the crowd.] TBC: I'm about to drop some fool on his head so I'll make this brief. I don't know what my business manager, Donny "Ice" Berg was thinking when he got me this gig, but I'm not liking it a bit! If I knew this was going to be two-bit, backwater establishment stuck in what might as well be Canada, I would have never agreed to it! I mean, you don't get much more backwater than Washington State! [The crowd jeers.] TBC: You call Seattle a city? Jesus H. Christ! I mean, when's the last time this "city" won a World Series, huh? Not like my boys back in the boogie-down Bronx! How about a Super Bowl? How about anything? What about this dump? Olympia? What the hell has ever happened here? [The crowd jeers.] TBC: See, this is how I knew you boo me. You have never even seen a winner! No wonder Gary Payton tries to get traded every damn year! Well, I'm sorry to disappoint. Tonight, you're going to see what you're used to seeing: someone come from New York and win! [TBC flips the mic over his shoulder onto the mat. The announcer scrambles to retrieve it.] AM: His opponent..From... [Tommy Rowlands charges the ring with a chair as Anderson dives through the ropes to get out of the way.] DT: Rowlands with a chair!! Come on now, somebody stop that loser. [CLANG... CLANG... CLANG] CB: Three hard shot with the chair. Rowlands is on fire. He's irate over the attack earlier. DT: That doesn't give him a right to kill a man with a chair! [Rowlands whips Chill into the ropes and clotheslines him with the chair. Rowlands jabs the chair into Chills throat.] DT: CHOKE..CHOKE..DAMN IT THATS A CHOKE!! CB: I don't think the bell sounded yet! [The ref finaly gets the chair away from Rowlands. DING..DING..DING] CB: Now the match starts. DT: Thank god for small miracles. [Rowlands perches himself on Chill and lands in some heavy shots.] CB: Rowlands is getting his payback. DT: Rowlands is a loser..loser I tell ya! [Rowlands whips Chill into the ropes. Chill comes off and Rowland connects with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker. Rowlands quickly picks Rowlands up and hits a belly-to-belly suplex.] DT: Did Rowlands take drugs before the match? CB: Rowlands not letting up. [Rowlands takes Chill over with a sidesuplex then applies a cross armbreaker. Chill screams in pain as Rowlands applies the pressure.] CB: Rowlands making Chill feel the pain. [Rowlands lets go of the armbreaker and pulls Chill up from the mat He whips Chill into the turnbuckle. Chill hits the turnbuckles with his chest, and stumbles out backwards. Tommy is waiting and hits a reverse Olympic slam. Chill lands on his face and chest.] DT: I have to admit that was a nice mover by Rowlands. CB: Rowlands taking Chill apart here. [Rowlands picks Chill up and tries for a pump handle slam. Chill slides over Rowlands back and pushes Rowlands into the turnbuckles. As Rowlands comes off Chill hits a swinging neckbreaker.] CB: Chill with a despiration move and both men are down now. DT: Chill never does anything out of despiration. He's a master in that ring. [Chill and Rowlands slowly to their feet. Both men exchange blows and Chill drops to his knee and hits a low blow. He follows with a quick DDT. Chill then drapes his knee over Rowlands throat as the ref counts.] CB: Who's choking now? DT: Doesn't matter. CB: I figuared as much? DT: Then why ask? [TBC irish-whips Rowlands into the corner, face first. As Rowlands is rebounding from the corner, TBC bounds into the near ropes and connects with a clothesline. Without breaking stride, TBC runs across the ring, bounds into the far ropes and comes back and drops a running elbowdrop across the throat of Rowlands.] DT: Chill with control now and he's on his way to victory. [Rowlands is down on his stomach as TBC rolls out of the ring. He grabs hold of Rowlands's arm and pulls him toward the corner. He places the injured arm against the ring post. TBC takes a few steps back and nails a dropkick to the arm, smashing it against the post. Chill now grabs a chair and slams Rowlands arm with the chair.] CB: Chill taking Rowlands arm apart. DT: Rowlands arm was crushed with that chair. [Chill gets back into the ring and picks Rowlands up putting his injured hand behind his back. Chill slams Rowlands on the hand. He then climbs to the second rope and drops straight down stomping on the arm.] DT: That was a beauty. CB: Rowlands screaming in pain. It wouldn't be a surprise if that arm is broken. [Chill whips Rowlands into the ropes. Rowlands puts on the breaks, grabs Chill and spins him around, then drops him with a inverted DDT.] DT: That was despiration! [Rowlands and Chill both slow to get up again. Rowlands the first up takes several shots at Chill. Chill blocks a shot and DDTs Rowlands on the already hurt arm. Chill rolls Rowlands over and goes for a pin.. ONE TWO THR..] CB: Rowlands kicks out. DT: Slow count..that was a slow count. [Big Chill picks up Rowlands and whips him into the ropes. Rowlands reverses it, Chill hits the corner hard and Rowlands charges with a spear. Chill moves and Rowlands crashes into the ringpost shoulder first. Rowlands stumbles out of the corner and Chill puts a sleeper on then drops down. Rowlands slams the back of his head into the mat.] CB: Chill with a cover... ONE~! TWO~! THREE~! DT: CHILL WINS~! CB: Chill wins. AM: The winner of the match...THE BBBIGGG CHHILLLL!!! [Rowlands is on the mat holding his shoulder.] CB: There may be some serious injury to Rowlands shoulder. DT: Boo Who.. CB: Rowlands gave it a hell of a shot. DT: He came up short...AGAIN! [The crowd cheers for Rowlands as he makes it to his feet holding his arm. Big Chill comes back into the ring and tries to hit Rowlands with the chair. Rowlands drop toe holds Chill who lands face first into the chair. Rowlands grabs the chair and smashes Chill with it knocking him out.] CB: Rowlands stands tall in the end!! DT: Yeah but he still lost!! CB: He's still a heavy favorite to make it to the end. DT: Hardly! CB: We're about out of time. We'll see you next week!! [Camera fades to black then flicks back on. We see Tonya Sanders in the back. Rowlands grabs the mic from Tonya..] "Akiva. You just saw what I am capable of doing. Unlike some, I am _PROVING_ my worth. Runner up in the battle royal. The destruction of a man who I was told was always better than me. Well, tonight I showed the world not only who the BETTER man was, but more importantly, who the _BEST_ man is. You are next my old foe. You shall meet me in the ring next week, and you WILL know what the meaning of pain is. I will finally wrap my hands around your throat, and drop you down with the Unadulterated Truth. You shall know the meaning of defeat. You will finally understand what _RESPECT_ is. I am sick of your games, and next week, it is all over." "As for you Hooks. Be weary. I am Tommy Rowlands, and my sights are set firmly on you. After I am done with Saul, You will be my prime target. You saw what I did tonight. I made an example of Motor City Madman. Not so much for Saul, but for you. By the time I am through with you, you will wish you had never finished with tag team wrestling. You will _NEVER_ forget the name of Tommy Rowlands. The man who was responsible for your downfall." "See you soon boys" (With these words, a mass of sweat and hair that is Tommy Rowlands hands the Mic to Tonya, saying something, although we don't know what that makes her laugh, before heading back to his locker room.
