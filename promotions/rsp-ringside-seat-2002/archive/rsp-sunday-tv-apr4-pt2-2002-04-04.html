[RSP] RSP Sunday TV [2/2]

4 April 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
CB: What a war this has been, Rowlands comes in with a stifling clothesline and Legacy is down again, quick cover.... ONE TWO Kickout by Damien Legacy. Tommy hauls him up and whips him to the ropes. [Rowlands connects with a BIG boot of doom knokcing Legacy down with a THWACK but he screams out and grabs hold of his left knee dropping to the floor.] CB: That's done it for Rowlands, he may have dislocated it. DT: Good good. [Legacy crawls over and makes the cover but Rowlands easily gets a shoulder up. Damien resorts to plan B and drags Tommy to the corner.] DT: WHOOOOO, OLD SCHOOL RULES! CB: Legacy is tieing Tommy up around the ringpost...its a FIGURE FOUR! DT: CLASSIC, I LOVE that! [It's illegal but, well, who gives a crap, its quality in a bite size packet. Rowlands is begging, literally for Legacy to give him a break...the only break on Damiens mind is a broken knee.] CB: FINALLY Legacy is forced to break the hold. DT: But...oh, he has the ring steps in his hand now! CB: So much for old school! [THWACK, Rowlands has his leg CRUSHED against the ringpost, Legacy hits it again for good measure.] DT: Who needs old school? CB; Tommy? DT: Point proven. Legacy rolls back into the ring and hooks the leg... ONE TWO THREEE... YES! NO! [Did I getcha?] CB: Tommy got a shoulder up, DT, can I call you that? DT: Everyone else does. CB: OK, this match has been an epic. We're nearly at the end of the scheduled time. DT: Well, we better get this over with son then! CB: AMEN! [Legacy pulls Tommy up before rocking him with a jaw-breaker. Tommy manages to stay on his feet but gets planted by a follow up drop-kick. legacy drops down for another close two count.] CB: Only a minute or so left! DT: Legacy has Tommy in the corner now, setting him up on the top rope. CB: Tommy fighting back but Legacy rakes the eyes. DT: Do whatever it takes, theres so much at stake. [Legacy springs up onto the ropes and takes Tommy over with a HURICANRANNA! Tommy hits the mat hard, Legacy falters and slowly crawls over for the cover.] CB: ONE TWO KICKOUT! DT: Legacy needs to do something quickly, if this ends in a draw barring a miracle they won't get the title shot on offer! [I'd run through the maths but time is ticking.] DT: Legacy tries the irish whip but Tommy reverses and sends him to the ropes. Legacy ducks the clothesline and springs off of the ropes with a cross body... ...Tommy ducks! CB: Legacy is pulled back up, bent over with a kick to the gut! DT: UNADULTERATED TRUTH IS COMING! CB: He won't have time to hit it! DT: LEGACY CLIPS THE KNEE AND POWERS UP! CB: LEGACY DRIIIIIIIIIIIVAAAAAHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHHH [TOMMY GETS DRILLED...] *DING FUCKIN DING* CB: WHAT THE...Thats it! OUT OF TIME! DT: Legacy has it won though, Legacy has it won! CB: NO, he doesn't... DT [SHOUTING] CARRY IT ON, CARRY IT ON...WE HAVE TO HAVE A WINNER! [The ref looks down to DT, the boss's son...its a dilemma. he waves for the bell to be rung again...ITS OVERTIME K'BABY!] CB: Can you do that? DT: Who knows...LEGACY MAKING THE COVER! ONE TWO T H R E E E E E E E E [...] CB: NO....ROWLANDS GETS A SHOULDER UP! DT: I CANNOT BELIEVE IT...DID HE? CB: YES! [Legacy gets up...STUNNED to say the least.] CB: The break gave Rowlands JUST enough time to get his head together! [Legacy is PISSED and STUNNED now, he is all over the referee, pushing him around the ring. Tommy is crawling over to him, he grabs a leg!] DT: SCHOOLBOY FROM ROWLANDS... ONE TWO THREEEEEEEE! [Holy MUDDA FUQQA, talk about Sports Entertainment! Rowlands got the victory! He stole it, the bastard! Utter bastard!] CB: I can't quite believe it! DT: Rowlands gets the win...how in the hell did he manage that! CB: I just don't know. DT: Collen? CB: What? DT: We have a tie in this bracket!! CB: You know..you're right. Both Akiva and Rowlands have two wins each. DT: Who gets into the title match? CB: I don't know. We'll have to find out. DT: When? CB: Not now..we're out of time!! DT: What? CB: Good night! DT: What? [Camera cuts to black.] [C] 2002, RSP
