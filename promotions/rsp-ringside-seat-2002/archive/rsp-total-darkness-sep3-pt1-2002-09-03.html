[BEEP] [BEEP] [BEEP] [Camera fades in and we see Sean Christopher laying in a hospital bed. His face is black and blue and his chest and arm connected to monitors.. BEEP BEEP BEEP] SC: Damn it. Look what they did to me? Look at me. [Sean holds his side and grimces a little. Sean caughs as the camera pans to the side a bit and we see RSP VP Desire. Desire is sitting at Seans bedside.] Desire: Sean take it easy. You'll recover. You know what the doctor said right? You heard what he said. SC: Yeah..Yeah I heard him. BUT THAT DOESN'T MEAN SHIT! Look Sinister, Chill, and The Professional need to pay!! They need to pay for what they did to me. Desire: They will, I'll make sure of it. But first.. [Desire pulls out a stack of papers.] Desire: I need you to sign these. SC: What are they? Desire: Just papers. Just papers. They don't matter. You need to relax. Sign these papers and we can concentrate on getting you better. [Desire hands the papers to Sean. Sean signs the papers and begins to look over them but Desire pulls them away.] Desire: They're not important. SC: Whatever. Just do me a favor. Get to the office, get on the phone, and call whoever you can find to end Sinisters career. Do that for me. Desire: I will..I promise you. After I'm done RSP will never be the same. [Desire walks off leaving Sean laying helpless in bed. RSP PRESENTS....... T O T D_A_R_K_N_E_S_S L [Cameras fade into the RSP "Newsroom" where we see Tonya Sanders sitting with RSP news and rumor man Rumor Mills. Tonya is dressed in a black business type pant suit and Rumor has on black slack with a white dress shirt.] TS: Welcome to Total Darkness. I'm Tonya Sanders along with my man Rumor Mills. Rumor all of us here at RSP are still feeling the after shock of Dangerous Games. RM: Indeed. Alot happend at that event and still more is occuring as the smoke clears. TS: Fabian Cruz is our new Western States Heritage Champion and as I understand it Kendra Stone has already got herself some muscle to help guard her new treasure. RM: I'm sure we'll be hearing from the Stone Foundation on Breakdown. TS: What about the return of Tommy Rowlands? RM: Rowlands has turned a new corner here in RSP. The attack on Big Chill was bloody and vile. TS: Then the formation here in RSP of Reign of Terror. RM: Big Chill, Sinister, and The Professional. Our boss felt first hand what those three can do! TS: Speaking of which lets go to a pre-recorded in-ring from two of those three. ("Hush" by KRS-1 begins to play as the lights dim and an anticipation comes over the crowd. The crowd, not recognizing the song to be anyone's theme, is curiously on edge. ) ###### Uh-huh, whatchu thought? Uh-huh, we was done? Uh-huh, whatchu thought?! Yeah, yeah Yeah, whatchu thought? Uhh, uhh, uhh Yo, yo, back again! What's that? Back again! What's that? Word! C'mon ###### (Out from the backstage area appear three men and the crowd explodes into cheers. Sinister and The Big Chill make up the two back points.) ###### A North Face and skully hat don't MAKE YOU A THUG A army suit, a pair of Timbs don't MAKE YOU A THUG An ill mug and a gat don't MAKE YOU A THUG A real thug is a thug that's HUSH! ###### (Mr. Greene walks gingerly with a large bandage upon his bald head wearing a white three-piece Armani suit, a black dress shirt with a silk red tie and shiny black dress shoes. Sinister wears a dark red Nike jumpsuit that has "ChiTown Monsta'" written on the front in black letters with a pair of black Nike Air Jordan's. He moves very gingerly and favors his left ribcage, undoubtedly a result of being burned by Sean Christopher. A few small bandages adorn his forehead and he moves with a slight limp of his left leg, but he appears, nonetheless, as intense as always. The "Soul Pole" sits very comfortably in his large right hand. The Big Chill is wearing a "TBC ain't nuttin' to F&*K wit" t-shirt, blue jeans and black Dr. Marten boots. He has let some of his blonde hair grow back and his goatee is neatly trimmed. He's got a bandage on his forehead. TBC has his arms up in the air, seemingly enjoying the acknowledgment from the capacity crowd. The begin to make their way to ringside.) ###### Yo, yo, yo, check it, check it Let us begin, KRS, winnin again, KRS-One did it again KRS bigger than them, look at them they bit it again I think my gun just gonna start clickin again Click-clack, I always spit back Anybody call my name I go get that In fact we draggin 'em out, to a deserted route The teacher returns, you must learn, the word is out No doubt highly respected, Front Page Records Off the hook, yet still connected Are you teachin yourself, teachin yourself? I'm like history repeatin itself "Criminal Minded", you've been blinded again Lookin for my style you can't find it again ###### (The three men make it to ringside. Mr. Greene, taking point, climbs into the ring and retrieves the house mic from the ring announcer. Sinister and TBC split at the ring, Sinister going to the right, TBC going to left. They are slapping hands and acknowledging the support. They meet up on the other side of the ring. The two men bump fists and climb into the ring. Mr. Greene waits while the music fades out. The house microphone is tossed to Mr. Greene with a nod and a smile.) Greene: "Good evening ladies, gentlemen and young people of the RSP." [HUGE OVATION AND POP!] [Mr. Greene's deep voice settles easily over the crowd and they respond very positively to him, giving him respect for his efforts to help Sinister avoid being cheated] Greene: "Thank you very much, you're all too kind. It is an honor to be here, especially after my head was used for target practice by a glass window by Z, who, by the way, continues to twitch in his hospital bed! [Laughter and cheers fill the arena] I know many of you were very surprised to see The Big Chill [THUNDEROUS POP as Chill smiles and nods respectfully around the arena] and my son, The Professional [VERY LOUD POP!] working together with Sinister and myself, but under the circumstances it was necessary." VR: "You know Blackwell, I have to admit something. For a long time I've not liked Sinister and I've DETESTED Mr. Greene, but after that battle against Sean Christopher, even though I lost a LOT of money I have newfound respect for them. Greene: "Without further ado, I'll allow these two men to speak about recent events and how they feel about them." [Mr. Greene hands the mic over to TBC as the crowd roars with approval. TBC turns to Sinister with a big smile, exchanges a few words and both men laugh. TBC waits a few seconds as the cheers die down.] TBC: Who woulda thunk it? Who would have predicted this kind of reception? (TBC turns to Sinister.) TBC: They must really love you, Sin, if they're giving rat bastard like me this kind of ovation! (Sinister laughs and raises a fist into the air and the crowd pops. TBC laughs.) TBC: Well, you know what they say about family. There might be a fight between family members, but if you're an outsider and try to get in on the family business, you're gonna get stomped. And make no mistake about it, Sean Christopher and X,Y,Z, you got stomped. Me and Sin, we got way back. It hasn't always been pretty, it hasn't always been nice, but when you guys tried to take Sin out of the game, the family had to come together. And what you didn't take into account was that we're no ordinary family. This is the Reign of Terror you're looking at. While the crew may not be officially, once you're R.o.T., you're always R.o.T. Christopher, you learned the hard way. You put one of us in the hospital, the R.o.T. puts one of you in the ground, six feet deep. You don't know viciousness, you don't understand cruelty. You haven't walked in our shoes. You haven't seen, let alone done, the things we have. Next time, jump on the 'net and see if you can find yourself any bootleg H.O.W.F. tapes. You might just get educated. (TBC runs his hand over his goatee.) TBC: What's done is done. And you know what, Sin? (Sinister looks at TBC with a questioning look.) TBC: Thank God I recover quickly, huh? (Sinister nods his head and chuckles.) TBC: I mean, Tommy Rowlands tries to commit murder one and less than an hour later, I'm busting up Alphabet Soup with the Pro and Mr. Greene. My own recuperative powers simply amaze me. Wooooo...Amazing ain't it? Good thing Rowlands only plays a thug on TV, imagine what he would have done to me if he really knew what he was doing with that weapon? I hope you listened to the song we walked in with. You see, me and Sin don't just come to the ring with the latest top 40 hit by Artist Du Jour. Everything we do means something. Rowlands, an ill mug and a gat don't MAKE YOU A THUG! Let me translate it for you. Looking tough and brandishing a weapon doesn't make you tough. Finishing the job is what makes you tough, Hell, you didn't even get the job done for one night. You help Damian "Someone, please, help me build my" Legacy take my RSP Television title... Christ, I've seen less people during the opening of the Olympics than I did during my match! (Mr. Greene laughs and shrugs.) TBC: Anyhow, you help that bag of monkey dung, Legacy, take my title and you don't even lay me up for the rest of the night? Chump, you had a free shot and you didn't make it count. You should have stuck me in the ground and started kicking dirt over me. But you didn't. Now, you have a problem to resolve. Let me give you a history lesson and Sinister can back me up on this. I haven't always had my arm raised at the end of the night. Anyone claims that is lying through their teeth. But I have always been last man standing! Whatever it takes, I make sure to get, dust off, wipe the blood off and look at you say "Guess what? I'm still here." And that's going to come back and bite you. (TBC pause momentarily.) TBC: Now, I could go on but I know the Man is dying to get in a word edge-wise, so let me present to you, RSP fans, the LAST MAN STANDING.....
