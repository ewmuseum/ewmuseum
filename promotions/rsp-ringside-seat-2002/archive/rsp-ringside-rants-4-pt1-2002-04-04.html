RINGSIDE_SEAT_PROMOTIONS R S P http://www.hockey-autographs.net/~rsp 4/01/02 RINGSIDE RANTS [~]Steve Greedy [Debut] [~] Tommy Rowlands [~]Damien Legacy =============================== [ Blank Screen ] Man's Voice : " Ouch. " Females Voice : " What is a matter. You don't like it ? I can do it a different way." Man's Voice : " It's not that, Just be more gentle will ya gorgeous..I am fragile there." [ There is silence for a moment, Until Suddenly...] Man's Voice : " DAYYYYUMMMMMM " [ We fade into a view of Steve Greedy sitting in a chair getting his haircut with a huge smile on his pretty little face. He has a towel over his nice suit, and even two seperate rags over his expensive shoes trying to cover them from the lose hair. A beautiful Blonde has some clippers standing behind him, and is looking at Greedy as he looks at himself in the mirror checking out his haircut. Which looks pretty similiar to the one he already had, For those out there who know Greedy from previous arrangements.] "The Rich One" Steve Greedy : " You did a good job girl. Makes me just want to kiss ya." [ The girl leans in towards Greedy expecting a kiss, Greedy glances at the girl, then glances away quickly, as if he didn't want to kiss her.The look on his face says something to the extent of "disgusting".] "The Rich One" SG : " But I better get going..I am running late already << Greedy gets up out of the chair tossing the Towel's, and rags to the ground. >> You did good though sweetie, Lil to much off the top, but overall not to shabby." [ Greedy hands the lady a One Hundred dollar bill, and smiles to her walking off. Once he walks out of the shop, He leans up against the door holding his head up high.] "The Rich One" SG : " I can't believe that nasty breathe bitch wanted me to give her a kiss...I mean ME...Steve Greedy..You would think she could of at least popped in some gum or something for me.. She did have a nice lil figure on her." [ Greedy then lowers his head, and notices the camera..He flashes his trademark smirk, then speaks.] "The Rich One" SG : " I didn't see ya there, How long ya been there ? << Pauses >> Oh nevermind, Doesn't matter...So let's see, What federation you from..SCW Maybe ? If so I aint talking bout my title defense this weekend, Maybe IWF/WOW ? Yes I attacked Seth Draken for a reason...Wait, Maybe your IGA ? I refuse to get on a plane and go to Australia again damnit.." [ Greedy leans in towards the camera, and notices the RSP logo on the side of it.He smiles, almost blushing and scratches his head.] "The Rich One" SG : " Oh...RSP Is it ? The new federation I signed up with. Well lemme see hear." [ Greedy digs around in his pocket searching for something. The first item he pulls out is a condom still in the package. He drops to that ground still blushing, The next item is tickets to Wrestlebowl IV, And then the next item is a piece of paper which is crinkled up. He puts the tickets back in his pocket, and trys to un wrinkle the pice of paper. Once he does so,He looks up smiling..] "The Rich One" SG : " Says here yall want me to fight Mark Hooks and Steve Jenson this Sunday in Tacoma for a shot at being in some Western States Heritage title match." [ Steve nods in agreement.] "The Rich One" SG : " I think I can arrange that. I don't got nothing else to do Sunday, Not like I am gonna go to Church or anything << Chuckles >> Plus it don't get much easier than fighting those two." [ Steve leans in towards the camera and whispers " Iv'e seen there tapes" Greedy leans back to his normal position and shivers.] "The Rich One" SG : " It's pretty sad...I watched the greatest moments of Steve Jenson once, and it showed him kicking out of an inside cradle..I don't know, I am sure somewhere somebody was on there couch going "Damn, That Steve Jenson is awseome" , But I personally wasn't very impressed." [ Greedy begins to walk down the concrete pavement, and the camera follows.] "The Rich One" SG : << Still walking >> " Last time I saw Steve was in AWP, And this Mark Hooks guy I have just heard about and seen glimpses of...Nuttin to serious, I don't think I have anything to worry about though, unless these two have got a little bigger, smarter and faster in the past couple months. Which I highly doubt." [ Greedy stops walking and turns around towards the camera.] "The Rich One" SG : " They haven't got bigger have they << Says in a serious tone >> Oh don't answer that It doesn't really matter.." [ Greedy gulps, and keeps on walking down the side walk area. We can see from this view, His suit is indeed Armoni..] "The Rich One" SG : " I have been a busy man as of late, Leaving my mark on many federations, and this one will be know different. Not just will I defeat these two men and take the spot at shooting for the title whenever that title match may be, but I will do something even more fun, and make these two men look like fools in there Sunday Night when that bell rings..I want you two guys to remember something very important, even if you block out every other comment I ever make in the RSP...." [ Greedy reaches a very nice Red Corvet ZR1, and stops at the door. He then turns to the camera.] "The Rich One" SG : " Remember, Money isn't everything, It's the only thing.." [ Greedy holds one finger up, and smiles toward the camera.] "The Rich One" SG : " That is the one thing you guys need to remember, and Gentleman..Nobody has more money than the man your looking at, I do mean _NOBODY_. So in that ring, even when your feeling good about yourself cause you landed a bodyslam or a suplex of some sort. Remember that saying in the back of your head, That way you will be prepared to go _BANKRUPT_ When the time is right, and the situation comes about when I am ready to win my debut match up in RSP. Which I do promise, I will win.." [ Greedy winks to the camera, and lowers his one finger down slowly to his side. ] "The Rich One" SG : " I don't mean to be rude, but that is the sad and true reality of the situation guys...Just keep in mind it's just business, Nothing personal. I am just a guy from Little Rock, Arkansas doing what I do best, Not a guy looking to torture with my above average skills week after week..If your lucky, This will be the first and last time you ever see me..Maybe cause the guys with the power will realize you guys don't need to be in the ring with me cause of the obvious talent diference, Maybe cause I will buy you guys out of ya contract telling you that you can still wrestle, as long as you avoid me, or maybe cause I will make you look so bad, You will never be able to bring yourself back into a wrestling ring. We will just have to wait and see which one happens." [ Greedy opens the door to the Corvet, and gets in..He starts the engine and rolls down the window waving to the camera as he peels off.] [Fade Out] ============================================== (As the scene fades in, we find ourselves located in a small village church. The building glows as a variety of multi coloured light all meets in the centre of the room. The light is no longer bright as it would be outside, but rather dull, and colour filled as it penetrated the stain glass windows. The floor is made of a marble mosaic, all white except for the pathway leading to the alter which is a deep blue.) (Exactly in front of us lies a small stone pillar with a stone bowl of holy water upon it. Suddenly, the cameraman is shoved, and he stumbles forward, barely missing the holy water. As the camera turns, we see it is none other than Tommy Rowlands. He steps forward, and slowly dips the tip of his left hand into the water. He bows his head, his eyes closing. He raises his hand to his forehead, leaving a small droplet of the water, before moving down to his stomach, and both pecks, leaving a small wet circle, before he shakes his hand. As he does so, small beads of shining water fly of, distorting the light that comes through the stained glass window.) (Tommy slowly turns, and faces the camera. We can clearly see a deep cut across his forehead, held close by numerous frail stitches. His hair is tied back, however two strands hang down across his face, one on either side. He walks up the isle, towards the altar. Before he reaches it, he turns and faces the camera.) TR: You know, even when it comes to religion, there is a line you cannot cross. You see this? (Tommy points downwards towards a set of the deep blue tiles that surround the altar.) This is a line I cannot cross. If I do, I sin. I will be thrown out of this church, and told never to step foot in it again, for I am a sinner. They say all can be forgiven in the church, but for some reason, crossing this line when not one of the clergy is seen as a deadly sin. TR: Odd that isn't it. However, by paying a penance, I can be forgiven, but never forgotten. This rings some bells. Another thing that rings some bells is the phrase that our saviour (a mocking tone takes over Tommy's voice as he continues), Our Lord Almighty, Jesus Christ once said "an eye for an eye". Well, last week, Saul, you and I met. Finally, THE CLASH OF THE TITANS took place. The match _EVERYBODY_ wanted to see. We beat each other like dogs. TR: Akiva, I told you anything you did to me, I would do back, and you had better believe that I lived up to that. I lived up to the whole "Eye for an eye" thing, which if you ask me is one of the ONLY thing Jesus said that did make sense. There was one difference between you and me though Akiva. I gave you everything you gave to me, only I_WAS_BETTER. Its that simple. And you know what. Even after the beating you gave me, and the beating you took, I still hold absolutely, (excitedly Tommy continues) positively NO DAMNED RESPECT FOR YOU! (As Tommy's voice raises, it bellows throughout the small church. Tommy ducks slightly, as if something had flown just over his head, he then looks up, and continues.) TR: And again, Akiva, another parallel with the church. I can forget our "past", and let you get on with your "career", but you'd better believe that I _WON'T_ forget. If you so much as breathe at me in a manner I interpret inappropriate, I swear to god I will END you, and the next time you see this chapel will be looking upwards from your coffin. TR: However, I have more important fish to fry than you Saul. That's right, its time to bury the past, and look to the future. My immediate future is the man who likes to call himself "Top Dog". It's not surprising really that he associates himself with such a nickname as it's about all that there is to be fearful of. I watched you, just two weeks ago being torn limb by limb y one Saul Akiva. Well, Legacy, think back to that match, and remember what Saul did to you. And then think back to last week, and remember what I did to Saul. Now, put those two images together, and you aren't even close to what I am going to do to you. (Tommy starts making his way back down to the aisle, before bursting the doors open, and walking outside. It's a bright spring day outside, birds are singing, and the sun is shining. Tommy walks out, and goes and leans against the wall. It's a deep red brick wall, which separates the grass in front of the church from the country lane on the other side.) TR: I think, when talking about this, I really should not be in the confides of a church! Legacy, this weekend in the ring, I shall be the cat, and you shall be the mouse. You see, like the cat, I have no intention of making this an easy run in for you. Like the cat, I shall be playing with you. Simply toying with you. Like the mouse, you will have absolutely no idea what is coming next. And then, when you least expect it, I will strike. Not with a lethal blow, oh no, that's just when the real pain will start. I will simply hit numerous near fatal blows to you. I will disorient you. I will hurt you. I will make you wish you had never lived. (Tommy places both hands on the wall, and then with both arms, lifts himself onto the wall, where he sits, looking deep into the camera.) TR: Then. And only then. When you can take no more, I will lift you up, and I will start all over again. Slam after slam. Suplex after suplex. You won't know what's hit you, let alone what's coming. And when that points come, when I am simply bored with you, that is when you will see the Unadulterated Truth, and know that finally, the torment is over. TR: Legacy, I will destroy you. I will show you a plane of pain that you didn't think could possibly exist. Legacy, if I was you, I would brace myself, because you are going to feel more pain than you can imagine. Sleep well little one, and conserve your energy, for I promise you, you will need it. See you in the ring mouse. (With this, as sure as night follows day, we fade to black.) =============================================================== [Fade in, inside the locker of Damien Legacy. He gingerly shakes his leg out as he reaches inside his locker, pulling out his gear bag. He turns and takes a seat on the bench, and begins unlacing his boots] "Truth hurts don't it Chill? But failure, _that_ is a very bitter pill to swallow. But one you where forced to swallow eh main man?" [Legacy flashes a smirk then winces in pain as he pulls of his left boot. He rubs his knee as he looks back at the camera once again] "For all the blathering you did about how big and how bad you are Chilly, I proved that you're nothing more than a pompous windbag with a chip on his shoulder because you just can't hang anymore. Just a small bump in the road Chill, thats what I said you where and I think I more than proved it after tonight. Can't blame your strategy at going after the wheels though, a big oaf like you would have to to even stand a chance. I think I've wasted more than enough time on you though, so I'm gonna move onto the next bump in the road, the next wictim..." "Tommy Rowlands" "The man I owe a big thank you for taking out that lump of crap Akiva. You've given me knew life in this tournament Tommy, back to square one again. We're all even. I bet Akiva's drowning in a river of tears after finding out he's just not as good as he thinks he is. That aside Tommy, your hopes and dreams of wearing the top gold all come crashing down around you in a matter of days. Chill honestly believed I had no chance against him, and I beat him in what many consider an upset. You better be prepared Mr. Rowlands because I'm no longer an "Underdog" as Chill so eloquently put it. After I beat you next week its going to be because everyone thinks I should. No surprises this time scrappy, after disposing of the guy that you couldn't, I'm no longer an afterthought in this tourney. Everyone wrote me off after getting drilled by Akiva, but now I'm right back in the thick of things" [Legacy tosses his boots into the bag and grabs a black wifebeater from inside the locker, sliding it on] "I'll give you all the credit in the world Tommy, you did what many people felt was impossible, and you stopped Saul. You found a chink in the armor, you wanted it _more_ than he did. You're not better than Akiva Tommy, but your heart, your desire, your _hatred_ for him carried you to victory. Those emotions won't be what drives me to victory over you, but the need to succeed, the need to be the very best in this industry, is whats going to make the difference when we step through those ropes. We're gonna see who wants it more Tommy, and I have no doubts that its going to be..." "_Me_" [Legacy unwraps his left fist tossing the tape into the garbage] "If the world hasn't realized how bad I need to win this tournament after defeating a man that was favored to win it all, then they will realize it after I get past you. When I got a phone call from the higher ups in the RSP offices I realized my time had finally come. When it took four guys to dump me in the Battle Royal I was starting to think that maybe these guys are a little scared of the new kid in the sandbox. And when I dumped Chill on his bloated head with a Legacy Driver and pinned him for the one, two, three.... I knew I had arrived. I had a revelation. That this tournament is mine for the taking. That getting to the finals of this thing, would be the hardest road because that joke of a wrestler hooks wouldn't stand a snowballs chance in hell at defeating me in the title match. So getting theres gonna be the bitch Tommy. Getting there means disposing of you in much the same matter that I disposed of TBC. But getting there also means more to me, than it ever could to you, or Akiva, or the Big Chill. Just remember that Tommy. Don't feel bad about losing, 'cause ya did it against the one man in Ringside Seat Promotions, that just would not be denied..." "The Top Dog, Damien Legacy" "I will be the flag bearer, mark my words" [Fade]
