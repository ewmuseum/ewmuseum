[RSP] RSP Breakdown [1/4]

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We cut backstage to the dressing room of Saul Akiva. The RSP Western State Champion is sat forward from the backless bench in his black 'Simply...' T-shirt and work jeans. His greying hair is damp and pushed back up from his scalp.] [Across his right shoulder is an unfamiliar belt, far bigger than the RSP Western States and it is adorned by three simple letters, R-A-W, across an etched globe. Across his other shoulder is the RAW New York regions Manhattan Title, again, the gold is clean and bright. Then, around his waist is the RSP Western States Heritage. Probably about 5lbs less gold on it, is that a leather imitation as well?] [Saul glances away from the camera with disdain before cocking his head slowly back.] "Ah guess your wonderin' just why you got to see me standing in an old ring talkin' about RAW rather than sat in a hole jabberin' about RSP? Well, it's pretty simple, I jus' wanted y'all to see that while all the RSP bitches like Fabian Cruz, Eric Emerson and alike are talking about dethronin' the champ, the champ has been off adding to his collection with a couple more. Hell, look at me boys, I've got more gold on me now than you're ever likely to have. While you've been sniffin' around Washington after this [pats the RSP belt] I've been stepping into the ring all over the country, in front of more than 3 families and a homeless guy an' setting about securing my place not just in RSP history, but in the whole freakin history of the business. First RSP Champion First new WAR! Champion First new RAW World Champion. How's that sit wit ya? All the little poodles sniffing around my ass tellin' anyone who'll listen that I ain't deserving of this RSP title. That they're all better, that they've got the skills to take it off of me...guess y'all feeling pretty stupid right about now when you see me sittin' here with about 50lbs of gold." [An unashamed smirk, Saul rolls his tongue about his mouth examining how dry it has become through all the talk. His lips to are cracked and for a champion, he doesn't look all to good. Jaundiced, dark rings under his eyes and he's lost weight. He looks almost ghoulish.] "Tha's why you had to see what you saw on Ringside Rants, I don't even need to be here people. I could up and leave RSP and say screw it right now if I wanted to and I could do it with more money and more of a goddamn reputation than any one of you is ever gonna get. See, I could up and leave but ah just don't want to. As long as I'm here I'm making all of you look bad, as long as I'm in RSP no one else is even gonna get close enough to taste the gold around my waist unless I break their freakin' faces open with it. Hows that sit wit ya? So I'm not gonna up and leave just yet because I like makin' all of yous look like the pieces of sh[BEEP] to this business and this company that ya' are. Know what I talk about in the back with the RAW boys? I tell ya, it sure aint about what an up an' coming star Fabian Cruz is. It ain't about Eric Emerson and what a career he's carved out for himself, don't think I've ever heard anyone talk abou' Vance Randhall. Same goes for TBC, 'cept of course he's been around the game getting' his ass kicked for years just like Sinister." [Saul leans back to reveal the full motif on the front of hi shirt '...the Champ'.] "An' I tell you something else, the reason you got to see what you saw on Rants wasn't just for your education, it was because ah told em to put it in. See, they ain't jus' gonna let the Champ run out on RSP without a fight, tha's why you saw what you saw. 'Cause the boys in the front office know exactly what I am to RSP, I'm its fu[BEEP]in' saving grace. I'm what set it apart from all the other feds lookin' to start up in Washington; I'm what they can build around for this Pay Per View. I'm the asset that none a' you other guys can be... Hell, I'm Saul Akiva. Who the hell are you? See, that's it, I'm the champ and I'm making the rules, I told 'em to put that video in the player and they did it because I make the rules around here. And the rules don't get to change 'till I say so." [Saul sucks up all the moisture in his mouth and rolls it around the end of his tongue, slowly working it into a mass before spitting it out to the beige tiled floor.] "But it ain't easy being Saul Akiva because I gots to deal with all the little poodles sniffing around and there's a whole lotta them in RSP. I gotta watch my back all the freakin' time and that's a damn problem for me. I work 18 hours a day in this business travellin' from one place to another. I've got a permanent case of jet lag and I'm banged up with injuries, I'm worn down an' I can do without havin' to worry about all the little sh[BEEP] in RSP getting' ahead of themselves and calling me out, I can do without some clusterf[BEEP] of a gauntlet match against 6 guys all lookin' to take me out so I'm gonna do what I have to do to stop it happening, I'm going to do what Saul Akiva need's to do to stay healthy and protect myself. So Cruz, bring your little whore to the ring if you want but make sure she knows that you'll be leaving that ring with a humiliating ass kicking an' a concussion to boot, same goes for any of the others that so much as look at me in a way I don't like. I make the fu[BEEP]in' rules now and you play by them or get hurt. Now, if you'll go hastle some other punk no doubt tellin' how much of a loser I am, I got to swallow down a cockatail of antibiotics, painkillers and whatever the hell else they put me on today. I'm Saul Akiva and that is all you need to know [Pats all 3 belts] because I've proved it, I Am Quite Simply The BEST."
