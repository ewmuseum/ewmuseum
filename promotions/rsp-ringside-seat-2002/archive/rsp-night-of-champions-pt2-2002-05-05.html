5 May 2002

[Camera returns and we see Fabian Cruz.] Fabian: I guess Linkin Park said it best in their song...in the end it doesn't even matter.... [Fabian sits down on a bench in his locker room as he finishes taping up his wrists and fingers. He looks 100% focused and ready to go as trickles of dew drip down his face and body. Cruz slaps his shoulders as he stands up and whines his arms around quickly limbering up] Fabian: It doesn't matter how much money Steve Greedy has...it doesn't matter who wins between Saul and Tommy. It doesn't matter how bad someone else wants this Western Heritage Title...because the belt is mine, these other bastards just don't know it yet. [Cruz starts to pace back and forth, his lips turning into a sneer as the anticipation builds.] Fabian: Also they don't know what's going through my head right now...they don't know what I am and am not willing to do to take that title back to Kendra Stone and the Stone Foundation...they've invested alot of time and money on me which I've made good on....now it's time for me to remove all doubt in my ability...not only from the board of directors but all the critics out there who have me listed as an underdog in this match...the fact is the only people who would even think about underestimating me would be those who are too blinded by jealous to recognize greatness. [Fabian slams his fist into one of the lockers, the rattling rings out as he turns and makes his way to locker room door his voice trailing off as he walks away] Fabian: Tonight...whoever's going to get in the ring with me tonight....I'm gonna go through like a Mother F[beep]n' Cruz Missle! [cuts back to the arena. We see Collen Blackwell and Dylan Thomas. They are joined by Damien Legacy.] CB: Damien what are you doing out here? You're match is up. The other are in the ring already. DL: You know, these people know, and that damn Sean Christopher knows I shouldn't be in this match. I should be in the Western States Herritage title match. I WAS SCREWED. DT: Yeah!! _________________________________ Motor City Madman-Big Chill T -vs- _ Johnny Sapdes-Damien Legacy _RSP _ _________________________________V AM: The following contest is scheduled for one fall and is for the RSP Television Championship. Introducing already in the ring the participants... BBBIGG CCCHILLL.. JJJJOHHHNNNNY SSSSPADES.... MOTOR CITYMADMAN...... DAMIEN LLLLLEGGGACY.... [Legacy remains at the broadcast table.] DL: I'm not wrestling!! Not going to do it!! The t.v. title is below me. [DING..DING..DING] CB: I think that's your cue! DL: Let those losers have at it. Have fun guys. [Damien Legacy waves to the guys in the ring. Big Chill looks down at Legacy as Madman and Spades attack him from behind.] CB: Chill wants a piece of you man. DL: I'm not hard to find. Hell, I'm sitting right here. DT: Collen, if the man doesn't want to fight why should he? DL: Excuse me for a minute. [Spades and Madman whip Chill into the ropes. As he bounces off he ducks a double clothesline. On the rebound Legacy reaches under the ropes and drags Chill to the outside. Legacy grabs Chill and rams his head into the mat. Legacy picks up Chill and hits the...] DT: LEGACY DRIVAAAAAH! CB: THIS IS RIDICULOUS!! [Damien spits on Chill and leaves him in a heap, calmly walking back over to the announcers, and sliding the headset back on as if nothing had happened] DL: Ladies [smirks] CB: What the hell was that? DL: WHAT? [flashes an innocent grin] I was just saying hello to my good buddy Chill. DT: That was quite a hello [smirks] [Inside the ring Madman and Spades turn on each other. Spades gives Madman a kick to the midsection followed by a DDT. Spades bounces off the ropes and hits an elbow drop.] DT: Spades and Madman going at it. Chill is starting to recover. DL: He is? Well I'll be right back!! [Damien once again leaves the broadcast booth. He grabs the ring steps and charges Big Chill with them. Chill kicks the steps into Legacys face. He then grabs Legacy and slams his leg against the ringpost. Chill positions the steps and dropkicks the steps into Legacys leg.] CB: Well I guess Legacy wont be joining us anymore tonight. DT: That was uncalled for. [In the ring Spades hits a spinbuster on Madman and goes for a cover... ONE TWO THREE..] CB: Three? No, Big Chill in at the last minute. DT: What? [Spades quickly to his feet and begins to tower over Big Chill. Chill offers a handshake.] CB: I think Chill is trying to work a deal with Spades. DT: That's smart thinking. CB: Our being a coward? [Madman gets to his feet. Chill pushes Spades out of the way and hits a clothesline on Madman. Chill pats Spades on the back and both men begin to stomp on Madman. Chill holds Madman as Spades lands some forearm shots.] DT: Classic double teaming. CB: Madman is in trouble. [Spades tries for a clothesline but Madman ducks and Spades hits Chill. Chill goes flying over the top rope. Madman picks up Spades and sends him over the ropes with a bodypress. Spades lands on top of Chill.] CB: This crowd is into Madman. DT: This crowd is full of idiots. [Madman goes to the outside and goes after Chill. Madman sends Chill into the steps and then grabs Spades. Madman picks Spades up with a suplex. On the way down Spades feet smack into Chill. Madman climbs to the apron and raises his hands to salute the crowd. The crowd repondes in kind. Legacy makes his way into the ring and knees Madman. Madman falls off the apron and falls chest first into the rail.] CB: Legacy back in this. At least he's not coming over here. DT: He makes a better commentator then me..uh..I mean you!! [Legacy to the outside rolls Chill and Spades back into the ring. Legacy grabs a camera cable and chokes Madman with it. Inside the ring Spades and Chill get to their feet and trade blows. Spades hits a knee to Chills stomach then whips him into the corner. Chill rebounds out and Spades attempts a beil toss. Chill reverses it, Spades reverses that. Chill stops his momentum and comes back with a clothesline that floors Spades.] CB: I guess that marriage is over. DT: Like most, they don't last long. [Chill grabs Spades by the leg and drops a elbow to the thigh. Chill hits another elbow to the knee then grapevines the leg. Chill begins to yank at the leg putting pressure on the knee.] CB: Chill keeping the larger Spades down. Working on the knee. DT: You can't walk if you don't have a leg. [Spades slams his fist on the mat. Spades reaches and grabs Chill by the chin. He begins to land several elbows to the face. As Spades gets up Legacy gets back into the ring and clips Spades. Legacy with the cover.... ONE TWO Chill makes the save. Chill and Legacy both get up and Chill hip tosses Legacy over the top rope. Madman gets back in the ring and hits a german suplex on Spades.] CB: The action is move after move in there. DT: It's like rush hour traffic. There's bound to be some type of accident. [TBC follows him to the floor. Chill picks up Legacy by the hair and then puts him in a bear hug. TBC falls backwards and drops him over the guardrail with a hotshot. TBC throws a few kicks at Legacy. then goes back to the ring. Legacy gasping for air crawls over the announce booth.] CB: Please no. DT: Be nice to our guest. [Legacy puts the headset on then gulps down and a cup of water then another..] CB: That's mine!! [Legacy spits the water out.] DL: You see what I mean? Those beasts have no class!! Chill just tried to kill me!! CB: Weren't you the one using the camera cable? DL: Shut up!! [Inside the ring Chill walks into a double clothesline by Spades and Madman. Spades and Madman begin to double team Chill. They double-irish whip him into the far ropes. Chill bound into the ropes and ducks a double elbow attempt. He bounds into the far ropes again and gets hit with a double foot to the face. Spades covers... ONE TWO.. Madman throws Spades off Chill and then goes for a cover himself. Spades make the save.] DL: It's like two woman fighting over a wedding dress. CB: They're not fighting over any wedding dress. They're fighting over gold. DL: Worthless piece of tin. CB: Bitter are we? DL: Damn straight!! [TBC gets thrown out and over the top rope by Madman and Spades. Spades offers a handshake to Madman but kicks him instead. Chill manages to hold on to the top rope to minimize the damage. Chill climbs to the top turnbuckle. Madman and Spades are exchanging fists, they turn to face the turnbuckle TBC is atop and TBC comes down with a double flying clothesline. Chill gets a cover on Spades. ONE TWO THR...] CB: Spades kicks out. DL: Lucky bastard. DT: Chill covers Madman.. ONE TWO THR.. CB: Madman kicks out. [Chill sets Madman against a corner. Spades gets up and picks Chill up in an electric chair position. Madman gets up and climbs to the top rope.] CB: Madman is a little to big to be up there!! DL: He's a dork! DT: Yeah..Dork! [Legacy gets up from the table and grabs the title. Legacy runs over and hits Madman in the back with the belt. Chill scratches at Spades eyes then drives him face first in a bulldog variation using his legs. Chill then quickly gets up and climbs the ropes. Chill grabs Madman and throws him off the rope with a belly-to-belly.] DT: Deep Freeze!!! CB: That's it!! ONE TWO THREE.. [DING..DING..DING] CB: Chill does it!! DT: Big Chill gets the win. AM: YOUR WINNER AND RRRRR SSSSS PPPPP TTELEVISSSION CHAMPION.. TTTTTTHHHHHHEEEEEEE BBBBBBIGGGGG CHILLLL. [Legacy still holding the title heads toward the back. Chill sees him and chaces after.] CB: Legacy has taken off with the the title!! DT: Chill is after him. CB: ..And we'll be right back!! [Camera Fades To Black]
