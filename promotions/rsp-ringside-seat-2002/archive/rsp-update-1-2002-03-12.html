RSP UPDATE March 10, 2002 [ http://www.hockey-autographs.net/~rsp ] -------------------------------------------------------------- OPENING COMMENTS ------------------------------------ Well 100% participation is definitely a great start, and with feuds developing nicely, I'd consider this a huge success for the first show. So without anything to compain about, I'll just congratulate everyone on a job well done. LATEST RSP NEWS --------------------------------- [~] Mark Hooks is the winner of RSPs first match ever. He gets a free ride straight to the Western States Herritage Title match. Although Hooks gets the free ride don't expect it to be an easy one...everyone has their eyes set on this man now! [~] Saul Akiva and Tommy Rowlands? Johnny Spades and Fabian Cruz? Man what a night!! [~] Even though most of the action took place in the ring. There was some stuff you didn't see. An example, Big Chill and Damien Legacy had a wild brawl in the parking lot! [~] Paul "Tacks" Barker has been named the replacement for Jimmy Taylor. The beer drinking, drug pushing, rebel of law was given a special license by the state athletic commission. Somehow this story might explode. [~] Motor City Madman wasn't to pleased with being the first man eliminated. However the happy go lucky badman from Detriot passed along a word to all his fans saying, "The title will be his when all is said and done!" ROUND ROBIN TOURNAMENT SET -------------------------------------------------------- RSP Round Robin Tournament for shot at Western States Heritage Title -Each man will have three matches, and the man with the best record in each group gets into the match with Mark Hooks for the WSHT. The second best records will square off for the Television Title. Both matches will take place April 7 in Yakima. Group A: Motor City Madman Fabian Cruz Johnny Spades Paul "Tacks" Barker Group B: Big Chill Damien Legacy Tommy Rowlands Saul Akiva UPCOMING CARD ------------------------------ Sunday March 17, 2002 Eagles Hall - Olympia, WA Roleplays to [detail withheld] by Friday Strats/Inrings to [detail withheld] by Friday Motor City Madman vs. Fabian Cruz Johnny Spades vs. Paul "Tacks" Barker The Big Chill vs. Tommy Rowlands Saul Akiva vs. "Top Dog" Damien Legacy Also: The winner of the Battle Royal, Mark Hooks, will be in the arena. What will Hooks have to say about his victory? What's next for the confident superstar? ---------------------------- Sunday March 24, 2002 Kitsap County Fairgrounds - Bremerton,WA Fabian Cruz vs. Paul "Tacks" Barker Johnny Spades vs. Motor City Madman The Big Chill vs. "Top Dog" Damien Legacy Tommy Rowlands vs. Saul Akiva ----------------------------- Sunday March 31, 2002 Cheney Stadium- Tacoma,WA Saul Akiva vs. The Big Chill Tommy Rowlands vs. "Top Dog" Damien Legacy Motor City Madman vs. Paul "Tacks" Barker Johnny Spades vs. Fabian Cruz ---------------------------------- Sunday April 7, 2002 Yakima Valley Sundome - Yakima,WA RSP Television Title Match -------------------------- Group A Runner-Up vs. Group B Runner-Up RSP Western States Heritage Title Match --------------------------------------- Group A Winner vs. Group B Winner vs. Mark Hooks ROSTER --------------- Coming - Paul "Tacks" Barker Going - Jimmy Taylor Current Roster -------------- Motor City Madman Johnny Spades Fabian Cruz Mark Hooks Saul Akiva Paul "Tacks" Barker The Big Chill "Top Dog" Damien Legacy Tommy Rowlands CLOSING THOUGHTS -------------------------------------- Once again, great job guys. The next three weeks are pretty much booked [give or take a match or two] so hopefully you'll come up with ideas on how to get your guys through this, some feuds are already taking shape so lets see how those play out. Best of luck to all of you and keep up the good work. If anyone wants to write matches [we don't require it but would appreciate it] just email Bryan or myself and let us know. The more people help out, the more matches we can book, as we don't want to burn ourselves out. That shoulde be all for the first week of RSP. Let's hope for the same participation as we did this week. Peace out all. RSP Staff
