[RSP] RSP Sunday TV Pre-show 3/10/02

12 March 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
(The scene opens showing an aerial view of the Hec Ed Pavilion in Seattle, as the RSP logo fades into view. A few brief moments later, the view switches to Dylan Thompson and Colin Blackwell sitting at a makeshift announce table (a simple foldout table with a couple of microphones and headsets on it...). Colin Blackwell: Welcome everyone to Ringside Seat Promotions' Sunday Night TV. I'm Colin Blackwell, and with me is our resident brat who can't hold down a job unless his father holds the fate of the company in his hands, Dylan Thompson. Dylan Thompson: Watch it oldtimer. I can easily have you replaced, and I CAN find a job anywhere I want, there's just nothing out there that fits my interests. Colin Blackwell: I can only imagine. But the show will start in about a half hour's time, so we managed to get some extra pieces for your viewing enjoyment. I believe we have James Wallace backstage right now... ---------------------------------------------- [Scene cuts to James Wallace standing backstage with a microphone] Wallace: I'm backstage at the arena here about to talk to one of the newest arrivals here in the RSP, the Motor City Madman. Let's try and get a few words with him about the recently announced battle royal for a shot at the Western States Heritage Title. [Wallace walks over to a locker room where the door displays the Madman's logo. Wallace knocks and pushes the door open to find Madman in the center of the room looking pissed as he's surrounded by 8 big boffo dolls. Wallace makes his way in slowly and unsurely.] Wallace: Uh, Madman? Can I get a few words with you? [Madman looks up and addresses Wallace in a hushed tone.] Madman: Come in, but stay out of the way. I'm strategizing. [Looks over the surrounding crowd of inflatables. Each figure has a name tag on it. Starting with Fabian Cruz and scrolling through the roster to Tommy Rowlands, each clown is labeled as a newly named member of the roster. Madman makes a wary look over the crowd, sets and moves in a blur, taking each and every clown down, sending them spiraling off to each and every direction. Madman collects himself, smiles triumphantly, and steps over to a confused Wallace.] Madman: See, Jimmy Olsen? Just that easy. I don't sweat these guys. I've seen better heads in a mannequin storeroom. Another federation comes calling, asking for the Madman's presence. So, like a good little trooper, I answer only to find that this set of circus peanuts hope to climb over each other and try to take the gold for themselves. Well, all I can say is, meet the man that's gonna swallow these peanuts and spit them out like a shot from a cannon. Wallace: That's some pretty bold words. Are you sure you can handle things so easily considering some of these guys you've never faced before? Madman: Like I said, man, no sweat! I know I'm capable of going to the top. I've climbed and climbed for years in other shows. But never has the ladder looked so short than it does here. So it's up to the top and then from the highest point I will shout "SPEAK TO ME --- MY MOTORHEADS!" Wallace: Well, from here, it looks like the Madman is confident and ready to go. [Madman resumes beating boffo dolls mercilessly behind James Wallace. Just as Wallace finishes his statement, a clown slams him from behind.] Madman (Off Camera): Sorry! I said stay out of the way! ---------------------------------------------- Colin Blackwell: (laughing) Looks like James is gonna be asking for some hazard pay soon if that keeps up. Dylan Thompson: (not getting the joke) Why? He got hit with a rubber clown. How is that a hazard? Are all of you old people just feeble or something? Colin Blackwell: (sighs) Oh nevermind. Just stick with the show, okay rookie? Dylan Thompson: I am not a rookie! I've been following this sport for many years, got it?!?!?! Colin Blackwell: And you've been commenetating for about ten minutes now. You are a rookie. But now we have been made aware of a feud that has spilled over from the now defunct American Wrestling Promotions. It is quite obvious that there is tensions between Saul Akiva and Tommy Rowlands, and one can only imagine how they will co-exist in the world of RSP. But we have some pre-recorded comments from both men. Let's cut to Tommy Rowlands... ------------------------------------------------- (As the scene fades in, we find ourselves in what appears to be a bear locker room. As the camera pans around, all we see is empty locker after empty locker. Eventually, the camera pans to one particular locker. A blood covered towel lies in a crumpled heap, the breaks of white glowing through the dark crimson blood. The locker next to it filled with what looks like wrestling equipment.) Voice: They say to every ending, a new beginning starts. They say we must move on, we must forget the past. Let old ties break, forgive those who do us wrong. They also say we must learn from our past, grow stronger. But mainly they say to every ending, a new beginning starts. Well, in most cases this is true. However, with all rules, there are exceptions. (The camera pans around the room further, until the man speaking is in shot. The man is sat on a doctors table, leaning forward. A doctor appears to be attending to the man, who as of yet is unknown. The man has long blonde hair, which has stuck to his blood covered face. Blood has streamed down his face, but from what, and why we do not know. The mans facial features are unclear for they have been covered with the crimson fluid. His hair too has fallen victim to the effects of the blood, leaving no more than a crimson mask of hair, with two eyes gleaming through the breaks.) Voice: In this case, the exception is Saul Akiva. You see, recently, a truly great promotion fell. Tonight, the last EVER AWP show was broadcasted. And what did we see? Let us revisit…… Richardson: What are your future plans? Akiva: Its funny you should ask that, I want respect, I deserve respect, I'm owed respect and if I have to choke it out of the whole of AWP I _damn_ well will. [Just then Tommy Rowlands walks onto the scene] Rowlands: Akiva, you proved nothing at the Battle Royale, you proved you ARNE'T good enough, give me one good reason why i should respect you. [Akiva thinks about it for a moment] Akiva: Well Rowlands, I can think of one very good reason... [Akiva knees Rowlands in the torso doubling him over] Akiva: THIS! [Rowland takes a second knee square on the nose, he reels back with a far away look in his eyes. Akiva grabs a steel chair before cracking it over Rowlands skull. Rowlands tries to rally himself following the shot but Akiva again cracks him over the skull. As Rowlands drops to the floor Akiva relentlessly begins to pound away on him. Rowlands face is left a crimson mask, a deep gash down one side.] Akiva: All I ask for is respect, is that to much AWP? IS THAT TO MUCH? TR: Yes, it is I, Tommy Rowlands. Akiva, I will never forget. One question. One simple question is all I asked, and you couldn’t give me an answer. Well, in an odd twist of fate, we meet again. A new city, yes. A new promotion, yes. But there is one thing that has not changed, nor will it. My hatred for you. My complete lack of respect for you. You do not deserve it. Never have, never will. (The doctor continues to tend to Rowlands, who has grown increasingly impatient. The fumbling doctors welcome wears out as Tommy stands and pushes him aside.) TR: (Voice raising and becoming increasingly intense) Akiva, all I asked is one question, one which you couldn’t answer. And what did it get? A chair shot? What sort of response is that. Do you really believe for one instant that you proved something to me? Anyone can hit me with a chair. Hell, this doctor could bust me open with a chair. In fact, get the hell over here…. (Tommy grabs the doctor, and puts a chair in his hand.) TR: DO IT!!! HIT ME YOU PUNK!!!! Doctor: No….. I won’t….. TR: I SAID HIT ME!!! I swear on all that is holy, if you don’t, even the lord will cry when he sees what I do to you….. Now HIT ME!!! (With this, the doctor winds up, and hits Tommy as hard as he can across the forehead with the chair. Tommy stumbles backwards, before regaining his composure. As he brushes the crimson bleached hair out of his face, it becomes clear Tommy has been busted again as an army of red blood cells make their way down his forehead.) TR: You have proved nothing Akiva. And this weekend, I will prove you are nothing. Your time here shall be short, for I will not cease until you are absent from this fed, the RSP. But now to move on. (Tommy sits back down on the bench, and takes a deep breath, calming himself.) TR: For those of you who do not know me, I am Tommy Rowlands. A former competitor in the AWP, I am now here in RSP to do no more than prove my worth. Gold, accolades and awards mean nothing to me. All I want to do is prove myself, to become all I can be, and most importantly, _EARN_ the respect of my peers. They say a man can be judged on his accomplishments in life. I do not believe in this. I believe a man can be judged on how he lived his life, on how he touched and influenced others. And I intend to do just this. I hope to touch those out there, inspire all the fans who have ever dreamed of becoming a wrestler, and move those who are wrestlers. TR: This coming weekend, there is a battle royal to determine who will be the number one contender for the Western States Heritage title. Well, I intend to do all that I can to win this honour. But if I don’t, well I will work my a** off to gain the privilege of facing the winner at the final of the round robin tournament coming up in Yakima on April 7th. But at the end of the day, when its all said and done and the last person leaves the arena, if I am not the owner of the belt, I won’t fret. I wont cry, or through a tantrum. I will simply work as hard as I can to get to the top, to work my way up, and become all I can. (Tommy slowly looks down, his voice growing low.) TR: But first, I will deal with Saul Akiva. I shall not forget him. This weekend, in the battle royal, I will teach you what respect is Saul….. I _WILL_ teach you the lesson you seem to be too obnoxious to comprehend. And in the battle royal, if you dare to stand in my way, I will show you a world of pain, I will open your eyes to the realities of this world. For you my friend are Nothing. (With this, Tommy signals the doctor over, as the scene turns to darkness) --------------------------------------------------- Dylan Thompson: This guy is a wrestler? (in a mocking voice) Oh please hit me with a chair! My brains are already scrambled!! Colin Blackwell: I think he was trying to make a point Dylan. It's quite apparent that no love has been lost in that corner. Dylan Thompson: And no common sense has been gained either. It's no wonder that he gets picked on wherever he wrestles. Now let's take a sneak peek at the guy who is probably gonna have me as his number one fan if he gets rid of Rowlands permanently. Colin Blackwell: You are one sick man, you know that? Dylan Thompson: One of my better traits I might add... ------------------------------------------------------- [Cut Backstage.] [Standing in front of the newly pressed, freshly ironed and recently hand-stitched by several hundred Filipino school girls is Saul Akiva. He wears a tight black muscle shirt and worn out blue jeans, frayed around the pockets and ankles.] "Ah yes, Tommy Rowlands. You remember me don't you Tommy. I'm the one who TATTOOED you across the face time after time after time in AWP and to tell you the truth. I enjoyed it." [Akiva flexes his powerful arms and runs a hand through his short black hair, a slight grey hint above his ears.] "In fact I enjoyed it so much that I signed for this [beep]fed when I saw your lowly name on the sheets. I ran you out of AWP and now I'm going to run you out of RSP simply because I can." [Saul takes a step forward and stares deep into the camera lens with a pair of deep green eyes, similar to those a crocodile would blink before snapping its killer blow.] "That's right Tommy, I don't want you in MY fed." [Akiva reels back and struts out his chest. Not by any means the figure of a dedicated gym-goer, but clearly worked at.] "So I guess that's the gauntlet being thrown down isn't it. So what are you going to do this time Tommy, run away again? Go home crying to your mother or stand and fight." [Akiva slams a fist back into the RSP logo.] "Fight Tommy." [A rye curl of the lips.] "I dare you." [And with that Akiva shoves the camera back with his powerful left hand and disappears from shot.] ------------------------------------------------------- Dylan Thompson: Dude, this guy is awesome! That's how you take charge of things. I almost pity Rowlands, or anyone else for that matter when Saul gets his hands on them. Colin Blackwell: Well it will be interesting to say the least when those two reunite tonight in the battle royal. But it appears we are about ready to start the match, so let's go to the ring and Anderson Michaels to announce the participants....
