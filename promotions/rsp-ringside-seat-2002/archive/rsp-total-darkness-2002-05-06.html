[Camera opens up to the closing minutes of the Western States Herritage title match from the last RSP Sunday TV broadcast.] CB: We have Greedy covering Akiva. VR: Greedy just blasted Akiva with that chair!! [Sinister goes down to make the count.. ONE!! TWO!! THR..] VR: Cruz in to make the save!! CB: That was close. Greedy thinks he should be champion. [Greedy up arguing with the ref.] VR: That might not be a good idea. It's not safe for anybody to get into Sinisters face! [As Greedy is yelling at Sinister. Cruz picks Akiva up and both men fight in the corner. Akiva picks up Cruz and sends him to the outside.] CB: Cruz despoisted to the floor by Akiva. Greedy turn around! [Greedy slaps Sinister whose eyes turn red. Greedy tries to slap Sinister again but Sinister blocks it and kicks Greedy in the stomach. Sinister picks up Greedy and hits a powerbomb.] CB: POWERBOMB!! POWERBOMB BY SINISTER!! [With Greedy down Akiva picks him up and hits a Death Valley Driver.] CB: DVD!! DVD!! [Fabian Cruz tries to get back into the ring but Damien Legacy runs down and blasts him with a chair. Sinister goes down and makes the count...] ONE~! TWO~! THREE~! [DING...DING...DING] VR: Akiva wins!! Akiva wins!! AM: THE WINNER OF THE MATCH AND NEW RINGSIDE SEAT PROMOTION WESTERN STATES HERRITAGE CHAMPION...SSSSAULLLL AAAAKIVVVA!! [As Sinister hands Akiva the belt the screen fades to black. It comes back up as "In the End" by Linkin Park begins to play and the opening video plays.] #One thing- I don’t know why# #It doesn’t even matter how hard you try# #Keep that in mind- I designed this rhyme# #To explain in due time# #All I know# [We see a shot of Tommy Rowlands and Saul Akiva battling on the streets.] #I kept everything inside# #and even though I tried #it all fell apart# #What it meant to me will eventually be a memory# #I triedso hard# #And got so far# #But in the end# #It doesn't even matter# [We see a shot of Fabian Cruz and Kendra Stone at a photo shoot.] #I had to fall# #To lose it all# #But in the end# #It doesn't even matter# [We see Damien Legacy in action. That is followed by a shot of Steve Greedy beating Mark Hooks.] #One thing-I don’t know why# #It doesn’t even matter how hard you try# [We see Eric Emerson unmasking and attacking Sean Christopher.] #I tried so hard# #In spite of the way you were mocking me# #Acting like I was part of your property# #Remembering all the times you fought with me# #I’m surprised it got so (far)# #Things aren’t the way they were before# [A shot of Big Chill pinning Johnny Spades is followed by a shot of Saul Akiva holding the Western States Herritage title.] #Not that you knew me back then# #But it all comes back to me# #In the end# [We finaly see the Total Darkness logo slam onto the screen... RSP PRESENTS T O T D_A_R_K_N_E_S_S L ...The camers then cuts into the empty RSP arena. As we see Collen Blackwell, Vince Richardson, and Tonya Sanders sitting on wooden stools in the ring.] CB: Welcome everyone!! This is RSP TOTAL DARKNESS~! I'am Collen Blackwell along with Vince Richardson and Tonya Sanders. VR: THE PRINCE IS BACK IN TOWN~! CB: Indeed, one of many surprises we saw at the last RSP Sunday TV show. VR: Well when I'm around expect nothing less then greatness! CB: I'm sure! TS: At the top of the show we saw the closing moments of the Western States Herritage title match as Saul Akiva claimed gold. CB: That was a wild and hard fought match. I'm sure Saul hasn't heard the last of Greedy or Fabian Cruz. VR: Talking with Cruz after the show he wasn't to happy. However, I think he'll get a shot down the line. CB: Well coming up next week on our new show RSP Breakdown Cruz and Greedy will face off in a number one contenders match. VR: Riight. In a warm up to that Cruz earlier this week took on a RSP trainee. Bilyo Baggins. Let us take a look =====================Total Fabian Cruz = -vs- = Bilyo Baggins = ==================Darkness Cruz upset over his loss in the Western States Herritage titls match went right to work on Bilyo. Cruz took apart Bilyo slowly and viciously. Cruz hit a Northen Lights suplex followed by a rolling german suplex. He continued working on the back of Baggins with a knee drop the center of the spine and a reverse surf board. Cruz almost got a tap out with an STF but released the hold wanting to add more pressure. Bilyo fought back for a little after Cruz missed with a second rope fist drop. Bilyo hit several clotheslines then an impressive missle dropkick. Bilyo tried to hit a moonsault but Cruz rolled out of the way. With Bilyo slowly getting to his feet Cruz hit the Cruz Missle spear and got the pin. Winner: Fabian Cruz Time: 5:06 [Camera fades to black then then we see the lineup for the next RSP card.] RSP presents BREAKDOWN Sunday April 5th live from RSP Arena Seattle, Washington Hosted by: Collen Blackwell, Vince "The Prince" Richardson In it's first show since the huge Night of Champions event RSP comes live from the brand new RSP Arena. There are several questions that need to be answered and scores to be settled. The action is sure to be hot. The main event will be a match signed by Sean Christopher. We will see "ELITE SENSATION" ERIC EMERSON with DERRICK DOUGLAS in his corner take on a special MYSTERY OPPONENT. The mystery man will be someone brought in by Sean Christopher. Also announced for this card will be a special number one contenders match. FABIAN CRUZ will face STEVE GREEDY one on one to determine the number one contender to the WSHT. TOMMY ROWLANDS will face "TOP DOG" DAMIEN LEGACY in a match that will decide BIG CHILLS opponent the following week in a television title match. Two newcomers will see battle as "THE FALLEN" JUDAS CHRISTIAN will face JOE "TWINK" JONES. Both men are said to be talented but which one will make it to the top of RSP first? ALSO We will have comments from our new champions SAUL AKIVA and BIG CHILL. [Camera fades back to the commentators at ringside.] CB: Cruz looks ready for his match with Greedy. VR: Well after the match last week I talked with Steve Greedy he said "FO" and handed me some money! TS: [Rolling eyes] I'm sure. VR: Well he is "The Rich One"! He likes to spread his wealth to his friends. CB: You don't have any freinds. VR: Then how did I get this job?!? CB: The last guy we hired was a joke! VR: Next match please? CB: Well speaking of Greedy he's up! =====================Total Steve Greedy = -vs- = "Tombraider" Croft = ==================Darkness Tombraider got an early advantage with his size and power. Croft tossed Greedy around for a little using high impact moves between suplexes and slams. Tombraider picked Croft up and slammed him to the floor. As Croft went after Greedy, Greedy took a soda from a fan at ringside and threw it into the eyes of Croft. Greedy then took a chair and smashed Croft over the head with it. Greedy got back into the ring and went to work on Crofts arm with an armbar. Greedy then draped the arm over the ring apron then jumped off the apron. Greedy slid back into the ring and hit several elbow drops on the arm. Greedy then picked Croft up and dragged him into a corner. Greedy went to work with fists of furry, landing several right hands. Greedy whipped croft into the corner as Croft came off the ropes Greedy hit a spinning neckbreaker. This was the end as Greedy hit a solid DDT and floated over for a cocky pin. Winner: "The Rich One" Steve Greedy Time: 4:23 [Camera cuts back to the announcers.] CB: Steve Greedy with the win! VR: Win?? That was an old fashioned squash! CB: Both Greedy and cruz look ready. Both men taking their game to the next level perhaps. Saul Akiva better not think of taking things easy. TS: Saul Akiva is a monster things have never come easy for him. He's a true champion but Cruz and Greedy can be as well. CB: Right now we are going to take you back once again to last Sunday night. It was supposed to be the Sean Christopher taking on one of the masked men..well lets take a look. [The masked man now stomping on Sean Christopher. Sean Christopher trying to get to his feet.] DT: Every time Sean tries to move this masked man beats him down. Doesn't Sean get the point? STAY DOWN!! CB: Sean is to tough a stubborn for that. DT: Stupid is more like it. [The masked man gets Sean up and brings him to a corner. The masked man whips Sean into the opposite corner and charges in. Sean moves out of the way and the masked man hits the corner chest first. Sean moves behind the masked man and clamps on a crossface chicken wing.] CB: The Chris Cross!! DT: Sean has the Christopher Crossface on the masked man!! CB: Whoever the masked man is, he's going out! [As Sean puts more pressure on the hold the masked man is about to go out. Derrick Douglas watching from ringside slides into the ring and attacks Sean from behind with a chair.] DT: Douglas is in now!! Derrick Douglas has saved whoever this guys is. [The masked man pulls his mask off..] CB: It's? DT: It's? CB: IT'S... ERIC EMMMERRRSON~! DT: OHMYGOD!! [Emerson begins to choke Christopher out with the mask. Douglas goes to the outside and gets a table.] CB: Douglas has a table? What are they going to do? [Emerson smacks Christopher with the chair. Emerson then goes to help Douglas set the table up. Emerson and Douglas set Sean ontop of the table as Douglas gets Sean in position.] DT: YEAH..DO IT..YEAH BABBY!! CB: SPIKE PILEDRIVER~! DT: Emerson and Douglas just drove Christopher through the table with a spike piledriver! [As Christopher is out on the mat Douglas and Emerson raise their hands standing over him. The camera cuts back to the announcers.] CB: You want to talk about impressive debuts. VR: Heh..how better then to take out your boss on the first day? CB: They indeed made an impact. TS: Emerson and Douglas both former world champions. Both greats in this sport. A good addition to the RSP roster. However, if they'll take out the boss what else will they do? CB and VR: ANYTHING TS: That's what I'm afriad of. CB: We were scheduled to have Eric Emerson in action. However he said "he's to good for this lowly show." VR: Indeed he is! CB: Well Emerson better be ready for next Sunday. He takes on a mystery opponent! VR: The dreaded mystery opponent! CB: We understand Sean Christopher has been talking with anybody and everybody to fill the spot. He wants to end Douglas and Emerson quickly. TS: Names like Brian Storm, names like Sinister, and names like Trey Lawrenze have all been mentioned. CB: Big names for sure. Who is it going to be? Only Sean Christopher knows! VR: Well lets get to our next match featuring newcomer Judas Christian. =====================Total Judas Christian = -vs- = "Suicidal" Jeff Edge = ==================Darkness The dark and mysterious Judas Christian made an impressive debut against the equally impressive Jeff Edge. This was a high flying back and forth match. With both men showing off their cruiserweight skills. Jeff Edge kept Judas Christian off balance with several hurricanrana variations and a Moonsault that got a two count. Christian took over with an Exploder Suplex which was followed by an cross arm straight jacket suplex. On the floor Christian hit a tornado ddt off the ring steps followed by a leg drop of the security rail. Christian sent Edge back into the ring and climbed to the top. Christian dived off and hit a corkscrew dive and got the pin. Winner: "The Fallen" Judas Christian Time: 3:03 [Camera cuts to the back of the empty arena where we see Tonya Sanders.] TS: Well coming up next week on RSP Breakdown will be a match where Damien Legacy takes on Tommy Rowlands. [We see Rowlands walk into camera range.] TS: Tommy, can I get a word with you about your match with Damian Legacy. Tommy: Yeah. All I have to say is this. Damian, get ready. You see, when we get in the ring tonight I am going to beat you like a dog. I dont really have much to say about it all, as I've said it all already. But tonight, Damian, I will end your legacy with the Unadulterated Truth. Sorry Tonya, but thats all I gotta say. [With this, Tommy starts to walk away. Out of nowhere, Legacy appears, sending Tommy to the floor with a clothesline. Tommy bounces back to his feetand as soon as he does Legacy drills his head into the wall. Legacy pulls Tommy back up, before pulling him along the corridor, and slamming him into the wall again.] CB: Well we got some action in the back!! Unscheduled action! VR: Leave it to Tonya to start a fight!! [Eventually, the two get to a table, and Legacy drills Rowlands head down into it. Tommy flicks up, and stubbles back before legacy grabs Tommy's head again, and tries to drill it into the wood again. Tommy manages to block it, and instead drills Legacys head down into the wood. As Legacy spasms up, Tommy meets him with a thunderous clothesline, sending Legacy to the concrete. As Legacy hits the floor, his head slams into the concrete with force. Legacy and Rowlands continue to fight as the camera fades to black.] CB: We'll see you next week..the debut of BREAKDOWN!! VR: Don't got now!
