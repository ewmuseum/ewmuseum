[RSP] RSP Breakdown [1/2]

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Black screen] V/O: Welcome to RSP BREAKDOWN~! ["Biggest & The Best" by Clawfinger begins to play.] #WHAT'S WRONG WITH BEING SELF POSSESSED? #NOBODY'S SATISFIED WITH BEING SECOND BEST #I'VE GOT THE GIFT AND I KNOW THAT I'M BLESSED #AND I'VE GOT TO GET IT OFF MY CHEST [The opening video plays.] #I'M THE BIGGEST THE BEST BETTER THAN THE REST...BETTER THAN THE REST #I'M THE BIGGEST THE BEST BETTER THAN THE REST...BETTER THAN THE REST #I'M THE BIGGEST THE BEST BETTER THAN THE REST...BETTER THAN THE REST #I'M THE BIGGEST THE BEST BETTER THAN THE REST...BETTER THAN THE REST [We see Saul Akiva pinning Steve Greedy for the Western States Herritage Title.] #I'm not afraid of competition 'cos I know that I'm the best and you haven't even got a chance to pass my little test #Even if you wanted to you couldn't pay the price #there ain't no room in my perfect paradise #'cos life is always based upon the weight of your success #So I'll make sure that I get the most and nothing less #If I love myself then everyone will love me to and there's nothing you can do for me that I can't do [We see Big Chill winning the Television title.] #WHAT'S WRONG WITH BEING SELF POSSESSED? #NOBODY'S SATISFIED WITH BEING SECOND BEST #I'VE GOT THE GIFT AND I KNOW THAT I'M BLESSED #AND I'VE GOT TO GET IT OFF MY CHEST #I'M THE BIGGEST THE BEST BETTER THAN THE REST...BETTER THAN THE REST #I'M THE BIGGEST THE BEST BETTER THAN THE REST...BETTER THAN THE REST #I'M THE BIGGEST THE BEST BETTER THAN THE REST...BETTER THAN THE REST #I'M THE BIGGEST THE BEST BETTER THAN THE REST...BETTER THAN THE REST [Finaly the camera fades out and into the arena. Crowd: RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! RSP~! ] Voice: RSP is once again on-air!! [The camera cuts to show Colin Blackwell and Vince Richardson standing by the ring. Colin has on a black sports coat over a white shirt and black slacks. Richardson is dressed in a blue polo shirt with black jeans.] VR: The world has gone on long enough without seeing my pretty face. CB: Hello everybody and welcome to the long awaited debut of Breakdown!! I'm Collen Blackwell along with Vince Richardson. Tonight is going to be an exciting night!! VR: We will see the match many people have been waiting to see as Sinister takes on Eric Emerson. Also we see Tommy Rowlands, Damion Legacy, Fabian Cruz, and Steve Greedy all in action. CB: Plus we will here from... [The arena lights slowly fade out; the crowd are plunged into an expectant silence. With all the chaos that RSP has seen in the past couple of weeks anything could be set to go down.] CB: This man. Coming to the ring it's RSPs Western States Herritage Champion Saul Akiva. [A single light flickers on and off over the entrance way as a long, whining guitar howls out. The crowd let up a slight cheer but all to soon they delve into a chorus of heat as a familiar repeating riff builds up.] [The music builds up to a chaotic crescendo as the layers of complex guitars hang from a simple base riff.] #Saaaaaaaay goodbye to you, But it always has taking it back to you in a hole, So what ya gonna do now? But ya got till sun down, Don't look too good, don't look too bright, Got it all, but wanna fight, So how you gonna feel now? But you got till sun down, Step in the car it's time to go,# [The lights kick in, a beam of strobe effects swings from one side of the ring to another as the music becomes deafening, slowly but surely the entrance way becomes illuminated and, standing at its peak, is the first RSP Western State Heritage Champion.] #You want it bad, You fell off track, A Thousand knives left in my back, Protecting me, Such an irony, And I told you once I'd never leave, And all I do, and all I see I'm true# [Struck across his powerful shoulder is the belt itself, freshly cleaned, freshly made. For the first time it has a rightful owner. Of course, that owner is Saul Akiva.] #So tell me why it all revolves around you! And this time I know what I must do! Now I know it's time for us to fade, Sail like sorrow, want it all today, now...# [Slowly Saul raises his heavily tattooed left arm to the crowd and with his head dipped he begins to walk to the ringside. As he steps forward into the light we get to see him in full. Short shaven greying black hair, square unshaven jaw-line and a deep Eastern European tan.] [He stops briefly by the ringsteps and unhooks the gleaming gold belt from his shoulder to reveal the moniker on his black T-shirt - On the front, 'Quite Simply...' and on the back, '...Better'. He parades around the ringside scowling at anyone who meets his gaze before rolling up into the ring microphone in hand.] [As the ref tries to take the belt off of him Akiva pushes him to the ground, the ref scuttles to the outside as Saul strides towards him. Slowly he walks around the ropes as the crowd shows just what hey feel about Da Champ.] "Will you people just SHOW SOME DAMN RESPECT!" [Akiva growls, not that that will stop the RSP faithful.] "You don't get to BOO me anymore because as of Sunday April 14th I became the best, the biggest and the baddest sonofabitch RSP has to offer. See this [tapping the title with the mic], this means that I am THE best, it means I'm better than every single one of you mindless, snot nosed little shi*ts out there who worked all week at the meat factory to get tickets to this flea-market show." [Not exactly in the frame of mine to endear himself to the fans, Saul continues.] "I told you all that this was my time but did you believe me? No, you said I was a loser. [A brief LO-SER chant breaks out.] I told you all that Saul Akiva was the rising star of the business but did you believe me? No, you all thought I was a joke. [Strike that, we now have a fully fledged YOUR AN ASSHOLE chant up and running.] I told you that this belt was going to be mine, did you believe that? No, you just called me an idiot...[And an ASSHOLE.] Well, who're all the idiots now? Because like it or not I am the RSP Western States Heritage Champion because I am that damn good, I proved it to you all by going through two matches in a night to lift this title and I did it because quite simply... I AM BETTER!" [Saul stops for a moment and looks around the crowd with a rye smile on his face, he waits long enough to just about piss off everyone.] "I've gone through hell to get this title and at times I wondered whether or not it would ever be mine. When Tommy Rowlands [slight pop] put me through a shop window I wasn't sure if I could get back up. When The Big Chill damn near broke my arm I wasn't sure if I could do it. When I was told I'd have to go through sudden death to even get the damn title shot I'd worked so hard for I wasn't sure if I could make it. When Sinister [BIG cheer] came out as the special guest referee I wondered if I could do it. When I was covered, shoulders to the mat I wasn't sure if I had it in me to get up and keep on fighting... BUT I DID! I wasn't going to let this slip away from me, not ever. And now, well, this title is around my waist to stay and it doesn't matter that RSP has got a couple of so called legends like Eric Emerson and Sinister on contract now because at the moment, the only legend that matters is mine. You see, whatever happens Saul Akiva is number one, I was the first champion and I'm going down in the history books as the man who was there from day one. I'm creating a legacy just by being here and it doesn't matter what's gone down in the past or what's going to be set up in the future because like it or not I am part of RSP right now so you just better swallow that down." [We've now got EMERSON, ROWLANDS, SINISTER et al chants bursting up from the fans, each having a preference for ANYONE to shut him up.] "Call for them all you want because they 'aint coming out here, not after I worked my way through not ONE but TWO matches to lift this belt. You see, all the boys in the back know that I am the better man, that I could quite simply just BREAK them and that's why, right now I'm standing out here with a nice, shiny Gold belt and they're stuck in a communal locker room watching ME. The only people who don't seem to get it are YOU. " [As the noise grows again, Akiva begins to pace up and down the ring. He raises a hand sarcastically telling the fans to shut it, finally they quieten.] "So how does it feel to all you guys in the back to know that I'm the top of the food chain. I tell you, from here it feels pretty fu*ckin' great. Now..." [Before Akiva could finish his last sentence Steve Greedy hits the ring..and Akiva!!] CB: GREEDY..GREEDY..GREEDY!! VR: Greedy just took a chair to the back of Akivas head. [Greedy beging to kick and stomp on Akiva. Greedy takes the title and holds it in the air. Suddenly Fabian Cruz hits the ring.] CB: FABIAN CRUZ!! VR: These two are going to face each other later tonight!! CB: The winner is the number one contender. [Cruz and Greedy stare each other down for a minute. Sean Christopher slowly walks to the ring. Greedy and Cruz look at each other then Christopher. Christopher nods and Cruz and Greedy begin to stomp on Akiva.] CB: WHAT THE HELL? VR: I don't understand that. CB: Cruz and Greedy working together? What was Christopher doing out here? [Cruz picks up Akiva as Greedy slams the title into his face.] CB: This is a bad situation. VR: A strange situation. [refs finaly hit the ring and get Greedy and Cruz to leave. Akiva is slow to get up he whipes his bloody head on the title.] CB: Right now lets go to Tonya Sanders. [Tonya is standing in the back.] TS: Thanks Collen I'm going to try to get... [Just then we see Sean Christopher walk by.] TS: Sean..Sean? [Sean walks into a locker room that reads "Steve Greedy"] TS: Well I was going to try to get some words with Sean Christopher. I'll work on that back to you guys. [Camera cuts back to Vince and Collen.] CB: We are about ready for the first match but lets talk about what just happend? VR: I don't know what to say? Greedy and Cruz who are supposed to face each other one on one tonight just attacked Saul Akiva. Then Christopher comes out nods and leaves. CB: What about Christopher walking into Greedy's locker? VR: Well that's simple. Greedy always has a party going back there wine and woman! Sean just wanted to party. CB: I'm not sure about that. Alot of questions to be answered right now it's time for the first match. BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBB BBBBBBBBBBBB B R E A K D O W N Winner gets a shot at Television Title Damion Legacy -vs- Tommy Rowlands BBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBBB BBBBBBBBBBBB [The opening power chords to Tool's 'Intolerance' blare loudly over the arena speakers as the crowd lets out a chorus of booes, for they know what's coming next. A few fans of the Northwest wrestling scene pop for the hometown boy. Some ladies in the audience pop as well] * BOOOOOOOOOOOOM! * * BOOOOOOOOOOOOM! * [Smoke pours out from in front of the entrance portal, as "Top Dog" Damien Legacy strides into view, looking out over the sea of peons] # Our guilt, our blame #I've been far too sympathetic # Our blood, our fault # I've been far too sympathetic [Legacy walks up the stairs, a cocky grin on his face, crazed look in his eyes.] # I am not innocent # You are not innocent # NO_ONE_IS_INNOCENT!!! [Legacy removes his t-shirt to another pop from the female contingent and throws it into the crowd. He then saunters confidently to the corner, smirking at the ring announcer as he rips the mic from his hand] "I almost feel sorry for you pathetic people out here. I'm sure you all really wanted to see me kick the crap outta Rowlands for the second time" [The crowd is giving him the business. He just smirks and waves them off] "And I'm sure you'd all love to see me get screwed again by another incompetent referee" [The boos quickly turn to cheers] "Alas boys and bitches, this ain't a [beeping] fairy tale. Tommy's not riding out here on his white horse in a suit of armor to slay this dragon. Your hero's going out in a bag tonight children, and no amount of your empty cheers is gonna make a damn bit of difference. I've got a date with Chill and the road runs right through Rowlands country." [He looks towards the entryway] "Lets go Tommy, I've been waiting for this" "THUD" [He drops the mic awaiting Tommy Rowlands.] CB: Legacy fills screwed that's for sure. VR: Well he may have a point. It's just time for him to stop bitchin about it and do something to correct the situation. Taking out... CB: HOLD ON!! [From the crowd, behind Legacy, Rowlands enters the ring and begins to attack Legacy. Rowlands using his power hits several hard clotheslines and then picks Legacy up and slams him to the arena floor.] VR: Well this isn't a good start for Legacy. CB: No..No it's not! [Rowlands goes to the floor and sends Legacy into timekeepers table head first. He then grabs the timekeepers hammer and smacks Legacy with it. Legacy slumps to the mat and tries to call for a time out.] CB: Rowlands having something to prove tonight. He wants a title!! He wants a title bad!! VR: He had the Western States Herritage title slip from his grasp. He needs this win to get a shot at Big Chill and the T.V title. [Rowlands moves in after Legacy who grabs him by the tights and sends him into the ringpost. Legacy gets up and knees Rowlands in the back once again sending him into the ringpost. Legacy grabs Rowlands by the back of the head and.. !!!CRUNCH!!! !!!CRUNCH!!! !!!CRUNCH!!! !!!CRUNCH!!! !!!CRUNCH!!!] CB: Legacy just rammed Rowlands into the ringpost repeatedly. VR: Legacy, just that quickly turns the match into his favor. [With Rowlands down Legacy grabs a chair and positions Rowlands leg between the steps and the ringpost. Legacy slams the chair into the steps !!!!CCCCLANG!! ] CB: Legacy just crushed Rowlands leg. VR: That was sadistic. [Legacy rolls Rowlands into the ring and begins to work on the leg. Legacy drops several elbows on the leg then applies a figuare four.] VR: Rowlands is done for! CB: Legacy like you said needed to do something and he's doing it. VR: He is. The committee in the back is glad Legacy is taking out the frustration on Rowlands instead of them. [Legacy positions Rowlands leg and sets to leap off the second rope with a elbow drop. Rowlands moves out of the way and Legacy hits the mat hard. Rowlands trying to get a breath of air slowly gets to his feet. Legacy gets up and both men charge in at each other. Legacy takes a wild swing but Rowlands blocks it and hits a DDT on the arm. Rowlands puts on a arm bar yanking on the elbow and shoulder.] CB: Rowlands now working on the arm. VR: Both men want this. The tide of the match turns left and right. [Rowlands drops a couple of knees into Legacy's arm then picks him up and slams him on the arm. Rowlands picks Legacy up again but Legacy slides over Rowlands shoulder. Legacy grabs Rowlands by the back of the head and slams Rowlands to the mat with a inverted facebuster.] CB: Nice counter there by Legacy. [Legacy climbs to the top rope and hits a frog splash. Legacy goes for the cover.. ONE TWO THRRR...] CB: Rowlands kicks out. VR: That was close. Can't get any closer. [Legacy waites for Rowlands to get up. As Rowlands staggers to his feet Legacy bounces off the ropes and hits a flying forearm. Legacy then climbs to the top of the opposite corner and leaps off hitting a tumbleweed legdrop. Legacy goes for another pin.. ONE TWO THR...] CB: No Rowlands kicks out again. VR: COME ON!! I mean hell the ref needs to lose some weight so he can count faster. [Legacy goes back to work on Rowlands leg kicking and stomping on it. Legacy applies a standing reverse figure four as Rowlands yells in pain.] VR: Rowlands is going to tap! CB: I don't think so. VR: I'm sure he is! [Rowlands kicks Legacy in the back sending Legacy shoulder first into the ringpost. The momentum sends Legacy to the floor. As Legacy gets to his feet Fabian Cruz runs to the ring and... !!!!SMACK!!!] VR: CRUZ WITH A CHAIR. CB: CRUZ WITH A CHAIR. [Fabian rolls Legacy back into the ring. Rowlands crawls over and puts a arm on Legacy.. ONE TWO THHREEEE.] CB: Three? YES!! YES!! YES!! [DING...DING..DING] VR: No..No. [The ref calls off the bell. The goes over to the corner and says it's only a two count. He points to Legacy's foot that is on the bottom rope.] CB: The ref is saying Legacy got his foot on the bottom rope. VR: It looked close but Legacy's foot did beat the refs hand. CB: THE MATCH CONTINUES!! [Rowlands gets up and sets for his finisher. As Rowlands sets for the Unadulterated truth. The ref is distracted by Kendra Stone. This allows Cruz to get on the apron and hit Rowlands with the chair. With Rowlands stunned Legacy is able to slip out of the move and hit the Legacy Driver. Legacy goes for a pin as Kendra and Fabian run to the back... ONE TWO THREE~!] CB: Three? VR: Yes. This time it's a three!! [DING..DING...DING] CB: The bell sounds but we heard it before. VR: This time it's for real. CB: Yes..the ref signals his decision to the ref!! VR: HE DID IT!! HE DID IT!! AM: YOUR WINNER OF THE MATCH...."TOP DOG" DAMION LEGACY!!! [Both Legacy and Rowlands are down on the mat. The ref holds Legacy arm but when he lets it go it slumps back to the mat.] CB: Both men are spent. It was back and forth. VR: It could of gone either way but Damn that Cruz!! CB: Fabian Cruz getting involved in the end but why? VR: Good question..Very good question!! CB: Well Legacy gets a t.v. title match next week. I'm sure Rowlands will have to say something about this. VR: ..and more questions surrounding Fabian Cruz arise. CB: Those questions will be answered when we get back. Right now we have this commercial break. [Camera fades to black.]
