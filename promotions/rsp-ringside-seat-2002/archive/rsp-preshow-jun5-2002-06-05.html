[RSP] The Pre-Show

5 June 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Cameras pan open to the backstage area of the RSP arena. We see Tonya Sanders standing by.] TS: Hello everybody and welcome to the Pre-Show! I'm Tonya Sanders and this is the 30 minute countdown to Breakdown. Tonight we will have comments from Tommy Rowlands as well as Big Chill. We will also see a special video from RSP Tag Team newcomers The Dime Notes and in action will be Judas Christian taking on Joe "Twink" Jones. [Tonya is joined by Tommy Rowlands.] TS: Tommy, Thanks for joining us. How are you tonight? You ready for your number one contender match coming up with Damion Legacy? You seem to be in good spirits why's that? Tommy: I just saw the re broadcast of our PPV. And I suddenly realized something. I was getting really worked up over nothing. I mean, look at the events. Saul Akiva. Champ. The funny thing about it is, he doesn't deserve any of the credit! I mean, I had him throughout our match. I made him squeal like the woman he is. And then, what happens? I get screwed over by Cruz and Greedy. Then the TV title. It looked like a bunch of old women fighting over who makes the best corn bread. And well, as for the world title, if it wasn't for Greedy being a jackass and getting in Sinisters face, we all know Akiva would have been pinned. But in the end, it was Sinister who won the match for Akiva.. So in an odd way, I guess he could lay claim to it. TR: It just makes me chuckle, that's all. Akiva, the man who "is better" has the title on behalf of 3 other people. Nice one. Well, all I can say is I'd rather him than me. His reign will be no more than a sham. But when I win the TV title, at least I know it will be of my own credit. Tonya: When? TR: Yeah. I mean, look who I have to fight. Legacy, a man who couldn't do anything in his match other than hide with the commentators, only getting in cheap shots, and The Big Chill, a man who won via the intervention of this little cry baby. Well its pretty simple. I beat Legacy before, and I'll damn sure do it again. He is nothing. A no one. He likes to build himself up, but really theres no point. He's no more than a w***e. I mean, he may as well be standing on the streets trying to pick up some business. But hell, when I'm through with him, that's all he will be good for. TR: You all saw what I did to Saul. I had him crying like a little girl. And apparently, he is the best in the game a the moment, he is our "worthy champion" (Tommy lets out a little chuckle). And well, if I can beat him like a dog, why the hell would Legacy, or Chill for that matter have any role to play in this? As far as I'm concerned, I am already the Television Champion. I mean, everyone knows that I was the runner up of my league, therefore I should have been in the match in the first place. As such, I would have won the belt on the night. But alas I wasn't included, so I will just have to wait my time. You could say that makes the TV champ a fraud as well. Ha. How amusing. Such worthy champions, they really did well!! Tonya: Well, you do have an intriguing point. What do you have to say about the actions backstage last week? TR: Not a lot really. Legacy attacked me, and I, to quote many wrestlers before me "Punked him out". He didn't have a chance against me. I was, and still am in no mood for anyone's crap. Its this simple. The way I feel at the moment, you stand in my way and I WILL hurt you. I don't care if you're Saul Akiva, Sinister, Eric Emmerson or Legacy [Tommy starts to laugh.] Tonya: What's so funny? TR: Sorry, just mentioning Legacy in the same breath as Sinister or Emmerson. Like he even comes close! But back to my point. It doesn't matter who you are. You get in my way, and I will put an end to it. Unfortunately, that's exactly what Legacy did last week. For the second time, he tried to jump me. This time, he wasn't so lucky. I took him out, and I will do it again. TR: And moving on. Cruz, Greedy. Please, don't think I have forgotten you. I swear, once I finish with Legacy and Chill, and win what is rightfully mine, I will get a small taste of revenge. You see, it may be in the ring for my belt. Then again, it could be in one of your most important matches. I don't care either way. I will screw you over, just a you screwed me. And guess what? I'll enjoy it. Legacy, I'll see you in the ring. Always a pleasure Tonya. [With this Tommy leaves. Tonya seems to stare at him as he walks away. She then notices the camera still on her.] TS: Umm..yeah. Roll the footage of the match between Jones and Christian. <>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<> Joe "Twink" Jones -vs- Judas Christian <>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<>RSP<> Jones and Christian had a competitive match both men trading hold for hold. Christian took the advantage when Jones went for a dropkick but Christian moved and Jones croctched himself between the ropes. Christian worked over the back and neck of Jones locking on a dragon sleeper for a few minutes. Christian then hit several suplexes followed by a brainbuster that got a two count. Jones fought back using speed and smaller size but that didn't last long as Christian hit a spin kick that almost took Jones head off. Christian leaped off the second rope hitting a elbow to the throat. Christian tried a leap off the top rope but missed. This was enough for Jones to get a surprise inside cradle and pin. TIME: 6:03 WINNER: Joe "Twink" Jones [Dissolve into a sparsely decorated apartment that looks strikingly like the old apartment set from Three's Company. Central living room with a love seat, entertainment center, and coffee table prominently placed. Writing table with phone off to the right. Planters with ferns near the door and window. A couple bedrooms and bathroom off to the left. Kitchen through the lone door to the right. The door to the apartment opens and three men enter. In the lead is a short, stupid-looking, slightly craggy old man, obviously the landlord by his futile attempt to appear important. He gives the impression he was ineffectual even in his prime 40, 50 years ago. With him are two prospective young renters. A real Mutt and Jeff combo. The smaller, sort of large cruiserwight sized young man wears a blue and brown herringbone t-shirt, jeans and penny loafers. A really old two-tone brown hardcover of Perry Mason and The Case of The Runaway Corpse is tucked under his arm. The larger man is more what Jim Ross would call a big, rangy ole Southern boy. He wears a Carbon Leaf: Echo Echo album t-shirt, jeans matching his partner's, and light brown leather boots. His hair is pushed back in a sort of brunette mullet and is built like an Acolyte or considering the mullet a young Barry Windham. If you've been following indie wrestling in the Pacific Northwest lately, you know these men respectively as Darby Sharpe and Justin Simmons, the OSW joint tag team champions, The Dime Notes. If you haven't, well it's your turn to meet them.] Landlord: Okay now, guys how about this one? Central living room. Two bedrooms so you can each have those wild nights with the ladies. [The landlord nudges the smaller Darby playfully in the arm. And then likewise with Justin. As they both look to the ceiling wondering why he's trying to live vicariously through them.] Justin: You know Pard, I like the way this guy thinks. Darby: Well, that makes one of us, Jackson. Justin: Come on, Darb. He's trying to be cool. Darby: I don't mean to be evil about it. But this flat has been gassing us like he's some big time operator. I don't know about you, Juss. But I'm here to rent a roost. Not to dig up the latest gravel. Justin: You'll have to forgive my partner, Mr. Fell-Knotts. He's still pining for his fly chick back on the East Coast: Fell-Knotts: I hear you, Son. Where was I? Oh yes, Two bedrooms. Bath. Kitchen to the right. All the furnishings you see here. For $400, it should make a real swinging bachelor pad if I may say so without upsetting your friend the Beatle. Justin: See, Pard, I keep saying a haircut. Darby: Oh, stick it up the north end of a south bound mule. Fell-Knotts: He always this hostile? Justin: Nah, one of role models died recently and that chick I mentioned. She was his first love. Darby: I am NOT still pining for Lydia Kilgore! Justin: Suuuure, Pard. That's why I'm the only one on this team that's been banging the rats since we got out on this coast. [Suddenly, old Mr Fell-Knotts's face lights up suddenly placing his would-be tenants.] Fell-Knotts: Wait, Boys. Haven't you lads been wrestling down in Old School Wrestling lately? Justin: Yes, sir. That we have. Fell-Knotts: I thought so. I knew I'd seen you two somewhere. Aren't you the Dime Notes? The joint OSW tag team champions? Justin: Yes, sir. That we are. Fell-Knotts: So what brings you lads up this way? Trying to get a breather from those Sexual Energy poofs? [Both Darby and Justin laugh at the description of the arch-nemeses in Oregon.] Darby: Actually, Pops, we've just signed on with RSP. Looking to expand our horizons if you will. And thinking maybe we can rent a roost from you that an equal commute to both. Fell-Knotts: Great. Maybe I can make it into Seattle and watch you lads sometimes. I always enjoy watching you on the satellite shows of OSW. Justin: Thank you very much, Mr Fell-Knotts. Despite the Sexual Energy problem we enjoy working there, we very much enjoy wrestling in OSW. And we hope wrestling for RSP can be just as much fun. And as for the apartment, unless my partner has any objections, I think we'll take it. Darby: No objections here, Jackson. I see us ruling this roost for many moons to come. [Dissolve out as Fell-Knotts leads them out of the apartment to fill out the paperwork. We cut back to Tonya Sanders.] TS: It looks like the Dime Notes have found a place in Washington and are set to turn up the heat in the newly formed RSP tag division. Right now lets go back to the ring and hear words from our Television Champion..The Big Chill. after that we will head into Breakdown!! [The crowd begins to boo and jeer as "Calm like a Bomb" by Rage Against the machine begins to play. The Big Chill walks out from the backstage entrance and stands still. TBC is wearing a New York Yankees away jersey (#23), blue jeans, black motorcycle boots, a "RoT" doo-rag and black Oakley sunglasses. TBC has his head shaved with about a day's worth of growth on it and a goatee. The RSP TV title belt is on his shoulder. A smug looks comes across his face. He begins to walk, strut more like it, down to ringside. As he walks down the aisle, he points at the crowd on either side of him, yelling "I told you, I told you, I told you." He gets to ringside and points at Collen Blackwell and tells him "to show the proper respect." He then high-fives Dylan Thompson. TBC climbs into the ring. He grabs the mic from ring announcer and heads to the middle of the ring. The music fades as he stands there with a smirk on his face. The crowd is booing.] TBC: Now, is that anyway to welcome the best wrestler on TV to this dunghole of a town? [TBC pauses as the crowd boos and jeers.] TBC: Now I know why all your great athletes leave this place? You don't appreciate greatness! You didn't appreciate Ken Griffey Jr., you didn't appreciate Alex Rodriguez and you certainly don't appreciate the T, the B and the C! You go nuts over a team that wins big in the regular season and then chokes when the money's one the line! The Mariners won 116 games last year and all it took was the Yanks coming into town and that was all it took. Don't even let me start in on the Sonics. And Jesus, do you guys even have a football team anymore? With all this underachievement all around, it kind of explains your fascination with "Underdog" Damien Legacy... [The crowd pops at the mention of his name. TBC looks disgusted.] TBC: Sure, sure. Go nuts. Cheer the hell out of him, but remember one thing. This title... [TBC slaps the title belt that is sitting on his shoulder.] TBC: ...is mine. Now, before I continue, I want to get something off my chest. Something that's been bothering me since the night of my victory. [TBC pauses.] TBC: Sinister... [The crowd pops wildly as TBC mentions the fan favorite's name. TBC pauses, looking annoyed.] TBC:...Sinister, are we back to square one again? Are you playing up to the crowd one more time? I thought we cleared all that up a few months ago. I would have thought that with all the history we have, I would have gotten a phone call at least, AT LEAST, to let me know you're in town. No, no phone call. No e-mail. Nothing. Not only did I not know you were going to be in town, but you pull this kind of stunt and help that chump Saul Akiva win the title? [Crowd pops wildly again at the mention of the new Western States champ.] TBC: Well, maybe me and you ain't that tight no more. Maybe that punk of a brother has gotten back in your ear. I don't know. But let me tell you what I do now. I do know that while you're on that side of the fence, you ain't as dangerous as you could be. That's bad. For you. So, here's what I'm thinking. I'm thinking you just showed me how its gonna be. I appreciate that. If you want to walk around and sign autographs and shake hands, that's your business. Mine is hurting people and winning titles. As long as our paths don't cross, everything's going to be fine. They do cross, well... [TBC smirks.] TBC: ...well, you remember the last time that happened. But, I digress. Back to the punk rookie. [TBC walks toward the near ropes.] TBC: I told that chump that he wasn't getting over me again and I went out there and proved it. The simple truth of the matter was that when it all mattered, I got the victory and that's gotta tear you up! You were so happy about one win, you didn't pay attention to the bigger picture. One little battle means nothing if you can't win the war. You want to build a legacy, "Underdog"? Not on my dime, pal. I didn't come here for a wet-behind-the-ears rookie like you to make a name for himself at my expense. Uh uh. Not in this lifetime, chump. But just like every other rookie punk, you wouldn't listen. You should have paid attention to me boy! That's why they call me the Truth back home! I always tell the truth, even when I lie! Don't think so? Well, you tell me, where's Motor City Madman? [TBC pauses momentarily and, for a moment, a curious look comes across his face.] TBC: He's watching the Tigers lose back in Detriot! I sent him packing! Tell me, where's Johnny Spades? [TBC pauses momentarily again and, once again, a curious look comes across his face.] TBC: He's working the door at the Luxor in Las Vegas again! I sent him packing too! And where are you, Legacy? [TBC pauses momentarily once again. This time, The Big Chill smiles.] TBC: Back...at...square...one. It happened just like I said it would happen. You want to pretend that this title is worthless or beneath you, hey you know what? Whatever gets your through the night, punk! And now, the man that everyone knew to be the best wrestler on television...is the best wrestler on television. And there ain't a single thing you can do about it. [TBC walks back to the middle of the ring.] TBC: But not to worry, not to worry. I intend to be a fighting champion.Yes, I will wrestle anyone at anytime. (Smirks) As long as they deserve it. And to be honest, I might be waiting awhile because none of the chumps here deserve anything! The only thing these chumps deserve is a good beating and a kick in the teeth! Take a good look people! Start appreciating greatness! [The crowd boos the overbearing heel.] TBC: This may the only time in your rotten, miserable lives that you come this close to greatness! And when you go home, to your one room shacks in this third-rate so-called city, and nothing around you is worth a damn, you'll understand what I'm talking about. You might even be so depressed that you'll want to end it all. And when you're fat cow of a wife or lazy, good-for-nothing husband looks at you and asks what's wrong, just look at them and say: "I wish I could be the T, the B and the C, but because I'm not, I want to D, I, E!" Ha ha! Ha ha ha ha! [TBC flips the mic behind him. He goes to a corner and climbs onto the second turnbuckle. He is yelling insults at the crowd as he pats the belt, resting on his shoulder. He continues laughing and insulting the crowd for a few more moments. He climbs down and leaves the ring area, all the while insulting the crowd as he walks by. Camera fades as Chill threatens to hit a fan.] [C] 2002 RSP Entertainment Inc.
