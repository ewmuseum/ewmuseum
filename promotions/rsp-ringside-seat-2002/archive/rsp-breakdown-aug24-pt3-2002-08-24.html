[RSP] RSP Breakdown [3/4

24 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera cuts in we go quickly to the ring as Vance Randhal is pacing around. Randhal takes the mic from the ring announcer and begins to speak.] VR: I'll make this short and simple. I know there's a show we need to get to. [Vance stops center ring and looks straight into the camera.] VR: However there is someone I need to address. ERIC EMERSON... [Crowd begins to chant "Asshole" as a loud boo is heard.] VR: No, No..not the Eric Emerson you all know and hate. I'm talking about the Emerson I know and grew up with. The Emerson that actualy had a heart and some spirit. [Vance bows his head and begins to pace again.] VR: Emerson? What happend brother? Let's go back a little. Not to far back but just before the New Year. Remember? I don't think you can forget. We were in the hospital, we were both sitting by the bed of a man we both cared about. A man who taught us everything when we were growing up. That man was your father Eric. You remember him? The man on whos deathbed you made a promise to. [Vance smiles.] VR: You promised your father you would change. Well Eric you lived up to that promise you have changed. Eric you lost more then your father you lost.. [Vance pounds his hand to his heart.] VR: Where did it go Eric? [Suddenly "Hail to the Cheif" begins to play as Sean Christopher makes his way to the ring.] SC: WHO THE HELL ARE YOU? [.....] SC: Don't answer please don't answer! I'm sure these people don't want to hear your voice anymore. You come out here and cry and complain about losing a friend. Who the hell do you think you are? ERIC EMMERSON is a star. He has better things to do then hear you cry and moan and bitch about the old days. [Sean enters the ring and gets into Vance Randhals face.] SC: GET THE HELL OUT OF MY RING!! [Sean Christopher looks around at the crowd then smiles. Vance Randhal just exits the ring and walks to the back.] SC: Sinister I'll deal with you later tonight. Right now I'm introduce you to the man who I hired to bring justice to the pay-per-view match at Dangerous Games. [Crowd pop huge.] SC: Ladies and Gentlemen the man who will serve as special guest referee. TTTTTOMMMMY DDDDDDAAAAAANNNNGERRR~! [It was at that point that half the banks of lights were shut down. The fans stirred. Off in the distance, in one section of the stadium, the chant had started up. This time it was a fan, wearing a "TMD 2002: Give Me Two Minutes and I'll Kick Your Ass" T-Shirt.] Crowd: U...S...A! U...S...A! U...S...A! U...S...A! [Soon, the entire place was filled with the chant as it grew louder, faster, the fans coming to their feet and beginning to cheer with the chants. Richardson and Blackwell were speechless and the RSP broadcast crew just kept filming, letting the fans fill in the air time. Then it happened.] Crowd: U...S...A! U...S...A! U...S...A! U...S....HUGE FACE POP] [Suddenly, the TRON that hung above the entrance way lit up with a what looked to be a floating image. The image was that of burning flames, which soon turned into a silhouette of a man, standing, arms crossed. The flames continued to burn and soon it burned a name across the screen. Tommy Danger. The PA also kicked up, but with something different. There was a scratchiness to the sound, a hollow voice, seemingly distant. The strum of a guitar could be heard over that distant sounding voice.] *Walking around I hear the earth seeking relief I'm trying to find a reason to live But the mindless clutter my path Oh these thorns in my side...* [Up on the TRON a video image could be seen. Many clips, in black and white. Tommy Danger. Past and present. Battles shown in fast snippets, foes he's defeated. Foes he's been defeated by.] *Oh these thorns in my side...* [More images. More recent. Cleveland, Ohio, where he made his IGA debut, standing next to Todd Bishop, facing off with the Dynasty. Then Donovan Keltrane, the classic 2 out of 3 falls battle at Requiem For Pain. Tommy having his hand raised after defeating Malkav, Pandemonium and Mark Davenport on November 2nd, 2001, in a Fatal Four way match to determine the number one contendership.] *I know I have something free I have something so alive* [Even more images flash across the screen as Creed's "Bullet's" continued to play, the tune slowly building. The fans stirring in their seats and cheering at as they watched between the TRON and the curtains. Danger pinning Ito Otagami on December 1st then images of his battle with Todd Bishop at Tournament of Warriors.] *I think they shoot because they want it...* [Tommy's loss to Maxime St. Jean at International Incident was shown, Maxime St. Jean raising the title over his head as he looked down at Tommy. Then the music kicked in full bore as Scott Stapp burst out with full lung capacity, the yell bringing the crowd to it's feet.] *I THINK THEY SHOOT BECAUSE THEY WANT IT!* [Tommy Danger stepped out from behind the curtains and out onto the ramp.] [INSANE FACE POP] Collen Blackwell: Wow! What a pop fropm the crowd. Vince Richardson: I don't even know who the hell this is. How do they know? Collen Blackwell: You really don't keep up, do you. Tommy Danger is coming to us by way of the IGA tonight, Vince. Former two time CWF champion, RWF superstar and current IGA superstar. Not to mention the fact that he's been around the e-wrestling biz since 1990. Details, Vince. Details. [Vince just grunted.] *I feel forces all around me Come on raise your head Those who hid behind the shadows Live with all that's dead* [Tommy walked to the edge of the stage, where it met with the ramp that led towards the ring. His taped up wrist were suddenly shoved up into the air, fingers on either hand curling onto fists as they were thrust upwards. The place came apart again even as the TRON continued to show images. This time Sean Smith could be seen. His raging glare seemingly looking down at a superimposed Danger as he did battle in the ring. Then it dissolved into a scene of Tommy tossing men such as the Nightstalker, Matt Allen, Wallace and Stalin over the top ropes during the Two Minute Drill at International Incident.] *Look at me...look at me... At least look at me when you shoot a bullet through my head... Through my head... Through my head... Through my head... In my lifetime when I'm disgraced By jealousy and lies I laugh out loud 'cause me life Has gotten inside someone else's head* [Tommy made his way down the ramp and made his way over to the steps. It was a new look for Tommy, as far as his attire was concerned. White boots with black laces remained the same, but the white tights were different. The right leg was emblazoned with black lettering that read "Armed". His left was likewise, though it read "Assault". Neatly draped over his shoulders and tucked into his tights, was a black T-shirt that also had white lettering. It read "Armed & Dangerous" an older style of sleeveless T that was making it's return. Tommy stepped through the ropes as "Bullets" continued to play over the PA system. He moved across the ring once he entered and hopped to the second ropes in the corner, his knee pressed to the top turnbuckle as his fist again were shot into the air.] Look at me...look at me... At least look at me when you shoot a bullet through my head... Through my head... Through my head... Through my head.... [The place came apart and Tommy hopped back down off the turnbuckle, his ice blue stare leveled to the entrance way as he wriggled from his shirt and tossed it towards some unsuspecting lucky fan in the crowd. Pacing in a circle, he finally ended up standing toe to toe with Sean Christopher.] SC: About time you showed up! [HUGE WE LOVE TOMMY POP.] [Tommy let his ice blue gaze wash over the ranting man, yet he remained quiet, sniffing back once as his hands went to his hips.] SC: Last week you stood me up. That's right folxs last week this man stood YOU ALL up. I was going to make this big announcement last week but guess who no showed? That's o.k these things happen. DON"T EVER DO THAT AGAIN. [Sean points his finger at Tommy and pokes him in the chest.] [Tommy's eyebrow arched a bit as he looked down at his chest where he was poked. He shifted a bit then crossed his arms over his chest.] SC: You hear me! This isn't some low level, under budget, three ring side show. You know? The kind of place you're used to working at! The kind of place run by a front office who rather put themselves over rather then the talent. The kind of place run by an inept front office. [Those ice blues narrowed a bit and frustration seemed to get a glare from Tommy at Sean.] SC: I'm getting off the subject. I hired you to do one thing. Bring me the title. You do what I say. I want you here at a certain time you better be here. I know you haven't been around here that long so let me review. [Crowd starts a "Shut the hell up!" chant.] SC: There's this man, Saul Akiva, who has been running around here as if he owns the place. He's been nothing but a disgrace to this company, to the title, and most importantly ME! He must be ended and you're the man I hired to do it. I'm sure you'll do whatever it takes to see that the match at Dangerous Games goes the way I see fit. Do you understand me? [Tommy snatches the mic from Christopher and the crowd popped. Tommy walked around the ring a little bit before he turned to face Christopher once more. He licked his lips and set the mic to rest a bit under his chin. His quiet cool huskiness broke the air.] Tommy Danger: Ya know... [Many of the fans attendence had seen this man before and like clockwork, they lit into the arena space with their own version of Tommy Danger's famous two words.] Crowd: YA KNOWWWW!!!! [Tommy rolled his neck as the fans popped for themselves, a small grin tugging at the corner of his mouth. But he soon lost that when he looked at Sean.] Tommy Danger: I once knew another Sean that liked to talk to much. [The fans popped at the hidden mention of Sean Smith, his long time rival.] Tommy Danger: I shut him up too. [HUGE POP.] Tommy Danger: So we have coming up the pay-per-view Dangerous Games. [HUGE POP.] Tommy Danger: How...fitting. [Tommy smirked over at Sean.] Tommy Danger: The most dangeous man in sports entertainment at Dangerous Games? Seems a perfect fit. And what a better man to call the big match between Fabian Cruz and Saul Akiva. [Sean snatched the mic back.] Sean Christopher: No no no... I brought you here to... [Tommy snatched the mic right back and got nose to nose with Christopher.] Tommy Danger: I don't remember saying I was done! [HUGE POP.] [Sean glared at Tommy and grit his teeth. Things were not going well.] Tommy Danger: But you were saying you hired me to bring you a title? [Sean nodded as he crossed his arms over his chest.] Tommy Danger: Well, let me ask you something. [Sean titlted his head as Tommy looked down at the mat, then up at Sean.] Tommy Danger: Officially, I was hired by the RSP to referee this match, right. [Sean shifted in his stance.] Tommy Danger: Then the only logical reason Tommy Danger is here is clear. [The crowd popped a bit.] Tommy Danger: And that's to bring a title to... [He paused to look over at Sean who was beginning to smile a bit.] Tommy Danger: ....Tommy Danger. [HUGE POP] Richardson: Oh oh. [Blackwell chuckled a bit and shook his head at Vince's reaction. Sean went ballistic and got right in the face of Tommy. Spit flung from his lips as he ranted, his voice loud enough for the mic in Tommy's hand to pick it up.] Sean Christopher: Damnit! We had a deal! You were hired for one reason and one reason only! Bring me a title! You will do what.... [Tommy had let the man rant long enough and the slap across the cheek that stopped his speech made Sean blink and backpedal.] {OH MY GOD DID HE JUST SLAP SEAN CHRISTOPHER POP!] [With Sean stunned, Tommy swung him around and stood behind the victim facing towards him. Tommy hooked both of Christophers arms and placed his head inbetween the Seans shoulderblades. Tommy then methodically turned around in a motion so he and Sean were facing the opposite direction and still holding onto Seans arms, as a result the Sean is now in a position similar to that Tommy's shoulder blades. Danger then dropped down driving Seans head into the mat with the Reverse Underhook DDT, otherwise known as the Tomakazi!] Crowd: Oooooooo! Collen Blackwell: Oh boy. Vince Richardson: Oh oh. [As Sean was laid out, face down in the mats, Tommy dropped to one knee and picked up the mic. He sort of settled onto his elbows in a lean in front of the down and out Christopher and sniffed back once. His voice was like that ice on ice quiet cool.] Tommy Danger: Tommy Danger is gona call that match at Dangerous Games....50-50. Right down the middle. And if Sean Christopher wants to dare step into that ring he better know this... [The crowd popped as Tommy paused, but still focused on the one man on the mat.] Tommy Danger: No one is imune to a Nightmare. [Patted Sean on the back of the head gently and then settled the mic to rest on the back of Sean. Rolling to the left, Tommy rolled from the ring and hit the mats with his feet in one motion before "Bullets" kicked up again.] *I THINK THEY SHOOT BECAUSE THEY WANT IT!* [As the crowd popped, Tommy made his way back up the ramp, only once turning at the curtains to see Christopher stir in the ring. The camera caught that dangerous smirk of his as it creapt acrsss his visage before he pushed through the RSP logo'd curtain. Camera cuts to Collen Blackwell and Vince Richardson.] Collen Blackwell: Tommy Danger has finaly arrived here in RSP!! Vince Richardson: ...And he's already adding flames to the fire. Collen Blackwell: I'm sure Sean Christopher isn't going to be happy. What about his comments though? He doesn't want Akiva as champion. Vince Richardson: Akiva, Sinister, and know Tommy Danger..Sean Christophers hit list keeps growing!! Collen Blackwell: Right now lets go to Anderson Michaels for the next match. [ - ]RSP-----------------------------------RSP[-] Greatest Team Alive -vs- Totaly Rad Connection [ - ]RSP-----------------------------------RSP[-] AM: The following contest is a special tag team match. Introducing first the team of TOMMY ROBERTS and BOBBY MICHAELS.....THE TTTOTTTALY RRRRADDDD CCCONNECCTION [A hush falls over the crowd as the lights go out. White strobe lights start to flash and the entranceway is covered in fog and smoke. "Kickstart My Heart" by Motley Crue starts to blast over the P.A. system and the crowd cheers as The Total Rad Connection starts to make their way to the ring] AM: Their opponents... V/O: Shut Up!! [Dax and Maddox walk slowly to the ring. Dax is limping and has his head taped up. Maddox has the mic in one hand and a crutch in the other.] Maddox: As much as we would of loved to put a show on for these fans and give you two california surfers a lesson in the profession of pro wrestling. We just can't do it! Dax: Not tonight anyway. You see, as must of you witnessed earlier me and my partner here tried and were successful at getting rid of the Dime Notes. We even did a favor and gave the tag titles to Jones and Bones Maddox: That would be Tyler Christian and Joe Jones. Dax: Yeah..Right..Who cares. Anyway, how did we get repaid for this favor? We were attacked. [Crowd begins a loud chant directed toward Dax and Maddox.] Dax: Boo us? Jones and Christian are the two you should be booing! They robbed you! They took away your opportunity and seeing two of the greatest talents... Maddox: The greatest team alive! Dax: They took away your opportunity... [SMACK] [SMACK] [SMACK From behind Chris Page smashes both Dax and Maddox with a chair. Page picks up Maddox and drags him toward the ring. Maddox tries to fight back but Page hits a low blow on Maddox before rolling him into the ring. Maddox begs off as Roberts and Michaels stands over him.] Vince Richardson: What the hell is Chris Page doing out here? Collen Blackwell: He's getting a little payback from earlier tonight. [Page walks back up the entrance and as Dax tries to get up Page pulls out some tape from his pocket. Dax begins to rap the tap around Dax's wrists and the guardrail.] Vince Richardson: Come on..Dax can't defend himself. Collen Blackwell: I think that's the point. [SMASH... Page cracks Dax with the chair one last time before flipping him off and walking to the back.] Vince Richardson: Where's Legacy? [Camera cuts to the backstage area where we see Damian Legacy laid out. Standing over him is RSP T.V champion The big Chill. Camera cuts back to the ring.] Collen Blackwell: The Big Chill just took out Damian Legacy. Vince Richardson: This isn't right!! This isn't fair to the trinity!! [Inside the ring Roberts and Michaels whip Maddox into the ropes. As Maddox comes off the ropes the Totaly Rad Connection hit a double dropkick. Maddox crawls to a corner begging off once again. The ref gets Michaels out of the ring. DING...DING..DING] Collen Blackwell: We have a bell. Vince Richardson: Yippie!! [Roberts sends Maddox into the ropes and as he comes off picks him up over his shoulder and hits a soman drop. Roberts sets Maddox up and hits a flying elbow smash followed by a flying forearm.] Collen Blackwell: Roberts and Michaels showing some high energy and this crowd is on their feet. Vince Richardson: This crowd is sick. It's two on one in there!! Collen Blackwell: I'm sure if it was the other way around you be loving it! [Roberts tags in Michaels who climbs to the top rope. As Maddox gets up holding his head Michaels comes off and hits a blockbuster. Michaels goes to the other side of the ring and perches himself on the top rope again. As Maddox gets up Michaels hits a top rope hurricanrana sending him to the floor. Maddox staggers to his feet and stumbles to his partner as the ref starts a count.... ONE TWO THREE FOUR FIVE SIX [Maddox gets Dax untied from the guardrail.] SEVEN EIGHT NINE [Dax and Maddox run to the back. As the crowd pops huge.] TEN~! [DING...DING...DING] Collen Blackwell: What a showing..What a showing for Roberts and Michaels. Vince Richardson: Maddox and Dax never had a chance. Collen Blackwell: We'll be right back..our main event is next!! [PLEASE SEE PART4]
