[RSP] Ringside Rants 8/2 [2/2]

3 August 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
RSP Ringside Rants continued... Tyler Christian =========== V/O: Why do I always have to save the day? [Camera cuts in as we see the blonde haired, blue eyes "gift from god" Tyler Christian. He is looking at himself through a mirror. He gives a big smile then a wink.] TC: Damn I'm good! [Also in the mirror we get a shot of the cameraman and production crew that's filming this.] TC: Don't you think I'm good? Hell..Don't you think I'm great? [Tyler dressed in blue jeans and wearing no shirt walks over to the couch. Christian sits and motions for the crew to do so as well.] TC: Now what is it you wanted to talk to me about? Off Screen Voice: Joe Jones... TC: Who? You mean twink? Off Screen Voice: Yeah, you're teaming with him this week. He beat you last week for the cruiserweight title. [Tyler laughs, smiles, then looks sternly into camera.] TC: Beat me? Beat me? You're kidding right? He didn't beat me, he beat that damn jailbait Greedy. Off Screen Voice: You were also in that match. [Tyler slams his fist on the coffee table beside him.] TC: HE DIDN'T BEAT ME!! My shoulder were not pinned to the mat. It was Greedy who got rolled up and ended up on his back counting the lights. As far as I go I'm undeafeated in RSP! [...] Voice Off Screen: Well this week you team with the man that beat..uhh..I mean the man that beat Greedy. The tag titles will be on the line. Can you two work together? [Tyler leans forward on the couch then smirks.] TC: Can we work together? Let me tell you something, we wont need to work together. I'm going to win those damn tag title by myself. Twink? He can stay home if he wants. Voice Off Screen: What about the pay-per-view then? Is it true you talked to Sean Christopher about a match? TC: Yes, I talked to Sean. I told him if he wants to get buys, if he wants to put people in the seats he needs me on the card. Voice Off Screen: What did he say? TC: Well it looks like I got a title shot at the pay-per-view. A title shot against Joe Jones. Voice Off Screen: So if you win the tag title you'll have two matches? TC: What can be better then that? The woman can feat their eyes on me twice!! AND it's not IF we win the tag titles it's WHEN we win the tag titles. Now if you'll excuse me. [Tyler gets up from the couch and exits the room.] Tylers Voice: You guys can show yourselfs out. See you at Breakdown!! [Camera fades to black.] ======================================================================= [Van Halen's "Hot For Teacher" plays on a hi-fi as the camera pans across the poster covered walls of a garage. The heavenly faces and bodies of Molly Ringwald, Debbie Gibson, Tiffany, The Facts of Life girls, and The Iron Shiek pass by before we settle on two tanned we built men playing Ms Pac Man] Blonde Guy: Oh damn! I so totally hate that Pinky! Black Haired Guy: Bobby, dude, you are so totaly toast. Bobby: Shut up Tommy. [Ladies & Gentlemen meet Bobby Michaels and Tommy Roberts- The Totally Rad Connection] Tommy: Want another go? Bobby: Sure thing. Tommy: Just don't choke like that this week when we take on the Greatest Team Alive. Bobby: Greatest Team Alive? Yeah, right. Not when Bret, C.C. Rikki, amd Bobby. Tommy: Otherwise known as Poison Bobby: Are still around. Both: I WANT ACTION TONIGHT! SATISFACTION ALL NIGHT! [They high-five and continue their game] Tommy: Still though dude, this is our debut match in this promotion and our biggest match yet. Bobby: Bigger than our match in Vegas? Tommy: I don't think- Bobby: GET THAT PELLET! GET THAT GHOST! DIE BINKY DIE! Tommy: I don't think a match in the parking garage at Circus Circus can really compare to this dude. Bobby: What about that match in Redondo Beach- Tommy: Again you Alex P. Keaton wannabe, I don't think- Bobby: With those totally hot cheerleaders. [Tommy pauses a moment] Tommy: Well dude that does come close Bobby: Damn right. Tommy: But these are totally different fans, we're gonna have to work harder on impressing them then we did at working on the ribik's cube to impress them. Bobby: Well taking risks and makin' all the pretty girls scream is what we do best. Tommy: Amongst other things Bobby: Well that goes without sayin dude. Tommy: But The Greatest Team Alive better get ready. Bobby: Cuz The Totally Rad Connection has arrived in RSP Tommy: And things will never...ever..be the same again ======================================================================= [Camera fades into Tonya Sanders sitting in the RSP control room.] TS: And we'll see you in a few as Ringside Seat Promotions presents...BBBREAKDOWN. [Camera fades as credits roll.] [C] 2002, RSP Entertainment Inc.
