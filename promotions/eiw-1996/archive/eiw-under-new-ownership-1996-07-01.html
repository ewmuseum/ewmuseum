EIW Under New Ownership

1 July 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
EIW UNDER NEW OWNERSHIP!!!!! Minnesota EIW has been sold! This shocking news being released from EIW headquarters late today. After what was apparently several grueling hours of negotiation, the papers were signed and EIW had a new owner. [name withheld], the now former owner of EIW started the fed earlier this year. He has watched some big names, including Ric Flair, Deisel, and Bret Hart become great stars while watching Fisto Flash, The Undead Stable and Blitzkreig appear, then quickly fade. He is proud of what he has accomplished, but feels it is time to move on. He took a brief leave of absence in June. During his absence Jason Clevett ran the league. Although the takeover was intended to be temporary, LaValle was so impressed by the skill in which Jason handled the league that he decided he was the amn to take over the leauge. "It's been a decision I've been mulling over for a long time" says LaValle. "I feel Jason is the right choice to take over. His implementation of a lightweight divison and the importation of some of the greatest stars around the world, as well as some little known stars has made EIW one of the most exciting feds. Jason Clevett has been involved with EIW for since April. He has assisted in making decisions and booking some of the great matches EIW has become famous for. He was contemplating opening up his own fed. "However, I didn't like the idea of starting from scratch. I knew from working with Eric that often you would get men like [name withheld] who could not roleplay. Running a fed is a lot of work. However when the oppertunity to take over EIW came along I couldn't resist. We already have a excellent staff and some of the best roleplayers available. Our members are loyal and really enjoy the cards, which have recieved high praise. I'm happy to be taking over EIW." LaValle will remain with the league as a advisor and a associate of Jason. Howver, his work will be minimal. "An occasional match, help when Jay needs it. But it's in his hands." Clevett made a number of stunning decisions within hours of his purchase. EIW's offical offices will be moving from Minnesota to Calgary, Alberta. As well, Jason Clevett releived the master of his presidential duties. "I feel a unbiased opinion is needed, and I do not feel The Master is capable of that." Clevett assigned good friend [name withheld], also fo Calgary, to the job of president. "[name withheld] is a great guy. I feel he will do what is best for the leauge." EIW has impressed with the skill of it's roleplayers, the surprise twists in plot and the exciting amtches. It promises to only go farther into greatness.
