Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Extreme Internet Wrestling introduced itself to the newsgroup on 8 April 1996 already two months old and “already full” at about 85 wrestlers, running five cards a week — Monday Night Madness, Wednesday Night, Freaky Friday, Saturday Night Fights, and Sunday Stampede — on a roster it described as “a combination of real and fantasy wrestlers, there's an even mix.”

Ten days later its sixteen-man North American Heavyweight Title tournament crowned its own first champion: “Flyin” Brian Pillman, previously stripped of a King of the Ring crown that the promotion's own storyline had converted into the new title, beat Scorpion in the final with a handful of tights the referee never saw.

On 1 July 1996 the promotion changed hands. Its founder sold the fed to a staff member who had been running it in his place during a brief leave of absence that spring. The new owner moved its offices from Minnesota to Calgary, Alberta, removed the sitting president, and installed a friend in the role, while the founder stayed on only as an advisor.

No further document survives past that ownership change. A fan's request for a lost contact address that September is the last dated trace, and whether the fed continued under its new owner isn't in the surviving record.
