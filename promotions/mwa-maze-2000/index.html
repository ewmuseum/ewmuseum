Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

A website federation calling itself the Maze Wrestling Association opened in February 2000, its own recruitment post promising cash prizes, strong card-writing, and a first card that Monday for whichever singles wrestlers and tag teams signed on.

By November 2000 the promotion had built, in its own words, one of the fastest-growing singles rosters on the internet, still short of tag teams nine months into its run.

Its one surviving results broadcast, Monday Night Warriors #1, opened from a Vancouver theatre with a hardcore title change, a debuting tag team's interview cut short by an ambush, and a new heavyweight champion crowned in its main event — the broadcast's own closing credits naming the federation in full for the only time in this record.

The bare acronym MWA was never this promotion's alone. Six other federations recorded elsewhere in this museum carried the same three letters, and three further 2000-dated federations share it as well; none of their own full names, hosts, or rosters overlap with this one's.

No further activity survives past that November broadcast, and no closing notice of any kind follows it.
