Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

NeWA Mid Atlantic Wrestling staged itself as a Mid-Atlantic territory of the NeWA hub, its every page bylined by an in-fiction authority figure billed "Jim Crockett, Jr." — the real historical Mid-Atlantic/Jim Crockett Promotions name, worn here as a kayfabe persona rather than naming any real contributor. On 27 January 1999 that persona vacated the Mid-Atlantic Heavyweight Title over the conduct of two champions, KRaZ-e and JD Johnson, ordering them into a best-of-seven series for the Television Title instead; the vacant Heavyweight belt was refilled by Mac Michaels in a Four Corners Match on 10 February 1999. Four titles are structured from the surviving record: the Heavyweight Championship, the Tag Team Titles (won once by Angels of the Night), the Television Title (three reigns, JD Johnson through Fred Rogers, with an unattested gap between the second and third), and a Light-Heavyweight Title won and retained by Ryan Carling — named only as an empty "Light-Weight" row on the fed's own Title History page, almost certainly the same belt under a variant spelling. A fifth title, for women, was announced but never awarded, "pending approval from Mid-Atlantic counsel."

Its own Application page and its Tag Team Titles both connect directly to the wider NeWA hub: the application itself is hosted at the same GeoCities address this project's wccw wing already holds, and the Angels of the Night, crowned here, are read as the same touring team already holding the National Tag Team Championship on the hub's own British sister territory, BCW — the hub's own news elsewhere describes exactly this kind of cross-territory movement. No wrestler's real name appears anywhere in the held record.
