EWA Extreme Wrestling Alliance Background info: The EWA (Extreme Wrestling Alliance) is a sort of a continuation of another great e-fed that collapsed because of internal problems. Many say that a fed can't fail because of one person, well, this fed did. Anyway, "Morpheus" [name withheld] and I ("Nemesis" [name withheld]) decided that the UPW had too much charisma and too much willing RPers to be let down because of one very annoying person, so we decided to start our own federation, without Harvester of Sorrow, the man who single-handily took down UPW. So if you see crazy remarks about him in RPs or cards, like the bell boy; Bell Boy of Sorrow, Mascot; Mini-Harvester of Steroids, etc. In conclusion, Mike and I both hope the the EWA will easily grow out of the UPW's shadow and establish itself as one fun ass activity to be involved in if everything else fails. After all, if its on the Net, its got to be fun. Rules: - All Roleplays must be at least 30 lines. - Any attacks, interviews, angles, or spots you want taking place at a card must be sent to Presidents; Dan ([detail withheld]) and Mike ([detail withheld]). Not to the entire list!! - All roleplays must be sent to the entire list before the card's deadline. - No punting, no b*tching if you lose a match, etc. Just don't be annoying, and if you're from UPW, you know what I'm talking about. - None of those 12-18 point font RPs allowed in the UPW. All roleplays must be at readable.. 8 point font.. and reasonable.. 11 point font. Any roleplays that are written in lower then 8 point font, or bigger then 11 point font will be disregarded and ignored as far as your match is concerned. - Please use your best grammar in your roleplays. It is really a pain in the ass to read roleplays with simple words, and to add to that, simple words that are misspelled. I will accept spelling mistakes here in there, but it is really your loss. Please write in your best grammar, and don't seem to be an unintelligent RPer trying to make it with simple quantity. - If you want to recruit, be my guest. Recruits are always welcome, just send them an application asking them to forward it to both [detail withheld] and [detail withheld]. - The Prezzes decide the winners of matches. In any case that either myself (Dan) or Mike (Morpheus) are involved in a match, Commissioner [name withheld] judges the Roleplays. - Its very simple, the prezzes will stay out of most situations like they should. - Pretty much eveything will be accepted in a roleplay (racial, sexual, personal remarks will be accepted as long as there is a Disclaimer, but if complaints are issued about it, actions will be taken). - There are few rules, but please respect them none the less. Presidents: [name withheld] [name withheld] Commissioner: [name withheld] Card Days: Tuesday -Tuesday Night Pheer Friday -Friday Night Funk Pay-Per-Views: December: Christmas Bloody Christmas Januray: Blizzard Destruction Commentators: Jon Rashad "Scarface" Joel Young Bell Boy: Bell Boy of Sorrow (BBOS) Mascot: Mini-Harvester of Steroids Title Belts: EWA World Title -The most prestigious and coveted Title in EWA. Equal to WWF's and/or WCW's Heavyweight Titles. EWAUnited States Title -The second most covetted title in EWA. Equal to WWF's IC, and/or WCW's US Titles. EWA Brass-knucles Title -Almost a title for those who can't quite compete in the top divisions of the EWA, but a big stepping stone towards EWA success none-the-less. Equal to ECW's TV Titles. EWA World Tag Team Titles -Figure this one out yourself, reply to me with what you think these titles mean, and I'll e-mail you a chocolate chip cookie. Then e-mail it to both [detail withheld] and [detail withheld]. My EWA 2 Cents: Welcome to the Extreme Wrestling Alliance. There aren't many rules, not too many regulations, not too much to fill out on the application, and basically, this fed is for the RPers, from the RPers, by the RPers, and to the RPers. If you're not having fun, I encourage you to quit. This is all for fun, and the wrestlers control the angle, interviews, almost everything. The only thing the Prezzes are here for is to decide who wins a match. Plain and simple. The main rule: Have Fun, and don't be Annoying!
