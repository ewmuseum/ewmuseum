What is the Mutant Fighter Federation? The Mutant Fighter Federation is a Wrestling Federation that involves wrestlers from Marvel, Image, and DC comics. The MFF started in 1992, and was called the MUTANT WRESTLING FEDERATION. Then, in 1995, it formed into The MUTANT FIGHTER FEDERATION (Started off with a Whole NEW Attitude), and it now is currently the Leading Mutant Wrestling Federation out there (Main competitor being Tony Stark's ... MUTANT INTERNATION FEDERATION). There are 2 sides to the M.F.F. (A & B). Cable is the President for the B, and Red Tornato is for the A. And the OWNER of the MFF is the Mighty Thor! The Matches are a bit different Here in the MFF. Here are some of the ways they are different: 1. The Pins go up to 5 2. In a Tag Team match, both members of the team has to be pined unless said otherwise 3. The Reffs are not as strict (You get away with more) 4. If you fall outside of the ring, it's over a 10 foot drop Here are some of the matches in the MFF: 1. Regular Matches 2. Danger Room Matches 3. Brawls 4. Bedlams 5. Steel Cage Matches 6. Mini-Rumbles (One every 100 matches-1 person comes in every 2 min.-16 people in Total) - Winner vs. Internation Champion 7. Super Card (Single or Tag Team-The names are drawn out of a hat, so the wrestler doesn't know who his opponent or partner is) 8. HandiCap Match (One wrestler vs. Two) 9. Casket matches 10. Super Star Match (To become a MFF superstar you have to win your First 3 matches) 11. Bridge match (The 2 wrestlers fight on a bridge, and can only pin in the ring-if fall off, 20 foot drop) 12. Clash Match (Get 4 Big Guys to battle it out in a Over-The-Top Match) There are 5 titles in the MFF, and each side has their own(ex:2 world//2 tag team).The titles are: Worlds - This is the Richest Title in the game. The Holder of the MFF World Champion, is often the TOP Wrestler at the time. Tag Team - This title proves witch Tag Team has the Best Team Work, stamin, and Carisma. Heavy Weight - This title most of the time, shows who is the Biggest and the Baddest at the time. International - This title shows who is Hot, and will have a Great Future in the MFF. Light Weight - For the wrestlers under 240 lbs. this title, to them, is like the Worlds. It shows who is the Most Athletic, and gives all the smaller Wrestlers a Great chance for Gold. Danger Room Champ (There is only 1 on this tite) - This title Represents who is the best HARDCORE Fighter in the game. There are well over 100 wrestlers in the M.F.F., and they all do a great job to get into conflicts for BIG EVENTS! Here is the List of the BIG EVENTS: Royal Rumble (January) Winter Wars (February) Wrestle Mania (March) Slam Fest (April) Battle Clash (May) King Of The Ring (June) Doom Fights (July) Summer Slam (August) Clash of the Champions (September) Halloween Havok (October) Survivor Series (December)
