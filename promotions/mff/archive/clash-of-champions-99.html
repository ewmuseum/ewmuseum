MFF Clash of the Champions '99

September 30 1. ??-Mystery Man-?? vs. Black Bolt - (INTER) - (14 min.) - (NEW INTER CHAMP!) Black Bolt, the INTERNATIONAL Champion was called in. Then, the announcer began to call in the SPECIAL OPPONENT ... AC/DC's HIGHWAY To HELL music began to PLAY, as the CROWD(knowing who's music that is began to cheer!) "WEAPON-X" came OUT! This was a Great Match! Both Black Bolt & Weapon-X showed Great Athletism and Speed! The match was about 50-50%, and it ended when Black Bolt was Stunned by a Kick out pick by Weapon-X, as W-X flipped him over! As Black Bolt turned around ... WHAP! The SUPER KICK! Weapon-X covered ... 1-2-3-4-5! Here is the NEW INTERNATIONAL CHAMPION ... WEAPON-X! 2. Night Crawler - (LIGHT WGT.) vs. Blizzard (15 min.) - (NEW LIGHT WGT. CHAMPION!) Blizzard was called to the ring. Following, was X-MEN member, and the LIGHT WEIGHT CHAMPION, Night Crawler! This was a Really good match! Night Crawler did More High-Risk Top rope moves than he normally does, and Blizzard had the crowd with Him the Whole Match! Near the Middle of the match, when Blizzard was giving it to Night Crawler, CANNONBALL came to the ring with a Steel Folding Chair! He sat down and watched! Near the end, Night Crawler was loosing again, and he gave Cannonball a Signal, that Blizzard seen! Blizzard threw Night Crawler to the ropes, as he ran to the other side. When, in the middle, instead of running to the other ropes, he Swung Night Crawler back to the ropes, and WHAP! Cannonball NAILED Night Crawler with the Steel Chair! From there, Blizzard FINISHED Night Crawler! He covered, and WON! Here is the WINNER, and NEW LIGHT WEIGHT CHAMPION ... BLIZZARD! 3. Destroyer vs. Midevil Spawn - (HEAVY WEIGHT) (13 min.) Destroyer was called to the ring. Following, was HELL'S ARMY member, and HEAVY WEIGHT CHAMPION, MIDEVIL SPAWN! This was a HUGE match sa Both Midevil Spawn & Destoyer used their STRONGEST Moves on one another! They battled Back & Forth like HELL! At about the middle of the match, Destroyer Closelined Midevil Spawn over the Top rope. From there, they battled outside the ring for a Few min. Then, Midevil Spawn began to win, as he threw Destroyer into the ring. As he was baout to go in, ROBO DESTROYER ran up behind Midevil Spawn, and WHAP! Nailed him with a Steel Chair! From there, he hit Midevil Spawn's Back "2" more times, and rolled him into the ring. Destroyer, then covered ... 1-2-3-4 ... Midevil Spawn kicked out! After that, Destroyer had an Advantage in the match! Destroyer continued to assult Midevil Spawn's back until, the match was Alomst over! Destroyer threw Midevil Spawn outside the tring again, beat him up, and then threw him back in. BUT, when he was coming in the ring, "REALM OF DARKNESS" member TERRAX ran out of Knowhere, and SPEARED Destroyer! From there, he FINISHED him with the TIGER BOMB! After that Terrax threw Destroyer into the ring, where Midevil Spawn gave him a Choke Slam, and then "2" Tombstones! After that, Midevil Spawn covered ... 1-2-3-4-5!! Here is your winner, MIDEVIL SPAWN! 4. UNSTOPPABLES (Dare Devil & Stryfe) vs. RED BLOODS (Dead Pool & Kane) (6 min.) - (1 FALL) The RED BLOODS came to the ring first. Following, was the NEW Team, UNSTOPPABLES! This was an AEWSOME Match with ALOT of ACTION! They ALL battled one another at first, until the Reff began to gain control! Stryfe & Dead Pool started it off! They battled Back & Forth, until Dare Devil gave Dead Pool a Cheap Shot, and Stryfe took control after that! Dare Devil & Stryfe continued to Tag oneanother, and did NOT give Dead Pool the Chance to TRY to make a TAG! Finally, about 9 min. into the Match, Dead Pool Closelined Dare Devil Outside the ring, and Tagged in KANE! Kane and Dare Devil battle for a Bit, but then Dare Devil tagged im Stryfe! Through out the Whole match, these 2 gave Cheap Shots on One another! While Stryfe & Kane was Clashing, Dare Devil began to attack Dead Pool, and FINISHED HIM! About 1 min, after that, Kane Tagged in Dead Pool, not realizing that he was Injured! From there, Stryfe & Dare Devil once again began to assult Dead Pool! Stryfe than began to fight with Kane, as WHAP! Dare Devil struck Dead Pool over the Head with a Steel Chair! From there, Dare Devil attacked Kane, as Stryfe went for the cover ... 1-2-3-4-5!! Here are your winners, The UNSTOPPABLES! 5. SPAWN vs. DEATHLOK - (WORLDS) (19 min.) - (SPECIAL Reff - GW BRIDGE) - (NEW WORLD CHAMPION) Spawn, the LORD of HELL'S ARMY was called down to the ring. Following, was the MFF's WORLD CHAMPION, Deathlok! Then, the GLASS Broke, as GW BRIDGE, the Special Reff for the match came out! This match was one of the BEST Title Matches EVER! Deathlok & Spawn gave it ALL they HAD & MORE! Throughout the match, at points where Spawn was winning, GW would sometimes TRY to Distract him! Then, Deathlok would nail him from behind! The match was abput 50-50%! In around the Middle of the match, Deathlok was winning, as GW went and brought a Steel Chair into the ring. At that point, Spean began to win, and as he picked up the chair, GW pulled it away from him, and they began to argue ... WHAP! Deathlok took the Chair and NAILED Spawn on the Back with it! He then gave Spawn a DDT on the Chair, and went for a cover ... 1-2-3-4- ... Spawn kicked out! Deathlok was some what Mad at GW Bridge, the FACT that he PINNED so SLOW! Eventually, Spawn began to win again, as GW gave him Another Distraction! Again, Deathlok nailed him from behind! Eventually, Deathlok got REALLY mad at GW's slow pins, and they began to YELL at eachother! While they were arguing, Spawn got himself up, took the Steel Chair ... WHAP! Nailed Deathlok across the BACK of the Head with the Side of it! From there, Spawn grabbed Deathlok by the Throat ... CHOKE SLAM! After that, Spawn (ignoring GW) Picked up Deathlok and TOMB STONE! He covered ... ... (GW was NOT paying attention - then he seen) ... 1-2-3-4 ... Deathlok kicked out! SPAWN was FURIOUS! Spawn and GW Bridge began to argue, as GW continually shoved Spawn, but Spawn knew that if he TOUCHED GW, the match is OVER! Deathlok, once again attacke Spawn from behind, but he didn't get much on that attack, because Spawn was SO mad! Spawn CHOKE SLAMMED him again, Picked him up, climbed to the TOP ... TOMBSTONE from the TOP ROPE!! Deathlok was OUT! Spawn covered, and GW Bridge pinned with ANGER ... 1-2-3-4-5!! HE GOT HIM!!!! Here is you NEW MFF "WORLD CHAMPION," HELL'S ARMY member SPAWN! Before GW Bridge could DO ANYTHING, Spawn looked at him, as the LIGHTS went out ... BOOM! There wasen EXPLOSION above the RING, as SPAWN's music began to play! GW Bridge looked up ... WHAT??? There was a DUMMY dress like GW, and it was being HUNG! Just before GW could move ... WHAP! Spawn nailed GW with the Steel Chair, and gave him a TOMB STONE!
