:::Pro-Wrestling Tour Handbook v 1.0::: ***Note*** this is a quick overview, not wanting to write out the bill of rights all over again. This should give you all the general info you need to see if this is the type of federation you would be happy and have fun in.
Index I. The Basic information you should know about PWT II. The Purposed "Alliance" III. Rating Wars IV. Wins and losses The Basic Information you should know about PWT Pro-Wrestling Tour travels from city to city each week. Broadcasted on cable each week as a "wrap up" style show. Meaning, 2 announcers talk about angles, happenings and cuts to matches, about 4-5 matches Play by Play by the announcers. These matches aren't live, but tapped from the weekend before.
The Purposed "Alliance" PWT is in league/alliance with 3 other federations right now. Deep South
Wrestling, Shootfire-Pro and In your Face Wrestling. All four federations, including PWT are in agreement in having an alliance and a mega-supercard.
Speculations are once every 3-4 months. Rating Wars All 4 federations have "TV Deals". With that comes ratings. Each show will be broken down into 15 minute blocks and ratings will be taken. A system is in the works to get accurate ratings. Nothing is final here, but word is outsourcing to thelowblow.com
. They will take ratings for each show. All shows go "Head-to-Head." So no one show will have the advantage over another.
TheLowBlow.com will also have coverage of all 4 feds, live newsboard, with news rumors by reporters. So be prepared to answer questions in character to reporters.
Role Plays, RP packet, RP Board(e-w aspect) This is a Hybrid federation. Utilizing both RP board fans, and Email Packet fans. Once a week a packet will come out, at the same time RP's compiled on the RP board will be made visible. I only expect 1 RP on packets/Boards, this cracks down on guys who rp 3 times for one match, trying to win by quantity. I am a firm believer in Quality. I expect everyone to have "Oncard" segments.
This fed is driven towards the show. Determining matches (Wins/Losses) There is no set % of what gets you a win or loss. I don't believe in that, some people are good at some stuff. While someone else maybe better at something else. I can tell you that you need to participate, have good creative angles. For your match you need to strat, have an RP or Oncard. I will examine everything on a whole, and decide from there.
Closing This is just a quick overview of what Pro-Wrestling Tour will be about. If this interests you then maybe PWT is the place for you. If not, well sorry hope you find a fed that better suits your needs.
