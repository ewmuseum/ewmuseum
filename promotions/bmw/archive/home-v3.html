More huge explosions of pyro go off, and out on the ramp steps.....LOUIS MIRANDA!! Daily: What the hell!! Shirley: IT'S THE BOSS!! IT'S LOU! HE'S BACK ON TV!! Louis Miranda makes a strut down to the ring, as the Hartford Civic Center goes nuts! Miranda walks up the steps and steps into the ring, his suit showing flare and sophistication. He grabs a microphone from Chantel Terence at ringside. Shirley: Shhh...Louis has a few things to say. Miranda: Hello, Hartford! The fans grow ecstatic! Miranda: Despite what many may have heard, I have to tell you great fans the truth. Even though we sold out at Madison Square Garden for Money, Power, Respect, which by the way was one helluva show! Miranda pauses again as the fans cheer on. Miranda: We have had a slip up of the ratings. Yes, the beloved, legendary, Big Money Wrestling has experienced a fall in ratings. Although it isn't anything to go into a deep panic, I refuse to let ratings drop. So, ladies and gentlemen, I have been at work in developing a plan to enforce the ratings once again. As if our NEW WORLD CHAMPION isn't enough...as if Da Thug's MILLION DOLLAR REIGN isn't enough..... Fans begin to boo at the mention of Da Thug. Miranda: As if those things weren't enough, I've selected two men, who are willing and ready to take this plan into action. These men are big money legends. The fans begin to boo again. Miranda: No. I assure you, they aren't Scott Phillips and Krazie. Instead, they are two of BMW's greatest. Ladies and gentlemen, allow me to introduce to you.... Shirley: WHO!!!??!?!?! Miranda: DA MAB and DONOMITE!!! Daily: WOW!!! Shirley: A CURVE BALL! 'Superstar' by Saliva blasts as Da Mab and Donomite come out on the entrance way. The fans go nuts as the duo makes their way to the ring. Both men split ends and step through opposite ropes. Louis Miranda extends his hand as Da Mab takes it. He turns around and shakes Donomite's hand also. Daily: Unbelievable, but here they are, in the flesh. Da Mab and Donomite! Miranda: People, I'm sorry, but I have some important business to tend to at this time. I'll turn things over to Da Mab and Donomite. Miranda hands the microphone to Da Mab, and leaves the ring. He walks up the ramp and goes to the backstage area. Da Mab: All ye, all ye, Da Mab is back! Daily: Indeed you are! Da Mab: I want to introduce a piece of our plan. I can't reveal all of it, because if I did, it'd be a boring show, now wouldn't it? First off, the tag team division....sucks! Big Money Legends and the Two Good Looking Studs. Well, we'll be changing that. The Tag Team division will be built to maximum standards! Second off, we have this title called the Blood Money title. Who owns it now? Tank? Violent B? I think it's Violent B. Or maybe it's Austin Powers. See, this is the fun thing about this belt. Anybody can win it, at any given time. It's the most contested belt on the long list. So, the second division to build up will be the Blood Money title! Daily: Nice plan so far. Shirley: These men are geniuses ya know? Da Mab: No, no, I'm not done yet! Ladies and gentlemen, please welcome Miss Sarah Jennifer Smith. Da Mab directs his attention to the entranceway as a beautiful young woman with black hair, strapped in a golf shirt and blue jeans makes her way down the ramp carrying a concealed item. Da Mab: Come on up here Sarah. Sarah slides into the ring and hands Da Mab the item, still concealed by the black cover. Sarah smiles and kisses Da Mab on the cheek. She leaves, bending over to do so. Da Mab: Uh-uh, gentlemen. Eyes over here. She's taken. This however is not! Da Mab reveals the beautiful item underneath the black sheet. It is a title of some sort. Da Mab: Confused? You don't remember this? Ladies and gentlemen, the TOP DOLLAR TITLE is BACK! Daily: Unbelievable! Shirley: This is a great show and we've hardly gone 15 minutes! Da Mab: That's right. The cruisers get a chance to show what they're made of...FINALLY! But no, no, no, that's not at all! Shirley: It's not? Daily: What is possibly left? Da Mab: You see, the same faces are coming on TV. So, BMW is making a fresh start. And to commemorate this fresh start, we're hiring new wrestlers. And who leads this corps of wrestlers? I believe you have met him at Money, Power, Respect. Explosions appear at the entrance area, and suddenly, a man appears. The fans cheer wildly. Daily: That's the guy that saved Tax's ass last night!!! Da Mab: This is The British Bomber! XWW and FAW World Heavyweight Champion! Daily: Unbelievable! WHAT ELSE?! The British Bomber rolls in the ring and grabs the microphone. Bomber: Ahaha! I'm finally here in Big Money Wrestling! I'm here as the law of BMW. You know, I hate to see stars get mistreated. So, that's the answer to the all-important question. I saw Tax get pummeled. He gave his life to BMW. He finally, FINALLY won that World title, and the 2GLS just ruined it for him. That's why I came in. I wanted to see Tax celebrate the biggest night of his life! I'm in this to make BMW better and successful. The lights immediately turn out but quickly fades to green as 'Greed' by Godsmack blasts over the sound system. Tax steps forward with his BMW's Best t-shirt and the World Title strapped over his shoulder. The fans cheer as he walks down to ringside. He climbs to the ring apron and sturdily steps in. He asks for The British Bomber to give him a microphone. He obeys. Tax: Listen British Bomber. I've been strutting around with this World title over my shoulder ever since I won it. I've been thinking. And DRINKING! The fans cheer. Tax: And I've finally thought up two words during the time that most people sleep. And I'm finally figured those two words out! And I wanna tell you face to face, man to man, to show that I'm not a coward like the 2GLS, I wanna say.......thank you. You see the truth is, you saved my ass. I'm not afraid to admit it. Tax extends his hands in a thank you handshake. The British Bomber looks at it, and takes it, proudly. Tax smiles and releases his hand. He tosses the mic to the ground, and begins to leave. Donomite quickly grabs it, and stops Tax from leaving. Donomite: Hey, wait a minute. The fans want to see it. I want to see it. Tax, would you do us the honor of being in our new little group? The fans cheer once again! Tax: I'll think about Dono. I'll think about it. The fans cheer once again as 'Greed' by Godsmack hits. The World Champ leaves the ring and walks up the ramp. Donomite, Da Mab, and The British Bomber follow behind him, raising their hands and allowing the fans to cheer on wildly. Shirley: Sheesh....twenty minutes into the show and I'm about to blow a pacemaker. Daily: You don't have a pacemaker. Shirley: Why do you always have to correct me?! I mean, I tried to be nice, and I tried to go along with you this time!
