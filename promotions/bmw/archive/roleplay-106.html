Written: 8-12-02 Location: Cleveland, OH.......at the Gund Arena site. [scene opens with the 'Nigerian Nightmare' Jon Mobootu entering the Gund Arena two days prior to the up in coming Withdrawn. There are people in the hallway seating down talking as Jon Mobootu walks in. Jon is wearing a blueish color silk shirt with black baggy pants. Jon has on a pair of sunglasses.] (jon walks into the building and is headed for the arena area. There is a group of people huddled around a table as others are putting the ring together. One of the guys stops jon and begans to speak to him........) Man: Excuse me, Can I help you? Jon: Nope I am just here to check out the area. Man: Is this your first time here in Cleveland? Jon: Yes, it is. I have a match here in a few days. And I am looking for someone. Man: o ya who? Jon: a guy named Balboa, do you know who he is....... Man: I have heard of him but I don.t know him like we were buddies. Jon: Have you seen him in the last couple of days? Man: No I haven't but he is scheduled to be here for a match. Jon: ya I know that but I wanted to catch up with him before his match. Well I am going to check out the ring area (jon gos and see a couple of camera guys setting up their cameras and other wires.) Camera guy: Hey Mobootu, Did you lose something? (camera guy is laughing out loud as the other camera man looks puzzled at him and Mobootu. Jon grabs the camera man and gives him the Sweet Dreams. Which is the claw to the face. Then jon points at the camera man to grab the camera and start recording. The camera comes on........) Jon: Look at this.......... poor piece of trash who thought being funny would earn them a warm spot in my heart. Well the fact is I do not have a warm spot in my heart. Espeacially when it comes to fool like this. (jon squeezes the mans face harder before leting go and smiling at his hand.) Jon: Just like I took 'B' to the another level. Like I said I would.....but to the Power that Be I did not come back with the gold. Well I will Not cry about some spilled milk. But What I will GO to the ends of the WORLD for my ZummBaW GOD.......You have just started something that you and all the help you can get can't stop......So Balboa I will see you soon, watch your back........because ZummBaW is calling me and I will have you repent to ZummBaW and to your soul I will take. Speaking of taking........ (jon looks up at the camera and tells them to come closer....) Jon: Now I have a hardcore match with this Robert Thompson........Well I will not talk trash but Robert be aware that I have alot of things on my mind and if you think I will over look you then you are wrong.......I look forward to seeing you soon. (jon looks down at the guy who is holding his head in pain and is trying to stand up....jon walks out of the arena area) [scene fades] View Biography
