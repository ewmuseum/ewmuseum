All the paperwork is complete, and the sites and schedules have been released. 'Slugfest' is set to come out in a few months. Meanwhile, Tax is still concentrated on Big Money Wrestling. His World Championship reign is being threatened to be put to an end. Tax, however, is more focused on other things. For those not caught up with the Tax saga, he was taken and drugged during the first week of BMW version 3. Since then, and throughout the Lethal Lottery and Money, Power, Respect, he has had difficulties, heading back in time to different places. Now, he has defeated the system, and he's ready to search for justice. He's going to find the man who started this....he will find Joe Richmond.* Man on a Mission (Episode Two) 'A Name, A Nemesis' Cleveland, Ohio, United States of America 'Dyno-Mite Auto Parts'. The sign lingers on the grass, with the arrow pointing towards an old building. The building is painted, although the yellow paleness is peeling from it. An '82 Monte Carlo slowly pulls into the lot, and parks beside a green Pinto. The door opens, and the BMW World Champion, Tax, steps out. He takes off his Oakley sunglasses, and looks at the place. He tucks the sunglasses on the neck of his 'Cleveland Indians' t-shirt. He steps slowly inside the store, his blue jeans well-fitted around his lowerbody. He looks around at the row of shelves holding auto parts. He looks to his right and sees a desk in front of a few huge rows of the bigger auto parts. He slowly walks forward to the desk behind a skinny 20-something man who is talking with the attendant. Attendant: Ok, listen, we don't have the brake pads for our Toyota. We only had a couple in stock, and we sold the last one about three days ago. Now, if you want to wait, we're getting a new shipment tomorrow. Customer: Are you deaf man?! Did you understand nothing that I just said? I'm leaving for Miami Beach this afternoon. I just came in to get the brake pads, put them on, and go on my way. Attendant: Well, we don't have it sir. A second attendant steps to the side, to another cashier machine. He looks at Tax. Attendant 2: Can I help you, sir? Tax steps over but can't help but overhear the petty whining the customer is doing to the first attendant. Customer: Listen, I have to leave this afternoon. There's a big party tomorrow night, and I want to be there relaxed. Tax: You know..stuff like this pisses me off. How often do you guys get whiny sons of bitches like this in here? Customer: Excuse me, are you talking about me? Tax: You're the only that's been whining so far. Customer: Whining? Listen, boy, I make 3,000 dollars a month. I'm going to Miami for a huge party with a very big friend. I can't go because these guys don't have a damn brake pad for my damn Toyota. I have the right to complain! Tax: 3,000 dollars a month, eh? That gives you the right to complain? Because you're rich? Customer: You're damn right you piece of trailer park trash. Tax's cocky smile disappears. His eyebrows lower, angrily. He grabs the customer by his Ralph Lauren shirt and pushes him against the counter. Tax: Guess, what?! Have you heard of a place called Rockford?! Have you heard of Baker Place?! That's where I grew up! That place is the Projects, and it's more dangerous than any place you've been. But guess what? I survived that. I survived the trailers, the violence, the broken down homes, the drugs, everything! And guess how much I make a damn week? Customer: Probably less then 3,000 dollars. Tax pulls the customer over, and punches him in the face. The customer falls back into a display of Castrol Motor Oil. Tax: 250,000 a week, punk. He turns back to the second attendant. Tax: Is Dyno here? Attendant: Yeah man, what you need? Tax: I need to speak with him. Uh...uh, Jimmy Wade referred me. Attendant: Ah, of course. Let me find him. The attendant picks up the phone and punches in a couple of numbers. Attendant: JW. Dyno Morgan. Now. 805103. He hangs the phone up, and directs his attention back to Tax. Attendant: You see that door back there....the one with the yellow strip on it. Tax: Yeah. Attendant: Go through there...walk down the office, and step in. Take a seat and he'll be there momentarily. Tax: Thank you. Tax follows the attendant's directions and opens the big heavy doors. It makes a path along red carpet in a narrow strip. Tax follows straight ahead and enters the only door available. He steps inside and finds himself in an office. Fancy illumination lights up the paintings on the wall. Tax steps behind the desk and makes himself comfortable in a leather chair. The speaker on the desk suddenly livens with static. Words are formed. Speaker: Hello, sir, Mr. Morgan will be with you momentarily. Tax, who had moved into a fighting stance when the speaker came on, now relaxes in the chair. He looks around at his enviroment; the computer, the papers, the file cabinet, and countless other office stuff. The door slowly opens and a man dressed in suit and tie steps forward. He frowns at the fact that this scoundrel is occupying his seat. Morgan: Ahem...excuse me, but I believe that seat is mine. Tax: Are you Dyno Morgan? Morgan: Who's asking? Tax: Are you Dyno Morgan?! Morgan: Yes, I am. Tax: Thank you. Joe Richmond, what do you know about him? Morgan: I know he's new on the streets. He's a traveler. We call him the nomad scientist. He's got a lot of drugs and stuff he makes...fuses it in his own travel lab. Some of it is new, and they give off effects. Tax: I see. Where can I find him? Morgan: I don't know. Richmond came in from Buffalo, I believe. He started off on the higher east, than I got reports that he was on the southeast plains. The last word I got was an hour ago...he was at lower west. Tax: You think he's still there? Morgan: No. How much can I trust you? Tax: About as much as you trust your gun. Morgan: Good. Listen up...you aren't blue are you? Tax: No, I'm not a cop. Morgan: I'm setting up a meeting with Joe's guys approximately 45 to 50 minutes from now. If you'd like, I can introduce you. Tax: He knows my face. Morgan: No matter. You'll be the secretive. Tax: Secretive, eh? I'll get to confront this Richmond guy? Morgan: Yeah, but you have to let my boys get out with the goods first. Tax: Fine. Let's talk. Tax rises and shakes Dyno's hand. The scene fades as they begin to go over their plan. In Memory: Chick Hearn View Biography
