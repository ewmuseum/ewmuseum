Written: 19 August 2002 Location: A broken down street The scene opens up in what looks to be a street in the middle of a suburb. Most of the houses have boarded up windows and there are empty crisp packets and sweet wrappers everywhere. In the middle of the road, which has too many holes in it to count, stand “The Violence” Vince Jones, Raymond Mercy and everyone’s favourite BMW-ite, Risk! Raymond: Sup playas? Risk: I am just thinking about something. Raymond: Thinkin’ bout what? Risk: I am thinking about Withdrawn on Thursday. Vince: Withdrawn is on Thursday? Raymond: Sup wit dat? Risk: Don’t you guys keep up with anything? Raymond: Withdrawn is on Wednesdays foo. Risk: No, we are on Thursday this week. Vince: We are? Risk: Yes, we are. But, that’s not what I am thinking about. Raymond: What you thinking bout then? Risk: I am thinking about why I have to face Billy Blaze…again. Raymond: You know you can beat him man. Vince: Fo sho. Risk: We have already had three matches. Vince: Go on… Risk: Out of those three matches Billy Blaze has won absolutely zero, zilch, none. Raymond: Really? Risk: Yes, three matches against him and I have never, ever lost. Vince: That is some achievement. Risk: Yeah, I know…and that was when he was good. Vince: Hahahaha. Raymond: Was he ever good? Risk: Well, he was never good enough to beat me but he was a champion. He was the champion. Raymond: Well…that counts for something doesn’t it? Risk: It means something, yes. It means he never came across me when he got the gold. Vince: Fo sho. Risk: What is up with Billy Blaze at the moment anyway? Vince: What ya mean? Risk: Why is he acting so weird? Raymond: He has always been like this man. Risk: Damn. I keep forgetting that you guys came to Big Money Wrestling as soon as he became even weirder than usual. Vince: What? He was weird before he became weird? Risk: I see what you are saying…I think. Yes. He has always been weird. He has always gone around saying “Repent and be saved” and the likes. To me, he is just an annoying frig. Vince: Yeah, I have never liked that son of a bitch either. Raymond: Fo sho. I dunna why he even has a job in da company. Risk: Pizza anyone? Raymond: Yeah man, I am starving. Vince: Yeah, so long as your payin. The three men walk off down the road and into the darkness ahead, looking for a shop which sells pizza. The scene then fades to black. View Biography
