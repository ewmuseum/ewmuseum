News: FedWars / Big 3 iPPV Cancelled

With that, several superstars were having to get a plane back to Chicago, Illinois for Withdrawn. The list includes the World Champion Tax, and the Million Dollar Champion, Da Thug among others. Tax was reportedly upset that he could not contribute to BMW during this time period. He was upset that it was a few days wasted. Da Thug could not be reached for comment. "If those FedWars suckers are gonna hold something like that, they need to follow through with it! Instead they wasted my time, they wasted BMW's time, and they wasted money. Am I pissed? Ya damn right, I'm pissed," said Tax, following the release of the news. Sunday, August 18, 2002 @ Violence, Violence! Chicago, Illinois ---BMW-ites who have watched the program for the last few shows have noticed a more hardcore technique with much attention centered around Sudden Violence. It seems as though BMW is taking the technique leading up to the most hardcore pay per view of the year, Hard Knox. Many fans enjoy the chaos, but the confusion is undeniable. Many fans don't even know who the Blood Money Champion is! In fact, in a recent poll, only 27% of the people knew who held the belt. Violent B is the holder of the belt. Jetlag Worries? Chicago, Illinois ---It seems as though many BMW superstars are a little worried about the upcoming schedule. Why, you ask? BMW is going on a treacherous tour in order to reach the Japanese destination for Hard Knox. Many wrestlers are worried that their performance will go down due to jetlag. BMW flies from Illinois, to Minnesota, to Colorado, to California, and then overseas to Japan. One must understand the wrestlers' complaints, but the schedule stands. "It's the only way to get to Japan. I wish we had less area to travel, but I can't help it. It's already booked, and it's final," said Louis Miranda. Monday, August 12, 2002 @ J-Man, Strong Efforts Miami, FL--- As seen on Withdrawn, BMW superstar J-Man declared war on Louis Miranda and all his supporters. The "Real Deal's" offensive against the BMW President was to set up a petition demanding Mr. Miranda's immediate resignation. It was reported that J-Man campaigned for signatures outside the offices of BMW HQ. However, the response to J-Man's mission was very poor. We're led to believe that by office closing time on Friday evening J-Man's petition had obtained all of one signature; that being the signature of Jim Townsend - an employee of the "Real Deal." With this attempt to undermine Louis Miranda's authority destined for failure, we're left to wonder what J-Man will try next. Sunday, August 11, 2002 @ Bobby Angel, Proverb Bobby Angel made his BMW debut on Withdrawn attacking "The Violence" Vince Jones until Risk and The Violence pummeled him. Sunset Killa came to Angel's aid but Bobby Angel had this to say about Risk and The Violence. Bobby Angel: "Proverbs 17:23 states that wicked men pervert the ways of judgment.I'm here in BMW to end the perversion of wickedness by passing my own judgment... And judgment has been stated, as of now, that I will be the death of those two fools yet!" Bobby Angel then hugged a few officials before making a necklace out of Razors. Violent B's Temper This past Withdrawn, Da Thug attacked Sudden Violence, and directed most of his attack at Violent B. News has it that Violent B is furious. Violent B was seen after the attack, punching holes in walls, throwing furniture, swearing, and breaking everything in sight. According to an eye witness, Paranoia tried to stop Violent B's rampage, and got punched in the eye for his trouble. Jake Cortez caught up with Violent B after the tantrum, and got some thoughts. Jake Cortez: Violent B, Da Thug attacked you and the rest of Sudden Violence earlier tonight. What are your thoughts on that? Violent B: WHAT THE HELL DO YOU THINK MY THOUGHTS ARE!? Violent B then punched Jake Cortez in the jaw, and knocked him unconscious, and left the arena in a storm. Friday, August 09, 2002 @ Blaze's Lawsuit Update Cleveland, Ohio--- Recently in Big Money Wrestling, Billy Blaze was reportedly making an attempt to sue Big Money Wrestling for the loss of his World Championship. I have done a follow up research on this situation and it turns out that he is no longer carrying the lawsuit out, all charges/attempts have been dropped and Blaze will continue with his every-day life. Da Thug is Charged! New York, New York--- Ladies and gentleman, this is the follow up on the first story I wrote about BMW superstar, Da Thug. As you all know, yes, he was arrested for the murder of a female and two males, all adults. But there is yet another twist on this shocking chain of events. Now, Da Thug is being charged with triple homicide, and a count of rape to the female who was supposedly murdered. Da Thug has been in prison for the last few days, but he will be released for his match at Withdrawn, then sadly for him, he will be locked up. Security and police will be following him every second he is out of his jail cell. The only reason he's getting the chance to wrestle is because Mr. Miranda reportedly payed the police station off...If he could pay them off that much, is it possible to get Thug out of this situation entirely? Da Thug's court date is set for this upcoming Friday, August 16. More to be posted soon. Fedwars' "Big 3"! Manhattan, New York--- You live long enough, you love the sport hard enough and then you realize that for every moment -- every milestone ... every kind of athletic drama that can be frozen in time to return with the joy of memory ... there is a voice. This month, a voice will speak out as Mr. Batee, FedWars President along with CEO''s David Jacobs & Louis Miranda proudly presents our Summer iPay Per View entitled: BIG 3! Lord of the Ring! Sometimes, if the contest were important enough, the voice whistles through the air like an exclaimation point, its timeless immedicay making of your memory a bookmark of times when we all were young. "It''s time for FedWars to get off the canvas and show why we are one of the best in the business," says Mr. Batee. "Memories are great but we are building the future and that requires us to make new highlites. The old timers tend to fixate on the past. The past is great but I''m here to create a future for FedWars! So don''t let anyone fool you! We are better than ever, and it''s that damn simple. Come Big 3, we will cut through all the bullshit and get right down to business. Take a look at our card you tell me... are we lacking in anything?" Wednesday, August 07, 2002 @ Da Thug, A Murderer? Harlem, New York--- You read correctly! Big Money Wrestling superstar, Da Thug, has just been caught by police after a high speed chase down many city streets and highways located in his hometown of Harlem, New York. Details are very blurry at the moment, but we do know that the NYPD wants and placed Da Thug under arrest for the murder of one male and two females. It's said that Thug went as far as stalking the females and had the murder planned out, we're not sure about the male. More details will be given off in this situation as time progresses. We'll try to keep you updated daily. If Thug is guilty, this is guarunteed to be the end of his wrestling career. Stay Posted.
