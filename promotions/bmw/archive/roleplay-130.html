Continued- | I sat in a cold corner watching the white padded walls seem as if they were closing in on me. When my mouth was shut, my thoughts grew louding. Screaming at me to do another wrong. The lights went out and I was there all alone. Followed by the AC they had supplied me with, was replaced with heat and I started to become woozy again. There was no escape, destiny picked the wrong day to remember me. Fate wanted the end... so did I. Someone sled the metal piece covering a peep hole as a familiar voice reigned in sweet tone. Light split through the blackness like a jagged knife. | 'This is only for your own good. If it wasn't for me, you would be dead by now. The Insane Asylum is the better place for you now.' The words she spoke simply of reminded me who this evil woman was... 'I will kill you mother!' The metal piece was sled back infront of the peep hole just as soon as it openned... My mind ran on and on, ' I can't remember a time when I wasn't alone, even when my parent were around. The sound of their fury was harsh, but the sound of their silence was deafining. It's devastation that left me buried beneath a mountain of solitude. My lonliness and despair traped me in a storm of unimaginable emptiness. What about me? The other kids playing ball outside, and me with them was am illusion I'd allow myself, but the stark reality was even if my parents would of allowed me to leave my bedroom dwell, the other kids wouldn't of allowed me to join in anyway. They said I didn't follow thier rules. Their stupid rules... ' | Or that may have been the program in my head speaking. It all confused me. Why me? Why has fate chosen my destiny of ruin? Do I deserve all this. As if fate answered me, memories of pictures flashed in my ill head of all the people I have killed. It wasn't my fault! They made me do it! Commotion ran on outside of people being killed left and right. I kept my position in the corner awaiting the evil to enter. The door creep open and a man wearing all black stood there. He was the man that knocked me out in the snow, he was the reason for my ill fortune! | 'How dare you! Why didn't you let me die! I belong here! The world isn't ready for me, it will never be will all those close minded idiots continuing their lifes forgetting more and more and those that were unthoughtfully mentioned!' It made me furious as I began to rise up. 'I got answers. Why you have Rockweiler and Sal only in your head. Why you feel you must make a destination to a place called BMW. Kill them! Finish their pathetic souls and relinquish to hell where they will burn in hell for EVER forgetting the people greater then them! Do it, and I will give you back your life.' The words he spoke were understandable. I got the message. I've killed before, what's more blood on my already dirty hands? It was a job that could be done without a problem. 'Don't kill Pola Bare...' I was stopped dead in my plan for their annihilation. 'Why' I replied back with perplexity. 'Wait and see... I got further plans for him. He will be kept alive until it happens.' Somehow I understood what he meant. It was starting to make sense so I trusted him. If he could give me back my life, he was worth trusting. 'They will suffer unimaginable pain. Not even god himself can undo the wrong I will commit on their forsaken souls! After the beating I will deliver, even their inner soul will leave the outer shell they call a body, making them empty. They will feel what I feel and that is a promise!' My eyes turned white as white, eerie, cold smoke filled the room. After setting, I was no where to be found. -Empty Shell- | Earth will die, Hell will freeze. The grave yard I stood before. No other place meant more than a grave yard. It was the only thing that broke the line of the real and the unreal. Looking around coldly, I felt I was finally home. But as I looked around all the tombstones began to read 'Itagaki Freeze.' Is that why my life is over?! Was I already dead?! ...to be continued. | View Biography
