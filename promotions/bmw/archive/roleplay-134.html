Written: 08/20/2002 Location: The Violence's Dressing Room [The camera fades into a hallway outside the dressing room of 'The Violence' Vince Jones just a few minutes after his loss to The Rockweiler. From the sounds of the yelling and the sounds of furniture being chucked every which way in the room it doesn't seem like 'The Violence' Vince Jones is in the best of moods. All of a sudden the door of the locker room opens up, Jasmine comes rushing out in fear, and slams the door behind her. From within the room The Violence can be heard yelling.] The Violence: AND STAY OUT!!! [Jasmine turns towards the door.] Jasmine: But I'm sorry. I know I shouldn't have mentioned the loss. The Violence: SHUT THE HELL UP AND LEAVE ME ALONE!! [She turns away and slowly walks down the hall and runs into Lance Sterling, one of BMW's most prominent representatives, who has a big smile on his face as she passes by.] Lance Sterling: Rough night? [Jasmine glares at him.] Jasmine: You again!? If I were you I wouldn't plan on stirring up anything with my man right now. He's in a foul mood and doing so might cost you an arm and a leg tonight. Lance Sterling: Me, stir up trouble with 'The Violence' Vince Jones? No way. I wouldn't do that. I just wanted to see how the man's doing after his first loss in Big Money Wrestling. [Jasmine shoves past Lance Sterling as he laughs outloud and makes his way down the hall to The Violence's dressing room. He pulls out a key, unlocks the door, and makes his way inside. 'The Violence' Vince Jones can be seen sitting down on a metal folding chair holding his head and literally still boiling with rage and frustration over his loss to The Rockweiler. Lance Sterling just shakes his head and laughs to himself.] Lance Sterling: Well, tough break out there, Mr. Jones. You were really lookin' good out there for awhile. I have to hand it to ya. Not many can say they've rocked Rockweiler like you manage to do... [The Violence cuts in with a low anger-filled tone.] The Violence: The Violence ain't in the mood to talk to ya b**ch! Now get the hell out! Lance Sterling: Whoooooa. Now we don't need the hostility. Don't go off on me because you dropped the ball in the ring tonight. You claim you're the man that's on top of things in Big Money Wrestling!? Well, what the hell happened out there tonight, big man!? [Lance Sterling laughs outloud and leans against the doorway of the dressing room.] Lance Sterling: Big Money Wrestling's One Man Dynasty was...silenced. Isn't that what you always like to say? [The Violence quickly rises to his feet and rips the dark shades from his face and slams them on the floor and just glares a hole in Lance.] The Violence: Ya know somethin'!? Maybe The Violence did lose tonight, but you think I could care less about that right now!? NO! You think my mind was on that b**ch, Rockweiler, out there!? Hell no! The only man on my mind right now is Paranoia, the man that's been prancin' his happy ass around Big Money Wrestling with the Cash Money Title around his damn waist for way too long! I came into this company sayin' that your champs were gonna be bowin' at my feet! The first of 'em to step to me, toe-to-toe's gonna end up gettin' lit up and smoked out! Now enough about tonight! You better be in here ready to say that 'The Violence' Vince Jones has got his Cash Money title shot! [Lance shakes his head.] Lance Sterling: Well, I see you're pretty fired up. Hope you can manage to maintain that same intensity when you face your opponent...Da Thug at this upcoming Withdrawn in a non-title street fight. Good luck you're gonna need... [The Violence rushes Lance Sterling and slams him against the wall in fury.] The Violence: Maybe you didn't hear me the first time I said it! 'The Violence' Vince Jones is lookin' for some BMW gold to sit around his waist! Get it!? Got it!? Good! Change the match! [Lance begins to gasp for air.] Lance Sterling: Release me...or...you're out of...BMW. [The Violence glares at Lance and slowly releases his death grip on him. Lance slowly regains his composure.] Lance Sterling: Good luck, pal. You're gonna need it. [Lance Sterling leaves the room and The Violence slams the door upon his exit and begins pacing around the room.] The Violence: So The Violence takes on Da Thug! What kinda s**t is that!? These BMW cats gettin' worried about givin' The Violence his shot at their gold!? [The Violence stops and slams his fist against the wall in fury.] The Violence: Well, they can let their champs hide in the corners and hide behind non-title matches all they want, but sooner or later the gold's comin' home! [The Violence looks up.] The Violence: Vachon, don't worry. The Violence gonna be wearin' the gold you were never given the chance to wear in due time! Until then, I'll wipe the floor with each and every punk ass BMW throws in my way! [The Violence bows his head.] The Violence: Rest in peace, Vachon. Rest in peace. [The camera slowly fades to black.] View Biography
