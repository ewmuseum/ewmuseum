Suddenly a small click is heard and a television screen quickly illuminates the room. The TV is a big screen and various pieces of furniture are seen scattered across the room. Pola Bare is also seen sitting down on a couch, his attire the same as usual, however his shirt cannot be read. Pola Bare pushes another button as a tape begins to play. Pola Bare watches the screen intently as footage from a recent BMW event begins to play. Pola Bare slowly lowers his head in disappointment for a few moments. However he quickly turns his attention back to the television as his last match starts to play. Television: Daily: A Pola Bare suit. I mean a guy wearing a suit kicking the hell out of J-Man is unpredictable! That move he just pulled off the top rope is called Air Barbie haha Pola Bare shakes his head in disgust. Pola Bare: Unpredictable? Who couldn't beat the real deal. Pola Bare watches the television as J-Man barely manages to kick out on a two count. Pola Bare lets the remote fall from his hand. Pola Bare: It should have been over right there. No questions, no excuses. What was I thinking, how could I have let him kick out? How could I let him barely crawl out of defeat? On a normal day the match would have ended right there. But I just had to lose. I didn't prepare adequately and I slipped. I choked, and now I have a number on my loss record. Losing to someone that you know you could get a win over, it is not a good feeling. I have no one to blame but myself, I can't blame it on a ref, on interference, just me, Pola Bare. But you know what they say, learn from your losses. I've certainly learned a few lessons, for one there's no way I'm walking into a withdrawn unprepared. I will not be caught offguard, I will not lose to a loser. I'm not going to lose to these idiots they've booked me against, even if I have to go through each and every one of those wrestlers I will not lose. If I get pinned in this match, if I get screwed, I'd hate to be the person that did it. I'm not sure if I'll be able to deal with it. Speaking of that if I even hear Gay-Man bragging about beating Pola Bare, especially after he was scratching and scraping at the very last minute to beat me, I'll drop him like I should have done a week ago. (Television- Daily: 1......2..... 3. J-Man wins! Shirley: With the J-Driver. This man is just too good. ) Pola Bare: That isn't going to happen again. I will not be slapped around by sally. Pola Bare shuts the tape off and turns a lamp on, bringing light back into the room. Pola Bare: This week they've just decided to throw me into a match of unimportance. Just slopping people together, and I just happened to have end up with 3 of the biggest idiots on the roster. Sensational Sally, Iwanky Please, Lassie?! It's not even worth my time. If I lose this match I might as well just hang up my boots. If I loose to these three I might as well just stop wrestling and take my mascot outfit elsewhere. But ti's not going to happen, I refuse to lose, and if I do, it's not going to end. I will not rest until I have beaten all three of them down in some shape or form. First there's this Sensational Sally guy. What are you thirteen years old?! I've seen third graders who are more feared then you. You walk around hiding behind your lack of humor, behind your petty little insults, then you dare to think that I'm going to just let you pin me? You think that you can take me? You were right when you said I dressed up in a polar bear costume to be different, not for the attention though. Honestly would I want someone to confuse me with you?! What if I were walking down the street and some little kid walked up and asked me if I was the sensual Sally. Neglecting the fact that you don't have any fans. Better yet, what if some gay bashers on the street got confused and kicked me around? You want to talk about putting on a monkey mask? Look in the mirror you look like a frickin' ape, you talk like one. Your promos make me want to fall asleep, they make me want to masturbate with metallic gloves. All I hear is ooga booga, grunt snort drool. Am I a petafile? What the heck is that supposed to mean!? You're like a first grader asking classmates if they hare hetermomosexual or not. It simple does not make sense. Just like you winning anything this week. You've already lost to rockweiler, what does that say about you? Carlton the Bear..tsk tsk. How dare a man named Sally even attempt to insult me. Especially when you suck so horribly. Let this week serve as an eye opener to you, after the match, just throw your wrestling gear away. You're career is going nowhere, and it doesn't help that you just plain suck. There's no ifs, ands or buts about it, you are just a little child in a grown man's body. Week in and week out you come with your lame sense of humor trying to impress, yet all you do is depress anyone that has to sit through your crap. Mush less wrestle you. Pola Bare gets up. He presses the eject button on the vcr and pulls out the tape that he was watching earlier. He throws it carelessly to the side. Pola Bare then hears a knock at the door and he goes to answer. He opens the door to see a man there. An old man, who looks rather unintelligent and rather dirty. The man smiles at Pola Bare as Pola looks at him trying to determine the reason that he's there. Man at Door: Is this the house of the famous Yogi Bear. Pola Bare: Yogi Bare, that's a car.. The man abruptly cuts Pola Bare off. Man at Door: Silly Willy! Yogi doesn't drive a car! Pola Bare just stares at the man not knowing what to say. Pola Bare: Um... Told ya! Man at Door: Are you the yogi bear? You look so much different in the cartoons. Pola Bare: That's probably because I'm not the bear from the cartoons. Man at Door: Then where else would he be? I know for a fact he lives around here somewhere. Pola Bare: I think you've got the wrong house. Man at Door: I doubt it. I know it's you, you silly little bear. Hey can you say 'Hey I'm Yogi Bear.' Can you say that for me it'd make my day. I love that. It makes me cream my jeans. Pola Bare: You've got to be kidding me. You get off watching Yogi bear?! Man at Door: Hey! I don't get mad at you for getting a little action with those crazy picnickers. Pola Bare: I'm just going to shut this door. Man at Door: Wait look at this real quick! The man pulls out a picnic basket. He practically forces Pola Bare to take a glance at it. Man at Door: Look Yogi! It's a picnic basket. Yummy The man then reaches back and swings with the picnic basic. He nails Pola Bare on the side of the face with it. Pola Bare doesn't strike back, rather he just shuts the door. The man at the door can still be heard talking. Man at Door: That crazy Yogi, he's always going for the picnic baskets. Where's the forest ranger when you need him? Pola Bare looks confused as he walks back into his house. Pola Bare: What's up with these idiots. Getting me confused with Carltons, and pandas and yogi. If I didn't know any better I'd say that could have been that sally guy himself. Pola Bare turns around and opens the door to check, however the guy is now gone. Pola shuts the door and goes back to sit down. Pola Bare: Oh well, I'll get my chance. Just like I'll get my chance with this Ibetyou'll sneeze, wankMe pleeze, Iknowmy abc's guy. Honestly if you've seen one of his promo's you'll know what I mean. 'Hello my name is Ismoke weeds, I was altered by genetic experiments and now I have super powers. I mean look at me, I can get whack off with my own mouth.' Or he'll say, 'Look it's my father! I wonder if he'll give me change for the candy machine.' It's pathetic, like reading a diary of some lunatic. This guy, though all of his trials and tribulations has forgotten one thing. He has to step into a wrestling ring. He doesn't seem to even be aware of the fact he is in BMW. I very precarious situation if you ask me. You can't just spend you time making snow angels in the woods and expect to come into the ring and go toe to toe with people that know what they are doing. You can't focus on what you ate for breakfast and the last time you got a measles shot and expect it to be enough to overcome me. What are you Ismelllike cheeze? Are you some kind of experiment, 'maybe if we let this guy out of the institute he may be able to wrestle.' I'm not afraid though. I don't care who you are, who you've killed or the fact that you can count your years of schooling one nutsack. The fact is that I'm not going to lose to you, so just quit while your ahead. The suspense isn't killing me, don't even bother telling me about the magical reunification of that 'special' bond between father and son. Don't even bother showing up, I'm sure even sensational sally could deal with you. Just shut up or get a clue, remember that we are in wrestling, this isn't the magic story telling hour. So get wrestling on your mind, or you'll find wrestling's feet across the side of your head. And last we have Lassie, or the rockweiler. Some big guy who goes around acting like a dog. When we get into the ring what are you possibly going to do to me? Will you pee on me and mark your territory? Are you going to bite? Maybe you'll start barking and try to scare me away. Or you might just drool and hump my leg, a habit you seem accustomed to. You try to make yourself look like some respectable guy who has it all figured out. Eating at nice restaurants, sitting back watching television in your house. But you don't. I caught your promo, your little visions of how things will play out. You think I'm going to go after Sally, or better yet Mr. Freezeit. You're wrong. I don't care about recognition. Who cares about who's established and who isn't. I'm coming after all three of you equally. What did you think? I was going to just walk into the match trying to take down sally 'to establish myself,' and that I'd 'try to go after Freeze,' while you just sat back and waited for the whole thing unfold. Anyway you slice it, If I win I'm the man who beat all three of you, and besides, I couldn't care less which one of you is established. All I know is that I'm not going to lose to lassie, you'll be to busy trying to save freeze from the burning flames that will be his defeat. And while you're being man's best friend, in all forms of the phrase, I'll be busy acting like a vet. I'm going to put you to sleep. You think that little submission you did to Sally was something, just wait until you feel the Bare's claw. Now I know this means nothing to the three of you. But just know that I'm going to walk into withdrawn intending to win. I'll be Itagislaki's estranged father if I let any of you stand in my way. Pola Bare then looks back at his television. It is on a wrestling channel of sorts where and old match between him and The Violence Vince Jones is being played. Pola Bare looks back on this match as he sees him defeat the violence, back in 360 wrestling. Now feeling a little more content, he happily relaxes as the scene comes to a close. Think about ti, if he can beat one of BMW's best, what makes you think he can't beat his opponents this week. View Biography
