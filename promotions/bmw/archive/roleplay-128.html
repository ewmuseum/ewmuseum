Scoring off of Sal? The hell?!? Written: Aug.20.02 Location: In a sports bar. §§§ SCENE BEGINS §§§ ~~~ Scene opens up inside a sports bar. Sal is having a few drinks. He lays back on the chair thinking of what has been said over the last couple of days. He turns to the camera. ~~~ §§: I once thought that I had the biggest mouth in BMW. I once thought that I talked the most shit. Looks like I've been outdone. Now normally I wouldn't mind. If a guy can talk good trash, then a guy can talk good trash but how can I take a guy who wears a mask seriously? What the hell is Pola Bare trying to be? Oh yeah, forgot, 'different'. Well you're not. You talk about how I'm thirteen years old because I make childish insults yet you're the one running around in a bear costume! You're the one using toys in your matches!! Yeah, everyone is boring except you Bare. Everyone sucks ass except you. Sure, and Oprah will stay thin this time! Why do you wear the costume? And don't say because you want to be different, tell me why you're REALLY wearing the damn thing! Could it be because you have no self-essteam? Are you ashamed of yourself? You suck that much that you NEED a costume to tell you apart from the rest of the jobbers? Hey, I got no problem with someone trying to be different, in fact I applaud it, but really now, this is childish and sad. I may act like a kid myself sometimes, with my insults, but you're doing the same god-damn thing. In fact, you're worse than me. Not only do you dress up in a bear costume, automatically ruining any shred of respect anyone would have for you, but you go around making hypocritical comments! My career is going nowhere? Damn, you're right 'bout that one. But the time will come when you'll see my ugly ape face with the BMW title and your face will still be hiding under that damn mask while you go around parading like Mickey Mouse with Minnie stuck up his ass! It's not the insults that piss me off it's the guy who's telling them. How can I let you punk me out? I might as well hang up the boots as you said! But I won't and here's the reason. You see, you may have heard what I've got to say but you have no idea what I'm capable of doing. My return HAS been a failure. I am nowhere near the top of my game. Even before BMW re-opended, I never really found my niche. I was starting to, but then BMW closed down and now, now I'm stuck wrestling a genetic popsicle, Suckweiler and a guy running around in a bear costume. But hey, Pola, if you're any good in the ring I can look past your costume. If you can hold up in the ring with the likes of me, then I won't bother you about your odd lifestyle. But I don't think you can. I AM the best here in BMW. I AM the best in North America. I AM the most technically sound wrestler EVER! I talk alot of shit, yes. I overlook quite too often, yes. I underestimate nearly everyone, yes. But I WILL NOT be a waste of time. I WILL NOT be an easy victory. And starting now, I WILL NOT overlook, I WILL NOT underestimate, and I WILL NOT LOSE! Especially to a man whose to cowardly to show his own damn face!! Pola, this match will be of great importance to you. You will experiance first hand that not everyone in BMW is a pushover. Not everyone is all talk and no walk. Not everyone is a fraud. I am not like everyone else, I am different and I don't need a freakin' costume to prove it! I'll PROVE IT in the ring. You WILL tap! Rockweiler thinks that I'll be gunning for him 'cause he beat me. Not so. He beat me when my mind was in the clouds. He beat me when I was just coming back from a LONG vacation. I don't care about tainted wins. I'm not gonna focus my energy on Itacky Feint either. Hell, I don't even know if HE knows he's in the goddamn match! I'll let the guy go hay-wire all by his lonesome. No, I'm gunning for you Bare. I'm gunning for the rookie that's even more cocky than a guy who can actually back himself up. How can you be so arrogant Bare? You're a newbie here who's already lost his respect before stepping into the ring. You're worse than me when overlooking your opponents. You're hiding your face under a damn mask from shame. You're talking about how no one is good enough to take you on. Well then why the hell are you here? Take your toy car and go home Bare. Or you can stay and get the REAL challange that you've been waiting for. At Withdrawn I WILL shut that talking black hole of yours and I will make you realize that BMW is not a place of just has-beens, never-was' and ignorant, talentless bums. It's a place with all those things... and The Prodigy. I am the exception and at Withdrawn you will be made an example of. At Withdrawn, I'm going to pull that mask off and show to the world what a coward and a fake you are. Just like Billy Blaze, you will be crying and BEGGING for me to let you walk again. Too bad I've already made my decision. I hate people like you Bare. I hate frauds like you. I hate them with a passion! And at Withdrawn there will be nothing you can do to stop me! There is nothing that you can do to hold me back from crippling you! When I look into your furry mask, when I look into Blaze's mask I see the same thing. I see the same damn person! An idiot to ashamed to be himself! Oh how I hate that idiot and there is nothing that idiot can do. There is nothing that idiot can do to prepare for Withdrawn, nothing. I only have one piece of advice for you Billy Bare, and you can take this as a joke if you want but you'd be wise to take it as a warning ... invest in a wheelchair, you're gonna need it. ~~~ Sal rises from the table and walks out of the sports bar as the camera fades to black. ~~~ §§§ SCENE ENDS §§§ View Biography
