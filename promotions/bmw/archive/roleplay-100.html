Roleplay Directory #100

captured 21 September 2002

I had intended to write and post it then but due to several other things going on I couldn't. [Voice] Grrrrrrrrroooooooooowwwwwwwwlllllllll!!! {The tiger looks back as perplexed as a tiger could look.} [Voice] And don't give me that look either, you overgrown and jumped up rat catcher. {Tigers, you say. Where on earth could J-Man be today, you ponder. The answer is not India, but somewhere alot closer to home. A zoo. In the Uited States. Now before you become lost in thought about the possible reasons for the zoo being chosen as a setting for the latest J-Man promo, it has absolutely nothing to do with any kind of fascination of animals or interest in zoology on the part of the 'Real Deal.' What it is is....well let's just find out for ourselves.} [The Real Deal] It doesn't look dangerous, does it? [Jim Townsend] It's teeth do. {Jim Townsend is still being paid by the hour to accompany J-Man where ever it is he goes. It's still not really clear what role Jim plays in the day to day running of J-Man's life. I suppose when you have millions to burn you can afford to employ a person just for the hell of it.} [The Real Deal] Can't be that dangerous. If Tarzan could slay a tiger with his bare hands then I'm sure I could do it. [Jim Townsend] But Tarzan is a fictional character. He didn't really exist and therefore without existence he could not slay and also non-existent tiger. {J-Man looks at Jim then sighs.} [The Real Deal] The proverbial Jim. I was talking in the proverbial. {J-Man gives Jim another look and shakes his head. He begins to walk from the tiger's pen.} [The Real Deal] C'mon Jimbo! This place is huge, it might take a long time to find. [Jim Townsend] What will? [The Real Deal] What will what? [Jim Townsend] To find? [The Real Deal] To find what? {Jim sighs. He shakes his head, gesticulating that they move on from this. J-Man smiles and prods Jim to the shoulder with his fist.} [The Real Deal] Lighten up Jim. {J-Man turns and begins to walk deeper into the zoo; Jim follows him. They pass several exhibitions then stop outside an area that is signposted....} [The Real Deal] 'Primates.' Hmmmm, well I've fought enough Sudden Violence members of late so I don't think we'll find much info in this section. [Jim Townsend] What information are you trying to find? [The Real Deal] Info about my opponent this week. {Jim looks confused.} [Jim Townsend] But how is coming to a zoo going to help you find out information about a wrestler? {Instead of answering though, J-Man walks off looking at all the exhibitions and different areas. Jim plods along after him, frustrated, and disappointed, that they are not stopping to observe the animals. Eventually they stop outside the panda bear section.} [The Real Deal] Ah...panda bears. We're getting close. However, I beat Billy Blaze last week and like this species time on this planet is coming to an end his career at the top is too. C'mon Jim, I think we're on the right track. [Jim Townsend] But aren't you going to take any photos or watch them. Look how cute they are. [The Real Deal] Cute? Watching cute animals too dumb to move their fat asses when the poacher is taking aim is not my idea of constructive preparation. I could just watch Suckweiler all week long if I wanted that. {Again J-Man marches through the zoo, past various exhibitions that he has no interest in. He stops outside the hyena pound.} [The Real Deal] Now this is interesting. I should kill all these bastards right here and now. [Jim Townsend] Why? Apart from them being vultures without wings. [The Real Deal] That's exactly why. I see these scum of the animal kingdom and I see the face of Louis Miranda and his supporters. The face of cowardice. The face that lurks in the shadows and attacks you from behind and as a pack because they know they can't do it face to face, one on one. They make me sick. Let's go. {J-Man moves on and stops with a look of delight on his face as they reach the aquarium.} [The Real Deal] Yes. This is it, Jim. We should find what we're looking for in here. {Before Jim can ask again what it is they are looking for J-Man is plodding onwards through the aquarium. Finally they reach the section where the Polar Bears reside. J-Man stops and watches in awe as these huge behemoth of creatures live in an environment that is a simulation of their natural habitat.} [The Real Deal] What great creatures these are Jim. Look at them. They are the most dangerous, most viscious and one of the most easily antagonised species on the planet. You don't want to meet one of these bad boys when their pissed off. They'll rip you apart with their hands, eat your remains and then shit you back out. Now I have two days to get ready. I'm going to camp it here for the next 4 or 5 days. I'm going to watch and learn all I can about these animals.....I'm gonna have to if I'm being forced to face one. By the way, did you contact my lawyer about this. I expected some punishment for having some balls and standing up against Louis Miranda but this is a joke. C'mon, fighting a monster like this. It can't be legal. [Jim Townsend] What the hell are you rambling on about? [The Real Deal] My opponent this week? You told me this morning I was fighting a Polar Bear on Withdrawn this week. [Jim Townsend] What!? I said you are fighting Pola Bare, not a POLAR BEAR! [The Real Deal] Huh? You've confused me Jim. You just said I'm fighting a Polar Bear but that I'm not fighting a Polar Bear. Are we talking different kinds of Polar Bear here? {Jim shakes his head. J-Man is an intelligent man, after all....he is just extremely ignorant at the same time.} [Jim Townsend] This week you are fighting a human. A wrestler. His name is Pola Bare. You are not fighting a Polar Bear, as in the animal we see before us. [The Real Deal] Oh! Oh right! Oh! Well that makes sense then. His name is Pola Bare? [Jim Townsend] Yeah. [The Real Deal] And who is he? I don't believe I've heard anyone of this name running their mouth. [Jim Townsend] He's a new guy. In fact, I'm suprised you don't know him. You put his head through a car window last night. {J-Man stops to think back [he's drank and smoked alot since last night], then it hits him.} [The Real Deal] That marmaduke!? I'm fighting an assclown like that. Well this is the last straw. I can't believe I'm being forced to fight another gimp. Well Louis Miranda has gone too far now. Who does this guy think he is. Last night, I beat Billy Blaze...Billy F##king Blaze. The ex-World Champ. Now I'm going to have to put on yet another display of overselling against some talent absent loser. Where does he get these guys, huh? I can see what Louis is doing now though. I see his plan, do you Jim? [Jim Townsend] Uh no. The way I see it is..... [The Real Deal] ...Is that Louis knows that he cannot screw me anymore. The world has been shown evidence of his games. He has been exposed. Now he has to adopt a new policy of keeping me down....booking me against losers. Well I'll beat the candy ass of Pola Bare. I have no problem with that. I'll do it to every other piss poor wrestler Lou wants to throw at me, from Suckweiler to Vacant B....it doesn't matter. The war has begun Jim. The enemy has struck. Now it's time to respond with a counter-offense. C'mon, let's get out of here. {The scene fades.} [End] View Biography
