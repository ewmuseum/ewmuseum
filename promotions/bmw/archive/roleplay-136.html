You're next | Written: Aug. 21, 2002 Location: ??? -Continued- | God works in mysterious ways. Grave yards filled with the soon undead haunted before my small white eyes. The wind took my hair off my shoulders and made it flow with it. My leather white coat mimiced my hair as well; flowing with the wind. Annihilation was only a couple of steps away. Was I honestly afriad of death? To the eye of a sane person that may be the case, but I'm not sane. I didn't know who I was. Like a doll made for the purpose of living a ill-fated life. Why was I put here? Does this grave yard have any meaning to me what so ever? Ghost walked as if it was perfectly normal. I must have been a ghost too because I was invisible to even fate which has yet planted me with a destiny. | 'How can warmth me inside this place they call 'Earth' when it's so cold on the outside... Ghost walk before me without a care while I stand here dead in my tracks as if I was one with them. People see right through me... But I guess that's a good thing. Why they can't see, will hurt them come judgement day. And Judgement day will come. The formation of the Ice Age will come forth and bury this world in total destruction at my volition.' With cold air spewing from my lips, the words all came out one after another with an incredibly important meaning. | It couldn't be so bad. Dead is just another place where the misfits go to fit in the 'social class' at their decision. But I didn't want to be 'social' or in 'society'. They could all freeze-in-hell for all I care... The funny thing, they will soon freeze. They will feel what I feel and it won't be pleasant. | 'Itagaki...Freeze... What is it's meaning? Why do thoughts cloud my mind and make me do things not even the black-hearted call watch? They scream at me in thousands of voices all speaking as one. I don't know if I can take this anymore. It makes my bones itch for pain, blood, destruction, total annihilation of everything nice. Society sickens me...' Or so my mind had told me so, 'Everyone that belongs to it, will soon suffer a beating so suffer, you won't have a soul to take to hell!' | I felt the presence of a man standing behind me. Was it another 'suprise' family member brung to me by fate in a sick joke for her amusement? | 'So I see your also the undead...' | My eyes shoot wide open. Was he it? Was he the one person that knew my past? He saw me through the fog which blinds vision before me. | 'Itagaki, you must carry out you mission as stated. It is vital you destroy Sal and Rockweiler but leave Pola alive. There is an experiment to be done, bring his body to me. All will be awaken to you and it will soon be clear to you cloudy mind.' Going on as if he was the solution to all my problems. I trusted no one but my self and the person that could help me. 'After I'm done with them, there pain will last them eternities to come! No one will stop me from doing what must be done! And if you don't come through, your blood will also be stained on my already dirty hands. All well, that ends well. And when they meet the end of their 'society binds', I will be there to end it all! They're to weak to even be considered at the moment. Time will tell all, and I shall preveal. So it is written. So it shall come to pass.' Without taking one look at his face, I already got his identy in my head. If he needed to be found, I'll find him, that wasn't the least of my worries... As if I had any to begin with. -Will the pain ever stop- Mrs. Landisis' 6th grade class. I sat in the third row next to Theresa Hayes. And it was there that I first discovered alienation and baron path. The taunting and the badgering by the other kids made me realize the depths of human evil and the dark underbelly of human society. I'd close my eyes and pretend I was someone different. That I wasn't a misfit. You can't erase the dream, you can only wake me up. I didn't care. I never obeyed the rules. Because even back then I realized the nothing... Nothing in nature behaves so consistently and rigidly as a human being in pursuit of destruction. | Like pieces of jagged razor blades, and old memory hurt my mind. It was as if the scientist I killed put something in my head to stop me from remembering the past, but 'remember' what is to be done in the future. I sat in the corner of an old abandon prison. Blood stained walls surrounded me in fours. A dirty mattress laid over rusted metal, and rats along with some roaches ran wild searching for food. But there I was in the cold corner listening to the wind make haunting sounds as they screamed through the barred window. What brought me here? Is destiny starting to give me direction? Or was it only thing I am 'remembered' to do in the future? Confusing the the worst thing to be trapped with for eternity. I will make everyone will my pain... | View Biography
