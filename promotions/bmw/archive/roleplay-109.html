Seems Billy Blaze's nearly week long silence was finally broken with the former BMW World Champion dropping a couple of promo's yesterday and today that have surely changed people's minds about his up-coming match with Sewaside. No longer does he seem to be the once great veteran struggling to regain what once made him a main eventer. Instead he seems to have that confident swagger back. He seems to have regained his self confidence. Somehow now he makes you feel like he actually believes he will be victorius this week against Sewaside. An important step as this was something that was missing in the BMW great's nearly month long slump. Now it seems all eyes are on Sewaside. Sure we all know he can dish it out, but he's yet to prove he can take it as well from a main event calliber opponant. Enough debating though see for yourself.... The scene opens in front of the Gund Arena in Clevland, Ohio at the very spot Billy Blaze cut his last promo. Sewaside is seen standing in front of the arena with a smirk on his face and a bottle of Moet in his right hand and as he looks up at the massive building the smirk dissappears, an angry scowl replaces it and Sewaside begins to speak in a loud commanding tone. Sewaside:Billy Blaze?! Is something inside that warpped brain of yours really telling you that you can somehow, someway force me to say 'I Quit'?! REALITY CHECK! In two years in this sport I've been beaten to a bloody pulp, I've had C4 explosives burn and mangle my body, I've survived the Japanesse Deathmatches that made Mick Foley famous, but I have never and you can check the tapes I HAVE NEVER IN MY CAREER SAID 'I QUIT'! You think cause you had the wit to conjure up a folly list of ways you are gonna defeat me and ask me to choose one that that's gonna make them words come outta my mouth?! FUCK NAH! I look at this arena, I look at what you called a historic site in wrestling lore and you know what I SEE?! I see a ring surrounded by blood thirsty fans cursing my name and screaming for my head! I see yourself fast becoming my hated rival staring me in the face across the ring! I see the referee already drenched in sweat before the bell rings knowing in his mind 'this one could go all night'. I see the owners of this very company sitting in there usual position in the luxery boxes near the upper deck patting themselves on the back for signing me. I see the bookers backstage scheming on how after what will surely be one of the greatest matches in BMW history they'll be able to screw me out of my position and keep me on the undercard. Then I see myself. I'm standing in the ring aching to get my hands on you. Aching to see your blood on the mat, shit aching to see my own. Billy this may very well be an 'I Quit' match but surely sucumming to the temporary pain from a submission hold would surely be a better fait then having me beat and pound you into submission. This is what I plan to do Billy, I've changed my strategy. Sure I may throw you in a Sharpshooter, or a figure four to wear you down, but I will beat you by pounding your face into hamburger meat. By smashing your ribs until it hurts just to laugh. By dropping on your head so many times you need a map just to find your way home from the arena. It won't be pleasent Billy. I don't know much of your personal life, but I would'nt suggest your wife and kids watch this. I would'nt suggest yo mama watch this. If by some miraculious act of god you survive this match and are able to continue your career here in the BMW your family would surely present you with an ultimatum after witnessing you take such a beating: QUIT THE SPORT, OR ELSE! Sewaside takes a swig from the bottle of Moet still dangeling from his clutches then re-continues.... Sewaside:Now I know your paid to make these people buy your foolish claims, I know your paid to make even yourself believe them Billy but your smarter then that. You know even a gun to my fuckin' head could'nt make me utter those two words so there's no way anything you dish out could. You claim you will take away my spirit to wrestle Billy but what you don't understand is I'm not motivated by the same things the majority of these competitors in the BMW are. My motives are much more selfish. I'm motivated by greed. The roar of the crowd or ten thousand people cursing the very ground I walk on means nothing to me. I could live the rest of my life and never crave that sound again. I DO love the pain and suffering I both inflict and recieve in the ring but shit a nigga could go to a dominatrix for all that. To put it simply Blaze this is how I make my living. I never went to college. I'm not cut out for a nine to five because it could'nt nearly provide enough revenue for my lavish lifestyle, SO THIS IS ALL I GOT! And you in your arrogent claims think your gonna take away my very way of life?! Sorry William but nothing could be further from the truth. Ya see right when I stepped in the door the so-called wrestling experts in the BMW immediately wanted to put a tag line on me so they threw me in with the Raymond Mercy's and Da Thug's of this federation and labeled me a gangster or a thug. Now this is all fine and dandy for some, but honestly I'm far to advance in my knowledge of life and far to diverse to just be branded with that label. Ya see these cats that accept that label thug or gangster they are content with the ghetto life and hood mentality. Now as we know that is in fact one of the aspects that makes up my personality, but unlike the others this is not the driving force behind me. This is what will defeat you William. You maybe not so follishly think you can approach this match as you would with any of the other thugs or gangsters in this federation cause you believe I have the same mentality, but you're wrong. Ya see while in Japan when I was'nt getting my limbs half blown off my body, I studied. I studied things like meditation and visualzation. Things that clearly at least to myself set me apart from the others. These are strengths which you have surely written off and do not yet posses. These are strengths which many weak minded bruts currently infesting this federation would laugh at. But alas the laughter will cease and the curiosity shall surely arise as the rest of the federation lays witness to me making you a former BMW World champion cry out again and again 'I QUIT!'..... Sewaside walks off into the distance after slamming the remaining contents of the bottle of Moet and smashing it against the Gund Arena. The scene fads as the up and coming superstar dissappears from the camera's view... View Biography
