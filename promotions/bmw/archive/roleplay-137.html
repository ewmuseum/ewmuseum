I was very bussy today sorry.) The scene opens at a local fast food restaurant. Pola Bare is sitting outside the restaurant in his usual attire, the polar bear costume and a T-shirt. He is sitting in a parking space in his Barbie jeep. Pola Bare looks at the camera and yawns. He then begins to talk. Pola Bare: You know I'm really just getting sick and tired of listening to Sensational Sally run his mouth. I'm sick of him trying to tell me what's up, to tell me what I'm holding back. He's worse then one of those psychologists that try to convince you that you're homosexual. You see this match has pretty much evolved into Pola Bare vs Sensational Sally. Rockwell, won't be a factor, Iyadksd Peeze won't even realize he's in the match. It's interesting though. Sensational Sally wants to talk about smoke and mirrors, he wants to talk about shoelaces and bells and whistles. Shoelaces and poo faces. Birdies and Gangsters. Who really cares what he thins. He wants to talk about being hypocritical. He's the one trying to tell me he's 'figured me out' yet he knows nothing. It's amusing, simply amusing, this guy faces some opposition and he crumbles. We haven't stepped into the ring yet and this guy already is about to snap. I've always liked playing around with people who have short fuses. Sensational Sally..why are you so mad? What did I do that made you so upset? Is it because you've wasted this whole week failing to convince me I'm hiding something. Pull it together, the BMW fans deserve something better than Pola Bare kicking you around. They at least want you to get up at least once. I'm not trying to offend someone to the point of rage either, but you're just an easy target. I bet you get all flustered about 'yo mamma' jokes. Well, get this, 'Yo mamma so...' Ah screw it you might start crying. Pola Bare chuckles to himself. I'm glad you're taking me seriously, most people have to find out the hard way. But that doesn't mean that just because you've figured out that I am indeed a skilled wrestler that you'll get the three count on me. But you also seemed to have some ego trip about yourself. 'Oh you haven't seen what I can do whan whan.' I've watched some tapes, I saw you pass out, I saw you get clobbered. But maybe you're holding something back. Either way it doesn't matter, I'm going to put everything I have into this match win or lose. You'll be lucky if you can even manage to stop the fight after the match ends. I'm not mad at you, I just want to make sure that what you get tomorrow night is exactly what you deserve, I don't want to hear you running your mouth after the match. I don't want to see you trying to pay me back for beating you. I just want you to get it through your head that you loss, and you are jus going to have to deal with that. It's simply, really simple. You think you've got me figured out, like I'm just some guy who expects to throw you off by dressing up in a costume. I don't even need a costume. It's just an idea I came up one day, no different from the Violence trying to act like a hardened thug. Is that so wrong? Is it so wrong to be original, not like the next guy? You said that me and you were similar, but ti isn't true. I don't try to get attention, and you couldn't get it if you tried. I'm not hiding behind my mask either, if anything it's you who needs to be hiding your apeman facetality behind one. Maybe I'll even give you a store to look for one at. I'm not arrogant either, I'm just telling you exactly the way it si. Why should I respect the person that doesn't earn it? Why should I treat you like your my toughest opponent yet, when you indeed aren't? It seems to me like you're just made because you can't get my respect, and you've realized by now that you can't get a win over me. All you can do now is cuss and yell hoping that your last ditch efforts are enough to even put a dent on me mentally. You can only pray that your words affect me. You've tried to do it your way, yet now all you can do is try to imitate the exact thing that I did to you. I've beaten you in a mind game, you can't even handle yourself and my words have changed you. All of this just by me telling you the truth. You aren't even yourself, your confused, your little ego is hurt, and you are just dying for me to respect you. Not a good way to go into a match. You're unprepared, you're not even yourself, your falling apart at the seams and you can't seem to figure out why I feel I have this one all wrapped up. Hate me if you must Sally, men naturally are jealous, they natural hate that which they cannot conquer. It's only human Sal, just like it's only human to make mistakes, and you'll see that tomorrow when you step into the ring with me. As for my other two opponents, you two have just dropped the ball. Freeze rambles on without a clue while Rockweiler seems to preoccupied with a pool party to figure out what's going on. It's OK I'm not going to cry about ti, I just hope you two realize that this match isn't about you. It's about Pola Bare and Sally, so when you two get eliminated just step aside. Just sit back and watch as Pola Bare finishes off what's he started. I've messed Sal up mentally, he's an emotional wreck cussing and yelling. Only thing that's left is the physical aspect of the game, and that'll be done in due time. You see Sal we are different, in that I couldn't care less about you while you're basically having a fit about this whole ordeal. Don't get over excited and botch the match, don't expect your words to hurt me. Just don't eb suprised when I win and leave you crying in the middle of the ring. With this the camera fades to black.
