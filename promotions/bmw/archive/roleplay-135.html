Written: Aug.21.02 Location: Chicago §§§ SCENE BEGINS §§§ ~~~ Scene opens up in front of the United Center, where BMW Withdrawn will take place. There is a single man sitting with his back against the ticket booth. He gets up slowly and walks towards the camera. He stops near the entrance and begins to talk as the camera moves in. ~~~ §§: Hello everyone, It's The §ensation§al One! I've heard alot of bullshit since I started wrestling but non quite as great as what I've heard recently. Pola Bare, who are you trying to fool? Setting up a trap? Just because I know that you're a fraud doesn't change the course the match will take. Just because I'm convinced that there is more to that outfit than 'show' it doesn't mean I'll be vunerable. If anything, you will. If anything, I'll be more dangerous. Is that the trap you speak of? I don't like you Bare. In fact I'm starting to hate you. Only one person has gotten me to hate them quicker and that's Billy Blaze but you're coming real close Pola. And before you say it, no I don't hate you because you supposedly 'figured me out'. I don't hate you because you're 'telling it like it is'. I don't hate you because you fire insults back at me. No Pola, I hate you because you won't admit to yourself or to the world, that there is more to the costume then you let on. There is more to it. I hate you Bare because you represent to me exactly what is wrong. You represent the two-faced, hyprocritical, assholes in the world. You can only put up the smoke and mirrors for so long until someone figures you out. Well that time has come. You may fool others but you're not fooling me. You are hiding something but unlike that guy who stole your mask, I'm not going to try and figure it out. If you need your little secret or you need your mask to hide then keep it. I'll do you a favour and not expose it. I will however, make you pay for trying to pull the wool over my eyes. I will look past the bells and whistles and take you as seriously as I've ever taken anyone in my life. If you think that by dressing up in a bear costume, coming out to pop music and driving a toy car will take me off my game you are dead wrong. You haven't seen what I am capable of. You do not know what talent I posses Bare. You are unaware of the risk you face when you step into that arena at Withdrawn. Yeah, I make petty insults and small jabs but that's for my own humour. I'm not trying to offend someone to the point of rage. I don't care if they're on top of their game or not because when it comes down to it, they're not good enough to beat me. And again, before you say it, I was NOT at the top of my game upon returning. And that may seem like a lame excuse, but for once in this goddamn place, it's the truth. I was, and still am to an extent, cocky. Arrogant. Overconfident. Whatever you want to call it. I was an idiot. But, thanks to you Bare, I'm all better again. Thanks to you, I won't look past my opponents as nobodies but as decent challangers, no matter how insignificant they are. You want to see what I can do, then at Withdrawn I'll give you a piece of the pie. Sure, I make alot of threats. I spew alot of insults. But Pola I can, and will, back it up every single time, now and here on foreward. No more cheap wins. No more easy wins. If you pin me, you'll have to earn it. You're right on numerous things Bare. I've never faced you in the ring but you haven't faced me either. And watching video tapes does nothing. Every match is a different match. You say I've overlooked you, yet you've overlooked me. You say I talk petty shit. You've been talking more of it each and every day. We're quite the same you and I, 'cept I'm not hiding behind a mask. I don't need the costume and the cheap tricks to get people's attention. I'll do that in the ring. That's where the similarities end Bare. That's where you find out why I demand so much respect. Why I get so much respect and why I AM so damn arrogant! You may not respect me now but I'll be damned if you don't respect me after I make you cry like a little school girl. After I make you tap and scream 'I Give Up!' Rockweiler got lucky and it won't happen again. Itacky is just fucked and he'll lose his way. You Pola, you're a man too lost to find the truth. Too deep in his own shit that he can't see anything else. If you can't see it now than by God you'll see it at Withdrawn, wether you want to or not. ~~~ Sal walks off into an awaiting limo. The limo drives off as the camera fades to black. ~~~ §§§ SCENE ENDS §§§ View Biography
