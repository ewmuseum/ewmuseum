Column: Big Money Ready for Prime Time?

20 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- BIG MONEY WRESTLING - Big Money Ready for Prime Time? [name withheld] - BMW Editor Sunday, January 20th, 2002 Should Big Money Wrestling be in the interfed business? On Friday, January the 18th, the rumor mills began kicking out information that the co-owners were in heavy discussion with PTC officials, leading many to suspect that BMWF is going Prime Time ! I'm not sold on this being a cut and dry good thing. Is Big Money Wrestling even ready for interfed exposure? Well, first, what is an interfed? One should know what an interfed is before deciding whether they want into one or not. This is something I had to do before casting my two cents, and so should you. I looked high and low in my search for a direct definition of interfed and to my surprise, it is not a real word. Interfed is not really a term, but rather jargon for the sports entertainment business to explain what a group of competing federations is. In simple terms, that is an interfed, but it is actually a little more complicated. I know that an interfed is more than one fed in a competition in an effort to gain ratings and support. So why am I not happy with my definition? Well, I had the opportunity to sit down and talk with a few people to attempt to gain a better understanding with what an interfed really is, what it does, how it works, and why we should join into it. Of the people I talked with, I gained a general feeling for what it's all about. The first person I was able to meet up with was Donomite, himself. He had apparently just spoken with someone when I entered his office. He was not available for an interview, but he had enough time to exchange a few words with me. The first question I asked him, of course, was whether or not Big Money Wrestling was leaving Fed Wars to join Prime Time Central. He had very few direct things to say. "Um, I'm not obligated to answer any questions of that nature, but we have been negotiating with a lot of other organizations about our currect status in FedWars.", he loosely commented, "But as far as Prime Time goes, I can't say that we are or aren't in contact with them. For obvious reasons, I'm not going to disclose anything. 5 minutes after you walk out of here, I may get a phonecall." We continued to exchange thoughts and I even took a few abstract shots at getting him to release a few facts to aid me in my quest. I did a better understanding on a few issues. When asked what his definition of an interfed is, he remarked, "You know, (Mark), I've never been too keen on interfeds. I don't look at them a lot, and I don't read up on them. From my general understanding, an interfed acts as a center of competition between feds to gain exposure and demonstrate the quality of your fed. It's all about exposure for (Big Money Wrestling)." "When I think about it, if it didn't spread our name or give us exposure, I wouldn't want anything to do with it." With that in mind, I decided to scribble a few notes. Interfeds act as an area where feds compete for publicity. Top notch talent can also take it upon themselves to better their fed. My conclusion sparked Donomite's interest in our conversation and he was quick to point out how right I am. The talent level of a fed is supposed to prove a lot of things. "I can't think of a better way to prove the quality of the fed than the loyalty of its workers and their determination to prove that no one comes into their house and steals their show" He then remarked, "You know, in wAw that's all we're about. We don't let anybody rock our show, but us. I don't know about those other guys (members outside of wAw), but so far their allegience is up in the air." So we know a few things now. We know that an interfed is a group of feds in a competition. We also know that it's a common place for gaining exposure - for free agents and rival talent to see a federation. Interfeds, now, are also proving grounds in wars between federations; a test between whose fed has got the most "hooked" talent. Obviously, a good fed will have loyal workers who will lift a fed to its potential, something Big Money Wrestling has lacked in the past and dealt with only months ago. Great. Having had a conversation with one of the co-owners, I could only wish to bump into another, but fate didn't have it in store at the moment. I didn't even catch a glance at any staff on my way out of the building. Once I got to my car I decided to pick up my cell phone and give someone else a call, and to my amazement he answered. The Phantom Booker, head of PCW and a major player in PTC, was in. We didn't talk long, but I did manage to find out a little more about our piece of slang, interfed . According to him, "(An interfed is) a governing body that sanctions interfed events." That's a bit of a new look on an interfed organization. He, too, wasn't free to discuss any happenings with PTC and only had a few sugar-coated comments about Big Money Wrestling. These guys play this game too tight to devouge any real info. So add that to the mix. An interfed is a governing body that organizes competition between wrestling federations who hope to steal, at least, some of the spotlight for the fed - to catch the eyes of potential talent and increase the number of viewers for their events. It's a big ratings war, folks. Wrestling Federations, including BMWF, are out to steal your attention and buy up talent, and that is no secret. My question is this: Should Big Money Wrestling be in the interfed business? Is this federation ready for such exposure? Make no mistakes, BMWF has already been in an interfed (FedWars) for the past couple of weeks, and so far I have seen no difference. Unlike now, though, PTC will most likely be a noteable difference, but with the current state of this company, can it operate under added pressure? Make no mistakes, this is a great fed with a great track record. It's entirely impossible to predict what will happen when pressure is involved. Tycoon's press statements on joining the interfed mix is that it's "only for the exposure". But how will the fed operate with expectations. BMWF has never been a fed under a heat lamp (spotlight) before. Exposure is fine, and the increased viewers is great, but how will our roster react? That's the biggest concern for the owners, in my opinion, which doesn't count for much, and they had better be looking into how to spice things up. Not that the current product of this fed isn't good enough, but if things begin to slip, we'll know why. For now, I can only look to the big guys running this show: Tycoon, Louis Miranda, Risk, Donomite, Scott Phillips, Ragio Versace, Reaper, Axel Teflon, and others. They put on a hell of a show before, so I don't see how they won't now.
