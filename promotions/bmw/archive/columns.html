It was a night that will never be forgotten and will live on in the greated memories of Big Money Wrestling for the rest of time. First, "MPR" was opened up with a dramatic introduction video featuring BMW superstars of the past and present, in tribute to the one year anniversary of BMW. Cameras then cut to the top of the entrance aisle and under the Money Tron, where The Lox, a famous rap group, was live to play the theme song, "Money, Power, Respect". The fans loved this, and ate up every minute of it. BMW v3 commentators, Cameron Daily and Christian Shirley, then welcomed the viewers at home and the sold out arena to BMW "MPR" ("Money, Power, Respect"). Then, everyone's attention was drawn to the Money Tron, where a special recap video showed the recent "Lethal Lottery" tournament. It then got down to the final four and did more in-depth recap. After showing Tax and Risk as 2002 Lethal Lottery Champs due to beating Da Thug and Violent B, the video then ended, getting a decent pop from the crowd. Next up, current "Sudden Violence" members, Krisis and Violent B, then took on J-Man and "Tank" Hank Gunyon in a non title tag team match. This match ended with J-Man screwing his partner, "Tank" over and then pinning Krisis after an attack with a steel chair. Next up, we saw a quick backstage segment. Basically, Anthony Balboa (Italian) walked down a hallway and witnessed Jon Mobootu (Nigerian) taking part in one of his cultural belief in a voodoo ritual. Anthony Balboa bumps the door on accident, informing Mobootu of his presence. The two men had a stare down, Anthony Balboa then begins laughing hysterically as he walked off. Jon managed to hold his anger in because the two men have a match coming up next. Cameras then cut back to ringside for a triple threat match, Paranoia vs. Anthony Balboa vs. Jon Mobootu for the Cash Money Championship. This was a pretty good match, mainly resulting in Balboa and Paranoia going back and forth, due to a major bump Mobootu took at the beginning of the match. In the end, Anthony Balboa hit a noticably incredible powerbomb after catching Mobootu in the air, spinning around, and then slamming his head in to the turnbuckle on the way down from the powerbomb. Paranoia then low blowed Balboa for the third time in the match, behind the referees match. Paranoia then brawled with Balboa for a few moments, he then set him up and hit Balboa with his finisher, "Careless Paranoia" (Reverse Shooting Star Press into Pinfall). Paranoia, in fact, did manage to get the three count. Paranoia won the Cash Money Championship. Then, while celebrating, Balboa and Mobootu have already left ringside. Newly signed BMW superstar, "The Violence" Vince Jones ran to the ring in a rage and attacked Paranoia from behind, while Paranoia was holding his newly won Cash Money Championship in the air to a rain of boos. After hitting his finisher ("The Silencer"), Vince Jones then grabbed a steel chair and assaulted Paranoia with it, causing Paranoia to start bleeding from the face, back, and shoulders. Another newly signed BMW superstar, Raymond Mercy, then ran down and ripped the chair out of Vince's hands. At this point, the commentators and viewers were completely confused and had no clue who was on what side. Raymond then stunned everyone by cracking Paranoia over the head many times with the same chair Vince Jones used. Vince pushed Raymond and took the chair back, Raymond then pushed Vince and took the chair back for himself. After this little dispute, Raymond and Vince Jones then put on one hell of a brawl and hellaciously beat the living hell out of eachother. Krisis ran down to ringside to pull his fellow Sudden Violence member, Paranoia, out of harm's way. Krisis carries Paranoia and the Cash Money Title backstage as the ring then floodes with officials and security trying to break up the brawl between Raymond and Vince Jones. After the match, cameras cut to backstage to reveal Million Dollar Champion, ICE, arriving at the arena. Cameras then cut backstage to Tax's locker room. He is basically warming up for his match in the main event later tonight for the World Title. Cameras then return to rinside as The Rockweiler and §ensation§al "bang heads" for the number one contendership to the Cash Money Title. Most critics say that this was the shortest and match of the night, and after watching it, I agree. Although for a shorter match, this one really brought us to the edge of our seat when Rockweiler made Sal passout at the end of the match with the "Weiler Hold". Rockweiler won this match, no confusion about it. Cameras cut backstage to Da Thug's locker room after the match. Thug was warming up, intensly, he then got angry and physically came after the camera man, forcing them to leave. After that, the BML vs. 2GLS match went down. This match was scheduled to be a Tag Team Inferno Tables match, and that's exactly what was. Krazie and Scott Phillips (Big Money Legends, BML) took on Jeremiah Wilson and Reality (2GLS, 2 Good Looking Studs) in what will go down in BMW history as one of the most extreme tag team matches EVER! The four men put on a very entertaining show that had the fans edgy and jumpy through the whole match. Huge, fantastic moves were executed by members of both teams. Many high flying attacks were also used. In the end, all four wrestlers were beaten down and bleeding. The sequence of moves that ended the match can only be described as "HOLY SHIT!". After all hell broke loose in the extremely hardcore, long match, Reality and Jeremiah Wilson double press slammed Krazie high over the top rope and through three, flaming, stacked tables...The fans were very shocked and highly entertained. Reality and Jeremiah Wilson (2GLS) were then awarded the previously vacated Tag Titles, as the new Tag Champs. Everyone then focused on the Money Tron where a recap video showed the key events in the feud ICE and Da Thug currently have going. It all started over Thug kissing ICE's now ex-girlfriend, they then pulled out all the stops in what most people consider the most heated feud in BMW right now. Cameras then took us to ringside, but it appeared that Da Thug wanted to start the match early and backstage. So Thug attacked ICE from behind, in what was the beginning of one very extreme match up, right after the hardcore match BML and 2GLS just put on. After a very long match of being thrown off stages, hit with various weapons, and after brawling through what felt like the whole arena, ICE then forcefully crammed Da Thug in back of a brown truck. ICE then drove to ringside through the side of the entrance aisle. They got out and began furiously fighting some more, giving the fans yet more entertainment. After getting out of the truck, Da Thug oddly poured a massive amount of gasoline on ICE...Which happens to come in to play later on. Through out this match, Christian Shirley, commentator, was obviously siding with Da Thug...He made quite some funny comments as the match unfolded. After more fighting, Kelly was seen walking to the ring around the same time as ICE hit Da Thuge with his finisher. ICE then hooked Thugs leg, in what could have and would have been a 3 count...Kelly then throws a burning match on ICE, ICE went up in flames as the entire world watched on in a very shocked state of mind. Kelly then runs in the ring and smacks ICE with his own Million Dollar Championship, ICE then fell to the mat and the flames were put out at impact. Da Thug then barely managed to roll over and get the three count. Da Thug is now the new Million Dollar Champion. The crowd began booing extremely loud and then threw pounds and pounds of trash in the ring. Kelly ran in the ring and jumped on thug with her legs around his waste and arms around his neck while giving him the newly won Million Dollar Title. Da Thug, still tired and beaten, then realizes just what he has done...He then walks over to ICE and hits him with his finisher "The Chronic" (Sambo Suplex)...Kelly and Thug then leave with the Million Dollar Title and a shower of boos. Cameras then cut to Risk's locker room, he didn't want the cameras in, so he forced them out. Now, the highlight of the night! The Main Event! Tax and Risk won the Lethal Lottery tourney, and all that work adds up to this moment, right here. Tax vs. Risk vs. Billy Blaze (C), for the BMW World Championship! The match has no confusion about it, it was all around a great match. It wasn't one sided, it was the perfect blend of wrestling that only places like BMW can bring to their product. After a long, well hyped match, Tax ended the match by hitting his finisher, "Stock Market Crash". Tax then covered Risk, 1...2...3! Tax is now the new BMW World Champion after 3 months of Billy Blaze dominating the title. Keep in mind, Billy Blaze didn't get pinned, Risk did. All in all, this was a very close match and any one of these three great competitors good have walked home with the gold...After celebrating, Tax is then assaulted by Reality and Jeremiah Wilson of 2GLS. Billy and Risk are not at ringside any longer. The 2GLS carry out their 2 on 1 assault for a few moments, angering the fans. A man then ran in from the crowd, he came in the ring and dominated the 2GLS with brute force. Security seemed reluctant to even attempt at stopping this guy. It's rumored that he is known as "The British Bomber", and has recently signed a contract with BMW. More information on this issue will get released at this upcoming Withdrawn, on Wednesday.
