The Rockweiler braced himself before turning the handle. Now it was the moment of truth, he would finally find out if his brother would suffer brain damage for the rest of his life, acting like a new born child and never growing up, or if he was just normal. He turned the handle and opened the door before entering the room. “Hey bro,” came the voice from inside the room. “How’s it hanging?” The Rockweiler let out a huge sigh of relief. So far so good, his brother was ok. Doctor Surenburg was about to walk into the room when The Rockweiler shut the door, unintentionally slamming it in the doctor’s face. A loud bang echoed from outside as the doctor fell to the floor and both The Rockweiler and Neil looked through the window. When they saw nothing, they shrugged their shoulders and The Rockweiler took a seat next to Neil. “Well, apart from having the biggest scare of my life, I’m fine,” The Rockweiler replied. “How are you feeling?” “Bruised, sore, beat up, aching all over,” replied Neil. “The usual,” he added. “What exactly happened?” Asked The Rockweiler. Neil then began to recap his story of how he was injured. Well you see, I was sat in my apartment one day when I gets this phone call from some bloke who calls himself ‘Chuggy Chi’. He had some kinda fetish for the Chinese or somethin’ like that, despite the fact he were whiter than snow on the mountains, he used a name he thought sounded oriental. He even owns a Chinese restaurant in New York, not far from here actually. They cook a mean bowl of noodles. Anyway, he phones me up and puts on this sort of old fashioned, Godfather type voice and he says, ‘Pitt Bull,’ coz they use my ring name when they’re talking to me gangster-like. ‘Pitt Bull,’ he says. ‘I hear you worked for Tony Ochello,’ that’s the italian guy who owns the pizza place across the road from his restaurant. ‘I have a job and he recommended you to me,’ he continues. By this point my eyes have dollar signs in them, I swear. This guy is supposed to be loaded so he’s willing to pay more than the going rates for a job. Anyway, he tells me to go to the warehouse in Manhattan and walk right into a trap this gang have set for him. He said not to worry because they aint expecting a guy with a gun, so they’ll be off their guard when I load a thousand bullets in their ass. So I accepts the job and grab my gear. I get into my viper and speed down to the warehouse. I park outside and make sure I gots my uzi under my shirt and my knife in my trousers. I waltz in the warehouse and its all dark. Suddenly the lights go on and a whole crowd of people yell, ‘SURPRISE!’ Without thinking I pull my uzi out and start unloading bullets. Blood shoots everywhere and-- “Uh, Bull,” The Rockweiler interrupts for a brief moment. “Do you want to keep the gore level down? There could be kids watching at home.” “Oh yeah, sorry,” apologises Neil. “Where was I?” Oh yeah. When I stop firing, there is not one person standing. I’ve basically massacred about a hundred people, I’m like some kinda mass murderer. I look around and I almost laugh at my own retardness. There is a huge banner with the words: Happy 40th Birthday Bill. There’s a cake and balloons and there was a stripper only she joined the rest of the people there. Luck had it I had walked into the wrong warehouse. I grab myself a slice of cake, say sorry to the guests and reload my gun before walking out of the warehouse. I see another warehouse directly opposite the one I was in and parked outside is a shitload of mobster vehicles. You know, black limousines and mercedes, there was even a tank there. Obviously this warehouse is the one I want so I walk into there. In this one there are a load of men in black suits walking around wearing sunglasses and carrying all sorts of automatic guns in their hands. I walk up to a fat man dressed in a black tuxedo and ask him what he wants. ‘Ah, Chuggy Chi, you made it,’ he says, twiddling with his tache. Obviously he had never seen Chuggy Chi in his life otherwise he would have known I aint him. I don’t say nothing because he might know Chuggy’s voice, and he says, ‘I know you came here for your money, but I’m afraid we’ve had a slight change in plan.’ With that I knew it was either kill or be killed so I pulled my uzi out (again) and unload the bullets into every single man in this building. When no one is moving anymore I stop. The fat man begins crawling towards me and talks, almost choking to death... well actually, he was choking to death. He says, ‘We have a group of... hookers for you and we’ve... sent sixt... sixty four million dollaars to your... restaurant. We hope you take this as a... token of our gratitude.’ Oops. I almost laugh once again. I walk out the back of the warehouse and find the hookers. I spend some time with them before taking the viper back to Chuggy’s restaurant. When I get there, we have a little talk. I tell him everything that happened and he gets pissed. He calls his boys in and they try to beat me. Luckily I’ve been wrestling so I know how to defend myself. I beat them to hell and back before pegging it out the door and into my car. There I am, driving casually away when I see Chuggy’s boys following me. I speed off and we have a huge car chase through the Manhattan streets. They ram me a few times but I eventually lose them. I look back to make sure there gone and, when I turn back to the front, I see a kid on a bike. I swerve and just miss the boy on the bike and I breath out. Then an old woman with a walking stick gets in my way and I swerve to miss her. This time I speed up a little ramp and overturn my car. At 80mph this is lethal. My car tilts twirls, does the macarena, everything before landing on the ground and knocking me unconcious. Then I end up here. The Rockweiler looks at Neil with a raised eyebrow. He remains silent before a few moments before saying, “right,” extending the sound of the word. “You sure you haven’t got brain damage?” “What?” “You’re telling me, you could have been shot, you could have been rammed to death and you could have died during intercourse -- hey, thespark.com seems to have a percentage chance of dying during intercourse -- and after avoiding those very high chances, you seem to hit the one in a million of swerving an old woman with a walking stick, flipping your car through the air and landing upside down?” “What’s your point?” “Nothing, I was just making sure I had it straight.” The Rockweiler sits back in his chair. “Well it’s good to see you healthy.” “I was watching Vincent Empires not long ago,” said Neil. “When?” “Before you came in.” “How long have you been awake?” Neil looks at the clock on the wall above him. “About two hours.” “Oh okay. What did you see?” “A couple of promos by some dude calling himself The Violence.” “He’s my opponent. He’s doing something with Risk and Raymond Mercy. I don’t know what though, I have no reason to pay attention to their business.” “Well he seemed to have a lot to say to you.” Pitt Bull recounted everything he had heard in The Violence’s promos. The Rockweiler excused himself from the room and asked a doctor if he could borrow a private room for a few moments. When he was in there he shut the door and looked towards the camera. “Okay, let me get something straight right now,” The Rockweiler began. “You had to ask me if you really give a damn about my brother’s condition? What, you actually think I want these cameras in this hospital? You think I want my life on TV week in and week out? Are you some kind of deranged moron? Heck no! I would much rather my personal life was left off of TV, but that won’t happen, will it? Because, unlike you, I’m a BMW star, not their rent-a-boy. “So the only thing people want to hear about is the Dynasty of Destruction?” Asked The Rockweiler, unimpressed. “Is that right? Good for you, you have a trio of men. Hell if you like to make your love life public, be my guest. As far as people wanting to hear about? Um, no. How many people have walked up to anyone in the BMW with a question about the DoD? One? Two? And how many are asking the question: ‘What is next for the most impressive rookie in the BMW, The Rockweiler? Will he face the SensationSal One and entertain us with another great match? Will they give him a non-title match against Tax?’ Nope, they give me an easy win against one of the most original wrestlers in the BMW -- “ The Rockweiler showed his sarcasm with an unconvinced look at the camera. “ -- ‘The Violence’ Vince Jones.” The Rockweiler took a long pause, collecting his thoughts and looking in the general direction of his brother’s room. “There is one good thing about this match,” The Rockweiler continued, breaking the silence. “You see, it’s people like you that put my brother in this hospital. People who think they are some kind of bad boy gangster. I’m unimpressed by all of you. You have a gun and a car and you hurt people. Nice. I have fists, boots and a brain which I can use to be as deadly as any weapon you can think of. This week gives me my chance to punish guys like you. To show you that you’re not the biggest man in the world, you just have the biggest ego. Yeah okay, you put some guy in the hospital or whatever it was, I don’t really care to be honest. You’re a wrestler-- well, you try at least -- you are supposed to put people into hospital. I’ve put my fair share of people in the hospital, but its so regular that I don’t keep them as trophies. Obviously this man must be your first for you to jump up and down like a kid with candy when you go near him. “You may be ‘The Violence’ Vince Jones, but I’m the Baddest Dogg in this business! This Wednesday is your chance to step up to the biggest challenge of your life. You finally get your chance to wrestle a man and not some boy who cowers at your feet. You meet BMW’s very own Godzilla, and like Tokyo, you will fear me!” The Rockweiler left the room with a look of disgust, slamming the door behind him. View Biography
