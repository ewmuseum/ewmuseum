Just like all roads, there is never an end to anything, and not everyone realizes this. But there are a few, the few and the limited. As we continue down the long career of Big Money Wrestling superstar, Da Thug, we witness a very real chapter of his life. Previously, on part one of the 'Big Bang Theory' promo series, Da Thug kept it real and told us how it is. He made a hilarious movie about Violent B, and then his promo took a drastic change. From laugther to mystery, it left many people in a state of confusion, which is yet to be answered... Last time we saw Da Thug, he was speaking to a very mysterious individual, who remains unidentified. This individual spoke about 'them' coming for Da Thug, the person also seemed to know something very dark about Da Thug, it appeared that Da Thug was a bit scared or paranoid. In the end, Da Thug was off running into the darkness after the SWAT team was after him. This is the 'Big Bang Theory'. And you're witnessing the sequel. Scene opens up inside of a police car. The police car, being followed by several other police cars, is speeding down a highway in Harlem, New York. The police officer inside of this car is speaking on his radio while driving his car. Police Officer: Yeah, I understand the plan of attack. Does he know we're coming for him? Mission Director: Ahhh, no, I don't believe so... Otherwise, that'd ruin the entire purpose of this mission. Police Officer: Okay, I'm just asking because if one small thing goes wrong, it'll blow our cover and we'll never catch him. We've been trying to capture him for a couple weeks now. Ya know, it's not every day that a cold blooded murderer can avoid a police department like New York's. Mission Director: True... But it's also not every day that a police department goes up against a sick, mentally ill, homicidal proffesional wrestler either. Police Officer: Hmmm, I see your point. I still find humor to that, we're chasing a pro wrestler, haha. Mission Director: Well you shouldn't find anything humorous about it, Chris is a highly skilled, calculating criminal and needs to be handled properly. Guys like this evolve into Charles Manson... But not this time, Chris is sicker than Manson could ever be. Police Officer: Oh, okay then. Here we are! We're approaching his house. The SWAT team is in movment, let's do this! Several units of police squad cars and a couple SWAT Team vans roll into Da Thug's driveway. The majority take cover by hiding behind their police cars with loaded guns ready to release a wave of ammo. The two SWAT Team units creep into Da Thug's front door, while the rest surround his back yard. They break his door down and all charge in with clubs. Screaming, the shattering of glass and breaking of furniture, and beating of clubs is heard... FIVE MINUTES LATER The SWAT Team crews are walking out of the house with their arms raised in victory. Da Thug's bloody, battered body is handcuffed and is being carried over one of the SWAT Team member's shoulders. From head to toe, Da Thug is simply beaten to a degree beyond recognition. The police units begin cheering wildly. Da Thug is placed against the hood of one of the police cars, and is then further beaten by the police in one hell of a 'Excessive Force' trip. Random Police Officer: Whoa! Stop! We can't be him that badly! Random Police Officer: Jesus!... We got to think of an excuse now, or we'll be in deep shit. Mission Director: What are you guys talking about down there?!... What did you do?! Random Police Officer: Ahhhh... Errr... Ummm... Nothing, boss. Nothing at all. Mission Director: That's sure as hell not what it sounds like!... Oh no... Did you beat him? Random Police Officer: Sorry... Mission Director: DAMNIT! You guys! WHY?! Random Police Officer: We didn't mean to, we just got carried away and then the next thing we know, he is lying on the hood of one of our cars in a puddle of blood. Mission Director: You morons, we needed this one, this was gonna be our big break, and you ruined it! Now we could be shutdown or re-located after screwing something up this big... How bad is he?... He isn't dead, right? Random Police Officer: Ummm... hmmm... Well, I don't know. I doubt he's dead, but it's pretty bad. Mission Director: Uhhhh... Fuck! Just leave him there, I'll be down there in a chopper in a matter of minutes with an ambulance and we'll take care of it. I want the rest of you to leave before reporters and news crews arrive, only one of you need to stay. I'll be there ASAP. I'm out. TEN MINUTES LATER The scene opens back up with a helicopter slowly lowering itself down to the street where Da Thug is lying on top of a police car in a puddle of his own blood. The Mission Director, who arranged this whole mess in the first place, gets out of the chopper and looks at Thug... Mission Director: Wow... He is pretty bad. Alright guys, he's comin' up! Random Police Officer: I'm sorry, boss... Mission Director: Do yourself a favor and get the hell out of here, we'll find a place to blame it on someone else. Just finish your day of work then go home to your family and get a nice night's sleep. The last remaining Police Officer gets in his car and speeds off. The Mission Director then lifts Da Thug's beaten body onto a stretcher and ties it to the helicopter, he then rises himself, as well. The helicopter takes off... The chopper flies through the air and shortly arrives at the nearest hospital. Da Thug is rushed into the E.R. and then instany surgery is started. When the doctor inspects Da Thug's body, he sees that there is surprisingly nothing to work on. Da Thug is just badly bruised and cut in several places. The doctor inserts some dissolveable stitches into Thug's cuts and then wraps him up in some areas. The Mission Director runs into the doctor after the doctor leaves Da Thug in a deep sleep. The doctor and the Mission Director are now in the Waiting Room. Mission Director: Is he gonna be alright? Doctor: Yeah, he's gonna need a lot of rest and gonna have to take it easy for a while... May I ask, what in the world happened to that young man? Mission Director: Well, actually, it's classified police business and I don't have the liberty to let you in on what happened. Doctor: Oh, so... I take it you guys were just using 'Extreme Excessive Force'? Mission Director: Whatever... Don't worry about it. Anyways, when is the soonest he can be released and put behind bars? Doctor: Hmmm... Probably he wakes up. I can rudely wake him up right now, he just might not be in the happiest mood towards you, and I don't blame him. But fine, I'll go get him. The doctor walks into the room and wakes Da Thug up. To no surprise, Da Thug is not in a happy mood, but he proceeds to the hallway and is placed under custody by the Mission Director. Mission Director: Thank you very much, doc. Doctor: Yeah, whatever... Mission Director: Well, Chris, we got a long ride to the station, you'll have time to take a nap there. SIX HOURS LATER The scene opens back up in a jail cell. Da Thug is the only one in the one-man cell, he is sleeping on his highly generic bed. He is now dressed in an orange prison uniform with the numbers '622099 0415' printed on his uniform front. Da Thug wakes up slowly, he sits up on his bed, rubbing his eyes and inspecting the unfamiliar surroundings. Da Thug sits up and sits over the edge of his bed while still rubbing his eyes, in attempt to wake up. He looks around and sees more jail cells and then realizes where he is. Da Thug looks directly straight in front of him and then notices another jail cell with a man sitting in the corner of the cell. The man appears to be extremely miserable and is just staring blankly to the floor. Da Thug: Aay... Pssst!... Prisoner: Me? Da Thug: Yeah, you! Where am I? Prisoner: A place I like to call 'hell'... Da Thug: Yo man, kick back with all the bullshit, seriously... Where am I? Prisoner: Prison... County C, Harlem, New York... Does that help a little? Da Thug: Yeah, lots... Prisoner: So why are you here? Da Thug: Murder, but it's not what you think... I had a reason to do it. And Rape too, I guess, but I didn't do that. It's a long story, but I've been avoiding the law for the past week or so, and the muthafuckaz finally caught me. Prisoner: Interesting, even though, that's what we all say. Da Thug: What about you? Why are you here? Prisoner: Ya know, I've been here so long... I forgot why I'm here. I think it had something to do with conspiracy or some shit like that. Da Thug: Damn, man. That's deep, how long you been here? Prisoner: I dunno, 20 years, something like that. As Da Thug and the prisoner are talking, a guard walks by, the footsteps are heard all the way down the silent hallway filled with depressed and miserable prisoners. The guard is a large, strong male with a 'crew cut' (airforce/military haircut). He approaches Da Thug's jail cell. Guard: Are you Chris [name withheld]? Da Thug: Yeah...? Guard: You got someone here to see you, his name is Jake Cortez. Come with me. The massively strong and large guard opens Da Thug's cell-door and then leads him to a visiting window. Da Thug sits down as the guard goes away and stands in the back of the room to keep an eye on everyone. Da Thug then sits down and finds BMW's lead interviewer, Jake Cortez, sitting in the visitor's side of the window with a camera man from BMW standing behind him. Jake Cortez: Hello, Thug. How ya doing? Da Thug: Wussup... Man, I never thought I'd be so happy to have one of you annoyin' interviewers in my face... Jake Cortez: Yeah, I gotta admit, an interview from prison is rather odd. Da Thug: Oh, and to answer your first question, I'm not doing good... How do you think it feels to be jumped by a SWAT Team and a Police Squard? Then not even have time to rest because they're in such a hurry to rush you to the hospital. I'm so tired of being rushed, I jus' can't stand that shit any longer. All through my life, I've been rushed to do something or another. Whether it be running from gettin' jumped, or runnin' to jump someone, I'm always on the run. And feelings like that are the most painful in the world, always having to be on top of your game. Always having to be the best and be aware of everything that is happening around you. It sucks, that's how I feel about it and life in general. Did that answer your question, Jake?... Shiiiit, that's how the fuck I'm feelin'. Jake Cortez: Oh... Sorry... Anyways, are you still gonna be able to be an active part of Big Money Wrestling? Da Thug: Aww hell naw! I can't believe you jus' asked me that!... What is wrong with you, man? Can't you see I'm beaten and behind bars? Is that all people expect out of me?... Work work work! That's all I fuckin' do anymore, nobody knows me for what I really am. Nobody knows Chris [name withheld], they know Da Thug, that's it. If I were to die right now, it would be 'Death of Da Thug', not 'Chris [name withheld] Dies'... Why? Why does it have to be like that, fame is fame, and it should only got to a certain extent. But yeah, if it's eatin' at you so badly, yeah, I'ma try to stay in BMW... Remember, I'm the Million Dollar Champion, I can't jus' leave like that. Jake Cortez: Okay, well, do you have any comments for us or Violent B, or anyone else for that matter? Da Thug: I've said all I need to say to all of you. You muthafuckaz don't have a clue what the pain and suffering is like. I'm the Million Dollar Champion, and in all honesty, it's only made my life more complex and harder. The life of a champion shouldn't be this hard, it should be something easy. But no, it's not like that. Instead, you gotta watch your back and keep track of your every action. And Violent B, you can bet your ass that there is gonna be hell for you to pay. I'm so tired of hearing you complain about how I back-stabbed you or attacked you from behind or any of that fuckin' trash. Why can't you just fuckin' open your eyes and come to terms... I beat your ass. Not once, not twice, hell, not even three times... But four times! Once you get your ass handed to you that many times, it shouldn't make a difference how I jumped you. The fact of the matter is that I beat your ass and you were foolish enough to let it happen more than once. That's not my problem, that's on your shoulders, that's your fault. Stop trying to bitch and whine about everything, you're da link. Realize it. Jake Cortez: Now Thug, every promo or interview that takes place with Big Money Wrestling superstars is recorded and placed on a section where the fans of BMW can view it. You're aware of this, but have you had time to watch Violent B's latest? Da Thug: No... What's the point? Tell me, does it not seem like the same exact thing goes down every fuckin' time? He punches walls, claims he's 'pissed off', and then lets the bullshit flow out of his mouth like toothpaste. Always talkin' about how he made me, or Sudden Violence made me. What the hell? Am I the only one who sees it anymore?... Sudden Violence didn't make me... Violent B didn't make me... I made myself! Hell, Violent B and Sudden Violence can't make a female orgasm, how would they ever be likely to make something of my caliber? That jus' doesn't make sense, Jake. Jake Cortez: Okay... Now, Thug, if you don't mind, could you please explain to us what this whole murder issue is about? It's not exactly clear to all of us. So many things with you and the police, and then this other figure have been happening lately, we just never knew what exactly happened to begin with... Da Thug: Well, first of all, it ain't your business, BMW's business, or anyone else's business for that matter. It's between me, the police, my lawyer, and the courts. So many people have been jumpin' to conclusions, people won't even wait for a damn week before they start spreadin' shit like, 'Da Thug's a murderer'. Lemme tell you, I killed someone, BUT, I had no choice to... And as far as that rape shit goes, hell naw, I didn't rape that bitch. Jake, when was the last time a girl didn't accuse someone of rape? I mean, fa real, you could jus' brush against their arm, then the next thing you know, you'd be in jail for rape or some shit like that... Back to what I was sayin', yeah, I killed, but I had a reason to... It was either me being killed or me killin' them. You'll learn more about this shit at a later time, because right now, I haven't gone to court yet, and I am not allowed to say anything else about it or I'd really be screwed over and possibly locked up for the rest of my life... As it stands right now, if all goes good in court, I'll be out of this muthafucka by next week... Guard: Chris, your time is up, move it! Jake Cortez: Thanks, Thug, or Chris, whichever you'd like at this point in time. Myself, and the rest of BMW wishes you well adn good luck. We'll stay in contact and see what happens from there. See ya. The scene fades to black as Da Thug is forced out of the visitation room in handcuffs and his orange prison uniform. PART III COMING SOON! View Biography
