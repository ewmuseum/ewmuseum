(sigh) Written: 8-10-02 Location: Cleveland, Ohio, Wal-Mart, what more do you need!? ~ The scene opens up to 'The Tank' Hank Gunyon walking through the local Wal-Mart of Cleveland, Ohio. Alex decided to go to an upscale mall to get some highclass outfits and things to toy with. Hank seemingly enough finds a bean bag and makes himself comfortable with a copy of BMW Weekly. Hank enjoys his magazine reading in his spot for a whole fifteen, thirty minutes until a brunette, very attractive Wal-Mart employee walks over to him and looks down on him. Hank continues to read his magazine, giving no regard to the employee standing before him. Right then she grabs his magazine and tosses it to the side. Hank then turns his head away and smirks then lets out a little laugh. Hank stands up and looks right into her eyes. Hank: 'Now, I'll ignore you did that, if you please go get my magazine and let me read in peace...' (looks at name tag) 'Betty Gerk' Betty: 'Well, I never! You're so rude, so inconsiderate!' Hank: 'How so? I wasn't the one who ripped one's magazine and threw it now, was I?' Betty: 'I think you should leave!' (points towards exit) Hank: 'I think you can f##k off, now if you don't mind, I have some loitering to get back to.' Betty: 'Don't make me call the authorities!' Hank: (mocking) 'Don't make me call the authorites, ah shut your cake hole woman! I ain't doin' nothin' but standin' my ground and my right to loiter. Nowhere in your store does it say 'no loitering'. Betty: 'It's to be assumed.' Hank: 'Take your assumptions and stick'em where da sun don't shine witch.' Betty: (rubbing temples) 'Please, just go.' Hank: 'F##k no, I want to read my magazine and I wanna read it here!' Betty: 'You're making a scene...' Hank: 'I bleed for a living woman! This ain't a damn scene!' (sighs) 'A scene is a capacity crowd roaring in support of you getting your ass kicked by yourself.' (smirks) 'People would most likely really be off the hook if you kick your own ass.' Betty: 'Ugh...' Hank: 'Ugh yourself to hell woman. I still have forty-five pages to read in BMW Weekly.' Betty: 'Please, I beg of you, buy it and leave!' Hank: 'Let me think... Nope, ain't workin', now if you please.' (shoes her away with one hand) Betty: 'Urge!' Hank: 'You constipated?' Betty: 'I'll be back with store management and some securty to escort you out.' Hank: 'Bring tasers, chains, and whips. I'm in need of a good thrashing. As much blood as possible.' (grins) ~ Betty storms off in a fit as Hank gathers his magazine and sits back down in his bean bag in another attempt of rest and relaxation. Hank: 'That witch gets under my skin.' (sighs) 'Not nearly as much as Reality and Tax, them being legends in the BMW and all. Are they hardcore legends? Are they really all that good in the ring? I think not, none better than me, 'The Tank' Hank Gunyon. Next BMW World Champion and boyfriend to the hottest girl in the BMW today! Alex Hollett. Reality, better get checked up on real reality or it's going to bite him hard in the ass, and Tax, well that stupid f##k is going to end up bankrupt after he loses Wednesday and little Billy Arnendez won't be able to get in school cause daddy isn't pulling enough money to even dig his own boot out his ass after I put it there.' (looks over) 'Ah great, here we go again.' (puts down his magazine and stands to his feet)(yelling, looking aorund) 'May I please read in f##kin' peace you moron!?' ~ Security grabbed Hank and escorted him out of the building as Betty puts her hands on her hips and smiles. She waves with her left hand at Hank. After Hank is thrown out, Alex drives by in the limo and opens the door. Hank crawls in and looks at his beautiful girl's face. Hank: 'God damn!' Alex: 'What happened this time?' Hank: 'All I was trying to do was read a damn magazine! Nothing all that bad, this time.' Alex: 'Yeah, management seems to be very angry whenever you enter their foundation.' Hank: 'Not my fault they all a buncha prudes.' Alex: 'It could be.' Hank: 'But it ain't that's the point.' Alex: 'Sure, anyway. Are you ready for your match for the world title?' (big smile) Hank: 'Sure whatever... Life is good, and so are you.' (grins) ~ Hank leans in and the two of them begin kissing as the scene cuts off to black. View Biography
