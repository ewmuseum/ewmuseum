Recently I uncovered a Bobby Angel Tombstone in Philadelphia belonging to a Bum. I followed Bobby Angel and three of his step brothers recently and I am now close to figuring out Bobby's motives. The Dead Man buried as Bobby Angel is Roger Grey. I tracked down some information on the streets and the puzzle is becoming more of an obstacle than I have imagined. I met Roger Grey's Neighbor who lived in a cardboard box. Grey had served in Desert Storm and had flown on Bombing Missions over Iraq. I got to admit, I don't understand why Bobby Angel's name was on the tombstone though. I met a beautiful Nurse at the Community Hospital that Roger Grey died in. She tells me that Four men with ponytails used to come and visit the brain traumatized Grey on a weekly basis. One of the men, who wore a White Robe, came Everyday and stayed 8 or 9 hours a pop. I showed a picture of Angel to her and she said he was the one. I caught up with one of Bobby Angel's step brothers, David Angel....He's a young, acrobatic stud who's learning the ropes of professional wrestling. He and I met at a Philly Steak Stand on the street Corner. I learned a lot about The Angel family and about Bobby Angel's philosophy. Here's a transcript of my conversation with the Young David Angel.... Dick: So David, I know you and your three step brothers killed a guy with a volvo. David Angel: There's not a day that goes by that we don't think about him. Bobby does more so. Bobby retired from NewECW because of the man's death. We kept telling Bobby that it isn't his fault but Bobby still points the finger at himself. Dick: Can you tell me what happened? David Angel: Bobby scored a huge win and we went out to celebrate. Kenny and Jared, which are our brothers, were drunk that night. Bobby doesn't drink. Neither do I. Kenny is joking around and pretends like he's going to vomit on Bobby who is driving. Kenny gets wound up and blocks Bobby's view and when he moves there's the kid standing in the road. All of a sudden, this guy in Camoflauge jumps across and pushes the kid and he stumbles to the sidewalk. But, there's a thud on the headlight and I look at Bobby and I see the hurt in his eyes. We get out and the guy is stiff as a board. DicK: The Court Documents didn't name your brother Kenny in any action. David: Bobby took the blame because he's a stand up guy. That's why Bobby will succeed in BMW because you can't hurt a guy who already feels dead. Dick: If Bobby is so depressed, why is he wrestling Paranoia this week? David: Bobby loves the fans. All of that Death and Gloom stuff about Destruction isn't what Bobby's about anymore. He used to be attracted to violence and groups like Sudden Violence or The Dynasty of Destruction. He had spent most of his carreer slicing up competition with bottles, razors and barbed wire that he enjoyed it. He honestly wants to do the right thing but doesn't know what that is. He thinks that by giving his blood to the ring, he'll make a difference. I think he's punishing himself. His first match where he got dominated....I don't think he wanted to fight. I think it'll take time to heal those wounds Bobby carries. I think you should let it rest. Dick: I don't buy your bleeding heart story. I'll be asking your brothers thier story as well. David: Ask away. We got nothing to hide. Just don't push it with Bobby. He's not the poster child for control. Dick: I'll find the truth out from Bobby.....because its my job. View Biography
