Column: Where Did They Go? And When Will They Be Back?

21 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
- BIG MONEY WRESTLING - Where Did They Go? And When Will They Be Back? [name withheld] - BMW Editor Monday, January 21st, 2002 On Sunday, January 20th, several server crashes shook the Big Money Wrestling network, and in the wake of the problems the message boards went down indefinitely. No one knows what the exact cause of the crashes were, but the server is running cleanly without the boards, meaning the boards were the cause. Most importantly, what's being done to fix this? During the day, on Sunday, many users reported problems with the site with numerous anomalies showing up as a result of the system's failures. I'm asking many of the same questions that other users are, but I'm also investigating what the future of BMWF's forums will be. I, like so many other employees, woke up bright and early on Sunday morning, and thought that I would log in to the site to see what's active. The second I clicked to see my last news story I noticed that the site had suddenly died. About 15 minutes later, it's running smoothly. By the time I logged into the forums it was dead again, which proved that I had plenty of work to do on my day off. *sighs* The joys of being a reporter. If Big Money Wrestling's site is down, so is the fed, and that's something the leadership will not allow. With that in mind, I set a course for work - destination = The Big Money Building. If I'm going to be there, then I know 3 other guys who will also be there: Tycoon, Louis Miranda, and Donomite. The entire substance of this article rests in the brains of those 3 guys. Along the way, I bank off of the stories of wrestlers who are having troubles with the fed.'s network and database. Former Big Money Wrestler, Jake Cage, was in the area on that day. He was showing some old friends what Big Money Wrestling has to offer, only they were disappointed to see things were malfunctioning. "I was just checking things out and showing some buddies around, but I couldn't access any of records on the computer", he commented. Jake Cage encountered an anomaly on the site, and was my first encounter with someone who experienced a freak occurrence. Jake Cage's problem, "At one point, I could never get the menus to work. I reloaded the page 3 or 4 times, but the menus would never load and work. I don't know what to think about this." The problems are a bit of a headache, but are a huge hassle on wrestlers who have business to conduct through the network. "I'm just glad I'm not still here, and having to deal with this. I can't even get the boards to load right now.", Jake stated, irritated with the computer. Not all of Big Money's wrestlers are making a huge deal out of this, but it is one more hassle on these guys, who are already working hard. "It's hard enough to plan promotions and prepare for the matches", 'Thee Obsession' says, "but it was all easy for me. This is nothing. The server going down is less of a problem for the roster than it is for me. I can't get on the Internet and conduct some real business! These guys think they have it so hard, pfff. They'll manage." Upon further questioning, 'Thee Obsession' refused to state what kind of business he had to conduct by means of the company's Internet access. Louis Miranda entered the building when I was about to go to the restroom. On a stroke of luck, I managed to hit him up for some of his own stories and a very odd anomaly that a few people have reported. "This might be of some help... (to explaining the server problems)", Lou stated, "I was in the forums yesterday evening when I noticed the date and time was off. It was reporting that it was 6:48 PM on the 18th when it was 21st, just past midnight." Could this be the problem with the site? The server clock does play a big part in how it operates. Other reports listed changing times on the board, and none of them were accurate: - at 9:15 P.M. PST, the site server reported January 17th, 5:38 am - at 9:42 P.M. PST, the site server reported January 18th, 8:09 PM - at 6:30 PM PST, the site server reported January 20th, 3:03 PM - at about 2 PM EST, the forum reported January 17th, 11 PM Other reports are still being noted. "I think this could be the problem.", Lou says, "I just want the forums back in operation. That's the main lines of communication and we have an event scheduled for Tuesday. We've got things that have to be done." Later in the day, Donomite agreed to speak with me, and he seemed full of details this time. "Tycoon and I have been working with this sense noon yesterday.", he and Tycoon have apparently been aware of this rising problem. He didn't want to be quoted on his statements to what has gone wrong, but he did give permission for the details to be reported: About 3 days ago, the server began experiencing problems. The resources were disappearing and the server would crash. The mail server was continually going down and wouldn't work. We contacted the server maintenance crew to look into the problem. This was roughly 3 days ago, and they reported no problems. On day 2 of the mail server's inability to function, we began to look into the running of the server. Nothing was adherent to the drain on resources. This did, however, show all the signs of being a problem within Big Money Wrestling's programs, unfortunately. 2 days ago is when the problems really became apparent. Donomite thinks that this is favored by the increase of members on the message boards. The boards have ran fine for over 3 weeks, but the move to the new server created an increase in board traffic and the database for the board swelled. The hit on resources became apparent due to so many underlying processes running inside of the board. It's not proven whether the board has a cgi leak, or if it's a problem with too many programs running on the server at the same time. The fact still stands that the forums will not be able to run off of Big Money Wrestling's main server, and everything lines up with the move which occurred about 4 days ago. "What we are currently in the process of doing is locating an adequate server to run our boards.", Donomite has been digging around for viable back-up solutions, "and until we get a sufficient fix to the problem, we're going to fall back on previous forums." "What we have discussed will remain inside of our circle. We have considered a ton of solutions, and the one we're looking at is the most financially sound", he said in regards to Big Money Wrestling's problems. "This company is already an investment, both in terms of money and time. What we hope to do is make everything look the way it did, without having any changes due to this problem" Donomite went on to say, "We have a lot of plans for the future, but at the moment, we need a middle ground. We can't expend ourselves right now, especially with a lot of other debates riding around. This hit at the worst possible time, but it also hit us while we're at our all-time high." He is right about that. Big Money Wrestling is quickly becoming a place you want to be in. At what costs will Big Money Wrestling go to fix the problem? Tycoon responded, "Right now, we just want to get it back up and running and worry about details later." He, too, didn't want to be quoted on exact details, but did hint around when the boards will be back. They should be back tomorrow. Everything will be in the same place it was before the boards went down. No one will have to re-register, but posts made today, should be re-posted so we don't mix things up and lose them. More details on what is happening with the boards: Big Money Wrestling is hoping to fix things with a separate server for the boards. They will transplant everything for the forums to a new server that will be used primarily for the forums. This will fix the problems with their network and restore what has temporarily gone down. Donomite, "If we can effectively put things back to the way they were, that will be a victory. It's untimely, but shit happens." The bottom line: Look for the boards to return by tomorrow. No one should have to re-register, but should be prepared to re-post anything that was posted after the boards went down. None of this is in stone, due to the lack of direct quotes, but the problem is being dealt with. The situation should improve.
