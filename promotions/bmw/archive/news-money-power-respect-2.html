Madison Square Garden fell witness to several new champions, a few debutes, some shocking assaults, and all around top notch matches. "Money, Power, Respect II", just like predicted by many, shattered the ratings, by far. Everyone knew "Money, Power, Respect" was the biggest event of the year for BMW, but nobody knew it was gonna be THIS big! The night saw the change of many titles, including the crowning of the new Tag Team Champions. We also saw the brutal debutes of Raymond Mercy and "The Violence" Vince Jones, although it wasn't a match. Da Thug and ICE took their hatred to the next level in what could be one of the most extreme Million Dollar matches ever witnessed. The Big Money Legends and The 2GLS also had what most people consider the baddest, most hardcore match ever! The main event was also more than entertaining, we have a new champion and a little "business" went down afterwards. Go check out "Money, Power, Respect II"...It's more than worth it!
