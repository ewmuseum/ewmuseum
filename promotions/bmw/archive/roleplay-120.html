Supposedly. Reality thinks he is in Alaska but what he didn't see when running away from the cat and dog was that he had actually crossed the border into The North Pole. How did he get there you ask? Well who knows, it's Reality and the guy is nuts. Reality is sitting down on a pile of snow. His arm length hair blowing is blowing side to side as the wind howls. Reality is wearing his usual long black robe, underneath he is wearing a red T-shirt that is covered with snow. Reality's beard has blotches of snow around it making him look like a deformed snowman. Reality picks himself off the pile of snow he is sitting on and looks around at the acres of snow around him. Reality falls back down to his knees and an icy cold drop of tear slides out of his eye down his face. Reality: Help me god. What have I done to be in such a horrid place? I had the vision of hell being a hot place well this place isn't hot but is sure as hell errr hell. I thought heaven was supposed to be the place that was al white? You trying to tell me heaven is like this? If so I may as well god hell!! Because the snow is so white you can't tell the difference between the ground and the sky. Reality is still looking up as the clouds begins to spread a blue sky appears. Outside the blue sky is still white cloud. Reality shrugs as a man begins walking down what seems to be an escalator. The man has a wrinkled up face and a long white beard. He is wearing a long white dress and sandals. The man lands in front of Reality who is now rolling on the floor laughing his ass off. The old man looks confused as Reality slowly picks himself off the floor and smiles Reality: The man is wearing a dress!! Reality falls back to the floor and the man begins to snicker Man: Hi I am god Reality, and you are wearing a thong so that makes us bas as each another don't it? Reality: Don't ask me. You should know you are 'GOD' God: Yep that's me Reality: Yeah my main man god wouldn't wear a dress God: OK Reality answer me this son how did I make the clouds move and come down here right in front of you? Reality: you heard of a guy called Houdini? God: Yeah of course, he was the man that I made escape from everything. Unfortunatly I told him that he was not invincible at the wrong time, that's when he performed that crazy trick and died. I think he was trying to prove me wrong. Imagine that, a man trying to prove God wrong. Reality: Shut up, well I wasn't talking about Houdini I was talking about the guy that's good at making optical illusions. God: That's me Reality: No you are miracle man God: That's me too Reality: What aren't you then? God: A woman Reality looks confused and begins thinking. God is still standing in front of Reality but he is now stroking his beard whistling the tune of Thong Song by sisqo. Reality suddenly gets up and smiles. Reality: Hey I heard you was all powerful and long you are everything God: Well you heard right then son Reality: Well if this is correct then you can move ANYTHING in the world right? God: Yep Reality: But then you being the greatest creator ever you can create something that even you can't move meaning you are not all powerful! God looks confused and sits down on the pile of snow Reality was sitting on. Reality begins jumping up and down happy that he confused God. Reality pulls a thong out of his pocket and throws it at God. God catches it and looks at it with a tear in his eye God: I created you to be one dumb sumbitch, but yet somehow you outdid me, somehow you used your knowledge to beat me. Do you understand how little you just made me feel? Reality: Well me being a stud'n'all you need brains. HEY maybe I am miracle man? God: Man suck Reality: Whatever, am I miracle man? God: All these years I thought I created you humans to be stupid enough not to question me! Reality: Yeah that's sweet but am I miracle man? God: All these years I didn't show my face on the Earth I created and look what I have become? Reality: Yeah that's real nice but can I be miracle man? Reality begins jumping up and down trying to get an answer out of God but God is still in a state of shock. God has his head in his hands and is leaning over his knees. Reality is still jumping not realising the discomfort he has put God in. Suddenly God leaps up and smiles at Reality God: Oh well shit happens. It's not the end of the world now is it? Reality: Not that I know of God: Look I came down here to take you to Alaska so you can throw the body of bull down the pit of hell! A clap of lightening can be heard a few miles away. Reality looks confused as he stands in front of god Reality: This is Alaska man God: No it isn't! Reality: Oh yeah? God: Yeah! Reality takes a few forwards and walks past the pile of snow he was sitting on before. He points in front of him and makes God look. Reality smiles Reality: That is Dono's house! God: No that is Santa's house! Reality: Oh so why is the pit of hell doing here? God: Look down, does that look like a pit of hell? It's the place where all of Santa's little helpers and faired live! Reality looks down a pit which is close by to see bright colours and a load of fairy's flying about. He then sees a few of Santa's helpers. Reality blushes and turns to God Reality: A cover up? God: NO! Come on I'll take you to Alaska Reality: OK thanks! the clouds reopen and Reality expects more blue sky's but instead he sees a huge spaceship. Reality doesn't take no notice as he begins to rise off the floor and fly towards the Spaceship closely followed by God. A door opens from the ship and Reality enters. God turns to face down and smiles sadistically. God turns around and follows Reality into the ship SCENE 2: 'Alien Enterprise' The scene opens with two men sitting by a window smoking cigars. We turn and see they are in a room that looks like the bridge of the Starship enterprise. Around them are other people who are al smoking cigars. The two men's faces seem ghostly as do the others. On a huge TV screen the people in the room are all looking at Reality wonder around in the ship. Captain: You think he has realised The Doctor isn't god and he is wearing a wig and fake beard? Spock: Well from the Earthlings I have encountered captain the man should soon realise he has been set up Captain: Sock he is a lower form of human. He is stupid and doesn't think much. We call these people trash. I doubt he'll realise he is walking around in a spaceship Spock: Captain that is incredibly stupid Captain: You're telling me. Imagine what I felt when I found out all humans are turning out like this man.... Called 'Reality' At the top of the picture the doors slide open and someone who looks like 'Data' walks down and stands next to Captain Kirk and Spock. Data: Well I guess you felt emotionally drained and shock Captain Captain: Data you are on the wrong ship, on the wrong time.... And on the wrong show! Data: Actually this is the right ship, just the wrong time period The captain, Spock and Data laugh as they look at the TV screen and see Reality sitting on the Doctor's check up table thingy. Suddenly everyone that is sitting in the room that looks like the bridge begin to take off the skin off their faces to reveal a new breed of Aliens. Everyone on the bridge laugh Captain: we are going to take over the world! Spock: haha We look back on the TV screen and see Reality is MISSING. The scene slowly fades away What is going to happen next? It looks as though Reality has been abducted by Aliens that have stolen the Starship enterprise! Will Reality escape? Where is he? Tune in next time If you guys found any of this role-play offensive I am sorry. All characters in the role-play are fictional and don't really exist, except for God and Spock. They work together! The end View Biography
