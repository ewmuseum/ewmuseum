Just as he is about to leave the locker room BMW reporter Paul Reinholdt bursts into the locker room with a camera crew in tow.} Paul Reinholdt : Breaking news BMW fans! The Reverend Billy Blaze is calling it quits! You heard it here first. Now for an exclusive final interview with the man. Reverend! Reverend Blaze a word please. Reverend Billy Blaze : Sure I always have a moment for the BMW fans out there. What's on your mind Reinholdt? Paul Reinholdt : Well Reverend Blaze, how does it feel to be a quitter? Reverend Billy Blaze :Huh? Paul Reinholdt : Come on Blaze, stop acting like you don't know what I am talking about. A ring attendant saw you enter the locker room with a long look on your face, he said you looked around the locker room for a minute before starting to pack up your locker. He ran to told me so I am here to see and hear your final words. Reverend Billy Blaze : First of all Reinholdt, I am not nor have I ever been a quitter! As for your little source might want to tell him to make sure what he is seeing before he runs and reports to you. Paul Reinholdt : Well, I um, I mean he assumed that--- Reverend Billy Blaze : Ah I see that's your first mistake assuming anything when it comes to me, the second is interrupting me when I am here to pick up my laundry. Now if you will excuse me. Paul Reinholdt : Well since I am here anyway, why not take this time to talk to the fans Reverend Blaze. Anything on your mind? {Reverend Billy Blaze stops in his tracks and drops his bag, he turns around with an irritated look on his face, he walks up to Paul and grabs his mic.} Reverend Billy Blaze : Now Mr. Paul Reinholdt, and the BMW world wants to know about what's going on in the mind of The Reverend Billy Blaze. A man that was rumored to show so much promise, has basically hit the so called wall already so they say. He had some easy matches that he won and when challenged by a real opponent he folds like a house of cards. When faced with the a stiff challenge he runs like a chicken with his head cut off. Those are the rumors I have heard about me. Well, my friends let me be the first man, the only man to tell you all that by no means am I washed up! I haven't even begun to shine, and after a little stumble people already want to write me off. Fine by me, you see for my whole wrestling career I have been overlooked because I didn't fit the profile of the typical champion. Well, take a long look at me! There is nothing typical about me. I will be the world champion again, believe me. I will win title after title in BMW and the people will continue to think it is a fluke but the only thing I am concerned with is the impression that people seem to have about me. They think I am a walkover, they think that because of the name I am some easy victory. Well, to all who believe that, I am more than willing to teach you what repenting is all about. I have held my tongue for long enough, I have had the others have their say about what is right and wrong with BMW. They have said how great they are, who they have beaten and a bunch of other nonsense that doesn't matter in the grand sense of things. Well, I will be the man that sets the BMW on its ear and teaches it what is important in this business. Respect! Respect for the history of it, respect for the present situation it is in, and respect for its future. Does it matter that I am only a so called opening match guy? No cause you see what it boils down to is this, I am ready willing and able now to do whatever is necessary to become the star that is in me. If it means ending careers, so be it. If it means forming alliances, consider it down. If it means attacking champions to get my point across, well to all the champions out there consider yourself warned. Paul Reinholdt : Well, The Reverend Billy Blaze finally decides to roll with the program it seems. Tell me Blaze, with your upcoming Submission match against Risk with Tax as the special ref, will we see a more aggressive Reverend Billy Blaze or will it be more of the same. Reverend Billy Blaze : Well Paul let me be the first to tell you that on Withdrawn the passive Blaze is no more, it seems the route of passive wrestling, and wrestling only to win hasn't been the best route for me. I don't know maybe I need to take a clue from the so called superstars around here and become more in your face. More aggressive wrestling and actions seems to be what the simple minded fans want. Well, if that's what they want, then The Reverend Billy Blaze will be more than happy to give it to them! Now Risk I want you to understand something before our match. You will be an example, an example to what happens to people who overlook me. You will lose the match my friend no doubt about it. But you will also lose something far more important than that. You will lose your spirit to wrestle. When you realize that you cannot beat me your pride will begin to kick in and tell your mind not to give up. Your body will only be able to hold out for so long, when your body finally screams out those two words, those oh so special two words you will be a broken and beaten man. I quit, words by themselves that don't mean to much, but in our business its the last thing you want to leave your mouth. You see when a man quits a match, when he allows the pain to control his emotions everybody looks at him just a little bit different, they treat him just a little different also. You see they tend to pity a quitter because he has nothing left in his life. Risk I am not a quitter. Those words will not pass by my lips. I would rather leave that ring in a body bag than say those words! And I promise those words will not come out of my mouth against the likes of you! So my friend you have several options. You can one, not shown up for the match and lose by forfeit, two, show up for the match and as soon as the bell rings scream out you quit and lose without suffering the pain I got in store for you. Or three, show up for our match, put up a good fight, and lose while suffering pain you have never felt before. The choice is yours, but if you notice all of them have one thing in common my friend. Regardless of what you chose to do you will lose! Now Paul if you don't mind I got laundry to do and I am sure you got some other lies, excuse me, I mean rumors to report on. Paul Reinholdt : Lies? On the contrary Billy. All my stories are hard to prove facts is all. But let up expand on your upcoming match. How are you going to deal with Risk ? I mean he has a meanstreak the length of a football field, how are you going to deal with that? With him part of the the newest stable in BMW how do you think you are gonna hold up? And on top of that, you got our world champion Tax being the ref, got any hesitation about that? Billy Blaze : Well dealing with Risk is something I have been wanting to do for a long time. There's bad blood there no doubt about it but I won't risk anything to extreme on this match since its just one match in what will be a long war with him. As for Tax , as long as he keeps his nose out of my business I don't think I have anything ot worry from him. Now I ain't one to put out a warning for anybody, but if anybody thinks they can stick their nose in Billy Blaze's business then they got alot of repenting and soul searching to do! Now if you don't mind Paul I got some laundry to do. {And with that The Reverend Billy Blaze drops the mic, picks up his bag of laundry and leaves the locker room with a shocked and surprised Paul Reinholdt just staring at the door.} View Biography
