Roleplay Directory #132

Anyway the halls are clean and children's bookbags hang on their little respective hooks. A janitor rounds a corner, and a kid scrambles down the hallway holding his crotch to prevent urinating on himself. The camera moves past a door where children can be heard uneagerly reciting the national anthem. Patriotism.. The camera pans around a corner and Pola Bare is seen. Aimlessly staring at the drawings of children which are on a wall. Pola Bare then notices the presence of the camera and stares at it. You can almost see the smirk on his little bare face. He continues to stare at the camera. He is in his normal attire, polar bear mascot costume. He is also wearing a shirt that says Sensational Sally. Why? Who knows. Pola Bare then begins to speak. Pola Bare: You know it's funny. In fact it's really laughable. You see Sensation Sally, you seem to want to complain. You want to try to make me seem like the bad guy. 'Oh Pola Bare underestimates me.' 'Oh Pola Bare has to hide behind a costume.' You want to talk about me running my mouth before I even step into the ring with you? The same goes for you, you don;t know me. You don't know what I can do. You just assume that I'm some kind of depressed man who has to hide behind a costume. Like I'm too ashamed to show my face. And I bet you think you're right. But you aren't. You see you aren't the only person I've faced in my career that has taken that attitude. In fact one guy went as far as to steal my mask, thinking I'd be just a shadow of myself without it. But you know what? That guy fell, mask or no mask. That guy learned that the mask is just for show. It's just a trap. I'm like that cute little dog that you see at the store, he seems innocent but you take him home and he mauls off your right testicle. You see Sensational Sally you've fallen for my trap, and now you're stuck. It's simple really. You see I come down to the ring, to weird music, in a weird costume, driving a weird vehicle. On first glance it almost seems as if I should have my own children's television show. It seems as I'm just a joke, someone they should have parade around the ring in-between matches. But then you step into the ring with me and you find out the hard way that this is not the case. You find out that I am not just some pushover, you'll find out that I'm not just some guy to be toyed with. You'll find out your mistake, you misjudged me. You see Sally, I probably should have let you learn that lesson first hand, I should have just showed up at Withdrawn leaving you engulfed in your little misconceptions. But know you know, you know that you've been set up. Just try to step into the ring thinking that I'm your favorite character from sesame street, see how quick you'll find yourself out on the ground struggling to even remember what happened. Just like when you stepped into the ring with Rockweiler. Pola Bare starts to walk towards a door. Pola Bare: You see it's quite simply Sally, you are nothing but the little bully in the elementary parking lot, nothing but a joke who likes to hide behind his word. Now I know you may be thinking the same about me, but that's your misinterpretation not mine. You see I'm not a fraud, I'm everything I say am. You can call me cocky but at least I'm not eating it. Sure I may come off as arrogant but it's simply because you don't deserve my respect. Your respect is not something I wake up in the middle of the night worrying about. I don't care if you respect me or not, respect and ability are two different things. You may hate me but it isn't going to stop me from walking through you, some fan may respect you but ti doesn't mean your going anywhere either. Face it your career is at a roadblock, your just the punching bag for rookies to beat on, and believe me I'll punch. You want to talk about where I'll be when apeman wins the world title, well I'll tell you I'll be in a fed because obvious by then BMW would have downsized into a 3 man backyard federation. Pola Bare opens the door and the sound of children are heard on the inside. Pola Bare: But with all that aside, I guess I've got some unique training to do. You said it best yourself I'm a unique person it all makes sense. Pola Bare walks into the classroom and the camera follows to reveal a group of children and a teacher. The teacher walks over and shakes Pola Bare's hand. Teach.: We have a special guest speaker today, his name is Pola Bare, he's a wrestler. Some of the kids familiar with wrestling stare at Pola Bare in awe. While the rest remain silent. However one child raises her hand. Child1: Pola Bare did you have to study a lot to be a wrestler. Pola Bare: No, I might as well not have even gone to school, but then I'd be a 'perfect speaker' like our friend Sensational Sally. But that's another story. Basically I've got a good paying job, without any school required. Pola Bare gets an angry look from the teacher. Pola Bare: What I meant was that I had to go through all the grades and study hard so I could wrestle around with idiots like the guarddog week in and week out. Pola Bare notices a child off to the side playing by himself. Pola Bare: Who's that?! Child2: That's Billy, he never talks. He just sits there all day, Pola Bare: Just like that Ikashi Meeze guy. I honestly doubt he'll even show up to the match. He'll be wandering around in the crowd trying to figure out where his mother went. The kids just stare blankly at Pola Bare. Pola Bare: Well I'm here today to talk about strangers. Strangers are people that want to get you in a bad way. Does anyone here not have a daddy? A child raises his hand. Pola Bare: We'll then your Mom probably met one of those strangers in a back alley. Pola Bare: You see people like Rockweiler and Sodomy Sally, and even Ismacki Steeve are strangers. No one knows them, no one knows about them. Child3: But Rockweiler is my favorite wres... Pola Bare: Oh shut your mouth. By now the teacher has gotten pretty disappointed with Pola Bare. She shakes her head and looks at him with disappointment. Because she's disappointed. Pola Bare: You see, if these strangers ever offer you candy, don't take it. If they ever offer you a ride, don't take it. And if they ever try to sell you a videotape of one of their promos, please don't take it. They go on and on and on about how they'll beat me and those other losers. Only to find out on Thursday that they will be the ones lying face down on the mat. But I'm going off on a tangent again. That's all II have to say, thank you and good day. Pola Bare simply leaves the classroom confused as he exits through the door. Pola Bare: That was boring. Well, it's pretty plain and simply, this Thursday I walk into withdrawn with Rockweiler and his master plan, with Freeze and his stupidity, and with the guy who is coming right after me. Sal, I hope the match comes down to you and me, so I can just kick you around the ring, and trust me it will be sensational. You hate people like me? You want to cripple me? Fat chance. You want to try to go on some mission to beat me senseless you'll find out it's mission impossible. When I walk out of withdrawn with the win, don't even bother mentioning my name again. Don't even bother trying to put me down, take your childish insults to your next opponent and see if it works there. I will not loose to you, that I promise. That goes for the rest of you too, Freeze, if you ever figure out we have a match, it'll be too late. Rockweiler, you'd better be all ready to go or else I'll choke you like a chain. This Thursday marks the beginning, just wait and see. After it's all said and done, you'll realize that there is no sensational one. That Rockweiler neither has bark or bite. And that Freeze is just plain stupid. The scene comes to a close. View Biography
