Trial And Error: PPV Preview

27 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Trial And Error - Live On Pay Per View January 27th, 2002 In what has already been the most impressive month in Big Money Wrestling history. Big Money Wrestling looks to turn it up another notch as they venture onto the sacred grounds of Madison Square Garden to present Trial And !Error! The last time we offered a PPV, Big Money Wrestling presented Corrupt Agendas and nobody can deny that it was one of the most extravagant events that Big Money Wrestling has ever thrown, but that was last time. This time the jury has gathered and is ready to deliver a verdict on wether Big Money Wrestling is ready to bring it up one more notch and take this game to another level. Well in this jurors opinion, the verdict has been passed and we are the next level in professional wrestling. Everyday Big Money Wrestling strides for bigger and better things and come Trial and !Error! it won't be any different. The superstars of Big Money Wrestling have worked long and hard to reach this point and there is no jury in this land that can ever convict us not being ready to step it up. On January 27th, 2002 the verdict is in our hand, because we are the Judge, Jury, and Executioner assigned to the case. And in this court of law we call professional wrestling, we can't be proven guilty. We have made it to the next level and now it's time for the rest of the World to prove they are man enough to step up to Big Money Wrestling. As continue to move along and not worry what the next man has to say, because we control our own fate.
