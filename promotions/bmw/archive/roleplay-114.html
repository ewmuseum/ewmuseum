It is My Time to shine and be known as the best! Written: 8-14-02 Location: Arena, Park(fountain) WW#3 (read this one) http://www.angelfire. ~ The scene opens up to 'The Tank' Hank Gunyon walking through the BMW Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio. Hank Gunyon walks into Lou's office to find it empty as he takes a seat down in a black leather roller seat. Hank puts his feet up on the desk and grabs a bag of potato chips from the drawer and starts to munch on them and wipe his greasy hands on files on Lou's desk as he comes across Reality and Tax's pay, before taxes. Hank: 'God, they're making too much money for being little pathetic jobbers. I think I shalt pencil in their new income. Make them make an appropriate amount of money. Maybe five dollars a show, no. The average jobber ocassionally makes twenty-five dollars. Now since they are BMW jobbers, I think they deserve 100 dollars a match, a show. Nah, I'll make it 500 dollars a show. That'll give them a good pay for one week I think.' (laughs) ~ As Hank changes in the records, Tax and Reality's income, the knob of the door begins to turn. Hank quickly changes the records and ducks behind the door. Once the door opens, a stagehand peers in to see if Lou is in. He closes it the second he knows he's not. Hank then exits the office and heads down the hall, acting if though he hasn't done anything. Hank then sees a familiar face as he comes up to the parking lot of the Gund Arena. Hank walks a bit closer at the man leaning against his car. Hank finds out it is none other than his boy The Drummer. Hank and Drummer embrace as Jenn comes from around the car and hugs Hank as well. Hank: 'Hey guys what's up?' Drummer: 'I decided to come here and be here at your greatest day in BMW. I mean BMW World Title gold? That's something to be damn proud of.' (dumbfounded) 'Boy, I'm so damn proud of you. You do deserve this title shot, yes you do.' (smiles) Hank: 'Yeah well Reality and Tax maybe supposed 'Legends', but when it comes down to being better than me? Oh god I really know they ain't better. I am 'The Tank' Hank Gunyon. Master of 'The Charge', 'The Splicer', 'Recharge', etc. I am have so many moves to use in my arsenal, it isn't even funny. To think Tax and Reality stand a chance of walking out the champion is reasonable, but to think of Hank Gunyon, me. 'The Tank' Hank Gunyon winning that BMW world championship? It's unbelievable, but I'll tell you what Drummer. I'll be damned if I'm not going into the main-event tonight and walking out the best damn world championship challenger. I'll be in the world title ranks if it's the last damn thing I do. I am the best thing to hit the BMW, with 'The Charge' to aid me as my finisher. Oh god the possibilities. Everything I've ever dreamed of, doesn't compare to this night. The night that I become the champion of the world.' (grins) 'And cement my status in the BMW as the single greatest son of a bitch to hit the ring.' Drummer: 'Now if I were in it I know I'd win, but you're in it. I'm still damn positive you'll walkout the champion. There is nobody in the business that deserves the shot even more than you, other than me.' (smirks) 'Then again I'm a multi-time world champion.' Hank: 'Yeah, keep livin' in the past, but the present, but for now the future. Well it's going to be me with the BMW world title. A title that you have never held and never will hold. I am 'The Tank' Hank Gunyon, next BMW Champion. Reality, will get checked, and Tax, will be in debt. It's my time, finally.' (grins) 'My time to hold the spotlight and be number one. To be the man everbody wants to see, to view. I am 'The Tank' damn it! It's my damn time damn it!' Drummer: 'Well Hank, good-luck I'm sure you'll walkout wit' the gold.' Jenn: 'Yeah Hank, do a damn good job and show the world who you are and how great you are in the ring. Not just as a hardcore wrestler, but as an all rounded wrestler. You'll give the people what they want to see. You being crowned BMW World Champion for the first time. Hank, we both have the upmost faith in your ability and know you'll try 150% tonight. You're the man tonight Hank. Tonight is your night, nobody can take that away from you.' Hank: 'Thanks, it's good to know I have at least a few friends, people that have faith in me, have my back. I'm glad to know you have the upmost faith in me. I'll get you ringside if you're not already.' (Drummer nods as he holds up their ringside seats) 'Ah, thought so.' (smirks) 'Well if you do not mind, I have a match to prepare for, and a woman friend to talk to. She's been here before me and didn't get her chance. Didn't take advantage.' ~ Hank gets in his car as he drives off as Drummer holds Jenn around her shoulder with his arm. She puts her hand on his chest, then her head on his shoulder. They smile as they turn into the BMW arena and head on in. ~ The a few hours later Hank Gunyon parks his car in a spot right in front of a fountain, where Christine sits on the cement ledge. Hank joins her, looking directly into her eyes. Christine: (smiling) 'So you do listen.' (Hank nods) 'Anyway, I just had to see you alone to really say how proud I am of you for getting in there and demanding your shot at the title. You're the next thing in the BMW hun. You're 'The Tank' Hank Gunyon and no matter how people look at your winning the title tonight, well just know I am in 100% support and in your corner. I know for a damn fact you'll walkout with that strap. You're the man to beat, the man to be looked upon as the greatest thing since, Tax. You're grand Hank, I can't believe I didn't see it when we were dating.' Hank: 'Ah! Shows you to dump a god then don't it?' (Christine laughs while nodding) 'Anyway thank you, Drummer, Jenn, Alex, and now you are all in my corner. You all have so much faith within me. I can't tell you what that means to me. Now as for Tax and Reality, there asses are in for one hell of a match. It's going to end wit' my winnin' the strap, cause it is my time, but it will be one hell of a match. A match to be remembered.' ~ Hank and Christine continue to talk as the scene cut off to black. View Biography
