Raymond: Yeah man, I am starving. Vince: Yeah, so long as your payin. The three men walk off down the road and into the darkness ahead, looking for a shop which sells pizza. The scene then fades to black. I'm fuckin starving... but there aint a pizza place in sight, and I really aint starving for pizza... I need me some ass if ya know what I mean. Its been like 3 days since I cut something, and damn dat aint me. So I parted ways with my Dynasty of Destruction comrades and started to walk down another run-down street. Man, now I can't fuckin remember where I parked the beautiful truck of mine... you know the 2003 sky-blue Suburban with the white leather interior. Too much fuckin beer, and weed... To all Yall smokers and drinkers, and little children... don't be like me no more... I aint a role-model... The scene opens like the last street this one is covered with debris ranging from empty bottles..... to scrap paper...... even a few "used" condoms. Raymond Mercy walks down the grimy street at an average pace, holding his Sean John jeans and trying to avoid scuffing up his brown Timberland boots. He keeps walking down the street, and notices a few homeless people sleeping under boxes. Mercy stops and shakes his head from side to side, then continues to walk down the street. Down the end of the block a corner store lies in the distance. Mercy pauses and digs deep into his pocket and pulls out a cigarette, and lights it up. He continues to walk, after putting the lighter back into his pocket. He thinks out loud as the store becomes closer and closer... Raymond Mercy: I think I should be pissed off... I think I should be ready to FUCK someone up!... But for some reason I'm calm... Yeah calms the way to be just wait until Thursday, and everyone will realize just what destruction is all about... The Scene fades to black... .....:::::Sorry this is just a settler rp, to set up for my next one:::::..... View Biography
