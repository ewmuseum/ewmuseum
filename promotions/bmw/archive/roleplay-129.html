He has kept this secret for three months and his private life has been a mystery to his employers at BMW. Bobby Angel has said to many BMW athletes that he has a calling. He talks in Proverbs from The Bible and seems to be genuinely happy one minute and secretive and elusive the next minute. My name is Dick Chasings and I'm a Private Investigator hired by BMW to investigate the past lives and current dealings of Athletes that BMW believes needs an eye kept on them. I don't like to brag, but I can tell you exactly which BMW Superstar likes to eat his Breakfast after a Daily Masturbation Routine. I know my job and I do it well.... Here are my findings on Bobby Angel. Bobby Angel left The Gund Arena in Cleveland, Ohio at approximately 11:34 am Last Wednesday. He carried with him two bags. He had arrived with three bags so he left one behind. I took it upon myself to hire the Janitor to unlock the area with the Subject's stuff. The Janitor gave me the black bag and within six minutes, I was hot on Angel's trail again. While driving down the Interstate, I rummaged through Bobby Angel's bag. I found a bunch of candles and a few shirts and an address book. The Address Book was a Dark Red book and contained several names and addresses. Girlfriends or Boyfriends, perhaps? I didn't think so....Angel didn't seem like the kind of guy who got involved in personal relationships. Anyways, I followed Bobby Angel to one of the actual addresses just outside of Lima, Ohio. A man answered the doorway wearing a Navy Blue Graduation Gown. Another car pulled into the driveway minutes later as if this visit was planned. I took notes. Two more men stepped out of the 2000 Volvo and each man wore a Graduation type Gown. The driver, who had long brown hair pulled back and a moustache and beard, was dressed in a Green Grad Gown. His passenger, also with a ponytail but with bright blonde hair and clean shaven, had on a Red Grad Gown. They seemed to be about the same age as Bobby Angel. The guy in the house in the Blue Grad Gown welcomed the two unknowns in the house. I got a glimpse of Bobby bypassing a window and seen that he had changed into a White Grad Gown. So, I got four guys in different colored graduation gowns at a house in Lima, Ohio. I read the name off the mailbox. The name was 'Berkowitz, Eli'....I ran a check on Eli Berkowitz and it turns out that Berkowitz is 78 years old and blind. I guess the three guys Bobby Angel is with ain't Eli.... At about 3:54 am Last Wednesday, all four Gown Covered men left the Berkowitz Home and rode together in the 2000 Volvo. I followed them to a Grave site in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania which took about a day of traveling. Angel and the three Unknowns kneeled alongside the tombstone of, get this....a Tombstone reading 'BOBBY ANGEL'.....dated 1968-2002.... The man known as Bobby Angel in BMW is visiting his own grave with three other guys. Spooky stuff. So, I did a bit of snooping in Philadalphia on the name Bobby Angel and it comes to attention that Bobby Angel died in his sleep from Brain Trauma in a Hospital in May 14, 2002. So, who the hell is the Bobby Angel of BMW? Some wack job? So, during the weekend I do some background on the Bobby Angel in the grave. His real name was Roger Gray. He was a bum from Philly who had no home or family. He pushed a kid out of the way of a car and accident reports show it was a 2000 Volvo!.....Thats right folks, Bobby Angel is responsible for the death of a Bum. So, I read Court Transcripts about the case and it turns out that Bobby Angel of BMW along with three of his step brothers were coming back from a wrestling match Bobby Angel had won. Bobby was driving and according to documents a kid about seven years old ran across the street but Roger Gray, a Philadelphia Bum, took the hit by pushing the kid out of the way. So, The Volvo Drivers and Passangers get off without a hitch and Roger Grey gets buried. Looks like I unraveled something very fishy. So, I need to find out who this kid is. I need talk with the Step Brothers to Bobby Angel. Then, I'll talk to Bobby Angel himself and find out why Roger Gray has a Bobby Angel tombstone on his grave. I'm Dick Chasings and I always get my man. View Biography
