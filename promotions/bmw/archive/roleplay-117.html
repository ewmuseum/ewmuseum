We are a nation of hollow stuffed people; bleeding together; heads stuffed with straw. Like wind in an empty street, or rats feet over broken glass in a dark and forgotten cellar. So it is written. So it shall come to pass.' :| Or so I thought. I woke up with sweat turning into frost upon my head before seeped out into a liquid state. Wind rustling, echoing at a haunting chorus. It all seemed dream real to me. Their I sat in the middle of the snow half buried as if fate was trying to shun me away from the world. To kill off my existence. But I couldn't let that happen to me. I couldn't let society win this battle of the known vs. the forgotten. My hair whipped wildly in the wind as I begun to stumble to my feet looking around for some sort of savior but that day, the cold was my savior. |: 'Looks like you need help.' Some figure stood before me at whose face I couldn't quite make out. My vision was blurry and thoughts where razors in my mind when I tried to think of a reply, so I mumbled: 'Who are you?' The one question you ask when your state of perplexity is so strong you don't even trust your own instants. 'What happened to you? Do you know where you are?' He kept asking me questions I knew nothing about. I felt as if my memory was deleted purposefully from my head but then again, I felt a lot of things... 'Is there a reason you stand before me? I sense the winds of fate have brought you to me to be my savior but I will not accept a gift from that of which have taken so much from me already. If you can not turn the tides of destiny that chosen me, me, me, only me-' Loosing it for a moment, I didn't let emotions get the best of me. Like any true person, emotions mean nothing when standing face to face with fear. They only make you weaker. 'Chosen me to live a life of torture.' :| At this point there was really nothing left to say to this stranger that stood before me. The wind bit away at my face like ramped fire ants whose purpose are to strike terror before a sudden sharp pain struck the back of my head and black filled my deleted thoughts. |: 'It was for your own good, when you wake up, all will be clear of your soul purpose.' Spoken like fate herself, the figure spoke to my unconscious body in a monotone that echoed through my empty mind as I lied their. *** Faded Dream *** 'Life of rage, blood stained cage, suicide is an escape from the only fate.' Oddly ringing of a familiarity in my head as I peeled my eyes open once again to see a small child glowing of a bright white light. 'There's a god?' Spoken clearly to the boy. 'It can't be!? I thought to myself, 'If there's a god, why have I been chosen to live in a world of misery and sorrow played out in a sick theme fate likes to call destiny?' Still thinking to myself before he eluded what he had said. 'You haven't been chosen to live by the rules of God himself. Fortunately, you were spawned by hell to spew the truth of a lie they currently live to. You are free, free from society. Free from emotions. And without those binding you down, you can unleash a power so great God himself couldn't undo your wrong!' Each word he pronounced stuck like glue in my mind while I watched the light glowing from him slowly decay into a dark place not even roaches would enjoy. :| Confused, I looked up at the boy who's face contorted into horns, sharp teeth, and horrible red skin which ugly bumps made up for the texture. |: 'FREEZE-IN-HELL!' -Aug. 16, 2002 :| Like being rewinded over and over again, I slip my eye lids apart slowly as if they were too heavy to open as a sudden light split through the darkness of a cold room. I could hear a couple of foot steps walk towards me as I finally saw them upside down through my incredibly blurry vision. |: 'Wake up sleepy head.' Said purposefully with evil intentions, I could feel it with his tone. 'Rise and shine.' Followed with chuckles I only barely made out. :| The doctor pierced a needle into my skin and injected some sort of crazy medicine which I found myself shaking violently to no end. Memories, thoughts, all hurt like razor blades cutting open old wounds and injecting unimaginable pain. Uncontrollable tears ran up my face and turned to ice before hitting the floor below. I was tied upside down. When I began to calm down I was immediately cut from what I was hanging on before hitting the floor below with a * thump! * |: 'Welcome Itagaki Freeze, this is your genesis. After testing your abilities in PCW, I have modified your intelligence so you will no longer make stupid mistakes.' The doctor went on while pacing back and forth in front of me, 'See, we have to see how the human mind works in order to change them. What better place than a couple of stupid guys fighting for money.' Again they laughed and like a *sing-a-long* song, I began to laugh myself as I lied in a stray-jacket shivering. :| While cluelessly laughing like a crazed drone, I noticed where I was. My laugh begun to fade... followed by my smile. Dead people hung all around me in this huge refrigerator they called a laboratory. As if I was some damn lab rat, the doctors studied every move I made and began to piss me off. |: 'I-am-going-to-kill-you...' I had begun to wiggle my way out of the stray-jacket while focusing on them with a wrinkleless face that meant nothing of good to come. 'Get the medicine; he's not pose to be talking yet!' 'Doctor, he's stats are reading 126! If he gets free... OH MY GOD!' She ran off for the medicine but it was too late. I was already to my feet. 'HURRY! GET HIS MEDICINE! IF HE ESCAPES, ALL HOPE IS GOOOAAAAAAA-' :| Stopping his air supply, I locked my hand around his throat that not even the Jaws of Life could pry off. At this point I didn't want answers. My mind was set on one thing. I couldn't quite tell you what it seemed as how the doctors made such extreme modifications to my mind I didn't know myself. Do you know how it feels to find out you were just a damn doll to gather information all this fucking time? He didn't know either so I squeezed his throat until I saw blood trickle from his mouth and flow down his body. Doctors came at me left and right but I killed every last one of those bastards. Kick after kick, punch after punch, neck snapped after neck snapped, I finally stood alone over a sea of dead bodies. No one could give me my life back, it wasn't over. The lady doctor ran back with the medicine only to be to late. I dropped the other doctor's body from my hands I choked the laugh from and turned to face her. |: 'OH-MY-GOD!' Said with pure fear, she looked around the bodies before looking up and feeling frightened to notice I wasn't there but behind her. I loved every minute as he shivered scaredly in my presence. 'Heavy breezes cast on thick vapors propelling curtains in dance on sultry winter night. Electrified air sets emotions afire, felt heavy on the skin, with a foreboding of doom, yet without really knowing. As a storm brews on dark horizons, announcing its approach with a light show of electricity. A low rolling symphony of percussion follows a crescendo portent of violence building. Exciting even at risk of possible destruction. Disaster or promise, merely a twist of fate, flashes of lightning cast flickering shadows, can you feel it?! I'm coming! Be afraid....' I let her run off soon after that being said. It was time for be to move on and forget this misery... or so I thought 'Hello son... it's been a long time.' View Biography
