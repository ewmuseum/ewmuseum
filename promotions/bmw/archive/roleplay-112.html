(Part 3) Written: 08/13/2002 Location: Another patient's hospital in New York Hospital. Who's room could it be Rockweiler? [The camera slowly fades into yet another patient's room within New York Hospital. As the camera pans around the room it catches sight of 'The Violence' Vince Jones standing at the foot of the patient's bed with his back turned to the camera. The camera continues to circle The Violence and focuses on him as he begins to laugh to himself and look down at whoever it is that's in the bed.] The Violence: Hey. Hey, b**ch. Wake up. Ahhh...good to see you're finally awake. So, you must be the little brother of The Rockweiler that I've been hearin' about. Nice to meet ya. Must feel pretty damn good to be the brother of a star. Don't it? Come on. Answer me! [The Violence laughs to himself.] The Violence: Don't look so surprised, man. I ain't gonna hurt ya. I just wanna talk to ya a bit. See, they call me 'The Violence' Vince Jones and it turns out that me and your bro gotta go toe to toe this Wednesday night. I bet you look up to him. I bet he's kinda like a hero to ya. Right? [The Violence laughs to himself and just shakes his head.] The Violence: Don't be shy. You can say what you gotta say. Oh, The Violence also hears that you're gonna be alright. Rockweiler thought you were gonna be brain damaged, but it turns out that he was wrong. Lucky you. Seein' a guy like you fight the odds... [The Violence pretends to wipe a tear from his eye.] The Violence: ...kinda brings a tear to a thug's eye. Now ya see, I'm not that bad of a guy. I'm a thug with a big heart. Ya see, I'm the type that can be pretty calm, cool, and collected when I'm not p*ssed off. Kinda like I am right now. [The Violence flashes a fake smile and laughs to himself.] The Violence: But then The Violence hears things. People say things that just push me to the edge and push me to the edge and push me to the limit and that's when all hell breaks loose and that's not good. Catch my drift? See, I hear your big bro thinks this s**t's a joke. I see he wanna take me lightly. Big mistake. Well, ya know somethin'? [The Violence's expression quickly switches to a look of pure rage and hatred.] The Violence: That's the kinda s**t that p*sses me off! Seein' that Rock's not around that sure as hell sux for you, son!! [The Violence begins pounding on the person that's laying in the bed that's hidden from the camera's view in a fit of rage. All of a sudden the door to the room opens up and Jasmine appears in the doorway. She sees The Violence pounding away at the person in bed and her eyes open wide in shock.] Jasmine: V!! NOOOOOO!!! [She rushes over to his side and begins tugging at him frantically trying to calm him down. All of a sudden The Violence stops the attack and begins laughing outloud as he turns towards the camera. Jasmine turns towards the camera and begins laughing outloud as well. The two of them step away from the bed revealing what was thought to be The Rockweiler's little brother as being only a dummy.] The Violence: I bet that shook him up a bit. [The Violence just shakes his head and looks up at the camera.] The Violence: Rockweiler, slippin' into your little brother's room and beatin' his ass like the dog he is would've been way too easy. I just had to put things in perspective for ya, son. You claim that you're a bad man. You claim that you're the 'Baddest Dogg in the business', but what does bein' the baddest have to do with me right here and right now? [The Violence turns and points to the dummy that's laying in the bed.] The Violence: What if that was your brother layin' in that bed right now? What if this was your damn little brother's room, huh? What could you do to stop me from beatin' him to within an inch of his life, until he ain't even breathin' no more. [The Violence laughs to himself.] The Violence: Not a damn thing. Ain't that a shame? You step up to the plate and go one-on-one with 'The Violence' Vince Jones this upcoming Wednesday night and just like I could've slipped into your brother's room just now and slaughtered him without you bein' able to do a damn thing about it, you're gonna step into the ring and get your sorry ass silenced, no questions asked. Whether you the big dawg, the bad dawg or even big, bad dog it don't matter. When it's all said and done, Rockweiler, the dawg's gonna meet the dog catcher and get muzzled. 'Nuff said! See ya Wednesday night. [The camera slowly fades to black as The Violence and Jasmine exit the room and shut the door.] View Biography
