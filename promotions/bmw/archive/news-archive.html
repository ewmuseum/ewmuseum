Beat Down Opens- Louis Miranda Posted 1-28 by Louis Beat Down (Edition One) Charlotte, NC- What once was just a dream and a development has slowly grown in the last couple of weeks. When people heard of Beat Down they expected a simple school of wrestling, boy were they wrong. Beat Down has slowly turned from a standard wrestling school, to a contest to determine who will survive weeks of grueling wrestling challenges. It has been decided that 10 participants will be teamed with 5 members, forming a 5 on 5 challenge. Each week diffrent challenges will be placed in front of the competitors and each week a diffrent member will be voted off by his peers. Beat Down will air evey Sunday night at 8:00 pm. The winner of the first ever Beat Down challenge will move on to be a part of Big Money Wrestling . Beat Down will be a way for wrestlers to learn and develope skills, not for the experianced. That is why Louis Miranda has asked that only inexperianced wrestlers give Beat Down a try. Another important regulation will be that in no way can the wrestler in Beat Down be affiliated to Big Money Wrestling . Louis Miranda From Trial and !Error! Beat Down- Louis Miranda Posted 1-17 by Louis In a press conference earlier today Louis Miranda announced that he along with members of the Big Money Wrestling family would be running a show similar to the famous television series Survivor. The show will be entitled Beat Down. Louis had the following to say: "You may know me as a bad guy on TV but behind the scenes everyone gets along great. I like the new idea of running a televised series like Beat Down. The idea was brought to me by Axel Teflon and The Underlord. They along with myself will be the producers of the show. The concept is simple, we will take unexperianced and unaffiliated wrestlers and put them in challenges that will test both their microphone abilities and their ring work. Their work rate will be a major factor in deciding who the first Beat Down Champion will be. I am hoping to have a group of 10-16 wrestlers compete. More will be available from the official Beat Down site later this week." So Beat Down will soon be joining the list of BMW shows.
