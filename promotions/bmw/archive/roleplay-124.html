So you might as well say Hell! §§§ Scene Opens §§§ ~~~ Scene begins inside, suprise suprise, a hotel. Sal is sitting at a small desk. He is playing solitare. As he flips the cards his face becomes more and more angered. Finally he throws the cards in his hands onto the table in a bit of rage. ~~~ §§: Dammit! Stupid game! I always lose! CC: You're not playing the game right! You don't have to spread out three at a time. Just flip it a card at a time. §§: That's the cheap way of playing! Anyone can win THAT way! It's the same as if Suckweiler were to pin Pola Bare 'The Mascot of Idiocy' at Withdrawn! He'd hold a victory over me thanks to Pola's lack of skill! CC: Why wouldn't he just pin you? §§: Because, you clod, I don't suck!! CC: But he's beaten you before! What's so differ- §§: Look, I was rusty okay?!? I had just returned from a long absence! Everyone was scoring off me! But now the rust is off and Sal is as good as new! And I'm not about to lose to some genetically enhanced freak, Carlton the freakin' Bear or the guy that really, really, really sucks, Suckweiler!!! CC: You're facing Carlton the Bear?!? The mascot for Hallmark?!? Yeah right?!?!? Oh can I come to ringside? Please?!? Can I?! §§: I'm not wrestling Carlton the Bear you idiot! It was a joke!! CC: Oh... well it wasn't a very funny one. Why get my hopes up only to crush them? That's just mean! Not to mention rude as well!! §§: A joke about Pola Bare you asswipe!! CC: Oh ... but why are you making fun of polar bears? What did they ever do to you? Plus, you should have sympathy, they're going extinct!! Or is that why you're making fun of them? Hmm ... Wait, it's Panda's that are going extinct not Polar Bears right? §§: Oy. CC I honestly think that it's because of you, giving me headaches before my matches, that has caused my return to be a major flop! Now, Pola Bare is a wrestler who dresses up in a fuzzy bear costume. And before you ask, No, I don't know why! Maybe 'cause he's the stupid guy to ever fall into the BMW roster!! Now, moving on, there is no way, that I'm going to get pinned by any one of these three, well, pinheads! ~~~ CC looks to be bored and wanders off into the hall. Sal sighs from relief. ~~~ Alright then, let's just take a look at what I'm up against shall we? Pola Bare. A complete retard that dresses up in a Polar Bear costume for no apparent reason! Oh right, he did have one reason ... to be different. Oh okay then, that makes it justifiable. I can dress up as an animal too. 'Look, I'm wearing a monkey mask! Promote me, I'm different! Market me, I'm original!' You really starving for THAT much attention Carlton? Or did you just grow up wanting to be an animal and then one day decided 'ah, what the hell! Look out world, I'm a bear from here on out!' What kind of an idiot would do this? Are you a petafile? Is this just some big ass scheme to get little kids running into your arms? Man, you're more screwed up then I thought! What else we got? Itagaki Freeze. More like I-Tacky Feint! Where did this guy come from again? The mountain tops of Osaka? Hey, Mr.Freeze, we don't care about your stupid dreams and illusions! So you forgot to take your medicine or whatever and now you're on a rampage, BIG DEAL! I have to put up with CC every god-damn day!! I think I can handle a genetic freak or two. Plus, you've never met a NATURAL, technically sound wrestler such as myself, so you'd be wise to take those pills after all! And finally the bottom of the barrel. The worst of the worst. The guy that everyone strives to NOT be. The Rockweiler. Or The Suckweiler. Or Just the guy that really, really, really sucks. Yeah, I know what you're thinking. 'Didn't he beat you?' Beat ... beat is such a strong word. I wouldn't go that far. More like ... lucked out. You see, some say that I passed out from his finisher, The Weiler Hold ... yeah I think it's a dumb name too but I disgress. Some say that I passed out because of the hold but the truth is, I passed out from the guy's B.O! My lord, with a stench like that, who needs wrestling skills? The Baddest Dog in the Business? More like the Saddest Song in the Business! What? That doesn't make sense? I'll explain. You see, Suckweiler has this song and dance where he tells everyone that he's this mean mofo that'll tear you apart! He's 800 lbs and taller than the Empire State Building! Yeah, and CC can kick my ass! Please, you're just a clown on stilts! Once I tear down those knees of yours boy, you'll be wheeling over to Louis Miranda's desk asking for a long vacation!! Yeah, maybe I do have revenge on my mind, but that just makes me all the more dangerous! So I say, be all you can be. Bring all that you have. Pola Bare, bring your bells and whistles to hide your shame. Freeze, bring your medicine to enhance your un-natural abilities. Rockweiler, bring your huge physic and look down on me if you want but when it's all said and done, I'll been the one looking down on your crippled ass! ~~~ Sal picks up the cards and squares them up. The camera is about to fade when CC bursts up onto the screen. ~~~ CC: That was sooo lame! §§: I thought you left! CC: I did. And that was sooo lame! §§: Oh and you could do better? CC: Watch! Who the heck is this Integra Anti-Freeze character? Does he drive an Integra? Is he a spokesperson for Integra? I mean, seriously folks what is an Integra?!? §§: It's a car you moron! And what about that Rock-a-baby? He's always whining and crying how he'll never be tall enough to ride the rides. §§: He is tall. And that bear! Carlton! My Gosh! At least bring a hallmark card to promote the company you're working for! What are they paying you for?!? §§: CC .... *scratching his forehead* What were you doing before? CC: Counting the hairs on my head! Why? §§: What number did you stop at? CC: Um ... 202 ... no, 203 ... oh curses! I forgot!! ~~~ CC starts to count the hairs on his head again. ~~~ CC: 1,2,3,4,5,6,- ~~~ Sal is slowly pushing CC out the door as he is counting. Sal shoves CC outside and shuts the door. ~~~ §§: So, Carlton, I-Tacky Feint and Suckweiler, Just Remember, When it's all said and done, And you've realized who's just won, You'll never forget that it was, The SensationSal One! ~~~ Sal picks up the cards and puts them into their pack as the camera fades to black. ~~~ §§§ SCENE ENDS §§§ View Biography
