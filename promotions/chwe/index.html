Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Canadian Hardcore Wrestling EFed lived inside its teenage operator's own first personal homepage, one tab among his Nine Inch Nails page, his anime page, and a page dedicated to his girlfriend. "I'm just starting up this efed," its own home page says, "so it probably isn't going to be any good at first" — and what survives is exactly that: one Round Robin to seed a five-match Ladder Tournament for the fed's own Heavyweight Title, and a separate King of the Ring bracket run alongside it, staged over a roster that mixed real WWF names (The Rock, The Undertaker, X-Pac, Triple H, Rob Van Dam, Viscera, several of them billed together as a borrowed "Ministry of Darkness" and "DX") with three of the operator's own inventions — Archangel, Bishop, and Phantom.

Phantom won the Ladder Tournament's own title match against Rob Van Dam to become CHWE Heavyweight Champion; a later champions page instead names The Rock as champion, undated and never reconciled against that documented result. Archangel won the King of the Ring Tournament outright, defeating Viscera in the final. The fed's own home page promised a first Thursday show and a name for it from anyone who'd email in; no such page survives in the capture, and the site itself was never substantially updated again.
