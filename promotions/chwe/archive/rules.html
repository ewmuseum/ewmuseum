Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Real Wrestlers and Fantasy Wrestlers can be used. You must roleplay at least once a week. When you roleplay, only use your wrestler within it. You can use others in your roleplay, if you get that person's permission. You just can't up and challege someone....start a feud with them. You can have as many wrestlers as you want, just as long as you keep your roleplaying up for all of them....if not, the ones that aren't getting roleplaying for will be fired. You can use swears in the roleplaying, but no racial stuff in it.
