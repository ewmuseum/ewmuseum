Ready to Rumble (Part 1 of 3)

14 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
V/O: It's been a history making week [Shots of a group of people holding up various signs "GORE For President",
"Busch Will Lead Us", "I DEMAND A RECOUNT", "I MESSED UP...I REALLY HATE
BUCHANNON" to name a few.] V/O: It's not over yet [A Shot of an American Flag which begins to wave in the air. Fireworks go off all around the flag.]
V/O: Who will lead the new era? [A split screen shot of both Busch and Gore desolves into the logo of AMERICAN
WRESTLING PROMOTIONS...]
AMERICAN WRESTLING PROMOTIONS "Above ALL Others" presents R E A D Y TO R U M B L E [The screen desolves into a shot of the Las Vegas skyline. Bright signs denote various hotels and casinos. The sidewalks are filled with people walking the strip enjoying the many differant side shows and, the streets are filled with many cars buzzing buy. The Camera zooms in on one perticular builiding and gets a shot of the billboard overhead. "MGM GRAND GARDEN ARENA WELCOMES
AMERICAN WRESTLING PROMOTIONS."] V/O: Tonight is the night!! HELLO AMERICA...This is American Wrestling Promotions.... [The camera cuts from the Billboard to inside the arena. The arena is just beginning to be filled with fans. The camera cuts to Colin Blackwell and Vince
Richardson who are standing in front of the broadcast table. Colin is dressed in black head to toe. Vince is dressed in a white AWP polo shirt with a pair of blue jeans.]
Colin Blackwell: All eyes are off of Florida and are on Las Vegas Nevada...Tonight American Wrestling Promotions takes over...I'm Colin Blackwell standing next to Vince Richardson and this is Ready to Rumble...
Vince Richardson: Colin..can you smell it..can you smell it.. Colin Blackwell: Smell what? Vince Richardson: My deoderont..cost me hella lot of money..better smell good. Colin Blackwell: I would ask for a refund..what I do smell though is the anticipation in the air..it's going to be a great night..
Vince Richardson: Of course it is...the higher ups hired me..and I only work for the best...first the AWMC then the AGW and now AWP...we're the best...
Colin Blackwell: Above all others... [The camera cuts from the broadcast booth to show the arena as people continue to take their seat. Camera cuts back to Colin Blackwell who is now sitting at the announce table ready for action.]
Colin Blackwell: As you can tell the fans are making their way into the arena..this place will be packed in a few minutes.
Vince Richardson: Everybody came to see.. Colin Blackwell: Vince I know what your going to say..DONT..Not tonight..these people just attended the press conference held across the way at the MGM conference center..Brittney Christopher our CEO and president opened the doors to AWP..these fans and a great deal of media were on hand for that..the AWP cameras were there as well. Lets take a look...
[camera fades out then back in...when the cameras open we're in a huge conference center. There is a huge stage on the right end of the room and to the left a gathering of fans and media. The stage has several tables and chairs setup and in the center a microphone stand.]
V/O: Attention..Attention [The mumble of the crowd dies down as everybody turns their attention to the stage.] V/O: Ladies and Gentlemen the Voice of American Wrestling Promotions...Anderson
Michaels [A young man dressed in a grey business suit walks on stage and over to the microphone stand. Anderson looks over some cards he has in his hand then begins.]
AM: On behalf of the crew, the staff, and the talent of American Wrestling
Promotions....WELCOME.
[Crowd cheers] AM: Tonight...Together...we embark on what will surely become a journey of many twists and turns...Tonight we unleash American Wrestling Promotions to the world...
[a louder cheer comes from the crowd] AM: And now I give you...the executive board of American Wrestling Promotions...Brittney Christopher, Sean Christopher, Shane Christopher and
Sebastion Manning...
[The executive board members walk on to the stage holding hands as a sign of unity. Brittney is in the middle with Shane on her right, Sebastion on her left, and Sean following. Brittney is dressed in a purple evening gown her blond hair fallling to her left shoulder. Sean, Shane and Sebastion are dressed in black business suits. Brittney walks to the podium and shakes
Anderson Michaels hand. Sean, Shane and Sebastion take a seat at the table next to the podium. ]
Brittney Christopher: It was a dream of mine since I was a very young girl. A dream that grew stronger well watching my father train his students in school.
A dream that was fostered as I became a manager for my ex-husband Sean then his brother Shane. A dream that came to a climax as I lead The Threat to championship goal. A dream that tonight has come true. American Wrestling
Promotions is alive and it's here..from my dream to a live and vivid reality.
[Crowd cheers as the media begin to take snap shots of Brittney] BC: I'm sure there are many questions out there, I will answer those now and then there will be a final statement..so the floor is yours.
[Many hands go up as the press begin to shout out questions.] Brittney Christopher: Yes..the man in a red polo shirt. [The man in a red polo shirt stands up then reads through his notepad.] Reporter #1: Brittney, you call American Wrestling Promotions a dream..what makes you think you can run an actual company though? What background do you have in business?
Brittney Christopher: I'm college educated..I've taken business classes in college. Although I didn't major in business I learned alot. My father ran a very successfull wrestling school, he taught me alot about the business. I've been in the locker rooms, I know the politics. Again it's my dream, everybody out there has a dream..everybody wants to see their dream come true...mine has and I wont see it fail.
[Again the reporters jockey for position to be the next to ask a question.] BC: Yes, the lady in yello in the third row. [The lady stands up and speaks into her mic.] lady reporter: Thank you Ms. Christopher...Sammantha Donaldson for USA Today.. BC: Hi Sammantha... SD: Hi Brittney..the question I have for you is, Your slogan "Above ALL Others" is that just a marketing campaign? Brittney Christopher: (laughing a little) Every business has a marketing campaign. Pepsi, Coke, McDonalds, Burger King..they all have a cute slogan to market their product. Above ALL Others, is it a marketing campaign? NO. Above
ALL Others is a way of life, it's how I choose to live my life and how I WILL
RUN THIS COMPANY. From the production team, to the talent, everybody will give all they have to make this company the best. As I was growing up my parents never asked much of me they just asked that I do my best. High school homecoming queen, Validictorian, honors upon honors in college, and championship gold in the wrestling industry. Above ALL Others a marketing campaign? HaHaHa..
[Before giving anybody a chance a man in a blue polo shirt stands up] Reporter #3: Brittney..you have Sean, Shane, and Sebastion at your side..when you were together as The Threat you did everything you could to get ahead..will you run the federation fairly, straight down the middle?
Brittney Christopher: I can..I will...your right about one thing we as a group, as the threat, did anything and everything we could to get ahead. We will do that here..we will do what we can to make American Wrestling Promotions the front runner in this industry. Will we be fair? We can be..We will be, we have nothing at stake but the success of the company and the only way this company can succeed is by working together. If the talent doesnt want to work with us however, then there's trouble. A couple more questions?
[ Another man stands up before anybody gets a chance.] BC: Please..lets keep some order..yes sir? Reporter: Al Busch, Florida Sun.. BC: Al Busch?? From Flordia..well the explains everything [crowd laughs] AB: If you don't mind I got a question for Sean. BC: Sure... [Brittney steps aside as Sean takes the mic.] Sean Christopher: Go ahead.. AB: Sean..you and Brittney had a storied past in the AWMC..with Brittney cheating on you with your brother Shane..is there any bad blood between the three of you.
Sean Christopher: Does it look like there is...we're standing up here together..we couldnt be stronger. There was some bad blood, very bad blood, that's in the past. It will not be a factor in the day to day dealings of
American Wrestling Promotions. it's that simple
[Sean again takes his seat but before doing so whispers something in Brittneys ear.] Brittney Christopher: Folxs, I'm sorry but we have to cut this short..we'll get to the closing statement..I'm sorry.
[The reporters begin to ramble about upset over the fact that Brittney cut them off.] Brittney Christopher: I'm sorry people but time is short..I will try to answer all other questions there may be at a later date.
[The crowd again comes to order as Brittney clears her throat and looks down at
Sean who seems distracted.]
BC: In just a few short minutes we will release you and open the doors of the arena. There will we present to you the first offical matches in American
Wrestling Promotions history. If you were on hand for the silent opening last week in New York you were witness to how talented the roster we have truely is.
Tonight we present to the world that talent. This group of bright young stars will lead this industry into the next era. That era belongs to American
Wrestling Promotions...let me lead the way..I welcome you to a new revolution..it is indeed a brand new day..
["Brand New Day" by Sting begins to play as red, white, and blue balloons begin to drop. Brittney, Shane, Sebastion, and Sean make their way off the stage as the crowd and fans begin to file out. The screen fades then cuts back to Colin who is sitting ringside.]
Colin Blackwell: Very pointed and sometimes strong words from Brittney Christopher. Vince Richardson: she is lovely.. Colin Blackwell: It's true isn't it? Vince Richardson: what's that.. Colin Blackwell: you were on the casting couch to get this job. Vince Richardson: I wont confirm that.. Colin Blackwell: I bet..folxs the crowd has made their way to the arena two action packed matches are coming up.
[The melow sax sound begins to play over the loud speakers as the arena lights dim and spot lights streak across the arena stopping on the enterence way]
#Diamond life, lover boy He move in space with minimum waste and maximum joy City lights and business nights When you require streetcar desire for higher heights# [Jean Paul Valley pulls the curtains back and steps through stopping right infront of the enterence way wearing a red sequence robe. Looking out over the crowd with a smirk as he is greeted with a course of boos]
#No place for beginners or sensitive hearts When sentiment is left to chance No place to be ending but somewhere to start# [JPV makes his way to the ring and places his knee up on the apron grabbing onto the 2nd rope and pulling himself up as he glances back out over the crowd with a slight smirk]
#No need to ask He's a smooth operator Smooth operator, smooth operator Smooth operator# [JPV climbs into the ring moving to the middle and raising his hands in the air ready for an ovation but is greated with boos. Valley drops his arms down by his side looking out over the crowd yelling out to them "What's wrong with you!?"]
#Coast to coast, LA to Chicago, western male Across the north and south, to Key Largo, love for sale# [JPV takes his robe off and slides it out to the ringside attendent and motions for the mic. He taps it and motions towards the back and slides his thumb across his throat as the music is cut out]
Colin Blackwell: Well I really shouldn't say this but..this isn't scheduled. Jean Paul Valley is out here but his match isn't scheduled until a little later... JPV: Sean Christopher, get out here...and bring that belt too! Hand it over to me, and then you'll be able to put the APW on the map.
Colin Blackwell: What? Jean is calling out a member of the executive committee. He wants Sean to hand over the belt, I doubt that's going to happen. [Sean Christopher makes his way to the ring dressed in a black business suit, his blond hair slicked back. Sean is carrying the Championship belt.]
Vince Richardson: Here comes Sean..and he has the belt. Colin Blackwell: This should be interesting..to say the least. {JPV stands back and smiles motioning to the belt and then around his waist. JPV holds the mic to his mouth and smirks] JPV: I'm glad you've come to your senses...now put that belt around my waist! [JPV turns around and raises his arms in the air as Sean Christopher looks back over the crowd and then looks back to JPV, JPV lowers the mic back to his mouth with his back to Sean still]
JPV: Come on! What are you waiting for! Give me that belt! [Sean looks at the belt, then looks back to the crowd, and finaly looks JPV eye-to-eye] Sean: You want me to give this to you? JPV: That's what I said, give it to me! give me the belt! Give it to me! Sean Christopher: You want it you got it! [Sean spins JPV around and drills him across the face with the belt. JPV lays sprawled out in the center of the ring as the fans give Sean a huge pop. JPV twitches a bit as Sean smirks down at him]
Sean: You want to be champ? You want the title? Bust your ass like everyone else and earn it!
[Sean climbs out of the ring and backs up the asile looking at the KOed Valley. JPV rolls slowly out of the ring and lands on the mats remaining there holding his head] Colin Blackwell: We'll be back
