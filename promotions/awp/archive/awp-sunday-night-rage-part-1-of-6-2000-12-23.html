Sunday Night RAGE (Part 1 of 6)

23 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[JPV stands next to Brittney and the official AWP physician Dr. Terrence Godly. Godly takes Brittney's blood pressure as JPV watches Brittney intently as she looks up at him looking scared to death. Godly looks down at the needle and shakes his head looking up to Brittney as JPV glances over to him]
JPV: Yeah... Godly: You're not wrestling tonight Brittney.. [Brittney blinked as she looked up at Godly] Brittney: Yes! I mean what!?! Why not?! Godly: Your blood pressure is too high, state law won't allow you to go out there and wrestle tonight.
[JPV's lips turn into a slight smirk as he places one hand on Brittney's shoulder and the other on Godly's]
JPV: That's a shame, I would have LOVED to see Brittney beat the tar out of
Troisi since we all know he's a bitch anyhow. [JPV looks to Brittney] Get changed...you need to go to the airport and get your replacement tonight.
[Godly smiles a bit to JPV and pats him around the waist] Godly: If you need anything else just give me a call, I'll be in the parking lot. [JPV reaches into his front pocket and takes out a set of car keys dangling them over Godly's hand and letting them fall into his palm]
JPV: I know where to find you, the black porche in lot G... [JPV and Godly give each other a knowing look as Brittney looks between the 2 a bit confused and quickly pick up on whats happening leaping to her feet. She leans up and kisses Jean quickly and rushes out as Godly follows behind her.
JPV leans back against the table and smirks to himself folding his arms across his chest] JPV: We ARE gonna have the edge in this match... [Camera fades as "Highway to Hell" begins to play. The screen fades back into the Superdome in New Orleans. It's a jammed packed crowd everybody wearing t-shirts of their favorite AWP Star. Some kids at ringside are holding up signs and jocking for the best position to be seen on tv. Fireworks blast from the ring as the camera cuts to Vince Richardson and Colin Blackwell.]
Vince Richardson: We're live!! Hello everybody and welcome to Sunday Night RAGE!! Colin Blackwell: Hey that's my job... Vince Richardson: Not anymore..I'm your boss!! It is the Holidays though so go right ahead.
Colin Blackwell: Wel... Anderson Michaels: The Opening match of Sunday Night RAGE.. Vince Richardson: HaHa!! Colin gotcut off by the ring announcer!! Anderson Michaels: Is scheduled for one fall introducing first standing at 6 foot 4 and weighing at 284 lbs. From Brahim's Village, India....Govindah!
(Daire's Dream plays inside the arena as the lights start to dim to pitch black. Within two bars of Daire's Dream the lights flicker on and off in the arena. A spotlight then swirls around the arena and when it hits the curtain and shines for a few seconds. It seems like the spotlight is waiting for someone to come out but no one does. The lights then come in the arena and the music stops. The camera then zooms around the fans in attendance and as it is doing this Govindah is seen sitting in a chair in the arena. He gets up from the chair and runs down to the ring. When he gets to the ring he slides under the rope and stretches his neck. He looks around the whole arena in amazement and walks around the ring with a smile of disbelief. Govindah then picks up his composure and walks to the ref. He bows to him and shakes hit hand.)
Colin Blackwell: Govindah ready for action..he has a uphill battle to fight as his... Anderson Michaels: His opponent standing at... Vince Richardson: HaHaHa!! Again Anderson cuts you off Anderson Michaels: A towering 7 foot 4. Weighing in at 387 lbs. From Los
Angeles, California. Trey Lawrence!!!!!!!!!
["Dirtiest Players in the Game" By OGC, and Heltah Skelter Filters in over the PA system as the entryway fills with smoke]
## Yo, I step in the ring weighing 420 Cause a dude got get money Mad crews wanna confront me## [Trey Lawrenze receives a nice heel pop as he emerges through the smoke on the entryway. black and silver tights, and black boots, his upper body is shirtless, and a silver bandana is tied around his head. Silver and blue Oakley Juliettes hide his emotion. He smirks at the booing crowd, thrusting a fist skyward, further inciting the sea of people]
##But they can't [beep] with my Speed and my power the combination will mess you up like weed and some powder## [Lawrenze slowly steps off the ramp and begins his ascent to the ring, purpose in every step, and focus prevalent in his features. His gaze never leaves the ring as he reaches ringside]
##The Iron Mike of this mat [Beep] Mad people appear in stories of my mat domination and all my glories## [Trey slides into the ring, stepping inside the second turnbuckle, and throws his hands to the sky as the crowd continues to boo.]
VR: This man means business. CB: You better believe it! [Govindah offers his hand to Trey Lawrenze. Trey Lawrenze accepts it and pulls him into a clothesline. Lawrenze then begins to stomp on top of
Govindah. Lawrence pulls him up and sends him into the turnbuckle. Govindah bounces out and Lawrence with a bull charge and sends Govindah back down to the mat.]
VR: Lawrenze is here to make some noise! CB: Noise is what he is making. [Lawrenze pulls Govindah up and Backbreaker! Lawrenze stands up and plays the crowd. The crowd boo's him throwing trash in. Lawrenze pulls up
Govindah and Govindah starts to fight back. Govindah starts to gain ground he is up on his feet he goes off the ropes and Lawrenze takes him down with a big boot!]
CB: Govindah showing some life, however he drops back down. VR: HA! Trey Lawrenze is all over this guy! [Lawrenze pulls Govindah back up. He lifts him high in the air Bench press slam to the outside. Lawrence steps outside. He pulls Govindah up and sends him into the guard rail. Lawrence with a boot to Govindah's throat. Lawrence picks up Govindah he sets him up for piledriver but Govindah has enough energy to flip him to the matt.]
CB: Govindah still has some fight in him. VR: Only for a short time. [Govindah getting up slowly he hooks his arm around Lawrence's head and BULLDOG DOWN TO THE CEMENT! Huge face pop for the guy from India. Govindah rolls Lawrence into the ring. Govindah leaps to the top ropes and Flying axe handle as Lawrence lays flat on the matt. Govindah locks a leg as the ref slides down for the cover.
1..... 2..... Trey Lawrence with a big kick out. Govindah sets Lawrence back against the ropes and sends him across. Standing dropkick by Govindah. Govindah sets
Trey Lawrence on the top ropes. Govindah climbs up slowly. He sets him up for a superplex. Lawrence blocks it. CHOKE SLAM OFF THE TOP ROPES BY TREY
LAWRENCE!!!!!!!!!!]
VR: DID YOU SEE THAT!?!?! CB: OH MY GOD! [Lawrenze isn't pinning Govindah. He is towering over the man.] VR: He is ruthless! [Quick shot of the many rows of fans, one of the typical shots. A familiar clown is seen, selling beer of all things. Some people tend to get beer for free, especially kids, the younger the better. The clown never strays too far up, always casting an eye toward the ring, every now and then.]
CB: Who the hell is that? [Trey Lawrence pulls up Govindah now. The slump Govindah trys to fight back. Trey Lawrence with the Westside Driver!] VR: It's got to be over! CB: Pin him already! [Lawrence covers 1..... 2..... Lawrence pulls Govindah up. He isn't finished with him. He pulls up Govindah and a second Westside Driver and the cover.
1..... 2..... 3!!!! Anderson Michaels: The Winner of the match Trey Lawrenze Colin Blackwell: Trey Lawrenze the winner in this opening match. [trey notices the clown who is still selling beer and quickly leaves the ring and jumps the barrier.]
Colin Blackwell: What's going on here? [The clown notices Trey coming toward him and takes off as Trey Follows him.] Vince Richardson: I guess Trey wanted some beer!! Colin Blackwell: Tonya Sanders is at ringside.. (The camera goes into the ring entrance area where Tonya Sanders is standing by with a microphone. Next to her is the big man known as Govindah. Govindah is sweaty from his match, and looks exhausted. Tonya brings the microphone to her face with a poised smile.)
Tonya Walker: Govin--- (Govindah quickly takes the microphone away from Tonya and signals Tonya to not talk. Govindah then puts the microphone towards his face and proceeds to talk.)
Govindah: Excuse me Tonya, but this is my interview. I was the man who fought in that ring against Trey Lawrenze therefore; I will be the one who will run this interview.
(Govindah takes a break and cracks his neck.) Govindah: AWP, tonight was the night that you saw the debut of Govindah, the man with the power. The power to hold, and caress, and kill, the power to do whatever I want. Finally, I had the opportunity to go into that ring and face Trey. It was a hard battle, but I enjoyed it. Looking straight into Trey’s eyes seemed to be one of the greatest things I ever experienced.
Seeing a glorious rage in his eyes, the rage of him wanting to destroy and totally annihilate me. It was great, and the greatest thing of that night was learning how to fight a real man. Trey by all means is a real man, and I will always remember this match against him… When he picked me up, not once but twice with the westside driver and totally destroyed me I saw the thrill in his eyes, and then when he got the pin and the one…two…three… victory, it was truly marvelous. I really felt a bit of the power of Nirvana come into me, and it was great,
it was why I came here and let me tell you I will be back in that ring soon enough and gain more of that power. So people around America get ready to see a lot of the man know as GOVINDAH. And hopefully next time I will have the strength to experience a win, but if I don’t that’s all right. Cause victories are meaningless.
(Govindah walks away from the camera. The camera cuts to the backstage area where we seeJoey "IQ" Johnson running out to the production truck. He slams the door open.]
IQ: REWIND THAT TAPE!!!!!! REWIND THE DAMN TAPE!!! I think I just saw that lowlife! VR: Who is Joey talking about! [The Production crew starts to rewind the tape to the introduction.] IQ: STOP RIGHT THERE! [They stop it on the front row where the among all the fans jumping around, waving signs and leaning towards the camera there are two men and a women sitting down, seemingly uninterested. One is a average size man in a Kansas
City Royals T-shirt and sunglasses. He sit just looking around with seemingly total distance. Beside him is a smaller man dressed in a three piece gray suit over a white dress shirt and silver tie. He is actually sitting and reading a newspaper]
IQ: GOD DAMMIT I KNEW IT!!! GET SECURITY! CB: Who is it?? VR: I don't know I think he plays for the Royals. [camera fades]
