Lowdown

23 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
["Final Countdown" begins to play as the black screen slowly fades into the backstage area of the arena. We see Tonya Sanders standing in front of a green poster with a red "AWP" logo. Tonya is dressed in a tan evening gown her red hair up in a bow in the front and pulled to the back on the sides. Tonya ruffles through some notes before the lights turn on.]
Tonya Sanders: Hello everybody!! Welcome to the debut of AWP Lowdown. For the next several minutes we will preview all the action that will be taking place on RAGE as well as give you some exclusive comments as the stars get ready for action.
[The melow sax sound begins to play over the loud speakers as the arena lights dim and spot lights streak across the arena stopping on the enterence way. A
2nd set of lights stop in the asileway and begin working their way to the ring spelling out J.P.V. and uncharacteristically he is meet with an amazingly unexpected pop]
Tonya Sanders: Well it looks like Jean Paul Valley is going to address the crowd. #Diamond life, lover boy He move in space with minimum waste and maximum joy City lights and business nights When you require streetcar desire for higher heights# Tonya Sanders: Colin? You out there? Colin Blackwell: I'm here, dont worry, Tonya.. [camera cuts to show Colin Blackwell and Vince Richardson sitting ringside going over some pre-show notes.]
Colin Blackwell: Valley as always making his own schedule. [Jean Paul Valley pulls the curtains back and steps through stopping right infront of the enterence way wearing a pair of black dress slacks and black dress shirt his arms raised in the air]
#No place for beginners or sensitive hearts When sentiment is left to chance No place to be ending but somewhere to start# [JPV stops at the top of the rampway and looks over the crowd nodding slightly to himself before motioning for the music to be cut.]
JPV: Tonight...I have an announcement to make, it is pertaining to next week's main event! With the PPV right around the corner, I want to give every worthy wrestler in the organization a chance to be in the main event...against me of course [the hometown crowd cheers as Valley glances up with a smirk]. So next week, there will be a special 3 way dance for the American Heavyweight Title!
It will feature..."The Answer" Johnny Troisi! [mixed reaction] "Heavenly" Heath
Quirk! [face pop]...and "Unbelievable" Vic Parker [face pop]!
Vince Richardson: Valley is a Genius! What a GREAT main event for next week! Collin Blackwell: Great Main event, yes! But don't you see what he's doing! These 3 men have to team up this week, and next week they've got to fight each other for the American Heavyweight Title! Valley wasn't looking out for the fans, he was looking out for himself:
Vince Richardson: Oh My God, you're right! Valley is so much more then a Genius it's not even funny! Valley is the smartest man alive, this I seriously believe in my heart of hearts, and so would you since I am the Directory of on air talent, and you don't have much talent as it is...so I think you're gonna start seeing things my way.
JPV: And to make sure that nothing funny goes on next sunday...*I* will don the black and white and be the official referee! Now with that said and done let's get Sunday Night Rage started!
[JPV throws his arms in the air as the crowd cheers franticly for him JPV grins nodding to himself as he moves the mic back to his mouth]
JPV: It's about time! These great cajnuns know a great wrestler when they see one! It's good to be home!
[JPV snaps his fingers a bit tapping a finger to his head] JPV: And before I forget! We have a bonus match for those of you who tuned in for lowdown! [JPV takes a step back and points up to the AWPtron as it cuts to the parking lot. Simon Hunter has "Bombastic" Johnny Fantastic backed up to the wall of the loading docks of the arena and throws several hard body shots]
VR: Whoa! Someone better call for the stretcher for Johnny Fantastic! Simon
Hunter is going to take him apart!
CB: Where are they!? It looks like this is nothing more then a Street Fight with a ref out there to make sure no one gets killed!
VR: Fantastic looks half dead in the back! How long have these 2 been fighting for? CB: I have no clue Vince! [Hunter pulls Fantastic away from the wall and heaves the back of his head into the concreat again!]
CB: Fantastic looks hurt! VR: Hunter made his debut last week..he's an obvious hitman for Valley!! [Hunter kicks up the door leading outside into the parking lot, he grabs Fantastic by the hair and drags him outside!] CB: Hunter pulls Fantastic up to his feet and drives his knee into Fantastic's face over and over! Johnny's head is just rocked by those knee shots and it looks like Hunter has busted Johnny open!
VR: It looks like Simon Hunter has busted Fantastic's nose open! And he's not done by a long shot!
[Simon grabs the back ot Fantastic's head and slams it into the hood of a car in the parking lot]
VR: WAIT! That's my Rental Car!! CB: [Laughs] I hope you took out insurance on it! VR: No! [Hunter slams Fantastic's face into the hood of the car again and starts to grind it back and forth visciously! Fantastic's blood is coating the paint job!]
CB: This match needs to be stopped now before Fantastic gets seriously hurt! [Hunter turns Fantastic around and sets him up against the car, Fantastic is out on his feet..Simon Hunter backs up...he charges at Fantastic and connects with a wicked closeline sending Fantastic over the hood of the car!!!!]
CB: I think he's broken Fantastic! VR: And Hunter isn't done! [He climbs over the car and drags the motionless Fantastic back up to his feet but Fantastic just collapses back to the ground! Hunter reaches down and tears the hubcap off the tire of the car! Simon drags Johnny back to his feet holding him up by his hair. He winds back with the hubcap and drives it into
Fantastic's head!] VR: Fantastic is out on his feet! This is great! CB: The only thing holding Fantastic up is Simon who draws the hubcap back and drives it into Fantastic's head again!! We're gonna need some EMTs out there now!
VR: No! No! No! It's not over yet! [Hunter scoops Fantastic up over his head with a Military Press! He's holding
Johnny up like he was nothing! He drops him onto his shoulder and powerslams him into the asphalt!]
CB: I belive he calls that move The Verdict! And he's still not done! Someone needs to stop him!
[Hunter drags the limp body of Fantastic up again and pressed him in the air a
2nd time...and he plants him with the Verdict again!]
VR: Someone call the corner! Hunter places a hand on Fantastic's chest as the ref drops to make the count!
1............. 2......... 3......... [The camera cuts back to ringisde where JPV smirks pleased with what had just happened. The Videotron continues to show the parking lot where medics rush out to attend to Fantastic as Simon Hunter rises from the pin and slides a hand across his brow and flicks the sweat down on his fallen opponent before walking off]
JPV: And the winner of this match...Simon Hunter!!! Thank You all and see you on RAGE!! [camera quickly cuts back to Tonya Sanders] Tonya Sanders: The action has already started..just think what RAGE is going to be like. With the card that's scheduled
[A Sunday Night RAGE logo appears on the screen.] Tonya Sanders: Let's run down some of the card. The main event... [A split screen of the 6 men involved in the main event flashes on. One side of the screen shows Bad Moon Rising and Brittney Christopher. The other side of the screen shows Vic Parker, Heath Quirk, and Johnny Troisi.]
Tonya Sanders: Inside a steel cage will be Bad Moon Rising and the President and CEO of American Wrestling Promotions Brittney Christopher. They will be taking on "Unbelivable" Vic Parker, "Heavenly" Heath Quirk, and "The Answer"
Johnny Troisi. [Various clips of Bad Moon Rising attacking Quirk, Parker, and Troisi play on the screen.] Tonya Sanders: All 5 men involved in this match have been at each others throat since day one. With Brittney Christopher proving to be the master mind behind
it all. That should be a interesting match to watch. Also on the card are two matches that came about after last weeks mystery partner tag match. [The camera cuts into four sections. On side is Brian Storm on the other is
Xavier Sterling. Jean Paul Valley and Ryan Violent take up the other two sections of the screen.]
Tonya Sanders: Last week it was Jean Paul Valley and Ryan Violent teaming to take on Sean Christopher and Brian Storm. As we all saw Christopher was attacked by Violent and Valley this lead to Xavier Sterling being added to the match. The final outcome saw Sterling and Storm with their hands raised in victory. Tonight Valley and Violent try to get pay back. We will review some more of tonights RAGE in a minute. Right now we're going to show you highlights of a match that took place a little while ago..two newcomers in action as Steve Greddy takes on "Deadly" Taylor Douglas
[ How do you want it by Tupac Shakur begins to play over the Pa System as the crowd turns there heads to get a peak at what's coming out. There is a slight delay as the music continues to play, but Nobody ever appears from the back.
The fan's begin to get restless and start a APW chant. This goes on for a good thirty seconds, untill finally we hear a voice...]
V/O : Mirror....Mirror on the wall.Whose the richest of them all ? [ Suddenly a previously recorded group of people echo over the arena and say
"Steve Greedy that's Who"...The fan's stop there APW chant and begin to boo as from the back comes Steve Greedy. He is wearing a pair of khacki pants, Dock
Martins, A nice Armoni Jacket plus shirt, With a tie and his rolex watch sparkles over the crowd. He slowly walks down to the ring as the boo's get louder and louder with every passing moment. As he get's closer to the ring we can see him pull a microphone from his coat pocket. With a smirk on his face the size of this New Orleans, Arena he enters the ring and holds the mic up to his lips.He acts as if he is about to speak but then just laughs.The fan's boo even more. The background music fades out, but the Booing continues all over the arena. Greedy then pulls a small mirror from his other coat pocket and with his right hand holds that up to where he can see his face. With the mic in his left hand, and his eyes on what is in his right hand he this time really begins to speak]
[Steve Greedy] : "Oh Mirror.....All those people who say I am the greatest,
Richest and most handsome. Obviously aren't as Dumb as they look [ Greedy
Chuckles as the crowd Boo's. He put's the Mirror back in his coat pocket and now holds the mic firmly in his right hand instead of left. When the crowd quiet's down just a bit he continues ]....Friday night you saw me compete on a house show in Washington. Very impressive performance if I do say so myself,
But for those who thought that was impressive."
[ Greedy pauses for a few seconds and looks over the crowd before continuing with his words.]
[Steve Greedy] : " Wait till you see me in the ring tonight. Taylor Douglas will be my first victim on a televised show. Boy, He should feel privledged that he gets to lose to me infront of so many people.."
[ Greedy shakes his head in disbelif ] [ Steve Greedy] : " But the more I think about this Douglas guy.The more I tend to beleive he won't be happy with doing What he is gonna have to do. He actually thinks he can beat me [ Greedy burst out into laughter and after three seconds of it suddenly stops and begins to get more serious ] Sorry...I thought that was funny. "Deadily" Taylor Douglas...I can't agree that your deadily, but if you want to really face me in this ring tonight.Then you might see that I am
"Deadily". I grew up not to far from New Orleans over in that incest state of
Arkansas. Little Rock to be exact..I am sure there is alot of Greedy Followers here in attendance..Who just can't wait for me to make my triumph return to the ring [ The crowd Boo's ] See what I mean APW ?.....About a year and a half ago
I was hurt over in Baton Rouge, Louisiana in a chaotic Cage match....I won the match of course, but afterwards it's safe to say I was feeling the pain..I tried managing a team ,but they obviously weren't good enough for my leadership. So here I am."
[ Greedy lets off a devilish trademark smirk...and walks over to a corner of the ring.] [ Steve Greedy ] : " Lucky you...Unlucky for me and most of all Unlucky for
Douglas. Yall are lucky because you get to see perfection in the ring, I am unlucky cause I am in New Orleans, Louisiana of all places and Unlucky for
Douglas...Because he has to face the rich one in a one on one contest...No pun intended, but that right there is "Deadily" My friend."
[ Greedy removes his jacket and gently hands it to a ringside person. He then removes his tie and Shirt. He does the same with it. You can now see a gold arrowhead necklace on which was buried under his other attire. He kisses it once for good luck an Old Arkansas tradtion...Then begins bounce around on the ropes for a bit....With the mic still in hand he stops and say's his final words.]
[Steve Greedy] : " Memories....Yes Indeed [ Sighs ]...Not all good ones, but memories None the less. Douglas, I think it is safe to say you can come on down to the ring now.....Well it might not be SAFE actually but the time has come to stop telling you and show you. Money isn't the only thing, It's _EVERYTHING_"
[ Greedy tosses the mic to the ground on the outside and continues to bounce off the roeedy-vs- "Deadly" Taylor Douglas pes waiting for Douglas to make his appearance.] ========================================= Steve Greedy-vs- "Deadly" Taylor Douglas ======================================== Taylor Douglas took early control of this match as Steve Greedy seemed to not want to lock up. Douglas used a variety of suplexs to take the opening advantage. Greedy went to the outside to get some fresh air but instead was sent into the steel steps, the ring barrier, and the timekeepers table.
Douglas set Greedy on the top rope which was the turning point in the match. Greedy reversed the momentum from Douglas and hit a superplex. Trying to end the match there Greedy made a pin attempt and got a two count. Greedy making the match on his side went to work on Douglas showing off his superior technical skills.
Douglas would try to fight back on a couple of occassions landing several neckbreaker combos however Greedy would fight back everytime. After several hard fought minutes Greedy was able to put Douglas away with the DDT variation he calls bankrupt.
Time: 9:45 Winner: Steve Greedy ============================== [Camera cuts back to Tonya Sanders who is holding what looks like several merchandise items.]
