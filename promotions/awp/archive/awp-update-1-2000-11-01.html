The purpose of which was to announce to grand opening of the newest professional wrestling promotion. American Wrestling
Promotions, which will be listed as AWPE on the New York Stock Exchange, held a silent opening this tuesday as they celebrated Halloween in the streets with four action packed matches. The offical grand opening event titles Read to
Rumble will be held on Sunday November 12th at the MGM Grand Garden Arena. The
CEO and President of American Wrestling Promotions would not offer much comments put promised a special press conference as part of the 2 match event.
The card for that event was scheduled to feature two (2) four corner challenge matches. The order of elimination in those matches will determine the offical championship rankings for AWP and will set the title matches for the first national TV taping of Sunday Night RAGE.
The matches are as follows MATCH #1 Johnny Troisi -vs- "The Principal" Simon Dreager -vs- Heath Quirk -vs- "The Warrior" Logan Gunz MATCH #2 Jean Paul Valley -vs- Billy Bluebie -vs- "The Underdog" Steven Underwood -vs- TBA Match Stipulations <>The matches will be single elimination <>elminination occurs by pinfall, submission, count out or DQ. <>Elimination will continue until there is one man left, he will be declared the winner. <>The order of elimination will determine the matches for the first ever Sunday Night RAGE. <> With the first person eliminated in either match facing each other, the second man facing each other and so forth.
<> The thrid man eliminated from either match will face each other for the Television title. <>The winners of their respected match will face for the American Heavyweight Title. <>The win/loss of those matches will determine overall rankings. ALSO on the card CEO and President Brittney Christopher will have a special Press conference about the future of American Wrestling Promotions.
A special FanFest which will feature the opportunity for the fans to meet and greet the stars of AWP.
