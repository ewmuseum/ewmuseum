Voices of Venom Vol1.1

5 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
######################################## AWP presents Voices of Venom Sunday, November 5th, 2000 [Inrings/Vignettes/Interviews to [detail withheld] by Wednesday November 8th at 12am EST] [Roleplays to [detail withheld] by Friday November 10th at 12am] ######################################## IN THIS EDITION: <>Jean Paul Valley <>"The Answer" Johnny Troisi <>Vic Parker WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW [The camera cuts away to Jean Paul Valley who stands unseen off to the side looking through a partially opened door at the other wrestlers of the AWP signing autographs. Leaning against the molding wearing a basic pair of jeans and a blue polo shirt, his hand slipped into his pocket and the other moving along the side of his face absently. He glances over his shoulder as Sean
Christopher walks towards him]
Sean: Are you gonna get out there? [JPV raises his brow and turns slightly to look at Sean] JPV: No. Sean: You said you'd do this, now you're gonna back out on us? JPV: I said I would be here today, I didn't say anything about signing any autograph for any of these idiots out there, you're lucky I lowered myself to join this backwater organization, I will not lower myself to have any contact with these trailor park pieces of trash.
Sean: Alright, then you got no reason to be here tonight, get out and be in
Vegas on the 12th or this "Backwater Organization" will have one less overpaid premadona to write a check for.
JPV: Overpaid [laughs] I took a cut to be here! If it wasn't for Lady B wearing out my pager battery and running up my answering machine, voice mail, email and fax line I wouldn't even have given the thought of AWP cross my mind, but with all the stalking she did, I figured it would just be easier to join then hire more bodyguards to keep the obessed woman away.
Sean: That's great jack ass, either you get out there or be at the MGM Grand on the 12th [JPV waves the back of his hand off slightly shooing Sean away as Sean just stands there watching him. JPV looks back to Sean and shakes his head rolling his eyes, he reaches out and opens the door and steps out into the meet and greet area behind the vast majority of the fans. He glances back over his shoulder looking at Sean and shakes his head making his way towards the main doors to leave as a few floating fans looking to complete their collection rush over to him. JPV stops in his tracks as they rush up to him about 5 fans surround him holding out their black and white glossy photos to him as he trys to push them away finally just grabbing one as he walks, reaching into his pocket he takes out a sharpe scribbles something on the picture. Reaching back he does the same to another as he pushes the door open. One of the teens who had his picture autographed looks down at it]
Teen: ~You suck, JPV~? Hey screw you man! You suck! Jerkoff! [JPV laughs as he gives the door a shove closed and cuts back to the others] WVWVWVWVWVWVWVW (Scene opens up on what appears to be some kind of open-plan appartment somewhere. The dark blue walls are absolutely plastered with posters. Che
Guevara, Talking Heads, Jean Paul Sartre, Nanni Moretti's film "Caro Diaro",
Disposable Heroes of Hiphoprisy, the list goes on and on. Possibly in order to add length to any future RPs. Anyway, seated in the middle of the room, at a desk, typing away at a computer. Sensing the presence of the camera crew, your man turns around. He's about 6'3", 315 pounds, and, yup, he's
AWP superstar "The Answer" Johnny Troisi. Deep hazel eyes, long straggly jet black hair, and a longsleeve tee-shirt with the legend "Freak
Intelligentsia" written on it. Anyway, looks like he's gonna talk. Listen up, fool) Ah, I'm assuming you must be the select group of trained monkeys and former
Chicago Hope extras that make up AWP's camera crew, no? So, what are you here for? Want me to indulge the many and varied riveting secrets of my life? Coin some catchphrases so you can get the tee-shirts printed already?
Or just make some hi-la-rious puns out of the names of the three jesters
I'll be taking on in my first match here? Because, remember kids, being different sucks. It's cool to conform.
(Our boy drags a hand through his hair) Yeah, that was irony. I know it might be a rather complex concept for all of you City Guys fans out there, but you're just gonna have to catch up.
Freak intelligentsia speaking, understand? Anyway, I suppose I might as well tell you the facts of my life before the National Enquirer starts claiming threesomes between me, the black girl from Sabrina, and much loved nineteenth president of this nearly great country I've been dispatched to,
Rutherford Hayes. As you can tell from my accent, I'm from England. No, we don't all talk like Austin Powers. If I spoke like Mike Myers around my hometown, I would have had a beer bottle smashed over my head, possibly by a large man in the possession of many varied tattoos. I'm from a town called
Northampton, a little bit like "A Town Called Malice", except it isn't a song by early eighties mod-punks The Jam. Celebrities from Northampton include bulimic dead person Lady Diana Spencer, early 80s Goth merchants
Bauhaus, and "Screamin'" Norman Smiley. A whole platitude of society, no? (He chuckles at an invisible joke) Anyway, if things go right, looks like I'll be joining that holy trinity soon. And, instead of doing something useful, like finding a cure for AIDS or playing bass in a alternative punk band, I've decided to opt for beating some random strangers unconscious, until their shoulders remain pinned down for three seconds. Gosh, my parents must be so proud...
Anyway, I'm sure you'll all have read my biography on AWP's website, you'll all know I was voted "Most Likely To Become A Serial Killer" in my school yearbook, and that I have a degree in English literature, but something tells me that Simon Dreager, Heath Quirk, and Logan Gunz ain't gonna be too interested or intimidated by my knowing about how the dancing of the tarantella shows Nora's true feelings in Henrik Ibsen's "A Doll's House".
So, allow me to talk about each man in turn. First up, we've got good ol' Simon Dreager. He's a principal, apparently. Now, excuse me for coming from a country where people still listen to Len, but I think a principal is something like a headmaster? The sort of crazy shenanigans that the characters we all knew and loved in Saved By The Bell springs to mind. Simon- I'll be honest with you. Back where I come from, teachers are overpaid, self-important, right wing, cultural conservative spawn of Satan. My dad worked on a production line before the back pains confined him to a wheelchair, and he did twice as much work as your boys, and for half the cash. Maybe it's different over here, I've noticed a lot of cultural differences. Your version of Big Brother sucks for a start.
And you appear to find Dharma and Greg funny as well. But, Simon, when it comes to me and you, I won't be treating you as a wrestler. I'll be seeing every single teacher I had who didn't believe, every single one of those grudge bearing public school educated c(bleep)nts, and it won't be pretty.
You're going to get broken like a blues singer's heart. Just be prepared. Next on my list, Heath Quirk. Heath, I'll admit, I know nothing about you. The link to your profile on the website was down, and I never saw you in PWI or Poweslam Magazine back in the UK. I don't know anything about you, whether you like the humorous cartoons of James Thurber, whether you're a big fan of Scandinavian chanteuse Lene Marlin, or whether you have sexual intercourse with dehumidifiers. Anyway, to quote Craig David, could you fill me in? Do you get Craig David over here? Yeah, you, (points beyond the camera), the one with all that going on with the hair, do you have Craig
David in Vietnam-bombing land?
(a voice shouts out "No") Lucky gets. And, finally, Logan Gunz. He calls himself "The Warrior". For some reason, I have this image of rope shaking and crap matches. Who can tell what the future holds? Well, Johnny here can. Logan- for you, the future holds continued high interest rates at your bank, a nice lobster dinner with your wife in three weeks time, and a good hard kicking from me at "Ready To Rumble". You may want to note the dates down in your diary, unless you have a pretty good memory. Good to see spelling names with the letter "z" (which Troisi pronounces as "zed", because he's British see?)
didn't die in 1991, huh? (Johnny coughs slightly) Anyway, that's all three done, correct? Don't think of me as arrogant, annoying, smug, whatever. We all wanna be loved, no? Just some of us go about it, to use an English expression, ass-upwards. Anyway, if you'll excuse me, I've got work to do. I'm going to write to every single music paper I can find, and complain about how little coverage they gave Ben Folds
Five's split, and instead just published another fawning interview with PJ
Harvey, or some other muso icon. Anyway, bye.
(Troisi swigs from a bottle of mineral water on his desk, before going back to typing away at his PC. The camera crew, after a few seconds, realise he isn't going to bless us with any other great comments, and just leave the room. Fade to the good old black drinking whiskey and rye)
WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW [Scene opens with "Unbelievable" Vic Parker holding a gum bag. It's a Toronto Maple Leafs logo on the bag. Parker has on a pair of work out pants and a Toronto Maple Leafs sleeveless shirt on. He is standing in front of a locker at the local Toronto gym.]
[Parker] Wassup AWFers!!! I know the AWF has seam "dull" so far. You haven't had anyone Exciting yet sign an contract. Looking down the roster,
I don't see anyone very exciting.
[Parker pulls out the list of the first card in AWF.] [Parker] Johnny Troisi, "The Principal" Simon Dreager, Heath Quirk, "The Warrior" Logan Gunz, Jean Paul Valley ,Billy Bluebie, "The Underdog" Steven Underwood... None of these guys sound or look to Unbelievable.... [Parker smirks.] [Parker] HOWEVER..... Never fear, the boredom will end. I will tell you what is Exciting. I will show you what is Unbelievable. 220 lbs of solid muscle. 220 lbs of true excitement. 220 lbs of what the ladies say is truly _unbelievable_. You see, AWF knew that these losers couldn't excite the crowd and in order for the AWE to make their mark in the crowded wrestling world they started to dig deep.
[Parker tears the list up.] [Parker] So they dug deep at the Toronto Maple leafs training camp, and scout happen to see something Exciting he happen to see something
UNBELIEVABLE, and that was me Vic Parker. So he handed me an offer I couldn't refuse and here I stand ready to add a little excitement to a fed in dire need of it.
[Parker starts to head into the gym. He stops and looks back at the camera.] [Parker] I'll be watching the first night, I'll decide who is worthy of stepping into the ring with the savior. Until then train hard while I style being strictly Unbelievable!
