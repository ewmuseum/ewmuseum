Sunday Night RAGE (Part 6 of 6)

23 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
ONE... TWO.... [Heath Quirk dives forward and nails Blades with a double axehandle across the back of his head breaking the count]
VR: Damn it! CB: "The Edge" almost had him but broken up by Heath Qurik! And it looks like
Troisi's bleeding now as well!
[Blades and Troisi begin to battle back and forth as Davidson finally recovers and clubs Quirk from behind with a forarm. Quirk continues to fight back against Davidson and Blades as Parker watches for a moment and quickly moves closer to the other side of the ring from the apron and begins to climb up to the top. Blades, Davidson, and Qurik continue to battle away as Parker stops climbing about 5 2 feet from the top and dives off backwards with a moonsault into the 3 as the crowd bursts into a hugh pop as all 4 men go down!]
CB: Oh My God! It looks like a car reck in rush hour on I-90! VR: That Vic Parker seems to be hurting as bad as Blades, Davidson and Quirk! And Troisi is still down and Underwood seems to be holding his lowerback in pain himself! [Jean Paul Valley reaches into his pocket and takes out a small solid piece of steel and slips it through the holes in the cage to Blades who palms it. The crowd begins to stir even more as Simon Hunter starts to walk down the asile towards the ring moving infront of the door]
CB: Valley just passed something to Blades! VR: I didn't see anything! [Underwood starts to slowly climb up the cage again as Vic Parker crawls out from the pile and moves towards Underwood again. Davidson reaches over and starts to gouge at the eyes of Quirk. Parker catches up to Underwood's side of the ring and begins to climb up the cage as well, Underwood almost at the top and Parker's right behind him! Parker nails Underwood with a kidney punch stopping Underwood cold and leaving him there in a dead hang. Parker caches up with Underwood and grabs him by the back of the head and begins to slam his head into the cage. After the 4th slam Underwood falls back into the center of the ring!]
CB: Underwood is going to need to be scrapped off the mat! [Troisi gets to his feet slowly as Blades waits on one knee, he flicks his wrist and a police baton spring forth.]
[WACK!] [Blades swings it with all his strength behind it into Troisi's ribs. Troisi lets out a scream and drops to his knees as Blades stands over him and drives the baton into his lowerback and kidneys! Blades turns around as Davidson looks up]
[WAAACCCCK!] [The Evil One is nailed between the eyes with the baton as Blades stomps him down] VR: What's going on! CB: Blades just may have killed Davidson! [Davidson doesn't move as Blades drops down to his knees and drives the butt of the baton onto his throat and begins to choke him out. The whole time Vic
Parker climbs to the top of the cage and perches there for a moment before standing straight up and blowing a kiss to the crowd. Parker leaps off the top of the cage with a Shooting Starpress onto Underwood in the center of the ring.
Parker lays ontop of Underwood as the ref turns back to the 2 and drops down to count the pin]
CB: OH MY GOD! ONE.... TWO.... THREE!!!! *DING DING DING* [Parker rolls off of Underwood holding his stomach as Brian Blades looks over at Underwood and Parker, Parker backs off quickly as Blades stalks over to him.
Parker grabs the ropes and pulls himself up quikcly as Blades quickly swings the baton at him but pulls up short handing the grip end to him]
VR: What is Blades doing!? [Simon Hunter reaches up grabbing the cage door with both hands and litterally rips it off it's hinges as the crowd is in shock at his strength and what's going on in the ring. Parker grins to Blades and then looks over the crowd as his right hand gribs the baton. Parker spins it around in his hand and drives it down into Underwood's head!]
CB: What's Parker doing! VR: He's making a career decision! He's siding with Valley and Comapny! CB: He's Selling out! [Simon Hunter moves over towards Quirk and drags him to his feet. Hunter whips quirk towards Parker who closelines Heath with the Baton taking him down. JPV and Brittney quickly rush into the ring as the crwod watches on stunned. Simon
Hunter and Brian Blades pick Underwood up and lift him straight into the air.
They hold him up for a moment and nail him with a double power bomb in the center of the ring]
VR: It looks like the Bad Moon has finally gone down! CB: It looks like Brittney has fired them! [Valley rushes over towards the downed Troisi and begins to kick away at his ribs, Troisi trys to cover up but to no luck, Valley delivers one last kick and turns back to the center of the ring. Brittney opens up the brief case and takes out 3 shirts throwing them to Parker, Blades, and Hunter. The 3 put the shirts on and Valley tears his shirt open tossing it aside wearing a "Blue
Chips" T-shirt. Blades, Parker, and Hunter are all clad in one as well at this point. Valley reaches into his back pocket and ducks down infront of the other
3 with Brittney placing her hand on his shoulder]
Valley: All of the Blue Chips...have just been drafted! [The camera focuses in on the 5 of them slowly fading to black]
