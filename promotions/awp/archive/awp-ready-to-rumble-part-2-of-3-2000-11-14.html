"THE_WARRIOR", LOGAN GGGGGUUUUUUUNNNNNNNNNZZZZZZZZZZ!!!!
[Several small patches of light strike the arena floor, giving the appearance of sunlight just breaking through a canopy of trees. Pouring out over the speakers is the sounds of the forest. Birds chirp happily amongst themselves, bugs sing to each other through tall grass, the other miscellaneous critters mill about. Nature's own music plays undisturbed as it has for thousands of years...]
CRUNCH... CRUNCH... CRUNCH... [Off in the distance the sound of dried leaves disturbed echoes. Though faint at first, the sound is slow, methodical, like an animal stalking it's prey. As the sound draws closer the sounds of the forest become more excited, as if the animals are warning each other of the impending doom that comes their way. The footsteps grow louder and louder, the animals more and more excited, finally it sounds as if the footsteps hover somewhere directly over the arena. Then, all goes silent, and thr roar of what must be a big cat cuts through the air followed by a voice....]
"DOOM_ON_YOU!" [Cannisters on either side of the entranceway explode as "Iron Man" By Black
Sabbath blares over the arena's PA system. The crowd roars with approval, their cheers near deafening.]
[Now standing in the entranceway is an imposing figure. His broad shoulders seem to stretch the expanse of the entranceway, his massive arms are cocked to either side of his body, his black gloved fists clinch and unclinch rythmically. His head is tilted slightly down as he stares directly into the ring with his pair of steel green eyes, the fire in them ever increasing with the cheers of the crowd. A clean shaven head is covered with a blue bandanna tied tightly around it, the word "GUNZ" written across the forehead in black lettering.]
[His countenance does not change, there is no smile...there is no expression of joy. There is only the fires that burn within him which drive him. He is the picture of intensity.]
[You can almost hear the seams of the black t-shirt he wears straining to cover his massive torso. The shirt simply has the word "WARRIOR" written on the front, fire rising from behind the steel lettering...a panther appears poised to strike atop it. His arms, tense with anticipation, also display a pair of matching panther tattoos. He wears a pair of deep blue, full length wrestling
tights. Down the right leg a black panther seems to almost cling to Logan's extremity for dear life, his long white incisors flaring...down the left leg the word "GUNZ" in simple black lettering. Across the back of his tights, also in black lettering are the words "Pao-Do", which translated from the Chinese means, "The Way Of The Panther".]
[Gunz begins his march to the ring, his eyes never unlocking from the ring. Fans on either side of the aisle reach out to touch him, but he pays them no mind...not out of indifference, but out of the intensity with which he approaches each war in the ring. He moves rather slowly, appearing to stalk the ring as he reaches ring side. Then, in one motion, he slides in under the ropes and pulls himself to his feet.]
[Gunz moves to the center of the ring and raises one gloved fist towards the fans, several respond with the same gesture. Gunz discards his bandanna and t-shirt and, with fire burning in his eyes, waits for the bell...]
Vince Richardson: Looks like someone has decided to show up to fight.. Colin Blackwell: Logan Gunz has made a name for himself in some circles of the wrestling industry. He is indeed a warrior...
[the lights in the arena quickly go out.] Colin Blackwell: What the hell... !!!BOOM!!! !!!BOOM!!! !!!BOOM!!! [Three loud blasts of fire erupt from the ring.] Vince Richardson: What is this? What's going on? Colin Blackwell: Damn..Logan was in the ring.. [The lights turn back on when they do two men are standingin the ring over a fallen Logan Gunz. Colin Blackwell: Who are these guys... Vince Richardson: My guess would be two guys who lost their money at the slots and are taking it out on Logan.
Colin Blackwell: They have Logan up now and...DOUBLE POWERBOMB... Vince Richardson: Logan landed hard on the back of his neck.. [One of the masked men goes outside and grabs a table from under the ring. The other man picks Logan up and set him on the top rope.]
Colin Blackwell: These guys are going to kill Logan..for what reason? [The table is setup in the middle of the ring as both guys climb to the top rope...they superplex Logan through the table.]
