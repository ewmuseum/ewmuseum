News and rumors from the locker room WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW <>Johnny Troisi win the American Heavyweight Championship. In what was a hard fought match between two great stars Troisi out lasted the outspoken Jean Paul
Valley. The featured outside activity from a mystery masked man and CEO
Brittney Christopher. Troisi however didn't get to celebrate very long as he was attacked by Valley who bloodied Troisi and stole the title.
<>Speaking of Jean Paul Valley is war with the Executive Committee stepped up several levels. In the opening segement of Sunday Night RAGE Valley and Sean
Christopher exchanged several heated comments. The altercation ended with Sean being sent to jail and Jean making a statement that shook Brittney Christopher up for most of the night.
<>The AWP Television Title has been held up. In what was indeed the show stopper match of the night Vic Parker and Heath Quirk went to a time limit draw. With no clear winner declared executive committee member Sebastion
Manning signed a 2 out of 3 falls match for the upcoming RAGE. <>"The Underdog" Steven Underwood has been fired from American Wrestling Promotions. Underwood was scheduled to face off against Eric Davidson. Underwood didn't make it for the match and as stipulated was fired by CEO and
President Brittney Christopher.
<>American Wrestling promotions and Public Access Wrestling are close to inkinga deal. Brittney Christopher invited the president and her assistant to attend the debut of Sunday Night Rage. Both parties seem pleased with the inital meeting. Brittney Christopher will make a trip to a PAW show later this week.

AWP American Television Champion--Vacant American Tag Team Champions---Vacant Upcoming cards WVWVWVWVWVW Flashes due: Tuesday by Midnight (EST) Strats due: Thursday by Midnight (EST) ~Send everything to [detail withheld] ~ Sunday Night RAGE Sunday, November th Chicago, Ill...United Center American Heavyweight Title Match "The Answer" Johnny Troisi[champion]-vs-"Evil" Eric Davidson JOHNNY TROISI scored a victory over Jean Paul Valley to become the Champion. Now he faces what could be his hardest fought challenge to date. ERIC DAVIDSON is a man that knows no fear and backes down from no body. Expect this match to be an all out brawl for the title. Will Troisi's first title defense be his last.
American Television Title Match---2 out of 3 Falls "Heavenly" Heath Quirk-vs- "Unbelievable" Vic Parker The show stopper match of the first edition of RAGE ended with no winner. Both men have what it takes to be champion but, only one man can wear the title. It's two out of three falls and there must be a winner.
IN OTHER ACTION... The challenge has been answered. The mystery masked duo that has been appearing in a cloud of smoke and fire as laid down several challenges.
Although Kuwasi Shakur and Epitome of Cool have yet to accept the challenge somebody has. Who will it be?
In what will be a wild debut match. The Physco known as Phoenix will make his debut against "Bombastic" Jonny Fantastic. Both men have what it takes to quickly move up the ladder here in AWP. This could be a sleeper match of the night.    
ALSO... <>Jean Paul Valley and Sean Christopher will be in the arena. Christopher is looking for payback after being sent to jail. Valley on the other hand is hot after losing the American Title. This could be wild!!
<>Brittney Christopher will be in the arena to make several announcments. Ms. Christopher has sent several invites to several huge names. What's this about? and who will accept the invitations? Only Brittney knows and shes to upset at this point to say anything.
