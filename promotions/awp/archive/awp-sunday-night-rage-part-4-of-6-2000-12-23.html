Sunday Night RAGE (Part 4 of 6)

23 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Subj: [AWP] Sunday Night RAGE (part4) Date: 12/17/2000 7:17:05 PM Pacific Standard Time From: RoadDog BW [The camera pans to the entry way as the lights go dark. There is sudden suspense and quiet in the crowd until a few erupt in cheers as "Fade To
Black" by Metallica begins to play over the over the arena speakers. Just as the words "Life It Seems Will Fade Away" are said in the now very loud music, "The Edge" Brian Blades begin making his down the aisle. He wears a black shirt with the words "The Edge" across the middle section in jagged white letters. He also wears his black trench coat and pants. He walks with no expression on his face, never taking his blue eyes of fire away from the ring. He slowly walks up the entrance steps and walks into the ring as the lights turn on. He looks around the arena and calls for a microphone.]
Blades: New Fed. New Cities. New Titles. New Victims of The Edge. [Small Pop] Blades: As i come to AWP, I look around at the talent here. Many names i have not seen before. But many, I have. To most of the ones I have, consider yourself lucky, you know the power I have and the ability I have to destroy my opponent. But to those of you who have never had the misfortune of being my opponent, consider this fair warning. Anyone who steps in my way while I am here, will be put through pain they have never felt, or even dreamt of feeling at the hands of one man.
[Blades pauses for a second while thinking.] Blades: Now down to one of the reasons I am here. Xavier Sterling, you and
I crossed paths in AWMC, and I admit, you got the better of The Edge. For
Now. But, I will have the final move in this game. Watch every dark shadow you think you might see moving out of the corner of your eye, because you never know what might be waiting on you in the dark. You may have been the
"Rookie Sensation" then, but you are no longer a rookie anymore. And it is a lot harder to escape with a victory the second time on a man, than the first.
[Blades look down for a second then begins to speak again.] Blades: Going through the fed, I see many more men I have wrestled. Many of them in a cage match, such as Storm and Violent. But, of these many, only one of the men here i have teamed with, Sinister.
[The Crowd gives a large pop in response to one of their favorite wrestlers.] Blades: But now it does not matter who I have teamed with or gone against. One night, you may think I am your best friend, then the next you find yourself laying on your back with the taste of iron from your own blood in your mouth looking up at The Edge.
[Small Pop] Blades: I am here. And I am here to stay, so get used to it AWP. I see the two men that hold titles here, both would be quality opponents for me.
But, I will get to the titles eventually, as of now, I will accept challenges from anyone in the back, or I'll even go as far as to say (Points
To Audience) ANY OF YOU.
[Large Pop as several of the "huskier" gentlemen in the back rows stand up and act like security is trying to hold them back.]
Blades: For the next person who steps into the ring with me, you will be my example to everyone. When you chose to walk down that aisle, it is like a lamb to the slaughter, and I show no mercy to anyone.
[Brief Pause.] Blades: Wrestlers, watch your every step and action, because if you happen to be the unfortunate victim in my way, you will feel pain, and you WILL be taken to THE EDGE.
[There is a small pop as Blades drops the microphone and begins walking back to the locker room.]
Colin Blackwell: Brian Blades has made his way to the AWP!! Vince Richardson: he is THE EDGE.. [The house lights in the arena dim and the crowd hushes in anticipation for whatever superstar is on his way. Suddenly the loud speakers crackle to life and as the sold out crowd hears that familiar tune the arena erupts into a loud series of boos.]
##GET SOME, GET SOME, GET SOME, GET SOME GO AGAIN!## [The jumbo-tron comes to life as well and we see a video performance of full moon which is covered partially by a black cloud. The name Violent suddenly appears overtop of the moon image to create an impressive logo of sorts. The screen changes and flashes the word "Revelation"… Another video segment with
Violent literally hanging a former wrestler is then shown. At the sight of that disgusting display the fans begin to booeven louder, nearly drowning out "Get Some Go Again" by Rollins Band. The camera continues to stay focused on
the entrance way making sure to get a shot of Violent as heenters.] ##YOU WANNA LOOK IN MY EYES AND KNOW WHAT I CAME HERE FOR## ##YOU SEE MY EYES AND YOU KNOW WHAT I CAME HERE FOR## ["The Revelation" Ryan Violent parts the curtains and is greeted with deafening jeers from the audience. Violent is attired in his usual ensemble of black wrestling pants. In blood red lettering, "Violent" is printed on one leg, "Revelation" on the other. Violent pauses at the top of the entrance ramp and snickers at the booing crowd.]
Colin Blackwell: Ryan Violent in the ring and he's ready to take on.... [Suddenly the lights in the arena fade to black as a series of red and yellow strobe lights begin to flash around the entrance way.]
#I AM THE BULL GOD# #AND I'M FREE# #AND I FEEL" #NOTHING'S GONNA CHANGE# Vince Richardson: Oh god no! [As Kid Rock's "I am the Bull God" kicks in the crowd looks towards the entrance in anticipation.]
Colin Blackwell: "WildChild" Brian Storm...listen to this crowd. They love this guy. [Slowly a lone figure emerges from behind the entrance way. His head is bowed low to the ground. He
pauses for a few minutes as the crowd continues to swell. He steps forward a few feet and raises his
head. As he does so he extends his arms to the side; with that motion the crowd explodes in a chorus
of cheers.] Colin Blackwell: Storm and Violent one-on-one it's going to be wild.. Vince Richardson: Violent is going to kill him... [Brian stands in the middle of the aisle soaking up the ovation. He is wearing a pair of red full length
wrestling tights; written upon the right leg, in very stylized white letters, are the words Wild Child. He is wearing white wrestling boots and white knee pads. His brown hair is spiked and he has a smirk upon his face. He looks at the ring and points at
Ryan Violent and smiles for a brief moment, before he once again walks towards the ring.]
CB: Storm is ready for this match. VR: Not as ready as Violent is..Violent got pinned by a rookie punk last week and he smells blood in the air tonight.
[DING...DING....DING] [As the bell rings the lights go out.] !!!BOOM!!! !!!BOOM!!! !!!BOOM!!! [The ring is filled with three blasts of fire.] CB: Oh hell...Bad Moon Rrising [As the smoke clears the lights turn back on.] VR: No!! No!! No!! JPV...JPV..JPV [When the ring is cleared of smoke it's Jean Paul Valley and Ryan Violent who are pounding on Brian Storm.]
CB: This was a setup!! VR: All part of Mr. Valleys plan...Valley and Violent are beating Brian Storm down..i love it!
[Violent picks up Storm and holds him as Valley lays in some heavy blows. Violent picks up Storm and gives him a cradle piledriver.] CB: Shit! Not like this..Not like this!! [Violent picks up Storm again and give him another piledriver.] CB: Get some help out here.. VR: Why? This is great. Yet another week we're treated to classic Valley
Vision. CB: ASAI!!! ASAI!! ASAI!!! [Bilal Asai having seen enough jumps the guardrail and enters the ring. He takes Valley and Violent down with dropkicks. He turns his attention to Ryan
Violent and nails a spin wheel kick. However Jean Paul Valley jumps him from behind and begins to lay in some blows.]
CB: This isn't right!! This isn't right!! The numbers game is on Valleys side. VR: Always one step ahead..ALWAYS. CB: Ed Salazar!!... [Valley and Violent set up Bilal Asai for the spike piledriver but Ed Salazar enters the ring with his ringside chair and nails Violent in the back with it.
Asai quickly climbs the top rope and hits a neckbreaker.] VR: Damn that flying monkey!! CB: Here comes Brittney.. VR: No, Brittney..dont do anything..your high blood pressure!! CB: Sterling!! Sterling!! [Sterling with his arm bandaged runs to the ring and takes out Eric Davidson and Steven Underwood with a chair. Brittney turns around and is met with a kiss from Xavier.]
VR: Get your lips off of her you perv!! [Xavier finishes his kiss and Brittney falls to the ground kicking and screaming. Valley and Violent exit the ring to check her out.]
CB: Sterling, Storm, Asai in the ring.. VR: Valley and Violent are checking on Brittney..my poor Brittney!! [camera fades and a black screen with a red "AWP" logo appears.] V/O: They said it wouldn't last [The "A" falls off the logo.] V/O: They said nobody would care [The "W" falls off the logo.] V/O: They said it was just a fade [The "P" falls off the logo.] V/O: THEY WERE WRONG ["Rollin" by Limp Bizkit blares as a new brighter and flashier "AWP" logo slams onto the screen.]
V/O: January 7th..JOIN US..Live from the Staples Center in Las Angeles as
American Wrestling Promotions presents...In High Gear
[Under the "AWP" logo a "IN HIGH GEAR" logo appears.] V/O: KICK THE NEW YEAR OFF...IN HIGH GEAR. [camera fades back to the arena. two unknown guys are in the ring] Colin Blackwell: We got two guys in the ring.. Vince Richardson: Waite a minute Colin..that's the guy IQ wanted kicked out of here...that's..THAT'S..PAUL STYLES..
[with everyone having left the ring from the earlier riot. The crowd just continues to boo as Paul walks around the ring with a huge smile on his face, smiling and yelling at the fans around ringside. Carl meanwhile goes over and has the ring announcer pass the microphone.]
Carl Stevenson: I would like to start off by thanking everyone for this warm reception. [crowd boos louder] Carl Stevenson: If it makes any of you feel any better, we feel the exact same way about having to look at each and everyone one of you. The only difference is we’re getting paid to be out here and see you, while you actually paid to come and see this worthless little show.
[Carl starts to slowly walk around the ring] Carl Stevenson: For those of you who don’t realize who we are, and there must be some of you because by the looks of things most of you probably can’ t even afford television.
[crowd boos louder. Carl looks a little disgusted he was interrupted by the crowd, drawing a loud laugh from Paul on the other side of the ring that’s actually picked up on the mic]
Carl Stevenson: My name is Carl Stevenson. The single greatest manager to ever enter the wrestling industry. I’ve lead each and every person I have managed to championship, after championship, after championship, after championship in every single federation we have been employed in for the last five years. A name you might not want to love, but a name you are all going to be forced to respect!
[Carl tosses the microphone to Paul] "Rolemodel" Paul Styles: As for myself, I’m your idol, your hero and your role model. [flashes a big smile for a second] I’m Paul Styles!
[the mention of his name draws a large round of boos from the crowd] "Rolemodel" Paul Styles: I am a former champion the world over and a former manager of champions the world over. Although I don’t really feel like spending enough time on your people to run through it all, so I’ll talk about the AWMC since that is what everyone seems to talk about around here.
[suddenly Joey "IQ" Johnson starts to storm down the aisle with a microphone of his own] Joey: PAUL, GET THE HELL OUT OF MY RING! [Joey reaches the cage and climbs inside] Joey: I was afraid you would show up eventually. I want you and you little friend here, [motions to Carl who simply shrugs] to give me back my microphone and get the hell out of here. No one cares about what you have to say, or what you do. This isn’t the AWMC, this is the AWP and here you are nothing! You are not even employed here. So if you don’t get out of here right now, I am going to have you arrested. I would have done this sooner but I was jumped in the back. [holds the back of his head and winces]
Paul: [starts to laugh] Nothing? Did you actually call me nothing? This might not be the AWMC, but it might as well be. Hell Joey, since you arrived that’s all you ever talk about. As a matter of fact that’s just about all anyone around here talks about. Everyone is still grasping onto the fame of a long dead promotion. A promotion that made them, and a promotion that made you! [points at Joey] I’m here to remind you about one very important fact
Joey.
[Paul starts to walk towards Joey] "Rolemodel" Paul Styles: While the AWMC might have made your career Joey…
[Paul is standing nose to nose with Joey, actually forehead to forehead now as Joey looks like he is about to explode with rage as his face is beat red.
Paul just has an arrogant smile across his face] "Rolemodel" Paul Styles: My career made the AWMC! [Paul backs off and walks around the ring a little again] "Rolemodel" Paul Styles: You are here because of me Joey. Takada is here because of me. Fang is here because of me. Trey is here because of me.
Armageddon is here because of me. Even your new friend is here because of me, Silk. There would have been no AWMC without Paul Styles, and we both know it. I built that federation and carried it on my back for an entire year while worthless pieces of crap like yourself became famous.
Joey: LOOK! The AWMC is the past, and the AWP is the present. In the present you are not welcomed or employed so I want security to come down here RIGHT
NOW AND GET YOU OUT OF MY RING! [Joey looks down the aisle but no one comes out] Joey: WHAT THE HELL IS GOING ON? GET DOWN HERE! [Carl slowly walks up behind Joey and taps him on the shoulder. When he turns around Carl hands him a piece of folded white paper]
Paul: Much to our surprise the other day Brittney gave Carl a call. [Joey looks at the paper in disbelieve and anger] Paul: So technically I am both welcomed in the AWP by Brittney, and as you can see by all those zeros on my contract, employed.
[Paul pauses and smiles Joey turns and starts to yell at Carl. Carl just backs off. As soon as Joey turns around though, Paul blasts him with a superkick and looks to have knocked him out, sending the microphone and contract in Joey’s hands flying.
Layla suddenly starts to play over the sound system. Paul throws down the microphone and climbs up to the top rope in one of the corners to a huge round of boos as Paul just smiles and motions around his waist that he wants the belt. Carl meanwhile stands in the middle of the ring looking down as Joey with a smile on his face]
