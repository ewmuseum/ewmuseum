The Application ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ An Introduction ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ American Wrestling Promotions.. A dream that has lingered in the mind of one wrestling fan for nearly all his life... American Wrestling Promotions is a federation that was dreamed about and formed by Brittney Christopher. "Lady B" as she likes to call herself has been around the wrestling scene for all of her life. Brittney has been known to be the type of girl to get what she wants when she wants..at age 26 she finaly has it.
From the time she spent as a young girl watching her father train the superstars of today to compete in the ring. To the time in the not so distant future she lead her men to championship gold as a manager. The one dream
Brittney always had was to have all the power, all the fame, and all the spotlight..she now has that. [God help us all!!]
~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ What You Can Expect ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ If and hopefully when you join AWP, The Lovely Ms. B has alot to offer you. Above all The AWP and Ms. Christopher will deliver all the fun you can handle [mind out of the cutter people]. AWP will provide you with on time cards [a true rare occurance in e-wrestling these days] . You can expect to be entertained. You put the hard work in doing the roleplays and such you deserve to be rewarded and I'll do what I can do make sure you get what you give.
Fun and Entertainment..who can ask for more? I'm sure you can think of a few things, well whatever it is I'll try to give it to you. Again the moto around here..you get what you give.
~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ The Weekly AWP Workings ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ Which brings us to the question..What should I give? What do I get in return?? 3.1 Roleplay packs Role-plays will be sent out in packs every Friday before the card and every
Wednesday after the card. Which means there will be two roleplay packs per week. This does not mean you have to roleplay two times a week but it gives time to hype of fueds and storys it also allows you to build your character.
3.2. Sunday Night RAGE! This is the AWP Weekly show..following the same concept of most other weekly shows this will be were titles are decided and fueds built. Expect 7 hard hitting matches to spotlight this show [three of which will be dark]
3.3. Wednesday Night Fanatix American Wrestling Promotions will hold house shows on Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday Road Rage will be the video packaged highlight show of those house show events and will also serve as a preview to RAGE.
3.4 Mega-Events Every other month the AWP will present a Mega-Event. An event like no other, all bets are off and all the major players are involved as the fueds come to a climax...like pay-per-views AWP Meg-Events will be something you dont want to miss.
3.5 Weekly Updates Weekly updates are sent directly after every event. Updates include the latest happenings and goings on in the federation, the rankings, the latest card schedule and usually a note from the desk of Ms. Christopher :)
~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ How to become a Star ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ Title Changes in the AWP are not as frequent as you may see them in other federations because we believe that titles, (similar to our mega-events) are something of prestige, honor, and utmost pride.
AWP American Heavyweight Championship Belt- This title is for the absolute best. The hardest worker need only apply for this title. If you hold this title you can proudly say that you are one, if not _THE_, best the wrestling world has to offer. This title is usually defended at least once a month.
AWP US Television Championship Belt- Second..to none...and without any doubt, very prestigious, is the Television Title. The Television champion represents the best the world of AWP televisied wrestling has to offer. This is as close as you can get before actualy becoming a American Champion. This title is defended more often than the American Title so if you can keep it you have proven a point.
AWP Tag-Team Championship Belt- This title is for the best two man tandem in all the land. Only the best team can hold this. The champs must defend it once a month.
~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ SSCW Personalities ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ 5.1 Head Honchos AWP CEO and President - "Lady B" Brittney Christopher See Introduction to AWP AWP Commissioner, Head of Talent Recruiting, Vice President of Wrestling Op -
Shane Christopher, Sean Christopher, Sebastion Manning
These three men together with Brittney Christopher were known as "The
Threat" in the AWMC/AGW. As a single unit The Christophers and Manning were one of the most hated, manipulitve forces in the business. Using any and everything they can to get an advantage The Threat knew what it took to get ahead. They plan to do the same in the AWP this time behind the desk.
5.2 AWP Announcers - -Colin Blackwell - Colin Blackwell is the main announcer for AWP programming. A former news anchor man for local news who learned the ropes of wrestling announcing to the
AWP. Colin is known for calling it down the middle and being pretty straight forward with his thoughts.
-Vince "The Prince" Richardson - Veteran announcer from the All-Star Wrestling of Motor City and Allstar
Global Wrestling promotions. Vince has the talent andskills to be one of the best in the business however he never really takes things serious.
Vince is the kind of guy who like to cheer a good eye gouge and a proud supporter of an old fashioned pull of the tights. It's belived Vince got his
AWP job by using his above average skills of kissing ass.
-Tonya Walker - The TNA of the AWP announce team..Tonya will handle all the backstage announce work as she uses her "talents" to dig under the sheets to get all the scoops.
AWP Ring Announcer - Anderson Michaels Anderson in his early 20's and is in good physical condition. Anderson is a young student of the game as he hopes to one day turn the mic in for a pair of boots. Anderson isnt afraid to take the lumps and bumps that may occur as he sits ringside.
AWP Timekeeper - Jay Howarth Another young student of the game some say he has been weird ever since losing the Ring Announcer job to Anderson Michaels. Anderson and Jay seem to have harbored a training school rivalry which could expolde as they sit
together at the same table at ringside. Always trying to elevate his status and garner attention, Jay gets into the spotlight wherever he can...evening taking the mic from Anderson on occassion.
AWP Head of Security - Athena Erikson A former independent wrestler herself, left the ring to become a mother. Athena is more the able to take care of herslef and has her crack security staff in check. rumors of a love triangle between Athena, Jay Howarth, and
Anderson Michaels may or may not be true.
AWP Referees Thomas "Tommy" Jones Always in tip top shape, and giving a 100%, this is the usual Main Event referee and AWP head offical.
Bryan Infinity Bryan always calls it straight down the middle...and is there to give the fans what they want...even if that means bending the rules a little.
Steve "Old Timer" Massaro Steve is AWP oldest ref, and perhaps the oldest on the AWP staff. Nearing 50 years old Steve has been around the ring long enough to know how to handle
himself. not the fastest, or the most in shape, the most able to see, or even able to hear Steve does his best and doesn't back down from anybody.. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ The Application ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ Well I hope you've enjoyed your ride through what I hope will become a long lasting and hugely successful federation.
