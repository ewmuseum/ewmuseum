Sunday Night RAGE (Part 1 of 3)

26 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera opens to a shot of the jammed packed Madison Square Garden. As the camera pans the arena it shows thousands of fans holding up signs trying to get a place on TV. The camera then gets an overhead shot of the ring which is empty. The camera zooms in on the AWP logo that is printed on the ring mat.]
V/O: Last week a champion was crowned.. [The camera cuts to a shot of Johnny Troisi pinning Jean Paul Valley] V/O: Blood was shead.. [A shot of a bloody Valley attacking Troisi and taking the championship belt.] V/O: Tonight..a new champion will be named as.. [A split screen of "Heavenly" Heath Quirk and "Unbelievable" Vic Parker] V/O: American Wrestling Promotions Presents...SUNDAY NIGHT RAGE.... [The screen disolves as "Timelessness" by Fear Factory begins to play. Various crowd shots are shown.]
#I've felt darkness closing in on me# [A "Who Are The Mystery Team?" sign is shown being held by a fan at ringside ] #Chilling shadows surrounding me# [A "Brittney LAID ME!" sign is old by a young kid.] #I've had the poison leak into my skin# [ A "Unbeliveable Television Champion" sign is held by a lady sitting ringside.] #And it corrodes my heart away# [ A man wearing a green and black AWP logo shirt.] #Bled Away# [ the scenes of the crowd break to actions scenes of Heath Quirk and Vic Parker fighting it out. ]
#Cut Away# [ Sean Christopher being arrested. ] #Dark night of my soul# [Eric Davidson and Steven Underwood fighting in the parking lot] #Dark night of my soul# [ Split screen of Johnny Troisi and Eric Davidson] VOICE: Two Men.... #Dark night of my soul# [Johnny Troisi holding up the Heavyweight title..cuts to Eric Davidson yelling in Brittneys face.]
VOICE: One Champion....One Challenger #Dark night of my soul# [Johnny Troisi pins Jean Paul Valley..Eric Davidson stands tall in the center of the ring.]
VOICE: Tonight The Title is on the line.... #Dark night of my soul# [The spilt screen of Johnny Troisi and Eric Davidson with the American Heavyweight title super-imposed.] VOICE: Tonight..Sunday Night...RAGE [ The strings selection ends as the camera fades out and cuts back to the arena. Again showing various shots of the crowd. The Camera finaly stops at the broadcast table where Vince Rchardson and Colin Blackwell are sitting.]
Colin Blackwell: The second edition of Sunday Night RAGE is on the air..hello everybody Colin Blackwell and Vince Richardson sitting ringside.
Vince Richardson: WE ROCK THE BODY THAT ROCKS THE PARTY!! WHOOO Colin Blackwell: Tonight the American Heavyweight title is on the line.. Vince Richardson: Tonight..WE WILL crown a Television champion.. Colin Blackwell: Tonight..we find out who accepted the challenge of the masked duo.. Vince Richardson: Tonight... [The melow sax sound begins to play over the loud speakers as the arena lights dim and spot lights streak across the arena stopping on the enterence way]
Vince Richardson: Jean Paul Valley is in the house... #Diamond life, lover boy He move in space with minimum waste and maximum joy City lights and business nights When you require streetcar desire for higher heights# [Jean Paul Valley pulls the curtains back and steps through stopping right infront of the enterence way wearing a black gucci suit, an attache case is clutched in his right hand]
#No place for beginners or sensitive hearts When sentiment is left to chance No place to be ending but somewhere to start# [JPVmakes his way to the ring and walks up the steps slowly stopping for a moment to glance back over his shoulder at a fan at ring side yelling something at him. JPV turns back around and looks at the fan and begins to yell at him]
#No need to ask He's a smooth operator Smooth operator, smooth operator Smooth operator# [JPV climbs into the ring moving towards the ropes and reaching out taking the mic from the announcer. He turns back to the center of the ring glacing back to the locker room motioning for the music to be cut]
JPV: If there has even been a travesty of justice like the one commited last week, by all means I would love to find out what it is!?
[JPV paces around the ring with the mic to his mouth] JPV: Troisi...you are nothing more then a paper champion...you didn't beat me...some masked son of a bitch with a baseball bat thugged me and you fell down, and the ref counted 3. Well that's all fine and good, but I said I would leave with the AWP Title, and that's what I did!
[JPV raises the case in the air as the fans begin to boo him throwing trash towards the ring.]
JPV: Now...Sean Christopher I know that was you under the mask, you're the only one around here stupid enough to get on my bad side. Now jealousy is something that I'm not very firmiliar with, because let's face facts there's no one around here to be jealous of, but Sean it's perfectly find to be jealous of me, because I've got everything that you never have. Good looks, talent, pure athletic ability, undaunted charisma, it's only logically you would try to take me out, and I fogot...your Ex stalking me because I'm twice the man you are with 3 inches left over!
[JPV drops the attache case in the center of the ring and points down at it] JPV: Troisi here's your belt...I've made my point..[smirks a bit] I think the belt is more fitting for you now anyhow, but don't get used to being the champion, you can only duck me for so long, and the exceutive board can only put you in the ring against the B & C squad wrestlers in the organization for so long. So use these matches to warm up for when you have to get into the ring against me. In the mean time, I've got a score to settle with Sean Christopher.
So Sean, when you grow a set of balls, I'll be waiting for you in the ring. Until then... [JPV flips the bird straight into the camera as his music begins to play again. JPV ducks out of the ring and ignores the crowd as he walks back to the locker room leaving the attache case in the center of the ring.]
Colin Blackwell: Jean Paul Valley with some pointed words directed toward
Johnny Troisi and Sean Christopher. This hasnt been a good week for Mr.
Valley. Vince Richardson: Not a good week? He was cheated..The title was stolen from him..speaking of which.
[Vince takes of his headset and leaves the announce table.] Colin Blackwell: Vince..Vince..Where you going. [Vince enters the ring and retrives the attache case. He brings it back to the announce table.]
Colin Blackwell: What you doing with that? Vince Richardson: Don't worry I'll give it back to Troisi later. Colin Blackwell: You better.. [Vince opens the case and starts to laugh when he sees whats inside.] Colin Blackwell: Whats so funny Vince.. Vince: HaHaHa..Valley said the belt is more fitting Troisi..look [Vince shows the contents of the case to the camera. The title belt inside is broken. The leather belt is torn and all the gold has been smashed and shattered off the leather. JPV had taken a hammer to it some of the gold just hangs by a thread on the belt and the majority of it is in the attache case, in short the belt is beyond repair....The belt looks like Trash.]
Colin Blackwell: That jerk, Valley. The belt is trash. Vince Richardson: Just like Troisi..Trash deserves Trash. ["Mr.Bombastic" by Shabba Ranks plays and Jonny Fantastic emerges from the back.] Colin Blackwell: It's time for our first match..we'll see the debut of Jonny
Fantastic and Phoniex.
Vince Richardson: Jonny is a man after my own heart..he's out to make himself look good no matter what the cost.
[Jonny continues toward the ring. He is wearing black tights with "Jonny" in white down the side of both legs and "Fantastic" on the buttocks. He has black athletic tape on both wrists, and his short blonde hair is gleaming in the lights. He saunters down to the ring, smiling and shaking his head.]
Anderson Michaels: "Making his way to the ring, weighing in at 229 pounds, and hailing from Phoenix, Arizonia... "Bombastic" Jonny
Fantastic! [Jonny climbs the steps to the ring and steps through the ropes. He walks to the center of the ring and drops to his knees, arms out, head tilted back. He stands up and snatches the mic out of Anderson
Michaels's hand.] Jonny: "New York City" [crowd pops] You are about to have the pleasure of watching the greatest technical wrestler in the history of wrestling in action! [crowd boos] And all you people at home, do not adjust your sets, I do look this good!
[he laughs and tosses the mic back to Michaels] Vince Richardson: A good attitude from the kid! Colin Blackwell: The greatest technical wrestler in the history of wrestling? What is this guy from Canada or something? He'll be able to put that claim to test as he faces Phoniex..
["Mission Impossible II" theme by Limp Bizkit begins to play as the crowd awaits his arrival for his match.]
Colin Blackwell: Not Again.. Vince Richardson: What? Colin Blackwell: To the back... [The camera pans back stage where Phoenix is being beat on by someone new. As the guy looks at the camera it is noticed to be long time wrestling veteran Sean "Dasniper" Naiper.]
Sean Naiper: "ARMAGEDDON WATCH THIS!". [ Pheonix continues to try to fight back however Naiper has Armageddon's old
War Hammer he used to carry to the ring. Naiper stole it the last AWMC card there was. Naiper continues to bash Pheonix on his knee. Naiper pulls the hammer up clear over his head and does a full swing down. The crowd hears a loud crack as Pheonix's ankle is seen broken. Naiper turns to the camera.]
Naiper: Armageddon no matter where you are I will find you and until then I will hurt every friend... every family member you have.
[A black limo pulls up as Naiper leaps in] Colin Blackwell: What the hell was that? Vince Richardson: Sean "The DaSniper" Naiper, a veteran of the sport and star of the now closed AWMC.
Colin Blackwell: what about the match? [Anderson Michaels and the ref talk things over as Sebastion Manning walks out. Sebastion signals for the bell to be rung..] [DING..DING..DING] Colin Blackwell: This is crazy Vince.. Vince Richardson: you expect differant from AWP? Anderson Michaels: The winner of the match as a result of forfit.."Bombastic" Jonny Fantastic... [The ref raises Jonnys hand. Jonny pushes the ref to the side and grabs the mic from Anderson Michaels.]
Jonny Fantastic: I told you!!..I told you New York..another win for the greatest technical wrestler in wrestling history..
[Jonny throws the mic down and leaves the ring.] Colin Blackwell: A another win? Jonny's happy about that win? Vince Richardson: Why wouldnt he be? A great technical win.. Colin Blackwell: please...Tonya you ready? [Camera cuts back to Tonya Sanders whos standing by in the back. Tonya has a short red dress on with a blue low cut shirt. She's standing out a locker room..the name on the locker room? Eric Davidson.]
Tonya Sanders: I'm ready Colin..I'm standing outside the locker room of Eric
Davidson. Davidson will take on Johnny Troisi later tonight for the American
Heavyweight Championship.
[Tonya knocks on the door of the locker room.] Tonya Sanders: Eric...Eric..It's Tonya..can I come in..Can I get a couple of words from you?
[After a few seconds a deep voice is heard from behind the door.] Eric Davidson: Come in... [As Tonya opens the locker room door and enters the lights in the room go out. The room is now only lighted with a few candles in the center of the room.] TS: Eric? Eric? Eric Davidson: Dont be afraid my dear.. TS: Eric where are you? Eric Davidson: I'm everywhere..I'm in the heart and soul of everyman..i'm in the air you breath, the food you eat..I'm in your bed when you sleep..I'm the reflection you see when you look in a mirror.
[Tonya takes a gulp of air..] TS: Eric, Tonight you have a title match..are you ready? Eric Davidson: Troisi has the mouth of a spider..small yet deadly to a fly. Am
I a fly Tonya..do I look like a fly to you?
TS: No..No..not at all. Eric Davidson: I am not a fly..Troisi is alot like another man who used to be in the AWP. A man that liked to call himself a dog. Like a dog this man put his tail between his legs and ran.
TS: Steven Underwood? Eric Davidson: Let's call him what he is...Dog. When Dog boy was faced with true evil he wasn't ready. Nobody is really ready to go one on one with evil.
Troisi isn't ready either. Brittney Christopher, My lovely sweet Ms. Christopher, she stood up to evil. She's done that before. TS: Brittney and you had a little altercation last week.. Eric Davidson: Yes..we've had many in the past. Last week was nothing new to the legacey between the two of us. Brittney knows the evil, she still has some in her.
TS: What? Eric Davidson: Time will tell the story..need not ask to many questions now
Tonya. You may be afraid of the answers. Last week Underwood was defeated by evil, this week Troisi will be defeated by evil. That's all that needs to be said.
[The candles flicker a bit then go out. Leaving Tonya in total darkness. Camera cuts back to Colin Blackwell.] Colin Blackwell: Vince? Vince Richardson: That guy is strange.. Colin Blackwell: Coming up later tonight is the two out of three fall match for the television title. We had a video sent to us by Heath Quirk let's get to that.
Vince Richardson: do we have to? -[The scene opens inside a typical gym. The camera is focused on a sweaty Heath Quirk, who is sitting on a bench doing dumbbell curls. Heath, dressed in a pair of loose black Nike shorts and a tight white singlet goes through with about 15 repetitions before placing the dumbbells down and standing up.
Quirk smiles at the camera, wipes the perspiration from his forehead and then speaks.]- HEATH QUIRK: I’m gonna keep this as short as I can, because training is very important, especially when the biggest match of my short wrestling career is coming up on Sunday Night. Hopefully this extra work will give me a bigger advantage over Vic Parker, who seems to waste all his time signing autographs like they’re goin’ out of fashion.
-[Heath grins.]- HEATH QUIRK: Don’t think I hate signing autographs, I just prefer not to endorse myself excessively. I know I’ve got support, simply for the way I act inside and out of the ring and that works fine for me. Moving right along, on Sunday Night, “Unbelievable” Vic Parker and “Heavenly” Heath Quirk shall do battle again. We fought it out for 10 hard minutes last week, only for it to end in a time limit draw. It won’t happen again, because the time limit has been thrown out. We could go for 30 minutes or even longer before a winner is decided, but you better check the fitness levels Vic, because I know that fatigue won’t be setting in on me for a fair while and all this training is helping every single step of the way.
-[We hear some muttering behind the camera and Heath nods, before resuming his monologue.]- HEATH QUIRK: Now, there were a few interesting things you said on Voices of
Venom Vic, and I’ll tell you what I think of ‘em. I agree with you when you said draws are for losers, because indeed, they are, but straight losses are much, much worse. Vic, as I said last interview, you earned my respect for your ring ability, but your cocky attitude still irritates me. I’m gonna make sure you pay for the little water bottle incident from last week’s show and the best way to do that is to win the title… of course, and I’m going to make sure that I do just that.
-[Quirk takes a deep breath and sits back down on the bench, running his hands through his moist hair.]- HEATH QUIRK: I’m gonna wrap this up now. Vic Parker, Sunday Night, first man to get 2 of 3 falls wins the contest and the Television Title. Are you really as unbelievable as you say you are? More importantly, can you put in a good performance once again? I’m ready for all that you’ve got to throw at me and possibly more, but will you be able to block my entire arsenal? I doubt it. The best of luck to you Vic in your conquest, and may the best man win.
-[Fade out.]- Colin Blackwell: Heath Quirk could be the television champion.. Vince Richardson: God shoot me if that happens.. [Camera cuts to the back where an ambulance is shown. Several EMTS and executive committee members are standing over a man holding his ankle in pain.
The man is Phoniex. As the EMTs load Phoniex into the ambulance a truck speeds up. The truck almost slams into ambulance. A man gets out of the truck and the crowd erupts a little. It's noticed the man is Armageddon.]
Armageddon: "You want to beat on my cousin? You want to take MY property? Go right ahead....but you'll have to pay for taking it."
[Armageddon looks up at the AWP banner.] Armageddon: "This may as well be where I kick your ass again. See you in the ring Naipier."
[Armageddon Looks around.] Armageddon: "Now were do I find the pres of this thing, We need to have a little talk." [Armageddon walks into the arena calling.] Armageddon: "Here pres, pres, pres." [camera cuts back to the announce booth.] Colin Blackwell: Armageddon is here and he's looking for Brittney Christopher. Vince Richardson: It's time for war!! Colin Blackwell: We'll be back... [camera fades out]
