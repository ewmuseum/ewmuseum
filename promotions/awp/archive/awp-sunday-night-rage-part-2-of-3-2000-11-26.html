[Camera fades back in as "The Underdog" Steven Underwood is seen sitting ringside. The camera cuts to the ring where Executive Committee member Sean

I told you there was no way in hell your arm would be raised in victory..and it wasn't. Your dreams crushed with one swift bat shot to the back of the head.
Vince Richardson: Sean is a loser..he's making that fact perfectly clear right now. Sean Christopher: Jean..I hate your guts you sonofa BI<BEEP>. You want a piece of me? You got it..

That will cost you a ass kicking you will never forget. [Sean drops the mic and leaves the ring.] Colin Blackwell: That's a match I can't waite to see. Vince Richardson: NO!! NO!! Sean will kill Valley, he's a mad man. Colin Blackwell: I thought you said he was a loser? Valley may have dug his grave. Vince Richardson: NO! Valley is to smart...he'll come up with something. Colin Blackwell: The television title match is next!! [The arena lights flash on and off a couple of times, as "Push It" by Garbage begins to roar out of the speaker system. The fans rise to their feet in anticipation as the music continues to pump out.]
Anderson Michaels: Coming to the ring now... hailing from Evansville Indiana, standing at 6 feet 8 inches and weighing it at 254 pounds, welcome "HEAVENLY"
HEATH QUIRK! [The crowd cheers as the lights return to normal and "Heavenly" Heath Quirk emerges from the back, dressed in his regular ring attire which consists of long light blue spandex shorts that extend to just above the kneecap with a yellow lighting bolt goes down each leg and the words
"Heavenly" are etched across the crotch section in crazy yellow writing with a black outline. He is also sporting white wristbands, black kneepads, a black elbow pad on his right arm and black boots with white laces. He slowly walks down the ramp, taking in every second of the atmosphere before cautiously climbing up the steel steps and stepping through the ropes and into the ring.]
[The lights go out. A Blue discoish light goes off at the entrance. "You're
Unbelievable" by EMF goes off as a huge pop from the fans is heard through out the arena. The fans look back at the isle as no one emerges from the back....]
CB: Ummm where is Vic. VR: Maybe he got scared. [Vic's voice is heard over the mic.] [Parker] No 10 minute time limit here tonight. You will receive a winner... Well Heath be afraid because you just might get what you wished for. [Vic Parker swings off the top balcony on a cable. He lands right in front of Heath Quirk face to face.]
CB: OH MAN!! WHAT A RIDE!! Vic lands right in the center of the ring and both men are right in each others faces.
VR: Bad blood is already boiling between these two baby faces. [Vic Parker and Heath Quirk are talking trash in each others faces. Quirk with a shove. Parker shoves Quirk back. Both men circle around. They lock up. Quirk with a quick armbar takedown. Parker standing up but Quirk uses his elbow to send Parker back down. Quirk backs up against the ropes and sends Parker across the ropes. Quirk with a standing dropkick that takes
Parker right down to the mat.] VR: Quirk did his home work. He is going to keep this match at a much slower pace this time.
CB: Last match was one of the best matches we have seen yet. It would be a shame if both men decided not to put on yet another show.
VR: Who wants to watch two teeny boppers in the ring. CB: Thousands of these fans. [Quirk pulls Parker back to his feet. Snap Suplex by Quirk. Quirk slowly pulling Parker to his feet, Parker with a quick small package. The ref slides in position.
1..... 2..... Kickout by Quirk.] VR: Almost the first fall here in the first few minutes. Parker playing dead and rolled Heath Quirk up.
[Quirk quickly on the offensive side. Pounding the back of the youngsters head. Quirk off the ropes and knee to the back of the head. Quirk quickly pulling Parker up. Quirk wraps his arms around Vic Parker and Belly to belly suplex.]
CB: Heath looks Unbelievable tonight. VR: Well Parker is a girly man anyways. [Quirk going up top. He leaps Parker rolls out of the way. Quirk and Parker are both down on the mat. The ref is counting both men. 1... 2... 3... 4... there is movement.... 5... 6... Both men are up on their feet. Quirk swings, Parker ducks under and with a neck breaker out of no where. Parker rolls on top of Heath Quirk.
1... 2... NO QUIRK KICKS OUT.] VR: Wow that was close there. CB: Parker hasn't thrown in the towel yet. [Parker pulls Quirk up. Parker with a slingshot suplex. Parker tosses Quirk to the outside. Parker leaps to the top ropes. CORKSCREW MOONSAULT ON TOP
OF HEATH QUIRK.]
CB: WOW!!! PARKER RISKED HIS OWN BODY! VR: Even if Parker pulls this one out he won't stay champion for long. [Parker gets up slowly. He starts to pull Heath Quirk up but Quirk trips Parker down to the cement. Quirk dazed from the fall gets up slowly. He lays his boot to the side of Parkers head and grinds it down. Quirk rolls
Parker into the ring. Quirk leaps on the ropes and flips on top of Parker.
The ref slides into position 1.... Parker with a quick kickout. Quirk wasting no time and pulls Parker up and
Quirk sends him down to the mat hard with a drop down DDT.]
VR: Quirk looks to want this match a little more. CB: Parkers inexperience is shining through tonight. [Quirk sends Parker in the ropes and SWINGING NECK BREAKER AND THE COVER FOR HEATH QUIRK!!!! 1...... 2........ THR, NO PARKER GETS A SHOULDER UP! Quirk points to the top ropes. He hops up top and FLYING HEADBUTT AND HE CONNECTS!!!! The ref back down in counting position.
1..... 2..... 3.... NO HE GOT A SHOULDER UP. The ref is saying not in time. Heath Quirk picks up the 1st pin.
Anderson Michaels: The winner of the first fall...Heath Quirk. Both wrestlers have 60 seconds to rest.
[Parker rolls out and Quirk sets on his knees in the ring.] VR: The flying headbutt yet again for Heath Quirk. He picks up the first pin here tonight.
CB: Parker is at a disadvantage here now. * DING * DING [Parker rolls back in to get a elbow to the back by Heath Quirk. Quirk sends him in the ropes, Parker bounces off with a Flying Body Press however
Quirk kicks out before a one count. Parker with a quick kick to Quirks head. Parker pulls Quirk up and Inverted Atomic Drop. Parker follows with a lariat. Parker slides outside. Pulls Quirk legs and racks him between the ring post.]
VR: A little dirty fighting by Parker. CB: Parker looks a little frustrated out there. [Parker slams his right knee into the ring post. Quirk rolls holding his knee. Parker leaps on the top ropes and 450 SPLASH!!! The cover.....
1..... 2..... 3!!!!!!] CB: A QUICK REBOUND BY VIC PARKER!!!!! LISTEN TO THE FANS!!!! VR: Parker wasted no time to counter his 1 - 0. CB: And it's all tied up.. Anderson Michaels: Vic Parker has tied the match..one fall apiece. Both wrestlers will have 60 seconds to rest then the next pin wins.
[DING...DING...DING] CB: And Parker goes right to work on Quirks leg VR: Parker very determined. [Parker yanks Quirk into the ring and begins to drop some knees into the leg. Parker twists the leg with an indian death lock.] CB: The ref in position and asks Quirk if he wants to give up. VR: Quirk is screaming like a girl..give it up punk!! [Parker drives another elbow into the leg of Quirk and yanks.] CB: Quirk not wanting to give it up, both men want the belt so bad. VR: The crowd begins to yell support at Quirk..Parker however wont let up on the leglock. CB: Parker sets up Quirk!! [Parker slams Quirk and positions his leg. Parker clims the ropes and balances himself before diving off with a legdrop.]
CB: QUIRK MOVED!!! QUIRK MOVED!! [Quirk moved out of the way at the last minute and Parker crashes hard on the mat. Quirk slowly tries to get up. Parker gets up and walks over to Quirk and gets caught with several shots to the stomach. Quirk gets up and starts to throw some shots to Parkers face.]
VR: Quirk is getting his second wind!! CB: Quirk sends Parker into the ropes!! [Parker goes into the ropes and his sent flying with a backbody drop. Quirk favors his leg after the move but is still right on Parker. Quirk hooks Parker and suplexes him, he holds on and sets up for another suplex. Quirk elevates
Parker and crushes him with a jackhammer.]
VR: COVER!! ONE TWO!! THR.... CB: NO!! NO!! Shoulder up..Parker gets the shoulder up with one inch before the three count.
[Quirk sends Parker into the ropes and tries for a spinning heel kick..Parker however side steps.]
CB: Parker moves out of the way of that sidekick.. VR: Parker drops a knee to Quirks leg [Parker drops a couple more knees into Quirks leg then yanks hard as Quirk screams. Parker Wraps up Quirks leg for a figuare four..]
CB: Quirk grabs Parker!! VR: SMALL PACKAGE!!! [Quirk rolls Parker into a tight small package as the ref slides down to count.] ONE!!! TWO!!! THREE!!! CB: He got him!! He got him!! He got him!! [DING...DING...DING] VR: Quirk is the champion? [Both men are on their knees in center ring. They look at each other then the ref not knowing what happend.]
CB: Quirk pinned Parker.. VR: I don't believe it [The ref takes the title and hand it to Heath Quirk. Quirk looks at the belt then at Parker. Quirk bows his head against the title. Parker slowly gets up then helps Quirk to his feet. Quirk and Parker look at each other before extending hands and shaking.]
CB: Yes!! True sportmenship. VR: Blahh..don't make me sick. [Quirk raises the hand of Parker as the crowd cheers for both men. Parker slides out of the ring and heads to the back.]
Anderson Michaels: The winner of the match..and American Wrestling Promotions
Television Champion...

The two masked men stand center ring holding the television title. They're dressed in the black and green and as always face covered with masks.]
Masked Man #1: Champions? People like this.. [points to Quirk] Masked Man #1: You call champions? Masked Man#2: Week after week we come out here..we lay down a challenge..we call your your heros, your legends, your superstars, and your champions. No wonder they don't show up. The heros, and legends, and superstars of today are yellow, they have no stomach..they have no soul. Champions of today are alot like you people out there..lazy, under class, losers.
[crowd boos are wild.] Masked Man #1: Last week what we did to Bluebie and Gunz was a message..a message I guess that went unanswered. Tonight we'll show you what happens when we get ignored.
