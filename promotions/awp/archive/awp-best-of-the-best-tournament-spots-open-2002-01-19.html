[AWP] Best of the Best Tournament..Spots Open

19 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hello All, For those interested [the few that are] a couple of spots are still open for the Best of the Best 2k2 tournament. already a couple of stars from WWO and IGA have signed up and I'm looking for more to get involved..I want have as much outside [not in AWP] involvement as possible. The info is below and apps are being accepted until the 25th.. Thanks, [name withheld] AWP President ================= Best_of_the_Best 2002 F.A.Q 1. What is this? (in character) The Best of the Best in design is meant to bring together wrestling talent from all over the globe. The brain child of current AWP champion and former wrestling star "Ultimate Male" Chris Del Rio. This open inventional sixteen (16) man tournament will show who is the best of the best. 2. What is this thing? (out of character) -An open inventional tournament. Setup for anybody who is looking for something extra to do e-wrestling wise. Weather your not active in a fed and looking to stay fresh on your roleplaying skills or, looking for some new competition outside of your regular "home" fed. 3. O.K, I know what this is but who are you? The name is [name withheld] for those who don't know me (There's a lot of you). I've been involved with e-wrestling since 96 and am the current president of AWP which has been around for a little over a year now. 4. You're the president of a promotion? Is this thing going to be fair? Although all BotB events will take place under the AWP banner this event will be 100% fair. I have selected five [5] outside sources to serve as a judge for all events. This will insure a nonbias atmosphere. 5. Sounds good but aren't AWP stars going to be involved? Yes, AWP stars who ask to be involved will be. However, there will be no more then six [6] regular AWP members allowed to enter. 6. We got rules around this place? Well of course we do, but they aren't that hard to follow. Have fun. Don't bitch and whine if you lose. Bring it up with me, I can explain things. Have fun. Work hard. The usual. I ain't gonna be too strict around here. You wanna have fun, that's why you're here...so have fun. 7. I'm sorta interested but what is expected of me? What are the deadlines for stuff? I prefer to take things one step at time. As of right now the only set deadline is for applications to be sent in. That deadline will be the 25th of January. After that the full schedule and run down will be announced with the first event taking place on Febuary 1st. 8. If I join what will I have to do to win? - There are several ways. Roleplaying of course is the big key. Strats is a second key to success, you don't do strats, your opponent does, a good chance you'll lose. Consistency, angles, heat...there are all sorts of contributing factors. The main thing as stated above is to have fun. If your not having fun and are forcing thing the judges can tell. 9. So, where do I sign up? Send the completed app to [detail withheld] 10. App? Yeah, it's below. ========================== APPLICATION FORM HANDLER'S INFO Your Name: Email: ICQ/AIM: E-Wrestling Experience: WRESTLER INFO Wrestler Name: Date of Birth: Hometown: Height: Weight: Character Description: (This should be fairly detailed not just 'Big, muscular, blonde hair,' All are very important for match writers and what not, so make it detailed) Character Background: (Believe it or not, this is an important factor for being accepted or declined and can make or break a character. Be sure to be descriptive.) Alignment(face, heel, tweener, etc): Personality(this should expand on your alignment): WRESTLING INFORMATION Ring Entrance (remember this is an indy style fed, _no_ fancy lights, titan trons or anything of the like. the biggest thing we have is a smoke machine and a guy at the light switches. This should be a cut a paste version for the writers to use with the wrestlers actions as he comes to the ring, lighting effects, lyrics if you like to use them, etc) Entrance Music: Strengths: Weaknesses (be sure to make sure they are equal to the strengths. No wrestler is perfect) : Wrestling Style: Comparable 'Real' Wrestlers: Finishing Move(s) and Description: Set Up/Trademark Moves Favorite Moves (10-30) MANAGER INFO Manager Name: Manager Info(appearance,etc): Relationship to wrestler/Background/etc: How do they act at ringside?(interfere? help? etc) Sample Roleplay(A MUST! If you don't send one your app is automatically deleted...unless I know you of course.) Send the completed app to [detail withheld] app due by Friday January 25th
