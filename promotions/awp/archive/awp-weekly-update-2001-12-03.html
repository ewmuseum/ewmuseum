News And Rumors WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW [~] AWP Television this week was a great success. With only two more shows left until the pay-per-view what else can happen? [~] Chris Del Rio president of AWP? The turmoil that has been going on inside AWP corporate has finaly been sorted out. With smoke clear it seems the "Ultimate Male" is the ultimate boss. [~] The matches for the pay-per-view have been decided and will be announced this week on AWP television. [~] "American Beauty" Ashlyn Summers became the AWP cruiserweight champion this past week by beating "Top Gun" Ben Tuckwell. Following the loss Tuckwell has asked for his release. AWP would like to thank Tuckwell for his long standing committment to AWP. [~] Several people have signed with AWP all of them have the potential to make it with the big boys. [~] Deathrow, Eric Emerson, or Hiroyoshi Takada..which one of these men will put an end to Sinisters title reign?
