######################################## AWP presents Voices of Venom 3 Wednesday, November 15th, 2000 [Inrings/Vignettes/Interviews to [detail withheld] by Wednesday November 8th at 12am EST] [Roleplays to [detail withheld] by Friday November 10th at 12am] ######################################## IN THIS EDITION: <>"Heavenly" Heath Quirk <>"The Answer" Jhonny Troisi <>"Unbeliveable" Vic Parker <>"The Underdog" Steve Underwood WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW -[The scene opens outside a brown timber, ranch-style household. The front lawn is trimmed short and small green hedges surround the residence. A caption appears on the screen in white writing, reading "EVANSVILLE,
INDIANA - 11/15/2000 @ 11:05 AM" as we hear the sound of a car approaching and a red Toyota Camry Station Wagon pulls into the driveway. The drivers side door opens and AWP wrestler Heath Quirk steps out, wearing a pair of long baggy black shorts that hang well past his knees, a blue AWP promotional t-shirt and a black Nike cap. The camera follows Heath as he makes his way onto the front porch and in the front door.]-
[Once inside, Heath enters the living area where his parents and three of his four brothers are seated around, watching the television. The television is swiftly turned off and Heath smirks, as his father speaks.]-
MATTHEW QUIRK: Heath! We’ve been waiting for you! So son, how’d you go? HEATH QUIRK: Well Dad, the four corners match I was scheduled to fight in was changed into a singles bout at the last moment, so I had to face Johnny
Troisi. Anyway, we fought it out for a good 10 minutes or so and I thought I had it won with a sleeper hold.
-[The biggest and youngest brother Reece starts laughing.]- REECE QUIRK: A sleeper hold? Haha! That’s pathetic Heath. You should have destroyed him with an Electric Chair Drop or somethin’ like that!
Hehe! -[Heath stares at him.]- Sorry man, proceed with your story! -[Reece tries hard not to burst into more laughter as Heath continues.] HEATH QUIRK: Thanks. So what happened was Troisi got out of the sleeper and started a brawl on the outside. I was under the impression the referee was going to call a DQ, so I allowed him to use the guardrail to his advantage.
It turned out to be a bad move, as he went on to smash me with a chair and make the pinfall for the three count.
SARAH QUIRK: Bad luck dear, maybe you’ll win next time! -[One of the twins from Double Trouble mutters something under his breath and Heath’s three brothers begin laughing again.]- HEATH QUIRK: You know what? I could blame the referee like you guys would, but I’ll take the loss on the chin. A true sign of a warrior is being able to admit defeat, and that’s what I’m doing. I’m looking to the future, and that’s Sunday Night RAGE on the 19th in Chicago.
MATTHEW QUIRK: This time we’ll actually be able to watch you in action. The cable guy is coming in tomorrow to hook us up with the station that AWP telecasts on. Who are you facing anyway?
HEATH QUIRK: “Unbelievable” Vic Parker. I’m not fighting for the Heavyweight
Title, but the Television Title is up for grabs in my match and I’ll definitely be giving 100 percent in order to win it. Mind excusing me guys, while I wrap up this interview?
-[Heath’s family stands up and leaves the room, with Axel from Double Trouble making some amusing faces at the camera before departing. Heath takes a deep breath, before speaking.]-
HEATH QUIRK: Vic Parker, a name I’ve never heard before. Over the last couple of days, I’ve been searching through old wrestling magazines, tapes and even on the internet for some details, but with no success. Are you a rookie like me, hoping to make their mark on the wrestling industry? Are you an experienced veteran that hasn’t received any recognition? I’m sure I’ll find out, oh “Unbelievable” one.
-[Heath grins.]- HEATH QUIRK: United Center in Chicago is the place we meet up and it should be a good contest. We’ve both got crowd support, but I think I exceed you in that area. I’ve got the respect of the fans, I’ve won them over already.
They know that I won’t do anything to jeopardize it, while you might. How do
I know this? I watched your first interview, that’s how. You we’re extremely arrogant to say that no-one on the roster is exciting, unbelievable and we were all losers. This was also all before the first event! Sheesh! I’ve got the size, my agent says I’ve got the all-round ability and it’s time for
Heath Quirk to step up. The AWP Television Title will be won by the best man on Sunday night and I’ll be working extremely hard to earn that honor. Good luck Vic, but you’ll need more than that when Heaven is over you.
-[Fade to black.]- WVWVWVWVWVWVWVW (Scene opens. Oh yes. Backstage, after Ready to Rumble. One of your two winners from the night, Johnny Troisi, is sitting on a bench, in his locker room. Even though he's just had a match, he isn't obviously bruised, beaten, or bleeding. However, he is obviously exhausted. Even so, he's still going to do an promo. So listen up crazy fools)
So, the first man falls like Ronald Reagan. "Heavily" He Quirks, it seems that you weren't prepared for the answer. Should've phoned a friend. Oh, you don't have any do you? I also notice, a fact I wish someone had told me before the match, that your theme tune is "Push It" by Garbage. Rather ironic, because your interviews are a bizarre series of noises that keep me awake, my head explodes, my body aches, as I stick my fingers in my ear and scream "Stop the pain". Nah, love you really. It's just a business. You might want to learn the correct manner for saying anything interesting though. And, next up on the agenda, it's going to be Jean-Paul Valley.
(He smiles to himself) Jean. I know you. Oh yes. Back in the UK, we used to get AWMC, and AGW, on a Wednesday night, Channel 4, just after Ally McBeal. And I always used to watch your matches with enthralment and great interest. I used to watch as Sinister beat you up, Retribution beat you up, Rob Cole beat you up,
Takada beta you up, OCD, Shakur, Geddings, Pharroh, each and every single one of them slaughetered you week in, week out. Jesus, 100 Percent could probably have beat you. Sorry, I'll say that as it should have been
intended. Wan Hundra' Percen' could prob'ly have beat yo'. And yet you stayed in the top five rankings. Suspicious? Nah. They needed you high in the rankings for entertainment matches. Let the other wrestlers have an easy night against you. Come on, most of your matches were shorter than
Sinister's interviews. (He laughs at his own feeble joke, the laugh combined with the breathing making a slightly disconcerting noise)
You're rich, and usually that goes hand in hand with success. Not in your case, huh? What's the highlight of your career? A win against a man called
Billy Bluetones, or whatever the hell he's called? I'm not rich, I'll make no bones of the fact. I'll be honest with you, I don't really want to be a wrestler. I want to be a scriptwriter. I have some great ideas for films.
Look, I'll share them with you (takes out sheet of paper). Even seen Austin
Powers? Yeah, it sucks, I know. But... they have that Mini-Me character, a
1/8 scale replica of one of the main characters. That wss popular, right?
So... why don't they remake other classic films with midget versions of the main characters? A midget Don Vito Corleone. Schindler's List, with a midget. Maybe even Coyote Ugly, who can tell. They rejected that idea out of hand. They also rejected 12 Angry Men acted out by just one man. I suggested Seth Green, y'know, for kids. They said "How is he as Juror #8 meant to attempt to fight him as Juror #3?" Ingrates. And, then finally, they rejected my idea for a new prime time TV show. I pitched it as "The
Sopranos meets Buffy The Vampire Slayer". I had high hopes for that, y'know
"F(bleep) you Giles, I don't give a s(bleep) about no goddamn f(bleep)king succubus" "Yeah, your mom's a succubus" "Wadda f(bleep)k you say?"
(He shakes his head) They didn't like that either. So, instead of writing scripts, I'm imprisoned wrestling idiots. Jean Paul. What else can I say about you?
You want the belt around your waist? Crazy little idea for you Jean: try wrestling for it. Can that idea penetrate your mind? You remind me of the kind of kids I went to upper school with. The precious little middle-class kids, with their ponies and piano lessons. I never got that Jean. My dad toiled on those f(bleep)king production lines for 20 years. You know what he got at the end of those twenty years? An overactive thyroid.
(Troisi's stare is becoming, um, weird) But next week isn't about my dad's thyroid, or whatever monkey is phoning you. It's going to be about Freak Intellegentsia, Destiny's Abortion,
Johnny Troisi, taking on whatever your nickname is Valley. Perhaps "The Man
With The Amazing Dancing Monkey" Jean Paul Valley. Or "No Cakes In Hell"
Jean Paul Valley. WAIT!!! I'VE GOT IT!!! How about... "The Bureau De Change Clerk" Jean Paul Valley! Come on, new gimmick time boys. Can you imagine that? You could have, I dunno, the Drachma Drop as a finisher. Or, alternatively, you could carry on being a repetitive charimaless drone, with mediocre ring ability, and a cack intro tune. It's your call...
(FTB, if you please) WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWV [Scene opens with "Unbelievable" Vic Parker signing autographs inside a local mall. The fans have gathered around chanting "VIC" "VIC" "VIC". A very very good looking blonde walks up next in line.]
[Blonde Hottie] Vic I want you to sign my breast. [Parker] You're what!?!?! [The Lady leans over and pulls her top down showing a huge amount of cleavage. Vic's eyes widen as he starts to sign her chest.]
[Parker] I can get used to this!!!! [Blonde Hottie] Vic you are truly Unbelievable! [The Blonde walks off as the line keeps going through. A Local reporter has been waiting for hours for Vic to give him a quick interview however Vic has put his fans first and told the reporter he wouldn't give him an interview until each and every fan got his John Hancock. However it's obvious there is an impatient look on the reporters face as Vic looks up with a smirk.]
[Parker] Alright... Alright don't get your panties in a knot. My time is precious and my fans need my attention so lets make this as quick as my match will be this next Sunday.
[Reporter] Well that's what I wish to talk to you about Vic. You see I am curious. You arrived in the APW only a week ago. You weren't apart of the
1st show. However some how you are one of the hottest wrestlers here in the
APW. You have an American Television Title Match in your APW debut. How can you possibly explain and back that up?
[Parker] Ahhh... Yet another nosey reporter that has no common sense. See it's simple. When you look down the APW's roster, there isn't much comparison. I back up my title, I am truly Unbelievable. I have excelled at everything I have tried. You haven't forgot that I would of been the CFL rookie of the year 2 years ago if it wasn't for that cheap shot that put me out for the year have you? The boys in the back are lucky I wasn't put in the Heavyweight title match. However the fans need a true Television champion so they don't waist their time watching losers on TV.
[Reporter] What do you know about "Heavenly" Heath Quirk? [Parker] What is there to know other then he isn't Unbelievable. I heard he was some chump that lost his luck in Vegas so he gave APW a try. However the difference between him and myself is. I was made to live the life as a champ, I was made to style the life of stardom. I was made to make the APW rich by giving the fans an Unbelievable performance. The fans have flocked here to get my John Hancock. They will flock to the APW arena and watch me win and defend the Television title night after night. Heath Quirk is yet another name on a roster staring up at the Unbelievable one.
[Reporter] Most men in this sport with a cocky attitude realizes after about a year that they are just another name. How do you feel you will be in a year from now?
[Parker] Cocky? Who said I was cocky? I just know a good thing when I see it. Just like all these people here they are great fans, They know a good thing when they see it. I am Unbelievable Vic Parker, I EARNED my nick name for the life I have and WILL live. I won't slow down for anyone. I am sure
I will suffer a loss here and there but not until they get me some REAL competition. Now for a stupid question I am going to give you only one more.
[Reporter] Fine. This Sunday you fight "Heavenly" Heath Quirk. Where does
Vic Parker go after this match?
[Parker] Simple onto my 1st title defense and then the next and the next until Vic Parker gets his American Heavyweight Title shot. You see there are two things to fight for in the APW. Titles and the fans other then that the money, the reporters, the life of stardom that all is just a perk. So let me attend my fans and you can get at the end of the line to get my John
Hancock and watch me win my title Sunday because I AM UNBELIEVABLE!!!
[Vic goes back to signing the autographs of the fans in the long line at the mall. The reporter has an puzzled look then walks to the end of the line to receive his autograph.]
