News And Rumors WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW - The next AWP PPV, International Incident, is shaping up to be something big. the show will be LIVE from Wimbley Stadium, so who knows what matches will be signed or what will happen. We'll see the first Sunday of August. - Could Eric Emerson's ego be getting to big for his own britches? There is some talk that is he one of the main reasons Reaper left, and since then, Emerson hasn't quit the gloating. Could someone knock him off of his pedestal very, very soon? - AWP has many plans of pushing its hard workers to the top. Look throughout the next few weeks to see who those workers are ... and what they are going to receive because of it! - Does Steppenwulf have his eyes set on another "mate?" God only knows. However, we've heard Shiang-Li has been keeping an eye on Acting President Desire. Alisha Bennis, Tommy Graysons umm intern is also still around. -Speaking of Grayson how will the new commish act after having his shinning inaugural moment ruined by Desire and her stable of men. Time will only tell, and time runs out this coming RAGE! FRIDAY NIGHT RAGE LIVE, FROM LOS ANGELES, CA FROM THE LA MEMORIAL COLISEUM ON JUNE 22nd, 2001 Your hosts: Colin Blackwell & Vince Richardson Live, this week, from LOS ANGELES, the AWP is on FIRE! NEW COMMISH, TOMMY "YOUR COMMISSIONER" GRAYSON, upset over being double crossed, has announced that this week on RAGE, the main event will be his choice. His secretary, ALISHA BENNIS, has sent out a memo for "all applicable wrestlers" to be preapared. It is said that just about everyone wants a shot at ERIC EMERSON'S gold... but, where does this all leave the FOUR number one contenders? This week, the TELEVISION TITLE will go up for grabs as young, hot newcomer "SOUNDBYTE" SAGE HOLDEN challenges reigning champion HIROYOSHI TAKADA for the crown! Will Holden be able to overcome the traditional stylings of Takada and walk out with the Television title? After the catastrophe last week, TOMMY GRAYSON has made a match between LANCE STUDD of the PPD, to go one-on-one with JONATHAN CONSPIRACY. Both these men are co-holders to tag team championships, so what exactly does that mean for their partners? "SENSUOUS" SAMANTHA BEVINS claims GRAYSON will get his, and that her boys are out to destroy the duo of CONSPIRACY and SUMMERS. The FIGHT CLUB TITLE will be on the line as BIG GUY issues an open challenge! The first two to step up to the plate were newcomer JACK KINCADE, who traded punches with the monster on the last RAGE, as well as DEATHROW, who watched BIG GUY and KINCADE from a distance. In a triple threat match, where anything goes, it's hard telling who will win! SHAWN JAMSION AND "THE REAL DEAL" BRIAN TAYLOR, two of AWP future stars will collide on RAGE this week. For the winner, they will move up the AWP title ranks, while the loser moves down. I wouldn't want to be in the ring when these two collide. Look for champions STEPPENWULF and STEVE JENSEN to be following this match. Tag Team newcomers SUBURBAN DISHARMONY and SOUTH SYNDICATE SOLIDERS will lock up. With a heated tag division ,like the one AWP has, which team will step up here? ALSO... DESIRE and her stable of superstars will be on hand. What will they have to say? What will TOMMY GRAYSON do about this group? Tune in!!! also... COUNTDOWN [recaps, debuts, and more] - This week, Countdown will be a recap for the past week's TOTAL DARKNESS and RAGE. Features special interviews, highlights, and more of your favorite AWP Superstars. STRATS/PROMOS/FLASHES etc. FOR THESE EVENTS ARE DUE ON WEDNESDAY JUNE 20th, 2001! ----- American Wrestling Promotions presents... TOTAL DARKNESS LIVE, FROM THE AWP STUDIOS IN LAS VEGAS, NEVADA ON WEDNESDAY JUNE 13th, 2001 Your hosts: Tonya Sanders & Rumor Mills With RAGE off this week, we're not going to leave you without excitement! Tonya Sanders and Rumor Mills are going to bring you a great time with TOTAL DARKNESS! They will have special guests, recaps, some awesome matches, and who knows what else! As the main event for Total Darkness, STEVE "THE BREEZE" JENSEN and FURIOUS STYLES member, THE PROFESSIONAL will collide for the AWP CAN-AM TITLE! These two competitors are two up-and-coming athletes in the AWP. Jensen is quickly making his way up the ladder ... but will that climb continue as he puts his title on the line against the aggressive Professional? Also In Action ... SCHEDULED LINE-UP (all darks) In what should be a great match between two up-and-coming superstars, "FUCKIN' AWESOME" RIPTIDE MADDOX will meet "THE BLACK DRAGON" CESAR MONTANO! Both men had great showings at Spring Riot and have been highly praised for their terriffic in-ring skills. This match will certainly be a treat for AWP fans! The first graduate of AWP's HOUSE OF PAIN (AWP's official wrestling training camp), TERRANCE GODLY takes on the debuting "CHERRY POPPIN'" RYAN WALTERS! Walters has found success in various "other" federations, but now, he attempts to acquire success in the AWP. Will he get past Godly? Well, that's probably a stupid question. Millionaire-turned-pro-wrestler JACK KINCADE shows his stuff on Total Darkness as he steps up to the monster known as "STEEL" JAY MILL! Mill is also a graduate of the House of Pain ... but he also has some experience in the independents. Will Kincade be able to score the pinfall over this three-hundred pound plus monster? Or will he get _squashed_? Wink wink, nudge nudge. "SOUNDBYTE" SAGE HOLDEN will square off against rock n' roll star wannabe DAVID LEE HAGAR, yet _another_ graduate of Las Vegas's own House of Pain! Holden had a good showing at Spring Riot, and he will go to great lengths to make a mark in AWP. Will the rabid Van Halen fan hold him back? Or will Holden send Hagar packing to PANAMA? ALSO... RUMOR MILLS will present his ever-famous RUMOR MILL, and TONYA SANDERS will be opening her own interview show! Tune in!!! STRATS/PROMOS/FLASHES etc. FOR THIS EVENT ARE DUE ON MONDAY JUNE 11th, 2001! ----- Pay-Per-View Vengeance: International Incident LIVE FROM: Wimbley Stadium in London, England Sunday Night June 15th MORE INFO COMING SOON!
