KNOXVILLE CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING and AMERICAN WRESTLING PROMOTIONS present SPRING REVOLT 2001 I. Introduction KNOXVILLE CHAMPIONSHIP WRESTLING based out of Knoxville, Tennessee and AMERICAN WRESTLING PROMOTIONS based out of Las Vegas, Nevada join forces to present a one of a kind pay-per-view event. For the first time KCW and AWP two of the leading promotions in the industry join together for a weekend of wrestling like never before. II. Format Coming to you live from the ASTRODOME IN HOUSTON TEXAS...The KCW and AWP present the 1st annual FanSlam 2001. A special weekend long convention. The event to take place on Saturday April 28th and Sunday April 29th will be headlined by a pay-per-view spectacular SPRING REVOLT. KCW and AWP will both present 2 matches on the first night of festivites. Then on the second night of action both feds will present 3 exciting matches. Ongoing for both nights will be the King of Cruisers tournament. Through out the event will present a special 8 man single elimination King of Cruisers tournament. The first two rounds of the tournament will take place on the first night of action. With the tournament concluding on the second night. IV. Programming Dates April 6th-Total Darkness: Road to Riot (dark match event with roleplays) April 13th -Total Darkness: Road to Riot(dark match event with roleplays) April 20th - Total Darkness (dark match event with roleplays) April 28th -SPRING RIOT (first two rounds of tournament) April 29th-SPRING RIOT (Final rounds of tournament) (deadlines for the events will be released in the first faq/update) V.
