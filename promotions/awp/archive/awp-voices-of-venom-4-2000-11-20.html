Voices of Venom 4

20 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
######################################## AWP presents Voices of Venom 4 Sunday November 19th, 2000 [Inrings/Vignettes/Interviews/Strats to [detail withheld] by Thursday @ 12am EST] [Roleplays to [detail withheld] by Friday @ 12am] ######################################## IN THIS EDITION: <>"Unbeliveable" Vic Parker <>Jean Paul Valley WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW [Scene opens outside the local gym in Las Vegas. A cherry red corvette convertible pulls up. Out hops the new and upcoming star in AWP
"Unbelievable" Vic Parker. He has on workout pants, muscle Toronto Argonauts t-shirt and a pair of Oakley sun glasses.] [Parker] Another day another work out. This Sunday I go one on one with Heath Quirk. A fellow newcomer, a fellow fan favorite, a fellow well respected athlete. However there can be only one winner. There can only be one TV champion. So Heath lets cut the buddy buddy crap. You and I will be doing battle in 4 days. You and I will be in front of thousands of screaming fans. Half of them yelling your name and half of them yelling mine. You will be pounding the life out of me and I will be pounding the life out of you. While you will be Heavenly. Heath I will be Unbelievable!
[Parker gives a smirk.] [Parker] You mentioned you have been looking every where to learn about me. Well I guess you didn't look in Sports Extra. A Canadian Sports magazine. You see I was the Quarterback for the Toronto Argonauts. I was going to be the best damn player in the league however two years ago while I was onto becoming the rookie of the year I blew my knee out. During rehab I just happened to catch a local AWMC show and then I knew what I wanted to be. So after working hard EVERY DAMN DAY, to become EXCITING!!! To become
"HEAVENLY" to become UNBELIEVABLE! I put in my blood, pain, swet, tears. I put everything left at that time into my rehab and I have gotten my shot here in the AWP.
[Parker takes his sunglasses off and looks into the camera.] [Parker] Heath, you may be an upcoming star. Your agent may have told you that you have what it takes. Your handful of fans may have went crazy over you. However there is one thing standing in your way. You want to know what that is Heath.....
[Parker smirks.] [Parker] It's 6 foot 1, 220 lbs of pure muscle. Its 220 lbs of Excitement. It's 220 lbs Heart. of TRULY UNBELIEVABLE NESS! So Heath you can let these people fill your small head full of false hopes. Full of false dreams because Sunday when that bell rings. When there is no one standing next to you to hold your hands. When your agent will be out in the stands not being able to stroke your ego. There will only be one man in that ring with you.
I hope your ready Heath because I have been ready for 2 years. I dreamed of this moment. I have prepped myself for this day for 2 years and Heath I don't care who you are or how Heavenly you think you are. This Sunday bring the goods because I _PROMISE_ you ol' Vic here will bring the artillery.
[Vic walks right into the gym.] WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW [Letting the shower water drip from his hair and down his face and chest Jean
Paul Valley steps out of the locker room shower with a towel wrapped around his waist. JPV tilts his head forward and runs a towel through it as he stops in mid stride glancing up to the camera. He smirks a bit folding the towel over his hand as he looks into the camera]
JPV: Of course this had to be Brit's doing, all the boys in the back know she's been wanting to get her hands on me, so sending the camera in here when I get out of the shower would be the only chance she would actually have of seeing what a real man looks like...
[JPV smirks a bit as he walks towards the lockers pulling one open and taking out a duffel bag. JPV drops it next to the bench and sits down leaning over and rummaging through it, he raises his eyes to the camera and smirks again as he takes out a dress shirt and unfolds it sitting up]
JPV: Bluebie, you're not even in my Class, you're not even in my league, hell..you're not even in my world, and I proved that without a shadow of a doubt Sunday, and come this Sunday, I'll prove once again why I am the most gifted athlete in this sport today.
[JPV slides the shirt on and begins to button the collars and button the shirt up taking his time not paying much attention to the camera as he speaks again]
JPV: The Question on everyone's lips is, who will be the AWP's 1st American
Heavyweight Champion. The "Answer" is simple...Jean...Paul...Valley...You see
Johnny Troisi, ya know me from my days on the AWMC and AGW, you've seen my matches...which is good to know, that you had nothing better to do then sit on your butt with a [speaking with a british accent] ginger beer [smirks] in one hand, and a pen in the other and a note pad in your lap taking notes of what it takes to make it to the big time, taking note of what it takes to be considered the best this sport has to offer. Now I don't know you Brits are still bitter about the whole Revolutionary War thing, but taking one of my match and chopping it apart to make the ending completly contradictary to the actually result is just low, and I'm sure there are some federal trade commision mandates against things like this.
[JPV finshes putting the shirt on and reaches back into the bag pulling out a pair of pants placing them in his lap and reaching into a side bag taking out a handful of jewelry and begins slipping his watch, rings and necklaces on as he speaks again]
JPV: Now, JT...I hope you don't mind me calling you that, I had the misfortune of listening to your last interview, now while you were watching my matches, being a smart mark, wishing you were in the ring, instead of leaving one around the tub, you should have also taken notes on my interviews too. As I sat staring blankly at the screne listening to you blather on, about your movie ideas, and what you wanna do with your life, I came to the realization that, the AWP doesn't need you costing them money. This is a low budget organization
[chuckles] we all know that, and you taking up camera and air time talking up
Mini-Me, is coming out of my paycheck! [JPV fixes the rolex around his wrist and polishes it a bit looking at it shine with a smirk]
JPV: It's a shame about your daddy, it almost brought a tear to my eye, then I realized if he was such an IDIOT to work in a factor for 20 years, and wasn't smart enough to better himself with a college education then he deserved everything he got. And so you know, I didn't have a pony! I had a pure breed stallion, and I made damn sure every day that the horse was washed and cleaned, and if they butler didn't do a good job I made him do it again! Which your utter jealousy of me stems from just one thing, you hate me...because you're not me.
JPV: And for your information the "Answer" to the question about my nickname is, I don't have one, I don't need one to get over. I don't need a catchy nickname or gimmick for my T-Shirts, they sell because I'm every bit as good as
I say I am, and Sean, and Brittney, and everyone that's in the exceutive commitee knows that, and admit it or not, they HAVE to RESPECT that. I am the most arrogant Son of a B*tch that has stepped into the ring in years, and I have every right to be. So you want a nickname for me JT, call me the "American
Heavyweight Champion".
JPV: Sean Christopher, knows I am that good, he knows that once that belt comes around my waist, it will be there until hell freezes over, because there is no one, and I repeat NO ONE that can match my level of compition that's in the AWP today, and because Sean knows I am that good, he knows that the moral in the back will be low, so low because everyone always comes up...just too short against Jean Paul Valley. Now....get the hell out of my locker room, before the
AWP becomes XXX...
[JPV smirks a bit as he stands up and the camera fades to black] (c) 2000, AWP Entertainment Inc.
