News and rumors from the locker room WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW <>Who controls the AWP? In what was one of the wildest matches in the short history of American Wrestling Promotions Jean Paul Valley beat Sean
Christopher. As a result JPV is part owner of AWP but can he get along with
Brittney. <>Speaking of Brittney what was going through her mind all this time. We finaly learned who was under the mask of Bad News Rising. However the bigger story may be the fact that Brittney was the masked lady.
<>In High Gear has been announced for January 7th. Matches for this event are still being talked about and several should be made final on next Sundays RAGE.
<>Several big names are set to debut on next Sundays RAGE. However, could there be more on the way?
<>American Wrestling promotions and Public Access Wrestling have signed a working agreement contract. Look for some exciting things between these to promotions. Perhaps this Sunday as AWP makes it's way to Texas.

American Heavyweight Title Match "The Answer" Johnny Troisi[champion]-vs-"Evil" Eric Davidson In a rematch of a wild match they had several weeks ago Johnny Troisi will defend the title against Eric Davidson. The $10,000 Eric won this past Sunday will also be on the line. Will Troisi be able to beat Davidson for a second time? Remember AWP CEO and President Brittney will be in Davidsons corner.
Special Tag Team Challenge Sean Christopher/Mystery Partner -vs- Jean Paul Valley/"The Revelation" Ryan Vilent Jean Paul Valley will team with Ryan Violent who made his way into the AWP last week. They will be taking on Sean Christopher whos out for revenge after losing his share of the company. Who will Christopher choose as his partner? It could be anybody..but Violent and JPV better be expecting STORM clouds in the future.
