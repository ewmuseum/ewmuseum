Ready to Rumble (Part 3 of 3)

14 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera fades in to a wide shot of the crowd. The camera then cuts to a shot of the front row crowd. It gets a shot of a group of fans sitting just to the left of the announce table. It's noticed that two chairs are empty...cut to the announcers.]
CB: It's been a interesting night to say the least.. VR: I can't think of a better way for American Wrestling Promotions to debut? CB: During the last match we told you of an altercation that took place in the back.. VR: Yeah what's up with that? CB: We're trying to get some detail..we do have a camera back there but due to some police action we wont be able to show the video right now..
VR: POLICE ACTION?? CB: We do understand that there was a fight earlier today in the hotel lobby between Steven Underwood and Eric Davidson..
VR: I heard about that..but..POLICE ACTION?? CB: The fight broke out again in the locker room.. VR: yeah..yeah..yeah..but POLICE ACTION? CB: I don't know Vince..what I understand the police got involved after Brittney Christopher was struck..both men have been escorted out of the arena. VR: WHAT?? BRITTNEY WAS STRUCK?? CB: We understand Brittney has a black eye.. VR: My poor Brittney.. CB: But again both Steve Underwood and Eric Davidson have been escorted out of the arena and have been taken out of the next four corners match.
VR: The AWP luck with four corners matches strikes again.. CB: The match will now be one on one..Billy Bluebie taking on Jean Paul Valley...let's get to that match as Billy Bluebie is already in the ring.. Anderson Michaels: Introducing from Hunnington Beach, California weighing in at 215 pounds....BILLY BLUEBIE....
[Billy stands in the center of the ring waiting the opposion.] Anderson Michaels: And now... [The melow sax sound begins to play over the loud speakers as the arena lights dim and spot lights streak across the arena stopping on the enterence way]
#Diamond life, lover boy He move in space with minimum waste and maximum joy City lights and business nights When you require streetcar desire for higher heights# [Jean Paul Valley pulls the curtains back and steps through stopping right infront of the enterence way wearing a red sequence robe. Looking out over the crowd with a smirk as he is greeted with a course of boos]
#No place for beginners or sensitive hearts When sentiment is left to chance No place to be ending but somewhere to start# [JPV makes his way to the ring and places his knee up on the apron grabbing onto the 2nd rope and pulling himself up as he glances back out over the crowd with a slight smirk]
#No need to ask He's a smooth operator Smooth operator, smooth operator Smooth operator# [JPV climbs into the ring moving to the middle and raising his hands in the air ready for an ovation but is greated with boos. Valley drops his arms down by his side looking out over the crowd yelling out to them "What's wrong with you!?"]
#Coast to coast, LA to Chicago, western male Across the north and south, to Key Largo, love for sale# [JPV takes his robe off and slides it out to the ringside attendent he then slides his thumb across his throat as the music is cut out]
CB: Well Jean not waiting for a traditional entrance..he has a look of determination in his eye.
VR: Well he got screwed over by Sean Christopher earlier tonight..wouldn't you be pissed? CB: He wasn't screwed..he was told to do what he gets paid for...Work..something he's not used to.
VR: Why should he have to work..he's the most well rounded star we have in the locker room..Sean should have been handed the belt.
CB: Take nothing away from Jean, he's a great worker but he's also a big cry baby..he's been feed with the golden spoon all his life.
VR: Yeah..and? CB: Well see how long Jean lasts when he's made to work.. [DING! DING! DING!] CB: and we wont have to waite long.. [...And Bluebie and Valley meet in the middle of the ring, a big grin plastered across Bluebies face, which is met by an ego filled smirk from
Valley] CB: Despite all the bad luck we've had in actualy getting four corner matches tonight..this is a great way to end the evening..a match between two great stars.
VR: Yeah, whatever. Jean Paul Valley is a one-man match here, and Bluebie's filling in as a punching bag. Billy is out of his league!
CB: Valley is indeed very heavily favored to win the whole thing, and Bluebie may be the underdog but anything can happen
[Still face-to-face, Valley smiles and laughs in Bluebies face before spitting on him. Valley takes a few steps back. He then takes a wild swing at Bluebie who ducks, spinning Valley around. Bluebie then pushes Valley into the corner.
Valley begs off as Blubie smiles] CB: Valley's cocky attitude may be his down fall... [Valley comes charging out of the corner, Bluebie send him face first to the mat with a drop toe hold. Valley gets up and is slamed back down. Valley gets up again and charges Blubie and his sent flying down wih an arm drag.]
CB: Valley is being taken to school.. VR: He is not, he's luring Bluebie in! CB: I'm sure.. [Bluebie locks in an armbar as Valley screams in pain..Valley makes it to his feet and nails a couple of short elbows to the gut...he bounces himself off the rope and is got once agin with an arm drag.]
CB: Billy Bluebie one step ahead of Valley so far in this match. [With control of Valleys arm Blubie paint brushes the back of Valleys head, invoking a resounding cheer from the crowd. Valley slides his body to the ropes and gets the ref to brake the hold. Valley then slides to the outside of the ring and grabs a chair, throwing it into the ring.]
CB: Valley is losing his cool VR: I told you Valley is suckering Bluebie in... it's the oldest trick in the book.. [Billy Bluebie take the chair and sits in it..inviting Valley to get in the ring. The crowd laughs amused by Bluebies antics. Valley get upset and jumps on the ring apron yelling at the ref. As Valley continues to argue with the ref Bluebie gets off the chair and slingshots Valley into the ring.]
CB: Valley crashes back into the ring and is begging off. VR: I can't believe what I'm seeing..FIGHT VALLEY FIGHT.. CB: I thought you said he was using the sucker tactic... VR: Shut up... [Valley on his knees begging off..Billy grbas him by the hair to get him to his feet but his caught with a low blow by Valley.]
VR: See..I told you... CB: Low blow by Valley sends Bluebie to his knees. [Valley begins to work over Bluebie with rapid fire left and rights followed by several kicks to the pick of the head. The crowd fill the ring with boos as
Valley just laughs and give a gentlemenly bow.]
VR: Jean Paul Valley..ever the gentlemen CB: Yeah right... [Valley picks Bluebie up and takes him over with a snap suplex, he holds onto the hold and rolls over quickly moving back to a vertical base and taking him over with a 2nd snap suplex. He rolls over a 3rd time and raises Bluebie in the air holding him in place for a moment and drops him midsection 1st over the top rope hanging him out to dry. Valley grabs Bluebie by the head and tosses him off the ropes backwards, the back of his head the 1st to connect to the floor as the crowd continues to boo Valley.]
CB: You could hear the thud as Bluebies head hit the floor.. VR: Don't get Valley pissed off..this is the result. [Valley now goes to the outside of the ring and sends Bluebie into the ringpost. Valley grabs Billy by the hair and begins to yell in his face.]
VR: Tell me Colin..Whos your daddy? Valley is Bluebies daddy.. [Valley send Bluebie face first into the ring steps. Valley continues to ram
Bluebie's face into the steps.]
CB: BLUEBIE'S BUSTED...BLUEBIE'S BUSTED OPEN.. VR: Valley will show no mercey..he's already upset over what happend earlier tonight... [Valley now grabs a chair and smashes it over the back of Bluebie. The ref finaly gets out of the ring and snatches the chair from Valley. Valley cocks back to hit the ref but the ref gets in Valley's face. Valley rolls Bluebie back into the ring.]
CB: A COVER... ONE TWO.... THR.... CB: No..Bluebie kicks out... [Jean picks up Bluebie and lands a series of suplexs; Belly to Belly suplex, a double underhook suplex, a Northen Lights Suplex, and a german release suplex.]
CB: Great string of moves by Valley and he covers... ONE!!!!! TWO!!!!! TH.. NO!!! [Bluebie kicks out at 2 and a half, both men struggle to their feet, Bluebie droggie from the suplexs. Valley runs in for a full body press and
Bluebie somehow takes the legs out from under him...and begins to swing him around]
CB: A GIANT SWING!!! VR: AROUND...AND AROUND THEY GO CB: Somebody stop this! I'm getting dizzy VR: AND AROUND...AND AROUND...AND AROUND [Bluebie finaly drops Valley, who lands with a thud in the middle of the ring. Bluebie dizzy himself falls on him the ref is right there to make the count.] ONE!!!!!! TWO!!!!! THREEEEEEEEE! NOOOOO!!!! VR: YES! YES! YES! Got his shoulder up!!! CB: Where did Bluebie get that move from!! I haven't seen that move since my days in Japan!
[Bluebie gets to his feet and falls back against the turnbuckle, gathering himself. Valley, a little more groggy, makes it to his feet only to get met by Bluebie and a flying forearm.]
CB: Bluebie now has his second wind... VR: That was a luck shot.. [Valley back to his feet his knocked down with a dropkick.] VR: That one as well.. [Valley up once more..down once more after a spin wheel kick.] CB: Bluebie..has control.. VR: NO..NO...NO [Blubie picks Valley up and whips him into the turnbuckle...Valley hits the corner hard and slumps down a bit, Bluebie runs full steam at Valley who spins out of the way at the last second and sinks in a sleeperhold. Valley takes a few steps back before dropping Bluebie straight down jarring his neck with "2
Days in the Valley" and covers him for the pin putting his feet on the bottom rope and hooking Bluebie's tights]
ONE!!!!!!! TWO!!!!!!!! THREEEEEEEEE!!!!!!!!! [DING! DING! DING!] Anderson Michaels: The winner of this match...JEAN PAUL VALLEY.... VR: Valley wins!! Valley wins!! Oh happy day..VALLEY WINS!!! CB: Damn it!! Jean Paul Valley with an great win..and that sets up our main event for next week...Jean Paul Valley will meet "The Answer" Johnny Troisi for the American Heavyweight Title..
[JPV rolls over and keeps rolling out of the ring holding the back of his neck and staggering down the aisle with one hand raised in the air. He stops halfway down the asile looking back to the ring and motioning for a belt around his waist and then turns to the fans doing the same. He turns back around holding the back of his head and troting back towards the locker room as he looks up seeing Sean Christopher standing there looking at him. JPV smirks a bit and motions for him to polish the belt up for him before moving back to the locker room]
VR: Christopher and Valley..CHRISTOPHER AND VALLEY..EYE-TO-EYE!!! CB: We're out of time...See you next week..THE DEBUT OF RAGE
