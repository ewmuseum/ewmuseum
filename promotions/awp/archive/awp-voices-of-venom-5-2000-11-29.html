Voices of Venom 5

29 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
A M E R I C A N W R E S T L I N G P R O M O T I O N S #################################################################### AWP presents Voices of Venom 5 Wednesday November 29th, 2000 [Inrings/Vignettes/Interviews/Strats to [detail withheld] by Thursday @ Midnight EST] [Roleplays to [detail withheld] by Friday @ Midnight EST] #################################################################### IN THIS EDITION: <>"The Answer" Johnny Troisi<American Heavyweight Champion> <>"Heavenly" Heath Quirk<American Television Champion> <>"Unbelivable" Vic Parker <>Sean "Da Sniper" Naiper<debut> <>Dreadnaut<debut> WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW (Scene opens on a shot of a man in his mid-40s, an obvious case of a bleeding ponytail if there ever was one. Anyway, he's sitting at a mildly cluttered desk, with a little plaque at the front of it saying "Kel O'Hare-
AWP Talent Liaison officer". He's dressed in an obviously expensive, ill-fitting suit, and his hair is what would be described by everyone but
Americans as being "Cesare Maldin-esque". Anyway, the intercom at his desk's side rings)
INTERCOM: Mr. O'Hare, your 11:15 is here O'HARE: Great. Bring him in. (As we wait for the door to open... ah, there it is. Yup, coming through the door is none other than your heavyweight champion, "The Answer" Johnny
Troisi. He has come dressed in "Bud Don't Need No Damn Posse" longsleeve tee-shirt, a decapitated Barbie head neckchain, and some black jeans. He approaches O'Hare rather uneasily, before being offered a seat)
O'HARE: Ah, Mr Troisi, great to see you. Look, I'll cut straight to the point, can you guess why I've called you in here?
TROISI: Not this again... look a) you really have no right to check the history folder, and b) Zoofuck was the name of a Greek philosopher, and I was trying to rehearse my next interview. A lot of the guys are using the greats as a shortcut to interview time now. Vic Parker's really into
Heidigger... O'HARE: No, no, it's not about that. Look, we've been doing a lot of research into AWP's fan demographics. And, it seems that each wrestler appeals to a certain social area. For example, Jean-Paul Valley's interview style makes him very appealing to the professionals, the so-called ABC1s, usually in their early 20s, with a high disposable income. Armageddon, with his very forceful interview technique, tends to appeal to early adolescents, who can convince their parents to shop on-line.
TROISI: And who does Heath Quirk's interview style appeal to? O'HARE: The deaf. But that's not important now. Look, do you know who comprises your entire fanbase?
TROISI: Shoot. O'HARE: Intravenous drug users, people in trenchcoats, self-proclaimed prophets, people who list their main hobby as "urban warfare", and former members of the Stone Temple Pilots.
TROISI: So I'm big in Cleveland then? O'HARE: Yeah, but we need more than that. A lot more. Look, have you noticed this week's schedule? What match have you got?
TROISI: I've got the Quirker again, haven't I? I had this great idea for a vignette to promote the match right, you'll like this one, Chicken Run Lola Run.
O'HARE: No, stop it right there. TROISI: The Parent Trapocalypse Now? O'HARE: I said no. TROISI: Beyond The Matrix? O'HARE: Stop it right now. Seriously. Look, there is a reason why none of your film ideas get accepted.
TROISI: Why's that then? O'HARE: It's because they are, to use a piece of wrestling terminology, are rather "Eric Davidson-esque".
TROISI: That's not true. When I met Jon Favreau, he really loved my impression of Sarah Michelle Gellar.
O'HARE: Johnny- the world of film is a complicated, hard, and strenuous one. It isn't one that can be mastered by some two-bit wrestler walking up to
Jon Favreau with a pane of glass, putting the glass up to your face so both of your nostrils turn upwards, and then going "Any hole you want". Anyway, can we get back to the point here? OK, you're fighting Heath. Now, have you noticed what the main event is?
TROISI: Jean-Paul Vallium up against the ring announcer or someone isn't it? O'HARE: Close enough. Now, with you as our champion, you'd have thought that you'd be headlining correct? Well, there's a reason why you're not.
And it's because of your fanbase. You're not popular enough with the right people. Anyway, me and the guys had a few ideas on how to increase your appeal. Firstly, the theme music.
TROISI: What's wrong with it? O'HARE: People tend not to associate Placebo with pro-wrestling, for some unknown reason. People tend to associate Placebo with glitter, bisexuality, and tiaras. None of which we really associate with pro-wrestling.
TROISI: Apart from Johnny Bombastic at weekends. O'HARE: Well, that goes without saying. But, even so, it doesn't really have that AWP vibe, does it? Now, us guys in the back contacted a few musicians, and we tried to see what we could come up with. There wasn't really that good a response to be honest. But... Papa Roach said that they could do it. This is what they came up with...
(O'Hare swivels around on his chair, and presses a button on a stereo that just happens to be at the back of the room. The following screech plays loudly)
Wah wah wah wah wah My father used to beat my mother And then she died And then he carried on beating her corpse Please buy all of my records JOHNNY TROISI- his pain is nowhere near as painful as my pain JOHNNY TROISI- because I'm so much more emotional than him Yeah, yeah, yeah, yeah Did you know that there are no black people in America That's why you'll find none in our videos And if we take a standpoint against violence and abuse Why the f(bleep)k are we touring with Eminem TROISI: Please, stop the torture (O'Hare turns the stereo off) O'HARE: Hmm, maybe on reflection, injecting them with a truth serum before the second verse may have been a bit of a mistake.
TROISI: What other ideas did you have? O'HARE: Um, just one more. TROISI: What was that? O'HARE: Well, we came up with the idea of a manager. We thought that an 18 year old in a cheerleader costume could get over big (presses intercom)
Julie, bring the cheerleader in. TROISI: Why should I have to lower myself to being paraded around with some lump of T&A? O'HARE: You won't. Trust me. We had trouble finding someone suitable... (The door opens. Through it walks... a lanky, Goth looking 18 year old adolescent male, dressed in an amazingly ill-fitting cheerleader outfit. He has about sixty plastic bracelets on one arm, and none on the other. He looks at Troisi)
CHEERLEADER: I don't like this any more than you do... (I think we might as well fade there) WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW -[The scene opens in the bedroom of Heath Quirk. The room has the usual things you would expect… a desk, a chest of drawers and a television. The walls are painted sky blue and posters of wrestling superstars from the past such as Andre the Giant line the walls. The camera then pans to the bed, where Heath is seated, holding the AWP American Television Title. Heath, dressed in black shorts and a white t-shirt has a huge smile on his face as he begins talking.]-
HEATH QUIRK: Hey. –[Heath gives the camera the thumbs up.]- Sunday Night RAGE, I faced Vic Parker in yet another gruelling contest. I can’t remember that much of it, but I did watch a replay and after picking up the first fall with the ever reliable diving headbutt, I got the final fall of the match with the small package roll-up and the three count. A small little tactic that we learnt in 5 minutes at wrestling academy paid off big dividends and now I am the AWP American Television Champion with only one professional win under my belt. Amazing, huh?
-[Quirk admires the belt he holds in his hands for a moment, before turning back his attention to the camera.]- HEATH QUIRK: Not only did I win the strap at RAGE, I was also involved in a little run-in afterwards. The masked men, who we now know are Bad Moon
Rising came in, knocked me out when the lights were down and annihilated me. It’s some bad footage I never want to see again and thanks to Vic for his assistance, even though it was a tad late. I’m feelin’ the pain in my neck right now, but it could’ve been much worse and I thank god that it’s not.
Bad Moon Rising… make sure your eyes are on everyone, because I know I’m out for revenge. Unprovoked attacks anger me, and your time will soon come to feel what Heath Quirk can dish out, and I tell ya, it won’t taste good.
-[Heath pauses.]- HEATH QUIRK: Anyway, coming up on December 3rd is another instalment of Sunday Night RAGE. It’s a big night, as I go up against Johnny Troisi for a second time. At Ready to Rumble, he defeated me after a chair shot, which led to him winning the American Heavyweight Championship over Jean Paul
Valley. It was tough to take, especially being my debut, but now that I’ve got some experience under my belt and the Television strap, things this time will be different.
For one, the stakes are higher. 10,000 dollars are for the winner to take home and this will make a nice little Christmas bonus to my salary.
Secondly, I won’t be allowing you to cheat in hope that the ref. disqualifies you. I’ve made that mistake before, learned from it and won’t be doing it again. Finally, my motivation is stronger than yours. I know that if I come out with a win, the executive committee will see my potential as a future champion and a title shot won’t be far away.
Johnny, I know you’ve got plenty of ability and you’ve shown that and there’ s no way I’m underestimating you, even though it might sound that way. I’m sure you’ve got plenty of little tricks up your sleeve to use but genuine talent should be able to counter it and if doesn’t, then congratulations to you.
-[Another brief pause.]- HEATH QUIRK: The Answer, I don’t care if I’m “boring” you. I couldn’t care more if you say you almost went into a coma, because I’ve heard it all before and it doesn’t worry me one bit. You would’ve lost to Eric Davidson last week if it wasn’t for an attack and that’s pretty poor if I say so myself. Cheap tricks won’t work this time, for there’s a certain individual watching my back who will be revealed soon. Troisi, bring it on my friend, because I’m ready for it more than ever and I’m looking forward to taking
10,000 dollars home… to heaven.
-[Fade out.]- WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW ["Unbelievable" Vic Parker is seen in the local gym. He has a small bandage over his right eye with Oakley sunglasses on. He has on a muscle shirt with workout pants.]
[Parker] Well I never said wrestling was easy, however I did do a cardinal sin. I got to cocky and overlooked a very talented wrestler. I guess that is the youth in my blood still. I will take my loss like a man and learn from my mistakes. Don't get me wrong I faced a very talented wrestler.
Heath Quirk gave me his all and last night it was enough to beat me. Heath
I respect you and I hope I earned yours. However I am going to have to ask you for a rematch down the road. However on to new business that has arose.
Last night the so called Mystery men and their mystery chick showed up again. They decided to take advantage of a man that earned my respect the man who pinned me earlier Heath Quirk.
[Parker smiles.] [Parker] So did I do the right or wrong thing? I think the fans out there would agree that, HELL YEAH I DID THE RIGHT THING.
[Parker rips off his sunglasses.] [Parker] Listen here Mystery men or Bad Moon Rising or what ever you guys call yourselves you tried to take advantage of a man who gave it all he had just earlier in that night. For what? I sure don't know but I hope it was worth it because now you have forced me to get involved. You see guys like
Quirk and I only come around once in a life time. Big thugs hiding behind masks are all over the place. So if you want to step in the ring with The most EXCITING wrestler today, THE MOST AWESOME wrestler out there today, THE
MAN WHO IS TRULY UNBELIEVABLE!!! Well screw it, You have done got my attention..... I will see you in that ring, and I would be honored to tag with Heath Quirk to pull those masks off and show the world how ugly you all are.
[Parker takes a deep breath.] [Parker] That will have to wait tell another night, DON'T think I will forget about you two... I just have to concentrate on "Evil" Eric Davidson.
You see Davidson isn't no slouch HOWEVER he is a bore. The fans can't handle watching his 5 minute interview never the less his 10 - 20 minute match. So I need to spice this up a little, I need to get the fans
EXCITED!!! I need to get the fans ROWDY!!! I need to show them UNBELIEVEABLENESS!!! So Davidson, I'm pissed I just lost a chance at becoming the TV CHAMP and guess what you first in line to step in the ring with me.
[Parker gives a huge grin.] [Parker] Now I don't know if you watched Quirk and I last 2 matches but I think some people would argue that they are two of the best matches they've seen in along time in wrestling. SO bring you're boring ass to Rage this
Sunday. Try to put on a show.
[Parker pauses.] [Parker] Wait I think I can carry your boring ass. Just show up because I will show you why I am TRULY UNBELIEVABLE!!!!
[Fade to Black.] WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW (Sean "Dasniper" Naiper is sitting on a park bench reading a spiderman comic book. He finishes the last page and shuts the book. He then looks up with a evil grin.)
Naiper: You know what I love about reading comic books? The goodguy always wins. That's everybody's dream these days. Watch the good guy triumph and win!
(Naiper tears the spiderman comic book in half. He throws it on the ground and spits on it.)
Naiper: WAKE UP PEOPLE! THIS ISN'T A COMIC BOOK. The good guys NEVER WIN. "Bombastic" Jonny Fantastic you are standing in my way. You are the first victim. You see I didn't join AWP for gold. I sure in the hell didn't join it for the money. I joined it to end the career of one man. My worst enemy.
(Naiper looks down at the tattoo on his right arm. It says Armageddon) Naiper: The sign of the beast. Armageddon your day will come when you meet me in the ring yet again. Your time will come and you will wish it didn't.
"Bombastic" Jonny Fantastic you'r time is here however. There is no way around it. Mr and Mrs. Christopher have signed your doom.
(Naiper gets up from the park bench. He walks over to his harley davidson.) Naiper: "Bombastic" Jonny Fantastic tonight I ride. This sunday I will pound the life out of you. Until then sweet dreams.
(Naiper looks down at his right arm and the tattoo of Armageddon and gives an evil smile.) WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWV (Dreadnaut is seen with a heavyback pack. He looks like he has been running. He is dripping with swet. Dreadnaut is standing infront a very steep hill.) Dreadnaut: AWP for weeks now you have had a small nice little fed. For weeks you have all been standing in a little circle eating cookies and holding each others hands. However you have awoke a monster. A monster with three heads...
(Dreadnaut flops his backpack off besides him.) Dreadnaut: On one of the heads, you have a 6 foot 7 master of disastor.... Fang, On the other head you have the master mind Sean "Dasniper" Naiper, on the third head you have a beast that only cares about chaos DREADNAUT. We are the
Hydra and we have come for the soul of ARMAGEDDON!!!!
(Dreadnaut gives an evil smile.) Dreadnaut: The prez in AWP must love us, he gave us an debut present. Dreadnaut - Fang - Armageddon in a 3 way dance? This is more like a 2 on 1. Armageddon how can you overcome the odds of this? I beat you on my own in your own home a cage... So Armageddon I ask you this.... What does it take to beat a Hydra? Wait I don't ask you, I want you to show me.
(Dreadnaut laughs loud.) Dreadnaut: The destruction has begun..... Armageddon it dosen't end until you
END...... And soon your life will become your worse fear your life will become an armageddon!
(Fade to black) WVWVWVWVWVWVW (c) 2000, AWP Entertainment Inc.
