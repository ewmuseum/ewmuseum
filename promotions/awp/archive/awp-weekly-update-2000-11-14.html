News and rumors from the locker room WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW <>Johnny Troisi and Jean Paul Valley have both earned a chance to become the first ever AWP American Heavyweight Champion. Troisi outlasted Heath Quirk well JPV defeated Billy Bluebie in single matches. Only one of these men can win the title...who will it be?
<>Masked Men lay down open challenge...The world watched as two masked men appeared out of a blast of fire to lay out "The Warrior" Logan Gunz. Who are these men? More important, who will they challenge next? As after their attacke of Gunz they challenged two huge names in the wrestling industry..Epitome of Cool and Kuwasi Shakur.
<>Simon Dreager and AWP officals come to mutual decision. After no showing his scheduled four corners match at Ready to Rumble Simon Dreager contacted AWP headquarters asking for his release. Executive committee members granted the star his wishes.
<>"The Underdog" Steven Underwood and "Evil" Eric Davidson set to square off. After a wild brawl in a hotel lobby, followed by another fight in the locker room both Steven and Eric were escorted out of the MGM Grand. The CEO and
President Brittney Christopher was involved in this altercation. more information will be available this coming Sunday on RAGE...

1) Jean Paul Valley 2) "The Answer" Johnny Troisi 3) "Heavenly" Heath Quirk 4)"Unbelievable" Vic Parker 5) "The Warrior" Logan Gunz Not Ranked... Billy Bluebie.."Evil" Eric Davidson..."The Underdog" Steven Underwood Champions and top contenders American Heavyweight Champion---Vacant AWP American Television Champion--Vacant American Tag Team Champions---Vacant Upcoming cards WVWVWVWVWVW Flashes due: Tuesday by Midnight (EST) Strats due: Thursday by Midnight (EST) ~Send everything to [detail withheld] ~ Sunday Night RAGE Sunday, November 19th Chicago, Ill...United Center American Heavyweight Title Match "The Answer" Johnny Troisi-vs-Jean Paul Valley After both men scored impressive victorys at Ready to Rumble their eyes are now focused on the American Heavyweight Championship. Will Johnny Troisi fills his hardcore lifestyle will be enough to overcome JPV and his ego. Jean Paul
Valley on the other hand fills the title should just be handed to him but, there is someone who thinks differant. Who will win the rights to call themeself the first ever American Heavyweight Champion?
American Television Title Match "Heavenly" Heath Quirk-vs- "Unbelievable" Vic Parker "Heavenly" Heath Quirk won the hearts of Las Vegas at Ready to Rumble. Now he wants to win the hearts of AWP fans and the TV title at the same time.
Heath will go one on one with fellow fan favorite "Unbelievable" Vic Parker. Parker has yet to set foot in a AWP ring but belives he has the talent and skills to make it to the top. This could be a match that will still the show.
IN OTHER ACTION... "The Underdog" Steven Underwood battles "Evil" Eric Davidson..these two had two hard hitting altercations this past week in Las Vegas. As a result CEO
Brittney Christopher has ordered them to settle things in the ring or BE FIRED... "The Warrior" Logan Gunz battles Billy Bluebie...Logan Gunz never had a chance to show his skills at Ready to Rumble as he was attacked by two unknown masked men. Billy Bluebie was on the losing end of his match with Jean Paul Valley, however it was a match that could have gone either way. Gunz and Bluebie will now meet to determine who will get a shot at the American Television Champion.
