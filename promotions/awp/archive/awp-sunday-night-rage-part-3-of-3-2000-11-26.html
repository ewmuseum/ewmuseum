Sunday Night RAGE (Part 3 of 3)

26 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[Camera fades back in.] *DING* *DING* *DING* CB: Folks we are underway in this American Title bout. VR: And I just can't wait to see Eric Davidson take the title from Troisi. Not that anyone would want the belt after what Valley did to it.
[Both Davidson and Troisi charge straight at each other. They lock up with the
6'7, 315 pound Davidson throws the champ into the corner. Troisi stays there.
rethinking his strategy. He walks out to the centre of the ring again. Davidson charges at him, but Troisi stands his ground and sends Davidson crashing to the mat with a clothesline. Troisi gets up and laughs at Davidson.]
CB: A playfull side of Troisi showing earlier..Davidson wont like that to much. [Davidson gets up and locks up once again with Troisi. Troisi slaps Davidson who just laughs at him...Davidson charges at Troisi who tries to Back Body Drop the much larger Davidson. It gets blocked by Davidson, and reversed into a piledriver by]
CB: Piledriver by Davidson.. VR: and the The Underground Martyrs head was driven into the mat CB: Underground Martyr [Davidson picks Troisi up by the throat and choke's him...laughing in his face] [REF] ONE.. TWO.. THREE.. FOUR... CB: Davidson braking the choke before the five count. [Davidson lets go of the hold...Troisi holds his throat as the ref warns Eric about choking. Davidson turns around to get a low blow by Troisi which goes undetected by the referee.]
CB: The Referee couldn't see past big Eric Davidsons back to catch the blatant low blow by Troisi.
VR: That was cheating!! CB: Remember Vince, as you would say, It isn't cheating if you don't get caught. [Troisi begins to blast Davidson with lefts and rights. Davidson catches Troisi and sends Troisi flying with a underhook suplex.]
CB: Huge move there by Davidson. VR: Troisi is down and holding his lower back in pain. [Davidson wastes no time in going straight to work on Troisi's back. He lands a couple of stiff kicks then picks Troisi up and nails a backbreaker. He then steretches Troisis back over his knee.]
CB: This move is absolutely excruciating and Davidson isn't making it any easier by bending Troisis back as much as he can over his knee.
[Davidson drops Troisi who rolls out of the ring under the bottom rope] VR: Troisi running from Davidson now CB: I doubt that [With Troisi holding his back on the floor Davidson mocks Steven Underwood whos sitting at ringside.]
CB: Davidson and Underwood exchanging some words now. VR: Why is Underwood even here..he was fired. CB: It's a free country..there's nothing to stop him from buying a ticket. [Davidson looks at Troisi lying on the floor. Davidson walks over to the ropes and is tripped up by Troisi. The two again trade right and lefts. Troisi lands a knee to Davidsons stomach then sends him into the corner ring barrier.
Troisi hops the barrier and grabs Davidson by the neck...he delivers a swinging neckbreaker over the rail.]
CB: What a move by Freak Intelligentsia. VR: Freak Intelligentsia? CB: You can give him a nickname so can I VR: Davidson holding his neck..it could be broken, he crushed it against the guarrail [Troisi gets chair from a fan at ringside and begins to smash the back and neck of Davidson with it.]
VR: The champ has gone crazy.. [Davidson turns around and his caught with a hard chair shot to the head which sends him to the mat.]
CB: Davidson is out... VR: He's busted..the chair shot busted Davidson open.. [Steve Underwood gives Troisi a high five and starts taunting Davidson.] VR: Someone please eject that reject from the arena CB: This match is no almost all Troisi's VR: Almost??? It is ALL Troisi's. Thanks to that chair.. [Troisi walks over to Davidson who is face down. Troisi picks Davidson up and spins him around. Davidson quickly throws a fireball in Troisis eyes]
CB: What the hell? VR: Fire!! Fire!! Fire!! CB: Davidson threw fire right into the champs eyes. [Davidson grabs Troisi by the throat and..] VR: WATCH OUT!! CB: CHOKESLAM!! [Davidson chokeslams Troisi through the announce table.] VR: God damn it..that does it I want hazard duty pay. CB: MY GOD!!! That brought the crowd to their feet... [Davidson now rolls Troisi into the ring. Before getting into the ring himself he throws two chairs in as well.]
CB: Whats Davidson going to do now..he has this match won. VR: He's going to end Troisi..SEND HIM STRAIGHT TO HELL ERIC. [Eric begins to climb into the ring but Steven Underwood jumps the guardrail and jumps Davidson. As both Underwood and Davidson trade blows the ref slides out of the ring and tries to brake things up.]
VR: Get that guy out of here.. CB: Troisi holding his eyes in the ring.. [security finaly come out and pull the two guys apart. Eric Davidson gets back into the ring and sets both chair up side by side.]
CB: This doesn't look good for.. VR: Destinys Abortion.. CB: Who? VR: Troisi.. [Eric Davidson picks up Troisi and signals for a powerbomb.] VR: Do it!! Do it Davidson!! CB: He's going to powerbomb him through the chairs.. [Davidson grabs Troisi and sets to powerbomb him. Underwood gets free from security and gets the attache case that was sitting by the broken announce table. Underwood slides in the ring and hits Underwood in the back of the head with the case. Troisi then gives Davidson a low blow and drives Davidson face first into the chairs.]
VR: NOOOOOOOO!! DAMN...DAMN....DAMN... CB: Underwood high tails it out of here with security on his tail. [Davidson slowly gets up well Troisi clears his eyes. Davidson walks into the waiting arms up Troisi and gives the big man the lifting mat slam.]
VR: DAMN..DAMN..DAMN CB: The Answer....a cover... ONE TWO THREE!!! VR: NOT AGAIN!!! CB: He did..Troisi wins!! [DING...DING...DING] Anderson Michaels: The winner..and still American Heavyweight Champion..."THE ANSWER" JOHNNY TROISI.... VR: Can't he win without outside help.. CB: Shut up Vince..Troisi is the champion and he deserves the belt.. VR: Yeah the broken piece of <beep> that it is. [Eric Davidson slides out of the ring holding the back of his head. Troisi picks up the attache case that has the broken pieces of gold that is the
American Heavyweight title. As Troisi celebrates Jean Paul Valley walks out and watches. Troisi holds the attache case in the air and challenges Valley to
"come get it" Valley just smirks and turns to leave. As Valley turns he's face to face with Sean Christopher.]
Colin Blackwell: Folxs..turn out the lights the parties over..we'll see you next week. [camera fades out.] (c) 2000, AWP Entertainment Inc.
