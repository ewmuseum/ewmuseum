Some of you might be thinking, "who the hell does this guy think he is looking so damn good and sounding so damn cocky?". Ok, maybe that is what I want the women of the audience to be thinking. Or maybe the two wrestlers in the ring are thinking, "who the hell does this guy think he is interrupting our match?" Well, the answer to all of your questions can be solved right here, I am Matt Allen, the man that can do whatever the hell he wants, and that does include leaving a whole building pondering the possibilities towards myself.
