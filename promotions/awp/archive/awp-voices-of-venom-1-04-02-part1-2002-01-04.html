[AWP] Voices of Venom 1.04.02 [1/2]

4 January 2002
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
======================================================== A M E R I C A N W R E S T L I N G ABOVE ALL OTHERS P R O M O T I O N S ======================================================== WVWVWVWVWVWVWWVWVWVWWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW <>VOICES OF VENOM<> Friday 01/04/02 WVWVWVWVWVWVWWVWVWVWWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW <>"Elite Sensation" Eric Emerson <>The Big Chill <>"The Real Deal" Brian Taylor <>"Saint" Ian Walsh <>"American Beauty" Ashlyn Summers [AWP Cruiserweight Champion] <>Saul Akiva ============================================================= EMERSON ------------------- [The scene opens inside a local Cedar Rapids bar & grill. As the scene opens fully a few people can be seen in the back of the bar shooting pool, a few others sitting at a smaller table watching a big screen tv, and a few others sitting at the bar with a bottle of beer placed in front of each of them. As the camera finishes rotating around the area the main door is heard opening. The camera turns and standing in the door way of the saloon is none other then "The Elite Sensation" Eric Emerson. Eric is wearing a pair of baggy bright orange cargo pants, a tan and green Colombia coat, and underneath the coat is a loose fitting black t-shirt. He is also wearing a pair of light brown Doc Marten boots, and he has a bright orange Auburn hat turned backwards on his head. As Eric closes the door behind him a few of the guys turn to him and and nod. Eric nods back and takes a seat on one of the bar stools. He takes off his jacket, and waves for the bar tender. As he does this the bar tender walks over.] EE: Whats up Joe... Joe: Not much, just been working for the most part. EE: Awesome... Awesome, I'll take a Miller Lite in a bottle. [Joe walks over to a cooler and pulls a bottle of Miller Lite out. He pops the cap and walks back over to Eric. He places a coaster down and then puts the bottle onto the coaster.] Joe: So, how have ya been? [Eric rolls his eyes.] EE: I guess I can say it's been doing nothing but going... JOE: The career not getting back to what it once was or something? [Eric looks around, and then takes off his hat showing the stitches he still has on his forehead.] EE: What do you think? [He smirks.] EE: Honestly though, I'm not going to complain. I've done just about everything I've wanted to do since my return. And now coming up on the PPV I've got to face this dude by the name of Big Chill. [Joe looks at Eric with an odd look.] EE: Yeah, you heard me right... Big Chill. Joe: What kind of a name is that? [Eric laughs slightly. He takes a decent sized drink of his beer, and sets it back down.] EE: I'm not really sure. At first I thought it was maybe a nick name. I mean if you've ever seen the guy you'd figure his sex life must be nothing but weak. Which in turn would make him a big chill. [Joe shakes his head.] Joe: Yeah, well, I hope thats not the best you can do when talking some smack. Because that was pretty weak it self. [Eric smirks.] EE: Yeah, well, I save my good shit for the in-ring promos. [Eric turns towards the camera.] EE: But you'll have to excuse me now Joe. I've got to cut a promo about my PPV match. I hope you don't mind I'm using your bar. Joe: Nah, go right ahead. [Eric picks up his beer and then grabs his coat. He walks over the one of the empty tables across the bar and places his coat on the back of the chair. He then takes a seat while he places his beer on the table. The camera rotates around moving to a frontal view of Eric.] EE: Welp, I guess you've all seen just where I like to hang out. And I guess you all know about the match I'll be having with the guy that looks like an over grown elephant ass. [Eric smirks.] EE: Anyway, I'm not going to sit here and say that I'm going to walk all over Chill. Of course I'm also not going to say that Chilly Willy is going to make me look like a little bitch. [Eric takes a drink of his beer.] EE: What I'm here to say has to do with the entire Reign of Terror in general... Sin, Lawrenze, and Chill... The three of you have gone around doing basically anything you've wanted for how long now. But it's all about to change. Ya see I've got a plan... A Plan that could make the Terror _LEAVE_ the AWP! [Eric looks around after he screamed slightly.] EE: So, I guess this promo is going to end with this. Look for a reformation... Look for a surprise so big it'll make all of your minds go numb... Look for the AWP to be turned up side down! [The camera fades to black as Eric finishes drinking his beer.] CHILL ---------------- [Scene opens in locker room shortly after AWP Breakdown has finished. Leaning against a wall is The Big Chill, bloodied and battered. Strangely, through the blood, there is some semblance of a smile on his face.] TBC (Laughing weakly): They simply....don't get it.....do they? [TBC catches his breath.] TBC: Pins. Wins. Losses. All this stuff...inconsequential. It's all about ... the mission. The mission...from the get go...was to take Tanaka and slap him around like a piñata. That...that was my job. You ain't showing up at Seasonal Insanity in the best of health, huh, Takada? No, you're not. Even healthy, Sinister would have destroyed you. Now...now, it's not even going to be...worth showing up. [TBC, once again, catches his breath.] TBC: But, Eric Emerson, the man who wants to be important, the man who thinks he's making a difference, sticks his nose where it don't belong. That's fine. That's perfectly fine. You feel like shortening your career, who am I to stop you? You see, right before I got hold of this chump holding the camera, I told the man in charge I wanted a piece of you. I got no one to dance with at S.I., so what the hell? You must have pissed the man off something fierce, cuz he did you a serious disservice. Come Insanity, it'll be the T, the B and the C who made all the children cry, in a First Blood match with you, chump. [TBC slumps down into a seated position.] TBC: Emerson, since I got here, I have dictated the direction. Think about it. Look at all I've accomplished. I didn't jump in without a direction, without a purpose, just to make a name for myself. But you did. Luckily, Emerson, I'm still in the Holiday mood and I'll give you exactly what you think you want. I'm going to make you important. There's only one problem, tough guy, you're about to get more than you deserved. You see, I serve up piping hot beat-downs in the ring like IHOP, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, no exceptions. That's what I'm about. [TBC laughs out loud.] TBC: You even got a step up on me right now. I'm not sure the stitches I'm going to need are going to heel up nice before the match. Savor it, Emerson, it's the only advantage you got right now. Enjoy your last pain-free days, chump, cuz you got TBC in a few days, and that ain't anyway to start off a new year. See you soon, tough guy.... [Scene fades to black.] TAYLOR --------------- [Fade from AWP Logo.] [SCENE: As the camera fades in, we immediately come upon a plain AWP Background. Nothing too fancy, just the AWP logo on a black canvas tarp. Standing in front of the background is Brian Taylor. He is still dressed in his ring gear, and has a cocky grin on his face. He looks into the camera and...] "TRD" BT: Seasonal Insanity, here we come. Another AWP Breakdown is in the books, and another man has been beaten. Tommy Rowlands, what did I tell ya. You never stood a chance... [He laughs.] Hell, most don't stand a chance against me. At the Pay Per View, I've got a big shot, a chance to become the Cruiserweight Champ. That would make me the_first_respectable Cruiserweight Champ AWP has had. The only guy worth facing was Ben Tuckwell, but he decided to run away. Now, Ashlyn Summers has the strap. You've had the belt for a few weeks, and they were probably a few good weeks. The fun ends at Seasonal Insanity. Your reign as Cruiserweight Champ is over... [He pauses.] Things have changed a lot since we both joined AWP. You don't have anybody to watch your back. Jonathan Conspiracy is gone. You don't have a boyfriend to make sure everything goes your way. Things are different, you're going to have beat me on your own. Trust me, that isn't going to happen any time soon. Face it Ashylyn, you're a person who just isn't worthy of the Cruiserweight strap. But... [He pauses again.] ...you do have a chance to prove yourself. All you need to do is_beat_me. What are the chances of that happening? They're not good if you ask me. Your time as Cruiserweight Champ has just about run out, Ashlyn. It's time for you to put up or shut up... [He smiles.] ...And I'm going to make you shut up... [With that, Taylor simply walks off the scene as we fade to black.] [Fade to AWP Logo.] WALSH -------------- [Fade in...] [Bright illumination... a thing we are not used to with Ian Walsh these days... Working camera equipment without the kinks... The "Saint" holds his right arm up with squinted eyes in attempt to block some of the light.] IW: "Christ... dim it a little, will ya?" [The illumination dims a bit and all is good. The background is nothing but a red brick wall with "AWP" spray painted in bold, black lettering diagonal from the camera's left upward to the camera's right. Ian, with his blonde hair parted in the middle... flowing down the sides seeming relaxed... Ian brings his arm down and adjusts his sight to the dim light.] IW: "So you did not think it would happen... You did not think it could happen... it did happen and you all have to live with it. That's right, I'm back and badder than ever... I'm here in the AWP to cause a ruckus, but moreso to show you all the way and the light, to show you all that I am the one you shall follow into the light." [The grin that we have all come to hate appears on his face as he makes motions presenting his body to the people at home... topless, Ian stands proud of himself.] IW: "Do I look like I can be beat? Perfection isn't skin deep when it comes to Ian Walsh... it is Ian Walsh. When I worked my way up the ladders, the ranks, the small time, the big time... title after title came my way and did I ever stop? NO! It's high time, after watching the imposters, the posers, the wannabes have this sport for the past two years... it is my time to bring this sport back to the glory that it once held. Wherever I went, I brought glory... I brought fame, fortune, class, finess... I brought skill... Nobody can ever take away what I have done in this sport... nobody! Everyone thought that I was done, that my best years have passed me by, that I was washed up, too battered with injuries to even continue to live a normal life. As you can see, it is not so. As I look at the list of people lined up for this Christmas battle royal, I realize that I am starting my way up the ladder... from the very bottom." [Stroking his chin...] IW: "...I find it all well and good. It's perfectly fine with me to start all over again because when I topple that ladder and make a new standard in this business, the people will know... the wrestlers will know and most of all, the critics will know just how true to my word I am. My word is God... and I am him... and the last I checked, nobody can defeat him. Merry Ianmas..." [Laughter... Fade...] SUMMERS ------------------ [The scene opens at the Malibu home of "American Beauty" Ashlyn Summers on a bright morning. Today, we find the young woman seated on her deck, enjoying a glass of lemonade. A large umbrella shields her from the sun's rays. She wears a purple, sports bra and matching, spandex shorts. She completes the look with Nike crosstrainers and her long, black hair is tied in a ponytail. She crosses her legs and flashes the camera a dazzling smile.] Ashlyn: It really seems that things have been looking up for me lately. First, I win the cruiserweight title from Ben Tuckwell, a man that has never been one of my favorite people. Second, I got to slap the taste out of Brian Taylor's mouth at the last AWP Rage. Third, I find out that I get to do it to Taylor all over again at the Seasonal Insanity PPV! Throw in a pair of Prada pumps and you would think that I had died and gone to heaven. I mean. What more could a girl ask for? [She giggles, shaking her head.] Ashlyn: Taylor, I hope that you enjoyed the feel of my palm striking your cheek because you are going to get much more where that came from. I thought you were a minor annoyance at first, with your blatant sexist comments, but you have quickly risen from pesk-that-I-can't-stand to moron-whose-ass-I'm-going-to-kick! Attacking me after my match with Ben Tuckwell was a definite no-no. It showed you for the gutless coward that the fans already knew you to be. After all, if you are as tough as you say and if I'm such a "weak female" then you wouldn't have needed to attack me, especially after I had already fought one of the toughest matches of my career. Guess that makes you a hypocrite as well as a coward. Well, I certainly hope that you don't come into the PPV, expecting to get the drop on me again, because I'm going to be ready for anything that you have to throw my way. Now that I've seen the dirty, little tricks that you've got up your sleeves, I'm more than ready to handle you. You managed to beat me up pretty good, when you were attacking me from behind, but now we're going to see how you do when we're face to face and I'm at 100%. Something tells me that you won't be so lucky. [She gives a toss of her ponytail.] Ashlyn: From the start, Taylor, you've been saying that the Cruiserweight title belongs to you. That you are the only person worthy of wearing it. I'm here to let you know that's a lie. Webster's defines a champion as a person who shows marked superiority in a contest or a militant advocate or defender. From your actions, its plain to me and everyone else that you hardly fit the bill. Cheating and sneak attacking your way to victory are hardly the marks of anyone deserving of championship gold. Heck, as much as I disliked Tuckwell, I'll admit that he was much more worthy of wearing the Cruiserweight title than you will ever be. At least he carried himself with some semblance of honor. You just go around screwing everybody else for your own benefit. [She sighs and shakes her head.] Ashlyn: Before, I always saw our eventual confrontation as a battle of the sexes, men against women. Now, I see it for what it truly is. It's a battle all right. But it's more along the lines of a battle between good and evil. Looking at this showdown, people can't help but see how opposed our beliefs are. While I prefer to take the high road, you wallow on the low. Well, that route can only lead to one place and that's your down fall. You see, I'm not like you. I've never taken the easy way out. Everything that I've gained, I earned with my own blood, sweat, and tears, including the Cruiserweight title. It wasn't just handed to me nor did I win it by "screwing" other wrestlers over. No, I won it by going out into that arena, busting my ass night after night and beating the best that the AWP had to offer. I didn't climb the ladder of success by sneak attacking the champ or whining to the powers that be like some gutless jellyfish. That's why I know you don't stand a chance against me. True, you may have the height and weight advantage but I have the advantage where it truly counts: my heart and determination. You've been running around this promotion for far too long, getting away with murder. It's time that someone taught you a lesson. You just can't go around thinking that you can do anything to anyone, without any form of retribution. Uh-uh. Things just don't work that way. So, you best get yourself ready, Taylor, because Miss Summers is taking you to class. I just hope that you survive the experience. (she lifts her glass, her face serious) Cheers. [She takes one last sip as the scene fades to black.] AKIVA ------------- Its A Matter Of Respect [ Black ] [ Slowly an ebbing orange light burns up through the top half of our view. It is splintered and pierced but slowly its presence grows. Now a couple of squares of light break into the well of black below as ' The City Of One Hundred Spires' Prague is given to us. All along the horizon the spires jut into the sunset, each one topped with a crucifix.] [ Somewhere a wooden wheel rolls over the cobbled streets. We follow the sky-line across gently, one spire replaces another, one square of light replaces the next until finally we fall upon a figure.] "When we are born, we cry that we are come To this great stage of fools." -- King Lear (Shakespeare) [ The broad shouldered figure steps forwards from the shadows and walks some way along the street, a street that resembles times gone by. The houses are supported by magnificent wooden beams, the doors all hand carved out of the finest oak, the walls are haphazard yet strangely beautiful in their construction. The man, for it is clear he is a man stops over a wide bridge, a small plaque reading Vltava to his left has been worn down by the frost. The gold barely gives up his reflection. ] " This is my home, the finest skyline anywhere in the world. The greatest architecture on this earth. Unrivalled beauty all around us and yet, it remains untouched, uncorrupted by its people and their money." [ The man steps forwards into the light of a burning oil lamp to reveal himself as Saul Akiva. His square jaw line is unshaven today, and perhaps yesterday. His clothing rests awkwardly around his body, layer upon layer to protect him from the cold air. The flames reflect out of his solitary green eyes. Each breath forces a cloud of steam out into the chill.] " It is a far cry from the skylines of America, compare your money driven world to that of my home. Symbols of power and greed soar up into the smog filled air, here, we have only symbols of respect. Each and every spire topped with a sign of our respect, not just to our maker but to each other. We have survived the rise and fall of communism in the most brutal fashion yet still we harvest dignity. That is because we are honest people." [ Akiva turns around and places his gloved hands on the edge of the bridge, below him the division between the old city and the new city powerfully soars. A red scarf falls down to the water like a sycamore, Akiva grabs the camera and pushes it over to the other side of the bridge. A few seconds pass, the dark green water surges through and then, then the red scarf follows. ] " We came through the other side just as I have done in my personal struggles." [ Akiva removes one of his finger-less gloves and drops it to the ground. He clenches his fist over and over again as his gaze is drawn to one of the many spires. ] " And for what? Why did I break through to the other side?" [ His other glove drops down, his head snaps back and buries a glare into the camera.] " To prove each and every doubter wrong. That is why I did it. I was left alone in a strange country, I was forced into circumstances beyond my control [ Akiva's eyes drop briefly ] but this is not the time for that story [ Back up to the camera ] I did it because I am a better man than to be caught in that trap. I fought my way up and I will be damned if I am to be thrown back to the basest level." [ His head slowly begins to shake as he unbuttons his long dark sheep skin coat, one button by one it falls open. His hands shiver as they stay out in the bitter cold.] " I deserve to be where I am and nobody, but nobody can take that away from me. I will not be cast aside like some piece of trash because I _deserve_ my place in this world, I am not here by chance, I am here through determination. I am not here for money, I am here because I should be. I demand your respect because you damn well owe it to me." [ Akiva sucks in a gulp of air, his whole body is taut through a combination of adrenaline and cold. His jacket slowly slips away from his shoulders and falls down around his feet. ] " I will create my own legend simply because I can. All these years I have been put upon, criticised and openly insulted, well now that has come to its end because I am Saul Akiva. One by one I will take you down, one by one I will break you either physically or mentally and one by one you will tell me that I _am_ better." [ Akiva's hat is thrown down into the Vltava, the veins in his powerful neck shudder backwards and forwards as the colour drains away from his tanned face. ] " How dare ANY of you doubt me, Saul Akiva? You should all damn well respect me because of what I am, BETTER. Who are you to doubt me? Names? Names? NAMES? That is all you are, a list of names that inspire resentment not respect. WHO ARE YOU?" [ Akiva's arms tense up, his woollen jumper is ripped away from his chest and he stands before us. A fire burns inside his eyes, the gateway to his soul.] " There are a lot of great names in the AWP, names that most people would be scared of, names that everybody out there knows. Well, names mean nothing to me. So far I haven't seen anything that lives up to your legends, so far I haven't seen a man who I respect." [ Akiva thumps a clenched fist against his heart, once, twice, three times. Each time the grimace on his face deepens, and settles.] " Forget past experiences." [ Akiva's tone drops as his eyes turn back to the river below.] " Forget past glory." [ His hands unclench, the colour returns slowly to his face. ] " It all means nothing to me." [ Shaking his head Akiva leans up against the side of the bridge wall, his cheeks are dotted with veins brought closer to his skin.] " I am the one person who will step into that ring fearless, undaunted and I will be the only man to walk out of that ring of my own free will. Because I am Saul Akiva, because I am BETTER. It is _that_ simple." [ ' That simple' he mouths, a spiral of steam darts out of his mouth only to be sucked back in.] " While all you others see this as a big opportunity, this insults me. This plagues my mind. How _dare_ they surround me with you people, barely worthy of working a backyard federation run by a 15 year old. Who are you to stand in my way, to hold me back and damn well try to ruin me by your existence?" [ Akiva pushes himself back into the centre of the bridge and pulls his tight T-Shirt up over his head to reveal his toned physique and sweeping barbed tattoo along his arm.] " I am BETTER than this." [ A sharp slap as Akiva rams his palm over his heart.] " I am better than you Tommy Rowlands, you scramble around like the federations lap dog talking yourself down. Rowlands, let me tell you something, you don't need to talk yourself down because you will always be below me. I know you aren't worth my time, I know that you are a boot boy in comparison to me and you know that I will break you come Seasonal Insanity." [ Slowly Akiva raises his arms out to his sides.] " And who else? The Motor City Madman? I say that with a foul taste in my mouth because you came so close to ruining everything for me. You were there to make up the numbers last week but those odds weren't good enough for you were they. You brought in another to steal a cheap victory. Like the coward that you so clearly are, I will break you first. You will learn to respect me on the 31st just as the rest of the world will as I eliminate, one by one, the dead-weight of this federation. Line them all up, ready to face the only man whose heart burns with the desire to succeed and not the desire to be rich. While men like Takada and Sinister battle each other for the biggest purse in this federation I will fight and succeed to prove you all wrong. If I must work my way up from these careless fools then so be it but I will be with you soon enough, and soon enough you will see just how much _better_ I am. 14 men will find out on the 31st and from there? Many, many more. " [ Slowly Saul moves towards the oil lamps, he cups his hands around the glass coating and lifts it before his face. His fingers are illuminated by the light shining through them, the long tattoo alongside his arm glows in the darkness...] " Who are you?" [...But all to suddenly the light is cast away as Akiva hurls the oil lamp to the cobbled road beneath, a whoosh of flame shoots up in a haze of red and yellow. ] " Who are you all compared to Saul Akiva?" [ Fade To Black.]
