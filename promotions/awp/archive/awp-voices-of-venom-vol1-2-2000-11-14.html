Voices of Venom vol1.2

14 November 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
######################################## AWP presents Voices of Venom Sunday, November 12th, 2000 [Inrings/Vignettes/Interviews to [detail withheld] by Wednesday November 8th at 12am EST] [Roleplays to [detail withheld] by Friday November 10th at 12am] ######################################## IN THIS EDITION: <>"Heavenly" Heath Quirk <>Jean Paul Valley WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW -[The scene opens with a camera shot of the light blue sky. The sun is shining brightly and fluffy white clouds slowly float across the atmosphere. After a couple seconds, a deep sigh is heard and the camera view quickly changes to a shot of a well-built man lying in the sand on the beach. He is dressed in a pair of black board shorts and as he sits up to be on eye level with the lens, he smiles and runs his hands through his short red hair.]-
HEATH QUIRK: Before I begin, allow me to introduce myself. My name is Heath
Quirk and the nickname I carry around is “Heavenly”. You’ll soon find out the reason for this and no, I don’t classify myself as a god like some others in wrestling do. In fact, American Wrestling Promotions is the first ever wrestling company I will participate in and I’d like to thank Brittney
Christopher for the opportunity. With that out of the way, let’s get down to business… Ready to Rumble on Sunday Night!
-[Heath smiles and stands up.]- HEATH QUIRK: At the famous MGM Grande in Las Vegas, Nevada, I shall make my professional debut as part of a four corners elimination match. The three other competitors are Johnny Troisi, “The Principal” Simon Dreager and “The
Warrior” Logan Gunz. Like the majority of wrestling interviews, I’ll speak about each one individually, beginning with good ol’ Johnny boy.
Johnny, I managed to catch your interview on Voices of Venom and you definitely said a few interesting things, but the majority of your comments were foolish, to put it simply. No, I am not like the humorous cartoons of
James Thurber, or a fan of Lene Marlin and I certainly don’t get it on with dehumidifiers. I have no idea where you get ideas like that from and I don’t really care, it’s disgusting! So is Craig David… “Fill Me In” is an absolutely shocking song! Ack!
-[Heath pretends to vomit, placing his finger in his mouth and coughing. He shakes his head a couple of times, before resuming his monologue.]- HEATH QUIRK: Johnny, you say you are “The Answer” but my finishing maneuver is the Quick Reply and I’ll be definitely aiming to get you with it. No, I’m not brutal, cruel or vicious, I’m just doing my job and you’ll get what comes your way.
-[He chuckles to himself.]- HEATH QUIRK: Logan Gunz, I’ll speak about you next! You are the one and only guy I know a bit about in AWP. If you have a good memory, you’ll probably remember my brothers Axel and Byron, also known as Double Trouble, from a past promotion. You we’re scheduled to face one of them at a pay-per-view event, before the league shut down and died. Anyhow, you call yourself “The
Warrior” and it’s a tag I believe you totally deserve. You ARE a distinguished fighter with tons of experience and it’ll take a perfect performance on my behalf if I’m to defeat you. You’ve got my respect, and I hope to earn yours with time. Good luck Logan, and may the best man win.
-[Heath grins and winks at the camera, giving the thumbs up prior to speaking again.]- HEATH QUIRK: Finally, we speak of Simon Dreager. I bet you we’re beginning to think I’d forgotten about you Mr. Principal, but I haven’t, so don’t panic. Principal is a word that means first in rank of importance but I’ve got a feeling that it’s all up in your head. You won’t be “Principal” until you capture the most prestigious belt in American Wrestling Promotions, the
World Heavyweight Title. All the best of luck to you in your quest to do so, but every other man in this league is aiming to do the same thing at one time or another, including myself. Time will tell what fate holds, but if you want to succeed, you’ll have to create your own luck and be aggressive as possible. I’ll be ready for anything you have to throw at me, and I’ll be giving it back as good as I get. That’s a guarantee, so bring it on!
-[He clears his throat and pauses for a brief moment.]- HEATH QUIRK: With that out of the way, I’ve had time to look at the rest of the AWP roster and there are a few big names on there, mainly Jean Paul
Valley, a guy who I’ve watched and despised over the last year. I should have the fan support on Sunday night, even though I’m a rookie, so Johnny
Troisi, Simon Dreager and Logan Gunz… watch out, because the ring is heaven to me, and God will be watching! Haha.
-[Quirk turns away from the camera and starts to stroll towards the water as the screen slowly fades to black.]- WVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVWVW [Bourbon St. is filled with people moving up and down the street crawling from bar to bar. The camera slows down as it takes in the front view of the "Famous
Door" jazz spot. The camera pushes it's way past the people forcing it's way through the door of the club. The loud jazz music echos through the building as people are forced to part as the camera manuvers through the club. The camera slows down as it pans over the band on stage dressed in their dixieland costumes and continues to circle around the club landing on a table off to the side where Jean Paul Valley sits. JPV takes a swing from the beer infront of him as he leans back in the chair tossing beer nuts absently into a bowl. JPV glances up quirking his brow as the camera moves in closer to him. JPV lowers the chair back down to the floor as he takes a swig from the beer pushing it aside. The camera comes to a hault infront of JPV as Valley throws a beer nut at the camera.]
Jean: Welcome to my night off, back home in the Big Easy. As you can tell, I'm not really excite to be here tonight, right now I'vegot my mind on what the AWP wants to put over as a 4 corner's match. or as I see it, a set up to bury me.
Normally I would be living it up, great jazz music, beautiful women around and it's not even Mardi Grade. But in a few days, it's gonna be make it, or break it here in the AWP, for the 1st time, in a long time, I've got my sights set on nothing but winning, no distractions, no one talking in my ear, the only thing that's gonna stop me now is me, and I have no intention of stopping now or anytime soon until I get what I want, until I get what I've sacraficed for in this sport and that's the crown jewel of any organization. In this case the
American Heavyweight title, now compared to some of the other belts I've won, and some of the other organizations I've competed in...this one would just be used as a door stop, but that's besides the point.
[JPV opens his hand over the bowl dropping all the beer nuts into it and clasping his hands togeather dusting the seasonings off. JPV crosses his fingers and moves them behind the back of his head kicking his feet up on the chair next to him]
JPV: But before that little trinket comes around my waist, I need to knock off
[smirking the entire time] Billy Bluebie, the name says it all, and that's not much "The Underdog" Steven Underwood, who will find himself under my boot, and
"Evil"..OOooo...Eric Davidson, the whole die, die, die schitck ain't gonna get you no where mona'mi. Now give me break, I can't even fathom why I'm even in this match, they should just hand the title over to me because it will be around my waist, very very soon, that's a promise. Now take a look at the other match..Johnny Troisi, no doubt currently under a witness relocation program,
"The Principal" Simon Dreager, let me guess, you're gonna take us all to school. "The Warrior" Logan Gunz, ahhh no, just no, Heath Quirk...now...I've seen his brother at work, probably...Heath actually has a little more talent then do, which would place him number 2 on the roster, behind me of course. So
Sean Christopher, "My Buddy" we can save everyone alot of time, and trouble over the next 2 weeks, and you can just put that belt around my waist this
Sunday, you know it's the right thing to do. Davison, Underwood, Bluebie if you wanna be able to walk around with your heads held high in the upcoming weeks, I would suggest not even bothering to show up, because you're all gonna leave disappointed by the end of the night. Now I know alot of you who haven't had the pleasure of being slapped around by me are saying.."Gee, I hate this guy"...but what it all comes down to, is the only reason that any of you hate me, is because of the simple fact, you're not me, and in all honesty I'd hate me too, if I was you, because you'd be better then I was, which is the only reason why you hate me in the 1st place.
[Jean smirks picking the glass up again and tilting his head back draining half of the contents into his mouth and swallowing as he raised from the seat and mixed into the crowd as the camera faded to black]
