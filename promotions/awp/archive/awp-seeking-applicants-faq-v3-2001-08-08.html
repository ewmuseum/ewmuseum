American Wrestling Promotions is currently seeking a limited number of applicants. We're looking for cruiserweights to help build an already strong cruiserweight division. Also, we're looking for 2 or 3 tag teams to add to a rebuilding tag division (AWP at one time had one of the best team divisions anyplace.). If your not a cruiserweight or tag, we're looking for a few heavyweight as well. If your interested check us out on the web. http://www.geocities.com/awp01ca or the handbook is below. The Application ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ INTRODUCTION ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ American Wrestling Promotions is a federation that was dreamed about and formed by Brittney Christopher. "Lady B," as she liked to call herself, has been around the wrestling scene all her life. Brittney has been known to be the type of girl to get what she wants when she wants... at age 26 she finally had it all. From the time she spent as a young girl watching her father train the superstars of today to compete in the ring. To the time in the not so distant past she lead her men to championship gold as a manager. The one dream Brittney always had was to have all the power, all the fame, and all the spotlight... and she had it. At least for awhile. Brittany managed her future husband, Jean Paul Valley, to the AWP American championship... however, that reign didn't last long. When Brittney was forced out of the awp office and JPV threw dowan the heavyweight title, he and Brittany disappeared. The position as President was given to Akira Musashi. Musashi hada brief reign over the company. After Musashi took ill (or became injured), his, er, "friend" Diva Desire (Brittany's cousin) took over the company as interim president. Desire continues to serve as president. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ EXPECTATIONS ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ Above anything else, the AWP will deliver all the fun you can handle. You can expect to be entertained. You put the hard work in, so you deserve to be rewarded and we'll do what we can do to make sure you get what you give. Fun and Entertainment... who can ask for more? Well..you can think of a few things I'm sure. Whatever it is, we'll try to give it to you. Again the motto around here... you get what you give. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ WEEKLY WORKINGS ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ Which brings us to the question: what should I give? What do I get in return?? 3.1. VOICES OF VENOM (flash packet) Role-plays will be sent out in packs every Tuesday before RAGE. Which means there will be one (1) per week. This does not mean you have to role-play once a week. You are allowed up to two (2) role-plays per packet, which gives time to hype up feuds and stories it also allows you to build your character. 3.2. RAGE~! This is the AWP weekly (or BI-WEEKLY) show, following the same concept of most other weekly shows this will be where titles are decided and feuds built. Expect several hard hitting matches to spotlight this show. 3.3. COUNTDOWN TO RAGE American Wrestling Promotions will hold several dark match before RAGE. Countdown will be the video packaged highlight show of these events and will also serve as a preview to RAGE. 3.4. MEGA EVENT Every month the AWP will present a Mega-Event. An event like no other, all bets are off and all the major players are involved as the fueds come to a climax. There are two types of AWP Mega-Events..Pay-Per-Views and television specials called Vengeance. 3.5. WEEKLY UPDATES Weekly updates are sent directly after every event. Updates include the latest happenings and going-ons in the federation, the rankings, the latest card, and schedule. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ THE CHAMPIONSHIPS ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/ Title changes in the AWP are not as frequent as you may see them in other federations because we believe that titles, (similar to our mega-events) are something of prestige, honor, and extreme pride. AMERICAN HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP: This title is for the absolute best. The hardest worker need only apply for this title. If you hold this title you can proudly say that you are one, if not _THE_ best the wrestling world has to offer. This title is usually defended at least once a month. CAN-AM CHAMPIONSHIP: This title is second to none. If you win this belt you can consider yourself among the best. Winning this belt also puts you in front of the line for a heavyweight title match. NEW AGE CHAMPIONSHIP: This is the title which most up-and-comers/impact players capture before heading on to the upper echelons of American Wrestling Promotions. Often captures attention from the other competitors... so, watch out. You'll be getting into some "situations." TELEVISION CHAMPIONSHIP: Very prestigious, is the Television Title. The Television champion represents the best the world of AWP television wrestling has to offer. This is as close as you can get before actually becoming a American Champion. This title is defended more often than the American or Can-Am Titles so if you can keep it you have proven a point. FIGHT CLUB CHAMPIONSHIP: Are you tough? Come from the streets? Well, then, this is the title for you! The Fight Club title represents all those tough SOB's in AWP. Wanna be one? Come get some! AMERICAN TAG TEAM CHAMPIONSHIP: This title is for the best two man tandem in all the land. Only the best team can hold this. The champs must defend it at least once a month. ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ PERSONALITIES ~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~/~ 5.1. HEAD HONCHOS AWP CEO and President: "Diva" Desire Desire is a take no prisoner type of ruler. It's either her way or no way at all. Desire will do what she has to keep the federation under control. Although her power has been challenged on a couple of occasions she has come up on top each and every time. 5.2 AWP Announcers - - COLIN BLACKWELL - Colin Blackwell is the main announcer for AWP programming. A former news anchor man for local news who learned the ropes of wrestling announcing to the AWP. Colin is known for calling it down the middle and being pretty straight forward with his thoughts. - VINCE "THE PRINCE" RICHARDSON - Veteran announcer from the All-Star Wrestling of Motor City and Allstar Global Wrestling promotions. Vince has the talent and skills to be one of the best in the business however he never really takes things serious. Vince is the kind of guy who like to cheer a good eye gouge and a proud supporter of an old fashioned pull of the tights. It's believed Vince got his AWP job by using his above average skills of kissing ass. - TONYA SANDERS - The T&A of the AWP announce team. Tonya will handle all the backstage announce work as she uses her "talents" to dig under the sheets to get all the scoops. She's certainly learned her lesson, however; she became pregnant. AWP Ring Announcer: ANDERSON MICHAELS Anderson in his early 20's and is in good physical condition. Anderson is a young student of the game as he hopes to one day turn the mic in for a pair of boots. Anderson isn't afraid to take the lumps and bumps that may occur as he sits ringside. On a side note, he and Tonya Sanders have a bouncing baby boy. AWP Timekeeper: JAY HOWARTH Another young student of the game some say he has been weird ever since losing the Ring Announcer job to Anderson Michaels. Anderson and Jay seem to have harbored a training school rivalry which could explode as they sit together at the same table at ringside. Always trying to elevate his status and garner attention, Jay gets into the spotlight wherever he can...evening taking the mic from Anderson on occasion. REFEREES TOMMY "I'M NOT _THAT_ TOM JONES" JONES Always in tip top shape, and giving a 100%, this is the usual Main Event referee and AWP head official. BRYAN INFINITY Bryan always calls it straight down the middle...and is there to give the fans what they want...even if that means bending the rules a little. "OLD TIMER" STEVE MASSARO Steve is AWP oldest ref, and perhaps the oldest on the AWP staff. Nearing 50 years old Steve has been around the ring long enough to know how to handle himself. not the fastest, or the most in shape, the most able to see, or even able to hear Steve does his best and doesn't back down from anybody.
