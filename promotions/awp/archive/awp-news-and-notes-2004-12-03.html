[AWP] AWP News and Notes

Dec 3, 2004, 12:00:00 AM

The First card is this Sunday, November 5th...live from Madison Square Garden in New York City. The Garden is close to being a complete sell out with a few select seats remaining.
The response so far to American Wrestling Promotions return has been nothing but positive. A few stars of the old school AWP have even talked about returning to the company.
The four qualifying matches look to be very competive but it's the fatal four
TLC match for the American title that people are buzzing about.
AWP president Brittany and her Sports Entertainment Executive, SEX, Desire have lined up a few surprises for this show. Word going around is a salute to the old AWP is planned.
Finally a couple more people have been in talks with AWP officials about coming in those names include Christopher Scarlet, Brett Young, and Matthew
Beecham. Matthew Beecham being the only one to sign an official contract so far.
