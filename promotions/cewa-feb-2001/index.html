Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

CeWA opened at the end of February 2001, run from a single Canadian university address, and settled quickly into a weekly rhythm: Monday Night Mayhem, card after card, carried by a small, consistent circle of writers.

Twice that year the promotion stepped outside its own weekly frame for something larger — a Canadian Chaos supercard in the spring, an American Invasion supercard at the height of summer — before returning each time to the weekly broadcast that was its real, ongoing work.

It ran small and it ran often, five months of broadcasts surviving in the record from March to August, never expanding much beyond the core group that built it.

The last surviving edition airs in August. Nothing records why the broadcasts stopped, or whether they simply went on unheard past the record's own edge.
