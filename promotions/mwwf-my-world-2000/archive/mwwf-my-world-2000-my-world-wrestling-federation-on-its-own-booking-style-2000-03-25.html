My World Wrestling Federation on Its Own Booking Style

25 March 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Here's what I do in my fed... I never just add something in if they haven't sent it in to me, but when they do send something in, I take creative control over it. Of course, if they do an interview, I pretty much leave it alone, but if they have some sort of interaction with other wrestlers, then I have to write out the interaction because if I didn't, they would have to discuss it beforehand and then, what's the point? One thing that happens often in my fed is that a handler will send in something for their wrestler to do at the card and include no detail. When they do that, I feel free to write it as I see fit and I've had no complaints so far. This sort of thing may be tough for a card writer who can't seem to jump into a character's shoes and fit his style...That's just something that I seem to be able to do and the people in my fed have been happy with the results thusfar...Of course, this could all change at my next card and I could get 100 complaints (I hope not, I only have 22 guys on the roster *lol*).
