Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The My World Wrestling Federation opened for business at the turn of 2000, a message-board promotion built entirely around handlers writing their own original characters rather than any real-world roster. Its earliest surviving notice, posted at the end of January, already described a working website, a weekly card, and a monthly pay-per-view kept deliberately separate from the week's regular show.

Recruitment stayed the promotion's public voice through the spring. Its commissioner, who also handled the page's own updates and wrote some of the matches personally, kept returning to the newsgroup with fresh appeals for talent, each one insisting on the same shape: original characters only, a message board for the roleplay, and a boss willing to work with whoever signed up.

One notice let the promotion describe its own editorial hand directly. Nothing was added to a submission that a handler hadn't sent in, but once material arrived the promotion's own writer took creative control of how it played out on the page — interviews left alone, physical interactions between wrestlers written out in full, and any submission missing detail filled in without apology. By this point the roster stood at twenty-two.

By the first of April the promotion described itself as a couple of months old rather than newly opened, still recruiting, still promising the same weekly card and the same monthly pay-per-view. No document survives past that date, and no closing notice of any kind was ever posted.
