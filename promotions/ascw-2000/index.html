Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

All-Star Championship Wrestling opened in the middle of July 2000 as a New York-based promotion answering to three co-presidents at once, a rivalry the federation's own founding handbook staged as an accident of business: two of them had done deals with one another for years without knowing it, and a signed contract now bound them together whether either liked it or not. A third co-president was brought in soon after to keep the peace between them, and mostly failed.

Its weekly broadcast, Showdown, ran Sunday nights from a New York arena and crowned the promotion's first title in a triple threat ladder match before the month of August was out, with a second championship changing hands the following week. A newcomer signed by one co-president over the objection of the other became the roster's own running symbol of the power struggle at the top, while a Light Heavyweight tournament and a New York State Title tournament kept the undercard moving from show to show.

Four numbered newsletters carried the promotion's news, rosters and rankings between broadcasts, and a second edition of the founding handbook followed a month after the first. A newsgroup thread inviting new talent drew a reply naming the promotion's own two presidents by their kayfabe titles; a further thread of newsgroup chatter about joining ran into September, drawing one of the co-presidents himself into the conversation.

No closing notice survives among the held documents, and the promotion's later history is not recorded here.
