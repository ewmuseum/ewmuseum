Introduction ------------------------------------------------------------------ All-Star Championship Wrestling is a relatively new booked e-Wrestling league. Hopefully you're reading this because somehow, you are interested in this particular federation. The ASCW is dedicated to its handlers, and promises a unique and fun e-Wrestling experience.
The federation is based out of New York State, and hosts the majority of its events at larger venues.
The people behind the federation have extensive experience within the game of -e-Wrestling. We're trying to create a friendly, relaxed enviroment for people to participate in. We're a federation focused on fun. If something isn't working out, please tell us, and we'll try to make your experience that much better. So without further ado, let's get on with the show.
2. The Committee [Fantasy Purposes] ------------------------------------------------------------------ The Committee is the brain behind the federation. It's made up of the three Co-presidents, who serve as the primary bookers and management behind the ASCW.
Co-President Higor Rosa - Higor Rosa, The People's Drunk, is a looseheaded president. Ironically, he actually likes to keep control of the federation. A hardcore wrestler himself, he believes that his accomplishments are truly legendary. His motto is "if it doesn't work, don't use it."
He's known to allow some hardcore matches into the cards, but keeps a relatively tight grip on the major workings. As long as he stays sober, he's not that bad of a fedhead.
Co-President Tommy Grayson - Tommy Grayson likes to abuse his power as president, thereby requiring Rosa and Fury (see below) to keep him in check. At age 28, he's one of the top cruiserweights in pro-wrestling. His brashness and lack of respect has created a lot of animosity towards him from the federation's sponsers, fans, and the other presidents.
Grayson has been filed up on gambling charges for betting on pro-wrestling (what a concept? :)), and with his position as a co-president, he's now more than ever ready to start rolling in the cash. He's the one most likely out of the trios to fill a card with hardcore matches. He's also been known to help out the bad guys in the federation on a relatively frequent basis.
Co-President Alex Fury - Fury is the third president in this threesome. He was brought in to stabalize the running aggressions between Grayson and Rosa. He's the middle man and keeps the tensions low behind the scenes, or atleast tries to. He's completely fair, straight down the middle. He's one of the most prominent faces on the indy scene today, having racked up wins in a variety of local federations. He's decided to call the ASCW his home for now, as he continues to compete in professional wrestling. His management style is along the lines of Rosa, also using the "if it doesn't work, don't use it" method.
Commissioner Tatiana Da Silva - Tatiana Da Silva is the cousin of President Rosa's. She is one of the few women who do not own a promotion, but have a high ranking within the company. She's the one who'll lay down the law when it gets rowdy. Da Silva is an experienced wrestler herself, so if things get out of control in or out of the ring, she's more than prepared to take control of the situation.
The storyline behind this federation is quite lengthy. Through lawyers and such, Higor Rosa and Tommy Grayson have done business together in the past, never knowing whom it was on the other end. Both knew the other had quite an interest in pro wrestling and hoped to one day run a federation of his own.
In early 2000, both signed contracts to co run a federation together and share everything down the line. When both found the time to have a meeting and talk it out in the newly built ASCW Towers, both men were shocked at what they had done. A fight nearly broke out.
Lawyers and security calmed them down and their lawyers explained the situation to the two. The length of the contract is unknown, but now
Higor Rosa and Tommy Grayson are forced to work together. In order to calm both sides of the "team" down, a quite successful and knowledgable wrestler, "Intensifier" Alex Fury was brought in to try and keep control of the federation, per the request of the backers for this particular organization.
Neither Rosa or Grayson is happy with this, but they have no choice, because if one breaks the contract, he loses everything he has. This brings their bitter rivalry to a whole new level, because now they are not the ones performing in the ring, but they are the ones running the show. Each side is doing whatever they can in their power to make the other side quit, with Alex Fury stuck in the middle. This rivalry is far from over.

4. The ASCW Style ------------------------------------------------------------------ The ASCW Style is what we refer to as our modus operandi for the the ASCW. We employ an ABS system (Action in Brackets), which has been used by many federations, the IIWF being one of the premiere ones.
Our idea is that quality is far more important than substance. We write matches that are clear, and well written, rather than trying to flood you with a bunch of useless garble.
The second part of the ASCW Style is the atmosphere we're going for. The ASCW isn't an enormous federation with unlimited funds, but does have a pretty decent backing. It fills its arenas, with regular show crowds reaching to about six or seven thousand people, with PPVs drawing ten thousand and over. The ASCW is about equal, if not moreso, than to the success of ECW in its heyday.
The ASCW "feel" is that of a very modern, semi-national federation. The ASCW has a lot of good workers, and wants quality applicants. It's very angle based, along the lines of the WWF, but we also stress in-ring action as well. In essence, we have something for everyone.

Whoever is hated more, that's who he likes. He refuses to do in-rings after his previous attempts, in which he was pelted with a variety of foreign objects from the crowd.

Riley's comments. He's usually dressed in fine armani suits, with a cuban cigar in one hand, and a Budweiser in the other.

6. Match Determination ------------------------------------------------------------------ The ASCW resolution system works on the "heat" concept. Heat is based on how much draw there is for your wrestler. This is created by doing in-rings, flashes, and getting involved in angles (which is a HUGE draw). Most important in match resolution is the angle. In reality, angles are what make wrestling interesting. If you're in this federation just to win a belt, you're in the wrong fed. This isn't to say you'll never be world champion, but you'll probably lose before you win.
Usually, an angle determines what the match is going to be resolved as. If Wrestler A has planned with Wrestler B to make the first match be a no-contest, it will more than likely be a no-contest, as to drawing "heat" for the second match, where Wrestler B will seek revenge by winning, and the feud continues. This, however, assumes a
"planned" angle, which is only one type, where match determination is resolved by the handlers.
The "unplanned" angle is an angle that is either simply set up by the handlers, or is created by happenchance, with the bookers in control.
This is where in-rings and flashes, etc. come into play. The heat that is being generated (whether heel or face) will determine who wins or loses the match. If you need a percentage breakdown, 60% will come from Heat, 30% from overall participation, and 10% from strategies
(covered later). 7. Long Term Involvement ------------------------------------------------------------------ ASCW likes to plan out storylines way in advance. The development of storylines entails handler cooperation to create huge, exciting detailed angles, critical to the success of any federation. Honestly, what do you remember more? Stone Cold's intense feud with Vince
McMahon, or the in-ring action? You remember the story more, rather than just bits and chunks of the story. Getting in touch with other handlers and working with them is one of the highlights of e-Wrestling. If you need help finding a long term storyline, please, contact the fedheads, we're always willing to help.
8. Roleplaying ------------------------------------------------------------------ Some quick points about roleplaying... No spamming. The maximum RPs we want to see from you (per week) is two. This should allow you enough time to develop two quality roleplays, rather than ten mediocore ones. Remember, quality over quantity.
Don't break kayfabe. It's unprofessional. Don't talk about "jobbers" and "heels" in your roleplays and in-rings. We don't like it, and if you think it is cool, we'll kindly show you the door.
Concerning the length of flashes, we like tight concise flashes. We don't want huge 15k stories. Say what you have to say, and leave it at that.
Swearing is fine, but don't over do it, and use it sparingly. If we find a flash is too profanity laden, expect it to be rejected and began in your email box with REWORK plastered on it.
The WWF, WCW, ECW, NJPW, AJPW, NWA, nWo, lWo, DX, The Rock, and Stone Cold don't exist. We don't want to see references to real wrestlers or real federations. If you'd like to use it in the background to your character on your application, we'll allow it, but that's it.
However, if you need to describe something, such as X-Factor, please ask us before you put "X-Pac's finisher" in your flash. We're willing to help, really!
Don't use other wrestlers in your flashes unless they give you consent. That's it. If you have problems with the above points, talk to us. Don't try to loophole around it. We have common sense, and will let you know if there's something wrong with your flash.
9. Strategies (kindly lifted from the AGW Players Guide :)) ------------------------------------------------------------------ A strat should describe your wrestler's behavior/attitude/intentions for a match. It should contain information on how your wrestler will be dressed, any special actions at the start of the match, e.g. will they push or shove their opponent during the instructions, attack before the bell etc. It should contain a list of the moves that they'll try and use during the match, any special combination of moves they'll use to try to get a win. Any special areas of attack they'll try, e.g. they attack their opponents knee because they know it was injured in an earlier match. Aspects of their own or opponents character/style that they'll try and exploit (e.g. they're bigger than their opponent so they'll concentrate on power moves, or they're smaller but faster so they'll concentrate on high flying moves). You may even consider a series of moves in response to what you know about your opponent and their ring style for example.
Also, a nice touch and the sign of a good worker/seller is to describe some possible counters to your own moves, that your opponent may or may not have thought of. This helps make for a great match and it helps the writers write the cards. This type of attitude also shows me that you are not doing this only out of ego, but that you care about the show.
Note there is no promise that any or all of the strat will be used, although we will do our best to use as much as is possible in the circumstances. Please don't get frustrated if it is the case that your angle/roleplay/strat/or challenge doesn't seem to get used. For a variety of reasons each is constrained by the 'big picture' and what others want as well.
10. General Rules ------------------------------------------------------------------ 1. Don't whine to us. We know what we're doing, really :) If you have an issue with something, figure out what you want to say, then tell us. Going off on fedheads without thinking first is a quick way to jobberdom :)
2. Stay active. Otherwise we'll be talking to you about your position on the roster. 3. ASCW has instituted a new way to bring on "rookie" talent. A three week probationary period occurs once a new handler/wrestler has been brought in. The ASCW expects an angle to be worked out between the fedheads and another wrestler or wrestlers within two weeks of being in the federation. This is to your advantage, because it'll keep you interested :) The ASCW brings a cut and dry formula to the table. If you don't participate within your first three weeks, you get cut
(unless you have a legitimate reason worked out with the fedheads). This was primarily instituted to avoid overcrowding and keeping the roster small. 4. Don't use other people's wrestlers in your flashes without their consent. 5. It's a game. Have fun dammit :) 11. Weekly Shows ------------------------------------------------------------------ The ASCW weekly show will be called SHOWDOWN and that card will be held on Sunday nights. At the moment the card will hold from 3-5 matches a week. PPVs will be held bimonthly, and will be held on
Sundays only. They will be held in the months of January, March, May, July, September and November. PPVs will hold more, and longer matches than the weekly. There will be a flash show in which flashes come out on. It will be held twice a week, and is named "Trash Talkin'." It will be hosted by
Robert Cline and Fred Riley. 12. Titles ------------------------------------------------------------------ ASCW World Heavyweight Title This is the highest ranked title of the ASCW. This title will be defended once a month. ASCW North American Title The ASCW NA Title is the second highest ranked title in the ASCW. Newbies may have a shot at this title right off the bat, but they have to be very active to be around this division. This title will be defended every other week.
ASCW Light Heavyweight Title This title is for the cruiserweights in the ASCW. Wrestlers must be 235 pounds or under to compete for this title. This title will be defended once a month. ASCW New York State Title The ASCW New York State Title is open for anybody. Newbies, guys needing the pushes, or just anyone else who just feels that they want to wear a title around their waist. This title will be defended every week unless the champion is a special guest referee in a match or is in a tag team match. A champion of this title may be out of action
[injured] for one week. If still injured the following week, he/she will be stripped of his/her title.
