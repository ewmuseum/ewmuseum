Showdown 5. This Past Week in ASCW 6. Rankings 7. Pay-Per-Views 8. ASCW Interactive 9. Closing 1. Introduction Okay, another week, another late card, but so what, right? Heh. J/K. I'm seriously trying to get the late card problem fixed but this week one of our writers had computer problems and wasn't able to come online to contact me. When he finally did, he explained to me what went down and eventually I found someone to write his match. Big ups to Jeff Keenan for taking that assignment. It helped a lot. Ummm ... On some good news [for the fed :-)], Ryan won't be going on vacation and will be staying. Another thing. No Showdown this weekend. Saturday I'll be out getting drunk and Sunday I'll be in NYC getting drunk again. Then on Monday which will be Labor Day, I'm expecting to either be on a hangover or drink some more and Tuesday the school week begins. I really wouldn't have much time for match writing at all this weekend for sure and It'd be good to give everyone more time because I know I'm not the only one starting school this week and next week so this should be helpful. Anyway, on with the update. ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 2. News and Rumors - This weeks Showdown got a 5.1 rating. Fans were really impressed by the way the main event finished. - The were no words from Dillon de Stride this week. Rumor has it that Edward Winston pressed charges and they are settling this in court. President Fury only excused them to be gone for 2 to 3 weeks at the most. If they miss anymore dates than that, they shall both be suspended without pay. - "Simply Golden" Jeffrey Keenan won the ASCW New York Title this past week. Something he's been wanting to do ever since he entered ASCW. The New York Title hasn't been this popular since the INWI days and Keenan seems to wanna do whatever it takes to keep on to it. - Two titles have already been decided in the ASCW and two are still left. This past week, an 8 man tournament for the North American Title started. Four men moved on. These men are Colin Cage, "Kaotic Nightmare" Sid Savage, Jason "The Bandit" Blake and "Die Hard" Darren Shaw. The semifinals will be the following Showdown and the two winners will qualify for the PPV title match to crown the first ever ASCW North American Champion. - In more title news. The ASCW World Title Tournament starts off this week. This'll be a 16 man tournament and 5 matches will take place this Showdown, then 3 more the following showdown. As the weeks go on, the men competing in this tournament will be revealed. - The ASCW PPV date has been set and a name was given. No matches have been signed yet. All we know is that the Light Heavyweight Title and New York Title will be defended that night, as well as ASCW's first ever North American Champion and World Champion. For news on the date and name of the PPV, go down to the PPV section. Showdown The Elk's Lodge Queens, NY 09.10.2000 ASCW WORLD TITLE TOURNAMENT MATCHES Round 1 of ASCW World Title Tournament In bracket number one, "THE DREAM" MICHAEL BAILEY seemed to have impressed someone in the front office. She has been put in the ASCW World Tournament to face a newcomer to the ASCW in "FANTASTIC" BRIAN KING. Round 1 of ASCW World Title Tournament In bracket number two, a double debut. Two promising young stars will go head to head. "THE GOLDEN LION" BILL DEVONS will be taking on ARAEUS "THE ANCIENT ONE" in what will show the ASCW crowd which newcomer to be looking out for. Round 1 of ASCW World Title Tournament Back in bracket number one, two more new faces debut in this tournament. KENNETH HALSTON will be debuting in the ring against one known only as GOVINDAH. A lot of people will keep their eyes on that match. Round 1 of ASCW World Title Tournament Going right back into bracket number two, yet another double debut. A popular name, CHERRY POPPIN' RYAN WALTERS will make his debut against another ASCW newcomer, DEVON CAIN. I know that both owners of this promotion will be keeping a close eye on this match. Round 1 of ASCW World Title Tournament Still in the second bracket, EDWARD WINSTON III has been causing a lot of trouble around the ASCW recently. This week he will be in round one of the ASCW World Title Tournament against the ULTIMATE W. FUCKMONSTER. This should be an interesting match as this is the only tournament match of the night that doesn't feature a new acquaintance to the ASCW. MAIN EVENT This weeks main event will feature the first ever title defense in ASCW by the first ever ASCW title holder. SOLOZZO BRACCI will defend the ASCW Light Heavyweight Title against a man who has been up and down his whole stay in the ASCW. He will be defending against TYRONE PARKER. This should be a very entertaining main event.
