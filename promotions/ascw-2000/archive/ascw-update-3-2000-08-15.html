News and Rumors - This weeks Showdown got a 4.5 rating. Pretty good for the second show. We hope it gets better and better by the weeks. - President Higor Rosa didn't seem too happy with Solozzo Bracci after their meeting prior to Showdown, and he is definitely not happy about the fact that Jeffrey Keenan lost to Solozzo Bracci either. - ASCW CO-President Tommy Grayson is not happy at all at the fact that President Rosa showed interest in Solozzo Bracci. He stayed quiet last week, but he promised that President Rosa would not get Bracci to join with him. - After a second incident involving Dillon de Stride, Summer Jewell Justus, and Edward Winston III, was threatening to kill Winston. Supposedly, Winston took this seriously and went to authorities. - Solozzo Bracci still hasn't decided what side in the ASCW he will be with. He is still in the middle. Both owners, Tommy Grayson and Higor Rosa have been showing a lot of interest, but the cards seem to be in Grayson's hands right now, as the meeting between President Rosa and Solozzo Bracci didn't go too well. - Rumor has it that one of ASCW's newest signed stars, "Kaotic Nightmare" Sid Savage, is related to ASCW Ring Announcer, Michael Savage. We're not sure what their exact relations are, but we just know that they might be related. Showdown The Elk's Lodge Queens, NY 08.20.2000 OPENING BOUT We have a double debut here tonight as newly hired, KURAYAMINO will take on "KAOTIC NIGHTMARE" SID SAVAGE. Both men will certainly have to show us something this week as they debut. Only one man will get their first win here. TRYING TO PICK UP THE PIECES XU JING just missed getting the win last week against Colin Cage and Tyrone Parker. This week, he will be taking on two heavyweights in another match that could mean a lot for this smaller guy. He will do battle with the ULTIMATE W. FUCKMONSTER and DILLON de STRIDE. This should be one of the more interesting matches for the night. ANOTHER DEBUT ASCW newest signee, "THE DREAM" MICHAEL BAILEY will be taking on another top Light Heavyweight in the ASCW, TYRONE PARKER. The winner of this match can certainly be looking for a shot at some gold soon. WE NEEDED TO FILL IN THE SPACE COLIN CAGE is on a hot streak here in the ASCW. This week he will take on "SIMPLY GOLDEN" JEFFREY KEENAN. Keenan is coming off a heart breaking loss to Solozzo Bracci which his boss, President Rosa isn't too happy about. Now he has to regroup and try to pick up a victory this week. MAIN EVENT The titles have been away long enough according to the trio of presidents here in the ASCW. They have decided to pick their number one choices as best Light Heavyweight of the ASCW and thrown them in a match for the ASCW Light Heavyweight Title. We have SOLOZZO BRACCI, EDWARD WINSTON III and "HOTSHOT" BRIAN JORDISON will battle it out in a ladder match, where the winner gets recognized as the first ever title holder in ASCW history and will forever be known as the first ever ASCW Light Heavyweight Champion.
