Rules and Regulations 6. Weekly Shows 7. Titles 8. Application 9. Closing 1. Introduction Hello. Hopefully you are reading this because you plan on joining a new fed and want to give us a shot, but not just because a friend asked you to just read this. The ASCW or All-Star Championship Wrestling promises to be a great and fun league to be in. We are based out of New York State. I am an experienced person when in charge of a fantasy federation. I have ran about
3 or 4 feds in the past and 2 of them were very successful. My companian, [name withheld] is an experienced e-wrestler as well and he has decided to put the time and dedication to help me run this fed and make it a fun one to be in.
He has handled the infamous Tommy Grayson. Most are familiar with his character, but not with his fedhead skills. This'll be the first fed he's been in some sort of control of, which is good because he is fresh to running a fed and that means he'll have newer and better ideas which he hasn't seen being used before. Anyway, keep on reading with the FAQ because the
Introduction part of it is done now, heh. 2. Who runs this thing? [Fantasy Purposes] A- CO-President Higor Rosa One of the two people in charge of this. Higor Rosa, who is The People's Drunk is a looseheaded president. He likes to keep good control of the fed. He, himself is a hardcore wrestler, but he doesn't think too many other wrestlers can do the things he has done. He allows some hardcore matches, but not too many. He tries to keep things calmer. As long as he is sober, he does good fed heading.
B- CO-President Tommy Grayson Tommy Grayson is CO-Prez number two. He's probably one of the more power abusing officials in ASCW, and it's _not_ something appreciated by President
Rosa. Grayson, at 28, is one of the best cruiserweight wrestlers in pro-wrestling today, however, he seems to take it for granted with his brash, cocky attitude. He often acts like an asshole, although, there is a face somewhere within his abrasive heel persona.
Grayson, being one lazy S.O.B., will allow the more, extreme and hardcore matches to happen... and he doesn't care who gets hurt. Reason? He's always placing bets. This, coming from a man who once called Pokemon "glorified cockfighting." When things go wrong, Tommy often makes excuses to Mr. Rosa, who he knows will totally kick his ass.
C- Commissioner Tatiana Da Silva Tatiana Da Silva is a cousin of President Rosa's. She is one of the few women who do not own a promotion, but have a high ranking within the company.
She lays down the law. She makes sure rules and regulations are followed and if not, they will be dealt with. She is an experienced wrestler herself, so she will stand up to anybody, no matter how big they are. Big or small she will be there to make sure nobody is breaking the rules.
3. Who runs this thing? [The Real People Behind This] Out of character, [name withheld] and [name withheld] run the ASCW. Experienced in all parts of E-wrestling, [name withheld] has owned feds, been CO-Prez, VP,
Commissioner, and flashed in many feds before. [name withheld] is experienced in all aspects of E-wrestling. He has done it all. In the game since December of
1994, he knows what he's doing. [name withheld] is an experienced e-wrestling handler, who is now willing to give the Fed Running idea a shot. He really hasn't had any past experience with running e-feds before, so this will be his first. One good thing about mixing an experienced fed head with a rookie fed head is the blend and mix of new ideas.
A- What is the storyline here? Through lawyers and such, Higor Rosa and Tommy Grayson have done lots of business together in the past, not knowing whom it was on the other end.
Both knew the other had quite an interest in pro wrestling and hoped to one day run a federation of his own.
Earlier in 2000, they signed contracts to CO-Run a federation together and share everything down the line. When both found the time to have a meeting and talk it out in the newly built ASCW Building, both men were shocked at what they had done. They almost immeadiately got into a fight.
Lawyers and security calmed them down and their lawyers explained the situation to the two. The length of the contract is unknown, but now Higor
Rosa and Tommy Grayson are forced to work together. Neither one is happy with this, but they have no choice, because if one breaks the contract, he loses everything he has. This brings their bitter rivalry to a whole new level, because now they are not the ones performing in the ring, but they are the ones running the show. Each side is doing whatever they can in their power to make the other side quit and lose everything that they have earned in their respectful careers. This hated rivalry is far from over now. It has just restarted.
4. Who are the staff? A- Interviewers A1: John Griff John Griff is a backstage interviewer. He is really a heel interviewer. Always around the heels backstage and taking their side on certain issues. Between heels he sides more with the one that is hated more. A2: Linda Simko Linda Simko is the neutral interviewer of the trio. She interviews in-ring or backstage. She tries not to side too much with anyone, rather they're neutral, heel, or face.
A3: Stewart Silverman Stewart Silverman, just like Linda Silverman, interviews in-ring or backstage. He is the exact opposite of John Griff. He's a face interviewer.
He sides with whoever he interviews, most of the times out of fear, but he refuses to side with heels. He usually gets picked on because of that.
B- Color Commentators B1: Robert Cline Robert Cline is the heel commentator. He never gets involved in interviews. He only works at his commentators table.
B2: Fred Riley Fred Riley is the face commentator. He works only as a commentator and just like Cline, never gets involved in interviews.
C- Ring Announcer C1: Michael Savage Michael Savage is the number one Ring Announcer. He only introduces the wrestlers in the beginning of matches and announces who wins at the end of the matches.
C2: Michele Witherspoon Michele Witherspoon is Michael Savage's back-up in case he gets injured or is sick by any chance. She will not be seen much. Just in case, she'll be backstage in case she's needed. She does the same as Michael Savage.
5. Rules and Regulations ASCW Rules and Regulations A: No spamming. By this I mean sending more than a necessary amount of flashes. I don't want to see someone sending my 5 RPs when his opponent has yet to RP. There will be 2 flash shows a week. The maximum of RPs per packet from a certain wrestler will be one. On some exceptions, I may except a second flash for a packet. If both opponents RP once a packet, I'd have a total of 4 flashes to review for the match and it would make it easier than if I had to review 5 flashes for one wrestler and 6 for his opponent.
B: Break kayfabe and you will be eliminated from the league. In absolutely no way can one bring something that has nothing to do with the fed into it.
By that I mean something personal about someone in the fed, or a personal "feud" that you may have with someone outside of character. I will not give a warning. You will immediately be removed from the mailing list and if you have an upcoming match, your wrestler will be jobbed. At this point you will no longer be in the fed as well as I will need an explanation for why you broke the kayfabe rule in order to decide if you will be allowed back into the fed sometime in the future.
C: Do not send me those long as hell flashes. The biggest of flashes I have ever read that wasn't my own was about 10K long. I can't read a bunch of
10K flashes though, so please try to stay simple and to the point with your flash. There's no need to add extra and useless stuff in there, as in an argument with somebody from the street and so on. That doesn't really help anyone towards their match. Try not to make your RPs too long. Keep them short and simple. That's all.
D: Swearing you can do, but please don't overdo it. If I find that a flash has too much swearing in it, I will simply send it back and ask you to reword it. If it is not done, I will simply overlook your flash and not send it out.
E: Please do not refer to real life wrestlers or federations in your flashes. First time you will be warned, second time you will be suspended for 2 weeks [if a champion, you will be stripped of your title], and if it happens a third time, you will be released from the fed. Keep in mind that this is an original fantasy league, not based at all on a real fed or on real fed names.
We like to keep it creative here. F: Please stay active. If you will be inactive in the fed, please let me know, because then I cannot book you, or I can keep you on hiatus until I know you will return. If you become hiatus without warning, I will book you until the roster is full, then I will replace you, or until I am notified of you being on hiatus and why you will be on hiatus. If your reason to be put on hiatus is because you don't feel like RPing anymore, then just quit and I will let you back on again in the future when you feel like you can RP again.
G: No other character may be used in your flashes without their consent. This means that you have to e-mail me saying that you both agreed on it and that other handler has to e-mail me saying that he agreed, then I have to allow it. On rare occasions, I may not allow it. When e-mailing me about it, add that person's e-mail address on the e-mail in a reply to all e-mail which would involve 3 different e-mail addresses. If the other person's e-mail address is one I am not aware of that he/she hasn't used yet, or told me about to add on the mailing list or to be used as an emergency, I will not allow the flash to use that character. Then I will check with that person to see if that is their e-mail address. If it is not, you will be eliminated from the fed.
H: Don't take what happens personally. Remember that this is a game. Don't take a loss or a draw as a personal matter. I may have done it to push the other person a little, or even to get a feud started between your character and that other person's character. A loss is not the end of the world. It just means that you should come up with something creative to work out off that loss.
I: Have fun. Without fun, E-wrestling becomes a job. If E-wrestling becomes a job, it will become boring and I do not want to have that happening. I will try my hardest to keep this federation fun. If you guys try to make it fun as well, there will be no problems and we could make this a great and fun fed to be in.
6. Weekly Shows The ASCW weekly show will be called SHOWDOWN and that card will be held on Sunday nights. At the moment the card will hold from 3-5 matches a week.
PPVs will be held bimonthly, and will be held on Sundays only. They will be held on the months of January, March, May, July, September and November.
PPVs will hold up to 8 or 9 matches. At some points, even 10 or more matches. Depends on the situation of match writers and angles, etc.
There will be a flash show in which flashes come out on. It will be held 2 times a week and it will be called "Trash Talkin'." It will be hosted by Robert Cline and Fred Riley. 7. Titles ASCW World Heavyweight Title This is the highest ranked title of the ASCW. Only the best are eligible to be ranked amongst this title. This title will be defended at least once a month.
ASCW North American Title The ASCW NA Title is the second highest ranked title in the ASCW. Newbies may have a shot at this title right off the bat, but they have to be very active to be around this division. This title will be defended every other week.
ASCW Light Heavyweight Title This title is for the best Light Heavyweights of them all. Its like the ASCW World Heavyweight title, but for the lighter guys. You must be 235 pounds or lighter to be able to contend for this title. The ASCW Light Heavyweight
Title will be defended at least once a month. ASCW New York State Title The ASCW New York State Title is open for anybody. Newbies, guys needing the pushes, or just anybody else who just feels that they want to wear a title around their waist. This title will be defended every week unless the champion is a special guest referee in a match or is in a tag team match. A champion of this title may be out of action [injured] for one week. If still injured the following week, he/she will be stripped of his/her title.
8. Application You may have a female or male wrestler. You can also have one of each or two of one if you want to. Right now there is no tag team division, although there will be tag team matches every now and then. This fed will not be as hardcore as others neither. There may be the occasional hardcore/extreme match, but the ASCW will not be based on it. Send all applications to
