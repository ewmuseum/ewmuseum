Showdown 5. This Past Week in ASCW 6. Rankings 7. Pay-Per-Views 8. ASCW Interactive 9. Closing 1. Introduction Heh, how many out there wanna kill me for sending out the card this late, eh? I wanna kill myself right now, but no biggie. It came out, nonetheless. It was a struggle actually. I wanted to get it out on time but a lot, and I do mean a _LOT_ of crap got in my way that I had to get done before I could go to the computer and finish the card. I apologize to those of you who worked so hard for this card to be out on time. And now that school is starting for a lot of us, myself included, I'll need the help of my writers more than ever.
I've kept a list of those whom have offered to write or that said they would in their application. I will email that whole list early to make sure of who will be available to write and who won't, every week. I wanna thank [name withheld] for once again being a major contributor to helping us get the card out. Without him we may have been another day or two late. Bah. That would set us back quite sometime, eh? Anyway, on to the update.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 2. News and Rumors - This weeks Showdown got a 4.8 rating. Week by week our rating goes up a little bit more. - Commissioner Tatiana da Silva is very frustrated with Edward Winston III. After he pressed charges on Dillon de Stride during Showdown and was nowhere to be found, then after his rude actions in the main event. She said it will be a long time before anything good happens for EW3.
- ASCW CO-Presidents Tommy Grayson and Higor Rosa had a little confrontation with one another this past week on Showdown. What level will they take to from there? We haven't seen these two in a ring against one another since LAW and there, they ripped each other apart.
- Solozzo Bracci is ASCW's first ever title holder as this past week on Showdown, he was able to defeat Brian Jordison and Edward Winston III for the ASCW Light Heavyweight Title. He looks to be a promising champion who will defend the title with honor.
- A few adjustments have been made to the Light Heavyweight Division. Due to the lack of Light Heavyweights, CO-President Alex Fury has decided to raise the weight maximum by 5 pounds. This definitely took care of the problem as the amount of Heavyweights and Light Heavyweights is almost even.
- The limit to wrestlers that will be wrestling in the ASCW will be 30 and we're nearing that with 24 men on the roster. Once 30 men are reached, the ASCW will be cutting those who haven't been doing much for the company and replacing them with others. The ASCW is still accepting Heavyweights and Light
Heavyweights. Send all news and rumors to [detail withheld] and title it 'ASCW RUMORS' ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 3. Roster SINGLES * Light Heavyweight 240lbs and under [i] Inactive 1. Showdown The Elk's Lodge Queens, NY 08.27.2000 ALL NIGHT LONG TOURNAMENT MATCHES ROUND 1 of ASCW North American Title Tournament COLIN CAGE picked up his first loss against "Simply Golden" Jeffrey Keenan, but this week he'll push his luck against a man who debuted last week with an upsetting loss, KURAYAMINO.
ROUND 1 of ASCW North American Title Tournament "KAOTIC NIGHTMARE" SID SAVAGE picked up an impressive win here last week. And a newcomer to the ASCW gets a chance to shine right into the ASCW NA TIlte tournament. Savage will be taking on "DIABOLICAL" CRISTOBAL CORTEZ.
ROUND 1 of ASCW North American Title Tournament This tournament features a lot of newcomers to the ASCW. Two more will do battle in this first round match-up as BRIAN CRIME takes on a well known name,
JASON "THE BANDIT" BLAKE. ROUND 1 of ASCW North American Title Tournament Two more newcomers, but better known in the world of wrestling will do battle in this first round match-up. "DIE HARD" DARREN SHAW will be taking on "ICE
STORM" CHRIS WINTERS in what looks to be the most exciting match-up in this first round and many think will decide the first ever ASCW North American
Champion. MAIN EVENT Las week we decided the ASCW Light Heavyweight Title. This week we'll decide the second piece of gold in the ASCW. The New York Title hasn't been popular since the INWI. This week that is all about to change. Last week, he showed a lot of heart in an upsetting loss, so this week "HOTSHOT" BRIAN JORDISON will get another chance at some gold. He will start off in the ring against TYRONE
PARKER. Then three men will be waiting backstage, because as one gets eliminated, another will enter the ring. In order of entering as soon as one is eliminated, waiting backstage will be "SIMPLY GOLDEN" JEFFREY KEENAN,
KURAYAMINO, and "THE DREAM" MICHAEL BAILEY.
