Showdown 5. This Past Week in ASCW 6. Rankings 7. Pay-Per-Views 8. ASCW Interactive 9. Closing 1. Introduction Okay, guys. Thanks for sticking with me here. The card finally is out. It took a lot longer than I had expected, but it came out. I think it came out good. Hopefully, next week we won't have this problem again. I just hope that some of you know what kind of angles to work with now that you saw what happened on the first card. I might need some of you as emergency writers in case this happens again. There also happened to be a confusion in the match writing so Parker/Jordison was written twice. Lucky you guys, heh. If anyone wants to read the match, email me back and I'll send that to you too.
Personally, I thought that one came out better, but I was too lazy and too tired to change it on the card and retouch it up. As for match writing once again, we have 3 presidents to write, and some weeks we'll switch around and one or two of us may write one or two matches and if we have emergency writers, we might have a better chance at getting the card out on time and maybe even early. Imagine that, heh. Anyway, read on.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ 2. News and Rumors - The first ever Showdown was aired and it was full of surprises. A lot of things happened. The show got a 4.1 rating for its first ever show. The ASCW is very happy with that.
- President Higor Rosa recently hired "Simply Golden" Jeff Keenan and brought him into his side to stop any plans that CO-Onwer Grayson may have for a full takeover. President Rosa said to watch out because he has more surprises for the ASCW and President Grayson as the weeks go on.
- ASCW CO-President Tommy Grayson seemed to be very interested in ASCW newcomer, Solozzo Bracci. What could his plans for him be? Bracci is a quiet character when it comes to his business and keeps a lot to himself. Could
President Grayson and Solozzo Bracci be working together and have something in store for President Rosa and "Simply Golden" Jeff Keenan?
- After the brutal attack that he took in the hands of Edward Winston III and Scotty Drake, the overconfident Dillon de Stride is looking for revenge on Winston this week, not only for himself, but for Summer Jewell Justus who was sexually assaulted by Edward Winston at the first ever Showdown. Commissioner
Tatiana da Silva gave them a hell in a cell, bring any weapon you want match and said that there will be a special guest referee to be announced just before the match takes place on Showdown.

Showdown The Elk's Lodge Queens, NY 08.13.2000 OPENING BOUT We shall have ourselves a great triangle match for our opening bout. This is not just some regular opening match. These guys will make sure that the level of excitement in this match is high. We have put XU JING, TYRONE PARKER, and
COLIN CAGE in this match. Parker and Cage coming off victories from the first Showdown. Jing is still looking for his first win, but don't sleep on him. He showed his talents on the first Showdown and will now get a chance to shine again and hopefully score himself a victory this time around.
A LITTLE PAYBACK Last week, on the first ever Showdown, "HOTSHOT" BRIAN JORDISON got involved in a match from the ULTIMATE W. FUCKMONSTER as he kidnapped CANDY because he was just grossed out by the two. The UWF then got involved in Jordison's match and cost him a win against Tyrone Parker. One of them will get their revenge this week. This one will be a New York Street Fight.
WHO'S THE GAME? Just before out main even, the newcomer who was just recently signed by President Rosa, "SIMPLY GOLDEN" JEFF KEENAN will be taking on SOLOZZO BRACCI in a match-up to mark out for. This should be a great match.
MAIN EVENT This week, in our main event, last weeks main eventers will repeat. DILLON de STRIDE will be looking for some revenge from the incident on the previous Showdown as he will take on EDWARD WINSTON III again, but this time in a bring anything you can, hell in a cell match. A special guest referee will be announced by Commissioner Tatiana da Silva next week just before the match takes place.
