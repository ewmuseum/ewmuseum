WICW (World Internet Championship Wrestling) — Rules

"New League WICW Needs Wrestlers!!!"

2 September 1996
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I am forming a new e-Wrestling league called World Internet Championship Wrestling, and I need new wrestlers! So far, I have the following guidelines set up for the league:
There will be four (4) singles titles in WICW: * WICW World Heavyweight Title * WICW Internet Title * WICW United States Title * WICW Cruiserweight Title There will also be two (2) tag-team titles in WICW: * WICW World Tag Team Titles * WICW World 6-Man Tag Team Titles -There will be three cards per week: * Monday Madness!: results will be out every Monday night by 7 pm my time (Central) * Wednesday Wars!: results will be out every Wednesday night by 8 pm * Friday Free-For-All!: results will be out every Friday night by 8pm Monday Madness! * 6 matches maximum * The main event will always pit the winner of the previous Friday's Free-For-All match against the WICW Internet Champion in a title match. This guarantees that the WICW Internet Title will be defended at least once per week. Whoever the champ may be will be stripped of the title and the Free-For-All winner will be rewarded the belt if the champ can't defend the title for whatever reason.
* The other five remaining matches will be decided by the first five challenges received and accepted by the wrestlers. Wednesday Wars! * 8 matches maximum * The main event will always be the match for the WICW World Tag Team Titles. The challengers will be determined by a challenge or if no challenges are made and accepted, I will pit the number one contenders for the titles against the champs. Whoever the champs may be will be stripped of the titles and the belts will be forfeited to their opponents if the champs can't defend the titles for whatever reason.
* The semi-main event will be "War of the Week" match which consists of the following: Two men, whoever I think has the hottest feud going at the time, will be put into a steel cage with whatever they can wear or carry to the ring, (no guns or knives, etc. I gotta draw the line somewhere!) Then they will battle until there is only one man left standing. It will be a Submit or Surrender type match. Winner recieves a U.S. Title Shot on Friday
Free-For-All! * The remaining six matches will be determined by challenges and should we not have enough to fill the 8 match max, I'll pit a few people against jobbers to round it out.
Friday Free-For-All! * There will an 8 match maximum. * The main event will always be the Free-For-All match which is: A ten man battle royal featuring the top contenders for the four titles. No champions will have to wrestle in these Free-For-All matches. This is a match for contenders only! The winner will automatically get a shot at the WICW Internet Championship the following Monday on Madness! It will be the standard battle royal with ten men going in, one man left in the end! It's an over the top rope format match.
* The remaining 7 matches will all be decided by challenges and on Friday Free-For-All we follow a "you be the promoter" type role. You challenge someone to any kind of match you desire! No matches except the main event will be sanctioned by the WICW Championship
Committee and anything goes on the Free-For-All card! We encourage you to come up with wild matches and the more violent, the better!
We will hold one Pay-Per-View spectacular each month and they will always take place on the first Sunday night of the month. The first
PPV will take place on October the 6th. No name has been decided yet, so when you decide to join, if you do, please enclose a name for the
PPV event, and I will choose the best. This PPV will be a big one where all the champs will be crowned. At all PPVs, the WICW World
Heavyweight Title must defended or it will be forfeited and given to the number one contender. His opponent at each PPV will be determined by a challenge or rankings according to win/loss record depending on the circumstances. The first PPV will host the WICW Cruiserweight
Title Tournament in full and will be a single elimination set up. The winner will be crowned champ. The other singles and tag titles will be decided there also excluding the WICW Internet Title which will be decided next Monday on Madness! The first card will be this Friday provided we have enough wrestlers. This week's main event will be a simple battle royal including all of the wrestlers signed up so far.
The two men left standing at the end will wrestle for the WICW Internet Title Monday. Odds and ends: * The cruiserweight division weight limit will be 225 lbs. * No cards are scheduled for Saturdays and Sundays, except for the one PPV per month, because I e-wrestle too in other leagues, and I need a little time to write for them. Also, I know some people have other things to do on the weekends besides e-wrestle, although I don't.
Therefore, I will hold the weekends open except for the once a month
PPV and amybe we can get better response this way.
* Not everyone will get to be on every card. I'll usually take the first challenges and acceptances first unless something unusual needs taking care of... (an injury or some feud needs resolving etc.)
* Don't expect to win every match and wrestle jobbers every time. That wouldn't be any fun would it? * Send me the following info on your wrestler to join: name, height, weight, heel or face?, manager?, gimmick?, wrestling type (aerial, power, mat technician, cheater, brawler, etc.), brief profile or story, tag team?, partner's name?, etc.
Of course, send a list of favorite moves... but keep it to ten or less. Also, make sure the wrestler's finisher is included with a description of how he sets it up and executes it.
Send in your characters to me at: [detail withheld] Any comments or suggestions welcome. Please sign up soon so we can get this league going! Thanks!
