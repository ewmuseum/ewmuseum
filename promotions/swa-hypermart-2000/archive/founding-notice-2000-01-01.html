Check Out the SWA

1 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SWA - http://swa-wrestling.hypermart.net/ Hey. I'm an 18 year old from the shores of New Jersey who handles a character in E-Wrestling named Mike Lloyd. Some of you may be familiar with him. He's been around for about three years now and has been in such feds as the TOP (Texas Outlaw Promotions) and countless others.
Myself and someone who I know like a book have decided to run an e-fed named the SWA, short for the Superstar Wrestling Association.
The two of us have ran successful e-feds in the past that have lasted for over 10 months. However, that was a year or two ago. Anyway, this fed will contain 3 singles titles and 1 tag title, 20 singles wrestlers and 7 tag teams at the maximum, 2 daily cards written in the Action
Bracket System and a weekly report, a moderated roleplaying system
(explained on site) and a website that will be updated daily.
We're currently looking for experienced handlers to fill the empty slots and have their characters become a household name. The website to this e-fed is at
http://swa-wrestling.hypermart.net/ . So if that fed seems to be the perfect (or close to it) one for you, I suggest that you join. But slow down there. We will only accept fictional characters. No "Stone Cold" Jimmy Austin's or any of that junk. You know what I mean.
So, be sure to check out that website and I'll hopefully see your character in the SWA. The website once again is...
http://swa-wrestling.hypermart.net/ Please try joining. I'm not really a big fan of younger kids joining the league as pimps, so if that's you, don't join. We're also in need of face & tweener characters, but heels would also be nice. If you have any questions then e-mail
[detail withheld] or don't ask them. - HoML (Handler of Mike Lloyd) or (Humps Many Ladies with a silent o) Sent via Deja.com http://www.deja.com/ Before you buy.
