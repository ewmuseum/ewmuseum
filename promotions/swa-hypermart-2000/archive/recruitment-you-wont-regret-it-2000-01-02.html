Recruitment: You Won't Regret It

2 January 2000

Have you wanted to join a decent e-wrestling federation for a long time? Looking for a fed with on-time results, a great roster, and dedicated employees? Well then you've read the right message!
This is the Superstar Wrestling Association. With a professional looking website, a new outlook on roleplaying, and dedication coming out of it's ass, the SWA is going to be the revolutionary force in e-Sports Entertainment!
The SWA is limiting it's roster to 25 singles and 7 tag teams because we only want the elite in the e-wrestling world. With 2, count 'em 2, dedicated prez's who hate late cards, results will be up at 8 PM EST on the night of the card!
No more waiting 'til 11 or even the next day for results! Mike Lloyd and Mark Faustus, two of the top tag team wrestlers in the world, have decided to open up their own promotion to give a shot at running their own fed. Come to the SWA and check it out. With big names like Mark Vanderloo,
"The Golden One" Jeff Jericho, T-Money, Diggity and more flooding the SWA, more are soon to follow.
Go here: http://swa-wrestling.hypermart.net or e-mail me at [detail withheld] to get info on the SWA or just to flat-out join this revolutionary promotion! Just look at it, and I promise, you will not regret it! ~[name withheld] Recruiter for SWA E-Fedder Extroidinare
