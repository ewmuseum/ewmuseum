DHW-Sunday Night Skirmish (03/28/99)

30 March 1999
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
SUNDAY NIGHT SKIRMISH 03/28/99-From the Civic Center in Boston, MA Dark Matches 1. "Shotglass" Jon Lerner defeated Chuck Portman by submission after the Buzz in 9:11 2. Gunner Sigmundson pinned Buck Portman after the Sugar-Water Sweet (German powerbomb) in just 0:33. 3. Debut: TylerDawg pinned Ron Tresvant after the Kamfoo Kick in 8:14 4. Brimstone pinned Yro Savini after a superpowerbomb in 5:14. The Show Announcer: Alex Tracer Tracer: "Good Evening, Ladies and Gentlemen, and welcome to Sunday Night
Skirmish!!! We've got a great night scheduled for you folks. We're broadcasting live on the Dark Horse Sports Satellite from the Civic Center in
Boston, Massachusetts."
"First things first, tonight's card has been switched around quite a bit, as
Darzanian has requested a short leave of absence. Tonight, Fixxxer will instead face 'Devil' Doug Allen. Kyle Cross will face 'The Angry Asian
Asssassin' Penatracio. 'The Rocket' Rick Marley will face Nate Wunder. "Tonight's first bout is between Pimpin' Ebon and Philip Featherstone. Ebon is still upset from the attack from Brother Grim last week. I doubt that
Featherstone will last a very long time." Pimpin' Ebon vs. Philip Featherstone Pimpin' Ebon came loaded for bear against the smaller Featherstone. While the two matched up well, Ebon eventually took control with a snap belly-to-belly suplex. Ebon followed that up with a set of power moves, including a powerbomb! Ebon set Featherstone up the PIMPS UP (pumphandle powerslam) for the three-count in 6:11.
Winner: Pimpin' Ebon Tracer: "I told you so. Pimpin' Ebon has been on a roll these past few weeks, with the exception of last week's attack by Brother Grim. Pimpin' Ebon says that he has a score to settle with the monk."
"Speaking of which, Ebon's gonna hafta get in line, as Xanthus has asked to become a WRESTLER for tonight, and commissioner Wasteland agreed for a
No-Disqualification match later tonight!" "Anyway, our next match pits Tiger-Man against a newcomer making his DHW debut here tonight - a man simply known as Creager. We know that Tiger-Man is an incredible luchador who performs marvelously in the ring, but we don't really have a whole lot on Creager. I just know he's English, and it's just my opinion, but I hate stuck up English snobs. Oh, well, nevermind, Creager doesn't look like one of them. He looks like a hobo, actually..."
Debut: Creager versus Tiger-Man Creager went over to shake Tiger-Man's hand. When Tiger-Man tried to take it,
Creager unsophisticatedly welcomed Tiger-Man's head with an UN-welcomed punch!
Creager then took it to Tiger-Man, hitting a brainbuster suplex followed by a middle-rope elbow drop. Tiger-Man tried to fight back, reversing a powerbomb with a hurucanrana for a
one-count. Creager only seemed to get pissed off at this, and scored with a short-arm clothesline. Creager picked Tiger-Man up and slammed him down with a body slam. Creager went ahead with the Cat o' Nine Tails (Garvin Stomp) and beat the living tar out of Tiger-Man for the pinfall in 7:08.
Winner: Creager Tracer: "Creager just beat the living (expletive deleted) out of Tiger-Man. This guy looks pretty tough... hmmm..." "Our next match consists of two brawlers. Fixxxer versus 'Devil' Doug Allen. Fixxxer is coming off of a very odd loss, but should not be counted out of anything. Allen's been bragging about his fighting ability, but has been on a losing streak as of late. We'll see, won't we? Tonight these two will lock horns, and there WILL be no interference, rest assured."
Fixxxer versus "Devil" Doug Allen Allen started off with a flurry of jabs and hooks that sent Fixxxer reeling to the outside. Allen posed for the crowd as the big Fix recomposed himself on the outside. As Fixxxer cautiously reentered the ring, Allen turned his back to the crowd and posed once again. This would have been an excellent time for
Fixxxer to gain control of the
match - heck, he even teased a clothesline - but he didn't. Fixxxer waited until Allen turned around, and greeted Wunder with a kick to the midsection, followed by a DDT! Fixxxer followed up with a legdrop onto Allen's legs (???).
Fixxxer then rolled Allen over for a two-count. Fixxxer decided to work on
Allen's legs to take the other man off of his feet. A couple kneebreakers, a legwhip, and a step-over toehold grounded
Allen. Fixxxer dragged Allen over into the center of the ring and planted the other man with a side belly-to-belly suplex. Fixxxer simply slapped on his anklelock. Allen screamed for a couple minutes but eventually tapped out to give Fixxxer the submission win in 9:10.
Tracer: "Excellent win for Fixxxer. It's good to see that he's making a comeback from his slump. Wait a minute... Allen's got a mike."
"OOOOWWWW!!! That's the loudest 'I QUIT' I have ever heard!" "Ouch, ach... anyway, on to our next match. 'The Angry Asian Assassin' Penatracio has put up a good showing here in DHW, despite losing the best-of-three match to Ninja-Master. And Kyle Cross, oww... what can I say?
He had a spectacular debut last week here on Skirmish. If he keeps up with what he's done so far, his initial momentum could carry him all the way to the top here in DHW. I believe this will be one of the better matches of the evening."
"The Angry Asian Assassin" Penatracio versus Kyle Cross The two competitors stared each other down. The Asian warrior from Mexico held out his hand, which Cross shook, much to the crowd's delight! The two men circled around each other before locking up. AAA got the first takedown in with an ultra-quick armdrag takedown. Cross applauded his opponent before locking up again. Penatracio locked in a headlock which the bigger Cross reversed by bouncing off the ropes. Penatracio built up enough speed for an incredible flying headscissor takedown! Penatracio took control for a while with a couple dropkicks followed by an amazing, and awe-inspiring, display of high-speed takedowns. Penatracio applied a cross-armbreaker for a few moments, until his larger and more experienced opponent rolled his way over to the ropes to break the hold. AAA went for a dropkick to the knee, but Cross jumped out of the way, answering with a jumping side kick! AAA collapsed in a heap. The Crossfire Kid took this opportunity to jump onto the ring apron for a springboard leg drop! Cross then hooked the leg for a twocount. Cross set up AAA for the ride with an Irish whip, but telegraphed a backdrop. AAA responded with a Rocker dropper. It looked like it was over for Cross as
Penatracio tried to slap on the Absolute Penatracion, but Cross escaped by using his head to somehow roll out of the move. AAA connected with a dropkick, but had a suplex attempt blocked. Cross, taking control, hoisted the Angry one into the air as if for a suplex, but instead drove his head into the canvas with
the Crossfire Driver (combination DDT/Brainbuster). Cross rolled Penatracio up for the threecount in 16:22. Standing ovation for the two competitors, who shook hands after that match-up.
Winner: Kyle Cross Tracer: "Excellent showing by these two, with Kyle Cross coming out on top. Cross has made one heckuva start here in DHW, and is well on his way to the top. Despite his loss, Penatracio is going to be a big star here as well."
"Whew. That was pretty intense. I'm going to sit down now.... why don't you all take a commercial break and we'll back soon."
I've gotta get a pair of those HAGER slacks, too!!!! Tracer: "Er, um, well, uh, that was odd. Well, uh, our next match is a television debut of Bio Hazard. Multiple world title holder, Bio Hazard is the tallest wrestler here in DHW. He will be going against another big man here in
DHW, R. F. Stone."
Debut: Bio Hazard versus R. F. Stone The veteran Stone started quickly with a boot to the midsection, followed by a flurry of fists. Bio Hazard absorbed the shots like they were nothing, and responded with a big right hand across the jaw! It didn't take long for Bio
Hazard to start dominating the grizzled veteran. Starting with a powerslam,
Bio Hazard cleaned out Stone's clock. It didn't take long following up a massive backbreaker, as Bio Hazard laboriously hoisted Stone up for the Acid
Spill (double-underhook powerbomb) and got the one-two-three in 7:13.
Winner: Bio-Hazard Tracer: "Strong debut here for Bio Hazard. His repertoire of titles, mixed with his devastating strength, will quickly make him a top competitor here in
DHW. I don't want to get anywhere near that guy. A name like Bio Hazard sounds kind of... dangerous."
"Well, anyway, we're joined here tonight by 'Stainless' Ray Allen Steel, the manager and trainer of the DHW World Champ... Dark Sky."
"Welcome Ray to the announce booth once again." Steel: "It's nice to be back." Tracer: "Two weeks ago, you showed up and you didn't answer any of my questions. I would like to get a response of some kind."
Steel: "Umm... sure." Tracer: "I'll make it easier on you... Where did you first meet your protege?" Steel: "He walked into my gym one day. He had no idea what was being done, and so we showed him. He liked it so much that he said he wanted to try it."
Tracer: "Interesting. So, what's next for the Champ?" Steel: "We're planning on staying on top for a while. After that, well, that's our business."
Tracer: "Okay, now here's where I left off last time. What can you tell me about Dark Sky's past. I mean, where's he from? It just says
'The Frayed Ends of Sanity' on the list." Steel: "To be honest, um... well, thing is, I don't honestly care about his past. That's his business. We are here and now. That's all that matters. Now, if you'll excuse me..."
Tracer: "Great... that's just great." "Speaking of Dark Sky, our special addition match of the night is up next. Brother Grim is set to take on a very PO'd Xanthus. Commissioner Wasteland ordered this match as a form of punishment for Brother Grim, who is on his way out to the ring right now. It is going to be No-Disqualification and
falls-count anywhere. This WILL get ugly." Special Addition- No-DQ, Falls Anywhere- Brother Grim versus Xanthus Xanthus showed up with a baton in hand, intent on doing some damage. Brother
Grim surprised him, as well as the audience, with a suicide dive from the top rope! Referee de Pinna was just standing around, trying to maintain some token control. "Shotglass" Jon Lerner made his presence known once again, this time equipped with a push cart full of weapons!!! Grim and Xanthus basically tore into each other with chairs, the baton, a couple tables, and a camera. Grim eventually ended up in control after nailing the DHW head of security in the head with the baton. Both combatants were bleeding pretty severely. Grim rolled Xanthus up for a twocount on a staircase in the back. Xanthus fought back with kidney punches which staggered the monastic monster. Xanthus connected with a lowblow and followed up with a powerslam. Xanthus rolled Grim onto a table. Xanthus then disappeared for about a minute, and when he came
back, he slammed the couch that he was carrying on his back on top of Grim, decimating the table as well. Xanthus took a sit on the couch and got the pinfall win in 13:22.
Winner: Xanthus Tracer: "Brother Grim suffers yet ANOTHER defeat! He has been on a steady decline since the tournament. If he ever gets himself back on track, he could do the damage that he continuously claims that he can do."
"We'll get to the main event right after these announcements..." Mrs. Fogelby, we found your kid inside the snack machine. If you have sixty-five cents you can get him back... Tracer: "It's time for our main event! It pits Ninja-Master against DHW World
Champ, Dark Sky! Have been on a roll here in the DHW. Ninja-Master defeated his long-time nemesis, Penatracio, in a great series of matches. These two high-flyers should make this one of the best matches in DHW's short history!"
World Title: Dark Sky vs. Ninja-Master The two wrestlers exchanged bows at the beginning of the match. Each man was well-oriented in the martial arts, so the first couple minutes looked similar to that of many good martial arts movies. Move after move was blocked or dodged. Every imaginable kick or punch was used in order to gain a weak spot in the other's defenses. It went on like this until Ninja-Master scored with a fingerstrike to the throat of Dark Sky. It didn't so much as hit that one backwards. Ninja-Master followed up with a tilt-a-whirl backbreaker and an
Asai moonsault for a two-count. Ninja-Master went for a side-Russian legsweep and Dark Sky countered it, turning it into a spinning drop toe hold. Dark Sky connected with a chinese flip-kick, knocking Ninja-Master between the middle rope and the top rope onto the apron. Ninja-Master dodged Dark Sky's shoulder block,
answering with a kick to the midsection and jumping over the top rope, maneuvering it into a Rocker dropper! At this time, a ninja came out to ringside. "Stainless" Ray Allen Steel came out to block the ninja. The ninja smacked Steel across the face and the two started brawling with each other.
Dark Sky kicked out of another pinning combination and the two went at it once again. A short exchange of kicks left Dark Sky large and in charge of the match. Dark Sky leapt to the apron and connected with a springboard missle dropkick, flooring his opponent. As Ninja-Master staggered back to his feet, Dark Sky locked on a full-nelson and slammed his opponent's face into the mat. Dark Sky simply rolled Ninja-Master over for the
hard-fought pinfall in 18:22. Loud applause for the champ and challenger, who shook hands and dragged their respective associates off in opposite directions.
Winner: Dark Sky Tracer: "A spectacular showing for both champion and challenger. Dark Sky comes off with another victory! Ninja-Master put out a greet showing, but tonight was the champ's night to shine."
"Sorry, folks, we're outta time, we'll be back next week at the Baltimore
Arena. This is Alex Tracer, saying good night!"
