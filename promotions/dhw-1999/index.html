Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

Dark Horse Wrestling announced itself on 28 February 1999 as “a new, small, original fed dedicated on being an independent league,” running on a closed-flash system and opening on 7 March 1999 with a tournament to crown its first Champion.

By mid-April, closing in on two months old, it added a second title, the North-Atlantic Championship, to be crowned in a 24 April pay-per-view billed as “Armored Core.”

Its weekly touring broadcast, Sunday Night Skirmish, ran 28 March 1999 from the Civic Center in Boston, hosted by announcer Alex Tracer, headlined by DHW World Champion Dark Sky's title defense against Ninja-Master. Dark Sky's manager, “Stainless” Ray Allen Steel, brawled ringside with a mystery ninja who interfered mid-match before Dark Sky retained with a full-nelson facebuster.

Elsewhere on the card, commissioner Wasteland ordered a punishment match: a no-disqualification, falls-count-anywhere bout between Brother Grim and a “PO'd” Xanthus, in which Grim armed himself from a weapons cart wheeled out by fellow wrestler “Shotglass” Jon Lerner before Xanthus won by dropping a couch on him through a table.

No closure notice survives; its last surviving document, 17 April 1999, still previews an upcoming pay-per-view.
