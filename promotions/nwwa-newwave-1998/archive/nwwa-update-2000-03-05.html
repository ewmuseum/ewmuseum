But basically I don't want a bunch of strats that say "I'm going to try to win by holding onto the ropes so I don't fall out." I'm going to assume that your wrestler wants to do everything possible to keep from going out, so you don't have to tell me that. Strats and flashes will be due on Thursday at 5 p.m. central time. Don't ask me to wait on you, because I won't. And it would be really nice if I could get some things in early rather than getting everything two hours before the due date. There are still some of you whose aplications I don't have, so please send those in to me ASAP or else I'll have to take you out of the Rumble. There are also still a few openings in the Rumble, and I will need to have those spots filled by tomorrow night, so if you're interested in getting in the match then let me know right away. ---> BASH OF THE TITANS II on MARCH 12 The NWWA returns to its regular schedule on March 12 with Bash of the Titans II. Last years Bash of the Titans was awesome, with the conclusion of the Godfather/Christopher Maxwell feud, tag team champions Mein Froinds defending against Vertigo's Justice, plus the crowning of the first NWWA Heavyweight champ, Johnny "Bad Boy" Coals when he defeated Rival in the tournament final. But that just got the night started for the incredible Royal Rumble that followed, and the two locker room matches between "Evil" Alex Roberts/Vile Vince Viper and Flaming Bob/R.A.D. This year there will be four title matches between title holders and the #1 contenders to their belts. To top it off, [name withheld], the fellow that wrote last years Royal Rumble will also be writing this year's Rumble! And the winner gets a shot at the NWWA Heavyweight title at Knights VII. ---> NWWA OFFICE IN ST. LOUIS TO BE TOPPLED The 12-story NWWA/UWA office building in St. Louis, Missouri is set to be demolished in the next few months. Adair will be moving to Kansas City, but the word is that he has some final plans for the old office building. When asked about his intentions for the building, Adair said the words "Maim Game 2000" and then closed the subject. ---> GORDON ADAIR UNHAPPY The word is that NWWA Owner Gordon Adair is very unhappy with the current direction of the NWWA. It is rumored that Adair may be planning to make an appearance at BASH II on March 12. If he indeed does show up, it will be a night to remember. Adair has not made a public statement since last June during the reign of Jonathon Tell.
