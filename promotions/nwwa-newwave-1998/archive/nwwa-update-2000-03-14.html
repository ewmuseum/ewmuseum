The buy rate was up from the lacklustre NWWA 2000, which was all in all an excellent event, but unorganized and under-hyped for it to catch on. Bash II was just the opposite. It was a much smaller event with only five matches, consisting
of four title matches and one Royal Rumble. An excellent start to the NWWA's rebuilding process if you ask me. Now, for a run-down of what happened at the card: The night began with the return of Gordon Adair, which we all knew was going to happen. What we didn't know was that he'd have Bob Muretic aka
Flaming Bob with him. Bob made it clear that ANOTHER Hell On Earth lineup was in his plans. Apparently Adair is going to stand with Hell On Earth, led by his favorite employee, to protect himself from those overzealous individuals like Lew Decipher and Jonathan Tell who both fancy themselves
as better leaders than Adair. Sabbath can't be happy either, after being fired over the phone after so many months of dedicated leadership. The Television title changed hands in the first match. Kenneth Morlock had held the belt for a few months. Morlock had his hands more than full with Sandman early on. But neither one of these men would walk out with the belt. No, it was Adair who allowed "Cream of the Crop" Mike Waldrop to jump into the match after almost ten minutes had already passed. The word
is that Bob Muretic discouraged this decision of Adair's since Muretic has problems with Waldrop in the DWF. But Adair, who obviously felt that Waldrop had something to contribute to the match, went ahead and allowed him to jump in. Waldrop won the belt after pinning Morlock.
Congratulations Waldrop, for winning the belt under shadowy circumstances. Next we saw Sabbath for the first time since his firing. It was kind of a surprise to all of us that Sabbath ripped on Ryan Stitt through his entire speach rather than Gordon Adair. Sabbath announced that he would be making his way out of the NWWA very soon. We are all wondering whether Sabbath will get a hero's goodbye or if he'll simply be kicked out the door like
Adair is kicking Nick Hart. It's a shame that the NWWA feels that Sabbath is the enemy. I think instead that we owe him a big thank you and an apology for putting a knife in his back.
A big surprise awaited in the Hardcore match when "Dark Knight" Voron Mikhailatnov announced that Hardcore just wasn't his style, so he wouldn't wrestle Chris Simmons for the belt. Voron was laid out quickly with a hockey stick to the head by the returning Supersonic who is also known as
"The Human Torch". Supersonic was scheduled to be Royal Rumble Mystery Wrestler #1, but debuted just a bit early when a spot came open in the hardcore match. Simmons was able to hold onto his table with some help from Vikky and her purse. But be warned Simmons, I don't think you've seen
Supersonic get nearly as nasty as he's going to. The most horrifying portion of this segment was not Chris Simmons violent fall onto the guardrail or the fact that he had 100 thumb tacks stuck in his back. No, actually we can credit it to Voron Mikhailatnov and
Sandman, who launched "Hellhound" Lew Decipher off a balcony and into the seats below. That was a tremendous fall of over 30 feet, and Decipher did not get up. Even more horrifying was the fact that the head paramedic who carted Decipher out was none other than Bob Muretic! What will become of
Lew Decipher? Only time will tell. The All-State title was on the line next as "Reckless" Ryan Stitt defended against the Hollywood Kid. Most of us thought that Ryan would only give a half-hearted effort since he boldly exclaimed on Recoil that he no longer wanted the All-State title. But his effort was much more than half-hearted. In fact, I would tend to say that he put on the match of his
life. Shockingly, Ryan was pinned by the Hollywood Kid. He lost the title then and there, and that makes THK the very first wrestler to win the Television, All-State, AND Heavyweight titles. He's certainly got a bright future ahead of him. The cheers were interrupted by Bob Muretic who once again made his presence known. This time he had a few Hell On Earth t-shirts with him, which he passed to Hollywood and Ryan. Muretic walked out, leaving THK and Ryan to
consider their options. It was THK who threw the shirt down in the aisle and left it. Ryan simply walked out with his in hand, leaving us all to wonder if he could be seriously considering putting that thing on!
Technically Perfect, consisting of Voron Mikhailatnov and Sandman, were next to make their appearance. Seemingly these two men wanted to be the stars of the show, as they kept showing up AGAIN and AGAIN. Their words this time were no different than ever. They bragged about their own abilities a little, and then they announced that they wanted a shot at the
tag team titles. I've got to say, if they got a shot at those belts...I don't think they'd have too much trouble walking away with them. The
Rock'n'Roll Connection are the current champions, but they aren't exactly the strongest representatives for the NWWA Tag Team division. The crowd was mroe than ready for the Heavyweight title match that followed between Agony and Billy Joe McCallister. McCallister came out and dominated for a while. But brawn won't get you nearly as far as a good brain. That's where Agony came in. In a night full of violent falls,
Billy Joe McCallister became another...being backdropped off the top turnbuckle and through a table. The fall jarred McCallister enough that
Agony was able to roll him in the ring and pin him. Folks, you've got to admit you didn't think he can do it. But he did, and now you have to consider him one of the best in the NWWA.
After that match, our questions as to whether Ryan Stitt would wear the Hell On Earth shirt were answered. As Billy Joe McCallister walked backstage to do an interview with Ron Donovan, he was attacked savagely with a wooden baseball bat. Ryan broke the bat over his back, which I must
say I wasn't terribly opposed to seeing. Of course I don't think any of us approve of Ryan's turn to the so-called "dark side". But in this business, you have to do what works. And the fact is that Billy Joe McCallister had been holding him back too long. Of course the Hollywood Kid came to the rescue for whatever reason. But as soon as he cleared out, Bob and Ryan picked up Billy Joe and carted him down to the ring just before Rumble time. They threw him in, where he would endure more punishment in the next
hour than he had in the past six months. Numbers 1 and 2 in the Rumble were Sandman and Alexander Ramul. But the ring was quickly full. Some of the pairings that were most memorable was
Voron Mikhailatnov vs. Alexander Ramul, Supersonic vs. Chris Simmons, Sabbath vs. Arashi, and who can forget Sandman vs. Mike Waldrop? Also in this match...some violent spots involving Triple-V and Bob Muretic sending Voron Mikhailatnov through FOUR tables...one of them glass...and open chairs between each table. Some other notable appearances..."Handsome"
Henry who made his last minute return to replace an injured Lew Decipher. The ever annoying Brian Calypso made a guest appearance. Prudence Pride and Wisecracker both debuted in the Rumble and made a respectable showing. The last five consisted of the Hollywood Kid, Ryan Stitt, Vile Vince Viper, Mike Waldrop, and Sandman. Several minutes passed until Ryan Stitt had made the final elimination...tossing out the Hollywood Kid. But was it over? Oh no. Voron Mikhailatnov, heavily bruised and cut to pieces returned to the ring. He had not gone over the top rope. We only thought
that he wouldn't return! Voron managed to make the final elimination and win the rumble! He will face Agony at Knights IV for the NWWA Heavyweight title! ---> Well, President Adair is making good on his promise. Two weeks ago he declared that all of those who had a match for Bash of the Titans II MUST turn out a promo before the pay per view OR have a legitimate reason as to why a promo would not be possible. Fortunately at this date, only one wrestler is to be released. That wrestler is "The Item" Nick Hart, who has
had little success in the NWWA since his signing late last year. I personally wish Nick Hart all the luck in the world in finding employment
somewhere else. ---> The 12-story NWWA/UWA office building in St. Louis, Missouri is set to be demolished in the next few months. Adair will be moving to Kansas City, but the word is that he has some final plans for the old office building. When asked about his intentions for the building, Adair said the words "Maim Game 2000" and then closed the subject. NWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWA EVENT CENTER NWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWANWWA =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= NWWA SUNDAY NIGHTMARE: The New Breed - 3/19/00 Loisville Gardens - Loisville, KY =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [NWWA TAG TEAM TITLE MATCH] Rock'n'Roll Connection [c] vs. Technically Perfect for Tag titles - Voron Mikhailatnov and Sandman proclaimed at Bash II that they would be throwing their names in for the tag team titles. Of course Gordon Adair was all smiles, despite the fact that he is considered an enemy of
Technically Perfect, because he sees this as a way to greatly strengthen the dying tag team division. Adair was quick to sign the match. Where are
the Rock'n'Roll Connection? And will they be in any condition to defend their titles against the awesome matching of Technically Perfect?
[DOUBLE DEBUT MATCH] Prudence Pride vs. Wisecracker - Both of these men debuted in the Bash of the Titans II Royal Rumble. They both made a respectable showing, but who can grade a man simply based on his performance in a Royal Rumble? This week we find out what each of these two men have to offer as they will be facing one another for survival in the NWWA. This match will be one of the most important matches of both men's NWWA career. The winner will take a small step forward, but the loser will be in danger of burial.
[DOUBLE DEBUT MATCH] Arashi vs. Joshua Shades - Mystery Man #3 was not "Evil" Alex Roberts or D-Man. No, it was Arashi...an unknown to the NWWA and most American wrestling fans. But Arashi's actions spoke louder than his name when he returned with Sayaka at his side. Sabbath was stunned by the appearance of his ex at the side of Arashi. But he was more stunned by Arashi's incredible offense. Sabbath was beaten and eliminated by Arashi in under two minutes. Now this week he debuts in singles competition. His opponant? Jushua Shades. But who is Joshua Shades? And does he have a chance against Arashi? [NON-TITLE/FALLS COUNT ANYWHERE] The Hollywood Kid [c] vs. Vile Vince Viper in non-title/falls count anywhere - A somewhat disturbing and very familiar duo paired together at Bash II. Vile Vince Viper and Bob Muretic, long time friends and enemies, seemingly have formed an alliance again. One might dispute this based upon their less than intense brawling in the crowd after the demobilization of
Voron Mikhailatnov. But has Vile Vince Viper become the newest member of Hell On Earth? Apparently Gordon Adair and his crew are VERY upset over THK's refusal to join Hell On Earth. Viper, on the other hand, is apparently very upset at THK for eliminating him from the Rumble. But did THK eliminate Viper? No, Stitt did. So what is the story behind the signing
of this match? [HARDCORE RULES] "Black Beast From the East" Sinister vs. "The Human Torch" Supersonic - Two of the most extreme wrestlers in the business go head to head under hardcore rules, which means that falls count anywhere and there are no disqualifications. Supersonic debuted last week a bit early when Voron
Mikhailatnov decided that he was in no mood to wrestle a hardcore match. Instead, the match became Simmons vs. Supersonic. And Simmons was in for the ride of his life, facing the man who was once known as the most brutal individual in all of wrestling. Supersonic used only half of his arsenal last week. What will Sinister do when he has to face the rest of it? [MAIN EVENT/GRUDGE MATCH] Billy Joe McCallister vs. "Mr. NWWA" Ryan Stitt - Even more shocking than Voron Mikhailatnov's Rumble victory was the turning of Ryan Stitt to the heelish lineup known throughout the world as Hell On Earth. Stitt, who had been acting kind of funny the past few weeks, indicating his official betrayal of friends and fans with the beating of Billy Joe McCallister with a wooden basebal bat. Some might dispute rather a baseball bat beating was overdue, since BJM had tried to
kill Stitt with an automobile a few weeks before. But the fans were not at all willing to cheer a man who wore a Hell On Earth shirt and attacked a beaten man from behind. Billy Joe McCallister, who suffered more pain than
probably anyone at Bash II, was defeated by Agony after being backdropped off the top rope and through a table outside the ring. BJM walked away from the match without a title, only to be beaten in the locker room by
Stitt and Muretic. He was then tossed into the Rumble before #1 ever made his entrance, where for nearly an hour he endured a beating at the hands of most of the NWWA's top stars. McCallister shocked everyone by lasting almost into the final five until his elimination by Bob Muretic. But
BJM's mind isn't on Muretic. It's on Stitt.
