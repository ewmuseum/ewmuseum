Champion, "The Cream of the Crop," Mike Waldrop will defend his title for the first time since winning it against "The Paragon of Perfection,"
Sandman. AU: Speaking of "Cream of the Crop," Mike Waldrop, he's our guest tonight. We'll be playing our usual game of twenty questions as we do with all of our guests. MA: Just a little bit of history about Mike Waldrop. I don't know if you've followed his short and exciting career, Amy, but I'd like to take this time to enlighten the viewers at home about what this man has done.

Mike Waldrop made his debut in the league and was a HUGE success. After his first match, which he won, the league suddenly closed down. Waldrop from that point left the
GCWA and went along to the NWWA. He won his debut in the NWWA against "The Item" Nick Hart. In second match in the NWWA didn't fare too well. It was a second round match in the Roseman-Coals cup where the winner would go on to face the world champion. Waldrop was forced to take on Sabbath, and while
Waldrop put on a great showing, Sabbath went on to beat Waldrop. Sabbath also went on to actually WIN the Heavyweight Strap AND the Roseman-Coals cup. So I guess if you're going to lose, it better be against a man of stature.
AU: Of course. MA: At that point, Mike Waldrop took on another challenge. He joined up with the DWF at that point. So Waldrop was working for two leagues at the time.
Also at the same time, Shootfire Pro Wrestling was knocking on the "Cream of the Crop's" door as well. In the DWF, Mike Waldrop's first match was tainted thanks to the hand of current NWWA Superstar, and Gordon Adair lackey,
Bob Muretic. Muretic actually COST Waldrop a shot at the World Title. Waldrop then went on to tag with Muretic in a tag team match where the winning team would go on to face off at the Atlantic Storm title, the DWF Secondary
Title. Muretic and Waldrop did infact come out of that match the winners, but not after beating each other into a bloody pulp.
AU: Mark, I followed that feud in the DWF, and it's pretty hot. Everyone around the DWF locker rooms was talking about it and even the dirt sheets are saying that Waldrop/Muretic is a feud that doesn't look like it'll cool off for a LONG time.
MA: That's true Amy. But while all this took place, Waldrop was put into a match up with Sinister. The winner of this match was to get a TV Title shot at Kenneth Morlock at Bash of the Titans II. Well, Waldrop went on to beat
Sinister by putting Sinister through a table and getting his title shot, or did he? When the lineup was announced for Bash of the Titans II, The
Sandman was scheduled to face Kenneth Morlock for the belt, NOT Waldrop. Sabbath, the current President, actually penciled Sandman in and NOT Waldrop. Waldrop was outraged and at this time, he took it to Gordon Adair, the man who took over the Presidency as Sabbath couldn't handle the duties anymore. At
Bash of the Titans II, Adair put Waldrop into the match and made it a three way dance. Not more than two minutes had passed and Waldrop got a pin fall on
Kenneth Morlock and basically SCREWED Sandman out of the TV Title. AU: A whole lotta screwing going on, eh Mark? MA: Boy I'll say. Sandman was furrious! He went on to beat the living piss out of the ref as Waldrop walked to the back with title in hand. Later on in that night, Sandman got his revenge as he and Waldrop battled it out for over thirty minutes in a battle royal and then both eliminated each other and continued their brawl. Then, the week following, Sandman and Voron
Mikhailatnov, the duo of Technically Perfect, went on to face the Rock and Roll connection for the tag titles. Mike Waldrop made his presence felt and attacked Sandman. They brawled throught the arena and even into the parking lot! At that point, Waldrop actually DDT'd Sandman through the
WINDSHIELD of a BUICK! AU: Oh my! MA: Sandman must have had some serious injuries after that horrendous brawl. And that's where we are today. Mike Waldrop invloved in two HOT feuds with two of the top superstars in the game...Bob Muretic and "The Paragon of
Perfection" Sandman. AU: Not to mention, Mike Waldrop is only 19 years old and is still a fresh face in this business. Right now he's creating LOTS of waves and causing many heads to turn. He displays some INCREDIBLE wrestling ability in the ring. Something we haven't really seen in a long time. Mike Waldrop is singlehandedly bringing back wrestling to the wrestling business. The days of hardcore TRASH wrestling are over.
MA: And without further adeiu, Mike WALDROP!!! ["Just Go" by Staind begins to play over the PA system in the studio as Mike Waldrop walks out from stage left. Waldrop is holding the NWWA TV Title over his right shoulder. Underneath the belt, Waldrop wears a black polo shirt which is tucked into a pair of khaki pants. His blonde hair is combed back and tied up in a pony tail. He takes a seat next to Amy Urrutia and shakes hands with both her and Mark.]
AU: The "Cream of the Crop," it's a pleasure to have you on our show. MW: And the pleasure... [Waldrop grabs her hand, notices there isn't a ring on her finger, and softly kisses her soft hand.]
MW: Is all mine. [Urrutia starts to turn red and gets flustered.] AU: Aww...why..thank you. MA: IN any event, Mr. Waldrop, you've been taking the country by storm. You've won the NWWA TV Title in less than two months with the promotion. You're really on a hot streak just about everywhere you've been. You're even partners with "The Professor" Nick Payne in Mark Rosendorf's Wrestlebowl
2000. That was a nice draw in a partner. MW: That's damn right I've been setting this country by storm, Marky. I mean, look at me, I'm young, I'm talented, and I can work the schtick like the best of 'em. Not to mention, I'm pretty damn good looking if I do say so myself.
AU: Ain't THAT the truth. MA: So, we've been talking about the next NWWA Pay Per View, Knights VII. You'll be battling Sandman, the number one contender for the TV Title. But let's go back to Bash of the Titans II. Mike, you basically DEMANDED that Gordon Adair instate you into that match. So, 10 minutes into the match, you come out and Adair instates you and you end up pinning Morlock and becoming the NWWA TV Champion.
MW: Alvarez, the reason why I did that was because Sabbath signed the match with myself and Sinister for the winner to get a TV Title shot at Bash.
Well, it was a tables match and the winner is the man to put his opponent through a table. I dumped Sinister through a table and was declared the winner. At this point, Adair took over. Adair convieniently forgot that
I won that match, so I just brought it to his attention that I was entitled to my shot, and he put me in the match. I walked in there, did my thang, and pinned Morlock, sending him packing on his way out of the NWWA. In no way was I intending to screw the Sandman. He just took this all the wrong way and now he's going ape s*it because of it.
MA: Please don't swear. It's bad for ratings. MW: Who the f*ck are you to tell me not to swear? I'm the human ratings point. AU: Ever since you've stepped foot into the NWWA, the ratings during your segments have jumped nearly a full point.
MW: That's correct. Why do you think I'm the TV Champ? Ya see, the TV Championship, what that is, is the champion of NWWA Television. MA: What the hell does that mean? MW: Ya know, ask Gordon Adair. Now, the purpose I have holding on to this belt is to be on TV week in and week out. Not neccessarily defending the
Title, but showing up on NWWA TV and drawing ratings. Gordon Adair is a man of numbers. And when he sees a man of my calibar come along, he'll jump at the chance to put a piece of gold around my waist and give me TV time.
AU: Last week on Nightmare, you definitely got your share of TV time. You and Sandman had quite a battle throughout the entire Nightmare telecast. In which you put Sandman's head through the windshield of a car! MW: And if you take a look at the Neilson numbers, you'll definitely see that the entire show had a steady climb throughout the evening. From my opening words of enlightenment, to the spot where Sandman's head went crashing through the windshield of a car, the numbers kept climbing and climbing. And that is, of course, thanks to yours truely.
MA: But let's look ahead. This weekend you have to team up with the Heavyweight Champion of the NWWA, Agony, to take on the team of Technically Perfect, Sandman and Voron Mikhailnatov with Bob Muretic as a special guest referee as appointed by Gordon Adair.
MW: See, Gordon Adair has realized my potential. He has realized that putting a man of my stature in the main event will cause his ratings to go through the roof. He realizes now that with me running the show, I'll take the NWWA to heights the NWWA could never have DREAMED of going. Mark, the
NWWA is on fire, and with me climbing that ladder of success, we'll continue to go higher and higher and set records.
MA: But what about your opponents, Voron Mikhailatnov and Sandman, two guys you shouldn't be taking lightly. They ARE the tag team champions.
MW: So? They won the belts off of some 80s wanna be team who just, simply put, sucked. I mean, take a look at the tag team situation wer have here in the NWWA. It's a total laughing stock. We've got a team called Hot Dogs and
Donuts for crying out loud! Wrestling has become a form of entertainment where catchphrases and cliche`s will get you over. Creating a team of
Hot Dogs and Donuts was some unfunny joke I'm sure Gordon Adair thought up in order to grab some sort of cheap heat for his tag division. Well, to make my point, the NWWA Tag Division has a history of being some sort of carnival circus and with Technically Perfect holding the gold, as long as they hold it, I'm sure the NWWA Tag Division will continue to be a laughing stock that holds the NWWA down for years to come.
MA: So you are taking them as jokes? MW: Yes, Alvarez, I am taking them as jokes. Look at Sandman for example, he's a total nut case. He'll snap just when he doesn't get his way. And that heinious bitch of his, Dream Girl, ohh, don't get me started on her.
MA: What about her? MW: Just LOOK at her! Dear god, she's a total disgrace to the term VALET. I swear, if she's at ringside for the match this week, or next, I will PERSONALLY put a muzzle around her. She's got one helluva bite. And that's a mean one at that.
AU: Oh, come on Mike, that's not very nice. MW: Amy, I didn't come out here to be a "nice" guy. What I came to do is speak the truth. And that's what I'm doing. Dream Girl is a disgusting looking woman and that's that. Sandman is a total psycho and needs the white jacket and padded cell. Voron Mikhailatnov is...well, BORING! I mean, have you guys actually heard his segments on NWWA TV? God, this guy just drags
ON and ON and ON and ON and ON! It gets to the point where you HAVE to THROW your TV out the window! I mean, sure, you COULD turn it off, but the ringing of Voron's monotonious voice in your head can drive a man INSANE! The only reason you see him getting a Heavyweight Title shot is because he's been talking to Adair. And he just keeps talking, and talking and talking.
Most of the time it's about useless nonsense that Adair doesn't really care about, but he's just annoying! Adair finally got tired of it and just gave in. But don't worry about it, Agony will take care of Mikhailnatov at
Knights VII. Trust me. MA: I don't know about this, Mike, you seem pretty confident. MW: Alvarez, I've been this way my whole life. Being level headed doesn't get you anywhere. You almost HAVE to be an arrogant asshole now a days to get anything done. You don't see successful people thinking, "Well, I can't beat him, but I'll try my hardest." Guys like that get slapped around and called Billy by big biker guys with whips and chains. And Alvarez, I'm not cocky, and I'm not over confident, I'm just simply that good.
AU: And what about Bob Muretic, he doesn't like you nor Voron Mikhailatnov one bit. MW: Muretic has been a thorn in my side since I stepped foot into wrestling. I'm sure you guys have known the scuffle he and I have going right now for the DWF Atlantic Storm title. Well, that feud over there may just very well spill over into the NWWA if Muretic decides to stick his nose in my business. And I won't be afraid to take Muretic and lay his ass out if he starts to screw me out of a win. So Muretic, I hope to God you're listening, but if you decide to stick your nose where it doesn't belong, you can guarantee that I'll put the boots to you and lay you out all over the
Gund Arena. MA: And Mr. Waldrop, do you have any closing comments? MW: Yes I do, this Sunday I plan on putting forth the best damn match of my career. And when the smoke clears and it's all said and done, Mike
Waldrop, and the current Heavyweight Champion, Agony, will walk out of the Gund Arena victorious and moving on to Knights VII to defend and retain our current singles titles. So Sandman, Mikhailatnov, just remember one thing....

I'm not claiming nepotism or anything because, frankly, Bob's never done much but run around getting his head beaten in, but still . . .

I' just not interested in irritating him anymore. The joy's gone out of it really, the joy's gone out of almost everything.

(Gena grabs two sodas and picks up the medicine. Agony's eyes follow her, a fond smile on his face.)
Agony: I am feeling morose I suppose. The after effects of finding out that one's life is slowly ending and there is nothing one can do about it. Not sure how long it'll be before the physical sensation, any and all, will be gone. Not sure at all. Hmm.

Beat you silly every chance I got, hit Bob a bunch of times with a chair then destroy them by myself. But things aren't quite normal, you see? I'm dying, metaphysically, spiritually speaking and it throws my karmic balance off. So you and I buddy, we'll work together like a
well-oiled machine. (Agony laughs) Agony: Hell, if Voron and I can get randomly paired up and win a tag team tournament than you and I can certainly beat him and Sandman. So
Mike, it's going to be you and me. Like the Brady bunch only with two of us. And Voron will be the man who hasn't used a technical move in a month and Sandman can be the guy whose still trying to decide whether or not he's going to make more money as a heel or a face.

Beat you silly? I think so, I think that's a plan I'm going to go with. After all, you're a perfectionist, I'll teach you, help you reach your goal. Pain management, it's a very important lesson and I'm glad that I' the one whose going to give it to you. Helping a fellow wrestler along to his goal. It's noble. I like it.
