What is NWWA? New Wave Wrestling Association, ran by Gordon Adair, started up in
October of 1998. Adair ran the Universal Wrestling Alliance from 1996 to
1998. In September of 1998, Adair resigned as president, just two weeks after the infamous UWA/UIWF/Lynch Mob scandal. He disappeared for a month, returning to find messages on his answering machine from several dozen of his former employees. All of them demanded the same thing, that the UWA be reopened so that they could fight again. Moved by the loyalty of his former employees, Adair established the New Wave
Wrestling Association on October 3, 1998 in Springfield, Missouri, so that the UWA legacy would continue on. Since this time the organization has been ever growing, and has moved out of the Springfield Convention
Center that it called home for the first three months of its lifetime.
With each passing month the NWWA grows in its talent and its popularity. Today it is considered one of the best promotions on the internet.
2. History of the NWWA. So you can understand a little bit about what has gone down in the NWWA so far, we have broken its history down into eras.
2.1 Hell On Earth Era: Oct '98 - Mar '99 Soon after founding the NWWA, Gordon Adair found himself once again under fire by employees. Though Adair was at this time innocent of what he was being charged with, he hired a stable of men to protect him from his accusers. This particular stable was led by Flaming Bob, and was named Hell On Earth. By associating with this unpopular faction, Adair himself became a heel. But Hell On Earth prevailed over Jay Cee and the rest of Adair's enemies. From that point, even after Adair's departure from the group, Hell On Earth consisting of Bob Muretic, "Evil" Alex
Roberts, Edward De Villiers, Jack "The Pearl" Asia, and Nick Wright dominated the ranks of the NWWA with sheer brutality. But in April of
1999, their dominance would be overcome almost over night.
2.2 KOTR/FEAR/Tell Era: Apr '99 - Sep '99 Months of problems within Hell On Earth allowed for an easy overthrow of their power. "The Walking Contradiction" Sabbath appeared in the NWWA in April of 1999 and brought a few friends along with him.
Sabbath, Rogue, "The Paragon of Perfection" Sandman, "Dark Knight" Voron
Mikhailatnov, and "Hellhound" Lew Decipher were collectively known as
FEAR. They reigned strong over the NWWA until D-Man, Billy Joe
McCallister, R.A.D., and "Reckless" Ryan Stitt gathered to recreate one of the most dominating stables of all time, the Knights of the Round.
It was then that President Gordon Adair made one of the most shocking announcements of all time. Adair, who was sick of the NWWA wrestlers and fans, turned over power to the evil FEAR stable. For the next few months, FEAR feuded with the KOTR over leadership of the NWWA. FEAR began to collapse as the KOTR began to grow. But still the KOTR were unable to take control from Sabbath and FEAR. In June, during a
Heavyweight title match between Ryan Stitt and Voron Mikhailatnov, NWWA
Commissioner Jonathon Tell reappeared after a long vacation and reclaimed the power the NWWA, as his contract clearly stated. Tell would go on to raise his own group, the Tell Administration, consisting of "The Human Torch" Supersonic, The Wasp, and "The Fatal Charm" Viola
Dagan. A three way feud between Tell, FEAR, and the KOTR erupted. By
September, FEAR had completely collapsed, the KOTR had gained Johnny
"Bad Boy" Coals, and Jonathon Tell's power was weakening. Tell was lured into a match with a masked loser named The Flying Frenchman for control of the NWWA. Tell was shocked to find out that the man under the mask was Sabbath, who pinned Tell and won control once again.
Unlike last time, the NWWA fans all over the world were behind Sabbath
100%. 2.3 The Sabbath Era: Oct '99 - Feb '00 Sabbath was undoubtedly the best leader that the NWWA had had up to this point. Many had thought that he would go back to his old ways and reform FEAR, but instead he hired a multitude of new talent and guided the NWWA to greater success than ever before. Television ratings rocketed through the roof, and tickets were sold like hot cakes. But like all leaders, Sabbath ran out of fuel in February of 2000, and a displeased Gordon Adair fired him without so much as a thank you.
3. How does one join the NWWA? NWWA is open to applications at the time. If you are, or have been interested in NWWA, surely you have been sent a creation sheet. If not,
e-mail Gordon Adair at [detail withheld] and one will be sent to you promptly. After filling out the creation sheet, and supplying us with a sample of your work, we will immediatly contact you regarding your entry, or refusal, into NWWA.
4. What match writing style does the NWWA use? NWWA encourages the ABS(Action Block System) of match writing. ABS means that the lion share of a match is summarized move for move within square brackets, and announcer comments are used to break up the paragraphs. For example...
DC: Well President Adair is right. This match now approaching the
30 minute mark. Viper getting up out of that table mess...Dao doing so at the same time...and now Viper has grabbed a bag from outside the ring.
I don't know what he thinks he's going to do. [Viper rolls back inside the ring and spills out a bag of thumb tacks all over the canvass. Dao sees this and seems amused, rolling back inside.] RR: How is Vile gonna get Dao down on those tacks! DC: I think he just realized that...and Dao has realized the same thing! If anybody goes on those tacks...it's going to be Viper!! Dao staring at Vile standing there with his empty bag. Vile swings that bag....RIGHT INTO THE FACE OF DAO!!! THERE'S SOMETHING IN THAT BAG!!
[Dao spins around and goes down on his knees in the corner as blood pours from his face. He shakes his head and roars, standing up with his face deeply cut by something worse than thumb tacks.]
GA: What the....what's in that bag!? DC: I don't know...but Viper came down here prepared it seems like! He had this stuff planted, possibly by Jay Cee or Jack Asia! And Dao is bleeding profusely from the head...blood pouring into that eye! He started toward Viper but blood got in his eye! OH MY GOD!!! VILE VINCE
VIPER BODYSLAMS DAO ON THE THUMBTACKS!!!!!!!! [The crowd all rises to their feet with a deafening crowd pop as Dao lays in the middle of the ring in pain from the thumb tacks that prick his back and behind. Viper slumps over in the corner.]
RR: Hahaha!!! I think things have just turned around a little bit!! GA: I can't believe what I just saw. DC: Dao getting right back to his feet...his back now an open wound from those thumb tacks...but he's coming right at Viper.....SATAN'S
STRUT!!!! Strutting eye gouge gets Dao right on target and Dao backpedals a bit backward...
[Viper with a sudden burst of energy slides outside the ring and grabs a steel chair. The crowd cheers him on as he quickly rushes back into the ring and smacks Dao over the head with it. The sound is sickening as Viper hits him three more times.]
DC: The unrecognizeable Dao now sinking back into the corner...and what is this? Viper hooking that chair around Dao's neck!
GA: Somebody's going to get hurt in there....I think.... RR: Viper going to the top turnbuckle...and I think........ [Viper jumps off the top and DDT's Dao into the thumb tacks with the chair placed around his neck. The chair contracts and crushes his head between, sending a new stream of blood flowing onto the canvass. Viper stomps the chair again for good measure and falls down upon him for the pin....]
The actual league style used is similar to that of the WWF or WCW, with great emphasis on storylines and vocal characters. Those who have the heat with the fans are generally going to be more successful.
5. Who's in charge? In reality, Gordon Adair is in charge of the NWWA. However, Adair seldom assumes an on-screen roll in the NWWA. He remains behind he scenes. From time to time Adair makes his presence known, but only under the most extreme circumstances.
6. What are the weekly NWWA shows? 6.1 NWWA Recoil Recoil is the weekly flash show, featuring the rants and ravings of the NWWA stars what happened on the previous card and what is to come on the next one. The show will be aired on Thursday nights almost every week.
6.2 NWWA Thursday Night Turbo TNT is the NWWA's equivalent of WCW Saturday Night. It features many of the NWWA's lower calibur talents as well as a few of the top
NWWA stars. No roleplays are required to excel in this show.
6.3 NWWA Sunday Nightmare NWWA's "big" weekly show, Sunday Nightmare, will be held throughout different arenas across the mid-west and just east of the Mississippi
River. States that most commonly host NWWA Sunday Nightmare include
Missouri, Arkansas, Oklahoma, Kansas, Nebraska, Iowa, Tennessee, and
Illinois. This show delivers in featuring pay per piew calibur action every week. It is hosted by Scott McBane, Gena O'Connel, and the pesky midget Billy.
7. Who are the personalities behind the microphones? There are three commentators within the New Wave Wrestling Association. 7.1 Scott McBane Scott McBane is your basic face commentator. He is pretty impartial. His stance on issues is only brought out when he's commentating with a good heel commentator who knows how to make his temper flare. McBane has and always will side with the good guys. He stands for truth, justice, and the American way.
7.2 Gena O'Connel With the looks of a goddess and a sharp wit to match, Gena O'Connel was the obvious choice for the much needed female presence in the NWWA's personnel. Gena was at one time the girlfriend of NWWA superstar
"Reckless" Ryan Stitt, but was won over by the insane Agony. Gena used to hate extreme violence, but now feels much differently about it as her boyfriend is after all one of the sons of hardcore.
7.3 Billy Billy is the midget manager of former NWWA wrestler, Happy Jack. He is a Jerry Lawler type commentator. Lots of insults towards both the other commentators and the wrestlers. He'll even go as far as to heckle the wrestlers loud enough for them to hear during their matches. He'll always side against wrestlers that have had rivalries with Happy Jack.
His opinions will always be contrary to what the face commentator thinks, just for the sake of being argumentive. He'll be very fond of pointing out that anything that any wrestler does, Happy Jack can do it better.
8. Who are the interviewers? Several interviewers and reporters have tried their hand at digging for the dirt, and currently there are two under employment.
8.1 Ron Donovan Donovan asks the controversial questions that everyone wants to know. Often he upsets the individual he is interviewing, and often to the point that he has to spend the night in the hospital. He takes pride in the bumps and scars he's earned though, and certainly he receives a little bonus on his paycheck for his efforts. Always wearing a bandage of some time, Donovan is one of the most respected announcers out there.
8.2 Chris "Air" Fair Formerly a wrestling manager, Chris Fair couldn't handle the bumps associated with the job and got into broadcast journalism. Fair has realized over the past few years that gimmicks are important, and since has developed his own style. He likes to wear lots of hair grease and leather jackets. Oh, and watch out for his switch blade comb.
9. Who is the official ring announcer? 9.1 Barry Nelson was brought from the defunct UIWF by Edward De
Villiers as a personal ring announcer for his men. Nelson soon became a thorn in the side of the official NWWA ring announcer, Jim Goade and another out of control rogue ring announcer, Michael Cook. A triple threat match resulted at Knights V in August of 1999 to determine who would be the official ring announcer. Nelson won, and soon after formed the Ring Announcers World Order with Jim Goade. The RAWO was disbanded three hours later.
10. Who signs the cheques? 10.1 Gordon Adair Gordon Adair owns the NWWA, therefore he has final say on who works there and who doesn't. He also gives the final word on which of his talents earn the six figure salaries, and which are deserving of less.
Adair lost interest in the NWWA for a while, and chaos resulted. But now he keeps a close eye on the fed, and is not afraid to hand out termination papers.
11. What titles does NWWA offer? At this time the NWWA offers five title belts. 11.1 Singles Titles There are four singles titles currently. The NWWA Heavyweight Title is _the_ top title within the NWWA. The person who holds this championship is recognized as the leader of the NWWA.
The All-State Championship is the secondary singles belt. The
Television Championship is the singles title of the third most value.
The Hardcore Championship is the fourth singles title, and of no less value than the two previously mentioned. It is a specialty title for only the wrestlers that like to take it to the extreme. The hardcore division has quickly gained in prevalence over the past six months.
11.2 Tag Team Titles The best duo within NWWA get the chance to wear these valuable titles. In a day in time where there are fewer tag teams than ever, the
NWWA tag team division has been one of the more successful in that it is more highly populated than the tag team division of most leagues. The tag team titles of the NWWA are considered one of the more valuable sets of tag team titles.
12. How many supercards does NWWA hold? The NWWA regularly holds one supercard every month. This is not always the case though, as there was no supercard during the months of October or November of 1998, and there were two supercards during back to back weeks in February of 1999. What are the main rules of NWWA? 13.1 Roleplaying Your top priority as a roleplayer in the NWWA is to provide a flash for the weekly flash show, Prime Time. This will be the main criteria by which matches will be decided on the upcoming card. Anyone who fails to produce flashes regularly, or remains silent for long periods of time, will be either jobbed or ejected from the league without warning.
Keep your swearing to a minimum. Wrestlers are _NOT_ permitted to physically attack any and all officials, ring announcers, television announcers or interview personalities _UNLESS_ you receive permission. Ron Donovan is the exception to this rule.
Some important actions that you can take to make your flashes better include using spell check and changing settings for your interviews from week to week. Also remember that your flashes are not only judged by how well you can flame. Character originality, and how much heat your wrestler can produce are also major criteria by which they're judged.
For a Guide to Roleplaying, e-mail [detail withheld] . 13.2 Strategies or Gameplans Strategies, or strats for short, are needed in order to win matches. It's up to you which kind of strategy you send in, a long drawn out one or a simple paragraph or two. "I wanna win, 'cause it'd be cool." is
_NOT_ a strategy. Focus on your opponent's weakness, and explain how your wrestler plans to wrestle against them. Failure to send in strats may very likely lead to your wrestler being jobbed. A strategy/gameplan form is included below for you to fill out. Fill one out each time your wrestler has a match and send it to
[detail withheld] by the given due date. Wrestler's Name: Match: Entrance: (Include Barry Nelson's ring introduction, commentary, appearance, just as it will appear on the card, etc.)
Match Plan: (Give me an overall strategy of what you want to do in the match.) Winning/Losing Situation: (What your character will do in each) For a Guide to Strat Writing, e-mail [detail withheld] . 13.3 Angles The mark of a good league is how well it's storylines and angles are thought out. Angles are what determines in most cases how and what matches are scheduled for the week. Flashes and strategies are dependant
on good angles, otherwise proposed attacks may not be run unless they're for a specific purpose. Angle ideas from roleplayers are encouraged.
13.4 Match Booking The roleplayers have the greatest say in who they want to have rivalries with. The president schedules matches based upon the current rivalries in the organization, and for the purpose of building further angles. Sending in challenges to other wrestlers are looked favorably on, and in most cases will be scheduled whenever possible. Again angles come into play, and those challenges that seem to "make sense" will be given preference in schedule dating. Title matches usually are given to those who prove themselves in past matches, and simply asking for a title
