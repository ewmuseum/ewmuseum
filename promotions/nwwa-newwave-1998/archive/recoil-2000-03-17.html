That belt is for mid-carder losers that can't handle the big time. It's as simple as that. I won't even take on a match for that belt. The simple fact of the matter is, Stitt can't get to that level because he doesn't have the ability, that's all there is to it. He can't shine under the bright lights, if you catch my drift.
