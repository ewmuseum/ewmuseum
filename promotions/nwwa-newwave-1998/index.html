Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

This promotion opened its doors on the loyalty of a rival league's laid-off roster, its own owner turning a wave of unemployed wrestlers into a second act barely two weeks after his first promotion folded beneath him.

Its own house history reads like a chain of palace coups: a heel stable seizing power by sheer brutality, a rival faction rising to challenge it, a returning commissioner reclaiming authority his own contract had promised him, and a masked impostor unmasking as the very challenger who had just lost control of the league. Each new era renamed the balance of power without ever closing the promotion's own weekly schedule.

A touring Sunday supercard carried the promotion across a wide swath of the middle of the country, while a Thursday flash show kept the week's storylines moving in between. A named tag division, a hardcore title contested in steel cages and buried-alive stipulations, and a recurring cast of interviewers and ring announcers gave the promotion a settled identity even as the office chair itself changed hands again and again.

By the spring of its second year, an old anniversary card had grown into a sequel bearing its own name, a returning banished star had come home to needle the front office, and the promotion's own owner remained, by his own account, unhappy with where his league now stood. No closing notice survives on the record; the promotion's own weekly schedule was still running when the surviving documents end.
