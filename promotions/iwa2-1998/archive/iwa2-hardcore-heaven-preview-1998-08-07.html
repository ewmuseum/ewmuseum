IWA Hardcore Heaven Preview

7 August 1998
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
I know what you thinking. "Yeah, right! Another guy who thinks his E-Fed is the best there has ever been! What ever!!!" Well, before you delete this message and go on to the next one, you might want to take a second to check out the card for the latest IWA Pay-Per-View ("Hardcore Heaven:) this weekend: : -------------------------------------------------- : Retribution vs. Havoc (Triple Table Match - The winner is the first man to stack all three tables, and crush his opponent through them!) : The Dirty Doggs vs. "Elegant" Emerson Phillips & Homie the Clown (First Blood Match - The first particpant to start bleeding is the loser. - The Loser gets his IWA contract revoked and must leave the IWA!) : Ben "The Breaker" Johnson vs. AWOL (Final Confrontation! - The loser of the match is covered in tar and feathers and then suspended in a 4' x 4' steel cage for the remainder of the PPV so that the IWA fans can heckle him and throw things at him.) : Dan Sweeney vs. Ice Cube (Freezer Match - Both men are locked in a 20' x 20' Meat Locker! The winner is the first man to find the key (Which is hidden in one of the big pieces of raw meat.) and unlock the door and walk out. Hopefully before they both freeze!) : The Scarecrow vs. Dickie Wreenkle (Bar Room Brawl - A general melee at the Anheuser Busch brewing plant located in St. Louis (The location of the PPV.). Anything goes. Last man standing is the winner!) : Gut's n Glory vs. Menace 2 Society (Stairway to Heaven Match - Stips are this: tables surrounding the ringside area, some double stacked, triple stacked or single stacked. Barbed wire hung from the ceiling across the ring(maybe 15 ft. above the ring). Different size ladders w/one that reaches the wire. You can climb the ladder to get the barbed wire and use it on your opponents. Winner declared by pinfall or submission only. No countouts, no DQ's.) : Bad Boy Marcus.T vs. Olyumpus vs. Apocalypse (TV Title Match - Television Melt Down! - The ring is filled with all sizes of TV sets. The TV Title is hung on a chain, 20 feet over the middle of the ring. The winner is the first man to stack up enough TV's to be able to climb up to the belt and grab it!) : (Extreme Title Match - 10 Man Pit Fight simulcast from Tijuana, Mexico - The rules are as follows. All 10 men enter a 30 x 30 foot pit that is dug 10 feet down into the Mexican dirt. On-lookers circle the pit and cheer and jeer the participants. Anything goes! The participants can bring any weopon into the pit (Except for obvious things like a gun, etc...). The Mexicans watching the fit can also throw things into the pit for the people to use. Each man in eliminated when he is either knocked unconsious or it other wise to incapacitated to continue. Last man standing is the new IWA Extreme Champion!) The particpants, in no particular order, are: : Elak : Rod "The Bod" Sterling : Neptune : Crazy Man Crasher : Eric Ray Vaughn : Ricky Showers : Cyrus : The Unknown Solider : Chris Kameiko : En Fuego : Tha Dogg Pound vs. Think Tank or Two Dudes with Attitudes (Depends on Wednesdays winner) (Tag Team Title Match - Lumberjack Match - The ring is surrounded by 20 of the toughest son's of bitches from the local biker hang-out. If one of the participants gets knocked out of the ring, these 20 guys will beat the living snot out of him, and then roll him back in the ring. The winners are the IWA Tag Team champs!) : Mike Haggar vs. Rav4 (IC Title Match - Electrified Barbed Wire Match - The ring ropes have been removed and replaced with razor sharp barbed wire. A high voltage electric current is being run through the barbed wire! Only way to win is by making the other man submit.) : Beld Painkiller vs. Jason Bagwell vs. Derek Tuttle vs. Halo (World Title Match - Fatal Four Way in a Sealed Cage) : -------------------------------------------------- The PPV, like ALL the cards in the IWA (We have two a week and one PPV a month.), are WRITTEN UP IN THEIR ENTIRETY!!! The IWA has been around since October of 1997 and is the NUMBER 1 RATED E-FED ON THE NET, ACCORDING TO WEB SIDE STORY! (If you don't trust me, just click on our WWS link on the IWA home page. The stat's are up there for everyone to see!). The IWA only accepts ORIGINIAL wrestlers and handlers who are only willing to role play 100%. So if you can't handle that, go ahead and quit reading now. The IWA isn't for you. If your still reading, go to the IWA and check it out at:
HTTP://www.geocities.com/~iwa2/ Thanks for your time, and come back Sunday night to see the results of the latest PPV! Sincerely, IWA Prez
