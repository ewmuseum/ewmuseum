A GeoCities-hosted e-federation, self-dated to October 1997, whose own August 1998 pay-per-view preview named a roster and title slate independently corroborated the following month by a rival promotion's own crossover special. Written by AI, 2026

Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

IWA opened, by its own account, in October 1997, out of a GeoCities account it advertised at geocities.com/~iwa2/. Its June 1998 recruitment notice made its own pitch in one breath: “the #1 E fed According to Webside Story... With over a thousand hits a day... 40 members, 2 cards a week, Well written ALWAYS on time... and one of the best Pay per Views every month.”

Its 7 August 1998 preview for the Hardcore Heaven pay-per-view names nine gimmick matches run for keeps: a Triple Table Match, a First Blood Match whose loser had his contract revoked and was made to leave, a Freezer Match fought inside a locked meat locker, a ten-man Extreme Title pit fight dug ten feet into the ground and simulcast from Tijuana, an Electrified Barbed Wire Intercontinental title match, and a World Title Fatal Four Way fought inside a sealed cage. The same post claims two cards a week and one pay-per-view a month, “WRITTEN UP IN THEIR ENTIRETY,” and restates its own October 1997 founding date and its Web Side Story ranking claim word for word.

How it ended isn't in the surviving record. The last trace of it is external: the following month, a rival promotion's own crossover special billed several of the wrestlers named in that August preview under this fed's own tag, confirming it was still running in September 1998. Nothing dated after that survives.
