JPA Press Conference, 2001.3.16, Pre-Show: Shinji Nakata

16 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[The camera fades into a shot of a table cluttered with microphones against a black backdrop featuring the Nakata clan emblem in red. The camera zooms out and pans slowly to the right, and we see Shinji Nakata calmly walking to the chair seated behind the array of microphones. Wearing his familiar red and black Adidas tracksuit, Nakata politely bows to the media representatives before sitting down.] Nakata: Thank you all for attending. I realize that many of you have busy schedules to deal with, so I'll attempt to make this as brief as possible. The main reason I've invited you here today is because I want to be the first to publicly declare myself as accepting Ryo Inoue's open challenge to face off against himself and his TAP cohorts Eiji Yatsu and Kangyo in a six-man tag match Ryogoku Sumo Hall on March 24, 2001. [Flashbulbs go off, and reporters begin to clamor for Nakata's attention. Nakata holds up a finger as if to say that he isn't finished with his initial statement, and the press halts their barrage.] Nakata: I realize that, with a few exceptions, up to this point I have been careful not to ignite a war of words between Inoue and I. However, my hesitance was more out of a curiosity as to whether Inoue would soon cease his tantrum over being outclassed and beaten by a practitioner of Strong Style Puroresu, namely Masashi "MASSIVE" Akita rather than any fear of stepping on any toes. As the events of these last few weeks have unfolded, Inoue has not only continued to act like a spoiled brat, but increase his verbal attacks on the sport in which he seeks to earn his livelihood. This has left me no other option but to stand up for my heritage, my father's legacy. And on March 24th, I aim to make Inoue put his money where his mouth is, so to speak. Questions? [a reporter raises his hand, and Nakata acknowledges him with a nod.] Reporter: Nakata-san, do you have any idea if anyone else in the JPA is ready to accept Inoue's challenge? Nakata: At this point, I do not. That's not to say that I don't expect anyone else to rise up soon...to be honest, I'm fully expecting Rick Taylor to accept this challenge as well. [another reporter gets his attention, Nakata again answers with a stoic tilt of the head.] Reporter: Do you intend to show up at the next TATAMI card to force the issue? Nakata: The thought has not even crossed my mind at this point. But if I do attend, it will be as a paying customer to scout an opponent, not to cause trouble as Inoue's contemporaries attempted at the last Battle START! taping. I will act in a manner becoming of a professional athlete and not a schoolyard bully. One more question. [looking out among the many shouting reporters, Nakata points to one.] Reporter: Are there any words you'd like to say to Ryo Inoue, knowing that he'll be able to hear this? Nakata: Hai -- Inoue, you talk a big game. But can you follow up and prove that your angry outbursts to be anything more than idle threats? You claim that you are trying to give the people what they want, but where are all these people? Certainly not attending your TATAMI cards. Soon you will realize that the legacy of Strong Style Puroresu is bigger than your "Guze", indeed bigger than you, myself, or any one individual. And I intend to be the one to bring you to this realization, as I defeat you in the name of my father, my peers, and all those who have come before me and wrestled in the Strong Style Tradition...
