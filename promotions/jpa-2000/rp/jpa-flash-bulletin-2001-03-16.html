JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.16

16 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.16 1) MASSIVE Akita 2) Shinji Nakata 3) Kenji Okano 4) Corey Hart 5) Iron Chef Keiji Kobe 6) Tasogare no Prince 7) Kathakano ---- [Outside a restaurant in Fukuoka. Nothing too fancy, but more than a typical soba stand. Emerging from the building is one Masashi Akita, more professionally known as MASSIVE Akita in the wrestling world. It just so happens that he is also the current JPA Double Titles champion.] [As he walks out, as is typical for these sorts of things, a young man with a tape recorder comes up to the champion. Akita-sama happens to be wearing a navy blue suit at the moment, and a wide pair of sunglasses, the kind you typically see police officers wear. The young reporter (we can only assume) looks a bit more unkempt, but is seemingly enthusiastic…] REPORTER: Akita-sama! Akita-sama!! AKITA: Hai? REPORTER: Kotsuke Araya, Baseball Magazine Sha! If it pleases you.. AKITA: Go on, please. ARAYA: How goes it between yourself and Tokaio-san? AKITA: It goes well. Tokaio-san and myself have not teamed much prior to this series, however I cannot complain. It is always a learning process, even at my age. I am satisfied with our performances together. ARAYA: Hai, and this Friday at Star Lanes in Hakata, you two are teamed once more, against a more experienced team, this one with your old adversary Jack Shaw. AKITA: Yes. ARAYA: Your feelings? AKITA: What can I say that has not been said before between myself and Shaw? Since perhaps before you were born, the two of us have locked up. Anything I say now, before we face again for the Double Titles on the 24th, will be wasted breath… ARAYA: And of Rick Taylor? AKITA: Ah. There is a fascinating topic indeed. Taylor…he bested me once, last tour… ARAYA: Hai. AKITA: He will be a dangerous person in the years to come. However, I also know of his weakness… ARAYA: And that is? AKITA: No, no, you misunderstand me. Not that he specifically has a particular one … just that he is weakened, or so it seems, at the moment. After his bout with Inoue, where both men had to be dragged away from the ring…ee. Taylor may yet be feeling the effects at Star Lanes. ARAYA: You wouldn't take advantage of a weaker opponent, would you Akita-sama? AKITA: Hah. Is that not what we do? We find weaknesses, and use them to our advantage? ARAYA: Well…uh… AKITA: Tokaio and myself will be prepared for Shaw and Taylor at Star Lanes, of that there is no question… ARAYA: Here's something that there is a question about: Super Tengu. AKITA: Ah. ARAYA: He has mentioned perhaps teaming with you? AKITA: Perhaps. I knew his father, Masami Matsumoto, and admired him. His death in America, lo those many years ago, was a shock to all of us. I was but a young trainee, or no better, at that time. We did mourn many times that day. ARAYA: I see. AKITA: If Yamato-san declares it possible, I would be honored to team with the son of Masami Matsumoto. ARAYA: And what of Ryo Inoue? AKITA: What of him? He has his path, and he chooses to walk down it. Others are opposed. Such as it has always been. I will say this: he seeks to prove himself worthy of my respect. I have respect for him. It is his respect for me, and the JPA, that is in doubt. Remember - he challenged me following Coronation. And it was he that withdrew the challenge. Should he be ready again…we shall meet then. No sooner. ARAYA: I…see. Well, domo Akita-sama! AKITA: Ee. Dozo yoroshiku. [With that, Akita-sama crosses the street, leaving the reporter with a wistful grin about his face, fresh with another exclusive from a living legend….] ---- (The camera fades into a shot of Shinji Nakata walking into his locker room. Shinji is wearing a black half zipped windbreaker jacket, which is not zipped, with the Nagata family emblem on the back on the back.Under that he has on a black t-shirt. He is also wearing a pair of black windbreaker tear-away pants with a red stripe that goes right down the side of the legs and a pair of white adidas running shoes. His hair is dripping wet. There is also a tired look on his face to go along with his beaten appearance. The hall walls are white with a dark forest green trim along the doors and around the bottom of the walls right above the floor. The doors are a dark forest green, like the border, with a silver door knob. On the door is a white sign that says "Shinji Nakata" in black bold letters. He opens the door and walks in. The camera follows him into his personal locker room. He takes off his windbreaker and tosses it into the corner. He now just has on just a black t-shirt that is sweat soaked around the front middle and the back middle. There are also two big sweat stains under both of his arms. The color is locker room is nearly the exact same as it is in the hallway the only difference is the hallway has marble floor. Shinji takes a seat in a big leather chair that is located in the south corner of the room. As always, the camera follows him.) Shinji: Training consists of many virtues. Will power, physical strength, mental toughness to block out the pain, and pride. I train and work harder than any one in this organization because I want to be at peak shape before every match that I participate in. Also because I want it more than anyone else. I want the gold, I want the respect of not only the fans but the other wrestlers as well. I have to live up to a reputation that my father made for the family name back in the late sixties and early seventies. His legacy is my destiny. For I will someday become what my father always wanted me to be, the best of the best. No matter what it takes I will lay it all out in the ring. Push my limits, push my partner's limits and end up victorious at any cost. A way to get respect and victory is to be trusted by those who choose to fight along side you in the little battles. Masanobu Akazi and Toshiaki Mizuno, both of you can be assured that I have both of your backs and that you two can trust me. No matter what the situation, you can rely on me to be there in our match with Tasogare no Prince, Kathakano and Ketiak. Which leads me to our opponents. Tasogare, I am sure many remember our meeting back in the days of Neo Japan. I have much respect for you and your ability in the ring. Without a shadow of a doubt in my mind you are on of the best in the world today. There is no question that whenever you and I hook up it will be a game of "can you top this?". This upcoming match will be another chance for everybody to see us go at it again. I am looking forward to March 16th at the Fukuoka Hakata Star Lanes because I know it will be a night to remember. For us and everybody else involved in the JPA. For I wish you good luck. Kathakano and Ketiak, I wish you two good luck as well and I look forward to wrestling you two the 16th. My fathers legacy will forever live on. For I will carry the torch of the eternal flame of Nakata pride which burns in my soul as strong as it always has and as it always will. March 16th Fukuoka Kakata Star Lanes will be a night to remember in the legacy of the Nakata name. (Shinji stands up picks up his jacket and then walks out of the room. The camera fades out to a shot of the Nakata Family Emblem which is hanging in a frame from the east wall of the locker room.) ---- (The camera fades into a shot of Kenji Okano sitting in his car, a black 2000 Chevy Monte Carlo, with a big smile on his face.) Kenji: C'mon boys, let's have a good time. (Kenji gets out of his car and walks across the street. You can see the sign of the local pub ahead. "The KO" is flashing about 12 feet from ground level in green neon florescent light. Kenji is wearing a pair of normal boot cut jeans with a pair of black army boots. He has a black leather jacket on, under which he is wearing his familiar red and gold Nagoya Grampus Eight soccer jersey. He is also wearing a gold chain necklace with a gold pendant that is the number 5, his old soccer number. He gets ready to walk in and looks up.) Kenji: They named this place after me. Camera man: No they didn't. Kenji: Seriously, they did. (Kenji opens the door to the club and walks in. While walking through you can see pictures of other sports figures from around the area and the country. Kenji stops and points to a picture on the wall.) Kenji: Handsome devil, if I may say so myself. (The camera gets a close up of the picture which is him back in his old soccer days. He is wearing a jersey much like the one he is currently wearing. The picture is of him running down field while looking at the ball. The picture you can tell was from a while back because he looks younger in the face and he doesn't have any facial hair. He walks into the main bar. You see people in the far corner playing pool and others in the near view watching games on TV.) Kenji: Hey boys! Most of the bar: HEY! Kenji: (turning towards the camera) See, they love me here. (He walks up to the bar and sits down in a stool.) Kenji: There has been many an hour of mine spent here just wasting time, having fun, having a few drinks, and telling stories about the "good old days". Jimmy! (There is a man about the age of 50 with black hair which seems to be turning white in a few areas. He is wearing a white short sleeved button up shirt with an apron over it.) Kenji: Jimmy my man. Now this is the best bartender in the world. Jimmy Togawa, no relation to that loser Jinshi Togawa, say hello to the world. Jimmy: Hello! What can I get for you Kenji? Kenji: I'm having the urge for a plain old beer. None of that Malt-Ale bullcrap either. (Jimmy reaches down below the main level of the bar and pulls out the classic bottle which can only be defined as "Budweiser") Kenji: Now we're talking. (looking towards the camera man) What do you want? Oh nevermind you don't have enough hair on your balls to drink yet do you? Don't worry you'll be there someday. So what do you want to talk about? Camera man: Well, they wanted me to get some thoughts of yours about your upcoming match with Corey Hart this upcoming Friday. (Kenji opens his beer and takes a sip. Then shakes his head as if to say he was a moron for requesting such.) Kenji: Man-o-man, I'll tell you what, I'm scared to death of this Corey Hart guy. When his music hits, I'm so afraid that I nearly piss myself. 'Cos those screeching sounds of Mariah Carey's voice just make me want to go and run to my mommy. Corey, I have a somewhat brief message for you. You have messed with the wrong guy. I will take you out and make you cry like a baby in front of the whole world. Go ahead take your vitamins, drink your milk, and say your prayers, brother, because the only chance of you beating me is if they disqualify me for beating you so bad. You're nothing but a sissy boy, never will be, American, who was probably such a laughing stock in America that you decided to come over here to the JPA, where the big dogs come out and pounce, hoping you would escape from all the jokes and cracks you took. But as soon as you stepped foot in this country, in the arena of your first match to be exact, you were made fun of all over again. You are a never will be who will never cut it over here. So go back to the land of the free home of the brave and take some ballet courses or something. And when I send you packing, because I beat you so bad that it embarrassed you in front of the world, promise me you will never disgrace a wrestling card with your name ever again. Some guys in the corner playing pool: Kenji! Get your ass over here and play some pool. Kenji: (to the guys in the corner playing pool) When I'm ready, baka! (to the camera) Corey Hart, never forget what I just said, okay? Well, I got more important things to do now, is that enough? Camera man: Sure, that's plenty. Kenji: Okay...get the hell out of here! (Kenji points towards the door. The Camera man backs up. Kenji stands up walks over to the guys playing pool and gives them fives. The camera backs all the way out of the club. The scene fades out to a shot of the club sign, the KO.) ---- ([The scene opens at a small, deserted gym in downtown Tokyo. The camera pans the darkened room, catching glimpses of expensive, exercise equipment and posters of famous athletes, littering the walls. Seated at a bench is a lone, male figure, his head bowed. Upon closer inspection, the mysterious, young man is revealed as "Captivating" Corey Hart. He wears a white, tank top and red shorts, completing the look with Asics, tennis shoes. He begins to wrap white tape around his hands and wrists as he addresses the camera.) Corey: I haven't been in this sport very longā€¦a little less than a year, to be exact. In that small amount of time, I've wrestled a number of opponents, all talented in their own right. And I've looked forward to facing each of them, using both victories and defeats as learning experiences. I've never taken those matches personally or allowed my feelings to get involvedā€¦until now. (Corey suddenly looks up and into the camera. Instead of the usual grin or smile, his face is a somber mask.) Corey: Kenji, I listened to your last interview and found myself agreeing with some of what you said. Surprised, huh? You said that you're tired of hearing my constant "whining and complaining" about your racist put-downs and inappropriate behavior. Well, I'm tired of hearing myself talk too. In fact, I promise that I won't waste your time with another promo talking about how wrong you are to discount my abilities and write me off as some talentless loser. Instead, I'll *show* you how I feel in the ring. You see, the time for talk has long passed. For months, I've tried to reason with you, using words. Now, it's time to use my fists and my feet, the only language that you truly seem to comprehend! I just hope you can take it. Good luck on the 16th...you'll need it. (With that, the scene fades to black.) ---- (The camera opens up onto the kitchen of the Iron chef. A pot rack hangs above a eight burner stove, with a huge air filter system above it. One the stove are four pots and a double boiler. The Iron Chef is decked out in what he wears to the ring, and orange chef's coat and a tall orange hat. Kobe: Espagnole... (The shot shows the first pot, full of a dull, sauce the color of rich excrement, but with a smooth consistency.) Veloute... (The shot now shows a second pot with a ivory yellow sauce with little black bits of pepper floating in it.) Hollandaise... (The shot shows a yellow sauce sitting a double boiler, a wet towel sits between the top and bottom section allowing for one handed whisking stability.) Tomato... (The shot cuts to another pot full of a pink, mess, like when your father tried to cook but ended eating take out as a child.) Bechemel (The shot shows a final pot with a white sauce in it, with a nice constancy, but slightly thinner than the others.) Kobe: Every classically trained chef knows these words like a mantra. They are the five mother sauces, the basis for all sauces in French cooking it is said. You might say they are the "Pure Sauce." Here is the problem, if you serve some one Bechemel on there food, they will pronounce it flavorless, colorless, and bland. And they will be right! It needs a flavor added to it before it can be eaten. (Kobe adds in some ementhaler cheese shreds, Guyre cheese, and dry mustard producing a morney sauce, suddenly the sauce is the right thickness.) Kobe: The same is true of Veloute, it needs a modifier before it becomes palatable. (The Iron Chef adds corn, green onion, cilantro, diced chicken and southwest seasoning, then dumps the thick sauce into a dish to then cover with cornbread batter and toss into a convection oven) Kobe: If you walk among the hip Bistros of New York, the great restaurants of France, or into the kitchen of my fellow Iron Chef Saki you won't ever see an Espagnole sauce, for we found a better way to make a rich sauce of beef and veal stock with Demi-glacé. (Kobe pulls another pot onto camera already at the boiling point, he adds a slurry of cornstarch and water to thicken it in an instant before adding sauté mushrooms and shallots.) Kobe: Similarly, one finds a drastically altered form of the classical tomato sauce, you will never find one thickened with roux as was the practice in olden times. (The shot shows Kobe pouring a more common Bollanesse, ie a tomato sauce with a mixture of veal, lamb, pork, and other seasoning, over polenta slices and topping with fontina before tossing this too into the convection oven.) Kobe Classically, Hollandaise lacked red pepper, but you will not find a recipe that does not call for it now. (The Iron chef adds from red pepper and some Tarragon to the sauce, leaving little red specks in the sauce and green bits of herb. Kobe now begins making creps as he talks) Kobe: In the time of Shogun, they fought with spears and arrows, and swords. Today we have guns, and tanks and planes to battle with. The rifleman has replaced the swordsman, the tank replaced the horse. Even the great fencer Miyamoto Musashi saw the age of the swordsman was drawing to a close in his classic, the Book of Five Rings, but you Eiji Yatsu seem clueless of this fact. New tools replace the old tools of war. New style replace the old styles of fighting. (Kobe pours up another creps as he continues to speak) Kobe: Times change... tastes change... People want lighter flavor, greater speed in dishes. The old stand bys no longer can deal with demand. So a Demi based sauce replaces the doughy Espagnole sauce. New sauces like mango salsas, red pepper pestos, and compound butters become the standard. Life goes on, the only constant is change, innovation takes precedent over tradition, and cooking evolves to take the challenge. So it is too with fighting. People want greater skill, fresh moves, greater speed. And while dinosaurs like Shaw-san and Oodora might be nice for nostalgia, the cutting edge of this sport is found among the Luchadores. We bring new faces to the arena, and keep them coming back. (The Iron Chef places two more creps under a warm towel, and makes a set of the thin French specialty.) Kobe: You claim that you that shooto is best, Yatsu, because it has been practiced by Samurai? It was to my understand they practiced something called that beard a little closer resemblance to Kendo than shooto, and had an honor system called Bushido while you have none, Yatsu. But perhaps you are right and the history books are wrong, where are these great fighters now if their fight you champion as there own was so strong? That's right! They are in the museums with the Dinosaur bones and other relics with too little meaning to be a part of our everyday lives. Look into your own world of shooto. There isn't just Judo or Jujitsu, there is Hap Ki Do, Jeet Kune Do, Capoeira, Sambo, American Kempo, Savte, Krav Maga, and sky full of fighting styles born outside of this tiny island I make my home. Each style preaches it is the true fight, and that if you practice it's tenants you will need not fear any man. (Another series of creps are prepared as Kobe speaks.) Kobe: But each system is flawed by the limits it imposes. A style will say "we focus on grappling," or "We are the best punching system," or "No one matches our kicking." But if all you know is how to grapple like a Judo man, will that be enough to defeat a Tae Kwon Do man? If all you can do is strike will you be able to subdue a wrestler? No, you must change, and that is what is at the core of Lucha Liebre, or Free Fight in our language. We are not Mexican Jumping beans, leaping in colorful outfits for the amusement of children. Luchadores have strong strikes too, as any man who has felt my Kenta kick can tell you. Luchadores have submissions, as any one who's been in my Medio Cangrejo or Quebradora Lagunera can tell you. Even if your style embraces change, it can't compare with Lucha because it IS CHANGE ITSELF. What ever is need to win the fight, we do in Lucha, and make no apologies for it, because it is the free fight. Like water, it flows, taking different forms in the face of different conditions. If you freeze water, it becomes hard and stiff, like rock. If you pour it out a room temperature it will wear down things, nut just pebbles in streams, but it will wear out canyons, create caves, and from lakes. But if you heat water, I takes to air as steam, were it can over come the heat limits imposed by physics of liquids and get hotter than any other cooking method. (Kobe begins working on some asparagus and other vegetables on a prep table.) Kobe: Adaptability, change, that is what puts Lucha on the for front. The Lucha of today is different from that of the 50's, or 70's, or 80's. We can be hard and stiff with our strikes like shooto. We can entangle and wear men down with our submissions like strong style grappler. But no one can take to air like us, defying logic and gravity to land with more impact than you can comprehend. Ryoku, Oodora, you think Lucha is a bunch of masked men bouncing like ping pong balls. No, we are wrecking balls! Balls of Iron that are going to smash down the great temple of Strong Style to it's foundation. We are cannon balls smashing into the field of battle. And if you write us off as a comedy act, something to amuse children (Kobe tosses some vegetables to be blanched in boiling water while he talks.) Kobe: Maybe you had a false perception of Lucha, Oodora and Yatsu, well let me assure you that when you come face to face with the real Lucha Leibre, but you will this weekend. You'll be facing myself and We Hyun Bae in a tag match, the Luchadore specialty. We will run circles around you while showing you why our style is to be feared. Yatsu, while you fell to no Prince I took him to his very limit when he was at 100% and I was nursing the usual tour ending soreness, stiffness, and exhaustion. I took Ryo Inuoe to a time limit draw, too. That's right, a small Luchadore took the great striker twice his size to a draw, what do you think I'd do a man my own size, Yatsu? I'd teach you meaning of respect, I'd beat you handily because your shooto is WEAK, just like Inoue, just like all shoot. You're a sheep, a lamb being being lead to the slaughter. An army of sheep is nothing to a man who knows how to butcher, tell that to Inoue next time he speaks of his army. It is too bad you are just Ryo Inoue's sheep Yatsu, because the cowardly shepherd Inoue would never allow you to be delivered into my hands. (Kobe removes the vegetables form the water, they are tender, but still firm and fresh, possessing a brilliant green color. Half of the asparagus he covers in a serving dish, the other half he begins wrapping first ham, then a crep and lays the package on a dish in a pattern like spokes on a wheel.) Kobe: And Oodora, don't believe Ryoku when he says he had and off day, that he was confused by new moves I used. I beat him at the top of his game, and our battle will be even more one sided, because now I have We Hyun Bae at my side. A man who I trust, a man I'm experienced with, I who I know the limits of because now I've wrestled him. For sixteen minutes of pure hell, Bae gave me all he had trying to beat me. I did the same, it was the ultimate sign of respect. Now that we fought, I know his fierceness and drive, and Ryoku's pales in comparison. I promise all my fans, that you will see Lucha Liebre at it's best. Not the watered down Lucha Yatsu spoke of knowing. Not the mongrel verity of no Prince. The real true Lucha Leibre of El Santo, Blue Demon, Mysterio, La Parka, Dr. Wagner Jr., and my friend the Iron Chef. It's time all of Japan got a glimpse of what Mexico already knows, Lucha Liebre is the supreme style in all the world. (Kobe pours his morney sauce over the creps then tosses on some parsley and cherry tomatoes while talking.) Kobe: Yatsu, you think your shooto is so strong? If you find the courage, I'll be waiting for your challenge. Come, try a bit of the true flavor of Iron Chef. Come and see why I'm called the best Middle weight in the world. Prince, they Russians say revenge is a dish best served cold, but my kitchen is always open, any time you want a second helping, I am ready. Oodora... Ryoku... let us now see just how strong your style is in the face of two wrecking balls of iron. (The camera pans over the table of food. The once bland Béchamel is a flavorful morney sauce on light and airy creps with ham and asparagus. The Veloute has been transformed into a Southwest Chicken pot pie. Crown roast of prime rid is to one side, and even a vegan would want some with the mushroom Demi sauce. The other asparagus is uncovered and topped with the tarragon Hollandaise, and a hearty polenta lasagna topped with a tomato Bollanesse rounds out the meal. As a whole, the meal is both classic and modern, well traveled, well seasoned, and utterly delicious looking. Those who question the Iron Chef's abilities in the kitchen must be silenced now. It is hard to criticize when your mouth is watering.) ---- [The camera sweeps over the rolling hills covered with waving grass. In the distance, the sun sinks below the horizon, streaking the darkening sky overhead with purple, pink and orange highlights. Standing on a small rise amongst the waist high grass, admiring the Twilight sky is Tasogare no Prince -- the Prince of Twilight. In his hand, he holds a solitary white orchid, which he holds up to his slightly upturned nose to partake of its fragrance. The breeze blows all around him, causing the open edges of his long purple coat to flutter and his long, silvery hair to flow all about his head and shoulders, almost as if it were floating upon the surface of a body of water rather than thin air. Slowly, he lowers the orchid from his pale face, opening his large, violet eyes and staring directly into the camera's unblinking lens...] It seems that with all the events taking place in and around the JPA, it is time for the Prince of Twilight to speak his mind upon them. Firstly, there are the recent appearances by Tetsuo Matsumoto. At first, I was intriqued, and gladdened by the presence of the self-proclaimed "Iron Man." His presence in the JPA led me to believe that I could entertain the hope that I could, at long last, have the opportunity to pit myself against him in the ring. Ever since we first encountered one another in SJPW, I have longed for such an opportunity. Needless to say, with all the rumors and discussion of his possible run here in JPA, those hopes have been rekindled. But, if Matsumoto has taken up with the likes of Alex RAPTOR, or whatever he is calling himself now, then perhaps his is not as worthy of my attention as I once believed. As for RAPTOR and his new found devotion to so-called "Shoot fighting," I can say it comes as no real surprise. He has never been able to rely upon pure wrestling ability to achieve any sort of victory, so the "anything goes" style of such an ungraceful form of athelticism is bound to appeal to the likes of him. [He pauses -- smelling the white flower he grasps in his hand...] It is pure wrestling ability, such as that possessed by myself, and my partners Kathakano and Ketiak, that will allow us to emerge triumphant this week against the team of Azaki, Nakata and Mizuno. Already our partnership has bore us a victory, and I see no reason to believe that it will not continue to do so. But it is not merely our skill that will insure that victory, but a far greater force. That being Destiny, of course. The three of you shall fall prey to Destiny, just as all others who enter into Twilight. Whether it be Azaki, Nakata and Mizuno, Alex RAPTOR, Tetsuo Matsumoto, or even you, Keiji Kobe, you all are helpless to fight it, or me. You will all fall to the Prince of Twilight, for that is your Destiny. Kore wa sakete toore me, anata no unmei -- "You cannot avoid your Destiny." Either one at a time, or as a group, all of you will enter into the Twilight, and all will leave it... [He takes a final whiff of the white orchid in his hand...] Forever changed... [As the Prince of Twilight continues to smell the flower in his hand, the camera pans over once again to the horizon, where the last of the sun sinks below the edge of the earth, plunging the world, and the scene, into darkness...] ---- [The scene fades into a park of statues. Each of these statues is marble in design, and boast the bust of demonic figurines in ferocious poses. On the base of one statue, a winged gargoyle, sits Kathakano, wearing leather pants and a long sleeve shirt that boasts a pentagram. His mask is present, obviously reverting back from the corpsepaint that he sometimes wears.] "My next venture shalt see me uniting with other forces of evil, Tasogare no Prince, and the vicious, bloodthirsty savage, Ketiak. I do not let my presence unite with other souls, but for this, I shall make an exception. I see many good qualities in my partners. Quickness, brutality, and a complete lack of compassion. Its almost as if our alliance will be comparable to a swift, deadly, blitzkrieg. A wave of unmitigated hatred being rained down onto those who oppose us. It is almost sad to see innocents slaughtered, alas, it was their decision. They decided to engage us in a battle, therefore, we shall decimate them." [Kathakano stands.] "This shall bring this to an end. I have nothing more to say, because the odds are too pitiful to even speak of again." [Kathakano leaves this place of statues, and as he leaves, a lightning bolt strikes, signalling a storm. We pan up to a statue of Jesus Christ. The statue cries a single tear of blood.
