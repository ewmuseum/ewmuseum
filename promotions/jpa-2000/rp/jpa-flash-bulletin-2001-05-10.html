JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.5.10

10 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.5.10 1) Keiji Kobe 2) Gustav Jaeger 3) Jason Ramsey ---- (The camera opens up onto the hall way leading to the locker rooms of Tokyo Differ Ariake after his match with Yatsu. He is wearing a towel over his shoulders, and his eye is looking a little puffy. As he walks to the back Mituso Masanobu, walks up to Kobe with a microphone in hand.) Masanobu: Kobe-san, could I get a word with you? Kobe: Of course, I always have time for you. Masanobu: I have to say I'm a little shocked, seeing you with out the mask. Kobe: (Rubbing his head as if to get used to the feeling) You aren't the only one. Masanobu: What brought this on? Kobe: It was time. Masanobu: It was time? Kobe: I lived under the hood for 5 years. Five years of keeping my identify a secret. In that time the Iron Chef went onto become recognized as one of the top wrestlers in all of Mexico, his reputation was no less well known here in Japan. But Keiji Kobe, he's still a mystery, the reporter who came up to Yatsu didn't even get my name right when talking about me. Anyhow, I decided to try this one on my own. So many of the competitors have turned to other's for help winning the Pan Asian title. If I'm going to win that belt, I want to know I did it myself, with out anyone's help or encouragement We've seen the Iron Chef roll over the competition, it is time to see how Kobe will fair. Masanobu: Not so well, you drew against Yatsu. Kobe: Not so well? How could you say a draw against Yatsu is not fairing well. The man is a machine. His conditioning is with out equal, the man trains like he is powered by plutonium. He works with the Steel Wind Dojo, one of the most respected fighting schools in Japan. He was a Collegiate Judo champion, and you say a draw against him is not doing well. When I last wrestled under my own name, I was setting up rings, and loosing to men well past their primes. Tonight, I faced off against a man in superb shape with superior training and discipline. Man to man and I went the distance. We threw everything we had at each other and after 30 minutes neither one of us was ready to quit. I'm not disappointed. I'm motivated, I can't wait to finish what we started tonight. Masanobu: So do you think you can beat Yatsu. Kobe: (With a coy smile) We'll see. Masanobu: You think you'll see Yatsu in the finals? Kobe: I enjoyed the challenge he presented, and the fans enjoyed the sporting contest we put on, but they want a conclusion. I want a conclusion, he wants one. Masanobu: But could you beat Yatsu? Kobe: It would be nice. Masanobu: I have to say, this is shocking departure from your usual self. Normally you only have unkind things to say in regards to TAP and you never are shy about your opinions. Kobe: I'm not shy now. Yatsu has shown great heart today and great insight. He saw what I was trying to do today. Masanobu: You mean confuse him with the strong style when he prepared for a Lucha attack Kobe: I see Yatsu seems to be the only one who saw what I was trying to do. When we entered that ring we said, let's cut through the name calling, the cheap shots, and the bickering. Let's get back to what this is all about, the sport. For 30 minutes we gave our all to the sport and to the fans. We issued a challenge to all the other wrestlers to reach our level. I'm proud of the work I did that day, and I'm proud that Yatsu was part of that work. He has what it a takes to be a great fighter.. a great champion in the making. Masanobu: And are you a great champion now, a man worthy to hold the Pan Asian title? Kobe: That's what I'm here to find out. Let's see if I can be the champion my other persona has been. Masanobu: Well you've put on one classic match with no Prince under the hood, are we going to see the rematch people have been asking for? Kobe: You will see great match no matter what. The Twilight Prince brings so much to the plate. So much speed, grace, and strength. All modesty aside, I'm no slouch either. I have the speed, the power, and spirit of my own. We match well, no Prince and I, in terms of size and background. What is more, we are both itching for another crack at one another. Masanobu: Yes, the Chef has stated he's already the champion, the best in the world. For his part no Prince has stated it is his destiny to be the first Pan Asian Champion. Kobe: Oh yes, it is his destiny to beat the Iron Chef. Well I wonder how you'll fair against me then? I won't make any allusions about my own greatness. I still have to prove myself worthy of the Pan Asian title, and to do that I've got to beat you, no Prince. You're fast, you're strong, but I'm hungry. The Iron Chef feasted on glory, but has only a few scraps tossed his way. You spoke of being above hate and emotion. You're never above emotion. You might of lost emotion, but that is no strength. Emotion is passion, it is strength. I wrestle with emotion, with passion. I wrestle with love of the sport and the fans, hatred of weakness and cowardice, and greed for my own glory. I'm an emotional wrestler, any one who has seen me wrestle knows this. You can call this passion a weakness, I know it to be my greatest strength, no Prince. I'm coming for you no Prince, and you had better believe I'll be ready. Masanobu: What about Fairchild and Okano. Kobe: Listen, no Prince is not a man you look past. Maybe I didn't make myself clear, this guy is one of the best in the world, and this is coming from a veteran of three nations experience. All my time, all my energy is going into this match. The Twilight Prince is always going to be a hard battle. But that is the point isn't it? To go out and show yourself to be the best against the best? That is what I'm out to do. I respect this guy as much as the next man, hell, more than the next guy for that matter, but I'm not going to say this is his victory. I'm coming into this one wanting to win, and it's going to come down to who want's it more in the end. I'm praying it's me. Masanobu: no Prince says he's moved beyond petty things like that. Kobe: Lip service, he wants the belt, and he wants the challenge I present. By the end of the night his heart will be racing, the adrenaline will be pumping, and the fans will be cheering. Masanobu: Will they be cheering you hand raised? Kobe: I'd like that. Masanobu: I'm sure you would, but will that happen? Kobe: If some one could see into the future, why would they wrestle? They could make more money writing for papers or playing the lotto. I'm a wrestler, plain and simple. I'm going out there with the intention of beating no Prince. The Prince is good, but so am I. I'm the one who was in the Heavy-weight picture, not him. I'm the one who's had the international experience. I've never backed down from a fight, and I've never failed to come prepared. I've been working towards a match with no Prince ever since we drew the first time out. As good as I was then, I'm even better now. I'm smarter, stronger, better. Masanobu: So you are predicting a win. Kobe: You'll have to buy a ticket if you want to see what happens. (Kobe walks off down the hall, whistling to himself, satisfied.) ---- (The camera fades in on a gym and judging from the grunts and snarls coming from off camera, a gym currently in use. Sure enough, when the camera pans around we see the massive form of Gustav Jaeger working out at the weight machine, pumping iron for all he's worth.) GJ: Massive Akita. The current JPA Double Title Champion. A true legend in the world of puroresu. (Gustav keeps working out as he talks and he's obviously been at this for quite awhile. His face is a bit flushed and sweat pours down his body but despite all that he seems exstatic. He has a big grin on his face and his eyes twinkle like a child on Christmas morning.) GJ: On the very next Battle Start, I get to face Massive Akita, one on one. Is it a match for the Double Titles? I am honestly not sure but it would not matter in any event. Regardless of wether this is a title match or not, I must give my absolute best effort, nothing less is appropriate for going into the ring against a legend like Massive Akita. In fact, I must give better then my best effort. (Gustav's face loses it's smile for a moment, as he remembers what happened at the last Battle Start.) GJ: Because at the last Battle Start, Herr Akita pinned me. It was a fair pin, I will admit that. I am not Ryo Inoue, I will not whine about being beaten fairly and I was beaten fairly. But in this match, things will be different. On the last Battle Start, Shinji Nakata got a legendary pinfall by defeating my tag team partner Jack Shaw. On the next Battle Start, I plan to equal his accomplishment by gaining a legendary victory of my own over Massive Akita. Perhaps I will become the Double Title Champion then, perhaps I will not, it does not matter. All that matters is that matter is that I bring give Massive Akita the best fight I can possibly give him und to defeat him if I possibly can. Is this possible? I belive that it is. Auf wiedersehn. (Gustav nods at the camera as he continues his workout and the camera fades to black.) ---- (Scene opens on the set of the popular Japanese wrestling show "Games Challenge R". The two hosts Nakato Asai and Satoro Motokata are seated at a news desk type podium and are both dressed like they are going to a rave after the show. They run through some of the top news stories of the week in puroresu including the results of the latest JPA cards. They then cut to a clip of Jason Ramsey pinning Jinshi Togawa with a Superdriver during the main event of the tour's opening night. As the clip concludes the hosts introduce Ramsey as their guest and he walks onto the set, dressed in a gray, double breasted Hugo Boss. Ramsey exchanges bows with both men and takes a seat in the interview area.) Asai: Welcome to Games Challenge R. We are honored to have you here. Ramsey: The honor is mine. Motokata: We are sure that the fans of JPA would like to hear about the effects of T.A.P. on the general psyche of the JPA during this tour. Ramsey: How did I know that you would open the interview with this topic? I have some things to say about T.A.P. and I will, but first I'd like to talk a little about the Juniors Struggle tournament. Asai: About Fairchild? Ramsey: Yes, Billy Fairchild and another young man who has recently started to impress me by the name of Corey Hart. Motokata: Corey Hart is teamed with Alliance members throughout the tour. You think, then, that he has improved as a fighter? Ramsey: Hart has the same spark to him that Fairchild has, the heart of a champion. He has shown nothing but fighting spirit ever since his debut in JPA and I see nothing but good things for his future. He and Fairchild are the future of this sport. I am glad to see that they decided to come and work in Japan as opposed to staying in the U.S. where wrestlers are subjected to the inane desires of corporate "entertainment". Asai: Would you like to make a prediction for the finals of the Juniors Struggle? Ramsey: Well, the field is full of great talent. Yatsu, Okano, Hart, Fairchild, they are all fantastic. However, Kobe and Tasogare no Prince are both considered the men to beat in this tournament. It is too close to call at this point though. Motokata: Yourself and Rick Taylor will be facing two new comers to JPA in VIOLENT Tsuchida and Super Genki Man at Korakuen Hall. How do you prepare for a match against such new talent at a historic venue such as Korakuen? Ramsey: Rick and I have watched hours of tape on both men and we travel together as well, so we have a lot of time to prepare strategies. Genki Man and Tsuchida are both very formidable foes, but Taylor and I are a machine when we tag together. His incredible power and ferocity combined with my technical ability and speed make us a powerful combination. We will simply use our talent and fighting spirit to overcome what ever they Tsuchida and Genki man throw at us. In the end we will be victorious. Asai: Later in the month you face Ryo Inoue in Ishikawa in singles competition. You have not faired well against Inoue in your past matches. Will this time be any different? Ramsey: Inoue is a man who I, for various reasons, dislike. Both professionally and personally, I have no use for the man. He is a bully, a prima donna, and a man without honor. He would publicly disgrace Yamoto-san, the man that pays him for his efforts on JPA cards, and claim that HE is the one who has suffered a slight? Inoue claims that his fighting form is the ultimate. I have stated on at least one other occasion that his bastardized fighting is a sloppily homogenized mix of the truly great systems of the past. Inoue has always prided himself on being the best there is on the mat. I will prove him wrong. Before I ever stepped foot in the rings of puroresu, I was a master of Collegiate, free-style, and Greco-Roman wrestling. I was an all american and was invited to the Olympic trials in wrestling. Inoue can scoff at this all he wants but one only needs remember what Dan Severn, a wrestler, did to Royce Gracie, a shoot fighter. I will shock the wrestling world in Ishikawa and will send a clear message to T.A.P. and the rest of the so-called "fighters" that would verbally attack JPA. Motokata: We are almost out of time, but before we go I would like to ask Ramsey-san to sign the "wall of champions" and meet our studio audience. (The studio crowd starts to cheer at this idea. Ramsey rises and follows both men to a white wall placed just off to the side of the news desk. They hand Ramsey a marker and he goes to the wall, looking for a place to sign. The entire "wall" is covered with autographs and small messages from several of the top names in puroresu including legends like Jack Shaw and MASSIVE Akita. Ramsey signs his name in english and Japanese then writes "Long life for puroresu, Puroresu for long life" in katakana characters. The crowd watching the close-up of the message on the monitors begins to cheer again. The credits begin to roll and the theme music plays as Ramsey follows Asai and Motokata into the crowd. He shakes hands, signs autographs, and has photos taken for almost everyone in the small audience, long after the cameras have stopped.) ---- Japan Puroresu Association http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/ --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
