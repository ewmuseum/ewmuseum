JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.1.17

17 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Just a reminder, the JPA's TV card, "Battle START!" debuts this Saturday, 2001.1.20!! Featured in the one-hour program will be 2 matches from the CORONATION tournament, as well as others, taped at JPA's tour opener on 1.13 at Tokyo Differ Ariake!! -- JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.1.17 1) Jack Shaw 2) Iron Chef Keiji Kobe 3) Billy Fairchild ---- (The huge form that is Jack Shaw is seen sitting down on a sunny day, outside somewhere, it could be at home in Abliene, maybe in Japan, perhaps England, either way, it is odd to see Jack outside rather than in a locker room.) Jack Shaw: Some times you win and you don't feel so good about it, other times you lose and it don't hurt as much. Life's funny that way. I know some of y'all don't want to see no old codger like me or Akita, while others seem to enjoy watching us old timers pound on each other. It sometimes crosses my mind that while the spirit is willing, the body just don't, can't keep up. Hell, I'm pushing 50 and still fighting hard, but the aches and pains sometimes get to me. (Smiling, another odd thing from the USMC vet, Shaw takes off his Grain Grower's cap in order to wipe some sweat from his brow.) Yet, I ain't hanging up my boots just yet. I still got plenty of fire left in this here old heart. It was like I was saying to this feller back in Angland, I may be just too stubborn or too stupid to quit, sort like a this ole bull I used to own. This bull was a big one, full of fire, piss, vinegar, and whatever else makes something onrey. Nonna my boys wanted to go in the pen with this monster, but I did. After I got out of the hospital, I knew there ain't no way I'm selling that bull to no rodeo or nuthin. It'd been a shame to let that bull become some sorta side show, so I kept him in this huge field, let him roam around and bust up whatever. Even when he became old he still had fire in his eyes and heart, and I sorta wished I'd be like him when I got to be that age. And wouldn't you know it, I sorta did become like him, still being the tough ole bastard who'll knock you out just as often as say hello to ya. (His huge hands rubbing together, Shaw looks directly at the camera.) It sure does take alot outta this old man to fly around, from here in Japan to the US, then to Angland, but I do it, because I love this Gawddam sport. I just can't wait to get another crack at Akita, because I'm still burning from that last one. (Jack tips his cap, in a final show of respect, and the scene fades out.) ---- (A blood red inferno fills the screen, slowly the fire moves inward concentrating to form the Japanese Characters meaning "True Flavor" in red over a black background. Over the blackness comes the voice of "the Iron Chef" Keiji Kobe.) Kobe V/O: Know your enemy; Know yourself. You shall fight a hundred battles, you shall win a hundred battles. So says the Art of War. As a student of this path, I've taken these words to heart as face my greatest challenge to date. Ryo Inoue, the pure fighter. A man who's style is a different from mine as night is form day. I have met him once in battle and we managed a draw. This next battle will be decisive. After my battle with Tora, I have learned of my own true flavor. Now I must learn of Ryo... (The shot cuts to a sad looking store front. The sidewalk in front of the store is cracked and covered with garbage. Straws, cups, fast food bags, and cigarette butts are piled in a corner and serve as the nest of a family of rats. The building itself is composed of bricks colored like excrement, many are broken and the grout is falling out. The two large store windows on either side of the central door are covered from the outside by posters advertising escort services, strip bars, and a sale on low grade whiskey from China. The glass front door is similarly covered, preventing any one from seeing what goes on inside from the street. Into this den of filth walk the "Iron Chef" Keiji Kobe. He has on a pair of white slacks, with a black leather belt and a silver buckle. He also is sporting a white wife beater, and over that is a green silk shirt featuring a dragon on the back. Kobe also has on his trademark orange and black mask as he enters the little shop. Before going into the building the camera tilts up to show a black and white sign over the ramshackled building. The sign reads: Steel Wind Fan Repair Service and Dojo Once inside the building the viewer can now see the shop is full of rusty old fans from a by gone era. Most makes seemed to be of the type you'd find in you grandmother's house. Not the box fan from the 70's she kept in her parlor, but the black desk fan she kept in her attic more as a reminder of days gone by than for any use Many of the fans lack the necessary wires to protect people's hands from getting hurt by moving blades. The entire room seems to be covered with a thick layer of dust, from the fans, to the tables and boxes they rest on, to the obese man sitting behind a wooden counter. Indeed, it seems as if the store was literally built around him, as he is hedged in with a larger amount of dust than almost any other item, and one wonders if he'd be able to get off his stool for anything. The fat man draws out a cigarette, a thin blue kind, and lights it with a brass zippo knock off. The first puff causes the man to cough wildly kicking up a dust storm that further worsens his cough. As the dust settles you can now see one of his eyes is lower than the other by 1/2 an inch. His nose is abnormally large, his cheeks covered with acne scars. His salt and pepper hair does not look so much like a birds nest, but rather an incomplete one, as much of his hair has fallen out, no doubt due to the high traffic this store receives. Sweat pours out of ever fold and crevice of his body, and combines with the dust to from something of a mud. The man is oddly reminiscent of a hippo or a rhino) Fat Man: Welcome to the Steel Wind Fan Service Center and Dojo. I am Bob Asamiya, chief fan repair technician and master of the Steel Wind style. Can I interest you in a fan? We sell only the finest quality factory seconds and reconditioned models. Kobe: No. Bob: Are you sure? You look like a who could use a good fan. Kobe: And you look like a man who could a good diet. Bob: Well, if you buy a fan from me, it'll go a long way to paying for my Liposuction. Kobe: What need have I for another fan? I've got thousands here in Japan, and millions in Mexico. Bob: Then some are in need of repair? Kobe: How should I know? Besides, I doubt you have the skills needed to reverse the damage caused to those who are forced to drink the water of Mexico for a life time. One wonders if Buddha can even save those souls. Bob: Well then, if you did not come to buy a fan, and you did not come to have your fan repaired, why are you here than? And if you say to use the John you had better pull out your wallet because the crapper is for paying customers only. Kobe: No, I came merely to basque in the eloquence of your tongue. Bob: That's enough out of you wise ass, now why are you here really? Kobe: I was told this was the house of the Steel Wind Dojo. Bob: Uh huh... Kobe: I've come to study the Steel Wind's techniques. (At Kobe's response the fat man kicks up another dust storm with a solid laugh. As his laughs a mirth quake builds up, as his bulbous sweat covered sack of flash reverberates with laughter.) Bob: Ha, ha, ha, ha, ha, ha! Alright buddy, I don't have to sit here and listen to your bull. Kobe: Oh, is that so, what are you going to do, throw me out? I don't think those shriveled little stumps you call legs and hold up that massive butt of yours. Bob: You're right, I am just going to sit here on my duff all day, but that don't mean I'm going to listen to you. I've got a stereo, and I can turn it up real loud. Kobe: Yeah, but it isn't plugged in. Now I came here to see, Keiji Asamiya, founder of the Steel Wind Dojo, I'd like to see his technique. Bob: Impossible, he doesn't see students anymore, he's retired. Kobe: He'll see me. Bob: Why's that? Kobe: Because I'll plug your stereo in so you don't have to get out of your chair if I can see him. Bob: You'll have to do better than that. Kobe: I've got a candy bar, half melted, that I sat on all the way from my home to here, but it's chocolate. Bob: Does it have nuts? Kobe: No plain. Bob: Ah, good, I swell up if I eat nuts. Kobe: Then I guess nuts haven't stopped you from eating many candy bars in the past, if I eat every piece of candy I saw I don't think I could swell that much. Bob: I'll have you know I've got a Thyroid problem. Kobe: Yes, too much food passes it on it's way to your fat gut. Now, fat one, show me to your father, Keiji Asamiya. Bob: I told you the only reason I'll get out of this chair is to take a crap. He's behind the beads. I'll warn you now, he is a little out of balance. I think the flumes have gotten to him. (Kobe walks behind to counter and pulls back the beaded door reveling another world it seems. The view of the room is blocked by stacks of newspapers eight feet high, that leave only two or three inches between the tops of the stacks and the rotten wooden boards of the ceiling. The aged papers are as yellow as urine, and a paper that fell from the top of one stack dates to the late 60's. The Iron Chef weaves through the maze of papers at one point a window to the room at large is formed as Kobe passes a stack of jars containing a saffron liquid. When Kobe emerges from the newspaper maze he finds himself in a room that is deceptively large for a building that seemed so small from the outside. In the left hand corner of the room is large mattress, that bears the tell tale signs of a bed wetter. On the opposite side there is work bench over which hangs rusted tools, that don't ever seemed to have ever moved from their places on the wall. A tangled nest of wires is pile to one corner of the table, and a man in blue cover all with gray stripes lays passed out on the table. On the center of the back wall there sits a large refrigerator. Kobe clears his throat. No response comes from the man.) Kobe: You sir, are you Keiji Asamiya? (The man remains passed out on the table. No sign of life comes from him.) Kobe: (Stomping his feet three times.) I am the Iron Chef, and I seek Keiji Asamiya, master of the Steel Wind Dojo, trainer of Ryo Inoue. (Hearing Kobe from the back) Bob: (Off screen) Tell him you need a fan repaired. Kobe: Keiji Asamiya! I seek to have my fan repaired. (Suddenly the man who lay on the table like so much dirty laundry stirs with interest. He wipes his eyes and turns to face Kobe. His eyes are wide open, like a child's on Christmas morning, trying to take in every detail. From his face hangs a long mustache, white as snow, and a three inch imperial graces his chin. Asamiya's ears stick out, and seemed to be cauliflowered. His ears and facial hair give him a rat like appearance, as does his narrow chin and large narrow nose. As Asamiya stirs his loud and crusty voice pops up with excitement at the prospect of fan repair work.) Asamiya: You need a fan repaired!?! Well you have come to the right place; I've been repairing fans since I was in diapers! Kobe: When you say since you've been in diapers, do you mean the first or the second time around? Asamiya: Eh? Speak up, lad! No need to whisper! Kobe: Are you the man who trained Ryo Inoue? Asamiya: Traine Air conditioners? You don't need them, just use a couple of our fans. Kobe: I want to learn the style of Ryo Inoue, they say he studied here. Asamiya: Ryo Inoue? Why Ryo it's so good to see you my old pupil? It has been too long, and I'm so glad you came! I heard you've maybe quite a name for yourself in the world of martial arts. Kobe: Quite a name indeed. Ryo's name is now known as with cowardice, cheating, backstabbing, and immortality. (As Kobe speaks Asamiya moves items about his desk trying to find something. He pushes aside a huge magnifying glass to retrieve a hopeless out of date, yellowed sports page. He holds it up and begins reading it with the aid of his magnifying glass while addressing Kobe.) Asamiya: That's so nice to here my pupil has continued on in the long tradition of this fan repair shop- I mean Dojo. I'm read you're going to join a shoot fighting organization called Tatami. Kobe: That organization has already came and went, Grandfather, most likely do to the fact Ryo and some other employees had less scruples than used car salesmen. Asamiya: Yes, I can see your training has paid off. But still this is a new challenge, and you will need something extra. That is why I have decided to teach you, Ryo, my prized pupil, my first student- Kobe: (Interjecting) And last, I hope- Asamiya: My secret steel wind techniques. Kobe: No, Grandfather, you have me confused, I am the Iron Chef, not the dog Ryo Inoue. (Asamiya places his paper and magnifying glass on his work bench and heads over to his refrigerator while talking to Kobe) Asamiya: Dog? No dog for you. No, to prepare you for your new challenges I have developed a special diet. (Opening the door to the fridge one can see it is nearly bear. There is a ketchup bottle (no doubt for fried rice.) baking soda, Japanese pickled vegetables, and a box full of parsley. Asamiya takes several bunches of parsley and walks across the room again.) Asamiya: I have found the secret of life, Ryo. Juice! Juice is the key. You've heard the saying man could live on pickles and rice alone? Well they lie! All you really need is juice! You know since I've been juicing I've lost ten pounds? Not only that, my vision has improve, my reflexes, and my hearing is so acute I can here the roaches in my walls talking. They're planning on killing me, but they can't as long as I have juice. Kobe: Grandfather, I care little for your lectures on nutrition, all I want to do is see your techniques so I can prepare for my match against Ryo. Asamiya: What makes you think you could even manage to see my skills? I tell you unless you drink the juice, your eyes won't be able to keep up. Now take this recipe down, you are to drink this five times a day during your training: at sunrise, a half way to noon, noon, half way to sunset, and sunset. If you do, you'll be able to make even stones bleed. You'll also have the most wonderful bowel movements. When I had mine this morning there were two pieces this big. (Holding his hands out to a size roughly two feet long. A moment later Asamiya turns on a fan set to blow upwards.) You see this juicer, my loving son got it for me? Now were was I? Oh yes, My super good start fight juice! Parsley, two bunches, nice and fresh. (Asamiya places parsley into the whirling blades sending chopped parsley into the air, it falls like wet snow about the room.) Asamiya: See, parsley is alive, it has all kinds of energy, and fiber, key to good bowel movements. Next add carrot for sweetness. (Asamiya shoves a carrot into the blades sending chips everywhere.) Then a handful of good soil, for minerals. Last juice a squid, for all the vitamins and microprobes that all the scientists haven't discovered. See, the squid has things in the sea that you can't get on the land, things you can't get unless you juice. (A blizzard of tentacles and bloody tissue fly as Asamiya adds a squid to the rotating blades. The sight of which causes Kobe's lunch to reemerge upon the ground.) Asamiya: Now drink up my pupil, so you will be strong. Then I will show you the secret techniques of the Steel Wind Fan Service Center, Shoe Repair, and Dojo! (Kobe is afraid to drink the potion at first, but seeing that the "juice" is on every surface of this back room up the cup set under the "juicer" he goes through the motions of drinking.) Asamiya: The flavor is good, no? I plan on bottling the juice and selling it to expecting mother to nourish the baby. I predict who ever drinks this when pregnant and nursing will create a super child. Kobe: Is that what was in those jars I pass on my way here. Asamiya: (shamefully) No, had some pluming problems awhile back. Still do. (Perking up) Now, then on to the secret techniques of the Dojo. As you well know, this Dojo derives it's style from the watching steel wind, or electric fans. I will now show you the secret technique taught to me by my father, based on the fan's movements. (Asamiya bends over like he's going to pick up something off the floor and begins spinning his arms around in circles in windmill style punches. Not aiming anywhere, just swinging his arms around hopping to hit something.) Kobe: Was it your father, or Andy Kaufman who taught you this? Asamiya: Go ahead and just try and come near me. You can't, you see the best defense in a real fight is a strong offense. And nothing is stronger than the Steel Wind Dojo's Whirlwind punch. Anything that comes near my fists will be cut to ribbons like a moth sucked into a fan. Come now, Ryo, why are you not attacking, or do you see this is unbeatable? Kobe: Yes, it is a strong style, only a man with a steel trap for a mind could come up with such a style truely this pure shoot style is better than anything in Lucha. Asamiya: Ha, ha! I forgot how powerful this technique is! I could take on a whole army of roaches like this. Come now, roaches, face me in battle. (With that Asamiya begins to run about the room in a great circle waving his arms about in circles knocking over stacks of paper, smashing bottles, and tipping over furniture. Asamiya slips and falls on the waste of some of his "juice" and begins screaming in pain about the dirty tricks of roaches. Kobe, in utter shock of what he has seen slowly backs up to the front of the Dojo/store. Once back in the front, Kobe turns to talk with Bob.) Kobe: That man needs help, serious help. Asamiya: (Off camera) Honorless Bugs, when I get up I'll destroy you! Bob: (Burying his head into his hands) My father doesn't need help. He needs a miracle. Kobe: A miracle you say? I know just the place. (The shot goes to white.) ---- [The young rookie sensation Billy Fairchild is standing back in the locker room area a sweaty mess after his fast paced match with Corey Hart. A translator is present as reporters are fielding questions to him through this translator.] Reporter: Fairchild-san, how do you feel about your performanace tonight and so far on the Coronation tour? Fairchild: Well, I think I've done very well so far. Of course there's always room for improvement but I'm still just a rookie. I'm trying to soak in as much as I can in all these matches. I suffered my first clean defeat against Kaz Hasegawa last night who is a tremendous talent and I really focused myself after that encounter and tried to learn from the mistakes I feel I made during that match. I came back tonight with my first singles win in an extremely quick paced match with Corey Hart. I think Corey and I proved that just because we are gaikokojin doesn't mean we can't captivate the Japanese audience with an excellent display of junior heavyweight style wrestling unlike anywhere else in the world. He brought his excellent high flying assault, I brought all my counter wrestling ability and I think we impressed a lot of people tonight and I'm honored and feel very lucky that I was able to come out on top in tonight's match. Reporter #2: What about your match on the opening nighht of the tour with Jason Ramsey against Kenji Okano and EIYUU? Fairchild: That was a great match. I love tagging with Jason Ramsey. He's a great partner and we are able to fit our styles together really well. I hope I proved the point I was trying to make to Okano that night, that he couldn't push me around anymore like he did in the dojo and he should take me a bit more seriously now. Reporter #3: You have wrestled a couple matches before but this is your first tour. How are you feeling? Fairchild: I feel pretty good actually, all things considered. I'm only 21 and the body is still very resiliant at this age. I'm really excited about my first tour and hope to make a good impression during it by always giving 110% and always having a good match. Reporter #4: How do you feel about your upcoming matches on the tour? Fairchild: Well, on the 18th I'm teaming with Jeremy Knyte for the first time against Mitsuhiro Kataka and Asamu Tatsu who I've never faced before. Three totally new faces in there for me that night so I really don't know what to expect from that match. I'll just have to bring my A-game and be very cautious against these new opponents. Then on the 19th I get to be in my first main event when I team with someone I have a lot of respect for, Rick Taylor, as we take on Shinji Nakata who I don't know much about and the always dangerous Ryo Inoue. I'd be lying if I said I wasn't a little nervous going into that match. Not only is it my first main event it's also my first time in the ring with Inoue and he frightens me to be quite honest with you. Very skilled and serious competitor. I'm going to have to be at the top of my game that night and I hope I can make some small impression on Inoue-san and show him my fighting spirit and what I'm made of. Then on the 20th I'm teaming with the man who beat me last night, Kaz Hasegawa, and we'll be taking on Goshin Star and XYZ. Those are two completely new opponents for me and I'm very interested to see hwat they bring to the table that night, especially XYZ since I'll be facing him the next night as well ... Reporter #5: Yes, at the TV taping on the 21st you'll be facing XYZ and EIYUU again in tag team competition. Your partner will be a man you beat in an upset for your first victory, Shiro Oodora. Your thoughts? Fairchild: Well ... I've faced EIYUU before and I think I have a pretty good feel for him now. Of course he could try and surprise me with some new moves or a different strategy but I think I can keep up with him. I don't know a lot about XYZ but from what I've heard he sounds anti-American or something like that. If he thinks I'm like all the American garbage wrestlers and "sports-entertainers" he wrestled during his stay in the States, he'll be sorely, sorely mistaken. As far as Oodora goes, yeah I upset him with a huge victory last time we were in a ring together. I don't know if he's going to hold a grudge against me about that or not. We've never teamed together before and EIYUU and XYZ were on a trio last night. They lost that match but they are still going to have more team experience than Oodora and I. I'll be real curious to see how Oodora and I gel together. I also have a couple hot moves in the tank I'd like to debut should I need them. So, if EIYUU and XYZ aren't careful and don't erase all their expectations about who I am and what I'm capable of, they're going to be shocked to their very core when I team with a former opponent for the first time and still beat them soundly. I'm going into this match with the mindset of caution and awareness. I'm going to be careful to not make any mistakes and be patient while waiting for an opprotunity to take the fight to EIYUU and XYZ, then I'm going to turn it up a notch and hope Oodora can keep up with me and help us acheive a victory. Thank you for all your questions, enjoy the rest of the show.
