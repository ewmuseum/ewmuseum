JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.10

10 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.10 1) Ryo Inoue 2) Team Tiger Claw: Tora Ryoku & Shiro Oodora 3) Tetsuhiro Morishita [TATAMI] 4) Billy Fairchild 5) Jack Shaw ---- [One by one the silver and red letters appear on the screen] T. A. P. Voice Over: Total Attack Puroresu, the War for respect rages. Fear the Violence... [open camera shot, we have a still shot from the Morioka Iwate Prefectural Gym rally. Rick Taylor is on the mic pointing an angry finger at a stoic Ryo Inoue] [a simulated camera flash signals a change in the still shot. Taylor stands eye to eye with Inoue, both deadly serious] Voice of Ryo Inoue: [almost a mocking voice] I will leanr what Total Attack is. Brave words Rick Taylor. Sadly bravery is not courage, it is more often than not stupidity trying to disguise itself as courage. March 10th is not far off Rick Taylor. I still keep counting the ways to make you scream and it still makes giddy with excitement. You thrive on confrontation without resolution. [There is a long pause as the camera remains on the shot of Inoue and Taylor] Voice of Ryo Inoue: There wil lbe resolution Taylor. I will resolve our issue by making you beg for me to break my Strangle Hold. You will wimper for mercy as my jujigatame tears out your arm. [Ryo sucks in a deep breath] The mere thought of what I will do to you makes my soul quiver. [The shot fades to a shot of Ryo Inoue, eyes closed, sucking in a breath] Ryo Inoue: I will not be denied my chance to beat you down. I crave this opportunity to humiliate you and CRUSH your nose to powder. But in the end you just don't understand what you are d you Taylor? [Ryo opens his eyes and smiles wickedly] Ryo Inoue: You are a stepping stone Taylor. I will beat you down and spring off your back to meet Jack Shaw the next night in Hiroshima. Yes, Hiroshima where I can not and will not be beat. Jack Shaw trying to beat nme in Hiroshima will be a sad sad mockery of a fight. Jack Shaw will still feel the effects of the beating I will deal unto him on 3.24 at Sumo Hall. [Ryo raises his fist before his face, steady] Ryo Inoue: The Quest for Pure Fight continues and both Taylor and Shaw will fall. Akita will defend his title against Shaw at Sumo Hall and afterwards wonder "Would this match been something more if Inoue had not beaten down Shaw". My "Guze" drives me to crush you all. To make you all see that your "Strong Style" makes weep for the men who really do fight for a living. HOW DARE YOYU PEDDLE YOUR WARES AS FIGHTING!! IT IS BECAUSE OF THIS... [Ryo closes his eyes and nods his head] Ryo Inoue: I must beat you... all... [Fade out] ---- <FADE IN> <We find both members of Team Tiger Claw, Tora Ryoku on the left and to the right is Shiro Oodora. They stand in a hallway at the top of a stairwell. Both are in black t-shirts with the Tiger Claw logo on the chest, and blue and white wind pants. The shine from a camera light is visible as it lights up the ceiling of the stairwell, and with the voice of an interviewer breaking the silence, we begin> <interviewer> "March 16th at the Fukuoka Hakata Star Lanes, Team Tiger Claw, you are squaring off against the lucha libre tandem of the "Iron Chef" Keiji Kobe and We Hyun Bae. For you Ryoku-san, it will be your first encounter with Kobe-san since the Coronation tournament. Do you see a different out come this time around?" <ryoku> "I expect a strong fight like our last battle, but this time I am much more prepared for Kobe and I will not be making the same mistakes that cost me a victory." <oodora> "Past errors aside, Ryoku-san and I are prepared to strike...and strike hard. Kobe-san, Bae-san, they are formidable opponents, but Team Tiger Claw knows no fear." <interviewer> "Recently, Kobe-san has proclaimed the superiority of Lucha Libre over that of Strong Style and Shooto. What are your thoughts on these statements?" <oodora> "Lucha is nothing. Were I so inclined, I too could bounce around the ring as if I were a child's rubber ball, but my style--indeed, all of Team Tiger Claw's style--is the Strong style. We are not impressed by acrobatics, because we're too busy making you hurt." <ryoku> "Hai! Lucha is impressive and breath taking, but to ever compare it's superiority to that of Strong Style or Shooto is ridiculous. The same is said of a trained circus performer, which is all Kobe and Bae are, trained circus acts who do impressive feats of acrobatics and nothing more. Kobe-san, Bae-san, you are "Iron Luchadores" you will need to be made of iron when you face the strong style warriors..." <ryoku & oodora> "TEEEEAM TIGER CLAAAAAW!!!!" <FADE OUT> ---- [Tetsuhiro Morishita is viewed in his Kobe townhouse, dining on an enormous bowl of Beef Udon. He grips the bottom of the dish with the palm of his left hand while working the hashi (chopsticks) with his right, intermittently slurping the broth after shoveling the noodles into his mouth. The cacophonous chirp of a cellular phone startles the former Alex RAPTOR, almost spilling his Udon] TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "Hai. Moshi-moshi.." "Mighty" Mitsuo Masanobu (MMM): "Moshi-moshi! Masanobu Mitsuo desu! TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "NA NI? Who is this? This better not be that baka calling again for Brett Weir." MMM: "It's me, "Mighty" Mitsuo Masanobu with Japan Puroresu Association, Morishita-san!" TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "This better be good, I almost scalded myself.." MMM: "Finally, I am able to get through to you.. SUGOI! I just have a few questions for JPA terebi!" TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "[reluctant] hai." [For the duration of the interview we see a freeze frame, 1/4 headshot of Morishita, with the text 'LIVE via telephone from Kobe, Hyogo prefecture' and the blurred silhouette of the interviewer] MMM: "During the recent post-match fracas at Korakuen Hall, you were spotted in the vicinity of Ryo Inoue's maverick shooto group TAP, --but-- you were also seated next to puroresuringu legend Tetsuo Matsumoto most of the night.. care to comment?" TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "Sure, I'm familiar with Super Tengu, I used to love watching him as a kid.. but it doesn't mean I want him sitting next to me, now does it?" MMM: [stammering] "well, I.. uh, umm.." TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "Look, I was at Korakuen Hall the other night as a guest of Keisuke Oyanami; and if I was seated next to the former Super Tengu then it was by coincidence, nothing more. MMM: "Clearly a schism now exists in JPA between traditional strong style practitioners and advocates of shooto, do you take any responsibility for the current friction?" TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "Oh, so this is somehow my fault? Listen, I'm not going to lie to you or any of the strong style pretenders.. does it rattle my cage that the last TATAMI card wasn't sold out nearly as quick as JPA's last show in that same venue; or that it took a top name in PURORESU to make a Mixed Martial Arts card a sellout? There's no accounting for people's taste, especially that of the otaku. Personally, I'm not going to stand still while somebody slaps me in the chest, nor am I going to allow myself to get dropped on my head by some roided-out 45 year old, which is just about the only thing I see in these fixed fights you call puroresu." MMM: "EIEEE! Certainly you cannot dispute your defeat in an MMA setting at the first TATAMI card at the hands--or should I say feet--of puroresu's favorite son, Genji Yamato.." [ZING] TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "Right you are, Poindexter, hai.. Yamato-senshu trained hard, fought harder; but I can't very well admire a man who grabs one NHB victory and then hides back behind theatrics and choreography, now can I? That fight in Saitama could have went either way and I know for a fact that at least one judge had me ahead on the scorecard had the match gone the distance. But my grapes aren't sour, I am proud of the way I fought that night." MMM: "Notwithstanding, do you have any ties to TAP? You were viewed leaving the Korakuen Hall venue with them after the post-match fireworks.." TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "Surely my own philosophies parallel those of Inoue Ryo,so naturally when jealous puroresu dogs outnumber and take a swipe at him because of his chosen fighting style, then it's kind of obvious which side I am going to take." MMM: "Will we see Tetsuhiro Morishita in a JPA ring any time soon?" TETSUHIRO MORISHITA: "I doubt it. I don't work fixed fights. Anyway, my Udon's getting cold. Ja mata, Masanobu-san卭h, and don't forget--TATAMI 2, "Everything Is Accepted" live from Osaka Castle Hall, Saturday, April 07. That is where I will be bending a certain Muay Thai kickboxer into a pretzel." MMM: "Hai, ja ne, Morishita-senshu! There you have It! Back to you in the studio!" [white noise] ---- [Backstage on 3/4 after Fairchild's 20 minute time limit draw afainst Eiji Yatsu. Fairchild is sweaty from the exhausting encounter and breathing somewhat heavy.] Reporter 1: Fairchild-san, you were not able to defeat Yatsu-san tonight but he was not able to defeat you either. Are you satisfied with your performance tonight? Fairchild: ... Yes, I am. ... I'll be the first to admit that my weakness is shoot style. I don't have any amateur background or anything like that. And Yatsu is skilled at judo ... so I thought that this would be a tough battle for me ... it was. ... But I'm proud of myself for sticking in there with a shooter like Yatsu and not tapping out or succumbing to my weakness. ... So yeah, I'm real proud of myself. Reporter 2: But you were not able to defeat Yatsu. Does that bother you? Fairchild: ... A little but not really. As I said before ... my main goal in this match was just to survive. ... To overcome my fear of shoot style and make some progress defending myself against it. Reporter 3: What are your opinions on the T.A.P. group? They are pundits of the shoot style which you so recently feared. Fairchild: ... Well ... they are all extremly talented athletes. There's no denying that. ... I can't say I like their attitude though or the way they seek to belittle what we do here in the JPA ... strong style pro wrestling. ... And Rick Taylor, a guy I've alligned myself with recently, really has a problem with Ryo Inoue and T.A.P. ... So I've got his back if he wants or needs it. Reporter 4: Yes, tell us of this new "Alliance" group you have joined. Fairchild: ... The Alliance is right now, myself, Jason Ramsey, and Rick Taylor. Ramsey really instigated the whole idea. ... Um, I'm not really sure what else to say because I'm really just the young guy in the group. Ramsey and Taylor would be much better to ask about it. [Fairchild laughs] Reporter 4: Okay, but what are your personal motivations for joing the group? Fairchild: ... I always kinda felt like a loner here. I mean, I trained at the JPA dojo for a significant amount of time and was the only gaijin there. Not only that, I never came into contact with any other gaikokojin at all. ... So I got a little homesick and having people around that I can talk to and relate to helps alot. It's also nice to know that someone's got my back if I should need it and vice versa. Reporter 1: How do you feel about your upcoming schedule? Fairchild: Good. ... I'm really beginning to hit my stride I think and feel relatively comfortable in the ring now. At the next TV I'll be tagging with Jason Ramsey against EIYUU and XYZ. ... Now, I've faced EIYUU countless times already and more often than not, come out on top. So I'm not too worried about EIYUU because for all practical intents and purposes ... he's my bitch. [The reporters laugh at the frank comment.] EIYUU is gonna have to step it up a notch or twelve if he wants to pull one over on me. And as far as XYZ is concerned, there;s some unfinished buisness there ... at least in my mind. He was so quick to disrespect me last time we faced simply because of my nationality. Well, if I didn't prove my fighting spirit in that contest I'll be sure to this time. ... So, simply put, they're both in for a rough time at the hands of The Alliance. Thank you gentlemen, enjoy the rest of the show. [Fairchild walks off to his locker room and the camera fades out.] ---- [Jack Shaw is in a different location. He has no shirt on, just a blanket drapped over his lower extremities. He doesn't have his Grain Grower's cap on. His thinning blond hair looks unruly, but he doesn't seem to notice as a young Asian massusse works on his lower back.] Jack Shaw: Whoa there, easy on the spine little lady. My back has been killing me lately, but I ain't gonna let that stop me from getting my hands on Akita yet again. It ain't no secret that I love to face him in the middle of that ring. Omph. [A wince comes over Jack, as the young lass continues to work on his lower back.] JS: I ain't no spring chicken, but I think there are a few hard miles left in this ole body. On the other hand Tokaio and Taylor, well they're just beginning. It seems Taylor and I spend alot of time tagging together, and it is always a pleasure to work with someone who is not only talented, but who wants to also learn about the sport. Nowadays alotta the kids I met in the United States were more worried about how they looked or sounded on a microphone rather than learning how to hold their own in a Gawddern match. That's why Taylor's confrontation with those TATAMI fellows surprised me. Part of me wanted to join in, but another part thought it woulda been better if he'd a just stayed out. But he's young and has a belly fulla fire. Hell, it was just a coupla ago I went chest to chest with some fella in England. [A deep breathe comes out of Shaw, as a great deal of his pain is combatted.] JS: Taylor and the rest of the young Westerners, well they're doing alright. Glad I can lend a hand every now and again, but when they asked me to join, I couldn't do it. I'd weigh'em all down, and that's the last thing they need, a big ole rock like me slowing'em up. So I'm going to enjoy this match, watch a kid who reminds me of me when I was just coming over. It's gonna be a doozy, but then again, when hasn't it been?
