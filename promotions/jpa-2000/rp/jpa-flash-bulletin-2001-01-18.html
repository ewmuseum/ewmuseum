JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.1.18

18 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.1.18 1) Ryo Inoue 2) XYZ 3) Jason Ramsey ---- [open camera shot. Ryo Inoue sits before a television screen, the word "livid" can best describe his mood as he watches "Iron chef" Keiji Kobe's interview belittling Steel Wind Dojo and Keiji Asamiya. As the interview goes on the camera pans down revealing Inoue slowly taping his right fist] [Fade out] Voice of Ryo Inoue: Kobe! [Fade in, Ryo Inoue is walking away from a sparring session with Steel Wind Dojo Teacher Junji Shirow. The students are all silent, a few rus to the mats to help Shirow, Inoue's old instructor up. Shirow's left arm hangs limply at his side. Inoue walks to the locker room] [Fade out] Voice of Ryo Inoue: I once respected you... [Fade in, Inoue works the heavy bag, body punches and head shots are mixed with strong kicks. The combos start slow but then get faster as they are repeated. This kata continues. Body, head, knee, kick, body head, knee, kick] [Fade out] Voice of Ryo Inoue: But now, you belittle me and all I seek to achieve... [Fade in, Ryo Inoue stands in the sauna, the steam penetrates his body, the sweat falls off his back and rolls off his forehead. It is supposed to be relaxing but the scowl of Ryo Inoue is maintained] [Fade Out] Voice of Ryo Inoue: You insult my school and master. I will punish you for this just as I made the others pay... [Fade In, a shot from a Neo Japan show last Summer, "Blackjack" Takahashi's face turns purple as he is trapped in Inoue's Strangle Hold GAMMA] Voice of Ryo Inoue: Takahashi... [the shot changes, Tripaq Ngyuenb is trapped in the same move] Voice of Ryo Inoue: Ngyuen... [The shot changes to alex Raptor trapped in Ioue mount as fists barrage his exposed head] Voice of Ryo Inoue: RAPTOR... [The shot changes, 2 clips are shown, first Ryo Inoue traps Hiryoshi Tenzaki in a flying hammerlock and releases him, ending Tenzaki crashing to the mat and second, Inoue trapping Tenzaki in his own dreaded STK submission hold, Tenzaki is passed out as the ref calls for the bell] Voice of ryo Inoue: and Tenzaki... they all sought to belittle me as you have Kobe. Thety sought ot break me and hurt me. I ask you this Kobe... Where are they now? They all got in the ring with me, they all felt power of the Pure Fight overpower them. I made them suffer tenfold as much as I had suffered. They felt my "Pure Sweat" pour out and down in to their throats. Ask them, if you can find them, ask them them how the it tasted, that bitter acrid taste of defeat. Ask them if it is wise to anger me, to boil my blood and bring out the true competitive fire within me... [The shot now changes to Ryo inoue, standing there, the anger in his face burns pure hatred into the camera] Voice of Ryo Inoue: You think some 30 minute draw prepared you for the anger you have unleashed? You think your vaunted skills as a jumping luchadore will save you from the power of my shoot? What will you do when my fist breaks your teeth Kobe? How will you gasp for air when my kicks force the breath from your body? What will you scream as break your arm? How will you beg for my mercy... [Ryo Ioue stands there, he IS anger personified, he IS Mr. Intensity...] Voice of Ryo Inoue: I think of all these things Kobe and... [a grin, a pure malicious grin, spreads across Ryo Inoue's face] Voice of Ryo Inoue: I smile... [fade out] ---- [Click…] [The camera bleeds onto the screen and the image you see is the typical set used for interviews. You see the light blue walls with the ever-famous paintings of sunsets by some unknown artist. You see a chair, with two stands to either side. One stand with what seems to be a fake plant; the other will a coaster and a glass of water filled almost to the brim. As the cooing sound of Rage Against the Machine's "Sleep Now in the Fire" plays in the background, you begin to turn your attention towards the small, Japanese man sitting in the chair known only as XYZ. He is wearing a blue jean jacket, a white under shirt with blue jeans; pair of black leather boots covers his feet. XYZ's hair, for once, is combed and parted neatly down the middle. XYZ appears to be calm and as gentle as a little baby as he reaches over to the stand, picks up the glass of water and takes a sip. He then sighs, takes a breath and sits back, staring deep into the camera. As the sounds of Rage Against the Machine fade way to nothing, XYZ begins to speak.] XYZ: Here in Japan I've been shown to the best and competed with the best. I've sat back in the locker room, congratulated on my victories, on my matches. I've been told I have great potential and I've also been told I have a bright future. But that is something I already knew. I sit here today wondering what happened on January fifteenth, I sit here wondering what went wrong. One loss to many means nothing; one loss to some means the world. To me, it's the latter. Losing, to me, is not an option. Losing, to me, is disgracing all that you stand for. I felt ashamed to lose to such opponents. But more disgracing than losing to such talent, more disgracing than even losing to an American is simply . . . wrestling one! [A brief smile comes to the face of XYZ.] I came here to Japan to get away from filthy wrestlers that lack serious talent. I came back to my homeland to… How do I say it… wrestle against the best, not the pathetic Americans that I had so easily defeated weeks ago! I came here to Japan searching for a new career, searching for something to set my career on fire. But what I've gotten in return for my hard work, my blood and sweat is less than acceptable. An American wrestling _ ME _ in Japan? That does not sit well with me, I'm sorry. But worse than wrestling Americans, with there horrid way of wrestling and lazy attitude is having to face a tag team composed of a American with Japanese brilliance! Yes, I am discussing the tandem of Billy Fairchild and Shira Oodora! Oodora-san, why must you lower yourself to the level of teaming with Billy? Why must you sink that level of teaming with a lousy American? I feel your pain Oodora-san; I feel the grieving you must be dealing with. But what astounds me even more than Oodora-san teaming with Billy is the fact he lost to Billy just days ago! [Takes a deep breath, trying to calm the energy rushing to the surface.] Oodora-san losing to a American when you are a great wrestler is not acceptable, but losing to Billy Fairchild? I just don't see how. But then again, I guess that is why they labeled it a huge upset. You see Oodora-san the only fault I can find in your wrestling style or your talent is the fact that you seem to choke when the going gets tough. That you seem to . . . How do I put it… Roll over and die? Yes, that is it. You seem to roll over and die when it comes time to bury the hatchet and end it all. Your loss to Billy might not mean anything to many but to me, that is a sign of disrespect to us Japanese! Losing to a American in America, that's one thing! But in your own country? In front of your own fans? Tisk, tisk, tisk… I just hope you show up to wrestle Friday Night Oodora-san for if you don't; you'll bring even greater shame to the already tainted Oodora name. [Cracks his knuckles, one at a time for a few seconds. XYZ is obviously thinking about what to say next.] And for you Billy Fairchild, I hope you change my mind about Americans and their lazy ways. I hope you are able to somehow prove that Americans have worth and a meaning on this pathetic world. Because if you cannot Billy, there is no possible way of victory coming to your side! Oh yes, EIYUU-san I thank you for the excellent performances you have been putting on lately. You have really impressed me and gained my trust. I do hope that you'll be able to keep up Friday Night because so far it has been bliss! Now I bid you all farewell and good luck and Billy, get ready for a war because I don't take being insulted and offended by a lousy American very well! [Fade out.] ---- Scene opens in a gym somewhere in Shinzuoka. Billy Fairchild is on his way out of the building when Jason Ramsey walks in. He is dressed in blue and white sweat pants with a black t-shirt and a blue and white JPA bomber jacket. Ramsey smiles as he sees Fairchild. "Hey Billy, are you done for the day?", Ramsey says as he jokingly looks at his watch. "I've been in here since 8:00am, nice of you to stroll in this afternoon!" "Hey man, we can't all be out the door at the crack of dawn. Some of us have responsibilities!" "Married life, huh?" "You guessed it." "Be careful not to over do it with the work out," Billy says "You've got Corey Hart tonight and he's fast as anybody I've seen here so far." "Bill, you should know by now that I don't take anybody lightly. Hart has been on my mind all day." "What do you have planned for him?" "Well, with out going into detail, I plan on slowing him down a notch or two. I was actually hoping I would catch you here so we could spar a little. You're just as fast as Hart is. I could use the practice." "Well..........." "Come on man, I'll take a half hour of your time." "Ok, let's go........" Ramsey and Fairchild walk over to the padded area where a couple of man have just finished a judo session. Ramsey bows to the mat before taking off his jacket and walking into the sparring area. Fairchild bows as well to show respect to the "arena" then joins Ramsey on the mat. Ramsey loosens up a bit and turns to Fairchild. "Ok, I want you to try to out move me every chance you get. Don't hold back because Hart sure as hell won't." "Ok man, you asked for it." Ramsey and Fairchild circle for a moment and then tie up collar and elbow. Fairchild goes behind Ramsey and ad tries for a waist lock. Ramsey spins around in a flash and hooks Fairchild's arms and lifts him straight up into Superdriver position before easing him back down. Fairchild stands straight and smile at Ramsey and then dives for a fast double leg take down. Ramsey, still on his back, wraps his legs around Fairchild's waist and and grabs him in a head and arm lock. Ramsey releases Billy and rises to his feet. Fairchild jumps to his feet and goes for a drop kick. Ramsey steps back out of the way and Billy lands face first on the mat. Ramsey reaches down and grabs his ankle and starts to apply an ankle lock before Fairchild rolls over and kicks his way out. Both men decide to take a breather for a moment. "Damn Jason, you're a fast S.O.B.! I guess I didn't know you could move like that!" "It's not that I'm all that fast, I just think fast. You are physically WAY faster than I am, but I had a counter or reversal ready for every move you made. That's the speed that wins matches. That's the speed I'll need to beat Hart." "I know what you mean! He and I were FLYING when we squared off and I managed to get a move or two ahead of him and got the advantage I needed." Ramsey looks at Fairchild for a moment and a smile slowly creeps across his face. He shakes his head and rises to his feet. He helps Fairchild up and slaps him on the back. "You, Mister Fairchild, have some VERY serious potential, and after I'm done with Hart, I plan on helping you reach that potential. Now get out of here, we have matches in a few hours. I'm going to do some weight work and get some steam and I'll be ready." "Thanks Jason, I'll see you at the Shin Messe......" Ramsey shakes Fairchild's hand and heads toward the weight room.
