JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.20

20 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.20 1) Gustav Jaeger 2) Billy Fairchild 3) Ryo Inoue 4) Rick Taylor 5) Iron Chef Keiji Kobe ---- (The camera fades in on the lockerroom of Gustav Jaeger. We know it's his lockerroom because he's sitting right there, lifting some dumbells and obviously preparing for his next match. As he sees the camera he smiles a bit and starts talking but he doesn't stop his exercising.) GJ: Guten Tag. Once again I come before you, ready to face my next opponent on Battle Start, Christopher Kojima. Kojima is an power wrestler like myself only he is not as big. He will make a worthy opponent I am sure. (Gustav grins into the camera and takes too more dumbells. Looking right into the camera with an odd grin he begins to juggle them, still talking.) GJ: There is no denying that Herr Kojima is quite talented. I have seen him wrestle before and he has a good style, it contains many aspects of other wrestling styles. For most people that would cause difficulties. I am not most people. (Gustav keeps juggling the dumbells. Despite the deceptively playful nature of what he's doing, it's obvious that it is not all fun and games as the dumbells seem to be rather heavy.) GJ: Granted, you have some talent. But so did Goshin Star and I defeated him rather easily, though I really shouldn't compare you to a soulless coporate dog like him. My last opponent, Jinshi Togawa, he was also rather talented and he too fell to the Freiburg Fury. In fact, I have faced many talented opponents here, most of whom were excellent fighters with great honour. All of them lost to me. (Gustav tosses the dumbells away, causing a rather loud clanging as they fall to the floor. The smile on Gustav's face is gone as he is suddenly serious once more.) GJ: Herr Kojima. There is no dishonour in losing to the Freiburg Fury. It is the natural state of things. Many have fallen to defeat at my hands, many more shall fall to defeat in the future. None can stand against the full power of the Freiburg Fury. It just cannot be done, the best you can hope to do is lose with honour. I hope you can do this. (Suddenly a scowl crosses Gustav's face and his eyes cloud over with rage. His hands ball up into fists and he becomes tense.) GJ(in a low growl): Obviously Ryo Inoue cannot. Herr Inoue, you were one that I used to have great respect for. This is no longer true. You had failed to win the titles against the legendary MASSIVE Akita. Though I have never tasted defeat in a one on one wrestling contest, I know there is no shame in losing to an honourable opponent. The true warrior lives with it and moves on. You are not a true warrior. (Gutav's face takes on a look of disgust and he hawks and spits on the floor.) GJ: Instead of acknowledging that you were defeated by a better man, you whine like a jackal and insult the skills of the man who defeated you. Instead of acting with the honour you claim to posess, you through insults and act like a young child throwing a tantrum. Not only this but you degrade yourself by using JPA airtime to put over your TATAMI fighting group and in doing so you become no better then the soulless coporate whore that is Goshin Star. You claim the superiority of your little group, your little band of Mixed Martial Arts fighers called TAP. Many Mixed Martial Arts fighters have fallen to the Freiburg Fury, Herr Inoue. I can only hope that you will be next. (Gustav glares menacingly into the camera as it fades to black.) ---- [Outside Ookawa Industrial Meeting Hall, Billy fairchild is walking out of the building carrying his gym bag. He showered and clean, wearing a black leather sports coat over a light-weight black silk button up shirt and dark blue jeans with a silver studded belt and a a pair of Magnum boots. The camera approaches him and Billy stops to talk a bit with the accompanying reporter.] Reporter: Fairchild-san, may I speak with you for a moment? Fairchild: Absolutely. Reporter: With the upcoming Junior Struggle tour on the horizon, the matches between the juniors here in JPA have taken on a whole new meaning as everyone is jockeying for position going into the tournament. Last night you earned a very big victory over the upstart XYZ. Do you think he's finally earned any respect for you or your abilities? Fairchild: I would hope so. I mean, I respect XYZ as an athlete and as a wrestler, but his attitude towards foreigners sucks. I respect virtually everyone here in the JPA, some more than others, but they wouldn't be here if they weren't worthy of respect. I bust my ass each and every night trying to be the best I can be out there, and lately it's been paying off. If people don't take me seriously, then that's just an advantage for me because they're underestimating my ability, my skill, and my heart. Reporter: Last night's win establishes you as one of the top jr's in the JPA, a spot that could've gone to either yourself or XYZ. Fairchild: Yeah, that was basically a tie breaker match between myself and X. With this win, I feel I'm right behind the established guys like Iron Chef and Tasogare No Prince on the pecking order. I'm probably a tad behind Okano as well. Reporter: Okano-san and yourself are rivals, are you not? Fairchild: I guess you could say that. We trained together for a bit at the JPA dojo. He didn't like me very much and treated me like shit. I have no problem admitting that I respect Okano's abilities inside the squared circle, why can't he be a man and do the same? Perhaps I'll earn some respect outta him the next time we're in the ring together. Reporter: You will have that opprotunity soon Fairchild-san on the next JPA TV taping, when you tag with Okano to face Iron Chef and his rival, Tasogare No Prince. Fairchild: Yeah, who the hell booked this match? This is messed up. Okano hates me and may ver well sabotage our teams chances just to spite me. Chef and Tasogare are no good buddies either. What the hell? Reporter: Switching gears for a moment, how is The Alliance going? Fairchild: It's going fairly well. I guess you could say it's going great since Rick Taylor just got moved into the Twin Titles match next week but that was at the expense of losing Jack Shaw to injury. I know Shaw isn't officially a part of our Alliance but we all respect him tremendously so it's still a little upsetting to see Shaw out. Let's see if we can go 3 for 3 at the taping topped off by Taylor winning the twin titles and pull an Alliance sweep and dedicate it to Jack. Reporter: Thank you very much Fairchild-san. Good luck to you and the Alliance in your attempt at a sweep. Fairchild: Thank you very much. Sayonara. ---- [We all know the drill by now as the letters appear onscreen...] T. A. P. Voice Over: Total Attack Puroresu, the War For Respect Is On, We'll Give You a Reason.... [fade out] [Fade in, we are backstage following the 3.16 Fukuoaka Hakata Star Lanes show. Ryo Inoue is ranting to the press as he dresses after a shower. The ranting is about to get worse...] Ryo Inoue: [mid-sentence] ... If Nakata truly wants a fight why does he not come? I am at Steel Wind Dojo when not wrestling. I am easy to find. Let him cry his little Strong Style tears after he bloody and crying for mercy... Riki Fudo: Hey Ryo what do you think of the injuries suffered by Shaw tonight? Ryo Inoue: I have no doubt Akita is taking credit for this, or perhapos his lap dog Tokaio. Jack Shaw was injured because I made him weak after our match last week. Jack Shaw was injured because he did not take me seriously. Rick Taylor is a wiser man. He took me seriously and brought out the fight. He tried to match my Violence but he wrestles tag amtches now because my Violence was too much for him. Riki Fudo: But he is getting Shaw's shot at Akita on 3.24 now. [For the first time Ryo Inoue is stunnedas he stares in disbelief. A page wearing a tap jumpsuit runs over to Ryo and hands him the Genji Yamato press release announcing Rick Taylor as the challenger to Akita] Ryo Inoue: [somberly] Tell me something, why can a Japanese man not recieve a shot at Japanese championship? Why is Genji Yamato resorting to disrimination of his own people? Are we not draws? I speak not for myself but for men such as Kaz Hasegawa or even Jinshi Togawa. Why must we be forced to this Nihonjin against Gaijin mentality? Rick Taylor couldn't even beat me and he gets a shot at Akita? Yamato is against his own people and it makes me sick. Not only is he a coward by not facing me at TATAMI but he shuts out the true native Japanese fighters who are worthy of the honor to fight for the J1 Rikidozan Double championships. I am disgusted that Rick Taylor is wrestling Akita. What is next? Keiji Kobe being taken seriously by the press? [Ther si some mild chuckling from the press corps] Ryo Inoue: Yamato is a cowar and he has proven it again by denying a Nihonjin to challenge for their own title. Be wary or else we all be drenched in the blood of his "DEMON" and be forced to do Garbage Wrestling!! Now let me dress, even a REAL fighter must be clothed and eat!! [Fade out] ---- [The JPA Dojo. The almost pitch, cave-like training room is illuminated just barely by the dim light of a single bare lightbulb, suspended above the training ring. In the middle of the ring, on his knees, but resting on his calves, wearing blue JPA athletic pants and a sleeveless Alliance shirt, his morbidly beautiful tattoos, covering his arms from wrist to shoulder on both arms legible in the pale, eerie light. Shadows cast upon his face hide his features, as his face is downcast, though through his voice and his ink we know easily who it is...] Taylor: Last night, a tragedy occured not ten feet from e, and I was powerless to stop it, or have any effect on it's outcome. [Cut quickly to a black and white still photo of Shaw getting kicked in the face by Tokaio. Cut back to the shot of Taylor in the ring.] Taylor: Jack Shaw will not be with us for the forseable future. That big Texan has some big damn feet and with his temporary departure, he has left some great big boots to fill. And it is I who have been chosen by Yamato-san to fill them... by stepping in the ring with on the 24th of March, at the Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall, to challenge Masashi Akita for the Double titles. It is a challenge and an honor that I accept with no small amount of gratitude, surprise and, I must admit, nervousness. Taylor: Gratitude because I feel honored that Yamato-san feels confident enough in me to grant me this chance and opportunity. Surprise because I wasn't expecting it in the least. And nervousness because I know what Akita brings to the ring, and I know he will hold nothing back. Taylor: But be aware, Akita-san and all others watching this-- I myself hold nothing back. I am no pushover. You know that. We've squared off in the ring several times. Sometimes you won, sometimes I won. This friday will be no different. The shuffling of challengers, first Ryo Inoue, who might still have had this shot had he not tucked tail and ran in cowardice. Then Jack, who now is laid up in his home, back in Texas, I know follwoing everything on his satellite TV. Now me. Now I must shoulder the burden of challenger on the 24th in Ryogoku Hall. I am not afraid, for I have no fear. I fear no one. Not Masashi Akita, not Ryo Inoue, not even God HIMSELF. I fear no one and nothing. Akita-san, when you enter that ring on the 24th, you have everything to lose, the Double Titles is on the line. When I step into that ring, there is nothing left for me to lose, and that only makes me more dangerous. Ryo Inoue talks about how his guzo being so dangerous. Nothing is as dangerous as a man who has lost everything, because a man who has lost everything has no fear. Professionally, my plate is full. Professionally, I am doing better than ever before. In am a top player here in the JPA, I have the love and admiration of the Japanese fans--the greatest fans in the wrestling world--, the Alliance is making waves here in the JPA, I have a shot at the Double Titles, but it means very little to me in the big picture. Though professionally my life is flourishing, my personal life is a mess. The woman that I have loved and spent my life with for the last six years has told me she is no longer happy and wants a seperation, and I know that I may never see my daughter again, because I am here in Japan and my family is in Philadelphia, which is a situation not looked on kindly by lawyers and judges. But I do not wish to air my dirty air to the JPA fans and work my problems out on JPA airtime. But now, my personal life is in shambles, and all I have left is my profession, my livelihood, my SHOT at the Double Titles and Akita-san on the 24th in Ryogoku Hall. Akita-san, I mean no disrespect to you when I say this, but I can only speak the truth. With the love of my life gone, nothing else matters. The only thing that matters any more is to walk into that ring on the 24th and defeat you for the Double Titles. As of this moment, everything in my life that I have ever held dear has been pulled out from underneath me, and I have been left high and dry without an answer. Now, the only thing in the world that means a damn to me is to walk out of Ryogoku Hall as the new Unified Rikidozan/Neo Japan Heavyweight champion. I set this goal not for the Alliance, not to stick it to Ryo Inoue, not for anything like that. I set this goal for me, to give my life back some meaning. I have nothing left to lose, Akita-san. Though I respect you very much, I do not fear you, and a fearless man is a dangerous man. Last monday, before my life fell apart, it was a whole different ballgame. Not only was Jack still the challenger, but I was blissfully unaware of the bombshell about to be dropped on me by the woman I loved, and still love. But now, in a mere few days, I am crushed by the woman I love, Jack's orbital bone is shattered, and I am named the new challenger. My only chance at redemption is this title shot. To prove to myself that there is still some fighting spirit left in me. This match is not about straing-style vs. shooto, not about a legendary rivalry, it is now about the aftermath to a series of tragedies. To bring myself out of the muck and mire and rubble that my life has become, this is what this match is about. It is about my redemption. Nothing left to lose... I am now more dangerous than ever before, Akita-san. This match will make my match with Ryo Inoue at the Okayama Budokan look like children playing. Akita-san, I respect you deeply, but you will be the sufferer of my fury and pain. And I will give my redemption one last chance... [Fade to black, which is then invaded by a flaming green and black Alliance logo, whoich is then dissolved into a dark green screen, before again going black.] ---- (The camera opens up onto the Iron Chef gathering his gear at the end of a long day at Kumamoto Gymnasium. He is in a simple warm up suit, black in color, and although he's had his match, his trademark orange and black mask is still firmly in place on top of his head. He takes a deep breath before addressing the camera.) Kobe: Feeling confident now, Okano and Fairchild? After all, today I teamed with the Double title champion, holder of the most prestigious belt in the land, a man I love and respect, and lost. So how will I fair when I team with Tasogare no Prince, a man whom I have little fondness for. No, that is the understatement of the year. I hate Tasogare no Prince. I hate the way he talks about his destiny as if he could see into the future with the aid of a magic book. I hate the way the girls squeal when ever his music is played. Most of all, I hate his poetry. I mean I hate it. I hate the way he waxes poetic like a school girl pining over an unrequited love. I hate no Prince, I really do. And make no mistake, he hates me. He hates the fact I never take off this mask in front of wrestlers even when I'm in the shower. He hates the way I brag about being the best in the world. He hates the fact I back up my big talk even more, he hates that I have shown myself time in and time out to be the very best in the world. I despise him, he despises me, but here is where things get complicated. We respect one another. As much I question his work ethic, insult his poetry, and resent his pandering to school girls, I respect him. I respect him because no one I have ever faced in all my career has challenged me as much as no Prince- no one. Not Inoue, not Taylor, not any one in Mexico, not any one in America, not anyone in Japan. Tasogare no Prince is the best wrestler I've ever faced, and I respect him for that, period. And you know what, guys? Tasogare no Prince respects me. He's never said the words, but that doesn't mean I don't know it. He trains a little harder before facing me. He hit a little harder when he's aiming at me. He moves a little quicker than normal when facing me. He does this because he knows he has too. He respects my ability, just as I respect his. Respect isn't enough, there needs to be more to make a team. There is an issue of trust. I mean can I trust no Prince to watch my back, more over can he trust me to watch his. I freely admit I don't like to be in same room as him, but less the same side of ring. And with the tournament to crown the JPA's first Junior champion weeks away, it sure would be convenient if one of us got hurt for the other's chances of claiming that prize. Well, I'd be a fool if I said I could trust no Prince as a partner. But I can trust him as a competitor. I faces him across the ring, and I know from experience the man does not like to lose. He likes loosing about as much as I care for his poetry, maybe even a little less than I do. So I know he'll be there to break the count if I need him to. He'll be there to make the tag, ready and willing to finish the job. I can trust him to give it his all, because he doesn't know how to give a match any less. How about you Fairchild, can you trust Okano to be there for you? Ask his pals from his old football club. Ask them if they trust him after what he's done. Billy, you had better ask yourself if Okano respects you. Ask yourself if there was any way he could respect a gaijin like you. But at a more personal level, forgetting about partners for a minute. Do either of you think you can beat me? Do either of you believe you hold some thing over the Iron Chef? I am the best Junior in the world, the best, and neither on of you can hold a candle to me. I'm good enough I could more then likely beat you two almost single handily, but I'm not facing you alone. I have Tasogare no Prince, the man who comes closest to be working at my level skill. But that said why should no Prince trust me to be his partner? It's a forgone conclusion I'll win the Pan Asian junior senshu with Tasogare no Prince out of the way. So why wouldn't I throw him to you two wolves. Well I could try and sell you a line about how much I respect him, but respect won't keep me from betraying him. I can also make the claim I hate losing, but that would only serve to aid fuel to flames suggesting I'd hate losing to him latter in the Junior struggle so I'd set him up now. No, I'll tell you why no Prince can trust me as a partner. Because I hate him. That's why he can trust me, because I'm a petty, greed, spiteful bastard. And if any one is going to be beating no Prince, it'll be me. Quite frankly if I left it in Okano's hands, I doubt he could cripple a man with the aid of a strain of polio. And I've seen Fairchild wrestle, he's the best his country has to offer, that's why I don't worry about him. Because the best American translates to a sub-par Japanese wrestler. You guys aren't going to hurt no Prince, as my partner would say, destiny has reserved that right to me. I am the only one in the company who is going to beat no Prince, and when I do I face him on 4/29/01 , I don't want him to have any excuses. So I'll be the perfect partner. I'll watch your back, no Prince. I'll be there to make the tag. I'll break those submissions, We'll be there for each other to double team. But remember that I still hate you and your poetry, and after 3/24/01, I'll be back to grinding you beneath my chef 's clog. I'm working with you not because I have any fondness for you, no Prince, but because I'm a professional, and I'll be damned if I let my fans down to get at you. Remember, one night only, this will be the first and the last time we ever team if I have anything to say about it. So enjoy the match, because it will be the last time you ever get this close to being the absolute best.
