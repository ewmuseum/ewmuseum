JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.5.4

4 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.5.4 1) Iron Chef Keiji Kobe 2) Tasogare no Prince 3) Eiji Yatsu 4) Jason Ramsey ---- (The shot opens up onto the baggage claim of number 8 of Tokyo's central airport. The Iron Chef, Keiji Kobe stands next to a young woman of obvious Latino decent, as he grabs pieces of co-ordinated luggage. The woman has long, curly dark hair, and high cheek bones. It is clear she would be taller than Kobe if she wore heels. She is dressed in jeans and t-shirt featuring what looks like a bootleg image of the Iron Chef. Kobe for his part is dressed in a pair of black slack, with a long sleeved polo shirt. He has on his trademark orange and black mask. One wonders how he got through customs dressed like that. From behind the camera rushes Mituso Masanobu, with a microphone.) Masanobu: Kobe-san, could I have a word with you? Kobe: Always, Masanobu-san, always. What is it? Masanobu: Well first I was wondering if you could introduce me to this lovely woman. Kobe: (Kobe's obvious pride beams through his mask as he squeezes the young Latino woman close to his side.) This is my wife. Masanobu: And does she have a name? Kobe: Sure, but the only part of it you need to know is Mrs. Kobe. Masanobu: And where are the children? Kobe: With their Grandparents. Masanobu: You sent them ahead? Kobe: Left them behind. Given the long tour, Mrs. Kobe thought I would not be faithful. Masanobu: (Intrigued) Really? Kobe: No, you filthy muckraker, she simple said she didn't get out enough, so I decided to take here with me this tour. But I believe you had more on your mind than my family's well being. Masanobu: To tell the truth, I wanted to get you thoughts on the Junior League tournament you've been entered for our Pan Asia Junior heavyweight Championship. Kobe: (Stroking his chin and smoothing out the whiskers of his beard.) I like the league format. I was in the tournament for the double titles, as you know, which was single elimination. Masanobu: Hai. Kobe: Which was a fine set up, but it didn't leave any room for error. I didn't take into account you might have an off night. Masanobu: You are still bitter about your loss to- Kobe: DON'T SAY THAT DOG'S NAME! I had an off night! He knows it, I know it, we all know it! (Kobe's wife begins to rub the chef's shoulders and he claims down a little) But that is in the past, my future is the Pan Asia Junior heavyweight Championship, and this league play suits me just fine. Masanobu: So you believe this league will give you a padding should you have another off night. Kobe: Padding? Padding? I am the Iron Chef, my record needs no padding. I don't need a safety net to make it to the finals. No, the reason I like this league style is not so I have a safety net. The reason I like it is so I can face every one in the tournament. There won't be any excuses come the 16 of June. I will have beaten Hart, I will have beaten Okano, I will have beaten Fairchild. I will have beaten no Prince. I will have shown the world that I am every bit as good as I say, I'll of shown the world I am the best middle weight in the world. Masanobu: There was a name you left off that list of men you plan on beating, Eiji Yatsu, you first opponent in the tournament. Kobe: I don't put my match with the Eiji Yatsu in the same league as my matches in the rest of this league. The men I face in the league, Fairchild, no Prince, I will beat to show I am the best in my class. I'm facing these men for a point in some grand game to win a prize. Eiji Yatsu... He is a different matter. Our match won't be about some point. It won't be an attempt to show one of us is better. When I face Eiji Yatsu, the traitorous dog, I won't have my eyes on any other prize than the joy of hurting him. Masanobu: Harsh words, but I suppose that can be expected given your history with Yatsu's mentor. Kobe: A lot more than harsh words can be expected, Masanobu-san. Yatsu is a disloyal dog, he stands proudly as a member of the violent gang, TAP. Masanobu: Chef-san, I would hardly put TAP in the same class as Buzuken and Yakuza gangs. Kobe: Why not? Neither has any respect for authority, or rules, or honor. And if that were not enough reason to hate Yatsu, he works for TOP as well, a rival to the JPA. I'm not about to let a dog like him hold our top prize. Masanobu: Yes, well the task may not be as easy as you think it will be, Ryo Inoue (Kobe spits on the ground) says he believes Yatsu may be a spoiler. Kobe: Correct me if I'm wrong, but did not this dog also say he could beat an over-weight out of work gaijin in under five minutes, and then went onto a split decision? Masanobu: Hai. Kobe: So let's say this dog is not much of a fortune teller. Yatsu may be strong, he may be confident, he might even be well trained, but I doubt that given his trainers, but he is not going to be a spoiler. TAP likes to dismiss me a joke, a circus clown, who knows nothing of fighting. I know more about fighting than you'll ever know. Just because I were a mask doesn't mean my passion is any less than complete, and just because you were a judo jacket doesn't mean you are any less of a quitter. Lucha was too hard for you, Yatsu, so you quit. You gave up your study of Lucha to practice shooto because you said your lucha was an inferior art. And you are right, your Lucha was inferior, not because of any flaw in the system, but because of a flaw in the fighter. You've done a good job of covering up the flaws, but they remain, and I'll expose them, and then I will exploit them. You thought learning Lucha was hard, Yatsu, wait until you try facing it. (Looking head long into the camera) You think you know real fighting, Yatsu? You don't know one thing about a real fight, but you will shooter... You'll know once I'm done with you. You'll know what real pain is. You'll know what it is to fight for your life. And you'll know one more thing. Masanobu: What is that? Kobe: You'll know what it is to lose, Yatsu. This interview is over. Come on, Honey, let's get our bags and head out. (Kobe takes his wife by his hand and his bag in the other and heads down the terminal as the shot fades to white.) ---- Title: The Struggle Begins... [Twilight. The setting sun streaks the darkening sky above with bright purple, pink and orange highlights. In the twilight sky, the whispy clouds burn bright red, set ablaze by the last rays of the burning sun, adding even more color to the glorious sunset sky. The camera pans swiftly over a vast rolling plain covered with blades of tall, green grass which wave back and forth gently in the light wind that blows across the hills. Finally, the camera comes upon a lone figure standing atop a small hill. He stands amongst the waist-high grass, the open edges of his purple trenchcoat blown open by the same breeze which causes the grass to dance all about him, revealing his trim, yet muscular frame. In his hand, he grasps a single white orchid, which he holds in front of his nose -- seemingly enjoying it's fragrance. As he smells the flower, the breeze blows his hair all around his head and shoulders, making it appear almost as if it were a living thing. After a few moments, he lowers the white flower from in front of his face and opens his large, violet eyes, which peer into the camera's lens, and seemingly into the very soul of the viewer. Finally, he speaks in his calm, yet deep and commanding voice...] At long last, the tournament to crown the JPA's first ever Juniorweight champion is beginning. This week, Junior Struggle will begin, and I will be facing Corey Hart in what will be the first of many matches in that tournament, or should I say, the first of many victories for me... [The corners of his thin lips curl upwards slightly in a knowing smile as he once again partakes of the flower's perfume...] Already, one man has dropped out of the tournament rather than face the failure that would be his Destiny. On the surface, it would appear to be a wise move, but in the end, it will prove futile. Whether it be in this tournament, or at an unspecified time in the future, it matters not. For his Destiny will be the same. The same Destiny he shares with _you_ Corey Hart, and with all the other men participating in this tournament, and that Destiny is to fall before me. [He takes another whiff of the flower's fragrance, his eyes closing in apparent pleasure as he does so...] And I have not forgotten you, Keiji Kobe. You too, will eventually have to face me once more, and in so doing, face your Destiny. The clock may have spared you once, but believe me when I tell you that it was a false mercy. At some point, you must face me again, and when you do, you too will fall. Just as you already should have. Soon enough, you will all have fallen before the Prince of Twilight, and I will be the first ever JPA Juniorweight champion, for that -- is Destiny. Kore wa sakete toore me, anata no unmei -- "You cannot avoid your Destiny." Corey Hart, when the Junior Struggle begins, you shall be the first to enter into the Twilight, and the first of many to leave it... [Once more, he lifts the white orchid to his face to smell the bloom. After inhaling deeply, he lowers the flower back to his side, and resumes speaking...] Forever changed... [With that, the Prince of Twilight turns his back to the camera, and begins to walk away, disappearing behind the hill he stood upon as he does so. As he takes his leave, the camera pans back towards the distant horizon, where the last bit of sun is disappearing below the edge of the Earth, plunging the scene, and the world, into darkness...] ---- [The white words, "Courtesy of Tokyo Organization of Puroresu" fade onto a black screen. They fade out and are replaced by "Eiji Yatsu vs. Shoji Suzuki - 4/28." The initial sounds of static from Garbage's "Dumb" are heard, and as the techno beats begin to pump out footage starts to play… [Suzuki comes off the ropes looking for a flying leg lariat, but Yatsu sees it coming a does a baseball slide underneath. Both men come to their feet quickly in a fighting stance but decide to ease up, circling around the ring near the ropes. The crowd gave a real nice round of applause for the exchange. Cut. [Yatsu takes his time meeting Suzuki back at center ring, trying to gather his thoughts and regroup. Both men face off in striking stances with Yatsu sticking out a few token kicks. They circle briefly before Suzuki looks to continue landing shotays, but this time it appeared as though Yatsu is only trying to draw him in as he quickly closed the distance and takes Suzuki down using a combination running knee and lariat. They jockey for position on the ground until Yatsu is able to get a side mount. From there Yatsu grabs another jujigatame in the blink of an eye. Suzuki frantically works to the ropes to get the break. Cut. [Yatsu is ready to get back to action, barely allowing Suzuki to his feet before charging in with a series of punches to Suzuki's ribcage. Yatsu grabs Suzuki's arm and sprints to the corner and up to the top rope, looking for a shoulder armbreaker, but Suzuki is able to kick his leg out from under him, leaving Yatsu sitting on the top turnbuckle facing the crowd. Suzuki gathers himself before locking on a waist-lock and pulling Yatsu off the top rope and to the mat with a German suplex. Suzuki hangs on and rolled to his feet, bringing Yatsu with him, before hitting another German suplex. Suzuki rolls through once more and lifts Yatsu up for another German suplex but Yatsu is able to counter at the last second, reversing his momentum and countering with a rolling knee bar. The crowd pops big for the sequence as Suzuki fought towards the ropes. Just when it looks as though Suzuki is going to get the break, Yatsu comes to his feet, drags Suzuki back to the center of the ring, and locks the reverse knee bar on once again. The crowd pops huge for this. Suzuki fights hard to the ropes, alternating between using his free leg to crawl and using it to kick at Yatsu. After struggling for nearly a minute he;s able to make the ropes for the dramatic break. Cut. [Suzuki lands a quick kick to Yatsu's stomach before pulling him into powerbomb position and lifting him up for a Spiral Bomb (Spinning Powerbomb). As Suzuki began to spin, Yatsu swings himself around into a sitting position on top of Suzuki's shoulders and hits a modified rolling jujigatame. Cut. [Both men come to their feet very slowly. Suzuki lands a couple of weak forearms across Yatsu's back before hooking on a half nelson, hammerlock combo in preparation for the Suzuki Special. Just as Suzuki gets Yatsu to the peak of the move, Yatsu is able to counter, hooking his feet on Suzuki's hips and executing a forward roll for the pinfall. [The bell rings and Yatsu is announced the winner. He shakes hands with Suzuki before climbing to the top turnbuckle. He throws a power fist, which much of the cheering crowd do in return. A close up shot of Eiji's face is shown and big red welts from Suzuki's shotays cover his face. Yatsu hops off the top turnbuckle and grabs the stick.] Yatsu: Total Attack Puroresu. Not JPA, not TOP, T.A.P. is the ultimate of groups in Nippon. There is no defeating us. This simply was a preview of what is to come in the Junior Struggle League. Suzuki is a good competitor, as are many of the men I will face are, but like all the others, Pure Fight became the end of him. Kobe, I will not be the first man you will defeat on your way to the Pan Asian title. I am just the next member of T.A.P. to destroy you. [The scene fades to Yatsu sitting on the steps in the back. He has just finished his contest with Suzuki, as apparent with his sweat-covered body. He's wearing his ring gear, red, wrestling pants with a silver stripe down each side and black wrestling shoes, light-combat gloves, and judo belt. His breathing is still heavier than usual, but he's calmed greatly since his match with Suzuki.] Yatsu: Respect. I have it for Inoue. I have it for Kangyo. I have it for Suzuki. Every man I face in the Junior Struggle League receives respect from me, that is, except for you, Kobe. You are an arrogant fool, parading around claiming your superiority. You attack me; you attack my honor with nothing to back that up. Where is your honor, Kobe? That pathetic mask you wear to conceal your face? When will you realize you are no longer in Mexico? That mask means nothing here in Nippon. Honor and respect must be earned, not synthesized by putting a piece of cloth over your head. I'll ask you again, where is your honor and respect, Kobe? I'll tell you. You lost it the day you mocked the Steel Wind Dojo. You ridiculed all the history behind one of the greatest dojos in the world. Trying to gain a psychological advantage over Inoue for your Coronation match with him is what you'll claim. When you attack that kind of nobility, Kobe, you will suffer the consequences. You call me a sheep. You claim that the evil Inoue is leading me astray. Look at what I have accomplished since I joined Total Attack Puroresu. You brag about the fact you were able to hold Tasogare no Prince to a draw, while I fell to him in only 20 minutes, therefore I must be a weaker wrestler than you. Wait and see, Kobe, just wait and see. [The camera fades out.] ---- (Scene opens in the back yard of a home in the suburbs of Tokyo. The cherry blossoms are falling from the trees and a small brook is babbling in the background. A sliding door opens and Jason Ramsey comes out in a silk kimono. He is carrying a steaming cup of strong coffee and greets the Japanese morning with a smile and a stretch. His wife, Candise comes up behind him and runs her hand along the back of his head. He turns and smiles.) Jason: I am soooo glad to be home! Working for Mike in Alaska has turned out to be alot of fun and great money, but there is no place like home! Candise: How's your shoulder, baby? Jason: Much better after the hot soak last night. Boy, Lennox really worked me over. It's been three weeks and I'm still sore. Candise: Does your head hurt? Jason: What? Candise: Does your head hurt? Jason: Uh....no...... Candise: It should! You cut off over a foot of hair! I can't believe you didn't tell me! I oughtta kick your ass! (laughs) Still, it's kinda sexy..... Jason: Well, I figured better now than later. This summer, when JPA hits Hokkaido and the rest of the north, it's gonna be HOT! It'll make New Orleans look like Billings, Montana. Candise: Well, I hope your shoulder is better by the time the tour starts. You're almost out of time... Jason: It'll be fine. Besides, my first tour match is an eight man tag so I won't be doing ALL of the ring work myself. Candise: You'll also be in the ring with the best natives in the company. Don't be getting cocky on me Mr. Ramsey. Jason: I wouldn't dream of it Mrs. Ramsey..... (The sound of a phone ringing in the background sends Candise into the house as Jason mumbles something about missing out on the cherry blossoms at their peak. Candise returns carrying the phone and a coffee pot. She refills Jason's cup and hands him the phone. She kisses him on the head before going inside.) Jason: Moshi-Moshi... Takamoto: Good morning Ramsey-san. I trust you had a good tour of Alaska. Ramsey: It was great. I wrestled three big matches and came away 2 and 1. Takamoto: I heard your match with Jim Dalton was fantastic! Hopefully you saved something for the tour. Ramsey: My best work will ALWAYS be in Japanese rings. Takamoto: I just wanted to check in with you and make sure you remember the autograph signing tomorrow at the main offices. Ramsey: I already have a suit picked out. How is Shaw-san by the way? Takamoto: Not only has he recovered, he seems to have regressed in age. He looks fantastic. Ramsey: I'm so excited about tagging with him for this elimination match! Jack has always been a hero of mine, and it's not often that one gets to team with his hero. Takamoto: I also wanted to ask you if you had seen the entire itinerary for the tour. You and Taylor-san are teamed for most of the tour. You two will be in some very big matches. Ramsey: All the better. We are very serious about winning the tag team titles and aa full tour to prepare before the tournament will be a welcome opportunity. We're going to be working with Bill too. I think Fairchild is about to become the superstar that he's always had the potential to be. In my opinion he's in the top three juniors in the world. Mind you, the other two are in the Juniors tourney too. Takamoto: Will I see you before tomorrow? Ramsey: I doubt it, I want to spend as much of today with my family as I can. The wrestling lifestyle is murder on relationships. Takamoto: You are correct. I've been married so many times, I get them confused, and I never wrestled. Ramsey: Yes, but when I'm your age, I won't be able to walk, and if I can, my knees will be factory made! Takamoto: (laughs) I look forward to working with you on this tour. When you come to the offices, bring the tape of your match with Dalton. It would be great to add to the video package we're putting together with Samurai. Ramsey: Consider it done Takamoto-san. I'll see you tomorrow. (Ramsey hangs up the phone, looks around his yard one more time and smiles as he goes back inside.
