JPA Press Conference 2001.2.15: Ryo Inoue

15 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[We are in the press room at Steel Wind Dojo and the press are rabid before a small raised platform. A table with chairs is set up on the platform with a dais and microphone. seated at the table are the steel Wind Dojo "Big Three", founder Keiji Asamiya, head trainer Junji Shirow and top graduate and shoot prodigy Ryo Inoue. Also seated next to Asamiya is Tetsuo Matsumoto, formerly known as Super Tengu in Super Japan Pro Wrestling... Junji Shirow steps to the podium with a smile] Junji Shirow: Ohaiyo Gozaimasu. I thank you all for coming here today. This is NOT a press conference. I can not stress that enough. You are here today at the request of Inoue-san, who has an important announcement. So without further ado I give you Steel Wind Dojo's pride, Ryo inoue! [There is much clapping and several flashbulbs go off as Inoue, newly bald and dressed in a t-shirt and training sweats, shakes Shirow's hand and approaches the podium] Ryo Inoue: Domo Arigatou. These past few eeks have ben a trying time for me. My failure to win the J1 Double titles nearly crushed my will to cimpete. It took many hours of discussion with both Shirow-san and Asamiya-sensei to locate my focus. That being said I am back in the gym and the Dojo training for a possible fight atthe next TATAMI card. With this in mind I need to focus my training towards shooto and not puroresu. I will not divide my training that way and risk being embarrased in a real fight. Keeping this in mind I am dropping my challenge to Masashi Akita's J1 Rikidozan Double titles. They are not a priority at this point as TATAMI is more important than some pieces of metal on a leather strap. Let Genji Yamato find someone else to challenge Akita, let Yamato do it himself since he is so "worthy" as a former holder of the Double Titles. I do not care. I will not abandon my other responsibilities in JPA. I will participate in the Royal Series when not training. I also have this to say to those disenfranchised by the dominance of "Strong Style" in Genji Yamato's Fiefdom. The day of the true fighters is at hand. We have been tread upon too long and overlooked to often. Now is our time to strike and strike fast. Let those who mock our dedication to the pure fight laugh, but it we who shall make them gag on their own bile as they beg to be let out of our submissions. This is the dawn of Total Attack Puroresu [Inoue turns around and displays the logo on his back, red letters with silver borders spell out the word "T.A.P."] Ryo Inoue: The true revolution begins with the Royal Series... [Ryo nods and bows and returns to his seat. Junji Shirow approaches the podium once more] Junji Shirow: [confused] um... I was... wasn't expecting that... um... we at Steel Wind do not approve of... [Shirow is interuppted as Keiji Asamiya looks at him sternyl and shakes his head] Junji Shirow: Nani? [Asamiya nods with closed eyes as Inoue sits next to him] Junji Shirow: We a Steel Wind support T.A.P. entirely [shakes his head in disbelief] Now an announcement from a man we have not seen in a fwe months, Matsumoto-san? [Tetsuo rises from his chair, his eyes fall hard upon both Asamiya and Inoue as he steps to shake Shirow's hand] Tetsuo Matsumoto: Ohaiyo. No doubt many of you have wondered about where I have been these last few months. With failure of GAMMA to even get one show done I was forced to hold off my own retirement tour. Well I am pleased to say that after talks with Genji Yamato, a man whom I respect, my retirement countdown might just happen in JPA. My body is fit and ready for the "FINAL FIGHT FIVE". Already the first fight agreed upon is with my own cousin and master of the Steel Wind Dojo, Keiji Asamiya. The other 4 fights have to be decided, I am hoping Yamato-san will put on the tights for one great farewell match but nothing is sealed just yet. Once I have reached terms with Yamato-san for the date of "FINAL FIGHT 1" the press will be the first to know. Domo Arigatou.
