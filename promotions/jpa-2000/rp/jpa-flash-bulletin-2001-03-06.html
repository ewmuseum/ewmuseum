JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.6

6 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.6 1) Ryo Inoue 2) Tetsuo Matsumoto - Press Release 3) Christopher Kojima 4) Iron Chef Keiji Kobe ---- [We are at the 3.4.2001 Korakuen Hall show. Ryo Inoue and his fellow members of T.A.P. have made their appearance. Footage plays of the brawl between T.a.P. and members of the JPA roster. Inoue maintains his cold stare throughout the entire debacle.] [Fade out] [Fade In] [The letters flash on screen one at a time] T. A. P. Voiceover: Total Attack Puroresu. The War for respect is on... Fear The Violence... [the logo of T.A.P. fades to Ryo inoue in the locker room by himself] Ryo Inoue: [angrily] You all mocked me! "An army of one you" all said. I AM NOT ALONE!! Eiji Yatsu is T.A.P. , Kangyo is T.A.P. We are our own men and bow to no one's authority save our own. [Ryo casts his eyes to the crowd of the media and spots Riki Fudo, formerly of PNM. He grabs the reporter suddenly by the collar and drags him closer] Ryo Inoue: YOU!! FUDO!! You doubt how serious we are? Riki Fudo: Oi!! Ryo, chill out man. It's me Riki neh? [Inoue let's go of Fudo and throws him to the ground] Ryo Inoue: Tonight Togawa gasped for air as I beat him. The battle lines are drawn. Rick Taylor can be the gaijin defender of the Stong Style philosophy all he wants. It won't stop me from making him choke on his own blood. Let the child like Strong Style fighters prove themselves against T.A.P. at Ryogoku Sumo Hall on 3.24. I offer myself, Kangyo and Yatsu to face any team Yamato-san wants to send at us. It is an open challenge. Let us see if there are any men still in this promotion or whether the remaining JPA roster needs to work with some Joshi puroresu groupo more their speed. T.A.P. awaits the ladies to reply... [Ryo turns and storms out] [fade out] ---- To: Yamato Genji, President Japan Puroresu Association From: Tetsuo Matsumoto, soon to be retired wrestler Yamato-san, Please forgive my actions at your 3.4.2001 Korakuenb rally as I actually was not planning to take sides in your internal matter. I am by no means a supporter of Total Attack Puroresu in any way, shape or form. I simply came ot the ring to congratualte the victor in the Inoue/Togawa bout. My only connection to T.a.P. is through my cousin Asamiya-san who is the Sensei at Steel Wind Dojo and Inoue-san's mentor. Everyone knows that I am an ardent supporter of Strong Style and have no interest in shooto at all. This being said I have no plans to involve myself in the problems with your talent. I simply wish to run my retirement tour in JPA for the fans. I have lined up my cousin Keiji Asamiya as my first opponent and he has assured me that the problems between you, he and Ryo Inoue have no impact on the planned match. I also would like to have several competitve matches separate from my retirement tour, mainly tag matches teaming with my freind Masashi Akita or with one of your young lions ( That Togawa boy who fought Inoue impressed me gretly.). i also hope that you are considering my proposal of facing me during my "Final Fight Five!" retirement countdown. I know we could tear down Sumo Hall or even The Tokyo Dome with such a match. I hope to hear from you soon, Tetsuo Matsumoto ---- [Fade into the outside of an unknown building. We see a man standing in front of it, wearing a pair of blue jeans, bomber jacket, and black sunglasses. As we coem closer, we see it is 'Japanese Gaijin' Christopher Kojima.] CK: So, I finally have recieved my first pinfall loss. [We hear something intelligible from behind the camera.] CK: The battle royal doesn't count. But now, I have to team up with two of my former partners to face a rag-tag bunch for a TV taping. First, Kaz. I have no problems with him. He preformed well when Samuel and myself teamed with him. I respect him. [Slight pause.] CK: Yes, I do respect people. Those who deserve respect. Now, on to Eiji Yatsu. Otomo will hopefully take care of you and prove he is the superior one in judo. Then there is Kentaro Ono. A *shooter.* A man who has no respect for this sport. Now, I may have disdain for various people Ono, but I have immense respect for this sport, here and overseas. That, you will learn. [Fade to Black as Kojima walks off.] ---- (The shot opens up onto one of the locker rooms of the Morioka Iwate Prefectural Gym. Dressed in his trademark orange and black mask as well as Orange shirt with the numbers 8, 9, and 3 written on the chest in black. Next to him stands Mituso Masanobu dressed in a suit holding a microphone.) Masanobu: Kobe-san, could I have a word with you? Kobe: Of course my good friend, but I must confess I believe I've been seeing you more often than my own wife. Masanobu: Yes, well try not to get to attracted to me because I don't want you to miss me during our break in scheduling. Kobe: It will be hard, but I shall do my best. (Both men laugh at Kobe's joke as if it were funnier than it is.) Kobe: So why have you brought the camera crew with you to speak to me this time? Masanobu: Well, in the past you have never been one to shield your true feelings on a certain subject, I hope this time is no different. Kobe: I assume you refer to the war of words going on between the most esteemed JPA and the outlaw organization know as TAP. Masanobu: You know me too well, Iron Chef. Kobe: Yes, well during this tour there has been much talk and boosting about styles. There are some that suggest that Strong Style of Puroresu is the best in the world, others say Shooto is the best. One side will make a boast, the other counters with insults and a boast of their own. It has gone on like this for some time, the two factions shouting at one another like an old married couple. To tell the truth I am getting a little tired of the stagnation, so if you will allow me the pleasure, I'd like to change the dynamic slightly. Masanobu: Please do. Kobe: I'll be the first to admit I'm biased, but speaking for myself and We Hyun Bae, we believe Lucha Liebre is the finest wrestling style on earth. Masanobu: Once again, Iron Chef, you have taken us deep into controversy. Kobe: I guess that is the case, but to me there is little cause for controversy. I've wrestled this style for some time, before I practiced Lucha I worked the strong style, and before that I was some what of a Judo man in high school. But I abandoned both style in favor of Lucha Liebre because I believe it is the best style of wrestling today. Masanobu: But what evidence do you have for this other than your own personal feelings and experience? Kobe: It is no secret that a rival strong style promotion headed by widow is having a hard time filling it's venues. Masanobu: True. Kobe: And from what I can piece together from my friend Hart-san, there is an American promotion with the same trouble. Masanobu: Hai. Kobe: Well do you know the solution the American company took to improve it's situation. Masanobu: Hai, but please tell our viewers. Kobe: The American promotion sought to hire students of the Ultimo Dragon to increase viewership. This American promotion looked to Lucha to improve it's situation. Masanobu: I fail to see how this shows the superiority of the Lucha style. Kobe: Perhaps you are right, all I have so far is proof of Lucha's universal appeal. Masanobu: It would not be the first time the public took style over substance. Kobe: But you see, that is my point, with Lucha is not a question of either sport or spectacle. Lucha is both. The breath taking leaps a luchadore performs is not only impressive, but effective. If you need further proof look at my record. I've scored many a upset in my time, and what better testament is there than that? Still, you may have a point, Lucha Still has much to prove to the Japanese sporting establishment in many's eyes. That is why I am so happy about my up coming match with Tora Ryoku and Shiro Oodora of team Tiger Claw. Here are two men that embody the best Strong Style have to offer. They work hard and stiff, and I know from first hand experience the depths of Tora Ryoku's fighting spirit. Masanobu: Hai, he is a truly great competitor. Kobe: Yes, well do not overlook myself and Bae. Certainly we lack the size of Team Tiger Claw. Indeed, experience as a team, the key to Hart-san and I's victory over no Prince and Ketiak is equal when compared with the experience of Tiger Claw. So what you are left with is two very evenly matched teams, so the way this match will be determined is by who has the better style, who wants it more, who trained harder. Masanobu: It is a tough one to call. Kobe: Tough one to call, try a tough one to fight. Bae and myself know full well we are in for an uphill battle all the way in this one. But we are Luchadores of the highest order, so this sort of battle is nothing new to us. Masanobu: While your skills are quite vaulted, there have been some voices who question Bae's skill. Kobe: Yes, what is they have called him? A "Want-to-be Luchadore" is it? Well better wanting to move forward then trying to dwell on the past. Bae isn't the greatest wrestler in the world, but he is far from the worst, and what he loses in improper technique he makes up for in passion. Bae represents the finest of our craft. A young man, passionate about the sport, a true student of the game. His spirit is without bounds, and he's a tough man to beat at any time. Shiro Oodora, Tora Ryoku , it's time you learn of the true flavor of Lucha Liebre. You'll be first to sample what two Iron Luchadores are really capable. Bae and I are coming to win this weekend, and we are not ones to lower our expectations. Get ready, Oodora, Ryoku can tell you about what spirit I posses. And as for my Sous Chef, well Ryoku, let's just say I'm glad he's on my side and not yours. Masanobu: Thank you for your time once again, oh Iron Chef. Kobe: A pleasure as always.
