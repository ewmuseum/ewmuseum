JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.23

23 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.23 1) Kathakano 2) Ketiak 3) Masanobu Akazi 4) MASSIVE Akita 5) Tasogare no Prince 6) Ryo Inoue 7) Kenji Okano 8) Shinji Nakata ---- [The screen opens to a shot of ambulances, fire trucks, and police cars stationed in front of an apartment building. As we pan upward to the rooftop, we see the same figure from the church, Kathakano.] "Ah, raised above civilization, the way it was meant to be. The beings superior to the scum and dregs of humanity live above it, casting their cold, harsh stares, silently judging, and waiting to strike when it feels that they've had too much." "I choose to be higher than the rest because society just cannot bear the burden of my face and presence on their backs. So, I sit, cloaked in my pallium, pondering, contemplating my next victim." [Kathakano removes his cloak to reveal a pair of leather pants and a black t-shirt, sporting an ankh symbol.] "Sometimes, I'd just like to go away, but I'd feel that I've let them win. I'd let them succeed in robbing me of the task I was sent to do. Providing eternal enmity for all people, man, woman, child, infant. My next task is to dispatch the bane of this company, Keiji Kobe, the so called "Iron Chef"." "El Chupacabras. Bah! Kobe, I think you can do better than that. Animals of this earth are the lowest of the low. Why do you think all people consume them like the droplets of water? How dare you compare me to that. I think the harsh reality of my bare hands ripping apart your jugular vein will bring you down to earth. Your crimson blood will spill on the cursed ground of this shallow world, and into my life force. Your Mexican lore and tales of years past cannot compare to the unspeakable horror that lies ahead of you, perched in a state of total cold blood, ready to destroy you in the methods predetermined by the ones who came before me." [Kathakano produces a 8x10 action shot of Keiji Kobe chopping an unsuspecting competitor between the eyes.] "This visage may be the only relic your supporters may have of you after I am through pillaging your body." [Kathakano nonchalantly tosses it off the roof onto the horde of ambulances.] "Visum et Repertum, Kobe. Vobiscum Satanas." [Kathakano puts his pallium back on, and walks off into the night. The camera pans down to the front of the apartment building. A reporter is show giving her analysis.] "This is a shocking series of events, a high profile defense attorney slain in his own home, no murder weapon found, only his jugular vein severed with what appears to be a long nail. More news as this develops........" [The camera fades to a shadow in the flashing lights of the scene. Fade to black...] Master of sin Take my cursed heart Bring me where I can find salvation For I am the damned ---- Title: Tanka [In the begining, there was darkness. Yes indeed, for all we see is a pitch-black screen. As boredom, confusion or fear wells up from the beings of those who are anticipating the image metamorphosis of the still-blank screen, a voice booms from within the darkness - the voice of One Who Calls From the Wilderness, Ketiak. Its tone is soft yet commanding, intense yet controlled, as words began to form.] V/O: Blooming Rafflesia... [The screen at last becomes alive as a photo of a giant Rafflesia gradually dithers on the screen. It is a majestic flower in its monstrousity - red petals with white blotches embedded in it, a good foot and a half long in circumference, yet repugnant in smell.] V/O: climax of fulfilled life glow... [A fade-in, fade-out sequence occurs, a manifestation of the transition of this photographic slide show. We then see a photo of the same flower, albeit from a slightly further distance. It is blooming proud and defiant, as if the treacherous surrounding rainforests are but a dream. Yet, we are cruelly reminded that it blooms for only once in a lifetime, and what we are seeing is but its zenith.] V/O: The distant sunset... [The sequence repeats itself, and we now view a photo of the same Rafflesia flower, albeit we notice the darker background - a signal of the day transcending into night, the sign of the sunset. The beauty of the twilight beckons, the habitat of Destiny herself is made a welcome abode.] V/O: brings dimness to sad petals... [After another bout of repetitiveness, we come across a similiarly coloured background. However, we see the Rafflesia in the latter stages of its pathetic existence. In its degradation, we realize how hopeless the life-cycle, about how cruel Mistress Destiny is. Even in the atmosphere we can sense the malevolent air of hoplessness and resignation in the face of inevitability.] V/O: as it withers eclisped black... [The Rafflesia dies as this tanka - a Japanese form of poetry - concludes. With nary cathartism nor significance. Alas, in its invinsibility of beauty, it is ultimately reminded of its frailty. The futility of going against Destiny is again highlighted. A lesson is learned as the scene fades to black... ...and a new scene opens to virginal white blank background, in contrast to the earlier scenes. We are perplexed, as this is not a place associated with interviews of champions, nor is it a place resembling the cellars where Tribal Madness, Ketiak resides. Neverthless, we see the camera zooming in to the squat powerful figure of Ketiak. Clad in nothing but his tribal war costume (comprising of head dress, war paint and loincloth), his face is contorted to a mask of intensity, his eyes fierce enough to scare demons. He closes his eyes for an instant as his head faces the ground, as if deep in thought. In measures of moments, he looks up again, preserving his intensity but softening his glare, he eyes the camera and speaks...] Ketiak: Destiny... (His voice trials for an instant) Misteress of misteresses. She is my friend, She is my aid, She is my lover. My solace is in her dwellings of the Twilight, whose secrets I'd discovered from Tasogare no Prince. (Yet another brief pause) It was the embrace of Destiny that brought me into the JPA. It IS the embrace of Destiny that brings me to face my fellow brethen very soon in the squared circle. Brethen - many had fallen to the Malaysian Assasin, stumbled by the trickery of Destiny. Rest assured, that all of you would have the same priviledges. (Ketiak's smiles a small smile) You see, by being drawn in facing me, you set off a chain of events. A chain of events that will eventually bring upon your sorry demise. I'm sorry to say that sorrow and agony will be coming your way real soon. [Ketiak blinks for a moment and continues.] I sense doubt in you brethen - about my harping on Destiny, about my seemingly harmless rantings. It is in good reason, for what manner of man knows Destiny, much less control Her? (Ketiak shrugs for a moment before relegating his voice just above whisper, soft but malovolent in nature) But when you face the Demigod of Deviousness, reasoning is futile. Destiny alone did not bring me to step above this pedestal I am in today - a former UEW Cruiserweight Champion, the one who broke both "Sexy" Rex Tatum and Victor Dougan's worthless necks. [Ketiak then gives his trademark sadistic grin, as if relieving his glorious bloody accomplishments. With a slight flicker of growing bloodlust in his eyes he continues...] No, it was the skills, the killer instinct, the tenacity that brought me this far. (Brief pause) However, all this is secondary come the following days... For I will back up my words by plunging you in through all seven levels of hell, and I would revel in all of your feelings of fear, your screams of pain. I will beat you all down again and again and again, till you can't get up to take it any longer. Brethen... You will all suffer the onslaught of Tribal Madness, and you would suffer levels of pain that only my gleeful hands will bestow. Brethen... YOUR - HEAD - IS - MINE!!! He sits in a candle-lit dojo; a sheathed sword in front of him. His mode of dress is simple: a large, design-laden kimono, that sits open, exposing part of his rather large (somewhere between corpulent and muscular) chest. His hair, black with some graying, is tied back, like a sumo wrestler's traditional style. He kneels, as if in prayer, however there is no altar to be found here. Just a sword, and candles, and a hard wooden floor. And the Double Titles Champion, MASSIVE Akita.] Inoue…all I have heard from you, in recent weeks, are the words of someone spurned. You, so assured of your victory over me at the Sumo Hall earlier this month, yet found those assurances squashed in less than an hour's time against myself. And so…you hide your true feelings against me in a veil of respect, Inoue; yet, you have gone on to insult this promotion, insult Yamato-san amidst barking not unlike a young whelp in a litter of dogs. You then, after making much of a show of challenging me, in front of the Sumo Hall, retract your challenge, still claiming to have respect for me. Inoue, I tell you this now: you have insulted Yamato-san. You have insulted the Japan Puroresu Association. You have insulted Strong Style. Yet you still claim respect towards me. Then why retract your challenge, Inoue? Did you not think that I would take that, too, as an insult? Did you not think for even a glimmer of a moment, the disrespect you have done to myself, and the Double Titles, by declaring yourself to be above them? Do not claim respect for me, Inoue. For all you have done *is* disrespect me, and disrespect puroresu by your words. And it seems…this old man, will need to teach his younger about respect, and about true fighting spirit. The type that does not allow you to go back on your words, and speak carelessly about respect at the same time. Inoue…you WILL know what respect is, at the start of the Royal Series… [Fade….] ---- [The scene is completely white. Slowly, blurry edges appear which then grown sharper as the previously undefined whiteness begins to take on the outline of what appears to be some sort of flower. The image continues to grow more and more defined until finally the scene is focussed upon an extreme close-up of a single white orchid. The orchid quivers slightly before beginning to rise from its place in the center of the screen, borne upon a now visible hand. The camera pans upwards and around, as the hand raises the white orchid upwards to a pale face. The thin, delicate hand holds the white orchid directly beneath a small, upturned nose, and the corners of a thin-lipped mouth directly below the nose curl upward slightly in pleasure. Finally, the camera pulls out slightly to reveal the pale, handsome face of Tasogare no Prince -- the Prince of Twilight, his eyes closed in apparent pleasure as he partakes of the flower's fragrance. His large, violet eyes open as he lowers the flower from in front of his face to focus upon something in the distance. The camera continues to pan as it pulls out farther from the Prince's face revealing his long, silvery hair which flows gracefully all about his head and shoulders -- across a vast rolling plain covered with tall, waving grass -- finally focussing upon the horizon, where the setting sun is streaking the darkening sky above with the bright purple, pink and orange colors of the twilight. After taking in the breathtaking scene for a few moments, the camera once again pans back to the form of Tasogare no Prince, who stands amongst the waist-high grass, smelling of the white orchid. Eventually, his wide, violet eyes lock upon the lens the camera, and he begins to speak in his calm, deep, and strangely soothing voice...] Firstly, my congratulations to you, Keiji Kobe. Not many man can say that they were able to take the measure of the Prince of Twilight. Omedettoo, tomodachi. However, I can not help but wonder -- you may well have been able to fend off the challenge of Tasogare no Prince for 30 minutes, but could you for 45? An hour?? I would be most interested in finding out... [The Prince smiles knowingly as he smells once again of the delicate white flower in his hand...] But perhaps, I already know the answer to that question, ne? Just as you most assuredly do, Kobe. But at this time, I find the more pressing matter at hand is you, Eiji Yatsu, for it is you whom I must pit myself against this coming week. I have heard your comments concerning both myself, and about Destiny, this past week, Yatsu-san, and I must say, I am not surprised. After all, how can you possibly be expected to understand the workings of Destiny? [another whiff of the orchid -- another slight smile...] It is only natural that you should have a limited understanding of Destiny, Yatsu-san. You, like so many others, think Destiny is limited only to those good things that occur to you. You are unable, or unwilling to realize that Destiny is both the good, and the bad. When you triumph, it must be Destiny. When you fail, it is nothing more than circumstance or bad luck. But you are wrong. [Again, the Prince of Twilight smells the orchid in his hand...] Everything that happens to us -- our triumphs and our failures -- is Destined for us. From the moment we enter this world, our path is pre-determined. I am no exception. I am only capable of seeing that which Destiny has in store for us. Not even I, the chosen of Destiny, can change that for which we are all fated. [Another whiff of the orchid, and the Prince's melancholy expression fades to one of smug satisfaction...] How fortunate for me, that Destiny has chosen me for greater things, ne? Some of us are fated for glory and honor, where others are Destined to fall. Much like you Eiji Yatsu. It will be your Destiny to fall before me -- a Destiny you share with the rest of the Jr. Weight division. There is no shame in it. You will merely be fulfilling the role that Destiny has written for you. In fact, that is all that any of us can do. Kore wa sakete toore me, anata no unmei -- "No one can avoid their Destiny..." Eiji Yatsu, you have been chosen for a very special Destiny. You shall be the first to enter into the Twilight, and the first to leave it... [He smells the orchid once more, closing his eyes in pleasure as he does so...] Forever changed... [With that, the Prince of Twilight turns and leaves, walking off through the tall, gently moving grass as the camera pans back once again to the horizon, where the very last bit of the sun is disappearing below the far-off edge of the earth, plunging the world, and the scene, into blackness...] ---- [open camera shot, one by one the letters flash on the screen to solitary drum beats] T. Nakata is swimming laps in a spacious pool, and as he notices the camera's presence, he changes direction in order to meet the camera at the nearest side. I know that this will be my biggest test since moving up to Heavyweight, yet I look forward to it confidently. If he wants to integrate a new element to the sport, I welcome that. I will continue to fight on in the tradition of my father, proving that my brand of Strong Style Puroresu is the best...
