JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.5.7

7 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.5.7 1) Ryo Inoue 2) Christopher Kojima 3) Eiji Yatsu ---- The letters appear on screen. Big Silver and Red...] T. A. P. Voice Over: Total Attack Puroresu, The War For Respect Is On, Fear Our Violence... [open cmaera shot] [We are in a small training facility. Against one white wall is a banner marking the kanji of the Steel Wind Dojo. In the center of the room 6 speed bags have been set-up before a young man dressed in a red gi. That man is Eiji Yatsu. Walking in from a door on the opposite side of the room is a man dressed in red sweats. He wears a whistle around his neck and is drenched in sweat. This man is Ryo Inoue. He walks upto to Yatsu and gets right in his face] Ryo Inoue: [sternly] You did well. I thought Kobe would finish you. I was sure all that knowledge and training I poured into you was going to go to waste. [Inoue pauses and smiles slyly] you proved me wrong. [Yatsu cracks a slight smile] Ryo Inoue: [sees the smile and snaps to immeadiate anger] DON'T SMILE! WHAT GIVES THAT RIGHT? DID YOU BEAT HIM? DID YOU CHOKE OUT THAT WORTHLESS IRON CHEF? WELL DID YOU? Eiji Yatsu: [softly, yet with resolve] No Ryo Inoue: [lowering his voice to a menacing whisper] No, that's right, you didn't. But that' ok. Neither of us could have forseen Kobe going Strong Style on you and all things considered you adjusted well. But I'm warning you Yatsu-kun, DO NOT GET COCKY! Billy Fairchild is next. If you underestimate I promise you that you will lose. [Inoue takes a deep breath] Let us begin the training. STANCE!! [Ryo steps aside as Yatsu takes his fighting stance] Ryo Inoue: KOBE! [Yatsu unleashes a stiff kick on the first speed bag. He stars to brin his kicking leg down] Ryo Inoue: FAIRCHILD! [As Yatsu's leg hits the mat he spins his body around and strikes the second speed bag with a wicked uraken] Ryo Inoue: KATHAKANO! [Yatsu finds himself exposed as his back is ot the next bag. He quickly snaps a rear elbow strike on the speed bag as a reflex and quickly turns to face the next bag] Ryo Inoue: TASOGARE NO PRINCE! [Yatsu unleashe a sick straight punch on the bag] Ryo Inoue: No! NO! NO! [he walks in front of Yatsu and grabs the still outstretched punching arm] That is wrong! WRONG! You overextended your reach! There was motion but no snap. YOU MUST THROW WITH FORCE AND SNAP THE PUNCH! Yatsu: But... Ryo Inoue: NO! There is no but! You snap the punch to stun the opponent to set up the knee strike or leg shoot. How many times must we do this before you get it right? Did that fluke over Shoji Suzuki make you forget your basic kata? Is this how you expect to fight Fairchild? If it is you might as well not show up and hand th at Pan Asia title to Kobe! TO KOBE! EVEN HE CAN DO THIS BASIC KATA! NO DO IT AGAIN! [Yatsu returns to the first bag, Inoue stands there watching intently as his protege begins again] [fade out] ---- [Fade into the hotel room of one Christopher Kojima. The Asian-American wrestler is currently on the phone.] CK: Yeah, exactly. Of course. Did the presents get there? Cool. OK. Hey, the cameras are in the room, gotta' cut a promo. Love ya'. [He hangs up the phone and turns to the camera.] CK: Quite the week I have coming up. A shooter, German, rookie, and Hart. Let's go down the list, shall we? [Pause.] CK: First, Eiji Yatsu. The Shooter. The man who says he will destroy the Junior Division. Well, Eiji. You're gonig to have a small problem with that. I'm no Junior. I never will be. You see, I don't care if you like being in shoot fights more than in the JPA. However, don't bitch and whine about how TAP is so much greater. If it's so great, have your career there. Then again, you probably want to get paid, so that's why you stay here. [Pause.] CK: Then there's Jaeger. This German wrecking machine. Jaeger, at the last supershow before our break, I admit, you destroyed me. But that was then, this is now. I've been in training, Gustav. I won't be a new toy to break apart this time bud. [Pause.] CK: Red Tiger. I know little about you. When I kick your ass, I'll proably know little more. [Pause.] CK: Corey Hart. The Original American Jackass. Hart, I don't like you. You're the ebodiment of America I hate. The pretty-boy high-flyer I hoped would get thrown to the earth when I watching NWA TV. You probably don't like me. But Corey, you can't back up what you say. I can. [Fade to Black] ---- [The screen fades into a black screen. The letters “T.A.P.” appear.] Voice Over: The war for respect is on. Victory will be ours. [Suddenly it cuts to a view outside the T.A.P. dressing room after the Yatsu/Kobe match. The door is half-open and yells can be heard coming from inside. One of the many reporters outside the dressing room knocks, which pushes the door all the way open. Yatsu, not noticing, picks up a chair a throws it at the wall, startling the young T.A.P./Steel Wind Dojo trainees. A look of anger covers Inoue’s face, but Yatsu’s is more of frustration. Kangyo is sitting in a chair across the room seemingly docile. Yatsu finally sees the camera and the reporters and some anger does come to him. Eiji comes to the door, holding onto the wall to support himself, obviously fairly exhausted from his match only minutes ago. Closer up, it can be observed the red marks that cover his body from the brutal contest.] Yatsu: I will give you guys a few minutes, after that, no more questions. Reporter: [Off camera] Just tell us what everyone wants to know. What do you have to say about Kobe? Yatsu: [He looks back at Inoue for a second, then to the camera.] As much as my teacher hates to even hear his name uttered, I’m glad you said that you said Kobe, not the “Iron Chef.” I said before tonight I had no respect for Kobe; now I must correct myself. While there is a complete void for respect towards the “Iron Chef” there is some for Kobe. Reporter: But aren’t they the same person? Yatsu: No, of course not. The “Iron Chef” was a dancing fool that knew nothing about puroresu. He goes on claiming the superiority of lucha libre, of his greatness. The man I faced was not the “Iron Chef.” He did not use his gymnast moves, he didn’t hid is face. I faced Kenji Kobe, the man, not the comedic “wrestler.” While some within T.A.P. will look down on the strong-style he used tonight, I realize that the Junior Struggle League has moved beyond the petty quarrel of whether shooto, strong-style, or lucha is the supreme art. The JSL is about whom among the junior heavyweights has the highest skill level. Who the true ultimate wrestler is. As much as I hate to admit it, tonight showed that both Kobe and I were wrong. We are fairly equal and it will have to wait to another day to discover who is better. Reporter: Do you have anything else to say to Kenji Kobe? Yatsu: Our match is over. I have much better things to worry about than a war of words with Kobe. I will not mention Kobe again until the day comes when we fight again. [Looks back at Inoue again.] You guys have one more question, make it good. Reporter: Your next match in the Junior Struggle League is against Corey Hart. What comments do you have about this match up? Yatsu: Hart also started out his quest for the Pan Asian title tonight with a result that he disliked. We’re both going to be working hard for our first win, but I believe that Pure Fight will prevail. While somewhat blemished, I will live up to my promise to win the JSL without a loss. Victory will be mine. [Yatsu steps back and shuts the door. Almost immediately after, the T.A.P. logo flashes across the screen and the picture opens up with Eiji standing by a white wall, assumingly a day or two after the tour opener due the fact that the red marks on his face have faded. He’s in street clothes, red cargo pants and a sleeveless silver shirt with the “YATSU” four pointed star “T” logo across the chest. Riki Fudo can be heard off camera giving last minute orders to the cameraman.] Fudo: Yatsu, I’d like to congratulate you on your hard fought match against Kobe on the sixth. You’re labeled as a spoiler in the Junior Struggle League and you certainly fit that role two days ago. Yatsu: [Slightly enraged.] I thank you; it really lived up to the billing. However, I must address the fact that everyone in the media continues to keep me at “spoiler” status. What does everyone think the final result of the round robin will be? In my mind there are only three men that really have a chance to advance to the finals, me being one of them. What do I have to do end this misconception? Fudo: If I may be so bold, I suggest that you must win in your contests against Kenji Okano and Tasogare no Prince. Yatsu: [A little surprised at Fudo’s answer.] It is my belief that the only way to guarantee that I reach the finals is to hold victories every single one of my remaining matches. As talented as all the competitors in the JSL are, we all know that my last true test will be up against Tasogare no Prince. It’s funny how the biggest matches of the JSL will be done in the first couple weeks. Fudo: You often criticized others for claiming their superiority, but right now, and in the past, you’re doing the same exact same thing. Yatsu: You speak the truth, but on everyone’s list for possible final standings no one has anyone I face from now on, besides Tasogare, anywhere near the top. Fudo: You’re sounding very confident, possibly overly. Yatsu: I am extremely confident. Inoue has busted me down to the lowest level, but he has rebuilt me as a better fighter. More skill, more confidence, more of everything that it takes to reach my zenith. Fudo: Quite obviously you don’t have much assertion of the skills of Kenji Okano. Are you over looking him? Yatsu: No, I don’t believe I am. I respect him and his abilities, but if anyone is overlooking anyone, it is KAYO of me. I laughed when I viewed his interview before the start of tour. I speak of big goals for myself, but I’m not a fool like Okano. Like all others he doesn’t actually see me as a threat. KAYO’s head is much too big and it will lead to his defeat. Fudo: Do you have anything to say to Tasogare no Prince, or about your up-coming match? Yatsu: Tasogare no Prince, I can’t blame you. You, like all others still believe that I am the same man you faced at the beginning of last tour. That defeat combined with my inability to capture a win against Fairchild finally unclouded my eyes. No longer am I using a modified version of your lucharesu, it is only Pure Fight. You must think that after your match at the next TV taping it will be a cakewalk to the finals. I am the man to show that it will be a long road. My progression will be your undoing.
