JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.1

1 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.1 1) Ryo Inoue: Post Match 2) Billy Fairchild 3) Rick Taylor 4) Gustav Jaeger 5) Iron Chef Keiji Kobe ---- [post match, Inoue stands alone. He hasn't eve wiped down from the match as he meets the press] Ryo inoue: Let Akita stand there and wave like an idiot saying he beat me. I wasn't pinned and I can say with confidence he won't do so for the whole tour. Akita claims that my respect for him was false? Fine! Let Masashi Akita be blind. Let him stand with Genji Yamato and try to stifle the pure fighters. Let the respect I was trying to show him blow away in the wind. The War For Respect continues and tonight was the beginning. Rick Taylor I'm coming ot crush your nose. Yuzo Moroboshi I come to show you what a fight really is. Shini Nakata... will suffer... The War Is On. I will earn the shot to face Akita on the next tour and not simply challenge him like a child. It is not his titles I want but his repect and I will have it... [Inoue tuns and walks off] ---- [Camera opens on a head & shoulders shot of a sweaty Billy Fairchild in front of a wall in the back hallways of Differ Ariake as cameras are snapping a few pictures and reporters are fielding questions to him one at a time.] Reporter 1: Fairchild-San, you must be happy with your performance tonight. Returning to JPA with a big singles win. Fairchild: Absolutely. I think I finally showed a bit of my true potential tonight and what I'm capable of. I've wrestled EIYUU countless times already and I think I sealed the deal on who the better man is. Reporter 2: You used an interesting variation of the crossface to achieve your victory. Fairchild: Yeah, that's a hold I picked up from watching Carl Malenko over in BattleArts. It's a modified Crippler Crossface with added pressure on the arm of your opponent with a chicken-wing which is held in place with your own leg so that you can pull up with both arms and apply full pressure to the cross face as your opponent is laying on his belly. Reporter 3: How do you feel about your upcoming tour dates? Fairchild: Good. I'm mostly in tag matches with my fellow Alliance members and Jack Shaw. So, being the percieved weak link on the team, I'm sure all my opponents are going to try and single me out for the victory but I'm okay with that. I'll be sure to carry my own weight and it will be an honor to hook up with a true legend in this sport like MASSIVE Akita on a couple occasions. Reporter 4: You'll also be facing the tag team of Samuel Otomo and Christopher Kojima on back to back nights. Kojima had some unkind things to say about you a few days ago. Fairchild: [grins] Well I'm sorry I didn't live up to Mr. Kojima's expectations. Excuse me for having a softer side outside of the ring Mr. Kojima. [The reporters laugh at that last sarcastic statement.] But if he thinks I'm some sort of wussy push-over, that's just an extra advantage on my side because I can assure you Kojima, I am NO push-over. And basically, you get what you give in this sport. I did nothing to Kojima and he feels the need to disrespect me. What for? To make a name for himself? [Billy rolls his eyes and says ...] Whatever. Kojima doesn't realize it but he just bought himself a one way ticket to a world of pain when he gets in the ring against me for the first time. A world ... of pain. [The reporters laugh at the way Billy over-dramatically said his last statement for comedic effect.] I am perfectly capable of respecting my opponents abilities and wrestling straight up ... but I can also be a real dick when provoked. [The reporters laugh at Billy's frankness.] So lace 'em up tight Kojima 'cause if you don't, I will knock you clean out of your boots. Reporter 5: Fairchild-san, your next opponent on TV will be EIji Yatsu. What are your thoughts on him? Fairchild: Truthfully, that will probably be one of my toughest matches on the tour. I have very little background in shoot style fighting and Yatsu has an extensive Judo background I believe. I've been working on my submissions lately and I'm going to study Yatsu's matches this coming week very closely to prepare for our match. Reporter 5: Okay, thank you very much Fairchild-san. And good luck to you. Fairchild: Your welcome. See you guys at the next TV taping I guess. [Fairchild grins and walks off to the locker room.] ---- [Night. Rick Taylor is standing outside Korakuen Hall. He is wearing blue jeans, boots, a Mean Jimmy Dattaglia memorial t-shirt, and a green and black leather jacket with the Alliance logo on the back. He looks at the camera and he begins to speak.] Taylor: Ryo Inoue, you know, you like to talk a big game. You like to talk about what a great fighter you are, and you like to talk about how you beat me in the Coronation tournament. In your promos you taunt me, like a schoolyard punk, taunting me with the fact that you broke my nose, that you beat me in the tournament. Whoa! Big deal! Inoue, it's not like you're the first guy to break my nose. I've had my nose broken 5 times before you did your damage. You taunt me by asking me if it drives me mad that your shooto style beat me. You got lucky that night, Inoue. You just happened to edge me out, just barely, that night. I have more important things to worry about than the fact that I lost to you in the tournament. You want to know how much that loss bothered me? I shook your hand and congratulated you on a hard fought victory, but I also warned you that it would be the last one. Your ego has betrayed you, Inoue. Your ego took a brutal blow when you lost to Akita-san in the finals and you couldn't handle it. Now you run like a scared child, because you fear another defeat at his hands. If you did not, you would not have dropped out of your initial challenge. Don't give me your BS story about changing your priorities or any of the other garbage you've been spewing as of late. You are running scared and you can't stand that men like myself and Kobe are pointing your cowardice out to you, making you face it when all you want to do is run and hide. Well, Inoue, we will meet at least once on this tour. I will not hold back. I am not fighting for tha honor of this company or for Akita-san, or Yamato-san. They are more than capable of doing that themselves. I am fighting you for the mere fact that you are seriously pissing me off as of late. I want another crack at you, and this one will not be like our first meeting, oh no. You claim how great you are all you want. You are nothing but a coward hiding behind all you have left, your mediocre fighting skills. I have dedicated myself to strong-style, and you cannot resist it. No matter how hard you try and put me down, Inoue, there is one thing that is completely alien to you, and it is the fact that you lack this very thing that causes you to eb such a coward that you are. If you had it, you would not have withdrawn your challenge. You would not be blaming Yamato-san for your failures and short-comings. You would not be running to rotting arms of an organization that is all but dead while you try and suck the marrow from the JPA, the very hand that feeds you. You are parasite, Inoue. You use the JPA as a springboard for your dying organization. Yamato-san has done his best to promote you and how do you repay him? With insults and accusations of favoritism. I was unaware that this had suddenly become the Japan chapter of the WWF, but you seem intent on turning it into such. You are not worth my time to worry about the fact that I lost to you. Yes, I lost, but why would I torment myself over it? You are not worthy of such an investment of my time in such a pointless endeavor. However, I am intent on meeting you once again in the ring and defeating you, with a definite strong-style victory. I am not disrespecting shooto, because it is a serious discipline, but I am insulting you, Inoue, because you are no exemplary fighter. You are a cowardly fighter, and a cowardly manipulator outside of the ring. I refuse to be manipulated into your trap. Because I have one advantage over you now that you seem to have lost any semblance you ever had to it: you lack HEART, Inoue. No matter how you stretch me, not matter how to twist my joints or pull my muscles, you will never break my spirit, Inoue, because you are not powerful enough to make me give up. There is no man alive who is powerful enough to break my spirit. And when you fluster from the frustration of your inability to put me down and out, then I will make my play and it is then that I will move in for my kill. And you will not escape, Inoue. Because I do not want to just beat you. I will beat you into unconsciousness, until you cannot fight anymore, for then I will have truly beaten you. You talk about the "pure fight,"but how pure can your fight be when you are beaten into quivering mass of flesh by, according to you, an "inferior" fighter? I do not need chokeholds and punches. I am able to defeat you a hundred other ways, Inoue. And you'll never see them coming, either. Not until it's too late, but by then, it won't matter. I will have already won, and you will have failed, AGAIN. This friday, watch me team with Shaw as we face Shinji Nakata and Yuzo Moriboshi as I send a message to you, Inoue. I am not a man to be taken lightly. If you want to try and prove me wrong, your welcome to it, but watch how I handle Nakata and Moriboshi. They will regret having signed on, because every match of this tour for me will be a message to you that you will get what is coming to you. It's coming close to time for payback, my friend. You won't like it. [Fade to black, then into a green flame, from which a neolithic looking ALLIANCE logo bursts from and stops amid the flames. Fade to black.] ---- (The camera fades in backstage at Tokyo Korakuen Hall, site of the next JPA BattleStart program. Front and centre we are treated to the sight of Gustav Jaeger warming up for his match against Inochi Ichihara. Today he's doing sumo style exercises, hitting vicious palm strikes against the concrete building supports. As he sees the camera, he starts talking but doesn't stop exercising.) GJ: Soon there will be another edition of Battle Star und once again I will be called to wrestle. However, this time my opponent is not a worthless one like the despicable Goshin Star. Nein, this time my opponent actually posesses some honour and fighting spirit. This time my opponent is the ever mysterious Inochi Ichihara. (Gustav launches a flurry of palm strikes that hit the post with astounding force, the echoes of the slaps echoing throughout the room.) GJ: Not much is known about Herr Ichihara. He wrestles in a mask and no one can tell what he looks like nor where he comes from. Some wonder if he is a Japanese man or if he is foreign to Japan, a gaijin like myself. All I need to know is that he is a very accomplished grappler and that he is almost as big as myself. (For the first time in this promo, Gustav looks into the camera and he has a large smile upon his face. It is clear that he enjoys the challenge that Ichihara might provide him.) GJ: A great submission oriented wrestler it is said, with a vast technical repetoire of moves. I will truly enjoy facing you Herr Ichihara. For you see, though it may not seem like it when you look at me, I too have a great technical repetoire. Ja, it is true that I use many power moves when I wrestle, since I am so big and strong it would be foolish for me not to. But I am very proud of the technical wrestling knowledge that I posess. I hope to match it with yours to see how well we compare. (Gustav's grin gets wider at the thought and it's very obvious that he's waiting with almost breathless anticipation for the match.) GJ: Possibly an opponent who is worthy, avery good possibility. Not a corporate whore like Goshin Star was, oh no, a wrestler who actually understands the value of fighting spirit. I truly hope you live up to your reputation Herr Ichihara. Auf Widersehn. (Camera fade to black.) ---- (Darkness, the kind of darkness you could cut with a knife, but to what point, as the night would only envelope you further. Draging you into an abiss devoid of light and life. Ok, maybe the screen is just black, but it sounds better the first way.) V/O Destiny... (The blackness is shattered with an explosion of action. First a clip shows Tasogare no Prince doing a handspring drive to the outside of a NJPW ring. It is replaced by a clip of the Iron Chef dropping a foe on his head with a Falcon arrow. That clip in turn is replaced with Tasogare no Prince hitting his twilight suplex. Another clip flashes on the screen showing the Iron Chef landing his La Guillotina Moral finisher. The action shots are suddenly replaced by a still of the Iron Chef and the Twilight Prince standing back to back looking forward.) V/O: For as long as there has been a history destiny has been there to shape it's course. (The still picture is replaced by a photo of Tasogare no Prince holding a singles title. That in turn is replaced a picture of the Iron Chef standing side by side with two other men, all holding identical title belts. Another photo shows no Prince with another singles belt. Yet another snap shot shows the Iron Chef and another masked man holding tag team belt. Another picture of no Prince surfaces with him holding another singles belt which is replaced with one of Kobe with another Mexican title.) V/O: Destiny- Urging great men onto great things- (The clip shows Kobe winning over Tora Ryoku) V/O: Destiny- Driving great powers into confrontation- (A clip of the Iron Chef starring Ryo Inoue right in the eye replaces the clip of the match) V/O: Destiny- Bringing vengeance upon the wicked- (The clip shows Kobe being dragged out on a stretcher in the aftermath of his confrontation with Ryo Inoue.) V/O: Destiny- Giving the fallen a second chance... (The montage now shifts to clips of the Iron Chef vs no Prince from the Coronation tour ending supershow. The shot fades into the Iron Chef, Keiji Kobe, dressed in his wrestling gear and trade mark orange and black mask on a blackened set with one canister light forming a column of light for Kobe to stand in.) Kobe: Destiny... My worthy opponents Tasogare no Prince and Ketiak like to speak of it often. I never put much stock in it. You see, I was never destine for greatness. My test scores were never strong enough to get me into a good university. I was never blessed with great size or height, so I'd never make it in the sports world. Destiny said I was to be a marginal man, a simple man with a low ranking job, and perhaps an unhappy marriage. That is the path destiny laid out for me. The problem is destiny didn't consult me about my future. I wasn't about to settle for a job in factory assembling portable phones. No, I was going to be a wrestler, and not just any wrestler, I was going to be the best wrestler in the world. It didn't phase me that I was never going to be a rich man doing this. It didn't matter to me that I didn't have the size to ever head line a card. I set in my mind to do something, and come what may I was going to make it happen. Since that age of 17 I've worked my butt off at wrestling. I set up rings and carried wrestlers bags. I got stretched by some of the true greats in the business. I've been thrown around like a rag doll on more than one occasion. I've been placed in submissions so painful the blood vessels in my eyes exploded, but I remained in this business. I've not just remained, I've become dominate, and destiny didn't have one thing to do with it. I got to this point in my career for one simple reason. I worked. I am the first one in the gym and the last one out. I study each and every one of my matches evaluating my progress and picking apart what lead to my mistakes. I've traveled to Mexico to expand my knowledge and teach myself new skills. I study the Book of Five Rings and the Art of War for insights into battle strategy. I got to bed each night, sore, physically and mentally beaten, but I wake up the next morning with a fire in my heart. And that fire drives me to push myself a little further, to run a little faster, jump a little higher, hit a little harder. Destiny didn't make me great. Discipline did. Destiny didn't make me great, courage did. Destiny didn't make me great. Dedication did. Destiny didn't select me to be the finest wrestler in Japan, I did. I made myself what I am today, the absolute best. Yet, here I stand the absolute best middle weight in the world, and I've lost my last three tag team matches. Now a lesser man, like Ryo Inoue, would look to blame some one, a referee, or perhaps my partners who were pinned on all occasions. But the only man worthy of the blame is myself. Our losses were not caused by a lack of fighting spirit by the part of my partners, but a lack of focus in my own heart. You see I've been wrestling with a pair of blinders on. All I could see was how good I am in singles matches, and I forgot I had to be just as good in tag matches. I forgot it was tag teams were where I gained all my experience. I forgot it was in tag teams were where I learned to ropes of this sport. The blinders are off now, and I can see clear what is happening. I was being a bad partner to keep my own teamates, Corey Hart and We Hyun Bae from being able to rival me. Subconsciously I wanted them to lose so I would look better. I can't take these last few matches back, but I can apologize and move forward. I apologize to you Bae-san for letting you down, and I apologize to you Hart-san, and I plan on making my mistakes up to you this weekend. Most of all I apologize to you the fans, for letting myself get distracted. Tasogare no Prince, you were right, I did leave our match changed. I became a selfish jerk of a wrestler, willing to put his partners ahead of himself. But that change wasn't for good. Because this weekend you'll be facing me, the old Iron Chef, along with Corey Hart, one of the absolute bests in the sport. Corey is a man just as capable as you, and perhaps even more capable of beating me than no Prince or Ketiak. Do you think you can beat two such as us? Does your destiny tell you that you can beat us, no Prince? Let me assure you that what ever little voice in your head is telling you that you can beat myself and Hart-san, a man I consider perhaps the most under rated wrestler in the company, is lying to you. I've tagged with Hart since day one in the JPA. We've faced off against the absolute best in the wrestling, and more than held our own. We've beaten the lion's share off our foes. Our last loss was because my head was somewhere else, but this weekend it's focused on one thing, beating you and Ketiak. Individually, you very well may be better than myself and Hart-san, but that won't mean a damn thing this weekend. If you go in thinking you can out power Hart and forget about me, you've lost. If you go in thinking you can out run me, and forget about Hart, you've lost. If you believe you can isolate either one of us and double team us, you've lost. We are a team, a tightly bound fighting unit. And what ever language barrier there might be outside the ring, it fades away when that bell rings. I know Corey, he knows me, and together fighting as one there isn't anybody in the world who can beat us. I am the left hand, he is the right. We are two arms of the same body, and we work in harmony to present an unbeatable defense, an unstoppable offense. I never put much stock in destiny, but it seems you make a good case for it. In the face of two such as myself and Hart-san, how can you hope for anything short of total defeat. How can you dream of more than unconditional surrender.
