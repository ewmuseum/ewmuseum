JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.22

22 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.22 1) Ryo Inoue 2) Billy Fairchild 3) Christopher Kojima 4) Iron Chef Keiji Kobe 5) Tetsuo Matsumoto - Press Conference ---- [open camera shot. Slowly one by one silver letters with red borders flash on the screen] T! A! P! Voice Over: Total Attack Puroresu is on the air. The war for respect is on... [The screen fades to Ryo Inoue, standing his eyes turned downward looking to the floor. He takes deep breaths. The area around him is darkness save for the spotlight that shines on him] Ryo Inoue: [eyes still looking to the floor] They say I lack respect for my fellow competitors. They say "Inoue is a mad dog that needs to be put out in the field with a shotgun". Men such as Rick Taylor think that I need to be "called out" for my stance on my chosen beliefs. Tell me Rick Taylor, is your nose still sore? Do you wake up at night cursing te fact that I got the better of you? Does it drive you mad that my shooto shattered your nose? Or do you sit there like some child plotting revenge? [Ryo runs a hand over his bald head, still his eyes are cast downward] Ryo Inoue: I listen to the buffoon Keiji Kobe. I cries that I hit to hard with my closed fist and that unfairly choke men to unconciousness. Tell me Kobe, you arrogant fool, Do you think my open hand would have been any less painful than my fist? Can you not accept the fact that you are a just slip of a man without a slip of fighting ability? The referee stopped our match because you stood no chance of escaping me and both he and you knew it. Someday, when you are more of a man, and not this whining child, we will fight again and I promise not to make you cry with my punches, I'll make you scream in pain from my picks and revel in your agony as I abandon my "choke holds" and break your arm in jujigatame. You are and always will be a fool Kobe. Perhaps you need me to remind you of this fact once again... [Ryo coninues to look downward. He takes a deep breath and closes his eyes before looking up to face the camera] Ryo Inoue: Genji Yamato... [Ryo's eyes flash open] You can not keep me down. I will not become one of your bastardized "Strong Style" babies. I have respect for Masashi Akita because he has done what no one has been able to do since Hiryoshi Tenzaki... he beat me. But you Yamato... you make me sick. You cast down your judgement on me from your office like some bastard child. Your dull blade known as Strong Style will be cut to shreds by the precision of my strong katana known as Shooto. You look at me with disgust, because you know that the untamed spirit that is Ryo Inoue will never call you "Master". The war is on, I will bring it to you at Korakuen, I will bring it to you at Differ Ariake and I will bring it ot you in Kobe. I declare war on "Strong Style" and all its pretenders. I will make an example of Rick Taylor on March 10th. I will break his nose again and I will teach him the meaning of pain. Then I it is on to Hakata Star Lanes on march 16th. Yuzo Moriboshi will fall. One by one thwy will all fall Yamato... until there is you and me... I will never bow to you... the war... is ON!! [Ryo Inoue turns his back to the camera and walks off into the darkness] [Fade out] ---- Press Release: Dear Sirs, I, Billy Fairchild, felt the need to explain my erratic behaviour of the past few weeks. Towards the end of the JPA "Corronation" tour I began to get depressed and homesick. This had a negative effect on my performance in the ring and I began to doubt myself. This doubt culminated in me deciding to up and quit the JPA dojo and return home to Portland, Oregon. After an almost year long struggle, I gave up my dream. I returned to my former life, pretty much as I had left it, but with my head cowered in shame. I took this time to reflect on myself, my life, my dreams. And deep down inside I realized that I still, above most everything, wanted to be a professional wrestler. I was helped along in this realization by a few people. My mother, who had always wanted the best for me and basically left me to make my own descisions through out my life, finally standing up and telling me to reach out and grab my dream. It was right there in front of me, if I'd only learn how to perservere and not give up hope so easy. A new friend I met in Portland who showed me more kindness from a stranger than I had ever experienced and helped me realize just how special I was and how much I could achieve. And lastly, Jason Ramsey. I recieved a letter from Mr. Ramsey while at home in Portland inviting me to train under his guidance at his home in Tokyo. In tagging with Mr. Ramsey over the past couple months I have gained a great deal of respect for his abilities and snatched the opprotunity to learn from him. I have learned quite a bit from Mr. Ramsey so far and look forward to displaying that newfound skill and knowledge on the upcoming JPA "Royal Series" tour. Thank you very much for your time. Sincerely, Billy Fairchild ---- [Fade into an undescript hotel room. Double bed, late model TV, table, lamp, random cheap picture. We all know them. We've all spent time in them. As we pan over to the left we see JPA rookie 'Japanese Gaijin' Christopher Kojima come out of the bathroom, dressed in blue jeans and a EMWC t-shirt.] CK: So, after my debut, this is my first true 'tour' with JPA and it seems I'm getting put with all the other 'new' kids. Even though I'm teaming up with Knyte tonight, my pain partner seems to be Samuel Otomo. Otomo, I saw you a few times on the judo circuit. However, I'm warning you. If you're American manager gets involved, I will not hesitate to drop his ass with a Spin Cycle. Sorry, but that's the truth. [Kojima takes a slight pause to drink a quick slug of Pepsi.] CK: Now, on to my opponents. Corey, you're a rookie, but still, you're a playboy at heart. Not a true to heart wrestler. That's why I will defeat you. Plus, your opponent is hardcore trash. Then, for some reason, a legend like the 'Iron Che'f got stuck with you two. Me, Kaz, and Samuel may be rookies, but we will put up our best fight against you three. That's a promise. [Another pause...] CK: Then, I face my former partner with Omoto. Knyte, we haven' talked yet, but I hope you will be able to help me kick Hart's and We's ass. Then, I face the gaijins Ramsey & Fairchild. Fairchild, that was a real nice promo I saw the other day. A couple more minutes of that sacchrine and you could've attached the Disney name to it. Plus, your little gaijin group is so cute I could puke. I will prove to this company and the crowds that I am a true gaijin. A Japanese gaijin. [Fade to Black.] ---- (The sky has opened up and it seems all the angle in heaven have begun to cry. A bone soaking deluge fills the sky and washes the earth below. On the ground a black stream flows down a hill, the camera fallows the stream up to the source, a the burnt out shell of a cathedral. The shot centers in on the Nave of the church, though badly damaged, the alter still stands, covered with ash, but with each drop of water a little bit of dirt is washed away and the alter grows a little cleaner. The shot moves forward down the center isle toward the once proud bell tower. In the shattered vestige of the tower among the pools of melted colored glass now frozen like ice on a lake there sits a man in an orange and black mask, "The Iron Chef" Keiji Kobe.) Kobe: Kathakano, El Chupacabras, you speak of wondering endlessly across the seven contents in a miserable existence. Never welcome no matter where you go, at every turn you are greeted with new enemies. A hard life indeed, El Chupacabras, I am sure it has made you very tough and cagey. But to suggest I never me some one of your special merits, well that... that is a statement full of false pride. I too, have been a vagabond, a stranger in strange lands. I've been down many a road with hardship as my only constant companion. In those travels I met many people and saw many things, so how can you be so sure I've never seen someone of your likeness. (Kobe gets up and walks into the poring rain, washing away the ash that covered his body. The colors of his mask seem to intensify with the life giving water. The Iron Chef begins addressing the camera as he walks toward another end of the church.) In my travels I met many a coward, El Chupacabras, like you who attack the defenseless. I've met so called men who would rather run from there problems than face them, men just like you. I've faced cowards like you before, Chupacabras, cowards I can deal with. In my travels I met leaches before. Those who draw their life from other's. Those who get rich from the sweat of other men's brows. I've faced leaches like you before, Chupacabras, I can deal with leaches. In my travels I met monsters, hideous creatures who had no respect for the gods or man alike. I met monsters who deprive men of there lively hood and starve his family as a result. I've faced monsters like you before, Chupacabras, I can deal with monsters. (The Iron Chef's pace is rather slow, and peaceful, not unlike a man going for a stroll through a park on a spring day. His once loose shirt now hugs close to his muscular frame, giving him the appearance of steel, or perhaps should I say iron, wrapped in silk. He seems ready for combat, but perfectly at peace.) The problem with man is he believes he is totally unique, different from the rest of the world. And to some point he is right, some men are tall, others short, some hairy, some bald. Some men are brown, others white, yellow, or red, but deep down in one's heart we are all the same. The same is true of evil, it too takes many forms, it could take the from of a storm, or a man, or luck. But evil is still evil at it's heart, and one form is not so different from another. No, Kathakano, I've never face a Chupacabras with your abilities. In your travels across the seven contents I'm sure you know some secret technique only taught by penguins on the south pole and may it serve you well. In the end, it will do you no good, I will prevail. (The storm picks up in intensity, lighting flashes behind Kobe and can be viewed from the circular hole where a window once stood. In his down pour Kobe stops in the middle of the fallen beams and rafters feet firmly planted on the ground. His own voice raises as if to drown out the thunder.) I will prevail because despite all your travels you know nothing of the Art of War. You think you can defeat me like you defeated this church. Break my bone, draw my blood, take away my air. Hurting my body will no more defeat me than damaging this building has served to weaken the strength of this church. The congregation has banded together, they already have plans to build a new church, your acts have only strengthened their resolve. So it is with me. The pain you cause will only serve to remind me that weakness is leaving my body. The struggle you cause me will only strengthen my fire to win. A fool like you should know the more you beat iron the purer and stronger it becomes. And so then I shall grow stronger as our battle rages. Make no mistake, I shall win, my blade is already sharp. I practice the pure Lucha Liebre of Mexico, my friend. Not the watered down wrestling of a no Prince or Toshiaki Mizuno, or Eiji Yatsu. My fight is the true fighting art. I practice a craft that dates back to before even puroresu. I do not walk your hidden path of short cuts, my road is hard arduous one, a path most are not capable of following. I walk the same path as El Santo, the hero of all Mexico, and the bain of Vampiros in the 50's and 60's. I walk the same path of Konnan el Bárbaro, the man who drove Vampiro Canadiense out of Mexico. You see, El Chupacabras, your kind has never been able to stand up to the heart and passion of real Lucha. (As suddenly as the storm picked up, it comes to a stop. The rain is replaced with the warming sun. Birds sing in the distance, and life seems to have returned to this place of death. The blackness has been washed away, the entire area shines with a dew like coating of fresh life.) All your life, El Chupacabras, you have ran. You ran all over the world... Every time you were compelled to run, you thought you were being forced out, but in truth, you were being pushed towards something. Every move you made brought you closer to your destruction. And now, El Chupacabras, your bloody terror will come to an end for you are about to come face to face with what you have been pushed towards. You are about to square off against the Iron Chef, the one true master of Lucha Liebre in the JPA, pound for pound the best wrestler in Japan. I never ran from the storm, I stood my ground and am now stronger for it. The twenty fourth of this month we will meet, and your running will come to an end, El Chupacabras. Like The saint and the barbarian before me, the chef shall emerge victorious over your wretched kind. (The shot fades to white.) ---- [open camera shot we are in Kobe World Hall and the media is in a feeding frenzy as Steel Wind Dojo has announced this special press conference on behalf of SJPW's former "Super Tengu," Tetsuo Matsumoto. As usual Junji Shirow takes to the stand first] Junji Shirow: Ladies and gentlemen if you all will just settle down we can get started... [Slowly the press quiets itself as Shirow smiles] Junji Shirow: Steel Wind Dojo is proud to announce that the contract for Keiji asamiya to face his cousin Tetsuo Matsumoto has been signed sealed and delivered to JPA President Genji Yamato. Asamiya-sensei talked with Yamato-san presonally to assure him that he and Matsumoto-san will meet in April. I'll now hand you over to Matsumoto-san. [There is some applause for Tetsuo as he takes the mic. His usual stern face is replaced by a smile] Tetsuo Matsumoto: First, I'll be taking questions in a moment so don't all of you get all annoyed at me [There is some polite laughter from the press] Tetsuo Matsumoto: For the record, this is my official countdown tour. "FINAL FIGHT FIVE" begins in April as I face my cousin and freind Keiji Asamiya. For those of you not in the loop, Keiji-kun and I have never faced each other in a professional singles match so this is a big event. I'm talking with other colleagues of mine as possible other opponents to face for the "Final Fight Five". [A hand shoots up from the crowd. It is Riki Fudo of PNM] Riki Fudo: Can you divulge any of those names Tetsuo-san? Tetsuo: [shaking his head with a smile] How did you get in Fuso-san? I thought we were keeping stragglers out? [A good laugh erupts from the media] Tetsuo: To answer Fudo-san's question, I have talked with Shoji Suzuki and have tried to contact TORA, we will see if Ed Dean can make it over for a possible beating [wide smile]. The matches during my retirement countdown will be my absolute FINAL matches. I do hope Genji Yamato can find the time to get out from behind his desk to be my last opponent. I will also wrestel a few tag matches with my old freind MASSIVE Akita, if Akita is up for it. OK that's it for now, when Yamato-san calls me back we will have a DEFINITE date for "FINAL FIGHT FIVE: FIRST FIGHT!" [The media starts rushing with the questions as Tetsuo waves wit ha smile] [fade out] ---- http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/ --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
