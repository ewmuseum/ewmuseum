JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.1

1 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.1 1) Tasogare no Prince 2) Masanobu Akazi 3) Iron Chef Keiji Kobe 4) Billy Fairchild 5) Ryo Inoue 6) Kenji Okano 7) Shinji Nakata ---- [The scene is a vast plain of rolling hills, entirely covered with tall grass, stretching clear off to the distant horizon. At that horizon, the sun is quickly sinking below the distant edge of the world, causing the sky overhead to grow steadily darker. As it sets, the last rays of the sun set the clouds in the darkening sky afire with brilliant purple, pink and orange highlights -- the colors of Twilight. A light breeze blows across the plain, causing the blades of green grass to wave to and fro in an elegant, almost hypnotic, dance almost like waves rippling across the surface of an enormous green lake. Standing amongst the waist-high grass is a lone figure wearing a long purple coat which is cinched tightly around his thin waist, which still allows the loose edges of the coat to flap noiselessly in the breeze. His long silvery hair reaches down well past his waist, and it too, flows gently upon the wind, moving about his head and shoulders almost like a living thing. His skin of his thin, handsome face is pale, almost an alabaster color, which is in sharp contrast to his piercing, bright violet eyes and he lifts a single white orchid, which he holds in his right hand in front of his small, slightly upturned nose. His large, violet eyes close and the corners of his thin lips curl upward slightly in a smile of pleasure as he inhales deeply of the flower's fragrance. After a few moments, he lowers the orchid from in front of his face, and his piercing eyes re-open, staring directly into the camera's lens as he begins to speak...] It has been a while since I found myself here in the Twilight... [He pauses, smelling the orchid in his hand again...] Perhaps too long. But once more, the siren song of Destiny has called me back to my beloved homeland of Nihon, and as her chosen -- and her loyal servant -- the Prince of Twilight I had little choice but to answer that call. [Another pause -- another whiff...] And when Yamato-san contacted me with an offer to appear in JPA, and to help with building it's Junior weight division, I felt that the time was ripe to make my return to the land where I was born, and where I first rose to prominence. But then, when Yamato-san informed me who my first opponent in JPA was to be, I no longer had any doubts, that this was truly the path that Destiny had chosen for me. [Once again, the Prince of Twilght's eyes close as he partakes of the white orchid's fragrance before he resumes speaking...] You see, Keiji Kobe, I am familiar with you. And had not unfortunate, yet unavoidable, financial circumstances caused the closing of NJPW, our meeting would have occured some time ago. And now, with my returning to the land of my birth to once again perform, I have been given the opportunity that was seemingly lost before. But, in truth, Kobe-san, there was never any doubt as to whether we would ever meet. For you see, as I have already stated, that meeting was Destined. And as anyone who knows anything about me should know by now, I am a firm believer in Destiny. [The Prince smiles knowingly, taking another smell of the white flower in his hand...] The closing of one organization, and my subsequent time away from Nihon, could not prevent it from occuring. And just like our Destined meeting could not be prevented, your subsequent defeat at my hands will also be unavoidable. For you see, Kobe-san, my returning to Nihon to face you is proof that one cannot prevent that for which he is Destined from occuring, and that is precisely why you will fall to the Prince of Twilight, for that is _your_ Destiny. Kore wa sakete toore me, anata no unmei, "You cannot avoid your Destiny." Just as you could not avoid your fated meeting with the Prince of Twilight, Kobe-san, you will find it impossible to escape your defeat. Destiny has seen fit to bring me back to the land of my birth for a reason, Keiji Kobe. How unfortunate for you that a part of that reason lies in your defeat. "Iron Chef" you will be the first in JPA to enter into the Twilight, but you will not be the last to leave it... [He takes a final smell from the white orchid he grasps...] Forever changed... [With that, the Prince of Twilight turns and begins to walk off through the tall, waving grass, leaving the camera to pan back slowly towards the horizon, where the last bit of the sun is disappearing, plunging the world, and the scene, into darkness...] ---- [The scene opens in well-worn gym. The walls are a faded brown, the equipment dirty and rusted, and the echoes of the past can be heard. A man who has seen as much of the past as anyone, Masanobu Akazi, stands center frame. His arms are crossed in front of his Central Japan Pro t-shirt. His face shows his 48 years with a wrinkled and tired appearance. Otherwise he is solemn, standing straight and looking into the camera.] Futsuki…indeed, it has been too long since I have stepped into the ring. After twenty-years, I thought that my time in the ring was completed…and then I found Japan Puroresu Association. In my time as a professional wrestler, most of my years were spent in Central Japan Pro; a place whose talent I never thought could be duplicated. Then a week or so ago, while watching television, a show called Battle START. Corey Hart, Keiji Kobe, XYZ, and others…and I thought simply "wow." [Masanobu turns, the camera rotates as well, placing a dingy ring in the frame.] It had been almost nine months since my last match, but I knew that JPA would soon be my new home. And here I am, less than a week away from battling Asamu Tatsu. [The camera returns its focus to Akazi.] From what I have seen of Tatsu, which is not much…I see a powerful, athletic man, I see a quick, powerful striker, and I see a man who can hurt, punish, and destroy. Every wrestling fan in Japan has seen my work at one time or another, whether it be headlining ABUNAI 95 at the Tokyo Dome or wrestling in a small gymnasium in one of the thousands of towns in Japan…everyone has seen me perform. And thus, everyone knows of the disadvantages in this match…I have slowed…anyone can see that, so how does the slow counter the fast? Strength? Only if you are stronger than your opponent, which, although possible, is probably nothing more than a stalemate. So what do I do then? [Masanobu turns from the camera once again, staring at the ring.] Use what I've learned in that (he points to the ring) right there and know that I've been there, I've fought stronger men, I've fought faster men, and I prevailed, and I will do so once again. I will prove, once again, that I am still the best wrestler in the world, and I will do so against the greatest talent there is to offer. "Densetu" shall be reborn. [Scene fades.] ---- (The shot opens up onto a twilight sky, blue, purple, and black shadows dance in the air as the sounds of soft cello playing sound in the distance. The forces of light and shadow come together to morph into a picture of Tasogare no Prince holding the WHCW Cruiser Weight championship.) V/O: He is a prince who's kingdom knows no bounds (The light show bends to morph into Tasogare no Prince with the IWC Can-Am championship.) V/O: He's held a title in every federation he's worked in. (The shot now shows Tasogare no Prince being crowned Super Japan Pro Wrestling's first Welterweight Champion.) V/O: No one has come close to matching his in ring intensity, his skill, or dedication (The shot morphs into a picture of Tasogare no Prince with the Neo-J middle weight title.) V/O: It seemed as if the twilight would last for ever. (On cue, a blast of trumpets sound, and the sun begins to rise in the distance banishing the night with it's red orange light. Mars Bringer of War replaces the soothing cello and the clips now feature a new wrestler, the "Iron Chef" Keiji Kobe in a victory in his first match in Neo Japan against Hasegawa Jin.) V/O: But a new light dawned, to challenge the twilight prince's claim to the throne. (The shot shows "Iron Chef" Keiji Kobe's victory over "Ecchi Kid" Kentaro "H-Bomb" Kawasaki and then a victory over Conserje de Infierno, and the huge Knick man) V/O: A new star filled the heavens, and banished the twilight, ushering in a new day in Purpresu. (The shot shows Kobe teaming with Yuichi Kitada in a victory over Tasogare no Prince and Kazuya Asakino in Burning Japan.) V/O: From then one, it seemed nothing to curb the Iron Chef as his pure Lucha style would make him loved by fans, respected by co-workers, and feared by enemies. (The shot now show clips form his work in the JPA, Teaming with Hart and Bae to defeat Okano and EIYUU and a heavy-weight no longer under contract, scoring the upset over Tora Ryoku, and defeating XYZ.) V/O: But this new day can not last for ever... And many have seen the skies above Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall begin to darken. But is this darkening the start of a new age twilight, or will it mark the storm that washes the high flying Twilight prince to earth? (The screen divides in half, on half in cool blue and purple tones shows the profile of Tasogare no Prince, the other half is in hot reds and yellows shows the masked profile of the Iron Chef. The shot finally cuts to the Iron Chef in a white room, with a piercing light behind him, that keeps the viewers from knowing whether he has his mask on or not.) Kobe: I entered wrestling with one goal, to be the very best, ever. I've sacrificed my family, my friends, and my body in pursuit of this goal. Over the last year I spent more time splitting hotel rooms with friends to save money than I have spent with my own wife. I've spent more hours in the gym than sleeping. I've worked through bruised ribs, black eyes, broken toes, and fractured fingers, never missing a show in three years on the road. I've flown all over the world in an effort to perfect my craft, but I've logged just as many hours in the air during my matches as I have getting to them. You can go though the entire locker room of the JPA and ask them. Ask them who is the first guy in gym and the last one to leave, and they'll all tell you the same thing. Kobe... Kobe is the one who's first in and last out. I've worked my ass off to get from the bottom of the company to stand proud a the top middle weight in the JPA. I took on all comers, regardless of size, style, or strength, until it was clear I was the real deal. I worked, and worked, and worked, until it was clear I was THE TOP MIDDLE WEIGHT, the champion without a title. But all my work, all my sacrifice, was tossed aside like last weeks left overs with you're arrival, Tasogare no Prince. I scratched, and clawed, and bit my way to get to the top of the JPA's ranks, but YOU, you walk in and immediately challenge me for my spot like it's owed to you. I respect, you no Prince, I do. While I was training in Mexico, you served as an inspiration to me. You were a man capable of taking on the heavies and wining, and I aspired to do just that. Here I am in 2001, doing just that, taking on the big boys, BETTER THAN YOU! I'm impressed with the way you blended strength and speed. I've stood in wide eye amazement at the way you used to take to the skies with the grace of dove, but stuck with the furry of a hawk. I respect your ability, no Prince, but that doesn't mean I don't resent you as well. (Anger and rage cause Kobe to raise his voice.) I resent the fact you're in the number two match of the tour ending supercard, and it's also your debut! My partners Corey Hart and We Hyun Bae, my foes like Kayo and EIYUU, and new comers like XYZ have given it their all this tour to show the fans that the middle weight division is the most exciting and competitive part of the JPA. They've endured long bus rides, sub par hotels, and bad food for the sake of our company and our class, but are denied being in the top of the card while, a man how sat at home this tour come in at the top of the card. I resent the fact I'm view as second class to you because I've never held a title in Japan. As if CAMPEONATO MUNDIAL WELTER or CAMPEONATO MUNDIAL DE PEQUEÑOS ESTRELLAS aren't credible titles. I don't just resent you, no Prince, I hate you. I hate you because your signing has shifted the entire dynamic of the JPA's roster. I hate you, because you are the one man who is capable of coming out of retirement right into the main event. (suddenly going into a whisper.) I hate you because you do things I only wish I could do... I hate you because you, more than any other wrestler, have driven me to be better than what I was the day before. (Returning to a normal speaking voice.) I pushed myself since joining the JPA in the hopes of proving myself to be better than you, and now, I've got the chance to prove it. Now it the time for me to either put up, or shut up. I respect you, no Prince, I resent you, no Prince. The one thing I don't do is fear you, no Prince. Where ever you've been, where ever you went to heal yourself in your land of shadows, I assure you your training was not a good as the train I got wrestling here in the JPA. It's the end of the tour, I'm beaten, battered and bruised. I've be hurt physically, mentally, and yes even spiritually, but I've yet to be weakened. Quite the opposite, this tour has only made me stronger. My star is still rising, no Prince, and it's not yet reached noon. I've still got a long way to go. Now I'm ready to give you a taste of the true flavor of the Iron Chef. Now I'm ready to banish your darkness for ever. (The shot cuts to the graphic of Kobe and no Prince with this written across the bottom.) Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall 2001.2.3 Iron Chef Keiji Kobe vs. Tasogare no Prince ---- [Camera fades in on the door of a locker room labeled "GAIJIN" as a hand knocks on the door. The young gaijin rookie Billy Fairchild answers the door. He's wearing his white Adidas sweatsuit with blue stripes that he wears to the ring before his matches. There are some other noticable gaijin wrestlers behind him in the locker room preparing for their matches and what-not.] Reporter: Billy Fairchild? Hi, I'm Steve Gurwin, with channel 8 news in Portland Oregon. We're doing a story on you and I was wondering if I could have a few words with you if you have the time. Fairchild: Absolutely, come on in. [The reporter and cameraman step in as Jeremy Knyte walks by and bumps the reporter on his way through.] Gurwin: Uh, is it okay for us to be in here? Fairchild: Sure, just ignore Jeremy Knyte, he's a total asshole, and see that rather large man in the brown trunks over there? [Fairchild points as he sets up a chair in for Steve.] Gurwin: Yeah. Fairchild: That's Jack Shaw, steer clear of him just to be safe. [Fairchild chuckles and smiles as he sits down on the bench in front of his locker..] Gurwin: Okay, will do. First up, I'd like to ask you how you're enjoying Japan so far. Fairchild: So-so. I mean, it's a nice place and all, but as they say, there's no place like home. Japan doesn't really feel like home yet. I don't speak the language, I can't read the signs, nobody looks like me, well, except these jokers here. [Billy motions to infer the other gaijin wrestlers in his staement.] I was really homesick the first couple months when I was at the dojo, then I just put all my emotions to the back of my mind and completely sealed that part of my brain off. I focused totally on my training. Then when JPA started running shows and brought in other Americans, those homesick feelings slowly came back. But, I'm going to stick it out because what other choice do I have now? [Billy smiles] Gurwin: Well, you could come back to America and wrestle. Why did you choose to wrestle in Japan in the first place? Fairchild: [Fairchild laughs a little] Well, as I've said many many times before ... American wrestling ... sucks. It just plain sucks. The fans suck, the gimmicks suck, the storylines suck, it all sucks. Over here we have honest WRESTLING fans who enjoy what we do out there each and every night. No boring chants, no "you f'ed up" chants, no "show your puppies" chants. They're WRESTLING fans. We don't have goofy ass gimmicks over here because we don't need silly-ass costumes and characters to get over with the fans here. Well, some do but those are the weaker of the roster in my opinion. It's not about cutting some ridiculous long winded promo where you threaten to rape your colleagues wives. Talking shit will get you shit. That's the bottom line. You have to get in the ring and back it up. That guy that bumped you when you came in, Jeremy Knyte, biggest asshole I've met here. Obsessed with playing some stupid psychotic character. All I know is they better get him out of here quick before that guy gets hurt. We don't take kindly to smack talkers here in JPA. Let someone translate what he said in that fucked up interview to Ryo Inoue and put them in the ring together. Knyte will end up in a coma with multiple fractures and severe bloodloss. Simply put, here in Japan, it's about the WRESTLING and I love WRESTLING. Gurwin: I've heard that the Japanese wrestle a much more physically demanding style as well. What can you tell us about that? Fairchild: Yeah. [Fairchild looks down in thought for a second then looks back up.] ... Sometimes it's so demanding that I don't know how much longer I'll be here. Like I said I absolutely LOVE pro wrestling and I wouldn't want to do it anywhere else but ... I'm not sure I fit in. I mean, I have no athletic background. The only thing I ever wanted to do was wrestle and now that I'm here I don't know how much longer I can keep it up. Hopefully this is just some sort of depression or anxiety linked with the returning homesickness. Sometimes I go to bed at night thinking about quitting and it scares me. I've come so far and fought so hard, but it might not be enough. I either need to snap out of it soon or ... Hopefully, if I just fight past this and fight harder something will "click" inside of me and allow me to refocus on being the best wrestler I can be. But something needs to happen soon or .. well, I don't wanna think about it anymore. Gurwin: Okay. Are you satisfied with your performance so far? Fairchilld: ... [Thinks.] No, not really. I mean, of course there's always room for improvement. I've heard I've gotten some praise in the Japanese press but it's pretty much focused on me being good for a rookie. I want to be good, period. [He laughs.] I don't think I've effectively asserted myself quite yet. I need to get focused and figure out what works for me and do it. I'm still experimenting with my style now so who knows when I'll finally settle in and get comfortable with it. Right now my philosophy is pretty much "balls to the wall". Throw everything I have out there and see what sticks. I really need to turn it up a notch though because I've been disappointed by some of my performances lately. Gurwin: What's your next big match? Fairchild: Probably the Tv taping in a couple nights. Ill be tagging with Jason Ramsey ... [Fairchild sticks his head up and looks around the locker room for Ramsey. He spots him and points him out.] ... and we'll be taking on SHOICHI 13 and Mitsuhiro Kataka. Gurwin: Feeling good about that match? Fairchild: Yeah. Ramsey and myself are a very effective tag team combination. I don't think SHOICHI and Kataka have tagged before so that gives us the team experience edge which could make up for my personal inexperience. SHOICHI was in the Twin Titles tournament so he's developing some name value around here. I'll be looking to make an impression by staying toe to toe with him and Kataka. Like I said before, I've really got to fire it up and go to the next level now. I'm not performing at my peak like I should be. I think I got a little of it back though when I beat EIYUU last night. We'll see. Gurwin: So we shall. Thank you very much Billy. Everyone in Portland wishes you luck in the future. And keep it up because we're all behind you. Fairchild: Thanks. [Fairchild and Gurwin shake hands. Fade out.] ---- [open camera shot, we are outside the main doors of the Steel Wind Dojo, the press is gathered here and trying to push forward to snap pictures of both Steel Wind Dojo founder Keiji Asamiya and Head Trainer Junji Shirow. We are 4 days removed from Massive Akita's pinfall win over Ryo Inoue in the 6 Man Tag Team match at Osaka Prefectural Gym. Since then the press has been desperately trying to get inside the Dojo, where Inoue remains in seclusion when not wrestling] Junji Shirow: [approaching the microphone] If you will all just please calm down... [Flash bulbs explode as the press quiets down] Junji Shirow: We understand that you all feel there is a story here but the fact is there isn't. It is true that Ryo inoue is staying at the Dojo but it is for strictly for training purposes to prepare for the J1 Rikidozan title match at the Tokyo Dome. [Shirow pauses and looks to Keiji Asamiya who slowly nods and steps forward] Keiji asamiya: [approaching themic] If you will all just please be silent... I have a statement from Inoue-san that he told me to read to you all. [Asamiya takes a long pause and looks over the crowd before being handed a clipboard by one of his seconds] Keji Asamiya: Inoue-san says: "This match means nothing. Being beaten in a tag team amtch where nothing is on the line but pride means nothing to me. I respect Massive Akita and congratulate him on actually getting my shoulders to the mat for a counbt of 3. But this is not an omen of things to come in the J1 Rikidozan Tournament Finals. I will be at the Tokyo Dome to win the title. I will be at the Tokyo Dome to prove my shooto is the strongest and I will be at the Tokyo Dome to make the once great Massive Akita feel very, very miniscule. The press seem to forget that I have lost matches before, they forget that I have always come back from those minimal defeats stronger and more determined. I will face Massive Akita on February 3rd and I will overcome this loss to be the first person to wear the J1 Rikidozan title that actually deserves it. I will not be denied." Junji Shirow: [stepping in front of Asamiya]: You see? Inoue-san is just here to train. Both Asamiya-sensei and myself have complete faith in Ryo that he will make the Steel Wind Dojo proud. Now please leave, this is private property and we will call the police if you do not leave! [fade out] ---- [Scene: Kenji "KAYO" Okano is seated on a leather sofa in front of a large video screen. Smoking a large cigar and drinking a martini, he wears a black and yellow smoking jacket, smiling as he watches the highlights of his matches thus far in the Japan Puroresu Association. As the footage begins to show a recent match against Corey Hart, the smile on Okano's face is slowly replaced by a scowl of contempt. Pressing the pause button on the remote, he stands up in front of the screen and acknowledges the camera.] Okano: This man right here...wait, this BOY right here...somehow fluked his way into a win over me. Now, everyone knows I'm as good a sport as the next guy (as evidenced by my J-League Young Player of the Year Award), but this is just incomprehensible to me! I mean, I could have handled it if I lost to a fine wrestler like my good comrade EIYUU, but to a lowly gaijin like Corey Hart?!? Ugh, I'm so humiliated! [Okano pauses for a moment, then regains his smile. He shuts off the video screen, and concentrates his full attention on the camera in front of him.] Okano: So, I see that EIYUU and myself have a match this weekend against Hart and "Even Worse" We Hyun Bae. Ah, when will the Stupid Gaijin Squad realize that they can only pull the wool over the eyes of the fans and Keiji Kobe for so long? It's only a question of time before the good people come to their senses and realize that these invaders have been fleecing them all along, and that good native defenders like EIYUU and I are only looking out for their best interest! [Okano sits back down on the sofa and stretches out. He takes a long puff from the cigar and makes smoke rings as he exhales.] Okano: So, after EIYUU and I humiliate the two of you, and show Kobe how lowly misfits like you can only drag him down, I'm sure you'll return to your respective sewers in short order. A few yen richer, maybe, but no amount of money can restore the shame of being thoroughly beaten in the ring. Even a pair of greedy gaijin like yourselves can understand that, surely. [Okano stands up and flashes a peace sign. Instead of giving the camera the finger, as is his trademark, he mists the camera's lens with his martini. The camera fades to black.] ---- [Scene: A darkened room full of video monitors. Four video screens are simultaneously playing back footage of recent Japan Puroresu Association matches. As the camera zooms in, we see that all of the footage is focused on Kaz Hasegawa. Shinji Nakata is seated at a table, taking down notes as he watches the film intensely. Taking his glasses off as he stands up, he walks over to the light switch and in an instant the fluorescent lighting flashes on, blinding the camera for a split second. Nakata returns to his chair, analyzing his notes. Finally, he looks up at the camera and speaks.] Nakata: My father always told me, one of the most important elements of puroresu is preparing for your opponents. It is an often overlooked aspect in today's era, as many prefer to fight purely on instinct. However, it is also a key factor in determining an opponent's weakness. We've already crossed paths in the JPA a few times over the last few weeks, Hasegawa. In the time that I've seen you, and in some cases, competed against you, you have proved yourself to me as a truly gifted wrestler in the strong-style tradition. We match up fairly well, in my estimation, and I'm eagerly looking forward to our match this Saturday in Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall. [Nakata stands up, and looks at a portrait of his father Naoki hanging on the wall.] Nakata: He once said to me..."There is no thrill like that which is acquired after defeating an opponent of equal skill, in the heat of battle". Saturday night in Tokyo, one of us will experience that euphoria. May the best man win... [Nakata bows to the camera, and the scene fades...] ---- http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/ --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
