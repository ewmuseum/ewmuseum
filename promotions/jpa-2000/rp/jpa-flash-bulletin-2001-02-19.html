JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.19

19 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.19 1) XYZ 2) Toshiaki Mizuno 3) Gustav Jaeger 4) Kathakano ---- [There the camera is. Turned on, filming some various scene. But what scene is it? A typical Japanese gym is what you are seeing. You hear the soft whispers of other men in the background; you hear the monotonous tone of a shower mixed in with the sounds of toilet flushes. But what your eyes are concentrated on is simply the Japanese wrestler sitting before you, wearing only a white towel around his waist. The man known as XYZ is sitting up, his back resting against the cold metal of the locker as he begins to speak.] XYZ: One tour ends, another begins. The Japanese Puroresu Association has once again hit the road for yet another tour. I ended the last tour on a high note, despite the loss to Tokaio-san on the final show. It was a great tour for me and a great learning experience as I gained many wins and few losses. I was tested countless times on "CORONATION" but now, I must do battle with even more elite Japanese wrestlers, some of which are foreign to me. For the first taping of "BATTLE START!" I must team up with a friend from the last tour to do battle with two beginners. KAYO-san I welcome you back to this tour and I hope that you and I can have the formidable success we did last time. I hope that you have been training since the last time I saw you and that you are in shape and ready to go. KAYO-san is our time now; it is our chance for success! We both work so hard; let us prove we can take it to the next level. And as for Masanobu Akazi and Toshiaki Mizuno, I welcome you both to the tour. I welcome you both to the brave world of Japanese wrestling, and I hope you do not go home crying to mother, weeping over a loss to a man such as I because it is bound to happen. [Brief pause.] XYZ: You see Akazi-san and Mizuno-san; I am a true Japanese warrior with years of success under my belt. I have competed with the best, and sometimes even overcame the challenge. I do hope you two can bring a challenge to the table, but if you cannot, oh well. Remember young warriors, do not go where a path may lead, but rather, go to where there is no path and leave a trail! [Fade out.] ---- [Fade up. We are met by the image of a young Japanese wrestler sitting on a bench. The bench is inside a Dojo, with the walls painted a menacing black. On the far side behind the bench is a silver tiger portrait, roaring in the center of the wall. The young wrestler now looks up as we see his brown eyes looking back at you, with a menacing hate in them. He now stands up, showing off his relatively well-built body. He is wearing his normal wrestling attire and there is sweat coming down his chest] Toshiaki Mizuno: I have been training here, in Tarui for weeks. I was waiting for my chance in JPA, and I finally received it. I am finally getting my chance to shine. And the unlucky men who have to be there when I set my example are none other than XYZ and Kenji Okano. Two already established men. Two men who are being fed to the Silver Tiger on the 24th of February. Now, you may not consider me a threat right now, but that doesn't matter for you are a bunch of idiots. Now you can think nothing of me or you can worry about me night and day. If I were you, I'd choose the second option. I was trained by a great and I am destined to become a great, but you two are nothing more than two pitiful excuses for wrestlers. I am planning on showing my Tiger spirit, and then getting rid of both of you. Now although this may be my first match, I am sure that I am ready. I have been training for so long to finally get this chance. I have spent most of my year in the dojo, getting ready for my big break and now it has arrived. For me to wait this long and then not prove myself would be a great disappointment to me. Even though I want to win more than anything, I will be happy by impressing everyone at the Differ Ariake. If I can make everyone there notice me, I will be very happy. If I lose and make everyone think I'm not worth the fuss then I will be ashamed to be me, because I have done many things before the JPA, but the JPA was my dream and I need to show off my skill for everyone to see. Then you will see why I have been called so many great names. XYZ, you left Japan to fight in Texas. You left this great land and fought in the US. I cannot blame you. There is more opportunity for wealth there, but now you have returned and on the 24th you shall fall prey to any number of moves but I think I will save the Niagara Falls Driver for you. You may not know about it, but when you feel it I am sure you are going to be finished. When I drop you viciously on you head, you will see why I sound a bit egotistical. Now, you can defend your traditions, but I am here to ruin them. I will break traditions, because we need a new generation not the same old wrestlers, and you are one of them. Maybe not old, but the kind of person I dislike. The kind of person, that thinks he knows everything. And XYZ, at the show on the 24th, you are in some serious trouble, whether you want to believe it or not! Now my tag team partner might be someone who respects tradition, but I don't mind simply because he has my back for the match so I will leave him be. As long as he doesn't get in my way during the match, I will protect him! Now on the other hand, Kenji Okano seam's to be someone of a new breed. And I can't get angry at a former soccer player for that is my second passion. In fact, I sort of like his attitude and it's really too bad he's on the other side of the ring. If I weren't fighting you, I would probably offer my hand in friendship. But you're on the other side of the ring so it's like feeding me prey. I am going to destroy you, I am going to make you hurt. I will make you wonder why in the hell you ever left soccer and then I will laugh at your misfortune. Frankly Okano, you are going to be shamed by the Silver Tiger... [As Toshiaki Mizuno is still talking when a 14-15 year old kid comes up to him and buts in] Kid: Mizuno-San, you must get back to training now. Mizuno: Who the hell are you to tell me what I'm going to do? Kid: Sorry, I am just delivering a message from Sato-san Mizuno: Okay, but next time you but in on me, you're going to go home crying! Kid: Sorry once again. [The kid now leaves as Mizuno pushes his hair back] Toshiaki Mizuno: As you can see, I most go even though that young punk gave me so much mouth. Well, I guess I would have done the same thing. But Okano, XYZ you are in trouble, you may not believe it now but it will come to be the truth. Now I must prepare for the match of my short career while you worry exactly what I'm going to do to you! [Toshiaki Mizuno now walks out of the camera view as we fade out] ---- (The camera fades in on the training area of Gustav Jaeger. We know it's his trianing area because he's right in front of the camera, working over a heavy bag with several stiff and brutal punches as well as a few kicks. As he sees the camera, he starts talking but doesn't stop working out.) GJ: Goshin Star. The corporate wrestler of Yoshiro Yohama, the man he hopes to use to cash in on the popularity of pro wrestling, as well as to gain exposure and influence for his TV network. (Gustav utters a low snarl and fires off a quick and brutal series of jabs into the heavy bag, causing the chain to jangle loudly. He's clearly quite upset about something, is Gustav.) GJ: A corporate wrestler. One who is only in it for the money. Not because he enjoys the competition, not because he wishes to test and prove his ability against others, not even because he wants to please the millions of puroresu fans that watch the JPA on a regular basis. Nein, he is only here because he is paid to be. (Gustav snarls again and assaults the heavy bag more viciously. Eventually, he lets out a roar of anger and viciously kicks the heavy bag, causing it to split in two and spray sand all over the floor. Gustav quickly turns to face the camera, a look of pure rage on his face. It is readily apparent how he got the "Freiburg Fury" nickname.) GJ: Goshin Star, you are a mockery. A mockery of a true competitor. Pure and simple. You do not have the competitive spirit burning in your soul as I have. You don not have the desire to prove yourself that I and others in JPA have. Nein, you're only here for the money. Quite frankly Mein Herr, you make me sick. On the next Battle Start, you and I will face each other, one on one as God intended. When this happens you will find out what Gustav Jaeger does to ones like you. And you will fall to the Freiburg Fury! (Camera fade to black.) ---- [The screen fades into a cathedral, ravaged by the pale moonlight's showering gaze, and created in the Gothic style. Its arches are higher than most other houses of "God". The sign on the front is in Japanese kanji characters. The camera pans to the rooftop of this church, near the bell tower and crucifix.] V/O: Listen to them......the children of the night. [The camera begins zooming in on a figure, sitting indian style next to the bell tower.] V/O: What sweet music they make. [We pan in very close. The figure is shroud in a black hooded robe, Grim Reaper style.] "So I sit, perched atop a pile of hypocrisy and cruelty. It never ceases to amaze me that this four walled orgy of ecumenical filth caused me to adopt this downright pitiful way of living." [He stands up, back to back with the tower, which is silent as the dead.] "When this church rejected me, it forced me to make a pact with the lord of the damned. Its denial reduced me to a wanderer, doomed to stalk the seven continents of the world, sail the seven seas, and climb the highest mountains in search of the people who had not seen horror in its embryotic state. For I am the man who epitomizes that horror that each and every one of you cower in fear at." [The camera zooms into the man's face, which is covered in a black mask.] "Traveling this earth, I learned many different fighting styles, studying under people like myself, those who are outcasts, rejected by the Christian society. From martial arts, to shootfighting, to high risk manuevers, to even forms of vampirism. All of these come together in a blend of deadly arts." [The man walks to the edge of the rooftop.] "About 3 weeks ago, the Japan Puroresu Association had its fate sealed by the ink of my pen. I have signed my contract, ready to hurt people legally. Sadistic, yes, but deep down inside, we are all sadists. We are all drawn to kill the man with the broken leg in order to get ahead in life. And, so shall I." [The man turns away from the camera.] "Fear my name." V/O: Kathakano, the child of horror, the hunter of the innocent. "Iron Chef, Keiji Kobe, you cannot prepare for what you do not know. Your spirits and life will be crushed by my iron fist. Do not deny, your fate is sealed." [The camera fades back out to the original shot of the far away church. Kathakano, the figure in black wields a torch. He touches the torch to the wooden doors, and the church goes up in flames. "Inno a Satana" by Emperor starts to play as the church blazes in the nightside eclipse.] ---- http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/ --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
