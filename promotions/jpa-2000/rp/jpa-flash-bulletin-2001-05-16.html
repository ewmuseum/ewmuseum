JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.5.16

Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.5.16 1) Taylor Threat 2) Tasogare no Prince ---- Welcome To My World...... Subject: Taylor ThreaT Location: Tokyo, Japan Time: 4:20 P.M. [Scene fades to backstage in The JPA arena, where there isn't a soul in sight. However, the camera has then picked up a man sitting on a folding chair in the middle of the ring. The camera zooms in, as it turns out to be none other than, the JPA new comer who's known as, Taylor ThreaT. Looking down at the ring, he slowly looks up, into the camera. He begins......] [T T] .Taylor ThreaT. Japan..... Taylor ThreaT's very first tour for a promotion that's not in the US, but an International Promotion.... And what better person to have touring for than Cyrus Williams. Now..... I don't know my other partner, and frankly, I'm not the type of guy to care. For those who don't know me...... I'm The great U-S-OF-A's Taylor ThreaT, and for those don't.... You will. [ACT I, SCENE I] [TOTALIMMORTAL] Now so far, I've noticed we've been kicking some.... Well, you know what, I been doing it back in the states, and here..... [polishing nailes] Here's no any different. Until then..... LATE. [FADE TO BLACK] [END OF ACT I] ---- [The scene is what appears to be an alley at night. A long limosine is parked at the curb, its engine running as it waits for its passenger. Finally, out of a nondescript metal door in the cement wall of the building next to the limo comes a familiar figure carrying a duffel bag. He is dressed entirely in black, with a long purple coat. His extremely long silver hair appears to be wet, and flows straight down his back past his waist. Despite the darkness, he wears a pair of sunglasses consisting of a single piece of purple plastic. As the limosine's driver holds the passenger door open for him, the Prince of Twilight puts his duffel into the back seat and begins to lower himself into the luxurious auto. Suddenly, a group of Japanese teenagers appears out of the rear of the alleyway, talking excitedly. The two boys and one girl seem to have been lying in wait for just such an opportunity as one of the boys speaks...] Boy 1: See, I told you they used this back alleyway exit sometimes!! Boy 2: It's Tasogare no Prince!! [Upon recognizing the Prince of Twilight, the girl's cheeks flush hotly and she takes a cautious step towards the limosine...] Girl: Tasogare no Prince - sama...?? [The Prince of Twilight turns towards the three young people, but appears to notice only the girl. As his driver steps forward to interject himself between the Prince and the three interlopers, but the Prince stops him with a gesture of his pale hand...] TnP: I'm afraid you have me at a disadvantage, watashi no hana. You know my name, yet, I do not know yours... Girl: M - Mayumi. [She bows nervously...] Boy 1: I'm Shinji! Boy 2: Tetsu!!! [Tasogare no Prince pays no mind to the two young men, smiling at the obviously embarrassed girl before him...] TnP: Mayumi, ne? What a lovely name. Tell me Mayumi-san, what can I do for you... and your freinds? Shinji: We just saw you wrestle Keiji Kobe. Tetsu: Yeah, it looked like he was going to beat you for sure this time!! [Mayumi shots an angry glare at her boisterous freind, but the Prince seems to pay him little mind...] Mayumi: Onegai shimasu, Prince-sama... TnP: You need not apologize for your companion, Mayumi-san. I can understand why someone without knowledge of the true events which transpired in the ring this evening could see it that way... [The second boy crosses his arms across his chest smugly...] Tetsu: Well, it sure looked that way to me! If the bell hadn't of rang, he'd have beaten you!! Mayumi: Be quiet, Tetsu!!! TnP: Iie, Mayumi-san. Is that what you think happened? [She bites one of her fingernails nervously...] TnP: [smiling} It's all right, hana, tell me. Mayumi: It certainly seemed that way to me, Prince-sama... [She looks away from him, embarassed. The Prince merely smiles and places a finger under her chin, bringing her face back around once again towards his...] TnP: Is it not possible, my young friends, that the match ended exactly as I _wished_ it to? [The two boys look at one another as the girl continues to stare into the Prince's hidden eyes...] Shinji: What do you mean?? TnP: You all seem to be under the mistaken notion that I consider defeating Kobe as my top priority. I assure you that is far from true. My only concern is winning the Pan-Asian title, and tonight, I took one step closer to that goal. After all, Kobe is the one who needs to defeat _me_. I do not have to beat him to prove myself. [He smiles at the girl, who's face turns an even darker shade of red...] Isn't that right, Mayumi?? Tetsu: Yeah, but if winning the title is so important, wouldn't getting 2 points for beating Kobe be better than just one for a draw?? [The Prince looks at him as one looks at puppy growling in false bravado...] TnP: A fair question. I could well have tried to defeat Kobe, but to do so may have required the expenditure of too much energy, leaving me at a disadvantage for my other tournament matches this week, and since I would be facing Eiji Yatsu, my closest competitor in the tournament this week, I could scarcely afford that, ne? To merely prevent Kobe from winning and forcing the draw was the more practical and certain way to insure that I keep my lead, and to insure that he remains well behind me!! Shinji: What about Yatsu?? TnP: What of him? Mayumi: Aren't you worried about facing him, Prince-sama? TnP: Why, Mayumi-san, do you think I should be? Tetsu: His Shooto style almost beat Kobe!! TnP: My dear boy, I am not Kobe. As I have said before, Shoot fighting is the last refuge of those not talented enough to study _true_ wrestling. He is of no concern to me. I am certain I will defeat him, and cement myself as the dominant force in the Pan-Asian tournament. What about you Mayumi-san? Do you believe that I will defeat Eiji Yatsu?? Mayumi: H - Hai, Prince-sama. TnP: If that is the case, then there is no way I can lose. Now, if you will all excuse me, it is late, and I must rest. That is, if you will excuse me?? [He takes the young girls hand, and bows to her deeply...] Mayumi: Of course... TnP: Ii desu ne? Farewell, watashi no itoshii hana. I can trust that you and your friends will be supporting me against Eiji Yatsu, ne? Mayumi: Hai, Prince-sama!! TnP: Arigatoo, gozaimasu... [With that, the Prince of Twilight gently kisses her forehead, and disappears back into the limosine, which pulls out of the alley and disappears down the Tokyo Streets, leaving the three young Japanese alone in the alleyway. As the two boys begin to walk off, the girl stares at the direction the black limosine drove off in, seemingly hypnotized, until her two consorts come back and take her with them...] ---- Japan Puroresu Association http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/ --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
