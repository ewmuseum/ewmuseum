JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.12

12 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.3.12 1) Eiji Yatsu 2) Jason Ramsey 3) Rick Taylor 4) Ryo Inoue ---- [One by one the silver and red letters appear on the screen] T. A. P. Voice Over: Total Attack Puroresu, the War for respect rages. [The camera fades into Riki Fudo and Eiji Yatsu sitting in front of a "T.A.P." logo. Riki Fudo is dressed in a red blazer with silver "T.A.P" logo, silver tie, white shirt, and navy slacks. Eiji has obviously changed his look with his dreadlocks only reaching to the bottom of his face. Yatsu's bangs are braided so they say out of his face. The dreadlocks are held together by alternating red and silver rubber bands. He's wearing a silver "T.A.P." t-shirt, his black judo belt, and nylon red adidas work-out pants.] Fudo: I am Riki Fudo, former PRN correspondent and I have been contracted to act as the head interviewer for T.A.P., and more specifically today to speak with a member of the fledgling ground, Eiji Yatsu. Yatsu, you haven't said much in the past few weeks, why is that? Yatsu: [A little anger in his voice.] Failure. Complete and absolute failure on my part to take two important opportunities to rise in prominence. First it was a loss to Tasogare no Prince. I was able to push Tasogare for over 20 minutes, but that pales in comparison to the 40 minutes by that fool Kenji Kobe. I should have been able to defeat Tasogare, let alone get a draw out of him, but I failed and feel to him. Then it was Fairchild. Billy is no more than a child with a tour of experience. He never practiced a fighting art as a youth, or even competed in any other sport. And I wasn't able to defeat him. I was devastated, and was considering leaving the company, until Inoue approached me. Fudo: Was it then that he asked you to join his new group? Yatsu: Hai. He said he saw my skill in the combat sports during my matches. He also said why I wasn't able to come out victorious in the important ones. Fudo: And what was that? Yatsu: It was what this whole war is about; style. Inoue made me realize that the lucha and strong-style of my attack was the reason the greatest style, shoot-style, wasn't able to win me any matches. Inoue said that he'd work with me, return me to Pure Fight, and give me a spot in T.A.P. Fudo: What do you say now the users of other styles? Yatsu: I have to laugh when they talk about strong-style's or now lucha's superiority. Someone claimed that the strong-style was the time-tested, mother-approved style of combat. As everyone knows, I practice judo, I wear my cherished belt around my waist to show my accomplishments in that sport. Judo is a fighting art, derived from an even older one, juijitsu. Let me ask you, Fudo, where the samurai in the days of the shogun going around giving each other Tiger Driver '91s? [Cutting off Fudo.] Of course not. It was juijitsu, judo, aikido, and other arts like those that were being developed and used, not this silly strong-style. Then they're the Iron Luchadores. Kobe and Hyun Bae are going around claiming that lucha libre is the greatest of them all. What is comical about this is, first, neither Kobe of Hyun Bae are from the country lucha was formulated, and second, lucha libre is more like a performing art than a fighting art. Lucha libre belongs more a three ring circus than in the ring used for wrestling. There is no doubt in my mind that the Pure Fight is the greatest style. Greater than strong-style, greater than lucha, greater than any other style on the planet. Kudo: What do you have to say concerning your up coming match with Ono and Hasegawa against the Japanese Gaijin and Jeremy Knyte? Yatsu: My partners don't concern me. Hasegawa practices strong-style, but Ono knows the power of Pure Fight. Either way we will work together, and we will win. As for my opponents, they obvious disagree with my views, but most importantly is the claim of Kojima that Otomo will show me who the real master of judo is. Otomo, I'm sure you did fine back in the states, probably winning numerous national tournaments. But I must ask you this, who has been superior in international judo competition. Not the United States, but Japan, over all others. Judo had its birth here in Nippon, and we have always been the best at it. Regardless, Otomo, I will show you that in this chapter of the story of world judo challenge, Japan will win once again. [The camera fades out.] ---- Scene opens in the back stage area of the Fukuoka Hakata Star Lanes. Jason Ramsey is making his way to the locker rooms when he runs into JPA referee Kentaro Honda. Ramsey puts down his duffle bag and starts to chat with Honda Ramsey: Looks like we're going to have a good house tonight. Yamato-san and Takamoto-san will be thrilled. Honda: Yes, it looks to be a great night night for matches too. By the way, how is Taylor -san? He and Inoue tried to KILL each other last time they locked up! Ramsey: Rick Taylor is one of the toughest men on planet Earth, and he's a total professional. To be honest, I think he gave Inoue the beating of his life. Taylor got banged up too but Inoue paid some dues. We talked about it and he says that he's back to 100%. Honda: He's teaming up with Shaw tonight. With Taylor, Shaw, Tokaio and Akita in one ring, it's going to be another very physical night for Taylor-san. Ramsey: Akita had better be at the top of his game tonight. Taylor and Shaw together make one hell of a dangerous team. Tokaio could bring a gun but it would probably just piss old Jack off! I wish I could have gotten him to accept my invitation to join the Alliance. He's really giving Rick, Bill, and myself a lot of support but I'd give my right arm for him to join us all the way. We respect his decision but all of us could learn a great deal from a legend like Shaw. He keeps saying he'd slow us down. I don't believe that for a second I bet he doesn't either. I know the fans don't. Honda: You have a match of your own tonight. Fairchild-san must be excited to get XYZ in the ring after all that's been said between the two of them. Ramsey: Yeah, I think Bill is gonna teach our alphabetical friend a little lesson about respect tonight. As far as myself and EIYUU go, I plan on taking him out of the equation early on so Fairchild and I can finish XYZ off and be showered and dressed before the main event. Honda: Very confident tonight, eh? Ramsey: Usually, I save my confidence for after the match but to be honest, tonight is different. EIYUU really doesn't have the talent to eve be in JPA, and XYZ is far too concerned with Billy to even be concerned with me. That will be his undoing. Honda: You stood up to both Inoue and Akita so far this tour, so I won't argue with you about that one. Ramsey: I was honored to be in the ring with a legend like Akita. To face the champion is always a thrill, and when that man is a legend like "Massive" Akita, it's even better. As far as Inoue goes, I've faced him three times in singles matches and he's beaten me three times. But each time it took him a little longer, and he had to work a little harder. The time will come when he faces me again and he learns yet another hard lesson about strong style superiority. Honda looks down at his pager and checks the message then looks back up to Ramsey. Honda: That's my cue. Takamoto is ready to meet with all of the referees. I'll see you later and good luck tonight. Ramsey: Thanks, and be sure to wish the same to EIYUU and XYZ. They'll need it! (Ramsey picks up his bag and waves to Honda before walking into the locker room. Scene fades.) ---- [The JPA Dojo. The lights are off, except for a single, bare bulb hanging above the center of the ring. In the dim light we can see a man sitting in the corner of the ring, his legs splayed in front of him, his arms resting on the bottom ropes, his head resting against the second turnbuckle. His wrists are taped with sweaty, ragged-looking athletic tape that is beginning to lose its adhesiveness and peel off. He is wearing the official JPA blue and white athletic pants, training boots, and a green Alliance logo t-shirt with the sleeves cut off. The intricate and somewhat morbid tattoos on his arms are a dead giveaway as to who it is before we ever see his face. His head still resting against the second turnbuckle, and therefore, still in the shadows, he begins to speak....] Taylor: A stepping stone? I am a stepping stone? I am nobody's stepping stone. Not Masashi Akita's, not Shiro Oodori, not Samuel Otomo, and certainly not an egomaniac with delusions of grandeur like Ryo Inoue. Inoue, you should leran to wathc what the hell you say before you open your mouth and start embarrassing yourself. You talk a big game about your "pure fight" and how Strong style is "weak." Well, you didn't look like you'd been partying after our match at Okayama. You were carried out by your friends. I find it hard to believe that you have many, but apparently you do. Thirty minutes. Thirty minutes of you and I beating the holy hell out of each other. Too bad that match won't ever see release on home video. Those 2800 fans that were there at the Budokan were lucky. Hell, I looked at the Observer this morning and Meltzer is already gushing his spleen about it, calling it "early match of the year material." Hell, our own in house press is calling it the "stiffest and most brutal" match so far in JPA's young history. I beat the hell out of you. You beat the hell out of me. You see, the difference between the two of us, Inoue, is that I am able to acknowledge that you did a number on me. You, on the other hand, cannot stand the fact that our match went the limit, and you couldn't get the job done. I know this. I saw your eyes as I offered my hand, not in friendship, not in congratulations for a great match. I offered my hand to you to see what kind of man you really are. No one won or lost that match, and yet you took it as a loss, I know you did. Your eyes gave that away. Like I said, I think it's a shame that only 2800 people got to see that match, because since only 2800 people saw the match, that means only 2800 people got to see what type of human being you are. I offered you my hand, no matter what my personal feelings about you, no matter my professional feelings about you, I offered you my hand in what I knew was a futile attempt to show respect, not for your shooto style or your attitude, because, quite frankly, I don't really think much of either, but to show respect for the fact that you and I had just beaten the ever-loving crap out of each other, the point of exhaustion and near incapacitation for both you and I, but no... just like I knew you would do, you just look at me. Telling me with that glare that you are embarrassed that you couldn't beat me. That you had promised all your Tatami buddies and playmates that you would crush the strong style-using gaijin, that you would crush my nose into powder. Even now, you still think that bothers me? Break my nose again, I dare you. I told you, Inoue. I told you that I would be ON for that match. Did I lie? No longer can you call strong style weak, Inoue, because if you do, you know you're a liar. For THIRTY MINUTES, I fought you with pure strong style, and you could not walk out to your dressing room without help. And I won't lie, I couldn't either, but I have no problem with admitting that you gave me one serious beating out there. [He pauses for a moment, exhales audibly, and sighs before chuckling a couple of times and then continuing.] Taylor: Who would have ever thought of this situation? Me, a gaijin, picking up the puroresu strong style banner and carrying it into battle as its defender? What a bizarre situation, eh? Inoue, you have offended me. Personally and professionally. I don't take kindly to people taking up after beating me ONCE and declaring how uch they know me, how horrible I am, how inferior I am to them... because that's all a bunch of B.S. You don't know a single thing about me, Inoue. All you know is that you beat me once, and you were lucky enough to get in dead-on shot that broke my nose. How impressive. Broken noses mean nothing to me, Inoue. This time, it was I that did the breaking. However, I broke something far more precious to you that your nose. I broke something that you hold especially dear. I broke the one thing you have, Inoue, and I didn't just break it... I all but shattered it. Not anything on your body, because we all know that wouldn't bother you, unless it was your mouth, because then you couldn't run it like you love to... no, I destroyed the one thing that keeps you going, Inoue. I broke your PRIDE, Inoue. I took and broke your precious false pride in your shooto tactics. You claimed and spouted off for weeks that all it would take was your shooto style to take me down for the count, and to put me away. Well, you, I, the entire JPA organization and the 2800 fans that were there at the Budokan all know that you could not back up those words. And it is tearing you up inside, I know it. You make false promises, Inoue. I never promised a win in our match. I only promised that you would receive a lesson in what "total attack" really is, and I, unlike you, made good on my word. So this last match was the most brutal and stiffest so far yet in JPA history? Well, I say that the JPA is still VERY young, and Inoue, I'm not finished with you yet. Not by a long shot. Beware, because you have not yet faced me for the last time. [Fade to black, which is then invaded by a flaming green and black Alliance logo, whoich is then dissolved into a dark green screen, before again going black.] ---- [open camera shot, one by one the silver and red letters appear on-screen] T. A. P. Voice Over: Total Attack Puroresu. The War For Respect Is On... [The letters fade. We are treated to hand held footage of Ryo Inoue and Rick Taylor being carried away from ringside by ring attendants following their 3.10.2001 match at Chugoku Okayama Budokan. Both men are exhausted and barely show any signs of energy] Voice of Ryo inoue: My "Guze", my focus has been respect Rick Taylor. I look for respect from Genji Yamato, he throws mud in my face. I seek respect from Akita and he turns his back on me claiming my respect for him is false. Then there is you Rick-chan. You who offer me a hand? [The handhel footage is replaced by a still shot of Taylor offering his hand to Inoue after the match as a sign of "respect"] Voice of Ryo Inoue: Your respect is meant to mock me. Oh you fight like a devil Rick-chan. You taxed me and my skills nearly to their limit to prove that you could "hang" with the real fighters. You answered every punch and kick I threw with one equal to it, if not more powerful. But you couldn't put me away Rick-chan. you tried and tried but couldn't do it. I had nothing to prove really. I've beaten you once already Rick-chan. I would've loved to have beaten you again to prove my point but your "pride" would not let you lose. Now I ask you... Was it worth it Rick-chan? Was the punishment I have you worth your foolish Gaijin "Pride". I curbed my Violence to see if you were capable of a true fight. I realize now that I should have unleashed my Violence to retire you and make you eat through a straw the rest of your life. You offer a hand to me? Why? So when Jack Shaw beats a less than fit Ryo Inoue you can take the credit for "Softening" me up? [[The still shot fades to Ryo Inoue's face, a lone finger before it waving back and forth] Ryo Inoue: The True Fighters do not fight that way. I went from my fight with you, sprained neck and all to fight Jack Shaw the next night. Tell me, did you enjoy your little tag match? did your puo Jason Ramsey serve you well while you sat on the ring apron? The True Fighter that I am would not let me change my match. i was offered as much by Yamato but threw his offer back in his face. I signed to fight Jack Shaw and i fought Jack shaw. Jack Shaw beat me and i say... "Good For Jack Shaw". He won't beat Akita. that won't happen. Why? Because it is not his destiny. [Ryo stops wagging his finger and now points it angrily at the screen] Ryo Inoue: THAT IS MY DESTINY NOT JACK SHAW'S! IT IS MY DESTINY NOT RICK TAYLOR'S!! LIKE IT OR NOT GENJI YAMATO, I WILL CHOOSE THE TIME AND PLACE AKITA WILL BE BEATEN... [Ryo raises his empty hands before his face] by my hands. No Ricjk Taylor, I will not shake your hand so you can mock me for losing to Jack Shaw. I look to the next tour when we meet again. I look to June and Yokohama Arean. I look to T.A.P. promoting the fights the fans demand. I look forward to hearing you scream again... and again... and again... [Ryo turns around and walks away] [Fade out] ---- http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/ --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
