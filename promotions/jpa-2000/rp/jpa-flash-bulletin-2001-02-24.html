JPA FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.24

24 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2001.2.24 1) The Great Tokaio 2) Corey Hart ---- [Darkness. Words in archaic script appear on the screen, as does a single candle light against the background of darkness.] On the Great Way, There is no Gate, Only a thousand pahs To choose from. Find the Gate And you may walk alone Between Heaven and Earth... [And just as soon as they appeared, the words disappear. And we come closer to the candlelight. It's luminance reigns down upon the face of only one thing- the face of the Great Tokaio.] Tokaio: Ryo Inoue is one of those who master one skill or one art who are called expert in their particular ways, but that is not called complete adepthood... [Tokaio is sitting on a wooden floor, with his knees on the ground and his hands cupped over his knees. His eyes are closed, and the little beads of sweat slowly fall down his forehead and onto his cheek.] Tokaio: In the days of the Samurai, bushi would charge into battle with sakki, or "death lust." Anyone in their way would face the Samurai with sakki or fear. And by meeting sakki with sakki, a samurai could vanquish his opponent with his sword. [Tokaio's eyes open, glaring through.] Tokaio: However, you cannot defeat that which does not exist. [Tokaio slowly rises to his feet, producing a bokken, a wooden sword, from the darkness as he rises above the lone candle's light.] Tokaio: Zen calls it taking the head of the lance and turn it around to stab the other. Whether your opponent strikes first or you strike first, if you fix your mind on the person at all, or on the defense, the attack, the distance, the timing, your actions WILL falter. The result is that you may defeated. [Tokaio holds the sword in front of him, holding above the light.] Tokaio: Since your mind is taken up with your opponent when you put yourself in an adversarial position, you shouldn't keep your mind on yourself either. [Tokaio suddenly swings the sword, flinging the still-lit candle into a hearth, and thereby enlightening the other half of the room.] Tokaio: In emptiness, there is no energy. There is no movement. The animal spirit you project, no matter how ferocious can only rebound back upon yourself. Those who do project so can be defeated. [The Great Tokaio swings again, repositioning himself to face the fire-light.] Tokaio: So people who stir up the courage of bloodlust with afflicted minds may at some point have the force to break through iron walls, but there is inevitably a time when bloodlust is exhausted and moods change. A sturdy heart, in contrast, is immovable and does not change. If warriors cultivate it, why wouldn't they develop such a sturdy heart? [With a minor movement, the bokken is hurled from his hand and into the fire...] Tokaio: If they have afflicted minds, and by afflicted any distraction or thought at all, even people of tremendous martial valor will find that when they face the end of their lives and the killer ghost of impermanence comes after them, their usual forcefulness will run out, their courageous ferocity will be gone, and they will be unable to muster any strength... [Tokaio returns to a sitting position, his eyes shut again.] Tokaio: Those who stand against Akita-sama and I will have no strength... For Akita-sama is the Double Titles Champion, and I... [The Great Tokaio opens his eyes once more, a cold piercing stare on his face.] Tokaio: I AM emptiness... [The fire dies out...] ---- (The scene opens at a small beach near Malibu, the sun just beginning to rise. As the birds soar past the clouds, the camera pans the glittering sand, the sound of water washing against the shore audible in the background. Suddenly, a lone figure is seen jogging along the sand, a small, brown terrier following at his heels. Upon closer inspection, the figure is revealed as "Captivating" Corey Hart. The young blond wears a white, tank top and navy blue, mesh shorts. He completes the look with a blue, baseball cap perched atop his short, curly mane. As he notices the camera, a smile moves across his handsome face, dimples visible. He pauses for a moment and stops to pick up a stick buried in the sand. With a flourish, he tosses it into the air and watches as the dog chases after it. Corey slows to a walk as he nears the camera.) Corey: Hey, looks like you guys caught me in the middle of one of my morning jogs, huh? You're probably wondering why I'm not in Tokyo right now. Well, I decided to take advantage of the JPA's down time and spend a few days back home until the next tour. (with a nod towards the horizon) Can you blame me? This place is beautiful. (sighs) Now I can see where Billy Fairchild was coming from. After spending just a few days here, I know it's going to be so hard to leave again. (He folds his arms across his chest.) You know, I'm really excited about the prospects of this new tour. I'll be the first to admit that I wasn't too pleased with my performance during The Coronation Tour, in particular the tag loss that We Hyun Bae and I suffered at the hands of EIYUU and Kenji Okano. I mean, I have no problem losing to any of the members of the JPA roster but losing to….to…some spoiled ex-soccer player and his arrogant buddy was more than a little disappointing. Everyone knows that I can't stand either EIYUU or Kenji. I've had problems with them since I first encountered them during the first JPA show. Their racist comments and snide remarks are just two of the main reasons that I don't particularly care for them. Their snobby attitudes are another, especially Okano's. I'm sure he's laughing it up about getting the win over two " no-talent outsiders". (he frowns) Well, enjoy yourself while you can, Kenji, because this isn't over. Our paths are going to cross again, whether in a singles match or a tag match, and I can assure you that you won't be so lucky next time. (With a dismissive wave of his hand.) Enough about Kayo. He'll get his in due time. I want to talk about my next opponents, Jeremy Knyte and Christopher Kojima. It seems that you two guys must have upset someone in the JPA booking committee because they're feeding you to two very pissed off wrestlers, namely We Hyun and I. As I stated before, he and I weren't at all happy about our last loss and we are looking to not only avenge ourselves but we're also looking to let off some steam, which is where you poor guys come in. We would prefer to be kicking the butts of Kenji, EIYUU, or even XYZ but you two are going to have to do. I guess I should apologize ahead of time for the beating that we're going to give you, huh? (smiles) I'm quite familiar with Knyte. He and I have faced each other before and I think he's a really talented wrestler even if he is a bit of a loose cannon. Kojima, on the other hand, I know almost nothing about but I couldn't help but be amused by his statements. I don't have the "heart" of a true wrestler, Christopher? We Hyun is "hardcore trash"? (He chuckles) Boy, the match hasn't even started yet and you're already making stupid mistakes. One of the first things I learned in wrestling school is to never underestimate an opponent because it could come back to bite you in the ass. I guess you were asleep during that lecture, huh, Chris? (He smirks) If you have any questions about the abilities of myself or We Hyun, I suggest you view some of the old JPA matches. You'll see that he is far from trash and I do indeed know more about having a "fighting spirit" than most rookies out there. While you're viewing the tapes, make sure you take note of how well we work together as a team. Take note of how well our styles compliment one another, almost as if we know what the other is thinking. That comes from the experience that we have tagging together, another strike against you and Knyte. You see, We Hyun and I aren't just tag partners. We're also friends and we know each other fairly well. Now, contrast that with yourself and Knyte, some make shift team that the JPA brass decided to throw together, and you will see that you are at quite the disadvantage. With such a big handicap already against you and your partner, do you honestly believe that you can afford to overlook and underestimate your opponents? (smiles) I don't think so. (Just then, the dog returns, stick in mouth. He bounces around, dancing around Corey's feet. Corey bends down and ruffles the dog's hair.) Corey: Good boy, Max. (He takes the stick from Max's mouth before returning his full attention to the camera.) I know that I'm just a rookie, Christopher, but I've got a little friendly advice for you. Come February 24th, I hope that you come to fight. I hope that you're ready for We Hyun and I because if you're expecting some "gaijin no-talents" then you're going to be in for quite a rude awakening. I'm just telling you because you seem like a cool guy…Heck, maybe with a little attitude adjustment, we could be friends…maybe. Anyway, I just wanted to tell you that We Hyun and I are going to be on a warpath. We want a victory and will settle for nothing less. So, you can definitely expect us to be giving 110% out there. We've been disappointed with a loss already and we won't settle for another one. Be warned! You can chose to ignore what I'm telling you if you want but that's up to you. (He shrugs his shoulders) Your funeral. I'm just trying to help a guy out. I don't want to see you make the same mistakes other unprepared rookies have made. (With that, he tosses the stick in the air, Max again giving chase.) Corey: Now, if you will all excuse me, I've got a jog to complete. See you in Tokyo. (He flashes the camera a smile before racing off after Max, the scene fading to black.) ---- http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/ --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
