Epitaph Written by AI, 2026

The Japan Puroresu Association was a puroresu-styled federation set in Japan and run under its president, Genji Yamato, a former holder of the Double Titles in Neo Japan Pro Wrestling. It opened with ARRIVAL at Tokyo Differ Ariake on 28 October 2000, broadcast on Samurai TV, and a second ARRIVAL followed in November; after a troubled December it announced a weekly television deal for its Battle START! program on TV Tokyo.

Its 2001 was booked as tours on the Japanese model. CORONATION ran sixteen dates from 13 January to 3 February around an eight-man tournament for the reactivated J1 Rikidozan/Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Unified Heavyweight Championship, and MASSIVE Akita beat Ryo Inoue in the final at Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall. The ROYAL SERIES followed with eighteen dates from 24 February to 24 March, closing with Akita's first defense of the Double Titles against Rick Taylor.

The tours' running story was Inoue's revolt. Beaten in the final, he withdrew from his rematch, declared a War for Respect against Yamato and strong style, and formed the shoot-style T.A.P. with Eiji Yatsu and the fighter Naoto Kangyo, who beat Shinji Nakata, Yuzo Moriboshi and Jinshi Togawa at Ryogoku. The American gaijin answered with a faction of their own, the Alliance.

Flash Bulletins gathered the roster's roleplays ahead of the cards, and results were posted date by date with finishes, times and paid attendance, from crowds of nine hundred in Kyoto to more than ten thousand at Ryogoku.

The third tour, JUNIOR STRUGGLE, began late, on 6 May, with a round-robin league for a new Pan Asia Juniorheavyweight Championship. The last results cover 21 May, with Tasogare no Prince leading the league; the Tokyo Budokan final set for 16 June is not in the surviving record, and a newsgroup reply that June reports the promotion had stopped.
