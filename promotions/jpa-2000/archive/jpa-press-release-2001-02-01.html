JPA Press Release 2001.2.1

1 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a statement before the press today, Japan Puroresu Association president Genji Yamato expressed a certain disappointment over comments made by CORONATION Tournament finalist Ryo Inoue. Said Yamato, "For Inoue, by his own words or via spokesperson, to proclaim he is the only person in the history of puroresu to be deserving of holding the J1 Rikidozan/NJPW Unified Heavyweight Titles, shows a deep and regrettable hubris on his part." Yamato, a former holder of the Double Titles himself in Neo Japan Pro Wrestling, continued, saying: "Clearly, Inoue is entitled to his own opinions, and a certain amount of enthusiasm is expected going into the tournament finals this Saturday. At the same time, for comments such as these to come at a time when the whole of the puroresu world in Japan and abroad is watching the outcome...and to further proclaim his devotion to shooto style alone as being the determining factor, leads me to believe that Inoue's zeal for shooto may make him somewhat of a loose cannon in the Japan Puroresu Association." He then concluded with: "Inoue is someone I firmly believe should have held this very crown over a year ago, and I do wish him the best of luck against a pwerful veteran, and champion in his own right, such as Masashi Akita." Yamato assured the press that his feelings about Inoue's comments would not affect the outcome of Saturday's tournament final, quipping "That's for the other side of the Pacific."
