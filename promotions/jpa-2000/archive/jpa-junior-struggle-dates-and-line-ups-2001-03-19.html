JPA JUNIOR STRUGGLE dates and tournament line-ups announced

19 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION OFFICIAL PRESS RELEASE 2001.3.19 TOKYO -- The Japan Puroresu Association has released the dates, venues, and tournament line-ups for the April/May JUNIOR STRUGGLE tour. First, the tournament: Eight of the top juniorheavyweights currently in the JPA will be involved in a league-style tournament (round-robin, points based), running throughout the tour, beginning on 4.21. The wrestlers with the top two scores at the end of the regular rounds will then advance to the Finals on 5.19. The winner of the tournament will be the JPA's inaugural PAN ASIA JUNIORHEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. One item of note is that the tour-ending show will not be held at Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall this time; rather, the JPA will make its debut in TOKYO BUDOKAN on 5.19. Budokan holds slightly over 16,000 persons, over 6,000 more than Ryogoku. The JPA is banking on this tour being its biggest to date.
