JPA JUNIOR STRUGGLE 2001.5.16 Joetsu Welfare Ministry Meeting Hall RESULTS

16 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Another overflowing show for the JPA today, with 1300+ people squeezing into what is normally a 1,250 seater venue, at the Joetsu Welfare Ministry Meeting Hall in Niigata Prefecture. Yamato-san mentioned that he is going to have to look into booking larger venues if the rises continue (although, they are not significant rises, to be fair, however twice this week alone, JPA officials have been politely warned by fire department officials about building capacities). In the big news, Tasogare no Prince assured his spot in the 6.16 Tokyo Budokan finals for the Junior Struggle League, and a shot at the Pan Asia Juniorheavyweight Championship, beating another heavy favorite, T.A.P.'s Eiji Yatsu, pinning the young shooter dangerously close to time limit, to take the assured lead in the league at 9 points. It should be noted, that Keiji Kobe, the "other" favorite, has wrestled but twice in the league thus far, and has 4 matches to go before the first rounds of the tournament are over. Yatsu declined comment, as did his second, T.A.P. leader Ryo Inoue. It is assumed both will address the media in their own manner.
