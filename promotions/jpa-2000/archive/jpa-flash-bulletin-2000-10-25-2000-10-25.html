JPA Flash Bulletin 2000.10.25

25 October 2000
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION FLASH BULLETIN 2000.10.25 http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net 1) The Great Tokaio 2) Aroku Murakami 3) Ryo Inoue 4) Corey Hart -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[It is the inside of a small, white room. American interviewer extraordinaire, Alex Tracer, kneels down on a small cushion. He's dressed rather formally in an expensive blue Italian suit.]
AT: [Smiling.] Konnichiwa and greetings, everyone. I'm Alex Tracer, and for this episode of "In The Limelight," is my guest tonight, the
Great Tokaio!
[Slowly the camera backs up, and there, across from Tracer and dressed in a simple robe, kneels the Great Tokaio. Tokaio, with no face paint, gently bows his head to the camera, and then turns to Tracer. A calm expression is presented on the face of Tokaio.]
GT: [Nods his head.] Kangei, Alex-kun. Arigato naru. AT: [Nods back.] Sir, domo arigato for allotting time out of your busy schedule for this interview. It is good to see you again.
GT: It is good to see you, too. AT: Shall we begin? GT: Hai. AT: I believe I should start by asking you what your plans are for the
Japan Puroresu Association?
GT: Fair enough question. As you know, for the majority of my career,
I have toured across the world- my homeland, America, Canada, South
America, Europe, and so forth. JPA is my first opportunity to "plant roots," so to speak. The constant over-booking and traveling wore me out. Not bad for a career, but it has worn me out several times.
AT: I understand. Every wrestler has their limit stretched by the road. GT: Unlike many of my peers, I do not claim to be "puroresu no kami-sama." I am just who I am. I am not a god. I am a warrior. No more, no less. I have done everything in my power to keep my focus and concentration on the task I have at hand. That is all my honor demands of me.
AT: You certainly seem to greatly value your honor. Would you say that you are the equivalent of a modern day samurai?
GT: [Pauses briefly.] No. The difference is that, unlike my distant ancestors, I am not a feudal retainer. My life I bind with my honor.
I will do no such thing to dishonor my family or me. The ring is my battlefield. My hands are my weapons. I am a warrior like the samurai of old, but I am not one. A samurai has a lord that tells him who he must do battle with and why. I have no such master.
AT: Then why do you fight? GT: [Intensely.] Why I battle is my own reason. The battle IS it's own reason. You do not ask why a plague spreads or a field burns.
You cannot ask why I fight. AT: Very well. May I ask why you've chosen this gimmick? The face paint, the mist... One might think it's a stereotype since it is done so much.
GT: One would consider the possibility that my choice of ring gimmicks is to honor many of the legends of Japanese wrestling- The Great Togo,
Mr. Pogo, Professor Tanaka, and so forth. However, my father, Hasiro
Tosinabe, also was a wrestler. He wrestled in similar garb, and this is to honor him.
AT: I see. I believe you will honor your father very well. GT: Arigato. AT: Now, I'd like to talk about your upcoming match with Harley Tower. He represents part of the gaijin element of JPA. What are your thoughts about that? [A puzzled look cross the Great Tokaio's face, but quickly subsides.] GT: That Harley Tower is gaijin? Ah, I understand the meaning of your question. Some of my peers believe that gaijin are a venom to
Japanese wrestling, and should be purged. They think that gaijin do not share the same spirit that we "homegrown" Japanese carry. I know better than that. I have wrestled all over the world. With the luchadores of Mexico, with the mat technicians of Canada, with the brawlers and "sports entertainers" of the United States. The Senshi spirit is individual. Those who have the heart and strength to carry it are those who I wish to face. Just because one has a different approach does not mean they do not have the fighting spirit.
AT: And what of Tower himself? GT: As far as Harley Tower is concerned, the image he has created of himself is what he wants to be. He may be "fabulous," but I will be the better wrestler at Arrival. I have a mission: to rain the blood of my family down on the JPA. The JPA will be taken to levels higher than ever seen before! It will be I who takes it there. It sounds ambitious, but it is very, very possible.
AT: I believe I have more than enough from this interview. Do you have any parting words for your opponent?
[The gentle look on the Great Tokaio's face turns intense.] GT: Hai. [Tokaio turns to the camera.] Tower-san, your only machigai was to be booked to face me. It is nothing personal, but my campaign starts with you.
AT: I do not wish to take up any more of your time, Tokaio-san. I wish you luck and honor in your future endeavors. [Bows his head.]
Thank you for your time. GT: [Returns the bow.] Thank you, Tracer-san. [Fade to black.] -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[The camera opens to an alley somewhere. There are tall buildings on each side of the alley and also one behind it, making the alley curve around and out of view. The moon is just rising so it is dark outside, but also very light. A breeze blows trash around in the alley. Some cars pass behind the camera but other than that and the wind there is no sound whatsoever.
The building's windows are all broken out and the bricks are colored as if they were burned some time ago, yellow police WARNING tape is seen strung all around the whole area marking that some sort of crime took place here a long time ago. The camera zooms around picking up nothing at all and continues to move forward. Within a few seconds a scratching sounds is heard and the camera jerks to he ground to reveal a crushed aluminum can skidding across the floor and then the picture jerks up to a man seen standing cross armed against the buildings rotted and burnt door. He is a man of
Asian/American decent. He's starting straight into the camera with his milky white pupil less eyes. He's wearing tight black leather black pants and a white wife beater showing off his chiseled physique. His jet black hair is combed forward and gelled into pointy spikes. He starts to speak but his nose and mouth aren't seen due to his black martial artist nose/mouth face guard
(think of the Mortal Kombat Ninja's). This man is none other than
"The Dragon" Aroku Murakami. Murakami: Forget about the formalities, catch phrases, and the gimmicks I am Aroku Murakami, the JPA's newest six figure addition to their talent roster. From a young age all kids in Vietnam learn to respect the land on which they walk on and the society they live in. During my youth I learned to appreciate all that Vietnam has to offer, the culture, the technology, the religion and I threw it all away because it meant nothing. When you are a child of undesirable wealth you have the power to appreciate whomever and
whatever you want. One thing that is sacred in Japan is wrestling. Unlike American wrestling, which is treated like a secret society of losers and hate mongers that just crave blood and sex in that form of "sports entertainment."
On the other hand wrestling here is treated with the respect and dignity this sport deserves. In America you wrestlers are treated as clowns and can make a career just coming up with silly catch phrases and performing
non-sensical moves that have all the impact of a slap in the face. Remember that
Gaijin's, you are lucky to be apart of a sport that you have no business partaking in but you're the least of my worries.
(Aroku walks down the alley a little way. A cop car races by on the nearby streets. Just then some thunder is heard and the wind picks up a little. The clouds roll in quickly and soon it begins to rain lightly. Aroku ignores it and keeps walking.)
Murakami: I am a champion, forged from the finest fires that Vietnam had to offer. I promise you this, October 28, the Tokyo Differ Ariake.
Thousands of eyes will snap open and be ripped from their private viewing screens. As this new face emerges and imprints itself on their memories and a new name lingers on the tips of their tongues. You won't forget me, or anything about me. I don't breath fire, I don't fly, and my body isn't obviously covered in scales but I possess the most lethal and crippling abilities of a
Dragon, the
abilities that most of you alone will feel. Kenji Okano, EIYUU....I know nothing about you and I I could care less about either of you, you both were spared when you were paired to be apart of MY team and as long as you stay out of my way you will be spared once again.
(Aroku slowly walks towards the camera as it starts to back up keeping him in it's picture.)
Murakami: Kobe, Hart, Hyun. You three are the unfortunates, none of you have half a clue what to expect and even if you did you'd still be misinformed. Don't underestimate me yet alone my partners, not even for a second, because once you do .. (pause).. Well, you'll see.
(The camera stops and spins in a 180 angle degree as Murakami is seen nearing his black 1999 Nissan Skyline. He opens the door)
Murakami: I will be your future.....I will be your ecstasy..... (Murakami gets into his car and fires up the engine as the power windows slide down simutaneously and the camera turns to Murkami in his drivers seat.)
Murakami: Come take your best shot as us! (Fade to Black) ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[open camera shot. We are in the infamous Steel Wind Dojo in Tokyo. The place is the main training hall. RThe gathered students are watching intently as Ryo Inoue is giving a demo on how ot throw a proper kick. Standing across from him is a young man, no doubt a trainee of the dojo, the young man is intense and ready. He takes a strong step toward Ryo and is met by kick so fast and powerful that it sends him sprawling on the canvas. The students watching the demo are in awe as Ryo stands calmly over the fallen youth]
[We are now in the locker room for instructors at the Steel Wind dojo. Ryo Inoue sits on a bench seated next to Yazuo Tomizawa] Tomizawa: Yazuo Tomizawa here for Japan Puroresu Assocition with former neo Japan star ryo Inoue. Ryo-san it is a pleasure
[shakes Ryo's hand] Ryo: Domo Tomizawa-san Tomizawa: Everyone has been eagerly awaiting to hear what you would have to say about JPA's firt card. Also the fans have been wondering what you've upto these past few months since Burning Japan folded.
Ryo: [nods a fwe times] Hai, hai... Well the fans have been restless it seems. Most know that I tested my skill in the ring against
Vhladislav Golychunk at the TATAMI MMA event in September. That was a tough fight but I came out on top in that struggle.
Tomizawa: Hai, that was tremendous struggle. But what of the JPA ARRIVAL? Ryo: [looking down slightly and nodding] Hai, hai... Tokyo Differ
Ariake gets it's first shot of my "Pure Sweat" eh? A main event tag match no less. I'm teaming with Hasegawa-san against Togawa and
Murayama. [Ryo nods slowly again nd sratches his chin] I have faith in Hasegawa as a partner. ButI really want ot see the fighters JPA is putting me up against. I plan on taking Togawa on full force. I will hold back nothing. Just as I did in the past. My record speaks for tisel no? Ask the bodies from Neo Jpan how they felt after I beat them down. Ask Hiroyoshi Tenzaki how his shoulder is after twisted it in nine unnatural ways last summer. Ask these men what makes Ryo
Inoue so dangerous and all will tell you it is because he holds nothing back.
Tomizawa: It sounds like you are trying to prove something in this first match. Ryo: [scratches his chin] Maybe I am. Maybe I'm trying to show these
"fighters" that my goal of attaining "Pure Fighting Perfection" will not be denied. Jinshi Togawa, I am talking to you. Bring your "A"
Game to the Differ Ariake. Bring the hardest punch, elbow or kick you can throw. Why am telling you this? Because if you do not then I WILL beat you silly. I WILL dominate you. If you come to that ring in
Differ Ariake in a less than serious manner I WILLcrush you and humiliate you so soundly that the fans who paid money to see you will boo you out of every major arena in Japan. As for you murayama... I haven't forgot you, don't worry. Hasegawa and I will show both you and
Togawa that as long as trur fighter is in the ring neither of you will stand a chance!
Tomizawa: OH!! SUGOI! Tokyo Differ Ariake!! Inoue-san any last comment? Ryo: [raising a fist to the camera] SHOOTO!! [fade out] ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(The scene opens to a crowded street in downtown Tokyo, on a cool afternoon, the sun peeking from the clouds. The street is lined with shops and restaurants. The camera pans the crowd of people before landing on one lone youth. The young man wears a white T-shirt, the word "QUEER"
emblazoned on the chest in bold letters. He completes the outfit with baggy, blue denim jeans and Nike tennis shoes. His platinum blond hair is short and curled, framing his handsome face. Slung over his shoulders is a blue backpack and dangling around his neck is a small camera. He pauses for a moment, stopping to snap photos of his surroundings, his blue eyes filled with a mix of astonishment and wonder. He stops as he notices the JPA cameras, a smile moving across his lips, dimples in his cheeks. Looking at him, the sinewy youth appears much younger than his 21 years.)
Youth: (waves) Hi, I'm Corey Hart, known as "Captivating" Corey Hart to some wrestling fans. I must tell you that I'm really stoked to be in Japan.
So far, everyone has been really helpful and accommodating. Plus, I've been catching some great sights. Heck, if it wasn't for the language barrier, I would almost feel like I was back home. But the most exciting thing about being here is being able to compete for the JPA. There is nothing quite like a Puroresu promotion. I find them to be quite innovative and second to none.
Don't get me wrong. I love American wrestling federations. In fact,
I've
wrestled for a few but nothing beats the work ethic of the Puroresu feds.
Besides, it should be great to finally be a part of a federation where skill and wrestling ability is valued over mic skills or gimmicks.
(Corey grins, folding his arms across his chest.) Corey: It's going to be really exciting stepping into the ring again, especially with a crowd that enjoys good wrestling. I must confess that it's been a couple of months since my last match so I hope that I'm not too rusty.
I have been training quite hard in preparation for my debut match. Considering the talent level in the JPA, I had best stay on my toes! I don't know much about my opponents but I offer them nothing but respect.
From my understanding, Kenji Okano, EIYUU, and Murakami are all great wrestlers. I'm sure that I will walk away from this match with quite a bit of knowledge.
Not only will I learn from those three but I'll also be learning from my own two tag partners, We Hyun Bae and Iron Chef Keiji Kobe. I haven't met either of these guys yet but I want them to know that they have my respect and I promise that I won't let either of them down. I promise to be at 110% for our tag match. I only hope that you two do the same. I've never worked a tag match before so this is all quite new to me but I know that I have the skills to hang with any of the talent here. Otherwise, the JPA wouldn't have hired me in the first place. With that said, I promise to hold up my end during the match. You two guys have my word.
As far as my opponents go, I don't know you guys very well but I do know that you're in for quite a fight. While I can respect your abilities and skills, that won't stop me from doing everything in my power to secure a win for my team. Well, I'm not really the type to toss around idle threats so
I'll save
my talking for the ring. Good luck, gentlemen. You'll need it.
(With that, Corey turns on his heels, snapping more pictures.) -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
