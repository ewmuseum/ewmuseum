JPA Press Release: January Tour Dates Announced

29 December 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association recently held a press conference, about its recent troubles which lead to the cancellation of the planned December "ARRIVAL" show, and its plans for the future. JPA President Genji Yamato had this to say: * Despite the difficulties faced by the promotion during the month of December, the JPA will continue to operate, and have announced the dates for their January "CORONATION" tour: TOUR DATES: 1/13: Tokyo Differ Ariake [TV TAPING] 1/15: Hachiiuji Multipurpose Hall 1/16: Kawasaki Gymnasium 1/18: Yokosuka Athletics Meeting Hall 1/19: Odahara Arena Sub-Arena 1/20: Niigata Phase 1/21: Shizuoka Shin Messe [TV TAPING] 1/22: Hamamatsu Gymnasium 1/25: Kobe Self-Defense Hall 1/26: Mie Yokka Australia Memorial Hall 1/27: Osaka Prefectural Gym #2 1/28: Kyoto KBS Hall 1/29: Shiga Prefectural Gymnasium 1/31: Fukuoka Hakata Star Lanes 2/1: Kumamoto Gymnasium 2/3: Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall [Megacard, TV TAPING] * The January tour will feature the namesake CORONATION tournament, during which the promotional heavyweight champion will be crowned, at the tour ending show at Ryogoku Sumo Hall on 2/3. The 8-man, 2-bracket tournament will hold its opening rounds on 1/13, the semi-finals on 1/21, with the finals on 2/3. * At this point, Mr. Yamato unveiled the title belts -- with the agreement of Mr. Genichiro Matsumura, the former CEO of Neo Japan Pro Wrestling, of which Yamato was a member during its operation, the promotional heavyweight title for the Japan Puroresu Association will be the reactivated J1 RIKIDOZAN/NJPW UNIFIED HEAVYWEIGHT TITLE. Consisting of two title belts (The J1 Rikidozan title, and Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Heavyweight Championship belt), the reactivation of this title adds to its lineage in the annals of puroresu history. * Mr. Yamato announced two signees to JPA: XYZ, a native juniorheavyweight who has been primarily working in Public Access Wrestling in Denton, TX, in recent times; and a newcomer, Asama Tatsu, a submission-based heavyweight wrestler. * Mr. Yamato confirmed rumors of the release of the following wrestlers: Aroku Murakami, Jinshi Togawa, Asakawa Kageyama, Tatsuya Murayama. He then added that he wished them well in their future endeavors. * Mr. Yamato ended by stating that despite the previous difficulties, JPA has cut a deal with TV Tokyo to show weekly, half-hour pre-taped broadcasts of JPA programming beginning on 1/20/2001, at 9pm JST. He added, that JPA will continue to broadcast on the satellite channel Samurai TV, showing the megacards in their entirity (pre-taped), before the footage is shown on TV Tokyo's weekly " JPA Battle START!" broadcasts. The full line-ups for the CORONATION tour will be announced next week. Happy New Year! [name withheld] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
