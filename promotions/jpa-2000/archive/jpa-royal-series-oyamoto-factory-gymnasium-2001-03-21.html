JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.21 Oyamoto Factory Gymnasium RESULTS

21 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
An opportunity not taken is an opportunity wasted. One of the JPA's young talents apparently knew this, and circumvented it in grand style, scoring the victory for his team in the main event of tonight's show. Gustav Jaeger was placed in tonight's main event match at Oyamoto Factory Gymnasium, subbing for the injured Jack Shaw. Jaeger rose to the occasion, ending up scoring the pinfall over Kaz Hasegawa. The tower of power from Freiburg, Germany looked like a seasoned pro tonight, and the crowd marveled at his size and strength. Time will tell if he will be able to repeat this tomorrow night, in Saitama at the Tota Sports Center, but Jaeger is rapidly proving to be a dominant up-and-comer among the gaijin talents.
