JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION OFFICIAL PRESS RELEASE 2001.2.24 TOKYO -- Standing outside of Tokyo Differ Ariake, hours before the opening rally of the Japan Puroresu Association's ROYAL SERIES tour, JPA President Genji Yamato railed out against the conduct of one of the top stars in his promotion, Ryo Inoue. Speaking before members of the sports media, Yamato declared "Inoue's campaign of insult and misinformation is going too far, and casts a bad aura upon the whole of the Japan Puroresu Association." "For all his talk of respect towards Double Titles champion MASSIVE Akita, never have I seen such disrespect, to withdraw a challenge at the eleventh hour leading up to this tour, and to constantly lash out against the profession which has given him his career to date. "Neo Japan Pro Wrestling sadly faltered, and TATAMI has not held a rally in months. With the exception of a slight setback in December, since October 2000 Ryo Inoue has had employment, and an outlet for his skills here in JPA. "I truly believe his conduct comes not for the passion of his chosen style of fighting, but rather that he cannot face the fact he lost at the Finals of CORONATION on the 3rd of this month, and that he is scared of the possibility of failing to gain the Double Titles against Masashi Akita once more. Inoue is merely seeking to draw attention towards himself like a spurned child who did not get his way, and in the years I have known Inoue, and fought with him, I expected better." With the rumors that TATAMI President Keisuke Oyanami is holding another rally in April, Yamato was asked if perhaps Inoue is trying to promote that card using JPA airtime. "I have no doubt of that. And he is using me as a vehicle to draw attention to it, with his challenges and other such statements. I myself have been approached by Oyanami-san to have another bout at the next TATAMI, but I have not given him an answer as yet. Right now, my duties towards the Japan Puroresu Association come first." When asked about the contract apparently signed by "Super Tengu" Tetsuo Matsumoto, and sent to the JPA offices, for Matsumoto to hold retirement matches in JPA rings, Yamato-san replied: "I have received the contract, and JPA's legal personnel are going over it. I have not yet decided whether or not such bouts will be held in the confines of the JPA..." [Yamato smiled] "However much of a draw they would be." With that, Yamato returned to overseeing the opening show of the ROYAL SERIES.
