JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall 2001.2.3 -- 9,893 PAID ATTENDANCE O = Win X = Loss MATCH ONE: 10-MAN PINFALL ELIMINATION STYLE BATTLE ROYALE O JINSHI TOGAWA [Hammerlock Exploder 24:21] Gustav Jaeger X (Order of elimination: Goshin Star, Samuel Otomo, Inochi Ichihara, Jeremy Knyte, Kathakano, Christopher Kojima, Eiji Yatsu, Yuzo Moriboshi, Gustav Jaeger) MATCH TWO: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O MASANOBU AKAZI [Double Armbar Camel Clutch 15:32] Asama Tatsu X MATCH THREE: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O THE GREAT TOKAIO [Brainbuster 16:09] XYZ X MATCH FOUR: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O KENJI OKANO Corey Hart [Top-Rope Uranage 16:38] EIYUU We Hyun Bae X MATCH FIVE: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT SHOICHI 13 JASON RAMSEY O [Superdriver '99 17:43] X Mitsuhiro Kataka Billy Fairchild MATCH SIX: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O SHINJI NAKATA [STF 16:54] Kaz Hasegawa X MATCH SEVEN: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT X Tora Ryoku JACK SHAW O [Leatherneck Bomb 17:29] Shiro Oodora Rick Taylor MATCH EIGHT: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT Iron Chef Keiji Kobe [TIME LIMIT DRAW 30:00] Tasogare no Prince MATCH NINE: CORONATION TOURNAMENT FINALS FOR THE J1 RIKIDOZAN/NEO JAPAN PRO WRESTLING UNIFIED HEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPIONSHIP -- NO TIME LIMIT X Ryo Inoue [Released German Suplex 36:58] MASSIVE AKITA O Award Ceremony: Following the Main Event, a short intermission occurred. Then, the collected JPA roster assembled in the ring, all members wearing the company training suit. A short congratulatory speech was given by JPA President Genji Yamato. Then, with the blessings of former Neo Japan Pro Wrestling CEO Genichiro Matsumura, Yamato-san then awarded the Double Titles to Masashi "MASSIVE" Akita, and officially declared him the new J1 Rikidozan/Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Unified Heavyweight Champion. Yamato-san then bestowed a gilded wreath to Ryo Inoue. However, Inoue refused the gesture, saying the only award he was willing to accept today was the Double Titles. He further challenged Akita to face him on 3.24, and put the Double Titles on the line in that match. Akita accepted the challenge to a staggering ovation. Inoue and Akita then bowed before each other in a show of respect, but Inoue failed to do the same for Yamato or Matsumura.
