JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.2.24 Tokyo Differ Ariake RESULTS

24 February 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association began its second tour, ROYAL SERIES, today before a sold-out crowd at Tokyo Differ Ariake. The public fighting between JPA President Genji Yamato, and one of his top draws, Ryo Inoue, definitely cast a pall over the proceedings, as Inoue sat at ringside through much of the show, wearing a red and silver TAP jumpsuit, and occassionally shouting comments to other wrestlers that he is scheduled to go up against later in the tour. However, half-way through the semi-main match, he left to get ready for his own bout, in the main event. This show was taped for future broadcast on the JPA's television program "Battle START!," shown Saturdays at 9pm JST on TV Tokyo network. The tour continues Monday, 2.26, at Yamagata Tsuoka City Gym.
