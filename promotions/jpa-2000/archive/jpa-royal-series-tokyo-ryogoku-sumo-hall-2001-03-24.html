JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.24 Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall RESULTS

24 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association today ran it's tour-ending show of the ROYAL SERIES, at Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall. The league drew more fans today than its previous outing at Ryogoku, a near sell-out at 10,052 paid persons in attendance. Among the notable persons in attendance was TATAMI President Keisuke Oyanami. Whether Oyanami was there to merely watch the event, or discuss further business possibilities with JPA President Genji Yamato is unknown at this time. Some of the notable matches on the card included: the first defense of the J1 Rikidozan/Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Unified Heavyweight Titles since MASSIVE Akita won them on 2.3, fighting against Rick Taylor; a bout billed as "JPA v. TAP," with Ryo Inoue, Eiji Yatsu and former Japan Fight League champion "Nai no Kami" Naoto Kangyo in his pro wrestling debut representing the "splinter faction" Total Attack Puroresu against a JPA team of "young guns," Shinji Nakata, Yuzo Moriboshi & Jinshi Togawa. Also of note, a match fought under "Relevos Increiblas" lucha libre rules, with men who are traditionally rivals, Tasogare no Prince & Iron Chef Keiji Kobe, fighting against another "rivals" team, Kenji "KAYO" Okano & Billy Fairchild. This match was essentially a preview of things to come in the next tour, JUNIOR STRUGGLE, running from 4.21 - 5.19. It should also be noted that the push for German powerhouse Gustav Jaeger continued today, in a match against "Japanese gaijin" Christopher Kojima.
