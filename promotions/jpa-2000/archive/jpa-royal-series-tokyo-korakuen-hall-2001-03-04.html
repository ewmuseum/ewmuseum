JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.4 Tokyo Korakuen Hall RESULTS -- Chaos post-show

4 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association's ROYAL SERIES continued today, with their debut rally at the famed Korakuen Hall venue in Tokyo. Prior to the sold-out show (taped for future broadcast on the JPA's "Battle START!" program on TV Tokyo network), Ryo Inoue held a personal rally outside the venue, draped in TAP colors, blasting the JPA's administration, and continuously declaring a "War for Respect" against JPA President Genji Yamato. Results: JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION Tokyo Korakuen Hall 2001.3.4 -- 2,100 PAID ATTENDANCE O = Win X = Loss MATCH ONE: 20 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O KATHAKANO [October Rust 16:33] XYZ X MATCH TWO: 20 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O GUSTAV JAEGER [Freiburg Fall 5:12] Inochi Ichihara X MATCH THREE: 20 MINUTE TIME LIMIT Eiji Yatsu [TIME LIMIT DRAW 20:00] Billy Fairchild MATCH FOUR: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O KENJI OKANO Shiro Oodora X [Top Rope Uranage 15:02] EIYUU We Hyun Bae MATCH FIVE: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O IRON CHEF KEIJI KOBE Tasogare no Prince [Tope de Cristo 17:48] Corey Hart Ketiak X MATCH SIX: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT X Tora Ryoku [Superdriver 17:23] JASON RAMSEY O MATCH SEVEN: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O MASANOBU AKAZI Samuel Otomo [Akazi Driver 13:42] Toshiaki Mizuno Christopher Kojima X MATCH EIGHT: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT X Shinji Nakata Jack Shaw [Spinebuster 19:12] Yuzo Moriboshi RICK TAYLOR O MATCH NINE: 30 MINUTE TIME LIMIT MASSIVE Akita SHOICHI 13 [Honor of Edo 15:27] O THE GREAT TOKAIO Deacon X MATCH TEN: 45 MINUTE TIME LIMIT O RYO INOUE [Strangle Hold Gamma 19:43] Jinshi Togawa X POST-MATCH: Following this match, Inoue was joined in the ring by fellow JPA member Eiji Yatsu, as well as known NHB/MMA fighter "Nai no Kami" Naoto Kangyo. Both Yatsu and Kangyo wore red and silver TAP jackets whilst in the ring; the group were joined on the outside by Tetsuo Matsumoto (aka Super Tengu of Super Japan Pro Wrestling), as well as Tetsuhiro Morishita, formerly known in wrestling circles as "Alex RAPTOR" Inoue spoke on the house mic, railing against JPA President Genji Yamato, decrying Strong Style wrestling, demanding once again that Yamato face Inoue at the upcoming TATAMI NHB/MMA event. Inoue declared his TAP group as a separate entity from the JPA, describing it as "The True Fighting Path for a New Era." At this point, several JPA talents, including Shinji Nakata, Rick Taylor and Yuzo Moriboshi arrived to the ring area, getting in the faces of Inoue, Yatsu & Kangyo. Shouting between the two groups were audible even without a microphone; however, all degenerated into chaos when a shoving match occured between Inoue and Taylor, causing Nakata, Moriboshi, Yatsu, Kangyo, and ringside attendants to get involved, separating everyone in the ring. Inoue, Yatsu, Kangyo, Morishita and Matsumoto then left Korakuen Hall through the crowd, while Taylor, Nakata, Togawa & Moriboshi hurled insults at them over the house mic.
