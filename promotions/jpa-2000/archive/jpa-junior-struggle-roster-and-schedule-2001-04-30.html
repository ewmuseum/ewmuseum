JPA JUNIOR STRUGGLE Roster & Schedule Officially Announced

30 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
In a statement given to the press today, JPA Director of Public Relations SEIJI OYUKI addressed the status of the JUNIOR STRUGGLE tour, and its tournament. Mr. Oyuki regrettably announced that one of the competitors originally scheduled to compete in the event, juniorheavyweight talent XYZ, opted to decline working on this tour. Therefore, the field has been cut down from 8 to 7-men in the Junior Struggle League. However, he does not feel this will diminish the enthusiasm and buzz about this tournament, which is being held to crown the inaugural PAN ASIA JUNIORHEAVYWEIGHT CHAMPION. The competitors in Junior Struggle are: Iron Chef Keiji Kobe, Tasogare no Prince, Kenji Okano, Kathakano, Eiji Yatsu [TAP], Billy Fairchild, Corey Hart. Mr. Oyuki then announced that due to some delays in the start of the tour, special JS BONUS! events have been booked between the end of the tour, and the Tokyo Budokan show, which had been rescheduled to 2001.6.16. Included in the Bonus shows is a previously-unscheduled defense of the Double Titles by MASSIVE Akita, who will finally meet Jack Shaw for the unified championship on 6.7 at Yokohama Bunka Gym. The full bookings for the Bonus shows will be announced as they are determined. The FULL Roster for the JUNIOR STRUGGLE tour is as follows: NATIVES ------------ MASSIVE Akita [Double Titles Champion] The Great Tokaio Shinji Nakata Yuzo Moriboshi Jinshi Togawa Tora Ryoku Shiro Oodora Iron Chef Keiji Kobe Tasogare no Prince Kenji Okano Kathakano Red Tiger (debut) Takeshi Sugiyama (debut) VIOLENT Tsuchida (debut) Super Genki Man (debut) Ryo Inoue [TAP] Eiji Yatsu [TAP] "Nai no Kami" Naoto Kangyo [TAP] GAIJIN --------- Jack Shaw Rick Taylor Jason Ramsey Billy Fairchild Corey Hart Ketiak Gustav Jaeger Taylor Threat (debut) Cyrus Williams (debut) Mr. Oyuki then declined questions at this time about the continuing assault on JPA President Genji Yamato by TAP's Ryo Inoue in the press, following a controversial decision in Inoue's bout at the last TATAMI NHB/MMA event. Oyuki-san promised that Yamato-san would address the press within the week about these issues.
