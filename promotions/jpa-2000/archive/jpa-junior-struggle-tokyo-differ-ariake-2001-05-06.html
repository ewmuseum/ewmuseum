JPA JUNIOR STRUGGLE 2001.5.6 Tokyo Differ Ariake RESULTS: Historic pinfall!

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association today began its third official tour, JUNIOR STRUGGLE, today in front of a sell-out crowd at Tokyo's Differ Ariake, competing for business with the Tokyo Organization of Puroresu's rally at Korakuen Hall (JPA runs a rally there on Monday 5.14). The cornerstone of this tour is the Junior Struggle League, a round-robin tournament to determine the inaugural Pan Asia Juniorheavyweight Champion. Scores are determined by points: 2 pts. for a pinfall/submission victory, 0 pts. for a loss, 1 pts. for a draw. In today's matches, one of the League favorites, Tasogare no Prince, defeated young gaijin up-and-comer Corey Hart for 2 pts., the lead in the league thus far. Hart broke out in this match, giving the Twilight Prince a bit of a struggle (no pun intended). Hart may end up surprising many in this tour. Another of the favorites, Iron Chef Keiji Kobe, surprised many today for a few reasons: first off, he fought unmasked, and not in his typical lucha libre influenced style, rather choosing a more traditional Strong Style approach to his fighting! However, his opponent today was T.A.P.'s representative, Eiji Yatsu, who many claim is the biggest spoiler in the tournament, and he may well have lived up to that claim today, as Kobe & Yatsu fought to a 30-minute time limit draw in one of the stiffest juniorheavyweight matches seen in JPA's history. Both Kobe & Yatsu currently stand at 1 pts. The biggest shock of the evening came during the 4 vs. 4 Elimination main event, however, as young gun Shinji Nakata scored a historic pinfall on the Texas Roughneck, Jack Shaw, to win the bout for his team. Afterwards, it was said Nakata was very emotional, and said to the press later "Only a pinfall on Akita would be bigger than this moment." Shaw later quipped to the press that he "was glad to help." The tour continues tomorrow with a 2-day stint in Chiba.
