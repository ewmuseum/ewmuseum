JPA: Yes, we're still alive, thanks. Roster + other tour info.

22 April 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Hi there. If you're receiving this note, it's because: a) You are a handler in the Japan Puroresu Association E-Fed b) You are currently on the waiting list to be an active handler in JPA c) You are a member of the non-handler mailing list and/or reading this on rec.sport.pro-wrestling.fantasy Usenet group If you got this in error, or no longer wish to receive JPA mailings, please let me know at this address. Anyway, we were supposed to be on tour by now, however toward the beginning of this month [detail withheld] occured, and things kinda shit the bed from there. However, we are getting back on track with things, starting with the delayed tour. The tour will NOW run from 5/6 - 6/16, a six-week outing rather than the typical 3-4 week one. This is basically to make up for lost time. In addition, along with the JUNIOR STRUGGLE LEAGUE to determine JPA's Pan Asia Juniorheavyweight Champion, there will be an added bonus DOUBLE TITLES DEFENSE from a contender to be named before the RP/Strat period begins. I was not originally going to have a Double Titles match on this tour, but I figure with the time we need to make up, it'll be OK to do it. The Double Titles match will happen on one of the added extra dates, whereas the Finals for teh Junior Struggle League will still happen at the tour ending megacard, now scheduled for 6/16 at Tokyo Budokan. THE FINAL ROSTER FOR THIS TOUR IS: Natives: MASSIVE Akita (Double Titles Champion) The Great Tokaio Ryo Inoue [TAP] Naoto Kangyo [TAP] Shinji Nakata Yuzo Moriboshi Jinshi Togawa Tora Ryoku Shiro Oodora Kenji Okano Tasogare no Prince Iron Chef Keiji Kobe Eiji Yatsu [TAP] Kathakano Red Tiger Hiroshi Takawa VIOLENT Tsuchida Super Genki Man Gaijin: Jack Shaw Rick Taylor Jason Ramsey Billy Fairchild Gustav Jaeger Ketiak Christopher Kojima Taylor Threat Cyrus Williams The roster pages, as well as the whole of teh website ( http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/ ) shall be updated this week, in conjunction with the announcement of the complete tour lineups. Concurrently, more "in-character" or "kayfabed" mailings will become common once again, as we near the tour's beginning. I thank all of you for your patience, and well wishes during [detail withheld], but we're about normal now. Thanks once again --- [name withheld] --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
