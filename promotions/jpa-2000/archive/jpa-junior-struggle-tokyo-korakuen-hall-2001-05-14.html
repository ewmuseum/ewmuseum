JPA JUNIOR STRUGGLE 2001.5.14 Tokyo Korakuen Hall RESULTS -- KOBE V. PRINCE!

14 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association steamrolled into the legendary Korakuen Hall in Tokyo today, amidst an overflowing sell-out crowd. The venue was reconfigured to seat 2,300 persons (200 more than normal), however even that didn't prevent a total sell-out, and left some upset fans outside without a ticket. All this on the strength of the main event of the card, a Junior Struggle League match, and re-match from the CORONATION Final show at Ryogoku Sumo Hall on 1.28, between the former Iron Chef, Keiji Kobe, and Tasogare no Prince. JPA President Genji Yamato quipped "Perhaps we should run this match every show, we'd sell out each time!" As it was at Ryogoku back in January, thirty minutes proved to be not enough for the two favorites in the tournament, as once more, the time limit expired, after a match that saw nearfalls galore, highspots as well as mat wrestling. Kobe's "new" style proved to be a tougher match for the Prince than last time, however Tasogare no Prince isn't considered one of the top juniorheavyweights of all time for nothing, as he deftly took everything Kobe threw at him, and returned it with grace and style. In the end, Kobe managed to surprise the Prince with a flash La Magistral, however he locked the pinning predicament on the Twilight Prince too late, as time expired, and the match was ruled a Time Limit Draw, giving each man 1 pts. for the match outcome. Speaking to the press after the show, a distraught and irate Kobe vowed that the rest of the league would be his cross to bear, and his Earth to scorch. "As it was before, I *had* him, and there was not enough precious time! Mark my words, I *will* meet him in the Finals at Budokan! I *will* not be denied!" The Prince had no comment for the press. Meanwhile, the rise of Shinji Nakata continues, as he managed to score the win over former NHB/MMA star "Nai no Kami" Naoto Kangyo, aligned with T.A.P. since his entrance into JPA. After the match, Nakata made a mic appeal to Korakuen, saying that when it is his turn at Inoue, he would end this T.A.P. nonsense, and show that puroresu WILL survive in the new millenium. Also still on the rise is German import Gustav Jaeger, who was said to look great in his match against Double Titles Champion MASSIVE Akita. Some journalists are already theorizing a Double Titles reign by Jaeger before the end of the year.
