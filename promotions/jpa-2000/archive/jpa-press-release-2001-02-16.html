Japan Puroresu Association Official Press Release 2001.2.16 TOKYO -- On the eve of the ROYAL SERIES tour, dur to run from 2.24 until 3.24, Japan Puroresu Association President Genji Yamato spoke out on the recent comments and actions of one of its top draws, Ryo Inoue. Said Yamato from his Tokyo office, "Once more, I find myself saddened and disappointed in Inoue-san, and his unprofessionalism in retracting his challenge to MASSIVE Akita at Ryogoku Sumo Hall." Yamato went on to say, "That Inoue would accuse myself, and my promotion, of stifling any sort of fighting style, or playing favorites, is absurd. Inoue and myself go back to Neo Japan Pro Wrestling; then, we fought side by side. Now, it seems with myself as his boss, he feels the need to 'rebel.' "If Inoue wishes to start a splinter group, or faction, that is his decision. However, given his history in Neo Japan, I would have thought he would steer clear of such tactics. However, such is not to be." When asked about the subject of who would replace Inoue as challenger for the Double Titles, Inoue replied "A decision has been reached, one which should satisfy puroresu fans across generations, as the new Challenger to the J1 Rikidozan/Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Unified Heavyweight Titles will be none other than Jack Shaw." Shaw and Akita have fought many classic bouts, stretching back to the days of the old United Japan Pro Wrestling promotion. Finally, when asked about the request by Tetsuo Matsumoto, formerly known as Super Tengu in Super Japan Pro Wrestling, for Yamato-san to face him in a retirement-series bout, Yamato smirked, but then gave "No comment" as his answer. The ROYAL SERIES tour begins next Saturday, 2001.2.24, at Tokyo Differ Ariake, and ends on 2001.3.24 at Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall.
