[crowd pops] Kobe then follows this move up with a stiff, Kenka Kick to the face which has no Prince loopy. MM: Kobe whips no Prince into the ropes looking for another Kenka kick, but no Prince is able to summon one last flourish of energy for a back flip into his Twilight Suplex. YT: Hai, but I think no Prince's back is too injured to sustain the bridge and releases. Tasogare no Prince drapes his arm over Kobe for a two count, but the Iron Chef is able to get his shoulder up. After kicking the bottom turnbuckle out of frustration, no Prince issues a series of rapid leg drops then ascends the top turnbuckle... MM: I think he's looking to end it all.. appears to be going for his Nightfall finisher. YT: Tasogare lifts off with perfect grace and form, leaping high into the air but falls short of his target. Tasogare no Prince's fatigue prevents him from properly gauging the distance. After sucking wind and struggling to his feet, Kobe somehow manages one last rush and plants the stunned no Prince with a stiff kenka kick! Both men are going all out h-- [SFX: BELL] MM: I think we're out of time here.. YT: Hai, I believe you are correct, referee Kentaro Honda motioning to Seiji Oyuki..
