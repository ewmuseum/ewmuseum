JPA Press Release: Tour Schedule for ROYAL SERIES

15 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association today officially announced the tour dates and venues for the February-March ROYAL SERIES tour. The tour begins on 2.24 at the JPA's "homebase" of Tokyo Differ Ariake, and will also feature its first rally at the famous Korakuen Hall in Tokyo. The first leg will venture into the northern provinces in Japan (incl. two dates on Hokkaido, Japan's northernmost island, on 2.28 and 3.1). Then the JPA moves towards southwestern Japan following the Korakuen Hall rally, before ending the tour on 3.24 with another megashow at Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall. The main event of the Ryogoku Sumo Hall show will be the first defense of the new J1 Rikidozan/NJPW Unified Heavyweight Champion, whomever it is that wears the Double Titles at JPA's 2.3 CORONATION tour-ender. The contender will be named prior to the tour's start.
