JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.11 Hiroshima Higashi Ward Sports Center RESULTS

11 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The ROYAL SERIES continued today in Hiroshima, at the Higashi Ward Sports Center. Of note, were the conditions of both Ryo Inoue and Rick Taylor in today's matches. After the sheer brutality these two men inflicted on one another at last night's show, both seemed a little worse for wear. Taylor seemed to stay back a little bit in his match, allowing his partner and fellow Alliance member Jason Ramsey some room to "flex his muscles," as it were. As for Inoue, in what some may say is an upset, the man who replaced him as Challenger to MASSIVE Akita's Double Titles on 3/24, wily veteran Jack Shaw, defeated him today after another stiff, if slower, bout. Inoue seemed to give Shaw a little more respect than he did Taylor last night, bowing to him post-match, however Inoue then left the ring as quickly as he could. Inoue then gave comments to the press about Shaw's "upset," and vowed that the next time they meet, he won't be as shaken up, and will "bring Shaw that much closer to retirement."
