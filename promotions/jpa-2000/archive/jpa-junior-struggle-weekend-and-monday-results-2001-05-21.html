JPA JUNIOR STRUGGLE Weekend & Monday Results (19 to 21 May 2001)

21 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served unedited. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
Tasogare no Prince ended his opener round in the Junior Struggle League over the weekend, beating his sometimes partner Kathakano to finish up with 11 points (5 wins, 1 draw). For now, the only other competitor that even comes close is T.A.P.'s Eiji Yatsu, trailing with 5 points. However, the brunt of Keiji Kobe's matches have kicked into high gear, with Kobe beating gaijin breakout Billy Fairchild on Sunday, to bring the Iron Chef up to 4 points. If Kobe can keep it up, and win all of his remaining matches, Kobe will be the one to face the Prince at Budokan on 6/16. Right now, the real struggle is who can win more matches, Kobe or Yatsu?
