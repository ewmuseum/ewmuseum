JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.16 Fukuoka Hakata Star Lanes RESULTS -- Shaw Injured

16 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The challenger to MASSIVE Akita's J1 Rikidozan/Neo Japan Pro Wrestling Unified Heavyweight titles, the veteran Jack Shaw, was taken to a hospital in Fukuoka after being knocked out in his tag match today at Fukuoka Hakata Star Lanes. At this time, the extent of Shaw's injuries are unknown. However, the tag-team match between Shaw & Rick Taylor v. Akita & The Great Tokaio was declared a no-contest following a stiff kick to Shaw's head by Tokaio, while Shaw was on the ground. Shaw fell over, but didn't get up. Referee Tamon Sugiura called off the match at that point, while ring attendants attempted to revive Shaw. After five minutes, Shaw stirred, but had to be helped out of the ring by attendants, Taylor, and Akita-sama. Shaw's face was black and blue on the side where it was kicked. Shaw was taken to a hospital in Fukuoka immediately after being revived. JPA will keep you updated as we hear more. Results from today's sold-out Battle START! Following the match, almost in a repeat of the Korakuen Hall incident, Shinji Nakata and Jinshi Togawa, dressed in blue/white JPA trainer suits, appeared; only this time, Nakata withdrew a letter from his jacket. Reading it aloud, he reiterated comments he made to the press, stating he accepted Inoue's 6-man challenge for 3.24, and declared himself, Moriboshi and Togawa would face the TAP team of Inoue, Kangyo, and Eiji Yatsu. Inoue then insulted the three men, declaring they could not beat him in singles, and they definitely will not overtake all of TAP at Ryogoku.
