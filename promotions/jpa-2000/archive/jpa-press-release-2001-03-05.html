JPA Press Release 2001.3.5

5 March 2001

JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION OFFICIAL PRESS RELEASE 2001.3.5 TOKYO -- Speaking from the JPA offices in Tokyo today, JPA President Genji Yamato and Vice-President Mitsuharu Takamoto gave statements about the chaotic outburst that closed yesterday's rally at Tokyo Korakuen Hall. Following the main event match between Ryo Inoue & Jinshi Togawa, Inoue adn several other members of his "TAP" group (incl. Eiji Yatsu and NHB/MMA fighter Naoto Kangyo) gave a speech blasting Yamato and the Japan Puroresu Association. Several JPA roster members took Inoue to task, and a brief shoving match occured before order was restored. Yamato-san, speaking to the press, declared the actions by Inoue to be "outrageous" and "out of hand." "Inoue has taken his beliefs, and his feelings about puroresu, and now seeks to plunge this organization into chaos by his actions. If Inoue truly believes he and his circle are a separate entity from JPA, then let TAP promote their own shows and their own style, rather than cause unrest within this promotion." Takamoto-san concurred, adding "What Inoue has done crossed the line of accord and respect we seek to maintain here between our competitors. Being that this is professional wrestling, we understand incidents such as these are a risk; however, Inoue is trying to galvanize not only our talent, but our fan base as well, and such conduct is unacceptable." Tetsuo Matsumoto, the former Super Tengu, was also present at the proceedings, as was Tetsuhiro Morishita, a fighter for the TATAMI organization, formerly known as "Alex RAPTOR" when he was active in puroresu. However, neither of them got involved with the scuffling. Yamato-san: "It is well-known by now that Matsumoto-san is attempting to have a series of retirement matches in JPA, but nothing has been decided as yet. As for Morishita, why he was here is unknown to me. Perhaps as a guest, perhaps to get exposure for TATAMI, perhaps to align himself with Inoue. Nothing is certain." Both Yamato and Takamoto confirmed that Naoto Kangyo, "Nai no Kami" as he is known in NHB/MMA circles, is not currently under JPA contract. Yamato-san also, when asked, neither confirmed nor denied whether or not he will take part in April's TATAMI 2 card. JPA's ROYAL SERIES tour continues on Tuesday, at Morioka Iwate Prefectural Gym.
