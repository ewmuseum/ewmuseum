JPA JUNIOR STRUGGLE 2001.5.7 Chiba Public Park Gymnasium RESULTS

7 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The JPA's JUNIOR STRUGGLE tour continued today, with the first of two shows in Chiba Prefecture. Another Junior Struggle League match occured here, as Kenji Okano and Kathakano both fought their first tournament match today. Okano ended up winning by Michinoku Driver II, bringing Okano 2 pts. He is currently tied with favorite Tasogare no Prince, however TnP is due to fight tomorrow against Billy Fairchild, so that could change.
