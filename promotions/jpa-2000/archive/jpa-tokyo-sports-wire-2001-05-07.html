JPA Tokyo Sports Wire 2001.5.7 -- Inoue accuses Yamato of misconduct

7 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
5.7.2001 Steel Wind Dojo At a press conference in Tokyo today, Total Attack Puroresu leader Ryo Inoue responded to JPA President Genji Yamato's comments made over the weekend. Inoue railed against what he called "Genji Yamato's shortsightedness that will lead this promotion to ruin!" Inoue claimed that Yamato took him too literally regarding his post-TATAMI match with Vic Garibaldi. "Did I say I was not yet ready to face Akita? Hai, I did indeed, but that meant that I would not be ready to face Akita if I needed to challenge him the next day or later in that week. It did not mean that I was to be passed by for Double Titles shot.." Inoue also responded to Yamato's comments about his conduct prior to the match with Garibaldi and how it affected the judging. "It is prejudice plain and simple. I was not judged by my fighting ability but how I conducted myself prior to the fight. Yamato said so himself." Inoue then threw down a copy of the JPA press release that Yamato's office released just prior to the first night of the Junior Struggle League. "I give it to you here, 'Inoue is an excellent fighter, however his shenanigans prior to his bout with Garibaldi hurt him, in my view. Despite his skills, it is his attitude that costs him more than anything.', I ask you, is that opinion of an unbiased judge?" Yamato's comments throw the verdict of the excellent Inoue/Garibladi match into question. Inoue commented further, "I have never lied to the public, I have remained honest with them through this entire personal vendetta that Genji Yamato has against me. I stated plainly that I had nothing to do with vandalizing of his office in Differ Ariake and he turns around and accusses me in print! IN PRINT! That my friends is slander and I WILL be taking Genji Yamato to court for that!" Inoue ended his tirade by tearing the JPA Press Release in half and mashing it under his heel before storming off. Inoue teams with fellow T.A.P. member Naoto Kangyo tonight in Chiba against the team of Shinji Nakata and Jinshi Togawa. --- [name withheld] - Formerly of The Westsiders [#28, RSPWF 2000 Year End Awards Top 50 Tag Teams] - Japan Puroresu Association - http://jpapuroresu.hypermart.net/
