JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.24 Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall Line-Up

17 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association today released the final line-up for their tour-ending megashow at Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall, next Saturday. First and foremost, as was announced previously, the Challenger to MASSIVE Akita's Double Titles is Rick Taylor. Taylor was announced as the Challenger following an injury last night in Fukuoka that will sideline Jack Shaw for the rest of the tour. Shaw's orbital bone was broken during a match at Fukuoka Hakata Star Lanes. Second, three young guns in the JPA -- Shinji Nakata, Jinshi Togawa & Yuzo Moriboshi -- will face off against CORONATION finalist Ryo Inoue's splinter group Total Attack Puroresu [TAP]. Inoue will fight alongside juniorheavyweight breakout Eiji Yatsu, and noted NHB/MMA fighter "Nai no Kami" Naoto Kangyo in his first JPA match. Also of note will be a match that the JPA is touting as a "preview of things to come" for its next tour, JUNIOR STRUGGLE. In a tag-team match, Iron Chef Keiji Kobe will team WITH his chief rival, Tasogare no Prince, against another "combative" team, rivals Kenji Okano & Billy Fairchild. The match, fought under what in lucha libre terms is called "Relevos Increiblas" rules, promises to give a glimpse of the JUNIOR STRUGGLE, and perhaps show who may or may not come out on top.
