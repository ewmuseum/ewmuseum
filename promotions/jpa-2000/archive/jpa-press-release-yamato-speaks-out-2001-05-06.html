JPA Press Release 2001.5.6: Yamato Speaks Out!

6 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION OFFICIAL PRESS RELEASE 2001.5.6 [TOKYO, Differ Ariake] - On the eve of the opening show for the Japan Puroresu Association's latest tour, JUNIOR STRUGGLE, JPA President Genji Yamato spoke out about certain events pertaining to the renegade T.A.P. gundan. "What they say, is beyond outrageous, and what they do, goes even further," said Yamato. Explaining further, Yamato said: "For Ryo Inoue to make outrageous claims, such as the JPA front office 'holding native talent down' because he is not handed a shot at the Double Titles on this tour, brings into question his maturity, I think, not as a fighter, but as a person. Inoue commented immediately after his bout in [NHB/MMA promotion] TATAMI against Vic Garibaldi, that he would not seek the Titles because of his failure to beat Garibaldi inside of five minutes. Thus, we took him at his word, and booked Jack Shaw for the shot he should have had back in March, but could not due to injury." Yamato paused. "Apparently, taking Inoue at his word is not something that should be a common practice, if this incident is anything to go by." When asked about the further accusations of prejudice against Inoue and his T.A.P. group, including charges that Inoue was unfairly judged to a split decision win in TATAMI, Yamato replied: "Absolutely false. I have no prejudice towards anyone that respects the company, and respects the sport in which they ply their craft. Inoue is an excellent fighter, however his shenanigans prior to his bout with Garibaldi hurt him, in my view. Despite his skills, it is his attitude that costs him more than anything. And if he feels for a second that I do not think he was behind the vandalism of my office, then he has taken one too many shots to the head. The evidence may only be circumstantial, but it is enough to make Inoue and his gundan look very suspicious." As for the recent flaunting of contractual duties by the members of T.A.P., Yamato was noticeably angry. "I congratulate Eiji Yatsu for winning his bout in [Tokyo Organization of Puroresu] last week, but the fact remains, his appearance in TOP was not cleared by this office." Continuing, he said "If Inoue, Yatsu & anyone else under JPA contract wishes to appear or fight on another's card, they must clear it with me. In fact, one other JPA wrestler has asked for permission to be at a TOP show. Yatsu gave no such courtesy. "Furthermore, for T.A.P. to be apparently holding their own card, and making challenges to JPA titles in the process, is absurd. T.A.P. can challenge for titles all they want here. At their own show? No way." Yamato concluded that line of questioning by saying, "Our legal department will investigate these matters, and if the TOP appearances or the T.A.P. card has any traces of contract breach, these matters will go to court. Inoue can claim moral victories all he wants, but legally, he and his ilk may not be on such sure ground." Yamato ended the interview by wishing the best of luck to Eiji Yatsu, and "all other competitors" in the Junior Struggle League, but added he will not be intimidated, and it is T.A.P. that has to answer to JPA and its fans, not the other way around.
