JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.22 Saitama Tota Sports Center RESULTS

22 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The final show on the JPA's ROYAL SERIES tour, prior to Saturday's megacard, happened today in Saitama, with some interesting buildup to the two most highly anticipated matches on Saturday's event at Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall. First, Rick Taylor, the Challenger to the Double Titles, scored the big win for his team against that lead by the Champion, MASSIVE Akita. After the match, Taylor made a mic appeal, stating he's beaten Akita before, and he'll do it again, for this time, both the Titles, and the pride of Jack Shaw will be on the line. Akita responded, saying he knows Shaw better than anyone active in wrestling today, and that he will prevail on Saturday, for no other reason than to give Shaw his rightful shot one last time. Observers also noted the hard work behind Gustav Jaeger, once again subbing for Shaw in the match while the veteran is out with an injury. The wrestling press is getting behind Jaeger, declaring him to be a powerful force in the years to come. Also on the show, Ryo Inoue's TAP group suffered a loss to 2 of the men they will face on Saturday. Yuzo Moriboshi pinned Eiji Yatsu just after the 18 minute mark in today's tag team matchup. Afterwards, Inoue made a speech, saying that he doubted that they will prevail over The True Fight when it mattered...
