JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.10 Chugoku Okayama Budokan RESULTS

10 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The Japan Puroresu Association's ROYAL SERIES tour resumed today, following a three-day break. Today's show was interesting because of the hyped Main Event match between Ryo Inoue & Rick Taylor; unusual for such attention to be brought to a non-TV-taping spot show. Nevertheless, Okayama Budokan was a near sell-out, not bad for the young league, traveling in the southwestern part of Japan for the first time. The Main Event was said to be the stiffest and most brutal one yet for the promotion; looks like they are definitely trying to establish a certain standard for their matches.
