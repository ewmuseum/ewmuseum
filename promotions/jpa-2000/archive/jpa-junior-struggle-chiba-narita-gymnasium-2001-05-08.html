JPA JUNIOR STRUGGLE 2001.5.8 Chiba Narita Gymnasium RESULTS

8 May 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The JPA concluded a 2-day stint in Chiba Prefecture today, in front of a near-sellout crowd at the Narita Gymnasium. All 7 competitors in the Junior Struggle League have fought at least once in the tournament now, with Billy Fairchild having his first match in the league today. He gave a very strong showing against Tasogare no Prince. The Twilight Prince once again almost being taken to time limit in the league, but the Prince prevailed in the final minutes to take the lead with 4 pts. T.A.P.'s Eiji Yatsu also won his match today, bringing him to 3 pts. The sheer determination of the younger talents against the favorites has been proving more difficult than perhaps TnP, Keiji Kobe, etc., counted on, but such is the spirit of competition in the Junior Struggle League! The JPA resumes its tour on Friday, with a weekend jaunt to Ibaragi Prefecture, before an extravaganza on Monday at Tokyo Korakuen Hall, with the main event the most anticipated match of the league: (Iron Chef?) Keiji Kobe v. Tasogare no Prince!
