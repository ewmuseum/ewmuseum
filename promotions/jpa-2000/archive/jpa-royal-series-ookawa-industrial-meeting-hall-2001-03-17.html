JPA ROYAL SERIES 2001.3.17 Ookawa Industrial Meeting Hall RESULTS

17 March 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
The JPA's ROYAL SERIES continued today, without one of their top veterans. As we've been reporting, Jack Shaw was injured last night in Fukuoka, and has been ordered by doctors to not wrestle until his orbital bone is healed. Rick Taylor, the new Challenger to the Double Titles on 3/24, took Shaw's place in a tag match today (cancelling a previously scheduled bout), picking up the win over SHOICHI 13, using a lariat. Taylor later explained to the press, "It's not quite ol' Jack's Chawliner, but it worked. I hope he gets himself healed up, and gets back in the ring where he belongs." The current Double Titles Champion, MASSIVE (Masashi) Akita, also commented on his old rival's injury, stating "It is a shame that it happened so close to our match for the Titles. Many were looking forward to seeing another Akita/Shaw bout enter the legends, but sadly this time it was not to be. I know Tokaio-san has visited Shaw and has expressed his apologies for what happened. Sadly, it is a risk in this profession, and Shaw has known that as long as anybody. He will recover, and Shaw and Akita will fight each other another time. Get Well, Jack."
