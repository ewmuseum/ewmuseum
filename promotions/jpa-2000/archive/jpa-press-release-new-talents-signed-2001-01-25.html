JPA Press Release 2001.1.25 -- New Talents Signed

25 January 2001
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
JAPAN PURORESU ASSOCIATION PRESS RELEASE 2001.1.25 In a press conference held for the Japanese sports and puroresu media today, veteran ring announcer & Japan Puroresu Association director of public relations SEIJI OYUKI teased the card of the upcoming megacard at Tokyo Ryogoku Sumo Hall. Oyuki's main purpose was to announce the signings of new talent to the JPA. He then added that most of these new signees would actually debut at the 2/3 CORONATION tour-ending supercard, in the 10-man battle royale that will open the show. The main event for the show will be Ryo Inoue v. MASSIVE Akita in the finals of the CORONATION tournament, to decide the promotion's J1 Rikidozan/NJPW Unified Heavyweight Champion. Oyuki then discussed the new signees: * EIJI YATSU: A young talent, whose style mixes the best of classic puroresu, shoot-style, and even lucha libre. * MASANOBU AKAZI: At 48 years old, a contemporary of MASSIVE Akita & Jack Shaw, however his experience is great, and skills have not left him. Should be a tremendous asset to the company, especially where training new talents in the JPA dojos is concerned. * YUZO MORIBOSHI: After a brief and promising start that was halted by the closure of Neo Japan Pro Wrestling earlier last year, Moriboshi has continued to train, and returns to active competition in JPA. * A pair of Asian-American talents also joins JPA: SAMUEL OTOMO and CHRISTOPHER KOJIMA. Both come from the United States, and both are ready to make their mark in the land of their ancestry. * From Freiberg, Germany, the Japan Puroresu Association has managed to secure the talents of GUSTAV JAEGER. Should be very interesting to see how Jaeger adapts from Euro-style pro wrestling to JPA's puroresu ways. Finally, Oyuki-san had a very proud look on his face when he announced the final signee, and consequently announced the semi-main event match at the 2/3 show: In order to bring back the juniorheavyweight greatness of such classic leagues as Super Japan Pro Wrestling, and of course Neo Japan, and merge them with the burgeoning talents of today's JPA juniorheavyweight division, Oyuki announced that we would see the best of both worlds collide at Ryogoku Sumo Hall, when IRON CHEF KEIJI KOBE, who stunned audiences by advancing to the 2nd Round of the CORONATION tournament before being eliminated by Ryo Inoue, will step into the ring against none other than... TASOGARE NO PRINCE Causing a buzz amongst the sports media persons in attendance, Oyuki-san declared that JPA President GENJI YAMATO has managed to convince "The Twilight Prince" to appear in JPA, and sign to a contract agreement. This couldn't be better news for JPA's juniors divison. JPA holds a show later today at the Kobe Self-Defense Hall in Hyogo Prefecture. The full line-up for the 2/3 Sumo Hall show will be announced later today.
