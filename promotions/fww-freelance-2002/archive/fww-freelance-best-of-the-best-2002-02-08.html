Freelance World Wrestling: Are You One of the Best of the Best?

8 February 2002

If any of you owners of e-feds have quality wrestlers who wanna show off there stuff for your federation.Get in contact with me asap.. FREELANCE WORLD WRESTLING "Its Time For a Change..And This Is It" FWW[]FWW Welcome to the Freelance World Wrestling ..a new idea in the world of e wrestling. I know that most of you people are saying that you don't have time for another federation. As DDP would say it " Thats not a Bad thing..Its a good thing". The FWW is not a federation where your going to be on the card week in and week out no matter what, because thats not how things swing in my federation. I would say that the FWW is closer to NASCAR format then it is pro wrestling. Each week we have an event or card as most of you it.Each week people will request matches for the upcoming week, if a man does not request a match then that man will not be competing on the card. In simple words you are your own booker, I say who your gonna wrestle, but thats only if you have let me know that you plan on being on that weeks event.Now I know what your thinking, if people don't have to wrestle on a weekly basis how are they ever going to defend their belts, well in the FWW there are no "belts" but there is one champion. Each month there will be three events and then a pay per view. Unlike most federations the big name stars are not promised a damn thing. At each event your have the chance to gain FWW points,after the three events the points are counted up and the two athletes with the most points compete for the tournament championship or pay per view champion. There are also end of the year events for different divisions.You see the FWW could have been called freedom world wrestling, because thats what you finnaly going to be given. Week in and Week out people are gonna be wrestling from many different feds, fighting for both of there respect. This is the first time two champions from different federation could face off just to see who the best in the business truly is. Well thats pretty much the bottom line read on if its something you might wanna try out. CHAPTER ONE "Who Am I and Why Should You Consider This?" My name is [name withheld], I have been roleplaying for about six or seven years and have many championships is many federations. There came a time in my life when I just didn't have time to produce the kind of quality roleplays that I was used to sending out, so I left the E Wrestling game for awhile, while gone I thought of ways to get back into the scene, I came up with the idea of Freelance World Wrestling, because of the way I felt. I know that most of the people I knew could only be in one federation because of time restraints. I knew that people like that who really wanted to compete against the best, but don't have time to work there way up the rankings of some federation. If I was still in the E Wrestling scene this is the kind of federation I would enter. No matches that you have to face or else your gone.Your the booker for your own matches,so you either screw or make your career suceed. Think about this..whats the one thing that always kills federations? People not roleplaying, and people crying about the way they feel there being treated, which is usually decent,but they still cry. Well heres the real deal with the FWW to make sure theres not what people call "dead weight" in the FWW. There are going to be two ways to make sure that there in no "dead weight" in the FWW. First off as I said earlier there are no roster members of the FWW, because its more of a weekly tournament then a running federation where people fill out applications and have to have matches. The second rule thats gonna keep "dead weight" out of the federation is that if a match is requested and that person does not roleplay he will be banned from the events for one month, if that same person requests a match in the next month and doesn't roleplay for that match he will not be allowed in the event again. Making sure that "deadweight" will never bring down the FWW. CHAPTER Two "Are The Weekly events Gonna Have Good Wrestlers?" This federation is six months in the making, and the answer to the question is Yes! I have been in contact with many federations who have expressed interest in having some of there athletes come and compete in the weekly event, hell at the end of each month the federation that the leading point total wrestler is representing will be the official sponcer of the pay per view, so when you compete in the FWW your not just trying to show who the best pound for pound wrestler is, we'll also be seeing where the true great roleplayers go to roleplay.If your one of those people that feels there just not enough talented people to face of a weekly basis then this is the chance you've been waiting for. Ever wanted to see that great two undefeated champions you would have liked to see? Well its finnaly time to see who has the balls to face people who arn't gonna lie down and take it like a ghetto rat with a dub sack. I remember when I was roleplaying everyone used to say the same thing..Shane Michaels should face Buh Bub Fungal or something like that, they both were champion over and over again. But federation rules kept them from getting the match that they truly wanted. Don't get me wrong I'm not going to be able to force those paper champions who really don't wanna face anyone to come and compete. Only people who want to come and compete will be at the event.In simple words the best man will finnaly win no bias fed prez to judge your match the FWW favors no federation and shows no favorites. CHAPTER Three "How can I earn points to enter Pay Per Views?" In the FWW there are many ways to earn points..most of them are by just plain out asking for matches, and roleplaying for the matches that your in. There are also of course bonuses for winning the matches you are entered in, and making the main events. Only people who are in the top 5 in the rankings will be given "Main Event" status and allowed to wresle for bigtime bonus points, and automatic berths in the yearend events.Heres a breakdown of how to earn those points. Get Chosen to compete on "event" card:10 Points Get Chosen to compete on "event" card and win:20 Points Win On Three Straight FWW "event" Cards:35 Points+Automatic berth in year ends Get Chosen as FWW Wrestler of the Week:25 Points Win 4 Pay Per Views:Assured Year End Main Event CHAPTER Four "Final Word" This is just a memo to the wrestling world about this new e division I'll call it. Anyone interested in requesting a match for the Sunday the 17th or 24th of this month contact me at [detail withheld] Hope to see you all someday in a FWW Event copyright FWW Freelance World Wrestling 2002
