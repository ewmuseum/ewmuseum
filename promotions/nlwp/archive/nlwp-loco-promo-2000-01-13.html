NLWP — LOCO Roleplay, 13 January 2000

13 January 2000
Content noticeA period document, served as recovered; withheld details are marked. It carries the humor, language, and content typical of its era and community — some of it crude, dated, or offensive, including material that wouldn't be written today. See Curation.
[name withheld], double pasted of my roleplay. Which follows: [The scene open with the "King of Indies" standing inside an empty ring in an empty arena, Anytown, USA. LOCO stands there waiting for the camera to get a good look at him. His long dark curly hair dangles over his face.
His eyes circling like a bird of prey. He wears black faded jeans shorts. His arms are raised and his fingers point to his trademark black T-shirt. The words read as follows:
"EMWC - 100,000 lemmings can't be wrong." written in blood red letters on the front. His hands, in one movement, thrusting to his crotch. He poses for a moment. Then raises his hands and begins to speak.]
LOCO: Once upon a time they called me, "King of Indies." This wasn't meant as such a compliment but rather a stigma. A stigma I choose to turn around and to make my own. I took their trash and turned it into gold.
Literally. Some may think this NLWP run is my first. Some might be surprised to see me doing as well as I am.
But I'm not surprised. I've always been this good. I've always been the man. So before you ask where I came fromů you best be worried about where I'm going. You see kids I'm not here trying to impress anyone. I'll leave that to the Bobby Taylor's of the world. They need people to impress. They need to be a king to someone.
Inferiority complex being what it is. I've never kissed ass. I've never done a damn favor to anyone to get over. I've done it my way and stayed true to myself. And God damn it, I'm still on top of the world. I'm living example you don't have to work the loop.
[A smile creases the Legend's lips.] LOCO: I say, "Break the loop." Smash it into a 100,000 little pieces! You worked so hard to silence me and I'm still here. Still in your face. Did you think it was going to be THAT easy? Did you think you where gonna somehow wish me away? Make me disappear forever? The attempt to silence a man is the greatest honor you can bestow on him. It means that you recognize his superiority to yourself. What you don't realize is that this is my world. When the first living thing was created, I was there, waiting. And when the last living thing dies, my job will be done. I'll put the chairs on the tables, turn out the lights and I'll lock the world behind me when I go.
Some may call this a shoot. Some may even try and pass it off as rhetoric. I say, the truth hits the hardest when it thumps you right between the eyes.
[LOCO manages a weak smile.] LOCO: That brings me to Kenny Curtis. Curtis, why won't you just die? Everywhere I turn you are there. In my match with O'Lachlan. In my face versus Moxy. At the damn carnival match. But the funny things is, until now, your name was never on the contract. I think you need to reexamine your obsession with yours truly. And your avoidance of the greatest wrestler the world has ever known?
So you want to go ahead and have a little "Country Whippin'" match? Want to go toe to toe with the great LOCO?
Well kid, I got news for you. I'll whip your ass here, there and everywhere. Regardless, all over the country.
[Weak smile.] LOCO: So before you get all excited and think that we've come to some kinda "conclusion," just be assured this is only the start. You're on my list and I won't stop till your 6 feet under clutching for your last breath.
You judge me but you don't know me. You will drown in your own hypocrisy before you realize I speak the truth.
They sayů Kill a man, and you are an assassin. Kill millions of men, and you are a conqueror. Kill everyone, and you are a God.
[LOCO smirks.] LOCO: You can call me God. [Camera fades to black.] -LOCO [yes...THAT LOCO]
