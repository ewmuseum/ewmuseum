-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Northern Lights Wrestling Promotions FAQ - 09/27/99 -=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Yes, after a year and a half of the great sleep, the NLWP is back! Northern Lights Wrestling Promotions was in existance from April 1994 - December 1996 offline, and went online in December 1996 until February 1998. The federation was host to such home grown talent as "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, Richard "Moxy" Blue, "To Excess" Rick Williams, as well as established stars like "Lone Wolf" Brody Thunder, Casey "Blackheart" James, and J.W.
Hardin. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Goal =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= With the help of established and new wrestlers, the NLWP will try to innovate once more. In this day and age cards have become so huge that they are almost unreadable, and many handlers only read their matches while skimming through the rest.
The administration would like to bring back the times when you could read a card from end to end without having to dedicate hours of your life to it. We would also like to help put together a product that captures that
"live" feeling you see on Monday nights TV. How to do this? More collaboration between the handlers. Shorter matches (6-15K). More time between cards to work on stuff. Read on.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Staff =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= [name withheld] ran the NLWP through it's entire existance. He has also served as VP of the SCRA, and is the handler of GLCW and EMWC's
"Heatseeker" Derek Mota. [name withheld] is the longtime handler of Kid Canuck in the NLWP, as well as presently running Chase Hunter in the UWF. Nick has written some classic
NLWP matches, and had been VP of the league for a short period of time. Nick will be the Vice President of the NLWP. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Cards =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The NLWP will be running on a bi-weekly schedule. This is for two reasons ... to give the handlers more time to react to flashes, and more time to write collaborative material. It takes time to do join in-rings and other flashes of the sort, so having a bit more time to do it should make it a bit easier.
NLWP Madness ... the flagship show of the NLWP. All the big matches, and many of the major angles will unfold here. 6-8 live matches per week.
Backstage Pass... a quick peek at wrestler's comments immediately following Madness. This is your chance for your wrestler to give his thoughts, or to setup angles for folowing cards. This one will only be a flash packet, broadcasted two days after Madness. Yes, this has been taken directly from the SCRA, but we started that card when I was running the league, so I take ownership of it. :)
This is the NLWP ... a full preview show, one week before Madness, covering each match one by one, as well as containing a small number
(3-5) of pre-taped matches. =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Titles =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= NLWP WORLD Title ... the big one. Previously held by "To Excess" Rick Williams, Nicholas Doom, "Cowboy" Ken Curtis, Casey "Blackheart" James and Jared Jameson. NLWP WORLD Tag Team Title ... although there won't be a dedicated tag team division in the NLWP, tag teams will be formed and broken, and a tag title will be defended on a part-time basis. There will be NO dedicated tag teams in the NLWP. However, handlers will be encouraged to enter the tag team division to further angles, or just have some fun.
NLWP North American Title ... previously held by Kid Canuck, Richard "Moxy" Blue, Jared Jameson, and Kid Ego. NLWP TV Title ... the starting point for most NLWP wrestlers. Many TV title matches will be fought under Triple Threat rules.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= The Cast =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Tony Mastino ... to many, Tony is the heart and soul of the NLWP. Mastino has many words to describe himself, all of them flattering. Is he really a made man? Do the "chicks" truly dig him? We're not sure, but he keeps us entertained week after week. He loves the technical wrestlers, which is different from many of the "heel" announcers.
Jim Vasquez ... the voice of reason in the NLWP. Vasquez is the consummate professional, not letting the constant ribbing of Tony Mastino get to him, even after all these years. Vasquez is a holds expert, knowing the names of all the moves, getting down and deep into the histories of the wrestlers that are fighting. He is the antithesis to
Mastino, trying to sell for everything that the "Mouth" is shooting down. Eric Eric ... the man with two first names ... and the same one, to boot! Eric is the ring announcer in the NLWP. Derek Anderson ... the former commissioner of the NLWP, now renegade. Is Anderson looking to manage some wrestlers? Is he just looking to cause chaos in general? After being dragged out of Maple Leaf Gardens by
Valtharius the Mad two years ago for gambling away the NLWP's equity, Anderson is back, and we'll only find out his agenda by watching NLWP Madness! The Jobber Gang ... the irrisistible "Handsome" Hance Johnson and his best friend "Adonis" Bobby Beauty ... Henry Pollen, the man who has only managed to win during allergy season ... Equinox Extreme, the mysterious masked jobber who usually ends up being a big name guy hidden under the mask ... Pasta Man, the 300 lb. italian pasta eating crazed wrestler ...
Tsunami, the 583 lb. mammoth ... The referees ... Andrew "Four Eyes" Jenkins ... head referee Mark Riles ... Ken Dorn ... Larry "Dr. Death" Williams (the man who "lets it go") =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Booking =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= As a handler in the NLWP, there will be a lot of teamwork and cooperation required in order to be successful. Yeah, you read that properly.
Working WITH the other handlers is highly encouraged. Because the league will be heavily angle booked, the wrestlers who are most "over" will receive the biggest pushes. Midcarders give their interviews, and then step into the ring to fight their matches, but the true main eventers are different.
How do you get your chunk of the spotlight, and get pushed? There's no real formula. But handlers who flash regularly and are usually involved in interesting feuds or angles will most likely be the ones with the biggest pushes. Have events occur during the weekly broadcasts, do in-rings or joint interviews with the wrestler you're feuding with. Just remember ... every fed has a wrestler that makes you want to skip to his matches or flashes just to see what's going to happen next with them.
That's the wrestler that it going to get pushed. The administration will assist any handler who is having difficulty finding angles for their wrestlers. We are idea people, and if we do get any we will share it with the handler.
Because it will be an angle based league, it is possible to push everybody in the league, and that is what the NLWP is trying to achieve.
Because the booking is done by giving wrestlers pushes, the wins/losses are a lot less important than the overall push. Yes, your wrestler will lose matches, no matter how good you are as a handler.
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= What we Expect =-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Well to be a bit different once more, there's two things that are expected. One flash per week. That's the standard thing. I don't think I need to elaborate here. One match per month. That's the different thing. :) Basically, there's a major shortage of writers in the game today. The same 6-8 writers pump out matches every week, and eventually get burned out, start writing poor matches, start handing them in late, etc. If we manage to spread the work out more evenly, hopefully there will be less burnout and the league will be able to survive longer.
Now I'll admit that sometimes you're unable to write a match. But you will be given monthly schedules, and will know well ahead of time of your monthly commitment. If you still can't write the match, I need to know 4 days in advance (when I do the assignments) that you can't write.
Backing out of matches at the last second WILL affect your wrestler's push, being the equivalent of not flashing before a match. Is this different? Yes. Is it asking too much? I don't think that an hour once a month is too much to ask to keep a federation going strong. The administration will be spending a lot of time on the fed. But we have regular lives too, and would like to go out once in a while, not just stay at home and write matches! Remember, keeping match lengths from
6-15K also makes the matches easier to write.
